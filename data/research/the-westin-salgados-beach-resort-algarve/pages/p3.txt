## In Season Restaurant

Inspired by the beauty of each season, the In Season is where fresh flavors and the rhythm of nature come together in perfect harmony, celebrating the finest local and seasonally sourced. Mornings unfold with a vibrant combination of buffet options, where signature ingredients that awakens the senses and set a refreshing tone for the day. As the day passes and evening arrives, the atmosphere transforms into an elegant and intimate atmosphere surrounded by nature and where à la carte refined options highlight the essences of the season for an experience that delights and inspires.

## Chiringuito Blue Al Fresco Restaurant & Bar

A true al fresco restaurante and pool bar, Chiringuito Blue al Fresco Restaurant & Bar captures the essence of relaxed, open-air dining. With the sun shining and the poolside ambiance setting the tone, guests can indulge in freshly grilled specialties, vibrant poke bowls, and crisp salads, all crafted with the finest ingredients for a light yet satisfying meal. The combination of breezy outdoor dining, bold flavors, and a vibrant atmosphere makes it the perfect spot to unwind and soak in the moment. During the high season, pool service allows guests to enjoy refreshing drinks and bites without leaving the water's edge, creating the perfect setting to unwind, refresh, and soak in the Algarve's radiant energy.

## Serenity Bar

A hidden oasis of tranquillity, Serenity Bar is a place to escape the rush and embrace pure relaxation. Designed as a serene retreat, it offers a soothing atmosphere, where natural elemnets and soft lighting create the perfect setting to unwind. Guests can indulge in a menu of refreshing, non alcoholic wellness beverages, infusions with fresh herbs and fruits, bringing a burst of flavor and vitality to every sip. As evenings fall, Serenity comes to life indoors and outdoor under the open sky. Warm summer nights unfold on the terrace, embraced by the ocean's gentle, salty breeze, where the glow of soft lighting and the rhythm of live music create an atmosphere of pure enchantment. Whether enjoying dinner al fresco or within serene indoor peace, every moment is designed to be savored in an unforgettable celebration of flavor, connection, and the Algarve's timeless beauty.

## In-Room Dining

Available 24 hours a day, In-Room Dining at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve invites guests to savor well-being at their own pace and rhythm. From energizing breakfasts and light afternoon snacks to wholesome meals and late-night snacks, every dish is thoughtfully prepared with balance and flavor in mind. Tailored options considered to every diet and family member are available so guests can enjoy nourishing meals, calming herbal tear, or revitalizing and energizing fresh juices. All, in the luxurious comfort and tranquility of their private room or balcony facing the sea, shimmering pools or resort gardens.

### Westin Eat Well Signature Programs

Our culinary philosophy honors Westin's commitment to nourishment, balance, and the joy of mindful eating. Thouthfully prepared with fresh, wholesome ingredients, each dish is craffted to restore energy and delight the senses. Designed for every age, every lifestyle, and diet, our menus make it effortless and delicious to eat and feel well throughout your stay.

### Westin Eat Well Menus

Restful sleep is at the heart of well-being. If you are unwinding after a day by the ocean or adjusting to the Algarve's gentle rhythm, every detail is designed to help you help you recharge. From the nourishing meals that promote balance to the soothing rituals that prepare both body and mind for rest, each element works in harmony to guide you into deep, restorative sleep so you awaken refreshed and renewed.

### Westin Eat Well Menus for Kids

Eating well begins at every age.
