# Discover the Essence of the Algarve

## Experiences

Embraced by the golden light and framed by the deep blue of the Atlantic Ocean, the Algarve is Portugal’s southern gem, a region of breathtaking coastlines, dramatic cliffs, and sun-kissed beaches where...See More every sense awakens. A year-round destination that blends natural beauty, cultural heritage, and Mediterranean gastronomy, the Algarve invites slow discovery and connection. Nestled within the Salgados Lagoon Nature Reserve, with direct access to Salgados Beach, The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve is surrounded by opportunities to explore and unwind. From an 18-hole golf course and birdwatching trails within a protected area to nearby waterparks, six refreshing outdoor swimming pools, an award-winning Wellness Hub, and inspiring activities for both families and children-focused, and access to a memoribilia of authentic and local knowledge through masterclasses and contact with local people.See Less

Family Adventures

## Experiences for Every Family Member

Family adventures unfold naturally at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve. From sunny days on the golden sands of the Salgados Beach to peaceful bike rides along the 10 km boardwalk that begins just steps from the resort and travels between the sea and the protected wetlands, every moment invites connection and discovery. Explore the Salgados Lagoon Nature Reserve, where birdwatching and wildlife encounters bring the landscape to life. And when the day slows, retreat to the comfort of your room or suite with the perfect setting for cozy family moments, from playing cards and board games to simply sharing stories and laughter. Here, nature, comfort, and togetherness exist in perfect harmony, creating memories that last long beyond the stay.

Family Adventures

## Westin Family Kids Club

At The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve, family moments are designed to inspire connection and well-being, especially for the younger guests.

The Westin Family Kids Club welcomes children aged 4 to 12 to a world where curiosity leads and creativity thrives. Rooted in nature and guided by Westin’s well-being philosophy, the program blends imaginative play with engaging, age-appropriate activities from creative workshops and nature walks to interactive games and themed days. Children are encouraged to explore, discover, and grow while families enjoy meaningful moments together. Here, every experience is designed to spark joy, curiosity, and balance for all ages. Aditional fees apply, full-day and half-day rates per child applies. Please refer to our Front Office Team for more information on how to enroll.

Swimming Pools

## Dive Into Serenity

Two family pool areas create the perfect setting for shared laughter and refreshing swims, while the main pool, designed for a more serene ambiance, offers poolside service (seasonal service) with Mediterranean delicacies and refreshing cocktails so guests can unwind in comfort under the Algarve bright and warm sun light. Guests over 16 can also enjoy access to the indoor vitality pool at the Wellness Hub Algarve, featuring soothing hydrotherapy elements that restore balance and ease the senses. Whether enjoying time together under the sun or embracing quiet solitude with a book, the tranquil setting encourages gratitude, mindfulness, and clarity. Here, every dip, every pause, and every view becomes a reminder that balance between energy and calm, play and focus is the true essence of well-being.

Local Authentic Activities

## A Journey Through the Authentic Algarve Roots

Immerse in the Algarve's rich traditions through experiences that connect culture, creativity, and well-being. At The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve, long-stay guests are invited to slow down and discover the region’s authentic soul through hands-on workshops and masterclasses that celebrate local artistry and flavour. Learn the ancient crafts of ceramic and tile painting, explore the delicate art of palm weaving, or take part in gastronomy sessions that showcase the Algarve’s most beloved delicacies. Each experience offers a meaningful connection to place and its people in  a mindful way to explore, create, and feel inspired by the timeless beauty of the Algarve. Please refer to our Front Office Team for local authentic experiences, subject to an additional fee.

Water Activities

## Where the Land Meets the Sea

With direct access to the golden sands of Salgados Beach, every stay at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve begins where the land meets the sea.

Guests can embrace the energy of the sea wonders through surf lessons for beginners and advanced riders, or explore the calm waters by kayak or canoe. For those seeking deeper discovery, scuba diving, fishing trips, and private boat tours reveal the Algarve’s coastal treasures from a new perspective. Adventurous spirits can soar above the ocean with paragliding, while families enjoy shared moments on guided boat tours along the Algarve’s stunning coastline and to discover the aquatic Algarve dolphin wonders. From thrill to tranquility, the ocean becomes your playground, and every experience, a reminder of nature’s beauty and balance.

## Golf in the Algarve

Renowned as one of Europe’s premier golf destinations, the Algarve offers world-class courses set amid breathtaking coastal and natural scenery.

Just moments from The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve, the Salgados Golf Course unfolds between golden beach dunes, serene lakes, and protected wetlands in a place where nature and sport exist in perfect harmony. From perfecting the swing to discovering the game for the first time, the region’s mild climate, exceptional cutting-edge facilities, and inspiring views create the ideal setting to play all year round. After a day on the course, the Recovery Room powered by Hyperice® at the Wellness Hub Algarve provides advanced therapies to relieve muscular tension and enhance recovery helping every golfer, professional or enthusiast, stay at the top of their game.

### Walk Straight to the Beach

Just steps away from the golden sands, The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve offers direct access to Salgados Beach where the sound of the ocean sets the rhythm of each day. Guests can begin the morning with a peaceful walk along the shoreline, enjoy invigorating swims, or take part in exciting water activities such as surfing, paddleboarding, kayaking, or boat tours. Families can spend long afternoons by the sea, while couples embrace tranquil sunsets framed by the Atlantic horizon.
