## The Restorative Power of the Heavenly® Bed

Embrace the transformative power of restorative sleep. Wrapped in crisp linens and cloud comfort, here at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve, the award-winning Westin Heavenly Bed invites you to drift into deep rest and awaken renewed, essential for you to embrance a newly day. As evening falls, unwind with the Sleep Well Lavender Oil Ritual, letting the soothing scent calm the senses and prepare the mind for peaceful dreams and a restorative sleep, so you wake refreshed and ready to greet a new day surrounded by the sunny Algarve.

## The Essence of Calm White Tea Aloe Amenities

Experience well-being in every detail, from the refreshing Westin White Tea Aloe bath amenities, to the gentle lighting and serene ambiance that invite calm and clarity throughout the day.

## A Stay Tailored to Every Journey

From one of the 95 serene rooms and suites, or one of 42 private residences, each accommodation is a sanctuary of comfort and style. With inspiring views over the sea, swimming pools or resort gardens, some of our premium and spacious accommodations with one, two or three bedrooms feature a fully equipped kitchen, separate room, and living area specially designed for family connection and shared moments, or quiet focus or a peaceful time. Whether seeking a quiet retreat for relaxation, shared moments or family connection, or even a peaceful space to work, every accommodation invites relaxation and renewal just steps from the Atlantic Ocean and embraced by the Salgados Lagoon Nature Reserve and the Salgados Beach.

## Inspired Views That Restore and Awaken

Each private balcony invites a moment of stillness, framed by the natural beauty that surrounds The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve. From rooms overlooking the panoramic deep blue of the Atlantic Ocean and the golden sands of Salgados Beach to those capturing the shimmering reflections of the swimming pools or the lush greenery of the resort gardens, every view offers a different perspective of serenity. For guests contemplating the horizon or the tranquil rhythm of life within the resort, each view connects you to the Algarve's essence, the sea, sun, and nature existing in perfect harmony and creating a sense of calm renewal that lingers long after a stay.

## In-Room Dining

Savor moments of calm and connection in the privacy of your room. From wholesome breakfasts on the sunny balcony to comforting evening meals, every dish if thoughtfully crafted to nourish body and mind. With balanced options designed for every preference and dietary in need, in-room dining becomes an experience of pure well-being, fresh, mindful, and deeply restorative.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve have a work space?

No, the rooms at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 6 at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve?

No, the rooms at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Salgados Beach Resort, Algarve have hair dryers.
