## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is The Savoy Hotel located?

A true 5-star icon in the heart of London, no hotel can rival The Savoy's superlative location at the Strand, on the Northbank of the River Thames. Ideally located for guests looking to explore the prestigious neighborhoods of Knightsbridge and Mayfair, the legendary West End and the lively scene of Covent Garden.

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Savoy?

Check-in time is guaranteed from 3:00pm and Check-out time is at 12:00 noon. Should you arrive in advance of check-in time and your room be in the final stages of preparation, the team would be delighted to store your luggage and extend the full guest facilities for your usage until your room is ready.

What are some of the property amenities at The Savoy?

**Concierge**

The Savoy has an expert Concierge team who will assist with restaurant reservations, city and tour information, theatre tickets, flight transfers, and any other requests guests may have.

**Florists**

The Savoy’s in-house creative floral team are on hand to advise and help plan every aspect of your stay or event down to the very last detail, whether it’s an exquisite bouquet for a special occasion, or an elaborate display. Flowers can be ordered by both In-House-Guests and non-residents.

**Indoor Pool**

Located on the third floor of the hotel, a magnificent swimming pool is situated in its own atrium with natural light and equipped with a jet-stream for the more energetic to swim against the current. For total relaxation there is a sauna and steam room in the separate male and female changing areas, and loungers around the pool.

**Gym**

Located on the third floor of the hotel, featuring state-of-the art facilities with weights and cardiovascular equipment in the two gym areas.

**Laundry Service**

The hotel is proud to offer same-day dry-cleaning and laundry services to our residents. Shoeshine service and pressing services are also available.

**Housekeeping**

The Housekeeping department are pleased to offer twice-daily maid service to ensure the best possible comfort to all our guests.

How can I get to The Savoy from the nearest airport?

**London Heathrow Airport**

Approximately 20 miles west of The Savoy, London Heathrow Airport is under 50 minutes by car, 1 hour 15 minutes by London Underground, or 15 minutes by Heathrow Express to Paddington Station and then a further 30 minutes by car.

**London Gatwick Airport**

Approximately 35 miles south of The Savoy, London Gatwick Airport is under 1 hour 40 minutes by car, or 30 minutes by Gatwick Express to Victoria station and then a further 15 minutes by London Underground.

**London City Airport**

Approximately 8 miles east of The Savoy, London City Airport is under 45 minutes by car, or under 40 minutes by London Underground and DLR services.

Is there a concierge service at The Savoy?

The Concierge desk is located in the Front Hall and is open from 8.00am to 10.00pm daily. They may assist with restaurant reservations, city and tour information, theatre tickets, return flight confirmation, sports and entertainment tickets as well as other requests you may have during your London visit at our luxury hotel. Airport transfers to be requested via email at: svy.concierge@fairmont.com

What is the cancellation policy at The Savoy?

No cancellation charge applies prior to 14:00 (local time), up to 1 day prior to arrival. Beyond that time, the first night will be charged.
