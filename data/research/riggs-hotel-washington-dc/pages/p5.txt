Our guest loyalty program is designed around you. LORE DISCOVERY gives you a world of benefits suited to the way you travel. Earn rewards and use them whenever you want, wherever you want. LORE DISCOVERY lets you write your own story.

## Loyalty, Your Way

Like everything we do, LORE DISCOVERY has been designed to put you first. Expect new experiences, unique upgrades, offers tailored just for you and countless other bespoke benefits allowing you tell your own story.

It all starts the moment you sign up with rewards from your very first stay – and each and every stay from then on.

The more you stay, the more you earn. As you progress through each loyalty tier you receive additional perks and rewards while banking DISCOVERY Dollars (D$), which you can spend in over 800 destinations around the world.

## Join

Signing up is simple. Once you’ve done this, you’re a LORE DISCOVERY member and immediately start earning rewards (D$) on your stays, both at our hotels and countless others around the globe.

## Earn

Earning rewards is the easy part. You’ll earn rewards whenever you book with us, or with your travel agent, using your membership. You’ll earn D$ on stays and in our bars and restaurants.

## Spend

Use your newly earned loyalty currency to book a stay, upgrade your room, or indulge in our bars or restaurants. Simply put your D$ towards your bill when you check-out, with us or over 800 other global hotels.

## The Lore Discovery Membership Tiers

### Silver - The Start of your Story

Say hello to rewards, from the moment you sign up. Start your story with us and enjoy local offers and experiences, plus you’ll be earning D$ to redeem wherever, and whenever you want.

- Earn/redeem D$ on eligible spend (4%)
- Local offers and experiences

### Gold - The Next Chapter

If you’ve spent a few nights with us, or over $1,000, it’s time to say hello to Gold. You’ll earn even more D$ whilst enjoying additional offers and experiences, so you can savour every moment.

- Earn/redeem D$ on eligible spend (5%)
- Local offers and experiences

### Platinum - Upgrades Aplenty

When you’ve stayed with us for 10 nights or spent $5,000, you’ve entered your Platinum era. You’ll be earning an enhanced rate of D$, plus all your favourite DISCOVERY perks. Oh, we always forgot, you’ll also be getting upgrades on every stay, on us.

- Earn/redeem D$ on eligible spend (6%)
- Local offers and experiences
- Late check-out (subject to availability)
- Upgrade your room (subject to availability)

### Titanium - Top Tier Status

At this point, after staying 30 nights or over $15,000, you’re practically family – and we’ll treat you like family. Earn even more rewards, enjoy more offers, more experiences, check-in even earlier, stay with us longer and of course, enjoy a double room upgrade on every stay.

- Earn/redeem D$ on eligible spend (7%)
- Complimentary breakfast
- Local offers and experiences
- Early check-in (subject to availability)
- Late check-out (subject to availability)
- Double upgrade your room (subject to availability)
