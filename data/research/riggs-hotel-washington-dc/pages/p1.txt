# Riggs Washington DC

## THE PAST, PRESENT, AND FUTURE OF PENN QUARTER

Constructed in 1891, as a headquarters for Riggs National Bank, the landmark was known as the Bank of Presidents, residing at the intersection not just of 9th and F Street, but of American history. In the present day Riggs DC is recognized as a 5-star hotel in Washington D.C., which delights countless guests every year.

## Reinvention of an Icon

#### A luxury hotel in downtown D.C., enriched with history

As one of the best luxury hotels in Washington D.C., Riggs is located in the historical former Riggs National Bank building on the corner of 9th and F Street. Paying homage to the building’s legacy, each of the 181 guest rooms and suites are reminiscent of a private safety deposit box, while moments throughout the 5-star hotel draw on a narrative inspired by the golden age of banking.

The heart and soul of the hotel is Café Riggs – a modern, all-occasion restaurant – as well as a lower-level bar housed in the original bank vault from the internationally-acclaimed Mr Lyan team.

Riggs DC is a storied landmark with a modern spirit.

## Silver Lyan

#### A REIMAGINED HOTEL BAR FOR INVENTIVE COCKTAILS

Silver Lyan – nestled in the former building’s original bank vault – is the first bar outside of Europe from Mr Lyan. Traveling the world, he sources the weird and wonderful, using bespoke techniques and experimenting with ingredients to develop new ways to capture flavor. Silver Lyan draws its inspiration from D.C.’s incredible depth of culture, food, drink, people and history to offer a bar that celebrates the notion of exchange – as well as a damn good time.
