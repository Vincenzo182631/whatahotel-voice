# Our Restaurant & Bars in Downtown DC

Sip on a globally influenced cocktail in Silver Lyan or relax in an all-day dining setting in Café Riggs, inspired by the grand brasseries of Europe. Riggs Washington DC is home to some of the best restaurants and bars in Washington D.C., awaiting you with unique dining experiences during your luxury stay in our 5-star hotel in Dupont Circle.

If you’re looking for places to eat in Washington D.C. or after a bar to try in Penn Quarter, visit our incredibly stylish and contemporary outlets for a world-class cocktail, a traditional dinner or book a private dining space for smaller celebrations.

## Café Riggs

Café Riggs offers the grand tradition of French cuisine with a contemporary American brasserie. Serving an all-day menu from the bar, dining room and café, guests are welcomed to enjoy a casual drink or celebratory meal against the backdrop of the space’s ornate ceiling and original Corinthian columns.

A truly unique and gorgeous café in Washington D.C.!

## Silver Lyan

Step into the inviting underground world created by bar visionary Ryan Chetiyawardana ("Mr Lyan") in Riggs Washington DC’s historic bank vault, where each drink pays homage to the spices, flavors and traditions that make up America’s diverse cultural heritage.

One of the most popular cocktail bars in downtown D.C. awaits with deliciously modern drinks that both respect traditional recipes and embark on new journeys for an unforgettable bar experience.

## Private Dining in D.C.

Special event spaces can be found in Café Riggs, which is home to a semi-private dining room for seated dinners and reception-style events. We also offer a beautifully designed and furnished private dining room that is perfect for seated meetings, lunches and dinners, and receptions.

For receptions in the main dining room, book our communal table that will put you and your guests in the center of attention. Our lower-level bar, Silver Lyan, features our Cabinet Room, offering semi-private lounge space with globally inspired cocktails from the acclaimed Mr Lyan.

Perfect for a variety of celebrations and smaller events in Washington D.C.
