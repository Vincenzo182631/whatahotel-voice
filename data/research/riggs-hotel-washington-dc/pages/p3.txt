# Café Riggs

In the grand tradition of French cuisine, Café Riggs presents a contemporary American brasserie that reimagines classic dishes within a vibrant setting in the heart of Washington D.C. With delicious flavors, impeccable presentation and a stunning space to remember, our downtown D.C. café at Riggs will be perfect for an unforgettable dining experience.

Whether you’re staying in our luxury 5-star hotel or visiting us for one night only, our restaurant will enchant you with its atmosphere, style and magical meals.

Book a table

## All Day Dining

Nestled on the Riggs Washington DC’s ground floor, the restaurant serves all-day menus from the bar, café and dining room, ready to provide a great dining experience no matter the time of the day.

Serving one of the best brunches in Washington D.C. over the weekends, Café Riggs offers limitless champagne and decadent classics like French toast and eggs royale for a hearty start of the day.

The dinner menu offers refined, French-inspired dishes including seafood plateaus and French onion soup.
