## Afternoon Tea at The Houston Grand

Step into the timeless elegance of our Tea Lounge and experience the cherished tradition of Afternoon Tea at the Houston Grand Hotel. Surrounded by graceful décor and a warm, inviting atmosphere, guests are treated to an exquisite selection of premium loose-leaf teas, artfully prepared finger sandwiches, freshly baked scones served with clotted cream and preserves, and a tempting assortment of handcrafted pastries. As the gentle melodies of live harp music fill the room, every detail is designed to create a relaxing and sophisticated midday retreat—perfect for celebrating special occasions, gathering with friends, or simply indulging in a moment of refined luxury.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks?

The check-in time at Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 80.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks?

On-site valet parking is available at Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks. The daily valet fee is $63.87 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks booking.

What is the closest airport near Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks?

The closest airport near Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks is William P Hobby Airport (HOU). HOU is located approximately 20.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Houston Grand Hotel - River Oaks has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
