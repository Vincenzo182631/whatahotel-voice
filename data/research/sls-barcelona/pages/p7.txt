# RESTAURANTS & BARS

#### AT SLS BARCELONA

Long lunches, sunset cocktails, rooftop celebrations and unforgettable nights await at SLS Barcelona. Start at Cosmico, the city’s newest rooftop pool club, where panoramic views, fresh sushi, signature cocktails and DJ-led sunset sessions set the mood. Continue the evening at Lora with vibrant Mediterranean dining that effortlessly transforms into lively after-dinner celebrations, before discovering Coral, where relaxed all-day dining captures the spirit of Barcelona’s waterfront.
