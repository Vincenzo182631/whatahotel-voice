# a WATERFRONT WONDERLAND In Barcelona

SLS Barcelona sets the stage for unforgettable days and electric nights. Chase the last rays of sunshine and settle into your own SLS escape. From stunning pool to vibrant restaurants, immersive dining and a social calendar filled with sunset sessions, rooftop DJs, after-dinner parties and exclusive events, every experience is designed around the energy of Barcelona’s waterfront.

Welcome to SLS Barcelona, a five-star waterfront resort that answers Barcelona’s famous spirit with a wonderland of extraordinary experiences.

## Pools

Three pools, three moods. Two rooftop infinity pools offer panoramic sea and city views during the summer season and are now closed until next year. At ground level, Coral remains open year-round, with its boardwalk setting and laid-back Mediterranean atmosphere. For a deeper escape, the spa’s immersive jacuzzi offers a serene wellness experience.
