# SLS BARCELONA LOCATION & SURROUNDINGS

SLS Barcelona is set on the marina at Port Fòrum, steps from the beach and only 15 minutes from the city center. From cultural icons like Sagrada Familia and Park Güell to shopping at La Roca Village and major venues like Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya, the best of Barcelona is within easy reach.

### PORT FORUM

**~5 min walk**

Offering direct marina access, guests of SLS Barcelona can charter catamarans and private boats from Port Fòrum, as well as hire jet skis for halcyon summer days spent on the water. A tranquil neighbourhood beach known only to locals and insiders is a five-minute stroll away, while the cultural landmarks and sights of Barcelona can be reached in twenty minutes by car (or boat, naturally).

### ICONIC ATTRACTIONS

Iconic attractions aren’t just within reach, they’re part of the playground. From the adrenaline rush of F1 Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya to the fashion vibes of La Roca Village, from the dreamlike wonders of Sagrada Familia and Park Güell to the golden stretches of Barcelona’s beaches, every hotspot is just a ride away.

City Center (Gothic Quarter, Las Ramblas): ~15 min drive

Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya (Montmeló): ~25 min drive

La Roca Village: ~30 min drive

Sagrada Familia: ~20 min drive

Park Güell: ~25 min drive

Tibidabo Park: ~30 min drive

Barceloneta Beach: ~10 min drive

Ocata Beach: ~20 min drive

### INTERNATIONAL BARCELONA CONVENTION CENTRE (CCIB)

**~10 min walk**

Just moments from SLS Barcelona, the International Barcelona Convention Centre (CCIB) is one of the city’s leading venues for conferences, exhibitions, and global events. Its waterfront setting in Diagonal Mar makes it easy for business travelers to move seamlessly between meetings and relaxation, with the hotel offering a stylish base only a short walk away.

### city events calendar

Explore the events that move us across the city. From the hottest festivals showcasing the world’s greatest musical artists – like Primavera Sound which we are minutes’ walk from – to the iconic sporting events turning Barcelona into race tracks for race drivers and cyclists alike, there’s always a reason to visit Barcelona, no matter the time of year. And there’s always so many more reasons to stay at SLS Barcelona.
