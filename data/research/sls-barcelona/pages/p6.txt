## THE HALCYON CIRCUIT

Discover everything Halcyon Water Circuit has to offer. Guests who have booked a treatment at the spa will enjoy 90 minutes of access to Halcyon facilities or 90 or 120 minute access is available for purchase.

### Jacuzzi

A multi-sensory centerpiece where water, light, and motion converge. Cinematic visuals surround the space as jets move through the jacuzzi and a gentle rain shower falls from above, creating a fully enveloping experience designed to reset the body and slow the mind.

### Sauna

**Finnish Sauna** – Intense dry heat (80–100°C) encourages deep relaxation, supports the respiratory system, and eases tired muscles, enhanced by the natural warmth and aroma of wood.

**Soft Sauna** – A gentler approach to heat. With lower temperatures (50–60°C) and balanced humidity, this sauna promotes relaxation and skin hydration, offering a slower, more restorative experience.

### Steam Bath

High humidity and enveloping heat encourage muscle release, stimulate circulation, and support a natural sense of wellbeing, leaving the body relaxed and the mood noticeably lifted.

### Experience Shower

**Dousing Shower:** A traditional contrast to heat-based experiences. A cascade of cold water invigorates circulation, sharpens the senses, and completes the sauna ritual.

**Tropical Rain Shower:** A warm, aromatic rainfall infused with passion fruit notes and bathed in amber light, designed to soothe the nervous system and restore balance.

**Cold Rain Shower:** A refreshing burst of cool water paired with green light and citrus notes, ideal for reawakening the body after heat or steam.

#### Heated Lounge

A sculptural space centered around a heated circular moonstone platform. Gentle warmth, soft lighting, and an enveloping ceiling design create an atmosphere of deep calm, encouraging rest, reflection, and quiet restoration.

#### Relaxation Lounge

A serene space designed for pause. Guests are invited to unwind before or after treatments, allowing time for the body to settle and the experience to fully unfold.

### Pool state of mind

SLS Barcelona is known for its pools. One perched just above the boardwalk and two on the rooftop with sweeping views of city and sea. Halcyon extends the signature into the spa with the property’s immersive jacuzzi and most transportive experience. Get ready to dive deeper.
