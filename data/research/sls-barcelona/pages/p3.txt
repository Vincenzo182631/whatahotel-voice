# POOL state of mind

Two rooftop pools offer panoramic views of the sea and the city. At ground level, a boardwalk pool inspired by the iconic summers of Miami’s South Beach captures a sun-soaked, laid-back vibe. Inside, the spa’s immersive jacuzzi provides a serene escape year-round.

### coral

Perched just above the boardwalk, Coral is inspired by the iconic summers of Miami’s South Beach, capturing a sun-soaked, laid-back vibe. Relax by the family-friendly pool, enjoy refreshing drinks and casual dining, and take in beautiful views over the marina and the Mediterranean.

Discover Coral

### cosmico pools

Escape to Cosmico’s two stunning infinity pools, where the Pool Club experience comes to life high above the Mediterranean. Unwind on luxurious sunbeds, sip signature cocktails, and soak up the vibrant atmosphere while enjoying breathtaking panoramic views over the sea, marina, and Barcelona skyline.

Whether you’re looking to relax under the sun or embrace the energy of poolside beats, Cosmico offers the ultimate rooftop escape.

Cosmico is now closed for the winter season. We look forward to welcoming you back next summer for the 2027 season.

Discover Cosmico Pool Club

### immersive jacuzzi

Located at Halcyon Spa, this multi-sensory centerpiece is where water, light, and motion converge. Cinematic visuals surround the space as jets move through the jacuzzi and a gentle rain shower falls from above, creating a fully enveloping experience designed to reset the body and slow the mind.

Discover Halcyon
