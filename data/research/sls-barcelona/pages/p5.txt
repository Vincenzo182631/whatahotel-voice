# FESTIVE SEASON

When the season arrives, SLS Barcelona knows how to celebrate. Nights unfold from candlelit dinners to late-night revelry, bringing together bold flavours, flowing cocktails, live music and the kind of energy that keeps the party going.

From After Lora Halloween edition to Christmas dinner, a glamorous New Year’s Eve and one unforgettable night of dancing until sunrise, each celebration has its own rhythm, its own attitude, and a little SLS mischief.

* * *

### AFTER LORA HALLOWEEN EDITION

**SATURDAY, OCTOBER 31**

Festive dinner that turns into a party. Join us at Lora on Saturday, 31 October for a special edition of After Lora with fire-led Mediterranean cuisine, cocktails, DJ set and a night that goes all the way until 2AM. Come for dinner. Stay for the party.

### CHRISTMAS AT LORA

Christmas night at Lora brings Mediterranean dining to life with a festive twist; wood-fired flavours, indulgent sharing plates, flowing wine, and a warm, glamorous atmosphere made for celebrating, lingering, and letting the night unfold.

On December 24th, 25th and 26th, discover our special à la carte Christmas dishes, available alongside the regular menu.

### NEW YEAR’S EVE

New Year’s Eve at SLS Barcelona is made for going all in. The night begins in the iconic Gala Ballroom with an indulgent dinner, flowing cocktails, live music and a little festive glamour, before the tables give way to the dance floor.

Then, as midnight comes and goes, the celebration shifts into high gear. Follow the music into the after party and dance all night with bold beats, cocktails and the unmistakable SLS energy carrying you into the new year.

#### DINNER AT LORA

Lora welcomes you into the New Year with a special menu created for the occasion, bringing together wood-fired flavours, Mediterranean warmth and celebratory indulgence. Raise a glass, savour every course, and let the night begin.
