# One of the Best 5 Star Luxury Hotels in Banjara Hills, Hyderabad \| Park Hyatt Hyderabad

## A Luxury Urban Hotel in Hyderabad’s Banjara Hills

Situated in the heart of the city’s most distinguished neighborhood, Park Hyatt Hyderabad embodies contemporary luxury for discerning global travelers. Surrounded by vibrant cultural landmarks, upscale shopping, and expansive green spaces, the hotel offers an intimate ambiance and personalized service. Featuring 209 elegantly designed rooms and suites along with 41 private residences, this residential-style retreat seamlessly blends understated sophistication with the cosmopolitan spirit of Hyderabad.

## Discover Park Hyatt Hyderabad

Art Walkway

Park Hyatt Hyderabad is home to some of the world’s finest art. Explore the Art Walkway, located on the lobby level, to view a stunning collection of paintings and sculptures that enhance the beauty of the hotel’s contemporary interior. Showcasing the finest in acclaimed and budding artists, Artwalk at Park Hyatt Hyderabad is one of the city’s leading galleries and includes all mediums for a complete experience.

Commitment to Sustainability

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available on request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring to ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

Premium Suites & Residences

Experience elevated urban living in our premium suites and private residences, designed with contemporary residential-style interiors and thoughtful details. Offering expansive layouts, warm finishes, and panoramic views of Banjara Hills, each space is crafted to deliver refined comfort and understated luxury in the heart of Hyderabad.

Pool

Revel in a panoramic outdoor pool and a pool deck spanning more than 500 square metres. The temperature-controlled pool is complemented by cosy cabanas with a spectacular view—a perfect place to relax and unwind whilst enjoying a variety of beverages and snacks from our menu.

THE SPA

Discover a luxury spa experience in the heart of Banjara Hills. Inspired by the wellness traditions of the Nizams and enhanced with modern therapeutic techniques, our spa is a serene retreat designed to restore balance and renew the senses. Indulge in signature rituals, couples’ therapies, and holistic treatments that draw on timeless healing practices.

Open daily from 10:00 a.m. – 10:00 p.m.

Fitness Centre

Whether you’re seeking a light workout or a full-body routine, our Fitness Centre is your playground. Located on the mezzanine, enjoy the stunning pool view as you work out.

Open daily from 6 a.m.–9 p.m.

Curated City Tours

Park Hyatt Hyderabad curates city tours on request. Enjoy the heritage and culture of the “City of Pearls” on a tailored tour by Park Hyatt Hyderabad. Witness and experience the local flavours and the unexplored parts of Hyderabad like never before, with expert guides who share their best-kept secret locales. From the famous monuments to authentic home-cooked food by locals, create memories of a lifetime with a curated Park Hyatt tour.

## Things to Do

A stay at this luxury hotel in the heart of Hyderabad puts you close to top historic sites, parks, golf courses and neighbourhoods. Explore Jubilee Hills and Banjara Hills, two residential neighbourhoods less than 10 minutes from our doorstep that feature scenic walking trails and greenspaces. Tee off at Boulder Hills Golf Course or tour the vibrant streets of Laad Bazaar. For a little history, visit the 16th-century Golconda Fort and the stunning Qutub Shahi tombs and mosques.
