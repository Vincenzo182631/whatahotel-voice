## Afternoon Tea at Fitz's Brasserie

Step into Bloomsbury, where the timeless ritual of afternoon tea is enriched with history and creativity. Inspired by the 19th-century innovation of Anna, Duchess of Bedford – a tradition favoured even by Queen Victoria – Kimpton Fitzroy London celebrates this elegant pause in the day with the return of Afternoon Tea at Fitz’s Brasserie. Set within interiors that echo Bloomsbury’s artistic legacy and grandeur, it’s an invitation to linger, converse, and savour a moment of indulgence.

### Fitz's Private Dining

Fitz’s Brasserie presents its recently renovated private dining rooms, combining contemporary style with quintessentially British charm.

At Fitz’s, we want your event to be inspiring and effortless. We pride ourselves on providing thoughtful touches, menus to suit any event and spacious rooms that evoke the same charisma and excitement expected from Fitz’s Russell Sq.

With traditional wall panelling, streams of natural daylight, and subtle modern touches, our private dining rooms accommodate up to 40 guests, offering various layouts and styling options. Our thoughtfully crafted menus showcase the finest seasonal British produce, and are customisable to suit your wants and needs.

Transform these spaces into intimate settings tailored for any event, from elegant soirées to heartfelt gatherings.

### Amenities

From hosted evening drinks to our fully equipped fitness centre, chic on-property restaurants and bars, and luxurious bath amenities, the refined perks at Kimpton Fitzroy London will elevate your stay to new heights.

View all amenities

### Rooms

Our 334 guest rooms and suites are chic, neutral-toned retreats equipped with modern amenities. Each room features commissioned artwork and luxurious linens. Some rooms even include Penguin clothbound classics to inspire the creative energy of Virginia Woolf and other Bloomsbury luminaries. Guests can enjoy curated amenities such as Nespresso coffee machines, yoga mats, and Ruark Bluetooth speakers, ensuring a comfortable and enriching stay.

View all accommodations

### Destination

Overlooking a lovely garden square in the heart of central London, Kimpton Fitzroy London is perfectly poised to take advantage of the city’s many delights. Bloomsbury, London’s bona fide cultural and literary district, is home to the British Museum and countless boutiques and bookshops. The neighbourhoods of Fitzrovia, Marylebone, Soho, and Covent Garden are only a moment away.
