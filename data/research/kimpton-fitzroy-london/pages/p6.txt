At Kimpton Fitzroy London, history and modernity intertwine seamlessly. Nestled in the heart of central London, this iconic hotel occupies the entire eastern side of Russell Square Gardens, inviting guests to experience the authentic charm of Bloomsbury. The 19th-century architecture, with its palatial grandeur, sets the stage for chic and contemporary interiors that beckon the discerning traveller. Whether you enjoy a cocktail at Fitz's Bar & Parlour or a delightful meal at Fitz's Brasserie, you'll find the perfect setting to unwind.

The vibrant local culture of Bloomsbury is just moments away, offering an enchanting retreat where you can explore literary history, lush gardens, and lively cafés. Kimpton Fitzroy London provides a luxurious base to experience London's rich history in style, with key attractions and landmarks just a stone's throw away. Discover the perfect blend of elegance and contemporary comfort at this extraordinary hotel.

### The Fitzroy Suite

Sitting proudly in iconic surroundings, enjoy our largest suite composed of four separate spaces in charming Bloomsbury - the ultimate central London retreat. Spread out on the indulgent king size bed; dine in-suite with breathtaking views of leafy Russell Square; or relax by the ornamental original fireplace with a book. Beautifully styled with subtle nods to the hotel’s past, this stylish abode is as charming inside as the views outside.

Does Kimpton Fitzroy London have a pool?

No, Kimpton Fitzroy London does not have a pool.

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Fitzroy London for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Fitzroy London have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Fitzroy London has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Fitzroy London?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Fitzroy London.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Fitzroy London?

Check-in at Kimpton Fitzroy London is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Fitzroy London offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Fitzroy London offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Fitzroy London from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Fitzroy London is conveniently located near London City Airport (LCY), approximately 10 miles away (16.09 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Fitzroy London?

Kimpton Fitzroy London is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Warner Bros. Tour: The Making of Harry Potter-32 miles away (51.5 kilometers)
- The British Museum-0.3 miles away (0.48 kilometers)
- The British Library-1 miles away (1.61 kilometers)
- Sir John Soane's Museum-1 miles away (1.61 kilometers)
- The Charles Dickens Museum-0.6 miles away (0.97 kilometers)
- Liberty London-1.5 miles away (2.41 kilometers)
- Covent Garden-1.2 miles away (1.93 kilometers)
- Regent's Park-2 miles away (3.22 kilometers)
- Borough Market-2.5 miles away (4.02 kilometers)
- Notting Hill-4 miles away (6.44 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Fitzroy London puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
