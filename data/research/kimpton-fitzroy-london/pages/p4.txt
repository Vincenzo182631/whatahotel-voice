Discover the ultimate way to stay well on the go at Kimpton Fitzroy. Whether you’re visiting London for business or leisure, we’ve teamed up with Technogym and Swell Sound Therapy to bring you a range of refreshing ways to relax and recharge during your stay.

### Sustainability

#### Sustainability Certification

- Our hotel has a third-party sustainability certification.

**Certifications: Green Key (FEE)**

Our hotel is certified with Green Key (FEE), a globally recognised eco-label run by the Foundation for Environmental Education that is awarded to tourism and hospitality facilities that demonstrate outstanding commitment to environmental sustainability. Certified hotels must have implemented a range of measures across multiple categories including energy (e.g. energy-saving lightbulbs), water (e.g. water-saving taps, showers, and toilets) and waste (food waste and single-use plastic reduction schemes).

### Dining

Our bars and restaurants range from Fitz’s Brasserie, the decadent splendour of Fitz's Russell Sq., a unique space comprised of a sophisticated eatery, to a glamorous drinking den, Fitz’s Bar & Parlour.

### Rooms

Our 334 guest rooms and suites are chic, neutral-toned retreats equipped with modern amenities. Each room features commissioned artwork and luxurious linens. Some rooms even include Penguin clothbound classics to inspire the creative energy of Virginia Woolf and other Bloomsbury luminaries. Guests can enjoy curated amenities such as Nespresso coffee machines, yoga mats, and Ruark Bluetooth speakers, ensuring a comfortable and enriching stay.

View all accommodations

### Destination

Overlooking a lovely garden square in the heart of central London, Kimpton Fitzroy London is perfectly poised to take advantage of the city’s many delights. Bloomsbury, London’s bona fide cultural and literary district, is home to the British Museum and countless boutiques and bookshops. The neighbourhoods of Fitzrovia, Marylebone, Soho, and Covent Garden are only a moment away.
