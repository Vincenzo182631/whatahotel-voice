#### **1General Terms and Conditions**

- The benefits are valid exclusively for new bookings for room-only or package stays made through the official website www.baglionihotels.com, for stays within the dates of the offer.
- To enjoy the benefits, check-out must take place by January 1, 2027.
- The 5% discount applies exclusively to semi-flexible rates, which allow cancellation free of charge up to 5 days before the arrival date. In the event of cancellation or amendment after the stated deadlines, as well as in the event of a no-show, a penalty equal to 100% of the total stay will apply.
- The 20% discount applies exclusively to the non-refundable prepaid rate.
- The offer may be combined with other promotions or special rates.

#### **215% Savings on Selected Suites**

- Savings of up to 15% apply exclusively to the following suite categories: Florentine Suite, De Pepi Suite, Da Verrazzano Suite.
- Savings are subject to availability at the time of booking and are not applicable to other room or suite categories.

#### **3Experience Credit**

- The Experience Credit of EUR 25 is granted per room, per night.
- For stays longer than three (3) nights, the credit amounts to EUR 50 per room, per night.
- The Experience Credit may be used exclusively toward the property’s dining offerings.
- The Experience Credit is non-convertible to cash, non-refundable, and non-transferable.
- Any unused Experience Credit during the stay will not be refunded nor converted into future credit.

#### **410% Savings**

- The 10% discount applies to minibar consumption and branded products.

#### **5Complimentary breakfast**

- **Complimentary breakfast is included for all guests included in the reservation, per room, for the duration of the stay.**
- Complimentary breakfast is served exclusively in restaurants at our hotels and resorts.
- Breakfast offerings may vary per property. Some properties may also offer additional breakfast complements at an additional cost.
- Complimentary Breakfast and Complimentary Stay for Kids & Teens may be subject to change or termination at any time without prior notice.

#### **6Complimentary stay for kids & teens**

- The "Complimentary Stay for Kids & Teens" promotion is applicable to kids and teens aged 0-17 years old.
- One paying adult must be in the same room in order to receive the "Complimentary Stay for Kids & Teens" promotion.
- Children's ages must be reported in the reservation and proof of age will be required upon check-in.
- If the child is 18 years old or above at the time of check-in, the difference will be charged directly to the guest at that moment.
- Maximum occupancy per room category applies.

#### **7Welcome bottle of wine**

- One (1) complimentary welcome bottle of wine per room, per length of stay.
- Bottle size and brand are subject to availability.
