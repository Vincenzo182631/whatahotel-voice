## Palazzo Firenze, Heritage and Design in Santa Croce

Stay in an 18th-century noble residence in one of Florence’s most evocative neighborhoods, a cradle of art and spirituality. Renewed in every detail, Palazzo Firenze welcomes you into intimate, elegant spaces where precious stuccoes, frescoes and original features meet high-end design, contemporary lines and refined comfort.

## A New Chapter in More Than Three Centuries of History

The eighteenth-century allure of Palazzo Firenze reveals itself today in a fresh light - brighter, more contemporary, and profoundly Florentine. Rooms and suites have been thoughtfully reimagined to offer an even more comfortable and refined experience, where carefully preserved historical details blend seamlessly with a curated selection of furnishings and materials that honor the property's storied heritage and authentic spirit.

Reserve your stay at the newly unveiled Palazzo Firenze, with enchanting views over the Basilica or the winding streets of Santa Croce, in suites featuring original frescoed or coffered ceilings. And discover the city's most spacious private residence.
