# Guelfi and Ghibellini Restaurant

Savor innovative Tuscan & Mediterranean cuisine that combines ancient recipes with modern and innovative cooking techniques. Marvel at Chef Antonino Pepe’s creative sense, which takes the dining experience to a new level. Each dish is carefully prepared and presented in an enticing way, creating an unforgettable visual and gourmet experience.

## Our Philosophy

Our restaurant draws inspiration from the concepts of the Guelfs and the Ghibellines, two medieval factions that symbolize the rivalry and balance between regional Italian culinary traditions. Today, Guelfi & Ghibellini strives to offer authentic and refined dishes, using high-quality seasonal ingredients sourced from local suppliers.

Available for lunch and dinner.
