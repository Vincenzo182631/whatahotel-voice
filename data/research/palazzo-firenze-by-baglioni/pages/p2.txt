## Palazzo Firenze

Built in the mid 1700s, this lavishly furnished residence features frescoed ceilings and authentic period artwork. The Santa Croce district is the perfect base for exploring the history & artistic heritage of the Renaissance.

## Leonardo’s Hidden Gem

A tour of the hotel uncovers a “secret” treasure from the Renaissance era. The “Trestle of Santa Croce” is an architectural marvel that was once studied by Leonardo DaVinci.

## Santa Croce Musical Experiences

## A 2026 of Unmissable Events in Florence

Florence in 2026 offers a rich calendar of events, from fashion to gastronomy, from music to the city’s great historic traditions. In the heart of the Santa Croce district, Palazzo Firenze is the ideal starting point to experience these world-unique occasions.

## Fragranze

(September 11-13, 2026)

A unique international event in Europe, dedicated to the world of essences and beauty. Fragranze celebrates the dialogue between creativity, craftsmanship, and scent innovation, attracting professionals and enthusiasts from around the world.

From Palazzo Firenze to Fortezza da Basso: approx. 2 km
