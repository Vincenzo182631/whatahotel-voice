## Your Exclusive Address in Milan

### Brought to life by award-winning design company, Paolo Moschino Ltd in collaboration with Olga Polizzi, Director of Design at Rocco Forte Hotels,  Rocco Forte House offers guests unrivalled privacy, comfort and style set against the rich tapestry of Milan’s colourful history.

### Honouring the building’s origins, design details such as mouldings, frescoes and marble floors have been interlaced with modern luxury to create the ultimate in refined living.

### Each charming apartment offers spacious living quarters including a sitting room, dining room, fully equipped kitchen, and either one or two bedrooms. For larger groups and families, two apartments on the third floor can interconnect to form a three-bedroom space.

### Downstairs replicates the atmosphere of a classic Milanese home with a traditional courtyard, a tranquil seating area filled with plants, available for all guests to enjoy. For a change of pace, the sub-level gym is open 24/7.

### Your dedicated House Concierge is always on hand to make your stay even more memorable. From housekeeping and grocery shopping, to arranging tours and making reservations, every detail is taken care of. Additionally, a personal butler, private chef, or even event planning service for a special occasion, are also available on request for a truly tailored experience.

## A Captivating Destination

### The undisputed capital of fashion and contemporary design, Milan sets its own pace. But the city’s grand style doesn’t stop there – its rich architectural history is interwoven with the hypermodern, a multilayered metropolis that is constantly evolving.

### Milan is a city filled with masterpieces. Da Vinci’s The Last Supper, the Gothic Duomo cathedral, and Teatro alla Scala, one of the world’s greatest opera houses, all call it home. Discover a city brimming with fascinating places to explore, and stories to tell.

FIND OUT MORE

### Central to this enigmatic city is the world-renowned Quadrilatero della Moda, or Quad d’Oro, the epicentre for luxury shopping in Milan. Named for the stylish streets that border it – via Montenapoleone, Corso Venezia, Via Manzoni, Via della Spiga – the Quadrilatero is lined with designer fashion boutiques, art galleries, and elegant cafes.

### Perfectly located at the intersection of Via Manzoni and Via della Spiga, in this historic quarter, is Rocco Forte House. Moments from some of the city’s most notable sights, including Teatro alla Scala and Palazzo Orsini, it's the ideal location from which to explore Milan’s myriad cultural hotspots.
