## An Elevated Urban Sanctuary

Enjoy an elevated experience in the Executive Lounge, where guests have exclusive access to daily food presentations, soothing surroundings and dedicated services to ensure a seamless stay. The Executive Lounge’s location on the hotel’s 27th floor also offers a breathtaking view of the city and a full menu of inspiration to feed an inquisitive mind.

### La Vallée SPA Switzerland

Inspired by Uzbekistan’s Silk Road heritage and the ancient art of navigating by the stars, La Vallée SPA Switzerland harnesses the power of three interconnected pillars to guide your journey toward total harmony and restoration. Nature is expressed through earthy textures and refined treatments, while the Cosmos pillar transcends traditional wellness with celestial inspiration. By focusing on energetic flow and spatial openness, the experience suspends the constraints of Time, evoking a sense of quiet awe that guides guests into a transformative state of clarity, balance, and inner calm.

Pool Bar

## ORO Pool Bar

Located beside the spectacular infinity pool, Oro Pool Bar invites guests to unwind in a open-air setting designed for relaxation and connection. Surrounded by serene water views and a vibrant atmosphere, the pool bar offers a curated selection of refreshing cocktails, chilled beverages, and light culinary bites. As the day transitions into golden sunsets over the city, the setting becomes even more captivating, creating the perfect backdrop for memorable moments. Whether enjoying a sunny afternoon by the pool, a leisurely gathering with friends, or a relaxed evening under the Tashkent sky, Oro Pool Bar offers an elevated escape for leisure, connection, and mindful moments in the heart of the city.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Indoor Pool (Indoor)Infinity Pool (Outdoor)

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent have an on-site spa?

Yes, LA VALLÉE SPA SWITZERLAND is located on-site at JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent?

Khast-Imam Complex, Chorsu Bazaar, Tashkent Metro, Independence Square (Mustakillik Square), Kukeldash Madrasah and more are nearby JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent.
