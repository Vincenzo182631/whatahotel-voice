Restaurants & Lounges

### ELYO Restaurant & Terrace

Influenced by Aegean, Anatolia, and Levantine cuisines, ELYO celebrates the essence of fresh Mediterranean dining, where every meal becomes a reflection of warmth, generosity, and renewal. It is also the destination to join fellow travelers for ELYO's complementary tea ritual that embodies the time-honored hospitality and respect at the heart of Uzbek culture.

### JW Market

Whether you are seeking a quiet moment or a quick bite throughout the day, JW Market offers elevated convenience in a warm and welcoming atmosphere. Guests can also unwind on the outdoor terrace — a cool, breezy courtyard-like retreat designed for relaxed moments away from the rhythm of the city.

### ORO Pool Bar

Located beside our seasonal outdoor pool, Oro offers a serene space to unwind and embrace mindful moments. Enjoy curated drinks and light bites with local and international flavors while taking in golden sunsets over the city. Whether for a quiet afternoon or a relaxed evening, Oro is an inviting sanctuary designed for pause, restoration, and elevated leisure.
