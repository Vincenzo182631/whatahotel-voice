# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent

## Wellness-Focused Hotel with Spa & Indoor Pool

Just as ancient Silk Road nomads once sought the legendary hospitality of Tashkent, the JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent stands today as a modern sanctuary for the global traveler. Every moment here is woven with the same spirit of connection, crafting a tapestry of soothing luxury and mindful moments. Perfectly positioned next to Tashkent City Park and City Mall, this five-star haven balances Uzbekistan’s...See More rich heritage with contemporary craftsmanship. After experiencing the warm welcome of a signature tea ritual, guests can retreat to one of 284 guestrooms or 42 expansive suites—all featuring sweeping city views. From the restorative tranquility of the Spa by La Vallée to the locally inspired flavors of three dining experiences, every detail is designed to nourish the soul. With over 1,693 sqm of sophisticated event space, including an 818-sqm Grand Ballroom, the hotel serves as a vibrant crossroads where history’s legacy meets today’s most memorable celebrations.See Less

### Stay in the Moment Tea Ceremony

Take time to slow down and let your thoughts steep as deeply as the tea leaves in your cup as you enjoy a complimentary tea experience at ELYO Restaurant & Terrace. Rooted in ancient Silk Road traditions, this ritual embodies the warmth, hospitality, and respect at the heart of Uzbek culture, preserved and cherished to this day. After three pours to warm and cleanse the cup, the fourth is the first...See More true sip. Fragrant, warm, and refreshing, each small serving honours the threads of connection and care that have woven cultures and people together for centuries. Servers pour their heart into the welcoming ritual. Yours will be infinitely touched.See Less

## Weave Well-Being Into Your Stay at la Vallée Spa

Rooted in Swiss heritage and defined by the energy of the cosmos, La Vallée Spa is inspired by the order, movement, and harmony of the universe — a celestial refuge where precision performance, elegance, and mindfulness converge. Immerse yourself in this urban sanctuary of calm where every detail is woven with silk threads of relaxation and rejuvenation to restore inner balance and awaken the senses. From the serene indoor pool to the traditional hammam, every ritual at La Vallée Spa blends locally inspired ingredients with mindful care, guiding you toward complete balance and renewal. Before you avail yourself to a signature spa service, begin your wellness journey in the Cosmos Lounge with a Pomegranate & Thyme Essence welcome drink, where the symbolic fruit of Uzbekistan representing vitality, abundance, and inner beauty sets the tone for a truly transformative journey.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 10.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent. Self parking is available for $4.17 per hour. Valet parking is available for $6.20 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent is Tashkent International Airport (TAS). TAS is located approximately 10.6 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
