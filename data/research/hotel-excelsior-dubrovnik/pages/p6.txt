#### Elafiti Restaurant

Panoramic floor-to-ceiling views over the enchanting Croatian islands await you at Elafiti. Start your day feasting on an abundance of breakfast dishes while soaking up the glorious views. Return after dusk to a transformed dining space to take your fill of the deliciously tantalising Mediterranean buffet.

Working hours

06:00 - 10:30 - breakfast

18.30- 22:00 - dinner

Cooling refreshments

#### Lanterna Glorijet Bar

Dive into the cool water and swim right up to Lanterna Glorijet Bar to order your favourite drink. Our al fresco poolside bar rests right on the Adriatic Sea and is a popular place for keeping cool in the heat of the day. After sunset, chill-out music starts playing and it’s the perfect time to order our signature cocktail, Summer in the Palace.

Working hours

10:00 – 22:00

\*working hours are changeable depending on the season

More information

Sunset cocktails

#### Sunset Lounge

Sunset Lounge is the place at Dubrovnik Palace Hotel to watch this daily natural phenomenon for which Dubrovnik is famed. Just before dusk, head through the hotel reception, find a chair and settle back to admire the spectacle. As day transforms to dusk and then to night, and the sky glows burnt-orange, sip a glass of local wine and relax as a pianist plays unobtrusively in the background.

Working hours

08:00 - 24:00
