#### Nevera

Set inside an atmospheric cave, you’ll be served some of the best fusion of local and Mediterranean delicacies in Dubrovnik expertly matched with Croatia’s most exclusive wines. Take the elevator to the beach, and find the intimate, whitewashed space of Nevera Restaurant, our most informal and romantic restaurant.

Working hours

11:00 – 17:00 (May–September)

\*Working hours are changeable depending on the weather conditions and season.

#### Spice Lounge

The open-air seafront Spice lounge area has had a complete redesign with a new layout, furnishings, artwork and lighting. The pulse of the hotel and the gathering area for morning breakfast, afternoon coffee and sunset cocktails, the space is punctuated by a chic colour palette of sophisticated beige and soft grey fabrics, complimented by marine-inspired accents, including pale walnut and warm wood tones.
