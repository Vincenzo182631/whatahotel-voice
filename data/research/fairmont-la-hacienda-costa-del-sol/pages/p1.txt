## Getting Here

### NEARBY AIRPORTS

The resort is conveniently accessible from three regional and international airports:

**Gibraltar International Airport (GIB): ~25-minute drive.**

- S class Mercedese for 2 people and luggage: €75
- V class Mercedese for 6 people and luggage: €100
- Mercedese Benz Sprinter and luggage: €225
- Mini Bus for 10 people and luggage: €320
- Mini Bus for 15 people and luggage: €345
- Mini Bus for 20 people and luggage: €370

**Jerez Airport (XRY): ~1 hour 15-minute drive.**

- S class Mercedese for 2 people and luggage: €255
- V class Mercedese for 6 people and luggage: €320

**Málaga-Costa del Sol Airport (AGP): ~1 hour 20-minute drive.**

- S class Mercedese for 2 people and luggage: €205
- V class Mercedese for 6 people and luggage: €220
- Mercedese Benz Sprinter and luggage: €330
- Mini Bus for 10 people and luggage: €465
- Mini Bus for 15 people and luggage: €475
- Mini Bus for 20 people and luggage: €500

**TEL:** +34 682 49 79 67

**Email:** _concierge.lahacienda@fairmont.com_

Transfers need to be booked 48 hours prior to arrival.

We inform you that if you wish to modify the schedule for your transfer, both on the day of your arrival and on the day of your departure, you may make changes up to 48 hours before the service.

However, we cannot guarantee the schedule change with less notice than the one indicated above.

### METRO & RAIL STATIONS

Directions from Malaga Central Train Station- Take the motorway AP7 A7 towards Algeciras. After 60 minutes driving, take the exit 1098 on A7 and drive for 6 minutes throughout Avenida del Golf. Follow the signage to reach the Resort.

If you need a transfer.

**Maria Zambrano Train Station Málaga ~1 hour 30-minute drive.**

- S Class Mercedese for 2 people and luggage: €220
