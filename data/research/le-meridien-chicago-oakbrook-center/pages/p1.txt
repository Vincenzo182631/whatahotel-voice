# Welcome to Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center

## Where Artful Stays Meet Inspired Surroundings

Discover an elevated Chicago experience at Le Méridien Chicago Oakbrook Center, where quiet sophistication blends with contemporary style. Ideally located beside the upscale Oakbrook Center, this hotel...See More in Oak Brook, IL provides effortless access to cultural favorites such as Drury Lane Theatre, the Elmhurst Art Museum and downtown Chicago. Retreat to spacious guestrooms featuring curated modern décor, plush bedding and sleek technology. Enjoy Spanish-inspired flavors and skyline views at El Tapeo Spanish Kitchen or relax with cocktails and local fare at Longitude 87. As an exceptional Oakbrook shopping center hotel, the property offers Spa Suites, a 24-hour fitness center and thoughtful amenities. Travelers with four-legged companions will appreciate that it is also a pet-friendly hotel in Oak Brook, IL. For events and gatherings, versatile venues including a dramatic Grand Salon with floor-to-ceiling windows create memorable settings. ## El Tapeo Restaurant

El Tapeo celebrates the bold flavours of a modern Spanish kitchen with a simple, ingredient-driven menu. Our team’s mission is dedicated to sparking connections between family, friends and the community through spirited food and wine.

## Discover Longitude 87

Longitude 87 embraces a casual ambiance as the Hub transfers from day to night and displays a European twist while using local midwest ingredients, featuring gourmet menu items ideal for the palate savvy guest.

Longitude 87 offers breakfast items to go. Illy coffee and other specialty drinks are available, transitioning to craft beers, premium wines, and cocktails in the evening.

## Art Programmes

We have partnered with the Elmhurst Art Museum and Lizzadro Museum. Guests can gain access to these premier contemporary art museums free of charge by presenting their artist designed key card - a card that opens the door to more than just the guest room.

Learn More

## Oak Brook Dog Friendly Hotel

We ensure that you and your pup will revel in the convenience of our special doggie amenities. Your pampered pooch will enjoy a dog bed, food and water bowls and a special floor mat, while owners will receive clean-up supplies and their own welcome kit. Please note, there is a per day pet fee when bringing your furry canine friends. A maximum of two dogs is permitted per room, each weighing no more than 40 pounds.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center?

On-site parking is available at Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for $20.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center is Chicago O'Hare International Airport (ORD). ORD is located approximately 16.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
