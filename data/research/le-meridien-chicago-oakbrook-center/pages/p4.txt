Spa Suites

Treat yourself to a day of pure relaxation at Le Méridien Chicago – Oakbrook Center. Spa Suites by Le Méridien is an unforgettable experience, offering an array of treatments in a tranquil private suite.

Scheduling an Appointment

We encourage our guests to schedule their appointments five to seven days in advance to ensure your preferred treatment or therapist is available. Preferences for male or female therapists should be requested at the time of booking. All reservations must be secured with a major credit and telephone number. To schedule your appointment, please call our front desk at 630.368.9900.

Policies

For spa services, one must be 18 years of age or older. If under 18 years of age, one must be accompanied in the Spa Suite by a parent or legal guardian. Reservations must be canceled 24 hours in advance of your check-in time. In the event of a no show or a cancellation in breach of this policy, the cost of the entire reservation may be charged.

Hotel Activities Highlights

### Unlock Art

At this exclusive art-based Chicagoland hotel, one way of seeing things through a new perspective is by offering guests unexpected and original interactive experiences that will enhance their stay. To achieve this, Le Méridien has developed Unlock Art, an innovative program that offers guests complimentary access to local contemporary cultural institutions.

## Elmhurst Art Museum

Le Méridien - Oakbrook Center has partnered with the Elmhurst Art Museum and guests can gain access to this premier contemporary art museum free of charge by presenting their artist designed key card.

Learn More

## Lizzadro Museum of Lapidary Art

Lizzadro Museum of Lapidary Art - Founded in 1962 by lapidary hobbyist and collector Joseph Lizzadro the Museum houses a world-class collection of Chinese jade and hard-stone carvings from around the world. Guests of Le Méridien Chicago – Oakbrook Center can gain access to this museum free of charge by presenting their artist designed keycard.

## Oakbrook Shopping Center

Enjoy outstanding shopping at the second-largest shopping center in the Chicagoland area. Oakbrook Shopping Center is ideally located across the street from our hotel; indulge in retail therapy and relax afterwards in our upscale spa and guestrooms.

More Local Area & Activity Highlights

Brookfield Zoo

Brookfield Zoo is one of the premiere zoos in the Chicagoland area. With over 450 species of animals in the vicinity and covering over 200 acres with many exhibits, it offers no shortage of exciting and fun things to do.

Morton Arboretum

The Morton Arboretum is a large collection of gardens, hiking trails, woodlands and prairies. There are over 4,100 different species of trees and 186,000 types of plants in the four-acre area.

Downtown Chicago

Experience all of the best things to do and see in the Windy City, including Buckingham Fountain, Navy Pier, Millennium Park, Willis Tower and the many museums.

Drury Lane Theater

The elegant 952-seat Drury Lane Theater features a proscenium stage and continues to dominate the Chicagoland theatre scene with a commitment to musicals, comedies, compelling theatre for young adults and concerts by legendary entertainers.

Oak Brook Polo Club

The Oak Brook Polo Club is the home to 13 polo grounds, 400 horses and a horse showground. Spectators can even fly right to the grounds.

Oak Brook Park District

The Oak Brook Park District represents five parks. Totaling 150 acres, and four facilities: Family Recreation Center (the Family Aquatic Center and Premiere Fitness Club), the Racquet Club, the Dorothy and Sam Dean Nature Sanctuary and Central Park West.

Oak Brook Golf Club

Oak Brook Golf Club is a 27 hole public golf facility. From fundraisers, to leagues, to instruction, to an afternoon out on the links by yourself, this course has everything you need.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa Suites by Le Meridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center is located on-site at Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center?

First Folio Theater, Tivoli Theatre Downers Grove, Go Ape Treetop Adventure Course, William F. Sherman, Jr. Interpretive Center, Willowbrook Wildlife Center and more are nearby Le Méridien Chicago - Oakbrook Center.
