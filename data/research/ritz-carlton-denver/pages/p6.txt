Experience our Denver Hotel Club Lounge
Experience our downtown Denver hotel and Club Lounge
The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level elevates the luxury hotel experience, offering an exclusive retreat where comfort meets the impeccable service and attention to detail that define The Ritz-Carlton. Often described as a “hotel within a hotel,” the Club Level enhances your stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a serene private Club Lounge, and thoughtfully curated in-room amenities. These exclusive privileges are designed to immerse you in the very essence of your destination. At our downtown Denver hotel, the Club Lounge redefines indulgence with five daily culinary presentations and an open bar available throughout the day, ensuring that every moment of your stay is as seamless as it is memorable. Discover the pinnacle of luxury with a personalized experience that is truly unmatched.
Club Lounge Temporary Closure
Notice: The Ritz-Carlton, Denver Club Lounge will be closed from November 1st, 2026, to mid-January 2027. We apologize for any inconvenience and look forward to serving our valued Club Level guests again soon!
