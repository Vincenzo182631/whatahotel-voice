A Modern Steakhouse
Dining
ELWAY'S Downtown offers exceptional fine dining in downtown Denver, CO, in a relaxed, inviting atmosphere. Savor the finest USDA prime steaks, expertly butchered in-house, alongside customizable private dining experiences and Colorado-inspired cocktails at Denver’s premier steakhouse. While steak is our specialty, our menu also features some of the freshest and most exquisite seafood in the city. Whether you’re joining us for breakfast, lunch, happy hour or dinner, ELWAY'S Downtown provides diverse options that cater to every palate.

Frequently Asked Questions
What restaurants does The Ritz-Carlton, Denver have on property?
The on-property restaurants at The Ritz-Carlton, Denver are:
ELWAY'S Downtown

First Light at Elway's

Does The Ritz-Carlton, Denver offer room service?
Yes, room service is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Denver.
What cuisine is offered at The Ritz-Carlton, Denver?

American at First Light at Elway's, In-Room Dining
