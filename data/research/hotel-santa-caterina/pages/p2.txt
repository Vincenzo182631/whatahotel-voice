A CORNER OF PARADISE

## LA DOLCE VITA

Between a cocktail at the Beach Bar and a Mediterranean style lunch at Al Mare Restaurant it is easy to spend the day in a state of total relaxation.

OPENED FROM MID-APRIL TO OCTOBER

With easy access to the sea and a salt water swimming pool, the beach club service includes sun beds, umbrellas, beach towels, changing and shower areas and everything you would expect for the perfect poolside day. The ultimate definition of Amalfi style Dolce Vita.

The deck is not fully accessible (including the pool), however access is made possible with the assistance of our staff.
