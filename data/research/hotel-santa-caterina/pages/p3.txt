## HOTEL

Iconic Villa Overlooking The Sea

Hotel Santa Caterina has been, and still is, a private home by the sea used by generations of guests. Over the last century, from the Grand Tour through to modern day travel, the hotel has been considered the much acclaimed ‘home of hospitality’ on the Amalfi Coast. A place where the world has fallen in love with its Dolce Vita.

## AFAMILYSTORY

Three generations of Gambardella have continued the works of expansion and refurbishment of the entire property, which now offers a selection of 66 rooms and suites plus 12 additional rooms in our new building, Casa Marchesa.

Today, Crescenzo’s daughters, Giusi and Ninni Gambardella, assisted by their children Crescenzo Gargano and Alessandro and Beatrice Camera, directly manage the day-to- day operations of the hotel and take a personal interest in the comfort and enjoyment of the resort guests.
