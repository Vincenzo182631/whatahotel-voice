## POSITION

At the gates of Amalfi

A short distance from the historic centre of Amalfi, Hotel Santa Caterina dominates a stretch of coast where the sea merges with the sky on the horizon, and is built into the cliffside surrounded by Mediterranean gardens and a series of terraces leading down to beach level.

## How to  reach us

By Car

**For those traveling by car from Rome or Naples:** follow indications for the A3 motorway in the direction of Salerno. Once on the A3, take the Vietri sul Mare exit, following directions to Amalfi. From Amalfi proceed in the direction of Positano and some 700 meters from the center of Amalfi you’ll come to the Hotel Santa Caterina on your left. Approximate duration of journey: 1h 30 m from Naples, 3h 30m from Rome.

**For those coming from the South and East:** from the A3 motorway take the exit for the center of Salerno. Turn to the left following indications for Vietri sul Mare and Amalfi and after circa 3kms you’ll find yourself on the famous Amalfi Coast. Once in Amalfi, follow indications for Positano and, some 700 meters from the center of Amalfi you’ll find Hotel Santa Caterina to your left.

**For those coming from the North:** from Rome or Naples take the A3 motorway towards Salerno and exit at Vietri sul Mare. Follow indications for Amalfi: once in Amalfi continue for a further 700meters from the center in the direction of Positano and you’ll find Hotel Santa Caterina to your left. Approximate driving time from Rome: 3hrs 30 mins, from Naples: 1hr 30mins.

By Train

The nearest railway station is that of Salerno (25 km – approx. 55 minutes). Naples is the second nearest (70 km – approx. 75 minutes). Consult official train times at Trenitalia.

**Salerno Station** – Piazza Vittorio Veneto – Tel. +39.089.252200

**Naples Station** – Piazza G. Garibaldi -Tel. +39.081.5543188

By Plane

Naples Capodichino International Airport is the closest to Amalfi, approximately a 90 minute journey from Hotel Santa Caterina, and served by flights to and from Rome, Milan, Venice, London, Paris and Frankfurt.

By Sea

Guests can book a private boat transfer directly to the hotel’s jetty from Naples and other towns along the Amalfi Coast. During the summer months, public ferries run from Sorrento, Capri, and Salerno to Amalfi.

The hotel offers a free shuttle service to and from the port and center of Amalfi every day of the week.
