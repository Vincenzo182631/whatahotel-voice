# Welcome to The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino

## Relax and recharge at our luxury family Costa Navarino hotel

Located along the Ionian Sea in Costa Navarino, the leading sustainable destination in the Mediterranean, The Westin Resort Costa Navarino is an award-winning resort where families reconnect, wellness comes naturally, and unforgettable experiences unfold. Surrounded by centuries-old olive groves, rolling golf courses and a spectacular sandy beach, the resort combines Westin's signature hospitality with spacious, redesigned rooms and suites, many with private infinity pools. Young guests can enjoy endless adventures at the Aqua Park, innovative children's facilities, and international sports programmes, including NBA Basketball School, Juventus Academy Resort Experience and Mouratoglou Junior Tennis Camp. Adults can embrace transformative wellbeing at Anazoe Spa, where Hippocratic philosophy meets modern science through personalized longevity experiences, experience world-class golf and tennis, and savor exceptional dining, from authentic Greek cuisine to Mediterranean and international favorites

A Paradise for Swimming

### Pools

Messinia’s year-round sun makes it a paradise for swimming, as reflected in our swimming facilities. From the Westin Pool, the Lagoon Pool, the unique Aqua Park with three waterslides, and the indoor pool at WestinWORKOUT, we offer a wide range of choices for all options.

Kindly note that the outdoor pools operate seasonal and the use of beach balls, pool floats and any other inflatable objects is only allowed at the designated areas of the pools.

Westin Pool

With a distinctive design and a relaxing backdrop of the waterfalls sound, the two-levelled Westin Pool covers a total surface of 851m2 across two separate pools with a maximum depth of 1.2m. The pool area offers sunbeds and umbrellas and the Westin Pool Bar serves inspired alcoholic and virgin cocktails, delicious kids’ dishes, light snacks and full of vitamins smoothies and juices to keep you well nourished.

Lagoon Pool

At the heart of the resort, you will find the elegant Lagoon Pool surrounded by palm trees. It consists of one pool with a total surface of 890m2 and depth of 1.2m, and a kids’ pool of 200 m2 and pool depth of 0.4m. After a restorative swim, you can enjoy a selection of revitalizing beverages, fresh salads, light dishes and delightful desserts served at the pool bar. The pool is heated in April-May and October.

Indoor Pool at WestinWORKOUT

For those who prefer to swim indoors or just want to keep up with their routine, our fully equipped WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio offers an indoor pool, making it an ideal choice for a calming dip, no matter the season

An Open-Air Destination for Dining, Shopping & Culture

### Navarino Agora

Located at Navarino Bay, near The Romanos and The Westin Resort Costa Navarino, Navarino Agora is an open-air destination uniting gastronomy, shopping, culture, and lifestyle in a vibrant coastal setting. Inspired by the traditional Greek agora, it serves as a lively meeting point for guests and locals day and night. Dining ranges from traditional Greek flavors to international cuisine and street food, alongside specialty cafés. Curated boutiques feature Greek craftsmanship, local creators, and international brands. Seasonal events, cultural experiences, wellness activities, family moments, and open-air cinema complete this contemporary expression of the Costa Navarino spirit.
