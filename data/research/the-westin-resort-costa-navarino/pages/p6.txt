# Young Guests

## Unique Children’s Activities

The Westin Resort Costa Navarino is one of the most acclaimed family friendly resorts in Greece, offering an array of day care options for infants, leisure and entertainment options for toddlers and children...See More of all ages as well as fun activities for the entire family. While parents are otherwise engaged, young guests can enjoy the SandCastle, one specially designed youth hub offering exciting recreational/educational programs, splash around in the Aqua Park, make use of the resort’s outstanding facilities for kids or take part in a variety of children activities, many of which are unique in the Mediterranean.See Less

## Cocoon Care Center

At the Cocoon crèche, infants and toddlers from 4 months to 3 years old are creatively engaged and encouraged to develop their skills under the supervision of qualified baby nurses and trained staff. For infants 8-18 months, the daily program ranges from sensory exploration to two-way communication, imaginary play, listening, singing, moving and playing with toys. For toddlers aged 18 months to 3 years, the daily program is designed to support the development of their skills and includes storytelling, role playing, singing, dancing and play-based activities. Kindly note that the Kids club operates seasonally. Please, contact kidsclub@costanavarino.com for any additional information.

## SandCastle

Families are given the opportunity to participate in a series of recreational activities and programs especially designed for children. The SandCastle is aimed at children aged 4-7 and 8-12, with day-long activities specifically tailored for each age group. With the support of experienced associates and staff, the children are engaged in activities such as adventures with Messinian pirates, recreating life in ancient Messinia, discovering the area’s rich natural heritage, following in the steps of Heracles, creating and painting ancient costumes. They also learn about the environment, discover Messinia’s natural wonders and rare species while staying at one of the finest luxury family resorts in Greece. Kindly note that the Kids club operates seasonally. Please, contact kidsclub@costanavarino.com for any additional information.

Book Now

### The Aqua Park

Being repeatedly acclaimed as a top family-friendly resort in Greece, The Westin Resort Costa Navarino offers nearly anything a family could dream of for the holidays - all in one place! The family attraction at Navarino Dunes is the Aqua Park with six waterslides for exciting rides. For security reasons the minimum height to access the water slides is 1.20 m. and only 1 person per slide is allowed. Kindly note that the Aqua Park operates seasonally, and that the use of beach balls, pool floats and any other inflatable objects is only allowed at the designated areas of the pools.

## NBA Basketball School

The NBA Basketball School at Costa Navarino, the first-of-its-kind NBA collaboration, combines a world-class coaching program with premium hospitality and FIBA compliant basketball facilities at Navarino Dunes. NBA Basketball School elite coaches will introduce players to the NBA approach during a comprehensive curriculum that covers on-court training, skill development and basketball education, while highlighting the values of teamwork, integrity and respect. The NBA Basketball School operates seasonally.

More Kids Activities

### Young Gardener

This fun outdoor activity encourages children to discover nature at first hand so that they can better understand the connection between farm and fork. It is aimed at kids aged 4-11. They become little gardeners as they learn to plant seeds and gather seasonal fruits and Costa Navarino's chef will prepare traditional recipes on the spot. At the end, each child will be given local seeds to take and plant at home

Book Now

### Golf for Kids

Kids’ golf classes include not only on-course instruction but also the latest technology such as video analysis, so that children learn quickly, gain confidence and develop techniques to improve their game, after which they can cool off at the Aqua Park.

### Indoor Climbing Walls

Release your inner Spiderman on a 100sqm artificial climbing wall with routes of varying difficulty, which make it both beginner-friendly and a challenge for the more advanced.

Book Now

### Natura Hall

Take part in a fun learning experience through discovery and creativity. At Navarino Natura Hall, kids will learn all about the area's unique environment and habitats in a way that is both entertaining and educational.

### Tennis Clinics

Learn to play tennis while bonding with friends or family in a motivating, inspiring and highly enjoyable environment under the expert guidance of our coaches. A great option for families, friends and small groups. Specially designed sessions for children aged 9 to 11 years old, 12 to 14 years old and for above 15 years old.

## Where Taste Meets Play​

The Six is your all-day escape, where poolside living, dining, and play come together effortlessly. Move between the pool, the lounge, and moments of fun, from games and bowling to water slides filled with energy and laughter. Catch your favorite match from the comfort of the lounge, drink in hand, while children dive into their own world of play and exploration. Here, everything flows naturally. No planning, no pressure. Just step in and let the day unfold.​
