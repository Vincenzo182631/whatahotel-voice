## Accommodations

Our 445 newly refurbished Costa Navarino rooms and luxury suites, including 131 with private infinity pools, are designed to connect you with the beauty of the resort through private terraces or balconies...See More and access to an extensive common pool area. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino have a work space?

No, the rooms at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino?

The largest occupancy room at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino is the Methoni with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino?

Some rooms at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino have fridges?

No, rooms at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Resort, Costa Navarino have hair dryers.
