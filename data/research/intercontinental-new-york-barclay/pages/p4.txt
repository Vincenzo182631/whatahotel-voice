### Fitness Center

Our Midtown Manhattan hotel's 24-hour Fitness Center provides top-of-the-line equipment to support your exercise goals. Included is a Peloton bike, cardio equipment, treadmills, ellipticals, free weights, and yoga mats.

### Club InterContinental

Club InterContinental combines personal service and exclusive privileges to make your stay extra special. Inquire at Front Desk to unlock your elevated club experience or email barclayres@ihg.com to add club access to an existing or new reservation.

### Our Sustainable Hotel Practices

The InterContinental New York Barclay’s responsible business plan follows the guidance of our parent company, InterContinental Hotels Group and focuses on the Triple Bottom Line of Economic Prosperity, Environmental Protection and Social Responsibility. We aim for our sustainability strategy – which aligns with the United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development – to become the benchmark for sustainable hotels.
