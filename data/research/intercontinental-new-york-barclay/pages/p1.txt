### Disappointed with this stay

14 hours ago

I have stayed here before and I must say the stay this time was disappointing. The hotel is starting to look rundown. The suite we got needed proper maintenance. The wood floors were very scratched, the floor heater in the bathroom didn’t work, the room thermostat would reset at night and the room would get too hot. I was never credited the $30 a day towards food and beverage. The restaurant had tables but we had to wait for breakfast. Servers were nice but never asked if we wanted an omelette or something outside the buffet which is not what it used to be. There are training opportunities everywhere but I get the feeling that management no longer cares about this property.

### Disappointing Experience Due to Fraudulent Charge

21 days ago

I recently stayed at the InterContinental New York Barclay for one night and, while there were a few positive moments, the overall experience left me extremely disappointed.

To start with the positives, despite a very long check-in line, the hotel quickly added staff to assist guests, and the front desk associate was able to switch my room from a king bed to two queen beds without issue. Those were the highlights of my stay.

Unfortunately, things quickly went downhill from there. After returning to the hotel from a concert, I requested a toothbrush from the concierge. My room number was taken down, but after waiting more than 30 minutes, nothing arrived and I had to call back to request it again.

The real problem came after checkout.

The morning of my departure, my friend and I left the hotel early and went downtown for breakfast before heading home. Later that day, while reviewing my folio, I noticed an unexpected charge of approximately $130 for the hotel's breakfast buffet. Since I had not eaten breakfast at the hotel, I knew immediately that something was wrong.

When I called the hotel for assistance, I was repeatedly transferred to a voicemail box that was full and unable to accept messages. Multiple employees transferred me to the same inaccessible line, and I was unable to reach anyone who could help. It became so frustrating that I ultimately had to call from a different phone number before I was finally able to speak to someone.

After an investigation, the hotel confirmed that four people had eaten at the breakfast buffet and split their bill between two rooms, one of which was mine. Somehow, the charge was assigned using both my room number and last name, despite the fact that I was not even on the property at the time.

While the charge was eventually removed, that does not address my biggest concern: how was another guest able to charge a meal to my room in the first place? The lack of verification and apparent failure of basic billing controls is alarming. As a guest, I should not have to worry about unauthorized charges appearing on my account.

What was even more disappointing was the lack of management involvement. I specifically requested a call from a manager regarding the situation and never received one. For a hotel that markets itself as a luxury property, I expected much better customer service, communication, and accountability.

Mistakes happen. What matters is how they are handled. In this case, a fraudulent charge was allowed to occur, resolving it required significant effort on my part, and management never bothered to follow up.

I would strongly encourage future guests to carefully review their bill before and after checkout. Unfortunately, based on this experience, I will not be returning.
