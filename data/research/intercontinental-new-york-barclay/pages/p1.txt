## FAQs for InterContinental New York Barclay

Does InterContinental New York Barclay have a pool?

No, InterContinental New York Barclay does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental New York Barclay?

At InterContinental New York Barclay, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 90 USD .

Valet parking is $90.00 plus tax for 24 hours or $130.00 plus tax for 36 hours with no in/out privileges. Oversized vehicles incur a $20 surcharge. No charging stations are onsite, but guests are encouraged to ask for nearby options.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental New York Barclay?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental New York Barclay.

Pet policy description. One pet per room. Pet must be 50 lbs. or less. Dogs should be nonaggressive and always leashed in public areas, and not left alone in the room. If the dog is barking, the guest will have to depart the hotel. 250 USD Pet Fee per stay.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental New York Barclay for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental New York Barclay have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental New York Barclay has 1 restaurant.

When is breakfast at InterContinental New York Barclay?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental New York Barclay?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental New York Barclay.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental New York Barclay?

Check-in at InterContinental New York Barclay is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is InterContinental New York Barclay from the nearest airport?

InterContinental New York Barclay is conveniently located near La Guardia Airport (LGA), approximately 13 miles away (20.92 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental New York Barclay?

InterContinental New York Barclay is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:
