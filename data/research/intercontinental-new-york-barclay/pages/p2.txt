Stylish décor. Gorgeous marble bathrooms. Eclectic artwork. Expansive city views. The city might never sleep, but you will. Our spacious Midtown Manhattan rooms and suites have a distinctly residential feel, creating a welcoming atmosphere for you to unplug, relax and rejuvenate. Enjoy all-day, in-room dining, premium linens, plush bedding and elevated amenities in every room.
