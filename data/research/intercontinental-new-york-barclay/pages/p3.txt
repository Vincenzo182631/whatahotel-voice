## 1920's Manhattan GlamourThe Parlour

Located just steps away From Park Avenue's hustle and bustle, you'll find New York's perfect intimate hideaway, The Parlour. The Parlour restaurant pays tribute to our Midtown Manhattan hotel's 1920's origin. Our food and beverage offerings are crafted in a way to take you on a journey of the hotel's history, city and culture, artistically paired in style with local, sustainable and seasonal ingredients.

### The Parlour Through The Decades

As The Barclay journeys toward its 100th anniversary on November 4, 2026, guests are invited to celebrate with The Parlour Through the Decades. This year-long culinary series honors a different decade each month through a rotating menu of iconic dishes and handcrafted cocktails inspired by the flavors, culture, and spirit of the era. From timeless classics to forgotten favorites, each menu offers a nostalgic dining experience that brings The Barclay's rich history to life, inviting guests to savor a century of hospitality one decade at a time.

### Sustainable

The Barclay champions sustainable dining in New York City through locally sourced, organic ingredients and on‑site rooftop gardens and beehives. Fresh herbs and house‑harvested honey are incorporated into dishes and handcrafted cocktails at The Parlour, creating a distinctive farm‑to‑table experience in Midtown Manhattan. All suppliers are located within 350 miles of NYC, reinforcing The Barclay’s commitment to responsible luxury and sustainable food and beverage practices.

### Breast Cancer Awareness Month

The Parlour is raising money for the Breast Cancer Research Foundation with our featured cocktail. Created in honor of Breast Cancer Awareness Month, this elegant cocktail pairs Grey Goose vodka with vibrant raspberry liqueur, fresh lime, and a touch of honey syrup. Finished with sparkling wine and a delicate raspberry garnish, each sip is bright, refined and beautifully balanced, raising a glass to strength, resilience, and hope. A portion of each breast cancer beverage purchase will be donated to the Breast Cancer Research Foundation.
