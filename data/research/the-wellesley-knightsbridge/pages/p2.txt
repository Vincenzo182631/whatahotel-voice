## The Wellesley Penthouse

The sixth and seventh floors of the hotel are given over to the beautiful Wellesley Penthouse that can be enjoyed as a one, two and four bedroom option. With stunning views over Hyde Park this is one of the most luxurious suites in London. The Churchill Suite, part of The Wellesley Penthouse, features its own winding staircase leading onto a private terrace offering unique rooftop views over Belgravia and further South-West London, providing London’s finest traditional views. Churchill novels populate the suite’s reading area, allowing you to step back in time to discover a different style of London. The spacious lounge is perfect for board meetings or private dinners for up to 8 guests, includes state-of-the-art technology, a private Humidor and Decanter Bar. To book The Wellesley Penthouse, please contact roomreservations@thewellesley.co.uk.
