## 1920's Feel and Decorative Features

The walls of the lavish Crystal Bar are adorned with glass unique display cases containing The Wellesley’s impressive selections of whisky, cognac and armagnac collections, including a number of extremely rare bottles from 1770. Our passionate bar team can create a cocktail of your choice or you may choose from the vast selection of classic cocktails on the menu. A food menu is also available. Enjoy a crisp glass of Champagne or take advantage of our large selection of Cognacs and whiskies that take pride of place in a specially designed feature wall.

Our Reservation Policy

To avoid disappointment, it is always advisable to make a booking in advance. Reservations can be made directly with us by phone on +44 020 3668 6530 or email to restaurant@thewellesley.co.uk.

Furthermore, guests can reserve through third party websites such as Opentable or Bookatable. Please note that for Christmas, New Year's Eve and Valentine's Day, all reservations via third party will not be valid without an email from the hotel confirming the booking.

Our team will confirm your reservation by email. We kindly advise you arrive at your reserved time as we will only be able to hold your table for 30 minutes past the booking time. Should unforeseen circumstances affect this, please contact us beforehand.

The Wellesley Knightsbridge is a boutique hotel with a limited number of seats available in our outlets. Credit/ debit card details will be required at the time of the booking. Guests who wish to cancel or rearrange a reservation are required to give 24 hours' notice prior to arrival. For cancellations within less than 24 hours' notice, a penalty fee applies.

Please inform us about any special dietary requirements or request for special occasions at the time of the booking.

## The Cigar Humidor

Housing Europe's Largest Collection of Cigars. Our bespoke Humidor holds one of the most exclusive and extensive selections of cigar outside Cuba. For the complete experience, our team of Cigar Experts can advise in selection tailored to personal tastes, cutting & lighting. For the cigar aficionado - a unique and intimate atmosphere that delivers on being one of the most exceptional places to enjoy a fine cigar in London. Our humidor specialist Giuseppe Ruo is always on hand with his fully trained team to ensure that the selection process is as pleasurable as the smoking.

### Cigar Lounge

Take the time to savour the exceptional. Our private cigar terrace houses our bespoke humidor and a connoisseur’s selection of only the finest cigars. Enjoy a Montecristo, a Cohiba, or a limited edition Bolivar in the most convivial surroundings.

### Terrace

The heated terrace and inviting leather seats offer the ultimate experience for cigar aficionados and novices alike so that you can take time to savour the exceptional whatever the weather.

### Cigar and Cognac Experience

Further your understanding of the art with a masterclass with our cigar experts. It includes a 2-3 hour session to indulge in two cigars with a complete commentary on the art of the cigar. Starting from £300 per person. For enquiries, email our team on restaurant@thewellesley.co.uk
