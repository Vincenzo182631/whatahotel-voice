### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima have a work space?

No, the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Hotel Lima?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 3 at JW Marriott Hotel Lima. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima have fridges?

No, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Lima have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at JW Marriott Hotel Lima have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
