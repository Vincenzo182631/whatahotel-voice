The JW Marriott is a longtime go-to for an upscale stay in the heart of Lima's coastal districts. Right across from the seaside Larcomar shopping center, this location is as central as it gets—and much of the hotel delivers sweeping ocean views, plus walking distances to bucket list restaurants and sites. The vibe is traditional luxury with a high-gloss exterior, and rooms that are crisp, clean, and modern. All the bases are covered on the amenities front: There's a serene spa, a restaurant, bar, a massive gym, business spaces, and a club lounge for JW Marriott Platinum members. For many first-time visitors to Peru, it's a plush place to land.
