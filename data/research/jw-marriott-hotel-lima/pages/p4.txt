## Experiences

Discover Lima, Peru’s sophisticated coastal capital, where rich history, vibrant culture, and contemporary elegance converge. Founded in 1535 and centered around the historic Plaza de Armas, the city showcases a remarkable blend of Inca heritage and Spanish colonial grandeur, reflected in iconic landmarks such as the Government Palace and the majestic Basilica Cathedral of Lima.

From the charming...See More streets of its colorful neighborhoods to the scenic parks overlooking the Pacific Ocean, Lima offers endless opportunities for exploration. Experience the enchanting Magic Water Circuit at Parque de la Reserva, where illuminated fountains create a spectacular display, and immerse yourself in the energy, gastronomy, and cultural richness that have made Lima one of South America's most captivating destinations.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Hotel Lima have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Hotel Lima offers a fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Strength Equipment, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Lima have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Lima has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Lima have an on-site spa?

Yes, Health Club - SPA is located on-site at JW Marriott Hotel Lima.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Hotel Lima?

Caballos de Paso Peruano, Centro Preincaico Puruchuco, Chinatown, San Francisco Convent, Magical Water Fountain Park in Lima and more are nearby JW Marriott Hotel Lima.
