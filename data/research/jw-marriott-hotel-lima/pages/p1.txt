# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Lima

## Experience the luxury of our hotel in Lima

A luxury escape awaits you at our waterfront JW Marriott Hotel Lima. Situated within steps of the beach and Costa Verde, our beachfront hotel in Lima, Peru is also convenient to Lima attractions like the Cathedral of Lima and Larcomar entertainment center. Our luxury hotel in Miraflores, Lima offers elegant rooms with plush beds and crisp linens. Spacious rooms and suites provide ocean views at our...See More Lima luxury hotel. With over 15,000 square feet of event space, our Lima beachfront hotel can host everything from large weddings to corporate conferences. Enjoy delicious Asian and Peruvian cuisine at our restaurants and stop by the lounge for a drink. Our Health Club is furnished with a full range of equipment, plus ocean views, while our seasonal outdoor pool provides refreshments for our guests on hot days. The staff of JW Marriott Hotel Lima look forward to welcoming you to Peru to help you create a memorable visit.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Lima?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Lima is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Lima allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Lima.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Lima?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Lima. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Lima have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Lima has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Lima booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Lima?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Lima is Jorge Chavez International Airport (LIM). LIM is located approximately 21.2 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Lima have an airport shuttle to Jorge Chavez International Airport (LIM)?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Lima has an airport shuttle to Jorge Chavez International Airport (LIM).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Lima have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Lima does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Lima have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Lima has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
