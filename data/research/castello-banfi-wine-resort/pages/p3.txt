## Public Tours

A journey through the winery, tastings, and many experiences, designed to help you discover places and traditions and fully immerse yourself in the world of Castello Banfi.

DISCOVER MORE

arrow-yuarrow-up

The tour takes place in groups with other visitors and lasts about an hour and is followed by a wine pairing lunch at La Taverna restaurant or a wine tasting at L’Enoteca.

For both options, the meeting with the guide is scheduled 15 minutes before the start time of the tour at the Enoteca.

You will need your own car to follow the guide from the Castle to the Winery, which is 4 km away.

Advance reservation is mandatory.

Public tour with lunch € 125/140 per person

Public tour with tasting € 60 per person
