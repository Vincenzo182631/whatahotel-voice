# Acclaim

## Award-Winning Service & Hospitality

Each of our staff members takes pride in providing a superior level of anticipatory service that defines the Triple Creek Ranch experience. Guests routinely marvel at the thoughtful ways they have been “Triple Creek-ed” and a passing remark manifests itself in an unspoken wish granted. A Dining Room server overhears a guest’s comment that her mother would just love the chocolate truffle petit-four served at the end of that night’s meal. Upon departure she is presented with a gift box of truffles hand made by our Executive Pastry Chef to bring home for her mother. A guest on a horseback ride spies a colorful feather alongside the trail in a location where it isn’t safe to dismount. He mentions the feather while recounting his adventure to an Experience Planner, who then slips away to hunt for the feather and presents it to our delighted guest later that day. Another guest is chatting with a Dining Room server about the dessert selection on the evening’s menu and confesses that she’s not really big on sweets. She jokes that she would honestly be just as happy with a meatball for dessert, which we of course deliver much to her surprise and joy! We take a genuine pleasure in providing this level of detailed attention.

Responses to our simple guest surveys are often filled with the names of Staff members who enhanced and enlivened a vacation here. We are pleased to hear the sentiment often repeated that “We make a point to never go to the same place twice… but we are coming back to Triple Creek Ranch!”
