# Horseback Riding

## On Ranch Trail Rides

## Saddle Up!

What better way to explore the beauty of the western countryside than on horseback?

Over wildflower meadows in spring… under blue mountain skies during the summer… through the richly scented needles of a Ponderosa pine forest in fall… or across a trail of freshly fallen snow, horseback riding at Triple Creek Ranch is a year-round activity.

Along the way you may even have an opportunity to witness the everyday lives of our favorite wilderness locals, including white tail deer, mule deer, elk, wild turkeys, an occasional bald eagle, and many more!

Rides that originate on the ranch are included in your stay, and experienced wranglers accompany all horseback trips. Trail ride group sizes are kept small, to allow for optimal personal attention and are tailored to participants’ level of abilities and interest. Because of the mountainous terrain of our area our horses are guided at a leisurely walk, and for the safety of our guests and horses we cannot accept riders over 250 lbs. Riders are asked to wear long pants and bring a long sleeve shirt or jacket. Tennis shoes are acceptable in warmer months. In winter, lined boots are recommended.

Horseback rides run for approximately 75 minutes and are offered throughout the day, year round. We also run daily lunch rides including pack lunches provided by our Triple Creek Ranch kitchen, with an approximate timing of 2.5 hours. There are a limited number of guests per ride, and lunch rides often fill up quickly, so we do recommend early scheduling with our Pre-Arrival Concierge if this is a priority activity.
