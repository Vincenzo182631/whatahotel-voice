# Accommodations

## Find your ideal mountain escape in one of our 25 private cabins

## Welcome to your Montana home

Are you seeking a secluded cabin nestled in the pines, or on the banks of the Bitterroot River? Or, perhaps you prefer to be close to the inviting bustle of the Lodge and just steps away from the outdoor pool. The mountain hideaway of your dreams is waiting for you at Triple Creek Ranch, where seven different cabin styles offer a variety of luxury cabin accommodations. Nightly Cabin rates are specific to each cabin style, inclusive of meals, beverages and most activities, and are designed for two people. These rates can be discovered on each cabin style page. Extended stays of 5 nights or more also receive a 10% discount.
