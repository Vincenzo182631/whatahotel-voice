## Kickin’ up dust, & makin’ memories!

Have you always dreamed of being a bona fide Cowboy/Cowgirl? Now’s your chance!

Back by Popular Demand!!!… We invite you to join us in an unforgettable all-inclusive 5-Day western event! Learn natural horsemanship, cowboy basics, games on horseback, and tricks of the trade passed down by generations of cowboys, while you explore the bonded connection that develops between horse & rider.

With a variety of terrain, challenges, and techniques to learn… whether you are a novice, or an experienced horseman this Event has something to take away for all!

Cowboy School is an extremely unique, complimentary event included with your stay! While participation each day is not mandatory, this event is designed to build on skills from the previous day’s courses, so attendance is encouraged to ensure you stay up to speed, build a strong bond with your horse, and have the opportunity to enjoy the full experience.

This Event will fill up FAST!, with a maximum number of participants for each event to ensure a fulfilling experience for all. With that in mind, we do recommend that you reserve your space right away when you book your stay.

While it is not required to stay with us throughout the entirety of the event, guests who do will receive 10% off their stay of 5 nights or more!

### SAMPLE ITINERARY

#### **Wednesday**

**Arrival Day – Welcome Reception**

Join Head Wrangler for meet and greet cocktails in the Rooftop Lounge.

#### **Thursday**

**Horsemanship 101 – Cowboy Basics**

Learn anatomy, markings, coloring as well as how to catch, groom and saddle your horse.

Afternoon roping lesson and trail ride.

#### **Friday**

**Workshop Sessions at Piquett Creek Ranch**

Observe colt work and the art of “joining up”.

Use what you’ve learned to build a special connection with your horse.

Cowboy style pack lunch corral side.

Obstacle course ride – learn to give your horse clear direction and ride independently.

Learn to feel where your horse is placing their feet and where your balance is in relation.

#### **Saturday**

**CB Ranch Game Day**

Team penning with cattle and games on horseback at the CB corrals.

BBQ lunch

#### **Sunday**

**Off Ranch Ride Day**

Cap off Cowboy school with an adventure into the hillsides.

Relax into your newly honed skills and take in the breathtaking vistas of the Bitterroot Valley.

Pack lunch on the trail

Group cocktails in the Rooftop Lounge

#### **Monday**

**Departure Day**
