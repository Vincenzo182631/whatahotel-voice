# Getting Here

- From Narita
- From Haneda

## From Narita

to 4-1-1 Toranomon, Minato City, Tokyo, Japan 105-0001

_Private car_

Transportation with a sophisticated touch, a private car to/from Tokyo Narita International Airport (NRT) can be arranged by the hotel concierge prior to your arrival/departure.

_Driving_

Head northeast towards Shinkuko Jidoshado. Join onto Shutoko Wangansen and keep left. Go onto Shutokosuko Toshin Kanjosen and then take the Shutoko Shibakoen exit on the left. Turn right and head towards National Route 1. Take a right turn at Akabane Minami Intersection to head onto National Route 1. Turn left at Kamiyacho Intersection and the hotel is on your left.

## From Haneda

to 4-1-1 Toranomon, Minato City, Tokyo, Japan 105-0001

_Private car_

Transportation with a sophisticated touch, a private car to/from Tokyo International Airport - Haneda (HND) can be arranged by the hotel concierge prior to your arrival/departure.

_Driving_

Head towards Shutoko Wangansen and at the Shutoko Shibakoen exit, take the left ramp. Turn right and head towards National Route 1, turn right at Akabane Minami Intersection onto National Route 1. Turn left at Kamiyacho Intersection and the hotel is on your left.
