# Services & Amenities

The Tokyo EDITION Toranomon creates a unique, one-of-a-kind experience together with the best and most personalized modern service available.

### For further information

Please contact us at info.tokyo@editionhotels.com

- Check-in 3:00 pm
- Check-out 12:00 pm
- Smoke free property
- Pets not allowed
- On-site parking hourly 1,000 JPY, daily 4,000 JPY
- Valet parking daily 7,000 JPY

GUEST SERVICES

- 206 lavishly appointed guest rooms including 22 suites, all with unobstructed skyline views
- 15 guest rooms including 3 suites with furnished terrace averaging 26 sqm (280 sqft) overlooking stunning Tokyo landmark views
- One-of-a-kind 1,550sqft (144sqm) Penthouse with expansive 180-degree views of Tokyo.
- Signature restaurant by Chef Partner Tom Aikens with private dining rooms accommodates up to 178 guests and features a new culinary concept in Tokyo with 74-seater outdoor terrace restaurant and bar for specialty dining in warmer months, the only skyscraper outdoor dining venue of this scale in Tokyo
- A convivial restaurant seating 82 guests, inspired by all-time classics serving breakfast, lunch and dinner overlooking commanding view of Tokyo Tower
- Sophisticated lobby bar – 120 seats plus standing
- EDITION signature cocktail bar seating 87 guests
- 2,982sqft (277sqm) of private event space with 1,184sqft (110sqm) of pre-function area.
- Customized menus for private events, luncheons, dinner parties and business meetings
- State-of-the-art audio, visual and production services available for private events up to 250 guests
- 45ft (14m) heated indoor long-lap swimming pool and 8ft (2.5m) diameter Jacuzzi with natural daylight
- Full-service spa with 6 treatment rooms; including 1 couple treatment suite with sauna and steam room and an outdoor relaxation deck
- 24-hour state-of-the-art gym powered by Technogym
- Concierge services
- 24-hour room service
- Multi-lingual staff
- Custom-built EDITION bikes by tokyobike for complimentary hire to explore the city

SUSTAINABILITY

SUSTAINABLE INITIATIVES AND PRACTICES:

1. Food Waste Reduction:
   - Implementing strategies to minimize food waste.
2. Food Waste Segregation:
   - Following Japan’s stringent food recycling laws to ensure food waste is properly segregated and recycled.
3. Reduction of Single-Use Plastics and paper usage:
   - Eliminating or reducing single-use plastics, such as plastic straws, chopsticks, and other disposable items in food and beverage outlets.
   - Using recyclable aluminum bottles for our complimentary water.
   - Using paper takeaway food containers, promoting compostable alternatives.
   - Minimizing single-use items throughout our food and beverage.
   - Reducing the use of paper products, through digital communications, minimized printing, and adopting eco-friendly paper alternatives and mobile check-in.
4. Vegan and Vegetarian Options:
   - Offering plant-based menu choices at all dining outlets to cater to guests seeking sustainable and ethical dining options.

ENERGY EFFICIENCY AND RESOURCE CONSERVATION:

1. Power and Utility Control:
   - Fully automated systems that control lighting and utilities.
   - Using energy-efficient LED lighting throughout guest rooms and public spaces.
   - Using solar panels on the rooftop to generate renewable energy, reducing reliance on conventional power sources.
2. Water Reduction Initiatives:
   - Implementing technologies and practices to reduce water consumption, such as the use of water-efficient appliances including showers and water taps and dual control plumbing fixtures, e.g., dual flush toilets and self-closing taps.
   - Using drip irrigation technology on hotel terraces and plants to efficiently water greenery with minimal waste.
   - Providing guests with the option to skip daily towel and linen changes to conserve water and reduce laundry-related energy use.
