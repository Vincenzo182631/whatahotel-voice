# Rooms

Designed by Kengo Kuma with the vision of Ian Schrager, all 206 guest rooms at The Tokyo EDITION, Toranomon offer breathtaking glimpse of Tokyo skyline including views of Tokyo Tower and Tokyo Bay, as do the first-of-its-kind accommodation with private terraces starting from level 31.

# Suites

Elegant suites with stylishly modern decor and posh detailing at the skyscraper hotel feature separate living and sleeping areas; some of which offering expansive outdoor terraces with landmark views and options for adjoining rooms with outer connecting door transforming them into luxurious 2-bedroom units.
