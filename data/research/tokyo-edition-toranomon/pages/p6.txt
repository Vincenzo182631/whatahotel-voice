# Lobby Bar

## Lobby Bar

RESERVE VIEW WEBSITE

The Lobby Bar is a new gathering place within the hotel for guests and locals to gather and enjoy bespoke cocktails, a unique selection of spirits and a unique afternoon tea service in an open yet intimate environment overlooking Tokyo Bay and the Tokyo Tower. The Lobby Bar can seat up to 75 guests with 23 seats at the bar, 26 seats on communal/working tables, while also offering 26 seats on couches.

For general inquiries and large group reservations, please contact our team by emailing diningreservations.tokyo@editionhotels.com.
