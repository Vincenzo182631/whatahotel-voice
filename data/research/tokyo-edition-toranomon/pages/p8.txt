# SPA

**SPA**

Share

The SPA reflects a sophisticated approach to beauty, wellness and well-being with an inspired blend of luxury and comfort, offering an array of healing treatments and beauty services using only products organically sourced, and proudly made in Japan, in a soothing, tranquil environment. Through our approach that encapsulates both tradition and minimalism, guests leave with noticeable results, awakening to their true selves.

OPEN DAILY10:00 AM - 10:00 PM

For reservations please contact us at Spa.Tokyo@editionhotels.com

Reserve

**Gym**

Share

The 24-hour Gym is powered by Technogym with 17 state-of-the-art multifunction equipment that fulfill the being of fitness and wellness.

Guidelines

The gym is open to guests over 16 years old and above.

OPEN DAILY24/7

**Pool**

Share

The private pool area is designed like a conservatory with high windows to bathe the area in natural light.

Guidelines

\- Reservations are required in advance.

\- The pool is open to children 4 years old and above; however, children under 15 years old must be accompanied by a parent or guardian at all times for safety reasons.

\- Only arm floats and inflatable swim bands are allowed in the pool, please refrain from bringing in other items such as floaties and balls.

OPEN DAILY7:00 AM - 10:00 PM
