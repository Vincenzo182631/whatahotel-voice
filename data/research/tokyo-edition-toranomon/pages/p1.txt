# DEBUTING A NEW GENERATION  OF LUXURY IN TOKYO

The Tokyo EDITION, Toranomon embraces both the authentic history of the ancient city along with the modern skyscrapers that have recently revitalized the city’s skyline. Conveniently located near the subway stations of Kamiyacho, Roppongi-Itchome and Toranomon, the hotel is steps away from Tokyo’s best neighborhoods and attractions. The Tokyo EDITION, Toranomon features 206 guest rooms including 22 suites with iconic views of the Tokyo skyline. The hotel’s design includes 3 restaurants, featuring a specialty restaurant with an outdoor terrace overlooking Tokyo Tower, an all-day dining venue and lobby lounge, 3 lounge bars, custom designed spa with 6 treatment rooms, state-of-the-art gym and 387 sqm of event space with 4 meeting studios.

View Rooms & Suites

## Home Away From Home

Designed by Kengo Kuma with the vision of Ian Schrager, all 206 guest rooms at The Tokyo EDITION, Toranomon offer breathtaking glimpse of Tokyo skyline including views of Tokyo Tower and Tokyo Bay, as do the first-of-its-kind accommodation with private terraces starting from level 31.

## A NEW GATHERING PLACE IN THE PULSE TOKYO

An Ian Schrager-conceived concept showcasing the best of dining and entertainment, the hotel will be an enchanting oasis debuting a true luxury lifestyle that is not yet seen in Japan. Graced by the backdrop of the same magnificent views of the sleek sky lobby are the inviting lobby lounge and evocative terrace of the specialty restaurant on level 31. Together with a first of its kind specialty restaurant in the city, the contrastingly striking all day dining and the sophisticated EDITION signature cocktail bar, Punch Room, a new gathering place will make its mark in Tokyo.

## SPA

The SPA reflects a sophisticated approach to beauty, wellness and well-being with an inspired blend of luxury and comfort, offering an array of healing treatments and beauty services using only products organically sourced, and proudly made in Japan, in a soothing, tranquil environment. Through our approach that encapsulates both tradition and minimalism, guests leave with noticeable results, awakening to their true selves.
