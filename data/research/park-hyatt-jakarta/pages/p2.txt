Experience the finest in luxury hotel stays at Park Hyatt Jakarta, featuring 220 oversized rooms, including 36 suites, within our smoke-free property. Our sophisticated, spacious, and contemporary guest rooms are designed with Indonesian touches and materials, all offering uninterrupted views of Jakarta from the 25th floor onwards. Unwind in the deep-soaking marble bath with custom Le Labo® products or enjoy the large flat-screen TVs and integrated media hub. Enhance your stay by upgrading to one of our elegant suites, many of which feature additional spaces such as dining and sitting areas.
