# Restaurants & Bars

Park Hyatt Jakarta offers a new level of luxury dining with its innovative bars and restaurants that provide stunning city views of the Central Jakarta and its iconic landmarks, Indonesia's National Monument (Monas). Explore unique live kitchen concepts, private dining rooms with views, carefully crafted dishes, and premium drinks made from the freshest seasonal ingredients. On weekends, enjoy live DJ sets and savor signature martinis and champagne from our Rooftop bar.

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.
