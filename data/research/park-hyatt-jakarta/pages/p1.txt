# Modern Luxury Hotels in Central Jakarta \| Park Hyatt Jakarta

## Modern, Luxury Hotel in Central Jakarta

Park Hyatt Jakarta is a sophisticated modern hotel, situated in Central Jakarta’s prestigious MNC Tower complex, just minutes from the Presidential Palace, Indonesia's National Monument (Monas) and Jakarta City Hall. Each of our 220 guest rooms offer a mix of comfort and style, supported by first-class service and luxurious amenities. Discover tranquility at The SPA, or explore a world of culinary wonders at one of several bars and restaurants, all with stunning city views. The hotel provides an elegant backdrop for celebrations and corporate events, with facilities to suit every occasion. Welcome to Park Hyatt Jakarta.

## Discover Park Hyatt Jakarta

Live Entertainment

One of the highest rooftop bars in the city, KITA Bar is a stylish sanctuary from the hustle and bustle of Jakarta. With stunning views of Central Jakarta from level 36 and a direct view of Jakarta’s landmark, this is an ideal venue for those who want to revel and unwind in a comfortable environment with live DJ performances.

Open 5 p.m.–1 a.m.

Prepare For a More Sustainable Stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring to ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

The Royal Glasshouse

Conveniently located in the center of Jakarta, the Royal Glasshouse at Park Hyatt Jakarta is an events venue that exudes sophistication. With panoramic Central Jakarta city views and beautiful gardens, this stunning space features a modern glasshouse design, allowing for abundant natural light and a serene atmosphere. From elegant outdoor celebrations to indoor corporate events and luxurious weddings, the Royal Glasshouse blends contemporary style with timeless charm.

Pool

After exploring Jakarta or having a busy day of meetings, relax at the outdoor pool at Park Hyatt Jakarta. Take a refreshing swim in the tranquil waters. The pool is on the 35th floor, next to the fitness center, and offers a sun deck and city views. Feel free to visit before or after your workout.

Open daily, 6 a.m.–8 p.m.

The Spa

Bringing Indonesian healing traditions to the 21st century, The Spa offers advanced aesthetic treatments and traditional body therapies to enhance your wellbeing. Experience the breathtaking view from the 35th floor while rejuvenating and revitalizing yourself.

Open daily from 9 a.m.–10 p.m., with last appointments booked at 9 p.m.

Fitness Center

Located on the 35th floor with sweeping views of Central Jakarta, the fitness center at Park Hyatt Jakarta offers an unparalleled, 24-hour workout experience. Customize your fitness routine with Technogym cardio equipment; stability, BOSE and medicine balls; and free weights. Experience our wellness program with full-body workout stations available in a separate studio.

Business Services

Elevate your casual business meeting at the Library on Level 23. Our lavish and cozy working space on the hotel lobby level caters to all your business requirements. Discover a comprehensive range of amenities in the Library, including high-speed Wi-Fi and printing services.

Open daily, 10 a.m.–10 p.m.

## Things To Do

Soak up the sights and sounds of this ancient city and modern capital, whether you visit nearby Merdeka Square and the National Monument, aka Monas, or Istiqlal Mosque or Jakarta Cathedral. Jakarta’s Old Town is about a 25-minute drive from the hotel, with many shopping destinations in the area. Head to Tiger Island, part of the picturesque Thousand Islands, and slip into the clear blue water to see vibrant underwater reefs and marine life.

Came back "home" to 3005 at Kebon Sirih. The Park Suite Deluxe Monas View there's just not a better premier city suite like this in town. As a Hyatt loyalist I enjoyed an early check in, went straight up to the pool for a few laps, after that a short workout in the gym, then took the elevator down for lunch at Li Lian, afterwards a short stroll to Sarinah and Menteng area, came back to the 22nd floor for the amazing Iftar Buffet at the Dining Room, then back to the Suite to cut my birthday cake from the Conservatory, and finally to close off the day I spent too much money on cocktails at Kita Bar enjoying the light show coming off the Monas. Just another amazing stay at my fave Hyatt hotel. My wife and I stayed at 3005 and I booked the 2 twin Beds Deluxe for my mom and brother who had an incredible first time at the PHJ. Another thing truly worthy of mentioning are the Lunch Tray Sets At Kita Restaurant....just WOW! Hotel Staff was so friendly and helpful we actually made some new friends along the way. I came back again a week later and from the ground floor to the 37th floor everyone greeted me by name which was amazing. At check in I left my passport in my suitcase but at the desk they said "No need sir, welcome back here's the key card to your home." Thanks for the Love and Care PHJ-Team, I'll be "Home" soon!
