### Not great

Unfortunately, our stay at the Thompson Savannah was a little disappointing. From the moment we arrived, the person who checked us in wasn’t particularly warm or welcoming, which didn’t make for the best first impression.

The biggest issue for us, however, was the comfort of the room. The sheets were extremely rough and uncomfortable, and all of the pillows provided were very flat. I had an extremely difficult time sleeping throughout the night because I just could not get comfortable between the bedding and the pillows. For a hotel of this caliber, I definitely expected a much more comfortable bed and better quality linens.

One positive was that they were very welcoming of our dogs, which we really appreciated and made traveling with them much easier.

Overall, the hotel itself is nice, but unfortunately the lack of hospitality at check-in and such an uncomfortable night’s sleep really affected our experience. I expected more from the Thompson brand.

### Nice hotel and great location

The hotel is very nice and a great location. We drove to lunch and parked but walked to the same area for dinner. Our family walked along the river to/from dinner on the riverfront and was really nice. Easy .5 mile walk along a wide, nicely lit path to/from the hotel. It's an easier walk than it looks on a map. The hotel also has bikes for more extensive excursions.

There is a nice open green space between the hotel and riverwalk and it has the cutest SAV sign perfect for photos.

Had drinks at Bar Julian the first evening before heading out to dinner. The view is wonderful and the drinks were delicious.

We did go to the bakery on site at 9am and they were sold out of everything except for 5 pasteries. We did purchase 2 of the 5 left and they were delicious. Went to bar julian on property to get a real breakfast and that took FOREVER as they were very short staffed. So if you are planning on getting anything for breakfast at the hotel, definitely go WELL before 9am. Other than that, the hotel was very nice.
