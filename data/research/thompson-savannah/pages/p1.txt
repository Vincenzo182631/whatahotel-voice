## REVEL IN LUXURY AT OUR SAVANNAH RIVERFRONT HOTEL

At Thompson Savannah, unwind in upscale sophistication along the Savannah River’s edge where the Historic District meets the Eastern Wharf. Experience the city’s best from our hotel near River Street, your urban oasis where you can relax, revitalize and rejuvenate. Experience ultimate comfort in chic, spacious suites or hotel rooms, or unwind on the pool deck in a private cabana. Visit Fleeting, our hotel’s signature restaurant celebrating the season’s best ingredients or Bar Julian, our elevated rooftop bar. Discover inspiring indoor and outdoor event and wedding venues. Minutes from the city’s treasured historic sites, our luxury Savannah hotel is your springboard to explore it all.

## Rooms & Suites

Wake up to gorgeous views from the comforts of your midcentury modern guest room or hotel suite near River Street. Each is outfitted with thoughtful amenities, including a rainfall shower and floor-to-ceiling windows. City-inspired botanical patterns, curated artwork and herringbone wood floors add an artistic touch during your luxury, boutique riverfront hotel stay in downtown Savannah.

A Taste Of Thompson Dinner Parties

**One Night Only, a Chef Curated Dinner Party at Thompson Savannah**

Following the epicurean philosophy of Thompson Savannah's signature restaurant fleeting, the Taste of Thompson Dinner Party features the season's best bounty from the local coastal landscape and waterways. Celebrating hyper-local ingredients with your choice of a four-course meal or cocktail party with paired bites, every detail will be tailored to your preferences.

No two parties are alike. Roll in the custom beverage cart with selections to include wine pairings, martini variations, champagne + champagne cocktails or a whiskey theme featuring our Thompson Savannah Select Single Barrel Rye.

Cocktail Parties can be hosted in the Thompson Suite, complete with Pool Table and can accommodate 8 to 10 guests. Dinner Parties can be hosted in the Sunroom or in the Bar Julian Lounge to accommodate 10 to 12 people.

Options to enhance your evening:

- A live DJ or Vinyl DJ with a curated live set

- Syd Nichole Designer Florals and Tablescapes
- Local artist, Adrienne Berkland Live-painting and more!

Pricing varies based on party size and custom additions. Minimum of 8 guests to reserve.

Pool Deck & Private Cabanas

Retreat to our downtown Savannah hotel pool deck for a day of basking under the sun, enjoying a cocktail poolside, and people watching. Recline in the splash pool’s in-water loungers that strike the perfect balance between refreshing and relaxation, or reserve one of our private cabanas that provide shade and exclusivity with daybeds overlooking the pool.

Fitness & Wellness

Keep up your workout routine in our modern fitness center, with a diverse mix of cardio machines, Peloton® Bikes, free weights, yoga balls, and strength training equipment. If you prefer to take it outdoors, courtesy hotel bikes are available to explore Savannah on two wheels.

Explore Savannah

This celebrated Southern city has much to offer and Thompson Savannah puts you conveniently close to many of the city’s best attractions, fine dining, and day trips. Stroll along the Riverwalk, delve into Savannah’s storied history and culture, discover emerging artists, or take to nature for a change of pace. The options are boundless when you stay at Thompson.

Hotel Happenings

From local live music and seasonal pop-ups to craft-cocktail specials and one night only culinary experiences with our favorite farm partners, our curated programming is the place to see and be seen. Don’t miss out on celebrating the best Savannah has to offer from our urban retreat.

### Pets are Welcome

We warmly welcome dogs of all breeds and sizes, with no additional charge. We are unable to accommodate cats. To ensure every guest enjoys a comfortable stay, a deep-cleaning fee may be applied at the hotel’s discretion should any accidents or damages occur in the guest room. While on property, dogs must be leashed in all common areas. For the comfort of all guests, food and beverage outlets are reserved for registered service animals only. We are honored to welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not considered pets and are not subject to this policy. Please note, our pet policy only applies to hotel guest rooms and common areas. Pets are not allowed in Fleeting, Bar Julian, and Stevedore Bakery. For further details or special requests, our team will be delighted to assist you.
