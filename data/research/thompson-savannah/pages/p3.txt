# Restaurants & Bars

Savannah is a world-class culinary destination. Classic Southern flavors — often with a modern spin — mingle with fresh seafood plucked right from the Georgia coast. You don’t have to go far to sample some of the city’s most enticing cuisine. Thompson Savannah offers a little of everything from outdoor dining at our rooftop bar & restaurant to a cozy date night at Fleeting. Our hotel is the perfect destination for any of your upscale dining needs.

## Fleeting

Set the scene at Fleeting, where subdued lighting, large windows, and an open kitchen create a moody ambiance. Think elevated coastal Southern cuisine with an ever-evolving menu that celebrates hyperlocal ingredients and blends global flavors with regional classics. Enjoy shareable dishes like pork belly, green tomatoes, and wood-grilled oysters with chartreuse-lemongrass butter, perfectly paired with handcrafted cocktails or zero-proof selections. Whether planning a special date night or simply indulging in exceptional cuisine, the hotel's signature restaurant offers an unforgettable experience.

## Bar Julian

21+ after 10pm

Above it all, with sweeping views of the Savannah River, Bar Julian delivers a creative Mediterranean-inspired menu. Enjoy elevated breakfast options, bar snacks, and locally-inspired pizzas. Beloved by locals and visitors alike, Bar Julian is the tallest rooftop bar in Savannah's modern history, offering outdoor dining and hand-crafted cocktails with panoramic views—making it a must-visit destination for unforgettable moments.

## Stevedore Bakery

Where artisanal grains and ingredients are intended to shine, Stevedore Bakery showcases local Southern farmers and growers in a mouth-wateringly delicious menu. The menu celebrates the region's flavors through local partners like PERC Coffee to create an iconic neighborhood staple. Located on the ground floor, just around the corner from the lobby entrance, step into your new favorite morning stop along the Savannah riverfront.
