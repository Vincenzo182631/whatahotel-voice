# Food & Wine

Roncolo is your introduction into Italy’s Food Valley – the culinary culture that defines the region. Our farm-to-table philosophy begins in the vineyards, olive orchards and gardens that surround you. Step into our historic _Acetaia di Canossa_ and discover the quiet maturation of traditional balsamic vinegar.

Every experience unfolds where it is made: in the vineyards, in the ageing rooms, in the kitchen. This is Emilia, experienced at its source.

#### Venturini Baldini wine tasting

In the cool, historic wine cellar of the seventeenth-century Dimora Ancini, our expert sommeliers guide guests through a curated tasting of Venturini Baldini’s award-winning wines. From the distinctive expressions of Lambrusco to refined sparkling and still selections, each wine is presented with context, technique, and insight into the estate’s organic philosophy. Tastings are paired with select local specialities.

_Private tastings available upon request._

INQUIRE

#### Balsamic experience

Established among the oldest acetaias in the region – and today one of the largest producers in the area – our Acetaia di Canossa carries forward a tradition defined by time, patience, and precision. Join us for a guided tasting tour in the historic acetaia, where wooden barrels quietly age and refine the _Aceto Balsamico Tradizionale_ over decades. The experience concludes with a tasting paired with aged _Parmigiano Reggiano_, allowing the depth, balance, and complexity of each drop to fully reveal itself.

_Private tastings available upon request._

INQUIRE

#### Fresh pasta cooking class

Our estate chef will let you in to the culinary secrets of the Food Valley in a fresh pasta cooking class. Learn how to create the famous _Cappelletti_ and _Tagliatelle_ of Emilia and transform the best of our local produce into a delicious Italian meal. Enjoy your own creations in a family-style private lunch.

_Maximum group size of 8 persons._

INQUIRE

#### Gourmet picnic

A basket prepared with care, a blanket laid among the vines, and time set aside for nothing but pleasure. Our gourmet picnic brings together estate wines and a selection of regional delicacies, thoughtfully arranged and ready to be enjoyed in one of Roncolo’s most scenic corners.

_Subject to weather conditions_

INQUIRE

#### Local farmer visits

Discover the typical specialities of the Food Valley in a guided tour to local farmers. From dairies of _Parmigiano Reggiano_ to producers of _Prosciutto di Parma_, these visits are a unique opportunity to meet the makers and see the origins of authentic Emilian farm produce.

# Motors

Emilia is slow food and fast cars. The Motor Valley is a paradise for racing and motor enthusiasts, home to the most iconic automotive brands, such as Ferrari, Lamborghini, Maserati, Ducati and many more.

Race into the supreme world of luxury supercars and feel like an F1 racer for a day or learn all about your favorite brand through a private factory visit, or simply enjoy a vintage car tour through the Apennine mountain range.

#### Museo Ferrari Maranello

Enter the fascinating world of Enzo Ferrari visiting the world-famous museum at his birthplace in Maranello. Live your Formula 1 experience by driving a Ferrari on Modena’s racetrack, followed by lunch at Massimo Bottura’s Cavallino restaurant.

INQUIRE

#### Luxury Car factory visits

Discover the making of Italy’s most iconic automative producers, first-hand in factory visits to Maserati, Lamborghini, Dallara or Ducati. Or, explore the world-famous Collezione Pagani just outside of Modena, only about an hour from Roncolo.

INQUIRE

#### Vintage dream

Get a taste of Italian dolce vita while driving one of our estate classic cars along the cyprus tree-lined, winding roads off the rolling hills of Emilia. Our classic Alfa Romeo Spider and our Fiat Abarth are available to rent on request.

INQUIRE

#### Fast cars

Make your visit to Emilia unforgettable driving the car of your dreams in the countryside or on the racetrack. Become a race car driver for a day on the track, driving a real race car on a real racetrack, or explore the beautiful landscape of our hills on board your dream car.

# Sports & Wellness

Roncolo provides a green sanctuary from modern hectic life, offering the chance to switch off and fall back into a slower way of being. Refocus and recenter with private yoga lessons, trekking or biking in the surrounding _Parco di Roncolo_. For sportier activities we offer private training sessions in our gym, as well as off-site sports activities like tennis, horseback-riding or golf.

#### Trekking & biking

Our 130 hectare estate is surrounded by a natural preservation area, the Parco di Roncolo, and offers many kilometers of trails to discover the natural beauty of the Apennine mountain range. For lovers of trekking and hiking, guided tours are available. Mountain bikes and electric bikes are available for rent to explore the Apennine countryside.

INQUIRE

#### Golfing

Just ten minutes from the estate, the 18-hole Terre di Canossa Golf Club offers a technically engaging course set within the rolling landscape of the region.

For those wishing to extend their play, further member clubs of the Emilia Golf experience and the renowned Modena Golf & Country Club can be reached within thirty-to-forty minutes’ drive from the estate.

_Our team will be pleased to arrange tee times and green fees, ensuring a seamless experience from arrival to final putt._

INQUIRE

#### Tennis & horseback riding

For guests who enjoy tennis or equestrian pursuits, courts and riding facilities are located within short distance of the estate.

Whether you wish to refine your technique with private instruction or simply enjoy a match on clay, tennis sessions can be arranged to suit your level and pace.

Equally, horseback riding offers the choice between guided lessons and leisurely rides through the surrounding countryside- a graceful way to experience the landscape from a different perspective.

_Our team will be pleased to coordinate every detail._

# Arts & Culture

Along the ancient Via Emilia, Parma, Modena, Bologna, Ferrara and Ravenna form a trail of remarkable historical and cultural depth. Romanesque cathedrals, Renaissance _palazzi_, opera houses, mosaics and contemporary museums coexist with effortless continuity.

#### Guided city tours

Discover the historic cities of Emilia-Romagna through curated guided visits. From the Romanesque grandeur of Parma to the vibrant elegance of Bologna and the breathtaking mosaics of Ravenna, each city reveals layers of architecture, music, and history.

INQUIRE

#### Guided museum visits

Explore the region’s artistic and cultural heritage through private museum tours tailored to your interests. Whether Renaissance painting, contemporary collections, or the rich legacy of opera and craftsmanship, these visits offer context, insight, and a more intimate understanding of Emilia’s cultural identity.

Indulge in a day of culture and discover the amazing collections of _Collezione Maramotti, Palazzo Magnani,_ the _Labirinto della Masone_ or the _Pilotta_ Palace through a guided visit.

INQUIRE

# Emilia: Italy's Hidden Gem

The Land of the Slow Mix – an authentic, unexpected, and unexplored region.

Enjoy the best of the Italian _Dolce Vita_: savor the Food Valley and cruise through the Motor Valley. From Piacenza and Parma to Ferrara, Modena, and Ravenna, all the way to Reggio Emilia and Bologna, the cities along the ancient Via Emilia are the beating heart of this region.

Experience the great outdoors in the bucolic hills of the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines for a vacation filled with sports and nature.
