# _La Terra dello Slow Mix_

Emilia-Romagna surprises visitors as Italy’s ultimate “hidden gem”. Most often referred to as Italy’s Food Valley and Motor Valley, this region never ceases to amaze: from the Renaissance harmony of Parma to the vibrant spirit of Modena’s food markets, and from breathtaking UNESCO Byzantine mosaics in Ravenna, to the medieval hilltop castles, the “Little Venice” of Comacchio, and the world’s oldest university in Bologna.

#### Terre di Canossa

Roncolo finds its home in the _Terre di Canossa_, a land shaped by history and nature in equal measure. These hills were once the stage of one of medieval Europe’s defining moments – the meeting between Matilda of Canossa and Emperor Henry IV. The ruins of Canossa Castle still overlook the landscape, a quiet reminder of the region’s historical weight.

Today, the territory is defined by rolling vineyards, olive orchards, wooded hills, and open countryside that rises gently toward the Apennines – a landscape that remains active, cultivated, and lived in.

#### Food Valley

A true paradise for foodies in search of tradition, authenticity and culinary techniques passed down through generations.

_Parmigiano Reggiano, Aceto Balsamico, Prosciutto di Parma, Culatello di Zibello_ – not just local specialities but expressions of culinary history. Served in Michelin-starred dining rooms and in traditional trattorie alike, they move naturally through every level of the region’s culinary life. Emilia-Romagna’s food and wine heritage is a reflection of the region’s culture with 19 museums dedicated to celebrating its food culture. Visitors can experience tastings, guided visits, and hands-on encounters with producers who define the standards of Italian gastronomy.

#### Motor Valley

A short distance from Roncolo lies the heart of Italy’s Motor Valley – a concentration of automotive excellence found nowhere else in the world.

This is the land of Ferrari, Lamborghini, Maserati and Ducati: brands that have shaped global standards in performance, engineering, and design. Workshops, private collections, museums and racing circuits form a network that celebrates both heritage and innovation.

Motor Valley is not only about speed. It is about precision, craftsmanship, and vision; values deeply rooted in the region’s identity. Visitors can explore historic factories, attend exclusive driving experiences, or simply trace the roads where some of the most iconic machines in history were conceived.

In Emilia, engineering is culture.
