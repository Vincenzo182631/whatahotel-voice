# A sense of place

Life slows down at Roncolo. Dawn breaks softly welcoming the day ahead, birdsong replaces alarms, and the outside world feels comfortably distant. Feel at home in the quiet luxury of our historic residence.

# Villa Manodori

Housed within the 17th-century Villa Manodori, the six rooms and suites retain the quiet elegance of a once-aristocratic home. Original frescoes, contemporary furnishings, and generous proportions speak gently of the villa’s past, preserving its history.

Set on the first and second floors, the rooms open onto views of the formal gardens and the surrounding Tuscan-Emilian countryside.

# The _Dimore_ of Roncolo

The rooms and suites in Dimora Ancini and Dimora Acetaia welcome you in a cosy atmosphere of simplistic luxury. Located on the first and second floors of the historic outbuildings, rooms and feature original wooden beams and are furnished with natural fabrics and modern antiques.
