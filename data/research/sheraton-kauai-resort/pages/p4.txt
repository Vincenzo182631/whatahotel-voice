### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sheraton Kauai Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Sheraton Kauai Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Sheraton Kauai Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, Sheraton Kauai Resort has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Ocean Pool (Outdoor)Whirlpool

What are some local attractions and things to do near Sheraton Kauai Resort?

Blue Dolphin Charters, National Tropical Botanical Gardens, Opaekaa Falls, Spouting Horn, Russian Fort Elizabeth and more are nearby Sheraton Kauai Resort.
