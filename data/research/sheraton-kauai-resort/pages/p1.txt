# Welcome to Sheraton Kauai Resort

## Feel the Hawaiian energy at our resort hotel in Kauai

Aloha from Sheraton Kauai Resort, where your stay unfolds along the golden shores of Poipu Beach. As a resort in Kauai Hawaii, the property places you close to spectacular beaches, lush rainforests, and...See More renowned golf courses that showcase the island’s unmatched beauty. You settle into welcoming guest rooms and suites offering soothing oceanfront or garden views designed to help you unwind. Days move at your pace, whether you relax in a shaded cabana or enjoy the Ocean Pool steps from the sand. Dining is a highlight with fresh Hawaiian flavors served in open‑air settings, including sunset views at RumFire Poipu Beach. Indoor and outdoor venues create an inspiring setting for retreats, weddings, or family gatherings. As an oceanfront hotel in Kauai, our resort brings you closer to the rhythms of the island, while the comfort and warmth ensure every moment feels restorative and memorable. Your stay at our hotel resort in Kauai, Hawaii, blends island beauty with Sheraton comfort to create a truly unique escape.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Kauai Resort?

The check-in time at Sheraton Kauai Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Sheraton Kauai Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Sheraton Kauai Resort.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Kauai Resort?

On-site parking is available at Sheraton Kauai Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for $20.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Kauai Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Kauai Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Kauai Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Kauai Resort?

The closest airport near Sheraton Kauai Resort is Lihue Airport (LIH). LIH is located approximately 14.1 Miles from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Kauai Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Sheraton Kauai Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
