### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Resort have a work space?

No, the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Resort do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Sheraton Kauai Resort?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at Sheraton Kauai Resort. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Resort?

No, the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Resort do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Resort have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Kauai Resort have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Resort have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Kauai Resort have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Resort have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Kauai Resort have hair dryers.
