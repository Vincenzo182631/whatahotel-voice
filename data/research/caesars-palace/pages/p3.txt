## Everything you want to do

Take your vacation from ordinary to spectacular at Caesars Palace with the best celebrity chef dining, Garden of the Gods Pool Oasis, and spectacular entertainment. Don't forget to pamper yourself at Qua Baths & Spa and The Forum Shops.

Take your vacation from ordinary to spectacular.

#### Qua Baths and Spa

Caesars Palace’s renowned sanctuary has garnered numerous awards since its 2007 opening. With its retreat-like feel, unmatched amenities, and a more holistic approach to spa-ing, the experience at Qua is an all-encompassing ride to relaxation.

Explore Spa

## Conquer Your Adventure

When in Rome, do as the Romans do. Rule your Caesars Palace stay by seeing one of Vegas' top headliners at The Colosseum. Wrap up your night with a party at OMNIA and then recover at Qua Baths & Spa or the Garden of the Gods Pool Oasis.

When in Rome, do as the Romans do.

#### Pools

Garden of the Gods Pool Oasis features seven distinct pool experiences, from the excitement of gaming at Fortuna Pool to the adults-only luxury of Venus Pool. Enjoy lavish cabanas, stunning pools and one of the most iconic places to spend the day on the Las Vegas Strip.

Explore Pools

#### Hotel

One of the most central locations on the Strip, Caesars Palace has the most luxurious collection of Las Vegas rooms and suites that are routinely spectacular.

Explore Hotel

#### Casino

Caesars palace offers a unique Las Vegas casino experience. Let loose and play all of your favorite Vegas casino games, including poker, craps and video slots.

Explore Casino

#### Shows

Raising the bar in both luxury and entertainment, Caesars Palace is the premier destination for Las Vegas headliners and events for all ages.

Explore Shows

#### Restaurants

Caesars Palace is the ultimate destination for an eclectic assortment of top chef restaurants and other delectable dining options. Plus, family friendly options are available for those under 21.

Explore Restaurants

#### Nightlife

From the lush cocktails of Vanderpump Cocktail Garden to the hidden speakeasy vibe of Caspian’s Cocktails & Caviar, and the high-energy dance floor at OMNIA Nightclub, Caesars Palace nightlife can be as relaxed or as electric as you want it to be.

Explore Nightlife

#### Shops

Caesars Palace is the definitive luxury shopping destination. The Forum Shops include approximately 160 specialty stores, fine restaurants and attractions for adults, teens, and kids alike.

Explore Shops

#### Omnia

This multi-level venue encompasses a seductive ultra-lounge, a high-energy main room and mezzanine, as well as a breathtaking rooftop garden, showcasing panoramic views of the Las Vegas Strip.

Explore Omnia

#### Salon

Hairdreams Salon by Michael Boychuck offers hair, nail, makeup and tanning services in a state-of-the-art salon that mixes modern design décor with classic glamour.

Explore Salon

#### Weddings

Why get married at a chapel when you can get married at a Palace? True to the Caesars Palace tradition, Wedding Services provides a superior level of attention and assistance in planning the perfect destination wedding.

Explore Weddings

#### Golf

Take a break from it all and enjoy a leisurely round of golf. Las Vegas' premier golf courses, Serket and Cascata, offer unparalleled experiences in gorgeous, pristine views.

Explore Golf
