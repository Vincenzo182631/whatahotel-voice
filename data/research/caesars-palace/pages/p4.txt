How is Caesars Palace laid out, and which towers make up the resort?

Caesars Palace is a large,  multi-tower Vegas resort spread across more than 85 acres, with guest accommodations located in several distinct hotel towers rather than a single building. The resort is organized around a central casino and retail corridors, with indoor walkways connecting all areas.

**Hotel towers at Caesars Palace:** Augustus, Octavius, Julius, Palace, Forum, Colosseum, and Nobu Hotel. Each tower is situated in a different area of the property. Tower location can affect proximity to the pools, the casino floor, The Colosseum theater, or The Forum Shops. Guests interested in pool access often consider towers closer to the pool complex, while guests attending Colosseum performances may prefer accommodations nearer to that venue.

**Nobu Hotel:** A luxury hotel located within Caesars Palace. Features a distinct check-in experience while still providing full access to the broader resort amenities.

**Getting around the resort:** Because Caesars Palace spans more than 85 acres, walking distances can vary depending on tower location and destination. Many guests find it helpful to review the Caesars Palace property map to understand tower locations, entrances, and key resort areas before arrival.

## Getting Around

Whether you are arriving by rideshare, rental car, or shuttle, or navigating between Caesars Palace, Nobu Hotel, and The Forum Shops, here is everything you need to get around with ease.

Where is check‑in located at Caesars Palace, and do all guests check in at the same location? What landmarks help guests find it?

The main hotel check-in at Caesars Palace is located in the central hotel lobby near the main casino entrance and valet drop-off. Most guests check in at this centralized location, regardless of which tower they are staying in.

**Main lobby check-in:** Self-service check-in kiosks are available in the main lobby to help expedite the arrival process. The check-in area is easy to recognize once inside the lobby. Guests often use the valet entrance as a reference point, as it leads directly toward hotel registration. Inside the lobby, a large marble statue of Augustus Caesar, along with prominent overhead signage and the surrounding lobby space, serves as a clear visual landmark.

**Arriving by different transportation:** Guests arriving by valet will be closest to the main check-in lobby. Guests arriving by  rideshare, taxi, or self-parking typically enter from other access points and follow clearly marked wayfinding signage directing them to hotel check-in.

**Dedicated registration for Caesars Rewards members:** Caesars Palace offers separate registration locations for certain Caesars Rewards members, including Diamond and Seven Stars guests. These dedicated registration areas are located near the Augustus and Octavius Towers and are clearly signed for eligible guests.

**If guests are unsure where to go:** Caesars Palace team members are available throughout the property to help direct guests to the appropriate check-in location and answer questions.

Where can guests park at Caesars Palace, and what should they know about parking options and overnight parking?

Caesars Palace offers both self-parking and valet parking options, with on-site parking available for guests arriving by car.

**Self-parking:** Available in an  on-site parking garage with multiple access points depending on how guests approach the property. Inside the garage, clearly marked signage and elevators guide guests from parking levels directly into Caesars Palace. Guests who need assistance can ask team members for help navigating from the garage to the hotel.

**Valet parking:** Available at the main hotel entrance and offers the most direct access to the hotel lobby. Many guests choose valet for convenience, especially when arriving with luggage or planning a shorter walk to check-in.

**Overnight parking:** Hotel guests may leave their vehicles parked overnight while staying at Caesars Palace. Guests can keep their car parked for the duration of their stay without needing to move it daily. Parking arrangements and availability are managed on property.

**Oversized vehicles:** Caesars Palace can accommodate oversized vehicles such as trucks and large passenger cars that exceed the height limit for the self-parking garage. Oversized vehicles are accepted at main valet only, accessible from Las Vegas Boulevard. RVs, trailers, or trucks equipped with hitches, trailers, or boats cannot be accommodated. Parking is subject to availability and restrictions.

**For questions about parking:** Caesars Palace team members are available to help direct guests and provide guidance upon arrival.

Rideshare services and taxis use designated pick-up and drop-off areas at Caesars Palace that are separate from the valet entrance.

**Drop-off location:** These transportation zones are located within the parking garage and are clearly marked with on-site signage. Guests arriving by rideshare or taxi are dropped off in the designated transportation area inside the parking structure rather than at the valet.
