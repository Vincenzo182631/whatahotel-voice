#### Caesars Palace 60th ANNIVERSARY EXPERIENCE

60 Years of Greatness. One Extraordinary Journey. For six decades, Caesars Palace has stood at the heart of Las Vegas, welcoming royalty, presidents, entertainers, athletes, and millions of guests from around the world.

Discover the legacy of Caesars Palace through a curated collection of photographs, memorabilia, and artifacts celebrating six decades of innovation, hospitality, and excellence.

#### Roots of Fight 60th Anniversary Collection

Celebrate six decades of legendary moments with the exclusive Caesars Palace x Roots of Fight 60th Anniversary Collection. Featuring premium t-shirts, sweatshirts, and bags, this limited-edition collection honors the rich history, iconic imagery, and enduring legacy of Caesars Palace.

Designed with Roots of Fight's signature vintage-inspired style, each piece pays tribute to the place where champions, entertainers, and guests have come together for unforgettable experiences for nearly 60 years.

The collection is available exclusively at Caesars Palace in the Lobby Store and Appian Way Essentials.
