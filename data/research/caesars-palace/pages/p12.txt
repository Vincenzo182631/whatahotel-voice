## Caesars Palace Las Vegas Hotel FAQs

How many hotel rooms does Caesars Palace Las Vegas have?

Caesars Palace hotel offers 3,500+ guest rooms and suites in six distinct towers, many of which have been recently renovated.

Where is Caesars Palace Las Vegas located?

Caesars Palace is located center strip at 3570 Las Vegas Blvd South Las Vegas, NV 89109 Phone: 866-227-5938

Does Caesars Palace Have Luxury Hotel Suites in Las Vegas?

Yes, the iconic Caesars Palace Hotel in Las Vegas offers close to 500 suites ranging from one-bedroom luxury suites to top-floor penthouses and opulent three-bedroom villas with private patios.  Select suites feature state-of-the-art technology and amenities like wet bars, jetted tubs, pool tables, and fireplaces.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Vegas Caesars Palace?

Currently, the check-in time for Caesars Palace is 4 PM. Check-out times are at 11 AM.

Can I bring my dog to stay at Caesars Palace Las Vegas?

Yes, bring your dog and stay in one of the best pet-friendly hotels in Las Vegas. Our PetStay Program offers in-room mats and dishes.  Visit our PetStay page for details.

Does the Caesars Palace Hotel in Las Vegas offer room service?

Yes, Caesars offers you their very own  Caesars Eats room service app. The food is fresh, and it's right at your fingertips when you order online for take-out or delivery to your room.

Does Caesars Palace Las Vegas have handicap-accessible rooms and showers?

Yes, Caesars Entertainment is committed to providing an excellent and inclusive experience for our guests with disabilities.  Please visit our Accessibility page for more details.

Are there microwaves and refrigerators in the hotel rooms at Caesars Palace?

Currently, refrigerators are based upon availability for a small daily fee - request upon check-in. Microwaves are not available.

Can I earn and redeem Caesars Rewards at Caesars Palace Las Vegas?

Yes, the  Caesars Rewards program lets you earn Reward Credits through both your casino gaming and your entertainment activities at over 50 destinations nationwide. With Caesars Rewards, you can also earn Reward Credits through our exciting Caesars Rewards Partners.

Does Caesars Palace Las Vegas have a Save Now, Pay Later feature?

Yes, Caesars Palace offers a  convenient Save Now, Pay Later option within our checkout when you book a room. Discover deals for your next adventure.

Can I Self Check-In and Self Check-Out at Caesars Palace Las Vegas?

Yes, Caesars Palace saves you time with our convenient self-check-in and check-out on our  Caesars Rewards mobile app. Download for Apple or Android.

Does Caesars Palace Las Vegas offer a shuttle or bus service from the airport?

Currently, we do not offer shuttle service, however commercial shuttle busses are available 24 hours daily outside of baggage claim and depart the airport every 15-20 minutes. The ticket price is $15 per person one way and discounted round trip fair is $18 (price subject to change).

Where is the Rideshare Lot for Uber / Lyft Drop Off and Pick Up at Caesars Palace Las Vegas?

The Uber/Lyft pickup and rideshare areas for Caesars Palace is located at the parking garage in the back of Caesars Palace. Map coordinates are 36.11733769854647, -115.17784239188187

Is Caesars Palace a part of Caesars Rewards?

Yes. Caesars Palace Hotel is a Caesars Rewards Destination. As a new  Caesars Rewards member you can save up to 15% off hotel rooms just for signing up. Plus, unlock exclusive dining perks and entertainment benefits.

What is the AAA Five Diamond designation awarded to THE VILLAS Caesars Palace?

THE VILLAS Caesars Palace has received the prestigious AAA Five Diamond designation, one of the highest honors in the hospitality industry. This recognition is given to fewer than 1 percent of hotels in North America and celebrates properties that deliver exceptional luxury, personalized service, and unforgettable guest experiences. From private butler service and gourmet in-villa dining to opulent design and exclusive amenities, THE VILLAS offers a stay that is truly fit for royalty.
