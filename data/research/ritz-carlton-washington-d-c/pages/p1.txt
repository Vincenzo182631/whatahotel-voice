Community Footprints
Making A Difference
Community Footprints is our social and environmental responsibility program, and is guided by these principles: to engage, to contribute and to inspire.
The Community Footprints program at The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. represents a shared commitment to giving back to the community that has warmly welcomed the hotel for over 20+ years. By aligning with esteemed organizations like Boys Town, Comfort Cases, Make - A -Wish and Martha’s Table, the hotel is taking significant steps to contribute to the betterment of the community. This collaborative effort is set to create a positive and lasting impact, embodying the hotel’s commitment to corporate social responsibility and community engagement.
Comfort Cases
Comfort Cases is an organization championing dignity and hope for children in foster care. The organization provides backpacks filled with comfort and personal care items for youth entering the foster care system. Typically, children are given a trash bag to pack up their belongings when they are removed from their homes.
Learn More
Make - A - Wish
Make - A - Wish creates life-changing wishes for children with critical illnesses For wish kids, just the act of making their wish come true can give them the courage to comply with their medical treatments. Parents might finally feel like they can be optimistic. A wish experience can be a game-changer for a child with a critical illness.
Learn More
Martha’s Table
This organization is renowned for its efforts to support families and children with nutrition, education, and family support programs, by operating nationally accredited education programs, pairing healthy food access with physical and mental health services, and promoting family success by investing in family leaders. At Martha’s Table, they believe that every Washingtonian deserves the opportunity to thrive.
Learn More
Sustainability
At The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C., we proudly partake in environmentally conscious practices. We aim to minimize our carbon footprint through our towel and linen reuse program, recycling, composting, and implementing processes in our kitchen to minimize food waste. We are also committed to the responsible sourcing of ingredients in our kitchen, such as cocoa, sugar, coffee & tea products, animal proteins, seafood, and the usage of cage-free eggs, as well as what we use, including textiles, paper products, cleaning supplies, and guest room amenities.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C.?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C.. with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C.?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C.. Self parking is available for $40.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $68.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C.?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. is Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA). DCA is located approximately 4.6 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. have an airport shuttle to Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. does not have an airport shuttle to Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
