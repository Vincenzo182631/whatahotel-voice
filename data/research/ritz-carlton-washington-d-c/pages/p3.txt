Exceptional Luxury Suites in Washington, D.C.
Signature Suites
Elevate your stay to a spacious suite at our renowned Washington, D.C. hotel. The design concept of our luxury suites combines the relaxed informality of residential living with elegant touches, providing an eclectic sense of style found throughout the hotel.
The Ritz Carlton Suite
Indulge in opulence and luxury in The Ritz-Carlton Suite and experience lavish accommodations, elegant décor, and personalized service. Inspired by the residences of Washington, D.C., The Ritz-Carlton Suite evokes a ‘home away from home’ experience while marrying accents of traditional American design and contemporary elegance. The suite features a primary bedroom, spacious living room with a cozy fireplace, formal eight-seat dining room with an adjacent pantry, one-and-a-half marble bathrooms with a Jacuzzi bathtub and rain forest shower, and an expansive outdoor terrace.
