Holidays
Celebrate the season in the heart of the nation's capital. From festive dining experiences and holiday gatherings to overnight escapes and seasonal traditions, The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. offers thoughtful moments designed to bring family, friends, and colleagues together. Discover a holiday season defined by warm hospitality, refined surroundings, and memorable celebrations.
