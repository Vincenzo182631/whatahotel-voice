From Landmarks to Nightlife, Your DC Adventure Begins in the West End
Experiences
Home to national monuments and international cultural experiences, Washington, D.C. has something for everyone. Our luxury Washington, D.C. hotel is conveniently located near many of the city’s most iconic attractions, making exploration easy. Immerse yourself in the magic of the city from our Foggy Bottom hotel, where nearby Metro lines easily transport you to any D.C. destination in minutes. Take in a show at the National Theatre, explore the monuments and museums on the National Mall, or visit the International Spy Museum. Take advantage of our hotel’s location near downtown D.C., Dupont Circle, and George Washington University to explore on your own, or allow the concierge to assist you in crafting a customized itinerary.

Discover Washington, D.C.
Washington, D.C. offers unforgettable experiences for all who visit. From the beauty of the Cherry Blossoms at the Tidal Basin and the excitement of the National Mall, to the grandeur of the city’s most notable sites, there is something for everyone to enjoy. Allow our Concierge to curate the perfect itinerary for your stay, from classic attractions to sites suited to your taste.
Presidential Cookie Display
From the time our guests arrive, we ensure that they get a taste of the District. A cookie display of past and present Presidents’ favorite cookie are the perfect treat upon checking in. Join us in the Main Lobby every day at 5:30pm for a presidential cookie tasting.
Ritz Kids
From the moment of arrival, we welcome kids to The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C., encouraging them to explore the sights and sounds of our family-friendly hotel. Guided by playfulness and wonder, Ritz Kids delights and engages children to interact with their surroundings in exciting ways, introducing them to four pillars of discovery: Water, Land, Environmental Responsibility, and Culture.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton, Washington, D.C. offers a 5:30 AM-10:00 PM fitness center available for a fee to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as 1 Basketball Court, 4 International Squash Courts, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
