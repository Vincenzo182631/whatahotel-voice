# The Palazzo at The Venetian Resort Las Vegas \| Hyatt Hotels & Resorts

## Experience an Elegant Escape in Las Vegas

A pure expression of “sprezzatura,” The Palazzo at The Venetian Resort is an exquisite escape into Italian elegance. Discover an exceptional array of upscale restaurants and bars, heated swimming pools and some of the largest luxury suites on the Las Vegas Strip starting at 720 square feet. Experience a destination you’ll want to indulge in again and again at The Palazzo at The Venetian Resort Las Vegas.

This resort is now part of the World of Hyatt loyalty program.

## Suites

Starting at 720 square feet, The Palazzo at The Venetian Resort suites are some of the largest on the Las Vegas Strip. Enjoy understated elegance in every suite with a marble bathroom, a workspace, a living area and a luxurious pillow-top bed. Prestige Club Lounge suites also include a welcome glass of prosecco upon check-in, a personal concierge and complimentary breakfast.
