### A True Paradise on the Roof of the World – Excepti

a year ago

My stay at the InterContinental Lhasa Paradise was nothing short of spectacular. As a traveler concerned about adapting to high-altitude conditions, I was deeply impressed by every aspect of this hotel—from the thoughtful amenities to the warm, culturally rich service.The hotel features a ​central oxygen supply system​ that ensures a comfortable and safe high-altitude experience. Unlike noisy oxygen machines common in other hotels, the diffuse oxygen here is quiet and seamless—I barely noticed the source while enjoying a restful sleep without any altitude sickness symptoms. Waking up refreshed in Lhasa’s thin air felt like a miracle! This scientific approach to oxygen enrichment truly sets InterContinental apart as a leader in high-altitude hospitality.From the moment I stepped into the grand, Tibetan-style lobby, the staff greeted me with genuine warmth. The breakfast buffet was a highlight, offering a diverse spread of Chinese, Western, and ​authentic Tibetan cuisine.nterContinental Lhasa Paradise isn’t just a hotel; it’s a holistic high-altitude experience that combines innovative oxygen technology, heartfelt service, and deep cultural immersion. Whether you’re a first-time visitor to Tibet or a returning traveler, this hotel ensures your stay is both healthy and memorable. I can’t wait to return!

Yes, I recommend this hotel.
