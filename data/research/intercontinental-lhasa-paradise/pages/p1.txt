## FAQs for InterContinental Lhasa Paradise

Does InterContinental Lhasa Paradise have a pool?

No, InterContinental Lhasa Paradise does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental Lhasa Paradise?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Lhasa Paradise for hotel guests.

Free parking is available at the entrance.

Parking fee: 0 CNY per day

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Bus, Rv, Trailer, Truck

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Lhasa Paradise?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Lhasa Paradise.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Lhasa Paradise for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Lhasa Paradise have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Lhasa Paradise has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Lhasa Paradise?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Lhasa Paradise.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Lhasa Paradise?

Check-in at InterContinental Lhasa Paradise is from 2:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Lhasa Paradise offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Lhasa Paradise offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Lhasa Paradise from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Lhasa Paradise is conveniently located near Lhasa Gonggar Airport (LXA), approximately 43.5 miles away (70 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Lhasa Paradise?

InterContinental Lhasa Paradise is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Potala Palace-5.03 miles away (8.1 kilometers)
- Jokhang Temple-4.1 miles away (6.6 kilometers)
- Norbulingka-6.59 miles away (10.6 kilometers)
- Sera Monastery-6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)
- Drepung Monastery-9.51 miles away (15.3 kilometers)
- Gandan Monastery-30.94 miles away (49.8 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Lhasa Paradise puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
