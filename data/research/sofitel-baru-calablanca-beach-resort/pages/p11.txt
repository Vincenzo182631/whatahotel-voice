# Family Accommodations

* * *

**Our family rooms offer ample space for a crib and additional baby furniture, ensuring maximum comfort for your little ones. Whichever option you choose for your luxury family getaway, we promise the legendary Sofitel MY BED experience, attentive service for both children and adults, and plenty of magical moments that will make your stay truly unforgettable.**
