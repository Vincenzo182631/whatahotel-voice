# Barú Serenity Ocean Suite

Discover the pure harmony of the Caribbean in the **Barú Serenity Ocean Suite Room**, a sanctuary where the calm of the sea becomes part of your stay. From your private terrace, the endless blue horizon invites you to pause, breathe, and let each gentle wave set the rhythm of your relaxation.

Natural light envelops every corner, revealing living and resting areas designed to offer deep well-being. Natural fibers, organic textures, and handcrafted artisanal details blend with a one-of-a-kind hand-stitched tapestry inspired by mangroves, birds, and the vibrant colors of the Caribbean, created by Colombian artist Eloin Rivera.

With 67 m² of space, this suite features a king or twin Sofitel MyBed®, a cozy living area, and a bathroom designed for indulgence, equipped with a shower, bathtub, and L’Occitane amenities. When you prefer to savor the moment without leaving your room, allow a gastronomic breakfast or our 24-hour in-room dining service to complement your experience.
