# Prestige Coral Suite

Step into the essence of Caribbean luxury in the **Prestige Coral Suite Room**, where serenity and sophistication blend in perfect harmony. Your private balcony opens to a horizon where water and sky become one, inviting you to pause time and embrace the pure beauty of Isla Barú.

Natural light fills every corner, revealing living and relaxation areas thoughtfully designed for sensory delight. Natural fibers, organic textures, and locally crafted artisanal details coexist with a unique hand-stitched tapestry inspired by mangroves, birds, and the vibrant colors of the Caribbean by Colombian artist Eloin Rivera. The result is an ambiance that celebrates the authenticity of nature with the elegance of French _art de vivre_.

With 78 m² of space, the suite features a king-size Sofitel MyBed®, an elegant living area, and a bathroom designed for deep well-being, equipped with a shower, bathtub, and L’Occitane amenities. From your balcony, the sea becomes a silent companion that turns each sunrise into an exceptional moment.
