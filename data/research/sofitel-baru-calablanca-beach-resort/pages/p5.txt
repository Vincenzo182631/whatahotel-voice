# Superior Room

The spacious, light-filled Superior Room has everything you need for a truly restful stay in Isla Barú. Each light-filled room offers distinct living and relaxation areas carefully curated for the enjoyment of guests. Alluding to the lush biodiversity of the region, the design highlights natural fibers and textures, locally crafted artisanal details and features one of a kind handstitched tapestry inspired by mangroves, birds, and the colors of the Caribbean by Colombian artist Eloin Rivera. 155 rooms each measuring 40m2, including the following features: king or twin accommodation with Sofitel “MyBed®” mattresses & bedding, a private balcony to sit and enjoy a panoramic Tropical Garden View, bathroom with shower and L´Occitane amenities.
