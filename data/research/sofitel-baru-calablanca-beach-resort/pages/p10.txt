# Presidential Ocean View Penthouse

Rise to a realm of absolute sophistication in the **Presidential Ocean View Penthouse Room**, a sanctuary where privacy, generous spaces, and the beauty of the Caribbean come together to create an extraordinary experience.

Located on the rooftop of Block 10, this 161 m² penthouse reveals a world of elevated serenity. The king-size Sofitel _MyBed®_ promises exquisite rest, while the private terrace — featuring a jacuzzi with panoramic sea views — invites you to pause and watch the horizon dissolve into endless shades of blue.

Inside, natural light flows through expansive spaces, highlighting a grand dining area, an elegant living room, and a dedicated butler kitchen designed for impeccable service. Every detail has been thoughtfully curated to offer moments of indulgence, intimacy, and refined pleasure.

The design celebrates the lush biodiversity of Barú: natural fibers, organic textures, and artisanal details crafted by local hands harmonize with a one-of-a-kind hand-stitched tapestry inspired by mangroves, birds, and the vibrant Caribbean palette, created by Colombian artist Eloin Rivera.

_For an elevated stay, please inquire about our exclusive gastronomic breakfast experience for suite guests._
