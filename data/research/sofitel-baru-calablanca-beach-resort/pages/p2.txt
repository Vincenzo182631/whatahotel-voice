# Rooms & Suites

* * *

**Understated refinement. Thoughtful, surprising amenities. Accents of stone and exotic wood with a color palette that evokes the Colombian seaside and landscape. Inspiring views of gardens and Caribbean waters of the Isla Barú from your room. Whether you stay a day, a week or longer, you feel immediately at home in this inviting cocoon. Sofitel Barú Cartagena Beach Resort creates a unique synergy between nature and architectural design. With LEED certification and drawing inspiration from surrounding biodiversity, each detail is carefully considered using natural materials to evoke a feeling of luxury, which translates to relaxation, sophistication and fun à la plage. Its ten towers feature 187 rooms, including 23 suites located on the top floor of towers 4-10.**
