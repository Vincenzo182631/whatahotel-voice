# Emerald Ocean Suite

Let yourself be enveloped by a sublime atmosphere of luxury in the **Emerald Ocean Suite Room**, where the serenity of the Caribbean sets the rhythm for an unforgettable stay. From your spacious private terrace with Jacuzzi, the endless blue horizon becomes an intimate stage that invites you to pause, breathe deeply, and surrender to the art of relaxation.

The suite unveils spaces designed for sensory delight: living and lounging areas bathed in natural light, organic textures that echo the essence of the island, and artisanal details crafted by local hands. Each element is complemented by a unique hand-stitched tapestry by Colombian artist Eloin Rivera, inspired by mangroves, birds, and the vibrant colors of the Caribbean.

With 84 sqm of refined elegance, this suite features a king-size Sofitel MyBed®, a cozy seating area, and a bathroom conceived as a sanctuary of well-being, complete with shower, bathtub, and L’Occitane amenities. When you wish to enjoy the moment without leaving your suite, allow a gastronomic breakfast or 24-hour in-room dining to elevate your experience.
