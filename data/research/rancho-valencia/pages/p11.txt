# Resort Experiences at Rancho Valencia

Taking full advantage of the year-round sunny climate of San Diego, Rancho Valencia Resort offers guests access to a variety of experiences both on and off our beautiful property. Explore the beautiful surroundings in Rancho Santa Fe and pedal off with our resort bicycles. Take in a yoga class to enhance your practice and attain a sense of inner calm. Hit the courts at our top-ranked San Diego tennis facility to work on your serve. Sail San Diego Bay on a luxurious sailboat or power yacht, whisk down to the Valle de Guadalupe wine country, go behind the scenes and cheer on the ponies at the Del Mar Races, take a private jet to the Santa Barbara wine country, hit the links, or simply take a picnic to the gorgeous beaches of North San Diego County. Experience all Southern California has to offer with these bespoke, curated, Rancho Valencia-style day excursions.

Please contact Concierge Services at concierge@ranchovalencia.com or 858-759-6228 to plan your experience today!

## Outdoor Activities Abound!

Enjoy the year-round sunny San Diego weather.  Book private kayak tours with the sea lions, snorkel with the leopard sharks, or e-bike excursions through La Jolla.  Hike the beautiful Torrey Pines trails with a naturalist, take a surf lesson, or just spend the day at the beach!

## Wine Country

Discover the enchanting Ruta del Vino and world-class wines of the Valle de Guadalupe just south of the border, or explore our “local” vineyards of Temecula with private, VIP custom day trips.

## Surf School

The warm, sunny summer days and mellow beginner and intermediate waves at Del Mar Beach are perfect for learning to surf!  Our fun, enthusiastic, and highly skilled instructors are available for full or half days to teach the whole family or for one-on-one instruction.

## Golf

Guests of Rancho Valencia Resort and Spa receive privileged access to the best San Diego golf courses, all within minutes of the resort. Last-minute tee time?  Enjoy the use of top of the line Callaway golf clubs, complimentary with your stay!

## Beach Day!

Spend a day at our local beaches in Del Mar, Solana Beach, and Cardiff.  Just a short ride from the resort, these world-class beaches are perfect for sunning and frolicking in the gentle waves.  Let us do the heavy lifting with setup, picnic and transport to create the perfect beach day for one or the whole family!
