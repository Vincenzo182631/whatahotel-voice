## Total escape begins the moment you arrive

This lavish five-bedroom, five and a half bathroom, 5,860 square-foot private residence combines Mexican-Morroccan styling with absolute luxury and offers unrivaled space for your entire family, group, or retreat.  Expansive, lush, beautifully landscaped outdoor areas include a sparkling pool and loungers, outdoor kitchen, dining, and seating areas, designed to encourage celebration of life and inspired gatherings.

An inviting living room and separate indoor dining area provide luxurious space to unwind, while a full gourmet kitchen, stocked to your wishes, with additional seating provides the perfect backdrop for any occasion. Private chef and concierge services can be arranged to attend to every need.

Casa Valencia offers five uniquely designed bedrooms with custom furnishings and gorgeous artwork.  Two king master suites, 1 king bedroom with ensuite bathroom, 1 queen bedroom with ensuite bathroom, and a flexible 5th bedroom with two double beds and bathroom provide the perfect private escape after a full day of endless activities at Rancho Valencia Resort and the surrounding San Diego area.

There is no need to forego your daily fitness routine or resort camaraderie while enjoying Casa Valencia.   Work out in your own private gym, or take your GEM electric Rancho Valencia golf cart to the Spa for a fitness class.  All the amenities of our world-class 5-star resort, combined with the elevated luxury and exclusivity of your own private retreat, create the perfect atmosphere for your next escape to Rancho Valencia Resort and Spa.
