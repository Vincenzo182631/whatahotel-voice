## The Spa

Tucked away amid lush gardens and olive groves lies an enchanting oasis of self-discovery, wellness, fitness, and relaxation—The Spa at Rancho Valencia. Celebrating the revitalizing benefits of herbs, minerals and oils along with leading edge product lines and offerings, The Spa offers a vast array of choices to soothe the spirit, rejuvenate the body and enrich the mind.

Every detail at our Forbes 5-Star rated spa is meticulously tailored to create the ultimate destination for wellness and tranquility, starting with the basics like freshly-made organic fruit and vegetable juices, to the Serenity Yoga Pavilion, Pilates studio, advanced exercise programs, and fitness sessions hosted by top-tier wellness professionals.

**Guests are invited to immerse themselves in our full spectrum of offerings, including a state-of-the-art fitness center, luxurious salon with hair, manicure and pedicure services, a variety of massage, facial, and body treatments, hydrotherapy, spa pool, and a thoughtfully curated retail boutique.**

HYDROTHERAPY

A Guided Hydrotherapy Circuit is the therapeutic use of different water temperatures. The purpose of these therapies is to increase heart rate, blood flow and respiration, thus flushing the internal organs of toxins. The increased blood flow increases circulation throughout the body. Fresh oxygenated blood brings nourishment to all of the cells of the body. Deep breathing opens and cleanses the lungs. Rancho Valencia’s Hydrotherapy Circuit offers these therapies in a tranquil and lush atmosphere with outdoor steam, sauna, cold plunge, and hand-painted tiled whirlpools with soothing fountains.
