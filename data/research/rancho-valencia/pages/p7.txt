# Valencia King Suite at Rancho Valencia

When you escape to the picturesque Rancho Valencia Resort and Spa, you are treated to timeless Mediterranean ambiance and a refreshing residential experience. Wake up to a glass of fresh-squeezed orange juice and a newspaper delivered to your door each morning. Pamper yourself with luxury bath products. In the evening, immerse yourself in romance with a glowing fire and a bottle of wine on your private patio at our Rancho Santa Fe resort.

## Total escape begins the moment you step into your suite

Our luxuriously appointed, 1300 square-foot Valencia 1-bedroom suites in San Diego feature sumptuous custom furnishings, 600-thread-count Frette linens and terry, down bedding, and your choice of natural or hypoallergenic pillows.Your cozy indoor fireplace, barista bar, customizable complimentary minibar, heated Japanese commode, Dyson hair dryers, Chromecast streaming system, state of the art 75-inch LG flatscreen television, top-line queen pullout couch, and spacious walk-in closet are our standard in all resort casitas.Enjoy fresh-squeezed orange or wellness juice, delivered each morning, on your private patio appointed with two chaise lounges and breakfast table, outdoor fireplace, and whirlpool. Cozy up into your evening with nightly turndown service, 24-hour in-room dining, and a magical night’s sleep on your top line Stearns and Foster mattress. The Valencia suites feature a deluxe King bed.
