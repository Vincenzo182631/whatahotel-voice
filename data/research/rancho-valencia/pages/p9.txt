# Restaurants and Dining at Rancho Valencia

Welcome to Rancho Valencia. Where lush citrus and olive groves meet sunset skies dotted with hot air balloons. Where cuisine is intentionally crafted using, seasonal produce sourced directly from California’s family farms, fisherman, and ranchers. It all adds up to an exceptional dining experience that is far from the ordinary, and simply perfect.

Our talented culinary team works personally with some of the country’s most notable farms to source the elements for Rancho Valencia’s cuisine, ensuring only the best ingredients and freshest flavors possible. The Pony Room brings together stunning valley views  in a captivating, candlelit modern hacienda setting serving up imaginative craft cocktails and tequila alongside fresh Coastal Ranch cuisine for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

## THE PONY ROOM

The Pony Room is a favorite among locals and resort guests alike. In addition to a wide selection of locally brewed craft beers and 100 variations of tequila, the bar also serves one of the area’s most impressive collections of high-end wines and hand-crafted, one-of-a-kind signature cocktails alongside fresh, locally-sourced Coastal Ranch cuisine.
