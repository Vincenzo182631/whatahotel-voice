# Welcome to Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse

## Experience Tampa history in a downtown boutique hotel

Travel back in time at the newly renovated Le Meridien Tampa, one of the finest boutique hotels in Tampa, FL. This century old Historic Federal Courthouse has been transformed into a one of a kind hotel....See More Located in the heart of downtown Tampa, this boutique hotel is the backdrop for exploring the city in style. Our onsite restaurant Sal Rosa Island Kitchen & Bar features a mix of Island Latin cuisine and Caribbean themed cocktails. Sal Rosa Cafe & Scoops offers specialty coffees, variety of house made gelato, pastries, and desserts. Iconic Tampa courtrooms have been repurposed as elegant hotel meeting rooms and event venues, offering 4,800 sq ft of meeting and event space. Contemporary facilities include an outdoor pool, 24 hr fitness center equipped with new Peloton bikes and Life Fitness equipment & a 24 hr sundries shop. ### Historical Building

## Unique Architecture

Le Méridien Tampa resides in a historic courthouse featuring stunning architecture, marble hallways and original oak doors from when the building was erected in 1905. An old judge's desk has been repurposed as the Concierge Desk, and skylights are located above many guest room doors.

Hotel History

First Floor

##### The Judge's Bench

Originally located in the fourth-floor courtroom, the judge’s bench is now the business center desk on the lobby level of the hotel. While we removed the Kevlar sheeting inside of the original bench, the judge’s nameplates and emergency buttons are still intact.

##### Blueprints Wallpaper

These blueprints are a replica of the original architect’s blueprints of the building’s lighting structures. The same lamps shown on the blueprints can be seen in the front of the building.

##### Post Office Desk

Also, on the first floor of our hotel is an antique post office desk. Before it was a courthouse, the building originated as a post office and customs house. The bustling cigar industry in Downtown Tampa/Ybor City made a customs house necessary.

##### Gold Elevator

The gold elevator doors have been preserved to maintain their original elegance. The contrast of the bright gold with the terrazzo marble make this elevator even more eye-catching.

##### The Pool

A century ago, the pool deck space was where paddy wagons would arrive with defendants accused of crimes. Before they were brought to court, the accused would be placed in holding cells which are now the guest rooms overlooking the pool. This space is a great option for those looking for a beautiful wedding venue with a story behind it.

Second Floor

##### Crown Moulding and Terrazzo Marble

While on the second floor, gaze up at the intricate detailing of the crown molding which highlights the striking terrazzo marble.

##### Witness Stand

The original witness stand from the courtroom was repurposed in Sal Rosa as the hostess stand.

Fourth Floor

##### Terrazzo Marble

Like the second floor, the fourth floor of the hotel has gorgeous terrazzo marble from the original structure.

##### Covered Atrium

Behind the shades in the fourth-floor foyer lay the atrium, which was eventually covered during World War II.

##### Gold Eagle

The gold eagle in the courtroom has remained untouched. The regality of the gold eagle and its elaborate detailing help make this room an excellent place to host a wedding or reception.

##### Original Wood Paneling

The sophisticated wood paneling throughout the fourth-floor courthouse is original.

##### Hand-Painted Ceilings

The ceilings on the fourth floor still display the intricate hand-painted designs from a century ago.

##### The Juror's Bench

Inside our restaurant, Sal Rosa, you will find the juror’s bench from the fourth-floor courtroom repurposed as a seating area.

### Unlock Art

Le Méridien Tampa’s Unlock Art program offers guests complimentary access to local contemporary cultural institutions.

## Access Local Art

The hotel partners with the nearby Tampa Museum of Art, where guests receive complimentary entry.

Details

At Le Méridien Tampa, a boutique Tampa hotel, we encourage the unfolding of creativity and a new perspective by offering guests unexpected and original experiences that will enhance their stay. To achieve this, Le Méridien has developed Unlock Art™, an innovative partnership that offers guests complimentary access to local contemporary cultural institutions.

Le Méridien Tampa has partnered with the Tampa Museum of Art. Guests can gain access to this remarkable museum free of charge by bringing along the Unlock Art pass located in each guest room - a pass that opens the door to more.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse?

On-site valet parking is available at Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse. The daily valet fee is $52.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse is Tampa International Airport (TPA). TPA is located approximately 7.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
