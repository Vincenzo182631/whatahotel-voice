## Dining

Welcome to Sal Rosa, where bold Latin-American flavors, handcrafted cocktails, and vibrant energy collide inside downtown Tampa’s historic Courthouse. More than a restaurant, it’s a vibe—a lively social...See More hub where every visit is a celebration of flavor, connection, and good times. Serving breakfast, lunch dinner and weekend brunch, the menu showcases a variety of dishes with a modern twist. For a quick pick-me-up, Café + Scoops serves up freshly brewed coffee, flaky pastries, house-made ice cream, and seasonal gelato. From weekly happenings to happy hour, late-night bites to private gatherings, Sal Rosa is where Tampa comes to savor, sip, and celebrate. Located on the 2nd floor of the Le Méridien Tampa hotel.See Less
