Do the rooms at Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse have a desk for working.

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse?

No, the rooms at Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse do not permit rollaway beds.
