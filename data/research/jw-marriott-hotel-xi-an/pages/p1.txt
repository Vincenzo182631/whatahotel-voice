# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an

Blending the ancient beauty of Chang’an with the sophistication of the JW Marriott brand, JW Marriott Hotel Xi’an is dedicated to providing guests a distinctive travel experience with the intriguing Xi’an history. This Xi’an CBD hotel is located in the Xi’an Economic and Technological Development Zone, adjacent to the administrative center of the city, with direct access to the Wenjing Road Subway...See More Line 4. The modernistic design of the luxury hotel in Xi'an reflects the contemporary character of this world-famous city and the refinement of the local culture. Standing in the lobby of our Xi'an CBD hotel on the 38th floor or in any of the 5-star guestrooms, you will be presented with a large panoramic mesmerizing portrait of the city view and feel the extraordinary energy of Chang’an city.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an is Xianyang Airport (XIY). XIY is located approximately 27.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Xi'an has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
