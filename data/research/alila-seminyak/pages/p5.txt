## Alila Moments

Shaped by land and craft, by culture and care, Alila Moments reveal a different way of seeing. These personalized experiences offered at our Alila properties draw you closer to the world around you, and your place within it. These are our Moments, crafted just for you.

### Seasalt Journey—The Salt & Tradition Trail

Never take traditional sea salt for granted as you visit the volcanic black-sand beach of Kusamba in East Bali to discover ancient methods of salt farming. This technique using sun and wind evaporation has been preserved and passed down through generations. Watch as salt farmers gather seawater in buckets to pour over finely raked sand and begin the process of making Balinese fleur de sel (flower of salt). After spending the first part of the day witnessing this time-honored art form, head to Tenganan village to see another tradition—the celebrated weaving process of making Gringsing cloth. Wind down the day of discovery with a serene coastal lunch prepared with freshly harvested salt and just-picked ingredients at the Seasalt restaurant, located at our sister resort Alila Manggis.

**Salt of the Earth**

Join local farmers on the black-sand beaches of Kusamba and learn how seawater is transformed into Balinese fleur de sel using time-honored techniques.

**Woven in Meaning**

At Tenganan village, observe the creation of Gringsing cloth—woven using double ikat methods and imbued with protective spiritual energy.

**A Coastal Feast**

Conclude your day with a seaside meal at Seasalt at Alila Manggis, celebrating the very ingredients and artisans you met earlier in the day.

**Duration:** 8 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Comfortable resort wear. Guests may bring a hat as Bali days can get hot.

**Additional Details:** Breakfast box included in the car, along with bottle water, cold towels, phone chargers and sun block (upon request). Lunch included at Alila Manggis before heading back to Alila Seminyak.

**Price:** IDR 3,500,000++ per person OR IDR 4,500,000++ per couple

### Spirit of Serenity—A Sacred Temple Ritual

Begin your day with a quiet step into Balinese tradition at the in-house Segara Temple, where centuries-old customs still live through the care of local landowners. In this sacred space you'll craft vibrant offerings rich with meaning and intention—Canang Sari, small handwoven palm-leaf baskets filled with flowers and incense placed daily at shrines, and Gebogan, towering arrangements of fruit and flowers carried on the heads of Balinese women during temple ceremonies. Led by Bapak Mangku, the temple’s spiritual guide, take part in a Melukat water blessing, releasing what no longer serves you and welcoming a sense of clarity and peace. The experience closes with the tying of the Tridatu bracelet, a powerful symbol of protection and spiritual connection. This is more than a ritual—it’s a day of stillness, insight and quiet transformation.

**Temple Awakening**

Explore Alila’s own Segara Temple, where stories of the land and spiritual legacy are shared with care and meaning.

**Offerings with Intention**

Create two meaningful offerings: Canang Sari, daily floral blessings of gratitude, and Gebogan, ceremonial towers of fruit and flowers made for temple rituals.

**Waters of Renewal**

Participate in a Melukat water blessing led by Bapak Mangku, the temple priest, to cleanse and recalibrate body, mind and spirit.

**A Thread of Connection**

Conclude the ritual with the tying of a Tridatu bracelet—three sacred threads worn for spiritual balance and protection.

**Duration:** 3-4 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Comfortable resort wear. Modest clothing is recommended, as the activity will be held at our resort's Segara Temple. Traditional Balinese sarong will be provided by Alila Seminyak.

**Additional Details:** Tridatu Balinese bracelets will be given out after the activity. Refreshments such as traditional sweets and ginger beverages are available for guests. The activity will be guided by the spiritual leader of the Temple, named Bapak Mangku.

**Price:** IDR 950,000++ per person

### Jeweler, Artisan, Storyteller—The John Hardy Workshop

In the quiet beauty of Mambal Village, a journey of artistry and purpose unfolds. Guided by Alila Seminyak and legendary John Hardy artisans, guests step into a world where tradition and craftsmanship converge. Inside the famed workshop, you’ll carve, draw and weave alongside master artisans, transforming raw materials into a personal creation. As hands shape wax, you'll feel a deeper connection to Bali’s creative heartland. After, gather around the Long Table, where stories are shared over a traditional feast and community and creativity are celebrated. This workshop is a celebration of community, heritage and the timelessness of wearable art.

**The Journey to Craft**

Guests will embark on a journey from Alila Seminyak to the John Hardy Workshop, located at the Kapal Bamboo Boutique in Mambal Village. The drive, which lasts about one and a half hours, takes you through stepped rice paddies and other stunning naturescapes. The workshop is nestled among the picturesque rice fields of Ubud.

**Hands of the Artisan**

Guests will have the unique opportunity to become artisans themselves as they work closely with the highly skilled master artisans of the John Hardy workshop. Participants will gain hands-on knowledge of the meticulous craftsmanship that goes into creating the exquisite handmade jewelry for which John Hardy is renowned.

**The Long Table**

Following the creative session, guests will enjoy John Hardy’s signature Long Table Lunch. This communal dining experience features a traditional Balinese feast, allowing guests to immerse themselves in the vibrant atmosphere that celebrates the brand’s 30-year tradition of sharing meals among artisans, employees and visitors.

**Duration:** 6 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Comfortable dress. Bring a hat.

**Additional Details:** Signature Long Table Lunch will be included by John Hardy at the workshop. After the wax is made, guests can give the design in the wax to the John Hardy team to craft into silver jewelry. This will take 4-6 weeks and be shipped to you upon completion.

**Price:** IDR 3,300,000++ per person

### Harmony Through Movement and Mindfulness

Begin your day of introspection with a calming Tai Chi session led by the resort's Ibu Wirati, where each mindful movement grounds the body and quiets the mind. A private numerology session invites deeper self-awareness, offering personal insight and gentle guidance for your current path. As the day unfolds, a Balinese massage with aromatic oils soothes the senses, while Gua Sha therapy clears stagnant energy. The ritual concludes with a traditional Boreh treatment—warm, herbal and deeply invigorating. More than a wellness day, this is a spiritual reset, an invitation to reconnect with yourself, find clarity and carry home a renewed sense of inner peace.

**Movement with Meaning**

The day begins with a serene Tai Chi session led by Ibu Wirati, a skilled Tai Chi master. This gentle martial art practice focuses on deliberate movements that awaken both body and mind.

**Numbers that Guide**

**Healing with Touch**

**A Warming Final Ritual**

### Feeding A Sense of Community

Just a short distance from Alila Seminyak, the RAGAM Community Kitchen offers a heartfelt opportunity to connect, give back and find deeper meaning in your journey. In this welcoming space, you’ll cook side-by-side with locals and fellow travelers, preparing nourishing meals with care and intention. But the true transformation happens as you deliver these meals to residents of communities around the resort—offering not just food, but warmth, connection and conversation. Sharing stories with families and learning about their lives leaves an imprint far deeper than any souvenir. It’s an experience that reminds you that kindness travels both ways—and a reminder of the deeper ways we can show up for the places we visit.

**Cooking with Compassion**

Located just 2 kilometers from Alila Seminyak, Ragam Community Kitchen serves as a beacon of hope and nourishment for the community. Not only will you have the opportunity to learn about the importance of sharing and giving back, but you will also engage in the joyful act of cooking alongside local and international residents.

**Meals with Meaning**

Once the meals are prepared, you will set out as a group to deliver them to designated locations in the city of Denpasar, Bali. This part of the experience is not just about dropping off food; it’s about connecting with the recipients.

**Stories that Stay**

You’ll have the chance to engage with families, share stories and learn about their lives. This interaction creates a profound awareness of the challenges they face and the resilience they embody.

**Duration:** 4 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Comfortable resort wear. Please bring a hat.

**Additional Details:** Complimentary transport from Alila Seminyak to Ragam Community Kitchen included. We will donate rice, eggs, oil and noodles using the proceed from guests.

**Price:** IDR 2,200,000++ per person This amount will be donated for food and other basic needs to support for greater goods of the local community in Bali
