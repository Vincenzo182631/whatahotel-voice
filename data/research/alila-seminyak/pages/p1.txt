## A Secluded Beachfront Sanctuary Steps from the Indian Ocean

Unfurling along a pristine beach on Bali’s southwest coast, mere steps from the Indian Ocean, Alila Seminyak epitomizes what laid-back luxury looks and feels like. This stunning beachfront resort radiates sophistication, vibrancy and vivacity, offering an indulgence of luxurious lifestyle facilities and refined spaces for relaxing and rejuvenating in impeccable style.

Infinity Pool

Beat the tropical heat with a dip in our infinity swimming pools, five in all, integrated throughout the grounds of the resort, including the Main Pool attached to Beach Bar, the Family Pool, and the Serene Pool.​

A Taste of the Sea

Balinese cuisine has long revolved around fresh catch, with fishermen heading out to sea on “jukung” or wooden canoes. Alila Seminyak honors this longstanding tradition with Seasalt and its menu of wild-caught and sustainably harvested seafood from local waters.

Sustainable Tourism

At Alila Seminyak, we respect and preserve the lifestyles and traditions of our local communities and endeavor to provide environmentally responsible experiences. The resort’s green initiatives range from sensitive property planning that works with the natural landscape to growing our own hydroponic garden and supporting local schools. As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. Learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

Sublime Beach Scene

Alila Seminyak occupies the quieter end of a sun-drenched beach that stretches 5 miles north from Kuta and Legian. Seminyak combines great beginner surfing, legendary sunsets and some of Bali’s most glamorous beach clubs, including Ku De Ta and Potato Head. Inland, emerald rice terraces and deep-forested mountains entice.

Cultural Immersion

Temple culture is a big part of Balinese life, and Alila Seminyak is near one of its most unique temples, Pura Petitenget. Witness festive anniversary celebrations on the Balinese calendar and take in traditional dance rehearsals at the Bale Agung pavilion across the temple. Afterward, visit the numerous galleries dotting Seminyak town to browse works by emerging and established artists.
