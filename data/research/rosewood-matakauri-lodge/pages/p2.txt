### Uncovering Local Flavours

The culinary philosophy of Rosewood Matakauri lies in celebrating the unique flavours in every dish, beginning with championing local ingredients, and establishing enduring relationships with handpicked suppliers.

-

**FROM GARDEN TO PLATE**

A curated garden brimming with rare produce inspires our dining experiences. Discover a world of flavour, grown just steps away.

-

**EXPRESSING A UNIQUE TERROIR**

Bees at Rosewood Matakauri thrive amidst native flora, creating distinctive honey that captures the essence of the land and enhances the resort’s cuisine.

-

**DOUBLE ROCK FARM**

Nestled in Kawarau Gorge, Double Rock Farm grows organic berries, quince, hazelnuts, and mulberries exclusively for Rosewood Matakauri's culinary delights.
