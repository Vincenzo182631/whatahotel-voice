The Peninsula Chicago Fitness Center item 2

Located on the 20th floor of The Peninsula Chicago, The Fitness Center provides the ultimate urban workout location with top-of-the-line equipment, weekly fitness classes, and access to private trainers. Enjoy both the main fitness area and adjacent yoga studio, flooded with natural light from the floor-to-ceiling windows with city views. The light-filled space provides ample room for the busiest of times while still maintaining an intimate, private feel.

This exclusive fitness haven is complete with three Peloton bikes, a Tonal machine, TechnoGym equipment, a full complement of free weights, and more. Don’t sweat if you didn’t pack your workout gear as The Fitness Center has it all covered.

The workout area only of the Fitness Center is open 24-hour per day for hotel guests and is accessible via a guestroom key.

The fitness class schedule is subject to change weekly. Please call the spa for the latest weekly class schedule.

_\*The Sun Terrace, Pool and Fitness Center are available to hotel guests, Spa guests enjoying two or more hours of services, and Spa Members._
