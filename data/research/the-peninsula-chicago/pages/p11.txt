# Celebrating 25 Years in Chicago

## A Legacy of Excellence, 25 Years and Counting

The Peninsula Chicago celebrates 25 years of luxury in the heart of Chicago on June 1, 2026.

We invite guests to share in this milestone anniversary with exclusive offers, guest experiences, community collaborations, and culinary events throughout the year. These offerings will reflect the hotel's enduring legacy of Far Eastern graciousness and Midwestern hospitality, celebrating both our heritage and role within the community.

## The Silver Suite Anniversary Offer

Throughout the anniversary year of 2026, reserve The Silver Suite Anniversary Offer to enjoy 25% savings on a second consecutive night in one of our well-appointed suites, including our most spacious Peninsula Suite.

## The Silver Set Anniversary Cocktails

Celebrate The Peninsula Chicago’s 25th anniversary with The Silver Set Anniversary Cocktails at Z Bar. Thoughtfully crafted, each cocktail reflects the artistry of mixology and the spirit of celebration at The Peninsula Chicago. A portion of proceeds will be donated to the Tales of the Cocktail Foundation, supporting the professional development of an emerging bartender, with a scholarship to attend Tales of the Cocktail® 2026.
