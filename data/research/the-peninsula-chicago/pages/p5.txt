# Shanghai Terrace

Shanghai Terrace presents a contemporary approach to Chinese dining under the direction of Chef de Cuisine Elmo Han. The menu features beloved and traditional Cantonese dishes alongside creative interpretations of the classics, including an acclaimed dim sum program. The space feels intimate, with the option of a semi-private dining room and a seasonal terrace that extends the experience beyond the dining room.

## Highlights

#### The Terrace

During the warmer Chicago months, Shanghai Terrace opens its doors to The Terrace for lunch, cocktails and dinner al fresco. Adjacent to Shanghai Terrace and located on the 4th floor of The Peninsula Chicago, guests savor warm summer days amid spectacular city views.

#### Design Details

The elegant nouveau interior at Shanghai Terrace blends wood chevrons, elegant furniture and walls adorned with Chinese silkscreen paintings. The piece de resistance is the restaurant’s spacious terrace, which at night is lit by the glittering lights of the Chicago skyline. On finer evenings, Chicagoans and visitors alike flock here to indulge in an array of exquisite cocktails and innovative small bites.

#### Private Dining

For corporate events, wedding engagements or private celebrations, look no further than Shanghai Terrace, one of Chicago’s most acclaimed restaurants. Shanghai Terrace’s private dining room seats up to 30 guests in semi-privacy with an Asian-inspired menu. For reservations and more details on private dining in Shanghai Terrace, please call +1 (312) 573 6695 or e-mail diningPCH@peninsula.com.
