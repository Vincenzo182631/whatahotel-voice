Why book?
The Peninsula Chicago is the Midwest representation of one of the globe’s leading luxury hotel brands, and you’ll find it all here: bespoke design; ample, world-class dining; a sought-after spa; and personalized, unflinching hospitality.
Set the scene
The Peninsula Chicago has helped set the pace for luxury accommodations in the city’s downtown district for decades, and its central location, standout service, and showstopping accommodations, restaurants, and common areas draw new and returning guests each year. Employees reflect back that loyalty—40% of the team have worked at the property for 10 years or longer. Of a big brand in a big city, staff retention can say much about a company's ethos and environment—and here, the guest feels it. Couples celebrating special occasions, business travelers seeking a top-notch stay, and families looking for a memorable getaway—especially around the holidays—comprise this scene’s passersby.
The backstory
Owned and operated by luxury group The Hongkong and Shanghai Hotels, Limited, The Peninsula brand has 12 properties across Asia, Europe, and the United States. For every opening, the group prioritizes premier locations and architecture. The Peninsula Chicago, a 20-story Art Deco building steps from Michigan Avenue, is no exception. The property opened in 2001, making this year its 25
th
anniversary. Keep an eye out for celebration plans ahead, including special room packages, a signature cocktail, new spa offerings, and projects with local artists and makers (including this spring’s afternoon tea collaboration with jeweler Sidney Garber).
The rooms
There are 339 rooms and 82 suites, each one outfitted with bespoke design and thoughtful amenities. Rooms begin at 531 square feet, making them some of the largest accommodations in the city. Neutral tones are balanced by locally inspired, colorful touches: A decorative wall panel of the chrysanthemum, Chicago’s official flower and one of Asia’s noble flowers; signature blue on curtains and rimming custom Fili D’oro linens, a hue inspired by Lake Michigan and the Chicago River. Tablet technology allows guests to customize their stay, with touchscreen lighting and temperature control, along with menus for room service and spa treatments on the 20
th
floor. Find comforts like bathrobes and slippers near the bathroom, stocked with signature scent bath products. Peninsula Hotels are known for gracious hospitality, and the “Peninsula Time” program is one clear example. With 24 hours' notice, guests can coordinate check-in as early as 6 a.m. and check-out as late as 10 p.m., adding unprecedented flexibility to the five-star stay.
Food and drink
There are multiple dining outlets here, and each one is worth a visit. Pierrot Gourmet, the street-level European café, is a favorite among locals and visitors for its tartines, flatbreads, and impeccable pastries, all enjoyed in cozy indoor seating or on its seasonal sidewalk patio. Book well in advance for dinner at Shanghai Terrace. The restaurant fills nightly for its contemporary takes on Shanghainese, Cantonese, and Sichuanese cuisine from chef de cuisine Elmo Han. The Peking Duck is the house signature, but try plenty of dim sum first (e.g., spicy beef potstickers or black truffle lobster and chicken dumplings).
On the 6th floor, guests will find Z Bar, a sleek rooftop venue with an imaginative, globally inspired cocktail list and several zero-proof options. Menu items like sliders, daikon fries, and warm, billowy beignets are expertly crafted and serve as perfect pairings. And off the main reception is The Lobby, a magnificent, sun-drenched room with floor-to-ceiling windows that overlook the Terrace and, in winter months, its onsite ice rink. Visit for breakfast and lunch for contemporary American fare, or for seasonal afternoon tea—one of the hotel’s biggest draws. Proceeds from afternoon tea throughout the year benefit local charities, making the experience even sweeter.
The spa
The property emphasizes wellness, dedicating floors 19 and 20 to a full-service spa, including a steam room within the locker rooms, and a state-of-the-art fitness center with a studio for classes throughout the week. The spa here is a destination in and of itself. Facial and body treatments incorporate Asian-inspired techniques and luxury product lines. Book with any of the seasoned team of massage therapists, estheticians, and nail technicians, some of whom have been with the spa for more than a decade. The Hot Stone Massage is the ultimate R&R, using warm volcanic stones to calm and soothe aching muscles in a full-body, 60- or 90-minute massage. Post-treatment, spend time in the eucalyptus steam room, indoor pool, or relaxation lounge (complete with plush blankets and housemade snacks).
The neighborhood/area
Situated just off The Magnificent Mile, the hotel is within walking distance of some of the city’s iconic attractions and best blocks of shopping, dining, museums, and entertainment.
The service
The team here demonstrates consistently warm and attentive hospitality, and makes stays particularly memorable with their personal touches throughout. The concierge team is known for their established relationships and insights on the town, helping travelers with everything from transportation to hard-to-snag theater tickets.
For families
Families are most welcome here. Come around the holidays to enjoy several family-friendly activations, including afternoon tea, visits with Santa and Mrs. Claus, a candy house in the lobby, and, on the outdoor terrace, an ice-skating rink and chalet with snacks and hot drinks. Skates are $20 to rent for adults and $10 for children, with all proceeds benefitting Ann & Robert H. Lurie Children’s Hospital of Chicago and Hephzibah Children’s Association.
Eco effort
The hotel has received EarthCheck Gold Certification for its efforts in sustainability. All of the restaurants provide compostable takeaway containers, in-room amenities feature natural ingredients and aluminum packaging, and guests can refill water bottles with the Flow Water station in the wellness center (saving upwards of 24,000 plastic bottles annually). They also donate their gently used soaps, shampoos, and lotions to Clean the World, an organization that distributes these amenities to in-need communities around the globe.
Accessibility
Elevators provide access to every floor, and accessible rooms are available, with accommodations such as wheelchair accessible routes, roll-in showers, and a visual alarm for guests who are deaf or hard of hearing.
Anything left to mention?
The fitness center features top workout equipment, including three Peloton bikes, a Tonal machine, and TechnoGym equipment. And on the lobby level, check out the on-site retailer Krigler, a luxury perfume house since 1879.
