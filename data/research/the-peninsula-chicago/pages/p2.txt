Pet-Friendly Accommodations

# Memorable Stays with Your Four-Legged Friend

The Peninsula Chicago is pleased to welcome your entire family to stay at the hotel – including your furry best friends, who are in for a real treat. From special welcome amenities and pet walking services to a doggie and kitty room service menu, your pets will be treated like the VIPs they truly are.

Special pet amenities and services include:

- Welcome amenity
- Plush pet bed
- Peninsula bottled water and food bowls
- In-room dining pet menu
- Peninsula Page dog walking service
- Doggie in-room massage from The Peninsula Spa

In addition, The Peninsula Chicago has access to 24-hour emergency veterinary care.

Pet amenities and services may incur additional fees. A pet waiver form must be signed upon arrival. A pet fee per pet per stay will be charged as follows:

- $350 plus tax for pets 30 pounds and under
- $500 plus tax for pets between 31 and 50 pounds

### Inquiries

For inquiries, please contact the Reservations Department at 312-337-2888 or ReservationPCH@peninsula.com.
