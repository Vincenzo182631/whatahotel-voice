The Peninsula Chicago Indoor Swimming Pool item 2

Floor-to-ceiling windows with sweeping views over Chicago’s breathtaking skyline surround The Peninsula’s indoor half-Olympic size swimming pool, complete with an indoor hot tub and outdoor Sun Terrace.

Recommended family hours are from 9:00 am – 1:00 pm and 3:00 pm – 7:00 pm.

Length: 25 m / 83 ft

Depth: 1.07 m to 1.22 m / 3.5 ft to 4 ft

Average Temperature Range: 82° to 84° Fahrenheit / 27° to 29° Celsius
