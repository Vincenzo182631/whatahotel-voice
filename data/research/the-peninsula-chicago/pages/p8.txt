# Sun Terrace

Available to hotel and Spa guests\*, the Sun Terrace is an outdoor oasis

Available to hotel guests and day spa guests who book a minimum of two hours of treatments, the stunning Sun Terrace is a relaxing escape to enjoy fresh air in the warmer months. Open seasonally and located on the 19th floor adjacent to The Pool, guests enjoy comfortable loungers, complimentary water, chilled towels and the beautiful Chicago skyline. The Sun Terrace may also be booked for private events.
