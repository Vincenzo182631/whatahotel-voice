## Experience Far Eastern graciousness where it meets Midwestern hospitality

Located in the heart of the Magnificent Mile neighborhood, The Peninsula Chicago brings traditional Asian service to a city already renowned for the warmth of its hospitality. The hotel has proudly received a Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star rating each year since its opening and was named to La Liste’s ‘World’s Best Hotels for 2026’, tying for #1 Hotel in the World.

## Inspired dining

## Shanghai Terrace

Shanghai Terrace offers Chinese cuisine highlighting contemporary interpretations of Shanghainese, Cantonese, and Sichuanese classics. With Chef de Cuisine Elmo Han at the helm, the menu encompasses a blend of traditional dishes and creative interpretations of classics, making it known as some of the best Chinese cuisine found in Chicago.
