Chicago's Urban Oasis The Peninsula Spa & Wellness Center item 2

Revitalize your mind, body, and spirit with The Peninsula Spa & Wellness Center’s extensive menu of both modern and timeless treatments inspired by Ayurvedic and Asian philosophies, ranging from the enriching, results-driven Subtle Energies Blissful Marma Massage to the regenerative Biologique Recherche Second Skin Facial.

Newly revitalized in 2025, The Spa is complemented by amenities including an indoor half-Olympic length swimming pool surrounded by floor-to-ceiling windows offering stunning views of the city, a relaxation lounge with a fireplace, a fully equipped Fitness Center, and a yoga studio.

\*The locker room, steam room, and relaxation lounges are available for Spa guests enjoying one hour or more of body services. The Sun Terrace, Fitness Center, and pool are available to Spa guests enjoying two or more hours of services, all hotel guests, and Spa Members. Please note, the relaxation areas are coed.

## Additional Information

#### ETIQUETTE

Our Spa environment is one of relaxation and tranquility. Please respect all Spa guests’ right to privacy and serenity. The Spa will provide towels, slippers and other amenities required during your visit and we invite hotel guests to arrive wearing the robe and slippers provided in their guest room. Please note, The Spa is a mobile phone, pager, and smoke-free zone.

#### ARRIVAL TIME

Guests are encouraged to arrive 30 minutes prior to the appointment time. Guests enjoying one hour or more of body services have access to the locker room amenities, steam room, and relaxation lounges. Guests enjoying two hours or more of services have additional access to the Sun Terrace, Fitness Center, and pool. Please understand that late arrivals may result in a reduction of treatment time.

#### SCHEDULING AN APPOINTMENT

To schedule an appointment for the perfect treatment, please speak with our specialist Spa reception team who will guide you to the right choice. Hotel guests can press the SPA button on the room telephone. From outside the hotel, please call +1 (312) 573 6860. To ensure the preferred time and service are available, we recommend advanced booking.

#### HEALTH CONDITIONS

Kindly advise any health conditions, allergies or injuries which could affect your service when making your Spa reservation. We have specially designed treatments for expectant women and nursing mothers. Please allow our Spa team to guide you in selecting which treatments are most suitable for your during this special time.

#### AGE REQUIREMENT

A minimum age of 18 is required for access within the Spa and for treatments, with the exception of facials (minimum age of 16 is required) and nail services (no age minimum, but children under the age of 13 must be accompanied by an adult).

#### HOMECARE

To continue your Spa regime and experience at home, all products used in the treatments are available for purchase from the Spa Reception.

#### GIFT CERTIFICATES

Gift certificates are available – an ideal present for someone special. Please contact the Spa for details or you may purchase online.

#### FACILITIES AND AMENITIES

The locker room, steam room, and relaxation lounges are available for Spa guests enjoying one hour or more of body services. The Sun Terrace, Fitness Center, and pool are available to Spa guests enjoying two or more hours of services, all hotel guests, and Spa Members.

#### PRICES

All prices are quoted in US dollars and are subject to change without prior notice.

#### CANCELLATION

Cancellation within 24 hours will incur a 100% charge. Parties of four or more will incur a 100% charge if cancelled within 48 hours.
