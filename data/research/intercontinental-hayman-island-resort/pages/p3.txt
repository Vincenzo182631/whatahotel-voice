# InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier ReefDining

Five restaurants and bars offer an eclectic array of gastronomic delights and extraordinary local flavour. Each dining experience is enriched by an exquisite setting of tropical rainforests or crystal waters of the Coral Sea.

**In-room dining hours:** 6:00 AM - 11:00 PM

### Signature Dining Experiences

Create memorable moments with our private and signature dining experiences. Indulge in Under the Stars dining on Hayman Beach, secluded gourmet picnics in idyllic beach locations, private poolside cabana lunches, and more.
