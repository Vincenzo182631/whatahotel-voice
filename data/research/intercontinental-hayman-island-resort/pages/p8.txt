# InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier ReefDestination

With 74 beautiful islands to navigate, the Whitsundays offers abundant natural attractions including one of the seven wonders of the world, the Great Barrier Reef. Hayman Island is nestled at the northernmost point of Australia’s Whitsunday archipelago.

## Things to do

### Whitehaven Beach

Whitehaven Beach, Whitsundays, QLD 4802

Whitehaven Beach is a pristine 7km beach in the Whitsundays regarded one of the world's most beautiful beaches with crystal-white silica sand and uninterrupted views out to the Coral Sea. Once you arrive at the beach, immerse in your iconic surrounds with complimentary activities including stand-up paddle boarding or beach cricket, go snorkelling or swimming or simply admire the beauty of this pristine stretch of sand.

Learn more

### Langford Island

Langford Island, Whitsundays, QLD 4802

Langford Island is an ideal location for snorkelling, picnics and exploring. Travel by resort speedboat to Langford Island and spend a couple of hours exploring this natural wonder. A sand spit appears from the water as the tide goes out where you can easily access the fringing coral reefs to spot the abundant fish life and maybe the occasional green sea turtle. Organise a picnic hamper from the resort (charges apply) and bring it with you to the island to top off your experience.

### Heart Reef, Great Barrier Reef

Heart Reef is located in the Great Barrier Reef and is a stunning composition of coral that has naturally formed into the shape of a heart. Positioned in Hardy Reef, Heart Reef is best experienced from the air by helicopter through our partner, Helireef, as visitors are unable to snorkel or dive there due it's protected status. Perfect for honeymooners, take a scenic tour to see the icon in all its glory.
