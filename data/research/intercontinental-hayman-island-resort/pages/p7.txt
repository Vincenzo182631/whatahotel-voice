### 6 Nights Beach Front Villa

4 days ago

An Excellent Stay at Hayman Island – With Some Maintenance Needed

We stayed for six nights in a beachfront villa, and overall, we had a fantastic experience. The villa was absolutely amazing, essentially offering your own private retreat with direct access to the beach and two reserved beach chairs directly out front. Despite being located in the centre of the resort, the villa and surrounding beach area felt wonderfully private.

The design of the villa was outstanding, particularly the large central pool within the room, which was a real highlight of our stay. The location, layout and privacy were exceptional. However, the villa would benefit from some maintenance. There was mould on the pool mattress, and some of the chairs and furniture were looking tired and worn. These issues detracted slightly from what was otherwise a beautiful and very special room.

The resort itself is stunning, and the transfer service on the Hayman catamaran was excellent. From the moment we boarded, our luggage was taken care of and the check-in process was seamless. Although our flight was delayed and we arrived late, the hotel was fully aware of our arrival time. We were even able to order room service through the catering team on the boat before arriving on the island, with our meal delivered to the room just as we checked in. This was an excellent example of thoughtful organisation and outstanding service.

The staff deserve particular recognition. A special mention goes to Lara, who looked after us on the catamaran on both the outbound and return journeys. She was excellent throughout. I would also like to acknowledge her partner, whose name I unfortunately cannot recall, who assisted us at the check-in and check-out desk. He was equally attentive, friendly and professional. All the staff we encountered throughout the resort were welcoming and helpful, and the level of service was consistently impressive.

We dined at Pacific and Amici, and both restaurants were excellent. The food was very good, the service was outstanding, and the prices were reasonable for a luxury island resort. We also enjoyed several nights of room service on our balcony overlooking the beach, which was a wonderful experience.

Breakfast was a buffet with a good selection of food, although it could feel a little crowded at times and was perhaps the least impressive aspect of the dining experience.

The main area for improvement across the resort is maintenance. In addition to the issues in our villa, several poolside loungers had mouldy or torn covers, some furniture was looking old and heavily used, and there were areas that would benefit from a thorough clean and general refurbishment. For a resort of this standard, these details are noticeable and are an opportunity to bring the facilities back up to the level of the location, design and service.

Overall, we had an excellent stay and would happily return to Hayman Island. The beachfront villa was exceptional, the setting was beautiful, the transfers were seamless and the staff were outstanding. With some additional attention to cleaning, upkeep and maintenance of the furniture and facilities, the resort could be even better.

I would highly recommend Hayman Island, particularly the beachfront villas, and hope the resort continues to invest in maintaining the beautiful facilities that make it such a special destination.

### Very disappointing stay.

12 days ago

It is hard to articulate just how disappointing our stay at InterContinental Hayman Island was.

We are an adventurous family who loves exploring destinations like the Great Barrier Reef, and we were genuinely excited about this trip. Unfortunately, the resort fell far short of our expectations.

First, the range of activities is incredibly limited for a resort in such an extraordinary location. There isn't even a boat available to take guests out snorkelling. Instead, you are dependent on extremely overpriced tours with very limited availability. We felt almost trapped at the resort, with very few opportunities to explore the natural beauty surrounding us.

The property itself also felt tired and outdated. It gave us the impression of a resort that must have been really nice 30 or 40 years ago but has since lost much of its appeal.

The service was equally disappointing. One evening, we ordered room service for our family of five. Despite ordering five main courses, we received only three sets of cutlery. We immediately asked the team member to bring two more, explaining that we had young, hungry children waiting to eat. We had to call room service twice, and the missing cutlery only arrived 30 minutes later, by which time the food was cold. I asked to speak with a manager, but no one ever got back to me. I had to bring up the incident the next day at reception to have any measure taken. This lack of basic service and follow-up is simply unacceptable for a hotel positioning itself as a luxury resort.

We also had a frustrating experience trying to hike the trail to Blue Pearl Beach. None of the staff seemed to know anything about the trail, how to access it, or what to expect. It genuinely felt as though nobody had ever walked it themselves. For a resort surrounded by such incredible nature, this lack of knowledge was astonishing.

Overall, our experience was deeply frustrating. We came hoping to discover and enjoy one of the most beautiful parts of Australia, but instead found ourselves at an ageing resort with limited activities and very disappointing service.

I am not looking for compensation, a refund, or anything in return. I simply wanted to share candid feedback from a family that loves adventure, nature, and exploring new destinations.

It is a shame, because Hayman Island is in an extraordinary location with enormous potential. But based on our experience, we would never return to InterContinental Hayman Island, nor would we recommend it to other families looking to explore the Great Barrier Reef.
