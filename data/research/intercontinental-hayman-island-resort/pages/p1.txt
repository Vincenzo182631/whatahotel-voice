### Helireef experience

Elevate your experience in the Whitsunday Islands and view the magnificent surrounds of the Great Barrier Reef by air. InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef offers bespoke helicopter adventures that take you high above the world-famous Heart Reef, and exhilarating helicopter tours over Whitehaven Beach and beyond.

Learn more

### Ultra Exclusive Hayman Residences

Nestled in the exclusive Hayman Island hillside is an exquisite collection of four private residences that redefine secluded opulence. Designed by multi-award-winning Australian Architect Kerry Hill, all Residences offer panoramic views across The Whitsundays. Perfect as a luxury family stay or groups of couples or friends holidaying together.

Learn more

### Stunning Weddings Destination

Set the scene with InterContinental Hayman Island Great Barrier Reef’s suite of extraordinary venues, from bridal moments inside the Three-Bedroom Hayman Beach House to barefoot vows exchanged on Hayman Beach’s pristine sands, whimsical Formal Garden receptions set under the stars, and show-stopping honeymoon moments and beyond.

Learn more

### Hayman Spa Immersion

Leave the world behind as you enter a timeless, serene haven in the Resort’s luxurious wellness destination. Guests will retreat into an intimate, indulgent environment with all-new treatment rooms, a couples' room, sauna and steam facilities nestled in the natural surrounds of Hayman Island.

### Poolside Cabanas

Nearby the Infinity Pool and Hayman Pool, the Poolside Cabanas are an ideal private abode with contemporary comfort, ocean views, and sundeck space. Rediscover relaxation for the day and indulge in Pan-Asian delights from Bam Bam or California-Mexican cuisine from Aqua.

## FAQs for InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef

Does InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef have a pool?

InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef features two pools for guest enjoyment, an Infinity Pool overlooking the Coral Sea and the iconic Hayman Pool which is the largest saltwater pool in the southern hemisphere.

Pool information

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef has 4 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef?

Breakfast at InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef is available from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef?

Check-in at InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 10:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef is conveniently located near Hamilton Island - Great Barrier Reef Airport (HTI), approximately 21.75 miles away (35 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef?

InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Whitehaven Beach - 18.77 miles away (30.2 kilometers)
- Langford Island - 2.3 miles away (3.7 kilometers)
- Heart Reef, Great Barrier Reef - 29.83 miles away (48 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Hayman Great Barrier Reef puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
