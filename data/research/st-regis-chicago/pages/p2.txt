# Stay in our hotel suites in Chicago

## Accommodations

Imbued with a sense of modern glamour and classic sophistication, transcend into The St. Regis Chicago’s 159 impeccable guest rooms and 33 luxurious suites. Take in the sights of the Windy City through floor-to-ceiling windows: immersive views from spacious suites and high-floor hotel rooms in Chicago reveal the most revered landmarks in the downtown area. The St. Regis Chicago boasts some of the largest hotel accommodations in the city, and our luxury accommodation in Chicago is adorned with exquisite furnishings and bespoke amenities, ensuring a comfortable and indulgent stay. Sink into plush bedding and unwind in your private marble bathroom with rainfall showers and luxurious bath amenities, savoring moments of relaxation in your own personal oasis.

Our Signature Commitment to Excellence

From Packing Service to Garment Pressing, the signature St. Regis Butler Service ensures each guest’s unique preferences are understood and realized, with meticulous attention to detail. The St. Regis Butler Service is accessible at any hour via guest phone, email, or text message.

## Explore Downtown Chicago

Treat yourself to a rewarding stay at The St. Regis Chicago located where the Chicago River meets Lake Michigan in downtown Chicago. Explore the city’s most revered downtown landmarks including the Magnificent Mile, Navy Pier, Grant Park, and the Chicago Riverwalk are all accessible from our hotel.

### The Two-Bedroom Presidential Suite

Indulge in the epitome of luxury and sophistication with our Presidential Suite at The St. Regis Chicago. Offering an unparalleled level of elegance and opulence, meticulously designed to exceed the expectations of the most discerning guests. Step into a 4,600 sq. foot jewel box enveloped in stunning views of downtown Chicago and Lake Michigan, serving as your backdrop. The spacious living area is adorned with lavish furnishings, exquisite artwork, and bespoke décor, creating an ambiance of timeless elegance and refinement. Partake in ultimate relaxation in the marble-clad principal bathroom overlooking Navy Pier, featuring an oversized, effervescent soaking tub, a separate rainfall shower, and premium bath amenities. Rejuvenate in your own clandestine personal fitness studio or unwind in the expansive living area, complete with a dining table for entertaining guests or enjoying intimate meals prepared in your private kitchen and bookended by your own exclusive St. Regis Butler.

## John Jacob Astor Suite

Named after the founding father of the St. Regis, the exquisite John Jacob Astor Suite offers premier, uncompromising views of Lake Michigan and Navy Pier from its sprawling living quarters. An unparalleled vantage point is enhanced by refined residential-style design crafted for the modern connoisseur, plus exclusive St. Regis Butler Service for your accommodations, promising a luxurious retreat like no other, where every detail is curated to exceed your expectations and create unforgettable moments.

## Caroline Astor Suite

This luxury suite in downtown Chicago is inspired by John’s mother, the tirelessly inventive Gilded Age doyenne, Caroline Astor. This inviting waterfront respite evokes the same elegant intimacy of the lavish gatherings “Mrs. Astor” was notorious for hosting.

## St. Regis Suite

Well-appointed with an atmosphere of sophistication, our hotel rooms near Grant Park, Chicago inspire a harmonious balance of luxury, technology, and functionality. With picturesque views of downtown and its revered waters, this exceptional suite truly captures the unbridled spirit of the Windy City.

## Metropolitan Suite

Quintessential and timeless, immerse in unique views of Lakeshore East Park and the iconic coastal Lake Shore Drive in this stylish and meticulously crafted hotel suite in Chicago. Enjoy contemporary comforts and a dazzling array of distinctive features.
