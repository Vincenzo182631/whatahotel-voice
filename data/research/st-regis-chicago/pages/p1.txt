# Welcome to The St. Regis Chicago

## Stay at our luxury hotel in Chicago

Our luxury hotel in Chicago downtown is an innovative, architectural marvel crystallized in tradition. Situated at a premier location where the Chicago River meets Lake Michigan, The St. Regis Chicago's impeccably appointed guest rooms and suites reveal the city's most revered downtown landmarks, including the Magnificent Mile, Navy Pier, Grant Park, and Millennium Park. Uncover meticulously inspired and thoughtfully considered spaces with the timeless Astor Ballroom and Executive Function rooms, where collaborative gatherings and celebratory moments come to life. Channeling the city's unbridled energy, immerse in this Chicago luxury hotel's expansive wellness floor and its distinctive dining destinations, from the sanctuary of The St. Regis Spa to sprawling riverfront terrace views at Miru and open-hearth, wood fire comforts at Tre Dita. Bookended by the signature St. Regis Butler Service for suites with its treasured rituals, The St. Regis Chicago presents a storied history steeped in modern elegance.

### The Signature St. Regis Traditions

Founded by John Jacob Astor IV, the St. Regis brand is renowned for its tradition of innovation, splendor, and impeccable service since 1904. Today, we celebrate the legend of the man who founded the St. Regis through treasured rituals that take place at every St. Regis around the world.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Chicago?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Chicago is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Chicago allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Chicago. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Chicago?

On-site valet parking is available at The St. Regis Chicago. The daily valet fee is $89.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Chicago have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Chicago has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Chicago booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Chicago?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Chicago is Chicago Midway International Airport (MDW). MDW is located approximately 13.2 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Chicago have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Chicago does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
