### Signature Rituals at the St. Regis Chicago

In 1904, John Jacob Astor IV founded the St. Regis brand and redefined luxurious living. Today, every St. Regis hotel celebrates daily rituals; each is a modern articulation of a timeless tradition, inviting guests to experience not only the St. Regis legacy, but also the story of each hotel. The St. Regis Chicago will offer each of these rituals, adapted ever so slightly to pay tribute to the heritage...See More of the Windy City, crafting each time honored experience into an elevated moment.See Less

### The Evening Ritual

Dating back to the battles of Napoleon Bonaparte, who famously said “Champagne: in victory, one deserves it; in defeat, one needs it”, the art of champagne sabrage is a glittering ceremony which takes place daily at sunset - bidding farewell to the working day and a welcome to the evening.

Learn More

### The Bloody Mary

Invented by Fernand Petiot, at The St. Regis New York in 1934, the Bloody Mary remains the signature cocktail of the St. Regis. Indulge in The 1871 at The St. Regis Chicago, a tribute to the Great Chicago Fire, which incorporates a local Rye Whiskey. The immersive concoction is served in a smoking glass finished with a rim of smoked salt, while the elongated height of its vessel celebrates The St. Regis Chicago.

Learn More

### The St. Regis Butler Service

When John Jacob Astor IV first conceived the St. Regis New York, his vision was to create "the finest hotel in the world.” As such, the St. Regis butler was born out of a mandate to fulfill every guest’s desire as if it were second nature. A signature suite offering, the essence of the storied service is discreet, personalized, anticipatory attention that begins before your stay and extends after you leave.

Learn More

### The St. Regis Spa \| Award-Winning Wellness

Awarded the Forbes Travel Guide 5-Star Spa distinction, The St. Regis Spa evokes a 12,000 sq ft urban oasis of calming sensory experiences. Sip the finest champagne at the Celebration Bar then transcend and connect in the Iridium Lounge, all before indulging in an exquisite spa treatment. The Celebration Suite offers a bespoke gathering space for couples, families, and friends to enjoy treatment time together. Overlooking the river, our spa hotel in downtown Chicago features a fitness facility with a heated indoor swimming pool, outdoor pool sundeck, yoga studio, and two private fitness rooms.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Chicago have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Chicago offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Chicago have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Chicago has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Chicago have an on-site spa?

Yes, The St. Regis Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Chicago. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Chicago?
