Exclusive, Intimate, and Scenic Weddings with Azur Lodge

At Azur, we believe in creating unforgettable experiences for couples on their special day. Nestled in the stunning landscapes of Queenstown, our boutique lodge offers an intimate and luxurious setting, with breathtaking views of Lake Wakatipu and the surrounding mountains. Whether you’re planning an intimate elopement or a grand celebration, our wedding package is designed to make your day as beautiful and memorable as possible.

Our Wedding Package Includes:

1\. Exclusive Accommodation for You and Your Guests

Stay in one of our 9 private villas, each offering panoramic views of Lake Wakatipu and the surrounding mountains. Collectively, our villas comfortably accommodate up to 21 people for overnight stays. The perfect base for close family and friends to relax and prepare for the wedding day.

For larger events, please inquire for details.

2\. Intimate Elopements & Weddings

Whether you’re eloping or hosting a small celebration, Azur Lodge offers a serene and romantic atmosphere for your ceremony.

3\. Helicopter Wedding Experience

Elevate your wedding day with a spectacular helicopter ride to one of Fiordland’s iconic mountaintops. Say “I do” amidst snow-capped peaks or capture stunning photos with a dramatic alpine backdrop—an unforgettable way to mark the occasion.

4\. Photography & Videography Services

We can help organise a professional photographer and videographer to capture every moment of your wedding day, from your first look to your vows and beyond.

5\. Beauty and Glamour Services

We can help organise experienced hair and makeup artists for you and your bridal party. Our professionals will ensure you look and feel flawless, whether you’re going for a natural style or something more glamorous to match the grandeur of your day.

6\. Catering for Your Celebration

In-house catering is available for up to 21 people, providing gourmet dining options for your reception. Whether it’s an elegant dinner or light canapés, our team can create a custom menu to suit your taste.

7\. Personalisation and Special Requests

While we do not provide full wedding planning services, we can assist with recommending and organising additional services such as florists, decorators, or any special touches to make your day complete.

8\. Romantic Post-Wedding Dinner

To end your day on a high note, enjoy a private, intimate dinner just for the two of you. Dine under the stars on your private villa’s deck or by the fire. An experience that will allow you to reflect on the magic of the day in complete privacy and comfort. If you prefer, we can also assist in organising a reservation at one of Queenstown’s finest restaurants, ensuring a memorable dining experience for your special evening.

Additional Options Available:

Extended Accommodation: If you wish to extend your stay or host a larger group, we offer additional accommodation options.

Live Music & Entertainment: We can help arrange for a local musician or band to provide music during your ceremony and reception.

Spa and Wellness Package: Treat yourself and your guests to a relaxing spa experience before or after the wedding day with our in-house spa services.

For more information or to book your dream wedding, please contact us today at stay@azur.co.nz

# Terms and Conditions

## Minimum stay

Two night minimum

## Deposit and Cancellation Policy

A deposit equal to one nights accommodation is required in order to secure one of our 9 villas. Your deposit will be manually processed within 24 hrs of making the reservation and a confirmation sent to you via email. In the unfortunate event that you should need to cancel or modify your trip, please accept our cancellation policy:

- Cancellation within 30 days of arrival results in one nights accommodation fee
- Cancellation within 14 days of arrival results in a 100% charge of the total cost of your stay

Group bookings of 3+ villas or more:

- Cancellation within 45 days of arrival results in one nights accommodation fee per villa
- Cancellation within 30 days prior to arrival results in 100% charge of the total of the booking

## Arrival and Departure

At Azur we pride ourselves on being adaptable. Although the normal check-in and check-out times are 3:00 PM and 11:00 AM respectively, we will do our upmost to accommodate your requests. Should we not be able to provide an early check-in or late check-out, we will ensure that you are comfortable in our main lodge area.

## Occupancy

Our luxury accommodation consists of nine one-bedroom villas and can be made up of either one king or twin beds. One extra bed can be accommodated upon your request. Maximum occupancy per villa is strictly 3 people. Extra person surcharge (13 years and over) is $475 per night. Children under 13 years old are not permitted at Azur Lodge.

## Children

Children strictly 13 years old and above are welcome at Azur Lodge. They will be treated as a third guest at an extra charge of $475 per night. Upon your advanced request, we will arrange an extra bed.

## Non-smoking

We have a non-smoking policy at Azur that applies to the main lodge and all private villas. Smoking is allowed on the outdoor sundecks.

## Visitors

Should you wish to visit Azur in consideration of an upcoming stay, we do ask if you could please contact us directly to make an appointment.
