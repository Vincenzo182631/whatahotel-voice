Maori legend warns that a giant demon's beating heart lies deep beneath the huge expanse that is Lake Wakatipu and from the spectacular vantage point of Azur Lodge, the myth seems but a breathe away. Commanding uninterrupted views of this ancient lake and the daunting, snow-swept Remarkables mountain range, the lodge and its 9 villas sit on steep hillside 3kms west of
Queenstown, in New Zealand's wild South Island. The region is an adrenalin-sport nexus offering everything from parabungy to extreme white-water surfing and Azur is perfectly positioned as a base for adventures. But once inside there is no reason to raise a finger, much less your pulse-rate. Each of the airy, stand-alone, stone and silver beechwood villas is an oasis of luxurious privacy, with under floor heating, super-king beds, flaming faux-log fires and an ensuite where the spa sits against fully opening windows to create the illusion of bathing in the open air. The tiny touches are cute (essential oils, spiced apple lip salve, apricot biscotti) and the larger ones - including gourmet canapés in the snug comfort of the lodge - well-thought through. Unexpected hitches (the promised villa wireless Internet coverage is actually weather dependent) are dispatched with speed, and the service so unobtrusively personalized that there is no excuse to do anything but relax and drink the legends in.
