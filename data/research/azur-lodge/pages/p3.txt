## Queenstown

“I once heard that the name Queenstown came about by the gold prospectors, who were captivated by the beauty of the surrounding mountains, lakes and rivers, they said it was “a town fit for a Queen”. As a New Zealander who now lives abroad, whenever I come home to Queenstown, I am in awe – it is truly the most spectacular place on earth for me.”

\- Joanne Ross, Founder

## Proximity

Azur is in close proximity to Queenstown downtown, Coronet Peak & Remarkables ski fields, Shotover River, Glenorchy, Arrowtown and the winery areas.

## Flexibility & Freedom

If you are signed up for an activity, wish to go shopping, going to one of our local cafés for coffee or out for a meal, we will drop you off where you need to be. This is a complimentary service during certain times of the day within the Queenstown downtown area, so you can have flexibility and freedom during your stay. When you wish to return back to Azur, just call and we will come to collect you.

We also offer an evening transfer service, in our late model Range Rover, to ensure you travel in style, as well as airport transfers and those places little further afield like to Arrowtown and Jack's Point.   These services are not included within the nightly rate and charges apply.  Simply let us know where you want to go and we can provide any applicable charges.
