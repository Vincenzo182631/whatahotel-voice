## A REGAL _icon_ IN THE HEART OF CALGARY

Welcome to Calgary’s premier hotel – a timeless landmark in Canadian history. Fairmont Palliser has been welcoming guests for over 110 years, and is renowned for its luxurious accommodations, exceptional dining and impeccable service. Conveniently located in the heart of downtown Calgary, our 5-star luxury hotel is within easy reach of premium shopping, cultural venues, exciting events, and attractions like the Calgary Tower and Calgary Zoo.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Palliser located?

Fairmont Palliser is located at 133 9th Avenue SW, Calgary - Alberta T2P 2M3, Canada.

How can I get to Fairmont Palliser from the nearest airport?

The nearest airport is Calgary International Airport (YYC), located approximately 12 miles (18 km) from the hotel. Taxi rates between Calgary International Airport and Fairmont Palliser is approx. $40 each way. For travel around town, taxis are readily available at the front of the hotel.

Is parking available at Fairmont Palliser?

Valet Parking is available at the hotel's main entrance on 9th Avenue. Rates are also extended to dining and meeting patrons of the hotel. Impark Lot (self-parking) is available with access from 10th Avenue East, or West, or from 9th Avenue (East of hotel).

Is Fairmont Palliser pet-friendly?

Fairmont Palliser is a pet-friendly hotel and will accept a maximum of two pets (cats or dogs) that are "house trained" per room at a charge of $40/night plus tax. At time of check-in, the owner of the animal must sign a Pet Damage Waiver. Guide dogs are always welcome at no charge.

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Palliser?

Fairmont Palliser has four guest rooms that meet Access Level Two requirements. These rooms have safety grips, bars in shower, raised toilet seats, lowered peepholes, and wide doorways - there are also two wheelchairs available on request. Additional supplies are available upon request.
