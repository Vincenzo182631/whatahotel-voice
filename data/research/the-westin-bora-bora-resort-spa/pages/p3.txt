# Enjoy Food and Drinks Offered at the Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa

## Dining

With menus that feature fresh local flavors and ingredients, the dining choices at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa are designed to be the perfect complement to your surroundings. From the beginning...See More of each day to the end, you’re presented with a variety of vibrant settings and menus to choose from. Here, you’re assured of celebrating each day with new tastes, new dishes, and fulfilling favorites.See Less

Memorable Dining at Maere

## Made for Your Most Special Moments

This is where everything comes together—your special occasion, a special menu, and a special setting. Maere features a menu that our head chef creates anew every evening, with a distinct theme for each, from an exciting local fisherman’s catch, to Mediterranean mezze, to Italian delicacies, the influences and offerings are deliciously unique. Combined with an intimate ambiance, heightened by views of our stunning lagoon, you’ll find that Maere, our Bora Bora restaurant always rises to the occasion.

Spectacular Waterside Dining

### A View Worth Savoring

The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa provides a variety of picturesque waterfront dining experiences where you’ll want to linger over every sip and bite. Swim up at Te Ava for refreshing beverages and fresh, casual fare. Put your feet in the sand at Honu Tiki Beach bar and enjoy frozen drinks and flavorful bites. Or, find a favorite spot in front of Mount Otemanu at O’A Lounge Bar to sip expertly crafted...See More cocktails at our swim-up pool bar in Bora Bora. Each destination offers much more than what’s on the menu, they give you a chance to take in a view that will last with you foreverSee Less

Exclusive Overwater Dining at Manahere

## A Night Created Just for You

Imagine watching the sun set behind Mount Otemanu, casting fiery shades of orange and pink across the sky. Then, as darkness falls, the soft glow of candlelight begins to flicker across white linen tablecloths, joining the moonlight dancing on the soft waves of the lagoon that surrounds you. This is Manahere, our exclusive overwater dining venue for private events, intimate dinners, and romantic occasions. Available for anniversaries, proposals, VIP receptions, and other notable celebrations, the exceptional atmosphere here is complemented by a level of personalized service that makes every moment feel like it was created just for you.

Enjoy Westin Fresh Blends by the Juicery

## A Smile With Every Sip

To support the Eat Well program The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa has partnered with The Juicery. Available at Tipanier, Varavara, and Te Ava, Westin Fresh by The Juicery juice blends are a delicious and healthy addition to your day. Highlighting local fruits and vegetables, one sip and you’ll be smiling inside and out.
