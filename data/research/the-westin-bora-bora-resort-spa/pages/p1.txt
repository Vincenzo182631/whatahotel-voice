# Welcome to The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa

## Let Your Dream Come to Life at our Beach Resort in Bora Bora

The dream of Bora Bora comes alive like never before at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa. With Mount Otemanu serving as a backdrop, our completely renovated Bora Bora, Tahiti, beach resort is alive with...See More wondrous possibilities. Offering the most overwater bungalows on the island, some with private saltwater plunge pools, alongside spacious beachside villas, your dream luxury escape awaits. Dive into water sports at our private lagoon or unwind by the infinity pool. Enjoy captivating encounters with rescued and rehabbing turtles at our Eco Centre. Elevate your wellness at WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio and Heavenly Spa by Westin™. Exciting dining choices abound as well—Tipanier is our French bistro with a Polynesian twist, Te Ava offers fresh, energizing fare poolside, Maere is our signature restaurant where our head chef constantly surprises with culinary triumphs, and Vara Vara is a laid-back beachside option. Watch the sunset behind Mount Otemanu at Otemanu Bar. Your Bora Bora dream is now a vibrant reality.See Less

## A Resort Built on Responsibility

The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa promotes sustainability throughout every aspect of our property. Beginning with waste reduction that includes limiting the use of plastics, to water conservation, to the biodiversity conservation at our Eco Center, to local sourcing of ingredients for menus. Everything we do at our hotel is with an eye to ensuring the dream of Bora Bora is a lasting one.

Are drones allowed on the property?

No. To protect the privacy and peace of mind of our guests, the use of personal drones is strictly prohibited throughout the property.

Why are drones prohibited?

Our property is located within an airport flight path, which is subject to specific aviation safety regulations. This policy also helps ensure the privacy, comfort, and tranquility of all our guests.

Do you use drones on the property?

Yes, but only in a strictly controlled professional context. Drones may occasionally be operated by our team or authorized service providers for technical inspections, maintenance, or official communication purposes.

Do your drones record or film guests?

No. Any drone operations are conducted in full compliance with applicable regulations and with the utmost respect for our guests' privacy. Flights and image capture are carefully planned to avoid recording or disturbing guests.

Can I request special permission to fly my drone on the property?

No. Due to aviation safety regulations, our location within an airport flight path, and our commitment to protecting guests' privacy, no exceptions can be granted for personal drone use.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa?

The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa is Bora Bora Airport (Motu Mute Airport) (BOB). BOB is located approximately 9.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa have an airport shuttle to Bora Bora Airport (Motu Mute Airport) (BOB)?

Yes, The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa has an airport shuttle to Bora Bora Airport (Motu Mute Airport) (BOB).

Does The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
