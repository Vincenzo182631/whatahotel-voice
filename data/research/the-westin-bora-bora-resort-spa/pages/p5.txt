The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa
The new Westin—Bora Bora’s first resort in nearly a decade—makes a splash as the next overwater bungalow wonderland.

Why book?
A collection of overwater bungalows offer a prized location under Mount Otemanu. A white sand beach is flanked by the largest swimming pool in
Bora Bora
, but don’t skip a dip in the crystalline ocean.
Set the scene
Traversing across the South Pacific Ocean on a private speedboat revealed the astonishingly turquoise hues that attract visitors from around the world. Then 30 minutes later, we rounded the corner to the largest collection of overwater bungalows in the region at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa. The seamless check-in included a deliciously tangy passion fruit welcome drink and tiare lei. A golf cart transfer to my room allowed for quick glimpses of the rest of the property, dotted with guests enjoying slices of sand or a glass of wine at the beachside Varavara restaurant. Nearby, the largest pool in Bora Bora offers shade by way of six cabanas that are filled with the sounds of laughter and popped Champagne corks. Guests on pink and blue bikes whisked by, a nice touch for traveling the hotel's wooden walkways and beach paths. The 148 rooms include a mix of overwater bungalows and beachfront villas, but no matter which accommodation you choose, the sapphire colored water nearby never disappoints.
The backstory
Previously known as Le Méridien Bora Bora, the Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa underwent an extensive renovation and rebranding before reopening last year as the newest resort in the region since 2017. The property sets itself apart from its nearby neighbors with a focus on Six Pillars of wellness: Sleep Well, Eat Well, Move Well, Feel Well, Work Well, and Play Well. Signature amenities that bring this ethos into focus include a WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio that offers pilates and yoga classes, the brand’s signature bedside Lavender Balm, a Heavenly spa that employs Polynesian techniques and ingredients, fresh-pressed juice offered in the restaurants, and daily activities that include beach volleyball and sunrise run classes. I loved that a day could include both a mezcal margarita and a Tibetan Sound Bath class—the perfect balance of relaxation and wellness.
The rooms
The moment I stepped into my overwater bungalow, I was immediately wowed by the large glass floors that invited me to peer into the cerulean sea below and look for marine life. Room decor is contemporary, with an emphasis on clean lines and natural materials that include wood and stone. Not to be outdone, the turquoise ocean takes center stage against more intentionally muted tones of cream and light gray. Out on my private wooden deck, a plunge pool and nearby steps provided direct access to the ocean and views of over 100 similar accommodations. For families or small groups, the resort offers two-bedroom overwater bungalows that feature separate living areas, private terraces, and plunge pools. On land, 14 family-friendly bungalows are flanked by a natural lagoon and shaded areas of sand.
Food and drink
Four restaurants and bars offer variety for guests. Varavara, a casual beachside restaurant that features dishes like pizza, a fresh catch of the day, and steaks, quickly became my favorite lunch spot. I learned early to arrive before 1pm for the best toes-in-the-sand seating to pair with the grilled mahi mahi. At the hotel’s signature restaurant, Maere, seats directly on the edge of the lagoon make for a memorable evening set against the stars. On the particular themed night I attended, their rotating menu featured
Italian
specialties like Milanese braised beef and eggplant parmesan. A breakfast buffet can be enjoyed at Tipanier, though you shouldn’t miss the in-room breakfast service in an overwater bungalow. A plate of juicy mangoes and fresh watermelon juice helped kick start each day for me. At sunset, it really doesn’t get much better than cocktails at ‘O’ A bar with views of Mount Otemanu at every turn.
The neighborhood/area
The ocean is your playground here, and snorkelers and divers will find plenty of reasons to explore the teeming coral reefs below. When you’re ready to see another side of Bora Bora, the concierge team can plan catamaran, ATV, or helicopter tours, as well as an excursion to the main island of Vaitape for shopping and a glimpse into local life.
The service
There’s a “room to roam but ready to help” approach by the staff that strikes the right hospitality note for the setting. I spent many afternoons in conversation with restaurant staff who shared their tips on what to order as passionately as why they enjoyed working at the hotel (
hint: the ocean.
) A concierge team is readily available to coordinate excursions that range from jet skiing to private helicopter tours.
For families
There are many way for families to bond over cultural activities that include Tahitian dance classes, pareo (sarong) painting, and coconut palm weaving. Off-site options include boat excursions and ATV adventures that can be coordinated by the concierge team, but the expansive pool will be more than enough for most kids to feel satisfied. Best of all, parents can sneak off to the daily, complimentary wine or rum tasting just above at ‘O’ A bar.
Eco effort
One of my favorite afternoons was spent at the properties Eco Center, home to a sea turtle education and rehabilitation facility. After a brief introduction to the anatomy of sea turtles in the region and how the center aims to protect the mammals and marine ecosystems, we were led to a room to watch the majestic creatures during a feeding. Around the property, endemic plants like tiare tahiti, tāmanu, and pōhue tātahi support local biodiversity and minimizes irrigation needs. Bath amenities are provided in large-format bottles to minimize plastic waste, and all guests receive water bottle containers to refill at water fountains throughout the resort to reduce single-use plastics.
Accessibility
There are four ADA accessible rooms, including two overwater bungalows with lagoon views and two beach bungalows with plunge pools. All upper floors on the property are wheelchair accessible via ramps.
