# Fully Renovated Polynesian Accommodations in Bora Bora

## Accommodations

Stay like never before at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa. From the most overwater bungalows in Bora Bora, to our spacious beachside villas, you’ll find an incredible variety of luxurious accommodations...See More to choose from. And all have been newly renovated to provide you with a stay that redefines the Bora Bora experience.See Less

Overwater Bungalows

### A Mix of Style, Seclusion, and Spectacular

A stay at an overwater bungalow at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa is truly the height of the Bora Bora dream. Inside, you’ll be surrounded by a modern design infused with striking Polynesian accents. Outside, immerse yourself in the beauty of crystal clear turquoise water and stunning views of Mount Otemanu.

Private Plunge Pools

## Float Above It All

Sixty-two of our Bora Bora overwater bungalows and villas come with their own saltwater plunge pool. This is the ultimate in luxury, allowing you to enjoy a refreshing dip whenever you like. Float at mid-day. Float under the stars. Even a floating breakfast is possible.

Beach Villas and Overwater Bungalows

## Dream Even Bigger

Our accommodation in Bora Bora, Tahiti provide you the space to enjoy your surroundings to the utmost. Perfect for families or groups of friends, most have saltwater plunge pools on their private decks, and afford you some of the most striking views of Mount Otemanu and our turquoise lagoon.

Sleep Well - Heavenly Bed 2.0

## Be Beautifully Rested

Whether you’re in an Overwater Bungalow, Overwater Villa, or Beach Villa, enjoy an exceptional sleep experience thanks to our Westin Heavenly® Bed 2.0. Combined with the full complement of our Sleep Well program, including lavender balm and Sleep Well menu, you’re assured of beautifully restful and restorative nights.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa have a work space?

No, the rooms at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa?

Some rooms at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa have coffee/tea makers?
