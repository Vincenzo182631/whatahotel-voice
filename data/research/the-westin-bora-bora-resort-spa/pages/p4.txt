## Experiences

The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa lets you actively engage with all the wonders around you. From watersports, to spa treatments, to unique fitness opportunities, to the conservation efforts in our Eco...See More Center, to offsite adventures, every moment offers the excitement of discovering something new. The Westin Heavenly Spa

## Feel Your Best

Step into The Heavenly Spa at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa and discover a sanctuary devoted entirely to your well-being. Soothing. Stimulating. Serene. Here you’ll find the perfect treatment to feel your best. And our Three Stones Ritual available in every Heavenly Spa, brings a focus to every visit, allowing you to carry a spirit of positivity with you long after you visit our Bora Bora spa resort.

Fitness Center

## Move Beautifully

The WestinWORKOUT Fitness Center at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa provides a multitude of ways to get you moving. With access to Technogym equipment, including treadmills, ellipticals, and weight machines, and the opportunity to take Sunrise Yoga, Pilates, and Paddle Yoga classes, or receive personal training, you’re sure to find a workout beautifully suited to you.

Wellness Classes

### Sunrise Yoga & Pilates

Join in an invigorating ritual offered once a week on our main beach for sunrise yoga under the majestic gaze of Mount Otemanu. For those interested in pilates, we offer several weekly classes in our fitness center.

### Paddle Yoga

Take your yoga practice to playful new heights. Join us as we flow through poses on stable paddleboards while gliding along on crystal clear waters. Surrounded by Bora Bora’s breathtaking natural beauty, this is yoga like never before.

### Tibetan Singing Bowls

Experience the transformative healing and meditative effects of sound therapy. The soothing tones and vibrations produced by our Tibetan Singing Bowls promote relaxation and mindfulness enhanced by a setting of unmatched serenity.

Water Activities

### Find Your Flow

Whether in, on, or at the water’s edge, you’ll find activities that fit what you’re in the mood to do. Go for an invigorating swim, snorkel in the lagoon, or paddle an SUPs or kayaks. When you hit the sand, play beach volleyball, beach badminton, and beach tennis. Every day is a chance to see where your flow takes you at our Bora Bora dive resort.

Cultural Activities

## Discover the Mana Around You

You’ll find a variety of fun and interesting ways to engage with Polynesian culture at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa. Get instruction on coconut palm weaving, shell craft, flower crown weaving and Tahitian Dance classes. Or take in the incredible culture, tradition, and skills on display at our Polynesian Show. You’ll gain a deeper appreciation and respect for the people that call Bora Bora their home.

Off Site Activities

## Explore the Wonders Of Bora Bora

If you’d like to expand your Bora Bora adventures beyond our resort, there are many different ways to explore the island. Whether it’s the excitement of a jet ski, catamaran, ATV, or helicopter tour, or the more relaxed discoveries of a stroll through the shops of Vaitape, our Concierge team will assist you in planning it.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Mats and Balance Balls, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa have an on-site spa?

Yes, The Heavenly Spa is located on-site at The Westin Bora Bora Resort & Spa. Click here to book a service now.
