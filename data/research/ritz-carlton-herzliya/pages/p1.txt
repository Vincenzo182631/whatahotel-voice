Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya
5 star luxury hotel on the Israeli coast
The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya is set on Herzliya Marina, overlooking the Mediterranean Sea in the affluent residential area of Herzliya, just moments from the beach. This luxury property offers an elegant oasis with convenient access to Tel Aviv and features 115 guest rooms and 82 suites, including studios, one- and two-bedroom suites, and the Presidential Suite. Our stunning rooms and suites feature private balconies with views of the Mediterranean Sea or Herzliya Marina, a world-class kosher restaurant for exceptional dining experiences, an elegant lobby bar with signature cocktails, and a 12th-floor outdoor rooftop pool and bar with Mediterranean views. For wellness-led stays, The Ritz-Carlton Spa offers six treatment rooms, a hammam, Swedish sauna, and a relaxation area overlooking the Mediterranean, while the spacious 24-hour gym also enjoys sea views. Meeting spaces include a ballroom, meeting rooms, and a business center, with concierge and butler service availability adding to the luxury experience.

Sabbath Procedures Each Friday & Saturday
The day of the Sabbath begins each Friday at sunset, until one hour after sunset on Saturday. According to the law of Sabbath, starting a flame is not permitted. Therefore, the Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya is prohibited from cooking during this time; hence creating dishes such as omelets or making lattes and cappuccinos are not served on Saturdays in honor of the Sabbath. (All cooked dishes for Friday night dinner at the Herbert Samuel restaurant are created prior to the start of each Sabbath). *Please note the In-Room-Dining service does not serve warm dishes during the Sabbath. Shabbat dinner is served every Friday night between 7-9 p.m. (buffet style). These hours can vary according to the season.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya, with rates of $8.00 per hour, and $21.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya is Ben Gurion Airport (TLV). TLV is located approximately 32.4 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya have an airport shuttle to Ben Gurion Airport (TLV)?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya has an airport shuttle to Ben Gurion Airport (TLV).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
