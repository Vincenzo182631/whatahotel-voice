Suites & Rooms
The beauty of the Mediterranean Beach oceanfront is the centerpiece for the hotel rooms and suites at The Ritz-Carlton, Herzliya. All our Tel Aviv accommodation features private balconies and ocean views, while suites offer large living spaces and marble bathrooms with standalone tubs and direct water views. The Ritz-Carlton Suite
Spacious and light, the Ritz-Carlton Suite blends classic modern design with interiors inspired by the luxurious marina lifestyle, highlighted by a sweeping balcony. A fully immersive bathing experience features double sinks, a sauna, and marble finishes, offering complete recovery after a long day. Named Israel’s Leading Hotel Suite 2026 by the World Travel Awards.
Penthouse Suite
Crowning The Ritz-Carlton Herzliya with its 12th-floor locations, the Penthouse Suites offer an exclusive experience with convenient access to the pool and its amenities. These opulent retreats boast the most expansive balconies among all hotel suites, providing guests with unparalleled vistas and an outdoor oasis to savor the Mediterranean ambiance.
1 Bedroom Mediterranean Suite
Surrounded by endless mesmerizing sea views these spacious suites have it all that a modern traveler craves. An extra room to greet family and friends and an exquisite bedroom and immersive bathroom to have well-deserved relaxation after discovering the city of Herzliya.
