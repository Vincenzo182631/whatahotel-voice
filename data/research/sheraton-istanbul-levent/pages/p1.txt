# Welcome to Sheraton Istanbul Levent

## Escape to our sophisticated hotel in Istanbul, Turkey

Located in Istanbul’s dynamic Levent district, Sheraton Istanbul Levent serves as a gateway to an unforgettable urban experience. Ideally positioned close to major business hubs, shopping centers and the city’s iconic attractions, this modern hotel in Istanbul is an ideal choice for both leisure and business travelers. After a busy day, guests can unwind at Shine Spa or relax in the tranquil atmosphere of our peaceful garden, offering a refreshing escape in the heart of the city. Dining at The Levantino Restaurant presents a refined culinary experience inspired by Eastern Mediterranean flavors, where authentic tastes are enhanced with contemporary touches. Cafe by Sheraton provides a welcoming setting with expertly crafted cocktails, perfect for relaxing moments throughout the day. Our spa hotel in Istanbul, Turkey promises a memorable stay, where every detail is thoughtfully designed to ensure comfort, relaxation and guest satisfaction.

### Sustainability and Community at Sheraton Istanbul Levent

At Sheraton Istanbul Levent, we proudly partner with Topla-Kazan for sustainable plastic waste management, reducing environmental impact and supporting a circular economy. We are committed to embracing eco-friendly practices, prioritizing sustainability, and inviting our guests to join us in building a greener future. Additionally, we support the Guide Dogs Association, honoring their invaluable contributions by naming our meeting rooms after guide dogs.

Sustainability at Sheraton Istanbul Levent

### Our Sustainability Journey

At Sheraton Istanbul Levent, we are committed to caring for our planet and our community. Through energy and water efficiency, responsible waste management, and socially conscious practices, we work to reduce our environmental impact. Aligned with Marriott International’s Serve 360 vision, we integrate sustainability into our daily operations to create a welcoming, responsible, and inspiring stay experience.

### Natural Resource Conservation

At Sheraton Istanbul Levent, conserving natural resources is central to our sustainability approach. We optimize energy and water use through efficient systems, encourage guest participation with towel and linen reuse, and reduce our environmental impact through responsible waste management and eco-conscious practices.

### Our Commitment

At Sheraton Istanbul Levent, we prioritize the well-being and development of our associates. Through initiatives such as Associate Appreciation Week and Marriott’s Take Care program, we support physical, mental, and professional growth while fostering a workplace where everyone feels valued and supported.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Istanbul Levent?

The check-in time at Sheraton Istanbul Levent is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Istanbul Levent allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Sheraton Istanbul Levent. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 7.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Istanbul Levent?

On-site parking is available at Sheraton Istanbul Levent. Self parking is available at Sheraton Istanbul Levent, with rates of €8.50 per hour, €12.30 per day, and €141.00 per week. Valet parking is available for €16.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Istanbul Levent have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Istanbul Levent has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Istanbul Levent booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Istanbul Levent?

The closest airport near Sheraton Istanbul Levent is Istanbul International Airport (IST). IST is located approximately 32.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Istanbul Levent have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Istanbul Levent has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does Sheraton Istanbul Levent have a Sheraton Club lounge?

Yes, Sheraton Istanbul Levent has Sheraton Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Sheraton Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
