### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Le Méridien Stuttgart have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien Stuttgart have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Le Méridien Stuttgart?

The largest occupancy room at Le Méridien Stuttgart is the 1 Bedroom Junior Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Le Méridien Stuttgart?

Some rooms at Le Méridien Stuttgart permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien Stuttgart have a Mini fridge.
