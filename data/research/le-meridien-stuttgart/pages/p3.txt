Spend Sunday indulging in an exquisite brunch buffet at Le Méridien Stuttgart. From regional specialties to international delights, desserts, and a variety of cheeses, our selection caters to every taste. Enjoy warm gatherings with your loved ones while savoring an abundance of delicious food. For €79 per person, our Sunday brunch package includes water, juices, sparkling wine, selected wines, and hot beverages.
