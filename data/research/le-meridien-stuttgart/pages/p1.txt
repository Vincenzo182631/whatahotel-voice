# Welcome to Le Méridien Stuttgart

## Stylish hotel in Stuttgart city center

Looking for a Stuttgart hotel in the city center? Le Méridien Stuttgart is a prime choice. Renowned for its German wineries, medieval castles and baroque palaces, Stuttgart is a long-time royal residence...See More and home of Mercedes and Porsche. In between those cultural hotspots, Le Méridien Stuttgart is located near the city center, the Old Castle, as well as the New Castle, and is also one of the few Stuttgart hotels near the train station. Our hotel boasts elegant guest rooms, as well as a spacious wellness area featuring an immersive pool, sauna area and a fully equipped gym. The green parklands of the Schlossgarten opposite our Stuttgart hotel are ideal for an iconic stroll or jog. Across the park, Stuttgart's central street, the Königstrasse, invites guests for shopping and entertainment. Enjoy an extensive brunch buffet on Sundays and creative cocktails at the Bar Lillet, complementing the hotel's invitation to savor the good life in Stuttgart.See Less

Welcome to Stuttgart

With our central location close to the main station, Le Méridien Stuttgart is positioned ideally to discover Stuttgart’s many sights and landmarks.

Stay Connected With Le Meridien Stuttgart

Stuttgart Main Station

Le Méridien Hotel Stuttgart is located close to the main train station + the Schlossgarten park. If you are arriving by train, follow the signs to the exit "middle castle garden" directly at platform 16, cross the beer garden + take the stairs up. Our hotel is on the other side of the pedestrian bridge. Due to construction site Stuttgart 21, the route may currently change. We are happy to help.

Learn More

Stuttgart Airport

To reach Stuttgart airport is easy: From Stuttgart-Echterdingen Airport, take the local public transport S2 or S3 to the hotel towards the city center to "Stuttgart Hauptbahnhof". The journey time is approximately 30 minutes. After a short walk through the castle park, you can reach Hotel Le Méridien via the pedestrian bridge.

Learn More

Stuttgart Trade Fair

You can easily reach Messe Stuttgart from the hotel in the center, by car as well as by public transport. From the main train station, take the S2 or S3 towards the airport to the "Airport Messe" stop. Travel time: approx. 30 minutes. By car you can reach the Stuttgart exhibition center in about 25 minutes via the B27.

Shopping OUTLETCITY METZINGEN

From Le Méridien Stuttgart there are many options for shopping: The popular OUTLETCITY METZINGEN is a special shopping pleasure with over 70 luxury brands in one location. It can be reached in 45 minutes by car. Another option is the convenient shopping shuttle, which is included with the VIP shopping pass.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Stuttgart?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Stuttgart is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Stuttgart allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Stuttgart. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of €25.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Stuttgart?

On-site parking is available at Le Méridien Stuttgart. Self parking is available at Le Méridien Stuttgart, with rates of €3.00 per hour, and €30.00 per day. Valet parking is available for €30.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Stuttgart have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Stuttgart has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Stuttgart booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Stuttgart?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Stuttgart is Stuttgart Airport (STR). STR is located approximately 16.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Stuttgart have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Le Méridien Stuttgart has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
