# Experience Germany From Our Hotel in Stuttgart City Center

## Experiences

Le Méridien Hotel Stuttgart is ideally located near the beautiful Schlossgarten Park, making it the perfect starting point to discover the Swabian capital’s vineyards, culture, shopping and automobile...See More industries. From our Stuttgart hotel with a swimming pool, you can easily walk to major attractions, including the popular Königstraße shopping strip, the Staatsgalerie, the state opera and both the New and Old Castles in the city center. For automobile enthusiasts, the Porsche Museum and Mercedes-Benz Museum are easily accessible by public transport. After a day of exploring, relax and unwind at our hotel spa in Stuttgart, or take a dip in our indoor heated hotel pool, featuring a sauna for the whole family to enjoy.See Less

A Walk From Le Meridien Stuttgart

### Unlock Art™ With Le Méridien Stuttgart

Thanks to the Unlock Art™ programme hotel guests enjoy complimentary access to one of the most inspiring museums in Germany: the Staatsgalerie Stuttgart. Discover modern and old paintings and sculptures of German, Dutch, Italian and even more European artists from the 14th century till now spread over two buildings in different architectural designs. The Staatsgalerie Stuttgart, Unlock Art™ partner of Le Méridien Stuttgart, hosts 800 years of art on 9000 m² of exhibition space. Hotel guests get free access to the art exhibitions by presenting their room key of Le Méridien Hotel Stuttgart.

Local Activities

### Staatsgalerie Stuttgart

Free Admission for Le Méridien Guests. The Staatsgalerie in Stuttgart is one of Europe’s leading art museums, opened in 1843. It shows painting from the High Middle Ages as well as sculptures from the 19th century and has an extensive graphic collection.

Learn More

### Mercedes-Benz Museum

The motorized vehicle was invented in Stuttgart 125 years ago. Since then, the city is inseparable from the history and tradition of the automobiles. Discover the impressive Mercedes Benz Museum which is only 10 minutes by car from Le Méridien Stuttgart.

Learn More

### Porsche Museum

Till now, Mercedes-Benz and Porsche hold their headquarters here and due to their impressive museums, Stuttgart became a magnet for all automobile lovers from all over the world. The famous Porsche Museum with its almost 100 vehicles and more than 200 small exhibits is definitely worth a visit.

Learn More

### Shopping Outletcity Metzingen

With more than 60 flagship stores of premium brands, international fashion and designer collections with discounts from 30 up to 70% Outletcity Metzingen offers a wide selection of fashion. Outletcity Metzingen is a 45-minute car ride away from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Stuttgart have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Le Méridien Stuttgart offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Le Méridien Stuttgart have a pool on-site?

Yes, Le Méridien Stuttgart has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.
