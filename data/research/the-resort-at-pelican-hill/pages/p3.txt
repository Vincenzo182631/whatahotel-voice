## Event Policy for Pelican Grill and Coliseum Restaurant & Grill

To maintain the comfort and experience of all our guests, we do not permit wedding ceremonies in any of our outlets, including Pelican Grill and Coliseum Restaurant & Grill. Intimate celebration dinners or lunches are welcome; however wedding gowns or white bridal attire, and wedding photography are not permitted in our dining outlets or around the resort.

Should you wish to host a wedding event, please contact our Catering Department, and our team will be delighted to assist you.
