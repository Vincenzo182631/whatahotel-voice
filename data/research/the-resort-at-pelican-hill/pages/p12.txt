The Resort at Pelican Hill is embarking on its next chapter as it prepares to debut as the world’s first St. Regis Estate in the Fall 2027. This evolution will touch every facet of the resort, pairing thoughtful enhancements across the resort with an even more elevated level of bespoke service.

The foundation of Pelican Hill remains unchanged: gracious hospitality, a rare Mediterranean climate, and timeless architecture. Arches, colonnades, and olive tree-lined promenades create an environment of quiet permanence paired with the radiant light, openness, and creative spirit of coastal California.

Pelican Hill is cultivating a new era of Estate Living for discerning guests and local community that is delivered by a team of passionate hosts. The Estate honors the Resort’s heritage while introducing the elevated rituals and anticipatory care of St. Regis. California’s Grand Coastal Estate emerges as a sanctuary to gather, linger, and live beautifully.

## OVERVIEW

The projects will focus on refining iconic spaces, interiors, and elevating the guest experience while preserving the timeless Palladian architecture and pristine grounds that defines Pelican Hill.

## FAQs

### Will the Resort remain open?+

Yes. Enhancements are sequenced to preserve a serene guest experience; work is confined to weekday daylight hours with buffer zones and pristine guest pathways.

### How will check‑in work while the Main Estate lobby is refined?+

Guests may check-in at the Villa Clubhouse or The Bungalow Arrival Lounge. Villa guests will continue to be directed to the Villa Clubhouse while Bungalow guests will be directed to the Bungalow Arrival Lounge.

### What about noise?+

Active work occurs **Mon–Fri, 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.**; we maintain quiet hours and protective buffer zones to safeguard guest comfort.

### What is happening November 2026?+

A series of projects begin in November including the Main Estate, The Spa, Andrea, Coliseum Pool & Grill, and Pelican Grill that will elevate and enhance the Resort experience. All guests will have access to the Villa Clubhouse and Pool during this time.

### Will layouts change?+

Guest‑favorite layouts remain unchanged across Bungalows, Villas, meeting rooms, and key venues; enhancements focus on interiors, finishes, and guest comfort.

### Where can I get coffee or casual dining during the Main Estate refresh?+

Coffee and light refreshments are available at the Bungalow Caffe on Via Bellagio from 7am – 5pm, daily. Afternoon refreshments and alcoholic beverages will be available at The Lounge at the Bungalows on Via Bellagio from 4pm – 11pm, daily.

Guests may also enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner and weekend brunch at the Villa Clubhouse. Hours of Operation include:

- Breakfast: 7am – 10:30am, daily including a complimentary breakfast buffet for Villa guests only. Bungalow guests may enjoy the breakfast buffet for $55 per guest. A la carte breakfast is also available.
- Lunch: 11am – 2pm, Monday – Friday
- Brunch: 7am – 3pm, Saturday and Sunday
- Dinner: 4pm – 8 pm, Daily
- Villa pool food and beverage service 11am – 6pm daily rate

In-Room Dining remains available to all registered resort guests. With breakfast from 6am-11am, daily, and an all day menu from 11am-11pm, daily. In Room Dining service will not be available at The Spa at the North Villas, though there will be complimentary refreshments like tea, coffee, water and healthy light bites.

### Can guests make dining reservations at the Villa Clubhouse?+

Resort guests can make a reservation for dinner at the Villa Clubhouse. Breakfast, lunch and weekend brunch are first come, first served.

### Which pools are available?+

During Coliseum and Bella Vista work, all guests may enjoy the Villa Pool with convenient transportation from the Main Estate. The Coliseum Pool is targeted to re‑open late‑February (pending) with limited food service 12–5 p.m. daily; Bella Vista returns late December (pending).

### When will the Coliseum Pool re-open?+

The Coliseum Pool will re-open in late February 2027 and the Restaurant will open early Summer 2027. Late December, guests will also be able to enjoy the refreshed Bella Vista Pool, which is located right behind the Coliseum Pool.

### Will Andrea Open?+

Yes, the restaurant will re-open mid-October 2027 with a new name and a brand-new concept. Active work begins in Andrea September 2026 and continues through October 2027.

### Can I still book golf?+

Yes. The Ocean South Course offers guests 18 newly restored holes. The Ocean North Course is nearing completion, with 9‑hole loops available to book online. Guests booking tee times for the 18-hole golf course can book through Reservations or online. The North Course will fully re-open early November 2026.

### Are meetings and weddings affected?+

**Events continue uninterrupted** in designated spaces away from active work. For date‑specific availability, submit an inquiry.

### What fitness options are available during the Main Estate Fitness closure?+

All guests may enjoy the Villa Clubhouse Fitness Center. Fitness classes will be offered at the Bungalows and Villas.

### How will guests be transported between areas?+

Transportation is readily available from the Main Estate to the Villa Pool and Clubhouse during related enhancements.

### What are the parking options, and will we continue to have electric vehicle charging stations?+

On-site valet parking remains available at the Villa Clubhouse and the Bungalow Arrival Lounge. Valet is complimentary for registered resort guests. Guests may continue to park in front of their Bungalow or at their Villa.

Locals who are visiting The Spa at the North Villas will valet park at the Villa Clubhouse and a shuttle will escort them to the spa location. Local spa parking will be complimentary.

The resort will continue to offer electric vehicle charging stations, subject to availability.

### What are the check-in and check-out times?+

The times remain the same. Check-in is at 4:00pm and check-out is at 11:00am.
