## Mosaic Mani Pedi

Indulge in a luxurious treatment that rejuvenates hands and feet with a sensorial massage experience. This treatment features a texturized Dead Sea salt exfoliant and a delicious massage oil infused with citrus fruits, cypress, sage, and rosemary.

A hydrating mask creates a satin veil, leaving skin soft and supple. Complete with your choice of a Shiny Buff or Dazzle Dry polish for a flawless finish.

Every treatment at The Salon at Pelican Hill is a luxury wellness experience. Great care is taken to ensure a personalized service that delivers results.
