## Elevate Your Extended Stay

Very few destinations offer the combination of a private residence with the service of a luxury resort. Thoughtfully appointed, the Villas are an ideal home-away-from-home.

The Villas Extended Stay Program offers a turn-key solution for a variety of home transitions, such as; home renovation, a job relocation or any other short-term housing need. Preferred rates begin at 14 nights, with additional benefits for stays of 30+ and 90+ nights.
