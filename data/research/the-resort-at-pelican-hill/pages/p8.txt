### Cancellation & No-Show Policy+

To avoid penalty, reservations must be cancelled at least 48 hours in advance of the scheduled tee time.  No Shows will be charged the full guest fee amount for the number of players in the reservation to the credit card on file or the member account.

### General Club Information+

- **Driving Range:**  The driving range is available for warmup prior to the round only and is included in the guest fee.  It is not available for practice sessions.  On Tuesdays the driving range will not open until 9 AM.
- **Golf Car Usage**:  In order to operate a golf car at Pelican Hill the driver must be 18 years of age or older and possess a valid driver’s license.
- **Minimum Age Requirement:**  The minimum age for players, riders and spectators at Pelican Hill Golf Club is 5 years of age.  Additionally, all children should be chaperoned by an adult at the golf facility.
- **Forecaddies:**  Pelican Hill Golf Club offers optional forecaddie service.  To make forecaddie arrangements or obtain more information about the program, please call 888/ 507-6427.

### Reservations+

Reservations are required in advance, book online or call 844.878.0942.

For General Public tee times, reservations may be made fourteen days in advance and availability is limited. For Resort Guests, tee times may be made at time of booking for your dates of stay.

### Reduced Rate Rounds+

**Twilight Rounds:**  Guests are not expected to finish 18 holes when starting play at the Twilight rate.

## Discover how a Forecaddie can enhance your golfing experience.

- Forecaddies are better than any GPS/Laser rangefinder, guiding you how to approach the hole, adjust for the change in elevation and save you shots.
- They assist with pace of play by locating errant shots, raking bunkers, fixing ball marks and replacing divots.
- A forecaddie enables you to spend quality time with your fellow players and allows you to walk if you choose. You will enjoy the round more.
- They will tell you if your putt is going to break towards the ocean or the canyon…or both!
