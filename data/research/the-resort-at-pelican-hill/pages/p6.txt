##### INSPIRATION & HiSTORY

## A work of art inspired by the timeless   curves of Rome’s famed landmark

Perfectly circular, **136 feet in diameter** and three feet deep throughout, the Coliseum Pool is one of the largest circular

pools in the world, holding **380,000 gallons** of chemical-free soft saltwater. It dazzles with classic architecture of vaulted

arches, corniced columns and amphitheater of terraced decks. Set like a precious gem amidst this stunning architecture,

the pool itself is a work of art. Its radiant blue bottom was created by hand-setting **1.1 million hand-cut glass mosaic** **tiles**.
