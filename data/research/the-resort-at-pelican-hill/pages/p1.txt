Perched upon **504 acres** of unrivaled coastal beauty in Newport Beach, Pelican Hill® is set apart as a world unto itself.

Pelican Hill® is a truly inspired adaptation of the architectural style of Italy’s Andrea Palladio, the most acclaimed architect of the High Renaissance whose work was based on the classic building principles of ancient Rome and Greece.

Offering a sanctuary of seclusion, a feast for the senses, and a retreat for family and friends.

At Pelican Hill®, Palladio’s legacy has been reborn in a unique incarnation: the wisdom of the ancients, interpreted and shaped by a genius of the Renaissance, now adapted to the 21st century to create a resort of timeless beauty, proportion and vision. To create an architectural masterpiece in the image of Palladio, built upon the philosophical pedestal that humans are uplifted by striving for perfection, Pelican Hill® mandated an emphasis on detail unsurpassed in the resort world.

Summer months are for living outdoors, where sunlight warms the afternoon, ocean air sweetens every breath, and golden hour lingers long after sunset. We invite you to experience Summer Al Fresco at Pelican Hill, a celebration of outdoor living along the Newport Coast.
