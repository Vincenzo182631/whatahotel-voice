# Directions

The Resort at Pelican Hill’s Southern California location is easily reached from several major airports. John Wayne Airport (SNA) is a short 15-minute drive from Newport Coast and Long Beach Airport (LGB) is just 30 minutes away. Both airports can accommodate domestic and private jets and offer an easy commute to The Resort at Pelican Hill.

Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) is also available as a travel choice and is only 45 minutes to the north.

#### Transportation

##### Directions from Los Angeles International Airport **(LAX)**

Los Angeles International Airport is a 45-minute drive to The Resort at Pelican Hill

1. Follow the signs exiting LAX to 405 Freeway South.
2. Proceed on Interstate 405 for approximately 35 miles.
3. Merge onto CA-73 South toward San Diego (toll applies- click here for details).
4. Exit Newport Coast Drive and follow to Pelican Hill Road South and turn right.

##### Directions from Long Beach Airport **(LGB)**

Long Beach Airport is just 30 minutes away from The Resort at Pelican Hill

1. Head west on Donald Douglas Drive.
2. Turn right onto N Lakewood Boulevard.
3. Take the I-405 South ramp.
4. Keep left at the fork and merge onto the I-405.
5. Take the exit onto CA-73 S toward San Diego (toll applies- click here for details).
6. Take the Newport Coast Drive exit and follow to Pelican Hill Road South and turn right.

##### Directions from John Wayne Airport **(SNA)**

John Wayne Airport is a short 15-minute drive to The Resort at Pelican Hill

1. Take a right out of the airport onto MacArthur Boulevard.
2. Follow MacArthur Boulevard for approximately 2 miles.
3. Merge onto the CA-73 South toward San Diego (toll applies- click here for details).
4. Take the Newport Coast Drive exit and follow to Pelican Hill Road South and turn right.
