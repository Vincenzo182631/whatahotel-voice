# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Valet parking is available and is complimentary for hotel guests or guests attending hotel-based events.

Complimentary

Free

### Self-Parking

Grand Hyatt Dubai offers on-site parking facilities for up to 800 vehicles. We have one of the largest on-site parking facilities in the city. Hotel parking services include on-site parking, indoor garage parking, convention centre parking and accessible parking spots. We also feature three complimentary EV chargers, available 24 hours, with no reservation required. For assistance, kindly contact our concierge team at +971 4 317 2244 or concierge.grandhyattdubai@hyatt.com.
