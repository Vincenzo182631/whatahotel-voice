## Award Winning Urban City Resort

Experience Dubai in extraordinary style at Grand Hyatt Dubai, a city resort surrounded by 37 acres of lush gardens overlooking the iconic Creek. Ideally located near the airport and major attractions, the hotel blends serene resort living with effortless city access. Revel in elegant rooms, exceptional global cuisine, a spectacular 20,000 sqm waterpark, and the acclaimed Ahasees Spa.

## ROOMS & SUITES

## EXPLORE

Ahasees Spa & Club

Guided by three core philosophies — Trade, Flourish, and Evolve — each experience is thoughtfully curated to nurture the body, calm the mind, and elevate the senses. From restorative therapies to immersive signature rituals, every treatment is designed to leave you feeling deeply renewed, strengthened, and effortlessly at ease.

Please be informed male and female steam rooms are not available due to enhancement. We apologize for the inconvenience.

Swimming Pools

Grand Hyatt Dubai offers 2 outdoor pools, including one of the largest leisure pools in the city, and a 20-metre indoor pool.

Grand Hyatt Dubai Waterpark

Home to 16 signature attractions, Grand Hyatt Dubai Waterpark is thoughtfully designed for all generations — where children dive into endless fun while parents relax, recharge, and enjoy the experience in elevated style.

Please be informed some of the rides are not available due to maintenance. We apologize for the inconvenience.

Grand Club Lounge

Discover the new adults-only Grand Club Lounge, an exclusive space for corporate guests, direct customers, and World of Hyatt Globalist Members. Guests can enjoy an extended continental breakfast, daily refreshments, and cocktail hours in the evening.

Take the kids to Motiongate Dubai theme park or show them 360-degree views of the city from the top of Burj Khalifa. See live performing art shows at the Dubai Opera or visit the iconic Dubai Fountain.

Exceptional Comfort and World-Class Hospitality at Grand Hyatt Dubai. Had a wonderful stay at Grand Hyatt Dubai — the property truly delivers on comfort, service, and atmosphere.
