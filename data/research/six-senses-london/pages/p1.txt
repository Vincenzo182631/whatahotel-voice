## A different rhythm for London

Step into a different rhythm. Find courtyard calm at the heart of the city, with Notting Hill color just around the corner and unexpected menus waiting at Whiteley’s Kitchen, Bar and Café. This is Six Senses London, a hotel set within The Whiteley, a West London landmark reimagined with purpose and care. Upstairs, 109 rooms and suites and 14 branded residences are designed for you to settle, slow down, and stay present. The first Six Senses Place invites members and hotel guests into a world of connection and well-being. Downstairs, a spa brings together pioneering treatments and timeless rituals, from cryotherapy to flotation. Everything is designed to restore, surprise, or shift your perspective. Leave with more than you came for.

## The first ever Six Senses Place

A new rhythm for the city. This is a space to nourish and restore, to move with purpose, to gather, learn, and feel a sense of belonging. Moments are shared or taken solo. The atmosphere is social but connections are never forced. What happens here is shaped by who walks through the door.

## Dining

Food with feeling, made from local produce and inspired by ideas from everywhere. Channeling the rebellious spirit of William Whiteley, it marks the return of a West London icon. From firelit breakfasts that start the day warm and easy to late-night drinks that keep the night alive, Whiteley’s Kitchen, Bar and Café invites neighbors, friends and newcomers to enjoy menus that follow the seasons, bringing whatever’s at its best to the table, always fresh, always full of character.

- #### Whiteley’s Kitchen

  - British cooking reimagined: seasonal, locally sourced ingredients transformed with fire, fermentation, and instinct.
- #### Whiteley’s Bar

  - Start with a classic cocktail and move on to inventive infusions and natural wines, all served with care.
- #### Whiteley’s Café

  - Homemade bakes and seasonal treats paired to perfection with small-batch coffee in a relaxed West London setting.

## Wellness & Spa

Six Senses Spa London quiets the city. A 20-meter long and 8-meter wide indoor pool anchors the space with two glass block walls inspired by the Maison de Verre either side. Immerse in a thermal journey with a seasonally infused steam room, cold plunge pool, and London’s first magnesium pool. Within treatment rooms, hands-on healing blends with high-tech wellness including cryotherapy and a floatation pod to shift you between energy and stillness.

The 325-square-meter Fitness Center includes a light-filled studio and a team of professionals to guide your training. Past the reflective pool, the Wellness Studio offers a space for mindful movement and restorative practices. Cross the bridge flanked by two towering natural tree sculptures and you reach the Alchemy Bar, where British seasonal herbs become blends to create and take home. Every detail is designed to restore your energy and return you to the city renewed.
