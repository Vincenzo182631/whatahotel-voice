# Rooftop Pool

Austin Proper Hotel

Perched on the 5th floor, Austin Proper’s rooftop pool is more than an amenity, it’s an urban retreat. Designed for both recharge and revelry, this sun-drenched perch offers a calm escape in the heart of the city. Take a dip in the saltwater, stretch out in the shade, or settle into one of our oversized cabanas for an all-day rhythm of rest, nourishment, and laid-back luxury.

## Pool Details

## TAKE A DIP

Exclusively for hotel guests, our rooftop pool invites you to linger longer-with plush loungers, oversized umbrellas, and a full-service pool menu. Set your own pace-from a meditative morning swim to an unhurried afternoon under the sun.

## Cabanas

## A PRIVATE HIDEAWAY

Each of our three 18-foot-tall cabanas is seamlessly tucked into the succulent-lined landscape, designed for up to 8 guests. Expect seasonal bites, Aesop sunscreen, and a few Proper touches to surprise and delight. Reservations include three-hour access, full food and beverage service, and a distinctly Austin backdrop for your day in the sun.

Reserve a Cabana

## Poolside Dining

## AT YOUR SERVICE

La Piscina brings bold flavor and Texas soul straight to your lounger. Enjoy wood-fired seafood, house-made tortillas, and signature cocktails, plus breakfast tacos on weekend mornings. Sip mezcal, snack on Proper guacamole, and let the day unfold poolside.

RESERVE TABLE

## Pool Deck Happenings

## MAKE A SPLASH

Join us seasonally on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday evenings for Proper Sounds on the Pool Deck, featuring live DJs, seasonal cocktails, and an ever-changing lineup of creative energy from La Piscina. The vibe is high, the pace is yours.

## FAQ

What makes the rooftop pool unique?

Perched above downtown Austin, the rooftop pool offers panoramic skyline views, chic loungers, curated cocktails from La Piscina, and an exclusive ambiance perfect for sunbathing, socializing, or simply unwinding.

Who has access to the rooftop pool?

Pool access is reserved for hotel guests and Proper Club members (membership includes weekday pool access only).

What are the rooftop pool hours?

The pool is open daily from 8am to 10pm, with food and beverage service from 11am to 10pm. Hours may vary seasonally-check the rooftop pool page or reach out to Guest Services for the latest information.

Can I reserve a cabana?

Yes, poolside cabanas are available by reservation. Email cabana.reservations@properhotel.com to secure your spot. Pool lounge chairs are first come, first served.

Is food and beverage service available at the pool?

Absolutely, daily from 11am to 10pm enjoy seasonal cocktails, light bites, and full poolside service curated by the team behind La Piscina, our rooftop Mexican-inspired restaurant.

Are there poolside events or special programming?

Yes, our rooftop hosts DJ sets and seasonal events. Visit the Happenings page for upcoming experiences and RSVP details.
