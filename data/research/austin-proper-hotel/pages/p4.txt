# Eat & Drink

Austin Proper Hotel

At Austin Proper, dining is designed for the way you live-vibrant, nourishing, and never without a touch of indulgence. In partnership with MML Hospitality-the local team behind iconic favorites like Jeffrey’s and Clark’s Oyster Bar-our four distinct concepts bring fresh energy and thoughtful sourcing to every plate.

Across all menus, you’ll find nutrient-dense grains, plant-forward options, locally sourced produce, cold-pressed juices, no artificial dyes, and low-temp cooking methods that preserve flavor and vitality-designed to support how you want to feel, without ever compromising on taste.

## Kappo Kappo

Kappo Kappo is a French Japanese Omakase in an intimate chef’s counter setting. Twin brothers Gohei and Haru Kishi prepare A5 wagyu and pristine seafood course by course, creating a dining experience that feels both refined and personal in a space designed by Kelly Wearstler.

EXPLORE KAPPO KAPPO

Reserve table

## The Peacock    Mediterranean Grill

The heart of Austin Proper’s culinary program, The Peacock draws inspiration from North Africa, Israel, Greece, and Lebanon. Seasonal mezze, wood-grilled meats, and vibrant vegetarian dishes come together in a space that feels as warm and textured as the food. Organic wines, light spirits, and cocktails with fresh herbs and citrus complete the experience

EXPLORE PEACOCK

Reserve table

## LA PISCINA CEVICHES & FAJITAS

Elevated but unfussy, La Piscina brings fire-kissed Mexican fare to the rooftop, with a focus on fresh seafood, hand-pressed tortillas, and grilled-to-order family-style plates. Frozen margaritas and sustainable tequilas flow all day alongside produce-packed aguachiles and poolside snacks built for summer.

EXPLORE LA PISCINA

Reserve table

## Goldie's Sunken Cocktail Bar

A jewel box of a bar tucked away in the heart of the hotel, Goldie’s is equal parts sultry and serene. With pink marble, copper finishes, and a rotating menu of boutique wines, garden cocktails, and low-ABV pours, it’s a retreat for elevated sipping and intimate conversation.

EXPLORE GOLDIE'S

Reserve table

## The Quill Room

Designed by Kelly Wearstler, The Quill Room is where Proper’s signature mix of style and substance meets Austin’s creative pulse. With views of the Butterfly Bridge, this second-floor lounge serves Texas-inspired small plates, carefully composed cocktails, and curated sounds-just far enough from the city’s buzz to feel like your own discovery.

EXPLORE THE QUILL ROOM

Reserve table

## Afternoon Tea

Indulge in elevated bites, including buttery scones with mascarpone cream, decadent caviar canapés, seasonal pastries, and delicate French macarons. Pair these delights with a curated selection of sparkling cuvées and specialty teas.

## FAQ

WHAT CUISINE IS FEATURED AT LA PISCINA CEVICHES & FAJITAS?

La Piscina features agave-forward cocktails, bright ceviches, sizzling fajitas, fresh seafood grilled over a wood fire and house-made tortillas from an open comal, all prepared without seed oils.

Reserve on OpenTable

What does The Peacock Mediterranean Grill offer?

The Peacock) serves vibrant Mediterranean cuisine for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and weekend brunch-featuring mezze, fresh seafood, grilled meats, and an award-winning wine list.

Reserve on OpenTable

WHAT IS THE CUISINE AT KAPPO KAPPO?

Kappo Kappo serves a French Japanese Chef’s Tasting Menu. Enjoy A5 wagyu and pristine seafood served at an intimate chef’s counter in a Kelly Wearstler-designed space that brings you into the experience.

Reserve on OpenTable)

What can I expect at The Quill Room?

The Quill Room) is an artful music lounge ideal for small bites, cocktails, and casual conversation with a cozy, residential ambiance.

WHAT IS GOLDIE'S SUNKEN BAR LIKE?

Goldie’s is a speakeasy-style bar offering inventive cocktails in an intimate setting tucked behind the hotel’s lobby with Afternoon Tea service on the weekends.

Reserve on OpenTable

Is room service available?

Yes-Austin Proper offers a full in-room dining menu curated from its signature restaurants, available throughout the day.

Are there options for private dining or events?

Yes-select venues offer private dining experiences or full buyouts. Contact the hotel’s Meetings & Events team to learn more.

WHAT DINING VENUES ARE AVAILABLE ONSITE?

The hotel’s dining outlets serve everything from casual breakfast to elevated cocktails and dinner. The Peacock Mediterranean Grill) is the flagship restaurant serving breakfast, lunch, dinner, and late-night drinks daily. The Quill Room) is our music lounge offering cocktails and light bites. Goldie’s Sunken Bar is our intimate cocktail lounge with weekend Afternoon Tea service.
