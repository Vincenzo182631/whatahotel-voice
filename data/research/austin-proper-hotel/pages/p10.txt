# About

Austin Proper Hotel

Situated in Downtown’s 2nd Street District, Austin Proper Hotel and Residences offers an artful immersion in luxury and well-being. With emphasis on collaboration, the passionate compelling vision of designer Kelly Wearstler meets the culinary creativity of Austin tastemaker, MML Hospitality. Essentials include 232 rooms and suites, branded residences, four unique settings for dining and drink, a focus on wellness with a dedicated spa plus a variety of atmospheric spaces for events. And it wouldn’t be Proper without a 5th floor pool deck and restaurant offering small-batch tequilas and sunset views.

##### Photography

Professionalphotographyrequests must be reviewed and approved with at least four weeks’ notice. This includes anyphotography or videography with professional equipment, drones, and/or media-use. Site fees start at $10,000 per shoot. Hotel rooms cannot be booked to be used as a shoot location. A member of our team must accompany the photographer in all public spaces and spaces must remain 100% intact.

Requests for professional photography can be made HERE.

Mobile phone photography is permitted with limitations:

- No more than 5 minutes of dedicated photo taking.
- Privacy and respect for hotel guests and colleagues must be observed at all times.
- Doors, hallways, passages, elevators, etc. may not be blocked for any reason.
- Spaces must remain 100% intact – furniture, design elements, seating, etc. cannot be moved, rearranged, or stood on.

## FAQ

##### Is Austin Proper Hotel pet‑friendly?

Yes, Austin Proper welcomes dogs (up to 45 lbs) for a $100 non-refundable fee per stay. Check our Pet‑Friendly policy for details and fee information.

##### Who designed the hotel’s interiors?

Kelly Wearstler curated the distinctive design throughout the hotel-fusion of modern elegance with local Austin spirit.

##### What wellness amenities are offered?

Guests can enjoy a full wellness program including a fitness studio, spa treatments, recovery room (with sauna, cold plunge, and red light therapy), pool deck, run clubs and yoga classes. View all options on the Wellness page.

##### What restaurants and bars are on-site?

The hotel’s dining outlets serve everything from casual breakfast to elevated cocktails and dinner. The Peacock Mediterranean Grill is the flagship restaurant serving breakfast, lunch, dinner, and late-night drinks daily. La Piscina Ceviches & Fajitas is our poolside restaurant offering daily lunch and dinner service along with weekend brunch. Kappo Kappo offers guests a one-of-a-kind French-Japanese tasting menu featuring A5 wagyu and pristine seafood served at an intimate chef’s counter . The Quill Room is our music lounge offering cocktails and light bites. Goldie’s Sunken Bar is our intimate cocktail lounge with weekend Afternoon Tea service. Explore them on the Restaurants & Bars page.

##### What fitness options are available?

The 24/7 fitness center is located on the 4th floor and is equipped with Peloton bikes, free weights, and cardio machines-is described in full on the Fitness page.

##### Where is the hotel located?

Situated in downtown Austin’s vibrant 2nd Street District-nearby 6th Street, Lady Bird Lake, the historic Texas State Capitol and the University of Texas campus. See travel tips on our Getting Here & Neighborhood guide.

##### What makes Austin Proper unique?

The hotel blends artful design, cultural engagement, and wellness, creating an immersive local experience in a landmark location. Learn more on the About page.
