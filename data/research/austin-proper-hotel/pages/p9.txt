# Guest Experiences

Austin Proper guest experiences are designed to extend your stay into the city and into the moment. Some are intimate and unhurried. Others take you somewhere new. All of them are worth planning around.

### Proper Afternoon Tea

An elevated twist on tradition, served in Goldie’s elegant, Southern-inspired setting. Choose from a rotating selection of hot or iced teas, paired with savory tea sandwiches and house-made sweets. Add champagne or caviar service for an afternoon worth lingering over. Starting at $89 per person

Reserve

### Poolside Cabana

Claim your corner of the pool deck. Cabana rentals include three hours of dedicated space, lounge seating, and $350 in food and beverage to spend however you like. Cold drinks, poolside bites, the works. Available daily, subject to availability. Three-hour rental. $350 F&B minimum. $150 rental fee

Reserve

### Custom Hat Fitting

South Congress Avenue has a new reason to visit. In partnership with Maufrais, Austin Proper guests can book a private hat shaping experience at the iconic SoCo shop. Your group gets one hat per person, complimentary accessories, a custom shaping for a perfect fit, and assistance with lunch reservations. Private room available for larger groups. Transportation available upon request. $500 per guest.

Contact

### Hill Country Wine Tour

Trade downtown for the open road. A private bus takes your group to three Hill Country wineries for a half-day of tastings, scenic views, and the kind of afternoon that’s hard to replicate. A professional guide handles the details. You handle the wine. 4.5 hours. Pricing from $155 per person. Private transportation included. Three wine tastings included

Contact
