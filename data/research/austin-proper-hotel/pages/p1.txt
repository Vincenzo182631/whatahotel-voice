## Truly One Of A Kind

Situated in Downtown’s Second Street District, Austin Proper Hotel and Residences is an iconic luxury destination just steps from Lady Bird Lake, ACL Live, and the city’s best shopping. With expressive interiors by Kelly Wearstler and culinary concepts by McGuire Moorman Lambert Hospitality, the hotel offers 232 design-forward rooms and suites, a rooftop pool, immersive wellness experiences, and menus built around local farms, vibrant produce, and nutrient-rich ingredients. Wellness, design, and culture, seamlessly woven into every stay.

## Rooms & Suites

With Austin as muse, the 232 guest rooms and suites have been meticulously detailed by designer Kelly Wearstler to create a sense of residence. Interiors are deeply influenced by local craft and artisans, featuring Arts & Crafts-inspired wall coverings, distressed hardwoods, and barefoot-friendly area rugs. Well-considered appointments include signature Proper beds, Aesop bath amenities, Kelly Wearstler x Parachute Home robes, and state-of-the-art connectivity.

## Eat & Drink

Dining at Austin Proper blends bold flavor with local sourcing and seasonal care. Led by McGuire Moorman Lambert Hospitality, The Peacock serves vibrant Mediterranean-inspired spreads, while La Piscina brings wood-grilled Tex-Mex to the rooftop. Downstairs, sip craft cocktails at The Quill Room or settle into the moody glow of Goldie’s Sunken Bar. The newest addition, Kappo Kappo, serves a French Japanese chef tasting menu straight from the counter. From brunch to nightcap, every menu reflects Austin’s energy. and a commitment to thoughtful, nourishing food.

## Wellness

Wellness at Austin Proper is integrated into every moment. Start with a session in our 2,000-square-foot gym, a cold plunge and sauna at Verbena Spa, or a trail ride along Lady Bird Lake. Stay in rhythm with cold-pressed juices, plant-forward dining, and poolside recovery. Whether you’re stretching at sunrise or unwinding with a garden cocktail, every experience is designed to support how you move, rest, and recharge.

EXPLORE WELLNESS

## A Uniquely Austin Experience

Located in the heart of the 2nd Street District, Austin Proper is surrounded by the city’s signature blend of culture, creativity, and ease. Step out to trails along Lady Bird Lake, browse local boutiques, catch a film at Violet Crown, or find live music just around the corner. Everything you love about Austin, within walking distance.

## Gatherings

Our well-appointed ballroom and intimate venues offer over 9,500 square feet of versatile indoor and outdoor spaces, perfect for meetings, weddings, special events, company retreats, and private dining. Elevate your event with our creative design and the expertise of the Proper team and MML Hospitality, who curate menus and styles of service to suit any occasion.
