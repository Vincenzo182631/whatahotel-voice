## FAQ

What is the Recovery Room?

Located adjacent to the spa and fitness studio, the Recovery Room offers a restorative space equipped with a cold plunge, infrared sauna, and red light therapy to accelerate post-workout healing. Learn more on the Wellness page.

Who can use the Recovery Room?

The Recovery Room is available to anyone by reserving a private session through Verbena Spa.

How do I book a Recovery Room session?

Sessions can be reserved online or by calling 512-628-1418.

What is the cost of Recovery Room use?

Fees vary depending on session length and access type (drop-in, member, or package). For current pricing, visit the Recovery Room page or contact Verbena Spa.

Can I combine recovery treatments with spa services or classes?

Yes. Many guests create custom wellness packages by combining Recovery Room sessions with treatments at Verbena Spa, fitness classes, or personal training. Visit the Wellness page or speak with Guest Services to plan your experience.

What is included in the Recovery Room?

Amenities such as towels, water, access to a Wifa bluetooth speaker, sunglasses, red light therapy, sauna access, and cold plunge use are included. Guests are also welcome to bring their own items like yoga mats or recovery tools if desired.

Are there any restrictions or guidelines?

Guests should be in good health and free from contraindications. Please notify Verbena Spa of any health conditions, especially those that may affect sauna or cold therapy use. Staff provide clear instructions and supervision during sessions.

How long is a typical session?

Standard sessions are 45–60 minutes. Reserve your preferred time via the Recovery Room page or at Verbena Spa.
