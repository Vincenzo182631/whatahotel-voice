# Fitness

Austin Proper Hotel

At Austin Proper, we don’t treat wellness as an add-on. It’s thoughtfully integrated into every stay, from sunrise movement to sunset recovery. Whether you’re here for a week or a weekend, our state-of-the-art fitness center, holistic recovery offerings, and daily rituals are designed to support the way you live, intentionally, and fully present.

## 24/7 FITNESS CENTER

Located on property, our 2,000+ square-foot fitness center is open 24/7 and outfitted with top-tier equipment for cardio, strength, and functional training. Weekly yoga and bootcamp classes, complimentary for hotel guests, invite you to stay grounded, while personal training sessions offer more individualized support.

## FAQ

What fitness facilities are available at Austin Proper?

The hotel offers a 24/7 fitness studio equipped with Peloton bikes, treadmills, ellipticals, strength machines, free weights, and functional training tools. Explore details on the Fitness page.

Are group fitness classes available?

Yes-guests andProper Club members can join yoga, HIIT, cycling, meditation, and wellness workshops. Class schedules and booking info are available on the Fitness page or via Guest Services.

Can I book personal training or private sessions?

Absolutely. Private training, fitness assessments, and personalized wellness sessions can be booked through Verbena Spa.

What are the studio's operating hours?

The fitness center is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week-always accessible for your exercise needs.

Is proper attire required?

Yes-fitness attire and closed-toe athletic shoes are required for safety and comfort while using gym equipment or attending classes.

Do guests need to reserve equipment or class spots?

Gym equipment is available on a first-come, first-served basis. Spots for group classes can be reserved through the Happenings page.

Is the fitness center accessible to non-hotel guests?

Yes- Proper Club members have access to the fitness center. Contact Verbena Spa for availability and pricing.

How can I combine fitness with other wellness services?

You can pair your workout with spa treatments and Recovery Room sessions. Explore more options on the Wellness page or contact Guest Services to create a personalized plan.
