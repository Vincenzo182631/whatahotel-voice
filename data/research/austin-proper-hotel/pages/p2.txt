# Rooms &    Signature Suites

Austin Proper Hotel

Our 232 guest rooms and suites were designed by Kelly Wearstler as modern interpretations of Austin’s iconic Craftsman architectural style. Featuring layers of textures and historical patterns in earthy tones, our rooms utilize local woods and stones from nearby quarries. Local art and decorative accessories combine to create warm, sophisticated spaces with a residential feel. Many of our rooms and suites offer stunning city views and breathtaking sunset views, ensuring a memorable stay. Beyond the rooms, guests enjoy access to our state-of-the-art fitness center and ability to book a private Recovery Room (sauna, cold plunge, and red light therapy) and treatments at Verbena Spa.

## FAQ

What types of rooms and suites are available at Austin Proper?

Austin Proper offers a range of accommodations including:

-King & Double Queen rooms

 -City View & Lake View rooms

 -Connecting Rooms

 -Balcony Rooms & Suites

 -The 1,110 sq ft Penthouse Suite with expansive views

View all options on the Rooms & Suites page.

What amenities are included in every room?

Each room features Kelly Wearstler-designed interiors, custom furnishings, curated artwork, Aesop bath products, blackout shades, complimentary Wi-Fi, minibar, Nespresso machine, 24/7 room service, a Vifa bluetooth speaker, a 55″ HD Smart TV, Parachute robes, and Proper beds with Fili D’oro linens.

More details are available on each room listing.

Are there rooms with lake or balcony views?

Yes. Select rooms and suites offer floor-to-ceiling windows, balconies, and views of Lady Bird Lake or downtown Austin. Be sure to filter by features and choose your preferred view when booking.

How many guests can each room accommodate?

Most King rooms sleep two guests, while Double Queens accommodate up to four. Suites vary by layout-check individual room descriptions for capacity details.

Are accessible (ADA) rooms available?

Yes. Accessible rooms with features such as roll-in showers, grab bars, lowered vanities, and visual fire alarms are available across several categories. Visit the Accessibility Details page for more info.

Are the rooms pet-friendly?

Yes. Dogs under 45 lbs are welcome with a non-refundable fee. See the full Pet Policy for details.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available upon request, subject to availability. Fees may apply, contact the hotel directly for more information.

How do I reserve a room?

You can book directly through the Austin Proper Rooms page for the best available rates. For group bookings or special arrangements, contact the hotel directly.
