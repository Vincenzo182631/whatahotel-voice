# Verbena Spa

Austin Proper Hotel

Steps from the city’s buzz, Verbena Spa is a serene, design-forward escape inside Austin Proper. Start with a detoxifying welcome drink, then explore personalized treatments guided by seasoned therapists. From targeted massage to tech-driven recovery, every offering is designed to restore balance. For a deeper reset, the Recovery Room combines cold plunge, dry sauna, and red light therapy to ground the body and mind.

Facials

**TheraFace Pro Facial**

Focusing on firming, and contouring this facial helps improve radiance, increase circulation and reduce swelling. Feel skin calm and facial muscles relax through this high-tech experience featuring Therabody’s Theraface Pro device and Monastery skincare. Modalities can include percussive cleansing & facial massage, hot & cold CryoThermal therapy, red, blue, & IR LED, and microcurrent, based on your particular needs. Therapeutic for all skin types, but especially suggested for those seeking notably youthful results or deeper facial massage.

**Monastery Glow Facial**

An instantly transformative treatment that combines state-of-the-art technology with ancient techniques for luminous, visibly lifted skin. Building on the Proper Facial, our estheticians are trained to customize a program based on your skin’s current needs. Choosing from different modalities such as contouring and lifting microcurrent, lymphatic-stimulating and collagen-remodeling Gua Sha, and rejuvenating LED treatment for the face and hands. This facial provides radiance, elasticity, and lift that improves with every session.

**Gua Sha Sculpting Facial**

Experience the ancient art of gua sha facial massage using Monastery, natural, small batch, aromatherapeutic skincare products and Lanshin traditional Chinese medicine tools. This lymphatic drainage technique has been performed for thousands of years and is known for its soothing nature and relaxing sensation. Perfect for any skin type needing to unwind, but beloved by those prone to inflammation and puffiness.

**Diamond Glow Facial by Natura Bisse**

An intensely revitalizing anti-aging treatment for the most demanding complexions. This facial uses sophisticated ingredients for long-lasting hydration. Diamond reinforces the skin’s natural defense system, improving elasticity and luminosity while simultaneously soothing and calming the skin. Designed for those looking for the ultimate in luxury skincare with radiant results.

**Vitamin C Recovery Facial by Natura Bisse**

Restore firmness and brighten with Natura Bisse vitamin C, the most natural prevention for visible signs of aging and hyperpigmentation. This antioxidant facial begins with a deep cleanse and enzymatic exfoliation, followed by a reparative serum formulated to stimulate collagen production and hydration.

Massages

**Tequila: The Proper Way** Embark on a transformative wellness journey honoring the sacred healing traditions of agave. Begin with a mindful sip of Austin’s own Lalo Tequila, followed by a guided desert meditation and therapeutic massage. Features traditional cactus dry brushing and ANCIENTS oil by Rootfoot, a native plant blend distilled in the method of tequila-making with blue agave, cypress, and citrus. Concludes with a cleansing palo santo ritual.

**Proper Massage**

A bespoke massage that will indulge your muscles, whether you are in need of a Swedish massage or Deep Tissue or an intuitive infusion of both.

**Stress Relief Massage**

Escape the daily hustle and indulge in a blissful de-stress massage, featuring our therapeutic Relief Balm. Powered by the natural benefits of arnica, invigorating cayenne, and soothing full-spectrum CBD, this massage melts away tension and soothes both body and mind. Embrace deep relaxation and recharge with this perfect blend of warmth and tranquility.

**Rootfoot Ritual Aromatherapy Massage**

Made exclusively for Austin Proper Hotel & Verbena Spa by Rootfoot Founder & Distiller, Laura Huth. Our aromatherapy collection provides a blend of local native plants and embodies the Texas landscape experiences, highlighting the scents & interactive properties of specific plants native to the region.

Recovery Room Touchless Service

- Red light therapy for cellular renewal

Yoga

Yoga is a form of non aerobic exercise that uses precise posture, breathing exercises & meditation. In ancient Sanskrit, the word yoga means “union.” Yoga is promoted as a system of personal development. Yoga is said to cultivate prana, which means vital energy or life force and is similar to qi (or chi) in traditional Chinese medicine. Yoga is claimed lead to a state of greater physical health, relaxation, happiness, peace, and tranquility. Guided Meditation

Meditation is a practice in which the mind can be guided to induce a heightened sense of well being.

The term meditation refers to a broad variety of practices that includes techniques designed to promote relaxation, build internal energy or life force and develop compassion, love, patience, generosity and forgiveness. The word meditation carries different meanings in different contexts. Meditation has been practiced as a component of numerous religious traditions and beliefs. ## FAQ

How do I book a treatment at Verbena Spa?

You can book your appointment by emailing us at atxp.verbenaspa@properhotel.com or by phone at **512-628-1515**. To view availability and schedule online, please visit our direct booking portal.

What services are offered at Verbena Spa?

We offer a curated menu of massages, facials, body therapies, and enhancements, including holistic and plant-based treatments. Specialized offerings include prenatal services and herbal-infused rituals.

Do I need to be a hotel guest to use the spa?

No, Verbena Spa is open to both Austin Proper Hotel guests and the public. Locals and travelers alike are welcome to enjoy our serene wellness offerings.

Where is the spa located?

Verbena Spa is located on the 4 **th floor** of the Austin Proper Hotel at **600 W 2nd Street, Austin, TX 78701**. Follow signage in the lobby or ask a team member to guide you to the elevator.

What are your hours of operation?

We are open daily from **9****AM to 6 PM**, with advanced booking available until 8:30pm. Holiday and seasonal hours may vary. To confirm hours or check same-day availability, call or email us directly.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for **24 hours’ notice** to cancel or reschedule any appointment. No-shows and late cancellations are subject to the full treatment fee.

Are gift cards available?

Yes, gift cards are available and make a thoughtful gift for any occasion. Please inquire at atxp.verbenaspa@properhotel.com or visit the spa desk in person to purchase.
