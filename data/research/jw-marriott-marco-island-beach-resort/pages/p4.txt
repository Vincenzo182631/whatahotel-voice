Fine Dining

True culinary artistry and refined ingredients set our two Marco Island fine dining restaurants, Tesoro and Ario apart. Indulge on the creations of our Chef de Cuisine as you experience a journey of exceptional flavors that will leave you craving more.

Golf Club Dining

Enjoy a day playing one of two championship courses set on naturally beautiful wetlands, then catch up over breakfast, lunch, dinner or drinks at Hammock Bay Grill or The Rookery Grill.

The Perfect Accompaniment

Balinese-Inspired Grill & Tiki Bar

Beach Views with Specialty Rums

## Tesoro Restaurant

Italian and Spanish for “treasure,” Tesoro, our adult-exclusive dining experience (21+), offers inspiring Mediterranean cuisine, served tapas-style. Situated on the fifth floor of the Lanai Tower, enjoy raw bar seafood, crafted cocktails, an extensive wine list and incredible sunsets. With its chic decor, contemporary lounge and indoor/outdoor bar, Tesoro is an escape from the everyday.

### Italian-Inspired Flavors

Mediterranean-inspired, shareable cuisine with Italian flavors, fresh Gulf seafood, housemade pasta, and seasonal ingredients. The stylish bar features Riviera liqueur, Sangria, craft martinis, and "Riviera Moments" cocktails, complemented by an extensive wine selection.

Korals Sushi & Cocktail Lobby Bar

Seaviews & Ocean-Fresh Flavors

Floor to ceiling windows looking out to the shimmering Gulf waters, dramatic wooden ribbing and a towering aquarium of colorful tropical fish set the scene for a sushi bar experience like no other. Mingle over sushi, seafood, small plates and inventive cocktails and enjoy the relaxed sophistication of Korals Sushi & Cocktail Lobby Bar.

Cocktails & Conversation

Korals offers a memorable take on a sushi bar with its soaring ceiling of freeform cherry wood ribs and mesmerizing 11-foot cylindrical aquarium housing colorful tropical fish. Here, the knife skills of sushi chefs are matched by our mixologists, concocting imaginative libations to accompany decadent sushi dishes. Sit back, take in the views, and let the music and atmosphere wash over you.

Small Dishes & Panoramic Vistas

Savor the setting, surveying the waves or the drinks menu. You will discover an extensive selection of handcrafted cocktails, craft, domestic, and specialty beers, great wines, and small-batch Bourbon and whiskey. Choose from freshly prepared sushi or Asian-inspired small plates.

Golf Dining Options

## Rookery Grill

Grab breakfast before your tee time, enjoy lunch after your round, or have dinner with your foursome at The Rookery Grill; it's the perfect place to relax and enjoy the day. Conveniently located at The Rookery at Marco's 18th green in Naples, Florida, this relaxed clubhouse favorite offers breakfast, lunch, and seasonal dinner in a welcoming setting just steps from the course.

After your round, enjoy chef-crafted specialties, signature sandwiches, crisp salads, and classic comfort dishes. Pair it all with a refreshing cocktail, local draft, or a glass from the thoughtfully curated wine list for the perfect finish to a day. Access is exclusive to registered guests of JW Marriott Marco Island Beach Resort and The Members Club at Marco.

## Hammock Bay Grill

With its rolling fairways and lush natural surroundings, Hammock Bay Golf Club sets the stage for an unforgettable day of golf and great dining. Hammock Bay Grill is the perfect place to enjoy breakfast or lunch or to gather after your round for cocktails, craft beer, and a menu of satisfying flavors.

Relax in the laid-back, poolside ambiance and enjoy wraps, fresh favorites, and the chef’s seasonal creations from the grill. Open daily for breakfast and lunch, and seasonally for dinner. Toast a game well played at the poolside Tiki Bar, or step into the English-style pub offering beers from around the world. After a day on the links, there’s no better place to unwind.

10k Alley & Gastropub

State-of-the-Art Gaming

Gaming meets rustic copper stills at 10K Alley, our innovative taproom experience in the Lanai Tower. 10K Alley melds a steam-punk style distillery look with the neon lights of a bowling alley, arcade games, and mini-golf. Dine on small bites paired with barrel-aged bourbons, and over 50 Florida-brewed craft beers. It’s a sophisticated dining environment for family fun during the day and a popular destination.

Over 50 Games & Bowling

Bring your competitive spirit and childlike sense of wonder to 10k Alley. This unique new taproom experience brings together a gaming emporium with pool tables, mini-golf, air hockey, bowling, and popular arcade classics. With over fifty games and prizes to be redeemed, this is an escape from the beach, pools and sun, with plenty of fun for all ages.

Elevated Dining at 10K Alley

Turn any moment into a celebration at 10K Alley. This stylized taproom and gaming emporium offers an enticing menu of bar favorites and small bites like the Pub Burger, Bavarian Pretzels or Parmesan and Herb Truffle Fries. Pair them with over 50 Florida-brewed craft beers, barrel-aged bourbons, and signature cocktails.

Complimentary parking is provided for non-overnight guests.

### Ario

Dine under the palms on the patio or reserve a private Chef’s Table dining experience at Ario, our signature restaurant. With its name derived from the zodiac elements of Fire and Water, this is fine dining with a primal touch, where the fiery sear of the grill and fresh Gulf seafood is masterfully orchestrated by award-winning chefs. The tantalizing menu is enhanced with international spices and rubs, 35-day signature dry-aged steaks, locally sourced ingredients and an impressive sommelier-curated wine list. At Ario, this is fine dining elevated to an art form.
