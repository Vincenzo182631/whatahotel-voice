## Golf

## Hammock Bay Golf & Country Club

Enriching experiences are often shaped by their surroundings. At Hammock Bay Golf & Country Club, the course showcases the area’s natural beauty while providing an enjoyable challenge for golfers of all skill levels. The redesigned course, featuring PlatinumTE paspalum turfgrass, enhances playability and offers a vibrant, memorable golf experience amidst the splendor of nature. Beyond the greens, guests can enjoy country club-style amenities, including a resort-style pool, indoor and outdoor dining options, and a pro shop, creating the perfect blend of relaxation and recreation.

## The Rookery at Marco

Play your way through a course that both challenges and inspires. Redesigned by acclaimed golf course architects Peter Jacobsen and Jim Hardy in 2014, The Rookery at Marco in Naples, Florida, combines a resort-private atmosphere with the stunning natural beauty of Marco Island. Complete your experience with exceptional amenities, including a fully stocked pro shop and an on-site restaurant, ensuring a memorable golf getaway.

### 19th Hole Celebrations

After your round, unwind with a drink or a meal at the Hammock Bay Tiki Bar, where refreshing cocktails and regional flavors are perfectly paired with relaxing poolside views. The Rookery Grill, open seasonally, offers breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Additionally, the clubhouse features a variety of amenities, including a game room, library, lounge, veranda, private dining areas, and an executive meeting room.

### Audubon Gold Certified

Hammock Bay is recognized as a "Gold Certified Signature Sanctuary" by Audubon International, one of just 45 designated communities in the world. The gold certification is the highest level of the Audubon Signature Programs, recognizing sustainable communities within the natural environment.

### Tennis Programs

Resort guests can play a friendly game together or take instructor-led lessons. Drill and play is the quickest way to improve your game through court time, clinics, and match play with USPTA certified tennis professionals, designed to keep players at all levels active and competitive. Available seasonally.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Are guests able to make online reservations to Hammock Bay Golf Club?

Online reservations are accepted at Hammock Bay Golf Club. **Reserve a tee time online**, or call us at +1 239-389-6610.

Does Hammock Bay Golf Club have a clubhouse?

There is a clubhouse at Hammock Bay Golf Club.Enjoy a bite to eat or a cool refreshment at our clubhouse restaurant.

What is par at Hammock Bay Golf Club?

Hammock Bay Golf Club is a par-72, 18-hole course, with a total length of 7100 yards. To learn more about the specifications of Hammock Bay Golf Club, please call +1 239-389-6610.

Does Hammock Bay Golf Club offer a driving range?

A driving range is located at Hammock Bay Golf Club. Amateur and experienced golfers alike can warm up or practice their shot ahead of their next round.

Does Hammock Bay Golf Club offer equipment rental?

Equipment rental is offered at Hammock Bay Golf Club.

Does Hammock Bay Golf Club offer golf school instruction?

Golf school instruction is offered at Hammock Bay Golf Club. Work with experts to improve your golf game.

Does Hammock Bay Golf Club provide lessons?

Lessons are offered at Hammock Bay Golf Club. Golfers in search of tips or tweaks to elevate their golf game can feel encouraged to do so.

Is there a children’s golf program available at Hammock Bay Golf Club?

There is not a children’s golf program available at Hammock Bay Golf Club.

What forms of payment does Hammock Bay Golf Club accept?

Yes, Hammock Bay Golf Club offers the following forms of payment: Cash,Credit Cards

Are spikes allowed at Hammock Bay Golf Club?

Spikes are permitted at Hammock Bay Golf Club.

Is there a putting green available at Hammock Bay Golf Club?
