# Paradise by Sirene

## An Escape for Adults

Paradise by Sirene at JW Marriott Marco Island Beach Resort is an exclusive, adults-only retreat designed for guests ages 21 and over. Elevated above the shoreline, this serene sanctuary offers immersive island surroundings, refined indulgences, and uniquely Florida experiences. Sirene, your muse and hostess, welcomes you to her thoughtfully curated enclave, featuring just 94 guest rooms and suites tailored for those seeking tranquility, sophistication, and a touch of the unexpected.

**JW Marriott Marco Island** 400 South Collier Boulevard

Marco Island, Florida

34145 USA​​​​​​​

###### Paradise by Sirene

## An Inspired Escape. A Hidden Haven.

At JW Marriott Marco Island Beach Resort, Sirene—your gracious hostess—welcomes you to her serene haven, thoughtfully designed for guests ages 21 and over. As a Friend of Sirene, you’re invited to enjoy all she has curated for your stay: savor a complimentary continental breakfast for two each morning, unwind on her private stretch of beach, or bask in the sun beside her rooftop adults-only pool.

###### Paradise By Sirene

## Luxury in an Intimate Setting

The true beauty of Sirene lies in her ability to make you feel like the only guest in this intimate, luxurious retreat on Florida’s Gulf Coast. Need something to enhance your stay? Simply text Sirene using the dedicated number provided upon arrival, and her personal concierge will be delighted to assist with any special requests. Paradise by Sirene offers a private enclave with exclusive services, all while providing full access to the world-class amenities of JW Marriott Marco Island.
