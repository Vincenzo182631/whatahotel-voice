# Restaurants & Bars

Park Hyatt Auckland’s dining experiences include Onemata, The Living Room, The Pantry, Captain's Bar, and our In-Room Dining Service. Enjoy a showcase of premium New Zealand produce, local wines, and craft beers. 15% surcharge applies on all public holidays.

## Onemata

Onemata is a waterfront dining destination celebrating the best of the season through thoughtfully crafted seasonal menus and exceptional local produce. Christmas & New Year's Eve bookings are now open.

Bookings essential, please contact onemata@hyatt.com for enquiries or assistant.

## Living Room

Experience waterfront dining in the relaxed elegance of the Living Room, where bespoke share-style menus are thoughtfully crafted for gatherings and memorable occasions. Enjoy our Waterfront Express Lunch from $39 per person, perfect for a refined midday escape, or indulge in Winter High Tea from $80 per person. Our Christmas menus are now available, with reservations open for end-of-year celebrations.
For bookings or enquiries, please contact livingroom@hyatt.com.
