# Boutique 5 Star Hotel on the Water's Edge \| Park Hyatt Auckland

## Retreat to our luxury hotel, perfectly positioned on Auckland’s waterfront

Park Hyatt Auckland is perfectly located on the water’s edge and offers expansive views across Wynyard Quarter's Lighter Basin and out to the Waitematā Harbour. Our sophisticated waterfront residence has 195 guestrooms, four restaurants and bars, versatile and elegant event spaces, a day spa, 25-metre infinity pool, and a full fitness center. Enjoy a luxurious, 5-star experience with us.
