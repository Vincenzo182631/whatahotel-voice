Our hotel’s 378 rooms, including 16 suites, embody an “East meets West” style of accommodations. Depending on the room type, guests may enjoy either city views or views of Canal City Hakata, the shopping and entertainment complex located directly next to the hotel. Our rooms feature plush beds, branded amenities and spacious bathrooms with large tubs and separate showers. Please be advised that the check-in time for group guests is after 4:00 PM.​
