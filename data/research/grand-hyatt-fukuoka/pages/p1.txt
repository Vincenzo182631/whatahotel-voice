## Explore a Luxury Hotel in the Heart of Fukuoka

Grand Hyatt Fukuoka is a luxury hotel located in the Hakata district in the heart of Fukuoka City, Japan. The hotel is connected to Canal City Hakata, a multidimensional complex of business, entertainment and shopping facilities. Explore a variety of shops, a cinema complex, a theatre and a host of eateries.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Our hotel’s 378 rooms, including 16 suites, embody an “East meets West” style and design—all with a warm and welcoming atmosphere. These rooms and suites reflect modern needs with a functional design and feature spacious bathrooms with large bathtubs and separate showers. ​

## EXPLORE FUKUOKA

Grand Hyatt Fukuoka is located just five kilometres from Fukuoka Airport in the Hakata district. With its downtown location and proximity to the airport and the city’s main entertainment area, Grand Hyatt Fukuoka is eminently suited for both business and leisure travellers. Visit several key cultural highlights of the city like the Kushida Shrine, the Sumiyoshi Shrine, the Rakusuien Japanese garden and the Hakata Machiya Folk Museum—all within a few minutes’ walk.
