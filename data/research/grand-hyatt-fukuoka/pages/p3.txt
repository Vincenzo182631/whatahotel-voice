## MARTINI'S

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

The bar of Grand Hyatt Fukuoka is located in the lobby and features the finest cocktails and martinis. Relax in the comfortable surroundings designed for intimate gatherings and special occasions. Martini's is a popular rendezvous where guests enjoy creative cocktails and a fine selection of champagne and whiskies with a warm and friendly atmosphere. An extensive cigar selection is also a particular feature of this bar.
