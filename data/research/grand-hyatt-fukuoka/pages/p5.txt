### Fukuoka - The Canal City Did Not Disappoint.

I had a great stay at the Grand Hyatt Fukuoka. The location is hard to beat — you're right next to Canal City and within easy reach of Hakata Station, which makes getting around the city simple.

The room was clean and well kept, and the bed deserves a special mention: it was exceptionally comfortable, easily one of the better hotel beds I've slept in. The spa facilities were a highlight as well. The steam room was lovely, having two saunas was a nice touch, and the cold plunge pool and whirlpool rounded things out perfectly. I especially enjoyed the massage chairs, and the amenities throughout were fresh and spotless.

Service was a strong point. Eason in particular went out of his way to accommodate a late checkout, which made the last day of the trip much easier.

A couple of minor notes for future guests: check-in is held firmly to 3 p.m., and any additional guest on the reservation needs to be present at check-in — my guest arrived after I did, so I ended up waiting in the lobby rather than getting settled in. Both are understandable as policy, just worth knowing in advance. The pool is also more functional than recreational, so set expectations accordingly if you're traveling with kids.

None of that overshadowed a genuinely enjoyable stay. I'd happily return.

### Excellent Hotel, Skip the Breakfast

This Grand Hyatt was an excellent hotel with a great location (near a train line, one stop from Hakata station) and the service was (in typical Japanese fashion) excellent. Our room was spacious and the view was great, and really my only real complaint was the quality of the breakfast buffet. For the price (over ¥5000, like $33) the quality and variety could have (and honestly should have) been better. For that price I’ve had AMAZING breakfast buffets at Grand Hyatts and Hyatt Regencys around the world. The meat was not great, the eggs were off, and the juices were not fresh. The only real saving grace on the breakfast for me was the “pancakes” that reminded me of eating flan. Other than the very skippable breakfast, the hotel was great and I still highly recommend. But unless the breakfast is free, skip it.

### Exceptional staff went above and beyond my needs

The hotel is located right next to Canal City, one of the biggest shopping malls in Fukuoka and is a walkable distance from nearby yatai overlooking the river. During the summer months, the mall has themed water fun events that my children loved.
Regarding the hotel, the rooms are suitable for families and the facilities are well kept. One key point about the swimming pool: Only children above a height of 130 cm are allowed in so younger children may not be able to utilize the pool.
The staff are easily the reason why I will come back to stay at this hotel. Everyone was courteous and resourceful. I purchased too many souvenirs to bring back to my home country and the staff helped me organize and pack a box to be delivered from the post office to my doorstep when I arrived. They also helped me book transportation on departure day so I wouldn't have to worry about looking for a taxi (I had many suitcases) during rush hour. I couldn't have been more satisfied with my stay at this hotel.
