## Transportation

Getting here

Fukuoka Airport (FUK)5.5 kilometers5.5 kilometers

Transportation Options

Subway

Take a subway from Fukuoka-kuko (Airport) Station to Airport Line Hakata Station (about a five-minute ride). Transfer to the Fukuoka City Subway Nanakuma Line (150m accessway, about a three-minute walk) and take a subway from the Nanakuma Line Hakata Station to Kushida Shrine Station (about a one-minute ride).​

Exit from Exit 2 near the West ticket gate and walk for about three minutes toward Canal City Hakata.​

JR Hakata Station1.1 kilometers1.1 kilometers

Transportation Options

City Subway Nanakuma Line

Take a subway from the Fukuoka City Subway Nanakuma Line Hakata Station to Kushida Shrine Station (about a one-minute ride). Exit from Exit 2 near the West ticket gate and walk for about three minutes toward Canal City Hakata.

Walking

From JR Hakata Station Hakataguchi, go straight on Hakata Ekimae-dori and turn left at the “Hakata Keisatsusho Iriguchi” intersection and walk toward Canal City Hakata. The first floor of Canal City Hakata is connected to the lobby level of our hotel.

Nishitetsu Fukuoka (Tenjin) Station0.8 kilometers0.8 kilometers

Transportation Options

Fukuoka City Subway Nanakuma Line

Take a subway from the Fukuoka City Subway Nanakuma Line Tenjin-minami Station to Kushida Shrine Station (about a two-minute ride). Exit from Exit 2 near the West ticket gate and walk for about three minutes toward Canal City Hakata.

Walking

Walk along the Kokutai-doro toward Gion and cross Haruyoshi Bridge. Turn right and walk along the Naka River and through Seiyu Park, located in front of our hotel’s main entrance.
