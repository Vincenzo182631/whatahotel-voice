# Seasonal pop-ups

Celebrate in style with our unique themed pop-ups at The Montague on the Gardens. Whether you're planning a summer party or looking to make the most of the festive season in London, our seasonal pop-ups offer the perfect setting. From November, our renowned Ski Lodge transforms into a winter wonderland to get you into the Christmas spirit. In the summer, join us on the Wood Deck at our fantastic pop-up Beach Bar. Enjoy real sand, refreshing cocktails, and delicious BBQ food in a vibrant beach setting.

11th May to 25th September
