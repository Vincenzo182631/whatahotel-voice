# The Cottage Collection

The Cottage Collection

## New England Charm with Modern Comfort

Scattered throughout the property’s beautifully maintained grounds, our classic Cape Cod cottage-style rooms offer a distinctive stay that blends New England charm with modern comfort. Each cottage is thoughtfully set near gardens, pathways, and the shoreline, offering a quiet sense of privacy. Many feature private decks or balconies, while select rooms include a gas fireplace.

## The Cottage Collection

Our classic Cape Cod cottage-style rooms are situated throughout our beautifully maintained 25-acre property with views ranging from gardens to a golf course to our private beach.
