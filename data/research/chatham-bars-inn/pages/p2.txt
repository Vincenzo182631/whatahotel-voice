## Where the Shore Meets Your Stay

Our accommodations reflect the Inn's legacy, timeless, distinctive, and quietly refined. As a historic Cape Cod landmark, each room and suite is unique, shaped by the character of the property and its coastal setting, yet unified by thoughtful detail and comfort.

#### No resort fees. Just genuine hospitality.
