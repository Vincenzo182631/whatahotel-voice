## Unrivaled Boating Experiences

Our fleet is owned and operated for Chatham Bars Inn guests, offering a range of high-end vessels suited for everything from intimate excursions to larger family outings.

Each experience is led by our U.S. Coast Guard–licensed captains with deep knowledge of Cape Cod’s waters and arranged seamlessly through our dedicated boating concierge, ensuring confidence, comfort, and exceptional service from departure to return.
