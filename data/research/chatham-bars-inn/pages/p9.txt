# The Farm

## The Farm

The Chatham Bars Inn Farm produces well over 100,000 pounds and 125 varieties of vegetables and features innovative farming techniques including hydroponics. The farm also offers a seasonal farm stand open to the public now through late November as well as farm-to-table dinners and creative group activities.

### Farm-to-Table Dinners

Each season, the farm hosts a number of farm-to-table events which are open to the public. Guests enjoy a welcome reception in a stunning setting followed by a 4-course dinner served family style. Dinner guests are seated at outdoor tables surrounded by the farm's fruits, vegetables and flowers with twinkling lights suspended above the dining area. During the cooler fall months, the farm-to-table dinner events move indoors to the farm's glass greenhouses.

GREENHOUSE DINNERS

### The Farm Stand

The Farm Stand is open seasonally and features fresh vegetables and beautiful flowers grown right here on the farm, along with a selection of specialty products from local makers and goods made at the Inn. Stop by to enjoy the season’s freshest offerings and discover a variety of farm-grown, locally sourced, and Inn-made favorites.

### Private Events

The Farm is now available for a limited number of private and corporate farm-to-table events, set among our landscaped fields and rows of seasonal vegetables and flowers. Ideal for larger gatherings, these experiences are perfect for celebrating special occasions, hosting family reunions, or hosting events for colleagues and clients.

### Team Building and Group Activities

The farm offers a full range of fun activities for groups of 10 to 100 people, perfect for your next get-together with a group of friends, club members or coworkers. Choose from "Farm Tour & Foraging", "Farmer & Florist", "Sunset Tour & Bonfire", "Pick for a Cause", "Beekeeping 101" or "Creating a Kitchen Garden".
