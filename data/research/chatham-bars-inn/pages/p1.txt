## Explore Chatham with Ease

Chatham Bars Inn places you at the heart of one of New England's most charming seaside towns. Just moments from Main Street's boutiques, galleries, and restaurants, the resort also offers effortless access to North Beach Island via its private shuttle, making it easy to spend the day exploring pristine beaches, sandbars, and the natural beauty that defines Chatham.

## Chatham Bars Inn

Your oceanfront home on the harbor with a quarter-mile beach, a private dock, and dining with a view. Steps from Main Street and everything Chatham has to offer.

1

## Atwood House Museum

One of Chatham's oldest homes (c. 1752), now a local history museum presenting 18th-century life through murals, maritime artifacts, and rotating exhibits. Outside is the Nickerson North Beach Camp – a historic 1947 beach shack used for decades by a local family on Chatham's barrier beach.

2

## Chatham Candy Manor

A Main Street institution since 1955, Candy Manor is known for hand-dipped chocolates and slow-cooked fudge you can watch being made through the shop window. During the winter holidays, they pull homemade candy canes by hand.

3

## Chatham Lighthouse

Built in 1877, Chatham Light is tied to one of the most famous rescues in U.S. Coast Guard history – in 1952, four local guardsmen saved 32 shipwreck survivors in a nor'easter, a story later told in the Disney film The Finest Hours. This active Coast Guard base offers free tours to the top of the lighthouse on select summer dates.

4

## Chatham Squire

A local institution with two personalities: a family-friendly dining room serving local seafood and classic dishes, and a high-energy tavern with live music and karaoke. Hundreds of vintage and custom license plates line every wall and ceiling.

5

## Eldredge Library

Listed on the National Register of Historic Places, this library looks more like a castle than a reading room. Stop in for lectures, children's programs, or just to take in the towering ceilings, historic fireplace, and stained-glass windows.

6

## Godfrey Windmill

This restored 18th-century windmill survived hurricanes and lightning strikes while grinding corn, wheat, and rye for the town for nearly 110 years. On select summer days, volunteers let the ocean wind spin the gears – and grind cornmeal just as they did in 1797.

7

## Kate Gould Park

Home to Art in the Park, Oktoberfest, holiday celebrations, and the town's beloved Friday night band concerts – a family tradition since 1936.

8

## Monomoy National Wildlife Refuge

This is 7,900 acres of protected dunes, salt marshes, and beaches that are home to migratory birds and gray seals. Explore the mainland on the scenic Morris Island Trail or reach North and South Monomoy Island by boat.

9

## Orpheum Theatre

Chatham's Main Street cinema shows blockbusters and indie films in a classic setting, with gourmet snacks from their café. Don't miss the hand-painted lobby mural featuring over 100 famous characters and directors from across cinematic history.

10

## Playground

Chatham's town playground features a custom train-themed play structure with two sections – low climbs and sensory panels for toddlers, and tall slides and rope webs for older kids.

11

## Railroad Museum

A free, hands-on museum inside a restored Victorian-era depot. One highlight is the 1910 red wooden caboose that logged more than one million miles between New York City and Chicago.

12

## Veteran's Field

Home to the Chatham Anglers of the Cape Cod Baseball League. Games are free June through August. Fun fact, over 400 alumni play in Major League Baseball!

13

## Chatham Fish Pier

Built in 1946, this active fish pier features an elevated observation deck with a front-row seat to watch local fishermen unload the day's catch of fresh cod, haddock, dogfish, and more. Keep an eye out for the seals swimming nearby hoping for a snack!

14

## Chatham Bars Inn Farm

The 8-acre Chatham Bars Inn Farm in nearby Brewster produces over 100,000 pounds and 125 varieties of vegetables annually, using innovative techniques including hydroponics. The farm also offers seasonal farm-to-table dinners, a public farm stand, and creative classes and group activities.

## The Historic Main Inn, A Cape Cod Landmark

The Main Inn stands as the heart of the property, a landmark that has defined this stretch of Cape Cod for more than a century. Built long before modern zoning restrictions, it occupies a setting that could never be replicated today.
