## Relax and Enjoy

From family beach days and seaside adventures to moments of quiet relaxation, Chatham Bars Inn offers experiences for every generation. Spend your days exploring the Cape by bike, enjoying time on the water, or unwinding at the spa, while younger guests discover engaging programs designed just for them. Here, every moment is an opportunity to reconnect and experience Cape Cod together.

#### We don’t believe in, or charge, resort fees. Experience genuine hospitality throughout your stay, with select experiences and amenities thoughtfully included for our guests.
