# Stay Above the Seaport

Settle into one of 66 guestrooms and suites designed as a calm counterpoint to downtown Manhattan’s energy. Thoughtfully appointed with teak veneer finishes, rainfall showers, plush linens, and 50″ interactive 4K televisions, each space balances comfort with understated style. Select accommodations feature private terraces overlooking the East River, Brooklyn Bridge, and the evolving skyline of Lower Manhattan.

**Exclusive Guest Perks** Experience more of the Seaport with exclusive offers from our neighborhood partners, including sailing excursions with Manhattan By Sail, classes at Seaport Pilates, coffee and pastries from Café Patoro, shopping at Mure + Grand, and pet-friendly services through DogHouse Seaport Canine Club. Connect with the Front Desk for details on accessing these exclusive neighborhood experiences during your stay.
