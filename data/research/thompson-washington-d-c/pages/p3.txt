# Restaurants & Bars

Our signature onsite restaurant draws inspiration from traditional American dishes and classic spirit-forward cocktails. Enjoy a true supper house experience at the truly exceptional Surveyor. After dinner, head up to the Rooftop at the Thompson and unwind with 360-degree views of Washington D.C. and the waterfront while sipping craft cocktails and taking in Navy Yard’s lively social scene.
