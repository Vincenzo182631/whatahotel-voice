## SOCIAL ANCHOR OF WASHINGTON D.C.'S NAVY YARD

Experience understated luxury at our hotel near D.C.’s Navy Yard, where the perfect blend of industrial architectural elements meets the hallmark glamor of Thompson Washington D.C. Embark on a bike adventure along the scenic Anacostia Riverwalk Trail, immerse yourself in the wonders of nearby Smithsonian Museums and take a leisurely stroll along the iconic National Mall or catch a baseball game at Nationals Park. Indulge in the culinary delights of our exquisite on-site American restaurant, Surveyor, before capping off your night with mesmerizing views of the nation’s capital from our inviting rooftop bar.

## rooms & suites

Our spacious rooms and elegant suites are designed with your luxury in mind. You will notice the subtle nods to the Navy Yard’s rich nautical history throughout the hotel’s public spaces and your private room. Indulge in the intoxicating waterfront view when you select a suite or guest room with a balcony, unwind in comfort and cozy up in one of our signature oversized Thompson robes.

### Pet Policy

Thompson Washington D.C. is a pet-friendly hotel. We welcome dogs at no additional charge for stays less than 30 nights. For stays greater than 30 nights, a deep cleaning fee may be charged at the hotel's discretion. Dogs are not allowed in our bars or restaurants. Dogs must be leashed at all times in common areas and cannot be left unattended in guest rooms. These restrictions do not apply to service animals.

#### Pet Fees

- Price: Free

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  50Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 75Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.

## Destination Fee

A daily destination fee of $32.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.
