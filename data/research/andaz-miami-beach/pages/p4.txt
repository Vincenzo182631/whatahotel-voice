### Wonderful Family Stay at a Beautiful Miami Property

We recently stayed at the Andaz Miami with our family and a large group for a wedding and had a wonderful experience. We received a great room upgrade, and our room was fantastic. The hotel itself is beautiful, in a terrific location, and the overall quality of the property really impressed us.

We spent quite a bit of time at the pool and used the valet throughout our stay, and both were excellent.

The only downside was the cost of food and drinks, which was definitely on the high side. Our family buffet breakfast was around $150, and the bar prices were higher than we expected.

Overall, though, it was a great stay at a beautiful property. We really enjoyed being there with our family and the wedding party and would happily return.
