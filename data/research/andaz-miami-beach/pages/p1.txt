# A Global Lens on Miami Beach

A new perspective on an iconic destination, Andaz Miami Beach Resort & Spa reflects the culture and rhythm of our surroundings. Located along Collins Avenue in Mid-Beach, this oceanfront retreat merges Art Deco character with modern design, infused with local texture and detail. Sunlight spills across breezy interiors as ocean rhythms set the tone. With intuitive, personalized service, guests are invited to explore freely—for a stay that feels deeply local and effortlessly your own.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Experience refined coastal living in our 287 accommodations, including 64 stylish suites and expansive 2-bedroom retreats. Located in the heart of Mid-Beach, many offer private balconies with sweeping oceanfront views or glittering city skylines. Inside, soothing sea-inspired palettes, rich textures, and deep soaking tubs create a serene sanctuary. Whether it’s a quiet sunrise or an energized evening, your perfect Miami getaway starts here.

### Aguasal by José Andrés

Welcome to Aguasal, where Miami’s energy meets Mediterranean ease. From award-winning José Andrés Group, this signature restaurant offers a seaside celebration of bold coastal flavors, drawing inspiration from Miami to Mallorca. Fresh Florida bounty and fragrant herbs anchor a menu that feels both rooted and transportive.

Details + Reservations

### Bar Centro by José Andrés

Bar Centro, a vibrant collaboration with José Andrés, moves seamlessly from day to night. Mornings feature expertly brewed coffee, fresh juices and flaky croissants and in the evening, the space shifts into a sleek cocktail bar offering inventive drinks and shareable tapas. Great taste, day and night.

Details + Reservations

### Bazaar Meat by José Andrés

Bazaar Meat by José Andrés at Andaz Miami Beach Resort & Spa brings a theatrical new energy to the modern steakhouse. Here, exceptional meats, live-fire cooking and inventive technique take center stage, while the dining room becomes part of the experience, from large-format cuts cooking over open flame to tableside presentations and an interactive build-your-own martini ritual.

More than a steakhouse, Bazaar Meat is a playful, boundary-pushing celebration of Michelin-starred chef José Andrés’ imagination, bringing together premium Wagyu, modern tapas, unexpected seafood, vibrant vegetables, reinvented classics and imaginative cocktails. Inspired by Miami Beach’s spirit and coastal setting, every dish is designed to surprise, delight and create a memory guests will remember long after the final bite.

Details + Reservations

### Soleo Pool & Beach

Surrounded by bougainvillea and set between two sparkling pools, our poolside bar and café are made for sun-soaked moments. With 360-degree access to refreshing cocktails, fresh bites, and breezy lunches, its where laid-back days unfold effortlessly.

### Room Service

Whether you’re easing into the morning or winding down after dark, our in-room dining brings coastal comfort to your door. Choose from a thoughtful selection of fresh, seasonal dishes, perfect for breakfast in bed, a relaxed lunch, or a late-night craving. Your moment of indulgence, delivered.

## Pets Are Welcome

Your pets are welcome at Andaz Miami Beach Resort & Spa. Our dog-friendly rooms are stocked with amenities that include a doggy bowl, doggy cookie, doggy toy and pet-in-room door hanger. Pets must be on a leash at all times when in public spaces and are not allowed in restaurants, the pool deck, beach, Andaz Lounge or fitness center. Pets must not be left unattended. Three-day advance notice required. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
