## Aguasal by José Andrés

Now through October 31, experience Miami Spice at Aguasal by José Andrés. This high-energy coastal Mediterranean oasis brings together the flavors of José Andrés’s travels through Spain, Greece, and Morocco with Miami’s vibrant seafood, cocktail, and social culture.

Welcome to Aguasal, where Miami’s energy meets Mediterranean ease. From award-winning José Andrés Group, this signature restaurant offers a seaside celebration of bold coastal flavors, drawing inspiration from Miami to Mallorca. Fresh Florida bounty and fragrant herbs anchor a menu that feels both rooted and transportive.

## Bazaar Meat by José Andrés

Now Open!

Bazaar Meat by José Andrés at Andaz Miami Beach Resort & Spa brings a theatrical new energy to the modern steakhouse. Here, exceptional meats, live-fire cooking and inventive technique take center stage, while the dining room becomes part of the experience, from large-format cuts cooking over open flame to tableside presentations and an interactive build-your-own martini ritual.

More than a steakhouse, Bazaar Meat is a playful, boundary-pushing celebration of Michelin-starred chef José Andrés’ imagination, bringing together premium Wagyu, modern tapas, unexpected seafood, vibrant vegetables, reinvented classics and imaginative cocktails. Inspired by Miami Beach’s spirit and coastal setting, every dish is designed to surprise, delight and create a memory guests will remember long after the final bite.
