# RELAX INTO  THE SUBLIME

## RELAX INTO THE SUBLIME

Sunlight and moonlight drift over the interior space through floor-to-ceiling windows. Indoor-outdoor showers invite restorative reflections. Each villa has a private outdoor terrace and pool where you can let your thoughts drift, replaced by the sounds of birds and gently rustling palm fronds.

Inside, renowned designer Paulina Morán works with the interplay between nature and design, illuminating Mexican artisanal sensibilities in small details.

Comforts like separate his-and-hers water closets in the bath area, plush slippers and robes and a Nespresso machine add to your enjoyment.

## VILLAS

### Treetop Pool Villa \| Treetop Double Pool Villa

### 1,152 SQ. FT (107M²)

### ONE KING OR TWO QUEEN BEDS

### UP TO 3 ADULTS, OR 2 ADULTS + 2 CHILDREN

Each Treetop Pool Villa and Treetop Double Pool Villa is elevated, offering breathtaking views of the lush vegetation and lovingly tended grounds that can only be found from their second-floor perch. Enjoy the utmost privacy and seclusion of feeling like you are nestled in the treetops. Your private plunge pool invites you to take full advantage of your stunning surroundings.

## Jungle Pool Villa

### 1,152 SQ. FT (107M²)

### ONE KING SIZE BED

### UP TO 2 ADULTS AND 1 CHILD UNDER AGE 12

Tranquility cocoons the Jungle Pool Villa, the quiet splendor of the substantive greenery around the spacious private outdoor terrace and plunge pool hinting at the inside’s peacefulness. Rich, tropical woods and inviting textiles are a conduit to the region’s artisanal traditions, while a grand walk-in bathroom, a Chablé signature moon shower, an indulgent king bed and thoughtfully subtle comforts to heighten your sense of escape.

## Serenity Pool Villa

### 1,206 SQ. FT (112M²)

### ONE KING SIZE BED

### UP TO 2 GUESTS

The vibrant greens of tropical plants surround your Serenity Pool Villa, and from your private terrace with heated plunge pool, a sky reflected in breathtaking blue invites you to fully relax into the present. A fully private hideaway, your Serenity Pool Villa features décor that interplays with the light beaming in from large windows onto natural textiles and exceptional Mexican artisanal design. Comfort and calm surround you here, and everyday life slips out of your consciousness as you find yourself in the here and now.

## Presidential Villa - 3 BDR

### 5,813 SQ. FT (540M²)

### 2 KING ROOMS + 1 DOUBLE ROOM WITH 2 QUEEN BEDS

### UP TO 8 GUESTS, OR 6 ADULTS + 2 CHILDREN

The space, privacy and comfort of a breathtaking private home is yours at the Presidential Villa – 3 BDR. Its sumptuous two-story layout offers a master space and ample living area with a side-perimeter plunge pool, plus a separate room, terrace and plunge pool on the second floor for additional guests.
