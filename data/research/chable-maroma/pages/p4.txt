## Frequently Asked Questions

What is Chablé Maroma and where is it located?

Chablé Maroma is a luxury beachfront wellness resort located on Maroma Beach in the Riviera Maya, Mexico,
offering a blend of nature, privacy, and holistic experiences by the Caribbean Sea.

Where is Maroma Beach and why is it special?

Maroma Beach is located in the Riviera Maya and is widely considered one of the most beautiful beaches in Mexico,

known for its white sand and turquoise waters

How far is Chablé Maroma from Cancun International Airport?

The resort is approximately 40 minutes by car from Cancun International Airport (CUN), making it easily accessible

for international travelers

What makes Chablé Maroma different from other Riviera Maya resorts?

Chablé Maroma stands out for its combination of beachfront location, private villas, and a strong focus on wellness,

offering a more secluded and personalized experience compared to traditional large resorts.

Is Chablé Maroma a wellness resort?

Yes. The resort offers a comprehensive wellness approach, including spa treatments, fitness programs, mindfulness

activities, and personalized experiences inspired by nature and local traditions.

Does Chablé Maroma offer a spa experience?

Yes, the resort features a full-service spa focused on holistic well-being, offering treatments designed to relax,

restore, and reconnect guests with nature.

What types of accommodations are available at Chablé Maroma?

The resort offers private villas and suites designed with natural materials, open spaces, and direct connection to the

surrounding tropical environment

Do accommodations at Chablé Maroma include private pools?

Many villas include private plunge pools or outdoor spaces that enhance privacy and relaxation, depending on the

room category.

What dining options are available at Chablé Maroma?

Chablé Maroma offers dining experiences centered on contemporary Mexican cuisine, with a focus on fresh, local

ingredients and coastal flavors.

Is the food experience aligned with wellness?

Yes. The culinary concept integrates balanced nutrition and high-quality ingredients, supporting the overall wellness

experience.

What activities can guests enjoy at Chablé Maroma?

Guests can enjoy beach activities, yoga, fitness sessions, spa treatments, and curated experiences that connect

them with the natural surroundings of the Riviera Maya.

Is Chablé Maroma a good option for couples or honeymoons?

Yes, the resort is ideal for couples seeking privacy, beachfront relaxation, and personalized experiences, making it a

popular choice for romantic getaways.

Is Chablé Maroma family-friendly?

While families are welcome, the resort is especially suited for travelers looking for tranquility, exclusivity, and

wellness-oriented stays.

Does Chablé Maroma offer airport transportation?

Yes, private transportation services can be arranged for guests traveling to and from Cancun International Airport.

Is Chablé Maroma an all-inclusive resort?

No, the resort offers a tailored experience where guests can customize dining, wellness, and activities according to

their preferences.

What is the best time to visit Chablé Maroma?

The best time to visit is between December and April, when the weather in the Riviera Maya is warm, dry, and ideal

for beach activities.

What attractions are near Chablé Maroma?

Nearby attractions include Playa del Carmen, cenotes, eco-parks, and Mayan archaeological sites such as Tulum

and Cobá.

Is Chablé Maroma located directly on the beach?

Yes, the resort is located directly on Maroma Beach, offering immediate access to the Caribbean Sea.
