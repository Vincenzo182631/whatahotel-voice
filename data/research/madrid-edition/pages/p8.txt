# The Spa

**The Spa**

Share

Experience a five-sense wellness experience with personalized treatments that harmonize traditional wellness philosophies with cutting-edge techniques. Nestled within an intimate sanctuary adorned in neutral tones, dark timber floors and antique baroque candelabras, The Spa exudes an ambiance of refined tranquility in the heart of Madrid.

Open to both in-house and day guests, The Spa features a Finnish dry sauna, steam room, relaxation lounge, three treatment rooms and two suites for two.

Every detail is enhanced by Natura Bissé, the celebrated Spanish skincare brand. The curated treatment menu includes the EDITION Signature Massage, crafted with a custom black tea-scented massage oil by Le Labo, inviting guests on a multi-sensory journey into deep relaxation and rejuvenation.

Located within The Spa, Limited EDITION brings together curated Spanish craftwork, cutting-edge brands and unique collectables. From travel essentials to fragrance and art, discover pieces designed to inspire.

Monday - Sunday11:00 AM - 7:00 PM

To book an appointment or for further information please contact us at spa.madrid@editionhotels.com or +34 919 545 421.
