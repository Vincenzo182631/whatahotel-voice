# Rooms

The Madrid EDITION has 177 guest rooms, 21 spacious suites and two penthouses with stylishly modern decor and posh detailing by Ian Schrager. Enjoy luxury amenities and two store accomodation, including ceiling windows that feature stunning views of the Royal Palace, the Almudena cathedral and Gran Via.
