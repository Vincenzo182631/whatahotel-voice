# The Summer EDITION

**The Summer EDITION**

Experience summer in the heart of Madrid. Escape to an urban oasis where sun-drenched afternoons by the rooftop pool, refreshing Oroya Spritz cocktails, lush greenery at The Roof and golden sunsets set the tone for the season. From leisurely moments under the sun to vibrant evenings above the city’s skyline, every experience is designed to slow you down, indulge your senses and celebrate the art of summer living. A place where luxury, elegance and lifestyle come together, creating the ultimate summer escape.

**Welcome to the Madrid EDITION**

Guest Directory Room Service

EDITION Hotels is an unexpected and refreshing collection of individualised, customised, one-of-a-kind hotels which redefine the codes of traditional luxury.

Displaying the best of dining and entertainment, services and amenities, each EDITION property is completely unique, reflecting the best of the cultural and social milieu of its location and of the time. The EDITION experience and lifestyle explore the unprecedented intersection and the perfect balance between taste-making design and innovation and consistent, excellent service on a global scale.

Nestled in a serene square at the heart of the Spanish capital, and surrounded by historic architecture, The Madrid EDITION is a modern urban resort offering 200 guest rooms and suites, including two penthouses with unparalleled city views. For families and groups of up to 8 guests, we offer 77 interconnected rooms.

The hotel features five exceptional restaurants and bars: Oroya, a Peruvian restaurant by renowned Chef Diego Muñoz with an award-winning cocktail bar and terrace; Scarpetta, a celebrated Italian restaurant; MISTICA at EDITION, a sophisticated nightclub with a curated DJ lineup; Punch Room, an award-winning speakeasy-inspired cocktail bar serving specialty punches; and the Lobby Bar, a welcoming space featuring a pool table and authentic artwork.

The rooftop terrace is home to an outdoor infinity pool with sunbeds, exclusive cabanas, and a pool bar, while the state-of-the-art gym and The Spa provide a tranquil sanctuary for wellness. 24-hour room service, laundry service, and valet parking are available to ensure your convenience.

Our private meeting and event spaces include three versatile studios, one boardroom, and one ballroom, offering over 350 sqm of space for both large and intimate gatherings.

If you need any assistance or additional information, please don’t hesitate to contact our Guest Experience team.

We wish you an enchanting stay with us at The Madrid EDITION.

**Oroya & the Roof**

RESERVE WEBSITE

Located on the 4th floor of The Madrid EDITION is the hotel’s sanctuary overlooking the hustle and bustle of the city. Here, you will find Oroya, the restaurant helmed by Peruvian Chef Diego Muñoz, its awarded cocktail bar, and The Roof, the unparalleled rooftop terrace, open to residents and local community alike.

In a relaxed, convivial greenhouse-like setting framed by a blackened steel cage clad with climbing plants and coloured glass panels that cast bright patterns across the room, Oroya, the restaurant helmed by Peruvian Chef Diego Muñoz, serves a relaxed menu with family-style sharing plates such as tasteful ceviches, Peruvian-style fried calamari or sweet lucuma desserts, telling the story of Peru’s cultural history with influences from Spain, Africa, Italy, China and Japan. These can be washed down with pisco-led cocktails such like the famous Pisco Sour or a Capitán, signature cocktails or a glass of wine from the perfectly executed drinks menu by expert bartenders, that has led Oroya to win at the Top Cocktail Bars Awards in 2024, for the third consecutive year.

Oroya’s terrace, The Roof, is an outdoor seductive garden in the sky featuring a fireplace and a vine-covered pergola beneath which sits a bleach teak bar and rattan bar stools alongside a long oak table, perfect for the cocktail hour, lazy lunches, intimate candlelit dinners or light bites with friends and family.

For colder and rainy days, the service continues inside at Oroya, offering year-round day and night service for guests and visitors alike.

No reservations accepted for drinks only; walk-ins only, first come first served basis.

Dinner reservations suggested from 7:00 PM.

For additional information and group reservations of 8 people or more, please contact us at +34 919 54 54 50 or email us at oroya.madrid@editionhotels.com.

**Punch Room**

Tucked away from the rhythm of the city, Punch Room Madrid is an intimate cocktail bar that revives the ritual of punch—the precursor to the modern cocktail, first created aboard 17th-century trading ships and carried across cultures, palaces and generations.

As part of EDITION’s global Punch Room concept, the space takes on a distinct character in Madrid, where heritage and craft are reinterpreted through a contemporary lens, creating a warm, inviting setting dedicated to connection and experience.

“Legends & Likes” brings this journey into the present, exploring the figures that have shaped each era. Each creation becomes a liquid story, designed to be shared and discovered, reflecting the evolving nature of influence, inspiration and the ritual of drinking together.

Punch Room will be temporarily closed from July 18 – August 31, 2026.

No reservations accepted, walk-ins only.

For private events and group reservations, please contact our team at +34 919 54 54 20 or email us at events.mad@editionhotels.com.

**Lobby Bar**

A dynamic and sociable space offering a new kind of gathering place for the city’s most discerning residents and visitors, dressed in a mix of custom-made bespoke furnishings – such as the u-shaped sofa with dramatically back-lit shelves – and custom made, bespoke pieces by well-known designers arranged in intimate seating groups. This includes Jean-Michel Frank sofas and armchairs, recycled bronze stools from Maison Intègre, rope-covered chairs by Christian Astuguevieille, and a shapely pool table, which designer Emmanuel Levet Stenne sculpted from a single slab of white Bianco Neve matt marble. Behind the hand-crafted scalloped copper bar is a backlit caged bottle display and, as ever, there is a strong emphasis on warm, indirect lighting, which has been thoughtfully considered to give the room a warmth, softness and approachability while illuminating key pieces under direct spotlight.

For additional information please contact us at +34 919 54 54 20.
