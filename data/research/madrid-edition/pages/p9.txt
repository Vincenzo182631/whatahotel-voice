Plaza de Celenque 2

28013 Madrid, Spain

+34 919 54 54 20

## POOL

Share

Located on the rooftop of the fourth floor, in an intimate and exclusive setting, lies the largest infinity pool in the city. Perfect for relaxing and enjoying the best atmosphere, it offers unique views of Madrid, with the Teatro Real, the Royal Palace, the dome of the Almudena Cathedral, and Casa de Campo forming its backdrop.

During the day, guests can enjoy refreshing cocktails and a carefully curated selection of light bites, seated across 25 sunbeds and 5 cabanas, while the light enhances the elegance of Madrid’s architecture.

Madrid reveals all its charm at sunset, when the light transforms The Roof into a vibrant and sophisticated space, with the sun setting behind the pool’s horizon.

Please note that the pool will be seasonally closed after October 18, 2026.

Pool hours 11:00 AM – 7:00 PM

Food service 12:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Beverage service 11:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Maximum capacity of 45 people.

Access is on a first-come, first-served basis due to high demand during high season, especially on Saturdays and Sundays.

Sunbeds cannot be reserved.

Cabanas are available on request, including for locals and external guests, with priority given to hotel guests. Please consult rates and details via the link.

The garden features additional sun loungers. Maximum capacity of 20 people.

For food safety reasons, external food and beverages are not permitted.

Outside opening hours and in the absence of the lifeguard, access to the pool is not permitted.

If a cabana is left unoccupied for more than one hour, it will be released and made available for sale.
