An exciting new chapter begins! Effective November 2, 2026, Impression Isla Mujeres by Secrets will join The Unbound Collection by Hyatt and become **Impression Isla Mujeres**.

The resort will remain part of Hyatt’s Luxury portfolio, reflecting the continuity of its positioning and the elevated guest experience travelers have come to expect, while beginning an exciting new chapter under a brand that more naturally reflects its distinctive character.

The Unbound Collection by Hyatt is a global collection of luxury properties set in story-worthy locations, making it a natural home for a resort built gracefully into the natural landscape of Isla Mujeres’ southern coastline, perched atop a breathtaking coastal cliff, with access to a private beach whose naturally sheltered setting is seldom affected by sargassum.

Existing reservations will continue to be honored as booked, and guests will continue to enjoy the same luxury all-inclusive experience, with no changes to their confirmed rates.  There will be no impact to the guest experience and no disruption to the hotel’s operations with this brand change.

World of Hyatt members will continue to be able to earn and redeem points and enjoy applicable program benefits in accordance with World of Hyatt terms.

For any questions regarding your stay or upcoming reservation, please contact Hyatt’s Global Care Center at 1-866-237-3267.

## Impression Isla Mujeres by Secrets FAQs

- How do I reach the resort from Cancun?

Guests are transported via a resort-exclusive catamaran from Marina Hacienda del Mar. The trip is approximately 45 minutes. Reservations for the catamaran must be made at least 72 hours in advance.

- What dining options are available at the resort?

The resort offers seven dining options, including Spezia (Italian cuisine), UNIK The Kitchen (Mediterranean, Mexican, Asian fusion) and Sake (Pan-Asian specialties). Dining is reservation-free and there are options for room service, gluten-free and vegetarian meals.

- What are the available room types and amenities?

The resort features 125 luxurious suites with amenities such as butler service, in-suite wine refridgerators, private balconies, dual rain showers and premium bath amenities. Room categories range from Junior Suites to Presidential Suites with ocean views.

- What activities and entertainment are offered?

Activities include snorkeling, paddleboarding, yoga, cooking and mixology classes and golf access at Puerto Cancun Golf Club. There are also live music and wellness classes on-site.

- What unique features does the resort have?

Highlights include rooftop infinity pools, a thrilling waterslide into the ocean, overwater cabanas and the Hammam and Floatarium treatments at the spa. The resort also focuses on sustainability with eco-friendly practices.

- What are the spa and fitness offerings?

PURE SPA by Natura Bissé offers hydrotherapy, Hammam cleansing therapy and a Floatarium. The fitness center is equipped with cardio machines, weights and personal training sessions.

- Is the resort pet-friendly?

Yes, small dogs (under 22 lbs.) are welcome for a fee of $75 USD per night. Guests receive amenities like a dog bed, food bowls and waste bags. Dogs are not permitted in certain areas like the pool or spa.

- Are weddings and events hosted at the resort?

The resort offers wedding and event packages with venues like The Temple (wedding gazebo) and the rooftop terrace. Packages include tailored ceremonies, receptions and additional services like photography and spa treatments.

- What sustainability initiatives does the resort follow?

The resort minimizes single-use plastics, uses solar panels for heating water and partners with the Saving Our Sharks Foundation. Most furniture is locally made and organic products are provided to guests.

- What is the dress code for dining?

Restaurants have varying dress codes: Casual (e.g., sandals, no sleeveless shirts for men) and Casual Elegance (e.g., dress shoes, collared shirts, long pants for men). Flip-flops are not allowed in certain dining venues.
