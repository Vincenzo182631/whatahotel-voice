Turn up the day-to-night rhythm at W Bali – Seminyak. Start with Bali’s best brunch and all-day Mediterranean dining with Asian flair at Starfish Bloo. Then savor bold, flame-grilled steaks at FIRE. As the sun dips, sip crafted cocktails at W Lounge and soak up vibrant sunset energy at Wet Deck.

W Bali – Seminyak Culinary Happenings

### Fridays on Fire

Turn up the heat at FIRE with Fridays on Fire, an all you can eat set menu experience with vibrant beats and fire dancers. Expect bold, flame-kissed flavors with options like 200-day-grain-fed striploin, crispy pork belly, grilled line-caught fish, and more. Every Friday, 6 PM onwards.

Book Now

### Sunset Grill

As the sun dips over the Indian Ocean, Sunset Grill comes to life with a curated set menu showcasing freshly caught local fish and a spectacular seafood tower. Live cooking moments, chef-led specialties, and a tempting sweet station complete the experience against breathtaking sunset views. Every Saturday, 6 PM – 9 PM.

Book Now

### Sunday Brunch

Known as one of Bali's finest, the Sunday brunch at W delivers a vibrant dining experience by the beach. Indulge in global flavors, fresh seafood, live cooking stations, and crafted cocktails while enjoying lively entertainment. Every Sunday, 12 PM - 3.30 PM

Book Now
