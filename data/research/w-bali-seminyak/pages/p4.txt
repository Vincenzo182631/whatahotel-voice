# Own the Villa Life

## Villas

Experience ultimate luxury in our 75 Oasis Villas. Ranging from 225 to 624 sqm, these one- to three-bedroom retreats in Bali’s most vibrant neighborhood, Seminyak, offer a total privacy. Meticulously crafted with private plunge pools, lush gardens, and expansive sundecks, each villa is a sanctuary of serenity designed for an unparalleled island escape.

Villas

### Oasis One Bedroom Villa

The Oasis One-Bedroom Villa is a 225 sqm serene retreat inspired by Bali’s paddy fields, with calming green tones and warm golden accents. Designed for relaxation, it features spacious living areas and a master bedroom with the signature W bed, 350-thread-count Egyptian cotton sheets, plush pillows, along with a 49-inch TV and BOSE® sound system.

Book Now

Villas

### Oasis Two Bedroom Villa

Spanning 352 sqm, the Oasis Two-Bedroom Villa is a vibrant island retreat with playful yellows and golden browns inspired by Bali’s timber and earth tones. Expansive indoor-outdoor living is paired with a stylish lounge featuring a state-of-the-art entertainment system. Two serene bedrooms offer double the indulgence.

Book Now

Villas

### Oasis Three Bedroom Villa

At 624 sqm, our Oasis Three-Bedroom Villa is a high-energy haven for elevated island living. Amplify your stay across expansive living zones and a landscaped sanctuary offering total privacy. Whether recharging in your retreat or entertaining in style, this is resort living redefined beyond your wildest fantasies.

Curated Villa Experiences

Private Dining

Turn your villa into your own private dining scene. From romantic dinners to special celebrations, everything is tailored to your mood. Enjoy freshly prepared dishes by our talented chefs, available 24/7, all in the comfort of your villa.

Floating Feast

Start your day with a floating breakfast in your private pool. A refined spread of fresh, chef-crafted selections served on water, designed for slow, indulgent mornings. Whether it’s a romantic start or simply because, we set the scene your way.

In-Villa Barbecue

Bring the heat to your villa with a curated barbecue experience. Indulge in premium cuts and rich, smoky flavors, expertly grilled live by our chef. Perfect for intimate nights or easygoing get-togethers, all enjoyed in total privacy.
