# Welcome to W Bali - Seminyak

## Enjoy our vibrant Seminyak beach resort and spa

Located in the pulse of Bali’s fashion and nightlife scene, our Green Key-certified escape plays by a different set of rules. Dive into our rice field-inspired WET® pool, or find your glow at AWAY® Spa and FIT gym. Taste the island’s most famous brunch at Starfish Bloo and turn up the heat at FIRE, our signature steakhouse. Take in garden views or sweeping panoramas of the Indian Ocean from your rooms...See More and suites, or retreat to the privacy of your villa with a private pool. Soak in a terrazzo bathtub, slip into a robe, order in-room dining, and sink into 350-thread-count cotton linens. For anything else, just ask Whatever/Whenever®.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Bali - Seminyak?

The check-in time at W Bali - Seminyak is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Bali - Seminyak allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at W Bali - Seminyak.

What are the parking options at W Bali - Seminyak?

On-site parking is available at W Bali - Seminyak. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Bali - Seminyak have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Bali - Seminyak has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Bali - Seminyak booking.

What is the closest airport near W Bali - Seminyak?

The closest airport near W Bali - Seminyak is I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS). DPS is located approximately 15.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Bali - Seminyak have an airport shuttle to I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS)?

Yes, W Bali - Seminyak has an airport shuttle to I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS).

Does W Bali - Seminyak have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Bali - Seminyak does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
