Dining at the Heart of the Laurus

### Laurus Table, Where Gathering, Flavour, and Place Come Together

Helmed by Michelin‑starred chef Fabrizio Ferrari, Laurus Table presents a refined blend of Amalfi Coast elegance and Singapore’s coastal bounty. Drawing inspiration from Southern Italy, the menu highlights sustainable seafood, handmade pastas, and clean, expressive coastal dishes crafted with finesse. Ingredients are thoughtfully sourced, featuring sustainably farmed fish from local waters, organic...See More produce from regional growers, and herbs handpicked from The Laurus’ own garden. The space is designed for warmth and connection, with sustainable details throughout, including tableware crafted from repurposed local wood. From an international breakfast with house-baked breads, locally made yoghurt, and farm-fresh herbs, to evening offerings of wood‑fired seafood and grilled dishes, Laurus Table offers a dining experience that reflects its coastal setting.See Less

Laurus Table: Signature Dishes

### Branzino in Crosta Di Sale, Finocchietto E Li

A whole sustainably farmed seabass is gently baked in a crust of sea salt, locking in natural moisture while concentrating aroma and flavour. Fragrant wild fennel and fresh lemon infuse the fish with bright, herbaceous notes, complemented by brick‑oven roasted seasonal vegetables. The result is a dish defined by balance, simplicity, and thoughtful preparation, allowing the aromatic flavours to take centre stage.

### Catalana Di Gamberi

Inspired by coastal Sicilian tradition and simplicity and sunlit, Catalana di Gamberi features Sicilian prawns gently flame‑roasted in a brick oven, layered over a bright puree of yellow tomato and sweet orange. Fresh red cherry tomatoes, crisp julienned celery, and pickled red onion add contrast and lift, finished with fragrant basil oil and a hint of crushed peperoncino for balance, freshness, and subtle heat.

### Panzanella Di Polpo

Panzanella di Polpo pairs slow‑cooked octopus, tender and delicately seasoned, with a traditional Tuscan panzanella salad of soaked bread, ripe tomatoes, and fresh herbs. The octopus brings depth and gentle sweetness, while bright candied lemon adds lift and contrast. Textural, balanced, and refreshed by acidity, the dish offers a light yet satisfying expression of coastal Italian tradition.

### Mafaldine Al Burro, Limone E Alici

Maldine al Burro, Limone e Alici brings together silky pasta with a creamy, tangy butter sauce brightened by Amalfi lemon. A delicate splash of limoncello lifts the palate with aromatic freshness, while prized Cetara anchovies add savoury depth and balance. Finished with fresh wild fennel, the dish offers a comforting harmony of richness, citrus, and a subtle seaside note, simple, expressive, and satisfying.

### Laurus Table Signature Calamansi Yoghurt

The Laurus Signature Yoghurt pairs velvety yoghurt with a bright calamansi curd, balancing gentle creaminess with refreshing citrus notes. Developed with a local artisan producer, it highlights quality ingredients and careful preparation, offering a light, clean finish.

### Scialatielli ai Frutti di Mare al cartoccio

Scialatielli ai Frutti di Mare al Cartoccio brings together scialatielli pasta sautéed with a selection of sustainably sourced seafood, gently cooked to preserve freshness and natural sweetness. Sealed in parchment and finished in a rich, aromatic seafood sauce, the dish captures the essence of the coast, briny, comforting, and deeply savoury, allowing flavours to mingle and unfold with warmth and depth.

In-Room Dining

## Dine in the Comfort of Your Suite

In‑room dining at The Laurus invites you to enjoy thoughtfully curated dishes from the comfort and privacy of your suite. Using the finest seasonal ingredients, each meal reflects careful craft, refined balance, and a deep respect for sustainable, high-quality ingredients.

Whether you’re easing into the day with a leisurely breakfast, pausing for a quiet meal between moments of rest, or settling in for a relaxed evening, our in-room dining allows you to shape your dining experience entirely around your own rhythm. There’s no need to rush, only the freedom to linger, savour, and enjoy each dish at your own pace.

Delivered with warmth and care, in‑room dining transforms your suite into a personal dining retreat. From elegantly simple to exquisitely intricate, each dish is designed to delight a variety of palates in a setting that feels unhurried, familiar, and effortlessly your own, where comfort takes precedence and every moment is designed to feel just right.

A Place To Pause, Explore, and Linger

## The Collector’s Bar, Curated Drinks and Collected Stories

The Collector’s Bar reflects the spirit of a well‑travelled collector, where heritage, craft, and curiosity meet in an intimate setting. Colonial‑inspired details, curated artworks, and tropical accents come together to create a space that feels refined yet inviting, designed for conversation and discovery from morning tea through evening cocktails.

The beverage menu is guided by a garden‑forward approach, placing freshness, sustainability, and balance at its core. Cocktails and mocktails feature herbs and botanicals from the Chef’s Herb Garden, bringing a direct connection between the landscape and the glass.

Inspired by Sentosa’s island setting, flavour cues draw from coastal freshness and tropical botanicals, shaping a distinct and cohesive identity. The Collector’s Bar invites you to pause and explore, with each visit unfolding at your own pace.

## Relaxed Tropical Poolside Bar Experience

The Pool Bar offers a relaxed yet refined setting, where the open-air space blends seamlessly with the resort’s tranquil pool landscape—creating an inviting space for crafted beverages and light bites. Framed by water, foliage, and natural daylight, it embodies the ease of tropical resort living.

At its heart, the pool is enveloped by verdant greenery, creating a serene backdrop that enhances the Pool Bar experience. Spanning 895 sqm, it offers secluded corners for quiet retreat alongside open, sunlit areas for leisurely dips—an inviting oasis that captures the essence of relaxed tropical living.
