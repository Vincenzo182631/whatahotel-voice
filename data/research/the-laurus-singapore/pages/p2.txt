Layered with bespoke furnishings, curated art, and thoughtful details, each space invites a slower,...See More more personal rhythm of stay. ## Premier King Pool View

At 72 sqm, the Premier King Pool View suite unfolds as a spacious, light‑filled retreat where comfort and quiet indulgence come naturally.

Floor‑to‑ceiling windows frame calming views of lush greenery and the tranquil pool below, setting a peaceful tone the moment you enter the suite. Inside, the living room flows seamlessly into a sunlit dining area, creating an inviting setting for slow mornings with coffee, leisurely afternoons of rest, and intimate evenings spent unwinding at your own pace.

The marble‑accented bathroom adds a sense of gentle indulgence, offering a serene space to retreat into after a day of exploration. Every surface, texture, and element is carefully considered, balancing refinement with ease. Thoughtfully composed and effortlessly comfortable, the Premier King Pool View suite invites you to slow down, settle in, and enjoy a restorative stay that feels private, personal, and quietly luxurious, like a place you instinctively belong to.

Comfort and Space, Thoughtfully Designed

### Private Suites Shaped by Heritage, Art, and Thoughtful Craft

At The Laurus, we imagine our suites as private chambers within a well‑travelled Singaporean collector's home. Each space is layered and intimate, as heritage and modernity blends together. Curated objects, botanical accents, and tactile Southeast Asian textures reflect the island’s cultural richness, while soft lighting and considered materials create warmth and calm. Art plays a meaningful role in...See More our suites. In partnership with ART:DIS, inclusivity and creative expression is part of the experience, while Natalie Holstad's abstract works adds emotional depth through expressive, colour‑rich compositions. Photographs by Isabelle Lim offer contemplative views of Sentosa’s natural landscape while bespoke artistic details such as the Presidential Suite's hand‑painted ceiling mural, add narrative and craftsmanship, celebrating nature, refinement, and detail. Together, these thoughtful layers create suites that feel personal, inviting you to slow down, reflect, and feel truly at home.See Less

Moments of Comfort, Woven Into Every Stay

### A Thoughtfully Designed Bathing Space

Enjoy the luxury of space and choice with a separate bathtub and shower, each thoughtfully designed to suit different moments of your day. Whether you enjoy a long, restorative soak in the evening or begin your morning with an invigorating shower, every experience provides comfort and daily rituals feel unhurried and personal, inviting you to slow down and make each moment entirely your own.

### In-Room Minibar

Each day, your minibar is thoughtfully replenished with a selection of refreshments by local artisans and producers. From heritage‑inspired biscuits and Southeast Asian coffee to locally crafted beers, every item reflects a connection to place, culture, and mindful living. Carefully curated, these everyday comforts invite you to pause, indulge, and enjoy a thoughtful moment within the privacy of your suite.

### A Pillow Selection Tailored to You

Rest comfortably with a thoughtfully curated pillow menu designed to suit your individual preferences. From ergonomic and neck‑support options to soothing lavender, breathable buckwheat, and soft micro‑gel pillows, each choice offers a different balance of support, texture, and comfort. Designed to help you settle in with ease, every choice supports a more restful, personalised night’s sleep.

### A Signature Scent, Distinctly The Laurus

Voyage Céleste is The Laurus' signature scent created exclusively by Maison 21G. Inspired by Sentosa’s landscape, it opens with crisp citrus and aromatic green notes, evoking fresh air and lush foliage, before soft coastal nuances settle into a warm, elegant finish. Gently diffused throughout the resort, the scent creates a sensory presence, anchoring your stay in place and memory beyond each passing moment.

### Inclusive Stays: Mobility-Accessible Suites

A selection of rooms at The Laurus is thoughtfully designed for guests requiring additional mobility support. These accessible rooms offer bathroom options with either a bathtub or roll-in shower. Each room supports ease of movement while preserving our refined design and thoughtful touches, with features seamlessly integrated for a comfortable stay.
