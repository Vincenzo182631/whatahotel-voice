Movement, Well Equipped

## A State‑of‑the‑Art Fitness Centre

At The Laurus, the Fitness Centre is designed for energising workouts and everyday movement, offering a comfortable space to maintain your routine while travelling.

Equipped with a selection of state‑of‑the‑art strength and cardio equipment, it supports a wide range of training styles, from focused sessions to lighter movement.

Overlooking tropical greenery, the space brings an added sense of openness to your workout, creating a more engaging and considered environment.

A Collector’s Journey Through Place and Heritage

## Destination Tour: The Collector’s Trail Through the Laurus

A curated journey through The Laurus, this guided trail explores the resort as an expression of place, culture, and craft. Set within Sentosa’s lush landscape, it reveals how nature, design, and heritage come together as a contemporary collector’s home.

As you move through its spaces, art, nature, and storytelling unfold subtly—inviting quiet discovery and a deeper connection to Singapore’s cultural and natural tapestry.

Unhurried Moments by the Water

### The Pool, a Tranquil Oasis at the Heart of the Laurus

At the heart of the resort, the pool offers a tranquil setting framed by lush greenery and natural light throughout the day. Designed for both leisurely swims and relaxed lounging, its generous layout balances open stretches with more private corners. Sun‑dappled seating invites moments of pause or quiet conversation, while the surrounding landscape adds a sense of calm and privacy. Move easily between time in the water and time at rest, with the space supporting different ways to unwind.

Our Epicurean Programmes

### A Taste of Tradition

In the lobby, A Taste of Tradition is a daily epicurean moment where guests are invited to indulge in refined local delicacies – an elegant showcase rooted in the rich culinary traditions of Singapore and its region. From artisanal Nyonya kueh to pastries with flavours inspired by the region, each treat celebrates our locale’s culinary heritage. This experience is available daily and complimentary for guests.

### Cocoa Journeys

Embark on a Cocoa Journey, a weekly chocolate and beverage pairing experience showcasing the craft of sustainably sourced chocolatiers. This epicurean moment highlights locally inspired flavours like pandan, gula melaka, and rose, paired with aromatic heritage-style beverages, including velvety Kopi Tarik variations and milk tea inspired by Bubur Cha Cha, a popular traditional Peranakan dessert.

### The Laurus Kitchen Sessions

The Laurus Kitchen Sessions begin with a guided walk through the Chef’s Herb Garden, followed by a bespoke three‑course dining experience in Laurus Table’s private kitchen. Guests are invited to take part in select moments of preparation alongside the chef, in an intimate setting that weaves together storytelling, culinary craft, and thoughtfully sourced ingredients.

A Sanctuary for Restoration and Balance

## BODHI Spa

At BODHI Spa, restoration unfolds through mindful touch, thoughtful rituals, and a deep respect for balance. Set within an intimate, softly-lit space, the spa offers a quiet retreat from modern pace, inviting you to slow down and release any hidden tension.

Blending time‑honoured healing traditions with contemporary wellness practices, each experience is thoughtfully personalised to support both physical renewal and mental ease. Within serene treatment rooms and private VIP suites, holistic therapies bring the body into relaxed alignment and the mind into calm focus. Signature offerings such as the Zerobody Dry Float and Recovery Red Light Pod encourage deep relaxation, cellular renewal, and recovery.

Beyond the treatment room, detoxifying heat therapies, gentle warming air rituals, and invigorating contrast showers restore natural rhythm and balance. At BODHI Spa, wellness is not rushed or prescribed, it is a personal journey, shaped with intention and lasting care.

The Laurus: Within Sentosa’s Nature Landscape

### Sentosa’s Nature and History, Explored at an Unhurried Pace

Nestled within the heart of Sentosa, The Laurus is surrounded by a landscape where nature and history exist in quiet dialogue. Beyond the resort, a network of trails invites you to explore the island’s rich history and biodiversity at your own pace. The Historical Trail traces Sentosa’s transformation, passing remnants of its military past from former barracks and officers’ quarters to sites shaped...See More by wartime history. Along the Mount Imbiah Nature Trail, lush greenery and diverse flora create a tranquil escape, with moments of discovery unfolding through forest paths, the Sentosa Nature Discovery gallery, and traces of World War II‑era structures. For a longer exploration, the Coastal Trail follows the island’s edge, weaving past historic landmarks and panoramic sea views near Fort Siloso. Together, these paths reveal Sentosa as a living landscape shaped by memory, resilience, and the natural world, offering space to slow down and connect more deeply with history and nature.See Less

Yes. The Laurus, a Luxury Collection Resort, Singapore offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Laurus, a Luxury Collection Resort, Singapore have a pool on-site?
