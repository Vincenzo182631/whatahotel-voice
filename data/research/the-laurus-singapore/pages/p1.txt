# Welcome to The Laurus, a Luxury Collection Resort, Singapore

## Discover an Oasis of Unparalleled Luxury in Singapore

The Laurus curates timeless journeys, where every detail—rooted in heritage-inspired design and meaningful encounters—reflects a love for story, beauty, and place.​​Drawing inspiration from the spirit...See More of a well-travelled collector’s home, the resort weaves together art, objects, and textures from diverse eras and cultures. Each element invites guests to linger, explore, and imagine. With storied elegance, the interiors blend Peranakan and colonial influences, complemented by tropical motifs that echo Sentosa’s natural charm.​​Nestled within one of Asia’s most iconic integrated resorts, The Laurus offers a gateway to discovery: from the marine wonders of the Singapore Oceanarium to adrenaline-pumping adventures, tranquil nature walks, and vibrant dining experiences. ## The Gateway to the Resort’s Most Coveted Experiences

Your stay at The Laurus is part of a larger journey, extending beyond the tranquillity of your suite into the vibrant world of Resorts World Sentosa. Perfectly positioned within the destination, The Laurus offers seamless access to award-winning dining, wellness experiences, world-class attractions and immersive entertainment.

From unforgettable moments at Singapore Oceanarium and Universal Studios Singapore to exclusive resort happenings and family-friendly adventures, a wealth of discoveries awaits just steps away. Beyond the excitement, The Laurus remains a serene retreat, a place to pause, reflect and recharge between moments of wonder.

A Collector’s Journey Through Place and Heritage

## Destination Tour: The Collector’s Trail Through the Laurus

A curated journey through The Laurus, this guided tour invites you to explore the resort as an expression of place, culture and craft. Set amidst Sentosa’s lush landscape, the trail reveals how tropical biodiversity, architectural elegance and heritage‑inspired design come together to shape The Laurus as a collector’s home.

Each step along the journey reveals subtle details that invite closer attention and appreciation. As you move through its thoughtfully composed spaces, art, nature, and storytelling unfold in quiet layers, reflecting a careful balance between past and present. Native flora and fauna sit alongside refined design details, offering moments of sensory discovery and quiet exploration along the way.

This journey invites a deeper connection to Singapore’s cultural and natural tapestry, where heritage is interpreted through a contemporary lens. Exploration here feels personal and unhurried, an invitation for you to see, feel and understand the collector’s eye for beauty.

Sustainability

## Our Commitment

Sustainability lies at the heart of The Laurus story.  Rooted in a philosophy of care for the community, for craft, and for the planet, The Laurus invites guests into a world where nature, culture, and community are seamlessly interwoven.

To honour Sentosa’s natural beauty and cultural richness, The Laurus has established thoughtful partnerships with local artisans, growers, and changemakers. From in-room amenities crafted with sustainable materials to menus that spotlight locally sourced ingredients, paintings by local artists to tableware made from repurposed wood. Every detail reflects our commitment to reducing waste, championing circularity, and supporting local enterprises.
