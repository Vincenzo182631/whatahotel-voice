### A Moment of Matcha

Each afternoon, hotel guests are invited to savour a bowl of premium matcha accompanied by seasonal sweets. Upon request, a hands-on experience learning to prepare matcha yourself can be arranged.

### Evening aperitif

Indulge in nightly complimentary evening aperitifs, with a choice ranging from Champagne and wine to sparkling sake. An array of snacks is also offered, meticulously crafted by Director of Culinary and F&B Operations Tatsuya Ozawa using locally sourced ingredients.

### Kyoto breakfast

Kickstart the morning with a nourishing Japanese breakfast. This healthy and long-lived diet includes fermented foods like pickles and miso soup, along with a wide array of proteins, vitamins, minerals, and fibre. Small dishes, including sashimi and obanzai, are elegantly presented in a wooden box while for the main course, a choice of Kuroge Wagyu beef or grilled fish is served with clay pot rice or soymilk porridge made with soy milk from Toyoukeya.
