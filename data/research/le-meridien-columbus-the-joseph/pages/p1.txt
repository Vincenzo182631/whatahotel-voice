# Welcome to Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph

## Get comfortable at our boutique hotel in Columbus, Ohio

Welcome to your newest adventure at Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph. Part of the thriving Short North Arts District, our boutique hotel in downtown Columbus is steps from High Street's acclaimed restaurants,...See More shops and galleries. As a hotel near Short North Columbus, we place you close to the neighborhood's vibrant culture and attractions. Within a mile of our spa hotel, you'll find Italian Village, North Market, the Greater Columbus Convention Center, Nationwide Arena and downtown Columbus. Experience a downtown Columbus, OH, hotel with seasonal, farm-to-table fare at The Guild House, cocktails at Soul at the Joseph, personalized spa suites and a 24-hour fitness centre. Our Columbus, OH, hotel also offers four event rooms with planning and catering services. Unwind in sophisticated rooms or suites featuring handcrafted décor, floor-to-ceiling windows, high-speed Wi‑Fi, LCD TVs, a minibar and room service.See Less

Experience a new level of comfort with our recent renovations—featuring a state-of-the-art fitness center with Technogym equipment and Peloton bikes, plus refreshed guest rooms with new mattresses, modern carpet and accents, and Nespresso machines in every room.

### Short North Arts District

### Over 500 Pieces on Display

Hotel features two art programmes (one of which is from the Owner's private collection) and over 500 total pieces are on display in the hotel. Additionally, Pizzuti Collection serves as Unlock Art Partner.

### Unlock Art

Le Méridien Offers Complimentary Access To Celebrated Galleries And Institutions For The Culturally Curious Traveler. Through the Unlock Art™ programme Le Méridien partners with cultural centres around the world to offer guests local, inspiring experiences, simply by presenting the Unlock Art™ room key.

Le Meridien Columbus, The Joseph has partnered with the Columbus Museum of Art (Downtown) and the Columbus Museum of Art at The Pizzuti (Short North). Guests can gain complimentary general admission access by presenting their hotel key card.

Art Programmes

### Joseph Editions, Ohio Portfolio

The Joseph Editions, Ohio Portfolio features limited edition master printed originals created by 15 contemporary artists, each with an Ohio connection. The Ohio Portfolio is currently on view in the guestrooms of Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph hotel and all pieces are available for purchase.

### Private Art Collection of Ron and Ann Pizzuti

The hotel features dramatic pieces on loan from the private art collection of Ron and Ann Pizzuti. These pieces are on view throughout the bar and reception areas and include international artists such as KAWS, Rob Wynne, Nari Ward and Fred Wilson.

Pet Policy

Le Méridien's pet care program ensures your furry friends are pampered just as much as you. Pets receive a bed, bowls, mat, welcome kit, and clean-up supplies. Concierge can recommend groomers, parks & shops.

Non-Refundable Pet Fee Per Night: $50, Max. Number of Pets (up to 100lbs each) in Room: 2

Smoke-Free Policy

We are a 100% smoke-free hotel. Smoking or vaping is not permitted in any guest rooms or indoor public areas. If smoking or vaping is detected in a guest room, a $500 smoking fee will be charged per incident to the room’s folio. We appreciate your cooperation in maintaining a smoke-free environment.

Cash Free

Cash is not accepted. A valid credit or debit card is required for payment.

Community Fee Notice

A mandatory 1% Community Fee applies to all room charges and incidentals (except for spa service and valet). This fee is additional to taxes and not subject to discounts or waivers. Fees may change without notice.

Our artistically inspired boutique hotels invite you to discover the destination and absorb the local culture of your surroundings. Celebrate the beauty of art, carefully crafted cuisine, and connect with the community while you call our sophisticated accommodations home.

The le méridien brand was established in paris in 1972 and was welcomed into the starwood portfolio in 2005. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 100.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph?

Off-site valet parking is available at Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph. The daily valet fee is $50.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph is John Glenn Columbus International Airport (CMH). CMH is located approximately 8.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
