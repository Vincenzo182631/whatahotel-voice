### Explore Columbus

Ideally located in the heart of the Short North Arts District, our hotel places you steps from vibrant galleries, unique boutiques, and some of Columbus’s top dining. Stroll to Goodale Park, Nationwide Arena, the Greater Columbus Convention Center, and Scotts Miracle-Gro Field, or explore nearby destinations like The Ohio State University and the Ohio Statehouse. Just a few miles from John Glenn International Airport, your gateway to the best of Columbus starts here.

### Explore the North Arts District

### Greater Columbus Convention Center

The Greater Columbus Convention Center is notable for its distinct architecture and ability to host a variety of events. Located in the center of Columbus, the convention center serves as a meeting point for all of Columbus' unique neighborhoods.

### North Market

At the renowned North Market in Columbus, discover fresh, authentic, local food. The knowledgeable merchants are happy provide unparalleled customer service and assist with all of their patrons needs, whether it is providing the freshest ingredients or crafting artisan goodies onsite or to take home. North Market showcases the best of the best.

### German Village

A historic neighborhood just south of downtown Columbus, German Village is home to a rich history, historic architecture, and a friendly sense of community.

### Huntington Ballpark

Located in the Arena District in downtown Columbus, Huntington Park is home to the Columbus Clippers baseball team, the Triple-A affiliate of the Cleveland Indians.

### Italian Village

Discover Italian Village, one of Columbus’s most historic and dynamic neighborhoods. Just moments away, this vibrant district blends charming brick streets and rich heritage with a modern energy driven by local breweries, acclaimed restaurants, art spaces, and creative studios.

### Columbus Museum of Art at The Pizzuti (Short North)

Le Méridien hotels and resorts have partnered with leading cultural centers to encourage our guests to explore the best their destinations have to offer. The Columbus Museum of Art at The Pizzuti (Short North) is located one block from the hotel. Guests gain complimentary general admission.

### Ohio State University

Home of the Buckeyes and one of the nation's largest universities, stop by campus for a visit as a prospective student, alumni, or supporting fan. Located in the lively city of Columbus, Ohio State University is a member of the Big Ten conference and a top university in the region. The campus is known for its passion for football, incredible school spirit, and a diverse student body.

### Ohio Theatre

Located in Downtown Columbus on the site of the old Columbus City Hall, the Ohio Theatre was designed by the noted theatre architect Thomas W. Lamb. The breathtaking décor and opulent Spanish-Baroque architecture and design make any night out at the Ohio Theatre an event to remember. The stunning 2,791 seat theatre hosts world-class dancers, musicians, and entertainers year round. A night at the theatre is sure to be a memorable Columbus experience.

### ScottsMiracle-Gro Field

Located less than two miles from the hotel, Scotts Miracle-Gro Field is home to Major League Soccer’s Columbus Crew and a must-visit destination for sports fans and visitors alike. Experience the electric energy of match day, enjoy sweeping skyline views, and immerse yourself in one of the city’s most exciting and modern venues.

### Nationwide Arena

Concerts, sporting events, and other performances, a ticket to Nationwide Arena opens the door to Columbus entertainment. The Arena is home to the Columbus Blue Jackets and is the best spot to catch the action on the ice. Meeting spaces and dining outlets complete the environment to create the full entertainment experience.

### Schottenstein Center

Value City Arena at The Jerome Schottenstein Center is Ohio State University's state-of-the-art, modern athletic facility for athletes, coaches, and Buckeye fans. The venue can seat up to 20,000 visitors and is home to Ohio State Men's and Women's Basketball, Men's Ice Hockey, and hosts numerous concerts and other Columbus events. Get your tickets for the next event and experience the arena culture of Columbus.

### Goodale Park

Located just one block from the hotel, Goodale Park is surrounded by Victorian Village, a cluster of homes with charming architectural features. The park, which is the oldest in Columbus and among the oldest in the United States, contains an expansive playground and tennis courts. Its main attraction is the view of the city skyline and the serenity offered alongside a scenic pond, charming gazebo and historic shelter. A walk through Goodale Park is a refreshing experience.
