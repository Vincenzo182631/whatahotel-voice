# Welcome to Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort

## Experience a World-Renowned Modern Resort on Waikiki Beach

Vivid by day, vibrant by night: Experience the energy of Waikiki at the Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort. Step into our exceptional Waikiki resort, which features recently renovated rooms and suites with...See More spectacular ocean views. Located on Waikiki Beach, the hotel is just minutes from some of Honolulu's top attractions like Leahi (Diamond Head State Monument) and offers easy access to upscale shopping, dining, and family-friendly activities. After a day exploring Honolulu, relax in our Waikiki hotel's award-winning outdoor pools, including Edge Infinity Pool, the longest oceanfront infinity pool in North America. Close out the day by working out in our modern fitness center or dining on globally inspired cuisine at one of our Waikiki hotel’s oceanfront restaurants. If you're planning an event, Sheraton Waikiki offers one of Honolulu’s largest ballrooms, 16 meeting rooms, and dynamic indoor and outdoor event spaces. ## Learn to Surf on Waikiki Beach

Did you know that one of the best places in Hawaii to learn how to surf is along Waikiki Beach? Since the 1920s, the Beach Boys have taught visitors how to surf Waikiki's long and gentle surf breaks. Book a lesson, try stand-up paddleboarding, or take an exhilarating outrigger canoe ride with Waikiki Beach Services.

## Sunrise Flow and Glow Flow

Don't miss Floating Yoga in the Edge Infinity Pool, the newest activity at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort. The morning class is held before the Infinity Pool opens, with instructors guiding you through sun salutations and core stretches on stable, anchored boards, with stunning views of Diamond Head in the background. The evening class takes place after hours, with the pool illuminated by underwater LED lights. Book now to secure your spot.

## Waikiki Beach Information

Did you know Waikiki is home to seven beaches? The soft white sands of Waikiki Beach front Sheraton Waikiki, The Royal Hawaiian and the Moana Surfrider. Kuhio Beach is the long stretch of sand along Kalakaua Avenue, anchored by the Duke Kahanamoku statue. Further east, past the Waikiki Wall, is Queen’s Beach, San Souci Beach, and Kaimana Beach. On the west end of Waikiki, you’ll find Fort DeRussy Beach and Duke Kahanamoku Beach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sheraton Waikiki offer late check out?

Yes, Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort offers late check-out, subject to availability. The fee may vary.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Waikiki?

We offer self-parking on-site for a fee of $55 a day. Valet parking is also available for $65 a day.

Is Sheraton Waikiki directly on the beach?

Yes, Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort is located on Waikiki Beach, with two direct access points from Helumoa Family Pool and near Edge Pool Bar.

Are there amenities such as robes and slippers available in the room?

Robes and slippers are delivered upon request.

What are pool hours at Sheraton Waikiki?

Our pools are open from 8 am to 8 pm. Our Towel Desks are open from 8 am to 6 pm.

Is there laundry service?

Yes, there is drycleaning available, and two coin-operated laundry facilities are located on the 5th and 18th floor of Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort.

Where are the water station and ice machine located?

It is located on each floor, in front of the 43 series.

Where is the fitness center located?

Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort’s gym/fitness center is located on the 4th floor. The resort also offers a number of complimentary wellness classes.

How can I report a lost item?

You can provide the details by going to https://www.chargerback.com/ We will review your lost item report and send you an email/text with an update. If your item has been found, the message will provide instructions for getting it back. If your item hasn't been found yet, we will keep your report active and contact you again if it is.

Are there any flat rate deals on transportation from the hotel to the airport?

In addition to our airport shuttles that can be booked ahead of time, our taxi partners TheCab offer a flat fee of $45 from our hotel to HNL airport. This is available both on-demand, and can also be pre-arranged either through our Concierge Desk or the TheCab’s phone number at (808) 422-2222. Additional bags and oversized or heavy baggage may incur additional fees.

How do I contact the concierge at Sheraton Waikiki?

Book your activities online at https://sheratonwaikiki.activiter.com/, call our main line and ask to be transferred to the concierge, or email our concierge team for personalized assistance planning activities and experiences across Oahu.

What is the minimum age a guest must be to check in at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

The guest must be 18 years old to check in at our Waikiki resort.

Do you accept Apple Pay at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

We do not accept Apple Pay as a payment option.

Is there luggage storage available at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

We would be happy to help store luggage at our bell desk. However, overnight storage of luggage is not available.

Is there a resort fee at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

Yes, please visit our What to Expect page to learn about what's included in the Resort Fee.

Do you offer babysitting services at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

Poppins Keiki Hawaii is located on our 4th floor and offers professional babysitting services and a kids program. Please visit their website for more details.

Is Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort located directly on Waikiki Beach?

Yes, our Waikiki resort is located on the beach.

What are the easiest ways to get to Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

Please call the main line and we will transfer you to the concierge for assistance.
