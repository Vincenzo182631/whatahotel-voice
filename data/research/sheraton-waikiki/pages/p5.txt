# Experiences at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort

## Experiences

Immerse yourself in a variety of Waikiki resort activities at our hotel. Located near Waikiki Beach in Hawaii, you'll find cultural activities, shopping, ocean adventures, and beachfront activities that...See More will make memories for a lifetime.See Less

### Chase Your Next Outdoor Adventure

The hotel's beachfront location near Kalakaua Avenue in Waikiki is perfectly placed for you to discover the spirit of Hawaii. Surf Waikiki’s classic rolling waves, hike nearby Diamond Head and experience its iconic views, paddle out to sea on an unforgettable outrigger canoe experience, or snorkel among bright fish and colorful reef ecosystems in Hanauma Bay.

## Lei Making

Sheraton Waikiki offers a variety of lei making activities Monday – Saturday. Make the perfect Hawaii keepsake at a Ribbon Lei class, or weave a Haku Kupee Bracelet that you can wear to your next event. These activities are great fun for both children and adults alike.

## Ukulele Lessons

Ukulele classes are taught daily at Sheraton Waikiki by Ukulele Puapua’s instructors. Hosted in the open-air porte cochere entrance to our Honolulu hotel, the tradition is enjoyed by guests of all ages.

From ocean activities to cultural classes, check out what’s happening during your stay at Sheraton Waikiki.

### Outrigger Canoe Rides

Canoe surfing is the original Hawaiian rollercoaster ride and Waikiki is one of the few places where you can experience the thrill of surfing a wave on an outrigger canoe.

### Sunrise Hiuwai Ceremony

Hiuwai is a traditional morning cultural ritual, signifying a rebirth that takes place along the Waikiki Beach shoreline and in the ocean. An inner self reflection at sunrise, it represents purification and cleansing of one’s self, with time to reflect upon your life, those who came before you and why you are who you are today.

### Build a Sandcastle

### Oahu Things To Do and Sightseeing

Sheraton Waikiki is located 8.6 miles from Daniel K. Inouye International Airport​ (HNL) on beautiful Waikiki Beach, just a few steps away from the best shopping and restaurants. From iconic visitor attractions to historic tours and outdoor adventures, our Waikiki hotel is located near Diamond Head and a variety of Oahu experiences.

Oahu Family-Friendly Activities

### Waikiki Aquarium

An 8-minute drive from the hotel, the Waikiki Aquarium features almost 500 types of marine plants and animals, many native to the islands.

### Waikiki Zoo

Popular spot for families covers 42 acres with lush gardens, a petting zoo and 900+ animals including, lions, tigers, and elephants.

### Dole Plantation

This historic pineapple plantation with a train ride & a 3-acre garden maze.

### Children’s Discovery Center

A museum located near Kakaako Waterfront Park, the center features five major galleries with hands-on interactive exhibits for children of all ages.

### Bishop Museum

Hawaii’s premier natural and cultural history museum, Bishop Museum features a planetarium, Hawaiian Hall and Science Adventure Center.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact the Sheraton Waikiki Concierge?

Call our main line and ask to be transferred to the concierge, or email our concierge team at SheratonWaikiki-concierge@hawaiitours.com for personalized assistance planning activities and experiences across Oahu.

Where is Waikiki Beach?

Waikiki Beach is located on the South Shore of Oahu. Once a playground for Hawaiian royalty, the name Waikiki is known in Hawaiian as "spouting waters.” Along the main strip of Kalakaua Avenue is world class shopping, dining, entertainment, activities and resorts. Waikiki Beach, which is approximately 2 miles long, runs parallel to Kalakaua Avenue.

Where is the best place to watch sunset on Waikiki Beach?

The sun sets off the South Shore of Oahu, and Sheraton Waikiki offers unobstructed views of the spectacular sunset. We recommend taking in the view from Rumfire, or the resort’s adults only infinity edge pool.

Where can I learn to surf at Waikiki Beach?

The Waikiki Beach Services Beach Boys teach surfing lessons daily

Where can I rent a surfboard on Waikiki Beach?

Waikiki Beach Services can assist with surfboard rentals on Waikiki Beach, and offer a Beachboys Valet program

What are the best places to surf on Waikiki Beach?

The rolling waves of Waikiki Beach are the perfect place to learn the basics of surfing. Surf breaks located steps from Sheraton Waikiki include: Three’s, Populars, and Canoes.

How far is Diamond Head from Waikiki Beach?

Sheraton Waikiki is located 2.3 miles from Diamond Head State Monument. It is a beautiful oceanfront stroll, or 5 minute car ride, from the hotel. Public transportation is also available.

How far is Pearl Harbor from Waikiki Beach?

Sheraton Waikiki is located 11 miles from Pearl Harbor National Memorial and the USS Arizona Memorial.

Does Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio, free-weight, and body-sculpting machines, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
