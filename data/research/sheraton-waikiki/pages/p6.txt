# Family Activities at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort

## Family

At Sheraton Waikiki, family vacations feel effortless from the moment you arrive. Located directly on iconic Waikiki Beach, the resort places families at the heart of Oahu's most famous shoreline, with...See More walkable access to shopping, dining, and top attractions so you can spend less time planning and more time together. Kids love the resort’s legendary Helumoa Playground, a two‑pool complex with waterslides, waterfalls, whirlpools, and plenty of room to splash and play. The resort also offers kid‑friendly activities that give younger guests a taste of native Hawaiian culture. Recognized with a 2025 Good Housekeeping Family Travel Award, Sheraton Waikiki stands out as the best resort for family travel in Waikiki.See Less

### Resort Activities for Children at Sheraton Waikiki

Keep young travelers engaged and entertained with a wide range of resort activities for children at our Waikiki Beach resort. From hands-on cultural experiences to outdoor fun by the pool and beach, the resort offers thoughtfully designed programming that blends play with learning. These Waikiki Beach family activities give kids the chance to get moving, get creative, and connect with the spirit of...See More the islands—all while parents enjoy a relaxing and memorable stay.See Less

## New Guest Activity Cabana: Tita's Hale

Enjoy enhanced guest activities at the all new Tita's Hale. The poolside cabana area will feature special summer activities like ocean animal origami classes, poolside mahjong, and Moonlit Movie nights.

## Hula Lessons For Your Keiki

Little dancers can discover the joy of hula through fun, friendly lessons that share Hawaiian culture and create lasting memories. Designed for children and families, our hula lessons combine movement, music, and cultural education in an interactive setting at our Waikiki resort. Kids will learn basic hula steps, hand motions, and the meanings behind traditional chants and songs, all guided by experienced instructors who bring the spirit of aloha to every session. These lessons not only encourage creativity and physical activity but also foster an appreciation for Hawaiian history and storytelling.

## Koi Fish Feeding

Join us as we feed our Nishikigoi, the multi-colored ornamental carp (koi fish) in the pond right outside our lobby. This activity is especially enjoyable for younger children.

## Lei Making

The family-friendly lei making classes at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort offer a refreshing, creative alternative to the beach while immersing guests in the rich traditions and symbolism of Hawaiian culture. Guided by knowledgeable instructors, each session blends artistry with storytelling. Design a one-of-a-kind Hawaiian keepsake with a hands-on Ribbon Lei class or create a stunning Haku Kupeʻe bracelet you’ll treasure long after your visit. A variety of lei making classes are available Monday through Saturday, making it easy to add this memorable cultural activity to your stay in Waikiki.

Waikiki Shopping

## Waikiki Beach for Children

World-renowned Waikiki Beach has generally calm water and is supported by lifeguards up and down the shore. Restrooms and showers are located next to the Diamond Head beach access point of our resort. Beach toys are available for purchase at Lawson Station, right outside our main elevators.

## Games and Activities

Guests of all generations will enjoy playing table games like billiards, shuffleboard, and foosball. These resort activities are available on the Lobby Level near the Koi Pond.

## Gotcha Arcade

Conveniently located in the lobby, Gotcha arcade features claw machines, Blind Box stations, and games galore—play, win and take home a piece of the excitement.

### Helumoa Playground

Helumoa Playground is the perfect setting for the entire family to enjoy. Make a splash at the award-winning, kid-friendly pool complex at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort. Featuring two expansive freshwater swimming pools, this vibrant oasis is designed for nonstop fun. A towering 70-foot waterslide delivers a fast, fun ride, while a smaller slide connects the two pools for younger guests to enjoy. Nearby,...See More the pool hut makes it easy to grab complimentary towels or purchase essentials like sunscreen and kids’ pool toys, so families can stay comfortable and prepared throughout the day. Kids can splash and play while parents relax poolside, all within one of the largest pool experiences in Waikiki.See Less

## Family Cabanas

Rent both mini-cabanas and luxury cabanas as a home base for a fun day at the pool. Mini cabanas come with two plush chaise lounges with a bimini shade top, while luxury cabanas include two lawn chairs, two covered chaises, and curtains for privacy.

## Sweet Treats for Everyone

After a hot afternoon of swimming, what could be better than shave ice or ice cream from Baskin Robbins? The storefront is conveniently located right beside Helumoa Playground.

### The Best Family Accommodations on the Beach

Learn what rooms and suites are best suited for family travel at Sheraton Waikiki. Thoughtfully designed with comfort and flexibility in mind, these family-friendly Waikiki accommodations offer spacious layouts, convenient in-room amenities, and options that make traveling with kids easier—from connecting rooms to oceanfront suites with room to spread out.

## Ohana Oceanfront Suite

The Ohana Suite is Sheraton Waikiki’s most popular choice for families, featuring two bedrooms, a spacious living area, and dual lanais which showcase both Waikiki sunrises and sunsets—ideal for multi‑generational stays and groups with multiple kids. The suite is 1112 sq ft (100 sq m) and comfortably accommodates up to five guests.

Learn More

## Kai Oceanfront Suite

The Kai Suite elevates family stays with a generous one‑bedroom layout featuring a spacious living and dining area, your choice of a king bed or two doubles, and two private lanais. With expansive Pacific Ocean views and more than four times the space of a standard room, it’s designed for relaxing, reconnecting, and making the most of every moment together.

Learn More

## Malia Oceanfront Suite

The Malia Suite is a refined one‑bedroom retreat with 875 sq ft of oceanfront living, featuring a spacious living room with pull‑out sofa, dining table for four, and two private lanais showcasing Waikiki Beach and Diamond Head views. The 43 Series features a serene, tucked‑away master bedroom set behind double doors, offering added privacy.

## Family Room: Two Doubles + Twin Bed

Designed for families with children who want to sleep in their own bed, the City View Family Room features two double beds and one twin. Playful touches around the room make this a fun homebase for exploring Waikiki.

## Kids Menus at Hinahina

See the brand-new kids menu at Hinahina on the Beach, featuring grilled fish, burgers, island style malasadas, and more. Available for children 12 years and under.

## Poppins Day Care

Take advantage of convenient childcare for your little ones from 3 months to 12 years old, without having to leave the hotel. For reservations, please call the main line and ask to be transferred to Poppins Day Care.

## Doctors On Call

Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort makes every effort to make sure your family enjoys their stay. Should you have need, a walk-in clinic is located right next to the porte cochere, offering a broad range of non-emergency medical care.

Oahu Family Friendly Attractions

### Children’s Discovery Center

A museum located near Kakaako Waterfront Park, the center features five major galleries with hands-on interactive exhibits for children of all ages.

### Kid City Kakaako

This indoor playground has zip lines, tube slides, and ball pits and is great for celebrations and birthdays.

### Bishop Museum

Hawaii’s premier natural and cultural history museum, Bishop Museum features a planetarium, Hawaiian Hall and Science Adventure Center.
