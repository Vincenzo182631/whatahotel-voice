## Stay At One, Dine At All

The “Stay at One, Dine at All!” program invites guests of Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort to sign their restaurant check to their room at any restaurant or bar at any of the four Waikiki Collection resorts, including The Royal Hawaiian, the Moana Surfrider, and Sheraton Princess Kaiulani. By signing the charges to your room, Marriott Bonvoy members will also earn additional points. Explore the options across the four hotels and resorts: click below for a list of participating restaurants.

When will Hinahina be open?

Hinahina will be opening to the public on September 24th, 2026.

Does Hinahina feature a breakfast buffet?

Yes, the buffet features local favorites like Pineapple Upside Down Pancakes and Island-Style Fried Rice, in addition to traditional dishes like a omelet station and a rotating selection of eggs benedict.

Does Hinahina offer oceanfront dining?

Yes, Hinahina is oceanfront with an incredible view of Pops (Populars) surf break.

Does Hinahina take reservations?

Yes, reservations can be made at www.opentable.com/booking/
