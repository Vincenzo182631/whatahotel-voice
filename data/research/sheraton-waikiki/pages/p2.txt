Frequently Asked Questions

Are there upgrades available for in-room amenities and special celebrations at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

Yes, please visit our What to Expect page for a link to in-room amenity upgrades.

Are there connecting rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

Yes, please call our main line to learn about connecting rooms and/or book connecting rooms.

Do you offer cribs in rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

Yes, you may request a crib upon booking your room or during check-in. Please note that cribs may be limited by availability.

Are rollaways available in all rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

Generally, rollaways are only available in King bed rooms and suites for safety reasons. Please call for more details.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 5 at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort?

Some rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort have hair dryers.
