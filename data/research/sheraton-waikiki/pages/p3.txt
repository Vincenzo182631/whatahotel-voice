## Premium Suites

Each suite in our Waikiki...See More resort is outfitted with modern amenities and features separate living rooms complete with entertainment centers, spa-like bathrooms, and stunning oceanfront vistas. The popular Kai Suite is a one-bedroom suite suitable for up to 4 people, featuring a spacious living room-dining room combination, bedroom with either one king bed or two double beds, two lanais, and expansive views of the Pacific Ocean. This is the perfect family suite in Waikiki Beach.

## Comfortable, Modern Oceanfront Living

Soak in unobstructed ocean views from the Kai Suite’s cozy sitting room. Offering 1,112 sq ft (100 sq m) of open, flexible space—over four times the size of a standard guest room—this ocean suite in Waikiki...See More is perfect for entertaining, sharing meals, or giving the kids plenty of space to play.See Less

## Kai Suite Living Room

The suite’s wide foyer opens to a spacious living room which features an entertainment center with flatscreen television, seating area with a comfortable couch that converts to a pull-out sofa bed, and an additional day bed. Also included are a full dining table with a sideboard, and wetbar complete with a mini-refrigerator.

3D Tour

## Kai Suite Bathroom

The Kai Suite's bathroom is off the main hallway, ensuring the bedroom has privacy, and has a spacious walk-in shower room with deep soaking tub, double sinks, and a water closet with TOTO fixtures.

## Kai Suite Bedroom Options: King or Double Beds

The Kai Suite bedroom is available in either a King or Double bed configuration, and has a private lanai with seating area for two guests. Many Kai Suites also have connecting room options through the bedroom, creating a two bedroom suite option. The Kai Suite (King bed) connects to an Oceanfront Double Bed guest room, and the Kai Suite (Double bed) connects to an Oceanfront King Bed guest room. Please call +1 808 922-4422 for more details.

Our two Presidential Suites are located on the 29th and 30th floors. Each suite features two bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and a total of three separate lanais, for a total of 2,200 sq ft (198 sq m) and a capacity of 5 people.
