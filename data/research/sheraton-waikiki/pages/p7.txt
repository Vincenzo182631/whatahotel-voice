## Sheraton Hawaii Bowl

Experience the excitement of college football in paradise at the Sheraton Hawaii Bowl. Held annually at the Clarence T.C. Ching Athletics Complex at the University of Hawaii, this iconic bowl game brings together top college football teams for an unforgettable celebration of sport, culture, and the spirit of aloha in beautiful Honolulu, Hawaii.

## A College Football Tradition in Paradise

The Sheraton Hawaii Bowl has been a beloved tradition since 2002, bringing the excitement of college football to the beautiful island of Oahu. With the stunning backdrop of Hawaii's iconic landscape and the warm aloha spirit, the bowl game offers an unparalleled fan experience unlike any other.
