### An Unforgettable Wellness Escape at Alila Wuzhen

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ A Peaceful Luxury Retreat in Wuzhen

Our stay at Alila Wuzhen was one of the highlights of our trip to China. The hotel is a peaceful sanctuary away from the busy city, with beautiful architecture that blends perfectly with the charm of Wuzhen.

We especially enjoyed dining at Si Shui Restaurant. The chef was incredibly accommodating, particularly in preparing delicious vegan meals for my son, which we truly appreciated.

A heartfelt thank you to Cindy, Abby, Zhou Zhou, Nichol, Andres, Herman Li, and Dennis for their genuine hospitality and outstanding service. They made us feel incredibly welcome throughout our stay.

One experience not to be missed is the daily Singing Bowl Meditation with Dennis. It was calming, meaningful, and became one of the highlights of our visit.

As a travel advisor, I highly recommend Alila Wuzhen to anyone looking for a relaxing luxury escape with exceptional service, wellness experiences, and authentic hospitality. I look forward to recommending this wonderful property to many of my clients.

### Great place to restore inner piece at Wuzhen

My second time here. Got a Garden Suite for my family vacation. Wuzhen is such a beautiful town, and Alila Wuzhen will always be my top choice in Wuzhen. It is located a 10-minute drive from the Wuzhen scenic area. It is quiet with exceptional privacy. Though rainy, the hotel itself is modern and full of designs.
