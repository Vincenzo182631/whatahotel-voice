## A Modern Retreat in a Town of Timeless Waters

Set on the edge of a storied water town, Alila Wuzhen offers a contemporary retreat immersed in centuries of heritage. Surrounded by winding canals, wooden corridors and stone bridges, the resort invites quiet exploration and peaceful reflection—where the pace slows, and the beauty of tradition unfolds at every turn.

## Accommodations

Inspired by a classic Jiagnan village—yet reimagined with modern, minimalist architecture—Alila Wuzhen offers a peaceful oasis with 124 thoughtfully appointed rooms and villas. Inspired in design by Wuzhen’s maze of quiet canals and ancient alleyways, Alila Wuzhen offers an extraordinarily peaceful escape and a luxurious base for exploring the town’s rich cultural heritage.​

## Discover Alila Wuzhen

An Ancient Water Town

Located in the Jiangnan region, Wuzhen is known as the last town surrounded by water in China. In the centuries-old water town, ancient buildings stand perfectly preserved, displaying skillful wood and stone carvings. Also referred to as the “Home of Silk,” Wuzhen is famed for its silk production, and you can learn about sericulture and explore age-old fabric dyeing traditions.

Commitment to Sustainability

Respect for the environment is at the heart of Alila Wuzhen’s vision. Throughout the resort, great care is taken to preserve and protect the delicate natural environment. Its structures are carefully situated around plantings of Metasequoia, an endangered native conifer.

Balanced by Water

A mosaic of tranquil waterways sets the tone for this serene sanctuary balanced by natural elements. Water flows through Alila Wuzhen, forming an inviting water courtyard and wrapping around spacious suites and villas. The sights and sounds of flowing water instantly soothe body and mind, promoting a sense of wellbeing while inspiring happiness, creativity and a sense of awe.
