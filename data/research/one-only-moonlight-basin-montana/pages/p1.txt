# Wonder in the wilderness

Uncover a destination defined by untouched wilderness and a liberating alpine lifestyle in the heart of Big Sky, Montana. Every detail is in tune with the natural landscape.

Discover the destination

#### Pet Policy at One&Only Moonlight Basin

- Pets must weigh less than 40 pounds.
- Pets are to be kept on leash at all times.
- Do not leave your pet unattended.
- Pets are not permitted in our restaurants, spa, fitness centre or pool areas. This does not apply to guide or service dogs.

#### Pet fees

- USD 150 charge per pet, per day will be applied to your account. An additional pet deposit of USD 3,000 is required for stays in our Private Homes.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Useful details you need to know before you travel to One&Only Moonlight Basin.

#### What are the passport and visa requirements for visiting Montana?

All visitors to the United States must present a passport valid for at least six months from the date of travel. Visa requirements for entering the United States vary significantly from country to country. Click here for more information.

#### What languages are spoken in Montana?

English is the country’s official language.

#### What is the electrical voltage at One&Only Moonlight Basin?

Electricity is supplied at 110 volts. There are in-room USB sockets located on the desk and near the bed.

#### How can I book transfers to One&Only Moonlight Basin?

Big Sky, Montana is around an hour away by car from Bozeman International Airport. The flight to Bozeman takes around 3hrs from Chicago, 2hrs from Los Angeles, and 4hrs from New York. Bozeman International Airport is served by a variety of airlines. All transfer options are subject to weather conditions. In the event of adverse weather, transfers may be delayed or rescheduled.

#### What internet services are available at One&Only Moonlight Basin?

Complimentary high-speed WiFi is available throughout the resort.

#### What is the time zone in Montana?

Montana is GMT/UTC -7h during Standard Time, and GMT/UTC -8h during Daylight Saving Time.

#### What is the weather like in Montana?

Big Sky, Montana, has cold, snowy winters ranging from -10°F to 30°F (-23°C to -1°C) and mild, sunny summers of 50°F to 80°F (10°C to 27°C). Spring and fall are cooler and unpredictable, with temperatures ranging from 30°F to 70°F (-1°C to 21°C). The area sees heavy snowfall, making it a prime destination for winter sports.

#### What is the local currency in Montana?

US Dollars is the official currency of the USA.

#### What are the check-in and check-out times at One&Only Moonlight Basin?

Check-in is at 3pm, local time; check-out is at 12pm, local time.
