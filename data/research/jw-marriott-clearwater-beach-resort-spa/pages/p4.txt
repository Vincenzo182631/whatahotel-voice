### Exploring Clearwater Beach

From our hotel near the beach, discover Clearwater, Florida's top-rated destination known for its soft white sand, clear turquoise waters, and vibrant coastal atmosphere. Visitors can enjoy sunset celebrations at Pier 60, explore marine life at the Clearwater Marine Aquarium, or take a boat tour to spot dolphins along the Gulf. With nearby nature parks, beachside dining, and lively shopping districts,...See More Clearwater Beach offers something for every kind of traveler.See Less

### Pier 60

Stay at our hotel near Pier 60 in Clearwater Beach and find sunsets, street performers, and seaside charm. Pier 60 is Clearwater Beach's iconic gathering spot, boasting a boardwalk, local craft shopping, and nightly celebrations as the sun sets below the Gulf. Just steps from the sand, this is where beach days turn into unforgettable nights.
