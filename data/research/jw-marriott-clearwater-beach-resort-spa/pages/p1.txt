## Residences

Each Residence is artfully designed with calming neutral tones, modern finishes, and convenient amenities like a spacious kitchenette, washer/dryer unit, luxurious living area with floor-to-ceiling sliding glass doors, and a private balcony boasting waterfront views. Choose from one-, two-, or three-bedroom options based on your groups size.

View Residences

### One-Bedroom

Perfect for couple getaways or stay-cations, our One-Bedroom Residence is fully equipped with a kitchenette, dining space, and living room area, separated from your spacious bedroom and modern bathroom. Your private balcony runs the length of this elevated unit, allowing you to stretch out and enjoy the view.

### Two-Bedroom

Ideal for trips of up to 6 people, our Two-Bedroom Residence is fully equipped with a kitchenette, dining space, and living room area, separated from your two spacious bedrooms and two modern bathrooms. The primary bedroom is home to a king bed, while the secondary bedroom is prepared with two queen-sized beds. Stretch out on your full length private balcony, accessible from each bedroom, and savor the view.

### Three-Bedroom

With our Three Bedroom Residences, groups of up to 8 people can gather for a memorable stay. These units are fully equipped with a kitchenette, dining space, and living room area, separated from three spacious bedrooms and three modern bathrooms. Perfect for entertaining, the private balcony runs the length of the unit, allowing you to enjoy and connect between all bedrooms and living spaces.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa. The daily valet fee is $48.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa is St Petersburg-Clearwater International Airport (PIE). PIE is located approximately 13.3 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
