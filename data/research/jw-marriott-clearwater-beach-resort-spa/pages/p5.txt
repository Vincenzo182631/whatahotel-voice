# Holiday Events at JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa

## Celebrate the Holidays in Coastal Luxury

Celebrate the season at JW Marriott Clearwater Beach Resort & Spa with festive dining, seasonal cocktails, and family-friendly surprises all season long. Your memorable holiday escape awaits by the Gulf.

### Festive Experiences

### Fall Cocktails

Embrace the season with a collection of handcrafted fall cocktails at Latitude 28 Bar. Thoughtfully curated with autumn flavors and elevated presentation, each cocktail offers a taste of fall on Clearwater Beach. Sunday, October 1 - Thursday, November 26

Learn More

### Christmas Eve Dinner

Make Christmas Eve truly special at Latitude 28. Indulge in a chef-curated holiday dinner, raise a toast to the season, and enjoy an evening of festive dining in an inviting coastal setting. Thursday, December 24 from 3pm-10pm

Learn More

### Thanksgiving Dinner

Give thanks over an unforgettable dining experience at Latitude 28. Enjoy the comforting flavors of Thanksgiving, beautifully prepared and served in a relaxed yet refined atmosphere. Thursday, November 26 from 3pm-10pm.

Learn More

### Holiday Pop-Up Bar

'Tis the season to sip. Discover a merry lineup of holiday-inspired cocktails at Latitude 28 Bar, where seasonal ingredients and expert craftsmanship come together in festive fashion. Friday, November 27 to Thursday, December 31.

Learn More

### Christmas Brunch With Santa

Celebrate the season with our Christmas Brunch with Santa Claus, a cheerful holiday gathering filled with festive flavors, family fun, and photo opportunities with the jolliest guest of honor. It's a holiday tradition you won't want to miss. Friday, December 25 from 10am-2pm.

Learn More

### Christmas Dinner

Embrace the joy of the holidays with Christmas Dinner at Latitude 28. Featuring a chef-inspired menu, festive flavors, and gracious hospitality, it's the perfect way to celebrate the season with family and friends. Friday, December 25 from 3pm-10pm.

Learn More

### Unwind in Luxury This Holiday Season

Black Friday Gift Card Promotion

Black Friday becomes a moment of pure indulgence at Spa by JW—purchase a $300 gift card and receive a gift with purchase, our way of adding a little extra bliss to your holiday giving. Treat someone you love (or yourself) to restorative relaxation and unwrap a bonus delight made for the season. Friday, November 27 from 10am-6pm.

Holiday Spa Experiences

Unwind into the season with Spa by JW’s holiday specials and wellness experiences, from festive scrub and aroma oil stations to glow-enhancing treatments designed for relaxation, renewal, and a little seasonal indulgence by the beach.

Christmas Spirit Room Décor Package

Make your holiday stay even more magical with our festive in-room décor package. Arrive to a beautifully decorated guest room filled with seasonal cheer and holiday touches. Available in select room types only. Tuesday, December 1 - Thursday, December 31.

### Gingerbread Display

Step into a sugary dreamscape with our enchanting Gingerbread Display. Located in the resort lobby, this edible art installation captures the spirit of the season with intricate details and whimsical charm. A must-see for guests of all ages, it’s the perfect photo op and holiday memory-maker.
