The shores of Clearwater Beach are packed block-to-block with resorts booked up by eager beachgoers, but the JW Marriott might just be the one to convince you to hang around a little bit longer before launching into Clearwater’s ombré coast. Seated on the quieter, south end of the beach, this is a remarkably secluded alcove made for retreat. Fit with a rooftop pool, four-room spa, and three on-site restaurants serving everything from New American classics to reimagined Nikkei, the reasons to stay on-property are plenty. Plush carpeting, touches of turquoise in a largely eggshell-forward palette, tasteful landscape paintings that border on abstract, and spacious private balconies make up the subtle suite design, allaying any worries of dowdy beach resort decor.
