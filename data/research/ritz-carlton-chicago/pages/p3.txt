Transcend the Ordinary
Signature Suites
Set along The Magnificent Mile, The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago's Premium Luxury Suites feature signature city style and offer expansive living spaces with elegant furnishings and exquisite artwork.
Extraordinary Experiences
The Presidential Suite
Encompassing two floors, the Presidential Suite spans 2,900 square feet and offers a taste of luxury living. Upon entering the room, guests are greeted by unparalleled views of Lake Michigan and the city’s skyline. The open living room features grand windows, plush sofas and lounge seating, ideal for intimate gatherings. The full-service kitchen, island and spacious dining area are perfect for entertaining. Other highlights include a media room, a wet bar, two bathrooms and a guest powder room. Upstairs, the primary bedroom features luxurious bedding, a walk-in closet and a glistening bathroom with marble floors, Jacuzzi bathtub & rain shower.
