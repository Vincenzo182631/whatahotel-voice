The Social Side of
Dining
Fine dining at our hotel begins at Torali, a modern Gold Coast restaurant in Chicago, favoring Italian handmade pastas and dry-aged meats. The experience extends to our lounge and rooftop bar in Chicago, internationally inspired and locally driven, and specialty coffees at the greenhouse-inspired Café.
