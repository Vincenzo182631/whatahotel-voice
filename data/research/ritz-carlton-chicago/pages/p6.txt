Explore the City from Our Chicago Hotel Near Millennium Park
Experiences
Chicago is a city rich in experiences, captivating the senses with its collection of architectural landmarks, culinary diversity, rich history, and local charm. Located near the luxury boutiques on Michigan Avenue in downtown Chicago, our hotel is located near the Magnificent Mile, and Navy Pier, and provides the ideal base for exploration, including Oak Street Beach, Millennium Park, Hancock Tower, and Wrigley Field.

Shopping in Chicago
900 North Michigan Shops
A visually stunning shopping destination that resides steps away from the downtown hotel and features luxe retailers including Bloomingdales, Gucci and Montblanc. The lifestyle-retail emporium also offers the chef-driven Aster Hall and Top Golf Swing Suite.
Explore More
Magnificent Mile Shopping
Stores such as Cartier, Chanel, Gucci, Neiman Marcus, Ralph Lauren, Saks Fifth Avenue, and Tiffany & Co. fill this glittering stretch of shopping on Michigan Avenue. Our hotel near the Magnificent Mile in Chicago is the ideal location to explore this iconic destination.
Explore More
Oak Street Shopping
The Oak Street shopping district is popular for its high-end designer shopping. Walking distance from The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago is a sight to see with its tree-lined streets and sophisticated stores. The area is filled with an expansive choice of dining and is just around the corner from Michigan Avenue.

Experience Chicago
Located on the Magnificent Mile within the landmark Water Tower Place, this luxury hotel in downtown Chicago near Navy Pier is close to the city’s most iconic sights, including Navy Pier, the Field Museum, Oak Street Beach, Wrigley Field, Hancock Tower, and the theater district.

Red Wagon Warm Welcome
Children enjoy a warm welcome with The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago’s famous red wagon. Ritz Kids are greeted by our Ladies and Gentlemen with the Red Wagon upon arrival and can pick their toy of choice to take home with them as a souvenir from their visit to Chicago.
Ritz Kids Dining Experience
When dining in the hotel’s restaurants, children can enjoy a Chicago themed coloring book and Ritz Kids Healthy Menus, providing healthy, tasty treats for our younger guests.
Hotel Art Collection
Paying homage to Lake Michigan, the Flying Wave floating sculpture is made from four different types of hand-blown glass in blue tones reminiscent of the lake waters. This piece is a testament to Chicago’s modern architecture and industrial ingenuity.
