Indulge in the ultimate Ritz-Carlton experience with Club Lounge guest rooms and hotel suites in downtown Chicago. Exclusive for guests seeking a heightened level of luxury and exclusivity, The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge provides access to a truly magnificent experience. Featuring five continuous Food & Beverage offerings throughout the day, The Club Lounge is perfect for conducting business meetings, planning family adventures, or simply unwinding. The oasis in the sky combines elegance and an unwavering commitment to service, with a Club Lounge Concierge Team dedicated to providing personalized experiences in the Windy City.

The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago Apartments
Daily life is elevated with sophisticated style and legendary attention to detail at The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago Apartments. Experience the pinnacle of luxury living in one and two-bedroom apartments with breathtaking city & lake views, lavish amenities, and unparalleled comfort. Each apartment features king beds, marble bathrooms, fully equipped kitchens with BOSCH appliances, and spacious living areas adorned with elegant furnishings. Enjoy the convenience of daily housekeeping services, complimentary high-speed internet, multiple TVs with premium channels, and exclusive discounts at our spa, restaurant, and in-room dining. With access to the pool, fitness center, residential washer and dryer, and valet parking, every aspect of your experience is designed to surpass your expectations.
