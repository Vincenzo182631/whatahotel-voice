Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago
Relax and unwind at our luxury hotel
The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago, offers an iconic address that unites the city, lake, and sky in a vibrant and luxurious setting on Chicago's Gold Coast. Our hotel's legacy lives on through generations of families, travelers, and trailblazers, providing a coveted setting to enjoy what matters most. Cherished heritage, community soul, landmark service, and Midwest sincerity are all to be found at this luxury hotel in Chicago. Our beacon of hospitality is the backdrop for new and established voices to connect, reconnect, and uncover the dynamic energy of Chicago.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago, with rates of $34.00 per hour, and $85.00 per day.
Off-site parking is also available near The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago. The daily valet fee is $85.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago is Chicago Midway International Airport (MDW). MDW is located approximately 12.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
