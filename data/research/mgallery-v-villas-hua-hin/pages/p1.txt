### V Villas Hua Hin - MGallery Collection

* * *

V Villas Hua Hin – MGallery Collection has been recognized as a **MICHELIN Key Hotel**, one of only four hotels in Hua Hin to receive this prestigious recognition. The MICHELIN Key Award underscores V Villas Hua Hin – MGallery Collection’s premier standing among Thailand’s luxury 5-star hotels and the resort’s fifteen years of excellence in delivering superior guest experiences.

This year marks the first time MICHELIN Guide awarded Michelin Key for the most outstanding hotels in Thailand. The award singles out hotels that are not simply a place to stay for a night, but rather elevate their guests’ overall travel experience. V Villas Hua Hin – MGallery Collection recognized for its excellence in the following categories:

**Architecture and Interior Design**

V Villas Hua Hin – MGallery Collection architectural design compliments the resort’s prime location on Hua Hin’s most picturesque white sand beaches. Nestled among tropical gardens, each private pool villa offers seamless indoor-outdoor flow, modern tropical interior design, and privacy.

**Quality and Consistency of Service**

Each villa comes with around-the-clock personalized butter service

**Personality and Character**

The resort exudes a welcoming vibe, perfectly balancing a ‘feel at home’ character with luxurious, attentive service.

**Value for Price**

V Villas Hua Hin – MGallery Collection’s premier pricing offers excellent value for guests seeking unparalleled luxury, privacy, and attentive guest services.

**Guest Experience**

V Villas Hua Hin – MGallery Collection strives to personalize each guest stay by accommodating requests and adding unique touches to  birthdays, anniversary, and celebrations.

**Accessibility Information**

We strive to accommodate guests with different accessibility needs; however, please note that accessibility facilities within the hotel are limited and some areas may not be fully wheelchair accessible. We kindly recommend contacting the hotel before your stay for further information and assistance.

### Location

* * *

Located along Petchkasem Road in the heart of Hua Hin town, V Villas Hua Hin, MGallery Hotel Collection is easy to find and close to seafood restaurants, local markets and a vibrant nightlife. The numerous transportation options include aeroplane, public bus, train, or by private car.

Hua Hin is 200 kilometres and a two-and-a-half hour drive from Bangkok. From Bangkok’s Suvarnabhumi Airport, take the highway to the Rama 9 Expressway until you see a sign for Samut Sakhon. Take Highway 35 to Highway 4, and continue on to Petchaburi. Take a left off Highway 4 to Cha-Am. Hua Hin is another 20 minutes’ drive past Cha-Am.
