## Experiences

Step through the bronze doorway to Iridium Spa at The St. Regis Istanbul for a world of pampering and relaxation. Exclusive to the St. Regis brand and often praised for delivering an exceptional spa experience, it glows by candlelight and is adorned with orchids and bonsai trees, inviting guests to choose from a refined menu of bespoke treatments. To support wellness-led stays, continue into our state-of-the-art Exercise Room with cardio and weight lifting equipment, guided by certified personal trainers. Enjoy a 24/7 fitness center, private indoor pools, and a curated wellness-focused culinary and beverage offering, all in close proximity to the Bosporus Strait and the old city.

## Personal Training

Available upon request, personal trainers provide cross-training, yoga, stretching instruction, and more. Sessions are a minimum of one hour, for a supplementary fee, and must be arranged 24 hours in advance at the spa reception desk.

## Indoor Relaxing Pools

An aquatic sanctuary apart from the city, our three indoor private relaxing pools feature soothing waters and a restorative ambiance adjacent to Iridium Spa. Featuring smooth marble walls and mosaic or marble floors, the pools boast soft candlelight and a shared lounge area populated by inviting couches and coffee tables. A decorative waterfall adds to the soothing space, while the spa’s Tea Bar presents a nourishing menu of teas, fruits, and salads. In-room dining options are available upon request. Separate men and women’s locker rooms are available.

## Explore Istanbul With St. Regis

Famously referred to as the 5th Avenue of Istanbul, the Nişantaşı District is the lavish, exclusive neighborhood that harbors The St. Regis Istanbul. This vibrant area amazes visitors with its luxury shopping boutiques by top international designers, housed in Art Deco and Art Nouveau buildings, and an exceptional array of restaurants and cafes. As a major social and business hub, the Istanbul Congress Center, Maçka Park, and the glittering Bosphorus are mere steps from our front door. A leisurely walk from our Istanbul hotel guides you to the Beyoğlu District and Taksim Square, famed for its Istiklal Street, a significant pathway in Istanbul’s cultural landscape. To bask in the sands of history, travel by car to Old Istanbul where the iconic Hagia Sophia (Ayasofya) and Blue Mosque stand majestically for all to behold. Clustered around these historic monuments, find world-class museums, hammam spas, and the Grand Bazaar, one of the world’s oldest and largest covered markets.

## The St. Regis Champagne Sabering

Allow us to take you on a bespoke journey of sabering, resulting in popping exalted champagnes as only they deserve - a dramatic, yet contained explosion that is truly the most pleasurable way to begin the appreciation of a fine wine.

Today, this St. Regis Aficionado experience is part of the bespoke Evening Ritual at the St. Regis Istanbul to enjoy the best of sparkling wines, welcoming the beginning of a new evening at Nişantaşı. A place where timeless elegance and informal grace are dedicated to those who appreciate the best in life, while the sun goes down over the timeless city.

### Boutiques

Anticipating the cultivated desires of its worldly guests, the boutiques at The St. Regis Istanbul highlight the designs of the world’s premier fashion brands. To further elevate the exclusive retail experience, a personal shopping butler will be pleased to assist with private viewing, in-room trial and purchase.

### Explore Istanbul with your Family

Discover Turkey's captivating treasures at Miniaturk: one of the largest miniature parks in the world. Housing over 122 models, it showcases everything from the Ottoman Empire to modern day Turkey, including historical structures like the Temple of Artemis at Ephesus and the Mausoleum of Maussollos at Halicarnassus. The park also includes entertainment such as the storytelling tree, helicopter simulation, remote-controlled boats, and more.

To explore the city’s stunning landscape, visit Forestanbul. Covering 65,000 square meters, it is Turkey’s largest outdoor adventure and activity park. The renowned destination invites guests to participate in over 95 activities that harmonize nature, recreation, and fun. Please note that October through April the park is closed on Mondays.

These experiences are perfectly suited for a family of four (two adults and two children), but can always be customized. The Concierge is pleased to provide more details and to help make arrangements. To reserve an experience, it is recommended that guests contact the Concierge at least 24 hours in advance.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Istanbul have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Istanbul offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio and strength training equipment, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Istanbul have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Istanbul has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Istanbul have an on-site spa?

Yes, Iridium Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Istanbul. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Istanbul?

Dolmabahce Palace, Galata Tower, Hagia Sophia, Bosphorus Strait, Taksim Square/Beyoglu and more are nearby The St. Regis Istanbul.
