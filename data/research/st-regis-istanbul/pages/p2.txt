### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The St. Regis Istanbul?

The largest occupancy room at The St. Regis Istanbul is the Presidential with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul?

Yes, all rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The St. Regis Istanbul have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The St. Regis Istanbul have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
