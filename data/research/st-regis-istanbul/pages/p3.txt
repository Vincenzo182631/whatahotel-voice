Misty Mary-The St. Regis Istanbul Bloody Mary

Inspired by the morning mist on the Bosphorus, the Misty Mary uses traditional ingredients such as Rakı, turnip juice, and red basil, referring to the rich tastes and cultural heritage of Anatolia and Istanbul. The ‘Misty Mary’ is served in an ‘ehl-i keyf’ with dry ice to create the mist effect.

Red Snapper - The Original Bloody Mary

## Tea Ritual at The St. Regis Istanbul

Since its creation in England in the early 1800s by Anna, Duchess of Bedford, Afternoon Tea has long been associated with the entertainment of society. In the late 19th century, it was Caroline Astor, mother of the St. Regis founder John Jacob Astor IV, who elevated Afternoon Tea to a new level by introducing the ritual at dignified gatherings at her house in Manhattan. Today, this ritual comes alive every afternoon at every St. Regis around the globe, where guests are invited to enjoy distinct interpretations of this legacy inspired by the respective traditions of each location. You’re invited to a wide choice of refined Teas at St. Regis Brasserie, selected by our Tea Sommelier, for those who appreciate the finest things in life.

A Celebration of Fire and Heritage

## Tastes of Adana

St. Regis Brasserie presents Tastes of Adana, an exclusive culinary experience honoring the rich traditions of Southern Turkish cuisine through a refined contemporary lens. Curated by acclaimed guest chef Kemal Yakut, renowned for his mastery of kebab culture and regional gastronomy, the menu showcases signature specialties prepared over a wood-charcoal fire with exceptional craftsmanship and authenticity. Served for lunch and dinner in the heart of Nişantaşı, this limited-time experience invites guests to discover the bold spirit of Adana elevated with the elegance, artistry, and impeccable service synonymous with St. Regis.
