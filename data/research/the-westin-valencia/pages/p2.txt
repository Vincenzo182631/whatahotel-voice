### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Valencia have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Valencia have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Valencia?

The largest occupancy room at The Westin Valencia is the 1 Bedroom Presidential Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Valencia?

Yes, all rooms at The Westin Valencia permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Westin Valencia have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Valencia have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at The Westin Valencia have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Valencia have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Westin Valencia have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Valencia have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The Westin Valencia have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The Westin Valencia have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
