### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Valencia?

The check-in time at The Westin Valencia is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Valencia allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Valencia. with a maximum weight of 30.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €30.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Valencia?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Valencia. Self parking is available at The Westin Valencia, with rates of €3.50 per hour, and €23.00 per day. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Valencia have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Valencia has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Valencia booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Valencia?

The closest airport near The Westin Valencia is Valencia Airport (VLC). VLC is located approximately 12.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Valencia have an airport shuttle to Valencia Airport (VLC)?

Yes, The Westin Valencia has an airport shuttle to Valencia Airport (VLC).

Does The Westin Valencia have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Valencia has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
