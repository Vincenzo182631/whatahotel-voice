# Welcome to JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai

## Book a 5-star hotel getaway in downtown Dubai

Anchored in the dynamic Business Bay district along Dubai Water Canal, JW Marriott Marquis Dubai continues to shape the future of luxury hospitality in the region. A recognized icon on the Dubai skyline, this downtown Dubai hotel caters to discerning business and leisure travelers with 1,608 thoughtfully designed rooms and suites, award-winning restaurants and lounges, and nearly 10,000 sqm of flexible...See More event space for everything from intimate gatherings to citywide conferences. Guests praise the attentive service, spacious rooms and skyline views, while Saray Spa, the outdoor pool, a fully equipped gym and the reformer-based BodyBase studio support wellness-led stays. Ideally located near Dubai Metro, Dubai Mall, Burj Khalifa, Dubai International Airport, DIFC and Sheikh Zayed Road, the property stands out among 5-star hotels in downtown Dubai.See Less

## Luxury at Every Height

Comprising two iconic towers, the JW Marriott Marquis Dubai is centrally located beside the Dubai Water Canal. The 1,608 rooms hotel has a collection of over 12 award-winning restaurants and bars, a sublime Saray Spa and Club Marquis, and over 8,000 sq m of spectacular indoor and outdoor event space.

## Our Commitment to Sustainability

Sustainability lies at the heart of JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai. From thoughtful innovation to mindful operations, we are dedicated to preserving our planet while curating purposeful moments for our guests. Every initiative is a step toward a more responsible, inspiring future.

True to its name and drawing inspiration from the historic Caravanserais located along the silk trade routes of old, Saray Spa offers a personal experience of restoration and renewal through rituals that have withstood the test of time.

### Bodybase Studios

Elevate your fitness at BodyBase Dubai with a balanced blend of high-energy workouts and restorative flow, powered by our signature BaseLine reformer. A smarter, more dynamic take on Pilates to build strength, mobility, and core control in just 45 minutes.

Club Marquis is a one-stop destination for all of your fitness and wellness needs. Enjoy a 5-star health club offering state-of-the-art strength training, cardio and functional equipment along with personalized fitness assessments from qualified instructors.

### Couple Treatment Suite

Our VIP couple suite is specifically made for two. Enjoy a soothing massage or one of our many treatments with your significant other in a complete privacy.

### Wellness Areas

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai is Dubai International Airport (DXB). DXB is located approximately 16.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
