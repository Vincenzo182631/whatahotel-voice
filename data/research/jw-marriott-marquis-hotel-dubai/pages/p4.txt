# Nourish Your Soul

## The Saray Spa at JW Marriott Marquis Dubai

Inspired by the historic caravanserais along the Silk Trade Routes, the Saray Spa offers restoration and renewal through timeless rituals. Located on the 3rd floor, it features 17 treatment rooms, separate male and female areas, private hammams, luxurious wellness facilities, and relaxation areas. Treatments are available for guests 18+, with facials offered to individuals aged 16 and older.

## Hammam Rituals

Each treatment incorporates heat and steam, authentic olive oil-based soap, a vigorous body scrub, and a nourishing cream for rejuvenation. Selected Hammam treatments also feature a full-body mask of mud or clay blended with healing botanicals.

## Facial Treatments

Our treatments are performed using potent, natural, botanical-based skincare products by our highly experienced professionals. Our synthetic-free formulations are highly effective and results-oriented, yet gentle for the most sensitive skin types. To ensure a personalized treatment, our skin specialists will discuss any specific needs you may have for your skin type.
