## Accommodations

Elevated above the city, JW Marriott Marquis hotel in Dubai offers 1,608 5-star hotel rooms and family suites with stunning skyline views, deluxe bedding, marble bathrooms and 24-hour room service. Guests often highlight the spacious, comfortable feel of each hotel room near Dubai airport, along with high standards of cleanliness. In-room amenities include complimentary high-speed Internet, a two-line...See More telephone, individually controlled air-conditioning, interactive TV, international channels, an in-room safe, iron and ironing board, bathroom amenities, bathrobe, hairdryer and daily newspaper on request. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai is the Penthouse Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai?

Yes, all rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have fridges?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at JW Marriott Marquis Hotel Dubai have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
