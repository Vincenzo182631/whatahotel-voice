# La Petite Fleur

La Petite Fleur, an 1,100-square-foot romantic oasis, offers complete privacy in a uniquely designed Suite.  Ladera’s signature open wall design showcases stunning panoramas of the majestic Pitons and Caribbean Sea. The spacious private veranda features a heated pool, and offers alfresco dining with handcrafted lounge chairs, an ideal setting for pure romance. Your private butler ensures that your every need is effortlessly met. La Petite Fleur also offers a closed 4th wall option with air conditioning.
