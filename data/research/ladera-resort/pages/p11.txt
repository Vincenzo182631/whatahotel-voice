# Villa at Paradise Ridge

The Villa at Paradise Ridge sits at the highest point at Ladera. Our pioneering Open Wall Design provides unparalleled views of the Gros and Petit Pitons and Caribbean Sea. A 1,900-square-foot accommodation, this is one of our largest villas offering a private swimming pool, set off to the side of the Suite offering the utmost privacy and seclusion.
