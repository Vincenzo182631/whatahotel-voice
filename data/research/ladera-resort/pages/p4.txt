# Villa La Fleur

Measuring 4,128 square feet, the two-level, two bedroom Villa La Fleur is a luxurious, air-conditioned retreat featuring a 900-square-foot Great Room with floor-to-ceiling glass doors that seamlessly connect with a ten-foot covered veranda. With high vaulted wood ceilings and hand-crafted wood furniture throughout, its open-air design offers a breathtaking view of Petite Piton, the Caribbean Sea, and Gros Piton. The 480-square-foot infinity pool and fully equipped pool deck with lounge chairs invites you to savor the dramatic views. Your villa’s private butler service ensures that your every need is effortlessly met.

Private Chef Exclusive for Villa La Fleur.

Elevate your stay with personalized, chef-prepared meals in the comfort of Villa La Fleur. Our chefs will craft exquisite, locally inspired menus, personalized and cooked before you in Villa La Fleur’s commercial grade kitchen, reserve for just one special evening or for your whole stay. It’s the ultimate romantic and luxurious touch—perfect for intimate dinners under the stars, celebrating special moments in total privacy or hosting a special gathering of up to 12 people.
