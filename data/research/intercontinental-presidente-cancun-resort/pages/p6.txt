## FAQs for InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort

Does InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort have a pool?

InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Our 605 m2 pool has 5 jacuzzis and easy access to the Deck Bar and just 5 steps from the Caribbean Sea. All our pools offer towel service and are located close to sanitary services.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort for hotel guests.

Parking is free for all guests, subject to availability, and it includes electric vehicle charging.

Parking for registered guests only

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort.

Pet policy description. Max of 2 pets are allowed in each bedroom. Pets are not allowed in any restaurant or consumption center. When moving through public areas of the hotel Lobby the pet must always carry a leash. Pet Deposit does not include taxes.

Pet fee per stay: 60 USD

Pet weight limit: 0

2 pets allowed

Pets allowed: Only dogs and cats allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort?

Check-in at InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort is conveniently located near Cancun International Airport (CUN), approximately 22 miles away (35.41 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort?

InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Chichen Itza
- Cobá
- Xcaret
- Xel Ha
- Garrafon Natural Reef Park
- Tulum
- Golf Courses

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
