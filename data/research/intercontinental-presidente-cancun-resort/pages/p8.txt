# InterContinental Presidente Cancun ResortDestination

Discover exciting tours and explore the must-see tourist attractions of Cancun, all convenient to our hotel. Enjoy pristine beaches, snorkel in coral reefs, visit Mayan ruins, and dive into crystal-clear cenotes near Cancun.

### Personalized experiences in Cancun

Explore a curated selection of activities designed just for you at a 5 star resort in Cancun. Whether you choose to relax, discover, or indulge, every corner of the resort invites you to create unforgettable memories.

Learn more

### Dining and Nightlife Just Steps Away

Looking for what to do in Cancun? From gourmet dining to vibrant clubs, the city’s best restaurants and nightlife await just steps from Presidente InterContinental Cancun.

### Enjoy a Sargassum-Free Beach Experience

Enjoy the Caribbean with sargassum-free waters in one of Cancún’s most privileged areas. Thanks to its prime location, Presidente InterContinental offers a remarkably clean beach year-round — a smart choice for those exploring Riviera Maya hotels and looking for the best things to do in Cancún.

### Premium location

We are conveniently located on the best beach in Cancun and 25 minutes from Cancun International Airport (CUN). We're near the Convention Center, Party Center, Puerto Cancun, and La Isla shopping centers. Move easily throughout Cancun and take an easy transfer to Xcaret, Isla Mujeres, Tulum, and Chichen Itza.

Learn more

## Things to do

### Chichen Itza

Explore Chichén Itzá: The majestic Pyramid of Kukulcán, the mysterious Great Ballcourt, and a sacred cenote await you at this astonishing Maya archaeological site in Mexico. Discover the grandeur of an ancient civilization!

Learn more

### Cobá

Unleash Your Inner Explorer at Cobá: Dive into the mysteries of the ancient Maya in Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula, where soaring pyramids and hidden jungle pathways await your discovery.

### Xcaret

Unearth Eden on Earth: Xcaret, the magical park of the Riviera Maya, where nature and culture intertwine in a paradise of underground rivers, cenotes, and dazzling performances. Embark on the adventure of a lifetime!

Learn more

### Xel Ha

Immerse Yourself in the Aquatic Paradise of the Riviera Maya with exciting things to do in Cancun. Explore the natural beauty of the Mexican Caribbean, swim with tropical fish, and embark on adventures like snorkeling and ziplining in the heart of the jungle. Live a magical experience in this aquatic paradise!

Learn more

### Garrafon Natural Reef Park

Discover this Adventure Paradise on Isla Mujeres. Immerse yourself in crystal-clear waters, soar on thrilling ziplines with breathtaking views, and explore a colorful coral reef. Relax on white sandy beaches and live an unforgettable experience in the Mexican Caribbean. Your adventure in paradise starts here!

Learn more

### Tulum

Where the Mayan past meets Caribbean paradise. Ancient ruins, dreamy beaches, and a bohemian atmosphere await you in this magical corner of the Riviera Maya. Discover the magic of Tulum!

### Golf Courses

The golf courses near the Hotel Presidente Intercontinental Cancun offer challenging layouts and breathtaking views of the Caribbean. Perfect for golfers of all levels and conveniently located for hotel guests. Golf Puerto Cancun is just a 15-minute drive away, while Cancun Golf Club at Pok ta Pok is just a 12-minute walk away.
