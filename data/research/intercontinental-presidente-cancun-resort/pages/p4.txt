### Charging stations for electric vehicles

InterContinental Presidente Cancun hotel provides electric vehicle charging stations, offering convenience and support to guests traveling with electric cars. With these available charging stations, you can conveniently charge your vehicle while enjoying your stay in our beautiful beach destination.

### InterContinental Presidente Cancun Resort Spa

Our spa is perfect for a health and wellness retreat. Visit our premier spa, where you can relax in a jacuzzi, melt the stress away in sauna rooms, and indulge your senses in our massage rooms. Energize your body, mind, and spirit with our special 80 minutes IKAL massage, a combination of four massage techniques from around the globe.
