## A family vacation in the place where magic begins

Celebration is a charming community in Orlando created by The Walt Disney Company and our hotel provides a welcoming and comfortable space next to the realm of dreams, ideal for enjoying an unforgettable family holiday. Meliá Orlando Celebration will exceed all your expectations with fantastic facilities designed to surprise you, such as its 360° infinity pool, spacious rooms, and every imaginable comfort. ### What are the benefits of being a MeliáRewards member?

Belonging means you get more: more value, because you get points, surprises and discounts; more possibilities, because you can earn and redeem your points in thousands of ways; and more time, because you become our top priority.
