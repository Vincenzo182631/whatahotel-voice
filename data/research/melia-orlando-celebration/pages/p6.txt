### Super Nintendo World

If you are passionate about classic video games, you can now immerse yourself in the colorful and exciting world of Super Mario Bros. The Mushroom Kingdom is full of mythical mushrooms, fire flowers, shells and pipes, making it the perfect setting to help Mario, Luigi and the rest of their friends beat the different levels that await them.

### The Magical World of Harry Potter

Experience the magic like never before! Enjoy an authentic experience in the world of magic, visiting from Paris in the 1920s to London in the 1990s. Perform magic tricks wherever you go, catch some of Newt Scamander’s fantastic animals or enjoy new adventures with Ron, Hermione or Harry Potter. Alohomora!

### How to train your dragon

Fly among dragons in a park that transports you to a Viking village on the Isle of Berk. Here you can meet your favorite dragons, train them, take part in a race or ride on a flying machine. With live shows, visit all the attractions and enjoy a great day with Hiccup, Astrid or Toothless.

### Dark universe

Confront the Universal freaks in an unforgettable terror experience. Experiments, mysteries and scares abound in a park where iconic creatures such as Dracula, the Mummy, the Werefoot and Frankenstein come to life in terrifying scenes from horror movies. If you like intense emotions and hair on your skin on end, this is the park for you!

### Celestial Park

Enjoy a cosmic park with attractions such as the Constellation Carousel and Stardust racers. A place that will transport you to a celestial universe where you can enjoy exciting intergalectic adventures, from spinning on a carousel inspired by the stars to futuristic races in space. A space experience between worlds!
