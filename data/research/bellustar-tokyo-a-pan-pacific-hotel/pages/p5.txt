### If you are coming by car

If you arrive by car, please park in the Tokyu Kabukicho Tower car park.

Parking tickets are sold at the hotel reception, so please inquire with a member of staff.

12 hours: 1,300 yen

24 hours: 2,500 yen

\*Please note that the car park has limitations on vehicle size, so check the Tokyu Kabukicho Tower website for details.
