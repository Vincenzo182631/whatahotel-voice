##### Your all-seasons playground

#### Melbourne, it’s time to play.

The Social Quarter is your all-seasons playground.

Whether it’s bowling or bao, mini golf or margaritas, there is something for everyone.

By day, enjoy long lunches in an open-air atmosphere, soak in city views from the sunlit terrace, or relish playful moments designed to delight all ages. As evening falls, the energy shifts – cocktails are poured, flavours from around the world come alive and we dial up the volume on fun.

Step into a destination where every moment becomes an experience to remember.
