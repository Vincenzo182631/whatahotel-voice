Enjoy the ultimate entertainment experience at Chadstone with your all-seasons playground, The Social Quarter. Whether it’s bowling or bao, mini golf or margaritas, there’s fun for all ages and occasions. Find an array of entertainment destinations, including LEGOLAND Discovery Centre and HOYTS. Together, they offer a complementary range of experiences, from a night out with friends to fun indoor activities the whole family can enjoy. Visit the one-stop destination where you can eat, drink, play and stay. Open daily until late.

Discover new life in ticket and arcade games, with dodgem cars, XD theatre and more rainy-day activities at **Archie Brothers Cirque Electriq**.

Let loose on the lanes at **Strike Bowling**. Inspired by the iconic New York Grand Central Station, this is not your average ten pin bowling alley.

**LEGOLAND DISCOVERY CENTRE** is your fun way to entertain little ones aged three to ten. This indoor play centre is one of a kind in Australia, boasting two awesome rides, a 4D cinema, interactive play zones and Miniland Melbourne – made from over 1.5 million LEGO bricks.

Play not stay at **Hijinx Hotel** with ten gamified rooms of fast-moving, mind-bending fun for all ages and group sizes. If you enjoy fun things for group outings, like escape rooms and immersive board games, Hijinx Hotel is your best indoor activity.

At **Holey Moley**, putt eighteen holes of indoor mini golf over two courses in a tantalising nostalgic labyrinth inspired by everyday items.

**HOYTS Chadstone** sets the pace in state-of-the-art entertainment. Get ready to sit back and relax in HOYTS’ signature extra-comfy recliners and enjoy a range of films for the ultimate cinema experience.
