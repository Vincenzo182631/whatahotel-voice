## The Fashion Capital

As Chadstone evolves, our reputation as The Fashion Capital remains central to who we are. Chadstone stands as the ultimate expression of style in the southern hemisphere – a world-class destination where fashion’s most coveted names come together under one roof. Roam beneath the iconic gridshell and discover more than 60 international luxury houses, Australia’s most celebrated designers, and a carefully curated mix of premium and high street favourites.

Take your experience to the next level with our exclusive Hands Free Shopping service. Indulge without interruption – then simply collect your purchases from a convenient point when you’re ready to go.  This way, you can shop ‘til you drop without lifting a finger.

## The Market Pavilion

At The Market Pavilion, you’ll find Melbourne’s best providores curated under one roof. This modern marketplace is overflowing with an abundance of produce and bursting with flavour. Shop fresh ingredients from Australia’s most celebrated foodies, pick up some grab-and-go meals or stop and savour a bite and raise a toast.

For the ultimate ease, our Food Concierge team is here to help – from storing your groceries while you shop to inspiring your next masterpiece with our innovative Recipe AI Tool. At Chadstone, dining and discovery are the perfect pairing.

## The Social Quarter

The Social Quarter is your all-seasons playground. Whether it’s bowling or bao, mini golf or margaritas, there is something for everyone. By day, enjoy long lunches in an open-air atmosphere, soak in city views from the sunlit terrace, or relish playful moments designed to delight all ages. As evening falls, the energy shifts – cocktails are poured, flavours from around the world come alive and we dial up the volume on fun. Step into a destination where every moment becomes an experience to remember.

## Hotel Chadstone

Welcome to the only five-star sanctuary in southeast Melbourne, a place where design and artisan culture isn’t just embraced but celebrated. Here, we curate every detail to make your stay unforgettable.

Each of our 250 rooms and suites is a personal haven — a seamless fusion of elegance and comfort. Whether you're seeking a romantic escape with a side of Melbourne’s best dining, some serene solo time at Holism Retreat, a stylish base to launch your shopping spree, or a pool-powered family getaway, your perfect escape awaits at Hotel Chadstone.

### Our commitment

We’re committed to making a positive impact. From our curated art collection to sustainability initiatives, we strive to foster inclusivity and actively support First Nations communities. We design our spaces with accessibility in mind, providing a safe and welcoming space for everyone.

Join us on this journey at Chadstone, where every visit is an opportunity to discover, connect and engage with a community that values inclusivity, culture and sustainability. We invite you to learn more about our art collection, sustainability programs, and other initiatives that help make Chadstone a leading lifestyle destination for all.
