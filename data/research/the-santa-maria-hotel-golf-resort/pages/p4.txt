# Explore the Attractions of Panama City

## Experiences

Panama City, a captivating destination, blends cosmopolitan elegance, Spanish Colonial charm, and natural beauty. Discover Costa del Este, a thriving enclave of fine dining, premier shopping, and a bustling financial district. Experience both the Pacific and Atlantic Oceans, with their stunning coastlines and marine adventures. ### Area & Activities

## Historic Treasures

Discover the cultural wonders that Panama has to offer.

### Treasures of Panama

Panama's ancient civilizations, cultural traditions and natural treasures come to life in this workshop. You'll see how reproductions of pre-Hispanic artifacts are created using the "lost wax process", the same method the ancient goldsmiths of Panama used thousands of years ago to make their wonderful pieces of gold. You'll see a live demonstration of metal casting and can participate in the process.

### Panama Viejo Historical Monument Complex

Just minutes from The Santa Maria Hotel & Golf Resort, the ruins of Panama Viejo showcase the remnants of the first European settlement on the Pacific Coast of the Americas, founded in 1519 by Pedro Arias Dávila. The site also reveals traces of the Isthmus's earliest inhabitants. A visit to Panama Viejo offers a captivating glimpse into Panama City’s rich history.

### Casco Antiguo

Founded in 1673 after Panama Viejo was destroyed by pirates, Panama City's historic district is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Its cobblestone streets and restored colonial architecture exude charm, while grand plazas, boutique shopping, exquisite dining, and cultural landmarks offer a blend of history and modern sophistication. Explore the rich heritage and vibrant present of this captivating area.

## Modern Marvels

Visit some of Panama's exciting modern attractions.

### BioMuseo

This stunning multicolor gem, designed by renowned architect Frank Gehry, celebrates Panama’s rich biodiversity through world-class exhibits. Eight galleries and "devices of wonder" reveal the origin of the Panamanian isthmus and its global impact on biodiversity, conceived by Canadian designer Bruce Mau.

### Panama Canal

One of the greatest engineering feats in history, the Panama Canal extends 80km from Panama City on the Pacific side to Colón on the Atlantic side. There are several ways to experience the canal, from the main Miraflores Visitor Center and the Interoceanic Canal Museum to the beautiful Amador Causeway Promenade.

Causeway Islands and Promenade

## Outdoor Adventures

The four man-made islands of Naos, Perico, Culebra and Flamenco were created with rock excavated during the building of the Panama Canal. Today, the narrow bridge connecting them is a favorite recreation spot for bikers, runners and walkers. Visitors enjoy a beautiful view of the city and the ships waiting to enter the Panama Canal.

### Shopping in Panama

There are a number of malls nearby so you can shop until you drop!

### TownCenter Mall

Located 2 miles from Santa Maria in the Costa del Este area, it offers shopping ranging from department stores to unique boutiques. An array of restaurants is also available, as well as movie theaters.

### Multiplaza Mall

One of the most impressive malls in Panama, Multiplaza Mall features over 200 stores offering a wide selection of world-renowned brands, fashions, shoes, bags and accessories and electronic goods. Visitors will also find over 30 restaurants ranging from local to international cuisine and more.

### Atrio Mall

Conveniently located 1 mile from Santa Maria.

### Albrook Mall

No matter what you’re shopping for, you’re sure to find it at Albrook Mall. Boasting over 350 stores, three food courts with more than one hundred restaurants, a movie theater, a bowling alley and casinos, it’s one of the top shopping and entertainment destinations in Panama City.
