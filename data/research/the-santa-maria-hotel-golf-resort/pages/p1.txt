### A Noteworthy Golf Course

## A Gorgeous 18-Hole Course

The centerpiece of our Panama City, Panama golf resort is a lush, verdant championship golf course designed by the legendary Jack Nicklaus. Meticulously groomed to perfection, this 18-hole, par-72 golf course features rippling fairways, beautiful greens and sculpted bunkers that offer a challenge for scratch handicappers and novice golfers alike. The layout measures 7,153 yards from the back tees and offers four tee box locations in order to accommodate players of all ages and skill levels.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City?

The check-in time at The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City.

What are the parking options at The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City?

On-site parking is available at The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City booking.

What is the closest airport near The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City?

The closest airport near The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City is Tocumen International Airport (PTY). PTY is located approximately 14.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Santa Maria, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Golf Resort, Panama City does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
