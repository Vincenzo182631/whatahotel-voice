### Terrible Hospitality, Not Worth the Money

We stayed The Beekman for one night on a weekend away to NYC and were terribly disappointed at the hospitality of this hotel. We expected to be awed and swept away by what was supposed to be an upscale hotel stay but we were treated as if we didn't pay $800 to stay there for one night. The hotel staff from the front desk to the concierge was rude and looked down at you. They acted as if they were inconvenienced by our presence and questions and as if we owed them something for letting us stay at their hotel. We just expected some respect but didn't even get that, we were given no information upon check in, were checked out earlier than our 2PM so were interrupted by 3 separate people waiting for us to leave, gave us a non functioning steamer, and then made it seem like we were the nuisance when we were actually checking out and had some questions about costs we didn't expect to see on our credit card that we weren't told would be temporary. Truly disappointing experience because of the hotel staff, do not recommend staying here unless you want to be treated poorly and looked down on.

### There are a lot of inflated 5-star hotels and 5-star reviews but sincerely, this was what a 5-star hotel should be.

I have stayed in literally hundreds of hotels, and many that claim to be 5 star but the Beekman stands out for incredible service and overall value. Dan at the Front desk treated us like long lost friends even found us a better room without asking when there was a cancellation (ps if you can go for the Studio it is worth the upgrade). It was our anniversary, and within minutes of arriving the Concierge brought us complimentary Prosecco and chocolate. The waitress at the lounge could not have been nicer and the dinner (it was restaurant week - was an outstanding value). There are a lot of inflated 5-star hotels and 5-star reviews but sincerely, this was what a 5-star hotel should be.
