## Made the List

We invite you to celebrate the shine of the season with us at W Brisbane. Here, no matter who you are, you're on our list. Discover our dynamic holiday programme that extends our iconic W Hotel's energy to the location we call home. Designed to inspire curiosity and engage each guest that joins us this year, we invite you to discover what's on.
