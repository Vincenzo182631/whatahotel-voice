Discover the epitome of cosmopolitan sophistication and dynamic energy at W Brisbane, our 5-star luxury hotel located in the vibrant heart of Brisbane's CBD, overlooking the iconic Brisbane River. Delight in an immersive dining experience at our signature restaurant,...See More The Lex, or enjoy expertly crafted cocktails at Living Room Bar. Pamper yourself at AWAY Spa or soak up the Sunshine-State sun at WET Deck, our rooftop oasis featuring a 24/7 pool and cocktail bar with stunning views. ## Our Commitment to Sustainability

At W Brisbane, we’re dedicated to sustainability at our luxury hotel in Brisbane. From premium electric vehicle chargers to eco-friendly design and energy-efficient practices, we’re always innovating to minimise our footprint. We’re proud to partner with Polestar to support luxury sustainable travel and with OzHarvest to reduce food waste and redirect surplus food to those in need

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Brisbane?

The check-in time at W Brisbane is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Brisbane allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Brisbane. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Brisbane?

On-site valet parking is available at W Brisbane. The daily valet fee is $70.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Brisbane have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Brisbane has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Brisbane booking.

What is the closest airport near W Brisbane?

The closest airport near W Brisbane is Brisbane Airport (BNE). BNE is located approximately 19.4 KM from the hotel.

Does W Brisbane have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Brisbane has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
