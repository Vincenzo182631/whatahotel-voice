## Dining

W Brisbane features three hotel dining destinations, including signature restaurant The Lex, a New York-style steak restaurant in Brisbane putting a modern spin on classic favourites. Kick back at our WET Deck for a sun-soaked afternoon of refreshing sips and light bites, or meet at Living Room Bar for small plates and curated cocktails shaken up by our expert mixologists.
