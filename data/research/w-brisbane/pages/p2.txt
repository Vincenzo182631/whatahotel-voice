### Penthouse I

It’s big, bold and beautiful. Penthouse I is located on the top floor of the hotel and exudes panoramic views of the Brisbane River all the way to the mountains and beyond. The 200sqm penthouse is ideal for entertaining guests, complete with a fully equipped bar, large dining space and state-of-the-art technology including 65-inch LG Ultra-HD TVs and LG Soundbars that offer the highest performance in vision and sound. For an additional charge, our culinary team can curate a private dining experience or soiree, taking your stay or celebration to ultimate luxury.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Brisbane have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Brisbane have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Brisbane?

The largest occupancy room at W Brisbane is the Supreme Double Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Brisbane?

Some rooms at W Brisbane permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Brisbane have fridges?

No, rooms at W Brisbane do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W Brisbane have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Brisbane have a Coffee/tea maker.
