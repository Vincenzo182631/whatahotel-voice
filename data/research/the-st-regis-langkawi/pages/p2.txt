# Unwind in luxury family villas in Langkawi

## Accommodations

Revel in the spacious beachfront accommodation in Langkawi at The St. Regis Langkawi. Book one of four overwater holiday villas in Langkawi with private pools for a luxury experience and soak in the sunsets and views of the Andaman Sea. Each morning, arise and take in the colors of the white-sand beaches, emerald-green waters, and lush foliage. Our hotel's three Sunset Overwater Villas and the Sunset Royal Villa each boast a private terrace and infinity pool, making them superb choices for your honeymoon or anniversary suite. Expect elegance in the residential-style rooms and suites of our luxury villa resort in Langkawi​. White timber paneling that contrasts with the dark timber flooring decorates each of our family villas in Langkawi. The Sunset Overwater Villas boast enormous windows for an open and airy feel, ensuring our guests feel at home amidst the secluded beauty of Langkawi. Rest well atop plush king beds with 300-thread-count, , Egyptian cotton linens and a down comforter
