# Exquisite culinary experiences await in Malaysia

## Dining

The St. Regis Langkawi features six distinct restaurants and luxury beach bars in Langkawi​, certain to please every palate. Enjoy signature Asian fusion cuisine at Kayuputi restaurant or relax at Pantai Grill, Langkawi's beachfront restaurant serving seafood grill. Additional dining options include our on-site deli and our hotel's signature St. Regis Afternoon Tea. We encourage our guests to dress in a smart casual style so as to remain comfortable from breakfast through dinner.

The St. Regis Bar

## Legendary King Cole Bar

The St. Regis Bar celebrates Langkawi’s natural beauty with its striking design, featuring an expansive installation of banyan tree bark, sculpted trunks, and suspended leaves set against a granite wall. Dark timber flooring and 200kg fossil-wood tables add depth and character to this luxury beach bar in Langkawi​. Guests can unwind with expertly crafted cocktails inspired by Langkawi’s flavours, accompanied by the smooth rhythms of our live band each evening.

## An Exquisite Afternoon Tea Affair

Following the dignified soirees hosted by Caroline Astor, mother of John Jacob Astor IV, The St Regis Langkawi offers guests an exquisite selection of fine teas along with classic sweets and savouries with a local twist. Available daily from 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM at Drawing Room, Lobby at MYR 180 nett for two persons.

## In-Room Dining Experience

With round the clock availability, our guests have the option of arranging a private dining service in the comforts of their own room while our chefs prepare your favorite meals from our handcrafted menu. Be it a lavish spread for an intimate gathering your villa, or suite, or a casual late night craving, our master chefs stand ready to indulge you with an exceptional meal.
