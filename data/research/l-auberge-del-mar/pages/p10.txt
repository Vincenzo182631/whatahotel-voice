## FAQ

What kind of atmosphere and views can guests expect at L'Auberge Del Mar's Pool Bar?

At L’Auberge Del Mar’s Pool Bar, guests can expect a vibrant “see-and-be-seen spot” that captures the ethereal and sun-kissed locale. It offers stunning ocean views and is ideal for warm conversation and sun-soaked sipping.

What food and drinks are served at the Pool Bar?

The Pool Bar at L’Auberge Del Mar serves a variety of poolside sips, including frozen cocktails, and specialty dishes from the kitchen. It provides a perfect combination of refreshing drinks and delicious bites to complement your time by the ocean-view pool.

Are there dedicated services or amenities provided by a poolside concierge at L'Auberge Del Mar?

Yes, the Pool Bar at L’Auberge Del Mar is supported by a dedicated poolside concierge. This service aims to provide every detail of the true cabana life, ensuring guests have everything they need for a relaxing and indulgent experience by the pool.

What are the operating hours for the Pool Bar and poolside service?

The Pool Bar at L’Auberge Del Mar is generally open daily. Poolside service is available from 11:00 am to 5:00 pm daily, and the Pool Bar itself is open until 7:00 pm, extending opportunities for evening sips and relaxation.

Is the Pool Bar a good place for relaxing and socializing during a stay at L'Auberge Del Mar?

Absolutely, the Pool Bar at L’Auberge Del Mar is designed as a central hub for relaxation and socializing. Its inviting atmosphere, coupled with ocean views and comfortable settings, makes it an ideal spot for guests to unwind and connect.

How does the Pool Bar contribute to the overall coastal experience at L'Auberge Del Mar?

The Pool Bar at L’Auberge Del Mar is integral to the coastal experience, offering “cocktail time anytime” by the sea. It captures the essence of a sun-kissed Del Mar day, blending refreshing drinks and delightful dishes with stunning ocean views to create a quintessential Southern California retreat.
