## FAQ

What kind of coffee and pastries can I find at Lyric Coffee at L'Auberge Del Mar?

Lyric Coffee serves specialty drinks made with Bird Rock Coffee Roasters, including lattes, espresso drinks, and more. You’ll also find a selection of fresh pastries that pair perfectly with your morning coffee.

What is the ambiance like at Lyric Coffee?

Lyric Coffee has a warm, inviting feel—comfortable enough to relax in, yet stylish enough to feel special. It blends the vibe of a favorite local café with the charm of a coastal getaway.

What are the operating hours for Lyric Coffee at L'Auberge Del Mar?

Lyric Coffee is open daily from 6:30 am to 5:00 pm, making it easy to stop in for an early-morning coffee or enjoy a relaxed break later in the day.

Does Lyric Coffee feature a specific coffee roaster?

Yes. Lyric Coffee proudly serves Bird Rock Coffee Roasters, known for high-quality beans and rich, flavorful blends

Is there a boutique available at L'Auberge Del Mar, and what does it offer?

Yes. A boutique is located inside Lyric Coffee, offering stylish clothing, resort essentials, branded drinkware, and premium spa products—perfect for picking up something special during your stay.

How does Lyric Coffee contribute to a guest's daily routine?

Lyric Coffee is designed to be a peaceful spot to start the day or take a mindful break. Whether grabbing a quick coffee or lingering for a moment of calm, it helps guests ease into their day at their own pace.
