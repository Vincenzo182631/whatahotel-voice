## FAQ

What types of luxury rooms and suites are available at L'Auberge Del Mar?

L’Auberge Del Mar offers a range of luxurious accommodations, including elegant guest rooms and spacious suites, as well as accessible rooms designed to be your home by the sea. These estate-style rooms feature plush bedding, private outdoor spaces, and luxurious amenities, providing a sophisticated and relaxing retreat.

Which suites offer ocean views or are closest to the coast?

The Pacific One Bedroom Suite has a private balcony with partial ocean views, perfect for enjoying the sea breeze. The Del Mar One Bedroom Suite also features multiple balconies with partial coastal views, offering a relaxing coastal experience.

What amenities are included in the suites at L'Auberge Del Mar?

All suites come with thoughtful touches like custom bookshelves, marble bathrooms, plush bathrobes and slippers, a Bluetooth sound system, a 42-inch TV, in-room coffee and tea, and a fully stocked minibar.

Are there options for families or multi-generational groups seeking spacious accommodations at L'Auberge Del Mar?

L’Auberge Del Mar offers luxurious suites that provide ample space, designed for a comfortable and stylish experience for both couples and families. These spacious accommodations are ideal for multi-generational family visits, providing separate living areas and room to spread out.

Are any of the suites good for hosting guests or larger families?

Yes. The Del Mar One Bedroom Suite is the best option for entertaining, with two large living areas and a dining table that seats six. For bigger groups or multi-generational stays, the Entertaining Patio Guest Rooms combine four Village King Fireplace rooms with a shared outdoor patio

How do the suite designs reflect the coastal setting?

The suites blend upscale design with a relaxed beach feel. Marble bathrooms and large private balconies make it easy to enjoy the ocean air and connect with the coastal surroundings.

Do any suites include private outdoor space?

Yes. The Junior Village One Bedroom Suite has two private balconies, and the Pacific and Del Mar One Bedroom Suites also feature oversized private balconies where guests can unwind outdoors.

What is the largest and most luxurious suite available?

The Del Mar One Bedroom Suite is the largest and most luxurious option, offering about 1,222 square feet of space. It includes three private balconies with partial coastal views, two roomy living areas (one with a fireplace), a dining table for six, and a master bathroom with a Roman tub.
