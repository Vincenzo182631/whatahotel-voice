## FAQ

What is the ambiance of The Living Room Bar at L'Auberge Del Mar?

The Living Room Bar offers a friendly yet stylish atmosphere, blending the comfort of a cozy lounge with a chic coastal vibe. Plush seating and a spirited energy make it an inviting space to relax and connect.

Does The Living Room Bar offer live music or other entertainment?

Yes. Guests can enjoy live music from a variety of artists on weekends and throughout the summer, adding to the lively and vibrant setting.

What types of drinks and cuisine are served at The Living Room Bar?

The Living Room Bar features inventive cocktails crafted by skilled mixologists, along with decadent, elevated cuisine. The combination creates a refined yet comfortable dining and drinking experience.

Is there a Happy Hour at The Living Room Bar?

Yes. Happy Hour is available Sunday through Thursday from 3:00 pm to 5:00 pm, offering innovative cocktails, delicious bites, and beautiful ocean views.

What are the operating hours for The Living Room Bar at L'Auberge Del Mar?

The Living Room Bar is open daily from 11:00 am to 10:00 pm Sunday–Wednesday, until 11:00 pm on Thursdays, and until 12:00 am on Fridays and Saturdays.

Does The Living Room Bar provide scenic views for guests?

Yes. The Living Room Bar offers impressive coastal views, making it a perfect spot to enjoy sunsets and sips in a relaxed, picturesque setting.

How does The Living Room Bar cater to personalized service for guests?

The Living Room Bar focuses on creating a warm, personalized experience, with attentive staff and mixologists who take pride in offering friendly, tailored service that makes guests feel truly welcomed.
