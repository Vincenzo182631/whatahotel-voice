## FAQ

Where can I find upscale oceanfront dining at a California beach resort?

Adelaide is the signature restaurant at L’Auberge Del Mar, serving locally sourced cuisine with dramatic views over the Pacific. Travel + Leisure has called the experience a “Perfect Southern California Getaway.” The resort keeps three more venues for lighter moments: the Pool Bar for poolside plates, the Living Room Bar for cocktails and small plates, and Lyric Coffee for mornings. All of it is steps from the sand in the heart of Del Mar Village.

Is Adelaide one of the best ocean-view restaurants in Del Mar?

Adelaide is the ocean-view restaurant inside L’Auberge Del Mar, the AAA Four Diamond resort in the center of Del Mar Village. Ranch & Coast Magazine named it San Diego’s Best Restaurant in 2022, and Sunset Magazine featured it in its 2022 Travel Awards for beachside dining. The menu is built with local farmers, fishermen and foragers, and the room looks straight out over the Pacific. It serves breakfast, lunch, weekend brunch and dinner, and reservations are open to everyone, not only hotel guests, through OpenTable or by calling 858-793-6467.

Does Adelaide have a private dining room for an anniversary dinner or a small group?

Yes. The Pearl is Adelaide’s private dining room, about 363 square feet and seating up to 12, with a menu customized for the evening. For a larger party, the resort’s Ocean Terrace seats up to 20 for a private meal with coastline views, the Library hosts up to 40, and the Ruby Room with its fire-pit patio holds up to 100 for a reception. Call 858-793-6467 to book The Pearl, or contact the events team at 858-793-6445 for the larger rooms.

What is Adelaide known for, and what's on the menu?

Breakfast puts a coastal twist on the classics, with the L’Auberge crab Benedict and CC’s Chilaquiles. Lunch brings gourmet salads, fish tacos and a popular raw bar, and dinner centers on fresh seafood, seasonal oysters and ingredient-driven entrees sourced from local farmers, fishermen and foragers. The wine list runs from Champagne to California and French benchmarks, and the signature cocktails nod to the hotel’s Hollywood past, with drinks named for Lucille Ball, Desi Arnaz, Bing Crosby and Jimmy Durante. Current menus are posted on this page.

What are Adelaide's hours, and do I need a reservation?

Breakfast is served Monday through Friday from 7 AM to 11:30 AM, brunch on Saturday and Sunday from 7 AM to 3 PM, lunch Monday through Friday from 11:30 AM to 3 PM, and dinner from 5 PM to 9 PM Sunday through Thursday and 5 PM to 10 PM on Friday and Saturday. Reservations are recommended and can be made on OpenTable or by calling 858-793-6467. A children’s menu is available, and in-room dining is offered for hotel guests.

Does Adelaide restaurant at L'Auberge Del Mar provide ocean views for diners?

Yes, Adelaide restaurant at L’Auberge Del Mar provides dramatic ocean views, turning even ordinary nights out into unforgettable experiences. This stunning backdrop enhances the refined and elevated coastal California dining experience.

Does Adelaide focus on locally sourced or sustainable ingredients?

Adelaide proudly works with local farmers, fishermen, and foragers to feature responsibly sourced, sustainable, and honest offerings. This ingredient-driven approach showcases ingredients at their peak, aligning with a focus on cuisine that is “rooted in the earth.

Are there private dining options available at Adelaide for special occasions or groups?

Yes, Adelaide features an exclusive private dining room called The Pearl. Dinner and event experiences at The Pearl are fully customized to deliver on the needs and desires of any gathering, reflecting the sophisticated ambiance of L’Auberge Del Mar.

What are the dining hours for brunch and dinner at Adelaide?

Adelaide serves brunch daily from 7:00 am to 3:00 pm. Dinner hours are from 5:00 pm to 9:00 pm from Sunday to Thursday, and from 5:00 pm to 10:00 pm on Fridays and Saturdays.

Has Adelaide restaurant received any awards or recognition?

Yes, Adelaide has been honored for its excellence in dining. It was recognized as San Diego’s Best Restaurant in 2022 by Ranch & Coast Magazine and featured in Sunset Magazine’s 2022 Travel Awards for Beachside Dining.

Can specific dietary needs, like plant-based options, be accommodated at Adelaide given its “rooted in the earth” approach?

Yes. Adelaide’s “rooted in the earth” philosophy highlights fresh, locally sourced ingredients, making it easy to accommodate a variety of dietary preferences. The menu features options like gourmet salads and raw bar selections, and the culinary team is happy to adjust dishes or offer plant-based choices upon request.
