## FAQ

What types of luxurious accommodations are available at L'Auberge Del Mar?

L’Auberge Del Mar offers a range of luxurious accommodations, including elegant guest rooms and spacious suites, as well as accessible rooms designed to be your home by the sea. These estate-style rooms feature plush bedding, private outdoor spaces, and luxurious amenities, providing a sophisticated and relaxing retreat.

How do the guest rooms at L'Auberge Del Mar cater to privacy and relaxation for couples?

Guest rooms and suites at L’Auberge Del Mar are designed as an elegant and relaxing retreat, featuring plush bedding, private outdoor spaces, and personal amenities to enhance intimacy and privacy. Our ocean-view rooms and suites provide a serene environment, ideal for romantic getaways where couples can enjoy seaside walks and sunset views directly from their private balconies.

What in-room amenities and comforts can guests expect during their stay at L'Auberge Del Mar?

Each guest room at L’Auberge Del Mar is designed with Barclay Butera decor and includes full marble bathrooms with signature bath amenities, a 42-inch or larger TV with premium channels, an iPod docking alarm clock radio, a fully stocked refreshment center, and reliable WiFi. These amenities ensure a comfortable and sophisticated experience during your seaside retreat.

Are there options for families or multi-generational groups seeking spacious accommodations at L'Auberge Del Mar?

L’Auberge Del Mar offers luxurious suites that provide ample space, designed for a comfortable and stylish experience for both couples and families. These spacious accommodations are ideal for multi-generational family visits, providing separate living areas and room to spread out.

Do the rooms at L'Auberge Del Mar offer ocean views or private outdoor spaces?

Yes, L’Auberge Del Mar features rooms with private outdoor spaces, allowing guests to make themselves at home in bright, airy settings. Many accommodations offer stunning ocean views, providing a picturesque backdrop for stress relief and connection to the Pacific Ocean.

Is L'Auberge Del Mar a pet-friendly resort, and what amenities are available for pets?

Yes, L’Auberge Del Mar is a pet-friendly resort, welcoming your beloved pets to enjoy the plush amenities alongside you during your stay. We ensure that every member of your family, including your furry companions, can relax and unwind in our luxurious coastal retreat.

How does L'Auberge Del Mar blend modern comforts with a serene coastal retreat feel in its accommodations?

L’Auberge Del Mar’s accommodations combine modern comforts with a classic Southern California retreat ambiance, featuring Barclay Butera decor and contemporary amenities. The design creates a serene environment that encourages relaxation, allowing guests to enjoy indoor-outdoor living spaces and a peaceful seaside experience.
