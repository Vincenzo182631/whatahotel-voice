# Rooted in the Earth. Cultivated energy. Signature experiences.

Adelaide pairs dramatic ocean views with a refined and elevated approach to coastal California dining that is both ocean-forward and rooted in the earth. Adelaide turns even ordinary nights out into unforgettable experiences.

## Adelaide

Discover an ocean-forward, ingredient-driven coastal Del Mar, CA restaurant that celebrates the bounty of the Pacific waters ranging from Baja to the North Salish Sea. We proudly work with local farmers, fisherman and foragers that feature responsibly sourced, sustainable and honest offerings to showcase ingredients at their peak for guests who seek out distinction and prominence through their experiences.

VISIT ADELAIDE RESTAURANT

## Pool Bar

Charged by the sun and supported by a poolside concierge providing you with every detail of the true cabana life—Pool Bar captures the ethereal and sun-kissed locale with its own heartbeat. Frozen sips and specialty dishes from the kitchen will round out the experience—a chaise, a frosé, and the Del Mar sun add up to Pool Bar perfection.

Something new is drifting your way.

## Lyric Coffee

Lyric Coffee isn’t a music mecca or a shrine to the guitar gods—it’s an ode to our individual rhythms and daily rituals. Proudly featuring Bird Rock Coffee Roasters, our elevated coffee shop fuses the energy of a local’s favorite café with the allure of Del Mar’s newest standalone coffee experience.
