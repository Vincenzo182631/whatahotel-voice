## Your Monthly Spa Ritual

Receive one 50-minute Signature Spa Treatment each calendar month, with the option to upgrade your service. Plus, enjoy 15% off additional spa services whenever you’re ready for a little more relaxation.

## Indulge Beyond the Spa

Enjoy 10% off retail, Adelaide, Lyric, and cabana experiences, giving you even more ways to enjoy your time at L’Auberge. Extend your day with a leisurely meal, discover something special from our retail selection, or settle into a cabana and make the most of your time by the sea.

Stay active with weekday access to the Spa L’Auberge fitness center, available Monday through Thursday. Fit movement into your routine with a dedicated space to focus on strength, wellness, and feeling your best.
