Dress Code:
For Gentlemen, it is required to wear long pants, shirt and dress shoes or dress sandals. Cocktail dress, long trousers, long skirt, blouse and elegant sandals is required for Ladies. Omani Traditional Attire is welcomed. Please adhere to appropriate clothing, and refrain from wearing flip-flops, sleeveless tops and athletic apparel.
