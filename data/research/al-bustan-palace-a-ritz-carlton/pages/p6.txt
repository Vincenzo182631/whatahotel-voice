Explore Oman’s Past and Present From This Luxury Beachfront Hotel
Experiences
From the moment of arrival at this secluded resort, when dates and coffee are presented in a gesture of welcome and the smell of frankincense wafts through the lobby, there is the profound sense of belonging to two worlds. There is the world of tradition, expressed in customs, cuisine and nearby attractions like the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque. Then there is the world of today, which appears in the newly renovated design, the resort’s six glittering pools and luxury shopping in Muscat. Experience the best of both at this Muscat hotel on the beach, where guests can swim or dive along the private sands, perfect their backhand through the resort’s tennis program and practice yoga in a private or group lesson. Or venture out to explore iconic attractions like the Muttrah Souk, the National Museum of Oman or Al Alam Palace, all located just 10 minutes away.

A Beachfront Spa Honoring Omani Traditions
Encompassing 33,000 sq. ft. and three levels within an Arabian fort-inspired structure, Al Bustan Palace Spa by The Ritz-Carlton offers a distinct wellness experience. The beachfront sanctuary pulls from Omani culture both for its design and its treatments, which feature locally sourced ingredients. Treatment rooms include six hammam rooms, and separate ladies’ and gentlemen’s relaxation areas feature infrared saunas, ice fountains and steam rooms.

Before You Come
When should I arrive?
For your enhanced comfort and relaxation, we suggest that you arrive 30 minutes before your scheduled treatment in order to complete your health assessment form and take time to relax in the tranquil surroundings of our spa before your treatment. We recommend using the infrared sauna and steam, prior to any treatment. This will stimulate your blood and lymphatic systems and enhance the benefits of your spa treatment.
Who can help me choose the right treatment for me?
Our Spa reception will help you plan the perfect Spa experience. Unique Spa combinations are specially designed to offer you a selection of our most popular treatments.
Should men shave before a facial?
Shaving isn’t necessary but it’s recommended. If you do choose to shave prior to your facial, be sure to do so at least 2-3 hours prior to your scheduled appointment.
What about my jewelry and valuables?
Please leave such items in the safe in your hotel room. There are lockers available for your personal items in the Spa facility.
What do I wear during my treatment?
Wear whatever is comfortable to you. All of our therapists are trained to drape towels discreetly over any areas of the body not involved with the treatment and protect your privacy at all times. Disposable undergarments are also available at the Spa.
Spa Policies
Is there minimum age?
We love children; however, we recommend that you avoid bringing children younger than 16 years old to the spa. Young guests, 16 – 18 years old are welcome to enjoy a selection of treatments under the supervision of a guardian.
What if I am late for my appointment?
Arriving late will simply limit time for your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest is not delayed. The full cost of the treatment will be applied.
What is the cancellation policy?
If you must reschedule your appointment, please notify us five hours before your scheduled appointment to avoid being charged the full service fee. Cancellations of packages or private parties require 24 hour notice.
What about payment for the Spa service?
All major credit cards are accepted. You may also charge Spa services to your hotel bill or membership account if applicable. All prices are quoted in OMR Omani Riyals and are inclusive of applicable taxes, service charges, and 5% VAT. For your convenience, payment is also accepted prior to your treatments.
What are the Spa hours of operation?
Spa: 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.
Treatment Times: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.
Ladies Beach: 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.
What about gratuities?
Gratuities are not included in the Spa treatment’s price. They are a matter of personal discretion.
What about taxes?
Prices are inclusive of applicable taxes, service charges, and 5% VAT.
Is Smoking allowed in the Spa?
Al Bustan Palace Spa by The Ritz-Carlton is a non-smoking environment.
May I request a male or female therapist for the treatments?
As per country law, your therapist will be of the same gender.
Can I keep my mobile phone at the spa?
In consideration of other guests, smoking and active mobile phones are not permitted in the spa. Al Bustan Palace Spa by The Ritz-Carlton is a sanctuary of peace and harmony, so please be aware of the volume of your voice so as not to disturb other guests.
Health Questions
What about my comfort?
This is your time and you should enjoy the experience to the fullest. Whether it’s the room temperature, the amount of massage pressure, or the volume of the music. Please notify us of any discomfort preferences.
What if I have special health considerations?
Please notify our Spa reception before booking your treatment if you have high blood pressure, allergies, other physical ailments or disabilities. If you have any concern at all, let us know in advance. You will be asked to complete a full medical questionnaire prior to your treatment.
What if I am pregnant?
While there are a few services that should be avoided during pregnancy, there are many beneficial treatments that can be enjoyed. If you are pregnant, please notify our Spa desk agent when you make your appointments so that they can offer guidance.

Attractions
Discover Oman, a country of breathtaking natural beauty that is rich in history, tradition and diverse culture. Our five star beachfront resort is conveniently located near the main attractions in Muscat such as the Muttrah souk, Muttrah corniche and Al Alam Palace. Oman has a lot of activities and memorable adventures to offer from its waters, deserts and wide ranges of mountains. A daily bus shuttle is available to our guests to visit the Muttrah Souk and more can be arranged through the concierge.

Points of Interest
Al Bustan Palace Area
Visit the nearby towns of Al Bustan, Haramel, Sidab on your way to old Muscat City see the coastline by driving through this road and maybe a chance of a free catch of the day by the locals in Sidab bring that back to Al Bustan Palace and ask the chef to prepare you a meal out of it for a total authentic Omani experience.
Fish Market
Leave the hotel early and head down to the Muttrah Fish Market and small vegetable market to watch the fisherman bring their catch directly from their boats and sell to the local community. You will find this lots of fun and exciting to see how the locals interact with the traders. This area offers great photo opportunities. You can find a lot of kinds of fish: tuna, shrimps, octopus and even shark sometimes.
Forts
Go back to the Glorious past of Oman by visting Nizwa Fort, Bahla Fort, Rustaq Fort, Nakhal Fort, Hazam Castle, Gibreen Castle where they date back before Islam Era.
Jabal Shams
Known as the Grand Canyon of Middle East, Jabal Shams is 3,000 meters above sea level. Located 250 kilometers away from Muscat City.
Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque
Considered one of the biggest mosque in the middle east, The Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque boasts an architecture affair that no other place has and looking to the biggest chandler and Persian Carpet this is an educational tour not to be missed.
Traditional Houses
Misfat Al Abriyeen village is located in Wilayt AlHamra in A’Dakhiliyah region. It is characterised by its alleys, traditional buildings, high mud buildings, and agricultural terraces that wrap around the mountain, along with the old buildings.
Sports and Entertainment
Diving at Bandar Al Khayran Reserve
The place is the ideal point with divers, especially with the presence of more than 22 diving locations, each with its own charm and beauty. 30 minutes away boat ride from Marina Bandar Al Rawdha which is five minutes away from Al Bustan Palace.
Dolphin Watching
Dolphin shoals visit the shores of Oman in large numbers. Muscat and Musandam are famous for dolphin watching, Take the ride from Marina Bandar Al Rawdha which is five minutes away from Al Bustan Palace for an early morning glimpse of the elegant mammal.
Fishing (All Around Oman)
The waters off Oman are home to some of the finest fishing in the world more than 30 variety species are found on the coastline of the country, these fishes are well known for their quality and can be found in big markets around the world such as Tsukiji Fish Market in Tokyo.

Jabal Sifah Golf Course
Designed by legendary Peter Harradine the 18-hole golf course comes equipped with a high end fully functional Club House, driving range and practice facilities, and mountain-links style holes that run along the beach front. The golf course has been formulated to preserve the environment with minimal disturbance to the natural area, and elevating the surrounding views and the sea of Oman.
Muscat Hills Golf & Country Club
Located in Airport Heights, The 18 Holes with 68 Pars Muscat Hill golf course has been designed by the internationally acclaimed golf course designer, Paul Thomas, of David Thomas Associates. The company’s design portfolio is impressive and includes golf courses at La Manga West (Spain), Seve Ballesteros Golf Club and the Top Players Golf Club (Japan).
Overnight Camping at Wahiba Sands
Get away from the hustle and bustle of the city and have an overnight stay at camping locations 1001 nights’ style. Located 300 kilometers away from Muscat, Try dunes bashing on your way to Bedouin family tent as you will be seeing the traditional hospitality of Omanis, relax back with a nice BBQ dinner and listen to the Omani Oud player bring you back to the past while feeling warm and comfortable front of the fire setup at night.
Al Mouj Golf
Al Mouj Golf is located along a two-kilometre stretch of pristine coastline, flanking the Indian Ocean with the stunning Hajjar mountain range. Designed by the legendary Greg Norman, the award winning 18 holes PGA Links golf course and 9 holes Par 3 Floodlit course are complimented by world-class facilities including our restaurant and fully equipped pro shop.
Turtles Watching
Go to Ras Al Jinz reserve 70 Km off Sur City and see the biggest Green Turtles Reserve in the world (50,000 Green Sea Turtles lives within 5 km ). Also famous as the first point to see sunrise in the Arab world Ras Al Hadd which is nearby from Ras Al Jinz has one of the country best beach spots.
