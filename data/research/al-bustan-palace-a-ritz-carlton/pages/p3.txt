Elevate your Stay at The Omani Palace
Signature Accommodations
Nestled against the dramatic backdrop of rugged mountains, Al Bustan Palace, a Ritz-Carlton Hotel, offers a luxurious retreat amid an emerald green oasis. Spanning 81 hectares of lush beachfront landscaping, the hotel overlooks the glittering azure waters of the Sea of Oman. Experience unparalleled luxury in our elevated rooms and suites, including Lagoon Access Rooms, Junior Family Suites, Executive Suites, and the prestigious Presidential Suite. Each accommodation offers breathtaking views of mountains, sea, lagoons, and pools, ensuring a serene and picturesque stay. Indulge in exceptional features such as an expansive private beach, beautifully renovated guest rooms and suites, unique dining experiences, and stunning vistas of the surrounding natural beauty. Whether you seek relaxation or adventure, Al Bustan Palace provides the perfect setting for an unforgettable escape.
