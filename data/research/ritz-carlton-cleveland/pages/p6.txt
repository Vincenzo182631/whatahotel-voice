Elevate your Stay at our Hotel Lounge in Cleveland
Discover our Cleveland hotel with an exclusive Club Lounge​
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience. Combining exclusivity and comfort, our private hotel lounge in Cleveland features signature personalized service and attention to detail that The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities, and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.
Reserve Now
A Private Retreat
Club Lounge Access
Begin your stay with us in our exclusive hotel lounge, where you’ll be greeted by our Club ambassador and enjoy a dedicated check-in, exclusively for our Club guests. For the rest of your stay in Cleveland, consider the Club Lounge your private sanctuary. For guests who reserve a stay on The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level, we welcome up to two guests per room to enjoy Club Lounge xaaccess. An additional charge per person per day will be applied to your account for any additional guests.
