Experiences
Cleveland’s past takes on new forms in the city’s present. Art galleries set up shop in former factories, the 1912 West Side Market thrives as a destination for locals, and an 1892 streetcar electric plant is now home to the Greater Cleveland Aquarium. Like the hotel, the city has reinvented itself, yet beloved elements remain. Guests at our hotel close to Rocket Arena in Cleveland can cheer on local sports teams at Progressive Field, visit Lake Erie, or attend events at the Cleveland Convention Center or visit the Cleveland Clinic.

West Side Market
Welcome to the historic Westside market, located in the Ohio city neighborhood. Discover Cleveland's foodie culture with this family-oriented marketplace and enjoy wide selection of meat, fresh produce, bakery goods, seafood, and more.
Rock and Roll Hall of Fame Museum
Our hotel by the Rock n Roll Hall of Fame is ideally located to experience one of Cleveland's most famous attractions. The Rock and Roll Hall of Fame and Museum is dedicated to recording the history of the best-known musicians and producers across all musical genres.
Rocket Arena
Rocket Arena is connected to our Cleveland hotel via an indoor walkway. Home to the NBA's Cleveland Cavaliers, this arena hosts some of the biggest names in entertainment.
Ritz Kids
Children receive a red-carpet welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Cleveland. The Ritz Kids program at our Cleveland hotel near Progressive Field offers daily, kid-friendly activities that introduce participants to the city’s history and culture. Cleveland is also home to many family-friendly attractions, and the hotel’s concierge can help create an exciting itinerary.
