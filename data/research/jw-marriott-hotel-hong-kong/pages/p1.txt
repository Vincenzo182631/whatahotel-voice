# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong

## Discover sophistication at our downtown Hong Kong hotel

Revitalize body, mind and spirit when you stay at JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong. Located above Pacific Place and directly connected to Admiralty MTR station, our luxury hotel in central Hong Kong offers easy access to the area’s shopping, dining and business districts, as well as Victoria Peak and Lan Kwai Fong. Warmly designed, sophisticated hotel rooms feature complimentary Wi-Fi, deluxe bedding, modern...See More amenities and views over Victoria Harbour, the scenic mountains or the glittering downtown skyline. Dining venues range from a Michelin-starred Cantonese restaurant and an all-day restaurant with live kitchens to an alfresco seafood bar and a stylish lounge. Plan your next meeting or event at our luxury hotel in Hong Kong city center, with distinctive venues including a pillarless ballroom, outdoor spaces and modern conference rooms. If you are interested in exploring, our prime location makes it easy to discover the city. Your 5-star escape awaits at JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong.See Less

### Man Ho Chinese Restaurant

Our Michelin-starred restaurant in Hong Kong city center serves authentic Cantonese fare. Enjoy homemade dim sum and exquisite Chinese specialty at this popular restaurant. Private dining rooms cater to different kinds of events or celebrations.

### Fish Bar

Our alfresco downtown Hong Kong seafood restaurant features sustainable seafood delights and dishes made with fresh herbs from JW Garden amidst lush greenery. The adjacent social dining venues, PRESIDEN and HarbourDen, boast stunning harbour views.

Explore

### JW Café

JW Café is a popular Hong Kong buffet restaurant downtown, serving an extensive buffet of international delicacies crafted with premium ingredients for breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea and dinner.

Explore

### Flint

Flint serves showcases delicious grills and modern classics to delight business and leisure diners in Hong Kong. This restaurant in the downtown area, presents delicacies with exceptional finesse, the a testament to Chef's creativity and skills.

### The Lounge

Boasting floor-to-ceiling windows with panoramic city views, The Lounge offers a stylish and cosy ambience to socialise and enjoy an elegant afternoon tea set as well as bountiful buffets over lunch, afternoon tea, dinner and Sunday free-flow brunch.

### Bar Q88

Our intimate Hong Kong bar offers a cosy respite from the hustle and bustle. Unwind with delicious bar bites, live music and an impressive beverage selection, from premium spirits and liqueurs, champagnes and whisky to craft beer and cocktails.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong, with rates of $100.00 per hour, $900.00 per day, and $6300.00 per week.

Off-site parking is also available near JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong. The daily valet fee is $100.00 per hour.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong is Hong Kong International Airport (HKG). HKG is located approximately 36.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Hong Kong has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
