## Japan’s luxury mountain retreat

Set in a spectacular natural landscape with stunning views across the Annupuri ranges and Mount Yotei, Park Hyatt Niseko Hanazono embodies personal luxury for global connoisseurs. A true year-round mountain resort with spaces and guestrooms designed for enriching experiences in an environment of carefully curated art, cuisine and contemporary design.

## Discover Park Hyatt Niseko Hanazono

Wellness at The Park

Plunge into the 25-meter pool, relax in the onsen baths or keep fit at the hotel’s fitness center, all under the care of our dedicated team. Our award-winning spa features a wide range of rejuvenating body and facial treatments, providing four single and two couples treatment rooms with private onsen.

Skiing & Snowboarding

Plunge yourself in the world’s finest powder with Park Hyatt Niseko’s ski-in and ski-out facilities. The wide-open runs and off-piste slopes attract skiers and snowboarders from all over the world. Ride over the ski slopes from Mount Annupuri to Hanazono.

Summer Activities & Golf

At Niseko Hanazono Resort, immerse yourself with a variety of outdoor summer activities, including the world’s largest mega zipline, Hanazono Zipflight, plus rafting and tree trekking. Adjacent to the resort, Hanazono Golf offers a refreshing 18-hole experience amid stunning nature with views of Mount Yotei.

Mountain Lights

“Mountain Lights” is an immersive large-scale art installation of light held in the summer, set in the vast nature of Niseko Hanazono Resort. This spectacular outdoor exhibition (1.3 km of light-based artwork) is created by world renowned British artist Bruce Munro and available from July 1, 2026 throughout October 12, 2026.​

- JOURNEY THROUGH JAPAN

Park Hyatt Tokyo, Park Hyatt Kyoto, and Park Hyatt Niseko Hanazono present **Three Destinations, One Journey with Park Hyatt** — a collection of three distinct experiences designed to showcase Japan through different perspectives. Each hotel offers its own individually bookable package, allowing guests to experience one, two, or all three destinations at their leisure.

While each experience is thoughtfully curated as a recommended three-night stay to fully immerse guests in the destination, shorter or longer stays may also be arranged based on individual preferences — whether for a single night, an extended seven-night retreat, or a fully customized journey across multiple destinations. Designed to stand beautifully on its own while also complementing the others, each experience may also be combined to create a seamless journey across Japan.

Spanning three iconic regions, the experience presents a layered look at the country through distinct lenses. **Tokyo**, the nation’s capital, moves to a contemporary rhythm shaped by fashion, design, and global influence. **Kyoto**, Japan’s ancient capital, offers a quieter counterpoint, where centuries-old traditions live on through temples, shrines, and unhurried streets. In **Hokkaido**, expansive natural landscapes and the changing seasons create a tranquil, nature-driven retreat.

Included in Each Recommended Three-Night Package

  - Accommodation at the selected Park Hyatt destination

  - One signature destination experience

  - Curated destination-inspired welcome beverage and gift

  - Private transfers between the nearest airport or train station and the hotel, upon arrival and departure

  - Daily breakfast for two

Please note that accommodations must be reserved directly with each individual hotel. Terms and conditions apply. Learn more here.
