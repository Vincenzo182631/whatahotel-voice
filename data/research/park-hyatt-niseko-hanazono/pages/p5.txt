### Beautiful hotel, spacious rooms, so many things to do onsite and around the area

Beautiful hotel, mountain view twin suite was very spacious, thoughtfully outfitted, incredibly comfortable beds. Loved the layout and views. Amenities in the room and QR code method for ordering any needed additional amenities worked seamlessly. 25- meter pool and adjacent jacuzzis were outstanding. Gym is modern and well equipped. Onsite activities through the Hanazano resort operator was great and also very convenient (ziplining, tree trekking course, Mount Yotei hanging bridge platforms, cute donut boats on the property's pond, gondola onsite, mountain biking, etc). Restaurants all excellent: breakfast daily was devine with many stations; Olivio served delicious Italian (including excellent pizza and pasta); Robata was great for grilled food (the chicken meatmall with poached egg was spectacular, along with literally every single dish we tried + the sake was outstanding); the Lounge had a great burger (we were at the very end of a 2-week trip to Japan and were happy to have a break from all seafood, all the time!-- though the seafood options on all the menus were also delicious, too). Spa is beautiful and right next to the onsen. Treatment was excellent amidst a very lovely and well appointed treatment room. 5-star staff service throughout our 4-night stay. We will be back and highly recommend this property without reservation! Going in the summer the third week of August was a relative bargain compared to peak ski season.

### Nearly perfect stay

This is a 4.75/5 point review\*

Our stay at Park Hyatt Niseko Hanazono was an incredible introduction to Hokkaido hospitality.

Our suite struck a beautiful balance of comfort and refined Japanese aesthetics, making it easy to settle in after several days of traveling around Hokkaido.

Director of Rooms J. Hwang greeted us personally at check-in, setting a warm tone for the entire stay, and followed up on our second day with a beautifully packaged box of macarons and a handwritten note congratulating us on our anniversary — a small gesture that made a big impression.

General Manager P. Wright went even further, leaving us a handwritten welcome note along with a welcome amenity of food and drink that included two tickets to the resort's gondola. We used them for the nighttime ride, which wound past a beautiful illuminated light art installation scattered across the grounds — one of the true highlights of the trip.

Dinner at the on-site restaurant hit a minor rough patch when our food took longer than expected to arrive. Assistant Manager F. Mitarai and our server handled it with real accountability, offering complimentary food and drink items without us having to ask. Food and Beverage Manager G. Pandey even sought us out the following morning at breakfast to personally explain what happened — a large group had been dining concurrently the night before, creating unexpected challenge in the kitchen. That level of follow-through said a lot about the culture at this property.

Breakfast itself was excellent every morning — fresh, beautifully prepared, and a great way to start the day. Many local Hokkaido ingredients were prominently featured in various forms - as part of a dish's recipe or standalone such as melon and milk.

As Hyatt Globalists, we've come to expect a high standard of service from Park Hyatt properties, and this stay lived up to it. Between the personal touches from leadership, the stunning mountain setting, and a team that treats a hiccup as a chance to make things right, Park Hyatt Niseko Hanazono delivered a truly memorable stay.

\*Rated 4.75 stars for the overall experience — shown here as 4 due to TripAdvisor's whole-point system, with the 1/4 point reflecting the one service delay at dinner, which was ultimately handled exceptionally well.

1\. In-hotel restaurants needs a reservation (for us , we tried to book a month in advance, still only get one late night slot) , you may seriously consider to have a rental car. The restaurant and market in the nearby town are good
