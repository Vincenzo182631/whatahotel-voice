Please be aware that any cancellations must be received 72 hours prior to the booking time. Cancellations received within 72 hours will incur the full (100%) fee. Please note, transfers may be delayed or cancelled due to (but not limited to) road/weather conditions, and without prior notice, for the safety of both our guests and operators. Park Hyatt Niseko Hanazono assumes no liability or responsibility for any onward travel arrangements, reservations, or other unforeseen consequences of delays or cancellations of service. Please be aware that any amendments to the booking may only be made 72 hours prior to the booking time. Amendments received within 72 hours prior to the booking time will be treated as a cancellation, which will incur the full (100%) fee, and will be rebooked at full price (subject to availability). ​
