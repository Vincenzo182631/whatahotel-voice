Why book?
Situated along the Caribbean Sea, the Fairmont Mayakoba, with its two kids clubs and five pools, is the perfect family-friendly stay in Mexico’s Riviera Maya.
Set the scene
Families will love spending time outdoors at one of Fairmont Mayakoba’s five swimming pools, including the three along the oceanfront with alluring views of the Caribbean Sea. The resort is especially accommodating for kids—they eat free when selecting a meal from the children’s menu and have access to free programming at the Balam and Coatí Kids Clubs, which offer plenty of cultural and wellness activities. Surrounded by emerald jungle and winding lagoons, a stay here is also about exploring Mayakoba, a 620-acre ecological enclave nestled within a mangrove jungle, featuring 20 restaurants and bars, as well as El Camaleón, the PGA Tour Championship Golf Course.
The backstory
Fairmont Mayakoba was the first resort to be built in the Mayakoba community, which began construction in 2003.
The rooms
The 401 contemporary accommodations are split between three categories: rooms, casitas, and suites. Depending on the room category, the views will be of the mangroves, the canal, or the Caribbean Sea. Rooms are bright and airy to reflect modern Mexican design and have deep marble soaking tubs. Book a private suite and you’ll have a pristine white beach at your doorstep.
Food and drink
The resort is home to several dining venues, including two breakfast spots, several lunch outposts, and a few evening dining options, including the Greek restaurant Gaia, and Ki, a beach bar and Korean grill offering dumplings, pork tenderloin, short ribs, and Wagyu beef. There are also three bars, including the lounge-style, adults-only Cielo Rooftop Bar with ocean views and the Las Olas Pool Bar, where drinks are served in the family-friendly main pool area.
The spa
The 37,000-square-foot Willow Stream Spa offers 21 treatment rooms, which include three couple rooms, a Vichy shower room, a quartz sand bed room, and 16 individual treatment cabins. In the garden, guests will find a grounding path lined with Melipona beehives, a cold plunge pool, and a temazcal sweat lodge. Most treatments draw inspiration from Mayan cosmology and are based on the lunar calendar.
The neighborhood/area
Designed around a network of crystal clear waterways, the property is situated deep within 240 acres of dense tropical forest. The resort is home to five pools—including an adults-only infinity pool—and El Camaleón, the 18-hole course designed by Greg Norman, has two water features. Set within the Mayakoba resort complex, guests also have access to the Dive & Water Sports Center, offering a wide range of water activities, including scuba diving, jet ski rentals, and private yacht charters, as well as the nearby El Pueblito square with its many shops, restaurants, and entertainment options.
The service
The Fairmont Mayakoba offers concierge services and is also pet-friendly.
For families
The resort is especially accommodating for kids—they eat free when selecting a meal from the children’s menu and have access to free programming at the Balam and Coatí Kids Clubs. Each week, guests can expect cultural and wellness activities to spark their children’s creativity, including how to make traditional Mexican arts and crafts such as piñatas.
Eco-effort
The resort is situated within a 620-acre private development that focuses on the protection of natural mangroves and is home to hundreds of indigenous flora and fauna. A dedication to a green philosophy has earned Fairmont Mayakoba the Preferred by Nature verification among other eco-accolades.
Accessibility
The hotel offers ADA-compliant rooms and suites that are thoughtfully designed to ensure guests’ comfort and safety during their stay, and many of the resort’s communal areas are fully accessible.
Anything left to mention?
The property offers over 46,000 square feet of indoor and outdoor meeting space.
