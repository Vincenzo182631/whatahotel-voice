Life moves irresistibly slow at Villa Sant’Andrea.

Originally built in 1919, it was an escape for an aristocratic family who sought to savour the Sicilian coast and the lush subtropical gardens that surrounded the estate. Over a century later, it still retains all the charms of a private residence.

A stay here is an invitation into the relaxed Sicilian spirit. Enjoy days enriched by the sound of the waves, or take the complimentary shuttle to Grand Hotel Timeo and reach the town centre, a treasure trove of cultural gems.
