## Where _Adventure_ Meets Elegance in the Heart of the Wild

Set in the northwestern corner of the Maasai Mara, at the foot of the Aitong Hills in the Olchoro Oirowua Conservancy, Fairmont Mara Safari Club is your gateway to the world's most iconic safari destination. Here, adventure and luxury converge to create an unforgettable experience in Kenya.

### EMBARK ON AN UNFORGETTABLE MASAI MARA ADVENTURE

Book your Masai Mara adventure with Fairmont and choose from 38 exquisitely tented rooms, each beautifully adorned in an elegant fusion of traditional Maasai and heritage safari styles. Indulge in plush amenities and savor in-room dining with breathtaking views of the Mara River, making your stay truly unforgettable.
