Rooms
50
Guests spot gazelles and lions along the banks of the Mara River at this 50-tent resort, which was renovated in 2009. Accommodations have Masai colors on the bedspreads and beaded curtains from South Africa in the bathroom area. Meals at the boma, designed to resemble a Masai manyatta, or village, are served both indoors and out.
