Powdery white sands. Lush forests. Encounters with rare wildlife such as the red colobus monkey and servaline genet. Zanzibar is one of the world’s most spectacular destinations, and it will soon be home to the signature hospitality of Anantara.

Located on the northern coast of the stunning Unguja Island, Anantara Zanzibar Resort & Residences will be an elegant beachside retreat with spacious suites and pool villas welcoming guests to tropical comfort.

Guests enjoy exclusive access to private pools and a lagoon, with water features strategically placed throughout the grounds. A variety of dining experiences will deliver culinary excellence, highlighted by a speciality beachside club and bar, tiki-themed bar, al fresco dining by the pool and a friendly café.

The luxurious Anantara Spa will offer indulgent and rejuvenating spa journeys, while outdoor enthusiasts can explore an array of beach and water sports as well as the resort’s fitness facilities.
