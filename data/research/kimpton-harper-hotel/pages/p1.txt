Housed in an architectural masterpiece from the 1920s, the Kimpton Harper Hotel blends Fort Worth history with modern style. In the 24th-floor lobby, sweeping views and a lively bar scene beckon in equal measure. Guest rooms are luxurious retreats with local art, and il Modo, our bustling restaurant, puts a contemporary spin on the Italian culinary traditions. Step outside and you're in the heart of Downtown Fort Worth, where the city’s commercial and cultural scenes collide.

### il Modo Restaurant

Classic Italian fare comes alive at Il Modo—Italian for “The Way”—our bustling ground-floor restaurant. The kitchen’s modern sensibility lends a vibrant edge to family night out and high-powered client dinners alike.

Learn more

### Specialty Suites

Whatever your mood, there’s a well- appointed room for it at The Harper. Stretch out in our spacious suites, which range from sumptuous studios to sprawling presidential- style digs. Whether you’re hankering for a well-stocked wet bar or a spa-like soaking tub, we’ve got just the amenities just for you.

Learn more

### Bar - Refinery 714

Saddle up for well-crafted cocktails with imaginative flair at Refinery 714, our penthouse lounge. Named for our address—714 Main Street—the bar’s luxe seating affords sweeping views of the Texas plains and downtown Fort Worth.

Learn more

### Weddings

Our event specialists will help you realize your dreams, from lavish affairs in our magnificent 3,000-square-foot ballroom to relaxed luncheons in one of our many intimate spaces. Customized to your personal desires and wishes, our facilities are ideal for couples with high expectations and cultured tastes.

Learn more

### Meetings + Events

Whether you’re hosting a stately board meeting or a blow-out bash, there’s a just-right event space to suit your unique needs. With sophisticated furnishings and gorgeous natural light, our rooms are sure to elevate any affair.

## FAQs for Harper Hotel

Does Kimpton Harper Hotel have a pool?

No, Kimpton Harper Hotel does not have a pool.

How much is parking at Kimpton Harper Hotel?

At Kimpton Harper Hotel, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 45 USD and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 55 USD .

Our parking fees are as follows:

Overnight Valet Parking: $55
Daily Visitor Parking: $24
Overnight Self Parking: $45
Restaurant: $10
Valet tickets must be stamped by iL Modo or Refinery 714
\*These fees include in and out privileges during your stay.

Parking fee: 45 USD per day

In and out privileges

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Harper Hotel?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Harper Hotel.

Pet policy description. All Kimpton Hotels welcome pet guests! Pets always stay free and no pet deposit is required. Any number of pets are welcome regardless of size, weight, kind, or breed. Our Kimpton property has bed bed, bowls and leashes available upon request.

Pet fee per night: 0 USD

Pet damage deposit: 0 USD

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Harper Hotel for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Harper Hotel have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Harper Hotel has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Harper Hotel?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Harper Hotel.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Harper Hotel?

Check-in at Kimpton Harper Hotel is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Harper Hotel offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Harper Hotel offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Harper Hotel from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Harper Hotel is conveniently located near Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (DFW), approximately 24 miles away (38.62 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Harper Hotel?

Kimpton Harper Hotel is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Fort Worth Convention Center

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Harper Hotel puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
