## Revel in Luxury & Year-Round Adventure From Our Beaver Creek Resort

With sweeping views from the base of Beaver Creek Mountain, our resort hotel and spa offers guests a luxurious year-round escape near Vail Valley, Colorado. As the only ski-in/ski-out hotel located at Beaver Creek Village ski base, our resort boasts the best access to Village activities, ski school, shopping and more. In summer months, discover exciting events in Avon or explore the breathtaking mountain area with golf, fly fishing, river rafting and 4x4 rides—or indulge with renewal any time of year at our resort’s full-service spa.

### Pets Are Welcome

Your four-legged dog family member is welcome at our luxury ski-in/ski-out resort near Vail. We welcome two dogs weighing up to 70 pounds each per room or suite. A one-time nonrefundable $200 fee applies. For the safety and comfort of others, guests are asked to keep their dogs leashed while on the resort. We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

#### Pet Fees
