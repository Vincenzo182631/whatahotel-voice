# Restaurants & Bars

Indulge and delight during your Park Hyatt Beaver Creek getaway with upscale dining options at our resort restaurants. 8100 Mountainside bar and grill offers delectable breakfast and dinner options to energize your day and a spacious heated patio boasting luxurious views of the ski base and valley. Brass Bear Bar is the hub of activity year-round, with floor-to-ceiling views of the soaring Beaver Creek mountains and largest outdoor fire pit in Vail Valley. The resort’s Fall Line Epicurean Market offers "grab & go" bites and coffee plus a selection of craft beer, upscale wines and snacks to stay fueled for mountain adventure. Discover seasonal and indulgent culinary events including Chef's Table dining, Raclette experience, beverages tastings and more. Explore Now.

## Brass Bear Bar

Unwind at Brass Bear Bar and experience the best spot in all of Beaver Creek for catching an iconic sunrise or sunset views over the Vail Valley mountains. Located at the heart of our luxury Beaver Creek resort, the bar features a grand fireplace, elegant seating, and a delicious selection of cocktails and bar bites.

## Fall Line Epicurean Market

Fall Line Market is a 24-hour Epicurean Market stocked with salads, sandwiches, pastries, hot coffee and espresso. You may also bring home a token from the mountains created by local artisans. Located in the lobby near hotel registration.
