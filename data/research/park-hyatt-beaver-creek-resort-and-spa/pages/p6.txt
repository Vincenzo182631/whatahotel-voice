Pet Policy

Your canine family member is welcome at our pet-friendly Beaver Creek hotel resort. At Park Hyatt Beaver Creek, we welcome up to two dogs weighing up to 70 pounds each, per guest room.

A one-time, nonrefundable $200 fee applies. We ask dogs to stay leashed for their safety when walking around the resort.

We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Smoking Policy

Park Hyatt Beaver Creek is a 100% smoke-free property. A cleaning fee of $500 per day will apply to any documented violations.

Rollaway Bed Policy

A rollaway bed may be reserved in advance of arrival in King Rooms only, based on availability, for a nightly fee of $55 per rollaway + tax.

Check-In/Checkout Policy

Check-in is 4 p.m. Checkout is 11 a.m.

Method of Payment

All reservations require a valid credit card to be present at the time of check-in. It will need to be authorized for the full room and tax, plus an additional $150 per night for any incidentals. If the reservation is prepaid, a credit card is still required and will be authorized for $150 per night for incidentals. Cash payments are not accepted at this time.
