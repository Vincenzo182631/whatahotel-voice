### A lovely place to stay and great location

The Park Hyatt Beaver Creek was a lovely place to stay and enjoy the area. The hotel is well maintained, the rooms were good size and the location was excellent. It was attached to the Village, where we could find many shops and restaurants. There were also hiking trails right at the resort. The spa was nice, but small. The treatments were great! I highly recommend Sharon if you are having a facial. The food was excellent. Our room included free breakfast every morning, and the buffet did not disappoint. Most of all, the staff working at the resort were friendly, helpful and went out of their way to make our experience unforgettable.
The only reason for my 4 star rating is that my bed was a bit uncomfortable in that it had divets on each side where people had slept. I tried to lay in the middle of the bed and could not because I rolled to one side or the other. The rooms could also use a bit more insulation because I could hear the people next door opening and closing the closet and moving around.
Overall, I would recommend this hotel and I would stay there again.

### A Superb experience from all angles

Beaver Creek is a charming little village that offers numerous amenities from great dining to breath taking views to immersions in nature to a variety of the arts and to a pace that entices you to enjoy the overall experience.

The Park Hyatt is a beautiful hotel that perfectly fits this community. It is very well run with the guest experience obviously being its highest priority.

The hotel and rooms are clean, well appointed, well serviced and modern. (And everything works!) The hotel also offers a wide variety of services and amenities. The staff are wonderfully customer centric and very approachable for any need or question or opportunity to help. The views are also spectacular.

The food offerings are creative and delicious. SPECIAL NOTE - we were there during the Cyclosporiasis outbreak. Though Colorado is not one of the states that was seriously impacted, I quickly learned that the kitchen was taking any and all measures to insure that the food that was served was safe. I was impressed!

Finally, as I like to do, I met the hotel GM. Edan is a smart, experienced and caring man who clearly has the hotel and staff operating in a professional, harmonious and customer centric manner. I was impressed.

My wife and I have become annual guests and already look forward to our next visit.
