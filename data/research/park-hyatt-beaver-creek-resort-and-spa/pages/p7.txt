Getting around

Shuttle Service

Park Hyatt Beaver Creek Resort and Spa offers point to point or hourly transportation within Beaver Creek, Bachelor Gulch and Avon with SnowCountry transportation. Guests are invited to confirm local transportation needs during their stay or contact our Resort Concierge at (970)-949-1234 for assistance.

Public Transportation

Buses are operated by two transportation companies:

· Town of Avon buses depart throughout the day from the Elk parking lot in Beaver Creek.

· ECO Transit operates a bus service between Vail, Beaver Creek, Avon and Edwards. Call +1 970 328 3520 for information.
