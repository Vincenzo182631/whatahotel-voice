## Casa Baglioni Milan

Casa Baglioni Milan is situated in the artistic Brera district. Inspired by 1960s Milan, the hotel offers a wine cellar and a restaurant by Michelin-starred Chef Claudio Sadler.

## Baglioni Experiences

\\
**Classic Milan: All the Highlights** \\
\\
Experience Milan’s iconic landmarks on a stylish walking tour—pass by the Duomo, stroll through Galleria Vittorio Emanuele, visit Piazza della Scala, and end at the grand Sforza Castle via the lively Via Dante. A must for lovers of art, history, and culture.
\\
**The Art of Milan: Brera District and Pinacoteca** \\
\\
Casa Baglioni invites you to discover the artistic heart of Milan with an exclusive tour of the Brera District—its own neighborhood. Stroll charming streets, browse art galleries, and explore the Pinacoteca di Brera, home to Italian masterpieces and timeless beauty.
\\
**Lake Como and Its Treasures** \\
\\
Experience a refined, private Lake Como trip from Milan. Explore Villa Carlotta’s grand interiors and gardens, sail to the Bellagio for lakeside dining and shopping, and enjoy panoramic views on this curated journey.
