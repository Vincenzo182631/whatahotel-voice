A View to Remember
The Height of Luxury
The Ritz-Carlton Suite spans 251 square meters, occupying the full length of the building’s top floors. This luxury hotel suite in Melbourne​ city centre provides a sanctuary that combines residential comfort with the elegance of a five-star hotel. Meticulously designed, the suite features bespoke furnishings and curated artwork inspired by Melbourne’s rich culture. The master bedroom is a tranquil haven, complemented by a marble bathroom with a deep soaking tub, rain shower, and dual vanities for a spa-like experience. Additional luxuries include a walk-through wardrobe, in-room sauna, and private pantry, blending comfort with elegance. Exclusive access to The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge offers personalised service and curated culinary delights.

Crafted for Comfort
Defined by Detail
The Yarra Suite offers an unparalleled blend of sophistication and comfort. Spanning an impressive 140 square meters, this suite provides expansive views of the Yarra River and Melbourne's dynamic skyline. Designed with meticulous attention to detail, the Yarra Suite features bespoke furnishings, curated artwork, and a palette inspired by Melbourne's rich cultural tapestry. The master bedroom offers a serene retreat, while the marble-clad bathroom includes a deep soaking tub and separate rain shower, creating a spa-like experience. Guests of the Yarra Suite enjoy exclusive access to The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge, where personalised service and curated culinary offerings elevate the stay. Whether for business or leisure, the Yarra Suite provides a sanctuary that combines residential comfort with the elegance of a five-star hotel.

Become Memories
Blending refined comfort with residential-style space, the Executive Suite spans 96 sqm and offers sweeping skyline views through floor-to-ceiling windows. A king bedroom, separate living area, and dining space create the ideal environment for both relaxation and productivity. Bathed in natural light, this luxury executive suite in Melbourne features bespoke furnishings and a marble-clad bathroom complete with a deep soaking tub, separate rain shower, and dual vanities - a serene, spa-like retreat in the sky. Ideal for both business and leisure, the Executive Suite is a true sanctuary above the city.

Exclusive Access
The Ritz-Carlton Club
The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge offers an exclusive retreat designed to elevate every stay with personalized service and exceptional amenities. Accessible only to Club-level guests and suite residents, the lounge provides a sophisticated setting for relaxation, work, or socialising. Guests enjoy curated culinary offerings throughout the day, including gourmet breakfast and evening hors d’oeuvres paired with premium beverages. The lounge’s serene ambiance is enhanced by elegant décor, comfortable seating, and panoramic views of Melbourne’s skyline. Dedicated concierge services assist with tailored recommendations, reservations, and seamless planning. Whether unwinding after a day of business or exploring the city, the Club Lounge delivers a private, refined experience that embodies the signature hospitality and understated luxury of The Ritz-Carlton.

A Private Retreat
Club Lounge Access
Begin your stay with us in our exclusive Club Lounge, where you’ll be greeted by our Club ambassador and enjoy a dedicated check-in, exclusively for our Club guests. For the rest of your stay, consider the Club Lounge your private sanctuary.
Experience the Club
Unparalleled Service
Dedicated Concierge
A hotel within a hotel, The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, bringing exceptional levels of personalized service. Guests who reserve a Club Room or Suite are hosted by their own dedicated Club concierge. From dining reservations to excursion recommendations, our dedicated Club Concierge team elevates your experience, catering to you personally to ensure your stay is exceptional. As a Melbourne hotel with exclusive club, our team is on hand to meet your individual needs.
Culinary Indulgences
Daily Culinary Presentations
In addition to being a sanctuary, the Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge offers spaces to dine, work and relax. Five inclusive culinary offerings are available throughout the day; breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea, hors d'oeuvres, and cordials, all curated by The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne’s culinary team.
Personalised Luxury
In-Room Amenities
A Club stay ensures that additional amenities are available to you in your luxurious room or suite. Guests who reserve a Club Room or Suite in our Melbourne hotel with an exclusive club will have access to an exciting array of complimentary services, from laundry pressing to elevated turndown and in-room dining experiences.
