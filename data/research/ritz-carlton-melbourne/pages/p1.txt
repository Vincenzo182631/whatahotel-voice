A sky-high luxury retreat
Rising 80 stories above the city, The Ritz-Carlton Melbourne offers panoramic views of the Dandenong Ranges, Victoria Harbour, and Port Phillip Bay. Condé Nast Traveler Readers' Choice Award
Recognized in the prestigious 2024 Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards, The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne was ranked the #1 hotel in Australia and New Zealand and #41 in the world—an acknowledgement of its exceptional luxury and guest experience.
MICHELIN Guide 2025
Awarded One MICHELIN Key, The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne is among a select collection of exceptional hotels recognized by the MICHELIN Guide for its architecture, service, and personality ensuring a truly memorable stay.
Restaurant and Bar
Atria Restaurant is widely recognized as one of Melbourne’s best restaurants. Awarded One Hat in The Age Good Food Guide and recognized by Gourmet Traveler, World of Fine Wine, and Australian Wine List of the Year. Cameo cocktail bar was awarded Australia’s Best Hotel Bar at the Bartender Magazine Bar Awards.
Travel Awards
Widely celebrated in Melbourne, Australia, and around the world, The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne has drawn the attention of Travel + Leisure, the Australian Hospitality Association, Hotel Management, and the Victorian Accommodation Awards. Upon opening, it has won key accolades and recognition that has attracted global acclaim.
Our Commitment to Sustainability
At The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne, we are committed to reducing our environmental impact and supporting the vibrant community that surrounds us. Located in the heart of Melbourne, a city renowned for its green spaces and sustainability initiatives, the hotel embraces responsible practices that reflect its urban environment and local heritage. Our operations are guided by a commitment to resource efficiency, waste reduction, and meaningful community engagement, ensuring sustainability is seamlessly integrated into the guest experience. Enjoy your stay at The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne, with the confidence that your visit supports a more sustainable future for our city and its people.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne. Self parking is available for $85.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $85.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne is Melbourne Airport - Australia (MEL). MEL is located approximately 22.5 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
