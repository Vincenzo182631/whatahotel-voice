The Ritz-Carlton Spa
Awaken the senses and rejuvenate mind, body, and soul at The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Melbourne. Locally sourced botanicals are combined with the timeless traditions of spa therapy to offer uniquely crafted treatments. Located on Level 64 of the hotel, The Ritz-Carlton Spa presents six treatment rooms, including a dedicated couple’s suite. As a complement to the holistic wellness experience, guests at our Melbourne spa can enjoy the tranquil surroundings of an elegantly designed sauna, steam room, and vitality pools providing the perfect setting for relaxation and rejuvenation.
Featured Treatments
Relaxation Massage
Unwind and de-stress with this aromatic bespoke massage at this luxury spa in Melbourne​ city centre. This soothing aromatherapy massage uses light to moderate pressure and a blend of essential oils to melt away tension.
Ultimate Radiance & Renewal Facial
Restore skin’s vitality and reveal a bright, smooth, and radiantly youthful-looking complexion. The must-have treatment for immediate results before a special event in our luxury spa hotel in Melbourne.
Age-Defying Facial
Instantly combat the most visible signs of skin aging - smoothing fine lines and reviving youthful radiance to your complexion.
Ultimate Body Balancer
Restore serenity to body, mind, and skin with this richly indulgent and deeply effective bespoke treatment at our day spa in Melbourne, Australia. Includes back exfoliation and massage, custom facial, and scalp massage.
Couples Spa Experience
Indulge the senses with an aromatic bath filled with rose petals & our signature Rose Otto Treatment Oil. Enjoy inspiring views of Melbourne’s skyline whilst you connect and relax together with a glass of champagne and hand-crafted chocolates. Following the bathing experience is a 90-minute massage to end your relaxation journey.
The Indulgent Rose
Nurture and revitalize both skin and mind with this rose-inspired experience. Warmed Rose Otto oil and advanced massage techniques comfort and soothe the body and mind leaving you in a place of profound relaxation. Includes a foot ritual, rose massage, and facial with rose quartz gua sha.
