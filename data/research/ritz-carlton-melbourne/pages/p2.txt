Melbourne Rooms & Suites with Skyline Views
Suites & Rooms
Perched high above the city, The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne offers a collection of exquisitely appointed luxury hotel rooms and suites that redefine contemporary living in Melbourne. Located between levels 65 and 79, each space is a serene retreat featuring panoramic views, custom-designed furnishings, and thoughtful nods to Melbourne’s creative and cultural heritage. From marble bathrooms with deep soaking tubs to curated local artwork, every detail invites rest, inspiration, and indulgent escape.

Our Signature Suites
For guests seeking an experience beyond the exceptional, our Signature Suites deliver a new level of elegance, privacy, and space with exclusive Club Lounge access. Situated on the highest floors of the hotel, these luxury hotel suites in Melbourne​ combine striking design with breathtaking vistas, creating the perfect setting for both celebration and serenity. The Ritz-Carlton Suite - The pinnacle of sophistication. Spanning 251 square metres, this full-floor residence includes a private sauna, spacious dining and living rooms. The Yarra Suite – A generous 140 square metre suite rich textures providing an elevated atmosphere for extended stays or special occasions. The Executive Suite balances understated elegance with modern comforts at a spacious 96 square metres.
