# Welcome to The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland

## Stylish modern luxury the most authentic Portland hotel.

The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland offers a sophisticated stay in the heart of downtown Portland, Oregon. Set within the historic Meier & Frank Building near Pioneer Courthouse Square, the...See More hotel blends historic character with contemporary luxury, art-filled interiors and stylish guest rooms and suites. Guests are steps from tax-free shopping at Pioneer Place, downtown dining and cultural attractions, with convenient access to MAX Light Rail for exploring the city. Discover nearby destinations including the Pearl District, Washington Park, Portland Japanese Garden, Oregon Zoo and Moda Center. Back at the hotel, take in downtown Portland views and experience acclaimed dining at Urban Farmer Steakhouse and Departure Restaurant + Lounge. With distinctive design, curated artwork, upscale amenities and a prime downtown location, The Nines offers an elevated home base for experiencing Portland.See Less

### Plan and Prepare for your Stay

Pet-Friendly

The Nines is a pet-friendly hotel and welcomes you and your four-legged friends to our luxury Portland hotel. Our Pet Guidelines will help ensure you, and other guests, have an enjoyable stay at our hotel.

1\. Dogs must weigh 40 pounds or less, be well-trained, appropriately restrained and in compliance with all local laws.

2\. Pets must be supervised at all times. Please contact Guest Services to arrange a convenient time for servicing your room. No attempt will be made to enter your room if your pet is unattended.

3\. Pets are not permitted in the fitness center or restaurants, with the exception of service and guide animals. However, please notify our associates prior to visiting these spaces so that we can best accommodate your needs.

4\. Please clean up after your pet on hotel property and in surrounding neighborhoods.

5\. Any excessive noise, such as barking, must be curtailed in both your guest room and all public areas to ensure fellow guests are not inconvenienced.

6\. Our concierge is happy to provide a list of pet sitters, walkers and daycare facilities.

7\. We understand that accidents are unavoidable. If an accident occurs, call Guest Services immediately. Please note that if damage is irreversible, you will be responsible for all cleaning or repairs.

A Rich History of Fashion

As the premier department store in Portland, Meier & Frank was once the place to see and be seen in the city.

In 2009, The Nines opened in the top nine floors of this historic building as one of Portland’s premier luxury hotels. The original exterior of the building was retained, while a seven-story light-filled atrium was created to showcase our beautiful city views. The architects had a nostalgic, modern approach to the renovation - infusing the space with elements of art and fashion, as well as eclectic nods to the Meier & Frank’s retail past. In line with our sustainable values, the building is also LEED Silver Certified.

The name “the Nines” pays homage to our roots as a beacon of “dressed to the nines” fashion. Like the original Meier & Frank, we're dedicated to providing impeccable service and an elevated customer experience.

Art at The Nines

**A Lobby Unlike Any Other**

The shared spaces at The Nines include dynamic and diverse installations from local artists. From a 35-foot LED chandelier, to abstract images of Portland, our lobby, restaurants and library are decorated with works from local artists including Melody Owen, Matt McCormick and Gus Van Sant.

**One-of-a-Kind Guest Rooms**

Each guest room at The Nines features a one-of-a-kind monotype silkscreen, created by a student at the Pacific Northwest College of Art. The monotypes utilize materials like crumpled paper to create textured shapes, while the silkscreen prints build imagery through a series of circles and rectangles, creating optical art.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland?

The check-in time at The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 60.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $85.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland?

Off-site valet parking is available at The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland. The daily valet fee is $67.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. What is the closest airport near The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland?

The closest airport near The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland is Portland International Airport (PDX). PDX is located approximately 9.6 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Nines, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Portland have electric vehicle charging stations?
