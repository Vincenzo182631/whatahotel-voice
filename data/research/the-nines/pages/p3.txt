### Largest Saké List in Portland

Featuring the largest saké list in Portland, Departure's saké and shochu menu represents many hand-crafted, artisan producers. Additionally, Departure features an exotic cocktail menu and a global wine list.

### Cosmopolitan Surroundings

Designed by Jeff Kovel of Skylab Architecture, one of Portland’s leading architectural firms, Departure features two rooftop patios overlooking downtown from the top floor of the historic Meier & Frank building.

### Rooftop Garden

Shared by Urban Farmer and Departure, our rooftop garden features a mushroom incubator, a honey bee farm and fresh herbs.

### Country Chic Décor

Designer David Ashen developed Urban Farmer’s country chic ambiance using organic, reclaimed and modern materials. This unique restaurant is situated within The Nines' living room.

### Eco-Friendly Menus

Urban Farmer’s menu honors national and local culinary traditions, sourcing ingredients from some of the Northwest’s finest eco-friendly producers.

### Local Abstract Art

Satellite, an abstract video installation by local artist Matt McCormick, is on display inside Urban Farmer. The video features various images of Portland’s Fremont Bridge at dusk.

### Extensive Wine Menu

The wine list at Urban Farmer features nearly 200 varietals from Oregon, Washington and California in addition to three-dozen world-class wines from Oregon’s Willamette Valley.
