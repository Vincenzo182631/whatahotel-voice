## THE _iconic_ HOTEL IN THE HEART  OF HISTORICAL PODIL

Fairmont Grand Hotel Kyiv is the perfect place for elegant travelers and the perfect luxury venue for the most iconic events. Discover the heart of Kyiv's historical area Podil, enjoy a walk along the wonderful embankment of Dnipro river, explore architectural landmarks and taste local cuisine at the numerous cafes and restaurants around.
