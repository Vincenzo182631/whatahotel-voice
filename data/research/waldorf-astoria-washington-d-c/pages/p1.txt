# A stunning landmark in Washington D.C.

Elegantly restored to its historic grandeur, the iconic Old Post Office has been reimagined as Waldorf Astoria Washington DC. Our hotel sits on Pennsylvania Avenue between The White House and the U.S. Capitol near National Mall museums, the Washington Monument, and Lincoln Memorial. Welcome to the home of D.C.’s most consequential events, groundbreaking meetings, and unforgettable experiences.

## Rooms and Suites

Showcasing a modern perspective on classic D.C. grandeur, our expansive guest rooms and suites are among the largest in the city. Enjoy spectacular views of Pennsylvania Avenue, exquisite furnishings, and luxury amenities.

Find a Room

## Spa

Rejuvenate the mind, body, and soul at the Waldorf Astoria Spa, featuring six luxurious treatment rooms, including a couple’s suite and a soothing Himalayan Salt Therapy Room.

Visit the Spa

# Dining

Dining

Sip cocktails under the glittering crystal sconces and chandeliers in Peacock Alley (open 6:30 am – 11 pm), step into a world of culinary imagination rooted in Spanish heritage and layered with luxurious detail at The Bazaar by José Andrés or experience the delights of Chef Daisuke Nakazawa in the Michelin-starred Sushi Nakazawa. In-room dining is also available 24 hours for your comfort and convenience.
