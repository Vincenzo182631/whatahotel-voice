## Accommodations

Le Méridien Barcelona welcomes guests with artful design infused with modern comforts. Each of our 231 luxury guest rooms and 40 suites in Barcelona enjoys views of the scenic city streets—including Las...See More Ramblas Boulevard, the charming hotel courtyard, or the panoramic skyline. Business travelers will appreciate the spacious working areas, equipped with complimentary High-Speed Internet Access, while a large LCD TV with satellite and in-room movies offer plenty of entertainment options. Spacious bathrooms with a rainforest shower and ample toiletries invite you to pamper yourself. At the end of the day, look forward to a blissful night’s sleep in the multiple soft layers of our signature LM BED. Experience a fresh approach at this Le Méridien with completely smoke-free hotel rooms and family suites.See Less

## More Space

Enjoy the relaxation in our spacious and luxurious suites. Choose from our Family Suites, Corner Suites, and our exclusive Panoramic Suite, each featuring two separate areas to provide maximum comfort and privacy. The Panoramic Suite also includes a separate office area, complementing the individual living space with an elegant style that blends business and leisure. Our rooms and suites can connect with each other, accommodating up to 8 guests, thus offering a spacious and luxurious environment for everyone. With up to 31 connection options between rooms, we guarantee the flexibility and comfort necessary for any type of stay.

Learn More

## Terrace

Immerse yourself in the charm of a unique getaway on the terraces of our suites. Our Grand Terrace Suites feature a spacious outdoor terrace of up to 51 m², equipped with loungers, sofas, and an outdoor shower, perfect for sunbathing and relaxation. Our exclusive Panoramic Suite, on the other hand, offers two terraces, one of which includes an outdoor dining area surrounded by breathtaking views. Enjoy the stunning cityscape or experience a romantic evening under the stars; our terraces promise to offer you truly unforgettable experiences.

## Breathtaking Urban Views

Discover unparalleled 360º panoramic views from the top floor of Le Méridien Barcelona in our Panoramic Suite. This sophisticated oasis offers a luxurious escape in the cosmopolitan heart of the city, featuring floor-to ceiling windows that frame the stunning skyline from the living area. Meanwhile, our Corner Suites exude special charm with a cozy living space overlooking the iconic Las Ramblas, perfect for guests who wish to soak in the vibrant cityscape and relax amidst Barcelona's lively atmosphere.
