### A Rich History

The building was erected in the plot where Almacenes El Siglo was before the devastating fire in 1932. The Grand Hotel Manila opened in 1957, which became a center for arts and culture visited by locals and world celebrities alike due to its proximity to the Liceu theater. The building was erected as Hotel Manila by Tobacco company across the street from their headquarters building.

## History Behind the Building

The hotel’s long history, paired with Le Méridien’s brand heritage, the contemporary art found throughout the building and the location in one of the city’s most creative neighbourhoods, form a one-of-a-kind recipe that sparks imagination, invites for social and business get togethers and stimulates the senses.

Awards and Recognitions

BREEAM Certificate - 2026/2029

Le Méridien Barcelona has been recognized by BREEAM® Spain, the certificate of sustainable construction, by the application of different measures in the day to day in favor of energy efficiency and environmental protection. It's the first hotel in Spain to achieve this recognition.

Biosphere Certification

Le Méridien Barcelona has been awarded the prestigious Biosphere Certification, recognizing our commitment to sustainability and responsible practices. This achievement highlights our dedication to protecting the environment while delivering unique and memorable experiences.

##### Tesla Destination Charging

Plug in and relax at Le Meridien Barcelona

Tesla’s Destination Charging programme provides convenient charging at premium destinations around the world exclusively for Tesla owners. Their charging technology can add up to 68 miles / 100 km of range per hour of charge for visiting Tesla owners. We are proud to partner with Tesla and contribute to the pursuit of sustainable transportation.

##### Green Council

Created in 2008,  its aim is: raising awareness of the environmental initiatives and coordinating and measuring its implementation.

##### Earth Hour

Each year, the hotel invites to participate in the "Earth Hour" (Earth Hour) to all guests staying at the hotel and all employees, by reducing electricity consumption in rooms, public areas and restaurant, during one hour.

##### Environment Week

Participation with sustainable menus in the restaurant and employee's dining-room. Conference for employees about recycling, climate change, etc.

##### Electrical Chargers

In Parking, for electric vehicles. 16 amps chargers, 100% compliance with legal regulations. Free service for Le Méridien Barcelona guests.

##### LED Bulbs

Regular bulbs have been changed by 4w LED's in the corridors from the 1st to the 5th floor, elevator halls and main staircase. A total of 660 bulbs have been changed, which means an annual saving of 112.320kwh.

##### Recycling

Recycling containers in all offices from the 1st to the 5th floor.

##### Sustainable Meetings

Actions to reduce the environmental impact of events, including repurposing surplus bakery and pastry products into new culinary creations such as puddings, truffles, and mini cakes, as well as providing visible recycling stations to encourage responsible waste management.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Barcelona?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Barcelona is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Barcelona allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Barcelona. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €45.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Barcelona?

On-site self parking is available at Le Méridien Barcelona. Self parking is available at Le Méridien Barcelona, with rates of €3.63 per hour, €47.19 per day, and €330.33 per week.

Off-site parking is also available near Le Méridien Barcelona. The daily valet fee is €47.19 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Barcelona have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Barcelona has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Barcelona booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Barcelona?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Barcelona is Barcelona-El Prat Airport (BCN). BCN is located approximately 12.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Barcelona have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Le Méridien Barcelona has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
