Le Méridien Barcelona
This centrally located gem really stands out for its bright, vivid colors—no gray or beige in sight!

Reviewed by
Isabelle Kliger
How did it strike you on arrival?
While most hotels of this caliber play it safe with grays, whites, and beiges, the lobby of Le Méridien is awash with color. Finally, a five-star hotel that isn’t afraid to stand out.
What’s the crowd like?
Chic and stylish but not too glitzy.
Tell us about your room.
Timeless and elegant, rooms are inspired by modernist design and architecture and reflect the style and feel of the city outside. Contemporary features include an Illy coffee machine, audiovisual panel with HDMI connection, USB ports universal power outlets, and Bose Bluetooth speakers.
What are the beds like?
Le Méridien signature king-size or double beds. A choice of feather or hypoallergenic pillows.
How's the bathroom?
Le Méridien’s bathrooms feature a stimulating shower system for anyone who enjoys a long, comforting rinse after a busy day exploring the city. Take advantage of the vitamin A5 body moisturizer, sage body wash, peppermint shampoo, and cilantro conditioner, all from Malin + Goetz.
How's the connectivity?
Guests of the hotel get up to 3MB of free Wi-Fi.
Anything stand out about other services and features?
Custom-made artwork and asymmetric designs run throughout the rooms and common areas, reinforcing the artsy vibe that throughout. Le Méridien also offers its “Unlock Art” program, with complimentary access to galleries and institutions for the culturally curious traveler. Le Méridien’s cultural partner in Barcelona is Palau Güell, a modernist masterpiece by everyone’s favorite local creative Antoni Gaudí.
What was most memorable about your stay?
Le Méridien’s keen interest in Barcelona's art and design is a huge part of any stay here. Ask the staff for more information about discovering the Art Nouveau pieces situated around the hotel.
Bottom line: worth it, and why?
The Deluxe Premium is a decent-size room in a centrally located five-star hotel. It's an undeniably good value for money.
