## Dining

Explore a stimulating world of flavors, textures, and settings at Le Méridien Barcelona’s acclaimed Mediterranean restaurant and cocktail bar. With a cosmopolitan yet relaxed atmosphere, CentOnze restaurant...See More invites you to a culinary exploration of fresh local flavors like authentic tapas in Barcelona and paella, reinterpreted with flair, and served in a bright, street-level setting with views of Las Ramblas. At the Signature Cocktail Bar, take in the essence of the 1950s through early ’70s playlist infused with New Wave music and pop-culture inspired graffiti. Cocktail hour is a social affair when our master mixologists serve up their effervescent creations as part of Le Méridien Sparkling. Located within the chic Le Méridien Hub, our casual Longitude Bar 02º 10' is the ideal spot to start the day with a stimulating breakfast in Barcelona, or simply to pick up a coffee and pastry as you head out the door to explore.See Less
