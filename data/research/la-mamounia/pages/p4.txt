# La Mamounia's four restaurants in Marrakech

## _Reimagined spaces_   for gourmet experiences

The cuisines of Asia, the Mediterranean, Italy and Morocco are renowned for being some of the finest in the world.

And they can all be found at La Mamounia, in the prestigious Hivernage district of Marrakech, thanks to the world-class chefs who orchestrate the culinary experience in our kitchens. Think exquisite confectionery creations by Pierre Hermé, Moroccan flavours infused with spices and reimagined by Chef Rachid Agouray, vibrant Italian specialities by Simone Zanoni, or a grand culinary journey through Asia curated by Jean-Georges Vongerichten… Join us on a true gastronomic adventure.

The shaded terrace of the Pavillon de la Piscine invites you to enjoy classic dishes and light specialities from the sumptuous buffets that accompany your poolside lunches.

This is also the place to head for Sunday Brunch, a not-to-be-missed event in Marrakech!

## There is something for everyone,   together with unforgettable moments of exchange.

Each restaurant has a warm and atmospheric setting, transforming meals into authentic culinary experiences.

While La Mamounia has always been a creator of enchanting settings, its 2020 metamorphosis by Patrick Jouin and Sanjit Manku has reimagined spaces that invite you to escape the humdrum of life in dream-like settings. At the Italien par Simone Zanoni, you are plunged into the Roman dolce vita, that of a luxurious trattoria with a winter garden feel.

## Menus of a thousand and one flavours

All our restaurants have two things in common: fresh ingredients and creative dishes.

Each menu is like a musical score offering its own variations.

Embodying the very essence of their countries or regions of origin, and encompassing the simplest to the most sophisticated, the traditional to the most imaginative, the specialities dreamt up by our chefs showcase the art de vivre at its very finest. There is something to tantalise every palate, for truly unforgettable moments of sharing and togetherness.

And where does the inspiration come from? Well, you don’t have to look far – a quick stroll through La Mamounia's vibrant kitchen garden is all it takes to find a wealth of fresh herbs and vegetables. Our fish and seafood are sourced from nearby waters. And with the arrival of each new season, our menus are updated with exciting new seasonal dishes, lovingly crafted by our teams.
