# The rooms, suites and Riads at La Mamounia

## Luxury 5-star hotel in Marrakech

According to the time-honoured expression in the hotel industry, La Mamounia has 209 keys.

That is 209 intricately crafted doors adorned with knockers that symbolise Moroccan hospitality, and each opening onto magnificent spaces that celebrate the finest in Moroccan craftsmanship: woodwork, mashrabiya windows with their poetic interplay of light and shade, sculpted stonework, zellige tilework... The whole combines to enchant the eye.

La Mamounia boasts 135 light-filled rooms, 71 suites offering guests true intimacy, and 3 Riads where you can enjoy the experience of a secluded stay tucked away from view. Each of these havens of refined, understated luxury, some with a terrace affording soothing views where you can soak up the peace and tranquillity of the surroundings at your own pace.

The proportions and dimensions of each room have been designed to achieve that perfect balance between space and intimacy.

## From the most intimate to the most palatial

Whether you are visiting alone, as a couple or with family and friends, La Mamounia offers the perfect setting for your stay.

Large and bright, our rooms are havens of tranquillity and luxury comfort. Featuring one to three bedrooms, our range of suites are ideal for families and friends.

Our executive Suites cultivate the very essence of the art of living, while our Exceptional Suites take you on an unforgettable journey into a unique and dazzling world.

With their own private entrances, pools and terraces, our Riads allow you to enjoy La Mamounia experience, tucked away from view and in complete serenity.

This is where the bounty of La Mamounia shines through: whichever room you choose, you'll enjoy wonderful views over the Gardens, pool, Hivernage area or the Koutoubia Mosque.

You'll be enveloped in a world of soothing beauty, where you'll savour each passing moment in the most refined of surroundings.

Enjoy the luxury of our bathrooms, all with separate shower and bathtub, sumptuously decked out in marble and mashrabiya wooden latticework.

## _Our services :_   offering care and attention at every turn

During your stay, we will do everything we can to make it as enjoyable as possible.

Our room service offers a gourmet selection of our various menus at all times.

Our concierge service is at your disposal to meet all your needs and assist you with your excursions and visits to the city. We will of course take care of your laundry and can accompany you on your trips.

Guests in our Exceptional Suites also benefit from a private transfer between the hotel and the Marrakech-Menara airport.

WHAT KIND OF BREAKFAST IS SERVED AT LA MAMOUNIA MARRAKECH?

La Mamounia offers a buffet breakfast at the restaurant Le Pavillon de la Piscine or room service breakfast a la carte.

DO SPECIAL CONDITIONS EXIST FOR EASTER, ALL SAINTS AND END OF YEAR FESTIVITIES?

A minimum stay is required for certain periods during the year including Easter, All Saints and for the end of year festivities. For further details please visit our website www.mamounia.com or contact our booking service at the following address: **reservations@mamounia.com**

HOW DO I GET TO LA MAMOUNIA?

La Mamounia is located at 6 km (3.7 miles) from the international airport “Marrakech-Menara”, which represents 10 minutes by car and at 200 km (125 miles) from the international airport “Casablanca Mohamed V”, which is approximately 2 hours by car.

WHICH AIRPORT IS CLOSEST TO LA MAMOUNIA?

La Mamounia is located at 6 km from the airport “Marrakech-Menara”. A journey time of 10 minutes by car.

HOW DO I GET FROM THE AIRPORT TO THE HOTEL?

We offer a private transfer service in a Porsche Panamera, Range Rover or Jaguar Daimler to and from the airports of Marrakesh and Casablanca. Please contact our concierge service to book this service: leconcierge@mamounia.com.

DOES LA MAMOUNIA PROVIDE SECURE CAR PARKING FACILITIES?

We offer a valet parking service with a secure outdoor parking area, monitored 24 hours a day and equipped with a car wash station.

Find here the location of the guest parking area and the access map to make your arrival easier.

La Mamounia provides accessibility for people with reduced mobility?
