# The Exclusives at La Mamounia

## _Innovative spaces_ injecting new life into La Mamounia

As a living legend in a state of perpetual metamorphosis

La Mamounia has reinvented itself once again through creative innovation. With its the Cinéma, the Œnothèque, the Salle de Jeux and charming the Lanternes and the Jardins, it offers guests out-of-the-ordinary experiences, which herald the next chapter in a magical story, sure to delight one and all.

In a haven of blue velvet, concealed behind a sliding door, lies a delightful little Cinéma. Three large Lanternes house the extraordinary Œnothèque and two private lounges, while lush Jardins beckon you to while away peaceful hours in fragrant surroundings.

And then there's the Salle de Jeux, where adults will enjoy spending time with their children or rediscovering their teenage years. La Mamounia’s Exclusives are delightful hidden treasures brimming with the promise of unforgettable moments.
