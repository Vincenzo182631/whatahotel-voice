**Information on the Dresden Accommodation Tax**

The State Capital of Dresden levies an accommodation tax. All individuals staying overnight in Dresden in hotels or similar establishments on a paid basis are liable to pay this tax. This applies to both leisure and business stays.

The tax rate is 6% of the gross accommodation cost. Please note that this tax is already included in our quoted rates. Hotel Taschenbergpalais Kempinski Dresden is legally obliged to itemise this tax separately on your bill and remit it to the city authorities.

Exemptions from the tax apply only to minors (under 18 years of age) and individuals who are required to stay overnight in the city due to verifiable and compelling medical reasons.

If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us at T +49 351 4912 636 or via email to reservations.taschenbergpalais@kempinski.com.

**VISA Requirements**

Citizens of EU member states do not need a visa. All other nationalities generally require a visa when staying in Germany/Dresden.

Please find more information on the official website of the Federal Foreign Office: https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/einreiseundaufenthalt/visabestimmungen-node

#### Arrival Information

**By Car**

Adhere to the guiding system through Dresden city centre by following signs saying "Hotel Route B" and it will lead you right to Hotel Taschenbergpalais Kempinski.

- Exit the A4 highway at "Dresden-Altstadt"
- Use "Kleine Brüdergasse" as a navigation point
- Follow the "Ostra-Allee" and next to Zwinger Palace you will see Hotel Taschenbergpalais Kempinski Dresden on your left-hand side

Our partner, the APCOA underground car park, offers direct access to the hotel and provides ample parking spaces for our guests at a rate of EUR 35 per vehicle, per day. Access to the car park is via the entrance on Kleine Brüdergasse.

Please note that a parking space in the underground car park can only be guaranteed when using our valet parking service.

The valet parking service includes the parking and retrieval of your vehicle by our team and guarantees a space in the APCOA underground car park. The valet fee is EUR 10 per service (parking and retrieval) and is charged per transaction. To utilise this service, simply hand over your vehicle at the hotel’s main entrance. Please note that the parking fee of EUR 35 per day applies in addition to the valet parking charge.

**By train**

Dresden's main station is about 3.6 km (2.4 miles) away from the hotel, which is about ten minutes by car. If you take tram 11 in the direction of 'Zschertnitz' from the 'Postplatz', it is an eight-minute ride to the main station.

**Public transport**

The closest stops to Hotel Taschenbergpalais Kempinski is "Postplatz" (Tram lines 1, 2, 4, 8, 9, 11, 12; Busses 62, 94). All three stops are located within walking distance from the hotel. For more information, please visit the DVB official website.

**Limousine Transfer**

Our concierge team will be happy to arrange a car or limousine-transfer according to your preferences. For further information as well as booking requests, please contact our concierge desk at T +49 351 4912 805 or via email to dre.concierge@kempinski.com.

#### **Directions**

Dresden International Airport is about 6.1 miles away from the hotel, which is about 20 to 30 minutes by car depending on the traffic; by taxi about EUR 40.

Please be aware that all prices an arrival times can vary due to the time of day and traffic conditions.

**Charging Stations for Tesla Vehicles and Electric Cars**

As the first hotel in Dresden, the Taschenbergpalais has one charging station for Tesla vehicles and two charging stations for electric cars. Please inform us during your reservation if you would like to use a charging station.

In addition to the parking fee of EUR 35/day per vehicle, a partial or full charge costs EUR 25/charge for electric vehicles and EUR 15/charge for hybrid vehicles.
