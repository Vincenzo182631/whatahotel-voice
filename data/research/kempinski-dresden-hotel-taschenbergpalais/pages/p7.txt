### Two MICHELIN Keys for Hotel Taschenbergpalais Kempinski Dresden

The MICHELIN Guide 2025 has awarded Hotel Taschenbergpalais Kempinski Dresden two MICHELIN Keys, celebrating hotels that embody exceptional quality, distinctive design and heartfelt hospitality.

According to The MICHELIN Guide, two Keys are reserved for properties that ‘guarantee an exceptional stay – unique in character, style and atmosphere, marked by passion and care.’ This prestigious recognition places the Taschenbergpalais among Germany’s most distinguished hotels, reaffirming its reputation for seamlessly blending excellence with warmth.

Nestled in the heart of Dresden’s historic old town, the Taschenbergpalais unites heritage, culture and modern luxury in perfect harmony. Every stay is a reflection of refined comfort, attentive service and the timeless Kempinski philosophy of hospitality.

We extend our deepest gratitude to our loyal guests and dedicated team, whose passion and commitment have made this achievement possible.
