# Ladies and Gentlemen in Red

At the heart of Kempinski is the remarkable people who look after each and every guest attentively and thoughtfully.

### The Most Attentive Host

Our Ladies and Gentlemen in Red extend a warm welcome to Hotel Taschenbergpalais Kempinski Dresden. To ensure your stay in Saxony’s historic capital is as seamless, luxurious and memorable as possible, our dedicated team is always at your service, ready to fulfil your every wish.

For more than a decade, every guest arriving at a Kempinski hotel has been greeted by our iconic Ladies and Gentlemen in Red. As our premier brand ambassadors, they provide attentive, bespoke and intuitive service tailored entirely to your needs. Distinctively dressed in striking red, your host possesses unrivalled knowledge of our historic luxury hotel in Dresden and the surrounding region.

Whether you are travelling for business, a cultural getaway or a romantic weekend in the ‘Florence on the Elbe’, our Guest Service Team delivers world-class concierge services designed to enhance every aspect of your stay.

Allow us to curate unforgettable moments during your time in Dresden:

- Culture & Entertainment: Last-minute tickets to the world-renowned Semper Opera House (Semperoper), the Royal Palace or the magnificent Zwinger Palace.
- Fine Dining: Priority reservations at the city’s most exclusive restaurants and hidden culinary gems.
- Bespoke Experiences: Private guided tours of the historic Old Town and tailored excursions to the breathtaking Saxon Switzerland National Park.
- Shopping & Lifestyle: Insider recommendations and personalised shopping experiences at Dresden’s most elegant luxury boutiques.

Discover the city’s best-kept secrets with our Lady in Red Dresden and immerse yourself in a stay defined by culture, history and unparalleled luxury.
