# Location

Conveniently located in the heart of Dresden’s historic city centre, our five-star hotel is close to the city’s most renowned sights.

### Where to find us

LOCATION INFORMATION

Grand Hotel Taschenbergpalais Kempinski is located in the capital of Saxony, directly in Dresden's historic city centre "Innere Altstadt" - only a few steps away from the most renowned sights, such as Semper Opera House, Royal Palace, Zwinger and the Church of Our Lady (Frauenkirche). We wish you a safe journey and we look forward to welcoming you soon!

Our concierge team will be happy to arrange a car or limousine-transfer according to your preferences. For further information as well as booking requests, please contact our concierge desk at T +49 351 4912 805 or via email to dre.concierge@kempinski.com. We wish you a safe journey:

Please find detailed directions here, if you are travelling by car.

Dresden International Airport is about 10.7 km (6.1 miles) away from the hotel, which is about 20 to 30 minutes by car depending on traffic; by taxi about EUR 30.

Dresden's main station is about 3.6 km (2.4 miles) away from the hotel, which is about ten minutes by car. If you take the Tram 11 in the direction of "Zschernitz" it is an eight-minute ride to the main station.
