# Concierge Services

Let us be your guide in Dresden - we would love to recommend restaurants or organise a city tour or concert tickets

### A table at the restaurant, seats at a concert or discovering the city on a tour?

Local Information

At the concierge desk we will be happy to assist you with information about Dresden and its localities as well as about everyday challenges like currency exchange, German area and zip codes, the public transport system or regional customs.

Limousine Services

The fastest and certainly the most comfortable way to travel whenever you need to and wherever you want to: Arrive in style with your private Limousine.Our concierge team will be happy to arrange for a car or limousine according to your preferences.

Table Reservations

Whether you prefer fine-dining or fast food - our concierge team will be able to make a suitable restaurant recommendation and reserve your table accordingly.

Ticket Reservations

Do you need tickets to the opera, the theatre, a show at the cabaret, to museums, galleries or art exhibitions? Just ask the concierge team for assistance.

Tour Bookings

Find out what is the best way for you to discover Dresden and its beautiful surroundings! At the concierge desk we will help you to book guided tours through the city centre, the art district or even to the rocky slopes of Saxon Switzerland to do some hiking or rock climbing.
