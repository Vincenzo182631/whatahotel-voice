### Semper Opera House (Semperoper)

Gracing Theaterplatz in the historic heart of Dresden, the Semperoper stands as a magnificent jewel of Neo-Renaissance architecture. Originally designed by Gottfried Semper in 1841, meticulously reconstructed and reopened in 1985, this world-famous opera house offers unmatched acoustics and aesthetic splendour.

Under the artistic leadership of Artistic Director Nora Schmid and Principal Conductor Daniele Gatti, the 2025/26 season presents a compelling blend of beloved Romantic masterpieces, contemporary stagings and premier ballet productions. Home to the legendary Staatskapelle Dresden – one of the world’s oldest and most prestigious orchestras – an evening at the Semperoper is an unmissable highlight of any stay in Dresden.

- **Distance from Hotel:** A two-minute stroll (200 metres) across Theaterplatz.
- **Dress Code:** Smart, elegant or formal attire is recommended for evening performances.
- **What’s On:** World-class grand opera, modern dance by the Semperoper Ballett and symphonic concerts by the Staatskapelle Dresden.

- **Ticket Reservations:** Tickets can be purchased directly from the opera box office or effortlessly arranged through our Concierge team, who can assist in securing the finest available seats.
- **Contact:** Saxon State Opera Dresden \| Theaterplatz 2, 01067 Dresden \|  T +49 351 4911 705

### Dresdner Musikfestspiele

One of Europe’s most distinguished classical music festivals, the Dresdner Musikfestspiele takes place every spring under the artistic direction of cellist Jan Vogler. The festival brings together world-renowned orchestras, virtuoso soloists and ensemble masters across iconic venues, including the Semperoper, the Kulturpalast and modern industrial spaces such as Kraftwerk Mitte. Each year features a curated central theme, bridging classical masterpieces with contemporary interpretations.

- **Distance from Hotel:** Various historic venues across the city (Kulturpalast: five-minute walk; Semperoper: two-minute walk).

- **Contact:** Dresdner Musikfestspiele \| Hauptstraße 21, 01097 Dresden \| T +49 351 478 56 0

### Staatsschauspiel Dresden

The Staatsschauspiel Dresden operates across two distinct venues. The grand theatre, situated immediately opposite Postplatz, presents classic and contemporary drama in a magnificent historic setting. Across the river in the vibrant Neustadt district, the Kleines Haus offers modern, avant-garde theatre, readings and intimate artistic performances.

- **Distance from Hotel:** **Schauspielhaus (Old Town):** three-minute walk (300 metres)

**Kleines Haus (Neustadt):** 15-minute walk across the Augustus Bridge.

- **Contact:** Staatsschauspiel Dresden \| Theaterstraße 2, 01067 Dresden \| T +49 351 4913 555

### Kulturpalast Dresden

Built in 1969 and spectacularly redesigned in 2017, the Kulturpalast is a modern architectural masterpiece, famed for its world-class concert hall acoustics. It is the official home of the world-renowned **Dresdner Philharmonie**. In addition to symphonic concerts, the venue houses the central city library and the celebrated cabaret theatre _Die Herkuleskeule_.

- **Distance from Hotel:** A five-minute walk (450 metres) along Wilsdruffer Straße.

- **Contact:** Kulturpalast/Dresdner Philharmonie \| Schloßstraße 2, 01067 Dresden \| T +49 351 4866 866

### Additional Cultural Highlights in Dresden

- **Kraftwerk Mitte** **:** A transformed industrial power station now serving as a vibrant cultural complex. It houses the **Staatsoperette Dresden**, celebrated for light opera and musicals, the tjg. theater junge generation and lively dining venues
- **Theaterkahn** **(Dresden Theatre Boat):** Moored at the Terrassenufer near the Augustus Bridge (a six-minute walk from the hotel), this unique floating stage offers charming theatrical productions accompanied by French cuisine at the on-board _Brasserie Bateau_.
- **Boulevardtheater Dresden** **:** A highly popular private theatre offering light-hearted comedies, entertaining shows and musicals suitable for all generations.
