# Dogs welcome

Bring your four-legged friends, they will also be treated like royalty.

### Dogs welcome: They are treated to a couple of amenities here with us.

Is your furry best friend travelling with you? We would be happy to give your four-legged travelling companion a warm welcome in Dresden.

Whether it is a dog bed and a cosy blanket, a shiny new feeding bowl, exciting walks, a selection of little snacks and titbits and, of course, the à la Carte Dog Menu – we will make sure your little travellers enjoy their stay as much as you do!

## Your dog is well cared for

Delicious food from the bowl:

Cooked salmon fillet with rice

Cooked turkey breast with potatoes

All foods are cooked without spices and bones. A room service charge applies per order.

We are also happy to fulfil any special requests you may have. Does your dog only tolerate a certain type of meat, or is he a vegetarian? Does he have intolerances? Please let us know what you need for your dog, and we will be happy to take care of it.

Please note that we charge a EUR 42.40 fee per pet, per night. We would also like to point out that pets are not allowed in the following areas: The hotel restaurants Das Palais, Breakfast restaurant, Alpine Chalet and Bar 1705. Pets are allowed in the lobby, on the terraces of our restaurants, in the Amalie Pâtisserie & Lounge, Kastenmeiers and, of course, in your room/suite (except the Royale Suite). Assistance dogs are, of course, allowed in all areas of the hotel.

**To cater to the needs of our four-legged guests, we welcome dogs exclusively.** We kindly ask for your understanding that other pets cannot be accommodated in our hotel.

## Walking along the Elbe

Enjoy the hotel’s proximity to the Elbe, and take the opportunity to go on a long walk along the banks of the river. You can reach the banks of the Elbe directly from the city centre in just a few minutes. Your dog will be just as happy.

Enjoy the green meadows and the marvellous view (Canaletto view) of the historic old town. Take the opportunity to stop at a beer garden, let your four-legged friend play with other dogs or simply enjoy the peace and quiet that you can reach after a few minutes’ walk.

With the Hotel Taschenbergpalais Kempinski Dresden, you have found a dog hotel that spoils your four-legged friend just as much as you do. Enjoy your stay in Dresden, and rest assured that your dog is perfectly welcome in our 5-star hotel.
