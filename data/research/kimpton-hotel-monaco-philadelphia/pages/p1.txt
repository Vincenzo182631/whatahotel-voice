Across the street from Philadelphia's iconic Liberty Bell is the Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia. Our bold and beautiful Old City home, an adaptive reuse project of an historic building located on the country’s most historic mile, expertly melds the old and the new. The globally-inspired venue features 268 guest rooms, 13,000 square feet of meeting space, a stunning ballroom with soaring ceilings and natural light, a lauded restaurant (Red Owl Tavern) and a rocking rooftop reprise. We've created a welcoming and inspiring space of which we know our forefathers would surely approve.

### Rooftop Lounge

Let freedom ring. Freedom from boring cocktails; freedom from uninspired surroundings; and most of all, freedom from ceilings. Eleven stories above the Liberty Bell Center, Stratus Rooftop Lounge is an indoor/outdoor hot spot that literally rises above the rest of Philly.

Learn more

### Red Owl Tavern

Located adjacent to Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia, the modern, three-meal tavern boasts a menu that gives regional favorites a global spin, featuring only the best local purveyors, cheesemongers, farmers and sustainable fisheries. Feel at home in this welcoming space, where neighborly meets sophisticated and fancy meets familiar.

Learn more

### Spa Rooms + Suites

In our sweet suites, find ample room in which to move around, whether it’s a designated working area you’re seeking or a space in which to host. Each of our 27 Spa Rooms features a deep, vintage soaking tub, standalone shower and bathroom with plentiful vanity space. Also, indulge with a Deluxe One Bedroom or the (truly) Majestic Suite.

Learn more

### Old City Philadelphia

Our hotel is located right in the most historic square mile of the country, just steps away from Independence Park, The Liberty Bell, Betsy Ross House, Elfreth’s Alley, national parks, to name a few.

Learn more

### Weddings

Impress your guests with our central, historic Philadelphia location and our hotel's alluring juxtaposition of classic architecture and modern, contemporary finishes. Celebrate in exquisite spaces like the soaring ballroom or Stratus Rooftop (arguably one of the hottest venues in town), and enjoy delectable catering from Red Owl Tavern.

Learn more

## FAQs for Hotel Monaco Philadelphia

Does Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia have a pool?

No, Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia does not have a pool.

How much is parking at Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia?

At Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 61 USD .

Overnight valet parking is available for $61 plus tax.

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia.

Pet policy description. We invite you to bring your pet no matter their size, weight, or breed, all at no extra charge. If your pet fits through the door, we will welcome them in.

Pet fee per night: 0 USD

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia?

Check-in at Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia is conveniently located near Philadelphia Intl Airport (PHL), approximately 10.4 miles away (16.74 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia?

Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Independence National Historic Park-0.1 miles away (0.16 kilometers)
- Liberty Bell-0.1 miles away (0.16 kilometers)
- National Constitutional Center
- Museum of the American Revolution
- Franklin Institute-1 miles away (1.61 kilometers)
- Independence Hall-0.1 miles away (0.16 kilometers)
- Philadelphia Museum of Art-2.8 miles away (4.51 kilometers)
- Xfinity Center-5 miles away (8.05 kilometers)
- Citizens Bank Park-5 miles away (8.05 kilometers)
- Lincoln Financial Field-5 miles away (8.05 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
