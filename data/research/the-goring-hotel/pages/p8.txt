# Hotel School

Based just a few streets away in the Westminster Kingsway College building, the school helps people who have experienced homelessness or who have had to flee their homeland enter the wonderful world of hospitality and build life-changing careers.

Based just a few streets away in the Westminster Kingsway College building, the school helps people who have experienced homelessness or who have had to flee their homeland enter the wonderful world of hospitality and build life-changing careers.

## Opening doors, in every sense

Hotel School teaches the core skills of our profession with the help of highly-skilled teachers, offering long-term mentoring and career progression to our graduates. Our work is powered by London’s wonderful hospitality scene, which supports us with teaching, employment, enormous numbers of volunteers and other valuable resources.

Hotel School graduates and alumni can be found enjoying careers in hotels, restaurants and cafés across our city. If you’d like to find out more, please do get in touch.
