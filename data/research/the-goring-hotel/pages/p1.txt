# About The Goring

## Run by the same family for over a century

The Goring has been championing fine food and good times since 1910, when our founder, Otto Goring, built his hotel for the pleasure of his guests. The great and the good of London came then, as they come now — to revel in the spirit of English high society at its most fun.

## Over a century of English eccentricity

### Edwardian Style

1910 was a time when London really knew how to enjoy itself. The Victorian era was over, World War I was yet to come, and this was a time of raucous suppers and extravagant Edwardian balls – a sense of high-spirited fun laced with mischief, which still runs through The Goring today.

**London’s Smartest Address**

### A leisurely stroll from London’s top sites

Set on an elegant Belgravia street, just a royal wave away from St James’s park and a stroll from Chelsea, Westminster and Mayfair (and a spy’s whisper from MI5’s HQ). There is, quite simply, no smarter address in London, and we have the inside track on how to experience it all in style.

**Suites fit for royalty**

### Make yourself at home

Really, staying at The Goring is more like stepping into your own private London residence, where comfort is king and style is a given. Created by leading British designers, relax with views over our lush gardens or onto our peaceful Belgravia street.

Discover our suites

**Hotel School**

Hotel School is not your typical catering college. Launched in 2017 as a collaboration between The Goring hotel and The Passage, the school teaches core hospitality skills to people affected by homelessness, and our mentoring team help create sustained careers in the wonderful world of hospitality.
