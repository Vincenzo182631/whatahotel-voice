## Signature dishes at The Goring

### When in doubt, add caviar

Take a seat in the Dining Room, and you’ll soon catch whispers of lobster, caviar, quail’s eggs, oh my! Our most requested dish, Eggs Drumkilbo – yes, it was HRM the Queen Mum's favourite – is still as beloved as ever. The Goring Native Lobster Omelette also has an almost obsessive following, and if we ever run out of our dry-aged Aylesbury duck, the streets will riot.

**Executive Chef Graham Squire**

### He’s extremely good at cooking

It says everything you need to know about Graham that he cut his teeth in the Michelin-starred kitchens of Claridge’s and Le Manoir aux Quat Saisons. His fondness for creating dishes of excellence is matched only by his love of seasonal produce sourced from British farmers and suppliers.

Formerly the Head Chef of Michelin-starred Trinity and The Lickford Inn in West Sussex, Graham has been heading up The Goring’s Michelin-starred Dining Room since 2019.
