# Afternoon Tea at The Goring

## Did somebody say scones?

The Goring has been perfecting the art of Afternoon Tea since opening its doors more than a century ago. Served daily in the luxurious surroundings of The Veranda, guests can enjoy freshly baked cakes, fragrantly warm scones, gourmet finger sandwiches and - why not? - a chilled glass of Bollinger.

### Dress Code

We consider afternoon tea a thoroughly joyful affair. We love to dress for the occasion - no sportswear, athletic trainers, shorts or ripped jeans for us - and we warmly invite our guests to do the same.
