**Signature Cocktails**

### Would you like ice with that?

The Goring’s signature cocktails are an homage to people of note. Grandpa Goring liked to order a ‘Garden Negroni’ in the mornings, which updates the timeless classic with Vetiver and Sakura, while a ‘Thank You, Your Majesty’ pays tribute to HRM The King with a dazzling blend of raspberry, yuzu syrup, gin and champagne. Chin-chin and down the hatch.
