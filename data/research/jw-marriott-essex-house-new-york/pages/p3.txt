# Introducing the Central Park Suite Collection

Discover our reimagined premier suites, inspired by our proximity to and the spirit of Central Park.

## LUXURY MULTI-BEDROOM SUITES IN MANHATTAN, NEW YORK

The JW Marriott Essex House New York is proud to debut the Central Park Suite Collection, four stunning, multi-bedroom, luxury 2-bedroom suites in New York that pay homage to the hotel’s iconic front lawn: Central Park. Each fully reimagined suite tells a distinct story through design, artwork, and other touches inspired by personalities who helped shape the cultural landscape of New York City.  With breathtaking views, thoughtful amenities, and an inspiring collection of art, the Central Park Suite Collection continues JW Marriott’s tradition of a mindful approach to luxury where guests can find balance in body, mind, and spirit.

### PROMENADE SUITE

Inspired by Central Park's iconic Literary Walk, the Promenade Suite offers a front-row perspective of New York's most celebrated landscape. Located on the 38th floor, this two-bedroom suite features panoramic Central Park views, a spacious parlor, open kitchen, dining table for six, and Bang & Olufsen sound system. Designed for gathering and entertaining, the suite pairs thoughtfully curated interiors with extraordinary views that transform every stay into a distinctly New York experience.

### Delacorte Presidential Suite

Rooted in nature with a palette inspired by the flora and fauna found in the park below, the Delacorte Presidential Suite honors George T. Delacorte, founder of Dell Publishing and a quintessential NYC philanthropist. A serene hotel suite perched in the sky above Manhattan, New York, the colors and textures mirror the abundant greenery below, bringing the outdoors in. The thoughtful redesign maximizes space through an impactful entryway, open living, and dining room which features a table for eight crafted from sustainably sourced wood.

### Meadow Terrace Suite

An embodiment of sophisticated, decorative, and feminine design, the classic yet contemporary Meadow Terrace Suite is inspired by the legendary Elsie de Wolfe, a renowned Manhattan interior designer and socialite known for her opulent, sumptuous eye. Embracing natural light with large windows and a terrace offering mesmerizing views of Central Park and Columbus Circle, the expansive vistas invite the beauty of the outdoors to merge seamlessly with the luxe interior. Gracious terrace views overlooking Central Park's Sheep Meadow provide an inviting space for guests to unwind and bask in the tranquility of nature. Savor the iconic view at our hotel with luxury 2-bedroom hotel suites in Manhattan, New York​.

### Artisans Terrace Suite

Named after the “Artisans Gate”—Central Park’s 7th Avenue entrance—this bold Art Deco-inspired suite honors New York’s craftsmanship legacy. Design nods to Gershwin’s “Rhapsody in Blue,” with a piano key installation and vibrant hand-painted mural. Highlights include a wraparound terrace, kitchenette with service entry, and private terrace contrast therapy with cold plunge and sauna.

### EXPANDED LIVING & MULTI-GENERATIONAL STAYS

Designed to evolve with your stay, the Promenade Suite can expand into a three-bedroom or four-bedroom accommodation through adjoining guest rooms. The entire 38th floor is also available for exclusive buyouts. Ideal for multi-generational family travel, milestone celebrations, guests traveling with childcare professionals, or those seeking enhanced privacy, every stay can be tailored by our expert Clefs d'Or concierge team, balancing meaningful time together with opportunities to experience New York on your own terms.

### CONTRAST THERAPY

Guests staying in the luxury Artisans Terrace Suite can elevate their wellness journey in Midtown Manhattan with private contrast therapy. The Terrace hotel suite features a Cold Stoic® 2.0 cold plunge and Zeno 4-person barrel sauna by Renu Therapy, pairing heat and cold immersion with sweeping city views. Guests may also reserve 1-on-1 sessions with an expert coach for guided breathwork and restorative rituals.  ​
