### Frequently Asked Questions

What restaurants does JW Marriott Essex House New York have on property?

The on-property restaurants at JW Marriott Essex House New York are:

Bourbon Steak New YorkIn-Room DiningExecutive Lounge

Does JW Marriott Essex House New York offer room service?

Yes, room service is available at JW Marriott Essex House New York.

What cuisine is offered at JW Marriott Essex House New York?

The cuisines offered at JW Marriott Essex House New York are:

American at In-Room Dining, Executive LoungeSteakhouse at Bourbon Steak New York
