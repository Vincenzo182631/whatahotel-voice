Our expert concierge team is ready to suggest itineraries to delight first-time visitors and seasoned travelers alike, from tickets to the most...See More popular Broadway shows to exclusive dinner reservations and cultural events. Situated in the Heart of New York City

### Central Park

Find two of NYC's most spectacular and celebrated attractions, Central Park and Central Park Zoo, just across the street from JW Marriott Essex House New York. With Central Park as the hotel's front lawn, guests have access to the breathtaking grounds and are encouraged to experience the wonders of the changing seasons, from the cherry blossoms in spring to the exquisite fall foliage in autumn.

### Experience the Magic from your Room or Suite

This November, join us at our hotel on Macy's Thanksgiving Parade route in New York​. Enjoy the excitement and luxury of having a front-row seat with unparalleled views of New York City’s most famous parade from inside your room or outside along Central Park South where you and your loved ones will experience holiday magic as the famous floats return in person. Parade-View rooms and suites are not available online and must be booked through our Thanksgiving Team. For more information, please email EssexThanksgiving@Marriott.com.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Essex House New York have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Essex House New York offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Technogym® and Peloton Bikes, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Essex House New York have an on-site spa?

Yes, PRIMP Spa is located on-site at JW Marriott Essex House New York.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Essex House New York?

Broadway, Lincoln Center, The Shops at Columbus Circle, NBC Studios, 5th Ave/Madison Ave Shopping and more are nearby JW Marriott Essex House New York.
