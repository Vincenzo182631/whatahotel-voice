# Indulge in Our Luxurious Midtown Manhattan Rooms & Suites

## Accommodations

Our recently reimagined luxury accommodations in Midtown Manhattan​ boast Art Deco designs, a variety of connecting room combinations, and luxury amenities such as plush bedding, marble bathrooms, and views of Central Park and NYC.

Breathtaking Views & Exquisite Style

### Explore the Central Park Suite Collection

Introducing the Central Park Suite Collection. Each of our luxury Midtown Manhattan hotel suites has a distinct story and look, inspired by the hotel’s location across from Central Park. Featuring thoughtful design, spacious terraces, expertly curated art collections, and stunning views of Central Park and Manhattan, these premier suites offer an unparalleled NYC experience. New this fall, the Promenade Suite joins the collection, overlooking Central Park from the 38th floor, with panoramic views and a parlor designed for gathering.

Learn More

## Picture Perfect Location

The JW Marriott Essex House New York proudly stands on Central Park South. At the heart of Midtown Manhattan, our luxurious penthouse-style hotel suites with Central Park and city views will take your breath away.

## Delacorte Presidential Suite

Boasting sweeping Central Park views, this lavish two-bedroom suite in Midtown Manhattan offers all the comforts of a residential apartment.

Above the City That Never Sleeps

## Artisans Terrace Suite

Our Artisans Terrace Suite features a wraparound terrace with views of the Manhattan skyline.

## Meadow Terrace Suite

Indulge in luxury with a stay in our Manhattan suite hotel in NYC. The Meadow Terrace Suite features sumptuous décor and a large terrace overlooking Central Park.

## Promenade Suite

Inspired by Central Park’s iconic Literary Walk, the Promenade Suite pairs extraordinary park views with richly layered interiors and thoughtfully curated artwork. An expansive parlor, open kitchen and dining area for six create an inviting setting for gathering above Manhattan.

To learn more about our luxury Manhattan suites please contact our team directly: CentralParkSuiteCollection@Marriott.com
