## A Landmark New York City Hotel

JW Marriott Essex House has been classified as a New York Historical Hotel since 1931. The iconic "Essex House" rooftop sign was installed in 1932 and has since become a fixture of the city skyline.

## Exquisite Art Deco Décor

The architecture and Art Deco décor at our Central Park South hotel in New York City preserve the building's rich history. Curated art, delicate flourishes, geometric carpet patterns, original ornate brass elevator doors, crystal lamps and chandeliers found throughout the hotel all reflect the glamour of the fashionable Art Deco era.

## JW Marriott x Flamingo Estate

JW Marriott has partnered with Flamingo Estate to create a sensorial experience that brings guests closer to nature through scent, sound, and taste—a signature expression of the brand’s commitment to mindful luxury. At JW Marriott Essex House New York, this collaboration comes to life from the moment of arrival, with an herbaceous, uplifting scent and immersive soundscape setting a calming tone. Guests are invited to bring the experience home, with a curated selection of Flamingo Estate products—crafted exclusively for JW Marriott, including the Expansion Candle and California Honey with Holy Basil & Bergamot—available for purchase.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Essex House New York?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Essex House New York is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Essex House New York allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Essex House New York. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Essex House New York?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Essex House New York. The daily valet fee is $105.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Essex House New York have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Essex House New York has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Essex House New York booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Essex House New York?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Essex House New York is LaGuardia Airport (LGA). LGA is located approximately 10.3 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Essex House New York have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Essex House New York does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Essex House New York have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Essex House New York has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
