Read The Story. Opens in a new window

The history of the estate winds back 2,500 years to the Etruscans, a time of booming trade and craft enlightenment. The heart of the hotel was once a medieval castle, built in 998 to capitalise on the location’s sprawling views. The aristocratic Bargagli family cultivated the site further during the 1800s, and families of nobility carried through the 20th century, issuing a call to the world’s international elite.

This was when the Visconti family stepped into the scene, with filmmaker Luchino Visconti’s legendary parties adding a splash of Hollywood grandeur that still resonates today.
