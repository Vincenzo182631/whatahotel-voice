## A tropical paradise for the soul

With dramatic cliffs backing the sparkling Caribbean Sea on one side and a secluded bay with sandy shallows on the other, Six Senses La Sagesse Grenada blends a back-to-nature feel with heartfelt hospitality. Located on the southeastern coast of Grenada, this serene retreat is both romantic and family friendly, faithfully incorporating the island’s vibrant culture, rich spice heritage, and laid-back rhythms.

Let Grenada steal your heart—and make it impossible to leave.

## Unwind in spacious accommodations

Set across 38 acres of lush tropical landscape, our 56 pool suites and 15 villas are designed for privacy and comfort. Influenced by traditional Caribbean architecture, they offer spacious interiors, natural materials, and dramatic ocean or garden views, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor living experience for couples, families, and private groups.

## A culinary journey for the senses

Join us to celebrate Grenada’s renowned vibrant island flavors in every bite. From seafood to nature’s bounty, everything is sourced locally and seasonally with sustainability in mind, ensuring dishes are delicious, nutritious, and mindful of the environment.

- #### Callaloo

  - A farm-to-table all-day dining experience.
- #### Panquai

  - Poolside relaxed dining and sophisticated cocktails.

## A home-from-home for exploration and relaxation

Beyond the resort’s tranquil setting, Grenada invites you to discover pristine beaches, scenic mountains, coral reefs, and hidden waterfalls. Whether you seek adventure or serenity, Six Senses La Sagesse places you at the heart of the island’s magic.

- Experiences

## Destination

Enjoy an ideal setting to reconnect with what matters in life. If you’re traveling with little ones, young adventurers will love the sandy bay and nature-based learning, and there are a host of activities on and off the resort to engage the whole family. The Grow With Six Senses program and Teens’ Lounge give you a well-needed break to enjoy a lazy lunch or time in the spa-aah and babysitting is available upon request. Bring your whole party to paradise.
