# The Spa at Bernardus

## A Sanctuary for Mind, Body, Soul

Imagine a sun-drenched spa sanctuary that heightens all the senses. The nature of our artistic Carmel Valley surroundings inspires a destination spa experience, a garden-to-treatment philosophy with truly mindful services, customized offerings, and complete itineraries that redefine effortless relaxation in the Wine Country. Welcome to The Spa at Bernardus—where our menu caters to individuals, couples, and groups in search of well-being.
