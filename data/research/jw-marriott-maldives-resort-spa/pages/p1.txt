# Welcome to JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa

## Stay in the Moment at Our Maldives Luxury Resort

Edging the azure waters of Shaviyani Atoll, JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa is a sanctuary of scenic luxury. Find your respite in stylish overwater villas and beach villas with plush bedding, private pools and decks with stunning views of the shimmering Indian Ocean at our 5 star beach resort in the Maldives. Delight your palate with distinctive Japanese, Italian and global cuisine, along with treetop...See More dining and buffet breakfasts at our diverse dining destinations. Discover a wide variety of activities, including snorkeling, scuba diving, jet-skiing and sailing. Children can also enjoy their own adventures at this multigenerational resort in the Maldives at our Little Griffins kids' club. Refresh in the sparkling waters of our pool or get in an exhilarating workout at our modern fitness center. Punctuate a memorable day in Shaviyani Atoll with a rejuvenating beauty, massage or wellness therapy at our luxury spa. Discover the warm and inviting hospitality that awaits you at our Maldives luxury resort.See Less

## SIgnature Villas

The Signature Villas at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa offer an unparalleled escape in the Maldives. The Duplex Overwater Pool Villas feature a spacious master bedroom and an upstairs lounge with breathtaking views of the Laccadive Sea, perfect for couples seeking romance and tranquility. For those who prefer the shore, the Duplex Beach Pool Villas are surrounded by lush tropical greenery, with private pools and direct access to the pristine blue lagoon. These villas blend elegance, privacy, and natural beauty, ranking among the best in the Maldives.

### More With JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa

Create unique and meaningful experiences at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa, a tropical island paradise situated on a private island in Shaviyani Atoll. Stylish villas, located both on the beach and on the water, offer guests their own private pools and decks with stunning views across the Indian Ocean. Sample the Flavors of the Maldives at our luxury resort as well as global specialties at our international...See More restaurants, which feature treetop dining, plentiful breakfasts, Japanese and authentic Italian cuisine. Discover a wide variety of activities ranging from snorkelling, scuba diving, jet-skiing and sailing to our Little Griffins Kid's Club. Spoil yourself at the rejuvenating spa or stay fit with our resort's state-of-the-art gym and fitness classes.See Less

Thakuru Service

At JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa, we understand the importance of personalized service. Allow our Thakuru team to assist you in arranging a personalized itinerary during your stay with us. Our Thakurus will be more than happy to recommend customized recreational activities on the island such as spa & wellness activities, private dining arrangements, and dolphin cruises to name a few.

Island Time

Experience meaningful moments together with family and loved ones with an extra hour of daylight each day during your stay at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa. The resort’s time zone is an hour ahead of the capital, Male’. Maldives Time (GMT +5) - Island Time (GMT +6)

Library & Business Center

Our Library & Business Center is located at the Arrival Pavilion. Opens 24 hours a day, guests may bring their own laptop to Library or use one of the personal computers available with wired internet connections.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa?

JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa is Velana International Airport (MLE). MLE is located approximately 192.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
