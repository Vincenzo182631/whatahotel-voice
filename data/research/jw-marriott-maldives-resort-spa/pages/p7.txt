### Festive Experiences

### Luxury Breakfast

Begin the day with an elevated breakfast experience featuring premium ingredients, refined presentation and indulgent morning favorites.

### The Enchanted Garden to Glass

An immersive garden-to-glass experience where fresh botanicals, tropical ingredients and inspired cocktails come together in a celebration of nature.

### Fire Dance

Experience the thrill and artistry of our Fire Dance, where skilled performers light up the night, creating a spectacular display that captivates and inspires.

### Omakase Indulgence

Embark on an intimate Japanese culinary journey, where our chef presents a thoughtfully curated progression of seasonal ingredients and signature creations. Delight in pristine seafood, premium cuts, delicate sushi and sashimi, and the refined artistry of Japanese cuisine.

### Churrasco on Fire

A Brazilian-inspired feast built around the theatre of open-fire grilling. Prime cuts are slow-roasted and carved tableside, accompanied by vibrant salads, traditional accompaniments, sauces and bold South American flavours.

### Silk Route Riha – Pan-Indian Delight

A celebration of India’s rich culinary heritage, inspired by the vibrant flavours and regional traditions of the subcontinent. From exquisite tandoor specialties and fragrant biryanis to coastal treasures and decadent desserts, each dish showcases the richness, diversity, and timeless allure of Indian cuisine.

### Little Garden Explorers

Step into the world of nature with our JW Garden Tour. Young guests can discover fresh vegetables, fragrant herbs, and the secrets of our lush resort garden while learning about healthy and sustainable living in a fun, hands-on way.

### Creative Corner

Unleash imagination and creativity at our Kids Club Arts & Crafts sessions. From painting to crafting, children can express themselves, create keepsakes, and take home their festive masterpieces as memories of their Maldivian escape.

### Splash & Play

Make a splash with our Kids Pool Activities, designed for fun and safe water play. From games to floating adventures, little ones can enjoy endless excitement under the watchful eye of our trained team.
