### Duplex Paradise Awaits

Embracing its idyllic setting, the Duplex Overwater Pool Villas are the ideal option for families or couples seeking to mark a special occasion with an extraordinary stay. Sleeping three across 274sqm, these overwater abodes include a spacious private pool & terrace area with steps leading directly into the lagoon, and an upstairs lounge area from where guests can take in far-reaching views of the Indian Ocean.

### Designed by Nature

Each villa draws inspiration from our natural surroundings and local Maldivian culture.
The thatched roofs are reminiscent of upside-down Dhoni boats, while the slanted roof tips recall white herons dipping their heads into the water.

### Barefoot Indulgences

Embrace the unending ocean views from the comfort of your sun-kissed pool deck or dip into your private plunge pool before soaking in the serenity at your marbled bathtub. Go ahead and explore, discover, and embrace your sense of adventure, and dive into the blue Indian Ocean to snorkel and see the world underwater.

### Duplex Paradise Awaits

Surrounded by paradise on all sides, our Two Bedroom Duplex Beach Pool Villa is the perfect venue for your quiet getaway. Enjoy an array of curated experiences like our floating breakfast, delightful in villa dining, or the exhilaration that awaits with our overwater activities.

## Escape To Luxury

Together is a wonderful place to be, especially when you are at our white sand paradise. Our sanctuary of spacious luxury makes for an ideal family getaway where each villa provides space for enriching experiences and meaningful moments. Make the most of your group gathering at our secluded island where the tans may fade but the memories will last forever.

### Private Paradise

Proud to be one of the most spacious villas in the Maldives, our airy accommodations are colored in beachy hues and accented with lighting inspired by the coral that surrounds our home atoll. Creating a natural hideaway with its wrap-around verdant foliage, our ocean-facing beach villas offer the pinnacle in seclusion with plenty of space for relaxation, play, and everything in between.

### Ultimate Family Vacation

Sitting beachfront, hidden away among verdant foliage that creates natural privacy, Parents can lounge poolside while watching the kids frolic on the soft white sand lapped by the Indian Ocean’s gentle surf. Discover and embrace your sense of adventure and dive into the blue Indian Ocean to snorkel, and see the underwater world from your own beachfront.

### Savor the Good Life

Island living takes a luxurious turn at our secluded Duplex Beach Pool with a private plunge pool, a pool shower, and your personal cabana, hidden away among verdant foliage that creates natural privacy. Enjoy an array of curated experiences like our floating breakfast, delightful in-villa dining, or the exhilaration that awaits with our overwater activities.

### Explore The Maldives With Your Loved Ones

Edging the azure waters of Shaviyani Atoll, JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa is a sanctuary of scenic luxury to nourish your mind, body, and spirit. Feel free to contact us at jwmaldivesreservations@marriott.com for more information.
