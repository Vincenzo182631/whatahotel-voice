# Experience Our Maldives Overwater Villas

## Villas

Enjoy the 60 luxurious Beach and Overwater Pool Villas at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa, which offer spacious living areas ranging from 234 sqm to 285 sqm. Contemporary in design with modern and airy interiors creating the ultimate privacy and tranquility, our spacious Maldives luxury overwater villas are ideal for both couples and families. Take in an unobstructed view of the Indian Ocean or our...See More island's lush tropical gardens from your private villa terrace with its own spacious private wooden deck, pool and outdoor shower. Each villa throughout the JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa draws inspiration from the natural surroundings and local Maldivian culture, incorporating colors and materials that highlight the island destination. Thatched roofs on villas are reminiscent of upside-down Dhoni boats, the traditional wooden fishing vessels of Maldives, and slanted roof tips recall white herons dipping their heads into the water.See Less

### Duplex Villas

Experience luxury island life in the Maldives, in luxury Maldives beach villas with private pools, spacious decks, uninterrupted views of the ocean and personalized butler service. With a reputation as one of the finest multigenerational luxury family resorts in the Maldives, JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa is designed to cater to the most discerning travelers. Contemporary in design with modern and airy interiors creating the ultimate privacy and tranquility, the two-story Duplex Beach Pool Villa or the Duplex Overwater Pool Villa promises an unforgettable experience for the whole family.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa have a work space?

No, the rooms at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa is the Duplex Beach Pool with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa?

Yes, all rooms at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa have fridges?

No, rooms at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa have hair dryers.
