# Welcome to Vana Belle, a Luxury Collection Resort, Koh Samui

## Embrace luxury at our Koh Samui beachfront resort

Surrounded by tropical forests, mythical gardens and the crystal-clear waters of the Gulf of Thailand, Vana Belle, A Luxury Collection Resort, Koh Samui, offers an unrivaled beachfront escape at Chaweng...See More Noi Beach. This luxury Koh Samui beach resort features elegant suites and villas, each with a private pool, where refined design meets natural beauty. Guests can unwind by our beachfront infinity-edge pool, complete with a relaxed pool bar serving refreshing cocktails as the sun sets over the ocean. Indulge in authentic Southern Thai cuisine at Panali, or savor tasty international dishes at Kiree. For moments of pure tranquility, rejuvenate at Vana Spa with our signature Siam Boran massage. This luxury beach resort in Koh Samui, Thailand, also offers an intimate beachfront wedding venue, perfect for romantic celebrations framed by the sea and sky. Ideally located, Vana Belle is just moments away from Koh Samui’s most captivating attractions, including the Elephant Sanctuary, Wat Phra Yai, and Chaweng Beach.See Less

Transportation Information

##### Benz Service

Our signature Benz Airport Transfer Service ensures you begin your luxury experience from the moment you arrive in Koh Samui. With a VIP welcome from the personal butler, enjoy a welcome cold towel, refreshments and entertainment services including Wi-Fi, iPad and personalized music menu. Reserve the Benz Service for expeditions around the island to ensure you always travel in luxury. Relieve any lingering feelings of jet lag with a welcome mini-massage upon arrival at the hotel.

##### Private Car Service

We are pleased to offer a variety of private vehicles piloted by a personal driver for transportation around the island, including to celebrated destinations such as Na Muang Waterfalls or the Big Buddha Temple. Vehicle options include a Mercedes E- Class for up to three guests, a Toyota Fortuner with 4x4 for up to four guests, and a six-person van.

Round-trip to Na Muang Waterfalls:

- Toyota Fortuner: 2,400 THB
- Van: 3,000 THB
- Mercedes E Class: 4,800 THB

Round-trip to Big Buddha Temple:

- Toyota Fortuner: 2,000 THB
- Van: 2,600 THB
- Mercedes E Class: 4,400 THB

##### Songthaew

Red pick-up trucks known as songthaews are commonly seen in Koh Samui, where they operate as private taxis and local buses. They travel on fixed routes all around the island but have no official stops and will circle around to pick up other riders as they drive.

Guests are encouraged to flag them down, hop on, and pay when they disembark, though we advise riders to negotiate their fare prior to departure, particularly at nighttime when additional charges may apply. Please note that only a few songthaews operate at night, and those that do are primarily located in the Chaweng area.

Fee: 200 THB - 500 THB

Note: Estimated charges, fees and schedules are not guaranteed by hotel. Driving distance may be affected by local conditions and traffic.

##### Motorbike

Motorbikes can be rented from numerous locations around the island. Please note that coastal roads are hilly in many places and motorbikes are not insured against accidents.

Fee: 200 THB - 400 THB

Note: Estimated charges, fees and schedules are not guaranteed by hotel. Driving distance may be affected by local conditions and traffic.

##### Taxi

Taxis to downtown and around the island can be hailed in front of the resort. Please note that taxis do not feature a fixed price meter and fees should be negotiated prior to departure, dependent upon the desired destination and time.

Fee: 300 THB - 800 THB

Note: Estimated charges, fees and schedules are not guaranteed by hotel. Driving distance may be affected by local conditions and traffic.

## The Luxury Collection Concierge

Available 24 hours a day, the Luxury Collection Concierge at our Koh Samui hotel on the beach​, a certified destination expert proudly wearing the Les Clefs d’Or gilt insignia of the celebrated international concierge society, will take pleasure in revealing to you the treasures of Koh Samui.

The Luxury Concierge

Among the concierge top recommendations are elephant trekking trips that transport guests through the forest atop elephants, combining traditional sightseeing with an exhilarating animal ride. Quad bike (ATV) tours provide another exciting way to see the island’s dramatic scenery. Meanwhile, memorable day trips and local tours include stops at The Big Buddha Temple, Wat Khao Hua Jook Lookout, Namueang Waterfall, and Grandfather and Grandmother Rocks.

Along with arranging these excursions, sightseeing, and more, They are available around the clock, committed to creating an unforgettable stay. Our concierge is pleased to assist with everything from securing local golf reservations to confirming dining reservations at in-house and local restaurants, such as Sabienglae and Spirit House.
