# Restaurants inspired by the splendor of Koh Samui

## Dining

Koh Samui is known for its fish and shellfish, spices, jasmine rice, rice noodles, rich curries and pungent dips, brought together with intriguing influences from China, Malaysia and even India. Each of...See More the restaurants and beachfront bars in Koh Samui at Vana Belle, a Luxury Collection Resort celebrates this wonderful culinary heritage. At our thatched Pool Bar in Koh Samui, your cocktail can be infused with one of the famed local Thai rums, or you can order a freshly made dish of a Koh Samui favorite like khao phad, Thai fried rice. Visit the Lobby Lounge for an unforgettable view of the Gulf of Thailand while enjoying afternoon tea or crafted cocktails. For a change of pace, sample the al fresco at Kiree, with our chefs preparing a selected menu of European and Asian-inspired dishes, presented in the elegant dining room with beach and sea views or outside amongst the sea breezes on the terrace.See Less

Situated Near Our Koh Samui Resort’s Welcoming Outdoor Pool

## Pool Bar

The Pool Bar offers a tempting menu of food and beverages under the shade of a thatched roof only steps away from the beach. Signature beverages include Golden Storm, Vana Belle’s very own Luxury Collection cocktail. Additional bar options include Mekhong Rum and other local Thai rums, together with famous international spirit brands, fresh juices and soft drinks. Equally flavorful, the menu at our pool bar in Koh Samui includes Thai classics such as Thai fried rice and chicken or pork satay. A children's menu presents tried-and-true favorites, like fish-n-chips and spaghetti bolognese.

## Panali

Food from the south has many influences, with Chinese, Malaysian and Indian recipes and ingredients added to the essential Thai style. Fresh fish and shellfish, usually locally caught, rice noodles, coconut juice, sun-dried ingredients, rich curries and spicy dips, all go to make a hearty meal. The chefs at our beachfront restaurants in Koh Samui, Thailand, prepare their recipes, using the finest local ingredients. Panali welcomes guests with the seductive touch of the soft sand, the sound of the ocean waves, and the breeze sighing in the coconut trees. Under the blue sky of daytime or the star-spangled sky at night, select from the menu of authentic recipes and choose the perfect beverage to accompany your meal. Our youngest guests will also find much to enjoy, with their own children’s menu.
