## Discover our hotel suites and rooms in Seattle

Wake up to stunning views in our rooms and hotel suites in downtown Seattle. Our accommodations feature floor-to-ceiling windows, ample space and high-speed internet access. Our 424 hotel rooms and suites draw inspiration from the Pacific Northwest, and feature city views, Davines bath amenities and a W signature pillowtop bed.

### Penthouse Suite

Elevate your stay 24 floors up in our 1,000 square foot Penthouse hotel suite in downtown Seattle, complete with expansive views, spacious master bedroom and bathroom, walk in closet and unique living room and dining space.

## Faces of Seattle

Located on the 14th floor, all guest rooms are home to an original hand-painted mural by resident artist Morgan Zion. Each has a unique portrait of a person representing each of Seattle’s vibrant neighborhoods. A personalized story is woven into each face showcasing the activities Seattle natives love to do, neighborhoods they love to play and live in, hidden gems

##### Whatever/Whenever® Service

Enjoy whatever you want, whenever you want it. At W Seattle, your wish is our command with our Whatever/Whenever® service. Our amazing concierge service - the soul of our Seattle hotel - transforms your dreams into unforgettable moments. Whether you want seats at the Seattle Opera, a personal shopper at the Nordstrom flagship store, a romantic seaplane ride over Lake Union, or bottles of bubbly, you got it. Simply dial zero on your guestroom phone and call us for whatever you want, whenever you want it (as long as it's legal).

If you can imagine it, we can get it. With the Whatever/Whenever® Service, W Seattle is truly an escape from the ordinary.

Drop us a line at wseattle.whatwhen@whotels.com.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Seattle have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Seattle have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Seattle?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at W Seattle. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Seattle?

Some rooms at W Seattle permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Seattle have fridges?

No, rooms at W Seattle do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W Seattle have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Seattle have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Seattle have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Seattle have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Seattle have in-room jettubs?

Yes, some room types at W Seattle have jettubs. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
