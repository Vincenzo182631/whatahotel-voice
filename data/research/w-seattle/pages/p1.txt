## Faces of Seattle

Welcome to Faces, a love letter to Seattle, by W Seattle. Located on the 14th floor, all guest rooms are now home to an original hand-painted mural by resident artist, Morgan Zion. Not one room is the same. Each has a unique portrait of a person representing Seattle’s vibrant culture. A personalized story is woven into each face, showcasing the activities Seattle natives love to do, neighborhoods they love to play and live in, hidden gems they enjoy, and the diversity they represent.

Learn More

## Stay Like a Local

### Whatever/Whenever® Service

Enjoy whatever you want, whenever you want it. At W Seattle, your wish is our command with our Whatever/Whenever® service. Our amazing concierge service - the soul of our downtown Seattle hotel - transforms your dreams into unforgettable moments. Whether you want seats at the Seattle Opera, a personal shopper at the Nordstrom flagship store, a romantic seaplane ride over Lake Union, or bottles of bubbly, you got it. Simply dial zero on your guestroom phone and call us for whatever you want, whenever you want it (as long as it's legal).

If you can imagine it, we can get it. With the Whatever/Whenever® Service, W Seattle is truly an escape from the ordinary.

Drop us a line at wseattle.whatwhen@whotels.com.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Seattle?

The check-in time at W Seattle is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Seattle allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Seattle. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Seattle?

Off-site valet parking is available at W Seattle. The daily valet fee is $80.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Seattle have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Seattle has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Seattle booking.

What is the closest airport near W Seattle?

The closest airport near W Seattle is King County International Airport/Boeing Field (BFI). BFI is located approximately 6.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Seattle have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Seattle has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
