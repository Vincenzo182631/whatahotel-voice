# InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach ResortDining

Six innovative restaurants and a rooftop bar await: refined dining at LAVA, beachfront BBQ at Sea Shack, Japanese and Vietnamese flavors at Sora & Umi, and island-high cocktails at INK 360, the island’s highest rooftop bar.

1. Sky-High Elegance

### Sky-High Elegance

Exuding glamour and style, the iconic INK 360 is designed by Ashley Sutton and boasts unparalleled panoramic views of the sparkling ocean from the 19th floor. Sip on a signature cocktail and enjoy a magnificent sunset.

## Sora & Umi

A picturesque sanctuary that connects guests to nature, Sora Umi on the 2nd floor offers an abundant international buffet breakfast and an extensive à la carte selection of Vietnamese favourites for lunch and dinner. Families will love a dedicated play zone with plenty of toys and activities to keep the youngsters busy while parents soak up the morning light and ocean views. Vegetarian or special dishes can be arranged for guests with allergies or other medical needs.

## Sea Shack

Experience island life at Sea Shack, a breezy beachfront restaurant inspired by the traditional architecture of local fishing villages. Find a cozy spot just steps from the waves and sit back to enjoy live music and an ocean breeze. On the menu are Vietnamese and international flavors, mouthwatering BBQ, and a wide selection of craft beers and tiki cocktails. Vegetarian or special dishes can be arranged for guests with allergies or other medical needs.

## Ombra

Relaxed yet sophisticated, the sunbathed pool bar and restaurant serves fine Italian fare in a laid back, beach club setting. Besides perfectly blistered Napoli style pizza, Ombra by the Oasis pool is also the place to sample refreshing poolside cocktails, smoothies, gelato, and snacks while basking in the casual atmosphere of the pool deck under the sun or at a shady table. Vegetarian or special dishes can be arranged for guests with allergies or other medical needs.
