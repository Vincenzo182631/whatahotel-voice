# InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach ResortRooms & suites

Combining modern comfort with island charm, all our accommodations feature extraordinary views of the sparkling ocean, vivid green mountains, lush gardens, or lagoons. Designed to meet the preferred needs of all discerning travellers, our accommodation options include the elegantly-appointed rooms and suites in our resort wings, the spacious residences in the Sky Tower, or the exclusive beachfront villas.
