### The Perfect Day

At InterContinental Phu Quoc, our dedicated Concierge team captures the island’s natural beauty, vibrant culture, and effortless luxury, turning them into tailored day plans for every traveller. Join us in discovering how we’ve crafted The Perfect Day – designed to transform your tropical escape into a world of fascination.

### Sky-High Elegance

Exuding glamour and style, the iconic INK 360 is designed by Ashley Sutton and boasts unparalleled panoramic views of the sparkling ocean from the 19th floor. Sip on a signature cocktail and enjoy a magnificent sunset.

### Lagoon Serenity

With beautifully appointed treatment suites that appear to ‘float’ on the lush Lotus Lagoon, HARNN Heritage Spa – 2025 Haute Grandeur Global Awards - Certified Spa is a place of natural serenity offering guests a unique and luxurious wellness experience inspired through the long tradition of healing Asian therapies.

### Meeting for Good

Host with purpose at InterContinental Phu Quoc. Our resort designs meetings with sustainability at the core—resources conservation and mindful touches such as plant-based coffee breaks infused with local flavour.

## FAQs for InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort

Does InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort have a pool?

InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

The resort features diverse leisure experiences for every family member, from two main outdoor pools for adults to two outdoor pools with water slides for children and toddlers, to one exclusive outdoor pool for Club InterContinental guests.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort for hotel guests.

Our indoor parking lot is located right inside the resort and can accommodate cars, SUVs and vans. In-house guests are welcome to self-park vehicles in our complimentary outdoor parking lot. Additional charges will be applied for outside guests.

Parking fee: 0 USD per day

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort has 6 restaurants.

Is there free breakfast available at InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort?

Breakfast is free with your stay at the InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort?

Breakfast at InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort is available from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM. On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort?

Check-in at InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort is conveniently located near Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC), approximately 7.46 miles away (12 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort?

InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Sao Beach - 8.2 miles away (13.2 kilometers)
- Dinh Cau - 3.11 miles away (5 kilometers)
- Tranh Stream - 10.56 miles away (17 kilometers)
- Night Market - 9.13 miles away (14.7 kilometers)
- Ho Quoc Pagoda (Truc Lam Ho Quoc) - 11.06 miles away (17.8 kilometers)
- Phu Quoc National Park - 24.23 miles away (39 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
