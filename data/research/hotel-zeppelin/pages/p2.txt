Amenities
Rooms
196
The groovier sister to San Francisco’s Hotel Zetta, the Hotel Zeppelin opened in early March of 2016. Two blocks from Union Square, the 196-room hotel celebrates the city with eclectic amenities—think vintage record players and crushed velvet pillows on the beds—that recall its counter culture roots. In what might be our favorite touch, the bathrooms are emblazoned with the names of local bands, from the Grateful Dead to Green Day, in a psychedelic wallpaper that pays homage to the classic rock posters of the ‘60s and ‘70s. There's also an epic game room, where you can play a few rounds of skeeball, shuffleboard, bingo, and more. For those interested in even more activity, the hotel has partnered with Martone Cycling to offer guests the chance to cruise the city on sleek electric bikes.
