# Welcome to HOTEL THE MITSUI KYOTO, a Luxury Collection Hotel & Spa

## Embrace the beauty of Japan at our luxury hotel in Kyoto

HOTEL THE MITSUI KYOTO, recently awarded the highest Three Key hotel distinction by Michelin, is the first of the HOTEL THE MITSUI brand of iconic, ultra-luxury properties, designed to embody and reflect...See More the harmony between the natural environment, the four seasons, and the five senses. Located in the heart of Kyoto, adjacent to the legendary Nijo Castle, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, this luxury hotel in Kyoto, Japan, is an idyllic sanctuary honoring ancient Japanese traditions and embodying the legacy and spirit of Kyoto. The central Kyoto hotel is built on a 250-year-old historic site that was once the home of the executive branch of the Mitsui Family, carefully designed to reflect a luxurious ambiance of refined sophistication. The first 5-star hotel in Kyoto to receive a Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star rating in the first year of operation, HOTEL THE MITSUI KYOTO is an awe-inspiring feast for the spirit and the senses and a cultural gateway to discover everything Kyoto has to offer.See Less

For us, the concept of Embracing Japan’s Beauty embodies the ideal essence of this Kyoto luxury hotel. Our 160-guestroom haven offers premium relaxation and luxury. Located on the former estate of the Mitsui family, our luxury hotel in Kyoto, Japan, reflects on its historical significance and honors ancient traditions. ### Thermal Spring

Central to our SPA Treatments is the concept of an "Oasis of Serenity," inviting guests to escape their daily routines and immerse themselves in the calming embrace of spring water. ### Private Onsen

A uniquely Japanese experience, we offer natural onsen bathing. Our expansive private onsen, exceeding 100m2, offers complete privacy for a truly memorable experience. ### The Garden

The central courtyard garden at our Kyoto luxury hotel, designed by landscape architect Shunsaku Miyagi, measures 1,300 square meters and reflects a deep respect for Kyoto. It embodies the harmony between structures and gardens, serving as a modern recreation of the garden that graced the former Mitsui residence. It is a place for meditation, yoga, and thoughtful reflection.

### SHIKI-NO-MA

The Abura-no-Koji Residence, once the Kyoto home of the Mitsui family, occupied the site where our hotel now stands. The heart of the house was the Okushoin, also known as the SHIKI-NO-MA, a room designed for observing the changing seasons of the garden. At HOTEL THE MITSUI KYOTO, our SHIKI-NO-MA pays homage to this historical room which was constructed using Japanese cypress. Through meticulous analysis...See More of historical documents and photographs, Yasui Mokuzoku, renowned for its work on the repair and restoration of numerous historical buildings including the Kyoto Imperial Palace, has recreated the SHIKI-NO-MA to accurately reflect the Okushoin. This room can be used for exclusive dining events, meetings, and tea ceremony gatherings.See Less
