# Experience our Kyoto hotel with private onsen

## Experiences

As the imperial capital of Japan for over 1000 years, Kyoto is a profoundly rich locus of Japanese history and culture. It remains the cultural heart of the country, having introduced many Japanese cultural...See More cornerstones such as the "Way of Tea," or the traditional tea ceremony, calligraphy, and various other traditional arts. The city is home to UNESCO World Heritage Sites and countless ancient temples and shrines. At our Kyoto hotel with an onsen, we put our heart into making your stay a memorable one, offering seasonal opportunities and activities for guests to capture and experience the unique culture and history of this ancient capital.See Less

Ceramics Experience

## Crafted by the Artisans of Kiyomizu-Yaki, a One-Of-A-Kind Vessel Just for You

At TOKINOHA, the ceramic studio led by master craftsman Daisuke Shimizu, you’ll find pieces that redefine the concept of traditional Kiyomizu pottery. This tour offers a visit to the TOKINOHA Ceramic Studio, where guests can explore the workshop, shop for unique items, and consult directly with Kiyomizu-san to commission one-of-a-kind, custom-made pieces. Additionally, guests will also have the chance to try their hand at kintsugi, the Japanese art of mending pottery with gold and lacquer. This ancient craft, now admired for its sustainable values, allows you to repair and restore a unique piece of your own to take home. Price :Custom-Made Course: JPY350,000 per group (1–4 guests) / Kintsugi Course: JPY250,000 per group (1–4 guests)

Delve Into Our Rich Culture and History

### HOTEL THE MITSUI KYOTO Ambassador Curated Art Tour

HOTEL THE MITSUI KYOTO is home to several pieces of cultural and historical significance. Ambassadors will guide you through the property, highlighting both small and not-so-small objects. Learn the stories behind these – their creation, significance, and enduring relevance. The tour begins at the iconic Kajiimiya Gate, the hotel's entranceway, and continues to the SHIKI-NO-MA, a space brimming with artifacts and features deeply connected to the Mitsui family legacy. Duration: approx. 45 minutes. Cost per person: Complimentary for our guests (Suitable for guests aged 13 and older).

### Meditation Yoga in Private Onsen 150 mins

In Meditation Yoga, you will relax your body with simple poses, focus on your inner senses through breathing, and deepen your meditation with the use of incense. After yoga at our spa hotel in Kyoto, enjoy a private spa bath, healthy smoothies, and vegan tea sandwiches. Price: JPY50,000 for 1～2 people.

### Experience Tranquility With a Bowl of Tea

Our Ambassadors, who receive personal instruction from a Mushakouji Senke tea master, will perform a ryurei-style seated tea procedure at CHAKYO. You may be tired after your journey to the hotel, or maybe you just want to sit and talk about what you have experienced in Kyoto. Either way, this is the time to take a moment for yourself and enjoy this authentic experience. Price: JPY3,500 per person.

### Transformative Morning Practice

This guided wellness breathing session is suitable for all levels. Gathering on the elegant wooden engawa at our Kyoto hotel with private onsen – the SHIKI-NO-MA room's garden-viewing corridor – the warmth of the morning sun will energize you as we explore mindful breathing. Immerse yourself in renewed energy and serenity as you take in the beauty of our Japanese courtyard garden. Price: Complimentary for guests

Experience the Natural Bliss of Hot Springs

### Spa Indulgence - Onsen Therapy Journey

Featuring the healing waters of Kyoto Nijo Onsen and treatments using camellia oil from Kazurasei. We are delighted to offer wellness program for staying guests, SPA Indulgence: Onsen Wellness Journey, inspired by the long tradition of hot springs as places of healing and restoration. The program has been reimagined around the theme of restorative sleep. Returning to the origin of the word spa, meaning...See More water, the experience focuses on supporting the body’s natural circulation of water to ease daily fatigue and gently guide guests towards deep, restorative rest. The spa treatments incorporate camellia oil from Kazurasei, a historic Kyoto brand founded in 1865 that has supported the beauty of Kyoto’s women for generations. Through original treatment techniques and the nourishing qualities of camellia oil, the program offers a deeply relaxing experience. Price (2-Night Plan): JPY 186,000 for one person / JPY 292,000 for two persons.See Less
