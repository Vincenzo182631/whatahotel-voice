# Experience our luxury hotel rooms in Kyoto

## Accommodations

HOTEL THE MITSUI KYOTO is located in the heart of Kyoto. Adjacent to the iconic Nijo-jo Castle, the hotel occupies the former Kyoto estate of the Kitake family, the executive branch of the Mitsui dynasty....See More For over 250 years, this site served as the family's residence. The tranquility and storied history of the location are reflected in our luxury accommodation in Kyoto, Japan. A modern reimagining of the traditional Japanese tearoom, the 160 guest rooms feature carefully chosen natural materials, elevated using artisanal skills. The bathtubs are fashioned out of stone, and their generous size makes them ideal for stretching out and relaxing. Some of our Kyoto suites feature access to a natural onsen for bathing and relaxation, sourced from an on-site spring. Guests can experience Kyoto’s rich history and traditions whilst unwinding in luxurious surroundings.See Less

Featured Stay

### Nijo Suite and Onsen Suite

Our central Kyoto hotel suites enhance your stay: The Onsen Suite features an outdoor bath fed by natural hot springs, over 101m2 of elegant space, and cherry wood decor for a classic Japanese aesthetic. Enjoy luxurious baths with garden views, indulging in ultimate relaxation. The Nijo Suite offers panoramic views of Nijo Castle, spanning 112m2. Admire the East Otemon Gate, an entrance to the UNESCO World Heritage site, whether bathed in daylight or illuminated at night, creating a breathtaking scene.
