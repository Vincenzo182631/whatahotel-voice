## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Nile City located?

Fairmont Nile City is located in the city center of Cairo, Egypt. The hotel's address is 2005 B, Corniche El Nil, Ramlet Beaulac.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Nile City?

Check-in is at 3:00 pm and check-out is at 12:00 pm.

Does Fairmont Nile City have a spa?

Yes. Fairmont Spa at Fairmont Nile City is ranked #1 of 45 Spas and Wellness Centers in Cairo on TripAdvisor, holds a Travelers' Choice 2026 award and is recognized by Haute Grandeur as the Best Destination Wellness Retreat in Egypt. The spa is open to hotel guests and outside visitors daily from 06:30 AM.

Guests must be 18 or older.

Does Fairmont Nile City have a swimming pool?

Yes. Sky Pool at Fairmont Nile City is located on the 25th floor and offers panoramic views of the Nile River and Cairo skyline, with glimpses of the Giza Pyramids on clear days. Open daily from early morning until sunset, it is one of Cairo's highest outdoor pool experiences and is complemented by a menu of hot and cold dishes and mocktails. Advance reservations are recommended.

Does Fairmont Nile City have a gym/fitness center?

Yes. Fairmont Nile City has a fully equipped, 24-hour fitness center with state-of-the-art equipment. Personal training is available. The fitness center is open to hotel guests and is located within the same wellness floor as Fairmont Spa.

What is the closest airport to Fairmont Nile City?

The closest airport to our hotel is Cairo International Airport. It is about 30 minutes away by car.

What is Fairmont Gold and what does it include?

Fairmont Gold at Fairmont Nile City is a private hotel-within-a-hotel experience across three dedicated floors. It includes private check-in in the Fairmont Gold Lounge, a complimentary Gold Lounge breakfast, light dinner, an all-day and all-night honor bar, evening canapés, 24-hour on-call butler service, a dedicated Gold Concierge and priority luggage handling. The Gold Lounge itself serves as a private working and relaxation space with Nile views throughout the day.

Are all dining venues at Fairmont Nile City open to non-hotel guests?

Yes. All dining venues at Fairmont Nile City — including L'Uliveto, Gingko, Bab El Nil, La Mie, Onyx Lounge, and Sky Pool — are open to outside visitors. Reservations are recommended for Bab El Nil, Gingko, and L'Uliveto on weekend evenings and are required for Sky Pool. Onyx Lounge afternoon tea (daily 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM, 430 EGP per person) also requires advance booking.

What awards has Fairmont Nile City received?

Fairmont Nile City holds recognition from Forbes Travel Guide, has been awarded a Michelin Key for 2025, is a Virtuoso Preferred Partner, and is recognized by Serandipians by Traveller Made. Fairmont Spa at the hotel is ranked #1 of 45 Spas and Wellness Centers in Cairo on TripAdvisor (Travelers' Choice 2026) and is recognized by Haute Grandeur as the Best Destination Wellness Retreat in Egypt.
