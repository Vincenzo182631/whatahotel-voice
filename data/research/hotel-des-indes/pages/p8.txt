## DES INDES    LOUNGE & RESTAURANT

The chandeliers, draperies and comfortable seats in the Restaurant are a living monument to the era that Des Indes was a town palace, where heads of state were entertained.

Our commitment to offer the highest standards is reflected in the delicious dishes that the Chef has created from premium quality products of Dutch origin.
