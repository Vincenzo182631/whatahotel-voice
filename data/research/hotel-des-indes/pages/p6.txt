## FAQ

- What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in at Hotel Des Indes is at 3:00 PM. On the day of departure, please check out at the reception before 12:00 PM.

- Are the rooms and suites accessible for people with disabilities?

All our rooms and suites are accessible for guests with disabilities. You can use one of our elevators to reach your accommodation.
