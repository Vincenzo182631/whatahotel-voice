How much is parking at Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa?

At Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 45 GBP .

Street parking is £6.20 per hour - limit of 2 hours. Valet and self-parking is available via Q Park Sauchiehall St. Valet parking is £45 per night. Self-parking must be prebooked at a 20% discounted rate simply using code BWH20GLA.

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa?

Check-in at Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa is conveniently located near Glasgow Airport (GLA), approximately 8.5 miles away (13.68 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa?

Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- House for an Art Lover-3.1 miles away (4.99 kilometers)
- Kelvingrove Art Gallery and Museum-3 miles away (4.83 kilometers)
- Shopping on Buchanan Street
- OVO Hydro
- Botanic Gardens
- The Clydeside Distillery

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Blythswood Square Hotel & Spa puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
