# Facilities

Immersed in the allure of the sea and the majesty of the natural world, Cala Beach Resort is the ideal place to relax and get active with your family or friends. Discover the many marvels that Maremma has in store with an array of activities you’re sure to love. Relish laid-back days on the beach, invigorating hours in the gym and an exciting round or two at **Punta Ala Golf Club**, for a unique blend of sun, sea, sport and feelgood factor.

## Punta Ala Beach

Spoil yourself with the blissful relaxing experience of Punta Ala’s beautiful beach. Revel in its pellucid water and soft golden sands, as you bask in the pure sensation of paradise, where the murmur of the waves becomes the soundtrack to a tranquil state of mind. It’s an invitation to savour every sublime moment, in a seaside getaway that will leave you with lasting memories of perfect peace.

## Beauty center

The beauty center in La Spiaggia Beach Club is your haven of relaxation, an intimate oasis for the senses. Rediscover your inner harmony and youthful beauty on a rejuvenating experience for both body and mind. Let our qualified professionals take care of you in this tranquil natural setting.

## Bike tours

Maremma is a cyclist’s paradise with a wide variety of quiet roads to explore on the flat, in the hills or on more challenging terrain. Whether you’re a keen beginner, a serious rider or a family on a quest for adventure, you’ll find an array of routes to suit your abilities and needs, aided by our specialist cycle-tourism guides.

## Golf in Tuscany

Unwind in fine style with a round of golf on a renowned international course right by the stunning Tuscan coast.

Discover more

## Fitness area

If you like to stay in shape even on holiday, the Cala Beach Resort’s nearby gym at La Vela beach is an opportunity not to miss. Featuring all the most innovative equipment, the facility offers free entry to resort guests. It opens daily from 9 am to 7 pm, so you can plan your training when you wish during your stay.

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your electronic device (PC, tablet, smartphone, etc.) while you browse a website and that provide certain information. Cookies act as a memory for a website, allowing websites to recognize your device each time you visit that same site. Cookies also allow your browsing preferences to be stored, offering you a more functional experience on the websites and making the content as personalized as possible.

Web browsers allow you to exercise some control over cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies or block cookies from specific websites.
