Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre
Luxury 5-star hotel in DIFC, Dubai
One of the city's standout Dubai luxury hotel experiences, The Ritz-Carlton is set within the prestigious Dubai International Financial Centre. Rising with a 14-story limestone facade, this refined retreat places business hubs, fine dining, culture, and nightlife within easy reach. A direct walkway leads to The Gate, while the Museum of the Future, Dubai Mall, Burj Khalifa, and World Trade Centre are minutes away. With two metro stations nearby and climate-controlled air conditioning in all rooms, our hotel in DIFC, Dubai, offers a polished stay for business travelers and luxury leisure guests alike. Guests praise the prime location, making it a compelling choice among 5-star hotels in Dubai.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre is Dubai International Airport (DXB). DXB is located approximately 15.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre have an airport shuttle to Dubai International Airport (DXB)?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre has an airport shuttle to Dubai International Airport (DXB).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai International Financial Centre has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
