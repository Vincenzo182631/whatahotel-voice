Experiences

Our Dubai hotel near DIFC places you in the heart of the city's attractions, offering convenient access to both downtown and the airport, just 20 minutes away. Whether you're interested in historical sites, cultural experiences, or relaxing at a world-class Dubai spa, our team will tailor your stay to match your unique tastes.

Arrival to Spa
When should I arrive?
For enhanced comfort and relaxation, we recommend arriving 15-30 minutes before your scheduled treatment.
What about my jewelry and valuables?
Lockers are available for personal items. If you are a hotel guest, it is advisable to leave valuable items in the safe in your guest room.
Should I shave before a treatment?
For gentlemen, shaving before a facial is recommended but not necessary. If you choose to shave, please do so at least two hours before your appointment. Shaving is not recommended before body treatments or hair removal services.
What should I wear during my treatment?
During all treatments, the body is fully draped, except for the area being worked on. Robes and slippers are provided. All treatments can be enjoyed by both male & female guests, but the gender of your therapist will always be that of the guest.
What about my comfort?
To maintain a tranquil environment, please switch off all cell phones and pagers. Your spa experience is meant to be fully enjoyed, so if you experience any discomfort regarding room temperature, massage pressure, music volume, or other conditions, please inform your therapist immediately.
Spa Policies
Is there a minimum age for treatments?
The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Dubai International Financial Centre services and facilities are for guests 18 years of age or older.
What if I am late for my appointment?
To enhance your spa experience, please arrive 30 minutes before your scheduled treatment to relax in the sauna and steam rooms. Arriving late will shorten your treatment time, affecting its effectiveness and your enjoyment. Treatments will end on time to avoid delaying the next guest, and the full value of your treatment will be charged.
What is the cancellation policy?
To avoid being charged the full-service fee, please notify the spa at least 12 hours in advance if you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment.
What about gratuities?
Gratuities are distributed to the spa staff who served you during your visit. Additional gratuities may be given at your discretion.
What about payment for spa services?
We accept all major credit cards. You may also charge services to your hotel bill. Prices are quoted in UAE dirhams and include a 10% service charge and 10% Municipality fees.

Spa Services
Who can guide me in choosing my treatments?
Spa services should be scheduled in advance. Our spa reservation agents are available to assist you in planning your spa experience, including recommending the best order of treatments.
Are Gift Certificates available from The Ritz-Carlton Spa?
Spa gift vouchers may be purchased and used towards spa services or product purchases.
Health
What if I have special health considerations?
When making a reservation please notify the agent of any medical conditions, allergies, physical ailments or disabilities, and if you are pregnant.
What if I am pregnant?
There are a few services that should be avoided during pregnancy, but many can be enjoyed.

Art Galleries
Attractions
The Dubai International Financial Center is home to numerous art galleries, showcasing both local and international art. The galleries showcase works by Middle Eastern and international artists, and frequently host events to present new works. Photography, painting, sketches, sculpture, video creations and a host of other art farms are represented by artists from every continent. Art Dubai, an art fair that occurs each March, features offerings from more than 75 galleries from all over the world.
