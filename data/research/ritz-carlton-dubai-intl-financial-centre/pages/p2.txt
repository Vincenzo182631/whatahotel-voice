The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge
The Club Lounge, located on Level 13, is an inviting and well-appointed space accessible exclusively to our Club Level guests. Often described as a "hotel within a hotel," with exclusive amenities and continuous culinary offerings, including breakfast, light lunch, hors d'oeuvres, and a variety of beverages and sweets. With a dedicated concierge at your service, it is the ideal place to conduct business, plan a family outing, or simply unwind. Please note that access to the Club Lounge is limited to two registered guests per room. Club Level guests may invite additional visitors, subject to availability, at a supplementary charge.

Local Tastes for Club Lounge Guests
Camels are a symbol of Emirati heritage and remain intrinsic to the UAE’s traditions and cultural practices. Camel's milk has supported Bedouin, nomad and pastoral cultures since the domestication of camels over 1000 years ago. Herders may for periods survive solely on the milk when taking the camels on long distances to graze in desert and arid environments. Emiratis like and drink camel milk as it is highly nutritious and rich in Vitamin C.
Guests at Club Level can enjoy an array of camel milk smoothies which include berry, mango, avocado and banana flavours accompanied by a selection of date delicacies from cakes to energy bars and pastries. There are also three types of dates available daily in the Club Lounge; khodri, madjool and sagil.
