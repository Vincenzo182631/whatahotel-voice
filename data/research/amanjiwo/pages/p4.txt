**Cultural Trails in Java**\\
\\
Overlooking the world’s largest Buddhist sanctuary and surrounded by rainforest, hills and rice paddies, Amanjiwo occupies a special place in the rural heartland of Central Java.

**Journey through Java by Train**\\
\\
After a fascinating seven-hour train journey in consummate comfort, you will arrive at Amanjiwo, named ‘peaceful soul’ for the serenity, privacy and beauty of its setting. The resort overlooks the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Borobudur, one of Buddhism’s most sacred Mahayana temples. Perfectly placed for exploring the region’s historic, artistic and cultural highlights, or for simply relaxing in blissful surroundings, the resort lies at the foot of the Menoreh Hills, cascading gracefully from a lofty rotunda down the hillside to a magnificent swimming pool.

**Discover Indonesia**\\
\\
The Indonesian islands are known as tropical idylls, but their individual Indonesia is the world’s largest archipelago with thousands of islands reinterpreting the tropical idyll with their individual landscapes and extraordinarily diverse heritage. Combine stays at any of Aman’s four Indonesian resorts and choose from exclusive experiences at each.

**Ancient Temples**\\
\\
Borobudur reveals its timeless beauty as the air carries the sound of birdsong and the mist slowly lifts, unveiling the surrounding volcanoes and terraced fields. Sunlight touches the stone reliefs and the many faces of Buddha, bringing them to life. Temple guides will bring to life the knowledge contained within the reliefs.
