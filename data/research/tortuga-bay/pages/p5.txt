## Eco

#### Ojos Indigenas Ecological Reserve

The Ojos Indígenas Ecological Reserve was generously donated by Grupo Puntacana to the Fundación Puntacana for the express purpose of serving as a private nature reserve. Encompassing a vast 1,500 acres of land, the reserve is a sanctuary for wildlife and a center for educational, scientific, and recreational activities. It is characterized by a transitional subtropical forest, harboring a rich variety of flora and fauna from humid and dry habitats. The reserve is home to over 500 species of plants, with an impressive 36% being endemic to the Dominican Republic, making it an incredibly significant ecological site. Additionally, our guests enjoy included access to the Ojos Indígenas Reserve, allowing them to explore its natural beauty and immerse themselves in its serene surroundings.

Named Ojos Indígenas in honor of the Taino Indians, the island's original inhabitants, the reserve is distinguished by its 12 freshwater lagoons. Due to unique geological features, these lagoons were formed by the emergence of Yauya, an underground river. Each lagoon is thoughtfully given a Taino name, adding a cultural and historical dimension to this pristine natural environment.

#### Los Ojos Eco Journey

Through a guided tour, experience the wonders of nature and gain insight into the renowned ecological projects of the Puntacana Foundation. Join an expert and discover the efforts being made to protect and restore the unique species of the Dominican Republic and develop innovative sustainable projects.

The tour requires a prior reservation and includes visits to the Apiary, YAUYA store, and Ojos Indígenas Ecological Reserve (Inriri Lagoon). It also includes a chance to see the endemic Rhinoceros Iguana and Ridway Hawks, with detailed explanations.

#### Bird Watching Tour

Take a guided tour through the stunning Punta Cana region and immerse yourself in the beauty of its endemic and native bird species. Follow the picturesque Sparrowhawk Trail, which leads to our Bird Observation Tower. There, you'll be able to witness migratory birds and the endemic Ridgway Hawk thriving in their natural habitat.

#### Marine Innovation Center Tour

Explore the Marine Innovation Center on a guided tour and get an up-close look at the innovative projects to restore and conserve Punta Cana's coastal ecosystems. Marvel at the coral restoration efforts, visit the ornamental fish nursery, and gain insights into the center's diverse conservation and research initiatives.

#### Puntacana Equestrian Center

Our location at the Puntacana Equestrian Center provides horse enthusiasts with various amenities. We offer 20 secure horse stalls, two versatile stable boxes in two covered barns, 21 outdoor enclosures, two paddocks, two riding arenas, and a round pen. In addition, we have a 3,000-square-meter garden shaded by trees, providing an ideal setting for outdoor activities. The garden is equipped with picnic tables and benches for the convenience of our visitors. Our center also offers a variety of classes suitable for riders of all levels, horseback rides for enthusiasts, and pony rides tailored explicitly for children.

#### Petting Zoo

Immerse yourself in a delightful and sustainable environment at the Petting Zoo near the renowned Puntacana Equestrian Center. Interact with a variety of friendly farm animals and treat your little ones to a fun-filled children's playground. This is the perfect setting for a memorable family outing where kids and adults can enjoy the beauty of nature and the joy of animal interaction.

#### Puntacana Polo Club

Improve your polo skills in the tranquil and breathtaking setting of the Puntacana Polo Club. Our club allows you to immerse yourself in polo, offering horseback riding and hands-on interaction with polo horses. With guidance from experienced instructors, you can refine your skills and knowledge of the sport. Our flexible options cater to your schedule, whether you prefer one-hour lessons or personalized courses and packages. The club boasts top-of-the-line facilities, including expansive fields for practice and play, as well as architecturally stunning open stables that add to the overall charm of the experience.

## Tennis

Our world-class tennis center offers an array of outstanding amenities, including a tennis pro shop, a Snack Bar, ten meticulously maintained tennis courts, four paddle courts, two pickleball courts, and a fully equipped Technogym for all your fitness needs. ## Padel

Our padel courts were opened in 2022. This is an amazing racquet sport that combines Tennis and Squash. It is great for all ages, since its easy to learn making it accessible to all players. Padel continues to grow globally, suggesting a bright future for this dynamic and engaging sport.

## Pickleball

Pickleball is a paddle sport that blends elements of tennis, badminton, and ping pong. It is played on a badminton-sized court with a modified tennis net. Our facilities include various outdoor courts. This sport is easy to learn, making it accessible for beginners, yet challenging enough to keep experienced players engaged.
