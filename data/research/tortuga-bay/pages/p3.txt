# Dining

Puntacana Resort boasts three, each under the guidance of world-class chefs. Additionally, guests can indulge in a wide range of in-room dining choices available 24 hours a day. The resort prides itself on offering diverse cuisines and atmospheres, with eight innovative restaurants to choose from.

## Bamboo Restaurant

Visit the Bamboo Restaurant on the resort premises for a memorable dining experience. The unique menu combines locally sourced food with Mediterranean influences to offer a variety of innovative fusion dishes in a cozy, graceful setting designed by Oscar de la Renta. For breakfast, we offer a la carte options.

Reservations are recommended

Monday through Sunday \| 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM

Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner

Dress Code: Resort casual

Menu Reservation

## Tortuga Bay Lounge

- Private in room Dining

Nestled beside the Bamboo restaurant, Tortuga Bay Lounge welcomes guests into an intimate setting where they can savor various Dominican delights. The lounge is renowned for its hand-rolled cigars and an impressive selection of fine spirits, perfect for leisurely sipping and enjoying the ambiance.

## La Yola Restaurant

Immerse yourself in the exquisite flavors of Mediterranean cuisine and seafood at the La Yola Restaurant. The restaurant's elegant setting and breathtaking views of the glistening turquoise waters provide the perfect backdrop for savoring the freshest catch. La Yola is designed to resemble a fishing boat, which is nestled within the picturesque Puntacana Resort Marina, offering a unique and unforgettable dining experience.

Hours Monday through Sunday \| 6:30 p.m. - 10:30 p.m

Dress Code: Resort casual
