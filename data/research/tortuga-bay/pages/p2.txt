This exclusive enclave was meticulously crafted by the globally acclaimed Oscar de la Renta. Drawing inspiration from his Dominican roots, he infused each villa with a captivating blend of simplicity and sophistication. Reflecting the area's diverse indigenous culture, the surroundings offer guests an immersive experience of the dynamic natural beauty of the Dominican Republic

Tortuga Bay offers elegantly furnished rooms decorated with locally sourced materials, featuring spacious interiors and balconies with magnificent views of La Cana Golf Course and the white sand beaches. Each room includes a comfortable kitchenette, luxurious coralline-stoned bathrooms, and an oak wood walk-in closet. Guests are provided with electrical golf carts and bicycles for convenient, eco-friendly travel around the resort. The hotel also features a pool, Jacuzzi, and beach exclusively for Tortuga Bay guests. Every room is assigned an Embajador to ensure guests can rest and rejuvenate while spending time in paradise

The luxurious four-bedroom villa in the stunning Dominican Republic is perfect for accommodating larger families or groups visiting the beautiful Tortuga Bay.
