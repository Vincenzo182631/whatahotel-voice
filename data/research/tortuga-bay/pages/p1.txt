# Tortuga Bay Hotel at Puntacana Resort

### Weather today  280

## Start your next unforgettable experience

Indulge in the epitome of Caribbean luxury at Tortuga Bay, an opulent resort nestled in the Dominican Republic and honored with the prestigious AAA Five Diamond Award. This exclusive property promises a haven of tranquility and unparalleled leisure. Tortuga Bay is also the only hotel designed by the legendary Oscar de la Renta, blending his signature elegance with the natural beauty of the Caribbean. Upon your arrival in Punta Cana, your dedicated personal Embajador will ensure that every aspect of your stay is meticulously attended to, leaving you free to relax and unwind in absolute comfort

## Where sunrise and sunset knock on your window

Tortuga Bay is situated in a private enclave within the resort and offers 13 luxurious villas. It provides a sustainable luxury experience, privacy, and unparalleled service. We are the only LHW (Leading Hotels of the World) property in the country, a distinction that underscores our commitment to exceptional quality and world-class hospitality. You can select from a variety of accommodations: 1-bedroom Junior Suites, 2-bedroom Suites, or 3 or 4-bedroom Villas. All of them are located along pristine white sand beaches, with every convenience within reach.

## Enjoy distinctive and exceptional fine cuisine

Puntacana Resort boasts three AAA Diamond Award-winning restaurants, each under the guidance of world-class chefs.

Additionally, guests can indulge in a wide range of in-room

dining choices available 24 hours a day. The resort prides itself on offering diverse cuisines and atmospheres, with eight innovative restaurants to choose from

## Make the most of your time by enjoying our amenities to the fullest

Nestled within a 1,500-acre ecological reserve, Tortuga Bay offers a serene escape with miles of private beaches and a perfect blend of simplicity and elegance. Each of the 30

rooms is thoughtfully designed, featuring kitchenettes equipped with microwaves, complimentary wifi, and flat-screen TVs for your comfort and convenience

## Experience all Tortuga Bay has to offer

Tortuga Bay Puntacana is a luxurious destination that caters to guests of all ages. Our spa offers a wide range of treatments delivered by skilled therapists, ensuring a rejuvenating and relaxing experience. Guests can enjoy the breathtaking views while golfing on our meticulously maintained courses. For those seeking friendly competition, our top-notch tennis, pickleball, and paddle courts are the perfect places to engage in lively games. The pristine white sand beaches provide the ideal backdrop for various water sports, allowing guests to immerse themselves in the beauty of the surroundings thoroughly. With countless adventures awaiting, a stay at Tortuga Bay Puntacana promises to be an unforgettable experience. For more information, we encourage you to contact your dedicated Embajador

## Unwind, Recharge and Rejuvenate

Relax and enjoy one of the ultimate wellness experiences at The Spa. Located in La Cana Club, the spa offers a variety of treatments, holistic techniques, and yoga classes where guests can refresh their bodies and minds. Maintain your routine with us at our fitness center, with personalized classes available upon request
