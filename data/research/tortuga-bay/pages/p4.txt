## Silvia Tcherassi Boutique

Situated amidst the luxurious villas of Tortuga Bay, the 1,000-square-foot store proudly
displays the exquisite creations of internationally acclaimed designer Silvia Tcherassi. The
store's architectural design draws inspiration from George Washington’s seed house at Mount
Vernon. It is crafted using the same pale coral rock as the other buildings on the premises,
creating a harmonious and elegant aesthetic that complements the surrounding environment

## Effortless VIP Airport Service

Upon your arrival at Punta Cana Airport, our dedicated VIP airport service will welcome you. You
will be personally escorted through the customs process to make it as smooth as possible. Once
through customs, you will be led to a private car pre-arranged for your convenience. You can
take in the area's beauty as you embark on a short drive through the scenic and vibrant
Dominican surroundings. Upon reaching Tortuga Bay, your personal Embajador will be waiting to
greet you and ensure that every aspect of your stay is tailored to your needs, providing
exceptional care and attention to detail throughout your time with us.
