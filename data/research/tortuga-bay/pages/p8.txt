## Do you accept kids at Tortuga Bay Punta Cana hotel ?

Children are welcome.

Children up to 4 years old can stay with their parents for free.

Adult rate applies from 5 years old.

Our prices for adults and kids vary based on seasonality. Please request a price simulation to see the best price available for your dates.

We don't offer a kids club.

We are sorry, but we don't have a fun zone.

Children under the age of 18 must always be accompanied by their family or legal guardians.

Please email us for more details: reservationstb@puntacana.com.
