# LANDMARK BEGINNINGS. ENDURING DESIGN.

The New York EDITION, originally constructed in 1909 as the Metropolitan Life Insurance Company Headquarters, continually shapes Manhattan’s skyline as a timeless Madison Avenue landmark. The only true luxury lifestyle hotel in the Flatiron neighborhood, the property is home to specialty suites and boutique accommodation, a tranquil hotel spa, chic event spaces, and a fine dining restaurant, all elegantly designed by Ian Schrager. The clocktower, featuring 360-degree views of New York City, is positioned steps from Madison Square Park and a short walk to Madison Square Garden, Midtown, and more.

## Hotel As Private Home

The design hotel’s 271 unique guest rooms and suites feature oak floors and dark oak wood-paneled foyers that create the atmosphere of a cozy, private residence. Expansive windows frame city views, ranging from an intimate view of Madison Square Park, to the world-famous Empire State Building, to the breathtaking skyline of New York City.
