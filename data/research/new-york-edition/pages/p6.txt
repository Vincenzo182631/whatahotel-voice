# Lobby Bar

## Lobby Bar

Expansive yet intimate, with oversized floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Madison Square Park, the Lobby Bars subtle play of modern and period design embodies The New York EDITION’s casual elegance. Featuring bespoke seasonal cocktails and light bites, the Flatiron District Bar is a new kind of gathering place for guests and NYC locals alike.
