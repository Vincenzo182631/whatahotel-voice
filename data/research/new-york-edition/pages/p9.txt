# Services & Amenities

The New York EDITION creates a unique experience paired with an elevated level of personalized service.

### For further information

Phone +1 212 413 4200 – Email: info.nyc@editionhotels.com

Guest Services

- 271 unique hotel rooms and suites
- Three 1,350 square-foot Penthouses
- Two 950 square-foot One Bedroom Flatiron Suites
- 4 Green Key Certified Property
- A collection of specialty suites
- Customized banquet menus for private events, luncheons, dinner parties and upscale business meetings
- Room Service
- Concierge Services
- Spa Services at urban spa used exclusively for hotel guests
- Complimentary use of the 24-hour fitness facilities with Peloton stationary exercise bikes
- Complimentary high-speed wireless internet access throughout the hotel
- State-of-the-art audio and visual services available for private functions
- Multi-lingual staff

Preorder Amenities

- Should you wish to further enhance your visit, amenities are available for purchase: The New York EDITION

Executive Services

- Executive Boardroom, Meeting Room and Private Event Space
- Video Conferencing
- Executive car services available
- Executive butler services available
- State-of-the-art audio/visual equipment
- Complimentary high-speed wireless Internet access throughout the hotel
- Smart boards and electronic flip charts available
- iPads, laptops and cell phones available
- 24-hour multi-lingual secretarial staff available
- Built-in junket floors to accommodate press junkets, television and movie filming

Pets

- The New York EDITION welcomes your pets to NYC.
- Accompanying pets will be subject to a $250 fee per stay.
