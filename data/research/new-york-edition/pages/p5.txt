# The Bar at The Clocktower

## The Bar at The Clocktower

Step into casual elegance at The Bar. Attached to The Clocktower restaurant, the NYC bar welcomes patrons with craft cocktails and an opulent decor, from its fiberglass globe chandelier to the 24k gold leaf bar and carved plaster ceilings. The intimate atmosphere and tempting libations combine to create the perfect place for pre- or post-event drinks just outside of Madison Square Park.
