# Getting Here

- From Newark Liberty Airport
- From Grand Central Station
- From PENN Station
- From JFK International Airport
- From Laguardia Airport

## From Newark Liberty Airport

## PRIVATE CAR

A private car to/from Newark Liberty will cost from approximately $195 each way inclusive of tax and gratuity and will take approximately one hour of travel time. A private car can be arranged by the hotel by calling +1 212 413 4200 prior to your arrival/departure.

## TAXI

Taxis are available at the airport on a first-come, first-served basis. This will cost you approximately $75 each way and will take approximately one hour of travel time.  Tips and tolls may be additional.

## TRAIN

If you do not have a lot of luggage, public transportation is available to and from the hotel and can take up to 1.5 hours of total travel time.

**From** **Newark Liberty:** Take the Air Train and connect to Newark Airport Railroad Station and take NJ Transit to Penn Station.  Walk to Herald Square  and take the R train downtown to 23rd St. via NYC Subway.  Approximate cost is $15.

**To Newark Liberty:** Walk along Madison Square Park to 23rd St. and take the uptown R train to Herald Square.  Walk to Penn Station and take the NJ Transit to Newark Airport Railroad Station and transfer to the Air Train.

## DRIVING

Follow signs as you exit airport to I-78 E. Head east I-78 using Holland Tunnel to enter Manhattan.  Take exit 3 towards Brooklyn.  Merge onto Ericsson Place and then continue straight onto Beach Street/ West Broadway.  Make a slight left onto 6th Avenue (Avenue of The Americas) and after 1.7 miles, make a right onto West 23rd Street.  After .5 Miles, turn left onto Madison Avenue and the hotel will be immediately on the right.  Hotel entrance is on 24th street.

## PARKING

Guests have the option either to self-park or valet. Our preferred parking garage is SP+ Parking at 120 W 21st Street (located on West 21st Street between 6th & 7th Avenues). Valet is offered at a nightly rate of $85 (including tax) for standard size vehicles and $95 (including tax) for oversize vehicles / SUVs. Rates apply to a 24-hour period with a one-hour grace period on the date of departure and do not include in-and-out privileges. Due to high volume, please allow 1.5 hours from time of request for car retrieval during peak arrival and departure times.

## TRAIN

If you do not have a lot of luggage, public transportation is available to and from the hotel and can take up to 1.5 hours of total travel time.

**From JFK:** take the Air Train to Jamaica Station/Sutphin Boulevard.  Take the E train via NYC subway toward Manhattan to Lexington Avenue/53rd St.  Walk to the downtown 6 train at 51st St. and take it to 23rd St. Total cost approximately $7.75

**To JFK:**  take the uptown 6 train from 23rd St. to 51st St. and walk to the Lexington/53rd St. Queens bound E train to Jamaica Station/Sutphin Boulevard and take the Air Train.

## DRIVING

Follow signs as you exit the airport to I-678 S. Head south on I-678 and take exit B to 130th Place. Merge onto 130th Place and continue straight onto Pan Am Avenue. Turn right onto Lefferts Boulevard and then left onto N Conduit Avenue. After approximately 1 mile, take the Cross Bay Boulevard Ramp and take a right onto Cross Bay Boulevard. After .8 mile, continue onto Woodhaven Boulevard and merge onto I-495 W using the midtown Tunnel to enter into Manhattan. Take the exit on the left side to E 35th Street and then turn left at the second cross street to Lexington Avenue. After .5 mile, turn right onto E 23rd Street and then a right onto Madison Avenue where the hotel will be on left, across from Madison Square Park. Turn right onto 24th Street to the hotel entrance.
