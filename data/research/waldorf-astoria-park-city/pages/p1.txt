## Slopeside Eleganceat Park City Mountain

Slopeside Elegance at Park City Mountain

Escape to a refined retreat in Canyons Village, where scenic trails give way to ski-in, gondola-out access to Park City Mountain come winter. Unwind in spacious suites and residences, complemented by a year-round heated pool and hot tubs, inspired alpine dining, restorative spa rituals, and Waldorf Astorias’

legendary service.

## Rooms, Suites, and Residences

Each accommodation is anchored by its own fireplace. Many include furnished balconies, soaking tubs, and full kitchens. Bi-level residences feature an upstairs primary bedroom overlooking a generous living room framed by mountain views.
