PRACTICE FACILITY & MAINTENANCE NOTICE

Known as a bucket list golf destination, the world-renowned Trump National Doral Miami Golf Resort is a golfer's haven. First opening our doors in 1962, Doral was fashioned with golf at its core. And today, more so than ever, guests will find that it is one of Florida's pre-eminent luxury golf resorts. Our Florida golf resort is home to four uniquely designed courses, including one of golf's most iconic courses, The Blue Monster.

With the most recent course transformations of The Red Tiger, The Golden Palm, and The Silver Fox, Trump National Doral Miami sets itself apart by offering distinct and exciting Doral golf experiences at one property. Rounding out the resort's golf offerings are the state-of-the-art LED practice facility and The Trump Golf Academy.

Learn more about our golf membership opportunities and golf offers and packages.

Enhance your group golf experience at Trump National Doral Miami with a compelling golf tournament or moonlight golf. For groups of 12 or more, please contact our golf sales team at doral.golf@trumphotels.com.

For players seeking variety, challenge, and prestige, Trump National Doral is home to some of the most respected Miami golf courses, offering four distinctive layouts that cater to every level of play. Each course delivers its own character and strategy, creating an experience that rewards both first-time visitors and seasoned golfers returning year after year.

As a premier Miami golf club, Trump National Doral also offers an elevated community for members and guests alike, with access to championship play, expert instruction, and exceptional practice facilities. Every experience reflects the resort’s legacy, combining championship play, expert instruction, and an uncompromising commitment to excellence.

**Trump Golf Academy After School Program**

Give your child the opportunity to learn golf in a structured, supportive, and fun environment.  Our after-school junior programs are designed for beginner and intermediate players and focus on fundamentals, confidence, and on-course readiness.

Our PGA and USKids certified coaches are dedicated to helping each student progress at their own pace while enjoying a world-class learning experience.
