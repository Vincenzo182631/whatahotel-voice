MIAMI SPA MONTHS MENU

Indulge your senses at our luxurious Miami spa resort, where a uniquely customized level of service ensures every moment aligns with your intentions. Step into a haven of relaxation, enveloped by careful sensory stimulation.

The Trump Spa offers world-class treatments and massages, thoughtfully designed for women, men, and couples, guiding you toward inner health and external beauty.

For a truly immersive experience, extend your escape with an overnight stay at The Trump Spa Suites.

**Spa Arrival and Parking Information**

The Spa at Trump® is conveniently located at 8755 NW 36th Street. The spa can be easily accessed from the Spa Parking Lot within the Pritikin Longevity Center.

We kindly recommended your arrival 30-minutes before your appointment time.

* * *

**Special perk for our local Miamians**

Guests booking spa services of 50 minutes or more (excluding salon services) are invited to enjoy complimentary parking at the spa on the day of their appointment, as well as access to the resort’s fitness center, group fitness classes, and the Royal Palm Pool.

**Hours:** 9:00am - 7:00pm, daily
