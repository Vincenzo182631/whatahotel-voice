ENVIRONMENT

At Trump National Doral, we invest in your wellness – mind, body and soul. Following a multi-million dollar renovation, we invite you to experience unrivaled relaxation at the completely re-imagined Trump Spa, and enhance your journey in the all-new state-of-the-art fitness center. Discover our daily group fitness classes – from the calming Sunrise Stretch to the intense cardio Spinning class – instructed by certified fitness coaches dedicated to your health and overall wellness.

**Fitness Center**

Our Fitness Center is located in the center of it all - the Resort Clubhouse. Step inside an inspired setting with panoramic views of the lush resort landscape and famed Blue Monster course. Set your own pace and pursue your wellness goals with all new Life Fitness equipment.

**Personal Training**

Schedule a consultation with one of our results-oriented fitness coaches. Get more out of your regular exercise routine with a program tailored to your unique body strength and health goals.

**Group Fitness Classes**

Resort and Spa guests are invited to partake in our complimentary array of fitness classes taught by certified fitness instructors. Engage in fun, inventive work out routines - like Golf Yoga and Warrior Ride - alongside friends, family and those working toward a common goal.

**Hours:** 6:00am - 7:00pm, daily
