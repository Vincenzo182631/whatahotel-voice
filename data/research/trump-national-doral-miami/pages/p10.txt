# **GOLF COURSES**

## GOLF COURSES IN DORAL, MIAMI

_UNIQUELY_ DESIGNED

GOLF COURSES

This golf resort is home to four uniquely designed Doral golf courses, including one of golf's most famous - The Blue Monster. With the most recent course transformations of the Red Tiger, Golden Palm and Silver Fox, Trump National Doral sets itself apart from all others by offering an array of distinct and exciting Doral golf experiences all at one location.

All golf course rates include golf cart and exclude taxes, caddie fees (if applicable) and gratuity.

**GOLF COURSE AND PRACTICE FACILITY UPDATE**

As part of our continued commitment to delivering exceptional playing conditions, select golf courses and practice areas are undergoing agronomic enhancements.

**Golden Palm Reopening:** The Golden Palm is open for 17-hole play while renovations to select greens continue. Temporary greens are located at the end of the fairways on holes 2 and 18. Hole 12 remains closed, with signage directing players to the next hole. Tee markers will be moved forward as renovations progress.

**Additional Course and Practice Updates**

- Driving Range: Remains closed
- Warm-Up Area: Temporary hitting nets are available for pre-round warm-up only

Guests seeking additional practice opportunities are invited to experience the Trump Golf Academy, featuring state-of-the-art technology and professional instruction.

We sincerely appreciate your understanding as our agronomy team completes these important enhancements to the golf experience at Trump National Doral Miami.
