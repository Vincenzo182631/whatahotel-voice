## HOTEL SUITES & HOTEL ROOMS IN MIAMI, FLORIDA

LUXURIOUSLY _APPOINTED_.

EXQUISITELY DECORATED.

Impeccably designed and exquisitely appointed, our hotel rooms in Miami, Florida provide a comfortable setting to enjoy the resort’s landscape from your private balcony or terrace. One of the largest hotels near Port Miami, we have 643 total guest rooms, 14 deluxe suites, 27 premier suites, 2 presidential suites, and 48 spa suites from which you can unwind. Designed as intimate villa-like lodges, our Miami luxury rooms and hotel suites are just steps from our main clubhouse, championship golf courses, signature restaurants, and Royal Palm Pool oasis and cabanas.

Trump National Doral Miami is a smoke-free resort. A $500 fee per day will apply if smoking occurs in the guest room. We welcome guests of all ages. Please note that guests must be 21 or older to check into a room on their own.
