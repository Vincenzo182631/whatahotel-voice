# **BLT PRIME**

## PREMIER DORAL RESTAURANT

THE HIGHEST

STANDARDS IN CUISINE,

_QUALITY_ AND SERVICE

+1 305 591 6606

RESERVE A TABLE

BLT Prime is a contemporary Miami steakhouse that showcases classic steakhouse fare with inspiring ingredients and modern accents. The menu, organized by category, offers an impressive selection of the highest-quality dry-aged steaks complemented by savory sides. Guests will start their meal with BLT’s signature warm popovers before indulging in menu items including our Wagyu Ribeye or 45-day salt-aged Prime-Grade Porterhouse Steak for two. In addition to steak, the BLT Prime menu will offer a variety of fresh and notable seafood dishes, from our classic Tuna Tartare appetizer and an impressive raw bar assortment to Pan Seared Halibut with Miso Beurre Blanc sauce and Dover Sole with Soy Brown Butter sauce and Capers. The menu perfectly pairs with a craft cocktail, a local beer, or a selection from our world-class wine list featuring both Old and New World Wines.
