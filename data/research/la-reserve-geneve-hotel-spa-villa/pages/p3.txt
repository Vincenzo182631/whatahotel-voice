  - La Réserve Genève

    ## La Réserve Genève

    La Réserve Genève – Hotel and Spa is an extraordinary haven where you can meet with your loved ones and enjoy the rare privilege of experiencing unforgettable moments. Everyone at their own pace, according to their individual desires. Between the lake and the mountains, nothing matters except the pleasure of simply living your best life, without having to organize or plan anything, as everything is already on hand: four restaurants, the Spa Nescens, outdoor and indoor swimming pools, Kid’s Club, sports facilities, a range of à la carte activities…

- Directions

Directions

  - Motoscafo

    ## Motoscafo

    Experience the unique charm of La Réserve Genève by arriving via our Motoscafo. Glide over the waters of Lake Geneva and take in the spectacular views, an elegant and memorable way to start or finish your stay.

  - Geneva airport

    ## Geneva airport

    Just minutes away from La Réserve Genève, Geneva International Airport is the ideal starting point for travelers. Avail of easy transfers and shuttle services on request for a hassle-free travel experience.

  - Geneva – Cornavin train station

    ## Geneva – Cornavin train station

    Cornavin Station, located in the heart of Geneva, provides quick and direct access to La Réserve Genève. Enjoy a comfortable and scenic journey, with frequent connections for an effortless arrival.

  - City Center

    ## City Center

    Discover the vibrant city center of Geneva, then return to the serenity of La Réserve Genève. With convenient transport links, the city center is within easy reach, perfectly blending urban excitement with the tranquility of our establishment.

- Cities and ski resorts

Cities and ski resorts

  - Courchevel

    ## Courchevel

    Courchevel, a luxury haven in the Alps, offers a unique experience only two hours from La Réserve Genève. Renowned for its prestigious slopes and vibrant nightlife, Courchevel is perfect for those seeking a blend of adventure and sophistication.

  - Chamonix

    ## Chamonix

    Chamonix, in the heart of the Alps, is an adventure destination near La Réserve Genève. Famous for its Mont Blanc views and year-round mountain activities, Chamonix appeals to thrill-seekers and nature lovers.

  - Montreux

    ## Montreux

    Discover Montreux, famous for its jazz festival and stunning Alpine views. Stroll along its flower-lined promenade and visit Chillon Castle, for a stay that blends culture with relaxation.

  - Evian

    ## Evian

    Evian, known for its thermal springs and bottled water, offers a wellness experience with its spas, gardens, and lakeside walks.

  - Lausanne

    ## Lausanne

    Lausanne, a vibrant city with a rich cultural and Olympic heritage, is ideal for an invigorating urban getaway, combining history with stunning lake views.

  - Interlaken

    ## Interlaken

    Interlaken, nestled between two lakes and surrounded by mountains, is a paradise for nature and adventure lovers, offering hiking, paragliding, and boat tours.

  - Crans Montana Ski resort

    ## Crans Montana Ski resort

    Crans Montana, featuring ski slopes, mountain panoramas, and luxurious amenities, offers an unmatched skiing experience, combining adventure with elegance.

  - Zermatt Ski resort

    ## Zermatt Ski resort

    Zermatt, at the base of the majestic Matterhorn, provides a unique alpine experience with its extensive ski areas and car-free village, for a memorable winter escape.

  - Megeve

    ## Megeve

    Escape to Megève, a jewel in the Alps close to La Réserve Genève. Known for its chic ambiance and exclusive boutiques, Megève offers a refined skiing experience. After the urban elegance of La Réserve, Megève will enchant you with its picturesque charm and authentic alpine allure.
