Make the most of your stay and explore all the happenings at our Rancho Mirage resort with a pool​ in California and the Greater Palm Springs area.

### Play Well

Play well, even when you’re on the go. Our Rancho Mirage hotel with waterslides, pickleball and tennis courts, lap pools, and an on-site championship golf course makes movement and fun come naturally. At The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa, every moment keeps you energized on the road.

## Pickleball and Tennis

The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa is your pickleball and tennis paradise. Grab your friends and family and find your sweet spot on our 12 fresh new pickleball courts. Or enjoy three lighted tennis courts, clinics, and workshops with an on-staff professional. The tennis program is available for players of all levels.

Reserve Your Court

## Waterslide at Las Brisas

Our Rancho Mirage hotel with waterslides features dual 125-foot waterslides at Las Brisas pool, inviting guests of all ages to enjoy a day of play. Take in resort views from the top before winding through twists and turns that end in a refreshing splash.

### Las Brisas Pool

Perfect for families, Las Brisas is our largest pool and is located behind the main building. This pool features dual 125-foot waterslides, cabana rentals, two bubbling spas, and pool attendants at your service throughout the day.

### Las Hadas Pool

Las Hadas is our Adult (18+) pool, surrounded by buildings 9 - 13. This oasis features one bubbling spa and an on-site bar where guests can order small bites and cocktails to enjoy poolside.

### La Paloma Pool

La Paloma Pool is located between buildings 3, 4, and 5 and offers a tranquil setting to relax and unwind. Small bites and bar service are available for guests lounging at La Paloma Pool.

## Westin Rancho Mirage Wellness Spa

Beyond a destination for individual renewal, The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort is the ideal spa retreat getaway for girls’ weekends, bridal parties, couples and group events. Treatments at our Rancho Mirage spa resort​ near Palm Springs include therapeutic massage, renewing facials, polishing scrubs, nurturing body wraps, manicure, pedicure and salon services for hair.

Book Spa Services

Signature Treatments

Lava Shell

A deeply relaxing massage using the natural benefits of warm shells from the tropics. The smooth shells are expertly glided over the body with varying pressure to meet your particular needs, creating a synergy of warmth and relaxation.

Lavender Lemon Hydrating Body Wrap

Cleanse and exfoliates using our lavender lemon body polish, followed by a luscious hydration body mask, and concluded with an application with our lavender lemon lotion.

Discover serenity in the Halo Infrared Sauna, where advanced infrared technology gently warms the body to promote deep relaxation, enhance circulation, and support natural detoxification. It’s a rejuvenating escape designed to elevate your wellness journey—mind, body, and spirit.

Westin WORKOUT ® Fitness Studio

At The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa, we're dedicated to making sure you leave feeling better than when you arrived. Power your performance with state-of-the-art equipment, elevate your energy on the treadmill, tone up with strength machines & free weights or grab a mat to stretch and release.

### Eat Well

Nourishment starts within. Discover the artisanal menu at our Rancho Mirage Golf Resort and Spa to feed the soul with our Eat Well Program options.

Pinzimini

This Italian-inspired restaurant serves flavorful dishes with local ingredients. The deep wooden hues and golden light offers a relaxing atmosphere perfect to mix and mingle with friends and family.

Pinz & Pints

Play a game of duckpin bowling or enjoy the classic arcade games all while sipping one of the ten beers on tap or tasting the robust menu everyone will enjoy at Pinz & Pints.

Coffee-ology

Open daily, Coffee-ology is the perfect start to your day with a café au lait, herbal tea or juice. Grab a fresh pastry, a few snacks, or other specialties before heading out for the day. All items prepared daily.

Chef's Secret Window

Severing nourishing local ingredients, this take-out restaurant hits the spot after a long day of swimming or playing a round of golf.

Fireside Lounge

Located on the 18th hole of the Pete Dye Golf Course, Fireside Lounge offers a backdrop of lush palm trees sure to add relaxation to your evening. Add a specialty cocktail and great friends for the perfect setting.

Las Brisas Bar

Serving a poolside menu with fresh sandwiches, burgers, and salads, Las Brisas is conveniently situated near our largest pool, Las Brisas. Hours vary seasonally.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa offers a fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bikes, Yoga Balls, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Las Hadas Adult Pool (Outdoor)La Paloma (Outdoor)Las Brisas Pool (Outdoor)Water Slide

Does The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa have an on-site spa?

Yes, Westin Wellness Spa is located on-site at The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa. Click here to book a service now.

Does The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa offer golf course access nearby or at the hotel?

Yes, hotel guests have access to Pete Dye Resort Course. Click here to learn more.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa?

Children's Discovery Museum of the Desert, Escape Games at The River, Michael S. Wolfson Park, The Living Desert Zoo and Gardens, Rancho Mirage Community Park and more are nearby The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa.
