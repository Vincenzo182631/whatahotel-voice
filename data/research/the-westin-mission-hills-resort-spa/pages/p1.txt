### Golfer's Dream

The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa is truly a golfer’s dream. Our resort near Palm Springs, CA, features a “must play” Pete Dye Course, known for its picturesque panoramic views of Rancho Mirage. It plays to 6,706 yards at a par 70. Set among beautiful rolling hills it offers spectacular sights of the surrounding desert mountains and includes Dye’s legendary trademark carries over water.

Learn More

### Racquet Sports

At our Rancho Mirage family resort, racquet sports enthusiasts can enjoy top-notch facilities, including three lighted tennis courts and dedicated pickleball courts. Whether you're playing a casual game or a competitive match, the resort offers on-site workshops, private lessons and group clinics and professional staff ensure an exceptional experience for players of all levels.

Learn More

### Wellness Spa

Our full-service spa and salon, features 9 massage rooms, 3 facial rooms, a therapy pool, eucalyptus steam rooms, and a relaxation lounge. Enjoy massages, facials, body treatments, nail and hair services, plus wellness classes like yoga and Pilates. Experience the exclusive HaloIR™, combining Infrared Sauna and Salt Therapy. Perfect for solo retreats, couples, bridal parties, and group getaways.

### Pinzimini

Share warm bread and a bottle of wine at our Rancho Mirage restaurant near Palm Springs. Enjoy flavorful Italian dishes in a warm atmosphere defined by golden light. Open for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Breakfast Buffet: Saturday-Sunday, 7 AM-1 PM.

### Fireside Lounge

Take in stunning views of the mountains and our golf course from a lush patio lined with palm trees. Located on the 18th hole of the Pete Dye Golf Course, this Rancho Mirage restaurant near Palm Springs offers tapas and cocktails.

### Pinz & Pints

Pint-sized bowling means big fun! Pinz & Pints in Rancho Mirage is home to four lanes of duckpin bowling, ten draft beers at the bar, brewpub eats, classic arcade games, and non-stop fun for all. Available for private events and parties of 2–150 guests.

### Chef's Secret Window

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa. with a maximum weight of 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa. Self parking is available at The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa, with rates of $6.00 per hour, $23.00 per day, $0.00 per week, and $0.00 perstay. Valet parking is available for $39.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa is Palm Springs International Airport (PSP). PSP is located approximately 6.7 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
