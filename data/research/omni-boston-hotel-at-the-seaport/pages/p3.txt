## Boston, Massachusetts

### Experience the seaport district at our Boston hotel

In the heart of the bustling Seaport District, Omni Boston Hotel at the Seaport is your gateway to the charming and the ever-evolving city. The AAA Four Diamond-rated hotel is conveniently located near South Station, the harbor, the Thomas Michael Menino Convention and Exhibition Center, and is less than three miles from Logan International Airport (BOS). Our Boston hotel is complete with 1,054 well-appointed and stylish guest rooms boasting floor-to-ceiling windows of unrivaled city and harbor views. With over 100,000 square-feet of flexible function space, our hotel is an ideal venue for meetings and conferences of all sizes. The hotel features a year round heated rooftop pool and hot tub, six distinct dining experiences, a state-of-the-art fitness center, and a luxe day spa. A stay at Omni Boston Hotel at the Seaport elevates your Boston vacation with warmth, personalized service, and unforgettable experiences.

### What is the closest airport to Omni Boston Hotel at the Seaport?

Our hotel is 2.8 miles (about 8 minutes) from the Boston Logan International Airport (BOS).

### Where is Omni Boston Hotel at the Seaport located?

Omni Boston Hotel at The Seaport is located in the Seaport District neighborhood, near downtown, with unobstructed Boston Harbor waterfront views. The hotel's physical address is 450 Summer Street, Boston, Massachusetts 02210.

### What are the property amenities at Omni Boston Hotel at the Seaport?

Omni Boston Hotel at the Seaport is home to world-class amenities including:

- A heated rooftop pool
- A full-service spa
- Rooftop fitness classes
- Fitness gear lending
- 100,000 square feet of meeting and event space
- Seven dining outlets - four full-service restaurants, a lobby bar, a pastry shop and a grab-and-go market
- Self and valet parking with a nightly charge

### What is the best area to stay in when visiting Boston?

The Seaport District is a great place to stay during your visit to Boston, MA. Offering convenient access to the Convention Center, Boston Harbor and more, our hotel in Boston is located in the heart of the Seaport District, near downtown.

### Is Omni Boston Hotel at the Seaport rated?

Yes, we are proud to have earned a AAA Four Diamond Rating, reflected across our Boston hotel’s 1,054 rooms, seven restaurants and full-service luxury spa.

### Do you offer complimentary fitness gear lending?

Yes, we have partnered with Boston-born high-performance fitness apparel company NOBULL to offer our Artist Tower guests access to complimentary gear lending! Maintain your wellness routine in our state-of-the-art Fitness Center with a selection of Peloton Bikes, treadmills, elliptical machines, rowing machines, stair masters, free-weights, medicine balls, yoga mats, kettlebells, vertical Smith machines, TRX pull-up stations and CYBEX strength equipment. Our fitness center also boasts 200 feet of continuous floor to ceiling windows providing vast amounts of natural light to enhance your workout from our hotel in Boston, MA.

### Is there a pool at Omni Boston Hotel at the Seaport?

The hotel has a rooftop outdoor pool located on the fifth floor with views of the city skyline, adjacent to Lifted Pool Bar. The pool is available exclusively to hotel guests, open daily from 6am – 11pm.

Availability is contingent on capacity limits and the pool deck is subject to closure for private events. For additional questions regarding our pool policy, or to learn more about pool availability during your stay, please contact the Front Desk.

### Do you offer Concierge Services?

Yes, our Concierge Team would be happy to make your stay extra special by helping with recommendations and reservations. You can reach them via email or by phone at 617-419-8025.

### What time is check-in and check-out?

- Check-in: 4 PM
- Check-out: 11 AM
- 4 PM late check-out may be available for an additional cost; after 4 PM the full nightly rate applies.
- Guests will be subject to an early departure fee of one night's room + tax if they shorten their stay.
- Guests must be at least 21 years of age to check-in.

### Is the hotel pet friendly?

Yes, Omni Boston Hotel at the Seaport is a pet friendly hotel for medium size dogs and cats. There is a one-time non-refundable fee of $125 per reservation. Service animals accompanying guests with disabilities are always welcome and will not be required to pay the one-time pet charge (no documentation necessary).
