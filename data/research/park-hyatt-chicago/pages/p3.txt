# Rooftop Dining in Downtown Chicago \| Park Hyatt Chicago

# Restaurants & Bars

Experience modern American cuisine rooted in French techniques at our onsite restaurant, NoMI Kitchen. Executive Chef Terence Zubieta offers an elevated menu using locally sourced, seasonal ingredients. Savor outdoor rooftop dining and take in striking views of downtown Chicago at NoMI Garden. Dine in an elegantly understated space featuring an open kitchen boasting a Molteni stove, mid-century furnishings and floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Michigan Avenue. For a vibrant cocktail collection, sophisticated small plates and a sushi counter, stop by NoMI Lounge. Our rooftop terrace on the 7th floor is a sought-after location to gather with friends and sip artisanal cocktails.

## NoMI Kitchen

Savor the artistry of Executive Chef Terence Zubieta and his culinary team as they craft modern twists on midwestern classics. Presented against floor-to-ceiling windows of Chicago's iconic Water Tower and Lake Michigan, our seasonal menu showcases the finest local ingredients, complemented by thoughtfully curated craft cocktails and an exclusive selection of wine.

## NoMI Lounge

Featuring a sprawling bar with a spotlight on local art and a renowned beverage program, NoMI Lounge is the ideal place to meet, eat and gather. Tucked away in its refuge, guests can experience an exclusive six-seat Sushi Counter featuring fresh fish flown in from Japan and a unique yet refined culinary experience. For those looking for an intimate setting for smaller groups, the Lounge’s wine cellar offers a secluded dining space for special events and private dining.

## NoMI Garden

Open During Summer Months

NoMI’s vibrant open-air rooftop terrace offers guests relaxed sophistication and a respite from the bustling city at its height on the 7th floor. NoMI Garden boasts a 30 ft. bar with an ivory granite countertop and a relaxed seating area, refined with parasols and prime views. Featuring an artisanal cocktail program and panoramic views, NoMI Garden is the rooftop destination for locals and visitors looking for an outdoor restaurant where they can take in the surroundings of downtown Chicago.

## Champagne Row

Inspired by Chicago’s historic Printer’s Row, Champagne Row offers an intimate champagne experience hosted in the Park Hyatt Chicago Library. Guests can explore a rotating selection of prestigious cuvées by the glass or bottle, paired with curated culinary bites. Limited to six seats at a time, Champagne Row offers a personalized champagne journey.
