# Luxury Michigan Ave Hotel in Downtown Chicago \| Park Hyatt Chicago

## Refined Michigan Avenue Luxury in Downtown Chicago

Mere steps from the historic Water Tower and iconic Michigan Avenue, Park Hyatt Chicago captures the essence of luxury hotel living in downtown Chicago. Designed for discerning travelers, this intimate retreat pairs refined accommodations with warm Midwestern hospitality, offering an elevated experience that feels both sophisticated and comfortably residential.

## Discover Park Hyatt Chicago

Pool

Our three-lane, 25-yard lap pool sits on the hotel’s 7th floor with views of the downtown Chicago cityscape. Please note: No lifeguard on duty; children 16 or younger must be accompanied by an adult.

Premium Suites

Discover authentic downtown living in our carefully curated premium suites. Featuring contemporary residential-style furnishings and unobstructed views of Lake Michigan, we invite you to experience the Windy City in ultimate luxury.

HERITAGE SERIES: Japanese Artistry \| October 16 & 17

Join us for two evenings celebrating Japanese craftsmanship through a six-course seasonal omakase, live gyotaku artistry, curated beverage pairings, and an optional Suntory spirits experience.

The Glenlivet 55 Legacy Experience

Forged through more than half a century of aging, The Glenlivet 55 Year Old stands as the rarest expression in Glenlivet’s history. Only 100 bottles exist in the world, with one finding its way to Illinois – at Park Hyatt Chicago. For the collector, the connoisseur, the giver who seeks rarity, we invite you to discover the ultimate experience that transcends time.

Champagne Row

Enjoy an intimate and exclusive Champagne-tasting at Champagne Row, a six-seat experience by Park Hyatt Chicago. Guests choose from a curated selection of champagnes by the glass or bottle, each paired with a delightful bite, in a cozy library setting.

NoMI Spa

NoMI Spa offers an artisanal approach to renewal, featuring a facial and massage treatment menu focused on improving your overall wellbeing. Choose your desired results, and our specialists will customize the products and techniques to suit your needs.

Family Getaway

Step into the comfort of our expansive one- to three-bedroom suites for families, complete with amenities tailored for kids of all ages. For added fun, experience Park Playcation, where your little ones can indulge in imaginative play featuring a ball pit or extravagant kitchen set. For family-friendly recommendations around the city, our expert concierge team is happy to assist.

### Dogs Are Welcome

Explore Chicago with your canine companions during your stay at Park Hyatt Chicago, conveniently located steps from Lake Shore Park, Washington Square Park and lakefront trails. Each dog-friendly stay includes a cozy dog bed, water bowl, and treats. We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to the hotel’s pet policy. For any questions, special arrangements or staying with your pet for more than 31 days, please contact the hotel directly.

#### Pet Fees

- 1–6 nights: **$150** _/ STAY_

- 7–30 nights additional cleaning fee: **$150** _/ STAY_

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  50Pounds

## Things to Do

Set in the historic Gold Coast, our luxury hotel places you moments from the best of downtown Chicago. Explore the shops and restaurants of the Magnificent Mile, including nearby Water Tower Place, or head to the top of 875 North Michigan Avenue, formerly the John Hancock Center for sweeping city views. Family-friendly favorites like Navy Pier and Lincoln Park Zoo are just a short drive away, and the scenic Chicago Riverwalk invites you to unwind with a riverside stroll.
