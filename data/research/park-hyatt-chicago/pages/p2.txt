Park Hyatt Chicago welcomes guests to 146 reimagined guestrooms and 36 suites, offering a residential oasis in the heart of downtown Chicago’s Gold Coast. Located on Michigan Avenue, our hotel offers sweeping views of Lake Michigan and Chicago Avenue through expansive triple-pane windows, and spa-inspired bathrooms featuring dual vanities and lavish Le Labo® amenities. Elevate your stay with one of our specialty rooms or suites, complete with balconies, enhanced wellbeing amenities, and private terraces.

With deep historical roots and a landmark address, we invite you to experience our city the Park Hyatt Chicago way.
