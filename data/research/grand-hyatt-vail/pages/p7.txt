# Hotel Policies

Resort Fee

A daily resort fee of $68.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

- Weekly yoga & singing bowl meditation classes
- Guided nature walks daily
- S'mores at the main firepit
- Live entertainment
- Champagne sabering and toast
- Lemonade and macaroons
- Fly fishing casting lessons
- Yappy hour dog-friendly event (Summer)
- Access to ice skating rink and use of hotel ice skates (subject to availability)
- Hot cocoa & chocolate chip cookies (Winter)
- Stocking and ornament decorating (Holiday Season)
- Shuttle loop into Vail and Lionshead Village
- Ski and bike valet services
- Kids entertainment center
- Electric vehicle charging station usage
- TV studio broadcasting destination programming live on-site--available in rooms/suites.
- Local Vail Daily Newspaper
- Vail postcard and postage stamps
- Family movie showings in the Grand Theater

Ski Storage Policy

Overnight ski storage is complimentary for personal ski equipment and rentals from Venture Sports Grand Hyatt Vail. Storage for equipment from off‑site rental locations will incur a $10 nightly fee.

Pet Policy

Your four-legged family members are welcome at our dog-friendly hotel. Grand Hyatt Vail room bookings will be assessed a non-refundable pet fee of $150 for stays up to 6 nights, and an additional non-refundable fee of $50 for deep cleaning fees from 7 + nights, per stay. Dogs may not exceed 70 pounds, limit two dogs allowed, per room, per night.

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Package Handling Policy

Any items shipped of delivered to the hotel will be associated with a fee and charged to your guest room. To retrieve a delivered package, please visit the Front Desk located in the lobby. For assistance with shipping, please contact the Concierge Desk and we are delighted to help facilitate your shipment.

Incoming Package Handling Fees:

- Envelope (~1 lb): $3.00
- Small (1–10 lbs): $10.00
- Medium (11–25 lbs): $15.00
- Large (25–50 lbs): $20.00
- Golf Bag – Large (25–50 lbs): $20.00
- Ski Bag – Large (25–50 lbs): $20.00
- Golf Bag – Oversize (50+ lbs): $30.00
- Ski Bag – Oversize (50+ lbs): $30.00
- Oversize (50+ lbs): $30.00
- Pallet (Standard Wood Pallets): $200.00

Outgoing Shipping: Prices will reflect to all outgoing shipments, in addition to the cost of shipping. Shipping rates will vary based on the size, weight, and destination of the package.

To ensure correct shipping, storage and delivery to a guest, please address as follows:

Grand Hyatt Vail

Attn: GUEST NAME / ARRIVAL DATE

1300 Westhaven Dr.

Vail, CO 81657

Shipments are held for a limited number of days. If the package has not been picked up after 30 days, the package will be returned to sender, who will be responsible for all additional shipping fees. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

Alcohol Policy

Outside alcohol may not be brought into or consumed within any hotel public area including the lobby and lobby terraces, restaurants and bars, lounges, pool and hot tub areas, meeting and event spaces, and outdoor public spaces. All alcoholic beverages enjoyed in licensed areas must be purchased through authorized Grand Hyatt Vail outlets. This policy ensures guest safety, supports full compliance with Colorado liquor regulations, and maintains a consistent, high-quality experience throughout the resort.
