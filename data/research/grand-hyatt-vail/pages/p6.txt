I asked if a menu item was spicy, was told no, but it was and I couldn't eat it. The server was dismissive and didn't do anything. The breakfast buffet was good but a little expensive for what you get. At dinner, the risotto that came with the salmon was terrible and we didn't eat it, the salmon itself was good though. The Spa was closed the days we were there and we couldn't even buy anything at the gift shop and the market was also closed which we were not aware of. No one mentioned these random closures. The hotel itself, the room, and bathroom were nice, and location of the hotel was really great. We actually saw a mink on the water, which was exciting. These were really the only highlights. Overall, not the best experience for a birthday trip.

### A wonderful 4 stars experience

First, a very nice hotel with great rooms- tasteful, clean, functional, and comfortable. I really liked the layout and found the many outlets and placement of outlets convenient for laptop, phone charging, ironing- it just made sense. The bedding was very comfortable and it didn’t feel generic, and it wasn’t crazy colored modern- it felt relaxing. Pool area was beautiful and fitness room was exceptional. Staff was genuinely friendly. The only reason I didn’t give 5 stars is that it really should be a 4 star hotel. It lacks the high-end luxury of other 5 star hotels in the area. That doesn’t detract from the experience and it is priced appropriately but if you’re expecting a truly 5 star experience you may be disappointed. So it met and maybe exceeded expectations for the price point in the area, but not the expectations I have when I’m looking for a truly 5 star experience. And, unless you like Japanese/Sushi, the dining options are lackluster. That being said, I will definitely stay there again and would highly recommend.
