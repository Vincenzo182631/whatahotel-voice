# Dining

Indulge in thoughtfully sourced seasonal cuisine and cocktails when you dine at Grand Hyatt Vail Resort and Spa! From an exquisite pairing of Creekside views and local Colorado-inspired cuisine at Gessner to fresh and creative takes on Japanese delicacies in Makoto Vail, a world of delicious flavors awaits. Fuel your day of mountain adventures with breakfast classics at Gessner or a fresh cup of Illy Coffee at Gore Creek Market. When it’s time to gather for some fun, our Top Golf Suites are the perfect place for friends and family to gather to play virtual golf, dodgeball and other games while enjoying personalized bar service and tasty bites. For those laying low, our In-Room Dining Service is a convenient way to enjoy a delicious meal without leaving the comfort of your room.

## Makoto Vail

World renowned Chef Makoto Okuwa invites guests and visitors to experience his signature blend of contemporary Japanese cuisine and curated recipes from his twenty-five years of culinary experience. From bright sushi platters and the freshest sashimi to a decadent selection of Wagyu beef, creatively unique desserts, and a breathtaking selection of specialty Japanese spirits and sake, Makoto Vail offers the freshest and highest quality ingredients to create an unparalleled dining experience. \*Seasonal hours vary

## Gessner Colorado Mountain Grill

Named after the Swiss natural historian, Gessner blends Rocky Mountain flavors with the European influence of Vail Valley to offer an exceptional dining experience. Celebrate the flavors of summer with dishes that showcase local, sustainably sourced ingredients prepared with a fresh, seasonal touch.

## Gore Creek Market

Gore Creek Market features a fast-casual atmosphere, shareable dining options, and a variety of healthy, indulgent, and simply delicious food. Enjoy breakfast in Vail with a bagel and coffee or a classic soup-and-sandwich lunch. Get your meal to go or dine in as you watch your favorite sports team or play Xbox games in the attached entertainment room. \*Seasonal hours vary.

## Topgolf Swing Suites

Enjoy casual food and beverage offerings alongside virtual golf and other interactive games such as soccer, hockey and zombie dodgeball. Each Swing Suite accommodates up to twelve people, with inviting lounge seating and service – great for teams, families or a night out with friends to enjoy lively indoor games, drinks and dining in Vail!
