Settle into luxurious alpine comfort with our 254 Colorado-inspired rooms and 29 suites at Grand Hyatt Vail Resort and Spa, where sunlight-filled spaces and sweeping mountain views invite you to a mountain retreat like no other. Many of our rooms and suites offer private balconies overlooking Gore Creek and the western slopes of Vail Mountain and are paired with fireplaces, perfect for getting cozy after a day of outdoor fun. Take your stay to new heights in the heart of Vail Valley with our spacious guest rooms and suites.

### Vail Residences at Cascade Village

Discover our residences ranging from one‑bedroom condominiums to expansive nine‑bedroom homes.

**Signature Stays**

Our luxury residences allow for a unique stay throughout Cascade Village, from liftside at Chair 20 to the serene waters of Gore Creek.

Relax with the comforts of a condo or home including full kitchens, large living areas, and thoughtful details that make extended stays feel natural while enjoying the full amenities and activities at Grand Hyatt Vail Resort & Spa.

Host an intimate wedding or executive retreat in one of our private homes ranging from four to nine bedrooms.
