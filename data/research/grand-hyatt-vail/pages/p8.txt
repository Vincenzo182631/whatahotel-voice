Hotel Shuttle Service

Our complimentary shuttle is reserved for registered hotel and residence guests, offering seamless access to Vail and Lionshead Villages from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. Our dedicated shuttles run non-stop throughout the day during the peak summer and winter months, or on request by calling +1 970 476 1234. (hours may vary, per season; check with guest services).
