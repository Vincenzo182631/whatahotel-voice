# THE SPA AT GRAND HYATT VAIL

### About Our Vail Resort Spa Experience

The Spa at Grand Hyatt Vail follows in the footsteps of traditional alpine spas, embracing the natural splendor and healing powers of our environment. Our Vail spa resort therapists are artisans of rejuvenation so you can unwind after your day of adventure. Enjoy a massage after skiing on the mountain, a facial after a relaxing day in the sunshine or a blow-out, manicure or pedicure before dinner.

**Contact Us:** 970-479-5942

**Spa Hours:** Open Tuesday-Saturday from 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM

## ALPINE-INSPIRED TREATMENTS & OFFERINGS

### Alpine Transformation Wrap

Renew and relax with our detoxifying Alpine Transformation.

Beginning with a gentle body peel, followed by a mud and micro-algae wrap and finished with a full body massage to firm and improve elasticity, this signature spa treatment will leave you feeling rejuvenated and blissful.

### Massage & Facial Enhancements

Elevate your spa experience with our variety of luxurious and targeted treatment enhancements to make your journey to relaxation even more restorative and refreshing.

Services include Aroma Therapy, CBD Facial or Full Body Oil, Himalayan Salt Stone and more.

### TheraFace Facial Restoration

Our resort spa's facial restoration uses TheraFace’s percussive LED and hot/cold therapy to reduce tension and relax facial muscles.

The microcurrent and cleansing treatments help lift, tone and rejuvenate, resulting in a lighter feel to the face along with a deeply relaxing experience.

## RESTORE & REJUVENATE

### Salon at Grand Hyatt Vail

Discover personalized beauty services with quality products from our dedicated professionals in the Salon at Grand Hyatt Vail. Our services prioritize personal self-care and align your inner and outer spirit with the surrounding natural beauty of Vail Valley.

From manicures to pedicures, our salon is here to pamper you and take care of every detail.

### Resort Fitness

Overlooking the outdoor infinity pool along Gore creek, our 24/7 fitness center promotes health and wellbeing for our guests. Located directly next to the Spa at Grand Hyatt Vail, the resort fitness center features Life Fitness cardio equipment, Peloton bikes, TONAL and free weights to ensure you stay on track with your health goals while on the road. Guests also can join our instructors for scheduled classes in our fitness studio.

Guests may scan their room key to access the fitness center 24 hours a day.

### A Wellness Journey at Grand Hyatt Vail

Embrace an effortless alpine wellness routine with relaxing saunas, snowy mountain trails for crisp-air movement, tailored spa treatments, nourishing superfood bowls, mindful movement classes, and year-round heated outdoor pools. Every moment is designed to restore balance and connect you to Vail’s natural rhythm.

Learn More

### Outdoor Heated Infinity Pool & Hot Tubs

Listen to the calming flow of Gore Creek while gazing at forested vistas from our heated outdoor infinity pool and two hot tubs as you relax among the mountains after a day of skiing adventures.

The outdoor saline pool at our Vail resort also offers spacious lounge chairs and rental cabanas both for guests to enjoy during their stay and for visiting guests after a renewing spa treatment.

### Cold Plunges & Saunas

A peaceful alpine spot on the pool deck invites guests to unwind in cold plunges and saunas, open daily from 7 AM to 8 PM and surrounded by Vail’s mountain views. Whether you begin your morning here or settle in after a day on the mountain, this serene alpine retreat is the ideal place to recharge.
