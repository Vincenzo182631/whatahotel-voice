## Your Winter Escape Begins at Our Vail Resort Hotel

Proudly ranked in the top five for Best Hotels in Vail by U.S. News & World Report 2026, Grand Hyatt Vail Resort & Spa offers a refined mountain escape for year‑round adventure. Let your Colorado ski vacation feel effortless with the best ski‑in/ski‑out access to Vail Mountain from Chair 20, just steps from our doors. In summer, enjoy the tree-lined path along Gore Creek to walk or bike to Vail and Lionshead Villages, and travel light in any season with on-site ski or bike rentals for everything you need to explore. Return from the great outdoors to relax in luxury with the resort spa, outdoor infinity pool, hot tubs, saunas, and cold plunge, or spend time with family in the hotel arcade and game room. For drinks and dining in Vail, enjoy après-ski with a view at Fireside Bar or a culinary adventure at Makoto Vail from acclaimed, James Beard‑recognized chef Makoto Okuwa. From exciting skiing and dining to quiet mountain renewal, let Grand Hyatt Vail Resort & Spa be the perfect luxury getaway to experience the best of Colorado.

## EXPLORE

Summer in Vail

Enhance your stay with the best of summer in Vail. From sun‑lit trails to scenic paths along Gore Creek, the season offers endless ways to explore the mountains. Guests can enjoy leisurely bike rides, guided outdoor experiences, and easy access to Vail’s vibrant village atmosphere. Whether you’re discovering new viewpoints, soaking in the fresh alpine air, or simply relaxing after a day outside, summer in Vail brings a bright, active energy to every stay.

Vail Residences at Cascade Village

Our spacious vacation homes and condo rentals at the Vail Residences at Cascade Village feature fully equipped kitchens, in-unit laundry, parking and access to all the resort amenities at Grand Hyatt Vail Resort and Spa. Explore these luxurious and spacious lodging options for family vacations, couple’s getaways, group retreats and gatherings with friends in Vail.

Winter Ski-In/Ski-Out Experience

Discover the best access to Vail Mountain and everything you need to gear up. Our on-site chairlift offers convenient access to skiing and snowboarding from Chair 20, located steps away from our world-class ski rental, concierge and valet. Offering a Vail Resorts ticket office for your last-minute lift ticket, our ski-in/ski-out hotel is the ideal location for winter adventure and Vail’s magnificent outdoors. Chair 20 is open select dates during the winter season - contact our hotel to confirm early or late season operations.

Venture Sports is your go-to destination for ski and snowboard rentals, offering premium gear, personalized fittings, and seamless service just steps from the on-site Chair 20. Their team gets you geared up quickly so you can hit the slopes and make the most of every powder day.

Vail & Lionshead Villages

Discover a hotel stay set amongst the trees with the ease of quick access to the iconic villages of Vail. With their European Alpine setting from the Bavarian-inspired architecture, Vail Village offers pedestrian-only streets filled with shops, cafés, art galleries and more.

Located only 1 mile and a short 5-minute drive from the resort, guests can reach the Villages with our complimentary shuttle transportation on constant loop from 8am-10pm.

Après In Cascade Village

After a full day on the mountain, settle into Makoto for an elevated après experience. Guests unwind with signature bites and cocktails that feel both comforting and refined. The atmosphere is relaxed, inviting, and perfect for easing into the evening. From there, drift into the lobby as live music begins, creating a warm, easy flow that carries your night forward.

### THE SPA AT GRAND HYATT VAIL

The Spa at Grand Hyatt Vail Resort and Spa takes inspiration from traditional alpine spas and achieves a harmonious balance by embracing both the natural beauty and the therapeutic properties of our environment along Gore Creek. We use premium moisturizing products from hydrafacial, HydroPeptide and Comfort Zone, as well as the best local products like Phia and Lost Range. Our resort spa therapists are artisans of rejuvenation, inviting guests to relax and unwind after a day of mountain adventures.

Your dogs are welcome at Grand Hyatt Vail Resort and Spa, where our pet-friendly hotel will be waiting with treats for your pup. Fee assessed at the hotel’s discretion. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
