### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Where is COMO Metropolitan London located?

COMO Metropolitan London is situated on Park Lane in the heart of Mayfair, offering a prime position in central London. The hotel overlooks Hyde Park, placing guests moments from major cultural landmarks, iconic neighborhoods, and transport links. With Green Park, Piccadilly, and Knightsbridge all close by, the location provides effortless access to London’s museums, galleries, dining, and luxury shopping streets. Its central yet calm setting makes it an ideal base for travellers looking for a well-connected luxury hotel in Mayfair.

#### 2What makes COMO Metropolitan London a luxury five-star hotel in Mayfair?

COMO Metropolitan London delivers a contemporary five-star luxury experience in the heart of Mayfair, combining minimalist design with warm, personalised service. Overlooking Hyde Park from Park Lane, the hotel features stylish rooms and suites, world-class dining at Nobu London, and holistic wellness therapies at COMO Shambhala Spa. Guests enjoy an elevated, discreet atmosphere with tailored concierge support — making it a standout luxury hotel in Mayfair for modern, design-minded travellers.

#### 3How close is COMO Metropolitan London to London’s top shopping districts?

COMO Metropolitan London is ideally located for London’s best shopping districts. Guests can walk to Bond Street, Oxford Street, Regent Street, and the designer boutiques of Mayfair. Knightsbridge — home to Harrods and Harvey Nichols — is just a pleasant stroll across Hyde Park. This Park Lane address makes the hotel a top choice for travellers seeking a luxury hotel near London’s shopping areas, including Bond Street, Oxford Street, and Knightsbridge.

#### 4Is COMO Metropolitan London a good choice for travellers seeking a luxury hotel near Hyde Park?

Yes. Located directly on Park Lane, COMO Metropolitan London is one of the most conveniently positioned luxury hotels near Hyde Park. Guests can enjoy immediate access to morning runs, leisurely walks, and outdoor activities in one of London’s largest green spaces. Combined with premium amenities — from COMO Shambhala spa treatments to Nobu dining — the hotel provides a serene yet central base for travellers wanting a refined Hyde Park stay.

#### 5Is COMO Metropolitan London suitable for a family stay in central London?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan London is ideal for a family stay in central London, offering spacious rooms, interconnecting configurations, and family-friendly suites that provide flexibility for parents and children. Its location near Hyde Park, Green Park, and Knightsbridge’s museums makes sightseeing simple and walkable. Families can also enjoy healthy dining options, attentive concierge assistance, and a calming residential atmosphere — making it one of the top luxury family hotels in Mayfair.

#### 6Is COMO Metropolitan London committed to sustainability and eco-friendly practices?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan London is dedicated to sustainable and responsible hospitality, using energy-efficient systems, eco-friendly amenities, and local suppliers wherever possible. The hotel actively reduces plastic use and supports COMO Hotels’ global eco-conscious initiatives.
