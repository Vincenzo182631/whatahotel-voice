### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Does COMO Metropolitan London have a spa?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan London is home to COMO Shambhala Spa, a boutique luxury spa in London known for its holistic therapies, expert massages, and restorative wellness treatments. The serene, minimalist space offers a calm retreat in the heart of Mayfair, making it a popular choice for travellers seeking one of the top luxury hotel spas in London.

#### 2What treatments and therapies are available at COMO Metropolitan London?

Guests can indulge in deep-tissue massages, aromatherapy treatments, facials, and body rituals using natural COMO Shambhala products. Wellness programs are also available to address stress relief, detox, and recovery.

#### 3Are yoga sessions offered at the hotel?

Yes. The COMO Shambhala Spa offers private yoga sessions, led by expert instructors. In-room wellness sessions can also be arranged for ultimate convenience.

#### 4Is there a fitness center in the hotel?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan London features a fully equipped gym with Life Fitness equipment, free weights, and personal training options for guests who wish to maintain their fitness routines.

#### 5What wellness facilities are available at COMO Metropolitan London?

Facilities include treatment rooms, a wellness studio, a relaxation area, and access to in-room spa services. Guests can also enjoy healthy dining options and guided mindfulness programs as part of COMO’s holistic approach.
