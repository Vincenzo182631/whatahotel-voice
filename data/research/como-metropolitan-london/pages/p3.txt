# Dining at COMO Metropolitan London

The opening of Labombe by Trivet is an exciting new addition to our dining repertoire featuring modern British fair and elegant wine selections with a twist. Our hotel guests can also enjoy priority bookings at the iconic Japanese-Peruvian restaurant Nobu, on the first floor of the hotel. We also offer all-day healthy in-room dining, including COMO Shambhala Kitchen classics.

**Festive Programme**

### SEE THE WORLD IN A NEW LIGHT

This festive season, celebrate at COMO Metropolitan London, with spaces designed to bring together groups of all sizes. Enjoy an intimate dining experience at LaBombe by Trivet, where Michelin-starred Pan-Mediterranean cuisine by Jonny Lake and Isa Bal is paired with an exceptional wine collection.

### November 3rd 2026

## Taste of COMO: Discovering Burgundy

COMO Metropolitan London's sister hotel, COMO The Halkin, hosts a rare Premier Cru tasting from Burgundy's Côte de Beaune this November. Seven celebrated growers, eight exceptional wines, one evening with wine expert Tom Harrow of Honest Grapes.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1What dining options and restaurants are available at COMO Metropolitan London?

COMO Metropolitan London is home to some of Mayfair’s leading dining venues, including the globally acclaimed Nobu London, known for its world-class Japanese-Peruvian fine dining such as black cod miso and signature sushi. Guests can also enjoy contemporary British cuisine at Labombe by Trivet, plus a stylish bar for cocktails and light bites. This makes the hotel a strong choice for travellers searching for fine dining restaurants in London, especially around Park Lane and Hyde Park

#### 2Are vegetarian and vegan options available?

Yes. Both Nobu and Labombe by Trivet offer vegetarian and vegan menus, thoughtfully crafted to highlight fresh, seasonal ingredients.

#### 3Does COMO Metropolitan London propose healthy food options?

Absolutely. The hotel offers COMO Shambhala Cuisine, a wellness-oriented menu focusing on organic, nutrient-rich dishes designed to boost vitality and well-being without sacrificing flavor.
