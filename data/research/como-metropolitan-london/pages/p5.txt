Home/London/COMO Metropolitan London/Experiences/Festive

See the world in a new light this festive season. Enjoy private seasonal dining at COMO Metropolitan London and celebrate your gathering your way with our event spaces and special packages.

### Every celebration, considered

From bespoke floral arrangements and personalised table settings to audiovisual support and carefully curated entertainment, every element can be tailored to your occasion.

Whether gathering with colleagues, clients or loved ones, our team ensures each celebration is delivered with the understated service and attention to detail that defines COMO Metropolitan London.

## PRIVATE EVENT SPACES

### The Met Boardroom

Perfect for smaller festive gatherings, the Met Boardroom offers a private, contemporary setting for intimate dinners and executive entertaining.

The room accommodates up to 10 guests for seated dining or 15 guests for a standing reception.

### The Met Lounge

With its relaxed atmosphere and contemporary interiors, the Met Lounge is ideal for informal festive receptions, welcome drinks or post-dinner gatherings.

The room accommodates up to 10 guests for seated dining or 10 guests for a standing reception.

### The Met Studio

Connected to the Met Boardroom, the Met Studio provides a flexible space for pre-dinner drinks and smaller gatherings, and can also be utilised as an additional entertaining space.

The room accommodates up to six guests for seated dining or 10 guests for a standing reception.

### The White Room

The White Room offers contemporary décor and a refined atmosphere beneath its striking glass pyramid ceiling. Our signature event space, it is the perfect backdrop for seasonal lunches, private dinners and cocktail receptions.

The space accommodates up to 50 guests for seated dining or 65 for standing receptions.

### The Met Space

Characterised by its clean, contemporary aesthetic, Met Space is a sophisticated setting for festive gatherings of every kind. The space can be tailored to suit both intimate occasions and larger celebrations, with an adjoining room providing a seamless extension for welcome drinks or refreshments.

The room accommodates up to 24 guests for seated dining or 30 guests for a standing reception.

### LABOMBE BY TRIVET

Celebrate in style with an intimate dining experience at Labombe by Trivet. Serving up Michelin-starred dining by Jonny Lake and Isa Bal, the menu features provenance-led Pan-Mediterranean cuisine, paired with one of London's most exciting wine collections.

Our private dining room welcomes up to 12 guests, with exclusive restaurant hire available for larger festive gatherings.

## STAY WITH US

Complete your celebration with an overnight stay at COMO Metropolitan London. Our contemporary rooms and spacious suites offer a calm retreat overlooking Hyde Park, just moments from London's festive shopping, seasonal attractions and celebrated Christmas lights.

Preferred accommodation rates and room blocks are available for guests attending private events, subject to availability.

For room bookings of six and above please contact the events team.
