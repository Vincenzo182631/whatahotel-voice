**Green initiatives at COMO Metropolitan London**

### Sustainability in Mayfair

At COMO Metropolitan London, we are dedicated to reducing our environmental impact in every way we can. From innovative wooden keycards and paperless concierge services, to partnering with local suppliers and working to eliminate single-use plastics, sustainability sits at the core of our operations.

### GETTING HERE

There are five airports serving London: Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton and London City. Our concierge can arrange transportation, as well as car hire and private drivers for the duration of your stay. We are also happy to arrange for further air travel or train journeys. For more information, email concierge.met.lon@comohotels.com

For private air travel: There is a heliport at Battersea and private airports at Biggin Hill (17 miles from city centre) and Farnborough Airport (39 miles from city centre).

### SEASONALITY IN LONDON

Temperatures in London roughly range from an average of 5°C (41°F) in the winter to 27°C (80°F) in the summer. The city's English gardens thrive with the seasonal shifts, including nearby Hyde Park.

## THE COMO EYE FOR DESIGN

Our hotel balances the contemporary and the timeless: cool, sophisticated yet entirely welcoming. Interiors feature light woods, sun-soaked whites with accents of vibrant yellow.
