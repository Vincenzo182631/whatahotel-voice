# Accommodation

COMO Metropolitan London has 144 contemporary rooms and suites overlooking Hyde Park or the Mayfair skyline. Sophisticated and calming, each room offers a serene hideaway in the heart of London. For longer stays or families who prefer more space, the nearby Brick Street and Hertford Street Residences offer expansive private apartments with all the comforts of a home away from home. Our suites also include a selection of privileges designed to enhance your stay.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1What types of rooms and suites are available at COMO Metropolitan London?

COMO Metropolitan London offers contemporary rooms, suites and private apartments overlooking Hyde Park or the Mayfair skyline. Accommodation ranges from City Rooms and Park Rooms to spacious suites such as the Metropolitan Suite and COMO Suite, alongside two and three-bedroom residences ideal for extended stays. Suites provide additional living space and a selection of exclusive privileges designed to enhance the guest experience.

#### 2Are there rooms suitable for large families or groups?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan London offers spacious multi-bedroom residences ideal for large families or groups. Options include two- to four-bedroom layouts with separate living and dining areas and fully equipped kitchens, providing the space and flexibility needed for longer stays. These residences are a top choice for travellers seeking family suites, large family rooms, or group accommodation in central London.

#### 3Are the rooms equipped with modern amenities such as air conditioning and Wi-Fi?

Yes. All rooms, suites and residences include air conditioning, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi, smart TVs, minibars, and bathrooms with COMO Shambhala amenities.

#### 4Can extra bed or baby cot be provided for children?

Yes. Extra beds and baby cots can be arranged in selected rooms and suites upon request, making COMO Metropolitan London a comfortable choice for families visiting central London.
