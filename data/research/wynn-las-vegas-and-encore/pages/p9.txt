# The Shops at Wynn and Encore

For the best in upscale retail destinations, discover The Shops at Wynn and Encore, where a global roster of designer boutiques offers luxurious apparel, accessories, fine jewelry, status watches, and more from the world’s most coveted labels. Also, don’t miss our newest retail offering, Wynn Plaza Shops, where wellness, dining, and retail experiences have been designed exclusively for Wynn Las Vegas.

## Mikimoto at Wynn Plaza

A symbol of refined elegance, Mikimoto is renowned for its unparalleled expertise in pearls and fine jewelry, revealing beauty through exceptional craftsmanship and timeless design. Visit us at our new boutique in the Wynn Plaza Shops.
