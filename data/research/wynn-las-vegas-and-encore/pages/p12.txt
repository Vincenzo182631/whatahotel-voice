## Tier Credits

## HOW TO EARN TIER CREDITS

* * *

Tier Credits are based on average bet, type of game you play, and length of play, and can be earned for your play on select reel slots, video poker machines, and table games accumulated within a calendar year. Your play is tracked whenever you insert your Wynn Rewards card into the slot machine or hand it to the dealer before table game play. Players are responsible for ensuring their play is tracked.

## FREE _**CREDIT**_

## HOW TO EARN SLOT POINTS FOR FREECREDIT

* * *

Slot points are earned when you play participating reel slots and video poker.

– Slots: Earn 1 point for every $5 coin in

– Video Poker: Earn 1 point for every $10 or $20 coin in (based on game)

For every 500 slot points earned using your Wynn Rewards card at Wynn and Encore Las Vegas, you will receive $5 in FREE **_CREDIT_**.

– Minimum redemption amount for FREE **_CREDIT_** is $5 for 500 slot points

– You have 12 months upon earning to redeem your slot points for FREE **_CREDIT_**– You have 30 days to redeem your FREE **_CREDIT_** once your slot points are converted

Please visit a Wynn Rewards desk for instructions on how to convert slot points into FREE **_CREDIT._** Players are responsible for ensuring their play is tracked.

## COMP _**DOLLARS**_

## HOW TO EARN COMPDOLLARS

* * *

COMP _**DOLLARS**_ are earned on select reel slots, video poker machines, table games based on your average bet, game type and length of play. COMP _**DOLLARS**_ are earned on live poker play based on length of play (excludes poker tournaments). Tracking your play is easy: simply insert your Wynn Rewards card into the slot machine, or hand it to the dealer before table game play or live poker play. Players are responsible for ensuring their play is tracked.
