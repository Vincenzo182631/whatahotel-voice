# Wynn Las Vegas Restaurants and Dining

At Wynn Las Vegas, dining is an art form. Home to some of the most acclaimed restaurants on the Las Vegas Strip, including Delilah, SW Steakhouse, Mizumi, and Casa Playa, Wynn brings together award-winning chefs, a commitment to thoughtful sourcing, distinctive design, and genuine hospitality. From morning coffee and leisurely lunches to celebratory dinners and late-night dining, Wynn offers memorable bites and sips for every occasion. Whether you are looking for an iconic Las Vegas steakhouse, coastal Mexican seafood, vibrant Japanese cuisine, or Forbes Travel Guide-recognized fine dining on the Strip, the restaurants at Wynn Las Vegas reflect a dedication to creativity, craft, and five-star service.
