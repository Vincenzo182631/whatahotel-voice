### Behind the Design with Todd-Avery Lenahan

Wynn Las Vegas guest rooms and suites are designed in soft, subtle hues, complemented by thoughtfully layered lighting that enhances both comfort and well-being. Every lighting element is tailored to the way guests use their space, whether working, preparing for an evening out, or simply unwinding.

“In addition to ensuring every room and suite is impeccably appointed, this is the space our guests experience most personally,” says Todd-Avery Lenahan, president and chief creative officer of Wynn Design & Development. “It becomes their home when they’re with us. As guest preferences continue to evolve, we’re constantly refining every detail not only to meet but to exceed expectations.”

Throughout each room and suite, carefully considered details are integrated into a design rich in texture, warmth, and scale. “The art, all commissioned for Wynn, is generous in size, while the bed has a really confident presentation,” Lenahan says. “Our guests are quite sophisticated, and they appreciate these and other nuances, all designed with their comfort in mind.”
