# THE PENINSULA SHANGHAI

The Peninsula Shanghai opened on the historic Bund in 2009, The Peninsula Hotels’ flagship hotel in Mainland China is a beacon of luxury hospitality on the city’s iconic riverfront promenade. Equipped with extraordinary facilities, world-class services and critically acclaimed Michelin star-rated restaurant, The Peninsula Shanghai effortlessly harmonises exquisite Art Deco-inspired interiors with contemporary comforts.
