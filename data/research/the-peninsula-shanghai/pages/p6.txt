The Peninsula Shanghai Luxury Hotel Spa & Wellness item 2

The Peninsula Spa is awarded the prestigious Five-Star rating from the pioneering Forbes Travel Guide 2026, where is a bastion of luxurious wellness treatments and relaxing retreats. You’ll leave feeling refreshed, according to Forbes, “whether you indulge in the Oriental Thermal infusion to eliminate toxins of your tired feet kneaded with some reflexology.”

Visually stunning and daringly different, The Peninsula Spa represents a new departure in spa décor, with a bold step back to the look and feel of the 1930s Art Deco period. Soothing tones of navy and ivory perfectly complement dark wood floors. Welcoming fireplaces and under-floor heating ensure maximum comfort, whilst large botanical art pieces adorn the walls.

## Additional Information

#### Poolside Snacks

Hotel and Spa guests can enjoy light meals and breakfast poolside, or on the outdoor terrace, together with spa cuisine from the Naturally Peninsula menu.

#### Thermal Suite

After treatments spa guests luxuriate in the spectacular Thermal Suite. Aroma mist is sprayed into the Steam Room to the glow of the iridescent light feature, with shaved ice for instant cooling. Sauna and showers with side jets, cool mist and tropical rain settings complete the total refreshment experience.

#### Treatments for Men

All treatments are available to men, but the Week Day Escape in the Yun Peninsula Vitality Room is idea for busy men with hectic schedules. Here we specialise in  Foot Reflex Zone Therapy and Neck, Shoulders and Scalp Massage. Treatments can be combined with a snack and last less than an hour.

#### Products and Packages

A variety of Peninsula Shanghai Spa gift certificates and Spa Half- and Full-Day Spa Journey Packages can be customised to guests’ preference. The full range of skincare products is also available, to continue the spa experience at home.
