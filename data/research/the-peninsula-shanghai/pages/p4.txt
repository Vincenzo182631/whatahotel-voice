# Sir Ellys Restaurant

Set high above the Bund, Sir Elly’s pairs a more contemporary expression of Northern Italian cuisine with expansive views across the Bund coupled with an atmosphere that promises a memorable experience

Chef Eugenio Cannoni works from his Piedmont heritage, reinterpreting tradition with a lighter touch, a more defined perspective and commitment to sourcing seasonal ingredients. The result is an experience that balances innovation, cross-cultural ingenuity and a deep, soulful connection to 'la cucina Italiana'.

## Meet the Chef

"I am thrilled to join The Peninsula Shanghai and lead the culinary team at Sir Elly’s,” says Chef Eugenio. “My passion lies in seamlessly blending local flavors with global influences, and I am excited to bring my vision to the vibrant culinary scene of Shanghai.”

#### Eugenio Cannoni

Chef Eugenio's culinary journey began in his family's restaurant in Piedmont. Eager to expand his expertise, he attended Alma di Gualtiero Marchesi, one of Italy’s premier cooking schools. His quest for culinary excellence led him to various prestigious kitchens across Europe and the world, enhancing his skills and knowledge along the way. His impressive career includes roles as Head Chef at the Michelin-starred Moss Restaurant at the Retreat Hotel in Iceland, Chef de Cuisine at La Scala restaurant at the Sukhothai Hotel in Bangkok, and Chef de Cuisine at the Michelin-starred Restaurant of Hotel Ca' Vittoria. In Shanghai, he blends culinary inspirations from the four places of his career path—Italy, Thailand, Iceland, and China—using modern techniques to create dishes rooted in the flavours of Northern Italy.
