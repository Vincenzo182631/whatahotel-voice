## Experience Shanghai chic redefined on the historic Bund

Opened on the historic Bund in 2009, The Peninsula Hotels’ flagship hotel in Mainland China is a beacon of luxury hospitality on the city’s iconic riverfront promenade. Equipped with extraordinary facilities, world-class services and critically acclaimed Michelin star-rated restaurant, The Peninsula Shanghai effortlessly harmonises exquisite Art Deco-inspired interiors with contemporary comfort.

### Recreating History

The Schindler Group specially produced this replica of an antique lift from the 1930s, originally housed in the Belgian Lift Museum in Brussels, for The Peninsula Shanghai. The original lift, whose origins remain surrounded in mystery, was transported from Brussels to Switzerland to be replicated along with minor modifications suited to The Peninsula. Guests are invited to experience a unique piece of history, as the lift works from the basement to the second floor of the hotel.

Read More

### Travel In Style

The only luxury hotel in Shanghai with a customised car fleet, The Peninsula Shanghai owns four bespoke Extended Wheelbase Rolls-Royce Phantoms, six BMW Peninsula Editions, and a seven-seat Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, all finished in signature Peninsula Green. Airport greeting service at the aerobridge can be provided upon request.

Read More

### The Rosamonde Aviation Lounge

The Rosamonde Aviation Lounge celebrates the fascinating history of aviation in China and, with its impressive collection of antiques and memorabilia, including a life-size replica of the Leoning at its entrance, sets a truly unique scene for any occasion. Perched on the 14th floor of the hotel, the glass-encased Rosamonde Aviation Lounge boasts spectacular views of the glittering city skyline.
