# The Magic Table

The Magic Table is a spellbinding dining experience where 3D projections, interactive visuals, and illusions transform the table into a stage of wonder. Guests become part of a magical narrative inspired by centuries of legendary magicians. Curated by the culinary team at Andaz Singapore, the five-course menu unfolds like chapters of a story, with four enchanting variants including vegetarian and kids’ options.​

**MENU**

Book Now

# Austrian All Time Favourites

Discover Austria’s most beloved dishes through a specially curated menu. Indulge in traditional favourites, from Crispy Styrian Fried Chicken to the iconic Kaiserschmarrn, celebrating the comforting flavours and culture of Austria. In collaboration with Julius Meinl, enjoy a selection of authentic Viennese coffee specialties, thoughtfully paired to complete the Austrian experience.

Book Now

# Weekday Set Lunch

A weekday feast that celebrates local and sustainable ingredients. From Artisanal Burrata and vibrant Poke Bowls, every starter is crafted with care. Mains range from the signature Beef Burger to Wok-Fried Nasi Goreng, while sweets like Bottomless Tiramisu and Coconut Crème Brûlée complete the experience.

**MENU**

Book Now

# Thanksgiving (10% Early Bird)

Gather for a festive dinner buffet featuring tableside-shucked oysters, a turkey carving station withall the trimmings, seasonal sides and autumn-inspired desserts. Join us on 26 November 2026, 6pm–10pm;$88 per adult and $44 per child, with 10% early-bird savings.​

# Alley’s Brunch Social ​

A vibrant buffet spread of cage-free egg creations, seafood on ice, smoked salmon, DIY poke bowls and made-to-order favourites, from laksa to wok-fried dishes. Add free-flow wines or Champagne to take it up a notch. Available on weekends and selected public holidays.

**MENU**

Book Now

# Alley in A Week

Indulge in a variety of dining specials from Tuesday to Friday, with each day featuring a different theme—from a seafood-focused buffet and grilled selections to unlimited wings, pizza nights, and mains from just $2. Enjoy casual, hearty dining in a relaxed and comfortable setting.
