# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Parking

There is no complimentary carpark access as this facility is managed by DUO Tower. A 10-minute grace period is provided. Weekdays (08:00 – 17:59): $2.40 first hour, $0.80 for every subsequent 15 minutes or part thereof 18:00 – 07:59: $3.82 per entry. ## Transportation

Getting here

Singapore Changi Airport (SIN)18 kilometers18 kilometers

Transportation Options

Taxi

Metered taxi services are available at all Singapore Changi Airport terminals. The
journey to the hotel takes approximately 20–30 minutes, depending on traffic
conditions. Taxi fares typically range from SGD 25–45, inclusive of airport surcharges,
though fares may vary based on traffic conditions, route taken and applicable
surcharges.​

Ride-Hailing Services ​

Private-hire ride services such as Grab, Gojek and TADA are widely available at
Singapore Changi Airport. Pick-up points are clearly signposted at each terminal, and
the journey to the hotel typically takes 20–30 minutes, depending on traffic
conditions and demand.​

Airport Transfer Services ​

Private transfer services can be arranged for a seamless journey to and from the
airport. Available vehicle options include a Mercedes E-Class (or equivalent) from SGD
160, Mercedes S-Class (or equivalent) from SGD 250, Mercedes V-Class (or equivalent)
from SGD 160, 13-seater coach from SGD 180, 19/23-seater coach from SGD 200, and
a 45-seater coach from SGD 250. Rates are subject to prevailing taxes and availability.

Getting around

Nearest Mass Rapid Transit (MRT)​ station - Bugis (Exit E)

To access Bugis Bugis Mass Rapid Transit (MRT)​ Station (Exit E) on Basement 3, use the lifts near the reception on level 25.

Nearest bus stop - Parkview Square (01139)

Parkview Square bus stop (01139) on North Bridge Road is a 2-minute walk from Andaz Singapore.
