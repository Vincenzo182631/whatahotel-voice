### Excellent Stay in a Prime Location

We stayed at the hotel for seven nights. It is a first-class hotel in every respect, particularly when it comes to service, cleanliness, room size, and comfort.

The hotel is strategically located right next to an MRT station, with direct access to Bugis Station, making it extremely convenient to get around Singapore. There are also dozens of restaurants and cafés in the immediate vicinity.

The reception is located on the 25th floor of the DUO Towers, with the guest rooms on the floors above. We stayed on the 34th floor and enjoyed a spectacular view of Singapore, especially at night.

Our stay fully met all of our expectations, and we had an excellent experience at the hotel.

### Five-star hardware, three-star service

Stayed 8 nights in September for a business trip. The hotel itself is great — good location, nice rooms, rooftop bar — but the service didn't match five-star expectations.
Check-in was a flat "Hi, check in?" with no welcome, from a front desk associate who seemed unfamiliar with the procedures. In-room dining refused my chicken rice order at 8pm even though it was within the stated ordering hours ("that's supper time"), and on two separate orders nobody told me how long the food would take. At breakfast, my half-finished coffee was cleared while I was at the buffet, and another morning my entire table was cleared and reset during a three-minute washroom break — with my briefcase and blazer still on the seat.
I emailed the Guest Experience team about all this during my stay and never received a reply, which was the most disappointing part. Hardware is five-star; service and follow-up are not there yet.
