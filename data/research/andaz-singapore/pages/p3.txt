## Austrian Gourmet Week

Embark on a culinary journey through Austria at Andaz Singapore. From 26 October to 6 November 2026, enjoy a selection of Austrian favourites at Alley on 25, featuring beloved dishes that celebrate the country's rich culinary traditions. On 31 October and 1 November 2026, Michelin-starred Chef Lukas Nagl of Bootshaus joins 665°F for an exclusive takeover, presenting a specially curated menu that showcases his contemporary take on Austrian cuisine.

## The Magic Table

The Magic Table is a spellbinding dining experience where 3D projections, interactive visuals, and illusions transform the table into a stage of wonder. Guests become part of a magical narrative inspired by centuries of legendary magicians. Curated by the culinary team at Andaz Singapore, the five-course menu unfolds like chapters of a story, with four enchanting variants including vegetarian and kids’ options.

## Wu Yue Ting (5 ON 25)

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

At level 25 of the hotel, Wu Yue Ting offers refined Cantonese cuisine in a modern upscale setting that is a sophisticated mix of romance and nostalgia. Special signature dishes include Wu Yue Ting Cantonese-Style Roasted Cage-Free Cherry Valley Duck, Hong Kong-Style Braised Fish Soup with Black Cod, Sea Cucumber, Fish Maw, Yellow Chives and Vermicelli, and Stir-Fried Maine Lobster and Jade Tiger Abalone with Chinese Yam, Golden Fungus and Green Asparagus, alongside an array of other refined Cantonese specialties. For intimate dining sessions, our two private dining suites are ideal settings and seat eight to 12 people. Reservations are recommended.

## 665°F

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

Located on Level 38 of Andaz Singapore, 665°F features a meat-centric dining experience incorporating Southeast Asian flavours. The Cellar, an extension within the restaurant, offers a wide selection of Old and New World wines. Signature highlights include a crisp Blue Swimmer Crab Cake, Pure Black F1 Wagyu Flat Iron Steak and Kombu-Roasted South Patagonian Toothfish, alongside an array of refined starters, seafood and premium meats. Three private dining rooms seating up to 6, 8 and 12 guests are available for booking. Reservations are recommended.

## Mr. Stork

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

Nestled on the rooftop on level 39, Mr. Stork is an al fresco drinking and dining spot with 360-degree views of the city below. This sophisticated bar houses 10 teepee huts inspired by the nests built by its namesake bird, with relaxed seating amid lush tropical landscaping designed to offer the best possible views. Must-try selections include the Aaliya and Purple Wave cocktails, as well as the Mr. Stork Sharing Platter. Other expertly crafted drinks and sharing plates are also available.
