## Dining

Taste the vibrant cuisine across our beachfront dining venues at The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket. Veranda, our relaxed beachfront restaurant, offers all‑day dining with an international...See More menu, beginning with a generous buffet breakfast and continuing through leisurely lunch and dinner by the sea. ## Private Dinner

Enjoy a romantic private dining in Phuket with a breathtaking sunset view, a refined six-course sharing menu, and the soothing sound of waves at our Private Beach Sala.

### In-Room Dining

Our In-Room Dining experience is curated to create memorable moments in the comfort of your room or villa. Enjoy a peaceful breakfast, in-villa BBQ, or celebrate an occasion with dining tailored to your stay. Our Thai Beer Boat and Floating Breakfast Tray are available with advance reservation, perfect for private dining experiences in Phuket. We accommodate dietary needs upon request. Available daily, 24 hours.

### Afternoon Tea

Relax and indulge in the afternoon at our restaurants or in the comfort of your private villa, and experience The Naka Signature Afternoon Tea. Enjoy a curated selection of savoury bites, artisanal pastries, and freshly baked scones with cream and homemade jams, complemented by seasonal fruits and your choice of fine teas.

### Floating Breakfast

Awaken to a floating breakfast in your private pool villa, where luxury meets tranquility. Choose from an array of delicious breakfast options and enjoy them on a floating tray. The serene waters and stunning surroundings create an idyllic start to your day. Embrace a morning of pure indulgence and relaxation.

## The Best Sunset Bar

Zbar, our oceanfront bar in Phuket, offers a curated selection of Asian tapas and crafted cocktails. Our beachfront bar in Phuket also features spectacular sunset views, creating an elevated setting for luxury dining in Phuket.
