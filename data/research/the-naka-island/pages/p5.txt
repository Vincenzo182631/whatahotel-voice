# The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket

## Naka Explorer

Every destination inspires a thousand stories, each tied to indigenous experience that make each locale distinctive. Discover a variety of activities during your stay with us.

## Sunset Ceremony

Join us in celebrating the significant beauty of the setting sun. Sunset Ceremony is an opportunity to take a moment and admire all that nature has to offer. Embrace the vibrancy of this special evening with the narration of Naka Island, come together with your loved ones and create unforgettable memories. Available everyday 17:45 hrs. at Zbar.

## Mangrove Kayaking Program

Discover nature surrounding The Naka Island, as part of the Destination Discovery Program of the resort. Take a kayak out to explore the calming mangrove forest which serves as a precious nursery for a variety of marine species. Become familiar with the beauty of nature, benefits we receive from mangrove, as well as preservation practices we could easily perform to save the mangrove for our next generations. THB 700 net per person for private guided tour.

## Epicurean Program

Experience the unique and authentic epicurean programs of The Naka Island that evoke lasting and treasured memories of your journey. Available everyday 14.00 – 14.30 hrs. at Tonsai Restaurant

### Naka Garden Tour

Explore our resort while getting to know the indigenous fruits and herbs that call this mythical island home. Finish the tour with a taste of what is in season with a delicious mocktail. Available every Monday, Wednesday and Sunday, meet at Concierge.

### Naka Bird Watching

Immerse yourself in an island life experience, discover the “Naka Bird Watching Activity”, be introduced to more than 50 species of our feathered friends around the island and learn about conservation practices. Available every Thursday & Sunday, meet at Recreation & Leisure Center.

### Hermit Crab Destination

Calling all little beach explorers! Dive into a world of wonder with our hermit crab adventure. Let's discover, learn, and have a blast at the beach. Please contact our staff Recreation & Leisure Center for more information and reservation.

## Beginner Yoga

Invigorate your mind with a series of slow and steady postures in sync with breathing exercises that stretch the muscles and assist to stimulate blood circulation, releasing energy blocks from various parts of the body. Available every Monday, Wednesday and Friday at Multi-Purpose Sala.

## Mountain Bike Tour

Explore the island by mountain bike, learn about local culture, and take in the unique surrounding of our tropical island. Highlights include local fishing village and the rubber tree plantation. Available for rental at Recreation & Leisure Center

### Activity Schedule and Making Reservation

All activities are subject to change without prior notice. To obtain Activity Schedule, further information or to make a reservation, we kindly request that you contact us via +6676 371 400 or naka.reservations@luxurycollection.com
