## Accommodations

Admire the unique details of our authentic, tropical island-style Phuket pool villas at The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket. Amid lavish greenery lies a resort with private pool villas...See More in Phuket, along the northwestern coast of the island. Our two-bedroom villas set the tone for an incomparable stay in Thailand thanks to striking views of tropical Phuket or the crystal-clear waters of Phang Nga Bay. ### Garden View 1 Bedroom Villa

Admire the unique detailing of our authentic tropical-style seaview pool villas at The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket. Amid Thailand's lavish greenery sits our tropical private pool villa set the tone for an incomparable vacation with spectacular views of the tropical paradise.

### Sea View 1 Bedroom Villa

Awaken to breathtaking vistas of the island’s majestic scenery, including the world-renowned emerald-green Andaman Sea, from Seaview Villa Phuket. Throughout, harmonizing blue accents echo the azure sky, which is visible through oversize windows that reveal a spacious outdoor area. Comprising a private pool, covered sala, and daybed, it is an ideal place to relax with a book and refreshments from the mini bar.

### Beachfront 1 Bedroom Villa

Situated mere steps from the island’s ivory beaches and sparkling sea, Beachfront Villas offer an unrivaled stay. Beachfront Pool Villas offer the additional benefit of a private pool. Stepping through glass doors into the spacious outdoor area, just feet from the beach, a covered sala and daybed await.
