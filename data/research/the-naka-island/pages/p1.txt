# Welcome to The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket

## An enchanting luxury Phuket beach resort

Escape to a hidden paradise. The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket is tucked off the east coast of Thailand, offering unmatched privacy in the Andaman Sea as the only property on Naka...See More Yai Island. Journey by speedboat to our luxury resort in Thailand while being immersed in Phuket's surrounding nature and beaches. Unwind with a cocktail at Zbar perched above the waterfront and enjoy unexpected flavors at our three beachside restaurants. Indulge at Spa Naka by HARNN with a revitalizing massage, facial or any of our numerous wellness offerings. Explore Phuket from our private island resort with our daily activities, including Naka School Community Outreach Program and mangrove discovery. Accommodations, be it a guest room, suite, or villa, are distinct and radiant, boasting scenic views of the sea, garden, beach, or outdoor terrace. Together with our vacation packages curate an unforgettable stay.See Less

### Bike Tours

Explore the villages and jungle of Naka Island with a bike tour. This low-to medium-intensity outing includes stops at quaint fishing villages and stunning tropical scenery. Subject to availability.

### Epicurean Program

Experience the unique and authentic epicurean programs of the Naka Island, that evoke lasting and treasured memories of your journey.

### Mangrove Kayaking

Discover nature surrounding the Naka Island, as part of the Destination Discovery Program of the resort. Take a kayak out to explore the calming mangrove forest which serves as a precious nursery for a variety of marine species. Become familiar with the beauty of nature, benefits we receive from mangrove, as well as preservation practices we could easily perform to save the mangrove for our next generations.

### Weddings at Royal Horizon Pool Villa

Looking out from the hilltop over the emerald sea, bordered by a lush, landscaped garden. Our luxury hotel in Phuket seamlessly combines inspired outdoor island living with refined indoor relaxation, our gated 1,600-square-meter Royal Horizon Pool Villa features 2 bedroom Villa, Oceanfront, pool access, plunge pool, pool cabana enchants at every turn, ideal for larger families, and secluded wedding reception and exclusive groups.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket?

The check-in time at The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket.

What are the parking options at The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket?

Off-site self parking is available near The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket at Ao Po Grand Marina.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket booking.

What is the closest airport near The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket?

The closest airport near The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket is Phuket International Airport (HKT). HKT is located approximately 22.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket have an airport shuttle to Phuket International Airport (HKT)?

Yes, The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket has an airport shuttle to Phuket International Airport (HKT).
