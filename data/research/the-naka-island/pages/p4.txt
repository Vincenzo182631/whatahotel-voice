# Experience our luxury resort near Phuket Airport

## Experiences

Discover the unparalleled beauty of Thailand at our resort near the Phi Phi Islands, The Naka Island, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Phuket. Our beachfront resort on Ko Naka Yai is surrounded by pristine...See More sandy beaches and crystal-clear waters, creating a serene destination in Phuket. With activities ranging from Muay Thai lessons and Thai cooking classes to Hobie Cat sailing, paddle boarding, kayaking, and birdwatching, guests can immerse themselves in the island's natural beauty and culture. We also offer guided kayaking tours through the mangrove forests of Naka Island as part of our environmental initiatives. Experience local wildlife with a visit to Phuket Elephant Sanctuary, where elephants roam freely in a natural setting. Stay within reach of the golden sands of Patong Beach, one of Phuket's most popular destinations. For a day of adventure, take a boat trip to the iconic Phi Phi Islands or kayak around the James Bond Island in Phang Nga Bay from our Phuket pool villa resort.See Less

### Phuket Attractions & Sightseeing

Ideally located in the southern tropics of Thailand, Phuket is a tropical gem that offers a variety of attractions and sightseeing options for travelers.

### Island Hopping

Island hopping in Phuket often includes a full day of kayaking through caves, discovering secret lagoons, learning how to navigate a sea walker or even saying hello to the colorful fish and marine life below through the bottom of a glass canoe.

### Experience the Marine Life

Take part in a fishing trip and later, enjoy a BBQ on the beach on a tombolo, known in Thai as Ta Lae Waek. The Andaman Sea is full of marine life and well worth experiencing.

### Sea, Sand, Sun and Serenity

Beautiful and tranquil, Phuket’s Banana Beach is relatively unknown. This 180-metre beach is in the middle of Bang Tao and Nai Thon in the northwestern part of the island.

### Local Tradition

Although most popular for its tropical, stunning beaches, Phuket is a unique island filled with loads of culture and local tradition. Discover the real Phuket by visiting some its popular attractions and sightseeing spots while staying at our hotel near Phuket airport.
