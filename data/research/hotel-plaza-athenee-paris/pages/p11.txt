What are the spa opening hours?

Dior Spa Plaza Athénée is open daily from 11am to 7pm.

What are the gym opening hours?

The gym is open daily from 6am to 10pm.

What facilities does the spa offer?

Hôtel Plaza Athénée is home to a Dior Spa, with an array of treatments and a fully equipped gym.

Our luxury hotel spa is just a few minutes' walk from the Champs-Élysées.

Can I book spa treatments?

Yes, treatments are available daily at the Dior Spa from 11am to 7pm. Please contact us to arrange this. Our spa can be reached by email at diorspa.HPA@dorchestercollection.com and by phone number at +33 (0)1 53 67 65 35.

Is the gym suitable for an early morning workout?

Yes, the gym is open from 6am.

What time does the gym close?

The gym is open until 10pm every day.

Who runs the spa at Hôtel Plaza Athénée?

Hôtel Plaza Athénée operates the spa, in partnership with Dior.

Do I need to book spa treatments in advance?

We recommend booking ahead to secure your preferred time; please contact us to arrange this. Our spa can be reached by email at diorspa.HPA@dorchestercollection.com and by phone number at +33 (0)1 53 67 65 35.

What time does the spa open and close?

Our Dior Spa in Paris is open daily from 11am to 7pm. The gym is open from 6am to 10pm.

Is the gym open longer than the spa?

Yes. The gym is open from 6am to 10pm, while Dior Spa treatments are available from 11am to 7pm.
