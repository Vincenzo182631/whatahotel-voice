# Restaurants and Bars

## The gastronomy of Haute Couture

Hôtel Plaza Athénée is home to five restaurants and bars, headed up by executive chef Jocelyn Herland and his talented team – Victoria Boller of Le Relais Plaza, Anthony Di Prospero of our Michelin-starred restaurant, and award-winning pastry chef Angelo Musa.

Bring the family

Plaza Athénée Chalet

Immerse yourself in a magical winter world in our twinkling Cour Jardin. Inspired by cosy alpine chalets, dinner includes melted chesse pulls and crisp glasses of champagne, as well as access to our picturesque ice rink.

Michelin-starred

La Grande Table du Plaza Athénée

Discover a magnificent dining experience where grand gastronomic traditions are reinterpreted with a contemporary edge.

Cosy and intimate

La Cour Jardin

Our seasonal menu celebrates the freshness of late summer, where delicate flavours and elegant simplicity accompany each moment of the day.

Creative flair

Le Relais Plaza

Our iconic Art Deco restaurant. With its vibrant atmosphere and authentic French dishes, Le Relais Plaza brings you Parisian brasserie culture at its best.

The place to be

La Terrasse Montaigne

For a limited time, La Terrasse Montaigne becomes La Terrasse Eden in celebration of our new Italian-inspired menu, curated by chef Salvatore Bianco from our sister property in Rome, Hotel Eden.

Relaxed

Afternoon tea

A bespoke tea menu, a selection of fluffy finger sandwiches and an exquisite array of cakes. Our afternoon tea is beautifully handcrafted by our award-winning pastry chef, Angelo Musa.

Stylish setting

La Galerie

For many Parisians, La Galerie is shorthand for pure pleasure. It's the ideal spot for breakfast, a light lunch, spontaneous pre-dinner drinks or our delicious afternoon tea.

Elegant glamour

Breakfast & brunch

Breakfast means something different for everyone – but for us, it’s a moment to savour, to linger over coffee and to take your time sampling each of our exquisite pastries. Our chefs bring their passion to every dish, for a wonderful start to your day in Paris.

Vibrant buzz

Le Bar

A 21st century palace must shine with modern life and exquisite beauty. Le Bar offers all of this and more, serving carefully handcrafted cocktails alongside the finest Dom Pérignon champagnes.

Michelin-starred

Le Cabinet des Conspirateurs

Set within our Michelin-starred kitchen and behind glass doors, a supremely private dining room awaits. Le Cabinet des Conspirateurs is our culinary hideaway, where you can watch the vibrant activity of the pass from the comfort of a softly lit table.

The Chefs

The Chefs

We take our food offering seriously, which is why we only work with the best. Executive chef Jocelyn Herland leads a talented team across our restaurants and bars: Victoria Boller brings her Lyonnaise spirit to Le Relais Plaza; Anthony Di Prospero adds his distinctive flair to our Michelin-starred restaurant; and Angelo Musa creates award-winning pastries, cakes and desserts.

What restaurants are available at Hôtel Plaza Athénée?

We have Plaza Athénée Gastronomic Restaurant, Le Relais Plaza, La Galerie, La Cour Jardin, La Terrasse Montaigne, and Le Bar.

Tell me about Plaza Athénée Gastronomic Restaurant

Gastronomic RestaurantPlaza Athénée is our two-Michelin starred restaurant, serving French fine dining. It's open for dinner on Tuesday to Saturday, and for lunch on Saturday (12.30 to 2.15pm). Please note, the restaurant is closed on Sunday and Monday.

Tell me about Le Relais Plaza

Le Relais Plaza is our classic Parisian brasserie, open daily.

What does La Galerie offer?

La Galerie serves all-day dining and afternoon tea. It's open daily.

Tell me about La Cour Jardin and La Terrasse Montaigne

La Cour Jardin and La Terrasse Montaigne are our outdoor dining spaces. La Cour Jardin is set in our leafy courtyard, serving sunkissed cuisine in spring and summer. It reopens in winter to host our sparkling ice rink and chalet. La Terrasse Montaigne is set on avenue Montaigne, at the front of the hotel. It's closed during winter and reopens every spring.

When is the bar open ?

Le Bar is open daily.

What time is breakfast served and where?

Breakfast is served from 7 to 11am. Guests can choose to enjoy it at Gastronomic RestaurantPlaza Athénée, or at Le Relais Plaza.

How much does breakfast cost?

Continental breakfast – €60

American breakfast – €75

Children's breakfast (under 13) – €50

Which days is Gastronomic Restaurant Plaza Athénée open?

Our Michelin-starred restaurant is open for dinner on Tuesday to Saturday, and for lunch on Saturday (12.30 to 2.15pm). Please note, the restaurant is closed on Sunday and Monday.

Where can I have afternoon tea?

Afternoon tea is served daily at La Galerie.

Where can I get a drink?

Le Bar is open daily for drinks.
