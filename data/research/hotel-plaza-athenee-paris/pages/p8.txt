BREAKFAST

The Parisian art of breakfast

Start the day with pastries created by an award-winning pastry chef. Angelo Musa presents freshly-baked cakes, croissants and daily creations, while Le Montaigne and Le Plaza selections mean you can begin every morning exactly as you choose.

Book your breakfast

BRUNCH

Sunday brunch

Our Sunday brunch menu includes pastries, seasonal fruits, hot drinks and champagne, starters to share, an iconic French dish of your choice, and our pastry chefs’ favourite desserts, including Angelo Musa’s chocolate mousse. The meal is served with hot drinks and a glass of champagne.

Book your brunch

IN-ROOM DINING

Breakfast in bed

Let us bring breakfast to you with our wonderful in-room dining menu, featuring all our delicious options for your own bedside banquet, or al fresco breakfast on the balcony.

BREAKFAST AT LA GALERIE

The choice is yours

Breakfast at La Galerie is an occasion you can make your own. At the heart of the hotel, it’s a calm, elegant space in which to enjoy our Montaigne or Plaza menus, or sample the dishes from our full à la carte.

Explore La Galerie

FRESH FRUIT

Colourful creations

Did you know we have our own fruit chef? From colourful platters and baskets to our famous acai bowl, every incarnation offers a new discovery, each expertly prepared by the master of fruit.

OUR BAKER

Fresh from the oven

The life of our bread begins at 3am, as our two bakers knead and shape the dough to bring fresh rolls to our guests every morning. Led by our chief baker, it’s a smooth operation that requires skill, patience and above all, passion.

HEALTHY

Beautifully balanced

Our healthy selection offers light, fresh and delicious options, such as avocado toast, acai smoothie and fresh fruit.
