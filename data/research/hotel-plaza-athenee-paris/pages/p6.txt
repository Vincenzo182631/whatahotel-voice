# La Cour Jardin

The Cuisine

Sunshine on a plate

Inspired by Brittany’s salt-kissed shores, our new menu celebrates sun-drenched days with fresh, local ingredients. Drift from dish to dish and linger a little longer in our lush green courtyard.

THE VIEW

A riot of colour

Sweeping curtains of greenery envelop the courtyard, punctuated by our signature parasols in that splendid ‘Plaza red’. This is our tranquil oasis in the heart of the city.

THE DESSERT

The grand finale

Fresh, light and yet irresistibly indulgent, our desserts are worth saving room for – from the buttery Kouign-amann topped with juicy red cherries to our « Far Breton» sundae.
