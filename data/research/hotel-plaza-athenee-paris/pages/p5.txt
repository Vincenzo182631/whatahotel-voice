DESIGN

Bringing modernity to a landmark

To create a link between this sophisticated space and the courtyard beyond the window, designer Bruno Moinard blends light and decoration to create the sensation of sunshine playing across the elegant furniture and mosaic floor.

CUISINE

All-day delights

Our menus present Parisian delights at any time of day, whether you join us for freshly-baked pastries at breakfast, Angelo Musa’s signature treats at tea time, or the very best of our Parisian cuisine for lunch and dinner.

CUISINE

Angelo Musa

Executive pastry chef Angelo Musa is a winner of the World Pastry Chef award and 2007 winner of ‘Meilleur Ouvrier de France’. With a wealth of experience within France and abroad, Angelo adds his own creative and distinctive stamp to our menus.
