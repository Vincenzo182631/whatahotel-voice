Where is Hôtel Plaza Athénée located?

Our luxury hotel is at 25 avenue Montaigne, 75008 Paris. It's within walking distance of the Eiffel Tower and Champs-Élysées.

What landmarks are near the hotel?

We're just a few steps from the Eiffel Tower, the Seine and the Champs-Élysées

How far is the hotel from the Eurostar?

The Eurostar station, Gare du Nord, is just 20 minutes away from our five-star hotel near the Eiffel Tower.

How do I contact the reservations office?

You can reach our reservations office on +33 187 164 460.

Is there a minimum size for a group booking?

Group booking terms apply from 10 or more rooms.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

Yes, we welcome up to two small dogs (under 7 kg/15 lbs) per room, at €40 per day to cover deep cleaning. We'll provide a bowl, bed and treats.

What is the parking fee?

Parking is complimentary in front of the hotel, or €45 per day in our underground parking facility.

What facilities does the hotel offer?

We have the Dior Spa with a gym, plus several acclaimed restaurants, a brasserie, all-day dining and a bar.

Is the hotel close to the Champs-Élysées?

Yes, the Champs-Élysées is within walking distance, along with the Eiffel Tower and the Seine

What makes Hôtel Plaza Athénée distinctive?

True to its extraordinary city, it's no ordinary hotel; here on prestigious avenue Montaigne, guests discover the very best of Paris.

Is the hotel well placed for shopping on avenue Montaigne?

Absolutely. We sit right on avenue Montaigne, one of Paris's most prestigious addresses for fashion and luxury.
