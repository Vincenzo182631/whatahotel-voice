ALL-DAY DINING

Feast on sunshine

Light, fresh cuisine, delivered with pure Parisian style. From the refined simplicity of beef tartare and our Croque-Plaza to our signature Wagyu Smash burger, our all-day dining menu promises to delight every palate.

THE VIEW

The centre of fashion

The perfect vantage point from which to admire the endless catwalk that is avenue Montaigne. Dedicated to luxury, this avenue hosts flagships of high fashion such as number 30, the historic building where Dior was founded in 1946.
