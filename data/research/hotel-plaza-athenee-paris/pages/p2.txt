What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is at 12pm.

Can I arrange early check-in or late check-out?

Early check-in or late check-out can be guaranteed for up to one hour before/after standard times. Check-outs after 4pm will be charged an additional night's stay. Please note that late check-outs are subject to availability, and it's unlikely that we will be able to accommodate them during major events or for high-demand units. If this is something you require, we suggest including an extra night in your stay on booking.

How much does an extra bed cost?

An extra bed for guests aged 13 and up is €105. An extra bed for children younger than 13 is complimentary.

How do I make a reservation?

Call our reservations office on +33 187 164 460.

How many rooms qualify as a group booking?

Bookings of 10 or more rooms are treated as group reservations.

Can I bring my dog to the room?

Yes, our luxury Paris hotel is pet-friendly. We welcome up to two small dogs per room (under 7kg/15lbs) at €40 per day.

Is breakfast included with my room?

Breakfast is generally charged separately (from €60 per person); please check your specific rate for inclusions.

Can I add an extra night beyond my booking?

Additional nights can be arranged at the daily rate. Please note that additional nights are subject to availability, and we may not be able to accommodate them during major events or for high-demand units.

Is parking included with my stay?

Parking is complimentary in front of the hotel, or €45 per day in our underground parking facility

Is an extra bed available for younger children?

Yes, an extra bed can be arranged for guests aged 13 and up. For younger children, we can arrange a complimentary cot.

What time do I need to check out by?

Check-out is at 12pm (noon); late check-out is guaranteed for up to one hour after, subject to conditions..
