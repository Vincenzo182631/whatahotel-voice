# Seteais of astonishment where nature is poetry

A palace for the most romantic. A mountain range for the adventurous. A heritage for Humanity. A place for everyone. The Valverde Sintra Palácio de Seteais evokes a chapter in the History of Portugal, of the parties in the courts, of the eccentricities of the aristocracy. Wrapped in an atmosphere of refinement and elegance, wants each guest to feel unique and special and that each experience is a surprise.

An intimate and exclusive experience with actors, centered on the private chambers of Queen D. Amélia. A different way to experience Pena.

In this staged visit, the Pena National Palace becomes the setting for a journey where history and live theatre intertwine, revealing the life, sensitivity, and actions of Queen Amélie of Orléans—in a close, immersive format carefully designed for a small group. It was at Pena Palace that D. Amélia received the news of the Proclamation of the Portuguese Republic. It was also here that she found refuge, surrounded by the art and nature of Sintra.

This visit brings that atmosphere back to life—not as an external explanation, but as a presence, where memory unfolds within the space itself.
