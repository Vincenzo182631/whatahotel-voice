### Royal Suite

Located on the first floor of the Palace’s historic wing, the Royal Suite is distinguished by its 50 sqm and the atmosphere of timeless elegance that defines this unique space. Its décor, inspired by the 18th and 19th centuries, is enhanced by Dona Maria-style furniture, in harmony with the building’s historic character. Featuring a french balcony, a living room, an open-plan office, a relaxation area, and a balcony, this suite offers a spacious and distinguished experience, complemented by views over the pool and the Palace gardens.

### Signature Suite

Located on the first floor of the Palace, the Signature Suites offer stunning views over the swimming pool or the Sintra valley, extending all the way to the Atlantic Ocean.\\
\\
Inspired by 18th and 19th century décor, they preserve the historic character of the building and stand out for their generous spaces and sophisticated details.\\
\\
Some of these suites have welcomed distinguished guests, including Eça de Queiroz and Agatha Christie.\\
\\
Each suite features a spacious marble bathroom equipped with both a separate bathtub and walk-in shower.

### Premium Room

Located on the first and second floors of the Palace, the Deluxe Rooms stand out for their generous space, elegant décor, and views over the hotel gardens or the pool. Available with either a double bed or twin beds, they offer a comfortable setting in harmony with the historic character of the property. They feature a fully equipped bathroom with either a bathtub or a shower, as well as a range of amenities designed to ensure a comfortable stay in a distinguished setting.
