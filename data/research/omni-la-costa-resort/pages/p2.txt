## Carlsbad, California

### Live the California lifestyle at our renovated resort near San Diego

Tucked inside a Spanish Mission-style property north of San Diego, 400-acre Omni La Costa Resort & Spa recently completed a multi-million dollar renovation in the spring of 2024 and welcomes guests with championship golf courses, eight sparkling resort pools and an award-winning spa. Our resort in Carlsbad, CA offers over 600 guest rooms, suites and villas that are welcoming retreats to unwind following resort activities or days exploring San Diego’s popular attractions including Legoland, the San Diego Zoo and Birch Aquarium. Share fresh southern California flavors overlooking fairways or feast on steak and seafood at our resort’s restaurants. Whether visiting for a meeting, celebration or well-earned vacation, or immersing yourself in the La Costa lifestyle with a year-round membership to The Club at La Costa, our Carlsbad, California resort promises warm, friendly service, and memories to be cherished for a lifetime.

## Traveler's Insights

### What time check-in and check-out?

Check-in begins at 4 PM If you arrive prior to 4 PM, you may check your luggage with the bell desk and enjoy our restaurants, golf courses, resort shops and pool areas until your room becomes available.

The person whose name is on the reservation must be at least 18 years of age to check into the resort. Photo identification is required for check-in.

- Check-in is at 4 PM
- Check-out is at 11 AM

### Is the resort pet friendly?

Yes, Omni La Costa Resort & Spa is a pet friendly resort for dogs. There is a one-time non-refundable pet fee of $75 per reservation for up to 2 pets. Service animals accompanying guests with disabilities are always welcome and will not be required to pay the one-time pet charge (no documentation necessary). Read more of our resort policies.

### WHERE IS OMNI LA COSTA RESORT & SPA LOCATED?

Omni La Costa Resort & Spa is located in Carlsbad, CA. The resort's physical address is 2100 Costa Del Mar Road, Carlsbad, California, 92009

### WHAT IS THE CLOSEST AIRPORT OMNI LA COSTA RESORT & SPA?

Omni La Costa Resort & Spa is near the following airports:

- About 3-4 miles from McClellan Palomar Airport (CLD)
- About 30-32 miles from San Diego International Airport (SAN)
- About 61 miles from John Wayne Airport (SNA)

### How much is parking at the resort?

Omni La Costa Resort & Spa’s daily parking charge is $45 per vehicle per night for self parking and $68 per night for valet. Please note valet parking is available at a discount when purchased at time of booking.

### What is included in the Omni La Costa Resort & Spa resort fee?

A daily resort charge of $55 is added to the daily room rate to give you access to the many resort amenities and services without incurring charges throughout your stay. The resort charge gives guests access to:

Golf bag storage

Poolside amenities including towels, lounge chairs, lifeguard supervision and access to toys

Priority access to tennis, golf, cabana and Kidtopia reservations

24/7 on-property shuttle access

Many more amenities

Visit the resort policies page for a full list of inclusions.
