### Staffing issues and item stolen from room

14 days ago

I have stayed several times at Intercontinental Buckhead (4 days in August) and loved it--from the service and wonderful staff to the fantastic food and clean rooms! However, my recent stay was horrible. I used my IHG points on Friday, Oct. 9; Saturday, Oct. 10; Sunday, October 11; and paid for October 12. When I checked in, I made sure to let the desk know it was the same two people, so we could keep the room and not have to change rooms over the four nights. We also paid for club lounge access for 4 days. I didn't mind having to renew my room card access on Saturday, but I then had to renew every night. Every single time I renewed, I would realize the club access was not added (despite copious notes) and me mentioning lounge access. Yes, there as a problem with the readers in the elevators and some elevators didn't work. But, I would have to go downstairs numerous times to get the club access rekeyed to the card. We would use the club, and then 3 hrs later we would not have access. Then I would get it rekeyed, and access was denied again later. The hotel was busy with 2 weddings, so there was not enough staff to handle the front desk or concierge. On Sunday we came back to our room and discovered an Anker charging plug and cord (USB-C 6ft) were missing. I had an extra battery plugged into the plug and into the bedside wall outlet. The battery was on the floor. So, someone unplugged the battery and took the cord and plug. We also noticed a dirty sheet piled on one of the room chairs. I immediately reported this to the concierge AND the front desk who claimed they would investigate. We did not hear back and kept checking at front desk and no one had any word. It was a problem as I had no way to charge my phone, Ipad or the battery. I had to buy new ones ($40). Monday, our room wasn't cleaned and by the evening when we told the manager she said she didn't know about the missed cleaning or the missing cord. The concierge and other managers did not let her know or didn't report it. Looking at our reservation, she said it looked like we checked out--even though we checked with the desk that morning to renew the key cards and to re check-in. She said the maids probably took the cord thinking we left it-although our clothes and personal items were all over the room. I am not sure why this makes sense to the manager, but more importantly I was offered no apology, no reimbursement, and no solution. The hotel was busy (although not on Sunday night or Monday), but the lack of service this time was noticeable. I also had a hard time understanding a manager condoning staff stealing from a room for whatever reason. Plus no apology? I also have a reservation at IHG Buckhead in November (two rooms) and I am considering canceling the reservation for another hotel.

### Disappointing Stay for a Luxury Hotel

a month ago

We chose the InterContinental Buckhead Atlanta expecting the level of service and attention to detail associated with a luxury hotel, but unfortunately our experience fell well short. Our room had several issues: the toilet only flushed if the lever was held down throughout the entire flush, the mini-fridge was not cold, the minibar/snack area was not fully stocked, and although the reservation was for two guests, only one robe and one pair of slippers were provided. Room service was also disappointing. My checkout screen showed approximately $32 including the service fee, but the paperwork presented at delivery showed approximately $42 before an additional tip, with no explanation for the difference. The burger was overcooked and arrived with ingredients that were not listed on the menu. Getting assistance was consistently difficult. Calls to the front desk and concierge frequently went unanswered, and an email to the concierge received no response. The worst interaction occurred at checkout. After requesting a luggage cart and explaining that we only needed the cart because we would load it ourselves, an employee told us, “We have 300 people leaving today, we don’t have time for that. You can call when you’re ready.” We followed those instructions and called again when ready, but no one ever came. We ultimately carried all of our luggage downstairs ourselves. None of these issues individually would have defined the entire stay. It was the accumulation of maintenance problems, inconsistent service, difficulty reaching staff, and the dismissive checkout experience that made this stay so disappointing. I contacted the hotel directly regarding these concerns and have still not received a response from management.

No, I do not recommend this hotel.
