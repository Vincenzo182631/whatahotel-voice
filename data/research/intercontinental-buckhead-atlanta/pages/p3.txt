### Meet the Chef

Inspired by his Italian heritage, Chef Conant’s restaurants are an award-winning blend of old-world hospitality and outstanding cuisine. James Beard Award winner, Food & Wine Best New Chef and Food Network personality, Chef Conant's career spans over 35 years with acclaimed restaurants, television shows and cookbooks.

### The Americano

The Americano is a bold and contemporary expression of Italian cuisine, a restaurant and bar where soulful dishes tell a story of place, season, and connection. Here, our menu is shaped by traditions of Italy and the voice of Chef Scott Conant, reinterpreted through the lens of his passion and culinary journey. This is not Italian-American, it’s forward-looking cuisine with intention, anchored in craft and delivered with the warmth and elegance that define true hospitality. A spirited bar program offers inventive cocktails that nod to Italy while embracing modern technique, joined by a focused, thoughtfully curated wine list that pairs classic bottlings alongside esoteric delights.
