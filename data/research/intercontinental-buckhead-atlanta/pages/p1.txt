Inspired by his Italian heritage, Chef Conant’s restaurants are an award-winning blend of old-world hospitality and outstanding cuisine. James Beard Award winner, Food & Wine Best New Chef and Food Network personality, Chef Conant's career spans over 35 years with acclaimed restaurants, television shows and cookbooks.

## Featured Artists

### Niki Zarrabi

The captivating works of Niki Zarrabi, an Atlanta-based mixed media artist, adorn the walls of the Great Hall. A visual expression of our luxury Buckhead Atlanta hotel'ssophistication and elegance, as well as our love of the community, her twin murals quite fittingly feature Georgia’s state flower, the Cherokee Rose.

### Heather Evans Smith

Heather Evans Smith is a photo-based artist whose work reflects her Southern roots, motherhood, womanhood, and a whimsical imagination she relied on as an only child in a rural town. Heather resides with her family in Chapel Hill, North Carolina.

How much is parking at InterContinental Buckhead Atlanta?

At InterContinental Buckhead Atlanta, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 49 USD and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 62 USD .

Make the most of our secure underground parking deck. Daily self-parking rates are USD 22 for 0–2 hours, USD 28 for 2–4 hours, USD 35 for 4–8 hours, and USD 49 for 8–24 hours. Overnight self-parking is USD 49 per night. Valet parking is also available at

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Buckhead Atlanta?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Buckhead Atlanta.

Pet policy description. Maximum two dogs per room and no more than 45lbs. each. A 125USD deep cleaning fee will be applied to your stay. Dogs must be kept on leash when in hotel public spaces and cannot be left unattended in your room unless in a cage or throughout the hotel.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Buckhead Atlanta?

Check-in at InterContinental Buckhead Atlanta is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is InterContinental Buckhead Atlanta from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Buckhead Atlanta is conveniently located near Hartsfield Jackson International Airport (ATL), approximately 19 miles away (30.58 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
