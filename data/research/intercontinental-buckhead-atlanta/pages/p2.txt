# InterContinental Buckhead AtlantaRooms & suites

Echoing a sophisticated aesthetic and modern style with luxurious textural elements and soothing accents, our newly reimagined Buckhead, Atlanta rooms and suites are a beautiful balance of timeless design and contemporary elegance. Enjoy the serene setting, adorned with designer furniture and monogrammed motifs inspired by Atlanta’s architecture. High-speed internet keeps you connected, while premium TV channels and a luxurious soaking tub make it easy to unwind at the end of the day.
