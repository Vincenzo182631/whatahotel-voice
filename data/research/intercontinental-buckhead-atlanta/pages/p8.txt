# InterContinental Buckhead AtlantaDestination

Vibrant and upscale, Buckhead's tree-lined streets offer luxury shopping, acclaimed dining, and premier attractions. One block from MARTA with complimentary house car service within two miles, the hotel provides access to Buckhead Village, Lenox Square.

### Experience Buckhead

Whether staying in or heading out, we have an experience around every corner. Atlanta offers a dynamic culture and a variety of fun things to do at Intercontinental Buckhead.

### Sports & Entertainment

Experience the excitement of Atlanta, a city where world‑class sports and unforgettable entertainment create energy in every season. From championship‑level games to iconic music venues and dazzling nightlife, Atlanta offers something for every fan and every traveler.

### Arts, Culture & Shopping

Discover the vibrant spirit of Atlanta, a city where creativity thrives and culture runs deep. From world‑renowned museums to groundbreaking music venues and street art that turns every corner into a gallery, Atlanta offers an artistic energy unlike anywhere else. And for those who love to shop, the city delivers—from luxury boutiques and iconic malls to locally owned markets filled with handcrafted treasures, every neighborhood invites you to explore, indulge, and find something uniquely Atlanta.
