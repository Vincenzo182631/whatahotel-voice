# A chic beach resort in Athens

#### Pet Policy at One&Only Aesthesis

- Pets must weigh less than 20 kg
- Pets are to be kept on a leash at all times around the resort, unless you are in your accommodation.
- Please do not leave your pet unattended. Should you require a pet sitter, please contact your host or Guest Experiences.
- Pets are not permitted in our restaurants, spa, fitness center, beach or pool areas. This does not apply to guide or service dogs.
- Guests are responsible for cleaning up after their pets throughout the resort. Please contact your host if you require any assistance with supplies.
- Any excessive noise, such as barking, must be curtailed to ensure fellow guests are not inconvenienced, both in guest villas and in public areas of the resort.
- Please contact your host or housekeeping to arrange a convenient time for servicing your bungalow/villa, as the owner/guest must be present if your pet is inside the villa.
- The resort will be obliged to charge for any damages caused by your pet to resort property. The owner/guest further agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the resort, its owners and its operator from all liability and damage suffered as a result of your pet

#### Pet fees

- €100.00 EUR charge per pet, per day will be applied on your account

### Frequently Asked Questions

Useful details you need to know before you travel to One&Only Aesthesis

#### What are the passport and visa requirements for visiting Greece?

All visitors to Greece must present a passport valid for at least six months from the date of travel. Visa requirements for entering Greece vary significantly from country to country. Click here for more information.

#### What is the weather like in Greece?

Greece has a Mediterranean climate, with hot, dry summers. Winters on the Athenian Riviera are refreshingly mild; thanks to warm temperatures, you can still swim in the sea in early–mid October.

#### How can I book transfers to One&Only Aesthesis?

One&Only Aesthesis is a 30-minute drive from the city centre or from Athens International Airport. The flight to Athens takes 3.25 hrs from London, 2.25 hrs from Zurich, 3.5 hrs from Moscow and 5.5 hrs from Dubai. The boat trip from One&Only Kéa Island takes 45 minutes.

#### What languages are spoken in Greece?

Greek is the official language, but English is also widely spoken.

#### What internet services are available at One&Only Aesthesis?

Complimentary high-speed WiFi is available throughout the resort.

#### What is the local currency in Greece?

The Euro is the official currency of Greece

#### What is the electrical voltage at One&Only Aesthesis?

Electricity is supplied at 230 volts. Power outlets are Type C (Euro) and F (Schuko). There are USB sockets located on the desk and near the bed.

#### What is the time zone in Greece?

Greece is GMT/UTC + 2h during Standard Time, and GMT/UTC + 3h during Daylight Saving Time.

#### What are the check-in and check-out times at One&Only Aesthesis?

Check-in is at 3pm, local time; check-out is at 12pm, local time.
