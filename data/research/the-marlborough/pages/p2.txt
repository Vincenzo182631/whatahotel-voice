# ACTIVITIES

Marlborough is at the top of New Zealand's South Island and is a treasure trove of food, wine, arts, adventure, culture and scenery just waiting to be explored and The Marlborough sits right in the heart of it all.

​

See what appeals to you and we can help you make the most of your stay with us. Self drive, guided private or with a group let us organise the best options with one of our excellent partners. Alternatively, if you wish to stay close to home, there is plenty happening on site everyday within our 16 acre estate or of course you could simply do nothing at all.

## ON-SITE EXPERIENCES

## GARDEN & VINEYARD  WALKING TOUR  PROPERTY WITH   OUR GARDENER

16 acres of sprawling gardens and a working vineyard, means there is plenty to explore whilst you are at The Marlborough. The  gardens have over 400 different native and exotic trees and shrubs that meander down to a spring fed creek that flows through the estate.  This secluded oasis provides a sanctuary for the abundant birdlife.  The estates working vineyards provide us with the Merlot, Malbec, Riesling & of course the world renown Sauvignon Blanc which can be found at Harvest restaurant or in our hotel rooms.  Our 1 acre of organic vegetables feeds directly into our Harvest Restaurant.

9am each morning or Private Tour on request at additional cost

Enjoy a tour of your favourites or let us assist you with either a Private, Group tour by vehicle or a Cycling tour.
