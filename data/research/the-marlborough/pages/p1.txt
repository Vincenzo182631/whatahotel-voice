# EXPERIENCE LUXURY IN THE MARLBOROUGH VINEYARDS

Welcome to The Marlborough, where tranquility and contemporary design combine to create relaxed elegance. Our boutique hotel in the heart of Marlborough vineyards offers a unique and intimate accommodation experience. Whether you're seeking a romantic getaway or a relaxed family gathering, our small luxury hotel in the midst of a working vineyard provides the perfect setting to create cherished memories. Indulge in spacious, elegantly designed guest rooms, and savour exquisite food and wine experiences surrounded by breathtaking beauty on 16 acre estate.

Main

## DISCOVER OUR PRIME LOCATION

Situated on 16 acres of lush gardens and a working vineyard, our estate is a mere 10 minutes from Blenheim airport and 15 minutes from the town of Blenheim. Located at 776 Rapaura Road, we provide easy access to State Highway 1 and State Highway 6, connecting you to Christchurch and Nelson. Immerse yourself in the tranquility of Marlborough vineyards while remaining conveniently close to key transport routes.

## INDULGE IN LUXURY

Unwind in one of our ten contemporary luxury rooms, each offering a private haven with spacious ensuite and balcony access, allowing you to bask in the stunning garden and vineyard views. Our elegant rooms provide the perfect retreat for relaxation and rejuvenation, ensuring a truly luxurious experience during your stay at The Marlborough.

## EMBRACE HISTORY

Immerse yourself in the rich history of The Marlborough. Originally built in 1901 for the Sisters of Mercy, our historic Victorian Convent has been carefully preserved to retain its timeless elegance. Discover the captivating story of the Convent's relocation to its present location in Rapaura, Marlborough, and experience the unique blend of heritage and contemporary design.

## CULINARY EXCELLENCE

Savour the finest dining experience at Harvest, our onsite restaurant. Indulge in a culinary journey that celebrates seasonal gourmet produce from the region and beyond, complemented by unique flavors from our Mibrasa charcoal oven. Our commitment to sustainability and locally sourced ingredients ensures a dining experience that is as environmentally conscious as it is delicious.
