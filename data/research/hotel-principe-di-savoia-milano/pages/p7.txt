What are the Club 10 spa opening hours?

Club 10 Fitness & Beauty Center is open every day. From Monday to Friday, it's open 6am to 10.30pm, and on Saturday to Sunday it's open 7am to 10pm.

What facilities does Club 10 offer?

Club 10 Fitness & Beauty Center features an indoor pool, hydro massage room, steam room, sauna, outdoor terrace, gym and spa.

When is the indoor pool open?

The hotel's indoor pool in Milan is open every day, and follows the same timings as the rest of Club 10. From Monday to Friday, it's open 6am to 10.30pm, and on Saturday to Sunday it's open 7am to 10pm.

Are there specific pool time slots?

No, guests are able to use the pool at any time during opening hours. From Monday to Friday, it's open 6am to 10.30pm, and on Saturday to Sunday it's open 7am to 10pm.

Can children use the pool?

Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult to use the pool.

Is there an age restriction for the gym?

Yes, children are not allowed in the gym until the age of 18.

Can children use the steam room and sauna?

No, children are not allowed in the steam room or sauna until the age of 18.

Can children use the jacuzzi?

Children are not allowed in the jacuzzi until the age of 16.

Is there a spa at the hotel?

Yes, the spa is part of the Club 10 Fitness & Beauty Center, alongside the hydro massage room, sauna and steam room.

Is there an outdoor space at the fitness centre?

Yes, Club 10 includes an outdoor terrace for guests to enjoy.
