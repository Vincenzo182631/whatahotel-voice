What dining venues are available at the hotel?

We have Il Salotto, Acanto Restaurant, and the Principe Bar.

Tell me about Il Salotto.

Il Salotto is our all-day dining restaurant, open daily from 7am to 11pm.

Tell me about Acanto Restaurant, Milan.

Acanto is our fine dining restaurant. It's open daily from 6.30 to 11am (breakfast), 12.30 to 2.30pm (lunch), and 7.30 to 10.30pm (dinner).

What are the hours for Principe Bar, Milan?

Principe Bar is open daily from 6pm to 1.30am, serving cocktails, drinks, and bar bites.

What time is breakfast served and where?

Breakfast is served at Acanto Restaurant. From Monday to Friday, it's available from 6.30 to 11am, and on Saturday and Sunday it's available 7 to 11am.

How much does breakfast cost?

Breakfast is €55 per person per day, €35 for children under 10.

Is there a casual daytime dining option?

Yes, Il Salotto is open daily for relaxed dining.

Where can I get an evening drink?

Principe Bar is open daily from 6pm to 1.30am, serving cocktails, drinks, and bar bites.

Is the main restaurant open every day?

Yes, Acanto Restaurant is open every day.

Is breakfast cheaper for children?

Yes, breakfast is €35 for children (under 10 years old).

Where do in-house guests have breakfast?

Breakfast for guests is served at Acanto Restaurant.
