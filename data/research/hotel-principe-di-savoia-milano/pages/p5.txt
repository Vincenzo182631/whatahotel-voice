THE COCKTAILS

Choices, choices…

THE EXPERIENCE

Creative flair

Board game nights

Every Monday and Tuesday, Principe Bar hosts a night of board games alongside signature cocktails. Play timeless classics like chess, checkers and backgammon with friends and family, as you enjoy your favourite drinks.

A Milanese creation

We’ve distilled the vibrant city of Milan into our own wonderfully aromatic gin, made by Cillario & Marazzi Spirits Co. using the finest artisanal methods. Its delicate notes of lime, honey, rosemary and ripe blackberry are best appreciated when served on the rocks or mixed into one of our signature cocktails.
