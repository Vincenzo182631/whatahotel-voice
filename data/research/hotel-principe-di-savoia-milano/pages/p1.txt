Where is the hotel located?

Our luxury hotel is at Piazza della Repubblica 17, 20124 Milan, in the centre of Porta Nuova.

How close is the nearest underground station?

We're a 2-minute walk from the Repubblica metro station.

How far is the hotel from Milan's main train station?

Our five-star hotel is 10 minutes from Milan's main train station, Stazione Centrale.

How far is the hotel from the airports?

Milan is served by three airports: Linate (20 minutes away), and Malpensa and Orio al Serio (both around 45 minutes away).

How do I contact the reservations office?

You can reach our reservations office on +39 02 9138 7010.

Is there a minimum size for a group booking?

Group bookings apply from 10 or more rooms.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

Yes, up to two small dogs per room (under 7kg/15lbs) at €35 per day to cover deep cleaning. We provide a bowl, bed and treats.

What is the parking fee?

Parking is from €70 per day depending on the size of the vehicle.

What facilities does the hotel offer?

We have the Club 10 Fitness & Beauty Center, featuring an indoor pool, hydro massage room, steam room, sauna, outdoor terrace, gym and spa, plus restaurants and a bar.

Is complimentary transport available?

Yes, complimentary transport to the centre is available for guests. This is a regular shuttle service in a Mercedes-Benz Viano – if you'd like to use it, ask at the concierge desk for an up-to-date timetable.

Is the hotel well placed for getting around Milan?

Absolutely, we're near Porta Nuova district and Brera district, two minutes from Repubblica metro station and 10 minutes from Stazione Centrale Milan.
