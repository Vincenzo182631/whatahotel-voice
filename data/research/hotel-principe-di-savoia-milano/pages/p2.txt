What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is at 12pm (noon).

Can I arrange early check-in or late check-out?

Early check-in or late check-out can be requested up to 1 hour before/after standard times. Late check-out (until 4pm) is chargeable at 50% of the daily rate, subject to availability. Blackout dates apply.

What if I need an even earlier arrival or later departure?

Check-outs after 4pm will be charged an additional night's stay. Please note that late check-outs are subject to availability, and it's unlikely that we will be able to accommodate them during major events or for high-demand units. If this is something you require, we suggest including an extra night in your stay on booking.

How much does an extra bed cost?

Additional cots or beds for children under 12 are complimentary, and we can arrange an extra bed in your room or suite for €100 per night (for guests from 13 years of age). Not available for some categories.

How do I make a reservation?

To reserve Hotel Principe di Savoia rooms or luxury suites in Milan city centre, you can book directly through our website or by calling us on +39 02 9138 7010.

How many rooms qualify as a group booking?

Bookings of 10 or more rooms are treated as group reservations.

Can I bring my dog to the room?

Yes, up to two small dogs per room (under 7kg/15lbs) at €35 per day. We provide a bowl, bed and treats.

Is breakfast included with my room?

Breakfast is charged separately (€55 per person per day); please check your specific rate for inclusions.

What does parking cost during my stay?

Parking is from €70 per day depending on the size of the vehicle.

Is an extra bed available for younger children?

Additional cots or beds for children under 12 are complimentary, and we can arrange an extra bed in your room or suite for €100 per night (for guests from 13 years of age). Not available for some categories.

What time do I need to check out by?

Check-out is at 12pm (noon); late check-out until 4pm is available at 50% of the daily rate, subject to conditions.
