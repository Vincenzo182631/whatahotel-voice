Our event spaces

Explore our event spaces

Here we offer a collection of utterly beautiful rooms and versatile spaces in which to celebrate, work and enjoy our warm welcome and experienced event planning.

Veranda

An effortlessly elegant space where crystal chandeliers and a high frescoed ceiling lend sparkle and glamour to your celebration. Step out through French doors to discover a beautiful Italian garden, ideal for an aperitivo party and after-dinner drinks.

Explore Veranda

Galilei

A wonderful room flooded with natural daylight and Milanese charm. Discreet sliding doors subdivide this ornate space to suit your individual size requirements.

explore Galilei

Cristalli

The sky-blue, frescoed ceiling makes a dramatic statement as you enter this dazzling room. Suitable for any event from a glamorous wedding to a Fashion Week exhibition, this is Italian style at its height.

Explore Cristalli

Marconi

To ensure the right impression from the first moment, Marconi makes its mark with a sense of grandeur. This flexible space is a great meeting room or hospitality lounge.

Explore marconi

Marco Polo

The elegance of frescoed ceilings combines with the fresh ambience of natural light. Our bespoke movable doors ensure a space that is tailormade to your exact needs.

Vespucci

Perfect for small meetings or a private office, these two elegant rooms are very close and can be used separately. Located discreetly tucked away for ultimate privacy. This creates the ideal venue for one-to-one business meetings, private office or small gathering.

explore vespucci

Cartesio

Beautifully light and equally comfortable for business or social events, Cartesio is a popular setting for break-outs and small receptions.

explore cartesio

Club Principe & Foyer

Positioned away from the other event spaces, Club Principe is ideal for any event where an extra element of exclusivity is crucial.
