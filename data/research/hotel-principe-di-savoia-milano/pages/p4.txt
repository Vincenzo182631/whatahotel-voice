All-day dining

Il Salotto is open for dining all day. This is the place to indulge – in a healthy breakfast, pastas and pizzas, a sweet treat, or simply a perfect cup of coffee – while watching the world pass by. Its cosy atmosphere makes this much-loved spot a favourite with Milanese locals and hotel guests alike.

Afternoon tea

We’ve given afternoon tea an entirely Milanese twist, where local delicacies fit perfectly into a delectable experience of scents and flavours. Skilfully prepared, this is a real treat to savour at your leisure.

Sweet delights

From Italian classics to international treats created by our talented pastry chef, accompanied by lots of coffee and our famous hot chocolate, our sweet offerings draw guests back time and time again.
