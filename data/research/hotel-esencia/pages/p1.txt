Originally the private home of an Italian duchess, Hotel Esencia is a place where the traditions of the past blend with the cosmopolitan art of living well today. This secluded oasis sits where the Yucatán jungle dissolves into the pristine sands of the Xpu-Ha bay, one of the remaining undeveloped shores of the Mayan Riviera.

DIVE INTO ESENCIA

In 2026, for the third year in a row, Hotel Esencia was the only hotel in the Riviera Maya to be awarded Michelin Guide’s highly coveted Three Michelin Keys. Hotel Esencia was also honored this year for the third time by The 50 Best Hotels academy. We are extremely honored and proud that our team has been awarded such recognitions.

Hotel Esencia is also the first and only independent hotel in Mexico to be awarded The Forbes Travel Guide Five Stars, five consecutive years, in 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026.
