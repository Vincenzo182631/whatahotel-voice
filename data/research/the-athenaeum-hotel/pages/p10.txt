# Weekend brunches

A perfect London weekend

Make a reservation

## Brunch with a twist

Ease into the weekend with one of our much loved brunches at The Athenaeum Hotel & Residences. This is your invitation to slow down, settle in and savour a Saturday or Sunday shaped around great food and relaxed Mayfair charm.

### Weekends at your pace

Take your seat in our cosy, inviting restaurant and ease into the weekend. It is an effortlessly uplifting way to begin the day, catch up with friends and brunch unfolds at an unhurried pace. Just £39 per person for three-courses.

Available on selected dates between 12:30pm - 3:30pm.

- April: 18th, 19th, 25th, and 26th

Book brunch

### Seasonal comforts

Your brunch is built around a hearty, warming menu, thoughtfully prepared with fresh, seasonal ingredients. Each dish is designed to nourish and satisfy, giving you the perfect start before heading out to explore Mayfair or enjoy a leisurely afternoon in the city.

Three-course brunch £39 per person.

View our brunch menu

Book brunch

### Celebrate the weekend

If you feel like turning brunch into a celebration, our bottomless option adds a little extra sparkle. For just £25 per person, enjoy 90 minutes of Mimosas, Prosecco or Bloody Marys from our drinks station, alongside wine served throughout your brunch. It's a joyful way to toast the weekend and set yourself up for a fun filled afternoon in London.
