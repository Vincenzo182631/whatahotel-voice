# Eat and Drink at the Athenaeum

Seasonal menus championing the very best  local, sustainable produce

## Food and Drink, Your Way

This is where you can eat and drink in the way that suits you best. Join us at 116 at The Athenaeum for seasonal British dishes, settle into the Bar for a well chosen drink, step out onto the terrace or get comfortably with in room dining.

Start the day with breakfast, pause for afternoon tea, linger over dinner or enjoy something simple in your room. However you choose to spend your time, the setting is always calm, the service thoughtful and the pace entirely yours.

## Menus to suit your stay

Our menus are designed to suit whatever you’re in the mood for. Our chefs are always happy to adjust dishes to suit your diet, and if you’re staying a while and craving something specific, just let us know. Celebrating something special? We’d love to help make it memorable.

Browse our menus to get started, and let us know if there’s anything we can arrange.

## 116 at The Athenaeum

116 at The Athenaeum is a contemporary British restaurant in the heart of Mayfair, where you can enjoy seasonal, locally sourced dishes in a relaxed yet stylish setting. Dine throughout the day, enjoy a signature afternoon tea, and explore a menu that evolves with the seasons.

Find out more Book a table

## Signature afternoon tea

Enjoy our signature _Fancies & Foliage_ afternoon tea, inspired by our living wall and the natural beauty of the nearby Royal Parks. Discover a seasonal selection of delicate sandwiches, freshly baked scones and beautifully crafted sweets, served in calm, elegant surroundings. A relaxed and delightful way to spend an afternoon in Mayfair.

## FAQs

Open for answers to everything you need to know

What are the opening times for 116 at The Athenaeum?

Our restaurant, 116, is open seven days a week, serving breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea and dinner.

Breakfast is available from 7:00am to 10:30am, followed by lunch from 12 noon to 2:30pm. Afternoon tea is served between 12 noon and 5:30pm, with dinner available from 6:00pm to 10:00pm, making it easy to enjoy dining at any time of day.

What time is The Bar at The Athenaeum open?

The Bar at The Athenaeum is open daily from 11:00am to midnight. To book a table, please call 020 7499 3464 or email 116@athenaeumhotel.com

.

Alternatively, feel free to drop in and enjoy a drink.

Do I need to reserve a table at the bar or on the terrace?

No reservation is needed. Simply drop into the bar or settle on the terrace whenever you feel like a drink. Our team will always help you find a comfortable place to unwind.

When is afternoon tea served?

Afternoon tea at The Athenaeum is available daily from **12 noon to 5:30pm**.

Is there a children’s menu at 116 at The Athenaeum Hotel?

Yes. 116 Restaurant at The Athenaeum offers a dedicated children’s menu with comforting favourites and lighter options designed for younger tastes. Our team is always happy to adapt dishes, so if your child prefers something simple or has any dietary needs, just let us know and we’ll take care of it.

Is in-room dining available at The Athenaeum Hotel?

Yes. In-room dining is available twenty-four hours a day, offering everything from early breakfasts to comforting late-night dishes. Guests can enjoy favourites from 116 at The Athenaeum in the privacy of their room or Residence, along with a selection of drinks and smaller plates. If you would like something tailored to your tastes, our team will be happy to prepare it for you.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Our team is always happy to guide you through each dish and ensure your meal is prepared exactly as you need.

Does the hotel have a food waste reduction programme?

Yes. In our dining outlets we work with ethically sourced local produce, transport food waste to an aerobic digester and recycle used cooking oil, all part of our ongoing efforts to reduce waste and support sustainable dining.

Do you source products and ingredients responsibly?

We prioritise locally sourced and ethically produced ingredients in our restaurants and carefully select suppliers who share our commitment to sustainability, so every meal reflects both quality and care for the planet.
