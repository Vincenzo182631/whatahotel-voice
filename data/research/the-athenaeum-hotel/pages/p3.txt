## Group stays FAQs

Open for everything you need to know

What meeting and event spaces does The Athenaeum Hotel offer?

The Athenaeum offers a collection of beautifully refurbished meeting and event spaces, each filled with natural daylight and designed for comfort and focus. The rooms are suited to everything from intimate meetings to mid-sized gatherings. Smaller suites host up to twelve guests, while our larger spaces can accommodate around a hundred guests, depending on the layout. Every room includes high-quality technology such as presentation screens, audio equipment, fast Wi Fi, and conferencing facilities, making it easy to connect with guests both in person and remotely. Our team will help shape the room setup and support you throughout your event.

Pick the perfect space for your Mayfair meeting or event HERE

Can The Athenaeum Hotel host private celebrations as well as business meetings?

Yes. Alongside corporate meetings and presentations, we host private dining, cocktail receptions, milestone celebrations, and social gatherings. Our events team will take care of everything from menus to mood, ensuring your occasion feels thoughtful and beautifully tailored.

How can I book a meeting or event at The Athenaeum Hotel?

Booking is simple. Share your date, group size, and requirements with our events team, and we will guide you from there. A site visit can be arranged at any time, and we are also happy to provide a virtual walk-through if you prefer to explore the spaces remotely.

How close is The Athenaeum Hotel & Residences to public transport?

We are a short five minute stroll from Green Park station, with the Victoria, Jubilee, and Piccadilly lines ready to take you across London. Hyde Park Corner is also close by, less than seven minutes on foot. Victoria Station is only a few minutes away by cab, and both Paddington and Waterloo can be reached in around fifteen minutes.

Can I book interconnecting rooms?

Absolutely. Perfect for families or groups who want to stay close. Many of our rooms and Residences connect; please speak to our Reservations Team to secure the best option.

What are the opening times for 116 at The Athenaeum?

Our restaurant, 116, is open seven days a week, serving breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea and dinner.

Breakfast is available from 7:00am to 10:30am, followed by lunch from 12 noon to 2:30pm. Afternoon tea is served between 12 noon and 5:30pm, with dinner available from 6:00pm to 10:00pm, making it easy to enjoy dining at any time of day.

Do you have accessible rooms?

Yes, we offer accessible Superior rooms. If you’d like guidance on the best room for your stay, our Reservations Team will be delighted to help.

What sustainability initiatives does The Athenaeum Hotel support?

Sustainability sits at the heart of how we operate. The hotel uses energy-efficient systems, responsible waste and recycling processes, and locally sourced products wherever possible. We also support community and charity projects throughout the year. Every part of our programme is designed to lessen our impact while ensuring guests continue to enjoy the comfort and generosity of a stay with us.

Read more HERE

Can The Bar at The Athenaeum be hired exclusively?

Yes, exclusive hire of the bar is available and can be arranged on request. Whether you’re planning a private celebration, corporate event, or special occasion, our team will be delighted to discuss your requirements and tailor the experience to suit your needs. Availability, minimum spend, and bespoke options can all be discussed directly with our events team to ensure a seamless and memorable occasion.
