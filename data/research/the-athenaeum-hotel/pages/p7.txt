# Menus

## Menus for Every Occasion

Discover refined dining in the heart of Mayfair at The Athenaeum Hotel & Residences, where you are welcomed with warm, attentive service and thoughtfully prepared cuisine. Our carefully curated menus invite you to enjoy seasonal ingredients, distinctive flavours, and beautifully crafted dishes, alongside expertly mixed drinks. Whether you’re joining us for a relaxed lunch, our signature Afternoon Tea, or an evening spent unwinding in elegant surroundings, your experience is shaped around comfort, quality, and a sense of individuality.
