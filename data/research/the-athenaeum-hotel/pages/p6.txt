## Let your troubles melt away

Discover our range of luxurious spa treatments in partnership with award-winning skincare brand Temple Spa, renowned for its Mediterranean-inspired formulas rich in natural ingredients. Choose from a reviving facial, a deeply relaxing full-body massage, or our signature bespoke treatment, thoughtfully tailored to you.

### All about the glow facial

An advanced, skin transforming facial with instant and visible results

£150 \| 60-minutes

Book Treatment

View Details

This advanced, skin transforming facial turns up your glow with instant and visible results for firmer feeling and brighter, healthy-looking skin. Your complexion is transformed thanks to intelligent active ingredients boosted by vitamin c and our glycolic acid complexes, add our unique skin-lifting massage techniques and the results are even better skin. Deep double cleanse is followed by a layer of our state-of-the-art oxygenating bubble peel that powerfully resurfaces to transform your complexion from lacklustre to positively glowing. Next up is a powerful skin workout that wakes up your skin and visibly lifts and irons out fine lines. Finally, our professional-only alginate mask drives product deep into your skin to work its magic. We like to call it the facial gym power hour.

A truly soothing and calming facial to restore your skin’s equilibrium, radiance and glow.

Your complexion is left looking brighter, clearer and full of vitality.
