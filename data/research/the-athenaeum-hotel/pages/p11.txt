# The Bar

## A place to settle in

Step inside and make yourself at home. The Bar at The Athenaeum is a welcoming retreat in the heart of Mayfair, a space created for you to settle in, slow down and feel quietly looked after. Designed with the ease of a private members’ club, it offers an atmosphere that is intimate, relaxed and unmistakably five-star.

## Simply sip, work or unwind

* * *

Begin the day at your own pace or pause between plans in a calm, welcoming setting. Enjoy speciality coffee, light bites and the comfort of space designed for ease and quiet connection.

As evening draws in, unwind with expertly mixed cocktails, a thoughtfully chosen wine list or our collection of over 100 whiskies. Settle in, take your time and let the night unfold.

For something playful, the Cocktail Masterclass invites you behind the bar with our expert mixologists. You’ll learn the art of crafting three signature cocktails, picking up insight, technique and a touch of theatre along the way. A fun, sociable experience for groups looking to shake things up.

## Whisky tasting

Settle in for our Whisky Masterclass, a one-hour experience hosted by our Bar team and designed for you to enjoy at an unhurried pace. You’ll taste four 25ml whiskies, each paired with carefully chosen cheeses. A lovely way to spend time together as a couple, or to share with friends who appreciate the finer details.

## Your occasion, your space

When you’re hosting something special, The Bar becomes your own private retreat in the heart of Mayfair. It’s a beautiful setting for intimate drinks receptions, work events and social occasions alike, relaxed and refined, designed to make your guests feel instantly at ease.

Our flexible team is on hand to help bring your vision to life with thoughtful attention to every detail. With exclusive use of the space, a late licence and service until 1am, your evening can unfold naturally at your own pace.

## The Bar FAQs

Open for answers to everything you need to know

What time is The Bar at The Athenaeum open?

The Bar at The Athenaeum is open daily from 11:00am to midnight. To book a table, please call 020 7499 3464 or email 116@athenaeumhotel.com

.

Alternatively, feel free to drop in and enjoy a drink.

Do I need to reserve a table at the bar or on the terrace?

No reservation is needed. Simply drop into the bar or settle on the terrace whenever you feel like a drink. Our team will always help you find a comfortable place to unwind.

Can The Bar at The Athenaeum be hired exclusively?

Yes, exclusive hire of the bar is available and can be arranged on request. Whether you’re planning a private celebration, corporate event, or special occasion, our team will be delighted to discuss your requirements and tailor the experience to suit your needs. Availability, minimum spend, and bespoke options can all be discussed directly with our events team to ensure a seamless and memorable occasion.
