# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Berlin

## Focus on what matters most at our luxury hotel in Berlin

Perfectly positioned beside Tiergarten, JW Marriott Hotel Berlin invites you to experience the city through moments of comfort, discovery, and connection. As one of the largest event hotels in central Berlin, Germany, our 505 refined guest rooms and suites reveal inspiring views of Potsdamer Platz, Tiergarten, and the Embassy District, creating a tranquil retreat after your day in Berlin. You enjoy...See More access to our wellness area with pool, sauna, steam bath, and a modern fitness center designed for personal wellbeing. From leisurely mornings to evenings of exceptional dining and crafted cocktails, every experience is designed with intention. Step outside and discover Berlin’s iconic landmarks, renowned museums, luxury shopping, and vibrant cultural districts, all within easy reach of the hotel. Whether you are visiting for a weekend escape, a longer stay, or planning a stay at a family hotel in Berlin, our intuitive service and thoughtfully curated experiences invite you to explore Berlin at your own pace.See Less

## THANKSGIVING BUFFET @ THE MARKET

On November 26, we celebrate Thanksgiving 2026 at JW Marriott Hotel Berlin – and you are warmly invited to join us for a festive dinner inspired by traditional American flavours and the spirit of gratitude. Look forward to a generous buffet starting with a rich Thanksgiving salad bar. At the carving station, enjoy a traditional whole roasted turkey with apple and bread stuffing, sweet potato purée and cranberry sauce, as well as prime rib with scalloped potatoes, green beans, mushroom cassoulet and red wine jus. The feast is complemented by comforting seasonal dishes and to finish, indulge in a selection of classic desserts including pumpkin pie, pecan pie, warm apple pie with vanilla ice cream, cheesecake cream with strawberries, chocolate fudge and cranberry pear crumble.

## JW Garden

We invite you to immerse yourself in a serene oasis amid the bustling city, inspired by the love of J. Willard and Alice Marriott for nature. Their passion for gardening—whether through long daily walks or growing sweet raspberries for homemade jam—continues to flourish in JW Marriott properties around the world.

Here at JW Marriott Hotel Berlin, we honor this rich heritage by cultivating fresh herbs and ingredients, carefully selected to enhance your culinary experience. In our garden, you’ll discover our signature herb, lavender, featured in exquisite dishes at JW Steakhouse and refreshing cocktails at Bar26.

As you explore, take a moment to see what else is in season and delight in the connection between our garden and your culinary experiences.

Step outside and let the serenity of the JW Garden enrich your stay—tranquility may not grow on trees, but we’re proud to let it thrive in our garden.

## Mindfulness - Made in Berlin

Inspired by the life reform movement of the 1920s, represented by Bauhaus Architecture at the hotel site itself, the hotel spreads a new German culture of mindfulness. Plenty of nourishment for the body and mind can be found at and around. The adjoining Tiergarten offers space for sporting activities and joyful moments of togetherness. This is also an ideal retreat for an urban but sensual nature experience. The adjacent Berliner Philarmonie and Kulturforum with ist many museums offer a variety of food for thought.

## Inspired Events

Occupying a prime location on Potsdamer Platz in the heart of the city between Kurfürstendamm and the Brandenburg Gate, JW Marriot Hotel Berlin is one of the capital’s largest event hotels. An homage on the Roaring ‘20s, the architectural style impresses with its clever combination of classical ambience and state-of-the-art infrastructure. Backed by professional service, a total of 19 flexible event rooms. Experience the practically unlimited possibilities, be it the Grand Ballroom, smaller meeting rooms or on the exclusive rooftop terrace with its fabulous view over Potsdamer Platz – we’ll help you plan your perfect event.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Berlin?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Berlin is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Berlin allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Hotel Berlin. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of €35.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Berlin?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Berlin. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Berlin, with rates of €5.00 per hour, and €35.00 per day. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Berlin have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Berlin has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Berlin booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Berlin?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Berlin is Berlin Brandenburg Airport (BER). BER is located approximately 26.5 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Berlin have electric vehicle charging stations?
