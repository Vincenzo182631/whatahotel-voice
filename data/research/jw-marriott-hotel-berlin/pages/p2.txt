# Luxury Suites with Views of Berlin

## Accommodations

Enjoy views of Berlin from one of our 505 rooms and luxury suites in Berlin overlooking surrounding greenery from Potsdamer Platz to Tiergarten. Our hotel accommodation in Berlin offers a calm base to connect with the neighborhood and discover the city's history. Guests often highlight the spacious rooms, while the quiet atmosphere adds to the sense of retreat.

Upgrade Your Stay

### Executive Rooms

Enjoy an exclusive retreat in one of our Executive Rooms. Newly renovated, high-floor located, the calm and comfort will provide an uplifitng experience during your stay. Designed to reflect the beauty of nature, these well-crafted Executive rooms present a serene color palette accentuated with architectural details to provide a break from a city in perpetual movement. Featuring floor to ceiling windows, the natural light floods the entire room, allowing you to rest and relax in a reimagined style and spirit.

### Pet Friendly Hotel in Berlin

Located near Berlin's biggest park, Tiergarten; JW Marriott Hotel Berlin is the luxury haven for humans and their best friends alike. Complimentary amenities include a bed, serving bowls and anti-spill placemat.

## Executive Suite

Each fully renovated Executive Suite offers a haven of tranquility with their separate living room and King bed. Unwind in a contemporary design, inspired by the principle of mindfulness, with marble bathrooms. The 51 m2 offers you all the space and comfort you need, to make you feel at home. The room offers ample amenities such as two TV screens.

## Explore Our Suites

A total of 505 elegantly furnished rooms from 31 sqm including 33 suites. All rooms feature TV with German and international channels, air conditioning with individual climate control, laptop-sized room safe and a kittle coffee and tea as well as free Wifi.
