# _The Ritz_ RESTAURANT

The Ritz \> DINE WITH US \> The Ritz Restaurant

The Ritz Restaurant is one of London’s finest dining experiences and holds two Michelin Stars. Set in one of London’s most iconic restaurants, our menu celebrates seasonal British ingredients through refined technique. From seven-course and five-course Epicurean tasting menus to our three-course lunch, each course reflects the calibre of fine dining you expect from a Michelin-starred establishment.

TWO MICHELIN STAR _Dining_

* * *

With sparkling chandeliers and stunning mirrors, the elegant Ritz Restaurant is one of the most beautiful dining rooms in the world. For those seeking exceptional Michelin Star dining in London, The Ritz offers an unforgettable blend of heritage, flavour, and finesse.

BOOK NOW

The Ritz Restaurant’s Executive Chef, John Williams MBE, crafts lunch and dinner menus showcasing seasonal local ingredients through his mastery of contemporary British cuisine.

Buying organic beef from the Cornish moors, lamb from the Lake District, and succulent lobsters from South West Scotland, our Executive Chef and his team sustainably source the finest, freshest ingredients from around the country for our restaurant’s menus.

**A sparkling atmosphere**

The Ritz Restaurant is a Two Michelin-starred dining destination in Mayfair, set within an elegant venue beneath a soaring glass roof. Dine on refined British cuisine in one of London’s most graceful and timeless settings.

As one of the best restaurants in the UK, The Ritz Restaurant has been awarded the prestigious title of Restaurant of the Year by The National Restaurant Awards 2025.

Live entertainment is provided on Friday and Saturday evenings. If you choose the à la carte or Epicurean menus, there is an additional entertainment charge of £58 per person.

DRESS CODE

* * *

The Ritz London has a dress code in different areas of the hotel as follows: Gentlemen are required to wear a jacket and tie (jeans and sportswear are not permitted for either ladies or gentlemen) for Afternoon Tea and for lunch and dinner in The Ritz Restaurant.

In all other areas of the hotel (The Ritz breakfast service, The Rivoli Bar, The Secret Garden, and The Cigar Shop), smart casual attire is acceptable.

Please note that sportswear is not permitted in any of the hotel’s restaurants or bars.
