# AFTERNOON TEA _at The Ritz_

The Ritz \> DINE WITH US \> Afternoon Tea

Experience the finest Afternoon Tea at The Ritz London, where a selection of premium loose-leaf teas and Champagne complement a reimagined British tradition. Enjoy sophisticated entertaining in one of London’s most iconic dining settings.

_The World’s Most Iconic_

Afternoon Tea Experience

* * *

One of the world’s most iconic Afternoon Tea experiences, The Ritz London’s service features elegantly composed sandwiches, freshly baked scones with Cornish clotted cream and strawberry preserve, and a refined selection of pastries and teacakes. Service is continuous, with courses replenished throughout your visit.

BOOK NOW

Our cakes are the finale to your Afternoon Tea. Served tableside from the guéridon trolley, they change seasonally; our Pastry Chefs craft chocolate, fruit, and signature creations to complement each season’s ingredients. This is the Ritz’s signature finishing touch.

This most charming English Afternoon Tea in Mayfair is served in spectacular surroundings.

The Ritz London is home to the UK’s only certified Tea Master, Giandomenico Scanu. He travels to tea plantations worldwide to source our loose-leaf teas, including our exclusive Ritz Royal Blend Tea, available only here.

Why not try our exclusive Ritz Royal Blend Tea? This is only available at The Ritz.

An elegant musical ensemble accompanies your service, completing the Afternoon Tea experience within the stunning 18th-century William Kent House. Children under 16 are welcome without our standard dress code.

Kindly note that our Afternoon Tea dress code does not apply to children under 16.

**Music to match the occasion** A live musical ensemble completes your Afternoon Tea experience at The Ritz, setting the refined atmosphere throughout your visit.

**Afternoon Tea sittings**

_Monday to Sunday: 11.30 am, 1.30 pm, 3.30 pm, 5.30 pm and 7.30 pm._

Afternoon Tea prices start from £95 for adults and £73 for children.

Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free Afternoon Tea menus are available upon request.  If you or any guest in your party has dietary needs, please inform us before your visit, and we will strive to ensure our menu options suit you. Kindly note that we cannot accommodate specific allergies with additional menu adjustments on the day. Your understanding and cooperation are valued.

DRESS CODE

* * *

The Ritz London has a dress code in different areas of the hotel as follows: Gentlemen are required to wear a jacket and tie (jeans and sportswear are not permitted for either ladies or gentlemen) for Afternoon Tea and for lunch and dinner in The Ritz Restaurant.

In all other areas of the hotel (The Ritz breakfast service, The Rivoli Bar, The Secret Garden, and The Cigar Shop), smart casual attire is acceptable.

Please note that sportswear is not permitted in any of the hotel’s restaurants or bars.

_Afternoon Tea with a glass of_ CHAMPAGNE

Indulge in a truly exquisite experience by enjoying a sparkling Afternoon Tea at The Ritz, enhanced with a glass of Champagne, setting the stage for an unforgettable celebration.

Our Afternoon Tea with Champagne offers a delightful range of treats, beginning with a selection of freshly made finger sandwiches. The experience continues with warm, freshly baked scones, accompanied by Cornish clotted cream and strawberry preserve and followed by a scrumptious assortment of cakes and pastries.  End your Afternoon Tea, with pure indulgence with our exclusive Ritz table-side service. Enjoy two generous slices of cake, skilfully served from our elegant guéridon cake trolley by our expert pastry chefs. We curate a selection of seasonal cakes that perfectly match the flavours of the moment. Whether you desire a decadent chocolate indulgence or a refreshing fruity delight, the choice is entirely yours.

**Mood music** Enhancing the ambience further is the enchanting musical backdrop to add an extra layer of charm to your afternoon.

A glass of Champagne starts at £26 and includes Value Added Tax at the prevailing rate at the time of payment.

CELEBRATION _Afternoon Tea_

Whether celebrating a birthday, anniversary or another special occasion, Afternoon Tea at The Ritz is the perfect way to mark the occasion.

Enhance your experience with a celebration cake, available in 4-inch and 6-inch sizes, a glass or bottle of Champagne, or both for an extra-special celebration.

Guests will enjoy a selection of 20 loose-leaf teas, finely cut sandwiches, freshly baked scones with Cornish clotted cream and strawberry preserve, an assortment of pastries replenished on request, and traditional English cakes served tableside from our iconic cake trolley.

CHILDREN’S _Afternoon Tea_

Afternoon Tea at The Ritz is a timeless experience for families to enjoy together. Our Children’s Afternoon Tea menu features the same quintessentially English finger sandwiches, scones, and pastries as our traditional menu, perfectly portioned for dainty fingers. Our Tea Master has chosen the perfect specially blended decaffeinated tea, so those little ones can partake in the same ritual as their parents. This famously British Afternoon Tea experience is sure to be the start of a new family tradition for decades to come.

Kindly note our Afternoon Tea dress code does not apply to children under the age of 16.
