# WHY STAY _with us?_

For over a hundred years, we have been providing guests with truly spectacular hotel service, whether they stay in our family rooms in London or in one of our luxury suites. We can guarantee that your stay with us will be unforgettable.

Situated in the heart of this vibrant city, The Ritz is one of the most famous hotels in London and the world. The sumptuous surroundings of this five-star hotel are matched only by the professionalism of our friendly staff. We are not just a London hotel – we are The Ritz.

## _Ritz_ CONCIERGE

* * *

Unleash the magic of London with our extraordinarily knowledgeable Concierge team, who possess unmatched connections throughout the city and a passion for creating memorable moments.  With a collective experience of over 150 years, our team is a treasure trove of information on the city’s finest attractions and activities. Whether it’s cultural landmarks or vibrant entertainment, we have the expertise to curate an unforgettable itinerary.

Access to the most exclusive venues is often just a phone call away – and they always know who to talk to.
