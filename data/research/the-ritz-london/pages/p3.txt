# GRAND DELUXE _Rooms_

The Ritz \> Grand Deluxe Rooms

Our Grand Deluxe Rooms are spaces shaped by London’s heritage of refinement, where craftsmanship, proportion, and material integrity matter more than trend or novelty. Unapologetically, distinctly London, our Grand Deluxe Rooms are firmly rooted in the hotel’s place, history, and infamously grand sense of occasion.

Each Grand Deluxe Room offers a deliberate pause from the pace of the city. The experience is not about separation from London, but refinement of it: the feeling of stepping back into an era where hospitality was personal, theatrical in its detail, and quietly exacting in its standards.

Hand-finished mouldings, richly woven carpets, silk wall coverings and period-inspired furnishings come together to create an atmosphere that feels timeless, with layered textures, warm tones, and refined detailing working to a sense of quiet grandeur.

The grand, spacious layout has been thoughtfully designed to feel both generous and intimate, addressing comfort through proportion rather than minimalism. A magnificent bed, complete with Royal Warrant awarded Sleepeezee ensemble and dressed in fine Italian linens, forms the centrepiece of the room, whilst a curated pillow selection allows each guest to fine-tune their comfort.

The marble bathroom continues this philosophy of considered luxury, with the Ritz’s signature precision craftsmanship at its forefront. A deep soaking bathtub is complemented by a separate rainfall shower, whilst twin marble vanities provide generous space and heated floors add a further layer of comfort.

Contemporary comforts are seamlessly embedded within the room. Bang & Olufsen Beosound speakers deliver rich, enthusiast-grade sound, while a 55” smart television provides truly immersive in-room entertainment. An integrated bedside control panel allows intuitive adjustment of lighting, blinds, and room ambience.

A coffee machine and kettle are discreetly positioned for morning or evening use, alongside a thoughtfully considered mini bar selection. Each Grand Deluxe Room also includes a personal safe for your valuables, ensuring peace of mind throughout your stay.

And of course, The Ritz’s renowned guest services will also be at your disposal. From turndown preparation to 24-hour Room Service and Concierge support, whether arranging theatre reservations, private transfers, or dining experiences, every detail is managed with quiet attentiveness, allowing guests to relax and enjoy their stay.

Rates include Value Added Tax at the prevailing rate at the time of payment.

For those seeking additional space and heightened seclusion, our Suites offer a more expansive expression of The Ritz experience. You can explore suite options here.
