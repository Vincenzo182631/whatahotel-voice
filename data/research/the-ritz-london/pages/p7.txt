# The Secret Garden

The Ritz \> DINE WITH US \> The Secret Garden

The Secret Garden, an enchanting outdoor dining and drinking destination in Mayfair, is a hidden oasis set within The Ritz London. Bringing together exceptional seasonal cuisine, expertly crafted cocktails and world-renowned Ritz service, this enchanting garden setting offers a refined escape from the bustle of the capital.

**The Secret Garden Food & Drinks**

Discover the essence of The Secret Garden, where a contemporary Mediterranean-inspired menu showcases the finest seasonal ingredients, expertly prepared by The Ritz London’s acclaimed culinary team. From beautifully presented small plates and vibrant salads to seafood specialities and decadent desserts, each dish reflects a considered approach to elegant yet relaxed al fresco dining.

The menu is designed for indulgent, leisurely dining, with highlights including the Dorset Crab Brioche Roll and Ceviche of Sea Bass with Citrus and Avocado, as well as rich small plates such as Lobster and Prawn Tacos.

For a more substantial experience, guests may enjoy signature dishes such as Harissa-Charred Poussin with Pumpkin Seed and Smoked Almond, or Red Mullet with Ratatouille and Bouillabaisse, both reflecting a contemporary interpretation of classic European flavours. A distinctive seafood selection is led by the luxurious Fruits de Mer, which combines lobster, langoustine, crab, prawns, oysters, and potted shrimps for an exceptional sharing experience.

At the pinnacle of indulgence, the caviar selection is presented with careful attention to detail, featuring Platinum Sturgeon, Oscietra and Beluga caviar, as well as the exclusive Caviar Epicurean Journey curated by Executive Chef John Williams MBE.

To conclude, desserts such as the Ritz Vanilla Trifle and Vanilla and Chocolate Ice Cream Mille Feuille provide a sophisticated finish to the experience, embodying The Ritz London’s signature elegance in an outdoor setting.

Complementing the menu is a carefully curated drinks list featuring botanical cocktails, exceptional Champagnes, and fine wines, available by the glass or by the bottle. Surrounded by lush planting, elegant seating and the timeless glamour synonymous with The Ritz London, The Secret Garden offers an elevated al fresco experience where gastronomy, hospitality and nature come together in perfect harmony.

Classic cocktails are also featured, including the newly launched Ritz 120, created to celebrate the hotel’s 120th anniversary. This signature serve combines Gold-Infused Belvedere Organic Vodka, Grand Marnier, The Newt English Ice Cyder, Manzana Verde and Champagne Barons de Rothschild ‘Réserve Ritz’ Brut.

**The Secret Garden Experience**

The Secret Garden is an invitation to linger. Designed for relaxed lunches, celebratory gatherings and elegant evenings, the atmosphere encourages guests to slow down and savour exceptional food, fine drinks and meaningful conversation.

Attentive and theatrical service enhances every visit, reflecting the standards that have made The Ritz London one of the world’s most celebrated hotels. Whether enjoying lunch beneath the shade of parasols, sharing cocktails as the sun sets, or settling into an evening of dining and entertainment, every moment is thoughtfully curated.

As day transitions into evening and the garden is bathed in warm golden light, the atmosphere evolves into one of understated glamour, creating a unique setting for memorable occasions in the heart of Mayfair.

**Entertainment**

A programme of live entertainment further enriches the atmosphere throughout the season, from 12 pm to 4 pm and from 6 pm to 10 pm on Thursday to Sunday, creating an atmospheric soundtrack to the garden experience.

Entertainment will be available until 31st October.

**The Secret Garden Design & Heritage**

Designed to blur the boundaries between gastronomy and landscape, The Secret Garden is a hidden oasis where nature and design exist in perfect harmony.

Created by Marcus Barnett Studios, the Garden has been conceived as an ethereal tapestry of grasses and perennials, featuring irises, hellebores, hydrangeas, crab-apple trees and white wisteria. Planted to follow the natural architecture of the space and the movement of sunlight throughout the day, the Garden continually evolves through the seasons with changing colours, textures and scents.

At its heart sits a striking water feature, providing a gentle acoustic backdrop to stylish seating areas sheltered by elegant parasols and canopies. As temperatures cool into the evening, bespoke cashmere throws by Johnston’s of Elgin invite guests to linger a little longer in comfort.

Thoughtful landscaping, refined design, and exceptional hospitality create an environment that feels both secluded and distinctly Ritz.

Opening times _(weather permitting)_:

_Monday to Sunday: 11.00 am – 11.30 pm_ ( _Last orders at 11.00 pm)._

Please note there is a £30 minimum spend per person.

While children are welcome, admission remains at the discretion of our team on the day, subject to capacity, existing reservations and the comfort of guests already enjoying the space.

DRESS CODE

* * *

The Ritz London has a dress code in different areas of the hotel as follows: Gentlemen are required to wear a jacket and tie (jeans and sportswear are not permitted for either ladies or gentlemen) for Afternoon Tea and for lunch and dinner in The Ritz Restaurant.

In all other areas of the hotel (The Ritz breakfast service, The Rivoli Bar, The Secret Garden, and The Cigar Shop), smart casual attire is acceptable.

Please note that sportswear is not permitted in any of the hotel’s restaurants or bars.
