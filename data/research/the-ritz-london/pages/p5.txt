# _The Rivoli_ BAR

The Ritz \> DINE WITH US \> The Rivoli Bar

The Rivoli Bar, an elegant cocktail bar in Mayfair, is set within The Ritz London. This intimate luxury bar in Mayfair offers a refined escape from the capital’s busy streets, where expertly crafted cocktails and attentive service reflect the timeless glamour associated with one of London’s most iconic hotels.

Renowned for serving some of the best cocktails in Mayfair, The Rivoli Bar combines classic mixology with contemporary creativity. Guests can enjoy signature drinks alongside beautifully executed classics, all prepared with precision by experienced bartenders using premium spirits and carefully selected ingredients. The result is a distinctive cocktail experience in London, where exceptional drinks, elegant surroundings and personalised service come together in one of the city’s most celebrated cocktail lounges.

## **The Rivoli Bar Drinks Selection**

The Rivoli Bar is celebrated for its expertly crafted cocktails in Mayfair, blending classic mixology with innovative creations. Signature drinks such as the Ritz 120, Iron Lady, and Churchill’s Courage highlight the creativity of the bar team, while favourites including the Garden of Eden, Quince Dairy, and Cocoa Reserve are prepared with precision using premium spirits such as gin, rum and tequila.

Each cocktail reflects the craftsmanship associated with The Ritz, where careful technique, quality ingredients and elegant presentation combine to create drinks that are both refined and memorable.

Guests are also welcome to enjoy a cocktail created especially for them. The experienced bartenders are delighted to tailor drinks to personal tastes, ensuring every visit offers something unique.

## **The Rivoli Bar Food**

Alongside its cocktails, The Rivoli Bar offers refined bar dining in Mayfair, designed to complement its drinks menu. Guests may enjoy indulgent options such as Royal Belgian Beluga or lighter dishes such as Charcuterie Board, Parmesan and Olives, Ceviche of Sea Bass, Citrus and Avocado, Caesar Salad, Crusted Squid, Celery, and Amalfi Lemon.

Seafood also features prominently, with King Prawn and Squid Paella, Red Mullet, Ratatouille and Bouillabaisse or Fruits de Mer providing elegant pairings for Champagne or cocktails. Seasonal desserts such as the Ritz Vanilla Trifle, Caramelised Banana and Chocolate Flan, along with a curated selection of artisan cheeses, offer a refined finish to the experience.

Menus evolve throughout the year, reflecting seasonal ingredients and a growing focus on responsibly sourced produce.

## **The Rivoli Bar Experience**

A visit to The Rivoli Bar is about more than simply enjoying a drink. From the moment guests arrive at The Ritz, the experience is defined by attentive service, elegant surroundings and expertly prepared cocktails, spirits, wine or Champagne.

Whether discovering a signature creation or revisiting a classic cocktail, guests can expect craftsmanship and personalised hospitality that have made The Rivoli Bar one of the most distinctive cocktail lounges in Mayfair.

## **The Rivoli Bar Design**

The Rivoli Bar blends historic character with contemporary luxury, centred around a striking red and green marble chequerboard floor inspired by William Kent. Restored historic vaulted ceilings, enhanced with hand-painted gold motifs, create a distinctive sense of place, while rich green and golden ochre tones, leather-lined seating, marble tabletops and decorative mirrors bring warmth and refinement. Arched timber detailing and glimpses through glass windows add depth, intimacy and intrigue throughout the space.

DRESS CODE

* * *

The Ritz London has a dress code in different areas of the hotel as follows: Gentlemen are required to wear a jacket and tie (jeans and sportswear are not permitted for either ladies or gentlemen) for Afternoon Tea and for lunch and dinner in The Ritz Restaurant.

In all other areas of the hotel (The Ritz breakfast service, The Rivoli Bar, The Secret Garden, and The Cigar Shop), smart casual attire is acceptable.

Please note that sportswear is not permitted in any of the hotel’s restaurants or bars.
