### Fitness Room

The Fitness Center was made to meet the needs of our guests. A comfortable gym with Technogym® technologically advanced equipment with everything for a perfect workout.

Hotel guests can enjoy a good workout in comfort and total privacy, to stay fit and continue their regular health habits even when they are far from home.

The Fitness Room is open every day from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm. Ask the Concierge Desk for a keycard and information.
