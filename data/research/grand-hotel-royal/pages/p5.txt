## Your private beach in Sorrento

### An exclusive Blue Flag private beach set on a platform overlooking the sea, accessible directly from the hotel via a private lift.

During the summer months, guests can enjoy the intimate and private atmosphere of a fully equipped private beach in Sorrento — the perfect place to unwind and take a refreshing dip in the crystal-clear waters of one of Italy’s most sought-after seaside destinations.

The beach can be reached via a private lift from the hotel’s garden, or by following a scenic path that winds through tunnels set within the characteristic tuff stone, interspersed with Mediterranean gardens.

Sun loungers, umbrellas and small tables are available for your comfort, with bar service directly on the beach.

Open from 15 May to 15 October.
