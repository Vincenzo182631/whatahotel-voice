## Rooms & Suites

Discover the new enchanting Suites, all renewed with fine details and provided with a 4K "Bang & Olufsen" wide flat-screen TV.

Most of the rooms enjoy a breathtaking view over the Bay of Naples or the magnificent gardens.

All the accommodations are equipped with modern comforts including High Speed Wi-Fi, individually controlled air-conditioning, safe, electric kettle ( tea and coffee for free ), hairdryer, minibar, radio and satellite TV.

NB: every guest room and suite may be slightly different from the others due to its unique architectural details: pictures below are provided as example for each category and may not exactly represent your assigned room.
