## Our Initiatives for the Future

### At Grand Hotel Royal, responsible practices are part of our daily operations

With practical solutions, we promote efficient water use and energy efficiency across our operations. We use energy recovery systems and measures designed to improve resource efficiency.

Grand Hotel Royal is certified according to UNI EN ISO 9001, ISO 13009 and ISO 14001 standards, covering quality management, beach operation and environmental management respectively.

We are progressively reducing the use of plastic across our hotel operations through measures aimed at limiting unnecessary plastic consumption.

Our culinary offerings include local flavors and ingredients, with a preference for locally sourced products whenever available.
