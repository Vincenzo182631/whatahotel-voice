## Restaurants & Bars

### A variety of local and international dishes with a creative touch

Everything stars in the morning in the breakfast room where you can savor the fresh morning delicacies before reaching out to the pool or to the exclusive private beach for a relaxing day in the sun.

The lounge bar with its terrace overlooking the Gulf of Naples, the poolside restaurant and the snack bar at the beach level, are part of a wonderful setting that, with Mount Vesuvius, the islands of Ischia and Procida and the coastal landscape in the background, leaves you speechless.

The indoor restaurant with its classy atmosphere as well as the open-air restaurant on the sea front terrace, pamper their guest with a variety of food and wine proposals to enhance the quality and freshness of the local products expertly prepared by our qualified chefs.

## Restaurant Le Relais & I Glicini

Book a table

I Glicini Room overlooks the hotel’s beautiful park, and boasts a spectacular panoramic view over the Gulf of Naples.

The restaurant "I Glicini" welcomes its guests in a spacious and elegant dining room, delighting them with specialties from both regional and international cuisine, reinterpreted with a creative touch.

Every morning is served in this beautiful location a rich hot and cold Breakfast Buffet serving sweet and salty dishes.

Our skilled chef is always in the breakfast room to prepare dishes on request.

The restaurant is particularly suitable for wedding receptions and banquets.

**Opening Hours**: breakfast from 7.00 am to 10.00 am

## Restaurant & Bar Bellevue

Book a table

Located on a cliff overlooking the sea with a wonderful view of the Gulf of Naples.

The Restaurant & Bar Bellevue is not far from the hotel’s private swimming pool and makes for the perfect place where to relax during summer days, with a cocktail, a drink or a homemade ice cream.

Lunchtime means grilled specialties, fresh salads or great Neapolitan pizzas, that guests will enjoy under the shade of lemon, orange and palm trees.

**The Restaurant & Bar Bellevue is open every day with the following time schedule**:

From 09:00 am noon to 06:30 pm

**Lunch**: From 12.00 pm noon to 05.00 pm

The Restaurant & Bar Bellevue is open every day according to weather conditions.

## Beach Club La Marine

Book a table

The Beach Club La Marine is located on the Hotel's private pier and is easily reachable with a direct lift.

Perfect for sea lovers, it’s a great place where to enjoy a refreshing break with a cool drink or a tasty afternoon snack.

**Opening Hours**: from 09.00 am to 06.00 pm

## Bar Le Caprice

Book a table

Le Caprice Lounge Bar is gracefully furnished with glass-topped tables, wicker chairs and an impressive hand-painted floor.It is the perfect location to taste an afternoon tea or a glass of the traditional regional liqueur Limoncello, ideal after dinner.

During summer time our skilled bartenders prepare coloured cocktails and tasty aperitifs, serving them on the beautiful terrace, where guests can fully enjoy the sunset. Moreover, at Le Caprice Lounge Bar, some nights guests can enjoy the pleasant and relaxing piano bar.

**Opening Hours**: from 06.00 pm to 12.00 am
