## Swimming Pool

### Grand Hotel Royal enjoys a unique location in the center of Sorrento, surrounded by a magnificent park; a green haven where to experience the history of one of the most well-known tourist spots in the world

Grand Hotel Royal has a private large swimming pool where guests can relax, with a Jacuzzi and a scenic terrace overlooking the sea.

On sunny days, the pool area offers a breathtaking view over the Gulf of Naples, crystal-clear waters and a warm sea breeze.

### Bar "Bellevue” at the poolside

Guests can relax while enjoying a drink or an inviting light lunch at the poolside Bar "Bellevue”, overlooking the sea, and admiring lemon, orange, and palm trees.
