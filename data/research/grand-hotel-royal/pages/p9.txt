## Grand Hotel Royal

### Classy and stylish five-star estate in the heart of Sorrento

Grand Hotel Royal, the member of Alberghi Storici d’Italia (Historic Hotels of Italy), is a classy and stylish five-star estate in the heart of Sorrento, perched over the sea in a privileged location overlooking the Gulf of Naples and Mount Vesuvius, surrounded by a large park boasting exotic plants, amazing camellias, orange and lemon trees.

#### The entrance hall features traditional, classic-style décors, with comfortable sofas, chairs and antique carpets, finely inlaid furniture, refined marble staircases, Murano crystal chandeliers and classy furnishings.

### A short history

Between the two wars, the hotel welcomed the Prime Minister of the Kingdom of Italy, Francesco Saverio Nitti. Built in 1898, this was the hotel for the aristocracy who flocked from all over Europe to enjoy the grand gala evenings on the terrace high above the sea, with the Gulf of Naples stretched out before them. It was requisitioned during the war and fixed up again after the 1980 earthquake. The buildings have lost none of their nineteenth-century charms. The interiors continue to maintain the elegance of the past with original pieces of antique inlay wood furniture from Sorrento.

### An unforgettable stay

Grand Hotel Royal has a private large swimming pool where guests can relax, with a Jacuzzi and a scenic terrace overlooking the sea.

Sea lovers will appreciate the reserved private beach that can be accessed through a lift located inside the park.

The Fitness Center was made to meet the needs of our guests.

A comfortable gym with Technogym® technologically advanced equipment with everything for a perfect workout.

### Variety of local and international dishes with a creative touch

The “Caprice” Bar is the perfect place to enjoy an afternoon tea or an “aperitivo” on the cliff-top terrace overlooking the sea, to the sound of music from the piano bar.

There is also a lounge bar, a terrace bar, a poolside snack bar and a beach snack bar.

The hotel restaurant, “Le Relais & Glicini Room”, with its classy atmosphere, pampers its guests with a variety of local and international dishes with a creative touch.

Equally unmissable is the à la cart restaurant “La Terrasse Royal”, with its breathtaking view over the Gulf and its excellent cuisine.
