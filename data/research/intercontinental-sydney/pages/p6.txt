### IHG Partners with OnUs Business Coalition

IHG Hotels and Resorts is a proud member of the OnUs Business Coalition, dedicated to enhancing safety for young people in our communities. As part of this commitment, we've teamed up with the Australian E-Safety Commissioner to offer guests access to up-to-date advice while using our Wi-Fi services.

Learn more

### EV Charging Station

When staying at the hotel, guests can charge electric vehicles using their own account for flexibility and control or the hotel account for a $25 fee per charge for a hassle-free experience.

### The Treasury Building history

Our hotel rests within the Treasury Building of 1851, rich in 19th century heritage. This building, with its intricate architectural façade, was the first purpose-built government office in Sydney. Our building houses the oldest operating lift in the Southern Hemisphere and the first vault in Australia.

Learn more

### In the heart of Sydney

Located at the crossroads of culture, dining, nature, entertainment and business, InterContinental Sydney is steps away from the best Sydney has to offer. Walk to the business hubs and harbourside icons like the Sydney Opera House, Sydney Harbour Bridge, Sydney Royal Botanic Gardens and The Rocks historic quarter.

Learn more

### Club InterContinental

Experience a world of exclusive privileges at the newly refurbished Club InterContinental. Enjoy an aperitif or appetizer in your own private oasis within the heart of Sydney. Our elevated club boasts views of Sydney Harbour, Sydney Opera House and Royal Botanic Garden from high above. There’s no better place to start or end your day.

## FAQs for InterContinental Sydney

Does InterContinental Sydney have a pool?

InterContinental Sydney has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

Dive into refreshing waters and reenergize in our heated hotel pool. Floating 31 stories high, soak up sparking views of the Sydney Opera House through soaring glass windows.

How much is parking at InterContinental Sydney?

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Sydney?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Sydney.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Sydney for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Sydney have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Sydney has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Sydney?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Sydney.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Sydney?

Check-in at InterContinental Sydney is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is InterContinental Sydney from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Sydney is conveniently located near Sydney Kingsford Smith (SYD), approximately 7.46 miles away (12 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Sydney?

InterContinental Sydney is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Darling Harbour
- Sydney Opera House - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- Sydney Harbour Bridge - 0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)
- Sydney Royal Botanic Gardens - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- Sydney Fish Market - 1.62 miles away (2.6 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Sydney puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
