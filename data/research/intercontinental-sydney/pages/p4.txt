### EV Charging Station

When staying at the hotel, guests can charge electric vehicles using their own account for flexibility and control or the hotel account for a $25 fee per charge for a hassle-free experience.

### Club InterContinental

Experience a world of exclusive privileges at the newly refurbished Club InterContinental. Enjoy an aperitif or appetizer in your own private oasis within the heart of Sydney. Our elevated club boasts views of Sydney Harbour, Sydney Opera House and Royal Botanic Garden from high above. There’s no better place to start or end your day.
