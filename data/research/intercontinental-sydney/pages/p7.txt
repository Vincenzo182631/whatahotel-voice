### Great hotel, but missed a couple things

a day ago

The InterContinental Sydney gets the physical aspects right. The location is prime, the room was lovely, and every staff member I interacted with was genuinely kind, polite, and professional. However, the operational execution fell well short of expectations for a premium hotel, especially for a birthday stay booked through Chase’s The Edit as an IHG Platinum member.

Unfulfilled Benefits
At check-in, I was given a printout detailing The Edit perks, which listed a welcome amenity. When I asked about it, the receptionist mentioned it varied and would be sent up later. Nothing arrived. When I followed up the next day, the agent was surprised, noted it was supposed to be a bottle of sparkling wine and chocolates, and promised to raise it with the team. It still never arrived. Failing to deliver promised program benefits—even after bringing it up twice—and offering zero acknowledgment of a birthday stay was disappointing. Unfortunately, this failure to deliver on The Edit perks seems to be a recurring pattern across IHG properties recently.

Housekeeping Disruptions & Late Check-out
I had a confirmed 3:00 PM late check-out, but internal communication between the front desk and housekeeping was clearly lacking. Just before standard 11:00 AM check-out time, two staff members rang my doorbell asking if I needed extra blankets. Then, shortly after, another staff member rang the bell, opened the door, abruptly said she would come back later, and ran off. Never said if she was housekeeping or what she was there for. Repeated disruptions on departure morning defeat the purpose of having a relaxed late check-out.

Verdict
The property itself is solid and the staff are wonderful on a personal level, but the complete lack of follow-through on promised benefits and poor internal communication dragged what should have been a 5-star experience down to a 3-star stay.

ProsSleep Quality, Room Cleanliness, Location

ConsAmenities, recognition for ihg rewards club members

No, I do not recommend this hotel.

11 days ago

We stayed five nights at InterContinental Sydney, but unfortunately, the experience fell short of our expectations, particularly as an IHG Diamond Elite member. Despite booking our three-week Australia trip with IHG hotels months in advance, the only upgrade we received was to a higher floor.
