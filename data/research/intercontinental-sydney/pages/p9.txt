In a nutshell
: It may lack the amenity list of some of its big-name rivals, but InterContinental Sydney impresses with seamless service and enviable access to harbor sights.
Sydney is a city best experienced outdoors, and InterContinental Sydney is an ideal jumping-off point. The city’s lush Botanical Gardens, which hug the harbor, are moments from the hotel, and the Sydney Opera House is an easy 10-minute stroll. The hotel rises more than 30 stories, affording many of the 509 guest rooms and suites impressive views of those attractions and of the water. A calm palette of blues and grays enhances the connection to the harbor, and the marble-clad bathrooms feel opulent. Drinks and snacks are served in the triple-height atrium on the first floor, or on the rooftop at Aster, which offers knockout views of the city. The compact pool overlooks the Opera House, and there is a small fitness center, but you will need to venture off-site for spa services. Still, a stay here feels luxurious, thanks to attentive personnel who understand how to keep a hotel of this size running smoothly.
