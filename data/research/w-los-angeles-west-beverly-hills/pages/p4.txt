# Satisfy your cravings at our West LA restaurants

## Dining

Savor dishes that ignite your imagination at W Los Angeles - West Beverly Hills. Home to two dynamic Westwood, LA, restaurants, Beach House and Living Room Bar, our Marriott hotel showcases the electric flavors of Californian cuisine, while placing you near UCLA and sightseeing hotspots. Join us for mixology cocktails, upscale entrées, and poolside bites in the heart of West Los Angeles.

### Taste of W

Indulge your senses with a gastronomic adventure at the diverse dining outlets nestled within the vibrant ambiance of W Los Angeles West Beverly Hills. From elevated cuisine to innovative cocktails, our West Los Angeles restaurants promise to tantalize your taste buds and leave you craving for more.

### She Sets The Tone

Sets the Tone is set to light up the Living Room Bar every last Thursday of the month from 7–9 PM, spotlighting female artists with powerful voices and undeniable presence. Led by singer songwriter Alison Albrecht, the series also welcomes a rotating lineup of women shaping the sound of now. Intimate, electric, and unapologetically bold—this is a monthly celebration of women who lead through music.

### Quiz Night at W LA

Brains meet buzz every first Sunday of the month from 6–8PM in the Living Room Bar.Bring your team, grab a cocktail, and compete for prizes as Quizmaster TJ Ramini delivers a night of head scratching trivia and general hilarity. Fuel up with a playful, quiz themed cocktail menu—think Berry Smart, Final Answer, Lightning Round, and Hot Take—plus $10 apps to keep you sharp between rounds. 6 players max per team
