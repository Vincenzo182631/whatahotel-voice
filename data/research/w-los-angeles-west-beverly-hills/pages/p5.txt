## Stay Where Champions Are Made

Perfectly located within walking distance of UCLA, W Los Angeles – West Beverly Hills offers comfort and convenience for visiting families and prospective students. With easy access to campus events at Pauley Pavilion and spirited game day festivities during football season, you’ll be in the heart of all the action. Our hotel’s central location – just minutes from Westwood Village and Ronald Reagan UCLA Medical Center – makes it the ideal home base for any visit. Students, families, alumni, and faculty affiliated with UCLA are eligible for our exclusive discounted room rate.

## Explore the Best of Los Angeles

Experience a journey like no other when you stay at the W Los Angeles – West Beverly Hills. Nestled in the heart of one of the city's most vibrant and glamorous neighborhoods, our luxury hotel is located near Geffen Playhouse, offering easy access to Los Angeles’ most iconic attractions - and is located less than 15 minutes from the Santa Monica Airport. Dive into the rich tapestry of art and history with renowned museums like the Getty Center, the Los Angeles County Museum of Art (LACMA), the Petersen Automotive Museum, and the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures. Don't forget to catch an open-air concert at the legendary Hollywood Bowl or enjoy the acclaimed Geffen Playhouse, a local, non-profit theater company owned by UCLA. Head to SoFi Stadium or YouTube Theatre for shows and sporting events. For a classic Southern California experience, visit the Santa Monica Pier and enjoy amusement rides and seaside charm. Whether you're here for culture, entertainment, or relaxation, every moment promises something extraordinary.

## Shop ‘Til You Drop

Welcome to the epicenter of luxury fashion. Experience world-famous Rodeo Drive and peruse the latest designs of the world's best fashion houses, then immerse yourself in the cinematic charm of the iconic Two Rodeo Drive’s cobblestone walkway. Just two blocks away, Cañon Drive offers a more intimate atmosphere with local boutiques, alfresco cafés, and celebrated restaurants helmed by celebrity chefs. For the height of high fashion high fashion, head to Melrose Avenue—home to trendy, designer boutiques like Reformation and upscale consignment gems such as The RealReal. Minutes from Melrose you’ll find Westfield Century City – an upscale shopping experience with a curated mix of luxury brands, dining options, and entertainment. And just a short drive away, Westwood Village blends style with a youthful, energetic vibe, featuring a mix of popular retail stores, local fashion finds, and the cultural flair of nearby UCLA.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Los Angeles - West Beverly Hills have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Los Angeles - West Beverly Hills offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Cardiovascular Equipment, Machine Weights, Free Weights, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Los Angeles - West Beverly Hills have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Los Angeles - West Beverly Hills has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.
