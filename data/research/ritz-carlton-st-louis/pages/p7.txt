Elevate Your Stay
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.
Request Club Access
A Private Retreat
Club Lounge Access
Begin your stay in St. Louis with Club Lounge access. Located on the 18th floor, Club Lounge provides a private sanctuary where guests can work, relax, and indulge in an array of delectable offerings, all while taking in stunning views of the Gateway arch. From the moment you step inside, our Ladies and Gentlemen are ready to provide the finest personalized services to make you feel at home.

Dedicated Concierge
From dining reservations to excursion recommendations, our dedicated Club Concierge is always at your service to ensure every detail of your St. Louis stay is spectacular. No matter the reason for your visit, they will elevate your experience and cater to you personally. The Club Lounge team is available for your individual needs from 6:30am - 10pm.

In Good Taste
Daily Culinary Presentations
Each day in the Club Lounge, guests can enjoy thoughtfully crafted, delicious culinary offerings that tell the story of our city. With four presentations per day — breakfast, lunch, hors d'oeuvres, and dessert — Club guests have the opportunity to indulge in chef-curated culinary presentations at their leisure throughout their stay.

For Your Convenience
In-Room Amenities
A Club stay offers a range of exclusive amenities designed to elevate your experience from arrival through departure. Guest rooms and suites are conveniently located on the 17th and 18th floors, making Club Lounge steps from your door — no elevator required. Enjoy an intimate and efficient check-in and check-out process within the lounge, along with thoughtful touches such as complimentary pressing for two garments per room, per day, and access to printing services. Whether traveling for business or leisure, Club Lounge guests receive attentive, personalized service.
