Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis
Experience luxury at our Clayton St. Louis hotel
Nestled in the heart of Clayton, The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis is a luxury hotel in St. Louis that blends timeless elegance with the warmth and character of the city it calls home. Thoughtfully appointed guest rooms and suites, exceptional dining experiences, and legendary service create a stay that feels both effortless and unforgettable. Savor authentic Italian cuisine at Casa Don Alfonso, gather for handcrafted cocktails in The Lobby Lounge, or retreat to exclusive amenities such as the Club Lounge and Cigar Club. Whether you’re looking for a hotel with a spa in St. Louis, MO for a weekend escape, visiting for business, or celebrating a special celebration, every detail is designed to inspire comfort and connection. Just moments from downtown St. Louis and St. Louis Lambert International Airport, this Clayton, Missouri hotel places guests at the center of a vibrant dining, shopping, arts, and entertainment scene. Discover a destination where refined hospitality transforms every stay into a lasting memory.
Celebrate the Magic of the Season at The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis
The most wonderful time of year is just around the corner, and The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis is preparing a festive season filled with unforgettable holiday experiences. From cherished Thanksgiving traditions and the enchantment of Teddy Bear Tea to elegant Hanukkah celebrations and festive Christmas gatherings, our holiday calendar offers memorable moments designed to create lasting family traditions.
We invite you to mark your calendars for these highly anticipated events. Thanksgiving experiences will be available for booking beginning October 1, followed by Teddy Bear Tea, Hanukkah and Christmas experiences on October 29.
We look forward to welcoming you and your loved ones to celebrate the season in the warmth, elegance, and timeless hospitality of The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis.

Luxury Hotel in St. Louis
Global Sophistication. Distinctly St. Louis.
Nestled in the heart of Clayton's vibrant Arts and Garden District, The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis blends refined luxury with the warmth and spirit of the Midwest. Surrounded by acclaimed dining, cultural attractions, and boutique shopping, our hotel offers an elevated experience in one of the region's most desirable neighborhoods. Relax in thoughtfully appointed guest rooms and suites, many featuring views of the St. Louis skyline. Savor the flavors of Southern Italy at Casa Don Alfonso, gather for cocktails and conversation in The Lobby Lounge, or restore balance with a rejuvenating treatment and state-of-the-art wellness amenities. Whether visiting for business, a weekend getaway, or a special occasion, guests will discover personalized service, sophisticated surroundings, and memorable experiences at every turn.
