A Luxury Spa Oasis in St. Louis
Spa
A holistic approach to fitness and healthy living requires more than just exercise. To that end, The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis offers both an expansive fitness center and a tranquil day spa featuring a carefully crafted menu of massage and spa services to address the impact of today’s fast-paced lifestyle. Unwind at our luxury spa in downtown St. Louis, where relaxation meets refinement.
The Ritz-Carlton Spa, St. Louis
A Luxury Spa Oasis
Spa treatments are focused on massage, with signature experiences incorporating ingredients such as red granite sourced from St. Louis area providers. Additional spa services include invigorating facials and body scrubs and wraps. Pre- or post-treatment, guests are encouraged to enjoy the steam and sauna facilities at the hotel.

Featured Treatments
Missouri Red Rock Massage
Inspired by the volcanic rocks that are the core features of some of Missouri's most spectacular scenery; this therapeutic experience combines the relaxing benefits of sweet orange and Missouri red granite to soothe aching muscles leaving your body relaxed and your mind calm.
Missouri Black Walnut Massage
Harnessing natural elements by using sticks carved from locally sourced Black walnuts; this fast-paced, free-flowing, and energetic treatment is perfect for slowing busy minds and stretching and elongating tight muscles while helping to improve flexibility and restore serenity.
Nourished Glow Body Exfoliation
Reveal your smoothest, softest skin ever with this body exfoliation, finishing with a deeply nourishing oil application and personalized back massage. Includes full body exfoliation with a salt scrub.
Fitness Center
At The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis, guests and private members can take advantage of an expansive, fully-equipped Health Club with natural daylight and floor-to-ceiling windows with views of the bustling financial and garden district of Clayton. For more information about Massage services or Health Club membership, please call the Health Club Reception at (314) 719-1488.
