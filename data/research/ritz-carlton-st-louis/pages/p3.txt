Savor a Glass of Whiskey and a Fine Cigar at This Intimate Bar
Located within The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis, The Cigar Club is a private bar and lounge designed for those who choose to smoke cigars (the rest of the hotel is entirely smoke-free). Only overnight hotel guests and Cigar Club members have access to The Cigar Club. This unique space offers a fine selection of scotch, whiskey, cigars, and casual fare in a welcoming atmosphere. Burnished with wood paneling and rich fabrics this space offers the ideal setting to unwind.
