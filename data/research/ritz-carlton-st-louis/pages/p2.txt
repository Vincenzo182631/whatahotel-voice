Classic Luxury with Modern Flair
Suites & Rooms
Relax in the largest, most stylish hotel rooms and suites in St. Louis, MO at The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis. After a complete transformation, the new design blends contemporary luxury and classic styles and features Juliet balconies. The Club Lounge was revamped to offer updated décor and experiences.
Rollaway Bed Fee
Rollaway beds are available upon request for $65 per stay.
The Extraordinary Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining intimacy, comfort, and exclusivity with the personalized service and attention to detail that are the hallmarks of The Ritz-Carlton. This oasis is often referred to as a hotel within a hotel where a dedicated Concierge attends to individual needs in a well-appointed, relaxed lounge environment. Continuous culinary offerings throughout the day (breakfast, light lunch, hors d' oeuvres, beverages, and sweets) and enhanced Concierge services create the ideal environment to conduct business, plan a family outing, or simply unwind.

Inspiring Stories
It was a special time at The Ritz- Carlton, St. Louis when a family from Washington was visiting for two weeks. During their stay, the family had been touching the lives of many Ladies and Gentlemen at the Hotel but Kelly from Club Lounge and Daniel from Loss Prevention in particular. During one of the conversations with the family, Kelly learned they were in town to get a very specific eye procedure done for their daughter – a procedure that was only available in St. Louis. She shared the family’s story with Daniel, who gave the girl his Loss Prevention lapel pin to help her continue to be brave.
The surgery was a success and Kelly and Daniel wanted to plan a special evening of fun and relaxation for them after the stressful months and days they had been through. They worked with all Departments of the Hotel to transform The Wine Room into a mega-screen movie theater complete with snacks, pillows, and blankets for the children plus two oversized teddy bears to complete the scene. Kelly and Daniel had also discovered that the parents were celebrating their 11th wedding anniversary but had been so overwhelmed with their daughter’s surgery and their journey, they had nothing planned. Kelly and Daniel worked with the culinary team to set a romantic dinner for two by candlelight in the back of the room, while their daughter and son watched their movie so they could still be with their children but especially enjoy their anniversary. By the end of the night, a photographer was on site taking a picture of the family, which was framed and presented to them. The family was completely blown away by the love and care from Kelly and Daniel. The duo later received a homemade children’s book created entirely by their daughter with a whole story and colorful pictures about her magical stay at The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis.
Dog-Friendly
The family-friendly experience at this hotel in St. Louis, MO is not exclusive to humans; it applies to pets, too. Small, well-mannered dogs and cats are welcome at the hotel and can enjoy an in-room Bark Bites dining menu and treats. The surrounding neighborhood is also home to pet-friendly restaurants, stores, and activities.
