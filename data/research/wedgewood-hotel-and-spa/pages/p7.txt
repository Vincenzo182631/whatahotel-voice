Wedgewood Hotel & Spa
From the formally dressed doorman to the incredible flower arrangements in the lounge, this hotel evokes an air of civility.

Reviewed by
Kyle Beechey
Best for:
A romantic getaway, travelers looking for a little old-school luxury
Location:
In the heart of downtown, steps from major attractions like the Vancouver Art Gallery and Robson Street shopping
Can’t-miss feature:
This charming hotel has all the boutique charm, but all of the comforts of a larger scale property.
In a nutshell
: Even though you’re in the heart of
Vancouver,
you feel transported to another time. The hotel has been family owned and operated for forty years and you feel that. The staff treat you like royalty.
Rooms
Rooms start around 250sqft for an entry level and go to 750sqft for the largest penthouse. Design wise it’s clean, neutral tones with hint of paisley or stripe on a chair or throw. Larger rooms can come with terraces, fireplaces and standalone bath tubs. They all have Abahna bath products and 50 inch smart TVs. Even the smallest rooms have separate sitting areas and work desks.
Food and drink
Bacchus, the onsite restaurant is Michelin recommended and recognized as one of Canada’s 100 Best. Afternoon tea is one of their marquee offerings. It’s available daily and puts a local spin on things by using a Vancouver purveyor, Murchie’s, to stock all their teas. Expect the usual scones, cakes and tea sandwiches in an opulent, yet intimate dining room. For dinner, the menu is globally influenced, but with a heavy use of local ingredients. Expect to find dishes like truffled beef tartare and pan seared BC halibut. Since it’s highly rated, it can be a tougher table than most hotel restaurants. Make sure to reserve in advance.
Amenities and experiences
If you have the time, the spa is certainly worth checking-out. They have an extensive offering of massages and can stack treatments to give you a full day’s worth if you’re looking to unplug. There’s a small, but serviceable gym with TechnoGym equipment. There’s also a eucalyptus steam shower for a postwork out treat. The hotel can also arrange bike rentals and private yoga classes upon request.
What to do nearby
No shortage of activities within minutes of the hotel, like the Vancouver Art Gallery, Robson street shopping and the beaches of English Bay and Stanley Park are a short bike ride or taxi away. They can also arrange visits to the aquarium, transportation to the Grouse Mountain or the Capilano Suspension bridge.
The backstory
The 40-year old property is second generation female owned. It’s currently part of Relais and Chateau. Greek born owner Eleni Skalbania, was the first woman in Canada to own a boutique hotel back in 1984. It’s now owned by Elpie Marinakis, Skalbania’s daughter.
Insider tip
A popular wedding and event venue in the city. They have multiple spaces in and outdoors to accommodate guests. Tea at Bacchus is pretty hard to beat as is grabbing a bike rental and heading for the Seawall to Stanley Park. It’s one of the best views of Vancouver Harbor and so easy to access from the hotel.
Final considerations:
This property feels like stepping back in time in the best way possible.
Family friendly:
Not particularly; one child under 12 is included in the room rate, any additional occur an extra charge
Accessibility:
Five accessible rooms are available
Wellness:
There's a spa as well as a gym; the property also offers bike rentals and yoga
Loyalty group:
Relais and Chateau
