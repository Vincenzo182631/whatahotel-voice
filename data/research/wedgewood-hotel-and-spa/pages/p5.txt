## Your Private Retreat

The Spa at the Wedgewood is a sanctuary of wellness and an oasis of serenity and beauty. Featuring a tranquil relaxation lounge, a eucalyptus steam room, well appointed private treatment rooms, and spacious couples massage suites to accommodate a variety of treatments. Our Spa team is dedicated to providing a truly luxurious and revitalizing experience for our guests.
