Spa
Set across three levels, the spa at The Ritz-Carlton Sanya, Yalong Bay invites you to unwind with signature treatments and exceptional facilities, including 10 treatment rooms and two luxury suites. Locally inspired therapies incorporate ingredients like coconut milk, bamboo and Himalayan salt, while other spa treatments showcase Valmont products for an indulgent experience.
