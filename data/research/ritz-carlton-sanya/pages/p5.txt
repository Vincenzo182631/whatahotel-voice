Escape to the Pristine Surroundings of Yalong Bay
Experiences
Lying on the southernmost tip of Hainan Island, Sanya is a beachfront city where the mountains, rivers, sea and sky are matched in perfect unison. It is here that The Ritz-Carlton Sanya, Yalong Bay resides, a resort where guests can immerse themselves in the area’s varied experiences, from golf and luxury shopping to historic temples and landmarks. Discover an alluring selection of amenities and family-friendly activities at this Yalong Bay hotel.

A Spa Where Worldly Concerns Give Way to a World of Comfort
Set across three levels, the spa at The Ritz-Carlton Sanya, Yalong Bay invites you to unwind with signature treatments and exceptional facilities, including 10 treatment rooms and two luxury suites. Locally inspired therapies incorporate ingredients like coconut milk, bamboo and Himalayan salt, while other spa treatments showcase Valmont products for an indulgent experience. To complement its menu of services, the spa also features distinctive spaces like an outdoor pool, courtyards, whirlpools, relaxation rooms, a jade steam room and fitness studios.

Fitness Center
The spacious Fitness Studio features state-of-the-art newly released units from Technogym including Cardiovascular, Resistance, Rowing machines and Free Weights. A separate space provides a dedicated Kinesis Studio, with more than 30 square meter reserved for Technogym’s Kinesis system, a unique line of equipment that uses cables for weight resistance training via natural body movements. Guests may enjoy classes of Tai Chi at the Centre Lawn, or Yoga and Pilates in the Chi Studio and personal fitness coaching is also available.

Ritz Kids Resort Experiences
As Jean-Michel Cousteau says, “All kids start out as scientists, constantly asking ‘Why? What’s that? How does it work?’” Ritz Kids resort experiences tap into that innate curiosity by introducing our youngest guests to both the natural wonders and the rich cultural traditions of our resort locations. Immersive and adventurous, the resort experiences provide your child with enthralling activities to reward their interest in the beauty and workings of the world we live in. With a diverse collection of activities based on the four pillars of the program – water, land, environmental responsibility and culture – kids are swept away by everything from coral reefs and forests, to recycling and sustainability, storytelling, art and even food.

Uncle Martin’s Secret Garden
In the lush greenery of Ritz-Carlton Sanya, Yalong Bay, there are many place of wonder that you won’t want to miss, one of which is our popular Uncle Martin’s Secret Garden, where features fruits, vegetables and a dozen of creatures. Parents can revisit their childhood here or simply bring their children to say hello to their natural neighbors. Originally created by Uncle Martin, the first Director of Engineering of this resort in his spare time, the garden is first of its kind in the vicinity, as part of the resort’s commitment in promotion natural living and environmental protection. Almost every feature of this garden is made from recycled materials including the small bridge, and the produces in the garden often end up in the hotel’s restaurants when in season. Remember to visit Uncle Martin’s Secret Garden when you stay with us next time.

Golf
Hainan Island offers golfers of all skill levels an unforgettable experience. When the north of China is covered in snow, Sanya, with its year-round temperate climate, is still the perfect place for swinging throughout the year. Located just a few miles away, the Yalong Bay Golf course and Sun Valley Golf Club offer championship golf in a lush setting. For more information, please contact the resort's concierge at +86-898-88586021.
