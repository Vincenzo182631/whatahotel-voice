Dining
Casual and Fine Dining in Yalong Bay - Dining at The Ritz-Carlton Sanya, Yalong Bay is anchored in China yet reaches beyond its borders to other rich culinary cultures. Each restaurant offers a distinct point of view, from the signature Chinese cuisine at Pearl, to the fine-dining experience at Sofia, to seafood at the beachfront Sand.

Enjoy Tea and Cocktails Against a Striking Sanya Backdrop
Located in the heart of the hotel, Scene draws on The Ritz-Carlton’s enduring heritage of elegance. Offering breathtaking views of the South China Sea, this refined lounge bar showcases a tempting Afternoon Tea as well as an enticing selection of light bites and mixologist-crafted cocktails. Our Ritz-Carlton selection of charming teas and seasonal favorites, freshly crafted by our pastry artists, will transform your traditional tea-time into the most elegant, yet indulgent experience. Seductive evenings in Sanya are punctuated by live music, making Scene the perfect place to unwind with friends.
