Suites & Rooms
Recently updated guest rooms and suites invite the beautiful Sanya landscape indoors with views of the ocean, gardens and pools, while 33 villas showcase private patios, plunge pools and butler service. Explore enhanced amenities and experiences with Club Level accommodation.
The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level
For guests seeking a heightened level of comfort and privacy, the Club Lounge at The Ritz-Carlton Sanya, Yalong Bay is an inviting, well-appointed space accessible only to our Club Level guests. This oasis is often referred to as a hotel within a hotel, with exclusive amenities, continuous culinary offerings and access to a computer station - it is the ideal place to conduct business, plan a family outing or simply unwind. You can access the Club Level as you reserve our Treat You package now.

Experience Distinguished Club Level Accommodations
The Ritz-Carlton Sanya, Yalong Bay sets new standards of excellence in China. Renowned for impeccable service and sophistication, The Ritz-Carlton Club offers extra measures of luxury, such as expansive accommodations, an exclusive 450-square-meter lounge and balcony with seating for 55 and a dedicated concierge staff. The Club Level features 51 Club rooms including 4 Club Ocean View Suites, 2 Two-Bedroom Club Ocean View Suites, the one and only Ritz-Carlton Suite, all of which offer panoramic views of beautiful Yalong Bay. Guests enjoy multiple privileges at the Club Lounge, such as complimentary laundry service of two garments (noncumulative) and swimwear rinse & dry service, as well as complimentary 24-hour coffee and tea services throughout the day.

Club Level Experiences
In addition to our undivided personal service that is the hallmarks of The Ritz-Carlton Sanya, Yalong Bay, the Club Lounge has initiated a series of interactive ateliers for all Club guests and their children to experience. Find the schedule of our Club Elevated Experiences as follows:
Tuesday
Coconut Splash – On Tuesdays, a scent of fresh coconut milk will perfume our Club Lounge, as you will learn the recipe of Hainan Qing Bu Liang – or how to compose your own coconut fruit desserts. Timing: from 3 to 4 p.m.
Thursday
Skin Care Class – On Thursdays, our professional physiotherapists use the essence extract from fresh local Xing Long coffee scrub DIY and combined with their experienced techniques to gently exfoliate and help to rejuvenate a better glowing skin from 3 to 4 p.m. (Make an appointment two hours in advance at SPA）
Saturday
Coconut Splash – On Saturday, a scent of fresh coconut milk will perfume our Club Lounge, as you will learn the recipe of Hainan Qing Bu Liang – or how to compose your own coconut fruit desserts. Timing: from 3 to 4 p.m.
Sunday
My Tropical Garden – On Sundays, you will meet our botanical expert for a floral art workshop, utilizing fresh flowers, as well as tropical plants from the resort’s garden. Timing: from 3 to 4 p.m.
