### Gagosian

Toggle

Gagosian Rome is the first continental gallery of the American network. Located in a 1921 building, the space was designed by the Roman architect Firouz Galdo.

### Villa Medici

Toggle

Villa Medici is an architectural masterpiece. Located inside Villa Borghese, it is now the headquarter of the French Academy.

External link to open location details via google maps Phone numberhttps://www.villamedici.it/ External link to website

### Palazzo delle Esposizioni

Toggle

The Palazzo delle Esposizioni is a neoclassical exhibition hall, cultural center and museum.

External link to open location details via google maps Phone numberhttps://www.palazzoesposizioni.it/ External link to website

### Galleria Borghese

Toggle

Galleria Borghese is an art gallery, housed in the former Villa Borghese Pinciana. The Galleria Borghese houses a substantial part of the Borghese Collection of paintings, sculpture and antiquities, begun by Cardinal Scipione Borghese, the nephew of Pope Paul V.

External link to open location details via google maps Phone numberhttps://galleriaborghese.beniculturali.it/ External link to website

### Palazzo Barberini

Toggle

The National Gallery of Ancient Art is an art museum located in Palazzo Barberini . It is the principal national collection of older paintings in Rome – mostly from before 1800.

External link to open location details via google maps Phone numberhttps://www.barberinicorsini.org/ External link to website

### Via Veneto

Toggle

Take a walk down Via Vittorio Veneto, one of the most famous and elegant streets of Rome. In the 1950s and 60s, Via Veneto acquired international fame as the centre of la dolce vita, when its bars and restaurants attracted Hollywood stars and jet set personalities from all over the world.
