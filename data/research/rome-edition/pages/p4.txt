# Anima

**Anima**

RESERVATIONS WEBSITE

The Rome EDITION’s signature restaurant Anima offers an Italian dining experience with bold flavors inspired by Rome and Naples, curated by the Chef Antonio Gentile and crafted from seasonal, high-quality ingredients.

All-day dining, late lunch, and early dinner are served throughout the day at Anima, where guests can expect standout flavors in dishes like the Cavalieri Spaghetti with three tomatoes, alongside some of the best homemade focaccia. The wine list is thoughtfully curated around food pairings and places a strong emphasis on Italian labels while also featuring selections from other regions. The light-filled restaurant, furnished with chartreuse and blue upholstery, is open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, offering an elegant indoor setting and a charming courtyard oasis. With over 400 flourishing plants, it’s a lush space to connect, savor, and indulge.

For inquiries please contact us at +39 06 45249009

**Punch Room**

Reservations

Punch Room, EDITION’s signature bar concept, is an award-winning speakeasy-inspired bar concept that follows the standard set at other EDITION properties worldwide. Born in the 17th century, traditional punches utilized the exciting new ingredients discovered on the trade routes. The perfect spot to recline on the amaranth-colored velvet sofas and explore Punch cocktails that examine the influence of the Roman Empire, it is a convivial and relaxed space.

The five traditional ingredients of punch highlighted spirits from Europe and the colonies, spices and citrus from Africa and the east, teas from China and India, and sugar from the Indies.

This spirit of exploration comes to life through Discovery, a new menu that draws on imagined travel journals, blending storytelling with globally inspired flavours.

For inquiries please contact us at +39 06 45249009

**Jade Bar**

RESERVE

Jade Bar is an intimate venue with elegant green marble interiors, offering an immersive experience and a selection of premium spirits and champagne.

At Jade Bar, sophistication is reimagined through a carefully designed journey that includes signature martinis, exclusive champagne cocktails, and elevated bites like caviar and anchovies.

Guests can immerse themselves in unique tasting rituals, including a sensory collaboration with Le Labo featuring the Tè Noir fragrance. This ritual now opens with a delicate scent infusion inspired by Tè Noir, setting the mood for the evening, and concludes with a farewell gift of a perfume sample leaving a lasting sensory memory of the experience.

Working alongside the world’s finest spirits and champagne houses, Jade Bar offers an exclusive yet playful experience that perfectly embodies The Rome EDITION’s spirit of style, sophistication, and discovery.

For inquiries please contact us at +39 06 45249009

**The ROOF**

RESERVE

The Roof, located on the seventh floor, features a spacious dining area with a panoramic terrace and a travertine plunge pool. Perfect for lunch, dinner or a sunset aperitivo, it captures the Riviera’s spirit in the heart of Rome, offering signature cocktails alongside breathtaking city views.

The vibrant yet sophisticated setting, with elegant design and a unique atmosphere, envelops guests in a contemporary, inviting vibe that reflects Rome’s timeless charm with a modern twist.

The cuisine celebrates freshness and authenticity, highlighting premium fish and meats prepared with traditional techniques like grilling to preserve their purest flavors, evoking the gentle breeze of Italy’s sun-kissed coasts.

By day, the menu is light and refreshing, ideal for a relaxed gourmet break with a view. From sunset to dinner, the terrace transforms into an elegant evening destination, where soft lighting and lively energy merge with a cuisine inspired by the harmony of land and sea.

For inquiries please contact us at +39 06 45249009
