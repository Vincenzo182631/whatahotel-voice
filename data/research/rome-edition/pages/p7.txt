# Jade Bar

## Jade Bar

RESERVE

Jade Bar is an intimate venue with elegant green marble interiors, offering an immersive experience and a selection of premium spirits and champagne.

At Jade Bar, sophistication is reimagined through a carefully designed journey that includes signature martinis, exclusive champagne cocktails, and elevated bites like caviar and anchovies.

Guests can immerse themselves in unique tasting rituals, including a sensory collaboration with Le Labo featuring the Tè Noir fragrance. This ritual now opens with a delicate scent infusion inspired by Tè Noir, setting the mood for the evening, and concludes with a farewell gift of a perfume sample leaving a lasting sensory memory of the experience.

Working alongside the world’s finest spirits and champagne houses, Jade Bar offers an exclusive yet playful experience that perfectly embodies The Rome EDITION’s spirit of style, sophistication, and discovery.
