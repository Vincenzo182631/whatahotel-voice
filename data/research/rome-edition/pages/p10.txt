# Getting Here

- From Roma Termini Train Station
- From Leonardo da Vinci Rome - Fiumicino Airport
- From Rome - Ciampino Airport

## From Roma Termini Train Station

## PRIVATE TRANSFER

A private car to/from Roma Termini (2Km) will take approximately 15 minutes depending on the traffic. The transfer can be arranged by emailing the hotel concierge on concierge.rome@editionhotels.com.

## TAXI

Taxis are available at the train station on a first-come, first-served basis. This will cost around 20€ each way and will take approximately 15 minutes depending on the traffic. Tips and tolls may be additional.

## From Leonardo da Vinci Rome - Fiumicino Airport

## PRIVATE TRANSFER

A private car to/from Leonardo da Vinci Rome Fiumicino Airport (32Km) will take approximately 1 hour depending on the traffic. The transfer can be arranged by emailing the hotel concierge on concierge.rome@editionhotels.com

## TAXI

Taxis are available at the airport on a first-come, first-served basis. This will cost a fix charge of 50€ each way and will take approximately 1 hour depending on the traffic. Tips and tolls may be additional.

## UBER

Uber service is available in Rome, the cost will depend on the type of the car, and it will take approximately 1 hour depending on the traffic. Tips are additional.

## TRAIN

A no-stop train service called Leonardo Express is available at the airport towards Roma Termini main train station, just follow signage to the train terminal. This will cost a fix charge of 14€ per way and it will take 32 minutes. Trains departure is every 15minutes: from the airport to Roma Termini the service starts at 06:08am until 23:23pm; from Roma Termini to the airport the service starts at 05:20am until 22:35pm. Once you get to Roma Termini we recommend you to take a taxi or UBER to reach The Rome EDITION

## DRIVING

This route will take approximately 1 hour. Leave Fiumicino and at the roundabout, take the 1st exit, Viale del Lago di Traiano. After 2km at the roundabout, take the 1st exit, Strada Regionale 296. After almost 2.5km turn right onto Via Alessandro Guidoni. After 3km at the roundabout take the 1st exit, Via Mario De Bernardi, towards A91. Continue along A91 for 22km and then other 25km on Viadotto della Magliana and reach Via Cristoforo Colombo towards the center. Turn left onto Viale delle Terme di Caracalla and then turn right onto Via Luigi Petroselli. Turn right onto Piazza Venezia. Turn right onto Via Cesare Battisti. At the roundabout, take the second exit onto Via 24 Maggio, continue on Via del Quirinale, then Via Venti Settembre and turn left on Salita di S. Nicola da Tolentino. Turn right on Via Barberini and then slightly on the left to reach Via Leonida Bissolati. At the next light turn left on Via di San Basilio. The destination is on the left around the corner with Salita di San Nicola da Tolentino.

## From Rome - Ciampino Airport

## PRIVATE TRANSFER

A private car to/from Ciampino Rome Airport (17Km) will take approximately 45 minutes depending on the traffic. The transfer can be arranged by emailing the hotel concierge on concierge.rome@editionhotels.com.

## TAXI

Taxis are available at the airport on a first-come, first-served basis. This will cost a fix charge of 31€ each way and will take approximately 45 minutes depending on the traffic. Tips and tolls may be additional.

## UBER

Uber service is available in Rome, the cost will depend on the type of the car, and it will take approximately 45 minutes depending on the traffic. Tips are additional.

## DRIVING

This tour will take approximately 45 minutes. Leave Ciampino airport and follow the road towards Piazza Francesco Baracca and at the roundabout take the first exit to Via Appia Nuova/SS7 for Roma Center for almost 10km and then turn left onto Via Appia Nuova. At the roundabout take exit 4 still on Via Appia Nuova. Continue on Piazzale Appio and Piazza San Giovanni in Laterano. Turn right and take Via Emanuele Filiberto. Turn slightly on the left onto Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II and then turn right still on Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II. Turn left on Via Carlo Alberto, then get to Piazza Santa Maria Maggiore and turn left on Via dell'Esquilino. Continue on Piazza Esquilino and straight on to Via Torino. At the roundabout take exit 3 onto Via Venti Settembre and turn right on Salita di San Nicola da Tolentino. Turn right on Via Barberini and then slightly on the left to reach Via Leonida Bissolati. At the next light turn left on Via di San Basilio. The destination is on the left around the corner with Salita di San Nicola da Tolentino.
