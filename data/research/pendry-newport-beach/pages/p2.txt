# Newport Beach Accommodations

## A Newport Beach resort layered with signature Pendry style in every guestroom and suite.

A beacon of Southern California style. Pendry Newport Beach welcomes guests with 295 guestrooms, suites and accessible hotel rooms layered with contemporary comfort, design and amenities. An Orange County resort featuring a world-class Spa Pendry, signature dining, stunning pool setting and more, all steps from some of the best shopping, dining and cultural attractions in Newport Beach.

## Rooms

Pendry Newport Beach features 295 guestrooms with sophisticated and contemporary interiors, gracious amenities and all the comforts of a true modern luxury getaway. Many guestrooms feature ocean, bay and/or harbor views and all are adorned with Exclusively Pendry bath products, Pendry signature bedding, state-of-the-art technology, curated mini bars and more.

View Rooms

## Suites

Perched above the Newport coastline, Pendry Newport Beach’s 114 suites feature spacious interiors by Studio Munge, generous amenities and a California casual sensibility infused with detail and luxury. Many suites feature ocean, bay and/or harbor views and all are adorned with Exclusively Pendry bath products, Pendry signature bedding, state-of-the-art technology, curated mini bars and more.

View Suites

## Accessible

Our Accessible selection of rooms, studios and suites offer adapted features such as accessible bathrooms with roll-in showers and handrails. Accessible accommodations are appointed with lowered closet rods, light switches and bedroom wheelchair access.
