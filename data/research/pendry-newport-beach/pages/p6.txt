# Things To Do In Newport Beach

## Explore Southern California in a Whole New Way.

There’s more to Newport Beach. Find it all here with personalized guides, special events, exclusive collaborations, neighborhood favorites and more. Only at Pendry.

Discover Experiences

Only the exceptional. Explore a one-of-a-kind selection of extraordinary experiences, catered exclusively to our Pendry Newport Beach resort guests with awe-inspiring opportunities to see Southern California in a whole new way.

- Discover Newport Beach Experiences

Beaches & Pool

Sun-soaked getaways start here. Discover laid-back luxury at Pendry Newport Beach’s pool or venture just moments away to the region’s most iconic beaches. From Corona Del Mar to Crystal Cove, explore a scenic stretch of surf, sand and style.

- Discover Beaches & Pool Experiences

Paintbox

Children first. Paintbox keeps your youngest travelers engaged and inspired all day with custom activities, events, arts & crafts and more, tailored to their age and personal interests and always filled with a serious dose of fun.﻿

- Discover Paintbox

Neighborhood

Insider’s outlook. An easy to access guide to our favorite everything in Newport Beach and beyond, including Orange County’s finest cultural attractions, coastal highlights and must-see destinations from Fashion Island to world-renowned theme parks.﻿

- Discover Neighborhood Experiences

Partners

Great minds think alike. Pendry is proud to partner with a curated selection of leading brands, including Cadillac, Heidi Merrick, UPPABaby and others, to offer exclusive services, amenities, activities and more to our guests, making every stay an exceptional one.﻿

- Discover Pendry Newport Beach Partners
