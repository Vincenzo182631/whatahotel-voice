## Cuisine: AmericanWillowsong

The World on the Wharf DC

The founding fathers made their homes in the idyllic mid-Atlantic region for a reason: our bountiful farmland, pristine waters, and moderate climate immediately made the New World feel like paradise. And that tradition continues today, with some of the world’s finest farms, fishing, wineries, breweries, and distilleries all located just minutes from the nation’s capital. At Willowsong, Chef Jeffrey Williams capitalizes on these spectacular seasonal products to serve an ever-evolving menu of creative, thoughtful American cuisine, perfect for every occasion – or no occasion at all.

### Happy Hour at Willowsong

Unwind at Willowsong with a thoughtfully crafted Happy Hour featuring seasonal cocktails, curated wines, and chef-driven bites, all set against the energy of The Wharf. Join us weekdays from 4 to 6 p.m. for a relaxed, refined way to gather.

### Thanksgiving at Willowsong

Celebrate Thanksgiving with a three-course prix fixe menu for $95 per person, featuring holiday favorites including Roasted Turkey and a selection of seasonal starters, sides, and desserts. Gather with family and friends over classics like roasted sweet potato, green beans, mac & cheese, and mashed potatoes & gravy, followed by a choice of festive desserts.

Make A Reservation

### Thanksgiving To-Go from Willowsong

Bring the flavors of Thanksgiving home with a chef-curated feast prepared by the culinary team at Willowsong. Enjoy all of your holiday favorites, including Roasted Turkey or Sea Salt & Herb Prime Rib, paired with classic seasonal sides, starters, and desserts. Select from our menu, leave the cooking to us, and enjoy a thoughtfully prepared Thanksgiving meal at home.

Contact Willowsong at 202-878-8560 for details and reservations.

### The Watering Hole

Located on lively Wharf Street, The Watering Hole, open seasonally, is an outdoor patio bar overlooking the Washington Channel. Enjoy a frosty beer, a glass of wine, or an expertly mixed cocktail while people watching or ship spotting.
