### We would definitely stay again!

6 days ago

I love the Intercontinental Brand, and the Intercontinental Wharf was lovely.

\\* We couldn't close our balcony door once we opened it. The door frame was out of whack. It was eventually fixed.

### Falls short

8 days ago

Unfortunately, this is not the stay I was anticipating. For the price and the 5\* rating, I expected more. Arrived late due to a delayed flight and check-in was fine enough. The person assisting gave a nice upgrade to a corner room. As new platinum elite, I expected a welcome amenity, none of which was asked/provided. When we got to the new room, it was set up for 1 guest. We asked for additional slippers that never came. Next AM, we inquired about the Wharf shuttle that is advertised on the site. Turns out, it's not run by the hotel and they have no info on it. That's fine but then don't advertise it. Turn down service needs to be requested, which I did at 6pm when we were leaving the room for dinner. I was told it was too late to request the service. Again, fine to say we have to request but if there's a cut-off, say so. Next day, we order to-go for lunch from Willowsong. They don't have what we'd like but they say it's available for in-room dining. I ask if we can do in-room but make it to-go because, we have to go. They say yes. In room dining comes with the full tray. Also, we asked for 2 waters. They send up 2 large GLASS bottles of Acqua Panna. If I told you I had to leave, perhaps this wasn't the smartest choice. We left them. After check out, then came the bill. Breakfast included as part of Chase perks. It says 2 daily breakfasts. When you go to the restaurant, you're given a separate menu with offerings, no prices. One can assume this is for the "free" breakfast. Turns out there's a max charge of $80. They don't tell you this, there's no way for you to know. When disputed, it has to go for "manager" approval. It's been about 5 days since check out and no resolution yet. Over all, disappointing. Will stay somewhere else next time.
