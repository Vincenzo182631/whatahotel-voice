## Wellness Awaits at The Spa at The Wharf

Escape to The Wharf Spa for rejuvenating facials and restorative massage experiences, designed for both hotel guests and locals alike. From indulgent, extended rituals to express treatments for on-the-go relaxation, every service is crafted to restore balance and elevate your day. Guests also enjoy access to private locker rooms, opulent showers, and the serene Oasis Lounge—perfect for unwinding before or after your treatment. Contact the spa at wharfspa@ihg.com or (202) 878-8484.

### The Wharf Executive Massage

Immerse yourself in a luxurious massage that blends Swedish techniques with the tranquil energy of the Potomac River.  This treatment is designed to leave you feeling completely at ease.

Massage Services

### Relax & Run

A perfectly efficient 25-minute reset for guests on the go. This targeted, fast-acting massage focuses on areas of tension to release tight muscles and boost circulation in minimal time. Ideal for lunch breaks, travelers, or anyone needing a quick refresh.

Massage Services

### Jet Lag & Restorative Sleep Experience

A deeply indulgent full-body experience designed to counteract the effects of travel, overstimulation, and disrupted sleep. This calming therapeutic massage eases fatigue and restores your natural rhythm with slow, soothing techniques.

Signature Treatments

### Couples Signature Massage

Reconnect and unwind side by side with a deeply relaxing shared experience. Flowing therapeutic techniques, warm compresses, and aromatic elements melt tension while improving circulation and calming the mind. Choose from a 50, 80, or 110-minute service option.
