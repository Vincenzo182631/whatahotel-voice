Overlooking The Wharf, Washington D.C.'s vibrant new waterfront district, InterContinental Washington D.C. – The Wharf offers a resort-like retreat with effortless access to the city's most iconic attractions. Enjoy spectacular views of the Potomac River, a remarkable rooftop pool, and a tranquil onsite spa. Explore the nearby National Mall, Washington Monument, and the Smithsonian museums. Rest in splendor in our sophisticated and spacious D.C. waterfront hotel rooms and suites.

How far is InterContinental Washington D.C. - The Wharf from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Washington D.C. - The Wharf is conveniently located near Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA), approximately 4.7 miles away (7.56 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Washington D.C. - The Wharf?

InterContinental Washington D.C. - The Wharf is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- The Anthem
- Museums
- Monuments and History
- Georgetown
- Washington Monument - 1.3 miles away (2.09 kilometers)
- Applaud a Show
- The Cherry Blossom Festival

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Washington D.C. - The Wharf puts you within easy reach.
