# Location

### Location

Just 35 minutes (14 km) from Ben Gurion Airport, The David Kempinski Tel Aviv is prominently located in the centre of Tel Aviv's promenade, facing the Tel Aviv Beach, between the Marina and Jaffa, with vast views of the Mediterranean Sea. Combining the waterside tranquillity with the city’s buzzing cultural scene, the hotel provides guests with an ultimate luxurious urban sanctuary.

**VIP meet & greet airport service and transportation** to the hotel is available at 530 USD for up to 3 guests. Please contact concierge.telaviv@kempinski.com or +972 74 766 2733 for various options and booking arrangements, as well as for detailed directions if arriving by car or different means of transportation. For more details, please visit the Concierge section of our website.

**Underground parking** at the hotel is available for the hotel guests (subject to availability; no advance reservations) at 30 USD per night, excluding VAT. Parking rates for public (non-hotel guests) may vary and are displayed at the entrance to the parking. Please note that valet parking is not offered at our hotel.
