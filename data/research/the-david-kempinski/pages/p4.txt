# Our Hotel

### Welcome to Tel Aviv

Perched in the centre of the world-renowned Tel Aviv Promenade, The David Kempinski Tel Aviv sets a new benchmark in luxury hospitality in the city. Combining personalised service and sleek interiors that are complemented by the expansive views of the Mediterranean Sea, the hotel is the newest place to be and be seen.

The David Kempinski Tel Aviv boasts sumptuous rooms and suites, kosher dining experiences, including a cocktail lounge and cigar bar, a swimming pool, a luxurious spa and an extensive events space for corporate and social gatherings. Discover the wonders of the city, then return to The David Kempinski Tel Aviv to relax and indulge in its lavish surroundings.

Kempinski White Glove Services has been implemented to ensure a healthy and safe environment for all our guests and employees worldwide, at all times.
