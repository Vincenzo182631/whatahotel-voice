# Serpenti Beach Club

## Serpenti Beach Clvb

A vibrant beachfront destination that pays homage to Bvlgari’s most iconic symbol, every facet of the experience of the Serpenti Beach Clvb reflects the Italian Art of Living. Long associated with magnetism, fluidity, and transformation, the Serpenti motif is expressed in the sinuous symbolism that appears on cabanas, daybeds, and terraces, their golden-orange hues vibrant against the turquoise waters of Jumeira Bay. Its charms are revealed throughout the day, shifting from the brilliance of the morning sun to evening’s languid rays. The beach moves to its own pace, a world with its own rhythms, expressed in an ongoing dialogue between location and aesthetic. Home to a distinctive ambience that transcends physical space, Serpenti Beach Clvb evokes a way of living, and the experience is punctuated by continuous refreshments, including fruit, light bites, and signature Bvlgari cocktails.
