# Alicetta Bistro, overlooking the sea

### Perched on terraces that float between the magnificent garden and the sea, Alicetta offers an enchanting view and it becomes a bona fide bistro.

### Here, one can enjoy artfully crafted aperitifs complemented by enticing starters and unforgettable mains that narrate the tale of Sicily and the very soul of the Mediterranean that surrounds us.

Venice regained

### The Concept

Led by Chef Fulvio Pierangelini, the kitchen celebrates exceptional raw ingredients, mostly local with a touch of the unexpected, expressed with passion and respect in every dish.

#### Every Thursday

### Dj Nights at Focu

Homegrown vegetables, Sicilian sausages and fine Italian cuts are grilled over open flames, for an evening built around the fire. Silver light shimmers on the sea as the DJ’s beats mingle with easy conversation.

#### Every Wednesday

### A Night in Palermo

Authentic flavours, music and the conviviality of a family table, enjoyed without haste as the night takes on the rhythm of celebration. An aperitivo at sunset, before anchovies with wild fennel, courgette parmigiana, and scorpionfish are served.
