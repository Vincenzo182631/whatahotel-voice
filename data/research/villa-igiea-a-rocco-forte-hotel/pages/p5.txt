# Igiea Terrazza Bar: a Cocktail Bar in Palermo

### Overlooking the shimmering Gulf of Palermo, Igiea Terrazza Bar is where elegance, heritage and Mediterranean light come together. Set among 1950s frescoes and timeless surroundings, discover the pleasure of aperitivo through refined cocktails and contemporary Sicilian flavours. From the panoramic terrace, take in the view of the sea as the evening unfolds with music, conversation and unforgettable moments.

Venice regained

### Synesthesia

Synesthesia is an act of love. A collection of emotions, where the senses converge and experience unfolds beyond itself.  Taste becomes feeling, feeling becomes place, and place becomes a presence beyond name, yet instantly recognised. Each cocktail a sensation, each sensation a story, revealed at last.

### Groovy Friday

Palermo's vibrant music scene comes to life at the Igiea Terrazza Bar. Enjoy live performances from talented artists every Friday, with cocktails by Maestro Salvatore Calabrese and dance under the stars.

### Afternoon Tea

Afternoon tea is always a treat but especially so on the terrace of Igiea Terrazza Bar. Take your time choosing from our selection of tea and coffee, as you anticipate the arrival of delicate finger sandwiches.
