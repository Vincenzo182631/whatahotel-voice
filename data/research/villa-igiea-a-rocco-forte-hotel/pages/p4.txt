# Florio: Restaurant by chef Fulvio Pierangelini in Palermo

### A sensory journey through Sicilian aromas and culture, evoking the timeless elegance of Palermo’s Belle Époque.

### The Menu

An expression of our savoir-faire and the bounty of this Mediterranean island: sea-fresh fish, raw crustaceans, baked and grilled specialties, alongside traditional Sicilian fresh pasta, fritti and arancini. Designed by Chef Fulvio Pierangelini, the menu reflects a philosophy of simplicity, generosity and ultimate quality, celebrating the true essence of Sicilian cuisine, sincere and authentic.

View menu

### Sunday Brunch

Every Sunday from mid October

Gather friends and family for a feast of traditional Italian dishes, with live music and stunning views of the Azure Tyrrhenian Sea on our sunny terrace. Save space for dessert, as you can take away a box of homemade pastries baked by our chefs.

### The Story of Florio

Built in 1900 at the height of the Belle Époque, Villa Igiea was transformed by the Florio family from a private palazzo into one of Sicily’s most refined hotels, welcoming artists, royalty and international elite. Step back into Palermo’s Belle Époque, where the Florios’ most distinguished guests once dined in this historic Louis XVI hall.
