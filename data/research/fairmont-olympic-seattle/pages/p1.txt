## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Olympic Hotel located?

The Fairmont Olympic Hotel is situated in downtown Seattle at 411 University Street, between 4th and 5th Avenues. The hotel occupies an entire city block, with Seneca Street on the opposite side.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Olympic Hotel?

Check-in Time: 4:00 p.m.

Check-out Time: 11:00 a.m.

Late check-out or early check-in is available upon request and subject to availability. Additional fees may apply. Please contact the front desk for more information.

Does Fairmont Olympic Hotel have a swimming pool?

Head to the second floor to enjoy our indoor heated pool and hot tub. The 42-foot pool is perfect for laps or relaxation. Located in a sunny solarium with a skylight, it's bathed in natural light. Pool and Hot Tub Hours: Daily \| 8:00 a.m. – 9:00 p.m.

Does Fairmont Olympic Hotel have a spa?

Discover Penelope & The Beauty Bar on the Lower Lobby for European skincare and body treatments such as facial toning, contouring wraps, massages, sugaring, and nail services. Explore our boutique for exclusive beauty products, fresh flowers, and unique gifts. Open daily from 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.

Is Fairmont Olympic Hotel pet-friendly?

Fairmont Olympic Hotel welcomes dogs and cats with a $75 USD cleaning fee per stay. Guests are responsible for any damage or injuries caused by their pets. Please notify our reservations or front desk team for treats and amenities.
