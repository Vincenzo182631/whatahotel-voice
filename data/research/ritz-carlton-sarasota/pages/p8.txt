Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Sarasota
A Sparkling Holiday Season
The Ritz-Carlton, Sarasota is proud to offer an extensive array of seasonal experiences, culinary delights, wellness offerings and family friendly activities taking place from late November through New Year's Day. Surprises are in store as the resort decks the halls, ensuring each moment is merry and bright. There is no better place to celebrate the end of 2026 than The Ritz-Carlton, Sarasota.
Elevate Your Holiday Stay
Plan ahead for a warmer winter escape to Sarasota. This season, indulge in luxurious accomodations that blend coastal elegance with modern style. Our hotel offers a variety of premium rooms, suites and a renowned Club Level designed for a memorable and comfortable stay this season.

Festive Experiences
Programming For All Ages
Thanksgiving Grand Ballroom Brunch
Let us do the cooking. Experience our legendary traditional Thanksgiving feast in The Ritz-Carlton Grand Ballroom featuring chef-attended action stations and an unforgettable buffet spread for adults and children alike. Reservations are required for this November 26 event.
Reserve Now
Nutcracker Tea
Celebrate the magic of the season at our Nutcracker Tea with The Sarasota Ballet School and Studio Company, where festive treats meet enchanting characters. Reservations are required for both seatings on December 6.
Reserve Now
Holiday Grand Ballroom Brunch
Tis the season to indulge in an exquisite brunch in The Ritz-Carlton Grand Ballroom on December 25 featuring chef-attended action stations, activities for children and festive entertainment. Reservations are required.
Reserve Now
Breakfast with Santa on the Bay
Have breakfast with Santa himself. Children will enjoy fun, interactive activities including face painting, balloon art, cookie decorating and photos with Santa. The breakfast buffet will be served on The Bayview Lawn. Reservations are required for the December 5 and 19 events.
Reserve Now
Gingerbread House or Dreidel Decorating
While donning chef’s hats and aprons, decorate a festive gingerbread house or gingerbread dreidel guided by The Ritz-Carlton’s pastry team. Light refreshments will be served. Reservations are required for the December 13 and 20 events.
Reserve Now
S'mores on the Beach
Savor golden hour and enjoy an evening of fireside indulgence with gourmet s’mores at The Beach Club.
Reserve Now
Hanukkah Celebration Bundle
Celebrate the Fetival of Lights with your family. Light a candle each evening and enjoy traditional Hanukkah delights. Reserve with the front desk for delivery December 4 through 12.
Holiday Retail Therapy Sip and Shop
Find yourself in need of the perfect gift for that special someone? Stop by the resort's Signature Shop for your last minute gifts and receive a glass of bubbly to enjoy while shopping. The Sip and Shop event occurs Tuesdays at 4 p.m. December 8 through 29.
Lobby Activations
Overnight guests can indulge in daily surprise and delight experiences in the lobby including culinary creations from the resort’s talented chefs, decadent holiday-inspired frosted drinks and mocktails, refreshing juices, themed activities and more.
Holiday Happenings and Amenities
Explore Resort Offerings
The Ritz-Carlton Club Level
The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge invites guests to discover their destination in distinct ways. Hosted by a dedicated concierge, this elevated stay extends beyond the luxury accommodations and into the Club Lounge with space exclusive to Club guests. Specialty holiday programming designed to delight all ages is available November 20 through January 1.
Learn More
The Ritz-Carlton Beach Club
Embrace the warmth of the season. Our private Beach Club on Lido Key offers guests a beautiful white sand Gulf-front heated zero-entry pool, distinctive beach activities, Ridley’s Porch restaurant and elegant ladies’ and gentlemen’s changing facilities. Located just three miles away, a complimentary shuttle is provided for resort guests. The Beach Club is exclusive to resort guests and members.

Closest to the Pole
Players at The Ritz-Carlton Golf Club that complete The Lion’s Den in par or better receive a shirt from the golf shop celebrating the achievement. Designed by Tom Fazio, our private course resides 13 miles away from the resort at Lakewood Ranch.The Golf Club is exclusive to resort guests and members only.
Learn More
Wintry Wellness
The holidays are a time of relaxation and rejuvenation. Our fully reimagined Spa and Salon is happy to offer festive services such as the Winter Luxe Infusion Skin Ritual and Silver Birch Manicure & Pedicure. Day passes are available for the Healing Waters amenities on ResortPass. Hotel guests can enjoy a 2.3 mile complimentary guided Jingle Jog (Dec. 14) or Bike Ride with Santa and his Reindeer (Dec. 15) over the Ringling Bridge.
