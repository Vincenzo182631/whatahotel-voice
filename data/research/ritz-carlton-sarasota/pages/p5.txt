Our Exclusive Paradise
The Ritz-Carlton Beach Club
Located just three miles from the main hotel on the beautiful white sand beaches of Lido Key, The Ritz-Carlton Beach Club offers guests a private Gulf of Mexico beach, a full array of distinctive beach activities, a zero-entry pool, elegant changing facilities and first-class dining. Our luxury private beach club in Sarasota is open daily from 9 a.m. to sundown and is available exclusively to resort guests and members.
Accessibility and Transportation
Access to The Ritz-Carlton Beach Club is available exclusively to resort guests and members only. Our beach club in Sarasota is located three miles from the main hotel on Lido Key. A complimentary shuttle runs hourly between the resort and the club beginning daily at 9 a.m.
Poolside Bliss
Spend a day under the sun in our zero-entry outdoor pool, heated year-round for guest comfort. Depending on preference, a jacuzzi is also available steps from the pool. The pool offers a calming respite for guests and boasts an unmatched view of the shimmering waters of the Gulf of Mexico.

Waterfront Dining
Ridley's Porch
Named for the rare Kemp’s ridley sea turtles that frequent our shores, this restaurant offers a relaxed waterfront dining experience centered on locally inspired dishes. Ridley's Porch is available exclusively to guests and members of our luxury beach club in Sarasota.

Beach Club Features
The Lido Key Tiki Bar
Open to the public daily from 11 a.m. to sunset, this authentic tiki bar serves expertly crafted tropical cocktails, island-inspired fare and Gulfside views.
Learn More
Kemp's Market
This convenient marketplace offers a healthy grab-and-go option to incorporate into your beach day. The market also includes beach day essentials, luxurious resort wear, and accessories.
Learn More
Elevated Ladies' and Gentlemen's Facilities
Available for guests' convenience are expansive locker rooms featuring storage, showers, hair dryers, beauty products and more. These facilities have everything necessary while away from the main hotel for the day.
Events at The Beach Club
The Beach Club is a breathtaking destination for events of every size and objective. Start forever with a sunset beach wedding, or host a business meeting or celebration overlooking the Gulf of Mexico. Events at our Sarasota hotel with beach access will wow your guests as they make memories that will last a lifetime.
Learn More
Beach and Poolside Food and Beverage Service
Whether lounging by the pool or enjoying the white sand of Lido Beach, our Ladies and Gentlemen are happy to cater to your dining needs. An imaginative menu is available for you to enjoy from the comfort of your beach or pool chair.
Luxurious Pool and Beach Seating
Enjoy poolside perfection by reserving your private pool cabana and other amenities alongside the zero-entry pool. For those who prefer beach accommodations, beach daybeds are available at our luxury private beach club in Sarasota.
