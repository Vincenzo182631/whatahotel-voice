Golf
Imagine a golf course where velvet greens gently undulate around 12 shimmering lakes. Where each of the 18 holes is expertly framed to be both challenging and rewarding. Where the only thing more beautiful than the game at hand is the surrounding view. With this in your mind’s eye, you have The Ritz-Carlton Golf Club in Sarasota. Designed by Tom Fazio, the Sarasota golf course resides 13 miles away from the resort at Lakewood Ranch. Play this par-72 course, and you will see why no golf vacation will ever be the same.

A Picturesque
Championship Golf Course
Located on 315 acres of lush, tropical landscape, this 18-hole course is magnificently sculpted to reflect the natural beauty of its environment. It features abundant elevation changes and charming lakes throughout, which is unique to this golf course in Sarasota, FL.
Accessibility
Access to The Ritz-Carlton Golf Club is available exclusively to resort guests and members. For more information about the golf experience, tee times, and dining, please call The Ritz-Carlton Golf Club directly. Transportation from the resort to the Club can be arranged with advanced notice at the same number.
The Lion’s Den
The last three holes of this Sarasota golf course are some of the hardest consecutive holes in Florida. The distances are 16th hole 231 yards par 3, 17th hole 472 yards par 4 and 18th hole 473 yards par 4. This trifecta is comparable to The Snake Pit at Copper Head and The Bear Trap at PGA National. Players that complete each hole in par or better receive a prize from the golf shop celebrating the achievement.

Expert Golf Instruction
Perfect your game with one of our PGA Member instructors with a one-hour lesson or 9-hole guided gameplay. Players can also take advantage of our state-of-the-art practice facilities featuring a chipping area, short game area, chipping greens, practice tees, and putting greens. To inquire about lessons, please call (941) 309-2900.
Caddie Concierge Program
Extending the unparalleled tradition of service of The Ritz-Carlton onto the green, this program pairs each group with a knowledgeable professional that anticipates your every need. Each Caddie Concierge offers course strategy and design information, hole description and target lines, golf ball location, yardage stats, golf club cleaning, and attending the flagsticks.
Membership
Become a member and enjoy exclusive access to up to three extraordinary clubs. For membership information, please contact the Membership Gallery at (941) 309-2521, or toll free at (888) 363-6237.
