Experiences
Between its colorful history and enticing natural landscape, Sarasota attracts visitors from around the world. Here, white-sand beaches give way to emerald fairways and Spanish architecture. Museums and art galleries are as alluring as the turquoise waves on the horizon. And at the center of it all stands our luxury hotel near downtown Sarasota, where Gulf Coast landmarks, cultural performances, championship golf and pristine beaches are all just moments away.

St. Armands Circle
Located just minutes from the resort, St. Armands Circle offers a variety of unique shops, restaurants, and boutiques. History connoisseurs can enjoy a stroll along the Circus Ring of Fame located on the inside of the circle.
Resort Activities
Fishing poles, kayaks, paddleboards, bicycles and gaming consoles are available for guest use. The resort offers one tennis court on property and two at The Ritz-Carlton Golf Club as well as racket rentals.

Sunset Sail Soirée

Available Wednesday and Saturday evenings exclusively to resort guests, resort members, and Ritz-Carlton residents.

The Wellness Center
The Wellness Center at our hotel in downtown Sarasota offers state-of-the-art cardiovascular fitness equipment including treadmills, ellipticals, recumbent and upright bicycles, personal screens, Peloton™ bicycles, a complete TechnoGym strength-training circuit, mirrored free-weight area, intimate movement studio for Pilates and yoga instruction, classes, and personal training. Open 24 hours.

Local Attractions
From its downtown location, The Ritz-Carlton, Sarasota hotel is near the airport and enjoys convenient access to the city’s array of attractions. Spend a day browsing the shops of St. Armands Circle or observing the impressive art collections of the Ringling Museum. Or head to nearby Marina Jacks for a dinner cruise along the Gulf.
