Relax and unwind in our luxury Sarasota suites and rooms
Suites and Rooms
With marine hues and spacious layouts, each of our luxury hotel rooms in Sarasota, Florida pays tribute to the tranquility of the landscape. Drink in sweeping Florida views from your private balcony, retreat to the marble-trimmed bathroom for a spa-like escape, or simply savor the luxury of modern design.
The Ritz-Carlton Club Level
The Ritz-Carlton, Sarasota features The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge® for guests seeking a heightened level of luxury and exclusivity. The Club Lounge caters to leisure guests and business travelers alike, offering a unique setting to conduct business meetings, plan family adventures, or just simply relax and unwind. It features a dedicated Concierge who is readily available to assist with your every need and offers multiple culinary presentations throughout the day.
Learn More
Ritz-Carlton Suite
Nestled alongside Sarasota Bay, the Ritz-Carlton Suite stands as the most lavish luxury accommodation in our Sarasota, Florida resort. The sizeable living area exudes an air of sophistication, allowing for a relaxing vacation or small gatherings in Sarasota, Florida. This hotel suite's expansive windows and private balconies offer panoramic views of the Bay and marina.
Learn More
Sarasota Suite
With views of the city and Bay, the Sarasota Suite allows guests to unwind comfortably. In addition to an opulent king bedroom, the suite features a separate parlor with ample dining area and a spacious seating area. Two full balconies provide guests the ability to step outside and soak in their surroundings.
Learn More
Bay View Suite
For those looking for an elevated stay, the Bay View Suite provides just that. Featuring a tranquil living area separate from the bedroom, guests are able to spread out during their visit. A private balcony treats its occupants to charming sunset views.

Pet Policy
The Ritz-Carlton, Sarasota welcomes up to two (2) dogs (max of 30 lbs each) per room. Dogs cannot be left unattended in rooms and must be on leash in common areas.
Dogs that are not classified as service animals are not permitted at dining outlets, the spa, and beach and pool areas.
Non-refundable pet deposit per stay: $250
Non-refundable pet fee per night: $20
