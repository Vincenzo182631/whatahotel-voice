The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” this Sarasota resort with Club Lounge enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.

Club Lounge Access

Dedicated Concierge
From dining reservations to planning excursions around town, the dedicated Club Concierge at our Sarasota resort with Club Lounge is here to ease your experience and help create lasting memories. Enjoy the finest personal service from our Ladies and Gentlemen who ensure all your needs are fulfilled, including stress-free checkout upon departure and garment pressing. The dedicated Club Concierge team is available from 7 AM - 10 PM.

In Good Taste
Daily Culinary Presentations
Five times each day, the Club Lounge offers complimentary food and beverage presentations. These daily offerings — breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea, hors d’oeuvres and cordials — tell the destination’s story through locally sourced ingredients, iconic dishes and signature drinks. Food and Beverage Presentations: Breakfast from 7 AM - 11 AM, Lunch from 12 PM to 2 PM, Light Snack from 2 PM - 4 PM, Hors d'oeuvres & Cocktails from 5 PM - 7:30 PM and Desserts & Cordials from 8 PM - 10 PM.
