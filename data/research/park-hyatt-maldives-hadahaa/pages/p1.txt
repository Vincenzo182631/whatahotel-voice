## Discover Hadahaa—Our Living Island, An Invitation to Explore

Welcome to Park Hyatt Maldives Hadahaa—a sanctuary where nature and luxury blend seamlessly in one of the Maldives’ most unspoiled settings. Located within the remote Huvadhu Atoll, the island is a retreat for ocean enthusiasts and mindful travelers, offering a chance to reconnect with the rhythms of wild island life. Encircled by a thriving 360-degree house reef, every experience—from marine conservation to castaway adventures—is grounded in authenticity and respect for the local environment. With just 51 refined villas and the exclusive Kiaali Residence, Hadahaa creates a setting of intimate luxury, genuine service, and effortless beauty. As our honored guest, discover a place shaped by natural abundance, meaningful connection, and memories that linger long after you depart.

Our Living Island​

At Hadahaa, nature is more than just a backdrop — it’s the heartbeat of every moment. Inspired by our founding family’s deep reverence for the ocean, this secluded island in the remote Huvadhu Atoll invites you into an extraordinary world where vibrant marine life, untouched landscapes, and a sense of calm define the experience. Here, mindful living meets meaningful connection, and heartfelt hospitality ensures you feel not just welcomed, but truly at home in our island sanctuary

Kiaali Residence

The 3-bedroom Kiaali Residence sits above the most vibrant part of our house reef, offering direct ocean access from your doorstep. This expansive retreat features a private infinity pool, 24-hour butler service and seamless indoor-outdoor living for the ultimate in luxury and tranquility.

Hadahaa’s House Reef

Our 360° house reef surrounds Hadahaa in a living ring, ensuring world-class snorkeling is always just steps away from every villa, every beach, and every sunrise. Here, there’s no single patch or “best spot”—the entire shoreline is an open invitation into a thriving underwater sanctuary. With every entry point, guests discover vibrant coral, schools of fish, and the remarkable diversity that makes Hadahaa truly unique. This sanctuary of biodiversity is all around you, accessible at all times—offering unlimited explorations and unforgettable encounters in every direction.

Kids Activities and Family Experiences in the Maldives

At Park Hyatt Maldives Hadahaa, we offer a unique approach to family holidays in the Maldives. Instead of a traditional indoor kids’ club, we provide outdoor learning and adventure-based activities that inspire children to explore, create, and connect with the natural beauty of the islands.​

​Our philosophy encourages young guests to engage with the outdoors. Children can discover the wonders of Maldives marine life through hands-on sessions in our marine lab with a resident marine biologist, build sandcastles on sun-kissed beaches, and take part in local crafts and sustainable activities. With no screens or gaming consoles, every moment is designed to spark imagination, curiosity, and a lasting connection with nature.​

Activities are carefully curated on a weekly schedule, offering a variety of experiences that combine education, creativity, and fun. Young guests develop essential life skills, gain insights into marine ecosystems, and enjoy artistic and recreational projects — all in a safe, inspiring, and nurturing environment.​

​At Park Hyatt Maldives Hadahaa, children don’t simply play — they embark on a journey of discovery, exploration, and unforgettable memories, surrounded by the pristine beaches, turquoise waters, and serene beauty of the Maldives.​

Hadahaa Ocean Lab & Immersive Educational Experiences​

**Explore marine life, coral reefs and conservation at Ocean Lab, with immersive marine experiences for all ages at Park Hyatt Maldives Hadahaa.​**

At Park Hyatt Maldives Hadahaa, the island becomes a living classroom. Surrounded by a thriving 360° house reef and the biodiverse waters of Huvadhu Atoll, the Ocean Lab invites guests of all ages to explore the marine life, ecosystems and natural wonders of the Maldives.​

Guided by our marine biology team, discover the science behind the ocean through immersive experiences in marine education and conservation. Learn about coral reefs and plankton, explore the fascinating world of marine life and gain a deeper understanding of the ecosystems that surround Hadahaa.​

**Discover, Explore, Connect​**

From Reef Talks with Marine Experts and Plankton Observation to Shark Ecology and, Snorkel with Purpose each experience offers a closer look at the underwater world. Whether you are discovering the Maldives’ coral reefs for the first time or deepening your knowledge of marine conservation, these experiences invite you to see the ocean from a new perspective. ​

For families, children can learn through engaging, age-appropriate activities, while adults and curious explorers can delve deeper into the marine environment alongside our experts.​

More than a place to learn, the Ocean Lab is an invitation to connect with the marine life and natural environment of the Maldives—and to understand why protecting it matters.

Curated Experiences

Park Hyatt Maldives Hadahaa, every moment invites you to connect—with nature, culture, and yourself. Our curated experiences go beyond the ordinary, seamlessly blending the island’s pristine beauty with authentic, inspiring encounters.

Whether snorkeling vibrant house reefs, savoring a private sandbank dinner under the stars, mastering Maldivian culinary arts, or sailing into the sunset on a traditional dhoni, each experience is personalized to your interests, making your stay truly unforgettable.

Diving, Snorkeling & Freediving

At Park Hyatt Maldives Hadahaa, vibrant marine life is just steps away. Surrounded by a pristine 360-degree house reef in the biodiverse Huvadhu Atoll, our island is a paradise for divers, snorkelers, and freedivers of all levels.​

Explore colorful coral gardens with reef fish, manta rays, and sea turtles. Dive with expert guides or snorkel from the shore to discover stunning underwater scenes. For a deeper connection, freediving sessions let you immerse in the ocean’s rhythm and explore the island’s remarkable marine life.

Spa & Wellness - The Vidhun Spa

At The Vidhun Spa, experience the essence of Maldivian holistic healing in five private villas. Enjoy authentic Ayurveda therapies and Dhivehi beys-inspired massages, along with skincare and wellness rituals crafted from natural herbs and spices sourced from the atoll and Hadahaa’s own herb gardens.

Fitness Experiences - stay active during your stay with our 24-hour fitness center, fully equipped with cardio and strength-training machines to support your exercise routine in paradise.

Prepare for a More Sustainable Stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

This is luxury redefined—combining exquisite comfort with respect for nature and heritage, ensuring the Maldives’ beauty endures for generations.
