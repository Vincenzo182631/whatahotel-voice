# Parking & Transportation

## Transportation

Getting here

Domestic Transfer Details

Upon arrival at Velana International Airport in Malé, your journey to Park Hyatt Maldives Hadahaa continues with a seamless domestic flight followed by a scenic speedboat transfer, offering a gradual introduction to the untouched beauty and tranquil rhythm of the Maldives before arriving at our private island sanctuary.​

Domestic flight transfer by Maldivian Airline — approximately 60 minutes​

Speedboat transfer — approximately 30 minutes

Domestic Transfer by Maldivian Airline​

To ensure a seamless journey to Park Hyatt Maldives Hadahaa, kindly provide the full names of all guests exactly as shown on
their passports, along with international arrival and departure flight details, in order for domestic transfers to be arranged.
Please note that Maldivian confirms domestic flight schedules one day prior to arrival; therefore, exact transfer timings will be finalized accordingly.​ Round-trip transfers between Velana International Airport and the resort, inclusive of domestic flight and speedboat transfers, are available at the following rates:​ USD 690 per adult​ and USD 590 per child​. Please note that Maldivian operates independently with regard to schedules, availability, route changes, baggage handling, and other aviation-related matters. While our team will gladly assist wherever possible, the
resort is unable to accept responsibility for any airline-related disruptions or inconveniences.
