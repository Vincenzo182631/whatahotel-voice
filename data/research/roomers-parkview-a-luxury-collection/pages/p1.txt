# Welcome to Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt

## Hotel with Fine Dining, Bar & Skyline Views

Opening in 2026, Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt offers a progressive alternative to the city’s traditional luxury hotels in the prestigious Westend district. The hotel features...See More 136 rooms and suites that blend comfort and a charismatic edge for explorers and collectors seeking the art of living well. Designed by Piero Lissoni, the hotel fosters openness and natural interaction among guests and locals. Culinary highlights include Golden Phoenix by renowned restaurateur The Duc Ngo, presenting refined Chinese fine dining. A vibrant Lobby Bar and the OneSeven Bar on the 17th floor with panoramic skyline views enhance the social scene. An exclusive spa, gym, and intuitive hospitality complete the experience, combining local character with global spirit and redefining luxury in Frankfurt.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt?

The check-in time at Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of €40.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt?

On-site self parking is available at Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt. Self parking is available at Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt, with rates of €5.00 per hour, €50.00 per day, and €350.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt?

The closest airport near Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt is Frankfurt Airport (FRA). FRA is located approximately 18.7 KM from the hotel.

Does Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Roomers ParkView, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Frankfurt has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
