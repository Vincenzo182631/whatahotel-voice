# Frequently Asked Questions

### Stay in the know

#### What time can I check-in and check-out?

Check-in time is 4PM, check-out is at 11AM.

#### What’s the hotel’s reservation cancellation policy?

Although we’d love for you to stay with us, we know things may come up. Hotel cancellation policies vary based upon rate and location – contact your hotel for more information.

#### 21 Minimum Age Requirement for Check-In

Guest must be 21 years or older to check in. A valid government-issued ID is required at the time of check-in.

#### Is there Wi-Fi access at The Benjamin Royal Sonesta?

Yes – we want our guests to stay connected. Wi-Fi is available in the lobby of, and access to in-room Wi-Fi is included as part of the Hotel Facilities Fee.

#### Does The Benjamin have a restaurant? Is there room service available?

Yes – enjoy our newly opened on-site restaurant, _The Parliament New York._ _The Parliament New York_ offers morning breakfast, lunch, and dinner, as well as thoughtfully curated cocktails. Room service is available to hotel guests during select hours. Learn more at The Parliament New York.

#### What is The Benjamin’s smoking policy?

The Benjamin Royal Sonesta is a smoke-free environment. Smoking in your room will result in a $250 cleaning fee.

#### What is the “The Benjamin Facility Fee” included on my bill?

Our Hotel Facilities Fee is $45 plus tax per room, per night and provides you with the following:

- $20 Food & Beverage Credit at The Parliament Restaurant, per day\*
-  $20 Laundry/Dry Cleaning Credit, per stay
-  $15 Minibar Credit, per day\*
- Premium Wi-Fi, Unlimited Devices
- Local Calls/National, Unlimited
-  10% Off Spa Service at The Royal Reflection Wellness Spa
-  10% Off Services at Sogo Nails
-  15% Savings Voucher at Bloomingdale’s

_\*Must be redeemed daily and cannot roll over. Not valid on day of departure_

#### Are there fitness centers at The Benjamin?

Our 24-hour fitness center is located on the 3rd floor of our Midtown East hotel. We provide treadmills, elliptical trainers, exercise bikes, a Peloton bike, yoga mats and free weights for guest use. Hand towels and water are provided, along with men’s and women’s locker rooms and shower area.

#### Can I bring my pet?

Absolutely! Make Traveling with Pets Simple. We’re thrilled to welcome guests and their pets with our PAWS (Pets Are Welcome At Sonesta) program at The Benjamin Royal Sonesta. When you and your pet check in, we’ll greet you with a PAWS Welcome Letter and complimentary dog treats. Plus, we’ll have your room ready with a water dish, feeding bowl and waterproof mat. Pamper your pet with the addition of a cozy pet bed at The Benjamin Royal Sonesta.

When planning to stay at The Benjamin Royal Sonesta with your pet, please inform the reservation agent or front desk.

**Our Pet Policy:**

All Sonesta hotels are pet-friendly and welcome well-mannered pets, with no breed or weight restrictions. Up to two pets are permitted per room or suite.

At The Benjamin Royal Sonesta, a minimum fee of $75 per stay applies.

Check out Sonesta’s PAWS (Pets Are Welcome at Sonesta) program for more details.

#### Where can I learn about The Benjamin’s special offers and promotions?

The Benjamin Royal Sonesta offers a wide variety of seasonal and hotel-based promotions and special offers, which are updated regularly – explore our Special Offers to learn more.

#### What is the Rest & Renew program?

The Benjamin Royal Sonesta’s signature sleep program, thoughtfully designed to help guests achieve deeper, more restorative rest – especially while traveling. Grounded in science and tailored for comfort, the program offers a curated collection of sleep-enhancing amenities and self-renewal experiences. Guests may select from our signature 10-choice pillow menu, along with a meditation cushion, Loftie Alarm Clock, Loftie Night Light, Nodpod Body Weighted Blanket, and complimentary on-demand meditations and reading selections from Bedside Reading – each designed to support a truly restful stay.

Visit our Rest & Renew page to learn more.

#### Do the hotels offer valet services?

The Benjamin Royal Sonesta and The Fifty Sonesta offer valet parking for $75/day for a sedan or $85/day for an SUV. No in/out privileges.

The Shelburne Sonesta offers curbside valet parking for $75 a day or $85 a day for oversized vehicles, without in-and-out privileges.

The Gardens Sonesta ES Suites offers valet parking for $70 per 24-hour period for cars and $80 for oversized vehicles (SUVs and minivans). The Gardens Sonesta ES Suites self-parking option is $45 per 24-hour period for cars and $55 for oversized vehicles (SUVs and minivans) per every 24-hour period. Please note there are no in-and-out privileges for either parking option.

#### What should I do and see during my stay at The Benjamin Royal Sonesta?

Check out The Benjamin Blog for more information about some of the best local events and attractions you won’t don’t want to miss during your stay.

#### What are the accessible features of The Benjamin Royal Sonesta?

The Benjamin Royal Sonesta features accessible areas at the hotel entrance, registration desk, meeting rooms, public restrooms and fitness center as well as select guestrooms and suites.

Accessibility features in the hotel include braille signage throughout hotel, accessible, fully automatic entrance door with wide openings, lobby free of any steps, accessible registration with lowered desk, service animals welcomed, two guest elevators and one service elevator serving each level of hotel, including guestrooms, meeting rooms, fitness center and ADA public restrooms on 2nd floor and all meeting rooms on 2nd floor wheelchair accessible and free of any steps.

Room categories at The Benjamin Royal Sonesta offer an ADA-accessible option for guests to enjoy. Relax in comfort, in the newly renovated rooms and suites at The Benjamin Royal Sonesta, made complete with lowered countertop height level for easy access in the kitchenette and bathroom, handheld showers, shower control locations, grab bars and shower or tub seat grab bars, and roll-in shower available upon request.

#### When was The Benjamin Royal Sonesta last renovated?

The hotel completed a full renovation in 2024 of the lobby, Sonesta Work Suite and all guestrooms and suites in partnership with design firm DLR Group, an integrated design firm delivering architecture, engineering, interiors, planning, and building optimization for new construction, renovation, and adaptive reuse across various building types.
