## An elegant oasis next to the heart of the Vatican

A stone's throw from Rome's most important artistic centers, this exclusive temple of luxury and relaxation blends perfectly with its surroundings. Wander through its cobblestone streets, where every corner exudes culture and history. This charming five-star hotel boasts exquisite design and is surrounded by Mediterranean gardens with breathtaking panoramic views of Rome and a relaxing atmosphere.

Villa Agrippina Gran Meliá, a member of The Leading Hotels of the World, is an exclusive oasis where you can indulge yourself just a stone's throw from the Eternal City's main artistic jewels, being the closest hotel to the Vatican and the Sistine Chapel. The hotel is named after the first Roman empress and features impeccable design that exudes culture and history. It also offers panoramic views, a lush Mediterranean garden, delicious local cuisine, and a spectacular outdoor pool – something truly unique among hotels in Rome – to complete a perfect stay in this magical city.

### Timeless luxury just steps from the Vatican and Rome's historic center.

Villa Agrippina Gran Meliá is a sanctuary located between the Vatican and the famous Trastevere district. Built on the ruins of an ancient Roman villa in the heart of the city, the hotel offers spectacular views of Castel Sant'Angelo and the Tiber River. It is the perfect base for exploring the rich history, art, and gastronomy of the Eternal City.

### Activities

### Agrippina the Elder

Go on a journey through time right back to the Ancient Roman era. Start from our hotel, dwelling place of the ‘Empress Agrippina the Elder, passing the Sant Angelo Castle, before crossing the bridge to the Ara Pacis altar, dedicated to the Roman Goddess of Peace and the Mausoleum of Augustus, Agrippina’s final resting place.

,regionOfInterest=(1476.5,984.5))

### Trans Tiberim: The Trastevere district

Check out the former dwelling place of the ancient Romans in this enchanting district. Wander through the cobbled streets and peruse the antique shops and stop for a refreshing beverage in a traditional tavern. Immerse yourself in the magic of the Eternal City, with its narrow alleyways and bustling squares, with history and culture jumping out at you round every street corner. Don’t miss the San Crisogono church or a visit to a XVII century apothecary with its ancient remedies and typical old furniture.

,regionOfInterest=(1476.5,984.5))

### Jewish quarters

Founded by Pope Paul IV in 1555, the unmissable Jewish Ghetto buzzes with quirky shops and kosher restaurants, nestled among historical buildings which tell the story of Rome’s Jewish heritage. Check out the Crypta Balbi museum and the "Area Sacra" archaeological area of the Largo di Torre Argentina square, where Julius Caesar was executed.

,regionOfInterest=(1476.5,984.5))

### Temple of Claudius caves

Enjoy a fascinating tour, passing the Colosseum and the Roman Forum before heading to the ancient Temple of Claudius, dating back to 54 AD. You will be led along underground passageways, discovering beautiful small lakes before heading above ground to the timeless Roman Houses of Caelian Hill.

,regionOfInterest=(1771.5,996.5))

### Janiculum Hill

Immerse yourself in Roman history: from the emblematic Acqua Paola fountain (referred to locally as "Il Fontanone") to the stunning Botanical Gardens. Created in 1660 by Alexander VII, this is part of the Ager Vaticanus archaeological site, that the Villa Agrippina is built on. Round off the trip by checking out the Villa Farnesina museum, home to some of Raphael’s masterpieces.

,regionOfInterest=(1771.5,1181))

### Vatican City

No vacation in Rome is complete without a trip to the historical Vatican City. Villa Agrippina Gran Meliá is ideally situated between the Tiber and Trastevere rivers, just a stone’s throw away from two unmissable historical sites: Ager Vaticanus and Horti de Agrippina. St. Peter’s Basilica, with its impressive dome which is referred to locally as is “Il cuppolone”, is just a short walk away.
