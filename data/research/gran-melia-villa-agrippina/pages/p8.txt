## Fly to Tuscany, Hunt for Truffles

A scenic helicopter flight over Tuscany's medieval villages leads to a private hunt guided by an expert truffle hunter, followed by a woodland lunch with live show cooking and wine pairing among the trees

Depart from Rome Urbe Airport for a scenic round-trip helicopter flight to Siena, soaring above Tuscany's rolling hills and medieval villages. Once on the ground, join an experienced truffle hunter and his trained dog for a guided introduction to this ancient tradition, learning about varieties, seasons and centuries-old history. The experience concludes with a private woodland lunch, featuring a live show cooking of a seasonal truffle-based menu paired with carefully selected wines, for an unforgettable day between nature and flavour.

- Duration: Approximately 4-6 hours (customizable); flight approx. 55 minutes each way.
- Number of people: 2 to 6 guests.
- Good to know: we recommend booking 48 hours in advance. Not suitable for guests with truffle allergies or reduced mobility.
- What is included: private chauffeur, round-trip helicopter flight, truffle hunting experience, woodland lunch with wine pairing, insurance and operational assistance.

Departing from Rome Urbe Airport, the panoramic helicopter flight carries guests over the Tuscan countryside to Siena. Along the way, medieval villages, vineyards and gentle hills unfold below, setting the scene for an immersive day dedicated to one of Italy's most treasured culinary traditions, combining adventure, nature and authentic gastronomy in a single unforgettable experience.

Guests are asked to wear comfortable casual attire with closed-toe shoes; pets are not allowed. Booking is recommended at least 48 hours in advance, and cancellations made up to 48 hours prior are free of charge. In the event of adverse weather conditions, the experience can be rescheduled or refunded, ensuring a seamless and worry-free booking process from start to finish.
