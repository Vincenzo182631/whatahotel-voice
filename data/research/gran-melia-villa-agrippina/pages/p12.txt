## The Colosseum and Roman Forum

Perfect for first-time visitors, this experience combines luxury and history, offering a unique opportunity to explore some of Rome’s most iconic sites. With private transportation and an expert guide, this experience provides a journey through time that mixes Roman grandeur with modern-day indulgence.

Begin the adventure with a seamless pick-up from Villa Agrippina in a private transfer, setting the tone for the exclusive Roman day ahead. The first stop is the Colosseum, a monumental feat of ancient engineering and the largest amphitheatre ever built. Here, guests will explore into the gladiatorial past and admire the architectural majesty that has withstood the test of time. Following this, participants will be guided through the Roman Forum, once the bustling centre of Roman life, filled with temples, markets, and political institutions that shaped the empire.

- Duration: Approximately 6 hours
- Number of people: 6
- Please note: Bookings are required at least 48 hours in advanced.
- What does it include? Private pick up and professional tour guide. Tickets to the Colosseum and Forum and Palantine Hill. Aperitif on the famous Terraza Cattarelli.

The experience continues with a visit to Palatine Hill, a place full of history and fascinating legends. This hill is renowned as the birthplace of Rome, where Romulus and Remus were believed to have been raised by a she-wolf. As participants walk through this historic landscape, they can take in the magnificence of ancient Rome from a stunning view point. Expert guides will provide fascinating insights into each landmark, adding depth and historical context to the journey.

To conclude this exclusive experience, guests will enjoy a sparkling aperitif at Terrazza Caffarelli. From this rooftop location, they can absorb the panoramic views of the Eternal City, reflecting on the remarkable sites recently visited. This tour combines the best of ancient Roman history with luxurious touches, making it ideal for those seeking a memorable and exclusive experience in the Italian city. Whether participants are history enthusiasts or simply looking to enjoy the city’s cultural richness, this unforgettable experience offers a unique perspective on Rome's timeless beauty.
