### Local heritage flavors

Alfonso D'Auria is a master of Mediterranean authenticity, seamlessly blending it with the culinary style of his Campania roots. His dishes are a perfect balance of tradition and innovation, enhanced by local raw materials. From light and tasty creations to reinterpreted classics such as sfogliatella and ragù. D'Auria's focus on freshness and diversity is a clear reflection of his heritage and a testament to the Roman culinary scene.
