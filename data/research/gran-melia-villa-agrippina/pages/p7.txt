Depart from Rome Urbe Airport for a scenic round-trip helicopter flight to Monte Argentario, flying over the Tuscan hills and the Tyrrhenian coastline. Once landed, embark on a private yacht journey along the coast, discovering hidden coves and crystal-clear waters, with stops for swimming and relaxation in complete privacy. A lunch prepared with fresh seasonal ingredients and Mediterranean flavours is served on board, for an unforgettable day between sea and nature.

- Duration: Approximately 5-7 hours (customizable); flight approx. 40 minutes each way.
- Number of people: 2 to 6 guests.
- Good to know: we recommend booking 48 hours in advance.
- What is included: private chauffeur, round-trip helicopter flight, private yacht experience, fuel and soft drinks, lunch on board, insurance and operational assistance.
