## Unlock rare access to hidden chambers and iconic masterpieces

This exclusive Vatican tour invites participants to discover centuries of history, culture, and artistic wonders. With access to secret rooms not open to the public, this experience offers a rare opportunity to explore the Vatican in depth. Led by knowledgeable guides, it combines iconic landmarks with concealed masterpieces, creating a remarkable journey through the very essence of the Vatican.

The tour begins with exclusive access to the Vatican Museums, home to some of the world’s most renowned works of art. Participants will have the chance to explore more than 20 rooms filled with historical artefacts and masterpieces, including the famous Sistine Chapel, with its breathtaking ceiling painted by Michelangelo. This experience goes beyond the usual tour, offering entry to rarely visited areas such as the Niccoline Chapel and the original Bramante Staircase—both veiled masterpieces not accessible to the general public. Expert guides accompany the journey, providing fascinating insights into the art and history of the Vatican.

- Duration: Approximately 180 minutes
- Number of people: Up to  6 people
- Please note: Bookings are required 48 hours in advance
- What does it include? Private pick up and professional tour guide, tickets to the Vatican museum and Sistine chapel, access to exclusive areas not accessible to the public.

As the tour continues, guests will discover the inspiring beauty of St. Peter's Basilica, the largest church in the world. This architectural work of art holds centuries of religious significance and artistic brilliance, with towering columns, grand domes, and complex sculptures that tell the story of the Catholic faith. Participants will have the opportunity to explore this monumental space in detail, learning about its history and the renowned artists who contributed to its creation.

What sets this experience apart is the exclusive access to areas typically closed to the public. The Niccoline Chapel, located in the oldest part of the Apostolic Palace, offers a glimpse into a rarely seen side of Vatican history, while the original Bramante Staircase, crafted in 1505, stands as a secluded architectural masterpiece. These intimate, rarely seen spaces offer a supreme glimpse into the Vatican's immense cultural and historical richness. This experience blends iconic sites with rare discoveries, offering participants a once-in-a-lifetime experience in one of the world’s most famous religious and cultural centres.
