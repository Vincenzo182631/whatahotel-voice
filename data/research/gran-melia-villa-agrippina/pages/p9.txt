## Family cooking memories, bite by bite

Guests begin their journey by collaborating with our chef to create a fully personalised menu based on their tastes and preferences. On the day of the class, adults and children are welcomed with aprons, chef hats and all the tools they need. Adults and kids alike, ready to cook, explore flavours and share joyful moments in the kitchen together.

Families are invited into the kitchen for a playful, hands-on journey. Before the class, our chef meets with you to choose a bespoke menu that reflects every taste, allergy, and preference. During the experience, adults and little chefs cook side by side. Afterwards, everyone enjoys the dishes together, while children take home their apron, chef hat and recipe booklet.

- Duration: 2 h
- Please note: Available upon reservation only. Please book in advance by emailing gran.melia.rome@melia.com. Suitable for children aged 6 to 12, accompanied by an adult.
- What does it include: The experience includes a full kit for little chefs (apron, chef hat, child-sized tools) and all ingredients needed to prepare the dishes.

Set in the heart of Rome, inside the charming kitchens of Villa Agrippina, this experience invites adults and children to discover the joy of cooking together. Before the class, our chef listens to every preference, allergy and curiosity to create a bespoke menu that feels truly personal. On the day, little chefs and grown‑ups tie their aprons, wear their hats and learn step by step how to prepare each dish, tasting, laughing, and sharing stories along the way.

When everything is ready, the family sits down to enjoy the menu they created, surrounded by the unique atmosphere of Villa Agrippina. At the end, children take home their own apron, chef hat and recipe booklet, turning a special moment into a lasting memory.
