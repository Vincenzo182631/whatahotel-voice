## A curated journey through Italy’s best labels

Savour la dolce vita at Villa Agrippina as our sommelier guides you through one curated path of Italian wine: From North to South, discover DOCG classics or iconic Supertuscans. Each pour is paired with refined canapés to explore perfect matches. A refined gift idea that turns curiosity into confidence, and wine into a shared memory.
