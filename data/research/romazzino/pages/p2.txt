Read the Story. Opens in a new window

“Green and grey mountains tumble into the sea, a carpet of purple and yellow, red and blue flowers perfume the air.”

This was how Romazzino’s founder, The Aga Khan IV, described Costa Smeralda. When the doors first opened in the 1960’s, Romazzino immediately became an icon of Italian glamour for the world’s jet-set. Today, the blossoming landscape and iconic Michele Busiri Vici architecture come together to create a timeless sanctuary.
