Sydney

#### Crown Towers Sydney

1 Barangaroo Avenue Sydney, NSW 2000, Australia

View Map

Residing in the heart of Australia’s global city, Crown Towers Sydney offers a prestigious waterfront location and top luxury accommodation for corporate and leisure guests alike. Guests can enjoy mesmerising panoramic views of the Sydney Harbour Bridge and Opera House from the outdoor pool, bask in pure escapism at Crown Spa, and explore the many acclaimed restaurants, bars and luxury retail stores within the resort.

Blessed with natural beauty and a warm climate, Sydney is home to a vibrant culture and community. From sprawling parklands to laneway bars, the streets of Barangaroo hum with city life. Located just a few minutes walk to the CBD and connected to the local and international airports by Sydney’s newest ferry terminal, Crown is one of the best places to stay and experience all that Sydney has to offer.
