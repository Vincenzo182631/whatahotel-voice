# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central

## JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central in the heart of Beijing

Explore historic Beijing from JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central, a luxury hotel in central Beijing for family holidays, sightseeing and business travel. From Xuanwumen, visit the Forbidden City (Palace Museum), Tiananmen Square, National Museum of China and Beijing’s Central Axis. Discover Qianmen Street, the Temple of Heaven, Liulichang Cultural Street and hutongs. Xuanwumen and Caishikou metro stations...See More serve Lines 2, 4 and 7, linking Beijing South and West railway stations, Xidan shopping and Financial Street.After sightseeing or meetings, retreat to 406 spacious rooms and suites. Residence Suites with kitchens suit longer stays; Sky Garden Suites with balconies invite moments of calm. Enjoy Cantonese, Italian and international dining, unwind in the lounge or recharge at the fitness center. For families and guests extending a business trip, JW Marriott’s thoughtful hospitality makes space to reconnect and stay present.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central, with rates of ¥10.00 per hour, ¥240.00 per day, and ¥1680.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central is Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK). PEK is located approximately 35.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Beijing Central has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
