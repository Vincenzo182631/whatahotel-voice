## Alila Moments

### The Fragrant Path – Incense, Memory and Meaning

Enter the centuries-old world of Chinese incense culture through this multisensory experience that blends heritage with mindfulness. Begin with a talk on incense’s role in traditional Chinese wellness and spirituality before touring a museum of rare tools and artifacts. Under the guidance of a master, you’ll create your own incense blend using time-honored ingredients, then examine delicate incense implements that reflect dynastic craftsmanship. The session closes with quiet reflection and your handmade incense packed for travel—a fragrant reminder of your moment of calm within the rhythm of the city.

**A History of Scent**

Begin with an introduction to the significance of incense in Chinese medicine, art and spiritual practice.

**Walk the Fragrant Past**

Explore the Incense Museum and view centuries-old tools, recipes and written records.

**Craft Your Own Blend**

Mix traditional herbs, woods and resins under the guidance of an incense master.

**Tools of the Trade**

Examine intricate incense tools and learn how they embody balance, intention and artistry.

**A Quiet Departure**

Close the session with reflection and pack your handmade incense to bring a piece of presence home.

**Duration:** 90 minutes

**Recommended Dress:** Casual Attire

### Ancient Roots—A Journey Through Traditional Chinese Medicine

Step into the quiet legacy of Lei Yun Shang, one of Shanghai’s oldest and most respected Chinese medicine clinics. Your visit begins with a personal health consultation by a senior TCM practitioner who will assess your condition using pulse diagnosis and holistic principles. Then, explore the herb hall, where dried roots, barks and minerals line the shelves. Learn to identify and weigh healing ingredients, gaining insight into ancient philosophies that still shape modern wellness. Whether new to TCM or reconnecting with ancestral wisdom, this experience will open new doors to living in balance.

**Historic Arrival**

Enter Lei Yun Shang, a renowned TCM clinic founded in 1734, and explore its storied role in healing.

**Personal Consultation**

Meet one-on-one with a senior practitioner for a holistic health evaluation and discussion.

**The Hall of Herbs**

Explore the apothecary-style herb hall and learn to identify raw ingredients and their functions.

**Wisdom to Take Forward**

Leave with new wellness insights and a deeper appreciation of Chinese medicine’s enduring relevance.

**Duration:** 60 minutes

### Threads of Tradition—The Art of the Chinese Knot Button

Unravel the threads of Chinese craftsmanship in this hands-on experience inside a traditional cheongsam tailoring house. Begin with a tour of the LongFeng studio, where custom dresses are still made using techniques passed down through generations. Learn the cultural history and symbolism of the iconic pankou (knot button), a small but meaningful detail that fastens more than fabric—it binds memory, heritage and art. Guided by a master tailor, you’ll make your own knot button, then try on a custom-fit garment. Leave with your creation and a deeper respect for the elegance of timeless design.

**Welcome to the Atelier**

Begin at LongFeng Cheongsam Studio and explore its living history of Shanghai fashion and craftsmanship.

**Heritage Revealed**

Dive into the story of the cheongsam and the cultural meaning embedded in each stitch and curve.

**The Knot’s Secret**

Learn to make a pankou knot button with an expert tailor—understanding its structure, meaning and legacy.

**Dressed in Story**

Try on a traditional cheongsam and reflect on the legacy of style and symbolism you’ve just touched.

**Duration:** 90 minutes

**Recommended Dress:** Casual Attire

**Additional Details:** For booking and more information, please contact the hotel Guest Service Center 24 hours in advance.
