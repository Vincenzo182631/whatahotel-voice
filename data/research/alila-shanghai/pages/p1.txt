## An Urban Resort Offering a Serene Embrace, Wrapped in Luxury

Alila Shanghai places you in the historic Jing’an district, surrounded by Shanghai’s rich history and culture and yet just a short distance from this coastal city’s modern existence as a center for business and finance. Nearby, discover ancient traditions of artisans, Shanghai Grand Theatre, museums, shopping and more. When your journeys are complete, return to your urban oasis built with comfort, luxury and sustainability in mind. Restore your body and spirit with a visit to the tranquil Spa Alila, a quiet moment in our library, a meal or tea at our restaurants, or some time at our Secret Roof bar featuring craft cocktails and outstanding views.

## Celebrations & Gatherings

### Gatherings

Perched above Shanghai’s bustling streets, Alila Shanghai’s meeting rooms span over 305 square meters. Four intimate spaces on the 3rd floor can each welcome up to 20 guests in a banquet setting while the outdoor terrace takes functions to new heights. For larger gatherings, Zen on the 5th floor can host up to 70 guests for a cocktail function.

## Things To Do

From an ideal location in Jing’an district, enjoy being within walking distance or a short drive to several Shanghai attractions, such as Shanghai Grand Theatre, People’s Park, Shanghai Museum and more. You’re also close to the bustling shopping street of Nanjing Road, which is packed with retail and dining adventures, while The Bund, Yu Garden and Oriental Pearl Tower offer other points of interest.
