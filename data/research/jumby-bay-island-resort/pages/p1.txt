## Dining

Gastronomy at Jumby Bay Island is a journey of taste and place, where reimagined island flavours, beachside feasts, and global inspiration unfold across three restaurants and four bars. Heritage rum tastings and special evenings celebrate the island’s spirited soul.

## The Jumby Bay Spa

A serene sanctuary, Jumby Bay Spa combines modern therapeutic practices and holistic treatments inspired by traditions of the West Indies. From personalised wellness rituals to ocean-infused therapies, the tranquil setting promotes deep relaxation and rejuvenation.
