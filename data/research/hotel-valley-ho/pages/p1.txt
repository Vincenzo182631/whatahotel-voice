# WELCOME TO **Hotel Valley Ho.**

Since 1956, Hotel Valley Ho has been setting the scene for unforgettable stays in Old Town Scottsdale. Discover iconic mid-century modern architecture, lush resort-style grounds, and a walkable location just steps from the area’s best dining, nightlife and shopping. Colorful, vibrant and endlessly memorable — this is Scottsdale done differently.
