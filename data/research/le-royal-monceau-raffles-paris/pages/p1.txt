Our story

## The most Parisian Palace

Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris is a historic landmark reinterpreted by celebrated French architect and designer Philippe Starck. From its ideal location on the prestigious Avenue Hoche, the 5-star Parisian Palace is the perfect embodiment of modern 5-star luxury hotel with a generous dash of je ne sais quoi – a magical place woven from heartfelt encounters, sensations and emotions. Since 1928, Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris has shared close ties with the artists and writers at the heart of Parisian cultural life. That heritage still runs through the hotel today: a place of encounter and discovery, where art accompanies your stay and shapes a distinctive sense of hospitality.

## a parisian address _near the Champs-Élysées_

At 37 avenue Hoche, Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris stands a few steps from the Arc de Triomphe and the Champs-Élysées, close to the Parc Monceau. It is an ideal base for discovering Paris, amid its gardens, galleries and couture houses.

To help you plan your arrival, allow around 45 minutes from Roissy-Charles-de-Gaulle and Orly airports, 15 to 20 minutes from Gare du Nord and 30 minutes from Gare de Lyon. Our concierge team is on hand to assist with your travel arrangements.

## the palace distinction : F _rench excellence_

Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris holds the Palace distinction, an official French recognition awarded to a small number of five-star hotels for their history, their architecture, their facilities and the excellence of their service. Here, it takes a more personal form: an attentive welcome in which every detail works to make your stay memorable.

## the art of living _at Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris_

Culture, gastronomy, wellbeing and personalised service shape the experience of Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris. The hotel and its venues have been recognised by Condé Nast Traveler, the Michelin Guide and the Forbes Travel Guide.

- An artistic life: a contemporary art gallery, a collection of more than 300 works, the Librairie des Arts and the guidance of our Art Concierge.

- The Cinéma Katara: a private 99-seat auditorium, home to the screenings of the Royal Film Club and to your events.

- The Raffles Spa & Wellness: 1,500 sq m devoted to wellbeing in a space imagined by Philippe Starck, with a 23-metre pool.

- Distinctive dining: the Michelin-starred Italian cuisine of Il Carpaccio and the Peruvian-Japanese creations of Matsuhisa Paris.

- A bespoke welcome: the Raffles Butler service and experiences designed for family stays.

DESTINATION

## A distinctive artistic _and cultural signature_

Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris combines the art of Palace hospitality with the spirit of an artists' house. A contemporary art gallery, a private art collection, the Librairie des Arts and the Cinéma Katara make up a cultural setting you can explore throughout your stay. The Royal Film Club carries that spirit further, with gatherings held both inside the hotel and out in the artistic life of Paris.

Dining

## A unique gourmet journey

Acclaimed for its exceptional culinary offerings, Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris features award-winning dining experiences. Explore the creative delights of Chef Yazid Ichemrahen’s sweet symphony, the vibrant atmosphere of Le Bar Long, and the renowned Matsuhisa Paris, offering exquisite Japanese-Peruvian fusion by Chef Nobu. For an unforgettable Michelin-starred experience, enjoy Il Carpaccio, crafted with the Da Vittorio family’s Italian expertise. Don’t miss the luxurious brunches and breakfasts at La Cuisine, the perfect start to your Parisian stay.

## Frequently asked questions

### What dining options are available at Le Royal Monceau - Raffles Paris?

Savor exceptional cuisine at the hotel’s restaurants, where world-class chefs elevate every dish. Matsuhisa Paris, created by renowned chef Nobu Matsuhisa, blends refined Peruvian and Japanese flavors. Il Carpaccio, a Michelin-starred Italian restaurant in collaboration with Da Vittorio\*\*\*, offers exquisite gastronomy. La Cuisine is famous for its outstanding breakfasts and brunches. Le Bar Long is your day-and-night destination for elegant Parisian brasserie dishes and creative cocktails.

### How can I book a stay at Le Royal Monceau?

Simply visit the hotel’s website or call their reservation team at +33 (0)1 42 99 88 77 for assistance.

### What amenities does the hotel offer?

For wellness seekers, the hotel features a luxurious spa, a stunning swimming pool, and a fully-equipped fitness center: everything needed for a complete mind-and-body retreat.

Art enthusiasts will discover a truly exceptional cultural offering: a curated art gallery, an impressive private art collection displayed throughout the property, an exclusive 99-seat private cinema and a concept store with unique souvenirs. A one-of-a-kind cultural hotel destination not to be missed.

### Is there a special program for families?

Yes, Le Royal Monceau offers family-friendly services, including tailored activities and amenities for children.

### What makes Le Royal Monceau unique?

Its blend of historic charm and modern luxury, along with personalized service, sets it apart as a premier destination in Paris.

### Is Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris a Palace in the official sense of the term?

Yes. Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris holds the Palace distinction, an official French recognition given to a select group of five-star hotels for their exceptional character and the excellence of their hospitality.

### What are the journey times between Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris and Paris's stations and airports?

As a guide, allow around 45 minutes from Roissy-Charles-de-Gaulle and Orly airports, 15 to 20 minutes from Gare du Nord and 30 minutes from Gare de Lyon. To help you plan your arrival, our concierge team can advise on your travel arrangements.

### Does Le Royal Monceau – Raffles Paris welcome pets?

Yes, dogs and cats are welcome in our rooms and suites. In the dining venues, only small dogs are admitted. Our team is on hand to help you plan your stay with your companion.
