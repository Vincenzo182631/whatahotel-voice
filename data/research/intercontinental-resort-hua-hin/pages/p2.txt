# InterContinental Hua Hin ResortRooms & suites

Experience the ultimate relaxation in our spacious 45 sq. m. accommodations located in the Garden Wing. The rooms are thoughtfully designed with exceptional features such as a private balcony, a luxurious marble-floored bathroom equipped with a separate shower and bathtub to enhance your comfort and tranquility. All rooms include 1-time access to Vana Nava water park per stay.
