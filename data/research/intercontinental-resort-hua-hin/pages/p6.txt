InterContinental Hua Hin Resort is a seaside luxury resort blending Eastern and Western design, alongside sparkling gulf waters. Two accommodation wings feature spacious rooms and suites and a swimming pool at each wing. Relax and rejuvenate at our spa and Fitness Centre, and enjoy exquisite food and drink options at our four restaurants and two bars. Explore Hua Hin, which will charm you with stunning cultural attractions and temples, world-class golf courses, and hillside vineyards.

Does InterContinental Hua Hin Resort have a pool?

How much is parking at InterContinental Hua Hin Resort?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Hua Hin Resort for hotel guests.

Our hotel provides complimentary parking for hotel guests and visitors. 24-hour complimentary valet parking is so available.

Parking fee: 0 THB per day

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Hua Hin Resort?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Hua Hin Resort.

Pet policy description. Up to 2 pets, dogs or cats, per guest booking with a weight of less than 20 kgs are allowed at Classic Rooms, 1 Bedroom Beachfront Villa, and 3 Bedrooms La Residence. The first pet stays free of charge, while a second pet is subject to an additional fee.

Pet fee per night: 1000 THB

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Hua Hin Resort for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Hua Hin Resort have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Hua Hin Resort has 6 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Hua Hin Resort?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Hua Hin Resort.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Hua Hin Resort?

Check-in at InterContinental Hua Hin Resort is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Hua Hin Resort offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Hua Hin Resort offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Hua Hin Resort from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Hua Hin Resort is conveniently located near Suvarnabhumi International Airport (Bangkok) (BKK), approximately 152.24 miles away (245 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Hua Hin Resort?

InterContinental Hua Hin Resort is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Hua Hin Beach
- Cicada Market
- Hua Hin Night Market
- Hua Hin Artists Village
- Vana Nava Water Jungle
- BluPort Mall
- Arena Huahin
- Maruekhathaiyawan Palace

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Hua Hin Resort puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
