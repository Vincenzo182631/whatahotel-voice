### Museo Auto Classica

The exhibition features a thoughtfully curated collection of over 40 classic automobiles, showcased in an atmosphere inspired by the 1950s to the present day, seamlessly blending each era with character and charm. Enhancing the experience are exquisite artworks, including paintings by National Artists and distinguished senior artists.
