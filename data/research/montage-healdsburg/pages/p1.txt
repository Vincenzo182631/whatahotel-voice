### A Fall Harvest Winemaker Dinner

October 16

As harvest season settles over Sonoma wine country, gather at Hazel Hill for a Montage Healdsburg winemaker dinner pairing a five-course menu from Executive Chef Sergio Howland with a curated selection of Croix Estate wines. Vintner Kirk Venge brings three generations of Napa Valley winemaking craft to the table for an evening of autumn vineyards and wine country at its finest.

### Artisan’s Table

Saturdays \| 8:30am – 10:00am \| Healdsburg Country Store

Celebrate regional creativity at our weekly pop up featuring handcrafted goods from a rotating lineup of makers. Each week highlights a different artisan, from culinary creators and authors to jewelry designers and visual artists, offering guests a chance to connect with the stories behind their work. Whether stopping in for morning coffee or a grab and go breakfast, guests are invited to discover a meaningful piece inspired by Healdsburg and beyond.
