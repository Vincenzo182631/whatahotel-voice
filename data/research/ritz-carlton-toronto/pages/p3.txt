Discover Our Luxury Hotel Suites in Toronto
Stay at our downtown Toronto hotel with connecting rooms​
Our elegantly appointed luxury hotel suites are spacious and thoughtfully designed, offering panoramic lake and city views. With exclusive Club Lounge access, guests enjoy an elevated urban stay in the heart of downtown Toronto.
The Ritz-Carlton Suite
Spanning 2,145 square feet, this vast, luxury hotel suite in downtown Toronto features a beautifully furnished king bedroom, a generous living room, and a refined foyer with a powder room. Designed for both relaxation and productivity, it offers a private study and a dedicated fitness area. For seamless entertaining, the separate dining room accommodates up to eight guests, complemented by a nearby pantry and a fully equipped kitchen with a discreet private service entrance.
Inspired by the City. Elevated for You.
Refined and timeless, our luxury hotel suites in downtown Toronto draw inspiration from the rich tapestry of the city: the cinematic glamour of theatre, the serene beauty of the waterfront, the sleek sophistication of Canada’s rail heritage, and the enduring elegance of Ontario’s early Parliament buildings — all brought to life through bold, saturated hues and thoughtfully modern finishes.
