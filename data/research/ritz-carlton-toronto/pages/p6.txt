Elevate Your Stay with Club Lounge Access in Toronto
Experience our Downtown Toronto Hotel with Club Lounge​
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalised service and the attention to detail the Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a "hotel within a hotel," Club enhances your stay at our downtown Toronto hotel by providing guests with dedicated concierge services, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite at our downtown Toronto hotel with Club Lounge access, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.

Club Lounge Access
Considered a hotel within The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto, the luxurious guest rooms and suites cater to the elevated experience within this downtown Toronto hotel with a Club Lounge. Outside of the exclusive club-level rooms, guests will enjoy the heated indoor saltwater pool, Spa myBlend, and multiple dining options. All of our hotel club rooms in Toronto feature a high-floor location with spectacular views of the Toronto skyline.

At Your Service
Toronto Hotel with Dedicated Concierge
From dining reservations to excursion recommendations, the dedicated Club team at our downtown Toronto hotel elevates your experience, catering to you personally to ensure your stay is exceptional. Along with our personalised Concierge service, there are additional benefits that the Club experience offers including complimentary pressing of two items per day, business centre services, enhanced Wi-Fi, and booking our exclusive Club Car. Please note that Club Car requires a minimum of two hours' advanced notice when making a reservation.

In Good Taste
Daily Culinary Presentations
At our hotel with Club Lounge access in Toronto, it is our pleasure to introduce local ingredients and flavors to our five daily meal presentations. Begin your day with breakfast items complete with fresh baked pastries, a light lunch, Hors’ d’oeuvres and desserts, and cordial selections. All are accompanied by crafted coffees and cocktails in the comfort of self-service or assisted by the Club Lounge hosts. The Timing of Our Presentations Is As Follows: Breakfast 7:00 a.m. - 11:00 a.m., Lunch 12:00 p.m. - 2:00 p.m., Mid-day Snacks 2:30 p.m. - 4:30 p.m., Hors d'oeuvres & Cocktails 5:00 p.m. - 8:00 p.m. and Desserts & Cordials 8:00 p.m. - 10:00 p.m
