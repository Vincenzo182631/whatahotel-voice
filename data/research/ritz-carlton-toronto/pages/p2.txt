Suites & Rooms
Set amid the city’s financial and entertainment districts, The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto has reimagined luxury, unveiling transformed Club Level hotel rooms and suites in downtown Toronto, an enhanced Club Lounge and coming February of 2027, a transformative spa experience.
Elevate your stay
Enjoy Club Lounge access, guaranteed early check-in or late check-out, and thoughtful amenities. From Ritz Kids adventures to exceptional dining and restorative spa experiences, every moment is crafted to feel effortlessly luxurious and deeply connected to the destination.

Luxury suites and hotel rooms in downtown Toronto capture the imagination with finishes inspired by the city including patterns and lines reminiscent of the lakeshore and railway system. Accommodations are among the largest in Toronto and offer skyline or Lake Ontario views.
