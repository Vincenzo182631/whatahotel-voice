Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto
A luxury hotel retreat within downtown Toronto
At The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto, you will discover what it means when a luxury hotel enlivens all five senses. We invite you to find a total sensory experience that awaits you at our luxury hotel in Toronto, Ontario. From the moment you arrive at our downtown Toronto hotel, you will begin to explore the city’s cosmopolitan culture through taste, touch, sight, smell, and sound. The hotel is close to the Rogers Centre, home of the Toronto Blue Jays, Scotiabank Arena, and the centre of the Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF).

Reducing Waste. Reimagining Hydration.
Bottled Water Program
As part of our commitment to sustainability and refined guest experiences, we’ve introduced an innovative in-house bottling system that replaces single-use plastic water bottles with elegant, reusable glass bottles across all guest rooms. Each bottle is meticulously washed, sanitized, and refilled on property, ensuring pristine quality while eliminating plastic waste. This initiative saves thousands of bottles annually, significantly reducing our carbon footprint and minimizing landfill impact. Designed with minimalist, recyclable labels, the bottles reflect our dedication to luxury with purpose. Their presence in guest rooms seamlessly aligns hydration with environmental responsibility. This program is a proud extension of our Ritz-Carlton “Serve 360” pledge — thoughtfully balancing guest comfort with measurable environmental impact.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 13.6 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto. The daily valet fee is $75.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto is Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport (YTZ). YTZ is located approximately 2.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto have an airport shuttle to Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport (YTZ)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto does not have an airport shuttle to Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Toronto offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
