## Highlights

There is never a dull moment at Vila Vita Parc! Be always in the know with what’s happening and keep an eye on our events, news and opportunities.

### Pumpkin Market

On 24 October, Vila Vita Biergarten welcomes autumn with a Pumpkin Market celebrating the flavours, colours and traditions of the season. An afternoon of local producers, seasonal cuisine, live music and activities for all ages, bringing together pumpkins, good food and good company in a relaxed setting.

Know more

### Festive Season - Lío in Rio

From the 6th of December to the 6th of January, Vila Vita Parc dresses itself in the colour, light and contagious energy of Rio de Janeiro. Forget the traditional winter: this year, our Festive Season is a warm embrace, a lively and spontaneous celebration where refinement meets the carioca euphoric spirit. This is Lío in Rio. Made of rhythm, flavours and unforgettable moments - an energy that can't be explained, only felt.

Know more

### Discover our Wellness Experts

Experience living a healthier lifestyle with the restorative practices of our experience guest practitioners, such as Caroline Alboneti, or John Sanchez. Each one of them brings their own expertise and treatments designed to bring balance, vitality, and long-lasting well-being.

Know more

### Vila Vita Christmas Market

Indulge in a festive shopping extravaganza at the stylish boutiques in the Piazetta, with a touch of Christmas spirit.

Know more

### Christmas Day - Gastronomic Events

Christmas Day is celebrated unhurried, in a radiant, rhythm-filled setting, around a sun-soaked lunch. From authentic Portuguese cooking and Bavarian classics to fresh seafood by the sea and a relaxed beach grill, our menus celebrate diversity and the joy of being together. A festive buffet, live music and a good commotion set the tone for relaxed gatherings and family memories.

Know more

### Grand Gala Lío in Rio

This is the great climax of the festive season: the night when the rhythm quickens and carioca magic reaches its fullest expression. As the year bids farewell, the Lisboa Ballroom becomes an irresistible explosion of percussion, colour, light and spectacle.

Know more

### Game season - Biergarten

As the season turns, we invite you to discover our new game menu celebrating the rich flavours of autumn. Our chefs have crafted a selection of dishes showcasing locally sourced seasonal ingredients, each designed to complement our curated range of German and artisanal beers, including the ever-popular Porches Craft Beer.

Know more

### Through Our Finest Tables

Discover the gastronomic identity of Vila Vita Parc over an unforgettable evening. Reserved for those who seek the extraordinary, Through Our Finest Tables is an intimate, guided immersion through our fine dining restaurants - with carefully curated pairings and a dedicated host, from the very first aperitif to the last digestif.
