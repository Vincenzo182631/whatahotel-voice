Moments away from the Opera, Kimpton St Honoré Paris brings a creative flair to its 1917 Art Nouveau facade. Its 149 stylish guest rooms including 26 beautifully designed suites are the work of renowned Paris-based interior designer Charles Zana. Inspired by minimalist Art Déco aesthetics and a light-hearted sense of Parisian chic, Kimpton St Honoré Paris encourages encounters between travellers and habitués thanks to its healthy, California-inspired restaurant and bar with a light-filled patio, a cutting-edge spa complete with an interior heated swimming pool and, cerise sur le gâteau, the unrivalled 360-degree views of its rooftop bar and garden.

## FAQs for St Honoré Paris

Does Kimpton St Honoré Paris have a pool?

Kimpton St Honoré Paris has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool - Codage Spa

Our hotel indoor heated swimming pool is open daily and is the perfect way to either start or end your day. 36ft long and 4ft deep, there’s plenty of room for those wanting to enjoy a leisurely swim as well as the experienced swimmers.

Pool information

How much is parking at Kimpton St Honoré Paris?

At Kimpton St Honoré Paris, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 45 EUR and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 45 EUR .

Covered parking spaces are available in the hotel basement.

Parking fee: 45 EUR per day

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton St Honoré Paris?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton St Honoré Paris.

Pet policy description. We invite furry, feathery or scaly family members no matter their size, weight, or breed, all at no extra charge

Pet weight limit: No weight limit per pet

NO\_LIMIT pets allowed

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton St Honoré Paris for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton St Honoré Paris have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton St Honoré Paris has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton St Honoré Paris?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton St Honoré Paris.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton St Honoré Paris?

Check-in at Kimpton St Honoré Paris is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton St Honoré Paris offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton St Honoré Paris offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton St Honoré Paris from the nearest airport?

Kimpton St Honoré Paris is conveniently located near Charles De Gaulle International Airport (CDG), approximately 19.76 miles away (31.8 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton St Honoré Paris?

Kimpton St Honoré Paris is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Tuileries Garden
- Arc de Triomphe
- Château de Versailles
- Musée d'Orsay
- Musée du Louvre
- Opéra Garnier
- Sainte-Chapelle
- The Eiffel Tower
- Musée Rodin
- Montmartre

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton St Honoré Paris puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
