Signature Accommodations
Experience the suite life at The Ritz-Carlton, Perth, where every Studio and Suite offers elegant design and stunning views overlooking the Swan River, Perth skyline, Langley Park, and Elizabeth Quay. Discover ultimate comfort and sophistication in the heart of the city.
Corner Studios
Our Studio Rooms offer stunning views of Perth’s skyline, Langley Park, or the Swan River. Bathed in natural light from floor-to-ceiling windows, these elegant spaces feature a luxurious bathroom with dual vanities, a standalone bathtub, and signature Ritz-Carlton amenities—providing a refined escape in every detail.
Soaking Tub Studio
Experience the allure of city living from the serene sanctuary of the Soaking Tub Studio. Overlooking Perth’s glittering skyline, this 64-square-metre haven features a sculptural standalone bathtub set against floor-to-ceiling windows—inviting you to unwind with unforgettable views. Thoughtfully designed with elegant finishes, a spacious bathroom, and signature Ritz-Carlton amenities, this studio offers a tailored and tranquil escape high above the city lights.

One-Bedroom Suites
Indulge in ultimate luxury in Perth, Australia, with our hotel suites, offering stunning views of the Swan River or Langley Park. Each suite features a separate living area, a spa-like bathroom with a standalone bathtub, and signature Ritz-Carlton amenities. With floor-to-ceiling windows and exquisite details throughout, your stay promises to be a truly elevated experience.
Langley Park Suite
Relax in 103 square meters of pure luxury in this suite in Perth CBD. The Langley Park Suite offers expansive park and river views, a spacious living area, and a spa-inspired bathroom with a standalone bathtub. An additional powder room, plush king bed, and corridor connection option make this suite the perfect balance of comfort and elegance.
