Explore Perth from our hotel near Langley Park
Experiences
At our waterfront hotel in Perth city center, nature plays a vital role in every experience. Set on Elizabeth Quay where the Swan River meets the city,
The Ritz-Carlton, Perth offers a refined base for exploring the waterfront and its expansive open spaces. Guests are invited to discover the destination on foot, from riverside promenade and acclaimed dining venues to public art installations, cultural attractions and premier shopping precincts.

Defining Experiences
Hearth Chef’s Counter
Seated at the heart of the kitchen, each course is unveiled by the chefs, sharing the provenance and inspiration behind every element. Reserved for one party per seating, this intimate encounter offers rare access to culinary artistry in motion. Guided by the sommelier, each pairing deepens the experience. Enjoy this immersive journey shaped by land and sea at our hotel near Kings Park, Perth.
Valley And Vine
Just moments from Perth, discover the Swan Valley through a private half-day journey celebrating its rich culinary heritage and artisanal spirit. Explore boutique vineyards for intimate tastings, followed by seasonal dining by local chefs. Meet passionate producers for an immersive experience connecting you to the region’s flavours and stories.
Discover Wooditchup
Discover Margaret River on a full-day journey across Wadandi land, where vineyards, forests and coastline converge. Enjoy curated tastings at boutique wineries, followed by a seasonal lunch celebrating local produce. Meet artisans of cheese, olive oil and chocolate, then take in limestone caves, karri forests and ocean vistas. An immersive experience into Western Australia’s soul.
Nature’s Embrace
Enjoy a restorative journey from bush to spa at our hotel with a spa in Perth CBD and reconnect body and mind with nature’s rhythm. Begin with guided forest bathing in the tranquil surrounds of Kings Park, inviting mindful immersion. Return to the spa for a 105-minute botanical body treatment inspired by native ingredients. Designed to restore balance, calm and inner harmony.
Wonder Of The West
Restore body and ease the mind at our hotel in Perth CBD with a pool and spa. Rejuvenate through a carefully sequenced series of holistic treatments, complemented by a nourishing lunch. Each moment guided by expert practitioners is to cultivate balance, vitality and a lasting sense of renewal.
Kings Park Cultural Tour
From our hotel near Kings Park, Perth, enjoy a private, immersive walk that blends spectacular scenery with truth telling, cultural teachings and stories passed down for generations. ​
Ritz Kids
The Ritz Kids experience at The Ritz-Carlton, Perth invites young guests to embark on their own adventure, beginning with a personalised check-in and Adventurer Passport to explore the hotel. Families can enhance their stay with an optional in-room teepee experience, creating memorable moments for little explorers. Set on Elizabeth Quay in Perth CBD, the hotel is moments from family-friendly attractions including the carousel, water playground, ferry connections to Fremantle and Rottnest Island, and a variety of waterfront dining experiences.

Area & Activities
An Urban Hotel Centered on Nature
Our hotel with a spa is located in Perth CBD, within walking distance of the city’s financial hub, transport, the Perth Convention and Exhibition Centre, and key attractions like The Bell Tower and Kings Park. River cruises take you to the Swan Valley, Fremantle, and Rottnest Island from Barrack Street Jetty and Perth’s stunning coastline is only a 20-minute drive away.

An extraordinary in-room experience at our hotel near Kings Park, Perth, the Ritz Kids Teepee Experience embraces the creative spirit and thirst for new adventures. This optional paid-in-room experience is suited for children five years and younger and features a teepee with a small mattress, feather duvet, and pillow.
