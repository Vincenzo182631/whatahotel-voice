Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Perth
Embrace luxury at our riverfront hotel in Perth, Australia
Cutting a striking silhouette along the Swan River, The Ritz-Carlton, Perth evokes timeless luxury and the joy of discovery. Set within a stunning building on Elizabeth Quay, our 205 elegantly appointed hotel rooms and suites overlook the river, skyline, and urban parks with floor-to-ceiling windows offering pristine views. The beauty and bounty of Western Australia are celebrated through culinary experiences at this 5-star luxury hotel in Perth, highlighting local ingredients, a river-view infinity pool, and a luxury spa featuring bespoke treatments. Set against the backdrop of Elizabeth Quay, our 5-star hotel in Perth CBD also offers a romantic venue for weddings and receptions.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Perth?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Perth is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Perth allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Perth.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Perth?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Perth. Self parking is available for $80.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $80.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Perth have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Perth has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Perth booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Perth?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Perth is Perth Airport (PER). PER is located approximately 16.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Perth have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Perth does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Perth have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Perth offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites.
