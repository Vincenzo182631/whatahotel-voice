Relax with a Treatment at our Spa in Perth, Australia
Spa
Devoted to offering guests a holistic wellness experience, The Ritz-Carlton, Perth uses its waterfront setting to encourage relaxation and reflection in Perth, Australia. Our spa hotel's heated outdoor infinity pool looks out over the Swan River and city skyline, while the separate ladies and gentlemen vitality pools, spa baths, sauna, and relaxation lounge areas offer a healing connection to nature. Four idyllic treatment rooms, including a couple’s spa suite, are where nature meets beauty with crafted pampering day spa rituals. The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Perth, Australia works collaboratively with national and international skincare brands LaGaia Unedited and Biologique Recherche on its bespoke spa treatments and has retail products available to purchase for both hotel and guests local to Perth. Settle into a day of relaxation at our day spa in Perth, Australia.
The Ritz-Carlton Spa
Our hotel with a spa, offers a curated space in Perth CBD for self-indulgence and reflection. There are four treatment rooms, a sauna, vitality pools and relaxation areas. Please email spa.perth@ritzcarlton.com for bookings outside business hours.
