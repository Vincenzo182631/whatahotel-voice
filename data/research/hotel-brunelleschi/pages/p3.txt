### Dining

### A Taste of How We Cook

An Osteria that serves pasta the way memory would like it to taste, a tower bar where the walls themselves seem to season the cocktails, and Liberty rooms that greet the day with a generous breakfast.

### Osteria La Pagliazza

At Osteria della Pagliazza, cooking becomes a simpler gesture: handmade pasta, seasonal produce, broths and sauces rooted in tradition and shaped by the same care and attention to detail that define our kitchen. It’s the sort of place where lunch can stretch into the afternoon, held by the tower’s ancient stone and a sense of quiet conviviality.

### Tower Bar

In the round stone of the ancient tower, the bar moves at its own tempo. Cocktails are built with precision and a sense of history, balancing classic rules with small, thoughtful twists. It’s a place for a pre-dinner pause or a late-night drink, where the atmosphere does half the work and the glass does the rest.

menu

### Breakfast

When it’s not enjoyed in pyjamas, in the quiet comfort of your room, breakfast is served in what was once a medieval church, a space where the day begins with light, calm, and a certain sense of history. The menu moves between fresh pastries, seasonal fruit, and warm dishes prepared to order, all approached with the same care and simplicity that guide the rest of our kitchen. However you choose to start, it should feel like a gentle welcome into Florence.
