### A MEDITERRANEAN FEAST IN THREE ACTS

**Cleo x Savor Baha Mar**

Price: $70 Three-Course Prix-Fixe Menu

When: August 15 – November 1

Where: Cleo

………………………………………………………………………………………….

The table is set. The first course arrives. And the evening begins. This Savor Baha Mar, Cleo presents a Mediterranean feast in three acts, bringing bold flavors and vibrant dishes together in a specially curated $70 prix-fixe experience.

**Act One** begins with your choice of Trio Dips, Spicy Cigar or Spanakopita. **Act Two** brings Chicken Shish, Fattoush Salad with Shrimp or Butcher’s Lamb Kefta to center stage. And for the finale, Cleo’s Chocolate & Espresso Opera delivers an appropriately indulgent last act. All set against Cleo’s warm, energetic atmosphere, where an open kitchen, conversation, and the occasional sound of **“OPA!”** are part of the experience.

### Beat The Heat Saturdays

**A signature Bahamian moment done the SLS way**

When: Every 4th Saturday of the month at 4:00pm

Route: Lobby → Bungalow Pool → Privilege Pool (finale)

………………………………………………………………………………………….

On the 4th Saturday of every month, experience an electrifying Junkanoo rush that turns the resort into a moving celebration and shares iconic Bahamian culture. High-energy music and unforgettable costumes wind their way through the resort, beginning in the Lobby – the perfect point for families – passing through Bungalow Pool and ending at Privilege Pool.

**No reservations required – simply be there when the first drum hits.**
