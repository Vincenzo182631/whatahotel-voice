## ALL-INCLUSIVE

A a wealth of dining, entertainment, and recreation experiences that eclipse expectations of modern luxury and all-inclusive travel

### WHAT’S INCLUDED

The vacation of your dreams comes standard.

#### Luxury Rooms

Ocean views, rooftop hot-tubs and swim out rooms are all on offer.

#### All Meals & Drinks

17 chef-led restaurants & bars, from beachside bites to elevated dining. Everything is included, from your coffee, smoothie or buffet in the morning, to your poolside lunch, afternoon gelato and your steakhouse dinner.

#### Pristine Beachfront

Clear turquoise waters and soft white sand – just as you imagined, only better.

#### Four Pools

A collection of pools, from family-friendly to adults-only, with space to play or retreat.

#### Kids Splash Zone

Slides, sprinklers and tipping buckets, where water spills, splashes and surprises.

#### Water Sports

Non-motorized water sports are perfect for exploring the clear, sparkling sea and coast.

#### Entertainment

An indoor theatre is just the beginning of a lineup of live music, performance and fun.

#### Coati Kids Club

For our youngest Mischief Makers, ages 4-12, a place and activities all their own.

#### Teens Club

A dedicated space to chill out, connect with friends, or engage in friendly competition

#### SLS Sculpt

Two 24/7 gyms and a schedule of group classes balance the indulgence.

#### In-Room Dining

Breakfast in bed or late-night treats, in-room dining brings the indulgence straight to you.
