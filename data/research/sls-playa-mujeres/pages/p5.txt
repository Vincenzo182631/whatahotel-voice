# FOR THE ADULTS

For the friends trips, the bachelorettes, the reunions, the long-overdue weekends aways. For the couples – on their first trip together, a last minute escape, or much needed time for two. For those who may be young at heart but are seeking something more considered, more indulgent – these are the experiences designed just for you.

## TWIGGY LA CANTINE

Twiggy by La Cantine is a chic, adults-oriented restaurant and pool club where laid-back Riviera charm meets modern indulgence. The pool is open daily to guests over 18 and features multiple jacuzzis, a central swim-up bar, sun-loungers, an exclusive SLS Elite area and private cabanas.
