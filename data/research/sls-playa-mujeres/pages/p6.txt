# FAMILIES

## AT SLS PLAYA MUJERES

In true SLS style, the family experience at SLS Playa Mujeres is far from the norm. This is an indulgent wonderland for every age, where dedicated programming, spaces and experiences come to life with a vibrant playful spirit to create unforgettable family-fun.

* * *

## COATI KIDS CLUB

Coati Kids Club is a dedicated space for ages 4–12, where imagination runs wild and high-energy play is encouraged, with a weekly rotation of activities from arts and crafts to robot building. Open daily from 10am to 6pm.

## TEEN LOUNGE

A dedicated space to switch off, link up and lean into a little friendly competition, with billiards, video games and a program of activities from Fortnite tournaments to water polo. For ages 12–17, open daily from noon to 9pm.

## FAMILY TIME

From beach discos to ping pong, family time turns into high-energy fun, shared moments and memories in the making.

## MORE TO EXPLORE

#### Dining

From more casual restaurants with something for everyone to places created expressly for our youngest guests – such as the Barracuda snack truck – dining options thoughtfully consider the entire family.

#### Family Pool

A sprawling family pool with space to play, unwind and make a day of it, with activities, loungers, cabanas and bites from Bungalow to keep things going.

#### Kids Splash Zone

The water playground of kids’ dreams sits alongside the family pool for endless hours of aquatic play. Slides, sprinklers and tipping buckets, where water spills, splashes and surprises.

#### Movie Night

What’s more fun than movie night? Grab some snacks and sit down for a classic at the super comfy indoor theatre.

#### Childcare

Enjoy a carefree date night while we look after the little ones. Available day and night, with support from the highly-experienced Cancun Nancy Service. Can be booked once on property via the concierge or for SLS Elite guests, with the Butler.

#### Swim-out Rooms

Perfect for families with kids that want to max our on splash time, swim-out rooms bring the water to you with a river pool accessible through a private garden. Even better, our Family Style Swim-Outs connect two of these groundfloor rooms for larger groups.
