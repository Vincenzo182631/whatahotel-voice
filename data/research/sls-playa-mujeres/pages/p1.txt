# An all-inclusive wonderland in cancun

SLS Playa Mujeres gives all-inclusive an audacious upgrade: bold, stylish, and unapologetically indulgent. World-class dining, wellness with flair, and entertainment that never clocks out unfold against a backdrop of unspoiled beaches, sparkling pools, and luxe suites. The result is a resort that delights friends, families, and couples in equal measure, where extraordinary isn’t the exception – it’s the rule.

## RESORT FEATURES

Who says you can’t have it all?

#### 17 CHEF-LED RESTAURANTS & BARS

A culinary hotspot, with world-class restaurants and tempting bars spanning bold flavors and vibes.

#### FOUR POOLS

From family-friendly to adults-only, with space to play or retreat, cabanas and food & beverage service.

#### BLISSFUL BEACHFRONT

A beachfront known for clear, turquoise waters set with sun loungers and waiter service.

#### KIDS SPLASH ZONE

Slides that thrill, sprinklers that spray, and splashes that surprise for our youngest thrill-seekers.

#### **DESTINATION SPA**

A 20,000 sqft haven to reset and recharge between sun-drenched days and electric nights.

#### COATI **KIDS CLUB**

Dedicated space and engaging activities to ensure fun and discovery for all while parents relax.

#### DOUBLE-GATED COMMUNITY

Multiple security check-points offer peace of mind from the moment you arrive.

#### SWIM OUT ROOMS

Step out of your room and into the water, in rooms perched along a winding river pool.

#### **SLS SCULPT**

Indulgence is all about the balance. Discover two 24/7 gyms (the views don’t hurt) & guest-favorite classes.

#### ON THE WATER

Answer the call of the crystal clear sea with motorized or non-motorized water sports to explore.

#### A STYLISH HANG FOR TEENS

For our younger, discerning guests, the Teen Lounge provides a cool space to connect, play and unwind.

#### ROOFTOP JACUZZI ROOMS

These bi-level rooms feature a private rooftop deck with a jacuzzi, lounge and panoramic sea views.

#### INDOOR MOVIE THEATRE

Whether you need a break from the sun, or the sun took a break from the sky, there’s always a showtime.

#### SLS ELITE ROOMS & SUITES

Rooms with benefits including personal butler service, private check-in and premium pool and beach spaces.
