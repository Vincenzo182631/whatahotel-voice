## Anis

An all-day, healthy food eatery offering delicious, hand-made, nutritionally-balanced food (including gluten-free options), coffees, juices and smoothies. Boasting direct street entrance, the restaurant is a social hub for health-conscious locals, office workers and visitors to Msheireb Downtown. Anis additionally offers alfresco dining on Sikkat Wadi, perfect for enjoying the milder weather during Doha's winter months with family or friends, or simply people watching in the heart of Msheireb Downtown.

## Sora Rooftop

With a balance of small sharing dishes, sushi, tempura and the unique culinary art of Robata (meat grilled over charcoal), Sora Rooftop offers a sense of place from arguably one of Doha's most magnificent outdoor dining terraces, where a pre-dinner sundowner while basking in the golden light of an Arabian sun is not to be missed. Sora Rooftop Restaurant, showcases modern Japanese drinks and dishes while merging the local culture. Perched 21 storey's above Msheireb Downtown, Sora provides panoramic views of The Doha Palace, Green Mosque, Souq Waqif, Corniche and Doha skyline. The cuisine is best enjoyed over Sora Rooftop Brunch, adding a flair of excitement to your Fridays in Qatar.

## Opus

Opus is a Mediterranean restaurant that serves a diverse array of delectable dishes inspired by the flavors of the Mediterranean coast. Guests can enjoy the vibrant atmosphere of the open kitchen, dine al fresco on the stunning outdoor terrace, or host events in the beautifully lit private dining rooms.
Opus is the hotel’s main dining venue for all-day dining. Opus offers parallel experiences of Meridional cuisines from an open kitchen.
The restaurant offers three private dining rooms and outdoor seating on the veranda hosting up to 170 persons.
A lively open show kitchen with a wood fired oven will set the stage for an interactive dining experience.
