### Very good stay overall, but disappointing incident with my glasses

We had a very good stay at Park Hyatt Doha overall. The staff were consistently kind and welcoming, the hotel was very clean, the facilities were excellent, and the food was also very good.

The only reason I am giving 4 stars instead of 5 is because of an unfortunate incident during our stay. One day we left the hotel in the morning and came back later in the afternoon, and I found my prescription glasses extremely bent, to the point that they were no longer usable and could not be properly repaired.

I reported the situation to the hotel, and the manager was very attentive, listened to me, apologized, and later sent a fruit basket to the room, which I appreciated. However, the fact remains that the glasses were damaged while the room had been serviced, which was quite disappointing.

Aside from this incident, everything else about the stay was very good, and I would still consider staying again.

Stewart C,
