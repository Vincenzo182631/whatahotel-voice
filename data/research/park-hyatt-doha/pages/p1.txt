# 5 Star Luxury Hotel near Msheireb Downtown Doha \| Park Hyatt Doha

## Luxury Hotel in Msheireb Downtown Doha

Park Hyatt Doha Hotel is a luxury retreat in the Heart of Doha, steps from Souq Waqif and Msheireb Metro Station. Providing easy access to Qatar's touristic attractions, and Hamad International Airport, ideal for both leisure and business traveler.

## Discover Park Hyatt Doha

Host Your Events at the Park

Host your ceremony in our light-filled 297-square-meter ballroom, then move effortlessly to an intimate Salon reception. Private entrances, dedicated wedding specialists, valet parking, and bespoke menus by our chefs ensure a refined celebration.

Your refined home away from home

Enjoy a thoughtfully designed retreat for families. The 130-square-meter Family Suite features one king and one twin room, a spacious living and dining area, a marble bathroom with soaking tub and rain shower, and panoramic views of Doha’s iconic skyline.

The Spa

Spanning two floors, The Spa at Park Hyatt Doha is a destination dedicated to restoration and well-being. Guests are invited to experience a curated journey through tailored treatments, mood showers, a hot tub, sauna, steam room and ice room. Each space is designed to encourage balance, calm and mindful renewal.

Open daily from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.

Pool and Fitness Center

Balance movement and relaxation with access to the 24-hour Fitness Center and the semi-covered pool at Park Hyatt Doha. ### Anis

An all-day, healthy food eatery offering delicious, hand-made, nutritionally-balanced food (including gluten-free options), coffees, juices and smoothies. Boasting direct street entrance, the restaurant is a social hub for health-conscious locals, office workers and visitors to Msheireb Downtown. Anis additionally offers alfresco dining on Sikkat Wadi, perfect for enjoying the milder weather during Doha's winter months with family or friends, or simply people watching in the heart of Msheireb Downtown.
