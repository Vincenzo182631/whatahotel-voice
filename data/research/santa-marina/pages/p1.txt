### Luxurious Villas with Private Pools in Mykonos

Designed as complete private homes, our 12 exclusive villas at Santa Marina all include a fully equipped kitchen, swimming pool and butler service. All have space for an extra bed or crib. Our villa guests can enjoy their privacy and independence, while benefitting from full access to the resort’s restaurants, recreation amenities, concierge and security teams.

### Villa Lapis Lazuli

Designed with a fresh, modern approach, this ultra-chic two-level villa showcases magnificent views of ancient hills and sparkling seas. The expansive terrace is the focal point – whether for lounging, dining or dipping into the freshwater free-form infinity pool.

## Hotel History

Once there was a house, a windmill, a pigeon house and a small chapel called Agia Marina. As Santa Marina has developed over the years, so too has its reputation as one of the loveliest spots on the island, with a love of that heritage and a deep sense of place. Because this is a beloved resort, the stuff of childhood summer holidays preserved, enhanced and nurtured. And this is a magical place, with breathtaking design, in the most beautiful setting, waiting to be enjoyed.

## The Luxury Collection Concierge

Our concierge team is always ready to deepen your connection to Mykonos, curating everything from exclusive restaurant reservations to unforgettable experiences. Whether you seek hidden gems, cultural landmarks, or bespoke adventures, we ensure you experience the island in the most exceptional way.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos?

The check-in time at Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos. with a maximum weight of 10.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos?

On-site parking is available at Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos booking.

What is the closest airport near Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos?

The closest airport near Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos is Mykonos Island National Airport (JMK). JMK is located approximately 3.5 KM from the hotel.

Does Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos have an airport shuttle to Mykonos Island National Airport (JMK)?

Yes, Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos has an airport shuttle to Mykonos Island National Airport (JMK).

Does Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos have electric vehicle charging stations?
