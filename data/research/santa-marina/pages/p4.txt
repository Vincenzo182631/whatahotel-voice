### Soothe Your Soul

Treatments exclusively feature natural skincare and thalassotherapy from two renowned spa brands, ESPA and 111SKIN. All products are paraben-free, powerful and effective – providing a journey of healthy aging and a balanced life.

## Oasis Infinity Pool

The ultimate destination for serenity and socializing, our infinity-edge Oasis Pool is unparalleled on Mykonos. This unique seawater pool features four private cabanas that appear immersed in the water. Framing the pool are comfortable sun beds with umbrellas – perfect for viewing the iconic panorama of the Aegean Sea. Exclusively for adults, the Oasis Pool embodies Santa Marina’s innately glamorous lifestyle.

## Elais Pool

With the Aegean as a stunning backdrop, the infinity-edge Elais Pool is expansive in size and features a nearby heated whirlpool. This pool is ideal for children and provides a safe space for youngsters to splash and play.

### Playground

To ensure our younger guests have their own fun holiday, we have a specially designed playground for children ages three and older. Shaded from the sun, this sea-view facility features fun equipment, including a slide, swings and a playhouse.

### Children's Facilities

Children can also splash and swim in our family friendly Elais Pool or in the shallow waters of our private beach – with golden sand that’s perfect for building castles.

### The Private Beach

With its own private sandy beach, Santa Marina offers an unrivalled sanctuary where guests can swim and sunbathe in blissful seclusion along the shores of the azure Aegean Sea.

## Exclusive Speedboat Services

For those who wish to enjoy the open waters, we offer exclusive speedboat services. Our rubber speedboats, available for an hourly rate, are ideal for exploring the beautiful beaches or nearby islands in comfort.

## Reef Boutique

Right on our private beach, Reef Boutique reflects the super-chic style of Mykonos with a selection of fashion-forward clothes, jewellery, sunglasses and accessories for women and men. The collection changes with the season, featuring airy caftans, stylish dresses, swimwear and beachwear. Some of the most exclusive Greek and international designers are highlighted, including Adriana Degreas, Blue Life, Celia Kritharioti, Juliet Dunn, Marysia, Melissa Odabash, Orlebar Brown, Poupette St. Barth and Soraya Hennessy. Open from 10:00 am to 10:00 pm.

Does Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort, Mykonos offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as A gym fully equipped with the latest Technogym., Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
