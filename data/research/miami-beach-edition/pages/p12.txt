# Getting Here

- From Fort Lauderdale Hollywood International Airport
- From Miami International Airport

## From Fort Lauderdale Hollywood International Airport

## DRIVING:

Start out going west on Terminal Dr toward S Service Rd. Merge onto I-595 W toward I-95. Merge onto I-95 S via EXIT 10B. Merge onto I-195 E via EXIT 4 toward Miami Beach. I-195 E becomes W 41st St. Turn right onto Indian Creek Dr/FL-A1A. Continue to follow FL-A1A. Turn left onto Collins Ave/FL-A1A. Hotel is on the right.

## TAXI:

Taxis are available at the airport on a first-come, first-served basis. This will cost you approximately $65 each way and will take approximately one hour of travel time.

## PRIVATE CAR:

A private car to/from Fort Lauderdale Hollywood International Airport will cost from approximately $150 each way and can be arranged by the hotel concierge by calling +1 786-257-4575 prior to your arrival/departure.

## From Miami International Airport

## DRIVING:

Start out going south on FL-953/NW 42nd Ave toward NW 14th St. Take the ramp toward NW 125th St. Merge onto NW 21st St. Turn left to take the FL-953 N/Le Jeune Rd ramp toward FL-112/I-95. Turn slight left to take the FL-953 N/Le Jeune Rd ramp toward FL-112/I-95. Turn slight right onto NW 42nd Ave/FL-953. Merge onto FL-112 E/Airport Expy E toward I-95/Miami Beach/Ft Lauderdale (Portions toll). FL-112 E/Airport Expy E becomes I-195 E. I-195 E becomes W 41st St. Turn right onto Indian Creek Dr/FL-A1A. Continue to follow FL-A1A. Turn left onto Collins Ave/FL-A1A. Hotel is on the right.

## TAXI:

Taxis are available at the airport on a first-come, first-served basis. This will cost you approximately $33 flat fee each way and will take approximately 30 minutes of travel time.

## PRIVATE CAR:

A private car to/from Miami International Airport will cost from approximately $130 each way and can be arranged by the hotel concierge by calling +1 786-257-4575 prior to your arrival/departure.
