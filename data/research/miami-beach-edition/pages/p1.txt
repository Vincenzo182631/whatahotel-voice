# THE URBAN RESORT FOR THE 21ST CENTURY

Surround yourself with luxury at The Miami Beach EDITION. At this beautiful tropical oasis, guests can enjoy the personalized service and privacy associated with boutique hotels and the amenities and access of a larger resort. Spacious rooms, suites, and bungalows benefit from modern design elements and city, pool, or ocean views. A luxury spa and fitness center, two outdoor ocean-facing pools, sophisticated indoor and charming outdoor venues, and extraordinary dining, including restaurants developed by Michelin-starred Chef Jean-Georges Vongerichten, await you.

Experience 24 Hours At The Beach

View Rooms & Suites

## Home away from home

Our boutique hotel in Miami Beach comprises 294 individually designed guest rooms and suites, including 28 private bungalow rooms and suites featuring terraces— some with two stories and decks overlooking the beach— and a spectacular rooftop penthouse. Every space enjoys soft, neutral colors, marble bathrooms, large desks, mini-bars, and fine linens. Luxury bungalows promote a sense of privacy with their terraces and sundecks with private plunge pools.
