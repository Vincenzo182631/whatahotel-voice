## FAQs for InterContinental Dhaka

Does InterContinental Dhaka have a pool?

InterContinental Dhaka has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

The outdoor swimming pool is temperature controlled and offers infinity views of the lush surroundings.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Dhaka?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Dhaka for hotel guests.

Cars can be parked all around the hotel and the hotel basement also houses a multi-level car park.

Parking fee: 0 USD per day

Parking for registered guests only

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Dhaka?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Dhaka.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Dhaka for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Dhaka have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Dhaka has 5 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Dhaka?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Dhaka?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Dhaka.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Dhaka?

Check-in at InterContinental Dhaka is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Dhaka offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Dhaka offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Dhaka from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Dhaka is conveniently located near Hazrat Shajalal International Airport (DAC), approximately 9.2 miles away (14.8 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
