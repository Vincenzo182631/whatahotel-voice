### Disappointing Honeymoon Experience at IHG

13 days ago

We chose InterContinental for our honeymoon, expecting a memorable experience and the quality of hospitality associated with the IHG brand. Unfortunately, our stay was disappointing.

I would like to acknowledge Mr. Zakir, who was courteous, professional, and helpful during the booking process.

The most disappointing part was the condition of the room amenities. Both the Wi-Fi and television were faulty. Despite contacting the front desk and requesting assistance, no one came to investigate or resolve the problems during our stay.

As we had specifically booked a honeymoon package, we expected greater attention to detail, prompt assistance, and an experience that would make the occasion special.

While we appreciated the initial booking assistance, the lack of responsiveness and unresolved room issues significantly affected our stay. For a property carrying the InterContinental name, this fell short of our expectations.

I hope the management takes this feedback constructively, improves guest complaint handling, and ensures that room facilities are checked before guests arrive, particularly those booking packages for special occasions.

ProsSleep Quality, Room Cleanliness, Location, recognition for ihg rewards club members

ConsAmenities

Yes, I recommend this hotel.
