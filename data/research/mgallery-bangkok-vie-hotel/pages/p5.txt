## The Edge – Pool Bar

* * *

The Edge – Pool Bar is the newest tropical hangout venue located in VIE Hotel Bangkok. With relaxed atmosphere that is perfect for enjoying the view between dips in the pool or for a casual meet-up with friends.

It can be found right at the edge of the infinity pool, which extends to the very front of the YTSB building with its glass screen at the end allowing guests to feel as though they are swimming towards the beautiful horizon.

Serving healthy, tropical fruit-based drinks and cocktails, The Edge – Pool Bar ideal for a refreshing sip whilst topping up one’s tan. It also offers a menu of light Thai and International meals for those hungry after their swim.

|  |
| --- |

The poolside furniture has been refurbished with increased comfort and in Aqua Blue theme reflecting the color of the water in the pool, giving it a very chic touch.

As dusk falls, the sun loungers are augmented with poolside tables and chairs allowing guests to watch the sunset whilst relaxing over a post dip cocktail. The experience is elevated by subtle and stylish lighting enabling guests to extend their poolside visit well into an enchanted evening in Thailand’s capital. It is an ideal way to enjoy the romance of Bangkok nightlife just as it awakens surrounded by the colorful nightscape skyline.

A haven of luxury and calm in the very heart of bustling Bangkok, modern and elegant VIE Hotel Bangkok is situated adjacent to Ratchathewi Skytrain station, and just 10 minutes’ walk from the city’s premier shopping district at Siam Square, the hotel is ideally placed for both business and leisure purposes. VIE Hotel Bangkok is part of the MGallery collection, in which each property  is noted for its individual personality of modern and stylish comforts.

**Opening Hours:** Open daily from 8AM. – 8 PM.
