## **Piano Bar**

### The Lounge

Situated in the heart of Bangkok’s commercial district, where fashion, art, business and travelers meet, Piano Bar is a natural gathering spot for the city’s eclectic movers and shakers. Impeccably chic and glamorous, Piano Bar features elegant Art Deco interiors, comfortable lounge seating and  jazz accompaniments.

|  |
| --- |

We practice “zero waste” mixology—in other words,  and refrain from the use of plastics and selects ingredients with minimal waste.  Piano Bar includes an extensive wine, sparkling and beer list, as well as mocktails and juices.
