In a nutshell
: Cheval Blanc Paris is one of those properties that truly makes your jaw drop—beautifully designed with every whim or fancy thought of ahead of time, a stay here feels like a taste of life as a royal.
Madison Flager
Madison Flager
Rooms
The Maison's 72 rooms and suites start at a generous 484 square feet, and more than half offer views of the River Seine (others look out at Parisian façades or the Louvre). From my Seine Junior Suite, I could sit and watch the Eiffel Tower glittering at night from a sort of solarium-style indoor balcony, replete with floor-to-ceiling bay windows. The king bed was one of the most comfortable I’ve ever slept on (Cheval Blanc doesn't disclose where the mattresses are from, unfortunately); pillow choices are, of course, fully customizable—just take a look over the beside pillow menu and your preferred style will be sent right up. It’s one of the many things your dedicated butler, trained to anticipate your desires, can accommodate during your stay. (On the day of my arrival from New York, I was delighted to return to my room from dinner and find a perfectly heated bath awaiting me.)
The marble bathrooms are unmatched in luxury—double sinks, a steam shower, a beauty fridge, and an amenities line commissioned from François Demachy, Christian Dior's longtime in-house perfumer. The two signature scents, one woody, one powdery, are woven into the room’s soaps and lotions, and are diffused via candles and room sprays throughout the Maison.
For the absolute utmost in luxury and privacy, book L’Appartement, a nearly 11,000-square-foot space on the 8th and 9th floors. It has its own 40-foot-long swimming pool, a wellness area and a projection room, seven bedrooms, a panoramic terrace, several lounges and dining rooms, along with private access and a secure entrance.
Madison Flager
Madison Flager
Food and drink
The Maison is home to a wildly impressive six Michelin Stars, awarded to three of its onsite restaurants: Plénitude (French fare, three stars), Hakuba (Japanese, two stars), and Le Tout-Paris (French brasserie, one star). Led by chef Arnaud Donckele and pastry chef Maxime Frédéric, these venues, along with the Italian-centric Langosteria and summer-hours Le Jardin, are committed to using seasonal ingredients from small producers. During my summer stay, I was delighted to taste the sweetest, ripest tomatoes in many variations: gazpacho, tarts, salads.
Request an outdoor table at breakfast if the weather is nice—the Seine and Eiffel Tower views more than warrant it. And be sure not to overlook the bread basket…I can still taste the Viennese roll, with its thin layer of vanilla and citrus, melting in my mouth.
Madison Flager
Madison Flager
Amenities and experiences
My top hotel criteria is always a pool, though I’ll be honest, indoor pools rarely make the cut. Not so when it’s designed by architect Peter Marino, situated under a mirrored ceiling, and surrounded by handcrafted mosaics by Michael Mayer and large-scale digital screens creating the effect of looking out at the Seine and Paris’s skyline. After the view from my room’s window, and then from the rooftop, this was the third jaw-dropping moment of my stay. The nearly 100‑foot pool is said to be the city’s (and continent's) longest hotel pool; even if you’re not doing laps, you can lounge around the edge with a book as I did. The extra-wide lounge chairs are a nice place to perch too.
Inside the spa itself, there are six Dior treatment suites, a marble-clad hammam, a sauna, cold plunge, and snow shower. Also on this floor: the 24/7 gym, complete the chicest Dior yoga mats, plus a Rossano Ferretti hair salon.
Another can’t-miss experience: a boat cruise on the Seine in a Cheval Blanc–chartered boat; hotel staff will accompany you with snacks, sweet treats, and bubbles. It’s one of the many offerings that can be coordinated by Christophe Seganti, the Clefs d'Or Head Concierge, and his team.
What to do nearby
What a prime location this is—Notre Dame is a 15-minute walk away on the Ile de la Cité, while the
Louvre
is just 5 minutes from the front door. Just beyond the renowned museum are the Arc de Triomphe, Jardin des Tuileries, and Musee de l’Orangerie.
This is an LVMH–owned hotel after all, so a visit to the Louis Vuitton Café next door is in order. Shop, pick up chocolates made in-house, or sit down to lunch (be sure to save room for ice cream, served in an iconic LV–printed waffle cone).

Madison Flager
The backstory
This is one of six Cheval Blancs around the world, and the only city-based one. The ultra-luxury hotels are the brainchild of Bernard Arnault, the chairman and CEO of LVMH, who conceived of the idea after building his own private residence in Courchevel. The French ski town became the site of the first Cheval Blanc under LVMH Hotel Management in 2006; since then, others have opened in destinations well accustomed to this level of luxury: St. Barts, the Maldives, Seychelles, and St. Tropez.
Fittingly, the Paris Maison sits within Henri Sauvage's 1928 Art Déco masterpiece, La Samaritaine, a designated historic site and the birthplace of French department stores along the banks of the Seine. This section of the building was meticulously restored and reworked into a hotel over the course of five years, opening in fall 2021. In 2026, Cheval Blanc Paris was
awarded “Palace” distinction
by France’s Ministry of Tourism; it's now one of just 33 French hotels with that status.
Insider tip
If you’re an art fan, be sure to look up and around—architect Peter Marino, who reworked the interiors in his first hotel project, collaborated with artisans, painters, photographers, and sculptors to add pieces to the shared spaces in the spirit of a private Parisian home. Pieces include a Frank Gehry horse sculpture (specially commissioned for the Maison); woven metal textiles by Sophie Mallebranche; gilded bronze panels by Ingrid Donat; and lithographs by French artist Sonia Delaunay.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Yes; the complementary Le Carrousel kids club offers daily activities for guests up to 12
Accessibility:
There are four wheelchair-accessible rooms in the King Deluxe Suite and Seine King Suite categories
Wellness:
Dior Spa, fitness center, in-room training available
