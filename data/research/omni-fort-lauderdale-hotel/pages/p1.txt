## Fort Lauderdale, Florida

### Escape to our hotel in the heart of Fort Lauderdale

Perfectly positioned along the Intracoastal Waterway and directly connected to the Broward County Convention Center, Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel offers a rare blend of waterfront serenity and urban accessibility. Steps from Port Everglades, the hotel is ideally situated for pre- and post-cruise stays, while its proximity to Fort Lauderdale Beach and easy accessibility for Florida drive-market travelers make it an inviting destination for a Fort Lauderdale staycation, weekend getaway or South Florida escape. Guests will enjoy sweeping views of passing yachts, glimmering canals and, from higher floors, the shimmering Atlantic Ocean beyond—all from the heart of one of South Florida’s most dynamic coastal destinations.

Upon arrival, guests will discover expansive meeting spaces, seven dining venues, an urban outdoor pool deck and Mokara Spa, featuring the area’s only Hammam experience. Signature dining and nightlife include Reef 76: Kitchen & Bar, serving fresh seafood and coastal dishes; Fair Ketch, a lively sports and entertainment bar featuring a Topgolf Swing Suite with two bays; and Ibis Sky Lounge, Fort Lauderdale’s highest rooftop bar, offering panoramic views from the 29th floor. Coming Fall 2026, Barchetta will bring modern Italian dining to the waterfront with handmade pastas, premium seafood and Italian-inspired steaks and cocktails. Spacious guest rooms, signature furnishings and state-of-the-art amenities complete the experience for convention attendees, cruise travelers, business guests, families, couples and Florida residents seeking a South Florida getaway close to home.

## Traveler's Insights

### What are the property amenities at Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel?

Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel has the following amenities available for guests:

Mokara Spa

Pool

On-site dining

24 hour fitness center

### Does Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel have a pool?

Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel has a swimming pool located on the 10th floor of the hotel that is available year-round to all hotel guests. Enjoy coastal views, chic loungers, and refreshing food and beverage service from the rooftop bar during your stay.

### What is the best area to stay in when visiting Fort Lauderdale?

Downtown Fort Lauderdale is one of the best areas of the city to stay in during your visit. Our hotel is conveniently located right next to the cruise port and is surrounded by endless things to do and area attractions.

### What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 4 PM and check-out is at 11 AM. Guests must be 18 years or older to check in at the hotel.

### What is the closest airport to Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel?

Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel is located near the following airports:

About 3.4 miles from Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL)

About 29.1 miles from Miami International Airport (MIA)

### Where is Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel located?

Omni Fort Lauderdale is ideally located in Broward County along the Intracoastal Waterway in Fort Lauderdale’s Marina District, directly connected to the newly expanded Broward County Convention Center. The hotel is adjacent to Port Everglades, one of the world’s busiest cruise ports, and just 3.4 miles from Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL), making it a convenient choice for anyone traveling to the area. The hotel's physical address is 1850 SE 17th St., Fort Lauderdale, Florida, 33316.

### How much is parking at Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel?

Parking at Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel is valet only and will have a charge on room reservations for the service. For more information, call the hotel at (754) 224-3700 782-5300).

### Is Omni Fort Lauderdale Is pet friendly?

Omni Fort Lauderdale Hotel is a pet-friendly hotel in Fort Lauderdale that allows two pets up to 60 pounds each. The pets must be registered to a room and a non-refundable cleaning fee of $200 must be paid upon check-in. Learn more about the full pet policy.
