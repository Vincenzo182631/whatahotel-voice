Make like Queen Elizabeth and check into this legend.

Give us the highlights.
The Royal York has been open since 1929 and has undergone several sweeping renovations to ensure it keeps up with the times. Built on the site of an even older hotel (from the 1850s), the landmark occupies a full block in the Financial District and is located across the street from Union Station and close to attractions like Rogers Stadium. Past a majestic Art Deco façade that stretches up to a central tower, the vast, bustling lobby still has its historic feel intact. But this is a hotel about the future, too: the premises are a certified Zero Carbon Building by the Canada Green Building Council.
Who else is checking in to stay here?
Given its location and size, the hotel draws a ton of business travelers—individuals and conference-goers alike. That said, Fairmont Gold lounge perks make it a hit with families and Fairmont loyalists, and you'll spot plenty of locals indulging in the legendary afternoon tea or in after-work martinis in the Library Bar.
Speaking of checking in—how is the process?
A whopping 1,363 rooms means things can get hectic at prime check-in and check-out times, so the hotel has implemented iPad check-in, paperless checkout, and digital path-finding monitors to help guests navigate the sprawling property.
Given the choice, where should we be staying?
Try to book one of the Luxury or Signature rooms, which were just revamped with upgraded bedding, extra seating, and fresh décor. All rooms have amenities like HDTVs, coffee and tea makers, windows that open, and automated mini-bars (so be careful about moving things around).
Most crucially, how's the food?
The hotel’s signature restaurant Reign offers a bistro menu with Canadian ingredients incorporated throughout, as well as an elegant bar area and bakery. A separate bar Clockwork Champagne & Cocktails feels more glamorous for after-hours tête-à-têtes. Don’t miss the Library Bar, a cocktail den tucked away from the crowds, if you want a more private-feeling drinks experience.
Any other perks?
The advantage of the valet's free in-and-out policy is to use your own four wheels. The newly-refreshed 24/7 Fairmont Fit Center has Peloton bikes, an array of TechnoGym equipment, an indoor lap pool with a whirlpool, a wading pool for kids, and steam and sauna facilities. The hotel is also connected to
Toronto
’s PATH, an underground, interconnected pedestrian walkway network, connecting numerous shopping centers, office buildings, movie theaters, and the TTC subway.
Bottom line: who is this best for?
If you're a fan of historic hotels—and don't mind ones with more than a thousand rooms—make like Queen Elizabeth and check into this legend.
