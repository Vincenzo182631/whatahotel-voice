## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Royal York located?

Fairmont Royal York is located at 100 Front St W, directly across the street from Union Station.

Does Fairmont Royal York have an airport shuttle?

There is a complimentary shuttle bus that runs between Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport and Fairmont Royal York every 15 minutes.

Union Pearson Express (Up Express) connects Toronto Pearson International Airport to Union Station, directly across from our doorstep, in just 25 minutes. Up Express trains depart every 30 minutes.

Is parking available at Fairmont Royal York?

Valet parking is available to guests of Fairmont Royal York. Located at the hotel east entrance, we can accommodate vehicles up to 6’4”. Five Electrical Vehicle (EV) charging stations are available.

Day Rate:

- $35 for the first two (2) hours, inclusive of tax
- Each additional hour: $5, up to a maximum of $75, inclusive of tax

- No in-and-out privileges permitted

Overnight Rate:

- $75 CAD per night, inclusive of tax
- Valid from the day of arrival until 4:00 pm on the day of departure

- In-and-out privileges included

_\*Rate subject to change_

Nearby Parking Alternatives:

Self-parking is recommended at 79 Wellington Street\*.

_\*Availability and cost not managed by Fairmont Royal York_

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Royal York?

Accessible accommodations are available at Fairmont Royal York. We strive to meet the needs of all our guests. Please contact the hotel if you have special requests or need additional accessibility information about the hotel's facilities and services.

Is Fairmont Royal York pet-friendly?

Small pets are allowed in the hotel but cannot be left unattended in guest rooms at any time and must be kept on a leash in common areas. There is a $60 CAD per stay pet fee and Deluxe Rooms are recommended for pets over 20 lbs (9 kg).

Please note pets are not permitted on our hypoallergenic floors, Health Club or Dining Venues.

Service dogs are welcome at the Fairmont Royal York at no additional fee, however, we kindly ask you contact the hotel in advance of your stay.
