# Birdie’s Diner

This slice of mid-century Americana takes you back to simpler times, with classic diner favorites served overlooking the 10th hole and main putting green. Choose from “Sweet Start” options and “Eggs & More” off the breakfast menu, or enjoy all-day dining with “Main Drivers” ranging from Local Fish Tacos to an All-American Wagyu Cheeseburger.

Pull up a chair at the bar or in a cozy corner booth. Watch players out on the fairway from the outdoor covered patio, complete with vintage golf cart. It’s all in a day’s play at Birdie’s Diner in Palm Beach Gardens.
