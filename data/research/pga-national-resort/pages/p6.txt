## Signature Treatments

#### **Seaside Renewal**

**90 min**

Surrender to the restorative essence of the sea with this luxurious experience combining a hydrating, shower-free seawater body polish and an express brightening facial. Skin is gently exfoliated with marine salt crystals and Phytomer’s Oligomer® trace minerals to smooth texture, dissolve dullness, and restore vitality. The experience concludes with a refreshing ocean-inspired facial that awakens, refines, and illuminates the complexion—leaving skin radiant, refreshed, and glowing with renewed vitality.

#### **Palm Breeze Ritual**

**90 min** Drift into tranquility with The Palm Breeze Ritual—a full-body experience that smooths, soothes, and restores. Begin with a marine oil and sea salt polish, followed by warm sachets of marine salts and lavender to ease tension and awaken energy flow. Thai-inspired massage techniques with stretching and pressure points promote balance and deep relaxation, leaving you refreshed, renewed, and restored.

#### **Calming Cocoon Treatment**

**90 min**

Unwind with this deeply relaxing body treatment designed to quiet the senses and restore balance. A full-body Himalayan salt and sugar exfoliation gently smooths and renews the skin while encouraging circulation. A nourishing botanical body lotion infused with calming CBD is then applied before you are cocooned in comforting warmth, allowing rich ingredients to deeply hydrate and soothe the body. As you rest in this tranquil wrap, a soothing scalp or foot massage enhances relaxation before the experience concludes with a flowing full-body massage and targeted relief for areas of tension.

#### **Prenatal Pamper**

**90 min** This head-to-toe ritual begins with a foot scrub and mask to soothe tired feet. An organic shea butter balm is applied to the abdomen to hydrate the skin. The treatment concludes with a full body massage and facial massage with a jade roller. Perfect for mothers-to-be in their second or third trimester.

## Wellness & Technology

## **Advanced Skin Therapy**

#### **OxyLight Infusion Facial**

**60 min**

Experience the next generation of skin rejuvenation with the advanced OxyLight® Ionix system. Diamond micro-abrasion refines skin texture while negative-pressure technology stimulates circulation and supports lymphatic detoxification. Concentrated oxygen therapy enhances the delivery of targeted serums from Phytomer, while LED light, ultrasound, and microcurrent technologies help improve tone, vitality, and product absorption. Skin is left visibly smoother, brighter, and deeply revitalized.

#### **OxyLight Ultimate Lift Facial**

**90 min**

An elevated extension of the OxyLight Infusion Facial designed to enhance tone and firmness. Precision resurfacing and oxygen infusion prepare the skin for deep delivery of concentrated serums from Phytomer, while lymphatic stimulation encourages circulation and detoxification. An extended MyoLight microcurrent sequence helps tone facial muscles and improve elasticity, followed by a restorative mask to hydrate and refine the complexion. Skin appears lifted, sculpted, and radiant.

#### **Radiance LED Renewal Facial**

**60 min / 90 min**

Experience advanced skin rejuvenation with the Radiance PT™ pulsed LED light therapy table. This customized facial pairs professional skincare with targeted red light therapy to support collagen production, improve circulation, and enhance overall skin vitality. As nourishing serums and masks are applied, pulsed LED light works beneath the surface to promote cellular renewal, calm inflammation, and restore a healthy, luminous glow. Skin appears smoother, brighter, and deeply revitalized.

#### **Deluxe Hydrafacial**

**60 min / 90 min**

Elevate your skincare with a Hydrafacial enhanced by DOCTOR BABOR's Collagen HA Booster. This powerful serum, rich in Collagen Peptide Complex, Marine Collagen, and Hyaluronic Acid, targets fine lines and loss of elasticity, promoting a firmer, plumper, and more youthful appearance. Perfect for those seeking advanced anti-aging benefits.

## Mind & Body Recovery

#### **MIND-SYNC RESET**

**25 min /** **60 min**

Experience a guided sound and vibration meditation designed to soothe the nervous system, enhance focus, and promote profound mental relaxation. Complemented by your choice of aromatherapy and a warming neck wrap, this session can be booked as a stand-alone experience or as a prelude to your spa treatments for a complete reset of mind and body.

#### **NUWAVE**

**25 min /** **60 min**

Drift into deep restoration with a full-body nervous system and circulation reset, blending zero-gravity wave motion, therapeutic sound, and lower-body compression with Therabody Jet Boots. Enhanced with your choice of aromatherapy and a warming neck wrap, this immersive session eases stress, enhances circulation, and leaves body and mind completely renewed.

#### **OxyLight Mineral Body Treatment**

**90 min**

Powered by the OxyLight® Ionix system, this advanced body treatment helps refine skin texture while restoring hydration and vitality. Targeted diamond abrasion gently resurfaces areas of concern while negative-pressure massage stimulates circulation and supports lymphatic drainage. Oxygen therapy enhances the absorption of remineralizing ingredients from Phytomer, helping replenish essential nutrients and leave the skin smoother, refreshed, and revitalized.

#### **Radiance LED Recovery Massage**

**60 min / 90 min**

This restorative full-body massage is enhanced by the Radiance PT™ pulsed LED light therapy table to support muscular recovery, circulation, and deep relaxation. While your therapist performs a customized massage using techniques tailored to your needs, red light therapy works beneath the body to encourage cellular repair and ease areas of tension. The combination of therapeutic touch and advanced light technology helps reduce muscular fatigue, improve circulation, and leave the body feeling restored and renewed.

#### **NEO Luna Whole-Body Cryotherapy Chamber**

**15 min**

The NEO Luna is a state-of-the-art electric whole-body cryotherapy chamber designed for single guests or couples. Cryotherapy serves as a powerful starting point for an immersive wellness ritual, supporting inflammation reduction, muscle recovery, circulation, and energy levels. Guests may integrate cryotherapy with the spa’s steam rooms, sauna, Himalayan Salt Chamber, and the signature “Waters of the World” experience, featuring three mineral pools—including Dead Sea and Salies-de-Béarn pools—along with an outdoor hot tub for guided hot-and-cold contrast therapy.

## Therapeutic Wellness

#### **Acupuncture**

**60 min /** **90 min**

Acupuncture is a holistic service designed to support the body's natural detoxification processes, ease muscle tension, and promote post-activity recovery. Ideal for those looking to reset, recover and revitalize, this treatment restores balance and supports a pain-free, refreshed state.

#### **Cupping Massage**

**60 min**

This therapeutic massage incorporates the ancient healing practice of cupping to release deep-seated tension and restore healthy circulation. Using gentle suction, silicone cups create a vacuum effect that lifts and separates tight muscle layers, promoting detoxification and encouraging lymphatic flow. Ideal for guests seeking relief from chronic tension, stagnation, or post-workout soreness, this treatment leaves you feeling lighter, looser, and deeply renewed.

#### **Himalayan Salt Room Experience**

**25 min**

Relax and restore in our Himalayan Salt Room by Salt Chamber. This tranquil halotherapy environment gently disperses micronized salt particles into the air to support respiratory wellness, promote clearer breathing, and encourage deep relaxation. As you unwind in the calming glow of Himalayan salt, the experience helps reduce stress, refresh the senses, and restore balance to both body and mind.
