King

This 1,449-square-foot suite features a king bedroom with ensuite bathroom, living and dining areas, a kitchen (minus stove and oven), wet bar, and half bath. Four balconies overlook the pool and lake, with two walk-in closets providing ample space. Additional amenities include two 65” LCD TVs, complimentary WiFi, two sofas, and a bathroom with walk-in shower and separate soaking tub.
