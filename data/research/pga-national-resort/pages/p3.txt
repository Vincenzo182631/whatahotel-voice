Perfect for girls' getaways, golf trips, and families, twenty-one stunning Serena & Lily-designed cottages bring the coast to the greens in elevated leisure. Residentially-appointed, crisp, and stylish, each 1,200 sq. ft. two-bedroom, two-bath retreat delivers unsurpassed privacy and prestige, plus tropical outdoor space that personifies Palm Beach bliss. This beautiful cottage has one King bed and two Double Beds.

In the vast living spaces, a palette of breezy coastal blues and whites elevates vacation vibes, designed in lacquered wood, tufted wool, glazed ceramics, and American-crafted upholstered pieces. In the bedrooms, handwoven rattan, and classic caning create nightly and daily escapes, the beds awash in cozy layered textiles. On the private terrace, the bistro table and chairs are a scenic spot to enjoy a pitcher of lemonade before teeing off or gathering for a game of cards.

The Cottages, designed by Serena & Lily at PGA National Resort – after pool day & rosé, the last putt, or a day of legendary Palm Beach shopping, returning to the comforts of home makes all the difference.
