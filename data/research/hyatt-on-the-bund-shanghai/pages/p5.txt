### Disappointing service despite advance notice

We were very disappointed with the handling of our arrival at Hyatt on the Bund. We had requested an early check-in in advance and fully understood that this was subject to availability.

However, after a long intercontinental journey we arrived at around 1 p.m. and were simply told that no room would be available before 3 p.m. We explained that we needed to rest, shower and change before a private tour at 4 p.m., but no alternative or practical solution was offered.

The explanation was that the hotel was fully booked and that it was a public-holiday period. Both circumstances were foreseeable. In a hotel of around 620 rooms, and with our request known in advance, we would have expected at least some effort to prioritise a suitable room or find another solution.

We have travelled to more than 70 countries and know many leading international hotel chains. We are well aware that early check-in cannot always be guaranteed. Our criticism is not the lack of a guarantee, but the apparent lack of organisation, flexibility and genuine effort to assist.

For a hotel of this category, this was extremely disappointing.

### Unbeatable Location, Amazing Views & Genuine Hospitality

We really enjoyed our stay here. The rooms are modern and clean, although I wouldn’t call the hotel ultra-luxury. What really stands out is the view — almost every room has a great view of the Huangpu River and the Bund. You really can’t beat the location.

The service was also a highlight, especially Chris Lee, the Hotel Manager. We had an issue with the shower water temperature, and Chris personally came with the engineer to check on it. He followed up with us several times and even stayed past his shift to make sure the issue was eventually fixed.

Chris was very attentive and really listened to what we needed. He also sent us a thoughtful gift box of mooncakes as an apology for the inconvenience, which was a very nice touch. We also asked him about the hotel’s sister property, and he was very knowledgeable and gave us a helpful, honest comparison of the pros and cons of each.

Overall, we had a great stay. The location and views are hard to beat, and the warm, personal service from Chris really made a difference. We would definitely come back.
