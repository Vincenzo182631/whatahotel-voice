## VUE Dining

VUE Dining consists of one main dining hall and five individually-designed private dining rooms. The cuisine features a selection of delicate dim sum and signature Cantonese dishes. Elegant with a distinctly residential ambience, VUE Dining is the ideal venue for family and friendly gatherings or for business dinners, with a vista of Shanghai’s colourful skyline as your backdrop.

## Room Service

Room Service offers the ultimate in convenience for a quick start for early risers, a romantic dinner for two, or a hospitable gathering for a group of friends. From all-day dining to light snacks, the Room Service menu provides an entire range of international breakfast – American, continental or Shanghainese. Beverage selection includes coffee, tea, soft drinks, milk, juice, beer, wine, sparkling wine and champagne.

## Xindalu - China Kitchen

At Xindalu- China Kitchen, offers a traditional and elegant ambiance, bright and comfortable natural lighting, creating a distinct dining atmosphere. The cuisine mainly features traditional flavors from Shanghai and the Jiangsu-Zhejiang region, delivering a delightful culinary experience in a relaxed and comfortable setting. The restaurant's signature dishes include the Peking Duck roasted in the wood-fired oven, delicious Beggar's Chicken and the Pyramid Braised Pork.

## Aroma

Experience a culinary adventure at our international hotel buffet which offers a sumptuous selection of authentic cuisines prepared in eight open concept kitchens ranging from Chinese (roast duck, wok and noodle stations), Western (seafood and salad bar, grill, and freshly oven-baked pizzas), Japanese (sushi, sashimi, teppanyaki, and tempura) and a Pastry counter featuring a colourful and abundant dessert selection.
With its "Dining in the Kitchen" concept, the restaurant also offers a la carte menu to satisfy all taste buds.

## VUE Bar

An elegant, comfortable and stylish fashion bar located on the top two levels of the hotel, commanding the breathtaking views of both sides of the Huangpu River - the Bund on the Puxi side and the colourful skyline of the Lujiazui area on the Pudong side. An extensive variety of premium beverages and a comprehensive selection of refined champagnes are on the menu.
