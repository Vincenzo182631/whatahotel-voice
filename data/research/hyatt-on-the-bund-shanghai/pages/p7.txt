Rooms
342
"Within walking distance of the Bund," this geometric tower with a pointed apex has "spacious, elegant" rooms—makore-wood panels, silk-textured wallpaper, jade-colored ceramics. Since the building rises 60 stories, "all you need to do is point to the sky when directing cabbies." Order afternoon tea or cocktails in the Lobby Lounge. The indoor lap pool is flanked by floor-to-ceiling windows.
