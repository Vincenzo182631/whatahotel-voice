Hyatt on the Bund houses a total of 620 guest rooms, including 52 suites, split between the East and West towers. Ranging in size from 42 to 300 square metres (452 to 3,229 square feet), these contemporary spaces feature floor-to-ceiling windows that allow guests to best experience the breathtaking views of Pudong or the Bund.
