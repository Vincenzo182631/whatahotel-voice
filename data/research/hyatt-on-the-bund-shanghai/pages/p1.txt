## A Dynamic Hotel in Shanghai

Grand stays start at Hyatt on the Bund Shanghai. Hotel suites and rooms offer spectacular views of the Bund and Pudong, with convenient access to Shanghai's airports and to Lujiazui CBD via Xinjian Road Tunnel within 10 minutes, and walk to the Bund within 5 minutes.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Hyatt on the Bund, Shanghai boasts 620 guest rooms, including 48 suites, split between the East and West towers. These contemporary spaces range from 42 to 300 square meters (452 to 3,229 square feet) and feature floor-to-ceiling windows that allow you to experience the breathtaking views of Pudong or the Bund.

Indoor Pool

Located on the Lower Lobby level, the indoor heated swimming pool is 25 meters by 12.5 meters in size with a depth ranging from 0.9 to 1.2 meters. A separate and spacious whirlpool bath can also be found in the pool area.

### VUE Dining

VUE Dining consists of one main dining hall and five individually-designed private dining rooms. The cuisine features a selection of delicate dim sum and signature Cantonese dishes. Elegant with a distinctly residential ambience, VUE Dining is the ideal venue for family and friendly gatherings or for business dinners, with a vista of Shanghai’s colourful skyline as your backdrop.
