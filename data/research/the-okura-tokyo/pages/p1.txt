## The Okura Tokyo

The peaceful, graceful essence of Japan \- since 1962

The Okura is a luxury hotel in Tokyo with a difference. Across two brands and buildings with intriguingly different styles - The Prestige Tower and The Heritage Wing - we provide impeccable hospitality, five-star amenities, and world-renowned luxury.

Yet, nowhere in the world matches The Okura for bringing the elegance of Japanese history and design to life. Welcome to Japan.
