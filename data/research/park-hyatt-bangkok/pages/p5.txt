### Love this hotel

I just love this hotel. You will be hard pressed to find staff at any of the luxury hotels in Bangkok with this level of professionalism. But it’s not just the level of service. There is a sense of being truly welcomed here that feels different from other hotels. I’m always remembered when I check in, or when I’m having breakfast. Not in a cursory way, but in a seamless, elegant way. The rooms are great, the decor is sophisticated luxury and the experience is one that brings me back again and again.

Plus, it’s connected to Central Embassy and the mall on the other side of that…which turns out to be quite a bonus is you’re staying there when it’s really raining, which is has from the end of September through early October.
