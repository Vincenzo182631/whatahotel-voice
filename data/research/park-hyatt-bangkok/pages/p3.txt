## Penthouse Bar + Grill

Penthouse Bar + Grill delivers a New York–inspired evening of live entertainment, vibrant cocktails, and classic steakhouse dining. At Penthouse Grill on Level 34, premium cuts of meat and fresh seafood are expertly prepared over organic charcoal and presented with theatrical tableside flair. On Level 35, Penthouse Cocktail Bar elevates the experience with innovative mixology and captivating live jazz performances. Above it all, the Rooftop Bar serves interactive cocktail experiences set against the dramatic backdrop of Bangkok’s skyline. A vibrant destination where exceptional steaks, live jazz, and expertly crafted cocktails come together to create an unforgettable night in the city.

## The Bar

The Bar, on Level 10, is a haven for wine enthusiasts, featuring an exceptional collection of fine wines curated for discerning palates.
