## FAQ's

What dining options are available at Paséa Hotel & Spa?

Paséa is home to four distinct restaurants, bars and dining venues. Lōrea, our signature restaurant, serves breakfast, lunch and dinner daily, plus has a full-service bar. Treehouse on PCH is our rooftop bar and lounge. Blend Café offers coffee, tea and grab-and-go fare while Ninth Island Pool Bar serves refreshing sips and bites poolside. In-room dining is also available for hotel guests.

Are the restaurants open to the public, or just hotel guests?

Lōrea, Treehouse on PCH and Blend Café welcome locals, hotel guests and other visitors. Dining at Ninth Island Pool Bar requires pool access; locals may purchase a day pass or cabana, based upon availability.

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended for Lōrea, especially for dinner, weekends and patio seating. They can be booked online or by phone. Seating at Treehouse on PCH, Blend Café and Ninth Island Pool Bar are walk-in only.

Do you offer gluten-free, vegetarian and/or vegan options on your menus?

Yes, our culinary team is happy to accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and other dietary needs and allergies across our restaurants. Menus denote available options for common dietary preferences, but please let your server know when you're seated, and they can assist with recommendations.

Are your restaurants kid friendly?

Families with children of all ages are welcome at Lōrea, which offers a kids menu with various options for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Kid-friendly snacks and drinks are also available at Ninth Island Pool Bar and Blend Café. Children are not permitted at Treehouse on PCH; all patrons must be 21 years or older with valid ID.

Are dogs allowed at the restaurant?

Yes, dogs are welcome at Lōrea, which offers a dedicated dog menu for four-legged friends. Treehouse on PCH and Blend Café also allow leashed dogs. Dogs are not permitted at Ninth Island Pool.

Can I host a private event or group dinner?

Yes. Private dining spaces are available for celebrations, rehearsal dinners, business events and other gatherings, with menus built around the occasion, timeframe and number of attendees. Large groups can also secure a buyout of Lōrea or Treehouse on PCH for an exclusive event.
