## Know Before You Go

Aarna Spa Guidelines

For the enjoyment of all guests, please read the following guidelines prior to your appointment at Aarna Spa. We appreciate your respect and cooperation.

Arrival Time

Please arrive at least 15 minutes prior to your service. Each service will start and end according to the time reserved. To accommodate all our guests as scheduled, late arrivals will receive the balance remaining in treatment time.

Reservations

Advanced reservations are recommended. To reserve your services, please call (877)382-0840 or book online. All spa services are guaranteed with a credit card.

Cancellation

Please provide a 24-hour notice should you need to cancel an appointment. The full cost of services will be charged for cancellations within 24 hours or no-show appointments.

Cashless Payments

For your convenience, Aarna Spa operates as a cashless outlet. All purchases and payments, including spa services, treatments and retail, may be made using all major credit cards, debit cards, contactless payments, or room charge. Cash is not accepted.

Spa Service Charge

For your convenience, pricing is inclusive of a 22% service charge, of which 16% is allocated to Aarna Spa staff, 2% to Aarna Spa support staff, and 4% is retained by Aarna Spa. Any additional gratuities are optional but always appreciated.

Age Requirements

We welcome guests 18 years or older.

Therapist Preference and Medical Conditions

Please state a preference for a male or female therapist at the time of reservation. Please advise us of any medical conditions or if you are pregnant.

Quiet Enjoyment

To preserve relaxation and privacy, please turn off your cell phones, cameras, and all other electronic devices.
