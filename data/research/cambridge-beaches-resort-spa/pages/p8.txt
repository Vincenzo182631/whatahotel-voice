# Top Bermuda Restaurant & Cocktail Bar

The Bermuda chapter of the renowned New York City cocktail bar is now open at Cambridge Beaches as a dockside tavern and tropical cocktail bar with an adventurous menu. Highlighted by authentic Bermudian fare and local ingredients, Sunken Harbor Club offers a one-of-a-kind dining experience featuring vibrant flavours and innovative libations in a lively waterfront setting overlooking the sparkling Mangrove Bay.

SUMMER SERENADE: A BERMUDA TALE BY EXECUTIVE CHEF JAMES WAMBUI

When Bermuda’s sunlight lingers late and the ocean breeze carries salt and jasmine, our summer menu comes alive. I recall eight unforgettable summers on this island—each one teaching me that sun-ripened tomatoes, sweet carrots, and freshly caught wahoo sing brightest under open skies. From vibrant ceviches and chilled melon gazpachos to herb-strewn lobster and decadent desserts, every dish at SHC celebrates the island’s warmth, energy, and effortless elegance.

This season, our dishes honor Bermuda's culinary visionaries:

  - **Tommy Poh**, the globetrotting craftsman, blending European precision with island warmth—proof that mastery and soul make magic.
  - **Jonny Roberts**, a minimalist maestro whose edible poetry reminds us that restraint reveals flavor's purest form.
  - **James Wambui**, the pink sands innovator, turning Bermudian bounty into bold, boundary-pushing creations.
  - **Damien Griffith**, the plant-forward pioneer, elevating the humble harvest into rich, radiant cuisine.
  - **Jean-Claude Garzia**, the French ambassador, whose Franco-island elegance shaped a generation of Bermudian fine dining.
  - **Keith De Shields**, the cultural alchemist, fusing heritage and haute cuisine in every bold, rooted dish.
  - **Shawn Ming**, the culinary steward, championing Bermudian flavors through mentorship and mastery.
  - **Serge Bottelli**, the transatlantic virtuoso, harmonizing Alpine technique with island bounty since 1979.

Here, culinary artistry meets the heart of Bermuda—every plate a tribute to sun-soaked days, balmy evenings, and the bounty of our fields and seas. “Faz Bem Comer”—“It’s Good to Eat”—as we celebrate farmers, fishermen, and the chefs before us on whose shoulders we stand, sharing the effortless joy of summer in Bermuda.

Bon Appetit and Fair Winds.

James Wambui

Executive Chef

### The Abyss

_Our strongest drinks come from the point of no return._

_Limited 2 Abyss Drinks per person per day._

THE COXSWAIN

A big, bold cross between an Old-Fashioned and a

Caipirinha, starring Sunken Harbor Club Navy

Strength Rum.

Served on crushed ice.

ROYAL BERMUDA MARTINI

A subtropical gin (or vodka) martini, with subtle

flavours of lime zest and spice.

Stirred and served up (with a sidecar).

REMEMBER THE MAINE

A beguiling variation of the Manhattan with dark

cherry and absinthe from Havana, 1933, recorded

### THE CULINARY VOYAGE

THE SUNKEN HARBOR TASTING EXPERIENCE

Tonight, aboard the Sunken Harbor Club, Chef James captains a culinary odyssey inspired by Bermuda's shipwrecks and the mentors who've shaped his journey. We invite you to surrender to tides of flavor and memory as we explore history's depths and creativity's heights. Our muse is the English brig Caesar, which in 1818 met its end on Bermuda’s reefs, its cargo of grindstones, bricks, glassware, and a grandfather clock scattered and transformed by time and tide. These lost treasures became Bermuda's soul—bricks in the Rum Runners Building, grindstones paving paths, Masonic flasks whispering secrets. Tonight, the Caesar’s story is reborn on your plate, each dish a tribute to its journey and serendipitous second life. In this omakase experience, taste the sea's salt, shared stories, and the artistry of a chef who has found his true north in Bermuda. Each course celebrates island bounty and inspiring collaborators. So, raise your glass, set your compass for an unforgettable voyage.

Bon Appétit and Fair Winds.
