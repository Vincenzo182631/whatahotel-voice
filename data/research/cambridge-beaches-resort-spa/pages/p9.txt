## Bermuda Private Dining

From intimate, candle-lit dinners for two to larger gatherings amongst family and friends, Private Dining at Cambridge Beaches is an entirely immersive event. With five secluded locations offering a spellbinding backdrop, and custom-crafted menus from our culinary team, your experience becomes nothing short of extraordinary

**_Available May - October, this is an experience that won’t soon be forgotten._**
