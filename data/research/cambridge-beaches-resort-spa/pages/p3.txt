Where Time Rests, and The Ocean Remembers

## King’s Point

Set on a remarkable 4.5-acre private peninsula at the furthest eastern point of Cambridge Beaches Resort & Spa, King’s Point is one of Bermuda’s most storied coastal homes—once the residence of legendary Bermudian explorer Teddy Tucker.

From these shores, Tucker set out in search of shipwrecks and hidden histories, shaping a legacy of discovery that still defines this place today. Surrounded by private coves, secluded beaches, and two private islands just offshore, this beautifully restored five-bedroom waterfront retreat offers the privacy of a standalone residence with the service and amenities of a luxury resort just steps away.

Here, generations of stories—from maritime exploration to modern coastal living—meet effortless comfort. Ancient shoreline features, water-wrapped views, and quiet beaches create a setting that feels both exclusive and deeply rooted in Bermuda’s past. Whether gathering with family, celebrating with friends, or simply retreating to the edge of the island, King’s Point offers an experience unlike anywhere else.

## A Private Peninsula Escape

At the tip of a slender peninsula overlooking Mangrove Bay, King’s Point feels worlds away while remaining connected to the warmth and hospitality of Cambridge Beaches.

Guests can enjoy private access to coves and beaches surrounding the property, with four beaches reached by steps leading down to the water. A private dock, hot tub, outdoor shower, and barbecue area invite long, easy days outdoors, framed by sea breezes and panoramic views. Inside, the home is designed for relaxed coastal living, with spacious gathering areas, a fully equipped kitchen, a stocked pantry, and a wine fridge ready for entertaining. A private butler ensures each stay feels seamless and personal.

### Rooted in Bermuda’s Story

Long before it became a private retreat, King’s Point was part of Bermuda’s living history. Appearing on some of the island’s earliest maps, the peninsula has been home to notable Somerset families, early hospitality at Cambridge Beaches, and the extraordinary legacy of ocean explorer Teddy Tucker.

From here, Tucker ventured into surrounding reefs and wreck sites, helping to uncover and document Bermuda’s rich maritime history. His work brought global attention to the island’s legendary shipwrecks, establishing Bermuda as a destination for discovery beneath the sea.

Today, that spirit endures—woven into the landscape, the waters, and the experience of staying at King’s Point.
