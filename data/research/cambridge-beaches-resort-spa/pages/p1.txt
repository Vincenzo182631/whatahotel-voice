### A Storied Destination

# Bermuda Luxury Resort

Steeped in tradition and Bermuda history, Cambridge Beaches has been a treasured destination for oceanfront escapes since 1923. With authentic cottage accommodations, dedicated service, and a wealth of amenities – there’s truly no better way to experience the luxuries of Bermuda.

### Stunning Views

# Rooms, Suites, Cottages & Residences

With classic Bermudian charm, thoughtfully appointed interiors, and plentiful indulgent amenities, every accommodation at Cambridge Beaches boasts a private entrance, a dedicated outdoor seating area, and stunning views. Multi-room cottages and adjoining suite options make for effortless travel with family and friends.

### Dine on the Water

With both sunrise and sunset views from our multiple waterview restaurants and bars, we invite you to choose your own adventure through a landscape of sensory delights. Sip your coffee while strolling through our sea gardens, break midday with a selection of salads at our poolside bar, celebrate the sunset with a swizzle at our oceanfront restaurant, and indulge in bold island flavors at our dockside tavern and tropical bar.
