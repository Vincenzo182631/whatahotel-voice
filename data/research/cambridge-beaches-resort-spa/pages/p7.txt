# Shoreline

Located in the lower level of our Main House commanding a picturesque location with panoramic views of Mangrove Bay. Enjoy daily breakfast along with seasonal weekly events including our popular Monday BBQ!

* * *

### Overlooking Mangrove Bay...

Offering an idyllic setting for daily breakfast accompanied by fresh ocean breezes, Shoreline is also home to our popular seasonal Monday BBQ (May–October), where guests and locals gather for an unforgettable evening of dining, entertainment, and island charm.

**Breakfast Served Daily 8:00 - 10:30 am**

**Coffee and Pastries 10:30 am - 12:00 pm**

## A Bermuda-Favourite, Monday BBQ

Join us each Monday for BBQ classics, live entertainment, waterfront views over Mangrove Bay, and exciting raffle prizes. New for this year, guests have the chance to win complimentary resort experiences throughout the evening.

**Monday BBQ Details:**

- Every Monday through October 26, 2026
- Adults: $70 per person ++
- Children (5-11): $35 ++ (Free 4 Years & Under)

_Prices do not include drinks_
- Entertainment: DJ Uncle
