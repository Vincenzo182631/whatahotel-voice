### Long Bay Beach

The longest of our four private beaches, Long Bay Beach offers a perfect locale for lounging in the sand and is a spectacular vantage point to take in magical Bermudian sunsets. Savor refreshing cocktails and delicious bites with service available from Breezes, just steps away.

### Afternoon Tea

### Explore Bermuda

Get out and discover Bermuda's rich history, diverse culture, iconic natural attractions, championship golf courses, and exciting vacation activities.

### Royal Naval Dockyard

Explore a historic naval base with picturesque waterfront, colonial architecture, shops, the National Museum of Bermuda, Dolphin Quest, and more!

### Gibbs Hill Lighthouse

Explore Bermuda's oldest cast iron lighthouse. Climb to the top for panoramic island views, learn about its history, and enjoy stunning coastal scenery.

### Port Royal Golf Course

World-class oceanfront golfing with stunning views. Challenging fairways, scenic landscapes, and top-notch amenities for an unforgettable golf experience. Exclusive resort guest rates and packages are available.

### Horseshoe Bay

Pink sands, turquoise waters, and iconic rock formations. Swim, snorkel, relax, and capture stunning photos at Bermuda's most popular beach.

### Downtown Hamilton

Bermuda's bustling capital city with colorful architecture, British charm, and a mix of shops, restaurants, and cultural attractions.
