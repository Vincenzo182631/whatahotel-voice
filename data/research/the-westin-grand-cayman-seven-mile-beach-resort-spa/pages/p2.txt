## Accommodations

Find Your Balance in spacious guest rooms offering invigorating island views of Grand Cayman or revitalizing views of the sea. Our inspirations for design began with an abstract interpretation of the ocean,...See More influenced by the colors and organic fluidity, our concepts are integrated with the surrounding environment. Our Grand Cayman Island suites and rooms exude a Caribbean elegance by combining a fresh palette with a sophisticated touch of timeless coastal design and a continuity of local Caymanian flair.See Less

### Island View Rooms

Offers a view of the interior of the island. The upper level rooms offer a view of Safe Haven golf course and the northeastern end of the island. Ground floor rooms offer patios.

## Partial Ocean View Rooms

These Grand Cayman hotel rooms offer partial views of Seven Mile Beach from the north and south wings of the resort.

## Ocean Pool-Lanai Rooms

Ocean/Pool View rooms are located in the interior of the hotel. They offer a view of the pool area as well as of Seven Mile Beach

## Deluxe Ocean Front

Our Deluxe Ocean Front rooms are located directly on Seven Mile Beach. They offer an unobstructed view of the ocean in Grand Cayman. The ground level hotel rooms feature patios and the 2nd floor rooms carry balconies.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa?

The largest occupancy room at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa is the Presidential Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa?

No, the rooms at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa do not permit rollaway beds.
