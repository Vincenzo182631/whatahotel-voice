# Welcome to The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa

## Discover paradise in our Grand Cayman beach resort

Escape to your dream Caribbean beach getaway at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa. Beautifully situated on prime oceanfront along Seven Mile Beach, our Grand Cayman hotel offers all...See More you need for a memorable stay. Activate your day with invigorating water sports on the beach or find pure refreshment at our poolside swim-up bar. Rejuvenate your body and mind with a visit to our spa or energize yourself in our 24-hour Westin Workout® Fitness Studio. Sample the variety of flavors at our restaurants, from a romantic dinner on the beach to Grand Cayman's freshest sushi, ceviche and poke at Woto. Welcoming guest rooms await you, with relaxing island-inspired designs, incredible views and thoughtful amenities. If business brings you to our Seven Mile Beach resort, you'll find that our elegant function venues and superb services will help to turn work into fun. We look forward to welcoming you to The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa.See Less

## Hurricane Policy

In the event of a hurricane on the island of Grand Cayman, the resort will re-schedule or cancel your booking without penalty. This guarantee applies to room and applicable taxes, cancellation fees will be waived. Please note, rooms and rates are subject to change should you choose to re-schedule your booking. This policy is applicable to transient reservations, group bookings should refer to your contract.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa is Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM). GCM is located approximately 4.3 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa have an airport shuttle to Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM)?

No, The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa does not have an airport shuttle to Owen Roberts International Airport.

Does The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Grand Cayman Seven Mile Beach Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
