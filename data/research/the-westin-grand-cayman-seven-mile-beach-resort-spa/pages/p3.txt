## Dining

Refresh your palate and your soul at one of our many restaurants on Seven Mile Beach, Grand Cayman. From an airy Caribbean setting for breakfast at Ferdinand's restaurant to a themed night on the beach at Tortugas to a AAA 4 Diamond experience in Beach House to locally inspired cuisine fresh from the sea at woto, you'll find a variety of flavors with a Cayman Islands influence.

## Introducing Executive Chef Jonathan Rivard

With more than 20 years of international culinary experience, Executive Chef Jonathan Rivard brings a distinctive blend of classical French training, global influence, and Caribbean inspiration to The Westin Grand Cayman. Formally trained in Paris, his culinary journey has taken him across France, Canada, Bermuda, and the Cayman Islands, shaping a philosophy rooted in authenticity, craftsmanship, and a deep respect for quality ingredients.

Prior to joining The Westin Grand Cayman, Chef Rivard served as Executive Chef at several of Grand Cayman’s most acclaimed restaurants, including Agua Restaurant, Luca Restaurant & Lounge, Craft Food & Beverage Co., and Mykonos Steak Club. He was also the founder and Executive Chef of Steak Social, a concept that has earned international recognition.

Having called Grand Cayman home for more than a decade, Chef Rivard is passionate about showcasing the island’s bounty through fresh, locally sourced ingredients and meaningful partnerships with local fishermen and farmers. His culinary style combines refined techniques with vibrant, contemporary flavors to create memorable dining experiences.
