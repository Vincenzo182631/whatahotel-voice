# REGENT SHANGHAI PUDONG

##### DELUXE HAVEN ABOVE THE BUSTLING URBAN SKYLINE

A dimming sunset slowly cascades through a wall of windows, peeking through the ever-expanding skyline of the Shanghai business district in Lujiazui. The glowing city lights bounce gracefully off marbled surfaces, creating a rhythmic sense of calm amid a bustling city. The sights and symphonies of the city are awaiting to be discovered, yet the hotel’s oasis of the sky-high infinity pool looks ever so indulging. Shanghai’s golden age is alive and well in Pudong, where past and present wistfully converge.

### An Urban Retreat

Inspiring views of the skyline enter each room through floor-to-ceiling windows. Art-deco-inspired designs create a personal haven and a sense of calmness in the vibrancy of the city.

DINE

### Excursions for the palate

Allow yourself to indulge at any of our three restaurants and discover what makes your taste buds truly sing. Will it be traditional Italian cuisine at Camelia, luring you in with the scent of baked mozzarella? The lively atmosphere and vibrant colors of sushi on each plate at Mi Teppanyaki? Or the intimacy of Shang-Xi, presenting delectable Cantonese fare in the most alluring of settings?

## Go beyond

Set amongst towering giants, you’ll want to compare your hotel room views with the record-breaking Shanghai Tower and the iconic Oriental Pearl Tower, both located nearby. Indulge your sense of curiosity at the Shanghai Museum, or immerse yourself in cultural history at Yu Garden. Not to be missed, the Bund provides a pleasant backdrop for an evening stroll with spectacular views of glowing Pudong, completing your day with serenity and reflection.
