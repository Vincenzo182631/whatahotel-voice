Elevate your stay in our 732 sqft / 66 sqm Heavenly Penthouse, a bi-level suite with a plunge pool. Whether seeking a Puerto Vallarta friends getaway resort or a honeymoon suite, enjoy spacious living areas, relaxing balconies, and flexible sleeping arrangements.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort?

The largest occupancy room at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort is the Wholesome Family with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort?

Some rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.

Can I request connecting rooms at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort?

No, connecting rooms are not available at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort.
