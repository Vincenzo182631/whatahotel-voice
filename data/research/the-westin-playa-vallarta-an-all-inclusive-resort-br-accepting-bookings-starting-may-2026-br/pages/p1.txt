# Welcome to The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort

## An All-Inclusive Beachfront Escape in Puerto Vallarta

A new chapter begins on the shores of Puerto Vallarta, inviting travelers to discover a refreshed all-inclusive experience rooted in wellness, connection, and the beauty of the destination. The Westin Playa Vallarta features 281 redesigned suites, including swim-up accommodations and private plunge pool options, all featuring the signature comfort of the Heavenly® Bed. Rejuvenate at the reimagined Westin Heavenly Spa®, enjoy botanical elixirs at Flora Mixology Bar, and savor diverse culinary experiences across six restaurants and four outdoor venues. Stay active at Fitness Workout®, enjoy tennis, pickleball, and padel courts, or unwind by one of two outdoor pools overlooking the Pacific. A thoughtfully curated entertainment program offers engaging activities for guests of all ages. Located in Marina Vallarta, minutes from downtown and the airport, this beachfront retreat blends recreation, culture, and restorative serenity.

### Reconnect With Nature at Our All-Inclusive Resort in Puerto Vallarta

Discover a holistic wellness experience at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort in Puerto Vallarta Mexico. With 281 redesigned suites—including 30 Junior suites and suites with plunge pool in addition to 16 new swim-up junior suites—this retreat fosters relaxation and renewal. Rejuvenate at the reimagined Westin Heavenly Spa, sip botanical elixirs at Flora Mixology Bar. Savor a tapestry of cuisines at the Gourmet Gallery, featuring six distinct dining venues and four outdoor options. Engage in immersive wellness experiences, from interactive entertainment to relaxing beach walks at our beach front resort. Located in the heart of the bustling Marina Vallarta and just minutes from downtown and airport, with 18-hole golf club nearby, this haven blends outdoor adventure, authentic culture, and enriching serenity in the heart of Puerto Vallarta.

## Savor Every Moment

At The Westin Playa Vallarta in Puerto Vallarta, an all-inclusive resort, our "Eat Well" brand pillar emphasizes nutritious dining options to promote overall well-being, with menus designed to nourish both body and mind. This philosophy shines in the Gourmet Gallery, a vibrant, spacious social hub with unique architectural flair. Here, guests are captivated by diverse gastronomic offerings, blending flavors and experiences into a unique culinary journey in Puerto Vallarta.

New to the Gourmet Gallery is the Agave Studio, where guests can indulge in tastings to learn about the rich heritage of mezcal.

Additionally, the outdoor culinary options allow our guests to savor their meals while enjoying the refreshing ocean breeze. The lively atmosphere invites guests to immerse themselves in dynamic social interactions, making our resorts culinary experience, global, unforgettable, and unique experience.

## Experience the Coastline Pulse

Feel the pulse of the Pacific. Start your day with oceanfront yoga or join a ceviche-making session with local flair. As the sun dips, sip cocktails to live beats or dance barefoot under the stars. At Westin, every moment is a chance to move, connect, and celebrate your vitality.

## A Wellness Sanctuary in Nature

Our resort’s focus is on enhancing emotional and mental well-being, offering amenities and services that promote relaxation and rejuvenation for a balanced stay. Heavenly Spa by Westin allows you to replenish your mind and body with a personal sensory experience. Nestled in a prime location, in Puerto Vallarta our resort embraces biophilic design, seamlessly integrating lush landscapes, open-air spaces, and natural light. We elevate well-being with experiences that nourish the body and mind. Whether it's a yoga retreat by the ocean, sanctuary retreats, or spa treatments infused with local ingredients, our resort fosters relaxation and renewal. With open-air spaces and mindful activities, wellness is seamlessly woven into every stay at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort .

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of being a Marriott Bonvoy Member at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort?

Marriott Bonvoy Members staying at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort are eligible for additional benefits only available at resorts in the All-Inclusive by Marriott Bonvoy Portfolio. You can refer to official benefits here.

What is included in the per night room rate at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort?

The nightly room rate at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort typically includes: room, meals, snacks, non-alcoholic & alcoholic beverages, entertainment, non-motorized water sports, kids’ activities, taxes, service fees, and gratuities.

What additional amenities and services are available for purchase at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort?

The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort may offer spa treatments, motorized water sports, laundry, transportation, tours, premium items, and services for an additional fee.

Are there any guest age restrictions at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort?

The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort is a Family-Friendly resort and is open to guests of all ages. The guest count of adults and children should be updated during the booking process to ensure appropriate pricing.

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort?

The check-in time at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $30.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort?

The closest airport near The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort is Gustavo Diaz Ordaz International Airport Puerto Vallarta (PVR). PVR is located approximately 2.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have an airport shuttle to Gustavo Diaz Ordaz International Airport Puerto Vallarta (PVR)?

No, The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort does not have an airport shuttle to Gustavo Diaz Ordaz International Airport Puerto Vallarta.

Does The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
