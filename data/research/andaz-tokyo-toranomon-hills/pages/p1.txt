# Culture and Sophistication Meet in the Heart of Tokyo

Located in Toranomon, Andaz Tokyo Toranomon Hills rises above the city on the upper floors of Toranomon Hills, offering a luxury stay that blends vibrant urban energy with genuine comfort and relaxation. In 2026, the hotel was recognized among the Top 10 Best City Hotels in Japan in the Travel + Leisure Luxury Awards Asia Pacific, awarded One MICHELIN Key in the MICHELIN Guide Hotel Selection, and named one of the Top 5 Best Hotels in Japan in the DestinAsian Readers' Choice Awards. Celebrated for its exceptional hospitality, the hotel welcomes guests with thoughtful amenities, including complimentary beverages and snacks at the Andaz Lounge throughout their stay. Inspired by Japanese aesthetics, the hotel features contemporary design, locally inspired artwork, refined guestrooms, and sweeping views of Tokyo Tower, the Imperial Palace, and Rainbow Bridge. Guests can also enjoy diverse dining experiences, including the internationally acclaimed rooftop bar overlooking Tokyo’s skyline.

## Rooms & Suites

Blending traditional Japanese design with modern conveniences, our 164 rooms, including eight suites, offer unparalleled views of the metropolis below. After a day of exploration, admire the sparkling lights of the Tokyo Tower or relax in our deep soaking bathtubs to prepare for a restful night’s sleep

## DINING

Details & Reservations

### The Tavern – Grill & Lounge

Inspired by authentic European cuisine and timeless classics, The Tavern strikes a balance between tradition and innovation. Rooted in refined culinary techniques, our menus showcase seasonal ingredients and products sourced from producers across Japan. Whenever possible, we prioritize locally sourced ingredients and certified products, including carefully selected seafood. Dining at The Tavern is an immersive and spontaneous experience, offering unique moments that celebrate genuine hospitality. We strive to create a vibrant and welcoming atmosphere where every visit becomes a memorable occasion.

Details + Reservations

### BeBu

Discover our American comfort food restaurant, BeBu, located on the ground floor of the Andaz Tokyo Toranomon Hills. At this restaurant you can taste classic American dishes that have been revisited by the Chef de Cuisine, Niko Martinez. The menu focuses on classic American comfort foods including original burgers, boneless Cajun chicken wings and classic American desserts. This casual restaurant will take you to a unique American dining experience. Pets are allowed on the outdoor terrace seating.

Details + Reservations

### Rooftop Bar

Crowning the hotel on the 52nd floor, the Rooftop Bar offers spectacular night views of Tokyo Bay and Odaiba in a sophisticated semi open-air terrace. Enjoy the lively atmosphere as experienced mixologists craft innovative cocktails highlighting local seasonal ingredients. SEATING CHARGE FOR TERRACE SEATS: No seating charge applies from 17:00-20:00. From March to December, a seating charge of JPY 2,200 (incl. tax) per person is applied for guests seated at the terrace after 20:00. Complimentary seating charge to registered hotel guests. AGE RESTRICTIONS: Visitors to the Rooftop Bar must be 20 years old or above.

Details + Reservations

### the SUSHI

Tucked behind the Rooftop Bar on the 52nd floor, an authentic ‘omakase’ experience awaits you at the SUSHI. Enjoy a glass of our original ‘52’ sake at the intimate eight seat sushi counter, and watch as master chefs prepare traditional edo-mae sushi using the freshest seasonal ingredients.

Details + Reservations

### Pastry Shop

Located on the street level of Toranomon Hills Mori Tower, the Pastry Shop offers a bright space filled with natural light. The marble counter features a variety of fine patisserie and signature sweets, including cakes and sweet jars prepared with seasonal and indigenous ingredients. Enjoy a sweet midday break in our open café or select a cake to takeout for a special occasion.

Details + Reservations

### Room Service

Savor a delicious breakfast, lunch, dinner, dessert or a personalized selection from our premium wines chosen by our sommelier in the comfort of your suite or room.
