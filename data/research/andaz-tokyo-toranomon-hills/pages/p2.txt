## 1 King Bed, Park View

Park View

Floor Plan

Drawing upon Japanese tradition, the design of this 50-square-metre guestroom incorporates natural materials and partitions that allow one to customize the space based on personal style. Locally made linens top one king bed and wide windows frame the captivating panoramas of the Imperial Palace, Tokyo Skytree, and Tokyo skyline perfectly. A bathtub awaits in the innermost chamber, while complimentary amenities include a non-alcoholic minibar and wireless Internet access.

In-Room Amenities

  - Complimentary minibar (nonalcoholic beverages and snacks)
  - Andaz hotels’ exclusive bath amenities, infused with the captivating essence of BYREDO’s signature fragrance, Eleventh Hour
  - Complimentary use of pool and fitness center at AO Spa & Club
  - 24-hour nonalcoholic beverages and local snacks at Andaz Lounge
  - Nightly wine and canapés from 5:00pm to 7:00pm
  - Complimentary Wi-Fi
  - Bathrobe and yukata (Japanese-style robe)
  - Slippers
  - Nespresso coffee maker
  - Electric kettle
  - Iron and ironing board
  - Shower and separate deep soaking tub
  - 55” flat-screen 4K UHD TV with casting, cable and satellite​
  - Bluetooth speaker
  - Blackout blinds
  - In-room safe with charging portal
