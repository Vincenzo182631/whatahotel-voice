Tokyo Station

Transportation Options

From Tokyo Metro Station (Marunouchi Line)

Take Marunouchi Line to Ginza Station then take Hibiya Line to Toranomon Hills Station. Walk from Toranomon Hills Station. This takes about 25 minutes.

Toranomon Hills Station (Hibiya Line)

Transportation Options

Directly connected to Exit B1 via underground passage 3-min walk

Guests can use the underground passage directly to the Tokyo Metro's "Hibiya Line".
