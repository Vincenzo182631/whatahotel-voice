## Beach House

Exceptionally private and north-facing, Beach House takes in panoramic views across the Coral Sea and Whitsunday Islands, with a 12-metre infinity-edge pool and secluded setting.

Generously proportioned and beautifully appointed, the light-filled, open-plan residence features a main bedroom with ensuite, spacious entertaining area and dining for 10. A separate guest house, with its own terrace and ensuite, offers additional privacy for friends or family.

qualia is exclusively for guests aged 16 years and over.
