## Pebble Beach

As evening settles, Pebble Beach presents qualia’s signature expression of dining, shaped by place and season. Set at the edge of the Coral Sea, the menu unfolds naturally, reflecting Queensland’s seasons, locally sourced ingredients and carefully selected global produce, brought together with thoughtful technique and restraint.

A carefully selected range of Australian and imported wines is available to complement the menu, with pairings offered for those who wish to be guided.

Open Tuesday to Saturday.

## Long Pavilion

Long Pavilion offers relaxed cuisine centred on peak-season Australian produce, with a carefully edited wine list and drinks menu. The bar and lounge overlook the Whitsundays, making it a natural place for sunset aperitivo or a nightcap.

Open daily.

## Dinner Opening Hours

**Pebble Beach bar**

Poolside refreshments from 10.30am to sunset, daily

**Pebble Beach restaurant**

Dinner from 6.00pm to late Tuesday - Saturday

**Long Pavilion restaurant**

Dinner from 6.00pm to late, daily

**Long Pavilion bar**

Bar service from 10.00am to late, daily

**In-Pavilion Dining**

Dinner from 6.00pm to 10.00pm, daily

A limited number of tables are available each evening. We recommend securing dining reservations ahead of your stay.

_A 10% surcharge applies on Sundays and a 15% surcharge applies on public holidays._

## qualia Resort Dress Code

Throughout the resort, an **elegant resort style** is encouraged.

Well-tailored, polished attire is appropriate across dining and public areas. Casual beachwear is reserved for pool and beach spaces only, with suitable cover-ups and footwear required when moving through the resort.

* * *

## Bookings recommended

A limited number of tables are available each evening. To make a reservation, book online or contact guestservices@qualia.com.au who will be delighted to assist.

A 10% surcharge applies on Sundays and a 15% surcharge applies on public holidays.
