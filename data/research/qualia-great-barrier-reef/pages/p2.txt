# Luxury in the heart of the Great Barrier Reef

## Private, spacious and beautifully appointed, qualia's pavilions are immersed in the natural Whitsunday landscape, each with its own perspective on the islands and Coral Sea.

## qualia Pavilions

Surrounded by native bushland and overlooking the Coral Sea, qualia pavilions offer a private sanctuary where understated design and the landscape come together in distinctly Australian style.

Choose from Windward, Seaward or Leeward Pavilions, each with its own perspective on the Whitsundays. Discover the Sunset Pavilion with self-catering features, or indulge in the expansive luxury of The Beach House or The Residence.

Explore each pavilion below.

## Sunset Pavilion

Adjacent to qualia’s private helipad and nestled close to the shore, the west-facing Sunset Pavilion takes in sweeping views across Dent Passage and the surrounding islands. Perfectly placed for memorable Whitsunday sunsets, its spacious interiors include one bedroom, a separate study, full kitchen and laundry, combining relaxed Australian luxury with the convenience of self-catering.

## The Beach House

Exceptionally private and north-facing, The Beach House offers spectacular Coral Sea views and generous spaces for relaxing and entertaining. Walls disappear to open the living spaces to a furnished entertaining deck, dining for 10 and a 12-metre infinity-edge pool, while a separate guest house offers additional privacy for friends or family.

## The Residence

Privately tucked away on the western side of qualia, The Residence takes in uninterrupted Coral Sea and island views. With two bedrooms and a standalone studio, it is the largest property at qualia, offering generous spaces for relaxing and entertaining.

An open-plan kitchen, lounge and dining area opens to two outdoor spaces and a private 25-metre infinity-edge lap pool, an indulgent setting to feel time unravel and take in the evening sunsets.

## qualia Guest Services

qualia’s guest services team provide intuitive and friendly service from the moment guests arrive at the Hamilton Island Airport or Marina. The thoughtful, expert team are on hand at all times to help with anything that is needed, including providing VIP transfers day and night. Guests also have access to an electric golf buggy for their own private use around qualia or Hamilton Island.

Once a reservation is made at qualia, book a pre-arrival consultation with the luxury concierge to find out more about qualia’s activities including tours to the Great Barrier Reef, diving, sailing in the Whitsundays, Spa qualia and qualia’s signature Talk and Taste experience.

**Pre-booking is required for dining, spa treatments, golf and other tours and activities.** Contact qualia guest services on email **guestservices@qualia.com.au** or phone **07 4948 9222**.
