## Windward Pavilions

Windward Pavilions are qualia’s most elevated pavilion experience, with a superior, light-filled layout that captures sweeping 180-degree views across the Whitsundays and Coral Sea.

Generously proportioned and beautifully appointed, each pavilion is immersed in the tropical landscape, shaded by Eucalypts with sea breezes moving through louvre windows and floor-to-ceiling glass opening the interiors to the view. Wake to the Coral Sea from the king-size bed, or step from the living space onto the private terrace and infinity-edge plunge pool, where the horizon stretches across the Whitsundays.

Polished hardwood floors, warm timber details and a restrained palette create a distinctly Australian beach house ambience.

qualia is exclusively for guests ages 16 or over.

### Windward Accessible Pavilion

The Windward Accessible Pavilion features a thoughtfully configured layout designed for easy wheelchair access.

Key features include a private outdoor patio and garden area with water views, a private plunge pool with step and handrail, wide internal doorways and a bathroom with hand railings and a lowered dual hand basin. The pavilion, balcony and courtyard are step-free, with fittings throughout adjusted for ease of use.
