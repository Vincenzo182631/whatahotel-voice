Experience the incredible geography of the Whitsundays by air and take a truly memorable scenic flight over the Great Barrier Reef and Whitehaven Beach, or helicopter out to the private helipad at Heart Reef. The aerial views and closeups of the iconic Australian natural world are breathtaking.

## Boating

Enjoying the surrounding Whitsundays by boat is what makes a luxury stay at qualia magical. Cruise on qualia’s private charter boat or speed across to Whitehaven Beach and be castaway with a gourmet picnic. Discover the Great Barrier Reef with a snorkel or dive tour, set off on a fishing adventure, or, let the qualia activities team take you to their pick of the best beach on a nearby island for a private beach drop-off and bask in the sun.
