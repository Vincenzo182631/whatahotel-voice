## Leeward Pavilions

South-west facing and immersed in the natural landscape, qualia’s Leeward Pavilions look across Dent Passage and the Whitsundays to the Coral Sea, capturing the changing afternoon light and iconic island sunsets.

Beautifully appointed, each pavilion opens onto a private terrace shaded by Eucalypts. Sea breezes move through louvre windows, while polished hardwood floors, warm timber details and a restrained palette create a distinctly Australian beach house ambience.

qualia is exclusively for guests aged 16 years and over.

### Leeward Garden View Accessible Pavilion

The Leeward Garden View Accessible Pavilion features a thoughtfully configured layout designed for easy wheelchair access. The pavilion opens onto a private outdoor patio and garden courtyard, with wide internal doorways and step-free access throughout.

The bathroom features hand railings and a lowered dual hand basin, with fittings throughout the pavilion adjusted for ease of use.
