## Breakfast at Long Pavilion

Start the day with breakfast at qualia’s Long Pavilion restaurant, facing directly out to Whitsunday Island. Cool sea breezes flow through an expansive open framework of timber and glass, creating the perfect early morning environment. The breakfast menu focuses on fresh, healthy food, with dishes prepared simply to highlight quality ingredients. Guests can begin their morning at the chef’s continental table, showcasing a selection of seasonal produce, house-made pastries, and local specialties, before choosing from the à la carte menu.

## qualia In-Pavilion Dining

qualia’s guest pavilions are tucked away into the surrounding bushland, creating a private sanctuary. Benefit from the beauty and stillness of the Whitsunday and ocean views from the pavilion at morning and take breakfast on the private sundeck. The Long Pavilion qualia breakfast is served from the In-Pavilion Dining menu.
