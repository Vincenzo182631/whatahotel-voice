## About

qualia is situated on the northernmost part of Hamilton Island, surrounded by the natural wonder of the Great Barrier Reef and with views across the Coral Sea and Whitsundays. An oasis of Australian style, qualia provides exceptional levels of award-winning luxury and intuitive, friendly service.

## Stay

Choose from two pavilion types at qualia. The Leeward Pavilions provide a hideaway with a mix of tropical bushland and sea views, and the Windward Pavilions offer private plunge pools. Additionally the exclusive Beach House has its own 12-metre infinity pool and private guest house.

## Taste

Delicious and seasonal, Joshua Hingston, qualia executive chef, sources the freshest Queensland produce for qualia’s two restaurants; Long Pavilion restaurant set high among the Eucalyptus trees with its sweeping views and Pebble Beach restaurant on the edge of the sand. ‘Talk and Taste’ is a unique food and drink pairing experience at qualia.

## Spa

Placed at the heart of the resort and perfectly situated to catch the soft breezes and tranquillity of the Great Barrier Reef, Spa qualia is the ideal place for wellness and relaxation. The spa ethos surrounds a contemporary yet natural approach to day spa, creating individual ‘me time’ with facials, massage, yoga and holistic therapies.
