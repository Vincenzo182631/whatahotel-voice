## Pebble Beach

By day, Pebble Beach offers a lighter expression of its dining philosophy, set against sweeping views of the Whitsunday Islands.

Lunch menus highlight contemporary Australian cuisine, shaped by seasonal produce and ingredients sourced from local farmers, producers and artisans. The atmosphere is easy and informal, allowing the setting to take the lead and the flavours to speak for themselves.

## Pebble Beach Poolside

The Pebble Beach lunch menu is available poolside and in private cabanas at the infinity-edge pool, with drinks service continuing into the afternoon from Pebble Beach Bar.

## In-Pavilion Dining

Set within the natural landscape, qualia’s pavilions offer a private setting for relaxed in-room dining with sweeping Coral Sea views. A selection of dishes from the Pebble Beach lunch menu is available throughout the day.

* * *

**Lunch Opening Hours**

**Pebble Beach restaurant**

Lunch from 11.30am to 3.30pm, daily

**Pebble Beach Poolside**

Poolside menu from 11.30am to 5.00pm, daily

Poolside refreshments from 10.30am to sunset daily

**Long Pavilion Bar**

Bar menu from 10.00am to late, daily

**In-Pavilion Dining**

Lunch from 11.30am to 3.30pm daily

## Dress Code

Throughout the resort, an elegant resort style is encouraged. Well-tailored, polished attire is appropriate across dining and public areas. Casual beachwear is reserved for pool and beach spaces only, with suitable cover-ups and footwear required when moving through the resort.

* * *

## Bookings recommended

A limited number of tables are available each evening. Reservations can be made online or by contacting guestservices@qualia.com.au.
