### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu is the Two Bedroom Family Studio with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu?

Yes, all rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu have fridges?

No, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu have hair dryers.
