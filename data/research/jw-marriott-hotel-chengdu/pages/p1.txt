# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu

## Surround yourself with luxury at our Chengdu, China hotel

Iconic Tianfu Square, the symbol of the city is now home to a symbol of luxury JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu. We welcome visitors to southwest Chinas city with refined style, culturally inspired dining and holistic wellbeing. Our casually elegant hotel rooms and suites boast warm, modern decor, spacious bathrooms and mesmerizing views of downtown. The onsite restaurant of our downtown hotel in Chengdu,...See More China celebrates the flavors of China, providing a modern twist on the traditional cuisine found along Chunxi Road, Chengdus busiest street. We feature eight venues, including a 10,635 square-foot Grand Ballroom with a pre function foyer, a rooftop JW Garden and space for 800. Rejuvenate body and mind at our well-equipped fitness center, indoor swimming pool or luxury spa. This luxury hotel in Chengdu, China offers a modern setting from which to explore Chengdu, Chinas historic past, with ideal placement near Kuan and Zhai alleys, Wenshu Yuan Monastery, Taikoo Li and IFS. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu is Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport (CTU). CTU is located approximately 13.5 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Chengdu has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
