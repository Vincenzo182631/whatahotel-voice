### charters

#### Resort guests can design their own charter or choose from a variety of the captain's preferred experiences. Dine at a sea side restaurant, or pick up some bites to-go and picnic on the boat. Stop for a swim at a secluded beach, cruise by the local lighthouses, and enjoy the beauty of Long Island's East End.      Customize your charter experience and time out on the water with a few of our captain's recommendations by seeing the best of what the East End has to offer.      South Fork: Sag Harbor, Montauk, Seal-watching on the coast of the East End   North Fork & Shelter Island: Duryea's Orient Point, Claudio's, Sunset Beach, Greenport
