A quieter Hamptons property that provides discreet luxury and waterfront views.

Reviewed by

Why book?
A quietly glamorous hideaway on the East Hampton waterfront, EHP draws a discerning crowd looking to sidestep the usual Hamptons scene. With its Mediterranean-meets-Nancy Meyers design, yacht-dotted marina, and sun-soaked restaurant Sí Sí, it’s the kind of place that feels both effortlessly stylish and refreshingly under the radar.
Set the scene
Nestled on the tranquil edge of Three Mile Harbor, EHP offers the kind of low-key luxury that’s increasingly rare in the Hamptons. The resort centers around the marina, giving it a lively, nautical energy without being overtly "beachy." It feels polished but never performative, and the crowd is a mix of well-heeled couples, young families who haven't sacrificed style for strollers, and Millennials rolling in for aperitivo hour, wearing Loro Piana loafers and whimsical Zimmermann dresses—outfits that were likely debuted in St. Barts over spring break. You'll spot the occasional celebrity dining at Sí Sí during golden hour, but the overall vibe is more discreet luxury than flashy display.
The backstory
EHP Resort & Marina underwent a significant transformation in recent years, and was reimagined with a modern Mediterranean aesthetic. While it retains the classic Hamptons charm of the former East Hampton Point, the redesign has infused it with a fresh, contemporary spirit. The owners have focused on creating a relaxed yet refined atmosphere, emphasizing the property's stunning waterfront location and creating a destination where you can be secluded or social, depending on your mood.
The rooms
EHP's accommodations offer a refreshing perspective on Hamptons style, with a collection of chic cottages and suites channeling a sophisticated coastal vibe. Think modern minimalism infused with subtle Mediterranean touches, a calming palette, and an emphasis on elevated comfort. Assouline travel tomes, Acqua di Parma bath products, Frette linens, and Vitruvi diffusers signal the resort’s quiet attention to detail. Families gravitate toward the one- and two-bedroom cottages, conveniently containing fully-equipped kitchens and generous living spaces, while couples often book the Magari Harbor House suites, prized for their Carrara marble bathrooms, rooftop decks, and design-forward interiors that are better suited to adults than toddlers (unless you want your holiday with a side of anxiety).
Food and drink
The resort's dining scene is centered around Sí Sí, a Mediterranean-inspired restaurant that has become
the
reservation to book for a front-row view of the sun setting over the water. The menu features fresh, seasonal ingredients, with standout dishes like the grilled octopus, the seafood paella, bright ceviches, and handmade pastas. The cocktail list is equally impressive, with creative concoctions that are just as photogenic as the view. This season, the space that formerly housed Sunset Harbor will transform into Wayan & Madé, Cedric and Ochi Vongerichten's popular Indonesian-French restaurants—undoubtedly poised to be a fashion crowd magnet. For a casual start to the day, head to Buongiorno, a café nestled within a curated marina boutique, which offers velvety lattes, fruit cups, and buttery croissants by the water.
The neighborhood/area
EHP's location on Three Mile Harbor offers a sense of tranquility that can be hard to find in the more crowded parts of the Hamptons. Yet, the allure of East Hampton Village, with its chic boutiques and acclaimed restaurants, is accessible within 15 minutes by car. Similarly, the area's pristine beaches are within easy reach. For a dose of culture, the Pollock-Krasner House offers an intimate glimpse into the region’s artistic history, while the understated charms of Sag Harbor and the boutiques of Newton Lane are also conveniently accessible. It's a location that feels serene without sacrificing the lively social swirl.
The service
The service is attentive and friendly, striking the perfect balance between polished and relaxed. The staff is knowledgeable about the area and happy to provide recommendations for things to do and see. The concierge can assist with everything from booking dinner reservations to arranging boat charters.
For families
EHP caters to families well, with spacious accommodations, a beautiful pool, a tennis court, and plenty of outdoor space for kids to run around. The resort can also arrange for babysitting services, allowing parents to enjoy a romantic dinner.
Eco effort
EHP's commitments to sustainability include using locally-sourced ingredients for its restaurants and aligning with the Town of East Hampton's environmental initiatives.
Accessibility
EHP meets ADA standards, with accessible rooms and facilities throughout the property.
Anything left to mention?
Be sure to book Sí Sí in advance, as reservations can be scarce over the summer months. Also, head to the bright blue courts for a spot of tennis—they have rackets and balls available for adults and children.
