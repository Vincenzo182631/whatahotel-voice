THE **_iconic_** CITY RESORT AT THE GATEWAY OF ABU DHABI

Fairmont Bab Al Bahr is a 5-star Abu Dhabi beach resort with a stunning setting on Abu Dhabi Creek. Our luxurious urban hideaway offers unparalleled panoramas over the glittering white-marble minarets of iconic Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Bab Al Bahr located?

The property is located at Khor Al Maqta, Abu Dhabi United Arab Emirates.

Is Fairmont Bab Al Bahr pet-friendly?

While Fairmont Bab Al Bahr offers a luxurious experience for many travelers, bringing furry companions isn't currently possible. However, we can recommend some fantastic pet-friendly options in the area! Let us know if you'd like some suggestions.

Does Fairmont Bab Al Bahr have a spa?

Unfortunately, a tranquil spa retreat isn't available on-site at Fairmont Bab Al Bahr. However, the hotel boasts a stunning pool complex and access to a nearby health club, offering opportunities for relaxation and rejuvenation. Contact our dedicated team to learn more about these options.

How can I get to Fairmont Bab Al Bahr from the nearest airport?

Our helpful concierge team is at your service to cater for either a Transporation or Car Rental.
