**Lounge bars, night clubs, cosy terraces... Get ready for the Monaco nightlife experience!**

In Monaco, the evenings are full of sparkle and the nights are magical! Bars, lounges, clubs… Indoors or on the terrace, on the seaside or under the stars, there’s always something trendy and fun to do after work: sip a delicious cocktail, listen to live music, or dance to the best DJs. In short: live it up!

Party time! For festive evenings after work, live music, DJs, clubbing and dancing all night long: La Rascasse never passes up an opportunity to have a good time!  Another unmissable stop in Monaco’s amazing night scene is the legendary Jimmy’z Monte-Carlo and its electro chic partition by Bob Sinclar, Martin Solveig, Mark Ronson, Fatboy Slim and other renowned DJs.

So trendy… With its terrace overlooking the sea, cosy atmosphere and billiard table, the Blue Gin offers cocktails, champagnes, whiskies and liqueurs that you can sip while listening to talented DJs on the decks. A bonus: the finger food menu by Marcel Ravin. At La Vigie, signature cocktails, champagne and rum abound. Here’s an idea: try a cigar or a hookah and spice up the party to the beat of live DJs. Finally, the Buddha-Bar Monte-Carlo enchants you with its dim lighting and musical atmosphere.

Chic & Chill… A legend in its own right, the Bar Américain has a casual, jazzy ambiance and a live music programme that is as appealing as its incredible signature cocktails.

## **Night clubs in Monaco**

### From lounge bars to night clubs and cosy terraces, get to know the nightlife in Monaco!

In Monaco, the evenings are full of sparkle and the nights are magical! When it comes to bars, lounges and night clubs in Monaco, whether indoors or out on the terrace, by the sea or under the stars, there’s always something hip and fun to do after work. Sip a delicious cocktail, listen to live music, or dance to the best DJs. Liive it up in Monte Carlo’s night clubs and make memories that will last a lifetime!

### It’s always party time in the bars in Monaco

For festive evenings after work, live music, DJs, clubbing and dancing all night long, La Rascasse night club in Monaco never passes up an opportunity to have a good time! Another unmissable stop in the amazing nightlife in Monaco is the legendary Jimmy’z Monte-Carlo and its electro-chic score by Bob Sinclar, Martin Solveig, Mark Ronson, Fatboy Slim and other renowned DJs.

### Monte Carlo night clubs and their inimitable style

With its terrace overlooking the sea, cosy atmosphere and billiard table, the ultra-hip Blue Gin bar in Monaco offers cocktails, champagnes, whiskies and liqueurs that you can sip while listening to talented DJs on the decks. Also very worth checking out is the finger food menu by Marcel Ravin. At super-exclusive La Vigie, signature cocktails, champagne and rum are lovingly prepared through the day. Here’s an idea: try a cigar or a hookah and spice up the party to the beat of live DJs. Finally, the Buddha-Bar Monte-Carlo enchants you with its moody lighting and musical atmosphere. New among night clubs in Monaco is the Amazonico Monte-Carlo, one of the most innovative places set to open in 2023.

### Bars in Monaco for a chilled night out

Chic and chill night clubs in Monaco aren’t hard to find… A legend in its own right, the Bar Américain Monte Carlo night club has a casual, jazzy ambiance and a live music programme that is as appealing as its incredible signature cocktails. And of course there’s the Crystal Bar with its splendid terrace: the perfect place to enjoy a grand cru or smoke a fine cigar.
