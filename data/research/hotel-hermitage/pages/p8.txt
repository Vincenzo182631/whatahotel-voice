## **Our 4- and 5-star hotels in Monaco satisfy all your needs and desires**

### Luxury hotels in Monaco certainly know how to please

Discover the understated, luxurious charm of the Hôtel Hermitage Monte-Carlo, the Riviera spirit of the Monte-Carlo Beach and recreation at the Monte-Carlo Bay Hotel & Resort. Each of these hotels has its own philosophy, and as many unforgettable ways for guests to experience Monaco. You are so spoilt for choice, the only tricky bit will be choosing your hotel in Monaco.

### Look no further for the best hotels in Monaco…

**_The_** **_Hôtel_** **_de Paris Monte-Carlo…_** This iconic palatial hotel is the perfect destination if you're looking for a luxury hotel in Monaco. With new and exceptional suites, Michelin-starred restaurants, a jeweller's courtyard, exclusive access to the Thermes Marins Monte-Carlo, Casino de Monte-Carlo and the haute-couture boutiques of One Monte-Carlo, the luxury hotel’s transformation is bringing a whole host of new sensations to life, in a location right in the heart of Monaco **_._**

### From great spas to top restaurants, Monte-Carlo hotels have it all

**_The_** **_Hôtel_** **_Hermitage Monte-Carlo…_** At this haven for cocooning and wellness, you can experience Monaco at your own pace and take away unforgettable memories. Located at the centre of Monaco, the hotel exudes elegance and charm. With the Michelin-starred restaurant, Yannick Alléno à l'Hôtel Hermitage Monte-Carlo, and the amazing Thermes Marins Monte-Carlo spa, it is the ultimate hotel in Monaco for an intimate stay that takes luxury to a whole new level.

### Luxury hotels in Monaco are an ideal starting point for your Riviera adventure

**_The Monte-Carlo Beach…_** Connect with nature and enjoy special moments in this villa overlooking the sea, nestled between a pine wood and the Mediterranean. Decorated in warm hues and inspired by the Riviera of the roaring twenties, this deluxe hotel in Monaco has been redesigned by architect India Mahdavi. It has amazing services, a private beach, an Olympic-sized pool and Elsa, a 100% organic and wild-caught fish certified gastronomic restaurant. Guests are invited to focus on wellbeing and harmony too, with the Monte-Carlo Beach spa offering treatments, massages, yoga, meditation classes and more. It's a space specially set aside for taking time to breathe.

### Monte-Carlo hotels with sun, sand – and a casino!

**_The Monte-Carlo Bay Hotel & Resort…_** With its sandy lagoon and lush green garden, Cinq Mondes spa dedicated to well-being, doubly Michelin starred Blue Bay restaurant and the party atmosphere of its casino, this hotel in Monaco is all about having fun and enjoying yourself in a relaxed setting. As the seasons change, the Monte-Carlo Bay Hotel & Resort is forever coming up with new ways to surprise and delight guests, from brunches to parties during the long summer evenings, the Sea Bay area and much more... No matter the time of year, excitement is everything here!
