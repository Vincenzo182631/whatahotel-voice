**The connoisseurs' address in Monaco**

A chic address for connoisseurs, the Hôtel Hermitage Monte-Carlo offers a haven of tranquility just steps from the vibrant heart of Monaco. In an intimate and warm atmosphere, its attentive service cultivates a discreet and authentic elegance.

Overlooking the sea and the Rock, its Belle Époque architecture and its Eiffel dome, the signature feature of the Winter Garden, compose an exceptional setting. Proud of its history and resolutely open to its era, the Hôtel Hermitage Monte-Carlo maintains a living dialogue with the arts and culture.

Gastronomy holds a place of honor with two of Yannick Alléno's tables: Pavyllon Monte-Carlo, a starred restaurant, and L'Abysse Monte-Carlo, a twice-starred Japanese restaurant. In elegant, intimate settings, these two addresses celebrate precision of gesture, richness of flavor, and a demand for detail.

With Gustave, the bar of the Hôtel Hermitage Monte-Carlo, the hotel extends its art of hospitality. Conceived by interior architect Shirine Zirak as a place for conversation and conviviality, in the spirit of a contemporary salon, it invites guests to savor creative cocktails in an elegant, hushed atmosphere.

The experience continues at the Thermes Marins Monte-Carlo, a true haven dedicated to beauty, relaxation, and well-being, to which hotel guests enjoy privileged access.
