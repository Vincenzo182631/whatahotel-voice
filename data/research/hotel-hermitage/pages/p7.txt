From page to page, **Monaco’s online travel magazine** takes you on an excursion through the Monegasque territory to discover its iconic landmarks, well-known establishments, and secret gems off the beaten path.

Whether you want to indulge in a culinary masterpiece, stay in absolute comfort, have fun, or relax, our **lifestyle publication** is your travel guide to Monaco, brimming with **trip ideas and places to stay**. It reveals what’s new from season to season, like the opening of a prestigious suite or luxurious spa, as well as key dates for the diary, so you will never miss out on a major event.

**Inspiration Magazine** features portraits and interviews with top chefs, famous stars, and professionals working at our Resort. These talented men and women share their impressions of this exceptional country and the secrets of “their” Monaco.

### **Things to See and Do in Monaco: Our Magazine Gives You the Lowdown!**

Make sure **the best sights in the Principality of Monaco** are on your itinerary by following our **online magazine’s** travel tips. From the breathtaking architecture of Monte-Carlo, the quaint narrow streets of the Old Town, and the lavish apartments of the Palais Princier to Le Rocher, Port Hercules, and the Jardin Exotique, **Inspiration** is your guide to **what to see and do in Monaco**, showing you everything the city-state has to offer. Enjoy an excursion to the Musée Océanographique, stroll through the Jardin Japonais, take in an exhibition at the Grimaldi Forum, or treat yourself during a shopping trip to the Carré d’Or district: there is an endless array of things to enjoy **in Monaco**!

**Monaco also offers a wealth of activities** for a memorable stay without a dull moment. Fancy a **wellness break on the Mediterranean coast**? An unbeatable range of treatments and massages await at the Thermes Marins Monte-Carlo and the Spa Cinq Mondes. Discover recommendations for the most enchanting beaches for watersports or a day at the seaside, the best places to go to work out, and ideas for family-friendly activities.

Throughout the year, our magazine also features the **Principality’s major events**, such as the Rolex Monte-Carlo Masters, Monaco Yacht Show, Monte-Carlo Sporting Summer Festival, the Monaco E-Prix and, last but not least, the legendary Grand Prix de Monaco. The Principality’s most popular event transforms the city into a Formula 1 track and brings together thousands of fans and stars to admire the race cars. **Inspiration** gives you a glimpse into **the Grand Prix** by revealing the most exclusive viewpoints for watching the race, the best bars for wild parties, and the choice offers for staying in Monaco during this unmissable event.

**Inspiration Magazine** leaves no stone unturned when it comes to **luxury living in Monaco**!
