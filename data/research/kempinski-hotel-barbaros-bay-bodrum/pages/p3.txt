### Location

Distance is a state of mind...

Kempinski Hotel Barbaros Bay is far enough away for solitude and close enough that you can have just as much civilization as you want. Kempinski Hotel Barbaros Bay welcomes sophisticated travelers to the most untouched part of the Bodrum peninsula, sprawling on 62.000 square meters of picturesque land on the shores of Barbaros Bay.

### **By Car: From Milas-Bodrum Airport (BJV)**

Duration: 45 min – 43 km

Exit the airport and continue for 1.7 km.

Keep right and continue towards Bodrum by taking the Exit to D330.

Stay on D330 for 29 km until you reach the Torba crossroads. Keep right and exit D330, following the sign towards Yali and turning left.

Continue for 12 km until you see the sign for Kempinski Hotel Barbaros Bay.
