# Selman Marrakech

## Your luxury dream

At the foot of the Atlas Mountains in Marrakech stands a palace of singular charm, a testament to the splendor of Moorish palaces and the epitome of Arab elegance.

A vibrant homage to the purebred Arabian horse, the finest of all horse breeds, Selman Marrakech was envisioned by its founders, deeply rooted in Moroccan culture, and brought to life by master artisans.

Embodying a vision unlike any other, this Arab-Moorish inspired palace, where the horse reigns supreme, proudly bears the signature of the renowned French architect and designer Jacques Garcia, the only one of its kind in Morocco.

Beyond its breathtaking beauty, Selman Marrakech promises an unforgettable experience, combining exceptional comfort, refined services, and unparalleled hospitality. It captures the true art of living, steeped in Morocco's rich heritage and legendary tradition of warmth and generosity.

This haven of peace and luxury is further elevated by the exclusive Chenot Spa. A sanctuary of wellness where the Chenot method and its range of treatments offer a new dimension of well-being, serenity, and balance to both mind and body.
