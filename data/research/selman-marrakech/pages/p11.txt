# SABO by Jean-François Piège

##    Jean-François Piège's new table at Selman Marrakech

In a Moroccan Belle Époque atmosphere designed by Jacques Garcia, this evening restaurant transports you into a joyful gastronomic universe with a spectacular scenography.

SABO invites you to discover a cuisine that celebrates the French savoir-faire and the exceptional Moroccan produce.

Each culinary creation reflects the bold vision of Michelin-starred chef Jean-François Piège.

Nestled beneath an open-roof Patio that reveals the starry sky, the restaurant also extends into the palace's library-bar for a more intimate setting. Every detail has been meticulously thought out, from the Ginori porcelain to the antique silverware specially sourced for the venue.

Let yourself be tempted by the joyful flavors of this new culinary destination.
