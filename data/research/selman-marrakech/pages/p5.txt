# SABO by Jean-François Piège

## The new fine dining and festive restaurant at Selman Marrakech

Selman Marrakech and Jean-François Piège continue their collaboration with the launch of SABO - a refined, festive dinner restaurant that blends French culinary expertise with a Moroccan Belle Époque atmosphere, created by Jaques Garcia.

SABO, a table with a spectacular scenography, where refinement is in every detail, is an invitation to a joyful gastronomic experience, imbued with the Belle Époque’s codes. A name chosen as a nod to horses' hooves, this subtle link between the table and the stables extends the soul of Selman Marrakech, where every detail - from decor to menu - narrates a story.

Though integrated into Selman Marrakech, SABO stands as a restaurant in its own right - where fine dining meets celebration.
