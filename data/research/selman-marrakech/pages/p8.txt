# Le Bar Selman

## A timeless oasis

The vibrant heart of the hotel, Le Bar Selman, presents itself as a timeless oasis, imbued with the refined artistry of Jacques Garcia.

The meticulously curated drinks menu offers a sophisticated selection of cocktails, wines, and spirits, featuring signature creations and reimagined classics. For cigar enthusiasts, a choice of the finest brands enhances these moments of relaxation. In this enchanting setting, let yourself be carried away by the magic of Selman Marrakech, where every moment transforms into a precious memory.
