# Le Pavillon Restaurant

## Marrakech finest brunch

Overlooking the verdant expanses of the stud farm and its paddocks, Le Pavillon invites you to an open-air gastronomic experience, nestled in the heart of the hotel's sumptuous Spring Garden.

Every Sunday, Le Pavillon orchestrates one of the most exquisite brunches in the Ochre City, featuring a sumptuous and varied buffet. To crown this sensory experience, let yourself be captivated by the spellbinding chords of a Cuban duo, while enjoying a horse parade featuring the hotel's prestigious Arabian Horses. A unique experience that transforms the culinary moment into an unforgettable experience.
