### BLICK - An Experience from Sunrise until Sunset

We create unique experiences, an orchestra for all senses: BLICK is the fusion of day and night. Find your favorite destination while you are on the 8th floor at The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie, high above the rooftops of the metropolis at Elbe river. The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie is located directly in the iconic Elbphilharmonie with a breathtaking view of the industrial harbor, the Elbe panorama and the vibrant harbor city. The ensemble of the tradition of the old warehouse "Kaispeicher" and the modern architecture of the Elbphilharmonie allows you to enjoy an extraordinary ambience.

## Explore the Diverse Fang & Feld Cuisine

Experience a culinary journey with family and friends. Fang & Feld Cuisine offers a wide variety of dishes that delight both the palate and the heart. Perfect for cozy gatherings and festive occasions, each meal is crafted from locally sourced ingredients to ensure the highest quality and freshness. Discover the many flavors and find new favorites with every bite. The dedicated chefs create dishes that not only taste incredible but also bring people together. Whether celebrating a special event or simply enjoying a meal with loved ones, this cuisine provides the perfect setting for memorable moments. With a commitment to sustainability and supporting local producers, the food served is both delicious and responsible. Enjoy the comfort of knowing that every dish is made with care and passion, reflecting a love for good food and great company.

Learn More

## Experience Our Cake Art

Behind the historic walls of the Kaispeicher lies The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie, home to our in-house Pâtisserie. In our seventh floor, a talented team crafts delicious pastries, petit fours, cookies, and chocolates. Led by our head pastry chef, we offer a seasonal selection of pastries and cater to special requests as well. From appletonka bean tart strips to cranberry-nougat tartlets, our guests indulge in a variety of sweet delights.

## Cake & Champagne - Treat Yourself to Something Special

Experience a cake & champagne day with a stunning view over Hamburg. Our sweet creations from the in-house Pâtisserie will impress you not only with their attractive appearance but also with their unique flavors. To fully enjoy this delightful experience, we recommend pairing it with a glass of fine champagne. The view over the harbor in our Blick-Bar completes the experience perfectly. Our BLICK Bar, located on the 8th floor of The Westin Hamburg, is a place where people from all over the world come together. Here, you can savor life while taking in the unique view of the Elbe and the HafenCity district.

The Westin Eat Well Menu for Kids

Since eating well isn’t just for adults, Westin has teamed up with the experts at SuperChefs™ to offer the Westin Eat Well Menu for Kids. Comprised of doctors, dentists, chefs, dieticians and other experts, SuperChefs is dedicated to educating kids on the importance—and the fun—of eating smart. Each of our special menu offerings is as delicious as it is nutritious, making kids and parents happy.

The Westin Sleep Well Menu

Whether you’re adjusting to a new time zone or recovering from your travels, restful sleep is the key to rejuvenation. Eating the right foods can have a dramatic effect on the quality of your sleep, easing you into a night of sound sleep and ultimately boosting your well-being. The items on the Westin Sleep Well Menu are packed with amino acids, vitamins and minerals that promote rest and recovery. Eat smart and sleep well with our curated selection of sleep-enhancing superfoods, available for in-room dining.

Westin Fresh by The Juicery

Fuel your day and enhance your wellness with Westin Fresh by The Juicery, an assortment of nourishing, healthy juices and smoothies. In keeping with our commitment to well-being, Westin offers a selection of wholesome juices and smoothies to fuel your day and enhance your wellness. Curated in partnership with the experts at The Juicery, each Westin Fresh blend packs a punch of revitalizing goodness.
