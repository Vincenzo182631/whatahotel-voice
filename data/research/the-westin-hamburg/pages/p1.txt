# Welcome to The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie

## The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie – Welcome to the top

The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie sits atop the iconic Elbphilharmonie directly above the River Elbe and offers 244 rooms and suites with floor-to-ceiling windows framing sweeping port and city views....See More Our hotel in Hamburg’s HafenCity pairs distinctive design with signature Westin services and amenities including the Heavenly® Bed. As one of the pet-friendly hotels in Hamburg, we welcome trained pets for EUR 40 per night. Enjoy international and local cuisine throughout the hotel including Northern German dishes at Fang & Feld and cocktails, coffee and champagne at BLICK on the eighth floor with panoramic views. As a Hamburg spa hotel, our 1,300-square-meter Heavenly Spa features a Westin WORKOUT® studio, a 20-meter indoor pool, therapy rooms, saunas and a Tea Lounge overlooking the Elbe. Guests also praise the peaceful atmosphere and convenient access to local attractions.See Less

### Staying at the Landmark

Rising like a wave above Hamburg’s harbor, the Elbphilharmonie is more than a concert hall - it’s a symbol, a statement, a place where architecture, culture and emotion come together in one unforgettable silhouette. Built on a historic warehouse and crowned by glass reflecting sky and sea, it doesn’t just shape the skyline, it defines it. And at the very top: your stay. For concert nights, special...See More occasions or simply because you can.See Less

## Urban Retreat

Visit Hamburg for the architecture, the music, the energy. Explore the city from the water on a boat, walk through the Speicherstadt, or step straight into the Elbphilharmonie for a guided tour or an unforgettable evening.​

And when the day winds down, come back up. Above the city, it’s quieter, calmer - a place to recharge, to slow down and gather new energy for whatever comes next.

Discover Hamburg

## Distinctly Elevated

Rooms and suites designed for special experiences - Instead of ordinary windows, entire glass façades open the space to the outside, with the Elbphilharmonie’s chromatic spots catching the light like an ever‑changing artwork. It creates that rare feeling of being above the city, not just in it.​

Generous layouts give you room to arrive, while the Heavenly® Bed, calm atmosphere and thoughtful details set the tone from the first moment - And whenever you feel like staying in, 24‑hour room service makes it easy to keep the moment going, uninterrupted.​

## Elevated Experience

The Elbphilharmonie is not only a masterpiece of modern architecture, but also a cultural landmark of historical value. With its undulating glass facade and breathtaking views of the Elbe River and the city of Hamburg, the Elbphilharmonie is a place that fascinates music and culture lovers as well as architecture enthusiasts. Inside it are not only two of the best concert halls in the world, but also our hotel. Combine your concert visit with an unforgettable stay with us and make the special evening as comfortable as never before. With only one step through our revolving door you are directly at the entrance of the concert hall. After the amazing concert experience you can retreat directly to your hotel room and relive the impressions of the evening in our elegant ambience.

Green Key Award 2024

Green Key is a leading standard for environmental responsibility and sustainability in the tourism industry. This certification indicates that establishments meet strict criteria set by the Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE). Guests choosing Green Key-certified venues actively support environmental sustainability. The certification process includes rigorous documentation, regular audits, and ongoing staff training to uphold high standards.

TOP Training Company DEHOGA

The "TOP Training Company" initiative by DEHOGA offers a nationwide certification for high training standards in hospitality and gastronomy. Certified companies commit to 12 guiding principles that ensure quality education and provide a competitive edge. Key elements include proper onboarding, trainee support, and a respectful work environment. Involving trainees in the certification process enhances credibility.

Good To Know

**Is an Early Check-In Possible?**

Yes, on site at check-in if the room is already available, but this can never be guaranteed in advance due to the booking situation.

**Where Is the Front Desk for the Check-In at the Westin Hamburg?**

The front desk for the check-in is located on the 8th floor of The Westin Hamburg.

**What Are the Parking Conditions at the Westin Hamburg and Which Entrance Do I Have To Use?**

There is an underground parking garage from an external company. As a hotel guest you benefit from a discount rate of 35€ per 24 hours. The second approach from the right - entrance 3 - leads to the hotel.

**Where Is the Breakfast Buffet at the Westin Hamburg and How Much Does It Cost?**

The breakfast buffet at The Westin Hamburg is on the 7th floor in the Fang & Feld restaurant.

34 € per person for 13 years and older

17 € per person for children between 6 and 12 years old

free of charge for children between 0 and 6 years old

**Where Is the Spa Area at the Westin Hamburg Located and Is the Use of the Spa Included?**

The Heavenly Spa at The Westin Hamburg is located on the 6th floor and is included for hotel guests.

**How To Get From the Hotel Lobby of the Westin Hamburg to the Elbphilharmonie Plaza and the Concert Halls?**

Through the revolving door on the 8th floor in the hotel lobby next to the reception with the key card of the room.

**Does the Westin Hamburg Offer Packages With Concert Tickets to the Elbphilharmonie?**

No, but the concierge team at The Westin Hamburg will be happy to assist you in booking concert tickets for the Elbphilharmonie.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie?

The check-in time at The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €40.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie. Self parking is available at The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie, with rates of €8.00 per hour, €35.00 per day, and €245.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie?

The closest airport near The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie is Hamburg Airport (HAM). HAM is located approximately 13.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
