# Sleep Well in Elbphilharmonie Suites

## Accommodations

Enjoy an unforgettable stay at The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie. Our Hamburg accommodation includes spacious, individually designed hotel rooms and suites with fantastic views, natural light and an airy ambience. Through the curved glass facade of our unique hotel, take in the urban panorama of Germany's most famous port city. Suites provide added comfort and elegance for a memorable visit, while the signature Heavenly® Bed, Heavenly® shower and breakfast set you up for the day. Guests often praise the generous room sizes, floor-to-ceiling windows, and peaceful atmosphere, and from select rooms in the Elbphilharmonie you can even watch the Queen Mary II enter port.

### Our Suites and Rooms

Discover our different room and suite categories and get inspired by the puristic interior that is inspired by water, sand and wind. The calming earth tones and the high-quality furniture let you feel comfortable. Dive into the most beautiful views in Hamburg and book your stay with us.

## Duo Grandioso

Our hotel is directly located in the Elbphilharmonie. Enjoy your stay at The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie and visit the iconic concert hall for an unforgettable concert by just going through a revolving door on the 8th floor of the hotel. The outstanding architecture of the iconic building is unique and offers you a once in a lifetime experience. As you make your way around the building that was designed by the Swiss architects Herzog & de Meuron, you will find there are amazing views to discover as the building was built in the middle of the Elbe River. Discover the new district "Hafencity" and get inspired by music, nature, waves and sand. Before you enjoy the concerts that await you, we recommend enjoying a glass of champagne and a delicious Hanseatic crab brioche in our BLICK Bar or Champagne Bar.

A view with a room

### Rooms With a View to the Atrium

Our Classic Rooms offer a view to the conical atrium and the impressive façade through floor-to-ceiling windows. Kindly note that Classic rooms do not offer a view of Hamburg or the harbor as these are located inside the Elbphilharmonie.

### Rooms With a View to the Harbor City

Enjoy the breathtaking view of the modern Hafencity through the floor-to-ceiling glass facades in our deluxe rooms as well as the premium city rooms, which are equal to deluxe rooms but on higher floors. Most of the Elbphilharmonie Studios, Family Suites, Junior Horizon & Horizon Suites, Maisonette Suites & the Presidential Suite offer direct or partial Harbor City views, depending on the location.

### Rooms With a View to the Harbor / Elbe River

Watch small boats and large cruise ships passing by on the Elbe from our Premium Waterfront rooms as well as a few Elbphilharmonie Studios, one of our Maisonette Suites and one Panorama Suite. The Junior Horizon Suites, Horizon Suites and the Presidential Suite are offering partial harbor view.

### Rooms With a View to the City of Hamburg

A few of our rooms are located with a direct view of the city of Hamburg. Guests will find the Dancing Towers, the city center and St. Michaelis church and the iconic Hamburg Harbor from the other side. Some of our Premium City rooms, one of our Panomara Suites and one of our Maisonette Suite have this view.

Family & Children

### We Are Family Friendly

Welcome to The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie, where family adventures await! Our family-friendly program is designed to create unforgettable memories for you and your children. Enjoy spacious family rooms and suites equipped with baby cribs and children’s bathrobes available upon request. For our youngest guests, a fun tipi for kids under six is also available. Dining is a delight with our Kids Eat Well menus, and each child receives a complimentary reusable Westin water bottle for hydration. Dive into family fun during exclusive pool times featuring water toys. Explore nearby attractions like the Elbphilharmonie, Miniatur Wunderland, and thrilling harbor cruises. Don’t miss Hagenbeck Zoo or indoor adventures at the Hamburg Dungeon. With The Westin Family Program, every moment is crafted to ensure your family leaves with cherished memories of your Hamburg adventure.
