## Celebrate the Festive Season at the Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie

Celebrate Christmas and welcome the new year at Fang & Feld, BLICK and BLICK Bar, with exceptional dining, drinks and panoramic views across Hamburg.

Festive Moments at The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie

### A Festive Christmas Eve at Fang & Feld

Celebrate Christmas Eve with a festive five-course dinner at Fang & Feld. Enjoy an elegant evening of seasonal cuisine in the unique setting of The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie.

### New Year's Eve at BLICK - Fully Booked

Welcome 2027 at BLICK with panoramic views across Hamburg and the harbour. From 20:00, guests can enjoy live music, a festive buffet and a glass of Champagne on arrival. The Plaza will be exclusively reserved for New Year's Eve guests.

## Winter’s Magic in Hamburg

As winter settles over Hamburg, the city reveals its most magical side. Twinkling lights, festive scents, and cozy moments invite you to press pause and embrace the season. From charming market stalls to sparkling waterfronts, Hamburg offers a unique blend of tradition and modern flair. Whether you're strolling through illuminated streets, enjoying seasonal flavors, or simply soaking in the atmosphere, every moment feels like something special. At The Westin Hamburg Elbphilharmonie, you're perfectly placed to experience it all. Discover a city that inspires, delights, and welcomes you to be your best version – this winter, you have arrived.
