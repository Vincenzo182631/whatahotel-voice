## Accommodations

Inspired by the natural beauty and energy of the surrounding area, our 516 hotel rooms in Orlando, Florida, invite guests to take in the views of Bonnet Creek Preserve and surrounding Disney parks. Floor-to-ceiling windows allow you to bask in the warm Florida sunlight, overlook our two sparkling pools, and even enjoy your personal glimpse of Epcot's nightly fireworks display. ## Welcoming and Accessible

The well-being of all our guests is always top of mind at the JW Marriott Orlando Bonnet Creek Reserve & Spa. Many of our suites and hotel rooms near Disney World Orlando​ are designed to welcome guests with mobility or hearing issues. Guest room accommodations include roll-in showers, visual alarms, and visual notification systems for doors and phones.
