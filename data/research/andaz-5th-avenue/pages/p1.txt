# Experience 5th Ave from our Luxury Midtown Manhattan Hotel near Bryant Park

Settle into our luxury Midtown Manhattan hotel on 5th Avenue located near the iconic New York Public Library and prepare to live like a local New Yorker. Connect with the city art scene through our Bryant Park hotel’s collection of NYC-based artists’ work and exchange inspiring ideas during our complimentary wine hour.

## Rooms & Suites

Designed by the celebrated Tony Chi, our spacious Bryant Park hotel rooms feel like luxury New York City lofts, with 12-foot ceilings and plenty of natural light. Check into the Wellness Suite for an incredible view of the Empire State Building. For additional space choose a two bedroom suite with a balcony terrace perched above Midtown Manhattan.

## Pets Are Welcome

We welcome your traveling canine companions to our pet-friendly hotel in Midtown Manhattan. To participate in our Pet Program, inform us that you’ll have a pet (or pets) staying with you at least three days in advance of your arrival. There is a one-time, nonrefundable pet fee of $100 for up to six nights. The Pet Program includes a doggie bed and two bowls. Enjoy pet designated rooms and easy access to city parks.

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
