THE CONCEPT

La dolce vita

Embrace the sweetness of life in a city where friends, family, and togetherness come first. Linger over life’s simplest pleasures: a plate of perfectly crafted Italian pasta, vibrant seasonal antipasti, or richly satisfying mains, all enjoyed against the backdrop of an unforgettable view.

THE VIEW

A beautiful history

From our wonderful location on the Pincian Hill, all of Rome awaits your gaze. Your views stretch from the majesty of St Peter's to the stately Borghese gardens – but it's so much more than a roll-call of history. This is Rome, full of life.

AN ITALIAN EXPERIENCE

A feast for all the senses

In Italy, good food is life itself; a joyful ritual where every meal brings people together. It’s about the  flavors, sharing stories, and creating memories that linger long after the last bite.

Sunday lunch

Taste Society

Long Sunday lunches are back, with a delicious new twist. Our series ‘Taste Society’ sees a new guest chef collaborate with Salvatore Bianco every month to create an exclusive menu. Joining us in the kitchen one Sunday of the month, each cook celebrates their menu's debut with a special launch event.
