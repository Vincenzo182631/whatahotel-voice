# La Terrazza

La Terrazza is the jewel in Hotel Eden’s crown. Located on the top floor, it offers guests mesmerising views over Rome alongside a remarkable Michelin-starred dining experience. Enjoy dishes rooted in local terroir and Mediterranean tradition, all given a contemporary twist with innovative global influences.
