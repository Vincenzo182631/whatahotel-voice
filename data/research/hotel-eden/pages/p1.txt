Where is Hotel Eden located?

Hotel Eden is at Via Ludovisi 49, 00187 Rome, perfectly positioned in the heart of the city.

What landmarks are near the hotel?

Our luxury hotel is near the legendary Spanish Steps and Trevi Fountain (5 minutes' walk) and the picturesque Villa Borghese with the Borghese Gallery.

How far is the hotel from the Vatican?

The Vatican is around a 30-minute drive away.

How do I contact the reservations office?

You can reach our reservations office on +39 06 8938 6470.

Is there a minimum size for a group booking?

Group booking terms apply from six or more rooms.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

Yes, we welcome up to two small dogs (under 7 kg/15 lbs) per room, at €35 per day to cover deep cleaning. We'll provide a bowl, bed and treats.

What is the parking fee?

Hotel Eden does not have an on-site garage or parking lot but offers valet parking in a private garage nearby. Rates apply (€65 - €95 per day depending on vehicle size), and this service is subject to availability. Street parking and public garages are available in the surrounding area.

What facilities does the hotel offer?

We have a beautiful spa and a 24-hour fitness room. Our luxury hotel in Rome also has a rooftop restaurant and bar, a rooftop Michelin-starred restaurant and a lobby lounge serving light bites and drinks.

Is the hotel close to the Spanish Steps?

Yes, the legendary Spanish Steps are just a 5-minute walk away.

What makes Hotel Eden distinctive?

First opened in 1889, Hotel Eden is infused with authentic Roman spirit and alive with the stories of a legendary city.

Is the hotel well placed for exploring Rome?

Absolutely. We're in the heart of Rome, a short stroll from the Spanish Steps, Trevi Fountain and Villa Borghese.

Is your hotel smoke-free? Are there designated smoking areas available?

No smoking is permitted inside hotel areas. Smoking is allowed in the open-air space at Il Giardino Ristorante.

Do you offer airport shuttle services?

At Hotel Eden, we offer private airport transfer services from Fiumicino and Ciampino airports upon request, arranged by our concierge and subject to an additional charge. Taxis are readily available at the hotel, and paid valet parking is offered on site.

What are the Wi-Fi details at the hotel?

Free Wi-Fi at 50Mbps is available for all guests of the hotel and the restaurants and bars.

Is there a pool?

Hotel Eden does not have a pool.

Are there meeting or event rooms?

Hotel Eden offers two fully equipped meetings and event rooms.

What is the main international airport, and how long does the transfer take?

Rome's main international airport is Leonardo da Vinci–Fiumicino Airport (FCO). Transfer services to Hotel Eden usually take about 45-50 minutes.
