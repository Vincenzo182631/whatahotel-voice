# Il Giardino Bar

Welcome to Il Giardino Bar, where Rome unfolds at your feet and every night feels electric in possibility. Here, the city’s finest crowd gathers for bold, mixologist-crafted cocktails, a fresh, flavourful bar menu, and the best views in town. More than just a bar, Il Giardino is a rooftop scene pulsing with energy, style, and the undeniable allure of Rome after dark.

THE COCKTAILS

All of Rome in a glass

Let us serve you a sensory journey with our expertly crafted cocktails, created to celebrate the beauty of Rome. Try the rose-hued La Grande Bellezza, which captures the warmth of a Roman sunset, or savour any other of our delightful creations.

DESIGN

Inspired by nature

Designed around the idea of bringing the outside in, our al fresco feel is brought to life by a ‘vertical garden’ wall. Crafted from hundreds of handmade metal leaves, the result is a backdrop beautiful enough to compete with the glorious view.

THE VIEW

Drinks with a view

From our wonderful location on the Pincian Hill, all of Rome awaits your gaze. Your views stretch from the majesty of St Peter's to the stately Borghese gardens – but this is so much more than a roll-call of history. This is Rome, full of life.

APERITIVO

Let the evening begin

Aperitivo is a glorious couple of hours in which to relax, Italian style, over a glass of something and a selection of cicchetti. It’s also a good way to start building an appetite for dinner at one of our restaurants.
