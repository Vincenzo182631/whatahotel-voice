What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is at 12pm (noon).

Can I arrange early check-in or late check-out?

Early check-in can be guaranteed up to one hour before, and late check-out up to two hours after standard times. Late check-out (until 4pm) is chargeable at 50% of the daily rate: after 4pm, a full night's rate will apply.

What if I need an even earlier arrival or later departure?

Check-outs after 4pm will be charged an additional night's stay. Please note that late check-outs are subject to availability, and it's unlikely that we will be able to accommodate them during major events or for high-demand units. If this is something you require, we suggest including an extra night in your stay on booking.

How much does an extra bed cost?

An extra bed is complimentary and can be accommodated in room categories from Junior Suites upwards.

How do I make a reservation?

To book our luxury hotel rooms in central Rome, call our reservations office on +39 06 8938 6470 or email reservations.HER@dorchestercollection.com

How many rooms qualify as a group booking?

Bookings of six or more rooms are treated as group reservations.

Can my dog stay in the room?

Yes, we welcome up to two small dogs (under 7 kg/15 lbs) per room, at €35 per day. We'll provide a bowl, bed and treats.

Is breakfast included with my room?

Breakfast is charged separately; please check your specific rate for inclusions. Hotel Eden offers the following breakfast options:

Eden Breakfast for €45

Continental Breakfast for €35

Breakfast à la carte

Is there a charge for an extra bed for children?

No, we can offer a complimentary extra bed for guests of all ages. This is available for all suite categories, from Junior Suites and up.

What time do I need to check out by?

Check-out is at 12pm (noon). Late check-out (until 4pm) is chargeable at 50% of the daily rate, subject to conditions.

What size are the beds?

Whether you're in a luxurious room or one of Hotel Eden's glorious suites, our beds are sized generously for your comfort. Specific measurements are below.

King-size – 200 x 200cm (79 x 79”)

Queen – 160/180 x 200cm (63/71 x 79”)

Twin – 180/200 x 200cm (71/79 x 79”)

Do you offer accessible rooms?

Hotel Eden has four accessible rooms which include facilities such as lowered entry door viewers and wide doors. Guests can find emergency codes/buttons inside the rooms for immediate assistance. Bathrooms in these rooms are wheelchair accessible and feature facilities including a handheld showerhead, lowered sink, roll-in shower stall with bathroom seat, and grab handles near the toilet. The hotel also has a special alarm system for the hearing-impaired.
