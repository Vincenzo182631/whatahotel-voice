# Dining

## The Italian art of good taste

In Italy, gastronomy is pure artistry—a blend of flavour, pleasure, and lively conversation. Each meal is a moment to savour and share, where food brings people together to laugh, to deepen connections, and to simply celebrate life with every delicious bite.

What restaurants are available at Hotel Eden?

We have Il Giardino Ristorante, La Terrazza, and La Libreria.

Tell me about Il Giardino Ristorante.

Il Giardino Ristorante is our relaxed rooftop restaurant, soaring above Rome's Spanish Steps. It's open daily.

Tell me about La Terrazza.

La Terrazza, Hotel Eden, Rome, is our one-Michelin starred fine dining restaurant. It's closed for lunch, open for dinner Tuesday to Saturday.

What does La Libreria offer?

La Libreria is our best-kept secret: a spot for impactful morning meetings, aperitivo hour with friends, or a final shot of grappa to wrap up the night. It's open from 9am to 9pm.

What time is breakfast served and where?

Breakfast is served at Il Giardino Ristorante, from 7 to 10.30am.

How much does breakfast cost?

Hotel Eden offers the following breakfast options:

Eden Breakfast for €45

Continental Breakfast for €35

Breakfast à la carte

Which days is La Terrazza open?

La Terrazza serves dinner only and is closed on Sunday and Monday.

Is there a rooftop dining option?

Yes. La Terrazza, Il Giardino Ristorante and Il Giardino Bar are all located on our rooftop.

Where can I get light meals and drinks during the day?

La Libreria, in the heart of the hotel, and Il Giardino Bar, on our rooftop, both offer light bites and drinks. La Libreria is open from 9am to 9pm, and Il Giardino Bar is open from 7am in the morning to 0.30am at night.

Are there any dates when La Terrazza is closed?

Yes, La Terrazza has short seasonal closures in January and August each year. Please check the restaurant page for more details.

Is there a dress code for Il Giardino Ristorante?

Il Giardino Ristorante has a ‘smart casual’ dress code. We kindly ask that you refrain from wearing caps, ripped jeans, sportswear, shorts or flip-flops.

Is there a dress code for Il Giardino Bar?

Il Giardino Bar has a ‘smart casual’ dress code. We kindly ask that you refrain from wearing caps, ripped jeans, sportswear, shorts or flip-flops.

Is there a rooftop bar?

Yes. Il Giardino Bar, located on the sixth floor, is our rooftop bar with enchanting panoramic views over Rome.
