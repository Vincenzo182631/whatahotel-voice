What are the spa opening hours?

Our luxury hotel spa in Rome is open daily, from 10.30am to 7pm.

What are the fitness room hours?

The fitness room is open 24 hours a day.

What facilities does the spa offer?

The Eden Spa offers a serene wellness experience with three private spa suites, including a couples suite and a manicure and pedicure suite. Each spa suite offers its own steam room and chromotherapy shower. A wellness lounger is also available. Services include: massages, facials, body treatments, spa rituals, private steam baths, manicure and pedicure, wellness lounger.

Can I book spa treatments?

Yes, treatments at Hotel Eden's spa are available daily from 10.30am to 7pm; please contact us to arrange.

Is the fitness room suitable for an early or late workout?

Absolutely. The fitness room is open 24 hours, so you can work out any time.

Do I need to book spa treatments in advance?

We recommend booking ahead to secure your preferred time; please contact the hotel to arrange.

Is there a gym separate from the spa?

Yes, our fitness room is separate from the spa. It's open 24 hours a day.
