### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort?

The largest occupancy room at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort is the Premium Family Suite with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort?

Yes, all rooms at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have hair dryers.
