# Welcome to Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort

## Escape to our luxurious Bali hotel in Kuta

Legendary Kuta Beach, an array of hidden local gems and signature services await you at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort. Discover a beach lover’s paradise at our 5-star hotel in Kuta, Bali, which is adjacent...See More to the Beachwalk Mall’s 250 premium shops and cineplex. Soak up the Bali sun by taking a dip in our outdoor infinity pool or stay connected with computer workstations in our lobby. Enjoy afternoon tea with ocean views in &More by Sheraton or elevate your stay by enjoying uninterrupted views of Kuta’s golden sunsets right at Sundowner Rooftop. Savor casual gourmet market cuisine at Daily Social, also known as the best place for Sunday Social Brunch in Kuta, or sit down for delicious Italian dishes at Bene Italian Kitchen. ## Family Suite Room

Designed for comfort and connection, our Family Suite Room is the perfect choice for parents traveling with children. The suite features a spacious layout with a separate master bedroom, a cozy living area with a sofa bed, and extra space to relax and play. Little ones will love the in-room kids’ tent — ideal for imaginative adventures or a cozy nap. Whether it’s a short getaway or an extended family holiday, this suite offers the space and flexibility your family needs to feel right at home in paradise.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort?

The check-in time at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort?

The closest airport near Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort is I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS). DPS is located approximately 5.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have an airport shuttle to I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS)?

Yes, Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort has an airport shuttle to I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS).

Does Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Bali Kuta Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
