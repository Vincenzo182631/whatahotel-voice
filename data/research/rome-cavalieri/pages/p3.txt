# Dining & Drinks

Dining & Drinks

Discover regional Mediterranean and global flavors and taste the mastery of Michelin-starred La Pergola, our rooftop restaurant from renowned chef Heinz Beck. We are equipped to meet any special dietary requirements. Many of our dishes can be tailored to your needs, with special selections available for coeliac, diabetic, vegetarian, vegan and low-calorie diets.

## La Pergola

Under the direction of the celebrated Chef Heinz Beck, the famous menu is accentuated with a wine list that comes from a cellar with over 70,000 world-class bottles, a water list featuring 45 choices, authentic Italian oil and balsamic vinegar from the best local producers and the finest Mediterranean-sourced produce. Dine on innovative and exciting cuisine, surrounded by classic pieces of art.

## Uliveto

Adjacent to the outdoor pool, Uliveto offers Mediterranean and international dishes with regional specialties from across Italy. Start your day with a delicious breakfast and enjoy a vast range of delicacies at Sunday brunch. In summer, tables are laid on the terrace for an alfresco dining experience.

## Tiepolo Lounge & Terrace

From morning espresso to after-dinner nightcaps, this is the place to see and be seen – especially when there is live jazz to soothe you into the evening. Enjoy pastries and light bites by day followed by an exciting array of innovative cocktails and aperitivo.

## Pool Bar

Enjoy a refreshing drink or light meal by the water’s edge at the stylish Pool Bar. Open as long as the sun shines, the Pool Bar is a fun and relaxed place to socialize or swim.

## Room Service

Order from a full menu and room waiters will serve the meal in your room or suite with the same care and attention you would expect in our restaurants.
