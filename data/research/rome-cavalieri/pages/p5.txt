# Things to Do

Things to Do

Make your journey memorable with activities tailored to your pace. Relax at the hotel or fill your days with wonder among Rome’s storied sights.

## Pools

Glittering in the sun beside a stylish poolside bar, the main pool is the ideal place to swim a few slow laps as you enjoy the scenery around you. Deeper into the park, discover two family-friendly pools – one exclusively for small children. Beneath a beautiful glass dome, our indoor pool and whirlpool are perfect for a winter dip.

## Fitness

Stay fit with exercise options to suit your style. Our fully equipped fitness center features the latest Technogym™ cardio machines and aerobics equipment, with separate rooms for cardio and weight training.

### Fitness Classes

Make an appointment for a one-on-one session with a personal trainer, or join a group for Pilates, yoga, and classes ranging from low-impact to high-intensity.

### Fitness Trail

Walk, jog, or run along the 800-metre fitness trail through the hotel’s exquisite gardens. Enjoy the views of Rome framed by our Mediterranean pines while stopping off at the exercise stations along the way.

### Tennis

Two clay tennis courts meet Davis Cup standard and are floodlit for evening games. They are frequently used by international professional players during their stays in Rome.

Open daily from 7 am to 10 pm, weather permitting.

## Kids Club

Available in summer, the IT Club – League of Young Cavaliers is complimentary to all hotel guests aged 4-12. Fun and learning mix with activities for all ages, including arts and crafts, arcade games, and the Nutella Chocolate Bar, which will run throughout the summer. Please contact the concierge for details and reservations.
