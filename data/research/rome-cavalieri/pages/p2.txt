# Rooms & Suites

Rooms & Suites

Choose from rooms and suites fit for emperors, kings, and your Roman holiday. Greet the day from your private balcony overlooking the park, the city, or our sparkling pools.

## Penthouse Suite

A sliding glass ceiling opens onto a private rooftop garden, where you can admire views of St. Peter's Dome from the hot tub. A veritable mini-gallery, this suite features works by Andy Warhol and furniture designed by Karl Lagerfeld.

## Planetarium Suite

Stargaze from the comfort of your bed, beneath the Planetarium Suite's twinkling LED ceiling. Or head to the rooftop terrace for a hot tub soak under the night sky.

## Petronius Suite

Four large balconies, a private wine cellar, old masters and rare 18th-century furnishings highlight our largest accommodation. Named for the Roman Empire's arbiter of fine taste, the opulent Petronius Suite hosts up to eight guests in impeccable luxury.

## Napoleon Suite

Rare empire furniture, including a writing desk once owned by Napoleon, features in this elegant suite. Admire 19th-century artwork and survey the Eternal City's splendour from three balconies.
