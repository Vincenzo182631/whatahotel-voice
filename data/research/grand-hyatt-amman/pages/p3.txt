## 32º North

Beaded curtains and an impressive fiber-optic chandelier provide the ideal backdrop to one of the most unique dining experiences in Amman. 32º North (a nod to the restaurant’s latitude and longitude coordinates) boasts an interior that is contemporary and innovative, yet mindful of tradition. Through clever glass walls, diners can enjoy a view of the custom-made, free-standing kitchen, bringing a little sense of the theatrical full circle.

## The Lounge

From the novel designer furniture and lighting fixtures to two large open fireplaces, floor-to-ceiling windows in the conservatory and a spectacular view, The Lounge is a truly exceptional and unique experience. It has become renowned over the past two decades as a venue for successful business meetings, offering a chic space for guests and visitors to meet throughout the day for coffee, the finest selection of imported teas, specialty cocktails or a light bite.

## The Terrace

Embark on a voyage of discovery at The Terrace, Grand Hyatt Amman. Every drink, bar stool and sofa has a unique tale to tell, crafted by late nights and long conversations. Spacious and cozy in equal measure with soothing water features and a sleek cocktail bar, The Terrace is the ideal place to escape and unwind after a long day.
