### A little tired and let down by the main restaurant

The hotel is in a great location and is secure. The staff \[in the main\] are great and keen to help where they can.

The rooms are tired and in need of a refurb and minor things \[cracked tiles; chipped baths; curtains not on tracks\] add up to giver a substandard impression.

The gym is great with lots of different equipment all of which is in great condition and looked after. The spa too is really good with excellent staff keeping it clean and tidy.

The main restaurant has a limited menu and inconsistency in the quality of food. There are lots of staff, but you can be ignored and left waiting to place an order, or even to receive a bill. The opportunity to fill empty glasses is missed and missing cutlery is all too common.

There is an Italian restaurant too which is excellent. The food and the service are great and just what is expected. The service is attentive and quick; the food is really consistent and high quality. The hotel can get it right.
