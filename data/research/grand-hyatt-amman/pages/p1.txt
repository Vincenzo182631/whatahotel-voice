## An Iconic Grand Hyatt in the vibrant center of Amman

At the heart of the city, where modern and historic Amman meet, a top one of the city’s most prominent seven hills, Grand Hyatt Amman stands out with its distinctive carved pink stone façade, offering sweeping 360-degree views of the city.

## EXPLORE

Zara Fitness Center

Located on the 1st floor, our exclusive fitness center is open 24 hours a day with floor-to-ceiling windows allowing natural light to flood the space. For more details, please contact +962 6 465 1234.

The Lombard Amman

Discoveran exquisite dining experience right in the heart of Amman, serving Northern Italian cooking with a modern take on traditional recipes. Every dish is meticulously prepared to take you on an irresistible and flavorful gastronomic journey. At The Lombard, we bring Italy to you.

Indoor & Outdoor Swimming Pools

Unwind in a serene setting at our indoor and outdoor swimming pools, located on Level 1. Whether you are enjoying a refreshing swim, relaxing by the poolside, or rejuvenating in the steam rooms, sauna, and whirlpool, every moment is designed for complete relaxation and wellbeing.

**Indoor Pool Hours:**

- Adults (16+): 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- Kids: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (with adult supervision)

**Outdoor Pool Hours:**

- Adults (16+): 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- Kids: 10:00 AM – 5:30 PM (with adult supervision)

Grand Hyatt Residences

Located in the Hyatt Tower adjacent to the hotel, the Grand Hyatt Residences features serviced apartments, ranging from 75 to 260 square meters in size, that make you feel at home. These one- to four-bedroom units feature full kitchens and private balconies offering breathtaking views of Amman.For more information, please contact us at +962 6 465 1234 or AMMGH-sales@hyatt.com.

Zara Spa

Rebalance your physical and spiritual energy with a transforming body treatment or vigorous workout at Zara Spa on Level 1. Open daily from 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM.

### 32º North

Beaded curtains and an impressive fiber-optic chandelier provide the ideal backdrop to one of the most unique dining experiences in Amman. 32º North (a nod to the restaurant’s latitude and longitude coordinates) boasts an interior that is contemporary and innovative, yet mindful of tradition. Through clever glass walls, diners can enjoy a view of the custom-made, free-standing kitchen, bringing a little sense of the theatrical full circle.
