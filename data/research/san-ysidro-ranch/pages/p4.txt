## PAMPERED PET PROGRAM

From the moment you arrive on property with your beloved pet, The Pampered Pet Program offers a heartwarming welcome and renowned 5-star service. Pets may sign in alongside their owner in the Pampered Pets registration book and receive a welcome treat from the pet-friendly cookie tray on the front desk.

The program is offered specifically in our pet-friendly cottages and includes:

- Plush San Ysidro Ranch pet bedding and water bowl placed in cottage
- Personalized Welcome Amenity
- Pet-friendly in-room dining menu available

Discover More

San Ysidro Ranch is delighted to host you and your pet. To ensure a comfortable and enjoyable stay for everyone, we kindly ask that you acknowledge the following guidelines:

- Pets must be on leash while on property
- Please clean up after your pet
- Pets are not permitted in the hotel’s restaurants, bars, salon & spa, fitness center, or pool areas
- Please make every effort to prevent your pet from barking or causing noise disturbances
- Do not leave pets unattended. Cottages are not fenced in, and pets can easily escape - even through hedges
- A non-refundable pet and cleaning fee of $250 will be applied to your account

By joining us with your pet, you agree to take responsibility for any property damage or personal injury caused by your pet. You further agree to indemnify and hold harmless San Ysidro Ranch, its owners, and its operators from all liability and damages resulting from your pet’s actions. The hotel reserves the right to charge your account for the cost of any damage(s) including compensation given to other guests related to your pet.
