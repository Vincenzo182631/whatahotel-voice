## Frequently asked questions

### What unique dining experiences does Raffles Boston offer?

At Raffles Boston, dining includes Long Bar & Terrace for vibrant all-day dining and signature cocktails, The Blind Duck speakeasy for intimate, design-forward cocktails, and La Padrona offers MICHELIN-recommended Italian cuisine. Café Pastel serves coffee and pastries, ideal for quick grab-and-go moments. Guests also enjoy 24-hour in-room dining for elevated private comfort. Together, these experiences balance lively social energy with discreet luxury in the heart of Back Bay.

### How does the butler service enhance the guest experience?

At Raffles Boston, the signature Raffles Butler Service elevates every stay with intuitive, highly personalized attention from arrival to departure. Dedicated butlers anticipate guest needs, from unpacking and garment pressing to dining reservations, bespoke itineraries, and in-room preferences. Available around the clock, they provide discreet, seamless support that enhances privacy and comfort. This hallmark Raffles Butler Service ensures each guest enjoys a refined, effortless, and deeply tailored luxury experience throughout their stay.

### What amenities can guests expect at Raffles Boston?

At Raffles Boston, guests can expect world-class amenities including a full destination wellness experience at the Guerlain Spa, a 20-meter indoor lap pool, hot tub, and sauna. The hotel also offers state-of-the-art fitness facilities, elegant social lounges, and signature dining venues. Thoughtful Raffles Butler Service and 24-hour Private Dining ensure seamless comfort, creating a refined urban retreat high above Boston's Back Bay skyline.

### Where is Raffles Boston located?

Raffles Boston is conveniently situated at 40 Trinity Place, placing you in the heart of the city's vibrant culture and history.

### When is the best time to visit Raffles Boston?

Spring brings blooming gardens and mild temperatures, ideal for city exploration. Summer offers lively waterfront energy and long evenings perfect for dining and cruises. Fall is especially iconic, with crisp air and brilliant foliage throughout Back Bay and nearby historic neighborhoods. Winter delivers a quintessential New England experience, with snow-covered streets, festive ambiance, and a cozy atmosphere ideal for spa days, fine dining, and embracing Boston’s winter charm. No matter the season, Boston offers something memorable, making it a destination worth experiencing year-round.
