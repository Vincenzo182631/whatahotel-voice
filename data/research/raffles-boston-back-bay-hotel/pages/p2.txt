## Frequently asked questions

### What amenities are included in the rooms?

At Raffles Boston, guestrooms and suites are designed with residential-style comfort and refined luxury, featuring spacious layouts, curated interiors, and premium bedding. Marble bathrooms and thoughtfully selected furnishings enhance the elevated in-room experience. Guests also enjoy signature Raffles Butler service, offering personalized assistance such as unpacking, garment pressing, dining arrangements, and bespoke itinerary support, ensuring a seamless and highly tailored stay from arrival through departure.

### How can I book a suite at Raffles Boston?

You can easily book a suite through our website or by contacting our reservations team for personalized assistance at reservations.boston@raffles.com.

### What makes Raffles Butlers unique?

Raffles butlers combine the discretion and grace of the traditional English butler with the efficiency of a personal assistant and the attentiveness of legendary Raffles hospitality, where service is like a gentle breeze. At Raffles Boston, they serve as the main point of contact before, during, and after each guest journey, seamlessly fulfilling needs with a subtle yet attentive presence devoted to comfort and well-being, elevating the experience while highlighting Boston’s brilliance.

### Are there dining options available at Raffles Boston?

Raffles Boston offers several dining venues under one roof, ranging from fine dining to casual options. Guests can choose from all-day New England classics at Long Bar & Terrace, refined Italian dishes at MICHELIN-recommended La Padrona, craft cocktails at tHE Blind Duck, a speakeasy-style bar, and Café Pastel for pastries and lighter fare.

### What attractions are near Raffles Boston?

The hotel is conveniently located within walking distance to Boston's top attractions, including Boston Public Library—America’s first large free municipal library—along Copley Square, plus Boston Common and the Public Garden, the city’s oldest park system. Newbury Street offers upscale shopping and dining in historic brownstones, while Copley Place features luxury brands and an indoor mall connected to Back Bay. All are within a short walk of the hotel.
