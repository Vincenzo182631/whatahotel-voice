## Our place in Boston’s past, present and future

From the Boston Tea Party to the start of the American Revolutionary War, Boston’s history shaped the world. An intellectual and artistic capital, with some of the most revered writers, composers, artists, painters and visionaries of the 20th century calling Boston home, it’s also an epicenter of academic excellence and sport. As a leader among Boston upscale hotels, Raffles Boston celebrates not only the exceptional character of the city, but the indomitable spirit of its inhabitants, channeling their passion, curiosity, approachability and warmth. Whether our guests are seeking the top places to visit in Boston or simply enjoying the neighborhood, we are proud to add the luxury, glamour and extraordinary service for which Raffles is known all over the world.

## Design inspired by the city's Emerald Necklace

Award-winning hospitality design firm Stonehill Taylor created all the rooms and suites and most of our public spaces, infusing the design with an elegant and comfortable residential atmosphere. As a premier luxury hotel near Boston Public Garden, inspiration was drawn from Raffles Boston’s historic cultural surroundings and the city’s Emerald Necklace, carrying botanical influences throughout the property. Art and sculptural design employ materials and patterns that play off Boston’s history and architecture. Copper accents are prevalent throughout, in a nod to Paul Revere’s copper plating company; historical illustrations adorn custom wall coverings; whimsical lighting is inspired by florals; and greenery references the charming window boxes found throughout Back Bay.

## Working for a sustainable future for Boston

At Raffles Boston, we are dedicated to protecting our vibrant community and historic surroundings while embracing sustainability in everything we do. With an eco-conscious mindset, we strive to minimize our environmental impact by pursuing responsible sourcing practices and decarbonizing our operations. We actively address key issues such as waste management, cultural preservation, and social equity, including our commitment to human rights and gender equality. Through these efforts, we aim to create a positive and lasting impact for our guests and the greater Boston community.

## Frequently asked questions

### How close is Raffles Boston to major landmarks and shopping districts?

We are perfectly positioned in the heart of Back Bay. As a premier luxury hotel near the Boston Public Library and just a short walk from the galleries of Newbury Street, our location is unmatched. Guests will also find us to be a convenient hotel near the Prudential Tower and an elegant 5 star hotel near Boston Common, putting the city's finest attractions right at your doorstep.

### Can the hotel assist with planning my visit and local activities?

Absolutely. Our legendary Raffles Butlers and expert Concierge team excel at curating the perfect Boston itinerary 3 days or longer. Whether you are looking for the top Boston tourist activities, exclusive private tours, or recommendations to experience the magic of Boston in spring, summer, fall or winter, we ensure your discovery of the city is seamless and extraordinary.

### Are you a pet-friendly property, and do you accommodate families?

Yes, we are delighted to welcome your well-behaved four-legged companions, standing out as a premier pet friendly hotel in Boston. A pet fee of $150 per accommodation, per stay applies, while service animals are graciously exempt. For guests traveling with children, our spacious suites and dedicated services provide the perfect setting for luxury family holidays in Boston, ensuring comfort and elegance for guests of all ages.

### Does the hotel offer facilities for business travel and corporate gatherings?

Yes. Beyond our leisure amenities, we offer some of the most sought-after high-end corporate meeting rooms in Boston. Spanning the 17th, 18th and 19th floors, our versatile event spaces include the Sky Gallery and magnificent 19th-floor Grand Ballroom, providing exceptional settings to host prestigious corporate meetings, conferences, and gatherings that seamlessly blend business efficiency with refined luxury.

### What are the hotel's sustainability initiatives?

We are deeply committed to operating as a sustainable luxury destination. Our efforts include responsible sourcing, decarbonizing our operations, and utilizing electric vehicle transportation for our guests. We focus on minimizing our environmental impact while actively preserving the cultural heritage of our historic surroundings.
