## The Setai Tel Aviv Restaurants

## Dining

A stay at The Setai Tel Aviv Hotel is synonymous with fine dining. You can expect a special menu at the Kishle restaurant, which harmoniously corresponds to the wonderful ambience within the historic buildings of the hotel which house the restaurant. Exceptional service accompanies a fine dining experience of dishes prepared especially for you – all made from carefully selected fresh ingredients purchased locally from local markets and infused with Mediterranean flavours and aromas.
