## The Beauty of Living  _**by the River**_

At Portrait Firenze, every stay is built around the guest. Residing just steps from Ponte Vecchio, it feels closer to a private home than a traditional hotel. Here, spaces, services and small details are designed with care, so that every moment feels truly personal. More than a place to stay, it is an immersive way of experiencing Florence, with the freedom, comfort and personalisation that define the Portrait style of hospitality.

- Portrait Firenze is pet friendly.

- Portrait Firenze is proud member of The Leading Hotels of the World.

## Conviviality and Tradition,   with an _**International Soul**_

Set beside Ponte Vecchio, Caffè dell’Oro reflects a contemporary idea of Italian dining. Soft tones, vintage touches, and a relaxed atmosphere define a place loved by locals, guests, and habitués alike. The menu draws from Italian tradition, open to international influences, with attentive yet informal service.
