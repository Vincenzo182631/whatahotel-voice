## _**Heartfelt**_ Hospitality

Set within the former Archbishop’s Seminary in the heart of Milan’s Fashion District, **Portrait Milano** brings new life to one of the city’s most remarkable historic buildings. Behind its seventeenth-century colonnades, 73 rooms and suites combine the privacy of a refined Milanese residence with an urban destination animated by dining, shopping, wellness and culture, centred around the hotel’s iconic Piazza. A place shaped by Italian style, craftsmanship and a contemporary vision of hospitality.

## The Piazza

Reached through the historic Baroque archway between **Corso Venezia** and **via Sant’Andrea**, the Piazza is the heart of Portrait Milano.

Spanning over 32,000 square feet, it is the largest public square in Milan’s Fashion District and one of the city’s most remarkable urban spaces. Reimagined on a more human scale, greenery, open walkways and places to gather soften the geometry of its historic architecture, creating a welcoming setting throughout the day.

Framed by seventeenth-century colonnades, the Piazza brings together restaurants, boutiques, wellness and culture, creating a natural meeting place that reconnects this historic landmark with the life of the city.
