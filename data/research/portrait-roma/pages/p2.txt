## The Company

Lungarno Collection is an Italian hospitality company born in Florence from the vision of the Ferragamo family. From the banks of the Arno to Rome and Milan, each address reflects a distinct sense of place, shaped by culture, craftsmanship and a close relationship with the city. Rooted in heritage yet open to a contemporary way of living, Lungarno Collection goes beyond the idea of a stay, offering a more personal approach to hospitality. Each property becomes a place to experience its surroundings with ease and authenticity, through attentive service, considered details and a natural sense of belonging.

## A Heritage of Excellence

Lungarno Collection was born in Florence, inspired by the Arno and the enduring presence of Ponte Vecchio. A form of hospitality shaped by the city and its identity.

It begins with Hotel Lungarno, the heart of the collection, together with its apartments, and Gallery Hotel Art. From Florence, this approach evolves with Portrait, extending to Rome and Milan, in locations that are both central and iconic.

In each destination, Portrait offers guests the keys to the city, establishing a direct and natural connection with the surroundings. A way of experiencing the stay as part of the place itself, in continuity with its character and everyday life.

Our Vision for Growth

## Our Values

The Lungarno Collection is rooted in the Ferragamo family’s values: Italianità, style, coherence and consistency, shaping each of our hotels and restaurants. Across every location, this approach is expressed through craftsmanship, attention to detail and a natural balance between tradition and innovation. Style is both visible and lived—in materials, objects and gestures, as well as in the way we relate to others. Coherence brings harmony to spaces and service, while consistency gives it substance, through a quality that is tangible, measured and sustained over time.

## A Visionary Journey

When creating the group of hotels, there was a strong desire to bring a familial way of welcoming—defined by good taste and elegance—into exceptional settings. This approach finds its roots in Salvatore Ferragamo’s vision. At Palazzo Spini Feroni in Florence, he received guests from around the world with a natural sense of hospitality, shaped by attention to detail, comfort and craftsmanship. His creativity and instinct for innovation redefined the role of footwear in international fashion, but it is this balance between artistry, precision and care that continues to inspire our path.

## Ethics & Governance

Code of Ethics
+

The Code of Ethics defines the values and principles that guide the conduct of Lungarno Collection and all its people.

It serves as a reference framework for acting with integrity, respect and fairness, shaping daily decisions and relationships both within the company and externally.

Download the code of ethics

Organisational Model 231
+

Legislative Decree 231/2001 introduced the system of administrative liability on the part of institutions for certain offences committed in their interest or for their benefit, by persons who hold senior positions or by persons subject to their management or supervision into the Italian legal system.

The Company’s actions are based on ethical conduct rules and complying with the provisions and requirements of Legislative Decree 231/2001. For this reason, the Company has adopted the Organisational, Management and Control Model, the latest update of which was approved by the Board of Directors on 21 April 2022 and has appointed the Supervisory Board with autonomous powers of initiative and control, responsible for monitoring the operation and observance of the Model and for promoting its constant updating.
