## Tailored hospitality in an exceptional setting

Set above Via Condotti, just steps from Piazza di Spagna and Trinità dei Monti, Portrait Roma is a beautiful Roman residence overlooking the rooftops of the city. Designed by Michele Bönan and inspired by the world of Salvatore Ferragamo, the hotel brings together contemporary lines, crafted materials and sartorial details across fourteen suites conceived around privacy, comfort and tailored hospitality. A rooftop terrace with panoramic views completes the atmosphere of discreet elegance that defines the experience of Portrait Roma.

- Portrait Roma is pet friendly.

- Portrait Roma is proud member of The Leading Hotels of the World.

## Dining with Views over the Eternal City

At Portrait Roma, dining unfolds in an intimate and relaxed atmosphere, between the privacy of the suites and the terrace overlooking the rooftops of the Eternal City. Breakfast centres around Italian specialties and carefully selected ingredients, while throughout the day the terrace becomes a natural setting for coffee, aperitivi or a glass of wine above Rome.
