# Your Best Friend’s Home in Shanghai

Andaz Xintiandi, Shanghai was the first hotel of the Andaz brand to launch in Asia, offering a unique concept in hospitality with exceptional levels of service. The Andaz name—“personal style” in Hindi—is driven by a philosophy that celebrates, respects and caters to guest’s personal style and preferences in an atmosphere that fully embraces the hotel’s locale. This guides our approach to guests’ complete travel experience from A to Z at Andaz Xintiandi, Shanghai.

## DINING

View all Dining

### Bleu Bar

Our whisky bar on the 1st floor near the lobby presents a world-class beverage selection, skillful wizardry from passionate bartenders and an atmosphere that invites speakeasy moments and epic Shanghai nights. Designed by renowned Japanese design firm Tofu Inc., the intimate space—22 seats inside and 25 seats on the terrace gives the feeling of escaping to a private and cozy nook within the city where you can let loose and let the good times roll.

### Kitchen Studios

Kitchen Studios is a creative restaurant that offers a wide variety of dishes and a versatile design, making it an ideal choice for hosting all kinds of events and gatherings. Rooted in the refined charm of Shanghai style and bringing together international cuisines, it presents a harmonious culinary experience in an elegant setting.

### 88 Sushi Bento Bar

The beautiful Japanese restaurant 88 Sushi Bento Bar is tucked in the stunning steel cocoon above Andaz Lounge. Designed by internationally renowned Japanese design firm Tofu Inc., with lead designer Yoshifumi Tanaka, the restaurant incorporates the true spirit of Japan. The menu highlights modern, high-end sushi, sashimi and bento using premium fish, seafood and specialty ingredients.

### Andaz Lounge

The Andaz Lounge is the heartbeat of the hotel and an inviting place where guests can congregate for a drink or snack. This casual all-day space is accessorised with home-style flower arrangements, picture frames and coffee-table books to set the mood. The drink menu focuses on coffees, teas in a variety of flavours, cocktails and mocktails, domestic and imported bottled beers and a great selection of wines by the glass. The signature drink is a Kir Royal, made with the original crème de cassis liqueur from France.
