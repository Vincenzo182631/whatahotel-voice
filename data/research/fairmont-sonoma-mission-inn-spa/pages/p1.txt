## THE _quintessential_ WINE COUNTRY ESCAPE

Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa is an iconic wellness retreat in the heart of Sonoma Valley. Guided by natural geothermal mineral waters, guests restore mind and body through immersive spa experiences, Sonoma-inspired dining, and championship golf. Surrounded by world-class wines and countless experiences to discover, your perfect wine country getaway awaits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa located?

Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa is located at 100 Boyes Blvd in Sonoma, California, 95476.

What are some of the property amenities at Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa?

Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa offers an array of amenities such as the 40,000 square foot Fairmont Spa, four signature restaurants, complimetary crusier bikes, Mission Kid's Lounge, and daily wine tasting.

How can I get to Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa from the nearest airport?

The closest airport Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa is Sonoma County Airport (STS), located just 27 minutes north of the hotel in Santa Rosa, California.

Is Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa pet-friendly?

Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa is proud to be a pet-friendly hotel in Sonoma. A $150 per pet fee will apply per stay. We provide bowls and a pet bed for your companion, available upon request. Complimentary biodegradable pet waste pick-up bags are available at the Front Desk and throughout the resort. We also offer an exclusive pet-friendly dining menu.

A portion of your pet fee will be donated to Pets Lifeline Sonoma, whose mission is to protect and improve the lives of Sonoma Valley’s dogs and cats in need through sheltering and adoption, humane education and community programs.

Does Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa have a spa?

At the heart of the resort is Fairmont Spa, a 40,000 square foot oasis that sits atop an ancient mineral hot spring, feeding five geothermal pools with mineral-rich waterss. Our 28-room treatment spa works hand in hand with nature to deliver beautifully curated spa experiences that offer simple and effective services for every need.

Does Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa have a loyalty program?

Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa is proud to be a part of ALL Accor, the most awarded\* loyalty program by travelers. ALL includes the prestigious Fairmont Hotels worldwide, offering access to generous rewards, personalized benefits, and unforgettable experiences that make every stay extraordinary. **Learn more.**

Does Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa have a swimming pool?

Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa has a family-friendly Resort Pool. Because it is filled with naturally-heated geothermal water, our Resort Pool is available year-round.
