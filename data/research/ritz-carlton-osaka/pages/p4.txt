Spa at The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka
Spa
Step into this luxurious sanctuary designed for relaxation and rejuvenation. The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka presents an extensive selection of facial and body treatments, tailored to provide a discreet and personalized spa experience. Immerse yourself in tranquil amenities, including vitality pools, an outdoor Jacuzzi, sauna, and various heat experiences, all crafted to elevate your sense of serenity.
Spa Sanctuary in the Heart of Osaka
Our spa offers a serene garden, steam rooms, and saunas to support guests on their path to relaxation. The spa menu includes locally inspired massage and body treatments, such as the Shisui Detox Facial infused with matcha and the Ki Purifier & Rebalancer featuring sake. Skincare experts at ESPA and La Prairie provide products for results-driven facials. Discover a tranquil oasis in the heart of Osaka at this exquisite spa sanctuary.

Featured Spa Treatments
Shisui Detox Facial
Overcome the effects of modern urban life with a holistic facial designed to restore balance and tranquility. Deep, therapeutic facial massage, guided breathing, and antioxidant-rich Matcha Green Tea formulas work together to detoxify and alleviate emotional stress. This treatment clears and soothes the skin while nurturing a sense of inner peace and renewed positivity.
Book Now
Ki Purifier & Rebalancer
Rejuvenate skin, body, and mind with this restorative treatment, inspired by the pure spring waters of Kansai. Antioxidant and mineral-rich formulas deeply cleanse and replenish essential nutrients, leaving your skin smooth and radiant. Active massage techniques release deep-seated tension, promoting a sense of strength and vitality for both mind and body.
Book Now
Ultimate Radiance＆Renewal Facial
A revitalizing treatment that enhances the skin's natural radiance, leaving it luminous, smooth, and youthful. Ideal for special occasions, it provides immediate results with a fresh, glowing complexion.

Policies and Services Details
Spa Services
How early should I arrive for my treatment?
Please arrive at least 10 minutes before your scheduled appointment time.
What should I wear for my treatment?
Guests may change in the spa locker room. Bathrobes and slippers are available, and guests may travel between guest rooms and the spa in these.
Are there age restrictions for spa services and facilities?
Yes, guests under 16 may access the indoor pool and spa area only with adult supervision. The gym and body treatments are available for guests aged 18 and above, and facial treatments from 16.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations must be made at least one day in advance. Late cancellations will incur a 100% fee.
Are rental items available at the fitness center?
Yes, t-shirts, athletic pants, shoes, and swimwear are available for JPY 550 per item.

Fitness Area Information
Open: 7 a.m. – 10 p.m.
Only the Gym Area is available 24 hours. (10 p.m. – 7 a.m. / No staff on duty).
Our gym is equipped with weight training and cardiovascular exercise equipment to suit guests’ needs.
Fitness Area Use
*For your convenience, we offer swimsuits and T-shirts rental services. For more details, please ask our hotel staff or contact us.
*Staying guests may come to Fitness Center from their rooms wearing the provided bath robes and slippers. Please relax, like you are at home.
*Staying guests can use the pool area from the time they check in until they check out.
*The gym is not available to guests under 16 years of age.
*Guests are kindly requested to cover all tattoos, including small fashion tattoos, to ensure a comfortable experience for all. We ask that tattoos be concealed using the taping provided by the hotel or by wearing a rash guard or rental attire. (Please note that the availability of rash guards and rental wear is limited.)
