Discover Top Experiences at Our Hotel Near Osaka Castle
Experiences
Often considered the gateway to western Japan, Osaka lies between the ancient capitals of Kyoto and Nara. Though today it serves as Japan's economic and cultural center, its centuries-old history survives. Discover this thriving metropolis and its nearby attractions such as the Osaka Castle, Osaka Aquarium, Universal Studios and the Tenmangu Shrine. Experience the lively Dotonbori district from our luxury spa hotel in Osaka, ideal for a night out.
Signature Experiences​
The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka offers complimentary, original activities for all staying guests. Our ladies and gentlemen, who are bilingual in both Japanese and English, will lead the activities, so guests can participate in these events irrespective of nationality. We endeavor to make guests' stay more unique and memorable with running tours, origami lessons or bike rentals. Inquire at the hotel.
Hotel Art Tour
The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka is home to an collection of approximately 450 piece arts, with an interior reminiscent of an 18th-century English aristocratic residence. We are pleased to offer an art tour, held exclusively for our in-house guests. Following the tour, guests are invited to savor a special beverage inspired by Osaka. Meet in front of the Grand Clock, 1st Floor Lobby. Time: Daily, 5:00 p.m.–5:30 p.m.
Osaka Run/Bicycle Tour
Our hotel near Osaka Castle offers a fun way to experience the city's natural and cultural beauty through our selected running routes that take you past a number of iconic Osaka landmarks. The duration is about 2 hours. The experience is complimentary, please contact the concierge for availability. It may be canceled due to the weather.
Origami Lesson
Origami is a fun craft that is famous internationally. Try your hand at folding a crane, Samurai helmet, or other shapes and experience Japanese culture, firsthand. Guests, young and old, can participate in this activity, and everyone can take home their finished origami as a memento of their stay. Please contact the concierge for availability.
Activity for the Club's Guests
Japanese Sake Tasting
We are pleased to introduce a new exclusive activity available in the Club Lounge: Japanese sake tasting. Guests can enjoy three types of sake— Ginjo, Junmai Ginjo, and Honjozo—sourced from three longestablished breweries in Osaka Prefecture. Experience the unique avors oocally crafted sake, as if taking a tour through Osaka’s historic breweries.
Furoshiki Wrapping
Elevate your gift-giving with Furoshiki wrapping, the beautiful Japanese art of wrapping gifts in cloth. Our expert instructors will guide you through creating personalized, eco-friendly gift wraps that can be used again and again. Discover the versatility of Furoshiki wrapping and add a touch of elegance to your gifts and items. It's duration is about 15 minutes.
Japanese Tea Ceremony
Discover the beauty and tranquility of Japanese tea ceremony, an ancient practice that has been perfected over centuries at our spa hotel in Osaka. Let our expert tea masters guide you through the intricate rituals and traditions of this meditative art form, and immerse yourself in the peaceful ambiance of the tea space. Please contact the concierge for availability. Removing your rings is required.
Ritz Kids
Let your little one embark on an adventure with the Ritz Kids program that taps into your child’s curiosity, available at our Osaka hotel.

Explore Osaka Attractions
Known originally as Naniwa and once the imperial capital of Japan, modern-day Osaka is the country’s third-largest city and one of its most vital commercial and industrial centers. Explore the centuries of history and heritage that awaits visitors of Japan’s traditional economic and cultural capital. Near the Osaka Castle Park and Umeda Sky Building, The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka offers an ideal place to stay while discovering the many treasures this city offers. Contact The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka’s concierge for additional destination information.

Spa Policies and Services
How early do I need to arrive for my massage or treatment appointment?
Guests should arrive at least 10 minutes prior to scheduled appointment times.
What kind of clothing should I wear for my treatment?
Guests can change clothes in the spa locker room prior to spa treatments. Our hotel also allows guests to travel between guest rooms and the spa wearing a bathrobe and slippers.
Is there any age restriction on spa services or facilities?
Yes. Guests under 16 years of age can use the indoor pool and spa area, accompanied by an adult. The gym is not available to guests under 16 years of age.
What is the cancellation policy?
Guests need to notify the spa of cancellation at least one day prior to the appointment time. Otherwise, a hotel charge of 100% of the treatment price will apply.
Does the fitness center have rental items?
Yes. We can provide t-shirts, athletic pants, shoes and swimming wear for a fee of JPY 550 per item.
