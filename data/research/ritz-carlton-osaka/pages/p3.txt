In-Room Dining
Enjoy a variety of in-room dining menus including savories and course menus in your own room. Stay in your own room and dine from our selected in-room dining menus such as a la carte, snacks, and drinks, as well as a set menu including salad, main dish, and dessert. Enjoy your relaxing morning with breakfast, cozy dinner with your family, or order an evening snack, all without leaving your room. Breakfast: 6:00 a.m.-11:00 a.m. (Japanese Breakfast: 7:00 a.m.-11:00 a.m. ) All Day menu: 11:30 a.m.-9:00 p.m. Late Night menu: 9:00 p.m.-6:00 a.m. Drinks: 24 hours available.

La Baie
La Baie, the hotel's signature restaurant, serves traditional French delicacies infused with the chef's originality. Pair these specialty dishes with carefully selected wines and champagnes. This French restaurant in Osaka, Japan, is recognized as a Michelin-starred restaurant. French Restaurant La Baie Awarded One Star in "Michelin Guide Kyoto・Osaka 2026". Exceptional shellfish and meat dishes are available on the course menus. Knowledgeable sommeliers assist you in choosing from our carefully selected wine list.

Hanagatami
Delight in the tradition and beauty of this Japanese restaurant in Osaka, Japan, enhanced with a conceptual décor that whisks guests away to a flower-filled garden. Dine at the restaurant's seven-meter cypress sushi counter. Taste sumibiyaki fired over high-grade ubame oak charcoal. True to the richness of Osaka culinary culture, guests savor five varieties of Japanese cuisine, married perfectly with the finest alcohol. The experience is a timeless epicurean oasis.
