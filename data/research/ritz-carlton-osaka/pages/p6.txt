Holiday Events at at The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka
Winter Wonderland
This year's Christmas theme is "Winter Wonderland." Throughout the hotel, where the graceful European elegance of the 18th century meets the rich culinary culture of Osaka, a 3.5-meter Rose Tree and radiant Christmas decorations take their place. With the 30th anniversary of our opening approaching in 2027, we welcome you to a space filled with more brilliance and anticipation than ever before. May your festive season be one of joy and wonder.
Embrace the Charm of The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka
This festive season, the Winter Wonderland theme comes to life throughout The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka. From sparkling afternoon buffets and festive lunches to elegant dinners and seasonal sweets, every offering brings together 18th-century European elegance and the rich flavors of Osaka. Discover the highlights of the season and celebrate with those closest to you.

Festive Experiences
Christmas Afternoon Buffet
Elegant sweets and savory creations inspired by a whimsical Winter Wonderland invite guests to indulge in a truly enchanting experience. Surrounded by a sparkling festive atmosphere, enjoy an unforgettable holiday season with your loved ones.
Learn More
Christmas Afternoon Tea
This is exclusive Afternoon Tea is inspired by Burberry’ s new fragrance, Burberry Goddess Amber Vanilla Eau de Parfum Intense. Available in two seasonal chapters, Autumn and Festive, it features elegant sweets and savory delights that capture the warm, alluring notes of vanilla, amber, and lavender, creating a refined and indulgent tea experience.
Learn More
Christmas Pudding
An indispensable sweet for a British Christmas, traditionally made with 13 ingredients over a period of five weeks. It is served with warm cream and brandy butter. We invite you to enjoy this authentic, Christmas-season-only taste from Pastry Chef Elliot Staden, who was born and raised in the United Kingdom, along with our Irish Cream Tea.
Learn More
Christmas Lunch Buffet
This lunch buffet features a diverse menu showcasing seasonal ingredients. Enjoy a heartwarming lunch experience as you savor dishes that highlight the flavors of the season.
Learn More
Christmas Dinner Course at Splendido
Drawing on his extensive culinary experience across Italy, Chef Masayuki Aoshima presents two distinctive Christmas dinner experiences. Inspired by Italy’ s cherished festive traditions, each course captures the warmth and spirit of the season. Featuring the finest seasonal ingredients, these elegant menus invite you to create unforgettable holiday memories with family and loved ones.
Learn More
Countdown Party 2026-2027 at The Lobby Lounge
At The Lobby Lounge, celebrate the arrival of the New Year at a countdown party featuring live performances of internationally beloved classics that have stood the test of time. In a refined and elegant setting, let the uplifting music and festive atmosphere create a truly special experience as you welcome the New Year in style and celebrate the beginning of a bright and memorable year.
Learn More
Countdown Party 2026-2027 at The Bar
At The Bar, a one-night-only seated countdown party offers an opportunity to welcome the new year while enjoying a live Jazz Quartet (vocals, saxophone, piano, and drums), complete with a champagne toast and special dishes from the chef. Additionally, a luxurious VIP room is available for one exclusive group booking (for 2 to 8 guests), o ering a special private space.
Learn More
Christmas Cocktail
Inspired by this year's theme, "Winter Wonderland," this special cocktail evokes a white Christmas enjoyed in a whimsical storybook world. Crafted with a vanilla vodka base and layered with sweet, rich flavors, it elegantly captures the magic and excitement of the holiday season.
Learn More

Christmas Collection of The Gourmet Shop
On the first floor, the Gourmet Shop presents a seasonal sweets collection inspired by the theme “Winter Wonderland” . The collection features five varieties of Christmas cakes (available in both whole and mini sizes), as well as panettone, chocolate specialties, and other festive treats available only during this special season.
