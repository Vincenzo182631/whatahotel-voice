Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka
Enjoy a luxury stay at our Osaka hotel
In Osaka, a city long celebrated as “the nation’s kitchen” for its rich culinary heritage,The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka stands gracefully in the heart of Umeda.A doorman in a distinguished silk hat welcomes each guest, and the moment you step inside, you are enveloped in an elegant world reminiscent of an 18th‑century British aristocratic residence.Since becoming Japan’s first Ritz-Carlton, we have spent more than a quarter of a century extending heartfelt, personalized hospitality to every lady and gentleman who walks through our doors.In 2026, the hotel was honored with the prestigious Five-Star rating from Forbes Travel Guide for the fourth consecutive year—remaining the only hotel in Osaka to receive this distinction.As we look ahead, we remain committed to delivering truly exceptional service, befitting a hotel that continues to define the luxury landscape in Japan.

“Green Key” International Environmental Certification
"Green Key" is an international eco-label awarded to environmentally conscious accommodations that started in Denmark in 1994. The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka has been certified by Green Key, an international environmental certification program for the hospitality industry. Recognizing that conservation of the earth’s environment is the most crucial shared challenge for humanity, we are making an organization-wide effort to reduce environmental load and preserve the natural environment. We are also working to realize a sustainable society by implementing the Community Footprints initiative for social and environmental responsibility, as enshrined in the Ritz-Carlton philosophy, The Gold Standards.

Environmental Initiatives
1. Energy We are taking steps to use energy more efficiently and to reduce our CO2 emissions, such as by incrementally switching to low-energy lighting, using renewable energy sources, saving water, and processing wastewater appropriately. 2. Waste We are reducing the use of guest room amenities containing plastic. 3. Conservation of the Natural Environment We are obtaining a deeper understanding of environmentally-friendly organic and natural farming techniques to raise awareness in wider society and contribute to conservation of the natural environment. We are also restricting the use of synthetic detergents, chemical pesticides and chemical fertilizers on the property to prevent environmental and atmospheric pollution, as well as refraining from unnecessary biological extermination with a view to preserving biodiversity.

Community Footprints Initiatives
At The Ritz-Carlton, we are committed to fulfilling the social and environmental responsibilities articulated in our Gold Standards. Through our Community Footprints initiatives, we actively promote sustainable practices and strive to contribute to the realization of a more sustainable society. As part of our monthly activities, we host a plogging initiative—combining jogging with neighborhood clean-up efforts—in the areas surrounding the hotel and the Nakanoshima district, inviting our guests to participate alongside us. Throughout the year, we also collaborate with the Japanese Red Cross Society to conduct blood donation drives within the hotel, encouraging both guests and Ladies and Gentlemen to take part in these life-saving efforts. In addition, we actively participate in charitable initiatives organized by Marriott International, including Road to Give and offer experiential programs that allow children to discover and engage with the world of hospitality. Through these initiatives, we are dedicated to inspiring the next generation by creating opportunities and a brighter future for children.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka, with rates of ¥600.00 per hour, and ¥2100.00 per day. Valet parking is available for ¥5400.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka is Kansai International Airport (KIX). KIX is located approximately 60.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites.
