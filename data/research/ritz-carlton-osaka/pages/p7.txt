Discover Our Osaka Hotel Club Lounge
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” our Osaka hotel with concierge provides a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities, and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.
Reserve Now
Club Lounge Access
All check-in and check-out services are graciously provided within the Club Lounge, ensuring a seamless and personalized experience throughout your stay.
*Children’s Access to the Club Lounge
Children 12 years and under are welcome to enjoy the Club Lounge between 7:00 a.m. and 7:30 p.m. We kindly ask that, when accompanying children in the lounge, guests remain mindful of those around them so that everyone may enjoy a serene and comfortable experience.
*Club Lounge Access Fees
For guest rooms occupied by three guests, an additional Club Lounge access fee will apply: JPY 15,180 for guests aged 13 and above, JPY 10,120 for children ages 6 to 12, and JPY 5,060 for children ages 4 to 5. (All rates include tax and service charge.)
Children under the age of 4 may enjoy the Club Lounge at no additional cost.
*Inviting Guests to the Club Lounge
During your stay, you are welcome to invite non‑staying guests to join you in the private Club Lounge and enjoy a relaxing time together. For invited guests, a fee of JPY 7,590 per person will be applied for each food presentation enjoyed.

Dedicated Concierge
At the Club Lounge on the 34th floor, dedicated Club Concierges are at your service—providing personalized check-in and check-out, as well as attentive assistance for any request throughout your stay. With thoughtful support and gracious hospitality, they ensure that every moment on the Club Level is one of refined comfort and exceptional ease.

An Exceptional Culinary Experience
Daily Culinary Presentations
A sense of elegance and contentment unfolds the moment you check in.The afternoon begins beautifully with an afternoon tea presented on a three‑tier silver stand, crafted by Executive Pastry Chef Frédéric Moreau.
Before dinner, enjoy delicate hors d’oeuvres, followed by a nightcap and sweets savored against the backdrop of the evening city lights—each experience shifting gracefully with the changing colors of the sky.
The next morning, choose from a variety of Japanese and Western breakfast selections to start your day. And before your departure, a specially prepared lunch offers a final touch of refinement, leaving you with lingering memories of an exceptional stay.
