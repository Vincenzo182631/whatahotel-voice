Overlooking the Cityscape of Osaka from the Upper Floors
Suites & Rooms
Located on the 24th through the 37th floors, all 291 guest rooms offer a profound sense of openness, overlooking the dynamic cityscape of Osaka from a lofty vantage point. The 2024 renovation preserved the refined elegance of the beloved European classic style while introducing contemporary sensibilities and enhanced comfort—creating a new ambience where timeless quality meets modern sophistication. From the magnificent Ritz‑Carlton Suite and the Japanese Suite inspired by traditional Japanese aesthetics to the exclusive club‑level floors with access to the Club Lounge, the hotel offers a diverse selection of accommodations. Each presents its own distinctive character, guiding every stay toward a deeper, more memorable experience.
Information Regarding Building Safety Measures
The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka is built in full compliance with Japan’s Building Standards Act and is designed to withstand seismic activity up to lower 7 on the Japanese intensity scale. Even in the event of major earthquake, the building remains safe. Your safety and well-being are our highest priority.
Beautiful, Luxurious Lifestyle
The Club Lounge, Osaka
The Ritz-Carlton Club Level is often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel.” Our dedicated Club Level concierge offers personal service to ensure your stay with us is unforgettable. Newly renovated The Club Lounge consists of five areas: the entrance, the living room area, the bar, the dining area, and the food presentation area. Enjoy the sweeping views of the city; the graceful, refined atmosphere reminiscent of an 18th-century aristocratic residence; and a stunning and unique experience. Located on the 34th floor, The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge is an exclusive space for guests to work or play while enjoying spectacular views of the Osaka skyline. With five daily culinary presentations throughout the day, club guests are invited to enjoy refined cuisine, unique experiential activities, or simply unwind.

The Ritz-Carlton Suite
This is the finest suite at The Ritz-Carlton, Osaka—a luxurious retreat comprising a living room, dining room, bedroom, parlor, and fully equipped kitchen. Throughout its expansive 233 square meters, you will find furnishings and appointments befitting this exceptional space: a grand piano, graceful works of art, and a generously sized bathtub designed for deep relaxation. Here, in this elegant “home away from home,” every moment invites you to unwind in comfort and savor an experience of unparalleled luxury.

Japanese Suite
The Japanese Suite evokes a refined sense of serenity, where traditional Japanese aesthetics meet an atmosphere of elegant composure. The entrance is lined with tateiwa stone, whose beauty deepens with time; the bathroom features cedar-paneled walls and a granite soaking tub; and the tokonoma alcove lends the space a quiet, contemplative presence. Every detail reflects the timeless artistry of Japan. The spacious tatami room offers a setting suitable not only for your stay, but also for intimate gatherings such as tea ceremonies or engagement celebrations— a truly exceptional space that embodies harmony, grace, and understated luxury.

Executive Suite
This 76-square-meter retreat features a spacious layout with a bedroom and living room. From the bed—dressed in sumptuous linens and crafted for exceptional comfort—you can enjoy beautiful views through expansive corner windows that open to two directions. The living room is appointed with a sofa and a refined table set, creating an atmosphere where you can unwind completely as if you were in your own home. A stay here invites moments of pure relaxation, wrapped in understated luxury.

Junior Suite
This 64-square-meter guest room offers an expansive layout featuring a foyer and bedroom. In the foyer, a writing desk provides a refined space for work or correspondence, while the bedroom is appointed with a quality chest, a comfortable two-seater sofa, and a table. With the welcoming ambiance of a private living room, the room invites you to relax in your own way and enjoy moments of genuine comfort.
