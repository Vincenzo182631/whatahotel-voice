El Chaltén

Experience the Cuisine of Argentinian Patagonia

At Explora El Chaltén, gastronomy represents another opportunity to discover the rich traditions of Argentine Patagonia. Open-fire cooking reflects the region’s deeply rooted heritage and takes center stage on the menu in our dining room. Savor fire-roasted beef and lamb as well as seasonal vegetables and tubers roasted in a traditional mud oven. Every thoughtful detail — from the tableware to fresh local ingredients to an impressive menu of Chilean wines — is curated to enhance your immersion and included as part of your stay.

El Chaltén

Experience the Cuisine of Argentinian Patagonia

At Explora El Chaltén, gastronomy represents another opportunity to discover the rich traditions of Argentine Patagonia. Open-fire cooking reflects the region’s deeply rooted heritage and takes center stage on the menu in our dining room. Savor fire-roasted beef and lamb as well as seasonal vegetables and tubers roasted in a traditional mud oven. Every thoughtful detail — from the tableware to fresh local ingredients to an impressive menu of Chilean wines — is curated to enhance your immersion and included as part of your stay.

All-Inclusive Dining

Designed to be seamless and stress-free, your stay at Explora El Chaltén includes every dining experience, including breakfast, lunch and dinner, as well as all beverages, regional wines and an open bar serving craft cocktails. Meals and snacks enjoyed during full-day Explorations are also included.

A Curated Wine List

Choose from a robust menu of wines and varietals, highlighting regions that include northern Patagonia’s Rio Negro and Neuquen, all included with your stay at Explora El Chaltén. Opt for pairings in the dining room and tastings at our inviting bar, where sharing the day’s adventures with new friends and fellow guests makes for the perfect end to the day.
