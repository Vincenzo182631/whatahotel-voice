Connecting Explora Lodges Across Patagonia

Discover the immensity of Patagonia by connecting Explora lodges on an unforgettable multi-destination journey. Travel seamlessly between Patagonia National Park, Torres del Paine, Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve, El Calafate, and El Chaltén via exclusive Explora flights and private overland transportation.

Connect Torres del Paine and Patagonia National Park by plane

Explore Chilean Patagonia with an exclusive lodge-to-lodge journey connecting **Patagonia National Park, Torres del Paine and Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve.**

Travel effortlessly with **Explora’s flights, operating twice a week from October to April.** The direct flight is a swift 1 hour trip, with a total journey time of approximately 5 hours.

Inquire for the departure schedule, minimum length of stay, and costs.

Connect Torres del Paine, El Calafate and El Chaltén by van

Explore Torres del Paine National Park, Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve, El Calafate, and El Chaltén in Argentine Patagonia, with a lodge-to-lodge connection. Travel seamlessly with **Explora ground transportation,** available everyday from **October to April**. Our lodges are connected by Explora vehicles, with transfer schedules coordinated with arrivals and departures from Torres del Paine. Available at an additional cost.

- The trip from Torres del Paine to El Calafate takes approximately **6 hours** by road and crosses the Chile/Argentina border.
- The trip from Torres del Paine to El Chaltén takes approximately **8 hours** by road and crosses the Chile/Argentina border.

Inquire for the departure schedule, minimum length of stay, and costs at reserve@explora.com

Connect five Explora destinations in Patagonia

From October to April, Explora Patagonia lodges are seamlessly connected through a combination of flights and overland transportation.

- **Patagonia National Park to Torres del Paine**: Weekly flights connects these lodges, with a total travel time of approximately 5 hours, including ground transfers—no need to return to Santiago.
- **Torres del Paine to El Chaltén, Argentina**: This journey is completed entirely by Explora van, taking approximately 8 hours, including the border crossing between Chile and Argentina. Available at an additional cost.

This carefully planned connection between lodges ensures a smooth and efficient travel experience and a in-depth exploration of Patagonia.
