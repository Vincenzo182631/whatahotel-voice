Enjoy a Stay at Our Argentinean Patagonia Lodge

Set on the banks of the Eléctrico River under the massive Marconi Glacier, dramatic views abound at our modern 20-room lodge as well as a thoughtfully crafted experience that’s all about immersing you in Patagonia’s wilderness. Discover architecture that prioritizes the environment, comfortable accommodations and a beautiful spa, and a one-of-a-kind location in a private nature reserve with easy access to best-in-class Explorations.

An Incomparable Location

Strategically built within the 14,000-acre Los Huemules Conservation Reserve — our long-standing conservation partners in the region — Explora El Chaltén offers a one-of-a-kind location in Argentine Patagonia. Perched on the Eléctrico River and wrapped in lenga and ñirre forests, the lodge is accessible to nearby Los Glaciares National Park and an ideal base camp for exploring the Southern Patagonian Ice Field, the world’s third largest fresh water reserve.

Our address: Provincial Route 23 km 16, Los Huemules Conservation Reserve, Argentina

Architecture with Intention

Designed by renowned architect Pepe José Cruz, our El Chaltén Patagonia lodge was intentionally constructed to deliver comfort with an inspiring focus on what you came for — the extraordinary Patagonian landscape. Every aspect of its modern, minimalist architecture pays homage to its natural surroundings by incorporating local materials, calming earth tones, and innovative practices that minimize environmental impact.

Full-Service Spa & Hot Tubs

At Explora El Chaltén, relaxation comes easy at our boutique spa nestled within a ñirre forest. Enjoy five open-air hot tubs and two indoor saunas as well as a relaxation lounge, outdoor sitting area and a shower with views of the valley. Guests can also soothe tired muscles with a recovery massage at our boutique spa with two treatment rooms.
