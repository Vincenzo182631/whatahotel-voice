## Welcome to Explora El Chaltén

With a privileged location in the 14,000-acre Los Huemules Conservation Reserve, our Patagonia lodge borders Los Glaciares National Park and boasts a one-of-a-kind setting with unique access to the region’s best adventures. At Explora El Chaltén, discover a welcoming refuge with a contemporary design, rooms with front-row views of the glistening Marconi Glacier and Eléctrico Valley, and an ideal base in the hiking capital of Argentina for launching myriad Explorations from glacial lagoon treks to high-mountain ascents.

El Chaltén

Join Expertly Led Explorations

Join our knowledgeable, multilingual guides for more than 30 guided Explorations, included as party of your stay at Explora El Chaltén. These adventures include treks, hikes, and overland explorations across Los Glaciares National Park with its rugged glacial peaks, the lesser-known trails of Los Huemules Conservation Reserve, and the wildlife enclave of Argentina’s Desert Lake. Every Exploration is customized daily to match your interests and fitness levels, promising a personalized adventure in El Chaltén.

El Chaltén

Stay in a Contemporary Lodge

Our award-winning El Chaltén lodge is an expression of modern style and sustainable architecture, intentionally designed to take a back seat to the lodge’s magnificent, rugged surroundings. All-inclusive stays here offer cozy rooms and suites ideal for deep rest, access to a forest-view spa and sauna, and authentic Argentine Patagonian cuisine, all within a private conservation reserve.

Dine on Traditional Cuisine

At Explora El Chaltén, look forward to an authentic culinary experience that enriches your understanding of Patagonia. Included as part of your stay, enjoy fresh local ingredients that highlight the gaucho legacy as well as craft cocktails and an impressive selection of regional wines.

Why Explora

**Award-Winning Lodges:** Recognized for their stunning architecture, low-impact design and essential luxuries, our intimate lodges offer an ideal base for Explorations – and retreat.

**Custom-Made Explorations:** Every expert-led excursion we lead is custom made for you and your skill level, designed to bring you closer to nature in a most authentic way.

**Passionate Expertise**: Our long-established presence in South America, deep connections in each unique territory and guides trained at our own exclusive school define our inspiring Explorations.

**All-Inclusive Experience:** Your stay, guided Explorations, culinary program designed by an award-winning chef, transfers and more are all included to provide a seamless experience for you.

**Commitment to Conservation:** We’re passionate about preserving the pristine environments we explore and invite our guests to join us in sustainable initiatives, including our Conservation Reserves.

##### OrrvilleRedRiderFeb

Ohio, USA

My wife and I spent four nights at Explora El Chaltén in February, 2024. I highly recommend the hotel. This was my 8th stay at an Explora property – with El Chaltén, I’ve stayed in all of the current (as of February, 2024) Explora lodges. El Chaltén provided fabulous hiking in a unique location. I’m glad we came. The lodge is about 40 minutes north of the town of El Chaltén – not that far in terms of actual distance, but traffic moves slowly on the gravel roads. It’s located in the Los Huemules Conservation Reserve, so it’s pretty much all by itself. You truly get a sense of wilderness there. If you’ve been at another Explora lodge, the architecture will be familiar. Lots of clean lines, pleasant public spaces with views of the countryside (mountains and glaciers, here), and lots of light. I kept taking pictures of the mountains from our table at breakfast and dinner, as the light changed, and sending them off to our son – almost every shot got a “wow”.
