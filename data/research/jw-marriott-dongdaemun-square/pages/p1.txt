# Welcome to JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul

## Experience the finer things at our Seoul luxury hotel

JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul immerses you in 5-star luxury the moment you arrive in Seoul, South Korea. Created with the sophisticated traveler in mind, our 5-star hotel in Dongdaemun, Seoul, offers a sought-after location near DDP, Myeongdong, high-end shopping, entertainment, and attractions. Every room brims with 5-star amenities, such as free high-speed Wi-Fi, luxury bedding, a mini-bar,...See More a coffee and tea maker and a marble bathroom. Immaculately appointed suites provide access to our stylish Executive Lounge. Take advantage of a cutting-edge fitness center, a wealth of restaurant options including a steakhouse and a chic rooftop bar overlooking Seoul, a heated indoor pool and a tranquil spa. Host your wedding or business occasion in our hotel's modern, versatile venues, featuring expert planners as well as a stunning Grand Ballroom. Be present in mind, nourished in body and revitalized in spirit on your next visit to our luxury hotel in Dongdaemun, Seoul, JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for ₩27000.00 per hour.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul is Gimpo International Airport (GMP). GMP is located approximately 18.9 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
