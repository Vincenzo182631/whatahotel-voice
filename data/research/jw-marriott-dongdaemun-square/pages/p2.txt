## Executive Sky View Room

Enjoy complimentary breakfasts, cocktails, and snacks at our Executive Lounge when you reserve one of our Executive Sky View hotel rooms in Dongdaemun, Seoul. Choose from one King or two Double beds topped with luxury bedding ensembles and enjoy free WiFi along with stunning Seoul skyline views.

Experience unparalleled luxury in our Presidential Suite. This stunning hotel suite in Dongdaemun, Seoul, is ideal for entertaining and includes a conference room for private business meetings.
