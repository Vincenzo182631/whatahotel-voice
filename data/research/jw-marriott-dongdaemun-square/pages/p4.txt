JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul
The gateway between a Joseon era landmark and the hustle and bustle of modern Seoul

Why book?
If you want a modern, full-service hotel, but also want to stay in a more historic part of
Seoul
.
Set the scene
Aside from Korean artist Bahk Seon Ghi’s sparkling acrylic and nylon thread installation suspended from the ceiling, this hotel’s lobby, upon first glance, doesn’t feel so different from its Seoul counterparts. But as you stroll up to the massive marble desks, where the suited-up staff await to check you in, that’s when it hits you: the stirring views of Dongdaemun. Also known as Heunginjimun Gate, this historic landmark is one of only two of Seoul’s eight gates that once protected the city and survived the brutal Japanese occupation. It’s an enduring symbol of how much South Koreans have suffered, and more importantly, how far they’ve come.
As I glanced around the lobby, I noticed a nicely balanced mix of affluent staycationers—many of them had their kids in tow—first-time visitors, and repeat business travelers. Clearly, this was a hotel that effortlessly appealed to all kinds of guests.
The backstory
Opened over ten years ago, JW Marriott Dongdaemun is the first JW Marriott property in South Korea. Unlike other corporate brand hotels in town, it maintains a more intimate and boutique-like feel with just 170 rooms and suites.
The rooms
I loved the studio-style layout of my Executive Sky View Room—it might just be my favorite hotel room in Seoul. The space was open, cozy, and reminiscent of someone’s chic flat. My favorite feature was the windows: they began at the ceiling, skylight-style, and extended down the wall, wrapping me in views of the sky and city. The bathroom was just as cool, with an open design and a doorless shower with a large frosted window that let me peer outside while washing my hair. Staying here also came with complimentary access to the hotel’s popular Executive Lounge, complete with a dedicated concierge team, breakfast, and evening cocktails with hors d'oeuvres.
Food and drink
In a city that’s packed with exceptional hotel buffets, Tavolo 24, JW Marriott Dongdaemun’s all-day buffet restaurant, stands out from the rest. While it’s well-stocked with a mind-boggling array of Western and Asian favorites—including a pho station—I appreciated how much space was dedicated to Korean cuisine. There were generous bowls of seasonal Korean namul (greens and roots), like wild parsnip greens and pickled victory onions, alongside made-to-order stations of freshly-grilled pork and a personal favorite, perilla oil noodles. And unlike other hotel buffets that typically have a weak spot, everything I tasted here—and trust me, I tried a lot—was fresh and high quality.
Across the hall from Tavolo 24 is BLT Steak. Though I didn’t dine there during my visit, my best friend in town—she’s the former editor-in-chief of a major magazine, so I trust her taste—said it’s one of Seoul’s best steakhouses, high praise in a city that’s world-famous for its beef. The ground-floor Lounge also came recommended for its themed afternoon teas.
Seoul has no shortage of wonderful places to drink, but I have a soft spot for The Griffin, located on the 11th floor. As with the rest of the hotel, the rooftop bar is unfussy, tasteful, and evokes the coziness of a living room. There’s live jazz on many nights, and the young and talented bartenders—I’ve become a regular of Brown’s, over the years—are happy to work off the menu or your preferences.
The neighborhood/area
In addition to Heunginjimun Gate, Dongdaemun is also home to one of Seoul’s biggest shopping districts—making it a big draw for tourists looking for affordable clothes and accessories. But for me, the neighborhood’s appeal lies in its scruffiness. It’s not dominated by shiny skyscrapers, but rather, low-slung, faded buildings that have remained unchanged for decades. It still feels like the Seoul of my childhood.
Eco effort
JW Marriott Dongdaemun was the first hotel in the country to earn the LEED Gold rating, indicating a high level of commitment to environmental responsibility.
