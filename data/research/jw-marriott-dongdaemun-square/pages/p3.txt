We Do the Best Steak

## BLT Steak

BLT Steak, originating in New York, redefines the classic steakhouse with a modern twist. Reflecting the vision of Chef Laurent Tourondel, it seamlessly blends tradition and innovation. The signature dry-aged steaks deliver bold flavors and tender textures, paired with popovers and a variety of sides. Experience authentic New York steakhouse culture at this Dongdaemun, Seoul, steak restaurant.

All-Day Culinary Delights

## Tavolo 24

Experience a unique all-day dining buffet at Tavolo 24, featuring Korean cuisine alongside a stunning view of Heunginjimun, Korea’s Treasure No. 1 with over 600 years of history. Indulge in premium grills, fresh seafood, and a selection of Korean, Western, and Asian dishes, complemented by an array of desserts and diverse live cooking stations at this restaurant in Dongdaemun, Seoul.

### Private Dining Room

Discover the exclusivity of Tavolo 24’s Private Dining Room, designed for intimate family gatherings, corporate dinners, or special events. This elegant and versatile space provides privacy and a tailored experience for up to 20 guests, making every occasion truly memorable.

### Authentic Korean Cuisine

At Tavolo 24, experience the authentic flavors of Korea with dishes like tender Galbijjim, sweet-savory LA Galbi, vibrant Bibimbap, and a variety of seasonal banchan. While offering a global buffet, our Korean section stands out, delivering a true taste of tradition.

### Private First Birthday Gatherings

Celebrate your child’s first birthday in the exclusive Private Dining Room at Tavolo 24. Enjoy an intimate gathering with loved ones, complemented by a diverse and exquisite menu curated by our chefs to make this once-in-a-lifetime occasion truly unforgettable.

Refined Afternoon Tea Experience

## The Lounge

Escape the city’s endless bustle at The Lounge, a serene retreat bathed in warm sunlight by day and framed by the dynamic nightscape of Heunginjimun Gate. Each season brings a unique concept to our iconic afternoon tea, transforming both the flavors and ambiance to reflect the mood of the moment. Indulge in thoughtfully crafted desserts, premium coffee, and signature seasonal delights in a space that evolves with the seasons.

Seoul’s Premier Rooftop Bar

## The Griffin

Perched atop the hotel, our rooftop bar in Dongdaemun, Seoul, is a sophisticated space where tradition meets modernity. Savor signature cocktails crafted with fine spirits, enjoy premium cigars in the exclusive cigar lounge, and immerse yourself in the enchanting views of the historic Heunginjimun Gate and Cheonggyecheon Stream. On select evenings, live jazz performances create an unforgettable ambiance under the night sky.

Where Every Bite is Freshly Baked

### Seoul Baking Company

Savor a curated selection of desserts and seasonal beverages, thoughtfully crafted to elevate every moment. From signature cakes and pastries to drinks inspired by the season’s finest flavors, enjoy these indulgences in our serene café ambiance or conveniently take them to go. Each creation is a masterpiece, designed to add a touch of sweetness to your day.
