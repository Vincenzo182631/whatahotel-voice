How did it strike you on arrival?
The crown jewel of the International Commerce Centre (ICC), The Ritz-Carlton occupies the 102nd through 118th floors, directly above Elements Mall. There’s an MTR stop below, but let’s be honest—most guests are pulling up in Rolls-Royces. The ground-floor lounge sets the tone with soaring ceilings, plush couches, and a patisserie, before the city’s fastest elevator zips you to the 103rd floor for check-in and your first jaw-dropping view.
Nice. What’s the crowd like?
Between the hotel’s corporate ties to the ICC and the constant flow of visitors heading up to its Ozone bar—the highest hotel bar in the world at 1,607 feet above sea level—the crowd is a mix of business travelers, luxury-seekers, and sightseers soaking up the premium atmosphere and views.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
The 312 rooms and suites start at 540 square feet and overlook either Victoria Harbour or the South China Sea. In the Club Deluxe Victoria Harbour Room, the palette of burgundy, deep green, and beige feels rich and warm. Leather-paneled walls and a generous workspace add to the comfort, as does that dazzling skyline backdrop.
We’re craving some deep, restorative sleep. They got us?
Absolutely. The king-size bed, 400-thread-count linens, and down pillows make for a blissful night.
How about the little things, like mini bar, or shower goodies. Any of that find its way into your suitcase?
Each room includes a Nespresso coffee machine, premium teas, and a 42-inch flat screen LCD TV. Club rooms and suites offer access to the 24-hour Club Lounge on the 116th floor—worth it for all-day perks like complimentary breakfast, afternoon tea, evening cocktails, and express check-in.
Please tell us the bathroom won’t let us down.
No chance. Expect marble floors, double vanities, a rain shower, and a soaking tub with an in-wall TV—perfect for watching the news or catching up on your favorite series while you unwind.
Maybe the most important topic of all: Wi-Fi. What’s the word?
Free and speedy Wi-Fi keeps guests (and diners) happy.
Anything stand out about other services and features? Whether it’s childcare, gyms, spas, even parking—whatever stuck with you.
Dining is a highlight: Be sure to reserve a table at two-Michelin-starred Tin Lung Heen for refined Cantonese, Michelin-starred Tosca for elegant Italian, and Café 103 for afternoon tea. Ozone tops it off with Asian tapas, sushi, and cocktails served high above the city lights.
The 116th-floor spa is home to nine treatment rooms (including two spa suites), all with harbor views. Before your treatment (try the warm lava shell massage for tense muscles or a bespoke La Mer facial), spend a few minutes unwinding in the relaxation room, the crystal steam bath, and the experience showers. Before or after every treatment, guests are encouraged to settle in the relaxation room. On the 118th floor, the hotel also has an indoor infinity pool and a spacious gym—both overlooking the harbor.
What was most memorable—or heartbreaking—about your stay?
If there’s a downside, it’s the trickle of tourists around Ozone and the area’s general lack of walkability. But with the West Kowloon Cultural District now open nearby, the area finally feels like it’s coming into its own as a worthwhile destination for leisure travelers. Another big perk: The hotel is on top of the Airport Express Kowloon station, where you can check your bags before your flight.
Bottom line: worth it, and why?
The Ritz-Carlton lives up to its lofty reputation with Michelin-starred dining, panoramic harbor views, and superlative service from start to finish.
