Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong
Embrace the Wonder
As Christmas approaches, a season of peace and joy, The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong invites you to step into an Enchanted Wonderland. Upon entering our enchanting realm, you will discover a whimsical escapade filled with festive delights. Indulge in an exquisite selection of festive dining, luxurious stays, rejuvenating spa services, and thoughtfully curated gifts. Amidst this breathtaking festive scenery, our dedicated Ladies and Gentlemen are committed to crafting unforgettable #RCMemories, ensuring your Christmas is truly magical.
Discover the Enchantment
Join us for a magical holiday season filled with enchanting experiences! Our Christmas Tree Lighting Ceremony on November 27 will kick off the festivities in the hotel lobby on level 9, featuring a mesmerizing acappella performance. Experience a poetic winter scene with Diptyque's festive displays at lobby, indulge in treats at the Snow Shed on level 9, and capture memories at the Gingerbread House on level 103. Plus, meet Santa Claus as he parades through the hotel, and enjoy uplifting choir serenades on December 24 and 25.
