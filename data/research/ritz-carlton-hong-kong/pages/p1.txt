Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong
TIMELESS JOURNEY IN THE SKY
Our hotel in Kowloon Tsim Sha Tsui is ten minutes to Central business district in Hong Kong Island. The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong is conveniently located atop of Elements Shopping Mall, with upscale luxury fashion brands, Chinese and Western dining options, movie theatre, China coach terminus bus station from Hong Kong to Shenzhen airport in China. This luxury hotel in Hong Kong, Kowloon occupies from 102/F - 118/F of the International Commerce Centre. All of the rooms and suites at our ocean view hotel in Hong Kong feature spectacular views of the city skyline, looking out over Hong Kong city, the harbour, or Victoria Harbour from 490 metres above sea level. The six dining venues at The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong offers Michelin-star dining experience in Hong Kong from authentic Chinese at Tin Lung Heen, innovative Italian fine dining at Tosca, lunch and dinner buffet at Cafe 103 buffet breakfast, signature afternoon tea, evening cocktails at The Lounge and Bar DJ parties and tapas at Ozone located on 118 F.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong, with rates of $90.00 per hour, and $800.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $800.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong is Hong Kong International Airport (HKG). HKG is located approximately 32.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
