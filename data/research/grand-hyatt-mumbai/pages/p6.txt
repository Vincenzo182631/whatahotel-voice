# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Valet parking is complimentary for all hotel guests and visitors to restaurants, the spa, banquet events and more.

Free

### Self Parking

Grand Hyatt Mumbai offers ample space for guests to park their vehicles at the hotel. The services and facilities offered at Grand Hyatt Mumbai include an underground parking area with space for 450 vehicles.

Free
