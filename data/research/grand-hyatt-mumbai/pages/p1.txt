## A World Within Mumbai​

Set across 12 acres of lush, landscaped gardens, Grand Hyatt Mumbai is an urban oasis in Santacruz, just minutes from the airport and the city’s vibrant districts.​ Experience the luxury of space throughout the hotel – from newly renovated rooms and suites to expansive meetings and event venues, and tranquil poolside settings.​ Indulge in destination dining, rejuvenate with immersive spa and wellness offerings, and enjoy exclusive privileges at the newly revamped Grand Club.​

## EXPLORE

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

LOOKS Salon​

LOOKS Salon at Grand Hyatt Mumbai offers a premium salon experience with expert hair, beauty, nail, and grooming services in an elegant setting. Located at the Plaza, the salon combines personalized care with skilled professionals and premium products to deliver exceptional results for hotel guests and the local community alike.​

The Magic Table Debuts in India at Grand Hyatt Mumbai

An extraordinary dinner experience where magic, storytelling and gastronomy come together at the table. Created by world-renowned mentalists Thommy Ten & Amélie van Tass: The Clairvoyants, The Magic Table unfolds through immersive 3D projections, illusion and unexpected interactions, paired with a specially curated 5-course dinner.

Hotel Pool

Located on the banquet level, our alluring outdoor pool and children’s pool offers an ideal excuse to leave your room. Choose from our refreshing cocktails and mocktails at the dedicated poolside bar—perfect for unwinding and celebrating. Open daily, 7 a.m.–10 p.m.

Attire: Polyester Fabric

The Grand Club

Experience opulence at the private and exclusive Grand Club lounge, accessible to Club room and suite guests. Indulge in a private breakfast, high-tea, cocktail hours, or boardroom facilities.

Kids below 16 years and pets are strictly not allowed.

Grand Residences

For a truly dynamic and imaginative stay, choose from over 100 serviced apartments at Grand Hyatt Mumbai Residences. Every luxurious apartment—whether one, two or three bedrooms—has access to a private swimming pool area, exclusive entry, lobby, parking, security, and customized dining and laundry packages for resident guests.

Grand Serenity at Club Oasis Spa

Feel your senses come alive with our invigorating and relaxing spa treatments. Experience the wonders of a well-rested body and mind with our blissful treatment options at the banquet-level Club Oasis, designed to rejuvenate you.

Open 9 am – 11 pm

Club Oasis Fitness Centre

Tap into vitality at the banquet-level Club Oasis Fitness Centre, featuring a fully equipped gymnasium, personal trainers, and innovative exercise programs. Choose from monthly, quarterly, biannual, and annual memberships for individuals, couples, and families. The Fitness Centre is accessible 24 hours a day for resident guests, while non-resident members may access the facility from 7:30 AM to 10:30 PM.​

### Pets Are Welcome

We’d love to welcome your furry travel companions, whether dogs or cats! Just let us know at least three days before you arrive if your pet will be joining you. We’ll have everything ready for them, including a cozy bed, food bowl, and tasty meals, so they feel right at home during your stay.

## EXPLORE MUMBAI

Step out from Grand Hyatt Mumbai and immerse yourself in the city’s vibrant energy. Right in BKC, discover inspiring performances at the Nita Mukesh Ambani Cultural Centre (NMACC) and indulge in shopping and dining at Jio World Drive.

Head to Lower Parel, a thriving hub for both business and retail, or take a scenic drive along the new coastal road to iconic landmarks such as the Gateway of India and the Haji Ali Shrine.
