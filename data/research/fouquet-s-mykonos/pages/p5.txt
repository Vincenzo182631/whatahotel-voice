### What are the check-in and check-out times? What should I expect upon arrival?

**Check-in:**

Check-in is from 3:00 PM. When you arrive at Maison Barrière Vendôme, our team will welcome you directly in your suite or apartment for a seamless, personalized experience. Please have your ID and the credit card used for booking ready. If your room is not yet available, we’ll gladly store your luggage.

**Check-out:**

Check-out is scheduled at 12:00 PM. For late check-out requests, please contact the reception team — subject to availability.

### Can I request a late check-out?

Yes, late check-out is available upon request and subject to availability. Please contact our reception team in advance.

### What is the hotel’s cancellation policy?

The standard cancellation policy allows free cancellation up to 24 hours before arrival. In case of late cancellation or no-show, one night will be charged. Non-refundable bookings are charged in full at the time of reservation and cannot be refunded.

### Are pets allowed at Maison Barrière Vendôme?

Yes, pets are welcome at our luxury hotel in Paris, subject to certain conditions. Dogs must be leashed in public areas and must not be left unattended in the room. Our staff will not service the room if a pet is inside. Pets are not allowed in the restaurant, bar, or fitness areas, with the exception of certified assistance dogs. Additional fees may apply.

### Are baby cots or extra beds available?

Yes, baby cots are available free of charge upon request. Extra beds can be arranged in select room and suite categories, subject to availability and a supplement. Please contact our reservation team for details.

### Do you offer connecting or adjacent rooms?

As a boutique hotel 5 étoiles à Paris with just 26 keys, connecting rooms are limited. However, our team can suggest ideal combinations of suites and apartments for families or small groups. Please contact us at reservation-vendome@groupebarriere.com.

### Are the hotel rooms and restaurant accessible to people with reduced mobility?

Yes, the hotel restaurant is fully accessible to people with reduced mobility. In addition, two of our rooms have been specially designed to ensure comfort and accessibility throughout the stay.

### What dining options are available at Maison Barrière Vendôme?

Our hotel de luxe à Paris features a unique dining experience:

Frida Bar-Restaurant: Inspired by the free spirit of Frida Kahlo, this vibrant restaurant in Paris offers elevated Parisian cuisine, bold cocktails, and a colorful setting blending art and intimacy — perfect for relaxed lunches or private dinners.

To view menus, opening hours, or to make a reservation, please consult our website or contact our concierge.

### Is room service available 24/7?

Yes, our room service is available 24/7. A curated menu is offered for both light bites and gourmet meals. Additional fees may apply for late-night service between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM.

### How can I arrange a transfer to or from the Paris airport?

To organize a private transfer to or from the airport, please contact our concierge at reservations-mbv@groupebarriere.com. Kindly include your flight number, arrival or departure time, number of passengers, and luggage details.

### Does the hotel offer valet parking?

Maison Barrière Vendôme offers valet parking upon request. For more details on availability and pricing, please contact our team before your arrival.
