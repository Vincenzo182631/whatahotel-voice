# Boutique LoDo Denver Hotel \| Thompson Denver, by Hyatt

## Modern Luxury Meets Urban Charm at Our LoDo Hotel

Located in the historic Lower Downtown neighborhood near the River North Art District, Thompson Denver welcomes locals and travelers to explore Colorado’s capital city. Our boutique hotel offers convenient access to Union Station, Larimer Square and the lively dining options of the Dairy Block. Within the property, guests can gather in sophisticated meeting spaces, dine on French cuisine at Chez Maggy or enjoy craft cocktails in Bar M. Positioned near top regional attractions, our hotel serves as a central base for business and leisure stays.

## rooms & suites

Urban sophistication intersects with casual mountain style from the American West at Thompson Denver. Relax in one of our 216 modern hotel rooms and luxury suites at our LoDo hotel. With interiors created by the internationally acclaimed, award-winning design firm Parts + Labor, our accommodation's natural palette and textures infuse the spaces with warmth and tranquility. Oversized windows offer guests striking views of downtown Dever and Colorado’s Rocky Mountains beyond.

### Chez Maggy

Michelin-starred chef Ludo Lefebvre brings his culinary magic to Chez Maggy, where French brasserie meets Mile High style in LoDo. An homage to his family in Denver, Chef Lefebvre combines his classic French technique with local inspiration.

NEW in 2025, experience Chez Maggy Terrace for the quintessential patio dining experience. Set on downtown's newly revitalized 16th Street, our outdoor patio will transport you with French bistro chairs and umbrellas for drinking and dining all day long.

### Meetings & Events

At Thompson Denver, a towering two-story fireplace is the centerpiece of the lobby that descends into the lower level and serves as the showpiece of our event and meeting venues.

Gather here amid richly appointed, residential-style meeting and event spaces. Reynard Social Spaces, located on the sixth floor of the hotel, offers a trio of premium event spaces boasting incredible city views while our signature restaurant, Chez Maggy, is also an ideal venue with a restaurant space, second floor mezzanine and private bar for celebrations and intimate events.

### Pets are Welcome

Travel with your pet to our dog-friendly hotel in downtown Denver. Thompson Denver welcomes dogs of all sizes at no additional charge. Guests may bring a maximum of two dogs for free and enjoy complimentary amenities for their furry friends. Please contact our hotel for more details. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
