DOG FRIENDLY HOTEL POLICY

Thompson Denver is a pet-friendly hotel, welcoming dogs of all sizes at no additional charge. Guests may bring a maximum of 2 dogs for free and enjoy complimentary amenities for their furry friends. Please contact our hotel for more details. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Guests and Colleague Safety & Wellbeing

One of our highest priorities for welcoming guests and colleagues is doing it with your safety and wellbeing in mind. For specific travel requirements, see Hyatt’s Global Care & Cleanliness Commitment here.

Smoking Policy

Thompson Denver is a 100% smoke-free property. A cleaning fee of $750 + tax per day will apply to any documented violations.

Package Handling

Package handling fees apply for shipping packages to the hotel.

Cash Free Policy

Please note that this hotel only accepts credit cards, debit cards and, where applicable, other contactless forms of payment. It is a cash-free environment.
