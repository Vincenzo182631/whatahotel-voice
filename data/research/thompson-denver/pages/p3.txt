# Restaurants & Bars

LoDo is Denver’s hotspot for dining, restaurants, bars and nightlife. Thompson Denver is a great place to start or end a day out in the 16th Street Mall. Grab a craft coffee and a delicious pastry from Duel before heading out to explore RiNo or Larimer Square. Grab breakfast, lunch or dinner at Chez Maggy where you can indulge in market-fresh French cuisine curated by acclaimed Chef Ludo Lefebvre. Then wind down your day with creative cocktails and stylish ambiance at Bar M, located at the corner of Market Street and 16th Street Mall.

## Chez Maggy

Michelin-starred chef Ludo Lefebvre brings his culinary magic to Chez Maggy, where French brasserie meets Mile High style in LoDo. An homage to his family in Denver, Chef Lefebvre combines his classic French technique with local inspiration.

NEW in 2025, experience Chez Maggy Terrace for the quintessential patio dining experience. Set on downtown's newly revitalized 16th Street, our outdoor patio will transport you with French bistro chairs and umbrellas for drinking and dining all day long.

## Bar M

Elegantly rooted in the bustling lobby of Thompson Denver, Bar M combines the unparalleled glamour of Paris's swankiest cocktail bars with the warmhearted authenticity of the Mile High City. Step into one of LoDo's premier mixological destinations with its impressive wine list, artfully curated cocktail menu and world-class fare by Chef Ludo Lefebvre.

## Duel Coffee

Legendary coffee, pastries and morning starters shine at Duel Coffee located in the heart of Thompson Denver.

Kickstart your day downtown or boost your afternoon with freshly made coffee and espresso drinks, stay on the move with market-style grab and go options for snacks and the beverages, or relax with spacious seating throughout the lobby and window seats for energizing views of Market Street.
