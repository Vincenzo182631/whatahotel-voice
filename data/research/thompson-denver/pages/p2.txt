# Denver Hotel with Mountain Views \| Thompson Denver, by Hyatt

# rooms & suites

Check-In4:00 PM

Checkout12:00 PM

Find casual yet sophisticated American West influences in our luxury Denver hotel rooms and suites with mountain views. Nestled in Denver’s Lower Downtown (LoDo), Thompson Denver features unique décor in a natural palette of deep browns, dark greens and rich grays — from the woven wool rug resting atop the wide-plank hardwood floors to the saddle leather headboard and hand-stitched graphic pattern on the soft sofa. Elevate your stay in our 1,032-square-foot Thompson Suite with a private terrace. After a day exploring the vibrant LoDo and RiNo neighborhoods, kick back and unwind in our boutique accommodations while taking in the stunning view of downtown Denver and the rising Rocky Mountains in the distance.
