## Amelia Island, Florida

### Relax at our oceanfront Florida resort

On the tip of a barrier island in northeastern Florida, award-winning Four Diamond-rated Omni Amelia Island Resort & Spa welcomes guests to a serene location overlooking the white sands of Fernandina Beach and the Atlantic Ocean. Enjoy days relaxing on the largest pool deck in northeast Florida, including an adults-only pool, family pool and splash pad. Indulge in a personalized spa treatment or squeeze in a workout at our Health & Fitness Center. Score a “birdie” on 36 holes of championship golf or hit an ace on clay tennis courts at our Amelia Island resort. Our 401 guest rooms and suites are modern and bright with furnished balconies to enjoy uninterrupted ocean views. The resort’s restaurants cater to all tastes with family-friendly options, signature buffets and prime steak and seafood eateries. With a pristine beach, walking and biking trails and access to preserved marshlands for kayaking and paddle-boarding at your doorstep, there’s no better place for a Florida vacation than our Amelia Island beach resort. You’ll also find that our resort is the perfect backdrop for your next meeting, event or gathering, with 80,000 square feet of on-site event space.

## Traveler's Insights

### What are the property amenities at Omni Amelia Island Resort & Spa?

Omni Amelia Island Resort & Spa is home to world-class amenities including:

- Adult and family pools
- A full-service spa
- 36 holes of championship golf plus a 10-hole short course
- Mini golfing at Heron’s Cove
- Access to 3.5 miles of uncrowded beaches
- A fully-equipped fitness center with Peloton bikes and an indoor lap pool
- 23 Har-Tru® tennis courts and 7 pickleball courts
- 80,000 square feet of meeting and event space
- Eleven dining outlets
- Over 7 miles of walking, jogging and biking trails
- Kayaking, paddleboarding, fishing and adventure tours
- On-site shopping
- Family-friendly resort activities
- Self and valet parking with a nightly charge

### What is the closest airport to Omni Amelia Island Resort & Spa?

Omni Amelia Island Resort & Spa is located near the following airports:

- About 4 miles (about 12 minutes) from Fernandina Beach Municipal Airport (FHB)
- About 29 miles (about 45 minutes) from Jacksonville International Airport (JAX)

### Where is Omni Amelia Island Resort & Spa located?

Omni Amelia Island Resort & Spa is located on the Northeast Florida coast just a short 45-minute drive from downtown Jacksonville. The resort's physical address is 39 Beach Lagoon Road, Fernandina Beach, Florida 32034.

### How much is parking at Omni Amelia Island Resort & Spa?

- Self-parking is available on-site for $25 per night
- Valet parking is available for $40 per night

\*Please note that self-parking will not be available during Amelia Island Concours Week, typically held during the first or second week of March every year.

### WHAT TIME IS CHECK-IN AND CHECK-OUT?

- Check-in is at 4 PM
- Check-out is at 11 AM

2 PM check-out may be available for $75 and a 4 PM check-out may be available for $200; after 4 PM full nightly rate applies.

### Is Omni Amelia Island Resort & Spa pet friendly?

Yes, Omni Amelia Island Resort & Spa is a pet friendly hotel. We allow one dog under 50 lbs, per room, at the resort. First floor rooms in the Sunrise Wing of the hotel have been reserved for guests bringing dogs. A $200 non-refundable cleaning fee per room per stay will be applied at checkout. Nassau County's Beach Ordinance states that dogs must be under a leash at all times and littering is prohibited. Pet owners are responsible for immediate clean up.

For more information on our pet policy, please visit our Policies Page.
