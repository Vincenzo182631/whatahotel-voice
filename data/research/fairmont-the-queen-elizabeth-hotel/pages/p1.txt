## WHERE timeless ELEGANCE MEETS THE HEART OF MONTREAL

An icon of downtown Montreal since 1958, the Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth embodies the excellence of 5-star luxury hotels. Combining history, contemporary elegance and exceptional service, the hotel’s refined rooms and suites, distinctive gastronomy and inspiring spaces promise a Montreal experience that is as authentic as it is unforgettable.

### UNVEIL THE ESSENCE OF MONTREAL IN REGAL SPLENDOR

Discover a timeless sense of comfort and elegance at Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth, where the vibrant energy of Montreal meets exceptional hospitality. Thanks to its direct access to Central Station and its excellent location in the heart of downtown Montreal, the city’s biggest attractions are all within reach, from the Bell Center and Sainte-Catherine Street to Old Montreal, the Museum of Fine Arts, and the Quartier des Spectacles culture district.

Enjoy fine dining at Rosélys, savor creative cocktails at Nacarat, and discover a carefully curated selection of local products and treasures at Marché Artisans. For a moment of absolute relaxation, treat yourself to a rejuvenating experience at Moment Spa. Whether you’re in town for business, a city break or a special celebration, Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth invites you to experience the authentic Montreal, where history, contemporary elegance and legendary service meet in the heart of the city.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth located?

Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth is located in the heart of downtown Montreal, directly above Montreal’s Central Station at 900 boulevard René-Lévesque Boulevard Ouest, Montreal, QC, Canada, H3B 4A5.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth?

Check-in is typically after 4:00 p.m. and check-out is by noon. Early check-in and late check-out may be available for an additional fee (subject to availability).

What is the closest airport to Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth?

The nearest airport is Montreal-Trudeau International Airport (YUL). It takes 20 minutes to get to our luxury hotel by car.

How can I get to Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth from the nearest airport?

There are a few convenient options: You can get a ride using Uber from the airport. Taxis are readily available at the airport and offer a flat fare to downtown Montreal. If you prefer, our concierge team can help arrange a limousine pick-up. Simply contact them at qeh.concierge@fairmont.com

Is Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth pet-friendly?

Yes we are a pet-friendly hotel! Please contact our reservations team (qeh.reservations@fairmont.com) for details on pet fees and restrictions.

Does Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth offer gift vouchers?

Yes, we offer a variety of gift vouchers and packages, from stays to dining experiences. You can find them here.
