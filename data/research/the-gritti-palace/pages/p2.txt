# Relax in Refined Elegance in Our Luxury Hotel Rooms & Suites

## Accommodations

A truly authentic experience awaits in accommodations in Venice, Italy, at The Gritti Palace. Rare antique furnishings, precious fabrics and Murano glass appliqués and chandeliers are embraced by walls...See More that bring back to life memories and anecdotes from over five centuries of history. Complemented by luxurious comfort and personalized service, the refined settings of our luxury accommodations including Grand Canal suites in Venice incorporate the latest in modern technology, with in-room movies and high-speed internet access. Each one of our Venice suites is unique, from our Grand Canal Suites boasting dramatic Grand Canal views to our Venetian '700 Style Double Rooms adorned with hand-painted wood to the romantic Venetian-style atmosphere of our Serenissima Suites and rooms with a view of the Santa Maria del Giglio Campo.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 2 at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice?

No, the rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice have fridges?

No, rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Venice have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
