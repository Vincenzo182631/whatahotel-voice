# Suite Collection

Hotel Overview Opens a new window

### Step Into the Intimacy of a Venetian Nobleman’s Residence

Uncover the authentic Venetian quintessence of our refined Suite Collection and immerse yourself in a truly indigenous experience complemented by matchless views over the Grand Canal. With every reservation of our Presidential, Royal, Redentore, Palazzo, Patron or Heritage suite at a flexible rate or member flexible rate enjoy the following amenities:

- Personalized in-suite check-in
- Seasonal welcome amenity & a bottle of Champagne
- Complimentary minibar, pressing & coffee machine
- Farewell gift

## Your Suite Escape in Venice

Seize the opportunity to discover the splendor of Venice like never before with a suite stay at the magnificent Gritti Palace. Book a stay of 2 nights or longer in one of our luxury suites and receive a Hotel Credit of 200 EUR per suite per stay in addition to our suite amenities.
