## La Biennale di Venezia

The Venice Biennale has been for over 120 years one of the most prestigious cultural institutions in the world. The history dates back from 1895, when the first International Art Exhibition was organized. In the 1930s new festivals were born: Music, Cinema, and Theatre (the Venice Film Festival in 1932 was the first film festival in history). In 1980 the first International Architecture Exhibition took place, and in 1999 Dance made its debut at La Biennale.

### Destination Discoveries

View a collection of experiences organized for guests of the Gritti Palace. Learn how to participate in these educational activities. Learn more about the history, art, and culture of Venice. Our head concierge will lead your experience through the Gritti Palace while you learn about what makes Venice one of the most desirable destinations in the world.

Experiences

**An Authentic Taste**

Learn more about the history and the making of the Bellini and Spritz cocktails, and indulge in their authentic tastes. Join us for a 30 minute long immersive activity as part of our series Destination Discoveries, A Concierge-Curated Experience. Head Barman Cristiano Luciano and his team are offering a cocktail-making demonstration featuring delicious flavors native to the region.

**Byzantine Gothic Venice**

Guests are invited to join a 30-minute long history lesson on Venice's Byzantine Gothic style as part of our series Destination Discoveries, a Concierge-Curated Experience. An expert guide is offering guests an explanation of the origins, characteristics and traditions of this unique architectural style indigenous to the city. Join us to learn more about it, and its most iconic examples around the city.

**Building Venice**

Learn how the Venetians built their city in a lagoon consisting of shallow waters and outcropping lands, and how they made this difficult terrain habitable, constructing the actual foundations on which the palaces lie, and devising a system for collecting fresh water in order to survive. Guests are invited to join our Concierge team for a 30 minute history lesson on Venice’s building system as part of our series Destination Discoveries, A Concierge-Curated Experience.

**Secrets of the Gondola**

Learn where Venice's typical flat-bottomed boat rowed by gondoliers is born in Venice, its evolution over the centuries, and its symbolism. Guests are invited to join Head Concierge Ivan Schultz for a 30 minute history lesson on the Gondola and its secrets and traditions, as part of our series Destination Discoveries, A Concierge-Curated Experience.

**Acqua Alta**

Learn more about the exceptional tide peaks that partially flood the city on a periodical basis, and which used to flood The Gritti Palace, in addition to learning about the 'MOSE Project' which is intended to protect Venice from the floods. For the first experience, guests are invited to join our Head Concierge Ivan Schultz for a 30 minute immersion into the phenomenon of Venice’s Acqua Alta as part of our series Destination Discoveries, A Concierge Curated Experience.

**Exceptional Hotel Art**

Guests are invited to join a 30 minute discovery tour unveiling the hotel’s treasures as part of our series Destination Discoveries, A Concierge-Curated Experience. The Gritti Palace is a place of exceptional art which has been collected throughout its history as a private palazzo and luxury hotel. An expert in antiques is offering guests a guided walk-around of the palace’s art, antiques and furniture.

**Burano Lace Industry**

Guests are invited to join for a 30 minute demonstration of lace-making as part of our series Destination Discoveries, A Concierge-Curated Experience. The lace industry on the island of Burano can be traced back to the 1500s. Burano lace is widely considered to be the first true lace, and while modern machinery does this job today, traditional processes still flourish on the island. One of the few lace-makers left in the country is offering a live demonstration for our guests.

**Making the Forcola**

Guests are invited to join a 30 minute demonstration of the making of a fórcola as part of our series Destination Discoveries, A Concierge-Curated Experience. The fórcola (oarlock) is an essential part of the gondola. In addition to satisfying the practical requirements of Venetian rowing, the fórcola is recognised as an objet d'art for the beauty of its fluid, curving forms. One of the few remaining fórcola Masters is offering a live demonstration workshop for our guests.

Local Area & Activity Highlights

Festivals

Attend some of the most culturally rich festivals in the world in Venice. You'll truly feel the city come alive when these celebrations happen before your eyes.

##### Carnival

The oldest and most well-known of all the Venice festivals, Carnival is an unmissable time to visit the city with costumes, masks and frivolity filling the streets.

Many tourists come to the city to take part in this intriguing event, all dressed in fancy dress taking in the city's magical scenery as they go. Be sure to wear your beautiful Venetian mask and see the city from behind your disguise

Carnival in Venice enchants every traveler with a unique once in a lifetime experience. Live the intrigue of the XVIII century dressed up in a masked costume at a party inside one of the historic palazzi.

Each year, Bar Longhi transforms for Carnival with special events and costume evenings in an inspired setting. Reserve your stay at The Gritti Palace and experience the Venetian Carnival, an event that has illuminated the spirits of its participants for centuries, leaving them enchanted by all the charm, romance and mystery.

##### The Festival of the Year

The Redeemer’s Feast, also known as the Redentore, is a much loved local festival which combines refined Venetian cuisine with sparkling entertainment and a stage set unmatched by any other.

There is no more magical setting to watch the spectacular fireworks display than at Restaurant Club del Doge and Bar Longhi on their terraces overlooking the spectacle.

Join us for an unforgettable gala dinner at Restaurant Club del Doge and experience a 7-course dinner paired with a special view.

Sightseeing

From Murano to Burano, there's something to see at every turn in Venice. View some of the most breathtaking scenery along the lagoons.

##### Murano

No trip to Venice can be complete without a visit to the island of Murano. Easily combined with a trip also to Burano, it's worth a stop to watch as glass blowers keep up their age old traditions.

##### Burano

Known for its colored houses and lace making tradition, Burano is an island definitely worth visiting. A popular stop for tourists, be sure to take advantage of the empty streets and go early in the morning.

##### Accademia Bridge

For the most incredible sunrise and just steps away from The Gritti Palace, head to the Accademia Bridge to literally take your breath away. It's worth the early wake up.

##### Palazzo Rubelli

Along the Grand Canal, just like the Gritti, the Rubelli historical archives offer a different location. Discover 16th century fabrics, which are present today in our rooms and suites of the hotel.

Golf

Hidden between the ruins of a XVII century Austrian fortress, the Mediterranean woodland and the Malamocco channel, the Venice Golf Club is home to an 18-hole international golf course, one of the first in Italy, located at the Alberoni, on the far south end of the Lido island. Centuries-old trees sprout all over this world-famous golf course, which reaches the seashore.

Inaugurated in 1930, the original nine-hole course was projected by the Scottish architect Cruickshank of Maxwell M. Hart of Glasgow and built on recovered sea land, proving to be a true sea-link. In 1950 the course was enlarged to 18 holes to a project by the architect C.K. Cotton.

Over the years the course characteristics have slowly changed with the growth of many trees; the rolling fairways have however remained, as have the deep bunkers and the grass roughs. Of the original sea-link, the splendid sandy foundation has survived rendering the course practicable and verdant year round.

The course requires a great deal of precision due to its difficult technical characteristics but guarantees even the less expert golfer an enormous amount of satisfaction. The course record belongs to Arnold Palmer.
