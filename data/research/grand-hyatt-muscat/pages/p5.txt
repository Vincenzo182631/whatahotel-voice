### Excellent first time Muscat experience for family

My family, including four young children had an excellent day at the Grand Hyatt Muscat. The remodeled pool area with the Children’s pool, splash pad, and lazy river is fantastic. The hotel is situated on a public beach and there was convenient access to the beach, which was also fun for our children. There was no nuisance from it being adjacent to a public beach and walkway. In fact, it was nice to walk out along the path and down to several restaurants at a nearby complex. We called ahead and were able to get adjoining rooms for our family of six. The buffet in the lobby restaurant was a great value and a great experience every morning. The staff throughout the hotel were exceptionally attentive and great throughout our hotel stay. The club access during the day and for evening cocktail hour was an excellent perk (although it should be noted that children under 12 are not allowed in the club/lounge during cocktail hour 6 to 8 PM).

The location of the hotel is quite convenient for the city. We were able to easily drive to some nearby, museums and attractions. The neighborhood is pleasant and quiet.

We would definitely stay here again.
