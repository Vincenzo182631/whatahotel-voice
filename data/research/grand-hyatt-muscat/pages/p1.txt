## Experience the Best of Muscat from Our Luxury Hotel

Stay in the Heart of Muscat
With spectacular views of the majestic Hajar mountains and just steps away from the Arabian Sea, Grand Hyatt Muscat embodies the richness of Omani warmth and culture within authentic Arabian architecture.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Grand Hyatt Muscat is one of the largest and most opulent luxury hotels in the city, providing the perfect setting for business or leisure. Whether you’re spending a weekend in Muscat or longer, enjoy relaxing in any of our 280 stylishly appointed hotel rooms and suites, featuring a host of luxury amenities and striking Arabian interior design.
