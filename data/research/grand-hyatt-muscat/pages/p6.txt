# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Valet parking is available. Our friendly valet staff will be happy to assist you.

Complimentary

Free

### Self-Parking

Hotel parking accommodates up to 300 cars. The parking area in front of the hotel offers shaded and unshaded parking spaces.

Complimentary

Free

## Transportation

Getting here

Muscat International Airport (MCT)19 kilometers19 kilometers

Transportation Options

Airport Transfers

Airport transfers are available to and from the hotel. Contact us for information.

Getting around

Shuttle Service

The hotel provides a shuttle service for groups of eight or more people, in accordance with government regulations. Contact the Front Desk for more details.

Hotel Transportation

The hotel can arrange transportation to any area within Muscat. Taxi services are also readily available from the hotel. Transportation can be arranged through our Concierge, located in the lobby.
