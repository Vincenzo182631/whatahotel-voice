## John Barry

Located on the lobby level John Barry provides a relaxed space, terrace seating and a lively atmosphere, perfect for post work drinks or a quick bite.

A contemporary lounge serving a selection of tapas with handcrafted innovative cocktails. John Barry has access to its own private terrace overlooking the hotel’s garden & pool, and Arabian Sea.
John Barry offers traditional afternoon high tea including freshly prepared scones, finger sandwiches and homemade pastries perfect for special celebrations or just to enjoy with friends.
Reservations are a must before arrival.

No flip-flops or shorts allowed.

## Sirj Tea Lounge

At Sirj Tea Lounge in Grand Hyatt Muscat, relax beneath silk-draped tents and enjoy specialty teas, Arabian coffee, and light dishes. Savor summer ice creams, fresh juices year-round, and traditional afternoon high tea. In the evenings, resident pianist Zara performs classical favorites three times a week.
