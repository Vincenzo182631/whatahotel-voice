## Rooms and Suites

Our hotel rooms in Scottsdale are your desert escape away from home. Surround yourself with vibrant décor and ceiling-high windows with sweeping views of the Sonoran Desert. Ask about our loft-inspired hotel suites in Scottsdale for full kitchens with overhead cabinets and views of the hotel's zen garden. High-speed internet access and spacious work areas help keep you connected. After exploring the Entertainment District, slip into a signature W bed with a featherbed mattress, awaken your senses with \[comfort zone\] skin care products, and recharge with our Whatever/Whenever ® in-room dining service.
