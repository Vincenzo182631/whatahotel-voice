## Dining

Escape to our downtown Scottsdale oasis, where every meal is a moment and every setting tells a story. Start your day in style with bold breakfast and lunch offerings at Sexy Roman and Cottontail Lounge. Mingle and unwind at the Living Room Bar, where live music sets the tone. As the sun dips, the energy rises—Cottontail Lounge transforms with cool cocktails and DJ beats on the WET® Deck. Dress to impress and head to Sexy Roman, where bold flavors meet bold fashion. Whether you're dining under the stars or soaking up the rooftop scene, every bite brings the vibe. For the ultimate Scottsdale experience, reserve a WET® Deck cabana—perfect for shareable bites and front-row views of the action.

Social Season Is Here

Due to special events, WET Deck pool and Cottontail hours are affected on Oct. 31st, Nov. 13th, 14th, 19th & 20th. Please contact Welcome directly for specific times and details.
