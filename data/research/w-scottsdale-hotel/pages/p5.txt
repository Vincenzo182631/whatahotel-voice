## Experiences

The energy never stops in Scottsdale where pulsing pool parties, DJ driven weekends, rooftop moments, and curated events set the rhythm day and night. Anchoring Old Town’s Entertainment District, our hotel places you at the center of Scottsdale’s fashion, art, live music, and nightlife, with over 100 bars, restaurants, and nightclubs steps away. From world class golf and indulgent spa escapes to shopping...See More at Fashion Square and Fifth Avenue, the city delivers at every turn. Craving adventure? Jeep tours, hot air balloon rides, and iconic Camelback Mountain hikes are all just moments away.See Less

### WET Deck 21+

Our iconic WET Deck hosts our day club pool events every Friday - Sunday! We make a splash with pulsating music with resident DJs at our hotel in Old Town Scottsdale, AZ. Our vibrant WET Deck is lively and draws a crowd. Service begins at 10AM and ends at 6PM. All guests must present valid ID and credit card upon ordering at bars, lounges, and the pool. WET Deck is an adult environment for 21+ only. Age restrictions could extend dates/times depending on holidays/special events.

### Dress Code

Only appropriate resort wear is welcome at WET. During the daytime, only upscale swimwear and coverups are allowed. At night, our dress code is upscale and "dress to impress". Enforcement of the dress code is at the discretion of Management.

### Lounge Chairs

Lounge chairs are complimentary for hotel guests, and are available on a first come, first served basis. Chairs cannot be held by placing personal items on them. Personal items left unattended will be turned over to hotel security and the chair will be released. Lounge chairs fill up quickly and seating is not guaranteed for all hotel guests. We recommend arriving as early as possible to get a seat.

## Scottsdale Waterfront

Located two blocks from our hotel in Old Town Scottsdale with a pool, the Scottsdale Waterfront is a 1.1 million square foot project that plays host to several projects, events and installations throughout the year. Lined with flowers, palm trees and public art, the waterfront pathway is the perfect spot for a scenic run, walk or bike ride.

## Hiking

Hiking in Scottsdale is the best way to experience our magnificent Sonoran Desert. With more than 400 miles of well-maintained trails in the area, it’s no wonder Scottsdale was named as one of the nation’s top 15 hiking destinations by National Geographic. From Camelback Mountain to Papago Park, there’s a variety of options for every experience level.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Scottsdale have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Scottsdale offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio and Weight Machines, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Scottsdale have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Scottsdale has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does W Scottsdale have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY Spa is located on-site at W Scottsdale. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Scottsdale?

Scottsdale Civic Center, Camelback Mountain, Frank Lloyd Wright's Taliesin West, Biltmore Fashion Park, Chase Field Arizona Diamondbacks and more are nearby W Scottsdale.
