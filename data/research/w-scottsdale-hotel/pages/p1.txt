# Welcome to W Scottsdale

## Limitless Luxury, Scottsdale Style.

W Scottsdale is your ultimate desert oasis in the heart of Old Town Scottsdale. Whether you're craving a midweek getaway or a high-energy weekend adventure, our luxury hotel puts you in the center of it all. Experience a new era at W Scottsdale with our transformed Living Room and FIT. Just steps from upscale shopping, world-class golf, and unforgettable experiences, you'll find Scottsdale's most dynamic...See More social scene right outside our doors. Inside our hotel, close to downtown Scottsdale, discover elevated dining from Sexy Roman to the WET Deck (21+ only), where DJ beats keep the vibe going strong all day. Unwind in one of our 242 modern, pet-friendly rooms and suites. ## P.A.W - Pets are Welcome

Luxury For You and Your Pets

At W Scottsdale, we think your pet deserves some special pampering. Our Pets Are Welcome (PAW) program\* is custom-designed to treat four-legged guests to the same level of luxurious service as their masters. Upon check-in at our hotel in Scottsdale, AZ, you'll receive the following pet-friendly amenities: an exclusive W Hotels pet bed, treat and water bowl and a ‘pet in room’ door sign. Ask about our Whatever/Whenever® pet services.

\*Please be advised that the room rate with a pet is $25 above the room charge, plus a nonrefundable $100 cleaning fee. We allow one pet per room, weighing no more than 40 pounds.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Scottsdale?

The check-in time at W Scottsdale is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Scottsdale allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Scottsdale. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Scottsdale?

On-site self parking is available at W Scottsdale. Self parking is available for $55.00 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near W Scottsdale. The daily valet fee is $55.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Scottsdale have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Scottsdale has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Scottsdale booking.

What is the closest airport near W Scottsdale?

The closest airport near W Scottsdale is Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX). PHX is located approximately 10.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Scottsdale have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Scottsdale has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
