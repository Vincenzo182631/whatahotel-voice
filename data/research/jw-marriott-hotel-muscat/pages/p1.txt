## Sustainability Efforts

At JW Marriott Muscat, we believe we can play a meaningful role to make the world a more sustainable place. We are Green Key certified, and we are also certified with Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design (LEED) Gold by the U.S. Green Building Council. We are proud of our commitment to be a more sustainable business and make a difference by conserving water and energy, reducing our carbon footprint, sourcing responsibly, procuring locally sourced produce and our main dining venue focus on locally grown ingredients with plant-based options from JW Garden and cage- and crate-free eggs.

Learn More

## Green Key Award

JW Marriott Muscat has achieved its Green Key. Green Key is an international eco-label for tourism facilities. Since 1994, Green Key has been a leading standard of excellence in environmental responsibility and sustainable operation within the tourism industry. The Green Key programme is run by the Foundation for Environmental Education. Green Key provides a well-proven framework for an establishment to work with its environmental management on sustainability issues, including awareness raising. For guests, a Green Key shows that the establishment is committed to reducing the environment impact of their stay. The Green Key programme is linked to the Sustainable Development Goals 2015-2030 addressed by the United Nations.
