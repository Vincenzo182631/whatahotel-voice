### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat?

Yes, all rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat have fridges?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat have hair dryers?
