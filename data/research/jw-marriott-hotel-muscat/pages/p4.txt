An urban resort in the heart of Muscat, our oasis offers a serene escape surrounded by bustling city life. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Hotel Muscat have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Hotel Muscat offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Skill MillConnect & Skill bike, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Muscat have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Muscat has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Lap Pool (Outdoor)Infinity Pool (Outdoor)Kids’ Pool (Outdoor)

Does JW Marriott Hotel Muscat have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa by JW is located on-site at JW Marriott Hotel Muscat. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Hotel Muscat?

Muscat Grand Mall, Qurum Beach, Mutrah Fort & Muttrah Souq, Al Muttrah Corniche, Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque and more are nearby JW Marriott Hotel Muscat.
