# A Vibrant Location in Carlsbad

## Experiences

The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa is located in the heart of Southern California. Located driving distance from San Diego's top attractions. Explore SoCal's top destinations like Los Angeles, Hollywood,...See More Disneyland, the Gaslamp District, and more. Have a blast without leaving Carlsbad. Play a round of golf at the adjacent coastal golf course in Carlsbad. The Crossings at Carlsbad offers an amazing golf experience to golfers at all levels. Afterward, grab a bite at our 7 Mile Kitchen featuring local brews, handcrafted burgers, and hand tossed pizza. Spend the afternoon at LEGOLAND California where our guests can enjoy a private entrance. Grab a coffee in Carlsbad Village before venturing to the vibrant Carlsbad Flower Fields or soak in the sun at Carlsbad State Beach. Our hotel is a drive away from the San Diego Zoo, Safari Park, and the Del Mar Racetrack. Whether you're exploring Carlsbad or Southern California you'll love staying with us at our central and relaxing location.See Less

## The Crossings at Carlsbad

The Crossings at Carlsbad Golf Course is located adjacent to our Carlsbad luxury hotel. Golfers visiting San Diego County will love staying with us for an elevated coastal retreat. The Crossings offer beautiful Pacific coast views and is just a five minute walk from your room. The Crossings at Carlsbad is a championship municipal golf course and event facility offering a magnificent setting in a relaxed California environment.

Course Offerings

The Crossings Group Clinic

Perfect your game while having a blast with our informative one hour clinics that will work on improving your swing to create properly timed, quality golf shots. Instructors will lead guests through various stations covering the key components of the full swing and short game.

## Carlsbad Flower Fields

Spend a few hours at one of nature’s wonders, The Flower Fields at Carlsbad Ranch®. Linger among 50 acres of extraordinary color blooming on a hillside overlooking the Pacific Ocean, with a special garden, nursery, gift shop by Armstrong Garden Centers, and festivals throughout the season. Guests staying at hotels near the Flower Fields in Carlsbad can discover acres of colorful blooms. For the first time in over 15 years, The Flower Fields at Carlsbad Ranch® has changed its color pattern to a new array of colors. Designed by a professional colorist, the 50 acres of Giant Tecolote Ranunculus flowers cascade down the hillside in a spectacular display of colors sure to take your breath away. You’ll love taking in the beautiful, vibrant fields and enjoying the weather in Carlsbad, CA. The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa is located less than a mile from The Flower Fields, and complimentary transportation is provided daily. Our hotel near the Carlsbad Flower Fields places you less than a mile from vibrant blooms.

## LEGOLAND California

Spend the afternoon at LEGOLAND® California, where our guests enjoy the convenience of a private entrance. LEGO® fans can explore interactive attractions, thrilling LEGO-themed rides, and hands-on creative experiences. With fun for all ages, it’s the perfect family adventure in Southern California. Our hotel near LEGOLAND California makes it easy to enjoy a playful family adventure.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa offers a fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton, Strength Equipment, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa have an on-site spa?

Yes, Ocean Pearl Spa is located on-site at The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa. Click here to book a service now.

Does The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa offer golf course access nearby or at the hotel?

Yes, hotel guests have access to The Crossings at Carlsbad. Click here to learn more.
