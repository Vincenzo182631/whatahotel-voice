# Fresh, Locally Inspired Menus

## Dining

Experience the beauty of Southern California at our best restaurants in Carlsbad. The 20\|Twenty is set amidst the backdrop of ocean views and offers farm to table bites that are locally sourced in Southern California. This atmospheric American contemporary restaurant offers happy hour and a decadent menu of California-inspired dishes. If you're in the mood for a casual bite, visit our 7 Mile Kitchen for pizza, gelato, burgers, or sandwiches. Our poolside eatery, The Break features cocktails, tacos, sandwiches, and local craft brews making it the perfect stop after a day of exploring Carlsbad, visiting LEGOLAND, or soaking up the sun at Carlsbad State Beach. For grab and go food, refreshments, and Starbucks Coffee, visit The Marketplace before heading out to explore San Diego. Whatever your Whatever it is your taste buds are searching for, The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa is home to a balanced and fresh restaurant scene.
