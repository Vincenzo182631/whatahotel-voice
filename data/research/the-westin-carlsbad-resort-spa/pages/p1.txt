# Welcome to The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa

## Luxury Retreat With Spa near San Diego, CA

Discover relaxation at The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa, a coastal retreat just north of San Diego, CA. Our Carlsbad hotel offers a serene escape along the scenic coastline, blending upscale accommodations...See More with thoughtful amenities. Among hotels in Carlsbad, San Diego, our resort invites you to recharge in rooms featuring the signature Westin Heavenly® Bed, spa-inspired bathrooms, and modern amenities. Unlike many Carlsbad resorts, our coastal retreat offers on-site dining, from casual poolside fare to dishes inspired by local ingredients. Our outdoor pool and fitness center provide inviting ways to relax and recharge. Conveniently located near San Diego’s top attractions, The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa is a welcoming haven where you can unwind and reconnect. Experience coastal beauty and sophisticated comfort when you book a stay with the whole family at our pet-friendly hotel in Carlsbad, CA. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 80.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $25.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa. Self parking is available for $38.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $43.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa is McClellan-Palomar Airport (CLD). CLD is located approximately 2.6 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa have an airport shuttle to McClellan-Palomar Airport (CLD)?

No, The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa does not have an airport shuttle to McClellan-Palomar Airport.

Does The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Carlsbad Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
