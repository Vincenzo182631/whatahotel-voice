## Explore a World of One-of-a-Kind Experiences

Nestled on the sunny Northwest coast of Saint Lucia, The Landings Resort and Spa is the ideal starting place for all sorts of exciting adventures on land and sea. Whether your perfect vacation involves thrilling water sports, visiting natural wonders, indulging in the most authentic Caribbean cuisine, or simply sitting back and relaxing as miles of gorgeous shoreline sail by on a sunset cruise, The Landings offers a curated series of itineraries, excursions, and activities you’ll remember for a lifetime.

### DEEP SEA FISHING

Saint Lucia is home to some of the best deep sea fishing in the world. Depending on the time of year, anglers can land a variety of fish including mackerel, king mackerel, barracuda, kingfish, sailfish and the legendary white marlin.

### DOLPHIN & WHALE WATCHING

Cruise down the west coast of Saint Lucia towards Marigot Bay and Soufriere, in the tried-and-tested routes used by a variety of whales including Pilot and Humpback.

Cruise down the west coast of Saint Lucia towards Marigot Bay and Soufriere, in the tried-and-tested routes used by a variety of whales including Pilot and Humpback. But the dolphins are not to be outdone! Spinner, Bottlenose and Spotted species usually appear as well.

On our return you will have the opportunity to swim and snorkel in the Marine Reserve. We stop either at Anse Cochon or Anse Chastanet, both known as some of the best snorkel sites on the island.

### THE EXPLORER

Your adventure starts at 9:30am on board your Catamaran. You’ll be fitted for flippers, snorkel and goggles as we make our way down the rugged west coast.

Your adventure starts at 9:30am on board your Catamaran. You’ll be fitted for flippers, snorkel and goggles as we make our way down the rugged west coast. Your knowledgeable and experienced Captain will select three amazing stops for swimming and snorkeling, and you will explore some of the most beautiful reefs in Saint Lucia, home to breathtaking coral gardens and an array of colorful fish. Paddle boards will be provided for the adventurous in your group, as well as water floatation devices.

Our crew will fish and gather underwater delights that your on-board Chef will prepare fresh on the grill, and lunch will be served along with our catch of the day. A fully stocked bar is available to refresh and cool you off throughout the day. This is really a snorkeler's dream!

Thursdays, 9:30AM – 3:30PM

USD$110.00
