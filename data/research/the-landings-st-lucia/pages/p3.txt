### Savor Every Moment at The Landings

## Eat, Drink & Have a Magical Time

Loose yourself in delectable cuisine and unique epicurean pleasures at The Landings Saint Lucia. Sophisticated venues and inspiring oceanfront settings with panoramic sea views provide the perfect atmosphere to enjoy enchanting culinary experiences. From fine dining and exciting theme nights to a casual lunch by the pool or on the beach and cocktails in one of our bars and lounges, creative cuisine and exquisite service provide the backdrop for moments you’ll remember for a lifetime.

Sourced from the warm waters of the Caribbean and the lush hills of Saint Lucia, every meal features the freshest catch and organic and sustainable produce and ingredients. Our dining options cater to diverse tastes to delight everyone from our youngest guests to the most discerning palates. With multiple sit-down restaurants, bars and lounges, on-location dining, grab-and-go favorites, and private and unique culinary experiences, prepare to eat, drink, and have a magical time at The Landings.
