### Elegant & Spacious Accommodations in Saint Lucia

## Make Yourself At Home in a Luxury Suite

Escape to a world of Caribbean sophistication. The suites at The Landings Resort and Spa are designed to offer all the comforts you crave for your vacation in an idyllic setting in beautiful Saint Lucia. We offer an array of accommodations for all types of travel parties, from couples and friends to larger groups and families. You’re sure to love all of the amenities and modern features to enhance your stay, as well as the extra room to spread out, unwind, celebrate, and entertain.

Unrivaled in luxury, The Landings’ stylish suites offer a bespoke escape from the ordinary. Elegantly appointed and spacious, each villa boasts expansive terraces and stunning views of the Marina or the Caribbean Sea, with many offering private plunge pools and exclusive butler service. Relax in your private oasis of comfort and tranquility, where the only limit to the experience is your imagination.
