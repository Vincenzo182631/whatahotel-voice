### Luxury Experiential Vacations in Saint Lucia

## Endless Possibilities to Explore Together

Acclimate to an idyllic tropical setting providing the perfect balance of ultimate relaxation, meaningful connection and enlightening exploration. The Landings Resort and Spa offers an immersive 5-star luxury resort beach vacation in Saint Lucia filled with unforgettable activities and excursions providing an authentic taste of Saint Lucia’s rich history, captivating landscapes and vibrant culture.

Whether you have a taste for a West Indies-inspired culinary journey or feel the draw of marine adventures on the Caribbean Sea, we specialize in bespoke experiences delivered with warm and welcoming island hospitality. With exceptionally spacious suites, curated itineraries, and intuitive service featuring pre-arrival customizations and available personal butlers—all things are possible when you explore Saint Lucia together at The Landings.

## Discover & Make Memories at The Landings

From our stunning white-sand beach, private pools and delectible resort dining and activities to the iconic Piton Mountains and unique sites and adventures, there’s no better place to experience a one-of-a-kind island escape in vibrant Saint Lucia.

## Enticing Culinary Experiences

Engage your mind and senses in a delectable selection of activities ranging from fun and educational classes to outings and excursions exploring Saint Lucia’s culinary past, present and future. With something on the menu for guests of all ages and tastes, Culinary Experiences at The Landings St. Lucia is sure to get a chef’s kiss.

## Explore Activities

There are nearly limitless things to do and experience in Saint Lucia. Whether you’re dreaming of an exhilarating hike in the iconic Pitons, a friendly pickleball match, sunset cruise, or teen and tween age-appropriate activities, the sky (and the sea) is the limit at The Landings Resort & Spa. Start planning a personalized Caribbean holiday designed for ultimate enjoyment.

## Vacation Better with a Butler

Enjoy personalized luxury with signature butler service at The Landings Resort and Spa. Included with our Beachfront and Ocean View Suite and available with Marina View on request, your personal butler is a trusted assistant and guide, from the moment you arrive and throughout your stay. From arranging for beachside cocktails and in-suite dining to wardrobe care, housekeeping requests and tips on must-experience island excursions, let your butler calmly handle all the details, so you can enjoy every unforgettable moment.
