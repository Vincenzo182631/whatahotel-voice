### An Exquisite Full-Service Spa in Saint Lucia

## Indulge in Relaxation & Rejuvenation

Surrender to the sublime with the ultimate escape on your Caribbean beach vacation at The Landings Resort and Spa. Located on the second floor of the resort lobby, our full-service Spa in Saint Lucia offers a deceptively expansive and serene setting, featuring eight luxurious treatment rooms, outdoor hydrotherapy terraces, and a dedicated space for the Oncology Program. In addition to our state-of-the-art Fitness Club, the Spa features separate men’s and women’s changing and locker rooms, showers, and steam rooms. A full-service salon complements the wellness resort experience, prioritizing tranquility and health.

The Spa at the Landings offers an array of services and products from Natura Bisse, offering an immersive luxury experience. Choose from a full menu of facials, massages, waxing services, couples treatments, and physical therapy sessions — and revel in true relaxation in an island paradise.

## Signature Spa Experiences

Treat yourself or someone special to our world-famous Signature Bamboo Massage Ritual. This locally sourced and produced therapy, derived from the national plant of Saint Lucia, focuses on women’s health and wellness. The spa offers a range of bamboo treatments and offerings, including a relaxing body oil massage, bamboo foot bath, and bamboo roll massage. Our full-service spa menu also features classic and deep-tissue massages, skin care and facials, body wraps and scrubs, and more. Experience the transformative power of nature at The Landings Resort and Spa.

## Female Focused Services

The Caribbean's only female-focused spa program includes an exquisite menu of services to relax and rejuvenate, as well as physical therapist and personal trainer services, yoga, and Pilates instruction. The Spa specializes in enhancing well-being through personalized treatments tailored to address issues such as menopause, hormonal imbalances, postpartum recovery, aging, and menstrual discomfort. We offer dedicated support and relief for women navigating these significant life stages, ensuring a nurturing and bespoke spa experience. Find out about our Intimate Packages for Women’s Health and our full range of services.

## Bridal Services

## Oncology Program

The Landings resort and Spa is proud to launch its Oncology Aesthetics Program, becoming the first in the Caribbean to offer specialized skincare treatments for cancer patients, in partnership with the Ricardo Fisas Natura Bisse\` Foundation. Our oncology-focused treatment menu is thoughtfully crafted to support those undergoing cancer care, offering soft, nurturing treatments tailored to soothe and restore your skin, body, and spirit. Performed by oncology-trained therapists, these comforting and replenishing treatments address the unique needs of sensitized skin, offering soothing hydration and a sense of well-being. Join us for restorative care and a soothing touch for your wellness and journey.

## Spa FAQs

Reservations & Groups

Advance reservations are highly recommended. To book a treatment prior to arrival at the hotel, call 1-(758)458-7333 or email: thespa@landingsstlucia.com. Reservations are subject to availability and must be secured with a credit card. It would be our pleasure to assist you in planning spa treatments, services or events for you and your group.

Valuables

The Spa provides secure lockers with a key. However, for your convenience, we recommend that you leave your valuables in your guest room safe during your visit to the spa.

Payment

We accept all major credit cards.
