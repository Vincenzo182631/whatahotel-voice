## Unlimited Fun for Our Younger Guests

Family vacations in Saint Lucia are better when they’re built for the whole family. The Landings Resort and Spa offers engaging special programs that not only bring families together but also give your kids a break to meet and be with peers and have the time of their lives. From a dedicated Kids Club welcoming little ones aged 4 – 12 to special programming for tweens and teens, everyone in your travel party can resort to fun!

Our family-friendly Caribbean beach resort offers the opportunity to personalize your holiday just as you like it. The Kids Club and Teens Program provide endless hours of fun and inspiration, while our concierge team and personalized butlers are experts in all-ages excursions and events. _The_ Landings is also pleased to offer babysitting services and baby proofing for all villa suites.

## Explore Our Kids Club

Give your younger travelers a break from “adult stuff” while at our exceptionally popular resort Kids Club.

## Join the Club

A warm and welcoming destination for your little ones, the Kids Club at the Landings Saint Lucia is a year-round, activity-filled escape for kids. Here, we believe in fostering creativity, promoting teamwork, and sparking curiosity through arts and crafts, exciting games, and cultural experiences. Included in your stay, we welcome children aged 4 – 12, 7 days a week from 9 am – 5 pm at our dedicated “Club House” space.
