# Dining

Discover inventive yet authentic cuisine that will inspire even the most discerning of palates within each of our six restaurants at Grand Hyatt Shenzhen. Our array of international fine dining options allows guests to savor flavors from around the world.

## The Show Kitchen

Located on the 32nd floor of Grand Hyatt Shenzhen, The Show Kitchen has long been cherished by food enthusiasts. Its bright panoramic floor-to-ceiling windows offer breathtaking views of both Shenzhen and Hong Kong, creating a pleasant and inviting atmosphere for diners. In line with the Hyatt Group's commitment to open-concept kitchens, The Show Kitchen features five open kitchens that allow guests to witness the live culinary creations of talented chefs while savoring their delectable dishes. The five stations specialize in Japanese cuisine, traditional Western cuisine, classic Chinese cuisine, cold seafood dishes, and signature desserts, offering over one hundred dishes in total. Among the highly acclaimed offerings are the grilled lamb chops from the Western cuisine station, and the assortment of fresh seafood from the cold seafood station. Furthermore, there are over thirty beverage options catering to the diverse tastes of all diners. True to its name, The Show Kitchen embodies the spirit of a joyful kitchen, where guests can fully immerse themselves in a delightful and engaging dining experience.

## China Lodge

China Lodge, a high-end Cantonese cuisine restaurant, is located on the 35th to 37th floors of Grand Hyatt Hotel in Shenzhen. Here, guests can enjoy an excellent panoramic view of the beautiful scenery of Shenzhen. Whether it is a business banquet or a family gathering, it is the perfect choice. The 35th and 36th floors consist of 14 grand and luxurious VIP rooms. The private dining rooms emphasize intimacy and a sense of ceremony, with each room uniquely designed and equipped with a separate food preparation area to ensure impeccable culinary creations.
Dining Hall, which is located on the 37th floor, features multiple open dining areas, creating a relaxed and lively atmosphere, ideal for gathering and chatting with friends and family. This flexible selection offers extraordinary dining experiences to a wide range of patrons. Regardless of which floor you choose to savor the exquisite Cantonese dishes, guests can witness the chefs in action as they prepare the cuisine in the dedicated open cooking area; a unique and delightful dining experience.

## 1881

Situated on the 1st floor of Grand Hyatt Shenzhen, 1881 follows the naming tradition of the Hyatt Group, deriving its title from the hotel's address. It is a Sichuan and Northern Chinese restaurant that welcomes guests with its warm and inviting atmosphere. It is adorned with red bricks and wine barrels, making it an ideal gathering place for friends and family. The signature dish at 1881 is the Beijing Roast Duck. To ensure the authentic flavor of the roast duck, the restaurant carefully selects ducks flown in from the suburbs of Beijing. They are traditionally roasted using hanging ovens, resulting in perfectly crispy skin and tender meat infused with the aroma of premium date wood. Additionally, the menu offers a variety of authentic Sichuan dishes such as Sesame Spinach and Spicy Chicken Cubes, providing diners with a diverse range of flavors to savor.

## La Terrazza

Located on the ground floor of Grand Hyatt Shenzhen, La Terrazza aims to bring the charm of Italian cuisine to the heart of Shenzhen. With its exquisite outdoor seating area bathed in abundant sunlight, the restaurant provides a captivating ambience reminiscent of an enchanting Italian piazza, nestled amidst the vibrant MixC. The restaurant's head chef is dedicated to showcasing the wonders of Italian dining. A must-try is their freshly baked pizza, prepared with care in an open kitchen equipped with a brick oven. The handcrafted dough, paired with a variety of blended fillings such as mozzarella cheese and grains, emits an irresistible aroma as it is heated at high temperatures. Additionally, La Terrazza offers a wide selection of flavorful Italian pasta dishes, presented in various forms such as long noodles, penne, and angel hair, providing a plethora of choices to satisfy every palate.

## Sugar Box

Located on the 1st floor of Grand Hyatt Shenzhen, Sugar Box is a patisserie with alfresco seating adjacent to MixC, offering homemade chocolates, cakes, pastries and light meals. Its cube shape forms a beautiful scenery with the surrounding MixC Plaza, and the glass curtain wall offers diners a wide view from three sides, while the world's top stores make it easy for guests to grab international fashion trends. Diners can enjoy a light breakfast of cakes and artisanal breads accompanied by coffee and tea, or share a variety of pies or light sandwiches with colleagues and friends for a relaxing lunch. The outdoor seats are a great place to gather with friends and enjoy a relaxing afternoon tea.

## The Lounge

Located on the 33rd floor of Grand Hyatt Shenzhen, The Lounge provides a stunning vantage point, showcasing the vibrant cityscape of Shenzhen on the left and the lush green mountain scenery of the northern Hong Kong region on the right. Within the spacious and elegantly designed setting, sleek chairs line the windows, allowing guests to immerse themselves in the breathtaking views up close. Whether accompanied by family, friends, or business partners, guests can also relax and engage in conversations on the comfortable sofas in the middle of the lounge. The Lounge periodically introduces themed Chinese afternoon tea sets that cater to the seasons. These delightful offerings tantalize the tastebuds of every guest while they enjoy surprising window views, allowing them to indulge in a leisurely afternoon experience at Grand Hyatt Shenzhen.
