## A Grand Experience in a Dynamic City

Grand Hyatt Shenzhen is ideally located in the heart of the city, within the city’s established commercial and financial district. Just minutes from the Luohu, Futian and Liantang border crossings, the property offers seamless access between Shenzhen and Hong Kong. Situated directly above a major transportation hub served by five intersecting metro lines, the hotel provides effortless connectivity across the city. Shenzhen Railway Station is only minutes away, with high-speed rail service connecting to Guangzhou in approximately one hour. Shenzhen Bao’an International Airport is approximately a 30-minute drive away, while Hong Kong International Airport can be reached in around an hour via convenient cross-border transfers. Major business and cultural landmarks including the Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition Center, Ping An Finance Center, Shenzhen Stock Exchange and Huaqiangbei Electronics Market are all within a 10-minute drive, and about 30 minutes from leading technology companies.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

24/7 Gym

Health-conscious guests can enjoy our 24-hour fitness centre, which includes a foot reflexology and hydrotherapy area, cardio machines with LCD screens, a yoga studio, relaxation lounge and steam room. Fitness and personal training sessions are also available.

Business Centre

Located on the 33rd floor across from the reception desk, our business centre offers secretarial and office services, phone and fax access, translation, word processing, photocopying and equipment rental—everything the international business traveler might need while away from the office. Pool

### The Show Kitchen

Located on the 32nd floor of Grand Hyatt Shenzhen, The Show Kitchen has long been cherished by food enthusiasts. Its bright panoramic floor-to-ceiling windows offer breathtaking views of both Shenzhen and Hong Kong, creating a pleasant and inviting atmosphere for diners. In line with the Hyatt Group's commitment to open-concept kitchens, The Show Kitchen features five open kitchens that allow guests to witness the live culinary creations of talented chefs while savoring their delectable dishes. The five stations specialize in Japanese cuisine, traditional Western cuisine, classic Chinese cuisine, cold seafood dishes, and signature desserts, offering over one hundred dishes in total. Among the highly acclaimed offerings are the grilled lamb chops from the Western cuisine station, and the assortment of fresh seafood from the cold seafood station. Furthermore, there are over thirty beverage options catering to the diverse tastes of all diners. True to its name, The Show Kitchen embodies the spirit of a joyful kitchen, where guests can fully immerse themselves in a delightful and engaging dining experience.
