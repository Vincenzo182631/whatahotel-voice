Architecture

### The Essence of Our Design

Envisioned by acclaimed architect Cuneyt Bukulmez of Turkey-based Atolye4N, this hotel near the airport in the Maldives​ is a thoughtfully designed sanctuary that harmoniously blends modern elegance with the spirit of Maldivian heritage. The arrival jetty, inspired by the graceful flight of the Maakana—the great grey heron—sets a serene tone upon arrival. Each of the resort’s 80 private pool villas...See More draws inspiration from traditional ‘Gedhoru’ island homes, featuring thatched kajan roofs and signature turquoise doors that open to immersive island experiences. Throughout the resort, subtle tributes to Maldivian artistry—like the vibrant hues and patterns of handcrafted lacquerware—create a sense of place that is both authentic and elevated. Designed to inspire mindful connection and effortless balance, our resort in the Maldives is a refined escape where nature, culture and comfort exist in perfect harmony.See Less

## Ocean Residence

Spanning 729 sqm, the Ocean Residence is a sanctuary of refined luxury and privacy. Thoughtfully designed for families or small groups, it features two master bedrooms, a third bedroom, and an expansive living room that opens to endless ocean views. A private dining room, pantry, and dedicated spa treatment room invite indulgent moments of connection and relaxation. Butler Service

## Safeeru

A Safeeru embodies the timeless spirit of Maldivian hospitality, rooted in the traditions of the Sultanate era, when trusted ambassadors were appointed to care for visiting dignitaries. More than personal butlers, they are gracious hosts and storytellers, guardians of comfort and connection, ensuring every guest’s stay is touched by the warmth, generosity, and authenticity that define the Maldives.

### Environmental Awareness and Protection

We actively conserve biodiversity, including our pioneering INVENA Project with MIT. This collaboration explores sustainable climate solutions. It has led to an underwater structure that mitigates beach erosion and creates new, thriving reef habitats. This site will evolve into a coral nursery, strengthening reef health and inviting mindful guest interaction via swimming and snorkeling.

### Energy and Water Management

The Green Book outlines our water stewardship and sustainability priorities. Though technical details are not listed, the plan includes initiatives to improve air quality. We are guided by responsible practices to safeguard the fragile ecosystem. Resource efficiency and resource efficiency are core commitments integrated into our mindful luxury.

### Responsible Waste Management

We minimize impact through reduce, reuse, recycle. We actively reduce our footprint and prohibit buying endangered marine life products (turtle shell, black coral). Guests must not remove shells or coral from the beach, as it is illegal and vital to the ecosystem.

Sustainability

## Invena

In our commitment to sustainability and environmental stewardship, we are proud to collaborate with INVENA on a groundbreaking project. Inspired by the challenges faced by island nations like The Maldives, this initiative introduces a 30-meter-long underwater coastal structure, designed with the Self Assembly Lab at MIT. The structure will reduce wave impact, combat beach erosion, and over time create a vibrant reef habitat, attracting fish, and corals. Guests will have the unique opportunity to engage with this innovative project and contribute to scientific efforts addressing climate challenges.

A Journey of Holistic Wellbeing

Our overwater treatment rooms offer the perfect setting for relaxation, where expert therapists use BABOR’s precision formulations and high-performance natural ingredients to restore balance and inner harmony.

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Maldives Kaafu Atoll Island Resort?
