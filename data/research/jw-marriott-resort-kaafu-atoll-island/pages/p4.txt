## Spa by JW

Set above the gentle waves, Spa by JW invites guests into a serene space of renewal. Featuring six treatment rooms, a steam room, sauna, jacuzzi and dedicated yoga and fitness areas, the spa at our beachfront resort in the Maldives offers a holistic wellness journey. Exclusive treatments using BABOR’s precision skincare—a first in the Maldives—elevate the experience, blending innovation with relaxation in an unforgettable overwater setting.

### Family by JW Kids Club

Family by JW™ Kids Club invites young adventurers to explore a world of fun, creativity, and discovery. Thoughtfully designed to captivate their imagination, this enchanting space includes a whimsical treehouse where kids can climb walls, swing from the ceiling, and engage in hands-on activities like building with blocks or creating arts and crafts. The adventure continues at the vibrant Splash Park, where little ones can immerse themselves in colorful water play.

Serene Spaces

### Elysian Shores

Nestled between the spa’s arrival area and treatment rooms. Here, overlooking the vast expanse of the Indian Ocean you’ll find an oasis of calm. Sink into plush seating or gently sway in a hammock as the rhythmic sound of waves mingles with soft, curated melodies from hidden speakers. The air is perfumed with the delicate scent of jasmine, cultivated from trees planted specifically to enhance this sensory haven.

### Jungle Whisper

This hidden gem, tucked away near Veyo restaurant, offers a stack contrast to the ocean views. Enveloped by lush foliage, this reclusive spot immerses you in the island’s verdant interior. The canopy above filters dappled sunlight, while carefully placed sneakers emit subtle nature sounds, creating a cocoon of serenity.

### Twilight Haven

Located adjacent to Pure pop-up, this intimate space lounge in a hammock or experience the gentle sway of a traditional Maldivian joli-undholi (swing), as you watch the last light fade from the sky. This space bridges the transition from day to night, offering a moment to pause and reflect on the day’s experiences.

## Discover the Boutique

Thoughtfully curated, our boutique offers an elegant selection of resort essentials, books, and refined resort wear. Guests can also explore a collection of regional treasures—from handcrafted jewelry and local art to artisanal home décor—celebrating the craftsmanship and cultural heritage of the Maldives.

### Into the Blue

Whether nestled above the lagoon or steps from the beach, each of the villas at our beachfront resort in the Maldives offers direct access to the sea—inviting guests to discover vibrant marine life just moments from their doorstep. From early-morning swims to spontaneous snorkeling adventures, experience the Maldives at its most immersive, no schedule required.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Maldives Kaafu Atoll Island Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Maldives Kaafu Atoll Island Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Maldives Kaafu Atoll Island Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Maldives Kaafu Atoll Island Resort has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott Maldives Kaafu Atoll Island Resort have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa by JW is located on-site at JW Marriott Maldives Kaafu Atoll Island Resort. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Maldives Kaafu Atoll Island Resort?
