### Fresh, Local & Thoughtfully Curated

At our Maldives oceanfront restaurants, we celebrate ingredients handpicked from the resort’s own JW Garden—organic, locally sourced and guided by the seasons. Curated daily by our chefs and tailored to guest preferences, each dish is a reflection of freshness, sustainability and a deep respect for nature’s finest flavors.

### A World of Flavor Awaits

Savor a variety of cuisine—from fresh seafood to international classics—all brought to life by the talented chefs in our beachfront restaurants in the Maldives across uniquely designed spaces.

### Veyo

Overlooking the stunning Indian Ocean, Veyo our all-day dining destination is where global flavors meet mindful practices. Each dish at this Maldives oceanfront restaurant is thoughtfully crafted, inviting guests to dine in perfect harmony with nature.

### Tito's

Tito’s is a heartfelt expression of home, warmth, and tradition at JW Marriott. From artisanal pastries to fresh-brewed coffee, every element is thoughtfully crafted to echo the love and care. Tito’s invites guests to slow down, savor the moment, and feel truly at home in every bite and every sip.

### Riva

At Riva, guests can experience the soul of the Mediterranean in the heart of the Maldives. Inspired by the Amalfi Coast, the Greek Isles, and the Spanish Riviera, each dish celebrates the bounty of the sea and vibrant coastal spices.

### Wahoo

Wahoo captures the energy of the ocean with a relaxed beachside vibe. Just steps from the main pool, this vibrant bar serves refreshing cocktails and light bites—perfect for those sun-soaked afternoons and golden-hour moments.

### Athiri

Athiri, meaning “shoreline” in Dhivehi, brings island elegance to a refined parrilla grill house. Set against panoramic ocean views, the restaurant highlights premium cuts and fire-grilled flavors—crafted for memorable evenings by the sea.

### Nikkei

Nikkei presents the bold fusion of Japanese precision and Peruvian soul. This overwater dining experience weaves together tradition and innovation, offering guests a sensory celebration of cultural harmony and culinary creativity.
