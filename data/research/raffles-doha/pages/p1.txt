## An architectural _icon_, a cultural _landmark_

Arching skyward with dramatic poise, discover the world’s most splendorous hotel, where every encounter has been crafted to create a sense of wonder. Raffles is located within the striking Katara Towers, inspired by the crossed swords of Qatar. This modern-day masterpiece is the symbol of a new dawn in Doha. Embark on your bespoke journey of discovery in one of our highly personalised suites, where innovative luxury is brought to life in the most dazzling of ways.

Katara Hall

## An iconic stage for Doha’s unforgettable occasions

Katara Hall is a spectacular space at the heart of the hotel, resplendent with soaring, arched ceilings, Arabesque artistry and panoramic terraces. This awe-inspiring ballroom, the largest in the city, can accommodate up to 2,500 guests making it the ideal setting for large weddings, galas, concerts and exhibitions.

Katara Hall's sweeping terraces that overlook fashionable Lusail Marina and the azure seas beyond are also yours to enjoy, while a team of Raffles Butlers awaits discreetly in the background, ready to serve.

## Frequently asked questions

### What makes Raffles Doha unique?

Raffles Doha is distinguished by several exclusive features:

- Every accommodation is a luxury suite.
- Every guest receives dedicated Raffles Butler service.
- The hotel occupies the iconic Katara Towers.
- Personalized luxury experiences are tailored to each guest.
- Multiple fine dining restaurants are available.
- The hotel combines contemporary luxury with Arabian hospitality.

### How can I book a suite at Raffles Doha?

You can easily book your suite by visiting the Raffles Doha website or contacting their reservation team directly for assistance.

### What is the location of Raffles Doha?

Raffles Doha is located in Lusail City, Qatar, inside the landmark Katara Towers. The hotel is approximately 25 minutes from Hamad International Airport and is close to Place Vendôme Mall, Lusail Marina, Lusail Boulevard, Lusail Stadium, Katara Cultural Village, The Pearl-Qatar, Westbay and Doha Golf Club.

### Does every guest receive Butler Service?

Yes. Every suite reservation at Raffles Doha includes dedicated Raffles Butler Service. The butler assists with unpacking, garment pressing, dining reservations, transportation arrangements, personalized requests, and other bespoke services throughout the stay.

### What dining options are available at Raffles Doha?

Experience an exceptional collection of restaurants and bars at **Raffles Doha**, from **Alba**, the hotel's Michelin-starred Italian restaurant, to **Aqua**, serving Mediterranean cuisine by the pool, and **Acoustic**, a rooftop venue with panoramic Lusail skyline views. Guests can also enjoy elegant lounges, signature bars, afternoon tea, and a variety of international dining experiences, all crafted to deliver the renowned luxury and hospitality of Raffles Doha.

### Is Raffles Doha suitable for business travelers?

Yes. Raffles Doha offers luxury suites with spacious work areas, high-speed Wi-Fi, meeting facilities, executive services, concierge support, and easy access to Lusail's financial and commercial districts, making it an excellent choice for business travelers.

### Is Raffles Doha suitable for families?

Yes. Raffles Doha welcomes families and provides spacious suites, family-friendly dining options, concierge services, and personalized experiences suitable for guests traveling with children.

### Can guests host weddings at Raffles Doha?

Yes. Raffles Doha offers luxury venues suitable for weddings, engagement celebrations, gala dinners, and private events. Dedicated event specialists assist with planning bespoke celebrations.

### Is Raffles Doha suitable for corporate events?

Yes. Raffles Doha provides premium venues, luxury accommodations, personalized event planning, business support services, and catering options for conferences, executive meetings, product launches, and corporate events.

### Does Raffles Doha have meeting rooms?

Yes. Raffles Doha offers elegant meeting rooms, executive boardrooms, conference facilities, and luxury event spaces equipped with modern audiovisual technology.
