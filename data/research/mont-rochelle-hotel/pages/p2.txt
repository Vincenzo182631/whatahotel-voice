# The winery

## Nurturing the finest grapes

At Mont Rochelle, we carefully tend to diverse grape varieties including Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Syrah, and Cabernet Sauvignon.

Our vineyards benefit from varied microclimates due to the region's Mediterranean-like conditions - dry summers and 1000mm of winter rainfall. The south-easterly winds in the Franschhoek Valley usher in cooler air, extending the ripening period.

## Wine tasting

A wine tasting at Mont Rochelle isn't just a special event; it's an everyday occurrence, woven into the rhythm of estate life. Whether you're marking something special or simply making the most of an afternoon in the valley, there's always a glass waiting to be poured and a story behind every vintage worth hearing.

## Our winemaker

Michael Langenhoven developed his craft at Boschendal, La Motte, in France's Rhône Valley, Sancerre, and California's Dry Creek Vineyards. His passion for winemaking is deeply rooted in his family heritage.

Michael's proudest moment was winning double gold at the VERITAS Wine Awards, and he aspires to further elevate Mont Rochelle's legacy of exceptional wines.

## The cellar

Our 1850s winery, once a fruit packing shed, now functions as a traditional wine cellar, processing nearly 150 tons of grapes each season.

Due to the winery’s National Heritage status, all of Mont Rochelle’s white wine tanks are horizontal, as the roof couldn’t be heightened to fit them all in! Our wines are available to buy and enjoy at home, or you can have them delivered straight to your doorstep.

## Franschhoek Wine Tram

Discover the Franschhoek Valley aboard the famous Wine Tram. This charming and traditional hop-on-off service visits some of South Africa’s oldest and most distinguished wineries, including Mont Rochelle.

Explore picturesque vineyards and taste a mouthwatering variety of fine wines, all the while enjoying the beautiful scenery. Is there a better way to spend the day?

Remarkably youthful with dark red hues. This full-bodied wine displays complex layers of Cassis, dark and red fruit on the nose with beautiful perfumy nuances. On the palate, there is a combination of rich dark fruit flavours from the Cabernet sauvignon and fresh vibrant red fruit, pencil shavings and perfumy notes from the Cabernet franc that is in perfect harmony. The tannins are very well integrated and velvety in texture. The rich finish of the wine is well-rounded with a beautiful, complex and layered aftertaste.
