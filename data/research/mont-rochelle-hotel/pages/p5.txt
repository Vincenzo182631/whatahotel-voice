Sir Richard Branson has taken over this 17th-century hilltop estate, with its 100-acre vineyard, expanding his Virgin Limited Collection to the mountainous vineyards of Franschhoek. Exposed-beam ceilings and pops of whimsy (like neon ceramic bulldogs), lend humor to the 22 airy rooms overlooking the valley through floor-to-ceiling windows. Guests can explore the grounds on foot or horseback and personalized tasting tours are on offer.
