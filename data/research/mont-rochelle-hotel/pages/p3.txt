# Discover Mont Rochelle Hotel

## Wake up in the heart of Franschhoek wine country

Experience the charm of our 26-bedroom luxury hotel in Franschhoek, nestled at the centre of the Mont Rochelle wine estate in the Cape Winelands.

Wake up to sweeping vineyard and mountain views, savour seasonal cuisine at MIKO, or enjoy homestyle delights at The Country Kitchen, and unwind at the Mont Rochelle Spa. Perfectly placed for exploring Franschhoek’s wine, culture, and scenery, Mont Rochelle Hotel offers the ideal Cape Winelands getaway.

## Staying at Mont Rochelle

Mont Rochelle Hotel features 26 beautifully appointed rooms and suites, each offering a serene retreat amidst the vineyards and mountains of Franschhoek. Choose from Shiraz and Merlot Rooms for cosy elegance, Pinotage Suites for spacious comfort, or the Cap Classique Vineyard Pool Suites with private terraces and plunge pools for complete relaxation.

Each accommodation combines contemporary style with Cape Winelands charm, offering sweeping views and thoughtful touches for a memorable stay. Whether you’re seeking a romantic escape or a peaceful getaway, Mont Rochelle’s rooms and suites provide the perfect base to experience the estate and the surrounding beauty.

## Dining

At Mont Rochelle, dining is a celebration of the region's finest ingredients, carefully combined with international influences. Surrounded by vineyards in one of the world's premier wine regions, every meal reflects the essence of the Cape Winelands.

Choose MIKO, our signature restaurant with sweeping 180-degree views of the Franschhoek Valley, or The Country Kitchen, a relaxed, informal spot just a short walk or drive from the hotel, beside our wine cellar. From fine dining to casual meals, Mont Rochelle offers a distinctive culinary experience in the heart of Franschhoek.

**The Country Kitchen is closed from 1st September for a little sprucing up. In the meantime, MIKO is taking care of lunch and wine tastings too, alongside its usual signature dining.**

## Things to do

Wine tasting is a must at Mont Rochelle, and the Franschhoek wine tram offers a fun and scenic way to explore the area while sampling some of the region’s finest wines.

Adventure enthusiasts can enjoy hiking and mountain biking, while those seeking a more relaxed pace can soak up the panoramic views of the Dassenberg Mountains from the pool or unwind with a treatment at the Mont Rochelle Spa.

For guests of all ages, there’s no shortage of activities. From lawn games and tennis to simply strolling the vineyards, every stay offers a mix of relaxation, adventure, and the natural beauty of the Cape Winelands.

## Spa and wellness

Step into the intimate Mont Rochelle Spa, featuring two dedicated treatment rooms and a revitalising hammam. Every treatment is tailored to your needs, blending luxury and wellness to restore energy and balance.

Whether you’re enjoying a serene getaway in the Cape Winelands or treating yourself to an indulgent spa day in Franschhoek, Mont Rochelle Spa offers the perfect escape.
