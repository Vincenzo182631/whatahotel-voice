# Discover The Manor House

## Luxury and privacy in the Cape Winelands

Discover The Manor House, our stunning four-bedroom villa set in the heart of Franschhoek’s Klein Dassenberg mountains at Mont Rochelle. Available for exclusive use year-round, it offers privacy and comfort just a short walk from the main Hotel and steps from our winery.

Perfect for families or small groups, The Manor House provides an idyllic Cape Winelands escape, combining luxury, tranquillity, and easy access to the best of Franschhoek’s wine, dining, and scenic beauty.

## Spacious comfort for every stay

The Manor House accommodates up to eight adults and six children in comfort, with a dedicated bunk room ideal for younger guests. Each bedroom features a luxurious en-suite bathroom with a bath, shower, and double vanity, while beds can be converted into twins to suit your group.

Every room includes a TV, a fully stocked mini bar (excluding the bunk room), and heating and air conditioning, ensuring comfort for all. Whether it’s a family getaway, a group of friends, or a private retreat for two, The Manor House offers space, style, and privacy in the heart of the Cape Winelands.

## Luxury indoor and outdoor living

Set against uninterrupted vineyard views and the stunning Dassenberg Mountains, The Manor House offers a perfect blend of space, comfort, and style. Gather in the expansive dining area for memorable meals, challenge friends and family in the games room with a pool table, or prepare gourmet dishes in the fully equipped, modern kitchen.

For relaxation and recreation, enjoy a swim in two private heated pools or stay active in your personal gym, all within the privacy of your villa. Whether entertaining, unwinding, or simply soaking up the spectacular scenery, The Manor House provides a luxurious and flexible retreat in the heart of Franschhoek.

## Dining experiences

Discover a range of dining experiences across the Mont Rochelle estate. Start your day with breakfast prepared for you at the Manor House, then savour seasonal, contemporary cuisine at MIKO, or enjoy the relaxed charm of the Country Kitchen, just a short stroll away. Make the most of the estate's beautiful grounds with a vineyard picnic, soaking up the Cape Winelands scenery as you dine.

For something more intimate, private dining and in-villa chef experiences can also be arranged, from a quiet dinner to a special celebration.

## Discover, play and relax

Wine tasting is a must at Mont Rochelle, and the wine tram offers a scenic way to explore Franschhoek while sampling some of the region’s finest wines. Adventure enthusiasts can enjoy hiking and mountain biking, while those seeking a more relaxed pace can soak up the panoramic views of the Dassenberg Mountains or unwind with a treatment at the Mont Rochelle Spa.

At the Manor House, there’s plenty of opportunity to relax by your private pool, enjoy the games room, or take a leisurely stroll through the vineyards. Across the estate, activities like lawn games and tennis ensure every stay offers a perfect balance of relaxation, adventure, and the natural beauty of Franschhoek.
