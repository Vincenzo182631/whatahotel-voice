An authentic expression of 1930s French Art Deco style, Grand Hotel d'Angkor is a destination redolent with history. Our beautifully refined rooms, suites and villas blend old-world charm with Cambodian influences and contemporary detail.

Our Story

## A timeless landmark

Opened in 1932 for society globetrotters eager to discover the marvels of Angkor Wat, Grand Hotel d'Angkor was the first luxurious grand hotel of its kind to open in Siem Reap.

Read More

Dining

## 1932 in the here and now

1932 - named after the year in which Grand Hotel d'Angkor was opened - is a magical restaurant that takes you on a journey through not just Cambodia's cuisine, but the kingdom's history itself. Behind every flavour unfolds a story: a memory of childhood homes, palatial kitchens and royal palaces.
