Featured Treatments
Ultimate Radiance & Renewal Facial
Restore skin’s vitality and reveal a bright, smooth and radiantly youthful complexion. A must-have treatment for immediate results prior to a special event. Includes brush cleanse, skin radiance mask, age-defying crystal massage, lifting mask and scalp massage performed with ESPA products.
Miner’s Mineral Mud Wrap
Our mineral inspired experience uses indigenous ore that is hand mixed during the treatment. Foraged from the depths of Colorado’s mines, activated charcoal mud is renowned for its powerful detoxifying properties. Amethyst oil, rose quartz salt, and shea butter join the mix. Includes full body exfoliation, body wrap, scalp massage, hydrotherapy bath, and full body massage.

Fitness Center Details
Personal Training
Certified fitness trainers are available to work one-on-one to establish and reinforce a customized exercise program in either individual or series formats.
Facilities
The Fitness Center at The Bachelor Gulch Spa boasts beautiful views of Beaver Creek Mountain and the Vail Valley. The cardiovascular room includes top-of-the-line Precor elliptical trainers, treadmills, step machines and stationary bicycles. All cardio machines are equipped with flat panel screens and iPod docks. Complimentary headphones are available.

Spa Frequently Asked Questions
When should I arrive?
For your enhanced comfort and relaxation, we suggest that you arrive 15-30 minutes before your scheduled treatment.
What about my jewelry and valuables?
Lockers are available for personal items, however if you are a guest of the hotel, it is recommended that you leave valuable items in the safe in your guest room.
Should I shave before a treatment?
Shaving is recommended but not necessary for gentleman prior to your facial. If you choose to shave prior to your facial, please do so at least two hours before your appointment. Shaving is not recommended prior to body treatments or hair removal services.
What should I wear during my treatment?
During all treatments, the body is fully draped, except for the area being worked on. Robes and slippers are provided. All treatments can be enjoyed by both male & female guests.
What about my comfort?
Our spa environment is one of relaxation and tranquility, for this reason we request that all cell-phones and pagers in the spa be switched off. Your spa experience is your time, and you should delight in it to the fullest. If you experience discomfort due to room temperature, massage pressure, music volume or any other condition, please notify your therapist immediately.
What if I have special health considerations?
When making a reservation please notify the agent of any medical conditions, allergies, physical aliments or disabilities, and if you are pregnant.
What if I am pregnant?
Select services may be enjoyed during pregnancy and only on women that are 13-36 weeks pregnant.
Spa Policies
Access to Facilities
Use of facilities is exclusively for guests of The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch, Bachelor Gulch Club members and individuals who are enjoying a treatment on the day of their treatment. All individuals must be 18 years of age or older.
Minimum Age
The Ritz-Carlton Bachelor Gulch Spa services and facilities are for guests 18 years of age or older.
Late Policy
To enhance your spa experience, please arrive 30 minutes before your scheduled treatment. This will allow you time to relax in the sauna and steam rooms. Arriving late will simply limit the time of your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest will not be delayed. The full value of your treatment will be charged.
Cancellation Policy
If you must reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify the spa 24 hours in advance to avoid being charged a service fee.
Gratuities
For your convenience a suggested 22% service charge, based on the full price of each treatment will be added to your bill.
Payment
All major credit cards are accepted. You may also charge services to your hotel bill. All prices are quoted in USD
