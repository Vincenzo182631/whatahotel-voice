Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch
Explore our Beaver Creek, Colorado hotel
One of North America's top hotels and Colorado's premier luxury ski resort, The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch is nestled among the majestic Colorado Rocky Mountains and Beaver Creek ski mountain. This year-round destination resort offers outdoor adventure and choice amenities. Enjoy ski-in/ski-out access in winter, numerous summer mountain activities and an array of sophisticated dining options. Our luxury hotel in Beaver Creek, Colorado​, offers access to world-renowned skiing as well as a winter wonderland of mountain adventures - including snowmobiling, snowshoe hikes and more. The 21,000 square-foot spa and fitness center features a comprehensive array of treatments, a manicure/pedicure salon, saunas, steam rooms, hot and cold plunge pools, expansive slope-slide 24-hour fitness center, Pilates and yoga studios and year-round outdoor heated pool and whirlpools. The legendary service of The Ritz-Carlton will create indelible memories throughout your stay at this luxury resort in Beaver Creek, Colorado​.
Mountain Luxury Hotel Rooms and Suites
Rooms and Suites
Drawing inspiration from the mountains and the earth, our luxurious rooms and suites are a translation of a modern mountain lodge, with wood accents and furnishings that bring the organic elements of the mountains indoors. Our spacious Ritz-Carlton Suite, Executive Suite and 1-and 2-bedroom Residential Suites offer unique and spacious layouts with additional amenities. Enjoy breathtaking views of the mountain or valley from all rooms.

Unique and Memorable Meetings & Events
Surrounded by the natural beauty of the Rocky Mountains, nestled at the base of Beaver Creek resort, The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch offers the ideal setting for meetings, executive retreats, incentive trips, and training sessions. Events at our hotel in Beaver Creek, Colorado, can be hosted indoors, where 15,000 sq. ft. of meeting space includes features such as Juliet balconies and floor-to-ceiling windows, or outside with venues such as the valley-view Skiers Bridge. The resort is a complete meeting destination with a host of specialty services including portrait photography sessions, wellness workshops, and team golf or ski outings.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch. The daily valet fee is $60.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch is Eagle County Airport (EGE). EGE is located approximately 28.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
