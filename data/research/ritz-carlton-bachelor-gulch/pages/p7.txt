The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Beaver Creek hotel Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities, and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Club Lounge Temporary Closure
The Club Lounge is closed Sept. 7 – Nov. 19; re-opening Nov. 20.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay at our luxury Beaver Creek resort with Club Lounge access by booking a room or suite which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.

In Good Taste
Daily Culinary Presentations
Five times each day, the Club Lounge offers complimentary food and beverage presentations. These daily offerings — breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea, hors d’oeuvres and cordials — tell the destination’s story through locally sourced ingredients, iconic dishes and signature drinks. Daily Culinary Presentations: Breakfast: 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM, Lunch: 12:00 PM - 2:00 PM, Light Snacks: 2:30 PM - 4:00 PM, Hors D'oeuvres 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM, Dessert and Cordials: 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM
