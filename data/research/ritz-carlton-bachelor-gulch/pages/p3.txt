Enjoy Our Epicurean Experiences in Beaver Creek
Dining
There's an inviting aura emanating from each of the Beaver Creek family restaurants and lounges at the Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch in Beaver Creek, Colorado. Stacked stone, rich wood, nature-inspired artwork, a crackling 3-story stone fireplace and lounge-worthy seating set the mood for vibrant regional cuisine, a contemporary Japanese sushi restaurant, barrel-aged cocktails, après ski fare and robust coffee. Dates and hours of operation subject to change.

Curated Dining Experiences
Enhance your stay with an exceptional collection of culinary and beverage experiences at our luxury restaurants in Beaver Creek, Colorado. From intimate private dining and picturesque gourmet picnics to expert-led mixology, wine, whisky, and sake tastings, each offering is thoughtfully curated to create unforgettable moments. Indulge in curated picnic baskets, VIP couch service on Mountainside Terrace, and an array of exclusive experiences designed to elevate your itinerary with the finest in luxury and personalized service.
Edge of WYLD
The Edge of WYLD is our private dining room at the heart of the resort. For up to 12 guests, delight in a unique and intimate culinary experience that's customized to your desires. Whether celebrating a milestone with friends and family or gathering with colleagues, this private dining experience will enliven your Rocky Mountain escape.
Private Dining Inquiry
Anderson’s Cabin
Nestled above The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch, Anderson's Cabin is the original home of John Anderson, one of seven bachelors who settled the area in the 1900s. Boasting sweeping views of Colorado's Beaver Creek, Anderson's Cabin offers an exclusive private dining experience for up to 10 guests.
Private Dining Inquiry
VIP Couch Experience
Reserve a couch at the Fireside Bar for an elevated alpine experience, complete with your favorite culinary selections and handcrafted beverages. Throughout the day, guests may also reserve heated seating on the Mountainside Terrace, offering a cozy retreat to unwind in comfort while taking in the breathtaking mountain vistas.

Picnic on the Grand Lawn
Gather with family and friends for a magical evening immersed in the beauty of the outdoors. This elevated picnic dinner features a thoughtfully curated family-style menu by our Chef, showcasing signature dishes inspired by WYLD's distinctive culinary offerings. Relax, connect, and savor exceptional cuisine under the open sky for a memorable and uniquely mountain-inspired dining experience.
Learn More
Curated Picnic Basket
Each personalized basket includes a selection of artisan sandwiches, seasonal fresh fruit, premium cheeses and crackers, and your choice of sparkling water or soda. Wine or canned cocktails can also be selected (where permitted). In addition we provide plates, glassware, cutlery, napkins, wet wipes, and waste disposal bags. Simply pick up your picnic and enjoy a beautifully prepared experience.
Learn More
Mixology, Wine, Whiskey & Sake Experiences
Join our beverage experts for an immersive tasting experience that explores the rich history, craftsmanship, and stories behind the cocktails, wines, whiskeys, and sake featured throughout the hotel. Through guided tastings and engaging conversation, you'll deepen your appreciation for each selection while discovering the techniques
and traditions that make them exceptional.
