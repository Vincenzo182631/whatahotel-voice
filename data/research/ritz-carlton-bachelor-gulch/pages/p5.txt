Elevated Ski-in, Ski-out Property in Beaver Creek
The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch Ski Experience
Experience the ultimate setting for a winter escape, with unparalleled ski-in, ski-out access, via the Bachelor Gulch Express Lift, to more than 2,000 acres of pristine terrain on Beaver Creek Mountain. Beaver Creek Mountain provides the ideal winter getaway from families exploring the expert grooming in the learning-friendly terrain of Bachelor Gulch and McCoy Park to adrenaline junkies checking off the best steep slopes. Some of Beaver Creek Mountain's best kept secrets are the bumps, glades and cliffs, where extreme skiers can tackle steeps, moguls, or powder stashes in the glades in Bachelor Gulch, Grouse Mountain and the world-famous Birds of Prey. There is truly something for every skiing ability and desire and The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch is ideally situated to provide unmatched access.
The Ultimate Convenience in Skiing
Upon arrival, our ski valet will transport your ski and snowboard equipment to our slopeside storage, where it will be available when you start your day on the mountain. Return your equipment at the end of the day and slide back into your slippers. Purchase lift tickets in advance or at our on-property ticket office. Beaver Creek Sports, also located on property, provides ski and snowboard rentals for all ages and abilities. Choose between Sport, Performance and Demo packages. A full-service retail store, Beaver Creek Sports features the latest in on-mountain apparel, gear and off-mountain après ski attire.

Elevated Après Ski Experience
Après Ski With Live Music
After a day on the mountain, gather with friends and family to relax and share stories with a signature cocktail, hot chocolate and light bites in The Great Room or on the Mountainview Terrace soaking in the views. During the week, après features live acoustic music, while the après party scene on the weekends (starting in January) is the place to be with live DJ's spinning tracks under the sun.

Beaver Creek Ski & Snowboard School
Explore more of the mountain with confidence this winter with Beaver Creek's Ski & Snowboard School. With options for both group or private lessons for children and adults, Beaver Creek Ski & Snowboard School has curated experiences to fit every age, ability, schedule and skill set. Advanced reservations are required. For more information, contact Beaver Creek Ski & Snowboard School.

We Make Skiing Easy for Families
Ski Nanny Program
Our Ski Nanny Program provides a seamless experience for your children’s ski lessons, giving you more time on the slopes. Designed for parents with children participating in full-day Beaver Creek Group Ski School lessons, we provide transportation to and from the lesson, along with chaperoned drop-off and pick-up in Beaver Creek. Before their day on the slopes, your child's day starts with a light breakfast at Ritz Kids, and after pick-up at the end of the lesson, an afternoon snack is provided. Advance reservations required. Ritz Kids closed until the Winter Season.
