Settle Into Our Bachelor Gulch Accommodations in Colorado
Suites & Rooms
Our luxury accommodations in Beaver Creek, Colorado, are a sophisticated translation of a modern mountain lodge, incorporating stone fireplaces, wood accents and refreshed furnishings that complement the surrounding environment. Our Avon, CO, hotel rooms and suites each offer unique layouts with views of Beaver Creek Mountain or the Vail Valley.

Retreat to a Penthouse or Residential Suite
The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch presents an exceptional collection of refined accommodations, thoughtfully designed to suit gatherings of any size. From elegantly appointed one-bedroom and two-bedroom Residences to expansive three-bedroom and 4-bedroom Penthouses, each offers a distinct sense of space and style - seamlessly paired with the legendary service of The Ritz-Carlton.

Elevated Accommodations
Drawing inspiration from the mountains and the earth, the stylish interiors of our luxury Beaver Creek hotel rooms and suites evoke a natural feel that brings the organic elements of the mountains indoors.
The Ritz-Carlton Club Level
The Club Level at our Beaver Creek resort offers the finest personal service and comfort. Guests with Club Lounge Access enjoy an endless array of beverage and culinary offerings throughout the day. These luxury Beaver Creek accommodations include various types of rooms which may be combined to create two-bedroom suites as well as The Ritz-Carlton Suite. Some room types include private balconies with Beaver Creek Mountain or Vail Valley views and in-room fireplaces (based on availability). The Club Lounge is closed Sept. 7 – Nov. 19; re-opening Nov. 20.

The Ritz-Carlton Suite
This 1,800-square-foot suite offers refined, residential-style luxury, featuring expansive living areas, a stone fireplace, private study, and an elegant dining room complemented by a butler’s pantry. A spacious balcony showcases sweeping panoramic views of Beaver Creek Mountain. Whether hosting family or gathering with friends, The Ritz-Carlton Suite provides an exceptional setting to relax, entertain, and take in the breathtaking alpine scenery.
