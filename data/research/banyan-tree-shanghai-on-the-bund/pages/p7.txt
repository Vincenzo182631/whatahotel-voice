### Meeting Facilities and Ballroom

With two meeting rooms and one ballroom that can host up to 150 people, our event venues come with complementary event coordinator services, executive amenities and state-of-the-art presentation equipment. The facilities are flexible and provide the perfect setting for accommodating both intimate and large-scale meetings. Choose to host a private reception, an extravagant banquet or a sophisticated gala at our hotel.

### Fitness Club

Take part in a soulful yoga asana in the Yoga Studio, or keep your body in top shape in the fitness centre offering state-of-the-art equipment in a quiet and relaxing atmosphere.

### Indoor Swimming Pools and Relaxation Pools

With an indoor pool and three relaxation pools, you can start each day relaxing or working out in cool, clear waters.
