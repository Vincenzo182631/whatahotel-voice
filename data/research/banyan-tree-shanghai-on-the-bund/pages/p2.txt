## An Urban Luxury Retreat in the Heart of Shanghai

A fine urban resort on the bund, with spectacular views of the Huangpu river and the iconic skyline from all of its 130 rooms and suites. This hotel accommodation in Shanghai is one you wouldn't want to miss.

\*Kindly note that effective from 1 July 2019, the Shanghai Municipal Administration of Culture and Tourism has issued a regulation to minimize and reduce the use of disposable items in the tourism and accommodation industry. Eleven disposable items including toothbrush, shaving kit, comb, shower cap, nail file, cotton pads/buds, sanitary bag, loofah, shoe mitt, sewing kit and mouthwash, will not be proactively provided by the hotel in guestrooms. These disposable items are available upon request during your stay. Thank you for your understanding.
