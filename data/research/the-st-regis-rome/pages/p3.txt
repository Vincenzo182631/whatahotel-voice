###### A Patrimony of Art. A Legacy of Service.

## The St. Regis Rome

A grand palace from 1894, inviting you to explore its curated collection of fine art and antiques, handcrafted stucco-adorned staircase, the historic lift, the glittering Murano chandeliers and the majestic symbol of La Belle Époque - The Ritz Ballroom. High-ceilinged and light-filled, the guestrooms and suites are styled with luxurious Rubelli fabrics, handmade glass and collectable curios - effortlessly sophisticated and distinctively urbane. Cleverly designed to combine spirited, high-end detailing with traditional artistry, each room has a distinctly residential demeanor. The full-scale restoration includes all of the hotel's 132 guest rooms and 29 opulent suites, elegantly layered with pastel hues that breathe new life into each space. Immerse yourself in our richly furnished rooms to capture the exquisite Roman spirit, featuring beautiful proportions and a unique décor.
