Discover Kimpton Aysla Mallorca, a boutique resort where Mediterranean calm meets contemporary luxury, just minutes from Palma. Surrounded by gardens and natural light, it’s a place to unwind, recharge and move at your own pace.
Start the day with an energising workout in our 24-hour gym, followed by a relaxed breakfast with almond horchata. Drift between three peaceful pools, including Mallorca’s largest indoor pool, or relax deeply at the award-winning CODAGE spa.

Near T Golf and Palma’s beaches, the resort also hosts memorable gatherings, from private celebrations to creative meetings. As the sun sets, gather over organic wines, crafted cocktails and Mediterranean flavours.
At Kimpton Aysla Mallorca, life flows differently: lighter, slower and just as it should.

### Eclipse Sundowner Experience

Join us for a three night retreat perfectly timed to witness the spectacular solar eclipse on 12th August. Indulge in exquisite dining on arrival. Enjoy the eclipse on board private ship and spa treatment prior to departure.

Learn more

### Embrace Wellness at Our CODAGE Spa

Relax and rejuvenate at the luxurious Maison CODAGE Spa at our Mallorca hotel. Our skilled therapists will pamper you with luxurious treatments featuring CODAGE PARIS products known for their exceptional effectiveness and quality. Step into a tranquil oasis and experience a new level of well-being as you unwind and revitalize.

Learn more

### Wining + Dining Excellence

Our Mallorca hotel offers three exceptional dining options. SABA Restaurant combines Korean-Japanese cuisines with fresh ingredients and progressive techniques. SABA Bar & Bodega offers a chic indoor-outdoor bar atmosphere. Zayt Pool Kitchen & Garden Deli provides a delightful poolside experience with casual Mallorca bites and classic cocktails.

Learn more

### Sip, Swim, and Soak in the Sun

Discover your personal oasis at our hotel within the lush native gardens of Mallorca. Bask in the warm Mediterranean sun and choose from a selection of outdoor pools for a refreshing dip. When you're ready to relax, unwind at our stylish Med Rest and Zayt.

Learn more

### Your Vision + Our Venue = Event Perfection

Host your Aysla Mallorca event in our luxury hotel with five flexible meeting rooms, a spacious ballroom for up to 200 guests, and a stunning rooftop terrace. Offering an irresistible blend of Mediterranean flair and Kimpton charisma, we make group gatherings, meetings and events, incentives, and destination weddings extra special.

## FAQs for Aysla Mallorca

Does Kimpton Aysla Mallorca have a pool?

Kimpton Aysla Mallorca has 1 indoor and 1 outdoor pools.

Indoor Pool

Introducing La Cala, our indoor heated swimming pool complete with hydromassage pool. Children below 16 years old can access between 10:30 to 12:00 and 14:00 to 15:30. Outside these hours, La Cala is exclusively for adult use.

Outdoor Pool

Your oasis nestled amongst the native Mallorca gardens. Lounge in the Mediterranean sunshine and take a plunge in your choice of outdoor pools. When you're ready, recline and dine at our poolside restaurant and bar.

Pool information

How much is parking at Kimpton Aysla Mallorca?

At Kimpton Aysla Mallorca, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 9 EUR and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 9 EUR .

All facilities are at your disposal to enhance your stay at Kimpton Aysla Mallorca, such as our on-site parking. We also have four electric vehicle charging stations available at additional cost.

Parking fee: 9 EUR per day

Vehicles allowed: Bus, Trailer, Truck

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Aysla Mallorca?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Aysla Mallorca.

Pet policy description. We welcome four legged guests with as much enthusiasm as we do their humans. If you plan on bringing the pup, just let us know in advance, we'll make sure the room is set to impress.

Pet fee per night: 0 EUR

Pet damage deposit: 0 EUR

Pet weight limit: No weight limit per pet

NO\_LIMIT pets allowed

Pets allowed: All pets allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Aysla Mallorca for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Aysla Mallorca have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Aysla Mallorca has 3 restaurants.

When is breakfast at Kimpton Aysla Mallorca?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Aysla Mallorca?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Aysla Mallorca.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Aysla Mallorca?

Check-in at Kimpton Aysla Mallorca is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Aysla Mallorca offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Aysla Mallorca offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Aysla Mallorca from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Aysla Mallorca is conveniently located near Aeropuerto de Palma de Mallorca Son Sant Joan (PMI), approximately 18.02 miles away (29 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Aysla Mallorca?

Kimpton Aysla Mallorca is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Mallorca Country Club
- Port Andriano Beach
- Golf Santa Ponsa
- Palma De Mallorca Old Town
- Castell De Bellver

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Aysla Mallorca puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
