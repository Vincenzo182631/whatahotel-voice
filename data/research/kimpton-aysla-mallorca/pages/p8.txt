# Kimpton Aysla MallorcaLocation

In Southwest Mallorca, Port Adriano, Portals Nous, Palma, our luxury hotel offers a serene escape near Port Adriano and Portals Nous beaches, Golf Santa Ponsa, and Mallorca Country Club. Discover adventure with our hotel shuttle to Port Adriano.

## Things to do

### Mallorca Country Club

Carrer dels Tords, s/n, 07180, Santa Ponça, Illes Balears,,

Across the street from our hotel, Mallorca Country Club offers an array of recreational activities. From tennis matches on well-maintained courts, to padel, and pickleball, this club caters to sports enthusiasts. Afterward, relax and savor Mediterranean cuisine at the on-site restaurant. With its serene surroundings, it's the perfect place for leisure and fitness. Book your leisure and fitness experience or lessons with a coach through our reception and reservations team.

### Port Andriano Beach

El Toro, s/n 07180, Calvià Baleares,

Discover the allure of Port Andriano Beach in Mallorca, Spain, where leisure and luxury meet Mediterranean charm. Savor a culinary journey with 11 restaurants offering flavors from around the world. Elevate your style with top-notch fashion shopping along the promenade. For adventure seekers, the beach offers diving, paddle surfing, kayaking, sailing, and snorkeling courses. Explore with ease from our hotel which provides a convenient shuttle service to this vibrant and sun-kissed destination.

### Golf Santa Ponsa

Carrer Riu Sil, s/n, 07180, Santa Ponça, Illes Balears,,

Golf Santa Ponsa, connected to our hotel, offers three exceptional golf courses set against a backdrop of Mallorca's natural beauty. Tee off on meticulously maintained fairways and greens while enjoying Mediterranean scenery. The club features a clubhouse with dining options, making it a perfect destination for golfers seeking challenging courses and breathtaking scenery. Golf lessons, driving range and rounds of golf are bookable with our hotel’s reception and reservation team.

### Palma De Mallorca Old Town

Various locations, within Palma de Mallorca Old Town, Illes Balears,

Palma de Mallorca's Old Town, with its historic charm and narrow streets, invites you to explore its rich heritage. Visit iconic landmarks such as La Seu Cathedral and Almudaina Palace. Wander through the labyrinthine alleyways, discover hidden courtyards, and indulge in local cuisine at quaint restaurants. This captivating district showcases Mallorca's history and culture in every corner.

### Castell De Bellver

Camilo José Cela, s/n, 07014, Palma, Illes Balears,,

Perched on a hill overlooking Palma de Mallorca, Castell de Bellver is a Gothic-style castle with a circular design. This architectural gem houses a museum where you can explore the island's history, art, and archaeology. As you wander through the castle and its surrounding park, you'll be treated to panoramic views of Palma Bay. A visit to Castell de Bellver is a journey through time and beauty.
