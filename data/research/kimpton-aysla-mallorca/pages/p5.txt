### Embrace Wellness at Our CODAGE Spa

Relax and rejuvenate at the luxurious Maison CODAGE Spa at our Mallorca hotel. Our skilled therapists will pamper you with luxurious treatments featuring CODAGE PARIS products known for their exceptional effectiveness and quality. Step into a tranquil oasis and experience a new level of well-being as you unwind and revitalize.

Learn more

### Sip, Swim, and Soak in the Sun

Discover your personal oasis at our hotel within the lush native gardens of Mallorca. Bask in the warm Mediterranean sun and choose from a selection of outdoor pools for a refreshing dip. When you're ready to relax, unwind at our stylish Med Rest and Zayt.

Learn more

### A Pet-friendly Paradise

Pets are family too, and we're thrilled to accommodate them, regardless of size, weight, or breed, at no additional cost. Whether you're traveling with a furry friend, a feathered companion, or a scaly sidekick, your four-legged, winged, or scaled family member is invited to enjoy a comfortable and memorable stay with us in beautiful Mallorca.

### Sustainability

#### Sustainability Certification

- Our hotel has a third-party sustainability certification.

**Certifications: Green Key (FEE)**

Our hotel is certified with Green Key (FEE), a globally recognised eco-label run by the Foundation for Environmental Education that is awarded to tourism and hospitality facilities that demonstrate outstanding commitment to environmental sustainability. Certified hotels must have implemented a range of measures across multiple categories including energy (e.g. energy-saving lightbulbs), water (e.g. water-saving taps, showers, and toilets) and waste (food waste and single-use plastic reduction schemes).
