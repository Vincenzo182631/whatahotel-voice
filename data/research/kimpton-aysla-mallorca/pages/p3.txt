### SABA Bar & Bodega

On entrance to our main restaurant, Saba is a wonderful and iconic destination bar and lounge. Enjoy pre, post, anytime drinks at our back bar with over 400 bottles to choose from, ensures guests want for nothing. Our iconic indoor outdoor bar allows guests to enjoy year round with seating for both in and out. During the day, great coffees, teas and sweets transform into an evening drinking spot with amazing cocktails, beer on tap and the adjoining wine cellar to ensure the best wine offering.
