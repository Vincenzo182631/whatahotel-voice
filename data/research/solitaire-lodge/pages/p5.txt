# Tarawera Suite

The Tarawera Suite features a spectacular uninterrupted view from the tranquil bay, across the glassy lake and up to the majestic summit of Mount Tarawera

70 SQM + Decks

The Tarawera Suite features floor to ceiling glass that yields a spectacular uninterrupted view from the tranquil bay, across the glassy lake and the majestic summit of Mount Tarawera. This suite features a generous bedroom, with super king bed and relaxing sitting area. Adjoining the bedroom is a seperate lounge with dining table and easy chairs. A Private balcony is perfectly positioned to take in one of New Zealand’s most spectacular views.

Enjoy a drink at the lodge bar or outdoor terrace, whilst taking in the spectacular lake and mountain vista. Dining is is always a highlight at Solitiare Lodge, our full board tarrif inludes a full country breakfast, light lunch, pre dinner drinks and a 5 course evening meal.
