# Stay

# Luxurious suites feature floor to ceiling windows and private balconies with a stunning vista over Lake Tarawera.

Solitaire Lodge has nine luxurious suites set amongst lush native bush on a private peninsula. The subtle design brings the outdoors in, inviting you to unwind and soak up the peace and quiet of this tranquil setting.

Enjoy panoramic views over Lake Tarawera’s still deep waters and towards the magnificent volcanic crators of Mount Tarawera.

Solitaire Lodge’s Full Board Tariff includes accommodation, complimentary mini bar with extensive selection of alcoholic and non alcoholic beverages, pre dinner drinks & canapés, five course evening meal, full country breakfast and light lunch.
