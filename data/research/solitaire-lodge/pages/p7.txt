# Solitaire Suite

The Solitaire Suite offers peace, total privacy and a breathtaking outlook through dramatic picture windows across the glassy lake and towards Mount Tarawera

1 bedroom (75 SQM) or 2 bedroom (88 SQM) + deck

Set in a separate building, this 1 or 2 bedroom suite offers peace, total privacy and an expansive outlook through picture windows across glassy Lake Tarawera and towards Mount Tarawera. Guests will enjoy a large master bedroom with ensuite bathroom, separate lounge and private outdoor deck. The second bedroom can be configured as a Super King or 2 single beds. Both bedrooms enjoy access to a second bathroom.

Enjoy a drink at the lodge bar or outdoor terrace, whilst taking in the spectacular lake and mountain vista. Dining is is always a highlight at Solitiare Lodge, our full board tarrif inludes a full country breakfast, light lunch, pre dinner drinks and a 5 course evening meal.

### Executive Suite

Our 40 SQM Executive Suites offer picturesque views over Lake Tarawera, lavish super king beds, comfortable chairs for lounging, picture windows and a private balcony with stunning vista.

### Premium Suite

Our 40 SQM Premium Suite offers spectacular and sweeping views over Lake Tarawera and the mountain, lavish super king beds, comfortable chairs for lounging, picture windows and a private balcony with stunning vista.

### Tarawera Suite

The 70 SQM The Tarawera Suite features uninterrupted views from the tranquil bay up to the majestic summit of Mount Tarawera. Guest will enjoy a generous bedroom and a separate lounge/dining area.

This stand alone 1 bedroom (75 SQM) or 2 bedroom (108 SQM) Villa Suite is all about space, large bedrooms, spacious living / dining area and private balcony overlooking the Bay. Ideal for families, groups or a spacious couple’s retreat.
