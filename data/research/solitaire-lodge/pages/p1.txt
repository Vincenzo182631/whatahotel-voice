# Solitaire Lodge enjoys breathtaking views from a private peninsula over Lake Tarawera, lush natural bush setting and dramatic volcanic backdrop.

Solitaire Lodge Rotorua, one of New Zealand’s premiere luxury lodges is the perfect place to simply relax, indulge and rejuvenate, left in the able hands of your warm friendly New Zealand hosts.

Large picture windows bring the outdoors in, inviting you to unwind and soak up the peace and quiet of this tranquil setting.

Our daily changing menus are a New Zealand taste experience, showcasing the best of local and seasonal produce.

Minutes from the lodge you will discover a multitude of premium activities set amongst stunning natural lakes and volcanic landscapes.

Solitaire Lodge is a member of Luxury Lodges of New Zealand and was recently awarded a Michelin Key for 2026.

### Full Board Inclusions

Solitaire Lodge’s Full Board Tariff includes:

- Accommodation
- Complimentary mini bar with extensive selection of snacks, alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages
- Pre dinner drinks & canapés
- Five course evening meal (wine matches extra)
- Full country breakfast
- Light lunch
- Complimentary WIFI
- Use of Lodge dinghies and kayaks

## Experiences

Moments from the Lodge you will discover the essence of unspoiled Lake Tarawera which offers an array of premium activities in a natural New Zealand lake setting.

Walk to a 65-metre waterfall or soak and unwind in natural thermal springs. Embark on an adventurous scenic helicopter flight over the surrounding volcanic landscape and land on a volcano. Catch a good-sized rainbow trout for dinner or explore tranquil waters in a kayak or motorized dinghy. Easy lake access allows exploration of surrounding waters and secluded private bays.
