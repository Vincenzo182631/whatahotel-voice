## Location

Lefay Resort & SPA Dolomiti is set in the splendid scenery of the Dolomites, declared by UNESCO a “World Heritage Site”.

## MADONNA DI CAMPIGLIO SKI AREA

150 km of slopes, 4 snowboard parks and a single ski pass valid for the entire ski area, the biggest in Trentino. The region is known for hosting sports competitions such as the Alpine Ski World Cup. The Resort is provided with a Ski Room and an internal ski shop where Guests can hire equipment or buy snow accessories and ski passes. Guests can also take advantage of a free private transfer to and from Pinzolo ski lifts.

## EXPERIENCES

An innate beauty which can be experienced in every season: in winter glaciers and snow-capped peaks are the ideal destination for skiers of all levels; while in summer the idyllic green mountains of the Dolomites provide the perfect sets for hiker. Two golf courses (9 holes) are located just a few minutes from the Resort and those who are looking for adventures may choose among horse riding and mountain bike excursions.
