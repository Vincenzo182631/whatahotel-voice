### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Phoenix Downtown?

The check-in time at The Westin Phoenix Downtown is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Phoenix Downtown allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Phoenix Downtown. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Phoenix Downtown?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Phoenix Downtown. Self parking is available for $36.00 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near The Westin Phoenix Downtown. The daily valet fee is $46.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Phoenix Downtown have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Phoenix Downtown has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Phoenix Downtown booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Phoenix Downtown?

The closest airport near The Westin Phoenix Downtown is Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX). PHX is located approximately 3.9 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Phoenix Downtown have an airport shuttle to Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX)?

No, The Westin Phoenix Downtown does not have an airport shuttle to Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport.

Does The Westin Phoenix Downtown have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Phoenix Downtown does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
