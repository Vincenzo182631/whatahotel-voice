# Experiences Await You

## Experiences

Discover the perfect balance of relaxation, wellness, and adventure at The Westin Phoenix Downtown. On property, unwind by the pool, energize in the WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio, or gather in our Game Room Parlor Suite. Step outside to explore downtown Phoenix on foot, via Light Rail, or ride-share, and enjoy sports, theaters, live music, galleries, museums, events, and vibrant dining and nightlife. For outdoor enthusiasts, hike scenic trails, tee off at nearby golf courses, or take a day trip across Arizona to Sedona, national parks or a sunrise hot air balloon adventure, each experience is designed to recharge, inspire, and elevate your stay

Play, Connect, Repeat

## Game Room Parlor Suite Package

Work and play come together effortlessly in Game Room Parlor Suites at The Westin Phoenix Downtown. Challenge friends or colleagues to a game of pool, dive into classic board games, or set the vibe with a portable speaker. A tech-enabled conference table seats up to eight, making it easy to shift from strategy to celebration. Kitchenettes keep food and beverages close at hand, while catering or In-Room Dining delivered to the suite lets you focus on fun. Whether hosting a game night, casual meeting, or post-event gathering, this playful space is designed for connection, laughter, and memorable moments in the heart of downtown Phoenix.

Phoenix Sports

## Root for the Home Team- Phoenix Sports

Experience the excitement of Phoenix sports from The Westin Phoenix Downtown, ideally located near the city’s premier venues. Just a short walk from the hotel, cheer on at the Mortgage Matchup Center or catch a game at Chase Field. From Football to Cactus League Spring Training, there’s always a game to inspire your day. Whether you’re a devoted fan or looking for a lively outing, downtown Phoenix offers unforgettable sports experiences just steps or a short ride from the hotel, making it easy to celebrate every victory and savor every moment.

Local Things To Do

### Orphuem Theatre

Step into the historic Orpheum Theatre, a downtown Phoenix landmark just moments from The Westin Phoenix Downtown. Experience Broadway shows, concerts, and live performances in an elegant, timeless setting that blends rich history with modern entertainment for a cultural escape in the heart of the city.

Visit Website

### Arizona State University

Just one block from The Westin Phoenix Downtown, Arizona State University’s downtown campus and Law School put you at the heart of campus life. Whether visiting for lectures, events, or campus tours, our central location makes it effortless to explore, connect, and experience the energy of ASU while enjoying the comfort and wellness-focused amenities of the hotel.

Visit Website

### Rosevelt Row

Bursting with color, creativity, and energy, Roosevelt Row is less than a mile from The Westin Phoenix Downtown and just a 9-minute ride on the Light Rail. Explore vibrant street art, galleries, boutique shops, and lively cafés, or experience local events and nightlife. This walkable arts district invites you to immerse yourself in the city’s culture while enjoying the convenience of downtown living.

Visit Website

### The Pemberton

Gather at this stylish downtown retreat where handcrafted cocktails, local spirits, and small plates set the stage for connection. With a lively atmosphere, modern design, and curated flavors, it’s the perfect place to toast the city, unwind after exploring Phoenix, or savor a night out with friends.

Visit Website

### Sedona National Park

Escape the city and discover the breathtaking beauty of Sedona National Park. A two-hour scenic drive from The Westin Phoenix Downtown, Sedona offers red rock vistas, hiking trails, and serene landscapes perfect for a day trip of adventure, reflection, and connection with nature—all easily accessible from your downtown base

Visit Website

### Local Downtown Events

Experience the energy of downtown Phoenix with a calendar full of live music, festivals, cultural celebrations, and citywide events. From sports and theater to art walks and seasonal happenings, there’s always something to explore. Stay at The Westin Phoenix Downtown and immerse yourself in the city’s vibrant culture, just moments from the action.

Visit Website

### Westin Wellness

Maintain your wellness routine at The Westin Phoenix Downtown with our WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio, equipped for every level of activity. Pedal on Peloton® bikes, strengthen with TRX and free weights, or get moving on treadmills and ellipticals. After your workout speed up recovery with HyperVolt massage tools, then refresh and unwind at the Lapis Pool, a heated outdoor oasis where you can soak up the Arizona sun and take in sweeping downtown views. Whether focusing on fitness, recovery, or simple relaxation, our wellness offerings are designed to help you move well, feel balanced, and make the most of every moment in the city.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Phoenix Downtown have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Phoenix Downtown offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Treadmills, Peloton, free weights, TRX, HyperVolt, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Phoenix Downtown have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Phoenix Downtown has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Phoenix Downtown?

Phoenix Zoo, Papago Park, The Japanese Friendship Garden of Phoenix, South Mountain Park and Preserve, Desert Botanical Gardens and more are nearby The Westin Phoenix Downtown.
