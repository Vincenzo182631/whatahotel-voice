# Rest Well in Downtown Phoenix

## Accommodations

Stretch out in the largest rooms in Downtown Phoenix, where every room features floor-to-ceiling windows with panoramic city views and warm, natural light. Sink into a plush Heavenly® Bed and wind down...See More naturally with our Sleep Well Lavender Balm. Begin every day refreshed with our White Tea Aloe bath amenities, and extra-large luxury bath sheets. Thoughtfully curated amenities from in-room coffee to 24-hour room service make each stay effortless. Share your wellness experience with your best four-legged friend in our Pet-Friendly accommodations, or upgrade your stay to a Club-level room for access to our Club lounge featuring hot breakfast and a premium selection of food and beverages throughout the day.See Less

More Space. More Comfort

### Suites and Premium Rooms

Upgrade your stay with Premium rooms and suites at The Westin Phoenix Downtown, and experience spacious, light-filled retreats designed for comfort and connection. Choose a Corner Studio Suite for elevated city views, a One-Bedroom Suite for generous living space and privacy, or a Parlor Suite for gatherings, meetings, or family time. Premium rooms and suites feature our expansive marble Heavenly® Bath with soaking tub and custom-designed bathrobes, and thoughtful wellness-focused touches perfect for relaxing, working, or entertaining in downtown Phoenix.

### Pet Friendly

Your four-legged travel companion is welcome at The Westin Phoenix Downtown. Start your stay with a special canine treat, then explore downtown Phoenix together, sharing every moment of your wellness journey, knowing your pup is part of the adventure. One dog per room, up to 50 pounds; dogs cannot be left unattended. Additional fee applies.

Meet in Comfort

## Parlor Suites

Our conference suites at The Westin Phoenix Downtown offer versatile spaces designed to inspire productivity, connection, and relaxation. Perfect for meetings, client presentations, or small gatherings, each suite features flexible layouts, modern amenities, kitchenettes to store food and drinks, and comfortable seating. Thoughtful touches from natural light and ergonomic workspaces to in-suite refreshments create an environment where work flows effortlessly. When it’s time to recharge, unwind on plush seating or retreat to private corners for a moment of calm. Whether entertaining, collaborating, or resting between engagements, our suites provide a seamless balance of business and comfort in the heart of downtown Phoenix.
