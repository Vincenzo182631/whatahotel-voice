### Jacy and Dakotas

A desert oasis in the heart of downtown Phoenix, Jacy & Dakota’s offers a full day of dining experiences. Start your morning in a vibrant indoor/outdoor setting with a sit down or grab-and-go breakfast, or an assortment of delicious beverages from
the Coffee Bar. Enjoy a casual afternoon lunch, then return for Arizona-inspired libations including a unique blend of beers, crafted cocktails, ciders and...See More wine at happy hour. For dinner or late-night light bites, savor a menu of seasonal, modern American cuisine crafted from fresh, local ingredients. Connect and linger with friends over weekend brunch on our beautiful, dog-friendly patio and enjoy favorites including Mesquite Chicken and Waffles and Lemon Ricotta Pancakes while you soak in city views.See Less

### Coffee Bar at Jacy & Dakota’s

Fuel your day at our Coffee Bar with Provision Coffee specialty drinks, from a soothing Honey Lavender Latte to a Salted Caramel Cold Brew or a warming Maya Spiced Chai. Enjoy fresh-brewed favorites, including our Prickly Pear Blend or Quindio Decaf, crafted to energize, inspire, and elevate every moment of your morning.

### Amenties

Enhance your stay with a menu of curated in-room amenities delivered to your room. Choose wellness-forward favorites like the Eat Well® Antioxidant Trio with blueberries, walnuts, and dark chocolate, or enjoy an in-room happy hour with local Arizona craft beers, chips and housemade salsa, or set a romantic tone with sparkling wine and chocolate-covered strawberries—each designed to elevate your experience.

### In-room Dining

Dine at your own pace from 6 am to 11 pm daily. Whether savored in the comfort of your guest room or suite, or packaged-to-go, every meal fuels your day or night. Enjoy a menu of fresh, farm-to-table cuisine, including crowd-pleasing favorites and kid-friendly options.

### Craft Cocktails

Sip thoughtfully crafted cocktails at The Westin Phoenix Downtown, from the smoky Piestewa with Amaras Verde Mezcal and mango puree to the Jacy & Dakota’s featuring black tea–infused bourbon with prickly pear syrup. Each cocktail blends fresh, local flavors with modern artistry—perfect for unwinding, connecting, and savoring each sip.

### Eat Well Menu

Nourish your well-being with Westin’s Eat Well® Menus, crafted with balanced ingredients and mindful portion options. From energizing meals to kid-friendly favorites, each dish supports your lifestyle. For a restful night, try the Sleep Well Menu—curated with sleep-enhancing superfoods, available through In-Room Dining.

## Club Lounge

Begin your morning in The Club Lounge at The Westin Phoenix Downtown with a thoughtfully prepared continental breakfast buffet. Enjoy hot selections including herb-roasted potatoes, eggs, and bacon, alongside fresh fruit boards, pastries, cereal, oatmeal with a variety of toppings. Starbucks® coffee, juices, soda, and water are available to refresh and energize. Throughout the day, return for light snacks and beverages in this private, elevated setting—ideal for fueling your morning, connecting with clients, or taking a quiet moment to recharge.
