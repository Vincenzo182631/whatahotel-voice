Official Walt Disney World® Hotel

As an official _Walt Disney World_ ® hotel, guests of Waldorf Astoria Orlando can take advantage of attractive Disney benefits. From the 30-minute early entry to the Theme Parks to our luxury Concierge’s convenience to help you arrange your Disney visit, you can be sure to experience all the magic when you choose to stay with us.

Access all four _Walt Disney World_ ® Theme Parks and _Disney Springs_ ® with ease by taking our continuous motorcoach transportation. Pick-up is conveniently located at the porte-cochere.

## LIGHTNING LANE

Skip the standby line and join a shorter one for select attractions and experiences. Purchase passes days before your visit, for added confidence when you arrive! Guests at Waldorf Astoria Orlando have the ability to purchase Lightning Lane experiences starting 7 am (EST), three days prior to their visit. Lightning Lane passes can only be purchased via the My Disney Experience app.

### Cool Kids' Summer

Walt Disney World is gearing up for a summer filled with family-friendly fun, featuring refreshed attractions, new shows, and limited-time experiences across all four parks. Highlights include Bluey and Bingo, Toy Story and Goofy-led interactive fun, a new Disney Jr. Mickey Mouse Clubhouse Live! stage show, and updates to Buzz Lightyear’s Space Ranger Spin. Guests can also look forward to major additions like The Mandalorian and Grogu on Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run, a revamped Rock ‘n’ Roller Coaster starring The Muppets, and a reimagined Soarin’ Across America debuting by Memorial Day.
