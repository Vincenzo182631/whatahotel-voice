### Room Features

Richly appointed with the finest amenities, all rooms and suites feature Aesop bath products, custom-made furnishings, original Nova Scotia landscape paintings and ceramics, and a handcrafted walnut bar.

### Décor & Furnishings

Full-size handcrafted bar with cocktail mixing station and curated collection of local and international wine, beers and spirits. It also includes a Nespresso machine, tea kettle and refrigerator.

### Design

Discover panoramic windows with unprecedented water views, custom furniture and lighting designed and crafted in Canada, lighting and drapery with custom dimming options and rooms with curved white oak walls and walnut-panelled flooring.

### Executive Suites

With over 60 feet of window line, the Shannon and Thrumcap Suites offer immediate ocean views. These spacious suites feature a finely-appointed living room, dining area and walnut bar. Designed by Canada’s Studio Munge, these residential-style suites include one king-size bed, living room, dining area and a full-wall inset handcrafted walnut bar with a curated collection of local & international wines & spirits, Nespresso machine, fridge and double sink. These granite bathrooms boast harbour views and feature a wet room, floating tub, Aesop bath products and custom Nova Scotia bath salts.
