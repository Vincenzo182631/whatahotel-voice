# Welcome to Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax

## Refined, distinctly Nova Scotian hospitality

Located in the absolute centre of downtown in the vibrant Halifax waterfront, in the heart of the new Queen’s Marque district, Muir offers elevated hospitality inspired by the spirit, culture and natural...See More beauty of Nova Scotia. At the forefront of a flourishing culinary district, guests will find chef-driven contemporary dining and waterfront seating. Rooms and suites are designed with honest materials, bespoke furnishings and original art. Muir offers an enhanced workout and wellness experience featuring an extensive collection of premium cardio and weight equipment, spin and yoga studio, halotherapy salt room, vitality pool, eucalyptus steam room and infrared sauna. Muir’s spectacular oceanfront location, distinct venues and unique memory making spaces include a fully private art gallery, secret speakeasy with Halifax’s most sought-after waterfront terrace, intimate and expansive suites and for truly extraordinary gathering, a 36-foot private yacht.See Less

### Meetings and Events

Memorable gatherings start with an extraordinary setting. Muir’s spectacular oceanfront location, distinct venues and memorable spaces create one-of-a-kind settings for business events, celebrations and other significant occasions.

### Muir's Yacht and Motorboat

From Little Wing, Muir's 36-foot Morris yacht to Reach, our high-speed 24-foot motorboat, guests will get a rare perspective of this much-loved coastline. Exclusive scheduled excursions and tours will treat guests to a uniquely Muir experience and the beauty of Halifax's coastline. Make the most of Muir's unprecedented location and direct water access with our kayaks and bicycles.

### Caring, Considered Hospitality and Service

Gracious. Attentive. Refined. Intuitive. At Muir, you can expect impeccable service and thoughtful amenities including complimentary meeting room, car and driver service with hybrid Range Rover and 24-hour in-room dining.

### Queen's Marque District

A new district in an iconic and historic neighbourhood with unprecedented ocean access - so close, you can touch it. Queen's Marque is distinctly local, with dining, retail, an extensive art collection and cultural spaces that invite visitors and locals alike to connect with this place and each other.

### Wellness

Discover a sanctuary offering premium workout facilities, vitality spa pool and studio fitness classes. Recharge in our ultra-premium 5000 square foot fitness centre or take a reviving dip in our vitality spa pool. Optimize your workout regime in Halifax's most premium fitness centre, fully equipped with an extensive selection of high-end cardio, strength and core equipment, as well as classes and a dedicated spin and yoga studio. Make the most of Muir’s unprecedented location and direct water access with our kayaks and bicycles.

### True Colours Art Gallery

True Colours art gallery is a distinct and memorable space for hotel guests featuring a rotating collection of regionally curated art. This flexible, convertible 1200 sq ft space is fully appointed, with custom lighting design and AV capabilities for polished boardroom and sales experiences. The artwork and harbour views act as a dramatic backdrop for intimate events and corporate receptions.

Learn More

### The Watch

Embraced by the sea on all sides, The Watch is a spectacular setting for high-profile business summits, private celebrations and other exclusive events. Fully appointed with bespoke furniture and a curated and commissioned art collection, The Watch is 2,100 sq. ft. with 270-degree ocean views.

Learn More

### BKS

Exclusively for guests and clients of Muir, BKS is a private retreat and ultra-premium experience, featuring a collection of carefully curated cocktails and savoury small plates. This secret speakeasy opens onto an elevated waterfront terrace, jutting out into the harbour with the best views in the city.

Learn More

### Art & Design

At Muir, art is present at every touchpoint. It is an expression of place, architecture, materiality and craft. Proudly profiled within our rooms, common areas, outdoor spaces and throughout the Queens Marque district, guests will find a significant collection of local and global art that pays thoughtful tribute to this region and its people.

### Drift

A culinary love letter to Nova Scotia, Drift features modern, elevated takes on classic Atlantic Canadian dishes. Evoking a one-of-a-kind sense of place, Drift is thoughtfully designed for entertaining colleagues or for quiet work at the spacious bar.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax?

The check-in time at Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room.

What are the parking options at Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax?

On-site valet parking is available at Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax. The daily valet fee is $35.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax booking.

What is the closest airport near Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax?

The closest airport near Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax is Halifax Stanfield International Airport (YHZ). YHZ is located approximately 34.3 KM from the hotel.

Does Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax have an airport shuttle to Halifax Stanfield International Airport (YHZ)?

Yes, Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax has an airport shuttle to Halifax Stanfield International Airport (YHZ).

Does Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Muir, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Halifax has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
