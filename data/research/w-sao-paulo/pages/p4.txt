### Baio Cozinha Sulista

Baio Cozinha Sulista is a culinary tribute to the vibrant cultures and traditions of Southern Brazil. Inspired by the indigenous roots, European immigration, and the fertile landscapes of the region, Baio reimagines classic flavors with a modern twist. From the ancestral wisdom of the Charruas to the culinary influences of Germans, Italians, and Iberians, every dish tells a story of connection, resilience,...See More and innovation. Our menu celebrates the seasons, honoring the land's bounty through thoughtfully sourced ingredients. From the iconic parrilla and churrasco to delicate charcuterie and handmade pastas, Baio delivers an authentic yet inventive dining experience. Immerse yourself in the rich heritage of Southern Brazilian cuisine here in Vila Olimpia, Sao Paulo, in a hotel restaurant that redefines creativity and respect for tradition.See Less

## L40 Cozinha de Latitude

Our Bar with a view in São Paulo, L40 Cozinha de Latitude, perched on the 40th floor, offers stunning city panoramas paired with a globally inspired menu. Drawing inspiration from 16 regions worldwide, including Portugal, Spain, Italy, Greece, China, Japan, and the United States, the restaurant seamlessly blends Brazilian ingredients with global techniques and traditions. The experience begins at 6:30 PM, with a vibrant happy hour featuring a DJ, shareable dishes, and a curated cocktail menu designed for after-work gatherings. As night falls, the transition to dinner brings a more refined menu, still emphasizing shared plates, paired with cocktails highlighting the botanicals and aromas of Brazil and the Latitude 40 regions. Live music and DJ sets guide the evening at our Rooftop Bar in São Paulo into a lively, unforgettable atmosphere, all set against the best skyline view in São Paulo. Opening Hours: Monday to Thursday \| 6:30 PM – 1:00 AM\| Friday and Saturday \| 6:30 PM – 2:00 AM. Last Call: Food - 11:30 PM (Monday to Saturday), Drinks - 1:00 AM (Monday to Thursday), 2:00 AM (Friday and Saturday).

## Yōso Bar e Café

Yōso Bar and Café, situated in the lively Living Room of W São Paulo, combines the precision of Japanese cocktail artistry with local adaptations to create an unparalleled experience. The bar program features modern classic cocktails with bold, distinct flavors and an air of mystique, alongside a curated selection of Japanese and American whiskies. Our signature OMAKASE cocktail service is a one-of-a-kind experience, offering personalized drinks crafted through intimate conversations with our guests. During the day, Yōso transforms into a vibrant café with a Grab and Go service, offering expertly brewed coffees, Japanese-style Cold Brews, teas, and pastries. The food menu, inspired by Japanese izakayas, elevates traditional flavors with haute cuisine techniques, offering shareable dishes that perfectly complement the bar's drinks. Yōso is more than a cocktail bar in São Paulor—it's a journey into Japanese-inspired excellence with a Brazilian twist.
