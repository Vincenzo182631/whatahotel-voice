# Rooms & Suites

## Accommodations

At W São Paulo, we redefine the concept of living in luxury through our elegant and sophisticated rooms. Each space is meticulously designed to combine comfort, functionality, and contemporary elegance, ensuring an unforgettable stay. Immerse yourself in unparalleled comfort, with luxurious bedding, high-quality sheets, and a range of personalized amenities. From high-speed Wi-Fi and state-of-the-art...See More entertainment systems to exquisite bath amenities and soft bathrobes, every detail is carefully crafted to enhance your experience. Whether for work or leisure, our Vila Olimpia, São Paulo hotel rooms provide the perfect retreat in the vibrant heart of the city. Welcome to a new standard of modern luxury, where local charm meets contemporary design.See Less

### Unique Design

W São Paulo’s unique design draws from distinctly Brazilian motifs, and places them in the context of a contemporary, international viewpoint. Stone, wood, curves, color and free-flowing energy all meld together to create a modern vision of streamlined tropical-chic, at once Paulista in flavor yet worldwide in their cosmopolitan finish.

## Skyline Panoramic Views

Reach new heights in Vila Olimpia, São Paulo. With hotel suites and rooms starting on the 25th floor, every side and corner offer a new, bold, breathtaking point of view of the city.

## Penthouse I

With 150m² of elegance and comfort, this penthouse delivers a unique experience for those who value style, privacy, and well-being. It features six spacious areas, including a lounge with a tropical-contemporary vibe, three bedrooms, two sleek bathrooms, and a relaxing bathtub. Premium finishes and natural materials—like Brazilian stones and organic textures—highlight the property’s distinctive design. Every detail has been thoughtfully curated to offer an exclusive stay where functionality and aesthetics blend in perfect harmony.

## W São Paulo Residences

Experience the comfort of a private residence with the services of a world-class hotel. Fully furnished and designed for modern living, our residences are available for short and extended stays, offering style, flexibility, and the signature W lifestyle.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W São Paulo have a work space?

No, the rooms at W São Paulo do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W São Paulo?

The largest occupancy room at W São Paulo is the Penthouse I with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W São Paulo?

Some rooms at W São Paulo permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W São Paulo have fridges?

No, rooms at W São Paulo do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W São Paulo have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W São Paulo have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W São Paulo have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W São Paulo have hair dryers.
