# Spa at W São Paulo

## AWAY Spa at W São Paulo

AWAY Spa at W São Paulo invites you to disconnect from the city and reconnect with yourself. Featuring exclusive treatments inspired by Brazil’s nature, the spa offers personalized rituals, sustainable details, and breathtaking city views. Each experience blends innovation and wellness, creating a sensory journey designed for deep relaxation and renewal.

## A Sensorial Urban Escape

Perched high above the city, AWAY Spa blends contemporary design with calming textures and elements inspired by Brazil nature. From customized treatments to serene relaxation spaces, each detail is curated to offer a transformative wellness experience with panoramic views of São Paulo.

## Beauty Salon US3

Inside AWAY Spa, discover US3, a beauty salon where tradition meets innovation. Born from the legacy of L’Officiel 3, US3 offers expert hair, makeup, and wellness services in a welcoming, modern space. With a team of seasoned professionals, it’s a destination for personalized care, connection, and impeccable results.

### Signature Spa Experience

Each AWAY Spa treatment is inspired by Brazil’s rich natural landscapes and cultural heritage. Customized rituals, premium products, and mindful techniques deliver a holistic approach to relaxation, renewal, and balance, creating a deeply personal wellness journey.

### Private Wellness Spaces

Designed for comfort and tranquility, the massage rooms at AWAY Spa offer a serene setting where sound, scent, and touch work together. Each space enhances relaxation, allowing you to fully immerse in personalized treatments.

### Wellness with a Brazilian Soul

At AWAY Spa, wellness is an experience shaped by place, rhythm, and intention. Each treatment is thoughtfully designed to reflect Brazil’s diverse landscapes and natural elements, blending mindful techniques, premium products, and a serene atmosphere high above the city. It’s a space to slow down, reset, and reconnect.
