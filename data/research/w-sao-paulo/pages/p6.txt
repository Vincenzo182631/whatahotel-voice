## Beauty Salon US3

Inside the AWAY Spa at W São Paulo, discover US3, a beauty salon where tradition meets innovation. Born from the legacy of L’Officiel 3, US3 offers expert hair, makeup, and wellness services in a welcoming space. With a team of seasoned professionals, it’s a destination for personalized care, connection, and impeccable results.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W São Paulo have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W São Paulo offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W São Paulo have a pool on-site?

Yes, W São Paulo has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does W São Paulo have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY SPA is located on-site at W São Paulo. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W São Paulo?

Parque do Povo, JK Iguatemi Shopping Mall, Museu da Casa Brasileira, Ibirapuera Park, MIS - Image and Sound Museum and more are nearby W São Paulo.
