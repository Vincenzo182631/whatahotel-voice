# Welcome to W São Paulo

## Reach New Heights

Located in the heart of Vila Olímpia, W São Paulo delivers a fresh perspective on urban luxury with its bold design inspired by Brazilian motifs and panoramic city views. Rooms start on the 25th floor, offering breathtaking sights of São Paulo. Enjoy top-floor dining at L40 and soak up the vibe at the WET Deck. Our hotel in Vila Olímpia, São Paulo, offers vibrant energy that extends through its unique activations—from live DJ sets and exclusive culinary experiences to wellness programs that include yoga and spa treatments featuring local ingredients. Guests are welcomed by Whatever/Whenever service, tailored with a Brazilian touch, ensuring an unparalleled stay. Close to cultural hotspots like Parque do Povo, JK Iguatemi, and Avenida Faria Lima, our hotel in São Paulo, Brazil, is the ideal base for exploring the city’s dynamic rhythm. It’s not just a place to stay; it’s where São Paulo’s spirit comes alive.

Stay with Purpose: UNICEF’s Check Out for Children

At W São Paulo, every stay can make a difference. Through Marriott International’s Check Out for Children program, guests are invited to contribute a small voluntary donation that helps UNICEF deliver life-saving support to children and families around the world.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W São Paulo?

The check-in time at W São Paulo is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W São Paulo allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W São Paulo. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 15.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W São Paulo?

On-site parking is available at W São Paulo. Self parking is available for $20.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $20.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W São Paulo have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W São Paulo has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W São Paulo booking.

What is the closest airport near W São Paulo?

The closest airport near W São Paulo is Sao Paulo/Congonhas Airport (CGH). CGH is located approximately 7.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W São Paulo have an airport shuttle to Sao Paulo/Congonhas Airport (CGH)?

No, W São Paulo does not have an airport shuttle to Sao Paulo/Congonhas Airport.

Does W São Paulo have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W São Paulo does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
