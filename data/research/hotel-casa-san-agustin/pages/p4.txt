## Luxury meets legacy in Casa San Agustin, a boutique hotel made of three houses all inspired by Cartagena’s colonial heritage

The wooden-beamed ceilings and wall frescos infuse your stay with the charm of our history as the breeze off the Caribbean Sea wanders over the white-washed walls and breathes fresh air into the present. Simple but sophisticated traditional decoration tells the story of our 17th century beginnings, while the sumptuous comfort of outdoor spaces, luxurious linens, and hand-picked amenities make sure that your time with us transcends.
