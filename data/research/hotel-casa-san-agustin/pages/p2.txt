## Aurum is the Latin word for “gold” or “brilliant sunrise”. The pre-Colombian civilizations revered the sun as an important deity and in ancient times the Sun was often thought to live on the Earth just like gold, a universal symbol of the utmost purity and value. A much sought-after element in alchemy, gold is the classic symbol of beauty, of wealth and of power.

## Hammam

This ritual is a Moroccan tradition, practiced today all over the world. The body is rinsed and cleansed in a moist steam room before a vigorous exfoliation with a scrub made of coffee, volcanic mud and pure tobacco, ingredients combined for their highly purifying effect. The treatment can be done on one’s own or by a therapist. Ideal before any other treatment to improve circulation, liberate tension and optimize the nourishment of the skin.

A romantic ritual.  Begin this experience with a soak in the jacuzzi, and a fresh drink in hand before a lemongrass & mimosa exfoliation, after which you’ll each relax in a nourishing wrap that prepares the skin for a relaxing massage.  The ritual finishes up with a delicious Wild Lime that revitalizes both the body and mind.
