# Atriums Suites in Sedona

Your exclusive

## Your Exclusive   (Red Rock) Recess

Set in the National Forest of Sedona, Ambiente’s collection of 40 atrium suites offers a unique hotel experience in Arizona. These elevated, free-standing structures are composed mostly of floor-to-ceiling, bronze-tinted glass, granting 180-degree views of the surrounding red rocks. Each Atrium suite combines modern luxury with architectural transparency, creating suites that maximize your connection to the natural landscape.

Vistas from our hotel suites in Sedona range from the massive Brins Mesa mountain range to ancient waterways threading through the property. Each Atrium suite presents a private rooftop deck with fire pit and daybed, ideal for stargazing and immersive enjoyment of the outdoors. Experience luxury suites that blend comfort, style, and unparalleled access to nature.

The interiors of our Sedona hotel suites are designed with casual elegance. Custom touches include unique wall scenes, live-edge wood, luxury Italian linens, and earth-toned textiles. The design philosophy embraces the panoramic views and beautiful red rock vistas visible through the floor-to-ceiling glass. Our suites offer the perfect Arizona getaway for couples.
