### Red rock vistas

## A Uniquely   Elevated Experience

Set on the border of the national forest, Ambiente® is a collection of 40 elevated Atriums perched above the natural landscape of Sedona. Composed of floor-to-ceiling, bronze-tinted glass granting 180 degree views of the surrounding red rocks, each Atrium offers a unique experience. From this Sedona hotel, enjoy breathtaking views that span from the massive Brins Mesa mountain range to a series of ancient waterways weaving throughout the property.

Complete with luxurious amenities, each Atrium presents a rooftop deck, fire pit and daybed for immersive enjoyment of the outdoors without leaving the comfort and style of your Atrium. Interiors are designed with casual elegance that soothes, with custom touches including unique wall scenes, live-edge wood, luxury Italian linens, and earth-toned textiles, creating the ideal setting for a romantic getaway in Arizona.
