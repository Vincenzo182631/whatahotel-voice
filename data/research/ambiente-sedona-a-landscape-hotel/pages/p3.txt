## Restaurant Forty1

At Ambiente Sedona, we express the spirit of Arizona through exceptional food, cocktails, and hospitality. Every dish and every pour reflects the landscapes, seasons, and artisans that define our home.

Led by Arizona natives Chef Bri Barker and Beverage Manager Rachelle Connolly, our culinary and cocktail programs are rooted in a local-first philosophy, built on enduring partnerships with the state’s farmers, ranchers, foragers, growers, and distillers.

Ingredients are allowed to speak with clarity and purpose. Cocktails are crafted with locally distilled spirits, house-made infusions, fresh juices, and seasonal botanicals. Together, they create an immersive expression of Arizona—refined, authentic, and unmistakably of this place.

At Restaurant Forty1, reservations are strongly encouraged. Use the booking link below to join us for dinner; breakfast and lunch are walk-in friendly for hotel guests.

Minimum Age: Ambiente is an adults-only property, welcoming guests 18 years of age and older.

At Ambiente, we believe extraordinary hospitality begins with extraordinary people — and we are honored to have Executive Chef Brianna Barker leading our culinary team.

A true Verde Valley native, Chef Barker’s culinary journey is a remarkable example of dedication, growth, and commitment to excellence. Born and raised in the Verde Valley, she has spent over 15 years developing her craft throughout the region’s most respected culinary destinations.

Chef Barker’s passion for the culinary arts began at an early age, helping in her grandparents’ bakery in Cottonwood, where she developed an appreciation for the craft, creativity, and connection that food brings to people. From those early experiences to her professional journey through local kitchens and leadership roles in fine dining, resort, and farm-to-table environments, Chef Barker has continuously grown through the ranks while building a reputation for culinary creativity, operational excellence, and team development.

Her deep connection to the Verde Valley, combined with her passion for creating memorable dining experiences, makes her uniquely positioned to lead the culinary vision at Ambiente. We proudly present a home grown, female Executive Chef who has built her career in and around the Verde Valley and continues to elevate the culinary landscape of Sedona through her talent, leadership, and dedication to hospitality.

## Inside the Experience at Forty1

Forty1 offers three distinct expressions of the dining experience: a refined bar program, open-air dining beside the pool, and in-room service delivered to the privacy of your Atrium rooftop. Many guests pair an evening meal with a treatment at Velvet Spa for a more restorative end to the day.

Attire is resort casual, and reservations are highly recommended.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to be a hotel guest to dine at Forty1?

Forty1 welcomes both Ambiente guests and off-property visitors for **breakfast, lunch, and dinner**. Reservations are strongly encouraged, especially for dinnerReservations are strongly encouraged for dinner; book through OpenTable or call the concierge.

### What cuisine does Forty1 serve?

Forty1 serves modern American cuisine built around Verde Valley produce, Arizona-raised proteins, and seasonal ingredients. Chef Bri Barker refreshes the menu each season to reflect what local growers are harvesting.

### Is Forty1 open for lunch?

Yes. Lunch is served daily from 10:30 AM to 5:00 PM. The lunch menu is lighter and pool-appropriate, with salads, elevated sandwiches, and a short list of warm plates.

### Can Forty1 accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes. The kitchen can adapt most preparations for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and allergen-sensitive guests. Note any restrictions in your OpenTable reservation or mention them to your server, and the kitchen will coordinate with you directly.

### Is there a dress code at Forty1?

Forty1 is resort casual at breakfast and lunch, with a slightly more elevated standard at dinner. Collared shirts and closed-toe shoes are appreciated but not required. Swimwear and cover-ups are fine for lunch by the pool but not for dinner.

### Does Ambiente offer in-room dining?

Yes. In-room dining is available daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM and is served directly to your Atrium or private rooftop terrace. The menu is a curated selection of Forty1 plates adapted for in-room service.

### Is Forty1 suitable for a romantic dinner or special occasion?

Yes. Forty1’s inviting outdoor patio and private dining options make it a refined choice for anniversaries, proposals, and milestone dinners. For more romantic dining options in Sedona, see our guide.

### Are children allowed at Forty1 or Ambiente?

Ambiente is an adults-only property and welcomes guests 18 years of age and older. This applies to the restaurant, spa, and hotel.
