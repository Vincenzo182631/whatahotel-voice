## Wellness Retreats at Velvet Spa​

Velvet Spa at Ambiente is built for guests who want a full wellness retreat without booking into a destination-only spa. The property is an adults-only landscape hotel in Sedona, which means private Atrium suites, no children’s programming, and red rock views. Most guests build their retreat day-by-day from the Velvet Spa menu, which keeps the structure flexible and the pace yours.

### An Adults-Only Sedona Wellness Retreat

Sedona has wellness retreats at every price point, but few are adults-only and fewer combine on-property spa with hotel-grade lodging in private standalone suites. At Ambiente, our treatment rooms are located on the lower level of the lobby building. Between treatments, guests can extend the retreat into the evening with private rooftop stargazing under Sedona’s dark skies.

### What's Included in a Velvet Spa Wellness Retreat

A wellness retreat at Velvet Spa is assembled from the same menu used by day guests, sequenced into a multi-day arc. Most guests draw from massage therapies (Ancient Salt Stone, Adventurist Ritual, Harmony Massage; 60 to 110 minutes), body treatments (Desert Dreams Cleanse, Sedona Sacred Clay Wrap, Timeless Body Wrap), Antara full-spectrum CBD treatments, sound journeys (Harmonic Alignment, Sound and Body Synergy), energetic experiences (Celestial Chakra, Natural Flow Reiki, Cranial Sacral), IV infusions (The Sedona Glow, Red Rock Recovery, The All-Inclusive), and an experiential space (O2 soaking bath) used between treatments. Treatments are booked individually rather than as a fixed package, which gives couples and solo guests room to pace a retreat across two, three, or five days – an arc that also suits a babymoon at Ambiente.

### Couples and Solo Retreat Formats

Couples can book the Couples Escape, Ultimate Duo, and Couples Massage in shared treatment rooms, then continue the day separately or together across the rest of the spa menu. Solo guests draw from the same menu without a packaged structure. The adults-only setting works for solo wellness travel in a way family resorts do not.

## FAQ

### Does Ambiente Sedona offer wellness retreats?

Yes. Velvet Spa at Ambiente offers wellness retreat experiences built from a menu of massage therapies, sound journeys, CBD treatments, energetic work, IV infusions, and the O2 soaking bath. Guests sequence their own retreat across two or more days in an adults-only landscape hotel setting in Sedona.

### What is included at this Sedona wellness retreat?

Velvet Spa wellness retreats are built from individually booked massages, body treatments, facials, CBD treatments, sound journeys, energetic experiences, IV infusions, and soaking baths. Couples and solo guests can combine treatments across two or more days, then add yoga, meditation, dining, hiking, or other Ambiente experiences to shape the wider retreat.

### Is Ambiente an adults-only wellness retreat in Sedona?

Yes. Ambiente is an adults-only landscape hotel in Sedona, and Velvet Spa offers treatments for adult hotel guests and visitors staying elsewhere. Each Atrium is a private standalone suite with red-rock, creekside, or forest views, creating a quieter setting for couples and solo wellness travelers.

### Can couples book a wellness retreat at Ambiente?

Yes. Velvet Spa offers couples treatments including the Couples Escape, Ultimate Duo, and Couples Massage in shared treatment rooms. Couples can pair these with sound journeys and body wraps. For a couples wellness retreat that is all encompassing, we recommend indulging in seasonal dining at Forty1, partaking in one of the many hikes surrounding the property, and exploring the nearby vortex sites.

### Can you book Velvet Spa if you are not staying at Ambiente?

Yes. Velvet Spa welcomes both Ambiente guests and visitors staying elsewhere. Treatments are booked individually from the current spa menu, including massages, body treatments, facials, couples rituals, sound journeys, energetic experiences, soaking baths, and other specialty services. Advance reservations are recommended because appointment availability is limited.

### Which Velvet Spa treatment should I choose?

Choose according to your goal. Ancient Salt Stone supports deep relaxation, Performance Refresh is designed for post-adventure recovery, the Sedona Sacred Clay Wrap offers a locally inspired body ritual, and Celestial Chakra combines massage, sound, aromatherapy, crystals, and energy work. Couples can choose shared treatments such as Couples Escape or Ultimate Duo.

### Is there an adults-only spa in Sedona?

Velvet Spa is located inside Ambiente, Sedona’s adults-only landscape hotel, with treatment rooms steps from private Atrium suites and no children’s programming on property.

### Can couples get a spa treatment together at Ambiente?
