### Your exclusive nature escape

## Panoramic Views of Sedona

The resort’s 40 individual Atriums are perched above the Sedona Landscape and feature breathtaking views of iconic Red Rock formations and vistas. Ambiente® Sedona is one of the premier places to stay in Sedona, offering an exclusive natural escape where you can surround yourself with beauty, inspire your spirit, nurture your well-being, and restore your senses.

Guests 18 years of age and older are welcome. Pets are welcome. Please contact the reservations department for more info.

#### Star

### ATRIUM

#### ISOLATED OBSERVATORY

Sedona, one of the just 20 certified Dark Sky Communities globally, provides a stellar stargazing experience. Retreat to the secluded rooftopterrace of your Atrium, where you can relax on the daybed while the firepit keeps you warm. Witness the breathtaking silhouettes of the iconic red rock mountaintops under the vast sea of stars from the visible Perseus and Scutum-Centaurus arms of the Milky Way Galaxy.

#### Creekside

### ATRIUM

#### Intimate Aqua therapy

Water is life, and life is water. The soothing sound of water is believed to influence the brain’s neuronal waves, promoting calmness, and offering psychological restoration. It’s no surprise that half of the world’s population chooses to reside near water bodies. At Creekside Atriums, residents can revel in the tranquil sounds of flowing creeks both inside and outside. These Atriums boast a rooftop terrace for stargazing and a creek-side deck for peaceful moments with the sound of running water. Conveniently situated between the lagoon, Velvet Spa, Restaurant Forty1, and a serene pool, Creekside Atriums offer a perfect blend of nature and comfort. The Creekside Atrium accommodations in Sedona are fully ADA compliant.

#### Reflections

### ATRIUM

#### TIME IN THE TIMBERS

Your inner artist, creative spirit, or nature lover will find inspiration as you observe native wildlife strolling by the full-height, floor-to-ceiling bronzed windows of your atrium. Enjoy a moment of tranquility while surrounded by the beauty of the outdoors. Within your atrium, views range from the serene pool, complemented by a variety of trees like manzanita, juniper, Arizona cypress, sycamore, and ponderosa pine that have graced the area for generations. For some, the scenery is enhanced by the ancient, rejuvenated creeks that flow through the property. Strategically positioned, your atrium offers breathtaking views of Sedona and the entire estate from your exclusive rooftop terrace.

#### Landscape

### ATRIUM

#### BEYOND THE HORIZON

As you wake up each morning, a simple touch of a button opens your blackout curtains, revealing your Atrium to breathtaking IMAX-like views. You can gaze upon views like the Coconino National Forest, the iconic red rock formations, or the serene flowing creeks, immersing yourself in the natural beauty that surrounds you. Watch as afternoon thunderstorms sweep over the mountain terrain or witness a winter snowfall dusting the Sedona Red Rocks. Climb the spiral staircase to your rooftop terrace and savor delightful treats and libations next to the fire pit, all while experiencing the stunning color palettes that Sedona showcases as the sun rises and sets over the red rock landscape.
