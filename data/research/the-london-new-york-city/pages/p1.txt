### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City?

The check-in time at The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 75.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City?

On-site valet parking is available at The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City. The daily valet fee is $95.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City booking.

What is the closest airport near The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City?

The closest airport near The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City is LaGuardia Airport (LGA). LGA is located approximately 10.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
