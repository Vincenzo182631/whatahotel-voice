Epicurean Moment

## A Celebration of the Season

Every evening at 5:30, a few small plates appear in Dabble. What is poured alongside them comes from New York State, and the menu changes with the month. It is complimentary, with no reservation and no occasion, only the hour when guests who spent the day apart in the city end up in the same place.

In-Room Dining

## A Table Already Set

Most hotels deliver breakfast to a bed. Ours arrives at a table. Because nearly every accommodation here is a suite, dining in is not the compromise you make on a tired night. It is a way of eating the room was built for. The kitchen is Dabble's. The setting is yours, with the door closed and the city left where it belongs.
