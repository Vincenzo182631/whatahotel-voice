# Your Flat in the Heart of the City

## Accommodations

Spanning 54 floors, The London NYC offers expansive accommodations that bring a sense of residential ease to Midtown Manhattan. Designed for living and recharging, our suites balance space and privacy...See More with refined interiors and thoughtful details. Separate living and sleeping areas create the comfort of a Manhattan residence, while select suites offer sweeping views of Central Park and the city.See Less

The Suite Standard

### Designed Like a London Flat

Every suite follows the logic of a London flat. Living space sits apart from where you sleep, and a dedicated workspace means the two never compete for the same corner. Bathrooms are generous, built for unhurried mornings. Materials are chosen for craftsmanship over effect. The result behaves less like a hotel room and more like an address of your own.

## A Window Onto Central Park

A park view is a rare thing to hold in Midtown. Our Park View suites and the 1,800 square foot Premium Park View Apartment look out across Central Park, where the outlook shifts with the season rather than the hour. Green and dense in summer, bare and architectural in winter, and quieter than anything else at this address. It changes how the room gets used.
