## Fitness

The London’s flagship fitness center brings together contemporary Technogym equipment and a thoughtfully designed setting for movement and well-being. From cardio to strength training, the space offers everything needed for a considered approach to fitness throughout your stay.

Fitness Club Amenities

### Technogym Kinesis Personal Heritage

A distinctive expression of form and function, the Technogym Kinesis Personal Heritage supports a wide range of natural movements through an elegant, space-conscious design.

### Strength & Conditioning

A comprehensive strength offering includes free weights, kettlebells, resistance equipment and dedicated strength machines for both focused and full-body training.

### Technogym Skill

Performance-focused equipment including SkillRun, SkillRow, SkillMill and SkillUp offers added versatility for running, rowing, climbing and dynamic training.

Destination Discovery

## The Art Tour

Discover the stories behind The London NYC’s art collection during our weekly Friday guided tour. The 15-minute experience moves through the hotel with works by Matisse and Warhol alongside a Venice Biennale sculpture and a Turner Prize winner, offering a closer look at the artists and ideas represented throughout The London.

A Taste of the Destination

### Epicurean Moment

Each month brings a new Epicurean Moment, a curation inspired by New York and the season. It is offered daily in Dabble at 5:30, complimentary, with seasonal bites paired against wines and beers made in New York State. The format holds and the discovery changes, so a longer stay means something new to come down for.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City?

SUMMIT One Vanderbilt, Central Park, Intrepid Museum, Empire State Building, The Museum of Modern Art and more are nearby The London, a Luxury Collection Hotel, New York City.
