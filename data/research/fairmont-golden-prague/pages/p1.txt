## Where _timeless_ grandeur meets the flowing Vltava River

Fairmont Golden Prague blends brutalist grandeur with Czech artistry and modern elegance. After exploring Old Town’s historic charm, indulge in six exquisite restaurants and bars, then unwind in our private garden. Enjoy Prague’s only indoor/outdoor pool, the lavish Grand Ballroom, and a relaxing spa—transforming your stay into a true celebration.

Proud holder of Two MICHELIN Keys which honors the most exceptional hotels worldwide.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Golden Prague located?

Fairmont Golden Prague is located at Parizska street 43/30, 110 00, Prague.

Does Fairmont Golden Prague have a spa?

Yes, we have a spa at Fairmont Golden Prague.

Does Fairmont Golden Prague have a swimming pool?

Yes, we have both an indoor and an outdoor heated pool available for guests at Fairmont Golden Prague.

Does Fairmont Golden Prague have a gym/fitness center?

Yes, we have a fitness center available for guests at Fairmont Golden Prague.

Is Fairmont Golden Prague pet-friendly?

Yes, pets are welcome at Fairmont Golden Prague. Please note that pets are not permitted within the restaurant, spa and bar premises. The fee is 875 CZK / 36 EUR per pet per day.

Is parking available at Fairmont Golden Prague?

Yes, parking is available for a charge of 1600 CZK / 60 EUR per day.

Can I buy breakfast in the hotel?

Yes, the breakfast cost is 1100 CZK / 45 EUR per person for outside guests.
