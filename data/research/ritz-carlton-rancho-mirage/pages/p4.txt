Locally Inspired Restaurants in Rancho Mirage
Dining
An elegant table overlooking Coachella Valley at our Palm Springs restaurant with a view. Seasonal cuisine highlighting local ingredients and wineries. Savor luxury dining in Palm Springs by the pool, under the sun. At The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage, each restaurant takes a distinct perspective on the possibilities of California cuisine.
Indulge in Culinary Excellence
The Edge Steakhouse
Seeking an elevated and deeply personal fine dining experience, The Edge Steakhouse is the Coachella Valley’s only mountaintop culinary destination. Set high above the valley, it pairs breathtaking views with dry-aged steak mastery, refined luxury, and heartfelt service—creating emotional, once-in-a-lifetime moments unlike any traditional steakhouse.
