Welcome to the Vista Residential Suites at The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage, where sophistication harmonizes with the stunning beauty of the desert landscape. You are invited to immerse yourself in a tranquil retreat, enhanced by sweeping panoramic views, exceptional design, and unrivaled amenities. Indulge in extraordinary comfort and personalized service as you relax in the private sanctuary of your luxury one or two-bedroom suite in Palm Springs.
Stunning Views
One Bedroom Vista Residential Suite
With sweeping western views of the San Jacinto Mountain Range, the One Bedroom Vista Residential Suite offers 900 square feet of privacy and exceptional service. This elegant residence features a king bedroom and a separate living area. The spacious chef’s kitchen boasts premium Wolf® and Viking® appliances while the luxurious master bath includes a soaking tub and a separate rain forest shower. Enjoy a perfect blend of sophistication and comfort in this luxury residential suite in Palm Springs.
Well Appointed Kitchen
Spacious Chef's Kitchen
Designed for culinary enthusiasts, it includes essential kitchenware and premium cookware, creating an ideal space for preparing gourmet meals. With ample counter space and storage, this inviting kitchen is perfect for both cooking and entertaining, making it a true culinary haven.

Two Bedroom Vista Residential Suite
The Two-bedroom Vista Residential Suite offers 1,350 square feet of luxurious privacy.
