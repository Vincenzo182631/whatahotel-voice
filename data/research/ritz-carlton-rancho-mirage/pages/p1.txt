Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage
Visit Our Luxury Palm Springs Hotel
Overlooking Palm Springs, Coachella Valley, and the San Jacinto Mountain Range, The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage offers an elevated experience of a timeless destination. The modern design of our luxury resort in Palm Springs, California, invites connections with the desert, from stunning valley views at The Edge Steakhouse to holistic spa rituals inspired by the land, while the storied past of Palm Springs complements the resort and spa's elegant luxury. Enjoy spacious accommodations, an exclusive Club Lounge, and more than 30,000 sq ft of event space at our Rancho Mirage luxury hotel in Palm Springs.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 35.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $350.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage. The daily valet fee is $50.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage is Palm Springs International Airport (PSP). PSP is located approximately 7.3 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
