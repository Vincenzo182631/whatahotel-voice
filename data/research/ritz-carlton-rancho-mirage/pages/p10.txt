The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage
Relaxed desert luxury with noteworthy cuisine and offerings for discerning travelers.

Why book?
Consider it your own glam desert oasis tucked away from the buzz of Downtown Palm Springs, but close enough if the itch arises.
Set the scene
Expect to see 30-to-60-somethings lounging in reserved cabanas at the adults-only Vista pool in retro patterned
Trina Turk
cover-ups and raffia slides, while young families congregate by the family-friendly Air Pool and golf enthusiasts in
Travis Mathew
polos duck in for lunch at
State Fare Bar & Kitchen
. Business executives from Silicon Valley, Chicago, Canada, Mexico, and beyond converge at the bar for happy hour, and gussied-up couples dine in sequined tops by
Paige
and leather clutches by
Cult Gaia
at The Edge Steakhouse.
The backstory
Originally opened 37 years ago, the resort closed its doors in 2001 and reopened as The Lodge at Rancho Mirage. In 2014, the hotel became a Ritz-Carlton once again. Flash forward to 2025, and the property received a massive, two-year, multi-million-dollar overhaul which included updates to the guest rooms, dining venues, and poolscapes.
The rooms
It’s desert modern with soft creamy tones and refined touches like woven leather table lamps; crisp, white
Frette
linens; and light sand-hued settees. Each guest room and suite is appointed with either a spacious ground-floor terrace, a walk-out balcony, or a Juliet balcony with sliding glass doors. For families, ground floor Fire Pit guest rooms with private terraces are a popular choice, as are the one-bedroom Executive Suites. Couples and groups can choose from the one- to three-bedroom Vista residential suites with chef’s kitchens and ample living and dining areas.
Food and drink
Three dining venues cater to a variety of palettes; go casual with hearty, made-to-order burgers and fries at Air Pool Bar or American fare (chickpea date salads, New York strips) at State Fare & Kitchen. For elevated dining with sweeping views of the Coachella Valley and San Jacinto Mountain Range, head to The Edge Steakhouse, helmed by celebrated specialty chef Nyitre Rodgers with a menu showcasing in-house dry-aged steaks, fresh seafood, and handmade pastas. Once seated, enjoy The Edge old-fashioned, a complimentary, two-ounce welcome drink expertly smoked to perfection within an elegant cloche.
The spa
Housed in a 28,000-square-foot building, the spa features 15 treatment rooms; a full-service salon; a 24-hour fitness center; and a boutique with a selection of athletic wear and signature “Mirage” candles created for the resort with notes of satsuma mandarin and black currant. Book a restorative and hydrating custom facial, or de-stress and detoxify with a calming indigenous desert clay wrap.
The neighborhood/area
Once home to the likes of Hollywood legends Frank Sinatra and Bob Hope, the quiet community serves as the ideal refuge given its proximity to the
Palm Springs International Airport
(approximately seven miles away), and nearby desert cities with high-end
shopping
and
museums
. In Palm Springs, don’t-miss vintage troves including
Market Market
and
Bon Vivant
for finds from Murano glass decanters to a leather jumpsuit worn by legendary stuntman Evel Knievel. To escape the heat and learn about area wildlife and history, set aside a few hours for the
Palm Springs Aerial Tramway
. At the top, poised at 8,516 feet, break for lunch at
Peaks Restaurant
framed by views of the Coachella Valley and Chino Canyon, or lace up hiking boots to explore more than 50 trails. Other area standouts include
The Living Desert Zoo & Gardens
,
Moorten Botanical Garden
, and
Sunnylands
.
The service
Consider it the crème de la crème. Need tips on what to do in a three-hour window between meetings, or want to book a
Red Jeep Tour
through
Joshua Tree National Park
? The knowledgeable, reference librarian-like concierge staff can handle any request. The staff is equally helpful. Locals like Ruben, a jovial front desk guest experience expert—who grew up in nearby Thermal—know the area like the back of their hand.
For families
The Ritz Kids program offers a slew of activities ranging from “Little Explorers,” or self-guided scavenger hunts for kids ages 5–12, and “Desert Discovery Nights” on Friday and Saturday evenings with light dinner, crafts, and stargazing.
Eco effort
Sustainability initiatives and practices range from organic waste and water conservation programs to refillable water stations.
Accessibility
The resort is a certified Autism Center by the International Board of Credentialing and Continuing Education Standards. It also features accessible parking, ramps, elevators, ADA-compliant restrooms, and ADA-accessible rooms. Desert View king and Resort View king options include roll-in showers, grab bars, and visual alarms.
Anything left to mention?
Splurge for
Club Level access
. The newly revamped lounge offers breakfast, lunch, canapes, and cordials, as well as dedicated concierge services and preferred tee times at select area golf courses. Appointed with artwork curated by
Dawson Cole Fine Art
in Palm Desert, the gallery-like space features rotating exhibits highlighting the work of artists such as
Hunt Slonem
. The property also has an exclusive offering with
indiGo Performance & Experience Center
for special guest access and driving packages for vehicles like Porsche 911 GTE3 RS and Ferrari 488. Craving an afternoon treat? Stop by the Candy Garden at the concierge desk for an assortment of sweets once favored by Hollywood luminaries like Lucille Ball.
