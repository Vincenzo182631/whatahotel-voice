Take Relaxation to A New Level at Our Fukuoka Spa Hotel
Spa
Natural fiber wall coverings and woven rattan furniture bespeak the relaxed atmosphere of The Ritz-Carlton Spa, a quiet sanctuary for rest and restoration located on the top floor of the hotel. The wellness facilities here include a pool, gym, steam and dry saunas, and private rooms for luxurious spa treatments at our on-site Fukuoka massage spa. In a destination buzzing with youthful energy, treat yourself to pampered comfort in an oasis of calm—the best of both worlds.
Connect with Nature in the Sasaguri Forest
Immerse yourself in a serene escape with personalized visit to Sasaguri forest, where you can experience a tranquil yoga session surrounded by rich nature, followed by private visit to the iconic Nanzoin Temple, with private meditation by monk to connect your body and mind to stay balanced.
Book Now
Mindful Mornings with Yoga & Kyushu Spa Journey
Begin your day in tranquility with a private transfer to Nishi-Koen, just a 10-minute stroll from the hotel, leading to a serene hillside setting for a mindful yoga session surrounded by nature. Return to the hotel to conclude your morning with a Kyushu-inspired spa treatment, thoughtfully designed to restore balance and harmony to both body and mind.

The Ritz-Carlton Spa
While staying in the very heart of lively Fukuoka, you can enjoy a relaxing getaway to soothe tired muscles after a flight or a busy day of exploration and reset the mind and body before your journey onward. Our Fukuoka spa offers treatments and formulas by the luxury British beauty brand ESPA along with original programs inspired by this destination.
See Spa Offerings
Oceanic Purity
The abundance of nutrients, vitamins, and minerals found in the nori seaweed that’s cultivated with care and harvested from the nearby Ariake Sea is unparalleled. This combination wrap and full-body massage harnesses the cleansing and nourishing benefits of both seaweed and marine algae to purify, tighten, smooth, and tone the skin. Emerge with your skin fully hydrated and your natural radiance restored.
Reserve
Itazuke Restore
No food is more central to Japanese culture than rice, and Itazuke in the heart of Fukuoka is said to be one of the earliest sites where it was cultivated. This therapeutic massage honors that local heritage with heated pillows filled with brown rice kernels and bran in addition to stress-relieving mugwort. Curative essential oils of rose geranium and sweet orange unite to ease tension and restore comfort.
Reserve
Botanical Revive
An emblem of Fukuoka prefecture, the ume blossom represents renewal, while its fruit is rich in antioxidants and polyphenols. This treatment begins with an ume drink to support both the immune system and gut health, and features essential oils of gardenia and bergamot for uplift. A massage performed with a warm bamboo wand eases muscle fatigue, and acupressure facial techniques help to brighten the complexion.
Reserve
Arrival Time
Please arrive at least 30 minutes before your scheduled treatment. This will allow time to enjoy the lounges, saunas, steam rooms, vitality pool, and indoor pool. Arriving late will limit the time for your treatment, thus decreasing its effectiveness and your enjoyment. Your treatment will end on time, so the next guest is not delayed. The full value of your treatment will be charged.

While Visiting the Spa
Pool Information
Guests aged 16 and above: Fee applies, including access to the spa facilities, such as the steam room, sauna, and vitality pool. Children aged 4 and above may use the pool complimentary daily from 12:00 P.M. to 5:00 P.M., when accompanied by an adult with paid spa facilities access. Outside of these hours, the pool is reserved for adult guests only.
Personal Belongings/Valuables
We cannot be responsible for any personal items and/or valuables brought into the spa or left in your locker. Please leave your valuables at home or in your room safe.
Use of Electronic Devices
To protect the privacy of our guests and to preserve the guest experience, the use of cell phones, electronic devices, and cameras is prohibited in the spa. Cell phones and electronic devices must be turned off.
Age Requirements
Guests must be 16 years of age or older to access the spa heat experiences, relaxation areas, and treatments. Children under 4 years old are not permitted in the Spa or the pool, in order to maintain a tranquil ambience.
Food and Beverage
Guests are prohibited from bringing any outside food or alcohol into the spa. Guests appearing intoxicated will be denied access to the spa.
