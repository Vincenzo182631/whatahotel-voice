Dig In to a Dynamic Food Capital
Dining
Our four restaurants and two bars in Fukuoka, Japan showcase the region’s finest local ingredients and flavors, celebrating the vibrant food culture of a city renowned for its hearty cuisine and strong sense of place.
Sustainably produced local ingredients are transformed into creative farm-to-sky Western cuisine at Viridis, while three of Japan’s finest culinary traditions—kaiseki, sushi, and teppan-yaki—star in feasts served on elegant Arita or Karatsu porcelain at Genjyu, our Fukuoka sushi bar & grill.
The Lobby Lounge & Bar serves as the welcoming heart of the hotel, an inviting setting for light meals and drinks throughout the day. On the 24th floor, The Bay Bar pairs chargrilled dishes and locally-inspired cocktails with sweeping views of Hakata Bay from its open-air terrace. Completing the experience, café Diva highlights Kyushu’s celebrated teas through a seasonal Afternoon Tea, along with premium infusions and indulgent desserts.
The Hakata Magemono Bento Experience
Tradition Meets Taste
Discover the art of Hakata craftsmanship through an immersive workshop led by master artisan Tamaki Shibata. Create a Hakata Magemono bento box, a refined expression of tradition and meticulous skill. The experience continues with a bespoke bento meal thoughtfully prepared by the chefs of Genjyu, where each dish reflects the depth and sophistication of Japanese culinary artistry. It concludes with a meaningful keepsake where the handcrafted bento box, elegantly wrapped in a Hakata-ori furoshiki, a timeless symbol of Fukuoka’s enduring elegance.

Dine in Comfort
Private Rooms
Choose from among six private dining rooms to host an intimate celebration or important business event in stylish comfort. We offer three rooms in our Japanese restaurant, Genjyu, and one in the Western-style Viridis, where creative farm-to-sky cuisine features on the menu. Two additional private rooms at Bay Bar on our top floor overlook Hakata Bay and the Fukuoka cityscape, respectively. For inquiries and reservations for our Fukuoka restaurants, please call +81 92-401-8882 between 10 AM and 5 PM or email us at

Takeout
Oven-Fresh Breads and Pastries
At Diva Café, pick up some croissants—plain or baked with almond cream—or our popular baguettes flavored with spicy pollack roe for takeout. Also on offer are sweet pastries filled with matcha green tea, chocolate, and strawberry cream. These choices and more are perfect for an afternoon pick-me-up or a snack on the go.
Artisanal Chocolates
Discover Diva’s collection of jewel-like handmade chocolates, crafted with carefully selected local Fukuoka ingredients, from aromatic coffee beans and ripe figs to fragrant honey and sweet strawberries. Beautifully boxed assortments make an ideal gift or indulgent treat.
Monaka Rice Wafers
The original sweet-bean rice-wafer sweets we serve as a welcome amenity in our guest rooms can be purchased at Diva in two flavors: strawberry and green tea. The former are made with plump local Hakata Amaou berries; the latter with tea leaves grown in Yame, the premier tea-growing region of Fukuoka.
Enjoy Sumptuous Meals in Private Comfort
In-Room Dining
Whether you crave something sweet or savory, hot or cold, light or substantive—and no matter what time of day or night—our culinary team is ready to deliver specialties and other delectable tastes from the kitchens of our Fukuoka restaurants right to your room or suite.
