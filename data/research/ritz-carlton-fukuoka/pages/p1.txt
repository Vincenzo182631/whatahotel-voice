Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Fukuoka
A refined hotel in Fukuoka, Japan, renowned for its cuisine
Dynamic Fukuoka on the island of Kyushu in southern Japan is a key flight hub in Asia with a youthful energy that befits its thriving startup scene. Situated in the heart of downtown in the main tower of the Fukuoka Daimyo Garden City complex, The Ritz-Carlton, Fukuoka is a sophisticated retreat with views across Hakata Bay. This luxury hotel in Fukuoka, Japan, features six dining venues that complement the city’s renown as a lively food capital, and playful references to the local textile art throughout its spaces. Conveniently accessed by air, land or sea, our Fukuoka hotel can be reached in less than fifteen minutes by car or public transport from Fukuoka Airport, the bullet train serviced by Hakata Station and Hakata Port.

True luxury means deep respect for our planet and its future. Your Sustainable Stay
We are committed to enriching your stay while honoring the local nature and vibrant culture of Fukuoka. Here's how we invite you to experience sustainable luxury:
1. Thoughtfully Curated Amenities: In our guest rooms, you'll find amenities crafted from bamboo, the only truly renewable, circular resource. Our bamboo toothbrushes, hair combs, and cotton swabs minimize environmental impact. Furthermore, our packaging uses FSC-certified paper, reflecting our dedication to reducing plastic and embracing natural materials wherever possible.
2. Premium In-Room Water Service: We offer a unique and environmentally conscious water program. All in-room drinking water is meticulously cleaned and bottled in reusable glass bottles within the hotel. This initiative significantly reduces plastic waste, conserves the energy typically used for recycling plastic, and lowers CO2 emissions associated with transportation.
3. Conscious Linen and Towel Service: While daily housekeeping is provided, we refresh linens and towels every other day, unless you prefer a more frequent change. This mindful approach helps conserve water and energy without compromising your comfort.
4. Innovating Through Upcycling: We actively champion upcycling initiatives, transforming used amenities and coffee grounds into new, valuable items, giving them a second life and reducing waste.
5. Embracing Local Flavors: Our commitment to sustainability extends to our culinary offerings. In all hotel restaurants, bars, and banquet venues, we prioritize locally sourced ingredients from Fukuoka and Kyushu. This supports the regional economy, reduces transportation energy, and ensures the freshest, most authentic flavors for your enjoyment.
6. Explore Sustainably: Discover the beauty of our surroundings with our rental bicycles, offering the most environmentally friendly way to explore the area.
7. Community Footprint: As part of The Ritz-Carlton's global "Community Footprint" culture, our Ladies and Gentlemen regularly engage in activities such as gardening at Ohori Park, assisting at children's cafeterias, and participating in neighborhood clean-up efforts. We are dedicated to contributing to the vitality and well-being of our local community.
