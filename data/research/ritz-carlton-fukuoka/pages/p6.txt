Discover Luxury at Our Fukuoka Private Lounge Hotel
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities, and exclusive experiences. Immerse yourself in the best of the destinations at our Fukuoka private lounge hotel.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access* at our Fukuoka hotel with a concierge and open the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.

A Private Retreat
Club Lounge Access
Upgrade your stay at The Ritz-Carlton, Fukuoka to the Club Level, located right at the top of the hotel. With spectacular views out across the Fukuoka skyline, the Club Lounge is the perfect place to get together with family and friends, meet up with business colleagues, or simply just to sit back and unwind.
*Our dress code is an important tradition. We appreciate that guests are dressed smart casual at all times.
*Guests below 20 years of age are welcome between 6:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.
*For a third guest enjoying Club Lounge privileges, a supplement of JPY 25,300 per person per night will be applied for adults and children 12 years old and above; and JPY 15,601 per person per night for children from 6 to 11 years old.

Daily Culinary Presentations
From personalized dining reservations to bespoke excursion recommendations, our dedicated Club Concierge team is on hand to thoughtfully curate every aspect of your stay, ensuring a seamless and exceptional experience. Available daily from 6:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., the team provides attentive, individualized service alongside the Club Level’s exclusive privileges, including continuous culinary offerings throughout the day from breakfast and lunch to afternoon tea, evening hors d’oeuvres, and cordials.

In-Room Amenities
