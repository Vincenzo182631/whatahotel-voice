# Qunu

## A unique culinary experience.

At Qunu, every dish is a masterpiece, meticulously crafted from the finest ingredients to create an extraordinary gastronomic experience.

Begin your journey with Chef Scott Dressel’s Playground, a curation of small plates designed for sharing, where local flavours are reimagined with creativity and imagination. From there, explore Qunu's à la carte selection of starters, mains, and desserts, or experience our three-course offering. Pair your dining journey with our expert sommeliers’ wine pairings, thoughtfully selected to complement each course.

Awarded One Star by the EatOut Awards 2026.
Awarded Two Plates by the Jenny Handley Gourmet Guide 2025.

#### Tables of Terroir \| Final Chapter

Qunu Restaurant x Le Petit Léon

This October, South African-born Chef Nick Honeyman returns to Qunu for the final chapter of Tables of Terroir, joining Chef Scott Dressels, Chef Matthew Foxon and Head Pastry Chef Catherine Adonis for three exclusive evenings.

Together with the team from Le Petit Léon, France, they will present a five-course menu celebrating exceptional ingredients, seasonality and culinary craftsmanship, with The Perfect Pour featuring Rémy Martin Louis XIII Cognac as part of the experience.

A fitting finale to the Tables of Terroir series, this collaboration brings together two distinctive culinary perspectives in an evening of technique, storytelling and exceptional dining.
