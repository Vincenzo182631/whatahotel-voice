### Saxon Hotel, Villas & Spa

Get the celebrity treatment with world-class service at Saxon Hotel, Villas & Spa

Featuring luxurious and spacious suites, Saxon Hotel, Villas & Spa includes a spa and outdoor pool. It is located in Johannesburg’s suburb of Sandhurst and includes 3 restaurants, free WiFi, and free parking.

Each elegant room at the Saxon Hotel, Villas & Spa incorporates African art. Each room has a balcony overlooking the garden and the pool. They also include a satellite TV.

The dining areas include Qunu and The Terrace.

Guests can relax and enjoy the massages at the spa. A steam room and a fitness centre are also available.

The Saxon is located 5 km from Sandton’s city centre and 10 km from the Rosebank Mall. Johannesburg International Airport is 40.5 km away and Lanseria International Airport is 29 km away. The Gautrain high speed train runs from OR Tambo International Airport to Sandton Gautrain Station, a transfer can be pre-booked with the hotel for collection at the Sandton Gautrain Station.

Couples particularly like the location — they rated it 9.6 for a two-person trip.
