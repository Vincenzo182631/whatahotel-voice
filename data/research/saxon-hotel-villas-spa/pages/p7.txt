# About us

An exclusive oasis.

## The most luxurious hotel in Johannesburg

At the heart of the City of Gold, beneath the warm sunshine in the opulent, tree-lined suburb of Sandhurst, lies the enchanting Saxon Hotel. A uniquely perfect blend of rich cultural history and contemporary five-star service, the Saxon is situated on ten acres of magnificent indigenous gardens, providing a private and peaceful retreat like no other.

our values

## Our philosophy

The Saxon is committed to a comprehensive green efficiency programme. We support our local cultural heritage, training and developing our team and supporting our community.

## Work with us

We believe in the people who make up the Saxon team, individuals whose values, morals and attributes mirror those of the hotel. We believe in finding colleagues that are passionate about what they do, and who offer the service that our valued guests deserve.
