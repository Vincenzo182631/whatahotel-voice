## Slopeside Luxury at Vail Square \ , opens in a new window

The Arrabelle at Vail Square is a premier ski-in/ski-out luxury resort in Lionshead Village, steps from the Eagle Bahn Gondola and direct access to Vail Mountain.

Surrounded by the Colorado Rockies, the resort blends alpine elegance with modern comfort. Enjoy refined accommodations, a full-service spa, and elevated dining after a day on the slopes or trails.

At the heart of Lionshead, The Arrabelle features a vibrant plaza with an open-air ice rink in winter, plus year-round shopping, dining, and après-ski, all just outside your door.

## More Information for Your Stay

Groups & Weddings

The Arrabelle at Vail Square is the ideal destination for your luxury wedding, effective business meeting, group seminar or corporate retreat.

The Arrabelle offers everything you need to conduct a successful event in the inspiring Colorado Rockies. Whatever the time of year or the kind of gathering, Vail is the perfect location and The Arrabelle is the superlative destination.

The Arrabelle features a conference center with a 2,600 square-foot Grand Ballroom, four additional meeting rooms ranging from 480 to 1,660 square-feet, as well as a large boardroom and pre-function space.

Our full-service Catering and Conference Services Department are on hand to coordinate all the details of your event so that everything you plan works flawlessly.

Submit your request to our Groups team here.
