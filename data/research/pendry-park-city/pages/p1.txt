## There’s More To The Mountains. Discover It at Pendry Park City.

Pendry Park City is a contemporary mountain enclave bringing the mastery of Utah’s awe-inspiring landscape to new heights. A ski-in / ski-out resort nestled in the center of Canyons Village and just steps from the new high-speed Sunrise Gondola, our slopeside retreat’s modernist design, luxury service and thoughtful amenities will help ensure an exceptional experience, time and again.
