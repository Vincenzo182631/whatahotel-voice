# Things To Do In Park City

## Explore the Mountains in a Whole New Way.

There’s more to Park City. From ski-in / ski-out access in winter to world-class mountain biking in summer, just steps from Pendry. Enjoy our personalized guides, special events, exclusive collaborations, neighborhood favorites and more. Only at Pendry.

Discover Experiences

Only the exceptional. Explore a one-of-a-kind selection of extraordinary experiences, catered exclusively to our Pendry Park City guests with awe-inspiring opportunities to see the mountains in a whole new way.

- Discover Park City Experiences

Summer Activities

A year-round playground. Explore Park City’s undeniable warm weather spirit with a look at the best activities, events and adventures within easy access of Pendry Park City’s home in Canyons Village.

- Discover Summer Activities

Winter Activities

Made for this. Your guide to winter in the mountains, from top to bottom. Featuring our favorite local snow day activities, events, outings and adventures to bring you the very best of Park City in signature Pendry style.

- Discover Winter Activities

Paintbox

Children first. Paintbox keeps your youngest travelers engaged and inspired all day with custom activities, events, arts & crafts and more, tailored to their age and personal interests and always filled with a serious dose of fun.

- Discover Paintbox

Neighborhood

Insider’s outlook. An easy to access guide to our favorite everything in Park City and beyond, including cultural attractions, outdoor excursions and must-see destinations.

- Discover Neighborhood Experiences

Partners

Great minds think alike. Pendry is proud to partner with a curated selection of leading brands, including Cadillac, UPPABaby, Nuzzles & Co. and others, to offer exclusive services, amenities and more to our guests, making every stay an exceptional one.

- Discover Pendry Park City Partners
