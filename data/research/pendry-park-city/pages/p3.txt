# Restaurants in Park City

## Dine in Canyons Village

Sometimes the best of the mountains is found at the base of them. At Pendry Park City, four distinct dining destinations welcome you throughout the day with Canyons Village’s most anticipated new culinary locales, family-friendly outposts for great food and casual gatherings and, of course, an après ski lounge that acts as the perfect footnote to your day. Wherever it takes you.

Après Pendry

Après-Ski Bar in Park City

Elevating the art of après. Après Pendry is the perfect place to end any day on the mountain, exploring town or marveling at the beauty of Park City. Located slopeside with ski-in / ski-out access, the lobby lounge and bar offers fireside seating, live music and a menu of shareable signature favorites in a stunning setting that blends comfort with style from morning to night.

- Discover Après Pendry

KITA

Japanese Steakhouse & Sushi in Park City

Where the delicate flavors of Japan mingle with comforting classics made for the mountains. Explore it all at KITA, a stunning, modern space where Japanese techniques and ingredients make up a menu of bountiful dinner favorites and signature creations.

- Discover KITA

The Pool House

Rooftop Bar in Park City

Whether you’re poolside in the summer or fireside in the winter, The Pool House is a year-round perch featuring the very best of Pendry. Come for cocktails, meet up for an alfresco après bike & ski like no other.

- Discover The Pool House

Dos Olas Cantina

Mexican Restaurant in Canyons Village

Great food, good times, repeat. Dos Olas sits alongside the slopes with a menu of Mexican classics and contemporary shareable favorites blending traditional flavors and cuisine with a coastal California influence with a lively spot in the center of Canyons Village, just steps from the mountain’s base.

- Discover Dos Olas

In-Room Dining

Room Service at Pendry Park City

At your service. Day or night. Pendry’s signature in-room dining features a specially crafted menu of small bites, entrées, beverages and sweets, specific to each hotel and its local culinary influences, all carefully delivered to your guestroom to satisfy any craving with convenience and comfort top of mind.

- Discover In-Room Dining Experiences

## Pendry Park City Dining FAQs

### How many restaurants and bars are located at Pendry Park City?

Pendry Park City offers 4 restaurants and bars, including KITA, a Japanese-inspired steakhouse and premium sushi restaurant and Après Pendry, a stylish slopeside lounge serving mountain favorites and craft cocktails. Guests can also enjoy Dos Olas, family-friendly cantina offering Mexican-inspired cuisine and cocktails and The Pool House, Park City’s only rooftop bar and restaurant, where guests can enjoy panoramic views.

### Does Pendry Park City offer in-room dining?

Yes, Pendry Park City offers in-room dining, allowing guests to enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner and late-night fare delivered directly to their guestroom, suite or residence at our luxury resort in Park City, Utah.

### Do any restaurants at Pendry Park City offer outdoor dining or scenic views?

Yes. The Pool House offers rooftop dining and drinks with panoramic mountain views, while Après Pendry features indoor-outdoor seating with views of the slopes and a lively après-ski atmosphere, creating a scenic setting for Park City dining. KITA and Dos Olas also offer seasonal al fresco patio seating with views of Canyons Village and just steps from the Sunrise Gondola.

### Which restaurants at Pendry Park City are ideal for romantic dinners or special occasions?

KITA offers a modern Japanese steakhouse and sushi dining experience at Pendry Park City, with a vibrant dining room and mountain views that set the tone for romantic evenings and special celebrations.

### Where is the best place to go for après-ski in Park City?

Located slopeside in Canyons Village, Après Pendry at Pendry Park City is a top spot for après-ski in Park City. With ski-in / ski-out access, it’s a convenient place to unwind after a day on the mountain, offering craft cocktails, beer and wine, alongside fireside seating and an outdoor terrace.
