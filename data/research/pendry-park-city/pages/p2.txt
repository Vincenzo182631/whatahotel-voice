# Park City Accommodations

## A signature Canyons Village resort featuring guestrooms and suites with Pendry polish.

An alpine escape like no other. Located at the foot of Canyons Village, Pendry Park City is a Park City luxury resort that embodies the best of Utah’s stunning outdoor lifestyle with the contemporary style and sensibility of Pendry. Featuring 175 guestrooms, suites and accessible hotel rooms, signature dining, cocktail and après ski destinations, ski-in/ski-out access to some of Utah’s best terrain and more.

## Rooms

Spacious and elegant, our inspired guestrooms provide striking views of the Utah landscape and surrounding resort, featuring luxury bedding and custom amenities throughout. Contemporary design and nature-inspired elements offer a signature style that promotes ultimate comfort.

View Rooms

## Suites

The collection of luxury suites at Pendry Park City resort perfectly balances modern design with natural furnishings inspired by the surrounding mountains. The elegant accommodations offer spacious kitchenettes, a nook seating area and designer amenities.

View Suites

## Residences

A refined sanctuary for those seeking a luxurious ski-in / ski-out retreat, the Residences at Pendry Park City resort contain all the comforts of home while providing the ultimate luxury experience. Each residence offers exceptional bedroom and living areas, fully equipped kitchens and spacious marble bathrooms.

View Residences

## Accessible

Our Accessible rooms, suites and residences are equipped with accessible features and captivating views of the Utah landscape and surrounding resort. Accessible accommodations are appointed with an accessible luxury bathroom, featuring grab bars and roll-in shower, and custom features throughout the room.
