# InterContinental KunmingDestination

Kunming, a fascinating city in southwest China. Nicknamed "Spring City" for its mild and pleasant climate. It showcases beautiful sceneries and a rich cultural heritage, attracting tourists around the world.

## Things to do

### West Mountain

West Mountain—Located by the west shore of Dianchi Lake, 15 km away from downtown, it was called the “Jade Rooster” mountain in the past. There are various plants on the mountain which made it obtain the reputation of “The Most Beautiful Land in Middle of Dian”. The scenic zone is mainly consists of three parts: the Huating Temple, Taihua Temple and the Dragon Gate and Sanqing Pavilion. Once stand on the top of West Mountain, the whole Dianchi Lake is in your eyes.

Learn more

### Yunnan Ethnic Village

Yunnan Ethnic Village—A 4A-grade national tourist attraction situated on the shore of Dianchi Lake, the park is a window to show the different cultures and traditions of the 26 nations in Yunnan. The environment is fresh and elegant, with well ranged ethnic architectures and lake-land ecotone. Tourists could not only experience unique features of each nation’s architecture, costume and culture but can also taste traditional ethnic cuisines.

Learn more

### Dian Lake

The largest freshwater lake in Yunnan Province, Dian Lake is known as the “Pearl of the Plateau.” Over the course of a day, its magnificence changes with the shifting skyline. The lake serves to complement historical monuments Daguan Pavilion, West Park and Haigeng, creating a scene reminiscent of a beautiful painting.
