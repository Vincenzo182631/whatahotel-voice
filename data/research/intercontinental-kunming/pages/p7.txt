19 days ago

本来想打1星的，介于酒店后面的补救措施，给到3星。
首先作为洲际酒店，入住前没有人提前联系，我多次打酒店前台电话问是否能够提前入住，都是回复当天才能确定，当天早上我只有直接过去，去了又有房间可以入住，这个应该是可以提前告知的啊，不理解，但是IHG会员办理的柜台办理非常满意。

第六，离店后也没有回访，总体感觉这是我住过的洲际酒店里面服务意识最差的
希望越来越好吧

### Good but not without its challenges

a month ago

We came to Kunming to get some fresh air and recharge from steamy Shanghai. We selected Intercontinental Kunming because of the location close to the lake.
The location is indeed a good starting point to visit some of the other tourist attractions but you'll need a car.
The hotel is a bit older and you can feel that in the rooms. However the outside with the garden and pond is great and very well kept.
The breakfast is superb with lots of choices both western and chinese. The service is very good indeed like bringing the eggs to the table and have proper barista making a good coffee.

Two things I need to mention is that we had a noise complaint for upstairs where kids just kept running and stamping for hours so we had to ask for another room. Noah at reception was able to change us to a much quieter room.
The rooms are very clean and the cleaning staff are very nice when we asked for some more tea bags and bottles of water.

Unfortunately since management can't seem to plan when to execute gardening with heavy equipment, we were rudely awakened Sunday morning at 8 am by loud chainsaws and mowers. Why do this on a Sunday morning when most guests want to sleep in and relax? Personally I find this ridiculous. You have a whole week to this work and instead you plan this on a Sunday morning? Nuts !!

The second issue is smoking on the terrace at breakfast. It is so nice to be able to sit outside and enjoy the surrounding. Unfortunately we had to move inside due to people smoking. Nothing is as off-putting as eating with second hand smoke. Especially there were quite a few toddlers and babies outside whilst people smoke. There should be a ban at least during breakfast hours. This is common place throughout the rest of the world.
