## The Market

The name, "The Market" is derived from a traditional custom of the Hani Tribe. Here, a variety of sumptuous Mediterranean and Asian dishes are on offer, as well as a number of Yunnan delicacies, all prepared in open kitchens for guests to enjoy. The Market is also specialized in an innovative, relaxed and open grill, chic Teppanyaki, and a stylish atmosphere with customized menu and inspiring a la carte menu at semi closed outdoor Bistro enjoying handcrafted cocktails and wines at the same time.

## Shang Tao Chinese Restaurant

Shang Tao is the verse from the Kunming "Daguan Pavilion Poen" that depicts gourmet food. The names of the seven private rooms of this namesake restaurant cleverly borrow from phrases found in ancient poems praising butterflies. Here, you will enjoy feasts of delicious Cantonese dishes and authentic Yunnan flavors.
