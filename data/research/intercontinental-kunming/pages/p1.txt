What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Kunming?

Does InterContinental Kunming offer free Wi-Fi?

How far is InterContinental Kunming from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Kunming is conveniently located near Kunming Changshui International Airport (KMG), approximately 25.23 miles away (40.6 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Kunming?

InterContinental Kunming is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- West Mountain-6.71 miles away (10.8 kilometers)
- Yunnan Ethnic Village-1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Dian Lake-0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Kunming puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
