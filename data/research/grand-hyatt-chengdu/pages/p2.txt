Experience Luxury Hotel Accommodation At Grand Hyatt Chengdu
Providing Chengdu accommodation and services, Grand Hyatt Chengdu features 370 lavishly appointed residential-style guestrooms in various styles, including the sophisticated Grand Room and Club Room; the double-height Atrium Deluxe Room; the elegant Grand Room; and the Grand Executive, Presidential and Chairman suites. All rooms come with floor-to-ceiling picture windows, which offer guests a bird’s-eye view of downtown Chengdu.
