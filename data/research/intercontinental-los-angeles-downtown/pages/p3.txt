# InterContinental Los Angeles DowntownDining

Los Angeles is known for its diverse culinary influences. Our downtown Los Angeles restaurants elevate fresh ingredients in surprising ways. Enjoy dry-aged wagyu at La Boucherie or champagne toasts at Spire 73, the tallest bar in the Western Hemisphere.

1. Curated Culinary Experiences

2. Admire Los Angeles from the 73rd Floor

### Curated Culinary Experiences

We are home to Los Angeles’ finest culinary experiences. Enjoy breakfast with a view at La Boucherie on the 71st floor. Indulge in unforgettable dining experiences at our renowned rooftop Spire 73 or enjoy exquisite steak and seafood at La Boucherie.

Learn more

### Admire Los Angeles from the 73rd Floor

Spire 73 is the perfect place to wind down your day or get your night started at the tallest open-air bar in the Western Hemisphere. Enjoy commanding rooftop views of downtown Los Angeles and a menu of mixologist-inspired cocktails, spirit-free cocktails, world-class wines, and celebratory bubbles on the 73rd floor.

## La Boucherie

La Boucherie offers an elevated steak and seafood experience with panoramic skyline views, bespoke cocktails, and an award-winning list of more than 300 hand-selected wines. From refined breakfast offerings to exquisite dinner service, every meal is crafted to impress.

Learn More

## Spire 73

Experience the tallest open-air bar in the Western Hemisphere at Spire 73. Enjoy craft cocktails, small bites, and a casual dinner by elegant fire pits, all while taking in the stunning Los Angeles sunset. It's the perfect spot to unwind or kick off your night under the stars.

## More Dining Options

### No Dive Bar

Open seasonally, No Dive Bar is located poolside on the hotel's 7th-floor. With a menu of elevated American favorites, adventurous cocktails, and refreshing spritzes, you can fuel your tanning session or pool hang without disturbing your towel. Cabana packages are available for those looking to make a day of it at our rooftop bar in Downtown Los Angeles.

**_Closed for the season._**
