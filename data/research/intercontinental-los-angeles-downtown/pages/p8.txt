## Explore Los Angeles

### Los Angeles Convention Center

1201 S Figueroa St., Los Angeles, CA 9001

Home to the city's most noteworthy conventions, exhibitions, and industry meetings, the Los Angeles Convention Center is where our grandest gatherings happen. The LA Convention Center, near our downtown Los Angeles hotel, hosts everything from LA Comic Con to international trade shows. The iconic building, with its towering glass facade, is a pleasant 15-minute walk from the hotel—so you can arrive looking your best whether you're in costume or not.

Learn More

### L.A. Live / Crypto.com Arena / Peacock Theater

1111 Figueroa St., Los Angeles, CA 90015

Along with neighboring Crypto.com Arena, L.A. Live offers an astonishing array of live entertainment and sports experiences. Commune with music legends at the GRAMMY Museum, see a concert at Peacock Theater or catch a Clippers, Kings, Lakers, or Sparks game near our downtown Los Angeles hotel. More of a movie buff? Regal’s 14-screen megaplex will bring your silver-screen dreams to life.

Learn More

### Disney Concert Hall and the L.A. Music Center

135 N Grand Avenue, Los Angeles, CA 90012

Housed in Frank Gehry's playful monument of gleaming stainless steel, Walt Disney Concert Hall is where the Los Angeles Philharmonic performs their season of renowned concerts. With its celebrated acoustics, everything from the classical canon's masterworks to bombastic film scores sound their legendary best. Part of the Music Center performing arts complex, Walt Disney Concert Hall is near our hotel and surrounded by music venues and public spaces offering a variety of events and shows.

Learn More

### BMO Stadium

3939 South Figueroa St., Los Angeles, CA 90037

Soccer fans will enjoy a riveting game at BMO Stadium. The famed stadium is home to both Major League Soccer's Los Angeles FC, and the National Women's Soccer League's Angel City FC. The venue also hosts concerts and other events.

Learn More

### Hollywood Walk of Fame

Hollywood Blvd. & Vine St., Hollywood, CA

The star-studded Hollywood Walk of Fame remains a proverbial, favorite thing to do in downtown Los Angeles. A historic landmark, the Walk spans 15 blocks along Hollywood Boulevard, and displays nearly 3,000 terrazzo-and-brass stars, each honoring a film industry legend.

Learn More

### SoFi Stadium

1001 S. Stadium Drive, Inglewood, CA 90301

Opened in 2020, SoFi Stadium is an entertainment destination in Inglewood, CA. The stadium can seat over 70,000 people and serves as home field for two NFL teams - the Los Angeles Rams and Los Angeles Chargers.

Learn More

### Santa Monica Pier

200 Santa Monica Pier, Santa Monica, CA 90401

The Santa Monica Pier is a large double-jointed pier in Santa Monica. The pier contains Pacific Park, a family amusement park with its one-of-a-kind, state-of-the-art, solar paneled ferris wheel. There are vendors and restaurants to enjoy. You can also soak up some sun on the popular Santa Monica beach.

Learn More

### Shopping on Rodeo Drive

Just twenty minutes by car, awaits the legendary Rodeo Drive - the epicenter of elegance, fashion and decadence. Flagship stores and designer boutiques pepper three blocks of the famous Drive. Spend the day shopping, then restoring over drinks and small plates at one of the legendary, chef-helmed restaurants.

Learn More

### Venice Beach

1900 Ocean Front Walk, Venice Beach, CA

Venice Beach is a fun promenade that runs parallel to the beach with shopping, food, beach fun, the famed Muscle Beach, handball courts, paddle tennis courts, volleyball courts, bike trail, basketball courts, and more.

Learn More
