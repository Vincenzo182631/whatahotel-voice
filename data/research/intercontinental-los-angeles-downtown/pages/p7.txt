### Quite awe full

10 days ago

Possibly the worst hotel stay I’ve ever had. There is no denying that the property itself is among the finest IHG has to offer, but the service provided by the staff falls woefully short of the standards its ambience and reputation promise.

Everyone in our group felt that we were treated poorly, and the staff demonstrated a disappointing lack of basic courtesy and etiquette. The most appalling incident was when they were unable to process my card payment and, instead of handling the situation professionally, sent security over in the name of hotel policy. I cannot comment on whether this was standard protocol, but the way the situation was handled was completely unacceptable. What makes it even more frustrating is that when I went to the reception and asked them to retry the payment, the transaction went through without any issue.

For a property of this calibre, such a shocking lack of professionalism, common sense, and basic hospitality is simply inexcusable. The beautiful property may create a great first impression, but the staff’s conduct completely ruined the experience. Without a doubt, this was among the most disappointing hotel service experiences I have ever had. IHG needs to seriously address the gap between the quality of its properties and the standard of service delivered by the people employed to represent the brand.
