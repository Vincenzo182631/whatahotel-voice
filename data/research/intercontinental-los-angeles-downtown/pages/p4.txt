### Rooftop Pool

The rooftop pool area at Intercontinental Downtown Los Angeles is located on the 7th floor and offers stunning views of the city. The pool is heated, surrounded by lounge chairs and cabanas for guests to relax in. There is also a hot tub and a poolside bar serving drinks and light snacks.

Hours of Operation: Daily 8AM - 8PM

_Pool is exclusively for hotel guests_

### No Dive Bar

No Dive Bar, located poolside offers a chic and vibrant atmosphere perfect for relaxation and socializing. Guests can indulge in a selection of gourmet bites, including artisanal flatbreads, fresh salads, and flavorful tacos. The bar also features an extensive cocktail menu, showcasing handcrafted drinks and providing a delightful culinary experience under the Los Angeles sky.

**Hours of Operation:** Saturday 11:30 AM - 6:00 PM

Sunday 11:30 AM - 6:30 PM

### Sustainability in Action at InterContinental LA Downtown

Luxury meets environmental responsibility at InterContinental Los Angeles Downtown. As the first LEED Gold-Certified building in Downtown LA, we’re committed to reducing our footprint while enhancing guest experiences.

Our sustainability initiatives include high-efficiency indoor plumbing fixtures, a 50,000-gallon storm water storage tank for cooling, and energy-efficient building design. We also prioritize local food sourcing, eliminate single-use plastics, and employ ORCA food waste digesters.

Experience elevated hospitality with a greener future in mind.
