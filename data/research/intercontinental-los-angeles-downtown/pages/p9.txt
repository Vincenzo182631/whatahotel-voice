# Club InterContinental

## For Making the Most Of Your Stay

Club InterContinental combines personal service and exclusive privileges to make your stay extra special. We aim beyond the obvious, turning our attention to thoughtful touches and signature moments.

Hours: 6:30 AM - 10:00 PM daily

### Seamless Arrival, Elevated Experience

Enjoy an exclusive welcome with **private check-in at the Club InterContinental Lounge**.

Skip the front desk and start your stay in style with personalized service, light refreshments, and a serene setting designed for relaxation.

Your luxury experience begins the moment you arrive.

### Guest Relations

We are here for you. From dinner reservations, to finding the perfect Los Angeles souvenir or arranging airport transportation, we look forward to making your stay a memorable and enjoyable one.

### Food and Beverage Offerings

**6:30 am – 10 pm** All day soft drinks, hot beverages, and fresh fruit

**6:30 am – 10:30 am** All American breakfast buffet

**5:30 pm – 7:30 pm** Cocktails & hors d’oeuvres

### As Well As...

- Complimentary usage of our elegant boardroom (seating up to 8 people) for up to 2 hours per day
- Complimentary access to PressReader, all-you-can read digital newsstand with thousands of the world’s most popular newspapers and magazines
- Instant access to PressReader via PressReader.com or by downloading the PressReader app
