How far is InterContinental Los Angeles Downtown from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Los Angeles Downtown is conveniently located near Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), approximately 17 miles away (27.36 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
