# InterContinental Los Angeles DowntownRooms & suites

## Contemporary Rooms & Suites Above Downtown Los Angeles

Rising high above the city, the rooms and suites at InterContinental Los Angeles Downtown offer a refined expression of modern luxury shaped by scale and perspective. Located on the upper floors of one of the tallest buildings in the city, each accommodation features expansive layouts, spa‑inspired design, and floor‑to‑ceiling windows framing panoramic views of the Los Angeles skyline and California horizon. Whether visiting for business or leisure, guests enjoy a serene retreat defined by space, light, and elevated comfort.

View Virtual Tour

### Elevated Accommodations with Panoramic Views

Designed to balance architectural presence with everyday comfort, every room and suite at InterContinental Los Angeles Downtown offers a composed and welcoming setting high above the city.

- Floor‑to‑ceiling windows with sweeping skyline views
- Generous layouts designed for comfort and productivity
- Contemporary interiors with refined finishes

- Spa‑inspired bathrooms with premium bath amenities
- High‑speed Wi‑Fi and in‑room dining available

#### Classic & Premium Rooms

Classic and Premium Rooms offer generous space and a tranquil atmosphere above downtown Los Angeles. Thoughtful layouts, modern finishes, and expansive windows create a refined setting well suited to both short stays and longer visits.

#### Suites

The suites at InterContinental Los Angeles Downtown provide an elevated residential experience defined by scale, privacy, and perspective. Featuring separate living areas and expansive views, these accommodations are designed for extended stays, entertaining, or occasions that call for additional space and distinction.
