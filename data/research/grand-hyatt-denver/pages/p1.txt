## Experience Denver From the Heart of Downtown

At Grand Hyatt Denver, some of the city’s best experiences are within easy reach, from 16th Street and Union Station to Coors Field and Ball Arena. Comfortable guestrooms offer city or Rocky Mountain views, while unique amenities include a rooftop jogging track and combined tennis/pickleball court, heated indoor pool and 24-hour fitness center. Enjoy locally inspired fare and craft cocktails at Courier. market \| bar \| kitchen, or settle in by the fire in our redesigned lobby featuring furnishings by RH. For meetings and celebrations, the Pinnacle Club, Denver’s highest indoor event venue, rises 38 floors above the city with panoramic downtown and Rocky Mountain views.

## ROOMS & SUITES

After spending the day exploring downtown Denver, retreat to some of the metro area’s most spacious and comfortable hotel accommodations. Enjoy the contemporary comforts and inspiring city views of our modern rooms and suites, or reward yourself with a luxurious stay and upgrade to our Corner King Room or Executive Suite for more space and ultimate relaxation.

### courier. kitchen & bar

Located on the ground floor of Grand Hyatt Denver, courier. kitchen & bar is a contemporary, newsroom-inspired restaurant that nods to the building’s history as the former home of one of Denver’s oldest newspapers. Start your morning with our daily breakfast buffet, featuring an omelet station, made-to-order eggs, fresh waffles and all your breakfast favorites. Stop in for weekday lunch from 11 AM–3 PM, or gather at Courier. Bar for daily Happy Hour from 3–6 PM, featuring drinks and bites in a relaxed downtown setting.

## WEDDINGS & MEETINGS

### Weddings & Celebrations

Our downtown hotel features 52,600 square feet of sophisticated wedding and reception venues designed to make your special day unforgettable. Host your wedding reception for up to 1,000 guests in the Colorado Ballroom, or exchange vows in the Pinnacle Club at Capitol’s Peak, with space for up to 330 guests and a backdrop of mountain views and Denver’s highest indoor event venue. Our team has experience with weddings of all sizes and traditions, including Indian weddings and multi-day celebrations, ensuring each event reflects your vision from start to finish.

VIEW DETAILS

### Meetings & Events

With 52,600 square feet of flexible meeting and event space, our downtown hotel offers the perfect setting for gatherings of every size. Plan a large convention in our connected conference center, host an event high above the city in the 38th-floor Pinnacle Club, or reserve one of our modern boardrooms for smaller meetings and training sessions. Our experienced event specialists, creative catering team, and convenient downtown Denver location ensure every detail runs seamlessly.

Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
