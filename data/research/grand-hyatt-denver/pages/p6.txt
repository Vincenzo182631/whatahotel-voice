# Hotel Policies

Destination Fee

Future Changes: Bookings on and beyond February 01, 2027 - A daily destination fee of $30.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

- 10% Off Merchandise in Courier Market
- Welcome drink upon arrival
- 10% off Lunch in Courier
- 15% off Colorado Symphony
- 20% off Spa Juniper
- $5 Off Red Rocks Concert Shuttle Per Ticket
- Premium Internet
- Board Game and Tennis/Pickle Ball Rental
- Appetizer in Courier for Dinner with a purchase of one craft cocktail. \*max 1 per table.

Pet Policy

Your pets are welcome at Grand Hyatt Denver, where our dog-friendly hotel will be waiting with treats for your pup. We welcome up to two dogs per room. Dogs may not exceed 50 pounds each or 75 pounds combined. Please note the following fees apply:

1–6 nights: $125 / 1 STAY

7–30 nights (additional fees): $125 /1 STAY

31 nights or more: Fee assessed at the hotel’s discretion

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Package Handling

To ensure correct shipping, storage, and delivery to a guest, please address as follows:

Grand Hyatt Denver

Attn: GUEST NAME / ARRIVAL DATE

1750 Welton Street Denver, CO 80202

A package handling fee of $20 will apply to each package received at the Grand Hyatt Denver. Shipments are held for a limited number of days. If the package has not been picked up after 30 days, it will be returned to sender, who will be responsible for all additional shipping fees.

Cash-Free Policy

**CASH FREE** \- Please note that this hotel only accepts credit cards, debit cards and, where applicable, other contactless forms of payment. It is a cash-free environment.
