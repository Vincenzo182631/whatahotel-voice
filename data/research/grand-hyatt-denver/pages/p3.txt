# Dining

Whether you are craving a classic burger, fine wine, or a house-made pastry and espresso, your choice awaits. Savor classic American dishes with locally-sourced ingredients at Courier., meet for drinks at Fireside \| The Bar, find your on-the-go selections in Courier. Market, or order meals anytime in the comfort of your room. Courier., once home to one of Denver’s oldest newspapers, features a private dining room for intimate celebrations or corporate gatherings. The Grand Hyatt Denver hotel restaurant and bars offer satisfying dining for travelers and locals alike.

## courier. kitchen & bar

Spooky season and bottomless mimosas go hand-in-hand. Join us for our bottomless spooky brunch on Saturday, October 17, 24, and 31 from 6AM - 11:30AM!

Located on the ground floor of Grand Hyatt Denver, courier. kitchen & bar is a contemporary, newsroom-inspired restaurant that nods to the building’s history as the former home of one of Denver’s oldest newspapers. Start your morning with our daily breakfast buffet, featuring an omelet station, made-to-order eggs, fresh waffles and all your breakfast favorites. Stop in for weekday lunch from 11 AM–3 PM, or gather at Courier. Bar for daily Happy Hour from 3–6 PM, featuring drinks and bites in a relaxed downtown setting.

## Fireside \| The Bar

A signature of our hotel’s dramatic lobby is Fireside \| The Bar. Here, guests can relax with their favorite cocktail, enjoy live music from local artists on select nights, and join friends and colleagues for our happy hour, before heading out to a downtown restaurant. Linear stacked fireplaces and dark wood panels overhead envelop the space, making it an inviting retreat during your time in Denver.
