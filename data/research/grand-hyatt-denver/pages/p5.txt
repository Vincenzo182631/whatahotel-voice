I stayed at the Grand Hyatt whilst at a conference. It's well located for the conference centre (5-10 minute walk) and fairly easy to get to from the airport (car or public transport). Overall this hotel gives a very good impression: staff are friendly and helpful; my room was spacious, clean and beautifully serviced each day; the public areas are nicely furnished and comfortable; and the breakfast was excellent. To be honest it surpassed my expectations, and I would definitely stay here again if in denver.

### Standard Hyatt, convenient, tad pricey with terrible coffee

This hotel is your standard fair for a hyatt. It is conveniently located in downtown and walking distance to several restaurants and about 10 minutes to the Denver convention center. Staff was friendly, and cleaning service even placed a small toothpaste packet seeing my current was just about out. Oddly friendly. There is no club lounge at this property that I could find. I had breakfast at the courier kitchen restaurant on the lobby floor. It is your standard breakfast buffet but with a custom omelette station where you can request one be made for you fresh. It's convenient because you can pick and choose what you want for breakfast without planning your morning to find a place for those where breakfast is important to them. The few caveats here is that breakfast was a whopping $30+ and the coffee was quite bad. It tasted sour and bitter.
