## CIELO Daylight

Looking towards the magnificent bay view, CIELO Daylight combines minimal design with a laid-back verdant atmosphere. From the comfort of your cozy sun lounger, sofa, or table, the stylish CIELO Daylight invites guests to enjoy blissful moments indoors or in an outdoor seafront setting.

## Plefsis Tavern

Situated right on the waterfront, the old-time favorite taverna "Plefsis" is the most genuine place to enjoy a unique magical sunset facing Grikos bay in a warm and friendly atmosphere. With a long gastronomic tradition, Plefsis tavern is famous for its authentic Greek recipes inspired mainly by the Dodecanese or local cuisine using fresh and seasonal ingredients.
