# Welcome to Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece

## Where ordinary is simply extraordinary

Nestled on the serene shores of Patmos island, Patmos Aktis Resort & Spa is a sanctuary of refined luxury and natural beauty. Overlooking the azure waters of Grikos Bay, the resort offers a harmonious...See More blend of traditional Greek charm and contemporary elegance. Immerse yourself in the breathtaking surroundings of the meticulously redesigned suites, each offering unparalleled comfort and sophistication. From private balconies and panoramic views to lavish amenities, every detail is crafted to elevate your stay. Savor Mediterranen flavors at the exquisite restaurants, where fresh, locally sourced ingredients are transformed into culinary masterpieces that tantalize the palate. Unwind by the shimmering waters of Aktis Beach or take a leisurely stroll along the pristine coast that surround the resort. At Patmos Aktis, rediscover the art of relaxation and rejuvenation. Whether seeking a tranquil escape, a romantic getaway, or an unforgettable family vacation, we promise an unforgettable experience.See Less

### ANASA Wellness Spa

ANASA Wellness & Spa is a serene haven where luxury meets holistic wellbeing, inspired by the Greek word Anasa, meaning breath. Every experience is designed to restore balance, nurture the mind, body, and spirit, and awaken a sense of calm. Treatments are carefully crafted to care for energy levels and skin rejuvenation while restoring inner balance, with access to sauna, rasul, hammam, indoor pool, fitness facilities, and tailored wellness programs created by expert therapists to support individual wellbeing goals.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece?

The check-in time at Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 10.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €145.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece?

On-site parking is available at Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece booking.

What is the closest airport near Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece?

The closest airport near Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece is Leros Municipal Airport (LRS). LRS is located approximately 24.5 KM from the hotel.

Does Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Patmos Aktis, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Greece has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
