## A Paradise for Swimming

Renowned as one of the world's most stunning coastal paradises, the beach in front of Patmos Aktis is nestled on the southeast side of the sacred Patmos Island in Grikos Bay. Framed by mesmerizing rock formations and serene, marine life-rich waters, Grikos Bay offers a tranquil, protected destination of natural beauty. The reputable Kalikatsou Rock, reflects its shape on the bay's waters. With sunbeds, beach towels, and umbrellas, guests can unwind in style under the shade of trees.

Monastery of St. John: UNESCO Heritage Site, showcasing Byzantine architecture and panoramic views.

Patmos Chora: Wander through the charming streets of Chora, known for its traditional buildings.

Skala: Explore the bustling port town with shops, waterfront cafes, and its vibrant atmosphere.
