Golf

Today, both guests and members at this luxury golf resort near Tucson, Arizona can experience the imaginative shots designed by Nicklaus, originally intended for top professionals and their friends.

A Picturesque
Jack Nicklaus Signature Golf Course
Renowned champion and designer Jack Nicklaus seamlessly merges tactical play, diverse challenges, and beauty into his Signature Course at The Golf Club at Dove Mountain, located in the Sonoran Desert. The 27-hole golf course near Tucson, Arizona, and expansive 45,000-square-foot clubhouse harmoniously blend with the saguaro-dotted foothills of the Tortolita Mountains.
Exclusive Tee-Times
Tee-times for The Golf Club at Dove Mountain are available exclusively to guests of the luxury golf hotel near Tucson, and members of the golf club. For more information about the golf experience, tee times, and golf club dining, please contact the Ladies and Gentlemen with The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain’s Guest Relations team.

Golf Club FAQs
What is the address of The Golf Club?
The golf club is located at 6501 W Boulder Bridge Pass, Marana, AZ
What are the operating hours?
The club is open daily from 6 AM - 4 PM
How many courses are there?
The golf club features three, nine-hole courses. The 27-hole championship course was designed by Jack Nicklaus.
What amenities are available?
The club offers a 45,000 square foot clubhouse, a driving range, pitching/chipping area, and putting green. They also provide rental clubs and carts.
Is there a dress code?
Yes, golf attire is required.
How do I book a tee time?
Guests are welcome to contact the resort to assist, or you are welcome to book tee times through their website, or by calling the golf club directly.
Is there a shuttle?
Yes, there is a complimentary shuttle for resort guests that runs throughout the day.
Are rental clubs available?
Yes, golf club rentals are available for those who need them. We encourage you to call and request rentals ahead of your tee time.
The Driving Range Is Available for Your Enjoyment
Guests of the resort are invited to hit balls on the golf club driving range including use of golf clubs and practice balls. Appropriate attire is required.

Activities & Experiences
Ancient Rock Tour
Visit well-known and secret locations where the ancient ones once lived and worked. This hike is mostly flat with minimal elevation change and is approximately 2.5 miles in length.
Cactus Hike
Explore the Sonoran Desert on the Lower Javelina trails. Sample edible cacti, walk among giant Saguaros, and take in sweeping views from Pride Rock on this unforgettable desert adventure.
Fireside S'mores
As the sun sets, join loved ones around the fire with a S’mores kit including house-made graham crackers, marshmallows, and chocolate.
Geocaching Quest
Join our self-guided, GPS-aided treasure hunt! Explore hidden gems from sunrise to sunset. One GPS unit per booking. Course takes about 2 hours.
Private and Group Hikes
Let our expert Rangers guide you through the Sonoran Desert with five unique hikes including the Cactus Discovery Hike, Views & Vistas Hike, Ancient Rock Art Hike, Sunset Hike, and the Night Hike.
Mixology Class
Experience mixology in our class and craft two cocktails using expert techniques. Includes one appetizer per two guests. Held Wednesdays and Saturdays at 2 PM. Reservations required.
Private Stargazing
Relax in comfort as you witness nebulas and galaxies projected in real-time on a stunning 55-inch monitor in full color. Discover why Tucson is considered the birthplace of the ‘dark sky movement’.
Tent Experience
Let your child experience the thrill of the great outdoors from the comfort of your own room with our in-room tent adventure.

Poolside Cabana Rentals
