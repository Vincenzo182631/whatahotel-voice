Information About Our Hikes
Private or Guided Options
We offer both private, customized hikes as well as group, guided hikes. Reservations are required.
Night Hike
When the sun goes down, nocturnal creatures come out. Discover the sights and sounds of the desert at night in this casual one-hour hike.
Sunset Hike
Experience the Sonoran Desert at the height of its beauty at sunset during this two-hour challenging hike.
Views and Vistas Hike
This moderate fitness hike offers a 500-foot elevation change and is geared towards getting your heart rate up while you enjoy stunning desert vistas.
Ancient Rock Art Hike
Discover ancient Hohokam petroglyphs found in Wild Burro Canyon on this casual hike.
Cactus Discovery Hike
Navigate the Lower Javelina trails on this moderate hike, and sample different cacti, cactus fruit, and other edibles, or sit atop Pride Rock and take in panoramic views of Wild Burro Canyon.
Refreshing Experiences
Take a dip at our hotel with pools near Tucson​ in three pristine swimming pools in a picturesque desert setting, including the expansive infinity-edge Turquesa Pool, the family-friendly Aventura Pool with a 235-foot waterslide, the children’s splash pad, or the adults-only Serenity Pool at The Spa at Dove Mountain featuring a 10-foot tanning island. All swimming pools feature Cabana rentals.
Cabana Rentals
Turquesa Pool
Relax at our infinity edge, salt-water, swimming pool with a welcoming environment, and a great view. The pool features a lounge deck, and hot tub, and sits adjacent to Turquesa Latin Grill.
Aventura Pool
The Aventura pool is easily accessed from the lounge chairs and cabanas on the lower level of the swimming pool. It is connected to action of children’s splash pad with dancing fountains, and our 235-foot water slide.
Waterslide & Splash Pad
The waterslide and splash pad are connected to the family-oriented Aventura pool which is easily accessed from lounge chairs and cabanas. Kids love the dancing fountains, and our 235-foot water slide. You must be 3-feet tall to go on the slide.

Top Nearby Attractions
The Ladies and Gentlemen of our Guest Relations team are available to assist with your reservations and excursions. Alternatively, most are available to reserve online at your leisure as well.
Arizona Sonoran Desert Museum
One of the most visited attractions in Southern Arizona, this destination includes a 98-acre zoo, aquarium, botanical garden, natural history museum, and art gallery. The musuem is home to over 230 animal species, 1,200 varieties of plants and two miles of walking paths over 21 acres of desert landscape.
Pima Air & Space Museum
The museum features 400 historic aircraft, from a Wright Flyer to a 787 Dreamliner on over 80 acres, with six indoor exhibit hangars (three dedicated to WWII).
Tucson Museum of Art and Historic Block
The Tucson Museum of Art and Historic Block encompasses an entire city block in historic downtown and houses a growing collection of nearly 12,000 works of art spanning over 3,000 years of history. Rotating and permanent collection exhibitions feature original and traveling shows focusing on Art of Latin America, Art of the American West, Indigenous Arts, and Modern and Contemporary, with related docent-led tours, educational activities, community engagement programs, and a Museum Store to delight and educate visitors.
Mission San Xavier Del Bac
A National Historic Landmark founded as a Catholic mission by Father Eusebio Kino in 1692. The oldest intact European structure in Arizona, the interior is filled with original statuary and mural paintings. Each year, over 200,000 visitors from all over the world come to view what is widely considered to be the finest example of Spanish Colonial architecture in the United States.

Ritz Kids
The Ritz Kids Rangers invite young explorers on a unique and exciting journey of discovery as they uncover the amazing facts and history of the Sonoran Desert. Ritz Kids Sessions are available for resort guests ages 5 through 12, and each unique morning or afternoon session offers curated activities focusing on life in Southern Arizona including Native American culture, desert survival skills, edible plant discovery, Southwestern art history, plants, and wildlife. Each half day consists of an interactive lesson followed by crafts and games. Afternoon sessions do not repeat the same activities as the mornings. The Ritz Kids Rangers program is available Friday through Sunday, and reservations are required at least 24-hours in advance. View additional details of our hotel with kids club near Tucson below.
Ritz Kids Ranger Programs
Tap into your little ones’ innate curiosity by introducing them to the natural wonders and rich cultural traditions of Southern Arizona at The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain. Designed for children ages 5 to 12, the Ritz Kids Ranger Program invites young explorers on a unique and exciting journey of discovery through the Sonoran Desert and the Tortolita Mountains.
We offer two distinct three-hour sessions daily, beginning at 9 a.m. and 1 p.m., hosted at the Dove Mountain Ritz Kids Ranger Headquarters. Children may participate in a Half Day Expedition by joining one session or opt for the Full Day Expedition to experience both. Full Day participants will enjoy a healthy lunch between adventures and benefit from additional personalized time with their Ranger guide.
Each session features curated, hands-on activities that explore the region’s Native American heritage, desert survival skills, edible plant discovery, Southwestern art history, and the unique flora and fauna of the Sonoran Desert. Morning and afternoon sessions offer different activities, ensuring a fresh experience throughout the day.
