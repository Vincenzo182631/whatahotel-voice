Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain
Embrace Wellness and Discover Adventure Near Tucson
The Ritz-Carlton Dove Mountain is a Forbes Travel Guide five-star hotel near Tucson in Marana. Nestled in the Sonoran Desert at an elevation of 3,000 feet in a canyon setting, guests enjoy an award-winning spa, three swimming pools, a 235-foot water slide, Jack Nicklaus Signature Golf, hiking, biking, stargazing, and five dining options. The spa at The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain is a haven of relaxation. Guests can unwind in the award-winning spa facilities at this luxury resort in Marana, Arizona, which include treatment rooms, relaxation lounges, and a fitness center. For those seeking adventure, we offer a variety of activities. Explore the scenic 26 miles of walking, hiking, and biking trails in the Tortolita Mountain Preserve, or go stargazing under our dark skies. Whether you're seeking relaxation or adventure, discover why The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain is a Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star luxury resort near Tucson, Arizona and a destination for travelers with a spirit of adventure.
At Core, A Sonoran Grill, the Desert comes to life
Guided by the rhythm of the seasons and inspired by ingredients gathered from the land and drawn from water, Core, A Sonoran Grill celebrates the cultures, traditions, and remarkable abundance of the Sonoran Desert. Join us for what’s next this fall.

Ritz Kids Ranger Program
Our Junior Ranger program takes young explorers on a unique and exciting journey of discovery as they uncover the amazing facts and history of the Sonoran Desert. Ritz Kids Sessions are available for resort guests ages 5 through 12, and each unique morning or afternoon session offers curated activities focusing on life in Southern Arizona including Native American culture, desert survival skills, edible plant discovery, Southwestern art history, plants, and wildlife.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 80.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain. The daily valet fee is $44.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain is Tucson International Airport (TUS). TUS is located approximately 33.5 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
