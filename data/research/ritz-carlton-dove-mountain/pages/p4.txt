Dining With Notable Views
Dining
Savor the spirit of Arizona’s Sonoran Desert through dining experiences that reflect both place and season. Each setting invites you to enjoy elevated cuisine paired with sweeping mountain views and warm, authentic hospitality. At The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain, dining becomes a journey as inspiring as the landscape itself.
At Core, A Sonoran Grill, the Desert comes to life
Guided by the rhythm of the seasons and inspired by ingredients gathered from the land and drawn from water, Core, A Sonoran Grill celebrates the cultures, traditions, and remarkable abundance of the Sonoran Desert. Join us for what’s next this fall.
Dining Experiences
Spirit of Adventure Celebration
Join us nightly at 5:15 p.m. around Ignite Lounge’s Patio. Experience the enchanting sounds of a Native American flautist as they play traditional songs to honor the end of the day in our restaurant with patio dining near Tucson. This serene and magical event lasts approximately 25 minutes, offering a perfect moment to unwind and reflect amidst the stunning backdrop of the Sonoran Desert.
Mixology Class
Experience the art of exquisitely crafted cocktails through a harmonious blend of recipes, techniques, and expert knowledge. Includes two cocktail creations during the class, and one appetizer per two people. Wednesdays and Saturdays at 2 PM. Reservations are required.
Reserve Online
Wine Experience
Join us on a wine journey through different varietals paired with chef-selected gourmet cheese. Limited availability, on Tuesdays and Fridays, with seating at 5 PM in Core Kitchen & Wine Bar.
Reserve Online
Sushi Nights at Ignite
Sushi Nights are a favorite and reservations are encouraged. Served Thursdays through Saturdays from 5:30 PM to 9:30 PM, Chef Paul has crafted an a la carte menu of Nigiri, Sashimi, Uzuzukuri, Tataki, Signature Maki Sushi, and Oshizushi.
The Agave Experience
Join us for this culinary experience offered each Wednesday and Thursday at 5 PM. Enjoy a handcrafted cocktail and a flight of three agave-based spirits that is paired with our Five-C’s of Arizona dish. Discover the five pillars of our beautiful state in one unforgettable experience. Reservations are required.
Learn More
Fireside S’mores
As the sun sets, join loved ones around the lower firepits with a S’mores kit including house-made graham crackers, marshmallows and premium chocolate. Our Rangers will get the fire going for you so you can enjoy!
