Signature Accommodations
The signature casitas and suites at our family suite hotel near Tucson provide a serene escape nestled amidst the stunning desert and mountain landscape for added privacy. Ladies and Gentlemen enjoy the spacious living areas, terraces, and patios with wonderful desert views. Whether you are traveling alone, or with extended family and friends, our casita and suite accommodations (many with connecting room options) offer the perfect blend of comfort and elegance, complete with Southern Arizona charm.
Fireside
Casita
Situated within the Casita Village at our family suite hotel near Tucson, the Fireside Casita welcomes guests with the peace and quiet of the surrounding desert, perfect for privacy and memorable views. The Fireside Casita is 1100 square feet and features a living area with a sofa sleeper and a half bath, as well as a private guest room with a king bed, fireplace and en-suite bathroom with walk in shower, soaking bathtub and dual sinks. There are two private terraces; one features lounge chairs and a firepit and is accessed off of the living room, while the other is accessed via the main guest room.
