Powering Curiosity & Creativity
Ritz Kids
The Dove Mountain Ritz Kids Rangers invite our guests ages 5-12 to join in a unique and exciting journey of discovery as they explore the meaning of the “Spirit of Adventure” that lives here at The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain.
Book Now
Private and Group Hikes
Allow our expert Rangers to guide you through the Sonoran Desert with five unique hikes including the Cactus Discovery Hike, Views & Vistas Hike, Ancient Rock Art Hike, Sunset Hike, and the Night Hike.
Reserve Your Hike Experience
Spirit of Adventure Celebration
Join us on the patio at sunset as we recount The Legend of the Spirit of Adventure where a brief tale of a Hohokam brave is shared. This is followed by traditional, live, Native American flute music.
Private Stargazing
Relax in comfort as you witness nebulas and galaxies projected in real-time on a stunning 55-inch monitor in full color. Discover why Tucson is considered the birthplace of the ‘dark sky movement’.
Learn More
Treats By The Fire
Fireside S'mores
As the sun sets, join loved ones around the fire with a S’mores kit including house-made graham crackers, marshmallows and chocolate.
Book Now
Arcade
Ready for some indoor stimulation? Head to The Arcade featuring game consoles, basketball, skee-ball and more.
Recruit-a-Ranger
Create a one-of-a-kind take home stuffed animal to remind you of the unique animals of the Sonoran Desert, and your magical stay at The Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain.
Geocaching
Geocaching Quest
Join us in a self-guided, tech-aided treasure hunt to explore our backyard for hidden delights. Guest will be provided one GPS unit per booking that may be used from sunrise to sunset. The self-guided geocaching course typically takes 2 hours.
Reserve Your Geocache
Explore On Bike
Electric Bike Rentals
With miles of scenic trails at our doorstep, two wheels is a great way to explore the surrounding Sonoran Desert. Rent an electric bike and enjoy a picturesque adventure as you wind through the spectacular terrain and soak in majestic views of the Tortolita Mountains.
Book Now
Tasting
Wine Experience
Join Rodney Remily as he leads you on a journey through different varietals accompanied by a Chef’s selection of cheeses. Reservations are recommended.
Reserve Your Wine Tasting
Mixology Class
Experience the art of exquisitely crafted cocktails through a harmonious blend of recipes, techniques, and expert knowledge. Includes two cocktail creations during the class, and one appetizer per two people. Wednesdays and Saturdays at 2 PM. Reservations are required.
Reserve Mixology Class
In-Room Adventures
Tent Experience
Let your child experience the thrill of the great outdoors from the comfort of your own room with our in-room tent adventure.
Book Now
Sunset Hike
Sunset Vista Tour
Leave the day's stresses behind you and experience the Sonoran Desert at its height of beauty... sunset. Take in the aura of a Saguaro cactus as the sun sets behind it, view different mountain ranges and get a bird's-eye view of The Golf Club at Dove Mountain.
Book Now
Ancient Exploration
Ancient Rock Tour
Visit well-known and secret locations where the ancient ones once lived and worked. This hike is mostly flat with minimal elevation change and is approximately 2.5 miles in length.
Book Now
Desert Exploration
Cactus Hike
Witness a multitude of cactus varieties, walk amongst giant Saguaros, and taste the bounty that is the Sonoran Desert. On this adventure you will navigate the Lower Javelina trails, where you have the opportunity to sample different cacti, cacti fruit and other edibles, or sit atop Pride Rock and take in vast panoramic views of Wild Burro Canyon.
Book Now
