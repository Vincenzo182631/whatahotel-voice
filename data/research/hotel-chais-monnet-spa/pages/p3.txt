# Bar & rooftop 1838

## THE bar

Located in the former Monnet cooperage, "Le 1838" offers some 300 cognac references from over 40 houses, as well as a wide range of spirits and champagne selected by our teams.

Every evening, the team showcases its latest creations in a festive atmosphere.

Discover some of the secrets of mixology, and create your own unique creation during our cocktail masterclass.

### A Custom Map

Here, every drink tells a story. The Head Bartender at Bar & Rooftop 1838 has crafted a menu of signature cocktails where creativity and expertise come together throughout the seasons. Alongside these, a carefully curated selection of over 200 cognacs invites you on a journey between tradition and exploration. To complement these moments of pure enjoyment, a custom-designed tapas menu—balancing indulgence with lightness—has been created to ensure the evening continues in the best possible way.

See the Bar Menu

## The rooftop

Perched above Cognac, the rooftop of Bar 1838 offers an exceptional view of the city. In an elegant and intimate atmosphere, it invites you to savor signature cocktails and tapas from a new menu to discover at sunset, in a setting designed for relaxation and summer escape, punctuated by games of pétanque on the terrace in a friendly and laid-back spirit.

### Closed for the season

Le Rooftop has closed for the winter season. Come back in the summer of 2027 for evenings filled with enchanting music, carrying the magic of Cognac’s sunsets well into the night.

‍

_Please note that during the winter season, Bar 1838 takes over from the Rooftop to welcome you._

## evening events

# Distillers' Gathering

Several times a month, Bar 1838 hosts the “Rendez-vous des Distillateurs,” a series of evening events dedicated to exploring its partner cognac houses.

‍

In an elegant and welcoming atmosphere, these events showcase the producers’ expertise through complimentary tastings provided by the guest house. It’s also an opportunity to discover signature creations crafted by the bartenders at Bar 1838, ranging from refined cocktails to contemporary takes on cognac.

‍

A unique sensory experience, open to all, designed for both enthusiasts and the simply curious.

‍

## HEAD BARTENDER MICKAËL ULRICH

Our talented head bartender, Mickael Ulrich, welcomes you to the pinnacle of the cocktail experience.

Mickael arrived at the beginning of 2025 and has breathed new life into our bar by completely reinventing the cocktail menu. His original creations, both daring and refined, are already winning over customers with their balance, aestheticism and inventiveness.

Creative, rigorous and passionate, he conceives each cocktail as a true sensory experience, designed to sublimate the moment and perfectly match the panoramic view from our rooftop.

Together with his bartenders Isaack, Vito, and Nicolas, he forms a close-knit and friendly team, where the joy of welcoming guests is evident every moment you spend at the bar.

Whether you're looking for a sunset aperitif or an evening under the stars, our team invites you to enjoy a unique culinary interlude of elegance, sharing and creativity.
