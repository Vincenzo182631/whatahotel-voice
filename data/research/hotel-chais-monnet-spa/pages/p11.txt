# STAY AT THE APARTMENTS

## A COMFORT WARM AND HOMELY

Housed in a separate building, Hotel Chais Monnet & Spa offers four contemporary one-bedroom apartments, three two-bedroom apartments and two three-bedroom apartments, fully equipped for optimum comfort. All apartments are equipped with air conditioning, refrigerator, dishwasher, coffee machine, shower, free toiletries and desk. Rooms include a wardrobe, flat-screen TV and en suite bathroom.
