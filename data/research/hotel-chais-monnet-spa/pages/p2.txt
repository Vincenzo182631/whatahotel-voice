## A REFINED DECORREFINED

Spread over four buildings, the 92 rooms and suites are decorated in a classic French chic style.

The combination of noble materials and natural materials ensures both aesthetic and sensory comfort. In the

"Chais", guests will experience the charm of a historic building that combines rich heritage and modernity.

### LA SUITE JEAN gabriel monnet

170 M2

The quintessential luxury of the Chais Monnet experience, our Jean Gabriel Monnet Presidential Suite boasts 170m² of hotel excellence.

The ultimate refinement, our suite offers privileged access to a private treatment room where our guests can enjoy a massage by one of our SPA therapists.

The Jean Gabriel Monnet Suite offers guests a grand reception room, dining area and cooking facilities, it is undoubtedly the most soothing and distinguished space in our establishment.
