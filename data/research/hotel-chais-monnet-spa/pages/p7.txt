# A CONTEMPORARY  HOTEL  IN THE HEART OF AN   INDUSTRIAL HISTORICAL SITE

.webp)

Located in the heart of Cognac, 1.5 hours from Bordeaux, the 5-star Hôtel Chais Monnet & Spa offers 92 rooms and suites, a Michelin-starred gourmet restaurant, a contemporary brasserie, a bar, a rooftop lounge, meeting rooms, and a wellness area including a spa, fitness room, jacuzzi, hammam, and sauna.

Spread over four buildings, the 92 rooms and suites are each adorned  in a classic French sophisticated decor.  The combination of noble and natural materials not only enhances the aesthetic appeal but ensures a sensory comfort that elevates the guest experience.

In the "Chais", the charm of a historic building that combines rich heritage and modernity lives on.

### Rémy Martin: An introduction to the world of Louis XIII

Treat yourself to a sensory voyage to the heart of the absolute icon of cognac: Louis XIII. Born in Grande Champagne, an exceptional terroir where the rarest eaux-de-vie are produced, Louis XIII embodies excellence and patience, the result of several generations of cellar masters.
