# THE DESTINATION

## THE SOUL OF COGNAC   , between vineyards and rivers

‍

Just 90 minutes from Bordeaux, the Hôtel Chais Monnet & Spa welcomes you to a place where time seems to stand still, in the heart of the magnificent Charente region. Cognac, a historic gem with elegant streets and golden stone houses, invites you on a journey combining art de vivre, history, and refinement.

‍

Between prestigious vineyards and tranquil rivers, Charente reveals itself in all its beauty: breathtaking picturesque landscapes, charming villages, and a rare gentle way of life. Here, every moment becomes an experience: tasting a fine cognac in its iconic production sites, strolling along the Charente River, or immersing yourself in the architectural and cultural heritage.

‍

Our prime location makes the Chais Monnet Hotel & Spa the ideal starting point for an unforgettable getaway, where luxury and authenticity come together to create lasting memories.

## MARITIME TREASURES  , just a stone's throw away

The Charente-Maritime region reveals to be our hidden gem, with the sea front and sandy beaches only a short drive away. Explore the wild “Île de Ré” and “Île d’Oléron” both famous for their oysters and enjoyable bike rides. Both islands have very different offerings Ile de ré is the most luxurious destination of the two with sophisticated terraces and gourmet restaurants. Whereas l’île d’Oléron is locally known as more of a family destination, with wild surroundings and authentic cobbled streets in town like Chateau d’Oléron or Saint-Pierre d'Oléron.  La Rochelle, Royan, and Saint-Palais sur Mer, towns offer guests’ ideal seaside daytrip getaways. The location of the Hôtel Chais Monnet & Spa allows you to discover the Atlantic coastline and the relaxed atmosphere of these sought-after destinations, all within a short distance from Cognac.

## VINEYARD LANDSCAPES

Wine lovers will be delighted by the proximity of famous wine regions such as Bordeaux, Saint-Emilion and Médoc. Take a trip into the world of wine and its vineyards. Discover renowned estates and taste exceptional nectars. Our privileged location offers easy access to these prestigious wine lands, allowing our visitors to fully experience the region's viticultural heritage.

Your stay at the Hôtel Chais Monnet & Spa is an opportunity to experience behind the scenes of wine making in the Charente and the Bordeaux area.

## ESCAPADES inour region

### AQUARIUM LA ROCHELLE

Dive into the fascinating world of the La Rochelle Aquarium, great for a day trip with children of all ages. Home to an impressive array of marine life, the aquarium showcases an extraordinary collection of species, from sharks to the smallest seahorses.

### TALMONT SUR GIRONDE

On a rocky promontory overlooking the Gironde estuary, Talmont has kept its original bastide layout, built in 1284 by Edward 1st of Aquitaine. Overlooking the village, the magnificent church of Sainte-Radegonde, symbol of the Poitou-Charentes region, is still fortified by ramparts.

### PALEOSITE

Step back in time at the Paléosite de Saint-Césaire, a unique archaeological site where the secrets of prehistory come to life. An immersive experience for history buffs and those curious about human evolution.

### SAINTES

Proudly bearing the "Ville d'art et d'histoire" label since 1989, Saintes offers a unique experience in stepping back in time. Stroll through its cobbled streets, explore its historic monuments and soak up its exceptional heritage.  Saintes is an enjoyable town with a selection of cafés, and restaurants to choose from.

### THE COMIC MUSEUM

Located in the heart of Angoulême, this captivating museum celebrates the evolution of this art form with a collection of more than 8,000 original drawings and over 110,000 magazines and comic books. Angouleme is one of the leading animation and digital art centers in France, the museum celebrates the authors of Tintin, Popeye and even Asterix & Obelix. An unmissable visit for comic strip fans.
