# ACTIVITIES ANDEXPERIENCES

## THE COGNAC HOUSES

### HENNESSY

Maison Hennessy invites you to enjoy a multi-sensory experience in Cognac, between the two banks of the Charente. An authentic visit to discover 250 years of history and expertise.

### REMY MARTIN

Discover the fascinating history of the Maison Rémy Martin and the different stages of cognac production in their ageing cellar. During a guided tasting, explore the rich aromas of the prestigious Fine Champagne cognacs.

### BACHE GABRIELSEN

To learn about the rich history of the Maison Bache Gabrielsen, the work of a Maison de négoce or that of its cellar master, come and discover the secrets of cognac and its production.

### DELAMAIN

Immerse yourself in a world where every drop of cognac tells a unique story. For a unique sensory encounter, Delamain invites you on a journey back in time.

‍

### CAMUS

After discovering the family passion that keeps Camus alive, and recognizing the many aromas during a guided tasting, create your own unique cognac and leave with your own personalized bottle to share with your friends.

‍

### LES FRÈRES MOINES

Spend an afternoon at the famous Frères Moine cognac house, where you can observe the craftsmanship of barrel-making, discover the secrets of cognac distillation, and enjoy an exclusive cognac or pineau tasting.

‍

### RAISON PERSONNELLE

Let yourself be seduced by the authenticity of Raison Personnelle. Meet the craftsmen behind each delicate note, discover the richness of a cognac shaped by love of the craft, and let yourself be transported to the heart of artisanal know-how.

‍

### MARTELL

Immerse yourself in the captivating essence of the House of Martell. A captivating sensory adventure awaits you, from vineyard and cellar tours to cocktail workshops. Become experts in Martell cognac thanks to this unforgettable experience.

‍

_6_11zon.webp)

### SHINING BEAUTY

Embark on a sensory adventure at the heart of Maison Brillet. Nestled on the banks of the Charente River, this charming estate invites you to discover a family tradition passed down through 10 generations. From exploring the cellars to tasting Belle de Brillet—an exceptional liqueur crafted from Williams pears and Charente cognac—let yourself be captivated by a unique and unforgettable experience.

‍

### HINE

Maison Hine offers you a glimpse into two and a half centuries of Franco-British history. From the cellars of Jarnac to your glass, let us guide you through an intimate, _quintessentially British_ experience, featuring exceptional tastings, food and cognac pairings, and an unforgettable afternoon tea.

‍

### Cognac Castle

At the Château de Cognac, French history and the art of cognac come together in a truly extraordinary setting. The birthplace of Francis I and a historic monument, it is now home to Baron Otard & D'Ussé cognacs—which you can discover as you explore its secret cellars and enjoy exceptional tastings.

## GASTRONOMY

### THE BORDERIES WALNUT GROVE

Located between Cognac and Saintes, the Borderies walnut grove offers tasting tours, gourmet picnics and a store selling a variety of local products.

### LE BAUME DE BOUTEVILLE

Explore the perfect blend of culture and flavor with Compagnie de Bouteville. Visit the exterior of the château, immerse yourself in the history of Bouteville and enjoy a panoramic view of the vineyards.

### COGNAC & COCKTAIL MASTERCLASS

Discover the inner workings of cognac production and its history. Learn fascinating tricks and master the art of tasting.

### CHOCOLATERIE LETUFFE

Explore the entire production process, from the delicate harvesting of cocoa to the creation of the final chocolate. Immerse yourself in the exciting world of the Letuffe chocolate factory, where every step is carefully orchestrated.

‍

### CITADEL

Immerse yourself in the history of an exceptional gin, discover the secrets of its production, and savor signature cocktails crafted with Citadelle Gin. A unique experience that blends tradition and modernity.

## SPORTS & LEISURE

### GOLF

For lovers of the greens, the Hotel Chais Monnet & Spa offers all-inclusive stays, including accommodation, transfers and access to the 18-hole Cognac golf course.

### BIKE RIDE

Venture out on a complimentary 2 hour bike ride on one of our classic bicycles, explore the Charente at your own pace and enjoy a timeless experience. Electric bikes are also available for rental.

### RIVER TRIP

With friends, family or professionals, come aboard and discover the lush green Charente countryside nestled between vineyards and limestone.

‍

### KAYAK CANOE

Discover the Charente and its beautiful landscapes on a canoe. From Cognac to Jarnac, the boat rental opens throughout the summer, making it the ideal time to explore the Charente's waterways.

‍

.webp)

### HORSEBACK RIDING

What could be more romantic than a horseback ride through the vineyards? Horseback riding in the heart of the vineyards, sharing the experience with a passionate oenologist... It's a unique immersion into the world of Cognac vineyards!

‍

### YOGA

Looking for a moment of relaxation? Let yourself be carried away by a yoga session close to nature in the garden of Maison Monnet.

‍

### SPORTS CAR

The perfect experience for racing car enthusiasts and thrill-seekers. Discover the Charente region at the wheel of an exceptional car.

### ESCAPE GAME

### MONTGOLFIÈRE

‍

### WATER SKIING

## CULTURE & SAVOIR-FAIRE

### CHATEAU DE COGNAC

Home of Baron Otard cognacs, birthplace of François 1er and a historic monument, the Château Royal de Cognac invites you to discover its experiences at the heart of French history and the history of Baron Otard cognacs.

### CHATEAU DE BOUTEVILLE

Listed as a historic monument since 1984, Château de Bouteville opens its doors to visitors after major renovations. Explore its thousand-year-old history through an interactive scenography, in the heart of the Grande Champagne region. Come and discover this treasure of regional heritage!

### MUSEE DES SAVOIR FAIRE DU COGNAC

Through the human, industrial and commercial adventure of cognac, discover the history of Cognac through the ages.

### ART AND HISTORY MUSEUM

Dive into the intimate sphere of cognac merchants, with rich and diverse collections recalling the glory days of cognac.
