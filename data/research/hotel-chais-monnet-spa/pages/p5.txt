### OUR SPA MANAGER

Immerse yourself in wellness and serenity with Alison, our spa manager, where every encounter promises moments of relaxation and revitalization. Place your well-being in her skilled hands, and let yourself be carried away on a relaxing and rejuvenating escapade.

The Spa de l'Hôtel Chais Monnet & Spa also offers children's treatments for a moment of well-being and softness with their parents. These natural, gourmet-perfumed treatments from Nougatine, for children aged 3 to 12, are adapted to the specific needs of their delicate skin. The program includes moisturizing and relaxation to soothe and relax the little ones. The beauty salon offers personalized beauty care: make-up, hairstyling, hand and foot care...
