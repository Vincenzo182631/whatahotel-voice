### The Amstel Wellness Experience

Indulge in a well-deserved escape from the demands of your daily routine. To secure your reservation, kindly reach out to our esteemed Amstel Health & Fitness Club via email at amstel.healthclub1@ihg.com.

Learn more

### The Amstel Health & Fitness Club

The Health & Fitness Club is a true wellness experience. From the moment you enter, you will find yourself in an oasis of peace. The large heated indoor pool offers a unique river-level view of Amsterdam. The horizontal shower refreshes and relaxes. You also have access to the sauna, steam cabin, and Jacuzzi.
