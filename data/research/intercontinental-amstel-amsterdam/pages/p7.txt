### Beautiful and Overpriced

15 days ago

The staff is absolutely wonderful. The hotel is in a great location and the interior is beautiful, with wonderful amenities. The breakfast was incredible, even if you are not a guest at the hotel I would recommend their breakfast. What I was not impressed about is how much they charge for EVERYTHING. We paid a premium price to stay there and the stay was just meh. The room was decent but the carpet was coming up in some places, not every outlet worked, and the wifi was terrible. We were supposedly "upgraded" but the room did not feel like an upgrade. The room and mini fridge is stocked with items that are charged at a premium price, and the prices are impossible to find. It was annoying how full the mini fridge was because if you purchased anything outside of the hotel, there was nowhere to store it. They provide two complimentary waters and after that you again have to pay a premium price for water. Everything felt like a money grab and it was frustrating to never clearly know what you are going to get charged for. For how much we paid per night to stay there I expected more to be included especially as a premium member and a member of the edit. If you want to splurge to stay in a beautiful hotel, with great spa amenities it is beautiful.

ProsSleep Quality, Room Cleanliness, Location, Amenities

Consrecognition for ihg rewards club members

No, I do not recommend this hotel.
