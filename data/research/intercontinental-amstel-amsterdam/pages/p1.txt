### The Amstel Afternoon Tea

Renowned for its seasonal afternoon tea, featuring ideal tea pairings, the lounge features traditional porcelain, adding a touch of our rich heritage to the magical ambiance. Bathed in natural sunlight, the lounge stands as the captivating heart of the hotel.

Learn more

### The Amstel Wellness Experience

Indulge in a well-deserved escape from the demands of your daily routine. To secure your reservation, kindly reach out to our esteemed Amstel Health & Fitness Club via email at amstel.healthclub1@ihg.com.

Learn more

### Meetings and Events

The Amstel Hotel offers 522 square meters of meeting and event facilities, spread over six rooms. The elegant and classic character of the seven rooms, all located on the ground floor, make the Amstel Hotel an ideal venue for both business and celebratory events.

Learn more

### Dine whilst overlooking the Amstel River

Nestled along the historic banks of the Amstel River, the Amstel Restaurant is a tale of culinary heritage meeting seasonal modernity. Rooted in authenticity, the menu narrates a vibrant story of flavours with Mediterranean comfort classics being its base and owning one of the most extensive wine lists in the city.

Learn more

### Weddings at the Grand Dame

A wedding in the Amstel Hotel is every couple’s dream come true and a milestone to celebrate with your dearest friends, family, and loved ones. At the Amstel Hotel, our events staff is here to plan the details and allow you to experience your dream wedding.

## FAQs for InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam

Does InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam have a pool?

InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

Heated indoor swimming pool at river height

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam?

At InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 100 EUR .

You can use the secure parking garage through our valet parking service. The rate for valet parking is € 8,50 per hour or € 90 per day. You can also park your car in various places in front of the hotel.

In and out privileges

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam?

Check-in at InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam is conveniently located near Amsterdam International Airport Schiphol (AMS), approximately 12.43 miles away (20 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam?

InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Yellow Bike - 2.36 miles away (3.8 kilometers)
- Classic Boat Tours
- Jewish Amsterdam Tour - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- The Canals walking tour - 3.73 miles away (6 kilometers)
- Private Cruise Lunch or Dinner Tour
- Rijksmuseum - 1.55 miles away (2.5 kilometers)
- Van Gogh Museum - 1.68 miles away (2.7 kilometers)
- Amstel river including The Hermitage - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Negen Straatjes - 3.73 miles away (6 kilometers)
- Anne Frank house - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Amstel Amsterdam puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
