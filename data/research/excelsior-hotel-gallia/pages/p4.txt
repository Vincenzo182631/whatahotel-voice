### Cigar Lounge

The Cigar Lounge is artfully designed with cigar and cognac connoisseurs in mind. Since its opening, Excelsior Hotel Gallia has nurtured a tradition of recreating the classic smoking rooms found in Milan’s grand railway stations.

### The Library

Discover a haven of private retreat in the Library. Adjacent to the Living Room, it is filled with inspiring books that reveal the passionate side of the city, from its dalliance in fashion and design to edifying epicurean journeys.

### Private Dining Room

Elevate your dining experience in the cozy Private Dining Room on the 7th floor of Terrazza Gallia, accommodating up to ten guests. This exclusive venue is ideal for both private lunch or dinner gatherings and intimate tasting dinners, featuring a choice between Chef Lebano’s expertly curated menus

A Unique Viewpoint Over Milan

## Milan's Aperitivo

Admire the panoramic view over the capital of fashion from the 7th floor of Excelsior Hotel Gallia; you will marvel at the landscape of Piazza Duca D’Aosta dominated by monumental buildings, sensing the grandeur and energetic vibes of the business district of Milan. Discover this all year round viewpoint fitted out with a clean and elegant design, styled by some of the most esteemed Italian furniture names.
