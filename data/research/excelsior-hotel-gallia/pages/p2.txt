# Relax in Our Luxury Accommodation in Milan, Italy

## Accommodations

Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan, offers more than 200 hotel rooms and luxury hotel suites in Milan, praised by guests for offering spacious, quite comfort. Inspired by the city’s...See More design heritage, each space blends handcrafted details by local creators with contemporary lines, bespoke leather furnishings, a sophisticated color palette, and architectural lighting, complemented by cutting-edge technology. Bathrooms showcase Botticino or Emperador marble set against Belle Époque bronze accents. Choose from 53 individually styled suites, from Executive to Gallia Presidential, crowned by the Katara Royal Suite, Italy’s largest at 1,000 square meters with gold and white leather, glass and crystal, and outdoor space. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan?

The largest occupancy room at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan is the Katara Royal with a maximum occupancy of 8.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan?

Some rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan have fridges?

No, rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan have in-room whirlpools?
