# Your Suite Experience in Milan

The Excelsior Hotel Gallia proudly presents 235 luxuriously appointed guest rooms, including over 50 exquisite suites. Our crowning jewel, the Katara Royal Suite, reigns as one of the largest in Europe, spanning an impressive 1000 square meters. Each guest room embodies Milan's renowned aesthetic, boasting a seamless blend of handcrafted and modern elements meticulously curated by local ateliers such as B&B Italia, Cassina, Fendi Casa and Flos.

## Executive Suites

Catering to both business and leisure travelers, the Executive Suites offer a delightful immersion into Milan's celebrated sophistication and culture. Guests can customize their experience by choosing a suite equipped with a Technogym stationary bike boasting a city view, ideal for fitness enthusiasts. Alternatively, those seeking relaxation can indulge in a suite featuring a steam and chromotherapy bath overlooking the tranquil courtyard.

## Panoramic Suites

Bridging the evocative history of the hotel with the vibrant culture of the city, the studio-style Panoramic Suites encapsulate the very essence of Milanese elegance. Situated in an unmatched corner location within the hotel's new wing, these suites present breathtaking views of landmarks and the cityscape through expansive floor-to-ceiling windows. Internally, they harmonize neutral tones, parquet flooring, and gilded accents, emphasizing a timeless yet modern aesthetic.

## Atelier Suites

Atelier Suites take inspiration from the renowned design workshops that populate Milan, defining it as a center of creative culture. Each suite offers either a privileged view of the bustling Piazza Duca D’Aosta or a calm setting overlooking the tranquil courtyard. These iconic views are matched only by the suite’s singularly enchanting design, which unites neutral tones and glittering accents. This rare meeting of past and present also appears in the sculptural light fixtures and the exclusively Italian-made materials and furniture.

## Signature Suites

Drawing inspiration from the sophistication and glamour inherent in Milanese fashion and design, the Signature Suites stand as luminous embodiments of the destination itself. Crafted with unique furniture pieces made in Italy, these suites provide an authentic Milanese experience tailored for creative minds. In the living area, a luxurious sofa and armchair beckon guests to unwind or enjoy a movie on the 55-inch LCD television, complete with surround sound and a subwoofer. Each element is meticulously curated to reflect the distinctive style and atmosphere of Milan.

## Gallia Presidential Suite

Honoring its namesake, our expansive one-bedroom Gallia Presidential Suite seamlessly combines the Excelsior Hotel Gallia’s prestigious past and progressive present by pairing a historical façade dating back to 1932 with a modern interior. Featuring an enchanting blend of neutral tones, the suite captivates guests with oversized windows offering panoramic views of the iconic Piazza Duca D’Aosta and railway station from its elevated fifth-floor vantage point.

## Katara Royal Suite

Indulge in unparalleled luxury and exclusivity within our extraordinary Katara Royal Suite, among the grandest and most opulent in all of Europe. This unique four-bedroom suite, located on the seventh floor, redefines Milanese grandeur. Elevating the experience are two expansive terraces, one showcasing a panoramic city view with a sophisticated dining setting, while the other offering a private solarium housing a pagoda spa, equipped with a whirlpool and a chromotherapy enhanced steam shower. This suite is a testament to lavish living, setting new standards of comfort and refinement.

### Special Moments for a singular location

Whether planning a birthday celebration, an anniversary or an intimate wedding, the Excelsior Hotel Gallia's suites provide  the perfect setting for any event. Elevating these special moments are the luxurious and comfortable surroundings, the meticulous attention to details, and the flawless service, ensuring an unforgettable experience tailored to cater to every need and preference.
