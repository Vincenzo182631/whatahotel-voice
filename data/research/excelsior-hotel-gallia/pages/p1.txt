# Welcome to Excelsior Hotel Gallia, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan

## Enjoy a 5-star escape at our luxury hotel in Milan, Italy

At the heart of Milan’s fashion, design, and culinary scene, Excelsior Hotel Gallia stands beside Piazza Duca d’Aosta and Milano Centrale station, placing guests at one of Italy’s most important transportation hubs. From the hotel, iconic destinations such as Lake Como, Venice, and Florence are just a convenient train journey away, making it an ideal base for exploring both Milan and northern Italy. Opened in 1932, the Art Deco landmark is now joined by a Marco Piva-designed steel-and-glass tower, seamlessly blending heritage and contemporary design. Among Milan’s most storied luxury addresses, the hotel offers elegantly appointed rooms and suites, exceptional dining experiences, and a world-class spa. Guests consistently praise the attentive service and prime location, while the complimentary city center shuttle makes discovering Milan effortless.

## The Gallia Family

The founder of our hotel, Carlo Gallia, was known to take great and meticulous care of his business; he wanted Excelsior Hotel Gallia to compete with the very best properties in Europe. The Duke of Bergamo called the hotel “truly regal,” which furthered and enhanced its pedigree. As it happens, Gallia had hospitality on his mind from a young age. At only 17, he left home to learn “the art of the guest.” He remained abroad for the next 15 years, working in several different European locales (London, Paris, Berlin, Lucerne, Monte Carlo, and St. Moritz). Upon returning to Milan with a wealth of experience, he founded our beloved property.
