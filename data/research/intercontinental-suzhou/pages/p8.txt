# InterContinental SuzhouDestination

InterContinental Suzhou occupies a coveted location on the shores of Jinji Lake, a showpiece destination in one of China’s most cherished tourism cities.The hotel is within easy walking distance to Jinji Lake and many other shopping and recreation areas.

## Things to do

### Suzhou Museum

No.204 Dong Bei Street, Suzhou, Jiangsu

Designed by renowed architect I.M. Pei, It has more than 15,000 historic artifacts in its collections, a perfect reflection of the rich history of Suzhou.

### Humble Administrator’s Garden

Dong Bei Street, Suzhou, JiangSu 215028

Honoured as one of the four best classic gardens in China, Humble Administrator's Garden is the largest private garden in Suzhou.

### Tiger Hill

Northwest of City, Suzhou, Jiangsu

Tiger Hill may be the most famous tourist attraction in Suzhou featured with China’s No.1 leaning pagoda.

### Jinji Lake

Jinji Lake, Suzhou Industrial Park, Suzhou, Jiangsu 215028

Jinji Lake, the largest inland city lake in China, as the core area in SIP, Jinji Lake Scenic Area occupies 10 square kilometers. Jinji Lake Scenic Area layouts according to the city's business travel functions, so that SIP itself is a scenic area and business is done in a tour.

### Eslite Spectrum Suzhou Store

NO.8,Yue Lang St.,Suzhou Industrial Park, Suzhou, Jiangsu

It is the first flagship store in Mainland China, only one step away from InterContinental Suzhou. You can experience bookstore, cultural and creative platform, lifestyle boutiques, gourmet restaurants and performance hall.

### Times Square Canopy

NO.268,Wang Dun Rd.,, Suzhou Industrial Park, Suzhou, Jiangsu 215100

Times Square‘s new repaired LED awning attention into the sense of engagement, experience and entertainment, in content production, general use of stage lights, 3D technology and many more, take the immersive experience to participants.
