InterContinental Suzhou occupies a coveted location on the shores of Jinji Lake, a showpiece destination in one of China’s most cherished tourism cities. The 27-storey crescent shaped hotel features 427 contemporary guestrooms, five eclectic restaurants and lounges, an indoor infinity pool – all boasting panoramic views of the willow-fringed Jinji Lake and the new Suzhou skyline. For MICE events, InterContinental Suzhou offers extensive meetings, conference and event facilities.

### Riva Mediterranean Steakhouse

Riva is Suzhou’s first Mediterranean Steakhouse, and also the only western restaurant in Suzhou rewarded by Black Pearl. The dining room has been renovated in 2022, and serves up premium char-grilled steaks and roast seafood from a wood-fired oven. Riva also specializes in deliciously decadent desserts and sophisticated wine list.

### Swimming Pool

With an infinity edge that appears to dip right into Jinji Lake, the 28-metre Indoor Swimming Pool is located on the 3rd floor of InterContinental Suzhou. The temperature-controlled pool is complemented by a large Jacuzzi, steam and sauna rooms, luxurious sun deck.

## FAQs for InterContinental Suzhou

Does InterContinental Suzhou have a pool?

InterContinental Suzhou has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

The 28-metre, temperature controlled pool is complemented by a large Jacuzzi, steam and sauna rooms, luxurious sun deck and a chil-out lounge with an infinity edge that appears to dip right into the Jinji Lake.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Suzhou?

Parking available at a charge, subject to length of parking, except for in house guests, outlet dining guests, SPA and Fitness center members.

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Suzhou?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Suzhou.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Suzhou for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Suzhou have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Suzhou has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Suzhou?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Suzhou.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Suzhou?

Check-in at InterContinental Suzhou is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Suzhou offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Suzhou offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Suzhou from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Suzhou is conveniently located near Wuxi Sunan International Airport (WUX), approximately 27.28 miles away (43.9 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Suzhou?

InterContinental Suzhou is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Suzhou Museum
- Humble Administrator’s Garden - 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)
- Tiger Hill - 9.32 miles away (15 kilometers)
- Jinji Lake - 0.06 miles away (0.1 kilometers)
- Eslite Spectrum Suzhou Store - 0.12 miles away (0.2 kilometers)
- Times Square Canopy - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Suzhou puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
