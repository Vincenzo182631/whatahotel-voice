### Outstanding Hospitality and Exceptional Service!

2 days ago

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Outstanding Hospitality and Exceptional Service!

I had a wonderful stay at InterContinental Suzhou, and the experience exceeded my expectations. Even before my arrival, the hotel staff proactively reached out to understand my needs and preferences, making me feel welcomed and well taken care of from the very beginning. This thoughtful, personalized service made a great first impression.

One particular highlight was the Xiu Chinese restaurant, where Thea provided truly exceptional service. She was warm, attentive, professional, and thoughtful throughout the dining experience. Her genuine care and attention to detail made the meal even more enjoyable and memorable. She is a wonderful representative of the hotel's commitment to outstanding hospitality.

Overall, I had a fantastic experience, thanks to the excellent team and their dedication to making guests feel special. I highly recommend InterContinental Suzhou and look forward to returning on my next visit!

A special thank-you to Thea and the entire hotel team for making my stay so memorable!
