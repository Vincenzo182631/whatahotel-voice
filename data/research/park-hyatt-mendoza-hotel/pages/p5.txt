### Wonderful staff, great location, memorable stay

We had a wonderful 11-night stay at the Park Hyatt Mendoza. The location is great, right in the heart of the city and easy to get around from. The staff were top notch, friendly and knowledgeable, and the manager, Lucas, was especially helpful. As a World of Hyatt Globalist, we very much appreciated the upgrade to a Governor's Suite, and we enjoyed being there for the hotel's 25th anniversary. Congratulations!

The concierge team, especially Natasha, went above and beyond, quickly arranging an airport transfer for us on short notice, even after we had checked out.

Two small suggestions: the interior and furnishings are showing their age and could use a refresh, and the breakfast buffet had few options for guests who don't eat pork. The smoked salmon benedict was a welcome choice, and a non-pork meat on the buffet would help. Overall, the people made our stay, and we would happily return.

Young K,

### A meh experience, lower expectations

Unfortunately, one has to lower expectations to come to this hotel. It’s not your typical Park Hyatt. Service is mid and food is underwhelming. Spa is the only thing worth coming for if you want to relax. Rooms are old and need upgrades and upkeep. Location is prime if yo want to walk around though.

My brother was offered a baby extra bed which he did not fit. After complaining, something was arranged.

Hyatt should lower this hotel to a Hyatt Regency instead of a Park Hyatt.
