# Restaurants & Bars

Whether you are looking for fine dining or a vibrant bar, our collection of restaurants in Mendoza embody the art of dining. We know our guests seek innovative style and timeless flavors with modern flair, and our celebrated outlets are the cornerstone of the culinary experience. Our concierge can also recommend local Mendoza restaurants to explore. A variety of places are making their mark by bringing a new take to traditional flavors. Our staff will gladly help you make a reservation so that you benefit from an authentic Argentinian experience.

## Bistro M - Restaurant & Terraces

This original restaurant offers an extensive menu inspired by Mendocinian cuisine. Roasted vegetables, fish and meats are showcased in our exclusive wood burning oven, visible in our open kitchen. Authentic flavors are created from seasonal, regional ingredients, so you savor the heritage of Mendoza with every bite.

## Diner Plaza

Located on the lower level of the Dreams Plaza Casino, this quirky Mendoza restaurant is a gastronomic experience inspired by the famous diners of the ’50s. Classics are given a luxury twist, such as the veal hamburger, and staples like french fries are served alongside Argentinian cuisine like empanadas.
