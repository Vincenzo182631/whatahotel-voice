Rooms

This restored Spanish colonial structure and seven-story modern addition have "a great location" on the Plaza de la Independencia. "It's the place to stay in Mendoza." The "classic with modern" interior incorporates granite, stone, and wood, and rooms have wood furnishings, soft fabrics, and bathrooms with white marble and crystal wash-basins. Bistro M, with "an excellent menu and wine list"—its cellar is two stories high—serves Argentine meats and Italian specialties. After visiting nearby vineyards, unwind with "one of the great vintages" at the Vines Wine Bar and Vinoteca. However, "staff need some customer-service training."
