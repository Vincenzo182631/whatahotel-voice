# 5-Star Luxury Hotels in Mendoza, Argentina \| Park Hyatt Mendoza

## Experience Rare Luxury In Mendoza, Argentina

The first 5-star hotel in the city, Park Hyatt Mendoza stands out for its luxury, location and hospitality, with a magnificent French Neoclassical façade. Situated in the city center opposite the historic Plaza Independencia, the hotel includes a casino and spa. At the foot of the Andes and surrounded by the vineyards of wine country in one of the great wine capitals, we are an ideal destination in Argentina.

## Discover Park Hyatt Mendoza

Art at Park Hyatt Mendoza

Our Balcony of the Arts hosts outstanding sculptures, paintings and other creations from Mendoza, including work by artists such as Julio Le Parc. We value and promote local talent at Park Hyatt Mendoza, acknowledging the area’s people, environment and cultural heritage.

Casino Dreams Plaza

Our hotel's on-site casino offers excitement in the form of 550 state-of-the-art slot machines, plus gaming tables and a VIP room. Pair your gaming entertainment with excellent cocktails and food from Lucky 7 Bar, Diner Plaza and Café Plaza.

Kaua Club & Spa

True to our hotel's location in Mendoza's wine country, Kaua Club & Spa offers a wide variety of olive- and wine-based treatments and products, which are known for their antioxidant properties. It also offers a sauna, a Turkish steam bath and more.

It’s hard to leave your best buddy at home when you travel. At Park Hyatt Mendoza, you can enjoy a pet-friendly extended stay-experience. Please reserve your pet-friendly room at least three days in advance of your arrival. 31+ nights: Please contact the hotel.

- 1–6 nights: **$100** _/ STAY_

- 7–30 nights: **$200** _/ STAY_

#### Weight Limits

- Combined pets weight limit: 75Pounds
