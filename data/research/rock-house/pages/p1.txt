# Rock House

## Turks and Caicos Resort

## Intimate Beach Experience

Rock House is unlike anything yet seen in the Turks and Caicos, this unique resort and residential vision is located on the north coast of Providenciales upon a majestic 14-acre oceanfront site with 600 feet of frontage and peaks soaring up to 95 feet above sea level.

## Rock House Receives Its First MICHELIN Key

Rock House has been recognized with a **2026 MICHELIN Key**, a distinction from the MICHELIN Guide honoring hotels that stand out for their design, service, personality, value, and connection to their surroundings. For us, receiving our first Key is a meaningful recognition of what makes Rock House so special, from its white cliff setting and Amalfi-inspired design to the intimate spaces and secluded beach that shape the experience. From quiet nooks overlooking the turquoise water to Italian-inspired dining at Vita, every part of Rock House invites you to slow down and enjoy the setting.

## ROCK HOUSE EARNS THE GREEN KEY CERTIFICATION

At Rock House, the wild beauty of Providenciales’ north coast is something we hold close and work hard to protect. Earning the **Green Key certification**, an internationally recognized standard for sustainable tourism, is a reflection of that commitment. Discover it through our ongoing sustainability efforts, including a _Refill, No Landfill_ program with complimentary reusable water bottles and filtered water stations, elimination of single-use plastics, and a water-recycling irrigation system to conserve resources. A native plant nursery supports local biodiversity, and on-site herb gardens provide fresh ingredients for our resort’s culinary offerings, reflecting a broader commitment to reducing environmental impact while enhancing the guest experience.
