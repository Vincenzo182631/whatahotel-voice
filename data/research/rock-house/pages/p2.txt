# Dining

The exclusive dining experience at Rock House will be as breathtaking as the views – Taking inspirations from the Mediterranean with a selection of gourmet pastas, fine wines and magnum bottle service. The Resort offers several dining experiences including Vita Restaurant and Cave Bar, in addition to pool and beachside dining.

## Vita Restaurant

At Vita restaurant, guests can enjoy stunning ocean views while indulging in fine wines and traditional Italian dishes with Caribbean influence. The upscale atmosphere that is perched over 30 feet above the sea allows guests to enjoy the exceptional creations by our master mixologist and world class culinary team.
