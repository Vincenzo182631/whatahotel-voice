## A Refined Fall Escape in Bucharest

This fall, discover Bucharest through a journey of culture, comfort and restorative well-being at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel.

Set near the Palace of Parliament, JW Marriott Bucharest offers the most exquisite stay for exploring Romania’s capital, from historic architecture and vibrant dining to moments designed simply for slowing down.

For the season, guests can choose from curated stays crafted for different travel moods: unwind with the Bucharest Retreat: JW Marriott and Therme Experience, featuring a Deluxe Room rate paired with a full-day wellness escape at Therme Bucharest; embrace mindful luxury with Stay in the Moment; arrive with effortless ease through Travel in Style: Journey in Elegance; or begin each morning beautifully with Stay for Breakfast.

Whether visiting for a city break, a wellness weekend or an elegant autumn getaway, JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel invites travelers to experience Bucharest with grace, warmth and a sense of place.
