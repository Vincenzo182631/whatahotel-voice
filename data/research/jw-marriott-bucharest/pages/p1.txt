# Welcome to JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel

## Romania's leading luxury hotel.

Located in an area of historic and national interest, JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel in Romania is the only Bucharest 5-star hotel in the district and is in close proximity to the stately Palace of Parliament and People’s Salvation Cathedral. Culturally rich and with a status that befits its quality offering, our luxury hotel in Bucharest provides a restorative setting where its legacy for intuitive...See More service, culinary distinction, and notable grandeur remains unsurpassed. Its seamless blend of modern luxury and authentic appeal enhances each stay in Bucharest, Romania with a holistic offering, that is both distinctive and deeply enriching. Our hotel is a premier luxury destination for business, leisure and family guests, it is just a 30 minute drive from Henri Coanda International airport and within walking distance of Old Town, with its diversity of architecture. ### Rich In History

Rich in recent history, the hotel is located on "Dealul Spirii" hill in Bucharest. It was built in the 80's as part of the same urbanism plan as one of the satellite buildings, adjoining Parliament Palace, the second largest building in the world.

### Authentic Heritage

The JW Marriott in Bucharest resides in a historical area which carries the legacy of King Carol II and his architectural plan dating back in 1935. Besides the Parliament building, the original plan also included the largest National Orthodox cathedral, currently under development.

### Grandeur and Style

The heritage of the hotel building boasts stately appointed ample spaces, luxury lobby with warm colors accented by natural daylight, marvelous marble staircases, and imposing cupolas.

## Sustainability and the Green Key Award

At JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel, we are committed to sustainability and creating a positive impact. By reducing our environmental footprint and engaging with our community, we encourage guests and associates to save water and energy, use eco-friendly transportation, and participate in recycling initiatives. Our JW Garden, located entrance to Cucina - The Italian Kitchen and Olea - Middleterranean Restaurant. JW Garden is more than a space—it is a source of inspiration and ingredients. From the aromatic thyme, our signature herb, to the vibrant peppermint, our local herb, the JW Garden provides fresh, sustainable elements that our chefs and bartenders incorporate into our culinary and beverage offerings. Recognized with the prestigious Green Key Award and the 2025 Verified Responsible Hospitality badge with Forbes Travel Guide, our efforts are driven by hotel leadership and the Associate Relations Committee, showcasing our dedication to environment. Together, we strive to create a more sustainable future—one thoughtful action at a time.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel. Up to 3 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of L105.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel, with rates of L15.00 per hour, L150.00 per day, and L1050.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel is Henri Coanda International Airport (OTP). OTP is located approximately 18.7 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
