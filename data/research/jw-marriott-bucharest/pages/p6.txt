# Holiday Events at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel

## Celebrate the Festive Season at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel

The festive season is a time to come together, celebrate cherished traditions and create memories that last long after the celebrations end. At JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel, discover a thoughtfully curated collection of experiences designed to make every gathering extraordinary. From indulgent festive brunches and enchanting moments for little ones to spectacular New Year's Eve celebrations, every...See More occasion is an invitation to reconnect with those who matter most. Complete your festive experience with a memorable stay in the heart of Bucharest, just moments away from the enchanting Christmas Market, and embrace the warmth, elegance and magic of the season.See Less

### Celebrate the Season With Exceptional Dining & Unforgettable Moments

### Winter Brunches

Savor the beauty of the season with delightful Sunday afternoons at JW Steakhouse. Discover a thoughtfully curated selection of seasonal flavors, signature specialties and inspired culinary creations, all coming together in an inviting setting where the pleasure of good food meets the joy of spending time together. Every Sunday, starting November 29.

Book a Splendid Moment

### Christmas Brunch & Dinner

Some moments deserve to be celebrated beautifully. This Christmas Day, gather around the table at JW Steakhouse for an occasion filled with warmth, timeless traditions and meaningful connections. From an indulgent afternoon brunch to an elegant evening dinner, discover the art of celebrating with those who matter most.

Book Your Festive Table

### New Year's Eve Celebrations

Welcome 2027 with The Gathering at Midnight at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel. Discover four distinctive celebrations, each offering exceptional gastronomy, captivating live entertainment and a unique atmosphere, coming together for a spectacular midnight countdown and fireworks display.

Book a Spectacular NYE

### Discover the Magic of the Season and Create Cherished Memories

### Saint Nicholas Children's Party

Step into a world of festive wonder at Champions, where little ones can enjoy joyful activities, delightful treats and enchanting surprises. With a special visit from Saint Nicholas, this celebration promises magical moments and cherished childhood memories.

Book a Joyful Moment

### Gingerbread Workshop

Let little imaginations flourish at Cucina – The Italian Kitchen, with a delightful gingerbread house-building workshop. Surrounded by sweet aromas and festive cheer, children can create their own edible masterpiece and take home a cherished reminder of the season.

Book a Sweet Moment

### Your Gateway to Bucharest's Christmas Magic

Experience the magic of the festive season with a stay at JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel. Just moments from Bucharest's Christmas Market, discover sparkling lights, seasonal delights and cherished traditions. Complete your festive escape with a special gingerbread house kit amenity, bringing an extra touch of holiday magic to your stay.
