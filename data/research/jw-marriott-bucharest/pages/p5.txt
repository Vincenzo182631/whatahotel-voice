### Local Area & Activity Highlights

### Parliament Palace

Located on Dealul Arsenalului, in Bucharest, Romania the Parliament Palace is the heaviest building in the world, and second largest administrative building, after the Pentagon. It houses the Senate and the Chamber of Deputies, along with three museums and an international conference center near the hotel.

### The Old Town

Located in the city center of Bucharest, Old Town became popular for its nightlife vibes. Vibrant and full of charming cafés, bars and nightclubs, Old Town is the perfect place to go if you want to have some fun near the hotel in Bucharest.

### Central University Library

Designed by French architect Paul Gottereau, the Central University Library’s building is one of the landmarks of Bucharest, and it's located just across the street from the National Museum of Art of Romania.

### Cișmigiu Gardens

In a sea of green, Cișmigiu Park is a vast English-style garden with fountains, monuments and a popular lake where you can take a boat ride under the sun, or enjoy a nice walk in the outdoors in Bucharest, Romania.

### Romanian Athenaeum

The Romanian Athenaeum is a concert hall in the center of Bucharest, Romania and a landmark of the city, home of the "George Enescu" Philharmonic and annual international music festival.

See More

### National Museum of Art

Housed in the marvelous building of the Royal Palace in Bucharest, Romania, it features collections of medieval and modern Romanian art, as well as the international collection of the royal family.

### Cec Palace

Built in eclectic style, the palace is topped by a glass and metal dome, and it’s now the headquarter of CEC Bank in Bucharest, Romania. The entrance features an arch, and the corners are decorated with gables and coats of arms.

### Stavropoleos Monastery

Located near the hotel in the city center of Bucharest, and built in Brâncovenesc style, one of the monastery's constant interests is Byzantine music, expressed through its choir and a large collection of Byzantine music books.

### Peleș Castle

Built for King Carol I, Peleș Castle is a charming building, located in Prahova County. Romantically inspired by Neo-Renaissance and Gothic Revival, this castle in Romania shelters a painting collection of almost 2,000 pieces.
