## Dining

Celebrated for its legacy of fine epicurean diversity, JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel is where guests and locals enjoy nourishing cuisine with a focus on authenticity and wellbeing. Signature JW Steakhouse serves premium USDA Black Angus beef alongside the finest selection of wines and bourbon in Bucharest. Flavorful dishes are created in Cucina, our city center authentic Italian restaurant reminiscent...See More of a Tuscan farmhouse kitchen, while those keen to catch the sporting highlights at our sports bar, Champions Sports and Bar can order burgers and beer against the backdrop of sporting memorabilia and numerous flat-screen TVs. Discover OLEA – a Middle’teranean restaurant where vibrant flavors meet the charm of a lush garden and sunlit terrace. ## A Truly Magical Dining Experience

Step into a world where exceptional dining meets immersive storytelling.

Located at JW Steakhouse, within JW Marriott Bucharest Grand Hotel, The Magic Table transforms every dinner into a captivating journey through spectacular 3D projections, interactive visual moments, and a thoughtfully curated multi-course dining experience.

As each course is served, the table comes to life, revealing a series of magical scenes that blend gastronomy, illusion, and imagination into an unforgettable evening. Guided by the internationally renowned illusionists The Clairvoyants as your digital hosts, each chapter unfolds as part of a seamless, immersive journey.

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or simply looking for an extraordinary night out, The Magic Table invites you to experience dining from an entirely new perspective, complemented by a choice of carefully curated menus to suit every preference.

## Discover our JW Garden

Inspired by our founders – J. Willard Marriott and Alice Marriott’s love for the outdoors, JW Marriott believes that a connection to the natural environment is a necessary part of nourishing one’s soul. JW Garden is a signature element of the JW Marriott experience focused on creating sensory connections with the natural world. Our JW Garden is located on the first floor, at the entrance of the restaurant Cucina - The Italian Kitchen. Among the other herbs that we harvest, and that our Chefs and Bartenders are using in our food & drink menus, in our JW Garden you will find THYME, our SIGNATURE HERB, and PEPPERMINT, our LOCAL HERB. We invite you to visit our JW Garden - Be Mindful & Present, Be Nourished, and Be Together.
