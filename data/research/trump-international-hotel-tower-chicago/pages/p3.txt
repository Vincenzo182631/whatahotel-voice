# **TERRACE 16**

## TERRACE 16 \| ROOFTOP DINING IN CHICAGO - RESTAURANT & BAR

OUTDOOR _DINING_ IN

CHICAGO

 +1 312 588 8030

RESERVE A TABLE

Terrace 16 is proudly voted among the best rooftop bars and restaurants in Chicago by  The Rooftop Guide.

* * *

Terrace 16 captures the spirit of Chicago's summer year-round with genuine hospitality, warm design, unmatched skyline views, and a seasonal New American menu. Located on the 16th floor of Trump International Hotel & Tower Chicago, the rooftop restaurant features a lively indoor bar and dining room, along with an expansive outdoor terrace overlooking the Chicago River, Lake Michigan, and the city's iconic architecture.

Rooted in seasonal ingredients, the menu showcases approachable New American cuisine alongside handcrafted cocktails, a thoughtfully curated wine list, and fresh options to suit a variety of tastes and dietary preferences.

With seating for more than 200 guests, Terrace 16's outdoor patio has become one of Chicago's premier rooftop dining destinations. Whether you are joining us for brunch, dinner, cocktails, or watching the city's spectacular summer fireworks over the river, every visit is elevated by breathtaking views and an inviting atmosphere.

We kindly ask all guests to observe our business and chic casual dress code. For dress code questions prior to your visit, please call Terrace 16 at +1 312 588 8030.

_**Enjoy front-row views of Chicago's iconic summer fireworks over the river every Wednesday at 9:00pm and Saturday at 10:00pm.**_
