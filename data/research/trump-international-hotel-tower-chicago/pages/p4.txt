# **REBAR**

## REBAR DOWNTOWN CHICAGO BAR

_SOPHISTICATED_

CHICAGO LOUNGE

Reserve a Table

+1 312 588 8034

tch\_rebar@trumphotels.com

Located on the Mezzanine level of Trump Chicago, Rebar provides a casual-chic, dynamic environment for dining and cocktails along the Chicago River.

Enjoy one of downtown's most luxurious bars with a view featuring unparalleled vistas of the Chicago River and the city’s famed Michigan Avenue Bridge paired with an ever-evolving menu of Neo Japanese fare created by Kamehachi, known for perfecting the art of making sushi. We invite you to experience the unique flavors, textures and artistry of Japanese cuisine.

An extensive wine, beer and small-batch spirits list is thoughtfully curated to spotlight leading trends, which along with an impressive craft cocktail program is sure to surprise and delight.
