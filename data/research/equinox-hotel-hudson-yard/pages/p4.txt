# About Us

## EQUINOX HOTELS

For Those Who Want It All.

Stay where high-performance lives. We created Equinox Hotels for those who rest and play as hard as they work. For those who seek a place that matches the scale of their ambitions and a space to restore and regenerate.

## THE PROMISE

Elevate Your Expectations.

Equinox Hotels redefines luxury as the ultimate in experience, designed as a dynamic interplay between spaces created to maximize your potential, performance and pleasure as they carry you from day through to the ultimate night’s sleep.

Equinox architected the fitness as a lifestyle movement and Equinox Hotels is the complete manifestation of our brand promise. This is high-performance living. This is a place where compromise simply doesn’t exist.

## THE PEOPLE

Equinox Hotels are destinations for individuals from near and far who know what they want and have mastered how to get it. These are places to live and work, to commune and engage, to revel and relish. Because it’s not just what you do, it’s who you do it with. Ready, set, connect.

## THE ROOMS

Dark. Quite. Cool.

Each room is a temple to total regeneration. The difference is in the details, borrowed from residential living at its most sublime. Designed to meet every need, our proprietary sleep system ensures that every night is a dream come true, leaving you primed to perform in ways you never thought possible.

Technology is seamlessly integrated into the room, from personalized sleep experiences to a bedside tablet that centralizes all controls—blinds, lighting, temperature, and privacy. Enjoy curated in-room entertainment, including exclusive Rituals content, and explore our reinvented RoomBar with 80+ products tailored for the modern traveler. Plus, indulge in our multi-modal shower experience for ultimate relaxation and rejuvenation.

## DINING  EXPERIENCES

Fresh. Local. Real.

Fresh, seasonal flavors. Market-driven menus. Dynamic spaces. Discipline and decadence come together in exciting new interpretations of modern American cuisine. Never restrictive, always thoughtful. Savor dining and drinking experiences that excite, indulge, and restore in equal measure, including an expansive and customizable RoomBar that redefines the mini bar. All day, all night, all the right ingredients.

## THE  DESIGN

High Performance, By Design.

The design of Equinox Hotels transcends traditional hospitality, merging the art and science of performance luxury into every aspect of your stay. From the moment you arrive, you’ll be immersed in a world that fuels your performance and health. Thoughtfully crafted environments and meticulously chosen elements create a space where comfort, creativity, and focus thrive. At Equinox Hotels, we invite you to reimagine how you move, eat, sleep, work, and live—unlocking your full potential in every moment.
