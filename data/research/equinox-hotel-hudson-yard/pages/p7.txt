## Meet the team

The Equinox Hotels team is led by a diverse group of innovators and leaders from both luxury hospitality to lifestyle brands pushing the limits. Learn more below.

### ALEX GASSER

PRESIDENT

Biography

Axel brings 25 years of luxury hospitality experience across four continents, having grown multi-property operations generating over $800M in annual revenue. As EVP at Ennismore, he built the operational foundation for the company’s fastest-growing region, growing LATAM and Caribbean from 2 properties to 22 and reaching $600M in topline revenue. At SLS Baha Mar, as VP, he restructured a 1,300-person organization, launched a flagship beach club, and delivered double the budgeted GOP in under a year. As Opening General Manager of Mondrian Doha, he established an ultra-luxury flagship in one of the world’s most competitive markets, earning major international awards. He is fluent in German, English, Spanish, and Italian.

### SHAFI SYED

SVP GLOBAL HEAD HOTEL DEVELOPMENT

Biography

Shafi leads the brand’s global expansion and brings over 30 years of experience in global hospitality investment, strategic brand development and expansion for luxury lifestyle hotels, branded real estate, F&B and entertainment. He recently held the CDO position at Jumeirah and various executive development and operations roles at Caesars Entertainment, Taj and Wyndham Hotels. He has lived and worked in Europe, Asia and the Middle East.

### ARA PATTERSON

VP FOOD & BEVERAGE + SPA INNOVATION

Biography

Ara was corporate F&B Director at Rosewood Hotels, guiding 16 properties and holds a B.S degree in hospitality management from Les Roches, Switzerland. She began her hospitality career with Four Seasons Hotels & Resorts holding senior management positions in F&B at The Pierre, The Beverly Wilshire, Four Seasons Denver. Ara was instrumental in working with the Wolfgang Puck team to launch the first Cut restaurant at the Beverly Wilshire.

### JANE LUCKER

VP SALES

Biography

Jane serves in the capacity of VP of Sales for the team, overseeing the entire Sales Platform for the Hotel Brand. With over 25 years of Sales and Marketing experience, she has a proven history of creating targeted, innovative and results oriented sales and marketing teams and programs for iconic brands such as Omni, Ritz Carlton, Hilton, Bally’s, LaSalle Partners, Prudential, ReMax and the legendary Hotel Architect, John Portman. Jane is also a licensed Real Estate Broker.

### KATIE TARDIF

BRAND DIRECTOR

Biography

A brand leader with 12 years of proven success in global marketing, branding and innovation leading to exponential growth in the areas of hospitality, technology and lifestyle brands. Recognized for a highly creative and strategic approach, Katie brings a unique and entrepreneurial approach to her work.

### NATHAN LOGA

VP GUEST EXPERIENCE

Biography

Nathan has opened and operated best in class hotels in the Four Seasons Hotels & Resorts portfolio, namely Singapore, Paris, Washington D.C, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Moscow and New York City. He brings over 20 years of hotel operations and union experience and has overseen multiple property openings, staffing and training that have been critical to the flawless Four Seasons service experience.

### Amelia Munning

Head of Design

Biography

As Senior Director of Design & Project Services for Equinox Hotels, Amelia leads design strategy across the brand’s global development portfolio, ensuring each project aligns with Equinox’s vision, operational goals, and long term asset value. She directs multidisciplinary teams and external partners from concept through completion, partnering closely with ownership and executive stakeholders.

With more than 20 years of experience across luxury hospitality, lifestyle, and high end retail, Amelia has collaborated with industry leaders including Robert A.M. Stern, Peter Marino, Tony Chi, Meyer Davis, and Ian Schrager, and has contributed to projects for brands such as Rosewood, Hyatt, and Four Seasons. She holds a Bachelor of Architecture from the University of Miami School of Architecture.

### George Groen

Director of Hotel Development

Biography

George oversees the evaluation and underwriting of prospective Equinox hotels and resorts globally.

With over 10 years of experience in hotel acquisitions and development, corporate development and asset management, he has held key positions in M&A and Development at Caesars Entertainment and began his career in the hotel investment space at Colony Capital and Barings.

George holds an MBA from Cornell’s SC Johnson Graduate School of Management and a BS with a minor in real estate from the School of Hotel Administration, also at Cornell.

### Tara Laksmono

Assistant Director of Marketing

Biography

Tara brings over three years of progressive experience in brand strategy, marketing management, and communications within the luxury hospitality sector.

She earned her Master of Science in Global Hospitality Management from New York University, where she served as President of the Hospitality, Tourism, and Events Society, and holds a bachelor’s degree in International Business from UC San Diego.

She is also certified with the WSET Level 2 Award from the International Wine Center.
