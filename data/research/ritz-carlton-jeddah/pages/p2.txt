Our Hotel Suites in Jeddah: Lavishly Designed
Suites & Rooms
Our Jeddah accommodation is inspired by the ornate, intricate details of traditional Middle Eastern design. The hotel rooms and suites offer breathtaking views, overlooking the Red Sea, King Fahad Fountain, or Jeddah cityscape. Our royal and executive suites in Jeddah offer elevated luxury with spacious layouts including living spaces and primary bedrooms.
A Luxury Saudi Arabian Grand Prix Retreat
A Marriott Bonvoy Moment like no other. Enjoy 3-day Grandstand access to the Saudi Arabian Grand Prix, a 3-night stay in a lavish 500 sq. ft. Royal Suite, circuit transfers, daily breakfast, and a bespoke dinner on your private balcony overlooking the Red Sea.
