Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah
Discover our luxury hotel in Jeddah
Overlooking King Fahd Fountain, The Ritz-Carlton, Jeddah pairs classical architecture with Arabic details in a landmark setting with easy access to the city center and tourist destinations. This Jeddah hotel offers opulent rooms and suites where glamor and heritage come together, and guests often praise the spacious rooms, comfort, and elegant ambiance. For business gatherings and city stays alike, it sets a benchmark for luxury events and dynamic meetings in Saudi Arabia. Among luxury hotels in Jeddah, it also stands out for two fine dining restaurants, a lounge with desserts, snacks, and beverages, and a gentlemen's spa with three treatment rooms. Attentive service and a prime location near the Corniche add to its appeal as a hotel in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah is King Abdulaziz International Airport (JED). JED is located approximately 31.7 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
