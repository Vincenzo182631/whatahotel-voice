Relax at Our Hotel With a Pool in Jeddah
Experiences
A gateway to Makkah, a center for international business, and a canvas for public art, Jeddah draws travelers from across the globe. Downtown in Saudi Arabia’s second-largest city, this luxury hotel sits along the Al Hamra corniche overlooking the Red Sea. Guests can explore commercial and diplomatic areas along Tahlia and Palestine Streets, the historical Al Balad, and the Floating Mosque. The hotel’s city center location, 45 minutes from the airport, makes it a popular conference destination. In the evenings, treat yourself to a relaxing massage at our Jeddah spa, before sleeping soundly in one of our comfortable and elegant rooms.

Planning Your Trip in Saudi Arabia

Being surrounded by deserts and a seafront city, you will enjoy the adventures of safari trips and watersports. And the ultimate gate to experience the mystique of Saudi Arabia is through the palatial Ritz-Carlton, Jeddah.

Does The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton Jeddah offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
