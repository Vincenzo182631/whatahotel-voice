Luxury Suites in Jeddah
Signature Accommodations
Experience refined luxury in the Royal and Executive Suites, where the elegance of traditional Middle Eastern design blends seamlessly with modern comfort. Take in breathtaking views of the Red Sea, the iconic King Fahad Fountain, or the dynamic Jeddah cityscape. Each suite features spacious layouts, sophisticated living areas, and beautifully appointed primary bedrooms for an unforgettable stay.
Royal Suite
Our Royal Suite features one king bedroom, three marble bathrooms, and spacious living with adjoining sitting areas cummilating to 500 sqm / 5,380 sq ft, with a private balcony offering Red Sea view. Equipped with 14-seater dining area, a butler room, full kitchen , and complimentary wired and wireless internet. It blends traditional Middle Eastern design with modern amenities for an exclusive stay.
