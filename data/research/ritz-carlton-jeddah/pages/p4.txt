Warm Flavors at Our Buffet Restaurant in Jeddah
Dining
Searching for indulgent places to eat in Jeddah? Experience a fine dining experience like no other in our Jeddah restaurant. Spices are the centerpiece of any kitchen in Saudi Arabia. Restaurants at this Jeddah hotel pay homage to their simplicity and flavor, highlighting the use of basil in global culinary traditions and the impact of the perfectly selected salt. To balance, sweets are celebrated in the lobby lounge.
Seafood Night Market
Discover an elevated seafood experience at Reyhana. Select from the freshest catch, prepared to your preference by our chefs, complemented by an international buffet and refined desserts. Enhanced by live entertainment and engaging children’s activities, perfect for a memorable evening.

Cake Boutique
Set in the vibrant and luxury ambience of Karamel Lounge, the Cake Boutique is the perfect place to delight your senses with decadent desserts, whimsical pastries and home-made chocolates. Blending the finest and freshest ingredients with passion, our skilled pastry chefs will bake-from-scratch the most sophisticated creations to satisfy the most discerning tastes. Entwined in the legendary roots of The Ritz-Carlton, the signature cake is always the star of the venue to enliven the heritage and dazzle palates by its remarkable flavor and complexity.
Sweets Served With Style
The elegant ambience and exquisite décor add to the lavish setting of Karamel Lounge where you can relax and enjoy a conversation with family and friends. Indulge in an appetizing array of crafted sandwiches, scones and pastries complimented with a unique tea & coffee experience. For a memorable Afternoon Tea experience please call +966 12 231 4449.
