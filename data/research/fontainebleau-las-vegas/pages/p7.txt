# Now Open

Ida and Harry

Level 2 \| 10AM – 9PM Daily

Ida and Harry is a lifestyle shopping destination built around elevated essentials and everyday pieces designed to last a lifetime. The boutique brings a fresh perspective to Fontainebleau Las Vegas, where heritage meets modern style, with a curated selection of sought-after international contemporary brands spanning ready-to-wear, designer jewelry and fragrance—including ÉTERNE, FRAME, Simkhai, Nili Lotan, Closed Denim, Greyson, Guest in Residence and more.

**About Ida and Harry:** _The boutique’s name is a tribute to the timeless style of Fontainebleau Development Chairman and CEO, Jeffrey Soffer’s grandparents, Ida and Harry Soffer. Curated by Fontainebleau Development Corporate Vice President of Retail Summer Soffer, great-granddaughter of Ida and Harry, the boutique opens its second location as a contemporary women’s and men’s concept drawing upon Summer’s personal sense of fashion-forward, effortless style._
