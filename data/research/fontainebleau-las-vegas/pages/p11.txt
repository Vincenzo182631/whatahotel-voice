# Parking & Travel

Situated on the north end of the Las Vegas Strip, Fontainebleau Las Vegas provides a prime and easily accessible location, less than a 20-minute ride from Harry Reid International Airport and a 10-minute walk from the Las Vegas Convention Center.

**Complimentary self-parking** is available for all Nevada residents and Fontainebleau Rewards members\*.

**Valet validation** – allows any guest dining at the resort’s world-class restaurant and bar collection, spending within retail spaces, or enjoying a treatment at Lapis Spa & Wellness to validate their valet.\*

**Vegas LOOP** – take the Vegas Loop to nearby destinations like the Las Vegas Convention Center and Harry Reid International Airport. Learn more here

_\*Complimentary self-parking is subject to special events and holidays. Valet validation is not available to registered hotel guests and is subject to special events and holidays._

## Self-Parking

**All visitors** receive **1 hour of complimentary self‑parking**, _subject to availability._

**Nevada residents** receive complimentary self-parking by scanning their valid Nevada Driver’s License after their ticket has been inserted at exit gates.

**Fontainebleau Rewards members** receive **complimentary self‑parking** by inserting their Rewards card at the entry or exit gates.

**Hotel guests** enjoy **complimentary self‑parking for the duration of their stay** by tapping their room key at the entry or exit gates.

**Self‑parking is $20 for up to 24 hours** after complimentary eligibility ends.

_Special event pricing may apply. Fontainebleau Rewards members and hotel guests continue to receive complimentary parking during their stay. View terms & conditions **here.**_

Parking & Travel FAQ

Do you charge locals for parking?

Nevada residents receive complimentary self-parking by scanning their valid Nevada Driver’s License after their ticket has been inserted at departure.

How much is self-parking at Fontainebleau Las Vegas?

Self-parking is included in the Resort Fee for hotel guests for the duration of their stay.

All visitors will receive 1-hour complimentary self-parking after receiving a ticket, subject to availability. Nevada residents will receive complimentary self-parking by scanning their valid Nevada Driver’s License after their ticket has been inserted at departure. All Fontainebleau Rewards members receive complimentary self-parking\* after scanning their rewards card at either the entry or exit gates.

Pricing for self-parking after the above-referenced complimentary hour is $20, for up to 24 hours _(except during certain events when special event pricing will apply)._

_\*Complimentary self-parking is subject to special events and holidays._

Does Fontainebleau Las Vegas offer complimentary valet?

Our valet validation program – allows any guest dining at the resort’s world-class restaurant and bar collection, spending within retail spaces, or enjoying a treatment at Lapis Spa & Wellness to validate their valet.\*

For all other guests, valet rates range from $35 to $40 per day, subject to availability.

_\*Valet validation is not available to registered hotel guests and is subject to special events and holidays._

Should I enter through the North or South Entrance?

If you are checking in, we recommend using the North Entrance. If you are visiting the casino, joining us for a meal, or coming to a concert or another event, we recommend using the South Entrance.

Are there car rental services on property?

Yes, we offer Hertz Rental on property.

I have an oversized truck, is there parking available?

The resort does not offer oversized parking. Please note that vehicles over 6’10” in height will not fit in our garage. For additional details, kindly contact Parking Management.

Frequently Asked Questions – Vegas LOOP

What is the Vegas LOOP?

The Vegas Loop is a high-speed, underground transportation system powered by Tesla vehicles. It offers a fast, comfortable way to travel beneath the Strip to key destinations like the Las Vegas Convention Center, nearby resorts, and more.

Where is the Vegas LOOP Passenger Station located at Fontainebleau Las Vegas?

Pickup and drop-off is located on Level V1 in the far lane of Rideshare Pickup, accessible from the South Lobby escalators or elevators.

What is the price to use the Vegas LOOP?

Standard Vegas LOOP pricing is:

Single Ride: $4.25

Round Trip: $7

Day Pass: $12.50

Where can the Vegas LOOP take me from Fontainebleau?

Guests can travel directly to and from Fontainebleau Las Vegas to:

Las Vegas Convention Center (all terminals)

Wynn

Encore

Resorts World Las Vegas

How long are rides?

Travel times vary by destination, but are significantly faster than walking or driving. For example, a trip across the Las Vegas Convention Center campus may take up to 25 minutes on foot but only 2 minutes on the Vegas LOOP.

Frequently Asked Questions – Electric Vehicle Charging

Where are the superchargers located?

The Superchargers are in P1 Self-Park, in the Northwest corner of the garage.

How fast can the superchargers charge?

Superchargers can deliver up to 180 kW, adding about 100 miles of range in 15–20 minutes.

How many chargers are available?

There are five stations located in P1 Self-Park. Each station is equipped with two cables allowing up to ten vehicles to charge simultaneously.

What vehicles are compatible?

The chargers use CCS1 connectors and are compatible with most fully electric vehicles except Tesla’s. Tesla drivers will need to bring their own CCS1-NACS adapter to use these chargers. Tesla NACS cables will be available in the future.

Do hotel guests receive complimentary charging in self-parking?

No. Charging in self-parking is not discounted or complimentary. Valet guests may receive charging as part of the valet service, subject to availability and on a first-come basis.

What is the charging fee?

Charging is billed at $0.56 per kW.

Is there an idle fee after charging is complete?

Yes. After a 15-minute grace period, idle time is billed at $1 per minute.

How do guests pay for charging?

After plugging in, guests tap or swipe a credit or debit card at the Nayax terminal. A $60 hold is placed to verify available funds. Any unused amount is automatically refunded. The charge will begin shortly after payment is processed.

Will customers know when charging is complete?

Most EV drivers receive notifications through their vehicle’s app. For those who don’t, the Nayax terminal displays a QR code that links to live session details through a web browser.

How long does a typical charging session take?

Charging from 40% to 80% typically takes 15–30 minutes, depending on the vehicle’s battery size.

What if a vehicle is parked in a charging stall but not plugged in?

Vehicles blocking or occupying a charging stall that are not plugged in and or actively charging are subject to towing.

What if a charger is not functioning or the screen is blank?

Guests should notify the valet desk on Casino Level 1 so the unit can be marked out of service.
