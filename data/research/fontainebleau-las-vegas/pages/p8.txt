# Hall of Excellence

The Hall of Excellence brings together the rarest memorabilia from the world’s most celebrated icons. Curated by seven-time Super Bowl champion Tom Brady and sportscasting legend Jim Gray, this museum experience reveals untold stories through extraordinary artifacts – from Muhammad Ali’s boxing gloves to Oprah Winfrey’s Presidential Medal of Freedom and memorabilia celebrating Elvis Presley’s era-defining return to Las Vegas in 1969.

Hall of Excellence FAQ

Where is the Hall of Excellence located?

The Hall of Excellence is located on Level 2 of Fontainebleau Las Vegas. Guests can follow signage to the retail promenade and take the escalator or elevator to access the venue.

What are the hours of operation?

The museum is open Thursday – Monday from 10AM – 8PM.

How much are tickets?

Tickets are $35 for general admission and for $20 for guests ages 5- 15. Seniors, Nevada residents, and military members receive discounted admission at $30 with valid ID.

What types of memorabilia are on display?

The collection features iconic memorabilia from legends like Michael Jordan, Jackie Robinson, Tom Brady, Muhammad Ali, Tiger Woods, Oprah Winfrey, Kobe Bryant, Serena Williams, and The Beatles. Visitors will also discover a Trophy Room displaying the Vince Lombardi Trophy, the Heisman Trophy, Olympic Gold medals, and major awards including the Emmy, Grammy, Academy Award, and Tony.

Is valet parking available? How much is self-parking?

Yes. Fontainebleau Las Vegas offers valet validation for guests who dine, shop, visit the Hall of Excellence, or enjoy Lapis Spa & Wellness services.

Self-Parking: Nevada residents and all Fontainebleau Rewards members receive complimentary self-parking.

How can I purchase tickets?

Tickets can be purchased online or in person at the Hall of Excellence.

Are there age restrictions to enter?

Yes, guests must be 5 or older to visit the Hall of Excellence.

Does the Hall of Excellence have any venue rules and restrictions?

Yes, please adhere to the following during your visit:

• Guests must wear resort attire at all times, including shoes, tops, and bottoms

• Ages 5+ only; guests under 16 must be accompanied by an adult

• No smoking, vaping, or e-cigarettes

• No food, gum, or drinks (water in closed containers only)

• Please silence mobile devices

• Only non-flash photography permitted; video recording is not allowed

• Please refrain from touching display cases

• Management reserves the right to deny or revoke entry for inappropriate behavior
