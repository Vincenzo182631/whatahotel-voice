Frequently Asked Questions

Is smoking allowed?

For the enjoyment of all guests, the Oasis Pool is non-smoking.

Can I sit anywhere?

Pool lounge chairs are available first come, first served. Cabanas & daybed reservations are available for registered hotel guests.

Is there a dress code?

Guests of and visitors of Fontainebleau Las Vegas must wear resort attire at all times including shoes, tops, and bottoms unless at Oasis Pool or LIV Beach. Sheer cover-ups, or cover-ups that are closed, are permitted while walking through the resort.

Can I bring outside food or drink to Oasis Pool?

Outside food and beverages that are not purchased at Oasis Pool are not permitted.

Are there any items not allowed at the pool?

The following items are prohibited on the pool deck:

– Outside food or beverage

– Water bottles are approved but must be sealed.​

– Glass​

– Coolers​

– Weapons​

– Luggage​

– Large rafts​

– Personal umbrellas​

– Personal speakers​

_Please keep in mind that any food and beverage for infants or medically necessary will be approved.​_
