# Savor Unparalleled Evenings

The hottest new destination in Las Vegas nightlife, Fontainebleau is home to an array of unforgettable late-night scenes, from sophisticated cocktail lounges to an awe-inspiring nightclub. Discover an unrivaled lineup of eleven venues that cater to every desire –  indulge in live music at Nowhere, mix and mingle at Bleau Bar, or party in style at LIV.
