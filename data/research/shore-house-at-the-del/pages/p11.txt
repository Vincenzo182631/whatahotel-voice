# Shore House Exclusive Experiences

## _Shore House_ Exclusive Experiences

A distinctive oceanfront experience within the iconic Hotel del Coronado, your stay at Shore House includes sophisticated amenities and exclusive experiences.

Your Shore House Concierge is available at 619.522.8811 or Concierge\_ShoreHouse@Hilton.com.

### An Oceanfront Pool

The centerpiece of Shore House is the stunning oceanfront pool framed by open skies and the rhythmic sound of the waves just steps outside the gates. Featuring a zero-edge entry that resembles the gentle slope of the shoreline, the Shore House pool is the gathering spot for friends old and new with sun loungers, day beds, private cabanas, and an ocean-view jacuzzi. Grab a cocktail from the mixologists at the open-air pool bar, or get food and drinks delivered to your lounge chair with a menu that highlights local cuisine.

The pool and jacuzzi are open from 8am to 8pm daily. To reserve a private cabana or day bed, please call the Shore House concierge at 619.522.8811 or reserve online.

### A Personal Concierge

Your stay at Shore House includes a dedicated concierge throughout your stay to handle every detail, all so you can relish the moment. Services include a dedicated valet and arrival experience, pantry stocking of your villa, curating an itinerary to immerse yourself in the local experience, priority reservations across the resort, and more.

Your Shore House concierge will reach out before your arrival to arrange your perfect visit. The concierge can be reached at 619.522.8811 or Concierge\_ShoreHouse@Hilton.com.

### Curated Adventures

Shore House guests enjoy unparalleled access to The Del and beyond. With a team of local experts and access to Southern California's legendary beach resort, you'll be immersed in local experiences. Discover something new or enjoy familiar comforts with a wide range of seaside diversions including private s'mores roasts, exploring Coronado Island by bike, docent-led historic tours, yacht excursions, surf lessons, ocean-inspired spa treatments, high-energy fitness classes overlooking the Pacific, and much more.

To plan your itinerary, contact the Shore House concierge at 619.522.8811 or shorehouse@hoteldel.com.
