Tivoli brings its timeless hospitality to the southern part of Tenerife, with a new 5-star hotel in Costa Adeje that is located on the island’s most exclusive stretch of coastline. Here, an oasis of relaxation, distinctive in its architecture and design, awaits overlooking the ocean.

Sunny days are spent in tropical gardens basking in one of three outdoor pools, playing padel, or sipping signature cocktails at any of our restaurants and bars. Adventures beyond include surfing, diving, sailing in search of whales and dolphins, or a day of golf at the local course.

Our resort is an ideal option for those that are looking for hotels in Costa Adeje for families with a wide range of services and tourist plans. Our culinary offerings draw connoisseurs from around the island, with the exceptional flavours of celebrity chef Olivier da Costa intriguing the senses and delighting the palate.

At our Costa Adeje resort, you can enjoy the first Anantara Spa in Tenerife, which offers moments of pure pleasure, combining the healing traditions of east and west for an experience to savour.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Where is Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort located?**

Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort is located in Costa Adeje, Tenerife. The hotel sits right on the oceanfront.

**How many stars is Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort?**

Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort is a luxurious 5-star hotel in Costa Adeje.

**Is there a charge for the Wi-Fi connection at Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort?**

No, Wi-Fi is free for guests at Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort.

**How far is Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort from the airport?**

Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort is a 20-minute taxi journey away from Tenerife Sud Airport. Alternatively, you can contact the hotel and book a shuttle.

**How far is Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort from the beach?**

Due to its location on the oceanfront, Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort is only 0.5 kilometres away from El Duque Beach. The famous beaches of Las Americas and Los Cristianos are also less than six kilometres away.

**What are the parking options offered at Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort?**

Free, on-site parking is available for guests at this 5-star hotel in Costa Adeje. However, please be aware that this parking is located outdoors. Garage parking is available for an additional fee.

**What are the check-in and check-out times at Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort?**

Guests staying at Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort can complete check-in any time after 15:00. Check-out must be completed by noon.

**Are there vegetarian or vegan options available at Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort?**

Yes, several. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are available in the hotel’s bars and restaurants.

**Are Electric Vehicle Chargers available at Tivoli La Caleta Tenerife Resort?**

We have 4 charging stations available for up to 8 vehicles. This service is subject to an additional charge
