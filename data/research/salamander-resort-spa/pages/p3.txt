Reviewed by
Laura Ratliff
Tell us a little about this place.
This is an opulent, sprawling, equestrian-themed resort with spacious rooms, each with horse decor and outdoor space (balconies or terraces). Activities abound—you'll find riding, golf, tennis, fishing, and, of course, a pool.
Who else is staying here?
Families with small kids; business travelers attending conferences.
Now, onto the good stuff: Tell us about the digs.
Rooms are spacious and bright. Some of the equestrian-inspired decor was designed by resort owner Sheila Johnson, the co-founder of BET. The Blue Ribbon Suite, on a corner, offers expansive views of the bucolic Virginia landscape and a stone terrace—the perfect perch for a glass of local wine.
How about the little things, like the mini bar, or shower goodies? What did you really love?
Rooms are well-appointed, with luxe bathrobes, in-room coffee makers, newspaper delivery, and nightly turndown service.
Anything else you want to shout out?
The 23,000-square-foot spa is a welcome retreat and unlike anything else in the area and the hotel's commitment to being pet-friendly is notable. Also, following the resort’s reopening in late June 2020 after closing due to COVID-19, it launched a Well-Meeting program to accommodate group events in different ways, including out-of-the-box venues like the 22-stall equestrian center, with its nine paddocks and riding arena. (It makes business meetings so much more pleasant.)
So, at the end of the day, what makes this hotel worth booking?
Given the poshness of the region, it's surprising that Salamander hasn't been around longer. The luxe retreat truly befits its surroundings.
