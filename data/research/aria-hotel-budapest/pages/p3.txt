Aria Hotel Budapest by Library Hotel Collection
A central location, great style, and long list of perks make this an easy choice for a Budapest base.

How did it strike you on arrival?
Past an elegant façade in Budapest’s historic center is a world inspired by music and powered by whimsy. Developed by New York-based hotelier Henry Kallan (whose other properties include the Aria Hotel, Prague, and Toronto’s Hotel X), this boutique charmer—opened in 2015—sits in a meticulously restored 19th-century former bank a short walk from the pedestrian shopping and dining streets. Topped by a state-of-the-art glass roof, the lobby-level Music Garden courtyard now serves as the hub of the hotel, where guests gather for breakfast, afternoon wine and cheese receptions, and live music—all complimentary daily.
Nice. What’s the crowd like?
As one of the latest upscale hotels in town, the Aria is attracting the new crop of luxury travelers now adding Budapest to their itineraries (including pre- and post-cruise guests.) The music theme draws guests interested in arts and culture, while the incredible rooftop sees a steady stream of locals, too.
How was check in?
Offering plenty of style and personality—and a world-class rooftop experience—The Aria Hotel is putting a modern twist on Budapest's luxury hotel scene. This outpost (fully launched this summer) sits in a beautifully restored, 19th-century former bank in the city's historic center, next to St. Stephen's Basilica and just steps from pedestrian shopping and dining streets. The 48 room and suites are separated into four musically themed towers—jazz, classical, opera, and contemporary—with each accommodation named for an artist or composer in that genre. But beyond a portrait of the room's namesake and related books and CDs, the decor isn’t theme-y—so non-music lovers will still enjoy the comforts of a luxury hotel room.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
The entire hotel has been designed to evoke a private residence, so the rooms and suites have been crafted with top-grade materials and furnishings, vibrant textiles, and eye-catching artwork. The colors and style vary depending on which musical genre the rooms represents, though, so you'll find Art Nouveau flair and dashes of Old Hollywood glamour in the Jazz tower, for example, and Neo-Baroque furniture and Murano glass chandeliers in the Classical wing. Techie perks abound, from 55-inch flat screens set on marble mantles (and equipped with Blu-ray players), phone docking stations (Apple and Android), and Handy 4G phones for free international calls, to access to a digital music library and an interactive multimedia entertainment system with home theater-grade sound projector. Good sleep is a priority: Beds can be made as plush or firm as you want by removing the pillow top or adding foam, and specialty pillows, hypoallergenic blankets, night lights, eye masks and other accessories are available upon request. The onyx-lined bathrooms feature separate showers and tubs, heated towel racks, Molton Brown products, and upgraded ionic hair dryers.
Room service and food: Worth it?
Gather in the central, glass-topped Music Garden for the complimentary daily breakfast—upgraded beyond the Continental to include made-to-order eggs, smoked salmon, home baked breads and more—and the daily two-hour wine and cheese reception (also included with the rate.) Three meals a day can be enjoyed in the Stadivari Restaurant—where farm-to-table, Hungarian-accented dare is presented amid a bold décor of floating musical notes—while the cozy, reto-chic Satchmo’s lounge is the place for casual bites, potent drinks, and cool jazz, either on the garden terrace or around the fireplace. To top it all off, the spectacular, year-round High Note Sky Bar sets the standard for rooftop hangouts in Budapest, thanks to 360-degree city views and Insta-worthy “love nest” seating nooks that seem to hover in the sky.
Anything stand out about other services and features? Whether it’s childcare, gyms, spas, even parking—whatever stuck with you.
Visit the CD- and DVD-lined office of the in-house Musical Director, who’s on hand to help craft musical itineraries in the city, score tickets, and even create playlists for your hotel stay, spa visit, or walks around the town. A small theater off the lobby hosts intimate performances and screenings, and can be booked by guests who want to watch a game or DVD on the big screen. The subterranean Harmony Spa’s got a heated pool with Aqua Bikes, Jacuzzi, small gym, steam room, Finnish and infrared saunas, and a menu of holistic treatments.
Bottom line: worth it, and why?
A central location, great style, and long list of perks make this an easy choice for a Budapest base.
