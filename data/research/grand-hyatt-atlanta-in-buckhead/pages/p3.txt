# Dining

At Grand Hyatt Atlanta in Buckhead, we offer breakfast, lunch, dinner and drinks across our upscale dining options, including two on-site restaurants, a cocktail bar and pool deck. Our curated menus are updated seasonally and feature locally sourced ingredients that celebrate our flavorful southern region. Whether dining alone, with a large party or hosting a private event in Buckhead, our restaurants provide the perfect setting for any occasion.

## The Fabled

The Fabled, beside our serene garden and waterfall, is Atlanta’s cocktail bar and culinary destination. Featuring Georgia’s finest local ingredients, garden-fresh herbs, and Southern elegance, it’s a place to connect and savor. Join us for Happy Hour, Mon–Fri, 4–6 PM, with rotating drink and appetizer specials.
