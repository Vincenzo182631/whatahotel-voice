The casual elegance of our fully redesigned guest rooms and suites awaits you. 439 newly renovated rooms—including 21 suites—at Grand Hyatt Atlanta in Buckhead provides splendid views of the surrounding Buckhead neighborhood, Atlanta's skyline, Stone Mountain or our peaceful Japanese Zen garden. Find your tranquil oasis in the heart of everything. Our refreshed and enhanced rooms feature luxurious bedding, a spacious work and dining area, built in charging stations and a granite bath with a vanity mirror. Select one of our luxury suites and enjoy additional amenities such as separate sitting areas, private balconies, wet bars, kitchenettes or extra half-baths.
