## Retreat to Extraordinary at Our Downtown Buckhead Hotel

Experience Atlanta in style at the reimagined Grand Hyatt Atlanta Buckhead. From our prime Buckhead location, explore the city’s iconic landmarks, shopping, and entertainment, then return to a sanctuary with a serene Japanese garden and waterfall, vibrant dining and cocktails, and elevated guest rooms designed for immersive comfort.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Stay in one of our luxury hotel rooms or suites in downtown Buckhead with gorgeous views of Atlanta's downtown skyline, Stone Mountain, Kennesaw Mountain or our Japanese Zen garden. Our central location provides a stunning and welcoming front row seat to the area’s cultural delights, an inspiring space to be productive and a rejuvenating opportunity to relax.

## EXPLORE

Resort-Style Heated Pool Retreat

Unwind at our outdoor pool, a refreshing oasis in the heart of Buckhead. Lounge in the sun, take a revitalizing swim, or relax poolside between meetings or city adventures. Designed for both leisure and escape, it’s your elevated retreat above Atlanta. Non-hotel guests can secure a daily pool pass through Resort Pass. The pool at Grand Hyatt Atlanta in Buckhead will be closed beginning October 25, 2026, through early Spring 2027.

Stay Enhancements & Experiences

Take your stay at Grand Hyatt Atlanta in Buckhead to the next level with thoughtfully curated experiences available to reserve before or during your visit. From in-room amenities like celebration packages and locally inspired snacks, to Peloton bike rentals delivered to your room, it’s easy to customize every detail of your time with us.

Whether you're planning a relaxing afternoon by the pool, or joining us for happy hour or outdoor yoga, elevating your visit is just a click away.

Japanese Zen Garden

Give yourself over to tranquility with winding paths, a bridge, the soothing sound of our 30-foot waterfall and plenty of greenery in our garden, designed by an award-winning Kyoto architect. This area is open 24/7, weather permitting.

Grand Club Lounge

Our Grand Club Lounge offers guests a private space to relax and enjoy exclusive amenities, including a dedicated concierge in the morning and afternoon, access to on-property computers and breakfast and dinner served in the Grand Club each day of their stay. The Grand Club Lounge is complimentary.

24-hour Fitness Center + In-Room Peloton Rental

While visiting Buckhead, maintain your workout regiment—or start a new one—at our state-of-the-art fitness center featuring cardio and wellness equipment by Peloton, Technogym and LifeFitness. Or, for just $50 per day, you can enjoy a private ride in the comfort of your guestroom, perfect for keeping up your routine. Availability is limited, contact atlgh-gcc.contact.group@hyatt.com to reserve your bike.

The Nest

A modern phone booth. A place to step out of the limelight for a quick call or video chat. Imagine enjoying a bite or cocktail in The Fabled and you find yourself in need of a quiet place for quick business call or to simply say goodnight to your family. No need to walk back to your guestroom, simply walk across the corridor to The NEST.

### Pets Are Welcome

We are happy to welcome your traveling canine companions to our pet-friendly hotel in Buckhead. We offer the following for our furry friends on request: hypoallergenic Pet Beds and Pet Bowls. To participate in the pet program: Inform the hotel you’ll have a pet (or pets) staying with you at least three days in advance of your arrival. Keep in mind, only dogs weighing 70 pounds and under are permitted.

#### Pet Fees

- Up to six nights: Free

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  50Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 70Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.

### Destination Fee

A daily destination fee of $30.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.
