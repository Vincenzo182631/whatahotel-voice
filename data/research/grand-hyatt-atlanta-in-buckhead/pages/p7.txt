## Transportation

Getting here

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL)18 miles18 miles

Transportation Options

MARTA transit system

Atlanta’s MARTA transit system runs to and from Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport. Fares are $2.50 each way. From the airport, Grand Hyatt Atlanta guests should board northbound trains (N to North Springs). Exit at the Buckhead Station N7 onto Peachtree Road S. The Buckhead Station is two blocks from our hotel.

Getting around

Shuttle Free

Grand Hyatt Atlanta in Buckhead offers guests a complimentary courtesy shuttle to restaurants, offices and shopping within a two-mile radius of our hotel. The Courtesy Shuttle runs Sunday-Thursday 3PM -11PM, and Friday-Saturday 4PM-12AM. Shuttle service is subject to availability and runs on a first-come, first-served basis. Call +1 404 358 2454 to request a Courtesy Shuttle.
