### Epona Restaurant

Located on the ground floor of the hotel, the restaurant gets its name from an extremely popular goddess of Gaul (Celtic) mythology, patron saint of horse-riding and travel. In the kitchen, young chef Mathieu Charrois opts for a menu with a predominantly local flavour, featuring timeless French classics with a contemporary twist.

Learn more

### Le Dôme bar

Located on the 1st floor, the hotel’s breath-taking Soufflot Dome, towering 32 metres high, has been one of the greatest private renovations of a classified site in France since 2013. The majestic Dome is the vibrant heart of the hotel, boasting a magnificent bar. Le Dôme bar is one of the highlights of the city for hotel guests and locals alike.

Learn more

### L'Académie by InterContinental

L'Académie is the conference centre of InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu, located in the most ancient part of the Grand Hôtel-Dieu. In this landmark filled with centuries of history, 1,500 sqm of stunning reception and meeting rooms have been designed, all equipped with the latest technology to welcome all types of events from 10 to 450 guests.

## FAQs for InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu

Does InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu have a pool?

No, InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu?

At InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 42 EUR .

Private parking is available at €42 per day for combustion vehicles, and €55 per night for electric or hybrid cars. Please note that charging cables are not provided.

Parking for registered guests only

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu.

Pet policy description. Dogs and cats are allowed in rooms with a fee of 39 euros per stay. The hotel must be notified in advance. Pets are, nonetheless, prohibited in the catering areas.

Pet fee per night: 39 EUR

Pet weight limit: 8

2 pets allowed

Pets allowed: Only dogs and cats allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu?

Check-in at InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Lyon - Hotel Dieu is conveniently located near Lyon–Saint-Exupéry Airport (LYS), approximately 22.99 miles away (37 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
