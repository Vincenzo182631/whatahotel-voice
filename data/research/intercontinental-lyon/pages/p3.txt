# InterContinental Lyon - Hotel DieuDining

At Epona, chef Mathieu Charrois creates a menu with predominantly local flavour, featuring revisited timeless Lyonnais classics. At this luminous restaurant, guests will enjoy dish after flavourful dish of French and international dishes.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

### Epona Restaurant

Located on the ground floor of the hotel, the restaurant gets its name from an extremely popular goddess of Gaul (Celtic) mythology, patron saint of horse-riding and travel. In the kitchen, young chef Mathieu Charrois opts for a menu with a predominantly local flavour, featuring timeless French classics with a contemporary twist.

Learn more

### Le Dôme bar

Located on the 1st floor, the hotel’s breath-taking Soufflot Dome, towering 32 metres high, has been one of the greatest private renovations of a classified site in France since 2013. The majestic Dome is the vibrant heart of the hotel, boasting a magnificent bar. Le Dôme bar is one of the highlights of the city for hotel guests and locals alike.

## Le Dôme bar

Le Dôme is a real hub and constitutes a space where the people of the city and international visitors meet and interact. The drinks menu offers a range of revolutionary and imaginative drinks, interblending a strong local influence with international creativity. This great mix of local and international is very much present in the dishes selected by chef Mathieu Charrois and the desserts curated by pastry chef Vincent Thomassin for the popular Tea Time.
