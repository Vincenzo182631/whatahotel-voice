## FAQs for Charlotte Square

Does Kimpton Charlotte Square have a pool?

Kimpton Charlotte Square has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

Our indoor 12-metre pool offers a tranquil oasis ideal for starting your day refreshed or unwinding after exploring Edinburgh’s many sights. Its serene, mood-lit ambiance enhances relaxation, ensuring a perfect retreat for guests seeking rejuvenation.

Pool information

How much is parking at Kimpton Charlotte Square?

At Kimpton Charlotte Square, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 45 GBP .

Kimpton Charlotte Square offers a valet parking service for our guests in a secure underground car park beneath the hotel (subject to availability). Valet parking is available for the price of £45 per 24h period and must be reserved in advance directly with the hotel by contacting:
concierge@kimptoncharlottesquare.com.

Should you be driving an electric car, we now also have Electric Vehicle Charging Stations available for our guests at a cost of £25 per charge.

To use our valet parking service, enter into your sat nav or maps app ‘38 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh, EH2 4HQ’. Please park outside our hotel main entrance and put on your car’s hazard lights. The front of house team will meet and greet you on arrival and assist with luggage before registering your vehicle with Concierge and getting you checked in and settled without delay. Please note this is a temporary situation given the closure of George Street.

Alternatively, street parking is free between 6:30 pm – 8.30 am every day, and on Sunday, parking charges apply from 12.30 – 18.30. Outside of these hours, there is a maximum parking time of three hours for £5.00 per hour. We also have a relationship with the NCP Car Park at Castle Terrace, Edinburgh, EH1 2EW, which is a 7-minute walk from the hotel. Before departure, we can give you a ticket which will discount the price at the machine (price varies on window of parking).

How far is Kimpton Charlotte Square from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Charlotte Square is conveniently located near Edinburgh Airport (EDI), approximately 7 miles away (11.27 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
