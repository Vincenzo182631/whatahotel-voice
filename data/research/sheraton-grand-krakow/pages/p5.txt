### Discover the Magic of Kraków

From the majestic Wawel Castle to the bustling Main Market Square and the soulful streets of the Jewish Quarter, Kraków is a city where every corner tells a story.

## The History of the Site: from the Royal Brewery to the Sheraton Grand Krakow

This riverside site has continually evolved with Kraków’s history. First home to the Royal Brewery in the 18th century, its position along the Vistula River provided ideal access to water and trade routes. In the 19th century, it was transformed into military barracks and later continued to serve various military and administrative functions. Following decades of changing uses and a period of decline, the historic complex was redeveloped into the Sheraton in 2002. Today, the hotel’s contemporary architecture incorporates a historic flood gauge, a subtle reminder of the site’s enduring connection to the river and its rich heritage.

### Your Ideal Gateway to Małopolska’s Treasures

Experience the best of the Małopolska region right at your doorstep. From the legendary Wieliczka and Bochnia Salt Mines, where centuries of mining history lie hidden beneath the surface, and the poignant Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial, to the breathtaking mountain landscapes of Zakopane, the area offers unforgettable journeys in every direction.

## Bochnia Salt Mine

Descend into the world’s oldest operating salt mine, where centuries of history unfold through vast underground chambers, historic mining passages, remarkable salt formations, and unique subterranean attractions. The Bochnia Salt Mine offers a fascinating journey through Poland’s mining heritage, combining rich history, engineering ingenuity, and the unforgettable atmosphere of a hidden underground realm.

Discover

## UNESCO Bochnia Salt Mine Experience

Discover the UNESCO-listed Bochnia Salt Mine on an exclusive private tour, featuring hidden underground chambers, a historic mining railway, a boat journey, and a private dinner beneath the surface. Complete your experience with a two-night stay at Sheraton Grand Krakow.

Uncover the Experience

## Wieliczka Salt Mine

Descend into one of Poland’s most extraordinary UNESCO World Heritage Sites, where centuries old chambers, salt carved chapels, shimmering underground lakes, and intricate sculptures create a world unlike any other. The Wieliczka Salt Mine offers a captivating blend of history, craftsmanship, and natural wonder.

Discover

## Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial & Museum

Discover one of Europe’s most important historical sites and gain a deeper understanding of the events of World War II. This UNESCO World Heritage Site offers a meaningful journey through history, with preserved buildings, exhibitions, and memorials that provide insight into the lives of those affected while honoring their memory.

Discover

## Zakopane

Escape to Poland’s mountain capital, nestled at the foot of the stunning Tatra Mountains. Discover breathtaking alpine scenery, traditional wooden architecture, vibrant local markets, and regional cuisine. Stroll along lively streets filled with cafés and artisan shops, or take in panoramic views of the surrounding peaks.

Moreover, Zakopane is just a drive or train ride away, making it easy to experience the quieter side of the region, the mountainside, on the same trip.
