# Explore Our Premium Rooms & Suites

## Premium Rooms

Elevate your stay at Sheraton Grand Krakow, where luxury and comfort come together in premium Krakow accommodation. Our hotel rooms and suites in Krakow feature 65-inch TVs, BOSE sound systems, and premium Nespresso machines, with views of Wawel Castle or the Vistula River. For business travelers, Club Lounge access adds complimentary refreshments, personalized service, and a stylish place to relax or work, while city-break couples can make the most of the river and castle setting. Guests often highlight the spacious rooms, comfortable beds, and memorable views, making these premium stays an inviting choice for work or leisure.

UNESCO Bochnia Salt Mine Experience
