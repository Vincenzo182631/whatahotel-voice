# Welcome to Sheraton Grand Krakow

## Discover our Krakow spa hotel

Located at the foot of the Royal Castle on the banks of the Vistula River, Sheraton Grand Krakow is a central hotel for discovering the city’s dining, cultural, and historic highlights, including Krakow...See More Cathedral, Main Market Square, and Kazimierz. For city-break couples, the riverside setting and walkable location make it easy to explore Old Town and enjoy evening drinks, while families can settle into 230 elegant rooms and suites at our hotel in Krakow. Guests often praise our location near key sights, comfortable beds, and views of the river and castle. The wellness area at our Krakow spa hotel offers a pool, sauna, and gym open 24/7. Dine at Anima Restaurant, take in sunsets from the seasonal Roof Top Terrace, or watch live sports at SomePlace Else. Planning an event? Our Krakow central hotel offers 495 sq m of flexible event space for up to 300 guests. Our 5-star hotel in Krakow is also pet-friendly and welcomes pets for a fee.See Less

## Discover Kraków

Krakow is a city brimming with history and culture. Explore the historic Kazimierz District, the heart of the Jewish Quarter, and visit the majestic Wawel Castle, a symbol of Poland's royal past. Wander through the Main Market Square and admire the iconic St. Mary's Basilica. Beyond the city, discover profound landmarks like the Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial, the awe-inspiring Wieliczka Salt Mine, and the scenic town of Zakopane in the Tatra Mountains, perfect for outdoor adventures.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Grand Krakow?

The check-in time at Sheraton Grand Krakow is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Grand Krakow allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Sheraton Grand Krakow. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 17.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of zł100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Grand Krakow?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Grand Krakow. Self parking is available at Sheraton Grand Krakow, with rates of zł18.00 per hour, and zł125.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Grand Krakow have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Krakow has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Grand Krakow booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Grand Krakow?

The closest airport near Sheraton Grand Krakow is John Paul II International Airport Kraków–Balice (KRK). KRK is located approximately 11.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Grand Krakow have an airport shuttle to John Paul II International Airport Kraków–Balice (KRK)?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Krakow has an airport shuttle to John Paul II International Airport Kraków–Balice (KRK).

Does Sheraton Grand Krakow have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Krakow has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does Sheraton Grand Krakow have a The Club lounge?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Krakow has The Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with The Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
