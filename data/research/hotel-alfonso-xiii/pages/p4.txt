# Dine at Our Exquisite Seville Restaurants

## Dining

Discover the dining experiences at Hotel Alfonso XIII, a Luxury Collection Hotel in Seville. Each of our restaurants offers a unique culinary journey, whether it's traditional Spanish fare, tapas, your...See More favorite tipple at our bar, or a classic brunch in Seville. Find authentic Andalusian cuisine and delicious brunch in Seville, served in Restaurante San Fernando. For a social dining experience, Ena Sevilla specializes in traditional tapas and dishes to share with friends. Inspired by the hotel’s heritage, Bar Americano is our esteemed wine bar in Seville, offering a choice of masterfully mixed cocktails in an Art Deco setting.
