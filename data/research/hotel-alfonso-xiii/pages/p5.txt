## Discover Seville

Hotel Alfonso XIII, a Luxury Collection Hotel, is located in the heart of Seville, a bustling city in the south of Spain. Seville is well known for its unique beauty and culture and offers many unique experiences to the visitors. A visit to the Reales Alcázares Palace and Seville´s Gothic Cathedral are a mandatory as these landmarks feature magnificent architecture and play an important role in the city´s history. The vanguardist sculpture “Metropol Parasol” creates an amazing contrast with its classical surroundings and is also highly recommended. A stroll through the Santa Cruz quarter and stopping to savor authentic Andalusian tapas is also a highly rated experience. An authentic horse-drawn carriage ride or bike rental are great ways to see the city, including Triana or El Arenal quarters, which offer characteristic Flamenco shows.
