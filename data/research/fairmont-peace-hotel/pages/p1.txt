## The _perfect_ place to explore Shanghai’s spectacular scenery and vibrant lifestyle

This landmark hotel has historically served as a glamorous playground for the elite, where every night was an extravagant gala event and veritable Parisian fashion show. Today, Fairmont Peace Hotel continues to be a sought-after venue for meetings, weddings and social events in the city.

Guests staying at the hotel are also invited to discover its legendary heritage through a complimentary 30-minute guided tour of the Peace Museum, offering an intimate glimpse into the stories, architecture and cultural legacy of this iconic Bund landmark.

### A world of experiences at your fingertips

A selection of four restaurants and lounges will include the endeared Jazz Bar and a terrace on the ninth floor with unrivalled panoramic views.

##### The Cathay Room

Located on the 9th floor, the Cathay Room was formerly Sir Sassoon's private banquet hall. It combines Art Deco elegance with a 270° riverfront terrace, offering refined French cuisine, afternoon tea, and a signature Sunday brunch.

EXPLORE

##### Dragon Phoenix

Located on the 8th floor, the restaurant features dragon-phoenix ceiling motifs and red pillars with views of the Bund and Lujiazui. Master Chef John Ma expertly crafts Shanghai specialties and other regional cuisines of China.

EXPLORE

##### The Jazz Bar

The legendary Jazz Bar, once known as “Horse and Hound,” invites you to immerse in nightly performances by the Guinness World Record-holding Old Jazz Band, complemented by signature cocktails that whisk you back in time.

## A Century of Heritage, A Journey of Peace

Home to a rich collection of historic artifacts, the Peace Museum traces the century-old legacy of the former Cathay Hotel, the predecessor of Fairmont Peace Hotel, from 1929 to the present day. Recognized with the 2026 Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice award, the museum invites guests to discover the architectural memory of the Bund and the timeless heritage of Fairmont Peace Hotel through a complimentary 30-minute guided tour.
