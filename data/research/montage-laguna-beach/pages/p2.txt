Why book?
Set amongst the bluffs along one of Southern California’s most cinematic stretches of coastline, Montage Laguna Beach is a timeless coastal getaway designed for special occasions and making lasting memories with the ones you love in mind.
Set the scene
Despite being spread across 30 acres of manicured lawns and dramatic coastline, staying at the Montage feels surprisingly intimate. The hotel is the essence of quiet luxury, a welcome contrast to its flashier Laguna neighbors. It’s not a see-and-be-seen spot, which takes the pressure off and lets you fully settle into vacation mode.
Designed to resemble a California Craftsman-style village, the property’s cliffside paths stay active throughout the day—couples walking hand in hand, families heading toward the beach, and dogs making their morning, afternoon, and sunset rounds. It’s the kind of place where people slow down, take in the view, and casually greet each other before moving on.
The Montage is also a special-occasion destination amongst the “moon” crowd. Honeymooners, babymooners, and family-mooners mix easily with stylish Bay Area and LA couples seeking total relaxation. You’ll also see East Coast snowbirds thawing out and families settling in for extended stays. Celebrities often slip in quietly for dinner at The Loft or a low-key afternoon by the pool. Popular with Laguna Beach and LA locals for a quick staycation, the property also draws a loyal set of Montage brand devotees who’ve stayed at one of the sister properties in South Carolina, Deer Valley, or Los Cabos.
All of these groups come together in the living-room-style lounge off the lobby. By day, guests with laptops and cappuccinos make remote work look appealing as they face the Pacific through floor-to-ceiling windows. By night, the mood shifts into a gathering space for families playing cards, friends clinking champagne flutes or martinis, and a piano player setting an atmosphere that’s convivial, intimate, and distinctly O.C.
The backstory
Built on a bluff overlooking Treasure Island Beach, the hotel opened in 2003 as the flagship property of Montage Hotels & Resorts. Its Craftsman-inspired architecture, residential-style rooms, and high-touch service quickly set a high luxury benchmark for Laguna Beach. The resort also introduced one of the area’s first large-scale destination spas with the 20,000-square-foot Spa Montage.
In 2019, the property underwent a major refresh to the lobby, guest rooms, and spa, updating the interiors while preserving the original architectural style. Most recently, in 2025, the hotel reopened its Mediterranean fine-dining restaurant, Studio Mediterranean, after nearly five years, and debuted a renovated Mosaic Pool with upgraded cabanas. Today, Montage Laguna Beach remains one of Southern California’s definitive seaside resorts, known for its stunning views, excellent service, and loyal repeat guests.
The rooms
There are 263 guest rooms, including 60 suites and six standalone villas, and every room offers an ocean view plus a private balcony or patio. Interiors lean into modern California coastal design with cool, neutral tones and clean-lined furnishings that nod to California Craftsman style. Artwork throughout the rooms highlights local artists and scenes from Laguna Beach, a nod to the area’s longstanding artist community.
Rooms are spacious, thoughtfully laid out, and anchored by what are genuinely some of the most comfortable, cloud-like beds you’ll ever sleep on. Bathrooms are large, with soaking tubs, walk-in showers, and double vanities, standard even in entry-level room categories.
As comfortable as the rooms are, the real star is what’s just outside: uninterrupted views of the property’s landscaped grounds, dramatic cliffs, and the glittering Pacific Ocean.
Food and drink
There are four dining options on the property, starting with Studio Mediterranean, which debuted in 2025 as the resort’s newest and most ambitious restaurant to date. Studio had some formidable shoes to fill, replacing the eponymous and long-beloved French fine-dining institution that had defined special-occasion fine dining in Laguna Beach for more than twenty years.
After closing in 2020, the Studio reopened in the same dramatic cliffside setting, and it’s every bit as romantic, especially if you can snag a table on the oceanfront patio at sunset. The menu draws inspiration from Mediterranean and Greek traditions, with a strong emphasis on seasonal ingredients sourced from California farms. Tableside dishes are a highlight, including a seasonal classic Greek salad and mouthwatering whole branzino baked in a coffee-grinds and sea-salt crust, then expertly deboned and served to perfection.
For something more relaxed, The Loft is the hotel’s all-day dining concept. Come dinnertime, be sure to chat with the restaurant's sommelier for a stellar wine to pair with your meal. Mosaic Bar and Grille delivers as the hotel’s poolside lunch spot and is available on a first-come, first-served basis. To round it out, the Lobby Lounge is ideal for a light bite or a pre- or post-dinner aperitif. No matter where you dine, you’ll be treated like family. The service and food across each of these outlets is top-notch, so no matter where you go, you really can’t go wrong.
The spa
Spa Montage is a 20,000-square-foot indoor-outdoor facility that is a destination in its own right. With a full hydrotherapy circuit, including a steam room, sauna, cold plunge, and hot tub, there are 21 treatment rooms, and the program here leans heavily into massage rituals and facials. There is also a full-service salon and 24-hour fitness center. Don’t miss the chance to sign up for a Beach Boot Camp, guided beach walk, or Montage Yoga Ritual, all of which are offered daily and included for guests of the resort. Last and certainly not least, this is also where you’ll find one of the property's most important amenities: a full-service adults-only oceanfront lap pool.
The neighborhood/area
The Montage is located at the southern tip of Laguna Beach, about a two-mile walk from downtown Laguna. The resort offers direct access to Treasure Island Beach, along with miles of hiking and biking trails in nearby Wood Canyons Wilderness Park and Crystal Cove State Park. Once you’re on the property, there’s plenty to see and do, but be sure to stock up on any necessities you might want or need before you arrive, as there’s not much else within direct walking distance.
The service
The service here is exceptional, and from the moment you arrive at the valet to the moment you leave, you’ll feel very well taken care of.
For families
This is an excellent option for families and kids of all ages. The beach is easy to access from the hotel, and kids will delight in exploring the tide pools along the shoreline. The hotel also offers several heated outdoor pools and hot tubs. The rooms are also very generous in size, and both standard guest rooms and suites can be conjoined to accommodate families. For families wanting to stay together, the Bungalow Suites offer spacious accommodations with separate living rooms from the primary bedroom and can connect up to four guest rooms in a row, ideal for multi-generational families and travelers. For young travelers, Paintbox and Paintbox Petite are the resort’s exclusive clubs for children ages 5 to 12 and 2 to 5. Youngsters enjoy crafts, arts, games, and outdoor recreation in both full and half-day sessions.
Eco effort
While the property has removed single-use plastics from the guest rooms and spa, small single-use plastic water bottles are still prevalent throughout the hotel.
Accessibility
There are 15 ADA-compliant guest rooms in the main building. An Ocean View King is the best ADA room option since these rooms offer direct, barrier-free access, close proximity to amenities, and indoor corridors with flat surfaces and wider hallways. There are also ramps both on the interior throughout the property, as well as ramp access to the beach.
Anything left to mention?
While the beach, pools, spa, and dining are all main attractions at Montage, the resort doesn’t have tennis or golf on property. To accommodate guests, the Montage has partnered with the nearby Laguna Niguel Racquet Club, which offers 22 championship courts and can be booked for an additional $50 per person. The Montage is also in close proximity to three golf courses, including Monarch Beach Golf, Talega Golf Club, and Ben Brown’s Golf Course at the Ranch at Laguna Beach, which can be booked through the concierge in advance.
