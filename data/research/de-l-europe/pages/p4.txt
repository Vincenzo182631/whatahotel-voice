# Freddy’s Bar

## A BAR WITH HISTORY AND AUTHENTICITY

A gentleman’s pub named after Alfred “Freddy” Heineken – who used to repose here. It is the place in Amsterdam where both locals and visitors come to socialize, connect and enjoy live jazz. Freddy’s Bar operates on a walk-in basis, so feel free to drop by without making reservations.
