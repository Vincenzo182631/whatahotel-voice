# ‘T HUYS

#### GUARDIAN OF LOCAL CULTURE AND CRAFTSMANSHIP

Welcome to ‘t Huys: a microcosm under our roof, where Amsterdam born and bred brands take up permanent residence, bringing perennial style, sophistication and a local ambiance.

Within the arty design confines of ‘t Huys, established names in couture, art, film, print, music and beyond carve out their own sanctuaries. Here, the lines between luxury hospitality and a vibrant creative community blur. A celebration of Amsterdam’s indomitable heritage, craftsmanship and essence. From the brilliance of fine jewellery to tender strokes of art, every visit to ‘t Huys is a new chapter in an ever-evolving narrative, across fourteen bespoke suites, local experiences, a concept store, and beyond.

**‘T HUYS IS AN OLD SPELLING OF HET HUIS, MEANING THE HOUSE IN DUTCH**.

## CONCEPT STORE IN-HUYS

Nesteld on the ground floor of ‘t Huys, you’ll find IN-HUYS, a concept store filled with unique items capturing Amsterdam’s charm. Each piece has a story, reflecting the city’s culture. The items are carefully curated, not just for the looks but for the tales they tell. The store goes beyond the usual, embracing creativity and diversity.

IN-HUYS is more than just a concept store; it’s a hub where artists come together to enrich your experience. Collaborating with like-minded partners, we ensure every visitor enjoys a truly inspiring time.

## The Wunderkammer

The Wunderkammer atelier is a tranquil oasis hidden in plain sight. Here, renowned floral artists and co-founders, Florian Seyd and Ueli Signer, display floral works of art, interior objects and curiosities. From rare artifacts and vintage curiosities to avant-garde art pieces, each item within The Wunderkammer has been thoughtfully selected to inspire awe and fascination.

As visitors explore the atelier, they are invited to embark on a journey through time and culture, where every object tells a story.
