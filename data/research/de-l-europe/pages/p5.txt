# Amsterdam High Tea

Discover the Sisters Janssen Afternoon Tea, a contemporary interpretation of the classic afternoon tea, where timeless tradition meets refined Dutch creativity. Inspired by their colourful artistic universe, each course combines refined Dutch flavours with contemporary creativity, from fine teas to handcrafted pastries and playful reinterpretations of classic favourites.

This limited-edition Afternoon Tea celebrates Amsterdam’s vibrant creative community, offering a unique seasonal experience at De L’Europe Amsterdam.

## A Dutch Afternoon Tea

Indulge in a luxury afternoon tea in Amsterdam, featuring a refined selection of sandwiches, artisan pastries, and handcrafted sweets created by our expert pastry chefs. This Amsterdam-themed high tea with a Dutch twist is paired with the finest loose-leaf teas, carefully curated to complement each flavour. Optionally furher elevated with a glass of Maison Ruinart Champagne or enjoy a sophisticated alcohol-free pairing.

Relax in our charming setting overlooking the Amstel River, where you can immerse yourself in an afternoon of sheer indulgence and impeccable service.

During warmer days and good weather, this refined high tea experience is served on our terrace, allowing you to enjoy the elegant surroundings and riverside views in the open air.

## Inspired by Sisters Janssen

Every course has been designed to tell part of the sisters’ story. Discover a refreshing palate cleanser referencing the playful Lollipop design, and handcrafted pastries inspired by their artworks and artistic philosophy. Signature creations include an Amsterdam Canal House crafted from Caramelia chocolate, alongside elegant desserts, freshly baked scones with raspberry hagelslag, and refined Dutch flavours reimagined with a contemporary twist.
