## Welcome to De L’Europe Amsterdam

A place for those who appreciate authenticity and luxurious hospitality, and are eager to embrace Amsterdam’s rich heritage in the most elegant way since 1896.

## A Cultural Stay

**KUSAMA AT THE STEDELIJK MUSEUM**

Immerse yourself in the extraordinary world of Yayoi Kusama with a cultural escape at De L’Europe Amsterdam. Combine an elegant stay along the Amstel with a visit to the Stedelijk Museum, where Kusama’s distinctive artistic universe takes centre stage.

Enjoy an overnight stay, daily breakfast at Brasserie Marie and complimentary museum tickets, thoughtfully arranged for a seamless cultural experience in Amsterdam.
