# Concept Store In-Huys

## Amsterdam’s Curated Concept Store

Welcome to concept store IN-HUYS, a dedicated homage to the vibrant spirit of Amsterdam.

**Nieuwe Doelenstraat 10, 1012 CP Amsterdam.**

**Every day from 12:00 – 18:00h**

As you step inside, you’ll find yourself surrounded by unique items and exclusive finds that embody Amsterdam’s unmistakable charm. Discover the allure of sustainable haute couture dresses by the renowned Ronald van der Kemp, or the intellectual richness of cultural books from local publishers, our curated selection ensures there’s something for everyone.

Every item in our collection has a tale to tell, a narrative woven into its fabric or etched into its design. We believe in the power of stories, and that’s why each piece is handpicked to not only be a unique creation but a storyteller in its own right. The authenticity of our offerings reflects the rich cultural tapestry of Amsterdam.

Indulge in our concept store that transcends the ordinary, celebrating the creativity and diversity that make Amsterdam truly exceptional.

You might describe it as the collective ethos, collaborative spirit, and creative community within the concept store.

It implies that our partners share similar values, aesthetics, and innovative approaches to their craft, contributing to a cohesive and inspiring shopping experience for customers.

## Intelligent Change

At InHuys, we believe in collaborations, and we are thrilled to announce that InHuys will be the temporary home and only concept store in Amsterdam where you can purchase the thoughtfully designed products of Intelligent Change. From journals to notebooks, from special oils to affirmation cards, these products help you realize your potential and live a happier, more fulfilling life, embodying the philosophy of Intelligent Change. Explore a curated blend of art, design, and local culture alongside Intelligent Change’s products, designed to empower you on your journey of personal growth and fulfilment. Join us on a journey where individual passion meets collective creativity, redefining the art of shopping and the pursuit of a fulfilling life.

## F.A.Q

- in-huys information

**Are the products made locally by the designers?**

Yes, our curated collection at In-Huys features products crafted by local designers, capturing the essence of Amsterdam’s creative spirit.

**Can I add the bill to my room services?**

Absolutely, you can conveniently add your In-Huys Concept Store purchases to your room services bill. Enjoy a seamless and integrated shopping experience during your stay.

**What is the concept of the store?**

In-Huys is Amsterdam’s Curated Concept Store, offering a unique blend of locally crafted treasures that embody the city’s distinctive charm. From sleek Kokke House chairs to cultural books from local publishers, every item tells a story. We celebrate diversity and creativity, ensuring that each piece is not just a product but a storyteller, reflecting Amsterdam’s rich cultural tapestry. Visit our store to indulge in a shopping experience that goes beyond the ordinary, showcasing the exceptional creativity that defines Amsterdam.
