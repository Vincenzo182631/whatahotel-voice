## Made in China

Made in China, Chinese Restaurant received the MICHELIN Plate in 2021 and 2020 Beijing MICHELIN Guide.

As the saying goes, you haven’t truly experienced Beijing until you’ve dined at Made in China. For over two decades, this award-winning culinary destination has been the place to savor authentically crafted Beijing classics, including its signature Peking duck. Over the years, Made in China has welcomed numerous heads of state, government leaders, and ministers from around the world. It has become a favored gathering place for celebrities and dignitaries alike.

## The Balcony

With its elegant, modern dining space and views of traditional Chinese architecture through floor-to-ceiling windows, The Balcony offers a dual feast for the eyes and the palate. Choose from an international buffet or an à la carte menu featuring flavours from around the world. Italian Executive Chef Luca Pulese selects seasonal ingredients to prepare dishes live in the open kitchen. Complemented by exquisite desserts and vibrant beverages, guests can enjoy a relaxed, sumptuous dining experience in the heart of the bustling city.

## Fountain Lounge

Designed by award-winning international interior design firm, Fountain Lounge has been conceived as a contemporary Beijing courtyard and aims to recreate a similar atmosphere of relaxation and tranquility.
Fountain Lounge features two elegant drinking and dining areas. Dominating the west end is a birdcage-like server’s area where a signature afternoon tea is prepared, while the east end showcases a glamorous Champagne Bar.

## The Patisserie

Showcasing a modern Art Deco aesthetic, The Patisserie is an elegant purveyor of luxury goods where baked confections and artisanal treats are treated with the utmost reverence. The Patisserie offers an array of inspirational gourmet items and gifts, including signature cakes, handmade pralines, chocolate truffles, and exquisitely packaged hampers, all of which are popular choices for guests to take away.
