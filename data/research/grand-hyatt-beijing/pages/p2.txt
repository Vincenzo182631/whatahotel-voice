Grand Hyatt Beijing at Oriental Plaza houses 518 rooms and suites with floor-to-ceiling windows showcasing striking views of historic Beijing. Ranging from 31–260 sqm, the rooms are designed to offer a private, luxurious sanctuary in the heart of the capital. Alongside every comfort and the latest technology, guests will discover a host of subtle luxuries from Bose speakers to Dyson hairdryers to toiletries in large eco-friendly bottles by French fashion house Balmain.　※ In accordance with the Beijing government’s guidelines, hotels in Beijing cannot voluntarily provide single-use amenities such as toothbrushes, combs, razors, etc. as part of the room setup. Please contact the hotel Guest Services for assistance if you would like to have these items delivered to your room during your stay. Also, smoking is prohibited in public places, indoor work environments, outdoor queuing areas and other such places.
