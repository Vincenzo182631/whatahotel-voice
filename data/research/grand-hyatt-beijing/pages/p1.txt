## The Capital’s Business and Lifestyle Landmark

Located on Wangfujing, within walking distance of The Forbidden City and Tiananmen Square, Grand Hyatt Beijing is part of Oriental Plaza with direct access to the adjacent shopping mall. Blending both western and oriental traditions encompassing contemporary design and classic standards of service and excellence, our hotel allows you to experience Beijing in our own way.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Unwind in one of our spacious rooms or upgrade to one of our 114 suites, spanning 55 to 260 square meters, for more space and a separate living room. For the ultimate indulgence, opt for the spacious two-story Presidential Suite with panoramic views of Chang’an Avenue where you’ll enjoy 24-hour butler service, a complimentary minibar, kitchen, dressing room and bathroom with a jetted tub, sauna and TV.

## EXPLORE

Club Oasis Swimming Pool

Dive into the breathtaking Club Oasis resort-style indoor swimming pool for a truly unforgettable experience. Escape the hustle and bustle of Beijing at this stunning 1,500-square-meter sapphire pool, surrounded by lush tropical vegetation, cascading waterfalls, majestic pillars, stone grottos and the enchanting sound of underwater music. Take a refreshing dip or unwind with a drink on a poolside lounger as you soak in the tropical ambiance. Complimentary access is offered for all in-house guests.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

Club Oasis Fitness Center & Spa

Fitted throughout with the latest exercise equipment from leading fitness firm Technogym, guided by a professional coaching team. The spa offers a wide range of body and facial treatments, as well as manicure services for you to rejuvenate and relax.

Club Lounge

Located on the 17th floor, the Grand Club offers an elevated experience with exclusive privileges, including personalized VIP check-in and check-out, dedicated concierge services, and access to private meeting facilities. The lounge features distinctive views on both sides: the south side overlooks the bustling Chang’an Avenue, while the north side offers panoramic views of the magnificent Forbidden City.

### Made in China

As the saying goes, you haven’t truly experienced Beijing until you’ve dined at Made in China. For over two decades, this award-winning culinary destination has been the place to savor authentically crafted Beijing classics, including its signature Peking duck. Over the years, Made in China has welcomed numerous heads of state, government leaders, and ministers from around the world. It has become a favored gathering place for celebrities and dignitaries alike.

#### Featured review

You really can’t beat the location of the Grand Hyatt - close to major sights and shopping, near subway and in quiet area. The rooms are spacious, the staff is attentive and helpful, knows English well, and the food/restaurants are all good. The spa, pool and gym are fantastic for a large hotel --especially the beautiful indoor pool. All in all, a good balance of comfort, service, location and value for money. My go-to hotel in Beijing.
