## Transportation

Getting here

Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK)28 kilometers28 kilometers

Transportation Options

Airport Transfers

Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK) is located in the northeast of Beijing, 31 kilometers from Tiananmen Square. There are three terminals. Transport is available via Grand Hyatt Beijing limousine (from CNY 900 for Benz E), taxi, the Airport Express Line (high-speed subway) or the Airport Bus Service.

Airport Limousine Service

Enjoy the privacy and comfort of our hotel’s private limousine to travel from Beijing Capital International Airport to our hotel. En route, cold towels and refreshing mineral water will help revive you after a long journey. Grand Hyatt Beijing provides various types of vehicles for its private limousine service. Half-day and full-day private tours are also available on request.

Beijing Capital International Airport Express Line

Location: Dongzhimen Station in central Beijing
Operating hours: 6 a.m.–10:30 p.m. daily
Frequency: Approximately every 15 minutes
Fees: CNY 25 (approximately US $4) per person
Reservations: Purchase tickets at the AEL Station at the airport

CN¥25.0025.00 Chinese yuan

Beijing International Daxing Airport (PKX)59 kilometers59 kilometers

Transportation Options

Airport Transfers

Beijing International Daxing Airport (PKX) is located in the south of Beijing, 59 kilometers from Tiananmen Square. Transport from the airport is available via Grand Hyatt Beijing limousine (from CNY 1500 for BMW 5 Series), taxi, the Airport Express Line (high-speed subway) or the Airport Bus Service.

Airport Express Line

Location: Caoqiao Station in South Beijing
Operating hours: 6 a.m.–10:30 p.m. daily
Frequency: Approximately every 10 minutes
Fees: CNY 35 (approximately US $5) per person
Reservations: Purchase tickets at the AEL Station at the airport

CN¥35.0035.00 Chinese yuan

Navigation Guidance for International Guests

For seamless navigation during your stay in Chinese Mainland, we recommend using local mapping applications such as Gaode Map or Baidu Map. International mapping apps may not be accurate in Chinese Mainland.

TaxiCN¥13.0013.00 Chinese yuan

Taxis operate 24 hours. Charges vary depending on the destination. The basic charge for a taxi is CNY 13 for 3 kilometers. The hotel can provide a taxi card that features bilingual address information that can be presented to taxi drivers to ensure that guests arrive at the correct destinations. To obtain your taxi card, please contact the hotel. Our concierge will be pleased to assist if you need further help.
