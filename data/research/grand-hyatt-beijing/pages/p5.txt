### Stand out club room

We had a wonderful stay at the Grand Hyatt Beijing, and the club lounge experience was a real highlight.

Libby was amazing and consistently attentive to every detail. She made us feel genuinely welcomed throughout our stay, and her attention to detail really stood out.

Hanna at the front desk was also exceptional. When we arrived, she personally escorted us to the club room, gave us a helpful lay of the land, and went above and beyond to help us find our way around the mall below the hotel. That extra personal touch made a great first impression.

Marvin, the club manager, was another standout. He checked us in personally on the 17th floor and was always attentive and welcoming throughout our stay.

The breakfast in the club was excellent. I especially want to call out the quality of the poached eggs, which were consistently very well prepared, as well as the exciting variety of Asian dishes. We really appreciated that the selections rotated daily, so breakfast never felt repetitive.

The evening offerings were also very enjoyable. The club could get a bit overfull at times, particularly in the evenings, but the quality of the food and drinks more than made up for it. We especially loved the Japanese sesame dressing and the convenience of being able to pour our own drinks.

Overall, the service, attention to detail, and quality of the club lounge made this a memorable stay. The team members mentioned above truly made a difference, and we would happily return to the Grand Hyatt Beijing.
