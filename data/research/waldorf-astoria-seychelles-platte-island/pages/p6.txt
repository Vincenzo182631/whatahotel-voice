# Location& Flight Information

Location & Flight Information

Situated in the Indian Ocean, around 130 kilometers south of Mahé Island, Platte Island is part of the Seychelles Outer Islands. It is accessible exclusively by private plane, with a 25-minute flight from Seychelles International Airport to our private landing strip.
