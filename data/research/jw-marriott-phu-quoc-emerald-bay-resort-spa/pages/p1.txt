## Enrich your stay in our Vietnam luxury beach resort

Edging the sand and surf of Khem Beach, JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort and Spa is a luxury haven and an eco-conscious oasis certified by the prestigious Green Globe amid a stunning travel destination. What was once a 19th-century French university has been reimagined as an eclectically themed, lavish beachfront playground by renowned resort architect Bill Bensley. Set along a ribbon of beautiful...See More shoreline in Vietnam, our luxury resort welcomes you with rooms and suites with private balconies, along with beautifully appointed villas, complete with sparkling plunge pools. Ignite your senses with a global range of gourmet cuisine at any of our five sophisticated restaurants and bars. Lounge by the mosaic-tiled, seashell-shaped pool or on the immaculate white sands of our beach. Rejuvenate your well-being with locally inspired massage, beauty and luxury wellness therapies at Spa by JW. Resort Story

### Lamarck University – Where Mythic Becomes Legendary

As the story goes, between 1880 and 1940 Lamarck University was thriving with both the children of wealthy locals and the children of colonial residents. In the 1940s the University was closed, mothballed, and stood still in time. In a remarkable transformation, acclaimed architect Bill Bensley breathed new life into the site, reimagining it as a luxury beach resort in Phu Quoc and an opulent international...See More five-star establishment, the JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay. Classrooms were artfully converted into guest rooms, offices transformed into welcoming public spaces, and the old mushroom mycology wharf was reborn as the enchanting Chanterelle Spa.See Less

### Sustainability at the Heart of Jw Marriott Phu Quoc

JW Marriott Phu Quoc continually develops and enhances sustainable solutions in line with our "Doing Good in Every Direction" philosophy, such as the Pure Water Filtration System for high-quality drinking water and the eco-friendly technologies in the Sewage Treatment Plant System.
