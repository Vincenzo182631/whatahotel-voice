## Accommodations

Nestled along the pristine beachfront, our Phu Quoc villas offer the perfect vantage point with balconies or terraces that frame mesmerizing sea views. Unwind in premium villas featuring luxury amenities and distinct, contemporary design elements, creating an idyllic escape on Phu Quoc Island. ### Emerald Bay Room Ocean View

Sit along one of Vietnam’s most breathtaking and secluded beaches, the view of Ocean Emerald Bay is truly worth the visit. Telling the romantic backstory of a long-lost French university, each room is influenced by a fictional academic department, incorporated into the signature Indochine design. Featuring a king-size bed or two double beds, a private balcony overlooking the stunning Emerald Bay, a...See More luxurious marble bathroom, and colonial-inspired décor, the Ocean Emerald Bay is not just accommodation - it’s an undiscovered experience.See Less

### Deluxe Emerald Bay Room

Enjoy direct views of the hidden paradise along Vietnam’s southern coast from your spacious Deluxe Emerald Bay Room—whether from your plush bed or expansive private balcony. Bringing you even closer to the iconic Emerald Bay, this Phu Quoc accommodation features a generous layout and sweeping ocean views, with the waves at your doorstep. As Vietnam’s only conceptualized JW Marriott by Bill Bensley, the room transports you back in time to a 20th-century French campus, enriched with academic aesthetics, French-colonial style, and Hoi An’s local architecture.

## Spacious Private Balcony

No matter which Phu Quoc luxury villa you choose, a spacious private balcony awaits, ensuring a perfect setting to welcome any serene soul into the beauty of the surroundings. With garden, seaside, or seafront views, your balcony becomes a personal retreat—ideal for sipping your morning coffee or taking in the breathtaking sunrise over one of Vietnam’s most stunning beaches.

### Elevated Stay, Inspired Details

At JW Marriott Phu Quoc, every stay is designed to be exceptional. No matter which accommodation you choose, you’ll be welcomed with thoughtful touches, from a spacious private balcony with direct ocean views to a bathroom sanctuary bathed in natural tropical light.    Beyond your Phu Quoc villa, we make your getaway even more memorable with complimentary access to the spectacular Kiss of the Sea...See More show.See Less

### Complimentary Kiss of the Sea Tickets

Kiss of the Sea – a spectacular nighttime show of fire, water, lasers, light, 3D mapping and music. This must-see show, which is just within 10 minutes from the resort, is the perfect magical touch to your stay

### Thoughtfully Designed Bathroom

The resort's thoughtfully designed bathrooms feature expansive double-height windows, dual washbasins, spacious soaking tubs, and elegant marble tiles, creating a private retreat for guests.

### Spacious Balcony

All accommodations feature a spacious balcony with a sofa bed, offering scenic views of the garden and ocean.

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa is the Lamarck House with a maximum occupancy of 21.

Yes, all rooms at JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
