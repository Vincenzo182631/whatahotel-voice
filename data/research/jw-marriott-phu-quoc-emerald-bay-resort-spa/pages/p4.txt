### Family-Friendly Beach

Khem Beach boasts gentle waves that resemble a tranquil melody, and the crystal-clear sea reveals the small fish dwelling within. This family-friendly beach is perfect for activities such as seaside picnics, shell collecting, and beach strolls, offering a safe environment for young children to explore nature.

### Complimentary Water Activities

At JW Marriott Phu Quoc, we offer an extensive array of daily beach activities for our guests, akin to a university's engagement with its students. Explore complimentary activities at this Phu Quoc resort with pools such as kayaking, sunrise yoga, and more to uncover the joyful, adventurous, and relaxing moments that await you throughout your stay.

### Beachfront Pools

Dotting the coastline, you'll find our unique pools—Sand Pool, Shel Pool, and Sun Pool. These award-winning pools feature designs that blend with the coastal landscape, forming a visual masterpiece where the pool's edge meets the sandy beach. The opulent splendor of our Phu Quoc resort can be admired from your guestroom or while lounging under palm trees along the shore.

## Snorkelling by JW

Experience a snorkeling day with JW, immersing yourself in the sparkling waters and discovering the stunning coral reefs of Phu Quoc that lie beneath the ocean's surface. With each breath, feel a seamless connection to the endless rhythm of the turquoise sea. (This is a seasonal activities. Kindly check with our Concierge Team for consultancy.)

Tailored for children aged five to twelve, it encourages quality family time, leaving lasting memories. ### Kid's Club

At our Kids' Club, every day brings new adventures with craft activities and workshops, igniting your little ones' imagination. Culinary experiences await with hands-on activities, and our attentive nanny service ensures a carefree stay for parents. With direct access to the Kids' Pool, where water activities abound, your little ones will make cherished memories in a safe and playful environment.

### Lamarck University Tour

Embark on a captivating journey through time at JW Marriott Phu Quoc, a resort reborn from the historic Lamarck University. Through our university tour steeped in mysterious stories, discover the harmony of Eastern and Western architecture with vibrant colors, offering kids a colorful journey of exploration and an opportunity to expand their cultural knowledge.

### Explore Cultural Quintessence

We blend education and fun, offering activities such as candle-making and traditional conical hat painting at the Lantern Room, alongside an immersive Vietnamese cultural experience with a custom Viet coffee-making class at French & Co.

### Wellness

With a wellness activity for everyone, plus a luxury spa, you'll discover the space to concentrate on what matters most.

Discover Southern Phu Quoc

### Hon Thom Cable Car

Experience the world’s longest ocean-crossing cable car, offering stunning views of the An Thoi Archipelago and Hon Thom. When purchasing One-way Ticket at JW Marriott Phu Quoc, guests will enjoy unlimited rides to Hon Thom, access to Aquatopia Water Park and Sun World Hon Thom, complimentary entry to the Kiss Bridge. All available with only one-time purchase.

Learn More

### Kiss Bridge

Experience the breathtaking sunset views from the new and iconic Kiss Bridge, where the setting sun perfectly aligns between two overhanging bridges. Enjoy complimentary access throughout the day (tickets will be required from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM every day.)

### Kiss of the Sea Show

A mesmerizing multimedia show that offers a captivating evening entertainment experience. Show time starts at 9:00 PM daily (except Tuesday), and caps off with a 5-minute firework performance boasting over the sky of Sunset Town.

### Sunset Town

Inspired by Mediterranean architecture, Sunset Town is a feast for the eyes with a harmonizing color palette of brick red, charming hues of orange, and the turquoise blue of the sky and ocean. Take a walk along the town - as there are a lot of hidden spots awaiting your camera's snaps.

### Vuifest Bazaar

From 4:00 PM till late at night, explore an exciting cultural exchange with more than 40 kiosks of multi-cuisine street food, cocktail bars, and local handcrafted souvenirs, complemented by creative street musical performances.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Shell Pool (Outdoor)Sun Pool (Adult Only) (Outdoor)Sand Pool (Outdoor)Whirlpool

Does JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa have an on-site spa?

Yes, Chanterelle - Spa by JW is located on-site at JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa?

Kiss Bridge, Kiss of The Sea, AOI Theater (Vietnamese Puppetry), Phung Hung, Fairy Mountain Peak (Đỉnh Tiên Sơn) and more are nearby JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa.
