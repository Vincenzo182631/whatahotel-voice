# THE ULTIMATE CULINARY DESTINATION IN PHU QUOC

## Dining

Dining is an art, and our array of exquisite Phu Quoc beach front restaurants promises a world of culinary delights that will enchant your taste buds. Situated by the pristine Khem Beach in the breathtaking Emerald Bay, JW Marriott Phu Quoc Resort & Spa is a haven for gastronomic indulgence. Begin at French & Co. bakery, where you can enjoy the French-style delights at anytime in a serene afternoon,...See More before stepping into Red Rum to embrace the sizzling Mexican burgers and mouthwatering pizzas. As the journey unfolds, sitting at Department of Chemistry Bar to watch the mixology expertise crafting signature cocktails could also be memorable, especially when the afternoon glow is showing its beauty in the sky. The voyage continues with a grand feast at Tempus Fugit, allowing your taste buds to explore a world of flavors, before ending at Pink Pearl,our signature fine dining restaurant led by Michelin-starred Chef Olivier Elz, where elegance intertwines with flavors.See Less

### Family by JW

### Afternoon Tea at French & Co.

### Lab-Themed Bar

Our exceptional Department of Chemistry Bar would make you feel like you are sitting in a scientific laboratory, with glassware, eperimental décor, and a periodic table on the ceiling creating a unique ambiance. As you visit this special laboratory, choose a sea-facing view and watch the talented chemists craft delightful cocktails - each sip tells a story of expertise and flavor.
