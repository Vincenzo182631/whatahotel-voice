# Where Mythic Becomes Legendary

## Lamarck University

As the story goes, between 1880 and 1940 Lamarck University was thriving with both the children of wealthy locals and the children of colonial residents. In the 1940s the University was closed, mothballed, and stood still in time. In a remarkable transformation, acclaimed architect Bill Bensley breathed new life into the site, reimagining it as Phu Quoc's most opulent international five-star establishment,...See More the JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay. Classrooms were artfully converted into guest rooms, offices transformed into welcoming public spaces, and the old mushroom mycology wharf was reborn as the enchanting Chanterelle Spa.See Less

A 19th-Century University on Natural Studies

### In the Honor of French Naturalist Jean Baptiste Lamarck

Lamarck University derives its name from the illustrious French naturalist, Jean Baptiste Lamarck. Renowned for his contributions to biology and evolutionary theory, his enduring legacy inspires our commitment to academic excellence and the exploration of the natural world.

### Dean's Library

Home to a collection of more than 1,500 books, the Dean's Library is now remastered into the resort reception lobby, exuding a bright and modern atmosphere with high ceiling and natural lights, offering a contrast the antique books, globes, and artifacts. Notably, you'll encounter numerous presses used in publishing the works of esteemed professors, whose pictures and statues can be seen across the area.

Legacies Live On

## Ridgebacks Rule

One of the most successful parts of Lamarck University was the numerous sports teams. The mascot for these teams since 1917 was the Phu Quoc ridgeback, a rare breed of dog that was known for their intelligence, speed, and diligence. The student body would often be heard encouraging the teams with the cry of ‘Ridgebacks Rule’ during the matches. The most famous success of the Ridgebacks sports teams was their 1929 success in the Champion of Champions where they won the Asian Rugby Championship. The giant trophy for this event has stood proudly ever since and is now a water feature created by the University Dean ten years later in 1939.

The Heart of Lamarck University

### Rue De Lamarck

Strolling along Rue De Lamarck, you can encounter beautifully converted aged buildings housing Chanterelle Spa, gift shops, and dining venues. These charming, small, and historic structures once accommodated shopkeepers who lived above their storefronts. Today, they've transformed into suites offered at the resort. Around the gates, the roofs of the walkway will remind you of the Japanese Covered bridge in Hoi An, in fact, the whole street has the same feel as some of Hoi An’s famous walking streets, which makes sense considering both locations were being inhabited at the same time.

### Department of Mycology

One of our university's remarkable transformations is the building that now gracefully houses Spa Chanterelle. Legend has it that this very structure was once the Department of Mycology, where agriculture students honed their skills in cultivating a wide array of mushrooms for culinary delights, tea production, and, perhaps, a bit of 'experimental' exploration.

### The Study of Mushroom

We can still see numerous nods to the building's past with mushrooms portrayed in the design and in the artwork covering the ceilings and the walls, or the old botanist’s cabinet which was used to store various samples of dried mushrooms.

### Professor Champignon Rouge's Room

Professor Champignon Rouge, as the story goes, was a notable leading professor of the department of that time. He resided above his beloved mushrooms. He designed his quarters to remind him of his chateau in the Loire Valley back in France with heavy beams and angled ceilings.

### Fairytale-Inspired Design

Our Department of Mycology design takes cues from "Alice in Wonderland," in which a magic mushroom can make Alice change in size/ Small, winding hallways and an oval entrance, akin to a rabbit hole, invite guests into this captivating and imaginative space.

Where the Ridgebacks Ruled

### Lamarck's Running Track

Standing at almost the exact center of the property, you will see the University running track and football pitch. A piece of the original stadium seating has been thoughtfully preserved, hinting at the echoes of enthusiastic cheers and the spirited cries of "Ridgebacks Rule" that once filled the stands. Today, our guests can relive the spirit of the past by engaging in a friendly game of football, making it an ideal space for team-building activities.
