# Kids Club at Kempinski Palace Engelberg

### Where Little Guests Create Big Memories

At Kempinski Palace Engelberg, our youngest guests receive the same exceptional care and attention as their parents. Our Kids Club, proudly certified by the Luxury Childcare Association, offers a safe and imaginative environment designed to inspire joy, creativity, and friendship.

Our Kids Club is thoughtfully divided into playful corners to spark every child’s imagination:

**Kitchen Corner**: For budding little chefs.

**Shop Corner**: Where pretend play comes alive.

**Reading Corner**: A cozy space for stories and quiet time.

**Arts & Crafts Corner**: Unleash creativity with colors and textures.

Here, children can explore, learn, and play — without electronic devices. We are proud to offer an experience that nurtures creativity and social skills.

### Seasonal Highlights & Festive Fun

Our calendar is filled with magical activities that make every season special:

- Before Christmas: Enjoy storytelling by the fireplace, gingerbread cookie decorating, and Christmas tree decorating.
- Halloween: A festive celebration filled with costumes, crafts, and treats.
- Summer: Celebrate with Swiss National Day festivities and refreshing popsicles made with fresh fruit and juice.
- Friday & Saturday Movie Nights: Relax with beloved Disney classics in a cozy, child-friendly cinema setting

### Meals Made Easy

To make family stays even smoother, we are delighted to offer room service for children directly in the Kids Club between 12 noon to 1pm and 6 - 7pm. Little ones can dine together in a relaxed and supervised setting while parents enjoy their own moments of leisure.

### Meet Katarina – The Heart of Our Kids Club

At the heart of our Kids Club is Katarina, a passionate childcare professional dedicated to creating a warm, safe, and inspiring environment for every child. With her wealth of experience and genuine love for children, Katarina’s mission is to provide little guests with meaningful experiences that balance fun, learning, and creativity. Under her guidance, every child feels welcomed, valued, and encouraged to explore their imagination.
