# Winter Experiences

### Winter Sports in Engelberg

Engelberg stands for guaranteed snow, fast accessibility, new transport systems and alpine winter adventures.

### Mount Titlis 3,238m

Discover **the largest ski area in Central Switzerland**: the ski season on the Titlis starts as early as October and does not end until May. From Zurich, you can reach the skiing area with unlimited snow fun for the whole family in one hour.

#### Titlis Rotair, Titlis Cliff Walk & Glacier Cave

Take the Titlis Express to Stand. From here, the Rotair gondola will take you from Stand to Titlis. The Rotair is the **world’s first rotating aerial tramway**. A ride on the Titlis Rotair is included in the ski ticket. A free public ski bus takes you from the train station directly to the Titlis cable car. Awaiting you on the Titlis is the Titlis Cliff Walk – **Europe’s highest suspension bridge**, the Ice Flyer chairlift; and the Glacier Park.

#### Titlis Ski Area

The longest slope in the Titlis ski area stretches over **12 kilometres and 2,000 metres in altitude**. With 82 kilometres of pistes, the Engelberg-Titlis offers plenty of variety. Most of the pistes have guaranteed snow from December to April. At the top of the glacier, skiing is even possible from October to May. On the lower slopes around the Trübsee, Gerschnialp and Untertrübsee, easier runs tempt you.

#### Beginner Areas

**Untertrübsee ski lift - the ideal family winter fun**

The ski lift right next to the Untertrübsee Restaurant offers you, your family and friends lots of fun. The funicular takes you from the Engelberg Valley station up to the Gerschni. Directly at the exit of the Gerschni mountain station, walk 20 metres to the right. From here, you ski directly to the Untertrübsee ski lift (please follow the signs).

**Gerschnialp**

It's a little paradise at the foot of Mount Titlis: the Gerschnialp. The traditional funicular takes you to the small plateau at 1,300 metres, high above Engelberg. The conditions here are almost perfect for skiing beginners. The three ski lifts with easy slopes offer ideal conditions for families and beginners.

### Brunni 1,850m

Local attraction, Local Attraction, Local Area, Off-Site

The Brunni is a hiking and skiing area above Engelberg that offers countless leisure activities in summer and winter, especially for families. It is situated on a sunny south-facing slope and offers a

wonderful panoramic view.

The cable car, the three-seater chairlift and the Schonegg ski lift take you up to 2,040 metres above sea level. This side of the mountain is wonderfully suitable for families and children.

Local attraction, Local Attraction, Local Area, Off-Site

**Highlights of the Brunni lifts: Yeti Park**

Yeti Park is a children's adventure park located directly behind the Ristis mountain restaurant at the top station of the cable car. Two conveyor belts and a pony lift ensure easy and comfortable transport up the mountain. Yeti Park is 6,000 sq m in size and can be used independently.

**Night skiing – Klostermatte**

On the Klostermatte, you can also ski in the dark. Night skiing is free for season and annual pass holders. Please note that day and multi-day passes are not valid.

Local attraction, Local Attraction, Local Area, Off-Site

**Klostermatte / Globis Winterland**

Visit Globis Winterland and experience a unique winter landscape for children. The area is the ideal training ground for skiing, snowboarding or sledging. It is a flat skiing area, perfect for the first hours on skis or a snowboard. At the Brunni lifts, there is a restaurant, ski hire and a ski school.
