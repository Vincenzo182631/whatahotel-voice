# Location

### Where to find us

Kempinski Palace Engelberg is situated right next to the Kurpark, in the middle of the charming village centre with its small shops, cosy cafés and world-famous monastery. The aerial cableways to Central Switzerland’s largest outdoor paradise are just a few minutes away on foot. With a direct train connection from Zurich via Lucerne, Engelberg-Titlis is the fastest and most easily reached large mountain sports area in Switzerland.

#### **Arrival Information**

**By car**

From any direction, follow the A2 motorway and take the Stans-Süd exit, then continue along the main road to Engelberg. Please note that on Swiss motorways there is a toll to pay (CHF 40). You can park your car for CHF 30 per night in the hotel's underground car park (increasing to CHF 35 as of 01.01.2027) or for CHF 25 per night on the outdoor parking lots. There are 110 paid parking spaces, of which twenty lots are equipped with electrical charging stations and seven wheelchair-accessible parking spaces at your disposal, all directly accessible by lift.

**By train**

Travel from Zurich or Lucerne to Engelberg on the "Zentralbahn" railway. The journey from Zurich takes around 1 hour and 40 minutes and the train station is just 100 metres from the hotel.

**By plane**

There are a wide range of scheduled flights arriving at Zurich Airport (ZRH) from destinations around the world. The closest airport for private jets is Buochs (BXO), just 20 kilometres away. There is a heliport only 300 metres from Kempinski Palace Engelberg.
