# Activity Concierge

### The Insider

The Kempinski Activity Concierge knows them all: scenic hiking trails for the whole family, single trails for professionals (or those who want to become one), the most beautiful slopes and the most valuable **insider tips**. He will be happy to put a programme together for you and organise cable car tickets, a shuttle service and a hiking guide. Or he can show you the region himself on an e-bike: you are sure to feel particularly close to heaven in Engelberg.

"I am someone who loves to take on challenges and enjoys bringing great joy to our guests. One must love answering unusual requests and occasionally solving problems."

_Laszlo, Activity Concierge_
