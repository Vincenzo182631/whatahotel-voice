## FAQs for InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City

Does InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City have a pool?

InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City has 1 indoor pool.

Heated Indoor Swimming Pool

Located on the 43rd floor, the heated indoor swimming pool allows you to enjoy a swim high above the city. The pool faces the Yangtze River where it offers a stunning view on the urban skyline.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City for hotel guests.

The 3-storey parking area can provide exclusive parking spaces for the disabled, and can also provide charging piles for new energy vehicles.

In and out privileges

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City has 5 restaurants.

Is there free breakfast available at InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City?

No, InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City does not offer complimentary breakfast.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City?

Breakfast at InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City is available from 6:30 AM to 10:00 AM. On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City?

Check-in at InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City is conveniently located near Chongqing Jiangbei International Airport (CKG), approximately 18.7 miles away (30.1 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City?

InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Yangtze River Cableway-0.75 miles away (1.2 kilometers)
- Chaotianmen-0.75 miles away (1.2 kilometers)
- Hongya Cave-0.75 miles away (1.2 kilometers)
- Huguang Guild Hall-0.93 miles away (1.5 kilometers)
- Jiefangbei-1.06 miles away (1.7 kilometers)
- Three Gorges Museum-2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- Great Hall of The People-2.05 miles away (3.3 kilometers)
- Liziba Light Rail Station-9.13 miles away (14.7 kilometers)
- Ciqikou Ancient Town-10.07 miles away (16.2 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
