## Botanica · Houdai Restaurant

The interactive restaurant Botanica lifts Chongqing's famous food fragrances from its streets to this 251 meter high forest. Local graffiti, food trucks, rustic wooden furniture, and green trees scattered in the restaurant bring the scenes of the alleys and streets of Chongqing's daily life to hotel guests. Botanica offers guests with an innovative Houdai concept, all you can order with a wide range of international and local options, over 100 dishes on the menu, encouraging a picnic feeling.
