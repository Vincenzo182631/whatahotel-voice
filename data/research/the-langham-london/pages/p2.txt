## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What makes The Langham, London a "Grand Hotel"?

The Langham, London opened in 1865 as Europe’s first purpose-built Grand Hotel. The Langham defined a new era of luxury, featuring then-innovative amenities like electric lights and hydraulic lifts. Today, it maintains that legacy through timeless elegance and world-class service.

Where is The Langham, London located?

The hotel is situated in the heart of the West End at the top of Regent Street, just a short walk from Oxford Circus, Marylebone, and Mayfair.

What time is check-in at The Langham, London?

Standard check-in begins at 15:00 (3:00pm). Early check-in may be requested and is subject to room availability upon your arrival.

What time is check-out at The Langham, London?

Guests are kindly asked to check out by 12:00pm.

Can I store my luggage if I arrive at The Langham, London early?

Yes, if your room is not yet ready, our 24-hour concierge and porter team are available to assist with luggage storage until you can check in.

What is the difference between a Classic Room and a Superior Room at The Langham, London?

The Classic Room is our entry-level option, ideal for guests on shorter stays. The Superior Room offers a more spacious experience with large picture windows that provide ample natural daylight and views of either the city or our gorgeous courtyard.

What are the benefits of Langham Club access?

Guests with Langham Club access enjoy an exclusive sanctuary offering complimentary breakfast, afternoon tea, evening cocktails, and canapés, alongside personalised concierge services and private check-in.

What privileges are included with a Club Executive Room at The Langham, London?

Guests staying in a Club Executive Room enjoy marvellous cityscapes and full Langham Club privileges. These include complimentary breakfast, free-flow Champagne, and access to a serene retreat with personalised service.

What makes the Club One Bedroom Suite The Langham, London’s most sought-after suite?

The Club One Bedroom Suite is highly desired for its spacious setting, featuring an elegant bedroom, a homely lounge area, and stunning views of the city.

Does The Langham, London offer specific rooms for families?

Yes, our Family Rooms are designed for an unforgettable experience, featuring two Queen beds and a sofa bed. These rooms can accommodate up to two adults and three children and include amenities such as kids' programmes and a children’s Tepee upon request.

Are child-minding services available at The Langham, London?

Yes, babysitting and childcare services can be arranged through our guest services team.

Is The Langham, London a pet-friendly hotel?

Yes, we welcome the whole family, including "paws". Dogs weighing 12kg or under are invited to stay for a fee of £100 per stay. We ask that they are kept on a lead in public areas and not left unattended in rooms.

Is there a swimming pool and spa at The Langham, London hotel?

Yes, guests have access to Chuan Body + Soul spa and wellness sanctuary. It features a 16-metre indoor swimming pool, a fully equipped fitness studio, and a range of holistic signature spa treatments designed to refresh your soul.

Are there suites available at The Langham, London that are suitable for long-term stays?

The Club Residence Suite is perfect for long, luxurious stays. This "hotel apartment" style suite is fitted with a fully equipped kitchen, a separate bedroom, and a living room.

Which suite at The Langham, London offers the most space and luxury?

The Sterling Suite is our penthouse, offering the grandest of entrances, a private media lounge, and up to 450m² of space for up to 12 guests. Alternatively, the Infinity Suite features a unique semi-circular sitting room and an irresistible infinity bath.

What dining options are available within The Langham, London hotel?

The hotel boasts several iconic dining venues, including Palm Court for afternoon tea, the Artesian cocktail bar, The Wigmore British tavern, and contemporary European cuisine at Sale e Pepe Mare.
