## STRAWBERRY SEASON AFTERNOON TEA

Celebrate the flavours of British summer with **The Strawberry Season Afternoon Tea** at Palm Court, available from 22 June to 2 August. Featuring vine-ripened strawberries from Kent-based growers J Myatt and Co Ltd, this limited-edition afternoon tea celebrates one of the season’s most iconic fruits. Enjoy seasonal pastries, homemade strawberry ice cream and sorbet served from a bespoke strawberry cart, and a choice of summer-inspired aperitifs.
