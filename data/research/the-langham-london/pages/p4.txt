# INSIDE THE LANGHAM: BRITAIN'S FIRST GRAND HOTEL

Step behind the scenes of The Langham, London in _Inside The Langham: Britain's First Grand Hotel_ – a landmark six-part documentary series offering unprecedented access to Europe's first grand hotel. Filmed over ten months, the series follows the extraordinary people whose expertise, craftsmanship and dedication bring more than 160 years of hospitality to life, while revealing how The Langham continues to evolve for a new generation of luxury travellers.

From world-class dining, iconic Afternoon Tea and award-winning bars to historic events, major restoration projects and the meticulous service that defines every guest experience, _Inside The Langham_ showcases the skill, passion and teamwork behind one of London's most celebrated luxury hotels.

Premiering on ITV1 and streaming on ITVX, the series is an intimate portrait of the heritage, innovation and people who continue to shape the legacy of Britain's first grand hotel.

### OUR HISTORY

In 1865, The Langham, London first opened its doors. Recognised as Europe’s first ‘Grand Hotel’, it sought to revolutionise the luxury hotel experience.

Over 160 years later, many things have changed, but the hotel remains one of the world’s most iconic hotels — famed for its legendary hospitality. Along with an innovative spirit and playful, pink heritage, The Langham, London continues to captivate.

Take a walk with us, and discover our history.

EPISODE 3: SPICING UP THE LANGHAM

_Premiering on ITV on 4 August at 7:30pm._

Competing with over 100 central-London luxury hotels, The Langham is under pressure to stand out by continuing to upgrade its facilities and deliver unrivalled service. Bob van den Oord, CEO Langham Hospitality Group, visits London from Hong Kong to inspect Abie’s new terrace project. Will the newly upgraded Portland Suite meet his expectations, particularly with his unmatched eye for detail?

Elsewhere, Italian duo Giulia, Head Bartender, and Lorenza, Bar Manager, run award-winning bar Artesian. Guilia is launching a new cocktail menu, experimenting with exciting, new flavours in her laboratory. Executive Chef, Berwyn, is challenged to come up with a complimentary menu, collaborating with Bibi, a high end Indian restaurant, testing if the complex, culinary dishes can be successfully recreated in The Langham’s industrial kitchen.

EPISODE 4: LANGHAM AROUND THE WORLD

_Premiering on ITV on 11 August at 7:30pm._

Marketing Director Alice and Managing Director Stefan visit Venice, the world’s “Floating City,” to recce the site of a new opening set for 2027. The project is a large-scale restoration of an old murano glass factory. The Langham is keen to continue to grow its global presence and the aim is to ensure this new location maintains the luxury standards synonymous with the brand, while reflecting the local history of the site. During their visit, they attend a master glass maker workshop to purchase traditional glass chandeliers, as a homage to the hotel's past.

Back in London, Michel Roux is bringing Michelin star food to The Langham.

EPISODE 5: THE BEST OF BRITISH

_Premiering on ITV on 18 August at 7:30pm._

In this episode, we will take a closer look at the international employees who make up the fabric of The Langham and have made London their home. Brazilian-born receptionist Rodrigo moved to London 25 years ago. A keen dancer, he is excitedly preparing for a certain Strictly Come Dancing stars visit. Lithuanian Spa Manager Ruta is looking to stock the spa with locally made products. In her search for the perfect brand, she meets Lord Newborough. With his entrepreneurial spirit, he delivers an important pitch to have his range, made from natural ingredients foraged from his own farm, stocked at The Langham.  Finally, we meet Ellie ‘The Flower Fairy,’ who works through the night to ensure The Langham is dressed beautifully with seasonal bouquets.
