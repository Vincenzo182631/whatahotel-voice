## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What are the best luxury suites at The Langham, London?

The Sterling Suite is the premier choice, recognised as one of London's largest and most luxurious suites. It offers a residential feel with multiple bedrooms, a private lift, and 24-hour butler service.

How far is The Langham, London from Oxford Street?

The hotel is located just 0.3 miles (approximately a 5-minute walk) from the heart of Oxford Street and the Oxford Circus station. Visit our Location Page for detailed directions.

When did The Langham, London first open?

The Langham, London officially opened on June 10, 1865, earning its reputation as Europe's first purpose-built "Grand Hotel."

Is The Langham, London in Mayfair or Marylebone?

The Langham, London is uniquely positioned at the junction of Marylebone, Mayfair, and Fitzrovia. This "triple-border" location allows guests to enjoy the independent boutiques of Marylebone Village and the high-end luxury of Mayfair within a 5-minute walk.

What is the closest London Underground station to the hotel?

Oxford Circus Station is the closest tube station, located just 0.3 miles (a 5-minute walk) south of the hotel. It provides direct access to the Bakerloo, Central, and Victoria lines, connecting guests to the City and Canary Wharf in under 20 minutes.

Does the hotel offer an executive lounge?

Yes, The Langham Club provides guests with a private sanctuary featuring complimentary breakfast, afternoon tea, evening cocktails, and dedicated concierge services.

Are there any 6-bedroom hotel suites in London?

Yes, The Langham, London offers one of the city’s most expansive and flexible luxury accommodations: The Sterling Suite. While many luxury hotels cap their suites at three or four bedrooms, The Sterling Suite is a residential-style penthouse that can be specifically configured as a 6-bedroom suite, spanning 450 square meters (4,844 square feet).

What are the best hidden gems to visit near The Langham, London?

Beyond the major sights, guests can explore The Wallace Collection (a free national museum in a historic townhouse), Handel & Hendrix in London (the preserved homes of the famous composers), and the unique floral scents at Floris London on Jermyn Street.

Can I book a private tour of London from The Langham hotel?

Yes, our concierge can arrange bespoke experiences such as the Small Car Big City vintage Mini Cooper tours, which pick guests up directly from Portland Place for a curated journey through London’s hidden alleyways.

What are the best casual dining spots near the hotel?

For a more relaxed atmosphere, The Wigmore (our modern British tavern) offers upscale pub classics, while the nearby Marylebone Village is home to local favourites like La Fromagerie and numerous chic cafes along Marylebone High Street.

Are there Michelin-star dining options near the hotel?

Yes, guests can visit Ikoyi (modern West African), Corrigan’s Mayfair (modern British), or Sketch Lecture Room and Library (French), all located within a short distance of the hotel and highly acclaimed for their culinary excellence. You can also explore our on-site options like Sale Pepe e Mare, The Wigmore or Artesian.
