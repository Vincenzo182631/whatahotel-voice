## FAQs for InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun

Does InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun have a pool?

InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun has 1 indoor pool.

Wellness Oasis

Wellness Oasis is managed by the third party. Covering over 300 square meters, the swimming pool is 1.3 meters deep. It is not only can be used for swimming, but also provide rehabilitation training for members.

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun has 3 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 10:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun?

Check-in at InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun is conveniently located near Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK), approximately 14.66 miles away (23.6 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun?

InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- The Forbidden City - 3.85 miles away (6.2 kilometers)
- Liangma River International Style Waterfront - 1.12 miles away (1.8 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
