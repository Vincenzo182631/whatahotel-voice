# InterContinental Beijing SanlitunDestination

InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun is located in the most stylish entertainment district in Beijing, with easy access to famous leisure and scenic spots. If you’re visiting the city on business, we’re adjacent to international embassies and the CBD area.

## Things to do

### The Forbidden City

景山前街4号, 东城区, 北京市, 北京市

The Forbidden City in Beijing is the imperial palace of the Ming and Qing dynasties in China. Formerly known as the Forbidden City, it is located at the center of the central axis of Beijing and is the essence of ancient Chinese palace architecture. The center of the outer court is the hall of supreme harmony, the hall of Zhong He and the hall of Bao He.
