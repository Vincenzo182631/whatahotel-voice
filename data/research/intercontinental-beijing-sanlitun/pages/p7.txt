Disappointing Experience – Below Expectations

Unfortunately, our stay at the InterContinental Beijing Sanlitun fell well short of our expectations for a five-star hotel.

During our stay, we experienced inconsistent housekeeping, occasions when the Club Lounge had no food available during advertised service hours, and several garments that appeared to have shrunk after being sent for dry cleaning.

Although management acknowledged our concerns and provided a room upgrade, the overall experience left us extremely disappointed.

As a frequent business traveller, I expected a considerably higher standard of service from the InterContinental brand.

Based on my personal experience, I would not return to this property or recommend it to colleagues travelling to Beijing.
