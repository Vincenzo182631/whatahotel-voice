Within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia’s groundbreaking wellness destination, Rosewood AMAALA offers a riviera of renewal. Guided by the regenerative rhythms of the Red Sea and infused with Arabian ritual and artistry, its sunlit coastal lifestyle reveals a new vision of wellbeing, one that restores and nurtures vitality over time.

Shaped over millennia, the Red Sea’s vibrant ecosystem serves as both mentor and muse. Guests seamlessly transition between invigorating swim rituals in circulation pools, via ferrata in the Hijazi Mountains, al fresco dining along the reef, and yachting at dusk. At Asaya, sea-inspired therapies harness the rare composition of the water, complemented by bespoke restorative formulations crafted in the Asaya Apothecary, offering a wellness experience unlike any other.

A Reef of Repose and Renewal

Complemented by thriving coral gardens and clear waters, the coastline invites both adventure and stillness.

* * *

WELLNESS

Wellbeing woven into every moment

Nature-inspired rituals to restore harmony and vitality

- Cascading pools that mirror the ebb and flow of the Red Sea, calming the breath and invigorating circulation
- The Arabian Apothecary at Asaya, where guests create bespoke healing blends from regional botanical oils and herbs, rooted in ancient wisdom
- Guided breathwork and soundbath experiences, curated to steady the nervous system and restore inner harmony

- Indoor and outdoor exercises set against a stunning backdrop of desert dunes and crystalline waters
- Seasonal ingredients and conscious cuisine at Asaya Lounge, celebrating nourishment, sustainability, and the art of mindful dining
- A daily Sukūn Hour, inviting a shared moment of stillness for reflection and renewal

* * *

DINING

Culinary journeys shaped by the Red Sea’s bounty

Saudi heritage expressed through visionary seasonal cuisine

- Progressive Middle Eastern cuisine at Saha, served within intimate pavilion-style settings
- Mediterranean cuisine at Olea, celebrating seasonal produce, olive oil, and vibrant regional flavours
- Live-fire cooking at The Grill Bar & Restaurant, informed by wood and charcoal

- Relaxed coastal dining at Puro Cove Club, with ceviches and crudos served alongside the rhythm of the sea
- Conscious cuisine and botanical beverages at Asaya Lounge
- Private and residential-style dining inside your villa or at curated gathering spaces

LOCATION

A pioneering desert destination embraced by the Red Sea

Resting between the desert terrain and open water, Rosewood AMAALA can be reached by a two-hour drive from Red Sea International Airport, with transfers arranged in private Red Sea electric vehicles, or via the marina by boat. Offering both remoteness and connectivity, the location offers a veritable bounty of nature, culture, and exploration at every turn.
