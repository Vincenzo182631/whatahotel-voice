## GOLF TOURNAMENTS

# Golf Tournaments at   Casa de Campo

## Watch the Greats Take On The Casa de Campo Courses

For those that actively engage in golf, there is nothing like the energy, excitement, and enthusiasm of a golf tournament, especially at Casa de Campo. At Casa de Campo it is much more than a resort that offers golf, it’s a world premiere golfing resort with some of the very best courses and tournaments available for this exciting sport. This page will provide you with all the details for our upcoming events happening at our legendary golf courses: Teeth of the Dog®, Dye Fore, and The Links.

#### Casa de Campo has proudly hosted the 2016, 2019, and 2022 LAAC Golf Tournament

Fourball Américas Casa de Campo – May 31 – June 6, 2027

**May 31 – June 6, 2027**

Ready to compete alongside your golfing partner? The Fourball Américas is the largest two-player team tournament in the region. The 2027 edition will mark its 6th anniversary.

Enjoy four rounds of Four-Ball competition on two of the Caribbean’s most iconic courses, both designed by Pete Dye: two rounds on the newly renovated Teeth of the Dog (#1 in the Caribbean, #27 in the world, featuring seven spectacular oceanfront holes) and two rounds on the challenging Dye Fore, renowned for its breathtaking views of the Chavón River. The experience includes six nights of accommodation, all meals under a Full Board plan, one golf cart per room throughout the stay, airport and course transfers, a welcome cocktail, awards dinner, and a Welcome Kit. Everything is taken care of.
