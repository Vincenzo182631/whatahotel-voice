Gourmet Catering Services

## Beyond the Table

Wherever your day unfolds – from the privacy of your villa to the open sea or sky – Casa de Campo brings fine dining to you with chef-crafted meals, boxed lunches, and bespoke catering services.

Email Catering Department

Call Catering Department

### Chef inVilla

Our chefs will come to you in your own private villa and cook you dinner in the comfort of your own home. Please note, this service must be requested 24 hours in advance.

### Private Aircraft & Yacht Catering

There’s no need to dine on the same boring airplane food, or boring boat fare when you stay at Casa de Campo. Our chefs can create a wide range of cuisine to take with you on board your flight or stay at sea

### Box Lunch

The perfect solution for day trips, business outings, concerts, and more, our boxed lunches offer Casa de Campo’s delicious gourmet meals on the go. Choose from a wide variety of menus expertly crafted by our talented culinary team.
