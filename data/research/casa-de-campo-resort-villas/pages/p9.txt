Whether you’re hosting an executive strategy session, annual meeting, or multi-day retreat, Casa de Campo offers a setting that inspires clarity, creativity, and connection. Spread across 7,000 private acres, the resort provides a rare balance of world-class meeting venues, open-air gathering spaces, and seamless hospitality. From beachfront receptions to state-of-the-art conference rooms, your attendees will find it effortless to stay engaged—and even easier to unwind between sessions.

For large-scale conferences, Casa de Campo delivers an unmatched combination of capacity, convenience, and Caribbean beauty. Planners can choose from flexible indoor and outdoor venues, professional A/V capabilities, and a resort infrastructure designed to keep thousands of guests moving comfortably. Between programming, attendees can enjoy championship golf, white-sand beaches, award-winning dining, and an energetic resort atmosphere that transforms your conference into a can’t-miss experience.

From milestone birthdays and anniversaries to family reunions and friends’ getaways, Casa de Campo turns every celebration into something unforgettable. The resort’s wide variety of accommodations—sprawling villas, private pools, beachfront suites—makes it easy for groups of all sizes to stay together. Pair that with endless activities, from sunset cruises to spa days to world-class dining, and you have the perfect place to relax, reconnect, and make memories that will last a lifetime.

For incentive travel, few destinations deliver impact like Casa de Campo. With its exclusive villas, private beaches, championship golf, deep-sea fishing, horseback riding, and extraordinary culinary scene, the resort offers experiences that feel both personalized and aspirational. Reward top performers with a setting that feels truly earned—one that energizes, inspires, and sends them home with a renewed sense of appreciation and motivation.

## Sample Itineraries

Discover how your group can make the most of every moment at Casa de Campo with these curated three-day itineraries, thoughtfully crafted to inspire connection, productivity, and unforgettable experiences.

### Three Perfect Days of Inspiration at Casa de Campo

Experience a seamless blend of productivity, relaxation, and world-class hospitality with this curated three-day group itinerary at Casa de Campo. Designed to spark connection and elevate your retreat, it balances purposeful meetings with unforgettable moments—sunset cocktails, oceanfront wellness, championship golf, and the resort’s most iconic settings. Use this sample schedule as a starting point to tailor the perfect stay for your team or group.

Day One

**08:00 AM** — Arrival, welcome drinks at the lobby.

**02:00 PM**— Team-building golf clinic on the Teeth of the Dog course (for novices & seasoned alike).

**06:30 PM** — Sunset welcome cocktail at Minitas Beach Club

Day Two

**07:00 AM** — Optional early morning group yoga/stretch at the Spa.

**08:00–10:00 AM** — Private breakfast buffet & plenary meeting: “Vision & Mission Refresh”.

**10:15 AM** — Breakout sessions: small-group workshops on innovation, collaboration, and digital marketing.

**12:30 PM** — Lunch at leisure.

**02:00 PM** — Free time: guests may choose beach time, spa treatment, or a round on the Dye Fore course.

**05:00 PM**— Group challenge: resort scavenger hunt (Marina → Altos de Chavón → Minitas Beach Club).

**07:30 PM** — Dinner at the resort’s fine‐dining venue La Piazzetta.

**09:30 PM** — Optional: cigar lounge or marina bar wind-down.

Day Three

**08:00 AM**— Breakfast at Lago Restaurant.

**9:00 AM**— Leisure time: oceanfront swim, paddle-boarding, or shopping in the resort boutiques.

**11:00 AM** — Check-out (or optional stay-extension).

**12:30 PM** — Farewell lunch overlooking the course.

**02:00 PM** — Departures.

### Three Days of Strategy, Leadership & Connection

Designed for senior leadership teams, this three-day itinerary blends strategic working sessions with restorative experiences across Casa de Campo’s most iconic settings. From high-impact executive forums to sunset receptions and wellness-focused moments, each day is crafted to strengthen alignment, spark innovation, and create space for meaningful connection.

Day One

**08:00 AM** — Arrival, welcome cocktail in the lobby with live instrumental music.

**11:00 AM** — Private executive session in Catalina Room: “Disruptive Trends in AI and Digital Transformation.”

**01:00 PM**— Networking lunch at La Cana Restaurant & Lounge.

**03:30 PM** — Interactive panel: “Post-Pandemic Growth Strategies” with guest facilitator.

**06:30 PM** — Sunset cocktail on the executive terrace at Dye Fore Pavilion.

Day Two

**07:00 AM** — Optional morning yoga for executives (focus on mindful breathing).

**08:00–10:00 AM** — Private breakfast + strategic session: Roadmapping.

**10:15 AM** — Departmental workshops: innovation, tech leadership, product vision.

**12:30 PM** — Lunch at leisure.

**02:00 PM** — Free time: golf at Teeth of the Dog, beach, or spa treatments.

**06:00 PM** — Formal dinner with CEO address at La Piazzetta.

**09:00 PM**— Private lounge at La Caña Bar: networking & unwind.

Day Three

**08:00 AM** — Executive breakfast at Lago Restaurant.

**09:30 AM** — Reflective group walk through Altos de Chavón.

**10:30 AM** — Closing session: key agreements & action plan.

**12:00 PM** — Farewell lunch at Minitas Beach Club.

**02:00 PM** — Departures.

### A Curated Retreat for Connection, Focus & Renewal

Crafted for deep rest and renewal, this wellness program surrounds your group with tranquility from the moment they arrive. Daily movement sessions, immersive spa therapies, mindful rituals, and nourishing cuisine create a calming retreat in the heart of the Caribbean.

Day One

**09:30 AM** — Personalized check-in + aromatherapy welcome at Flamboyan Conference Center.

**11:00 AM** — Full hydrotherapy experience: sauna, steam, vitality pool, Kneipp path, and thermal lounge.

**01:30 PM** — Healthy lunch at The Spa Café.

**03:00 PM** — Spa treatments at leisure, full-body exfoliation, massage, facial, and scalp/hair treatment.

**05:30 PM** — Guided sunset meditation and stretching at Spa’s private outdoor space.

**07:30 PM** — Group dinner at La Caña Restaurant.

Day Two

**08:00 AM** — Outdoor Pilates + breath awareness session at Spa.

**09:30 AM** — Breakfast at leisure

**11:30 AM** — Spa treatments at leisure, full-body exfoliation, massage, facial, and scalp/hair treatment.

**02:00 PM** — Lunch and free time for beach, spa, or pool.

**07:00 PM** — Group dinner at Minitas Beach Club + closing reflection circle.

Day Three

**08:30 AM** — Breakfast at leisure

**09:30 AM** — Restorative yoga class followed by mini facial: “The Extreme Facial by EviDenS de Beauté” (80 min).

**02:30 PM** — Departures.

Our Philosophy

A place to come together in the Dominican Republic. The carefree spirit of Casa de Campo has a certain magic when it comes to satisfying groups. It is as though everyone shares the same thoughts and ideas – new friendships are made, teamwork is strengthened, bonds are forged  over memories that will last a lifetime.

The Best Meeting Resort in the Dominican Republic

Our meeting facilities include two-state-of-the art conference centers, a ballroom, outdoor courtyard space, flawless beaches, lush gardens, restaurant and club-style venues, villa homes, plazas and a 6,000-seat amphitheater. Legendary personalized service and attention to detail combine to set Casa de Campo apart from all other Caribbean business resorts and the rest of the world.
