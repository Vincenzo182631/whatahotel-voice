## Frequently Asked Questions

What is Chablé Yucatán and where is it located?

Chablé Yucatán is a luxury wellness multi-awarded resort located in the Mayan jungle of Chocholá, near Mérida,

Mexico. It combines a restored historic hacienda with private villas and nature-based experiences rooted in Mayan

culture.

How far is Chablé Yucatán from Mérida and the airport?

The resort is about 40 km (25 miles) from Mérida and approximately a 45–50 minute drive from Mérida International

Airport (MID).

What makes Chablé Yucatán unique among resorts in Mexico?

Chablé Yucatán offers a distinctive blend of privacy, wellness, and authentic cultural immersion, featuring a spa built

around a natural cenote and architecture that integrates seamlessly with the jungle environment.

Is Chablé Yucatán a wellness-focused resort?

Yes. The resort is internationally recognized for its wellness programs, including personalized treatments, yoga,

meditation, and traditional Mayan healing practices.

What is the cenote spa experience at Chablé Yucatán?

The spa is centered around a natural cenote, a sacred freshwater sinkhole in Mayan culture, offering a unique

environment for relaxation and holistic treatments.

What types of accommodations are available at Chablé Yucatán?

The resort offers private casitas (200 sq m/2,200 sq ft) and villas designed with spacious layouts, natural materials,

and strong indoor-outdoor integration.

Do the villas at Chablé Yucatán include private pools?

Yes, all villas feature private pools, terraces, and secluded spaces designed for comfort and privacy.

What dining options are available at Chablé Yucatán?

The resort offers multiple dining venues focused on contemporary Mexican cuisine, using local and seasonal

ingredients

Is the culinary experience aligned with wellness?

Yes. The gastronomy is designed to complement the wellness concept, focusing on fresh, balanced, and locally

sourced ingredients.

What activities can guests enjoy at Chablé Yucatán?

Guests can enjoy yoga, spa treatments, cycling, nature walks, sustainable and cultural experiences, as well as

excursions to cenotes and nearby Mayan archaeological sites.

Is Chablé Yucatán suitable for couples or honeymoons?

Yes, the resort is ideal for couples seeking privacy, relaxation, and immersive experiences in a natural setting.

Is the resort suitable for families?

While families are welcome, Chablé Yucatán features a state-of-the-art Kids Club; the resort is particularly suited for

travelers looking for tranquility and wellness-focused stays

Does Chablé Yucatán offer transportation services?

Yes, private airport transfers can be arranged for a seamless travel experience.

Is Chablé Yucatán an all-inclusive resort?

No, it offers a customizable stay where guests can select dining, wellness, and activity experiences individually.

When is the best time to visit Chablé Yucatán?

The best time to visit is from November to April, when the weather is dry and ideal for outdoor activities.

What attractions are near Chablé Yucatán?

Nearby attractions include Mérida, Uxmal, cenotes, and other archaeological sites that showcase Mayan history and

culture

Is Chablé Yucatán located in a jungle environment?

Yes, the resort is surrounded by the Mayan jungle, offering a secluded and nature-driven atmosphere.
