# LUMINOUS SPACES,   HARMONIOUS WITH NATURE

At Chablé Yucatán, each accommodation encourages indoor-outdoor living, which nurtures your awareness of being fully present in the here and now.

The Villas’ spacious layouts include high ceilings and large windows that invite the interplay of light. Casitas are equally luxurious, with king-size beds, comfortable seating areas, indoor and outdoor showers and oversized bathrooms.

All accommodations include enchanting outdoor spaces, where sky, greenery and design converge within the private terrace and pool.

Inside, you will find an artful blend of traditional Mexican architecture and modern design, featuring natural stone floors, locally crafted textiles, and hand-carved wooden furnishing. Intricate tilework and carefully selected artwork add layers of intrigue.

Thoughtfulness, design, quality and fluidity abound in the accommodations here – aspects that undoubtedly played a part in Chablé Yucatán’s Villas and Casitas having been a recipient of the Prix Versailles, The World Architecture Design Award.

## The Residences

### A PRIVILEGE THAT LAST FOREVER

From the moment its doors open, The Residences evokes the history and culture of Mexico.

Its central courtyard, typical of Spanish house, incorporates characteristics of Maan homes, such as the palm roof and Tzalam wood. The marble floors, so iconic of Chablé Yucatán, add a touch og sophistication and a sense of freshness.
