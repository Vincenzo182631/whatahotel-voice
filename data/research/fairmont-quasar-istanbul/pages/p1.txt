## _welcome_ to Fairmont Quasar Istanbul

Fairmont Quasar Istanbul stands tall in the city center – over the slopes of the Bosphorus in the Mecidiyeköy business district, within easy reach of Istanbul’s famous attractions. Whether you’re in Turkey’s largest city for a leisure break or business trip, make our 5-star hotel your unparalleled home away from home.

Set against the modern city skyline, our leisure and business hotel in Istanbul was created with discerning travelers in mind. In addition to our Fairmont Spa, Fitness Center, and swimming pools, we offer impressive event venues and a stately ballroom for luxury society weddings in Istanbul.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Quasar Istanbul located?

Fairmont Quasar Istanbul is located in the Mecidiyeköy business district, one of the most central locations in the city, offering close access to both European and Asian sides of the metropolis. Our address is Mecidiyeköy, Büyükdere Cd. No:76, 34393.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Quasar Istanbul?

The check-in time at Fairmont Quasar Istanbul is 2 pm, while the check-out time is 12:00.

Is Fairmont Quasar Istanbul pet-friendly?

Fairmont Quasar Istanbul is a pet-friendly hotel and has been greeting its visitors along with their Canine Ambassador named Gofret. Please note that restrictions may apply to your furry friends.

Is parking available at Fairmont Quasar Istanbul?

Yes, there are parking facilities in Fairmont Quasar Istanbul. Guests may prefer to use the facilities on their own or choose to take advantage of the valet services.

What is the cancellation policy at Fairmont Quasar Istanbul?

The cancellation policy at Fairmont Quasar Istanbul can differ depending on your reservation, therefore it is best to contact the reservations department.

Does Fairmont Quasar Istanbul have a swimming pool?

Fairmont Quasar Istanbul has two swimming pools; a heated (28.5°C) indoor pool located at Fairmont Spa and a seasonal infinity pool (only open during summer months).
