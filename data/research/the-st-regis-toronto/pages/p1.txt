# Welcome to The St. Regis Toronto

## The Finest Luxury Downtown Toronto Hotel Experience

With exceptional dining, an on‑site spa, and downtown’s most coveted address, The St. Regis Toronto continues to redefine 5‑star hotel luxury in the heart of downtown Toronto. Trendsetting and boundary‑breaking, this 5‑star hotel marks the brand’s distinguished Canadian debut at an enviable downtown location. Rising above Canada’s international centre for business, finance, and culture, our luxury...See More hotel, ideally positioned in downtown Toronto near the Toronto International Film Festival, offers an unparalleled setting just moments from the city’s most iconic landmarks, including the CN Tower, Scotiabank Arena, Rogers Centre, and Toronto’s finest shopping, theatre, and entertainment. Experience modern elegance and bespoke Butler Service in our 258 guest rooms and suites, among the most spacious in the city. We also offer pet‑friendly accommodations, ensuring every member of the family may enjoy an exquisite stay.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Toronto?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Toronto is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Toronto allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Toronto. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Toronto?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Toronto. Self parking is available for $70.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $70.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Toronto have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Toronto has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Toronto booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Toronto?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Toronto is Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport (YTZ). YTZ is located approximately 3.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Toronto have an airport shuttle to Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport (YTZ)?

No, The St. Regis Toronto does not have an airport shuttle to Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport.

Does The St. Regis Toronto have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Toronto has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
