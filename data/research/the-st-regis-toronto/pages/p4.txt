# Unwind at our Spa in Downtown Toronto

## Discover wellness at our luxury spa in downtown Toronto

Unwind at The St. Regis Spa Toronto, our 31st-floor sanctuary in the sky where every moment is crafted to dazzle the senses and celebrate the mind, body, and soul. Away from the hectic life below, the 15,000-square-foot spa hotel near downtown Toronto is an urban oasis that invites guests to renew and recharge with ultra-luxurious amenities, a fitness centre, and an infinity pool overlooking the glittering city skyline.

### Bespoke Wellness

The St. Regis Spa Toronto offers a bespoke approach to wellness and rejuvenation, treating you to an urban oasis unlike any other. Framed by spectacular city views, a day at our luxury spa in downtown Toronto begins with curated, attentive services from our experts, who greet you and anticipate your every desire.

Discover More

### Spa Menu

Uncover our highly curated spa menu, which dazzles the senses and celebrates your mind, body, and spirit through massages, facials, body treatments, and more. Relax even further at our infinity-edge lap pool and whirlpool on the hotel's 32nd floor, or in our detoxifying dry sauna and steam room.

Discover More

### Enhance Your Experience

End your day in the serene atmosphere of our spa lounge. Located within our luxury spa in downtown Toronto, the lounge offers complimentary water, tea and healthy refreshments. Enhance your experience with a wellness bento box post-treatment.

Inspired by the Gilded Age of New York High Society, this face and body experience elegantly and stylishly combines the rarest ingredients for a sensorial escape for mind and body. After a full body reviving granita scrub smooths skin, drift away with an aromatic relaxing full-body massage. Continue the luxury with a triple active facial for youth, plumping, and radiance. Performed with Sothys Products.

Available at our spa hotel in Toronto, performed with Sothys Products.
