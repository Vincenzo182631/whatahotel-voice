## Accommodations

The St. Regis Toronto features 124 hotel suites, where modern elegance and bespoke service unite. Our hotel also boasts 258 of the largest luxury accommodation in downtown Toronto, Canada, which start at 550 square feet. Recharge in an exquisitely furnished hotel room decorated in a modern residential style. Our rooms and suites offer a bright, airy ambience accentuated with impeccable details. High ceilings add to the spacious feel, while oversized windows provide commanding views of the downtown Toronto skyline.
