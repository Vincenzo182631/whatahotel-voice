### LOUIX LOUIS

A mystical and alluring place to enjoy forgotten classics and rediscover the lost charm of conversation, LOUIX LOUIS soars 31 floors above the glittering skyline. Inspired by Toronto’s heritage as one of the largest distillery districts in the world during the turn of the 19th century, the dramatic interior is designed to represent the inside of a glistening crystal whiskey glass, each sip revealing...See More a new layer of discovery. At the heart of this fine dining restaurant in Toronto is the glamorous two-story-high grand bar showcasing over 500 dark spirits – one of the largest collections in North America.See Less
