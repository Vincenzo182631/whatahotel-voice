## Sea and Tuscany

From the Hotel Plaza e De Russie in Viareggio, in just a short amount of time, you can visit places with timeless charm, Tuscan cities of art famous around the world for their masterpieces of inestimable value, and natural environments with inimitable landscapes: from the crystalline waters of Elba Island to the Cinque Terre, with its suggestive medieval villages set into the coast overlooking the sea.

Cultural events, such as the Palio of Siena or the Puccini Festival of Torre del Lago, or even the Carnivale of Viareggio itself are opportunities not to be missed to experience a region replete with charm.

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your electronic device (PC, tablet, smartphone, etc.) while you browse a website and that provide certain information. Web browsers allow you to exercise some control over cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies or block cookies from specific websites.
