## Step Inside Three Centuries of History

Built in the 18th-century and restored in 2022, Casa Pestagua stands within Cartagena’s walled city, a UNESCO World Heritage Site for its historical and cultural significance.

A member of **Relais & Châteaux** and the **Virtuoso portfolio**, and awarded **Two Michelin Keys** in 2025, Casa Pestagua is a place where history, beauty, and genuine hospitality arrive together, quietly and without reservation.

## Rooms & Suites

Eleven suites. Five rooms. Each one shaped by three centuries of architecture and an insistence on quiet comfort. Colonial ceilings rise overhead. Light moves slowly across the walls.

Outside, Cartagena hums — but in here, time is entirely your own.

## Acasí Rustic Beach

Forty-five minutes by boat from Cartagena, the Caribbean opens up completely. **Acasí is Casa Pestagua’s private beach on Barú Island** — white sand, turquoise water, and the kind of stillness that only distance provides.

You can spend the day or extend your stay. Contact our Concierge for inquiries.
