## Policies

Check-in/-out Times

Check-in Time: 3:00 PM Check-out Time: 12:00 PM

Early Arrivals and Late Departures

If you plan to arrive early in the day and would like immediate access to your room, please note that early check-in is subject to availability; to guarantee access, we recommend booking the room from the night before. Likewise, for late departures, late check-out is subject to availability; reserving an additional night will allow you to keep your room until you leave. If you prefer not to make an extra reservation, we will be happy to store your luggage for you.

Accommodation

The Hotel charges an additional fee for children of 12 years of age, from 0 to 11 years, there will be no charge. Please, also note that the hotel does not have Twins or connected rooms. If you need a crib or extra bed, contact the hotel directly to confirm the availability.
