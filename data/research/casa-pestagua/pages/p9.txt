# Gastronomy

A celebration of ancestral recipes and Caribbean soul, where Colombia’s rich culinary heritage comes to life in every detail.

## An immersive culinary journey rooted in Caribbean land and legacy

### Animare Restaurant

AniMare offers sophisticated settings that invites guests into a deeply sensorial culinary experience. Here, tradition comes to life through a concept, that pays homage to the ancestral richness of Colombia’s Caribbean and Pacific coasts through the hands of our Executive Chef, Heberto Eljach.

### Animare Bar

From fresh, vibrant creations to bold reinterpretations, each cocktail is a celebration of artistry, technique, and flavor. Animare Bar is a refined sanctuary at Casa Pestagua, where time seems to stand still amid timeless details and an intimate, captivating atmosphere.
