## WHERE _luxury_ MEETS ADVENTURE IN CANADA'S COAST MOUNTAINS

Immerse yourself in luxury at Whistler's iconic ski-in/ski-out haven where timeless elegance meets modern alpine ambiance. Nestled at the foot of Blackcomb Mountain, Fairmont Chateau Whistler offers exceptional dining, incomparable resort amenities, championship golf course, and unparalleled access to Whistler’s endless outdoor adventures.

### IMMERSE YOURSELF IN MOUNTAINSIDE LUXURY

Enjoy a true resort experience in Whistler. Choose from 519 thoughtfully appointed guest rooms and suites, featuring modern alpine décor and stunning views of the Whistler Valley, woodlands, and surrounding mountains. Relax and reconnect at Whistler’s premier destination and landmark hotel at the base of Blackcomb Mountain. Celebrated as Canada’s #1 Resort by Travel + Leisure® and Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards, Fairmont Chateau Whistler sets the standard for unforgettable moments in Whistler.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Does Fairmont Chateau Whistler have a swimming pool?

Yes. Our slope-side pools are a guest favourite. Our 60-foot heated lap pool is the perfect place to get your heartrate going, while an indoor/outdoor heated leisure pool provides fun for all ages. Or ease your tired muscles with a soak in our four rejuvenating whirlpools.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Chateau Whistler?

Check in is at 4 PM and check out is at 11 AM.

Late check-out or early check-in is by request only and based on availability. Additional fees may apply. Please inquire with the front desk for details.

Is Fairmont Chateau Whistler pet-friendly?

Fairmont Chateau Whistler welcomes small pets for a rate of CAD $50 plus applicable taxes per day. Pets can be supplied with beds and a bowl upon request. Pets are not allowed in the Fairmont Gold Penthouse Suites. Service dogs will be allowed on the property free of charge.

Is there a concierge service at Fairmont Chateau Whistler?

The Concierge desk is located in the main lobby and attendants are ready to advise and assist you with your activity, dining, and transportation arrangements.
