## More about our suites

What suite types are available and how do they differ?

41 suites across five types. The Garden Suite Hammock (640 sq ft) is ground-floor, opening onto a Japanese-inspired desert zen garden with an artisan hammock. The Garden Suite Soak Tub (640 sq ft) has the same footprint with an outdoor soaking tub on the terrace. The Sky Suites, Mountain View and Cacti (780 sq ft each), rise to rooftop terraces with built-in star nets for lying flat under open sky. The Saguaro Suite (2,517 sq ft) spans three levels on a corner lot with 360° views that reach no other suite. Every suite has a private terrace, indoor-outdoor rain shower, A/C, Wi-Fi, blackout drapes, and an in-room safe.

What makes the Saguaro Suite different?

Scale and perspective. Three levels. 2,517 sq ft. A corner position that produces views unavailable from anywhere else on the property: the Sierra de la Laguna mountains, Las Palmas Beach, and La Mesa farming fields simultaneously. Designed for those who want the most privacy and the deepest sense of disconnection.

What is distinctive about the Garden Suite Hammock?

640 sq ft on the ground floor, opening directly into a zen garden with a desert orientation. The defining detail is the artisan-made hammock that extends into the landscape, a place to be still at ground level, surrounded by Baja air and plant life. The design draws from both Mexican hacienda heritage and Japanese spatial minimalism.

What views and features do the Sky Suites offer?

Both Sky Suite variants have private rooftop terraces with suspended star nets, not for decoration, but for lying down and actually watching the sky. The Mountain View faces the Sierra de la Laguna, farmland, and the ocean horizon. The Cacti View faces the cacti forest and is oriented toward stillness. Both are 780 sq ft.

Which suite has an outdoor soaking tub?

Only the Garden Suite Soak Tub. The tub sits on the ground-floor terrace within the zen garden. All other suites (Hammock, both Sky Suites, and the Saguaro Suite) have indoor-outdoor rain showers.

Does Paradero allow children or pets?

Paradero Hotel is adults-only. No cribs, no extra beds, no exceptions. Families with children are directed to Paradero Villas, opening 2026. Pets are welcome with prior notice to the reservations team and applicable charges.
