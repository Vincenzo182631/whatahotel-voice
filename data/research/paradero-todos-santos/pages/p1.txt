## Getting to Paradero

Located between Todos Santos and Pescadero, Paradero Todos Santos is conveniently accessible from three nearby airports, each approximately one hour away:

- Los Cabos International Airport (SJD)
- La Paz International Airport (LAP)
- Cabo San Lucas International Airport (CSL)
