Dining

A restaurant for all to gather around.

The Villas will also be home to a brand new Southern Italian destination restaurant, Cosimo, which will be available to guests of the Villas, Suites and Paradero Homeowners. Cosimo cuisine is heavily inspired by Chef’s visits to Southern Italy where he was able to pull elements from top culinary destinations such as Capri, Naples, Puglia, and Sicily. The team has long been captivated by the commonalities between Baja and Southern Italy, from the quality of the produce to the strong oceanic connection, making this cuisine a natural match for cooking techniques. Guests can expect a range of delectable dishes, from stellar seafood options to handcrafted gelato.

## Learn more about Paradero Villas

When does Paradero Villas open and who is it for?

Opening March 1, 2027, adjacent to Paradero Hotel in Todos Santos. Where the Hotel is designed for stillness and adults-only restoration, the Villas are built for families: multi-generational, vibrant, inclusive. Infinity pool, Italian restaurant, Kids Club, and adventure center all on-site.

How many suites will the Villas have?

24 suites across three floor plan configurations. Designed using the same architectural language as the Hotel (earth-toned materials, indoor-outdoor integration), adapted for extended family stays.

What is the Coconeh Club?

A supervised children's program with its own pavilion, outdoor gym, and expansive gardens. Activities include cooking classes, crafting, and movie nights under the stars. Day and night childcare is available for homeowners. Built around creativity, nature connection, and active discovery.

What is Cosimo?

A Southern Italian destination restaurant within the Villas. The menu was shaped by the chef's time in Capri, Naples, Puglia, and Sicily: stellar seafood, handcrafted pasta, hearth cooking, produce-forward dishes, and handcrafted gelato. Open to guests of the Suites and Villas, and to Paradero Homeowners.

Will Villas guests have access to the Hotel's spa and infinity pool?

Only guest over 18 years old. The Villas guests have access to their infinity pool, and the full daily programming. The Villas add Cosimo and Coconeh Club.
