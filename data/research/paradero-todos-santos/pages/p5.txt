The Torote tree takes decades to look the way it does. Gnarled, unhurried, rooted so deep in the rock that nothing moves it. It does not perform its permanence. It simply belongs. The Homes at Paradero were designed with that same logic.

Welcome to the first gated community in the private enclave of Paradero. This community was created with the intention of providing space for individuals to explore, expand, and come back better. Each private home is built upon purchase and designed in tandem with the land and culture. To help you disconnect and reimagine a new way of living.

Residents are invited to be a part of a thriving community, rooted in sustainability. Invest in a future set amidst the merging landscapes of desert, oasis, beaches, and mountains. Participate in the largest No-Till farming project, and enjoy exclusive access to sailing adventures on the Sea of Cortez, while creating memories with family and friends.
