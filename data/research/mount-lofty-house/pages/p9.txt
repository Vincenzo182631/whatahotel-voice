##### Conference & Function Rooms in Adelaide

## **Historic, modern** and functional venues.

From intimate boardrooms to our stunning fully enclosed air-conditioned Piccadilly Pavillion, we have a selection of function venues and state-of-the-art conference facilities.

Coach House

Our **Coach House (view 360˚ tour)**, a beautiful heritage building ten metres away from the main house, opens out onto a large deck and grassed area. Overlooking the Pinot Vineyard, the venue features its own bar and amenities, built in data projector and screen. Can cater for up to 60 delegates.

Piccadilly Panorama

**Piccadilly Panorama (view 360˚ tour)** offers ample room for many different setups. With plenty of natural light and views over Piccadilly Valley, the pavillion has direct access to our gardens while the restaurant features its own bar. Can cater for up to 200 delegates.

Hardy's Room

Our heritage venue **Hardy’s Room (view 360˚ tour),** perfect for smaller meetings and intimate gatherings in the main House. Can cater for up to 22 delegates.

Sequoia Lounge

Within the **Sequoia precinct** guests have exclusive use of the lodge’s lounge which features multiple fireplaces, comfortable sunken lounges, 180° views across the valley and a panoramic sun deck. The lounge offers an ideal mixed space for small meetings through to group dining and team building experiences. The boardroom may be set for a meeting of up to 16 delegates.

Garden Dome Round Table

Nestled in the garden beneath the 170-year-old Sequoia Tree is our Garden Dome. Seating up to 6 guests at a round table it features heating and cooling, Bluetooth audio and our exclusive Estate Butler service. A great location for smaller more intimate meetings. This venue is ideal for small meetings, corporate luncheon and team building.
