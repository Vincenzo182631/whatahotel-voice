# The Art of High Tea at Mount Lofty House Adults Retreat

Mount Lofty House was built in 1852 by prominent statesman Arthur Hardy as his summer house, set up high in the Adelaide Hills the manor house shares breathtaking views of the Piccadilly Valley. Today Mount Lofty House Adults Retreat welcomes guests for luxury escapes, three hat fine dining and indulgent day spa experiences. Join us, for a self-guided art session in our exquisite botanical gardens, enjoy a luxurious one-of-a-kind High Tea experience for Two people in Hardy’s Verandah Restaurant, with its breathtaking views across the Piccadilly Valley and immerse yourself with the House collection of works by local South Australian artist Stephen Trebilcock.

**This experience is available:** Monday – Thursday (11am- 3pm),

_IMPORTANT:_ Guest must provide own art supplies for self-guided art session in The Terrace gardens before or after High Tea at 12pm.

_Not valid 7-9 October 2026_
