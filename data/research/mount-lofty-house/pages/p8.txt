## Experience the festivities at Mount Lofty Estate

Celebrate the most wonderful time of the year in true Mount Lofty Estate style — where timeless elegance, breathtaking scenery and world-class hospitality come together for an unforgettable festive season.

Perched high above the Piccadilly Valley in the Adelaide Hills, Mount Lofty Estate transforms into a sparkling haven of celebration. Whether you’re planning a joyful gathering with friends, a refined Christmas Day Lunch, or an elegant end-of-year corporate celebration, the Estate offers a setting unlike any other.

Guests can enjoy a spectacular Christmas lunch at Mount Lofty House, including festive dining at Hardy’s Verandah Restaurant, creating one of the most memorable Christmas dining experiences in the Adelaide Hills.
