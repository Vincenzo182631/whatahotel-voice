# Hardy's Verandah Restaurant

3 Hat restaurant in the Adelaide Hills

Hardy’s Verandah Restaurant, located within the historic Mount Lofty House Adults Retreat, celebrates the tradition of luxury. Offering an elegant High Tea and the renowned HVR fine-dining Degustation Dinner Experience, complemented by breathtaking panoramic views across the Adelaide Hills, Hardy’s Verandah promises an unforgettable epicurean journey.

##### Open 7 Days  Dinner – everyday Sitting 6pm  High Tea – Weekday Sittings 12pm – Weekend Sittings 12 & 2pm  Contact Us: PHONE +61 (0) 8 8130 9292 / Click to EMAIL US

_(17.5% surcharge applicable on public holidays)_

## HVR Ultimate Journey

Hardy’s Verandah Restaurant offers an intimate, refined dining experience designed for guests aged 16 years and over. The culinary journey unfolds on our elegant fine dining verandah, featuring spectacular views of the Piccadilly Valley.

Throughout the evening, our attentive host will guide you through your immersive experience, ensuring every moment is memorable. Our sommeliers will be on hand to guide you through our Award Winning, Three Glass Wine List featuring more than 1300 bottles of Australian and International Wines which are housed in our historic 1852 wine cellar.

## High Tea

## at HVR

Indulge in the epitome of refinement at Hardy’s Verandah Restaurant, located within the historic Mount Lofty House Adults Retreat in the heart of the Adelaide Hills.

Nestled in the heart of the home, this experience transcends traditional tea service, offering a sensory journey. Immerse yourself in opulence as you savour exquisitely crafted pastries, delicate finger sandwiches, and the finest loose-leaf teas.

## Celebrations at HVR

##### Private Dining experiences & Celebrations

Hardy’s Verandah Restaurant at Mount Lofty House offers an exquisite venue for social events and private dinners, combining historic charm with culinary excellence.

The stunning Adelaide Hills backdrop sets the stage for memorable gatherings, whether it’s an intimate private dinner or a celebratory social event.

The restaurant’s elegant interiors and attentive service provide a sophisticated atmosphere, while the menu features a culinary journey of expertly crafted dishes using locally sourced ingredients.

With personalised service and a touch of luxury, Hardy’s Verandah creates a bespoke experience for social occasions, ensuring each event is a tasteful blend of ambiance, cuisine, and celebration.

## Meet the team

##### exceptional dining begins with exceptional people

At Hardy’s Verandah Restaurant, exceptional dining begins with exceptional people. From our chefs and sommeliers to our front-of-house team, each member shares a passion for hospitality, seasonal produce and creating memorable guest experiences. Together, they continue to shape one of the Adelaide Hills’ most celebrated dining destinations, delivering the warmth, expertise and attention to detail that define the Hardy’s Verandah experience.

## One of the best wine lists in the country

##### Arthur’s wine cellar

Hardy’s Verandah Restaurant is renowned for its spectacularly curated wine list. The original stone cellar underneath the restaurant is home to some cult wines from South Australia, the Australian continent, and of course, includes some of the great vintages of the European classics.

Embark on a culinary journey through South Australia and beyond with our head sommelier.

Perfection on the Mountain

We had been planning to eat at this establishment for some time- We had the Degustation menu- My wife and I were led to a beautiful table overlooking the Piccadilly Valley, met by some lovely staff and a very knowledgeable Sommelier. Each course from the Amuse Bouche, Beetroot Tartare, Kingfish, Wagyu beef. Chocolate Forte and Petit fours, were all flawless. They were individually amazing- each element of each dish was exquisite. We have eaten at some very nice restaurants around the world and had some very fine meals- this will go down as one of/if not the finest we have eaten. Highly recommend- Perfect way to finish our 30th anniversary holiday.

## January 2024

Christmas lunch without the drama

We had an marvellous Christmas degustation lunch here, with matched wines (and matched mocktails for our 17yo).

Catered beautifully for my wife’s shellfish allergy with a couple of minor but very good substitutions.

This was a great lunch option, far from a traditional Christmas lunch - rather it was what I expect is their typical high-quality degustation with just a nod to the seasonal in a pudding-themed dessert. Excellent across the amuse bouche, seafood, and into a really good beef wellington.

The wine pairings were excellent and creative, with champagnes bookending proceedings, among a surprising chenin blanc and an Australia versus France Shiraz matchup.

However the adults were VERY jealous of our son’s superb paired mocktails, created on the fly by a really talented barman!
