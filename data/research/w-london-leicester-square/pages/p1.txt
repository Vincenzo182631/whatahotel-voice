# Welcome to W London

## Discover Iconic Spaces & Bold Design at our Luxury Hotel

For next-generation travellers, who are the connectors in their circles and the link to what’s happening, we’re the unconventional luxury hotel in London, doubling as a social hub. A catalyst for the collective with unique programming and a service that bends to our guests, not the other way around. When your world needs to expand, we push the boundaries. When you crave the capital, we bring it closer....See More When London calls, we’re the answer.See Less

### London’s Unconventional Luxury Hotel

When you want the front row in Soho and to feel the pulse of its streets on every corner, W London is your backstage pass to the city’s most vibrant and creative hotspots. All access, all yours. At the crossroads of entertainment, culture, and cuisine, our illuminated hotel rises as a photogenic landmark in its own right, overlooking Leicester Square, the West End, and Chinatown. This is where conversations start and stories never end. Where history meets hedonism under a metropolitan glow of lights. This is our liberated take on luxury: personal, playful, and always in motion.

P.A.W. - Pets Are Welcome

You’re invited – and your pet is too. Experience London with your four-legged friend by your side. While we welcome your pet, please note that a one off supplementary room charge of £50 will be applied to your account. This fee supports our pet-friendly services and amenities while ensuring comfort for all our guests. In the event of damage to the room or the need for additional cleaning due to your pet, a £100.00 cleaning fee will be charged at checkout. Engineering

Replaced light bulbs with energy-efficient bulbs.

Installed low-flow shower heads and toilets.

Introduced water/energy-saving linen program.

Installation of energy efficient gas boilers.

Redesign of the domestic hot water system to reduce heat loss and water usage.

Interior and exterior LED lighting.

Heat and cooling recovery coils on extra systems.

Kitchen

Separate food waste compartment which goes to composting.

Reduction of food waste through new plated meals concept as opposed to a buffet breakfast.

Offering leftovers from events and breakfast in staff canteen.

Style

Currently using ‘MAGC’ (Make a Green Choice) program where guests are given the option to receive cleaning service every third day. With this, guests can choose to have points each day they don’t receive cleaning service, or donate to

plant a tree in Africa. This program helps by recycling the use of linen, towels, water, and harsh chemicals used to clean the room, as well as linen and towels.

Hotel waste is recycled and separated in three ways: glass, recyclable paper and boxes, and general waste. Larger bathroom amenities, or residential-sized bottles, to be launched in every guest room. A 300ml bottle replacing small bottles in each room along with a five litre bottles for refilling. This strategy will drastically cut down on single-use plastic bottles as the residential-sized amenity bottles contacts the same amount of product.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W London?

The check-in time at W London is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W London allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W London. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of £50.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W London?

W London does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W London have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W London has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W London booking.

What is the closest airport near W London?

The closest airport near W London is London City Airport (LCY). LCY is located approximately 9.1 Miles from the hotel.

Does W London have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W London does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
