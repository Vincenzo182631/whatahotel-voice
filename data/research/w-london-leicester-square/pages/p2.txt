## Accommodations

Nestle yourself in one of our 192 luxury guest rooms or suites with a sleek and stylish interior. Each space features wall-to-wall mirrors, work desks, and playful design features from local artist Jack Irving. Refresh under rainforest showers and crash on the sleep-easy signature W beds in your extra fluffy bathrobes. Mind the umbrella in your closet—you're in London, after all.

### Soho Superlative

The iconic Soho Suite and Supreme Penthouse suites take exclusivity to new heights. These headquarters of luxury overlook the best of Soho, penthouse-style. With bold colours and design furniture, the interior makes for appealing accents. Stretch out on a Chesterfield sofa while controlling the whole room with the swipe of an iPad. Everything from temperature to entertainment and in-room dining is at your fingertips. Start the night in good spirits with the Mega Bar service, complete with your own mixologist. Time well spent.

### Personalise Your Experience

All 192 rooms and suites have been injected with the latest in-room technology courtesy of Digivalet. The first hotel in the UK to implement this technology, W London redefines luxury with one of the most advanced tablet-based systems in the industry, allowing you to personalise your stay from check-in to check-out.

## You're in Charge

The Digivalet system puts you in control of your stay, allowing you to amplify your mood with custom settings. This includes: Lighting, sound and temperature, syncing personal streaming services, the latest in-room entertainment including Netflix, ordering suggestions based on your previous purchases and preferences.

### The Mega Bar

Custom designed for W London, the Mega Bar’s exterior takes inspiration from a music freight case, which opens out to reveal an opulent bar, featuring luxurious design details including an onyx worktop, deluxe leather panelling and a handcrafted gold-hammered sink.

How to Book Your Mega Bar Experience?

Simply book one of the following suites and your Mega Bar experience will be included free of charge:

Screening Suite

Supreme Screening Suite

Soho Suite

Soho Penthouse

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W London have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W London have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W London?

The largest occupancy room at W London is the Soho Penthouse 3 Bedroom with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W London?

Some rooms at W London permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W London have fridges?

No, rooms at W London do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W London have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W London have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W London have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W London have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W London have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W London have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
