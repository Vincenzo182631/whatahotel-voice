## Dining

The Perception and W Lounge are our stage for the spirited, a daily performance of bold pours, magnetic energy, and unmistakable Soho attitude. Agave leads the charge. From mezcal to tequila and beyond, our menu showcases rare spirits from across the globe, each one chosen for its story, its soul, and its ability to start a conversation. Our bartenders? Part curators, part characters. They create with...See More purpose and serve with unmistakable style. Every drink is deliberate. Every visit, different. Gone are the days of velvet ropes and tired club nights. This is a lifestyle bar experience with rhythm, flow, and feeling. The crowd evolves, the playlist shifts, and the atmosphere moves like the city around it. It’s electric. And always in motion. So come early. Stay late. Order something you’ve never tried.
