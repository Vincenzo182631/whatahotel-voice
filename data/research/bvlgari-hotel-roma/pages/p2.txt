## The Bvlgari Bar

.JPG/jcr%3Acontent)

The Bvlgari Bar is the perfect place to socialise while sipping iconic cocktails. The sumptuous black marble bar top is finished with 110 Murano glass twirls that are hand blown with gold dust by Barovier&Toso. These precious jewels diffuse the light, infusing aperitivo hour and evening with a magical glow. For fine weather there is an outside bar and two large terraces, one that looks onto Via di Ripetta, and the other onto Piazza Augusto Imperatore. The Bvlgari Bar can welcome 108 guests indoors and 108 outside.

For reservation please call +39 06 36080410 or email dine.roma@bulgarihotels.com

### The Aperitif

Few traditions are as cherished as the Italian aperitivo and The Bvlgari Hotel Roma features a number of destinations from which to experience that sunset ritual. From the majestic views at the villa-like La Terrazza to the refined elegance of the Lobby Lounge, visitors can experience classic cocktails, aperitifs and light bites to mark the transition from day to night in the essential Italian way.

### Opening Time

From 5.00 pm to 1.00 am

The Bvlgari Bar: Via Di Ripetta, 73 Rome

For reservation please call +39 06 36080410 or email dine.roma@bulgarihotels.com
