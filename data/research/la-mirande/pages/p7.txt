# Seminars

Choosing La Mirande for small conferences, seminars, meetings or executive committee gatherings in Avignon, Provence, offers genuine added value. The inspiring atmosphere helps motivate group members and provides an outstanding setting in which to strengthen a team or build business relationships.

Our function rooms can accommodate up to 40 participants in theatre-style seating and 24 in a boardroom configuration. An elegant Napoleon III-style dining room, dating from the time when a member of the Pamard family—former owners of La Mirande—served as mayor of the city, can host up to 16 guests. The historic 19th-century kitchen can also be privately reserved for memorable evenings, with meals prepared in front of the guests (see La Table Haute).

The various lounges and spaces can host professional events throughout the year, including team-building activities, incentive programmes, showrooms, exhibitions, product launches and press conferences, all in an exceptional setting in the shadow of the Palais des Papes. Bespoke luxury events in the very heart of Avignon’s historic centre.

## Classical Concerts and Live Music Entertainment

The owners being a music-loving family, there have always been concerts at La Mirande. Paul Esswood, countertenor, Gustav Leonhardt, harpsichord, Hopkinson Smith, luth, Christophe Coin, cello, Véronique Gens, soprano, and more recently, Philippe Graffin, violin, Henri de Marquette, cello, Amy Norrington, cello, Natacha Kudritskaya, piano, and many others have honoured La Mirande with their memorable solo or chamber music performances. After the concert the audience and the artists share a convivial dinner. Immagine the pleasure to encounter these famous musicians on the intimate scale of a music room.

We will be of good advise for the most virtuoso music orchestras for all sorts of styles to animate private events before, during and after dinner. The excellence of the musicians allows a minimum of amplifying and great mobility. There is the possibility to privatize the ground floor for disco music (DJ) and, in case of exclusive use of the first floor the patio can be used as dance floor.
