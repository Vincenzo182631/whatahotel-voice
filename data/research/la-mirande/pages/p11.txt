Open seven days a week, Bistrot Pamard has long been a favourite dining destination for the people of Avignon and visitors alike.

## The Elegance of the Dining Room, _The Charm of the Garden_

The elegance of the dining room and the charm of the garden terrace create a setting of understated refinement, perfect for lunch or dinner in the Louis XV salon beneath its Venetian chandeliers, or outdoors facing the Palais des Papes.

## Chef   Michele Donvito

Chef Michele Donvito creates a cuisine rooted in exceptional local produce, faithful to the great French culinary tradition and enhanced by subtle Italian influences inspired by his heritage.

## Three Starters,  _Three Main Courses, Three Desserts_

The Chef embraces bistro cuisine in its most authentic form, celebrating the simple pleasure of sharing a good meal. The deliberately concise menu evolves with the seasons, showcasing the finest market produce while delighting returning guests with new discoveries.
