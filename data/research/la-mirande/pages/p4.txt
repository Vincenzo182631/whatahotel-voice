An invitation to travel through time over a drink, a cup of tea or a homemade pastry, in four exceptional settings.

## Four Settings,  _One Shared Pleasure_

From the patio furnished with Gae Aulenti armchairs to the garden overlooking the Palais des Papes, each setting tells a different chapter in the story of La Mirande.

## Simple Pleasures,  _Elegantly Enjoyed at Any Time_

Seasonal cocktails, Single Malts, traditional aperitifs and signature creations showcasing the wines of Châteauneuf-du-Pape alongside spirits from Avignon's artisanal Manguin Distillery.

## At Tea Time,  _Homemade Pastries & Fine Loose-Leaf Teas_

Homemade pastries, a selection of teas from the world's finest plantations, kombucha and organic 100% Arabica espresso from Mexico. Art books and French and international newspapers are available for guests to enjoy.
