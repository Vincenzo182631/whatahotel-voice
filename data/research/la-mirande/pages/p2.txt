# Suite

Amenities

57 m² / 1-4 guests

Mirror TV

Dimmable light switches

Complimentary mineral water

An exceptional 18th-century suite, suspended between the gardens and the Palais des Papes, offering La Mirande’s most exclusive experience.

Book a Room

Carved wood panelling, door surrounds and cornices give rhythm to the space, showcasing sumptuous panels adorned with the Montgeoffroy motif, a re-edition of a 1770 cotton inspired by French floral ikats once known as “Pompadour silk”. Silk-lined curtains, antique parquet flooring, period furniture, engravings and precious rugs create a setting of rare elegance.

The bathroom unfolds as a "Belle Époque" composition in its own right, where an Arabescato marble bathtub, Edwardian-inspired fittings and draped wallpaper are bathed in natural light from a window overlooking the garden.
