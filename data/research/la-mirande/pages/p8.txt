# Le Mirage

Hours

Tuesday to Saturday

Dinner · 6:30 pm - 11:00 pm

A relaxed tavern where you can enjoy good food and drink, made with excellent local produce and offering outstanding value for money.

Book a Table

## A Series of  _Three Historic Rooms_

A magnificent suite of three spacious rooms within the 17th-century building, preserving the remarkable remains of the former Cardinal's residence of Saint-Martial.

## Local Produce,  _Wine by the Carafe, No Pretence_

A concise and flavourful menu created by the La Table Haute team, showcasing the finest local produce. White, rosé and red wines from regional vineyards are served by the carafe.

## Mirande + Garage  = _Mirage_

The name is a playful portmanteau, blending _Mirande_ and _garage_ \- a light-hearted nod to the history of the place and the delightfully unconventional spirit of this restaurant.
