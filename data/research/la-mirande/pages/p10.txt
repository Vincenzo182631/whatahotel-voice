# The Art of Living  at La Mirande

Staying at La Mirande means experiencing life in an 18th-century Provençal townhouse, where every room, every fabric and every stone tells a story, and where the French art of living finds its finest expression.

## A private residence transformed into a hotel, _with its soul intact_

Marc Leonetti, jardinier amoureux de la nature, y a composé un désordre organisé où se côtoient haies de buis, arbres fruitiers, chênes verts, roses et herbes aromatiques — le tout cultivé selon les préceptes de l'agriculture biologique. En plein été, le jardin conserve sa fraîcheur naturelle, havre de paix au cœur historique d'Avignon.

## Authentic, living fabrics from the 18th century

Each room is adorned with a historic French printed fabric — Indiennes and Toile de Jouy from the workshops of Braquenié, heirs to the Manufacture Royale de Jouy.

## A carefully cultivated, organised wilderness

Marc Leonetti, a gardener with a deep love of nature, has created an organised wilderness where box hedges, fruit trees, holm oaks, roses and aromatic herbs grow side by side — all cultivated according to the principles of organic farming. Even at the height of summer, the garden retains its natural freshness: a peaceful haven in the historic heart of Avignon.

## From sunrise to dinner,  _a table facing the Palais des Papes_

On either side of the garden, four distinct settings unfold throughout the day — from breakfast beneath the shade of the plane trees to evening cocktails, by way of afternoon tea and a gastronomic dinner.

## A four-hectare estate  _to sustain La Mirande_

In keeping with their commitment to sustainable development, the owners cultivate an estate in Sarrians, in the Vaucluse — some twenty kilometres from the hotel — supplying the kitchens with seasonal produce.
