# Grand Deluxe Room

Amenities

33-43 m² / 1-3 guests

Mirror TV

Dimmable light switches

Complimentary mineral water

A tribute to the distinctive printed fabrics that were so fashionable in the 18th century, these rooms celebrate the joy of living and offer romantic views of the Palais des Papes, the world's largest Gothic palace and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Book a Room

Wood-panelled wainscoting, door surrounds and cornices elegantly frame expansive panels of stretched printed cotton, inspired by the celebrated Indian chintzes and historic Toile de Jouy patterns. Matching curtains lined with silk, original parquet flooring, and a curated collection of antique furniture, paintings, engravings and rugs complete this refined setting, enhanced by splendid views over the nearby Palais des Papes.
