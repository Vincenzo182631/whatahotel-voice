Enjoy an unforgettable stay in a Deluxe Double Room at a historic private mansion in the heart of Avignon, in the shade of the Palais des Papes.

Indian chintzes, Toile de Jouy fabrics, antique parquet flooring, period furniture and engravings create an interior steeped in history, where every detail evokes the charm of a bygone era. Antique-glass windows frame a striking view of the Palais des Papes.
