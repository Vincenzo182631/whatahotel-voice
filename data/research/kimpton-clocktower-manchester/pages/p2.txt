## Luxury, Without The Fuss

Commanding attention with its grand Victorian façade, Kimpton Clocktower Hotel invites you to experience the historic charm of Manchester in unforgettable style. An icon among Manchester city centre hotels, our thoughtfully appointed rooms blend timeless character with contemporary comfort, making every stay feel like coming home.

Each room is complemented by luxurious touches like a bathrobe and slippers, a fully stocked tuck box, bottled water, tea and coffee making facilities, and a convenient mini fridge, all curated to elevate your experience.

## Our Suites

### Suite No.51

Our most luxurious suite, Suite No.51, sets the standard for guests seeking a new level of unrivalled luxury without the fuss.

This suite offers up to three bedrooms, four bathrooms, and a large open plan sitting and dining room with plush velvet furniture, a marble dining table, and an alcove seating area with floor-to-ceiling curtains.

For those looking to entertain, the living area features a wireless music system, a 65″ 4K smart TV, a PS5 gaming console, and a Project T1 Record Player with its own private record collection.

Those staying at No.51 will enjoy a true VIP experience, with a dedicated Kimpton Clocktower ambassador available at the click of a button and their own private entrance. For more information, please email manchesterreservations@ihg.com.

**Capacity:** Up to 6 guests.

**Room size:** 345 sqm.
