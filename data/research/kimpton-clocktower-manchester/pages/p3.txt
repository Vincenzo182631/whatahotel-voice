## The Refuge

Blending British ingredients with global influences to create an eclectic menu, The Refuge Bar & Restaurant features small and large plates, perfect for sharing with family and friends. Using the finest ingredients, our chefs combine flavours from all over the world with an unmistakable Mancunian flair. Whether you're a local resident, a business traveller, or a leisure visitor, The Refuge's inviting atmosphere and exceptional service make it the ideal spot to enjoy a memorable meal in the heart of Manchester city centre.

## Cocktails reinvented

Step into The Refuge Public Bar, where cocktails come with a twist of creativity. Our mixologists blend classic craftsmanship with inventive flair – from house-made vermouths and bespoke infusions to bold flavour pairings you won’t find anywhere else. Whether you’re in the mood for a signature serve, a timeless classic, or something a little unexpected, every drink is designed to surprise and delight.

No reservations needed, just pop in, take a seat, and let us pour you something extraordinary.
