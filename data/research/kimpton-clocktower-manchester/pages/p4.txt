## Hotel Programs + Partnerships

### Corner Shop

With a convenient location in the hotel lobby, the corner shop is poised at the ready should you need anything: a morning pick-me-up after a late night, a magazine to read while taking a hot bath, a gift for someone back home, or a quick snack before you head out for the evening.

**Hours of Operation:** 24/7

### Accessible Stays

We’re excited to have teamed up with AccessAble, the UK’s leading accessibility information provider, to put together a handy guide for your visit to Kimpton Clocktower. This guide covers everything you need to know, from walking distances to accessible facilities, all checked out by trained surveyors. We want to make sure every guest feels right at home during their stay, so you can explore with total confidence.

Learn more

### Kimpton Plant Pals

We all know that plants are great for our mental and physical health, but they're not exactly travel friendly. Enter ‘Plant Pals’, a programme that allows guests at Kimpton Clocktower to request a plant to be placed in their room for the duration of their stay. Simply ask for a Plant Pal when you book!

Learn more

### George House Trust

At Kimpton Clocktower Hotel, our motto is Come As You Are and we’re committed to supporting the LGBTQ+ community by partnering with organisations that make a difference. We’re proud to partner with George House Trust, , a non-profit organisation providing support services to people living with HIV, their careers, partners, and families, that has been giving this vital support since the 1980s. The importance of their work along with the history of how, and why they were set up, is beautifully—and heartbreakingly—portrayed in Russell T Davis’ series, ‘It’s A Sin’.

Stay up-to-date with everything happening at Kimpton Clocktower Hotel!
