### Sunday Dinner & Stay

Make the most of your Sunday with a relaxing overnight stay in a Premium Double Room, complete with a delicious Sunday platter for two and half a bottle of house wine served in Refuge to enjoy together. Wake up to complimentary breakfast the following morning, then take your time with a leisurely late checkout at 2pm on Monday.

Learn more

### Manchester's Best Afternoon Tea

We've teamed up with local tea connoisseurs, Tea from the Manor, to provide an Afternoon Tea like no other. Available every Friday and Saturday from 12 - 3:30pm in our stunning Winter Garden, guests can expect all the afternoon classics you know and love - with a Refuge twist, of course!

Learn more

### Practices for a Better World

At Kimpton Clocktower, sustainability is part of everything we do. From reducing our environmental impact to supporting our local community, we’re committed to responsible practices that make a difference. Together with our guests and team, we’re creating a more thoughtful, low-impact stay.

Learn more

### Luxury Family Getaway

Enjoy quality time together in a spacious Premium Double Double Room, designed for families. Includes breakfast for up to four, a kids’ tuck box, and flexible timings with early arrival from noon and late check-out at 2pm. Little ones can enjoy Social Hour activities, while adults unwind with a £75 dining credit.

Learn more

### Stay Well

From tailored workouts (that can be completed in your room or in the gym) to luxurious self-care treats, Stay Well seamlessly integrates wellness for every guest. Think foam rollers, Theraguns, LED face masks and luxury bath salts. But that’s not all – click the link below to find out more!

## FAQs for Clocktower Hotel

Does Kimpton Clocktower Hotel have a pool?

No, Kimpton Clocktower Hotel does not have a pool.

How much is parking at Kimpton Clocktower Hotel?

At Kimpton Clocktower Hotel, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 20 GBP .

Discounted rates for the NCP car park on Oxford Street are payable to the front desk upon check-out. Height restrictions apply, max 2.1 m. Car park postcode is M1 5EJ. Car parks are suggested based on proximity to the hotel.

Parking fee: 20 GBP per day

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Clocktower Hotel?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Clocktower Hotel.

Pet policy description. At Kimpton we invite you to bring along your furry family member regardless of their size or breed and all without any extra charges. We provide all the essentials to keep your pet feeling pampered and ensure a smooth check in process.

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Clocktower Hotel for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Clocktower Hotel have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Clocktower Hotel has 1 restaurant.

When is breakfast at Kimpton Clocktower Hotel?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Clocktower Hotel?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Clocktower Hotel.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Clocktower Hotel?

Check-in at Kimpton Clocktower Hotel is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Clocktower Hotel offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Clocktower Hotel offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Clocktower Hotel from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Clocktower Hotel is conveniently located near Manchester International Airport (MAN), approximately 10 miles away (16.09 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

## FAQs for Kimpton Clocktower Hotel

### Does Kimpton Clocktower Hotel have a pool?

No pool here, but there’s still plenty of charm to enjoy! The stunning interiors and lively social spaces more than make up for it.

### How much is parking?

While we don't have parking on-site, there is plenty of parking available nearby from £20 per day.

### Can I bring my pet?

Absolutely! Kimpton Clocktower is super pet-friendly. There are no extra charges or size restrictions, and your pet will even get a few special touches to feel right at home. For a special treat, book our PAWfect Stay package.

### Can I cancel for a full refund?

This depends on the rate you book; some rates are flexible, while others are non-refundable. Just double-check the cancellation policy when you make your reservation.

### Is there a restaurant on-site?

Yes! The hotel’s restaurant, The Refuge, is a Manchester favourite. It’s perfect for everything from coffee to cocktails, with a great menu for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and even Sunday roast. Discover more here.

### Is there a complimentary shuttle?

There’s no shuttle service, but the hotel is right in the heart of the city, so getting around is easy by taxi, tram, or on foot.

### What are the check-in and check-out times?

You can check in from 3:00 PM, and check out by 12:00 noon. If you need a little extra time, just ask our friendly reception team. Late check-out is subject to availability.

### Does the hotel offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, Wi-Fi is free for all guests throughout the hotel, whether you’re working, streaming, or just scrolling.
