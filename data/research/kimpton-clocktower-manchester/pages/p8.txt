# Kimpton Clocktower HotelOur City Centre Manchester Destination

In the heart of Manchester city centre, our hotel is close to a number of galleries and museums, alongside the famous Manchester Cathedral. A short stroll will take you to the city’s renowned Gay Village, as well as the vintage stores and craft cocktail bars of the bohemian Northern Quarter.

## Live Music

From grassroots venues to the 23,500-seat AO Arena and the latest Manchester opening, Co-op Live, our city has hosted some of the biggest names on the planet. And with even more global icons booked this year, there’s plenty of reasons to visit!

Culture-vultures of all persuasions will find something just for them here, whether it’s a performance by a legendary power-balladeer or a family-friendly evening at Disney on Ice. Manchester has something exciting going on every week, so be sure to check the city’s online events calendar before your trip.

## Take In A Game

Etihad Campus, Manchester, M11 3FF, United Kingdom

### Etihad Football Stadium

Home to Manchester City Football Club, Etihad Stadium is a premier sports venue known for its electrifying atmosphere and world-class facilities. The stadium also offers guided tours, giving fans an exclusive behind-the-scenes look at the players' tunnel, dressing rooms, and pitch. Located just a short distance from the city centre, it’s a must-visit for sports enthusiasts and tourists alike.

Learn more

Sir Matt Busby Way, Old Trafford, Stretford, Manchester M16 0RA, United Kingdom

### Old Trafford Football Stadium

Home to the legendary Manchester United, Old Trafford is a must-visit for any footie fan. Feel the roar of the crowd and the thrill of the game as you cheer on the famous Red Devils in the UK’s largest club football stadium. Just a short drive from our hotel, the aptly named ‘Theatre of Dreams’ is the perfect spot to soak up the history and passion of one of the world’s most famous football clubs.

## Enjoy World Class Theatre

97 Oxford St, Manchester M1 6FT, United Kingdom

### Palace Theatre

Step into the glamour of yesteryear at the Palace Theatre Manchester. Just a stone’s throw from our hotel, this iconic venue brings the best of the West End to the North. Whether you fancy a musical, a gripping drama, or a bit of comedy, there’s always something to tickle your fancy. Grab a programme, and let the magic of theatre sweep you off your feet.

Make your trip to the Palace Theatre one to remember with our theatre package 'The Show Starts here'.

Learn more

3 Quay St, Manchester M3 3HP

### Manchester Opera House

The Manchester Opera House is one of the city’s most historic and iconic theatres. Located on Quay Street in Manchester city centre, it first opened in 1912 and has since been a cultural landmark, hosting everything from grand opera and ballet to West End musicals, comedy shows, and live concerts.

Learn more

Northern Quarter, Manchester M1, United Kingdom

## Northern Quarter

The Northern Quarter is Manchester’s hipster haven, brimming with quirky bars, indie shops, and vibrant street art. A leisurely stroll from our hotel, this buzzing neighbourhood is perfect for a day of exploring. Sip on craft beers, rummage through vinyl records, and marvel at the colourful murals. Whether you’re after a vintage find or the coolest cocktail, the Northern Quarter has got you covered.

Learn more

Manchester City Centre, Manchester, UK

## Explore Manchester City Centre

Experience the bustling energy of Manchester City Centre, just steps from Kimpton Clocktower Hotel. Discover historic landmarks, world-class shopping at Market Street and Arndale Centre, and a thriving culinary scene with diverse restaurants and cafes. Stroll through the Northern Quarter, renowned for its street art and independent boutiques, or enjoy the lively nightlife and cultural events that make Manchester a dynamic destination for every visitor.

Piccadilly Station, Manchester, UK

## Piccadilly Station

Piccadilly Station, a major transport hub just a short walk from Kimpton Clocktower Hotel, offers convenient access to Manchester and beyond. With frequent trains to London, Edinburgh, and other major cities, it is ideal for both business and leisure travelers. The station also features shops, cafes, and easy connections to local public transport, making it a perfect starting point for exploring Manchester’s vibrant neighborhoods and cultural attractions.
