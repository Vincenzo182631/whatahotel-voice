## Designed to Connect with Nature

Each of the accommodations at Etéreo has dramatic forest and ocean views, featuring floor-to-ceiling windows framing the natural landscape. Large terraces, many with private plunge pools, and expansive sliding doors complete the setting for extraordinary indoor-outdoor living. Both the architecture and interiors juxtapose contemporary aesthetics with regionally inspired design elements, and natural materials.

##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is Etéreo located?** Etéreo floats atop a lush mangrove forest amidst the unspoiled white sand beaches of Kanai, the top luxury resort destination in Riviera Maya.

**How do I get to Etéreo from the nearest airport?** Etéreo is located approximately 24 miles, or 45 minutes by car, south of Cancún International Airport (CUN). Private airport transportation is available upon request.

**Is Etéreo located directly on the beach?** Yes. Etéreo is an oceanfront resort with direct access to a private white-sand beach overlooking the Caribbean Sea.

**Does Etéreo have a pool?** Yes. Etéreo features Tranquilo, an adults-only pool; Vivo, a family pool; and a children’s pool and splash pad.

**Is Etéreo a good fit for couples?** Etéreo is well suited to couples, offering ocean-view studios and suites, private dining, SANA spa rituals, beach settings and couples-focused wellbeing experiences.

**Does Etéreo have a spa at the resort?** SANA, Wellbeing by Auberge, offers a cenote-inspired pool and emerging wellbeing trends from Mexico and beyond. Explore SANA’s exquisite sauna, full-body shower, clay room, and sound room as you rejuvenate with signature treatments such as the Cleanse.

**Is Etéreo family-friendly?** Etéreo welcomes families with studios and suites, a Chavitos Kids Club, a family pool, beach activities and larger multi-bedroom accommodation options.

**When is the best time to visit Etéreo?** Etéreo welcomes guests year-round in Riviera Maya; the best time depends on preferred Caribbean weather, beach conditions, seasonal demand and desired local experiences.
