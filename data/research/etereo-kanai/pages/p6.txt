# Our Social PurposeProtecting the Ecosystem

## Enriching Lives

In partnership with SacTun, Etéreo will further efforts to regenerate the local ecosystem.

## Protect. Regenerate. Conserve.

## Etéreo + SacTun

We strive to create exceptional experiences that enrich the lives of our guests, our team members and the community at large. Etéreo sits atop a lush site of native flora. Guests can view the importance of this vibrant ecosystem during their stay. As we grow our program, we will find more ways to connect our staff and guests to efforts that will protect endangered species in the area, including turtles, whale sharks, jaguars and scarlet macaws.
