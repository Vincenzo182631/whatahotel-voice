## RIVIERA MAYA’S TOP LUXURY RESORT DESTINATIONKanai

Located in the heart of Riviera Maya, Kanai is tranquil, private paradise nestled between Mexico’s tropical jungle and spellbinding white sand beaches. Home to three luxury hotels, this repose destination offers an unforgettable luxury vacation on the Mayan Riviera. From solo travelers and couples looking for an intimate experience to large groups of friends and families, Kanai is the perfect paradise for travelers seeking an authentic Mexican experience. Embrace a sense of curiosity and experience the magical haven of Kanai just minutes from the airport and steps away from Mexico's best beaches.

- Three award-winning resorts

- More than 400 luxury rooms and residences

- 11 premier restaurants and bars

- Tennis & Recreation Club

# LUXURY RESORTS ON THE CARIBBEAN COASTLINE

## A Beachfront Paradise

Discover a luxury resort haven, surrounded by the white sand beaches of the Caribbean. Kanai is home to an endless stretch of beach featuring sparkling crystal waters and tropical marine life of the Mayan Riviera. Boasting 1.2 miles of sunkissed beaches and less than half a mile away from an undisturbed coral reef part of the second largest system in the world dazzling arrays of different types of coral form this underwater wilderness, and provide homes and food to hundreds of fish species, marine turtles, and other marine creatures, it's an adventure-lovers paradise. Spend your days lazing away under the Mexican sun, exploring the coves with a paddleboard, or taking stroll along the Caribbean coastline at sunset. Your iconic oceanfront retreat awaits at Kanai.
