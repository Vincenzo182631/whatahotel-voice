A Five STAR RESORT ON LOCH LOMOND

# Live Well by the Loch

Awarded 21st Best Resort in Europe by Condé Nast Traveller UK Readers’ Choice Awards 2026.

Set on the shores of Loch Lomond, within Loch Lomond & The Trossachs National Park, Cameron House is a five star Scottish resort shaped by water, woodland and wide open air. Stay in elegant rooms, suites, apartments, cottages and lodges, with days that move easily between spa, golf, boating, dining and time outdoors. Whether you are planning a romantic escape, a family break or a longer lochside stay, this is a place to slow down, explore well and live well by the loch.
