## Frequently Asked Questions about Cameron House

Are there any loch cruises from Cameron House?

We offer our very own Celtic Warrior. Each cruise on the loch takes in some of the loch’s islands, including Inchmurrin, and hidden coves only visible by boat.

Why type of resort is Cameron House?

Cameron House is a resort that includes 208 rooms and suites, 115 lodges, 7 F&B outlets, a 250+ berth marina, a destination spa, and 27 holes of golf spread across 380 acres.

Is Cameron House 5 star?

We have been awarded 5 Red Stars by AA Rated Trips, showcasing our excellent levels of quality, and outstanding levels of hospitality and service throughout.

Do you have EV charging points?

We have 24 charging points available across the resort including 22kWh, 50kWh and 100kWh.

**Prices**

- 22kWh – from 59p per kWh
- 50kWh and above – from 70p per kWh

We use Connekt EV Charging who are a leading electric vehicle charging business in Scotland. For every charger installed, they’ll plant a tree on our behalf, supporting our commitment to our sustainability goals.

Is Cameron House Accessible?

At Cameron House, we are committed to providing a welcoming and comfortable stay for all our guests. Our hotel is accessible, with a number of lifts and dedicated accessible bedrooms available.

Our self-catering lodges, cottages and apartments are not specifically adapted for accessibility. The apartments within Mansion House have lift access, while our bungalows are all on one level, although some steps are required to enter.

For full details of our accessibility provisions, please read our Accessibility Guide.

What is the overnight spa break booking cancellation policy?

All reservations incur a deposit of 50% of your total stay amount paid at the time of booking. The minimum cancellation period is 7 days prior to arrival; if you cancel before this timeframe your deposit will be refunded. The 50% deposit will not be refunded for any cancellations within 7 days prior to arrival.

What time is check in and check out?

On the day of your arrival you can check in from 4pm. If you arrive early, we’ll be happy to store your luggage for you while you enjoy lunch or use the leisure facilities. On the day of departure check out is 11am.

Is Cameron House a dog friendly hotel?

Yes, Cameron House Hotel is dog friendly. You can bring a maximum of two dogs to stay, for an additional charge. Learn more about our fee inclusions in our pet policy.

Unfortunately dogs are not permitted in apartments, cottages or lodges except for dogs which are a support animal for a person who is disabled as defined in the Equality Act 2010 (which includes persons who have a mental or physical impairment and the impairment has a substantial and long-term adverse effect on their ability to carry out normal day-to-day activities). Cameron Lodges shall be entitled to request that supporting documentation is provided to verify that any dog taken into or kept in any accommodation type is a support animal.

How much is it to bring my dog to Cameron House?

The cost is £50 per day, per dog. Please note, a maximum of two dogs per room are allowed regardless of size.

Where will my dog be allowed on the resort?

Dogs are allowed to be with owners outside on the grounds of the resort. Inside, dogs are welcome within The Lobby Bar, The Tavern, La Vista, The Clubhouse, Great Scots’ Bar terrace and in The Wee Shop.

Please note that dogs are not permitted in The Cameron Grill, main area of Great Scots’ Bar, within The Leisure Club, Cameron Spa, Cameron Cinema, in resort vehicles or on the Celtic Warrior.

Are my dogs allowed off the leash in public areas?

For consideration to our staff, guests and other pets, pets must be on a leash at all times when not in your guest room, regardless of the perceived temperament of the pet.

Can I purchase gift vouchers?

Gift vouchers can be purchased online. E-vouchers can be emailed to the recipient. A special gift box or wallets can be posted at a surcharge.

How many restaurants will there be available on the resort?

At Cameron House, we have several dining options available onsite. Choose from informal dining to fine dining. Explore our restaurants and bars here.

How close is Cameron House to Glasgow?

While we are situated in one of the most beautiful landscapes in Scotland, we’re only 30 minutes away from the city centre of Glasgow—ideal for trains or transport links.

How do I book an overnight spa break?

To book a spa break, call 01389 312 210 or email reservations@cameronhouse.co.uk

How many children do your family rooms and suites sleep?

Within the hotel our luxury family suites sleep 2 adults and up to 3 children. For Cameron Lodges, our self-catering lodges can sleep up to 8 guests.

Which restaurants within Cameron House are family friendly?

Our family hotel in Loch Lomond has several restaurants including the Cameron Grill, the Tavern and The Clubhouse.

What family friendly activities do you offer at Cameron House?

We offer a range of activities to keep your little ones occupied including bike hire, paddle boarding, horse riding, a swimming pool, a cinema and so much more. We also host a variety of family and children’s activities during school holidays. Keep an eye out for our itineraries curated by our activities team.

What time is breakfast served from?

Breakfast is served daily from 7am – 10:30am. Room service can be arranged from 6am – 11am. A surcharge applies.

What credit cards will you accept for payment?

We are happy to accept Visa, MasterCard, and American Express.

Is there a designated smoking area?

Yes, we do have designated smoking areas across the Resort.

Can I hire a boat on Loch Lomond?

Cameron House’s luxury motor cruiser Celtic Warrior is available for loch cruises. Bookings can be made online or you can contact our Resort Reservations team on reservations@cameronhouse.co.uk or 01389 312 210, who will be delighted to make a booking for you.

What is your reservation payment policy?

Bookings made after 10th December will be charged on arrival. Card details will be required at the time of booking to guarantee a reservation. Policy applies to bookings on Flexible rate. Please note, offers and advance purchase rates will be charged at time of booking and are non-refundable/non-transferable.

What is the closest train station to Cameron House?

Balloch train station is approximately 2 miles from the hotel.

What is the closest airport to Cameron House?

“Glasgow Airport is only 17 miles from Cameron House. Edinburgh Airport is 64 miles from Cameron House.”

How far from Glasgow and Edinburgh is Cameron House?

Edinburgh is 71 miles and Glasgow is 25 miles from Cameron House Hotel.

What is the closest whisky distillery to Cameron House?

- Littlemill distillery, Scotland’s oldest distillery, is only 8.5 miles away from Cameron House
- Auchentoshan distillery, producing the only triple distilled Scotch whisky, is mere 12 miles from Cameron House
- Glengoyne Distillery is 14.5 miles from Cameron House.

Do you offer group discounts?

To book 10 or more rooms, please contact our Events team at events@cameronhouse.co.uk to learn more about the best group rates.

What is the dress code for the restaurants?

Dress code is smart casual in the hotel restaurants.

Are children allowed in the restaurants?

Children are welcome to dine in all the restaurants onsite, with an accompanying adult.

Do you offer car parking? And is there a cost?

Free on-site car parking is available for guests and we offer valet parking for guests staying overnight and dining at LOMA.

Do you allow three adults in one bedroom?

The maximum capacity is two adults per room.

Is the hotel and resort family friendly?

“Yes we love to welcome families at Cameron House Hotel and The Leisure Club, our teams love creating wonderful experiences for everyone from fun family activities, children’s menus across our restaurants, activity packs, and ensuring that all the little ones feel just as special during their time with us. Please read our family policy below;

- Children must be accompanied by an adult.
- The Cameron Spa is for over 18 years only.
- Children from 0-14 are welcome to share a family room with an adult.

What amenities do your suites include?

Our suites offer a range of luxury amenities including complimentary Wi-Fi and bar, bespoke toiletries and bathrobes, a Nespresso coffee machine, full Sky television package, nightly turndown, and much more.

What’s the difference between the Auld House and the rooms and suites?

All suites and bedrooms within Cameron House are beautiful, serene and luxurious, offering guests a peaceful night’s rest. The main difference between Auld House suites and the others is their increased space, size and additional amenities like terraces or balconies.

Do all the bedrooms have king or queen size beds?

All suites, double rooms and family rooms have king size beds.

How many bedrooms do you have at the hotel?

Cameron House offers 208 bedrooms in various categories, ranging from suites, family rooms, loch view, and garden terrace bedrooms.

What TV channels are on offer?

We offer a full Sky package in all accommodations.

Are there hairdryers in the bedrooms?

Hair dryers are available in every room for use during your stay.

Do you provide an iron and ironing board in the bedrooms?

An Iron and ironing boards are available in every room.

Can you provide extra beds in the bedrooms?

All children can be accommodated in our spacious family rooms on bunk beds.

Do you offer cots?

Yes, cots are available on request.

What are the maximum capacities for the bedrooms?

All our bedrooms have a maximum capacity of two adults or one adult and one child per room. Family rooms can accommodate two adults and two children aged 4-14 years in bunk beds plus one cot. Please contact Resort Reservations Team on reservations@cameronhouse.co.uk who will be happy to discuss the different options available and the suitability for your family or group.

Does the resort have wheelchair access?

There are wheelchair accessible entrances and lifts throughout the resort. Cameron House also has a range of accessible rooms on ground and first floor.

Is there a shuttle service?

A shuttle runs daily from 08:45am-10:15pm from Cameron House hotel to the Cameron Club. It leaves from the front door of the hotel quarter to the hour (xx:45). It departs from the Clubhouse on the quarter past the hour (xx:15). For more information, please contact reception or concierge desk on 01389 312 255.

I’m staying at the hotel, can I use Cameron Spa?

Rates do not include access to the Cameron Spa, however residents can choose to add a thermal experience and treatments onto their stay. A two-hour thermal experience is £42 per person for hotel residents and £55 per person for non-residents, and can be booked by our Spa Team at spareservations@cameronhouse.co.uk or on 01389 312 267.

I’m staying at the hotel, can I use The Health Club?

Hotel residents can enjoy complimentary access to the Health Club throughout their stay. The Health Club is open daily from 6:30 am – 10pm.

Reservations can be amended online. Alternatively, you can contact our dedicated Resort Reservations team to amend your booking at reservations@cameronhouse.co.uk or 01389 312 210.

How do I amend my reservation?

Reservations can be amended online. Alternatively, you can contact our dedicated Resort Reservations team to amend your booking at reservations@cameronhouse.co.uk or 01389 312 210.

What is your cancellation policy?

To enhance your experience at the Resort, we offer various cancellation policies. For details regarding our cancellation policy, prior to booking, please check online. If you already have a booking with us, please do check your confirmation for cancellation policy.

Is late check out available?

Late check out is available on request. A surcharge of £30 per hour is applicable.

If you want to arrive earlier, can I store luggage?

Yes, our Concierge team can store your luggage for you.

How can I make a reservation?

Our resort reservations team are available 7 days a week, Monday – Friday from 8am-8pm, and Saturday & Sunday from 9am-5:30pm on reservations@cameronhouse.co.uk or 01389 312 210.

You may also book online at any time.

Where are the swimming pools located?

The 18-metre pool, family splash zone, and bubble pool are located within the main hotel building, at the Health Club. Cameron Spa also has an indoor pool and a rooftop infinity pool.

I’m staying as a guest. Can I access the rooftop pool at the spa?

Cameron Spa access is available for an additional cost. It is located 3 miles from the main hotel building, and offers a complimentary shuttle service. Guests have the option to add on spa treatments and the thermal and hydro experience for an extra £42 per person which includes the rooftop infinity pool by contacting our Spa Reservations team at spareservations@cameronhouse.co.uk or on 01389 312 267.

I’m staying as a guest. Can I use the swimming pools?

Yes, guests have complimentary access to The Health Club and swimming pools throughout their stay. Please follow our child swimming supervision policy:

- All children must be accompanied by an adult
- Children under 8 years old must have one adult in the pool for every two children
- Children aged 8–15 years must have an adult present within the pool hall

Are children allowed in The Health Club?

Yes, children are welcome to play in the swimming pool and enjoy our indoor water slide. Children can access the swimming pool with unrestricted swim times. Please read our child swimming supervision policy below.

- All children must be accompanied by an adult
- Children under 8 years old must have one adult in the pool for every two children
- Children aged 8–15 years must have an adult present within the pool hall

I’m staying at the hotel, can I use the gym?

Yes, our hotel have complimentary access to the Health Club gym and swimming pool throughout their stay. Health Club members also have access to the gym and swimming pool. Under 16’s are not permitted to use the gym facilities.

What time are your Health Club and swimming pool open?

The Health Club and facilities are open from 6.30am – 10pm, seven days a week.
