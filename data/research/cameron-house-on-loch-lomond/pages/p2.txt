Private Dining

## An Exclusive Experience

For those occasions that call for something special, our private dining rooms create the perfect atmosphere for your group, with impressive décor, captivating views, and custom menus.

- Explore Private Dining

Can Cameron House host a barbeque or other outdoor dining events?

Yes, we can cater for outdoor dining occasions including barbeques. This is possible at La Vista and The Clubhouse, or within a marquee on our grounds. Please get in touch to discuss options.

Can I order from the La Vista and Cameron Grill menus if I’m booked within the restaurant private dining areas?

Yes, the menus on offer for private dining areas will reflect the outlets main menu. Please note, bookings of more than 15 people may be subject to a reduced menu offering.

What are the terms and conditions if I need to cancel my private dining booking?

Restaurant outlet private dining bookings are refundable up to 14 days in advance if cancelled in full. If numbers reduce after the 14-day period, deposits are held and credited against the final bill. Large function space bookings are subject to specific cancellation terms that are agreed within the contract.

Does my private dining booking require a deposit?

Yes, bookings within restaurant outlets would require a £20 per person deposit which is credited to the final bill on the day of your event. Large function space bookings are contracted, and deposit schedules are given, typically 25% of booking value at time of booking, 50% 3 months out and the remaining 25% at 1 month in advance.

Can you cater for special dietary requirements when booking private events?

Yes, we can cater for any dietary needs for your gathering.

Is La Vista available to hire out all year round?

La Vista restaurant can be hired out as a whole space all year round, however it is not available to hire over weekends and is subject to availability. Advance booking is highly recommended.

I’ve booked a private dining space for my occasion – can I decorate it?

Yes,we can connectorganisers withlocal recommended suppliers.

How far in advance should I book my private dining event?

As soon as possible, our function spaces are popular for all kinds of celebrations.

How many restaurants will there be available on the resort?

At Cameron House, we have several dining options available onsite. Choose from informal dining to fine dining. Explore our restaurants and bars here.

Do you offer gala dinner venue bookings?

Yes. We have a wide range of configurations across the suites and meeting rooms we have at the Cameron House Hotel.

Which restaurants within Cameron House are family friendly?

Our family hotel in Loch Lomond has several restaurants including the Cameron Grill, the Tavern and The Clubhouse.

What time is breakfast served from?

Breakfast is served daily from 7am – 10:30am. Room service can be arranged from 6am – 11am. A surcharge applies.

What is the dress code for the restaurants?

Dress code is smart casual in the hotel restaurants.

Are children allowed in the restaurants?

Children are welcome to dine in all the restaurants onsite, with an accompanying adult.
