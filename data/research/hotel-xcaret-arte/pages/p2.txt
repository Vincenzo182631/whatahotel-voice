# Hotel Xcaret Arte Suites

The Hotel Xcaret Arte suites celebrate the connection between nature, art, and wellness, offering spaces designed for deep and inspiring rest.

Each category features contemporary Mexican interior design, organic amenities, and artistic expressions that pay homage to Mexican culture.

* * *

## All suites at Hotel Xcaret Arte are All-Fun InclusiveTM

### All-Fun InclusiveTM

- Food and drinks included in all restaurants, except Xaak ($) and Kibi-Kibi (exclusively for Casa de los Artistas guests)
- Unlimited access to all our parks: Xcaret, Xel-Há, Xplor, Xplor Fuego, Xenses, Xoxi and Xenotes.
- Xcaret Xailing: ferry service on the Cancun-Isla Mujeres and Playa del Carmen-Cozumel routes, as well as the Xcaret Catamaran tour, transportation included.
- Art workshops: Textile, Painting (exclusively for guests of Casa de los Artistas), Pottery and Latin Dance
- Transportation to Xcaret, Xplor, Xplor Fuego and Xenses every 30 minutes.
- Transportation included to the rest of the parks. Under reservation.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Hotel Xcaret Arte Suites

### What types of suites does Hotel Xcaret Arte offer?

Hotel Xcaret Arte offers a wide variety of suites. Categories include: Garden Suites, River Suites, Ocean View Suites, Ocean Front Suites, Junior Suites, Master Suites, and Presidential Suites.

### Do all suites include the All-Fun InclusiveTM concept?

Yes. All suites at Hotel Xcaret Arte include the All-Fun InclusiveTM concept, which includes unlimited access to Grupo Xcaret parks, airport-hotel-airport transportation, transportation to parks, dining experiences, and artistic activities.

### Are the suites at Hotel Xcaret Arte for adults only?

Yes. Hotel Xcaret Arte is an adults-only hotel (+16), designed for guests seeking rest, wellness, art, and cultural experiences in the Riviera Maya.

### What views can be enjoyed from the suites?

Depending on the category, the suites offer views of the jungle, gardens, protected green areas, the emerald river, or the Caribbean Sea, with panoramic views that vary depending on the location, the House, and the category.

### Are there suites with direct access to the pool?

Yes. Some categories have a Swim Up option, which allows direct access to the pool or rivers from the suite's terrace.

### Are there suites with access to the spa?

Yes. The Garden Spa, River Spa, and Junior River Spa allow direct access to the Muluk Spa, offering relaxation experiences inspired by ancestral worldviews.
