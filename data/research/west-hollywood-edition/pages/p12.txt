# Services & Amenities

The West Hollywood EDITION creates a unique, one-of-a-kind experience together with the best and most personalized modern service available.

### For further information

Phone +1 310 953 9899 – Email: info.weho@editionhotels.com

Share

Guest Services

- 140 unique guest rooms and 48 suites
- 2 penthouses
- Signature restaurant
- Customized banquet menus for private events, luncheons, dinner parties and business meetings
- Housekeeping service twice daily
- Concierge service
- Complimentary use of the 24-hour fitness facilities
- Spa services exclusively for hotel guests
- Rooftop pool
- Complimentary high-speed wireless internet access throughout the hotel
- Executive Boardroom, Meeting Room and Private Event Space
- State-of-the-art audio and visual services available for private functions
- Multi-lingual staff
- Valet Parking
- Safety deposit boxes, located at front desk

Executive Services

- Over 6,500 sq ft of meeting and event space
- Executive Boardroom, Studio Meeting Rooms, Ballroom and Ballroom Theater event spaces
- Smart boards and electronic flip charts available
- Video conferencing
- iPads, laptops, Polycom, and LED screens available
- Executive car services available
- 24-hour multi-lingual secretarial staff available
- State-of-the-art audio/visual equipment
- Complimentary high-speed wireless Internet access throughout the hotel

Fitness Center

- State-of-the art fitness equipment including cardiovascular machines with personal flat screen TVs
- Personal training sessions available upon request
- Free weights
- Stretching area
- Complimentary towels
- Headphones and refreshments
- Accessible 24 hours a day

Spa

- Multiple treatment and steam rooms
- Extensive retail collection of luxury spa products

Accessibility

- Mobility accessible rooms available for certain room types
