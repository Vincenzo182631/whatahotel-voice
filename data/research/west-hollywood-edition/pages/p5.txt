# Sunset

## Sunset

VIEW WEBSITE

Creatures of the night will be drawn down below to Sunset, a modern, new kind of gathering place inspired by the dozens of clubs that dotted the Sunset Strip in the 60’s and 70’s. Join us on Fridays for incredible vibes provided by DJ MOS, and Saturdays for a dance party curated by Zen Freeman.

For general inquiries and large group reservations, please contact our team at contact@sunsetatedition.com.

- Friday & Saturday 10:00 PM - 2:00 AM
