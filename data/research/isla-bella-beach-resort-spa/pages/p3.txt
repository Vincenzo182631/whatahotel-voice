# Florida Keys Oceanfront Restaurants

At Isla Bella, you’re pampered wherever you go, and dining is no exception. Discover standout restaurants in Marathon, FL, from an ocean inspired dining experience to fresh seafood on the water or a casual burger on the beach. With flavors to suit every palate, every activity and every mood, this is Florida Keys dining at its finest.

## Mahina, A Signature Oceanfront Dining Experience

Dine with a view at Mahina, Isla Bella’s signature oceanfront restaurant. Inspired by the flavors of the Florida Keys and beyond, Mahina showcases fresh seafood, seasonal ingredients, coastal favorites, and thoughtfully crafted cocktails

View Menu & Hours

## Sushi Cabana

Join us at Sushi Cabana, our new al fresco sushi restaurant, open now. Take in the ocean views from 55 outdoor seats beneath umbrellas, palm trees, and enchanting string lighting next to our Yellowfin pool.

View Menu & Hours

## The Beach Bar

You can’t help but feel connected to the ocean at Isla Bella, and enjoying a meal shouldn’t be any different. Pull up a stool at the best beach bar in the Florida Keys and feel the refreshing breeze on your face. Enjoy freshly caught seafood—from marina to menu— plus tropical cocktails and lively music.

The Beach Bar is open from noon – 11 pm daily (serving food until 10 pm).

View Menus & Hours

## Burger Palace

Enjoy lunch options just steps away from your poolside lounge chair. With a special poolside menu including options for the kids, you can enjoy perfect days under the Florida sun without having to leave the resort.

The resort pool serves food from 11am – 4pm.

View Menu & Hours

## Marketplace Café

The cornerstone of Isla Bella’s abundant watersports and activities, our Marketplace is also a quaint café and an authentic Florida Keys shopping experience. Grab your morning coffee and a fresh pastry to enjoy by the water. We can’t think of a better way to start the day.
