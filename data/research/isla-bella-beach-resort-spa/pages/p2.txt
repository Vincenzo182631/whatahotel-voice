## FAQs

### Do all rooms at Isla Bella Beach Resort have ocean views?

Yes. Every guestroom and suite at Isla Bella Beach Resort includes an ocean view and a furnished private outdoor living area, either through a ground-floor beachfront veranda or an elevated balcony terrace.

### What is the difference between a veranda room and a balcony room?

Veranda rooms are located on the ground floor and provide direct beachfront walkout access. Balcony rooms are located on the second or third floor and feature a furnished private terrace overlooking the ocean.

### Does Isla Bella offer hotel rooms for families and groups?

Yes. Accommodation options include Double Queen rooms, one-bedroom suites with sofa beds, two-bedroom guestrooms and select suites with kitchenettes. Available layouts can accommodate couples, families and larger travelling parties.
