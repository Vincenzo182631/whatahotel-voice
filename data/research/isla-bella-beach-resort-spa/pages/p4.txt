## The Ultimate Florida Keys Couples Spa Experience

Our couple’s massage remains one of our most sought-after experiences, designed to help you fully unwind and reconnect. We recommend arriving early to make the most of your visit—beginning with time in our separate men’s and women’s steam rooms to relax and reset. Afterward, reunite in our tranquil relaxation room, where the calming atmosphere sets the perfect tone for your shared treatment.

To enhance your experience even further, we’re introducing two new side-by-side offerings in our Wellness Lounge. This inviting space differs from the traditional couple’s massage room, featuring plush lounge chairs with footrests for a more casual, yet indulgent setting.

Guests can enjoy a soothing 20-minute couple’s foot massage, or opt for a 20-minute Detoxifying Foot Soak—available as a standalone experience or as the perfect complement before or after the foot massage. These additions offer a unique way to relax together and extend your time of connection.
