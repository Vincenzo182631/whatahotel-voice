Situated in the dynamic Business Gate area, Fairmont Riyadh is a luxury hotel located just 15 minutes away from King Khalid Airport showcasing Saudi Arabian hospitality and sophistication. ## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Riyadh located?

Fairmont Riyadh is situated in the vibrant Business Gate, Qurtubah area, just 15 minutes from King Khalid International Airport. This prime location offers easy access to major business hubs and attractions in Riyadh, making it an ideal choice for both business and leisure travelers.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Riyadh?

Check-in at Fairmont Riyadh is at 3:00 PM, and check-out is at 12:00 PM.

Does Fairmont Riyadh have a spa?

Yes, Fairmont Riyadh features a luxurious spa offering a range of rejuvenating treatments and therapies designed to relax and revitalize our guests. Enjoy a tranquil atmosphere and a personalized experience tailored to your needs at Fairmont Spa.

Does Fairmont Riyadh have a swimming pool?

Yes, Fairmont Riyadh features a beautiful indoor swimming pool where guests can relax and unwind. Please note that the pool is open during scheduled hours for men and women.

Does Fairmont Riyadh have a gym/fitness center?

Yes, Fairmont Riyadh includes a state-of-the-art gym and fitness center, equipped with modern exercise equipment and facilities to help you maintain your fitness routine during your stay at our 5-star hotel.

Is parking available at Fairmont Riyadh?

Yes, Fairmont Riyadh offers convenient on-site parking for guests. Both self-parking and valet services are available to ensure a hassle-free experience during your luxury stay.
