# Clement Restaurant

Clement Restaurant brings people together across three distinct themed rooms, offering notable American fare with an approachable Midtown sensibility. Clement, along with its adjoining bar, features fresh new dishes, a hand crafted cocktail menu, and seasonal events. Weekends come alive with a vibrant brunch scene on Saturdays and Sundays.

## Meet The Chef

Luxury hospitality is ultimately about people. The finest ingredients and the most beautiful presentations only matter when they are supported by a team that genuinely cares about creating memorable experiences. My role is to cultivate an environment where our colleagues can thrive, because when people feel valued and inspired, our guests feel it in every interaction.

#### Malte Kontor

As Executive Chef of The Peninsula New York, Malte Kontor brings more than two decades of international luxury hospitality and fine dining experience to one of Manhattan's most iconic hotels. Originally from Germany, Chef Kontor has built his career across renowned five-star hotels and acclaimed restaurants, developing a culinary philosophy rooted in classical technique, exceptional ingredients, and a deep respect for hospitality.

## Highlights

#### Ceramic Anthology

Two ceramic installations, Anthology 1 and Anthology 2, greet guests upon arrival and act as an artistic partition between the reception and communal dining table. Anthology, an ode to pottery, designed by Pascale Girardin, utilizes more than 900 hand-thrown bowls and vases stacked to create an artistic installation of columns. The gray tonality of the finished installation ranges from blue to brown-gray.

#### The Peninsula Sunday Brunch Buffet

Indulge in a one-of-a-kind Peninsula Sunday Brunch Buffet at Clement, where exquisite flavors and elegant ambiance create an unforgettable dining experience.

#### Fifth Avenue Saturday Brunch

Our Fifth Avenue Saturday Brunch features sweet and savory classics with a focus on local and seasonal ingredients. From our decadent truffle egg omelet to Catskills smoked salmon and Maplebrook burrata, this brunch menu selection is accompanied by exceptional cocktails.

#### Exquisite Private Dining

The Private Dining Room at Clement adds a luxurious intimate dining experience for guests. The elegant space is enveloped by a series of staggered, wood wing walls and a bespoke table of wood and metals that can seat up to 12 guests.

#### A Home in Manhattan

Clement seamlessly blends its tony midtown surroundings with the liveliness and eclectic feel of the downtown art world. The restaurant feels like a quintessential New York home - with different rooms that cater to a range of moods, desires and occasions. The carefully planned spaces scale gracefully from an intimate lounge to larger dining areas providing a setting for both power lunches and lively suppers.

#### A Perfect Combination

Designed by leading international firm Yabu Pushelberg, Clement’s overall design scheme is glossed in an array of warm, soft finishes juxtaposed with moments of deep color, bespoke art and texture, all intentionally orchestrated to create a visual cohesiveness and meaningful design. Wood paneling with brushed metals and mirrors craft a strong glamorous statement, while linen fabrics and rich leathers soften the space and evoke a relaxed, residential atmosphere.

#### Wild New York

The splendour of nature and city is unleashed upon the walls of the “Color Room” of Clement. Guests dine surrounded by a sixty-foot-long, hand-etched jungle growing over the buildings of New York, entitled “Wild New York.” The etching is washed in layers of okra-colored paint to create a sense of depth and dimension.
