The Fitness Center item 2

The Peninsula Spa and Wellness Center is thrilled to partner with Technogym to unveil an exquisite new fitness space, featuring cutting-edge equipment and the latest innovations in the industry. Our facility boasts a serene, sunlit studio, creating an inviting atmosphere for all. In addition, our team of highly qualified personal trainers is available to provide personalized coaching sessions and design tailored fitness programs according to individual needs. The distinctive bamboo floors, teak accents and natural ambient lighting combine perfectly to highlight the spectacular Manhattan skyline views.

Guests must be 18 years of age or older in order to utilise the fitness facility.
