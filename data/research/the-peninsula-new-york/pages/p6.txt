# Vu Hair New York

Vu Hair New York is the first salon from renowned hair stylist Vu Nguyen and is comprised of expert hair stylists, color technicians and a makeup artist. Nguyen has been cultivating his talent over the last eighteen years, formally training under Frederic Fekkai, contributing to top fashion runway shows such as Diane Von Furstenberg, John Varvatos, Valentino and Chanel, and working as a personal hair stylist to prominent New York figures.

Services are available for special events and weddings, and are also available in-room.
