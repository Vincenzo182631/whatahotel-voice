# The Peninsula New York

Superbly situated at the center of Manhattan’s most glamorous midtown shopping and entertainment district, The Peninsula New York occupies a 23-story, 1905 landmark Beaux-Arts building at the intersection of Fifth Avenue and 55th Street. The hotel is the US flagship property of The Peninsula Hotels.
