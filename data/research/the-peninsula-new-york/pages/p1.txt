## Live the classic Manhattan lifestyle at Fifth Avenue’s premier address

The Peninsula New York offers refined luxury in the heart of Manhattan. Steps from Central Park, the Forbes Travel Guide Five Star hotel features newly refurbished rooms, elegant public areas, and convenient access to Rockefeller Center, premier Fifth Avenue shopping, acclaimed museums, and the legendary theaters of Broadway.

The Peninsula New York, 700 Fifth Avenue at 55th Street, New York, NY 10019, USA
