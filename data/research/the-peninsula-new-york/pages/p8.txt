The Peninsula New York Luxury Hotel Spa & Wellness item 2

The Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star Peninsula Spa & Wellness Center is an intimate, indulgent environment featuring innovative spa treatments, magnificent facilities and the most gracious, expert service. Leave the world behind and try one of the popular facials by the French luxury skincare line Biologique Recherche or opt for the ultimate in relaxation with the signature Deep Tissue Experience. The Spa features a stunning collection of 11 treatment rooms including a spacious Private Spa Suite, men’s and women’s private relaxation lounges, a tea lounge, and changing rooms with luxurious heat experiences.

_Guests must be 18 years of age or older in order to utilize The Spa and receive treatments._

## Additional Information

#### Arrival Time

You are encouraged to arrive 60 minutes prior to your appointment to enjoy the exclusive facilities at The Spa. Please understand that late arrival may result in a reduction of treatment time.

#### Heat Experiences

Enjoy a luxurious range of heating and cooling experiences which cleanse the body and skin, relax and soothe aching muscles and prepare your body and mind for the treatment that will follow.

Facilities include separate male and female:

- Aromatherapy Steam Room

- Sauna

- Experience Shower

- Ice Fountain

#### Relaxation Areas

Your Spa experience is made more memorable by beginning and ending in one of The Spa’s relaxation areas. Begin your journey in the Tea Lounge, which offers a selection of organic teas. Once your treatment has come to an end, we encourage you to continue your peaceful journey in our separate male and female relaxation rooms, featuring relaxation beds with individual music and lighting selections.

#### Health Conditions

Kindly advise us of any health conditions, allergies or injuries, which could affect your service when making your Spa reservation.

#### Fitness and Pool Access

Day Spa guests who book a minimum of two hours of services receive full access to the glass-enclosed pool and fitness center. Hotel guests receive complimentary access to the above facilities during their stay.

#### Pregnancy

We have specially designed treatments for expecting and nursing mothers after their first trimester. Please allow our Spa team to guide you in selecting which treatments are most suitable for you during this special time.

#### Cancellation Policy

A 100% cancellation charge will be incurred for any treatment not cancelled at least 12 hours prior to an appointment and 24 hours prior to Spa Retreats.

#### Spa Etiquette

Our Spa & Wellness Center environment is one of relaxation and tranquility. Please respect all Spa guests’ right to privacy and serenity. In light of this, The Spa & Wellness Center is a cellphone and smoke-free zone. Our Thermal Experience facilities are separate for male and female clients. Therefore, the use of swim wear is optional and of personal preference. We invite hotel guests to arrive at The Spa wearing their provided robe and slippers. Guests must be 18 years of age or older to use The Spa, unless accompanied by a parent or guardian.
