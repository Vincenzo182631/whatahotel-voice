### Make it Pink!

For the fifth consecutive year, Royal Champagne is turning pink to support breast cancer research and raise awareness of the importance of screening.

**A symbolic creation**

For Pink October, our Head Bartender has created _Make It Pink_, a fresh and fruity cocktail specially crafted for the occasion.

**A fund-raising for research**

The hotel is joining forces to raise funds for LISE, the Champagne Breast Institute, an association providing personalised support to breast cancer patients throughout their journey, from screening to post-treatment care.
