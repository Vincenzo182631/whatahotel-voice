Located amid the buzz of downtown Seville, the striking minimalism of the rooms is in lovely juxtaposition to the splendor of the public space with its marble staircase and ornate stained glass dome. Perhaps the crowning touch is the terraced hot tub from which to gaze at the rooftops and history of the city.
