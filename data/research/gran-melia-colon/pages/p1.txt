## An iconic hotel that reflects both the culture and the essence of Seville

Seville brings together Andalusian culture, flavor, and folklore. This 5-star Gran Lujo hotel is located just a few steps from the main attractions, such as the cathedral, and the most popular leisure spots. An icon of the city that perfectly combines modernity and tradition in an avant-garde style that can only be found in the Andalusian capital..

Located in a neoclassical building under a modernist dome that is the epicenter and heart of the hotel, Hotel Colón Gran Meliá is an icon of Andalusian art and culture. Elegance, warmth, and avant-garde design come together to create an irreplaceable contrast between modernity and tradition in a space that can boast of having been the favorite place of renowned artists since it opened for the 1929 Universal Exposition. This hotel is a member of The Leading Hotels of the World.

A place to immerse yourself in the history of Seville with the assistance of historical works that reflect the culture and art of the city. Each of the shelves is dedicated to a specific part of its traditions, such as bullfighting, the religious brotherhoods of Seville or the history of the Hotel Colón Gran Meliá.

### Historical hotel

Since the hotel opened for the World’s Fair in 1929, Hotel Colón Gran Meliá has been a key emblem of the city, and is now considered part of its heritage. A popular haunt for bull-fighters, artists and celebrities, our hotel stands out for its elegance and distinction.

### Leisure and fun

If you fancy an afternoon of shopping, we make it easy for you: We are located just a few minutes from Sierpes Street, one of the main shopping areas in the city. An area full of great shops and urban atmosphere. Isla Mágica, an iconic theme park located in the old Expo 92 facilities, is just a short walk from the Hotel Colón Gran Meliá.
