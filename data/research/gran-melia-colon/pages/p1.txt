## Hotel Colon, A Gran Melia Hotel - The Leading Hotels Of The World

ex. Hotel Colon Gran Melia - The Leading Hotels Of The World

Offering spacious rooms and Wi-Fi in public areas, the 30-storey Hotel Colon, A Gran Melia Hotel - The Leading Hotels Of The World Seville is just 1.5 km from Barrio Santa Cruz and 14 minutes' walk from the Chapel of the Sailors. Located within reach of the Historic iron Puente de Isabel II Arch Bridge, the comfortable hotel with views of the cathedral is well-known for its amenities including a solarium, a hot tub, and a sauna.

The hotel lies in the very heart of Seville, in the Old town district, 5 minutes' stroll from the Seville Museum of Fine Arts. The Calle del Infierno street, 2.3 km away, is definitely a must-see for family tourists in Seville. Colon, A Gran Melia Hotel - The Leading Hotels Of The World Seville also takes you to the filled Las Duenas Palace, which is a 19-minute walk away. There is Campana bus stop just near this 5-star property.

Along with complimentary wireless internet and a flat-screen TV with satellite channels, the soundproof rooms offer coffee/tea making machines, and separate bathrooms with a bidet, a separate toilet, and a sauna. The romantic interior features soundproof windows, along with a sofa bed. Bathrooms inlude a bidet and a separate toilet, and such fine comforts as a hair dryer and a shower cap.

Colon, A Gran Melia Hotel - The Leading Hotels Of The World offers a kid-friendly buffet breakfast daily. Guests can relax near a swimming pool or savour delicious Mediterranean cuisine at La Abaceria del Colon restaurant. This elegant hotel has an à la carte restaurant for on-site dining with city views. The lounge bar is an inviting place for enjoying delicious drinks right at luxurious Colon, A Gran Melia Hotel - The Leading Hotels Of The World. You can visit a restaurant situated close by and taste European cuisine.
