## The fighting bull in its habitat

Discover the culture of bullfighting at Dehesa Majavieja, an exclusive experience behind closed doors, to learn how the fighting bull feels and lives in its natural habitat and in an exceptional setting on the estate of the legendary bullfighter Espartaco, a key figure in Andalusian culture and one of the bullfighters who has led the bullfighting ranks with eight bullfighting seasons.

You will tour the estate in a pick up truck, contemplating the bulls free in their habitat, to later enjoy an aperitif and lunch in the middle of nature with Andalusian natural produce and cuisine, together with the Espartaco family. The Dehesa Majavieja experience offers guests the opportunity to contemplate the technique known as "tentadero del toro", a unique opportunity to understand part of the Spanish tradition.

- Duration: Approx. 8 hours.
- Number of people: Up to 6 people.
- To be taken into account: It does not include transfer. (Offered for an extra charge)
- What is included?: Guided tour of the farm by pick up truck to see the bulls. Welcome aperitif and lunch with the Espartaco family. Tentadero of a cow.
