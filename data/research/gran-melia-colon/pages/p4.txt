### The Terrace

Nestled among the historic streets of Seville

El Burladero offers a charming terrace with a traditional al fresco dining experience. Savor a glass of local Rioja paired with the exquisite taste of oxtail, slow-cooked to perfection. Guests can soak up the local atmosphere in the heart of the historic center, accompanied by rich Andalusian flavors.
