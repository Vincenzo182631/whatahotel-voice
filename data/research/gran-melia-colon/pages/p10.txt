# Wine therapy

## Hotel Colón Gran Meliá, Sevilla Spain

,regionOfInterest=(1312.5,1750))

Connect with the destination during your stay at Hotel Colón Gran Meliá, Sevilla, Spain

## Let yourself be cared for

Treat yourself. Make time to take care of your body and soul. Quench your appetite with tasty and healthy products. Surrender to pure relaxation with a water circuit in the Arab baths and enjoy a relaxing treatment.

You will enjoy a delicious brunch at Naturalmente Real Food in the very heart of Santa Cruz. A beautiful space with a very neat Mediterranean aesthetic where over 75% of its products are of organic origin (CAAE certified). A tasty avocado and salmon toast, a yoghurt with cereals and nuts, a smoothie... All you need to begin a beautiful morning.

- Duration: Approx. 5,5 hours
- Number of people: Up to 2 people.
- To be taken into account: Reservation must be made at least 24 hours in advance.
- What is included?: Guide. Treatment at Aire with Ribera del Duero grape. Brunch.

,regionOfInterest=(1312.5,1750)),regionOfInterest=(1312.5,1750))

After brunch, the guide will accompany you to Aire Ancient baths, in the narrow Aire street in the Santa Cruz neighbourhood itself; an old Mudejar style palace house where you will begin your 3 hour wine therapy session.

This part of the experience consists of a thermal circuit in the Arab baths, flotation pool, body massage and antioxidant treatment thanks to the properties of red grapes, in the wine thermal bath, which is the source of a centenary Venetian well.
