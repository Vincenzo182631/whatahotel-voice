## Signature Dining Experiences

### Masterclass at Ascua

Step behind Ascua’s wood-fire grill for an exclusive, chef-led experience. Guests can select between:

- Tomahawk Masterclass: Learn to prepare and grill Stirling Ranges tomahawks, then enjoy your creation with signature accompaniments.
- Seafood Masterclass: Explore WA’s coastline flavours with Albany kingfish, Exmouth prawns, and Fremantle octopus, grilled to perfection and served with chef’s accompaniments.

Each two-hour experience includes recipes and a trio of Ascua’s signature sea salts to take home. Available Fridays 4:00 - 6:00 PM, minimum two guests. Advance bookings essential.

### Sounds of Friday at Loft Lounge & Bar

Experience the elegance of live music, expertly crafted cocktails, and gourmet dishes every Friday from 4:30 PM. Set against the backdrop of His Majesty’s Theatre and historic Hay Street, enjoy your evening on the terrace or at a comfortable lounge table beside the bar.

**Every Friday \| 4:30 – 7:30 PM**

### Ascua: Perth's Refined Wood-Fired Grill

Experience elemental and soulful dining. Our open kitchen showcases the theatre of a wood-fired grill, transforming the finest Western Australian dry-aged steaks and local seafood into bold, unforgettable flavours. An exceptional wine list of the region's best artisanal producers makes Ascua a culinary destination for the discerning palette.

### Celebrate Western Australian produce in style

Savour every moment at our celebrated dining destinations: the spirited and alfresco-friendly tapas bar of Heno & Rey, the theatre-style open-kitchen of Ascua delivering wood-fired steaks and fresh seafood, and the Loft Lounge & Bar with terrace views for cocktails into the evening. Each venue champions Western Australia’s finest produce.
