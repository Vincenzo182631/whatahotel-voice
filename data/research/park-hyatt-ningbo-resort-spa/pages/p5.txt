### Cannot live up to the Park Hyatt name

Park Hyatt is supposed to be the highest tier of the Hyatt chain. The hotel surrounding is quaint and classy. The Chinese gardens were exquisite and the view was breathtaking. However the rooms were terrible. The lamps in the room turned yellow, and they did not fix it even when they are slanted. The wooden furniture are already chipping on the edges. The curtains and textiles were full of dust mites that it triggered our sinus. The bedding are also not up to standard, not even as good as Marriott Courtyard. Apart from that, the bathroom was full of mould, there is even mould on the walls where we hang our towels.

When we feedbacked about the rooms, the manager offered to let us change our rooms. However when we came back to the lobby and the manager left for another meeting, the room change wasn't communicated to the other staff and they could not find any information on the system. The staff are trying to be helpful, however the wait for every requests was quite distressing.

The check-in experience was also bad. Despite booking directly from the World of Hyatt website, the staff could not find our bookings. From the side we can see that they are using a very old system that makes it hard for them to scroll through the bookings. When we offered to provide our booking number, they just ignored it and continue scrolling through the list. The check in process took us about half an hour, it was not productive.

My experience in Grand Hyatt was way better than this Park Hyatt. Which makes me question if it was worth the stay.

### Two day vacation

Park Hyatt Ningbo is right on the edge of Dongqian Lake. The hotel has its own pier and cruise ships. There are shuttle buses that shuttle through the lobby, parking lot, restaurant, and tea garden. The service is online and the rooms are good. There are not many varieties of breakfast, but the taste is okay. The Chinese restaurant is worth trying. The glutinous rice balls are very good.
