# Ningbo Resort and Spa Hotel at Dong Qian Lake \| Park Hyatt \| Hyatt

## Greater China’s first Park Hyatt Resort in Ningbo

Park Hyatt Ningbo Resort and Spa is situated on the banks of Dongqian Lake – which translates to ‘lake of money in the east’, one of the most scenic destinations in Ningbo, Zhejiang Province. It is just 2-½ hours’ drive from Shanghai, making it the perfect weekend retreat.

### Pets Are Welcome

Your traveling canine companions are welcome at Park Hyatt Ningbo Resort and Spa. Dogs must be housebroken and up to date on vaccinations. We reserve our garden-view rooms for your furry friends. In addition to the nightly pet fee, we charge a cleaning deposit of CNY 1,000 that will be refunded after the hotel confirms there’s no damage to the room.

## Things to Do

Explore this beautiful lake town when you stay at this luxury resort situated in the heart of Ningbo. Spend your day around Dongqian Lake: Cycle around the area, explore its many temples or sample local cuisine nearby. Visit Taogong Island, just 3 kilometers from the hotel, and tour its secret art museum. For book lovers, you won’t want to miss Ningbo’s coveted Tianyi Pavilion, considered one of the three oldest libraries in the world.
