## Austin, Texas

### Experience True Hospitality at our Texas Hill Country Resort

* * *

### Conde Nast Traveler Readers' Choice Award Winner 2026

* * *

Tucked into the Texas Hill Country just outside downtown Austin, Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa offers the perfect blend of relaxation and adventure. Set across 4,000 acres of scenic countryside, the resort features thoughtfully appointed guest rooms and suites, four championship golf courses, and the world-class Mokara Spa. Spend the day teeing off on award-winning courses, unwinding with rejuvenating spa treatments, or lounging poolside with sweeping hillside views. Savor bold Texas flavors across nine distinct dining destinations before heading into Austin to experience why it’s known as the “Live Music Capital of the World.” Whether you’re planning a weekend escape, golf getaway, or family vacation, unforgettable moments await in the heart of the Texas Hill Country.

## Traveler's Insights

### What are the property amenities at Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa?

Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa is home to world-class amenities including:

- Three pools, including an adults-only pool
- A full-service spa
- 72 holes of top-ranked golf
- Full-sized tennis courts
- A fully-equipped fitness center with Peloton bikes
- 76,192 square feet of meeting and event space
- Nine dining outlets - six full-service restaurants, a lobby bar, a coffee shop and a pool bar
- Self-guided nature trail
- Seasonal events
- Family-friendly resort activities
- Valet parking with a nightly charge

### What is the best area to stay in when visiting Austin?

Barton Creek is one of the best locations to stay in during your next visit to Austin, Texas. Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa is ideally located in the Texas Hill Country just outside the hustle and bustle of downtown Austin while still being easily accessible to the city and its surroundings. Discover the endless things to do in Austin and the surrounding areas.

### Where is Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa located?

Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa is located just outside of downtown Austin, situated on 4,000 wooded acres in the beautiful Texas Hill Country. The resort's physical address is 8212 Barton Club Drive Austin, Texas 78735.

### What is the closest airport to Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa?

Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa is located near the following airports:

- About 18 miles (30 minutes) from Austin-Bergstrom International Airport (AUS).
- About 82 miles (1.5 hours) from San Antonio International Airport (SAT).

### How much is parking at Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa?

##### Self Parking:

- Complimentary valet parking for Bob’s Steak & Chophouse, Blind Salamander Kitchen & Bar and Mokara Spa with valid receipt

##### Overnight Valet:

- $49 plus tax per night.

##### Day Valet:

- 0-4 hours - $15 plus tax
- 4-8 hours - $27 plus tax
- 8+ hours - $40 plus tax

### What time is check-in and check-out?

- Check-in is at 4 PM

- Check-out is at 11 AM

### Is the resort pet friendly?

Yes, Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa is a pet friendly hotel for animals under 60 pounds. There is a one-time non-refundable pet fee of $150 per reservation. Service animals accompanying guests with disabilities are always welcome and will not be required to pay the one-time pet charge (no documentation necessary). Read more of our resort policies.
