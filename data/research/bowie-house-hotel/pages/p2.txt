# Stay at Bowie House

## High West Charm. Luxe Sleeping Spaces.

Surpassing the basics of a typical stay, the guest rooms at Bowie House are imbued with the warm spirit of the West. From Studios overlooking our courtyards to gracious open floor plan Lofts and specialized Suites, all guest rooms feature luxe amenities like personalized bar carts, lounge spaces and original artwork that nod to Fort Worth’s Cultural District, located just outside the front door.

## Striking Accommodations

## Studios

A modern take on high Texas style, each Studio features a rich interior color palette and nods to Texas living with features like hat racks, boot benches, boot jacks and leather wrapped furniture. Enjoy views of the cultural district or sleep in our Balcony Studios to enjoy your morning coffee outdoors on your own private balcony. Accessible rooms available.

##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is Bowie House located?** Bowie House is located at 3700 Camp Bowie Boulevard in Fort Worth’s Cultural District, near the city’s renowned museums, Will Rogers Memorial Center and Dickies Arena. The Fort Worth Stockyards are approximately four miles away.

**How far is Bowie House from the airport?** Bowie House is approximately 28 miles, or 35-40 minutes, from Dallas Fort Worth International Airport. The hotel’s Itinerary Design team can also assist with arranging car or sedan service.

**Is Bowie House a good fit for couples?** Yes. Bowie House is ideal for a couples getaway in Fort Worth, with intimate dining and cocktails, restorative treatments for two at Ash Spa and private cabanas at the rooftop pool. Its Cultural District location also puts museums, galleries and some of the city’s best attractions within easy reach.

**When is the best time to visit Bowie House?** Bowie House is a year-round destination, with each season offering a different way to experience Fort Worth. Spring and fall are ideal for exploring the Cultural District and enjoying the hotel’s gardens and outdoor spaces, while summer invites leisurely days at the rooftop pool and cooler months bring a lively calendar of dining, arts and cultural experiences.

**Does Bowie House have a pool?** Yes. Bowie House has a second-floor outdoor terrace pool surrounded by garden views, with chaises, private cabanas and poolside dining and drinks from Whinny’s. The pool is open daily from 8AM to 8PM.

**Does Bowie House have a spa?** Yes. Ash, an Auberge Spa, is a full-service spa offering massages, facials and innovative wellness treatments alongside an infrared sauna, steam room and relaxation lounge. The spa is located on the hotel’s second floor and is available to guests ages 18 and older.
