## Treatments

Ash, an Auberge Spa, is a serene sanctuary on the hotel's second floor, inspired by the ubiquitous barns of Texas. We welcome our local neighbors and hotel guests to our luxury day spa, a tranquil space where the ashes of everyday life are gently swept away – only to reveal a revitalized version of your truest self. Sundry treatments, both traditional and non, impart a sense of balance. ## Curated Treatments

-

### Deep West

A treatment that releases head-to-toe deep-seated tension using acupressure, stretching, cupping, and heated pads. An integrated bodywork session that targets every body muscle, including the scalp and facial muscles. The treatment highlights the advanced techniques of the West.

-

### The Gold Buccal

This ultimate facial embraces the latest in advanced skincare techniques to rejuvenate your skin both biologically and aesthetically. An advanced sculpting buccal facial massage is performed to tone facial muscles and soften muscle tension in the upper chest, neck, head, face and buccal area. Precision positioning of the Qi Beauty matrix follows your buccal massage and elevates your results. The overall experience is deeply relaxing and soothing.

-

### Time Together

Enjoy the ultimate wellness experience for two in our beautiful couples' suite, featuring a private shower. Nourish your body, mind and spirit and find deeper connection with yourself and each other. Enjoy champagne and chef’s chocolates together afterward in our Relaxation Lounge.
