# Dine at Bowie HouseSpirited Connections Through Food

## A luxurious spin on Texan cuisine

Indulge in a stiff whiskey cocktail and a perfectly cooked, dry-aged rib eye. Dining at Bowie House is inspired by the rich ranch culture and agricultural bounty of the West. Join us for a quick breakfast, poolside cocktail, business lunch or Texas-sized bar bites; our locally-sourced meat and produce are some of the best around, so pull up a chair.

## Bricks and Horses

Down-home Texas cuisine meets city sophistication at our signature indoor-outdoor restaurant, Bricks and Horses. ## More Dining Venues

-

### The Bar at Bowie House

Transforming from an ideal place for a morning coffee into a relaxed setting to wile the day away, The Bar at Bowie House centers Fort Worth’s bustling social scene. If you’re a whiskey fan, we’ve got a vast selection of the best; martinis are expertly crafted, and flavorsome snacks to munch on while you imbibe.

  - Monday-Sunday \| 11AM - 12AM
  - Daily House Social \| 4-6PM

-

### The Yard

The Yard is Bowie House's vibrant outdoor social hub and the perfect spot for guests and locals to unwind and connect.

-

### Mulberry Room

Sit back, and relax with a glass of bourbon in your hand. The Mulberry Room is Bowie House’s cozy library, a perennial place to engage in deep conversation over drinks and snacks. Also, this is a place for learning as libraries inherently are—visit us for monthly gatherings and lessons or teach yourself more about bourbon with our vast collection.

-

### Billet Room

Become part of the club. This is a buzzy space to unwind. Kick back, play a game or two, and enjoy bespoke cocktails, beef jerky, kettle corn and sharing bites at the ready. Join us weekly to compete in your favorite game leagues whether it’s a few rounds of cowboy bingo, Texas hold em’, or an exhilarating billiards tournament grab your friends and come on in.

-

### Whinny’s

Imagine sitting poolside, savoring the ideal menu for warm-weather snacking – light salads, crudités, ice cream sandwiches and frozen drinks. Welcome to Whinny’s, Bowie House's poolside restaurant and bar. Whether you are enjoying our terrace pool or relaxing in a cabana beneath the Texas sun, enjoy the perfect refreshing menu.

## Meet the Chef

Executive Chef Antonio Votta developed his passion for cuisine in his formative years, graduating from the prestigious hospitality school at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas. Chef Votta honed his craft working under acclaimed culinary visionaries in San Francisco and training with Michelin star groups, such as M.B. Post, and the MINA Group. He has dedicated years to developing recipes through ever-evolving menus, sourcing only the finest ingredients from each region. Today, Chef Votta is carving his niche in Fort Worth’s thriving culinary scene. He is establishing his heritage in Texas by creating a menu that honors the bounty of the land and local purveyors across the entire Lone Star State. Using artful creativity and his entrepreneurial spirit, Chef Votta's method to this new epicurean institution is both refined and imaginative.
