# An imaginative and bold urban retreat, where cowboys and cattle once roamed

## Two MICHELIN Key Hotel – MICHELIN Guide

## The home for the new West

Merging the classic Western personality of Texas with a decidedly modern feel, Bowie House, Auberge Collection is the only luxury hotel that embodies the unique character of Fort Worth. Our urban retreat captures the approachable sophistication of the city, infusing long-standing traditions with a globally-inspired sensibility that welcomes everyone. Travelers and locals alike are invited to explore the rich culture of this part of town, visiting museums and experiencing a buzzing equestrian scene that harkens to the days when cowboys and cattle once roamed the area. Inviting communal spaces, epicurean dining and social activities abound onsite and make the property an exciting place to gather – come any time; our door is always open.

## Wellbeing at Bowie House

Ash, an Auberge Spa, is a serene sanctuary on the hotel's second floor, inspired by the ubiquitous barns of Texas. We welcome our local neighbors and hotel guests to our luxury day spa, a tranquil space where the ashes of everyday life are gently swept away – only to reveal a revitalized version of your truest self. Sundry treatments, both traditional and non, impart a sense of balance. Non-sleep deep rest massage, made-to-measure apothecary and curated infrared therapy are just a few options to cultivate a lasting sense of harmony and inner radiance post-visit.
