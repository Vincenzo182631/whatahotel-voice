**A coastal haven where Bali's spirit unravels**

# REGENT BALI CANGGU

On Canggu Beach, surfers are silhouetted in the morning sun, boards under arms ready to take on Bali's famous reef breaks. Watching the waves, listening to the call of the Indian Ocean, you might catch the scent of incense or the melody of the gamelan emitting from Pura Batu Mejan, a centuries old sea temple set on rock as black as jet. A blessed way to begin each day.

## REGENT CLUB

A sanctuary reserved for those who seek time, space, and effortless privilege. At the Regent Club, days unfold with quiet abundance. Guests enjoy refined all-day culinary offerings and a carefully curated selection of premium wines and spirits, accompanied by afternoon tea rituals and evening cocktails served with champagne.

You are invited to join intimate cultural experiences, relax within serene adults-only cabanas, and unwind by the private infinity pool, a peaceful setting designed for moments of stillness and unhurried comfort.

**An Oasis of Gastronomy**

## RESTAURANTS & DINING

Wake up to the soft glow of Balinese sunlight, the sound of the Indian Ocean breaking in the distance, and the scent of tropical flowers in the air from the comfort of your meticulously designed suite or villa.

### CURE bali

A laidback take on European dining, gently shaped by Asian flavours. Set by the Canggu coast, it's guided by simplicity, good food, and easy evenings from sunset onward.

Tuesday to Saturday 5:00 pm - 12:00 am

Discover CURE

### Sazón

Led by award-winning Chef Andrew Walsh, experience the neighborhood Mediterranean hideaway on Batu Bolong, where vibrant atmosphere meets authentic flavours.

Mon - Thurs 06.00 pm - 12.00 am

Fri - Sun 12.00 pm

Discover Sazón

### Beach House

A stunning addition to the Canggu coastline, the Beach House lures with its shimmering pool, and ocean breeze. An ideal setting for craft cocktails and the delicate flavours of the sea.

Open 08:00 am - 09:00 pm every day.

Discover Beach House

### Taru

The signature culinary destination of the resort, Taru offers an immersive dining experience, through reinvented authentic flavours of essential Indonesian dishes, innovated by touches of modern cooking techniques and ingredients, balanced with contrasting international specialties.

Open 07.00 am - 10.00 pm every day.

Discover Taru
