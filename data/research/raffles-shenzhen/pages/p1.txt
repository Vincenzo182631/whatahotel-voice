## Frequently asked questions

### What amenities does Raffles Shenzhen offer?

Raffles Shenzhen features luxurious accommodations, a world-class spa, and gourmet dining options to enhance your stay.

### How can I book a stay at Raffles Shenzhen?

You can easily book your stay online or contact the hotel directly at (86) 755 8666 8666 for assistance.

### What attractions are near Raffles Shenzhen?

Explore the vibrant Nanshan District, Shenzhen Bay Park, and the bustling shopping areas just a short distance from the hotel.

### When is the best time to visit Shenzhen?

The best time to visit Shenzhen is during the spring and autumn months when the weather is mild and pleasant for outdoor activities.

### Why choose Raffles Shenzhen for your stay?

Choose Raffles Shenzhen for its unparalleled luxury, exceptional service, and prime location that offers easy access to the city's attractions.
