# Solage, Auberge Collection

View Photos

Modern mountain-framed escape.

ShareSave

Framed by the nearby Vaca and Mayacamas Mountains, Solage, Auberge Collection offers a modern starting point for an exploration of Napa Valley. Within walking distance of downtown Calistoga, the resort mixes small-town charm with urban sophistication.

Insider Tip

Don’t miss out on participating in the Art of Salsa Making experience at Picobar.

Where You’re Staying

Each studio features earth friendly interiors and vaulted ceilings that echo Napa Valley’s local vineyards. Suites include separate living areas and extended private patios.

Where to Dine

Solbar serves Napa Valley cuisine with an international twist. Dishes are made with farm-fresh seasonal ingredients. Enjoy modern Mexican cuisine in an open-air, poolside setting at Picobar.

Where to Relax

Spa Solage offers a new twist on traditional Calistoga mud and mineral water therapies. Enjoy the Solage Signature Mudslide, a three-part detoxifying treatment that includes mineral-enriched mud, geothermal mineral waters, and state-of-the-art sound chairs.

What to Do

Walk to downtown Calistoga to explore the town’s art galleries and restaurants. Learn about the area’s history at the Sharpsteen Museum. Take one of the resort’s cruiser bikes for a tour through the local vineyards and wineries.
