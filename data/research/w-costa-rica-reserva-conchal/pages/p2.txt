## Accommodations

Bold design meets natural wonders in each of our 150 rooms and suites, boasting sweeping views of the Pacific Ocean and Playa Conchal. Every airy space is decked out with a private balcony, marble bathroom, and a MixBar. Check into a Superior Suite to hide out among the mangroves, or spread out in a Premier Suite for epic ocean views from your own personal plunge pool. Channeling local culture and...See More surf vibes, our Playa Conchal, Costa Rica hotel rooms and suites remix the local spirit with details like hand-painted indigenous patterns, nature-inspired headboards, and surfboard bar tops.See Less

### Our Residences

Suspended above the treetops with panoramic Pacific Ocean views, our Private 3-, 4-, and 5-Bedroom Residences offer an elevated escape where bold design meets effortless luxury. Featuring expansive open-plan living, private outdoor pools, and the signature service and world-class amenities of W Costa Rica, every stay is uniquely yours. Curate unforgettable moments with exclusive in-residence experiences, including private chef dining, hands-on cooking classes, personalized yoga sessions, and bespoke wellness experiences.
