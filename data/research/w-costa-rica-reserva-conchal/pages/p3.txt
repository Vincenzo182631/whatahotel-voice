# Guanacaste resort restaurants

## Dining

From vibrant market flavors to trending mixology, W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal is all about fresh ingredients and bold flavors. Start your day in Guanacaste with a homemade local breakfast and fresh juice at Cocina de Mercado. Soak up the sun on Playa Conchal, then eat something light poolside on the WET® Deck or beachfront at Zona Azul. Gear up for an evening out with modern cocktails and small...See More bites in the Living Room. Or make a night of it at Latitud 10° Norte, serving up modern, elevated tasting menus. Each of our resort restaurants in Guanacaste reinterprets classics with a modern edge, remixing Latin with international inspiration.See Less

## Poolside Bliss

Light, nourishing bites keep it easy, so you can linger longer by the pool. Pair fresh-pressed juices with handcrafted tropical cocktails—WET® Deck has just what you're craving.

## The Pulse of the Escape

A vibrant social hub, the Living Room is where every evening begins. Settle in with a handcrafted cocktail while taking in a sunset that needs no filter. Enjoy Costa Rican bocas before dinner or keep the night going with shareable bites. Signature mixology, local beats and curated DJ sets create an atmosphere that evolves from laid-back afternoons to unforgettable nights. Lounge seating invites you to relax, connect and soak in the unmistakable spirit of W.
