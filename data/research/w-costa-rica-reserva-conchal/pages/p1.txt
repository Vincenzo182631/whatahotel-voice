# Welcome to W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal

## Indulge in the vibrant life of our Guanacaste luxury hotel

Immersed in the lush landscapes of Guanacaste, W Costa Rica – Reserva Conchal is a vibrant retreat where nature, wellbeing and bold design come together. Surrounded by the biodiversity of Reserva Conchal and the sugar-white sands of Playa Conchal, the resort invites you to slow down and experience Costa Rica at its best. Inspired by the vibrant culture of Guanacaste, our luxury hotel reimagines modern...See More luxury with a playful, conscious spirit. Wake to sweeping reserve views from plush bedding before slipping into your suite’s private plunge pool. Spend the day golfing, kayaking, exploring nature or soaking up the sun, then retreat to AWAY® Spa for locally inspired treatments designed to restore and recharge. As the sun sets, savor bold flavors at our restaurants before discovering fresh beats from our Resident DJ in the Living Room. From mindful moments in nature to vibrant nights, experience wellbeing, connection and the unmistakable energy of W at our Reserva Conchal resort in Costa Rica.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal?

The check-in time at W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal?

On-site parking is available at W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal booking.

What is the closest airport near W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal?

The closest airport near W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal is Tamarindo Airport (TNO). TNO is located approximately 16.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
