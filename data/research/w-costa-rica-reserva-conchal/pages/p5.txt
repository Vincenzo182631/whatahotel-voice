# Holiday Events at W Costa Rica

## We All Made the List

Make the most of it by spending quality time with your favorite people and immersing yourselves in unique moments at our extraordinary property.
Prepare to leave behind the ordinary and embark on a celebration journey at W Costa Rica. We have curated a range of activities to commemorate an unforgettable season of events and experiences.
See you at W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal

### Festive Events

### Taste

From Asian cuisine to trending mixology, W Costa Rica - Reserva Conchal is all about fresh ingredients and bold flavors. Each of our resort restaurants in Guanacaste reinterprets classics with a modern edge, remixing Latin with international inspiration.

### Sound

Throughout November and December, guests will enjoy an exciting lineup featuring acclaimed local artists including Sebas Guillen, Kurt Dyer, Editus, Barock, and DJ Fabian Rois with his signature all-vinyl sets, alongside many other talented musicians.

### Body

Elevate your energy and find your rhythm with daily body programming designed to keep you moving, balanced, and fully immersed in the W Costa Rica lifestyle. From beach yoga to high-energy aquatic workouts, each session is crafted to fuel your body and awaken your senses.

### Away Specials

Discover seasonal wellness experiences designed to help you relax, restore, and reconnect. From poolside treatments and mindful moments to celebratory wellness rituals, AWAY® Spa invites you to indulge in a playful approach to self-care.

### Scene

Discover the vibrant surroundings of Reserva Conchal through guided outdoor experiences that blend adventure, wellness, and the thrill of exploration.

### Stance

Experience the local scene through artisan-led workshops and vibrant market experiences, bringing the creativity and culture of Costa Rica closer to your stay.

### Marriott Bonvoy Elite Specials

This season, dining at W Costa Rica is all about bold tastes and celebratory that Made our List. Our chefs remix Latin roots with global inspiration, bringing seasonal ingredients and modern flair to every dish. From lively brunches to crafted cocktails and shareable dinners, each venue amps up the festive spirit with flavor, style, and a hint of mischief.

### Families

Celebrate the season with family-friendly adventures filled with creativity, discovery, and holiday cheer. Whether decorating treats, joining a treasure hunt, or enjoying a movie under the stars, there is something for every member of the family to enjoy.
