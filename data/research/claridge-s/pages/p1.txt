## Claridge's

### DISCOVER

Claridge’s, in the heart of Mayfair, is shaped by centuries of enduring artistry - a rich symphony of experience, where guests are warmly cared for and celebration unfolds with unforgettable flair.

For more than two hundred years, Claridge’s has been chosen for life’s finest moments. In doing so, each guest becomes part of its brilliant and ever-evolving legacy.

## Mayfair

### In The Heart Of

Since its founding in 1812, Claridge’s has stood as a beacon of elegance in the heart of Mayfair. Nestled among the city’s grand streets and historic squares, it overlooks the vibrant pulse of London while offering an unforgettable setting for every moment - whether celebrating, gathering, or simply experiencing the city in all its glory.
