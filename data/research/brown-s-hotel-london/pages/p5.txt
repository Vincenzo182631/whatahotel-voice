- Brown s Hotel

### Welcome to our cherished corner of Mayfair. We call it Charlie’s, after Lord Charles Forte, our patriarch and patron diner

## Our Story

We also call it classic British with a modern twist. That means stalwarts venison en croute and roast beef, gliding by on a gilded trolley, carved tableside. It means evolving with the seasons: local ingredients, hand-sourced by us. Crisp Cornish crab salad; sage-roasted summer squash. Nods to Italian, Spanish and French. A menu by Michelin-starred Adam Byatt, whose grandfather worked here too.

## Chinese Breakfast

by A.Wong

Explore China through a special breakfast menu by Andrew Wong, the chef behind London’s renowned two-Michelin-starred restaurant, A.Wong. Returning after his successful residency at the Donovan Bar, he brings China’s rich and varied breakfast traditions to Charlie’s. Enjoy a different start the day, with dim sum filled with dressed crab, wok-fried noodles and seasonal fruit with chilli-lime salt.
