# The Drawing Room at Brown's Hotel: Afternoon Tea, Breakfast and Dinner in Mayfair

## Our    Story

From its nineteenth-century beginnings to the icon of quintessential London it is today, our Drawing Room has witnessed the passage of history’s most illustrious figures. Lords and Ladies, politicians and presidents, royals and the Hollywood elite, have woven their stories into this elegant room, whose original wood-panelling is as polished now as it was then.

### Afternoon Tea

There are Afternoon Teas, and there are Afternoon Teas fit for a queen. Our quintessential London tea room has hosted one or two. Settle into a cosy corner and take some time to enjoy neatly crimped sandwiches, the daintiest of pastries, and pillowy scones topped with fresh cream and jam - all paired with a bespoke blend from our tea library.

our Afternoon Tea

#### Every Thursday

### Drawing Room After Dark

Every Thursday evening, our historic Drawing Room transforms into a secluded London speakeasy where the city’s leading jazz musicians connect with their audience in bespoke performances. Unwind with a cocktail and enjoy the rich, enveloping sounds.

Discover What's On

### Breakfast in the Drawing Room

Start your day with the warm reception our hotel’s founder Sarah Brown set the standard for in 1832. Our team will be on hand with a smile and a hot coffee as you peruse our menu. Breakfast in Mayfair is an art form - will it be _Adam’s brûlée porridge with tea-soaked prunes and evaporated milk_ or _Smoked back bacon sandwich with Brown’s sauce_?
