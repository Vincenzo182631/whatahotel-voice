## Welcome to Explora Torres del Paine

With an unrivaled location in Torres del Paine National Park, our award-winning lodge features stunning lakefront views and a striking design, meticulously integrated into this natural landscape, known for its towering granite peaks, golden grasslands, ancient forests, and glaciers. At Explora Torres del Paine, discover a singular launching point for adventure and over 40 guided Explorations, all included as part of your stay. Your all-inclusive experience continues back at the lodge, complete with a spa, beautiful accommodations, and every meal featuring locally inspired cuisine.

Torres del Paine

Discover Unique Explorations

At Explora Torres del Paine hotel, discover more than 40 expertly guided Explorations designed for small groups. These unique experiences span everything from the iconic to Explora-only, and feature Torres del Paine’s famed Towers, glaciers, lakes, and Patagonian forests, as well as wildlife and gaucho culture. Guests also enjoy access to Explora’s own horse stable as well as horseback riding and equine Explorations with our own rare breed of Patagonian horses.

Explorations are included as part of your stay, customized to accommodate your interest and skill level, and led by experts trained exclusively at Explora’s own School of Guides.

FAQs

- Is Explora Torres del Paine all-inclusive?

Yes. Every Explora stay in Torres del Paine is fully all-inclusive. Your rate covers all accommodations, unlimited guided Explorations, all meals and beverages including Chilean wines and spirits, spa facility access, national park entrance fees, and round-trip transfers between Puerto Natales and the lodge. There are no supplementary charges for activities or guided experiences during your stay

- What is included in an Explora Torres del Paine stay?

Your stay includes accommodations in a 50-room lodge on the shores of Pehoé Lake, access to more than 40 guided Explorations — from treks to the iconic Towers to kayaking, horseback riding, and glacier hikes — all meals prepared by Explora’s culinary team, all beverages including wine, spa and pool access, national park entrance fees, and transfers. The only extras are personal purchases and optional spa treatments beyond the included facility access.

- How do I get to Explora Torres del Paine from Puerto Natales?

Explora Torres del Paine is located approximately 112 kilometers (70 miles) north of Puerto Natales along Ruta 9 and the Sarmiento road into Torres del Paine National Park. The drive takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours through Patagonian steppe and grasslands. Round-trip transfers between Puerto Natales and the lodge are included in every all-inclusive stay. Guests typically fly into Punta Arenas International Airport (PUQ), then travel to Puerto Natales by shuttle or private transfer before continuing to the lodge.

- What kinds of guided Explorations are available at Explora Torres del Paine?

Explora Torres del Paine offers more than 40 guided Explorations, all included in your stay. Options include day treks to the Base of the Towers (Las Torres), hikes to Grey Glacier, kayaking on the park’s lakes, birdwatching for condors and flamingos, horseback riding with a rare breed of Patagonian horses on Explora’s own reserve, and gaucho cultural experiences. Every Exploration is led by guides trained at Explora’s own School of Guides and customized to your fitness level and interests.

- What is the best time to visit Torres del Paine?

The main trekking and expedition season in Torres del Paine runs from October through April (Southern Hemisphere spring through early fall). November to February offers the longest daylight hours and most stable weather, though wind is a constant factor year-round in Patagonia. Explora operates the lodge year-round; shoulder seasons (October–November and March–April) offer fewer visitors and lower rates while still providing full access to guided Explorations.

- Is it better to stay inside Torres del Paine National Park or in Puerto Natales?

For travelers who want to maximize time in the park, staying inside is the better option. Puerto Natales is approximately 112 kilometers from the park entrance — a 1.5 to 2-hour drive each way — meaning a full day of exploration is lost for every night based in the city. Explora Torres del Paine is located inside the park on the shores of Pehoé Lake, with guided Explorations departing directly from the lodge each morning. Puerto Natales makes sense as a base for travelers on a tight budget or those doing a single day visit; for an all-inclusive expedition stay focused on depth of experience, the park lodge is the right choice.

- Is Explora Torres del Paine suitable for non-hikers?

Yes. While the park is most famous for multi-day trekking routes like the W Trek, Explora’s program of 40+ guided Explorations goes well beyond hiking. Options for guests who prefer lower-intensity or non-hiking experiences include horseback riding on Explora’s private Conservation Reserve, kayaking on the park’s lakes, wildlife watching for pumas, condors, and guanacos, boat excursions to Grey Glacier, and gaucho cultural experiences. Explorations are matched to each guest’s interests and fitness level daily — a non-hiker can have a full, varied stay without setting foot on a technical trail.

- Does Explora Torres del Paine have a spa?

Yes. Explora Torres del Paine has a full-service spa and wellness area that includes a heated indoor pool, steam sauna, and open-air hot tubs with views across the national park. Spa facilities are included in the all-inclusive rate. Individual spa treatments are available to book separately and carry an additional charge. Appointments can be arranged at the lodge during your stay.
