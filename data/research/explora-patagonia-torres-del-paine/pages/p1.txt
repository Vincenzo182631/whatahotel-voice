Torres del Paine

Discover Our Lodge in Torres del Paine

Set on the shores of Lake Pehoé in Torres del Paine National Park – the very heart of Chilean Patagonia and a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve — Explora’s 50-room lodge is emblematic of this very special region. Step inside to experience the luxury of the essential with ideal amenities, an emphasis on comfort and relaxation, and a thoughtful design meant to connect you to Torres del Paine’s most awe-inspiring landscapes. Guests also enjoy complimentary transfers across the lake aboard Explora’s own ferry.

Much of the beauty of our luxury Torres del Paine lodge lies just outside your door. Award-Winning Design

Constructed by renowned Chilean architect José Cruz Ovalle, Explora Torres del Paine is an inspired wilderness lodge designed to connect you to the mysteries of nature. We do this with intention, prioritizing the luxury of the essential so that you may experience the essence of this remote Patagonian location without distraction. Cypress, lenga, and almendrillo woods play a fundamental role in the design of this unique lodge, considered to be the crown jewel of all Torres del Paine lodging options.

Our Stables

Discover our equestrian culture in Torres del Paine. Explore these landscapes on horseback, accompanied by our gauchos and riders, crossing pampas, rivers and trails. An authentic and privileged experience that lets you live Patagonia from a singular perspective.

Get to know our horses up close — how they are handled and their particular way of relating — and share with our gauchos the traditions and knowledge that are part of life in Patagonia.
