Best for:
Solo sojourns, suite-seeking small groups, and families who want London’s landmarks at their doorstep
Location:
Perfectly poised between buzzy Knightsbridge and the charming, Georgian residential houses of Belgravia
Can’t miss feature:
In the summer: the rooftop pool (notably, London’s first ever); In the winter: the hotel’s wellness sanctuary, Surrenne Belgravia
In a nutshell:
A classic, 125-year-old heritage hotel, masterfully made modern with carefully considered collaborations, a standout spa, and delightful details.
Louis A. W. Sheridan
Rooms
Stepping into a room at The Berkeley feels like the first sip of its famed afternoon tea—calming, refreshing, and instantly soothing.
There’s a spaciousness to the rooms that is very hard to find in such a central London location, with a contemporary East-meets-West aesthetic. Across 190 rooms and suites, the common design themes are: inviting soft furnishings, teak accents, striking modern art, park views, and generous Italian marble bathrooms.
The crown jewel? The penthouses. Highlights include the André Fu–designed Opus Suite (a sprawling two-bedroom showstopper), the Hyde Park Penthouse with firepit and panoramic park views, and the Grand Terrace Suite overlooking St Paul’s Church.
Food and drink
La Môme, the legendary Cannes restaurant, arrived here in early 2025, bringing Riviera glamour and joie de vivre with it. Expect dishes like truffled sea bass crudo marinated in passionfruit, a meltingly tender Chateaubriand, and a signature French chocolate meringue dessert by the name of ‘Le Concorde’. The La Môme experience is completed with nightly live music and a Gatsby-era interior humming with energy.
Come morning, make a beeline for pastries by renowned chef Cédric Grolet–from cake-sized cinnamon rolls to sculptural waffle flowers and fresh-out-the-oven viennoiseries.
Louis A. W. Sheridan
Louis A. W. Sheridan
Amenities and experiences
The spa is exceptional. Spanning across four floors, guests can experience a wealth of wellness, from Tracy Anderson classes to bespoke facials in partnership with Dr. Lara Devgan or Dr. Macrene skincare.
Elsewhere: a light-filled pool with underwater meditation soundscapes, sessions with FaceGym, longevity smoothies by Rose Ferguson, and even a medical-grade Hyperbaric Oxygen Chamber.
What to do nearby
Some of London’s most enjoyable experiences are just a walk away. On a sunny day, take a stroll through the neighboring Green Park or Hyde Park for boat rides across the Serpentine. Shoppers should head to Harrods and Harvey Nichols, where they will undoubtedly lose hours and suitcase space.
Or for a slice of culture, pop into the V&A and National History Museum followed by a show at the Royal Albert Hall.
Insider tip
You don’t need a room key for a taste of Berkeley magic. Book The Snug in the downstairs bar for intimate private gatherings, complete with its own sound system, call-for-service button, and a mural by New York artist TM Davy, celebrating female friendship and conversation.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Yes
Accessibility:
There’s a step-free entrance, accessible rooms for guests with disabilities, and step-free access to public areas.
