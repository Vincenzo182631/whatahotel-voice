## The Berkeley, Perfectly Styled for Autumn

Nestled between Belgravia, Knightsbridge and a golden Hyde Park, The Berkeley is perfectly styled for autumn in London. Open the door to a beautiful collection of suites and rooms, each the work of an acclaimed designer, before stepping out to landmark vistas, where sweeping terraces offer front-row views of the season's changing colours. Home to Cedric Grolet, our world-renowned pâtissier-in-residence, along with sense-stirring mixology, access to Surrenne Belgravia and two sublime pools, The Berkeley offers a stylish retreat as the season unfolds.

## Our Suite Inclusions
