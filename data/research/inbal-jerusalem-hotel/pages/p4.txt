# The Inbal. Where Jerusalem Begins.

Indulgence  \|  Culinary  \|  Journey  \|  Tradition

The Inbal is more than a luxurious hotel – it is where Jerusalem begins. A true reflection of the Golden City, The Inbal captures the timeless beauty and vibrant spirit of Jerusalem, inspiring guests to forge a deep connection with its unique essence.

Blending modern luxury with the city’s ancient charm, the hotel offers a wealth of experiences that are uniquely Jerusalem. Enjoy opulent elegance in our new room collection and indulge in modern, authentic culinary delights that celebrate local flavors and cherished traditions- a taste of history brought to life.

Every detail, from the design to the exceptional service, creates a harmonious balance of tradition and sophistication, ensuring an experience that is as luxurious as it is unforgettable.

Indulgence

With our new room collection, The Inbal offers elegant comfort and warm, modern design. Our premium amenities and services create a stay that is both serene and indulgent in the vibrant heart of Jerusalem.

The Inbal Spa provides a tranquil retreat with luxurious treatments and a heated pool, open year-round – a peaceful oasis amidst the city’s lively energy.

Culinary

Renowned for its exceptional cuisine, The Inbal Hotel is a culinary landmark in Jerusalem. The experience begins with our famous breakfast buffet – a celebration of fresh, local flavors. The sophisticated creations of our signature 02 chef restaurant further elevate the hotel’s culinary excellence, capturing the essence of authentic fine dining.

The Inbal is also celebrated for its innovative pop-up dining experiences, offering guests exciting and unique culinary adventures. Adding to its vibrant culinary scene, the hotel’s Bar 22 presents a refined lounge experience, where guests can indulge in world-class spirits alongside chef-crafted dishes in an elegant, charming setting.

Journey

Ideally located, The Inbal offers easy access to Jerusalem’s historic and cultural treasures. Our concierge team is here to elevate your visit and help you fully experience the city. We offer exclusive insights into Jerusalem’s cultural, historical, and religious sites, along with recommendations for top restaurants. Let us guide you to the city’s best entertainment destinations, family-friendly activities, and iconic events.

Tradition

At The Inbal, heritage and elegance come together in our signature traditional events. From the serenity of Shabbat to the warmth of Friday night dinners, each occasion is steeped in meaning. The Passover Haggadah comes to life, bringing history and tradition to the table. Celebrate Sukkot beneath a beautifully designed modern Sukkah, where past and present meet.

Whether you’re hosting a luxurious Bar or Bat Mitzvah, an elegant wedding, or a cherished family milestone, The Inbal is your ideal Jerusalem venue – where tradition is not just honored, it is truly lived.
