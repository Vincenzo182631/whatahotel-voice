## The Inbal Spa

### Enriched by AHAVA

The Inbal Spa pampers guests with a full array of expert massage therapies and body treatments. ## Swimming Pool

Our semi-Olympic heated lap pool and spacious sundeck are a year-round delight. Heated to a comfortable 29°C (84°F), the pool is opened to the skies in summer and fully covered in winter. A separate toddler pool is also available, and a pool lift ensures accessibility for guests with disabilities. Framed by a lush border of tall, green pines, the pool offers a serene retreat in the heart of Jerusalem.

## Winter

In preparation for the winter season, the entire spa area, including the pool, wardrobes, spa treatment rooms, sauna, and gym, will be closed from October 18 to October 22, 2026, and will reopen for full operation on October 23, 2026.

## Fitness Center

The Inbal’s state-of-the-art Fitness Center offers everything you need for a serious workout. Designed to help you reach your full fitness potential, the center features the latest Technogym equipment, including treadmills, recline bicycles, elliptical cross trainers, abdominal and strength-training stations, Smith and Lat machines, chest presses, Ercolina cable stations, leg extension and curl machines, and a full range of free weights. Each machine is equipped with a personal built-in TV screen for your convenience.

**The Fitness Center is open 24/7, exclusively for hotel guests.**
