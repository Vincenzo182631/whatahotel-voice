# Rooms & Suites

The Inbal’s new room collection blends elegant luxury with contemporary, warm design. Enhanced by state-of-the-art amenities and our exceptional service, your stay will be luxuriant and serene –in the heart of the thriving, bustling city of Jerusalem.

Our wide range of accommodations is designed for both couples and families, offering everything from spacious family rooms and convenient connecting options to premium rooms and suites with exclusive access to our Executive Lounge. Select rooms and suites also feature private balconies overlooking the magical Old City walls.

Choose your ideal haven from our thoughtfully designed suites and guest rooms – each combining comfort, warmth, and refined elegance, ensuring a truly exceptional Jerusalem experience.
