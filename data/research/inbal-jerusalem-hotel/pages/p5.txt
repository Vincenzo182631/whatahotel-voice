# Dining

## Bar 22

Behind a beautifully crafted wooden door awaits Bar 22, The Inbal’s distinctive bar in the heart of Jerusalem.

Intimate yet vibrant, it offers original cocktails alongside classics, a selection of whisky and gin, and curated wines and beers.

Chef Kfir Hadad’s menu brings Jerusalem flavors with a modern twist. With elegant design and a warm atmosphere, it is perfect for a relaxed evening, Happy Hour, or private events and cocktail workshops.

## Shabbat Meals

Renowned as an experience that is uniquely Jerusalem, Shabbat meals at The Inbal are rich, festive, and filled with a special sense of warmth and tradition.

Our Shabbat menu features beloved traditional dishes from Jewish communities around the world – including braised beef, goulash, roast chicken, Moroccan fish, and the traditional _cholent_ (stew) – complemented by a delightful selection of local specialties, international favorites, and irresistible desserts.

Shabbat meals at The Inbal are served on Friday evening and again at Saturday afternoon lunch.

## Pool Bar

Whether you’re in the mood for a light bite or something more substantial, the Pool Bar, open during the summer months, offers a wide range of options to satisfy every craving.

For a refreshing poolside pick-me-up, enjoy a full menu of fresh salads, crispy pizzas, warm focaccias, pastas, quiches, and perfectly seasoned home fries. And for the ultimate summer treat, savor ice cream, chilled watermelon, and our signature iced coffee. A full selection of hot beverages, soft drinks, cocktails, beer, and wine is also available.

## The Soup Festival

**Winter in Jerusalem may be cold and rainy, but it’s always warm and welcoming at The Inbal!**

Join us for The Inbal’s beloved Soup Festival – a long-standing winter tradition featuring a tempting array of rich, creamy soups celebrating the season’s finest flavors. This all-you-can-eat soup buffet is complemented by an array of freshly baked breads, dips, croutons, cheeses, herbs, and a variety of flavorful toppings.

The Soup Festival takes place throughout the winter months, Sunday to Thursday, from 12:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. Reserve in advance via the hotel’s website.
