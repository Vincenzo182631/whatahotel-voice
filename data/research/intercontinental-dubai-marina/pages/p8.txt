# InterContinental Dubai MarinaDestination

InterContinental Dubai Marina is in the heart of the marina, walking distance from the Jumeirah Beach, Dubai Marina Mall, and the Dubai Tram. You're in the center of it all, close to Burj Khalifa, Palm Jumeirah, Expo City Dubai, and more.

## Things to do

### Burj Khalifa

Downtown Dubai, Dubai,

The tallest skyscraper in the world! Enjoy the scenic view from At The Top, the observation deck on the 124th floor. The elevator ride is one of the fastest in the world. Don't leave Dubai without seeing The Fountain. Evening demonstrations are performed every 30 minutes from 6:00PM to 11:00PM, offering breathtaking light and water shows accompanied by pieces of music from around the world.

Learn more

### The Dubai Creek

Deira, Dubai,

The heart of the old city! This is where it all began. The picturesque area offers a glimpse of an undisturbed past: merchant wooden dhows load and unload their stock and small traditional abras can get you from one creek side to the other for just two dirham.

Learn more

### Emirates Golf Club

Emirates Hills 2, Dubai,

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced player, this club located in the heart of the city offers some of the best golf greens in the city. And if you are busy during the day, you can still practice and play at night.

Learn more

### Jumeirah Beach Residence

Jumeirah Beach Residence, Dubai,

Sandy beach, creative restaurants and shopping. The Walk and the Beach on Jumeirah Beach Residence have it all and they are just a short stroll from our hotel

Learn more
