## FAQs for InterContinental Dubai Marina

Does InterContinental Dubai Marina have a pool?

InterContinental Dubai Marina has 1 outdoor pool.

Urban Lounge

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Dubai Marina?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Dubai Marina for hotel guests.

Basement parking is available round the clock. A complimentary valet parking service is also available for guests.

In and out privileges

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Dubai Marina?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Dubai Marina.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Dubai Marina for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Dubai Marina have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Dubai Marina has 5 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Dubai Marina?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 11:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Dubai Marina?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Dubai Marina.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Dubai Marina?

Check-in at InterContinental Dubai Marina is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Dubai Marina offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Dubai Marina offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Dubai Marina from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Dubai Marina is conveniently located near Dubai International Airport (DXB), approximately 25.6 miles away (41.2 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Dubai Marina?

InterContinental Dubai Marina is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Burj Khalifa - 14.17 miles away (22.8 kilometers)
- The Dubai Creek - 15.91 miles away (25.6 kilometers)
- Emirates Golf Club - 3.73 miles away (6 kilometers)
- Jumeirah Beach Residence - 0.68 miles away (1.1 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Dubai Marina puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
