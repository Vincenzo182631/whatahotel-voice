## Discover the pinnacle of luxury at Andaz Lisbon

Exquisitely positioned at Praça do Comércio, at the very heart of Lisbon, Andaz Lisbon captures the essence of the city’s storied past. More than merely a hotel, Andaz Lisbon serves as a gateway to the essence of Lisbon, inviting guests to discover its hidden tales and dynamic contemporary pulse. Once home to a medley of residences and financial institutions, this architectural gem now unites centuries of history under one roof. Andaz Lisbon beckons you to immerse yourself in the city's labyrinthine streets, vibrant cultural tapestry, and daring gastronomic delights. Whether it's art, music, history or the local vibe, Andaz Lisbon is a sanctuary for the curious, those in search of depth, connection, and transformative discovery - So Get Lost and find your Lisbon.

## Explore Our Hotel

Delve into the Neighborhood

Andaz Lisbon is nestled in the heart of the dynamic Baixa district, a lively area renowned for its majestic squares, historic landmarks, and vibrant shopping streets. A short stroll away, the bohemian enclaves of Chiado and Bairro Alto invite you to discover a world of art galleries, cultural treasures, and eclectic dining options. For a deeper immersion into Lisbon’s traditional spirit, venture into Alfama, the city's oldest quarter. Wander through its enchanting, labyrinthine streets, experience the soulful resonance of Fado music, and visit the iconic São João Castle, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the city. Whether you're seeking to delve into Lisbon’s rich history, vibrant culture, or exquisite gastronomy, these neighborhoods provide an abundance of experiences, all just moments from Andaz Lisbon.

### Luzzi: A Culinary Journey at Andaz Lisbon

Perched atop the Andaz Lisbon, Luzzi is the newest hotspot in town, featuring two distinct spaces: our chic terrace lounge and stylish restaurant.
Showcasing modern Lusitanian cuisine in a sleek, contemporary setting, Luzzi is a fusion of travel, exploration, and over 500 years of Portugal’s vibrant history and cultural influence. From Goa and Macau to São Paulo, the menu reflects Portugal’s far-reaching impact on both local and global culinary traditions.
Immerse yourself in the authentic Lisboeta vibe, mingling with locals while savoring expertly crafted cocktails. Where the city's timeless charm meets the glow of twilight, accompanied by live music that lingers into the night—this chic retreat offers the perfect escape.
