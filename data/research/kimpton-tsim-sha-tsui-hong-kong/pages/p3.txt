# Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong KongEat + Drink

Savor the unexpected with five one-of-a-kind dining experiences that marry bold innovation with genuine authenticity. From vibrant flavors to comforting classics, each bite is served with a side of Kimpton’s signature style and warmth.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

international

## Birdsong

Sip exclusive Elephant Grounds coffee and savor house-fermented delights and artisanal sandwiches in a chic, relaxed setting.

international

## Hillside

Satisfy morning cravings with an all star breakfast buffet by day, savor modern dai pai dong dining with bold twists on Chinese classics by night.

international

## Swim Club

Indulge in exotic, playful cocktails crafted with innovative techniques and infused with local flavors on our 50th-floor poolside oasis.

chinese

## JIJA

An independent Chinese bistro inspired by Yunnan‑Guizhou flavors, led by Michelin‑starred Chef Vicky Lau and Leading Nation Hospitality.

## Restaurant + bar highlights

### Birdsong

A barista-driven, third-wave space featuring Elephant Grounds’ single-origin beans, delivering bold, nuanced flavors with every pour. Paired with daily handcrafted pastries from the in-house bakery. This inviting space is perfect for savoring exceptional coffee and artisanal baked go.

### Swim Club

This poolside paradise invites guests to indulge in exotic, playful cocktails crafted with innovative techniques and infused with local flavors as they lounge inside the gorgeous bar or out on the terrace.

### Hillside
