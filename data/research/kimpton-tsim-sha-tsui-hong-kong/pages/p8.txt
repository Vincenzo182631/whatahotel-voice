# Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong KongLocation

Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong is a "culture of time" that blends modern interpretations of local heritage with multicultural cues. Nestled along the iconic Victoria Harbour, this 495 rooms haven invites guests to connect with the vibrant neighborhood while enjoying unparalleled luxury. Highlights include five restaurant and bar outlets, a 50th floor heated rooftop pool, a spacious wellness center, and elegant harbor view accommodations.

## Things to do

### Hong Kong Space Museum

10 Salisbury Road, Tsim Sha Tsui,

Embark on a journey through the cosmos at the Hong Kong Space Museum, where interactive exhibits and the immersive Space Theatre ignite curiosity and inspiration. Uniting wonder with discovery, this captivating experience brings the magic of the Universe to life for explorers of all ages.

Learn more

### Hong Kong Cultural Centre

10 Salisbury Road, Tsim Sha Tsui,

Perched on the iconic Kowloon waterfront, the Hong Kong Cultural Centre is a beacon of world-class artistry and innovation. With state-of-the-art facilities, it plays host to an eclectic mix of performances—from captivating concerts and operas to grand dance productions, experimental theatre, and refined musicals.

Learn more

### Former Kowloon Canton Railway Clock Tower

10 Salisbury Road, Tsim Sha Tsui,

Standing tall in Tsim Sha Tsui, the Clock Tower is an iconic Hong Kong landmark that beautifully weaves the city’s rich history with its modern allure. A symbol of timeless heritage, it also offers one of the best vantage points to take in the breathtaking harbour views, making it a must-visit destination for locals and travelers alike.

Learn more

### Hong Kong Museum of Art

The Hong Kong Museum of Art celebrates the city’s vibrant cultural heritage. Home to an impressive collection of over 19,500 artworks and collaborations with renowned art institutions, it offers an inspiring journey through creativity, history, and innovation.

Learn more

### Avenue of Stars

Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront,

Set against the stunning backdrop of Victoria Harbour, the Avenue of Stars is a tribute to the legends of the region’s film industry. This iconic destination seamlessly blends cinematic history with breathtaking views, offering visitors an unforgettable experience that celebrates the artistry and glamour of Hong Kong’s cinematic legacy.

Learn more

### Xiqu Centre

88 Austin Road West, Tsim Sha Tsui,

A masterpiece of modern design and cultural heritage, the Xiqu Centre is Hong Kong’s premier destination for Cantonese opera and traditional Chinese theatre. Featuring two state-of-the-art theatres, it offers an immersive experience where timeless artistry meets contemporary innovation, celebrating the rich legacy of xiqu in a truly captivating setting.

Learn more

### Temple Street Night Market

Temple Street, Jordan,

Radiating with vibrant energy, Temple Street has been reimagined as the ultimate destination for Hong Kong’s iconic nightlife. Bustling with captivating night vibes, its lively street scene is complemented by irresistible roadside food stalls serving up classic Hong Kong snacks and unique specialty finds.

### Hong Kong M+ Museum

38 Museum Drive, West Kowloon Cultural District,

Located in the West Kowloon Cultural District with sweeping views of Victoria Harbour, M+ is Asia’s first global museum of contemporary visual culture. Showcasing diverse collections spanning visual art, design, architecture, and moving image, M+ offers an immersive creative experience. With 33 galleries, an LED-lit tower facade, rooftop views, dining, and shopping, it’s a must-visit for art and culture enthusiasts.

Learn more
