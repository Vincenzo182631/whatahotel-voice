Located in the heart of Tsim Sha Tsui, Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong puts you steps from Victoria Harbour, the Star Ferry, and the lively Avenue of Stars. Enjoy easy access to the MTR for seamless city exploration.

Discover iconic attractions like Harbour City, M+ Museum, the Hong Kong Museum of Art, and the bustling Temple Street Night Market all nearby. Kowloon Park and major shopping streets are just minutes away.

Relax in stylish rooms with stunning harbour views. Enjoy the rooftop pool, 24-hour fitness center, five unique restaurants, and the luxury Spa. Experience daily Social Hour and seamless service throughout your stay.

## FAQs for Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong

Does Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong have a pool?

Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong has 1 outdoor pool.

Rooftop Pool

Soak in breathtaking views of Victoria Harbour and Hong Kong’s iconic skyline from the hotel’s heated rooftop infinity pool. Surrounded by plush sunbeds and private cabanas, this elevated oasis offers a serene escape.

Pool information

How much is parking at Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong?

Our hotel offers 21 indoor parking spaces, including 16 electric vehicle charging docks. Valet parking is also available for your convenience. Please note that fees apply for all parking services.

Parking & transportation details

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong?

Check-in at Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong is conveniently located near Hong Kong International Airport (HKG), approximately 20.82 miles away (33.5 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong?

Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Hong Kong Space Museum -1.12 miles away (1.8 kilometers)
- Hong Kong Cultural Centre -1.12 miles away (1.8 kilometers)
- Former Kowloon Canton Railway Clock Tower-1.12 miles away (1.8 kilometers)
- Hong Kong Museum of Art-0.93 miles away (1.5 kilometers)
- Avenue of Stars-0.56 miles away (0.9 kilometers)
- Xiqu Centre-0.93 miles away (1.5 kilometers)
- Temple Street Night Market-1.49 miles away (2.4 kilometers)
- Hong Kong M+ Museum-1.62 miles away (2.6 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Tsim Sha Tsui Hong Kong puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
