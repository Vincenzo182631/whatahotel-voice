### Frequently Asked Questions

Useful details you need to know before you travel to One&Only Cape Town

#### What are the passport, visa and vaccination requirements for South Africa?

All persons are required to present a valid passport in order to enter South Africa.

Passports must be valid for no less than 30 days after the end of your visit. An entry visa

may be required by certain nationalities or in certain circumstances. For more up-to-date

information on visa requirements, please click here.

#### What is the weather like in South Africa?

Cape Town enjoys a four season Mediterranean climate with mild winters and warm summers: September–February (Spring/Summer) has an average temperature of 20°C/68°F; March–August (Autumn/Winter) has an average temperature of 17°C/63°F.

#### What languages are spoken in South Africa?

English is the primary language spoken in One&Only Cape Town, followed closely by Afrikaans.

#### What is the local currency in South Africa?

South African Rand is the official currency of South Africa.

#### What internet services are available at One&Only Cape Town?

Unlimited complimentary WiFi access is available throughout the resort.

#### What is the time zone in South Africa?

Cape Town, South Africa is two (2) hours ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT +2).

#### What is the electrical voltage at One&Only Cape Town?

Electricity is supplied at 220 volts. Adaptors are available.

#### What are the check-in and check-out times at One&Only Cape Town?

Check-in is 2pm and check-out is 11am.
