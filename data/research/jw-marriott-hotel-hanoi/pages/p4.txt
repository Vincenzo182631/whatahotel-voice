### World of Fun for Children and Families

Each day unfolds with moments designed to inspire curiosity and quiet joy for all ages. Spark curiosity with a visit to the JW Lakeside Gardens, where children engage with friendly animals, creating memorable and nature friendly experiences. Our children corner at Kids Café by JW also provide variety of activities as Cookie Decoration, Statue Painting, Paper Origami, Game Room and much more to discover.

Book Activity

### Vietnamese Coffee Making Class

Brewing and drinking Vietnamese coffee is a leisurely and social experience. If you're a coffee enthusiast, immerse yourself in the art of Vietnamese coffee, where each cup tells a story of tradition and connection. Guided through the nuances of local beans and the timeless ritual of brewing with a 'phin' filter, you’ll discover a slower, more mindful way to savor coffee.

Book Activity

### Cooking Class With Our Signature Herb

During your stay at our Hanoi luxury spa hotel, you can participate in Cooking Class at JW Lakeside Gardens. Guided by our chefs, you'll learn to make some Vietnamese dishes by using the fresh, home-growth herbs from our gardens.

Book Activity

### Yoga Class on Weekend

Join us for Yoga Class in Hanoi at our hotel and spa's lakeside garden setting or Wellbeing on 8. Our instructor will guide you through basic to advanced stretching and breathing exercises to bring relaxation to your entire body and mind.

### JW Greenhouse

It’s a home of several plants, grown with our love for nature and treasure for sustainability. Local herbs are taken care of with organic fertilizers from grounded oyster shells to preserve the soil quality. Absorbing nutrition, plants are fresh for different drinks and dishes served at our restaurants and bars. From the garden to your table, a sustainable journey makes an experience at the hotel meaningful!

### Discover Hanoi, Refined by the Luxury of JW Marriott Hanoi

At JW Marriott Hanoi, every journey is designed to be extraordinary. From sacred cultural landmarks nearby to effortless connections to the city’s vibrant heart, discover Hanoi with the comfort and sophistication you deserve.

## Temple Tour

Nestled just moments from our lakeside hotel near Hanoi, the revered Miếu Đầm Temple offers a timeless escape into Hanoi’s spiritual heritage. Known for its sacred rituals and serene ambiance, the temple provides a rare opportunity to immerse yourself in the city’s cultural soul. Allow us to guide you on a journey of discovery, where tradition and tranquility create an unforgettable experience, perfectly complementing the elegance of your stay.

Book Your Tour

## Complimentary Shuttle Service

Exploring the capital from our Hanoi hotel near the convention center has never been more effortless. With our complimentary shuttle service to Hanoi’s city center, guests enjoy seamless access to the iconic Old Quarter, Hoàn Kiếm Lake, and the city’s finest shopping and dining. Return at your leisure to the serene luxury of JW Marriott Hanoi, where every detail is thoughtfully curated for your comfort.
