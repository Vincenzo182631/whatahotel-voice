### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Las Vegas?

The check-in time at W Las Vegas is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Las Vegas allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Las Vegas. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at W Las Vegas?

On-site parking is available at W Las Vegas. Self parking is available for $20.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $40.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near W Las Vegas?

The closest airport near W Las Vegas is Harry Reid International Airport (LAS). LAS is located approximately 2.5 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Las Vegas have an airport shuttle to Harry Reid International Airport (LAS)?

No, W Las Vegas does not have an airport shuttle to Harry Reid International Airport.

Does W Las Vegas have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Las Vegas has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
