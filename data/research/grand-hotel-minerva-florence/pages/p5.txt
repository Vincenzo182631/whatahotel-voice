## Historic Luxury Hotel in the Florence Historic Center

**Grand Hotel Minerva is a 4-star superior luxury hotel in the Florence historic center, located directly in Piazza Santa Maria Novella.** The building began as housing for the monks of Santa Maria Novella over seven-hundred-years ago and served as home to Florentine aristocratic families before opening as an inn for travelers in 1848 and transforming into a grand hotel in 1958. Situated in a pedestrianized piazza within the UNESCO World Heritage site, the property is located **350 metres from Santa Maria Novella station** and a **6-minute walk from the Duomo**.

### Key Property Highlights

- **Location:** P.za di Santa Maria Novella, 16 in the Florence historic center.
- **Heritage Recognition:** Certified member of **Locali Storici d'Italia** and featured in the 2025 Michelin Guide.
- **Architecture:** 1958 transformation by architects Edoardo Detti and Maestro Carlo Scarpa.
- **Distances:** 350 metres from the train station and a 6-minute walk from the Duomo.
- **Rooftop Amenities:** Heated pool and panoramic terrace at the Three Sixty Rooftop Bar.

## Carlo Scarpa Architecture and Design Legacy

**In 1958, Maestro Carlo Scarpa and Edoardo Detti transformed Grand Hotel Minerva into a symbol of Italian modernist architecture.** While deconstructing the property, Scarpa preserved original 14th-century frescoes and hand-painted ancient wooden beams in select rooms, combining traditional crafts with modern manufacturing processes.

A defining architectural element is the customized window frame designed by Carlo Scarpa in 1958, identical to the frames he created for the Uffizi Gallery. This custom detail physically links the hotel's design to Florence's historic art spaces.

# “IT’S A SHOWPLACE OF ART, DESIGN AND THE MAGIC OF ITALIAN CREATIVITY”

An authentic experience in Florence requires a full immersion into the beauty of Italian creativity.

Grand Hotel Minerva has been a hub of art for generations.

The building began as housing for the monks of Santa Maria Novella over seven-hundred-years ago and was home to various aristocrat Florentine families through the centuries. Hospitality came around in 1848, when the palazzo was converted into an “Inn” for travelers and finally transformed into a Grand Hotel in 1958, by two important Italian architects: Edoardo Detti and _Maestro_ Carlo Scarpa.

In the fifties, when “modern innovation” was conceived as the destruction of the past, Grand Hotel Minerva was lucky enough to be put in the hands of an artist such as Carlo Scarpa. The _Maestro_, as he was known, deconstructed and transformed the hotel, leaving unaltered only a few rooms, with beautiful frescoes and original hand-painted ancient wooden beams that still exist today. While transforming the hotel, Scarpa created spaces and design details that would become a symbol of Italian modernist architecture.

In time, the cultural soul of the hotel has bloomed into a vast collection that spaces from modernist design elements, to futuristic sketches, to ancient paintings and iconic sculptures. Around the rooms you will find many original pieces – some of them were born here, others have been brought in in some moment in time we have lost track of, others were hand-picked or donated. Different time periods, different social eras, every piece telling a part of the story.
