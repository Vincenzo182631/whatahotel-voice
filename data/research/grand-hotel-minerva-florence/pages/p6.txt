Grand Hotel Minerva

Reviewed by
Nicky Swallow
Best for:
A cultural weekend; fans of Carlo Scarpa and mid-century design; families
Location:
The hotel stands on Piazza Santa Maria Novella, steps from Florence’s main train station and the tram stop for Peretola airport- The designer stores in Via Tornabuoni are a short stroll away.
Can’t-miss feature:
The rooftop pool's city views
In a nutshell
: This four-star favorite offers a heated rooftop pool with 360-degree views of Florence’s skyline and Art Deco-influenced rooms in a stately 13th-century palazzo that was designed by modern genius Carlo Scarpa in the 1950s. The address is perfect; it stands on lively, pedestrian-only Piazza Santa Maria Novella with its magnificent swirling green and white-fronted church. And, on a practical note, it’s within minutes walk of both the train station and tram stop for Peretola airport.
Rooms
The 97 rooms and suites are spread over five floors; around 50% offer views of the piazza and the swirling facade—designed by star Renaissance architect Alberti—of the church of Santa Maria Novella. The look is safe and pleasant, the neutral colour palette broken with pops of cherry red and pistachio green. Expect warm wood floors, the odd antique piece mixed in with modern furnishings and touch of retro styling. Even the entry level rooms are a decent size for Florence. The contemporary marble bathrooms are supplied with local perfume guru Lorenzo Villoresi’s classy products.
Food and drink
Rooftop lounge bar La Terrazza is one of the best places for a light lunch or cocktails on a sultry summer evening, and it has many local fans although the pool and bar are reserved for hotel guests until 7.30pm. Pizza is served up here in the evenings too. On street level, with a summer terrace on the piazza, is La Buona Novella, the hotel’s gourmet options where chef Edoardo Papiani whips up elevated versions of regional classics; beef tartare, tomato bruschetta with candied capers, ravioli cacio e pepe, grilled sea bass and bistecca alla Fiorentina.
The excellent buffet breakfast is served in a time capsule of a room; Scarpa’s ground floor space—pale sea green with a jazzy monochrome floor and his original chairs—has been left virtually untouched
Amenities and experiences
The USP here is the rooftop pool and terrace, a true rarity in Florence. Get up early and do your sightseeing before the crowds move in, then come back to the hotel and spend the rest of the day lounging. There is a small gym with a whirlpool too. Off-site activities include leather workshops with a local artisan, pizza-making classes and a perfume experience creating personal fragrances with celebrated Florentine perfumier Aquaflor. I highly recommend booking an exclusive dawn visit to the Duomo and Baptistery.
What to do nearby
The hotel is located in the very centre of Florence, so just about everything you will want to do and see is within walking distance. The Uffizi Gallery, the Duomo, the Accademia, the church of Santa Maria Novella, the Bargello museum; it’s all on the doorstep. Then there are the chic designer boutiques of Via Tornabuoni, the independent shops and artisan studios of the Oltrarno and the foodie paradise of the San Lorenzo market. There will not be enough time for everything. And last, but very much not least, it is just round the corner from the wonderful Officina Profumo-Farmaceutica di Santa Maria Novella
The backstory
Once part of the convent complex of Santa Maria Novella, the Minerva was a pilgrim’s hostel before becoming one of Florence’s earliest grand hotels. It was completely redesigned by Venetian architect and interior designer Carlo Scarpa in the 1950s, and his imprint is still very much in evidence today.
Insider tip
Fans of mid-century design will love what remains of Scarpa’s original input; the staircases and stairwells and the wonderful retro breakfast room.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
children are welcome and will be gifted with welcome packs on arrival. There are plenty of connecting rooms.
Accessibility:
there are six adapted rooms with adapted bathrooms and the hotel is fully accessible.
