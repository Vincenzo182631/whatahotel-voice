## Luxury Accommodations with Views of the Historic Center in Florence

Grand Hotel Minerva is a 4-star superior luxury hotel in Florence featuring 97 guest rooms and luxury suites located in the pedestrian-only Piazza di Santa Maria Novella. Situated in the UNESCO historic center, the property provides direct views of the historic center, framing the Florence Duomo, Giotto’s Bell Tower, and the cloisters of Santa Maria Novella through soundproofed windows.

### Room and Suite Configurations

The hotel offers five accommodation categories tailored to couples, individual travelers, and multi-generational families:

## Historic Architecture and Signature Suites

Set in a historic 14th-century building, Grand Hotel Minerva combines curated Italian art-design interiors with Florentine heritage. Unique Rooms such as Suite 103 feature preserved 18th-century frescoes and high ceilings, providing historic architecture alongside modern luxury amenities.

### Landmark Views from Guest Accommodations

Accommodations including Deluxe View Rooms and the Suite Privilege Collection offer direct perspectives of key landmarks across the Florence skyline:

- **Florence Duomo:** Direct views framing the iconic cathedral architecture.
- **Giotto’s Bell Tower:** Clear sightlines over the historic center.
- **Cloisters of Santa Maria Novella:** Frontal views looking directly toward the historic cloisters.

## Hotel Services and Pedestrian Accessibility

Guests staying in all 97 rooms and suites have access to on-site amenities and transit connections:

- **Free High-Speed Fibre Wi-Fi:** Accessible in all guest rooms and public areas.
- **Panoramic Rooftop Pool:** Seasonal heated terrace pool offering 360-degree views of the Florence historic center.
- **Fitness Centre:** Complimentary access for all hotel guests.
- **Arrival Location:** Situated 350 metres from Firenze SMN Train Station (a 4-minute walk) and 500 metres from the Duomo (a 6-minute walk), with valet parking available in the car-free pedestrian zone.

### What guest rooms and luxury suites are available at Grand Hotel Minerva?

Grand Hotel Minerva offers 97 guest rooms and luxury suites ranging from 18 m² contemporary spaces to expansive 50 m² suites featuring original 18th-century frescoes. Every accommodation is soundproofed and equipped with free high-speed fibre Wi-Fi, curated Italian art, and access to the panoramic rooftop pool.

### Do rooms at Grand Hotel Minerva provide views of the historic center?

Yes, many accommodations at Grand Hotel Minerva provide views of the historic center, framing direct views of the Florence Duomo, Giotto’s Bell Tower, or the cloisters of Santa Maria Novella through large, soundproofed windows.

### How does Grand Hotel Minerva accommodate families and multi-generational travelers?

Grand Hotel Minerva accommodates multi-generational travelers with connecting rooms and specialized duplex layouts measuring 45 to 60 m² that accommodate up to 5 guests. The property also provides soundproofed family suites, dedicated children's kits, welcome gifts, and a specialized Kids' Concierge.

### Which accommodations at Grand Hotel Minerva feature original 18th-century frescoes?

The Unique Rooms at Grand Hotel Minerva, including Suite 103, feature perfectly preserved 18th-century frescoes and soaring high ceilings, blending historical Florentine heritage with luxury art-design interiors.

### How close is Grand Hotel Minerva to the Florence Santa Maria Novella train station?

Grand Hotel Minerva is situated 350 metres from the Firenze SMN Train Station (approximately a 4-minute walk) and 500 metres from the Florence Duomo, located directly in the pedestrian-only Piazza di Santa Maria Novella.

### What services and amenities are included with rooms at Grand Hotel Minerva?

Every room at Grand Hotel Minerva includes free high-speed fibre Wi-Fi, individual climate control, soundproofing, and full access to the seasonal heated rooftop pool and fitness centre, with valet parking and in-room dining from La Buona Novella also available.
