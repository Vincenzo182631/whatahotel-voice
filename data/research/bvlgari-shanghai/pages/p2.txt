## The Bvlgari Bar

Previous

Next

Sharing the 47th floor with Il Ristorante – Niko Romito, The Bvlgari Bar shimmers like a jewel against the panoramic backdrop of the Bund riverfront and Pudong skyline. The Bvlgari Bar’s centrepiece oval counter, inspired by Rome’s famed Fontana della Barcaccia, has become an icon of the Bvlgari Hotels collection and is reimagined in each property with different materials.

For Shanghai, the bar was made from hand-hammered bronze topped with mirrored steel, recalling the expert craftsmanship of the Roman jeweller. Its water-like texture references Suhe Creek visible through the windows. Before or after dinner, guests can repose in style courtesy of sleek chrome and black leather furnishings. An even more dramatic panorama awaits on the private outdoor terrace, celebrating sumptuous Shanghai views in the open air.

## The APERITIVO

The Bvlgari Bar’s menu mixes classic Italian cocktails, fine wines, teas and snacks throughout the day and night. The Bvlgari _Aperitivo,_ made famous by the Milan property, is one of the most cherished Italian traditions and a unique experience to try at The Bvlgari Hotel Shanghai. At sunset hours, every drink ordered is paired with fresh canapés, enjoyed either at the oval bar or on the outdoor terrace overlooking the river.
