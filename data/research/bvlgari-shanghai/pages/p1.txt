## BVLGARI HOTEL SHANGHAI

Located on the Suhe Creek waterfront, Bvlgari Hotel Shanghai forms part of the SUHE CREEK project, a riverside urban revitalization comprising residential, retail and art spaces, as well as parks.

Balancing nature, contemporary design and heritage architecture, the property combines a 48-storey tower with the majestically restored former Chamber of Commerce Shanghai building dating from 1916, surrounded by lush gardens.

Bvlgari Hotel Shanghai is home to 82 exquisitely crafted guestrooms and suites, as well as Bvlgari Hotels' signature lifestyle and dining experiences, including The Bvlgari Spa, Il Ristorante - Niko Romito, The Bvlgari Bar, La Terrazza, Bvlgari Dolci, and Bao Li Xuan, the Chinese restaurant specializing in Guangdong cuisine.
