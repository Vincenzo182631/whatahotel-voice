# An elevated experience above Times Square

The Times Square EDITION introduces the next generation of modern luxury. Here, urban energy fuels an enchanting escape as sweeping views of the New York City skyline transport you to an oasis above the bustle. Featuring 452 guest rooms, a state-of-the-art fitness center, performance venue, and three distinct dining experiences developed by Michelin-starred Chef John Fraser, The Times Square EDITION punctuates the area as the city’s epicenter and crossroads of the world… again.

Dining & Entertainment

## Culinary Genius in Times Square

The Times Square EDITION has partnered with Michelin-starred chef John Fraser to helm all of the food and beverage outlets at the area’s first luxury hotel. The hotel offers three distinct dining experiences with indoor and outdoor spaces to enjoy the finest in one of New York City’s most iconic neighborhoods. Elegantly positioned above the bustle, the property features an all-day modern brasserie set within an all-seasons garden, a tranquil lobby bar with craft cocktails and light bites, and a high-production nightlife and cabaret venue with weekend programming.
