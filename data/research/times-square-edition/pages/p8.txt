# Services & Amenities

The Times Square EDITION creates a unique, one-of-a-kind experience together with the best and most personalized modern service available.

### For further information

Phone +1 212 398 7017 – Email: gem.tmsq@editionhotels.com

Guest Services

- 452 unique rooms and suites
- One 1,300 square-foot Penthouse
- Signature restaurant, The Terrace and Outdoor Gardens, with chef partner Michelin-starred Chef John Fraser. Open breakfast, lunch, dinner
- Lobby Bar, serving coffee, cocktails, and light bites open daily
- Paradise Club nightlife programming every Friday and Saturday
- Customized breakfast, brunch, lunch, and dinner menus for private events
- Room Service
- Concierge Service
- Complimentary use of the 24-hour fitness facilities
- Complimentary high-speed wireless internet access throughout the hotel
- State-of-the-art audio and visual services available for private functions
- Multi-lingual staff

Preorder Amenities

- Should you wish to further enhance your visit, amenities are available for purchase: The Times Square EDITION

Executive Services

- Private Event Space
- Video Conferencing
- Executive car services available
- State-of-the-art audio/visual equipment
- Complimentary high-speed wireless Internet access throughout the hotel
- Smart boards and electronic flip charts available
- iPads, laptops, Polycom, and LED screens available
- 24-hour multi-lingual secretarial staff available
- Built-in junket floors to accommodate press junkets, television and movie filming

Fitness Center

- State-of-the-art fitness equipment including cardiovascular machines with personal flat screen TVs
- Personal training sessions available on request
- Open air wellness deck
- Free weights
- Stretching area
- Complimentary towels
- Headphones and refreshments
- Accessible 24 hours a day
