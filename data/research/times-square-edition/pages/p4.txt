# Lobby Bar

## Lobby Bar

Lobby Bar features an unfussy and elegant approach to bar fare, taking it to a new level. All-white accent furniture including custom bleached leather armchairs and ivory hand- tufted silk area rugs offer luxurious respite from the bustling scene outside.
