Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel is a sleek, contemporary landmark inspired by the elements and overlooking the waterfront of Grand Canal Square. This luxury hotel in Dublin is in an ideal position for exploring the city’s main tourist attractions and its main business and financial district. It is conveniently close to the IFSC and the Convention Centre Dublin.

Part of the regenerated Docklands that exemplify the city’s cosmopolitan future, the hotel is ideal for both business and leisure guests. The area is home to tech and finance giants, chic restaurants and world-class theatres.

The hotel is close to the National Museum, the Science Museum, the National Gallery, the Bord Gáis Energy Theatre, the 3Arena and the Aviva Stadium. Additionally, it is within walking distance of College Green plaza and the Grafton Street shopping hub.

The restaurant showcases the best of modern Irish cuisine, while the rooftop bar & terrace draws crowds from across the city with its 360˚ views of the mountains and sea. Hide away in the cocoon spa with award-winning treatments, an indoor pool and extensive wet facilities, named Dublin Spa of the Year by Irish Tatler.

Frequently Asked Questions

**When can I check in and out of Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel?**

The check-in time at Anantara The Marker, a 5-star hotel in Dublin, is 3:00 pm while the check-out time is 12.00 pm.

**Can I check in early to Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel?**

The official check-in time is 3.00 pm. However, should the room not be ready, we can store any luggage and you will still have complimentary use of the Spa and Wellness Centre. The Spa and Wellness Centre includes an infinity pool, sauna, jacuzzi and eucalyptus infused steam room along with showers, lockers and changing facilities. The Marker Bar & Lounge will be available as well for drinks, tea, coffee or lunch.

**What are the closest points of interest to Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel?**

This luxury hotel in Dublin has a prime location in the revamped Docklands. It overlooks the waterfront of Grand Canal Square and is conveniently located near main points of interest such as the 3Arena, Bord Gáis Energy Theatre and the Grafton Street shopping hub.

**Is parking available at Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel?**

Yes, we are pleased to offer premium parking options at Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel. Guests may choose from:

- Self-Parking – €32 per night: Enjoy convenient access to your vehicle at your leisure, with secure, well-maintained facilities.

- Valet Parking – €43 per night: Relax and allow our expert valet team to take care of your vehicle from the moment you arrive.

Please note that all parking services are operated off-site in collaboration with a trusted local partner and are subject to availability.

**Can I charge my electric vehicle at Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel?**

There is an electric charging station around the corner at Sir John Rogerson's Quay. We do have a charging point in our concierge car park but the above location is faster.

**Are there restaurants near Anantara The Marker Dublin?**

Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel features the Forbes Street by Gareth Mullins restaurant and its sophisticated modern Irish menu as well as the charming Rooftop Bar & Terrace. Popular dining options near the hotel include Charlotte Quay, Mackenzines and Osteria Lucio.

**Does Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel have a rooftop bar?**

Yes, Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel boasts a stylish Rooftop Bar & Terrace, where guests can enjoy stunning views of Dublin along with drinks and a Mexican-inspired menu. Please note The Rooftop Bar & Terrace is only open during warmer summer months (April – September).

**Does Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel allow you to organise meetings inside the hotel?**

Yes. Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel is a prime setting for your meetings and events. It offers nine meeting rooms with high-tech equipment and capacity for up to 350 guests as well as an expert team of event planners.

**Does Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel have a spa?**

Yes. Anantara The Marker, a luxury hotel in Dublin, has a Condé Nast award-winning spa offering holistic treatments and facilities including a eucalyptus-infused steam room, sauna and an infinity pool.

**Can I smoke in my room at Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel?**

No, all indoor areas of the hotel, including guest rooms, are strictly non-smoking.
