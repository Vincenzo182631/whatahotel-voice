## From garden walls to open hills, one spirit guides.

Welcome to Borgo Pignano, a soulful Tuscan retreat where timeless elegance meets the quiet richness of place. Here, you are welcomed into a way of life, embraced by tradition, nature, and the understated pleasures of daily living.

Each day is shaped by simplicity and care. Whether nestled in the countryside or sheltered within Florence’s garden walls, the same authentic spirit prevails.

An experience that unfolds in two worlds, offering a deeper and more complete way to experience Tuscany.

### A dual-destination experience

Whether you seek the tranquil serenity of the Tuscan countryside or the vibrant energy of Florence, experience timeless tradition, natural beauty, and warm hospitality, while being welcomed to the best that Tuscany offers.
