## A Luxury Hotel On Sukhumvit Road

Nestled gracefully in the heart of Bangkok's vibrant Sukhumvit district, Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, offers a lavish retreat. With a backdrop of the stunning Lake Rachada and the awe-inspiring city vistas, our 5-star hotel in Sukhumvit, Bangkok, blends in perfectly among the urban and natural landscapes. Our prime location that features convenient access to the BTS Sky Train... See More and MRT Subway, and near Bangkok shopping malls including the EmSphere and Terminal 21, makes our hotel the place to be. Our oasis-like pool and cutting-edge technology provide modern indulgence within an ambiance of timeless luxury. Come discover the pinnacle of Bangkok hospitality at one of Sukhumvit's most sought-after addresses.

Hotel History

The magnificent 33-storey tower, located near Bangkok's major tourist and shopping destinations, was built to face both magnetic city views and the soothing vistas of Lake Ratchada.

With interiors by the internationally acclaimed design firm Hirsch Bedner Associates and exteriors by the Bangkok-based architectural firm CASA, Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel graciously caters to its sophisticated guests by delivering a "package of fantasy, drama and creature comfort". Enhancing the unforgettable atmosphere of our luxury Bangkok hotel, vivid glass sculptures by acclaimed artist Steven Gormley are artfully displayed in the lobby and public spaces. Their turquoise green color and dramatic proportions-emblematic of Gormley's designs-are prized by Asian art collectors worldwide.

Conceived of as a destination that holds appeal for both leisure and business travelers, Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel has blazed a trail among Bangkok luxury hotels as a leader in technological advancement and convenience-featuring the fastest possible internet connections, wider bandwidth, wireless access throughout all guest rooms and public spaces, as well as state-of-the-art business and meeting facilities. For its farsighted approach, the hotel has garnered several technology awards since 1996.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok?

The check-in time at Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 7.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of ฿3531.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok?

On-site parking is available at Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok?

The closest airport near Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok is Don Mueang International Airport (DMK). DMK is located approximately 23.2 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bangkok has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
