### Exploring the City of Angels

Start your enthralling adventure through this bustling city from the luxurious amenities of our luxury hotel and spa in Sukhumvit, Bangkok. Explore the beautiful temples like Wat Arun and Wat Phra Kaew to learn about the city's cultural riches, or indulge in some shopping therapy in the midst of the hopping marketplaces in Chatuchak and Chinatown. ### Discover Bangkok in 3 Days

A Curated Journey from Timeless Heritage to Modern Sophistication

Day 1 in Bangkok: Sukhumvit on Your Doorstep

Your first day asks nothing but to step outside. Connected by covered skywalk to Asok BTS and Sukhumvit MRT, Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, puts the best of the neighborhood within a short walk or a single stop. Begin with a sunrise walk along the elevated skywalk of Benjakitti Forest Park, a restored wetland of lakes and birdlife minutes from the lobby. Cross to Terminal 21 Asok, the city themed mall next door, where the Pier 21 food court serves some of Bangkok's best-value Thai food. In the afternoon, ride two stops to the EM District, the Emporium, EmQuartier and the dramatic new EmSphere, for international fashion, design and gourmet halls. As the light softens, rise to EmWonder, EmSphere's open-air rooftop, for a skyline sunset, then return home to basil, the hotel's acclaimed modern-Thai restaurant. A day spent close to home, yet rich with the city.

Day 2 in Bangkok: The River of Kings

Old Bangkok is closer than it appears. From the Sukhumvit MRT station beneath Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, a single uninterrupted ride carries you to the spiritual heart of the city, no changes, no traffic. Step out at Sanam Chai among the temples of the Rattanakosin quarter. At Wat Pho, the immense 46-meter gilded Reclining Buddha rests in serene majesty; this is also the birthplace of traditional Thai massage, where a treatment is a rare indulgence. A short walk away, the Grand Palace and the Temple of the Emerald Buddha dazzle with gilded spires. In the afternoon, cross the river by ferry from Tha Tien to Wat Arun, the Temple of Dawn, its porcelain spire most beautiful in the late-afternoon light. As night falls, a curated Chao Phraya dinner cruise glides past floodlit temples and riverfront landmarks, a fitting close to a day among kings. Reserve through the concierge.

Day 3 in Bangkok: Creative Thong Lo & Thai Silk

The final day belongs to Bangkok's creative soul. From Sheraton Grande Sukhumvit, a Luxury Collection Hotel, a short Skytrain hop reaches the Jim Thompson House Museum, a cluster of antique teak houses in a lush, canal-side garden, once home to the American who revived Thai silk before vanishing without trace. Return east to Thong Lo, a single stop from your door and the neighborhood Bangkok's creative set calls its own; at The Commons, an open-air community mall, independent cafes, a Michelin-recognized kitchen and design studios gather under one airy roof. In the afternoon, choose your immersion: a guided walk through Khlong Toei Market, the city's largest fresh market, or a hands-on Thai cooking class learning to balance the four sacred flavors — each paired with a restorative afternoon Thai spa treatment. Our concierge can arrange every detail.

### Cultural Sites

Discover opulent grandeur at The Grand Palace, adorned with intricate architectural details, or marvel at the resplendent beauty of Wat Arun's glistening spires as they shimmer in the sunlight. Indulge in serenity at Wat Pho, home to the magnificent reclining Buddha, or delve into the world of artistic refinement at the esteemed Himapan Gallery, where guests can partake in the luxurious art of lotus leaf painting, a cherished tradition that embodies elegance and grace. Visit these prestigious sites and take part in a special session that reveals the appeal of Thai creative heritage, encounter the pinnacle of cultural magnificence.

### Museums

Step into the enchanting world of the Jim Thompson House, a treasure trove of Thai art and architecture, where you can immerse yourself in the opulent lifestyle of the renowned silk entrepreneur. Surrounded by lush gardens and captivating artifacts, offering a glimpse of refined tastes of Thai nobility, experience the elegance of Wan Suan Pakkad Palace. Discover a world where majestic sculptures and intricate masterpieces intertwine transporting you to a realm of luxury and mystique at the Erawan Museum. Witness how art, history and wealth are all intertwined at these prestigious museums.

### Local Markets

Embark on a lavish shopping and culinary expedition through the vibrant aisles of Chatuchak Market and the bustling streets of Chinatown, where discerning shoppers can uncover unique designer pieces and artisanal crafts amidst a backdrop of bustling energy. Immerse yourself in the captivating allure of Khlong Lat Mayom Floating Market, where the art of market shopping merges with the enchantment of a picturesque canal, offering an exclusive experience of indulging in rare delicacies and handcrafted treasures. Discover the hidden gems of Prasanmit Flea Market, a haven for connoisseur of elegance where vintage knickknacks and haute couture clothing converge to create a real paradise for those seeking upscale luxury in the center of Bangkok's dynamic market scene.

### Hidden Historical Sites

Travel to the Ancient City, a magnificent living museum showcasing Thailand's imperial past, where imposing palaces and historic temples will transport you to a time of sophisticated opulence and cultural glory. Travel back in time to explore a historical gem with enchanting colonial architecture and elaborate temples at Kudeejeen, where the streets echoe tales of the past and modern elegance fuse together to offer a unique view of the city's enthralling history. Immerse yourself in the splendor of these historical sites, where the height of luxury mingles with the history of long ago, and you'll be spellbound by the attraction of Bangkok's illustrious past.

### Benjakitti Park

Escape to Benjakitti Forest Park, a peaceful haven where the symphony of nature and luscious vegetation call. As you stroll down the lovely walkways, breathe in the fresh, crisp air, and take in the serene ambiance, begin a sumptuous journey of calm. Watch the park come to life as the sun sets for a breathtaking stroll after dark. In this urban haven, you may escape the hustle and bustle of the city and rediscover nature while taking in the splendor of the park's untainted surrounds.

### Cultural & Spiritual Journey

Embark on a journey of profound elegance and cultural immersion as you experience the timeless tradition of “Tak Bat” within the luxurious embrace of our luxury hotel and spa in Bangkok. Indulge in the exquisite pleasure of offering sumptuous alms to the saffron-robed monks, their presence infusing the moment with an aura of spirituality and refinement. Elevate your experience further by participating in our exclusive Lotus Flower Workshop, a quintessential touch of opulence where you'll create your own exquisite lotus blossoms, perfectly complementing the grace and refinement of your Tak Bat encounter.

Discover the cosmopolitan spirit of Thai culture at CentralWorld, the vibrant lifestyle mall in the heart of Bangkok. ### Central Chidlom

Bangkok's original downtown shopping mall, Central Chidlom has been a landmark in the center of the Thai capital since 1973. It has been a popular destination for local residents and visitors ever since, with a range of key stores including a large food hall, sports shop, electronics outlet, book store and more. The mall is easily accessible from BTS Chidlom station.

### Central Embassy

Elevate your retail experience to new levels of luxury at Central Embassy, the premium shopping mall in the heart of Bangkok. Just steps from Ploenchit or Chidlom BTS skytrain stations, this cutting-edge lifestyle hub showcases leading designer labels and exclusive fashion brands within an iconic architectural landmark, crowned with sky terraces and top floor restaurants.

### Siam Paragon

One of the most iconic retail malls in Bangkok, Siam Paragon continues to attract local residents and international visitors alike. Directly connected to Siam BTS skytrain station in the center of Bangkok, this landmark features a wide range of international designer brands, a large department store, a fantastic food court, an IMEX cinema, and even the SEA LIFE Bangkok Ocean World aquarium.
