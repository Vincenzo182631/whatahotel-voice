## L27 Rooftop Pool

A luxurious oasis perched 27 floors above the vibrant Nashville skyline, this rooftop paradise is the perfect setting for a day of sun-soaked enjoyment. Bask in the warm Tennessee sun while savoring delectable light bites and refreshing cocktails, making every moment a delightful celebration at our rooftop pool in Nashville. ## Bike Around Nashville

The Westin Nashville's Bike Program offers guests complimentary access to bicycles, inviting them to explore the vibrant neighborhoods and attractions of Music City. From our hotel near Music City Center, Nashville, pedal through The Gulch, Music Row and downtown, experiencing the city's culture and energy firsthand. You can also visit local gems like Centennial Park, the Parthenon, the Nashville Farmer's Market, and Vanderbilt University. This eco-friendly initiative not only promotes a healthy way to explore the city but also helps embrace Nashville's unique charm. So, hop on a bike and discover the magic of Music City at your own pace!

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Nashville have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Nashville offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bike, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Nashville have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Nashville has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Indoor Pool (Open Year-Round) (Indoor)Rooftop Cocktail Pool - Seasonal (Outdoor)

Does The Westin Nashville have an on-site spa?
