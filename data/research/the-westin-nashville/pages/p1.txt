## Our Hotel in Nashville, Close to the Music City Center

Discover an enriching hotel in downtown Nashville. Enjoy music, culture, food, and shopping just steps from The Westin Nashville. Near Broadway, our luxury spa hotel connects you with iconic attractions...See More such as Music City Center. At our hotel near Music Row, Nashville, relax at Rhapsody Spa with massages, facials, and body treatments. Maintain your routine in the 24-hour WestinWORKOUT Fitness Studio, and stay energized with Decker & Dyer, offering all-day dining. L27 showcases rooftop views, light bites, and craft cocktails. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Nashville?

The check-in time at The Westin Nashville is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Nashville allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Nashville. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The Westin Nashville?

On-site valet parking is available at The Westin Nashville. The daily valet fee is $64.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Nashville have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Nashville has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Nashville booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Nashville?

The closest airport near The Westin Nashville is Nashville International Airport (BNA). BNA is located approximately 9.1 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Nashville have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Nashville does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
