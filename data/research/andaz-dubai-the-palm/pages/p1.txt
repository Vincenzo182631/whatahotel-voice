# Discover Hyatt’s 5-Star Lifestyle Hotel on Palm Jumeirah

Capturing Dubai’s eclectic style and heritage, Andaz Dubai The Palm is a 5‑star lifestyle hotel on the trunk of Palm Jumeirah—set along Palm East Beach and offering upscale living, a strong sense of community, and a vibrant, personalized guest experience.

## Rooms & Suites

Weaving the sights, scents and textures of Dubai into every aspect of the design, our 216 rooms—including 30 suites and four premium suites—and 116 Family Suites provide the perfect space to relax, unwind, entertain and truly express your andaz.

## DINING

View All Dining

### Le Petit Chef

Step into a world where dining meets theatre. Le Petit Chef & The Magical Cooking Book brings the world’s smallest chef straight from France to your table. Across four exquisite courses, each dish is unveiled with a mesmerizing projection that transforms your meal into a modern fairy tale.

Details + Reservations

### The Magic Table

Discover The Magic Table at Andaz Dubai The Palm, an enchanting 3D dining show where world-renowned magicians Thommy Ten & Amélie van Tass blend mind-blowing illusions with gourmet cuisine. Watch the magic unfold across your table through immersive storytelling, stunning projection, and culinary artistry. Ideal for romantic evenings, celebrations, and adventurous food lovers.

Details + Reservations

### La Coco Social

Welcome to La Coco Social—Palm Jumeirah’s laid-back kitchen, bar & lounge where all ages come together.

Enjoy international comfort food, handcrafted drinks, and a cozy space to connect, play board games, and unwind by the beach.

Details + Reservations

### The Locale

Bringing together the city’s favourite culinary stories, The Locale weaves a blanket of culinary flavours that only Dubai has to offer. The Locale, your neighbourhood eatery, is a true reflection of the multitude of threads that stitch the fabric of the city together.

Details + Reservations

### Cabana Pool

Be inspired when taking a dip in the scenic 14th floor adult-only Cabana Pool. A unique sense of personal style is reflected through the bold, colorful tiles while inspiring tunes permeate the refreshing atmosphere. Indulge in our selection of fresh salads, snacks, and drinks, all while soaking in stunning views of the Burj Al Arab.

Details + Reservations

### Andaz x Mokha 1450 Co-Working Lounge

A Collaboration Brewed in Culture, Creativity, and Connection

The Andaz Co-Working Lounge evokes the coziest of dens — with plush seating, large tables, and a library of books inviting guests to catch up, swap stories, or simply unwind. Now, in collaboration with Mokha 1450, Dubai’s pioneering specialty coffee brand, the space transforms into a creative and social hub where guests can work, connect, and savor limited-edition brews, cold infusions, and coffee-based mocktails crafted to inspire meaningful conversations.

Catch this pop up daily, from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM

Details + Reservations

### In-Room Dining

For the ultimate convenience, enjoy your meal from the comfort of your room with our extensive in-room dining options. Whether you want a relaxed breakfast in bed or a late-night snack, browse our menu and enjoy food and beverage in the privacy of your Palm Jumeirah hotel room.

### Asia Asia

Welcome to Asia Asia Palm Jumeriah – where the Silk Road unfolds on our rooftop. Enjoy 360-degree views of The Palm, Burj Al Arab, and Atlantis. Whether you’re looking to indulge in a multi-course dinner or sip on hand-crafted cocktails at the bar, Asia Asia Palm Jumeirah offers an unparalleled dining experience.
