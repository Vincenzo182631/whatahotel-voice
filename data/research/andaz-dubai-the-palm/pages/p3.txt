## Andaz x Mokha 1450 Co-Working Lounge

Now, in collaboration with Mokha 1450, Dubai’s pioneering specialty coffee brand, the space transforms into a creative and social hub where guests can work, connect, and savor limited-edition brews, cold infusions, and coffee-based mocktails crafted to inspire meaningful conversations.
