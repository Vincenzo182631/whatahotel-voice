The best of both worlds – a business district hotel, close to the convention center, with striking valley views belying its location. City views are equally exceptional. Rooms are large and airy, even in the entry category and spare, modern furnishings throughout the property provide comfort without distraction. Pinzimini gets high praise for its Tuscan fare.
