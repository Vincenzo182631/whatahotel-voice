## Room Amenities

Our guests will feel well cared for throughout their stay. All rooms feature unique characteristics and premium amenities. Our rooms also support The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City’s sustainable practices and embrace technological trends. Also, we are proud to offer smoke-free guest rooms to keep the air fresh.

## Presidential Suite

At The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City, this unique and spacious retreat is surrounded by floor-to-ceiling windows offering spectacular views of the bustling city and the valley's green reserves. The Presidential Suite is ideal for those traveling on business in Santa Fe, Mexico City, who wish to take their travel experience to the next level.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City?

The largest occupancy room at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City is the Club lounge access with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City?

Some rooms at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have fridges?

No, rooms at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have a Coffee/tea maker.
