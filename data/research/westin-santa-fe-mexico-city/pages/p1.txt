## Kids Eat Well Too

Eating well isn’t just for adults; that’s why Westin has teamed up with the experts at SuperChefs to cater the Westin Eat Well Menu for Kids. Comprised of doctors, dentists, chefs, dieticians and other experts, SuperChefs is dedicated to educating kids on the importance—and the fun—of eating smart. These healthy recipes are much more than just fruits and vegetables. Each of our special menu offerings is as delicious as it is nutritious, making both kids and parents smile during their stay at our wellness hotel in Santa Fe, Mexico City.

## Fresh Farm-to-Table Food

Created by renowned French Chef Jean-Georges Vongerichten, Market Kitchen embraces its farm-to-table concept by using local ingredients in innovative ways. Choose from culinary ambiances rarely seen in Mexico, including an exclusive artisan cheese and charcuterie cellar, an espresso bar with a broad range of local coffees, and Eleven Bar & Lounge with the best panoramic views of Mexico City and the valley.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City?

The check-in time at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City. Self parking is available at The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City, with rates of $2.00 per hour, $8.00 per day, $336.00 per week, and $8.00 perstay. Valet parking is available for $2.00 per hour.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City?

The closest airport near The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City is Benito Juarez International Airport Mexico City (MEX). MEX is located approximately 25.1 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have an airport shuttle to Benito Juarez International Airport Mexico City (MEX)?

Yes, The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City has an airport shuttle to Benito Juarez International Airport Mexico City (MEX).

Does The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have an airport shuttle to Felipe Angeles International Airport (NLU)?

Yes, The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City has an airport shuttle to Felipe Angeles International Airport (NLU).

Does The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have an airport shuttle to Lic. Adolfo Lopez Mateos International Airport (TLC)?

Yes, The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City has an airport shuttle to Lic. Adolfo Lopez Mateos International Airport (TLC).

Does The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City have an Executive lounge?

Yes, The Westin Santa Fe, Mexico City has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
