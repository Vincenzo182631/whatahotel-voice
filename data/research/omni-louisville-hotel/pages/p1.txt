## Louisville, Kentucky

### Countless possibilities at our downtown Louisville Hotel

At Omni Louisville Hotel, experiences are tailored to the history and rhythm of the city. Take a dip in the heated rooftop pool, dine with family or bowl with friends while sipping signature cocktails at Pin + Proof, our Prohibition-style speakeasy. Relax at the full-service Mokara Spa or enjoy built-in entertainment and uninterrupted connectivity in one of 612 charming guest accommodations. Our downtown Louisville hotel is steps from the Kentucky International Convention Center and plenty of things to do in the city. Main Street, Fourth Street Live, Whiskey Row Distilleries and Louisville Slugger Museum & Factory are all less than a mile away from our hotel in Louisville, KY. You’ll also be a quick drive away from Churchill Downs, home of the Kentucky Derby, and University of Louisville.

## Traveler's Insights

### Does Omni Louisville Hotel have a pool?

Omni Louisville Hotel has one rooftop pool located on the third floor. Enjoy light bites from The Water Company seasonally.

### What is the best area to stay in when visiting Louisville?

Downtown Louisville has plenty of things to do for families, couples, business and solo travelers alike. Be near countless museums, dining options and more when you stay at our hotel in downtown Louisville.

### What are the property amenities at Omni Louisville Hotel?

Omni Louisville Hotel is home to world-class amenities including:

- A rooftop pool
- A full-service spa
- A fully-equipped fitness center
- 70,000 square feet of meeting and event space
- Seven dining outlets - two full-service restaurants, two bars, a grab-and-go market, a speakeasy and a tasting room
- Valet parking with a nightly charge

### Where is Omni Louisville Hotel located?

Omni Louisville Hotel puts guests in the heart of all the action in downtown Louisville, KY. The hotel's physical address is at 400 S 2nd Street, Louisville, Kentucky 40202.

### What is the closest airport to Omni Louisville Hotel?

Omni Louisville Hotel is near the following airports:

- Louisville International Airport (SDF) is located 5.9 miles (approximately 10 minutes) from the hotel.
- Cincinnati International Airport (CVG) is located 95.8 miles (approximately 1 hour and 30 minutes) from the hotel.

### How much is parking at the hotel?

Overnight valet parking is $45 per night, per car.

Omni Louisville Hotel also offers Special Event parking rates:

- 0-4 hours: $37
- 4-8 hours: $40
- Over 8 hours and overnight $45+ tax

Additional public self-parking options are available directly behind the hotel and can be accessed from 3rd Street These garages do not have direct access into the hotel. Please be prepared to walk 1-2 blocks, depending upon the garage you choose. Rates vary based upon the garage, but you can expect rates to range between $15 and $27 per night without in & out privileges.

### WHAT TIME IS CHECK-IN AND CHECK-OUT?

- Check-in: 3 PM
- Check-out: 11 AM
- 4 PM late check-out may be available for additional $60; after 4 PM, the full nightly rate applies.

### Is the hotel pet friendly?

Yes, Omni Louisville Hotel is pet friendly for dogs and cats up to 25 pounds. There is a one-time non-refundable pet fee of $125 per reservation. Service animals accompanying guests with disabilities are always welcome and will not be required to pay the one-time pet charge (no documentation necessary). Read more of our hotel policies.
