## FAQs

Are the restaurants open to visitors who are not staying at the hotel?

The dining venues at L’Ermitage welcome both hotel guests and visitors seeking a refined dining experience in Beverly Hills. Whether joining for rooftop cocktails, a relaxed meal, or an evening of coastal Italian cuisine, guests may enjoy the hotel’s restaurants without an overnight stay.

Is rooftop dining available at the hotel?

Poza offers rooftop dining beside the pool, where guests may enjoy inventive cocktails and California-inspired fare while taking in sweeping city views.

Does the hotel offer Italian dining in Beverly Hills?

Costa Covo Osteria presents a coastal Italian menu shaped by Mediterranean influence and seasonal ingredients, offering an elegant setting for lunch or dinner in Beverly Hills.

Are the dining venues suitable for special occasions?

Several dining settings throughout the hotel provide an intimate atmosphere well suited to celebrations, gatherings, or special evenings in Beverly Hills.

Can guests enjoy dining without leaving their suite?

Guests may enjoy a curated in-suite dining menu delivered directly to their accommodation, allowing for a relaxed dining experience in complete privacy.
