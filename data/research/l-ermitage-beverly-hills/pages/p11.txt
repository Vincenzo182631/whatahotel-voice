## FAQs

How do I schedule an appointment?

For optimal convenience, please schedule your Joanna Czech treatment in advance. Please contact our Concierge at 310.278.3344 or Concierge@lermitagebeverlyhills.com to arrange your session.

What are your hours of operation?

Treatments are available Tuesday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Where is Joanna Czech Studio located?

Joanna Czech Studio is situated on the seventh floor of the hotel.

What is your cancellation policy?

As your treatment time is reserved exclusively with a trained specialist, Joanna Czech Studio requires twenty-four hours’ notice for any modifications or cancellations. Sessions cancelled or missed with less than twenty-four hours’ notice will be charged at the full treatment rate.

What treatments are available?

The menu features bespoke facial treatments, selective body treatments, and brow services. Facial offerings include the Teen Facial, LED Facial, Enhanced LED Facial, and Joanna’s signature Ultimate Facial. Body treatments address specific areas including the neck and décolleté, back, upper body, lower body, and abdomen. Precision brow shaping and tinting services are also offered.

What can I expect during my treatment?

Each treatment is thoroughly personalized to address your skin’s unique requirements. Your session will be guided by experienced estheticians, extensively trained in Joanna’s exclusive methods and protocols, ensuring the same elevated standards whether you visit the Beverly Hills, New York, or Dallas locations.

How should I prepare?

For facial treatments, please arrive with clean skin to maximize the benefits of your session. Additionally, please inform your esthetician of any relevant health history or specific skin concerns during your consultation.
