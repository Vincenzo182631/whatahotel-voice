An Exceptional Retreat in Beverly Hills

Welcome to L’Ermitage

Established in 1975 on a palm-lined residential boulevard, L’Ermitage is a discreet, refined getaway located a stone’s throw from the beating heart of Beverly Hills. As an award-winning hotel holding MICHELIN Guide Two Key status and AAA Five Diamond distinction, L’Ermitage is committed to offering travelers excellence in hospitality.

## The Suites at L’Ermitage

Our 116 suites preserve the residential elegance of the hotel’s original design while providing guests with a range of spacious layouts and modern appointments. Inside, guests will find sumptuous walk-through dressing rooms with ample wardrobe space, full-length triptych mirrors with warm adjustable lighting, and expansive vanities that harken back to Old Hollywood glamour.

Dine & Drink

Cultured Cuisine

Dining at L’Ermitage moves effortlessly from morning to evening. Discover coastal Mediterranean flavors and seasonal California ingredients at Costa Covo, head to the rooftop for New Californian cuisine and cocktails at Poza, or enjoy 24-hour in-suite dining in the privacy of your accommodation. Each experience brings a distinctive sense of place to dining in Beverly Hills.

Wellness at L’Ermitage

Wellness at L’Ermitage brings together expert skincare, beauty, restorative technology, and movement within the privacy of the hotel. Discover personalized treatments from Joanna Czech, salon services by Alon Shalom, and a fitness center designed to help guests maintain their routine while away.

## A Quiet Address, Steps from Rodeo Drive

Tucked within a peaceful residential enclave, L’Ermitage offers a rare sense of privacy in the heart of Beverly Hills. Guests are just moments from Rodeo Drive and within easy reach of the neighborhood’s designer boutiques, acclaimed restaurants, galleries, gardens, and cultural attractions, while remaining removed from the busiest streets of Los Angeles.

Whether you’re visiting for a weekend of shopping, a romantic Beverly Hills escape, or an extended stay, the hotel provides a discreet base for experiencing the city at your own pace.

## An Extended Stay, Elevated

For guests spending longer in Los Angeles, L’Ermitage combines the space and privacy of residential living with the service of a five-star Beverly Hills hotel. Generously proportioned luxury suites provide room to settle in, unwind, and establish a sense of routine beyond a traditional hotel stay.

With dining, wellness, fitness, rooftop experiences, and Beverly Hills just beyond your suite, an extended stay can feel both effortless and distinctly personal.

## FAQs

What distinguishes L’Ermitage from other luxury hotels in Beverly Hills?

L’Ermitage offers an atmosphere defined by discretion, spacious residential-style suites, and highly personalized service within a quiet residential setting.

How close is the hotel to Rodeo Drive and central Beverly Hills?

The hotel is a short walk from Rodeo Drive and the city’s premier boutiques, galleries, and restaurants, while remaining tucked away from the main thoroughfares.

Is the rooftop pool open year-round?

The rooftop pool offers a refined retreat with city views throughout the year, with seasonal offerings available at select times.

Are there curated experiences or local events available to guests?

Guests may explore seasonal offerings and cultural happenings through the hotel’s events calendar and concierge recommendations.

Why stay at L’Ermitage Beverly Hills?

L’Ermitage combines spacious all-suite accommodations, personalized five-star service, dining, wellness, and a quiet residential setting just moments from the heart of Beverly Hills.

Is L’Ermitage Beverly Hills close to Rodeo Drive?

Yes. L’Ermitage is located within easy reach of Rodeo Drive, allowing guests to explore Beverly Hills’ luxury shopping, restaurants, galleries, and attractions while staying in a quieter residential setting.

Does L’Ermitage Beverly Hills have restaurants?

Yes. Guests can dine at Costa Covo for coastal Mediterranean-inspired cuisine or visit Poza for seasonal Californian dishes, cocktails, and rooftop views across Beverly Hills.

Does L’Ermitage Beverly Hills offer spa and wellness experiences?

Yes. Wellness experiences include personalized skincare at Joanna Czech Studio, salon services, fitness facilities, and additional experiences that can be incorporated into a stay.

Is L’Ermitage an all-suite hotel?

Yes. L’Ermitage is an all-suite Beverly Hills hotel offering generously proportioned accommodations designed around privacy, comfort, and residential-style living.
