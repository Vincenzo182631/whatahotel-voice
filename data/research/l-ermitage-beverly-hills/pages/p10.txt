# Costa Covo

## Coastal Mediterranean Dining in Beverly Hills

A love letter to the Mediterranean coast, Costa Covo brings seasonal California ingredients together with fresh, coastal-inspired flavors in the heart of Beverly Hills. From locally sourced seafood and vibrant produce to handcrafted cocktails and curated wines, the restaurant offers a refined yet relaxed dining experience designed for long lunches, evening gatherings, and intimate dinners.

## Seasonal Mediterranean Dining in Beverly Hills

Inspired by the coastal regions of Southern Europe, Costa Covo pairs seasonal California ingredients with fresh Mediterranean flavors. The menu features seafood, grilled proteins, handcrafted cocktails, and shareable plates designed for relaxed dining in Beverly Hills.

Whether stopping by Alcova for drinks or joining for Friday evening live music, Costa Covo offers a refined yet approachable dining experience.

## FAQs

Does Costa Covo accept reservations?

Yes, reservations are recommended and can be made online in advance.

Is Costa Covo open to non-hotel guests?

Yes, Costa Covo welcomes both hotel guests and local diners visiting Beverly Hills.

What type of cuisine does Costa Covo serve?

Costa Covo serves coastal Mediterranean-inspired cuisine made with seasonal California ingredients and fresh local produce.

Does Costa Covo offer live music?

Yes, live music is offered on Friday evenings at Costa Covo.

Can Costa Covo accommodate private events?

Yes, Costa Covo is available for private dining events, celebrations, and group gatherings.

Where is Costa Covo located?

Costa Covo is located within L’Ermitage Beverly Hills in Beverly Hills, California.
