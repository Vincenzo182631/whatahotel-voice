## A Quiet Wellness Retreat in Beverly Hills

Wellness at L’Ermitage Beverly Hills is shaped by the hotel’s calm, residential setting and a more personalized approach to rest and renewal. Rather than a traditional Beverly Hills hotel spa experience, guests can create their own wellness journey through expert skincare, restorative technology, beauty services, movement, and time to simply slow down.

Begin with a personalized treatment at Joanna Czech Studio, reset with the SAVA Sound Pod by TERSA, or spend a quiet afternoon beside the rooftop pool. Each experience can be enjoyed individually or incorporated into a wider wellness-focused stay in Beverly Hills.

## Expert Skincare & Beauty Treatments

L’Ermitage brings some of the beauty industry’s most respected expertise directly to Beverly Hills. At Joanna Czech Studio, personalized facials, selective body treatments, and brow services are tailored to individual skincare needs using Joanna Czech’s renowned methods and protocols.

## Wellness Beyond the Spa

At L’Ermitage, wellness extends beyond treatments and appointments. Guests can maintain their routine in the Fitness Center, unwind beside the heated rooftop pool, or make time for restorative experiences such as the SAVA Sound Pod by TERSA.

For those who prefer to get outside, the Concierge can also help arrange personalized experiences including guided Runyon Canyon hikes and Malibu surf lessons. From movement and recovery to relaxation and time outdoors, wellness can be shaped around each guest and become a natural part of their stay in Los Angeles.

## FAQs

Does L’Ermitage offer spa services in Beverly Hills?

The hotel offers a curated range of spa and wellness experiences, including advanced skincare treatments at Joanna Czech Studio and professional salon services by Alon Shalom. Each service is designed to complement the calm, residential atmosphere of the hotel.

Can spa treatments be booked without staying at the hotel?

Select wellness services may be available to visitors seeking a discreet spa experience in Beverly Hills. Availability may vary depending on treatment schedules.

What wellness facilities are available at the hotel?

Guests may enjoy a range of wellness offerings, including expert skincare treatments, salon services, a rooftop pool, and a fully equipped fitness center designed to support both relaxation and daily routines.

Can the concierge arrange additional wellness experiences?

The concierge team may assist with arranging wellness services, beauty appointments, or personalized experiences designed to complement your stay.
