With panoramic sunset views across Beverly Hills, Poza brings together rooftop dining, seasonal Californian cuisine, and handcrafted cocktails in an elevated open-air setting. ## Rooftop Dining Above Beverly Hills

Poza combines panoramic rooftop views with seasonal Californian cuisine, fresh seafood, and handcrafted cocktails. Designed for sunset dining, poolside drinks, and relaxed gatherings, the rooftop offers an elevated open-air experience in the heart of Beverly Hills.

## FAQs

Does Poza accept reservations?

Yes, reservations are recommended, particularly for sunset dining and weekends. Guests can reserve a table online in advance.

Is Poza open to non-hotel guests?

Yes, Poza welcomes both hotel guests and local visitors looking for rooftop dining and cocktails in Beverly Hills.

Does Poza have rooftop seating?

Yes, Poza offers open-air rooftop dining and lounge seating with panoramic views overlooking Beverly Hills and Los Angeles.

What type of food does Poza serve?

Poza serves seasonal Californian cuisine with fresh seafood, market-driven ingredients, shareable plates, and handcrafted cocktails inspired by the West Coast lifestyle.

Can Poza accommodate private events?

Yes, Poza is available for private rooftop events, cocktail receptions, dinners, and social gatherings in Beverly Hills.

What drinks are available at Poza?

The rooftop bar features signature cocktails, spritzes, local beers, wines by the glass, and a curated beverage menu designed for rooftop dining and sunset drinks.

Where is Poza located?

Poza is located at L’Ermitage Beverly Hills in Beverly Hills, California, offering rooftop dining just minutes from Rodeo Drive and central Los Angeles.
