Gracefully Designed Suites
Signature Suites
Graciously sized, each suite offers a serene escape steeped in quiet luxury. Select suites feature private balconies with panoramic views of the Kamo River and Higashiyama mountains, while others open onto beautifully maintained Japanese gardens—designed for moments of reflection and stillness. Step inside to discover a choice between sleek, modern suites and traditional Japanese-style rooms. The latter feature tatami floors, plush futons, and deep hinoki bathtub—inviting a full sensory immersion into Kyoto’s refined way of life. Every detail is meticulously curated to honor tradition while offering the comfort, sophistication, and elegance expected of a world-class stay.
