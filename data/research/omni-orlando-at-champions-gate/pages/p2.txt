## Orlando, Florida

### Explore endless activities at our Orlando Resort

Spread across 800 sprawling acres of lush palms and sparkling water, the Four Diamond-rated Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate is a haven of rest and relaxation. Two championship golf courses, tennis or pickleball courts, a full-service spa, sparkling resort pools, a lazy river and water park, all-new Toptracer Range Eagles Edge at ChampionsGate and an abundance of resort activities make this Orlando resort the perfect family-friendly getaway. Breezy guest rooms, two and three-bedroom villas and studio suites ensure that your Florida vacation is both comfortable and memorable. Choose from 10 premium restaurants offering a variety of cuisines from Chinese to Italian to American, and unwind with dinner and drinks after a day at the resort. We are located just a short drive from the best of Orlando, including Walt Disney World, Universal Studios and SeaWorld. With easy access to area attractions from our resort in Orlando, guests can enjoy all that the city has to offer. You’ll also find that our resort is an ideal location for your next meeting, event or wedding, with 255,000 square feet of available event space.

## Traveler's Insights

### What are the property amenities at Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate?

Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate is home to world-class amenities including:

- Multiple pools (including an adults-only pool), a water park and a lazy river
- A full-service spa
- Championship golf courses
- Eagles Edge at ChampionsGate hitting bays with Toptracer technology
- Family-friendly mini golf
- An on-site sports field complex
- A fully-equipped fitness center with Peloton bikes
- Tennis and pickleball courts
- On-site shopping
- Family-friendly resort activities
- 255,000 square feet of meeting and event space
- Nine dining outlets - four full-service restaurants, a sushi and sake bar, a deli, a lobby bar, a pool bar and dining at Eagles Edge
- Self and valet parking with a nightly charge

### Where is Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate located?

Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate is located 12 miles from Disney World and 21 miles from Universal Studios Florida. The resort's physical address is 1500 Masters Boulevard, ChampionsGate, Florida 33896.

### What is the closest airport to Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate?

Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate is near the following airports:

About 26 miles (approximately 53 minutes) from Orlando International Airport (MCO)

### How much is parking at Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate?

Daily Self-Parking: $33

Overnight Self-Parking: $38

Valet Parking: $50

##### The Villas and Sunset Lodge and Suites Parking

- Self-parking is conveniently located at the Villas and Sunset Lodge & Suites, and during your stay, you may request a complimentary shuttle to any resort location (main resort, pools and golf).

### What time is check-in and check-out?

- Check-in is at 4 p.m.
- Check-out is at 11 a.m.
- In order to reserve a room and check in at our property, you must be at least 18 years of age. A photo ID is required at check-in.

### Is the resort pet friendly?

Yes, Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate is a pet friendly hotel for animals under 50 pounds. There is a one-time non-refundable pet fee of $225 per reservation for one pet and $100 additional for a second pet. Service animals accompanying guests with disabilities are always welcome and will not be required to pay the one time pet charge (no documentation necessary). Read more of our resort policies.

### What is included in the resort service charge at Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate?

The resort charge at Omni Orlando Resort at ChampionsGate allows guests to enjoy the resort's many amenities without having to incur charges during their stays. Some of these items include:

Scheduled transport service Walt Disney World® theme parks

Access to Crane's Adventure Golf

24-hour access to the fitness center

Premier Wi-Fi

For a full list of inclusions, visit the policies page.
