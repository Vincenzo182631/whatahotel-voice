# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Madrid

## Find the city's essence at our 5-star hotel in Madrid, Spain

This 5-star JW Marriott Hotel Madrid, stands as a premier destination among the best hotels in downtown Madrid, Spain, offering unparalleled sophistication. Situated near iconic landmarks such as Puerta del Sol, the Royal Palace, and Plaza Mayor, this Madrid city center hotel exudes a luxurious and refined atmosphere. Stroll through the Art Walk, part of the UNESCO World Heritage List as The Landscape...See More of Light, and discover the best art museums in the world. Inspired by mindfulness, the hotel’s warm décor resonates with the building's historical past, revealing old 19th-century wrought iron columns. Our 139 rooms and suites, most with private balconies, provide ample havens with elegant touches for a holistic well-being experience. Reconnect in our lobby or savor tradition at our restaurant. ## The Place That Captures the Essence of Madrid

The new JW Marriott Hotel Madrid is ideally located in the city center, what is the city’s most prized area and where its biggest luxury hotels are located and close to attractions such as Puerta del Sol, Gran Vía and Plaza Mayor. To spend time here is to connect with Spanish culture itself. From here, you can enjoy a luxury commercial atmosphere or stroll in the renowned Art Walk, included in the UNESCO World Heritage List as The Landscape of Light, where you will find some of the best art museums in the world: El Prado, the Thyssen-Bornemisza and the Reina Sofia.

## The JW Garden

Inspired by Alice Marriott’s love of gardening, the JW Garden is a serene sanctuary that welcomes guests and grows herbs and ingredients as part of the culinary journey. JW Garden Madrid is a green oasis in the middle of the city, created to find calm and tranquility, that invites guests to connect with nature. A petite patio to find a moment of peace and relaxation. Our signature herbs are present in a vertical kitchen garden, where our chefs and cocktail designers come to find inspiration to draw the customized menus. This circular experience fills in the five senses: starting with the botanical esthetic of the lavender flowers, followed by the surrounding rich aromas of the maturing lemons, completed with the mouthwatering flavors of the rosemary and the thyme. All collected in a cozy haven with warm corners, conceived to delight in a book, while enjoying the sounds of soft involving music.

## History & Heritage

The hotel has been crafted into an environment that is the ultimate expression of elegance and calm. Each space soothes the senses while still preserving the authentic and historic character of features like the 19th century wrought iron columns, which invite guests to appreciate the beautiful heritage of this site. Hosting one of the oldest perfumeries in Madrid that opened its doors in 1899 to a shop called Alvarez Gómez, the building has a unique legacy of capturing the true essence of the city. While in the front of the store perfumes made of the finest, freshest ingredients were sold, in the back room perfume mingled with the smell of cigars and coffee as the city's finest minds met to socialize and debate. That legacy continues today, with the hotel attracting those who wish to discover more deeply the essence of the city and the building.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Madrid allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 25.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €50.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid?

Off-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid. The daily valet fee is €45.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Madrid has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Madrid booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Madrid?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Madrid is Adolfo Suárez Madrid–Barajas Airport (MAD). MAD is located approximately 15.5 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Madrid has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
