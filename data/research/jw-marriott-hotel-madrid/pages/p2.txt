# True Disconnection After Intense Connection

## Accommodations

At JW Marriott Hotel Madrid, we provide a serene escape where guests can disconnect from stress and focus on what matters. Our 139 elegant Madrid suites and thoughtfully designed hotel rooms offer a revitalizing oasis that soothes the senses. Crafted with natural materials and calming, neutral tones, each space is meticulously designed for comfort, allowing guests to enjoy moments of peaceful reflection...See More amidst the vibrant energy of Madrid's bustling capital. From tranquil interiors to tailored services, we ensure every stay is a rejuvenating experience. Contact the hotel to know more about our connecting rooms and special deals: traveling with your family was never so comfortable and enjoyable.See Less

### A Holistic Stay

Those staying in a suite on the upper floors can enjoy large, one-of-a-kind terraces with striking views of the city center below. In addition to wellness area in the hotel, guests can indulge in special in-room experience to enjoy a personalized wellness treatment. With yoga mats and video workouts that can be enjoyed in the privacy of their room, guests can nurture their physical well-being whenever they want. The same can be said about their emotional health, as guests can instantly access a meditation moment by playing calming ambient noise or a guided meditation.

## Signature Suites

The Signature Suites are the charming jewels of the hotel. Their terraces, comfortable little oasis of peace and light under Madrid’s beautiful blue sky, are the place to be. With incredible views of the cradle, it invites you to lose yourself in the skyline while appreciating the amazing city's roofs from your terrace.

## Junior Suite Terrace

### Suite Canalejas

Our Suite Canalejas which can be connected with a Junior Suite, invites you to enjoy the ¨dolce far niente¨ at the huge terrace with sunbeds, amazing 180o views and the unique atmosphere of the Spanish capital.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have a work space?

No, the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid is the Canalejas Suite with a maximum occupancy of 3.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have fridges?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have hair dryers.
