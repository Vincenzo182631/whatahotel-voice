# JW Marriott Madrid Dining

## Dining

At JW Marriott Hotel Madrid, dining is not just an experience but a destination itself. Botánicos Álvarez, the hotel's gastronomy concept, brings together two distinct spaces united by a shared philosophy: honest cooking, quality ingredients, and the art of mixing flavours and moments. Whether you stop by for a cocktail in the vibrant atmosphere of La Botica or settle in for a long, unhurried dinner...See More at Sala de Mezclas, every visit tells the story of Madrid, past and present, on a plate.See Less

### Botánicos Álvarez

Inspired by the ancient tradition of the botica, where aromas, ingredients and knowledge were blended with precision, Botánicos Álvarez is JW Marriott Madrid's gastronomic identity. The concept takes its name from the historic Perfumería Álvarez Gómez that once occupied this building, channelling that legacy of ‘careful mixing’ into the kitchen, the bar, and every detail of the experience. Two complementary...See More spaces share one spirit: product-led, flavour-forward and rooted in the pleasure of gathering around a table.See Less

## La Botica Bar

La Botica is where it all begins. A lively, social bar in JW Marriott Hotel Madrid, designed for guests and locals alike: a place to meet, converse and share.

The menu revolves around honest, straightforward cooking, built on quality produce. Small sharing plates, from charcuterie and cheeses to the popular Sando sandwiches, classic jamón ibérico, croquetas, and ensaladilla rusa with ventresca, pair effortlessly with a curated selection of cocktails, wines and vermouths that evoke Madrid's beloved aperitivo culture.

The cocktail programme, developed in collaboration with Amarguería and mixologist Alberto Villarroel, revisits the classics through a sensory lens inspired by the historic Perfumería Álvarez Gómez, blending botany, fragrance and flavour in every glass.

### The Gastronomy

Botánicos Álvarez puts the product at the centre. At Sala de Mezclas, traditional Mediterranean recipes are reimagined with care and technique, sharing-friendly, flavour-forward and always rooted in the best seasonal product.

### The Bar

La Botica is Madrid's new living room. The cocktail programme, created in collaboration with Amarguería and mixologist Alberto Villarroel, revisits the classics through a sensory lens inspired by the historic Perfumería Álvarez Gómez, blending botany, fragrance and flavour in every glass.

### The Breakfast Experience

Start the day with a breakfast that reflects the same care as every other meal: seasonal products, house-baked pastries and a kitchen committed to quality from the very first bite. Available daily for hotel guests.

## The Art of Mixing

At Botánicos Álvarez, every dish, every cocktail and every moment is the result of careful combination. Inspired by the ancient art of the botica, we blend flavours, aromas and people.
