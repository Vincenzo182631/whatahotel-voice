Sensory Experience

## Art Through Aromatherapy

JW Marriott Madrid Hotel, ALQVIMIA and the Museo Nacional Thyssen-Bornemisza join together on a sensory journey through art and aromatherapy, under the inspiring theme of the four seasons. A unique experience that begins at the Museo Thyssen with aclosed-door visit, where guests can enjoy a guided tour featuring carefully selected works, chosen for their thematic related to the four seasons, accompanied by an olfactory experience through essential oils. The experience culminates magnificently at the JW Marriott Madrid Hotel with a relaxing cabin ritual, aromatically linked to the theme and exclusively designed for this experience. For more information, please inquire at the hotel reception.

### SPA at JW Marriott Madrid

Welcome to a wellness space prepared to accommodate all your needs. Elysium Luxury Wellness is a private space to relax and reconnect with yourself. Ask for our variety of services, treatments and massages for your wellbeing. Reservation is required.

## The Gym

Keep in fit at the large and modern JW Marriott Madrid Gym, only for our guests. A fully equipped fitness room with towels and other amenities open 7 days a week, 24 hours a day complimentary for in-house guests and including the following equipment available: Cardiovascular equipment, elliptical machines, exercise bikes, free weights, strength equipment and weight machines.

### The Wellbeing Sessions

Guests can also enjoy the Wellbeing Sessions two times a day. Both recorded sessions, leaded by the personal coach and sound meditation specialist Andrea Klimowitz. Please, advise your assistance prior to the starting times at the Reception Desk.

### Noon meditation

From 12.00h, Guests can gather at the JW Garden to get ready for the 12.15h meditation with Tibetan bowls. Sound Meditation help us get into deep states of meditation, clearing the mind and relaxing the body. (Approximate duration of 13 minutes).

### Evening meditation

From 17.00h, Guests can come together at the JW Garden to enjoy a cup of tea while joining a breathwork. Inspired by the well know Wim Hof (Dutch athlete also known as Iceman for his high resistance to cold temperatures), this is a short, very dynamic practice. (Approximate duration of 11 minutes).

But while the hustle and bustle of Madrid's streets can be thrilling, it can also leave visitors moments of calm and revitalization. Offering an oasis at the heart of Madrid, JW Marriott Hotel Madrid gives the opportunity to connect more deeply...See More both with themselves and the city. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Hotel Madrid offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Madrid have an on-site spa?

Yes, JW Marriott by Elysium is located on-site at JW Marriott Hotel Madrid.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Hotel Madrid?

Serrano Street Shopping area, Museo del Prado, Museo Reina Sofia, Museo Thyssen - Bornemisza, National Archaeological Museum and more are nearby JW Marriott Hotel Madrid.
