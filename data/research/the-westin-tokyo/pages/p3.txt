# Dining at The Westin Tokyo

## Dining

Join us at one of our downtown Tokyo restaurants with a view of the city and unique culinary experiences. From hearty all-day buffets featuring Eat Well menus and Japanese restaurants in downtown Tokyo offering sushi, tempura and teppanyaki, to authentic Cantonese dishes, seasonal sweets, and first-class beverages, including a sky bar in downtown Tokyo with Tokyo Tower views, indulge in our extensive and versatile dining offerings.

Our in-room private dining menu at our hotel near Shibuya Tokyo, offers an exceptional culinary experience in the comfort of your room. The menu features Westin’s signature Eat Well selections alongside popular dishes from our renowned restaurants.
