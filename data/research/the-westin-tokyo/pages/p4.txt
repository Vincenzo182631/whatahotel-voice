## Westin Garden

Discover the Westin Garden - an urban oasis designed by world-renowned landscape artist Kazuyuki Ishihara. Boasting 300 flower varieties, seasonal blooms, and a tranquil stream, it offers breathtaking views and natural beauty year-round. Unwind with afternoon tea or cocktails at The Lounge and The Bar, surrounded by scenic views by day and enchanting lighting by night.

Westin Wellness Programs

### Sensory Welcome

The lobby is designed around the concept of an orangery, seamlessly connecting as one space in harmony with the beautiful natural surroundings of the hotel, together with The Westin's signature white tea scent to help you feel well upon your arrival and get ready for a great stay at our hotel near Shibuya Crossing.

### Eat Well Menu & Westin Fresh by the Juicery

International restaurant The Terrace, a space that embodies the idea that wellness comes from healthy eating. From its interior design to its remarkably healthy cuisine, the restaurant invites guests to experience the deeper meanings behind the signature Westin “Eat Well” philosophy through dining experiences that replenish the body, mind and soul, as well as the earth.

### Heavenly® Bed & Lavender Balm

A good night’s sleep is essential to well-being. Restore your body and mind with truly restful sleep in Westin’s signature Heavenly® Bed. Infused with calming lavender and chamomile, the Lavender Balm can be applied to temples or wrists at bedtime to ease tension and soothe the senses.

### RunWestin

Not only are we a downtown Tokyo hotel with a fitness center, our group run program helps you maintain your workout routine on the road. Discover scenic routes around the hotel recommended by our Run Concierge, or join a group run led by a Run Concierge when available.

### WestinWORKOUT® Gear Lending

Pack light and stay active at our downtown Tokyo hotels with fitness center. Borrow workout gear anytime to support your routine. Contact Service Express or the front desk to request a kit for JPY 500 per night.
