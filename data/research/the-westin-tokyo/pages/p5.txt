Green Key Certification

**Green Key Certification**

The Westin Tokyo, a wellness-focused urban hotel offering unique programs for physical and mental well-being, has received the international eco-label Green Key, awarded to environmentally responsible hotels and restaurants.

Established in 1994 by the Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE) in Denmark, Green Key is granted to facilities that meet strict criteria across 13 categories, including 75 mandatory and 75 optional items. It recognizes sustainable operations and environmental policies. Over 6,000 facilities in 70 countries have earned this certification.

**Environmental Initiatives**

1. Waste Separation & Recycling

All hotel waste is carefully sorted, with over 80% recycled after collection by Yebisu Garden Place.
2. Food Waste Reduction

The hotel uses the AI-based Winnow Waste Monitor to track and analyze food waste, improving demand forecasting and reducing food loss.
3. Water & Energy Conservation

At the hotel, water consumption is being reduced by limiting the flow rate to no more than 8 liters per minute at more than 75% of the faucets throughout the property. As a result, the hotel has achieved a 22% reduction in water consumption, exceeding its target of a 15% reduction by 2025. A guest room green program also reduces laundry, saving water and energy.
4. Eco-Friendly Detergents

75% of cleaning products used are certified by Green Seal or USDA BioPreferred. The hotel continues to reduce harmful chemical use.
5. Plastic & Disposable Reduction

Plastic and single-use items have been replaced with paper or plant-based alternatives in restaurants and guest rooms.

**Sustainability & Social Responsibility**

1. Vegetarian & Vegan Options

Restaurants offer menus for vegetarians and vegans.
2. Sustainable Ingredients

The hotel promotes organic, eco-labeled, fair trade, and locally sourced ingredients, including those certified with Japan’s Organic JAS mark.
3. Gender Equality

Events are held for International Women’s Day. The hotel promotes equal opportunities, fair evaluations, and reduced gender pay gaps.
4. Parental Leave & Career Support

The hotel supports smooth returns after maternity/paternity leave and fosters a supportive environment for career development, especially for women.
