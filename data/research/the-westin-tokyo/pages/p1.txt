# Welcome to The Westin Tokyo

## Embrace the city life at this Tokyo, Shibuya, hotel

Just steps from Ebisu Station in Tokyo, our hotel is set within the lush greenery and cultural charm of Yebisu Garden Place. Located one stop from Shibuya, one of Tokyo's most vibrant districts, it offers...See More both convenience and a serene escape in the heart of the city. Perfect for travelers seeking for Tokyo hotels Shibuya or hotels in Tokyo city center, our rooms feature sweeping urban views, including Tokyo Tower, creating a refined stay in central Tokyo. Whether for business or leisure, discover a central Tokyo business hotel where comfort and sophistication meet. As a wellness-focused urban oasis, The Westin Tokyo is designed to help you Sleep Well, Eat Well, and Move Well-restoring body and mind from the moment you arrive. From award-winning event spaces to renowned dining and spa facilities, every detail elevates your stay. Experience a destination where relaxation meets luxury and well-being prepares you for what lies ahead.See Less

## Green Key Certification

As an urban wellness hotel dedicated to offering both peace of mind and revitalization, The Westin Tokyo has earned the internationally recognized Green Key eco-label certification. This distinction is awarded to hotels, restaurants, and other establishments that demonstrate environmentally responsible operations.

The certification is granted by the Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE) to facilities that meet its standards, which include 75 mandatory criteria and 75 recommended practices across 13 categories. Today, more than 9,000 establishments in over 90 countries have received this certification.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Tokyo?

The check-in time at The Westin Tokyo is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Tokyo allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Tokyo.

What are the parking options at The Westin Tokyo?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Tokyo. Self parking is available for ¥1200.00 per hour. Valet parking is available for ¥2000.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Tokyo have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Tokyo has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Tokyo booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Tokyo?

The closest airport near The Westin Tokyo is Tokyo International Airport - Haneda (HND). HND is located approximately 15.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Tokyo have an airport shuttle to Tokyo Narita International Airport (NRT)?

Yes, The Westin Tokyo has an airport shuttle to Tokyo Narita International Airport (NRT).

Does The Westin Tokyo have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Tokyo has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does The Westin Tokyo have a Westin Club lounge?

Yes, The Westin Tokyo has Westin Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Westin Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
