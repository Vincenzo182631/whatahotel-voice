# The Guerlain Spa

Escape to a secret sanctuary hidden in the heart of Beau-Rivage Palace in Lausanne, Switzerland. Here in our spa retreat, surrounded by undulating wood, gentle light and beguiling aromas, inner calm reigns.

Across 1,500 square metres, the spa brings together one elegant suite for two, eight rituals and treatment rooms, a calming relaxation room, two changing areas with hammam, sauna, ice cave and ice fountain, a hairdressing studio, as well as heated indoor and outdoor pools.

Daily opening hours: 6:30 a.m. – 10:00 p.m.
