### Restaurant & Bar 19.20 Paris by Norbert Tarayre

Paying homage to the enduring influence of French bistronomic cuisine, Restaurant & Bar 19.20 Paris by Norbert Tarayre ushers in a new age of brilliance to a cherished culinary destination. Adding fresh perspective to the bistro concept, Chef Norbert engages with tradition through his unique approach. Positioned on the illustrious Avenue George V, the restaurant encapsulates the timeless elegance of Paris. Evocative of the Art Deco era, when Parisian life was synonymous with art, music, theatre and lifestyle, the venue is styled to reflect the glorious 1920s.

## A Renowned Chef

Chef Norbert Tarayre’s journey to Restaurant & Bar 19.20 Paris at the Prince de Galles Hotel has been, as he professes, one of a ‘lucky accident’. Taken under the wing of iconic Chef Bernard Loiseau who imparted his wisdom in the art of French cooking, he went on to work with celebrated Chef Marc Veyrat and then Chef Roland Mazere. He supported Chef Mauro Colagreco as Sous Chef when achieving his first Michelin star in 2012. Chef Norbert became a household name in France when he made his TV debut in ‘Top Chef’, a programme he continued to feature on for the next ten years, sharing his gastronomic insights, served with comedic repartee. His goal now is to offer belle époque cuisine that is not only affordable but also highly accessible, all supported with strong, dynamic service. He brings his talent and soul to the kitchen where he rustles up perfectly executed bistro dishes, crafted using local produce, always served with a side of panache and honesty.

## Tempting Sweet Treats

Talented Pastry Chef Hélène Kerloeguen has developed a unique collection of pastries that are both visually stunning and deliciously tasty. Renowned for her range of inventive flavors, her sweet and savory cakes, flans, tartes, and more are classics that have been revisited and given an imaginative twist. Supported by a skilled team of pâtissiers, the venue focuses on homemade recipes, delivered with uncompromising attention to detail. For those dining at Restaurant & Bar 19.20 Paris, Chef Hélène has created a dessert cart of ‘fait maison’ delicacies, whilst the coveted ritual of afternoon tea presents a moment to enjoy a 1920s inspired menu. The many enticing treats include a selection of firm French favorites, such as, pistachio-flavored Paris Brest, delicious Basque cake, tangy lemon tarts, pear bourdaloue and many more. Her passion and creativity for the art of pastry making extends further, to include signature desserts and celebratory confections for special times of the year.

## An Extraordinary Chef

A new restaurant opened its doors beginning of 2022 at Hotel Prince de Galles. At its helm is Akira Back - an extraordinary, Michelin-starred chef who already manages 23 restaurants around the world. Since his early days, he has been influenced by modern Japanese, Korean and international cuisine taking diners on a delicious journey to discover his own free and individual cuisine in an electrifying atmosphere that deliberately breaks the usual codes of gastronomy. The melting-pot of culinary traditions brings new flavor experiences and wild creativity. He is now bringing his cuisine to Paris for the first time.

The team set off to explore the great cognac terroirs and came up with ‘La Cognathèque’. Esteemed small producers and private houses are featured on the front page of the Journal du Cognac and tell stories of the authentic cognac houses.
