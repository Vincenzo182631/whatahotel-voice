# Rooms and Suites at Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris

## Accommodations

Steps away from the Champs-Elysées, one of the most beautiful avenues in the world, Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel in Paris, provides a luxurious retreat amidst the energy of France's capital...See More city. Select between 116 Art Deco-inspired luxurious hotel rooms and 43 spacious family suites, many of which include private balconies with stunning views of the Paris cityscape. All rooms and suites at our hotel include thoughtful amenities such as complimentary bottled mineral water and your choice of local Paris or international newspapers, daily. Enjoy our sleek marble bathrooms that feature mosaics inspired by the Art Deco era, as well as plush terry cloth robes and slippers and toiletries from the Lalique collection.See Less
