### Suite Lalique

This 165 sqm duplex Suite is a unique celebration of stylish elegance. Prince de Galles has teamed up with Lalique, the iconic French crystal glass manufacturer, to offer an experience like no other to be found in Paris. Nestled on the 8th and 9th floors, this 180 m² duplex embodies the values of excellence and respect for tradition that unite Prince de Galles and Maison Lalique.

Virtual Tour

Suite Lalique

## A Parisian Apartment Like No Other

The first level features a spacious entrance and office on the first floor. The golden mosaic decor, the deconstructed clock and the doors opening onto the terrace welcome guests in search of the utmost discretion.

## Elevated Elegance

The rooms upstairs are accessible either by a private lift or via a spectacular staircase. The second floor opens on a remarkable black-and-gold living room, where the Eiffel Tower perfectly integrates with the decor. An unobstructed view of the Sacré-Coeur can be enjoyed from the dining room through which a second, 72 m² terrace can be accessed.

## Comfort Meets Sophistication

The bedroom is decadence itself, composed of crystal and Coutard panels and the marble bathroom is the crowning feature of this exceptional suite, with its free-standing bathtub and a view of the Eiffel Tower.

### Suite Lalique

The Signature Suite Lalique offers an expansive 165 sqm (1775 sqft) of luxurious space, featuring a grand King-sized bed. The suite includes a spacious living and sitting area, a separate living room, and a dedicated dining room, perfect for both relaxation and entertaining.
