# Welcome to Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris

## Experience Paris Through Its Most Iconic Art Deco Landmark

The Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel in Paris, has intrigued guests since opening in 1929. We welcome you to storied Paris with five-star service and Art Deco chic. Set on Avenue George V, steps...See More from the Champs-Elysées, this hotel in Paris, France places you near luxury boutiques, the Seine and landmarks including the Arc de Triomphe. For couples' city breaks and luxury shopping stays, the serene Wellness Suite encourages relaxation and renewal, while concierge support can assist with reservations, transfers and itineraries. Rooms and suites at our luxury five-star hotel in the city center of Paris, France, are immaculately designed with home-like comforts and sweeping skyline views. Guests often highlight the central location, Art Deco atmosphere, comfortable rooms and attentive service. The 19.20 restaurant by Norbert Tarayre honors Parisian dining, while Akira Back brings his first European restaurant to the hotel.See Less

Prince de Galles Hotel, Art Deco Icon

## Elegance in Movement

From the moment Prince de Galles opened its doors in 1929, during the height of the Art Deco movement, it became the Parisian hotspot for the world’s most distinguished guests. Designed as a peaceful luxury home, it welcomed icons like Winston Churchill, Marlene Dietrich, and royalty, drawn by its charm and sophistication. Today, this legacy lives on, offering discerning travellers a true taste of Parisian elegance.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris?

The check-in time at Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris. with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €70.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris?

On-site valet parking is available at Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris. The daily valet fee is €30.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris booking.

What is the closest airport near Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris?

The closest airport near Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris is Paris/Orly Airport (ORY). ORY is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
