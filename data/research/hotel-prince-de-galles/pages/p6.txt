## CALMA PARIS

CALMA PARIS, a prestigious French Maison, offers a collection of experiences where high-performance treatments meet a profound sense of inner serenity.

Driven by advanced concentrated formulas and 100% natural, organic, and vegan ingredients, the brand delivers intensive treatments and rituals designed to reveal an instant glow, suitable for all ages and skin types.

This pursuit of balance is elevated by its signature ingredient: Precious Orange Blossom Absolute, whose therapeutic properties soothe the mind and melt away stress.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris have an on-site spa?

Yes, The Wellness Suite is located on-site at Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris?

Bateaux Mouches, Moulin Rouge, Crazy Horse, Lido, Château de Vaux-le-Vicomte and more are nearby Prince de Galles, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris.
