## A Haven of Peace

The Prince of Galles invites you to enjoy its culinary experience all year round on its terrace. Le Patio, surrounded by gardens and footpaths. A true oasis of calm in the heart of the bustling 8th arrondissement of Paris. Le Patio is the ideal place for lunch, dinner or simply sipping a cocktail al fresco.

## An Art Deco Jewel

The beauty and elegance of the Patio's architecture, which has remained intact since its inauguration in 1929, are unique and protected by the 'Bâtiments de France' (the French architectural review board) as a historical monument. With a little imagination, you will be able to hear the echo of Chopin’s recitals. As you may know, the famous composer resided in the building that stood in place of the hotel and his apartment was located just above the current Patio.
