Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai
Where the Glistening City Meets the Tranquility of the Sea
The call of a chaise on a pristine beach or the lure of shopping on an exclusive promenade. Rarely do luxury Dubai resorts pose such a delightful dilemma for their guests. Yet The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai places both within easy reach in the heart of Dubai Marina, where superlative shopping, dining and beaches shape days and nights to remember. Wake to Gulf views from luxurious resort accommodations and discover private beach access, landscaped gardens, relaxing pools, exceptional restaurants, a spa, Ritz Kids and the warm, attentive service guests consistently praise. For family beach stays and romantic escapes alike, this destination stands out among 5-star hotels in Jumeirah Beach Dubai. The city beyond is yours to explore.
Advisory - Travel to Middle East
We continue to closely monitor the current regional situation in the Middle East as it evolves. Our hotel remains open and operational and our teams are focused on supporting our guests at this time.
Learn More
In the heart of JBR, Dubai’s lively beachside boulevard lies The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai, the luxurious oceanfront oasis that blends a relaxing intimate retreat with the opulence and vibrancy of Dubai. Spacious guest rooms and suites feature private balconies with luxury amenities and breathtaking views of the Arabian Gulf, while 9 restaurants and bars present an array of casual and relaxed dining options. Onsite recreational activities include water sports, archery, volleyball, tennis, and yoga along with 6 swimming pools and Ritz Kids programming. The Ritz-Carlton Spa connects guests to the local heritage as they embark on a restorative journey of healing and relaxation. The Ritz-Carlton Club provides a relaxed lounge environment with a dedicated concierge to take care of your every need. Meetings go beyond the boardroom at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai. A dedicated conference center, with its own standalone entrance offers 32,000 square feet of flexible event space with the latest technology.

Sunset Walkthrough
Embrace the vibrancy of the city as it transitions from day to night with a 30-minute walking tour after sunset. Begin at the front of the resort and continue along JBR, pointing out some of the most significant landmarks across Dubai’s skyline, as they glisten in the night sky. Returning to the rear of the hotel, guests are invited to join a guided meditation to the sound of the waves, as they relax barefoot on beanbags at the beach. The experience is a thoughtful representation of the juxtaposition between the bright lights and high energy of the city versus the calming nature of the sea. It’s an opportunity to embrace both in harmony, for a truly fulfilling and transformative stay.

The Arabia Felix
The ancient lands of the Southern Arabian Peninsula were known as the Arabia ‘Felix’, literally translated as fertile, happy, fortunate, or blessed. Inspired by its romantic connotations of wellness, The Ritz-Carlton Spa has created a unique well-being experience, designed to connect our guests with the heritage of the region and to render happiness over your mind, body, and soul. Begin your Arabia Felix journey dressed in light Arabian robes, surrounded by the calming scent of bakhoor and the flicker of candlelight. Relax as your feet and legs are gently exfoliated with Dead Sea salt and sands derived from the Emirates and your feet are gently doused in soothing camel milk. Argan oil from North Africa is heated and massaged into your back whilst the natural healing vapors of frankincense immerse you in a deep state of rest, and a warm cup of hibiscus tea brings the journey to a relaxing end.

The Ritz Reef
The Ritz Reef cocktail combines the finest ingredients, expertly blended to create an unforgettable taste experience. It begins with the smooth notes of Coconut Rum, imparting a tropical flair that transports you to the sun-kissed shores of the Arabian Gulf. To complement this, a touch of Apricot Brandy adds a luscious sweetness, reminiscent of the succulent fruits found in the local markets. A hint of Aromatic Bitters provides a sophisticated complexity, accentuating the cocktail's rich flavors and adding a touch of refinement. The Ritz Reef cocktail is an invitation to embark on a sensory journey through Dubai's captivating atmosphere, available for guests to enjoy across the entire resort. Each sip is a celebration of the destination, as it encapsulates the essence of Dubai's opulence and cultural fusion. Encouraging guests to savor the flavors of the destination, The Ritz Reef is a testament to the hotel's commitment to providing an exceptional and immersive experience.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai is Dubai International Airport (DXB). DXB is located approximately 35.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
