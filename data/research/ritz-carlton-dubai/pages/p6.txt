A Dubai Beach Resort Offering Curated Experiences
Experiences
With a prime location on The Walk at JBR, The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai is the perfect place to begin your Dubai adventure. This luxury spa hotel in Dubai features easy access to attractions including Burj Khalifa, local golf courses, and an extensive offering of activities, including watersports and birdwatching. The Ritz Kids program educates and engages young guests with creative activities to expose them to Dubai’s environment and culture.
Signature Experiences
Mixology Class
Embark on a captivating journey of mixology mastery at the Library Bar, where guests are invited to indulge in a signature experience: a Mixology Class led by expert mixologists. Immerse yourself in the art of crafting exquisite cocktails as you learn the secrets behind the perfect balance of flavors and the intricate techniques that transform drinks into works of art.
Memorable Moments for Little Guests
At the Ritz Kids Club, little guests are invited to enjoy experiences designed to ignite their imaginations and create lasting memories. With a thoughtfully curated daily program of fun and educational activities, children can embark on a journey of discovery and delight. From engaging arts and crafts sessions to interactive storytelling, outdoor adventures, and themed games.
The Ritz-Carlton Spa
Amid Dubai Marina, The Ritz-Carlton Spa blends Eastern traditions and European techniques to relax and restore. It features 12 treatment rooms, private terraces, steam rooms, sauna, lounges, plunge pools, a fitness center and outdoor pool.

A Luxury Spa Blending East and West
Amid Dubai’s thriving Marina District, The Ritz-Carlton Spa heals, relaxes, cleanses and energizes with a careful blend of Eastern traditions and European techniques. Offering the luxury of pampering and the ability to hold time at a standstill, the hotel spa features 12 treatments rooms (three private rooms each have their own terrace), ladies’ and gentlemen’s tranquility lounges, eucalyptus-infused steam-rooms, an outdoor relaxation pool, hot and cold plunge pools and a state-of-the-art fitness center.

The Tennis Academy
Ladies and Gentlemen in search of an active lifestyle have unlimited recreation facilities to choose from with the four tennis courts being a highlight. In partnership with The Super Sports Tennis Academy, one of the leading companies in Dubai, we offer a plethora of tennis programs to suit every level - beginner or advanced. With 22 full time professional instructors across the city, the Academy offers the most action-packed tennis coaching program for juniors and adults alike – catering for all ages and abilities from tennis lessons and coaching to tournaments, competitions and special events.
Juniors' Tennis Coaching (Ages 4 to 18)
Encourage the tennis player within your child in a fun, friendly and interactive coaching environment. Students will be assessed and placed into various groups with other students of a similar age and ability so that they are comfortable within their peer group, giving them the opportunity of developing at their own pace.

Ritz Kids
Specifically Tailored to Kids Between 4 – 12 Years Old, the innovative Ritz Kids offering at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai enables young guests to discover Dubai and the other Emirates with their own eyes. The hotel’s family-friendly program features activities based on four concepts – water, land, environmental responsibility and culture which instills in children, fond memories and life-long knowledge. Tailored to resort guests aged four to 12, children enjoy art and crafts, sand castle making and more.

Ritz Kids Evening Program
Our Ritz Kids Evening Program is depending on the season, however, it can take place upon request for AED 250 per child and differs greatly from the regular day schedule, which also includes food, snacks, and beverages. This is a perfect opportunity for you to leave your child in our tender care while you enjoy a relaxed evening in one of our many restaurants. Advanced booking is required as space is limited.
Our team of professionals will ensure that your young ones will have a fantastic time with us!
Terms and conditions apply.
Ritz Kids Specialty Courses
Our Ritz Kids professionals offer a 2-hour specialty courses where children can join ‘Arts & Crafts’, ‘Handicrafts’, and ‘Origami Arts’. These are available for AED 80 per child on daily basis. Advanced booking is required.
Sightseeing and Attractions

Walking Into The Sunset
Embrace the vibrancy of the city as it transitions from day to night with a 30-minute walking tour at sunset. Begin in the resort gardens and walk through the winding pathways as one of our Ladies and Gentlemen point out some of the most significant landmarks across Dubai’s skyline, as they glisten in the early evening sky. Ending the walking tour with an Instagrammable sunset, and beachside moment as the guide regales tales and history of the country.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai offers a complimentary, 9:00 AM-7:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai have a pool on-site?
