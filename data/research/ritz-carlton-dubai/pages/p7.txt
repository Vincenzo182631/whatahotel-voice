Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai
A Season of Joy, Celebration and Wonder
Discover the wonder and merriment of the festive season with sensational year-end experiences at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai. Renowned for detail and excellence, the resort offers enchanting dining, exquisite views, and unparalleled entertainment throughout December.

Festive Feast At Home
Let The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai chefs bring the festive feast home, with a beautifully roasted whole turkey or an expertly cooked and newly introduced Beef Wellington. Both feats at home are accompanied by sumptuous sides, fresh salads and an indulgent selection of Christmas themed desserts. Thanksgiving: 21 to 30 November 2026. Christmas: 1 December to 27 December 2026
Legendary Christmas Day Brunch
Celebrate the season with the most exquisite outdoor feast, lively entertainment and magical Ritz Kids moments, including a special visit from Santa. Friday, 25 December 2026, 1 pm to 4 pm.

Christmas At Tamoka
Spend Christmas by the beach at Tamoka, savoring a six-course menu by Chef Sergio, breathtaking Ain Dubai views and captivating live performances. Friday, 25 December 2026, 1.30 pm to 4.30 pm.
Book Now
Festive Afternoon Tea At The Lobby Lounge
A refined festive Afternoon Tea awaits at The Lobby Lounge, with delicate savouries, seasonal sweets, live entertainment and an optional sparkling indulgence. Daily, 12:30 pm to 5 pm 4 to 30 December 2026, excluding 25 December 2026 Live entertainment on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.
Book Now
Christmas Eve At Splendido
Celebrate ‘Buon Natale’ at Splendido. Dine under the stars on the Tuscan-style piazza or indoors for classical sophistication. Enjoy a specially crafted seasonal menu of Italian delights and a visit from Santa. Thursday, 24 December 2026, 7 pm to 11 pm.
Book Now
Christmas Eve At Caravan
Join us the night before Christmas for an incredible festive dinner buffet at Caravan. An exquisite buffet of global flavours awaits, including festive roasts, fresh seafood and grills, alongside live entertainment and a special visit from Santa. Thursday, 24 December 2026, 6:30 pm to 10:30 pm.
Book Now
