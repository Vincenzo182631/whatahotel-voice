By harvesting a variety of herbs, microgreens, lettuce, and other ingredients at the on-site hydroponic facility, just a few steps away from the kitchen, the resort promises daily delivery of the most nutritious ingredients in dishes served across numerous award-winning restaurants.

La Baie Day Pass
Dip into the adults’ only pool and afterward, cool off with the finest grub. The La Baie day pass is available daily, with a min spend of AED 150 per person Mon. to Fri., and AED 300 per person on weekends and public holidays, both fully redeemable on food and beverages at La Baie. Please note the pool and cabana access is for La Baie only.
Happy Hour
Get into the spirit and take advantage of views across the resort’s gardens, Arabian Gulf or the dazzling JBR skyline while sipping a crafted beverage, at one of the eight restaurants and bars at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai. Enjoy a selection of happy hour deals with buy one, get one with our compliments across a range of house pours, mixed beverages, grapes, and hops. Hours 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM at Gulf Pavilion
Mixology Class
Unleash your inner mixologist at the Library Bar. Join a Mixology Class and learn the art of crafting three unique drinks of your own under the expert guidance of our talented mixologists. Pre-booking is required for this exclusive class. AED 150 per person | Every Friday & Saturday, from 3 pm - 5 pm.
Ritz Kids
At every restaurant, a special menu has been curated for the little ones to enjoy. Ritz Kids are encouraged to explore the various cuisines around the resort’s award-winning restaurants, with dishes prepared meticulously to entice their taste buds, and inspire a sense of adventure and exploration within six different cuisines. The Kids menu delights and engages your children to interact with the various nationalities and cuisines the resort offers, along with cultivating the little ones to explore the healthy options harvested at the on-site vertical farm.
