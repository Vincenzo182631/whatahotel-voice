Customized Experience
Located on Level 3 with breathtaking sea views, The Club Lounge is an intimately exclusive venue for Club Level guests.
Club Level Guests May Enjoy the Following Food and Beverage Presentations Daily:
Continental breakfast 7 to 11 a.m.
Light lunch 12 to 2 p.m.
Afternoon Tea 3 to 4:30 p.m.
Hors d’oeuvres 5:30 to 7:30 p.m.
Cordials and desserts 8:15 to 10 p.m.
Please Note:
Children under the age of 10 must be accompanied by an adult at all times; No children under the age of 10 are permitted after 8:00 p.m.
Appropriate clothing and footwear is required at all times.
Club Level guests may invite visitors, subject to availability, at a supplementary charge.
Applicable food and beverage charges will be applied to guests who choose to enjoy breakfast in their room or one of our resort restaurants.

Savor the Sunrise
Awaken to a Sumptuous Breakfast with Mesmerizing Ocean and Ain Dubai Views. As you step out onto your private balcony, a breathtaking panorama unfolds before your eyes – the vast expanse of the sparkling ocean, with the iconic Ain Dubai standing tall on the horizon. Immerse yourself in the tranquil ambiance as the morning sun casts its golden hues across the water. Sip on a freshly brewed cup of coffee or tea, savoring the aroma as you take in the magnificent view. A sumptuous breakfast spread awaits, elegantly presented with an array of delectable treats. From flaky pastries to fresh fruits, from made-to-order omelets to tantalizing local specialties, every bite is a delightful journey for your taste buds. Relish this intimate and serene start to your day, as you indulge in a truly unforgettable Breakfast with a View experience, immersing yourself in the beauty of Dubai's coastline and the grandeur of Ain Dubai.
