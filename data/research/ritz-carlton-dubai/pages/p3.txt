Indulge in personalized services, world-class amenities, and the legendary hospitality of The Ritz-Carlton, ensuring a stay that is both effortless and unforgettable.

Royal Suite
Perched on the highest floor of the resort, the Royal Suite at The Ritz-Carlton, Dubai offers an unparalleled vantage point over the shimmering Arabian Gulf. Spanning an expansive 308 square meters, this regal two-bedroom suite features a master bedroom with a king bed and a second bedroom with two queen beds, designed to host families or discerning travelers seeking elevated privacy and space. A private balcony and floor-to-ceiling windows invite in natural light and serene sea views.
