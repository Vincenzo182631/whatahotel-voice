# Indulge Your Senses at This Lush Tropical Retreat on the Oceanfront

A charming coastal resort in a peaceful sanctuary, just a short drive from many scenic attractions and tourism hotspots of Pattaya, Thailand, Andaz Pattaya Jomtien Beach offers a welcoming destination for well-traveled explorers and families to reconnect with nature and discover Thai culture. Enjoy a relaxing escape enriched with myriad dining, wellness and leisure activities that will indulge your senses and inspire curiosity.

## Rooms & Suites

The resort offers 204 spacious, locally inspired rooms with a variety of options to choose from, including lifestyle beachfront suites and lavish pool villas. For larger families and groups, two houses with multiple rooms provide the perfect balance of space and privacy.

## DINING

View All Dining

### Sunset Serenade

Experience the enchantment of an intimate beachfront dining, where the soothing sounds of the waves create a magical atmosphere. Surrounded by the tranquil beauty of the ocean and the warm glow of candlelight, our dedicated staff will fulfill your every wish, guaranteeing an unforgettable evening of romance and allure.

### The Sundown Grill

Indulge by the shore with an irresistible spread of premium meats, fresh-caught seafood, vibrant salads, and decadent desserts - served just steps from the waves. Stay for the thrilling fire shows and soak up the unforgettable coastal vibes.
Book now and make your favorite night with World of Hyatt discount at 10%.

Available exclusively on every Friday evenings \| THB 1,790++ per adult and children under 12 enjoy 50% off!

### La Cucina

Enjoy homemade pastas, freshly baked pizzas straight from the oven and other culinary favorites and specialties from Italy’s Mediterranean coast at La Cucina. The restaurant’s blue and white hand-painted tiles capture the breezy tropical feel of coastal dining.

Details + Reservations

### Village Butcher

Village Butcher steakhouse brings you the finest cuts of meat from around the world, including grass-fed beef and boutique-farmed meats, perfectly grilled to your liking. Complement your meal with a selection of wines spanning old-world classics and contemporary offerings.

Details + Reservations

### Wok Wok

Take your pick from a feast of authentic Thai flavors at Wok Wok. Savor specialties from different regions of Thailand and experience an exciting journey for your taste buds in a relaxed, convivial atmosphere—a delicious way to spice up your day.

### Fish Club (Seafood Grill)

At our beachside restaurant and bar, enjoy the sea breeze and the enticing aroma of fresh seafood caught by local fishermen as it’s expertly grilled the traditional way over coconut husks. A selection of light Asian and Western fare is also available. Come evening, enjoy beautiful sunset views and creatively crafted cocktails as live DJ music sets the tempo for chilling out.

Details + Reservations

### Teak Lounge

Overlooking the serene Raintree Court and water lily pond, Teak Lounge exudes an ambiance that feels homely and relaxed. It’s an idyllic spot to unwind with a morning coffee, light bites or pre- and after-dinner drinks and cocktails.

### Ruen Thai

Make yourself at home with a refreshing cup of tea or coffee in our cozy Thai teahouse. Traditionally crafted from teakwood, Ruen Thai offers indoor and outdoor seating across two levels, complete with soothing views of beautiful banyan trees and Thai-inspired décor that feels regal and restful. It’s the ideal place to disconnect and let your imagination run free.
