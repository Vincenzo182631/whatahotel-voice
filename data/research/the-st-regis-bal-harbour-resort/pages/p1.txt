## St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort Design

The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort, situated on nine acres of stunning oceanfront property and designed by Sieger Suarez Architectural Partnership, is truly the modern interpretation of St. Regis. Delight in the masterful design of our Miami Beach luxury resort, with interiors by world-renowned Yabu Pushelberg that pays homage to Miami’s Art Deco style and an impressive art collection by local and internationally known artists - all chosen to engage the observer and spark intrigue and conversation.

Arrival

Inigo Manglano-Ovalle’s “Cloud Prototype No. 4”, a captivating silver cloud sculpture suspended from the ceiling motions newcomers and return visitors closer to the Atlantic Ocean. From there, dramatic design in the main entrance hall reflects light against a mirrored backdrop with large glittering rock crystal chandeliers – a vision of more grand spaces to come.

Inspired Accommodations

Thoroughly elegant and luxurious space offers an unmatched experience in an incomparable location. Beautifully appointed guest rooms and suites are each thoughtfully configured to take full advantage of the destination’s spectacular unobstructed azure blue ocean views. Rooms reflect a contemporary style with Art Deco elements, a nod to Morris Lapidus, the legendary architect of neo-baroque modern Miami, and are a sanctuary of relaxation with cool blues and grays, mirrored surfaces and sea-inspired motifs.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $200.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort?

On-site valet parking is available at The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort is Miami International Airport (MIA). MIA is located approximately 16.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
