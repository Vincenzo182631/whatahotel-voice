# Discover Our Hotel Near Haulover Park

## Experiences

Situated among lush tropical gardens, white sand beaches, and turquoise waters, The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort is located in the north Miami Beach enclave of Bal Harbour. Considered among the area's most distinguished addresses, our luxury hotel near the Art Deco District in Miami Beach, sits on this exclusive island village, ideally poised on the only inlet of the barrier island between the Atlantic Ocean and the Intracoastal Waterway. Enjoy easy access to all that North Miami Beach has to offer from our oceanfront hotel. This island is home to the exquisite Bal Harbour Shops. Located near the hotel, directly across Collins Avenue, these luxury shops serve as an international destination, attracting both guests and residents with prestigious designer boutiques, bistros, and outdoor cafes. Take a short drive and discover the rich culture and vibrant energy of South Beach from our hotel near downtown Miami

### Elevate Your Stay in Bal Harbour with Cultural Entertainment

Indulge in the fine art, music and theatrical options found in Miami and surrounding areas at our hotel near South Beach, Miami.

Culture & Entertainment

Mere minutes from the whirlwind energy of the world-renowned South Beach, Ocean Drive, and the Art Deco District, The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort, Miami Beach is the perfect place to expand your incomparable lifestyle amid the vibrant energy and artistry of South Florida. Guests of our Bal Harbour resort will enjoy easy access to the refined entertainment options of Miami as well as the surrounding area.

Greater Miami and the Beaches provide countless ways to indulge in Miami culture and entertainment and experience a myriad of popular Miami activities, from the Miami City Ballet and Miami Orchestra to an array of botanical gardens such as Fairfield Tropical Botanic Garden and Vizcaya Museum & Gardens.

The Miami City Ballet has grown to be one of the largest, most acclaimed companies in the U.S. and the Vizcaya Museum & Gardens is the former estate of businessman James Deering of the Deering McCormick-International Harvester fortune.

In addition to South Beach's indelible nightlife, its historic district is host to museums, theaters, art galleries, fashion, fine restaurants and a rich architectural history.

Among many is The Fillmore Theater, a celebrated performing arts center and previous host to the 1970's Jackie Gleason Show, the renowned New World Symphony, and The Pérez Art Museum Miami (PAMM) - a contemporary art museum located within the new Museum Park in Downtown Miami.

A plethora of recreational opportunities abounds in the surrounding areas, also a short drive away include:

- \- The Bank Atlantic Center - home to the NHL Florida Panthers and Ft. Lauderdale's premier concert venue
- \- Sun Life Stadium - home to the NFL Miami Dolphins and numerous concerts and events
- \- Marlins Stadium - home to the MLB Miami Marlins
- \- Kaseya Center - home to the NBA Miami Heat and one of the city's headline concert venues
- \- Miami Beach Botanical Gardens
- \- Bass Museum of Art
- \- New World Symphony
- \- Pérez Art Museum Miami
- \- Fairchild Tropical Botanical Garden
- \- Miami's Design District
- \- Bal Harbour Shops
- \- Broward Center for the Performing Arts
- \- The Miami Arscht Center for the Performing Arts
- \- Coconut Grove's Cocowalk area
- \- The Miami Seaquarium
- \- Zoo Miami
- \- Miami Children's Museum
- \- Lowe Art Museum- located in Coral Gables and one of the largest museums in Florida
- \- The world-renowned Everglades, located southwest of Miami are an intriguing eco-system that's host to more than 45 varieties of plants that grow nowhere else on Earth. As well, the Everglades are home to over 350 types of birds, 500 types of fish and dozens of reptiles and mammals.
- \- The islands of the Florida Keys run south west from the city of Miami. They span some 110 miles and are connected by bridges and causeways.

### Family Traditions at St. Regis

At The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort, we are dedicated to crafting an exceptional stay for our youngest guests. Just as our founding family, the Astors, reveled in their cherished traditions, our Family Traditions program is a suite of tailored activities and thoughtful amenities, curated to foster an atmosphere of wonderment while exploring the enriching culture and nature of Bal Harbour. Our hotel near downtown Miami offers an immersive opportunity to relax as a family, where you can share the magic of quality time or the chance for an inspiring break, regardless of your age. From the moment you arrive, we have prepared a selection of recreational activities, dining options, childcare services, and delightful touches that celebrate the simple joys of being a kid. We welcome your child with a personalized pack, with anything from coloring books to a library card that unlocks a library based on interests and reading levels.

Kid-Friendly Amenities & Services

While in residence, discover an array of thoughtful services and amenities designed especially for children and families. Allow our dedicated team to provide a flawless experience and every convenience a family needs to enjoy cherished time together.

**Recreation**

Open to children of all ages, the lower-level Resort’s Family Pool is ideal for families spending time together. Pool attendants are pleased to provide pool towels, sun protection and cold, infused fruit water any time throughout the day. The beach is also open to children of all ages.

**Necessities & Provisions**

Our resort is supplied with a variety of family necessities, such as cribs plus custom bath time amenities for our youngest guests including no-tears shampoo & conditioner, stepstools, and children sized robes and slippers.

To make reservations or for more details, please contact the Concierge at least 24 hours in advance.

Enriching Experiences

We look forward to creating indelible memories for your family to share.

**In Room Movie Night**

Make memories that last a lifetime, with a movie night and complete it with popcorn or snacks pre-ordered from our Private Dining in the comfort of your room or suite.

**Children’s Afternoon Tea - La Gourmandise**

Children of all ages will delight in this bright and festive take on a timeless tradition, with sweet treats, exquisite desserts, indulgent milkshakes and more.

Please inquire with concierge for reservations.

**Explore the Exotic Everglades**

Take a family Jeep excursion to the Big Cypress National Preserve, one of the most beautiful locations of the Everglades National Park. On the Jeep tour you will see amazing birds, interesting wildlife, alligators and gorgeous scenery. After the Jeep ride you will take a private airboat ride with a guide. The Everglades tour Jeep will pick you up and drop you off at our resort and can be customized to include a special, casual picnic. The tour lasts approximately five hours or more from beginning to end.

Please see the Concierge for availability and current pricing.

**Miami Children’s Museum**

Play, learn, imagine, create and explore at the Miami Children's Museum, featuring fourteen exciting galleries full of interactive exhibits for the whole family. Get active by climb "Mt. MiChiMu," a challenging rock wall or climb through a two-story sandcastle at the "Castle of Dreams." Families looking for a learning adventure can investigate the mysteries of the deep while peering into a 900-gallon marine tank at "Ocean Odyssey."

**Seasonal Activities Calendar**

The St. Regis Family Traditions program includes a calendar of activities for our youngest guests. Please see our Guest Recognition Hosts for the current schedule of times and locations.
