# Stay in Our Elegant Hotel Suites in Miami Beach

## Accommodations

Escape to your perfectly appointed sanctuary in Miami Beach. Relax, unwind, and indulge in the privacy and style of our beachfront accommodations in Miami Beach. Each of our 216 guest rooms and suites harmonizes the luxury of our Bal Harbour resort with the vibrant turquoise waters of the Atlantic Ocean and the pristine white sands of Miami Beach. Choose from a hotel room, suite, or 2-bedroom suite and take in the tropical vista. Our hotel rooms in Miami Beach offer floor-to-ceiling windows, which lead to a spacious, glass-walled balcony furnished with recliners or lounge chairs to enhance your relaxation. Inside our hotel suites in Miami Beach, find your furnishings awash in a palette of neutral tones combined with organic textures, such as bleached Black Limba wood and oak herringbone wall paneling. Sea-life details in the carpet, fabrics, and artwork further reflect the surrounding natural beauty. In the evening, retreat to your room or suite and sink into your signature pillowtop

Oceanfront Suites

### Stay Where Ocean Views Meet Pure Luxury

Our perfectly appointed suites with glass-walled balconies offer stunning ocean views and exclusive amenities: complimentary roundtrip airport transfers (MIA, FLL, or Opa Locka), two 60-min spa treatments at our Forbes Five-Star Spa, $120 daily breakfast credit at Atlantikos or through Private Dining, a VIP welcome amenity, daily oceanfront villa rental (11 a.m.–5 p.m., max 4 guests per villa), $500 resort credit (towards food, beverage, & spa), in-suite non-alcoholic minibar, valet parking for 1 car, and bespoke Butler Service with unlimited packing services, steaming, and expedited requests.

### A Commitment to Excellence in our Bal Harbour Suites

A signature offering for more than a century, the butler service for guests in our oceanfront suites in Miami Beach ensures that your unique preferences are understood and realized.

Learn More

St. Regis Butler Service has been a treasured hallmark of the St. Regis experience for over one hundred years. From a perfectly pressed suit ready for an important meeting, to a favorite book at hand for an evening read, to a thoughtful, last minute gift for a loved one, no request is too small or unattainable no matter what the hour of the day. St. Regis Butler Service ensures that unique preferences are known and addressed flawlessly at our St. Regis hotels around the world.

At every St. Regis hotel, guests have access to St. Regis Butler Service via the Butler Service Desk. Should you need something for your travels or just for your convenience, St. Regis Butler Service will be sure to have on hand, or source your required item. Whatever the request, guests may dial for prompt service on their guest room phone.

##### Available in Suites Only

**Garment Pressing:**

Once unpacked, simply let your St. Regis Butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. Two garments per person per day may be pressed complimentary.

For all other wishes or requests, St. Regis Butler Service is pleased to assist.
