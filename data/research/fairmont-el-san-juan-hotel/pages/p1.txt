## Puerto Rico's most _iconic_ hotel since 1958

Made legendary by the people and performances that define San Juan, Fairmont El San Juan Hotel invites locals and travelers to experience authentic Puerto Rican celebrations across generations. The hotel’s breathtaking décor, inspired cuisine, lush poolscape, and iconic entertainment, boast a vibrant atmosphere where sophisticated celebrants unite.
