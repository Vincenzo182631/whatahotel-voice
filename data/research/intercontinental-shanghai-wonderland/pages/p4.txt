### Pickleball Competition · Extraordinary Vitality

The trendy sport pickleball has arrived at the hotel. This internet-famous sport combines the rhythm of tennis, the agility of badminton and the skills of table tennis, with simple and easy-to-learn gameplay. It is not only a fashionable interactive choice but also a fun way to burn calories.

### Subterranean Adventure · Water Show

The lobby takes "Subterranean Adventure" as its theme, with bronze-colored wall facades reproducing the natural appearance of quarry rock layers, winding and undulating. Black decorative materials echo the natural cliff walls, emitting golden light that makes guests feel as if they are in the center of the rock formation.
