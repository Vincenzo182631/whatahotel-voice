Lowest altitude hotel in the world iconic
Sheshan Smurf Park

1. Architectural Legend

### Architectural Legend

Nestled in the Sheshan Tourism Resort of Songjiang, Shanghai, the hotel is built on the cliff face of an abandoned quarry 88 meters below sea level. It is the world’s first luxury hotel constructed inside a quarry. Honored as one of the "Top 10 Architectural Wonders of the World" by National Geographic’s Megastructures.
