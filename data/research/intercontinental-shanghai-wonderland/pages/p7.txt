### Disappointed

26 days ago

On arrival we were told the spa facilities and pool were out of action for our stay. We had not received any notification prior to our stay. Shuttle bus was put on to use facilities at different hotel times were limited to go.
Hotel was decorated in very dark colours making it very dark inside, obviously being built in a quarry you would expect some lighting issues but we literally had to put our torches on in day time at times. Lifts were very slow often waiting a good five+ minutes for one.
Furniture in bedroom seemed quite basic scratched and tatty. Our kettle lid did not stay closed when boiling.
