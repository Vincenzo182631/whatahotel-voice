### Pickleball Competition · Extraordinary Vitality

The trendy sport pickleball has arrived at the hotel. This internet-famous sport combines the rhythm of tennis, the agility of badminton and the skills of table tennis, with simple and easy-to-learn gameplay. It is not only a fashionable interactive choice but also a fun way to burn calories.

Learn more

### Subterranean Adventure · Water Show

The lobby takes "Subterranean Adventure" as its theme, with bronze-colored wall facades reproducing the natural appearance of quarry rock layers, winding and undulating. Black decorative materials echo the natural cliff walls, emitting golden light that makes guests feel as if they are in the center of the rock formation.

Learn more

### Underwater Dining Delight

The specialty restaurant, Mr. Fisher is hidden beneath the water surface. Inspired by crystal caves carved by flowing water, its dazzling light and shadow blend seamlessly with the space, creating a romantic and mysterious underwater ambiance. You may immerse yourself in the vivid scene of underwater fish swimming.

Learn more

### Cai Feng Elegant Feast

Cai Feng Lou Chinese Restaurant inherits the essence of Chinese culinary culture, interpreting Chinese food charm through elegant private rooms and crafted delicacies. Its offerings have repeatedly won the authoritative recognition of "Restaurant Review Awards - Best Chinese Restaurant" .

Learn more

### Architectural Legend

Nestled in the Sheshan Tourism Resort of Songjiang, Shanghai, the hotel is built on the cliff face of an abandoned quarry 88 meters below sea level. It is the world’s first luxury hotel constructed inside a quarry. Honored as one of the "Top 10 Architectural Wonders of the World" by National Geographic’s Megastructures.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Shanghai Wonderland?

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Shanghai Wonderland?

Check-in at InterContinental Shanghai Wonderland is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is InterContinental Shanghai Wonderland from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Shanghai Wonderland is conveniently located near Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA), approximately 18.58 miles away (29.9 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
