# A Lotte Guide to the Holidays in NYC

At Lotte New York Palace, you’re in the midst of it all, with the season unfolding on both sides of our doors. Something rather wonderful happens to New York for the holidays. Fifth Avenue dresses for the occasion. The Rockefeller Center tree glows as Manhattan embraces the winter season.

A Few Traditions of Our Own

New Year's Day At Villard

January 1, 2027

The first brunch of the year should set a certain standard. Return to Villard for its signature Build Your Own Benedict, with a generous selection of ingredients for starting 2027 precisely to your taste.

A New York holiday tradition returns on November 23. Discover Lotte New York's famous holiday décor as the Palace courtyard and surrounding spaces take on their signature seasonal look, creating an unforgettable setting in the heart of Manhattan.

A Visit From Santa

November 29, 2026 – December 20, 2026

Every Sunday \| 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM

A beloved holiday guest returns to the Grand lobby. Gather the family for a visit with Santa Claus, share Christmas wishes, and capture a photograph to remember, with a little holiday wonder along the way.

Letters to Santa

December 1 – 24, 2026

Some correspondence deserves special handling. Our youngest guests receive a personalized postcard upon check-in, ready to be slipped into our larger-than-life North Pole mailbox. Keep an eye on the room. Santa has been known to write back.

## The Season’s Best,   Courtesy of the Palace

### Radio City Christmas Spectacular

#### November 4, 2026 – January 4, 2027

Dress for the occasion and settle in for high kicks, toy soldiers, and precision only the Rockettes could make look effortless. The Palace tip: dinner before, a nightcap after, and plenty of holiday spectacle in between.

### Thanksgiving Day Parade

#### Thursday, November 26, 2026

Start Thanksgiving with a New York tradition a century in the making. Watch larger-than-life balloons, floats, and performers make their way through Manhattan for the 100th parade. Our advice? Arrive early. Some traditions are worth setting the alarm for.

### Rockefeller Center Christmas Tree

#### Tree Lighting: December 2, 2026

Go after dark. Take in the tree in full glow, then make your way up Fifth Avenue while the storefronts are still shining. The Palace awaits just a few blocks later, which is rather convenient when the temperature drops.

### Fifth Avenue Holiday Windows

Start at Bergdorf Goodman and follow Fifth Avenue south, where holiday windows turn window shopping into something closer to theater. Take your time. For once, arriving nowhere in particular is precisely the plan.

### Ice Skating in New York

#### Location: Wollman Rink & Bryant Park

For skyline views, make it Wollman. For a skate followed by holiday shopping, Bryant Park has our vote. Either way, rosy cheeks and something warm afterwards are highly recommended.

### New York Holiday Lights

Once evening falls, take the long way back. Follow the glow of Fifth Avenue storefronts, grand holiday trees, and Midtown displays on your way to the hotel. Comfortable shoes beneath the good coat are strongly advised.

### Holiday Shops at Bryant Park

#### October 28, 2026 – January 3, 2027

Browse the little shops, find something you won’t see on Fifth Avenue, and keep something warm in hand while you do it. Go toward dusk and stay for the lights.

### New Year’s Eve in Times Square

#### December 31, 2026

New York hardly needs an excuse for a late night. Join the revelry in Times Square for the countdown, confetti, and a certain rather famous ball drop. Dress accordingly, raise a glass, and welcome 2027 from a city that knows a thing or two about making an entrance.
