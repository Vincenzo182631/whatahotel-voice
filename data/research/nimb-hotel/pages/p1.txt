## Exclusive luxury in the heart of Copenhagen

In the heart of Copenhagen, you find unforgettable Nimb that offers views to the magical Tivoli Gardens amusement park.

Nimb is a 5-star superior boutique hotel with an exclusive wellness area and a roof terrace with pool. Nimb also offers a world of culinary delight at its gastronomic offerings.

## A culinary experience like no other

Restaurant & bar

In the very heart of Copenhagen in magical Tivoli Gardens, you find Nimb’s gastronomic universe with everything a culinary soul can desire. From French cuisine at Nimb Brasserie to Danish open-faced sandwiches at Fru Nimb.

Nimb Bar entices you with well-crafted cocktails and afternoon teas served under the splendid chandeliers, while Nimb Roof in the summer months offers the city’s most magnificent sunsets, summer drinks and exciting events. Our very own patisserie, Cakenhagen, completes the culinary experience. Every day, their display stands are filled with freshly made cream cakes, pastries and the finest petits fours.

## Condé Nast Traveller Triple Crown winner

We are deeply honored to be named a Condé Nast Traveller Triple Crown winner!

As the only hotel in Denmark to receive this distinction in both the UK and the US editions of Condé Nast, Nimb now joins a select group of hotels worldwide that, over the past three decades, have been recognised across Condé Nast Traveller’s three most prestigious accolades: the Hot List, the Gold List, and the Readers’ Choice Awards. This recognition means a great deal to us, not only because it reflects the appreciation of travel experts and editors, but because it is ultimately shaped by the guests who choose to stay with us and return time and again.

We accept this honor with immense gratitude and humility. To our guests, our partners, and our dedicated team: thank you for being part of Nimb’s journey and for making moments worth remembering, year after year.
