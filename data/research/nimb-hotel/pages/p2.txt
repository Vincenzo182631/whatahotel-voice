### Taste Copenhagen with Nimb’s Gastronomic Experiences

_Nimb is a culinary universe in the heart of Copenhagen, where quality, passion, and flavor come together. With several distinctive dining venues under one roof, Nimb offers everything from timeless classics to refined gourmet experiences — always with a focus on craftsmanship, atmosphere, and attention to detail._

## Gourmet restaurants in Copenhagen

Discover French classics at Nimb Brasserie or enjoy traditional Danish open-faced sandwiches and popular dinner dishes at Fru Nimb. Both restaurants combine well-loved cuisine with an elegant and welcoming atmosphere.

## Cocktail bar in Copenhagen with atmosphere and views

For cocktail enthusiasts, Nimb Bar offers expertly crafted drinks and elegant afternoon tea in sophisticated surroundings. During the summer months, Nimb Roof transforms into a stylish cocktail bar in Copenhagen, where guests can enjoy the sunset over the city with a refreshing cocktail in hand.

## Desserts, pastries, and sweet creations from Cakenhagen

Indulge in delicate pastries, desserts, and petit fours from Cakenhagen — Nimb’s own patisserie. Here, classic craftsmanship meets modern flavors, creating sweet moments to end a meal or enjoy as a refined treat during the day.
