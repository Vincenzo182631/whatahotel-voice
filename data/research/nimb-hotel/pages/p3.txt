## About Nimb Wellness

We have created a true 300 m2 oasis for you at Nimb. Experience the wonderful hammam and steam area with beautiful changing rooms and a relaxation area with free access to refreshments. Nimb Wellness also offers treatments and massages in the spa area.

Nimb Club members and hotel guests enjoy free access to Nimb Wellness, including the fitness studio, hammam and relaxation area.

The minimum age to access the facilities is 16 years. However, younger children in the company of an adult are welcome in the hammam and relaxation area.

Guests from outside can book treatments with one of our skilled therapists. They can furthermore purchase access to the hammam and relaxation area for a fee of 300 DKK.

## A space just for you

## The Moroccan Hammam

At the heart of Nimb Wellness, you find our beautifully vaulted Moroccan hammam. Here, you can relax in the 50°C steam bath after training, before a treatment or when you simply crave a little extra pampering.  A visit to the hammam is beneficial for your health as it stimulates blood circulation.

## Fitness

The gym is fully equipped with state-of-the-art Technogym machines including TRX equipment. Nimb Wellness also offers access to cardio-training in a specially designed 34 degrees hot room. Hot workouts help you burn more calories and leave you with a wonderfully detoxed sensation.

## Nimb Club

As a member, you will gain access to Nimb Roof and Nimb Wellness – usually reserved for hotel guests only.

You will also receive a Tivoli Pass Gold, invitations to exclusive events, and unique experiences in the magical Tivoli Gardens.
