# Luxury rooms in the center of Madrid

The 114 rooms and elegant suites at **Hotel Villa Real 5\*** feature privileged balconies or terraces with spectacular views of the **Congress of Deputies**, **Plaza de las Cortes**, and central Madrid.

\\
\\
**Grand Suite**\\
\\
The 56m² Grand Suite, located on the hotel's fifth floor, offers a spacious bedroom, bathroom, foyer, and balcony for a luxurious stay in Madrid. \\
\\
**Suite**\\
\\
The 40m² Suites at the 5-star Hotel Villa Real are spread over two comfortable levels and each features its own private terrace of over 15m². \\
\\
**Junior Suite Duplex**\\
\\
The Duplex Junior Suites are 35m² rooms spread over two levels, featuring a living room completely separate from the sleeping area. \\
\\
**Junior Suite**\\
\\
The Junior Suites, luxurious 38m² rooms with balconies and splendid views of Calle del Prado, combine exquisite interiors with the magic of the outdoors. \\
\\
**Family Room**\\
\\
The family rooms, accommodating up to four people and spread over two levels, feature a bedroom and en-suite bathroom with shower on the lower level and a double bedroom and en-suite bathroom on the upper level. \\
\\
**Double Superior with views**\\
\\
Superior rooms with views are 25m² double rooms with a sitting area and sofa. All rooms overlook Plaza de las Cortes or adjacent streets. \\
\\
**Double Superior**\\
\\
Superior Rooms are 25m² double rooms with a sitting area and sofa. They are very quiet as they overlook the interior courtyard.
