# Hotel Villa Real 5\* in the center of Madrid

Located in the heart of the Art Triangle, formed by the iconic **Prado Museum, Museo Nacional Centro de Arte Reina Sofía and Thyssen-Bornemisza Museum**, Hotel Villa Real 5\* blends elegantly into one of the most authentic architectural settings in Habsburgs' Madrid and the **Literary Quarter**.

Hotel Villa Real has received the **EarthCheck Silver Certified** recognition as part of Derby Hotels’ commitment to sustainability.

## ALL THE SERVICES OF A GREAT 5-STAR HOTEL

DISCOVER EVERYTHING HOTEL VILLA REAL HAS TO OFFER

Blending art, modern comfort and thoughtful service, Hotel Villa Real 5\* turns every stay into a distinctive experience. Our Guest Relations & Concierge team provides attentive, personalized support to care for every detail with the utmost dedication.

Enjoy exclusive services such as fine dining, a fully equipped gym and elegant event spaces, together with personalized concierge service. Our team is available 24 hours a day to attend to your needs and make your stay truly memorable.
