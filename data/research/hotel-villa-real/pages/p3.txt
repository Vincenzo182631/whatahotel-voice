# 5-STAR HOTEL IN THE HEART OF MADRID

Hotel Villa Real 5\* is located on **Plaza de las Cortes**, opposite the **Congress of Deputies** and just a few minutes from Atocha and Recoletos stations.

Its unbeatable location **in the very heart of Madrid’s cultural, political and financial center** ensures a comfortable stay with every convenience to make the most of your visit to the city.

Located just a few steps from the **golden museum triangle** formed by the **Prado Museum**, the **Thyssen-Bornemisza Museum** and the **Reina Sofía Foundation**, it is the ideal starting point for exploring the city on foot and visiting major attractions such as Puerta del Sol, Retiro Park and Habsburgs' Madrid.

Take advantage of the nearby Gran Vía, famous for its shops and international boutiques, to go shopping and enjoy the wide range of shows that Madrid’s nightlife has to offer.

The nearest metro stop to the hotel is Sevilla (L2).
