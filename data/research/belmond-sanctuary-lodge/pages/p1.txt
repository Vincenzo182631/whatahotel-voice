Built in the 15th century as the estate of the royal Incan emperor Pachacuti, Machu Picchu is comprised of tiered terraces and temples that rise into the clouds.

Cultural ceremonies have endured to this day. Guests at Sanctuary Lodge – which originally housed explorers and researchers before becoming a Belmond hotel in 1999 – take part in time-honored rituals as we collaborate with the best guides and storytellers who continue to Incas’ legacy.
