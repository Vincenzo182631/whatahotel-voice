## Our Food

The Pan-Asian dining experience at Gemma features bold, expressive flavors with signature dishes that include delicate dim sum, an extensive ‘from the wok’ menu, a selection of pristine sushi, and premium grilled steaks – including Japanese Wagyu. Enjoy delectable seafood offerings, including sashimi, langoustine, Maine lobster, and whole Thai branzino.

## Our Story

The best rooftop in Los Angeles returns with a brand new identity. Chef Peleg Miron and the award-winning chefs of Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills created Gemma. It features bold dishes and cuisine that are inspired by Southern California’s sunshine. Gemma balances panoramic openness with a design-forward space that gracefully transitions from day to night. A simple color palette of light blue, green, and light browns goes well with intimate tables, soft chairs, and a cozy bar. Open daily for lunch and dinner, or anything in between.

## Our Bar

Gemma’s drinks are inspired by different cultures. They include homemade cocktails, a special list of sakes, and light wines that go well with Asian food. Indulge in a Scorpion bowl, Mai Tai, or Piña colada from our tiki-inspired creations, or select from a wide collection of bubbles and wines by the glass to accompany your panoramic views of the Hollywood Hills.
