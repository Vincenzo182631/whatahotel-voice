# Salon de Lucy

Salon de Lucy

A space built on trust, care, and beauty.

At Salon de Lucy, we believe beauty should feel effortless, elevated, and personal. Our vision is to create a welcoming space where every client feels both at home and luxuriously cared for whether they are local to Beverly Hills or visiting from around the world. The vibe of our salon blends the elegance of the Waldorf Astoria with the warmth of getting ready among friends, making every appointment feel both special and comfortable.

Located inside the Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills, our salon has been part of the hotel since its opening, with Lucy herself styling here from the very beginning. Now, under her leadership, Salon de Lucy offers a wide range of expert services including precision haircuts, color, styling, makeup, eyebrow shaping, threading, tinting, lamination, and full men’s grooming. From children’s cuts to bridal styling, from celebrity red carpet looks to refined grooming for professionals, our team brings decades of experience across all beauty disciplines.

Services can be scheduled by our concierge staff, or directly by phone or by email.

## Hours and Policies:

Salon is open 7 days a week by appointment.  Registered guests can charge their salon services to their room.  Cancellation is 24hrs prior to service.
