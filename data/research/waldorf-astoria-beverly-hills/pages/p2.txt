Wake up in your California king bed to sunrises through floor-to-ceiling windows and a vast private terrace. A secluded mini-gym offers your own private workout station and entertain guests in your personal home theater.

## Signature Suites

### Villa Suite

Villa Suite

Enjoy the 360-degree view from your spacious terrace. Each suite features a king-sized bed and a private dining room, private dressing room, separate powder room, and walk-in closets.

### Waldorf Penthouse Suite

Waldorf Penthouse Suite

A grand Italian marble entryway welcomes you to relax in more than 1,500 square feet of combined indoor and outdoor space, highlighted by panoramic city views on your private terrace.

### Governor Penthouse Suite

Governor Penthouse Suite
