## Destination Guide

We’re surrounded by Hollywood landmarks, Beverly Hills boutiques, and Southern California charm. Enjoy shops, museums, theme parks, stunning beaches, and famous landmarks like the Hollywood Walk of Fame.

### Rodeo Drive

Rodeo Drive is one of the world’s most luxurious destinations for fashion and entertainment. Discover an unparalleled selection of designer goods, delicious cuisine, and exciting entertainment.

### Hollywood Sign

The Hollywood sign is one of the most famous, recognizable attractions in the world. Come see for yourself why this iconic emblem is a universal symbol of glitz, glamour, and American cinematic culture.

### Hollywood Bowl

The Hollywood Bowl has been synonymous with music and entertainment in California since 1922. This iconic venue has hosted the biggest stars from Billie Holiday to the Beatles. See all your favorite bands and musical artists live in concert.

### Santa Monica Pier

Take in the ocean views from the Santa Monica Pier. One of the most famous boardwalks in the world, the Pier boasts delicious food, a stunning beach, and carnival activities and rides for the whole family.

### Getty Museum

The J. Paul Getty Museum offers a stunning art collection ranging from ancient styles to contemporary. Visitors of all ages will enjoy the rich and informative cultural and educational content.

### L.A. Live

Called ‘the most entertaining place on the planet,’ L.A. Live is a downtown entertainment complex with all your favorite sports, movies, musical artists, and restaurants. See a concert, catch a game, and watch a 3D movie.
