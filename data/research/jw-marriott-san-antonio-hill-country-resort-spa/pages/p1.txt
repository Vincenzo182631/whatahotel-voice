## Home of the Valero Texas Open

Located in the heart of the Texas Hill Country, TPC San Antonio is a private resort only accessible to guests of the JW Marriott San Antonio Hill Country Resort & Spa and Members. As part of the prestigious TPC Network, guests at our luxury resort in San Antonio, Texas Hill Country, will receive the same preferential treatment and exclusive amenities normally reserved for PGA Tour professionals.

Learn More

## Designed by Golf's Most Innovative Architects

TPC San Antonio opened in January 2010 with two 18-hole championship golf courses designed by two of golf’s most innovative architects: Pete Dye and Greg Norman. Both courses have played host to TOUR events with The Oaks course serving as the host of the PGA Tour’s Valero Texas Open since 2010, making it one of the premier golf resorts in San Antonio, Texas.

Frequently Asked Questions

**When did the resort open?** JW Marriott San Antonio Hill Country Resort & Spa opened on January 22, 2010.

**What is the hotel address?** The resort is located at 23808 Resort Parkway, San Antonio, TX 78261

**How far is the resort from San Antonio International Airport (SAT)?** The resort is 14 miles NE of the airport and drive time is estimated at 35 minutes. Please allow extra time based on time of day if needed.

**How do I get to the resort from SAT?** Take US Highway 281 North to the TPC Parkway exit. Exit to the right (east) and follow the TPC Parkway east to the Marriott Parkway exit. Continue on the Marriott Parkway to the resort entrance.

**How many rooms does the resort have?** The resort has 1,002 guest rooms in total. This breaks down to 917 rooms and 85 suites.

**When is check-in and checkout?** Check-in is at 4:00 pm while checkout is at 11:00 am.

**May I check-in early?** While we cannot guarantee early check-in, you may request early check-in upon arrival. Guaranteed check-in starts at 4:00 pm.

**What is the minimum age required for check-in?** 18 is the minimum age required for check-in.

**May I upgrade my room?** Upgrades and/or Suite or View rooms are based on availability at the time of check-in. Please see a Front Desk agent for more information on availability and pricing.

**Does the resort offer connecting rooms?** Yes, while connecting rooms cannot be guaranteed and are based on availability, we do offer connecting rooms with one king guest room connecting to a two-queen guest room.
