### Dive Into Our Water Park Oasis in San Antonio, TX.

Resort guests have exclusive access to the River Bluff Water Experience at our hotel in North San Antonio, with a nine-acre water park and water slides. Float the 1,100-foot lazy river, enjoy multiple activity pools, or relax in the adults-only infinity pool overlooking the Texas Hill Country. With private cabanas, temperature-controlled pools, and whirlpool spas, River Bluff Water Experience offers year-round enjoyment in an outdoor paradise.

### River Bluff Water Experience Guide

Sand Beach Pool \| Enjoy relaxing at our River Bend pool holding 97,000 gallons of water near the sand for everyone to create their own sand castle fun! No inner tubes are allowed into the Sand Beach Pool and life-jackets are highly encouraged for weak or non-swimmers.

Activity Pool \| Venture out in the 261,000-gallon pool while having fun in the sun! No inner tubes are allowed into the Activity Pool and life-jackets are highly encouraged for weak or non-swimmers.

Adult Pool \| Adults can relax and enjoy the views while listening to the waterfall created by the infinite edge along one side of the adult leisure pool or from the adjacent adult whirlpool.

Delight in Together Time \| At JW Marriott San Antonio Hill Country Resort & Spa, we celebrate every moment of sun. We invite you to relax, splash and play at our River Bluff Water Experience. Guests of the resort enjoy exclusive access to the River Bluff Water Experience and are limited to five (5) entry wristbands per room.

Tube Slides \| Guests can ascend the Water Tower adjacent to the children’s pool to access the Acequia Tube Slides. At the 35’ high start platform, guests will not only experience an expansive view of the surrounding landscape and Water Experience, but also the excitement of the twists and turns awaiting them in the two 275-foot-long Acequia Tube Slides.

Body Slides \| The Canyon Oak Twister and Hill Country Plunge body slides soar above the Hill Country. Guests can take the ultimate plunge down the Hill Country Plunge body slide as they reach up to speeds of 20 mph. The Canyon Oak Twister has the dramatic door dropping excitement as you swish from 60 feet in the air downhill. Riders must be at least 48” tall to ride the attraction.

Lazy River \| Grab a tube and enjoy the 1,100-foot lazy river! This is a perfect opportunity for guests to take a leisurely float around the 9-acre waterpark.

Pedernales River \| The Pedernales River is a 650-foot innovative and unique water ride experience. Utilizing lower gradients and higher volumes of water, the slide is very similar to a natural river. The swift current produces a fast moving, powerful ride experience that literally carries rider and tubes from one curve into the next.

How to Spa

**Spa Arrival**

Please arrive 30 minutes prior to your appointment at one of the best day spas in San Antonio to begin your relaxation experience and take advantage of our amenities. Arriving late will limit the time for your treatment, lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest will not be delayed.

Spa guests can park in the hotel self-parking tiers.

**Spa Attire**

We suggest that you wear comfortable clothing and do not bring jewelry or valuables to the spa. A robe and slippers will be provided. Our therapists are professionally trained in proper draping techniques to ensure your privacy and comfort so that you can most fully enjoy your relaxing experience at our day spa in San Antonio.

**Courtesy**

While enjoying our treatments and facilities, we kindly ask that you engage in quiet conversation and refrain from using cell phones and other electronic devices to maintain the serenity within our day spa in San Antonio. The spa and pool are smoke-free environments. To ensure complete privacy, please avoid camera usage in the spa.

**Reservations**

Please reserve appointments at least two weeks prior to arrival so that we may best meet your scheduling requests. Bear in mind that Lantana is one of the best spas in San Antonio, so appointments fill up fast. Contact us at 210-276-2300 to book yours today. A credit card will be required at the time of reservation.

**Cancellation**

Treatments are reserved especially for you at our San Antonio day spa. We kindly request a 24-hour notice of cancelation or reschedule to avoid a full service charge of the treatment price. Group reservations are subject to contracted agreement.

**Gratuities**

Gratuities may be given at your discretion in appreciation of outstanding service.

**Age Requirement**

Lantana Spa is available to guests 18 and older.

**Health Concerns**

If you have allergies or physical disabilities, or are pregnant, please notify our spa concierge before booking treatments. Your well-being is our primary concern.

Seven Knots Ritual

Experience the relaxing rituals of the Curanderos. Upon arrival, Lantana Spa guests are given a piece of red ribbon. Part of the Seven Knots Ritual to release worries and stress, this relaxing spa service helps guests to fully immerse into the healing renewal of our San Antonio hotel's spa. Historically, Curanderos suggested tying seven knots in the ribbon, one for each worry. The seventh knot creates a complete circle. At the end of the spa journey, guests leave the ribbon behind - along with their worries - to start anew.
