## Tequila Bar

Taste the authentic flavors of San Antonio at Cibolo Moon. Featuring one of the first tequila bars in North San Antonio, TX, that is “T” certified by the Tequila Regulatory Commission, our restaurant has a trained bar staff who are experts in educating patrons on the intricacies of tequila.

Tequila Bar Details

Learn more about tequila at our acclaimed Cibolo Moon restaurant. Many people know little about tequila production, such as where the juice comes from and how it is extracted, fermented and bottled. Our bar staff enjoys changing customers' perceptions and helping them appreciate tequila.

It starts with the basics: Tequila is made from the juice of the blue agave; it has to be from the tequila designated origin, or DO, which includes areas in five states in Mexico, although 95 percent of tequilas come from Jalisco; and 51 percent of the liquor must come from blue agave. The best tequilas are 100 percent agave, which is stated on the bottles. Anything less means there are fillers.

After the blue agave is harvested, the leaves are cut off, and the piña is roasted for approximately 24 hours. Then, the juice is pressed out and either bottled immediately for blanco, fermented and aged in barrels for two to 12 months for reposado, or fermented and aged for one year or longer for añejo.

Cibolo Moon features over 100 tequila varieties behind the bar and showcases five different tequila infusions to create many different cocktails. The Strawberry Jalapeño is a must-try for anyone visiting the San Antonio Hill Country.

The Strawberry Jalapeño Margarita begins with our house tequila and fresh-cut strawberries and jalapeño infused for 72 hours. The jars are on display for patrons to see the process. After it has matured through the infusion process, house-made agave syrup and freshly-squeezed lime juice are added, and it is topped-off with strawberry wedges and a jalapeno wheel. It's a refreshing cocktail that starts with a sweet strawberry flavor and finishes with a jalapeño sensation.

The menu also includes many allocated and hard-to-find specialty tequilas like the Patron family: Piedra & Burdeos along with the Tears of Llorona. If you are new to the tequila scene, ask our knowledgeable bar staff about our many tequila flights to sample a few of the offerings.

Local Purveyors

Our culinary team utilizes the rich and flavorful produce, dairy and protein that's hand-raised and produced just miles from our San Antonio hotel restaurant. These delicious and nutritious local ingredients form the basis of our exceptional steaks, celebrated recipes and Sunday brunch buffet. Our staff has formed numerous relationships and exclusive partnerships with ranches and farmers across the Texas Hill Country and the State of Texas.

##### Some of the local vendors include:

**Thunder Heart Bison, Texas Hill Country**

Grass-fed bison contains one of the highest known concentrations of omega 3 fatty acids. High in selenium, beta carotene, taurine, and the all-important CO-Q 10. The grass and field harvesting are the foundations of the nutritional profile that make Thunder Heart unique. Bison from this ranch is used in dishes throughout the resort. The ranch also supplies guajillo jelly to the resort.

**Texas Olive Ranch, Dallas, Texas**

Texas Olive Ranch 100% Pure Texas Extra Virgin Olive Oil is pressed from arbequina variety olives. The Arbequina variety was developed in Spain and is especially suitable for growing in the sandy loam soil of the Middle Rio Grande Valley in Texas, where the climate is very similar to southern Spain. The resort's restaurants and kitchens use this top grade olive oil.

**Mozzarella Company, Dallas, Texas**

A consistent American Cheese Society Award Winner, this handmade, fresh mozzarella is a staple in many of the resort’s dishes. The Mozzarella Company provides the resort with handmade mozzarella cheese that follows a centuries-old recipe.

**Pure Luck Dairy, Dripping Springs, Texas**

Pure Luck is a family farm and goat dairy made up of 100 Nubian and Alpine goats, raised on certified organic land on Barton Creek, in Dripping Springs, Texas. They specialize in handmade artisan goat cheese used throughout the restaurants and kitchens at the resort.

**Veldhuizen Farm, Dublin, Texas**

A small, family-run dairy and cheese maker, the Veldhuizen Farm supplies the resort with its unique Texas Gold Cheddar, a classic buttery cheddar, and Redneck Cheddar made with St. Arnold’s dark beer.

**Lucky Layla, Plano, Texas**

This third generation dairy produces high quality milk and artisan dairy products from award-winning Guernsey and Jersey cows. The all-natural, premium milk and butter from Lucky Layla, is served throughout the resort.

**CKC Farm, Blanco, Texas**

Produced just up the road from the resort on a small family farm, this handmade, fresh goat cheese is used in the resort restaurants.

**Psencik Peaches; Fredricksburg, Texas**

Sweet and juicy, Texas Hill Country peaches are a summer tradition. This small family orchard provides the resort with these ripe, sweet beauties, only available during June, July and August.

**Farm House Delivery C.S.A. - Texas Hill Country**

A collection of small farms that are harvest and delivered weekly to 18 Oaks fruits, vegetables, and lettuce to ensure we have the best products available.

**Good Flow Honey - Cedar Creek, Texas**

Family run business that produces great wild flower honey from south Austin since 1975.

**Kitchen Pride Mushrooms - Gonzales, Texas**

Kitchen Pride Mushroom Farms is the only Texas based family-owned full-service mushroom supplier in the state since 1988.

**Bella Verdi Farms - Dripping Springs, Texas**

Bella Verdi (Italian words meaning “Beautiful Greens”) was conceived in 2003 out of a desire to harness a passion for fine food and sustainable agricultural practices combined with a desire to support the Community. Dedicated to providing the finest quality product, producing high-quality herbs, lettuces and micro greens using sustainable agricultural practices.

**Homestead Gritsmill - Waco, Texas**

Local stoneground yellow grits since 2001. Use the single-step process of grinding with natural granite stones which preserves the nutritional value of the grain.

**Beeman Ranch - Harwood, Texas**

Raises High Quality Texas Wagyu Beef. The Red Akaushi Cattle are crossbred with Black Angus to make for a delicious and tender cut of beef.

**Broken Arrow Ranch - Texas Hill Country**

A collection of game lands throughout central and south Texas. Specializing in Venison, Antelope, and Wild Boar that are free range and truly wild since 1983.

**Prasek’s Hillje Smokehouse - El Campo, Texas**

A family business for over 35 years with a verity of different types of sausage and smoked meats. Prasek’s is featured throughout the resort and showcased at 18 Oaks Sunday Brunch.

**Diamond H Ranch - Bandera, Texas**

Coturnix Quail are raised on an all-natural diet and neither antibiotics nor growth hormones are used. Relatively, the quail are provided with 5 times the space compared to free range chickens.
