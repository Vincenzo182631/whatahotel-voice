FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Where is Tivoli Avenida Liberdade?**

Tivoli Avenida Liberdade Lisboa is situated in the heart of Lisbon, on the iconic Avenida da Liberdade. The hotel is just steps away from Baixa, Chiado, and Príncipe Real, and within walking distance of Eduardo VII Park and the Botanical Garden - offering guests easy access to the city's historic districts, luxury shopping, and cultural landmarks.

**How do I get to Tivoli Avenida Liberdade?**

Our luxury hotel in the centre of Lisbon is easily accessible by car or public transport.

The nearest metro station, Avenida (Blue Line), is just a 2-minute walk away. Rossio and Restauradores stations are also nearby. Lisbon Airport is approximately a 20-minute drive, and private transfer services are available upon request.

**Does Tivoli Avenida Liberdade offer airport transfers?**

Yes, private airport transfers can be arranged upon request and for an additional fee. We recommend booking in advance to ensure availability and a smooth arrival experience.

**Does Tivoli Avenida Liberdade offer parking?**

Yes, the hotel offers private indoor parking with access via Rua Júlio César Machado. Valet service is available for added convenience. Daily charges apply.

**What leisure facilities are there at Tivoli Avenida Liberdade?**

Guests can relax at the Anantara Spa, enjoy the outdoor swimming pool surrounded by lush gardens, or stay active at the fully equipped fitness centre. The hotel also features elegant social spaces perfect for unwinding and connecting.

**What dining options are there at Tivoli Avenida Liberdade?**

Guests can enjoy Portuguese seafood and traditional dishes at Cervejaria Liberdade. For panoramic views and a vibrant atmosphere, SEEN Lisboa by Olivier offers eclectic cuisine and cocktails. SEEN Sky Bar is ideal for al fresco lunches, while the Lobby Bar provides light bites and live music. The Pool Bar offers light meals and refreshing drinks in a serene garden setting.

**Are meeting rooms available or offers for congresses, events and celebrations at the Tivoli Avenida Liberdade?**

Yes, Tivoli Avenida Liberdade Lisboa features 12 versatile meeting rooms, including a grand Conference Centre. Audiovisual equipment, catering services, coffee breaks, and group menus are available. Our dedicated events team ensures every detail is tailored to your needs.

**Are there wheelchair-accessible rooms at Tivoli Avenida Liberdade?**

Yes, the hotel offers wheelchair-accessible rooms with spacious layouts, step-free access, and adapted amenities. Please contact us directly to ensure your specific accessibility needs are met.

**Is Tivoli Avenida Liberdade family friendly?**

Absolutely. Our luxury hotel in the centre of Lisbon welcomes families with suites, connecting rooms, babysitting services, and a tranquil outdoor pool. Its location makes it ideal for exploring the city´s attractions with children.

**Are pets allowed at Tivoli Avenida Liberdade?**

Yes, small pets (dogs and cats up to 25 kg) are welcome. A nightly additional feed per pet applies. 50€/night/pet. Please contact the hotel in advance to ensure a comfortable stay for your pet.
