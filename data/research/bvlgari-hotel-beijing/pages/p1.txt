## Bvlgari Hotel Beijing

Bvlgari Hotel Beijing is an urban oasis of tranquillity in the Embassy District, set along the Liangma River where art and nature meet. Housed within the Genesis Beijing, the hotel stands beside the Genesis Art Gallery designed by Tadao Ando, while its lush riverside gardens are curated by Swiss landscape architect Enzo Enea. ACPV ARCHITECTS Antonio Citterio Patricia Viel was responsible for the entire interior and exterior design. The hotel's 119 rooms and suites include the iconic Bvlgari Suite with sweeping city views. The 1500-square-metre Bvlgari Spa features 11 treatment rooms, a Bvlgari Gymnasivm and a 25-metre mosaic-tiled pool inspired by the ancient Terme di Caracalla. Dining venues include the Michelin-starred Il Ristorante – Niko Romito, The Bvlgari Bar, Lobby Lounge and Bvlgari Dolci.
