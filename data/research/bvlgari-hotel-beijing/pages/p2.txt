## The Bvlgari Bar

Previous

Next

With its sophisticated atmosphere, The Bvlgari Bar at The Bvlgari Hotel Beijing was conceived as a gathering place for Beijing elite. Here guests can enjoy cocktails, fine wine and champagne during Bvlgari’s renowned Aperitivo Hour either at the signature oval bar or on the outdoor terrace, overlooking the riverside.
