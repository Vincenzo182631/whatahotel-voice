## The BVLGARI SPA

The calming 1,500-square-metre The Bvlgari SPA at Bvlgari Hotel Beijing occupies two floors, and features treatments blended with traditional Chinese techniques. The spacious gym and adjacent movement studio feature the latest Technogym and Kinesis equipment, the Bvlgari Gymnasivm comprehensive workout, as well as Pilates. Guests can simply indulge in the SPA’s harmonious atmosphere or enjoy a swim in the 25-metres pool to round off their Spa Experience.

**The beauty journey**

- 11 treatment rooms
- Relaxation Lounges
- Steam and Sauna
- Refreshment Bar
- 25-metre indoor heated pool
- Wellness shopping
- Personal Gym Space
- Technogym Fitness Studio
