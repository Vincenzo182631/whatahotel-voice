This stucco resort on the
Big Island
's Kohala Coast is fronted by a white sand beach. The lobby overlooks the ocean, and in the evenings, you’re greeted by a 'ukulele player or local musician. Rooms feature lanais, rattan furniture, and carpeting with bamboo motifs. Brown’s Beach House focuses on fish dishes such as crab-crusted mahimahi and an excellent kids menu. Alternatively, belly up to the 14-seat sushi bar at Binchotan Bar & Grill, where they serve you dinner in style: Standout dishes include sea urchin eggs, Hawaiian abalone, and octopus. Try out dishes like sea urchin eggs, Hawaiian abalone, and octopus. Save Friday nights for Fairmont’s Hawaii Loa Luau under the stars.
