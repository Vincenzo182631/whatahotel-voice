## Oceanfront _oasis_ on Hawaiʻi Island

Welcome to our luxury oceanfront oasis, where bucket list adventures and rich cultural experiences await. Discover vibrant marine life in our secluded bay, savor fresh island-inspired cuisine and unwind with the most breathtaking sunsets on the Big Island.

_This is where the aloha spirit comes alive._

GUEST SERVICES

MAP & CONTACTS

### DIVE INTO BUCKET LIST ADVENTURES

Snorkel in our private lagoon, watch the sunrise from an outrigger canoe, and indulge in oceanfront dining at our 5-star beach resort. Embrace Hawaiʻi's heritage through hula, lei making, and storytelling. Make a splash in our expansive oceanfront pool. Relax in our open-air Spa Without Walls, or play 18 holes at the award-winning Mauna Lani Golf Club.
