Experiences
Surrounded by 500 acres of secluded luxury, discover a destination designed for families, couples, and group retreats where experiences come alive. Dive into a world-class pool deck where guests can enjoy an array of amenities including luxurious cabanas and daybed rentals offering a heightened level of bespoke personalization and undisturbed privacy. Fuel your curiosity for adventure through Grande Lakes Sports – an exclusive collection of outdoor experiences offered in our backyard. From our hotel near Discovery Cove, explore the Orlando Everglades on a sunrise safari, take to one of eleven lakes for an afternoon of fishing, navigate the Hidden mountain bike trail, or discover the art of falconry. With a full program of Ritz Kids activities and a scheduled shuttle to Disney, Universal Orlando, SeaWorld, and other area theme parks, family-friendly fun is always close by and never in short supply.
Waterpark & Cabanas
Discover the Grande Lakes Waterpark, where you can enjoy a relaxing lazy river, three exhilarating waterslides, and luxurious cabanas. Don't miss out on AquaVenture for even more fun!

Terms & Conditions
Cabanas are a resort amenity for overnight resort guests and Ritz-Carlton Club Members only. Reserve at Grandelakes.ipoolside.com. Reservation confirmation number or Ritz-Carlton Member number required to secure. A 48-hour notice is required to cancel a reservation of a cabana or daybed, please contact reservations at 407-393-2409. All rentals are from 8 am-8 pm. No pets or outside food and beverage are allowed. No smoking, loud music, or glass is permitted within the pool or cabana areas. No refunds due to weather. Multiple bookings need to be paid daily. All cabanas and daybeds will be charged tax and 18% gratuity. A credit card will be required, cancellation fee if the cabana is not canceled. Cancellation fees are charged to the credit card on file. For cancellations within 24 hours or less, a 50% cancellation fee applies; for cancellations within 72 hours, a 25% cancellation fee applies.

Eco-Tours
Travel back in time on a kayak or canoe through Shingle Creek during your two-hour guided Eco-Tour. You will be met by a trained guide who will take you on a specifically designed Outfitter's all-terrain golf cart through the property to the Headwaters of the Florida Everglades where you will have a chance to encounter the various wildlife that Florida has to offer.
Falconry Experiences
With our resorts situated on over 500 acres of the most enchanting natural surroundings found in Florida, the opportunity to participate in a Falconry class is an exciting new addition to our ecological and outdoor activities.
Fishing
Take to one of eleven private lakes and ponds on the Grande Lakes Orlando grounds, including the 48-acre Shingle Lake, for fishing excursions or angling expeditions by boat or from the shore.
Mountain Bike Trail
Get ready for an exhilarating mountain biking experience on our extensive grounds. Our two-mile radius trail is tailored for intermediate and advanced riders, providing an unmatched blend of adventure and fun.
Sunrise Safari
Take a ride on the wild side with an early-morning two-hour tour of the resort's 500-acre campus. Hop aboard an all-terrain golf cart led by a guide to observe and encounter the wildlife of Grande Lakes, including whitetail deer, bobcats, alligators, and owls.
Bocce Ball
Delight in a nostalgic game of bocce ball on two outdoor courts with private cabanas.
