Explore our Orlando resort with waterslides
Whether you’re in search of rip-roaring family fun or in need of a little relaxation and escape, you’ll find exactly what you’re looking for at our resort. With waterparks amid our lush Orlando property, it's one of many attractions that make Grande Lakes a destination unto itself.

Choose Your Adventure
Exciting and inviting all year round, our Orlando hotel with a lazy river offers exhilarating waterslides, restful lagoons, and the Oasis VIP firepit lounge. There’s something for everyone, including Knifeburger, a tempting poolside eatery at Grande Lakes Waterpark.
Epic Waterslides
Make your way to the Headwaters Slide Tower for sensational thrills and epic good times on three high-flying slides. Just how fast do you want to go? Each slide offers an entirely different experience than the next, so everyone can choose the thrill level they’re most comfortable with. All three slides are heated for year-round fun.

The Chute
Dive into our Orlando resort with a lazy river and super-fast slide for the bravest family members. Features a fast drop and tight turns, this thrill ride is for single riders only. Height requirement 48” (121cm).
The Rapids
This tube slide takes you through twists and turns and drops you into a vortex funnel for the ride of your life – medium speed for single and tandem riders. Height requirement 42” (106cm).
The Current
A light sensory slide with moderate speed for single riders. Height requirement 40” (102cm).
Lazy River
Grab your float and drift away at an unhurried pace at our Orlando water park hotel. The Lazy River is 670,000 gallons of winding waters over a quarter-mile. Long a primary attraction, the Lazy River meanders through the heart of the water park, flowing through lush tropical greenery with jets, waterfalls, and bridges from one area to the next–connecting the pleasing pools and coves.

Splash Cove
There's always something for kids to discover at our resort in Orlando, Florida. A zero-entry cove lets little ones ease into the water at their own pace, while the AquaVenture course, PlayVenture playground, and hands-on games and crafts at JW Studio offer endless opportunities to play.

AquaVenture
Water abounds as kids navigate balance beams, bridges and inflatables suspended four feet off the ground.
PlayVenture
A 1,000 square-foot playground featuring a wooden, octagon climbing structure with a slide, rope web and bridge. Lawn games that can include cornhole, connect four, giant jenga and mini putt-putt.
JW Studio
A dedicated space for imagination and play, including pinball machines, Pac-Man, Xbox, and Playstation, plus crafts, an infinity table, giant scrabble, air hockey, foosball, pool tables, and ping pong tables.
Luxury Cabanas
Relax and reconnect at our luxury Orlando resort. With private cabanas available in a variety of sizes and configurations, Grande Lakes Water Park will make your experience truly extraordinary.
