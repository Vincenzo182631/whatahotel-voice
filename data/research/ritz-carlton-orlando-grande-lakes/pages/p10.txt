Discover our Orlando resort with a Club Lounge
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
Our Orlando resort with a Club Lounge redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail for which the Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” the Club Lounge enhances your entire stay with concierge service, unique in-room amenities, and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay at our hotel with club lounge in Orlando, plus exclusive amenities and services, by booking a Club room or suite. Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.

Club Lounge Access
Begin your stay at our Orlando hotel with a concierge greeting in our exclusive Club Lounge, where you’ll enjoy a dedicated check-in, exclusively for our Club guests. For the rest of your stay, consider the Club Lounge your private sanctuary.

Daily Culinary Presentations
Enjoy complimentary food and beverage presentations five times a day at our hotel club lounge in Orlando. These daily offerings — breakfast, lunch, afternoon snacks, hors d’oeuvres, and cordials — tell the destination’s story through locally sourced ingredients, iconic dishes, and signature drinks.

For Your Convenience
In-Room Amenities
A Club stay ensures that additional amenities are available to you in your luxurious room or suite, such as Harry Josh hairdryers and styling tools. Our complimentary shoeshine and pressing of up to two garments per day are delivered to your room within two hours and will ensure you’re ready for your day of meetings or a night on the town. Club guests can also request a Dyson Airwrap and Technogym equipment to work out in their room. If your suitcase is overflowing with souvenirs, make sure to ask for our luggage scale before heading to the airport.
