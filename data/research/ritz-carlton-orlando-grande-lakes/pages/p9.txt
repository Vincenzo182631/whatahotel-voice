Introducing Our Multi-Million Dollar Redesign
Thoughtfully refreshed, and thoroughly refreshing, The Ritz-Carlton’s multi-million-dollar renovations are now complete. From our expansive guest suites to our welcoming common areas, every interior detail has been impeccably updated with the highest quality fixtures, luxury textiles and furnishings.
Modern but welcoming, the stunning updates refresh our gracious accommodations and draw on and open out to the breezy beauty of Grande Lakes’ lush grounds and 11 lakes.
Design Inspiration
From the natural beauty of its boat- and wildlife-filled waters, to the wealth of onsite activities across its lushly landscaped grounds, there is a world of inspiration waiting for guests to discover and enjoy at The Ritz-Carlton Orlando, Grande Lakes. So naturally, designers drew upon those same elements to fashion a fresh and appealing renovation of the hotel’s guest rooms.

New Spaces for Inspired Meetings
Inspired by Florida's natural beauty, these renovations complete a sweeping, resort-wide transformation that began with guest rooms and restaurants. The refreshed spaces bring a fresh, sophisticated ambiance to gatherings, blending design and timeless elegance.
Guest Rooms & Suites
The guest room redesign experience begins just upon entry, where guests are welcomed with a streamlined, marine-inspired courtesy ledge dotted with nautical details and offering ample space for keys and sundries. A step or two inside, guests are granted their first look at curated artwork alluding to the gentle, more refined and unexpected slices of local life that coexist with the renowned amusements of Orlando
Restaurants
Our newly refreshed restaurants draw design inspiration from the food and atmosphere of each location. Redesigns introduced more seating options for more guests to enjoy the revamped dining.
Elevated Accommodations
Unwind in our spacious, 2,400 square feet Presidential Suite, indulge in the open-air privacy of our Club Level rooms with deluxe balconies, or enjoy the pinnacle of luxury in our Royal Suite, where a personal butler tends to your every need. Each remodeled suite reflects the elevated aesthetic of our stunning new interiors, and all include access to the culinary, cocktail and concierge services of our legendary Club Lounge.

Private Poolside Cabanas
Introducing Orlando’s most luxurious way to lounge poolside: The Ritz-Carlton’s stunning front-row cabanas. Elegantly designed as private, fully serviced sanctuaries—and beautifully appointed in comfortable contemporary upholstery—our modern cabanas feature a private butler, wireless internet, HDTV, a refrigerator and a security safe. With only 12 cabanas available, advance reservations are recommended.

Knife & Spoon
Knife & Spoon is a signature American steak and seafood-forward company conceived and led by award-winning Chef John Tesar. The bar will evoke a fun atmosphere with an innovative approach – less is more.
Highball & Harvest
This restaurant pays legacy to its namesake, both the cocktail and the way that trains transported Florida’s harvests. The menu features ingredients from local farms and the resort’s own garden.
Lobby Lounge
The Lobby Lounge design draws inspiration from the Mediterranean through a subtle and tasteful approach. The contemporary design integrates rustic, distressed elements: wood, both stressed and polished, diaphanous curtains, decorative tiles, and white marble tile. Artwork and artifacts are unique and unusual, not mainstream.
Bleu
Inspired by vibrant colors, patterns, and architectural elements of Key West, The Bleu Pool Bar is designed to be a whimsical and tranquil respire from the everyday.
Vitale Spa Café
The spa restaurant features new furniture, paint and décor along with dual umbrellas to provide additional share over the outdoor dining area.
Fairways Pub
The design at Fairways Pub evokes a sense of timeless familiarity. Rich textures, warm and natural woods and opulent leathers create an authentic environment reflective of an exclusive modern day tap house.
First Drop
Savor an authentic artisanal beverage experience at First Drop, the new signature lobby café. Taste locally roasted coffee, fresh juices, handcrafted milkshakes and smoothies, as well as select small bites including breakfast and lunch sandwiches, salads, pastries, candy, and snacks.
