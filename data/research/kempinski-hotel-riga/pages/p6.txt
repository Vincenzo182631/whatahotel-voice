# Concierge Service

### Concierge Service

Grand Hotel Kempinski Riga's friendly and experienced concierge knows the hotel and the city by heart and will gladly give you tips on what to do in Riga. You may also experience them telling one or two of their amusing stories, or unravelling the exciting history of the building and its curious guests. Whether looking for opera or ballet tickets, restaurant table reservations, private jets or last-minute gifts, our concierge will take care of every detail, allowing you to relax and focus on more important matters.

Our concierge stand is located on the first floor next to the reception. You may also get in touch by phone from 11:00 - 19:00 by calling +371 25480609, or by emailing concierge.riga@kempinski.com.
