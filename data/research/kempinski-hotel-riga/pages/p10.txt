# Business Aviation Center FBO RIGA

### Business Aviation Center FBO RIGA

FBO RIGA, a part of Flight Consulting Group, is one of the largest in the EU and the largest in the Baltics. Business Aviation Center offers a full range of services for the ground handling of aircraft, passengers, and flight crews of charter and business aviation flights at the Riga International Airport. The business aviation center includes a state-of-the-art business aviation terminal (1,200 square meters) with an adjacent car park, a heated hangar (8,400 square meters), an apron with parking stands for business jets, and a helipad.

FBO RIGA complies with the highest standards of safety and customer service quality and holds the

first prize of Sapphire Pegasus Award as the best FBO in Europe. It’s also the first FBO in the Baltic States that received the IS-BAH Stage 2 certificate (the business aviation ground handling standard).

The philosophy of FBO RIGA is a personalized approach to every customer and an exceptional quality service meeting the highest international standards.
