# Kids' Activities

### Kids' Activities

We are always happy to welcome our little guests at Grand Hotel Kempinski Riga. They will be treated to a real hotel experience, from a personalised check-in to enchanting activities at the hotel.

For the little ones who wish to see the life of the hotel, we will gladly organise a bespoke hotel tour based on your child's age and interests. Many interesting backstage rooms open their doors for our younger guests.

The tour will finish with a cocoa drink in the luxurious Lobby Lounge, where they will have the opportunity to tell their parents about everything they have experienced.
