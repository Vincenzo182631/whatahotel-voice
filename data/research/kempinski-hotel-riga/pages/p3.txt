# Pet-friendly Stays

### Pet-friendly Stays

Grand Hotel Kempinski Riga goes above and beyond to ensure that every guest's four-legged companions are treated with the utmost care and attention.

Tailoring their experience to the needs of our furry friends, the hotel offers a comprehensive range of amenities and services. From food and water bowls to disposal bags and tempting treats, no detail is overlooked. Our dedicated team takes great joy in accompanying canine guests for leisurely walks through the serene surroundings of Opera Park, providing them with the opportunity to stretch their legs and soak up the fresh air. But the pampering doesn't stop there – our culinary experts have curated a special dog-approved menu, available both in our restaurants and for in-room dining. This means that our furry guests can dine in true luxury, with delectable options tailored just for them. And for a small supplement of EUR 55 per night, the guest's beloved pet can enjoy all these perks and more.

This commitment to creating a warm and welcoming environment extends to every member of our guest's families, ensuring that everyone - including our furry companions - feels right at home.

For more information about pet policy, kindly contact our reservations team at reservations.riga@kempinski.com.
