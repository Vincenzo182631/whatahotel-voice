# The Rooftop Garden Suite

Set atop the most northerly refurbished heritage building, this penthouse suite’s sweeping 180-sq-m rooftop terrace – with its dining and lounge areas, and expansive Bosphorus views – is one of the hotel’s largest alfresco spaces. The suite’s interior, decorated in luxe, understated modern Turkish style, includes a separate bedroom, a living area with a telescope for gazing over the historical peninsula, a refined study, and both a bathroom and powder room.
