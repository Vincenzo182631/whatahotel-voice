# The Art Suite

Housed on the third floor of the hotel’s main heritage building, this suite offers the luxurious comfort of a private apartment and the ambiance of an exclusive art gallery. The separate bedroom, study, dining and living-room areas, and even the bath and powder room are adorned with splendid original paintings, drawings, photographs, and sculptures from a variety of contemporary and classical Turkish artists. The bespoke furnishings, traditional patterned rugs, and rich fabrics all employ a serene, understated colour scheme, to better showcase the vivid artworks.
