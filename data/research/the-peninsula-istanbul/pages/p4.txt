# The Peninsula Suite

Occupying the top floor of the hotel’s southernmost, refurbished heritage waterfront building, The Peninsula Suite offers the most opulent and palatial hotel accommodation in Istanbul. The suite features a luxuriously appointed bedroom with custom-made, inlaid-wood furnishings and Turkish textiles; a spacious living room with a baby grand piano; a lavish private hammam; fitness room and screening rooms; and extravagant Marmara marble bathroom and powder room. The 120-sq-m private rooftop, with unobstructed views over the Bosphorus and Galata Tower encompasses both a private pool area and an exquisitely designed glass-house solarium for dining and lounging.
