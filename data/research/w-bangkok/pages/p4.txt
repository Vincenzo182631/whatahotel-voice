### Rooftop Garden

The rooftop garden is part of W Bangkok’s broader mission to create a circular economy, reduce waste, and engage guests in meaningful sustainability efforts. By composting food waste from the hotel's kitchens and transforming it into nutrient-rich soil, the hotel is able to grow fresh, organic vegetables directly on the rooftop. These farm-fresh ingredients are then incorporated into the hotel's menus, offering guests farm-to-table dining that reduces the environmental footprint of food transportation and provides the freshest, most sustainable ingredients.

Bangkok's Must Trys

Although famous for its old world charm and eclectic hustle and bustle, Bangkok is also quite popular for its cuisine. Whether it be classic Thai favorites, fresh seafood, piping hot noodles or sweets, the Bangkok food scene has travelers covered. Delicious meals at real value can be found everywhere in Bangkok, from street corners to department stores to five star eateries. Here, we’ve compiled some of our favorites. Get out there and explore our wonderful city, but don’t forget to enjoy eating!

Yaowarat

Bangkok's Chinatown, is a vibrant hub famous for its bustling street food scene. As night falls, the streets come alive with neon lights and the aroma of sizzling dishes. Vendors line the sidewalks, offering a mouthwatering array of treats, from savory roasted duck and crispy pork belly to sweet mango sticky rice and refreshing coconut ice cream. Each stall showcases the rich culinary heritage of Thailand.

Bangkok's Intriguing Nightlife

Thailand attracts a certain fascination from around the world for its gregarious nightlife reputation. Bangkok in particular strikes a certain curiosity that’s made its way into Hollywood films, jokes and rivalled destinations such as Las Vegas for making a night out an adventure. While expectations are high, the night is what you make of it in Bangkok.
