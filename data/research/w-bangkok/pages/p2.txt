# Comfort for all in a spacious family room hotel in Bangkok

## Accommodations

Welcome to the lap of luxury. Our modern hotel accommodations in Bangkok city center pulse with energy. Use a touchscreen to illuminate your room or suite and match one of Bangkok's many playful moods. Satisfy your cravings no matter the time of day with our hotel's Whatever/Whenever® philosophy and 24-hour, in-room dining. If you're craving a cocktail or something light to eat, look no further than...See More the W Mix Bar in your suite. Finish up work or prepare for a meeting in your room with a sleek work desk and high-speed Wi-Fi. Our spa-like bathrooms feature large showers, separate tubs, and floating vanities with an ultra-modern style. Book a single room for a business trip or an expansive suite for a family vacation. Enjoy a breath of fresh air in the middle of Bangkok with our hotel's 100% smoke-free policies.See Less

Experience luxury liberated in the vibrant heart of Sathorn at W Bangkok. Immerse yourself in the dynamic energy of the city while enjoying the serenity and elegance of your luxury downtown Bangkok hotel room.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Bangkok have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Bangkok have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Bangkok?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 3 at W Bangkok. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Bangkok?

Yes, all rooms at W Bangkok permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Bangkok have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Bangkok have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at W Bangkok have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Bangkok have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Bangkok have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Bangkok have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Bangkok have in-room jettubs or whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Bangkok have jettubs or whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
