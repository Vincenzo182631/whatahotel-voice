THE HOUSE ON SATHORN

Paii Outdoor

As the heart of the house, Paii outdoor garden welcomes those who want to escape the busy city. Guests can choose to enjoy modern Thai seafood, indulge in the signature afternoon tea or sip their favorite cocktails from The Bar.

Paii at The House on Sathorn

Paii, serving modern Thai seafood in one of Bangkok's most iconic landmarks. Taking its name from the Thai word ‘Paii’, which means to paddle, our city center Bangkok hotel restaurant presents the region's best local and imported seafood while honoring the legacy of the founder of The House on Sathorn, Luang Sathorn Rajayutka, who created Sathorn Canal.

Bar Sathorn

Whether for a casual meeting or the opulent tradition of high tea, Bar Sathorn effortlessly accommodates a day-to-night scene. Once the sun sets, this bar is alive with an enticing mix of seasonal cocktails, cutting-edge sounds, and tempting bites. Doors are open to welcome those in need of a retreat from the bustling city life. Sip your favorite drinks and share stories at our bar in Bangkok's Sathorn district.
