# Welcome to W Bangkok

## Revel in the moment at our hotel in Bangkok, Thailand

W Bangkok seamlessly blends a striking 31-story glass tower with the century-old, European-inspired heritage landmark, The House on Sathorn. Recognized in Condé Nast Traveler's 2026 Readers' Choice Awards as the No. 2 hotel in Bangkok and No. 20 among the World's Best Hotels, the hotel is ideally located in Bangkok's vibrant commercial district, steps from Chong Nonsi BTS Skytrain Station. Featuring...See More 403 stylish guestrooms and suites, W Bangkok offers exceptional comfort alongside AWAY® Spa and FIT gym. Beyond accommodation, guests can immerse themselves in the city's dynamic culture through distinctive dining and social experiences. Enjoy contemporary cuisine at the hotel's signature venues or discover The House on Sathorn, home to Paii and Bar Sathorn, where innovative cocktails celebrate Thai culture, local ingredients, and Bangkok's rich heritage.See Less

### Revel in the Moment at Our Hotel in Bangkok, Thailand

Since opening in 2015, The House on Sathorn has become one of Bangkok’s most distinctive dining and social destinations. Housed in a beautifully preserved neo-classical landmark dating back to 1889, the building has evolved from a private residence to a hotel, the Russian Embassy, and today, a treasured part of W Bangkok. Blending rich heritage with contemporary luxury, it offers exceptional experiences...See More through Paii’s modern Thai seafood cuisine, innovative cocktails at Bar Sathorn, and elegant event spaces and courtyards, creating a unique setting where history and modern hospitality seamlessly come togetherSee Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Bangkok?

The check-in time at W Bangkok is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Bangkok allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Bangkok. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of ฿3531.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Bangkok?

On-site self parking is available at W Bangkok. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near W Bangkok. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Bangkok have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Bangkok has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Bangkok booking.

What is the closest airport near W Bangkok?

The closest airport near W Bangkok is Don Mueang International Airport (DMK). DMK is located approximately 28.2 KM from the hotel.

Does W Bangkok have an airport shuttle to Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK)?

Yes, W Bangkok has an airport shuttle to Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK).

Does W Bangkok have an airport shuttle to Don Mueang International Airport (DMK)?

Yes, W Bangkok has an airport shuttle to Don Mueang International Airport (DMK).

Does W Bangkok have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Bangkok has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
