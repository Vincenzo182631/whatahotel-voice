Love Bangkok for its infectious hustle and bustle? The W Bangkok doesn't skip a beat. The hotel stands as a synecdoche for the vibrant Thai capital itself with its two parts: the historical, century-old The House on Sathorn and the sleek 31-story glass tower. Here, you stay in the heart of the commercial district within walking distance of the Chongnonsri BTS Skytrain station, an ideal locale if you're keeping things pedestrian. With 403 rooms, there's no shortage of places to rest your head in this swanky hotel, whether you're traveling alone on business or bringing your whole family on that Southeast Asia trip you've had on the books for forever.
