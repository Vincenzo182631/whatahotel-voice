# facilities· at il san pietro di positano

**Whether indulging in world-class dining, sipping a glass of vintage perfection,** unwinding at the private beach club, or enjoying a match on the seaside tennis court, every experience at Il San Pietro is an invitation—to belong, to indulge, to escape.

## The beach club

**Welcome to the only private beach in Positano, an exclusive natural cove with crystal-clear Mediterranean waters.**

Take an elevator cut into the cliff straight from the lobby to this hidden gem, where the vibe is laid-back and social. Stretch out on the sun deck, take a dip in the sea, and enjoy ice-cold cocktails, fresh fruit smoothies, and refreshing drinks from the lively beachside bar. Whether you’re soaking up the sun or cooling off with a swim, the Beach Club is all about fun, relaxation, and pure Amalfi Coast vibes.

* * *

Exclusive to resident guests \| Open daily: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM

## The swimming pool

**Dive into the vibrant atmosphere at our swimming pool, set on a terrace overlooking the sea and the charming coastal village of Praiano.**

The perfect spot for both relaxation and connection, whether you’re lounging on a comfortable sun bed with friends or enjoying a refreshing dip with new acquaintances. Sip on cold drinks from the professional bar service, mingle with fellow guests, or simply soak in the lively energy while surrounded by the stunning Amalfi Coast scenery. Exclusive to ISP guests, the pool is a place where moments of relaxation and social enjoyment blend seamlessly.

* * *

Open daily: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

## The tennis court

**One of the most beautiful tennis courts in the world, nestled between the rugged rock face and pristine pebble beach, our regulation-size court, with its dramatic backdrop is the perfect setting for a friendly match by the sea.**

Whether enjoying a casual rally or a competitive match, playing here is a once-in-a-lifetime experience. Complimentary and exclusive to our guests, the court can be reserved in advance through our concierge. Rackets, balls and even a private trainer are all available on request.

## the san pietro· boutique

**Each piece of The San Pietro Boutique embodies the rich history and artistry of “Made in Italy”, featuring bold Amalfi Coast-inspired colors, and signature patterns unique to Il San Pietro.**

Our curated collection is a true expression of elegance, tradition, and passion for Italian design.

Exclusively branded Il San Pietro, this collection is the result of collaborations with renowned Italian artisans and designers, blending local craftsmanship with the hotel’s iconic vision of hospitality.

Designed by Fausta Gaetani Studio, the boutique, located at the entrance of the Michelin-starred Zass restaurant, infuses Mediterranean charm with Saracen influences, cobalt blue and teal hues, and minimalist furnishings that showcase every item’s beauty.

Take home a piece of Il San Pietro—a cherished keepsake that captures the elegance and soul of Positano and reminds you of your unforgettable stay—from our home to yours.

## Shuttle Services

**Our complimentary shuttle service between the San Pietro and Positano runs at all hours.**

A water shuttle to Positano departs from the dock at the Beach Club at specific times between 9:15 am and 10:15 pm. Please ask at the front desk for information on the times and booking.

* * *

_This service is subject to sea conditions_

## Complimentary Boat Cruise

**From May to mid-October, Il San Pietro invites guests to experience a complimentary two-hour coastal cruise, offering a unique perspective of the Amalfi Coast’s dramatic rockfaces and hidden coves.**

Departing daily (except Sundays) at 11:30 AM, this exclusive voyage on The San Pietro boat includes a refreshing swim stop in crystalline waters and a complimentary drink, allowing you to unwind and take in the unparalleled coastal beauty from the best vantage point—the sea.

* * *

_Please reserve your place on board at the Reception. Note that in the case of exceptionally rough seas, the cruise will not depart._

## Private Charter Boats

**Explore the captivating beauty of the Amalfi Coast, Capri, Ischia and the Gulf of Naples aboard Il San Pietro’s private yachts.**

Perfect for more intimate excursions, _The Dreamer_ and _The Joey II_ provide unrivaled comfort, privacy, and five-star service. Both boats feature onboard drinks, snacks, plush towels, and a dedicated captain to enhance your experience. Set sail and discover the coastal splendor of Positano from an exclusive perspective.

* * *

_To charter one of our boats, please speak directly with the concierge._

## the dreamer· luxury, speed & italian craftsmanship

**The Dreamer is the epitome of Italian elegance and high-performance sailing, blending sophisticated design, artisanal craftsmanship, and superior comfort.**

This 51-ft Maxi Dolphin luxury yacht is perfect for both day trips and extended cruises, offering a seamless fusion of sophistication, speed, and relaxation.

With its sleek, modern aesthetic, the yacht features spacious sunbathing areas at the bow and stern, a generous outdoor lounge with comfortable seating, and a dining area for alfresco meals with a sea view.

Premium materials—teak, leather, and stainless steel—enhance the yacht’s minimalist yet refined design. The bright and airy interior includes a double cabin with a private bathroom, where natural light floods through expansive windows, creating a serene and inviting ambiance.

A spacious sunbathing area at the bow and a generous cockpit with lounge seating create the perfect setting for leisurely cruising and social moments, all while surrounded by the dreamy seascapes of the Amalfi Coast.

Designed with a nautical aesthetic, the interior features premium materials and a layout crafted for unparalleled comfort, offering a serene retreat after a day on the water.
