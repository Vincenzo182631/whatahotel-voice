# welcome· to Il San Pietro

**Il San Pietro di Positano is more than a hotel.** **It is a home–where warmth, tradition, and unparalleled comfort meet the unfiltered beauty of the Amalfi Coast.**

Nestled into the cliffside, this exclusive sanctuary embodies the essence of timeless Italian hospitality. Effortless elegance meets contemporary design to create an experience that transcends luxury.

Surrounded by lush Mediterranean gardens and panoramic sea views, this three-Michelin keys hotel is more than a destination. Il San Pietro di Positano is a feeling, a dream carved into the cliffs, a paradise where time pauses.

## our rooms· & suites

**Cradled by the cliffs of Positano, each room at Il San Pietro di Positano is an intimate retreat, designed to feel like a home away from home.**

Every space is a seamless blend of understated luxury and artistic craftsmanship, where hand-painted ceramic tiles, custom-made furniture, and fine Italian textiles evoke the region’s rich heritage. Interiors are bathed in natural light, while terraces frame jaw-dropping views of Positano and the Amalfi Coast’s rugged coastline.

Soak in the beauty of the coastline in complete serenity on your private terrace overlooking the sea.

## restaurants· & bars

**Take a sensory journey at Il San Pietro, where Mediterranean flavors dance with world-class gastronomy.**

Chef Alois Vanlangenaeker reimagines Italian classics, crafting exquisite dishes from the freshest seasonal ingredients—many harvested from San Pietro’s own organic gardens.

Indulge in a candlelit evening at the elegant Michelin-starred Zass Restaurant or bask in the seaside charm of Carlino. Every dish is a love letter to the coast. Sip a signature cocktail on the legendary Grand Terrace, paired with a tasty selection of Bits & Bites. As the night deepens, linger over a vintage from our wine cellar, home to over 600 prestigious labels.

Every moment, every flavor, every glance at the endless sea is a story unfolding in real time.

## facilities· at the San Pietro

**Whether you crave the pulse of adventure or the serenity of a hidden retreat, Il San Pietro di Positano offers a range of amenities designed for indulgence and relaxation.**

Drift along Positano’s dramatic coastline on our complimentary boat cruise, or for those seeking an ultra-private escape, charter one of our luxury yachts and let our captains carry you to secret coves and storied islands.

Sip cold cocktails at our exclusive beach club, a secluded cove carved into the coastline and indulge in the art of “il dolce far niente”. Or lounge by our panoramic swimming pool overlooking the endless horizon. A match on our seaside tennis court—one of the most scenic in the world—is an experience like no other, with the scent of salt air and the sound of waves as your backdrop.
