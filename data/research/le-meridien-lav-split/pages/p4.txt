### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Le Méridien Lav, Split have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Le Méridien Lav, Split offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-10:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, TRX, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Le Méridien Lav, Split have a pool on-site?

Yes, Le Méridien Lav, Split has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Indoor Heated Pool (Indoor)Outdoor Pool (Outdoor)Whirlpool

Does Le Méridien Lav, Split have an on-site spa?

Yes, RE:CUPERA Wellness & Spa is located on-site at Le Méridien Lav, Split. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Le Méridien Lav, Split?
