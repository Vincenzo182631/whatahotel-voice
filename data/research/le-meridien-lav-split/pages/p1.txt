# Welcome to Le Méridien Lav, Split

## Our resort in Split, Croatia, is a base for exploration

Le Méridien Lav, Split invites you to see Dalmatia differently through our unique resort experiences, concierge services, and the beautiful setting on the Dalmatian Coast. Set nearby in Podstrana, this...See More Split beach hotel boasts an 800 m stretch of beach and fantastic sea views toward the city and surrounding islands. Each of the 379 guestrooms and suites features floor-to-ceiling windows opening to large balconies, a 50" smart TV, Wi-Fi and high-speed internet access, separate sofa seating, individually controlled air conditioning, international direct dial telephone lines, a hairdryer, and an in-room safe. Praised by guests for the memorable beachfront setting, spacious rooms, friendly staff, and relaxing atmosphere, our beach resort in Split, Croatia features a world-class spa, along with numerous restaurants and bars. Discover Le Méridien Lav, our 5-star resort in Split, Croatia for relaxed coastal escapes and professional stays.See Less

### Cocktails and Bites

Gooshter Beach Club is a trendy gathering place for world travelers serving innovative cocktails and delectable bites. This popular venue offers a unique mix of culture and cuisine from East and South. Gooshter, stemming from the Croatian word "goosht" meaning something good or a good feeling, or doing something fun, reflects the trendy concept at Gooshter Beach Club. Furthermore, "gušter", in Croatian "lizard", is the symbol ubiquitous in the Mediterranean area as well as at the Gooshter Beach Club.

### Green Key

Le Meridien Lav is a proud holder of the Green Key Certificate, the leading standard for excellence in the field of environmental responsibility and sustainable operation within the tourism industry globally. We provide exceptional hospitality while minimizing our ecological footprint, support local community and foster a culture of sustainability among guests and staff.

### Eco-Friendly Practices

These practices rest on three core pillars: Care for the environment through a series of energy, water, and waste reduction programs, socially responsible business practices supporting local communities, and environmentally conscious partners, and providing excellent guest experiences while minimizing ecological footprint.

### Give Back to Our Planet

Know before you throw: Food and paper waste still have great value even when they end up in a bin. That is why we have implemented 3 eco-friendly devices: Bio-digestor that mixes and aerates food remains, Composter producing superior nutrient-rich substrate that would otherwise be lost, and Compactor that shreds and compacts waste, allowing easier transportation.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Lav, Split?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Lav, Split is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Lav, Split allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Lav, Split.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Lav, Split?

On-site parking is available at Le Méridien Lav, Split. Self parking is available at Le Méridien Lav, Split, with rates of €20.00 per day, and €140.00 per week. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Lav, Split have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Lav, Split has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Lav, Split booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Lav, Split?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Lav, Split is Split Airport (SPU). SPU is located approximately 16.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Lav, Split have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Le Méridien Lav, Split has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
