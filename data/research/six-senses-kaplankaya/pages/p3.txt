## Community, spirituality and celebration

When you travel to Six Senses resort on the northern tip of Ibiza, you not only arrive at a destination, you travel to a different frequency. Escape to a crystalline Cala Xarraca, where the 137 guest accommodations, residences, mansions, pool suites and beachfront caves are a meaningful setting for deep spiritual experiences rooted in the local culture, celebrating music, art, sustainable fashion, wellness and spirituality that has become synonymous with the island.

## Farm Learning Center: A new hub for regenerative living

Discover a space where nature, wellness, and sustainability come together. The Farm Learning Center is a living classroom designed to reconnect you with the land through immersive experiences: seed collection, yoga, seasonal harvesting, and more. Supported by the Regenerative Impact Fund, the center also hosts youth programs and collaborations with NGOs, extending its impact beyond the resort. Arrive via electric vans and step into a world of mindful living, where every experience is rooted in nature.

## Six Senses Ibiza Journal

Since the 1950s, Ibiza has attracted a certain type of free-spirited seeker; artists, creatives, musicians and mystics who have all made their homes on this dazzling white rock. Drawn by the beauty, the light and the natural rhythms of Ibiza’s seasons, many have chosen to settle in the north, where a strong community spirit and ‘live-and-let-live’ attitude hark back to Ibiza’s traditional past.

### Al fresco Mediterranean dining at Partal Restaurant

Partal takes its name and its spirit from the El Partal palace, the 14th-century Moorish jewel of Andalusia. The restaurant pays tribute to the fortified citadels of southern Spain, meeting points for art, ideas, and flavors. That sense of convergence runs through every dish, bringing together the Mediterranean, the Moorish and the Moroccan in a soulful table.

## Vibe with your tribe: Private Residences and Mansions

Spanning from three to ten bedrooms, Six Senses Ibiza's masterfully designed residences and exclusive mansions are perfect for families and groups, answering to the desire to reconnect with loved ones away from everyday routines.

The 19 Residences and 2 Mansions are the perfect setups for a sophisticated villa vacation, offering a stunning location, large en-suite bedrooms, indoor gathering areas, private pools, outdoor spaces for sunrise salutations and sundowner cocktails, and special services such as an in-villa chef, concierge, spa treatments, and more.

## Destination

Nothing in Ibiza is too far away. The resort is beautifully secluded yet only an easy 35-minute drive from Ibiza’s international airport, which in itself is around a 2-hour flight from most major European cities.
