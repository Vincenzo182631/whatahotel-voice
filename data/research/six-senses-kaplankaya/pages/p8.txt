## Discovery, wellness, and community

Welcome to our urban oasis in the historic heart of the Eternal City, offering 96 rooms and suites reflecting the city’s rich heritage with tranquil courtyard or cityscape views. Enjoy a serene and peaceful retreat that is still well connected to celebrated monuments, with the Trevi Fountain, Pantheon, and Colosseum an easy stroll from the door. Whether immersing in vibrant local culture, legendary Italian cuisine, or rejuvenating spa treatments, join us for an unforgettable escape in one of the world’s most enchanting and beautiful destinations.

## Dining

Our two dining destinations offer unique culinary experiences. BIVIUM is a sociable space seamlessly blending restaurant, bar, and café culture to bring you a delightful fusion of Italian cuisine and international flavors. NOTOS Rooftop, our garden-in-the-sky restaurant, provides a sunset dining experience with showstopping panoramic views, featuring Mediterranean dishes, seafood, and plant-based delights.

Both BIVIUM and NOTOS embrace our Eat With Six Senses philosophy, where flavors, sustainable sourcing, and well-being come together to create a truly memorable culinary journey.

- #### BIVIUM Restaurant-Café-Bar

  - A buzzy lifestyle space with an all-day open kitchen and dedicated deli corners.
- #### NOTOS Rooftop

  - Enjoy a secret garden feel with panoramic Rome-at-your-feet 360-degree views

## Accommodation

Six Senses Rome features 96 exquisite guest accommodations, including Double Rooms, Junior Suites, and one- and two-bedroom Signature Suites – some with private terraces. Each room offers a calm and tranquil atmosphere, making them ideal launchpads for exploring the city. Rooted in Italian heritage and Roman tradition, our accommodations provide comfort and style, perfect for enjoying Rome’s culture, cuisine, and wellness offerings.

## Wellness & Spa

Escape the bustling streets of Rome and sink into tranquility at Six Senses Spa. Why not combine a bespoke treatment with a high-energy fitness class or relaxing yoga class? Complete your spa visit with our signature modern-day Roman bathing experience that takes you on a journey through a caldarium, tepidarium, and frigidarium thermotherapy circuit.

Our treatments include high-touch care backed by high-tech science to help you feel restored, revitalized, and rejuvenated as part of your city break. Embrace wellness with personalized facials and signature body massages, biohacking, sound therapy, breathwork, and more, all performed with love by our skilled therapists.
