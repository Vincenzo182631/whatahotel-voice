## FAQs for InterContinental Kuala Lumpur

Does InterContinental Kuala Lumpur have a pool?

InterContinental Kuala Lumpur has 1 outdoor pool.

Swimming Pool

Located on level 5, the swimming pool offers a relaxing sanctuary surrounded by lush green landscape where guests can take a leisurely dip, sunbathe or enjoy a cold beverage served from the pool bar.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Kuala Lumpur?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Kuala Lumpur for hotel guests.

A third party manages the hotel basement car park, Secure Parking Corporation Sdn Bhd. As such, the hotel will not be held liable for any loss or damage incurred.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Kuala Lumpur?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Kuala Lumpur.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Kuala Lumpur for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Kuala Lumpur have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Kuala Lumpur has 5 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Kuala Lumpur?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Kuala Lumpur?

Yes, InterContinental Kuala Lumpur offers a complimentary local area shuttle.

Check our Transportation page for more details.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Kuala Lumpur?

Check-in at InterContinental Kuala Lumpur is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Kuala Lumpur offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Kuala Lumpur offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Kuala Lumpur from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Kuala Lumpur is conveniently located near Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) (KUL), approximately 38.46 miles away (61.9 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Kuala Lumpur?

InterContinental Kuala Lumpur is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Petronas Twin Towers
- Aquaria KLCC
- Royal Selangor Visitor Centre
- KL Bird Park
- Central Market
- Petaling Street / Chinatown
- KL Forest Eco Park
- Batu Caves
- Merdeka Square
- Kuala Lumpur Tower

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Kuala Lumpur puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
