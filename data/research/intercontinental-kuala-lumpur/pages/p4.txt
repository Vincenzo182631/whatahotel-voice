### A Luxurious Stay Awaits

Nestled amidst the vibrant cityscape, our luxurious rooms and suites provide an exceptional home away from home. Featuring 473 spacious rooms, our diverse room categories ensure ultimate comfort and convenience, boasting super king beds, a Club lounge and amenities suitable for business travellers, solo adventurers, couples, and families alike.

### Club InterContinental

Elevate your InterContinental experience with exclusive access to the luxurious Club InterContinental lounge, a refined space with an impressive level of hospitality and enhanced guest benefits. We appreciate if guests kindly adhere to the Club dress code: smart casual with appropriate footwear when inside the lounge.
