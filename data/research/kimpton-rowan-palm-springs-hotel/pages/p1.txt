### Juniper Table

Savor exceptionally fresh fare, a full barista program and an array of tasty drinks in a sunny, casual environment that draws locals and visitors alike. Fast service belies the time and care behind the Mediterranean-inspired cuisine, which uses local, seasonal, organic ingredients. Stop by and satisfy every craving.

Learn more

### 4 Saints

Seven stories above vibrant Palm Springs, 4 Saints serves creative cocktails, California wines, and a thoughtful menu of modern American fare that is guided by the bounty of California's ranches, farms, vines, and coastlines.

Learn more

### Pickleball Courts in Palm Springs

This unique outdoor venue boasts six expansive pickleball courts (over 11,000 square feet) and an unforgettable atmosphere adjacent to our downtown hotel, available exclusively for hotel guests to enjoy.

Learn more

### Rooftop Pool + Cabanas

Unique in the sprawling, low landscape of Palm Springs, our tranquil rooftop pool sits atop our seven-story hotel and offers unparalleled views of the stunning mountain scenery.

Learn more

### Wedding Weekend

In addition to hosting gorgeous wedding ceremonies, the Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs Hotel makes a dreamy desert headquarters for all your wedding events. Featuring both indoor and outdoor spaces, a picturesque setting, and a talented team dedicated to bringing your vision to life, you and your guests just might want to stay longer than the weekend.

## FAQs for Rowan Palm Springs

Does Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs have a pool?

Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Pool information

How much is parking at Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs?

Complimentary parking is available at Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs for hotel guests.

On-site parking is valet and is included in the Resort Fee

Parking for registered guests only

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs.

Pet policy description. We invite you to bring your pet no matter their size, weight, or breed, all at no extra charge. If your pet fits through the door, we will welcome them in.

Pet fee per night: 0 USD

Pet damage deposit: 0 USD

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs?

Check-in at Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs is conveniently located near Palm Springs International Airport (PSP), approximately 2.4 miles away (3.86 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs?

Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival-20.4 miles away (32.83 kilometers)
- Downtown Palm Springs Shopping-0.3 miles away (0.48 kilometers)
- Palm Springs Art Museum-0.2 miles away (0.32 kilometers)
- Palm Springs Convention Center-0.5 miles away (0.8 kilometers)
- Tahquitz Canyon-1 miles away (1.61 kilometers)
- Palm Springs Aerial Tramway-6.6 miles away (10.62 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Rowan Palm Springs puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
