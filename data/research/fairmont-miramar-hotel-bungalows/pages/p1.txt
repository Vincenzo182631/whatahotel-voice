## CHARMING, _seaside_ SANTA MONICA

Classy and timeless, savor seaside bliss at Fairmont Miramar Hotel & Bungalows, Santa Monica's singular 5-Star resort with private bungalows nestled upon five lush acres atop the bluffs of Los Angeles's golden beaches. Born from a grand 19th-century estate, discover wellness, upscale dining, leisure time and lifelong memories with friends and family.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Miramar Hotel & Bungalows?

Check-in is from 4:00 pm and check-out is by 12:00 pm.

Does Fairmont Miramar Hotel & Bungalows have an airport shuttle?

The closest airport is Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), located approximately 9 miles (14 km) from the hotel. Travel time is approximately 30-45 minutes. There is no complimentary shuttle service between the hotel and airport. Hotel Address: 101 Wilshire Blvd, Santa Monica, CA 90401

Is parking available at Fairmont Miramar Hotel & Bungalows?

Valet Parking is provided for registered hotel guests. $70 plus tax, per night including in-and-out privileges.

Valet Parking for Visitors:

- First hour: $20
- Additional hours: $7
- Full day: $70 _plus tax_

All parking rates are subject to 10% city parking tax.

Complimentary electric charging stations available.

What are some of the property amenities at Fairmont Miramar Hotel & Bungalows?

Our luxury hotel in Miramar offers a variety of premium amenities. Some are:

- Pool & Jacuzzi (heated)
- Spa
- 24-Hour Fitness Center
- Business Center
- Grooming: Barbershop & Salon
- Laundry Services (fees apply)
- Coffee Shop

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Miramar Hotel & Bungalows?

The hotel is wheelchair accessible and offers a variety of accessible room types. Please contact the hotel for any requests related to your disability or for additional accessibility information about the hotel's facilities. We will do our very best to ensure your utmost comfort.

Is Fairmont Miramar Hotel & Bungalows pet-friendly?

At Fairmont Miramar, pets are a part of the family!

Pet Fee: $125 USD cleaning fee per pet, per stay will apply.

All animals must be on a leash at all times and must not be left unattended in the room. Service animals and seeing-eye dogs are always welcome and exempt of restrictions and fee.
