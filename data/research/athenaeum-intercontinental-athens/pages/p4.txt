The outdoor pool at InterContinental Athens offers a serene urban escape, surrounded by elegant loungers and a relaxed atmosphere-perfect for enjoying the Athenian sun from May through October.

### Spa Serenity Meets Fitness Vitality

Inspired by Greek hospitality and the harmony of natural beauty, I-Spa offers a restorative retreat for the mind, body and soul. ### Authentic works of art

The crowning jewels of the Athenaeum InterContinental Athens are our authentic works of art by prominent Greek artists. A rare collection of fine art owned by the hotel is exhibited throughout the hotel, in both public and private spaces, as an intentional and integral design aesthetic.

### Sustainability

#### Sustainability Certification

- Our hotel has a third-party sustainability certification.

**Certifications: Green Key (FEE)**

Our hotel is certified with Green Key (FEE), a globally recognised eco-label run by the Foundation for Environmental Education that is awarded to tourism and hospitality facilities that demonstrate outstanding commitment to environmental sustainability. Certified hotels must have implemented a range of measures across multiple categories including energy (e.g. energy-saving lightbulbs), water (e.g. water-saving taps, showers, and toilets) and waste (food waste and single-use plastic reduction schemes).
