# InterContinental Athenaeum AthensDining

From Première’s elegance to Cafezoe’s acclaim and the cosmopolitan Artlounge & Artbistrot, our dining venues offer a refined culinary experience, blending exceptional cuisine, style, and hospitality to create a feast for the senses.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

1. Fine Arts & Fine Drinks

2. Exquisite Culinary Journey

3. The Première Restaurant

### Fine Arts & Fine Drinks

Athenaeum InterContinental joins forces with Line, ranked sixth in the World’s 50 Best Bars 2024, in a three-year collaboration that redefines fine drinking in Athens. Signature cocktails inspired by the hotel’s art collection debut at Artlounge, Artbistrot, and Première with Acropolis views.

### Exquisite Culinary Journey

Embark on a culinary journey at our diverse Athens hotel restaurants, each offering a unique dining experience. From the top-rated Première with Acropolis views to breakfast at Cafezoe, cocktails in the retro-deluxe Tobar, and coffee in the cosmopolitan Artlounge & Artbistrot, delight your senses with exquisite flavours and exceptional service.

Learn more

### The Première Restaurant

Boasting sweeping views of the Acropolis, Première overlooks Athens from its lofty rooftop perch, and boasts exquisite flavours and one-of-a-kind service. Enjoy a distinct dining experience during your stay. During summer months, the restaurant opens its doors out onto its stunning rooftop terrace.
