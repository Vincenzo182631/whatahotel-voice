## FAQs for InterContinental Athenaeum Athens

Does InterContinental Athenaeum Athens have a pool?

InterContinental Athenaeum Athens has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Our serene outdoor pool, available from May to mid-October (weather permitting), boasts a two-tier sun deck for your relaxation. Indulge in refreshing beverages and invigorating snacks at Cafezoe Terrace.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Athenaeum Athens?

At InterContinental Athenaeum Athens, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 24 EUR and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 24 EUR .

Monitored indoor parking of 130 spaces, at a fee.

Parking fee: 24 EUR per day

Parking for registered guests only

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Athenaeum Athens?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Athenaeum Athens.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Athenaeum Athens for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Athenaeum Athens have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Athenaeum Athens has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Athenaeum Athens?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Athenaeum Athens.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Athenaeum Athens?

Check-in at InterContinental Athenaeum Athens is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Athenaeum Athens offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Athenaeum Athens offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Athenaeum Athens from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Athenaeum Athens is conveniently located near Eleftherios Venizelos Airport (ATH), approximately 22.37 miles away (36 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
