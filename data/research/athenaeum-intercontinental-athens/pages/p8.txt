### A Quick City Escape

Experience the heart of Athens at a relaxed pace with this concise itinerary, designed to showcase the city’s highlights without feeling rushed. ## Things to do

### Aegina Island

Aegina, Aegina, 18010

Catch a hydrofoil to Aegina island where you can get a glimpse of island life very close to Athens. Be sure to buy the locally grown pistachio nuts.

Learn more

### Corinth

Gefyra Isthmou, Corinth, 20010

Visit the impressive Corinth Canal and the ancient city of Corinth situated on the hilltop.

### Kifissia

Alsos Kifisias Irodou Attikou, Kifisia, 14561

Go to Kifissia and walk through the shopping area. Admire the classical homes among beautiful gardens and walk to Kefalari, where you can have lunch or dinner in one of the many restaurants.

### Changing of the Guards

2, Leoforos Vasilissis Amalias, Athina, 10557

Watch the changing of the Evzones guards (which occurs every hour, on the hour) in front of the tomb dedicated to the Unknown Soldier.

### Flisvos Marina

Flisvos Marina, Palaio Faliro, 17561

Boasting the blue seas of the Saronic Gulf as its backdrop, Flisvos Marina is Greece’s first exclusive marina to offer large-scale mooring capacity for mega-yacht owners from Greece and abroad.
Apart from the extensive berthing capacities, a commercial complex that stretches across an area of 3.800 sq.m., features a majestic Mediterranean esplanade lined with fine dining and shopping venues as well as a roster of special events during the calendar year.

### Byzantine Kaisariani Monastery

Kaisariani Monastery, Kaisariani, 16121

Visit the Byzantine Kaisariani Monastery. It is situated in an oasis of pine trees on Mount Hymettus. The church was built in the late 11th century and is of historical importance.

### Mikrolimano Marina

Akti Koumoundourou, Piraeus, 18533

Have a coffee or drink in one of the many outdoor cafes at Mikrolimano Marina.

### Stavros Niarchos Foundation Cultural Centre

364 Syngrou Avenue, Kallithea, 17674

The Stavros Niarchos Foundation Cultural Centre is a modern cultural hub in Athens, housing the National Library of Greece and the Greek National Opera. Designed by architect Renzo Piano, the centre features state-of-the-art facilities, including a reading room with 360-degree views of Athens and the sea. The complex is surrounded by the Stavros Niarchos Park, offering green spaces, walking paths, and a variety of cultural events and activities throughout the year.

Learn more

### Jogging path

Jog towards the Panathinaiko Stadium, then cross the avenue to enter the Zappeion and the National Garden.

### Philopapou Hill

Lofos Philopappou, Athens, 11851

Walk to the hill opposite the Acropolis and admire the breathtaking view. This area is not frequented by tourists.

### Acropolis

Acropolis Theorias, Athens, 10558

Visit one of the wonders of the ancient world - which remains a wonder to this day. You can either purchase a ticket to the Acropolis site only or a ticket that gives you access to the following sites: Acropolis, Ancient Agora, the Temple of Olympian Zeus, the Theatre of Dionysos and Keramikos.

### Ancient Agora

Stoa Van Attalus, Athens, 105 55

An oasis in the heart of the ancient city where you can admire a variety of historic buildings. This was the political, financial and religious centre of ancient Athens.
Tip: This site hosted the European Summit in 2003.

### Cape Sounio

Sounion, Attiki, 19500

Visit the temple of Poseidon at the southernmost tip of Attica and admire the breathtaking view.
Tip: Don't miss the world-famous sunset.

### Monastiraki

Plateia Monastirakiou, Athens, 10555

A large fleamarket where you can buy a variety of objects.
Tip: The best day to visit Monastiraki is Sunday, with most of the Athenians.

### Mount Lycabettus

Athens, 11471

Rising approximately 300m above sea level, Mount Lycabettus is the highest point in Athens, offering panoramic views of the city and the Aegean Sea. Accessible by a funicular railway or a hiking trail, the summit features the Chapel of St. George, a 19th-century whitewashed church. It's a popular spot, especially during sunset, providing a serene escape from the city's bustle and a vantage point to appreciate Athens' sprawling landscape.

Learn more

### National Archaeological Museum

National Archaeological Museum, 44, Patission, Athens, 10433

Here you will find the most impressive and diverse collection of ancient artifacts from all over Greece.
Tip: Entrance is free on Sundays from November to March.

### Plaka

Plaka, Athens, 10558

This is the heart of the old city of Athens. Walk through the narrow, pedestrianised streets.
Tip: Do not miss a visit to the Anafiotika area.

### Syntagma Square and the Parliament

2, Vasilissis Amalias, Athens, 10557

Syntagma Square is the centre of the city and is within walking distance of the main shopping areas. The Parliament building is located directly opposite Syntagma Square.
Tip: Buy a koulouri (circular bread with sesame seeds) from the street vendors.

### Acropolis Museum

Dionysiou Areopagitou 15, Athens, 11742

The Acropolis Museum is a world-renowned institution housing artefacts exclusively from the Acropolis of Athens and its slopes. Its modern design offers a unique exhibition experience, including the Gallery of the Acropolis Slopes, Archaic Acropolis Gallery, and the Parthenon Gallery. Explore ancient artefacts, sculptures, and architectural elements that narrate the rich history of the Acropolis. The museum also features an archaeological excavation visible beneath its glass floors.

Learn more

### The Temple of Olympian Zeus

Temple of Olympian Zeus, Vasilissis Olgas street, Athens, 105 57

This is the biggest temple of antiquity and was dedicated to the father of the god Zeus. The temple was constructed in 131A.D.
Tip: The temple is within walking distance from the hotel.

### Panathenaic Stadium

Leoforos Vasileos Konstantinou, Athens, 11635

The Panathenaic Stadium, also known as Kallimarmaro, is a historic marble stadium that hosted the first modern Olympic Games in 1896. Originally built in the 4th century BC for the Panathenaic Games, it was reconstructed in marble by Herodes Atticus in 144 AD. Today, it stands as a symbol of athletic heritage, hosting ceremonial events and serving as the finish line for the annual Athens Marathon. Explore its rich history and enjoy views from its tiers.

Learn more

### Athenian Riviera

Coastal stretch from Piraeus to Sounio, Athens,

The Athenian Riviera is a 60-km coastal stretch extending from the port of Piraeus to Cape Sounio. Known for its beautiful beaches, upscale marinas, and vibrant nightlife, the area has seen significant development, including luxury hotels and residential projects. One of the most ambitious developments is The Ellinikon, featuring the Riviera Tower, Greece's tallest skyscraper. The Riviera offers a blend of relaxation and entertainment, making it a popular destination.

Learn more

### Mount Lykabettus

Athens, 11471

Mount Lycabettus is one of Athens’ most iconic landmarks, rising 277 metres above the city. A short funicular ride or scenic walk to the top rewards visitors with panoramic views stretching to the Acropolis and the Aegean Sea. At the summit, you’ll find the whitewashed Chapel of St. George, a charming café, and a romantic spot to watch the sunset over the Greek capital.

Learn more
