Luxury Hotel Rooms in Cairo That Capture the City’s Beauty
Suites & Rooms
Inspired by the royalty of ancient Egypt, our Cairo hotel rooms and suites offer a serene retreat with contemporary elegance and local charm. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame views of the Nile River or the city, while plush bedding, marble bathrooms, and modern amenities add comfort throughout. Guests appreciate the spacious rooms and comfortable beds. Choose from a variety of rooms, or elevate your stay in our Cairo hotel suites with added space, refined decor, and exclusive services. For family stays, family connecting rooms with Ritz Kids activities add flexibility, while practical comforts support both business and leisure travel. Discover exceptional accommodation in Cairo, Egypt, at The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo.
Luxury Guest Rooms and Suites
Hotel guests may choose to upgrade their guest room or suite accommodations to include the privilege of accessing our exclusive Club Lounge overlooking the Nile and the iconic Cairo Tower.
Cairo Rooms and Suites Offering Additional Luxury
Set apart from the rest of the hotel, the Club Level offers an elevated stay in Egypt’s capital. In addition to the service of a dedicated concierge, guests enjoy exclusive access to the Club Lounge. The relaxing lounge features complimentary culinary offerings throughout the day and floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the Nile and Cairo. Those on the Club Level may also share the experience with guests (based on availability; fee applies).

The Club Lounge Experiences
A Taste of Egypt
Every morning, The Club Lounge guest will get to discover the taste of the oriental and popular Egyptian Shakshuka. This authentic dish contains eggs poached in a sauce of tomatoes minced with chili peppers, onions and pinch of cumin.
Start Fresh
Nothing better than starting your day with a natural detox, our signature fresh Ginger tea, with Lemon and Honey has been marked as the most favorite drink for The Nile Ritz-Carlton’s Club Lounge guests.
Make Your Own
Every day from 5 to 8 p.m., guests of The Club Lounge will savor the flavors of their favorite dish as they watch our chefs cook to perfection the dish they personally chose its ingredients.
