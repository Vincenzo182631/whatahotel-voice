Dining
Discover a refined culinary journey at the restaurants of The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo, where distinctive dining is paired with remarkable views of the Nile and the Egyptian Museum. An experience not to be missed while visiting Cairo, the hotel's collection of restaurants showcases international cuisine, local specialties, and gourmet delicacies, with each venue offering its own atmosphere and unique city, Nile, or museum backdrop. Guided by internationally acclaimed chefs, every dining concept delivers its own distinctive flavours and style. Start the day at Culina, the hotel's all-day dining restaurant, where guests praise the breakfast for its variety, quality, and attentive service, before returning later for exceptional dining experiences. In the evening, unwind at one of Cairo's finest bars, where expert mixologists craft signature cocktails in a sophisticated setting. Guests also value the hotel's exceptional dining experiences, making every meal a memorable part of their stay.

Friday Brunch
Brunch at Culina, where you can enjoy magnificent views of the iconic Egyptian Museum while feasting on a wide range of cooking stations, including Sushi, Asian, Oriental, Grills and Seafood station.
Culina Restaurant
Friday Finds Its Flavor at the Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo
The “Farm to Table Brunch” at Culina is a seasonal open-air dining experience that focuses on fresh local foods featuring fresh local produce, fruits and vegetables, quality meats, fresh seafood, honey, dairy products and live music. Experience the Friday brunch at Culina, where guests can enjoy views of the lush greens of the Egyptian Museum whilst feasting on fare guaranteed to tantalize the taste buds. The must-try experience elevates weekends to a celebration of good food and company. A line-up of cooking stations including sushi, Asian, Oriental, pasta, grills as well as hot and cold mezzes and an array of desserts. For seafood lovers, Culina restaurant also offers sea bass, prawns, lobsters, crabs, calamari and fish selected from a fresh seafood counter and cooked to order.

Quintessential Afternoon Tea
Glamorous throughout the day, the Lobby Lounge at The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo invites guests for a delectable treat.
