Welcome to The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo
Relax in the heart of the Nile at our luxury hotel in Cairo
Set in the heart of downtown Cairo between the iconic Nile River and Tahrir Square, The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo, embodies timeless luxury and a storied legacy. This 5-star hotel in Cairo, Egypt, offers an elegant retreat for leisure and business travelers, with beautifully appointed rooms and suites that blend classic charm with contemporary comfort. With breathtaking river and city views that take in the Nile and the Egyptian Museum, world-class dining, and exceptional service, our luxury hotel in Cairo creates an atmosphere of refined sophistication. Guests praise the prime location near major cultural landmarks at our downtown Cairo hotel. It also stands out as one of the leading family hotels in Cairo, offering a central setting in the vibrant heart of Egypt.

Rooms & Suites

Overlooking the Nile River lies the iconic luxury hotel, offering leisure and business travelers an opulent retreat in the heart of Cairo. The hotel marries the “bucket-list” city with world-class services; offering splendid amenities including more than nine distinctive restaurants and bars, a lavish spa, and unprecedented service promising you an unforgettable getaway. The elegantly appointed 329 rooms including 49 suites are designed to pamper your senses with the finest amenities. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo?
The check-in time at The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs.
What are the parking options at The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo?
On-site parking is available at The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo. Self parking is available at The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo, with rates of $2.50 per hour, $10.00 per day, and $70.00 per week. Valet parking is available for $2.50 per hour.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo booking.
What is the closest airport near The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo?
The closest airport near The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo is Cairo International Airport (CAI). CAI is located approximately 15.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Nile Ritz-Carlton, Cairo offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
