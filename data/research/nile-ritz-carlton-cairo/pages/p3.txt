Signature Suites
Nestled in the heart of the Nile, our premium suites offer the ultimate 5-Star escape featuring world-class service. From the moment you step foot into your luxurious suite you are immediately captivated by the floor-to-ceiling panoramic windows that reveal breathtaking views of the Nile and Downtown Cairo’s iconic landmarks. Each space is elegantly designed and spaciously laid out to cater for your comfort and delight; whether you are here for relaxation or business.
Executive Suite
Explore Intimate Luxury
Treat yourself to a lavish setting complemented with unmatched panoramic views of the world-famous Nile River and Cairo Tower. Our Executive Suites are designed to immerse you in the beauty of Cairo’s skyline and the serene flow of the Nile, from both its spacious bedroom and separate living room, while you indulge in intuitive service. As evenings approach, this premium suite is the perfect spot from where you may witness the sun setting into the glistening Nile from the comfort of your own accommodation.

Blending in comfort with luxury, the signature suite features a lavish living area, separate from the bedroom, exhibiting a laid-back setting where you may unwind or work in style.
