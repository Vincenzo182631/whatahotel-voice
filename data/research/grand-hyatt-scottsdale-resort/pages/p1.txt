## Indulge in Desert Serenity at Our Luxury Scottsdale Resort

Step into a haven designed to excite the senses and calm the soul. Welcome to Grand Hyatt Scottsdale Resort where unparalleled hospitality, cultivated experiences and elevated luxury await. Inspired by Frank Lloyd Wright, open spaces mingle harmoniously with the natural landscape. Revel in the resort’s 2.5-acre outdoor pool oasis, discover renewal at Spa Avania and dine on chef-inspired dishes at multiple resort restaurants.

## ROOMS, SUITES & CASITAS

Experience the essence of the desert at our Scottsdale resort, where every accommodation promises renewal and relaxation. Our rooms blend warm colors and rich textures, while luxury suites offer spacious elegance and elevated amenities. Private casitas boast dramatic floor-to-ceiling windows, expansive living areas and secluded outdoor spaces. Discover the transformative allure of the American West in unparalleled comfort.

## EXPLORE

Scottsdale Summer Family Experience

Make a splash with the whole family at the heart of Grand Hyatt Scottsdale’s 2.5-acre water playground—perfectly centered around sun-soaked memories. From our winding waterslide and sandy beach entry to family-friendly pools and private cabanas, there’s something for every age to enjoy.

Blais Dining

We invite you on a culinary journey brimming with bold flavors and captivating experiences, all masterfully crafted by celebrated chef and James Beard-nominated cookbook author Richard Blais. From daily hand-made pastas and succulent seafood to expertly grilled chops, steaks and the artistry of Japanese sushi and Spanish tapas, each dish embodies the distinct flavors of Arizona and the Southwest. Ignite your senses at one of the resort’s six restaurants.

What's Happening this Season

Experience the magic of autumn and the holidays in Scottsdale. Explore our lineup of chef-led events, s'mores around the fire, and special holiday activities to create lasting memories.

Live Music in the Lounge

Unwind in style at Grand Vista Lounge, where the ambiance is as inviting as the drinks. Enjoy live music in our vibrant lobby bar setting, sip on handcrafted cocktails, and explore local brews with our daily beer tastings. It’s the perfect way to ease into the evening—just steps from the heart of the resort.

Gainey Golf

Test your skills at Gainey Ranch Golf Club, one of the top-rated golf courses in the Southwest, exclusively available to private club members and guests of Grand Hyatt Scottsdale Resort. This golfer's paradise offers 27 holes of championship golf and breathtaking views of the McDowell Mountains. Set amid incredible scenery, this award-winning course offers an exciting year-round challenge for golfers of every level.

Spa Avania

Journey to a sanctuary of serenity and inner peace at Spa Avania. Awaken in the morning, rejuvenate at midday and unwind in the evening—your body’s daily journey toward balance is our foundation. Discover a holistic approach with personalized treatments and embrace the ideal state we call Avania.

### Pets Are Welcome

As a dog-friendly hotel in Scottsdale, we understand that dogs are part of the family and aim to make our four-legged guests feel right at home. Upon arrival, a special welcome letter is given along with complimentary amenities in your room, suite, or casita. Please note dog amenities are subject to change. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

#### Pet Fees

- 1–6 nights: **$150** _/ STAY_

- 7–30 nights (additional deep-cleaning fee, nonrefundable): **$150** _/ STAY_

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  50Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.

### Resort Fee

A resort fee of $55.00 will be applied per room for each night of the stay, granting resort guests access to a variety of services and amenities designed to enhance their stay in Scottsdale. Please contact Grand Hyatt Scottsdale Resort directly for more information.

#### Featured review

Exceeded my Expectations. We had such a great family trip at the Grand Hyatt Scottsdale! The resort is perfect for families — tons of space to explore, amazing pools for the kids, and plenty of shade and lounge spots for parents to relax. My son loved the beach attached to the pool. The staff was so friendly and helpful everywhere we went. We especially loved that there were quiet, adults-only areas too — the spa and adult pool were the perfect escape after a busy day with the kids. Everything felt clean, welcoming, and well thought out. Breathtaking grounds make this a perfect place for any events. It’s rare to find a place that’s equally great for kids and adults, but this one truly nails it. We’ll definitely be back!
