# Dining

Embark on a culinary journey at Grand Hyatt Scottsdale Resort, where dining transcends the ordinary. Sip, savor and socialize in both indoor and outdoor settings that merge Arizona’s natural beauty with vibrant, curated flavors. Discover La Zozzona, a sophisticated fine dining tribute to Italian American cuisine, or indulge in the vibrant fusion of Japanese sushi and Spanish tapas at Tiki Taka. Lounge in a private cabana and enjoy delicious bites and festive cocktails at our pool bar, H2Oasis. Under the direction of celebrated chef and James Beard-nominated cookbook author Richard Blais, each one of our six Gainey Ranch restaurants is a masterfully crafted experience.

## Tiki Taka

Highlight your Scottsdale resort stay by dining at Tiki Taka, where Japanese sushi and Spanish tapas collide in a vibrant, playful setting. This dynamic restaurant by Chef Richard Blais infuses the unique flavors of Arizona and the Southwest into every dish. Sip on inventive cocktails and experience a lively outdoor patio, perfect for a quick bite or a leisurely meal. Tiki Taka promises a spirited fusion of culinary delights in a fine dining environment that is refreshingly unconventional.

## La Zozzona

Experience Weekend Brunch at La Zozzona, offered every Saturday and Sunday, 9AM - 1PM. Reservations highly recommended.

Step inside La Zozzona, where Italian American cuisine comes alive in an elegant and sophisticated fine dining setting. This vibrant Gainey Ranch restaurant, led by celebrated Chef Richard Blais, excites the palate with handmade pastas, charcoal-grilled steaks and a dynamic daily menu. Complement your meal with prosecco and other Italian wines from our chic wine bar. Serving dinner nightly, La Zozzona merges exquisite flavors and exceptional service, taking you on an unforgettable culinary journey that ignites the senses.
