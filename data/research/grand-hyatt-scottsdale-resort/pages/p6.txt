# Hotel Policies

Resort Fee

A resort fee of $55.00 will be applied per room for each night of the stay, granting resort guests access to a variety of services and amenities designed to enhance their stay in Scottsdale. Please contact Grand Hyatt Scottsdale Resort directly for more information.

Exclusive benefits at our resort, include:

- Welcome beverage
- Once per stay $10 credit toward retail purchases at Spa Avania
- Daily family fun activities at the Resort Pool (varies seasonally)
- Bicycle rentals for adults & children ages 5 and older
- Access to Resort Fitness Classes
- Daily Access for 2 adults to The Village Health Club at Gainey Ranch
- Access to Resort Tennis & Pickleball Courts with equipment rental
- Access to Gainey Ranch Golf Club Putting & Driving Range Facilities
- Preferred tee times at Gainey Ranch Golf Club
- Unlimited long distance calls from your room
- 2 In-Room Reusable Water Bottles, refillable throughout Resort Hydration Stations

Pet Policy

**Pets Are Welcome**

As a dog-friendly hotel in Scottsdale, we understand that dogs are part of the family and aim to make our four-legged guests feel right at home. Upon arrival, a special welcome letter is given along with complimentary amenities in your room, suite, or casita. Please note dog amenities are subject to change.

**Fees**

1–6 nights: $150 per stay

7–30 nights (additional deep-cleaning fee, nonrefundable): $150/stay

**Weight Limit:**

Individual weight limit:  **N/A**

Maximum number of dogs:  **2**

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Rollaway Bed Policy

One rollaway bed is permitted in king rooms only for a fee of $25 per day.

Firearm Policy

The Grand Hyatt Scottsdale Resort strictly prohibits the possession of weapons on resort premises, in accordance with Arizona code section A.R.S. 4-229. No exemption to this policy is allowed for private persons, even those licensed and permitted to carry a firearm, and/or where applicable, to conceal-carry a firearm. Guests who fail to abide by this policy may be asked to leave the hotel premises.

Non-Smoking Policy

Grand Hyatt Scottsdale Resort is a 100% smoke-free property. A cleaning fee of $500 per stay will apply to any documented violations.

On-Site Photography & Videography

At Grand Hyatt Scottsdale Resort, encourage our guests to take personal video or photography while enjoying their stay for personal, non-commercial purpose. For photography or videography purposes including (but not limited to) advertising photography, engagements or wedding-related shoots, class, family or individual portraits, model/fashion related shoots and unapproved influencer marketing, written permission from the resort is required to utilize our any of our hotel indoor or outdoor spaces. Kindly note that unauthorized professional photographers/videographers will be asked to leave the hotel property immediately.

For any photo shoot or filming request, please contact our Sales & Marketing Team: 480-444-6065.
