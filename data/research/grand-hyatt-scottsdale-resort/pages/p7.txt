# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

We look forward to welcoming you to Grand Hyatt Scottsdale Resort. Valet parking is available with our third-party service, PMSI. For guests visiting to dine with us, validated valet parking for up to 3 hours is available for $10.

### Self-Parking

Self-parking is provided on a complimentary basis for up to three hours, with validation, for guests using our restaurants or spa. Note that the garage clearance is seven feet. Taller vehicles, including RVs, may park in the complimentary parking area. Please note: No service hookups are available. Please contact hotel security to receive authorization for RV parking.
