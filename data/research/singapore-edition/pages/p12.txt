# Services & Amenities

The Singapore EDITION creates a new, unique experience complemented with an elevated personalized service.

### For further information

Please contact our Guest Experience Team at reservations.sgedition@editionhotels.com.

Share

Guest Services

- 180 sophisticated guest rooms including 24 uniquely designed suites
- One-of-a-kind 200 square meters EDITION Penthouse, surrounded by an outdoor terrace with a jacuzzi
- Signature restaurant, FYSH at EDITION, serves premium seafood and meat grilled on the Josper with regional flavours
- Customized banquet menus for private events, luncheons, dinner parties and business meetings
- Concierge service
- Rooftop bar with swimming pool
- Spa services
- Complimentary use of the 24-hour fitness facilities
- Complimentary high-speed wireless internet access throughout the hotel
- Press Reader available with global newspaper selection
- Multi-lingual staff
- Valet Parking
- Safety deposit boxes, located at front desk

Executive Services

- Executive Boardroom, Studio Meeting Rooms, Ballroom and Ballroom Theater event spaces
- Executive car services available
- State-of-the-art audio/visual equipment
- Complimentary high-speed wireless Internet access throughout the hotel

Pool

- Experience daily access to our rooftop pool, featuring a sprawling 43-meter-long pool nestled under the open sky.
- With a refreshing depth of 1.2 meters, our pool invites you to unwind and rejuvenate from 7 am to 10 pm every day.

Fitness Center

- State-of-the art fitness equipment including cardiovascular machines with personal flat screen TVs
- Personal training sessions available upon request
- Free weights
- Stretching area
- Complimentary towels
- Headphones and refreshments
- Accessible 24 hours a day

Spa

- Wellness spa with sauna, steam room and vitality pool
- Six treatment rooms and a couple treatment room with en suite steam shower and sauna
- Changing rooms with shower facilities
- Relaxation lounge with light refreshments

Accessibility

- Mobility accessible rooms available for certain room types
- Additional accessibility information available here
