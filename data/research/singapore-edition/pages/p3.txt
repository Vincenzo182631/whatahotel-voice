# Punch Room

## Punch Room

RESERVATIONS

EDITION’s signature bar concept, and a global brand in its own right, places punch at the centre of both cocktail culture and everyday ritual. At The Singapore EDITION, Punch Room is housed in an intimate space swathed in Yves Klein blue: oak-panelled walls and ceilings, a sculptural blue ceiling pendant by Parisian artist Eric Schmitt and a stone fan-shaped floor mosaic that evoke the quiet atmosphere of a classic London gentleman’s club.

The selection of rare spirits here have histories of their own; some patiently aged, some sourced across continents, some pursued with singular curiosity. Each cocktail is crafted with care and precision and the story behind it is as much a part of the experience as what is in the glass.

Behind the bronze bar, the team shakes up a punch cocktail menu rooted in Southeast Asian spices, served with imaginative bar snacks, bringing a distinctly cosmopolitan sensibility to one of Singapore’s most singular bar experiences.

For additional information please contact us at +65 6329 5000 or PunchRoom.sgedition@editionhotels.com.
