# Tanica

**Tanica**

Tanica is a lofty space celebrating modern Asian cuisine, anchored by regional herbs and spices, grilled over the Josper or char-fried on
the wok. With dishes designed for convivial sharing around a communal table, Tanica draws inspiration from the essence of Singapore and the country’s only UNESCO Heritage Site, the Singapore Botanic Gardens.

Weekday Set Lunch

Enjoy a three-course set lunch from Monday to Friday, showcasing highlights of Tanica's modern Asian cuisine and bold regional flavours with herbs and spices.

Wine-nesday

Gather your circle of four every Wednesday and enjoy a bottle of Louis Roederer Collection 246 NV Champagne, France, at SGD88++ per bottle.

Sunday Table

Sundays are made for gathering, for conversation and togetherness over a shared table of modern Asian cuisine, flavoured with regional herbs and spices, grilled over the Josper or char-fried in the wok.

For additional information please contact us at +65 6329 5000 or tanica.sgedition@editionhotels.com.

**Lobby Bar**

RESERVE AFTERNOON TEA

As with all EDITION hotels, the lobby is the beating heart of the hotel while the great personalized service is its soul.

The Lobby Bar is an eye-popping space sheathed in sharp pink velvet where a rose-colored pool table of Calacutta marble and oversized pink backlit bar display hold center-stage.

Christian Liaigre doe leather chairs and lamps accent the space, while the bar itself is styled with Lalique-inspired crystal decanters and sculptures.

AFTERNOON TEA

Not your typical three-tier affair. Our Afternoon Tea begins with savoury bites served across small plates, before giving way to a series of exquisite bite-sized desserts, each one crafted with intention. The menu rotates every quarter, shaped by the season's finest ingredients and the kitchen's ever-evolving creativity.

Reservations for Afternoon Tea are recommended. For additional information please contact us at +65 6329 5000 or LobbyBar.sgedition@editionhotels.com.

**Punch Room**

RESERVATIONS

EDITION's signature bar concept, and a global brand in its own right, places punch at the centre of both cocktail culture and everyday ritual. At The Singapore EDITION, Punch Room is housed in an intimate space swathed in Yves Klein blue: oak-panelled walls and ceilings, a sculptural blue ceiling pendant by Parisian artist Eric Schmitt and a stone fan-shaped floor mosaic that evoke the quiet atmosphere of a classic London gentleman's club.

The selection of rare spirits here have histories of their own; some patiently aged, some sourced across continents, some pursued with singular curiosity. Each cocktail is crafted with care and precision and the story behind it is as much a part of the experience as what is in the glass.

Behind the bronze bar, the team shakes up a punch cocktail menu rooted in Southeast Asian spices, served with imaginative bar snacks, bringing a distinctly cosmopolitan sensibility to one of Singapore's most singular bar experiences.

For additional information please contact us at +65 6329 5000 or PunchRoom.sgedition@editionhotels.com.

**WONDER ROOM**

WEBSITE RESERVE

Wonder Room at The Singapore EDITION is the city’s newest nightlife destination, transporting guests to a world of glamor and style at every turn. Located on the hotel’s Lower Ground (LG), the luxurious micro club is where elegance and intrigue collide. Sip curated cocktails and savor elevated bar bites while enveloping in the rich interiors and timeless décor. Cementing The Singapore EDITION as one of Singapore’s latest cultural hubs, Wonder Room will feature an ongoing calendar featuring exciting programming moments and immersive cultural events. Wonder Room is available for hosting events for every occasion, with a multifaceted space that transforms every event to an unforgettable experience.

**The ROOF**

RESERVATIONS

Dressed in terracotta and shades of blue, The Roof, with its semi-al fresco setting, is the ideal venue to chill and catch the tropical breeze, whilst being enveloped on all sides by luxuriant greenery.

All while snacking on Southeast Asian favorites in the comfort of either sunbed or shaded dining area, and as the balmy tropical evenings descend, the mood shifts to classic cocktails infused with tropical notes.

For additional information please contact us at +65 6329 5000 or TheRoof.sgedition@editionhotels.com.
