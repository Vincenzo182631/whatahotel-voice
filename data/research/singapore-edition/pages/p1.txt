# A NEW GENERATION OF LUXURY IN SINGAPORE

Nestled at the pulse of the upper Orchard Road shopping district and ideally located a short walk from the UNESCO-lauded Singapore Botanic Garden, The Singapore EDITION features 204 guestrooms and suites in addition to sophisticated public spaces that include several innovative restaurant and bar concepts, a rooftop pool, spa, gym and versatile meeting spaces.

VIEW ROOMS & SUITES

## HOME AWAY FROM HOME

Guest rooms are meticulously designed for comfort and serenity, blending sophistication with minimalist elegance. White oak-planked floors are adorned with warm white rugs, while hand-sewn damask throws swathe the beds. Thoughtfully designed curtain sheers, black-out shades, and adjustable louvres maintain a cool ambiance in every room for a soothing experience.

VIEW DINING & ENTERTAINMENT

## INTERNATIONAL CULINARY SENSATION

Alongside 204 luxurious accommodations, The Singapore EDITION features a signature restaurant and four bars. Wander from level one past the Lobby Bar to find Tanica for a sumptuous Josper-grilled meal, then let the spiral staircase lead you down to lower ground, where the night lights up with cocktails and music.

Whether you are in search of an ideal setting for a business lunch or a quiet night over unique cocktails in local flavour, our dining options will let you connect over exceptional food and hospitality, with experiences that balance elegance in a relaxed setting.

VIEW DINING & ENTERTAINMENT

VIEW SPA

## WELLNESS OF MIND & BODY

Elevate your wellness journey at The Spa at The Singapore EDITION, situated on the second floor of the hotel. Discover a sanctuary of tranquility and rejuvenation with seven exquisite treatment rooms, where skilled therapists soothe your body. Indulge in his-and-her changing suites, complete with invigorating thermal pools, an ice fountain, sauna, steam rooms, and a serene relaxation lounge, offering the perfect space to unwind. Additionally, an impeccably equipped gym awaits, furnished with top-of-the-line weights and exercise equipment, ensuring a comprehensive fitness experience.

VIEW SPA

VIEW MEETINGS & EVENTS

## Sophisticated Gatherings & Celebrations

Nestled in grandeur, the 81-square meter boardroom at The Singapore EDITION stands adjacent to an awe-inspiring 340-guest events space. This magnificent space boasts a sprawling 600-square meter pillar-less gallery, which can be effortlessly divided into three smaller areas. Bathed in the gentle embrace of diffused natural light, these spaces are adorned with floor-to-ceiling glass, offering a seamless connection to the surrounding beauty.

Immersed in the forefront of technological innovation, the spaces are equipped with state-of-the-art multimedia technology, ensuring every event is flawlessly presented. From a high-octane society wedding to a formal corporate shareholders’ meeting, the ambiance can be tailored to perfection, catering to the diverse needs and aspirations of each gathering.
