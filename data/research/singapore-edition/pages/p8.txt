# Lobby Bar

## Lobby Bar

RESERVE AFTERNOON TEA

As with all EDITION hotels, the lobby is the beating heart of the hotel while the great personalized service is its soul.

The Lobby Bar is an eye-popping space sheathed in sharp pink velvet where a rose-colored pool table of Calacutta marble and oversized pink backlit bar display hold center-stage.

Christian Liaigre doe leather chairs and lamps accent the space, while the bar itself is styled with Lalique-inspired crystal decanters and sculptures.

AFTERNOON TEA

Not your typical three-tier affair. Our Afternoon Tea begins with savoury bites served across small plates, before giving way to a series of exquisite bite-sized desserts, each one crafted with intention. The menu rotates every quarter, shaped by the season’s finest ingredients and the kitchen’s ever-evolving creativity.

Reservations for Afternoon Tea are recommended. For additional information please contact us at +65 6329 5000 or LobbyBar.sgedition@editionhotels.com.
