Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago
Create meaningful memories in our Santiago, Chile, hotel
Nestled between sandy Pacific beaches and the snow-capped Andes Mountains lies The Ritz-Carlton hotel in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile​. Our Santiago Chile luxury hotel​ redefines personal service with a personal passion, making each stay more memorable than the last. In the heart of El Golf, one of Santiago’s most prestigious neighborhoods, this sophisticated hotel delivers unforgettable experiences – like soothing spa services, a rooftop Health and Fitness Center or exquisite dining at Restaurant Estró – all complemented by impeccable service.

Retreat to one of 205 redesigned guest rooms and suites at our hotel in the El Golf District of Santiago, Chile including 49 Club Level accommodations with breathtaking city views.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago, with rates of $3.00 per hour, and $15.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago is Arturo Merino Benitez International Airport (SCL). SCL is located approximately 25.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago have an airport shuttle to Arturo Merino Benitez International Airport (SCL)?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago has an airport shuttle to Arturo Merino Benitez International Airport (SCL).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
