Experiences
Set within Santiago’s affluent El Golf neighborhood, the hotel is ideally located for experiencing Chile's diverse landscape. Beautiful beaches, skiing in the Andes and the Maipo Valley wine region are all easily accessible, while the hotel’s immediate area is filled with luxury shopping and fine dining restaurants.

Ritz-Carlton Rooftop Spa
A Day Spa Overlooking the Santiago Skyline
Tucked away on the upper floors of this hotel in El Golf, Santiago—which features The Ritz-Carlton Spa—discover a rooftop haven of peace dedicated to healing and relaxation. The treatments address guests’ needs from head to toe and include therapeutic massages and body treatments using natural, locally sourced ingredients. Spa guests also enjoy access to the indoor pool, surrounded by a glass dome and opening onto an outdoor terrace with views of Santiago.

Local Attractions
Cultural Finds
From the historic Palacio de La Moneda to the most modern high-rises, and from humble street vendors to the many chic designer boutiques that grace its elegant streets, Santiago is a place to discover one-of-a-kind finds and experiences.
Vineyards and Wineries
Explore the many vineyards and wineries conveniently located nearby or take a guided tour showcasing the natural beauty of the area.
Outdoor Activities
For sports enthusiasts, our luxury Las Condes, Santiago, Chile hotel's proximity to the Andes Mountain Range offers countless recreation opportunities with access to our pool and spa providing some quality relaxation moments.
Ritz-Kids Santiago
Children's Program
Although Santiago may be appealing to adults, it also offers many options for young, curious minds. With its “Ritz Kids” program, this hotel encourages children to explore and participate in daily activities that highlight Chile’s culture and nature.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-9:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Recumbent bike, Multi-station cable, Smith machine, and more, Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Indoor Pool (Indoor)
