Spa
Tucked away within the upper floors of this hotel in Santiago, Chile, The Ritz-Carlton Spa provides a rooftop sanctuary rooted in healing and relaxation. Treatments address concerns from head to toe and include therapeutic massages and body therapies using natural ingredients addressing an array of skincare concerns. Spa guests also enjoy access to the indoor pool, enveloped by a glass cupola and offering access to an outdoor terrace overlooking Santiago.

Spa Policies
What if I am late for my appointment?
Arriving late will simply limit the time for your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest will not be delayed. The full value of your treatment will be applied.
What is the cancellation policy?
If you must reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify us 24 hours before your scheduled appointment to avoid being charged the 50% service fee. No-shows are also charged at 50% service fee.
Health Questions
What if I am pregnant?
While there are a few services that should be avoided during pregnancy, there are many beneficial treatments that can be enjoyed. Please notify our Spa Desk Agents if you are pregnant when you make your appointments so that they can offer guidance.
Guest FAQs
How do I know if a certain treatment will damage my skin?
At The Ritz-Carlton, Santiago, therapists have all the knowledge required to serve you in the best way. Before submitting you to any of our treatments, there is a pre-study of your kind of skin, to identify products that may harm you. A therapist will never agree to give you a treatment if it is harmful for your health.
Are children accepted in the Health & Fitness Center?
Children are very welcomed to join us in the Spa accompanied by parents, but we recognize that some activities may not be appropriate for our younger guests, such as the fitness center facilities, sauna, steam room and whirlpool. Parents are totally responsible of their safety and well-being, especially around the swimming pool.
Is there a minimum age for treatments?
Children under 18 years old are not allowed to have treatments, especially massages, since that is the legal age of consent in Chile.
How can I make a reservation?
Reservations can be made by calling 56-2-24737548 or at extension 2148.
