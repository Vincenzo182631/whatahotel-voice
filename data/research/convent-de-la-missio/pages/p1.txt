## **How to get to the hotel**

Transfer

Request information if you wish to arrive at the hotel via private transfer with pick-up at the airport.

From the airport

The Hotel Convent de la Missió is located 12 km from the airport. At the exit, take the Ma-19 road towards Palma, then follow the Autopista de Llevant/Av. by Gabriel Rock. Arriving in the city center (11km), take Via Roma and La Rambla towards Carrer de la Missió.

In the taxi

At the exit of the airport you will find a taxi rank. If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us and we will help you.
