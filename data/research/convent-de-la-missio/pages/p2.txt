## **Convent de la Missió: history, art and great luxury in Palma**

## Luxury 5-star boutique hotel

Our elegant design hotel is located in the former Convent de la Missió in the historic centre of Palma. Built during the 17th century, the building was originally dedicated to the training of missionaries. Today, it’s a unique place for those seeking comfort and relaxation in the heart of the city and within walking distance to numerous restaurants, shops, markets, art galleries, historical monuments and more.

## Our Rooms & Suites

The Hotel Convent de la Missió has 12 rooms and 15 suites designed to offer guests maximum comfort and high-end amenities. These include a comfortable king size bed, free Wi-Fi and television, as well as air conditioning and heating. You’ll also find a coffee machine and toiletries in each room. The security of your personal items is ensured through the us of a private safe.

## Restaurant Marc Fosh & Art Bar

You can enjoy the exquisite cuisine of Marc Fosh, one of the best chefs on the island with a Michelin star, or the exhibition space offered by The Art Bar, excellent for sharing a drink and discovering emerging contemporary art artists.

## The city of Palma

You can also enjoy the exquisite cuisine of Marc Fosh, a Michelin-starred chef who has established himself as one of the island’s culinary greats, or enjoy a drink at our Art Bar, where we immerse our guests in works from emerging contemporary artists.

Exclusive gallery

## **ArtBar**

The Art Bar houses works of various artistic disciplines and for all tastes: painting, ceramics, sculpture. An exclusive gallery to enjoy the works of some local contemporary artists, while sipping a good wine or one of our signature cocktails.

Terrace Pool

## **Rooftop**

Convent de la Missió has an idyllic terrace to disconnect from the daily buzz and enjoy the island's sunshine, relaxation is guaranteed. Our rooftop has a solarium area equipped with comfortable sun loungers and an outdoor swimming pool for cooling off, heated in winter, as well as a chill out area and a small gym.

Relaxation and well-being

## **Spa & Wellness**

The Hotel Convent de la Missió has sauna services, indoor swimming pool and relaxation room, where you can relax with the best specialists. Enjoy a massage, a facial treatment or a body exfoliation adapted to your skin type.
