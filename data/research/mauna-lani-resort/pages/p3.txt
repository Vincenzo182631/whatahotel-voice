## The most elevated way to experience Mauna Lani

Retreat to Mauna Lani's private club lounge experience, where exclusive privileges and personalized service elevate every stay. Spanning four private oceanview spaces, the Francis Brown Club offers daily breakfast, all-day culinary offerings, signature evening gatherings, priority access to the resort's most sought-after experiences, reserved pool and beach seating and preferential room placement, creating a seamless experience from arrival to departure.

Open daily from 6:30AM - 9:30PM

Signature Pau Hana gatherings, late-night “Final Final” and one-of-a-kind pop-up experiences

Priority reservations at CanoeHouse and HāLani

Priority reservations and access to Auberge Spa, the Mercedes-Benz drive experience, Mauna Lani Golf and the Sports Club & Tennis Garden

## Distinguished Access

### The Spaces

Enjoy at your leisure this sanctuary within the resort, where each space invites a different rhythm to your day. Move between the Lounge, Lanai, Library and Winnie’s Pantry. Savor chef-selected bites, indulge from the extensive bar, explore a playful collection of classic candies, or pour yourself a Final Final as another great day draws to a close.

### The Experiences

With priority access to the resort’s most coveted signature experiences, each stay unfolds through thoughtful moments reserved exclusively for Club guests. Set out with Kainalu Ocean Sports for a guided SUP tour along black sand beaches, explore ancient petroglyphs alongside the Living Culture team or refine your swing or backhand with our teaching pros on the course or court.

### Signature Pau Hana Gathering

As the sun slips into the Pacific, the Lānai comes alive with Pau Hana, a signature gathering marking the transition from day to night. Guests enjoy chef-prepared bites alongside a rotating selection of signature cocktails, wines and spirits, thoughtfully paired with storytelling and moments with Mauna Lani’s ‘ohana, creating an evening that feels both lively and distinctly of Hawaiʻi.
