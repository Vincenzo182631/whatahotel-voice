## 3-Day Bangkok Itinerary, a Curated City Escape

Discover Bangkok through a well-balanced 3-day itinerary that blends cultural icons, world-class shopping, and serene green escapes. From timeless landmarks to vibrant lifestyle destinations, this itinerary is designed for travelers seeking an authentic yet refined Bangkok experience, right in the heart of the city.

Day 1 – Cultural Icons

**The Grand Palace & Temple of the Emerald Buddha**

Thailand’s most revered royal landmark, celebrated for its intricate architecture, centuries of royal history, and the sacred Emerald Buddha. An essential first stop for discovering Bangkok’s cultural and spiritual heritage. Approximately 30 minutes from the hotel.

**Wat Pho & the Reclining Buddha**

Visit one of Bangkok’s most historic temples, home to the famous Reclining Buddha and closely associated with the traditions of Thai massage. A fascinating introduction to Thailand’s spiritual, cultural, and wellness heritage. Approximately 30 minutes from the hotel.

**Sunset Dinner Cruise Along the Chao Phraya River**

Enjoy an evening cruise along Bangkok’s iconic river, taking in illuminated temples, historic landmarks, modern riverside architecture, and lively waterfront districts. A memorable way to experience the city from a different perspective, especially at sunset. Approximately 30 minutes from the hotel. Please contact our concierge for assistance with reservations.

Day 2 – Shopping & City Life

**Bangkok’s Premier Shopping Districts**

Explore some of Bangkok’s most renowned shopping destinations, from expansive contemporary retail complexes to luxury shopping galleries. The city’s central shopping area offers international fashion, Thai designers, dining, entertainment, and cultural experiences all within easy reach.

One of the city’s largest shopping complexes is approximately 2 km from the hotel and just one BTS Skytrain stop away. Continue onward to Bangkok’s celebrated luxury shopping destination, located two BTS Skytrain stops from the hotel, for designer fashion, fine dining, entertainment, and cultural experiences.

**A New Integrated Urban District**

Discover one of Bangkok’s newest mixed-use destinations, bringing together contemporary retail, dining, entertainment, cultural spaces, and landscaped public areas. Located along the Witthayu–Rama IV corridor beside Lumphini Park, the district provides a modern contrast to Bangkok’s historic attractions. Approximately 1.2 km from the hotel, or around 5 minutes by car.

**Ratchada Night Market**

Experience Bangkok after dark with a visit to a lively local night market. Browse casual fashion and lifestyle finds, sample popular Thai street food, and enjoy the energetic atmosphere that makes Bangkok’s evening markets a favorite with visitors and locals alike. Approximately 20 minutes from the hotel.

Day 3 – Urban Escape

**Lumpini Park**

Explore Lumpini Park, Bangkok’s first major public park spanning over 500,000 sqm. A calm green refuge ideal for morning walks and observing local life. It sits about 3 km from The Athenee Hotel, roughly 17 minutes by car depending on traffic.

**Benjakitti Forest Park**

Visit Benjakitti Forest Park, an urban wetland with elevated skywalks, lakes, and sweeping skyline views. Easily reached from nearby BTS/MRT stations, it’s approximately a 10 minute walk from the Asok/Sukhumvit area, making it perfect for relaxation and photography.

**Hanuman Rooftop**

Experience Hanuman Rooftop, a multi level Thai cultural sky bar atop One City Centre, rising over 275 meters with 360° city views, immersive art, and live Thai performances. Conveniently located at Phloen Chit BTS, just one stop from The Athenee Hotel, it’s an iconic setting for sunset drinks and nighttime cityscapes.

## Destination Discoveries

Embark on your cultural journey from one of our complimentary Destination Discoveries & Epicurean Moments crafted by our Concierge to reveal the people, history, and culture behind The Athenee Hotel. Begin your journey with A Voyage to Our Royal Roots, where guests enjoy complimentary Thai Dress while exploring the heritage of Kandhavas Palace, discovering Thai floral arts, energizing Muay Thai sessions, sustainable initiatives supporting Thai communities, and savoring the royal “MIENG DOK BUA” lotus snack, each moment connects you more deeply to Thailand’s culture and spirit.

### A Voyage to Our Royal Roots

An immersive 40‑minute heritage journey, enhanced with complimentary Thai Dress for an authentic cultural experience. Explore the story of Kandhavas Palace, a former royal residence. Discover how the legacy of Princess Valaya Alongkorn continues to influence the refined design elements found throughout The Athenee Hotel. Friday & Sunday \| 3:00 – 4:00 p.m.

### Floral Discovery

Explore Thai floral heritage with hands on traditions. Learn lotus folding, a practice tied to Buddhist rituals since the 13th century era where it symbolized purity. Or craft pandan leaf, a highly aromatic tropical leaf used across Thai cuisine, wellness, and craftwork. The leaves release a vanilla-like aroma when crushed. Thursday & Saturday \| 3:00 – 4:00 p.m.

### Our Sustainability Journey

Discover how we advance sustainability through initiatives that reduce environmental impact, support Thailand’s agriculture sectors, and uplift local farmers and communities. These efforts have earned us ISO 20121, Forbes Travel Guide: VERIFIED™ Responsible Hospitality, Travelife Gold Certification, and numerous other sustainability honors. Tuesday \| 3:00 – 4:00 p.m.

### Epicurean Moment

Savor “MIENG DOK BUA,” a royal lotus herbal snack crafted with petals of Royal Lotus (Nelumbo nucifera), a flower deeply rooted in Thai culture for its sacred symbolism and long standing use in ceremonies and art. Each bite layers herbs and condiments inside fresh lotus petals for an authentic taste of Siamese royal tradition. Daily \| 5:30 – 6:30 p.m.

### Muay Thai

Train with a skilled instructor in our Sky Garden, where open-air serenity meets sweeping views of the Bangkok skyline, an energizing cultural experience rooted in place. Monday & Wednesday \| 6:00 – 7:00 p.m.
