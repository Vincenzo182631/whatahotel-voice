### Feel at Home in Bangkok With Our Butler Service

Our butler service, associated with select luxury accommodations in Bangkok, ensures your unique needs are anticipated and fully addressed with flawless attention to every detail. From the moment he greets each guest with a well-chilled welcome drink, to making sure the departure goes like clockwork, The Athenee Butler is at the guest’s disposal 24 hours a day. The butler service includes unpacking and packing of luggage, shoe shining, suit/dress pressing, beverage service throughout the day, and newspaper delivery. As for life’s other little necessities, he will always aspire to see if there is anything else he can do to enhance your stay and experiences.

Exclusive Benefits of the Athenee Butler Service

Private Check-In

Apart from Private check-in within the Royal Club Lounge or in-room with The Athenee Butler

Beverage Services

Apart from services at The Royal Club Lounge, allow The Athenee Butler to arrange hot beverages delivered to your luxury hotel room in Bangkok city center, be it after your morning wake-up call or before bedtime.

Unpacking & Packing Services

Maximizing your most deserved time to explore the city endlessly or prepare for your important meetings, allow The Athenee Butler to take care of unpacking and storage of your luggage, or fold your garments and pack before a journey home.

Garment Pressing

Dress up wrinkle-free. Unpack in your 5-star hotel room in Bangkok, then simply let The Athenee Butler know which garments require pressing, and they will be pleased to assist. This service includes pressing of two garments per person, per day.

Shoes Shining

Allow The Athenee Butler prepare your shoes ready for your important meetings, city discovery, or fine-dining entertainment.

Travel Arrangement

Whether travel plan requires any adjustment from booking or amending a flight and accommodation, or reserve the best out of Bangkok exploration, allow The Athenee Butler and Luxury Collection Concierge to take care of your every need.

Postal Services\*

Domestic or international postal and parcel services you may require, The Athenee Butler is pleased to provide coordination to ensure that items sent and delivered to the destination.

Personal Shopper\*

Enjoy the shopping paradise Bangkok City is best known for, The Athenee Butler knows every need, style, and venue to bring out the most to your tastes and preferences.
