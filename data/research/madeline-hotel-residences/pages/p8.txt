## Perched at 9,545 Feet

Situated in the spectacular San Juan mountains, Crest Club is a private members-only community located in the five-star Madeline Hotel & Residences. With unparalleled amenities and insider access to all that Telluride has to offer, Crest Club is home to slopeside state-of-the-art facilities designed to create the ultimate base camp for adventure. Bringing together an elite group of vibrant individuals who share a passion for the alpine lifestyle, outdoor adventure, and the one-of-a-kind destination that is Telluride, Crest Club’s mission is to provide the most seamless ski-in/ski out experience, an engaging social community and a new tier of après in Telluride.

Members of Crest Club will enjoy valet parking, private lockers, ski valet and access to a private club lounge, as well as a full service bar: Falcon Room with a vibrant après scene

Membership also offers a dedicated concierge staff, access to Madeline's heated outdoor pool and fitness center, along with preferred reservations at Timber Room and Black Iron Kitchen + Bar
