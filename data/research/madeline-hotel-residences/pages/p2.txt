##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is Madeline Hotel & Residences located?** Madeline Hotel & Residences is located at 568 Mountain Village Boulevard in the heart of Telluride’s Mountain Village, surrounded by the San Juan Mountains. The hotel is connected to the historic town of Telluride by a complimentary gondola.

**How far is Madeline Hotel & Residences from the airport?** Madeline Hotel & Residences is approximately 15 minutes from Telluride Regional Airport (TEX) and about 75 to 90 minutes from Montrose Regional Airport (MTJ). The concierge can assist with arranging private or shared airport transportation.

**When is the best time to visit Madeline Hotel & Residences?** Madeline is a year-round mountain destination, with winter offering world-class skiing and snow-filled adventures and warmer months bringing hiking, biking, festivals and other alpine activities. The hotel routinely closes for periods in spring and fall, though residences remain available during these shoulder seasons.

**What experiences and activities are available at Madeline Hotel & Residences?** Madeline is a year-round basecamp for adventure in the San Juan Mountains, with experiences curated by a team of local Adventure Guides. Depending on the season, guests can ski and snowboard, hike, bike and explore the surrounding mountains alongside distinctive outdoor, cultural and culinary experiences throughout Telluride.

**Is Madeline Hotel & Residences ski-in/ski-out?** Yes. Madeline offers true ski-in/ski-out access at the base of Telluride Ski Resort, with a full-service ski valet just steps from the slopes. The hotel’s Adventure Guides can also assist with lift tickets, equipment rentals and ski or snowboard lessons.

**What is the Crest Club at Madeline Hotel & Residences?** The Crest Club is Madeline’s private slopeside club experience, offering an elevated retreat in the heart of Mountain Village. Members enjoy an exclusive lounge and terrace, personalized ski services, dedicated concierge support and curated culinary offerings, creating a seamless home base for days spent exploring Telluride.

**Does Madeline Hotel & Residences have a pool?** Yes. Madeline’s Alpine Swim Club features a heated outdoor pool, two hot tubs and a sundeck surrounded by views of the San Juan Mountains, offering a place to relax after a day of adventure in any season.
