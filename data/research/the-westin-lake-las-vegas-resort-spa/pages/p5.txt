## Pools & Poolside Dining at The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa

Discover resort-style relaxation at our luxury hotel in Henderson, NV, just minutes from the heart of Las Vegas. Nestled on the shores of Lake Las Vegas, our property seamlessly blends upscale amenities with a serene desert-lakeside atmosphere. Enjoy our hotel pool in Henderson, NV, featuring sparkling waters, stunning views, and exceptional service. Upgrade your day with our hotel pool cabanas in Lake Las Vegas for added comfort and privacy. Whether you're a local seeking special offers, a traveler searching for a luxury escape, or a couple planning a Lake Las Vegas wedding, our resort delivers unforgettable experiences.

**Pool Highlights At a Glance:**

- Heated Yes
- Water Type: Chlorinated freshwater
- Max Depth ~ 6.0 m / 19.7 ft
- Min Depth ~ 3.0 m / 9.8 ft
- Operation Hours: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM (seasonal)
- Private Cabanas Available (upper and lower pools)
- Special evening pool events

## Our Resort Pools

At The Westin Lake Las Vegas, poolside recreation takes center stage.

## Upper Pool

Visit the upper-level pool and take in views of the lake and beach from a comfortable lounge chair, or rent a private cabana for a more pampered experience. After practicing your backstroke, slip into the adjacent whirlpool to soothe tired muscles.

## Lower Pool

The beachside, lower-level pool was designed for thrill seekers of all ages. Spend an afternoon racing down the waterslide or splashing in the crystal-clear waters. Nearby, join a pickup basketball game or improve your putting on our mini-golf area.
