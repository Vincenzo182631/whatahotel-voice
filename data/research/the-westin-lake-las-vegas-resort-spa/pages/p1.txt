# Welcome to The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa

## Create lasting memories at our Lake Las Vegas resort

Escape to The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa, a tranquil Lake Las Vegas hotel offering luxury accommodations and resort-style amenities minutes from the Las Vegas Strip. Set along the scenic shoreline,...See More our resort blends relaxation and recreation in a beautiful lakeside setting. Unwind in spacious guest rooms and suites with soothing décor, modern technology, and lake views. Lounge at the two-level Nuala Pool, work out at the WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio, or enjoy nearby Jack Nicklaus Signature golf courses. Whether planning a getaway, business retreat, or special event, our hotel in Henderson, NV, provides exceptional experiences for every traveler. Dine at Marssa Restaurant, known for Certified Angus Beef and Wagyu steaks, and take advantage of stunning indoor and outdoor event venues. Traveling with a pet? Our pet-friendly hotel in Las Vegas, NV, welcomes four-legged companions for a memorable stay.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 45.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for $20.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa is Harry Reid International Airport (LAS). LAS is located approximately 13.9 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
