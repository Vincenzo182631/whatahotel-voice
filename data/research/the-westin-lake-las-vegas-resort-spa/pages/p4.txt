### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa offers a complimentary, 9:00 AM-7:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio and strength training machines, Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Upper Nuala Pool (Outdoor)Lower Nuala Pool (Seasonal)Waterslide - CLOSED (Outdoor)Beach (Seasonal) (Outdoor)

Does The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa have an on-site spa?

Yes, Ah Spa by Ageless Human is located on-site at The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa. Click here to book a service now.

Does The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa offer golf course access nearby or at the hotel?

Yes, hotel guests have access to Reflection Bay Golf Club. Click here to learn more.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa?

Ethel M Botanical Cactus Garden, Sloan Canyon National Conservation Area, Las Vegas Bay Marina Overlook, Sphere Las Vegas, One World Interactive Aquarium and more are nearby The Westin Lake Las Vegas Resort & Spa.
