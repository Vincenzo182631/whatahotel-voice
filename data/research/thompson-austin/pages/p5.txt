# Downtown Austin Luxury Hotel \| Thompson Austin, by Hyatt

## Tailored Luxury Awaits at Our Downtown Austin Hotel

Located just steps from Austin’s vibrant music and nightlife scene, Thompson Austin is a luxurious hotel perched above 5th and San Jacinto in a stylish 32-story sustainable development. Inside, you’ll find sleek architectural elements, bold design and open spaces that capture downtown Austin's energetic atmosphere. Experience unmatched hospitality and curated adventures that invite guests into the city’s lively playground. Our guestrooms provide a modern sanctuary, while the Upper Stories suites offer elevated living. Enjoy lounging by the rooftop pool, savoring cocktails on the patio and indulging in a delicious dinner at our rooftop restaurant. Discover an unparalleled Texas escape at our hotel.

## Rooms & Suites

From modern luxury to understated touches, our 229 hotel rooms, 14 suites and 17 Upper Stories suites invite you to relax in style at our downtown Austin hotel. All accommodations feature floor-to-ceiling windows with dramatic cityscape views of Austin, hardwood floors, leather furnishings, cast concrete and stone mosaics. Elevate your stay in a suite or Upper Stories suite designed with artful architectural elements and inviting touches.

## Meetings & Weddings

### Meetings & Events

Austin wouldn’t be Austin without a little drama, but the capital city’s never seen stagecraft quite like this. Located on our 2nd floor, accessible by elevator or dramatic lobby staircase, our downtown Austin event spaces offer 6,300 square feet of modern ballroom elegance and carefully-designed meeting venues. A little secret behind the five names of our distinguished special events spaces: Kelley, Red River, Singer, Newton, and Bass — all were famous outlaws around Texas in the early 1800s until the 1900s. The city of Austin is often considered the outlaw of Texas, and these spaces capture the city’s rebellious nature.

### Pets are Welcome

Bring your furry friends on a downtown Austin adventure. Our pet-friendly hotel in the heart of the city welcomes dogs at no additional charge. Please complete and sign the pet agreement upon check-in. If you need recommendations on local parks, walking trails, restaurants and shops that allow pets, one of our pet-loving staff will assist. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
