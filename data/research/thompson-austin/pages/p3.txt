# Restaurants & Bars

Immerse yourself in the heart of downtown at Thompson Austin where you can sip, savor, meet and mingle. Whether you’re a culinary enthusiast or seeking the latest hotspot to kick off your evening, our hotel offers a variety of dining and drinking options to soak in the urban vibe. Start your day with coffee from Texas Coffee Roasters at The Coffee Bar. Indulge in delicious Mexican cuisine and downtown Austin views at our rooftop restaurant and bar, Arriba Abajo. Have a top-tier martini at The Royale Room. In-room dining is also available, allowing you to enjoy a meal from the comfort of your guestroom.

## Arriba Abajo Rooftop Cantina

Nestled on the 4th floor of our iconic establishment, Arriba Abajo is a rooftop dining oasis where flavors come alive against panoramic downtown Austin views and a relaxed ambiance. From sizzling fajitas to handcrafted tacos bursting with flavor, each dish embodies a story of tradition, innovation and exceptional quality. Our bartenders craft cocktails with the skill of a rattlesnake’s shake. Immerse yourself in the joy and laid-back vibe of the Mexican lifestyle at our little slice of paradise. Whether you seek warmth or coolness, unwind poolside in one of the rooftop Mexican restaurant and bar’s daybeds or cabanas with a margarita in hand.

## Fifth Street Diner

Fifth Street Diner is a nostalgic nod to the classic American diner, blending retro charm with a contemporary twist. Indulge in all-day dining staples such as buttery omelets, crispy bacon, crackling chicken tenders, and the crowd-pleasing, melt-in-your-mouth Diner Smash Burger with golden fries. The Diner also offers regional nods such as the Smoked Brisket Hash and Huevos Rancheros with a house hot sauce. Sip on strong Coffee, creamy Milkshakes, or a spicy Bloody Mary, as you are serviced by a generous and warm team, turning your all-day dining dreams into a delicious reality from this home away from home- “Good food, good mood!”

## The Royale Room

The Royale Room sits in the heart of Downtown Austin, steps away from the energy of Sixth Street & surrounded by the city's best music, nightlife, and cultural destinations. A seventies inspired cocktail lounge with ice cold martini and contemporary cocktails, reimagined with the Austin spirit. The Royale Room pours a range of subzero house martinis and cocktails including our OG dirty, celebratory shot combos & our monthly rotating dirty martini series. Our bar snacks are inspired by the things people know and love. Pair your cocktail with the perfect prawn cocktail, fresh spinach dip, pigs in a blanket, or a steakhouse burger with golden truffle fries.

## The Coffee Bar

Start your day at The Coffee Bar. Enjoy curated coffee drinks by day, local craft brews and natural wines in the afternoon, and light snacks any time. Community tables create an inviting space to mingle or gather with co-workers, while cozy nooks offer a spot to kick back and relax. This lobby-area space is awash with natural light and offers guests and locals a neighborhood vibe that is uniquely Austin.
