### Oversized Rooms & Suites

Designed with space and comfort in mind, all rooms and suites feature expansive layouts, king or double-king beds, and private terraces or balcony, offering an airy, elevated all-inclusive experience. Deluxe guest rooms from 700 sq ft / 63 sq mt and above.

### Spa-Inspired Bathrooms

Every adults only all inclusive rooms in Punta Cana includes a rainfall shower and deep soaking bathtub, creating a spa-like retreat that blends modern luxury with effortless relaxation.

### Modern Design & Elevated Comfort

Sleek, functional, and intentionally minimalist, each room is thoughtfully designed for modern living, featuring a premier minibar stocked daily and nightly turndown service for a seamless, elevated stay.
