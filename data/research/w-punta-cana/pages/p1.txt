# Welcome to W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive

## A BOLD AND UNSCRIPTED ISLAND RESORT

Set against a backdrop of lush coastline, W Punta Cana redefines modern luxury in the Dominican Republic by blending expansive spaces with personalized verve. At our all-inclusive hotel in Punta Cana for adults, each of our 340 oversized guestrooms and suites features striking ocean or resort views, private balconies, and select swim-up or plunge pool options, all inspired by Punta Cana’s beauty. Fuel your day with sunrise yoga and later, unwind at the stunning 80-meter Wet Deck infinity pool that stretches along the shore. Indulge in bold flavors and crafted cocktails across 12 exceptional restaurants and bars, then turn up the energy with vibrant programming that pulses with Dominican spirit. For the ultimate upscale experience, immerse yourself in our world-class AWAY Spa, designed to encourage the creation of your own escape for deep relaxation. From the moment you enter this tropical destination, infinite days are amplified by the perfect adult playground.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of being a Marriott Bonvoy Member at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive?

Marriott Bonvoy Members staying at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive are eligible for additional benefits only available at resorts in the All-Inclusive by Marriott Bonvoy Portfolio. You can refer to official benefits here.

What is included in the per night room rate at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive?

The nightly room rate at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive typically includes: room, meals, snacks, non-alcoholic & alcoholic beverages, entertainment, non-motorized water sports, taxes, service fees, and gratuities.

What additional amenities and services are available for purchase at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive?

W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive may offer spa treatments, motorized water sports, laundry, transportation, tours, premium items, and services for an additional fee.

Are there any guest age restrictions at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive?

W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive is an Adults-Only resort. The guest count should be updated during the booking process to ensure appropriate pricing.

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive?

The check-in time at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 30.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive?

On-site self parking is available at W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive booking.

What is the closest airport near W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive?

The closest airport near W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive is Punta Cana, Dominican Republic (PUJ). PUJ is located approximately 41.8 KM from the hotel.

Does W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
