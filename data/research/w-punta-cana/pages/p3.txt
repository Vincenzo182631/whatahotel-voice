### All-Inclusive Eat & Drink at W Punta Cana. Distinc Culinary and Mixology

Turn every meal into a moment. At W Punta Cana, dining goes beyond all-inclusive with Multiple World-Class Restaurants and Bars designed to match your rhythm—day to night. From chef-driven concepts and live-energy kitchens to elevated casual bites, rooftop sunsets, and late-night cravings, every venue delivers flavor with attitude. Expect global cuisine infused with Caribbean soul, premium cocktails...See More poured without limits, and spaces made to linger, connect, and indulge. Here, you don’t follow a dining schedule—you follow your mood.See Less

At This Hotel

## TRADE MARKET

Multiple cuisines

Diverse cuisine with global inspiration and local ingredients creates one of Punta Cana’s signature grand food halls at this adults-only, all-inclusive destination. This food hall is an open door to unforgettable flavors.

Open for breakfast, lunch and dinner

## SCENA

Eclectic

Chef-driven concept that re-imagines traditional Dominican ingredients in an open-kitchen, experiential setting. A Punta Cana resort restaurant where island elegance meets culinary innovation, with uniquely plated dishes and expertly crafted drinks.

Open for Dinner

## NOODLE BAR

Asian-Fusion

Inspired by Asian street cuisine, this is a Pan-Asian restaurant in Punta Cana resort, reinvented—fearless dishes, steaming broths, wok-fired flavors, and creative perfection. Pair with elevated oriental spirits and craft cocktails.

Open for Dinner

## TAMAN BEACH CLUB

Mediterranean

Adjacent to the ocean, it blends beachside calm with vibrant energy—a social hub inspired by the Mediterranean. By night, it transforms into an oceanfront grill featuring fire-kissed flavors, sea rhythms, and shareable cocktails.

Open for lunch and dinner

## SATSUMA ROOFTOP

Other

A blend of breathtaking views and a flavor-forward experience. An inventive selection of Nikeii cuisine, raw bar, and artfully plated tiraditos, crafted with the finest local products. Unexpected cocktails and an unmistakable scene.

Open for Dinner

## OUTPOST CAFÉ

Coffee House

Organic coffee and cacao from local artisans and international brands. Handmade bakery offering a curated menu with fresh, natural ingredients. Pair your handcrafted espresso with crave-worthy pastries, decadent sweets, or a fresh, guilt-free bite.

Open for breakfast, lunch and dinner

## THE PLAZA BAR

An open concept gathering spot where Latin American mixology meets a high-energy ambiance, serving modern cocktails with a tropical twist. Sunset aperitifs or late-night revelry — whatever, whenever.

Open for Dinner

## WET® Deck

Sun-drenched days, picture-perfect moments, and cocktails that hit just right — WET Deck is the place to be. A beachfront setting with beautiful views of the ocean, featuring a refreshing menu, paired with a selection of drinks.

Open for Lunch

## CHILL POOL BAR

An idyllic swim-up bar at our Chill Pool. Surrounded by lush landscaping, this Punta Cana resort pool bar features effervescent spritzes, healthful appetizers, invigorating bites and herbaceous tonics crafted to refresh and revitalize.

Open for Lunch

## LIVING ROOM BAR

The mixology experience at the luxury resort in Punta Cana, based on infusions and spirits claims the spotlight. Lounge, mingle, or explore your way into fine spirits, and a distinct soundtrack sets the mood of this bar where the energy meets the moment.

Open for lunch and dinner

## 33 1/3 SPEAKEASY

Picture this speakeasy bar like a secret space where the sound of vinyl takes the protagonist. Unscripted mixology allies with vintage arcade games. Step beyond the ordinary in an intimate setting hidden behind a casual shop.

## TAPROOM

Our distinct take on a traditional beer hall. Discover locally crafted and international brews, from crisp lagers to craft beers, all made to capture the island’s authentic spirit. Pair with delightful bites and let the draft flow.

Open for lunch and dinner

## SENSAZIONI

A chameleon-like space that adapts to the hotel’s mood, with a touch of theatricality for special occasions or private events at this Punta Cana resort restaurant. Opens for special happenings.
