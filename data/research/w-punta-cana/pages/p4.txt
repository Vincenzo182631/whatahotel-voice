# Holiday Events at W Punta Cana

## Punta Cana Made the List. A Season Worth Talking About.

The holidays hit differently in Punta Cana. Think sun-filled days, beachfront gatherings, local traditions with a fresh point of view, destination dining, wellness rituals and nights led by vibrant entertainment. At W Punta Cana, the holiday season is not defined by a single event, but by an ever-evolving experience built around connection, discovery, and celebration. ### Holidays, All Included. You’re on the List.

Start Christmas week with yoga at sunrise, sound healing at sunset and THE RESET rituals designed to move, restore and reconnect. In between, follow the rhythm of Dominican aguinaldos, make your own charamico, pull up a chair at a shared table or stay out a little longer. ### Holiday Season Programming

Halloween: Expect the Unexpected

### Aqua Affair: Mystery Edition

Halloween starts before sunset at WET Deck. DJ sets, pop-ups and cocktails with a local twist set the mood around the pool as the afternoon starts to shift. Come for the sun, stay for the mystery, and see where Halloween takes you next.

### Last Bite

Dinner takes a darker turn at Scena. A three-course menu unfolds alongside theatrical performances and unexpected encounters, bringing a little mystery to the table. Settle in for dinner but keep your eyes open. Not everything happening tonight is on the menu.

### Halloween, Rewound

Press rewind at 33 1/3 Speakeasy. VHS-era Halloween, neon lights, vintage arcade games and candy-fueled nostalgia set the scene, with Halloween punch and DJs taking it late into the night. The ’80s and ’90s are back for one night. No curfew required.

Thanksgiving: Gratitude, Remixed

### Gather Different

Thanksgiving becomes more than a meal. Begin with a sunrise gratitude ritual by the ocean, discover wellness experiences throughout the day, and gather on the beach for festive dining, live entertainment, and an evening under the stars. One day. Countless reasons to linger a little longer.

### First Light

Welcome Thanksgiving at sunrise with a shared moment of gratitude by the sea. Native botanicals, artful expression, and guided meditation come together on Uvero Beach, creating a meaningful start to a day designed around connection, presence, and appreciation.

### Rise & Refuel

Wellness takes center stage at TAMAN. Discover nourishing flavors, hydration stations, recovery rituals, and AWAY Spa experiences designed to help you recharge, reset, and celebrate at your own pace.

Christmas: Tropical Edition

### Nutcracker Unscripted

A holiday classic gets a fresh perspective. On November 28th, ballet, aerial artistry, contemporary circus, and modern interpretations of Tchaikovsky come together in an immersive performance that culminates with the lighting of W Punta Cana's festive tree.

### The Aguinaldos

Follow the sounds of the Dominican holiday season as musicians move throughout the resort performing contemporary interpretations of traditional aguinaldos. Local rhythms, festive treats, and unexpected encounters bring a distinctly Caribbean spirit to December evenings.

### Charamico Studio

Inspired by one of the Dominican Republic's most beloved Christmas traditions, this creative atelier invites guests to discover the story behind the charamico while designing their own contemporary keepsake using natural materials and local inspiration.

### The Awakening

The first chapter unfolds over dinner as music, immersive performances, shifting light, and unexpected characters begin to transform the atmosphere. The boundaries between reality and imagination start to blur.

### Reality Reimagined

As the energy builds, dining gives way to spectacle. Performance art, fashion, music, and larger-than-life moments take over the night, creating a celebration unlike anything else in Punta Cana.

### Crossover

The countdown begins. DJs, performers, immersive entertainment, and fireworks converge for the final moments of the year. One world ends. Another begins. And after midnight, the celebration continues at Speakeasy

See More

### The Posadas

A contemporary Latin celebration where food, culture, music, and discovery share the spotlight. Explore seasonal flavors, local makers, interactive experiences, and festive traditions designed to bring guests together in true holiday spirit.

### December Social Club

A table set for conversation. Meet fellow travelers over a chef-curated shared dining experience where stories, laughter, and unexpected connections unfold throughout the evening. Arrive as strangers. Leave with new memories.

### Silent Night

An immersive wellness ritual designed for reflection and renewal. Guided breathwork, aromatherapy, and sound experiences create a quiet moment to pause, reconnect, and set intentions before the year comes to a close.

### Brush & Bite

Holiday baking becomes a creative experience. Decorate seasonal treats with edible colors and festive flavors, creating your own edible masterpiece before deciding whether to admire it or enjoy it.

### The Reset a Different Way To Spend Christmas

Six days. Two experiences a day. One invitation to reconnect.
From sunrise yoga and meditation to sound healing, wellness rituals, culinary discoveries, and intentional moments of reflection, THE RESET transforms Christmas week into a journey designed to restore, awaken, and inspire.

### The Giving Table

As the sun sets, Thanksgiving gathers on the beach. Raise a glass, add your message to the Gratitude Wall, and settle in for seasonal flavors, live music, and meaningful moments beneath Caribbean skies before the evening continues with Cinema Club under the stars.
