## FAQs for InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu

Does InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu have a pool?

InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

Guests should book a reservation via calling the hotel or send an email to spabyclarins.marseille@deepnature.frChildren over five years old can access the pool from 10:00am to 6:30pm.Children over five years old can access the pool from 10:00am to 6:30pm.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu?

At InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 49 EUR .

Parking is available for guests at basement level, on booking request and upon avaibility charged at 39 EUR per night, except during peak season from May 1st to September 1st when the it is 49 EUR per night.

Parking for registered guests only

Parking & transportation details

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu?

Check-in at InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu is conveniently located near Marseille Provence Airport (MRS), approximately 11.18 miles away (18 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu?

InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Notre Dame de La Garde - 1.55 miles away (2.5 kilometers)
- Vieux Port - 0.16 miles away (0.26 kilometers)
- La Vieille Charité - 0.25 miles away (0.4 kilometers)
- Les Calanques
- FRAC - 0.35 miles away (0.56 kilometers)
- Cathédrale de la Major - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- MuCEM - 0.55 miles away (0.89 kilometers)
- Palais Longchamp - 2.05 miles away (3.3 kilometers)
- La Corniche - 3.79 miles away (6.1 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Marseille - Hotel Dieu puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
