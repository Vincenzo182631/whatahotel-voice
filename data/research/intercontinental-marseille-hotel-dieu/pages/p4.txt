**Front desk:** +33-4-13424242

**Email:** marseille@ihg.com

1 Place Daviel Marseille, 13002 France

**Check-in**: 4:00 PM

**Check-out**: 11:00 AM

### Parking

- Parking is available for guests at basement level, on booking request and upon avaibility charged at 39 EUR per night, except during peak season from May 1st to September 1st when the it is 49 EUR per night.

- Car parking available

- Daily valet parking fee: 49 EUR daily

- Parking available for all hotel customers, guests, visitors
