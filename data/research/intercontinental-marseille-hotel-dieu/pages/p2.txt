# InterContinental Marseille - Hotel DieuDining

InterContinental Marseille offers two restaurants: Les Fenêtres restaurant and the Capian bar. Both feature breathtaking views of the Vieux Port and a selection of dishes and cocktails incorporating the freshness of Provence.

**In-room dining hours:** 6:30 AM - 2:00 AM
