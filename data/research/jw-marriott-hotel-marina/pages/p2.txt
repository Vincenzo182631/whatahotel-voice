## Premier Club Suite

Unwind in the understated luxury of the Premier Club Suite, offering 70 sqm (753 sqft) of thoughtfully designed space. Featuring a plush king bed, a sofa bed, and a comfortable sitting area, this suite blends comfort and style with effortless ease.  Ideal for both business and leisure stays, the suite provides a serene retreat in the vibrant heart of Dubai Marina.

## Marina Executive Room

Immerse yourself in sweeping views of Dubai Marina from the comfort of the Marina Executive Room. This sophisticated space includes a private balcony, elegant king-sized bed and contemporary amenities. Designed for both relaxation and productivity, the room features sleek décor and a tranquil environment perfect for extended stays or discerning business travelers.

## Our Executive Lounge

Located on the Ground Floor, the Executive Lounge offers an exclusive retreat featuring both indoor and outdoor seating areas. Designed for seamless productivity and relaxation, guests can enjoy complimentary daily offerings including breakfast, afternoon tea, and evening cocktails, alongside dedicated business services and personalized concierge assistance.

Lounge access is available to guests staying in Executive and Club Rooms & Suites, as well as Marriott Bonvoy® Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Marina have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Marina have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Hotel Marina?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Hotel Marina is the Presidential Suite with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Marina?

Yes, all rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Marina permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Marina have fridges?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Marina have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Marina have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Marina have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Marina have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Marina have hair dryers.
