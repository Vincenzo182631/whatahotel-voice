JW Marriott Hotel Marina offers a delightful culinary journey with exceptional venues tailored to every taste and occasion. ### Elevated Dining by the Marina

Celebrate life’s special moments with a private dining experience overlooking the shimmering Dubai Marina. From bespoke menus to beautifully curated settings, JW Marriott Hotel Marina provides the perfect backdrop for timeless occasions; be it a romantic dinner, a family celebration, or an intimate business gathering.

## Joyful Gatherings For Every Celebration

At JW Marriott Marina, we truly celebrate the spirit of the season. Whether you’re joining a grand gala to toast the New Year with a lavish feast or sharing an exquisite spread to break fast during Ramadan with a heartfelt spirit, the Marina Grand Ballroom provides the perfect setting for every festive occasion. Keep an eye out as we get ready to make this season one to remember or if you want to plan your own special celebration in the Marina Grand Ballroom, just reach out as we’d love to help make your vision a reality.
