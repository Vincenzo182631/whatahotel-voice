# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Marina

## Explore our waterfront hotel in Dubai Marina

JW Marriott Hotel Marina is a luxurious waterfront retreat where contemporary elegance meets the serene beauty of Dubai. This 5-star hotel in Dubai Marina features 200 thoughtfully designed rooms and suites, offering stunning views of the marina and cityscape. Amenities include a rejuvenating spa, a state-of-the-art fitness center, a kids’ club, and an infinity pool. A variety of dining experiences...See More are available on-site, ranging from Afternoon Tea at Griffin Lounge, to casual bites at Harbours Bar and Lounge, and international dishes at JW Kitchen, all designed to satisfy diverse tastes. For business travelers, the hotel offers 22 versatile meeting and event spaces, including the Marina Grand Hall—ideal for large-scale occasions and conferences. Conveniently located in the heart of Dubai Marina, the hotel provides direct access to Dubai Marina Mall and is just steps away from the vibrant nightlife of JBR beach, and iconic landmarks such as Ain Dubai, Blue Waters Island, and Dubai Marina Walk.See Less

## Discover Dubai Marina

Dubai Marina, one of the city’s most vibrant waterfront neighborhoods, offers something for every traveler. Enjoy leisurely strolls along the promenade or adrenaline-filled adventures like a luxurious yacht cruise or soaring across the city on the XLine zipline. For a slower pace, relax by the palm-lined walkways with stunning skyline views. Explore Dubai Marina Mall for boutique shopping and dining, then as night falls, experience the lively scene of restaurants, rooftop lounges, and nightlife. From thrills and shopping to relaxation and world-class dining, Dubai Marina delivers unforgettable moments at every turn.

### Dubai Marina Mall Shopping

Enjoy world-class shopping just moments from your doorstep at Dubai Marina Mall. Home to over 140 international and local brands, it offers a curated mix of fashion, beauty, and lifestyle stores, along with waterfront dining and entertainment. Whether you're browsing for the latest trends or the perfect gift, this vibrant destination delivers a seamless retail experience in the heart of the Marina.

### JW Garden

The JW Garden is a lush indoor sanctuary offering fresh herbs like mint and parsley for the hotel’s culinary creations. Designed as a tranquil retreat, it invites guests to relax and reconnect with nature. Blending sustainability, wellness, and culinary artistry, the JW Garden provides an enriching experience at the heart of the hotel.

## A More Mindful Stay by the Marina

Discover thoughtful touches throughout your stay at JW Marriott Hotel Marina. Selected guest amenities feature bamboo, cotton and plant-based materials, including dental and shaving kits, slippers, shower caps and straws. Refillable glass bottles are also used in place of single-use plastic water bottles for in-house use, supporting efforts to reduce single-use plastics.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Marina?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Marina is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Marina allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Marina.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Marina?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Marina. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Marina have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Marina has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Marina booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Marina?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Marina is Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC). DWC is located approximately 35.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Marina have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Marina has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Marina have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Marina has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
