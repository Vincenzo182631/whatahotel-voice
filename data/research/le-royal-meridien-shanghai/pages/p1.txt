# Welcome to Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang

## Ignite your inspiration at our hotel in Minhang, Shanghai

Situated near Qibao Ancient Town, Le Meridien Shanghai Minhang offers stylish comfort in a legendary travel destination. Find your respite in contemporary accommodations with signature comforts, modern...See More essentials and floor-to-ceiling windows with sweeping views of the Qibao Area. Delight your palate with bountiful buffets and delectable international fare at our distinctive hotel restaurants. Stay within moments of an eclectic array of attractions within minutes of the National Exhibition and Convention Center Shanghai, Hongqiao Train Station, Shanghai Zoo and Hongqiao International Airport. Bring your big ideas to life in dynamic meeting and event spaces, enhanced by expert planners and custom catering professionals. Punctuate an unforgettable day of business or sightseeing with a refreshing swim in our sparkling pool or an exhilarating workout in our fully equipped fitness center. Perfectly placed in the Minhang District, our hotel is a stylish retreat in Shanghai, China.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang?

On-site self parking is available at Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang. Self parking is available at Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang, with rates of ¥8.00 per hour, ¥64.00 per day, ¥448.00 per week, and ¥0.00 perstay.

Off-site parking is also available near Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang is Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA). SHA is located approximately 6.2 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang have an airport shuttle to Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA)?

No, Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang does not have an airport shuttle to Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport.

Does Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang have a Le Meridien Club lounge?

Yes, Le Méridien Shanghai, Minhang has Le Meridien Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Le Meridien Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
