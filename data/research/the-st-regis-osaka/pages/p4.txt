## Dining

The St. Regis Osaka offers three exceptional restaurants, each distinguished by its unique interior design and refined atmosphere. At La Veduta, guests can indulge in beautifully crafted Italian cuisine inspired by the chef’s creative vision and signature “Stagione” seasonal dining experience. Experience the casual sophistication of Brasserie RÉGINE, a modern French brasserie ideal for gathering and elegant dining, or discover innovative teppanyaki at Teppanyaki WAJO, curated under the culinary direction of Michelin two-starred Chef Ryuta Iizuka. At the timeless The St. Regis Bar, guests can immerse themselves in the sophisticated spirit of New York through signature cocktails, fine champagne, and elegant afternoon tea experiences, all inspired by the enduring traditions of St. Regis.

## Timeless Tradition

Across the world, each St. Regis Hotels creates its own signature Bloody Mary inspired by the destination and local culture. In Osaka, this tradition comes to life through the “Shogun Mary”, a distinctive cocktail crafted with uniquely Japanese flavors and seasonings, including yuzu, wasabi, soy sauce, and premium vodka.

Learn More

##### **The Bloody Mary**

The St. Regis Signature Cocktail

First created in 1934 at the original St. Regis Hotel in New York, the Bloody Mary is an indelible part of the St. Regis experience.

Today each St. Regis hotel presents its own interpretation of the original recipe. Our signature Bloody Mary is the Shogun Mary

##### **Bloody Mary Vs. Shogun Mary**

The original “Bloody Mary” was born at King Cole Bar at The St. Regis New York in 1934 by Fernand Petiot, Bartender. The St. Regis Osaka has created our original “Shogun Mary” with soy sauce, yuzu citrus and wasabi powder, delicately put on the edge of a glass. Enjoy Japanese interpretation of this signature cocktail. Click here to read more about St. Regis Bloody Mary rituals.

## Afternoon Tea

Afternoon Tea at the St. Regis is never the same. Caroline Astor, mother of St. Regis founder John Jacob Astor IV, transformed the tradition of afternoon tea into one of society’s most sophisticated rituals. Today, each St. Regis Hotels & Resorts property reinterprets the experience through local flavors, culture, and traditions, ensuring that no two Afternoon Tea rituals are alike. At The St. Regis Osaka, our Afternoon Tea ritual evolves through the seasons, inspired by distinctive themes and occasional exclusive collaborations, offering new discoveries with every visit. Each experience is thoughtfully crafted around seasonal inspirations and refined concepts, featuring handcrafted sweets, savories, and freshly baked scones prepared by our pastry chefs in Osaka, accompanied by expertly brewed teas curated to suit every occasion. Surround yourself with the comfort of friends and transform tea time into a timeless moment of indulgence and elegance.

Embracing Italy's rich traditions and culture, each dish incorporates the finest seasonal ingredients from Japan, especially the Kansai region.
