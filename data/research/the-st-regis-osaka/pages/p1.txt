# Welcome to The St. Regis Osaka

## Embrace luxury at our hotel in Osaka, Japan

The St. Regis Osaka welcomes guests to the heart of Midosuji, the Champs-Élysées of Osaka. Located in downtown Osaka, this luxury hotel offers convenient access to popular attractions including Universal Studios Japan, Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan, Osaka Castle, and premier shopping and entertainment districts. Ideally situated near major train and Osaka Metro stations, the hotel provides easy access to Kyoto, Nara, and nearby destinations, making it an ideal base for leisure and luxury travel in Japan. Guests can enjoy exceptional dining at the hotel’s signature restaurants, including La Veduta, Teppanyaki WAJO, and Brasserie RÉGINE, before relaxing at IRIDIUM featuring SOTHYS Spa. The hotel’s iconic 12th-floor terrace offers sweeping views of Osaka skyline and a sophisticated urban retreat. The hotel features 160 luxury guestrooms and suites with spacious layouts, elegant décor, and refined residential comfort. Every stay is elevated by the signature St. Regis Butler Service, available any time, day or night.

## Hotel Heritage

Designed by famed Japanese architect firm Nikken Sekkei, The St Regis Osaka stands 27 stories above Midosuji Avenue, known as Osaka’s Champs-Elysées.

Learn More

Located on Osaka's prestigious Midosuji Boulevard, The St. Regis Osaka is a landmark of timeless luxury, offering signature St. Regis hospitality in the heart of the city.

Midosuji Avenue has a long history and is considered a symbol of Osaka's urban development from the late Taisho Era (1925) through the end of the Showa Era (1989). Since then, the avenue has been expanded and its utility lines placed underground to preserve its elegant streetscape. Lined with beautiful ginkgo trees, Midosuji is now recognised as one of Osaka's 100 Best Green Spaces and is designated as a cultural asset of the city.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Osaka?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Osaka is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Osaka allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Osaka.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Osaka?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Osaka. Self parking is available at The St. Regis Osaka, with rates of ¥800.00 per hour, and ¥3500.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Osaka have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Osaka has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Osaka booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Osaka?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Osaka is Osaka International Airport - Itami (ITM). ITM is located approximately 16.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Osaka have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Osaka does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
