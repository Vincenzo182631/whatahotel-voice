Discover the finest expression of luxury at The St. Regis Osaka. Our newly elevated Suites are inspired by Osaka's flowing waterways and the artistry of the Azuchi-Momoyama era, blending contemporary sophistication with rich cultural heritage. Graceful lines, refined textures, and luminous gold accents create a design story that flows throughout each suite, reflecting the hotel's connection to Osaka's...See More natural beauty and timeless artistry. Expansive living spaces and residential-style comforts invite effortless relaxation.

Every Suite stay is enhanced by the signature St. Regis Butler Service, 24-hour breakfast served at your convenience, and Evening Cocktails at the iconic St. Regis Bar. ### St. Regis Suite

The St. Regis Suite embodies timeless elegance and refined residential luxury, offering generous living spaces thoughtfully designed for both relaxation and entertaining. Inspired by Osaka's flowing waterways and the artistry of the Azuchi-Momoyama era, the suite elevates the hotel's design narrative through graceful lines, layered textures, and subtle gold accents that create a seamless sense of sophistication...See More throughout the space. ## Tranquil Views

## Residential Elegance

Designed as an elegant private residence above the city, the 87 sqm St. Regis Suite draws inspiration from the gentle movement of Osaka's waterways and the beauty of morning mist. Soft textures, luminous gold accents, and bespoke Nishijin-ori silk weave a refined design story throughout the suite, creating an atmosphere of timeless serenity. Thoughtfully appointed for both quiet moments and sophisticated entertaining, the suite balances residential comfort with the signature elegance of St. Regis.

## Timeless Artistry

The king-size bed is framed by bespoke Nishijin-ori craftsmanship, inspired by the refined aesthetics of the Azuchi-Momoyama era. Intricate textures, soft illumination, and luminous gold accents unfold throughout the space, creating a harmonious dialogue between Japanese artistry and contemporary luxury. Designed with quiet elegance in mind, the bedroom offers a sophisticated retreat enriched by heritage, craftsmanship, and timeless design.

### Butler Service

St. Regis Butler Service is the signature of the St. Regis experience, providing you with personalized, anticipatory attention before, during, and after your stay. Upon arrival, allow a St. Regis Butler to take care of packing and unpacking, welcome beverage service, pressing, shoe shining, arranging dinner reservation, and any other assistance you may require.

### The Evening Ritual

Experience a moment of elegance by taking part in the 'Evening Ritual,' featuring our signature Champagne Sabrage ceremony performed daily at St. Regis hotels worldwide. Celebrate with a glass of champagne as the sky transforms during the magical twilight moment. We invite you to join this ceremonious ritual daily at 5:00 p.m. on the 12th floor.

### Exquisite Dining Experience

Savor ultimate culinary creations at our Italian, French, and Teppanyaki restaurants, where discerning interior aesthetics and a refined ambiance curate unforgettable dining experiences.
