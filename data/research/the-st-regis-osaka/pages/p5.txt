## Experience Osaka

Discover the harmony between the past and present of Osaka. Renowned for its exceptional cuisine, vibrant culture, and rich history, Osaka offers an array of unforgettable local experiences. Connect with the signature St. Regis Butler Service at our luxury hotel near Shinsaibashi, Osaka, for personalized recommendations and exclusive access to the city’s hidden gems and cultural discoveries.

### Concierge Recommendations

Our Concierge Desk is at your service, 24 hours a day. Count on us for everything from securing dinner reservations to golf tee times and tickets for nearby tours. We are happy to arrange for laundry services, floral arrangements, or any special request, including transportation.

### Kaiyukan

Visit some of the world’s most exotic fish in Japan’s largest aquarium and enjoy a ride on a Ferris wheel, just 20 minutes by train from this hotel near Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan.

### Minami Area

Osaka’s main shopping area is one train station away from the hotel.

### Arima Onsen

Spend a day at these hot springs with beautiful view of the Sea of Japan. About one hour away from the hotel by train.

### Osaka Castle

This beautiful castle and large park area is about a half hour away from the hotel by foot. In addition to touring, the area is a wonderful venue for jogging.

### Yodogawa Fireworks

Ranked among the top 10 most popular firework in Japan, this spectacle has close to 500,000 people watching the massive display of over 20,000 fireworks.

### Beautiful Fall Leaves at Minoo Park

The closest spot to the busy metropolis of Osaka to find a spacious natural recreation area. The park can be reached in less than 30 minutes from the downtown Osaka area.

### Universal Studios Japan

Universal Studios Japan offers exciting entertainment just a short journey from the hotel. Experience the magic of “The Wizarding World of Harry Potter” or step into the vibrant universe of "SUPER NINTENDO WORLD™ inspired by beloved Nintendo adventures.

## Walk the History of Osaka

Experience the 16th-century Japan of Azuchi-Momoyama at our hotel near Osaka Castle. Located within walking distance, Osaka Castle is one of the most famous iconic landmarks of Japan.

Learn More

### Dotonbori

Our hotel is located near Dotonbori in Osaka, which has long been well known as the city's shopping and entertainment mecca. At night, it is stunningly lit by hundreds of neon lights and mechanized signs, including the famous Glico Running Man sign and Kani Doraku crab sign.

### Umeda Sky Building

Modern landmark of Osaka offering an unobstructed 360-degree view of Osaka. Witness the vibrant modern city of Osaka in a new light, close to our hotel near Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan.

### Tsutenkaku Tower

A symbol of Naniwa, also known as the Eiffel Tower of Osaka. There is an observatory with a view of Osaka, and statue of Biriken, a god that is said to bring good luck to those who stroke the bottom of its feet.

## Tenjin Festival — One of Japan’s Three Major Festivals

Dating back to the 10th century, the Tenjin Festival is one of Osaka’s largest and most celebrated summer festivals, held annually each July and recognized as one of Japan’s three great festivals. Contact the signature St. Regis Butler Service for more information and personalized local recommendations.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Osaka have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Osaka offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Chest Press, Leg Press, Adjustable Pulley, Lat Machine, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Osaka have an on-site spa?

Yes, IRIDIUM featuring SOTHYS is located on-site at The St. Regis Osaka.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Osaka?

Nakanoshima Park, Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan, Abeno Harkus Observatory "HARUKAS 300", Osaka Castle, Umeda Sky Building / Kuchu Teien Observatory and more are nearby The St. Regis Osaka.
