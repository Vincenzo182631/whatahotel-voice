## The Emory

### Discover

Situated in Belgravia, London, The Emory is a modern masterpiece hotel where the truest luxury is time well spent. Each of its 61 suites & studios, including the top floor Penthouse, features elegant modernist interiors by a collection of the world’s leading designers. Guests of The Emory enjoy access to Surrenne Belgravia, Maybourne’s award-winning private members’ club for longevity and wellbeing.

- Book Your Stay

## Suites & Studio Suites

From the sweeping views of the city to the elevated and contemporary interiors created by designers André Fu, Pierre Yves Rochon, Alexandra Champalimaud, Rigby & Rigby and Patricia Urquiola, each suite and studio at The Emory is a welcoming, versatile, unhurried space – made for living, entertaining and unwinding.

- View Suites & Studios

## The Emory

### Private Floors

For those seeking ultimate privacy, generous space in the heart of London, or an outstanding setting to host family, friends or colleagues, The Emory's Private Floors provide the rare opportunity to enjoy exclusive use of an entire floor.

Featuring a selection of suites and studios accessible only to your guests, each floor has been individually crafted by a celebrated designer, including André Fu, Patricia Urquiola, Champalimaud Design, Rigby & Rigby and Pierre-Yves Rochon, offering the feel of a distinctive private residence.

- Complimentary fully stocked mini bar

- Unpacking and pressing service on arrival

## Wellness

### Surrenne Belgravia

Guests of The Emory have exclusive access to Surrenne Belgravia, a new era of experiential wellbeing and longevity. Sitting at the intersection of modern innovation and timeless wonder, Surrenne holds a transformative journey for all the senses, setting a new paradigm for wellbeing. Surrenne Belgravia's extraordinary treatments blend time-honoured rituals, ancient wisdom and pioneering wellness science.

- Discover Wellness at Surrenne Belgravia

## City Circadian Reset

The Emory’s City Circadian Reset is a retreat with the capital as its inspiration, designed to promote self-restoration. This personalised programme echoes the Surrenne philosophy and has been crafted to correct the circadian rhythm: the body’s natural, internal 24-hour clock that helps to regulate sleep, alertness, metabolism and hormone release, driven primarily by light and darkness.

- Discover City Circadian Reset
