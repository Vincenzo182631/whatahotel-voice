# Our differences

Contained within a trio of historic buildings in the heart of London’s fashionable Kensington, the charming Milestone is something of an undiscovered hospitality gem for many. But once discovered the hotel is never forgotten, for it offers sumptuous accommodations, unsurpassed service, genuine hospitality and a wealth of first-class food and beverage options to satisfy even the most discerning of travellers.

## Genuine hospitality

For our warm and attentive team, literally nothing is too much trouble day or night. Preparing for your stay is how we spend our day.
