# Family time, beautifully hosted

Set beside Hyde Park in the heart of Kensington, The Milestone Hotel & Residences offers an elegant, welcoming home for family stays in London, with spacious suites and private residences, flexible room configurations, and a team that takes genuine pride in hosting every generation.

Whether you are planning a school holiday escape, a birthday celebration, or a special gathering with grandparents, parents and children together, our home becomes yours.

Enquire about family stays

### A London address made for families

From our front door, London’s most iconic family experiences are within easy reach. With park strolls, museum days and theatre evenings on the doorstep, it is a location that suits every generation.

- Hyde Park and Kensington Gardens directly opposite, perfect for scenic walks and outdoor adventures

- Complimentary admission to selected Historic Royal Palaces attractions, including Kensington Palace, through our exclusive partnership with Historic Royal Palaces (subject to availability)

- World-renowned museums nearby, including the Natural History Museum and Science Museum

- Concerts and performances at the Royal Albert Hall close by

- Luxury shopping in Knightsbridge within easy reach

- London’s theatre district and a calendar of seasonal events easily accessible

## The Milestone's Local Favourites

Experience Kensington like a local - from royal stories and hidden gems to interactive fun for all ages, thoughtfully selected from our team’s favourite local recommendations.

Enquire now

### Discover

- **Victoria: A Royal Childhood Exhibition**

Discover where Princess Victoria grew up with a self-guided or themed family walkthrough of Kensington Palace - a perfect mix of British history and interactive discovery.
- **BTS Tour of the Royal Albert Hall**

Step beyond the stage on a guided tour of this iconic South Kensington concert hall - discover secret corners and stories that captivate both adults and curious youngsters.

### Explore

- **Leighton House Museum Family Trail**

Explore this stunning 19th-century artist’s home & studio with its extraordinary Arab Hall - a creative treasure so close you’ll wonder why more people don’t know about it. A local hidden gem.
- **Local History Walking Tour (Kid Friendly)**

Join our local resident historian Andy Williamson on a tailored walking tour, from royal footsteps to Victorian tales, bringing Kensington’s rich past vividly to life.

### Enjoy

- **Kid’s & Adults Zero Alcohol Cocktail Masterclass**

Enjoy a creative, fun cocktail masterclass (0% alcohol) for all ages - a memorable way to connect over fresh flavours and playful presentations.
- **Family Picnic in Kensington Gardens**

Spend a leisurely afternoon in Kensington Gardens with a Milestone-prepared picnic, enjoyed against the backdrop of Kensington Palace. Available May - September.

### A Royal Day Out, Complimentary for Hotel Guests

Make your family stay even more memorable with exclusive access to some of the nation's most iconic royal palaces. Through our partnership with Historic Royal Palaces, guests can enjoy a limited allocation of complimentary tickets to renowned attractions including the Tower of London, Hampton Court Palace and Kensington Palace.

Available exclusively to hotel guests, complimentary tickets are offered on a first-come, first-served basis and are subject to availability. We recommend contacting our concierge team as early as possible to secure your preferred attraction and visit date.
