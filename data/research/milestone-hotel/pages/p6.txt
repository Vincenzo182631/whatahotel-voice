# Stables Bar

Step inside The Milestone's newly renovated Stables Bar, named in homage to the building's original purpose. Rich dark wood panelling, sophisticated leather and tartan furnishings, and sporting artwork by renowned Belgian artist Elie Lambert create a refined yet welcoming atmosphere. Green leather banquettes and softly lit corners contrast beautifully with the natural light that floods the elegant conservatory, offering an inviting setting from morning through to evening.

Adjacent to the bar, The Hideaway provides a candlelit setting for intimate gatherings, relaxed dinners and elegant private celebrations. Whether joining us for an afternoon drink, pre-dinner cocktails or an evening with friends, The Stables Bar offers one of Kensington's most characterful settings.

## Opening hours

A favourite among hotel guests and locals alike, The Stables Bar offers a relaxed setting to unwind in one of London's most distinguished neighbourhoods. Perfectly placed moments from Kensington Palace, Kensington Gardens and the Royal Albert Hall, it's an inviting spot for everything from afternoon refreshments to expertly mixed evening cocktails.

### A Journey in Every Glass

Our cocktail menu celebrates flavours from around the world, combining premium spirits, house-made infusions and fragrant botanicals in every glass. Each signature cocktail has been thoughtfully created to offer a memorable drinking experience.

To accompany your drinks, Executive Chef Sam Saunders presents a menu of elevated sharing plates, bringing a contemporary touch to classic bar favourites with the finest seasonal ingredients.

### Meet the Manager

Victoria Micaela is the Bar Manager at Stables Bar, having been part of The Milestone since 2022. Her career in hospitality began 12 years ago, guided by a deep love for service and food, and driven by a desire to continually challenge herself. This curiosity and ambition led her to the world of mixology, which she regards as a space of absolute creativity.

In her own words: “ _The beauty of being able to create an experience is that every day feels like an adventure; the greatest gift we take from this life is the moments we live in it_.”

### Elevated Bar Dining

Executive Chef Sam Saunders brings a contemporary approach to bar dining, showcasing premium seasonal ingredients across a menu designed for sharing. From luxurious caviar and globally inspired small plates to indulgent burgers, vibrant salads and elevated comfort food, each dish is thoughtfully prepared to complement the relaxed yet refined atmosphere of The Stables Bar.

Our menu also features expertly recommended food and drink pairings, allowing you to discover combinations that enhance every flavour and elevate your dining experience.
