Discover what to taste and sip, where to slumber and venture, what experiences to enjoy, and who to connect with during your stay at The Milestone Hotel & Residences.

Delight your palate with our signature dish; our succulent Lamb cooked to tender perfection. The presentation is as impeccable as the taste, making it a must-try for any discerning guests visiting Chenestons at The Milestone Hotel & Residences.

Step into our Stables Bar and indulge in the classic sophistication of a Smoking Old Fashioned. Crafted with precision and care, this cocktail promises an unforgettable first sip in our atmospheric bar.

Indulge in the splendour of our Hermès Suite, where every detail is designed to immerse you in luxury and history. Perfect for relaxation and indulgence, this suite offers a unique blend of classic charm and modern comfort, making your stay with us truly unforgettable.

The Royal Albert Hall offers unparalleled experiences with its stunning Victorian architecture and exceptional acoustics. Don’t miss the chance to attend a diverse array of performances, from classical concerts to contemporary shows, in this breathtaking setting.
