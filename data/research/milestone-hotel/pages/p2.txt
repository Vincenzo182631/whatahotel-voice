### Interconnecting rooms

**Interconnecting rooms** have a door inside that connects two rooms together.

**Interleading rooms** feature an external door that links multiple rooms, allowing access to all rooms booked as interleading.

These rooms can accommodate four to 11 people, depending on availability, and must be booked directly through our reservations team. To check availability, please email us.
