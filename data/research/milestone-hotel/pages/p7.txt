# Wellness Club Membership

At The Milestone Wellness Club, each space has been thoughtfully designed to support both your energy and your ease. Set discreetly within the hotel, it offers a more private and personal approach to wellbeing, where every visit feels calm, considered and entirely your own.

You are welcome to enjoy our intimate wellness studio, with space for independent training or sessions with your own personal trainer, ensuring your routine remains uninterrupted. The resistance pool, cold plunge and infrared sauna provide a restorative setting to unwind and rebalance at your own pace. Whether you visit for gentle movement or quiet relaxation, this is a sanctuary shaped around you, in every season.

### Exclusive access

Enjoy exclusive access to a collection of calming, restorative spaces designed for privacy and ease. The Wellness Club is reserved for private use, ensuring every visit feels completely personal and entirely your own.

Move between the resistance pool, infrared sauna and cold plunge, each offering a gentle way to restore and rebalance. Our discreet wellness studio provides space for independent training or sessions with your own personal trainer, while elegant changing areas are thoughtfully appointed with premium amenities.

Complimentary lockers, water and herbal teas ensure every visit feels effortless, allowing you to arrive, unwind and focus entirely on your wellbeing.

### Members' Hours

Members enjoy exclusive use of the Wellness Club, with multiple visits permitted each day for up to 90 minutes per session.

Advance booking is required via our dedicated Concierge Team.

Open daily from 8:00am to 8:00pm.
