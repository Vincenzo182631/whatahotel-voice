## Kamikaze Golf

We’ll divide your group into four-person teams and use the first four holes on our golf course to present you with a variety of different challenges!

$150 per person \| 8-32 participants

### 1st Hole: Frisbee Best Ball / Pitching

Each player will maneuver their way down the first hole with a Frisbee, counting how many throws they make until they hit the pin on the green. The best score will be selected. Then, the team captain will choose one player to do the pitching challenge to add to the team’s Frisbee score.

### 2nd Hole: Hockey Best Ball / Chipping

Each player will make their way to the second green with a hockey stick and tennis ball, counting every shot. Once on the green, they will putt the tennis ball into the hole and the best team score will be counted. The team captain then will select one player to do the chipping challenge to add to the team’s hockey score.

### 3rd Hole: Tennis Best Ball / Putting

With a tennis racket and ball, players count how many shots it takes to get to the third green. Then they will putt the tennis ball into the hole, with the best score counting for the team. One player selected by the team captain will then complete the putting challenge, adding to their tennis score.

### 4th Hole: Golf Scramble / Bunker Shots

Each player will tee off from the forward tee. After selecting the best tee shot, each team member hits again from that spot, continuing the process until the ball is holed. This will be followed by a bunker challenge.

## Indoor Swing Analysis

* * *

Whether it’s a cocktail party, business meeting or evening event, Carmel Valley Ranch can help you create something both unique and memorable with indoor golf instruction!

Our state-of-the-art video analysis is entertaining for all levels of players, from beginners to advanced. Our two-camera system will record both front and side views of each player’s golf swing; then, using our software, our pros will provide analysis and tips.

Perhaps the most exciting feature of our equipment is the ability to compare swings to a database of top PGA and LPGA Tour professionals, including Tiger Woods and Annika Sorenstam.

Players also can e-mail their swing analysis to themselves, ensuring they can use our instructors’ comments once they get to their home course!
