# Get cozy with us   this fall

* * *

It’s cozy season at The Ranch, when morning mist over the vineyards burns off into sunny days perfect for lounging by the pool, savoring seasonal dining, and trying new activities. The kids might be back in school, but it’s still playtime here!

## Halloween weekend

October 30-31

* * *

Get spooky with us as we celebrate Halloween with activities that are more fun than frightful!

##### Bats & Brews

Enjoy the sights and sounds of twilight at The Ranch on this scenic guided stroll. You’ll join one of our naturalists on a dusk walk as you sip on a cold beverage of your choice while you dive in to the fascinating world of bats and other nocturnal wildlife. _October 30_ \| _Ages 21+_

##### Costumes & Cocktails

Celebrate Halloween with an enchanting evening in the garden. Harvest seasonal herbs and botanicals before crafting your own festive cocktail or mocktail inspired by the flavors of fall. Guests are encouraged to arrive in costume and embrace the spirit of the season! _October 31_ \| _Ages 16+_

##### Hilltop Meet & Greet & Halloween Costume Parade

Celebrate Halloween with a special Hilltop Animal Meet & Greet featuring our beloved herd! Join our Halloween Costume Parade, where guests and animals parade together in a festive celebration of the season. This fun-filled event includes candy stations, family-friendly games, and opportunities to meet our Ranch Hands and animal ambassadors. _October 31_
