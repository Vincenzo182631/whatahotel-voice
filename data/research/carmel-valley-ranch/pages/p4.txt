## How to Spa

- ## Reservations & Appointments

We encourage you to book your spa experience in advance. We request 12 hours advance notice to cancel or change a booked/or secured spa service. No-shows will be charged in full.

- ## What's included?

With a scheduled spa service of your choice, you’ll enjoy full access to Spa Aiyana and its facilities, including our saltwater pool, infinity hot tub, men’s and women’s locker rooms with eucalyptus steam, and co-ed warming room.

- ## Spa Aiyana Boutique

Sometimes the best gifts come in the smallest packages, like our Aiyana gift cards, available in any denomination to grant wishes of all sizes. The Spa Aiyana Boutique also offers an indulgent array of spa products, activewear, lifestyle items and thoughtful gifts.

- ## Age Restrictions

All spa facilities are designed for adult use. Minimum age for facials is 16 years old and minimum age for massages is 18 years old.

- ## Chill Time

Our lounges are places of relaxation and respite, encouraging you to immerse yourself in utter tranquility. The use of cellular phones during your service and in the Spa Aiyana facilities is strongly discouraged.

- ## Gratuities

For your convenience, gratuities are provided to Spa Staff through a 20% Service Charge, which is included in your treatment total.
