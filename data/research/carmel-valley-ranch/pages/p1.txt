LIFE AT THE RANCH

## YOUR DESTINATION FOR DISCOVERY

* * *

Carmel Valley Ranch is a place to roam, to laugh, to make memories with those you love. At The Ranch, you never know what’s next—you just know it’ll be awesome.

Here, artisans craft cheese with milk from our own goats, grow grapes for our own wines, press oil from our own olives. We walk you through our gardens and guide you on off-road excursions. Goats, alpacas and the rest of our herd offer the chance to both learn and make new friends.

Whether you’re swinging on the golf course or pickleball courts, unwinding on the massage table, or even just lounging at one of our pools, at The Ranch you’ll discover that play is for everyone—from the young to the young at heart.
