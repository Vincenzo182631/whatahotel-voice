# The Taste of Waldorf

Waldorf Astoria Berlin offers an exceptional variety of culinary experiences: ROCA, a modern tribute to Italian Soul Food with subtle New York vibes inspired by the urban spirit of the metropolis; Times Square, our light-filled breakfast restaurant serving a rich and refined morning selection to begin the day with indulgence; the Lang Bar, capturing the glamorous spirit of the 1920s with its urban, characterful atmosphere; and the Library on the 15th floor, a serene retreat with a fantastic view over City West, perfect for quiet moments above the rooftops of Berlin.

## ROCA

In the heart of Berlin’s City West, ROCA blends Italian soul with the urban spirit of New York. Mediterranean flavors, modern city energy and effortless lightness shape a cuisine that feels both familiar and refreshingly cosmopolitan—infused with Italian joie de vivre and the dynamic rhythm of Manhattan. Fresh colors and natural, sustainable materials create a tasteful setting, highlighted by a flamboyant hand-painted ceiling mural. On warm days, the terrace becomes a favorite spot to unwind while the vibrant life of Berlin passes by.

## ALL DAY BREAKFAST FEAST

A breakfast like no other, the all-day ‘Champagne and Eggs’ feast provides the perfect option for sweet and savory palates alike. Waldorf Astoria inventions such as Eggs Benedict are served alongside fresh, stacked, blueberry pancakes.

## LANG BAR

The bar is operated by Stilian Laufer and Oliver Timm under their own label.

Sit back and relax in the lush Art Deco surroundings while you enjoy one of many Film Noir-inspired drinks created by Waldorf’s master mixologists.

## HIGH TEA AT THE LIBRARY

A private guest space with its own business center, guests can take care of work while enjoying afternoon tea with views across the city from the 15th-floor Library Lounge.

## PEACOCK ALLEY

Enjoy the unique atmosphere with classical afternoon tea or contemporary cocktails. Named after a grand corridor connecting the original Waldorf Hotel to its sister, the Astoria, in New York, Peacock Alley at the Waldorf Astoria Berlin is a modern interpretation of its New York sister.

## Times Square Restaurant

The Times Square Restaurant in the heart of Berlin offers an exquisite breakfast experience in an elegant atmosphere. Guests can start the day with a first-class selection of culinary delights: The buffet offers regional and seasonal specialties, homemade pastries, freshly squeezed juices and champagne as well as a selection of sweet and savory à la carte dishes.
