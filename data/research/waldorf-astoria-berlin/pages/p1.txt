## IDYLLIC ESCAPISM

Discover a sanctuary of tranquility in the heart of Berlin at the Waldorf Astoria Spa. Our exclusive signature treatments are uniquely available in Germany only at Waldorf Astoria Berlin, bringing together two legendary brands to create an unforgettable spa experience in the center of the city.

In addition to our luxurious treatments, the Waldorf Astoria Spa Berlin features a heated indoor pool, a sauna area, and a newly equipped fitness room. After your sauna session, unwind on our sun terrace while enjoying beautiful views of the iconic Kaiser Wilhelm Memorial Church.
