# Rooms & Suites

Discover unforgettable skyline views, latest technology and a unique balance of comfort and contemporary style in one of Waldorf Astoria Berlin’s 228 rooms and suites.

All rooms and suites feature a marble bathroom, Nespresso coffee machine, TV, dining table, WiFi access and safe. Upgrade for exclusive extras including tower elevator access.

All our rooms are non-smoking, smoking is only possible on the balconies of the Tower Suites.
