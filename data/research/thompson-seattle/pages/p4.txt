### The Hardwood Floors Won Me Over

I had a wonderful experience at the Thompson Seattle during a recent business meeting. From the moment I arrived, I was impressed by the cleanliness, comfort, and thoughtful amenities throughout the hotel.

My room was exceptionally clean, cozy, and well-maintained. One of my favorite features was the hardwood flooring, which I greatly prefer over carpet and which made the room feel fresh and inviting. I also appreciated the black slippers and plush robe provided for guest use, which added a nice touch of comfort. The bed was immaculate and comfortable, and I appreciated having complimentary bottled water available, along with additional water and snacks for purchase.

While I would have loved a more direct view, I was still able to catch a glimpse of the ocean from my room by looking to the right, which was a pleasant bonus.

I was visiting for a business meeting, and the meeting experience exceeded my expectations. The meeting space felt both intimate and spacious, creating a comfortable environment for collaboration. There was an ample supply of water and tea available throughout the day, which was greatly appreciated.

The food was truly outstanding. Everything tasted fresh, healthy, and thoughtfully prepared. The halibut was a standout dish, and the dessert selections were equally impressive. It's rare to find meeting and conference food that feels both nutritious and delicious, but the culinary team delivered on both.

Overall, the Thompson Seattle provided an excellent experience with comfortable accommodations, exceptional food, and welcoming meeting facilities. I would gladly stay here again and would highly recommend it to both business and leisure travelers visiting Seattle.

### Great views, great staff

Located just a block away from Pike Place Market, this hotel offers amazing views of the waterfront from my room. The room was clean and tidy without any issues. Bathroom was wonderful especially due to the full size hair and body products that smelled wonderful. The reception staff was very helpful and able to help very promptly with our request. We went to the rooftop bar for drinks around sunset after our early dinner and they were pretty good. Not really a dinner option by itself as they only had light bites. Highly recommend for anyone who is able to spend a little bit more to stay right in the Pike Place area. We were only slated to stay for two nights and spend one full day in Seattle so it was a great home base for us.
