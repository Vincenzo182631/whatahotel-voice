# Restaurants & Bars

Savior something spectacular at our hotel's restaurant and rooftop bar near Pike Place Market. ## The Nest

Above the urban hustle of Pike Place Market and Seattle’s downtown, The Nest — our iconic rooftop bar, cocktail lounge, and terrace — showcases views of Elliott Bay and the Olympic Mountain Range beyond. The Nest specializes in handcrafted cocktails, wine and small snacks featuring seasonal fare highlighting the Pacific Northwest. Ages 21+ only. Please note this bar is cashless. Credit cards, debit cards and other contactless forms of payment are accepted.
