# Hotel Policies

### Destination Fee

A daily destination fee of $29.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

- 5% off any Hotel experience (such as yoga classes and guided tours; see Front Desk for current offerings)
- Priority Entry at The Nest Rooftop Bar & Lounge
- Sparkling Wine at Check In
- Local Herkimer Coffee & Tea Service in Lobby, 6am-10am
- Bang Trims & dry styling refresh at Antonio Salon, plus, 20% off regularly priced hair services
- 20% off all regularly priced spa services at Antonio Spa
- 10% off any regularly priced class or spa service at Urban Yoga Spa
- 10% off regularly priced purchases at Seleuss Chocolates
- $10 daily Food & Beverage credit for Conversation Dinner only, non-cumulative or transferable, excludes specials
- Priority access and discounted tickets to on-site programming (such as dinners with local vendors and movie nights; see Front Desk for current events)
- 20% off all Argosy Cruises (Booked through Concierge)
- Bogo- Purchase one Tasting at Mark Ryan Woodinville Tasting Room and get one free
