# Boutique Hotel Near Pike Place Market \| Thompson Seattle, by Hyatt

## EXPLORE DOWNTOWN SEATTLE FROM OUR BOUTIQUE HOTEL NEAR PIKE PLACE

With a sleek glass façade offering dramatic views of Puget Sound, Thompson Seattle embodies the beauty of the Emerald City, just steps from Pike Place Market. At the connection of downtown and Belltown, explore the culture of Seattle from our hotel while soaking in its sights, sounds, and flavors from a distinctly local perspective. Our boutique hotel’s 152 rooms and suites ensure you live like a local in modern style, while our lobby-level restaurant and rooftop cocktail lounge—The Nest—bring the best of downtown Seattle and the waterfront within sight.

## rooms & suites

Our downtown Seattle hotel rooms feature floor-to-ceiling windows that showcase views of Pike Place Market, Puget Sound water views, and the Olympic Mountains beyond. Sleek, luxurious surroundings befitting a luxury suite are infused with warm details, including hardwood floors and rainfall showers.

Hotel Happenings

A Taste Of Thompson Dinner Parties

Prepare for an unforgettable evening at Thompson Seattle’s signature ‘Taste of Thompson’ dinner party experience, where the theme is an exquisite Asian-Pacific Northwest fusion. As you set foot into your Penthouse Suite for the evening, you'll be enveloped by the aromas of tantalizing flavors mingling in the air. The centerpiece of the menu is a mouthwatering tomahawk steak, expertly cooked by our highly skilled culinary team in the suite’s fully equipped Chef’s kitchen, and presented for guests to share, allowing for a sense of togetherness and communal dining.

Accompanying the steak are an array of thoughtfully curated sides, inspired by Vietnamese cuisine, specifically designed to create delectable and authentic wraps bursting with fresh flavors and textures. To complement the culinary delights, a dedicated bartender will be on hand, skillfully crafting cocktails infused with local ingredients and teaching mixology techniques, to elevate the overall dining experience. With every bite and sip, this intimate dinner party promises to transport guests on a unique gastronomic journey that fuses Asian and Pacific Northwest influences into a harmonious and unforgettable culinary affair.
