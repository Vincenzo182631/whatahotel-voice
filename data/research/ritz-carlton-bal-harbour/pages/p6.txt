The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami
An elegant waterfront resort with easy access to upscale Bal Harbour.

Why book?
For a luxurious, family-friendly resort removed from the South Beach fray with easy access to upscale shopping in Bal Harbour.
Set the scene
Perched at the northern end of Bal Harbour, the Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour is almost entirely surrounded by water and features spectacular views overlooking the Atlantic Ocean on one side and the Intracoastal Waterway on the other. The spacious rooms are comfortably laid out, with only two per floor—maximizing privacy—and each features a private terrace. While it’s a large resort, the lack of crowds and personalized service gives it an intimate feel—a welcome contrast to the sometimes raucous atmosphere of Miami hotels. It's the perfect home-away-from-home for families who want a quiet, boutique-ish escape that still offers the full-service amenities of a large resort.
The backstory
Originally opened as the Regent Bal Harbour in 2008, the hotel has gone through several different owners and management iterations over the years, eventually becoming a Ritz-Carlton property in 2014. One of only a handful of hotels in the tiny enclave, it occupies an envious spot, offering views of speedboats and fishing charters crossing the inlet. Beyond the amenities spread out over its five acres, its location offers easy access to the boutiques and restaurants at the Bal Harbour Shops while remaining a comfortable distance from thumping South Beach.
The rooms
With sumptuous materials like dark mahogany floors, bamboo walls, and leather tiles, the rooms feel plush and cozy, a refreshing change from the stark white minimalism of most hotels on South Beach. Efforts have been made to include high-tech elements like flat-screen TVs embedded into bathroom mirrors and surround-sound systems that give the cushy rooms a futuristic feel. The overall result is a handsome, restful retreat in a town that usually goes all in on drama.
Food and drink
If you want to dine on the property, the options are slim but good—pick from the poolside Water’s Edge, with its seasonal favorites and signature cocktails, and Artisan Beach House, which specializes in Mediterranean cuisine like meze and brick oven pizzas, enjoyed on a waterfront terrace that feels like you’re on the bow of an ocean liner. Artisan Beach House also hosts live music on the weekends and a sumptuous weekend brunch overlooking the water from its glass-enclosed dining room.
The spa
The on-site Exhale spa offers a full schedule of fitness and yoga classes in addition to spa treatments in an oceanfront hall of pampering. Expect an expansive menu of massages, facials, manicures, and waxing, among other offerings.
The neighborhood/area
The storied village of Bal Harbour, with its million-dollar penthouses and upscale shopping, conjures images of bauble-clad ladies sipping Kir Royal cocktails on café patios with piles of shopping bags at their feet. But that is changing. Today, you’ll find young couples pushing strollers along the newly refurbished beach path. The remodeled Bal Harbour Shops is attracting cross-generational shoppers just as much in search of the perfect tuna tartare as luxury goods. Plus, there’s a cultural presence here, with galleries and a town-sponsored public art program that infuses this enclave with a jolt of creativity and edginess. Despite its small size, this coastal town is constantly evolving, welcoming new shops, restaurants, and attractions with each season.
The service
Service is warm enough that you feel looked after, but also chill enough that you have room to just be. From check-in to check-out, staff remain attentive and efficient. Fashion lovers should take advantage of the hotel’s “Couture Concierge,” a personalized shopping experience linked to Bal Harbour Shops—or to one of the many other shopping districts in Miami—with stylist and editor Elysze Held.
For families
While this is the perfect escape for couples and adult groups, children are very much welcome. The semi-private beach ensures you won’t have to worry about crowds, and the hotel pool hosts kid-friendly activities like swimming contests and smoothie tastings. Fur babies are also welcome here with a pet-friendly policy and an oceanfront dog park steps from the hotel.
Eco effort
The Ritz-Carlton brand is committed to supporting the development of local communities, protecting natural resources, and promoting sustainability education. Guests can participate in the
Community Footprints program
, which is a partnership between each Ritz-Carlton hotel and local charities and communities.
Accessibility
This is an ADA-accessible property with elevator access to all floors, including the restaurant and lounge. Several rooms are ADA-compliant, with bathroom handrails and roll-in showers.
Anything left to mention?
If you're keen on remaining active while staying here, you have plenty of options. There are daily fitness classes at the in-house Exhale spa, access to bicycles, and the beach path that runs along the ocean behind the hotel is the perfect place for a jog or power walking session.
