Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami
Where Coastal Calm Meets Contemporary Sophistication
A coastal classic, reimagined. Reopening January 2027, The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami returns as an intimate coastal retreat where thoughtful design, vibrant social energy, and a profound sense of privacy converge. Set on one of Miami Beach’s most coveted and remarkably rare waterfront sites — where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Intracoastal Waterway — the reimagined resort will introduce a chef-led signature restaurant, an exclusive Members Club, and an elevated Ritz-Carlton Spa with enhanced wellness and fitness experiences designed to inspire restoration and renewal. This transformative evolution will touch every aspect of the guest experience, from guest rooms and suites to dining, pool and beach, and public spaces — all while preserving the intimate, residential character that has long defined the resort. Our Ladies and Gentlemen look forward to welcoming guests back to Bal Harbour’s most secluded sanctuary — beautifully redesigned, thoughtfully reimagined, and unlike anywhere else in Miami Beach.
A Resort Reimagined
Our resort is undergoing a transformation with plans to welcome you again starting January 2027. We hope you share in our excitement as we create an enduring escape for all who visit.

Unscripted Bal Harbour
Unscripted Bal Harbour is an arts and culture program that provides unique access to the arts for residents and guests through the Art Unscripted Museum Access Pass. We are pleased to offer guests complimentary access to Miami’s premier arts institutions, art tours, museums, and private collections. Pet-Friendly Features
With its year-round sunshine, beautiful beaches and dog-friendly parks, our hotel in Bal Harbour is the perfect place to travel with your four-legged friend. The resort offers an array of amenities and services to accommodate canine guests including food and water bowls, disposal bags, homemade treats and connections with local groomers and vets. Just outside, the hotel’s new dog park is ideal for walks and features winding pathways, plenty of grass, palm trees for shade and views of the Atlantic Ocean

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 35.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami. The daily valet fee is $55.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami is Miami International Airport (MIA). MIA is located approximately 17.3 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
