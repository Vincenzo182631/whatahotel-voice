Ocean-View Rooms & Suites in Miami
Suites & Rooms
The ocean takes center stage in all our suites and hotel rooms in North Miami Beach​. With just two accommodations per floor, enjoy enhanced privacy and uninterrupted views. Floor-to-ceiling windows, private terraces, and standalone tubs overlook the water, while the décor reflects the surrounding sea’s tranquility through natural tones and textures. Our Bal Harbour hotel suites offer up to two bedrooms and include living rooms and kitchens.
A Resort Reimagined
Our resort is undergoing a transformation with plans to welcome you again starting January 2027. We hope you share in our excitement as we create an enduring escape for all who visit.
