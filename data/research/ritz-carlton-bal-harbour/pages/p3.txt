Experiences
Enjoy a variety of enriching experiences, from daily wellness classes and live music to beachfront games and guided activities with Ritz Kids. Krigler
Born out of love in 1904, Krigler is a legendary fragrance house offering mysteriously enticing and elite fragrances from the Krigler dynasty. Each bottle of Krigler narrates history; a story of an era. Offering classic fragrances along with updated versions delivering exciting twists to popular favorites, Krigler continues its time-honored tradition of bringing the world of fragrance exceptional craftsmanship.

Area & Activities
Explore Art, Shopping & the Outdoors
Secluded on Bal Harbour beach, guests can relax at the beach and spa or set out to explore the city. Art lovers enjoy complimentary access to the city's top museums and private collections with the hotel's Unscripted Art Access pass. Private cabanas and beds can be reserved at the pool or semi-private beach. Reservations at South Beach’s most coveted clubs and restaurants can be arranged, or escape the city with an outing to the Everglades.

Photography Concierge
Remember your time at The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami through the lens of a professional photographer. This luxury hotel near Miami Beach Convention Center is close to these attractions and others, providing the perfect base for exploration.

Does The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
