# Five-star luxury hotel in the centre of Bologna

## Perfetto, Italiano.

The Grand Hotel Majestic “già Baglioni”, the only luxury five-star hotel in Bologna and member of The Leading Hotels of the World since 1990, is a historic palazzo dating back to the 18th century.

Its rooms and suites stand out with the most refined finishes in the city, with elegant drapery, refined fabrics and finely worked marble. The hotel's impeccable service and cutting-edge technologies integrate harmoniously with the aristocratic atmosphere which has always been characteristic of the structure.

## The Art of Hospitality

At Grand Hotel Majestic “già Baglioni”, the art of hospitality is expressed through a set of experiences curated with sartorial detail: from the I Carracci restaurant and social spaces, where Italian culinary tradition meets frescoes from the Carracci school, to the spaces for meetings, conferences and events, designed to host any professional appointment with style and discretion. The hotel's historic spaces transform into the ideal settings for weddings, receptions and private celebrations, while personalised tours and experiences allow guests to discover Bologna through its art, history and excellences. Hospitality which goes beyond simply hosting: it accompanies, interprets and enhances every moment.

Majestic Terraces

### Majestic Terraces

Filled with flowers and the scent of jasmine, our terraces represent the ideal setting for moments of true relaxation. Located on the piano nobile, reserved for the suites, the Terrazza Torre - overlooking Palazzo Fava - and the Terrazza Foyer offer an intimate and discreet atmosphere. The terraces can be booked for drinks or light snacks, with a fee for the dedicated service.
