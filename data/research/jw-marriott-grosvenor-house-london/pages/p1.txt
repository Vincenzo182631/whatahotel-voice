### Heritage and Culture

## The Heritage and Star-Studded History of Grosvenor House

JW Marriott Grosvenor House London is a Park Lane icon with a story that has created a hotel as unique as its past. The former home of the Dukes of Westminster and Gloucester was originally designed and rebuilt as a hotel in 1929 by famed architects L. Rome Guthrie and Sir Edwin Lutyens and has long been associated with royalty, international dignitaries and celebrities alike.

### Iconic Events

Our magnificent Great Room and art deco inspired Ballroom have played host to some of the most significant and prominent national events of the last century including the BAFTA's, Pride of Britain Awards and The Royal Caledonian Ball to name a few. Grosvenor House is a landmark hotel of scale with one of the most desirable address for society and celebrity occasions.

### The Great Room

The Great Room has played host to significant events since 1929. A host to Royalty, dignitaries and business leaders for more than 90 years, the Great Room is one of Europe’s largest hotel ballrooms, seating up to 2000 guests for a sumptuous dinner. Celebrating the rich heritage of the room as a venue of scale for society and celebrity events and awards since 1929. The Great Room was once an ice rink where late Queen Elizabeth II and her sister Margaret learnt to skate as children.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Grosvenor House London allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London. One pet is allowed per room.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London. The daily self parking fee is £60.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Grosvenor House London have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Grosvenor House London has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Grosvenor House London booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Grosvenor House London?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Grosvenor House London is London City Airport (LCY). LCY is located approximately 14.4 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Grosvenor House London have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Grosvenor House London has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Grosvenor House London have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Grosvenor House London has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
