### Steak

Driven by a deep-rooted passion for traditional American steakhouse dishes, our JW Steakhouse menu boasts a selection of seven steak varieties, each offered in seven distinct cuts. Sustainably sourced from world-renowned producers, the selection brings together premium cuts from Creekstone, USDA and Kansas Ranch in the USA, to Australian Wagyu and UK-reared cattle.

### Bourbon Bar

Over 500 rare, single-batch bourbons are showcased in our bourbon cabinet, sitting adjacent to the bar, ready for you to explore. Our curation of rare and luxurious bourbons is almost a century in the making, allowing you to experience rare and luxurious flavours right here at our London address.

### JW Cheesecake

On a trip to New York, our chefs fell in love with a cheesecake. Inspired, they took to the development kitchen and here, the iconic JW Steakhouse cheesecake was born. A twist on the classic, our creamy, sweet and tangy filling sits within a rich Graham cracker base. Delicious and decadent, there’s always room.

## The Tales of Beatrix Potter Afternoon Tea

In celebration of the 160th anniversary of Beatrix Potter, Grosvenor House draws inspiration from her timeless tales with the Tales of Beatrix Potter™ Afternoon Tea. From mischievous adventures to much-loved characters, every creation is thoughtfully designed to evoke a sense of nostalgia and delight - inviting guests to step into the charming world of Beatrix Potter.
