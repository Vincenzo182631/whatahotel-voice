# Accommodations at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London

## Accommodations

Commanding one of Mayfair’s most desirable locations, our luxury hotel rooms and family suites in London exude design sophistication and a contemporary British feel throughout. Our rooms take cues from autumn and spring in Hyde Park while our suites showcase a palette of warm sultry neutrals, creating depth and adding warmth to the space. A delicate foliage pattern gently winds through the hallways...See More and rich golden leaves layer the soft carpets. Offering warm, intentional service, we present our guests with the opportunity to replenish their spirits, feel inspired and fulfilled; a full home-away-from-home experience whether travelling for business or pleasure. ## Explore our Premium Suites

Perfectly positioned in the heart of Mayfair, since opening its doors in 1929 as Park Lane’s first hotel, JW Marriott Grosvenor House London offers exceptional suites with plush pillowtop beds, elegant marble bathrooms, beautifully curated amenities and sweeping views of Hyde Park, some boasting private balconies.

Explore Now

### Executive Suites

The Executive Suites boast delicate foliage patterns with rich golden leaves, taking cues from autumn in Hyde Park. These suites offer a haven away from the bustle of London and with separate living quarters, are perfect for entertaining and relaxing in Park Lane’s first hotel.

### Park View Suites

The Park View Suites offer a spacious and luxurious residential feel in the heart of Mayfair. A home away from home, enjoy waking up in a comfortable king-size bedroom with separate living room overlooking scenic views of Hyde Park and iconic Park Lane.

Explore Now

### Premium Park View Suites

Luxuriously designed for the discerning business or leisure traveller, the Premium Park View Suites offer contemporary British style and some boast private terraces overlooking the greenery of Hyde Park. Enjoy the luxury of a king-size bed, chaise longue to relax, separate living and dining quarters and opulent marble ensuite bathroom.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London is the Family Room with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Grosvenor House London permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
