# Seamera Restaurant

### THE ULTIMATE BREAKFAST EXPERIENCE IN MYKONOS

## Seamera Restaurant

Welcome to Seamera, Mykonos Riviera’s latest multisensory breakfast concept, designed to kick-start your day with an unforgettable experience. Nestled among luscious terraced olive trees, this stunning sea-view restaurant invites you to indulge in a breakfast like no other, where every bite is infused with the flavors of Greece and the essence of Cycladic tradition.

Start your morning with our authentic Greek buffet, or explore a selection of carefully curated à la carte dishes, thoughtfully crafted to satisfy every taste and preference. From light and refreshing choices to hearty and indulgent delights, our menu is designed to elevate your breakfast experience.

Unwind in a picturesque setting reminiscent of a charming Greek village square, where the ambiance perfectly complements the cuisine, creating a harmonious blend of flavors and scenery. As the Aegean breeze gently embraces you, sip on freshly brewed coffee, savor artisanal pastries, and immerse yourself in the timeless elegance of a Mykonian morning.

For those who love to linger a little longer, Seamera thoughtfully serves breakfast until 11:30 AM, ensuring that even late risers don’t miss out on the magic of the island’s most important meal.

After all, Mykonian days are among the longest and most luminous in the world—to fully embrace them, you need a nourishing and delightful start. Discover why Seamera isn’t just a restaurant, but the epitome of a luxurious breakfast experience, set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Aegean Sea.
