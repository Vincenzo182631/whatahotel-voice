### A SENSORY DINING EXPERIENCE UNDER THE STARS

## Lafs Restaurant

Discover a new, sensational gastronomic concept in Mykonos, where Mykonian-inspired recipes are reimagined through the lens of contemporary Greek cuisine.

### Fine Comfort Dining

Blending Greece’s rich gastronomic traditions with modern style and presentation, LAFS Restaurant offers one of the most sought-after fine dining experiences on the island. Every dish is crafted with the freshest, handpicked local ingredients, delivering flavors that captivate and inspire.

Perched at the hotel’s most exclusive vantage point, overlooking the marina and the endless blue of the Aegean, LAFS is the ultimate destination—whether you’re celebrating a special occasion, marking a romantic moment, or simply indulging in an unforgettable evening with friends and family.

### A Multisensory Dining Experience

At LAFS Restaurant, dining is more than just a meal—it’s an immersive journey. Renowned Executive Chef Stergios Bitsis masterfully reinvents time-honored Greek recipes with contemporary flair, creating a symphony of flavors, textures, and aromas. His seasonal, seafood-centric approach has earned him a stellar reputation among culinary aficionados.

### An Unforgettable Ambiance

Beyond exquisite cuisine, LAFS offers an enchanting alfresco setting, where candlelight, live saxophone performances, and ambient DJ sets elevate your dining experience. Bask in the glow of the Cycladic sunset, and let the starlit sky provide the perfect backdrop to an evening of indulgence.

### A Tribute to Mykonian culinary history & Fine Wine

Celebrating Mykonos’ rich heritage, the LAFS menu highlights fresh, locally sourced ingredients, with thoughtfully curated vegetarian and vegan options available.

To complement your meal, our extensive wine cellar features a handpicked selection of Greek and international vintages, while LAFS’ expert mixologist crafts innovative, island-inspired cocktails using fresh fruits, fragrant herbs, and premium spirits.

### Indulge in an exquisite tasting menu

For a truly immersive culinary experience, LAFS Restaurant presents a 5 course & a 9 course tasting menus, showcasing the finest creations from Executive chef Stergios Bitsis.

Through this exceptional culinary journey, guests are invited to savor the richness of Greece’s traditions, gastronomic heritage, and diverse flavors, shaped by the nation’s unique herbal influences and religious food customs

Each course is expertly paired with select wines to elevate every flavor and texture.

Served at your candlelit table by the sea, this reservations-only haute cuisine experience begins after 19:30 PM at LAFS’ stunning outdoor veranda
