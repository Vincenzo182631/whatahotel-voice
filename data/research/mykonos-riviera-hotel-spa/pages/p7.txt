### THE MYKONOS RIVIERA 8 BEDROOM VILLA

## Overview

- 490 m² / 5274 ft²
- 30 guests
- 5 Outdoor heated pools

Perched at Mykonos Riviera’s highest point, this brand-new luxury villa offers breathtaking Aegean, sunset, and Mykonos Town views. Spanning 490 sq.m. across three floors, it accommodates up to 30 guests with private access, 13 bathrooms, three 39 sq.m. rooms, and five 75 sq.m. suites—four with private infinity pools. A fully equipped kitchen, elegant lounge, and semi-outdoor living room enhance the experience. Outdoors, an 80 sq.m. heated infinity sky pool, an outdoor bar, designer daybeds, and a shaded dining area create the ultimate retreat, ensuring privacy and sophistication.
