## The Pool Club

Life is cooler by the pool—especially when it’s an infinity-edge architectural masterpiece and one of Mykonos’ most Instagrammable spots. Welcome to the Mykonos Riviera Pool Club, the vibrant heart of our resort, where unforgettable moments unfold. Whether you’re here to swim, sunbathe, socialize, or spark a timeless romance, this is the place to be.

Take a refreshing dip in our crystal-clear heated infinity pool, where the sky meets the sea in a seamless horizon. Enjoy a leisurely swim under the warm Mediterranean sun, or float effortlessly as you soak in breathtaking Aegean views. For those seeking a more invigorating experience, aqua fitness sessions and morning laps add an energetic touch to your day.

After your swim, indulge in the culinary delights of Mykonos’ finest sea-view restaurant. Our menu offers a perfect balance of light bites and refined dishes, all crafted with the freshest ingredients and infused with authentic Greek tradition. From flavorful appetizers to exquisite entrées, every dish is designed to satisfy every palate.

Pair your meal with a refreshing mocktail or a signature cocktail inspired by the essence of summer. Sip on selections from Greece’s finest vineyards, sway to uplifting music, and immerse yourself in an atmosphere of endless possibilities.

Stay for lunch, unwind through sunset, and embrace the magic of the night. As the glow of fire pits illuminates the evening, enjoy a post-dinner brandy at LAFS, setting the perfect stage for an unforgettable Mykonos experience.
