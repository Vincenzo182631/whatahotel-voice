## Luxury Spa Therapies in Mykonos

Welcome to Oqua Spa & Thalasso Center, a world of wellness, beauty, and relaxation at the Mykonos Riviera Hotel & Spa. Nestled in the heart of the Aegean, our luxury spa sanctuary blends ancient Greek healing rituals with modern techniques for an unforgettable spa experience in Mykonos. Whether you seek rejuvenation, detoxification, or pure indulgence, Oqua Spa offers the widest variety of therapies in Mykonos – from holistic treatments to advanced beauty services, personalized rituals, and exclusive bridal packages.
