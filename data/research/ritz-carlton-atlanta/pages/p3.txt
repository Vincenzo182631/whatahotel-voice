## Executive Chef Quinn Bullard

With a distinguished career spanning luxury hotels, Executive Chef Quinn Bullard brings exceptional leadership, expertise and creative vision to the food and beverage program. His culinary philosophy focuses on the innovative integration of sweet and savory elements, masterful smoking techniques and commitment to sustainability. An avid outdoorsman, motorcyclist and music enthusiast, his spirit of discovery drives both his work and personal pursuits.

## Dining al Fresco - Open Seasonally

The AG Veranda provides a southern city feel as you enjoy locally flavored dishes and refreshing cocktails while overlooking Peachtree Street. Bask in the beautiful sunny weather or gaze into the city lights when the sun sets at our luxury restaurant in Atlanta's​ downtown. The Veranda makes for the perfect setting all season long.

### The Distillery

Welcome to The Distillery, a private dining room with an elegant and stylish design. With seating for 16, this is the perfect setting for an intimate gathering of friends, and colleagues, or a special celebration.

### Lumen Lounge

Relax in a refined, luminescent setting as you experience artistry in motion at our hotel bar in downtown Atlanta, Georgia. Enjoy locally sourced, globally inspired cuisine that speaks to the avid traveler, complemented by masterfully crafted mixology and live entertainment on select evenings.

## Jittery Joe’s Coffee Shop

Elevate your morning ritual at Jittery Joe’s local coffee shop. Savor a housemade pastry or hearty breakfast sandwich designed for life on the go. Pair gourmet bites with our signature coffee blend, Eighty-Four Pour, a nod to the hotel’s opening in April 1984.
