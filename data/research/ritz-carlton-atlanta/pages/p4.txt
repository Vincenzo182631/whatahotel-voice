# Discover Atlanta from our hotel near Georgia Aquarium

## Experiences

Known as the Gateway to the New South, Atlanta attracts visitors from around the globe with its dynamic blend of history, culture and world-class attractions. This is a city where you can step back in time at the Margaret Mitchell House, explore the city’s contributions to social justice at The Center for Civil and Human Rights or simply head out from our hotel near the Mercedes-Benz Stadium to Centennial Olympic Park to spend an afternoon strolling through 22 acres of green space in the heart of Atlanta. Immerse yourself in the excitement of this iconic city when staying at our hotel near State Farm Arena. Delight in the convenience of Peachtree Street, where attractions like the Georgia Aquarium and the College Football Hall of Fame are just moments away. With MARTA public transit close by as well, all of Atlanta unfolds from the hotel’s doorstep. Explore on your own, or allow the hotel concierge to craft an itinerary tailored to your interests.

## Ritz Kids

With an abundance of kid-friendly museums and attractions, walkable neighborhoods and a rich culinary scene, Atlanta is the ideal destination for a family vacation. The Ritz-Carlton, Atlanta welcomes young guests with a Ritz Kids program designed to channel children’s natural curiosity into experiences. Children are “Very Important Kids” (VIK) and our youngest guests receive a warm welcome with a complimentary Ritz Kids activity book, crayons and other special treats. Our luxury hotel near Georgia Aquarium in Atlanta offers a selection of overnight packages for guests seeking family-friendly experiences and unforgettable vacations. Enhance your stay and bring starry outdoor adventures into the comfort of your guest room with our Ritz Kids In-Room Camping experience.

Georgia Aquarium, Ponce City Market, State Farm Arena, The Battery Atlanta, Piedmont Park and more are nearby The Ritz-Carlton, Atlanta.
