# Experience Our Downtown Atlanta Hotel's Club Lounge​

## The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience

The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay. With a dedicated Concierge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences, our downtown Atlanta hotel's Club Lounge​ immerses you in the best of the destination.

### Upgrade Your Stay to Club

Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite at our downtown Atlanta hotel with Club Lounge access, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.

A Private Retreat

## Club Lounge Access

We invite you to The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge in Atlanta to experience luxury redefined on the 24th floor. Enjoy captivating southern sunsets in a sophisticated and relaxed lounge setting, while exploring elevated cuisine designed by Executive Chef Quinn Bullard and his talented team. The dedicated Club Concierge Team at our downtown Atlanta hotel's Club Lounge is ready to assist with recommendations, reservations and personalized itineraries to ensure your memorable stay.

At Your Service

## Dedicated Concierge

From dining reservations to excursion recommendations, the dedicated Club Concierge team at our luxury Atlanta hotel with Club Lounge elevates your experience, catering to you personally to ensure your stay is exceptional. The team is available for your individualized needs.

In Good Taste

## Daily Culinary Presentations

Our downtown Atlanta hotel's Club Lounge offers beverage service for your refreshment, including craft beer, fine wine, and specialty cocktails. Enjoy continuous culinary offerings throughout the day in our hotel lounge in Atlanta including breakfast, lunch, afternoon snacks, hors d'oeuvres and desserts. Our talented chefs dazzle with innovation, as they incorporate locally and globally-inspired artistic creations with elevated flavor profiles.

For Your Convenience

## In-Room Amenities

A Club stay ensures that additional amenities are available to you in your luxurious room or suite. Our complimentary shoeshine and pressing of 2 garments delivered to your room will ensure you’re ready for your day of meetings or a night on the town. Retreat to serenity and refinement amidst the downtown bustle. The Luxury Suites provide a spacious layout for a luxurious residential experience including elegant marble bathrooms outfitted with thoughtful amenities, kitchenettes, personal studies and formal dining and living rooms overlooking stunning panoramic views of Atlanta.
