### Resort Fee

La Bahia Hotel & Spa charges a nightly resort fee of $50.00 which includes a welcome beverage upon check-in, Guestrooms are offering Nespresso Vertuo coffee, assorted teas, complimentary wine, variety of beverages and local snacks, bottled water, unlimited Wi-Fi access in guestrooms and public areas, nightly turndown, 24-hour fitness room access, complimentary beach cruiser bicycles and Tommy Bahama beach chairs based on availability. E-bikes are also available for rental. This resort fee is taxable at 12% and is not in itself a tax. The total resort fee inclusive of tax is $56.00. Resort fee is a standard charge and subject to change.

### Valet Parking

A $55.00 nightly parking fee applies. A $10 surcharge applies for any oversized vehicles. Parking is taxable at 10% and is not in itself a tax. Parking fee is a standard charge and subject to change.

### Spa Services

- The Spa at La Bahia has a total of four (4) indoor and (4) outdoor treatment rooms and is open daily from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- The spa is equipped with his/hers steam room and outdoor sauna
- A credit card is required for all appointments. Treatment times are reserved especially for you. We kindly request that cancellations or changes to appointments be made at least 24 hours in advance for single services and 48 hours in advance for multiple treatments. A 50% charge will be applied to your credit card for failure to follow this policy. No show appointments will be charged in full.
- Please note that a mandatory 20% service charge will be added for all spa services. Service charges are based on the full non-discounted price of each service. 18% of the service charge is given to your treatment technician, while 2% is divided among the support team. Additional gratuities are optional and left to your discretion. Additional gratuities are retained in full by the spa team member specified by you.
- To ensure a calm and relaxing environment, audible cell phone use is not permitted. Spa experiences are sacred, we ask that you please respect our quiet atmosphere. Smoking is not permitted.
- Spa guests must be 18 years or older to enjoy our services and facilities.
