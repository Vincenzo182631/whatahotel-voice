## Classic Bar Nights

The hotel bar of a grand hotel is a place with a very special flair. This is where life's stories have always been written, romances experienced and business deals concluded. In addition to classic drinks and selected dishes, the right music, which gives the bar the right atmosphere, is an absolute must. On all Thursdays until May 8 and after the summer break from September 18, different artists play atmospheric background music and invite you to spend a relaxing evening. We serve freshly shucked oysters and first-class champagne. Advance booking is strongly recommended.

### 7-Paintings

7 Paintings is a unique dinner show that fuses art, fine dining, and technology into an unforgettable experience. During a 7-course meal, guests journey through iconic masterpieces, guided by Leonardo da Vinci and the Mona Lisa. The paintings come to life in spectacular ways. Book directly with the organizer via our gift shop on this page.

### Candlelight Concerts

Our impressive historic Richard Wagner Hall awaits you, full of candles and the music of your favorite artists - classically interpreted and performed live! There are two performances per event day. First performance - admission: 18:00, start: 18:30. Second performance - admission: 20:00, start: 20:30. Booking directly through the organizer via our gift shop on this page.

### Candlelight Dinner

Immerse yourself in the world of love and treat yourself to an unforgettable Sunday evening by candlelight. Enjoy a sophisticated 4-course menu, accompanied by a sparkling glass of champagne as an aperitif. Bookable for evenings for two. Every Sunday evening outside of events and during the summer break from July 1 to September 8. Booking at least 48 hours in advance. EUR 72 per person
