## Leisure Activities in Nuremberg

Nuremberg and its surrounding area offer a wide range of attractions and activities near our hotel. Nuremberg Castle and the Franconian Lake District are ideal places for your leisure pursuits. Whether it's shopping in Nuremburg, a visit to the zoo, a climbing or hiking excursion or a visit to the nearby Christkindlesmarkt, which is certainly the most famous Christmas market in the country - you'll be delighted with the range of leisure activities on offer in and around Nuremburg! Visit our city hotel in Nuremberg to experience the city at its best!

Things to Do in Nuremberg

##### Hiking, Climbing or Mountain Biking?

Franconian Switzerland is located in the middle of the tri-city area of Bayreuth, Bamberg and Nuremburg. In the idyllic natural landscape at Nuremburg's doorstep you can go hiking or climbing, play golf, go mountain biking or go bouldering. Visit nearby Franconian Switzerland during your stay at the hotel. It's well worth it!

##### Sailing, Surfing or Swimming fun?

The Altmühlsee, Brombachsee, Rothsee, Dennenloher See and Hahnenkammsee lakes are 30 km from Nuremburg and form the Franconian Lake District. In addition to sailing, surfing or enjoying the lakes' bathing spots in summer there is even more to do here. There are also well-maintained biking and hiking trails.

##### For Young and Old Alike

Located near the hotel, the zoo doesn't just appeal to children, it also attracts adults with its abundance of attractions. At Nuremburg Zoo, where Flocke (""Flake"") the polar bear was born, you can marvel at the dolphins in Southern Germany's only dolphinarium and of course pay a visit to the offspring of the numerous animal families.

##### Activities with Children

With its indoor and outdoor play areas, the Playmobil FunPark in Zirndorf offers diverse leisure activities for young children. Here, on the 90,000 square metres of space, the concept of 'playing, moving and experiencing' can be fully put into action. All the young discovers' senses are stimulated at this park. Visitors can entertain themselves at the amusement park's various colossal Playmobil worlds and marvel at all the attractions, even if the weather leaves a little to be desired.

##### Neues Museum Nuremberg

Start your journey of discovery through the international contemporary art scene just a few minutes’ walk away from Le Méridien Grand Art Hotel Nuremberg. The complex of the New Museum itself is an impressive testimony of the most modern architecture, which integrates harmonically into the historic cityscape with its clear shapes and transparent elements.

Inside the building you will be astonished by 1,500 works of the Collection of Art. It focusses on ensembles of the artists Guenter Fruhtrunk, Horst Antes, Gotthard Graubner, François Morellet, Bridget Riley, Armando, Nam June Paik and Thomas Ruff.

##### New Perspectives

Observable are various forms of artistic expression such as painting, plastics, photography, video arts and installations.

Enthusiasts of modern design will be excited about the Collection of Design. The boundaries between both collections are fluid. The fact that there is no separation in terms of space or content opens up new perspectives to the visitor.

You will find the New Museum of Art near the hotel in the historic Nuremberg city center's Klarissenplatz.

River cruises on the Main-Danube Canal departing from Nuremberg

The port of Nuremberg lies directly by the Main-Danube Canal, making it a popular starting point and base for river cruises along the Main-Danube Canal, via the River Danube and out as far as the Black Sea. After setting off from Nuremberg, the cruise passes through some of Europe’s most beautiful cities. Enjoy the comfort that a river cruise offers, and discover some of Europe’s most attractive cities on the way. The river cruise port of Nuremberg is located near our hotel and is easy to reach in 15-20 minutes by taxi or via the hotel limousine service.

Local Area & Activity Highlights

Beer Tradition

Excellent beer is also brewed here. There are more than 270 breweries in the vicinity of Nuremberg. The city is known for its famous red beer. The brew is served in rustic inns and goes incredibly well with the most well-known dish of the region, the Nuremberg Bratwurst, which is typically enjoyed with sauerkraut.

Landscape

Nuremberg is nestled in a truly inviting landscape. The city is surrounded by rolling hills. The Franconian Switzerland, a low mountain range whose mountains reach a height of up to 600 m, spans to the north of Nuremberg. There thrives the franconian grapevine, which is later sold all over Germany.

Franconian Metropolis

Today, Nuremberg is a lively city with a unique historic flair due to its almost 1.000-year-old history. Shopping fans will find a wide array of shops and boutiques, and not only in the Old Town. Everything can be conveniently reached on foot from the train station, above all, the world-famous Christmas market, which attracts and entertains thousands of tourists every year.
