## Accommodations

Le Méridien Grand Hotel Nuremberg combines timeless elegance with modern design. Our 190 hotel rooms feature luxurious Art Nouveau-inspired marble bathrooms, contemporary artworks, and thoughtful details...See More that blend classic style with modern comfort. Each hotel room in Nuremberg is equipped with a flat screen television, mobile charging with multiple USB slots, and high-speed wireless Internet access. The highlight of every room is the signature Le Méridien bed, ensuring an exceptional night’s rest. Located just a few minutes from Nuremberg's Old Town and with easy access to the underground right outside the door, our hotel offers convenient Nuremberg accommodation for both business and leisure. Whether you’re here for a trade fair, cultural exploration, or a relaxing getaway, Le Méridien Grand Hotel Nuremberg provides the perfect setting. The largest occupancy room at Le Méridien Grand Hotel Nuremberg is the Executive Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.
