Amenities
Rooms
186
Overlooking the town’s medieval center, this 1885 residence was renovated with Art Deco interiors but still offers the ambience of a "classic old hotel that exudes elegance." Blending classic and modern style, "very comforting rooms" provide views of the courtyard, historic train station, or Old Quarter, and have Art Nouveau marble baths and iPod docks. "The hotel staff were exceptionally accommodating." Restaurant Brasserie serves regional specialties; Atelier Bar is the spot to enjoy a cocktail.
