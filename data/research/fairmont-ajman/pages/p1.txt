## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Ajman located?

Fairmont Ajman is located at Sheikh Humaid Bin Rashid Al Nuaimi Street, Al Nakhil 2, Ajman. Our 5-star hotel is only a short drive away from the Sharjah International Airport.

Does Fairmont Ajman have a spa?

Yes, our luxury Ajman hotel offers a spa with 5-star wellness facilities.

Does Fairmont Ajman have a swimming pool?

Yes, Fairmont Ajman has a swimming pool that allows guests to keep up with their workout routines during their holidays or to enjoy a relaxing pool day in the city.

Does Fairmont Ajman have a gym/fitness center?

Yes. Our hotel offers a state-of-the-art gym with modern equipment and fitness facilities for guests to work out during their 5-star stay at Fairmont Ajman.

Is parking available at Fairmont Ajman?

Yes, parking is available at our hotel, making it easy to reach by car or taxi from the airport.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Ajman?

Check in is at 3:00 pm and check out is at 12:00 pm.
