### Frequently Asked Questions

Useful details you need to know before you travel to One&Only Portonovi.

#### How can I book transfers to One&Only Portonovi?

One&Only Portonovi is conveniently accessible from several international airports, including Tivat (approximately 22 km), Dubrovnik (approximately 30 km), Podgorica (approximately 105 km), Split (approximately 230 km), and Tirana (approximately 200 km). All airports are served by a variety of airlines and are well equipped to accommodate private jets.

Please note that travel times vary depending on the season, border crossings, ferry operations, and traffic conditions.

For more information about airport transfers or to arrange transportation, please email reservations@oneandonlyportonovi.com.

#### What are the passport and visa requirements for visiting Montenegro?

All visitors to Montenegro must present a passport valid for at least 6 months from date of travel. Visa requirements for entering Montenegro vary significantly from country to country. Click here for more information.

#### What languages are spoken in Montenegro?

The official language of Montenegro is Montenegrin, although Bosnian, Serbian, Croatian and Albanian are also languages in official use. The resort team members speak English for our guests.

#### What is the weather like in Montenegro?

Montenegro has a Mediterranean climate on the coast, with alpine conditions in the mountains. The average summer temperature is 27°C (81°F) and the sea is warm enough to swim from May to October.

#### What internet services are available at One&Only Portonovi?

Complimentary high-speed WiFi service is available throughout the resort.

#### What is the local currency in Montenegro?

The Euro is the official currency of Montenegro.

#### What is the time zone in Montenegro?

Montenegro is GMT/UTC +1h during Standard Time, and GMT/UTC + 2h during Daylight Saving Time.

#### What is the electrical voltage at One&Only Portonovi?

Electricity is supplied at 220 volts. Power outlet is Type F (Schuko). There are USB sockets located on the desk and near the bed.

#### What are the check-in and check-out times at One&Only Portonovi?

Check in is at 3pm local time and check out is at 12pm local time.

#### What is the Smoking Policy in at One&Only Portonovi?

Smoking, including electronic cigarettes and vaping devices, is permitted only in designated outdoor smoking areas. Smoking is not permitted in any indoor areas and is allowed only on open terraces and in exterior spaces.

#### What is the Pet Policy in at One&Only Portonovi?

Pets are not permitted in indoor public areas, including restaurants, spa and pool areas. Dogs up to 12kg are welcome in designated outdoor areas and must be kept on a leash or in a crate at all times. A supplement of EUR 55 per dog per night applies year-round. Certified service dogs are exempt from these restrictions and fees, provided valid service dog certification is presented upon arrival.
