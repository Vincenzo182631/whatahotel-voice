### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The St. Regis London have a work space?

No, the rooms at The St. Regis London do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The St. Regis London?

The largest occupancy room at The St. Regis London is the Penthouse Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The St. Regis London?

Some rooms at The St. Regis London permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis London have fridges?

No, rooms at The St. Regis London do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis London have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis London have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis London have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis London have hair dryers.
