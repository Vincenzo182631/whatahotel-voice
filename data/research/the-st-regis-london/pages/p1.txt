# Welcome to The St. Regis London

## Conduit of Cultural Glamour

The St. Regis London stands as Mayfair's gathering place for art, fashion and influence, at the junction of Conduit Street and Bond Street. A house of celebration, the hotel is fashioned for culture, set amongst London's designer boutiques, leading art galleries and extensive dining and social hubs.The spirit of St. Regis is reflected in the hotel’s modern glamour, featuring 193 guest rooms, including...See More 66 suites, along with its signature venues: Le Perroquet by Daniel Rose; and The Regis inspired bar with live jazz and masterful mixology, soon to debut. Beyond its social heart, the destination offers two indulgent spa suites, a fully equipped gym and a calendar of personalised rituals: from the signature St. Regis Butler Service to Champagne Sabering and Midnight Suppers, each inspired by the storied Astor legacy and the enduring spirit of Caroline Astor herself.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis London?

The check-in time at The St. Regis London is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis London allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis London. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of £150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis London?

On-site valet parking is available at The St. Regis London. The daily valet fee is £85.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis London?

The closest airport near The St. Regis London is London City Airport (LCY). LCY is located approximately 10.4 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis London have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis London does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
