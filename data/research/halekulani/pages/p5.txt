# Premier Suites

Discover the individual character of our six Premier Suites overlooking Waikiki Beach, each with distinctive interior design, spacious accommodations and views of the Pacific Ocean and Diamond Head. Privacy, sophisticated design and meticulous attention to detail are standard in these unique lodgings.

It is our pleasure to serve you with luxury amenities to ease your journey, including a dedicated personal butler, in-room guest check-in and complimentary roundtrip airport SUV transfers.

Aligned with our new Art of Wellbeing program, Premier Suite guests can enjoy an exclusive opportunity to reserve a personalized spa experience (massage, reflexology, shiatsu, acupuncture, or personal training session) in the privacy of their suite.

#### New Premier Suites

Halekulani unveils two stunning Premier Suites: The Penthouse and The Bungalow. Thoughtfully designed for discerning travelers, each suite features bespoke details, curated artwork, and breathtaking views, paired with our signature impeccable service.

#### Royal Suite

The largest of our Premier Suite accommodations, the Royal Suite offers guests a regal experience, with the utmost attention to privacy and comfort. Hand-selected, unique and rare furnishings and accessories complement the refined style of the rooms.

#### Halekulani Suite

Designed by Vera Wang, the Halekulani Suite embodies style and sophistication. The Halekulani Suite features a private entrance foyer, covered wrap-around balcony, master bedroom suite with spa-styled bathroom, and a spacious living and dining room.

#### Orchid Suite

Located on the original site of the Lewers family residence, the Orchid Suite offers floor-to-ceiling windows that open onto a private lawn, landscaped to maintain privacy, in this unique ground-floor suite with views of Diamond Head.

#### Executive Luxury Suite

Designed with comfort and luxury in mind, this one-bedroom suite offers privacy and unparalleled service. Enter the second-floor residential-styled accommodations via private entrance foyer. A separate living room and an office offer ample space to entertain and tend to work.
