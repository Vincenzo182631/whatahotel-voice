# House Without A Key

See House Without A Key 360° See House Without A Key Video

One of the most iconic restaurants in Waikiki, House Without A Key is Halekulani’s popular indoor/outdoor gathering spot for sunset cocktails, appetizers, and Hawaiian entertainment.  Drink in memorable sunsets under our 130+ year old Kiawe Tree with views of the Pacific Ocean and Diamond Head.  This setting is the perfect backdrop to preserve the honored tradition of cocktails and Hawaiian music under the stars and by the sea.

Immortalized in a 1925 Charlie Chan novel, House Without A Key is known for its signature mai tai, a classic to be enjoyed any time of the day. We invite you to try this Halekulani classic at home.

Our iconic poolside restaurant, known for its sunset cocktails and live Hawaiian music, will now be open a little later.  Dinner service starts at 5:00 pm with the last seating at 8:30 pm.

House Without A Key Chef Jarrin Otake has created various choices for breakfast, lunch, and dinner using different grilling techniques, brick oven flat breads, as well as local favorites such as fresh catch of the day, steaks, and burgers.  We suggest one of our select wines by the glass carefully curated for the restaurant to enhance your dining experience.

**Entertainment**

With entertainment starting at 6:30 pm, you don’t have to rush to catch the sunset…or the green flash.  Our lovely hula dancer continues to make her appearances from 6:30 – 8:30 pm. Cheers!

Sunset Cocktails in the lawn area at House Without A Key is on a first come, first served basis.  Reservations are not accepted for our daily Hula show and Sunset Cocktails.

JARRIN OTAKE, CHEF DE CUISINE – HOUSE WITHOUT A KEY

## Bringing Hawaii style to cuisine

House Without A Key Chef, Jarrin Otake, is excited to share his creative spin on local favorites with our guests.

A winner of the Hawaii / Pacific winner of the Jeunes Commis Culinary Competition for Le Chaine de Rotisseurs, Otake brings his local knowledge and strong cultural understanding to the hotel’s fast-paced dining venue to promote Hawaii Regional Cuisine on the highest level.

Some of his favorite ingredients are fresh garlic, ginger, chili peppers, reef fish, and…BACON.

#### _“We came from Maui for a 3-day getaway. As world travelers we can honestly say that the Halekulani is one of the most wonderful hotels we have ever stayed in! The location is amazing and of course the food but most of all… it is the warmth and professionalism of the staff. House Without A Key is one of our favorite places… not just in Hawaii but in the world. It feels like you are stepping back into old Hawaii with the tropical breezes and gentle music. We can’t wait to come back!”_

###### _Cathy Detrick_
