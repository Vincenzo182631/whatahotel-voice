# Guestrooms

Settle in and enjoy the restful atmosphere created by the natural, neutral tones of our “Seven Shades of White” signature décor. From poolside convenience to sweeping, high-floor ocean views, our rooms are designed with your comfort in mind.

Each of our spacious guest rooms (ranging from 419 to 485 square feet / 39 to 45 square meters), feature a private balcony, deep soaking tub with separate glass shower, Toto washlet, SpaHalekulani bath amenities, a new beverage center with Nespresso coffee maker, kettle, mini refrigerator and WiFi, all with no resort fees.

#### Diamond Head Oceanfront Prime

Higher-floor rooms offering sweeping views of landmark Diamond Head, Waikiki Beach and the Pacific Ocean. Amenities include complimentary Wi-Fi, deep soaking tub, business center and fitness room access, and turndown service, all with no resort fees.
