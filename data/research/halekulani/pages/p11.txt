HALEKULANI PASTRY TEAM

## Creating edible artistry

Ever wonder where Halekulani’s signature coconut cake and the delectable popovers are made?

The unassuming, but extremely talented pastry team works behind the scenes to create signature breads, pastries, and desserts for all of Halekulani’s restaurants including La Mer, Orchids, House Without A Key, Veranda, Lewers Lounge, as well as Halekulani Bakery located at Halepuna Waikiki.
