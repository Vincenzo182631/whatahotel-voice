EVENING DINING

# Terns

A PLACE TO GATHER, SHARE & RETURN

By evening, the Halekulani Bakery terrace transforms into TERNS, an intimate open-air gathering place at Halepuna Waikiki. Thoughtfully crafted shared plates, inventive cocktails and warm hospitality come together in a relaxed setting designed for conversation, connection and discovery.

Inspired by the Hawaiian White Tern, a symbol of return and belonging, TERNS invites guests and locals alike to gather, linger and savor flavors influenced by culinary traditions from around the world.

## Gather, Share & Discover

TERNS celebrates the pleasure of sharing a table. Chef Vikram Garg brings together influences from across cultures in a menu of thoughtfully composed plates created to be enjoyed together, complemented by handcrafted cocktails and wine.

Seasonal ingredients and global inspiration shape an experience that feels both considered and effortless, encouraging guests to explore new flavors while settling in for an unhurried evening.
