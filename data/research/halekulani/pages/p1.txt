One of the first and finest hotels in Hawaii, Halekulani has welcomed guests to these tranquil beachfront grounds for more than a century. We believe in anticipating your needs and making sure you want for nothing. Top-tier service, award-winning cuisine, unique amenities and no resort fee surprises, are all here for one reason: You. We look forward to welcoming you to our House Befitting Heaven.

UNIQUELY SUITED FOR YOU

### Premier Suites

Discover the individual character of our six Premier Suites overlooking Waikiki Beach, each with distinctive interior design, spacious accommodations and views of the Pacific Ocean and Diamond Head. Privacy, sophisticated design and meticulous attention to detail are standard in these unique lodgings.

See Premier Suites See All Rooms & Suites

UNDERSTATED ELEGANCE

### Suites

Enjoy the extra space in our expansive suites. Junior Suites offer dedicated sitting areas and two private balconies. One- and Two-Bedroom Suites are designed with separate areas for sleeping, dining and entertaining, allowing you ample room to relax, socialize and—if you must—work.

All suites feature extra-large bathrooms with deep-soaking tub and a separate powder room in full-size suites, a walk-in closet, at least one private balcony and complimentary Wi-Fi. Select suites offer extra-large balconies or third bedroom options.

See Suites See All Rooms & Suites

SPACIOUS, SERENE AND SOOTHING

### Guestrooms

Settle in and enjoy the restful atmosphere created by the natural, neutral tones of our “Seven Shades of White” signature décor. From poolside convenience to sweeping, high-floor ocean views, our rooms are designed with your comfort in mind.

See Guestrooms See All Rooms & Suites

ADA-COMPLIANT ACCOMMODATIONS

### Accessible Rooms

At Halekulani, we are committed to providing equal access and opportunities for individuals of all abilities. Allow us to make your stay effortless with our range of ADA-accessible accommodations from mobile impaired rooms to audible impaired rooms.

See Accessible Rooms See All Rooms & Suites

REOPENING & RENEWAL

### A Continued Legacy of Excellence

A commitment to providing unparalleled quality and service underlies every aspect of Halekulani’s restoration, including the hotel’s physical infrastructure and grounds, guest accommodations, and House Without a Key - our beloved outdoor gathering place. We hope you'll enjoy a renewed Halekulani experience.

Read More

LEGACY TOUR

### Discover the Real Halekulani

Halekulani’s historian takes you on a tour that reveals the rich past and the traditions of the property. History, architecture, the story of the kiawe tree, the famous coconut cake, the healing power of the area – all will be revealed during this informational tour through the property.

Contact Us for Tour Information

COMPLIMENTARY ACCESS TO THE FINEST

### For You, Everything

As a Halekulani guest, we invite you to enjoy complimentary access to some of the island’s finest arts and cultural events. Enjoy the wonders of Bishop Museum, Shangri La, the Hawaii Symphony Orchestra, Honolulu Museum of Art, Iolani Palace, Manoa Heritage Center and more.

Enjoy Arts & Culture Experiences

HALEKULANI LIVING TV

### \#HawaiiFromHome

From our home to yours. Halekulani Living TV brings stories from the Islands to you, wherever you are. Enjoy #HawaiiFromHome as each season connects you to the vibrant arts and culture of Hawaii and celebrates the artists, creators and curators who embody the art of living well.
