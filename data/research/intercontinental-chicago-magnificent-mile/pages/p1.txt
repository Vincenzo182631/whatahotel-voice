### The best steaks in Chicago are under our roof

Serving high-quality, elevated cuisine worthy of its namesake, Michael Jordan’s Steak House is widely recognized for its exceptional prime, dry-aged, hand-cut beef dishes. Signature chicken and fish options are also on the menu of this downtown Chicago Steakhouse located in InterContinental Chicago Magnificent Mile.

Learn more

### A family-friendly escape in the heart of Chicago

Downtown Chicago’s most beautiful indoor pool offers a family-friendly retreat in the heart of the Windy City. Swim 14 stories above the Magnificent Mile in our historic indoor Jr. Olympic size swimming pool, featuring its original 1920s Spanish tiles and a terra cotta Neptune fountain deliver a stunning backdrop for family fun.

### Our culture-rich history

Our hotel was originally built in 1929 as the men’s Medinah Athletic Club, during which time Olympic gold medalist and Tarzan actor, Johnny Weissmuller, used the swimming pool to train. The building became a hotel in 1944, and opened as InterContinental Chicago Magnificent Mile in March 1990.

Does InterContinental Chicago Magnificent Mile have a pool?

InterContinental Chicago Magnificent Mile has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

Featuring an indoor junior Olympic-size swimming pool, this is the perfect place to swim invigorating laps, splash around with your family or relax in a chair poolside. Built in 1929, the pool area and upper gym studio is not wheelchair accessible.
