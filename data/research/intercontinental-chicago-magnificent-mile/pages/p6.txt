### Poor Service and Management

2 days ago

Extremely Disappointing Experience at InterContinental Chicago — Poor Service and Management

I recently stayed at the InterContinental Chicago Magnificent Mile while attending a conference, and unfortunately, this was one of the most disappointing hotel experiences I have had, particularly from a luxury hotel carrying the InterContinental name.

I booked two rooms at a total cost of approximately $8,000, expecting the level of hospitality, professionalism, and attention to detail associated with a premium hotel.

Unfortunately, the problems began almost immediately.

Upon check-in, one of our rooms was not properly cleaned. Later that evening, the electronic key to the second room stopped working. I had to make four separate trips to the front desk before maintenance was finally contacted.

Maintenance took approximately 45 minutes to arrive, and even then, the technician could not open the door. He had to leave to retrieve additional equipment to access the electronic locking mechanism, eventually disassembling it to replace the batteries.

The following day, things became even worse.

While I was out at dinner, my attorney arrived to collect a key to our second room, only for us to discover that the hotel had checked us out of that room without notifying us or making any attempt to confirm our reservation.

When I questioned the front desk, I was confidently told that I had booked only one night and that the hotel was sold out.

Rather than investigating the reservation history and acknowledging the possibility of an internal error, the initial response was dismissive.

What disappointed me most, however, was the response from Diego, the manager who was supposed to help resolve the situation. Instead of beginning with a sincere apology and taking responsibility for what had happened, he offered me a complimentary breakfast.

With all due respect, someone spending approximately $8,000 on two hotel rooms is not looking for a $20 breakfast. I was looking for professionalism, accountability, and basic courtesy.

A simple acknowledgment — "We made a mistake, and we sincerely apologize" — would have gone a long way.

I have approximately 700,000 IHG One Rewards points, reflecting my long-standing relationship with the brand. Unfortunately, this experience has made me seriously reconsider whether I want to continue staying at IHG properties.

Luxury hospitality is not simply about beautiful buildings, expensive rooms, or a prestigious name. It is about how guests are treated, especially when something goes wrong.

I hope InterContinental and IHG corporate leadership take this feedback seriously and address the customer service and management issues at this property.

\*\*Very disappointing, and certainly not the experience I expected from InterContinental.\*\*

### Historic Grandeur, Unresolved Security Concerns

12 days ago

Intercontinental Chicago is an architecturally remarkable property. Its Moorish-inspired ornamentation, intricate columns, murals, elaborate ceilings, and historic furnishings create an atmosphere of old-world grandeur. I genuinely admired the craftsmanship and documented many beautiful details.

There were memorable moments of hospitality. The steak delivered to my room was exceptional, and the gentleman who presented it demonstrated poise and thoughtful attention to detail. I also appreciated the pleasant snack-shop attendant and the engineer's prompt technical assistance.

Unfortunately, the overall guest experience did not consistently reflect the property's prestigious presentation.

I booked directly and called twice to confirm my arrival following a red-eye flight from California. Despite assurances that my late arrival had been documented, the front desk questioned my check-in in a manner I found unnecessarily confrontational, and I noticed I was being treated differently than other guests.

Across two rooms, I encountered inconsistent maintenance and comfort. The first bathroom's shower allowed water to escape into the sink area, and the bathtub appeared dated and stained. The second room was quieter and cleanre, but a window-shade system was inoperable for one of two shades. Dim lighting made ordinary grooming difficult, while the bathroom amenities felt limited and questionable in origin, despite the quality of the towels.

My most serious concern occurred on October 10. Returning from shopping, I discovered my room's door handle conspicuously loose and belongings, including my wallet, apparently disturbed. Engineering repaired the handle, and security examined electronic access information. However, their explanations conflicted, leaving significant questions about room access and privacy unanswered. I have photographs and recordings documenting my observations, the subsequent discussions, and the engineers' take on the door versus security's version: contradictory.

Feeling insufficiently reassured, I checked out that evening, despite arriving around 7 a.m. The disruption affected my professional commitments and resulted in additional travel expenses. It also left me considerably distressed, with effects on my normal sleep at home, often on alert.

I recognize the employees who demonstrated genuine professionalism. Nevertheless, individual courtesy cannot substitute for consistent institutional accountability.

I request a documented investigation, preservation and review of relevant records, and a substantive management response.

The property's historic beauty deserves recognition, but luxury must extend beyond architecture and presentation to include functional comfort, transparent communication, guest dignity, and confidence in personal security.

Until these concerns are meaningfully addressed, I cannot recommend the property based on my experience.
