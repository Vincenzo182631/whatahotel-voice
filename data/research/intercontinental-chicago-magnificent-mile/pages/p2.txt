# InterContinental Chicago Magnificent MileRooms & suites

## Luxury Rooms & Suites on Chicago’s Magnificent Mile

Located along Michigan Avenue in the heart of downtown, the rooms and suites at InterContinental Chicago Magnificent Mile reflect the grandeur of a historic landmark paired with contemporary comfort. Set within a storied building renowned for its architectural legacy, each accommodation offers generous space, refined finishes, and views of the city skyline. Whether visiting for business, leisure, or a special occasion, guests enjoy a sophisticated stay rooted in timeless Chicago elegance.

### Timeless Design, Contemporary Comfort

Designed to honor the hotel’s historic character while meeting the expectations of today’s global traveler, every room and suite offers a composed and welcoming retreat above the city.

- Spacious layouts with comfortable seating and work areas
- Elegant interiors inspired by classic architectural details
- Select rooms and suites featuring city or river views

- Marble bathrooms with premium bath amenities
- High‑speed Wi‑Fi and in‑room dining availabl

Each detail reflects InterContinental’s commitment to enduring comfort, discretion, and elevated service.

#### Classic & Premium Rooms

Classic and Premium Rooms offer generous space and a refined setting above the Magnificent Mile. Thoughtfully designed with timeless finishes and modern conveniences, these rooms provide a comfortable and quiet retreat in the center of downtown Chicago.

#### Suites

The suites at InterContinental Chicago Magnificent Mile present an elevated residential experience defined by scale, privacy, and sophistication. From Junior Suites to the iconic Royal and Presidential Suites, each offers expansive living areas ideal for hosting, extended stays, or occasions that call for additional space and distinction.
