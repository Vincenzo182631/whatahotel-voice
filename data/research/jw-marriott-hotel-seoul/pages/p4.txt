## Finest and Freshest Ingredients

Tamayura introduces a variety of high-end Teppanyaki menus for dining with premium seasonal ingredients sourced both locally and from around the world. Every dish at this Japanese restaurant in Gangnam, Seoul is made from premium ingredients in a manner that highlights the essence of each flavor to exceed the expectations of every guest.

### Private Events in Secret Garden

Two glass house style private dining rooms with natural sunlight are available for special events upon request. Please contact us by phone for details and availability.

### Locations and Capacity

Located on the second floor of the hotel, Tamayura features seven private dining rooms with tables for eight, six, four, and two. Our teppanyaki station and sushi counter each offer seats for eight, while the tea bar in Gangnam, Seoul​ offers seats for six.

### Exceptional Grill Dishes

Indulge in the trendy and creative Gangnam, Seoul dining experience in our signature grill restaurant. The Margaux Grill offers an array of fresh seafood and premium beef cooked on its bespoke wood fire by our grill masters.

### An Extensive Wine Cellar

A haute cuisine matched by rare and exquisite wine selections handpicked by the hotel's Director of Wine will tantalize each palate.

## A Diverse Space of Gourmet Experience

Flavors is a diverse space of gourmet experience where guests converse with chefs at live cooking stations and dedicated servers guide diners at each table on bespoke journeys through the culinary highlights of this restaurant in Seoul. Servers offer indispensable tips on how to enjoy each dish at this buffet restaurant in Gangnam, Seoul in the most enjoyable order, and the best combinations of flavors and textures. Meanwhile, tips and explanations from master sommeliers paint a picture of unforgettable pairings of traditional liquors and Korean wines with Flavors’ diverse cuisine, accompanied by in-depth descriptions of each beverage.

## Location and Capacity

Flavors is located on the second floor of JW Marriott Hotel Seoul. Total of 218 seats including a private dining room for ten and a semi-private dining for fourteen.
