## Celebrate the Magic of the Season at JW Marriott Hotel Seoul

Indulge in exceptional seasonal experiences at JW Marriott Hotel Seoul. Enjoy an abundant selection of seasonal desserts, premium dining, and refreshing escapes, designed to bring you and your loved ones the season’s finest moments.

Seasonal Getaway

International guests (non-Korean nationals) staying at JW Marriott Hotel Seoul until December 31, 2026, can enjoy exclusive shopping and dining benefits at Shinsegae Department Store Gangnam, including complimentary drinks and discount vouchers. Further details will be provided at check-in.
