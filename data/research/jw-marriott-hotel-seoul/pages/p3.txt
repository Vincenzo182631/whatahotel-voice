## Executive Lounge

Enjoy an exclusive retreat of luxury and serenity, reserved for the guests staying in the executive lounge access rooms and suites. A personal express check-in and check-out, food & beverage and business services are provided. Dining offerings include well-being breakfast, evening hors d’oeuvres, evening cocktails, cordials & desserts.

### Executive Lounge Access Rooms & Suites

Experience elevated luxury reserved for the guests staying in the executive lounge access rooms and suites. Guests staying in the rooms with the designated benefits can enjoy complimentary access to Marquis fitness club, indoor swimming pool and sauna. Explore our variety of relaxation facilities catered for your genuine rest, relaxation and replenishment.

## Elegant and Expansive Private Lounge

Our expansive private lounge with elegant and modern interior notes is the perfect place to relax, socialize or meet for business. Our lounge located on the 9th floor takes up the whole floor accommodating up to 155 people. Hours of Operation: Everyday 6:30 AM – 11:30 PM

## Business Center

Stay connected at our business center with a full range of essential services to guests who need to keep up with business during the stay. Modern amenities and services including internet-connected desktop PCs, fax and printer, 3 private meeting rooms will promote smooth and efficient business transactions on the global stage.

### Express Check-in and Check-out

Enjoy the benefits of personalized express check-in and check-out service at the executive lounge.

### Complimentary Dining and Beverage Service

Relax and unwind your day with array of light meals and beverage during evening hors d’oeuvres. Get your day started with well-being breakfast served daily. Well-being breakfast: Everyday 6:30 AM- 10:00 AM / Evening Hors D'oeuvres: Everyday 4:30 PM - 9:00 PM \*Evening Hors D’oeuvres can be accessed once per night for 1 hour. Hours of operation are subject to change without notice.

### Essentials for Business

Our Business Center is equipped with desktop computers, printers and fax machines to meet basic business requirements. Take the advantage of complimentary use of meeting room to set up the business meeting and have your suit looking perfectly pressed for a last-minute deal.

Notice From Executive Lounge

'Please note that children and young adults aged up to 18 are allowed at the Executive Lounge until 6:00 pm and children aged up to 12 should be accompanied by an adult.

Charges for additional guests 13 years old or more is KRW 120,000 per person  and 37 months – 12 years old is KRW 50,000 per person (exclusive of service charge and tax). Marriott Bonvoy Platinum Elite or above members can accompany 1 guest at the Executive Lounge.

Please refrain from wearing sportswear, pajamas, bathrobes or in-room slippers.

### Available Relaxation Facilities for Your Perfect Wellness

Follow a full day of work, shopping or sightseeing in Seoul with healthful journey of physical, mental and emotional healing and rejuvenation through true relaxation and replenishment.

## Marquis Fitness Club

An expansive fitness complex offers optimal convenience for a variety of sports and activities. Our Marquis Fitness Club features 350 state-of-the-art professional exercise machines including aerobic and anaerobic exercise equipment. The 85-meter jogging track is a perfect place to work up a sweat and release energy.

## Indoor Swimming Pool

The pool area features a six lane indoor pool, a therapy pool, a children's pool, a scuba diving pool, a whirlpool and cabanas for relaxing.

## JW Garden

Located on the 7th Floor, escape the urban bustle for a moment of calm and sensorial experience amid stunning sunsets, fresh breezes in green tranquility.
