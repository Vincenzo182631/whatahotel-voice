### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Hotel Seoul have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Hotel Seoul offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Body analysis system, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Seoul have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Seoul has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Seoul have an on-site spa?

Yes, The Spa is located on-site at JW Marriott Hotel Seoul.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Hotel Seoul?

Namsan Mountain Park, Changdeokgung Palace & Secret Garden, Deoksugung Palace, Gyeongbokgung Palace, Banpo Hangang Park and more are nearby JW Marriott Hotel Seoul.
