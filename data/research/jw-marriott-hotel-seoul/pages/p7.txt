There's nothing ostentatious or over-the-top about a stay at the JW Marriott Seoul – but travelers who don't mind being a few metro stops from attractions or the city center will find a quality stay. Situated above shopping and the express bus terminal/subway, with a much-vaunted fitness center, high floors provide majestic river views.
