## Accommodations

JW Marriott Hotel Seoul offers a range of luxury hotel accommodation in Gangnam, Seoul, including 376 guest rooms, including 2 penthouses and 32 suites, all featuring stunning city views. Elegantly combining traditional Korean and modern interior notes, guest rooms with expansive bedrooms and bathrooms as well as window-side relaxing sofas are designed to offer the perfect ambiance to achieve true...See More relaxation, help heal and enjoy a sound sleep. The Presidential Penthouse and Ambassador Penthouse located at the highest levels of the hotel are designed as multi-level suites that offer spectacular city and river views. ## Presidential Penthouse

The pinnacle of luxury awaits in our Presidential Penthouse. Spanning 1,937 square feet of space, this two-bedroom duplex hotel suite in Gangnam, Seoul, features an elevator as well as floor-to-ceiling glass windows to soak in picturesque views of Seoul, the Han River and Mt. Namsan.

## Ambassador Penthouse

Take in the scenery from the full glass wall on one side of the Ambassador Penthouse Suite. The Ambassador Penthouse is adorned with artworks by renowned artists. The music room features Bang & Olufsen's Signature speaker 'BeoLab 50' for the highest quality sound, making the Ambassador Penthouse ideal for luxury events and private meetings.

## Business Suite
