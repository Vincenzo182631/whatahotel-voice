Discover classic luxury in the heart of South Korea's Gangnam District at JW Marriott Hotel Seoul. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Seoul?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Seoul is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Seoul allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Seoul.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Seoul?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Seoul. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for ₩35000.00 per hour.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Seoul have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Seoul has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Seoul booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Seoul?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Seoul is Gimpo International Airport (GMP). GMP is located approximately 27.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Seoul have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Seoul has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Seoul have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Seoul has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
