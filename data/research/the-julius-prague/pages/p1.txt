## Dining at The Julius

The Brasserie is where the day begins. Breakfast moves easily into lunch and into evening, shaped by seasonal ingredients, considered preparation, and the same attention that runs through everything here.

The setting is calm and unhurried. Italian-inspired design, warm light, and a drinks list chosen with as much care as the food. It's a good place to be, at any hour.

## Finding Your Way

You’ll find us on Senovážné Square, in the heart of Prague, moments from the city’s historic streets. Václav Havel Airport is around 30 minutes away by cab. The Main Train Station is just a six-minute walk, and Florenc Bus Station can be reached in six minutes by car. However you arrive, the journey here is simple and the welcome, assured.

## **Since 1862**

The Julius comes from the House of Julius Meinl – a Central European heritage built on craftsmanship, quality, and genuine care for people.

Since 1862, that approach has shaped how we think about hospitality: not as a service to be delivered, but as something felt. Today it lives in the spaces we've designed, the details we've chosen, and the way we look after everyone who stays with us.
