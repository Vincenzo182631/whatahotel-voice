The Brasserie is where the day begins and where it ends well. Breakfast moves easily into lunch and into evening, shaped by seasonal ingredients, considered preparation, and a drinks list chosen with the same attention as the food.

The setting is Italian-inspired, calm, and unhurried. A place rooted in the Julius Meinl tradition of hospitality, where quality is felt rather than announced.
