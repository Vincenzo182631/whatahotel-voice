# **Vienna, 1862**

Julius Meinl's story began in 19th-century Vienna, a city defined by culture, craftsmanship, and a particular attention to quality. Our founder introduced a new approach to coffee roasting that set a standard others followed. That standard has guided the House of Julius Meinl ever since.

## An Enduring Standard

Over generations, Julius Meinl became synonymous with a certain kind of quality, one that doesn't announce itself but is immediately felt. The flagship store at Am Graben in Vienna still reflects those values today: a curated selection, presented with care, in the heart of one of Europe's great cities.

The Julius carries the same philosophy forward, expressed through spaces designed with the same attention to detail, balance, and quiet refinement.

## A New Chapter

The Julius is where the heritage of Julius Meinl meets contemporary hospitality. The same principles – quality, attentiveness, genuine care for people – now expressed through city-centre hotels designed for how you actually stay.

Generous space, considered design, and everything in place. Comfort that feels natural rather than declared.

## A Contemporary Way to Stay

At The Julius, hospitality is shaped around you. Thoughtfully designed spaces and integrated services create an environment that works for business or leisure, a single night or an extended stay, without ever feeling like either is an afterthought.
