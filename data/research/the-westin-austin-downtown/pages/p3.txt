# Savor the Flavors of Austin from our Downtown Restaurants

## Dining

Ascend to the 20th floor and take in sweeping skyline views at Azul Rooftop, Austin’s highest rooftop bar, where handcrafted cocktails and shareable bites pair beautifully with golden sunsets. Down in...See More the lobby, discover Skipjack Oyster, our newly opened restaurant that brings a lively coastal spirit to the heart of the city. Savor a fresh raw bar, oysters on the half shell, seafood plateaus, and house-made canned and carbonated cocktails, all inspired by the flavors of the Carolina coast. At The Westin Austin Downtown, every dining experience is thoughtfully designed to nourish, delight, and inspire; just steps from Austin’s best music, nightlife, and culture.See Less
