# Holiday Events at Westin Austin Downtown

## Where City Lights Meet Holiday Delight

The holidays sparkle a little brighter at The Westin Austin Downtown. From festive rooftop celebrations at Yule at Azul to coastal-inspired dining at Skipjack Oyster, every experience is crafted to capture...See More the magic of the season. Whether you’re joining us for a holiday getaway or a spirited night out, discover Austin’s most dazzling downtown destination for winter cheer.See Less

### Yule at Azul Holiday Pop-Up

Celebrate the season 20 floors up at Yule at Azul, Austin’s highest rooftop holiday pop-up. Sip festive cocktails and take in glittering skyline views that make spirits bright. Cozy up in a private cabana or enjoy the lively rooftop scene surrounded by twinkling lights, holiday music, and good company.

### Holiday Happenings at The Westin Austin Downtown

Holidays at Skipjack Oyster

### Thanksgiving Feast

Thanksgiving is better with a full plate. Join us at Skipjack for a festive spread of Thanksgiving favorites including sage-roasted turkey, maple Dijon-glazed ham, cornbread and sage stuffing, sweet potato casserole, mac and cheese, warm pecan bread pudding, and more.

Book Now

### Christmas Dinner

Make Skipjack part of your holiday tradition. Join us for Christmas Eve and Christmas Day brunch, served until 3 p.m., and gather around the table for a festive holiday feast featuring your choice of Black Pepper-Crusted Prime Rib, Orange & Brown Sugar-Glazed Pork Loin, Mushroom & Lentil Shepherd’s Pie and more!

Book Now

### New Year's Eve, Coastal Style

Celebrate the new year with coastal flair and Austin soul. Enjoy fresh seafood, crafted cocktails, and good company as you welcome 2026 at Skipjack Oyster, where every toast feels like the start of something special.

Book Now

Holidays at Azul Rooftop

### Yule at Azul Holiday Pop-Up

Join us at Yule at Azul, November 19–December 30, for a festive rooftop escape above downtown Austin. Sip curated holiday cocktails and mocktails, take in dazzling décor and twinkling lights, and enjoy seasonal specialties that capture the magic of the season, Azul style.

Learn More

### Holiday Cabanas

Make your visit to Yule at Azul extra special with a private cabana experience. Each cabana is decked out in holiday décor and includes plush, private seating, fresh blanket service, and a signature menu of festive cocktails and bites. Gather your friends, sip something seasonal, and celebrate the season in cozy rooftop style.

Book A Cabana

### NYE Rooftop Party

Ring in 2027 with skyline views and sparkling vibes at Azul Rooftop’s New Year’s Eve Party. Enjoy signature cocktails, live DJ beats, and an electric atmosphere high above downtown Austin. As the clock strikes midnight, toast to new beginnings surrounded by glittering lights, good company, and the city’s best rooftop energy.
