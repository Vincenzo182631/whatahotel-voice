366
Opened in July 2015, this 366-room hotel sits squarely in the center of the Live Music Capital of the World, just a few minutes' stroll from three of Austin's major entertainment districts. Both the hotel's decor and its guest offerings riff on the local music scene, and manage to do their part to "Keep Austin Weird." To wit: there's a music concierge on hand to curate Spotify playlists of the best hometown acts; the lobby ceiling is carved up with cutouts to look like a Dobro guitar; and the “Guitar Rockstar” experience gives music-lovers (and talented air guitarists) the chance to take guitar lessons with a local artist and test their skills on stage at one of Austin’s famed music venues in front of a live audience. The hotel is heavy on wellness, too. Not only do they have a Run Concierge on staff, who leads guests on trails around Lady Bird Lake, but they also offer rooftop yoga classes, daily fresh-squeezed juices, and New Balance gear for the borrowing. As for your much-deserved cool-down session? The hotel's (heated) rooftop pool and bar, Azul, is the highest in Austin, offering some of the city's best views of downtown Austin and Lady Bird Lake, so that you can soak in the sights during your dip. Grab a bite at the hotel's Southern-style restaurant, Stella San Jac, where the open sight lines let you keep one eye on the lounge, where local musicians take to the stage, and one on the open kitchen, to track the grill marks (and the bubbling of melted cheddar) on your Stella burger.
