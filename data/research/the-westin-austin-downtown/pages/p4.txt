##### Take in the Best View of Downtown Austin

Azul, located at The Westin Austin Downtown on East 5th Street, is the ideal place to spend sun-drenched days lounging in the pool and escaping the Austin heat, or chilling with friends as this space transforms into a lounge in the evenings. Hotel guests and visitors alike are welcome to enjoy Travel Agent Central's Coolest Hotel Pool.

As the tallest hotel rooftop bar and lounge in Austin, Azul boasts spectacular views across the cityscape with transparent panels surrounding the deck. Our modern-designed deck is sleek and chic and will make you feel right at home as you relax with your crew, lounging by the pool with a drink in hand. When the sun goes down, our fire pits turn on, and Azul transforms from a pool and bar to a chic lounge. Experience why Eater rates us as Austin's #1 best outdoor bar.

**Minor Policy:**

While we do not recommend minors at the pool on the weekends after 12 PM, we do allow it. All minors (under 21) must be accompanied by an adult hotel guest (parent or legal guardian) at all times to access the pool as there is not a lifeguard on duty.

### SXSW®

The South by Southwest® Conferences & Festivals offer the unique convergence of original music, independent films, and emerging technologies. Fostering creative and professional growth alike, SXSW® is the premier destination for discovery.

### Zilker Botanical Garden

Take a leisurely stroll through wildflowers, butterflies, koi ponds, and even a recreated local dinosaur habitat within Zilker Botanical Garden’s 26 acres, often called “the jewel in the heart of Austin.”

### Congress Avenue Bridge Bats

In the spirit of keeping things weird, we should mention that March-November is bat season here in Austin. Watching the largest urban colony of Mexican free-tailed bats in North America emerge from beneath the bridge at dusk is incredible, uniquely Austin, and a must-see event!

### Brew Pubs & Beer Gardens

The best bars in Austin are just around the corner. Visit Easy Tiger, Waller Creek Pub House, The Gingerman or Scholz Garten during your stay to enjoy the weather and experience Austin.

### For Foodies

Check out Austin restaurants that have made the city a foodie's paradise. From Tex-Mex and barbecue to burgers and dogs, we have you covered.

### South Congress

It’s safe to say Austin just wouldn’t be the same without South Congress, and it’s a must-see when in town.  Peppered with shopping, restaurants, bars and events.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Austin Downtown have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Austin Downtown offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton bike, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Austin Downtown have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Austin Downtown has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Austin Downtown?

Lady Bird Lake, State Capitol tours, Double Decker Austin, Antone's Night Club, Paramount Theater and more are nearby The Westin Austin Downtown.
