Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas
Unwind in an inspiring uptown hotel in Dallas, Texas
In the heart of Uptown Dallas, The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas is Texas’s first Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star hotel, where contemporary elegance reflects the spirit of Texas. Moments from Downtown Dallas, guests enjoy effortless access to premier shopping, celebrated dining, and the city’s most compelling cultural experiences. Thoughtfully appointed accommodations balance residential warmth with modern sophistication, offering a serene retreat above the city. At Fearing’s Restaurant, James Beard Award-nominated Chef Dean Fearing’s bold Southwestern cuisine defines Dallas dining, while curated signature experiences invite deeper discovery. A serene luxury spa anchors immersive wellness offerings, with personalized treatments and elevated rituals designed to restore and renew. Discover a luxury hotel in Dallas where personalized service, modern glamour, and authentic Texas charm come together to create an inspiring and unforgettable stay.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas. The daily valet fee is $70.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas is Dallas Love Field (DAL). DAL is located approximately 5.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas have an airport shuttle to Dallas Love Field (DAL)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas does not have an airport shuttle to Dallas Love Field.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
