Discover luxury suites in Dallas
Stay in luxury hotel suites near Downtown Dallas
Discover the epitome of luxury in the heart of the city. Inspired by the vibrant spirit of Texas, premium accommodations at the five-star hotel blend refined elegance with the warmth of the destination. Overlooking the city’s stunning skyline, our luxury Uptown Dallas suites offer indulgent comfort with spacious living areas, sophisticated décor, and modern amenities. Whether you're seeking a serene retreat or an elevated urban escape, The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas, invites you to experience unparalleled luxury in the heart of the city.

The Ritz-Carlton Suite
Indulge in the pinnacle of luxury in The Ritz-Carlton Suite. This expansive 2,385-square-foot sanctuary offers stunning city views, elegant décor, and modern comforts. Enjoy a full living and sitting area, a separate dining room, fully equipped kitchen and a private study. This luxury hotel suite near Downtown Dallas features a master bedroom with a spa-inspired bathroom, along with Club Lounge access for personalized service and an unforgettable stay.
