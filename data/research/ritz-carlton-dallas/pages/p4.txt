Private Dining
The Wine Room
A sophisticated and intimate space for up to 12 guests, The Wine Room features a beautiful, enclosed wine display and rustic chandeliers, making it the perfect setting for an intimate gathering of friends, colleagues, or a special celebration.
The Sendero
Spanish for “the path,” The Sendero’s movable glass walls allow for a temperature-controlled or open-air setting with beautiful views of the outdoor gardens. The perfect indoor-outdoor atmosphere, The Sendero’s inviting space is perfect for celebrations of all occasions.
The Gallery
Nestled in a secluded corner, The Gallery offers a level of intimacy that is unmatched with a privacy curtain and refined atmosphere. Whether celebrating a special occasion or simply looking for an elevated dining experience, the Gallery’s magnificent artwork and beautiful table settings will elevate any event.
