# La Dolce Vita Suite

## The most exclusive space for your journey

Welcome to the **La Dolce Vita Suite**, the most exclusive space for your journey, where time slows down and Italian elegance takes shape in every detail. Here, you step into a small, suspended world where travel becomes an art, a celebration. A place where the most refined Italian design meets the slow rhythm of the train, inviting you to experience la dolce vita in all its essence.

## Step Inside a Dream Cabin

This exclusive 30 m² retreat is arranged in four distinct yet perfectly integrated areas, accommodating up to three guests. A bright living area with a lounge and dining space, a bedroom with an en suite bathtub, a main bathroom with double sinks and exquisite polychrome marbles, and a practical walk-in wardrobe: each area is designed to combine comfort and elegance, while the dual aspect offers ever-changing panoramic views of the Italian landscape in motion.

The floors alternate the softness of wool carpet with the preciousness of Venetian terrazzo, a mosaic of dark and warm marbles that captures light with refined subtlety. The walls, clad in Alcantara and lacquered surfaces, are enhanced with antiqued mirrors and brass details, creating a warm, enveloping, almost theatrical atmosphere. Every surface, every material invites touch; everything is meant to be experienced.

## Enter a world where every detail whispers luxury

Experience the art of refined travel and indulge in the pinnacle of elegance.

Reserve the La Dolce Vita Suite

## A Private Glimpse

Explore the luxurious comforts, bespoke décor, and exclusive amenities that make the La Dolce Vita Suite an unforgettable journey.

On the walls, artworks on silk crêpe specially created by artist Marta Benet evoke abstract Italian landscapes and the poetic geometries of Carlo Scarpa. Small textile architectures transform the suite into a space not only to live in but also to contemplate.

The bespoke furnishings and seating interact with elegant glass and brass light fixtures that diffuse soft, sophisticated illumination. In the details, small carefully chosen treasures - Murano vases, books, decorative objects - add personality and create a sense of intimacy. The intimate and cozy sleeping area features an en suite bathtub, while the main bathroom, a masterpiece of style, showcases double sinks and polychrome marbles such as travertine and the precious Portoro, creating a material harmony that expresses the most authentic Italian luxury.

## Tailored Service

Guests of the La Dolce Vita Suite are welcomed with an unparalleled experience, designed to offer the utmost in comfort, elegance, and personalized attention.

From the moment of booking, a dedicated Journey Curator will handle every detail, ensuring flawless preparation for the journey. During the stay on board, guests will have a personal Suite Conductor, an exclusive host who coordinates and customizes every aspect of the experience, discreetly anticipating desires and preferences.
For transfers to and from the departure and arrival stations, a private luxury vehicle is available, ensuring maximum comfort and privacy.

Upon entering the suite, a special welcome cocktail, exclusively created and inspired by the great classics of Italian cinema, greets guests with a touch of charm and creativity. The gastronomic journey includes a dedicated menu, carefully crafted from local ingredients and designed by three-Michelin-starred chef Heinz Beck. Dinner can be served directly in the suite, and whenever possible, Chef Heinz Beck himself will personally present the menu.

Finally, to complete a truly memorable stay, guests will have access to optional exclusive offboard experiences at each destination, tailored to immerse them in the local culture and its finest offerings through unique and fully personalized activities.
