## An iconic voyage

Launched in 1883, the first **Orient Express** linked Paris to Constantinople in less than 76 hours: a marvel of elegance and innovation, where exquisite decor, fine dining and exceptional service redefined the art of travel.

Today, its legend continues: Orient Express, a symbol of adventure and refinement, partners with the Musée des Arts Décoratifs to celebrate the centenary of the 1925 International Exhibition of Modern Decorative and Industrial Arts.

## L'Orient Express

By rail

All aboard for a journey like no other. Retracing the **inaugural route from Paris to Istanbul**, the Art Deco splendor of the original **Orient Express** returns within seventeen sumptuously-reimagined carriages.
