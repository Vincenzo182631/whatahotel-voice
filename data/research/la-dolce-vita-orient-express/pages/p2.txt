# Cabins and Suites

Choose from two categories on board La Dolce Vita Orient Express: Deluxe and Suite Cabins. Each carriage radiates sophistication and offers thoughtful amenities and traditional room service.

All CabinsSuites

**Deluxe Cabins**

The Deluxe cabin features cool colors, mirrored back walls and wooden slats ceilings. The sofa unfolds to a double bed at night, and the ottomans serve as coffee tables and stools.

7 m2 / 75 sq ft

Sofa bed (140 cm x 190 cm)

Ensuite bathroom

**Suite Cabins**

The Suite cabins are presented as master bedrooms, equipped with a sofa and a table with two armchairs, to encourage conviviality on-board.

11 m2 / 118 sq ft

140 cm x 190 cm
