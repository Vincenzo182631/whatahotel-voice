## The art of sailing reimagined

Orient Express Sailing Yachts

Orient Express Corinthian stands as a testament to modern naval architecture, harmoniously blending advanced technology with timeless artistry.

This exceptional vessel transcends conventional boundaries, embodying visionary craftsmanship and boundless innovation.

Discover the Yacht

## Revolutionary design

The development of Orient Express Sailing Yachts project presented a unique set of challenges, as contemporary standards for safety, crew, and efficiency differ vastly from those of traditional sailing ships. Designing a 1,500-square-meter (16,145-square-foot) sail posed another formidable task; many sailmakers abandoned the idea due to the immense forces involved. Yet, the  engineers at Chantiers de l’Atlantique embraced the challenge, creating a patented sail using carbon fiber-reinforced glass panels instead of standard woven cloth.This innovative sail plan, paired with powerful hull lines, endows OrientExpressCorinthian with its unmistakable silhouette and remarkable performance on the open sea.

### Information

- 220 Meters / 721 Feet Length
- 16,000 Metric Tons
- 3 "Solid Sails", 1,500 M² / 16,145 Ft² each
- 54 Luxury Suites
- 110 Guests
