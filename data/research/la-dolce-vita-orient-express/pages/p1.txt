# La Dolce Vita Orient Express

## A Love Letter to Italy

Step into a new golden age of slow travel aboard La Dolce Vita Orient Express, the unique luxury train that captures the timeless allure of Italian glamour and design, as it journeys through many bespoke itineraries across Italy’s most enchanting landscapes.

It is a love letter to Italy, to its rich heritage, culture and way of life.

The sophisticated aesthetic of La Dolce Vita Orient Express pays homage to the iconic Italian design legends of the 1960s, blending elegance with contemporary luxury.

It is a tribute to the essence of Italian gastronomy, where the dining experience aboard is curated by three-Michelin-starred Chef Heinz Beck.
