## The luxury of lingering

Many grand cities lie along your route, and **Orient Express hotels** beckon you to discover their myriad stories. Step off the train platform and into **palaces** awash in art and history, restored to modern splendor by master artisans and architects. Here, the **Transformative Voyage** extends beyond the rails: **welcome to Orient Express Hotels.**

## Orient Express La Minerva

Rome

Roman grandeur resides in every detail of this **17th-century palace,** the **first Orient Express hotel** in Rome, conceived by world-renowned artist-architect **Hugo Toro.** Just steps from the Pantheon, **La Minerva** offers 93 meticulously-appointed rooms and suites, crowned by a panoramic terrace.

Learn more

## Orient Express Venezia

Venice

Once home to Venice’s most illustrious noble families, this **15th-century palace** rises from the waters of the Cannaregio district, where a more authentic Venezia still endures. Breathtakingly transformed by **Aline Asmar d'Amman** for **Orient Express Venezia**, invites guests on a transformative voyage through the centuries across 47 rooms and suites.

Learn more
