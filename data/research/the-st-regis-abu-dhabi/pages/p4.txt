## Experiences

Located at the heart of the Corniche, our spa hotel in Abu Dhabi is directly connected to the Nation Towers Mall and is just minutes from Marina Mall, Qars Al Watan Palace, and the sophisticated World Trade Centre. It is also in the close vicinity of many of the embassies, oil and gas companies and other business institutions in the city. Guests will delight in discovering the local pulsating cosmopolitan lifestyle, turquoise Arabian Gulf and beach clubs in Abu Dhabi. The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque is one of Abu Dhabi’s most revered architectural treasures. Ferrari World Abu Dhabi is a must-visit for families, Ferrari fans and excitement enthusiasts. Souk at Central Market is a reinterpretation of the traditional marketplace. Manarat Al Saadiyat is an all-encompassing historical, cultural and artistic enclave. The numerous islands in Abu Dhabi offer different activities for visitors.

## Discover The Spa at The St. Regis Abu Dhabi

The Spa at The St. Regis Abu Dhabi is committed to creating an exceptional experience for each guest. From luxurious throws to customised treatments, our goal is to surpass all expectations and to help you escape to a sanctuary of indulgence and relaxation. Our staff is dedicated to making your visit spectacular.

Learn More

Sisters Beauty Lounge

On the third floor of The St. Regis Abu Dhabi is the sixth Sisters Beauty Lounge location in the United Arab Emirates. Amid sophisticated interiors and rich color schemes, the 2,000-square-foot lounge allows stunning views of sparkling waters. Just outside the lounge’s double doors is a private garden terrace where guests can enjoy healthful drinks and snacks from the spa’s café.

Featuring multi-channel TVs, three spacious treatment rooms are utilized for waxing, threading, and add-on massage treatments. An experienced international team offers styling and coloring services in the hair salon. A luxury nail room is designed for enhancements as well as classic and spa manicures and pedicures. Bespoke eyebrow treatments, eyelash extensions, and makeup applications are provided in a private room.

The lounge uses only the finest salon products, including Kérastase, Redken, Lycon Precision Waxing, Lash Perfect, Hairdreams, Essie, Shiffa, and CND. Every professional service is complemented by exquisite homecare products that are available on our retail shelves.

Walk-ins are welcome, but reservations at least 24 hours in advance are recommended. Please contact the salon for more information or to make reservations.

Family Experiences

##### Yas Waterworld

The 15-hectare Yas Waterworld comprises dozens of rides, slides, and attractions. This exciting theme park was inspired by the Emirati legend of Dana, a young girl who sought a pearl to benefit her village. This theme is carried through the park’s attractions, including an activity that highlights the importance of pearl-diving in the history of the United Arab Emirates.

##### Half Day Desert Safari

An exciting drive across sand dunes begins the Half Day Desert Safari. After watching the sunset at a camel farm, guests travel to a traditional Arabic camp to experience the centuries-old customs of henna painting, belly dancing, and service of local sweets and coffee. A barbecue dinner under the stars is accompanied by live entertainment. This experience is not recommended for children under the age of 3, pregnant women, and people with heart problems or other sensitive ailments.

These experiences are perfectly suited for a family of four (two adults and two children), but can always be customized. The Concierge is pleased to provide more details and to help make arrangements. To reserve an experience, it is recommended that guests contact the Concierge at least 24 hours in advance

Attractions in Abu Dhabi

##### Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque

The majestic Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque was initiated by the late president HH Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan and is one of Abu Dhabi’s most revered architectural treasures. The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, is seen as a construction to ‘unite the world’, using artisans and materials from countries as diverse as Italy, Germany, Morocco, India, Turkey, Iran, China, Greece and the UAE. Equivalent to the size of five football fields, the mosque can accommodate over 40,000 worshippers. The Mosque is open to visitors and guided tours are available free of charge.

##### Ferrari World Abu Dhabi

Ferrari World Abu Dhabi is the world’s first and largest indoor theme park. It is an intense multi-sensory experience and a must-visit for families, fans and enthusiasts. The Park offers more than 20 Ferrari-inspired rides and attractions including the fastest rollercoaster in the planet.

##### Souk At Central Market

Designed by Foster + Partners, the Central Market is a reinterpretation of the traditional market place. The project comprises a combination of lower-rise, ecologically sensitive levels of retail combining luxury goods boutiques with food markets and craft-based trades.

##### Manarat Al Saadiyat

Manarat Al Saadiyat, which means ‘place of enlightenment’, is an allencompassing historical, cultural and artistic enclave that brings to life the vision behind Saadiyat and its future plans. The island will become the only place in the world to house architecture designed by five individual Pritzker prize winners, including the Louvre, Guggenheim, National and Maritime museums as well as the Performing Arts Centre.

##### Falcon Hospital

Falconry is an integral part of desert life which has been practiced in the UAE for centuries. The Falcon Hospital provides a deep insight into the world of falcons, the history of falconry as well as the life of this majestic bird in modern times.

##### Abu Dhabi Islands

There are numerous islands in Abu Dhabi offering different activities for the visitors. The majority of the islands are flat, sandy and uninhabited, but each has its own character.

##### Saadiyat Island

Saadiyat Island is set to become the most important art centres in the world. Some of the major developments which are scheduled to be completed after 2015 are: a Louvre Museum by Jean Nouvel, a Guggenheim Museum by Frank Gehry, the Zayed National Museum by Foster + Partners, a Performing Arts Centre by Zaha Hadid, a Maritime Museum by the Japanese architect Tadao Ando, as well as art faculties of key universities such as the New York University.

Saadiyat is also famous for its pristine beaches where Hawksbill turtles nest and Bottlenose dolphins are frequently seen.

##### Yas Island

Yas Island is one of the UAE’s prime leisure and entertainment destinations. 25 minutes from Abu Dhabi, 45 minutes from Dubai, and 10 minutes from Abu Dhabi Airport, Yas Island houses the Yas Marina Circuit, which hosts the Formula One World Championship, Ferrari World Abu Dhabi, home to the world’s fastest rollercoaster, and Yas Waterworld. The Yas Links Golf Club boasts an 18-hole golf course with ocean views and the soon-to-open Yas Mall will offer more than 600 retailers.

##### Al Reem & Al Mariah Islands

Al Reem and Al Mariah Islands are a residential, commercial and business project being developed off the north-eastern coast of Abu Dhabi city.

##### Bu Tinah Island

Located approximately 130km west of Abu Dhabi, Bu Tinah is part of the Marawah Marine Biosphere Reserve, a UNESCO recognized site and the region’s first and largest Marine Biosphere Reserve. Visitors are not permitted to enter the island in order to preserve its rich marine and terrestrial ecosystem.

### Award Winning Golf

Three award-winning golf courses pepper the area. The 27-hole Abu Dhabi Golf Club —designed by world-renowned course architect Kyle Phillips—features seven salt-water lakes. Just 20-minutes away, Saadiyat Beach Golf Club—designed by golf legend Gary Player—presents spectacular vistas of the Gulf.
