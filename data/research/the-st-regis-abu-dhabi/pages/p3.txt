### Helipad Sunset Supper

Not for the faint-hearted, 20 guests, once per month, will be able to experience a very unique Helipad Sunset Supper, 255 metres in the air.

Details

90 minutes during Sunset

The St. Regis Abu Dhabi unveiled the first Helipad Sunset Supper in October 2016 and since then is has continued to exceed the expectations of the adventurous dining aficionados. Located 255 meters above sea level, making it the highest active helipad in the Middle East, as well as one of the most attractive locations to experience the magnificent sunset views of the Arabian Gulf. Creating and crafting boutique experiences for its’ luminaries, The St. Regis Abu Dhabi is hosting during the desirable winter months of the year the “Helipad Sunset Supper”, providing memorable moments and experiences for the most enchanting hour of the day.

Upon your arrival to The St. Regis Abu Dhabi’s lobby, you will be escorted behind the hotel scenes and through secret back hallways and elevators to the secure rooftop and greatly anticipated helipad. You will then be welcomed by our dedicated team including the St. Regis butlers, chefs, mixologists, and waiters that will be ready to assist you and exceed your expectations. Be ready to enjoy the soothing ambiance of a live saxophonist or violinist while you are savouring distinctive world-class premium beverages, extravagantly crafted canapés, a variety of delightful cakes, and the St. Regis Signature Afternoon tea. Gaze the complex, interesting, and enormous structures and landmarks of the city such as the wonderful Qars Al Watan, Emirates Palace, Abu Dhabi Theatre, and various other fascinating skyscrapers.

This spectacular elevated destination event is selectively hosted twice a month providing exclusive access only to 20 attendees. Secure your next venture to extraordinary heights and be captivated by the magnificent views of Abu Dhabi.

Helipad Sunset Supper prices start from AED 750 per person and attendees must be over 21 years old.

Reservations must be made in advance. For more information and details on dates, kindly contact our Restaurant Reservations at +971 (0)2 694 4553 or e-mail at restaurants.abudhabi@stregis.com
