## Accommodations

The St. Regis Abu Dhabi elevates luxury accommodation for every affluent traveler with exceptional service and private tailor-made experiences. Respecting The St. Regis brand legacy, our hotel rooms and suites in Abu Dhabi offer alluring amenities and iconic sea or city views in a contemporary yet lavish Arabic-influenced décor. Statuario Royal marble immediately communicates each hotel room's elegant ambiance while hand-tufted wool carpeting imbues a relaxed refinement. State-of-the-art technology - a flat-screen television and iPod docking station - adds to the modern offerings, while complimentary high-speed internet access ensures guests stay seamlessly connected during their visit to Abu Dhabi city. Plush beds wrapped in crisp, 300-thread-count white linens guarantee a restful night’s sleep; serene marble bathrooms enhance the rejuvenating experience, thanks to dual vanities, spacious bathtubs, and separate showers with rain shower heads, exclusive bath amenities.
