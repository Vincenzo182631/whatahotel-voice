# Kimpton Era Midtown New YorkLocation

Located in the heart of Midtown Manhattan, Kimpton Era offers unparalleled access to New York City’s most iconic attractions, cultural institutions, and corporate headquarters. Steps from Rockefeller Center, guests can easily explore world‑renowned landmarks, Fifth Avenue shopping, MoMA, Broadway theaters, and vibrant neighborhood dining. With effortless connectivity to major transit hubs, Kimpton Era serves as the ideal home base for both business and leisure travelers looking to experience the very best of New York City.

## Things to do

### Rockefeller Center

45 Rockefeller Plaza, NY, NY 10111

Rockefeller Center is a complex of 19 commercial buildings covering 22 acres (8.9 ha) between 48th Street and 51st Street in the Midtown Manhattan neighborhood

Learn more

### Central Park

orth 110th Street to Central Park South, NY, NY 10019

Central Park is located in New York City, in the heart of Manhattan. It is a national historical landmark that draws in approximately 40 million visitors from all over the world every year.

Learn more

### Empire State Building

20 W 34th St., NY, NY 10001

The Empire State Building is a 102-story, \[c\] Art Deco-style supertall skyscraper in the Midtown South neighborhood of Manhattan, New York City, United States.

Learn more

### Times Square

Broadway, 7th Avenue, 42nd and 47th St, NY, NY 10036

Times Square is a major commercial intersection, tourist destination, entertainment hub, and neighborhood in the Midtown Manhattan section of New York City. It is formed by the junction of Broadway, Seventh Avenue, and 42nd Street.

Learn more

### St. Patrick's Cathedral

631 Fifth Ave, NY, NY 10022

St. Patrick's Cathedral is a Catholic cathedral in the Midtown Manhattan neighborhood of New York City. It is the seat of the Archbishop of New York as well as a parish church.

Learn more

### 5th Avenue

One of the world’s most famous shopping streets, Fifth Avenue pairs shimmering luxury flagships with historic stone façades and dazzling seasonal windows. St. Patrick’s Cathedral rises between boutiques, all just moments from our front door.

### Broadway

The heartbeat of the theater world, Broadway glows with neon marquees, the murmur of opening-night crowds, and the electric buzz of nearby Times Square. From long-running legends to innovative debuts, the stages here are alive. And from Kimpton Era, they’re only a short stroll away.

### Midtown

The cultural and commercial heart of Manhattan, Midtown pulses with nonstop energy. You’re steps from MoMA, Bryant Park, and Fifth Avenue—plus icons like the New York Public Library, Empire State Building, Madison Square Garden, and Grand Central.
