Redefining hospitality at Rockefeller Center while honoring its cultural legacy, Kimpton Era is a vibrant hub where tourists and locals converge. A dynamic blend of the new and the familiar, serving as both a destination and a launchpad for everything the city has to offer.

Blending the sophistication of its iconic setting with the vibrant energy of modern New York, every stay immerses guests in the city's cultural heartbeat; from the buzz of Rockefeller Plaza to the magic of our rooftop bar overlooking the skyline.

In true Big Apple style, our rooms are petite yet thoughtfully designed, offering everything you need and nothing you don't. New York is a place to experience— whether enjoying one of our four destination bars and restaurants or hitting the town; from here, the city is theirs to uncover.

## FAQs for Era Midtown New York

Does Kimpton Era Midtown New York have a pool?

No, Kimpton Era Midtown New York does not have a pool.

How much is parking at Kimpton Era Midtown New York?

At Kimpton Era Midtown New York, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 65 USD and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 110 USD .

Self Parking located at Icon Garage 53 W48th street

Parking fee: 65 USD per day

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Era Midtown New York?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Era Midtown New York.

Pet fee per night: 0 USD

Pet damage deposit: 0 USD

Pet weight limit: No weight limit per pet

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Era Midtown New York for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Era Midtown New York have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Era Midtown New York has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Era Midtown New York?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Era Midtown New York.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Era Midtown New York?

Check-in at Kimpton Era Midtown New York is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Era Midtown New York offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Era Midtown New York offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Era Midtown New York from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Era Midtown New York is conveniently located near LaGuardia Airport (LGA), approximately 11.4 miles away (18.35 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Era Midtown New York?

Kimpton Era Midtown New York is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Rockefeller Center-0.1 miles away (0.16 kilometers)
- Central Park-1 miles away (1.61 kilometers)
- Empire State Building -0.8 miles away (1.29 kilometers)
- Times Square-0.6 miles away (0.97 kilometers)
- St. Patrick's Cathedral -0.2 miles away (0.32 kilometers)
- 5th Avenue-1.4 miles away (2.25 kilometers)
- Broadway-0.4 miles away (0.64 kilometers)
- Midtown-0.4 miles away (0.64 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Era Midtown New York puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
