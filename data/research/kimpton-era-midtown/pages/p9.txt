Kimpton Era Midtown
Sleek and contemporary with plenty of amenities, this Rock Center hotel is ideal for bopping around Midtown Manhattan for the first (or fifth) time.

In a nutshell:
This new Kimpton property checks every box—location, style, convenience, and dining alike knock it out of the park. Ideal for bopping around Midtown Manhattan for the first (or fifth) time, you're within walking distance of dozens of major tourist attractions, from The Museum of Modern Art to Times Square.

Rooms
The Kimpton Era Midtown is new to the Manhattan hotel scene, having just opened in March 2026. My friend Justine and I, New Yorkers on a quick staycation, stayed in a corner double on the 25th floor, with a close-up, cinematic view of the Empire State Building (I highly recommend booking a room with a city view if it’s available!). While the room was compact in size, the plush, cloud-like double beds and iconic views more than made up for any lost space.
Design-wise, the room’s aesthetic was sleek, minimalist, and urban, with a wall of fluted blond wood backdropping the white sheets, black accents, clean lines, and ambient lighting. The steamy walk-in shower felt especially luxurious. In-room amenities included a TV with streaming services, complimentary water bottles with refill stations in the hall, a hair dryer, and a modest closet. Being brand-new, every inch was spotless. The hotel is mid-size, with a total of 529 rooms.

Food and drink
The food offerings are a major draw. The hotel has three—yes, three—on-site restaurants: Amasa, a Latin American steakhouse; Bar Amasa, the upstairs taqueria; and Bar Rocco, an Italian restaurant that also serves an elegant breakfast, helmed by celebrity chef Rocco DiSpirito. Like most fun NYC hotels, there’s a rooftop bar, Jade Rabbit, with glittering sights of surrounding skyscrapers and cocktails inspired by Asian flavors.
Amenities and experiences
Pretty much everything you need is covered. The fitness center is huge and open 24 hours—it offers ellipticals, free weights, a stair stepper, a stationary bicycle, and a treadmill. The hotel is also pet-friendly, allowing two animals of any size free of charge.
What to do nearby
This location is genuinely in the heart of the action (and I don’t throw that stock phrase around casually): As I was drinking coffee in the lobby before check-out Friday morning, I literally realized five minutes in that I was gazing down at Zara Larsson and her backup dancers performing on
The Today Show
right outside. The address is walking distance from major attractions like Radio City Music Hall, Top of the Rock, St. Patrick's Cathedral, FAO Schwartz, and MoMA. The BDFM subway lines are steps away, thanks to the nearby 47th–50th Streets–Rockefeller Center subway station.
The backstory
This 33-story hotel was built from the ground up—it’s been in the making since 2018, when urban developer Extell bought the air rights, and finally opened its doors in March 2026. Kimpton Era Midtown is joining the city’s other three Kimpton properties: Kimpton Theta New York Times Square, Kimpton Hotel Eventi (which is in NoMad), and Kimpton Ashbel New York Park Avenue. The brand belongs to IHG.
Insider tip
If you have time, I definitely suggest hanging out in the third-floor lobby, called The Parlor. The space is chic but cozy, with ample table space, soft chairs, and a visually stunning bar anchoring it all. It transitions between coffee shop and bar, depending on the time of day. They serve complimentary coffee until 9 a.m., and complimentary beer and wine between 5–6 p.m., but you can order a beverage whenever you want. It’s a great spot to clock a laptop hour or two.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Totally. The hotel is appropriate for kids, and, with a registration fee, 3–15-year-olds can participate in a Central Park day camp.
