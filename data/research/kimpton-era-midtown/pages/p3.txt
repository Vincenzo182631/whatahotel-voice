## Bar Rocco

### The Parlor

The Parlor unfolds as an elegant Midtown retreat—part lobby lounge, part private living room. From the first espresso of the morning to a well‑earned late‑night pour, it’s a place designed for unhurried moments, thoughtful conversation, and casually refined bites, all infused with an understated European ease.

Bar Hours: 6:30 AM - 11:00 PM

### Jade Rabbit

Floating above the city’s grid, Jade Rabbit blends sweeping skyline views with a bold, Asian‑inspired cocktail program. Arrive for golden hour, linger as Midtown ignites, and experience New York from a rooftop where the view does half the talking.

Bar Hours: 6:30 AM - 11:00 PM
