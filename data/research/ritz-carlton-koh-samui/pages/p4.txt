Featured Spa Treatment
Samui’s Coconut Heritage
Coconuts are synonymous with Koh Samui, with a plethora of plantations and attractions across the island. At our luxury spa in Koh Samui, we enjoy sharing the heritage of the nutritional, health, and beauty benefits of this amazing fruit. A deluxe Songkran Shower and a Coconut Scrub are followed by a 90-minute Coconut Flow Massage. Top it off like the locals do with a nourishing Coconut Hair Masque.

Featured Spa Treatment
Blessed Flowers
The magic of flowers has bloomed in the hearts of mankind from the beginning of time, their universal language instantly, wordlessly communicating love, grace, and joy. The vivid colors and euphoric fragrances of our island’s native flowers delight the spirit and play an intrinsic role both in beauty and ritual. Celebrating the vibrant power of Samui’s abundant gift, this treatment at our luxury spa in Koh Samui begins with a “table-side mixing” of raw flowers, nourishing aloe, and fruit enzymes to exfoliate the skin. Warmed flower-infused compresses gently hydrate the skin in preparation for a flower balance massage utilizing strong rhythmical strokes along energy meridians, leaving you truly in a state of harmony.

Spa Special
Thai Herbal Facial
This facial is another unique Thai practice that uses traditional aromatic herbs of tamarind and turmeric, along with rice, honey, yoghurt, coconut oil, and din saw pong (Thai white mud). It helps increase circulation, promote cell growth, and brighten the skin. This luxurious treatment includes an exfoliation, a relaxing facial and shoulder massage with a Prakrop pouch, and a finishing masque that soothes, hydrates, and balances. The traditional Thai herbal compress helps clarify the skin and ease inflammation. It’s a must-try facial for all skin types.
