A Dining Journey for the Senses
Dining
Our Koh Samui restaurants mirror the island’s offerings: chili-spiked southern cuisine, bustling food markets and an impressive bounty of local ingredients. Each restaurant offers its unique interpretation of gastronomic delights, transforming a meal into an unforgettable journey.
HOLIDAY FLAVORS - Crafted for Celebration
This festive season, discover a series of curated dining experiences at The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui—seasonal menus, ocean-breeze settings, and impeccable ambience that turns every meal into a shared moment. Gather with family and friends and savor the warmth of an island holiday, crafted for you.
