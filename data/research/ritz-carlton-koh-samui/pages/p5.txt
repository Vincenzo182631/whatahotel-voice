Luxury Resort near Fisherman's Village in Koh Samui
Experiences
Situated along Southern Thailand’s spectacular coastline, our spa hotel near Chaweng Beach​ on Koh Samui island offers the best examples of beach culture. From outdoor adventures to cultural discoveries, a host of activities awaits. Learn the art of Muay Thai, explore the intricacies of southern Thai cuisine or embark on a cruise aboard a luxury yacht. Samui was a remote haven for fishermen, sailors and sea traders sheltering from storms and high seas in the late 1800s. Mainly of Chinese and Malay origin, these early settlers speak Pak Thai, a unique dialect in the south that still survives to this day.
Spa Village Koh Samui
Inspired by regional traditions and culture and using natural local ingredients and age-old healing practices, the spa at our hotel near Chaweng Beach features holistic and rejuvenating programs. Experience our signature pre-spa Songkran Shower ritual.

Ritz Kids®
Ritz-Kids provides a secure and enjoyable space for children to explore and grow. Set in a charming two-story building, the facilities at our hotel with a kids club in Koh Samui boast both indoor and outdoor playgrounds, complete with ball pools, slides, swings and a mini-golf course. Tech-savvy kids can also indulge in gaming on Xbox consoles. Our dedicated Ladies & Gentlemen organize daily activities, from arts and crafts to treasure hunts, guaranteeing fun-filled moments and learning opportunities. Parents can rest assured as their little ones are supervised by our attentive Ladies & Gentlemen. Opening hours: daily, from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Big Buddha Temple
Home to a 12-metre high gold Buddha built in 1972, our spa hotel on Koh Samui island enjoys close proximity to a major landmark with great views.
The Art of Muay Thai
Learn the art of eight limbs through a revered skill-based sport for a total workout with trained instructors. Be a part of an exclusive community during your stay at our Koh Samui hotel near the airport.
Swim Reef
Discover the largest, one-of-a-kind swim reef in Southeast Asia within a resort at The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui. Teeming with over 50 species of marine fish by the thousands, our unique feature will delight and engage all ages through immersive snorkeling discoveries and scheduled supervised fish feeding. The advanced filtration systems ensure that pollutants, jellyfish, and suspended solids in the seawater are removed, resulting in a temperate and safe environment all year round. Strategically placed artificial coral reef structures allow for optimum fish viewing and cultivate a conducive habitat for clownfish breeding and coral propagation programs supported by the local authorities.

The Southern Gastronomic Journey

Fisherman’s Village
Our resort is near Fisherman's Village in Koh Samui, a charming Bophut coastal town lined with old wooden shophouses and a walking street market every Friday evening.
Chaweng Beach

The Perfect Location to Explore Tropical Paradise
Nestled in a private sun-kissed bay on the northeastern tip of the island and six kilometers from Samui International Airport, this hotel near Chaweng Beach​ is the perfect location to explore tropical paradise. Framed by azure waters, beaches and mountainous jungles, our hotel is near the Big Buddha Temple, Fisherman's Village and Wat Plai Laem. Our concierge can help you make the most of your visit by recommending places to see.

Planning Your Trip to Koh Samui
Over the years, Koh Samui has grown from a small fishing community into a lavish island retreat that beckons beach lovers worldwide. Known for its stunning white-sand beaches and breathtaking views, there is so much to do on the Thai island of Koh Samui — from exploring the region’s myriad cultural attractions and dining on the fresh fare to hiking through the jungle and sunning on pristine swaths of the beach.
