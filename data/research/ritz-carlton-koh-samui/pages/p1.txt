Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui
A Journey of Culture and Inspired Island Living
Surrounded by crystal-clear waters, our luxury resort in Koh Samui is located in a world-renowned island paradise that boasts turquoise waters, perfect beaches, and a more laid-back, authentic lifestyle. The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui, nestled within the private bay at the island’s northern tip and set on 58 acres of a former coconut plantation, features a variety of suites and private pool villas that suit every guest's needs. It is perfect for well-being and leisure with its unique facilities, guest activities and experiences that embrace the southern Thai culture and tradition within the resort. Highlights at our Koh Samui beachfront resort include Muay Thai boxing classes, locally inspired treatments at Spa Village Koh Samui and a vibrant culinary journey at restaurants and bars as well as southern Thai cooking classes.
A Stunning 58-Acre
Oceanfront Resort
The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui, located on the northeastern part of the island, is a stunning 58-acre oceanfront property. Built on the site of a former coconut plantation, our Koh Samui five-star hotel pays homage to its past through the use of coconut and natural materials. Embrace a gentle rhythm and energetic way of life through outdoor and cultural discoveries and recreational facilities. A myriad of experiences are available to suit your relaxed state of mind.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui. Self parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui is Koh Samui Airport (USM). USM is located approximately 6.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui have an airport shuttle to Koh Samui Airport (USM)?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui has an airport shuttle to Koh Samui Airport (USM).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
