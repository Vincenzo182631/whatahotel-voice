## Best Luxury Hotel in Gangnam, Seoul

A luxury stay awaits you here in Seoul! Perfectly located in the Gangnam near the COEX Convention, Park Hyatt Seoul provides an uncommon experience, promising elegant and gracious service on a personal level. From top-class gourmet options to tranquil spa services, you will enjoy an unparalleled level of comfort and sophistication that exceeds every expectation.

## Discover Park Hyatt Seoul

Kid-Friendly Amenities

Park Hyatt Seoul offers a range of personalized services for guests traveling with children. Upon request, the VIB (very important baby) program is available for infants under 3 years of age, featuring a specially prepared children’s menu and homemade baby food crafted with care by our chefs. To ensure a comfortable stay, we also provide Hartan stroller and Daiichi baby bed rentals, as well as babysitting services. For children 5 years and older, board game rentals are available, creating memorable moments for the whole family to enjoy together.

Prepare for a More Sustainable Stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available on request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

Pool & Sauna

The granite-lined indoor swimming pool at Park Hyatt Seoul is designed in an infinity pool style, offering a unique atmosphere each hour as natural sunlight streams through the floor-to-ceiling windows. Children and teenagers under the age of 16 may use the pool only when accompanied by a responsible adult. The saunas are private spaces equipped with individual shower facilities and luxury amenities, with the dry sauna available for male guests and the wet sauna for female guests.

Fitness Studio

The Park Club Fitness Studio features the latest in cardio-vascular equipment combined with a wide array of entertainment options and weight training facilities. Our qualified fitness instructors are on hand to ensure a quality workout and Personal Training sessions may be arranged through the Spa Concierge.

Spa

Ocelas Spa at Park Club brings together the artful techniques of seasoned therapists with the finest professional spa products to deliver new energy for body and mind, amid stunning views across central Gangnam. Three private treatment rooms offer undisturbed rest and relaxation, while a citrus bar serves a selection of Korean-inspired refreshments.

### Cornerstone

Cornerstone, our Italian restaurant, features floor-to-ceiling windows and interiors finished with natural wood and stone, creating an atmosphere that feels both warm and modern. From breakfast and weekend brunch featuring premium ingredients prepared to order to authentic Italian home-style dishes served à la carte or as part of a course menu, guests can enjoy a dining experience that feels both comforting and refined. A thoughtfully curated selection of premium wines, beverages, and desserts is also available.

Guests may also discover a gift selection from Pierre Hermé Paris, including macarons and chocolates, at Cornerstone.
