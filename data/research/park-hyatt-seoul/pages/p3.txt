# Park Hyatt Seoul \| Best Korean & Italian restaurant in Seoul

# Restaurants & Bars

Discover a variety of exquisite dining experiences and exceptional service at Park Hyatt Seoul’s restaurants and bars. Using only the freshest, highest-quality ingredients, the chefs at Park Hyatt Seoul create savory, palate-pleasing cuisine with flair and creativity.

Located on the 2nd floor, our main restaurant, Cornerstone, offers authentic Italian dishes, a vast array of premium beverages and a convivial and relaxing atmosphere. Offering panoramic views of Seoul, The Lounge, on the 24th floor, serves modern Korean cuisine specially created for the enjoyment of Koreans and foreigners alike, as well as a wide selection of beverages including traditional Korean teas and cocktails. At The Timber House, a premium whisky and cocktail bar, enjoy curated pairings of fine drinks and diverse dishes, elevated by live music performances.

## Cornerstone

Cornerstone, our Italian restaurant, features floor-to-ceiling windows and interiors finished with natural wood and stone, creating an atmosphere that feels both warm and modern. From breakfast and weekend brunch featuring premium ingredients prepared to order to authentic Italian home-style dishes served à la carte or as part of a course menu, guests can enjoy a dining experience that feels both comforting and refined. A thoughtfully curated selection of premium wines, beverages, and desserts is also available.

Guests may also discover a gift selection from Pierre Hermé Paris, including macarons and chocolates, at Cornerstone.

## The Lounge

The Lounge is the hotel’s most romantic setting, distinguished by its elegant atmosphere, harmonious blend of traditional and modern style, and panoramic city views from the 24th floor.

Alongside a premium collection of traditional teas and signature afternoon tea, The Lounge presents contemporary dining that brings together Korean cuisine made with seasonal ingredients, French cooking techniques, and delicate plating. A creative selection of Korean-inspired desserts is also available.
