Located in Gangnam, the business hub of Korea, Park Hyatt Seoul boasts 185 modern and luxurious guestrooms including one Presidential Suite, two Diplomatic Suites and 35 Park Suites. Sleek designed bathrooms appointed with spa-inspired rain showers and spacious bathtubs will make your stay extraordinary.
