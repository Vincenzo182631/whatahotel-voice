# Best Dining in Changbaishan \| Park Hyatt Changbaishan

# Restaurants & Bars

Park Hyatt Changbaishan, one of the most distinguished Luxury hotels in Changbaishan, international resort features two restaurants, Living Room and Park Hyatt Dining Room.

## Living Room

Located in level 1, this residential style outlet serves as the hotel’s all day dining venue, serving a selection of Western and Chinese comfort food for breakfast, lunch and dinner. The Living Room of Park Hyatt Changbaishan is setting in a serene environment and enjoying beautiful views of the ski slopes and surrounding mountains.
The menu, which is available throughout the day, is very simple, focusing on home style, comfort dishes. Guests are encouraged to speak with the butlers or the chefs directly about what they feel like eating, rather than just be given a menu to choose from. Like at home! A beautiful open-style pantry / cold kitchen, double up as dispense bar and cold buffet counter in the morning for breakfast, with a chef in attendance, slicing cold cuts right there from a beautiful slicing machine, portioning little plates of cheese, carving smoked salmon, etc. In between meal periods, the Living Room is the perfect place to have a cup of coffee or tea, while checking e-mails or reading the newspaper.
