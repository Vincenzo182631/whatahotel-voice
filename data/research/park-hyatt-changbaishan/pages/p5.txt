### Staff was great! Refrigerator a bit loud

Came here for 2 nights for a wedding. The room was really nice and good value for the money, might be because of the location.

If you’ve never been to changbaishan, be prepared for being snowed in for your flight. It happened to us and everyone was stuck. The staff tried their best to help us with that and called the airlines for us. They were super helpful.

The refrigerator is a bit loud for my sleeping personally. Otherwise, the room is large and great. The food downstairs was good too.

I would definitely go again.
