Spacious Rooms With World-class Views
Suites & Rooms
High ceilings and oversized windows frame views of Philadelphia and bring a sense of light and scale to our accommodations. Located in the heart of Center City, our guest rooms and suites pair sophisticated design with the comfort and thoughtful service of The Ritz-Carlton. Select suites offer residential-style living and dining spaces, while signature suites feature expansive layouts, multiple bathrooms, and added conveniences for extended stays or private entertaining. Guests reserving Club accommodations enjoy access to The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge on the 30th floor, featuring continuous culinary presentations, personalized concierge service, and sweeping city views.
Timeless Grandeur Awaits
The Presidential Suite
Experience luxury and comfort in our Center City Presidential Suite. Boasting sophistication and charm, enjoy expansive city views, a master bedroom, 1.5 bathrooms, a large living room, a dining room featuring a wet bar, a powder room and state-of-the-art amenities throughout.

Legacy of Luxury
The Ritz-Carlton Suite
The Ritz-Carlton Suite is perched on the top floor with lofty ceilings, large picture windows and elegant white paneled walls - in keeping with the building's era, while its contemporary furnishings have a distinctly Art Deco aesthetic. Its neutral color palette exudes calm and as well as two bedrooms, the 1,900-sq-ft suite offers an expansive living and dining room with a bird's eye view of Philadelphia’s iconic City Hall, as well as valet parking and coveted access to the Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge. For added privacy (and exclusivity) the suite boasts a private elevator to zoom guests from Avenue of the Arts, 30 stories skywards.

The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge
The Ritz-Carlton, Philadelphia features The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge for guests seeking a heightened level of luxury and exclusivity. This delightful oasis on the 30th floor is often referred to as a hotel within a hotel, where a dedicated concierge attends to individual needs in a well-appointed, relaxed lounge environment. With continuous culinary offerings throughout the day (breakfast, light snacks, hors d' oeuvres, beverages, and sweets) and access to a computer station − it is the ideal place to conduct business, plan a family outing, or simply unwind while enjoying spectacular views of Philadelphia.

More About Club Level
The Ritz-Carlton Club lounge is exclusively for the comfort and convenience of Club guests. To provide the highest levels of service, we kindly restrict the use of the Club lounge and the food and beverage presentations to registered Club guests only.
In keeping with The Ritz-Carlton tradition, appropriate attire and footwear is required when visiting the Club Lounge.
In order to maintain the relaxed and refined ambiance of the Club lounge, children must be accompanied by an adult at all times and assisted in selecting items from the food presentations. Strollers are not permitted in the lounge.
In consideration of all guests, we kindly request that cellular phones not be used while in the Club Lounge.
As a Club Level guest you receive complimentary services such as:
Complimentary Internet Access in the Club Lounge
Pressing of Two (2) items per day
Concierge Services and Business Center Services
Complimentary coffee or tea with a wake-up call
Your comfort is very important to us and we want to ensure that your visit to Philadelphia is truly memorable. If there is anything we may do to assist you during your stay, please let us know.
Club Level accommodations allow for two guests per a room. An additional charge for guests of 7 years and older of $100.00 per person per night applies. Charges will be applied beginning at the time of check-in.
