A Hotel Near Philadelphia’s Storied Attractions
Experiences
Our Center City location places guests at the heart of Philadelphia, directly across from City Hall and within easy reach of the Avenue of the Arts, Rittenhouse Square, Reading Terminal Market, the Pennsylvania Convention Center, and the city’s celebrated cultural and historic attractions. On property, guests may enjoy restorative treatments at Richel D’Ambra Spa + Salon or maintain their wellness routine in the third-floor fitness center, open 24 hours a day. Personalized destination recommendations and thoughtfully arranged in-room amenities add another level of care to both leisure stays and special occasions.
Richel D'Ambra Spa
The Richel D’ambra Spa + Salon has been recognized as one of the best spas in Philadelphia, PA and across the nation. Offering a variety of salon services, unique treatments, and luxury rituals, it is the ideal location to escape to a world of relaxation and comfort.

Escape for the Day to a Holistic Hotel Spa
Treat yourself to a transformative spa day in Philadelphia. Rooted in the Balinese reverence for the Earth and those who inhabit it, the hotel’s Richel D’Ambra Spa & Salon blends physical healing and beauty with inner well-being. Treatments feature classic massages as well as rituals that borrow from other traditions including chakra balancing and reiki. Facials target specific skin concerns, and the HydraFacial offers a non-invasive, medical-grade treatment that leaves skin smooth and glowing. In the salon, experts offer an array of services including wedding hair and makeup.

Spa Frequently Asked Questions
What if I need to cancel my scheduled appointment?
All services are reserved especially for you. To avoid a full fee charge, we require 24 hours advance notice of cancellations.
Are gratuities customary for spa services?
If you are pleased with the services, it is customary to give roughly 20% of the cost of the service as a gratuity. Gratuities are not included in the prices of spa services.
When should I arrive for my service?
We encourage you to arrive 30 minutes before your treatment to enjoy our steam room and sauna.
Do you schedule before and after hours?
We will gladly accommodate special requests, including those for group appointments or private parties in the spa.
Can I purchase gift certificates?
Yes, gift certificates are available at the spa desk or by phone.
A City Rooted in American History & Culture
Philadelphia, PA is a city with it all. Renowned art collections at the Barnes Foundation and Philadelphia Art Museum. Performing arts at the Kimmel Center and Merriam Theater. Shopping on Rittenhouse Row and Market Street. And important historic sights including Independence Hall and Terminal Market. These attractions, and others nearby, are easily explored from the hotel.

Does The Ritz-Carlton, Philadelphia have an on-site spa?
Yes, Richel D'Ambra Spa and Salon is located on-site at The Ritz-Carlton, Philadelphia.
