Latin Cuisine, Grilled to Perfection
Dining
Set beneath the soaring columns of our landmark lobby, dining at The Ritz-Carlton, Philadelphia brings together a dramatic setting, Latin-inspired flavors, and warm, attentive service. Aqimero offers a vibrant dining experience in the heart of Center City, while the Lobby Lounge provides a relaxed setting for cocktails, conversation, and light fare. For guests who prefer to dine privately, in-room dining is presented with china, glass, and silver, bringing the refinement of the hotel directly to the guest room or suite.
At This Hotel
Latin American
Aqimero
Located in the Ritz-Carlton lobby, Aqimero is a standout Latin American restaurant in Philadelphia, offering modern, sustainable flavors by chef Richard Sandoval. Enjoy refined dining in downtown Philadelphia, including breakfast daily.
