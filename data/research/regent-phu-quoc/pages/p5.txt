ART LOVERS

Photography Exhibit ‘The Memory of Flowers’

At the heart of Regent Phu Quoc, art becomes an invitation to pause and reflect. Discover The Memory of Flowers, a solo exhibition by Vietnamese visual artist Pham Tuan Ngoc, where delicate blooms are transformed through the rare camera-less lumen print technique. A poetic meditation on light, time, and the fleeting beauty of nature. On view from now until 31 October 2026.
