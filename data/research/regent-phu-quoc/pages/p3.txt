DINING

Follow the fragrant aromas. Let new flavours excite.

From traditional local fare to the innovative and inspiring, our Restaurants and Bars are sure to excite. Open your mind and your taste buds as we take you on a culinary journey with pop-up dinners and exciting guest chefs. Whether you’re looking to sample the best Japanese French cuisine or sip a sundowner in our rooftop pool, Regent's Restaurants and Bars have something to make every moment special.

Rice Market

Bustling and atmospheric. A celebration of the humblest ingredient in Vietnamese cuisine: Rice. Showcasing the diversity of such a simple staple, in the form of Rice and Noodle dishes, which are sure to please when combined with fresh herbs, spices, and recipes native to Vietnam.

Explore

Ocean Club

Soak up the beach club vibe with our specially curated menu and rum-inspired cocktails. Lounge poolside with framed views of the ocean as the steady beat of the music mingles with the crashing waves on the shore.

Explore

Oku

Our signature dining restaurant, featuring an Omakase Atelier and Salon de Boeuf, serves contemporary Japanese-French cuisine showcasing the finest techniques and ingredients from both worlds. Here, creative minds come together to curate a truly sensory dining experience.

Explore

Aqua Bar and Lounge

Set against that mesmerizing backdrop with the gentle whispers of sea breezes, Aqua Bar and Lounge is where the curves and contours of the sea snail design create a sense of flow and harmony, mirroring the gentle ebb and flow of the tides. Designed by Vietnamese visual artist and architect Mr. Trung Nghia, this sea snail-inspired pop-up restaurant is sustainably built using locally sourced bamboo.

**The restaurant closes from 1 June 2026 until further notice**

Explore

Bar Jade

Sample inspired libations or request off-menu innovations, liquid artistry awaits with a laidback speakeasy cocktail bar. Discreetly tucked away in a hidden nook within the resort, let our mixologists take you on a journey with bespoke cocktails using artisanal spirits and fresh local ingredients.

Explore

Fu Bar

Unwind under the sun and stars at the adults-only sky pool terrace and rooftop bar. Explore the gin and herb-focused cocktail menu. Take a dip in the rooftop pool. The sweeping views of the island are yours to savour.

Executive Sous Chef Huan Tran

Originally from central Vietnam, Chef Huan always dreamed of becoming a great chef, so since childhood he wanted to learn more about the cuisines of the regions of Vietnam—the cuisines change in each region, as do the cultures. He has experimented with the authentic local ingredients and special spices to bring out the characteristic flavours of Vietnamese cuisine.

He adapts his cooking to his surroundings, priding himself on using the freshest and seasonal products. Chef Huan loves to innovate and build bridges between north and south regional cuisines, creating unique taste combinations.

Executive Pastry Chef Duyen Nguyen

Chef Duyen Nguyen grew up in Ninh Binh, a Northern province in Vietnam that’s known for its magical riverine landscape and an UNESCO’s world heritage site. Chef Duyen is a traditional French pastry cuisine aficionado with a passion for art and colour that he translates into his cuisine. Inspired by the picturesque nature of his upbringing, Chef Duyen incorporates local ingredients and flavours of his homeland into the pastries he makes.

As the Executive Pastry Chef at Regent Phu Quoc, Chef Duyen aspires to create unique and sensory experiences for guests. He uses rose myrtle fruits or peppers, both of which are found only in Phu Quoc island in his sweet and savoury creations.

Executive Chef Francesco Andreoni

Executive Chef Francesco brings two decades of culinary experience, perfected in some of the globe's most renowned hotels and upscale restaurants in cities such as Milan, Paris, Chicago, Melbourne, and southern Switzerland. His rich background is set to enhance the dining experience at Regent Phu Quoc, where he will oversee all culinary operations at the resort.
