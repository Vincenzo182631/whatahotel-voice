## Experiences

A true oasis within the Costa del Sol, The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort and Spa, Benahavis, Marbella is surrounded by the Sierra Blanca’s hills in the heart of renowned Golf Valley. Close to cosmopolitan...See More Marbella, the resort is just 10 minutes from Old Town’s picturesque settings and the vibrant nightlife, exclusive shopping, and gastronomic options of Puerto Banús Marina Harbor. Malaga Airport (AGP) and a high-speed train station are 45 minutes away, and the resort features convenient direct access from the highway as well. Enjoy our hotel facilities after a long day of exploring Marbella, from gorgeous swimming pools and a gym, golf or our spa treatments, ensuring an experience for all.See Less

### Sprunch: Spa + Brunch

Pamper yourself with a unique experience, the Sprunch: Spa + Brunch. Savor a revitalizing brunch and access to the relaxing thermal circuit of our The Westin Heavenly Spa™. Ideal to giveaway or to enjoy with your beloved one, friends or family. When: Daily from 11 am to 4 pm. Where: At The Westin Heavenly Spa™ and at the Clubhouse Restaurant. Thermal circuit duration: 90 minutes. Price: 48 € per person (VAT included). More information and bookings: Email: heavenlyspa@westinlaquinta.com

Located just 45 minutes away from Malaga International Airport (AGP), the resort enjoys a convenient direct access from the highway that links the major cities within the south of Spain.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, TRX Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella have an on-site spa?

Yes, Heavenly Spa by Westin™ is located on-site at The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella. Click here to book a service now.

Does The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella offer golf course access nearby or at the hotel?

Yes, hotel guests have access to La Quinta Golf & Country Club. Click here to learn more.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella?

Paseo Marítimo de Marbella, Casco histórico de Marbella, La Almazara de Ronda Experience and more are nearby The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella.
