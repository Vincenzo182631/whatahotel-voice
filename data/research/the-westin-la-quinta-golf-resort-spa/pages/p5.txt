# Heavenly Spa by Westin™

## Awaken Your Senses

For those seeking total renewal, our spa hotel in Benahavís, Marbella offers a place of inspiration and peace. Dedicated to the passion found throughout the history of Andalusia, Heavenly Spa by Westin™...See More is a complete Wellness center where all your senses are touched. Covering 1,500 sqm, our spa in Marbella includes reception and changing rooms, six treatment rooms, two VIP rooms, a Mediterranean Sauna and Aromatic Steam Room, Cascade Showers, Two Sensorial Showers, an Ice Fountain, WESTINWORKOUT™ Fitness Studio open 24 hours a day with the latest equipment, a relax zone, and an outdoor pool with sun beds. For wellness seekers, this spa in a Marbella setting supports relaxation-led stays with thermal circuits, treatment rooms, fitness facilities, and peaceful surroundings. Guests often highlight the spa, pool, treatments, and relaxing atmosphere. Our massages are part of the wider spa and wellness offering at our hotel in Marbella.See Less

## Sprunch: Spa + Brunch

Pamper yourself with a unique experience, the Sprunch: Spa + Brunch. Savor a revitalizing brunch and access to the relaxing thermal circuit of our The Westin Heavenly Spa™. Ideal to giveaway or to enjoy with your beloved one, friends or family. When: Daily from 11 am to 4 pm. Where: At The Westin Heavenly Spa™ and at the Clubhouse Restaurant. Thermal circuit duration: 90 minutes. Price: 48 € per person (VAT included). More information and bookings: Email: heavenlyspa@westinlaquinta.com
