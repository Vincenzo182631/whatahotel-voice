## Accommodations

Private terraces and balconies offer captivating views across our 170 spacious hotel rooms in Benahavís, Marbella, including 163 guest rooms, 3 Junior Suites, 3 Executive Suites, and the Presidential Suite....See More Connecting rooms are available, providing added convenience for families and groups. Our Marbella accommodation combines elegant décor, soothing tones, and organic elements with the Westin Heavenly® Bed. Classic Rooms feature a king-size or two twin beds and views of the pool, gardens, or golf course. Deluxe Rooms provide larger living areas with a king or two double beds, while Classic and Deluxe Panoramic Rooms offer views of the pool, gardens, and mountains from private terraces. Junior Suites provide additional space, golf course views, and open floor plans with a sofa, dining table, and bureau. Guests often highlight the spacious rooms, comfortable beds, private outdoor spaces, and peaceful views.See Less

### Premium Rooms & Suites

Stay in one of our top category guest rooms and suites and embark on a sensory journey to wellbeing. Featuring an elegant soothing style and extra spaciousness, our most coveted rooms and suites are equipped with all that is needed to live a renovating experience. From the Westin Heavenly Bed®, which guarantees a deep restorative sleep, to the possibility to connect with adjacent rooms for a comfortable family stay, to private terraces with captivating views of the golf course, the gardens, and the mountains, and the city.
