# Welcome to The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella

## Well-being and Golf in Perfect Balance

Ideally located in the "Valley of Golf", The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavís, Marbella is a unique destination for golf lovers, with a 27-hole course plus a golf club and buggy hire. Surrounded...See More by the Sierra Blanca mountains and the Mediterranean Sea, this oasis of relaxation is only 10 minutes from Marbella. This 5-star hotel in Benahavís, Marbella, Spain features 170 rooms and suites in an elegant eclectic style, with neutral tones, organic elements, and the Heavenly Bed® for restorative sleep. For wellness-led stays, Heavenly Spa by Westin™ offers thermal circuits, treatment rooms, fitness facilities, and peaceful surroundings. Guests at our resort in Marbella, Spain will find Sunsa, The Club House, and three bars. Guests praise the helpful service, breakfast, golf, and relaxing atmosphere. This Marbella resort hotel also offers external locations and 950 m2 of indoor event space for weddings, social gatherings, and corporate meetings.See Less

## Sustainability

Sustainability and awareness of our environment is a focus point for Marriott International. At The Westin La Quinta we have been working in different eco initiatives to contribute to reduce the Environmental Impacts following best sustainable practices.

Sustainability Measures

Consumption and Suppliers: We work with local suppliers and source products from the region, supporting local businesses.

Food & Beverage: Food waste is tracked daily. Waste is separated at source, with dedicated containers for different waste streams and appropriate waste management practices to support waste recovery.

Responsible Sourcing: We follow the Responsible Seafood Program, using reference lists of species to support purchasing decisions and encourage the selection of abundant, lesser-known species.

Energy and Mobility: We use renewable electricity across the property. Electric vehicle charging points are available and compatible with electric vehicles.

Local Community Contribution: We support local community initiatives, including waste collection and cleaning activities, blood donation and clothing donation.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella?

The check-in time at The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 12.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella. The daily self parking fee is €12.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella?

The closest airport near The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella is Malaga-Costa del Sol Airport (AGP). AGP is located approximately 67.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella have an airport shuttle to Malaga-Costa del Sol Airport (AGP)?

Yes, The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella has an airport shuttle to Malaga-Costa del Sol Airport (AGP).

Does The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin La Quinta Golf Resort & Spa, Benahavis, Marbella has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
