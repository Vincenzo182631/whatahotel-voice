## FAQs for InterContinental Presidente Mexico City

Does InterContinental Presidente Mexico City have a pool?

No, InterContinental Presidente Mexico City does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental Presidente Mexico City?

At InterContinental Presidente Mexico City, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 10.5 USD and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 12 USD .

Parking is available for a fee.

Parking fee: 10.5 USD per day

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Presidente Mexico City?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Presidente Mexico City.

Pet policy description. We welcome pets , including dogs, cats, and small caged animals. However, for the comfort and safety of all guests, we request that dogs be kept on a leash or carried in public areas. We also request that you do not leave your pet unattended in the room.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Presidente Mexico City for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Presidente Mexico City have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Presidente Mexico City has 8 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Presidente Mexico City?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Presidente Mexico City.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Presidente Mexico City?

Check-in at InterContinental Presidente Mexico City is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Presidente Mexico City offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Presidente Mexico City offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Presidente Mexico City from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Presidente Mexico City is conveniently located near Benito Juarez International Airport (MEX), approximately 11.18 miles away (18 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

- Presidente Masaryk Avenue - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)

- Estadio Azteca - 15.53 miles away (25 kilometers)
- National Auditorium - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- Plaza de Toros Mexico - 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Presidente Mexico City puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
