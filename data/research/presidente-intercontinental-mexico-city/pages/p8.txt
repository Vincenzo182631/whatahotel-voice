# InterContinental Presidente Mexico CityDestination

Discover the charm of Mexico City with its rich culture and history. Enjoy a variety of things to do in Mexico City, from exquisite cuisine to exploring museums, parks, and historic streets.

1. Prime Polanco location

### Prime Polanco location

Our luxury hotel in Mexico City is conveniently located in Polanco, steps away from world-class restaurants and the Auditorio Nacional. We are close to Reforma, the Anthroplogy Museum, and Presidente Masaryk, an avenue well-known for luxury shopping. Our guest rooms boast exceptional views of Bosque de Chapultepec.

## Things to do

### Modern Art Museum

Paseo de la reforma, Bosque de Chapultepec, Mexico, Mexico City 11510

Visit the Modern Art Museum on the corner of Reforma and Gandhi Av. One of the most important modern art galleries in the city.

Learn more

### Lincoln Park

Luis G. Urbina St., Polanco, Mexico, Mexico City 11560

Parque Lincoln is great for walking or jogging, and for the temporary exhibitions.

### Castillo de Chapultepec

Paseo de la Reforma, Chapultepec, Mexico, D.F. 11510

Explore Castillo de Chapultepec, an architectural gem turned historical museum, nestled within Chapultepec Park—one of the places to visit in Mexico City.

### Chapultepec Zoo

Reforma, Chapultepec, Mexico, D.F. 11510

You can easily while away two hours in Chapultepec Zoo. Founded in 1923, it is home to more than 2000 animals. Entry is free.

### Palacio Nacional

Plaza de la Constitución S/N, Centro Histórico, Mexico, D.F. 06066

Dating back to 1693 the Mexican government palace sits on the site of Monteczuma's residence. Inside you'll find a collection of murals painted by Diego Rivera. One particularly famous mural condenses two thousand years of Mexican history. Admission is free.

### Chapultepec Park

Reforma, Miguel Hidalgo, Mexico, D.F. 11510

Inside the park you will find a circuit called 'La Milla' where the local people go to jog. Open from 5am to 5pm.

### Presidente Masaryk Avenue

Presidente Masaryk, Polanco, Mexico, D.F. 11510

If you are looking for the main shopping brands from New York, don't forget to visit this Avenue, one of the things to do in Mexico City. Also, a good place to stroll along and people-watch.

### Anthropology Museum

Paseo de la Reforma, Chapultepec, Mexico, D.F. 11510

The most important museum of Mexican history.

Learn more

### Estadio Azteca

Calzada de Tlalpan 3465, Santa Ursula Coapa, Mexico, Mexico City 14310

Tip: You can book your tickets with the concierge.
If you like football (soccer), don't forget to visit the historical Estadio Azteca.

### National Auditorium

Paseo de la Reforma, Miguel Hidalgo, Mexico, D.F. 11560

Tip: You can get your tickets three months in advance. Ask the concierge.
All the major performances and concerts are held in this spectacular auditorium.

### Plaza de Toros Mexico

Augusto Rodin No. 241, Col. Nochebuena, Mexico, D.F. 06720

Tip: Ask the concierge for the calendar and we can get the tickets in advance.
The biggest bullring in the world, where the most famous bullfighters ply their trade. There are two seasons a year - the best starts in October and finishes in May
