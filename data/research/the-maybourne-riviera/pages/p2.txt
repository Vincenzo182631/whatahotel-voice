## New Worlds of _Wonder_

From world-class therapies to bespoke fitness programs, we’ve crafted a holistic approach to wellness that blends ancient wisdom with the latest in innovative scientific research. Whether indulging in a sensory escape or embracing the latest technology, each of our wellness experiences is expertly tailored to balance, energise, and inspire.

## Visionary Wellbeing

### _Surrenne Belgravia_

Voted the World's Best Private Members' Club Spa 2024 and 2025, Surrenne Belgravia is a wellbeing Members’ Club in Belgravia dedicated to longevity and wellness. Spanning four floors, it brings together world-class fitness, holistic health care, and of-the-moment beauty therapies, sparking boundless wonder for the body and mind.

Every experience at Surrenne Belgravia is enhanced by the collective wisdom of our wellness experts, including Tracy Anderson and Rose Ferguson. This holistic approach nurtures both emotional and physical wellbeing, guiding Members and guests on a transformative journey toward lasting vitality.

## Our Wellness Experts and Partners

**Rose Ferguson**

Nutritionist and qualified functional medicine practitioner, Rose Ferguson, empowers wellness at Surrenne with holistic health strategies for a healthier, vibrant life.

**Tracy Anderson**

At Surrenne Belgravia, global fitness icon Tracy Anderson combines dance principles and athletic training to transform body and mind through her innovative methodology.

**Dr. Barbara Sturm**

Dr. Barbara Sturm’s powerful Sturmglow™ therapies debut at The Maybourne Beverly Hills with a curated menu of tension-taming, complexion-perfecting facials.

**Ross J. Barr**

Specialising in stress management and fertility, Ross J. Barr combines Five Element Acupuncture and Chinese medicine to treat modern ailments at Claridge’s.

**Josh Wood**

World-renowned colourist Josh Wood brings his signature red-carpet hairstyling to the comfort of Claridge’s Spa, in a by-appointment-only exclusive.
