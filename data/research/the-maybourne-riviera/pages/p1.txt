## Discover the Hotel

The striking modernist architecture of The Maybourne Riviera emerges from the rocky peninsula of Roquebrune-Cap-Martin as if forging its way into the glistening Mediterranean Sea. Perfectly positioned near Monaco, enjoy plunging views out to the azure waters. Explore the surrounding Côte d'Azur, a landscape wild with promise and discover the coastline dotted with quaint villages perched on cliff edges with winding pathways leading you through pine forests and maquis.

- Book Your Stay

## Our Signature Suites

Each of our 65 suites and studios opens to sweeping views of the Mediterranean, with private balconies that catch the light as the sun slowly sets over the glittering sea. Suites come with a range of complimentary inclusions - moments of quiet indulgence like an in-room massage, a sunset cruise and a seasonal welcome with a complimentary bottle of chilled champagne waiting on arrival. Our hotel is accessible to guests with disabilities, with 3 accessible rooms available for guests with reduced mobility (PRM).

## Events & Special Occasions

Presiding over the glittering Côte d’Azur, The Maybourne Riviera is a must-visit venue for celebrating special occasions – an architectural marvel shimmering against the sea and sky. Events unfold to the rhythm of the Mediterranean across distinctive spaces, ideal for private dinners, cocktail receptions or weddings. Each is complemented by breathtaking views, seasonal menus and thoughtful, personalised details.

## Every Stay Includes

- Full access to Surrenne Riviera, our wellbeing club, for the duration of your stay
- Daily English breakfast, served in your suite or studio, or at the Riviera Restaurant
- Arrival transfer from the Nice area\*
- Exclusive access to our private beach club, with complimentary sun loungers, during the summer season, subject to availability
- Complimentary shuttle service to and from Monaco and to our private beach club
- A selection of complimentary activities and masterclasses
- Seasonal welcome amenity & complimentary bottle of champagne served chilled in your studio on arrival\*
- Mini bar stocked with complimentary treats, including soft drinks and snacks
- Complimentary Wi-Fi
