## Kämp Spa _Treatments_

Every treatment at Kämp Spa is rooted in the same philosophy: that care is most effective when it's both purposeful and personal. Choose from our selection of signature massages, advanced facials, longevity-focused bodywork and express treatments.

Every spa treatment also includes 2-hour access to the spa area.

## Food & Drinks

We believe what nourishes you should also delight you. Every item on our menu is designed with intention: to energize, restore, hydrate, or support recovery. At Kämp Spa, what you eat and drink becomes part of your wellness journey, not a pause from it.

Our Spa Lounge draws on Nordic flavours and functional ingredients, from protein-rich bowls and soothing broths to signature drinks rooted in Finnish nature.
