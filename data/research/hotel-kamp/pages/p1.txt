# In the Heart _of the City -_ and Its Story

Overlooking Esplanadi Park in the heart of the city, Hotel Kämp has been Helsinki's grand hotel and meeting place since 1887. A second home to many of Finland's most iconic artists and thinkers, it has inspired songs, poems, and trailblazing ideas… But most of all, it has always been the place for locals and travelers alike to gather over food, drink, and the company that makes both better.

Stay the night, visit the spa, share a meal, or come for coffee — however you spend your time here, we'll look after every detail.

## Tradition and _Modern_ Wellness

Finnish wellness isn't a passing trend — it's centuries of sauna heat, cold water, and long winters that taught us stillness and rest. Kämp Spa combines Finnish traditions with modern wellness: heat and cold therapies, results-driven treatments, and technology designed to support real recovery. This is wellbeing the way Finns have always understood it — not an indulgence, but a rhythm you come back to.

### Eat, Drink, Kämp

Hotel Kämp has a storied history as Helsinki’s premier social and dining hub. Restaurant À La Kämp weaves together contemporary influences with authentic dishes from the archival menus of Hotel Kämp, some dating back more than a century. Kämp Bar, no stranger to a good story either, is known for hand-crafted cocktails and a deep affection for champagne. Kämp Delicatessen transforms from a neighborhood deli by day into an intimate wine bar by night.

## The Story _So Far_

Hotel Kämp is Helsinki's most historic hotel, but it's always been ahead of its time. Opened in 1887 as Finland's first European-style grand hotel, it introduced the city to elevators, moving pictures, and a taste for eating out — and quickly became the place where the city's artists, business leaders, and politicians gathered. It played a real part in turning Helsinki into the cosmopolitan capital it is today. That's still what Kämp does best: it is and always has been a place to gather, to explore, and simply to be merry.
