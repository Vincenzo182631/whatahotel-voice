## Reasons to Stay

There's more than one way to stay at Hotel Kämp. Each room and suite carries its own character, from twin rooms to grander suites suitable for entertaining. Whatever the occasion — a night passing through, a longer stay, or a family travelling together — there's a room built for it. Take a look through the categories to find yours.

## Connecting Rooms & Extra Beds

Combining two adjoining rooms or suites gives family and friends a chance to spend more time together, while still having plenty of space. Children can sleep in one room while parents can stay up a little longer, and friends can get together while keeping separate bathrooms.

All of our room categories can accommodate a third guest with an extra bed, and select rooms can host larger groups.

LearnMore

## Accessibility

For us, accessibility is a top priority. Public areas — the lobby, restaurant, corridors, and toilets — are designed with wheelchair access in mind. Rooms include features for guests with mobility or hearing impairments, and allergy-friendly rooms are also available.

Learnmore

## Pets Welcome

Hotel Kämp welcomes up to two pets per room, with a welcome gift waiting on arrival. Well-behaved dogs are also welcome at À La Kämp and Kämp Bar. Esplanadi Park is right outside, and the city's dog parks aren't far beyond it.

Learnmore

## Christmas Room

Celebrate Christmas at Kämp with festive decorations, prepared in your room before you arrive — a real Christmas tree, seasonal flowers, Kämp's signature mulled wine, and small gifts for your stay.
