# Kämp Bar

Located in the heart of Helsinki, Kämp Bar has long been the city's social hub — the backdrop for celebrations, ceremonies, and the important moments in between.

Newly restored alongside the rest of the hotel, Kämp Bar reopens with over a century of legacy behind it, ready for what's next.

The cocktail menu spans era-defining signatures, beloved classics, and a deep affection for champagne. Restaurant À La Kämp's full dinner menu is also served at Kämp Bar.

Welcome to Kämp Bar, where Helsinki has come together since 1887.
