## Celebrate at Kämp

Every December, Helsinki fills with lights — and snow, if we're lucky. Hotel Kämp marks the season with festive decorations, menus, events — and of course, a Christmas tree.

Planning a private festive gathering or a corporate holiday event? Our event spaces are available to host your celebration.
