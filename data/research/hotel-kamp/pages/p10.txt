# About  _Hotel Kämp_

Hotel Kämp has been Helsinki's grand hotel and meeting place since 1887. Since the beginning, it has brought together locals and travelers alike — people with an eye for the finer things and a taste for good company.

Hotel Kämp has grown alongside Helsinki for close to 140 years, and that history still shapes its character today as a modern luxury hotel. The city around us changes, but how we welcome you doesn't.

## The Story of Kämp

**A Landmark of Helsinki**

Hotel Kämp has stood as a landmark through Helsinki's growth from a provincial town into an international, European capital. Its colourful history is closely intertwined with the development of the city itself.

**A Place for Culture and Society**

For over a century, Kämp has been where Finnish artists, business leaders, and politicians met — from the Symposium circle's late-night debates to the deals made in its private cabinets. That history shaped the country's cultural and social life as much as it shaped Kämp itself.

**A History Worth Carrying Forward**

Hotel Kämp's rich and prestigious past continues to define the soul and character of the hotel. Tradition remains an important part of the experience, and connect today's Kämp with its history.

**Heritage Meets Modern Comfort**

Today, Hotel Kämp combines its heritage with the modern comforts, services and functionality expected of a contemporary grand hotel – balancing tradition with everything needed for an exceptional stay.

## Hotel Kämp Today

The refurbished Hotel Kämp has 179 rooms and suites, from Deluxe Rooms up to the Mannerheim Suite with a maximum floor space of 258 m².

The hotel also has ten event spaces for meetings and celebrations, including the Mirror Room, the most storied ballroom in Helsinki.

## Dining at Kämp

Hotel Kämp has been Helsinki's dining and social hub since the late 1800s, and that role now lives across three spaces.

**Restaurant À La Kämp** reworks the hotel's own archival menus through French technique and a Finnish eye for ingredients, seasonal and often local.

**Kämp Bar** is known for hand-crafted cocktails and a deep affection for champagne.

**Kämp Delicatessen** is a neighborhood deli by day, and turns into an intimate wine bar by night.

## Service at Kämp

Our 24-hour room service and concierge are here for whatever your stay needs. Turndown service is included in your room and laundry service is also available. Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel. For guests arriving by car, our valet will take care of parking, while our bellboys bring your luggage to your room. ## Kämp Spa

Rooted in Finnish tradition and shaped by modern longevity science, Kämp Spa is a wellness destination within Hotel Kämp.

Heat and cold therapies, non-invasive technologies, and personalized treatments work to restore energy and balance, support circulation, and promote long-term vitality.

Traditional Finnish, steam, and infrared saunas complete the contrast therapy alongside a vitality pool and cold plunge, with the Spa Lounge on hand for food and drink after.

The hotel’s new State-of-the-Art gym is also located adjacent to the spa.
