## A Suite for Every Stay

Whether you're planning a weekend for two or hosting a larger gathering, there's a suite to match. Hotel Kämp has eight suite categories, each with a character of its own — a park view, a kitchen, a private sauna, even a grand piano. All suites include breakfast served in the suite, access to Kämp Spa and Upper Lounge, and other exclusive services.

Suite guests have exclusive access to the Upper Lounge from 2–6 PM, with small bites and beverages available.

## Here For You

Hotel Kämp's service tradition goes back to 1887, and it still shapes how the hotel runs today. Our concierge carries that tradition forward, on hand for whatever the stay calls for. Whatever you're looking for in Helsinki, the concierge can help arrange it — from a restaurant reservation to something further off the usual path.

## Connecting _Rooms_ and Extra Beds

Combining two adjoining rooms or suites gives family and friends a chance to spend more time together, while still having plenty of space. Children can sleep in one room while parents can stay up a little longer, and friends can get together while keeping separate bathrooms.

All of our room categories can accommodate a third guest with an extra bed, and select rooms can host larger groups.

## Accessibility

For us, accessibility is a top priority. Public areas — the lobby, restaurant, corridors, and toilets — are designed with wheelchair access in mind. Rooms include features for guests with mobility or hearing impairments, and allergy-friendly rooms are also available.

Learnmore

## Pets Welcome

Hotel Kämp welcomes up to two pets per room, with a welcome gift waiting on arrival. Well-behaved dogs are also welcome at À La Kämp and Kämp Bar. Esplanadi Park is right outside, and the city's dog parks aren't far beyond it.
