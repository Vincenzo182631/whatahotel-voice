Traditional materials and design create an exclusive enclave modeled on Andean village life, but five-star lavishness is also paramount at this intimate and secluded hotel on the banks of the Urubamba and close to the Ollantaytambo train station. The atmosphere is ultra-tranquil (no TV's!) and eco-conscious, with gourmet and organic Peruvian fare.
