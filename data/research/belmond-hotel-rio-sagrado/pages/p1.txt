Cradled between Andean mountains, Rio Sagrado rests in the Sacred Valley of the Incas, where ancient rhythms still guide daily life. For thousands of years, the Urubamba River has nourished these fertile lands; its waters flowing past terraced fields first cultivated in 800 BCE.

Along the valley’s winding paths lie storied villages, each linked by the Qhapaq Ñan, the Inca Empire’s extraordinary network of roads. Here, master weavers continue the work of their ancestors, handcrafting intricate textiles.

At Rio Sagrado, every view, taste and touch offers a direct connection to the heart of the Sacred Valley.
