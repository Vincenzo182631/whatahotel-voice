A Downtown Hotel Near Tianjin’s Most Enticing Attractions
Experiences
Located downtown near the railway station, this hotel with a swimming pool in Tianjin offers the perfect location for exploring China’s third-largest city. Drink in a kaleidoscope of architectural styles, from the Western-inspired marvels of Italian Style Street to the modern engineering of the Tianjin Eye. Cultural icons like the Great Wall, natural landmarks such as Pan Mountain and the eclectic architecture of the Five Great Avenues are just a few of the nearby destinations awaiting discovery.

A Hotel Near the Five Avenues and Tianjin Eye
Attractions
The port of Tianjin has been one of China’s key commercial centers for more than 600 years, drawing people from around the world. A look at the city’s diverse architectural styles is a direct reflection of this past. While Tianjin continues to be a leader in trade and investment, it’s also home to the Tianjin Medical University and attractions including Ancient Culture Street, the Five Avenues and Tianjin Eye.

Planning Your Trip
Though it’s just half an hour from Beijing via high-speed train, beguiling Tianjin packs a unique, East-meets-West punch that’s all its own. The former concession port city boasts a promenade-friendly river, European-flavored neighborhoods and rich Chinese culture.

Art Tour
Art Tour is for guests who stay in The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin to define the unforgettable and artful moments in the journey. Guests can enjoy a tour of art appreciation-themed “East Meets West” without leaving the hotel. The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin presents more than 1,200 pieces of artwork on display with rich background stories from the western to the eastern world. Our Ladies and Gentlemen selected the most famous pieces and will take guests through the Art Tour for half an hour to introduce the stories.

Ritz Kids Tianjin
Young guests feel special the moment they arrive at The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin. The hotel’s Ritz Kids program offers a family-friendly experience guided by the culture, history and setting of Tianjin. The program, designed for kids ages four to eight, includes welcome and in-room amenities, special menus and an array of things to do each day.
Participation in Ritz Kids must be reserved at least three days in advance. Please call (86 22)5857 8888.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-10:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin have a pool on-site?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin have an on-site spa?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Tianjin is located on-site at The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin.
What are some local attractions and things to do near The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin?
Great Wall (Huang Ya Guan), Ancient Culture Street, Haiher River, Shi Family Grand Courtyard, Tianjin Culture Center and more are nearby The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin.
