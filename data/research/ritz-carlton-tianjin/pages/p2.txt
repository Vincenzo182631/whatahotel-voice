Executive Residences
The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin
Adjacent to a luxurious residential area, high-class business district and landmarks including the Five Avenues and Ancient Street, the Residences is situated in a prime location. Located near to the hotel, the Residences boasts its own lobby, elevators and casual dining facilities such as Breakfast Room, Media Room, Kids’ Room, Cigar Lounge, Gym and Sauna Rooms. The Executive Residences features 118 luxurious accommodations, including a variety of single, double and triple units, ranging from 77 to 196 square metres in area.

Luxury hotel suites in Tianjin city ranging from 77 to 123 square metres; Comfortable and spacious rooms; Monthly Residences Activity; Complimentary wireless and wired Internet access. For inquiries, please call +86 22 5857 8888
Two Bedroom Residence
Ranging from 123 to 185 square metres; second bedroom with twin beds; stand- alone air purifier and inbuilt air purification system; separate living area and fully equipped gourmet kitchen. For inquiries, please call +86 22 5857 8888.
Three Bedroom Residence

Club Lounge Offerings
The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin
Designed as a residential concept, the Club Lounge features five distinct areas: an open kitchen, a private dining room, a family room, a library and a bar. Available 24 hours a day, the lounge features five complimentary culinary presentations, children’s activities, computer stations, and more. Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Tianjin. Let our staff pamper you with personalised services to create unique, valuable memories. In doing this, we have customised several colourful activities for guests and their children at Club Lounge.
Exclusive to Club Level Guests
The weekly, 30-minute parties are organised by Club Lounge staff and will take place based on customers’ preferences and needs. These fun-filled and colourful events include Mid-Autumn Festival Riddles, Easter Egg Drawing Class, Cube Performances, Chinese Class, Hand-made Present Class, Magic Performance and many more.
Characteristic Flavours
Between 5 p.m. and 8 p.m. each day, our Chinese, Western and Japanese cuisine experts will create flavourful delicacies for you at the Open Kitchen. Special treats that will stimulate your taste buds include, but are not limited to, Northern Chinese Fried Dumplings, Sichuan Tasty Noodles, Italian Panini and Mexican Fried Corn Rolls.
Little Ladies and Gentlemen
To enrich your cherished children’s public holiday experience, our Family Entertainment Room will be turned into Children’s Zone, where your little ones can enjoy a wide variety of fun activities, such as the Drawing Class and Video Class, in the comfortable company of their parents.
