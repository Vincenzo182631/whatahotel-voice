### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE. The daily valet fee is $71.50 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE is Hollywood Burbank Airport (BUR). BUR is located approximately 16.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
