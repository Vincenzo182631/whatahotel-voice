A culinary journey down the Pan American highway

## Sendero

Featuring sweeping views of downtown Los Angeles from its elevated perch on the hotel’s 24th floor, Sendero is a culinary journey south down the Pan American highway. This downtown L.A. LIVE restaurant offers a collection of dining experiences dedicated to the cuisine found across Latin America – from the seafood-rich Baja coastline and plains of Argentina to cosmopolitan urban capitals.

### Día de los Muertos in The Agave Library

Celebrate Día de los Muertos from October 6–31 in the Agave Library with a chef-curated six-course menu, premium Don Julio Tequila pairings, breathtaking Downtown Los Angeles views, and a stunning ofrenda honoring the spirit of the season.

Learn More

### Salsa & Bachata Nights in Corteza

Join us in Corteza for a spirited Día de los Muertos edition of Salsa & Bachata Nights. Sip, dance, and celebrate as professional instructors guide you through the rhythms of the evening, complemented by a welcome cocktail, festive flavors, and an atmosphere inspired by the colorful traditions of the holiday.

Learn More

### Live Music in Sendero

Dine on a high note with live musical performances by talented local artists every Friday and Saturday evening. Enjoy vibrant rhythms, stunning city views, and an unforgettable culinary experience.

### Thanksgiving Day Brunch

Gather with friends and family to indulge in a delicious Thanksgiving meal featuring an artful twist on classic holiday dishes. Each of Sendero’s dining spaces offers a delicious experience featuring special holiday prix-fixe menus.

Reserve Now

### Christmas Day Brunch

Celebrate Christmas Day with loved ones and savor a festive brunch featuring endless chef-inspired stations and holiday favorites.

Reserve Now
