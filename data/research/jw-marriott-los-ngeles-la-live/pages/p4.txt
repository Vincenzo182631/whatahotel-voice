## Explore the Cultural and Entertainment Heart of L.A.

Radiating across six city blocks, L.A. LIVE is anchored by Crypto.com Arena – home to the Peacock Theater – host of the annual Primetime Emmy Awards and a multitude of live performances. Our Los Angeles hotel is also close to the GRAMMY Museum featuring interactive exhibits and a schedule of live shows, as well as additional nightclubs, restaurants, a bowling alley, and movie theaters.

### The JW Garden

Rooted in Marriott family tradition, the JW Garden is inspired by JW’s founder J. Willard Marriott who took daily nature walks, along with his wife Alice, who grew fruits, vegetables and herbs for her legendary Sunday night dinners. Today, we grow fresh produce and herbs in our own JW Garden on the hotel’s fourth floor, which are used in our recipes, cocktails and more.

Explore the Best of Downtown Los Angeles

### Your Gameday Base

Discover unbeatable access to the city’s biggest sporting moments – from global soccer showdowns to major league matchups and the world’s largest tournaments. Our hotel is within walking distance to pro basketball and hockey action at Crypto.com Arena, just 10 minutes from Dodger Stadium and the LA Memorial Coliseum, and 20 minutes from SoFi Stadium.

### L.A.’s Biggest Nights

Experience the pulse of L.A.’s entertainment scene just steps from our doors. From chart topping artists to rising stars, L.A. LIVE delivers nonstop performances and the city’s most iconic red carpet moments—including globally televised award shows celebrating music, television and film.

### Artful Adventures

Find inspiration within the diverse museums of downtown Los Angeles, where modern installations, contemporary collections, and renowned masters are on display. Take in the unique honeycomb exterior of The Broad Museum, where postwar and contemporary art is showcased under natural light. Or visit the soon to open Lucas Museum of Narrative Art, exploring all forms of visual storytelling.
