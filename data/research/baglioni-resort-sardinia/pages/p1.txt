## Baglioni Resort Sardinia

Reconnect with nature in a stunning resort setting within the Tavolara Marine Reserve, a short distance from the incomparable Lu Impostu beach, and one of the best wine regions in Sardinia.

## Luxury in the Heart of Nature

Located on Sardinia’s northeast coast, this peaceful resort offers a sublime natural landscape without peer in the Mediterranean, including the Tavolara Marine Reserve and Lu Impostu, one of the island’s most beautiful & exclusive beaches. You’ll find 78 spacious rooms & suites. Each elegant apartment features modern furnishings and soft colors that create a perfect oasis of elegance and comfort. Culinary highlights include the Michelin-starred "Gusto by Sadler"; the Mediterranean-themed Ruia Restaurant; a Pool Bar and a Sunset Terrace Bar.
