## Edmonton's _celebrated_ chateau on the river

Since 1915, Fairmont Hotel Macdonald has effortlessly delivered timeless luxury in the heart of Edmonton. Nestled above the river valley, the 5-star luxury hotel is proudly one of Canada’s iconic castle hotels, with a charm and elegance that has earned it recognition as one of the city’s most sought-after destinations, as well as a prestigious 2026 MICHELIN Key and a place among the Condé Nast Traveler 2025 Readers’ Choice Awards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Hotel Macdonald located?

Fairmont Hotel Macdonald is located in the heart of downtown Edmonton, Alberta at 10065 100th Street.

What are some of the property amenities at Fairmont Hotel Macdonald?

Our luxury amenities feature a two-story health club with a saltwater pool, and whirlpool, outdoor sun deck, squash court, massage services, and all of the equipment one would expect from a state-of-the-art facility.

Is parking available at Fairmont Hotel Macdonald?

Fairmont Hotel Macdonald offers a trusted parking experience, featuring both valet and self-parking options, complete with seamless in and out privileges.

Are there accessible rooms at Fairmont Hotel Macdonald?

Our accessible guest room is thoughtfully designed to ensure your comfort and safety during your stay, featuring a spacious layout overlooking the river valley.

Is Fairmont Hotel Macdonald pet-friendly?

Home to Canine Ambassador, Hendricks, Fairmont Hotel Macdonald is delighted to welcome your favorite pets along for the experience.
