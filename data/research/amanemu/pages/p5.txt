\\
**Discover sake**

Crisp, dry, fruity, punchy, subtle, clean – the spectrum of flavours that can be identified in sake – and the vocabulary used to describe them – is as rich and varied as the wold of fine wine.

\\
**Ise Jingu shrine breakfast**

Start your refreshing day with Amanemu’s signature breakfast, enjoyed while overlooking the serene Ago Bay and served in a handcrafted white wooden box inspired by the traditional breakfast offerings near Ise Grand Shrine.

\\
**Ise Shima Roman Course**

An extraordinary gourmet experience designed to showcase the most extraordinary flavours of both Japan and the wider world, Amanemu’s Roman course includes caviar, truffles, lobster, wagyu, abalone and more.

\\
**Tea tasting**

Amanemu’s serene and elegant library provides the setting for tasting tour of the region’s plethora of teas. Discover an extraordinary variety of flavours, sampling the rare blends and brews of Mie Prefecture.
