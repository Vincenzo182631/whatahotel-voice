**Ama Hut Lunch**

Ago Bay has been home to the Ama for centuries – a community of female free-divers who gather fresh bounty from the seabed. Enjoy a fresh charcoal-grilled seafood lunch while listening to stories shared by Ama divers and visit an Ama hut.

\\
**Ama-no-Iwato Waterfall Purification**

At a sacred site associated with the legends of Ise Jingu, experience a traditional waterfall purification ritual. Guided by a local expert, let the flowing water cleanse body and mind as you embrace a moment of reflection and spiritual renewal.

\\
**Ise Grand Shrine (Gekū) Visit & Shrine Approach Stroll**

The Gekū (Outer Shrine) of the Ise Grand Shrine is dedicated to the deities of food, clothing and shelter – during a half-day visit guests can enjoy a peaceful stroll along its ancient approach lined with traditional shops for an experience rich in history and connection.

\\
**Ise Grand Shrine (Naikū) Visit & Oharaimachi Stroll**

Enjoy a special half-day visit to the revered Naikū (Inner Shrine) of Ise Grand Shrine, followed by a leisurely stroll through the charming Oharaimachi district, known for its traditional streetscape and local specialties.

\\
**Become a Shrine Parishioner**

A ujiko is someone who supports and venerates the deity of a local shrine. Guests can gain insight into their enduring customs and the quiet spirit of Japanese devotion by experiencing traditional rituals such as purification, offerings, and receiving a goshuin stamp.

\\
**Ago Bay Cruise**

Experience the beauty of Ago Bay bathed in golden sunset hues on an elegant, private sunset cruise. Enjoy a moment of serenity as the sea glows with the last light of day. Ago Bay Charter Cruise and Ago Bay Charter Fishing are also available.

\\
**Authentic Pearl Jewelry Making**

Craft a one-of-a-kind accessory using authentic Ise-Shima pearls, renowned for their beauty and heritage. After the experience, enjoy a moment to explore the picturesque surroundings of Kashikojima at your own pace.

\\
**Gikakushi Paper Craft**

Gikakushi is an imitation leather washi paper known for its refined texture and the gentle way it catches the light. In this workshop, guests can enjoy crafting their own cherished item using this material that dates back nearly 350 years.

\\
**Gokasho-Sengensan Hiking**

Feel the sea breeze and connect with the natural beauty of the season with a guided walk through serene forest trails to the summit of Gokasho-Sengensan, where sweeping views of Gokasho-Bay and the ria coastline unfold.

**Sai-shi Mai Dancing**

At dusk, a mystical ritual called Sai-shi Mai is performed on the Sunken Terrace against the serene backdrop of Ago Bay. Embrace the prayers and joy in each movement, from those wishing for world peace to those seeking a bountiful harvest, while taking a moment to share gratitude for the blessings of the sun, and those performing the sacred dance.

_Available every Tuesday and Saturday_

**Stories from Ise-Shima – Ama Divers**

Amanemu has close bonds with an array of local guides and merchants, who often visit the resort to share stories about the Ise-Shima region. Learn about the lifestyles and culture of the region's female divers in this informative session led by a local Ama.

**Japanese Calligraphy**

Select a kanji character inspired by Ise-Shima and create your own calligraphy artwork on traditional shikishi paper. A master calligrapher will provide step-by-step guidance, offering an engaging introduction to this timeless Japanese art form.

**Roasting the Shima Arare**

Discover Shima Arare, a traditional glutinous rice cracker beloved across the region for generations. Watch a local Ama diver demonstrate the traditional cooking method, then savour the freshly roasted arare before trying your hand at the technique, passed down through generations.
