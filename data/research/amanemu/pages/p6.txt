\\
**Forest & Water – Anniversary Journey**

Inspired by the rhythms of Ise Shima’s natural surrounds, Amanemu’s anniversary journey begins with grounding ritual harnessing a blend of Japanese botanicals, blended from native flora and fauna, combined with warm forest herbs. The body softens as warmth and aroma ease tension and invite calm, accompanied by flowing massage techniques inspired by ocean movement.Amanemu’s natural hot spring water head treatment follows, a signature moment that quiets the mind and deepens clarity.

\\
**Essence of Tokowaka**

Inspired by the Shikinen Sengu, a sacred ritual of Ise Grand Shrine, this program combines six yoga sessions with six treatments to gently guide body and mind to better align with the elements and chakras, uncovering an innate rhythm.

\\
**Sacred Dance (Saishi-mai) Lesson**

Through the traditional Shinto ritual dance passed down through generations, this lesson offers an opportunity to connect with local culture. Based on the _Asahi-mai_, a dance that honours the blessings of life and nature, practice a series of elegant motions in harmony with the sounds of _gagaku_ (ancient court music).

\\
**Forest Bathing**

Forest bathing offers a chance to step into nature to let go of thoughts and gently reconnect with your body and mind. As you walk and breathe in the vitality of the greenery, meditation and breathing exercises create an overall feeling of calm.

\\
**Medicinal Bathing**

In the Aman Spa’s private onsen pavilions, new medicinal baths blend Japan’s traditional hot spring culture with the healing properties of herbs and botanicals of Japanese Kampo philosophy. Three types of therapeutic baths are based on Eastern medicine’s essential elements, designed to suit each guests’ needs and preferences.

\\
**Acupuncture and Shiatsu**

Traditional Japanese therapies, acupuncture and shiatsu help regulate the nervous system, enhance the body’s natural healing and benefit mental wellness and anti-aging.

While shiatsu involves applying pressure mainly with the fingers and palms to relax muscle tension and restore the body’s balance, acupuncture treatments consider the condition of the entire body. Moxibustion is also offered, using dried mugwort (moxa) to apply direct or indirect thermal stimulation to the skin in turn warming the body and promoting the flow of Qi (energy) to alleviate various ailments.

\\
**Seasonal spa journey**

Designed to harmonise with the annual rhythms of nature, Amanemu’s seasonal spa journeys use different kampo herbal formulations and local ingredients to achieve specific wellness goals in line with the needs of the season.

\\
**One-day wellness programme**

For guests who wish to explore Immersions without committing to a multi-day programme, spa specialists will devise a personalised itinerary offering a condensed Immersion experience.
