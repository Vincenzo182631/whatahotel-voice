## Immerse Yourself in Luxury at Our Downtown Nashville Hotel on Broadway

Stay in the middle of the action at Grand Hyatt Nashville. This iconic Broadway hotel features one of the highest outdoor rooftop bars in Music City, a rooftop pool, a spa and seven food and beverage experiences. Located near The Gulch and Midtown, our downtown hotel puts you close to a rich music scene, exquisite dining and top attractions in Nashville, Tennessee.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Relax and unwind in one of our 591 upscale hotel rooms on Broadway, including 53 suites, during your stay in downtown Nashville, Tennessee. Our luxury rooms and suites feature gorgeous hardwood floors, floor-to-ceiling windows for plenty of natural light and upscale bath amenities.

## EXPLORE

Grand Club Lounge

Discover a refined retreat that captures the spirit of Nashville sophistication in the Grand Club Lounge, located on the Lobby Level. This inviting yet elevated space blends contemporary design with Southern warmth, featuring open seating, a private conference room, and a serene outdoor terrace overlooking the energy of downtown. Savor a curated selection of breakfast offerings, all-day refreshments, evening hors d’oeuvres, and premium beer and wine. Access to the Grand Club Lounge is reserved exclusively for registered guests of Club Access rooms and suites, as well as select World of Hyatt tiers.

24-Hour Fitness Center

Re-energize during your Nashville stay in our 24-hour fitness center, complete with weight equipment, cardio machines and three Pelotons, each with dual-sided pedals with SPD clips with toe cage.

Meetings & Events

We're proud to be the No. 1 Event Top Meeting Hotel in North America in 2023 and 2024. Explore our 84,000 square feet of space, meeting and event rooms in the heart of downtown Nashville.

R+R WELLNESS

Paying homage to the culture of downtown’s Nashville Yards and railroad region, R+R stands for Recharge and Replenish. Amidst the vibrant energy of Music City, our hotel’s luxury spa serves as a perfectly timed invitation to slow down and breathe in the moment. Redefine the meaning of relaxation through personalized wellness experiences – there’s never been a better time to focus on you.

Outdoor Pool

Soak up the season with a swim in our heated outdoor pool or relax on the sundeck, where panoramic views of downtown Nashville set the scene. The pool and Solstice poolside restaurant are open seasonally from early spring through late October, weather permitting, on our 5th floor Lifestyle Level.

### Pets Are Welcome

Life is never ruff with your furry best friend by your side. Our hotel will only allow dogs in your room or suite. Add “PETS” to request field when booking your stay. For stays beyond 30 nights a deep cleaning fee will be at the hotel’s discretion.

#### Pet Fees

- Stays 1-29 days: **$100** _/ STAY_

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  50Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 75Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.
