# Getting Here

- From Dali Fengyi Airport

## From Dali Fengyi Airport

## PRIVATE TRANSFER:

Private transfer is available at CNY 680 per vehicle, one way, inclusive of tax and gratuity. The journey takes approximately 70 minutes. To arrange your transfer, please contact our Concierge at +86 (0)872 699 8888 prior to arrival or departure.

## TRAIN:

Private transfer is available at CNY 580 per vehicle, one way, inclusive of tax and gratuity. Travel time is approximately 60 minutes. For arrangements, please contact our Concierge at +86 (0)872 699 8888 in advance.

## DRIVING:

From Dali Fengyi Airport, exit and turn left onto Airport Road. Continue for approximately 5 km, then merge onto Binhai Avenue. Follow along the edge of Erhai Lake before turning right onto Erhe North Road, then right again onto Xingsheng North Road. Continue onto Wanhua Road, then join National Road 214 (G214) toward Cangshan. Turn onto Wanfu North Road.The Dali EDITION will be on your right.

## PARKING INFORMATION:

Complimentary parking is available for in-house guests. For visitors, parking is complimentary for the first 30 minutes, followed by CNY 10 per hour for the first three hours (partial hours charged as a full hour), with a maximum of CNY 50 per entry per day.
