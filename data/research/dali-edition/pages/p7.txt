# The Spa

**The Spa**

View Details

Share

The Spa at The Dali EDITION combines thoughtful design with expert treatments using mineral salts, seasonal blossoms, and selected botanicals. Each experience restores energy, clarity, and a sense of effortless balance.

To embody “Nature to Wellness,” we curate the finest seasonal native ingredients (pines, blooms, etc.) from Dali’s Cangshan Mountain, support sustainable local foraging communities, and preserve the region’s ecological integrity, while showcasing Yunnan’s unique natural heritage at our spa.

OPEN DAILY10:00 AM – 10:00 PM

last appointment at 9:30 PM

For more information call +86 (0)872 266 0180 or email eh.dlueb.ge@editionhotels.com.

**Gym**

Share

The Gym offers a bright, inviting space designed for strength, movement, and balance. Floor-to-ceiling windows fill the room with natural light and showcase views of the surrounding landscape, while a dedicated studio provides space for yoga or stretching, Featuring thoughtfully curated equipment in a setting that is both energizing and restorative.

OPEN DAILY24 HOURS

Staff on duty7:00 AM – 10:00 PM
