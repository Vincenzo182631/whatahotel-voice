# Services & Amenities

The Dali EDITION creates a new, unique, original experience that combines luxury and lifestyle with authentic design, outstanding dining and entertainment as well as friendly and personalized service.

### For further information

Please contact our Guest Experience Team at +86 (0)872 699-8888.

GUEST SERVICES

- 150 unique rooms and suites
- Room Service
- Concierge Service
- Complimentary use of the 24-hour fitness facilities
- Complimentary high-speed wireless internet access throughout the hotel
- Unique dining venues: MARKET at EDITION, KUSHO, LOBBY BAR
- Ballroom and outdoor event space
- State-of-the-art audio and visual services available for private functions
- Multi-lingual staff

EXECUTIVE SERVICES

- 1565 square meters of indoor meeting space including ballrooms and studios
- Exceptional outdoor lawns for events and weddings
- Video conferencing
- Executive car services available
- Executive butler services available
- Customized banquet menus for private events, luncheons, dinner parties and business meetings
- State-of-the-art audio and visual equipment
- Complimentary high-speed wireless internet access throughout the hotel

GYM

- State-of-the-art fitness equipment including cardiovascular machines
- Personal training sessions available on request
- 2 Peloton bikes
- Free weights
- Stretching area
- Yoga room
- Complimentary towels
- Headphones and refreshments
- Accessible 24 hours a day
- Indoor pool

SPA

- 5 treatment rooms and suites
- Male and female changing rooms with saunas, steam rooms and jacuzzi
- Deep relaxation lounge
- Extensive retail collection of luxury spa products

ACCESSIBILITY

- Accessible rooms available for certain room types
- Additional accessibility information available upon request
