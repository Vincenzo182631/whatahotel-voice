# KUSHO

**KUSHO**

RESERVE

Step into an intimate, smoke-kissed haven where traditional Japanese flavors meet the primal allure of wood-fired charcoal where we harness the intense heat of local aromatic hardwoods and fire to craft bold, smoky robata-grilled offerings-each bite infused with the essence of flame.

Inspired by Japan’s lively izakaya culture, our menu balances smoky indulgence with delicate precision, showcasing local produce from charred vegetables kissed with umami to succulent grilled meats from native poultry and pork. And from our specialty bar, sip on curated Japanese inspired cocktails and beverages.

For additional information please contact us at +86 (0)872 699 8888 or email us eh.dlueb.ge@editionhotels.com.

- OPEN DAILY 5:30 PM – 12:00 AM

**Lobby Bar**

RESERVE

A theater of panoramic theater where the landscape vibrant tapestry unfolds before you, and the art of arrival is celebrated with quiet grandeur where the space evolves throughout the day.

Whether you are pausing between adventures, awaiting a guest, or dedicating an evening to the sublime pleasure of doing nothing at all, this is your sanctuary. A place to connect, to reflect, or simply to be, wrapped in comfort and framed by an ever-changing, unforgettable vista.

For additional information please contact us at +86 (0)872 699 8888 or email us at eh.dlueb.ge@editionhotels.com.

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY
- Bar service 12:00 PM – 12:00 AM
- Afternoon Tea 2:30 PM – 5:30 PM

**Market at EDITION**

RESERVE

Here at MARKET, we intend to provide a dining experience that honors the rich flavors of the land and reflect our commitment to crafting dishes inspired by seasonal, sustainable and locally sourced ingredients within Yunnan and our immediate neighbors.

At MARKET, every plate tells a story, a marriage of classical techniques with a contemporary twist to deliver a truly and unforgettable dining experience. From our thoughtfully curated wine list to our artfully prepared dishes, we invite you to indulge in the harmony of flavor, craftmanship and sustainability mother nature has given us.

For additional information please contact us at +86 (0)872 699 8888 or email us at eh.dlueb.ge@editionhotels.com.
