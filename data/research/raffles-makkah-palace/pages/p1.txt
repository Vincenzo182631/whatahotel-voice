## Living in a sacred city   Makkah

Raffles Makkah Palace stands as the pinnacle of luxury in Makkah — an all-suite haven offering majestic, unobstructed views of the Holy Kaaba, just steps from Al Masjid Al Haram. Known for its award-winning service and timeless elegance, the hotel is a sanctuary of privacy and serenity, favored by royalty, dignitaries, and travelers seeking a deeply spiritual experience. Awarded as the Best All-Suite Hotel and home to the Best Hotel Spa in Saudi Arabia, Raffles Makkah Palace blends authentic Arabian hospitality with refined sophistication. Each suite is generously spacious, exquisitely designed, and complemented by personalized royal service and panoramic sacred views in one of the world’s holiest destinations.

## Frequently asked questions

### What amenities can guests expect at Raffles Makkah Palace?

Guests can expect an ultra-luxury experience featuring spacious all-suite accommodations, 24-hour butler service, an award-winning spa, a fully equipped health club, and a selection of refined dining experiences. The hotel also offers exclusive prayer facilities overlooking Al Masjid Al Haram and an exceptional location just steps from the Holy Kaaba.

### How close is Raffles Makkah Palace to Al Masjid Al Haram?

Raffles Makkah Palace is approximately a 5-minute walk from Al Masjid Al Haram, offering direct and convenient access. Guests also benefit from dedicated elevators within the Clock Towers Complex that lead directly to the ground floor, as well as private elevators within the hotel exclusively for Raffles Makkah Palace guests, ensuring a seamless and comfortable journey.

### What dining options are available at Raffles Makkah Palace?

Guests can enjoy a refined selection of dining experiences, featuring international cuisine and regional specialties, thoughtfully crafted to deliver exceptional quality and variety.

### Why is Raffles Makkah Palace considered a spiritual haven?

Raffles Makkah Palace offers a serene atmosphere and an exceptional location overlooking the Holy Kaaba, creating a deeply spiritual and tranquil experience for guests seeking reflection and connection.

### When is the best time to visit Makkah for a stay at Raffles?

The best time to visit is during the cooler months, from October to March, allowing for comfortable exploration of the city and its sacred sites.
