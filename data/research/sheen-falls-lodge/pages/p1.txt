# Visiting from United States

## Plan your trip to Ireland

Located in the heart of Kenmare, County Kerry, Sheen Falls Lodge promises the warmest of welcomes for those arriving from the United States.

Arrive to the Lodge and immerse yourself in the hotel’s luxurious surroundings and make our choice of guest rooms, suites, and cottages your home away from home.With 5 star self catering options available as well as luxury hotel accommodation to suit all your needs.

As 5 star hotel located in the heart of Kenmare, Ireland, Guests will find arrange of activities and things to do during your stay with us, from our on-site activity options ranging from horse-riding, falconry, clay-pigeon shooting and so much more. Our experienced team will be on hand to help you make the most of your experience.  Be sure to pick up an estate map during your visit to discover the many trails through our 300-acre grounds.

Ireland is known for its award-winning produce so you are sure to indulge in many culinary delights during your trip to Kerry. Whilst enjoying your stay with us here at Sheen Falls Lodge you can avail of our choice of dining options, from a laidback bite at The Stables Brasserie  to something truly unforgettable at The Falls Restaurant.

For further travel planning advice and to book any activities during your stay, please speak with our Guest Relations team.

## Experience Kerry

## plan your five star vacation

The stunning heritage town of Kenmare is an excellent destination for your Kerry vacation. Our location is just five minutes away from the town, where you can explore a lively market town filled with colorful gift shops, exceptional restaurants, cafes, high-end boutiques, independent shops, galleries, and a heritage center. Sheen Falls Lodge is the perfect base for your next Ireland adventure, situated at the intersection between the Ring of Kerry and the Ring of Beara.

For those who haven’t yet experienced Ireland, there are numerous beauty spots located right on our doorstep. The Ring of Kerry is a renowned touring route that takes in bustling towns and picturesque villages along the way. A scenic drive from our front doors will take you through mountain passes, pretty villages, and world-famous views.

Some of the highlights of the region include taking a food tour of Kenmare, a quick ferry trip to Garnish Island in the neighboring Glengarriff village, or a visit to the nearby Gleninchaquin Park.

Despite its size, Kenmare boasts many impressive culinary gems that you can experience on an artisan food tour. During the tour, you can meet the makers and locals, from locally roasted coffee to organic local vegetables.

Gleninchaquin Park is a short drive from the town and home to some truly magnificent views. As a working sheep farm open to the public, the park has a selection of walking and hiking routes for all ages and abilities. The waterfalls and mountain views make the park a pitstop to add to your list during your trip to Kerry.

The Kingdom of Kerry will delight you with its beauty and rugged coastline, with picture-perfect pitstop’s along the way. You can find much more information and travel tips on Kenmare and Kerry throughout our website. If you need more travel planning assistance, our concierge team will be happy to help you with any inquiries during your stay with us.

## Getting Here

## Travelling to Sheen Falls Lodge

Sheen Falls Lodge is 52km south of Kerry International AirportOpens in new tab, 97km north west of Cork International AirportOpens in new tab, 164km south west of Shannon International AirportOpens in new tab and 343km of Dublin International AirportOpens in new tab.

Dublin and Shannon Airport are the only airports in Europe with US Pre-Clearance. Meaning that you can access pre-clearance and customs prior to your departure back to the United States, making your trip home as seamless as possible.

Sheen Falls Lodge is located 2km outside the town of Kenmare. On arriving in Kenmare, follow the signs on the N71, south, signposted Glengarriff. After crossing the suspension bridge, take the first turn left, signposted Sheen Falls Lodge. Drive 1km and the lodge entrance is located on your left-hand side.

**VIEW OUR MAP** For detailed directions and Eircode’s during your stay, please check our map.
