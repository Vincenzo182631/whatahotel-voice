Welcome to Sheen Falls Lodge, where timeless luxury meets the wild beauty of Ireland’s southwest. Sheen Falls Lodge has earned international recognition for its excellence. In 2024, Sheen Falls Lodge was awarded Two Michelin Keys, a distinction it continues to hold. It has also been named the number one resort in the UK and Ireland in the Travel + Leisure World’s Best Awards 2026. In October this year, Sheen Falls Lodge was delighted to welcome NBC’s TODAY Show, which broadcast live from the estate to millions of viewers across America. Watch the TODAY Show highlights from Sheen Falls LodgeOpens in new tab.

Our five-star Relais & Châteaux estate occupies a spectacular setting on Kenmare Bay, providing the perfect gateway to the iconic Ring of Kerry and the Beara Peninsula on the Wild Atlantic Way. Originally an 18th-century fishing lodge, the property now offers 72 luxurious guestrooms and suites, exceptional dining, the Easanna Spa, and a wide range of outdoor pursuits.

Guests can explore our 300-acre estate through golf, fishing, falconry, horse riding, clay pigeon shooting, tennis and heritage walks, or simply relax beside roaring fires while taking in the beauty of the surrounding landscape. Also located on the estate are five luxurious cottages and villas, each offering stunning views of Kenmare Bay and access to all hotel facilities.

At the heart of Sheen Falls Lodge is a commitment to exceptional cuisine, authentic Irish hospitality and creating unforgettable experiences in one of Ireland’s most remarkable destinations. We look forward to welcoming you to Sheen Falls Lodge.
