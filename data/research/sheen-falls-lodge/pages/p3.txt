# The Falls Restaurant

## An Evening of Indulgence

Step into a world of culinary artistry at The Falls Restaurant—where each evening unfolds as a celebration of flavour, finesse, and the finest Irish hospitality.

Nestled within the wild, romantic beauty of Kenmare in County Kerry, and overlooking the cascading waters of the River Sheen, The Falls offers a dramatic and enchanting dining setting. Here, nature’s grandeur meets gastronomic precision. At the helm is Executive Chef **Mark Treacy**, whose innovative, seasonal menus pay homage to Ireland’s rich bounty. With ingredients sourced from the lush landscapes of Kerry and beyond, where traditional Irish flavours are elevated by refined French technique.

Expect menus that evolve with the seasons—thoughtfully composed, artistically plated, and designed to delight the palate. Every element is intentional, from the amuse-bouche to the final flourish of petit fours.

The ambience is effortlessly graceful. Crisp linens, candlelight, and the soft cadence of live piano set the tone for a truly immersive experience. Whether you’re marking a milestone, sharing an intimate evening, or seeking a refined escape, The Falls Restaurant invites you to dine with intention, and to linger.

The Falls Restaurant is open for dinner **Tuesday to Saturday** and closes on **Sunday & Monday**, with the exception of Bank Holiday weekends, when the restaurant closes on Monday and Tuesday.
