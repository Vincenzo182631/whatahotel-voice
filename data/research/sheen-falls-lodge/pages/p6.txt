# Ring of Kerry Golf Club

## by Sheen Falls Lodge

Owned and managed by Sheen Falls Lodge, the Ring of Kerry Golf Club is located five miles from the hotel. The spectacular course, designed by Eddie Hackett and Ron Kirby, offers golfers a unique blend of heathland and parkland experiences, featuring sweeping, diverse terrain and winding, mature, tree-lined fairways.

This scenic 18-hole, 6,923-yard, par 72 course is neatly tucked between the Caha Mountains and the mighty MacGillycuddy Reeks. Each hole provides magnificent sweeping views of the Kenmare Estuary, designed to challenge golfers of all skill levels. Signature holes include the testing 6th hole, “Ladies View”, due to its scenic splendour with a tee shot that is surely the most breathtaking in Ireland. On the back nine, the par four 14th requires a second shot through a stunning avenue of trees. The PGA-standard course also offers golf buggies and a stylish restaurant and bar, **Nua Vista** **,** the perfect place to refuel.

All Sheen Falls Lodge guests are welcome to avail of our complimentary transfer service during their stay if they wish to experience this true hidden golfing gem.

For those looking to make the most of their visit, we offer overnight golf packages, allowing you to combine a round of championship golf with a stay at Sheen Falls Lodge for the ultimate getaway.

## Nua Vista Restaurant

Set within the Ring of Kerry Golf Club, Nua Vista Restaurant offers breathtaking panoramic views of the Caha Mountains and McGillycuddy Reeks. Whether you’ve enjoyed a morning on the golf course or spent the day discovering the beauty of Kerry, we’re here to welcome you in.

Our new menu brings a fresh, seasonal feel with dishes perfect for spring and summer. Enjoy new additions such as pan‑roasted Irish hake, Tom Crean beer‑battered fish of the day, and a chargrilled 8oz Black Angus sirloin cooked to your liking. Pair your meal with a choice from our full bar menu, featuring artisan gins, whiskeys, and creative cocktails.

Nua Vista is also an ideal setting for celebrations and gatherings. From intimate private dining to larger events for up to 130 guests, we love helping to bring special occasions to life, from milestone birthdays to anniversaries and everything in between. With cosy indoor spaces and a spacious terrace for al‑fresco dining, your event will be framed by spectacular mountain views that create a memorable backdrop.

Nua Vista also makes a stunning wedding location, offering a relaxed, contemporary setting with extraordinary views,  the perfect place to celebrate your day with family and friends.
