# Directions

### ADDRESS

Sheen Falls Lodge, Kenmare, Co. Kerry, V93 HR27, Ireland

### TELEPHONE AND EMAIL

For any questions, enquiries or to book, please don’t hesitate to get in contact with us. Please call +353 (0)64 664 1600.

Our email address is info@sheenfallslodge.ie

### DIRECTIONS

The lodge is located 2km outside the town of Kenmare. On arriving in Kenmare, follow the signs on the N71, south, signposted Glengarriff. After crossing the suspension bridge, take the first turn left, signposted Sheen Falls Lodge. Drive 1km and the lodge entrance is located on your left hand side.

### ELECTRIC CAR CHARGING POINTS

In order to further our sustainability efforts, we have installed electric car charging units for our guests to use during their stay. Our Electric Car Charging Stations are free to use and are located by the stables on the Sheen Falls Lodge grounds.

### AIRPORTS

Sheen Falls Lodge is 52km south of Kerry International AirportOpens in new tab, 97km north west of Cork International AirportOpens in new tab, 164km south west of Shannon International AirportOpens in new tab and 343km of Dublin International AirportOpens in new tab.

For more detailed directions, or to find out more, please contact the lodge

directly on \+ 353 (0)64 6641600 or email info@sheenfallslodge.ie
