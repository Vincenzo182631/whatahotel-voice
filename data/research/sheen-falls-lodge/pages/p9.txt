# Dining

## at Sheen Falls Lodge

At Sheen Falls Lodge, our ethos is to create truly unforgettable culinary experiences, making every meal a highlight of your luxury stay in Ireland, where every detail is thoughtfully considered to deliver exceptional food and a memorable ambience. From contemporary casual to immersive fine dining, each outlet celebrates the rich flavours of the Wild Atlantic Way.

Each carefully curated menu at Sheen Falls Lodge is a vibrant celebration of the Irish landscape. We are committed to seasonal produce, directly sourced from our network of wonderful local suppliers. This dedication to farm-to-table dining in Kerry ensures that every dish on your plate is a testament to the freshness and quality of Ireland’s bounty, prepared with passion and precision by our talented culinary team.
