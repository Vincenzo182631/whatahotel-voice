# Easanna Wellness & Spa

Escapism, that’s what we are here for. To relax you. Refresh you. Soothe you. Help you glow.

We believe that nature has healing powers for the mind and body, and here at Sheen Falls Lodge, we are surrounded by an abundance of nature. Step into Easanna Spa and surrender to the serenity of the flowing waters and uplifting views of the encircling mountains and Kenmare Bay.

Mother Nature and Easanna are the perfect allies in your quest for relaxation, with a holistic approach to all treatments at Easanna Spa. Our award-winning spa features signature treatments and showcases partnerships with elite brands Elemis, Ground Wellbeing and Seabody, with a range of bespoke treatments that can be tailored to your individual needs. Our team of therapists provide the highest quality care during your visit. Choose from massages, skin-nourishing facials, to soothing body rituals.

We are immensely proud to have garnered numerous accolades, including Spa Manager of the Year 2024, Spa of the Year and Spa Team of the Year 2024, as well as a finalist position in World Spa Team of the Year. We are proud to offer a range of specialised spa treatments, tailored to those living with and beyond cancer. Created by Ground Wellbeing aims to provide comfort, relief and support to individuals at a time when it’s needed the most.

During your stay at Sheen Falls Lodge, we welcome guests to avail of our complimentary facilities, which include a heated swimming pool, sauna, steam room, jacuzzi, relaxation room, and fitness suite with state-of-the-art equipment.

We are open from 9 am-8 pm daily. Please note the last entry for adults is 7.30pm.

Children’s hours between 9am to 6pm, we kindly ask that all children be supervised by an adult.

Spa treatments are available to guests aged **18 years and over only**.

To ensure that you get the most out of your spa experience with us, we recommend booking at least 7 days before your arrival to avoid any disappointment. Unfortunately, we cannot guarantee availability at short notice, so booking in advance is always the best option.

Treatments can be reserved online, or by contacting our Easanna Spa team:  + 353 (0)64 664 1600 or via email.
