## Luxury Cottages & Villas

## at Sheen Falls Lodge

_**Charming, private, and deeply restful—your hideaway awaits.**_

Nestled on the 300-acre estate of Sheen Falls Lodge, you will find our collection of thatched cottages that seamlessly blend traditional Irish charm with the comfort of five-star living. Whether you are seeking a romantic escape, a family break, or a gathering with friends, these exclusive residences offer the space to unwind—and the freedom to do things your way.

Each home is beautifully appointed, with:

- Fully-equipped kitchens

- Elegant dining and living spaces

- Private outdoor terraces with views of Kenmare Bay

- Access to all the five-star services, amenities and activities of Sheen Falls Lodge

From sunlit breakfasts to candlelit dinners, riverside walks to indulgent spa moments, every day can be as peaceful or as adventurous as you wish. For further enquiries on our Cottages and Villas, you can check availability and book here online and for more information, please contact Reservations
