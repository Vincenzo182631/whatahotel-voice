# Activities

## at Sheen Falls Lodge

Beyond the luxurious confines of your room, a world of immersive experiences awaits at Sheen Falls Lodge.

From the classic countryside pursuits of horse riding, fishing, falconry and clay-pigeon shooting to the modern-day thrill of axe-throwing or archery, here you will find a plethora of immersive pastimes to enjoy.

Our activity pursuits offer something for all tastes. Whether you wish to hone your golfing skills or simply try something new, you will find a range of pursuits to explore during your stay at Sheen Falls Lodge.

We are here to help you make the most of your stay in our beautiful corner of Ireland. Perhaps a more leisurely pace beckons. Curl up with a captivating novel in our warmly inviting Library. Meander through the ancient woodlands that surround our secluded estate, or enjoy a truly relaxing visit to our award-winning Easanna Wellness and Spa.

We advise all activities be pre-booked before your arrival to ensure availability. For any queries or to discuss trip planning in detail,  please contact our Guest Relations Team.
