# Superior Deluxe Rooms

A gorgeous space to awaken in, hide out in, or return to after a thrilling outdoor adventure, our Superior Deluxe Rooms are truly superior in style and nature. Similar to the lodge’s Deluxe Rooms, they offer extra space and superb waterfall views, all wrapped up in the privacy of a truly luxurious bedroom.

Few properties in the world offer rooms this close to a star attraction – namely the cascading waters beneath a bridge that’s over 240 years old. Naturally, our Superior Deluxe Rooms include deep armchairs and generous windows to best enjoy the Sheen Falls’ theatrics.

Unwind in the serenity and luxurious Superior Deluxe Guest Room, each decorated with handmade design pieces by local artisans, from the art on the walls to the espresso cups and sumptuous seating. Our Superior Deluxe Rooms are, on average, 505 square feet (47 square metres) and can sleep two adults with an optional extra bed for a child.
