# Celebrate Christmas

## 3-Night Package

Embrace the magic of the festive season at our Two Michelin Key Hideaway. Here at Sheen Falls Lodge, we have had the distinct privilege of hosting families and friends from near and far over Christmas for many years. This festive season, we invite you to step into a winter wonderland where enchantment is around every corner.

A warm familiar welcome, with roaring log fires and a sense of tranquillity and enchantment awaits you. Be welcomed with a warming beverage whilst you soak up the enchanting setting. Watch the light fade over the snow-capped mountains and allow our wonderful team to take care of you and your family in the serenity that is the essence of the festive season at Sheen Falls Lodge.

## Christmas Package

## at Sheen Falls Lodge

Our meticulously crafted three-night Christmas package invites you and your loved ones to experience the true spirit of the holidays in the heart of Kenmare.

**Included in your magical three-night Christmas package:**

• Three nights of accommodation in a spacious and luxurious room with a water-view

• Welcome drinks on arrival on Christmas Eve

• Dinner on Christmas Eve will be served in The Stables Restaurant/The Falls Restaurant

• Traditional Christmas Day Lunch in The Falls Restaurant

• Christmas Evening Buffet with seasonal favourites and accompanying wines

• St Stephen’s Day enjoy the annual Sheen Falls Lodge Treasure Hunt takes place.

• Drinks & Canapé reception on the evening of the 26th of December

• A 5-course Gala Dinner in The Falls Restaurant with wine on 26th December

• Full Irish breakfast each morning is included.

Please note, children sharing with their parents will be charged a nightly accommodation rate of €110, inclusive of breakfast each morning.

## Why Celebrate Christmas in Kenmare

## Co. Kerry, Ireland

**A Luxury Christmas Escape in Ireland**

Celebrate Christmas in the heart of Kerry at Sheen Falls Lodge, one of Ireland’s most enchanting five-star hotels. Nestled on the Wild Atlantic Way and overlooking the cascading waterfalls of the River Sheen, our Christmas package is the perfect way to experience a luxury festive break in Ireland. Guests can enjoy crackling fires, award-winning dining, and the warmest of welcomes in our country manor house setting.

**Your Christmas Break in Kerry**

Located in the colourful town of Kenmare, Sheen Falls Lodge offers a magical setting for your festive getaway. Whether you are looking for a romantic Christmas hotel break for two or a family Christmas holiday in Kerry, our three-night residential package combines indulgent seasonal menus, elegant accommodation, and festive entertainment.

**Family-Friendly Christmas Breaks**

Christmas at Sheen Falls Lodge is filled with joy for all ages. Little ones can look forward to children’s activities and a special visit from Santa, while adults may choose to relax with spa treatments, invigorating nature walks, and mulled wine by the fire.

**A Christmas Getaway to Remember**

Enjoy the finest Irish hospitality this festive season, with a programme of events including a traditional Christmas Eve dinner, Christmas Day celebrations, and live music.

Should you require any further information, please feel free to contact our Christmas Coordinators on **+353646641600Opens in new tab** or email your enquiry to **Bookings@sheenfallslodge.ieOpens in new tab.** We look forward to speaking with you, to creating a truly special Christmas for you and to welcoming you to Sheen Falls Lodge.
