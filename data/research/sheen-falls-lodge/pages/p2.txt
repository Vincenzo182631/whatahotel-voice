At Sheen Falls Lodge, each room is a room with a view that invites you to immerse yourself in the captivating beauty of our five-star location in Kerry.

Our luxurious interiors reflect the soothing colours of Kenmare Bay. Every aspect of our exquisite rooms, from the plush bedding to the thoughtfully curated décor, is designed for ultimate relaxation.

Choose from elegantly designed lodge rooms, to spacious Suites, each offering their own unique features and understated palettes that reflect the changing seasons.  Riverside, you will look over the cascading Sheen Falls, while Bay-side you will find sweeping views across Kenmare Bay.

For the ultimate in privacy, our charming thatched cottage residences provide all the comfort of home with all the amenities and activities available at the lodge.
