# Serenity Your Way \| Golf or Spa Escape

## Two nights of luxury & exceptional dining

**Serenity Your Way**

Escape together and unwind your own way. With Serenity Your Way, each guest chooses their own path to relaxation — an 18-hole round of golf at our Ring of Kerry Golf course, or a restorative 60-minute spa treatment.

Play the fairways together, retreat to the spa side by side, or split off and enjoy your own experience before reconnecting in the evening. The day comes together over a memorable three-course dinner, the perfect finish to time well spent.

**Your Package Includes**

• A two-night stay in a beautifully appointed room or suite

• Dinner in The Stables Brasserie on one evening

• Full Irish breakfast each morning

• Your choice of either a 60-minute treatment at Easanna Wellness & Spa, featuring bespoke treatments from renowned brands including Seabody and Ground Wellbeing, or an 18-hole round of golf.

• Full access to Easanna Wellness & Spa, including the heated indoor pool, jacuzzi, steam room, sauna and fitness suite

• Complimentary use of Sheen Falls Lodge resort bikes

• Complimentary use of the tennis court

**\*\*\*Natura Bisse Treatments are not included\*\*\***

## Terms & Conditions

We would like to kindly remind you of the following booking terms and conditions that apply:

- Please note that check-in time is from 3 pm and check-out time is 12 noon
- Any children sharing a room with their parents on this package will have breakfast only included in their stay. All other meals are charged on consumption
- Our cancellation policy is 14 days prior to the arrival date. Any cancellations received after this time will be charged in full.  Reservations made within 14 days of arrival are subject to the same hotel cancellation policy outlined. No changes or amendments can be made to the reservation.

What is the Serenity Your Way package at Sheen Falls Lodge?

Serenity Your Way is a two-night break where each guest chooses their own way to relax. You can take either a 60-minute treatment at Easanna Wellness & Spa or an 18-hole round of golf at our Ring of Kerry Golf course. The stay also includes dinner on one evening, full Irish breakfast each morning and full use of the spa facilities.

Can my partner and I choose different experiences — one golf, one spa?

Yes. Each guest chooses their own experience, so one of you can play the golf course while the other takes a spa treatment. You’re equally welcome to both choose golf, both choose the spa, or simply go your separate ways for the day and reconnect over dinner in the evening.

Do I have to choose between the spa treatment and golf, or can I have both?

The package includes one or the other per guest — either a 60-minute treatment at Easanna Wellness & Spa or an 18-hole round of golf, not both. If you’d like to add the second experience during your stay, please contact the hotel directly, as any additional treatments or rounds are not part of the package.

How many nights does the Serenity Your Way package include?

The package is a two-night stay, with full Irish breakfast included on each morning and dinner included on one of the two evenings. Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is at 12 noon.

Where is the golf played?

Golf is played at our Ring of Kerry Golf course, which is a 10 minute drive from the hotel. The package includes a full 18-hole round for each guest who chooses golf rather than the spa treatment. If you’d like details on tee times, course facilities or equipment hire, the hotel can advise when you book or you can contact guest relations on guestrelations@sheenfallslodge.ie

Do I need to bring my own clubs and is a buggy included?

The package includes the 18-hole round itself. Club hire, buggy hire and any other course extras are not included, so please check directly with the hotel before you travel if you’ll need equipment. Confirming this at the time of booking is the safest approach.

What spa treatment is included in the package?

Guests who choose the spa receive a 60-minute treatment at Easanna Wellness & Spa. The spa offers bespoke treatments from renowned brands including Seabody and Ground Wellbeing. The specific treatment menu and how to reserve your slot can be confirmed with the hotel — booking ahead of arrival is always worth doing.

Are Natura Bissé treatments included in Serenity Your Way?

No. Natura Bissé treatments are not included in this package. The 60-minute treatment covered by Serenity Your Way is drawn from the spa’s other offerings, which include bespoke treatments from brands such as Seabody and Ground Wellbeing. If you’d like a Natura Bissé treatment during your stay, please enquire with the hotel separately.

Should I book my spa treatment or tee time in advance?

Booking arrangements aren’t specified in the package details, but treatment slots and tee times are typically limited. Contacting the hotel ahead of arrival to secure your preferred time is the sensible approach, particularly if you and your travelling companion are choosing different experiences on the same day.
