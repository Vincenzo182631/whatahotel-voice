# 3 for 2 By The Falls

## 3rd Night Complimentary, with DBB on 2 Nights

Three Nights of Luxury at Sheen Falls Lodge

Make the most of your escape to Sheen Falls Lodge with three luxurious nights overlooking the water, combining elegant accommodation, exceptional dining and restorative relaxation — with your third night complimentary.

Your stay includes:

• Three nights in a beautifully appointed room overlooking the water, with the third night complimentary.

• Full Irish breakfast each morning

• Dinner for two on two evenings — one evening in The Falls Restaurant and one in The Stables Brasserie & Bar

• Access to Easanna Wellness & Spa, including the indoor pool, jacuzzi, steam room, sauna and gym

• Complimentary use of Sheen Falls Lodge resort bikes, perfect for exploring the grounds and surrounding area

• Complimentary use of the tennis court

Set against the tranquil beauty of Kenmare Bay, this is the perfect opportunity to slow down, indulge and enjoy a little more of what makes Sheen Falls Lodge so special.
