# Stay Four Longer Cottages and Villas

## Your Fourth Night, With Our Compliments

A wonderful way to make your stay go further, with an extra night to relax, indulge and enjoy everything Sheen Falls Lodge has to offer. Guests who stay for four nights in one of the lodge’s luxurious cottages or villas receive their fourth night complimentary, along with thoughtful extras, dining experiences and access to the lodge’s exceptional leisure facilities.

The package includes:

• A four-night stay, with the fourth night complimentary.

• A welcome hamper and breakfast hamper provided on arrival.

• Dinner on two evenings: one evening in The Falls Restaurant and one in The Stables Brasserie & Bar.

• Fresh pastries delivered to the cottage or villa every morning, with the lodge’s compliments.

• Complimentary access to Easanna Wellness & Spa, including the heated indoor pool, jacuzzi, steam room, sauna and fitness suite.

• Complimentary use of Sheen Falls Lodge resort bicycles.

• Complimentary use of the tennis court.

• 20% off spa treatments.

Please Note: Dinner is included for up to eight people when staying in a villa and up to four people when staying in a cottage.

Terms and Conditions Apply

## Terms & Conditions

• A non-refundable deposit of 25% is required at the time of booking to secure your reservation.

• Cancellations must be made at least 30 days before your arrival date.

• Travel plans and reservations may be amended up to 30 days before arrival.

• All reservations must be guaranteed with a credit card.

• Check-in is from 4:00 p.m. Please note that cottages are guaranteed to be ready by 5:00 p.m.

• Check-out is at 10:00 a.m.

• Unfortunately, we are currently unable to accommodate early check-in or late check-out requests.

• A welcome hamper will be provided upon arrival.

• Housekeeping service is provided every second day during your stay.

• We welcome medium-sized house dogs weighing under 50 lbs.

• A maximum of two dogs is permitted per stay.

• A pet fee of €50 per dog, per night applies, together with a €100 cleaning fee per stay.

• Please let us know in advance if you will be bringing a dog.
