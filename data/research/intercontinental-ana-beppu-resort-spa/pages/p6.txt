## FAQs for ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa

Does ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa have a pool?

ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa has 1 outdoor pool.

Infinity Pool

Enjoy an incomparable view under the open air of Beppu. After the swim, lie back on the patio in one of the 12 sunloungers or relax in the jacuzzi. Light meals and beverages are also available.

Can I bring my pet to ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa?

No, pets are not allowed at ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa.

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa has 3 restaurants.

What are the check-in and check-out times for ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa?

Check-in at ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa offers free WiFi!

How far is ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa from the nearest airport?

ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & Spa is conveniently located near Oita Airport (OIT), approximately 25.23 miles away (40.6 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
