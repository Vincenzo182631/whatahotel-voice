### HARNN Heritage Spa

Created by HARNN, a luxury brand from Thailand, HARNN Heritage Spa draws inspiration from the time-honored traditions of the East and natural healing practices. Embrace a unique spa experience inspired by the nature and culture of Beppu, Oita, and step away from the everyday to enjoy a tranquil moment that awakens the senses

Learn more

### Myoban Onsen Retreat

Crafted from Beppu stone, our rustic open-air bath offers panoramic views of Beppu Bay and the city below. Discover the soothing waters of Myoban, one of Beppu’s renowned hot spring areas, celebrated for their skin-beautifying qualities. Immerse yourself in the natural beauty of Beppu and enjoy a moment of pure relaxation.

Learn more
