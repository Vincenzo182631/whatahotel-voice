# ANA InterContinental Beppu Resort & SpaRooms & suites

ANA Intercontinental Beppu Resort & Spa features spacious rooms and suites of refined natural beauty and immersive relaxation. Each room has been planned for optimal comfort, with calming Japanese-inspired design and modern amenities to meet every need. We're also pleased to welcome guests with a glass of original blend TWG tea.
