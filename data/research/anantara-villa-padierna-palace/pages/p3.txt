Reviewed by
Agnish Ray
It may be a half-hour drive from the center, but Anantara’s Spanish property is an opulent palazzo for those with a strong sense of beauty, and any round-up on the most lavish Marbella hotels would be incomplete without it. Once a stately home belonging to the Count of Villa Padierna—a local dandy notorious for his lavish lifestyle—this palatial hideaway is a spectacle, from the striking ochre-pink building to the splendid interior filled with paintings, sculptures and tapestries. Its enormous Roman spa offers sensational hydrotherapy, hammam, aromatherapy rooms, and more, while restaurants like La Veranda and La Loggia celebrate Italian, French, and Spanish decadence.
