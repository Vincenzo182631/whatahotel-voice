Anantara Villa Padierna Palace Resort assumes a picturesque setting in Andalusia, between Marbella, Benahavís and Estepona in the south of Spain. This luxury resort in Marbella offers unrivalled access to the famed sandy beaches and Mediterranean Sea of the Costa del Sol. Leisure travellers can take advantage of the hotel’s proximity to cities such as Málaga, Ronda and Gibraltar.

The resort’s location in the Golf Valley is conveniently accessible from Málaga Airport and Marbella’s transport hubs. It is surrounded by lush green gardens and the Andalusian golf courses, close to the diving and sailing spots of Puerto Banus and the Costa del Sol.

Retreat to an elegant sanctuary surrounded by three golf courses, a tranquil lake, Roman amphitheatre and more than 1,200 original works of art. Dine on Andalusian haute cuisine or fresh seafood at the beach club. Make the most of luxurious leisure and wellness facilities, with a private racquet club and thermal spa. This 5-star resort in Marbella is also in an ideal location to explore the region's sandy beaches and traditional villages perched in the mountains.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Are meeting rooms available at the Anantara Villa Padierna Palace Benahavís Marbella Resort?**

Yes, there are 6 meeting rooms and an amphitheater. The largest room seats 660 people and the amphitheater 300, ideal for holding events near Marbella.

**What are the main points of interest close to the Anantara Villa Padierna Palace Benahavís Marbella Resort?**

The resort is located in a picturesque setting between Marbella, Benahavis and Estepona. It is close to Saladillo beach and 12 km from the Puerto Banús stores and acclaimed marina.

**What are some of the property amenities at the Anantara Villa Padierna Palace Benahavís Marbella Resort?**

This luxury resort in Marbella is surrounded by 3 golf courses. It also has a wonderful spa where you can rest and relax with various treatments and swimming pools after a day of meetings or sight-seeing through southern Andalusia.

**Does the Anantara Villa Padierna Palace Benahavís Marbella Resort offer any food or drink options?**

At the Anantara Villa Padierna Palace Benahavís Marbella Resort, you will find 10 restaurant concepts (during high season) where you can enjoy a whole range of Mediterranean-flavored cuisine. The Loggia, with its special décor, offers a quiet dinner atmosphere, the Pergola is great for a snack by the pool or the By The Sea Beach Club is great for paellas, the local espetos and the perfect cocktail to enjoy while you view the Mediterranean sunset.

**Does the Anantara Villa Padierna Palace Benahavís Marbella Resort offer parking service?**

Yes, this 5-star resort in Marbella offers its guests parking, guaranteeing all the comforts of access to the hotel in this idyllic setting in Malaga region. This service is available for an additional daily fee.
