# CHARLESTON SANTA TERESA HOTEL

## Located in Cartagena, Colombia

* * *

##### An iconic and historic hotel within the **walled city of Cartagena**, this hotel boasts significant heritage value, having built as a 17th-century cloister. Today, it blends modernity and luxury with the charm of a colonial atmosphere. Explore its courtyards, hallways, arcades, **rooftop with panoramic views, Santa Teresa Spa**, and each of its **86 rooms and suites**, decorated in a colonial and republican style.

##### The hotel offers an exclusive culinary experience designed by **Chef Harry Sasson for Harry's Cartagena**, Plaza Santa Teresa, La Terraza Rooftop, room service, events and **Santa Teresa Weddings.**

### Turndown Service

Twice a day, our maids get each room and suite ready for rest. Impeccable order and cleanliness during the day and at night, it is especially suitable to receive the guest after a vacation or business day.

### Spa & Wellness

Located on the second floor of the hotel, SANTA TERESA SPA, offers massages, therapies, rituals, wet areas and a 24-hour gym.

### Pool & Solarium

360° view of the entire city that integrates in a single panorama, the red roofs of the old city, the imposing skyscrapers of the modern city, the vibrant port of the city with its large cruise ships and the wonderful blue of the Caribbean Sea , make this unique and exclusive place to enjoy the magic of Cartagena from the Heights.
