##### What types of rooms are available at Hotel Charleston Santa Teresa?

The **Hotel Charleston** offers a wide variety of rooms designed to provide comfort and elegance, including Classic Rooms, Ocean View rooms with stunning sea views, rooms with two Queen beds ideal for families, Premium Balcony rooms overlooking our charming inner courtyard, and spacious Premier Deluxe rooms. In addition, the hotel features several suite options such as Junior Suites and Courtyard View Suites, as well as the exclusive Colonial Suite, distinguished by its unique architecture, the expansive Master Suite with multiple living areas, and finally the Grand Suite, an exceptional accommodation that stands out for its two fully separated living spaces.

##### What are the key features of the Classic Ocean View rooms?

The Classic Ocean View rooms provide elegant interiors and stunning views over the Caribbean Sea and historic walls, located on the 4th and 5th floors of the Republican Building, with modern facilities for a comfortable stay.

##### Are there any suites available for guests seeking luxury?

Yes, guests can choose from luxurious suites such as the Grand Suite and Master Suite, which offer spacious layouts, living and dining areas, and views of the Caribbean Sea and the modern city.

##### What is the check-in and check-out policy at the hotel?

Check-in typically starts at 3:00 PM, and check-out is before 12:00 PM. For specific requests or early check-in, guests are encouraged to contact the hotel directly.

##### Can guests access free Wi-Fi during their stay?

Yes, Hotel Charleston Santa Teresa offers complimentary Wi-Fi throughout the property, allowing guests to stay connected during their visit

##### What types of guests typically stay at Hotel Charleston Santa Teresa?

The hotel caters to a variety of guests, including tourists, business travelers, families and couples seeking a relaxing getaway or a venue for events
