##### What dining options are available at Hotel Charleston Santa Teresa?

The hotel features **three restaurants**, two of which are open to both guests and the general public. **Harry’s Cartagena**, one of the city’s most renowned restaurants, offers an international culinary proposal with an exquisite fusion of Colombian flavors, created by chef **Harry Sasson**. **Plaza Santa Teresa**, operated under the same concept as Harry’s, is an open-air venue where guests can enjoy Cartagena’s most beautiful sunsets, live music, and signature cocktails. Lastly, the hotel offers **La Terraza**, a restaurant located on the sixth floor and exclusive to hotel guests, where visitors can enjoy the hotel’s swimming pool, unique sunsets, and breathtaking **360-degree panoramic views** of Cartagena.

##### What type of cuisine is served at Harry's Sasson?

Harry's Sasson restaurant specializes in exquisite dishes created by one of Colombia’s top chefs.
