Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C.
Welcome to a historic hotel in Georgetown, Washington, D.C.
With its iconic smokestack dominating the neighborhood skyline, The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C., is one of the most distinctive hotels in Georgetown. This historic hotel in Washington, D.C. preserves the character of a turn-of-the-century industrial building built in 1932 while remaining an integral part of the Georgetown cityscape. Recognized as a pet-friendly hotel in Washington, D.C., it offers exceptionally spacious guest rooms and luxury suites designed for comfort and sophistication. Guests can enjoy a renowned spa, upscale dining, and elegant event venues at our hotel on the Potomac River. Rooted in history yet focused on modern luxury, this spa hotel in Washington, D.C. delivers transformative wellness experiences, personalized service, and contemporary comforts that exceed every expectation.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C.?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C.. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $165.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C.?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C.. The daily valet fee is $75.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C.?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. is Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA). DCA is located approximately 4.7 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. have an airport shuttle to Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. does not have an airport shuttle to Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
