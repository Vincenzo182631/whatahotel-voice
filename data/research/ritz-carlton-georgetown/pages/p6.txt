Experience Washington, D.C. From Its Most Charming Neighborhood
Experiences
Washington, D.C. is a city of neighborhoods, and Georgetown is among its most desirable. Once an industrial center, the NW DC area is filled with historical landmarks and architectural treasures. Located near the Potomac River, our luxury Georgetown, D.C. hotel invites guests to unwind at our luxurious spa or set out to explore the area with Ritz-Carlton Cruisers, city-friendly bikes that easily travel to D.C.’s most iconic attractions, including Georgetown University, the National Mall, and the Smithsonian Museums.

Discover Georgetown
Explore an iconic city that is both rich in tradition and busy shaping the future. Located in the heart of Georgetown, just steps from M Street and Wisconsin Avenue, The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. is the perfect hotel to discover all the history, culture, and excitement the Nation’s Capital has to offer. Widely known for its historic charm and a European feel, the Georgetown neighborhood allows guests to stroll the C&O Canal, shop at upscale boutiques, and dine overlooking the beautiful Georgetown waterfront.

Discover Our Historic Georgetown Hotel
With its iconic smokestack dominating the neighborhood skyline, The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C. is one of the most distinctive hotels in Georgetown. This historic landmark hotel incorporates the renovation of a turn-of-the-century industrial building erected in 1932 and serves as an integral part of the Georgetown cityscape. This architectural treasure invites guests to explore its historic spaces and discover its hidden details. Exposed brick walls, charming fireplaces, and lush gardens add to the sophisticated ambiance of this luxury escape, making it a must-see for your visit to the Nation’s Capital.
