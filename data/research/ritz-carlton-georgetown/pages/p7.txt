Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C.
Experience the Magic of the Season
Celebrate the most magical time of the year with friends, family, and loved ones at The Ritz-Carlton, Georgetown. Cozy up by the warm fireplace, savor delicious s’mores, and enjoy enchanting live music that fills the air with holiday cheer. Create unforgettable memories that will last a lifetime in the heart of the season's festivities
25 Days of Cocktails
Countdown to Santa's favorite holiday with a daily, rotating selection of 25-holiday cocktails thoughtfully crafted to warm the spirits. The specialty menu will feature a new cocktail offered for the day in The Living Room nightly from 5 p.m. to Midnight from December 1 through December 25.

Holiday Dining Experiences
Thanksgiving Dinner
Join us at Degrees Bistro for a Thanksgiving celebration to remember. Delight in a thoughtfully curated, holiday-inspired three-course prix fixe menu, expertly prepared by our Executive Chef. Enjoy an evening of exceptional cuisine, warm hospitality, and the festive spirit of the season.
Reserve Now
Great American State Fair
Wander the National Mall during a vibrant state fair featuring all fifty states and U.S. territories, with regional cuisine, live entertainment, and cultural experiences at every turn.
Discover More
New Year's Eve Dinner
Ring in the New Year with style as you sip and savor a delightful evening of culinary excellence. Enjoy an exquisite three-course prix fixe menu, thoughtfully designed for the occasion by our Executive Chef, and toast to a night of celebration and indulgence.
Discover More
Fireside S'mores & Hot Cocoa Bar
Cozy up by the fire in The Living Room and indulge in decadent s’mores and a hot cocoa bar with all the irresistible fixings, prepared by our Chef from 5:30 PM to 6:30 PM every Friday, December 1 through December 31. Join us for this sweet holiday tradition and make your evenings even more magical with every bite.
Discover More
Letters to Santa
Join us in spreading the magic of the holidays! From December 1st to 25th, children can write their heartfelt letters to Santa Claus and send them straight to the North Pole through our special Santa Mailbox in The Living Room.
Discover More
Holiday Spa Retreat
Indulge in a luxurious holiday retreat at the spa, where serenity and rejuvenation create a haven from the festive bustle. Whether you’re seeking a moment of pampering, a pre-holiday glow, or a full wellness escape, the Ritz-Carlton Spa, Georgetown, offers an array of treatments designed to restore balance and inspire calm.
