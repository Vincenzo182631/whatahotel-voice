Exceptional Restaurants in Georgetown DC
Dining
At The Ritz-Carlton Georgetown, Washington, D.C., each restaurant and private dining experience has its own personality, yet they all make diners feel at home. Fine dining experiences in our Georgetown, D.C. restaurants, Degrees Bistro and The Living Room, are sure to delight the senses. At Degrees Bistro, artisanal fare is artfully prepared in variety of dishes, and in The Living Room, expert mixologists bring together the finest local ingredients, paired with specialty small plates from our culinary team, in an exclusive cocktail bar environment.
Valentine's Day Dinner
Indulge in an evening of romance this Valentine’s Day at Degrees Bistro. Delight in a refined chef‑crafted menu, a warm, intimate ambiance, and the heartfelt hospitality of The Ritz‑Carlton—perfect for celebrating the one you love.
