# Boutique Narita

A World of Style at Kempinski Cancún

### Boutique Narita

Nestled within Kempinski Hotel Cancún, Boutique Narita offers an exclusive selection of international fashion for those who appreciate luxury in every detail. Our boutiques, **Camilla** and **La Perla**, feature globally renowned brands celebrated for their design, sophistication, and exceptional quality.

### **Exclusive Collections**

**Camilla**

Australian luxury fashion infused with vibrant prints and flowing silhouettes. From kaftans and dresses to beach ensembles, each piece tells a story inspired by cultures around the world.

_For women who embrace freedom and bohemian elegance with a luxurious twist._

**La Perla**

Iconic Italian lingerie and ready-to-wear, crafted with impeccable artistry. Silk sets, refined sleepwear, and intimate apparel celebrating femininity and timeless elegance.

_For those who value refined intimacy and sophisticated comfort._

**Escada**

Contemporary luxury with bold colors and structured silhouettes. Cocktail dresses, tailored suits, and statement accessories designed for modern elegance.

_For powerful women seeking confidence and personality in every look._

**Ramy Brook**

New York sophistication meets versatility. Feminine blouses, premium denim, and transitional dresses perfect for day-to-night styling.

_For urban women looking for chic, effortless pieces for every occasion._
