# Welcome to JW Marriott Quito

## Mindful experiences await in the heart of Ecuador.

In the middle of the world, Ecuador welcomes you to its capital, where elegance and comfort meet at the impressive JW Marriott Quito. Located in the heart of the city, our luxury hotel in Quito offers an unparalleled experience. Our luxurious rooms combine elegant style with first-class amenities. For an even more exclusive stay, choose one of our suites or executive rooms, which feature private balconies...See More and additional amenities. Discover some of the finest restaurants in Quito without leaving the hotel, featuring Ecuadorian specialties, international cuisine and signature dining experiences. Relax at the Zumay Health Club and Spa with body therapies, facials, manicures and pedicures, or stay active in our outdoor pool and fully equipped fitness center. Recognized among the leading business and event hotels in Quito, JW Marriott Quito features 20 elegant meeting and event venues, outstanding catering services and expert planners for conferences, weddings and social celebrations.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Quito?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Quito is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Quito allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Quito.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Quito?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Quito. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Quito, with rates of $1.50 per hour, $5.00 per day, and $35.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Quito have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Quito has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Quito booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Quito?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Quito is Mariscal Sucre International Airport (UIO). UIO is located approximately 37.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Quito have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Quito has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Quito have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Quito has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
