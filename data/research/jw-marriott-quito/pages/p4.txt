# Discover Quito

## Experiences

Explore Quito's rich history and culture from our centrally located hotel. Discover mindful moments in the heart of Ecuador's vibrant capital.

## Swimming Pool

Indulge in ultimate relaxation at our resort-styled swimming pool. Immerse yourself in pure bliss as you unwind by the crystal-clear waters. Escape to paradise, where luxury and serenity meet at our exquisite poolside oasis.

## Relaxation and Well-Being

At JW Marriott Quito, your well-being is our priority. The exclusive spa at our luxury Quito hotel offers an oasis with relaxation and rejuvenation as top priorities. Enjoy a wide range of indulgent treatments and immerse yourself in absolute tranquility. We also provide a fully equipped fitness center for those who wish to stay fit during their stay.

## Allow Quito To Leave You Breathless

Just minutes from JW Marriott Quito, explore Quito's Historic Center, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Latin America's best-preserved colonial districts. Discover charming boutiques, renowned restaurants, museums, and magnificent churches that showcase Ecuador's rich cultural heritage.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Quito have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Quito offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-10:30 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cable machines, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Quito have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Quito has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Adults' Pool (Outdoor)Outdoor Adults Only Whirlpool (Outdoor)Children's Pool (Outdoor)Whirlpool

Does JW Marriott Quito have an on-site spa?

Yes, Zumay Health Club is located on-site at JW Marriott Quito.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Quito?

Banco Central Museum, Bird Sanctuary - Mindo Nambillo, Casa de la Cultura, Cotopaxi Volcano National Park, Fray Pedro Bedón Dominican Museum and more are nearby JW Marriott Quito.
