### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Quito have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Quito have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Quito?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Quito is the Presidential Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Quito?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Quito permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Quito have fridges?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Quito have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Quito have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Quito have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Quito have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Quito have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at JW Marriott Quito have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at JW Marriott Quito have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
