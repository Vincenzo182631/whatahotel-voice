## Transportation

Getting here

Narita Airport (NRT)74 kilometers74 kilometers

Transportation Options

Chauffeur Services

A chauffeur service is available between Narita Airport and our hotel. The journey takes about 90 minutes. Please click the link below for more details.

Chauffeur Services

Narita Express

Take the Narita Express to Tokyo Station, then take a taxi or the subway to the hotel. The journey takes about 90 minutes total. Please see below for detailed directions from Tokyo Station.

Airport Limousine Bus

Take the Airport Limousine Bus to Roppongi. The journey takes about 120 minutes. Please click the link below for reservations and the latest information.

Airport Limousine Reservation Website

Haneda Airport (HND)18 kilometers18 kilometers

Transportation Options

Chauffeur Services

A chauffeur service is available between Haneda Airport and our hotel. The journey takes about 40 minutes. Please click the link below for more details.

Chauffeur Services

Airport Limousine Bus

This service between Haneda Airport and Grand Hyatt Tokyo is currently suspended.

Tokyo Monorail

Take the Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsuchō Station, then take a taxi from Hamamatsuchō Station. The journey takes about 50 minutes total.

Keikyū Line

Take the Keikyū Line (connects to Toei Asakusa Line) to Daimon Station. Take the Oedo Line to Roppongi Station then walk from Roppongi Station. The journey takes about one hour total.
