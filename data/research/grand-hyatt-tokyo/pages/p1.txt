## Discover a True Luxury Hotel in the Heart of Tokyo

Experience moments of more at a dynamic lifestyle destination hotel in the heart of Roppongi, Tokyo. Grand Hyatt Tokyo is the perfect base from which to explore the city, centrally located in a lively international district and directly connected to Roppongi Hills, a cultural hub which houses 250 high-end shops and restaurants, a cinema, an art museum and an observatory. The hotel is just a 3-minute walk from Roppongi Station and provides easy access to popular areas such as Ginza and Shibuya, making it ideal for business and leisure.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Grand Hyatt Tokyo’s 387 contemporary rooms, including 28 suites, combine Japan’s aesthetics with rich mahogany furnishings to create a warm ambience. Apart from luxury amenities, bathrooms are spacious and beds are lined with soft Egyptian cotton sheets that elevate relaxation.

### The Oak Door, Steakhouse

The Oak Door is one of the best iconic steakhouse in Tokyo featuring premium quality meats and seasonal produce that are dynamically prepared and presented. Savor perfectly-cooked dishes ranging from the juicy Tomahawk steak to the signature burger, pair them with a varieties of New World wines in this elegant steakhouse that has a vibrant open kitchen, intimate booth and outdoor terrace seating, a lively bar and a luxurious private dining room. ■ All areas are non-smoking including the bar, terrace, and private room. ■ Please note that access to The Oak Door Bar is not permitted for guests under the age of 20 after 10:00 p.m. even if accompanied by parents or an adult guardian.
