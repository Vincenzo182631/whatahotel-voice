With over 387 hotel rooms, including 27 executive suites and a presidential suite with a private pool, Grand Hyatt Tokyo has an abundance of luxury rooms that are perfect for travelers across both business and leisure. Grand Hyatt Tokyo incorporates subtle Japanese aesthetics into the design and offers rich mahogany furnishings made with natural woods and fabrics to create a warm residential ambience. Featuring high ceilings, furniture by B&B Italia and light earth-tone colors and textures, these spacious hotel rooms offer the ideal environment for relaxation. The suites include luxurious beds with elegant Egyptian cotton linens by Frette, as well as spacious limestone bathrooms with deep-soaking hot tubs and invigorating rain showers that add to the overall pampering experience. Offering personalized service and exclusive accommodations, the Grand Club is a private lounge located on the 10th floor. Guests staying in suites and rooms with Club access may enjoy a private check-in and checkout service, a personalized concierge, meeting room usage, and complimentary breakfast, evening cocktails, canapés and refreshments at the Grand Club Lounge.
