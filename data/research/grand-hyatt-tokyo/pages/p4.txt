## Accessibility At Our Hotel

### Accessible Hotel Parking and Transportation

- Valet Parking
- Van-Accessible Self-Parking
- Self-Parking for Cars
- Accessible Transportation with Advance Notice

### Additional Accessibility Information

- Hotel employees will read printed/visual information to guests who are blind/low vision and provide oral directions to locations within the property
- Service animals are welcome
