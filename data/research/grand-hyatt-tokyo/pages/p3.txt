# Restaurants & Bars

At Grand Hyatt Tokyo in Roppongi Hills, you can indulge in a range of sophisticated cuisine in the luxurious atmosphere of our restaurants and bars. Our 10 stylish venues offer unique menus, including Japanese, Edo-mae sushi, French bistro, and steakhouse options, as well as a jazz lounge where you can enjoy live music. Several locations have open-air terrace seating, allowing you to choose the ideal restaurant or bar for any occasion.

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.
