# Accommodations at JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo

## Accommodations

Elevated above Tokyo’s emerging Takanawa Gateway district, the rooms and suites at JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo offer a refined sanctuary where contemporary luxury meets serene design. Expansive floor-to-ceiling windows with captivating views of Tokyo Bay and the city skyline, inviting natural light to flow through elegant interiors inspired by the principles of Zen.Crafted with warm natural materials and...See More soft textures, each space reflects a harmonious balance of comfort and sophistication. Thoughtfully designed layouts, luxurious bedding, and curated amenities create an atmosphere that encourages relaxation and renewal. Whether welcoming the calm of morning light or the quiet glow of Tokyo by night, every room is designed to inspire mindful moments. Here, guests are invited to pause, reconnect, and experience the signature sense of tranquility and understated luxury that defines JW Marriott.See Less

Mindful Experiences at JW

### JW Mindful Stay for Rest and Renewal

Experience a stay designed for balance and renewal in our Mindful Rooms. Calming interiors and thoughtfully curated amenities, including yoga mats, meditation tools and organic herbal teas, create space for mindful moments. Guests also enjoy a private check-in and check-out experience at Spa by JW, offering a serene and seamless arrival and departure.

Discover More

### Restore and Renew at Spa by JW

Restore balance at Spa by JW, where personalized treatments are inspired by mindfulness and holistic wellbeing. Thoughtfully designed rituals combine relaxation, movement and restorative therapies to help guests pause, renew and reconnect in a tranquil setting.

Explore Spa by JW

### Serene Space

Serene Space is a quiet sanctuary located on the lobby level, open to all guests seeking a moment of calm. Designed for meditation and reflection, the space invites guests to pause, breathe and reconnect. Soft lighting and tranquil surroundings create a peaceful retreat within the rhythm of the city.
