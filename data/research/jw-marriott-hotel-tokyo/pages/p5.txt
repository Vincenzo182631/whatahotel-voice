### A Night Above the City

A truly exclusive experience awaits with our Private Night Pool, available exclusively to in-house guests. From 9:40 PM to 10:40 PM, the pool may be reserved for one group per evening, offering a fully private setting. Surrounded by the Tokyo skyline, including views of Tokyo Tower, this intimate experience invites guests to unwind in complete tranquility. Availability is limited and subject to reservation. Additional charges apply.

Reservations must be made at least 7 days in advance. For inquiries and bookings, please contact the Spa.

## Morning Yoga

Begin your day at our wellness hotel in Shinagawa with a guided morning yoga session designed to gently awaken the body and mind. Set in a calm and light-filled environment, this experience encourages balance, focus, and a mindful start to the day. Available for in-house guests, reservations may be arranged at check-in, subject to limited availability.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Free weight equipment, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa by JW is located on-site at JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo. Click here to book a service now.
