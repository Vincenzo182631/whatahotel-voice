# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo

## A Tranquil Urban Retreat in the Heart of Tokyo

JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo invites guests to experience refined luxury in the heart of Takanawa Gateway City. Inspired by Zen philosophy and the natural beauty of Goten-yama, this luxury hotel in Shinagawa offers tranquil, artfully designed guestrooms with panoramic city views. Every detail—from the tactile materials to curated lighting—supports a journey of mindfulness and well-being. Guests at our downtown...See More hotel in Shinagawa can savor world-class dining, including omakase-style Japanese cuisine and modern Mediterranean fare, alongside a curated selection of premium sake and champagne. With thoughtful service and elevated design, the hotel near Takanawa Gateway Station is a true sanctuary in Tokyo.See Less

### Hotel Information

JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo is located in Takanawa, just a one-minute walk from Takanawa Gateway Station. From the South Gate, turn left and proceed straight ahead along the pedestrian walkway, without going down the stairs. The downtown hotel in Shinagawa is located within the distinctive white lattice building. Sengakuji Station is within a 7-minute walk. Haneda Airport can be reached in approximately 20 minutes.

### Presidential Suite Experience

Elevate your stay in our Presidential Suite with a collection of exclusive privileges. Your stay includes airport transfer by hotel car, a curated selection of Mediterranean wines and Japanese spirits, and your choice of a private in-suite sake or Japanese tea experience. Each element is thoughtfully designed to create a more personalized and memorable stay.

Explore the Presidential Suite

### Dining Experience

Discover thoughtfully crafted dining experiences at JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo, from Japanese cuisine and modern Mediterranean dining to Afternoon Tea and Cocktails. Our restaurants and bars celebrate seasonal ingredients and distinctive flavors, inviting you to slow down, connect, and savor the moment. A variety of dining experiences and party plans are available for business gatherings and social occasions.

Explore Dining

### Private Night Pool

An exclusive private night pool experience, available for one group per day. Enjoy a serene hour from 9:40 PM to 10:40 PM in a calm, elevated setting. This experience is available to in-house guests only and is offered at an additional charge. For reservations and inquiries, please contact Spa by JW.

### Parking

Valet parking is available at the hotel for JPY 8,000 per day. Self-parking is available at the nearby TAKANAWA GATEWAY CITY Parking P1 South (Times) for JPY 1,000 per hour, with a maximum charge of JPY 2,500 for up to 24 hours. Vehicle entry and exit are available from 6:30 AM to 11:00 PM. Please note that parking fees are charged each time the vehicle exits the parking facility.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo, with rates of ¥1000.00 per hour, and ¥2500.00 per day. Valet parking is available for ¥8000.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo is Tokyo International Airport - Haneda (HND). HND is located approximately 14.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo have an airport shuttle to Tokyo Narita International Airport (NRT)?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo has an airport shuttle to Tokyo Narita International Airport (NRT).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo have an airport shuttle to Tokyo International Airport - Haneda (HND)?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo has an airport shuttle to Tokyo International Airport - Haneda (HND).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
