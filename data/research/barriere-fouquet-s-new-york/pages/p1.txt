Nestled on Tribeca’s cobblestone streets, Fouquet’s New York channels the spirit of its iconic Parisian maison with a New York flair. Designed by Martin Brudnizki, the hotel blends 1930s Art Deco glamour—brass, velvet, and soft lighting—with an intimate, residential feel. Guests can savor clas...sic French cuisine at the Brasserie, cocktails at the speakeasy Titsou Bar, Mediterranean flavors at Élysée’s, and seasonal rooftop moments at Le Vaux. A full service Spa and panoramic terrace suites complete the experience, uniting Parisian refinement with New York’s vibrant energy.See more

For all World Cup related questions and experiences at Fouquet's New York, please visit the page here.

### What time is check-in and what time is check-out? What is the process?

**Check-in:**

Check-in is at 3:00 PM and we ask guests to visit the Front Desk and provide their credit card and photo identification. The credit card provided at Check-in will not be charged at this time. We aim to accommodate you as early as possible, but in the case that you arrive prior to 3:00 PM and your room is not quite ready, we can store your luggage.

**Check-out:**

Check-out is at 12:00 PM. If you require additional time, please contact Front Desk to request a late check-out, which will be subject to availability.

### What is the cancellation policy at Fouquet’s New York?

Fouquet’s New York’s standard cancellation policy is up to 48 hours prior to arrival before 3:00 PM for stays booked directly. If a contractual agreement has been signed, the agreed-upon policy applies. In the event of late cancellations or no-shows, a penalty of 100% of the first night will apply. This rate will not include applicable hotel occupancy taxes and fees.

Non-Refundable Cancellation Policy: In the event of a non-refundable reservation, please note your entire stay will be charged in full in the event of cancellation.

### How can I arrange an airport transfer?

An airport transfer can be arranged by emailing our Concierge Team prior to your arrival at concierge-ny@fouquets.com with your flight number, estimated arrival/departure time, size of your party, and the number of pieces of luggage you will be traveling with.

### Does Fouquet’s New York offer valet parking?

Fouquet’s New York does offer valet parking for hotel guests only and there is no requirement to arrange services in advance. The daily rate for parking is $110 for sedans and $120 for SUVs per day, and this will be charged to your master bill upon check-out. Please note, additional fees will occur for multiple same-day in and outs.

### What is the pet policy at Fouquet’s New York?

Fouquet’s New York is dog-friendly, welcoming dogs that weigh up to 30 pounds, complimentary. We kindly ask that you keep your dogs on a leash in all public areas, and not to leave your pet unattended. Dogs are not permitted in Restaurants, Bars, Spa, Fitness Center, or Cinema with the exception of Certified Service Dogs. Please note, for the safety of our team, rooms will not be serviced while your pet is left unattended in your room. Guests are responsible for any damages incurred by dogs. Please note, Fouquet’s New York only permits dogs (cats and other animals are not permitted on premise).

### What is the smoking policy at Fouquet’s New York?

Smoking is prohibited in our public areas, rooms, or suites. Guests who violate this policy will be responsible for a cleaning fee of one night's room charge. Additional fees may incur should the room require a deep cleaning.

### Do you offer cribs and rollaway beds?

Cribs and rollaway beds are available upon request and will be placed in your room upon arrival. Please note, rollaway beds are only available for placement in One- Bedroom Suites, Terrace Suites, and the Penthouse for a fee of $150 per night.

### Do you offer connecting or adjoining rooms?

Fouquet’s New York offers both connecting and adjoining rooms as options for your stay.

Connecting rooms will guarantee access to each room within each guest room. We offer up to two and three-bedroom connecting suites.

Adjoining rooms will ensure your rooms are nearby and on the same floor, but may not connect within.

Please reach out to our reservations team at reservations-team-ny@groupbarriere.com to best assist in choosing the best rooms for your stay.

### What is included in the minibars at the hotel?

Please find the minibar menus for our guest Rooms and Suites. If you have specific preferences or dietary restrictions, please contact reservations-ny@fouquets.com prior to your stay with your requests.

### What are the dining options at the hotels and the hours of operation?

In-Room Dining: 24 Hours.

Brasserie Fouquet’s: Brasserie Fouquet’s is open for dinner service on Sunday-Thursday from 5:00PM - 10:00PM and Friday and Saturday from 5:00PM - 11:00PM. Brunch is available on Saturdays and Sundays from 7:30AM - 4:00PM. Reservations are available on Resy or through our Concierge at concierge-ny@fouquets.com for hotel guests.

Élysée’s : Élysée’s is open for breakfast Monday through Saturday from 7:00AM -11:00AM. Lunch is served Monday through Saturday from 11:00AM - 4:00PM. Dinner is available Thursdays through Saturday from 4:00PM - 10:00PM. Reservations are available on Resy or through our Concierge at concierge-ny@fouquets.com for hotel guests.

Titsou Bar: Titsou Bar is open Sunday 4:00PM to 11:00PM, Monday through Wednesday, 4:00PM to 12:00AM; Thursday through Saturday 4:00AM to 1:00AM. Seating is open to the public on a first come, first served basis. Only hotel guests can reserve a table through the Concierge at concierge-ny@fouquets.com.

Le Vaux: Seasonal Guest-Only Rooftop reopening in May. Service hours to follow.

### Does the hotel offer 24-hour in-room dining?

Yes, guests can order room service at any hour by pressing the “Room Service” button on their guest room phone. (Rooms Menu, Suites Menu)

### What are the wellness and fitness offerings at the hotel and the hours of operation?

Spa Fouquet's New York is our signature wellness center featuring five treatment rooms, a hydrotherapy pool, sauna, and steam room. Our facilities are open to guests and members daily from 9:00AM-7:00PM. The spa offers a melange of services including massages, facials, body treatments, and couples treatments, available to be booked by both hotel guests and non-guests online or by emailing spa-ny@fouquets.com. All Spa guests will have access to spa facilities up to two hours prior or after their scheduled treatment time to enjoy.

DOGPOUND Fitness Center is open 24 hours for hotel guest and member use. Hourly personal training sessions with a DOGPOUND fitness trainer are available to book in advance of your stay. Please email spa-ny@fouquets.com to schedule your session.

### What services are complimentary with my stay? What services are available at an additional cost?

Complimentary Services: Daily housekeeping and turndown (upon request), daily access to Spa Diane Barrière and DOGPOUND Fitness Center, high-speed WiFi, Devialet bluetooth speakers, Chromecast Televisions, iPads, daily newspaper upon request, Nespresso Coffee and tea in guest rooms and suites.

Upon Request at an Additional Cost: Airport transfer, unpacking and packing services, laundry services (including pressing and dry-cleaning), shoeshining, personal training sessions, private theatre rental or meeting room rental. Please contact reservations-ny@fouquets.com prior to your stay to arrange any of these services in advance.

### What is the guest-policy at the private rooftop, Le Vaux?

Le Vaux is a guest and members-only rooftop. Le Vaux is currently closed for the winter season and will reopen in late May. Hotel guests may invite up to three guests.

### Can guests access movie screenings in Cannes Cinema?

Cannes Cinema is available for private rental, please email events-ny@fouquets.com to inquire about hosting a private showing. Ticketed movie screenings are hosted on a seasonal basis. Please visit Resy for upcoming show times and details or email dine-ny@fouquets.com for more information.
