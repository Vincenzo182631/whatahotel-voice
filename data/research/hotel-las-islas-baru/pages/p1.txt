# What's new at Las Islas: More reasons to return

**Las Islas**is much more than a destination; it’s a place where nature and luxury meet in perfect harmony. With**new exclusive experiences**, a more focused approach to**sustainability**and**inclusion**, and the comfort of our**bungalows in Barú**,**Las Islas**becomes the ideal paradise for those seeking something different, something that leaves a mark in every corner.

Every season,**Las Islas**reinvents itself. This year, we’ve added new activities that allow for an even deeper connection with nature, without losing the comfort we are known for. We’ve taken another step in our commitment to the environment with initiatives like mangrove planting, a tangible gesture to regenerate the ecosystem around us.

## Diversity and inclusion: A real commitment

We are excited to announce that**Las Islas**is in the process of being certified as a**Friendly Biz hotel**. This certification, granted to companies that promote inclusion and respect for the LGBT+ community, reinforces our commitment to diversity and creating a safe and welcoming environment for all.

The**Friendly Biz**program seeks to recognize companies that implement inclusive policies, ensuring that all individuals, regardless of identity or sexual preference, feel welcomed and respected. By advancing towards obtaining this certification,**Las Islas**not only strengthens its commitment to**inclusion**, but also positions itself as an**accessible and respectful destination**for all, contributing to positive change in the hospitality industry.

This step is part of our mission to offer an authentic experience where every guest, regardless of identity, feels respected and valued.**At Las Islas**, inclusion is a core part of who we are.

## A worry-free stay: The full experience plan

We’ve launched our new**Full Experience**plan, designed for those who want to enjoy a stay with no worries. With**exclusive accommodation**in one of our**bungalows in Barú**, offering views of the sea or the dry tropical forest, this plan includes**à la carte meals**—lunch and dinner— transportation, and two unforgettable experiences: a spa treatment at the**Spa Niña Daniela**and a**bioluminescence tour**.

This plan is designed for stays of**two nights and three days**, but we also offer options for those who wish to extend their experience, including an upgrade to**bungalows with private pools**. Additionally, all our special plans —such as**Honey Moon**,**Mujeres**(perfect for a girls’ getaway), and**Amor y Amistad**— include a 20% discount, making every moment even more special

## Six ways to experience a perfect day: day pass plans

For those who prefer a shorter experience, our**Barú accommodation**offers six**day pass plans**ideal for different types of travelers:

- **Standard Day Pass**: Access to our facilities and non-motorized aquatic activities.
- **Gourmet Day Pass**: Includes lunch with a preset menu at one of our restaurants.
- **Complete Day Pass**: Everything from the gourmet day pass, plus round-trip land transportation. ﻿

- **Relax Day Pass**: Includes a massage for two people at the**Spa Niña Daniela**.
- **Overflight Day Pass**: Arrive at the hotel by**helicopter**, enjoying the aerial view of the Caribbean.
- **Bungalow Day Pass**: The most exclusive option, with the use of an**elevated bungalow**for the full day.

﻿

Each of these plans offers access to the facilities, unique experiences, and the best of**Las Islas'**gastronomy.

## Sail towards the golden magic of Barú

One of the most exclusive experiences we’ve created this year is the**Sunset in Casa Navegante**. This unique two-hour tour departs every evening towards**Ciénaga de Cholón**, offering**canapés aboard**and the option to enjoy a selection of drinks, while the sun begins to set over the horizon.

It’s the perfect experience to enjoy the Caribbean in an unparalleled way, while sailing in the**Casa Navegante**boat, a unique space with**jacuzzi**,**bar**, and**lounge areas**. If you’re wondering**what to do in Barú**, this tour is just one of the many exclusive experiences we offer, connecting you with the essence of the Caribbean.

**At Las Islas, the extraordinary awaits.**This is not just a destination: it’s an invitation to live moments that redefine what it means to be in the Caribbean. Every sunset, every activity, every corner is designed to leave an indelible mark on your soul.
