# Tree Top Bungalow WITH JACUZZI

(8 or 12 meters above ground)

These treetop bungalows offer partial views of the sea and our tropical forest; they are between 70-80 square meters in size and are either eight or 12 meters above the ground. They include a furnished deck with a hammock and a hydromassage Jacuzzi tub, a Double or two Twin beds, air conditioning, complimentary Wi-Fi and a flat-screen TV. The bathroom is stocked with premium products, as well as a washlet toilet, a freshwater and warm shower, a robe and slippers.
