# Bars & Restaurants

## OFFERING EXQUISITE CUISINES AND DRINKS

We are part of something greater, and our gastronomy tells a story; it is a dialogue with the land, an invitation to focus on the present and enjoy every moment. Our restaurants and bars at Hotel Las Islas offer a wide culinary selection. We take advantage of the generosity of nature and our location by the sea to create fresh, seasonal cuisine with an emphasis on fish and local products. Room service is from 06:00 am to  11:00 pm , allowing you to dine in the comfort of your bungalow.
