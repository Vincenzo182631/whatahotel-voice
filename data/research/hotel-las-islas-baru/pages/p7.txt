# Contact & Location

**Hotel Las Islas** welcomes you to a unique and exclusive accommodation, located on the seafront within **the Rosario Islands National Park**. A broad space in the middle of nature inspired by rest and well-being.

## HOW TO GET HERE

Transport options

## Hotel speedboats

The hotel offers a daily shared boat service departing at noon (12:00 pm) from the hotel and at 2:00 pm from Cartagena. It is also possible to arrange a private boat transfer at custom times during the day. The boat ride from the La Bodeguita dock in Cartagena to the hotel takes approximately 45 minutes.

## Private Boats

Guests with their own boats can arrive directly at the hotel's dock.

## Helicopter

Our hotel has a heliport, and one of the most rewarding experiences is enjoying the aerial view of the Rosario Islands. We offer helicopter service to and from Cartagena, with an approximate flight time of 18 minutes. It is also possible to coordinate private flights from other cities such as Santa Marta and Barranquilla. Coordinates: LAT 75 ° 41'03, 5''W - LON 10 ° 09'0,1''N. This service must be coordinated and confirmed in advance.

## Private vehicles

You can also reach the hotel by private vehicle. The journey from Rafael Núñez International Airport takes approximately 90 minutes.
