Transfer to Isleta is gratuitous for guests of Las Islas,**subject to availability**and should be coordinated with hotel and Isleta reception staff.

## Tour the Archipielago of San Bernardo

The San Bernardo Islands are made up of a group of 10 islands located on the Gulf of Morrosquillo: Boquerón, Palma, Panda, Mangle, Ceycén, Cabruna, Tintipán, Wonder, Múcura and the artificial island of Santa Cruz del Islote, a hidden wonder in the Carribean sea. During the tour you will visit Santa Cruz del Islote, famous for being one of the most densely populated islands on earth.

## Tour the Rosario Islands (Islas del Rosario)

The archipelago of the Rosario Islands is made up of a group of islands of varying sizes surrounded by crystal clear waters and a fantastic coral reef. On this tour you can take a panoramic tour around most of the islands with the possibility of visiting the beach on Isla Arena or Casimba, snorkeling or diving in select spots, or visiting the Rosario Islands Oceanarium. ​
