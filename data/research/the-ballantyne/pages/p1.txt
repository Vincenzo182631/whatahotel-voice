# Welcome to The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte

## Experience the charm of our 4-star hotel in Charlotte, NC

The Ballantyne, A Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte provides luxurious accommodations in an exceptional location, moments from premier shopping in SouthPark, exciting outdoor recreation at Whitewater...See More Center, Ballantyne neighborhood dining and entertainment. Luxuriate in Charlotte, Ballantyne, at our 4-star hotel or wade in the refreshing pools. Treat yourself to afternoon tea served with exquisite sweets and savories. Experience Dunbar Social by Topgolf Swing Suite for golf and game entertainment. In your spacious hotel room, explore the in-room dining menu and order scrumptious cuisine from Gallery Restaurant, offering delightful dining and weekend brunch. Savor cocktails at The Ryal bar offering picturesque outdoor views. Stroll through Stream Park featuring walking paths and a playground. Walk to The Amp at Ballantyne, a live music venue located within a couple of blocks of The Ballantyne. Choose from a collection of indoor and outdoor event venues for meetings, weddings, and occasions.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte?

The check-in time at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte.

What are the parking options at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte?

On-site parking is available at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte. Self parking is available at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte, with rates of $0.00 per hour, and $20.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $30.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte booking.

What is the closest airport near The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte?

The closest airport near The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte is Charlotte Douglas International Airport (CLT). CLT is located approximately 18.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
