# Discover our hotel in Charlotte, NC with an indoor pool

## Experiences

The Ballantyne neighborhood surrounding our hotel in Charlotte, NC, offers the best of Southern entertainment, fashion, dining, architecture, and art. Discover restaurants at The Bowl at Ballantyne™ within...See More a short walk or experience live music at The Amp at Ballantyne™. Our luxury hotel is near SouthPark Mall, Charlotte, while South Carolina is just three miles down Johnston Road. With our free shuttle, reach the offices at Ballantyne Corporate Park or shopping and dining at Ballantyne Village. Exit east off the I-485 for Charlotte Motor Speedway and west for the U.S. National Whitewater Center. Venture into Uptown Charlotte, the country's third-largest financial district, or Levine Center for the Arts and Bechtler Museum of Modern Art in the city center. Seek out the AvidXchange Music Factory or Bank of America Stadium and BB&T Ballpark sports arena. Pool Hours & Information

Swim year-round at our hotel in Charlotte at the indoor grotto inspired pool, ranging in depth from 3.5 to 4.5 feet. A soothing whirlpool can be found in the indoor pool facility which features lounge chairs and an outdoor patio with picturesque views. Locker rooms with dry saunas are also available.

Relax at the seasonal outdoor pool during your stay in Charlotte from May - October. Enjoy enhanced seating options, summer cocktails, exclusive amenities, a full pool bar and a delicious pool menu. Cabana rentals are available at (704) 248-4141

The Indoor pool is open April – October from 8am – 8pm and includes a whirlpool and his and her lockers with saunas.

Outdoor pool open 10am – 6pm March 30 – October 31, 2020

The outdoor pool may be closed during inclement weather.

Cabana rentals (704) 248- 4100

Accessible pool lift is available.

Pool towels are provided.

Contact the concierge in advance about pool information at (704) 248-4055.

**Please Note:**

Be aware that there are no lifeguards on duty, swim at your own risk.

Children under 10 should be supervised.

Diving is not permitted.

Outside coolers are not permitted.

Please be respectful of guests relaxing in the pool areas.

## Ballantyne Bikes

Hotel guests enjoy complimentary cruiser bike rental at The Ballantyne. We offer transportation to nearby Greenway trail so riders can explore the Ballantyne Community and activities in Charlotte, NC. Helmets are available; inquire at the concierge desk.

### About the Ballantyne Area

The Ballantyne is set in the lovely Ballantyne community of South Charlotte. Enjoy shopping, dining, and retail at the Bowl, when you stay at our luxury hotel near Southpark Mall in Charlotte, NC. Our convenient location is 18 miles from downtown and Charlotte Douglas International Airport.

## Ballantyne Highlights

Ballantyne area shuttle is offered within one mile, complimentary to guests on a first come, first served basis. Please arrive up to 30 minutes prior to your anticipated departure time.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bikes and Hydro Rower, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
