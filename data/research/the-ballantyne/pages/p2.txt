### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte?

The largest occupancy room at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte is the 4 Bedroom Cottage with a maximum occupancy of 8.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte?

Some rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have fridges?

No, rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The Ballantyne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Charlotte have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
