# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Beijing

## JW Marriott Beijing·CBD·Metro to Forbidden City & Universal

Located in China Central Place, Beijing CBD, JW Marriott Hotel Beijing is near the Embassy District and just a 3-minute walk from Dawanglu Station (Subway Lines 1 & 14). Our business hotel in the CBD, Beijing, offers direct metro access to the Forbidden City and Universal Beijing Resort, with both airports 45–50 minutes away by car. The hotel boasts 588 spacious rooms (starting from 42 sqm) with panoramic...See More windows, marble bathrooms, rain showers, soaking tubs and signature bedding. Griffin-themed family rooms come with playful welcome gifts for kids. Guests can relax in the 1,000-sqm JW Garden, featuring a pickleball court, yoga lawn and fresh herbs. Asia Bistro serves a Chinese and Japanese buffet with alfresco garden seating. Twelve event venues include a 1,230-sqm ballroom. The hotel's Executive Lounge and 24-hour gym blend business and leisure seamlessly. Where calm meets the capital—stay in the moment at JW Marriott.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Beijing allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Beijing, with rates of ¥12.00 per hour, ¥288.00 per day, and ¥2016.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Beijing have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Beijing has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Beijing booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Beijing?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Beijing is Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK). PEK is located approximately 27.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Beijing have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Beijing does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Beijing have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Beijing has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
