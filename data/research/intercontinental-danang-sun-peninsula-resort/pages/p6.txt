### Penthouse and villa collection

A uniquely Vietnamese experience is in store for those staying in our Club Penthouse and Villa Collection. These accommodations define privacy, seclusion and style, featuring lavish spaces with expansive indoor and outdoor areas, authentic design touches, and private pools.

Learn more

### Explore the rainforest

Our lush gardens are just the start; the surrounding forests are home to unique flora and fauna, offering opportunities for natural encounters found nowhere else. Enjoy walks in the jungle or join our Nature expert on a Wildlife Expedition.

### La Maison 1888

Step in from the rainforest to Paris with Michelin-chef Christian Le Squer. La Maison 1888 remains Central Vietnam’s only Michelin-starred restaurant, retaining its star for the second consecutive year. It features the best wine selection in Vietnam, with award-winning sommeliers guiding you through a cellar of one-of-a-kind, prestigious treasures.

## FAQs for InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort

Does InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort have a pool?

InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort has 1 outdoor pool.

L\_O\_N\_G Pool

Dive right in or relax in a chaise longue at any of our three inviting swimming pools.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort for hotel guests.

Free parking is available next to the hotel reception.

Parking fee: 0 USD per day

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort has 4 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort?

Check-in at InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort is conveniently located near Danang International Airport (DAD), approximately 13.05 miles away (21 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort?

InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Hue - 62.14 miles away (100 kilometers)
- Hoi An - 29.2 miles away (47 kilometers)
- My Son Sanctuary - 58.6 miles away (94.3 kilometers)
- Cham Museum - 11.62 miles away (18.7 kilometers)
- Linh Ung Pagoda - 11.12 miles away (17.9 kilometers)
- Bana Hills - 27.96 miles away (45 kilometers)
- Marble Mountains - 13.05 miles away (21 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
