# InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula ResortDining

A true dining destination, InterContinental Danang invites you to savour Michelin-starred French cuisine at La Maison 1888, sample Vietnamese specialties or Sunday brunch at Citron, or enjoy modern Italian flavours at Terra Mare.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

### La Maison 1888

Step in from the rainforest to Paris with Michelin-chef Christian Le Squer. La Maison 1888 remains Central Vietnam’s only Michelin-starred restaurant, retaining its star for the second consecutive year. It features the best wine selection in Vietnam, with award-winning sommeliers guiding you through a cellar of one-of-a-kind, prestigious treasures.

## Citron

Citron is a true culinary map of Vietnam, with specialties from the north, central and south of the country. Enjoy a lavish international buffet breakfast or dining throughout the day, including lunch, afternoon tea and dinner. The views are extraordinary as the restaurant soars high over the resort and our private bay. Uniquely designed open air dining booths shaped like traditional non la hats are a perennial favourite.

## The L\_o\_n\_g Bar

Our uniquely designed beachfront bar is among Vietnam’s longest (50 meters!), with extra size daybeds, an elongated surfboard table and hanging chairs inspired by fishermen’s baskets. The daybeds are the perfect place to soak up the sea breeze while enjoying a light snack, sipping an exotic juice or sampling our signature Sala Negroni. There is no age restriction to enter The L\_o\_n\_g Bar.
