# The Terrace

##### Your space in the open air

Welcome to the relaxed charm of The Terrace at The Killarney Park—an inviting extension of The Garden Bar, ideal for casual get-togethers, special occasions, or simply enjoying food and drinks in the open air.

The indoor heated sunroom opens up to let the outdoors in, while the outdoor area features spacious dining tables, comfortable deck seating, and views across the garden. Whether you're marking a moment or just unwinding, The Terrace offers a laid-back, welcoming setting.

## The natural choice for important celebrations

The Terrace at The Killarney Park offers a relaxed and stylish setting for private gatherings and special events. Whether you're planning a family celebration, a milestone occasion, or a casual get-together with friends, this versatile space provides a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

With a heated sunroom that opens to the garden and an outdoor area featuring spacious tables and comfortable seating, The Terrace blends comfort and charm. It’s the perfect spot to celebrate moments that matter, surrounded by nature and great company.
