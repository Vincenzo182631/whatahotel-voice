# Afternoon Tea

- First Sunday of every Month

View Menu Reserve My Afternoon Tea Experience

##### Delightful decadence. A timeless tradition

It begins with the setting. The warm elegance of The Peregrine provides the perfect backdrop for the gracious ritual of Afternoon Tea at The Killarney Park. Soft velvet, glittering chandeliers, fine china and napkins of crisp Irish linen: every lovely detail comes together to create a joyful ambiance of refined indulgence.

It may be called Afternoon _Tea_, but the real star of the show is the iconic tiered cake stand of tempting treats. Dainty savouries and sandwiches, delicate pastries, sweets and scones with handmade preserves and rich clotted cream, this procession of perfect bites is a feast in perfect miniature. We have also created a vegan and vegetarian Afternoon Tea menu of plant-based delicacies. Of course, a fragrant pot of Sustainably Certified tea completes the scene, but if the occasion calls for it, you might enjoy the addition of a creative, hand-crafted cocktail or a sparkling glass of prosecco or champagne.

## Set aside an afternoon for magical treats and tea

The most wonderful meal of them all does not happen every day! At the Killarney Park, Afternoon Tea is served on the first Sunday of each month, and reservations are highly encouraged.

If you are feeling fancy, Afternoon Tea in The Peregrine is a wonderful opportunity to dress up, but it is absolutely not required! Wear whatever brings you joy: it is the company, not the clothes, that makes the occasion.

Our Afternoon Tea menus change frequently to reflect the shifting seasons and the creativity of our talented pastry chefs. If you have questions or specific dietary requirements that we should know about, please let us know when you book, and we will be happy to help.

## Everyone should enjoy the magic of Afternoon Tea

At The Killarney Park our vegan and vegetarian menus are thoughtfully created with the same care and creativity as our classic offering—featuring plant-based delights, freshly baked scones, and elegant sweet treats.

We are also happy to accommodate a wide range of dietary needs and special requests. Simply let us know when booking, and our team will ensure your Afternoon Tea experience is tailored to you, without compromise.

## Festive Afternoon Tea

This December, experience the season’s finest moments with our **Festive Afternoon Tea**, hosted in the elegant surrounds of **The Peregrine**. Guests are invited to savour an afternoon of indulgence, beautifully complemented by festive entertainment.

Enjoy a carefully curated menu of seasonal delights — from delicately spiced pastries and warm, buttery scones to elegant finger sandwiches and handcrafted desserts, each crafted with the flavours of Christmas in mind. The enchanting atmosphere of The Peregrine, enhanced by live festive performances, creates the perfect backdrop for unhurried conversation and shared celebration.

Our Festive Afternoon Tea will be priced at €60 per person and served on the following dates:

- **Sunday, 6th December**
- **Sunday, 13th December**
- **Sunday, 20th December**

More than a dining experience, it is a moment of pause — a chance to enrich time with loved ones and embrace the joy of the season in luxurious comfort.

Festive entertainment will be provided by The Three O's to enjoy during your Afternoon Tea.

Availability is limited, and early reservations are highly recommended. To secure your place at this special celebration, please reserve your slot below or contact our dedicated team via the link.
