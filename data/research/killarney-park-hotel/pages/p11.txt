# Fitness Centre

- Open Daily from 8am to 9pm

##### Elevate the everyday with a ritual of wellness

Begin your day with intention or unwind after a fulfilling day of discovery in the serene embrace of our state-of-the-art Fitness Suite. Designed as a sanctuary for body and mind, this exclusive space offers a curated selection of premium equipment tailored to support both invigorating cardio sessions and focused strength training. Whether you're seeking a moment of energetic clarity in the morning or a reflective pause as evening falls, every visit is time well spent. Embrace the rhythm of self-care, where wellness becomes a ritual and each movement renews your mental balance. Let this be your personal reset—a luxurious interlude where health, harmony, and quiet sophistication meet in perfect synergy.

## A path to personal power

Whether you seek a revitalising cardio session or a bespoke running experience through the breathtaking beauty of Killarney National Park, our expert Personal Trainers are here to elevate your fitness journey.

With tailored one-to-one sessions and thoughtfully curated classes, each moment is designed to _inspire, challenge_, and _restore._

Embrace a program that meets you where you are, crafted to align body, mind, and environment in perfect harmony. Discover the joy of movement, guided by professionals who prioritise your wellbeing with elegance and precision.

##### Peloton bikes are the ultimate high-quality, immersive workout experience.

With a sleek design, smooth ride, and the ability to stream live and on-demand classes from top instructors, Peloton bikes are the perfect choice for fitness-minded travellers.

Our Peloton bike offers a range of benefits, including the ability to track progress, customise workouts to individual needs, and improve cardiovascular health. Whether you’re a beginner or an experienced cyclist, Peloton bikes are a great way to stay active and healthy during your stay.

Booking your Peloton bike experience is easy – simply visit our fitness centre and reserve the bike. Our team of experienced fitness professionals will be on hand to help you get started and ensure that you get the most out of your workout.
