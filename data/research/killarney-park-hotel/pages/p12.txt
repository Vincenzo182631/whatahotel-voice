# The ideal golf destination

The Killarney Park offers an ideal five-star base for your championship golf holiday. Located in the heart of Kerry, our hotel puts you within easy reach of some of Ireland’s most iconic courses. Lakeside fairways, coastal challenges, soaring mountain backdrops—Kerry’s dramatic landscapes make this region a favourite with golfers from around the world.

We know your time on the course is precious, but the hours before and after matter just as much. Let us make them exceptional with early breakfasts to set you up for the day, lively post-game hospitality, and deeply restful nights in five-star comfort, so you can do it all again tomorrow.

After your round, gather in The Peregrine for an unforgettable meal, unwind with a guided whiskey tasting in The Garden Bar, or simply retreat to your room and recharge. Travelling with friends? Our interconnecting rooms and suites offer the perfect balance of privacy and togetherness.

## European Hotel of the Year

We are deeply honoured to have been named **European Hotel of the Year** by IAGTO, the global authority in golf tourism.

Selected by over 700 specialist golf tour operators across more than 60 countries, this prestigious award is a true testament to our unwavering commitment to excellence, hospitality, and the guest experience. For us at The Killarney Park, it reflects the passion and dedication of our entire team.

To be recognised on an international stage for creating memorable stays for golf travellers is both humbling and inspiring. We remain devoted to delivering the very highest standard of service for every guest.

## Ireland’s finest courses, all within easy reach of Killarney

### Ballybunion Golf Club

Located on the North West coast of Kerry along a vast stretch of sand dunes, right on the rugged Atlantic coastline. This golf club has two unique and unforgettable links golf courses, with every hole, challenge and shot set by nature. Ballybunion deserves its place at the top of every avid golfer’s bucket list.

Two Championship Links Courses

1Hr10mins Drive from The Killarney Park

### Tralee Golf Club

Without a doubt one of the finest golf links courses in the world. A beautiful course situated beside Tralee Bay and the Slieve Mish Mountains. This outstanding course was designed by Arnold Palmer, one of the greatest golfers of all time. It is a course for beginners and professionals alike.

Championship Links Course

50min Drive from The Killarney Park

### Dooks Golf Club

One of Ireland’s oldest golf clubs founded in 1889 and located in Glenbeigh, Co. Kerry on the Iveragh Peninsula. The course is wrapped up in coastal and mountain views and is well suited for beginner golfers and those with years of experience.

Link Course

45min Drive from The Killarney Park

### Killarney Golf Club

Situated only a mile from Killarney town nestled in Killarney National Park. Golfers are surrounded by picturesque lakeside views and stunning views of Ireland’s largest mountain range the McGillycuddy Reeks. A very beautiful place to play a round, or two of golf.

Two Championship Parkland Courses

10min Drive from The Killarney Park

## Early starts, easy days, and a warm return

We know what golfers need: early starts, warm gear, and everything ready when you are.

That’s why we offer early breakfasts, packed lunches, secure golf club storage, and a purpose-built drying room that gently circulates warm air to dry your clubs, shoes, and clothing overnight—because in Kerry, a little rain is just part of the game.

Whether you're here for one unforgettable round or a full golf tour, we're here to help you stay focused, comfortable, and ready for whatever the next day brings.

## Expert local help to plan your perfect golf itinerary

Our concierge team has deep local knowledge and strong relationships with nearby golf courses. We’re delighted to share recommendations on the best courses to suit your preferences, provide local insights, and assist with information on transport, dining, and additional experiences to help you make the most of your stay. Whether you're seeking a championship challenge or a relaxed round in spectacular surroundings, we're here to help you enjoy an unforgettable golf holiday.

**Important Booking Information**

We strongly recommend reserving tee times at your chosen golf course(s) **6–8 months in advance**, as availability can fill very quickly.

Please note that a valid credit or debit card is required to secure tee time bookings. As a result, **our Concierge Department is unable to make golf bookings on your behalf**. We kindly ask that you contact each golf club directly to reserve your tee times. If required, our Concierge Department will be pleased to provide the appropriate contact details for each course.
