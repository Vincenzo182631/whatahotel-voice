### The Kingdom at your feet

For over two thousand years, the beautiful county of Kerry has been known as The Kingdom. Our location in Killarney’s town centre places you mere steps away from the very best that Kerry has to offer. We know this wonderful town like the back of our hands, and our greatest joy is to share its secrets and splendours with you.

-

### Stay present in the moment.

Your time is so precious. Our warm, thoughtful service and deep local knowledge give you the chance to slow down and savour each moment you spend here. Our close connection to the natural world and our intensely rooted sense of place make it easy to stay grounded and present. Soak it all in. Make vivid memories. Make the most of it.

-

### Hospitality is our family’s heartbeat.

Authentic Irish hospitality is the work of our lives—a labour of love passed down through the generations. Because The Killarney Park is family-owned and independent, we have the privilege of getting to know our guests individually, and the freedom to offer you a fully personalised experience. That’s a luxury we never take for granted.

## Luxury Suites

Luxury Suites

Our suites are where The Killarney Park experience resonates most deeply—where time unfolds slowly and luxury reveals itself in quiet, meaningful ways.

Each suite tells its own story, through bespoke panoramic wallpapers created from historic watercolour paintings of Killarney National Park as it appeared two hundred years ago, and handcrafted furnishings sourced from local artisans. ## Sense Of Place

Sense Of Place

Wilderness, wonder, and a living legacy...

Our namesake and design inspiration, Killarney National Park, is a 26,000-acre UNESCO biosphere reserve of pristine wilderness and breathtaking scenery. Just a two minute walk from the hotel gate brings you to its ancient oak forests and Ireland’s highest mountains, home to the country’s only wild herd of native red deer.

Songbirds, red squirrels, badgers, and foxes inhabit the trees and undergrowth, while peregrine falcons and white-tailed sea eagles soar above shimmering glacial lakes and cascading waterfalls. This unique biodiversity inspires awe and respect—and it is a legacy we are deeply committed to protecting through sustainable and regenerative tourism in our hotel and community.
