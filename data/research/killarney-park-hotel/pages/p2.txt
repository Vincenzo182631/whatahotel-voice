## Suites at The Killarney Park: Quiet luxury, amplified

Each suite tells its own story, through bespoke panoramic wallpapers created from historic watercolour paintings of Killarney National Park as it appeared two hundred years ago, and handcrafted furnishings sourced from local artisans. The decor harmonises in a self-contained world of colours, textiles, and textures, echoing the natural beauty outside your windows.

The boundaries between our elegant five-star interiors and the untamed Kerry landscape seem to blur, creating a retreat that feels at once grounded in place and elevated in comfort.

From the ethically sourced materials to the energy-efficient technology discreetly integrated throughout your suite, every handpicked detail has been chosen to enrich your experience while honouring our commitment to tread lightly on the land we love.

## Your Kingdom within The Kingdom

The Suites at The Killarney Park offer you more than just extra space. They provide a private realm, where you can fully relax and experience the warmth of Irish hospitality at its most personal and refined.

Whether you’re treating yourself to some well-deserved time to yourself or gathering with your favourite people for a special occasion, your suite is your sanctuary.

Whatever you require to feel truly at home—from extra pillows to a fully secluded block of interconnecting rooms—we are here to make sure you experience the full measure of five-star comfort you deserve and the genuine welcome that defines us.

Discover our Interleading Room Options

## Luxury, personalised

One of the best things about being a truly independent family hotel is that we are allowed to be creative.

That creativity has shaped our suites. Each one has its own handcrafted personality, and in time, most returning guests find their favourite. Nature lovers gravitate to our Garden Suites, with their private wraparound balconies—perfect for alfresco breakfasts or dreamy stargazing.

Those who value space to breathe find sanctuary in our One Bedroom Suites, where separate living and sleeping areas offer an extra measure of comfort and privacy. And for those who are travelling with family or who simply want it all, the magnificent Muckross Suite delivers five-star delight on an unforgettable scale, offering the ultimate expression of our quiet, authentic luxury.
