# Our Guest Rooms

View Photo Gallery Book Now

## Cross the threshold. Close the door. Look around slowly. Take it all in.  Then throw yourself on the bed and _breathe_.

That’s the feeling we’ve worked to capture in each of our guest rooms. The relief, the relaxation, and the gentle giddiness that lights you up when you know you’ve finally arrived. That first, perfect moment of your stay, when the reality of where you are really sinks in.

These rooms are a very personal expression of our love for Kerry and of our bone-deep devotion to real hospitality. When you’re in such a beautiful place, surrounded by the glories of the National Park, the last thing you want is to begin and end your precious days in a room that could be anywhere on earth. So we’ve designed guest rooms that could only exist in Killarney. Rooms that wrap you up in the natural beauty and living culture of our beloved home.

## Luxury that honours the land we love

We have handpicked every detail to create an atmosphere of gentle luxury that reveals itself in layers, like the landscape itself.

Soft palettes and softer textiles. Handmade furnishings and bespoke finishings. Intelligent lighting and climate control systems that work seamlessly and silently behind the scene to ensure your absolute comfort—while treading lightly on our precious earth.

Everything we have chosen for your room reflects our commitment to authenticity and environmental mindfulness. This is luxury that feels good in every sense.

## A Room for _Every Reason_

Whether you're seeking a romantic hideaway, a family adventure base, or a peaceful solo retreat, our rooms welcome every kind of traveler. Couples cosy up in our Classic Kings with their luxurious beds and sweet dining nooks. Families and friends enjoy our spacious Double Doubles with space to spread out after days of exploration. Groups travelling together appreciate the option for connecting rooms that offer a balance of togetherness and privacy.

No matter what brings you to our corner of Kerry, you'll discover five-star luxury delivered with authentic warmth, with guest rooms that will make you feel unmistakably welcome and unforgettably connected to this place.

## The secret luxury of small things

These are the little experiences that linger. The sensory details that shape each moment. Sweeping views. Quiet corners. Soft natural textiles brushed by morning light. Burnished brass that catches the evening glow. Playful prints and family antiques. Local craftsmen's skill and stories, expressed in gentle curves and graceful angles.

This is the secret. This is how time slows down. It’s an exquisite awareness of where you are.

A sense of place that stays with you, long after you’ve left us.
