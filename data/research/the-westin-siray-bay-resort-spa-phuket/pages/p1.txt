# Welcome to The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket

## A luxury resort in Phuket, Thailand, with sea views

Five-star luxury awaits at the Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket. Overlooking Siray Bay, our luxurious 5-star beachfront resort in Phuket, Thailand, provides an ideal locale for quiet getaways, romantic...See More holidays, and corporate retreats. We are just moments from pristine white sand beaches and local water sports. Enjoy amenities like Heavenly® Beds, rainforest showers, spacious desks, free Wi-Fi, and LCD televisions. Admire panoramic sea views or relax on a furnished balcony. Select suites and resort villas include plunge pools and generous entertaining spaces. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket?

The check-in time at The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket.

What are the parking options at The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket?

The closest airport near The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket is Phuket International Airport (HKT). HKT is located approximately 37.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket have an airport shuttle to Phuket International Airport (HKT)?

Yes, The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket has an airport shuttle to Phuket International Airport (HKT).

Does The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
