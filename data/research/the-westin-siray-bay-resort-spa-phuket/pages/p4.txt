### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Splash Pool - will be closed from 1 June 2026 (Outdoor)Horizon Pool - will be closed from 1 June 2026 (Outdoor)Prego Pool (Outdoor)

Does The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket have an on-site spa?

Yes, Heavenly Spa by Westin™ is located on-site at The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket?

Saphan Hin Park, The Upside Down House Museum and Fun Attractions Phuket, Phuket Big Buddha, Monkey Hill, Old Phuket Town and more are nearby The Westin Siray Bay Resort & Spa, Phuket.
