## THE _iconic_ OASIS OF LUXURY IN MARRAKECH

Backed by the snowy peaks of the Atlas Mountains, Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech is a luxury resort for business and leisure travelers. Just 15 minutes from Marrakech Menara Airport, this Moroccan-inspired haven features 135 exquisite accommodations, four dining venues, a top golf course, majestic pools, a Fairmont Spa, and diverse sports activities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech located?

Our 5-star hotel, Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech is 12 km away from the bustling old medina of the city and 20 minutes away from Marrakech Menara Airport.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech?

Check-in time is at 4:00PM and check-out time is at 12PM.

How can I get to Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech from the nearest airport?

Our luxury hotel is only a short drive away from the airport; taking a cab is an option for the 20-minute drive or arrange a personal transfer through our concierge. Reservations are required at least 20 min in advance.

Does Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech have a gym/fitness center?

Yes, Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech features a 1,500 sqm Fitness Centre equipped with the latest equipment, including a cardio room, 2 weight training rooms, a yoga studio, a boxing area, 1 squash court, 4 clay tennis courts, 2 padel courts, 2 table tennis tables, and a central sports bar, offering a dynamic and premium fitness experience for all levels. A dedicated sports coach is available daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Please note that access to the spa's indoor swimming pool is reserved for guests aged 18 and over.

Does Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech have a spa?

Yes, Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech features the Fairmont Spa, a 3,500 sqm sanctuary designed like a modern riad, offering private treatment rooms for massages, facials and traditional hammam rituals, as well as a hair salon and a dedicated barber shop, all set around a large indoor pool and surrounded by tranquil gardens for a deeply relaxing experience. To ensure a calm and safe environment for all our guests, access to the spa's indoor swimming pool is reserved for adults aged 18 and over.

Does the hotel have a heated swimming pool?

Yes, our main pool, as well as the Fairmont Spa and Fitness Centre pools, are heated to 28°C. Private villa pools can also be heated upon request, depending on guest preference and weather conditions. Please note that the Penthouse Suite features a private, non-heated plunge pool, and the Country Club pool is not heated.

Does Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech have a golf course?

Yes, the Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech features an exceptional 18-hole golf course designed by Cabell B. Robinson, set against the Atlas Mountains, offering a unique blend of natural beauty and world-class design.

How can I get to the Medina, city center, and Marrakech’s main landmarks?

Our concierge offers complimentary shuttle service to several points in Marrakech city center. Please check the shuttle bus schedule and drop-off locations with the concierge team.
