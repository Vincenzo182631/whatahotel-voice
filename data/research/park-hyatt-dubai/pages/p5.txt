### A Flawless Sanctuary: My Dubai Favorite for 20 Years

The Park Hyatt Dubai remains my gold standard for luxury. Even after 20 years of visiting, it maintains a rare sense of intimacy—a true sanctuary that feels worlds away from the city's main tourist bustle.
The staff are without fail exemplary; check-in is seamless, attentive, and perfectly balanced without being over-fussy. The rooms are beautifully airy, and the bathrooms are a highlight with their deep standalone tubs, rain showers, and stunning views over the Creek.
The dining is consistently world-class, whether you are enjoying The Thai Kitchen, Noépe, or Lakeview. For relaxation, the pool at Amara maintains a calming vibe even when busy, while the Lagoon remains a genuine piece of heaven for adult guests. Amara also stays my favorite spa in the city—the treatments are exceptional and the entire setup is incredibly serene.
I truly cannot fault this hotel. It was the perfect backdrop for a long weekend with my teen, offering sophisticated calm in the heart of Dubai.

### Staycation at Park Hyatt

We did a staycation for one night with Half Board. The property is beautiful with options to access the Golf Course, Paddle Courts in addition to the pool, Gym and SPA facilities. The room we stayed in was spacious with a beautiful bath tub and Shower. The Balcony faced the Beach Lagoon with the most beautiful view. The Staff were super helpful and polite. The food was excellent, we dined at the restaurant Nappe. The breakfast was wonderful relaxed with some great French Toast and Waffles ! We as family really enjoyed the mini golf and cooking classes. Overall it was a very memorable and relaxed stay !

Sonali S,
