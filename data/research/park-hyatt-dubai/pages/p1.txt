## Luxury Beckons with Dazzling Dubai Vistas & Gorgeous Grounds

Situated on the banks of majestic Dubai Creek and adjacent to the Dubai Creek Golf & Yacht Club, Park Hyatt Dubai provides an idyllic respite for a luxury getaway. World-class, award-winning restaurants offer diverse and gourmet cuisine, while a luxury destination spa set among serene private patios awaits to help erase tension and soothe your body and soul. Every moment at the hotel is meticulously curated to ensure a sophisticated, enriching and uncommon experience.

## Discover Park Hyatt Dubai

Amara Spa & Fitness

Set amid the calm of private courtyards, Amara Spa provides an idyllic setting for relaxation. Enjoy a wide range of spa treatments including luxurious massages, rejuvenating facials and exclusive rituals. Find peace of mind and body during your visit to Amara Spa. Adjacent to the spa, the fitness center has floor-to-ceiling windows and is fitted with Technogym equipment, offering a multitude of choices to fit your workout regime. Its Moorish-inspired patio also provides an ideal location for outdoor fitness activities.

The spa is open daily 9 a.m.–10 p.m.; the fitness center is open 24/7.

Outdoor Venues

The 672-square-meter Palm Garden is not only a wedding paradise, but also a stunning venue to host glamorous events. With the palm trees lining the lush lawn, The Palm Garden never fails to offer a premium look and feel, from its exclusive entrance to its gorgeous staircase—perfect for creating a dramatic grand entrance for any bride and groom. So be it a wedding party, product launch, or corporate engagement, this hidden gem will help you and your guests craft unforgettable memories.

Perfecting Elegance

As Park Hyatt Dubai continues its long-term enhancement journey from May 2026 onwards, the award-winning waterfront retreat introduces Perfecting Elegance, a new campaign that reflects its continued commitment to refinement, craftsmanship and timeless hospitality ahead of its next chapter.

The Lagoon Beach Club

Experience the Lagoon Beach Club, an oasis in the heart of Park Hyatt Dubai. Nestled on the shores of the Dubai Creek, this true hidden gem unveils a majestic 100-meter infinity lagoon leading into a beautiful private beach. Transport yourself to a world of resplendent tranquility with glistening waters and pristine white sands perfected with an indulging culinary escape. Available from 10am until sunset, the Lagoon Beach Club is exclusive for adults and children aged 16 years and above.

Golf

Hit the links surrounded by the gorgeous grounds of the golf resort at Park Hyatt Dubai. Set in a prime location on the banks of the Dubai Creek and adjacent to the world-famous Dubai Creek Golf & Yacht Club, our luxury Dubai resort provides the perfect haven for a golf escape. Capped by its iconic clubhouse, the course at the Dubai Creek Golf & Yacht Club provides a refreshingly different golfing holiday experience. Guests can also opt for a round at the Emirates Golf club in Dubai Marina, one of the finest golf courses in the Dubai UAE region. The concierge team at Park Hyatt Dubai is happy to arrange tee times at both golf clubs for hotel guests.

Pool

Park Hyatt Dubai features a stunning 25-meter swimming pool with inset palm trees and four whirlpools, one in each corner, and surrounded by lush tropical gardens. This family-friendly pool is open daily from 6 a.m.–10 p.m. and fully supervised by lifeguards. To keep our little guests entertained in the pool, a dedicated play area for children is available, offering them more fun with foosball, building sandcastles and more.

### Pet Policy

Park Hyatt Dubai welcomes your pets, offering pet-friendly rooms that face the lagoon beach. Pets are also welcome at NOÉPE restaurant, the Lakeview terrace and Jones the Grocer terrace. We’ll have you review a short pet agreement on arrival, and we’ll provide some pet-specific amenities for your stay.
