# Restaurants and Dining in Dubai Creek \| Park Hyatt Dubai

# Dining

World-class cuisines, stylish decor and spectacular views characterise the best restaurants and bars in Park Hyatt Dubai, the city centre luxury retreat. Superbly located on the banks of Dubai Creek, Park Hyatt Dubai offers the finest in Western, Mediterranean, Spanish, Thai and French cuisines. With floor-to-ceiling windows, wood-fired ovens and theatre kitchens, the hotel brings excitement and style to dining, both indoor and al fresco.

## The Thai Kitchen

The Thai Kitchen is renowned for its authentic Thai cuisine prepared by native chefs. The award-winning restaurant features three live kitchens - one each for wok, grill and noodles as well as an additional kitchen devoted to the preparation of traditional Thai desserts, recreating the excitement of bustling streets of Thailand. Discover the true taste of Thailand with traditional flavours created using the freshest of ingredients, imported directly from Thailand.
SAWASDEE SUNDAY BRUNCH
Indulge in the authentic flavors and signatures of Thai cuisine with a Sunday Brunch experience. Feast on an unforgettable family-sharing style menu selection including som tam green papaya salad, chicken satay, the world-famous Pad Thai noodle dish and many more.
Price: AED 220 inclusive of soft beverage and AED 375 inclusive of house beverage

Timings: Every Sunday, 1pm – 4pm
