# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Park Hyatt Dubai offers a secure indoor parking facility with valet service for hotel guests.

Complimentary

Free

### Self-Parking

Park Hyatt Dubai offers on-site parking facilities for up to 450 vehicles, located on the north side of the property. Designated spaces for people with disabilities are available close to the hotel’s main entrance.

Complimentary

Free

### Convention Center Parking

Designated convention center parking is available with coach drop-off points that can accommodate up to four buses (50 seats) for hotel guests.

Complimentary

Free

### EV Charging

EV charging is available at the hotel, which has two universal EV chargers. No reservations are required.

Complimentary

Free

Getting around

Limousine service

Pickup and drop-off is available within the city and can be arranged on an hourly basis for AED 250/hour. Transportation services can be arranged in advance through Park Hyatt Dubai’s concierge services. Please contact: +971 4 602 1635 or email DXBPH-Concierge@hyatt.com.

Private taxis

Private taxis are available 24 hours. Pickup and drop-off are available within the city and can be arranged on an hourly basis for AED 100/hour. Transportation services can be arranged in advance through Park Hyatt Dubai’s concierge services. Please contact: +971 4 602 1635 or email DXBPH-Concierge@hyatt.com.

Dubai transport taxis

Dubai transport taxis are available on request. Transportation services can be arranged in advance through Park Hyatt Dubai’s concierge services. Please contact: +971 4 602 1635 or email DXBPH-Concierge@hyatt.com.
