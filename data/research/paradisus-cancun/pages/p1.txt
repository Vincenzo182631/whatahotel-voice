### Services & Activities

Exceeding customer expectations is how Paradisus Cancun defines success. The resort’s iconic pyramids, elegant decorations, exceptional facilities, warm and well-trained staff, and numerous activities offered onsite are a top reason why guest return to us and recommend us to their friends. The natural beauty of our location sets the stage and we take it from there. Whatever your pleasure, whether you want to pamper or play, Paradisus Cancún offers activities and services specially designed to delight, from the YHI Spa to golf and yoga classes.

##### YHI SPA

Prepare to be pampered in an authentic temple of pure relaxation. YHI Spa is a full-service, indoor spa that treats you exceptionally well with body and facial treatments created with Mexican honey, local ingredients and essential oils, and a host of services that will absolutely delight you.

##### ECO SPA

An authentic Eco Spa dressed in Eastern architecture, bamboo and bonsai trees transports you across the globe as you enjoy a calming Balinese aromatic massage or traditional Thai massage, all at the hands of expert Asian massage therapists.

##### YHI MOVES

Stay fit and fabulous while on vacation. Visit our modern Spa Studio, complete with Technogym equipment and an area for yoga, Pilates, spinning, HIIT Tabata, ABS classes and more, and always accompanied by a team of professionals at your service at any time. All guests are welcome.

##### FUN FOR ALL

**GOLF:** Practice your swing and enjoy the enviable views from our nine par, three-hole golf course. Located next to the resort, It’s the perfect place and way to unwind with friends and loved ones.

##### LIFE-ENRICHING ACTIVITIES

Expand your horizons with a host of enlightening and exhilarating activities—including sushi workshops, wine tastings, pool parties and yoga classes.
