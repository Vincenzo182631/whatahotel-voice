# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self-Parking

Andaz Shenzhen Bay houses a 24-hour indoor parking area with direct access to the hotel. Complimentary parking is available for overnight guests. Three hours of complimentary self-parking is available for event, dining and wellness center guests.

Free

## Transportation

Getting here

Shenzhen Bao’an International Airport (SZX)26 kilometers26 kilometers

After exiting Shenzhen Bao’an International Airport, take a cab from the official taxi stand. The 26-kilometer journey to Andaz Shenzhen Bay should take roughly 35 minutes and cost approximately RMB100.

Transportation Options

Taxis

Catching a cab in Shenzhen is relatively easy and inexpensive. Taxis are air-conditioned, metered (make sure it’s switched on) and available 24 hours a day. Red taxis take you anywhere in Shenzhen, yellow taxis operate inside the Special Economic Zone and green taxis operate outside the zone.

Limousine Service

A four-seater Mercedes-Benz S-Class limousine and seven-seater Buick GL8 minivan are available for journeys to anywhere in Shenzhen. For cross-border journeys, guests can choose from a four-seater Mercedes-Benz S-Class limousine or seven-seater Toyota Alphard minivan. Bookings can be made up to four hours in advance.

Navigation Guidance for International Guests

For seamless navigation during your stay in Chinese Mainland, we recommend using local mapping applications such as Gaode Map or Baidu Map. International mapping apps may not be accurate in Chinese Mainland.
