Waldorf Astoria Los Cabos Pedregal
A luxurious secluded retreat with cinematic views, exceptional service, and delicious food

Why book?
With private plunge pools in every room, from the exceptional service and standout spa to some of Cabo’s best dining, Waldorf Astoria Los Cabos Pedregal delivers a romantic luxury experience that will keep you coming back year after year.
Set the scene
They say love is in the details, and carved directly into the cliffs on the southernmost tip of Mexico’s Baja California Peninsula, from the moment you make your way through the private tunnel and into the world of Waldorf Astoria Los Cabos Pedregal, you’ll have crossed over into an alternate universe you’ll never want to leave. This hotel is made for lovers and those who appreciate the small details that make a big difference on vacation. Couples on their honeymooners and babymooners linger alongside gym rats fresh off their second Peloton ride of the day in the hotels state of the art gym. The hotel is also a boon for wellness junkies, foodies, and anyone craving a true sense of seclusion. It’s far from the only game in town, but it’s easily one of Cabo’s most exceptional places to stay.
The backstory
The property originally opened as a Capella Pedregal in 2009 and quickly became known for its dramatic arrival through a privately owned mountain tunnel, which continues to serve as one of the most unique hotel entrances on the Pacific-facing side of the peninsula. Waldorf Astoria assumed management in 2019, marking the brand's first resort in Mexico. Recently, the Waldorf completed a multi-year, property-wide renovation, refreshing all 112 guest rooms, suites, casitas, and villas. The redesign leans heavily into Mexican craftsmanship and features custom furniture, textiles, and decorative pieces sourced from artisans across Oaxaca, Baja California Sur, and Jalisco. Today, Pedregal is one of Cabo’s most romantic luxury properties, balancing dramatic natural scenery with an outstanding culinary program and some of the best service in town.
The rooms
Every one of the property’s 112 rooms has a private plunge pool, a signature Pedregal feature that sets the bar high, especially in winter when whales breach just offshore. Accommodations range from multi-bedroom residential-style villas to two-bedroom beachfront suites and standalone cliffside casitas, all designed to feel more like private coastal hideaways than standard hotel rooms. Interiors use natural materials, warm neutrals, and handcrafted details. Expect in-room fireplaces for cooler nights, spacious indoor–outdoor layouts, and terraces angled for full Pacific views.
Food and drink
Food is a major calling card at Pedregal. Don Manuel’s sets the tone with elevated, Baja-inspired dishes built around local seafood, handmade tortillas, and produce sourced from regional farms. Just a few steps away, El Farallon is tucked dramatically into the cliffs and is one of those hotel dining experiences that stays with you long after you’ve left. It’s a largely seafood-centric menu here, choose your catch from a market-style display as the waves crash below and lanterns flicker overhead. The Agave Study is the spot for tequila and mezcal tastings that go deep into regional expressions, terroir, and lesser-known producers. Across the property, cocktails lean spirit-forward. Neutral Coffee Lab keeps the morning hangovers at bay with specialty-brewed coffees, and the Beach Club delivers casual daytime dining alongside rotating chef collaborations that spotlight Mexican flavors. Across the board, the food and drink here punch well above typical resort fare. Perhaps one of the coolest hotel amenities is the daily delivery of chips and freshly made guacamole straight to your room.
The spa
Designed around the forces of the moon, the ocean, and traditional Mexican folk healing, the spa is a destination worth visiting in its own right. Blending indigenous rituals and the spa’s signature “moon phase,” therapies that stand out include options like The Awakening Moon, Calming Moon, Nourishing Moon, and Restoring Moon, full-body journeys that combine exfoliation, warm wraps, and massage with botanicals and techniques inspired by each lunar cycle. The Intention Treatment, developed with local curanderos, layers energy cleansing with craniosacral work and Mexican folk massage for a holistic reset. The spa also offers steam rooms, saunas, ice rooms, whirlpools, and a serene salt-water meditation pool with float therapies.
The neighborhood/area
The hotel’s location is both its greatest strength and its one true trade-off. Tucked behind a private mountain tunnel in an exclusive residential enclave comprised of luxury homes and villas, it offers absolute seclusion, but very little within walking distance. Then again, once you’re on the property, you won’t feel any urge to leave anyway.
The service
Service is highly personal yet non-intrusive, thanks to dedicated concierges who handle everything from pre-arrival planning to daily check-ins, seamless transportation coordination, and on-demand support throughout your stay. Staff remember preferences, anticipate needs, and resolve requests quickly, which keeps the experience smooth and consistent across the property.
For families
There are no kids’ clubs or child-focused amenities beyond standard food options. While families are technically welcome, the property largely caters to adults or families with adult children.
Eco effort
Part of the hotel’s recent renovation was the deliberate shift toward using Mexican-made furnishings, textiles, and artisan work throughout the guestrooms and public spaces. This supports regional craft economies and reduces the need for long-distance shipping.
Accessibility
ADA-compliant guestrooms and suites are available, including an accessible oceanfront category with a roll-in shower and wider doorways; however, due to the hotel’s cliffside location and multi-level design, travelers with limited mobility may encounter challenges.
Anything left to mention?
The resort is set along the Pacific Ocean (not the Sea of Cortez, which tends to be calmer), so strong waves and a constant undercurrent make ocean swimming prohibited here. The hotel compensates for this with private plunge pools in the guest rooms and pools across the hotel, but for guests hoping to take a dip in the sea, this isn’t the place to do that.
