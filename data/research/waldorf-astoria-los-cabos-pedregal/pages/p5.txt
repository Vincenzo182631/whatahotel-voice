# Experiences

Experiences

Whether you crave adventure or an escape from the day-to-day, your Personal Concierge will help you make the most of every moment in Pedregal. Beachside romance, family fun, bucket-list excursions and more are yours to discover, with personalized recommendations and itineraries tailored to your whims. Take it easy poolside, pick up the pace in the fitness center, take in the sights of nearby Cabo San Lucas – the choice is yours.

Email the Concierge

## Spa & Wellness

At the center of the resort, Waldorf Astoria Spa is a lagoon for rest and rejuvenation powered by the natural healing culture of Baja.

Visit the Spa

## Pools

Relax in two beachfront infinity pools, a saltwater relaxation pool, and a zero-edge kids’ pool. Each room, suite, and villa features a private plunge pool.

## Fitness

Start your day with complimentary yoga classes, reserve time with one of our fitness instructors, or let our tennis pro assist in brushing up your game on one of two tennis courts. Energize your fitness-center workout with views of the marina and Sea of Cortez.

## Baja Exploration

Go beyond the beach and explore Baja’s land, wildlife, and culture. Choose adventures from Panga reef cruises and surfing lessons to breakfast on our private yacht and farm-to-table lunches in Todos Santos. Contact our concierge to book.

### Whale Watching

Follow hundreds of majestic whales on their journey from the Arctic to Baja. From December to April, ride a high-speed boat with a small group and watch the whales' annual migration.

### Swimming with Whale Sharks

Swim beside one of the ocean's largest creatures, a 40-foot-long whale shark.

### Surfing Baja

Chase the ultimate summer thrill along the Baja Sur coastline with surfing excursions tailored to first-timers, experienced surfers, and every skill level in between.

### Camel Rides

Take a family-friendly camel ride on a safe trail designed for kids, meeting tropical wildlife and exploring exotic plants along the way.

### ATV Tours

Explore the desert landscapes and mountains of Los Cabos on an ATV ride. Join us at 9 am, 12 pm, or 3 pm for a 15-mile excursion lasting four hours.

## Tortugitas Kids Club

With a full schedule of kid-friendly activities, Tortugitas Kids Club invites our youngest guests to make their own Cabo memories.

Kids can learn and play with half-day, full-day, and evening programming that includes scavenger hunts, sandcastle building, and language and cultural immersion.
