## Don Manuel's

Satisfy daytime cravings with breakfast or lunch in a laid-back hacienda atmosphere. Casual daytime dining transitions a gourmet fine dining experience in the evening as warm lighting and live local musicians set the scene for Executive Chef Gustavo Pinet’s culinary wonders. A curated wine program elevates a menu of eclectic regionally-sourced dishes.

## El Farallón

Overlooking the Pacific Ocean, listen to the waves crash below and select from an ocean-to-table menu, where the best of the local fishermen’s catch is prepared by Executive Chef Gustavo Pinet in an open air kitchen. Explore a curated collection of 20 unique, rotating champagnes on the restaurant’s Champagne Terrace during a tasting, by the glass, and paired with the best seafood in Baja in our Savor the Sea experience.

## Neutral Coffee Lab

Neutral Café is an elevated coffee experience at its finest. With coffee varieties from around the world, including a focus on local coffee communities, our guests experience culture-rich traditions in a nature-conscious, sustainable environment, learning the origins, methods, and people behind the beans. Our goal is to bring an elevated coffee experience to Los Cabos, which is intentional, well-made, and always respects coffee culture.

## Mar y Leña

Experience an unforgettable evening at The Beach Club Palapa, where the spirit of Baja comes alive through fire-kissed flavors and the gentle rhythm of the Pacific. Mar y Leña is our signature steakhouse— an intimate culinary journey that blends wood-fired cooking, premium cuts, and the unmistakable essence of the ocean.

## Agave Study at Peacock Alley

Multi-sensorial tastings pair handmade spirits and local delicacies in a singular, culturally rich experience centered around the craftsmanship of agave spirits native to Mexico. Explore agave spirits from tequila in Jalisco to mezcal in Oaxaca, and every plant in the lands between. Here, the focus is on provenance and ancient traditions that pay homage to Mexico, the land, and its purveyors and guardians.

Tasting hours: 5 pm – 11 pm
