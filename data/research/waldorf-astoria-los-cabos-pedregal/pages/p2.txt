Rooms & Suites

Each of our 112 rooms and suites features a private plunge pool, a terrace with breathtaking ocean views, and an invigorating rain shower and soaking tub. To make your stay even more special, a bottle of Premium Tequila, our gift to you, will welcome you in your room. Reserve a premium beachfront suite, villa, casita, or private home and relax at your own pace. Our butler team is ready to help you with anything you need.
