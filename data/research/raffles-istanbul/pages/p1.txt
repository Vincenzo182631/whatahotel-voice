## A luxury hotel above _the Bosphorus_ in Istanbul

Raffles Istanbul brings together modern luxury and the city's rich heritage in the heart of Zorlu Center. Its blend of contemporary design and traditional elegance creates a distinctive sense of place, reflecting both Istanbul's vibrant energy and its historical depth. From its hilltop setting, the panorama stretches from the Black Sea to the Marmara Sea, embracing the Bosphorus and the three bridges that connect two continents.

## Rooms and suites with Bosphorus views

Set in Zorlu Center, the hotel's 185 spacious rooms and suites feature floor-to-ceiling windows and private balconies, with majestic views over the Bosphorus, the Princes' Islands and the Old Town of Istanbul. Interiors pair contemporary design with a large collection of commissioned art.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Istanbul hotels have Bosphorus views?

Raffles Istanbul stands on a hilltop above the strait in Zorlu Center. Its rooms and suites open onto majestic views of the Bosphorus, the Princes' Islands and the Old Town through floor-to-ceiling windows and private balconies.

### What amenities does Raffles Istanbul offer?

Raffles Istanbul offers 185 spacious rooms and suites with private balconies, 24-hour personalised butler service, a spa with hammams and pools, and a choice of dining venues including İsokyo for Asian cuisine and the Long Bar for signature cocktails.

### How do I book a room at Raffles Istanbul?

You can book by calling +90 212 924 0200, by email, or by selecting Check Rates at the top of the page to view availability, rates and offers.

### What dining options are available at Raffles Istanbul?

The hotel offers İsokyo for Asian cuisine, Lavinia Lounge for all-day dining and afternoon tea, the Long Bar for cocktails, Writers Bar for a literature-inspired setting, and Bosphorus Lounge for meetings and quiet moments with a view.

### What attractions are near Raffles Istanbul?

The hotel sits within Zorlu Center, with direct access to high-end shopping and the Zorlu Performing Arts Center. Nearby, guests can explore Beşiktaş and Ortaköy, walk along the Bosphorus, or visit Dolmabahçe Palace.

### When is the best time to visit Istanbul?

Spring, from April to June, and autumn, from September to November, offer the mildest and most pleasant weather.
