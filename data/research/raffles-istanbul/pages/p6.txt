## A family-friendly luxury hotel in Istanbul

For families looking for a luxury base in Istanbul, Raffles Istanbul makes room for younger guests without losing its sense of occasion. Children are welcomed at the Legendary Raffles Brunch, where under-6s dine free and 7 to 12s at half price, and the spa offers dedicated experiences for children and teens alongside its treatments for adults. With spacious rooms and suites above Zorlu Center, and the city's sights within easy reach, it is a place where parents can unwind and children feel looked after.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Raffles Istanbul a good hotel for a honeymoon?

Yes. It combines Bosphorus-view suites with private balconies, a couples spa experience and private dining, which makes it well suited to a honeymoon or a romantic stay in Istanbul.

### What romantic experiences are available for couples?

Couples can book the Raffles Couples Romance at the spa, a private suite with a jacuzzi and side-by-side massages, enjoy a private dinner for two, and stay in a Bosphorus-facing suite with a balcony overlooking the water.

### Which room is best for a romantic stay?

A Bosphorus-facing category, such as the Deluxe Bosphorus Room or the Bosphorus Suite, offers a direct view of the strait from a private balcony, ideal for a honeymoon.

### How do I plan a honeymoon at Raffles Istanbul?

The hotel can arrange the elements of a romantic stay together. Contact Raffles Istanbul on +90 212 924 0200 or at reservation.istanbul@raffles.com to plan the suite, spa and dining.

### Is Raffles Istanbul suitable for families with children?

Yes. The hotel welcomes families, with children dining free at the Legendary Raffles Brunch for under-6s and at half price for ages 7 to 12, and a spa that offers experiences designed for children and teens.

### What is there for children at Raffles Istanbul?

Families can share the Legendary Raffles Brunch, where children eat free or at a reduced rate, and the spa offers dedicated experiences for younger guests alongside the pools and wellness facilities.

### Are the rooms suitable for families?

The hotel's rooms and suites are among the most spacious in the city, with private balconies, offering room for families to stay together in comfort.
