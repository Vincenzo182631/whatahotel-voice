## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of cuisine does İsokyo serve?

İsokyo serves pan-Asian cuisine, a journey from Tokyo to Istanbul that blends Japanese, Thai, Korean and Chinese flavors with authentic Turkish ingredients.

### What are İsokyo's opening hours?

İsokyo serves breakfast daily from 7.00am to 11.30am as a buffet, and dinner daily from 5.00pm to 11.00pm, with last orders at 10.30pm.
