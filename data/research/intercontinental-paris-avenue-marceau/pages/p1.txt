## FAQs for InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile

Does InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile have a pool?

No, InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile?

At InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 59 EUR and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 59 EUR .

Public parking is available near the hotel.

Parking fee: 59 EUR per day

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile.

Pet policy description. We are pleased to welcome small pets under five kilograms at our hotel. Additionally, we gladly accommodate guide dogs and service animals.

Pet fee per night: 50 EUR

Pet weight limit: 5

NO\_LIMIT pets allowed

Pets allowed: All pets allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile has 1 restaurant.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile?

Check-in at InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Paris - Champs-Elysées Etoile is conveniently located near Roissy Charles de Gaulle (CDG), approximately 12.43 miles away (20 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
