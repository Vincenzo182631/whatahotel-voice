### A three star hotel advertised as five stars.

a month ago

This intercontinental hotel has renovated rooms, but we had no air conditioning during our four nights stay. We were disappointed that the front desk withheld the fact that our room had no air-conditioning. They should have informed us of that when we checked in, but they did not. We asked to change rooms several times, but were told no rooms were available. For a four night stay, they should have accommodated us but didn’t.

The hotel has no restaurant, other than a breakfast room; no fitness facilities that we were aware of; and has no bar. So it’s difficult to understand how it’s considered a five star hotel.

On the positive side, the concierge, Jeremy, was very helpful. He made excellent dining recommendations and provided information on tourist activities. The breakfast was excellent and the manager, Daniel did a great job.
