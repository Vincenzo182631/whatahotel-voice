Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale
A Season of Timeless Elegance
Step into the festive season with a coastal escape at The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale. From exceptional dining experiences to thoughtfully curated holiday amenities, every detail is designed to capture the magic of the season. Whether celebrating with loved ones or relaxing by the ocean, our holiday escape offers the perfect setting for an unforgettable Fort Lauderdale holiday experience.
Magic in Every Moment: Breakfast with Santa
Join us for a festive morning of holiday cheer, seasonal favorites, and memorable moments with Santa at Breakfast with Santa, hosted in the Grand Ballroom Foyer on Saturday, December 5th, from 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m.

Seasonal Dining Experiences
Thanksgving Feast at Burlock Coast
Celebrate Thanksgiving at Burlock Coast with family and friends over a festive three-course, family-style feast. From seasonal favorites to indulgent desserts, every dish is thoughtfully prepared to create a gathering filled with flavor, warmth, and unforgettable memories.
Reserve Now
Christmas Dinner at Burlock Coast
Celebrate the magic of the holiday season with a festive evening and a curated three-course dining experience by Executive Chef Adrienne Grenier and her culinary team. Gather with family and friends, indulge in seasonal flavors, and enjoy a Christmas celebration filled with warmth, elegance, making it an evening to remember.
Reserve Now
New Year's Eve at Burlock Coast
Welcome the New Year with an extraordinary evening of celebration, beginning with a thoughtfully crafted four-course culinary experience. As midnight approaches, enjoy live music, dancing, and the iconic New York City Ball Drop, making it a memorable start to 2027.
