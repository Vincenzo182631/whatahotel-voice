Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale. The daily valet fee is $59.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale is Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL). FLL is located approximately 7.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
