Exceptional Club Lounge Experience in Fort Lauderdale
Discover our Fort Lauderdale Beach hotel with a Club Lounge
The Ritz-Carlton Club at our Fort Lauderdale Beach hotel with a Club Lounge and concierge, redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay at our Fort Lauderdale Beach hotel with a Club Lounge by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.

For Your Convenience
In-Room Amenities
Indulge in a lavish retreat at The Ritz-Carlton, Fort Lauderdale, where you can experience enhanced accommodations in our well-appointed suites and rooms. Elevate your stay with the Club Lounge upgrade, granting exclusive access to a Club Level room that boasts expansive rooms, private balconies, sweeping ocean vistas, and supplementary amenities for an even more luxurious experience. In addition to our standard Club experience, the Club Level provides additional advantages, such as complimentary pressing for two items daily, access to business center services, upgraded Wi-Fi, and the option for tea and coffee service in the Lounge daily.
