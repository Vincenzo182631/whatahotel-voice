Dining
At this beachfront resort, dining on Fort Lauderdale Beach is an elevated yet relaxed affair. Burlock Coast restaurant offers Prohibition era-inspired cocktails and farm-to-table cuisine in distinct settings, and the Sea la Vie Sundeck features tropical drinks, light bites, and an ocean view.
