Experiences
Fort Lauderdale is known as the Venice of America with the Intracoastal Waterway cutting through the oceanfront city to create a charming ambience. With the water taxi, visitors can see the city and make stops at places such as Las Olas, where the street is lined with galleries, restaurants, bars and boutiques. An adventure in the Everglades is just over 30 minutes away and features wildlife spotting and a stop at Sawgrass Recreation Park. The hotel near Hard Rock Stadium also has offerings including golf at Grande Oaks Golf Club and private poolside cabanas. For additional Fort Lauderdale, Florida tourist information, please contact your hotel concierge.

Grande Oaks Golf Course
Created amidst natural oak hammocks, the private, members-only Grande Oaks Golf Course is a par 71 and measures 6,680 yards from the tips. In keeping with tradition, all tees at Grande Oaks are perfectly square and are aligned to the middle of the landing area offering a variety of distances from which to play. Guests at our family-friendly hotel on Fort Lauderdale Beach will enjoy the privilege of a warm personal greeting upon arrival, their own personal locker for the day and a professional golf attendant to introduce them to the course and answer any questions they may have. Guests will receive VIP treatment and will have access to all the club amenities, including the restaurants and bars, throughout the day.
* Please note, the Grande Oaks Golf Club is closed Mondays. Weekend tee times for Ritz-Carlton guests will be honored after 12 p.m.
For more information please call (954) 916-2900 or contact the hotel concierge.
