Luxury Accommodations with Ocean Views
Signature Suites
Experience Fort Lauderdale from an extraordinary vantage point. Our Signature Suites offer expansive layouts, breathtaking views of the Atlantic Ocean and Intracoastal Waterway, and a residential sense of comfort enhanced by legendary service. Each of our suites feature a spacious layout, a balcony and sweeping view of the Atlantic or Fort Lauderdale’s Intracoastal Waterway. Our luxury suites near Fort Lauderdale Beach are available with one, two or three bedrooms and can be upgraded to include Club Lounge access.
Residential Style, Exquisite Comfort
Oceanfront Suite
Spanning 2,150 square feet, this luxury residential-style suite on Fort Lauderdale Beach features three beautifully appointed bedrooms, each with a king-size bed, and a versatile sofa bed. Guests will enjoy three sophisticated bathrooms, complete with tub and spray jets, a full kitchen equipped with modern appliances, and separate living and dining areas. The suite's expansive balcony provides breathtaking ocean views, enhancing the overall experience of relaxation and refinement.

The Coastal Suites Series
The Coastal Suites Series is a unique collection of guest rooms joined with a separated foyer entrance, which allows for exceptional privacy and yet a connected guest room concept. These suites offer a welcoming retreat with views of either the Atlantic Ocean or Intracoastal Waterway, harmoniously blending comfort and privacy. Ideal for families, or friends wanting proximity and privacy.
