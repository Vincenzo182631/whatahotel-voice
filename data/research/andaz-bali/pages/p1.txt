# A Luxury Beachfront Resort Offering a Distinctively Sanur Experience

This luxe beachfront resort—a modern interpretation of a Balinese village—in Sanur invites you to experience an eclectic mix of old and new. Andaz Bali has been carefully built around the lush natural landscape, creating a welcoming ambiance in a beautiful, immersive setting, much like Sanur itself. It provides a canvas for exploring who you are.

## Rooms, Suites & Villas

Our beautifully designed rooms, suites and villas are spread around clusters of courtyards, each with its own character and charm. Rooms and suites boast a spacious balcony and upscale amenities you'll love, while the stunning Garden and Beach Villas offer added privacy and tranquility.

## DINING

### Village Square

The dining experience starts in the heart of the resort at Village Square, with four intertwining restaurants: Wok Wok, featuring Indonesian favorites, Blue Oven that offers Mediterranean cuisines, Fire Fox brings Hansik Korean BBQ & Pocha Bar, and Deli & Bakery that serves coffee, tea, dessert and more.

Details + Reservations

### Fisherman’s Club

Our chic beachfront restaurant offers a refreshing, contemporary take on classic Jimbaran cuisine. Discover an inspiring selection of light bites, meat and the freshest seafood catch of the day by local fishermen. As the sun goes down, chill out with craft cocktails with weekly live entertainment.

Details + Reservations

### In-Room Dining

Loosen up in the comfort of your room and indulge in a selection of sumptuous dishes from our EAT IN menu. From breakfast to late-night munchies, enjoy food delivered right to your guestroom door. To order, dial 0 through the guestroom phone.
