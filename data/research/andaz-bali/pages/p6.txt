### A lovely stay

We had a beautiful stay at Andaz. Conveniently located in the heart of Sanur. Rooms were clean and breakfast is at a very highly standard. Absolutely loved the quiet pool especially if you want some peace and quiet time.
Dinner dining options were a little upsetting as most of them were very rigid about catering halal or requests to avoid pork. Minimum/almost non existent halal options available on menus. Mostly pork dishes on menu and due to language barrier, it’s hard to explain that you don’t want food contaminated. Friendly staff overall and great all day complementary coffee and snack bar all day. Addicted to disco peanuts since our visit.

### One of Sanur’s Best Beachfront Hotels

Andaz Bali offers a wonderful stay along Sanur, boasting a prime beachfront location with direct sand access and spotlessly clean, comfortable rooms. The service throughout the property is warm and attentive, easily making it one of the top hotels in the area. The only drawback is having to share the spa and gym facilities with the neighboring Hyatt Regency—walking over to the gym early in the morning while it is still dark isn't particularly convenient. Overall, it remains a fantastic choice in Sanur, and I would happily stay again.
