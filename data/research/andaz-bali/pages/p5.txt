# Andaz Twilight​

_Thu to Sun, 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM​​_

Enjoy a laid-back golden-hour session by Sanur’s beachfront with curated drinks, chef-crafted bites, and live food stations. The experience continues into the evening with rotating live entertainment, a beachfront bonfire, s’mores, and a relaxed atmosphere by the sea.​

# Day Pass

Take advantage of our swimming pool and beach facilities with a Day Pass that can be used from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

**Adult**: IDR 700,000+ +

(includes ID 350,000++ F&B credits at Fisherman's Club and pool amenities)

**Children aged 6-12 years old**: IDR 350,000++ per child

(includes IDR 200,000++ F&B credit at Fisherman's Club and pool amenities)

**Children under 6 years old**: Free
