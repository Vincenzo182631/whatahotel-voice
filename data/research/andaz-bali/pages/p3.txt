## Village Square

The dining experience starts in the heart of the resort at Village Square, with four intertwining restaurants: Wok Wok, featuring Indonesian favorites, Blue Oven that offers Mediterranean cuisines, Fire Fox brings Hansik Korean BBQ & Pocha Bar, and Deli & Bakery that serves coffee, tea, dessert and more.

Our chic beachfront restaurant offers a refreshing, contemporary take on classic Jimbaran cuisine. Discover an inspiring selection of light bites, meat and the freshest seafood catch of the day by local fishermen. As the sun goes down, chill out with craft cocktails with weekly live entertainment.
