# Accommodations

Check-In2:00 PM

Park Hyatt Jeddah offers 142 refined guestrooms, including 15 luxurious suites, in a stunning location along Jeddah’s Corniche with panoramic views of the Red Sea, lush gardens, and King Fahad Fountain from private balconies. The hotel seamlessly blends European architecture with Andalusian design, envisioned by designers Gilles Quiffet and Patrice Hart. Sea view rooms offer 40 square meters of refined comfort, while the suites range from 80 to 200 square meters, embodying residential luxury in a serene coastal setting.
