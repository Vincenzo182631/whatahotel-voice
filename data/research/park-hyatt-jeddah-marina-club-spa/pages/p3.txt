# Restaurants & Bars

At Park Hyatt Jeddah, dining is a refined journey blending flavors, elegance, and authenticity. Overlooking the Red Sea, our Saudi and international chefs create signature dishes like Cherry Kebab, Pumpkin Risotto, and Date Cake using fresh, local ingredients. Explore diverse cuisines including Moroccan, Japanese, and Indian, each presented with a modern touch. From serene breakfasts to sunset dinners, every moment is crafted to be unforgettable.

Nafoura Restaurant offers an exquisite all-day dining experience, specializing in contemporary Mediterranean cuisine crafted from the finest organic, seasonal ingredients. Delight in a selection of tantalizing mezzes and dishes prepared in our open kitchen and traditional charcoal grill, while enjoying the serene ambiance of our expansive outdoor terrace, which overlooks the breathtaking Red Sea and the iconic King Fahad’s Fountain. Indulge in an unparalleled culinary journey where every detail reflects the essence of refined luxury.
