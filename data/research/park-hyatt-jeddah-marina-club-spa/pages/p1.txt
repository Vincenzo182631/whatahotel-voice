# Luxury Hotel in Jeddah \| Park Hyatt Jeddah

## A Luxury Escape on Jeddah’s Iconic Corniche

Park Hyatt Jeddah is a refined sanctuary on the Corniche, offering understated luxury with panoramic views of the Red Sea and King Fahad Fountain. Set amid lush gardens, the resort blends contemporary design with Arab-Andalusian elegance, featuring serene rooms, inspired dining, expansive event spaces, and a world-class spa ideal for sophisticated business and leisure escapes that encourage calm connection and memorable gatherings. ## Discover Park Hyatt Jeddah

Fitness Center

Our Fitness Center reimagines your fitness journey, combining cutting-edge design, seamless connectivity and a sense of fluid movement. With expansive views of the outdoor pool, spa and gardens, the gym offers a bright, open space to elevate your workout, equipped with the latest cardio and strength-training Technogym equipment and integrated TV screens. Dedicated personal trainers are available to support you in achieving your health and fitness goals. Male spa hours: 7 a.m.–10 p.m., female spa hours: 9 a.m. to 10 p.m.

Sustainability

The solar power system in the car park is designed to charge our golf cart fleet with renewable energy, enhancing your experience by providing seamless, eco-friendly transport, while reinforcing the hotel's commitment to sustainability.

Lounge & Terrace

This elegant, open-air retreat with breathtaking views of the Red Sea and iconic King Fahad Fountain invites you to savor a refined selection of gourmet cuisine and freshly crafted juices in a serene, sophisticated ambience.

Evania & Seba Spa

Indulge in a rejuvenating experience where modern wellness meets timeless healing traditions at our spa, a serene escape designed for ultimate relaxation and renewal.

Aquamedic Pool

Experience the transformative power of our exclusive therapeutic aquamedic pools, where water works to melt away muscle tension, stimulate circulation and alleviate stress. This hydrotherapy treatment offers a blissful escape, providing complete rejuvenation and relaxation for body and mind.
