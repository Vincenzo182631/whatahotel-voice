## A Challenging Hike Adventure in the Supreme Wilderness

Mount Meru, an active volcano standing at 4,566 metres, is a stunning trekking destination located just 70 kilometres west of Mount Kilimanjaro. Whether as a standalone challenge or a preparation for Kilimanjaro, this trek offers a diverse terrain, abundant wildlife, and a tranquil, remote atmosphere. With its rich biodiversity and dramatic landscapes, Mount Meru is an unforgettable adventure for nature enthusiasts.

The fertile slopes of Mount Meru rise majestically above the savannah, supporting a thriving forest ecosystem. As participants trek through its lush terrain, they encounter a remarkable array of wildlife, from nearly 400 bird species to monkeys and even leopards. The trail winds through varied landscapes, offering close-up views of vibrant flora and fauna, making it a haven for wildlife lovers. Each step reveals the mountain’s unique character, from tropical rainforests to alpine meadows. With fewer crowds than Kilimanjaro, Mount Meru offers an intimate, peaceful trekking experience where adventurers can connect deeply with the natural beauty surrounding them.

- Duration: 9 hours
- Number of people: Up to 8 people
- Please note: Bookings are required 48 hours in advance
- What does it include? Transfer, expert guide, adventure to Mount Meru, an active volcano at 4566 metres, stunning views of the Ash Cone in the crater, opportunities to witness vibrant flora and fauna up close.

The ascent of Mount Meru takes trekkers through an ever-changing landscape, showcasing the mountain’s incredible diversity. Starting in open grasslands teeming with wildlife, the route gradually climbs into tropical rainforests alive with birdsong and playful monkeys. The trek continues through alpine meadows and moorlands, offering breathtaking views of the surrounding savanna. Higher up, trekkers cross desert uplands, where the sparse, rugged terrain hints at the challenges ahead.

The final stretch is a thrilling climb along a narrow, barren ridge leading to the summit. This exhilarating push rewards trekkers with stunning views of the Ash Cone, which lies several hundred feet below within the crater.

As the sun rises over the peak, the dramatic landscape of Mount Meru comes to life, providing an unforgettable moment of triumph and awe.

Throughout the journey, participants encounter not only breathtaking scenery but also a sense of tranquillity and remoteness that sets Mount Meru apart from its famous neighbour. With its combination of wildlife, diverse ecosystems, and challenging terrain, this trek is both a physical and spiritual adventure, offering a profound connection to Tanzania’s unspoiled wilderness and the beauty of Mount Meru.
