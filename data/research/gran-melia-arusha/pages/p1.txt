## A five-star hotel with exclusive views of Mount Meru

This spectacular hotel is located between the Serengeti and Kilimanjaro National Parks, opening out onto all of Tanzania’s majestic natural beauty. An oasis of five-star comfort and elegance, with extensive, unbeatable facilities, exclusive suites, and international cuisine, including the outstanding The Grill restaurant. With spa, infinity pool and gym. Perfect for a safari trip or luxurious business meeting.

The stunning Gran Meliá Arusha rises up among coffee and tea plantations. An exquisite first-class hotel with a wide range of luxurious comforts, located in a privileged natural environment. African exoticism and attention to detail, with a modern conference center and the personalized assistance of our RedLevel service.

### Tarangire National Park

Located just a few hours drive from the city of Arusha, Tarangire is a popular stop for people traveling on the northern safari circuit en route to Ngorongoro and the Serengeti. The park is spread over two areas full of wild animals. Wildlife moves freely in the region. Before rain season, herds of gazelles, wildebeest, zebras and giraffes migrate to the scrub plains of Tarangire National Park, where the remaining pasture lands are located. The stunning views of the Maasai steppe and the southern mountains make a stopover in Tarangire a memorable experience.
