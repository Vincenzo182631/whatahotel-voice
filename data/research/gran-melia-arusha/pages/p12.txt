## Gran Meliá Arusha, Arusha Tanzania

## Soar Above Africa’s Iconic Peak

Experience the awe of soaring above Mount Kilimanjaro, the world’s tallest free-standing mountain. From the comfort of a private plane, take in breathtaking views of its glaciers, rugged peaks, and crater. This unforgettable journey offers a front-row seat to some of Tanzania’s most iconic landscapes, creating a unique opportunity to see Kilimanjaro’s beauty from a perspective few get to enjoy.

This one-hour flight departs from Arusha Airport, offering morning flights at 07:00 or evening flights at 17:00. As the plane ascends, guests enjoy stunning views of Arusha city and the nearby national park, before moving toward the majestic Kilimanjaro. Highlights include the West Kilimanjaro area, Shira Plateau, and the magnificent glaciers and peaks, including Reusch Crater and Mawenzi Peak. The experience culminates with a scenic return over Mount Meru, offering another chance to admire Tanzania’s breathtaking landscapes. Whether it’s the golden tone of morning or the soft glow of evening, the flight promises an extraordinary perspective on these remarkable sights.

- Duration: 180 minutes
- Number of people: Up to 8 people
- Please note: Bookings are required 48 hours in advance
- What does it include? Taxes and fees, oxygen masks. Warm drinks upon arrival or after the flight, breathtaking views.

The Kilimanjaro flight experience combines comfort and unforgettable views for a journey like no other. With only eight window seats available, every passenger enjoys unobstructed views of the stunning scenery. From the vast plains of West Kilimanjaro to the jagged peaks and shimmering glaciers of the mountain itself, the sights are truly breathtaking. Highlights include a close view of Reusch Crater and Mawenzi Peak, showcasing the mountain’s dramatic natural beauty.

The return flight offers equally stunning views, including a flyover of Mount Meru, another of Tanzania’s iconic peaks.

Whether guests choose the crisp light of the morning flight or the softer evening glow, they are treated to supreme photo opportunities throughout.

To ensure a comfortable experience, the flight includes oxygen and masks, as well as onboard drinking water. Upon return, warm drinks are served to cap off the adventure. This carefully designed flight not only provides a seamless journey but also creates memories that last a lifetime. With its combination of exclusivity, comfort, and extraordinary views, this aerial adventure is the perfect way to experience Kilimanjaro’s majestic beauty in a way few others can.
