# A day in Ngorongoro

## Gran Meliá Arusha, Arusha Tanzania

,regionOfInterest=(2557,1704.5))

## Wildlife and natural wonders in a UNESCO World Heritage Site

Embark on a journey to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, departing from Arusha at the break of dawn. This exclusive experience takes guests on an adventure to one of the world’s most special natural wonders. Along the way, they will discover local towns and breathtaking landscapes before descending into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater. An unforgettable experience blending scenic beauty and thrilling wildlife encounters.

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and International Biosphere Reserve, the Ngorongoro Crater is a haven for wildlife enthusiasts and nature lovers. Within its unique ecosystem, participants will have the chance to spot elephants, lions, and the critically endangered black rhinos in their natural habitat. As the morning unfolds, the crater reveals its abundant life and picturesque views, offering countless moments to admire the diversity of its flora and fauna. Around midday, the journey reaches its peak with a picnic lunch at the scenic hippo pool, where guests can enjoy their meal while admiring hippos, vibrant birdlife, and the beautiful surroundings.

- Duration: 9 hours
- Number of people: Up to 4 people
- Please note: Bookings are required 48 hours in advance
- What does it include? Transfer to Ngorongoro, picnics, expert guide, entrance to a UNESCO World Heritage Site, descent into the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater: a once in a lifetime adventure.

,regionOfInterest=(2736,1824))

This one-of-a-kind experience offers more than just wildlife sightings; it provides a deeply personal connection to the serenity and grandeur of nature. From the tranquillity of the crater floor to the vibrant life thriving within its walls, the experience invites guests to pause and reflect. Expert guides enhance the trip with fascinating insights, ensuring participants walk away with memories of breathtaking views, close wildlife encounters and a deeper understanding of the conservation efforts that protect this extraordinary place.

By the end of the day, guests will feel a sense of rejuvenation and their spirits lifted by the majestic beauty and tranquillity of Ngorongoro. As the sun sets and the journey back begins, this remarkable day trip leaves a permanent mark, turning into a treasured highlight of any traveller’s adventures.
