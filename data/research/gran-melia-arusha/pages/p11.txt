# The Splendour of Materuni Waterfalls

## Gran Meliá Arusha, Arusha Tanzania

## Where Cascading Beauty Meets Local Traditions

The eastward journey to Materuni Waterfalls offers a scenic 2.5-hour drive through stunning landscapes, vibrant towns, and lively local markets. The route is a delightful introduction to the natural beauty and cultural charm of the region. Along the way, the changing scenery sets the tone for an adventurous and memorable day amongst the lush greenery and lively atmosphere of rural Tanzania.

Upon arrival, the adventure begins with a hike to the base of Materuni Waterfalls. This invigorating 1.5 to 2-hour trek winds through picturesque avocado, mango, lemon, and banana farms, immersing guests in the area’s natural beauty. The closer guests get, the sound of cascading water and a refreshing breeze heighten the anticipation. At the waterfall, the breathtaking view invites guests to relax, take photos, enjoy their packed lunch, or even swim in the clear, cool pool at the base of the falls. The experience blends physical activity with moments of tranquillity, leaving guests refreshed and captivated by this natural wonder.

- Duration: 9 hours
- Number of people: Up to 6  people
- Please note: Bookings are required 48 hours in advance
- What does it include? It includes: Transfer eastward through picturesque landscapes, arrival at the waterfalls, picnic lunch, coffee plantation tour, expert guide at all times.

After the visit to the stunning waterfall, the journey continues to Materuni village, perched at around 1,800 metres above sea level. This charming village provides an authentic glimpse into local life, offering insights into the community’s history, traditions, and agricultural practices. Guests can engage in the vibrant culture and learn about the daily rhythms of the welcoming Chagga people.

The experience becomes even richer with a captivating coffee tour, where participants step into the heart of the village’s coffee culture. Walking through coffee and banana plantations, guests will discover the traditional coffee-making process, from hand-picking beans to roasting them over an open fire. The journey culminates with the preparation of freshly brewed coffee, where guests can taste the rich flavours of a cup they helped to create.

This blend of nature, culture, and community creates a deeply enriching experience. The day offers a balance of adventure, connection, and relaxation, leaving guests with unforgettable memories of Materuni’s stunning waterfall, the warmth of the local community, and the unique joy of participating in traditional coffee-making in the picturesque Chagga village.
