### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive?

No, the rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive have fridges?

No, rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive have hair dryers.

Can I request connecting rooms at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive?

No, connecting rooms are not available at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive.
