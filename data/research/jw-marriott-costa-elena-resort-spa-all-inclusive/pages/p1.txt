## Inspire Curiosity Together

At JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort All-Inclusive, Family by JW brings generations together through meaningful shared experiences. From nature-inspired activities and creative workshops to outdoor exploration and guided discovery, young guests are invited to learn, play, and connect in engaging, thoughtfully designed settings. Rooted in curiosity and togetherness, these family-friendly moments celebrate the natural beauty of Costa Rica while creating lasting memories for all ages.

### Kids Club

The Kids Club at our all-inclusive Costa Elena beach resort offers a welcoming, nature-inspired environment where young guests can explore, create, and connect. Thoughtfully designed activities and attentive hosts encourage curiosity, creativity, and discovery, allowing children to engage in enriching experiences while families enjoy the ease and comfort of a seamless stay.

### Fitness Center

The Fitness Center at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort All-Inclusive provides a bright, energizing space designed to support balance and well-being. Featuring modern equipment and an inviting atmosphere, it encourages guests to move with intention, stay present, and maintain their wellness routine with ease throughout their stay.

### Pool Cabanas

Private pool cabanas at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort All-Inclusive offer a tranquil retreat designed for effortless relaxation. Surrounded by natural beauty, each cabana invites guests to slow down, unwind in comfort, and enjoy attentive service in a serene setting—creating a peaceful space to recharge and savor the moment by the water.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of being a Marriott Bonvoy Member at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive?

Marriott Bonvoy Members staying at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive are eligible for additional benefits only available at resorts in the All-Inclusive by Marriott Bonvoy Portfolio. You can refer to official benefits here.

What is included in the per night room rate at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive?

The nightly room rate at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive typically includes: room, meals, snacks, non-alcoholic & alcoholic beverages, entertainment, non-motorized water sports, kids’ activities, taxes, service fees, and gratuities.

What additional amenities and services are available for purchase at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive?

JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive may offer spa treatments, motorized water sports, laundry, transportation, tours, premium items, and services for an additional fee.

Are there any guest age restrictions at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive?

JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive is a Family-Friendly resort and is open to guests of all ages. The guest count of adults and children should be updated during the booking process to ensure appropriate pricing.

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 15.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive is Daniel Oduber Quiros International (LIR). LIR is located approximately 90.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
