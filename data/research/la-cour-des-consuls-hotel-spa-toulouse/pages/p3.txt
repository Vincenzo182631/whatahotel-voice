# Relaxing

## Space   well-being

### The Spa at La Cour des Consuls

Our Spa is the touch of serenity in your luxury interlude at La Cour des Consuls Hôtel & Spa in Toulouse. Designed to resemble a bourgeois flat in a world of white colours, the spa works with the Graine de Pastel and Gemology brands to offer a cocoon of softness and well-being. Four cabins bathed in natural light, a steam bath, a traditional hammam, as well as a relaxation room and an herbal tea room.

## Fitness area

### Take care of your fitness

The hotel's fitness centre is open exclusively to hotel guests, 24 hours a day, every day. To help you stay in shape even during your stay with us, it has treadmills, exercise bikes and an elliptical.
