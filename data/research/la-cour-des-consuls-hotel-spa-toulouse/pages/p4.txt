## Events

### Private meals

The cachet of La Cour des Consuls is an undeniable asset for your private events. Weddings, christenings, birthdays, celebrate your loved ones in this marvellous 18th-century building. The beauty of its noble materials, such as marble, its contemporary style, its mouldings and parquet floors, and its high ceilings will leave their mark on your event, making for unforgettable memories. You and your guests can also sleep on the premises, in rooms overlooking the two inner courtyards.
