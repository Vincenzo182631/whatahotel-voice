# Access & Contact

Getting to the hotel

## La Cour des Consuls hotel & spa Toulouse Mgallery

Located at 46 rue des Couteliers in the heart of Toulouse, La Cour des Consuls Hôtel & Spa MGallery member is easily accessible, whatever means of transport you choose.

### By car

Our hotel is located in the centre of Toulouse, 2h40 from Bordeaux via the A62 and 2h45 from Montpellier via the A9 and A61.

Private parking is available on site (without prior reservation) for €36 per day. We also have a valet parking service and a charging point for electric vehicles.

### By plane

Toulouse-Blagnac airport is a 20-minute drive from our hotel via the A621.

### By train

La Cour des Consuls hotel & Spa is easy to reach from Toulouse Matabiau TGV station. Just 15 minutes by metro on line A to the Esquirol stop. By car, the station is less than 20 minutes away.
