# Cour des Consuls Hotel & Spa

### MGallery Hotel Collection  The 5 stars of the Ville Rose

A confidential address housed in two 18th-century private mansions, La Cour des Consuls Hôtel & Spa is a remarkable 5-star hotel full of charm and character. Our luxury hotel is housed in a historic building in Toulouse, behind a neo-classical pink brick façade. The period decor and family coat of arms coexist with contemporary design, a bold harmony imbued with poetry.

**Toulouse Marathon, Sunday 1 November – Getting to the hotel**

On Sunday 1 November, from 6.30 am to 5 pm, it will not be possible to access La Cour des Consuls by car due to the Toulouse Marathon. We would advise you to plan your journey in advance and to use the metro (Esquirol – line A or Carmes – line B).

## A word from the director

### Sandra Lampée Baumgartner,   operations manager

I am delighted to be taking over as manager of La Cour des Consuls Hôtel & Spa, a unique establishment nestled in the heart of Toulouse.

Behind its historic façade, our house offers an intimate and historic setting where guests come as much to recharge their batteries as to enjoy warm and welcoming moments.

Our facilities – the gourmet restaurant Le Cénacle, La Brasserie des Consuls, Bar 46, the Spa, and our function rooms – have been designed to welcome both visitors and locals alike, in a spirit of sharing and conviviality.

Every day, our team draws on its expertise and attention to detail to create an authentic experience that respects the heritage of the location and meets everyone’s expectations.

My aim is to preserve this unique atmosphere, characterised by warmth, heritage and elegance, so that every visit is an unforgettable experience in one of the most cherished spots in the ‘Pink City’.
