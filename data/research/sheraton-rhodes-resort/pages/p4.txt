# Discover Our Rhodes Town Hotel With a Private Pool

## Experiences

Discover the famous Greek island of the Dodecanese that has enchanted visitors and conquerors with its irresistible beauty. Cosmopolitan, with a rich past reflected in multiple monuments of historical and architectural interest and integral parts of the international cultural heritage, Rhodes unfolds its beauty everywhere you go. The innumerable sandy beaches, the small and big clean bays with fine pebbles, the traditional villages, the delicious cuisine, and the people. Enjoy our Rhodes hotel facilities after a long day of exploring, with everything from a fitness center, tennis and basketball courts, beach access, swimming pools and a spa, we cater for the whole family.

### Explore Rhodes

With the year round warm climate, Rhodes is the perfect place to enjoy all your activities.

### Local Area & Activity Highlights

/

Prev Next

An UNESCO World Heritage Site, this medieval city holding in its folds leftovers of ancient times, unfading trails of the Byzantine Period and the loud reverberation of the ‘passing’ of the Knights Hospitalier that led Rhodes to the wealth and fame that follows it even today.

In Mandraki, past and present, wealth and tradition, history and commerce come together. The two tufa columns with the deer and doe, the 1464 castle of Saint Nicolas are just some of the sights to behold.

Discover the therapeutic waters, the famous springs of Kallithea and their healing properties for treatment of conditions like arthritis, diabetes and skin conditions.

The Acropolis of Rhodes impresses as it rises prominently with a view to the entire city, all the way to the Anatolian coast, where the gaze is lost into the blue of the sea.

Lindos is a travel destination of its own, a famous village inhabited and loved already from the pre-Hellenic years – as its name testifies – with evidence of life from the Neolithic Age.

Valley of the Butterflies

The valley is one of natures' most beautiful gifts to Rhodes, with its walkways and rustic bridges, winding rivers and flowing streams tumbling into little waterfalls.

The Aquarium of Rhodes

Housed in a building of unique architectural style, the Aquarium is the ideal place for those wishing to get acquainted with the marine wealth of the Aegean and the Eastern Mediterranean sea.

Throne of Helios - 9D Entertainment Center

The Throne of Helios is a modern entertainment center that combines cinema and thematic area, historical exhibits and screenings. Enjoy a spectacular 9D entertainment and educational production, showcasing the history of Rhodes, according to legends, myths & traditions.

Water Park Rhodes

Built amphitheatrically on an area of 100.000 sq.m. the Water Park offers a big selection of slides for all ages. Extreme speed slides, open and closed slides, Twisters, Black Holes, Turbo, Free Fall and Kamikaze slides are just a few that can be experienced.

Mini Market

Inside Sheraton Rhodes Resort your will find a small, fully stocked Mini Market, providing from all necessary daily items to the most specialised ones, like provisions and equipment for a day outdoors.

Manoukian Wine Store

Located in the hotel lobby, indulge yourself with a fine start to a culinary evening and allow us to introduce you to the finest local wines curated by our wine expert.
