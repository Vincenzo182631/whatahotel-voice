### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Sheraton Rhodes Resort?

The largest occupancy room at Sheraton Rhodes Resort is the Aelia Suite with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort?

Some rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Sheraton Rhodes Resort have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at Sheraton Rhodes Resort have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
