# Welcome to Sheraton Rhodes Resort

## Welcome to our superb hotel in the heart of Rhodes

Rhodes, a city with a 2400-year history, the place that gave birth to legends and hosted significant personalities of politics, the arts and culture, with a beauty that enthralled its conquerors through the centuries, hosts the welcoming Sheraton Rhodes Resort. Our Rhodes hotel is cocooned in the beauty of its attractive lush gardens with the blue expanse of the pristine waters in front of its exclusive access beach; it is ideally situated in a key spot in Ixia, just 4 kilometers from the famous Medieval Town of Rhodes, the best preserved Medieval City in the world and an UNESCO World Heritage Monument. Our Rhodes resort is ideal for families and services the very things that Rhodes offers: natural beauty, unique architecture, a variety of entertainment options, modern infrastructure and traditional hospitality.

## A Refreshed Look, Same Sheraton Warmth

Step into our reimagined public spaces designed to bring people together. From the revitalized lobby and social hubs to inspiring meeting areas, Sheraton Rhodes Resort now offers dynamic settings for collaboration, relaxation and celebration. Our refreshed interiors blend contemporary design with the island’s natural light, creating welcoming environments to unwind, work or meet.

## Elevated Rooms & Suites

Spacious guest rooms of approximately 33–40 square meters set the tone for a relaxed resort stay, each featuring a private furnished balcony with mountain or Aegean Sea views. Our 45 suites offer enhanced space, with dedicated living areas, while the newly introduced Penthouse Suite elevates the experience with panoramic sea views and seamless indoor-outdoor living. Newly renovated, all 401 rooms and suites combine Sheraton’s signature bed, smart amenities, and high-speed Wi‑Fi for effortless comfort and connection by the Aegean.

### Location & History

Discover the famous Greek island of the Dodecanese.

Learn More

Ancient Roots

The place that gave birth to legends and hosted significant personalities of politics, the arts and culture, with a beauty that enthralled its conquerors through the centuries.

Medieval City

Just a short drive from the resort, the UNESCO-listed Medieval City of Rhodes invites visitors to wander through cobbled streets, historic architecture, and one of the best-preserved medieval settlements in Europe.

Island Landmarks

Some of Rhodes’ historic location jewels include The Medieval City, the Mandraki area, Lindos, Kallithea Springs, Philerimos Hill, the Valley of Butterflies and the famous beaches of the island.

Coastal Escapes

With over 200 km of coastline, Rhodes is home to beautiful beaches ranging from sandy shores to secluded coves with crystal-clear waters. Highlights include the golden sands of Agathi Beach and the picturesque Anthony Quinn Bay, alongside numerous spots for relaxation, swimming, and water sports.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Rhodes Resort?

The check-in time at Sheraton Rhodes Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Rhodes Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Sheraton Rhodes Resort.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Rhodes Resort?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Rhodes Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Rhodes Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Rhodes Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Rhodes Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Rhodes Resort?

The closest airport near Sheraton Rhodes Resort is Diagoras International Airport (RHO). RHO is located approximately 8.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Rhodes Resort have an airport shuttle to Diagoras International Airport (RHO)?

No, Sheraton Rhodes Resort does not have an airport shuttle to Diagoras International Airport.

Does Sheraton Rhodes Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Rhodes Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
