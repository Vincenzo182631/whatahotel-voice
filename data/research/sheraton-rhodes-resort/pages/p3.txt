## Dining

An excellent choice of Rhodes restaurants and cocktail bars is available at the resort, offering cuisine that ranges from traditional Greek to international tastes.

New All-Inclusive Package

Experience effortless all‑day dining with our All‑Inclusive Package, featuring buffet breakfast and dinner, an à la carte lunch, and refined evening dining. With flexible dining hours and seamless wristband access, it offers a complete and carefree culinary experience from morning to night, including daily snacks, branded beverages, and dedicated kids’ options.

Discover the Meal Plan

In-Room Dining

Our In‑Room Dining service offers something for every taste. Enjoy a delicious breakfast between the sheets or a quiet dinner in the intimacy of your room—now enhanced with our new mobile ordering option for quick, effortless ordering from your device.

Kids Menu

Food for our little friends should be truly enticing and irresistible to satisfy them! The chefs of our restaurants have created menus especially for children.

Themed Nights at Castellania

Make every night a new experience. Join us to a fine gastronomic experience and allow us to introduce you to a brand new set of flavors. From the exotic aromas of Asia to the hot recipes of cocina Latina and from the spices of the Middle East to the beloved Italian galore.

Freshest Local Ingredients

Fresh ingredients from the local produce, selected traditional products and the best Italian produce has to offer, are blended masterfully to give your dish the unparalleled Italian feast of flavours.
