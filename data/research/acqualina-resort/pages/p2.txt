## LuxuryAccommodationsinMiamiBeach

The luxurious accommodations at Acqualina Resort feature glamorous detailing with echoes of surf, sand, and shimmering water infusing the palette. The spacious guestrooms and suites all have private balconies with breathtaking views of the Atlantic Ocean or the Intracoastal Bay.

## Miami’sOnlyDirect-to-BeachResort,WhereSeclusionMeetsSophistication

### Guest Experience

Lush, richly appointed accommodations are the hallmark of the guest experience at Acqualina.

Designed to offer a sense of modern luxury with classic furnishings and finishes, each of the 54 guest rooms and 44 suites present oversized floor plans and private terraces. Celebrated Miami designer Isabel Tragash and her team of designers imbued the hotel room design with their unique vision of a South Florida Five Star, Five Diamond resort.

The comprehensively designed guestrooms utilize a modern luxury vocabulary that includes zebra wood, walnut, champagne bronze and smoked glass.

Glamorous detailing includes channel-tufted walnut wrapper on wrap-around headboards, modern Italian design sofa sleeper, and signature style wingback club chair in every room fit for a prince or princess.

## FrequentlyAskedQuestions

### Does Acqualina offer beachfront accommodations in Miami Beach?

Yes. Acqualina’s spacious oceanfront guestrooms and suites offer uninterrupted views of the Atlantic Ocean, allowing guests to wake up to the sound of the sea. Designed to evoke the grandeur of a Mediterranean villa, every suite and guestroom offers a private balcony.

### What types of accommodations are available at Acqualina Resort in Miami Beach?

Our collection includes elegant guestrooms with Intracoastal or oceanfront views, expansive oceanfront suites, and exceptional three-bedroom residences designed for extended family stays. Each space is appointed with luxurious finishes, Italian furnishings, full kitchens in select suites, and the signature Acqualina touch.

### Are there long-term stay options at Acqualina Resort in Miami Beach?

Absolutely. Acqualina’s spacious suites and multi-bedroom suites are ideal for longer stays, offering the comfort of home with five-star service. Please contact our reservations team for details on tailored long-stay offers.

### What amenities are included in the rooms at Acqualina Resort in Miami Beach?

Every room and suite include a private balcony, marble bathroom, Seed to Skin Tuscany amenities, high-speed Wi-Fi, and an Illy espresso machine. Suites also offer full gourmet kitchens and living areas—perfect for families or entertaining.

### Are the accommodations at Acqualina Resort pet-friendly in Miami Beach?

Yes, we welcome small dogs up to 25 pounds. Your pet will receive a special amenity upon arrival, and our concierge is happy to assist with pet-friendly services nearby.
