## FrequentlyAskedQuestions

### Is Acqualina Spa open to non-hotel guests in Miami Beach?

Yes. Acqualina Spa welcomes both hotel guests and outside visitors to enjoy our serene oceanfront sanctuary. Reservations are required to ensure a personalized experience.

### What spa services are offered at Acqualina Spa in Miami Beach?

The award-winning Acqualina Spa offers a full menu of massages, advanced facials, body treatments, and wellness experiences using ESPA, Swissline and Seed to Skin products—all in a stunning 20,000 sq. ft. beachfront setting.

### Does Acqualina Spa offer couples treatments in Miami Beach?

Yes. Our Royal Spa Suite is a private haven designed for couples, featuring a steam shower, private balcony, and side-by-side treatment beds for romantic and restorative escapes.

### What are the operating hours of Acqualina Spa in Miami Beach?

The spa is open daily from 9:30 am to 7:00 pm. We recommend reserving treatments in advance to ensure availability.

### Are there any wellness amenities included with a spa visit in Miami Beach?

Yes. Spa guests have access to our crystal steam room, sauna, Himalayan salt lounges, experience showers, and our private outdoor spa terrace with a heated jet pool and Roman waterfall.

### Can I book a spa package or half-day experience at Acqualina Spa in Miami Beach?

Certainly. We offer curated half-day and full-day spa journeys that include signature treatments, wellness amenities, and nourishing refreshments. These packages are ideal for solo indulgence or shared relaxation.
