## FrequentlyAskedQuestions

### Are there dining options with a view of Miami Beach at Acqualina Resort?

Yes, dining at Acqualina is designed to immerse you in our breathtaking oceanfront setting. From the fresh Mediterranean flavors at Costa Grill just steps from the sand, to the elegant ocean view terraces at Il Mulino New York, Ke-uH, and Avra Miami, each restaurant invites you to savor world-class cuisine with the Atlantic Ocean as your backdrop.

### What dining options are available at Acqualina Resort in Miami Beach?

Acqualina features four distinct dining venues that cater to every palate:

- **Avra Miami** offers a refined Greek-Mediterranean menu in a glamorous indoor-outdoor space.
- **Il Mulino New York**, known for its fine Italian fare and impeccable service, delivers a lively yet sophisticated experience.
- **Ke-uH** presents innovative Japanese fusion in a chic, art-inspired setting.
- **Costa Grill**, exclusive to guests and residents, delivers casual beachside dining with a Floridian flair.

Guests may also enjoy breakfast at Acqualina, daily afternoon gelato and coffee, in-room dining, and intimate beachfront dinners under the stars.

### Is outdoor dining available at Acqualina Resort in Miami Beach?

Absolutely. All of our dining venues feature beautiful outdoor spaces, allowing you to enjoy your meal alfresco—whether it’s a beachfront lunch at Costa Grill, dinner on Avra’s oceanfront terrace, or sunset cocktails at Ke-uH.

### Are Acqualina Resort restaurants open to the public or only for guests?

Avra Miami, Il Mulino New York, and Ke-uH are open to the public and welcome outside guests.  Reservations are highly recommended. Costa Grill is exclusively reserved for hotel guests, Acqualina residents, and Beach Club Members to preserve a serene, private atmosphere.

### Can I make a reservation at Acqualina Resort’s restaurants in Miami Beach?

Yes, and we highly recommend it. Reservations can be made through our website, concierge, or directly via each restaurant. Popular times, especially for oceanfront seating, book quickly—so we encourage planning ahead.

### Does Acqualina Resort host private dining events in Miami Beach?

Yes. Whether it’s a beachfront celebration, intimate proposal dinner, or elegant corporate gathering, we offer customized private dining experiences. Our team will tailor every detail to create an unforgettable culinary moment.
