## Everglades Adventure

Discover one of the world's most unique ecosystems on a private 5.5-hour tour of Everglades National Park with expert naturalists from Wild Lime Adventures.

Discover

Discover one of the world's most unique ecosystems on a private 5.5-hour tour of Everglades National Park with expert naturalists from Wild Lime Adventures. Your journey includes a guided nature walk, a visit to the park's visitor center, and an exciting private airboat ride through the “River of Grass.” Spot wildlife, visit a rescued animal sanctuary, and even hold a baby alligator for a photo. Enjoy a catered picnic lunch on a secluded Native Island. The experience includes round-trip private transportation from Acqualina Resort and is priced at $1,350 for up to 2 guests or $1,495 for up to 5 guests. Please inquire for groups of 6 or more.

**What's Included**

- Round-trip private transportation from Acqualina Resort
- Guided Tour
- Lunch, snacks and non-alcoholic beverages
- Wildlife-inspired gift
- Binoculars
- Sunscreen

## Pools & Beaches

Three outdoor swimming pools provide a haven for relaxation. Sundries, including suntan lotion, sunscreen, and various floaties and pool toys are available. Acqualina’s pools and beach service are exclusively available to Acqualina residents, hotel guests and beach club members and include complimentary chaise lounges and towel service.

Discover

**The Recreational Pool**

Located just in front of our Miami Beach luxury resort’s gorgeous beachside front lawn, the Recreational Pool is everything a sunny South Florida vacation should be. As with other pools, private cabanas are available, as is poolside food and beverage service. The Recreational Pool is the epicenter of fun pool games, activities, aqua fitness classes, and other diversions led by Acqualina Resort’s staff.

**The Beach Club Pool**

The Beach Club Pool is the perfect place for guests and residents of all ages to dive into a carefree day. Comfortable chaise lounges, friendly poolside food and drink service, private cabanas, and sunny atmosphere all combine for another vibrant day at our Miami Beach luxury resort.

**The Adult Pool**

This oceanfront tranquility haven for guests 18 years and over has become more sublime with curated menu offerings, personalized service, and refreshed interiors designed in the resort’s signature red hues. The Adult Pool experience features four expansive cabanas, modern chaise lounges with side tables, love seats, wide umbrellas, and eight custom day beds in rich red tones.
