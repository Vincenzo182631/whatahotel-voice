# Children’sActivitiesatAcqualina

## AcquaMarine Camp

Dive into a world of discovery, creativity, and ocean-inspired adventure with our AcquaMarine Camp, a marine-themed children’s program designed to spark curiosity and create unforgettable memories. Acqualina has partnered with Miami Fitness Connection, South Florida’s premier children’s camp provider, proudly serving families for three decades.

Held Friday through Sunday from 10am to 4pm, the AcquaMarine Camp experience invites children ages 4 to 12 to explore the wonders of the sea through a blend of hands-on learning, imaginative arts, and engaging outdoor play—all set within the luxurious and inspiring surroundings of Acqualina.

Each day introduces a new theme:

- **Ocean Explorers (Friday):** Campers uncover the mysteries of marine life and ocean environments through interactive lessons and creative projects.
- **Life of a Sea Turtle (Saturday):** Children follow the incredible journey of sea turtles while learning the importance of conservation and environmental care.
- **Tropical Treasures (Sunday):** A day of island-inspired adventure featuring treasure hunts, tropical crafts, and playful exploration.

The AcquaMarine Camp is designed to balance education and excitement, featuring:

- Interactive, age-appropriate learning experiences
- Hands-on arts and crafts to inspire creativity
- Active outdoor games that encourage teamwork and movement
- A warm, welcoming environment that fosters friendship and confidence

Whether joining for a single day or the full weekend experience, campers will leave with new knowledge, new friends, and a deeper appreciation for the ocean.

Schedule:

**Morning session:** 10 am to 12:30 pm or 10 am to 1 pm (includes lunch between 12:00 pm and 12:30 pm at a charge)

**Afternoon session:** 1 pm to 4 pm

**Full day session:** 10 am to 4 pm (includes lunch between 12:00 pm and 12:30 pm at a charge)

AcquaMarine is complimentary for children ages 4 to 12, and a lunch option is available at a cost.

Reservations are required at least 24 hours in advance for camps offered Friday through Sunday and select holidays. Please contact the Concierge for more information and reservations.
