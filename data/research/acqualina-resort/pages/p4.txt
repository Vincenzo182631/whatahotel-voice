# Il Mulino New York

Il Mulino New York at Acqualina Resort brings the celebrated legacy of one of America’s most acclaimed Italian restaurants to the shores of Sunny Isles Beach. Renowned for its vibrant atmosphere, authentic Italian cuisine, market-fresh daily specials, and exceptional hospitality, Il Mulino offers a sophisticated dining experience in an elegant oceanfront setting. Guests can enjoy lunch and dinner indoors or al fresco overlooking the Atlantic Ocean.

Recognized with the Forbes Travel Guide Recommended Award and celebrated as New York City’s #1 Italian restaurant by Zagat for 20 consecutive years, Il Mulino New York at Acqualina continues to set the standard for exceptional Italian dining in Miami.

## A New Era of Il Mulino

Il Mulino New York at Acqualina unveils a newly reimagined dining experience, marking an exciting new chapter for the celebrated restaurant. The refreshed dining room and bar introduce an elegant new look that enhances the restaurant’s timeless atmosphere, while the menu welcomes inspired new dishes alongside the beloved Italian classics guests have come to know and love.

Lunch at Il Mulino is an invitation to savor the flavors of Italy in an elegant seaside setting.

Discover a refined midday dining experience at Il Mulino New York with our three-course lunch menu, offered for $49 per person, plus tax and gratuity. Begin with a selection of authentic Italian antipasti, including favorites such as Burrata Caprese and Insalata di Polipo, followed by signature entrées featuring house-made pastas, fresh seafood, and classic Italian preparations. Complete your meal with a traditional dessert, from the beloved Torta Della Nonna to Artisanal Gelato.

## Happy Hour by the Sea

Join us daily from 4 PM to 6 PM for an elevated happy hour experience featuring handcrafted cocktails, curated wines, and signature Italian-inspired bites. Unwind in our newly reimagined bar or enjoy oceanfront views from the terrace as you sip and savor the flavors of Il Mulino New York.

Whether gathering with friends or easing into the evening, Happy Hour at Il Mulino offers the perfect blend of sophistication and seaside ambiance.

## Sunday Supper

Gather around the table for a timeless Italian tradition with Sunday Supper at Il Mulino New York. Available every Sunday from 4 PM to 11 PM, this four-course dining experience invites guests to savor a curated selection of authentic Italian favorites for $56 per person, excluding tax and gratuity.

Thoughtfully crafted and generously served, Sunday Supper offers the perfect opportunity to enjoy the warmth of Italian hospitality and the rich flavors that have made Il Mulino a beloved dining destination.
