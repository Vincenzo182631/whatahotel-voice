Through culinary innovation and exceptional service, Mediterraneo has made a name for itself as the Dominican Republic’s premier fine dining experience.

Showcasing Mediterranean cuisine with a local flair, the restaurant is inspired by the resort’s founding principles: European Inspiration, Perfected in the Caribbean.

The menu of Mediterraneo Restaurant, which changes seasonally to take advantage of the local growing season, blends fresh seafood and meats, regional produce and artisanal specialties flown in from Europe to create a supreme dining experience for those who enjoy the nuances of fine dining.

Complementing the menu is the restaurant’s uniquely alluring ambiance, which combines the subtle elegance of classic European luxury with the bold colors and textures of the Caribbean. For alfresco dining, the restaurant has a charming terrace area overlooking the pool and lush greenery outside.

True to Eden Roc’s commitment to culinary excellence and memorable celebrations, BLUE features fusion culinary offerings, a show-stopping bar and wine showcase, cigar cellar and poolside rotisserie.

BLUE allows a unique vantage point on the preparations by our international Chefs in the special show kitchen as the sea’s freshest catch and finest meat cuts are turned into exquisite and flavorful creative dishes and grilled delicacies.

For lunch, enjoy the option for more casual fare from the restaurant’s poolside rotisserie and authentic pizza by our Neapolitan pizzaiolo as you relax on the stunning terrace with views of the pool and sea beyond.

In addition to dinner, the restaurant serves breakfast daily. The extensive breakfast buffet spread includes traditional continental offerings and local staples. Guests can also enjoy a delectable Sunday Brunch.

Breathtaking views of the Caribbean Sea; fresh, local ingredients; globally inspired cuisine: La Palapa by Eden Roc has the recipe for a truly memorable dining experience. The restaurant’s stunning position on an oceanfront point, surrounded by the soothing sights and sounds of the sea, provides the inspiration for the cuisine.

At La Palapa, the freshest fish and seafood is expertly prepared and can be enjoyed as sushi, savored as ceviche, or paired with sumptuous handmade gnocchi and homemade pasta. A sophisticated fusion approach that blends Mediterranean favorites with traditional Caribbean cuisine rounds out the restaurant’s refined offerings.

The restaurant’s surroundings set the relaxed, chic ambiance, making the most of its beachside location with seating available over the water and in an open sided pavilion complete with a namesake palapa roof.

Inspired by the exquisitely crafted Riva Aquarama pleasure boat, the symbol of glamour in 1960s Monte Carlo, Riva Bar offers all the sophisticated charm of that legendary golden age.

Dedicated to La Dolce Vita, Riva Bar is a warm and elegant setting to create and celebrate special moments. Sleek nautical details reflect the sexy appeal of the legendary namesake craft, enhanced by warm wood, exotic leather and vibrant pops of color. Live piano music and views of the stunning main pool and lush gardens enhance the spirit of care-free days spent along the Côte d’Azur.

Resort guests and locals alike are drawn to Riva Bar’s creative cocktails, signature drinks, fine wines and fresh juices, flawlessly served. Refined, gourmet bar bites from our Executive Chef complete the menu.

As in bygone days, Riva continues to inspire legendary evenings.

## Adriano Venturini  Executive Chef & Culinary Director

With a strong background in luxury and Michelin star restaurants, Venturini has a wealth of experience and passion – especially around sustainability. With a focus on the hotel’s environmentally friendly food sourcing, ethical fishing methods, responsible farming and ‘head to tail’ zero waste menus.

Currently, Adriano oversees all the dining venues and Bars at Eden Roc Cap Cana, including Mediterraneo Restaurant, La Palapa, Blue Grill + Bar and Punta Espada Restaurant. Through his leadership and dedication to culinary excellence and sustainability, he has elevated the dining experience at the hotel to a level that aligns with the expectations of the guests who seek not only exquisite cuisine but also a commitment to ethical and environmentally responsible practices.

Under his guidance, Eden Roc Cap Cana characterizes the culinary experience with a unique blend of local flavors and international sophistication, making it the only Relais & Châteaux property in the Dominican Republic.
