## Play in Paradise

With breath-taking scenery of cliffs, beaches and waterfalls, the intricate layout of Jack Nicklaus Signature Punta Espada is a perfect setting to find inspiration for best shots. Named the Best Golf Course in the Caribbean and Mexico (in 2011 by Golfweek), the 18‐hole championship is a masterpiece where emerald‐green fairways hug the coastline and course designs blend effortlessly into the natural setting. Admire and celebrate the natural beauty of Cap Cana while challenging every swing and putt.

The course has hosted the Cap Cana Championship, a PGA Champions Tour official event, three years in a row (2008, 2009, and 2010). This was the first PGA TOUR event ever in the country, contributing to the solidification of Dominican Republic and of Cap Cana as a world class golf destination.

Punta Espada has a Club House with all the necessary services to enjoy a memorable golf experience (lobby, a pro shop, a bar, a restaurant, a library, dressing rooms, lockers and other facilities).
