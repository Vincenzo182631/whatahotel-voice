## Heavens of Intimacy and Peace

At Eden Roc Cap Cana, privacy and elegance define the guest experience, with accommodations designed for cherished intimacy. The resort features 34 lavish stand-alone pool suites, each with generous interiors and relaxation areas ideal for an In-Suite Spa Experience. Twenty-six Beachfront Suites offer a romantic seaside escape, while our unique Oceanfront Bungalows provide complete privacy. Expansive verandas and balconies extend the living spaces, seamlessly blending indoor comfort with the beauty of the surroundings. Bathrooms are spacious and thoughtfully designed for a refined retreat. Additionally, our five Villas, ranging from 3 to 11 bedrooms, offer a residential-style sanctuary. With diverse accommodations to choose from, every traveler can find their perfect retreat.
