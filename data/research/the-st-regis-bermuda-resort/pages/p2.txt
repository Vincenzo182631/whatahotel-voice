# Rooms, Suites & Residences

## Accommodations

The resort features 102 exquisitely appointed guest rooms, including 11 suites and private residences at The Residences at The St. Regis Bermuda. Each accommodation features a private balcony and is designed to immediately draw the eye to expansive ocean views, reflecting the palette, movement and life of the ocean. Ideal when traveling with the family or larger groups in Bermuda, guests staying in our residential hotel suites will indulge in the perfect blend of residential comfort along with the services and amenities of a luxury resort.

## John Jacob Astor Suite

Luxury living at its finest and the largest suite at our St. George, Bermuda, resort. This sophisticated hotel suite offers a large bedroom and dressing area with master bathroom, office space, living room, butler pantry, and is named after our founder John Jacob Astor IV.
