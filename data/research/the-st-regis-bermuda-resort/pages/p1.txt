# Welcome to The St. Regis Bermuda Resort

## Pursue your passion for luxury at our St. George Resort

Experience effortless relaxation at our St George's Luxury Resort, where considered details create an atmosphere of refined indulgence. Named a Condé Nast 2022 “Traveler's Hot List” winner, our St. George, Bermuda, hotel is a haven of oceanfront luxury, just steps from St. Catherine’s Beach. The tranquil turquoise waters and breathtaking views complement the resort's distinctive setting. Impeccable service and exceptional amenities enhance each stay within our luxury resort accommodations in Bermuda. Be immersed in unparalleled leisure and wellness facilities during your time here. Overlooking Bermuda’s famed azure waters, the resort's pool deck features two swimming pools, luxury loungers and private cabanas. Refined relaxation awaits at The St. Regis Spa, offering a menu of personalized treatments using natural elements. Young resort guests are invited to enjoy enriching activities at The St. Regis Children's Club, creating lasting memories of their St. George adventure.

### Five Forts Golf Club

Breathing new life into a historic landmark, the scenic Five Forts Golf Course presents an 18-hole course designed for players of all levels, offering compelling ocean views throughout the game. Named for the five notable forts that surround it, “beginning near Fort Victoria, players will enjoy the surrounding ocean views as they make their way across the 4,436-yard course, concluding their game at Fort St. Catherine. State-of-the-art, GPS-enabled golf carts will ensure the brand’s bespoke service reaches guests no matter where they are on the green.

Timeless Traditions

### Afternoon Tea

Introduced in the 1800s to New York's elite by The Caroline Astor as a joyful opportunity to host her closest friends for a mid-day gathering that helped curb hunger pains between meals, The St. Regis Afternoon tea has become a defining part of the brand's culture and is one of the most beloved signature rituals.

### The Original Bloody Mary

Invented in 1934 by famed barman Fernand Petiot at The St. Regis New York's King Cole Bar, this iconic cocktail has crafted its own unique, locally inspired portfolio at St. Regis properties worldwide. In Bermuda, the Gates Bay Mary is named after the turquoise bay bordering the resort. It features native fennel, Goslings Black Seal Rum, Outerbridge’s original sherry pepper sauce, and a special island spice mix.

### Evening Ritual

Dating back to the battles of Napoleon Bonaparte, who famously opened champagne with his saber in both victory and defeat, the art of sabrage is the signature ceremony of the St. Regis Evening Ritual series and is offered daily. “Champagne: in victory, one deserves it; in defeat, one needs it.”

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Bermuda Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Bermuda Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Regis Bermuda Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Bermuda Resort. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Bermuda Resort?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Bermuda Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Bermuda Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Bermuda Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Bermuda Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Bermuda Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Bermuda Resort is L F Wade International Airport (BDA). BDA is located approximately 6.8 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Bermuda Resort have an airport shuttle to L F Wade International Airport (BDA)?

No, The St. Regis Bermuda Resort does not have an airport shuttle to L F Wade International Airport.

Does The St. Regis Bermuda Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Bermuda Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
