# Escape to our serene Bermuda spa

## The Spa

Escape to a sanctuary where Bermudian serenity meets the timeless glamour of St. Regis. At our hotel spa in Bermuda, every moment is thoughtfully crafted to restore, elevate, and indulge.

### Outdoor Plunge Pool

Step into refreshing serenity at our Outdoor Plunge Pool surrounded by open sky and gentle coastal breezes. This alfresco sanctuary offers the perfect moment of renewal before or after your hotel spa treatment in Bermuda.

### Spa Specials

Indulge in the ultimate relaxation with our exclusive Bermuda spa specials at The St. Regis Bermuda. From soothing treatments to rejuvenating experiences, every moment is designed to restore your body, refresh your mind, and elevate your senses. Treat yourself to the luxury you deserve — your perfect island escape begins here.

### Steam Room

Step into a world of enveloping warmth and gentle mist in our steam room, where the cares of the day dissolve and your body and mind are renewed. Designed to open pores, detoxify, and soothe tired muscles, this serene retreat enhances circulation and leaves you feeling purified.
