# Tee off in Bermuda at our golf resort

## Five Forts Golf Club

Named after the rich heritage of St. George's, former capital of Bermuda, the golf course is surrounded by the five forts neighboring including the largest and most celebrated in Bermuda, Fort St. Catherine, situated just steps from The St. Regis Bermuda resort. Wherever you play, you will be enchanted by the natural splendor and stunning panoramic ocean views of our golf resort in Bermuda.

### The Course

Crafted to suit players of all skill levels, Five Forts Golf Club invites you to enjoy a truly exceptional golfing experience. This thoughtfully designed links course offers a distinctive journey from start to finish. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Are guests able to make online reservations to Five Forts Golf Club?

Online reservations are accepted at Five Forts Golf Club. **Reserve a tee time online**, or call us at +1 441-543-6715.

Do kids golf for free at Five Forts Golf Club?

Kids golf for free at Five Forts Golf Club.

Does Five Forts Golf Club have a clubhouse?

There is not a clubhouse at Five Forts Golf Club

What is par at Five Forts Golf Club?

Five Forts Golf Club is a par-62, 18-hole course, with a total length of 4436 yards. To learn more about the specifications of Five Forts Golf Club, please call +1 441-543-6715.

Does Five Forts Golf Club offer a driving range?

A driving range is located at Five Forts Golf Club. Amateur and experienced golfers alike can warm up or practice their shot ahead of their next round.

Does Five Forts Golf Club offer equipment rental?

Equipment rental is offered at Five Forts Golf Club.

Does Five Forts Golf Club offer golf school instruction?

Golf school instruction is offered at Five Forts Golf Club. Work with experts to improve your golf game.

Does Five Forts Golf Club provide lessons?

Lessons are offered at Five Forts Golf Club. Golfers in search of tips or tweaks to elevate their golf game can feel encouraged to do so.

Is there a children’s golf program available at Five Forts Golf Club?

There is not a children’s golf program available at Five Forts Golf Club.

What forms of payment does Five Forts Golf Club accept?

Yes, Five Forts Golf Club offers the following forms of payment: Credit Cards

Are spikes allowed at Five Forts Golf Club?

Spikes are not permitted at Five Forts Golf Club.

Is there a putting green available at Five Forts Golf Club?

A putting green is available for guests at Five Forts Golf Club.
