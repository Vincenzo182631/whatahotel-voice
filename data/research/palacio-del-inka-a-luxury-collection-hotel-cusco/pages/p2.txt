# Rooms and suites designed to offer an extraordinary stay

## Accommodations

At our Cusco, Peru hotel, our enchanting rooms and suites are restored to offer guests modern comfort while still preserving our treasured colonial heritage. Rich textiles, gilded antiques and hand painted...See More ceilings evoke the characteristic beauty of historic Cusco and provide guests with a unique hotel experience. Sleep peacefully on our ornate beds with crisp cotton linens and plush mattresses. Our marble bathrooms with luxury bath amenities offer guests a spa-like experience in the comfort of their own hotel room. Lounge on your private balcony for stunning views of downtown Cusco or the distant Andes mountains.See Less

### Bedroom Presidential Suite

This Cusco luxury suite has been thoughtfully designed for families and guests traveling in groups. Each room provides in-bed entertainment and a walk-in closet ensures ample storage space, work area and one and half bathrooms.

## Imperial Jr. Suite

Presenting an indelible combination of space, amenities, and historic details, our Imperial Suite offers characteristic street or Kusicancha courtyard views from a private balcony in select suites.

### Signature Luxury Collection Beds

Our bed, designed exclusively for The Luxury Collection Hotels, turns every night into a time for enriching the soul with a lavish pillow top, modal yarn quilting, and cashmere fabric.

### In-Room Dining

Enjoy our delicious restaurant fare from the privacy and comfort of your room. The room service menu offers the best from our kitchen and serves it to you fresh, hot, and in no time at all.

### Stay Connected

Stay connected with complimentary high speed Wi-Fi in each of our Cusco hotel rooms and suites.

Do the rooms at Palacio del Inka, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Cusco have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Palacio del Inka, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Cusco have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Palacio del Inka, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Cusco?

The largest occupancy room at Palacio del Inka, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Cusco is the 2 Bedroom Presidential Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Some rooms at Palacio del Inka, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Cusco permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
