### Epicurean Moments

Epicurean Moment

## Marquis' Heritage Experience

In the heart of the historic Casona de los Cuatro Bustos, the Marquis' heritage is reborn. A gastronomic concept and place to eat in Cusco, Peru, inspired by the life of Marquis Juan de Salas y Valdez, a figure who embodied the sophistication, power, and hospitality of colonial Cusco.

Epicurean Moment

## Pisco Masterclass

Our bartenders have prepared a hands-on experience where you will learn how to prepare the perfect Pisco sour. They will guide you every step of the way and tell you all about the history of Peru’s national drink.

Epicurean Moment

## Native Potatoes Tasting

You can enjoy a delicious tasting of native Peruvian potatoes, prepared with inputs from the peasant community of Huaccoto, with which we work and support.

Epicurean Moment

## Apus Ñam

Immerse yourself in Andean history and enjoy an extraordinary journey that will lead you to discover the Apus Ñam at our Cusco, Peru, bar. This encapsulates the flavors of a sacred place for one of the most important civilizations in history. On this journey, we want to share the taste of our cocktails, highlighting Andean spirits and ingredients produced in this city.

Epicurean Moment

## Ancestral Tasting Menu

As a tribute to our regional products and fusion of cultures, our chef has put together a sumptuous 8-course tasting menu, offering the best international cuisine with a real touch of Novo-Andean flavors. This experience includes 2 amuse-bouches, 3 starters, 2 main dishes, and 1 dessert.
