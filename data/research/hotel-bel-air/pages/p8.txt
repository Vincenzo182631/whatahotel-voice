## Making a splash

For over 75 years, the signature oval-shaped pool has been the sunny focal point of the good life at Hotel Bel-Air.

HISTORY

A tranquil setting

This former horse-riding ring was the vision of owner Joseph Drown, who transformed the property in the 1940s by personally directing his team of builders and gardeners.

CELEBRITY STATUS

Strike a pose

Marilyn Monroe loved this place, so it’s very fitting that one of her most famous photo shoots for Vogue took place here. Robert Wagner started out his career as one of our pool boys, and we star on Bryan Ferry’s 1974 Another Time, Another Place album cover.

LEGENDARY

Movie location

The opening sequence of the 1965 movie The Silencers sees Dean Martin getting a soaking from his co-star Stella Stevens whilst relaxing beside our pool.

At a glance

Meet me at the pool

Take your place on a poolside lounger and experience this magical setting for yourself. The pool is open daily for hotel guests from 8am – 6pm with food and drinks served from 8am – 4pm.

An aerial view

Known for its distinctive and unmistakable shape, our oval-shaped pool measures 23m x 10m/76ft x 34ft.

The perfect temperature

The pool is heated to 27.8˚C/82˚F for your comfort and year-round enjoyment.

Facilities

We’re here to take the very best care of you. Luxurious loungers, sun shades and towels are provided along with luxurious skincare products, including sunscreen.
