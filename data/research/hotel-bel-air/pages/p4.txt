What restaurants are available at Hotel Bel-Air?

We have The Restaurant at Hotel Bel-Air, the Bar & Lounge, The Pâtisserie, and afternoon tea service on the weekends in The Living Room.

Tell me about The Restaurant at Hotel Bel-Air.

The Restaurant at Hotel Bel-Air is our signature dining venue, open daily. Fresh, seasonal cuisine is served in a beautiful indoor-outdoor setting.

Tell me about the Bar & Lounge.

Hotel Bel-Air's Bar & Lounge is open daily for drinks and a full bar menu, served in a relaxed atmosphere with nightly live music.

Is breakfast à la carte or buffet?

Breakfast is served à la carte in all available outlets.

Is The Restaurant open every day?

Yes, The Restaurant at Hotel Bel-Air is open daily until 10pm.

Where can I get a drink?

The Bar & Lounge is open daily at 2pm for drinks.

Where do in-house guests have breakfast?

Breakfast for guests is served at The Restaurant from 7am to 11am, or through 24-hour in-room dining. Guests can also enjoy casual house-made items from The Pâtisserie.

Can non-residents dine at the hotel?

Yes, our restaurant and bar welcomes outside guests; we recommend booking in advance.

Is there a relaxed spot for a daytime drink?

Yes, the Bar & Lounge offers a relaxed setting and is open daily from 2pm.

What dining style can I expect at The Restaurant?

The Restaurant offers refined all-day dining in an indoor-outdoor setting open daily. West Coast ingredients meets European timing.

Is afternoon tea served at Hotel Bel-Air?

Yes, afternoon tea is served in The Living Room on Friday, Saturday and Sundays from 3 to 6pm. It's inspired by our beautiful lush gardens, and reservations are recommended.
