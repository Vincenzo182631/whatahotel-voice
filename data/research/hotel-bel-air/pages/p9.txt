Frequently asked questions

What are the spa's opening hours?

The Spa at Hotel Bel-Air is open daily from 9am to 7pm.

What facilities does the spa offer?

The Spa at Hotel Bel-Air offers signature face, massage and body treatments. It has seven treatment rooms, one private enclave, a nail suite and steam rooms.

Can I book spa treatments?

Yes, treatments are available daily at the Spa at Hotel Bel-Air; please contact us to arrange or reserve online.

Are the spa hours different on weekends?

No, the spa is open from 9am to 7pm every day.

What are the gym's opening hours?

The gym is open 24-hours a day, with access for hotel guests and spa members only.

When is the pool open?

The Hotel Bel-Air pool is open for hotel guests daily from 8am to 6pm.

Can spa guests use the pool?

The Hotel Bel-Air pool is reserved for hotel guests only.

Are there specific pool time slots?

No, there are no specific time slots; the pool in our Los Angeles hotel is family-friendly. Please note, access to the pool is reserved for hotel guests only.

Is the pool suitable for families?

The pool is open to all hotel guests. It's family-friendly with no specific time slots. Please note that all children under the age of 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Can children use the pool?

Children must be accompanied by a parent or guardian until the age of 16.

Is there an age restriction for spa treatments?

• Guests under age 12: Nail services only. Please note, a parent or legal guardian must be present for the service, and we are unable to allow access to the locker room, relaxation lounge, steam, or other adult spa amenities.

• Guests ages 12 to 15: May receive approved massage, facial, manicure, and pedicure services. A parent or legal guardian must sign the Minor Consent Form and remain in the treatment room for the entire duration.

• Guests ages 16 to 17: May receive approved massage, facial, manicure, and pedicure services with a signed Minor Consent Form. A parent or legal guardian must accompany the minor to the spa, complete the required paperwork, and remain on hotel grounds for the duration of the service.

• Body treatments, specialty treatments, or other age-sensitive protocols are exclusively for guests aged 18 years or older.

Is there an age restriction for using the spa facilities?

• Locker rooms, relaxation lounges, steam rooms, saunas, fitness facilities, and other adult spa amenities are exclusively for guests 18 years of age and older.

• Minor guests should be escorted directly to and from the treatment area and should use a private changing area or treatment room when changing is necessary.

• Minors may not remain unaccompanied in spa common areas before or after their service.

Do you offer a spa membership?

Yes, we do. Please contact the spa for more details. You can reach us by phone at +1 310 909 1681, or email us at spa.HBA@dorchestercollection.com.

Do you sell spa or hotel gift cards?

Yes, we sell Hotel Bel-Air gift cards at the spa which can be redeemed in the spa or the hotel. Please contact us for more details. You can reach us by phone at +1 310 909 1681, or email us at spa.HBA@dorchestercollection.com.
