Frequently asked questions

Where is Hotel Bel-Air located?

Our luxury hotel in Bel Air, Los Angeles, is located at 701 Stone Canyon Road, CA 90077. It's a mile west of Beverly Hills and half a mile from Sunset Boulevard.

How far is the hotel from the airport and beach?

Both the airport and the beach are around 30-40 minutes away.

What's close to the hotel?

We're a 10-minute drive from Rodeo Drive, Sunset Boulevard, or Century City, and just two miles from the 405 freeway.

How do I contact the reservations office?

You can reach our reservations office by calling +1 424 421 0070.

Is there a minimum size for a group booking?

Group booking rates apply from six or more rooms.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

Yes, Hotel Bel-Air is a dog friendly luxury hotel in Los Angeles. We welcome up to two small dogs per room (under 7kg/15lbs) at $150 per stay to cover deep cleaning. We provide a bowl, bed and treats for our furry friends.

What is the parking fee?

Parking is $70.00 per day.

What facilities does the hotel offer?

We have The Spa at Hotel Bel-Air, a 24-hour gym, a family-friendly pool, a restaurant, bar & lounge, Pâtisserie and afternoon tea in The Living Room.

Can spa guests use the pool?

The Hotel Bel-Air pool is reserved for hotel guests only.

How far is the hotel from the closest freeway?

We're conveniently located just two miles from the 405 freeway.

What makes Hotel Bel-Air distinctive?

Hotel Bel-Air is a legendary hideaway and one of Los Angeles’ most private addresses, tucked within twelve acres of gardens. Just minutes from the excitement of the city, but with a serene way of life that’s all its own.

Is the hotel close to Beverly Hills?

Yes, we're just a mile west of Beverly Hills and a short drive from Rodeo Drive and Sunset Boulevard.
