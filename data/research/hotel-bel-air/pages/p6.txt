CUISINE

The secret is in the ingredients

Our innovative dishes are based around LA sunshine and Mediterranean freshness. We love nothing more than mixing things up on the menu, but some things simply can't change. We still serve the famous tortilla soup that debuted here in 1980.

THE VIEW

A true LA moment

The alcoves peer over Swan Lake and glow like secret dens of vibrant conversation. The distant melody of the grand piano twists into the evocative evening hum. This is understated Hollywood in full glory. This is glamour at her shining best.

PRIVATE DINING

An intimate space

Just off the main dining room, behind towering glass wine displays and a double-sided fireplace, you’ll find an intimate private room seating up to 18 people.

EXPERIENCE

The Bel-Air Picnic

A picnic is one of life’s simple pleasures. By our tranquil Swan Lake or other local beauty spots, there’s no better way to feast al fresco. Our chefs have created a gourmet menu including artisanal cheeses, sandwiches and seasonal pastries.
