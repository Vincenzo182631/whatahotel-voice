## Dining, tailored to you

Every meal here is shaped around you, wherever the mood takes you. Lunch on the sand at Turtle Beach, dinner under the stars on the Great House roof terrace, or simply help yourself, any time, day or night; Necker is yours to treat like home.

Our chefs draw on a melting pot of Caribbean flavour and fresh island ingredients, turning out menus that shift from barefoot-casual to candlelit and formal, with a floating sushi bar on hand for something different. Drinks are just as generous: cocktails at the Beach Bar, wine with dinner, and a bar that's always open.

## On the island, and beyond

There's a whole island to explore, and half of it happens off dry land. Take a paddleboard out into crystal-clear water, hike the ridge trails that trace the coastline, or let the spa work its quiet magic at the water's edge. Wander far enough and you might meet the island's resident lemurs, or find the flamingos gathered at their pond.

When you're ready to venture further, a boat trip to the Virgin Gorda Baths waits, granite boulders and hidden rock pools included. However you spend your days here, a beach is never far.
