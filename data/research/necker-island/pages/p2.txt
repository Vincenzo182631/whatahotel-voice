# Necker Island Individual Stays

## A slice of paradise

Yes, you really can stay on one of the Caribbean's most exclusive private islands without hiring it exclusively. During select dates each year, you can book an individual room or suite on Necker Island.

Bring your favourite people, and still get the luxurious, all-inclusive, island experience: fine dining, an excellent wine and cocktail list, and endless water sports, all included.

## How it works

Book a room as an individual or couple. Or, take a few rooms in your own private Balinese house to enjoy the luxury of the island with family and friends.

You'll experience everything Necker has to offer, from all your food and drinks to endless tailor-made activities and water sports. Necker Island Rate Card 2025 Individual Stays 18 Sept 24

## The Necker Island experience: What's included

### Island life made easy

- Luxurious accommodation with everything you need for a comfortable and relaxing island stay
- Return boat transfer from Virgin Gorda or Beef Island airports
- A dedicated team on hand throughout your stay to arrange experiences, dining, activities and anything else you need
- A Kids Club and curated programme of activities to keep younger guests entertained, with plenty of adventures for the whole family to enjoy together

### Dining & drinks

- Breakfast, lunch and dinner across a variety of dining locations
- 24-hour à la carte dining
- Afternoon treats and late-night snacks
- All drinks, including cocktails, spirits, beer, wine and soft drinks
- Bespoke dining experiences arranged by the team

### Land and sea adventures

- A wide variety of watersports: snorkelling, paddleboarding, kayaking, Hobie sailing, e-foiling, seabobbing and more
- Use of the tennis, padel and pickleball courts, with access to a dedicated on-island tennis professional for lessons and coaching
- Complimentary island hikes and yoga classes, with a range of additional wellness experiences available at an additional charge, including spa treatments and sauna
