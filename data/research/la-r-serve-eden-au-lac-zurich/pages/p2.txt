# Rooftop Bar

«Magnifique», «Unique» and «Dreamlike» – this is what our local and international clientele have to say about the rooftop bar in the Philippe Starck-designed building on the shores of Lake Zurich.

In summer, our rooftop opens with a fine selection of snacks – perfect for a relaxed aperitif above the rooftops of the city. Enjoy a glass of champagne from Michel Reybier or a rosé from the Château La Mascaronne estate in Provence with a breathtaking 360° panoramic view of the historic city center, the lake and the Alps in the distance.

For special occasions, our rooftop is also available exclusively for private events. If you are interested, please contact us at guestservice@lareserve-zurich.com.

Cheers – we look forward to a relaxing summer with you!
