# A menu inspired by culinary traditions from Mediterranean-Italian cuisine

### 1 MICHELIN STAR, 17 GAULT-MILLAU POINTS & 50 TOP ITALY AWARD

As a young man, Marco Ortolani dreamed of discovering the world. Today, the Italian Executive Chef and his talented team bring its flavours to the Eden Kitchen—shaping a culinary experience that embraces the standards of fine dining without the formality that so often accompanies it. Together, the team prepares around twenty à la carte dishes, complemented by a changing selection of daily specials—each one crafted in the open show kitchen using seasonal ingredients sourced locally whenever possible. «When an ingredient inspires us, we follow it back to its roots. We seek to understand where and how it grows, what influences its flavour, and the cultural significance it holds. For us, cooking isn’t about standing out—it’s about belonging. It’s about culture.»

At Eden Kitchen, the chefs don’t see themselves as artists but as artisans—interested not in spectacle but in honest, truly exceptional food: It begins with top-quality ingredients and carefully selected wines, served with generosity in a relaxed atmosphere where the joy of sharing a meal—and a profound love of food—takes centre stage. «We want our cuisine to be about more than just food—we want it to be a complete experience. And at Eden Kitchen, that experience can feel different every single day you choose to visit. New dishes to discover, new faces at the tables nearby—but the food remains authentic.»

Whether in winter, surrounded by snow-covered trees, or in summer, with a fresh breeze drifting through the wide, lake-facing windows—and always accompanied by the soft sounds of a live DJ—a meal at Eden Kitchen, set within the Philippe Starck-designed space, is a feast for all the senses. From Swiss classics like Zürcher Geschnetzeltes to international favourites like the cheeseburger, from Squid Carbonara to the legendary Paccheri Trafilati in Oro with Breton lobster: every dish at Eden Kitchen is a tribute to culinary craftsmanship. And to the people who bring it to life with passion, love, and dedication.
