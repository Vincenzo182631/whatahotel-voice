# Eden Bar

A NEW KIND OF BAR CULTURE

The Eden Bar stands for modern bar culture, creative craftsmanship and enjoy-ment in all its forms. At its heart is our **50/50 concept**: the menu is deliberately divided equally between cocktails and mocktails – with the same commitment to quality, flavour and experience.

Classic cocktails are reinterpreted using homemade ingredients and modern techniques. Each cocktail has a non-alcoholic counterpart, created with the same care and attention to detail. Rather than focusing on compromise, the Eden Bar embraces equality: drinks with depth, character and balanced flavours – with or without alcohol.

Whether you choose a cocktail, mocktail or a glass of rosé from Château La Mascaronne in Provence, the Eden Bar provides the perfect setting for an aperitif, a relaxed evening or a special occasion. A selection of culinary delights completes the experience.

In the evening, our “Three Angels for La Réserve Zurich” set the mood from the DJ booth. Every day from 6 pm to 10 pm, electronic sounds, house music and energetic rhythms complement the unique atmosphere of the Eden Bar.
