### Explore Fiji’s top sights

Home to idyllic beaches, exquisite ecosystems and a warm and welcoming culture of hospitality, Fiji is the ideal destination for travelers looking for a personal slice of paradise. Drawing thousands of visitors each year, you don’t have to look far to see why this destination remains a bucket-list getaway.

Bouma National Heritage Park

Find waterfalls, rainforests, and tropical vegetation on this National Park located on the third-largest island in Fiji.

Beqa Lagoon

Just South of Viti Levu, Beqa Lagoon offers some of the best scuba diving conditions in Fiji, along with a variety of coral and marine life to explore.

Garden of the Sleeping Giant

A horticultural paradise, this unique garden is bursting with over 2,000 varieties of orchids and native plants.

Frequently asked questions for traveling to Fiji

## Where is the best place to stay in Fiji for first-time visitors?

For first-time visitors to Fiji, Castaway Island in the Mamanuca Islands and Viti Levu are excellent places to stay. Castaway Island offers a classic Fiji island experience with white-sand beaches, clear lagoons and a relaxed, all-inclusive feel that is ideal for families and couples. Viti Levu, where OUTRIGGER Fiji Beach Resort is located on the Coral Coast, provides easy access from Nadi International Airport along with cultural experiences, dining and a wider range of activities. Both locations offer a memorable introduction to Fiji, depending on whether travelers prefer a private island escape or a main-island resort experience.

## Is Fiji a good destination for families, couples or honeymooners?

Yes. Fiji is an excellent destination for families, couples and honeymooners. Families appreciate the welcoming culture, calm beaches and resort activities designed for all ages. Couples and honeymooners are drawn to Fiji’s romantic island settings, oceanfront accommodations and opportunities for relaxation and connection. Fiji’s friendly atmosphere makes it appealing for a wide range of travelers.

## Are there beachfront resorts in Fiji close to popular attractions?

Yes. Fiji is known for its beachfront resorts. Staying beachfront allows guests to enjoy immediate access to the ocean while being close to snorkeling areas, boat excursions and cultural experiences. Many of Fiji’s top attractions are water-based and easily accessible from island resorts.

## What are the best things to do in Fiji near Castaway Island or Viti Levu?

Near Castaway Island, visitors can enjoy snorkeling in clear lagoons, swimming, kayaking and relaxing on pristine beaches, along with cultural activities and sunset cruises. On Viti Levu, travelers staying along the Coral Coast can explore local villages, waterfalls and nature trails, enjoy river excursions and experience Fijian culture through music, dance and cuisine. Both locations offer a mix of ocean adventures and cultural experiences that showcase the warmth and natural beauty of Fiji.

## When is the best time of year to visit Fiji?

Fiji is a year-round destination, with warm temperatures throughout the year. Many travelers prefer the dry season from late spring through early fall for pleasant weather and outdoor activities. The warmer months bring lush landscapes and fewer crowds. The best time to visit depends on preferred weather, activities and travel dates.

## Do I need a car when staying in Fiji?

Most visitors do not need a car when staying in Fiji, especially when staying on the islands. Transportation is typically arranged by boat, resort transfer or guided excursion. On the main island of Viti Levu, taxis and transfers are widely available, making it easy to explore without renting a car.
