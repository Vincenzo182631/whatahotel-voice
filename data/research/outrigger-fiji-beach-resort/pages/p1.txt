FOOD & DRINK

### The flavors of Fiji

From late night cocktails to family-friendly favorites, Fiji Beach Resort offers something for everyone.

Sundowner Bar & Grill

This beachfront restaurant offers a casual lunch, elegant dinner and relaxing sunset cocktails. Don’t miss our signature wood-fired pizza.

Baravi Restaurant

A culinary journey through India, Thailand, Singapore, and China. Traditional and contemporary preparations for lunch and dinner.

Ivi Restaurant

Award-winning fine dining restaurant serving creative Pacific Continental cuisine. It’s all located adjacent to its namesake – a 100-year old Ivi tree.

Kalo Kalo Bar

Feel like a star at this hilltop perch overlooking the resort. Sensational Pacific Ocean views and elegant service await. Diners can enjoy a mix of contemporary cocktails and tapas, plus oysters and champagne at sunset.

Kana Buffet Restaurant

From the freshest seafood harvest to a traditional Fijian feast with Meke, family- friendly Kana Buffet Restaurant is simply delightful!

Takia On The Go

Located in the lobby, Takia on the Go offers take-away sandwiches, focaccia, pastries and aromatic coffee. Enjoy scenic coastal views and the convenient Internet kiosk.

The Reef Cocktail & Lounge Bar

Gather for drinks and tapas before dinner. After dinner, live music fills the lounge with infectious beats and specialty cocktails flow. Dress code is smart casual. Adults only after 9pm.

Vahavu Bistro

A la carte adults-only breakfast menu and contemporary tapas menu with services to pool side sunbathers. Vahavu also features a swim up pool bar.

How do I get to OUTRIGGER Fiji Beach Resort?

## How do I get to/from the OUTRIGGER Fiji Beach Resort?

Travel to the resort by Private transfer, Taxi, Coach or Helicopter; our recommendation is a private transfer with Tewaka Transfers.

Tewaka Transfers is an award-winning, 100% Fijian-owned and operated transport company with a fleet of sedans, SUVs, mini-coaches, and 53-seat coaches.

Your experience begins on arrival at Nadi International Airport where you will be personally met by the friendly Tewaka’s Airport Team. The guest transfer rate is inclusive of Shell Lei, Bottled Water and Wi-Fi connection in vehicles.

A luggage trailer can be fitted to larger vehicles, accommodating families and groups traveling together. The vehicles are all fully air-conditioned and equipped with GPS tracking devices.

The guest transfer rate (FJD):

FJ$258 in a private car/ one way – Max 4 persons

FJ$330 in a private van / one way – Max 6 persons

FJ$500 in a private van / one way – Max 12 persons

FJ$738 in a private van / one way – Max 15 persons

(Terms and conditions applies)

_A fuel surcharge will be applied until further notice and is subject to change without prior notification._

## How far in advance should I leave for the airport from the property to safely make it through security?

Depart the resort 4 hours prior to departure time. It takes an hour and 10 minutes from the resort to the airport.
