DISCOVERY Dollars questions

Introducing DISCOVERY Dollars (D$), our exclusive rewards currency. Effortless to earn, and all yours to spend however you like. How will you use your D$?

## What are D$?

Upon joining OUTRIGGER DISCOVERY, you'll be eligible to receive a percentage back on all eligible purchases, ranging from 4% to 7% back in D$ every time you visit one of our hotels.

## How do D$ work?

As a member, you are eligible to start earning D$ on eligible stays and purchases at any participating hotel. Apply your D$ towards your eligible bill at check-out on your next stay.

## What can you spend D$ on?

Your D$ are yours to spend however you like. Spend D$ at participating hotels, from rooms to dining, Experiences to Local Offers. Save for your next dream getaway or treat yourself to an upgrade. There are countless ways to spend your D$ across the GHA DISCOVERY program.
