Fiji's original private barefoot island resort

- A private island resort situated on a 174-acre island with lush rainforests, white sandy shores, vivid coral reefs and azure waters
- Beautifully designed traditional thatched-roof bures (bungalows) with vaulted tapa ceilings and modern amenities
- Partnered with the Mamanuca Environment Society, guests can plant coral in the waters fronting the resort to help preserve our oceans

Find us at: Castaway Island, Private Mailbag, Nadi Airport

Resort: +679 666 1233

E-mail: reservations@castawayfiji.com.fj

### Island charm with modern amenities

Enjoy island serenity in our traditional bure bungalows. Sixty-five spacious free-standing bures featuring Fijian style thatched roofs are nestled amongst lush tropical gardens or at the water’s edge. Inside, you’ll find vaulted ceilings lined with hand-painted ‘Tapa’ cloth – each one a unique and individual design. Plus, air conditioning and modern amenities keep you cool and comfortable.

### Beachfront views on the menu

Local flavors and island scenery await. Find everything from quick bites to upscale eats - all without ever leaving the resort.

Restaurant 1808

Dine on the beach under sun and stars, toes in the sand. Enjoy Fijian-Asian fusion cuisine that incorporates farm-fresh organic fruits, vegetables, and herbs.

Nuku Marau Pool Bar and Grill

Find casual dining at our poolside bar. Asian-influenced, Polynesian-inspired, and always crowd pleasing.

Sundowner Bar & Grill

Perched above the beach, this is a relaxing venue for casual cocktails. Made-to-order wood-fired pizza is the house specialty. Takeaway pizza is also available for a romantic picnic or family get-together.

Water’s Edge

Dine under shady umbrellas at the resort’s main restaurant overlooking the beach. Snap and share a stunning sunset, the perfect appetizer to an evening meal.

Fijian Cultural Day

Experience a Fijian Village, complete with activities like Fijian cooking demos, Fijian storytelling, a kava ceremony, artifacts display and even traditional dancing.

Welcome and Farewell

Guests arriving and departing are greeted with melodies of welcome and farewell songs on the beach, where they receive a traditional tekiteki (flower on the ear).

Modriki Excursion

There’s a reason why Fiji was chosen as the filming location for films like Castaway. On our guided excursion with “Wilson,” you’ll visit the island of Modriki to see famous sites where the movie was shot.

## How do I get to/from the Castaway Island, Fiji Resort?

Castaway Island, Fiji Resort is located on the 174-acre private Castaway Island in Fiji's Mamanuca Island group. The resort is easily accessed from Nadi International Airport (NAN) on Viti Levu Island.

**South Sea Cruises - Catamaran**

South Sea Cruises operates daily air-conditioned catamaran service between Port Denarau Marina and Castaway Island. Crusing time is approximately 90 minutes. Please check in 30 minutes prior to departure time.

Three transfers daily:

Depart Denarau 09:30

Arrive/Depart Castaway Island 11:00

Arrive Denarau 12:00

Depart Denarau 12:15

Arrive/Depart Castaway Island 13:50

Arrive Denarau 15:00

Depart Denarau 15:15

Arrive/Depart Castaway Island 16:20

Arrive Denarau 18:00

**Mamanuca Express - Water Taxi**

Private water taxi service for up to 30 passengers is available from Port Denarau Marina. Schedule custom departure times to accommodate late night arrivals and early morning departures. 24/7 service.
