## Historic 5-Star Hospitality in Sorrento

On a cliff above the Bay of Naples and Mount Vesuvius, Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria brings together family heritage, discreet luxury and the effortless rhythm of Sorrento.

Family-owned since 1834, the hotel opens directly onto Piazza Tasso while remaining suspended above the sea. Capri, Positano, Pompeii, Naples and the Amalfi Coast are all within easy reach, with the pleasure of returning to a private garden retreat in the heart of Sorrento.

Frequently Asked Questions

## Before Your Stay

A few essential details for planning a stay in Sorrento, with the sea, Capri and the Amalfi Coast within easy reach.

### Where is Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria located?

Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria is in the heart of Sorrento, on Piazza Tasso, with panoramic views over the Bay of Naples and Mount Vesuvius.

### Is Sorrento a good base for visiting the Amalfi Coast?

Yes. Sorrento is a refined and convenient base for exploring the Amalfi Coast, Capri, Pompeii and Naples, while returning to the comfort of a central sea-view hotel.

### Does the hotel offer sea-view rooms and suites?

The hotel offers elegant rooms and suites, many with views over the Bay of Naples, private terraces or balconies, and interiors shaped by classic Italian style.

### How can guests reach Capri and the coast from the hotel?

The hotel offers private lift access toward the port area, making boat days to Capri and coastal itineraries particularly convenient.

### Does Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria have a Michelin-starred restaurant?

Yes. Terrazza Bosquet offers Michelin-starred dining with refined Mediterranean cuisine and panoramic views over the Bay of Naples.

### Is there a spa at the hotel?

Boutique Spa La Serra is set within the hotel’s private gardens, inside a restored 19th-century greenhouse, with treatments, rituals and private spa experiences.
