## Cozy Room

Welcome to our cozy room, a perfect space for guests seeking comfort and relaxation. This delightful room features a connecting guest room, bedroom 1 with a luxurious king-sized bed, and bedroom 2 with a comfortable double bed. There is also a convenient sofa bed available for extra sleeping arrangements. Enjoy the fresh air on the balcony or unwind in the spacious living/sitting area. With two bathrooms and a generous size of 1075 sqft, this room provides ample space for your utmost convenience and enjoyment.

## Marvelous Jacuzzi Suite

Indulge in the extraordinary luxury of our exclusive Marvelous Jacuzzi Suite. This lavish one-bedroom suite, spanning from 184 to 344 sqm, is a haven of sophistication and tranquility. It boasts a spacious bedroom with a king-sized bed, offering a captivating skyline view. The suite is designed with separate living area and a dining room. The crowning glory of the suite is the private outdoor whirlpool, nestled on your personal balcony, providing a unique sanctuary to unwind while basking in the stunning skyline panoramas. With its opulent design and premium amenities, the Marvelous Jacuzzi Suite is indeed an unparalleled retreat.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Dubai - The Palm have a work space?

No, the rooms at W Dubai - The Palm do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Dubai - The Palm?

The largest occupancy room at W Dubai - The Palm is the Extreme Wow Suite with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Dubai - The Palm?

Some rooms at W Dubai - The Palm permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Dubai - The Palm have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Dubai - The Palm have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at W Dubai - The Palm have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Dubai - The Palm have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Dubai - The Palm have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Dubai - The Palm have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Dubai - The Palm have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Dubai - The Palm have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
