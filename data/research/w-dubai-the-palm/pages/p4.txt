### More About Akira Back

Indulge in a breathtaking culinary experience against a panoramic backdrop, whether you’re seated indoors or out on the terrace. The restaurant’s entrance is a vortex, drawing you one step closer to an ethereal experience. Upon entering, you’ll notice intricate abstract design elements, including artwork by Chef Akira’s mother, cloud-like structures on the ceiling, wabi-sabi finishes, and tiger stripes that honor his zodiac sign.

The rooftop terrace is perfect for a pre- or post-dinner drink or small bites, with the floating bar as its striking centerpiece. For those seeking a more intimate setting, a private dining room is available, ideal for special occasions.

As an authentic Japanese restaurant in Dubai, Akira Back was selected as one of the Michelin Guide Dubai recommended venues for 2022 - 2025.
