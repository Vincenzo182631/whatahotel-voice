# Welcome to W Dubai - The Palm

## HIT ESCAPE AT DUBAI'S MOST ICONIC BEACH RESORT

Relax into the good life at the first W Escape in the Middle East. Along the pristine Palm Jumeirah coastline, this Palm Jumeirah hotel brings together a white-sand private beach, adult-only pools, dedicated family activities and extraordinary Dubai skyline views. One of the luxury hotels in Palm Jumeirah, our Palm Jumeirah beach hotel Dubai offers vibrant design and dreamy amenities across 349 guest rooms and suites, and is also pet friendly. Guests praise the beachfront location, stylish rooms and attentive service. Experience the Michelin-recommended restaurant Akira Back which brings you an adrenalin-filled menu of contemporary Japanese Korean cuisine; brought to you by the name-sake celebrity chef. WET Deck is an adults-only playground with a live DJ. SoBe, the only rooftop bar on Palm Jumeirah with 360° views of the city and the Arabian Gulf, is the perfect place to watch the sunset. For family stays, the resort also offers activities designed for all ages.

Advisory - Travel to Middle East

We continue to closely monitor the current regional situation in the Middle East as it evolves. Our hotel remains open and operational and our teams are focused on supporting our guests at this time.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Dubai - The Palm?

The check-in time at W Dubai - The Palm is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Dubai - The Palm allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Dubai - The Palm. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of د.إ350.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Dubai - The Palm?

On-site parking is available at W Dubai - The Palm. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Dubai - The Palm have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Dubai - The Palm has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Dubai - The Palm booking.

What is the closest airport near W Dubai - The Palm?

The closest airport near W Dubai - The Palm is Dubai International Airport (DXB). DXB is located approximately 40.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Dubai - The Palm have an airport shuttle to Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC)?

Yes, W Dubai - The Palm has an airport shuttle to Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC).

Does W Dubai - The Palm have an airport shuttle to Dubai International Airport (DXB)?

Yes, W Dubai - The Palm has an airport shuttle to Dubai International Airport (DXB).

Does W Dubai - The Palm have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Dubai - The Palm has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
