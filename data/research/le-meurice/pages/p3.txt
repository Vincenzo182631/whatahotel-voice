## The art of the kitchen

# Restaurants and Bars

The gastronomic passion and artistic courage of celebrated chef Alain Ducasse underpins every dish served at Le Meurice. We’re proud to invite you to share in a delicious range of delights that celebrate life, encourage conviviality and warm the soul.

Fine dining

Restaurant le Meurice Alain Ducasse

Overseen by Alain Ducasse with executive chef Amaury Bouhours, Restaurant le Meurice Alain Ducasse has become the epicurean address in Paris, presenting a whole new way of enjoying the two-Michelin-starred experience.

Fine dining

The Chef's Table

Rare and refined. Enjoy a unique dining experience at the Chef’s Table of Le Meurice Alain Ducasse, with a view into the kitchen and a two-Michelin-starred menu by chef Amaury Bouhours.

Creative flair

Restaurant Le Dalí

A unique place with a singular and elegant spirit, a cosy and welcoming hideaway. Inspired by Dalí and designed by Philippe Starck, Restaurant Le Dalí is a lively celebration of local, seasonal French cuisine.

Cosy and intimate

Afternoon tea

At Le Meurice, chef Cedric Grolet reimagines Tea Time as a truly extraordinary moment.

Gourmet experience

La Pâtisserie du Meurice par Cedric Grolet

Our pastry boutique is dedicated to the wonderful collections of our talented pastry chef, Cedric Grolet. Here you'll discover the simple pleasures of freshly-baked Madeleines and brioche Bundt cakes, alongside innovative creations such as the legendary ‘trompe-l’oeil’ sculpted fruit.

Warm and welcoming

Breakfast & brunch

Whether enjoyed in Restaurant Le Dalì, or in an opulent setting inspired by the Salon de la Paix at Versailles, or even served in the comfort of your own beautiful room, breakfast becomes a moment to savour at Le Meurice.

Cosy and intimate

Bar 228

The famous bar of a thousand stories. This is where Parisians settle down into deep leather armchairs and discreet conversation, enraptured by our nightly live jazz.

What restaurants are available at Le Meurice?

We have Le Meurice Alain Ducasse, Le Dali, Bar 228, and the Cedric Grolet patisserie.

Tell me about the Alain Ducasse Paris restaurant.

Restaurant Le Meurice Alain Ducasse serves up two-Michelin star fine dining, in a magnificent space inspired by the Salon de la Paix at Versailles. It's open Monday to Friday.

What does Restaurant le Dalí offer?

Restaurant le Dalí serves seasonal French cuisine in an artistic setting designed by Philippe Starck. It's open every day.

What are Bar 228's hours?

Bar 228 is open from 2pm to 1am, with last orders for food at 0.30am.

Tell me about Cedric Grolet's Le Meurice patisserie.

La Pâtisserie du Meurice par Cedric Grolet serves our renowned pastry chef's creations, including the emblematic Fruits Sculptés. It's open Wednesday to Sunday, from 12pm until everything has sold out.

What time is breakfast served and where?

Breakfast is served from 7 to 10.30am. Guests can choose to enjoy it in Restaurant Le Meurice Alain Ducasse, or in Restaurant le Dalí.

How much does breakfast cost?

Continental breakfast – €62

Healthy breakfast – €73

American breakfast – €75

Which days is Alain Ducasse open?

Restaurant Le Meurice Alain Ducasse is open weekdays and closed on Saturday and Sunday.

Is there a daily dining option?

Yes. Restaurant le Dalí is open every day, serving seasonal French cuisine.

When can I visit the Cedric Grolet patisserie?

La Pâtisserie du Meurice par Cedric Grolet is open Wednesday to Sunday, from 12pm until everything has sold out. To avoid disappointment, we recommend arriving early.

Where can I get a drink?

Bar 228 is open daily for drinks.
