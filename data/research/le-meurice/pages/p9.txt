What are the spa opening hours?

La Maison Valmont pour Le Meurice is open daily from 9am to 7pm.

What are the gym opening hours?

The gym is open daily from 5am to 12am (midnight).

What facilities does the spa offer?

Le Meurice features the only Valmont spa in Paris, with an array of treatments and a well-equipped gym.

Can I book spa treatments?

Yes, treatments are available daily at La Maison Valmont pour Le Meurice from 9am to 7pm. Please contact us to arrange this. Our spa can be reached by email at spa.LMP@dorchestercollection.com and by phone number at +33 0(1) 44 58 10 77.

Is the spa suitable for an early morning workout?

Yes, the gym is open from 5am.

What time does the gym close?

The gym is open until midnight (12am) every day.

Who runs the spa at Le Meurice?

Le Meurice operates the hotel spa, in partnership with Swiss skincare brand Valmont.

Are dogs allowed in the spa?

No, dogs are not permitted in the spa.

Do I need to book spa treatments in advance?

We recommend booking ahead to secure your preferred time. Please contact us to arrange this. Our spa can be reached by email at spa.LMP@dorchestercollection.com and by phone number at +33 0(1) 44 58 10 77.

What time does the spa open and close?

Our luxury hotel spa in Paris is open daily from 9am to 7pm, with the gym open from 5am to midnight.
