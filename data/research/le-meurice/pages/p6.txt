THE CONCEPT

Modern café society

This colourful and fascinating restaurant creates a wonderful place to enjoy a moment at the heart of Le Meurice. Meet, relax with friends, or simply watch the world go by.

THE TEA TIME

Sublime tastes

At Le Meurice, chef Cedric Grolet reimagines Tea Time as a truly extraordinary moment. Delicate savoury treats set the stage for Cedric's spectacular trompe l'oeil Fruits Sculptés, which bring the seasons to life in an art-filled, masterful display. Warm madeleines, brioches and scones add their own soft delight, and a menu of exquisite teas invites exploration.

THE CHEF

Clémentine Bouchon

Chosen by Alain Ducasse, chef Clémentine Bouchon redefines tradition at Le Dalí. Her dishes celebrate French classics, each one subtly elevated by her modern, heartfelt touch.

THE CUISINE

A moment to savour

The restaurant's creative spirit shines through in every detail, from Dalí-inspired touches to Alain Ducasse’s vibrant cuisine, and Cédric Grolet’s exclusive, artful desserts.

THE PASTRY CHEF

Cedric Grolet, the master of pastry

His afternoon tea at Le Meurice is one of the most sought after in Paris, and he's one of the most popular pastry chefs in France. We're proud to have Cedric Grolet with us – and not just because he was named ‘The World’s Best Pastry Chef’ in 2018.

AWARDS

Savouring tomorrow

Restaurant Le Dalí isn't only a sparklingly beautiful setting. It now proudly wears an Écotable label, awarded in recognition for the restaurant's commitment to sustainable gastronomy.
