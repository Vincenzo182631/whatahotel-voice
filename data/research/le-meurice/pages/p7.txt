THE EXECUTIVE CHEF

Rising star Amaury Bouhours

Executive chef Amaury Bouhours is our rising star. Beginning his career at Le Louis XV in Monaco, he went on to work for Alain Ducasse restaurants around Paris before joining Le Meurice as chef de cuisine, moving to executive chef in March 2020.

THE VISION

The legendary Alain Ducasse

Alain Ducasse expresses himself with conviction: before cooking, there is nature. The cook strives to preserve the authentic flavour of the produce until it reaches the plate. Each dish expresses the truth of the product around which it is built. In this cuisine of the essential, the pleasure of the dinner requires that the cook's technique is placed in the background.

THE PASTRY CHEF

Cedric Grolet, the master of pastry

His afternoon tea at Le Meurice is one of the most sought after in Paris, and he's one of the most popular pastry chefs in France. We're proud to have Cedric Grolet with us – and not just because he was named ‘The World’s Best Pastry Chef’ in 2018.

THE CONCEPT

The experience, the story, the food

At the very heart of the restaurant is a celebration of instinctive flavour combinations with an environmental conscience. Sophisticated techniques transform simple ingredients through the talented touch of our chefs. A journey of discovery awaits.

THE DESIGN

A symphony of Grand Siècle splendour

Inspired by the Salon de la Paix at the Château de Versailles and reinterpreted by Philippe Starck, this is a room of majestic, classical elegance combined with contemporary delight.

Sustainable excellence

For the fourth consecutive year, the Restaurant Le Meurice Alain Ducasse has been awarded three Écotable macarons — the highest rating of this label, which rewards commitment to sustainable gastronomy. We're proud to be working to build a more eco-conscious culinary community.
