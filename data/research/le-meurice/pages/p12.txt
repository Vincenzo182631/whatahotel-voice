Where can I host a luxury corporate event in central Paris?

Facing the Tuileries Garden on Rue de Rivoli, moments from the Louvre, Le Meurice sits on the historic axis of Paris. Its salons host corporate events, meetings, executive retreats, conferences, gala dinners and client receptions, with the address doing much of the work before a single guest arrives.

Does Le Meurice offer meeting and conference facilities?

Yes. Le Meurice has three event spaces: Salon Pompadour, Salon Tuileries and Salon Jeu de Paume. Each one suits board meetings, executive meetings, workshops, conferences and private business gatherings.

What types of corporate events can be organised at Le Meurice?

The hotel hosts a wide range of corporate events, including:

Leadership meetings

Board meetings

Conferences and seminars

Product launches

Press events

Networking receptions

Luxury brand activations

Gala dinners

Incentive groups

Executive retreats

Each event is planned with our events team and shaped around what you want it to achieve.

Why choose Le Meurice for a corporate event in Paris?

Known as the hotel of artists and free spirits since 1835, Le Meurice brings together Parisian elegance, attentive service and the two Michelin-starred cuisine of Restaurant le Meurice Alain Ducasse. Set between Place Vendôme, the Louvre and the Tuileries Garden, it places your event at the centre of the city's cultural life.

Which event space is best for a gala dinner or luxury reception?

Salon Pompadour is the hotel's signature ballroom. Inspired by Versailles, with gilded detail and crystal chandeliers, it's the natural choice for gala dinners, award ceremonies, weddings, receptions and corporate celebrations.

Can Le Meurice host weddings, anniversaries and private celebrations?

Yes. Le Meurice regularly hosts weddings, anniversaries, family celebrations, private dinners, milestone birthdays and social occasions. Our events team works closely with each host to shape a celebration around the people in the room.

Is Le Meurice suitable for luxury brand events and fashion industry gatherings?

Absolutely. With its artistic heritage, its address on Rue de Rivoli and salons shaped by French craft, Le Meurice is a natural home for brand launches, private client events, fashion presentations, media gatherings and VIP experiences, particularly during Paris Fashion Week and Art Week.

Does Le Meurice offer private dining for events?

Yes. Private dining ranges from intimate executive dinners to large receptions, with menus guided by the kitchens of Restaurant le Meurice Alain Ducasse. Every menu can be shaped around the occasion, whether it's a private celebration or a corporate evening.

Can I book the entire hotel for an event?

Yes. Le Meurice offers exclusive use for corporate events, weddings, fashion brand takeovers, celebrations and private gatherings. During a buyout, the hotel becomes your own private residence in Paris.

What is included in exclusive use?

Exclusive use includes:

All 160 rooms and suites

Private use of Restaurant le Meurice Alain Ducasse, Le Dalí and Bar 228

Salon Pompadour, Salon Tuileries and Salon Jeu de Paume

Dedicated event and hospitality teams

Together, they let you create a fully immersive event within one of Paris's original palace hotels.

How many rooms and suites are available for exclusive use?

A full buyout includes all 160 rooms and suites: 121 guest rooms and 39 suites. Guests, delegates and VIP attendees can all stay under one roof, overlooking the Tuileries or the rooftops of Paris.

Can I reserve part of Le Meurice for a private event?

Yes. You can reserve a single salon (Salon Pompadour, Salon Tuileries or Salon Jeu de Paume) or combine several, depending on the scale of your event.

Is Le Meurice suitable for luxury destination weddings?

Yes. Facing the Tuileries since 1835, Le Meurice brings together classical Parisian architecture, the cuisine of Restaurant le Meurice Alain Ducasse and light-filled rooms and suites for guests travelling from afar. For couples marrying in Paris, it's a setting with real presence.

Can luxury brands book Le Meurice exclusively for product launches or VIP activations?

Yes. Exclusive use suits fashion, beauty, jewellery and lifestyle brands that want complete privacy, flexibility to bring in their own branding and an immersive guest experience. The hotel has a long history of cultural collaboration.

Does exclusive use include access to La Maison Valmont pour Le Meurice?

La Maison Valmont pour Le Meurice stays open separately during exclusive use, and treatments can be arranged individually for your guests throughout the event.

Why choose Le Meurice for an exclusive-use event in Paris?

Named the N°1 hotel in Paris in La Liste's 2026 ranking, Le Meurice brings together artistic heritage, attentive hospitality, Michelin-starred dining and salons inspired by Versailles. Whether it's a private celebration, an executive retreat, a corporate takeover or a wedding, the hotel holds it all, facing the Tuileries on the historic axis of Paris.
