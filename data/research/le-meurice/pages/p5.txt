BREAKFAST ROOM

The Parisian art of breakfast

A parade of sweet and savoury delights, enjoyed exactly as it should be – in style. Breakfast in our restaurant is an experience to remember, where antique mirrors, crystal chandeliers and frescoes add a touch of glamour to the first meal of the day.

PASTRIES

Afternoon tea at Le Meurice

Our award-winning pastry chef will now serve his freshly-baked pastries and breads at breakfast. It’s a wonderful chance to enjoy Cedric’s famous pain au chocolat, croissants and raisin bread, alongside a new gluten-free selection.

IN-ROOM DINING

Worth waking up for

There’s no better feeling than waking up to the smell of freshly-baked pastries. Enjoy an indulgent breakfast in bed or open the windows facing the Tuileries Garden to dine al fresco.

BRUNCH

Sunday feast

Sunday afternoons unfold leisurely with our brunch, as our chefs present a colourful array of Parisian delights, now served at your table. Finish with Cedric Grolet’s famous sculpted fruit and freshly-baked pastries for a perfect Sunday treat.
