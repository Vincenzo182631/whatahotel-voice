Frequently asked questions

Where is Le Meurice located?

Le Meurice is at 228 rue de Rivoli, 75001 Paris, opposite the Tuileries Garden and a 5-minute walk from The Louvre.

What landmarks are near the hotel?

Our five-star hotel is in front of the Tuileries Garden. From its entrance, you'll see the Louvre to your left, just five minutes' walk away, and the Eiffel Tower to your right.

How far is the hotel from the train station?

The Eurostar (Gare du Nord) is around 15 minutes' drive away.

How do I contact the reservations office?

To book a stay in our luxury hotel near the Louvre, call our reservations office on +33 187 164 450.

Is there a minimum size for a group booking?

Group booking terms apply from 10 or more rooms.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

Yes, we welcome up to two small dogs (under 7 kg/15 lbs) per room, at €40 per day to cover deep cleaning. We'll provide a bowl, bed and treats.

Are there any areas where dogs aren't allowed?

Dogs are not permitted in Bar 228, in Restaurant le Meurice Alain Ducasse, or in the spa.

What is the parking fee?

Parking is complimentary.

What facilities does the hotel offer?

We have a Valmont spa with a gym, plus several acclaimed restaurants, a patisserie and a bar.

Is the hotel close to the Louvre and Tuileries Garden?

Yes, we're opposite the Tuileries Garden and just a 5-minute walk from The Louvre.

What makes Le Meurice distinctive?

Le Meurice is the original palace hotel, the epitome of quiet elegance, with newly-restored rooms and suites alive with an artist's soul of wit and magic.
