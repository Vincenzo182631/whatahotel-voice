## Legacy of care

# ESG

At Le Meurice, we recognise the impact we make on society, the economy and the planet, and aim to make a positive difference in the place we operate, in alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. That’s why environmental, social and corporate governance (ESG) aren’t simply pillars that guide us – it’s who we are.

Fostering a circular economy

It is our primary objective to limit waste as much as possible and give a second life to everything that can have one.

Polyamide recycling

We partner with the French industrial DeepTech company REC, which recycles polyamide found in complex textiles and transforms it into valuable resources.

In 2025, we recycled and extracted 100% of the polyamide found in 886 pairs of tights.

Soap recycling

We work closely with the non-profit organisation SapoCycle to reprocess our used soap bars into hygienic products, which are then distributed to children and families in need.

In 2025, nearly 30kg of soap was collected from Le Meurice.

Cork recycling

We recycle and resell our corks to raise funds for France Cancer, helping to support their fight against cancer through national research organisations.

In 2025, we recycled and sold over 48kg of cork.

Fabric recycling

We donate our luxurious curtains to the sustainable fashion brand Séléné Hélios, which upcycles and repurposes the fabric into unique clothing pieces.

In 2025, we donated over 70 curtains, cushion covers and trimmings to be redesigned into dresses, co-ords and handbags.

Floral donations

Every Friday, we donate our surplus bouquets to Hôpital Bretonneau for staff and visitors to enjoy.

In 2025, we donated just over 800 flower arrangements to the hospital.

Reducing food waste

Le Potager Petit Moulin supplies its premium produce to our restaurants, and in return, we donate our leftover bread to feed pigs at a local farm.

In 2025, over 5 tonnes of bread were collected from Le Meurice.

Food distribution

Each week, we repurpose our surplus vegetables into fresh soup for the French charity Restos du Cœur to distribute to those in need.

In 2025, we donated over 800 litres of fresh soup.

Les Hôtels Solidaires

In partnership with the association Les Hôtels Solidaires, we donate uneaten pastries, hygiene products, bedding and furniture, which are then redistributed to people in vulnerable situations.

In 2025, over 6,000 pastries were collected from Le Meurice, alongside six pairs of shoes, 63 shirts, 114 suits and 30kg of toilet paper.

Systemised sorting

In line with our objectives, we have designed an effective system to sort, reduce and optimise our waste as much as possible.

Waste recycling

We have reached an average waste recovery rate of 60%, a figure that has been steadily increasing since 2021.

Waste reduction

We’ve introduced a series of measures to significantly reduce the amount of waste we produce on a monthly basis.

Since 2021, we’ve reduced our waste by 4,000kg per month.

Plastic reduction

We’re working to remove plastic from our rooms and suites and significantly reduce plastics in delivery packaging.

Traceability

We use the AI-powered platform Eqolux to track and calculate the carbon footprint of our supplier purchases, helping to promote sustainability and performance.

Energy consumption

To reduce our energy consumption by 40% by 2030, we’re working to optimise our current energy sources and outgoings.

Technical building management

Our technical building management strategy allows us to control our energy consumption by operating, maintaining and optimising our mechanical, electrical and structural infrastructure while preserving our heritage.

Renewable energy

100% of our energy comes from renewable sources such as wind and solar.

Cooling network

Our air conditioning is provided by Fraîcheur de Paris to effectively and sustainably cool our historic building.

Responsible transport

We work with Chabé to provide our private chauffeur services, who use a low-carbon fleet of vehicles as well as hybrid and fully electric models.

Eco-conscious dining

Both Restaurant Le Dalí and Restaurant le Meurice Alain Ducasse have been awarded the sustainable mark of an Écotable macaron – with the latter receiving the highest award of three macarons for the fourth year in a row. This prestigious label reflects our commitment to sustainable and responsible dining.

For our people & communities

Explore our We Care philosophy

A ‘We Care’ philosophy, alongside our values of passion, personality, respect, working together and creativity, sits at the centre of everything we do. We welcome our people to somewhere legendary. A place where they can thrive and be themselves.

We’re deeply rooted in the community and are committed to continuing our support to the local area.

Diversity, Inclusion and Belonging

Diversity, Inclusion and Belonging (DIB) are core beliefs of our We Care philosophy. We continually strive to build and nurture a culture where inclusiveness is part of our DNA, reflected in our values of passion, personality, respect, working together and creativity.

Sustainability training

We regularly participate in training and development to embed sustainability into our operations while keeping on top of any environmental government changes.

Charity support

Alongside our charity partners, we also regularly collaborate with the non-profit Refugee Food to support and train refugees in the restaurant industry.

Collaboration

Collaboration across our different departments is key to our progress. Our Green Team represents each department and manages sustainable development issues as they arise.

Promoting French excellence

Promoting French excellence

We’re delighted to have been awarded the prestigious Entreprise du Patrimoine Vivant label – the government certification that celebrates excellent French craftsmanship. More than a title, it reflects our ongoing commitment to preserving our remarkable heritage through exceptional expertise and artistry.
