COCKTAILS

Craft of the cocktail

At Bar 228, the cocktails mean business but are absolutely all about pleasure – and every barman is a master of the craft. Alongside this, fine wines and rare Cognacs make up a menu that invites you to linger long into the night.

DESIGN

Designed to be remembered

Philippe Starck has refreshed this famously beautiful room with a Carrara marble bar counter and flashes of pink copper, brass and stainless steel. The dark woods and painted ceiling remain, alongside the intimate atmosphere and wonderful service.

DINING

Something to eat?

We’re proud to source fresh, local ingredients for our daily lunch and dinner menus at the Bar 228. Try our signature Club 228 burger or spend an evening sharing grilled antipasti and seafood from the South of France.

EXPERIENCE

The hideaway

Tucked away from the eyes of the world, this is a room to sink into. Let your eyes adjust to the sultry lighting and hear little but the murmurings of jazz and low conversation.
