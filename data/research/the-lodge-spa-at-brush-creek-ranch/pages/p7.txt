# French Creek

Stand on the edge of adventure. Our elite all-inclusive enclave is for passionate outdoorsmen and women who are ready to connect with nature and embrace tranquility in a remote location. Located on the historic Sanger Ranch, French Creek offers access to the nation’s premier fly-fishing waters and hunting grounds, as well as a number of other outdoor pursuits. Your luxury all-inclusive vacation includes two activities daily, three meals daily, and select beer, wine, and spirits. \*Must be 21+ years of age to consume alcohol.

Luxury All-Inclusive Ranch

## Remote, & Creekside Destination

The most secluded and intimate property in our collection of luxury ranches provides peaceful privacy for you and your group. Enjoy quality time with family and close friends as you occupy only four single-room cabins and a luxury yurt. If you’re traveling with youngins, our all-inclusive Lil Wranglers program educates and entertains them while providing parents adult time.

Accommodating up to only 14 guests, French Creek’s service is exceptionally personalized, including your own concierge, guides, and personal chef for the entirety of your customized retreat. Wake each morning to the sound of the running creek just steps from your door and end each evening making memories fireside under a blanket of stars.

Fly-Fishing & Outdoor Adventure

## 2026 Orvis Fly-Fishing Lodge of the Year

A true destination for anglers, French Creek is nestled along The North Platte River watershed, revered as one of the most iconic fly-fishing destinations in North America, as well as the world. Flowing through expansive canyons and sloping valleys, its tributaries, including Brush Creek, French Creek, and the Encampment River, offer over 20 miles of pristine, exclusive private water. Whether by raft, drift boat or waders, our Orvis-certified guides will outfit you with top-of-the-line gear and know-how, while the Wyoming countryside provides the views and memories that will last a lifetime.

## Luxury Fly-Fishing Ranch in Wyoming

Part of the Brush Creek Luxury Ranch Collection, French Creek is your all-inclusive sporting destination in south-central Wyoming. Secluded, private, and serene, this creekside property is 25 minutes into the peaceful wilderness from the gates of the Lodge & Spa. Private air into Saratoga Jet Center will land you closest to our historic ranch, or scenic views can be enjoyed along the I-80 and WY Hwy 130 driving approximately one hour from the Laramie Regional Airport or three and a half hours from Denver International Airport.
