# Spa & Wellness

Drawing inspiration from both indigenous ingredients and American Western tradition, our premier spa is ready to pamper you with everything from massages to facial services. We also feature a soothing sauna and steam room to further relax mind, body, and spirit after a memorable day exploring our Ranchlands.

Restorative Journeys begin here

## About the Trailhead Spa

Welcome to the Trailhead Spa, where the adventure of the west meets the tranquil healing of the Platte River Valley. We believe in genuine listening—to the rhythms of our bodies, to the sounds of our valley, to the longings of our spirit. Perfectly crafted for you, our treatments incorporate a nurturing blend of organic products, skillful techniques, and a holistic setting to bring about a healing renewal only possible at the soul-inspiring Brush Creek Ranch.

## Wellness Experiences

Whether standing before breathtaking mountainscapes, journeying to a historic schoolhouse, or enjoying the premier wellness center, our yoga and fitness classes are expertly curated to unravel your deepest tensions. Here, the busyness of life melts away, the balance of body and mind is embraced, and the company of kindred spirits is discovered. No matter where you find yourself at Brush Creek Ranch, there’s room to decompress and deeply breathe in renewal.

#### Trailhead Spa Menu

Facials

The High Plains Detox Facial

60 min

This deeply restorative facial harnesses the power of antioxidant-rich raw cacao, activated bamboo charcoal, mineral-dense salts and clays, and a curated blend of healing prairie herbs to reset and revive stressed skin. Designed as a full-spectrum skin renewal treatment, it works in layers—drawing out impurities, calming inflammation, and reawakening natural radiance. The result is skin that feels reset: calmer, clearer, and visibly brighter, with a natural glow that looks like fresh air and open space made visible.

The Prairie Gold Hydration Facial

60 min

A high-desert inspired infusion of restorative botanicals and mineral-rich elements designed to deeply hydrate, soften, and restore luminous skin vitality. This exquisitely crafted facial unites raw organic honey, trace-element silver, ultra-fine white halloysite clay, and aromatic plant oils in a sensorial treatment that feels both grounding and indulgent. A gentle, naturally derived exfoliating acid works in harmony with honey’s enzymatic properties to dissolve dull surface buildup, revealing fresh, hydrated, supple skin beneath without disruption or irritation. The result is skin that feels comforted, renewed, and effortlessly luminous.

The Silver Sage Age Reversal Facial

60 min

A masterfully layered western apothecary ritual where desert minerals meet forest botanicals, and hydration meets deep cellular renewal. This is an advanced anti-aging treatment designed to visibly refine texture, restore luminosity, and support collagen integrity through a dual-phase system of detoxification and enzymatic nourishment. The result is skin that appears visibly brighter, firmer, and more even-toned, with a renewed elasticity and a lit-from-within radiance that feels both raw and refined.

##### Spa Policy

_\*Please note, prices and hours of operation are subject to change._

**HOURS OF OPERATION**

The Trailhead Spa is open daily from 9 A.M. to 6 P.M. Common area bathroom, shower, steam room, and sauna are open 24 hours. Spa Locker Rooms are closed after the final spa guest of the day.

**SPA RESERVATIONS**

Reservations may be made through the concierge team or directly through the Spa. You can reach the Spa in person, via phone at 307-327-2041, or by email at Spa@brushcreekranch.com. Guests are encouraged to pre-book appointments; however, we will do our best to accommodate walk-in requests.

**CHECK-IN**

We encourage you to check in 15 minutes prior to your appointment to unwind and enjoy our sauna and steam room.

**CANCELLATION & RESCHEDULING POLICY**

24-hours advanced notice of changes or cancellations is required to avoid being charged in full. Morning appointments must be cancelled by 6 P.M. the evening prior.

**EARLY MORNING OR LATE-NIGHT APPOINTMENTS**

Appointments may be made outside of normal operating hours for a 40% increase in the treatment price.

**LATE ARRIVALS**

Arriving late may interfere with the length of your treatment. All appointments will end at their scheduled time with consideration for the next guest.

**PAYMENT INFORMATION**

All spa services include a 20% service fee as well as local taxes and will be placed on your guest folio, unless otherwise requested.
