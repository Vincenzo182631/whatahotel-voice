# Accommodations

Our luxury dude ranch accommodations combine legacy, tradition, and heritage with modern extravagance. The original homestead architecture and design inspired the present build of our log cabins and Trailhead Lodge. Authentic, western exteriors are accompanied by modern amenities and lavish accents to favor your ultimate comfort. Our accommodations provide a seamless semblance of the “old west” while maintaining luxury. With 45 units featuring 74 total bedrooms, our Wyoming lodging options include 19 rooms in the main Trailhead Lodge and 26 private, luxury log cabins.

## Wyoming at Your Window

Awaken to the sun rising over mountain peaks and step out onto vast landscapes of seemingly endless wilderness. Peer out your guestroom windows to find grazing horses, deer, and pronghorns. Set in secluded nature, your soul will restore and rejuvenate to the sounds of silence under star-studded Wyoming skies.
