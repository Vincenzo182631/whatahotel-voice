# Lodge & Spa at Brush Creek Ranch

Brimming with western luxury, The Lodge & Spa is an exclusive destination abundant with authentic ranch culture, unbridled adventure, and limitless panoramic landscapes. This place will leave you speechless, while providing your family and friends with a lifetime of stories.

## Your Grand Adventure

Inspired by the majestic beauty and rich history of the region, The Lodge & Spa is where traditional rusticity meets unmatched luxury. Rest and restore your soul with a vast array of wellness experiences and vitality-fueled moments among family and friends. Whether you find your inner rancher on the back of a horse, casting lines over peaceful waters, or reveling in unforgettable culinary experiences sourcing ingredients from the land, there’s something truly remarkable waiting to be discovered by everyone.

Stay at the Lodge & Spa

### Luxury dude ranch accommodations

Western-style accommodations pay homage to the old west design and architecture of the original homestead, built in 1884, while providing the comforts of modern luxuries. Authentic décor and lavish accents provide a rustically elegant setting where the true spirit of the American West is felt in every detail.

With 45 individual units, our Wyoming lodging options include 26 private, luxury log cabins ranging from 1- to 4-bedrooms, and 19 rustically-elegant lodge rooms. Groups of up to 155 guests are welcome.

## True Spirit of the American West

The original destination within the Brush Creek Luxury Ranch Collection, the Lodge & Spa is located among endless, pristine wilderness in south-central Wyoming. Retreat to a private log cabin or rustically elegant lodge room set between the ancient Sierra Madre mountain range and stunning Medicine Bow National Forest, just minutes from the remote town of Saratoga. Guests can fly by private air into Saratoga Jet Center, only 16 miles from our front gates. Scenic views are enjoyed along the I-80 and WY Hwy 130 driving approximately one hour from the Laramie Regional Airport or three and a half hours from Denver International Airport.
