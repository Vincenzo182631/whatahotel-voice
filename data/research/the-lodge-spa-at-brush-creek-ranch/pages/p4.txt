# Our Culinary Philosophy

The American West’s culinary heritage is one of legendary proportions. Whether you’re indulging in summer tomato salads, our Medicine Bow Creamery chèvre, or hearth-grilled 100% American Wagyu, you’ll experience world-class cuisine crafted with a Ranch twist by our Executive Chef. Guided by our seed-to-table philosophy, we honor the land in everything we craft, from fruits and vegetables grown in our Greenhouse to the grains used to distill our one-of-a-kind spirits at Brush Creek Distillery. When staying with us, you’ll enjoy three gourmet dining experiences a day and have access to select beer, wine, and spirits. \*Must be 21+ years of age to consume alcohol.

Rustic Ranch Flare

## Trailhead Lodge

A tribute to the sweeping beauty and storied heritage of the American West, Trailhead Lodge offers a setting as inspiring as its cuisine. Panoramic views of the Platte River Valley’s mountains, open meadows, and grazing horses create a breathtaking backdrop for the morning meal. Inside, a warm and welcoming atmosphere unfolds around crackling fireplaces, stately wooden beams, and antler chandeliers, complemented by attentive service. Guests can savor refined interpretations of traditional breakfast favorites alongside dishes crafted with creativity, each thoughtfully designed to highlight the region’s distinctive flavors and ingredients.

_\*All-inclusive daily breakfast_

Rooted in Freshness & Innovation

## Pioneer Kitchen

Founded on the bounty of our region and farm-sourced ingredients, cuisine at Pioneer Kitchen is inspired by West Coast culinary traditions of sustainability, seasonality, and hyper-local ingredients. Sometimes rustic and rugged like the terrain, sometimes refined and elegant, dishes served here blaze the trail for a new expression of what the region has to offer in terms of natural resources and culinary expression. Soft, neutral-toned furnishings and thoughtfully arranged seating lend a sense of calm refinement, while abundant greenery brings the essence of the outdoors inside. The open kitchen adds a lively, engaging energy as culinary teams work in full view, illuminated by warm lighting that highlights natural wood textures and subtle Western detailing.

_\*All-inclusive lunch and dinner._

Fine-Dining at The Farm

## Cheyenne Club

Indulge in fine‑dining, pasture‑to‑plate cuisine sourced directly from the ranch’s expansive agricultural initiatives, including produce from the 20,000‑square‑foot greenhouse, artisanal cheeses from the on‑site creamery, and highly sought‑after ranch‑raised Wagyu beef, expertly transformed into inventive seasonal menus by a chef‑driven culinary team and served within one of the most elevated dining atmospheres in the West. Tour one the world’s 10 largest, and _Wine Spectator_ award-winning wine collections to select the perfect pairing for your gourmet dinner, learn about our 100% American Wagyu cattle program, delight in craft cocktails utilizing spirits distilled onsite, and end your evening behind the secret doors of The Spirit Vault, encasing exceptionally-rare whiskeys and bourbons.

_\*All-inclusive Tuesday, Thursday, & Saturday Dinners._

A Historical Tribute

## The Saloon

Our Saloon is a cultural icon, much like the western saloons of the old American West. It’s a place where the good times are always rolling. From the minute you walk in through our swinging doors, you’re met with an energizing atmosphere full of sounds from games like pool and shuffleboard, and maybe even an occasional live performance. We’re ready to serve you your preferred beverage of choice, whether it’s a delicious cocktail, or select spirits and wine from our fully-stocked bar. Kick up your feet on the deck nightly at Social Hour, sip your drink, and soak up the sights of gorgeous sunsets and rolling meadows.

_\*All-inclusive with upgraded à la carte options._

Surrounded by Natural Beauty

## Creekside Camp

Sit along the banks of Brush Creek and enjoy a peaceful meal outdoors among the nature you seek. Let the little ones run free and make memories during roping lessons, hatchet throwing, and bb gun range. Listen to live tunes and kick up your boots with a drink in hand. Gather ’round for expertly prepared open-fire meals under wide-open Wyoming skies.

_\*Summer Only (mid-June through Labor Day). All-inclusive Monday and Friday dinners._

Open-Air Private Dining

## U-Line Garden

Set within a serene outdoor haven that comes alive in the summer months, this private venue welcomes up to 20 guests for an unforgettable open‑air dining experience. Evenings begin and end with drinks on the spacious reception lawn, where towering evergreens frame sweeping mountain views and lush garden pathways. As lanterns glow and the Wyoming sun sinks behind the peaks, guests gather beneath the open‑air pavilion to enjoy a gourmet, chef‑prepared feast featuring ranch‑raised Wagyu beef, freshly harvested produce, and vibrant seasonal dishes served family‑style.

_\*À la carte private dining venue available in the summer._
