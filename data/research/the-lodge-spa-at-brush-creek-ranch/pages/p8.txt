# Magee Homestead

The pinnacle of sophisticated western luxury, Magee Homestead is a private guest ranch available for exclusive resort buyouts for families, friends, and corporate groups seeking a completely private Western getaway. This Forbes Recommended, award-winning property is available for complete buyouts, allowing groups of up to 27 guests to enjoy sole access to the Homestead's private cabins, amenities, all-inclusive dining, pool, spa, and all-inclusive guided outdoor activities. With the entire property reserved exclusively for your group, guests can create a fully customized retreat, family gathering, milestone celebration, or executive getaway while enjoying unparalleled privacy, personalized service, and the serene beauty of Wyoming's North Platte River Valley.

## A Romantic Hideaway

A reprieve from your everyday busy life, Magee Homestead is an adults-only property within the Brush Creek Luxury Ranch Collection in south-central Wyoming. Find your sense of renewal nestled between the ancient Sierra Madre mountain range and stunning Medicine Bow National Forest, just minutes from the remote town of Saratoga. Guests can fly by private air into Saratoga Jet Center, only 11 miles from our front gates. Scenic views are enjoyed along the I-80 and WY Hwy 130 driving approximately one hour from the Laramie Regional Airport or three and a half hours from Denver International Airport.
