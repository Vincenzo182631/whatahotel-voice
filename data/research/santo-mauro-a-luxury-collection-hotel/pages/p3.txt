# Succulent Cuisine and Cocktails in Downtown Madrid

## Dining

The traditional gastronomy of Madrid is included in Spanish cuisine in general, and in particular Castilian gastronomy. Some of the most characteristic preparations are the cocido madrileño, Madrid-style...See More tripe (callos a la madrileña), garlic soup, and desserts such as rosquillas tontas y listas, huesos de santo, the churros (prepared in the churrerías), the bocadillos de calamares, the patatas bravas, the chopitos, etc. The restaurants and cocktail bars in Madrid and restaurants give off this scent through the streets at almost any time of the day. According to some authors, Madrid's cuisine is a combination of fine dining restaurants, bars, and diners, of dishes prepared quickly and showing flavors accompanied by wines from the region. Many of the dishes that have the denomination "a la madrileña" originate in the bars and taverns of Madrid. Today, you can see foods from other cultures installed in numerous streets, making the variety richer and opening the doors to exciting cuisine.See Less

## Gresca de Santo Mauro

The Gresca Wine Bar is set to become the meeting point for the city's most discerning gourmets. Undoubtedly, its extensive cellar of more than 250 references stands out, betting on national and international wines, as well as natural wines, from small producers and special vintages. To accompany, it has a magnificent offer of dishes to share, based on small bites that pair perfectly with the winery's proposal. At Gresca Wine Bar we bet on seasonal products, with recognizable flavors. Enjoy a superb selection of cocktails and our daily epicurean moment of the hotel, the Casilda cocktail service and taste the most well-known and acclaimed dishes of Rafa Peña's restaurant in Barcelona, such as his trompetas de la muerte sandwich with egg yolk, which is already making Madrid diners fall in love.

## El Jardín de Santo Mauro

The Santo Mauro garden offers the choice between an exquisite formal meal or the range of imaginative and innovative snacks served in the lounge. In the halls of Santo Mauro and in the garden, Gresca offers its most casual version. An informal proposal ideal for an aperitif, after work or nights out thanks to its non-stop cuisine, with uninterrupted hours from 1:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m. All this in a relaxed atmosphere and a vibrant musical accompaniment thanks to the work of composer Lucas Vidal. An environment conducive to having a wine, from the extensive cellar of organic and natural wines selected by Rafa Peña, accompanied by the famous Gresca bikini, the restaurant's house brand. The landscape gardener Fernando Valero has once again made a centennial garden shine, updated with indigenous plants. All this forming an essential set in the architecture and soul of the center of Madrid.

### El Desayuno Tardío - A Moment to Savor

For many, day of rest is not just a day of indulgence but also the high point of their week, a spirit we capture every Sunday as guests and locals gather at Palacio Santo Mauro to enjoy brunch in the sun-soaked garden or Duke's library. Blending a late Spanish breakfast with the local hora de vermut tradition, guests can sample traditional Madrileño flavors.

### A Taste of Past and Present - Rafa Peña

Chef Rafa Peña studied classical cuisine in Paris and Scandinavian cuisine in Stockholm, and has now created a contemporary, creative, simple, seasonal style of cuisine that is also influenced by his travel experiences. He is passionate about wine, which is one of the main pillars of his cuisine, especially natural wine. He is currently chef at Gresca restaurant in Barcelona.
