# Feel at Home in an Elegant Hotel Suite in Madrid

## Accommodations

The rooms at Santo Mauro Hotel have been designed to make your stay in our hotel suites an unforgettable refined luxury experience in the heart of Madrid. Curl up in your bed and experience a deep, restful...See More sleep. All the rooms and suites are designed with high-quality finishes, marble bathrooms, luxury bedding, and high-speed Wi-Fi, integrating the design of its historic 5-star hotel building where luxury, art, and design meet together.See Less

## Luxurious Plush Bedding

Perfectly plumped pillows are your bed's must-have accessories to ease you into a superior slumber. Reflecting our commitment to ensuring guest wake up feeling recharged, relaxed, refreshed and rejuvenated.

The Luxury of Living in a Palace

### The Design and the Essence are Very Important for Us

We were very thoughtful in design to maintain and renovate our rooms. Lorenzo Castillo thoughtfully designed some of the most luxury rooms in Santo Mauro hotel. The Suite Royal The Duke's , was the original Duke's bedroom. The walls are all covered with a marvellous green velvet and the ceilings with lamps that are relics. Our cozy Luxury Suite he decoration is a subtle mix of precious Indian style...See More design and stunning bespoke fabrics which offer comfort as a peaceful retreat that inspires the luxury of living in a palace.See Less

### Turn Down Service

When you return to your room in the evening you'll find a warm light, a turned-down bed, a bedside mat with your sleepers ready & local chocolates - everything to make your night more comfortable.

### La Pajarita

La Pajarita is one of the oldest and most traditional confectionary stores in Madrid that has been run by the same family since the eighteenth century and still uses the same artisanal process to make their inimitable candied violets & special sweets.

### Selected Local Products

The minibar contains some authentic examples of the gastronomy of Madrid: a local wine selected by our sommelier, delicious indigenous pastries and traditional Spanish crisps are just some of the treats to enjoy.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid?

The largest occupancy room at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid is the The Duke´s Royal Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid?

Some rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have fridges?

No, rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have in-room whirlpools?
