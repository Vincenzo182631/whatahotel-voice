# Welcome to Santo Mauro, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid

## Madrid Nobility With a Disruptive Edge

The Hotel Santo Mauro invites you on a journey in Madrid in the 19th century, at the home of the influential Duke of Santo Mauro. With its French-influenced design and architecture, our 5-star, historic,...See More boutique hotel in the city center consists of three buildings set in a sophisticated private garden, with fountains and terraces shaded by ancient chestnut trees. The 49 guest rooms and suites are full of special details and historical references that immerse you in the past. Each public room is unique: The Red Room reflects pure French Classicism, while the oriental pieces in the Chinese Lounge recall the objects the Duke used to collect on his travels. The Wine Bar offers an extensive list of wines and small dishes, while the main restaurant, housed in the Duke's former Library, offers a level of cuisine commensurate with its palatial setting. Music, fresh flowers, arts and crafts, and highly attentive service from the household staff round off the unique experience of staying in a palace.See Less

## A Jewel in Madrid's Crown

The name Santo Mauro comes from a principality in Sicily, and the title "Príncipe de Santo Mauro de Nápoles" was first granted in 1705 by Philip V. In 1890, Mariano Fernández de Henestrosa was bestowed the title of Duke of Santo Mauro as a reward for his services to the crown. The ducal family still retains ownership of the palace, acting as its stewards.

## The Palace of Santo Mauro

The Palace of Santo Mauro was built by architect Juan Bautista Lázaro between 1889 and 1902 as a residence for the Duke of Santo Mauro. As a French-style palace, the building is unique because it does not conform to the traditionalist style that was popular in this region during that time. The palace's main courtyard is curvilinear and was originally accessed by carriages, where the gorgeous iron and glass marquee at the entryway helped to protect the duke and his family on rainy days. The hotel retains its original interior design, boasting unique details such as the Modernist staircase in the entrance hall, a small oval courtyard, an old chapel, and the Duke's library.

### Feel Right at Home in a Palace

Not many hotels can boast of occupying such a unique space that has survived so many different periods in Spanish history, that has shared its cuisine, its gardens and its lounges with ambassadors and intellectuals. And which still today retains its considerable appeal and fascination. In addition, since the hotel stands in the capital of a country that enjoys such international culinary acclaim as Spain, it opens up a world of experiences for guests to discover the recipes and ingredients of every region.

## Best Location

The palace is located in the neighborhood known as the Golden Triangle, which for centuries was the center of aristocratic life in Madrid and has been a popular location for aristocratic residences, embassies, and high-end art galleries.

### Enchantment in the Garden

An oasis in the center of a city, located in the neighborhood known as the Golden Triangle, Santo Mauro's lush garden offers the perfect backdrop for romantic moments, group gatherings, and fine dining. Here our staff can personalize special moments depending on our guest's desires, giving them the luxury to escape into greenery while still having every amenity at hand.

### Wine and Music

Crafting an evening and atmosphere of enjoyment, accompanied by music and hosted in an exclusive space, the experience is designed to please all of the senses. The Wine Bar is set to become the meeting point for the city's most discerning gourmets. Enjoy a superb selection of wines and cocktails or indulge in the creative 'small plates' designed to accompany your drink.

### Feel Right at Home

A palace is more than a place, it is a legacy. You will feel as if you are in an art gallery thanks to its period furniture and marble. All kinds of hand-crafted amenities help to make your stay a genuinely local experience. Beyond the conventional standards of luxury with a combination of Modernism and French Classicism.

### A Fresh Escape

Possibly the loveliest garden in Madrid. The garden is the jewel in the Palace's crown, offering a lush oasis in a privileged position at the heart of the city. Under the guidance of landscape artist Fernando Valero the natural beauty of this space, complemented by its striking fountains and historic trees, invites relaxation, reflection, and reinvigoration.
