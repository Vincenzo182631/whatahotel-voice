## Experiences

Madrid has become one of the most visited cities in Europe. A large number of tourist, recreational and cultural activities take place in the city, close to our hotel in Chamberi neighbourhood. It is one...See More of the European cities with the highest proportion of green areas per inhabitant, and with nearly 300,000 trees, it is the second city in the world in number of these in the streets and walks, only surpassed by Tokyo. This magical city is an influential cultural center and has museums of international reference, including the so-called Triangle of Art (the Prado Museum, the Reina Sofía National Art Center Museum, the Thyssen-Bornemisza) and CaixaForum Madrid, which rank among the most visited museums in the world. In 2021, the Paseo del Prado and Buen Retiro, landscape of the arts and sciences, was included in the list of World Heritage Sites by UNESCO. Madrid is also a national leader in terms of fairs and exhibitions.See Less

### Experience Epicurean Elegance at Santo Mauro

### Casilda Cocktail: Tradition & Delight

Crafted in honor of Jasmina de Casilda, our signature cocktail is more than a drink—it’s an experience. Inspired by Madrid’s magnificent gardens and rich heritage, it blends chestnut syrup, vodka, Campari, and fresh orange and lemon juice for a perfectly balanced, unforgettable taste. Served complimentary daily from 6 to 8 p.m., inviting in-house guests to unwind and savor timeless elegance.

### Torrija Masterclass: Spain’s Sweet Tradition

Discover Spain’s rich culinary heritage in our Torrija Masterclass, held every Tuesday at 5 p.m. in La Biblioteca. Led by our executive chef, learn the history and techniques behind this dessert—from soaking bread in infused milk to perfect caramelization. Enjoy curated pairings and stories connecting each bite to Spanish tradition. Price: €45 per person. Reservations are required and subject to availability.

### Discover the Essence of Chamberi Neighborhood

Immerse yourself in Madrid’s elegant Chamberí with our exclusive walking tour. Stroll charming streets, intimate plazas, and refined architecture while uncovering the stories behind this captivating district. This walk reveals hidden gems and rich heritage. Reservations required, subject to availability.

### Santo Mauro: The Definition of Madrid

Our vision for the Hotel Santo Mauro is for it to become a benchmark for cosmopolitan explorers who value the distinctive personality of local architecture and appreciate the opportunity to truly inhabit and experience the history of the cities they visit. This type of guest expects a level of sophistication and service commensurate with what they might find in a palace - a place where they can immerse...See More themselves in tradition while still enjoying an artful and contemporary twist. Both dynamic and discerning, they seek out an atmosphere that lets them feel completely at home, rather than like a visitor who is only passing through.See Less

### Madrid - A World Within a City

Madrid enjoys an exclusive range of cultural, gastronomic, historical, and commercial attractions that make it a popular destination within Europe as well as a major gateway between Latin America and the Continent. With its warm and distinctive atmosphere, travelers can always find an element of themselves reflected in the spirit of the city. Beyond serving as the symbolic heart of the country, Madrid’s geographic location at the center of Spain extends the possibilities it offers, making it a hub for travelers interested in experiencing other landmarks of major interest.

## A Fascinating Experience of Aristocratic Life in Madrid

In the past, Madrid’s aristocrats were among the privileged few who could travel the world, cultivating not just their intellect and social connections but also their collections of exotic valuables, which they used to decorate their homes, express their unique interests, and entertain their guests. The Santo Mauro keeps that experience alive with its cabinet of curiosities.

## A Journey Through One of Madrid's Most Striking Districts

Exploring the district that much of Madrid's aristocracy made their home during the 19th and 20th centuries, our carefully curated tour of Chamberi shares highlights and hidden gems that are all within 20 min walking from the hotel and are spread across the neighborhoods.

### Spain - A Country Without Compare

Spain has a reputation of being one of the world’s primary tourist destinations. No other country can compare to the richness and diversity of its attractions: Spain has the most Blue Flag beaches in the Northern Hemisphere, is the 2nd country in the world with the most World Heritage Cities and 3rd in the number of natural areas that have been declared Biosphere Reserves. Its long sun-filled days, rich local culture and diverse landscape, country’s architecture, gastronomy, music, traditions, people, art, and historic sites, offer endless opportunities to immerse in its authentic experiences.
