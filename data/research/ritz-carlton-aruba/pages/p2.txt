Ocean-View Hotel Suites in Aruba
Suites & Rooms
At The Ritz-Carlton, Aruba, the island's relaxed charm is captured by suites featuring colorful, island-inspired décor. All suites offer a private balcony with ocean views. With spacious 2-bedroom suites, our hotel is ideal for family getaways in Aruba. In the Club Lounge, enjoy classic amenities designed to enhance your stay.
Island-Inspired Room Design
A debut of refurbished suites and guest rooms pays homage to the hotel’s unmatched location along Aruba’s acclaimed Palm Beach, with locally inspired design motifs by Aruba’s beaches, sea, and desert landscapes.

Explore the Ritz-Carlton Club® Level
Perched on the seventh floor, Club® Level guests are invited to enjoy beautiful ocean views in a comfortable lounge. Views from the Club Lounge and Club® Level guest rooms feature stunning seascapes of the Caribbean Sea and Aruba’s famed sunsets. Toast the sun's descent with a glass of champagne on your personal balcony or join us in the Club Lounge to view an unforgettable performance of day becoming night.
Savory Food & Beverage Presentations
Club® Level
Soaring above the sea and secluded from the rest of the hotel, The Ritz-Carlton Club Level offers an elevated experience reserved for guests staying in Club Level rooms and suites. Open daily from 7:00 a.m. to 10 p.m., the Club Lounge boasts an endless array of culinary delights and beverage offerings throughout the day. Enjoy five daily rotating food presentations - breakfast, lunch, mid-day snacks, hors d’oeuvres, and desserts and cordials – created in-house by our talented chefs. A superb selection of still and sparkling wines, liqueurs, beers, spirits, and non-alcoholic beverages are available to accompany the meals or to celebrate the day. A dedicated Club Concierge team is available to assist guests with airport transfers, dining and spa reservations, sightseeing suggestions, and any other special requests.

Delightful Treats
Glass candy jars are always full of sweet treats and house-made cookies. Children and adults alike will enjoy our ultimate assorted cookies and candy display, featuring delectable cookie and chocolate selections, butterscotch, mints, and caramel candies.
Golden Hour
Take in the beauty of Aruba's sunsets with the best views from the Club Lounge accompanied by complimentary cocktails and wine.
A Taste of Local Treats
Guests of the Club Lounge can enjoy a variety of delicious and authentic local delicacies in an exclusive tasting presentation. From Aruba’s famous ‘Pastechi’, a must-try when visiting the island and sweets like coconut rum cake and handcrafted local peanut brittle and coconut candies.
Very Important Pooch
At The Ritz-Carlton, Aruba every member of your family deserves the utmost in luxury and comfort, and that includes your four-legged friend. Some of the services and amenities provided are a dedicated Pet Concierge, elevated in-room amenities such as a dog sofa, bowls, treats, disposable waste bags and size-appropriate dog toys. We also offer complimentary shared dog cabanas at the pool and beach area and our Signature Store now offers a curated selection of dog products. For guests looking for more information, please connect with the Pet Concierge at aruba.petconcierge@ritzcarlton.com

Pet Policy
The Ritz-Carlton, Aruba allows one dog per room throughout their stay. There is a $250 USD non-refundable Dog Stay Fee per stay. An additional Deep Cleaning Fee applies based on the room type category.
Dogs must be leashed and accompanied by their owner or carried in all common areas of the hotel.
Dogs are not allowed in public areas where food and beverages is served or consumed, in the swimming pools, fitness center, spa facilities, and Club Lounge, with the exception of certified service dogs.
Signature Suites
Peched atop the most coveted address in Aruba, our luxurious Ritz-Carlton Suites offer sweeping views of the turquoise Caribbean Sea and the island's pristine coastline. Designed with refined elegance and curated finishes, this private sanctuary blends tropicla serenity with world-class sophistication.
