Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Aruba
A Season of Enchantment
The most cherished holiday memories are often born not from grandeur, but from the quiet beauty of each day. This season, indulge in a constellation of curated experiences inspired by the enduring legacy of The Ritz-Carlton, Aruba and the serenity of island skies. From opulent buffet soirées to intimate family moments, each day invites you to pause, reconnect, and revel in the art of living amidst paradise.
New Year’s Eve Feast
Step into a world of opulence as we transform the evening into a grand celebration inspired by romance and mystique. Indulge in a lavish dinner buffet curated to delight the senses, sip on fine wines and sparkling cocktails, and dance beneath the shimmer of lights.

Signature Experiences
Art of the Cocktail
Elevate your evening with our refined Mixology Class, where expert bartenders guide you through the craft of creating sophisticated, island-inspired cocktails. Noori Sushi Lounge | Tuesdays, Thursdays & Saturdays | $100 per person or $80 per person for groups of 5 or more.
Sushi Masterclass
Discover the art of sushi-making in an elegant, hands-on culinary experience. Guided by our expert chefs, learn to roll, slice, and plate exquisite creations—perfectly paired with island ambiance and refined flavors. Noori Sushi Lounge | Mondays, Wednesdays & Fridays | $100 per person or $80 per person for groups of 5 or more.
Live Vocal Evenings
Unwind to the soulful sounds of live singing performances, where talented vocalists set the perfect mood with elegant melodies and island charm. A refined night of music awaits. Firepit | Fridays | 7PM
Holiday Spa Retreat
This festive season, give yourself the gift of restoration. Step into serenity and emerge refreshed, balanced, and renewed.
