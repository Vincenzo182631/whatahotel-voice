The Mystery of the Islands Is Revealed at This Caribbean Spa
Spa
Within a 15,000-sq. ft. beachfront sanctuary, The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Aruba offers healing and relaxation with a distinctly local touch. Aloe, coffee and other indigenous ingredients are showcased in signature treatments and paired with science-driven skincare from ESPA and Eminence for results-oriented facials. Relaxation lounges include whirlpools, saunas and steam rooms, and an oxygen bar helps reduce stress and boost energy. The resort’s luxury spa is ideal for couples or bridal parties and includes 12 treatment rooms, a hair and nail salon and a boutique.

Prior to Arrival
When should I arrive?
Please arrive at least 30 minutes prior to your scheduled treatment. This will allow time to relax in the lounges, steam rooms, whirlpools, and relaxation room.
Arriving late will simply limit the time of your treatment thus lessening its
effectiveness and your enjoyment. Your treatment time will not be extended as we cannot delay the next guest and the full value of your treatment will be charged.
What about my jewelry and valuables?
If you are staying in the hotel, please leave valuable items in the safe in your guestroom. Otherwise, lockers are available for your personal items in the Spa. We are not responsible for any personal items and/or valuables brought into the Spa or left in your locker.
Use of cell phones, electronic devices, cameras
To protect the privacy of our Guests and to preserve the Guest experience, use of cell phones, electronic devices and cameras is prohibited in the Spa. Cell phones and electronic devices must be turned off.
Spa Policies
What is the cancellation policy?
If you must reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify the Spa at least six hours in advance of your appointment to avoid being charged the full service fee. Any appointments rescheduled within the six-hour window will incur a move fee of $25. Cancellation of packages or private parties require one week’s notice. Groups are subject to a four-week cancellation policy.
What about gratuities?
For your convenience, a 22% service charge will be added to each spa service you receive. You may opt to leave additional gratuity at your discretion.
What about payment for spa services?
We accept all major credit cards.
Is there a minimum age for treatments?
Guests must be 18 years and older to enjoy the Spa relaxation areas, locker areas and soaking tubs. Massages cannot be performed on anyone under the age of 18 years.
Salon services are available for children accompanied by a parent for the entire service.
What if I am late for my appointment?
Arriving late will simply limit the time for your treatment, thus lessening its
effectiveness and your enjoyment. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest will not be delayed. The full value of your treatment will be charged.
Health
What if I am pregnant?
While there are a few services that should be avoided during pregnancy, there are many beneficial treatments that can be enjoyed. Please notify our spa agents if you are pregnant when you make your appointments so that they can offer guidance.
What if I have special health considerations?
Please notify our spa reservation agent before booking your treatments if you have high blood pressure, allergies, any other physical ailments or disabilities, or if you are pregnant. If you have any concerns, please let us know. If at any time during your services you experience discomfort, please alert your therapist immediately.
Additional Information
Who can guide me in choosing my treatments?
Our spa reservation agents can help you plan the perfect spa experience, including the best order for your treatments. Ask about a spa consultation.
Parking
Complimentary parking for Spa Guests is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Aruba.
Please be sure to check-out at the spa front desk before you depart.
Food and Beverage
Guests are prohibited from bringing any food or alcohol into the Spa unless provided by the Spa. No glass is permitted at the soaking tub or locker areas.
