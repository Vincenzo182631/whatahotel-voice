The Ritz-Carlton, Aruba
From the jaw-dropping spa to the impeccable service, the Ritz-Carlton Aruba is where to go if you're looking to get spoiled.

Why book?
Set on one of the best stretches of beach on the island, the Ritz-Carlton is the place to go when you're looking for luxurious accommodations, high-end service, and an amazing spa.
Set the scene
The property is located at the far end of the high-rise district, adjacent to Hadicurari Beach. Expect to see newlyweds, young families, and longtime visitors to the island. Here, you’ll overhear everything from models booking international gigs to lawyers chatting about arbitration when you’re poolside. The vibe is upscale without being stuffy, with guests in the elevators side by side wearing pool towels and Prada.
The backstory
The property opened in November 2013 and was the first-ever Ritz-Carlton resort in the Dutch Caribbean. It was the island’s first true five-star resort.
The rooms
The 320 rooms and suites are spacious and chic with a classy Caribbean vibe, and outfitted with basket weaves, upscale rattan, and dark wood. The oceanfront rooms offer some of the best views on the island—front row to endless vistas of Aruba’s famous turquoise water.
Food and drink
The property has several on-site restaurants serving lunch and dinner throughout the day, as well as around-the-clock room service. The pastry team at the property steals the show, baking up some of the best breads on the island. BLT Steakhouse is an award-winning steakhouse on the ground floor; the complimentary popovers served are worth a trip alone. Be sure to make a reservation, or if you’re feeling spontaneous, grab a seat at the bar. Guests can either visit the Madero Pool & Beach Grill for lunch or order from the menu directly to their chairs and cabanas. Expect well-made favorites, including mahi mahi tacos so good I ordered them three days in a row. You can’t miss the property's poolside pop-up bar that’s set up shop in a renovated VW bus, mixing up cocktails in decked-out coconuts and pineapples with towering tropical garnishes. Unlike a lot of other drinks that beg to be Instagrammed, these cocktails aren’t just photogenic—they taste fantastic. If you’re on island time and want a slow start to the day, enjoy the room service. Be sure to try the pastechi, a classic Aruban dish similar to an empanada.
The spa
The 15,000-square-foot spa at the Ritz is one of the largest and best on the island, featuring saunas, steam rooms, and large outdoor whirlpools available to all guests throughout their stay. The spa showers are also complimented by several salt scrubs, which shouldn’t be missed. After your treatment, be sure to order the mignardises—mine featured an aloe cookie crumble and a lavender macaron.
The neighborhood/area
The resort is located on the outskirts of the high-rise hotel district on Palm Beach, directly next to Hadicurari Beach where you can spot kite surfers taking advantage of Aruba’s signature winds. It’s an easy walk to other properties in the area via the beach boardwalk. It’s also the closest large property to the island’s California Lighthouse.
The service
This resort is where you come to be truly spoiled. Servers cruise around the shore on Segway scooters, effortlessly balancing trays of tropical cocktails. Guests lounging by the pool are treated to surprises throughout the day, including complimentary ice pops. The concierge can secure reservations for you at sold-out restaurants, set up offsite tours, and suggest itineraries for any kind of traveler. The pool and beach servers greet you by name with a fist bump and a genuine smile.
For families
The resort is a fantastic pick for families, with two swimming pools—including a dedicated adults-only option—and water activities like snorkeling, kayaking, sailing, and paddleboarding. The property also has an on-site kids club, the Ritz Kids, with offerings that keep children entertained while they get to know the local culture and biodiversity of the island. The supervised program offers activities for children ages 4 through 12 based on water, land, environmental responsibility, and culture.
Eco effort
The property has implemented LED and energy-efficient lighting throughout the hotel. You'll find native plants in the property's landscape, great for reducing water consumption. The hotel has also eliminated the use of styrofoam and plastic straws, using recyclable cups at the pool and beach. Plans are on the horizon for the installation of a container for hydroponic gardening and to start a composting program.
Accessibility
The hotel offers accessible rooms and common spaces and has an elevator for easy use. While it can be difficult to navigate the sand with limited mobility, past guests have reported being offered swift assistance when arriving on the beach.
Anything left to mention?
The old adage of getting what you pay for rings true here, and while it may be a splurge, it will be an unforgettable stay. The rooms are quiet, the beds are comfortable, and in my opinion, this is the best stretch of beach on the island. If you’re looking for luxury—and budget isn’t an issue—the Ritz is the top of the line. From the fresh, cold hand towels offered at the beach cabana to the complimentary bottles of sunscreen in a wide range of SPF available for use, The Ritz has you covered.
