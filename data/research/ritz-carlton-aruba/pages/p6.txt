Experiences
Discover the beauty of Aruba at our resort, where modern luxury meets the island's inspiring natural surroundings. Immerse yourself in the turquoise Caribbean Sea with snorkeling, sailing, and kayaking adventures, or relax by our beachfront pools in a luxury cabana at our Aruba hotel with fitness center.
Enjoy Luxury Vacation Experiences
Indulge in your every wish inspired by the azure waters of the Caribbean. From its spectacular Palm Beach location, The Ritz-Carlton, Aruba offers endless resort and recreational activities to rejuvenate and relax while the sun shines. Whether you prefer relaxing in a beachfront cabana or more active pursuits like snorkeling, paddle boarding, pedal boating, and kayaking, there are plenty of ways to make your Aruba experience simply unforgettable.

Ritz Kids Programs Are Offered Seasonally
Ritz Kids
As Jean-Michel Cousteau says, “All kids start out as scientists, constantly asking ‘Why? What’s that? How does it work?’” Our resort’s experiences tap into that innate curiosity by introducing our youngest guests to both the natural wonders and the rich cultural traditions of our family resort’s barrier island location. Immersive and adventurous, the experiences provide your child with enthralling activities during your family vacation. With a diverse collection of activities based on the four pillars of the program – water, land, environmental responsibility and culture – kids are swept away by everything from sand dunes and ancient forests, to recycling and sustainability, storytelling, art and even food.
Ritz Kids introduces our young guests to the natural wonders of the island through a variety of hands-on adventures. Designed for children ages 4 through 12, the supervised program entertains kids with educational games and activities featuring different themes throughout the week.

Activity Guide
Create, Learn & Play
– This program offers a unique opportunity to get to know a little bit more about Aruba and its history, culture, language and animals through fun and interactive activities. Come and paint with all the colors of the wind and soar with us on this journey to uncover all this island has to offer.
Something Fishy
– Learn about some of our favorite underwater friends – fish! We dig into what makes a fish a fish and why fish are shaped and colored the way that they are. Become a fish detective as we play games to discover how much we can learn about a fish just by looking at it, as well as what makes fish so different from you and me.
Pajama & Storytelling: Myths & Legends
– This activity offers a chance to discover how and why myths came to be and look at inspiring legends from around the world. Investigate what is a hero and how not all heroes are legends of old. They still exist today! Who are some heroes in your life? Create your own story to share and inspire.
Sharks
–
Whether you are fascinated by or scared of the world of sharks, this activity is for you. Through a variety of games, activities and videos, we learn about different kinds of sharks, why sharks are so misunderstood and how sharks are important to us and to the ocean.
More about Iguanas
– Did you know that Aruba’s native lizard is called the “Aruban Whiptail”? This unique activity offers our young guests a cool and interactive way to learn more about Aruba’s beautiful Iguanas & Lizards.
Coral Reefs
– Ever wonder what is a Coral Reef? Is it a plant or an animal or both? Through games, Multimedia, and crafts, explore the magical ecosystem known as the “City under the water “.
Shell & Tell
– Take a walk with us along the shore of the beautiful Palm Beach to collect shells and sand and create your one-of-a-kind art piece that you can take with you home.
Rock the Backpack
– Join us to bedazzle and paint your own backpack with vibrant colors and glitter.
Pajama Party
– Whether you want to join us with your cool dinosaur pajama onesie or your princess puff dress or regular clothes, we have a night full of games and activities planned! Sit back and relax while we have dinner together and then continue our journey of F.U.N.
Under the Sea
– Did you know that around Oceans cover about 70% of the planet's surface and hold about 96.5% of all Earth's water. We will learn about the ocean and the animals that live there as well as creating cool art.
Fun with Waffles
– Who said that eating breakfast couldn’t be fun? We have all the fun toppings for your waffles from strawberries to whip cream to chocolate chips and many more to indulge your taste buds!
*Complimentary family activity. Guardians are required to accompany children under 18.
