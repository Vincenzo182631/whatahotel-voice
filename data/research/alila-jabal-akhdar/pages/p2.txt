# Jabal Akhdar resort \| Alila Jabal Akhdar

# Accommodations

Check-In2:00 PM

Checkout12:00 PM

At 2,000 meters above sea level, Alila Jabal Akhdar offers 86 suites and villas inspired by Oman’s ancient forts. Built from mountain stone and adorned with hand-painted juniper leaves by local artists, each space blends heritage, sustainability, and modern elegance—offering seclusion, serenity, and breathtaking views across the Al Hajar Mountains.
