# Jabal Akhdar Hotel \| Alila Jabal Akhdar

## Breathtaking Skies, Landscapes and History in Oman’s Mountains

Discover the gateway to the Al Hajar Mountains, Oman’s most stunning range. Perched more than 6,500 feet above sea level, this luxury resort sits amid an alpine landscape dominated by Jabal Akhdar itself, which translates to “Green Mountain” in Arabic. Clear skies in a majestic setting make stargazing a must, while farther down the mountain, history and Nizwa Souq await your exploration. Back at our desert sanctuary, take a refreshing dip in our infinity pool and indulge in an array of relaxing and rejuvenating treatments at our heavenly spa.

## Discover Alila Jabal Akhdar

A Taste of Oman

Savor regional influences and old-world traditions. Jabal Akhdar, meaning the Green Mountain in Arabic, is considered the fruit bowl of Oman, where pomegranates, apricots and roses grow in abundance in its Mediterranean-like climate.

Committed to a Sustainable Future

From collecting rain to water plants to using reverse osmosis for our drinking water, incorporating green initiatives into every aspect of the Alila experience is a core focus of our operations. As an Earth Check-certified property, our dedication is not only to minimizing our impact but also to maximizing our preservation efforts.

Old World Modernity

Historic influences meet contemporary design at this resort in the heart of the majestic Al Hajar Mountains. Alila Jabal Akhdar takes inspiration from the ancient forts in the region using traditional building techniques, fort-like doors, wooden rafters and Omani design motifs for spaces that are authentic to place and time. Locally sourced materials and classic-style stonework combine with contemporary architecture to create a truly unique setting where a sense of connection to nature resounds.

A Gateway to Oman

Located in one of the most remote corners of Oman’s northern Al Hajar mountain range, Alila Jabal Akhdar is perfectly camouflaged into its clifftop setting just an hour outside the ancient city of Nizwa. With breathtaking vistas of the surrounding desert landscape and dramatic ravines 2,000 metres above sea level, this secluded oasis nestled high in the mountains is perfectly situated to showcase the area’s spectacular natural beauty.

A Portal to the Past

Alila Jabal Akhdar offers a gateway to the area’s incredible history, culture and nature. A draw for adventure enthusiasts and history and culture devotees alike, visitors are spoiled for choice in this treasure trove for rich experiences. At Alila Jabal Akhdar, you are well placed to explore one of the most fascinating regions in the world, engage in an endless variety of activities, and experience the warm hospitality of the Omani people.
