Transportation Options

A range of seamless transport options is available to ensure your journey to and from Alila Jabal Akhdar is effortless and comfortable. From airport pickups and city transfers to scenic tours and curated connections from key landmarks like Birkat Al Mouz and the desert camps, our team is here to tailor every detail to you. Group transfers can also be arranged for coordinated arrivals. All transportation services are chargeable—please contact us at jabalakhdar@alilahotels.com to curate your personalized journey.
