### A true hidden gem in the mountains

Alila Jabal Akhdar was one of the best stays we’ve ever had. The stargazing was a highlight, and our two guides knew an incredible amount about the night sky. Via Ferrata was a blast with a super friendly guide. Every room has a stunning view, so you can’t go wrong, and the property is spotless and looks brand new. The food, much of it grown in their own garden, was some of the best we’ve ever had. Staff remembered our names, constantly checked in, and jumped to help with every little thing.
