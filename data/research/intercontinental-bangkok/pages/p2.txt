# InterContinental BangkokRooms & suites

Overlooking the vibrant city, InterContinental Bangkok's 381 newly renovated luxury rooms and suites begin on Level 17, all offering stunning views of the Bangkok skyline. The light-filled accommodations feature pastel-hued silk embroidered bedheads and custom-made furniture throughout. Exquisite wooden floors and textured carpets offer both hygiene and comfort, whilst bathrooms feature deep soaking tubs and separate rain showers for ultimate relaxation. Handcrafted artwork showcases Thai floral welcoming traditions, with floating flowers also a prominent design feature in the accommodations.

All rooms and suites offer high-speed Internet access as well as flat panel Chromecast televisions and Bluetooth sound bars. The beds are fitted with Posturepedic Sealy mattresses, luxurious linens and a pillow menu on offer to suit all preferences. Guests will enjoy the ergonomic well-lit work spaces equipped with writing stationery and universal outlets for devices. All rooms and suites feature a fully stocked minibar and Nespresso machine.

Bathroom Amenities: Byredo of Stockholm products are featured in Classic Room, Classic Room High Floor, and Premium Room with Club Lounge Access.

Swiss luxury brand Chopard bathroom amenities are featured in Junior Suite, Premier Suite, Executive Suite, Ambassador Suite, Diplomatic Suite, and Royal Suite.

### Club Lounge Arrival Experience

For the best pre-arrival and arrival experience at InterContinental Bangkok, book a Club-access room or suite with exclusive Club Lounge access. Enjoy personalised pre-arrival contact 48 hours in advance, followed by a warm welcome and private check-in at the Club InterContinental Lounge.

### Thai Elegance at InterContinental

Experience Thai-inspired elegance at InterContinental Bangkok. Each room and suite showcases pastel silk-embroidered bedheads, handmade artwork of floating flowers, and custom furniture. Wooden parquet floors add warmth, while deep soaking tubs and rain showers ensure ultimate relaxation, all with stunning Bangkok city views.

### Royal Suite Privileges Redefined

Royal Suite guests enjoy unparalleled access to Club InterContinental Lounge on Level 37, showcasing stunning skyline views. Relish complimentary high-speed internet, express check-in/out, buffet breakfast, afternoon tea, evening cocktails, and a meeting room for one hour daily.
