### Luxuriate with Chopard Elegance

Indulge in luxury with complimentary Chopard wash amenities in your ensuite, featuring a spacious rain shower and a separate tub for ultimate relaxation.

### Club Lounge Arrival Experience

For the best pre-arrival and arrival experience at InterContinental Bangkok, book a Club-access room or suite with exclusive Club Lounge access. Enjoy personalised pre-arrival contact 48 hours in advance, followed by a warm welcome and private check-in at the Club InterContinental Lounge.

Learn more

### Dining in Downtown Bangkok

Indulge in an extraordinary culinary experience, from an Asian and Western-style breakfast at Espresso to French-inspired delicacies. Delight in the bold flavours of the Fireplace Grill and Bar, vibrant street side cafe at SoCal, and exquisite Cantonese at Summer Palace, or unwind with signature cocktails at Balcony Lounge or the Humidor.

Learn more

### Sky-High Serenity Awaits

Dive into relaxation at InterContinental Bangkok’s rooftop pool, a serene oasis 37 stories above the bustling city. Enjoy a refreshing swim in the free-form pool while soaking in panoramic views. Unwind with signature cocktails and light bites from the Oasis Pool Bar, making it the perfect escape in the heart of Bangkok.

Learn more

### Elevate Fitness at Infinity

Infinity Fitness at InterContinental Bangkok offers state-of-the-art cardio machines, a boxing ring, resistance training, and a stretching zone, all with panoramic city views. Enjoy exclusive facilities like a pool, jetted bathtub, saunas, and grooming stations. Personal trainers are available for tailored workouts.

## FAQs for InterContinental Bangkok

Does InterContinental Bangkok have a pool?

InterContinental Bangkok has 1 outdoor pool.

Oasis Pool

Float above the city’s rhythm in our rooftop pool that offers a front-row seat to Bangkok’s most breathtaking spectacle: the sunset. Sip on cocktails from the rooftop bar as twilight paints the sky in gold and amber, enveloping you in sheer magnificence.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Bangkok?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Bangkok for hotel guests.

Enjoy the ease of our expansive indoor car parking, accommodating up to 800 vehicles. Complimentary for in-house guests, this service ensures your stay is as effortless as it is luxurious, blending modern comfort with thoughtful hospitality.

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Bangkok?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Bangkok.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Bangkok for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Bangkok have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Bangkok has 6 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Bangkok?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Bangkok.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Bangkok?

Check-in at InterContinental Bangkok is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Bangkok offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Bangkok offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Bangkok from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Bangkok is conveniently located near Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), approximately 14.54 miles away (23.4 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Bangkok?

InterContinental Bangkok is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Phaya Thai Palace - 1.55 miles away (2.5 kilometers)
- Ancient City
- The Royal Grand Palace - 31.07 miles away (50 kilometers)
- Chatuchak Weekend Market - 5.47 miles away (8.8 kilometers)
- The Temple of Emerald Buddha - 4.35 miles away (7 kilometers)
- Lumpini Park
- Sea Life Bangkok Ocean World
- Klong Thom
- Pak Klong Talad Flower Market
- Asiatique
- Wat Pho
- Wat Arun

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Bangkok puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
