# InterContinental BangkokDining

Embark on a culinary journey in Bangkok that makes every meal a masterpiece. Awaken yourself to a breakfast spread at Espresso, savour the artistry of European-inspired cuisines at Fireplace, and immerse in the charm of vibrant street side cafe at SoCal.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

### Dining in Downtown Bangkok

Indulge in an extraordinary culinary experience, from an Asian and Western-style breakfast at Espresso to French-inspired delicacies. Delight in the bold flavours of the Fireplace Grill and Bar, vibrant street side cafe at SoCal, and exquisite Cantonese at Summer Palace, or unwind with signature cocktails at Balcony Lounge or the Humidor.

Learn more

### Seafood Feast Sunday Brunch

Dive into Espresso’s renowned Sunday Brunch, a seafood lover’s dream in Bangkok. Enjoy more than six kinds of crab, including golden king, Chilean king, snow and blue swimmer crab, alongside fresh seafood and made-to-order specialities. Live music from 12.00 to 15.00 sets the mood for a leisurely Sunday feast.

Learn more

### Authentic Cantonese Dim Sum

Indulge in all-you-can-eat Dim Sum at Summer Palace, where Executive Chef Yau, a Michelin-star expert from Hong Kong, serves authentic Cantonese cuisine. Experience a delicious journey through vibrant flavours and new à la carte dishes, bringing the rich taste of Hong Kong’s streets straight to your table.

Learn more

### Ultimate Indulgence at Humidor Cigar Bar

Experience sophisticated indulgence at Humidor Cigar Bar, an intimate haven by InterContinental Bangkok’s passionate team. Savour a curated selection of premium cigars and unique offerings, crafted to delight true connoisseurs seeking the ultimate in quality and craftsmanship.

Learn more

## Fireplace Grill and Bar

Since 1966, InterContinental Bangkok Fireplace Grill and Bar has defined fine dining in Bangkok legendary Ratchaprasong District surrounding Siam. Indulge in classic heritage dishes, from escargot to Dover sole from Brittany, that are reimagined with finesse and are elevated by an impeccable wine selection sourced from small family vineyards and renowned estates. Settle into a sophisticated ambience as our open show kitchen brings culinary artistry to life before you.

## Summer Palace

Marvel in the craftsmanship of Michelin laureate Executive Chinese Chef Shui Wing Yau, Dim Sum Chef Pui Kwan Chan, and their skilled colleagues as they bring flavours from the streets of Hong Kong to the heart of Bangkok with Cantonese culinary classics done the right way. Summer Palace is a long standing and widely acclaimed Bangkok favourite for Cantonese specialties, offering delightful a la carte selections and an all you can eat dim sum option.
