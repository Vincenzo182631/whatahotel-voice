3 days ago

We had a lovely stay at InterContinental Bangkok. The hotel's location is excellent, with easy access to shopping malls, restaurants and many of the city's attractions.

During our stay, we were relocated from the 34th floor to the 31st floor due to a technical issue. Unfortunately, we experienced some noise disturbances from the neighbouring room on the 31st floor, which affected our sleep.

I would particularly like to acknowledge Oak, the Night Manager, who took the time to investigate our concerns following my complaint. I also appreciated the receptionist's sincere apology and reassurance that the neighbouring guest had checked out. Their response was professional and reassuring.

Despite this minor inconvenience, our overall experience was very pleasant. The hotel's convenient location and the team's attentiveness made our stay enjoyable.

Thank you to the InterContinental Bangkok team for your hospitality. We look forward to visiting again!
