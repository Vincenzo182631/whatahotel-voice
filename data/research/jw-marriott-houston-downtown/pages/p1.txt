A Stunning Hotel With a Story To Tell

## A Historic Setting

The JW Marriott Houston Downtown building has a rich and storied history. Constructed in 1911, the building originally served as the Houston National Bank and was designed by the renowned architecture firm, Mauran, Russell & Crowell. The building's Beaux-Arts style is a nod to the bank's early 20th-century prosperity and is a perfect example of the grandeur and opulence of the era. After being vacant for several years, the building was converted into a hotel in the 1980s and was one of the first to open in the city's revitalized downtown area. Today, the JW Marriott Houston Downtown continues to stand as a symbol of the city's rich history and enduring vitality, offering guests a one-of-a-kind experience in a truly historic setting.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Houston Downtown?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Houston Downtown is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Houston Downtown allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Houston Downtown. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Houston Downtown?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Houston Downtown. The daily valet fee is $62.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Houston Downtown have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Houston Downtown has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Houston Downtown booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Houston Downtown?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Houston Downtown is William P Hobby Airport (HOU). HOU is located approximately 10.7 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Houston Downtown have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Houston Downtown has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Houston Downtown have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Houston Downtown has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay.
