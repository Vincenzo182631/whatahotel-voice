## Griffin Executive Lounge

Our Executive Lounge is located on the 11th floor and offers a refined and sophisticated ambiance, complemented by attentive service and luxurious amenities. As a guest of the Griffin Executive Lounge, you will enjoy a host of exclusive benefits, including complimentary hot breakfast, afternoon snacks and refreshments, evening hors d'oeuvres, access to a fully equipped business center, stunning downtown views and complimentary high-speed internet access. Our lounge is designed to provide an intimate setting for guests to relax, work, or socialize. Our Marriott Bonvoy® Platinum Elite, Titanium Elite, and Ambassador Elite Members will have complimentary access to the Griffin Executive Lounge. Daily access is available for purchase for non-qualifying guests.
