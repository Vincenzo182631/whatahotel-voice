## Introducing the Studio Tower: A Sophisticated New Era of Luxury Hotels in Downtown Houston

The Studio Tower expands JW Marriott Houston Downtown, breathing new life into the historic Battelstein’s Department Store. This luxury addition offers vibrant public spaces, refined accommodations, and immersive experiences that connect guests with Downtown Houston’s culture, dining, entertainment, and dynamic city energy.

## Your Downtown Houston Luxury Hotel Retreat Awaits

The new Studio Tower features 56 exquisitely designed guest rooms, including 32 suites. Combining Battelstein’s historic elegance with modern luxury, each space delivers comfort, style, and timeless sophistication.

## Elevated Spaces, Signature Style

Designed for modern indulgence, Studio Tower rooms feature exquisite millwork, high-floor and city view options. Every detail blends luxury comfort and style, offering a refined retreat in the heart of downtown Houston.

## Indulgence in Every Detail

Open layouts and artful tilework define Studio Tower bathrooms. Select rooms include inset or sculptural soaking tubs, creating a space of curated relaxation and pure luxury.
