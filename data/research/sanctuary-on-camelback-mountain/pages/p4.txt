# Fall Flavors of Jade

This fall, Scottsdale's Jade Bar is just the place to gather and relax. Indulge in spectacular pours and delicious bites all week long with these seasonal specials Sunday - Thursday.

### Ideal for

Groups of Friends

Girlfriends

Couples

Individuals

###### Available 5:00pm - 9:00pm

###### SUNDAY: BURGER & BEER $26

Savor our signature Elements cheeseburger with house-made fries and a pint of beer

###### MONDAY: MARTINI & OYSTERS $40

Indulge in a half-dozen oysters and your choice of a classic martini

###### TUESDAY: TACO TUESDAY $30

The southwest tradition continues - taste savory carnitas tacos paired with a refreshing margarita

###### WEDNESDAY: BOTTLE & BOARD $50

Choose from three featured bottles of wine paired with an artisanal charcuterie board. Wine options include:

_Sparkling: Prosecco, Torresella, Extra Dry, Veneto, ITA_

_White: Chardonnay, Mundaka, Central Coast, CA_

_Red: Cabernet Sauvignon, Grant Burge 'Paso Ink', Paso Robles, CA_

###### THURSDAY: SUSHI & SAKE $50

Pair your choice of fresh sushi roll with a bottle of Tozai 'Snow Maiden' Junmai Nigori - perfect for two

_Pricing is per person and does not include tax and gratuity. No reservations required._
