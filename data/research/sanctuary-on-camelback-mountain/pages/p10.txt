# Private Mixology

Learn more about the art of cocktailing! Sanctuary offers the ultimate Scottsdale mixology experience with private, interactive 90-minute mixology classes. These private sessions are perfect for gathering with friends, family get-togethers, or executive retreats.

All private sessions are customized, must be arranged at least 7 days in advance, and are subject to availability.

Up to 9 guests – $500 fee, plus $75\* per person

10-20 guests – $1,000 fee, plus $75\* per person

Pricing includes instruction, preparation of three craft cocktails per guest, take-home recipe cards, and gratuity. Subject to availability. Start times at 1:00pm or 2:00pm.

\* _Per person pricing is subject to change based on featured spirits and cocktails._
