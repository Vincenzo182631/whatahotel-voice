# Matcha & Mahjong Experience

Our Matcha & Mahjong event is a curated celebration of community fun. This popular event showcases spectacular panoramic mountain views and invites guests to engage in a friendly game of Mahjong while sipping on handcrafted Matcha refreshments.

### Ideal for

Individuals

Couples

Girlfriends

Groups of Friends

##### Friday, October 16

###### 10:00am - 12:00pm \| Praying Monk

Matcha & Mahjong brings together two timeless rituals in a meaningful community gathering. Sip on handcrafted matcha, shop seasonal boutique items, and connect with others through a friendly game of Mahjong. To enhance the fun, fresh, made-to-order Matcha teas will be crafted along with light and healthy bites.

##### Player Options Include:

###### Open Play \| $35 per person

Open Play is ideal for Mahjong players who are ready to gather and enjoy social play without instruction.

###### Learn & Play Guided Instruction \| $55 per person

Learn & Play is a guided workshop that will introduce guests to an overview of Mahjong before putting their skills into practice.

Guests familiar with either the traditional or American version of Mahjong are welcome to bring their own set - tables will be set up for four players.

_Due to popularity and limited seating, advanced reservations are required._
