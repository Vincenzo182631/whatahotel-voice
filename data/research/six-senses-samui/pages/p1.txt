## A spectrum of exceptional island experiences

Perched on a secluded headland at the northern tip of Koh Samui, Six Senses Samui invites you to slow down and savor the simple pleasures of island life. Our 67 villas blend seamlessly into the natural landscape with views across the Gulf of Thailand. Restore at Six Senses Spa, where private treatment Salas overlook a rock-fringed bay and explore Thai flavors in our dining venues.

As part of our ongoing resort enhancement, Dining on the Hill and main pool will take a pause from October 7 to November 30, 2026. Our beach club pool at Drift at the Beach, Dining on the Rocks, and other dining experiences remain available.

## Accommodation

All of our 67 spacious villas offer glorious ocean or tropical landscape views, and 60 have private pools. Choose views from the hill or be right on the ocean, or enjoy extra space in our signature two-bedroom Ocean Retreat and three-bedroom Beach Reserve.
