## Borgo degli Ulivi

Discover our exquisite new Borgo degli Ulivi for up to 22 guests, perfect for family getaways or group retreats with friends. Your private Puglian palace with stunning interconnected rooms, comprising four magnificent Grand Suites with a private gardens and pools, six elegant Junior Suites, leading directly from tranquil bathrooms and gardens to secluded patios and a breathtaking Itria Suite with private garden, a testament to Apulian craftsmanship, offer an immersive experience into the heart of Puglia.

## Lido Bambù

Lido Bambù is located in one of the most beautiful places in Puglia south of Monopoli, in Capitolo with a backdrop of white sands and the soothing sound of the crystal-clear Adriatic sea. The perfect spot for spend all day at the beach, have a relaxed lunch with friends, enjoy a date night with a loved one or an aperitivo at sunset accompanied by live music. Our hotel shuttle service is available to take you to our welcoming, pet-friendly beach club.
