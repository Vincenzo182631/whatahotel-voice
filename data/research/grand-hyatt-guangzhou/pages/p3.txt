## G Bar

Well-known for its wide selection of wines and champagnes, G Bar features a stylish “walk-through” wine cellar and suspended lounge seating areas that are ideal for business meetings and social gatherings. It offers the perfect bar lounge ambience for a pre-dinner cocktail or a casual drink.

## G Restaurant

G is a stylish bistro with an open kitchen featuring grilled steak and seafood prepared in a wood-fired oven. Indulge in a delectable semi-buffet during lunch and savor an extensive à la carte menu for dinner, all set against the backdrop of Hua Cheng Square.

## The Market Café

The Market Cafe is a trend-setting buffet restaurant that offers Western and Asian cuisines from eight live cooking stations. With a casual and modern ambience, it extends from the linked corridor. Diners can also enjoy panoramic views of Huacheng Square, Guangzhou's new central axis, as well as our transparent kitchens to see the chefs in action. Enjoy a pleasant dining experience with the best buffet in Guangzhou!

## The Penthouse

With a main dining room and 12 luxurious, residential-style private dining suites and panoramic views of Huacheng Square, Guangzhou’s new central axis, The Penthouse restaurant at Grand Hyatt Guangzhou serves authentic Cantonese cuisine in a stylish and contemporary ambience.

## Guanxi Lounge

The Guanxi Lounge is an elegant spot that serves afternoon tea with European savories, pastries, and homemade ice-cream specialties. It offers a stylish and contemporary ambience, with panoramic views of the Pearl River and the city’s new Central Business District. The lounge’s private “box” seating areas give a sense of intimate comfort ideal for either a quiet drink in the evening or weekend afternoon tea. Located at 22 F, on the hotel’s linked corridor.
