## Experience our Luxury Guangzhou Hotel Steps from Zhujiang New Town

Located in the CBD of Pearl River New Town (Zhujiang New Town) of Guangzhou City, Hotel’s Sky Lobby offers panoramic views of Canton Tower, East & West tower, easily visit Guangzhou Opera House, Guangdong Museum, Haixinsha and Xinghai Concert Hall. Hotel is 15-minute drive to Guangzhou East Train Station (train station to HKG) and a 15-minute drive to Pazhou International Convention Center which hotel offers complimentary shuttle service to buyers during the annual Spring & Autumn Canton Fair. 5-star Grand Hyatt Guangzhou offers 368 luxurious rooms with free Wi-Fi access to international websites and social media.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Grand Hyatt Guangzhou features 368 elegant, contemporary rooms—including 48 suites—ranging from 45–270 square meters, each offering modern design, complimentary Wi-Fi, luxurious amenities and panoramic views of Zhujiang New Town.

## EXPLORE

Indoor Pool

The 20-meter swimming pool is surrounded by large windows, allowing natural light to flood in and creating a bright, inviting atmosphere. Male and female changing rooms with lockers are available along with a sauna, whirlpool, cold plunge pool and grooming stations. The pool is for use by in-house guests and members only and is open 7 a.m.–10 p.m. daily. Swimmers are required to wear a swim cap.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

Grand Club

The Grand Club is located on the 20th floor of the hotel, an exclusive and prestigious space created by Grand Hyatt Guangzhou for guests staying in Suites and Club Floor guest rooms. Designed around the core of luxury and elegance, it offers panoramic views of the lush greenery of Huacheng Square and the stunning urban scenery of Zhujiang New Town, allowing you to indulge in comfort and tranquility in the heart of the bustling city.​

During your stay, you can enjoy a range of exclusive privileges:

1. Complimentary continental breakfast which served at the 20th floor:​

Monday to Friday: 06:30 AM - 10:30 AM​

Saturday, Sunday and on Public Holidays: 06:30 AM - 11:00 AM​

2. All-day Grand Club coffee, tea, soft drinks and light refreshments​

3\. Complimentary Grand Club evening drinks and hors d’oeuvres: 5:30 PM - 8:00 PM daily​

4. Complimentary Grand Club meeting room usage for one hour per stay. For every​ additional hour a charge of CNY 300 is applicable​

5. Complimentary pressing of up to two (2) garments per person per day​

6. Concierge services with an exclusive check-in and check-out in the Grand Club Lounge​

​Grand Club Operating Hours : 06:30 AM to 11:00 PM

Fitness Center

Located on the hotel’s top floor, our modern fitness center offers stunning city views along with the latest cardio equipment, all featuring built-in TV systems. A range of classes and training sessions are available. The fitness center is for use by in-house guests and members only and open 24 hours daily.
