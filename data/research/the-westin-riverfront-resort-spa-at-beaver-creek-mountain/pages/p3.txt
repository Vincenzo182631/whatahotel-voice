## Dining

The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek offers on-site dining options to satisfy your cravings from morning until night. Colletta offers a contemporary Italian dining experience that combines Northern Italian traditions with modern culinary techniques, showcasing seasonal ingredients in thoughtfully crafted dishes. At The Lobby bar, enjoy the stunning mountain views while savoring Colorado micro-brews and handcrafted cocktails. If you're looking for something quick and casual, pick up a snack, sandwich, or beverage from the Gondola Market + Cafe.
