### Discover the Best of Vail Valley in Every Season

Nestled at the base of Beaver Creek Mountain and surrounded by the breathtaking Rockies, The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas is the perfect year-round escape. In winter, enjoy skiing, snowboarding, tubing, and ice skating. When warmer weather arrives, explore hiking, whitewater rafting, kayaking, and more. No matter the season, there's an adventure for everyone.

Snowy Escapes

### Winter Activities

Just steps from your Vail Valley villa, the Riverfront Express Gondola provides seamless access from Avon to Beaver Creek Mountain, one of Colorado’s premier winter destinations. Enjoy skiing pristine slopes, snowboarding through fresh powder, and exploring scenic mountain trails. No matter how you choose to spend the day, the Rocky Mountains provide the perfect backdrop for adventure, connection, and relaxation.

### Summer Activities

Beyond ski season, Beaver Creek offers endless ways to explore the Rockies. Hike scenic mountain trails, kayak winding waterways, cast a line in renowned fly-fishing streams, or experience the thrill of whitewater rafting. As the seasons change, golden aspens create a stunning backdrop for every outing. From peaceful time on the river to unforgettable days outdoors, there's something for every type of explorer.

### Explore More Local Area

## Beaver Creek Village

Beaver Creek offers a refined Rocky Mountain escape with boutique shopping, dining, wellness experiences, and outdoor adventure. Enjoy seasonal activities like hiking, mountain biking, scenic gondola rides, fly fishing, ice skating, and live entertainment, all set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Vail Valley.

## Lionshead Village

Experience the best of Vail from Lionshead Village. This walkable alpine destination offers shopping, dining, seasonal events, an ice rink, and direct access to the Eagle Bahn Gondola for year-round outdoor recreation, from skiing and snowboarding to hiking and biking.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Free Weights, Strength Equipment, and Treadmills, Ellipticals, Arc Trainers, Peloton Cycles, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Outdoor Lap Pool (Outdoor)Whirlpool

Does The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa Anjali is located on-site at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain.
