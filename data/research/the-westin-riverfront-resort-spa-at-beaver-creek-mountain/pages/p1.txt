# Welcome to The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain

## Escape to our Beaver Creek vacation resort

Escape to the awe-inspiring beauty of Avon, Colorado, at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek. Discover this vacation ownership resort, a veritable sanctuary of rejuvenation amidst the majestic peaks of Beaver Creek Mountain. Enjoy year-round resort activities like skiing, hiking, biking, fishing, and thrilling whitewater rafting. Experience the renowned ski slopes of Beaver Creek close...See More to our resort via the nearby Riverfront Express Gondola. Treat yourself to delectable cuisine and cocktails at our scenic bars and restaurants. Our villa vacation rentals feature fully equipped kitchens, washers/dryers, gas fireplaces and signature Westin Heavenly Beds and Baths. Relax on your private balcony or patio while immersing yourself in the unparalleled beauty of Beaver Creek, Colorado. Find your bliss at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas.See Less

### No Resort Fees. Free Wi-Fi. Just More to Enjoy.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain?

The check-in time at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 10:00 am.

Does The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain.

What are the parking options at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain. Self parking is available for $35.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $45.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain?

The closest airport near The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain is Eagle County Airport (EGE). EGE is located approximately 35.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
