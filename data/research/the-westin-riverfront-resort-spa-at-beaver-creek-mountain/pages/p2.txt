# Retreat after a day in Beaver Creek in our villa rentals

## Accommodations

The more challenging your day's adventures in the Vail Valley, the more welcoming our Beaver Creek vacation rentals will feel. Your renewing retreat at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas is a stylish studio, one- or two-bedroom villa offering relaxing moments on furnished balconies or patios. You'll also enjoy premium amenities such as the signature Westin Heavenly® Bed — with layer after layer...See More of refreshing down bedding — as well as the Westin Heavenly® Bath, a fully equipped kitchen or kitchenette, HDTVs, DVD players, a Bose® sound system and a washer and dryer, bundling a truly rejuvenating time for you, your family and your guests.See Less

### Like-Home Comforts

Villas come complete with luxurious like-home comforts including a kitchen or kitchenette, washers/dryers, living spaces, complimentary Wi-Fi, and resort amenities without the resort fees.

### Mountain Views

Nestled at the base of Beaver Creek Mountain, The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas offers majestic views.

### Westin Heavenly® Bed & Bath

After a full day of shopping, skiing or family adventures, sink into your Westin Heavenly® Bed or unwind with Westin Heavenly® Bath amenities.

Accommodates Up to 8 Adults

### More Comfort, More Space in Our Two-Bedroom Villas

Ideal for group vacations in Beaver Creek, this spacious villa features two inviting living areas, separate dining spaces, and a well equipped kitchen and kitchenette. King bedrooms showcase Westin Heavenly® Beds and well appointed bathrooms, while a furnished balcony or patio invites you to relax and take in Avon’s scenic views.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain?

The largest occupancy room at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain is the 2 Bedroom Villa with a maximum occupancy of 8.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain?

No, the rooms at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Riverfront Mountain Villas, Beaver Creek Mountain have a Coffee/tea maker.
