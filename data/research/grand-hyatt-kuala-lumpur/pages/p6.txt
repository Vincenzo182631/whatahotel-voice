# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur offers valet service for up to 50 vehicles on a first-come, first-served basis. Parking levels have a clearance of 2.1 metres. Only Touch N’ Go and credit cards (Visa and MasterCard) are accepted.

In-House Guest

MYR 30.0030.00 Malaysian ringgits

Non In-House GuestMYR 45.0045.00 Malaysian ringgits

### Self-Parking

Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur offers self-parking for up to 400 vehicles on a first-come, first-served basis. Parking levels have a clearance of 2.1 metres. Only Touch N’ Go and credit cards (Visa and MasterCard) are accepted.
