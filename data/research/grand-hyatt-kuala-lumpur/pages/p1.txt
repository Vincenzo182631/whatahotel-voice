## Discover the Upscale Elegance of Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur

Overlooking the iconic Petronas Twin Towers and adjacent to Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre, Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur is one of the best hotels in the city, strategically located in the heart of the action and within walking distance of the Golden Triangle shopping, dining and entertainment district.

## EXPLORE

Spa

ESSA Spa at Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur offers guests the chance to experience the deepest relaxation and calmness. The luxurious solitude of our spa sanctuary features the powerful benefits of ramuan, the traditional Malay preparation of herbal healing and invigorating ingredients.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our ongoing commitment to environmental sustainability, we are annually eradicating approximately half a million plastic bottles that would otherwise end up in landfills by making the switch to reusable glass bottles and an in-house water-bottling facility.

Grand Club

Experience unparalleled luxury at the Grand Club Lounge in Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur, offering exclusive benefits such as personalized check-in and check-out, complimentary breakfast, all-day refreshments, and evening cocktails with canapés.

Business Centre

Stay connected at our full-service business centre, located on our highest floor in the Sky Lobby. Executive amenities, computers and modern services are available at your convenience, ready to make your stay more efficient and productive.

Fitness Centre

Enjoy an invigorating workout at our sophisticated fitness centre, which is well-appointed with the latest cardiovascular and strength-training Technogym machines. All cardio machines include internet access so fitness enthusiasts can watch tutorials or TV while they work out. The fitness centre also features an outdoor swimming pool and relaxation areas, and personal trainers are available on request.​

​ **Open 24 Hours**

Outdoor Pool

Our outdoor swimming pool—exclusively for the use of registered guests—offers a resort-like environment in the heart of the bustling city, with a depth of 1.2 metres and a view of the Petronas Twin Towers. We also offer a wading pool ideal for children with a depth of 0.45 metres and complimentary kids’ amenities. Please note that there are no lifeguards on duty and that all children must be accompanied by an adult.​

**​7:00 AM to 9:00 PM**
