### Another Wonderful Stay at Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur!

It’s always a pleasure to return to Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur. From the friendly staff and well-maintained facilities to the consistently excellent service, we always feel welcomed and well taken care of every time we visit.

A special thank you to Esther, who assisted us during check-in with so much care and warmth. She even arranged a complimentary upgrade to a Deluxe Room for us, which made our stay extra special. We were so happy and truly appreciated the thoughtful gesture! The next day, Nicole kindly assisted us with our late check-out request, making everything smooth and hassle-free. Thank you both for making our stay even more memorable! ❤️

Breakfast at THIRTY8 is always amazing! Despite the restaurant being fully occupied, the staff remained helpful, attentive, and accommodating. We requested a table change because the original seating area felt a little too small, and the team assisted us efficiently without any hassle.

The breakfast spread was absolutely splendid, with an impressive variety of dishes to choose from. The Teh Tarik was the bomb, as always! The Eggs Benedict with salmon was another favourite, and I kept going back for more because there was just so much good food to try. Definitely a 9/10 experience, every single time!

We also had lunch at JP Teres before heading home, and of course, we had to order our favourites — Chicken Rice and Rojak Buah! 😍 The food was amazing, and the staff were helpful and attentive. JP Teres has always been one of our favourite places to visit whenever we have no idea what to eat. You can never go wrong with good food and great service!

While my son enjoyed his swimming time, we also ordered the Wagyu Burger. The portion was generous, and even after sharing, we were both feeling so full! We did encounter a minor issue with the payment terminal, which kept showing errors a few times. In the end, we paid in cash instead. LOL! Luckily, we had some cash with us. Other than that little hiccup, we were happy with the food and overall experience.

Being a World of Hyatt member is truly worthwhile! Throughout our staycations and vacations, we earn points that we can redeem for complimentary hotel stays. We’ve done this several times, and it has become our go-to option for spontaneous staycations. It’s such a wonderful way to enjoy more memorable experiences while making the most of our stays.

Overall, another lovely stay filled with good food, thoughtful service, and precious family moments. Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur continues to be one of our favourite places for a relaxing getaway. Thank you to the entire team for always making us feel special and giving us reasons to return.

Looking forward to our next staycation! ❤️✨

### Missing a little warmth

Lovely location in between two iconic areas - Bukit Bintang and Suria KLCC. Atmosphere on G floor is very public and open, but more exclusive on the Sky Lobby. There is a very distinct shift between G floor and Sky Lobby. Rooms are modern and bathroom/walk in closet is big. Value is decent if you consider the Balmain shower essentials and turndown service, but it’s as expected. Nothing extraordinary. Rooms and common spaces were always clean and never had any issues. Noise level in hallway can be heard but it’s moderate. Service is just alright, can definitely be improved, especially at check in desk and JPteres. Again, love the Balmain toiletries.
