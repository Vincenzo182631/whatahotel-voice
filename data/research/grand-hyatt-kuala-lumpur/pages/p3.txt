# Dining

Revel in Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur’s finest dine-in options for an exquisite dining affair that offers an indulgent kaleidoscope of taste, sight and sound. In view of overwhelming patronage, our dining outlets may encounter full-house situations where seats will not be available for walk-ins. To avoid disappointment and to help us manage expectations, we strongly recommend making advanced reservations.

## JPteres

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

JPteres, a name significant to its home address at Jalan Pinang, invites diners to embrace our locality through authentic Malaysian flavours, a cozy and nostalgic ambiance, as well as the liveliness of satay grills, tandoor ovens, roti hot plate and wok stations. Set against a cove of greenery at the Ground Lobby, JPteres offers both indoor casual dining experience and outdoor sports bar relaxation.
