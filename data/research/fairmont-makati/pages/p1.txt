# Philippines, an archipelago _shaped_ by sea and skyline

Stretching across more than 7,000 islands, the Philippines balances volcanic landscapes, coral-fringed coastlines, and dense metropolitan centers. Spanish-era churches, American-influenced boulevards, and contemporary high-rises reflect centuries of cultural exchange. From remote lagoons in Palawan to the commercial towers of Metro Manila, the country moves between maritime heritage and modern urban development.

Location within Makati’s central business district

Direct access to commercial and financial institutions

Connectivity to Ninoy Aquino International Airport and major retail centers

### Our Fairmont hotel in the Philippines

The Philippines spans mountainous interiors, active volcanoes, and extensive coastlines along the Pacific Ocean and South China Sea. Northern Luzon preserves ancient rice terraces carved into highland slopes, while central islands host coral reefs and white-sand beaches. In the capital region, historic districts such as Intramuros recall the country’s colonial past, contrasting with Makati’s contemporary skyline.

#### Maritime heritage

As an island nation, the Philippines has long depended on sea routes for trade and cultural exchange. Coastal settlements and historic ports shaped regional economies across centuries.

#### Volcanic terrain

Located along the Pacific Ring of Fire, the archipelago includes active volcanoes and fertile plains. This geology influences agriculture, settlement patterns, and landscape character.

### Urban commerce

Metro Manila, particularly Makati, anchors the country’s financial system. High-rise districts, retail corridors, and multinational headquarters define the capital’s economic presence.

### Planning your stay at Fairmont hotels in the Philippines

Ninoy Aquino International Airport (MNL) serves as the primary international gateway to Metro Manila. Fairmont Makati is located in the Ayala Center complex, approximately 20 to 30 minutes from the airport depending on traffic conditions.

Major roadways and business corridors connect Makati to other districts within Metro Manila.
