## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Makati located?

Fairmont Makati is located at the center of the financial business, commercial and entertainment district of Makati. The hotel is 12 kilometers or approximately a 15-minute drive from The Ninoy Aquino International Airport.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Makati?

The check-in time at Fairmont Makati is from 3 pm local time.

The check-out time is at 11 am local time.

Does Fairmont Makati have a spa?

Yes. Fairmont Spa is at located on the fourth floor, open from 8 am to 10 pm.

Does Fairmont Makati have a swimming pool?

Yes. Our outdoor swimming pool is located on the fourth floor at Fairmont Makati and is open from 6 am to 10 pm. Pool towels are also available upon request.

Does Fairmont Makati have a gym/fitness center?

Yes. Our fitness center is open from 8 am to 10 pm.

What is the cancellation policy at Fairmont Makati?

No cancellation charge applies before 6 pm (local time), up to one (1) day prior to arrival. Beyond that time, the first night will be charged.
