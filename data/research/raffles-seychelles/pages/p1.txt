# Your barefoot luxury _paradise_

Designed with the carefree understated beauty of island living, our 87 spacious private pool villas cascade down the verdant hillsides of Praslin Island. From mountain-top eyries with panoramic views of the palm forests and opalescent ocean to the secluded embrace of a tropical beachfront garden, your haven in nature awaits.

## The first Raffles resort

Opening its doors on the island of Praslin in 2011, Raffles Seychelles was the first Raffles resort, an all-villa retreat in a UNESCO area occupying a tropical hillside and beachfront setting of rare natural beauty.

## Frequently asked questions

### What unique dining experiences are available at Raffles Seychelles?

Enjoy curated culinary delights that showcase local flavors and international cuisine, crafted by expert chefs.

### How can I book a stay at Raffles Seychelles?

You can easily book your luxurious escape by calling (248) 429 6000 or emailing praslin@raffles.com.

### What activities can I enjoy at Raffles Seychelles?

Engage in water sports, explore nature trails, or relax at the spa, all designed to enhance your luxurious experience.

### Why choose Raffles Seychelles for your vacation?

This resort offers a unique blend of luxury, privacy, and stunning natural beauty, making it an ideal getaway.

### When is the best time to visit Raffles Seychelles?

The best time to visit is during the dry season, from May to October, ensuring perfect weather for your luxury escape.

## Our Ongoing Transformation

Raffles Seychelles is currently undergoing a comprehensive transformation of our culinary and social spaces, scheduled to continue until 15th October, 2026. While this redesign is impacting some of the resort’s restaurant offerings, thoughtfully curated alternative locations have been created for our guests’ enjoyment.

Some renovation work is visible around the property; however, all projects have been carefully planned to ensure guests are not disturbed by noise or undue disruption.

In the meantime, we continue to curate thoughtful experiences and complimentary activities designed to enhance our guests’ stay, ensuring time spent with us remains enriching and memorable as we refine our surroundings.

We thank you, in advance, for your understanding as we further elevate the Raffles experience.
