4/5

### Almost flawless except…

Location: Great location right next to the Liverpool tube station.
Atmosphere: Felt like a US hotel and definitely like a Hyatt. Very nice way to be welcomed into the hotel in that huge lobby. Our floor 3 , past the bridge, just in the first hallway has a very odd smell - salty, almost sewage-ey. Very brief but definitely there.
Rooms: Odd, they are long and narrow, with TALL ceilings. Our small window looked out onto other rooms, this makes the natural light in the room almost non-existent.
Value: It’s London, it can get expensive but given 20% off food and bev, the free breakfast and wine, beer, etc from 5-6 I’ll give it a 5 star.
Cleanliness: Like I said, a very odd smell as you walked by a few set of rooms on floor 3, otherwise our room was very clean as was the lobby.
Noise level: No issues
Service: I have 1 complaint. The week prior to check in we were walking past the hotel so we stopped by the front desk and asked for a reservation at their Japanese restaurant for our last night in London (Sat 10/17). 10 minutes before our reservation we found out that that restaurant is not open on Saturday’s. Now it’s 7pm in London on a Saturday. Try finding a good restaurant without a reservation or 2 hour wait. Any why would they take the reservation if that restaurant was closed? Disappointing.

### A memorable stay at the beautiful Andaz Liverpool Street. Cant wait to go back!

A wonderful stay at the beautiful Andaz Liverpool Street, very conveniently located next to Liverpool St station, we were greeted with a very warm friendly welcome as well as a glass of bubbles to begin our stay.
We had 6 rooms booked for 12 family members, all of which were very kindly upgraded to king rooms, we had a family room travelling with our 2 teenage children, we had 2 huge rooms with an interconnecting door and 2 lovely bathrooms, robes, slippers, soft drinks in the mini bar were all complimentary, everything and more was thought about.
We had pre dinner drinks in the bar as well as taking advantage if the 5-6pm happy hour, we then had a wonderful anniversary meal at the East way Brasserie where all of the staff were very attentive and made our evening memorable.
After a lovely comfy sleep, we had breakfast again in the Brasserie, which had everything you could ever wish for in a breakfast! We didn't want to leave the hotel at all!
Thank you to Flynn and his team on reception and to all the team at the Brasserie who looked after us from 12-13/09/26. We cant wait to come back.
