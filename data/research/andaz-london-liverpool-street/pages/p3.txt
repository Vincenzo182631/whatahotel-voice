Rake’s Café Bar – All-Day Dining, Cocktails & Live DJs Near Liverpool Street.
Brunch, Beats, and Cocktails in the Heart of East London.
Just steps from Liverpool Street Station, Rake’s Café Bar is your all-day escape from the city’s hustle. A laid-back café by day and a vibrant cocktail bar by night, Rake’s offers an eclectic mix of comforting dishes, artisan coffee, and creative cocktails — served in a setting that’s as stylish as it is welcoming.
Join us for Brunch Paradiso, our signature Saturday experience, where indulgent plates and limitless drinks meet bold East London energy. As the sun sets, the mood shifts with live sets from our resident DJs every Thursday, Friday, and Saturday evening, transforming Rake’s into one of the best spots in London for music, drinks, and social vibes.
Whether you're here for a working lunch, pre-dinner cocktails, or a late-night gathering, Rake’s is where Londoners come to eat, drink, and unwind — all without the rules.

A Bold East London Pub Full of Character, Curiosity, and Culinary Delights.

Miyako – Authentic Japanese Restaurant Near Liverpool Street Station. Sushi, Sashimi & Teriyaki in the Heart of the City of London.

Led by Chef Sakamoto, Miyako is one of the most authentic Japanese restaurants in the City of London, located just moments from Liverpool Street Station. With over 20 years of culinary expertise, Chef Sakamoto brings traditional Japanese flavours to life using the freshest ingredients and time-honoured techniques.

Enjoy a refined menu of sushi, sashimi, teriyaki, and expertly selected sake in an intimate, contemporary setting that’s perfect for business lunches, romantic dinners, or friends gatherings.
Whether you're a seasoned sushi lover or discovering Japanese cuisine for the first time, Miyako offers a truly authentic dining experience in the heart of East London.
