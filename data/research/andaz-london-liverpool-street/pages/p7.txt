Built in the late 1800s by the Great Eastern Railway Company, this two-wing redbrick hotel sits on the eastern fringe of the City (London’s financial district). Its design is a mix of Victorian and modern, with oak-paneled walls in the George pub and Eames chairs and Jacobsen lamps in contemporary guest rooms that also have "great amenities and bathtubs." "Excellent restaurant choices" include Japanese cuisine at Miyako and afternoon tea at 1901 restaurant. While staff are "attentive and friendly," certain services aren’t immediately available: "I was disappointed the massage therapist was not there in the morning so that I could get a massage before going to the office." The property has a hidden Masonic temple on-site, accessed via a heavy studded door and winding staircase.
