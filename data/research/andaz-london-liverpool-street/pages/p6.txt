# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Paid self parking available nearby

The hotel doesn’t provide on-site parking. The closest public car park is the London Wall Car Park (23 London Wall, London, EC2V 5DY), located underground between Aldersgate and Moorgate, with 217 spaces.

## Transportation

Getting here

Heathrow Airport (LHR)

Take the Heathrow Express (every 15 minutes) to Paddington station and then a taxi or underground to the hotel (25 minutes). Alternatively, travel via the underground (60 minutes) to Liverpool Street station, which is next to the hotel.

Transportation Options

London Liverpool Street Station

Access the Central, Circle, Elizabeth, Hammersmith and City and Metropolitan lines, London Overground and TFL rail routes.

Stansted Airport (STN)

The Stansted Express takes 45 minutes and delivers you to the hotel. Alternatively, take a bus from Stansted Airport to Liverpool Street station, which takes 60 minutes.

City Airport (LCY)

A taxi from City Airport takes approximately 30 minutes. Alternatively, take the DLR and underground to Liverpool Street station, which takes 25 minutes.

Gatwick Airport (LGW)

From Gatwick Airport, take the Gatwick Express (every 15 minutes) to Victoria station and then a taxi to the hotel (35 minutes). Alternatively, go via the underground from Victoria station to Liverpool Street station (25 minutes).

Luton Airport (LTN)

From Luton airport, take a shuttle bus to Luton Airport Parkway Station (5 minutes), then either take a First Capital Connect train (21 minutes) or an East Midlands train (21 minutes) to King’s Cross Station. Then take the underground to Liverpool Street station (10 minutes).
