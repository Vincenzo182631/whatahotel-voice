### Spa Rooms + Suites

Book a Spa Room or Suite to further elevate your stay. Each of our 19 Spa Rooms comes with a deep soaking Fuji tub, standalone shower, bathroom with a view plus plentiful vanity space and luxury bath amenities. Alternatively, choose the Deluxe One Bedroom or the decadent, 1,175-square-feet Presidential Suite.

Learn more

### Square 1682

With its delicious, approachable menus and upbeat ambiance, Square 1682 has become a beloved Center City gathering place thanks to its great food, great drinks and great people. Our New American fare could be described as: worldly, progressive, delicious, artfully presented, healthful and comforting.

Learn more

### Private Dining

Every private dining event proves delicious and inspiring, thanks to Chef Mackenson “Mack” Horebe of Square 1682, who focuses on local, seasonal menus that emphasize colorful, hearty dishes. Let us host your private dining event for parties of 10 to 250 guests in spaces from an intimate library and sleek boardroom to an elevated ballroom.

Learn more

### Weddings

We adore helping couples celebrate love in customized ways that speak to each unique vision. You and your guests will adore our hotel's Center City location, the rooms' luxurious amenities as well as our venue's delectable catering and naturally lit spaces like the stunning, 1,750-square-foot penthouse ballroom.

Learn more

### Group Experiences

A fun and unique activity can turn any event into a memorable experience that guests will cherish long after it ends. At Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia, our exclusive partnerships and curated experiences can elevate any occasion that brings you to Philly.

## FAQs for Hotel Palomar Philadelphia

Does Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia have a pool?

No, Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia does not have a pool.

How much is parking at Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia?

At Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 71.05 USD .

Valet parking is available at a charge of $71.05 for overnight and $43.20 for day parking.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia.

Pet policy description. We invite you to bring your pet no matter their size, weight, or breed, all at no extra charge.

Pet fee per night: 0 USD

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia?

Check-in at Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia is conveniently located near Philadelphia Intl Airport (PHL), approximately 8.5 miles away (13.68 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
