# Kimpton Hotel Palomar PhiladelphiaLocation

In the heart of Center City, Kimpton Hotel Palomar Philadelphia is steps from Rittenhouse Square and Philadelphia City Hall. Browse the area's many boutiques, restaurants and museums, then stroll the Schuylkill River Trail or catch a major league game.

## Things to do

### Independence National Historic Park

Independence National Historical Park is a United States National Park in Philadelphia that preserves several sites associated with the American Revolution and the nation's founding history.

### Rittenhouse Square

Rittenhouse Square is one of the five original open-space parks planned by William Penn and his surveyor Thomas Holme during the late 17th century in central Philadelphia.

### University City

Education Center host of two of the largest and most influential institutions in town: University of Pennsylvania and Drexel University.
