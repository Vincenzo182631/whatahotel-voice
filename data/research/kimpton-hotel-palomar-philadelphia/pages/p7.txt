### Staying at this hotel was like going to work

2 days ago

I stayed at this hotel on official government travel, and will never go back again. The room was loud, the fire alarm went off twice while I was there and at any given time only half the elevators were working. One morning it took me nearly 20 minutes to get from floor five to the lobby because there was only one elevator operating. I found the front desk staff ill informed and argumentative and it simply was not worth a stay given the fact that there are hundreds of other options in Philadelphia. I was on government, travel, provided government identification government credit card, and travel authorization, but had to go back to the front desk four different times to provide them additional information to prove that this is government travel, and thus tax exempt. By comparison, I showed my ID and government credit card at a Marriott a week before and it was done staying at this hotel took way too much effort for what it was worth.

### Not What It Used To Be

3 days ago

I like this hotel and location but it seems to be headed in wrong direction in terms of both the facility and service. On our recent visit, the room had multiple issues which could, and should, be addressed through routine maintenance. For example, the heavy handle to the sliding door to the bathroom fell off, one of the window blinds would only descend on one side making it impossible to lower the entire shade, and the toilet handle came off each time it was used.

The fire alarm went off one morning at 6am which I would normally attribute to just bad luck/timing but apparently it also went off the day before in the late afternoon (pointing to possible maintenance issues, again). Later that morning, when trying to get a cup of coffee, the urn was empty. The desk was told and after about 10 minutes someone came out. However, they did not bring coffee but rather looked in urn, said it was empty and that would return. After another 10 minutes with no coffee I gave up. Later when attempting to get coffee, their was coffee but no cups. Similar scenario - no one showed up with cups after they were requested.

I do not know anything about running a hotel but I don't think this is the way it should be done. If I were running it, though, not only would I make certain there was coffee the morning after a fire alarm caused the building to be evacuated, I would have some danish or bagels, too, to let customers know we actually do care.

### Great location, average stay

9 days ago

This hotel gets 5 stars on the location as it's right in the middle of it all! The lobby is nice but the rooms are missing the finishing details of accent pillows or throws. The rooms are also generally dark. Staff was nice and friendly for the most part. The bellman was really hit and miss all week long. This is an older building with a lot of charm but the "luxury" is missing. From basic shower heads and toiletries to the lack of additional bedding comforts like throws and accent pillows it just felt like a nicer version of the holiday inn. I'll try the Sofitel across the street next time.
