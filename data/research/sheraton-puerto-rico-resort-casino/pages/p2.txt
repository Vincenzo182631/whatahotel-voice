# Connection, Ease, Balance - Suites & Rooms

## Accommodations

Set in the heart of San Juan’s District, our rooms and suites are designed for effortless comfort, blending plush details, thoughtful space, and views that connect you to the island.

### Explore Our Rooms and Suites and Find the Perfect Match for Your Stay in San Juan

## Presidential Suite

Our two-bedroom Suite, Designed for Elevated Stays. Featuring generous living and dining areas, and a private terrace with bay views, this suite offers the space and privacy to settle in comfortably ideal for longer stays and connections.

## San Juan Suite

Our second-largest suite offers 1,300 square feet of thoughtfully designed space, featuring a separate living and dining area with sofa bed, walk-in pantry, and one and a half bathrooms with views of the San Juan Bay and Convention District. This spacious accommodation is perfect for family gatherings.

## Bayview Suites

This one-bedroom suite features a separate parlor area and private balcony overlooking the San Juan Bay, along with modern essentials like a wet bar, mini fridge, and the Sheraton Signature Bed for a seamless, comfortable stay.

## Deluxe Room

Deluxe Rooms offer a stunning view of the San Juan Bay with a private balcony, high speed internet access, mini fridge, 55" LCD TV, coffee machine with coffee and tea, safe with lock key card system, and supremely soothing Sheraton Signature Bed.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino?

The largest occupancy room at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino is the Presidential Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino?

Some rooms at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have hair dryers.
