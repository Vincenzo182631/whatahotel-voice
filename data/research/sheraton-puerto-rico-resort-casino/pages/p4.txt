### Explore Old San Juan

Discover Old San Juan, known for its blue cobblestone streets and historic buildings dating back to the 16th and 17th centuries.

## San Juan Beaches

Spend the day by the turquoise waters of Escambron Beach, just one mile away from our Resort, where soft sand and open skies create an easy escape just minutes from the resort.

## San Juan Nightlife

From lively nightclubs to laid-back lounges, San Juan comes alive after dark with salsa, live music, and local spots where you can experience the island’s rhythm up close.

Plaza Las Américas

Plaza Las Américas is the premier shopping center in the Caribbean, and it is found just minutes from our San Juan Resort. It boasts over 300 shops from world-famous department stores and high-end clothing boutiques to specialty stores.

The Mall of San Juan

The only Saks Fifth Avenue and Nordstrom department stores in Puerto Rico are found at The Mall of San Juan. Explore exquisite dining, entertainment and upscale retailers. This shopping center features architecture that reflects the rich culture and heritage of San Juan.

Museo de Arte de Puerto Rico

The museum’s permanent collection features works by Latin American artists from the 17th century onward. It includes everything from paintings to mixed media installations, this museum features the “Obras Maestras” and masterpieces from Puerto Rican artists like José Campeche and Francisco Oller.

Bahia Beach Resort & Golf Club

Bahia Beach Resort & Golf Club is committed to preserving the natural integrity of the site, which features undulating fairways, scenic lakes, breathtaking ocean views and world-class play for golfers of all abilities. Positioned on 483 acres of pristine coconut palms and native maritime forest, this golf course is within easy driving distance of our San Juan Resort.

Other Attractions

Our hotel provides easy access to a wide variety of attractions, plus transportation options like the cruise port and local airports, helping you maximize your time in San Juan, Puerto Rico.

DISTRITO T-Mobile

Puerto Rico's premier entertainment destination, the El Distrito San Juan combines dining, music, technology and attraction-based entertainment. The party begins Winter 2019.
