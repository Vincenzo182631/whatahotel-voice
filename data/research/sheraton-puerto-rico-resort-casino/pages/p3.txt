### Frequently Asked Questions

What restaurants does Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have on property?

The on-property restaurants at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino are:

Ocean LoungeThe CaféIn-Room DiningChoicesMutuo Sabor Nikkei The Bay Rooftop Bar

Does Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino offer room service?

No, room service is not offered at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino. You can view other dining options on our dining page.

What cuisine is offered at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino?

The cuisines offered at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino are:

American at The Bay Rooftop BarMultiple cuisines at In-Room DiningAsian-Fusion at Ocean Lounge, Mutuo Sabor NikkeiCoffee House at The CaféInternational at Choices

Does Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino offer breakfast?

Yes, breakfast is available onsite at Choices for a fee. Breakfast is also available through in-room dining for a fee.
