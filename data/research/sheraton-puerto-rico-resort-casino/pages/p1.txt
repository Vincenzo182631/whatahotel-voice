# Welcome to Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino

## Stay at the most convenient urban San Juan Casino Resort.

Experience the rhythm of the capital at the Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino, the premier gathering place perfectly positioned in the thriving Convention District just steps from Distrito T-Mobile...See More and the Convention Center. Sophisticated guestrooms with a clean, crisp aesthetic provide a modern space before you soak in the sun at our iconic rooftop infinity pool featuring panoramic views of San Juan Bay. Whether you are testing your luck at our 24/7 casino, enjoying exceptional dining experiences, unwinding at our full-service spa, or taking our complimentary shuttle to the beach or Old San Juan, we provide the effortless connections and local proximity that make us the top-rated choice for an urban San Juan escape.See Less

DISTRITO T-Mobile

DISTRITO T-Mobile is set to showcase Puerto Rico's culture and talent through various entertaining and crafted experiences for all guests, a one-of-a-kind hub that will feature the best of culinary experiences, entertainment, music, technology and hospitality.

Main facilities:

Central Plaza: a futuristic 50,000 square feet that aims to be San Juan´s new heart, featuring high tech services and amenities including a 14,000 square foot LED screen – one of the largest in the U.S.

TOROVERDE Urban Park:

Bringing all the fun of the mountains of Orocovis to San Juan with indoor and outdoor adventures, featuring ziplines that gives an amazing aerial tour of DISTRITO T-Mobile as well as other fun activities such as Bull Maze and arcade games.

Caribbean Cinemas VIP:

The ultimate theater experience with laser projectors, oversized luxury recliners and a bar. Moreover, the movie theater will feature ScreenX, a multi-projection technology with a 270-degree panoramic screen.

Coca-Cola Music Hall:

A 129,000-square-foot concert facility that can be described as a state-of-the-art venue unlike any other. The Coca-Cola Music Hall can host award shows, concerts, theater plays, corporate meetings, family shows, among other activities.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino?

The check-in time at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino. Self parking is available at Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino, with rates of $5.00 per hour, and $20.00 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino. The daily valet fee is $28.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino?

The closest airport near Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino is Isla Grande Airport (SIG). SIG is located approximately 1.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have an airport shuttle to Isla Grande Airport (SIG)?

No, Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino does not have an airport shuttle to Isla Grande Airport.

Does Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
