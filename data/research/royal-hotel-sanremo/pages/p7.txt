# Royal Hotel

Since 1872 we have endeavoured to offer you a work of art, as Oscar Wilde used to say: the magnificence of the past and the comfort of the present combined together for your pleasure.

Each sense is stimulated: the sight by the subtropical park, the swimming-pool designed by Gio Ponti and the sea in front; the smell by the scent of flowers and the fragrances of our cuisine; the hearing by the silence enhanced by the sounds of nature; the taste by the flavors of our chefs; the touch by the immersion in an ambience designed to lighten your body and your mind.

**1\. WHAT ARE COOKIES?**

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your electronic device (PC, tablet, smartphone, etc.) while you browse a website and that provide certain information. Cookies are then sent back to the originating website during each subsequent visit, or they are sent to a different website that is able to recognize that specific cookie.

Cookies act as a memory for a website, allowing websites to recognize your device each time you visit that same site. Cookies also allow your browsing preferences to be stored, offering you a more functional experience on the websites and making the content as personalized as possible.

Web browsers allow you to exercise some control over cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies or block cookies from specific websites. Browsers can also help you delete cookies when you close the browser. However, you should keep in mind that this may mean that any opt-outs or preferences you have set on the Sites will be lost. We encourage you to consult the technical information for your browser for instructions. If you choose to disable the setting of cookies or refuse to accept a cookie, some parts of the service may not function properly or may be significantly slower.

**2\. TYPES OF COOKIES AND PURPOSES**

Cookies can be technical, statistical/analytical, or marketing cookies. Specifically:

- The technical cookies used by our Sites serve to enable and facilitate your browsing, as well as to provide you with and allow you to use the Sites’ services;
