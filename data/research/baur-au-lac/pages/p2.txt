## The essentials, at a glance

### Where is Baur au Lac located?

Baur au Lac is centrally located in the heart of Zurich, nestled in a private park right on the shores of Lake Zurich. Just a short walk away are the famous Bahnhofstrasse, Bürkliplatz, and the historic Old Town, home to cultural institutions such as the Tonhalle and the Opera House.

### What makes Baur au Lac such a special retreat?

Since 1844, Baur au Lac has been synonymous with timeless elegance and discreet hospitality. This family-run 5-star hotel in Zurich is positioned in the ultra-luxury segment and is now in its seventh generation of management. Every room and suite is individually designed and offers views of the private park, the city or – depending on the category – Lake Zurich and the Alps. The experienced concierge team ensures that guests’ wishes are often fulfilled before they are even voiced.

### Are there any exclusive gifts or vouchers available?

Yes, Baur au Lac offers a selection of personalised gift ideas for special occasions. In addition to gift vouchers and restaurant vouchers, which can be redeemed flexibly for stays or culinary experiences, the Baur au Lac Shop stocks a carefully curated range of the hotel’s own products. You will find fine wines, premium spirits and delicacies in the separate wine shop, Baur au Lac Vins.

### What category does Baur au Lac fall into?

Baur au Lac is a family-run 5-star luxury hotel in Zurich, positioned in the ultra-luxury segment. The hotel is a member of The Leading Hotels of the World and Swiss Deluxe Hotels, making it one of Switzerland’s most prestigious hotels. Since its opening in 1844, the hotel has been run by the same family – now in its seventh generation. Furthermore, Baur au Lac has been awarded the prestigious three keys by the Guide Michelin for two consecutive years and was named Best City-Hotel by the Falstaff Travel Guide.

### What services does Baur au Lac offer its guests?

Baur au Lac in Zurich offers a service focused on comfort, discretion and personalised care. This includes, amongst other things, a 24-hour concierge, a limousine and transfer service from the airport or main railway station, valet and garage services, as well as a twice-daily housekeeping service. In addition, the concierge team arranges bespoke experiences – such as private trips on Lake Zurich aboard the nostalgic “Tugenia”, cultural recommendations or special bookings in the city. Depending on the room or suite category, a minibar stocked with selected drinks is included in the stay.

### Is the hotel suitable for families with children?

Yes. Baur au Lac, of course, warmly welcomes families too. The hotel is ideal for multi-generational stays. Rooms and suites with connecting doors offer space for spending time together whilst also providing privacy. Special attention is paid to our youngest guests: with plenty of care, small gestures and attention to detail, the team ensures that children feel just as welcome as adults. The hotel’s own soft toys, Leo and Eli, add to the fun, charmingly keeping both young and old company.

### Are dogs allowed?

Yes, dogs are very welcome at Baur au Lac, at a cost of CHF 45 per dog per day. With custom-designed beds, Baur au Lac bowls, and a special dog menu, your four-legged companion enjoys the same comfort as you. Please note that dogs are not permitted in our restaurants, with the exception of the Marguita terrace.
