## The Adventure Hub

At The Adventure Hub, young explorers can embark on a journey of fun and discovery. As part of our Family Traditions programme, children can enjoy a wide range of interactive activities designed to inspire creativity and adventure. From arts and crafts to exciting outdoor games, The Adventure Hub offers a safe and engaging environment for children to create unforgettable memories.

Wellness

### Guerlain Spa

Nestled within our resort, Guerlain Spa blends indulgence and serenity. With 11 treatment rooms, including 2 couple suites, and wet facilities featuring steam rooms and saunas, it offers a private sanctuary for soothing rejuvenation. The spa showcases Guerlain’s makeup, perfumes, and skincare collections, along with a captivating scent bar. Guerlain Spa presents a bespoke wellness menu inspired by Omani philosophy and spirits, promising unparalleled immersion. At Guerlain Spa, every moment is an unforgettable experience, enveloping you in pure relaxation.

Recreation

### Watersports

Discover an array of watersports at our resort, including paddleboarding, kayaking, and snorkeling. Explore vibrant marine life and crystal-clear waters. Additional activities are available upon request, such as sunset cruises along Muscat’s coast, diving, and dolphin-watching. Create unforgettable memories in this idyllic setting

### Padel tennis

Experience the thrill of padel at our resort’s rooftop court, set against the backdrop of our iconic architecture. Whether you’re a seasoned player or new to the sport, the rooftop padel court provides a vibrant and engaging environment for all skill levels. This unique facility offers an exciting way to stay active while enjoying panoramic views of the surrounding landscape.

### Golf

Just a few steps away from our resort, the award-winning Al Mouj Golf Course, designed by golfing legend Greg Norman offers stunning views of the Indian Ocean and the Hajar Mountains, and provides a picturesque and rewarding experience for golfers of all levels. Stretching along a six-kilometer coastline, this par-72 championship course offers lush green fairways, challenging bunkers, and natural dunes.

Fitness

## Gym Memberships

Experience the pinnacle of fitness and luxury at The Fit Lounge, located at the heart of our resort. Three exclusive membership tiers are available: Silver, Gold, and Platinum, each designed to cater to unique fitness journeys. All memberships include unlimited access to the state-of-the-art gym, featuring both female-only and mixed zones. Whether maintaining a routine or aiming to elevate fitness, The Fit Lounge provides an inspiring and luxurious environment to achieve wellness goals.

Muscat Destination Guide

### Sultan Qaboos Mosque

A true architectural marvel, the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque stands as a symbol of Oman's spiritual and artistic heritage. Its intricate mosaics, majestic chandeliers, and grand prayer hall invite admiration, offering visitors a serene glimpse into the country’s devotion and craftsmanship.

### Muttrah Corniche

Stretching along the serene waterfront, Muttrah Corniche blends old-world charm with coastal beauty. Lined with traditional markets, fishing boats, and sea-view promenades, it offers a captivating stroll through Oman’s maritime soul, framed by the elegance of the Arabian Sea.

### Royal Opera House

An icon of artistry and elegance, the Royal Opera House Muscat embodies Oman’s cultural sophistication. Showcasing world-class performances in a setting of architectural grandeur, it offers an exquisite experience where tradition harmoniously meets contemporary refinement and the universal language of music.

Destination

### Experience Oman

Oman, known for its warm hospitality and delicious cuisine, seamlessly blends tradition and modernity, making it a captivating destination for all travelers. This treasure trove of natural beauty and cultural richness offers myriad experiences, from exploring stunning marine life at the Damaniyat Islands Nature Reserve to hiking in lush wadis with clear pools. Discover the vibrant heritage of Muttrah's...See More old town and souq, admire the exquisite Islamic design of the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, and embark on adventures in the rugged landscapes of Jebel Akhdar and Jebel Shams mountains.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort have an on-site spa?

Yes, Guerlain Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort?

Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, Royal Opera House Muscat, Al Alam Palace, Mutrah Souq, Nizwa Fort and more are nearby The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort.
