# Relax and Enjoy the Stunning Views From Our Muscat Suites

## Accommodations

Crafted for unforgettable getaways in Oman, our Muscat accommodation features 250 luxurious rooms and suites, including 49 suites and 201 rooms with ocean or city views. Designed for couples, families, business travelers, and golf stays, our 1, 2, and 3-bedroom duplex suites offer expansive space for cherished family moments, with select views of the ocean or city. Guests often highlight the spacious, elegant design, balconies, immaculate cleanliness, and modern facilities. Enjoy exceptional service from the St. Regis Butler, ensuring an unparalleled resort experience.

Ideal for extended stays or family vacations, our 1, 2, and 3 bedroom duplex suites provide the perfect backdrop for treasured family moments. St. Regis Traditions

### Family Traditions

Family Traditions offers engaging activities and thoughtful experiences for younger guests and their families. This program lets travelers of all ages explore Oman’s rich culture and stunning landscapes while celebrating the art of play. From guided tours and cultural workshops to fun adventures and creative crafts at the Adventure Hub, Family Traditions ensures every moment is memorable and enriching.

### Butler Service

At The St. Regis, our butler service epitomizes luxury and personalized attention. Led by our Executive Butler Ibrahim Mabrouk, our butlers are available around the clock to ensure your experience is seamless and memorable. Indulge in the timeless elegance and exceptional service that The St. Regis is renowned for, in the stunning setting of Oman.

### Afternoon Tea

The enchanting tradition of Afternoon Tea invites guests to gather and socialize, drawing inspiration from the lavish parties once hosted by Caroline Astor. At The Lounge, in the heart of the resort, guests can now indulge in our own interpretation of this timeless tradition. Whether choosing to sit indoors or outdoors with views over the pool, this experience offers a perfect blend of elegance and relaxation.

## The Royal Suite

Welcome to The Royal Suite, the epitome of luxury and sophistication. This expansive suite offers breathtaking ocean views and is adorned with a coastal palette of navy, white, and tan, evoking the elegance of a superyacht. The meticulously designed interiors reflect refined taste and attention to detail. The extensive terrace, complete with jacuzzis, lounge areas, a dining table, and a private swimming pool, seamlessly wraps around the suite, providing direct access from the dining area, living room, bedroom, and bathroom. Additional features such as walk-in wardrobes, elegant bathrooms, and plush furnishings further enhance the opulent resort experience.
