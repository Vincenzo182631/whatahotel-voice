## Suites

The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort offers a luxurious retreat with 250 elegantly designed rooms and suites, each thoughtfully inspired by the elegance of luxury yachts and nautical themes. Located in the prestigious Al Mouj area, our accommodations provide stunning golf and sea views, creating an extraordinary experience for discerning travelers. Each room is meticulously crafted with sophisticated décor and modern amenities, ensuring both elegance and comfort. Whether you're visiting for business or leisure, our resort serves as a tranquil oasis amidst the vibrant energy of Muscat, complemented by our renowned St. Regis Butler service and world-class dining options.
