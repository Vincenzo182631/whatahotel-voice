# A Vibrant Culinary Destination

## Dining

Indulge in a world of culinary artistry across a distinguished collection of dining venues. Experience fine Cantonese cuisine at Hakkasan, authentic Lebanese flavors at Em Sherif, elegant Italian dining at Roberto's, vibrant Peruvian cuisine at COYA, Mediterranean favorites at Novikov Café, French patisserie and café culture at Fauchon, the classic French brasserie experience of Entrecôte Café de Paris, contemporary Japanese cuisine at Nozomi, and the relaxed Riviera-inspired beach atmosphere of Vilebrequin La Plage. Complementing these destinations are our signature restaurants: Karibu, an Omani restaurant inspired by the spice route with Tanzanian and Indian influences, and Zorba, serving authentic Greek cuisine crafted by our Greek chef. The Stage Bar & Lounge and The Lounge, home to an elegant Afternoon Tea experience, complete the collection, offering a diverse range of flavors, settings, and memorable dining experiences.

## Zorba

A lively haven where we take pleasure in offering Mediterranean-inspired lunch and dinner dishes by the enchanting seafront. Chef Thanos infuses each dish with passion and authenticity, recreating his grandmother's cherished recipes from the Greek Islands.
