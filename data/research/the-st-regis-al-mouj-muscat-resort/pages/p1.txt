# Welcome to The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort

## Experience the elegance of our Muscat beach resort

Stretching along a 360-meter beachfront, The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort graces the shores of the Sultanate of Oman as a five-star resort in Muscat, where our guests can experience in refined style. This luxury beach resort in Muscat features 250 guest rooms and suites with sweeping beachfront and golf course views. A refined collection of amenities enhances each stay, including a beautifully positioned swimming pool, a Vilebrequin beach experience, an adjacent award-winning golf club, a luxurious Guerlain Spa, and thirteen distinctive dining venues such as COYA, Em Sherif, and Hakkasan. For couples, families, golf travelers, and business guests, this Muscat resort offers a prime location in Al Mouj near the marina, airport, opera house, and islands. Muscat’s largest ballroom, the 1,350-square-meter St. Regis Grand Ballroom, offers state-of-the-art facilities, three divisible sections, a daylight-filled pre-function area, and ten additional meeting rooms for MICE events, weddings, and celebrations.

World Class Dining Experiences

### Karibu

Karibu invites guests on a captivating culinary journey through Oman and Zanzibar. With authentic interiors and a menu inspired by the rich flavors of the Spice Route, it celebrates vibrant traditions and regional hospitality. Throughout the week, themed nights bring each culture to life through flavor, rhythm, and atmosphere.

### Zorba

A celebration of Greek culture and coastal living, Zorba brings the spirit of the Aegean to Muscat. Its Mediterranean ambiance, sea views, and fresh seasonal creations evoke the charm of a Greek island escape—where every meal feels like a joyful gathering by the sea.

### The Stage Bar & Lounge

The Stage Bar & Lounge is the resort’s distinguished social destination, where live music and refined mixology meet effortless sophistication. From curated themed evenings to intimate gatherings, it offers an atmosphere of rhythm, elegance, and connection at The Best Address in Muscat.

Recreation

### Watersports

Discover an array of watersports at our resort, including paddleboarding, kayaking, and snorkeling. Explore vibrant marine life and crystal-clear waters. Additional activities are available upon request, such as sunset cruises along Muscat’s coast, diving, and dolphin-watching. Create unforgettable memories in this idyllic setting.

### Padel Tennis

Experience the thrill of padel at our resort’s rooftop court, set against the backdrop of our iconic architecture. Whether you’re a seasoned player or new to the sport, the rooftop padel court provides a vibrant and engaging environment for all skill levels. This unique facility offers an exciting way to stay active while enjoying panoramic views of the surrounding landscape.

### Golf

Just a few steps away from our resort, the award-winning Al Mouj Golf Course, designed by golfing legend Greg Norman offers stunning views of the Indian Ocean and the Hajar Mountains, and provides a picturesque and rewarding experience for golfers of all levels. Stretching along a six-kilometer coastline, this par-72 championship course offers lush green fairways, challenging bunkers, and natural dunes.

## A Commitment to Sustainability

The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort is dedicated to sustainability and social responsibility, integrating several practices across our operations. We strive to source sustainable and local ingredients, eliminate plastic straws, and replace small toiletry bottles with larger dispensers to reduce waste. Committed to Marriott International’s Serve 360 program, we aim to cut our environmental footprint by 50% by 2025 and achieve net-zero emissions by 2050. Additionally, we actively support our local community through various initiatives.

### Discover Oman

Oman offers a wealth of enriching experiences, from exploring the vibrant capital city of Muscat with its stunning Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque and bustling Mutrah Souq, to the breathtaking natural beauty of Wadi Shab or Bimmah Sinkhole. immerse yourself in the rich cultural heritage of Nizwa, marvel at the dramatic landscapes of Jebel Shams and Jebel Akhdar, and experience the vast, golden dunes of the Sharqiya Sands desert. Whether it’s diving in the crystal-clear waters of the Daymaniyat Islands or witnessing the ancient fortresses of Bahla, Oman promises unforgettable adventures.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort is Muscat International Airport (MCT). MCT is located approximately 8.1 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Al Mouj Muscat Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
