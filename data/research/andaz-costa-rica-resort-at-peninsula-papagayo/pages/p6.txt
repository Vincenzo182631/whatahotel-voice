## Transportation

Getting here

Guanacaste International Airport (LIR)32 kilometers32 kilometers

Andaz Peninsula Papagayo Resort, Costa Rica is only 35 minutes from Liberia International Airport (LIR) with more than 20 airlines servicing this airport.

Transportation Options

Private Transfer Service

Please contact gscenter.papagayo@andaz.com to inquire about pricing for our private transfer with service directly from Guanacaste International Airport to Andaz Costa Rica Resort.

Airport Taxis

Taxis are always readily available from the airport and reservations are not necessary. Tip: Make sure to negotiate a fare with the driver before your departure.

Rental Cars

Rental cars can be arranged at the resort. Additionally, there are several well-known rental car companies located inside of the Guanacaste Airport in Liberia. A reservation is highly recommended.

San Jose (SJO)236 kilometers236 kilometers

Arrivals into San Jose (SJO) requires a 50-minute connecting flight on SANSA Airlines, which offers multiple daily flights and charter options, or a 3.5-hour drive.

Getting around

Shuttle

We offer two complementary transportation options from the resort to our beach club, Casa de Playa: You may take our boat, Nimbu, departing from Playa Sombrero Oscuro every 30 minutes, or you can use our private shuttle, which departs from the main lobby every 30 minutes to move around Peninsula Papagayo.
