### Beautiful Property, but Lacking in Atmosphere for a Honeymoon

We recently stayed at Andaz Peninsula Papagayo for our honeymoon. While the property itself is beautiful, exclusive, and tucked away in a gorgeous setting, we ultimately felt the experience didn’t compare to other destinations we’ve visited.

The resort has nice amenities and plenty of natural beauty, but we found the overall atmosphere rather boring. There was virtually no ambient music throughout the property, so if you enjoy complete silence and prefer hearing only the sounds of nature, this may be the perfect place for you. However, we were hoping for a little more energy and atmosphere, especially for a honeymoon destination.

We also got the impression that the resort caters primarily to families with young children. As two young adults, we found ourselves with very little to do in the evenings. After dinner, there wasn’t really a lounge or lively bar where we could enjoy a few post-dinner drinks, so most nights ended with us heading straight back to our room.

Interestingly, we found Papagayo Park to be more enjoyable and entertaining than the resort itself. The mixology class and barista class on property were definite highlights and helped make our stay more memorable.

Overall, Andaz is a beautiful property with a lot of potential, but for couples looking for a lively atmosphere, engaging evening activities, and a more romantic social scene, it fell short of our expectations. It may be a better fit for travelers who prioritize tranquility and nature over entertainment and nightlife.

### Beautiful Andaz worth the visit!

The Andaz in Costa Rica is a beautiful resort that manages to feel exclusive and luxurious without being the least bit stuffy. Everything is polished, but the atmosphere is relaxed and welcoming.

The food and drinks were consistently excellent, the pools are gorgeous, and the setting is spectacular. And there are monkeys everywhere, which never gets old! It is definitely a pricey resort, but the experience reflects that.

One thing worth mentioning is that while the resort has an upscale, exclusive feel, it is also very family-friendly. There is an adults-only pool, but children are certainly part of the overall experience. For example, there were always families with kids at breakfast. That didn't bother us a bit, but if you're looking for a completely adults-only atmosphere, this might not be your best choice.

There is also plenty to do without ever leaving the property. We kayaked, hiked, snorkeled, and swam. One of the highlights was a complimentary guided jungle walk with Melanie. She was fantastic, and we learned so much about Costa Rica, its wildlife, and the surrounding environment. I would highly recommend taking advantage of that tour!

Overall, the Andaz strikes a wonderful balance between luxury, adventure, and relaxation. It's a beautiful place to spend a few days, with excellent service, great food, and plenty to explore. We'd happily return!
