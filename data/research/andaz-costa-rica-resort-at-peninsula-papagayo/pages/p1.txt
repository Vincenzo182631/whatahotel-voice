# Discover Paradise at Our Luxury Costa Rica Beach Resort

Relax, reconnect, recharge and stimulate your senses during your stay at our luxury eco resort hotel in Guanacaste, Costa Rica. Offering beautiful villa rentals, three beaches, world-class golf, a beach club, an extensive activity program and distinctive dining experiences within the pristine, private 1,400-acre Peninsula Papagayo, your life-changing journey at our idyllic beach resort awaits.

## Villas, Rooms & Suites

Floor-to-ceiling windows and folding glass doors invite the magic of Costa Rica’s tropical forest into your living space. Luxury villa rentals and suites offer a deep soaking tub, plunge pool or both. A river stone rain shower opens out to a bird’s nest-inspired balcony in each of our rooms and gives you gorgeous views of Culebra Bay that is alongside the Papagayo Peninsula.

## DINING

Enjoy a casual toes-in-the-sand meal at Andaz Beach House or escape to the quiet romance of Ostra, our signature seafood restaurant. Whether you visit Chao Pescao, our neighborhood Latin tapas bar or take in the breathtaking views at Rio Bhongo, Andaz Peninsula Papagayo Resort, Costa Rica and preference.

### OSTRA

Ostra brings the vibrant, aromatic cuisine of Thailand to the shores of Papagayo. The menu celebrates the balance and depth at the heart of Thai cuisine, expressed through a contemporary interpretation inspired by the tropical spirit of Guanacaste. Relaxed, lively, and generous, Ostra pairs authentic Thai flavors with a strong sense of place.​

Details + Reservations

### Chao Pescao

Chao Pescao brings the vibrant flavors of Latin America to life through a fresh, generous menu inspired by the abundance of Papagayo, with live-fire cooking at its heart. ​

### Meso

Explore our newest restaurant and prepare your taste buds for “Mesoterranean cuisine,” an innovative blend of vibrant Mediterranean flavors with a touch of Mesoamerican influence, complemented with refreshing tropical renditions of classic Mediterranean cocktails. Located on Nacascolo beach at Casa de Playa

Details + Reservations

### Gaio

Gaio celebrates the flavors of Southern Italy with handcrafted pastas, artisanal pizzas, local seafood, and a curated wine selection.​
Gaio Bar offers a more relaxed setting from afternoon through the evening, with classic cocktails and easygoing bites—a welcoming spot to meet friends, unwind, and enjoy the night at your own pace.​

Details + Reservations

### Coffee & Vinyls

Coffee & Vinyls serves good vibes and offers an ideal coworking space for our digital nomads. Celebrate Costa Rica’s coffee culture paired with a unique musical experience, inviting guests to select and play a vinyl of their choice. Offering a diverse music selection, specialty coffee, caffeinated cocktails, organic wine and sandwiches. Coffee & Vinyls also has a retail section for on the go essentials.

Details + Reservations

### Eat In

Relax in bed, watch a movie with the kids or just enjoy a romantic dinner for two served on your private balcony. Treat yourself to room service and celebrate the comfort and style of your room or suite.

### Casa de Playa

Discover Casa de Playa, a culinary destination bringing the relaxed spirit and vibrant flavors of the Greek islands to Papagayo. ​
​Set on Nacascolo Beach, its cuisine celebrates the simplicity of Greek cooking through exceptional seafood, grilled meats, fresh vegetables, and seasonal ingredients. Generous and uncomplicated, Casa de Playa is made for leisurely lunches, chilled drinks, and time spent together by the water.​

## Pets Are Welcome

Our pet-friendly hotel, Andaz Peninsula Papagayo Resort, Costa Rica is pleased to accommodate guests traveling with pets. Pets can be walked on leashes around the property, but they are prohibited inside the restaurants. Contact our Guest Services department for more information about our pet program: gscenter.papagayo@andaz.com.
