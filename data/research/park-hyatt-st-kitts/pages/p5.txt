### Beautiful resort but some significant disappointments

We recently stayed at the Hyatt St. Kitts and had a mixed experience. There were definitely some highlights, but overall, the stay did not meet our expectations given the amount of points and money we used for the visit.

The resort itself is beautiful, and the staff we interacted with were friendly and helpful. We appreciated the clean gym and the pools, which our kids absolutely loved. The staff were also very helpful with the kayaks and paddleboards, and we enjoyed being able to snorkel right from the resort and see a variety of fish. These were all great activities for our family.

Dining was our biggest disappointment. While we understand that occasional delays happen, service was consistently slow throughout our stay. One dinner took nearly 3 hours, and it took about 45 minutes to receive two gin and tonics. Staff also seemed unfamiliar with the menu at times. Poolside lunches typically took 45–60 minutes, and we experienced several incorrect or missing items. The food itself was good, but there was not much variety, especially with only the pool bar and The Great House being open.

There was also a significant amount of dried sargassum on the sand, as well as sargassum in the water. We understand that sargassum is a seasonal issue, but more frequent removal from the beach would have made the experience much more enjoyable. We ended up visiting Reggae Beach next door, which had no sargassum. We also had ants on our outdoor porch, which unfortunately made us reluctant to sit outside and enjoy the space.

For dinner, Orchid Bay Beach Bar & Grill was an experience and, while expensive, had some of the best food we had on the island. Spice Mill and Boozie’s on the Beach were also good options outside the hotel and offered a more casual dining experience.

If you have the opportunity, I highly recommend taking the water ferry to Nevis and spending the day exploring the island. We especially enjoyed visiting Pinney’s Beach, where the calm, clean waters were perfect for the kids. It was also easy to snorkel and see plenty of fish, starfish, and clams right in the water. Stay for the sunset and enjoy dinner at the Four Seasons—it made for a wonderful day and was one of the highlights of our trip.

### End of season disappointment

This just isn’t a great property. It’s very spread out, which requires a lot of walking. The beach is not good and has lots of sargassum. Food was mediocre and very expensive. Gym equipment was worn out with most treadmills unusable. Service was decent, but there was not much to deliver on.

The rooms are pretty nice, but nothing spectacular considering the Park Hyatt brand and pricing.

Our 7 and 10 year old said kids club was not fun at all and asked to leave after 1 hour. There were practically no activities when we went in August (which, I know, is end of season).

If you’re going to St. Kitts and Nevis and considering this property, just go to the Four Seasons on Nevis. Infinitely nicer property and beach at a similar price point.

### Nothing short of exceptional!

We just returned from an incredible stay at the Park Hyatt St. Kitts, and I can't say enough good things about this property. From the moment we arrived, it was clear this was a special place — the grounds were immaculate, every corner beautifully maintained, and the staff made us feel welcome from start to finish.

Check-in set the tone for the entire trip. Novellene at the front desk had us checked in quickly and even took a moment to engage with my son, giving him some fish food so he could feed the koi near the lobby. After a 6-hour flight, watching him light up with excitement was exactly what we needed.

Breakfast each morning at the Great House was a genuine highlight — fresh, delicious, and something we looked forward to every day. The concierge team was equally wonderful, helping us book golf and pickleball lessons with zero hassle.

The Sugar Mill Spa deserves special mention. I had a deep tissue sports massage with Yeshim that was nothing short of transformative — she worked out weeks of tension in my neck and shoulders, and I left feeling like a new person.

Our son had an amazing time at the Camp Hyatt kids club at Island Fort, where Kenya took wonderful care of him — he couldn't wait to go back each day. Over at the Recreation hut, CJ was fantastic, helping us with pool floats and patiently guiding us through two pickleball lessons.

One moment really stood out: my son lost his bucket hat on day two, and between security and housekeeping, it was found and returned to us that same day — no small feat at a resort this size with so many guests to look after.

Park Hyatt St. Kitts is a true gem on the far side of the island. We had a wonderfully relaxing vacation and are already looking forward to going back.
