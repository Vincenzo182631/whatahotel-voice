# Restaurants at Fisherman's Village \| Park Hyatt St. Kitts

# Restaurants & Bars

Savor some of the best restaurants in St. Kitts at our resort’s three signature restaurants. Park Hyatt St. Kitts Christophe Harbor serves local and international cuisine and offers various dining options for the entire family. For a luxurious setting with exceptional views, Great House serves breakfast, lunch and dinner. At Fisherman’s Village, dine over-the-water with island-inspired cuisine in a casual atmosphere with ocean views, or seek an elevated dining experience in Stone Barn where Japanese and Peruvian flavors inspire the culinary journey. Delight in light fare and crafted beverages at the pools or on the beach and enjoy in-room dining for your convenience.

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.
