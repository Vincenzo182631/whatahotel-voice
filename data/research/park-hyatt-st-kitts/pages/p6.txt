Pet Policy

Park Hyatt St. Kitts Christophe Harbour is a pet friendly resort that permits dogs and cats weighing up to 50 pounds to visit the property.  Guests are permitted to bring one dog or cat per room which will receive a bed, bowls and treats in their guestroom.  There is a $75US non-refundable cleaning fee per night.

Pets are only permitted in standard 1 King Bed or 2 Queen Beds room types and must be leashed at all times except when confined to the guest room. Pets are not allowed in food and beverage outlets, the spa or the pool areas of the resort. For additional rules and local health regulations regarding traveling into St. Kitts with a pet, please contact the hotel directly.

**Rules:**

1.     Pets are only permitted in standard 1 King Bed or 2 Queen Beds accommodations.

2.     All pet owners must register their pet with the hotel prior to arrival, providing a picture and name of the animal. Service dogs are excluded.

3.     The resort will accommodate your pet in the guestroom, provided that your pet is fully trained, appropriately restrained and complies with local health requirements.

4.     Pets must be kept on a leash in the resort and on the property except when they are in a guestroom.

5.     Pets are not allowed in any of the food and beverage outlets, the spa and the pool areas of the resort.  This exclusion also applies to service dogs with the exception of outdoor dining at food and beverage outlets. Service dogs are not allowed in the spa or at the pool areas.

6.     Guests are responsible for cleaning up after their pets on the resort property and in the local neighborhood area.

7.     Any disturbances such as barking must be curtailed to ensure that other guests are not disturbed or inconvenienced.

8.     Guests must contact the housekeeping department to arrange for a convenient time for the cleaning and servicing of the room.  Unless the owner is present, housekeeping is unable to enter the room when there is a pet guest.  If the pet is in the room while it is being cleaned, the owner must be present and the pet must be leashed.

9.     Guest agree to be responsible for all pet related property damages and personal injuries.  Guests agree to indemnify the resort, its owners and its operator from all liability and damage suffered as a result of a pet guest.  The resort reserves the right to charge the guest account commensurate to the cost of such damages.

OUTSIDE FOOD & BEVERAGE POLICY

**General Restrictions**

Outside food and beverage items are not permitted to be stored in hotel facilities, prepared, heated, or reheated using hotel equipment or by hotel staff. This includes:

1\. Storage of any food items in hotel refrigerators, freezers, kitchens, or any facility other than the in-room mini-bar

2\. Heating, warming, or reheating of any outside food items by hotel staff or using hotel equipment

3\. Use of hotel kitchen facilities, equipment, cookware, utensils, or preparation areas

4\. Requests for dedicated cookware, microwaves, hot plates, or food preparation equipment

5\. Cooking appliances or heating equipment in guest rooms (excluding designated villa kitchens)

6\. External catering services without prior management approval and appropriate fees

7\. Outside food and beverage in pool areas, restaurants, bars, and other public spaces

**No Heating or Preparation Services**

The hotel will not heat, warm, reheat, cook, or otherwise prepare any outside food items. This applies to all food items regardless of dietary, religious, cultural, or personal requirements. Our culinary team is trained to prepare meals meeting various dietary needs and guests are encouraged to contact F&B management in advance to discuss specific requirements.

Exception - Infant Feeding: The hotel will warm sealed, commercially packaged baby food, infant formula, and baby bottles upon request at no charge. This exception applies only to sealed commercial products for infants and does not extend to other outside food items.

**No Hotel Storage**

The hotel will not store any outside food items in hotel refrigerators, kitchens, or any facility. This policy exists to maintain HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) compliance, prevent cross-contamination, ensure proper temperature control documentation, and protect all guests from potential food safety risks. The hotel cannot verify the safety, origin, handling, or storage conditions of outside food items.

**Kitchen Access**

Guest access to hotel kitchen facilities is strictly prohibited in accordance with health code regulations. The presence of non-employees in food preparation areas creates liability concerns and violates HACCP compliance requirements.

**Fire Safety**

Cooking appliances, including but not limited to hot plates, electric grills, portable stoves, and heating elements, are prohibited in guest rooms without designated kitchen facilities. Cooking equipment accounts for a significant percentage of hotel fires and poses an unacceptable safety risk.

2\. Under no circumstances will additional refrigerators or cooling equipment be provided to guest rooms.

3\. Storage capacity is strictly limited to the existing in-room mini-bar unit only.
