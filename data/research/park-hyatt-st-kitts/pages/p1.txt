# Luxury Resort Hotels St. Kitts & Nevis \| Park Hyatt St. Kitts

## Escape to a Secluded Beachfront Caribbean Resort in St. Kitts

Discover the enchantment of St. Kitts where true Caribbean hospitality, vibrant culture and rich history combine for a remarkable stay at our beachfront hotel resort. Our spacious contemporary designed guestrooms, suites and villas offer expansive floor-to-ceiling windows that frame stunning views of the sea and neighboring island of Nevis, seamlessly blending luxury with the timeless charm of St. Kitts. Indulge in curated dining experiences, unwind at the serene spa, or immerse in the resort’s thoughtfully crafted activities and experiences, each designed to captivate and delight.

## Discover Park Hyatt St. Kitts

The Park Reserve Collection

Allow each of your guests the opportunity to relax and retreat in the privacy of their own room or suite while enjoying the seamless convenience of hosting your entire party in a singular complex. With multiple configurations available, the options are endless to ensure every guest’s needs and preferences are thoughtfully accommodated.

### Pets Are Welcome

Park Hyatt St. Kitts Christophe Harbour is a pet-friendly resort for up to one dog or cat per room. Your pets will receive a bed, bowls and treats in the room.

Pets are only permitted in standard 1 King Bed or 2 Queen Beds room types and must be leashed at all times except when confined to the guest room. Pets are not allowed in food and beverage outlets, the spa or the pool areas of the resort. For additional rules and local health regulations regarding traveling into St. Kitts with a pet, please contact the hotel directly.

#### Pet Fees

- Nonrefundable cleaning fee: **$75**

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  50Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 1.
