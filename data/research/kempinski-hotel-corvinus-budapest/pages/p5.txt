# Parking

Safe as houses, the Kempinski Hotel Corvinus Budapest manages its own 24/7 guarded car park in the hotel basement.

### Parking / Valet Parking downtown Budapest

Worry not, your vehicle will remain a short lift ride away on hotel premises, self-parked or valet parked for your convenience and comfort.

Garage capacity: 210 places.

Please note, that vehicles up to 2 meter high and 2,4 meter wide can drive in. In the hotel’s underground garage, there is a parking space reserved for people with reduced mobility on all levels, from which the guest can take the elevator up to the lobby.

**PARKING FEE**

Hourly: HUF 1,400

Daily: **HUF 14,000 (ca EUR 39)**

Monthly: from HUF 154,000 (ca EUR 429) per car

For a long-term parking agreement, kindly contact us directly via e-mail at reception.corvinus@kempinski.com

In the underground car-parking facility of Kempinski Hotel Corvinus, six high-quality, fast 22kW electric chargers are available for public use.

Our guests can access the electric chargers via the ‘Parkl’ app. To use the chargers, they simply need to download the app to their phone and complete the registration. All parking payments, as well as requests for technical and other assistance, are handled directly through the app.

Depending on the capacity of the inverter of the vehicle, the fast chargers require approximately 2 to 5.5 hours of charging time.

Please note that the car park at Kempinski Hotel Corvinus charges a parking fee during the car charging.

\\* Car wash is available at an additional charge, opening hours: Monday to Friday from 8:00 - 16:00, Saturday from 8:00 - 14:00, Sunday: closed.
