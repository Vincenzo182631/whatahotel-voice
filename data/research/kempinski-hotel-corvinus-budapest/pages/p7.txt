# Clothing Services

Kempinski Corvinus’ professional cleaning services restore your clothes’ mint condition, cotton or cashmere.

### Laundry and Dry Cleaning in Downtown Budapest

Delicate cleaning? Express service? Alterations? Special requirements? Kempinski Corvinus' five-star warrant goes all the way and offers quality guarantees on its Laundry & Dry Cleaning Services so you get your clothing back in tip-top shape — and immaculately cleaned.

Pressing, washing, dry cleaning and alterations — we take the utmost care of the finest materials and protect your clothes, be they everyday wear or of the most intricate design.

Should you choose to do your own pressing, Corvinus Rooms and rooms above that category have a flat iron and ironing board hanging in the cupboard. Otherwise, please contact Guest Services, and we will deliver an iron and ironing board to your room.

If you are staying at the hotel, your laundry will be collected. Simply place it in the laundry bag in your room or suite and enclose the completed order form. We offer regular and express service to ensure you receive your laundry back in time.
