# Lady in Red

At the heart of Kempinski is the remarkable people who look after each and every guest attentively and thoughtfully. Let us introduce you to the Lady in Red.

### Lady in Red - The most attentive host

The Lady in Red was conceived as a distinctive figure to embody Kempinski's elegance. During a 2008 general managers’ meeting in Jordan, at a brainstorming session in Wadi Rum, the concept began to take shape – born from a desire to enhance guest relations beyond traditional roles and inspired by the image of a stunning red haute couture dress.

Stephan Interthal, General Manager at Kempinski Hotel Corvinus Budapest, played a key role in bringing this idea to fruition, aiming to create a globally recognised yet locally relevant character.

### Lady in Red – A symbol of genuine connection, now and always

For 15 years, our Lady in Red has welcomed every guest with thoughtful, individual and intuitive assistance. Dressed in striking red, this impeccable host possesses unrivalled knowledge of the hotel and the local area, so please don't hesitate to approach her with any questions.

To enhance personal and bespoke guest care, Kempinski Hotels’ signature and now iconic institution, our Ladies in Red, provides all the guidance and assistance you may need during your stay at our hotel.

Would you like an overview and tour of Kempinski Corvinus Budapest? Do you wish to organise something memorable for a wedding anniversary or birthday? Are you missing extra toiletries or accessories in your room? Do you have a specific comment?

You’ll find our Ladies in Red in the lobby, in a dedicated area, ready to assist you face to face. They address every issue on an individual and private basis, resolving it instantly or referring you to the appropriate professional on our team.  This is our way of ensuring your stay is not only Kempinski-perfect but also tailor-made for you.
