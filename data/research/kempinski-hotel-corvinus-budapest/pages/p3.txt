# In-Room Dining

On business, a break or an adventure, our Private In-Room Dining is on call for you – just the way you like it.

### In-Room Dining - Wide variety of dishes, in the privacy of your room

You don’t have to leave home to enjoy Budapest: absorb yourself in the sweeping vistas of Pest’s waking hours, day buzz or night-lit cityscape and savour Kempinski Hotel Corvinus’ top restaurants’ scrumptious selection of delicacies from our Hungarian and international menus. Stay in, in the privacy and comfort of your home, away from your home, dial room service and enjoy every bite of your long-lasting breakfast in bed, late lunch or candlelit dinner at your own tempo on top of the town, on top of the world.

A charming chocolate night or a romantic tête-à-tête to make the moment super sensational? For those very special moments that mean the world to you but that the world doesn’t need to see, consult our chefs for a bespoke menu that suits the occasion just the way you need or want it. Themed meals or unique events, the restaurant services of Kempinski Hotel Corvinus Budapest will do the thinking, cooking and entertaining – only for you and your companions.

Our In-Room Dining service is available every day as follows:

from 6:00 to 11:00 - breakfast menu only

from 11:00 to 22:30 - full menu.

from 22:30 to 06:00 - night menu (soup, sandwiches and dessert)

To place your order, please call the In-Room Dining Service from your room.
