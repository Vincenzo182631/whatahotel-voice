## Your Home in Tokyo—A Timeless Classic Refined

​We welcome you home, thoughtfully crafting each moment with intuitive care to ensure every stay feels personally tailored. Located on the west side of Tokyo in Shinjuku, Park Hyatt Tokyo occupies the 39th to 52nd floors of Shinjuku Park Tower, a modern Japanese architectural masterpiece designed by Pritzker Prize-winner Kenzo Tange. We invite you to experience a sense of quiet exclusivity—tucked away from Tokyo’s busiest streets, yet deeply connected to its pulse.​

## Discover Park Hyatt Tokyo

Fitness

The fitness and stretching studios flank either side of the pool and feature an extensive selection of Technogym strength training and cardio equipment. Personal fitness trainers are available for an additional fee to assist guests and members, tailoring fitness programs to your needs.

Library

Nestled on the 41st floor, The Library includes more than 2,000 hand-selected books on art, culture, history and more. Each book was thoughtfully curated by the hotel’s interior designer John Morford, creating a warm, sophisticated space that invites quiet reflection and inspires discovery.

Club On The Park

Club On The Park is a two-story skylit oasis with a 2,100-square-meter (22,600-square-foot) spa and fitness center, offering a cosmopolitan retreat where guests-in-residence and private members can work off the stress and frenetic pace of modern city life.

Indoor Pool

The centerpiece of Club On The Park is an 8-by-20-meter (26-by-65-foot) pool with fitness and stretching studios on either side, plus sweeping panoramic views of Tokyo and Mt. Fuji through floor-to-ceiling glass walls.

**Important Notice​**

The swimming pool on the 47th floor will be closed on _Thursday, December 10, 2026_, for annual maintenance. The gym and studio will remain open during regular operating hours.​

- Journey Through Japan

Park Hyatt Tokyo, Park Hyatt Kyoto, and Park Hyatt Niseko Hanazono present **Three Destinations, One Journey with Park Hyatt** — a collection of three distinct experiences designed to showcase Japan through different perspectives. Each hotel presents an immersive offering, a signature cocktail, and a thoughtfully curated gift.

At Park Hyatt Tokyo, you'll enjoy:

  - Immersive Offering: a private architectural experience paying homage to Kenzo Tange, the visionary behind the hotel’s own Shinjuku Park Tower, and Kengo Kuma, whose contemporary work emphasizes lightness, nature, and human scale. Throughout the tour, guests are guided through Tokyo’s most significant landmarks, from Olympic-era icons to nature-centric museums, tracing how design has mirrored Japan’s evolution on the global stage. Between stops, guests explore a quieter side of Tokyo through visits to artisan studios, a private viewing of museum-quality ukiyo-e and a kaiseki meal that reinterprets tradition.

  - Signature Cocktail: The “LIT” cocktail, inspired by the Academy Award–winning film Lost in Translation and its iconic scenes at the New York Bar in Park Hyatt Tokyo, pays homage to the hotel’s cinematic legacy. This delicate, pale cherry blossom–colored drink blends Japanese sake, sakura (cherry blossom) liqueur, peach liqueur, cranberry juice, and fresh lime juice for a light cocktail with a smooth, refreshing, and delicately balanced profile.

  - Curated Gift: An exclusive single malt Japanese whisky by Hinomaru Whisky, matured in bourbon, sherry, and rare sakura (cherry blossom) wood casks; and an original Japanese yukata designed by Masaru Mineo in the hotel’s signature green and drawing on the centuries-old kasuri textile tradition.

Please note that accommodations must be reserved directly with each individual hotel. Terms and conditions apply. **Learn more here.**
