Recognized as one of the premier wellness and fitness destinations, The Lodhi boasts a range of exceptional facilities that are a rarity in Delhi. # Swimming Pool

The 50-meter temperature controlled lap pool offers the perfect setting for both leisurely swims and rigorous training sessions. Whether you’re looking to swim laps for exercise, enjoy a relaxing dip, or simply soak up the peaceful surroundings, the pool caters to every need. With crystal-clear waters and state-of-the-art filtration systems, the pool ensures a refreshing and rejuvenating experience. Dedicated pool attendants are always on hand to ensure your comfort and safety, making it an ideal space for guests seeking both relaxation and active pursuits.
