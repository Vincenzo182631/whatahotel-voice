### KERRY HILL

Kerry Hill, a distinguished Australian architect born in 1943, is celebrated for his refined and contextually sensitive designs that seamlessly merge with local traditions. A graduate of the University of Western Australia, Hill founded Kerry Hill Architects in 1979, and his renowned projects—including The Sukhothai in Bangkok and The Datai Langkawi in Malaysia—have left an indelible mark on the global architectural landscape. Honoured with prestigious awards such as the Royal Australian Institute of Architects Gold Medal, Hill’s work is synonymous with elegance, sophistication, and a deep understanding of cultural contexts.

The Lodhi, Hill’s magnum opus in New Delhi, stands as a testament to his architectural mastery. A harmonious blend of contemporary design and traditional Indian elements, The Lodhi exudes understated luxury. The hotel’s architectural grandeur is defined by its use of rough and smooth stone, expansive ceilings, and intricate jaali screens, creating an atmosphere of serene elegance. A curated selection of contemporary Indian art, in partnership with Apparao Galleries, further elevates the hotel’s ambiance, offering guests an ever-evolving cultural experience. The Lodhi is not merely a hotel; it is a sanctuary of style, culture, and refined luxury, offering bespoke journeys that celebrate the very essence of India.
