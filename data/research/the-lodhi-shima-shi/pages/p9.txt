## Victorian Hi-Tea

Step into an elegant world of tradition and indulgence with our **Victorian High Tea at Elan**, where luxury and heritage converge. Every Friday and Saturday from 4th April, 2025 onwards, join us at Elan to experience a curated selection of both **Indian and Western delicacies**, offering the perfect balance of classic flavors and contemporary flair.

Delight in a set menu with Charlie’s finest offerings, complemented by a lavish buffet and a selection of premium teas, coffees, and an exclusive champagne trolley. Enjoy the serene atmosphere of Elan, with a regular restaurant setup that exudes sophistication and warmth.

**Timings:** 3:30pm – 6:30pm

**Price:** INR 1800++ (Tea/Coffee) , INR 2899++ (With Sparkling Wine)

This refined experience promises an unforgettable afternoon of indulgence, where every sip and bite is a celebration of opulence and taste.
