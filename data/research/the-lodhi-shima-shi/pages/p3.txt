### FHRAI Award

General Manager of the Year (Luxury) – 2024 & 2025

The Federation of Hotel and Restaurant Associations of India (FHRAI) recognises outstanding contributions in the hospitality industry through its annual awards. These awards honour professionals who demonstrate exceptional leadership, operational excellence, and the ability to set new benchmarks in service and guest experience.

Mr. Abhimanyu Singh Lodha’s recognition reflects his visionary leadership and steadfast dedication to elevating luxury hospitality standards. Achieving this distinction for the second consecutive year, he has become the first in the luxury category to do so, marking a rare milestone. This accolade underscores his role in driving innovation, inspiring his team, and consistently delivering world-class experiences that strengthen both the brand and the industry.

### General Manager of the Year 2025 - Luxury Hotel Segment
