# Welcome to The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort

## Luxury Beachfront Residences with Elevated Dining & Spa

Welcome to The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana, an exclusive offering private homes in the heart of the Dominican Republic’s premier coastal destination. Nestled within the secure, scenic community of Cap Cana, these beachfront and golf-view residences offer a lifestyle defined by comfort, beauty, and ease.From expansive penthouses to distinctive Golf Terrace homes, each residence is designed...See More with thoughtful craftsmanship and seamless indoor-outdoor living. Owners enjoy privileged access to celebrated dining, including Nina, led by renowned chef Diego Muñoz, as well as The St. Regis Spa, tranquil pools, and a private beach.Gather with loved ones, relax in quiet seclusion, or explore the best of Cap Cana—all supported by the renowned service St. Regis is known for. Here, home is more than a place—it’s a gateway to unforgettable moments and a life well-lived.See Less

Marriott Bonvoy® Points Eligibility

Please note that stays at The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana are not eligible to earn Marriott Bonvoy® points, and points cannot be redeemed toward a stay, in accordance with Marriott Bonvoy program policy.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort?

The check-in time at The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 30.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort?

On-site parking is available at The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort?

The closest airport near The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort is Punta Cana, Dominican Republic (PUJ). PUJ is located approximately 9.2 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Residences at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
