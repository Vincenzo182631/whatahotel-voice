# Welcome to The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali

## Find your balance at our Ubud, Bali, resort

The Westin Resort and Spa Ubud, Bali, invites guests to a restorative family destination devoted to wellness and personalized attention. From the open lobby and bar, fuel your day on one of three decks boasting views of Ubud Forest and the infinity pool. A sense of well-being extends throughout this Ubud resort, from the 24-hour WestinWORKOUT Fitness Studio to the delights of Heavenly Spa and lifestyle programs at our riverside Wellness Pavilion. Tall Trees elevates ancient recipes into healthy cuisine, while all-day dining at Tabia reflects our Eat Well philosophy through a balanced, wholesome menu. Kids from four to twelve get a dose of adventure with our Westin Family activities. Settle into spacious hotel rooms, suites, and villas filled with natural light and comforts such as a Heavenly Bed, Heavenly Bath, free Wi-Fi, and other essential amenities in Ubud, Bali. Pack light, stay fit, and look forward to a travel experience for the entire family at our Ubud, Indonesia resort.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali?

The check-in time at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali.

What are the parking options at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali?

The closest airport near The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali is I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS). DPS is located approximately 29.4 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
