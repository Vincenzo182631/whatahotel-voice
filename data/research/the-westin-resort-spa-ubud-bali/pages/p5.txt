# Good Travel with Marriott Bonvoy

Opens a new windowOpens a new window

## Experience Good Travel with Marriott Bonvoy

Good Travel with Marriott Bonvoy is a program that offers meaningful travel, allowing you to create a positive impact while exploring and building deeper connections with local communities.

Discover how you can bring this to life by connecting with Balinese culture through local traditions, rituals, and dance experiences at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali.

## Explore the Nature at The Farm

### A Journey into Bali’s Living Culture

Guided by an Explorer Expert, guests are invited to explore the close relationship between Balinese spirituality, community, and nature through sacred temples, gardens, and sustainable farming.

Accompanied by an Explorer Expert, guests are invited to embark on Explore the Nature at The Farm—an experience that reveals the close relationship between Balinese spirituality, community, and nature. The journey begins with guided visits to sacred temples, where guests are introduced to the meaning of daily offerings, rituals, and Balinese cultural values. Guests then continue to the resort’s farm, exploring vibrant gardens, learning about local plants, and observing the gentle rhythm of sustainable farming. Guests may enjoy feeding the animals and, when lucky, collecting fresh eggs or harvesting seasonal plants directly from the garden.

The experience also includes insights into the Melukat purification ritual, providing a deeper understanding of how spiritual cleansing is practiced in Balinese life and the island’s traditional Subak irrigation system.

## Balinese Dance Experience

### An Expression of Spirituality and Community

Guided by local Balinese dance teachers, guests explore an art form deeply rooted in spirituality and community identity through philosophy, movement, and shared learning.

Art enthusiasts can further explore Bali’s cultural expression through a dedicated experience centered on Balinese dance—an art form deeply rooted in spirituality and community identity. Guided by local Balinese dance teachers, guests are introduced to the philosophy behind the dance before learning its signature gestures, eye movements, and body posture. Dressed in traditional attire, guests receive gentle, step-by-step instruction, and on certain occasions may practice alongside local Balinese children, creating a warm and meaningful exchange that celebrates learning, curiosity, and cultural connection. By engaging directly with local Balinese dance teachers, guests help support the community dedicated to preserving and sharing Bali’s dance traditions
