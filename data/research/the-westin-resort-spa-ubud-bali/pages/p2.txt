Do the rooms at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali have a desk for working.

The largest occupancy room at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali is the 2 Bedroom Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Some rooms at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
