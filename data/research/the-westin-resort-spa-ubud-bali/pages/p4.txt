### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Weight Machines, and yoga area, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Whirlpool - Water Jet Aqua Therapy (Outdoor)Infinity Pool (Outdoor)Kids Pool (Outdoor)Whirlpool

Does The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali have an on-site spa?

Yes, Heavenly Spa by Westin is located on-site at The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali?

Taman Saraswati Temple, Serayu Pottery, ATV Quad Ubud, Tegenungan Waterfall, Campuhan Ridge Walk and more are nearby The Westin Resort & Spa Ubud, Bali.
