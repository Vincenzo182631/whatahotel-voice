# About _Maybourne_

## About Us

Maybourne operates some of the world’s most storied and extraordinary bespoke hotels, including Claridge’s, The Connaught, The Berkeley, The Emory, The Maybourne Beverly Hills and The Maybourne Riviera. Maybourne has a history of creating stories of distinction and unique experiences that reflect the individual nature of its properties, its guests and its people, whilst maintaining a timeless and intuitive service style that are the hallmarks of its values.

## Careers at Maybourne

At Maybourne, we’re more than just a team – we’re a blend of talent from across the globe, all driven by a shared dedication to outstanding hospitality. With over 2,500 colleagues speaking more than 50 languages, we celebrate diversity in every department. If you’re ready to grow in a world-class environment, we’d love to meet you.

## The Maybourne Tapestry

The Maybourne Tapestry is the work of up-and-coming illustrator, Martha Walmsley. Based in the north of England, her playful designs are carefully considered, using colour and composition to draw the eye in. Often inspired by themes of nature, culture and belonging, Martha has created a fluid piece that, like our hotels, blends timeless elegance with a contemporary spirit inspired by nature, culture, and a sense of place.
