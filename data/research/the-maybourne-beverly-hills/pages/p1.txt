## In the heart of Beverly Hills

A warm welcome and a cool spirit define The Maybourne Beverly Hills. Blending Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star service with the energy of Beverly Hills, guests can expect culinary creativity and contemporary comfort, all wrapped in a landmark 90210 location, just one block from Rodeo Drive. The Maybourne Beverly Hills is California, through and through.

## Endless Summer

### Rooftop Pool

With breezy Mediterranean beach resort ambiance, teeming with native trees and blooming flowers, delicate hummingbirds add to the natural atmosphere of our idyllic 9th floor pool.
