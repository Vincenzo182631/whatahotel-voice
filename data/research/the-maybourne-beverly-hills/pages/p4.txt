## The World's 50 Best Hotels

In the fourth edition of The World’s 50 Best Hotels, which celebrates hotels that define their industry, Claridge’s and The Connaught were recognised among the world’s finest hotels, with a special nod to The Maybourne Riviera, which was named in the Top 100.

## _The Emory_

### Time well spent

Maybourne’s modern masterpiece hotel in Belgravia, The Emory is situated on a discreet and secluded laneway overlooking Hyde Park. Each of its 61 suites and studios, including the top floor Penthouse, features elegant modernist interiors by a collection of the world’s leading designers.
