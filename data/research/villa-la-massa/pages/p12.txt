# Gastronomic experiences of excellence

At Villa La Massa, cuisine is a sensory journey celebrating the authentic soul of Tuscany. Whether on the terrace overlooking the River Arno, by the pool, or in a frescoed salon, local flavours blend with breathtaking scenery that nourishes both the eyes and the spirit.

Open from April to November

# Il Verrocchio

Il Verrocchio is conceived as an expression of regional gastronomic culture, where cuisine becomes a narrative of territory and its deepest roots.

Its cuisine is a homage to Italian and Tuscan tradition, reinterpreted with elegance: local ingredients give life to dishes that tell the story of the chef’s passion and the richness of the land.

Discover more

Open from April to November

# L’Oliveto

Amid the breathtaking landscapes of the Florentine countryside, L’Oliveto welcomes guests to a former stone hay barn, now transformed into an elegant and intimate poolside bistro, where local ingredients are the true protagonists.

Discover more

Open from April to November
