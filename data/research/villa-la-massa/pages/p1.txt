# At Villa La Massa, each room and suite is a quiet retreat where time slows and beauty unfolds in every detail.

Here, Tuscan elegance is not just seen — it is felt, in the light that dances through windows, the warmth of noble materials, and the hush of perfect stillness.

## The Rooms

Cradled within the estate’s six buildings, our rooms embody the harmony between Renaissance beauty and contemporary comfort — surrounded by a natural landscape that inspires stillness and awe.

### Deluxe

Spacious and filled with light, Deluxe Rooms open onto natural paintings: olive trees, cypresses and lemon groves grace each morning with poetic charm. Classic furnishings, marble bathrooms and Tuscan accents create a tranquil refuge for those seeking authenticity and peace.

30 m²Starting from 710 € per night

### Prestige

Wrapped in a soft Renaissance glow, Prestige Rooms feature boiserie, frescoes and linen fabrics. Elegant and generous in space, they offer sweeping views over the gardens and the Arno — inviting the gaze to wander in search of beauty.

40 m²Starting from 830 € per night

Discover more

## Exclusive

Set in the quiet of the Limonaia building, Exclusive Rooms reimagine Tuscan soul through a contemporary lens. Expansive windows, natural fabrics and refined marble bathrooms are complemented by private gardens or terraces overlooking the river — for moments of absolute comfort and serenity.

40 m²Starting from 940 € per night

Discover more

## The Suites

Generous in space and thoughtfully designed, our suites are refined sanctuaries for families or friends seeking a truly memorable stay. Overlooking the park, each suite invites rest, beauty and connection — in a setting that feels both noble and deeply personal.

### Junior Suite Top

Each Junior Suite Top is a world of its own, adorned with artisanal textiles and period details. A quiet realm of elegance, crafted to honour the most authentic expression of Florentine hospitality.

50 m²Starting from 1.220 € per night

### Suite

Each Suite, unique in style and atmosphere, blends understated luxury with warm simplicity. Cosy living rooms, Tuscan craftsmanship and views across the park create an experience to savour and remember.

### Suite Colonica

Located in the 19th-century Casa Colonica farmhouse, these suites celebrate rural Tuscany through a modern lens. Wood, terracotta, Santafiora stone and vibrant textiles create a warm, authentic atmosphere with spacious lounges and jasmine-scented courtyards.

79 m²Starting from 2.850 € per night

### Suite Exclusive

Spacious and refined, the Exclusive Suites echo centuries of elegance. Wood panelling, fine linens, exposed beams and frescoes converse with contemporary comfort. Outside, the Tuscan landscape paints each morning with golden light and soft silence.

55 m²Starting from 1.750 € per night

## Parco Suite

At the heart of Il Villino, the Parco Suite is the epitome of Tuscan relaxation. Two bedrooms, noble materials, elegant fabrics and enchanting views over the Arno make this an intimate, romantic escape.

Tucked away in the estate’s most private corner, the Arno Suite opens onto a secluded garden of stillness overlooking the river. A stone fireplace, lilac tones, marble bathrooms and riverside views create a retreat of pure intimacy and romance.
