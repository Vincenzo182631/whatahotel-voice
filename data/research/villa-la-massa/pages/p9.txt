# Florence, a place to call home

Defined by harmonious architecture and meticulous attention to detail, the Suite Exclusive welcomes guests into luminous spaces with elegant living rooms separated from the sleeping area. These suites are ideal for precious moments with family or friends, offering a peaceful retreat with enchanting views over ancient olive trees, slender cypresses and the gentle flow of the River Arno.

Each suite is a world of its own: in the Villa Nobile, Il Villino and Il Mulino, boiserie, linen drapes and frescoed ceilings evoke the noble Florentine art of living. In the Limonaia, Tuscan style is reimagined with a contemporary flair. Marble bathrooms, artisanal fabrics and finely crafted furnishings create an atmosphere of warmth and elegance, where every guest feels perfectly at home.
