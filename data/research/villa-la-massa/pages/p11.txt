# Enchanting views over the river

A microcosm of elegance on the banks of the Arno, the Presidential Suite unites the noble spaces of the Arno Suite with the modern comfort of the Junior Suite Top, in perfect harmony. Spacious interiors and secluded corners create a stay infused with quiet charm and the fragrance of jasmine.

The Presidential Suite offers two expansive bedrooms, refined marble bathrooms, and a welcoming lounge where convivial moments are shared beside the fireplace. Outside, a private riverside terrace unfolds across 270 m² of pure poetry: the perfect stage for romantic moonlit dinners, accompanied by the sounds of nature and the gentle flow of the water.
