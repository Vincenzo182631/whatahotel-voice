# Welcome to The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius

## A Glamorous Haven on the Iconic Beach of Le Morne

Set against the dramatic backdrop of the storied, UNESCO-listed Le Morne mountain, on one of the Indian Ocean's most iconic beaches, The St. Regis Le Morne stands among the most distinguished luxury resorts in Mauritius—an invitation to Live Exquisite and experience the true refinement of island living. With its striking colonial-inspired architecture and seaside manor atmosphere, this 5-star hotel in Mauritius is a place where time seems to stop, allowing the senses to transcend. The sense of place envelops you like a delicious, heady scent. This all-suite, all-butler glamorous hideaway has long been appreciated by its legion of Luminaries. More than a Mauritius resort, it is the Best Address to toast and celebrate the quintessential, the rare, and the impeccable. Whether marking a honeymoon or romantic escape, a family retreat or a journey of wellness, every stay unfolds with the grace of St. Regis Butler Service, the celebrated ritual of Sabrage at Sunset, and the timeless serenity of Le Morne beach.

### Festive 2026 - The Season of Light

From 20 December to 8 January, our Season of Light follows the rhythm of the west coast sky. The day softens, the beach gathers, and crystal catches the last glow of the sun. You won’t find excess here. Instead, lanterns line the sand, fire bowls warm the shoreline, and music drifts lightly through evening air, never competing with the sea. At The St. Regis Le Morne, we don’t rush the light. We let it lead.

### Families & Young Luminaries

Family Traditions at The St. Regis Le Morne offers engaging activities and thoughtful experiences tailored to younger guests and their families. Whether it's our Kite Flyers Kids Club or our family-bonding Chef's Table and cooking class, we give travelers of all ages the opportunity to explore the island and celebrate the art of play. The St. Regis Le Morne is an accredited member of The Luxury Childcare Association developed by Worldwide Kids, the leading specialist with over 35 years of experience which ensures all member hotels deliver professional childcare with clear requirements for quality and safety.

## The Kite Flyers Club

The Kite Flyers Club is a vibrant retreat for young explorers aged 4 to 12, blending color, comfort, and adventure. Attentive childcare hosts guide children through a thoughtfully designed space, filled with engaging activities and enriching toys. Outdoors, a shaded playground with a climbing frame and shallow pool invites joy and discovery beneath tropical skies. Open daily from 09:00 to 22:00. Babysitting services for infants and toddlers are available for an additional fee. We recommend advance reservations to ensure a seamless experience.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius is Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU). MRU is located approximately 57.4 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
