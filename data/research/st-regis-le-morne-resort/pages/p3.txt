# Welcome to The St. Regis Le Morne Resort

## Dining

At The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, dining is an exploration of exceptional flavors, artfully crafted to reflect the essence of Mauritius. From refined international cuisine to vibrant island-inspired dishes, our 5-star restaurants in Mauritius offer a range of experiences that are as memorable as they are exquisite. Whether you’re savoring classic Mauritian and French elegance at Le Manoir, enjoying the vibrant flavors of Southeast Asia at Floating Market, or experiencing the modern twists of Indian cuisine at INDYA, each meal is thoughtfully designed to awaken your senses and elevate your stay. Every moment, every flavor, meticulously curated for the discerning palate.

Restaurant Attire

Embrace an island chic attire while dining here. To maintain the elegance of the atmosphere, tank tops, swimsuits, cover-ups, and athletic wear are not suited for our dining venues. This is your time to experience the resort in style.

## Exquisite Dining Experiences

Savor the extraordinary with bespoke culinary experiences in Le Morne, Mauritius. From intimate honeymoons and proposals to innovative mixology sessions and themed dinners, each setting is carefully curated to reflect the best address for dining excellence. Every meal becomes an exceptional occasion, crafted to connect you with the island’s rich heritage and its unparalleled flavors.

### Teppanyaki at Atsuko

The tradition of teppanyaki, originating in Japan in the 1940s, combines "teppan" (iron plate) and "yaki" (grilled). Initially used for meats, fish, and vegetables, it evolved in the 1960s into its now-iconic theatrical style. Our teppanyaki experience brings you a front-row seat to the Chef’s craft, adding an element of excitement and spectacle to your meal, with every dish prepared right before your eyes.
