# Holiday Events at The St. Regis Le Morne Resort, Mauritius

## Festive 2026

There are places where the festive season is decorated. And then there is the St. Regis Le Morne, where it is illuminated. From 20 December to 8 January, our Season of Light follows the rhythm of the west coast sky. The day softens, the beach gathers, and crystal catches the last glow of the sun. And just like that, evening begins. You won’t find excess here. Instead, lanterns line the sand, fire bowls warm the shoreline, and music drifts lightly through evening air, never competing with the sea. Christmas leans into warmth and generosity. New Year’s Eve builds slowly: sunset first, midnight later. And the days in between are designed for indulgence without urgency. At The St. Regis Le Morne, we don’t rush the light. We let it lead.

### The Societies of the Season

December arrives differently at Le Morne. The days stretch a little longer. The sea softens at dusk. And just before the sun slips behind the horizon, there’s a moment, a collective pause, when everything feels perfectly timed. We have built this year’s festive season around that moment. Between the warmth of Christmas and the crescendo of midnight on New Year’s Eve, we’ve created moments that feel elevated but never overstated. Thoughtful. Joyful. And occasionally surprising. We call these dates the Societies of the Season.

### The Candlelight Society 24 December

Christmas Eve begins at dusk. Champagne sabrage against the mountain. Tables dressed in linen and crystal. Refined Mauritian flavours unfolding course by course, paired with seasonal signatures from our chefs. A gentle progression from sunset into candlelit glow. Festive. Elegant. Beautifully measured.

### The Sunset Society 31 December

Midnight is the headline. Sunset is the beginning. Our New Year's Eve Beach Dinner opens at golden hour. Downtempo DJ. Barefoot elegance. Gold-accent dress code. Crystal catching firelight. Silk moving with the breeze. The countdown builds gradually a crescendo of music, champagne, and subtle light choreography beneath the stars. Bold. Elevated. Never chaotic. Exactly how a year should end.

### The Everlight Society 6 January

Orthodox Christmas is celebrated with quiet warmth. The pace softens as family and friends gather around beautifully dressed tables, sharing cherished traditions and generous festive flavours inspired by the season. Candlelight flickers across crystal, conversations linger a little longer, and every toast feels more personal. An evening of togetherness, timeless hospitality and understated elegance.

### A Program Thoughtfully Curated for Families To Celebrate the Season Together

### The Golden Hour Ritual

Every evening, we gather at the foot of Le Morne Brabant for a curated sunset ceremony. Champagne sabrage. Acoustic strings or soulful vocals carried by the wind. A poetic dance on the sand. Illusionist moments.

### Days of Glow

Between the celebrations, the season lingers. Golden hour mixology masterclasses invite guests behind the bar. At sunrise, “Light Within” wellness rituals welcome the day with meditation and yoga at the foot of Le Morne. Creative moments unfold across the resort: aquarelle workshops capturing the island’s light, lantern-making ateliers for young explorers, and artistic encounters that bring colour and rhythm.

### The Luminous Villa Experience

For those who prefer their celebrations more private. A lantern-lit beach dinner prepared exclusively for you. A personal sabrage ceremony at sunset. Bespoke festive turndown details in soft gold. A sega trio or fire dancer appearing beneath the stars, if the evening calls for it. Intimate. Elevated. Entirely yours.
