# The Art of Living Exquisite

## Experiences

At St. Regis Le Morne, guests are immersed in cherished traditions, reimagined for today. Whether marking the start of the season, toasting special occasions, or gathering for simple moments, there are myriad things to do in Mauritius. We offer exquisite experiences that Luminaries can come to expect, whenever and wherever they may be.

## Evening Ritual, Sabrage at Sunset

Inspired by Mrs. Astor’s legendary champagne toasts, we honor the St. Regis tradition with Sabrage at Sunset. Join us on the beach at golden hour for a dramatic champagne-opening ceremony, set against the stunning backdrop of the setting sun. A Christofle-crafted saber adds artistry to the moment. Enjoy vibrant Sega dancers, tales of Mauritius, and the origins of sabrage—a tradition dating back to Napoleon Bonaparte.

## Bloody Mary Ritual, The L’Île Mary

The legendary Bloody Mary experience is reimagined with a vibrant twist, celebrating the island’s culinary heritage. At the stylish St. Regis Bar, an elegant cocktail trolley presents all the ingredients for the signature drink, adding to the sense of occasion. L’Île Mary, our distinctive version, offers a sensory tribute to Mauritius’ rich spices and Creole influences, blending tradition with a uniquely island-inspired flair.

## Afternoon Tea, By the Sea

Set between the breathtaking backdrop of Le Morne and the Indian Ocean, the afternoon tea experience takes place on the beach with a toes-in-the-sand vibe or in the shade of the grand colonial veranda. Savor a curated selection of teas, fragrant herbal blends, and local Bois Chéri offerings, while our chefs present an exquisite array of pastries that celebrate Mauritian ingredients.

The Luxury of Love: A St. Regis Celebration

### Intimate Beach Wedding at The Best Address

Some of the most extraordinary love stories have been celebrated, framed by the stunning silhouette of the UNESCO-listed Le Morne Mountain. Pristine sands, endless horizons, curated floral arrangements, and signature culinary offerings. Your intimate beach ceremony is tailored to reflect your unique style. Each moment is elevated by our expert team, ensuring a celebration as extraordinary as your love.

### Exquisite Wedding Celebration

Say "I do" in a postcard paradise with the majestic Le Morne mountain as the backdrop. Our seaside manor is the ideal spot for celebratory moments. We create weddings tailored to your vision. Whether it’s an exclusive beach reception or a grand celebration with bespoke décor and signature culinary creations, our expert planners ensure every detail reflects the island’s essence.

### House Of Celebration

At The St. Regis Le Morne, the House of Celebration elevates life’s most treasured moments into extraordinary, once-in-a-lifetime experiences. Whether for intimate weddings, enchanting engagements, milestone birthdays, or joyous baby showers, every occasion is transformed into a lavish affair, carefully curated with the exceptional attention to detail that defines the St. Regis legacy.

Experience Le Morne, Mauritius

## Le Morne Leisurely Stroll

Our Luminaries come to St. Regis for a reprieve from daily life, transported to a haven of glamour, timeless rituals, and house comforts. Here, we invite you to do one thing: be at leisure. Embrace the art of relaxation with our specially curated Leisurely Strolls to the stunning slopes of Le Morne Mountain. This guided hike leads to the first plateau, where breathtaking views and lush, tropical landscapes await.

## Exquisite Bathtime

Each suite features a spacious bathtub, inviting moments of pure relaxation. Enhance your ritual with our bespoke bath journey—curated for both adults and young luminaries. Locally sourced scrubs, oils, salts, and aromatherapy candles transform your soak, while luxurious toppings elevate the experience into a sensory escape, steeped in the spirit of Mauritius.

## Family Traditions from Mauritius

Our Young Luminaries program invites children to discover the rich heritage of Mauritius through immersive, hands-on experiences. From crafting traditional gato coco and harvesting tropical fruits to exploring forgotten island games and birdwatching, each moment is thoughtfully designed to inspire curiosity and connect young minds with the island’s vibrant culture.

## The Chef's Table

The Chef’s Table offers an extraordinary dining experience, tailored for those seeking privacy and connection. Set within the refined atmosphere of Le Manoir, this fully exclusive dinner setting ensures complete privacy for guests. Tailored for a couple, a group of friends, or family, this intimate experience is crafted to your exacting preferences.

## Tennis Court

Our complimentary outdoor tennis court is set within the resort grounds and available daily (between 7am - 8pm) for both casual play and focused training. Whether you prefer a relaxed rally or a private coaching session, the space is designed to accommodate your pace. Rackets are provided. Balls may be purchased on property or brought with you. Private Coaching is available on request - an additional fee applies. Call front desk to book.

### Luxury Childcare Association Accredited Member

The Luxury Childcare Association sets the benchmark for childcare in luxury hotels, ensuring consistent standards across the global hospitality sector.

Developed by Worldwide Kids, the leading specialist with over 35 years of experience, this framework ensures all member hotels deliver professional childcare with clear requirements for quality and safety.
