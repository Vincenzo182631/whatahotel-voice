# Suites & Villas

## Accommodations

The 172 suites and private villa at The St. Regis Le Morne Mauritius offer a refined take on island living. Designed as private retreats, each space in our luxury accommodation in Mauritius blends the island’s heritage with the signature sophistication of St. Regis, set against the iconic backdrop of Le Morne Brabant and the Indian Ocean. Interiors reflect Mauritian craftsmanship through clean lines, natural textures, and distinctive details. A neutral color palette creates a sense of calm, while spacious marble bathrooms feature deep soaking tubs, double vanities, and rainfall showers. Select suites extend to private terraces or balconies, offering panoramic views that invite peaceful reflection. Every element has been meticulously considered, and with the legendary St. Regis Butler at your service, your stay is defined by a level of personalized attention that reflects the essence of true luxury.

Meet our Travel Ambassadors

Our all-suite, all-butler glamorous hideaway invites you to experience the true refinement of island living. Our dedicated hosts ensures a personalized stay, crafted to your unique preferences - reach out to our Travel Ambassadors at +230 460 6522 or email booking@stregislemorne.com

Featured Stay

### Grand Beachfront Villa

Private and serene, the Grand Beachfront Villa offers an exceptional setting on a secluded beach. Designed for families and friends, it features four elegant suites, each with a private deck, accommodating up to eight adults and eight children. Three plunge pools and a large infinity pool provide space for relaxation. The villa’s spacious interiors and outdoor dining areas create an effortless blend of comfort and style. With a St. Regis Butler at your service and curated resort experiences, your stay is defined by thoughtful detail and refined hospitality.

Exceptional Living

### Le Morne Beach Access Suite

At 150 square meters, this suite offers the largest layout at the resort. With direct access to the beach, it invites you to connect with the island’s natural beauty. Inside, warm wood accents and a soft, earthy palette create a sense of calm. The spacious lounge, dining table for four, and sea-facing terrace offer refined spaces to relax, reflect, and enjoy the view.

### Peninsula Beach Access Suite

Refined and inviting, the Peninsula Beach Access Suite features a separate living room, a dining area for four, and a spacious balcony with expansive views of the Indian Ocean. The master bedroom offers direct beach access, designed to accentuate the panoramic sea vistas and create a true connection with the island’s natural beauty.

### Manor House Spa Suite

Located in the heart of the resort, the Manor House Spa Suite is styled as a private retreat. The spacious bedroom transitions into a tranquil en-suite spa area, featuring two massage tables. With uninterrupted views and a dedicated wellness space, it offers a peaceful setting for you to relax and restore.

### St. Regis Butler Service

The St. Regis Butler Service has been a distinguished hallmark of our legacy since 1904. At the St. Regis Le Morne Resort Mauritius, it is an experience elevated to its finest expression. In this all-suite, all-butler resort, your personal butler is available around the clock, devoted to anticipating your every need with seamless precision and impeccable discretion.

St Regis Butler Signature Services

### The Quintessential Welcome

First impressions are everything. From the moment you arrive, your Butler ensures your stay begins with effortless ease, thoughtfully unpacking and arranging your belongings with care. When it’s time to depart, your garments are carefully folded, and your luggage prepared with the same attention to detail, allowing you to leave as seamlessly as you arrived. Every arrival. Every farewell. Perfectly orchestrated.

### Beverage Services

Your day begins with quiet sophistication. At your request, your St. Regis Butler arrives with your preferred coffee or tea, elegantly presented in-room. As the shades are gently drawn and your morning begins, a serene ritual unfolds—crafted with care, available 24 hours a day. Every detail is a reflection of our signature service and timeless attention.

### Beauty Rest

As the day winds down, your St. Regis Butler prepares your room for the perfect night’s sleep. The Beauty Rest turndown service includes carefully selected beauty essentials, along with a menu of additional offerings available for purchase. Every detail is thoughtfully arranged, ensuring a serene and restful end to your day, with nothing left to chance.
