# Rooms, Suites & Residences

## Accommodations

JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi offers a unique accommodation experience with meticulously designed rooms and residences that blend luxury and comfort. With modern architecture and elegant interiors inspired by local culture and landscapes, our hotel ensures the comfort and satisfaction of our guests in every detail. We offer a variety of rooms, suites, and residences to suit different needs and tastes....See More All rooms and residences provide a spacious atmosphere with large windows offering views of the Central Business District and Westlands entertainment hub. Electric sheers and curtains, and walk-in showers and high-quality bathtubs add to the convenience and sophistication. Our residences, ideal for longer stays and larger families, combine the comfort of home with the luxurious services of our hotel. Featuring fully equipped kitchens, separate living and dining areas, guests staying in our residences can enjoy all the facilities and services of the hotel while feeling the warmth of home.See Less

### Expressions of Africa

The hotel features a carefully arranged selection of genuine African artwork, chosen to highlight the region’s cultural depth and artistic expression. These pieces are not merely decorative, they are available for purchase, allowing guests to take home a meaningful reminder of the creativity and skill found throughout Africa.

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi is the 3 Bedroom Apartment with a maximum occupancy of 4.
