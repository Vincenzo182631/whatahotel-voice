## Relax & Rejuvenate

At Spa by JW, our treatments are thoughtfully designed to match how you want to feel, whether that is calm, indulged, invigorated, or renewed. From soothing massages and revitalizing facials to luxurious body rituals and express services, each experience blends traditional techniques with modern wellness to deliver personalized results in a serene, elegant setting.

## Pool With a View

Discover Nairobi from a new perspective at our heated infinity pool, a serene escape on the fifth floor.  Lounge in a private cabana, sip handcrafted cocktails, and enjoy light bites as you soak in the sun and skyline. Surrounded by lush greenery and panoramic city views, it is the perfect blend of luxury and tranquility, right in the heart of the city.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi offers a fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa by JW is located on-site at JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi. Click here to book a service now.
