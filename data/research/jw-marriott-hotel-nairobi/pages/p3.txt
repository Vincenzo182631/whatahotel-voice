# Dining at the JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi

## Dining

Indulge in a world of culinary excellence where every dining venue is thoughtfully designed to inspire connection. Savor global flavors with an African touch at Myna, a refined setting featuring live stations and a scenic terrace. Enjoy artisanal coffees, light bites, and decadent pastries at Midi Café & Patisserie, a contemporary space perfect for any time of day. Elevate your evening at Mr. Pang...See More Sky Bar & Lounge with bold Pan-Asian flavors, signature drinks, and breathtaking views. At Hudson Tavern Bar & Grill, relish premium steaks, gourmet burgers, and craft beers in a lively, sports-friendly ambiance. Experience the rich traditions of Indian cuisine at Mughal, where an open kitchen brings flavors to life. The Pool Bar offers refreshing beverages and light bites in a serene atmosphere with stunning sunsets over the Nairobi cityscape. ### Exclusive Private Dining Experiences

Savor an exclusive culinary experience in the refined privacy of JW Marriott Nairobi. Whether for an intimate celebration or a discreet business gathering, our private dining spaces offer personalized service, exquisite cuisine, and an ambiance of understated elegance.

## Breakfast at Myna

Savor a refined breakfast experience at Myna, where every detail is crafted to delight. Awaken your senses with the aroma of freshly brewed Kenyan coffee or tea, sip on vibrant, freshly squeezed juices, and enjoy the artistry of a live egg station. Indulge in an elaborate selection of chef-curated regional specialties, all designed to elevate your morning.

## In-Room Dining

Delight in the luxury of dining in the comfort of your room at JW Marriott Nairobi. From freshly brewed morning coffee to late-night gourmet selections, every dish is thoughtfully prepared and delivered with care. Scan the QR code in your room to explore our curated menu and order at your convenience.
