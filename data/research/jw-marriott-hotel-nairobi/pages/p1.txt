At JW Marriott Nairobi, world-class amenities and a convenient location on Chiromo Lane deliver this and more. ### Sophisticated Spaces for Memorable Meetings and Celebrations

At JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi, every gathering is elevated into a purposeful experience. Located in the heart of Westlands, our hotel offers over 1,700 square meters of thoughtfully designed event space, including a grand ballroom, executive boardrooms, and intimate meeting rooms. Each venue is infused with natural light and panoramic views of Nairobi’s skyline, creating an inspiring setting for connection...See More and collaboration. Whether hosting a corporate summit, private board meeting, or elegant celebration, our dedicated event specialists ensure every detail is flawlessly executed. From bespoke menus curated by our culinary team to advanced audiovisual technology and seamless service, we craft experiences that are both memorable and meaningful. With effortless access to the Nairobi Expressway, the CBD, and Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, JW Marriott Nairobi is the city’s premier destination for refined meetings and extraordinary events.See Less

Elevated Luxury

### Redefining Luxury Living in Nairobi

JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi rises 35 stories above the vibrant Westlands district, offering 315 luxuriously appointed rooms and 51 exclusive JW Marriott Residences. As the tallest hotel in Kenya, it boasts panoramic views and seamless access via the Nairobi Expressway, just 20 minutes from the Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. A refined haven for business and leisure, guests enjoy world-class amenities and proximity to Nairobi National Park, Nairobi National Museum, Karura Forest, the Diplomatic and Central Business Districts, premier malls, and the Maasai Market.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi is Wilson Airport (WIL). WIL is located approximately 7.8 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi have an airport shuttle to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO)?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi has an airport shuttle to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi have an airport shuttle to Wilson Airport (WIL)?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi has an airport shuttle to Wilson Airport (WIL).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
