## FAQs for Shinjuku Tokyo

Does Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo have a pool?

No, Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo does not have a pool.

How much is parking at Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo?

At Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 4000 JPY .

Paid parking space is available for hotel guests.
・Guests staying overnight: Set fee of 4,000 yen for 24 hours with unlimited entries and exits to the car park during the duration of your booking
・Restaurant guests: 250 yen per 15 minutes

Parking fee: 4000 JPY per day

Parking for registered guests only

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo.

Pet policy description. We invite you to bring your pet no matter their size, weight, or breed, all at no extra charge. If your pet fits through the door, we will welcome them in. Up to one pet per person can stay. Available from our 1 King Essential Walk in Shower or avobe.

Pet weight limit: No weight limit per pet

Pets allowed: All pets allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo?

Check-in at Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo is conveniently located near Haneda Airport (HND), approximately 10.87 miles away (17.5 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo?

Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Kabuki-cho-1.49 miles away (2.4 kilometers)
- Yoyogi Park-1.18 miles away (1.9 kilometers)
- Shinjuku Station-0.5 miles away (0.8 kilometers)
- Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden-1.1 miles away (1.77 kilometers)
- Metropolitan Government Building Observatories-0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- Shinjuku Golden Gai-0.87 miles away (1.4 kilometers)
- Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal-0.5 miles away (0.8 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
