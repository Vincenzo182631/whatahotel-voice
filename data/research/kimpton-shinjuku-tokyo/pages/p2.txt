### No Paw Left Behind

Welcome to Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo, where your pets are VIPs (Very Important Pets). Enjoy a meal together at our pet-friendly restaurants—because good times are better shared. A trip is always better when your best friend is by your side.

### Morning Kick Start

Kickstart your day and shake off the jet lag. We’ve got the fresh-brewed coffee and tea waiting for you, because the best mornings start with a little fuel and a lot of good vibes.

### Exclusive to Suite Rooms

All guests staying in one of the hotel’s suites will receive a complimentary mini travel kit from NONFICTION, the coveted lifestyle fragrance brand from Seoul known for its sophisticated sensibility and elevated self-care essentials.
