# Kimpton Shinjuku TokyoAmenities

## The highlights

1. Exclusive Suite Privileges

2. Evening Social Hour

3. Stay Inspired, Surrounded by Art

4. 24h Fitness Center

### Exclusive Suite Privileges

Enjoy exclusive suite privileges, including all-day access to our breakfast menu and a complimentary one-way transfer within Tokyo's 23 wards, with the added benefit of a flexible 24-hour stay exclusively available for guests of the Shinjuku Suite.

Learn more

### Evening Social Hour

Sip & socialize with complimentary drinks & bites at our evening Social Hour. The much-loved Kimpton Social Hour is held daily to provide guests an opportunity to connect with others over complimentary drinks.

Learn more

### Stay Inspired, Surrounded by Art

Step into Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo and experience an art lover's paradise. Inspired by New York's vibrant art scene, our hotel features work from over 20 talented artists, turning our space into a living gallery.

Learn more

### 24h Fitness Center

Keep your rhythm, no matter the time zone. Our state-of-the-art fitness center is open around the clock, so you can squeeze in a workout whenever inspiration strikes.
