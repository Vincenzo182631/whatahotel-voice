## Restaurant + bar highlights

### District - New York Steakhouse

Indulge in a bold dining experience that brings the spirit of a NYC Steakhouse to the heart of Tokyo. Think premium cuts, a sophisticated atmosphere, and an unwavering focus on flavor.

### Shinjuku’s Rooftop Haven

Elevate your night at Shinjuku's crown jewel, rooftop bar "86. Perched high above the neon glow of Shinjuku, our rooftop bar is the place to see and be seen while sipping on expertly crafted drinks.
