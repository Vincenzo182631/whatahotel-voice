# Kimpton Shinjuku TokyoLocation

Located in the heart of Tokyo’s most iconic district, Kimpton Shinjuku Tokyo serves as your sophisticated home base for exploring the "Skyscraper District" and beyond. Whether you're here to hunt down the city's best omakase, find a hidden vinyl bar in Golden Gai, or just want a stylish spot to walk your dog, we’ve got the inside track.

1. ## The highlights

2. Discover the Pulse of Tokyo in Shinjuku

3. Spin Through Shinjuku

### Discover the Pulse of Tokyo in Shinjuku

Nothing is lost in translation in this buzzing neighborhood. Some of Tokyo’s most notable attractions are nearby, including the throwback drinking district of Golden Gai and the peaceful park of Shinjuku Gyoen.

Learn more

### Spin Through Shinjuku

Explore Tokyo like a local. Grab one of our custom-designed Kimpton bikes and cruise through the streets of Shinjuku. It’s the ultimate way to see the city at your own pace.

## Things to do

### Kabuki-cho

Experience the electric pulse of Tokyo’s most famous nightlife district. With its iconic neon gates, giant Godzilla, and endless energy, Kabukicho is a cinematic playground that never sleeps.

Learn more

### Yoyogi Park

Step into an urban escape in the heart of Tokyo. From morning jogs to weekend picnics with your pup, soak in the green vibes and the ultimate Tokyo lifestyle.

Learn more

### Shinjuku Station

The world’s busiest hub and a destination in itself. From high-end fashion to underground food halls, dive into the electric energy of Tokyo’s most iconic transit maze.

Learn more

### Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden

Where Zen meets the city. Explore vast lawns, traditional tea houses, and seasonal blooms. It’s the perfect spot to find your zen just moments away from the Shinjuku buzz.

Learn more

### Metropolitan Government Building Observatories

Catch the best view in town! Head up to the observatory for a breathtaking 360-degree panorama of Tokyo. On clear days, you might even spot Mt. Fuji on the horizon.

Learn more

### Shinjuku Golden Gai

Dive into Tokyo’s nightlife! This maze of tiny bars and neon lights is packed with character. Discover hidden gems, meet locals, and experience the raw energy of Shinjuku.

Learn more

### Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal

Your gateway to Japan. This modern terminal connects you to the whole country via highway bus. Effortless travel starts here, right in the heart of the city.

Learn more
