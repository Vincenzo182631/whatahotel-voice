# Dining & Drinks

Dining & Drinks

Discover culinary creations for refined taste-making experiences at our four exquisite restaurants and bars.

## Bar Raa

Join us in this glamorous Jazz Bar with a ‘Mythical-yet-modern’ atmosphere.

Savor vibrant drinks crafted by our passionate mixologists with your friends and colleagues.

Reserve a Table

## Brasserie Ayda

Ayda is a sophisticated Brasserie serving an eclectic ‘Mediterranean-without-Borders’ menu.

Find a haven of escape, whether you arrive solo or with a large group.

Reserve a Table

## Le Jardin Pool Bar

Unwind in the sun and enjoy the secret garden atmosphere in Le Jardin Pool Bar.

Enjoy refreshing mixology creations and a selection of light meals, perfect for a day in the sun.

## Peacock Lounge

A Waldorf signature, Peacock Lounge pays tribute to the original Peacock Alley from the Waldorf Astoria New York.

Indulge in an unforgettable Afternoon Tea with an exclusive selection of quality tea and coffee, along with sweet and savory bites curated just for you.
