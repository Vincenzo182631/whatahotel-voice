## Stay in the heart of Heliopolis

Immerse yourself in the splendor of Egyptian culture and enjoy a range of authentic experiences. Discover exceptional services with our personal concierge, rejuvenating spa, and carefully curated dining concepts. Our Grand Ballroom is an elegant and versatile space that can accommodate up to 1,600 guests. Located in Heliopolis, we are easily accessible to Cairo’s landmarks.

## Rooms & Suites

Discover light and spacious interiors with modern décor in our rooms and suites. The floor-to-ceiling windows let in an abundance of natural light, creating a serene and calming ambiance.
