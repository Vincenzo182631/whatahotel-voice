# Welcome to The St. Regis Chengdu

## Live Exquisite

Conveniently situated steps from IFS Panda, Taikoo Li and Chunxi Road, The St. Regis Chengdu is a 15-minute drive from Kuanzhai Alley and Jinli Ancient Street, and a 30-minute drive to Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding. All deluxe guest rooms span a minimum of 65 sqm with 3.7m lofty ceilings, featuring timeless American classic styling paired with versatile functional layouts. 24-hour butler service free drinks, luggage handling, two daily garment presses per guest, and free access to the fitness centre, indoor heated pool and seasonal outdoor pool.

## The Study

Welcoming to both business and leisure guests, our third-floor study, with reception area, features floor-to-ceiling windows that bathe the space in natural light. The fully equipped business center features an array of services and amenities designed to facilitate seamless communication and fulfill all business needs.

## Concierge Desk

Our friendly concierge staff is available around the clock to attend to all requests. They are pleased to provide local area information, from tour and ticket services to restaurant recommendations and reservations. At the same time, the team is also on hand to assist with hotel amenities, such as spa reservations, business services, flower services, and laundry services.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Chengdu?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Chengdu is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Chengdu allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Chengdu.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Chengdu?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Chengdu. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near The St. Regis Chengdu.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Chengdu have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Chengdu has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Chengdu booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Chengdu?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Chengdu is Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport (CTU). CTU is located approximately 19.3 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Chengdu have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Chengdu does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
