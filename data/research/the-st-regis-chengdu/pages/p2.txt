# Iconic Views Exemplified by Timeless Elegance

## Accommodations

Exemplifying our hotel’s commitment to timeless elegance, each one of our 282 guest rooms and suites greets guests with iconic views of Chengdu or Tianfu Square and a rare assortment of deluxe amenities. Experience Chengdu from a dazzling array of accommodations. ### Presidential Suite

Home to a 12-meter lap pool, palatial living room and private terrace, the Presidential Suite boats an unrivaled 29th floor location underscored by rare city skyline and Tianfu Square views.

## Empire Suite

Exquisitely designed to emphasize its extraordinary 28th floor location, our Empire Suite combines elegant décor with nearly panoramic views of the Chengdu business district.

## Butler Service

St. Regis Butler Service has been a treasured hallmark of the St. Regis experience for over one hundred years. From a perfectly pressed suit ready for an important meeting, to a favorite book at hand for an evening read, to a thoughtful, last minute gift for a loved one, no request is too small or unattainable no matter what the hour of the day. Our Signature Services include Beverage Service, Unpacking & Packing Services, Garment Pressing, and eButler.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The St. Regis Chengdu?

The largest occupancy room at The St. Regis Chengdu is the 2 Bedroom Presidential Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu?

Yes, all rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The St. Regis Chengdu have in-room jettubs?

Yes, some room types at The St. Regis Chengdu have jettubs. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
