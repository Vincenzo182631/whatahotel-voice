## Bretton Woods, New Hampshire

### Elevate your mountain adventure at our New Hampshire resort

A place where historic moments shaped the world, the Omni Mount Washington Resort & Spa invites you to enjoy cherished experiences with friends and loved ones. Soar through the mountain air on a zipline canopy tour, take a dip in our indoor or outdoor pools, or play a round of golf when you stay at our New Hampshire resort. Book a ski trip in winter, a guided adventure in the summer, or indulge in a pampering session year-round in our 25,000 square-foot spa and wellness center. Enjoy resort activities like tennis, scenic gondola rides, hiking, lawn games or winter activities, and end your day with dinner at our resort’s restaurants and bars. Grab a bite at Stickney’s Steak & Chop Pub or at the elegant 1902 before grabbing a nightcap over your favorite game in the Cave - a prohibition-era speakeasy tucked within one of the remaining grand hotels. Retreat to our hotel rooms and suites, featuring sweeping mountain views, plush bedding and more. Our Bretton Woods mountain resort is also the perfect backdrop for your next event, with 30,000 square feet of available space to host your gathering.

## Traveler's Insights

### What are the property amenities at Omni Mount Washington Resort & Spa?

Omni Mount Washington Resort & Spa is home to world-class amenities including:

- A full-service spa
- Two award-winning championship golf courses
- Access to New Hampshire’s award-winning, largest ski area ( Alpine and Nordic skiing )
- Red clay tennis courts
- Zip lining and canopy tour excursions
- An indoor pool, an outdoor pool and a hot tub
- A fully-equipped fitness center
- Family-friendly resort activities (both during the summer and winter)
- 30,000 square feet of meeting and event space
- Six dining outlets - three full-service restaurants, two bars and a speakeasy
- Self and valet parking with a nightly charge

### What is included in the Omni Mount Washington Resort & Spa resort fee?

The $45 resort fee at Omni Mount Washington Resort & Spa allows guests to enjoy the many amenities and services without extra charges during their stay, including:

In-room Wi-Fi

Resort Shuttle Service

Access to fitness center and pools

24-hour business center

For a full list of inclusions, visit the resort policies page.

### Where is omni mount washington resort & Spa located?

The four-diamond Omni Mount Washington Resort & Spa is located in New Hampshire's majestic White Mountains. We are located 160 miles from Boston, 350 miles from New York, 95 miles from Portland Maine, 185 miles from Montreal and 240 miles from Hartford, Connecticut.

The resort's physical address is 310 Mount Washington Hotel Road, Bretton Woods, New Hampshire 03575.

If you are having difficulty entering the town of "Bretton Woods" into your GPS unit, please try "Carroll," as Bretton Woods is a village of the town of Carroll, New Hampshire.

### What is the closest airport to omni mount washington resort & Spa?

Omni Mount Washington Resort & Spa is located near the following airports:

- Manchester-Boston Regional Airport (MHT) is the closest airport to the resort. It is approximately 105 miles or 2 hours.
- Boston Logan Airport (BOS) is approximately 165 miles or 2.5 hours.

### How much is parking at the hotel?

24-hour valet parking is available for $40 per vehicle per day for guests staying at the Omni Mount Washington Resort & Spa only. Valet parking includes unlimited in-and-out privileges. Overnight self-parking is also available for $20 per day. Day guest valet parking is available for $30, or self-parking for $10. Complimentary parking available at nearby lot with shuttle service.

### What time is check-in and check-out?

- Check-in is at 4 PM and Check-out is at 11 AM
- Early Check-in and Late Check-out may be available for additional fee.
- Guests must be 18 or older to make a reservation. A photo ID is required at check-in. Guests 12 and under are considered children.

### Is the resort pet friendly?

One well-behaved dog 60 pounds and under are welcome at the Omni Mount Washington Resort & Spa, with dog-friendly rooms located in select guestrooms of the main hotel along with 1st floor guestrooms of the Presidential Wing. A fee of $200 per stay + tax applies.
