# Discover the Best in West Hollywood Luxury Hotels

## About The London West Hollywood at Beverly Hills

Ideally located at the crossroads of Beverly Hills and central LA, The London West Hollywood is a tranquil urban oasis that epitomizes modern luxury for the most discerning guests. Our spacious, all-suite accommodations combine sophisticated comfort, awe-inspiring views, and a signature sense of California cool. The best in West Hollywood luxury hotels, our impeccable service, exquisite food and drink offerings, and wide array of unparalleled amenities create a truly unforgettable experience.

Experience the height of laid-back luxury, LA-style, with amenities including:

- 226 bright, spacious suites designed by David Collins Studio and Richard Design
- Oversized spa-like luxury baths by Waterworks
- Elite personal concierge service offering access to LA’s most exclusive attractions
- An exquisite range of dining options
- Complimentary Continental Breakfast Table - 6:30-10:30am every day
- 24-Hour Room Service
- Non Smoking Hotel. Smoking is only allowed in designated outdoor areas.
- Complimentary calls to London (0207 & 0208 area codes only)
- Rooftop pool **(exclusively reserved for hotel guests)** with spacious sundeck, private cabanas, and panoramic views.
- Inspired meeting and private event spaces with top-notch dining
- Mini-bars with a full selection of gourmet items are placed inside each room
- Baileywick Concious Market (hotel retail store)
- High speed internet
- Dog friendly
- 110-seat state-of-the-art screening room
- Valet Parking
- Courtesy House Car\*

_\*The house car holds up to 3 guests and is available daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, is on a first come first served basis and cannot be reserved in advance. Courtesy drop off service is available within a 2-mile radius of the hotel. No return pickup service is available.  To use this service, please speak to the valet in front of the hotel driveway and they will call our driver._
