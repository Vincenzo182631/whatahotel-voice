## Honu of Kauai

On the ancient shores of Kaua‘i, a honu (Hawaiian for sea turtle) hatched under the moonlight on Poʻipū Beach. Guided by the stars, the honu journeyed across the Hawaiian Islands, exploring reefs, currents, and friendships. Yet no matter how far it swam, the honu always returned to Kaua‘i, drawn by its golden shores and the spirit of aloha. One day, it discovered The Royal Sonesta Kaua‘i Resort, filled with laughter, families, and hosts who cherished their island home. Since then, the honu has been a symbol of wisdom and welcome, reminding guests to tread gently, swim kindly, and carry the aloha spirit with them.

## Best Hiking Trails in Kauai

The island of Kauai boasts a variety of spectacular hiking trails for outdoor enthusiasts. Some of the most popular hikes include:

- Kalalau Trail (39 miles away) – A challenging 11-mile hike along the Napali Coast with breathtaking views

- Waimea Canyon Trail (39 miles away) – A moderate 3-mile hike through the "Grand Canyon of the Pacific"

- Hanakapi'ai Trail (39 miles away) – A 2-mile hike to a secluded beach, with the option to continue 8 miles to Hanakapi'ai Falls

- Awaawapuhi Trail (42 miles away) – A moderate 3-mile hike with panoramic views of the surrounding cliffs and coastline

- Alaka'i Swamp Trail (44 miles away) – A strenuous 5-mile hike through Kauai's high-elevation rainforest
