# Glamour in the Heart of Washington, D.C.

## Accommodations

Experience 5-star luxury in the nation's capital at The St. Regis Washington, D.C., where you'll encounter flawless service and an impeccable location. Stay at our hotel and pursue your passion to relax, unwind and get away. Our 182 luxury hotel rooms and suites feature spacious layouts and have played host to dignitaries, royalty and refined travelers visiting D.C. since 1926. Book one of our Presidential Suites for separate living, sleeping and work spaces, or combine the Presidential Suite with an adjoining Superior Room to create a two-bedroom suite. All suites come with signature St. Regis Butler Service to elevate your stay in D.C.

### The Presidential Suite

The 2,510 square foot Presidential Suite is The St. Regis Washington, D.C.'s largest and most lavishly appointed. Decorated by the celebrated interior design firm, Myron Wolman Designs, the Presidential Suite represents a truly unique expression of luxury. The suite connects with a Superior Room allowing the rooms to be configured as a two-bedroom suite.

## Washington, D.C. Diplomatic Accommodations

Unrivaled splendor awaits at The St Regis Washington, D.C., an icon of restored elegance where glamour meets tradition. The hotel is desirably located just two blocks from The White House and conveniently close to numerous embassies and other diplomatic offices.

## Washington, D.C. Law Accommodations

Unparalleled experiences await at The St Regis Washington, D.C., an icon of restored elegance where glamour meets tradition. The hotel is desirably located just two blocks from The White House and nearby numerous law firms and courthouses. The St. Regis Washington, D.C. offers dedicated office space, which can serve as temporary law firm offices for trial preparation, deposition space or a convenient "war room". Whether it is a one-day deposition, recruitment trip, or an extended, two month trial, the office space is dedicated exclusively for your administrative support needs.

## The St. Regis Washington D.C. Butler Service

Guests in residence at The St. Regis Washington, D.C., take pleasure in our signature St. Regis Butler Service, with our suite guests receiving an elevated level of this treasured service. For over one hundred years, St. Regis Butler Service has delivered exceptional personalized service, ensuring custom preferences are known and expertly executed at St. Regis hotels throughout the world.
