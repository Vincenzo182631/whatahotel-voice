Since its 1926 opening as the Carlton Hotel, the St. Regis—two blocks from the White House—has hosted royalty, business magnates, and presidents, including FDR and Truman. The lobby’s hand-carved wood ceiling has gold leafing and detailing that recall work found more commonly in Italian palazzos. Residential-style guest rooms and suites vary in color palette from warm yellows and golds to rich purple or deep blue; each room has Pratesi linens and wonderful bathrooms with Remède Spa products. A personal butler is available around the clock and will carry a smartphone if you prefer to e-mail him instructions. Adour, an Alain Ducasse restaurant, serves fresh, seasonal French-American cuisine.
