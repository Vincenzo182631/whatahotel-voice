# Important Information Regarding Dining Options

## Dining

Discover an elegant yet inviting atmosphere paired with exquisite cuisine at The St. Regis Washington, D.C.’s dining and imbibing venues. Exceptional dining awaits at the hotel’s signature restaurant, Alhambra. Named after the landmark Alhambra palace and fortress in southern Spain, the menu exemplifies the beauty of cultures and cuisines coming together with shareable plates that draw on the flavors...See More of the Iberian Peninsula and the Middle East. Open for breakfast, brunch, lunch and dinner, guests are invited to dine in the restaurant’s stunning space, which celebrates the historic nature of the hotel while using modern finishes. Elsewhere in the hotel, enjoy seasonal cocktails, a global wine list and extraordinary small bites in the iconic St. Regis Bar; or join us for our nightly Champagne Sabering ritual at 6:00 PM daily in our grand lobby. Guests are also invited to experience the timeless tradition of Afternoon Tea, with seasonal iterations on this cherished St. Regis ritual.See Less
