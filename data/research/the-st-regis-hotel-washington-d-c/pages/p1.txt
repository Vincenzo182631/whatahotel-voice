# Welcome to The St. Regis Washington, D.C.

## Find your sanctuary at our Washington, D.C. historic hotel

For nearly a century, The St. Regis Washington, D.C. has welcomed world leaders, dignitaries, royalty, and Hollywood icons. Ideally situated just steps from the White House, this iconic address defines the experience of a luxury hotel in Washington, DC. Guests arrive at a grand lobby adorned with ornate Florentine gold ceilings, crystal chandeliers, and refined décor that reflects timeless elegance. As a celebrated historic hotel in Washington, DC, the property blends heritage with modern sophistication, offering beautifully appointed rooms and suites with bespoke amenities, including the signature St. Regis Butler Service. Perfectly located as a premier hotel in downtown Washington, DC, guests enjoy easy access to the National Mall, museums, and landmarks, along with exceptional dining at Alhambra and handcrafted cocktails at the St. Regis Bar.

Festive Season at Washington, D.C.

This holiday season, we are thrilled to invite you to experience festive offerings sure to make your holidays bright. From a seasonal take on the afternoon tea ritual, to an extravagant Christmas Brunch and New Year's Eve celebration, we are pleased to present a collection of exquisite experiences.

## Honoring a Legacy With Historic Hotels of America

The St. Regis Washington, D.C. proudly partners with Historic Hotels of America, reflecting its enduring legacy as one of the nation’s most distinguished addresses. Originally opened in 1926, the hotel blends timeless Beaux-Arts architecture with a rich history of hosting presidents, diplomats, and cultural icons. As a recognized member of this prestigious collection, The St. Regis Washington, D.C. preserves its heritage through elegant design, storied interiors, and a continued commitment to exceptional service—offering guests an experience where history, luxury, and modern sophistication seamlessly converge.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Washington, D.C.?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Washington, D.C. is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Regis Washington, D.C. allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Washington, D.C.. with a maximum weight of 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Washington, D.C.?

On-site valet parking is available at The St. Regis Washington, D.C.. The daily valet fee is $85.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Washington, D.C. have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Washington, D.C. has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Washington, D.C. booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Washington, D.C.?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Washington, D.C. is Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA). DCA is located approximately 5.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Washington, D.C. have an airport shuttle to Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA)?

No, The St. Regis Washington, D.C. does not have an airport shuttle to Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport.

Does The St. Regis Washington, D.C. have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Washington, D.C. has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
