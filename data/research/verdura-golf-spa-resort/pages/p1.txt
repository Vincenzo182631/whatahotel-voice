## The Mediterranean Home of Golf

## Championship Sicilian Links

With 45 holes of world-class golf designed by Kyle Phillips, Verdura Resort offers a golfing experience unlike anything else in Europe.

Our links-style courses span 230 hectares of Sicilian coastline, weaving through lush green landscapes and blessed with panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea.

_This winter, our golf Clubhouse and full range of services remain open, inviting you to play against one of Sicily’s most breathtaking backdrops. Join us for year-round, unforgettable golfing experiences in Sicily._

Book your Tee Time

### Enhancement Programme Notice

The Executive Course is undergoing an important turf enhancement programme, including the introduction of Bermuda grass, selected to deliver improved playing conditions over time, greater resilience and a more sustainable use of water resources.During this period, the Executive Course will be temporarily closed to allow these improvements to be completed by Spring 2027.
