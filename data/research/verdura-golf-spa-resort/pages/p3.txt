## Verdura West Shore

You’ll need to think about every shot to score well on this journey through Verdura’s serene estate – where an unforgettable finale on the shoreline of the Mediterranean Sea awaits.

Discover

## Verdura East Links

Embrace golf in its purest form on Verdura East Links, where narrow fairways and stunning views define this Kyle Phillips modern classic. Hold your nerve - this is the challenge of a lifetime.

Discover

## Executive Course

Hone your skills on our nine-hole Executive Course, designed by Kyle Phillips to offer a thrilling range of shorter holes for players of every ability level.

_The Executive Course is undergoing turf improvement works, including the introduction of Bermuda grass, to optimise playing conditions and water use. The course will remain temporarily closed until spring 2027._
