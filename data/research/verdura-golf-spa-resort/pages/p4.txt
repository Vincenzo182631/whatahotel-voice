# Golf Clubhouse  & Pro Shop

Set beside the Torre, a 16th-century watchtower guarding ancient Roman grounds, our Clubhouse connects golf, history, and Sicilian charm. This central hub, within easy reach of Verdura Resort’s restaurants, bars, and boutiques, features everything players need before and after their round, including an extensively stocked bar, state-of-the-art changing facilities, and a Pro Shop with renowned brands. Our spacious terrace, overlooking our Verdura West Shore course, is the perfect place to unwind after taking on the challenge of our Mediterranean Links.

## The Club Lounge

Set against Sicily’s breathtaking natural coastline, our Club Lounge is a testament to the enduring spirit of the game. Imbued with classic clubhouse elegance, the Lounge pays homage to golf in every detail – from walls adorned with curated memorabilia to a bespoke rug that mimics a putting green. Open all day, it’s a sociable space to sip a coffee before teeing off, refuel with a sandwich between holes, or _cheers_ to a triumphant round. Gather with friends to watch major sporting events on our widescreen TV, or simply sink into an armchair to enjoy your favourite sports channels.

## Pro Shop

Discover a curated selection of golf equipment and accessories from world-leading brands such as Callaway, Titleist, Ping and Taylormade. Whether for a competitive match or a casual round, find everything you need alongside expert advice to ensure you play your best. Browse golf apparel collections, featuring distinguished brands such as Chervò, Hugo Boss, Footjoy, Peter Millar, Jack Nicklaus, designed for exceptional style on and off the course. Rent the latest Callaway golf clubs, including steel and graphite shaft options of various flexes and dexterities. Make use of our fleet of Club Car buggies, each with fully enabled GPS, or rent our Bushnell Range Finders and walk the courses with electric and push trolleys.

## Beyond the Green

Located in the heart of our two world-renowned courses, the Club Lounge is close to our resort’s boutiques featuring labels such as Ortigia, St Barth and Mariù alongside Lady Aliai Forte's exclusive clothing lines. When it's time to recharge, our restaurants offer diverse dining options. Experience Fulvio Pierangelini’s seasonal Japanese-Sicilian inspired cuisine at Nagori or enjoy quintessential Sicilian dishes at Liolà, our trattoria and pizzeria. At golden hour, allow time to slow at Sunset Bar for an aperitivo paired with sweeping sea views.
