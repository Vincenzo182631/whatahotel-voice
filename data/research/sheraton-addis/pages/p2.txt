# Luxury Accommodation in Addis Ababa

## Accommodations

Sheraton Addis, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Addis Ababa lives up to the remarkable standards of Marriott International’s Luxury Collection and features a selection of hotel rooms in Addis Ababa, including junior suites, a presidential suite and three-story villas, to meet your every need. Guests enjoy complimentary access to our heated outdoor pools, sauna and jacuzzi. In addition, we provide free bottled water, a paper-free alternative news app (enquire at concierge) and also press two garments on the day of your arrival. Complimentary morning coffee or tea is served from 6am to 8:30am upon request. Let us know your preference when you set your wake-up call or hang the appropriate request card outside your door before 4am. Wireless internet access (Wi-Fi) is offered at our accommodations in Addis Ababa free of charge, while high-speed internet is available in guest rooms for a fee of USD 60 per day.

Municipality fee

Effective September 2026, a government-mandated 5% municipality fee will apply to all room accommodation charges.
