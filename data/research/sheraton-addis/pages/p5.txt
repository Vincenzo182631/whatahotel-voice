"Centrally situated on the axis between National Palace and Menelek Palace, the fountain-encircled Sheraton Addis is the most luxurious hotel in the city (with one of the best pools). The go-to hotel for visiting dignitaries and NGO heads, the hotel itself has become something of a hub for a who’s who of Addis--politicians, entrepreneurs, musicians--some of whom can be found congregating in its clubby cocktail lounge, Stanley’s."
—Alex Postman
