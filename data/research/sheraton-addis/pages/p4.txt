# Explore Addis Ababa’s Attractions From Our Hotel

## Experiences

Often called “the political capital of Africa” because of its historic, diplomatic and political role in the continent, Addis Ababa lies at the foot of Ethiopia's Mount Entoto, which rises over 3,000 meters...See More (9,800 ft) in the mountains to the north. Sheraton Addis, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Addis Ababa sits on a hilltop overlooking the city and stands opposite the National Palace, formerly known as the Jubilee Palace. This landmark was built to mark Emperor Haile Selassie's Silver Jubilee in 1955. Our hotel near the National Museum in Addis Ababa is seven kilometers (11 miles) away from Bole International Airport, and our central location provides easy access to Addis Ababa’s commercial districts, the UNECA Headquarters, the United Nations Conference Center, the AU Headquarters and other business establishments and NGOs. Find many of Ethiopia’s most famous attractions within easy reach of Sheraton Addis.See Less

## Aqva Club

Come and discover the Aqva Club, our hotel spa designed in ageless beauty and providing an atmosphere of relaxation with attentive services. This recreational facility includes outdoor swimming pools, sauna, massage, and a jacuzzi.

## Massage Treatments

The hotel’s Aqva Club provides a full menu of intensive, expert massages: Swedish, reflexology (foot massage), deep tissue, sport, face, neck and shoulder massages, as well as mineral body scrubs leave skin invigorated. Hours of availability: 9am to 8pm. Advance reservation is required. Please call (251)(11) 517-1717 extension 6054.

## Nechisar Fitness Center

Nechisar Fitness Center offers a unique exercise experience in an outdoor setting that overlooks our flower gardens. Comprising an open-air workout area and a 600-meter jogging track, the hotel gym is for registered hotel guests only.

## Swimming Pools

The heated outdoor pools, including the children’s pool, are opened daily from 6am to 8pm.

More Information

The 720-square-meter main pool features soft underwater music and is beautifully illuminated at night. The pool water circulates five times a day through our treatment plant in order to provide fresh water continuously. Guests enjoy a comfortable pool temperature of 29 °C throughout the year. Changing rooms and showers are available. Towels, sun beds, and umbrellas are provided. Snacks and beverages are served at the poolside bar, the Baywatch Bar.

At 30 cm deep our children's pool is the perfect place for young guests to play. Children under 13 years are not allowed to swim without supervision of their parents. We regret that there is no life guard on duty.

### Gazlight Night Club

Explore Addis Ababa's nightlife and dance the night away at the city's finest nightclub. Music from around the world keeps the dance floor packed and the state-of-the-art light and sound systems ensure a continuous high-energy atmosphere.

## Sunset Bar

The bar is located within the Gaslight Nightclub and offers a wide variety of whiskies, Cognacs and international beverages. Only Sunset members and Marriott Bonvoy members can enjoy the eloquent surroundings accompanied by live jazz entertainment.

## Simba Kids Club

The Simba Club welcomes toddlers to pre-teens with fun, supervised activities for select age groups. A climbing wall, spring rides, an elf house, a pirate ship, and more exciting adventures await. Since all this outdoor fun might work up appetites, children menus are available at the club, along with favorite treats such as cotton candy, popcorn, and soft ice cream. Age requirement: 3 to 12 years old. Access to the club is complimentary to all our young guests.

## Community Events & Corporate Social Responsibility

The Sheraton Addis takes great pride in its support of the local Ethiopian cause by way of hosting special events such as the Annual Bag Show, Art of Ethiopia and Addis Foto Fest as well as other activities.

More Information

The Sheraton Addis takes great pride in its support of the local Ethiopian cause by way of hosting special events such as the Annual Bag Show, Art of Ethiopia and Addis Foto Fest as well as other activities.

The Bag Show is a fundraiser event organized by Prologue and Cactus organised to support local bag designers and manufacturers to showcase their latest designs, while raising funds for Our Father’s Kitchen. The show seeks to support local designers, (primarily women) to showcase their bag designs while raising funds. Top 11 innovative & creative Ethiopian bag designers have been selected to showcase their work on stage this year. This annual exciting show is rich with beautiful design of bags and available for sale at the pop-up shop.

In our goal to promote Ethiopia, we feel that it is necessary to promote Design & fashion to be recognized globally. The show provides a unique opportunity for the bag designers to showcase their creative works & for invited guests to view & purchase one of a kind gorgeous bags beautifully crafted while supporting a great local charity. The show also endeavors to highlight new leather artisans every year.

Our Father’s Kitchen is a children’s feeding program, working to make a difference in the lives of children. Many underprivileged children, in particular orphans and those living with HIV/AIDS, are fed via this program every day to help them have healthier lives.

As part of our hotel’s community outreach program and in keeping with our mission, community service is one of our highest institutional priorities.
