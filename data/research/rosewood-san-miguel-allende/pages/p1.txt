# Rosewood San Miguel de Allende

The modern Mexican hacienda is an enchanting city sanctuary in the historic quarter of San Miguel de Allende. Central highland heritage awakens to cosmopolitan life, woven through the richness of ultra-local culture and craftsmanship.
