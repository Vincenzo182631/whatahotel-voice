# whatahotel.com copy issues (pilot hotels)

These come from fact-checking each hotel page against official and press sources on 2026-10-01; the evidence is in `data/research/<slug>.json`. **Contradicted** means a source says otherwise, and **unverifiable** means no source could be found. Neither is used in the audio profiles.

## The Peninsula Tokyo

https://www.whatahotel.com/hotels/1887/the-peninsula-tokyo.html

**Contradicted**

- *Sits directly across from the Imperial Palace East Gardens*: The hotel is opposite the Imperial Palace (outer) gardens and Hibiya Park. The East Gardens are a separate area to the north, on the former Edo Castle Honmaru/Ninomaru grounds. Say 'overlooks the Imperial Palace gardens and Hibiya Park'. ([source](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/discover/property/Japan/Tokyo/The-Peninsula-Tokyo))
- *Building design mirrors the palace's traditional architecture*: The 24-storey tower's design concept is a traditional Japanese lantern, not the palace. ([source](https://www.forbestravelguide.com/hotels/tokyo-japan/the-peninsula-tokyo))
- *Unobstructed sunrise views over the Imperial Palace grounds*: The palace lies west of the hotel, in the same direction as the Mount Fuji views from Peter's west-facing seats, so palace-view rooms face sunset rather than sunrise. Drop 'sunrise'. ([source](https://www.uniqhotels.com/peninsula-hotel-tokyo/))
- *Thousand-year-old East Gardens*: The East Gardens were built on Edo Castle grounds (shogunate seat from 1603) and opened to the public in October 1968. They are not a thousand years old. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Palace_East_Garden))
- *Rooftop Peter bar offers 360-degree city views*: Peter is an indoor restaurant and bar on the 24th (top) floor, not an open rooftop. Forbes describes 180-degree views. Since 1 October 2025, Peter has been a Modern French restaurant (formerly a steak and grill) with Peter: The Bar. ([source](https://www.forbestravelguide.com/hotels/tokyo-japan/the-peninsula-tokyo))
- *A stunning rooftop pool*: The pool is indoors: a 20 m (65 ft) heated pool with a vitality pool in the 5th-floor health club, overlooking the palace gardens and Hibiya Park. ([source](https://www.forbestravelguide.com/hotels/tokyo-japan/the-peninsula-tokyo))

**Unverifiable**

- *Offers rare views of 'Japan's most sacred grounds'*: The palace-garden and Hibiya Park views are confirmed. 'Most sacred grounds' is editorial hyperbole. ([source](https://www.forbestravelguide.com/hotels/tokyo-japan/the-peninsula-tokyo))
- *Palace View rooms on floors 20+*: A Grand Deluxe Imperial Palace View room category exists. No source ties it to floors 20 and up. ([source](https://www.booking.com/hotel/jp/the-peninsula-tokyo.html))
- *Within walking distance of Tokyo Station*: Plausible, but not stated in the fetched sources. A verified alternative: direct basement access to the Hibiya subway station. ([source](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/discover/property/Japan/Tokyo/The-Peninsula-Tokyo))
- *Cherry blossoms bloom in the East Gardens in spring*: Not confirmed in the fetched content. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Palace_East_Garden))
- *Mount Fuji visible from Peter on clear days*: Only a secondary source says this, referring to the west-facing seating. ([source](https://www.uniqhotels.com/peninsula-hotel-tokyo/))
- *Limited tranquil outdoor space*: The hotel has no resort grounds, but Hibiya Park and the palace gardens are across the street, so this con is overstated. ([source](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/discover/property/Japan/Tokyo/The-Peninsula-Tokyo))

## Four Seasons Maui at Wailea

https://www.whatahotel.com/hotels/1421/four-seasons-maui-at-wailea-.html

**Unverifiable**

- *Ancient Hawaiian royalty once walked here*: No source found. The resort is adding a Queen's Garden cultural sanctuary honouring Queen Lili'uokalani, but that does not support this claim. ([source](https://press.fourseasons.com/maui/hotel-news/2026/renovated-club-floor-and-lounge/))
- *Whale watching right from your balcony*: Humpbacks winter in Maui waters and most rooms face the ocean, but no source confirms sightings from rooms. Soften to 'often visible from shore in season'. ([source](https://hawaiihumpbackwhale.noaa.gov/visit/boating.html))
- *Property built on lava rock formations that create natural tide pools*: No fetched source mentions tide pools or lava-rock foundations. ([source](https://www.forbestravelguide.com/hotels/maui-hawaii/four-seasons-resort-maui-at-wailea))
- *Indigenous plant collection of over 100 native Hawaiian species*: Sources mention only 'lush tropical gardens'. No species count found. ([source](https://www.gohawaii.com/listing/four-seasons-resort-maui-at-wailea/83))
- *Resort provides complimentary binoculars at the concierge desk*: No source found. ([source](https://www.forbestravelguide.com/hotels/maui-hawaii/four-seasons-resort-maui-at-wailea))
- *Nightly sunset conch-shell ceremony at Wailea Point*: No source found. Wailea Point is a separate headland and condominium area, not part of the resort. ([source](https://www.forbestravelguide.com/hotels/maui-hawaii/four-seasons-resort-maui-at-wailea))
- *Snorkel at the 'protected' Wailea Beach where green sea turtles visit*: Wailea Beach is a public beach that is good for snorkelling when calm. It is not a designated protected marine area, and turtle sightings were not verified. ([source](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/05/21/travel/top-10-us-beaches-2026-dr-beach))
- *Resort can feel crowded during peak seasons*: Not directly sourced. A travel-agent report says a new pool-chair policy reassigns unoccupied chairs after an hour. ([source](https://www.ellitravel.com/post/our-seasons-resort-maui-wailea-2026))

## Four Seasons Safari Lodge Serengeti

https://www.whatahotel.com/hotels/2682/four-seasons-safari-lodge-serengeti.html

**Contradicted**

- *Calving draws predators 'right outside your suite'*: The lodge is in central Serengeti (Seronera). The calving grounds are on the southern plains, so guests reach them by long game drives. Migration herds pass the central area mainly around May-June and November-December. ([source](https://www.privatetourscapetown.com/blog/four-seasons-safari-lodge-serengeti-review))
- *Remote location requires chartered flights*: Access is by light-aircraft flight from Arusha to Seronera Airstrip (about 50 minutes) and then about a 50-minute drive. Guests can also drive from Arusha (about 8 hours). A private charter is not required. ([source](https://www.fivestaralliance.com/luxury-hotels/arusha/four-seasons-safari-lodge-serengeti))

**Unverifiable**

- *Each suite features floor-to-ceiling windows*: A review mentions glass walls in the architecture. Floor-to-ceiling windows in every room were not confirmed. ([source](https://www.privatetourscapetown.com/blog/four-seasons-safari-lodge-serengeti-review))
- *Elevated walkways keep you safely above the action while lions, elephants and leopards roam freely below*: Elephants do visit the waterhole by the pool. However, the lodge uses strategic fencing and Maasai security, and big cats roaming beneath the walkways was not confirmed. ([source](https://www.fivestaralliance.com/luxury-hotels/arusha/four-seasons-safari-lodge-serengeti))

## Il San Pietro di Positano

https://www.whatahotel.com/hotels/1720/il-san-pietro-di-positano-.html

**Contradicted**

- *Private beach accessible only by the elevator descent through 60 meters of solid rock*: A staircase path also connects the lobby to the sea, so the elevator is not the only way down. The 60 m depth is unverified. ([source](https://www.ilsanpietro.com/faq/))
- *Complimentary boat service to Positano's main beach*: The complimentary transfer to Positano is a 24-hour shuttle, not a boat. The hotel's free boat offering is a daily 11:30 a.m. morning cruise (May to mid-October) on its own vessels. ([source](https://www.ilsanpietro.com/faq/))
- *Remote clifftop location requires shuttle for beach access*: The private beach is reached in-house by elevator or stairs. The shuttle only serves trips into Positano, a few minutes away. ([source](https://www.ilsanpietro.com/faq/))

**Unverifiable**

- *Book lower-level rooms for the best sea views and easier beach access*: Michelin says every room and terrace has sea views, so 'best views on lower levels' is not supported. ([source](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/article/travel/every-three-key-hotel-italy-michelin-guide-2024))
- *Sunset cocktails at the poolside bar*: A heated outdoor pool and a Terrace Bar exist, but no dedicated poolside bar is listed. ([source](https://www.ilsanpietro.com/restaurants-and-bars/))
- *Morning boat trip to Capri departing from the hotel's private beach*: There is a complimentary 11:30 a.m. cruise (May to mid-October), but its route is not stated, and Capri trips are by private yacht charter. Rephrase as 'complimentary morning cruise'. ([source](https://www.ilsanpietro.com/faq/))
- *Rare San Marzano tomatoes grown on cliff terraces*: The tomato variety is not stated. ([source](https://www.ilsanpietro.com/restaurants-and-bars/))

## Four Seasons Orlando at Walt Disney World

https://www.whatahotel.com/hotels/2878/four-seasons-orlando-at-walt-disney-world-.html

**Contradicted**

- *Sole luxury hotel inside Disney World's gates*: Disney's own deluxe resorts (for example the Grand Floridian Resort & Spa) are also inside Walt Disney World. Four Seasons is in the gated Golden Oak community on Disney property. ([source](https://touringcentralflorida.com/2019/07/14/walt-disney-world-spas-engage-the-senses/))
- *Direct park access*: There is no walk-in or direct access. Guests ride a complimentary motor coach to the parks; for Magic Kingdom they are dropped at the Ticket & Transportation Center and continue by monorail. ([source](https://press.fourseasons.com/orlando/hotel-news/2026/spring-programming/))
- *The only spa within Disney property*: Senses, A Disney Spa operates at Disney's Grand Floridian and Disney's Saratoga Springs. ([source](https://touringcentralflorida.com/2019/07/14/walt-disney-world-spas-engage-the-senses/))
- *Prime Disney World location with exclusive park access*: The location on Disney property is confirmed. Park access is by shuttle, and the early entry is shared with other Disney resort hotels, so 'exclusive park access' is wrong. ([source](https://press.fourseasons.com/orlando/hotel-news/2026/spring-programming/))

**Unverifiable**

- *Many rooms offer unobstructed Magic Kingdom fireworks views from private balconies*: Some rooms and suites have park and fireworks views, but 'many' and 'unobstructed' are not supported. The fireworks are seen at a distance. ([source](https://www.visitorlando.com/en/places-to-stay/hotels-and-resorts/39625))
- *Book a Magic Kingdom-facing room on floors 10-17*: The building has 17 storeys and Capa occupies the 17th floor, so 'floors 10-17' for rooms is doubtful. No source specifies floors. ([source](https://press.fourseasons.com/orlando/hotel-facts/))
- *Sunset cocktails at Capa bar during fireworks*: Fireworks are viewed from Capa's rooftop terrace, but the fireworks are a nighttime show, so 'sunset during fireworks' is misleading. ([source](https://press.fourseasons.com/orlando/hotel-news/2026/spring-programming/))
- *High demand requires advance booking*: No source found. ([source](https://press.fourseasons.com/orlando/hotel-facts/))
