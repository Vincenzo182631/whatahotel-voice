# Le Méridien Hotel Rooms and Suites in Nice

## Accommodations

Explore modern luxury accommodation in Nice at Le Méridien Nice, where the Mediterranean setting inspires every design detail. Choose from refined rooms with balconies to panoramic sea-view suites overlooking...See More the Baie des Anges, each space enriched by the destination. Our 4-star hotel in Nice blends mid-century modern styling with driftwood tables, teak flooring and wingback chairs for a chic atmosphere dedicated to comfort. With 324 rooms including 17 suites, we cater to leisure, business and family stays. Families can enjoy spacious suites, family rooms in Nice and interconnecting rooms availability, while luxury travelers may choose the Presidential Suite. Guests often highlight comfortable beds, clean spaces and restful room categories. High-speed Wi-Fi and smart TVs support seamless connectivity in all rooms and suites, promising an exceptional stay at the heart of the French Riviera.See Less

### Presidential Suite

One-of-a-kind in every sense, our 120m² Presidential Suite pays homage to the sophisticated Côte d’Azur. Boasting incredible sea views and uncompromising luxury, the space offers inspired residential design and a wrap-around terrace. Thoughtfully arranged, the accommodation flows across separate living and dining spaces, into a spacious master bedroom with an adjoining marble bathroom. Adding a unique touch, the suite features three exclusive paintings by local artist Guillaume Cavalier.

## Mediterranean Suite

Redesigned by Galeries Lafayette, the Mediterranean Suite combines mid-century modern design with an exclusive selection of accessories from the Department Store. Enjoy stunning views over the Mediterranean Sea, shop directly from your suite with Remote Shopping, and elevate your stay with a personal shopper experience.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Le Méridien Nice have a work space?

No, the rooms at Le Méridien Nice do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Le Méridien Nice?

The largest occupancy room at Le Méridien Nice is the 2 Bedroom Family Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Le Méridien Nice?

Some rooms at Le Méridien Nice permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Le Méridien Nice have fridges?

No, rooms at Le Méridien Nice do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at Le Méridien Nice have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien Nice have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Le Méridien Nice have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien Nice have hair dryers.
