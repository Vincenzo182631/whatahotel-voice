# Dining in Nice at Le Méridien

## Dining

When it comes to a restaurant in Nice, France, Le Méridien invites you to discover unique dining with Mediterranean flair. Start the day in L’Espace Riviera with an upscale breakfast buffet facing the...See More sea, often praised by guests for quality, variety and views. If you’re looking for an evening vibe, our rooftop restaurant overlooking Nice is an exceptional choice. Sip cocktails at Elaïo Bar with panoramic views, or indulge in dinner at Elaïo Restaurant, where menus are inspired by the olive, a true emblem of Mediterranean culture. Each dish offers a refined culinary experience above the Promenade des Anglais, ideal for rooftop moments after beach days or city exploring. Whether you are unwinding at sunset with a signature cocktail at Latitude Bar or enjoying vibrant flavors by Chef Marc Salichon, dining at Le Méridien Nice promises a memorable Riviera experience.See Less

### Elaïo Restaurant

Discover Elaïo, where the beauty of the French Riviera meets the essence of the olive, for a unique journey into the soul of Mediterranean cuisine.

## From the Top

As the highest restaurant on La Promenade des Anglais, Elaïo offers a breathtaking panorama over the Baie des Anges — a rooftop destination where Mediterranean elegance and the essence of the olive come together in perfect harmony.

## Every Week a Refreshed Menu

At Elaïo Restaurant, the olive is our muse. Each week, our chef reimagines Mediterranean cuisine through the lens of this iconic ingredient — blending seasonal produce, local specialties, and creative flair. Want a sneak peek? Head to @elaio.nice on Instagram every Monday to discover the week’s new suggestions.

### Latitude Bar

Welcome to Latitude Bar, a signature destination for enriching leisure time.

## Tropical Vibes

A mixture of inspiring Rio energy, relaxed Ipanema spirit and the Golden Sixties, Latitude Bar brings chic design, glamorous colours and tropical planting to Nice. Palm tree prints and exotic furniture craft a vintage and elegant backdrop that embodies the character of the Côte d'Azur. The bar comes from the creative brains of Saguez & Partners in collaboration with celebrated designer and interior architect, Didier Gomez.

L'Espace Riviera

### Riviera Space

Awaken the senses each morning with a deliciously prepared breakfast. Spark creativity and enliven conversation while taking in the iconic view of Promenade des Anglais. Buffet breakfast, from 29.00 EUR, Continental breakfast, from 22.00 EUR.

### Space to Inspire

With stunning views across the Mediterranean Sea and a footprint that spans over 200m², L’Espace Riviera is the ideal private venue. Our team is on hand to transform the space to host a range of gatherings, from cocktail parties and intimate dinners to larger scale exclusive events.

### Inspiration Brewed Here

Enjoy expertly crafted coffee at any time. In partnership with illycaffè, Le Méridien Nice master baristas serve the perfect cup of coffee, brewed from the finest beans.
