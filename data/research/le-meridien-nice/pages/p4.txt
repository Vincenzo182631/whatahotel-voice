# Things To Do in Nice From Le Méridien

## Experiences

Stay at Le Méridien Nice and unlock the limitless glamor of the Côte d’Azur. Our prime seafront spot ensures you're in the right place to explore all the things to do in Nice, whether you're here for business...See More or leisure. Located just minutes from the international airport in Nice, France, our hotel with a sea view is perfectly positioned between Nice Old Town, Promenade des Anglais and the shopping district. For wellness-led stays, relax at our hotel in Nice with pool access, spa options, a gym and the rooftop terrace, or step outside to stroll along the palm-fringed beach. Guests often highlight the rooftop pool atmosphere, Mediterranean views and easy walk to the Old Town, shops and restaurants. Bicycle and e-scooter rental on-site make it simple to discover more attractions, from cultural experiences to a stunning sunset in Nice. Our teams can help you plan the perfect Riviera itinerary.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Le Méridien Nice have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Le Méridien Nice offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Le Méridien Nice have a pool on-site?

Yes, Le Méridien Nice has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Le Méridien Nice?

Museum of Fine Arts, Museum of Modern Art, Parc Phoenix, Promenade des Anglais, Nice Etoile and more are nearby Le Méridien Nice.
