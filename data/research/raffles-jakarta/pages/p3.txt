## Our Philosophy

At Raffles we create an environment that nurtures wellbeing holistically through harmonious design, serene rituals, nourishing cuisine, and world-class wellness experiences. Everything that touches the senses is consciously crafted to take care of your physical and emotional wellbeing. At Raffles Spa, a soothing oasis amid Jakarta's hustle and bustle, discover bespoke spa journeys tailored to your individual needs, alongside state-of-the-art fitness facilities. We partner with world-class wellness and beauty brands, while thoughtfully sourcing our products with environmental sustainability in mind, reflecting our commitment to responsible practices.

## Spa experiences

Escape into a thoughtfully curated retreat where luxurious accommodation and the restorative rituals of Raffles Spa come together in one seamless experience. From calming treatments inspired by traditional healing techniques to the quiet comfort of your room in the heart of Jakarta, every detail is designed to encourage deep relaxation, restore balance, and extend the feeling of wellness throughout your stay.

### Signature Heritage Massage

Experience Indonesian heritage through a head-to-toe massage incorporating Lomi Lomi, Balinese and Javanese techniques.

-

### Aromatic Couples Romance

Indulge in time together with an aromatic candlelit soak, followed by a 60-minute massage.

### Lavender Dreams Experience

Embrace the calming and balancing benefits of lavender in this uplifting treatment, featuring a body scrub, pressure point scalp massage and healing stones.

-

### Heated Relaxation Loungers

Located within our relaxation area, the heated loungers provide gentle radiant warmth to soothe the body and encourage complete relaxation.

-

### Personal Training

Personalized one-to-one strength, fitness and lifestyle coaching with dedicated trainers at our fitness centre.

-

### Hydrotherapy Wellness

Enjoy access to our steam room, experience showers, outdoor whirlpool and ice fountains, designed to relax and refresh.

## Frequently asked questions

### What types of massages are offered at Raffles Jakarta Spa?

The spa features unique treatments like the Signature Heritage Massage, blending traditional Indonesian techniques for a holistic experience.

### How can I purchase a gift card for Raffles Jakarta Spa?

You can easily buy a gift card online or inquire at the spa reception for personalised options.

### What amenities are available at Raffles Jakarta Spa?

Enjoy a steam room, experience showers, outdoor whirlpool, and ice fountains designed for relaxation and refreshment.

### When is the best time to visit Raffles Jakarta Spa?

Visiting during weekdays often means quieter sessions, allowing for a more serene and personalised experience.

### Which products are used in the spa treatments?

The spa uses premium products of Kerstin Florian for facials and Tropical Spa for body care, ensuring high-quality care and results for your skin.
