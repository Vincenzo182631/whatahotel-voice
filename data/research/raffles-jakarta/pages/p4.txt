## Our Story

Arts Cafe at Raffles Jakarta is a captivating haven for art enthusiasts and culinary connoisseurs alike. Vibrant paintings adorn the walls, and diners are treated to a gastronomic journey to savour an array of international and local delicacies crafted by culinary maestros. From delectable pastries to sumptuous main courses, each dish is a work of art.

Within the restaurant, two beautifully decorated private rooms offer an inspiring dining experience. These vibrant, comfortable spaces are a setting of rare beauty for family celebrations, anniversaries, business dinners and seasonal events in Jakarta.
