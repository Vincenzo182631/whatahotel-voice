#### A taste of relaxation

# Montagu's Mews

Fresh, local ingredients, inspired style and flawless service. Somewhere to relax and treat yourself to special moments that become lasting memories.

With skilled staff to help you discover a new favourite, or mix up a classic concoction, the bar team also offers crafted local botanicals, small batch spirits and imaginatively curated wines and beers as we welcome you to linger and enjoy the stylishly snug interiors.

Alternatively step outside to enjoy dining on our terrace that comes complete with cantilever parasols, seasonal heaters and views over the idyllic gardens.

There's no formal dress code as we want you to feel comfortable and at ease in our surroundings
