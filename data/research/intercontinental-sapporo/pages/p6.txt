### AuBlanc

Discover AuBlanc (オーブラン), a French restaurant in Sapporo where modern casual French cuisine meets the flavors of Hokkaido. Enjoy breakfast, lunch and dinner with buffet, set menu and à la carte options, featuring locally sourced ingredients. Our on-site bakery offers freshly baked bread and pastries.

Learn more

### SAWAKA

Discover SAWAKA (サワカ), a contemporary kappo restaurant in Sapporo showcasing premium ingredients from Hokkaido and beyond. Experience refined dishes that blend traditional Japanese techniques with modern creativity, prepared at a live chef’s counter and complemented by expertly crafted sake and drink pairings.

Learn more

### Norva

Enjoy our signature Afternoon Tea with premium teas, seasonal sweets, and savory bites by day. At night, the lounge transforms into a bar serving original cocktails and mocktails made with fresh Hokkaido ingredients, alongside light snacks perfect with champagne or whisky.

Learn more

### Poolside Wellness

Start your day with complimentary morning wellness at InterContinental Sapporo. Enjoy poolside yoga, Pilates and a floating sound bath in our indoor heated pool on the 9th floor. Designed for hotel guests, these relaxing wellness sessions offer a refreshing way to restore balance and begin your day in Sapporo.

Learn more

### Club InterContinental

Located on the 14th floor, the exclusive Club InterContinental Lounge offers a personalized retreat with attentive service, featuring gourmet breakfasts, seasonal afternoon tea, and evening cocktails. From arrival to departure, every detail ensures a luxurious experience in Sapporo.

Learn more

## FAQs for InterContinental Sapporo

Does InterContinental Sapporo have a pool?

InterContinental Sapporo has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor pool

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Sapporo?

The hotel offers on-site parking for guests at ¥3,300 per night. Restaurant guests can park for 3 hours at ¥600, and fitness gym members can park for 3 hours free of charge. Reservations are not accepted.

In and out privileges

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Sapporo?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Sapporo.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Sapporo for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Sapporo have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Sapporo has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Sapporo?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Sapporo.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Sapporo?

Check-in at InterContinental Sapporo is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Sapporo offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Sapporo offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Sapporo from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Sapporo is conveniently located near New Chitose Airport (CTS), approximately 32.81 miles away (52.8 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
