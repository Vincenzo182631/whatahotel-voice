Discover true comfort and inspiration in our spacious guestrooms, thoughtfully designed with meticulous attention to detail. With expansive layouts and carefully curated amenities, you’ll experience a peaceful retreat where comfort meets sophistication, complemented by beautiful views of Hokkaido’s scenic landscape.
