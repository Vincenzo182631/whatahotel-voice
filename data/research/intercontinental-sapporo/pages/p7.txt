### 5 Days at Intercontinental Sapporo

6 days ago

We had a delightful 5 day 4 night stay at the IC Sapporo. Staff is fantastic - Kinoko one of the concierges was super helpful in setting up meals and tours. Giorgio a server in the morning breakfast buffet (very yummy BTW) was great.

Rooms are spacious and the onsen style tub is perfect for 2 people so no real need to go offsite for that. Rooms are quiet and super comfortable.

We enjoyed taking the bikes out for a spin upstream along the river trails to the parks. If you have time try playing Park Golf across the street from the hotel on the river - its a fun combination of golf and croquet with 18 holes. Course can be played in under 2 hours - harder than it appears to par a hole. Nakajima Park is across the street from the IC and its a beautiful place to walk and relax. Sapporo's concert hall is also in the park so ask the concierge for what's playing.

Local food is fabulous - the staff provided a few local places, which while a challenge to find in some cases (ask a local person when you get out of the cab) are so so worthwhile. One local we asked personally walk us around until we found the elevator in a building to get to the 6th floor.

Traveling to Jonzenkei spa area for a day of fall foliage, a tour of the Hoheikyo dam further upstream, a local foot bath and a lunch over "Jingisukan" lamb and vegetables was memorable. We went with a fantastic tour guide - "Waka" which the IC concierge can connect you with and a driver for the day. It can also be done via bus - just plan on some extra time to make the connections. Enjoy your stay - almost everything is walkable.

### A Warm, Serene, and Truly Luxurious Stay

15 days ago

We had an exceptional stay at the InterContinental Sapporo. As an IHG Diamond Ambassador, I booked an award stay using points and applied a Confirmable Suite Upgrade (CSU) for a Junior Suite.
​From arrival, the experience was seamless. One outstanding benefit for guests in Junior Suites and above is the complimentary station transfer service. Being met at the station by a spacious luxury vehicle with deeply reclining, ultra-comfortable seats made for a truly impressive welcome before we even stepped through the hotel doors.
​The property itself is recently built, showcasing sleek, modern architecture and immaculate upkeep. The location is ideal: situated directly facing a serene park, it offers a peaceful, quiet sanctuary slightly removed from the hustle of central Susukino, while still being an easy, pleasant walk away when you want to head into the center.
​Calling our room a "Junior Suite" almost felt like an understatement—with its vast open footprint and floor-to-ceiling panoramic views, it could easily pass for a full Panorama Suite. Even with two Queen beds combined, there was abundant space to move around and unwind in comfort. We also happened to stay during a local fireworks festival, and watching the display unfold directly from the panoramic windows of our room was an unforgettable highlight.
​The staff delivered exemplary hospitality. We spent considerable time in the Club Lounge, where the team welcomed us warmly from check-in and consistently checked in throughout our stay to ensure everything was taken care of.
​The dining offerings were consistently satisfying:
​Club Lounge: Afternoon tea was well-curated and perfect for unwinding. The evening cocktail selection, while not designed to replace a full sit-down dinner, was far more substantial and refined than the modest cold spreads often found across European or US properties.
​Breakfast: Superb on all fronts. The lounge featured exclusive made-to-order à la carte dishes, with the traditional Japanese breakfast set standing out in particular. The main buffet was equally delightful, offering authentic local Sapporo culinary specialties.
​Whether visiting solo, with a partner, or recommending to family and friends, InterContinental Sapporo is an easy top recommendation. I will undoubtedly return on my next trip to Hokkaido.
