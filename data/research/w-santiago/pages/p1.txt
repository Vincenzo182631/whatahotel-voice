# Welcome to W Santiago

## DISCOVER AN OASIS OF URBAN LUXURY IN THE HEART OF THE CITY

W Santiago, a pet-friendly hotel in El Golf, Chile, is the ideal destination for the bold traveler. Inspired by life itself, the hotel combines daring design with a vibrant color palette, creating a sophisticated atmosphere that attracts the curious. Enjoy local cuisine at NoSo and Nikkei dining at Karai by Mitsuharu, or unwind at Red2One, our rooftop bar with panoramic views of the Andes Mountains...See More and the city of Santiago de Chile. ### Live the High Life

## Penthouse II

Who doesn't love an oversized one-bedroom Penthouse II where contemporary sophistication is always in style, in out-of-this-world proportions? In this luxury hotel suite in El Golf, Santiago, chill out amid stunning displays of Chilean colors, art and flavors, innovatively blended with modern European sensibilities by award-winning designers Tony Chi and Sergio Echeverria.

Learn More

### Mix and Mingle

Presenting a globe-trotting gastronomy of culinary excellence—you'll taste all over the world without leaving W Santiago. At NoSo, go French with classic reimaginations defined with modern sensibilities, or get fresh with sensational sushi at Karai, a Nikkei restaurant that crosses borders. Chill out at our Chilean bistro Terraza where we're mad about Mediterranean influences, or nibble your way across the tapas with a twist at W Lounge.

## Bars and W Lounge

Not your ordinary lounge. Watch the action unfold in the spirit of a literary salon, where you'll meet, greet, flirt, play, sip and savor in this modern playground where mixing and mingling sizzles to a Cueca beat. The stunning wall of 1,600 wine bottles sets the tone for your sojourn. Groove to our cool live DJ mixes or sip a mixed libation from the colorful cocktail list.

Learn More

### Karai

Karai, our award-winning Japanese restaurant in El Golf, Santiago, stands out for the depth of its flavors and the use of high-quality local Chilean products. Blending the best of Peruvian and Japanese cuisine, Karai offers top-tier fine dining.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Santiago?

The check-in time at W Santiago is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Santiago allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Santiago. One pet is allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Santiago?

On-site self parking is available at W Santiago. Self parking is available at W Santiago, with rates of $2.00 per hour, and $15.00 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near W Santiago.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Santiago have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Santiago has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Santiago booking.

What is the closest airport near W Santiago?

The closest airport near W Santiago is Arturo Merino Benitez International Airport (SCL). SCL is located approximately 22.9 KM from the hotel.

Does W Santiago have an airport shuttle to Arturo Merino Benitez International Airport (SCL)?

Yes, W Santiago has an airport shuttle to Arturo Merino Benitez International Airport (SCL).

Does W Santiago have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Santiago has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
