## Dining

Looking for a show-stopping culinary experience you won't soon forget? Look no further than W Santiago. Our bold hotel restaurants in El Golf, Santiago, showcase a variety of international dishes, enhanced by our own imaginative flair. Sample flavorful tapas at W Lounge or enjoy Mediterranean-inspired dishes and amazing views of Santiago at Terraza, our Chilean bistro. Kick back and take in the scene from one of Las Condes' highest points, Red2one, our stylish rooftop bar overlooking the vibrant city. W Lounge on the plush velvet sofas, cocktail in hand, at our glam Tea Library, located in the W lobby. ## The Brunch

THE BRUNCH at NoSo is an experience you can’t miss on Sundays at W Santiago, available from 10am to 2pm. Enjoy an unlimited breakfast buffet at our hotel restaurant in El Golf, Santiago, featuring our specialties, including happy hen eggs Benedict, artisanal sandwiches, and French toast, all paired with mimosas and sparkling wine.

## Night Market

NIGHT BRUNCH at NoSo transforms Fridays at W Santiago into a unique experience. Enjoy a street food buffet paired with local beers, sparkling wine, and classic Chilean pisco, all in a vibrant atmosphere with a live DJ. On the 4th-floor terrace, sophistication meets Santiago’s relaxed, modern vibe, creating the perfect spot to gather with friends, enjoy great music, and take in spectacular city views.
