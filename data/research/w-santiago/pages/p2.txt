## Accommodations

Make yourself at home at W Santiago. Our 190 guestrooms were conceived by famed designers Tony Chi and Sergio Echeverria, inspired by the beauty of the vibrant city surrounding our hotel. Each luxury hotel room features sleek décor, intriguing design elements and plush velvet or leather accents. Large-scale abstract murals highlight the walls and lucite light fixtures cast a warm, inviting glow. A...See More moody color palate of rich jewel tones sets the scene for a restful night's sleep on our signature W beds. Plush seating allows you to lounge by the floor-to-ceiling windows and soak up the stunning views of Las Condes. Freshen up each morning in our marble bathrooms with Bliss® Spa luxury bath amenities and a walk-in rain shower or deep soaking tub. Tune in to your favorite shows on our 42" LCD TV with premium cable and movie channels. Stay connected with high-speed internet access and Wi-Fi available in all our El Golf, Santiago, hotel rooms and throughout the hotel.See Less
