## Discover Fort Lauderdale’s _premier_ spa oasis at   Pier Sixty-Six

Designed for rejuvenation, Zenova features thoughtfully curated spaces—including nine luxury treatment rooms, a Couples Spa Suite, a full-service salon and the Co-Ed Via Aquae—that promote detoxification, recovery and overall well-being.

## Ceremonial Sauna

Inspired by the traditions of Europe’s esteemed Aufguss Saunas, our Ceremonial Sauna blends sound therapy, opulent aroma-infused indulgences and guided rituals led by a traditional Sauna-Meister. Each moment is crafted to cultivate mindfulness, soothes the senses and elevates your wellness experience.

## Snow Room

As the region's first authentic Snow Room, this transportive and custom room maintains a crisp 10 to 14°F, while emitting soothing alpine forest scents. Like an amplified cold plunge, it reduces inflammation and swelling, accelerates muscle recovery, boosts circulation and supports immunity.

## Sensory Shower

A dynamic experience delivering a powerful combination of hot and cold therapy, chromotherapy, aromatherapy and oxygenation. The Sensory Shower invigorates circulation, energizes the body and enhances overall wellness.

## Vitality Pool

Zenova’s signature therapeutic pool warms the body, stimulating the lymphatic system. Reducing tension and promoting deep relaxation. It revitalizes the body’s natural healing processes for a full sense of well-being.

## Does Pier Sixty-Six have a spa?

Yes. Zenova Spa & Wellness is located on the third floor of Pier Sixty-Six and offers massages, facials and other restorative treatments inspired by ancient rituals and modern wellness techniques.

## Can non-hotel guests visit Zenova Spa & Wellness?

Zenova Spa & Wellness is currently available exclusively to Pier Sixty-Six resort guests and club members.

## What treatments are available at Zenova Spa & Wellness?

Zenova Spa & Wellness offers a collection of restorative treatments, including massages and rejuvenating facials designed to support relaxation and well-being.

## What is the Via Aquae at Zenova Spa & Wellness?

The Via Aquae is part of Pier Sixty-Six's 13,000-square-foot spa experience, with amenities including a Snow Room and Vitality Pool.

## How old do you have to be to visit Zenova Spa & Wellness?

Guests must be at least 18 years old to book spa services. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult age 18 or older.

## What wellness and fitness experiences are available at Pier Sixty-Six?

In addition to Zenova Spa & Wellness, Pier Sixty-Six offers a 24/7 fitness center, Movement Studio and scheduled wellness and fitness classes ranging from yoga and meditation to Pilates and strength training.

## Does Zenova Spa & Wellness offer yoga and meditation?

Pier Sixty-Six offers scheduled wellness programming that includes meditation and yoga classes. Programming and schedules vary by day.

## How can I contact Zenova Spa & Wellness?

For more information about Zenova Spa & Wellness, guests can call 754.318.6950 or email spa@piersixtysixresort.com.
