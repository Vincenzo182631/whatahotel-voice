## October Signature Experiences

**October 1–4 \| Harvest Happenings**

October begins with the warmth and creativity of the harvest season. Little explorers can create Paper Plate Scarecrows, Corn Cob Paintings, Leaf Rubbings, and Harvest Friendship Bracelets.

Afternoon experiences continue with a Cardboard Pumpkin Patch, Apple Basket Collage, Toilet Paper Roll Corn Stalks, and Paper Bag Harvest Puppets—inviting children to celebrate the season through hands-on creativity and imaginative play.

**October 8–11 \| Fall at the Pier**

Fall comes to life through colorful and nature-inspired creations. Children will explore Corn Mosaic Art, Harvest Wreaths, Apple Basket Collages, and Paper Bag Scarecrows.

Afternoon favorites include Pumpkin Patch Dioramas, Apple Core Art, Coffee Filter Fall Leaves, and Corn Cob Bubble Wrap Prints for a playful celebration of the season.

**October 15–18 \| Nature’s Keepsakes**

Inspired by the beauty of autumn, children can create Owl Cardboard Canvases, Paper Bag Fox Puppets, Clay Acorns, and Fall Nature Headbands.

Afternoon experiences include Coffee Filter Autumn Trees, Leaf Nature Art, Toilet Paper Roll Squirrels, and Button Leaf Trees—perfect for little explorers ready to discover the textures and colors of the season.

**October 22–25 \| Fall Into Creativity**

A playful week of seasonal creativity invites children to explore Treehouse Canvases, Paper Plate Spider Webs, Ghost Suncatchers, and Black Cat Silhouette Art.

Afternoon experiences feature Bat Wing Canvases, Seed Fall Mosaics, Pumpkin Lanterns, and Autumn Tree Fingerprint Canvases, creating a festive and imaginative close to the month.

**October 29–November 1 \| Spooky at Pier**

The month concludes with a spirited celebration of fall and Halloween-inspired creativity. Little explorers can create Pipe Cleaner Spiders, Pumpkin Patch Photo Frames, Jack-o’-Lantern Masks, and Fall Bracelets.

Afternoon favorites include Cardboard Tube Monsters, Pumpkin Canvases, Coffee Filter Ghosts, and Paper Bag Jack-o’-Lantern Puppets—bringing a playful finish to October.
