# Maldives Seaplane Transfer

## Book Your International Flights

- Once you’ve made room reservations at Baglioni Maldives Luxury All Inclusive, you’ll need to book your flights to Velana International Airport, located on Malé Island (flights & transportation are not included). We recommend booking your international flights as soon as possible as flights can be limited.
- Please note: All guests traveling to and from the Maldives must submit a self-declarative health declaration within 96 hours of their journey. This declaration can be filled out and submitted electronically via the Maldives Immigration portal (IMUGA):http://imuga.immigration.gov.mv.
- Be sure to download the QR code from the IMUGA portal. All travelers must then present their QR code during the Immigration process in Maldives.
- Important update for Maldives guests: Starting Nov 15, 2024, all vape devices and parts are banned from entry. Cigarettes are limited to one in-use pack. From Dec 15, possession and use of both will be prohibited, including for tourists. Please plan accordingly. ¹
- ¹President Ratifies 19th Amendment to Export-Import Act of the Maldives - The President's Office
- These procedures are subject to continuous change, so we advise you to verify the updated requirements on the Maldives Ministry of Tourism website.

## Seaplane Baggage Allowance for Each Guest:

- 25 kg (55 lbs.) suitcase + 5 kg (11 lbs.) hand luggage
- However, only a small bag (less than 3 kg (6.6 lbs.) will be allowed as a carry-on (trolleys are not considered hand luggage).
- Any extra baggage will require a fee that can be paid directly at the resort (the fee is determined by the Trans Maldivian Airways). It’s important to notify us of extra baggage when sending your international flight details to: reservations.maldives@baglionihotels.com
