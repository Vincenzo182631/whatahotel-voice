## All Inclusive Barefoot Luxury at Baglioni Maldives

Situated in the midst of the Indian Ocean, Baglioni Maldives Luxury All-inclusive is one of the few truly natural resorts in the Maldives, where every guest is treated as part of the family and will want to return for years to come.

## Unique Experiences

\\
**Diving For Beginners** \\
\\
Discover the magic of the Maldivian underwater world with your very first dive—a simple and safe introduction to breathing underwater and admiring the vibrant coral reef and its colorful marine life up close.
\\
**Children’s Diving Experience** \\
\\
Accompanied by a certified instructor, young guests can have their first encounter with marine life and learn the fundamentals of diving, up to a maximum depth of 2 meters.
\\
**Whale Shark Excursion** \\
\\
Dive into the crystal-clear waters of the Maldives, in the heart of the Dhaalu Atoll, and swim alongside the whale shark—the gentle giant of the ocean. A snorkeling experience you’ll cherish forever.
