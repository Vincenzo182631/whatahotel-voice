# Restaurants & Bars

Taste the Thompson side of Palm Springs. Sip poolside, enjoy outdoor bites and ease into daily happy hour at The Perch with mountain views and desert light. Move into Levantine‑inspired share plates at Lola Rose Grand Mezze, continue with craft cocktails in the bar and lounge, or explore fine wines at HALL Napa Valley Tasting Room & Wine Lounge. Venture out to Bar Issi for coastal Italian ease and effortlessly chic vibes. Rather stay in? In‑room dining runs from breakfast to dinner.
