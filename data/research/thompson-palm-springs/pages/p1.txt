# Hotel In Downtown Palm Springs \| Thompson Palm Springs

## EXPERIENCE THE LIFESTYLE AND DESIGN SPIRIT OF OUR DOWNTOWN PALM SPRINGS RESORT

Located in the heart of downtown, Thompson Palm Springs blends iconic midcentury modern design with the city’s Rat Pack‑era heritage. Discover distinctive rooms, spacious suites, our 21+ Upper Stories adults‑only retreat, and a stylish two‑bedroom Penthouse all with expansive balconies or patios. Enjoy resort‑style amenities including an award‑winning Middle Eastern restaurant, two outdoor pools with sweeping views, a state‑of‑the‑art fitness center, the HALL Napa Valley Tasting Room, and 35,000+ sq ft of retail and dining options open to locals and visitors alike.

## Rooms & Suites

Winding through a lush open‑air path, guests reach 168 bungalow‑inspired rooms set just above the city’s buzz for added privacy and breathtaking views. Inside, warm hues, soft greens, and wood‑like textures echo the landscape, while private outdoor spaces and refined details give each room and suite a modern, residential edge distinctly Thompson.

### Pets are Welcome

Thompson Palm Springs is a pet-friendly hotel where pets sleep free. Pets are not allowed in our bars, restaurants or pool areas. Pets must be leashed at all times in common areas and cannot be left unattended in guest rooms. A signed waiver is required upon check-in. These restrictions do not apply to service animals. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

#### Pet Fees

- Price: Free

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  40Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 50Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.
