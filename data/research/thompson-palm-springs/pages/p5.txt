Pet-Friendly

We welcome dogs at no additional charge for stays less than 30 nights. For stays greater than 30 nights, a deep cleaning fee may be charged at the hotel's discretion. Dogs must remain in their assigned guest room. A signed waiver is required for all pets. Thompson Palm Springs does not charge a pet fee. Individual pets are restricted to 50 pounds. Maximum number of pets is 2.

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Photography

Professional or hired photography is not allowed in resort public and/or event spaces unless contracted with the hotel. If you are interested in hosting an event and/or contracting space, please contact us directly by email at palmspringssales@thompsonhotels.com

Smoke-and Vape-Free Resort

Please note that our entire resort is a smoke- and vape-free environment, including your room and balcony or terrace. Smoking and vaping are only permitted in designated smoking areas outside the resort perimeter. In the event it is determined that smoking or vaping has taken place inside a room, a smoking fee of $500 will be placed on your folio at management’s discretion.

Cash Free Environment

Please note that this hotel only accepts credit cards, debit cards and, where applicable, other contactless forms of payment. It is a cash free environment.
