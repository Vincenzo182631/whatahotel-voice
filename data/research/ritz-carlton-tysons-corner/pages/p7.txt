Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Tysons Corner
Experience an Unforgettable Holiday Season
Celebrate the magic of the season at The Ritz-Carlton, Tysons Corner. Enjoy exciting holiday events, including festive brunches, Breakfast with Santa, Gingerbread House Workshops, Nutcracker Afternoon Teas, and more.
Festive Experiences
Thanksgiving Brunch
Experience an elegant Thanksgiving Brunch, where seasonal flavors and artful presentation come together in a luxurious setting. Indulge in curated holiday offerings, crafted to elevate your festive celebration.
Discover More
Thanksgiving Brunch to Go
Celebrate Thanksgiving with a perfectly roasted turkey, golden and succulent, or indulge in a luxurious prime rib, tender and richly seasoned, in the comfort of your own home. Available to-go, these curated dishes promise an unforgettable holiday experience.
Discover More
Nutcracker Afternoon Tea
Gather with family and friends for a memorable Afternoon Tea inspired by the spirit of the season. As you savor festive delicacies and fine teas, enjoy a live performance of The Nutcracker, bringing holiday magic to life in an unforgettable setting.
Gingerbread House Workshop
Celebrate the magic of the season with an enchanting Gingerbread House Workshop. Led by our skilled pastry team, this cherished holiday experience invites children to craft and decorate their own gingerbread creation, surrounded by festive cheer and sweet seasonal delights.
Discover Now
Brunch with Santa
Celebrate the spirit of the season with an elegant Brunch with Santa. Enjoy a thoughtfully curated holiday buffet while Santa Claus makes a magical appearance, bringing festive joy, wonder, and unforgettable memories for guests of all ages.
Discover More
Gingerbread House to Go
Bring home a festive holiday tradition with our Gingerbread House To-Go kit. Includes a jumbo gingerbread house, assorted candies and icing, ½ dozen holiday cookies, and Chef's hot chocolate mix for four. Perfect for creating sweet holiday memories together.
