Experience Northern Virginia & DC
Experiences
Set in the upscale Northern VA suburb of McLean, Tysons Corner is a premier destination minutes from the excitement of Washington, DC, offering exceptional shopping, dining, and entertainment, all surrounded by the area’s picturesque natural scenery. Located adjacent to Tysons Galleria mall, our hotel is a short walk to the Silver Line Metro and near the Capital Beltway, inviting guests to explore Virginia's vineyards, luxury shopping, and must-see museums, or visit the White House, Smithsonian museums, Arlington National Cemetery, Rock Creek Park, and more.

Hotels Near Wolf Trap
Our hotel near The Smithsonian Air and Space Museum invites guests to discover northern Virginia. Take your pick from local events offering arts, stage productions, historic re-enactments, an astronaut academy and a convenient location near Wolf Trap Park in Vienna.
Wolf Trap National Park for the Performing Arts
This summer’s lineup at Wolf Trap National Park for the Performing Arts is a who’s who of entertainment, says the concierge at The Ritz-Carlton, Tysons Corner. From Lionel Richie to Sing, the B-52s to Wilco, the musical guests are many. Events featuring writers David Sedaris and Neil Gaiman are also part of this summer’s lineup.
Photo Cruise of Mount Vernon and the Potomac
See Mount Vernon from a different perspective aboard a sightseeing cruise of the Potomac. The 40-minute narrated excursion offers insight on the river’s history and how it contributed to George Washington’s business ventures. Through October 31.
Smithsonian’s National Air and Space Museum
From Charles Lindbergh’s historic flight to the modern spacecraft of today, discover the major aviation milestones that have transformed our world as we know it. See the iconic Spirit of St. Louis, the Viking Lander and more at the Boeing Milestones of Flight Hall.
Parks & Gardens
Discover Parks & Botanical Gardens Near Our Hotel
Among the outdoor experiences available near our hotel in Tysons Corner, guests can enjoy beautifully landscaped grounds at Meadowlark Botanical Gardens, 15 miles of hiking trails in Great Falls Park and historical family-friendly programs at Frying Pan Park.
Great Falls Park
Hiking, fishing, climbing, bicycling, bird-watching and boating are the main activities at this beautiful park, recommends the concierge at The Ritz-Carlton, Tysons Corner. Get ready for 15 miles of hiking trails, including 5 miles used for horseback riding and biking. Three falls overlooks are within a 10-minute walk from the Visitors’ Center, and a bicycling trail starts just behind the center.
Meadowlark Botanical Gardens
Explore 95 acres of paths lined with cherry trees, peonies and irises, plus ornamental display gardens, wandering trails, majestic lake views and a restored 18th-century log cabin. The recently added authentic Korean Bell Garden is the only one of its kind in the Western Hemisphere.
Living History Program at Manassas National Battlefield Park
History comes alive at this national park. Ceremonies and living history events replicate the battles on the plains of Manassas with infantry and artillery demonstrations atop Chinn Ridge, at Groveton Confederate Cemetery and Brawner Farm in July and August.
Green Springs Garden
Add this secret garden tour to your itinerary. Green Springs Garden offers an “outdoor classroom” ideal for families and kids, or adults eager to learn gardening and landscaping skills. Be sure to visit the tropical greenhouse surrounded by a wooded stream valley with ponds. Parched? The Historic Green Spring Manor House offers popular tea programs.
Frying Pan Farm Park
Flashback to rural America. Frying Pan Farm Park re-creates a time from the 1920s to the 1940s when mechanical machines like the tractor and milking machines were among early advances to aid farmers. Kids can meet the farm animals and take classes, workshops and horseback-riding lessons.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Tysons Corner have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton, Tysons Corner offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Tysons Corner have a pool on-site?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Tysons Corner has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Tysons Corner have an on-site spa?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Tysons Corner is located on-site at The Ritz-Carlton, Tysons Corner.
