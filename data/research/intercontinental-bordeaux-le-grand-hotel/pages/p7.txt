### Great Stay in Bordeaux

4 days ago

We stayed at the Intercontinental in Bordeaux for a wine country trip in October for 4 nights. We loved this hotel. It is right in the heart of Bordeaux and we could walk everywhere for shopping and dining. The hotel is very classic and beautiful inside, but also very comfortable. The room we had was an upgraded room, but it was VERY spacious with an awesome bathroom situation with 2 different toilet rooms, both a shower and a tub, and 2 sinks in different rooms. Worked well for us. Our only complaint about the room was that the mattress was VERY firm, and it bothered my wife's sleep. But otherwise, the food was awesome, the staff VERY attentive and helpful, and the location cannot be beat. It was also VERY quiet in our room. Just a very nice stay.

ProsRoom Cleanliness, Location, Amenities, Connects you to the destination, recognition for ihg rewards club members

ConsSleep Quality

Yes, I recommend this hotel.
