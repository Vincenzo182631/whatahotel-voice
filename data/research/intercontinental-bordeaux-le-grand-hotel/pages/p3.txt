# InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand HotelDining

Gordon Ramsay's award-winning restaurant, Le Pressoir d Argent and Le Bordeaux, offers a culinary experience focused on classic French gastronomy while Le Bordeaux features gourmet cuisine inspired by history and the southwest region of France.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

### Relax on the rooftop

Elevated luxury awaits you on our rooftop. This urban oasis features unrivalled panoramic views over the city, lush plants, bird cages, lounge furniture, and our rooftop bar and is the place to see and be seen during the warmer months. Enjoy an immersive experience between nature and urbanism, while sipping a crafted cocktail with friends.

Learn more

### Our two Michelin star restaurant

Le Pressoir d’Argent Gordon Ramsay boasts a unique atmosphere alongside delectable and creative cuisine. Chef Gordon Ramsay's menu shines a spotlight on local produce. Delight in foie gras from the Landes, organic vegetables from Basque country, or Breton lobster, all of which pay tribute to French culinary heritage.

Learn more

### Le Bordeaux Restaurant

With respectful treatment of fresh local and regional produce, the menu of Le Bordeaux features the specialties of southwestern France. Our Pastry Chef showcases contemporary twists on traditional dessert recipes, while the wine menu boasts a large range of varietals, including a premier selection of Bordeaux wines.

Learn more

## Brasserie Le Bordeaux

Respectful of the quality and freshness of local and regional products, the menu of Le Bordeaux proposes specialities of southwestern France. Among the favourites on the menu: the roasted chicken supreme or the SouthWest duck foie gras will be on offer. Concerning the desserts list, our Pastry Chef revamped some traditional recipes. The wine menu offers a large range, including a fine selection of Bordeaux wine.

Breakfast is served in this restaurant: the price is EUR38 per person.

In a cozy atmosphere with a refined decoration largely imbued with English style, winter bar l’Orangerie offers a generous and creative selection of drinks and snacks to enhance moments. Come and enjoy the real “Goûter”, where guests meet in this colorful and friendly environment for a moment of relaxation around a selection of teas and pastries fines.
