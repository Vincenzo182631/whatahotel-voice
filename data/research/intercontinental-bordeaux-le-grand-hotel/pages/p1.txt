### Relax on the rooftop

Elevated luxury awaits you on our rooftop. This urban oasis features unrivalled panoramic views over the city, lush plants, bird cages, lounge furniture, and our rooftop bar and is the place to see and be seen during the warmer months. Enjoy an immersive experience between nature and urbanism, while sipping a crafted cocktail with friends.

Learn more

### Our two Michelin star restaurant

Le Pressoir d’Argent Gordon Ramsay boasts a unique atmosphere alongside delectable and creative cuisine. Chef Gordon Ramsay's menu shines a spotlight on local produce. Delight in foie gras from the Landes, organic vegetables from Basque country, or Breton lobster, all of which pay tribute to French culinary heritage.

Learn more

### Private and corporate events

By selecting InterContinental Bordeaux – Le Grand Hotel to host your event, you choose more than a destination. You favor a real art-of-living and the promise of unforgettable moments. Whether you're hosting an intimate event or grand meeting, our dedicated team will develop a custom event plan down to every last detail.

Learn more

### Le Bordeaux Restaurant

With respectful treatment of fresh local and regional produce, the menu of Le Bordeaux features the specialties of southwestern France. Our Pastry Chef showcases contemporary twists on traditional dessert recipes, while the wine menu boasts a large range of varietals, including a premier selection of Bordeaux wines.

Learn more

### Spa Guerlain

Guerlain, the prestigious house of fragrance and exceptional cosmetics, takes over the spa at the InterContinental Bordeaux – Le Grand Hôtel to offer a vast menu of relaxing treatments. Indulge in wellness during your stay and experience the feeling of rejuvenation.

Learn more

How much is parking at InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel?

At InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 45 EUR .

Reservation is required at the time of booking as there is limited availability. Private and secure parking under the hotel, with a valet service included. EV charging is available.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel?

Check-in at InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel is conveniently located near Aéroport de Bordeaux Mérignac (BOD), approximately 12.43 miles away (20 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel?

InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Cathedrale Saint-André - 0.43 miles away (0.7 kilometers)
- The Miroir d'Eau - 0.37 miles away (0.6 kilometers)
- CAPC Museum of Contemporary Art - 0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)
- Jardin Public - 0.56 miles away (0.9 kilometers)
- Arcachon Bay - 44.12 miles away (71 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand Hotel puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
