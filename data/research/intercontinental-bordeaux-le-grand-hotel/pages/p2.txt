# InterContinental Bordeaux - Le Grand HotelRooms & suites

Discover elegant rooms and suites inspired by the eighteenth century, featuring state-of-the-art modern amenities. The fabrics, upholstery, and carpets were designed by Jacques Garcia, creating an ambiance timeless beauty and grandeur. Some rooms offer views of the Opera and all accommodations promise an inviting place to relax after a day of exploration in charming Bordeaux.
