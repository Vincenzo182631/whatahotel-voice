# Steigenberger Icon Wiltcher's

Only the most legendary Steigenberger hotels are permitted to call themselves “Steigenberger Icons”. Nestled in the most exclusive district on the most prestigious venue Avenue Louise, the Wiltcher's invites you to feel the heartbeat of the city.
A unique blend of sophistication, history, and warmth make the Steigenberger Icon Wiltcher’s the ideal place for a quintessentially Belgian experience.

### INSPIRING, VIBRANT AND CHARMING

The passion and uncompromising five-star superior services are the Wiltcher’s mantra. Unrivaled attention to details ensures a smooth and comfortable stay, with the highest degree of pampering you ever dream of in one of our high-quality and elegant rooms.

Are you looking for a conference room in Brussels? Let our conference rooms convice you.
We will be happy to prepare a suitable offer for you.

Let us spoil you with culinary delights in our restaurants & bars.

Escape the stressful daily routine and relax in our SPA and wellness centre.

### Highlights

WiFi (complimentary), generous breakfast in the café, Loui Cocktail Bar & Restaurant with regional and international cuisine, refined bar, terrace, library, pool. spa, fitness gym (complementary) with modern cardio equipment, 13 conference rooms for up to 600 people.

### The Library

The library is equally popular among locals and hotel guests of the Steigenberger Icon Wiltcher's. Enjoy your drink in a cozy atmosphere and relax after an exciting sightseeing tour.

### Culinary highlights exclusively in the Loui Bar & Restaurant

Unique dishes and culinary delights await you at any time of the day at the award-winning Loui Bar & Restaurant. You can also enjoy a variety of delicious wines and end the evening in a relaxed atmosphere.

### Sightseeing in the Belgian metropolis

Discover the European capital during an extensive walk and get to know the most beautiful corners of Brussels. In addition to traditional foods such as Belgian beer, Belgian waffles or Belgian fries, you should also include the following attractions on your itinerary:

- Atomium - the Brussels landmark
- Grand Place/ Grote Markt - marvel at the magnificently decorated houses
- Manneken Pis - which of his 950 costumes is he wearing today?
- "European Quarter" - most of the institutions of the European Union are located here
- Brussels museums - from musical instrument collections to the works of René Magritte, there is much to see in Brussels museums!
