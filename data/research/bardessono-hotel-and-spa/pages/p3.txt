Bardessono Hotel and Spa
This secluded, eco-minded hideaway feels like your very own sophisticated, modern residence.

Why book?
A secluded, eco-minded hideaway that feels like your very own grown-up modern residence. It's the ultimate place to slip completely under the radar while being surrounded by world-renowned art and nature.
Set the scene
This chic Yountville property was recently awarded two Michelin Keys by the Michelin Guide, reserved for the most prestigious hotels in the country. It's quiet luxury here. Guests range from high-fashion socialites and international art and design aficionados to mother-daughter duos and wine enthusiasts. What sets the hotel apart is not only the modern architecture and design with a calming, earth-toned palette, but the eco factor. It's only one of seven LEED Platinum-certified hotels in the country (the highest rating by the
Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design
program) with zen-like gardens dotted with small wooden footpaths and water features; large-scale sculptures; an on-site restaurant; a spa with four treatment rooms; an outdoor rooftop pool; a fitness center; and a culinary garden. Walking through the serene setting, you might feel as though you've landed in a public art garden. The hotel serves as a backdrop for an expansive art collection with partner gallery, San Francisco–based Andrea Schwartz Gallery, with upcycled highlights including a digital photograph,
Green Earth, New Sky
, by
Cara Barer
; a life-size dog sculpture,
Stella
, made from remnant metal scraps by
Brian Mock
; and an abstract stainless steel sculpture,
Ace
, by
Albert Dicruttalo
.
The backstory
The property, dating to 1926, is the former homestead of Italian immigrants John and Lucy Bardessono, who along with John's brother-in-law, Martin Zucco, originally acquired the 20-acre land as a private estate and vineyard. (Lucy and John's portraits, along with black-and-white photos of their seven children, hang in the lobby.) Five acres now remain as the site of the hotel. Lucy carefully maintained a garden, aptly named Lucy's Garden, which still exists on the grounds—and serves as a culinary garden for
Lucy Restaurant & Bar
—with fresh veggies and herbs spanning pineapple quince, yuzu, tomato, garlic chive, strawberry, chocolate mint, and eggplant. In 2009, the hotel, restaurant, and spa opened as the greenest luxury hotel. A year later, it was awarded LEED Platinum Certification by the US Green Building Council.
The rooms
Bardessono unveiled a $1.8 million room renovation in 2024 that takes a refreshingly different approach to the wine country aesthetic. Think of these 62 spaces as sophisticated sanctuaries—modern, sleek, and intimate—done up in a palette of earth tones punctuated with pops of maroon and warm yellow, dark wood and leather. But what really sets these rooms apart is their dual identity: Each is designed to transform into a private spa suite—a concept that feels both innovative and somehow obvious (why hasn't everyone always done this?). Privacy screens glide into place, massage tables appear as if by magic, and suddenly the room becomes your own personal wellness retreat.
If you are really looking to splurge, the crown jewel is the Maple Grove Villa, where wine connoisseurs can enjoy one-on-one tastings with Master Sommelier Erik Elliott from Lawrence Wine Estates—the kind of intimate experience that makes even the most jaded wine country travelers take note.
Food and drink
At Lucy, Bardessono's signature restaurant, farm-to-table is built into the restaurant's fabric. The culinary team harvests vegetables and fruits from the property’s onsite garden (often landing on the plate the same day they're picked). What makes Executive Chef Rick Edge's approach interesting is how he flips the usual fine dining script: instead of starting with proteins and building around them, vegetables are the star of the menu. The garden becomes a kind of living pantry, with the team even germinating their own hybrid, organic seeds. This vegetable forward approach is evident in the thoughtful pairings of the food, whether it is the short rib with turnip purée, truffled cabbage, or the roasted cauliflower with house red curry and spinach.
The spa
B Spa is equipped with a private, one-to-two-person sauna accessed via the locker room, and an extensive spa menu with a salt stone massage using warm Himalayan salt stones, and four types of age-defying oxygen facials utilizing an exclusive line of skincare products by London-based
Linda Meredith
. (It's the only spa in the state that carries it.) Just in case you don't feel like leaving the room, let the spa come to you. There's a massage table in each room closet for added convenience, and a spa butler can be arranged to draw a customized bath in the soaking tub.
The neighborhood/area
Bardessono is based in Yountville, a small Napa Valley town that feels like a charming French village transplanted to California. Days here have a cinematic quality. In the early morning hot air balloons drift over the vineyards that surround the town; locals and visitors line up for golden flaky croissants at
Bouchon Bakery
. By afternoon, visitors wander between Michael Chiarello's
Bottega
restaurant and the
V Marketplace
, a historic winery-turned-retail complex housed in a beautiful 1874 stone building. The pace here is deliberately slow, and the location is excellent for food enthusiasts who plan their vacations around restaurants and wine, and for those who appreciate being able to walk to dinner after a day of wine tasting. Yountville has earned its reputation as a culinary paradise, anchored by Thomas Keller's legendary
The French Laundry
, and local favorites like
Bistro Jeanty
and
Mustards Grill
.
The service
The service is impeccable here from the bellmen to the front desk staff. The on-site concierge, Matt Brush, can arrange for everything from dinner recommendations and reservations to transportation via the house Lexus car (within 2.2 miles to the north and 1.4 miles to the south) to nearby wineries such as
Mira Winery
,
Elyse Winery
,
Chandon
, and
Hailstone Vineyards
. The hotel also has a partnership with
Quintessa
and
Alpha Omega Winery
for complimentary tastings. If you prefer to go at your own pace, check out one of the resort's bikes available for use during your stay.
Eco effort
As a LEED Platinum–certified property, there's not much about the hotel that isn't thought out from an eco-friendly lens, from bathrooms with low-water flow toilets and earth-conscious Terra Pure green tea bath products to the salvaged Monterey Cypress, walnut, and California Bay trees used for exterior cladding on select buildings.
Accessibility
Bardessono offers four ADA-compliant rooms that maintain their signature sophisticated aesthetic and high-end amenities. These accessible suites, ranging from 550 to 780 square feet, neatly incorporate necessary adaptations. You'll find all the accessibility features here—extra-wide doorways, roll-in showers with hand-held heads and benches, and strategically placed grab bars. Portable hearing assistance devices are standard in every ADA suite, and the ground-level locations ensure easy access to the property's amenities. Some suites offer flexible configurations, like the 780-square-foot option with both, a king bed and a queen bed in a separate alcove—ideal for those traveling with caregivers or family. The rooms maintain Bardessono's signature spa suite concept, allowing for in-room treatments with proper accessibility considerations.
Anything left to mention?
Bardessono's Vintages in the Villa, a partnership with Lawrence Wine Estates, has to be one of Napa Valley’s most exclusive wine experience. We began at Heitz, where we met Andrea, our Estate Host. We stood among the vines, glasses in hand, tasting wines in their birthplace against the stunning backdrop of the Mayacamas mountains. The experience culminated at The Salon at Heitz Cellar with vertical tastings of Trailside Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon and pours of the legendary Martha's Vineyard Cab, all in a breathtaking space that manages to feel both rarefied and remarkably comfortable. While at Stony Hill, Estate Director Michaela welcomed us with chamomile tea in crystal—a sophisticated pause between tastings. The Residence at Stony Hill has a lovely patio offering expansive views across steep terraced vineyards and beautifully designed indoor spaces where we tasted limited portfolio wines, including some rare library vintages, while John Coltrane vinyl played in the background.
