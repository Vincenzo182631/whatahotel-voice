## Exceptional by Nature

@HOTELREANIMAR

Bardessono Hotel & Spa in Yountville, California was awarded Two Michelin Keys in 2025 by the renowned Michelin Guide. This honor places Bardessono among just 70 hotels in the U.S. recognized for offering an experience that goes beyond accommodation.

The distinction celebrates excellence in design, service, character, and a hotel’s overall contribution to the guest journey. In the heart of Napa Valley, Bardessono offers a stay defined by refined luxury and a true sense of place.
