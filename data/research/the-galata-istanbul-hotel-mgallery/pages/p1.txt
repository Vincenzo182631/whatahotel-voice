## The Galata Hotel Istanbul Collection

### The Galata Hotel Istanbul - Mgallery Collection

* * *

From the very first step you take into Galata, you are embraced by thousands of years of fascinating history. The traces of different civilizations are still vividly present in this historic district, where the Golden Horn meets the Bosphorus and the spirit of Istanbul reveals itself in every detail. Here, peace, comfort, and refined hospitality await you in one of the city’s most distinguished hotels.

The Galata Istanbul Hotel – MGallery, reborn within the historic walls of Baltazzi Han, one of Galata’s most magnificent and rare buildings dating back to 1836, has been welcoming its guests with an unforgettable accommodation experience since 2018. Blending timeless heritage with contemporary elegance, the hotel offers a unique sense of place that honors its past while embracing modern luxury.

Hidden within the hotel’s garden, the Historical Cesme Hammam has been sharing the healing power of water since 1720. Honored with the World Luxury SPA Awards in 2022 and 2023,2024 and 2025 the hammam offers an exceptional wellness experience admired by guests from around the world. Our restaurant complements your stay with memorable culinary moments, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere where you can enjoy delicious experiences with your loved ones. During the summer months, the open air setting with views of the Golden Horn offers a truly unique backdrop for unforgettable moments.

At The Galata Istanbul Hotel  MGallery, you are invited to touch the history and spirit of Istanbul, indulge yourself, and create lasting memories while experiencing the privilege of being—and living at the very heart of the city.
