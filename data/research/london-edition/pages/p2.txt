# Rooms

Stylish in their simplicity and decadent in their details, guest rooms recall the interior of a luxury yacht with oak floors and walls paneled in light or dark walnut wood. A cantilevered desk, tufted George Smith lounge chair and gold-framed photograph from Hendrik Kerstens adds an artful touch.

# Suites

Our suites offer a large living area that includes a George Smith sofa and a gold‑framed photograph by Hendrik Kerstens. Select suites, including the Penthouse, feature oversized terraces with views of London.

# Connecting Rooms & Suites

For those seeking additional space, select rooms and suites at The London EDITION can be connected to form expansive two-bedroom configurations. These spacious combinations blend the sophisticated warmth of wood-paneled walls with timeless interiors, offering a tranquility in the heart of the city.
