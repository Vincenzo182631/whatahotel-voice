## From London City Airport

## **FROM LONDON CITY AIRPORT**

_Private Car:_

A private car to/from London City starts from approximately £190 each way and can be arranged by emailing the hotel concierge on concierge.london@editionhotels.com prior to your arrival/departure.

_Taxi:_

Taxis are available at the airport on a first-come first-served basis. This will cost you approximately £50 and will take approximately 50 minutes.

_Train:_

Dockland Light Railway trains make trips to Bank station in central London. The trip takes approximately 25 minutes and costs £5 per person each way.

After arriving at Bank station:

• Take the Central Line (westbound)

• Get off at Oxford Circus

• Exit the station and walk down Oxford Street toward Tottenham Court Road. Berners Street is on the left (opposite Wardour Street)

Alternatively, you may take a Docklands Light Railway train and then a taxi. A taxi from Bank station is approximately £15.

_Driving:_

This route will take about 50 minutes and includes tolls (our Hotel is within the Congestion Charge and Ultra-Low Emission Zone).

• Head west on Hartmann Road towards Camel Road

• Turn right onto Connaught Road/A112

• At the roundabout, take the first exit and stay on Connaught Road/A112

• At the next roundabout, take the first exit onto Connaught Bridge/A1020

• At the next roundabout, take the third exit onto A1020

• Take the ramp to Isle of Dogs/Blackwall Tunnel/A102/A13

• At the roundabout, take the second exit onto Lower Lea Crossing/A1020

• At the roundabout, take the second exit onto the Aspen Way/A1261 ramp to the City/A13/Canary Wharf/Isle of Dogs

• Take a slight left onto Aspen Way/A1261

• Make a slight right onto Limehouse Link/A1203 and continue to follow A1203

• Turn left onto Tower Hill/A100

• Continue onto Lower Thames Street/A3211

• Turn right onto Northumberland Avenue/A400

• Turn left onto A401

• Turn right onto Wardour Street and continue onto Berners Street

_Parking:_

Parking is available at Q-Park on Poland Street and is a 5-minute walk from The London EDITION. Charges are approximately £65 per night.

## From London Gatwick

## FROM **LONDON GATWICK**

_Private Car:_

A private car to/from London Gatwick starts from approximately £250 each way and can be arranged by emailing the hotel concierge on concierge.london@editionhotels.com prior to your arrival/departure.

_Taxi:_

Taxis are available at a kiosk within Gatwick Airport and cost approximately £220 each way. The journey to the hotel is approximately 80 minutes.

_Train:_

Gatwick Express trains make non-stop trips to Central London (Victoria station). Trains depart daily every 15-20 minutes between 5am and midnight. Single journey tickets start at approximately £20 per person each way and take 30 minutes to reach Victoria Station.

After arriving at Victoria station, take the Underground (Victoria Line, northbound), and get off at Oxford Circus. Exit the station and walk down Oxford Street toward Tottenham Court Road. Berners Street is on the left (opposite Wardour Street)

The Victoria Line train is approximately £5 per person, each way. Alternatively, you may take the Gatwick Express and a taxi. A taxi from Victoria station is approximately £15-£20.

_Driving:_

This route will take approximately one hour, and includes tolls (our Hotel is within the Congestion Charge and Ultra-Low Emission Zone).

• Head southwest towards Ring Road North

• Exit the roundabout onto M23 heading to London/Brighton/Crawley/Dartford/Heathrow Airport/M25

• At the roundabout, take the first exit onto the M23 ramp to M25 London/Dartford/Heathrow Airport

• Merge onto M23

• Take a slight left onto Brighton Road/A23

• Take the first left onto Banstead Road/A23, then turn left onto Purley Way/A23

• Turn left onto A203, then right onto Clapham Road/A203/A3

• Turn left onto Parry Street/A23036

• Take the first right onto Wandsworth Road/A3036, then turn right onto Millbank/A3212

• Take a slight right onto A302

• Turn left onto Parliament Street/A3212

• Take the third exit onto Trafalgar Square/A4/A400 at the roundabout and turn left onto A401

• Turn right onto Wardour Street, and continue onto Berners Street

_Parking:_

Parking is available at Q-Park on Poland Street and is a 5-minute walk from The London EDITION. Charges are approximately £65 per night.

## From London Heathrow

## FROM LONDON HEATHROW

_Private Car:_

A private car to/from London Heathrow starts from approximately £190 each way and can be arranged by emailing the hotel concierge on concierge.london@editionhotels.com prior to your arrival/departure.

_Taxi:_

Taxis are available at the airport on a first-come, first-served basis and are approximately £120 each way. The journey to the hotel is approximately one hour.

_The Underground (Elizabeth Line):_

A single journey from Heathrow on the Elizabeth line costs approximately £13–£15 per person (using contactless) and takes around 40–50 minutes to reach the hotel.

• From Heathrow Airport, take the Elizabeth line toward Abbey Wood or Shenfield

• Get off at Tottenham Court Road station

• Follow signs for the Dean Street exit (Central / Elizabeth line)

• Walk west along Oxford Street toward Oxford Circus

• Turn right onto Berners Street

• The London EDITION will be on your right

_Train:_

Those traveling light can take the Heathrow Express overground train, which makes nonstop trips to Paddington Station in central London. Trains depart daily every 15 minutes between 5am and midnight. Single journey tickets start at approximately £25 per person each way and take 15 to 20 minutes to reach Paddington Station. From there, take a taxi to the hotel, which takes about 15 minutes and costs approximately £15.

_Driving:_

This route will take approximately one hour and includes tolls (our Hotel is within the Congestion Charge and Ultra-Low Emission Zone).

• Head south on Control Tower Road towards Inner Ring West

• Take the first right onto Inner Ring West, and continue onto Tunnel Road

• Take a slight left onto M4 (following signs for Central London/The West/M25/Gatwick Airport)

• At the roundabout, take the third exit onto the M4 ramp to Central London/Hammersmith/Hayes/Heathrow Airport

• Merge onto M4

• Continue onto Great West Road/A4 and continue to follow A4

• Turn right onto Knightsbridge/A4

• Turn left onto Picadilly Circus/A4

• Turn right to stay on Piccadilly Circus/A4

• Continue onto A401

• Turn left onto Wardour Street and continue onto Berners Street

_Parking:_

Parking is available at Q-Park on Poland Street and is a 5-minute walk from The London EDITION. Charges are approximately £65 per night.
