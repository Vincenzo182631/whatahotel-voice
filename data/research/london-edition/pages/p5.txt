# Lobby Bar

**Lobby Bar**

A twist on a traditional hotel space, the Lobby Bar was conceived for both work and play. Eclectic art, including a stainless steel orb reflecting the entire scene, is paired with iconic British and European design elements. And connections, real and virtual, are encouraged with a communal table and complimentary Wi-Fi. The bar offers twists on classic cocktails and overlooks intimate seating areas with tufted sofas and a snooker table.

No reservations. Walk-ins only.

\*Please note this is a sample menu and subject to change.

**Punch Room**

RESERVE

Echoes is Punch Room’s newest cocktail menu, inspired by the idea of call and response. Taking classic cocktails as a point of departure, each serve offers a contemporary interpretation through unexpected flavour pairings, refined techniques and the bar’s signature approach to cocktail craftsmanship.

From the rich, layered notes of The Third Act to the elegant complexity of Blue Tie and the refreshing character of Bella Vista Punch, the menu explores the ways in which great cocktails continue to evolve over time. Blending heritage with innovation, Echoes celebrates familiar favourites not as fixed recipes, but as living ideas that inspire new perspectives with every pour.

Discover more and reserve your table to experience Echoes.

For inquiries please contact us at +44 (0) 20 7781 0000 or James.Bradley@editionhotels.com.

**Private Dining Room**

RESERVE View Website View Brochure

Whether you’re planning an intimate dinner, a milestone celebration, or a discreet business meeting, Berners Tavern provides the best private dining London experiences with exceptional cuisine, attentive service, and refined ambiance.

Situated above Berners Tavern, our Private Dining Room offers an intimate and refined setting for up to 12 guests—ideal for special occasions, celebrations, and discreet gatherings. Designed around a modern dark-walnut table with rose-upholstered chairs, this space is illuminated by an original historic skylight, creating a warm and atmospheric backdrop. This thoughtfully designed room stands apart among the most distinctive private dining rooms London has to offer.

For reservations, you can contact our team at +44 020 7908 7979 or by emailing bernerstavern@editionhotels.com.

**The Social Table**

RESERVE Website

The Social Table in the Berners Tavern main dining room offers an exceptional setting for gatherings of 9 to 16 guests. Positioned close to the bar for seamless hosting, yet set slightly apart from the vibrant centre of the restaurant, it strikes the ideal balance between atmosphere and comfort. Perfect for everything from business breakfasts to celebratory dinners, this spacious table is available for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, allowing guests to enjoy the signature buzz of Berners Tavern.
