# Berners Tavern

## Berners Tavern

RESERVE VIEW WEBSITE

Berners Tavern, helmed by acclaimed chef Jason Atherton, seamlessly combines historical charm with modern sophistication. Located within The London EDITION on Berners Street in Fitzrovia, serving contemporary British fare for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, including Saturday brunch and Sunday lunch on the weekends.
