# Services & Amenities

### For further information

Please contact Concierge at +44 020 7908 7924 or concierge.london@editionhotels.com

GUEST SERVICES

- 173 unique rooms and suites, some with oversized furnished terraces
- One-of-a-kind 195 square metre / 2098 square foot Penthouse with expansive wrap-around landscaped terrace and 360 degree views of London
- Berners Tavern Restaurant with Private Dining Room by Michelin starred Executive Chef Jason Atherton
- Customised banquet menus for private events, luncheons, dinner parties and business meetings
- 24-hour Room Service
- Concierge Service
- Twice daily housekeeping
- Complimentary high-speed WiFi access throughout the hotel
- Complimentary use of the 24-hour fitness centre
- Executive Boardroom, Meeting Room and Private Event Space
- A restaurant – Berners Tavern – and two bars, Lobby Bar and Punch Room

EXECUTIVE SERVICES

- Executive Boardroom, Meeting Room and Private Event Space
- Complimentary high-speed WiFi access throughout the hotel
- State-of-the-art audio and visual equipment
- Video Conferencing
- Executive car services available

FITNESS CENTRE

- State-of-the-art fitness equipment including cardiovascular machines with personal flat screen TVs
- Personal training sessions available on request
- Free weights
- Stretching area
- Complimentary towels
- Headphones and refreshments
- Accessible 24 hours a day

ACCESSIBILITY

- Mobility accessible rooms available for certain room types
