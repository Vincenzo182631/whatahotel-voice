# Goldener Hirsch

## A European-style Chalet with Modern Mountain Residences

Goldener Hirsch seamlessly blends a charming Bavarian-inspired inn with sleek modern residences designed by award-winning architect Tom Kundig. Nestled mid-mountain at world-renowned Deer Valley Resort, guests are granted immediate access to endless picture perfect pistes, where 202 runs wind through the region’s majestic aspen groves and evergreen glades. Located steps from the lifts, this family-owned property has been an iconic fixture in Silver Lake Village for more than 30 years.
