# A Winter Wonderland Awaits in Park City

Check Rates Contact UsCheck Rates 435.649.7770 Email us

A 45-minute drive from Salt Lake City International Airport puts you at 8,100 feet in the Wasatch Mountains of Park City, where hundreds of runs are kept in perfect condition for skiers of all levels. Located mid-mountain in Deer Valley Resort, Goldener Hirsch is steps from the chairlifts and offers private ski lockers, ski valet, and transportation to the region’s various world-class resorts. Carve the slopes in the company of an Olympic champion at Deer Valley, known for its impeccably groomed pistes and heated sidewalks. Or hit the tracks at Park City Mountain and finish off the day by skiing or snowboarding right into town for a High West whiskey cocktail.

- Deer Valley chairlifts are located steps from Goldener Hirsch \| Deer Valley is a ski-only resort

- Private ski lockers \| Ski Concierge available on property \| Convenient on-snow ski storage in Silver Lake Village

- Opening weekend for the 2026-27 season at Deer Valley Resort begins Friday, December 5 for Season Pass holders, with the resort opening to the public in early December

- Opening weekend for the 2026-27 season at Park City Mountain Resort begins Friday, November 20

- 202 runs in Deer Valley and over 7,300 skiable acres in Park City Mountain \| Suitable for beginner to advanced skiers

- Our ski concierge can arrange regular transport to and from any other mountains

### First Tracks

Glide through untouched powder in Deer Valley's First Tracks program. On select dates throughout the 2025-26 season, Deer Valley and IKON season pass holders head straight to the chairlift to access the mountain at 8:30AM, giving them a half hour of skiing before the public opening.

-

### Ski School

Earning a reputation as the “gold standard” of instruction, Deer Valley’s award-winning ski school provides customized lessons for adults and children to improve their technique and have more fun on the mountain. Many guests return to the same instructor season after season to keep honing their skills and reaching their goals.

-

### Ski with a Champion

Embark on an unforgettable adventure across Deer Valley’s expansive terrain with a world-class Olympian by your side. Enjoy a half- or full-day experience with one of six Olympic ambassadors, complete with access to the Ski School lane at every chairlift. This isn’t just a lesson; it’s a unique opportunity to carve the mountain alongside a snowsport legend.

## Heli-Skiing & Snowboarding

For those who truly want space, solitude and an unrivaled off-the-beaten-path experience, Goldener Hirsch’s sister property, The Lodge at Blue Sky, offers helicopter adventures further afield in Utah's remote, high-alpine wilderness. While Deer Valley is a ski only resort, snowboarders will find runs for all abilities at Park City Mountain Resort.
