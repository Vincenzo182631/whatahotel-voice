## High Altitude Healing

Nourish. Restore. Align. The clean air of the mountains and healing powers of the surrounding herbal landscape inspire the bespoke treatments for holistic wellbeing - using pure, intentional products.

- **Skin Rejuvenation**– Reveal your natural radiance with treatments that harness mineral-rich ingredients, alpine botanicals and mountain-sourced hydration to leave skin luminous.

- **Massages**– Restore balance and vitality with personalized bodywork inspired by the rhythms of the Wasatch Range, using warm oils and grounding techniques to melt tension and revive the senses.

- **Bath, Sound & Wellbeing Experiences**– Surrender to the serenity of the mountains through immersive rituals that feature reiki, resonant sound, and mindful intention to harmonize body and spirit.

## Offerings for Renewal

-

### Post-mountain Restoration

Return from a day of activity to an in-room massage or private yoga session, resting and recuperating your muscles to maximize your performance on the slopes.

-

### Sauna

Relax in our dry sauna, which can be found on the top floor of The Residences. The benefits of sauna bathing include detoxification, increased metabolism, weight loss and improved blood circulation. Stretching in the sauna can result in a 205 percent improvement in flexibility.

-

### Pool

Take in the fresh mountain air and views over Deer Valley from the rooftop pool and infinity hot tub, whilst resting weary muscles from a full day of activity.
