## Timeless Alpine Charm. Modern Elegance.

A longstanding architectural gem in the heart of Deer Valley, Goldener Hirsch blends the iconic charm of an Austrian-inspired chalet with the sleek lines of modern mountain residences. The 64 guestrooms, suites and residences set across three buildings, house alpine-styled accommodations with antique furnishings as well as luxurious multi-bedroom residences idyllic for extended stays and multi-generational families.

## Unforgettable Accommodations

## Residences

Modern silhouettes featuring high ceilings and massive windows house one to three-bedroom residences with private balconies, chef’s kitchens, and gracious dining areas. Evoking the spirit of the Austrian Alps through a contemporary design lens, these mountain retreats are idyllic for multi-generational families and extended stays. Warm and welcoming, the Residences bring family and friends closer to nature and closer to one another. Accessible rooms are available.

##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is Goldener Hirsch located?** Goldener Hirsch is located at 7520 Royal Street East in Silver Lake Village at Deer Valley Resort, offering a mid-mountain setting just steps from the chairlifts and easy access to year-round adventures in the Wasatch Mountains.

**How far is Goldener Hirsch from the airport?** Goldener Hirsch is approximately 40 miles, or a 45-minute drive, from Salt Lake City International Airport (SLC). Airport transportation can be arranged in advance through the concierge with one of the hotel’s trusted private transportation providers.

**When is the best time to visit Goldener Hirsch?** Goldener Hirsch is a year-round mountain destination, with each season offering a different way to experience Deer Valley. Winter brings world-class skiing and snow-filled adventures, while warmer months are ideal for hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding, fly-fishing and exploring the surrounding Wasatch Mountains.

**What experiences and activities are available at Goldener Hirsch?** Goldener Hirsch offers year-round experiences inspired by its mountain setting and Austrian heritage. Depending on the season, guests can enjoy skiing, hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding and fly-fishing alongside wellness programming and hands-on culinary experiences such as pretzel making, tastings and cocktail classes.

**What ski amenities and services does Goldener Hirsch offer?** Located steps from the Deer Valley chairlifts, Goldener Hirsch offers private ski lockers, a dedicated Ski Concierge, ski valet and convenient on-snow storage in Silver Lake Village. The Ski Concierge can also assist with equipment and arrange transportation to other area mountains.

**Does Goldener Hirsch have a pool?** Yes. Goldener Hirsch has a heated rooftop pool and infinity-edge hot tub with views of the surrounding Wasatch Mountains. Both are available to hotel guests year-round, offering a relaxing place to unwind after a day on the mountain.

**Does Goldener Hirsch offer transportation around Deer Valley and Park City?** Yes. Goldener Hirsch offers complimentary transportation within a four-mile radius of the hotel, making it easy to explore Deer Valley and nearby Park City. Historic Main Street is approximately five minutes away and accessible via the hotel’s complimentary shuttle.
