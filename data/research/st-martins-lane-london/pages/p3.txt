## You ask…

### We answer

##### **What dining options are available at St Martins Lane?**

The hotel offers Restaurant 45, EllaMia café, The Den, and Blind Spot, a hidden speakeasy bar.

##### **Does the hotel offer breakfast?**

Yes, breakfast is served daily at Restaurant 45 with a variety of seasonal and international options.

##### **Is there a bar at St Martins Lane London**?

Yes, Blind Spot is a hidden cocktail bar, alongside The Den which serves drinks in a relaxed setting.

##### **Does St Martins Lane offer afternoon tea?**

Yes, afternoon tea is served in The Den, including themed and seasonal experiences.

##### **Can non-guests visit the restaurants and bars?**

Yes, all dining venues are open to both hotel guests and visitors.
