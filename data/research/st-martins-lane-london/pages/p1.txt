## You ask…

### We answer

##### **What makes St Martins Lane London unique?**

St Martins Lane London is a design-led hotel by Philippe Starck in Covent Garden, blending bold interiors with vibrant dining and a hidden speakeasy bar.

##### **Where is St Martins Lane London located?**

The hotel is located on St Martin’s Lane in Covent Garden, in London’s West End, close to major attractions and theatres.

##### **Is St Martins Lane a boutique or luxury hotel?**

St Martins Lane is a lifestyle boutique hotel offering upscale design, spacious rooms, and premium amenities in central London.

##### **Is St Martins Lane London suitable for business or leisure stays?**

The hotel is ideal for both business and leisure, offering central access, meeting spaces, and a vibrant West End location.

##### **Who designed St Martins Lane London?**

The hotel was designed by internationally renowned designer Philippe Starck, known for his bold and innovative style.
