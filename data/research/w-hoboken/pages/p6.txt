## Discover our newly renovated hotel rooms in Hoboken

Step into the reimagined W Hoboken, a destination designed to inspire fabulous moments. The hotel is fresh off a multimillion-dollar renovation, with a new vision inspired by the waterfront, W/H social, and soft power. A newly refreshed Welcome Area and Living Room Bar set the stage for socializing and unwinding with modern design, while guest rooms, suites and our iconic Penthouses have been boldly transformed at our renovated waterfront hotel in Hoboken.

## Reimagined Luxury: The Penthouses Return Anew

The newly reimagined Penthouses at W Hoboken debut as bold, high‑energy retreats designed for elevated gatherings. Sculptural curves, sunset‑inspired hues, and mirrored accents create an immersive backdrop that shifts effortlessly from day to night, while subtle nods to Hoboken’s architectural and industrial heritage ground the spaces in a sense of place. Expansive living areas with statement seating, layered textures, and integrated dining and bar setups transform each penthouse into a modern social hub in the sky, perfect for celebrations, bridal parties, and unforgettable group moments.
