# Explore Hoboken from our hotel, located near Journal Square

## Explore Hoboken and the New York City Metro Area

Sitting on the Hudson River waterfront overlooking the Manhattan skyline, our NJ hotel near Manhattan makes for an enchanting urban escape. Experience the vibrant town's streets of bustling local restaurants, bars and boutiques combined with lush parks and walkways including Pier A Park, Sinatra Park, and nearby Liberty State Park. The NYC scene can be at your fingertips in moments, with effortless access to a Path train or ferry ride.

## W Hoboken is Redefining Elevated Luxury—And You’re Invited.

Where sweeping skyline views converge with modern maritime aesthetic, our newly reimagined spaces invite connection and celebration, inspired by the rich heritage and charm of Hoboken. From bespoke mixology and curated bites in the Living Room Bar to guestrooms that embrace tranquility while showcasing New York City, discover all W Hoboken has to offer. Welcome to the Sixth Borough.

### Prime Location

In a prime location, our hotel near the Hudson waterfront, W Hoboken, is at the epicenter of this tight-knit city that offers top dining, exhilarating nightlife, and noteworthy cultural attractions all within a mile.

### Explore the Area

Take advantage of the many things to do in the Mile Square City and the exciting array of restaurants, bars, stores, and attractions that are just steps from the hotel spa in Hoboken. Enjoy proximity to Manhattan – just one stop away via train or ferry - and nearby attractions like the Statue of Liberty, Liberty State Park, and the Meadowlands.

Local Area & Activity Highlights

### Washington Street Dining & Shopping

Experience the sights and sounds of bustling downtown Hoboken along Washington Street. Indulge in a variety of culinary experiences, shop retail stores across beloved local and national brands, and explore the beautiful architecture of Hoboken where modern day designs meet untapped historical landmarks.

### Pier A Park and The Hoboken Waterfront

Enjoy uninterrupted skyline views of Manhattan from Pier A Park, perched perfectly along the breathtaking Hoboken Waterfront. Enjoy lush and expansive green spaces and gorgeous outdoor walkways that extend out to the pier. Sit along benches or gazebos within the park to soak in the views or enjoy a plethora of recreational amenities, dining experiences, and endless outdoor experiences along the waterfront.

Explore

### Brookfield Place and World Trade Center

The ever-evolving downtown Manhattan is home to the bustling World Trade Center site boasting contemporary architecture and luxury dining and shopping experiences at Westfield and Brookfield Place. Make a day along the Manhattan waterfront with endless retail experiences from the world’s most sought-after designers and enjoy cocktail & culinary culture like never before at some of downtown's best restaurants.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Hoboken have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Hoboken offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as 2 Peloton bikes, boxing gloves & speedball, yoga mats, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Hoboken have an on-site spa?

Yes, Woodhouse Day Spa is located on-site at W Hoboken. Click here to book a service now.
