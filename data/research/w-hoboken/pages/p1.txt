# Welcome to W Hoboken

## Stunning NYC views on the Hoboken waterfront

We just know you're going to love your stay at W Hoboken. Perched on the waterfront directly across from New York City, our Hoboken waterfront hotel is steps from the PATH and NY Waterway Ferry, enabling you to access Manhattan with ease. Closer to home, you can stroll to Hoboken’s vibrant dining, exhilarating nightlife and noteworthy cultural attractions like the Hoboken Historical Museum. Stretch...See More out in gorgeously designed hotel rooms and suites, many of which showcase grand NYC views. Break a sweat at FIT, our 1,520-square-foot gym, which offers Hudson River and city views, followed by a local, sustainable meal at Halifax. And cap off your evening in Hoboken with cocktails and conversation at The Living Room. If you're planning an event in the Hoboken area, you'll be captivated by our hotel’s venues, which include the stunning Great Room and Lulu's Outdoor Patio in our hotel in Hoboken, NJ with an NYC view. When it comes to hotels that have it all, W Hoboken is one of the best deals around.See Less

### Taking a Stance with Sustainability

W Hoboken is keeping it green by following a wide range of environmental practices.

General

**Tracks energy use** Regularly measures and records energy use (at least 4 times a year)

**Towel reuse program** Programme includes housekeeper training and regular checks

**Linen reuse program** Programme includes housekeeper training and regular checks

**Recycles waste** Recycles at least two types of waste, such as paper, glass, plastic and cardboard

**Recycling bins in guest rooms** Guests sort trash and recyclable waste into separate bins in their room

**Recycling sorted by staff** Staff separate trash and recyclable waste

**Staff training on green practices** Staff trained at least once a year on implementing the hotel's green practices

**Education for guests on green practices** Guests can learn about the property's green practices before or during their stay

Energy

**ENERGY STAR kitchen appliances** ENERGY STAR appliances are used in the kitchen

**A/C temperature set to save energy** Indoor temperature set at a minimum of 72 F during the summer

**Heating temperature set to save energy** Indoor temperature set at a maximum of 72 F during the winter

**Efficient heating/air-conditioning system** Efficient boilers, chillers, furnaces and/or heat pumps

**Preventative maintenance plan** Regular checks to ensure energy and water equipment is working efficiently

**Completed energy assessment of building** Evaluation to identify ways for property to save energy

**Sensors adjusts the temperature when a guest room is empty** Sensors in over 90% of guest rooms

Water

**Tracks water use** Regularly measures and records water use (at least 4 times a year)

**Water-efficient faucets in guest bathrooms** Over 90% of guest room faucets use 1.5 gallons per minute or less

**Water-efficient faucets in public restrooms** Over 90% of staff and public faucets use 1.5 gallons per minute or less

Purchasing

**Recycled office paper** Over 50% recycled content

**"Green" certified cleaning products** Over 75% of cleaning products are certified

**Over 75% of flooring/carpeting has "green" characteristics** Such as recycled content, renewable or salvaged materials, or being locally sourced

Waste

**Tracks waste** Regularly measures and records waste (at least 4 times a year)

**Completed waste assessment of building** Property has evaluated the amount and type of waste it creates, in order to plan how to reduce it

**Recycles used cooking oil** At least 90% of used cooking oil is recycled or used for biodiesel

**Safe disposal of hazardous materials** Such as paint, printer cartridges, batteries and fluorescent lightbulbs

**Leftover materials donated or re-used** Such as toiletries, food, furniture and electronics

**Composts yard waste** Yard waste is composted on-site or at an offsite composting facility

Site

**Non-toxic pest control** Integrated pest management uses non toxic methods (e.g. traps, baits, gels) to control pests

Education & Innovation

**Education for guests on local environment** Education about local environmental issues such as local habitats and conservation efforts

**Low VOC paints** Low volatile organic compound (VOC) paints, primers, adhesives, and sealants are used

Transportation Options

### NY Waterway Ferry

One of the fastest and easiest ways to go between Hoboken and Manhattan, the NY Waterway Ferry takes just 10 to 15 minutes from the Pier 11/Wall Street Ferry or World Financial Center Ferry to Hoboken.

### Wheels Valet Parking

Arrive in style. Let Wheels(sm) valet parking take care of your vehicle while you revel inside. Valet is available 24/7. Self-parking is not available.

### City Rail System

All seven lines of the NJ Transit run through Hoboken from statewide — fares and schedules vary depending on travel destination, day, and time. The NJ Transit connects Hoboken to the airport on the North Jersey Coast Line.

### Path

City hop your way to Manhattan in under 15 minutes with this primary transit link, the PATH. The Port Authority Trans-Hudson (PATH) is a 13.8-mile rapid transit system in the northeastern New Jersey cities of Newark, Harrison, Jersey City, and Hoboken, as well as Lower and Midtown Manhattan in New York City. An easy transition from our Hoboken hotel near NYC.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Hoboken?

The check-in time at W Hoboken is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Hoboken allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Hoboken. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Hoboken?

Off-site valet parking is available at W Hoboken. The daily valet fee is $50.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Hoboken have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Hoboken has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Hoboken booking.

What is the closest airport near W Hoboken?

The closest airport near W Hoboken is Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR). EWR is located approximately 11.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Hoboken have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Hoboken has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
