### Discover JW Marriott's Epicurean Journey

Discover the culture, flavors, and traditions of New Orleans through a collection of daily culinary and cultural experiences. Inspired by the city's rich heritage, each experience offers a unique opportunity to connect with the authentic spirit of the Crescent City.

A Curated Week of New Orleans Flavor & Tradition

### Friday/Wed: Bitters, Botanicals & Bubbles

Inspired by New Orleans' legendary cocktail culture, this sensory experience features locally influenced botanicals, aromatic bitters, and sparkling creations that pay homage to the city's rich mixology heritage. Live music in the Lobby Lounge completes the evening's celebration of local flavor and culture.

Learn More

### Saturday: Expressions of the French Quarter

Explore the artistic spirit of New Orleans through encounters with local poets, artists, and storytellers whose creativity reflects the culture, character, and enduring charm of the French Quarter.

Learn More

### Sunday: A Taste of New Orleans

Indulge in a quintessential New Orleans ritual with warm beignets and café au lait, a beloved pairing that celebrates the city's French heritage and appreciation for life's simple pleasures.

Learn More

### Monday: A Timeless New Orleans Tradition

Celebrate one of the city's most enduring culinary customs with red beans and rice, a dish traditionally enjoyed on Mondays throughout New Orleans. Discover the history behind this cherished local favorite and its lasting place in the city's culture.

Learn More

### Tuesday: Crafted in Louisiana

Explore the distinctive flavors of Louisiana through a curated tasting of regional craft beers, showcasing local ingredients, brewing traditions, and the spirit of hospitality that defines New Orleans.

Learn More

### Thursday: The Art of Bourbon

Embark on a sensory exploration of expertly selected bourbons as you uncover the craftsmanship behind each pour. From grain composition to barrel finishing, discover the elements that shape the distinctive flavors and character of America's most iconic whiskey.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott New Orleans have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott New Orleans offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton bikes available, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott New Orleans have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott New Orleans has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott New Orleans?

Audubon Aquarium & Insectarium, Audubon Zoo, Blaine Kern's Mardi Gras World, Cabildo, Cafe du Monde and more are nearby JW Marriott New Orleans.
