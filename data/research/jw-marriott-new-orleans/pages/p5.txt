# Surround Yourself with Modern Luxury

### Stay at our hotel near Frenchmen Street, New Orleans

Modern luxury is at your fingertips in the Big Easy when you stay at JW Marriott New Orleans. Our hotel on Frenchmen Street, New Orleans, is also close to historic Canal Street on the cusp of the French Quarter and within walking distance to must-see attractions, high-end shopping and award-winning restaurants. From reimagined guest rooms and spacious suites that deliver a sleek style to our exclusive Executive Lounge and first-class service, you'll feel right at home the minute you arrive. When you're ready to venture out and explore the city, upgrade your vacation to the next level with the help of our excellent concierge team. We will help you plan a custom escape in the heart of downtown that fits your discerning taste.

## Shop 'til You Drop

Retail therapy never felt so good. High end national and local retailers are within your grasp when you stay at JW Marriott New Orleans. Experience a wide variety of luxury brands and retailers at the Shops at Canal Place located three blocks from the hotel near Frenchmen Street in New Orleans. Stroll down Royal Street and Chartres Street to find one-of-a-kind relics at the antique shops and the latest trends at local boutiques.

## Classic Creole Cuisine in the Heart of the French Quarter

ARNAUD'S

Founded in 1918, Arnaud's is one of New Orleans' oldest restaurants offering classic Creole cuisine and first-class service in beautifully restored turn of the century dining rooms. Sip on cocktails at French 75, the award-winning bar and indulge in true fine dining for the quintessential New Orleans culinary experience.

## A New Orleans Dining Tradition Since 1905

GALATOIRE'S

Upon its opening in 1905, Galatoire's offered a menu and familial dining style inspired by the small village of Pardies, France, the birthplace of its founder, Jean Galatoire. More than 100 years later, this internationally-renowned restaurant continues to honor its culinary customs and reservation statues with little change.

## A Brazilian Churrasco Experience

FOGO DE CHÃO

Satisfy your taste buds and your appetite with Brazilian-inspired small plates, award-winning wines and cocktails along with a selection of premium, Churrasco-cooked meats at Fogo de Chão. The restaurant offers a glimpse into traditional Southern Brazilian culture in the heart of the Big Easy.

## Contemporary, Seasonal French-Southern Cuisine

HERBSAINT

Herbsaint, the flagship, award-winning restaurant of the Link Restaurant Group, offers a blend of French-Southern cuisine with elements of rustic Italian cooking. This restaurant, which opened on St. Charles Ave in 2000, earned Chef Donald Link a James Beard Award in 2007 for Best Chef South and is consistently named one of the best restaurants in the city year after year.

## Lift Your Spirits with Sazerac House

Take an interactive cocktail tour, enjoy exclusive spirit tastings and experience timeless traditions of the Sazerac, the official cocktail of New Orleans. The Sazerac Company's roots in New Orleans date back over 200 years. Explore spirits that all have a special connection to the city of New Orleans. Raise your spirits and your glass at the Sazerac House.
