### Executive Lounge Overview

Executive Level rooms and suites receive exclusive access to our newly renovated and reimagined two-story Executive Lounge. Modern conveniences and personalized services are the hallmark of the JW Marriott brand, and our Executive Lounge is where you can experience these aspects to the utmost.

### Executive Lounge Diversions

From morning to evening, you have access to an executive staff and extraordinary perks dedicated to ensuring that your time spent here is effortlessly enjoyable including a library, pool table, flat screen TVs and a family room.

### Executive Lounge Provisions

Our deluxe continental breakfast buffet is offered seven days a week. Coffee, tea, snacks and non-alcoholic drinks are available throughout the day.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott New Orleans have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott New Orleans have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott New Orleans?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott New Orleans is the 2 Bedroom Bi-level Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott New Orleans?

Some rooms at JW Marriott New Orleans permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott New Orleans have fridges?

No, rooms at JW Marriott New Orleans do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott New Orleans have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott New Orleans have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott New Orleans have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott New Orleans have hair dryers.
