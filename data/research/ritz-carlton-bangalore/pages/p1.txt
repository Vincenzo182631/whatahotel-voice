Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore
Luxury awaits at The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore
The distractions of a fast-paced city recede as guests arrive at The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore, a five-star luxury hotel in the high-tech epicenter of India. The hotel is centrally located on Residency Road and offers 277 rooms and suites, the largest in the city, a curated collection of over 1,200 artworks and a traditional jaali design woven throughout its architecture creating a distinct sense of place. It features 9 fine dining restaurants and bars, Tequiro the new high energy cocktail bar, The Cloak Society, a theatre inspired speakeasy cocktail bar, Zarqash by Michelin-starred Chef Rohit Ghai serving North Western Frontier cuisine, Yàn Yǎn serving Chinese, Izu serving Japanese Nikkei fusion cuisine, The Market a global dining destination featuting lavish buffet lunches and Sunday brunches and BANG the event-led rooftop bar. The hotel also features a wellness floor with a luxurious spa, gym and an outdoor swimming pool making it stand out as an ideal venue for leisure, business and weddings.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore is Kempegowda International Airport, Bengaluru (BLR). BLR is located approximately 42.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore have an airport shuttle to Kempegowda International Airport, Bengaluru (BLR)?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore has an airport shuttle to Kempegowda International Airport, Bengaluru (BLR).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
