Fine Dining Driven by Experiences
Dining
Our fine dining restaurants in Bengaluru​ city centre offer beautifully designed spaces and global fare. The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore, presents a collection of award-winning restaurants and bars: Zarqash by Michelin-starred Chef Rohit Ghai celebrates progressive Indian cuisine, Izu, a contemporary Japanese and Ceviche bar; Yàn Yǎn, a modern Chinese restaurant and dim sum house; and The Market, offering a global culinary journey. Experience exquisite cocktails and panoramic views of the city at BANG, our iconic rooftop bar and much more.
Exploring the Art of Couverture
Philosophy unfolds as a curated half-day journey into the art, science, and soul of fine chocolate. From cocoa farm to atelier, the experience culminates in an intimate tasting, where each expression reveals chocolate as a celebration of craft, provenance and refined indulgence.
The Ritz-Carlton Art High Tea
The legendary Ritz-Carlton Afternoon Tea is reimagined as an immersive experience inspired by the hotel’s celebrated art. Conceptualized by renowned experience designer Eeshaan Kashyap, it blends visual storytelling with refined gastronomy. Guests savour curated delicacies and champagne at The Lobby Lounge, where each element reflects creativity, craft, and elevated indulgence.
Ayurveda: A Journey of Balance Through Food
Rooted in Ayurveda and refined through culinary artistry, this personalized wellness dining experience begins with a one-on-one consultation. A bespoke menu is then crafted to align with each guest’s dosha, lifestyle, and goals transforming dining into a restorative ritual that nurtures balance, vitality and well-being.
