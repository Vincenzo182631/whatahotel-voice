Experience 5-Star Luxury at Our Hotel Suites in Bangalore
Suites & Rooms
Hotel room and suite accommodation at The Ritz-Carlton, Bangalore blend modern silhouettes with traditional Indian design elements to create inviting retreats. Features include spacious layouts, Dean & DeLuca-curated minibars, and views of Bengaluru. Nearly all our 5-star accommodations in Bengaluru​ city centre include access to luxury services in the Club Lounge.
The Club Level
Located on the top floor, The Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge is an exclusive space for guests to work or relax while enjoying scintillating views of the Bangalore skyline. The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience commences on arrival and offers guests extraordinary service and sophistication.

Club Privileges
Guests of Club Rooms and Club Executive Suites enjoy exclusive access to The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge, along with a collection of thoughtfully curated privileges.
Upgrade to a Club Deluxe Room, subject to availability.
Complimentary high-speed wireless internet throughout the hotel.
Complimentary coffee and tea available throughout the day in the Club Lounge.
Two complimentary pieces of laundry or pressing per room, per day (cumulative during the stay).
Up to two hours of Board Room access, subject to availability.
Business Centre services.
Personalized check-in and check-out at The Club Lounge.
The Club Lounge is open daily from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.
Guests below 21 years of age are welcome between **8:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.** We kindly request families to help preserve the tranquil ambience of the Club Lounge for the comfort and enjoyment of all guests.
Signature Experiences
Sketch the Skyline
Discover a quiet moment in The Library, where easels and sketching materials invite you to capture Bengaluru's skyline and create a lasting keepsake.
Open Kitchen Experience
Watch our chefs at work and enjoy freshly prepared breakfast specialties from the live cooking stations.
The Board Room
An intimate setting for meetings of up to eight guests, thoughtfully appointed with modern business amenities. Available upon request; additional charges apply.
Bartend Your Way
During Hors d'oeuvres each evening, discover the Cocktail of the Day and enjoy the opportunity to craft your own signature creation alongside our Club Lounge Ladies and Gentlemen.
