Book a Relaxing Treatment at Our Luxury Spa in Bangalore
Spa
The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Bangalore is more than a day spa; it is a wellness destination. This 17,000 sq. ft. retreat offers luxury healing experiences drawing from Indian and Western traditions. The 10 experience rooms at our luxury spa in Bangalore include a VIP Suite for a special celebration. The spa is perfect for an individual indulgence after a busy day, a romantic honeymoon or an anniversary treat.
Resonance Within: Chakra Sound Therapy
Resonance Within is a one-hour immersive sound healing experience that gently aligns the body’s seven chakras through the power of vibration. Using singing bowls, gongs, chimes and voice, the session guides guests into deep relaxation and inner clarity. Each frequency restores balance, inviting a renewed sense of harmony and connection.

The Serenity Sleep Journey
Your journey begins with a rejuvenating full-body massage that eases tension and promotes deep relaxation, followed by an aura-cleansing ritual to restore energetic balance. The experience concludes with a calming Yoga Nidra session, lulling the mind into profound rest. Afterwards, unwind in your room, embraced by soft blue light and soothing sleep music for complete serenity.
Soul of the South
Rooted in the lush landscapes of Karnataka, this restorative ritual celebrates the region's natural treasures. Choose the warmth of sandalwood, the energising touch of coffee, or the soothing essence of coconut, each thoughtfully sourced and sustainably grown. Your journey begins with a gentle scrub, followed by a nourishing wrap and a calming massage that bring harmony to body and mind.
Shirodhara
An ancient Indian therapy that nourishes the deepest level of ones being. Post the completion of a gentle body massage, lukewarm herbal oil is poured in an even stream onto the forehead to pacify and revitalize the mind.
Travel Reviver
This wellness journey is tailor made to the time of the day it is performed. In the morning Energizing being the focus of the experience. Begins with an exfoliating body scrub followed by a stimulating massage targeting tired muscles to ensure rejuvenation. In the evening Blissful Calm being the focus of the experience.
Hot Stone Experience
A therapeutic massage using warm volcanic stones with moderate to deep pressure to intensely soothe tired, aching muscles and aid in relaxation.
Blissful Indulgence
Embark on a serene journey of togetherness, where every moment is crafted for connection and calm. Begin with an indulgent therapy that restores balance and harmony, choose a 60-minute full-body massage, or elevate your ritual with a 90-minute experience featuring either an extended massage, a mini facial, a body scrub, or a nourishing wrap.

The Ritz-Carlton Spa Suite Experience
This wellness journey at our spa in Bangalore features our Spa Suite, which offers an exclusive sanctuary for the ultimate spa experience. This opulent suite features two individual treatment rooms, a changing area, a private steam room, a shower and a hydrotherapy bath. Ideal for indulging in relaxation with a friend, loved one, or simply in solitude, the suite serves as your tranquil retreat for a minimum of three hours. During your time, savor Hi-Tea, soft beverages and two glasses of Champagne, enhancing your serene escape.
