## Soar Above the Serengeti at Sunrise

Experience the magic of the Serengeti from above with the Balloon Safari. This once-in-a-lifetime adventure takes guests on a serene flight over the plains at dawn, offering unparalleled views of the wildlife and landscape below. As you float through the morning air, enjoy the quiet majesty of the Serengeti from a truly unique perspective.

The balloon safari begins before dawn, with an early morning pick-up from your lodge. This timing ensures guests can witness the Serengeti in its most magical light, just as the sun begins to rise and the landscape awakens. The quiet beauty of the sunrise, combined with the sweeping aerial views of the plains and wildlife, offer an unforgettable experience. After an exhilarating flight, guests are treated to a bush breakfast, set amongst the wild surroundings. Following the meal, guests are comfortably transferred back to the lodge, leaving them with unforgettable moments from an extraordinary morning in one of the world’s most iconic landscapes.

- Duration: 6 hours
- Number of people: Up to 16 people
- Please note: Bookings are required 48 hours in advance
- What does it include? Pick-up at the lodge/night safari, balloon flight, bush breakfast, drop-off to visitor centre in Seronera and concession fees.

The balloon safari offers a peaceful yet breathtaking way to experience the Serengeti from above, blending adventure with serenity in a truly unforgettable manner. As the soft glow of dawn paints the horizon, the journey begins with a gentle ascent, offering sweeping views of the vast plains and the wildlife stirring below. From majestic elephants and herds of antelope to the occasional predator on the prowl, the sights are nothing short of spectacular. Floating silently through the cool morning air, guests can fully immerse themselves in the tranquillity of this iconic landscape, soaking in the beauty of the Serengeti from a point few are privileged to experience.

Adding to the experience is the post-flight celebration—a bush breakfast right in the heart of the wilderness. Freshly prepared and enjoyed in the untamed surroundings, the breakfast is the perfect way to soak in the magic and wonder of the safari. From the sweeping views of the golden savannahs to the tranquil journey back to the lodge, the Balloon Safari is thoughtfully designed to leave a lasting impression, creating memories that stay with guests long after the adventure ends.
