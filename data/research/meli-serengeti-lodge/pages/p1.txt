## Where the thrill of African nature awaits you

This hotel blends into the exceptional surroundings of the Serengeti National Park, allowing you to experience it intensely without sacrificing the comforts of the most exclusive accommodation. Elegant rooms inspired by Maasai traditions with spectacular views of the hotel gardens or the Serengeti plains. A dining experience that reinterprets local cuisine using ingredients inspired by international cooking. And magnificent sustainable facilities that include an incredible infinity pool, gym, and spa.

The magic of Africa permeates every corner of the Meliá Serengeti Lodge Meliá Collection. Savor the experience on the fabulous terrace of your room and marvel at the vastness of the Serengeti National Park. If your dream was to go on a safari, you are in the right place. Visit with the family. Children will also enjoy their own little African adventure. Discover cuisine as surprising as it is delicious, including African coffees, teas, and spirits. The stunning infinity pool with views of the Serengeti, a relaxing spa, and a fully equipped gym complete this unforgettable adventure.
