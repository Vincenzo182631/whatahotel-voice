## Serengeti’s wild beauty on foot

Embark on a one-and-a-half-hour walking safari through the heart of the Serengeti, an experience that allows guests to explore wildlife up close and at their own pace. Accompanied by a knowledgeable guide, local Maasai, and a ranger, this walk offers an intimate encounter with the Serengeti’s natural beauty.

A Serengeti walking safari offers a truly unique way to experience the wilderness—one that feels both personal and immersive. Unlike the pace of a traditional game drive, walking allows guests to slow down and take in the landscape in a more intimate and thoughtful way. Each step opens the door to quiet moments of observation and discovery, revealing details that might otherwise go unnoticed.

As guests wander across the Serengeti’s vast plains, they’ll uncover the subtle signs of life that tell the story of this remarkable ecosystem.

- Duration: 3 hours
- Number of people: Up to 8 people (18 and over)
- Please note: Bookings are required 48 hours in advance
- What does it include? Guided walk through the Serengeti, professional guide, local Maasai, and ranger. Close-up wildlife encounters and a deep connection with the Serengeti’s landscapes and biodiversity.

With expert guides leading the way, guests have the opportunity to uncover hidden trails, interpret the behaviours of elusive predators, and observe the detailed connections between the Serengeti’s flora and fauna. Every step reveals new details that are easy to miss from the confines of a vehicle, offering a more personal and grounded perspective of this extraordinary wilderness. The Serengeti’s plant life is as captivating as its wildlife, with iconic silhouettes of towering acacias and resilient grasses carpeting the plains. Each tells a fascinating story of survival and adaptation over centuries. These often-overlooked details reveal the vital role plants play in sustaining the ecosystem, offering a deeper understanding of how life thrives in perfect balance.

A walking safari is much more than simply exploring the wilderness—it’s an opportunity to form a profound connection with the landscape. This immersive experience encourages guests to engage with the Serengeti on a deeper level, creating lasting memories of its untamed magic and instilling a renewed appreciation for the delicate harmony of the natural world.
