## Discover Serengeti’s Wildlife in a Personal Safari

Embark on an extraordinary game drive through the heart of the Serengeti. This immersive safari offers guests the chance to explore the Serengeti's vast plains and diverse ecosystems, witnessing its remarkable wildlife. From Africa’s "Big Five" – lions, elephants, hippos, black rhinos, and buffalos – to elusive predators like leopards and cheetahs, framed by the stunning backdrop of this natural wonder.

Guests will embark on a truly personalised safari adventure, immersing themselves in the awe-inspiring landscapes of the Serengeti. Guided by an expert safari guide, each journey is designed to provide profound insights into the region's remarkable wildlife, unique ecology, and captivating natural beauty. Whether opting for a full or half-day game drive, participants will cross the Serengeti’s iconic plains, renowned for their rich biodiversity and vast, untouched views.

The experience is an intimate exploration of nature, where every moment unfolds into unforgettable encounters.

Duration: 4 h (half day) 6 h (full day) approx.

Number of people: Up to 6 people

Please note: Bookings are required 48 hours in advance

What does it include? Personalised safari experience, expert guide, opportunities for intimate wildlife sightings in their natural habitat.

Guests will have the opportunity to observe majestic lions, graceful zebras, swift gazelles, cunning hyenas, playful monkeys, and an incredible array of bird species, all in their undisturbed natural habitats. The thrill of witnessing the wildlife up close is matched only by the stunning scenery, which stretches endlessly under the African sky.

With the added flexibility of a private vehicle, each game drive is tailored to the guests’ interests and pace, creating an experience that feels uniquely their own. This personalised approach ensures that no two safaris are the same, offering the freedom to linger at sights that captivate and move on when ready.

A game drive through the Serengeti offers an unforgettable journey into the heart of Tanzania’s remarkable wilderness. With its sweeping plains and iconic landscapes, this world-famous destination provides the perfect setting to experience nature at its most raw and unspoiled. Guests will be surrounded by the untouched beauty of the Serengeti, experiencing moments that leave a lasting impression.
