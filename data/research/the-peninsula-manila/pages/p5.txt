The Peninsula Manila The Swimming Pool item 2

Escape to a luxury hotel swimming pool in the heart of Makati, surrounded by swaying palm trees, lush tropical gardens, and tranquil resort-style scenery. Take a refreshing swim in the hotel's stunning 22-meter outdoor swimming pool, perfect for lap swimming, relaxation, and leisurely afternoons under the sun. After your swim, enjoy handcrafted beverages and light bites from The Pool Snack Bar, featuring an internationally inspired menu.

### Location

Ground Floor, Makati Tower

### Hours

6:00 am - 10:00 pm
