# Old Manila

A Manila institution since 1976, Old Manila presents a contemporary expression of French cuisine, recognised by the MICHELIN Guide. Set within an Art Deco dining room, Chef de Cuisine Matthieu Fournier reinterprets French classics with precision and restraint. A considered wine programme and sommelier-led pairings complete the experience, with the Magsaysay Room offering a private setting for intimate gatherings, executive dinners and revelrous celebrations.

Old Manila presents a contemporary art exhibition featuring five free-form sculptures by El Taganas Collection. The exhibition invites guests to experience a thoughtful dialogue between art, memory, and the enduring spirit of hospitality.

## Meet the Chef

Old Manila has long been a benchmark for French fine dining in Manila, and I am excited to contribute to its next chapter. Together with the team, I look forward to creating memorable culinary experiences with our guests that will celebrate both tradition and innovation.

#### Matthieu Fournier

French-born Chef Matthieu Fournier is the Chef de Cuisine of Old Manila, blending classical French techniques with a modern sensibility. Under his leadership, the restaurant continues to uphold its longstanding reputation for excellence while offering a fresh take on French cuisine.

## Highlights

#### Privacy Please

The Magsaysay Room, Old Manila’s private dining room, is named after the Philippines’ most beloved President, Ramon Magsaysay, whose oil portrait commands the room. The original on which this painting is based hangs in the reception hall of Malacañang Palace, the official residence of the President of the Philippines.
