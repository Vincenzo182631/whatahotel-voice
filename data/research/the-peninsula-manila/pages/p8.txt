## Home of Christmas

For 50 years, The Peninsula Manila has been where families and friends gather to celebrate Christmas and create lasting memories.

As we mark our golden anniversary, the festive season shines even brighter with cherished traditions, wondrous music, and joyful holiday experiences.

This Christmas, come home to The Peninsula Manila.

## Marvelous Holiday Dining

From festive feasts and elegant afternoon tea to cherished holiday traditions shared around the table, each dining experience is thoughtfully crafted to capture the magic of Christmas.
