## Share the history of an icon of sophistication in the Filipino capital

For five decades, The Peninsula Manila has defined luxury in the heart of Makati. Called the "Jewel in the Capital’s Crown," it offers timeless sophistication, warm service, and outstanding cuisine. In 2026, it celebrates its 50th anniversary with Condé Nast Traveler Gold List recognition and MICHELIN-selected awards for Old Manila and Spices.

### Halo-halo

Halo-halo is a sinfully sweet dessert of sweetened fruits and beans with shaved ice and milk, and The Peninsula’s sumptuous variation of this Filipino favorite has been hailed the best in town. Served in a giant glass, The Peninsula Halo-halo Harana is a combination of egg-yolk custard, chickpeas, sweetened kidney beans, colored gelatin, nata de coco, purple yam preserve, sago, jackfruit, makapuno – a rare variety of creamy coconut, ube ice cream and evaporated milk. It’s no surprise that Time Out Manila named it the “Best Legal High” in the city.

### Echoing Culture

The Jeepney is the most popular means of public transportation in the Philippines, originally constructed from WWII US military jeeps. Known for their crowded seating, the word “jeepney” is a combination of the words “jeep” and “knee” due to knee-to-knee seating. The Peninsula’s bespoke jeepney puts a lavish twist on this cultural icon, which like the rest of the hotel’s fleet, is painted in Peninsula Brewster Green and fitted with the luxuries and comforts that our guests know and love.

Read More

**Art Gallery in the Sky**

The Gallery Club Lounge is an intimate 90-seat lounge overlooking The Lobby and the “Sunburst” sculpture and surrounded by works by Philippine masters. The exclusive access offers breakfast, afternoon tea, and evening cocktails with a curated selection of spirits. Additional privileges include express early check-in and late check-out, complimentary garment pressing, two hours’ use of the boardroom, and 24-hour business and concierge services.
