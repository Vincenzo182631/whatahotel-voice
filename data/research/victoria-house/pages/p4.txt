# Our Villas

## The Largest and Most Luxurious of Our Belize Resort Options

Looking for luxurious and spacious accommodations for your family or group? Ranging from two to five bedrooms – and up to 5000 square feet – our standalone Villas are sure to impress.

All units feature locally hand crafted wicker and mahogany furniture and private verandahs offering stunning views of the Caribbean Sea. Casa Playa Blanca and Casa Azul both have their own private pools.

## CASA PLAYA BLANCA   (BEACHFRONT)

This lovely two-bedroom villa offers vaulted hardwood ceiling located directly on our white sand, palm shaded beach. Both bedrooms and living room opens onto a spacious deck and private infinity pool.

#### Sleeps up to 4 Guests — From $1,430 Per Night

LEARN MORE

## CASA DEL SOL   (BEACHFRONT)

Next door to Playa Blanca is Casa Del Sol, a spacious three-bedroom house with a custom made king bed and large ensuite bathroom. A shared balcony overlooks the beach and barrier reef.

#### Sleeps up to 6 Guests — From $1,585 Per Night

LEARN MORE

## CASA AZUL (BEACHFRONT)

A magnificent five-bedroom beachfront villa with a living area over 3,500 sq. feet. This house offers a private pool set in lush landscaping overlooking the barrier reef.

#### Sleeps up to 10 Guests — From $2,395 Per Night

## LUXURY VILLAS

The Victoria House offers three exquisite two-bedroom luxury villas. Each villa offers traditional thatch roofs, mahogany poster king size canopied beds, luxurious travertine bathrooms and more.
