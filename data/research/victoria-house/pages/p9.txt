# PRIVATE DINNERS

## Romantic, Secluded Dinners on the Beach or on Our Pier Palapa

For that very special occasion, what could be more romantic than a private, candlelit dinner on the beach overlooking the sea, under the stars?

Let our staff help you create a magical night where you dine in the seclusion of either our pier palapa or at your private beach table, lit with tiki torches under the tropical sky.

Complimenting this exotic setting, the specialized and highly trained chefs at Palmilla Restaurant will prepare your choice of culinary delights from the dinner menu, making for a memorable evening. Alternatively, you may choose to enjoy the fresh catch from your fishing excursion earlier in the day, expertly prepared by our chefs. Either way, our chefs will produce a culinary experience you won’t soon forget!

### PRIVATE DINNER RATES & RESERVATIONS

Reservations are required. Please call us at +501-226-2067, email us at dining@victoria-house.com, or visit the front desk to make your booking.

- Cost for beach or palapa location: $150 for up to 4 guests.
- Add $35 per person for parties larger than 4 (to a maximum of 8 in the palapa).
- All rates are in USD and do not include the 12.5% tax.
- Please note that we require reservations to be made a minimum of 24 hours in advance.
