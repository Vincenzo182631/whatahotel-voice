# Meal Plans

## The Ultimate in Convenience

With our convenient meal plans your options are as diverse as they are tasty. Order your choice of breakfast, lunch, or dinner from our à la carte menu\*.

**Free Breakfast**

A complimentary continental breakfast buffet is included in the room rate. The continental breakfast buffet includes: fresh baked pastries, seasonal fruit, yogurt with granola crumbles, juice of the day, coffee or tea. Guests have the option of ordering additional items from our a la carte menu at an additional charge.

**Breakfast Only**

Daily Rate: $20 per person. Breakfast plan includes any meal from our a la carte menu, as well as juice of the day, coffee or tea.

**MAP (Breakfast and Dinner)**

Daily Rate: $90 per person. Children 10 and under (ordering from the children’s menu) $30 per child per day.

**FAP (Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner)**

Daily Rate: $110.00 per person. Children 10 and under (ordering from the children’s menu) $39 per child per day.

_\\* Please note that a 23.75% tax and service fee is charged on meals and meal plans_

_\\* Meal surcharge applies for peak travel period, special events and holidays: Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve and Day, and New Year’s Eve_
