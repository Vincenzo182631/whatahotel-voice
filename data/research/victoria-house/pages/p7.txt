# DINING AT VICTORIA HOUSE

## Palmilla Restaurant, Admiral Nelson’s Beach Bar, Poolside Patio and More

From private candlelit dinners on the pier to a freshly prepared ceviche and a cold beverage at Admiral Nelson’s Bar, dining at Victoria House Resort and Spa can be as casual or luxurious as you like.

Victoria House offers three conveniently located, on-site options for all your dining needs:

**Palmilla Restaurant**

**Poolside Patio**

**Admiral Nelson’s  Beach Bar**

Palmilla Restaurant is an elegant, full-service restaurant located on the stunning waterfront that has been creating gourmet cuisine for over 20 years. Our highly trained team of chefs use only the finest local ingredients, with fresh, local seafood being a particular specialty. Start your day with a delicious homemade omelette or traditional Caribbean breakfast, or stop by for a lunch of lobster tacos or snapper salsa verde. In the evening, dine on a decadent surf and turf and finish with our famous molten chocolate dessert. We are also happy to tend to your personal dietary needs.

We also offer a Poolside Patio, extending from Palmilla Restaurant onto the pool deck. The patio is perfect for al fresco dining, serving breakfast, lunch and dinner.

At lunchtime, or late into the evening, you might prefer our Admiral Nelson’s Beach Bar. A casual, Belizean-style palapa bar, Admiral Nelson’s is located right on the beach offering beautiful views of the Caribbean Sea. Quench your thirst with a delicious Caribbean-inspired cocktail!

Planning to celebrate a special occasion with a romantic evening? Let our staff help you create a magical night where you’ll dine in the seclusion of our pier palapa, or at your private, candlelit table on the beach, overlooking the Caribbean Sea and the stars above. Our specialized chefs at Palmilla Restaurant will be happy to create a personalized menu for your experience, making it a night to remember.

For more information about dining at Victoria House, contact dining@victoria-house.com.
