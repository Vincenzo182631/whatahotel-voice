## The Quintessential Belize Casita

Lie in your hammock with your partner, sip an icy tropical drink, and watch a stunning sunrise from the private veranda of your authentic thatch-roofed Belize Casitas at Victoria House Resort and Spa.

At 375 square feet, our tropical Casitas are self-contained and feature a private veranda and hammock for pure relaxation. All seven Casitas face the Caribbean Sea, visible over a wide expanse of verdant lawn, stately palms and a white sandy beach.

Most suitable for two guests, each unit has a private entrance, and includes a queen bed draped in decorative netting, as well as handcrafted mahogany furniture and a bathroom with shower. All units also feature air conditioning and ceiling fans to help you stay cool, as well as a mini bar for nights when you feel like staying in and a safe so your valuables stay protected. Booking one of our Casitas also allows you to access our complimentary bicycles and kayaks for exploring Ambergris Caye.

Prices start at $385 USD/night.

For a true Caribbean experience, look no further than our Belize Casitas.
