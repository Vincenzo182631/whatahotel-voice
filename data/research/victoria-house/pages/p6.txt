# Our Suites

## Spacious Suites on Ambergris Caye, Belize

## CORAL SUITE

Our Ocean View Coral Suite overlooks Victoria House’s picturesque pool and expansive beach. This suite features a king bed, a shared verandah, and air conditioning to ensure your comfort.

## HIBISCUS SUITE

Step inside our Garden View Hibiscus Suite, which overlooks the lush tropical grounds of Victoria House. This suite features a king bed, a shared verandah, and air conditioning to ensure your comfort.

#### Sleeps up to 2 Guests — From $435 Per Night

## RAINFOREST SUITE

This beachfront villa is our Honeymoon Suite and features a king bed, large tiled bathroom, a wraparound veranda with private hammock and stunning views of the Caribbean Sea.

#### Sleeps up to 2 Guests — From $795 Per Night

## TOWER SUITE

This spacious, beach-level suite is part of Casa Azul. Featuring a private entrance, king bed, and a large veranda with views of the Caribbean Sea and barrier reef.

## INFINITY SUITES (1 BEDROOM)

These one-bedroom suites are the newest addition to Victoria House. Featuring a gourmet kitchen and three private verandas, including a secluded upper terrace with incredible ocean views.

#### Sleeps up to 2 Guests — From $785 Per Night

## INFINITY SUITES (2 BEDROOM)

These two-bedroom suites are our newest accommodations. Furnished with a king bed in one bedroom and a queen bed in the other, these suites also offer a gourmet kitchen and three private verandas with ocean views.

#### Sleeps up to 4 Guests — From $875 Per Night

LEARN MORE

Perhaps what sets Victoria House apart from any other resort in Belize is our diverse range of Ambergris Caye suites, each uniquely configured and furnished.

Our Rainforest, Tower Suites and Coral & Hibiscus Suites are all spacious and luxurious, offering verandas, four-poster bamboo or mahogany king beds, full bathrooms, comfortable chaise lounges and more.

If a little more space is required, or you love to cook, then our one and two bedroom Infinity Suites, complete with gourmet kitchens, may be the perfect option for you.

In preparing for your visit to, we invite you to learn more about our Ambergris Caye suites below or contact us to explore the best option to meet your needs.
