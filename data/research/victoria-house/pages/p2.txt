## Luxury Accommodations in Belize

Victoria House Resort and Spa is a boutique resort, offering exclusive Belize accommodations for those wondering where to stay in Belize and looking for seclusion and intimacy. We’re small enough to allow for that privacy you want, but without sacrificing **luxury accommodations**, world-class amenities, renowned service. From arrival to departure, total relaxation awaits at this **ideal Ambergris Caye accommodation**. Victoria House is unsurpassed in the art of pampering guests with an abundance of services and conveniences. We offer 42 different Belize accommodation options, from cozy Casitas and Palmetto Rooms to luxurious Suites and Private Villas, all with stunning views of our white-sand beach or the colorful gardens found throughout the property. Along with our comfortable accommodations, we offer a range of amenities well-suited to your vacation needs. Relax in our infinity-edge pool, our full-service spa, or dine in our 5-star Palmilla Restaurant. Given our comprehensive range of accommodations, whether you are looking for a romantic Belize getaway, planning a dream wedding, or arranging an unforgettable family vacation, you cannot go wrong at Victoria House.
