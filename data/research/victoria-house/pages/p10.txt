# PALMILLA RESTAURANT

## Culinary Excellence on Ambergris Caye

Certificate of Excellence 2019

Read Reviews

We invite you to discover the Palmilla Restaurant, Victoria House’s onsite, full-service restaurant. Serving breakfast, lunch and dinner daily, guests can relax and enjoy world-class dining while taking in beautiful ocean views.

Enjoy the comfort of an air-conditioned indoor setting or dine under the stars on our veranda. Either way, you’re guaranteed a casually elegant experience.

Our chefs delight in creating exquisite dishes with a local twist. Every evening we feature a different Amuse Bouche to pique your appetite. We prepare each and every meal to order, make our own breads, and all of this to ensure the peak of freshness, quality and flavor.

Start with a fresh conch ceviche, and for a main course, how about cashew crusted grouper, beef tenderloin, or grilled lobster? Or maybe you’d like us to prepare the fresh snapper you caught earlier in the day?

Our vegetarian, vegan and gluten free options are made with care and are a refreshing find. We just need a little advance notice.

Be sure to ask about the “Chef’s Special of The Day”, as it’s always a sumptuous experience utilizing the best of what is fresh and available locally.

**Reservations are encouraged, please call us at 011-501-226-2067 to book your table.**

**Restaurant Hours: 6:30 AM – 9:00 PM _(last order by 9:00 PM)_**

The best restaurant in Amberfris Caye, hands down My wife and I had lunch at Palmilla (restaurant in the Victoria House) prior to spa treatments and were so impressed we had dinner there the next night. We were in Ambergris Caye for a week and tried almost every higher end (by reviews) restaurants on the island and Palmilla stood out as the best, without question. It is upper end, white table cloth dining for dinner (lunch was much more casual) and was absolutely fantastic. The service was great, ambience on the elegant side and the food amazing. I had one of the best filets I have ever had with an amazing red wine reduction sauce. My wife had the beef tenderloin and it was amazing as well. Sticky Toffee pudding for dessert was likewise over the top good. Not surprising, it is a bit on the expensive side, similar to a US Steakhouse but well worth it. If you are interested, our close 2 and 3 restaurants were Frenchies and Black Orchid.
