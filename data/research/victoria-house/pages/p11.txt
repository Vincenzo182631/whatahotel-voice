# ADMIRAL NELSON’S BAR

## Our palapa style beach bar

Admiral Nelson’s is our palapa style beach bar overlooking the Belize Barrier Reef and the Caribbean Sea.

It is a gathering place, offering a fun and laid back atmosphere, where many of our guests meet to share their exciting stories from the day’s adventures in Belize. We also have a wide selection of traditional board games available for your enjoyment.

Admiral Nelson’s is a place to kick back and enjoy some tasty appetizers. Perhaps our delicious fish and chips, some coconut shrimp or a fresh fish tacos and an ice cold beer.

Come dine, or simply try one of our refreshing signature cocktails like our Ginger Lime Aide with Tito’s Handmade Vodka.

Bar hours are **7:00 AM to 11:00 PM**, but it has been rumored that this hub of fun does not close until the last guest leaves. So pull up a chair and stay a while!
