# Getting Here

- From Dubai Airport

## From Dubai Airport

## FROM DUBAI AIRPORT

_Private Car:_

A private car to/from Dubai Airport will cost from approximately AED 150 to AED 200 each way depending on the type of car and can be arranged by the hotel concierge by calling +971 (0) 4 602 3333 prior to your arrival/departure.

_Taxi:_

Taxis are available at the airport on a first-come, first-served basis. This will cost you approximately AED 60 each way and will take approximately 15 minutes of travel time.

_Driving:_

This route will take approximately 40 minutes and includes tolls.

• Head southeast on Airport Rd/D89

• Take the exit toward DXB Airport Terminal 1

• Stay slight left

• Take the ramp onto Airport Rd/D89

• Merge onto Airport Rd/D89

• Take the D70 exit toward Al Garhoud/Cargo Village

• Keep right at the fork and merge onto Casablanca St/D70

• Keep right at the fork, follow signs for E11/Jebel Ali/Abu Dhabi and merge onto Sheikh Rashid Rd/E11

• Toll road

• Take exit 56B for E66 E toward Al Ain

• Continue onto Al Ain - Dubai Rd/Oud Metha Rd/D79/E66

• Keep left to continue on Al Ain - Dubai Rd/E66

• Take exit 3B for Al Khail St

• Merge onto Al Khail Rd/D68

• Keep right at the fork, follow signs for Financial Center St/D71/Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall and merge onto Financial Center Rd/D71

• Slight left toward Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Blvd

• Turn left at the 1st cross street onto Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Blvd

• Turn left onto Dubai Fountain St

• The Dubai EDITION will be on the right

_Parking:_

Valet parking is available for hotel guests.
