# Gym

**Gym**

Share

The 24-hour Gym at The Dubai EDITION is powered by Technogym, with state-of-the-art multifunction pieces that fulfill the being  of fitness and wellness. Access to the Gym is complimentary to in-house guests.

**The Spa**

Share

Located on the second floor of The Dubai EDITION, The SPA offers guests a wide range of services in a soothing, relaxing environment. Featuring three treatment rooms including a couple's suite, The Spa blends luxury and comfort with an extensive list of treatments including a signature EDITION treatment.
