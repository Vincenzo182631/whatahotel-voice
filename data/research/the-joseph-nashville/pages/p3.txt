## Dining

Fine Italian Cuisine

### Yolan - A Love Letter to Italy

Yolan is an authentic experience of Italian cuisine done right. In its menu, its wine selection, and its warmth, The Joseph's signature Italian restaurant in downtown Nashville, embodies the best of what Italian cuisine can be: refined, welcoming, carefully curated. A true love letter to Italy, Yolan pays homage to our founding family's heritage and Joseph's wife, Yolanda.

Breakfast, Lunch and Espresso Bar

## Daytime at Yolan

Rooted in The Joseph’s Italian heritage, Yolan welcomes guests each day to this Italian restaurant in downtown Nashville​ with house-made pastries, perfectly pulled espresso and signature morning fare served in a bright, inviting dining room. From classic breakfast to weekend brunch favorites like seasonal French toast and Eggs Benedict, mornings unfold with warmth and ease. Weekday lunch brings refined, regional Italian dishes crafted with the same care as dinner, while Afternoon Aperitivo offers a spirited pause with cocktails and the striking Stuzzi Tower—a savory tasting designed for sharing.

Hidden Cocktail Bar

## Four Walls

Tucked away within The Joseph Nashville and named one of Esquire’s Best Bars in America, Four Walls is an intimate cocktail bar in downtown Nashville. Holding the tension between elegance and edge, a visceral experience of unexpected textures awaits. Hide on the wall. Fringe in the air. Fizz. Marble. Leather. Experience a celebration of the classic cowboy culture of the American West through an Italian lens with an artfully crafted selection of signature cocktails, negronis and forgotten classics. Indulge and be sated... at last.

Rooftop Bar

## Denim

Denim is a poolside rooftop bar in Nashville, TN, for locals and travelers looking to unwind in comfortable luxury. Stylishly designed for gathering, Denim draws inspiration from the city’s vibrant maker culture, while delivering service epitomized by the best of Southern hospitality. Expertly crafted cocktails, elevated shareable plates, and an atmosphere of creative energy await at Nashville’s premier rooftop bar, 21 stories above the city.
