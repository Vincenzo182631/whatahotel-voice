## Rooftop Pool

Perched high above the bustling city streets, our Italian-made, saltwater pool centers a deck experience that feels private, secluded and intimate. The rooftop pool at our luxury hotel near Music City Center, Nashville​ creates an unpretentious and comfortable vibe with lounge seating and luxury cabanas.

## Fitness Studio

As with all components of the experience at The Joseph, the fitness offering has been well-considered and highly curated. Exclusively for guests at our hotel near Bridgestone Arena, Nashville​, the Fitness Studio affords guests one of the most unobstructed views of the city – landing it on Men’s Journal’s list of the “Most Beautiful Luxury Gyms in the World.” State-of-the-art cardio equipment, Peloton bikes and weight training equipment – from machines to free weights – complete the wellness offerings.

Does The Joseph, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nashville have a pool on-site?
