### Art Is in the Details

Marble tile floors in a custom geometric pattern and full marble tile walls in the en-suite baths, red oak doors and casings, and custom designed bath furnishings are all highlighted by Nashville-inspired leather, denim, and metal detailing throughout to create the feeling of a truly unique and carefully curated environment rich with small luxuries and artful touches.

### In-Room Italian Espresso

Inspired by our Italian roots, The Joseph features complimentary in-room Nespresso for the perfect morning pour within the comfort of your guest room or luxury hotel suite in Nashville, TN. Our refreshment centers also feature a variety of snacks and beverages... Sweet or spicy. Full-bodied or bubbly. Internationally distilled or locally brewed. Offerings curated to quench your thirst or late-night craving.

### Living With Art

Rooms and suites at The Joseph feature prints by Joseph Editions, a fine art publisher, as well as unique artworks sourced from the Pizzuti Collection. Each room has at least one print from the Joseph Editions' Tennessee Portfolio, featuring artists with a connection to the state. With hundreds of artworks and countless curatorial combinations, a new art experience awaits with each stay.

### Instrumental Design

Design elements create a chic, sophisticated homage to Nashville's musical roots. Within your Nashville accommodations in downtown, pillows feature the intricate chain stitch embroidery of the famed Nudie Suits worn by country music stars like Hank Williams and Dolly Parton. Burl wood furniture reflects the body of a guitar, while shell end tables are inspired by guitar picks and drawer pulls mimic tuning knobs.
