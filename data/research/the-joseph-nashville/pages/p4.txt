# Unwind at Our Spa in Downtown Nashville

## Unwind at our downtown Nashville spa hotel

In modern life, so many forces pull us away from ourselves, making the chance to sink into the present moment a luxury. Rose is a refuge; it offers us an opportunity to pause time in the middle of it all....See More Nestled 21 floors above the bustling city streets, Rose is a luxury spa in downtown Nashville. Here, the power of nature and ancient techniques come together to provide guests and visitors with an experience of connection, balance, and harmony. We believe in reconnecting with purity—in ingredients, in techniques—to gain more than just physical renewal, restoration, and rejuvenation. On the top floor at The Joseph, in quiet and stillness, guests retreat to re-establish the natural rhythm within.See Less

## The Rose Journey

After a warm welcome at the 21st-floor reception area with a handcrafted copper welcome desk adorned in roses, we will prepare you for your journey at our luxury spa in downtown Nashville. Rosewood lockers and a staging area complete with plush robes, small luxuries and amenities invite you into ultimate relaxation, personal discovery and a renewed sense of vitality. Welcome to Rose at The Joseph. Bleached oak and woven fabrics, high ceilings and soft neutral tones impart an impression of being at home, where one may fully relax. Your journey may begin or end in the deluxe steam rooms, Nashville’s only Experience Showers, or the co-ed Relaxation Lounge. Awaken the senses with a cup of hot tea paired with a savory or sweet treat. Indulge in the deep relaxation and restorative effects of your treatment. The world can wait – a little while longer.

### Day Spa Package

Spend the day at our downtown Nashville spa hotel in Rose. Each experience includes use of the steam room, ice fountain, Experience Shower and co-ed lounge, plus the 21st-floor Fitness Studio overlooking downtown Nashville. This escape invites you to restore balance through personalized care.

Reserve Package

### Rose Indulgence

This 100-minute experience harnesses the healing vibrations of rose quartz crystals and biodynamic rose oil to release tension, revitalize the skin, and soothe both body and spirit. Targeting skin and senses, an all-over scrub prepares the body for a massage while alleviating tension and restoring physical and mental health. Inner equilibrium is restored, finishing with an ultra-hydrating mini facial.

Book Experience

### Hydrafacials

From a 30-minute Express—perfect for a glow on the go—to the 80-minute Premium with lymphatic drainage, each Hydrafacial experience at Rose offers a tailored journey toward radiance. Harnessing vortex fusion technology and targeted boosters, these facials purify, hydrate, and replenish the skin. Through gentle exfoliation and nourishing serums, clarity is restored, texture refined, and luminosity unveiled.
