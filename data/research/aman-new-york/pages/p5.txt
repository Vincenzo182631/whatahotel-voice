# What's on at Aman New York

Make an enquiry

### Halloween at The Jazz Club

The Jazz Club presents a Día de los Muertos celebration, honouring the tradition of remembrance through an immersive evening of candelit rituals, abundant marigolds and standout live performances.

### Restorative Wellness

Whether training for the city's most iconic five-borough race or inspired to train alongside one of the world's most anticipated tennis championship tournaments, discover wellness itineraries tailored to your personal goals.

### Thanksgiving Celebrations

Create new traditions this Thanksgiving with seasonal pie provisions by Aman New York's exceptional pastry team, crafted for sharing with friends and family.

### Curated Shopping Experiences

On the Crown Building's first floor, just moments from the fashion houses on Madison Avenue, Aman New York places guests at the heart of the city's most anticipated shopping season. Curating private appointments at flagship maisons, or within the comfort of your suite, preview the season's latest collections.

### Capture Your Portrait

Ideal for capturing life's milestone moments, have your portrait taken by a professional Leica photographer. Whether seeking individual or family photographs, our dedicated sessions are ideal for creating holiday mementos to treasure.

### Kaiseki at Akari

Crafted with the day's finest ingredients, Kaiseki at Akari offers guests a revelatory, immersive dining experience that is never the same twice. Guided by the principles of washoku, each course builds upon on the last, expressing the character of the season through a thoughtful progression of flavour, presentation and expert technique.

**Alfresco Dining**

Encompassed within the cityscape, and heated and covered during winter, delight in the pleasure of al fresco dining. From Akari’s seasonal terrace, where renowned sake complements fresh, seasonal ingredients, to the year-round Garden Terrace serving light bites or Arva’s signature Italian dishes.

\\
**Afternoon Tea**

Reimagined by Aman New York, delight in an array of teas, perfectly paired with sweet and savoury delicacies that venture beyond the conventional. Where East meets West, our culinary offerings converge, showcasing creations infused with the bold, rich Italian tastes of Arva, alongside the traditional Japanese flavours of Akari.

\\
**Provisions**

Aman New York extends its culinary offering beyond the Crown Building through thoughtfully crafted Seasonal Provisions. Discover menus based on fresh ingredients and inspired by one of the world’s finest culinary destinations, whether at home or just passing through.

\\
**Weekend Brunch**

Savour seasonal dishes inspired by Arva’s namesake, ‘cultivated land,’ designed for sharing with those you cherish most.

**Omakase & Kaiseki at Akari**

At the intimate hinoki wood counter, Akari presents the Japanese traditions of Kaiseki, centred on a progression of intricately crafted dishes, and Omakase, a sushi-led tasting, with menus shaped by the finest seasonal ingredients sourced that day.

**Detoxification Programme by Novak Djokovic**

The tennis legend brings his unrivalled athleticism to Aman New York with a three-day Detoxification Programme. Spread across the three-storey Aman Spa New York, the programme unfolds as a guided reset for body, mind and spirit.

\\
**Strength & Recovery by Maria Sharapova**

Tennis icon and Aman Global Wellness Ambassador, Maria Sharapova, has collaborated with Aman New York to offer a three-hour champion’s start to your day. This athlete-inspired experience includes a nourishing breakfast at Arva, a personalised strength and conditioning session and a revitalising Banya or Hamman treatment in a private Spa House.

\\
**Spa Houses**

A private temple of wellbeing, Aman New York’s Spa Houses offer reprieve from the spirited energy of the city that surrounds. Centred around either a traditional Banya or Hammam experience, each Spa House offers its own transformational wellness journey, complete with double treatment rooms, an interior lounge and a private terrace with a cold plunge and hot bath for leisure use.

\\
**Movement**

Aman Spa New York offers tailored fitness, movement and mindfulness sessions with in-house trainers and visiting practitioners. Utilising state-of-the-art diagnostic tools, cutting-edge equipment and personalised programmes, your wellness journey is continuously supported long after each session.

\\
**Medical Aesthetics by bodySCULPT**

In partnership with bodySCULPT Wellness & Aesthetics, Aman New York offers minimally and non-invasive medical aesthetics and wellness services. From radiance-boosting Hydrafacials to IV therapies and expert consultations, each treatment utilises the latest technology and practices for exceptional results.

### New York City Ballet

In collaboration with New York City Ballet, Aman New York offers a rare immersion into the world of ballet with curated experiences centring on the iconic productions of George Balanchine's _The Nutcracker_ and Alexei Ratmansky's _Romeo & Juliet_.

\\
**Storm King Art Center**

Embark on a transcendent journey, departing the city by helicopter, vibrant foliage painting a breathtaking panorama below. Arrive at Storm King Art Center, an esteemed open-air museum in New Windsor housing one of the largest contemporary outdoor sculpture collections in North America, followed by a guided tour with the curator and an Aman-prepared picnic lunch.

\\
**Lincoln Center for Performing Arts**

Explore the iconic campus with a private tour led by the head of the Visual Arts programme or go behind-the-scenes of their renowned performances spanning orchestral performances, opera, jazz, ballet, theatre and more.

\\
**Museums & Galleries**

Through curator-led museum and gallery tours, explore the diverse artistic expression that defines this iconic city and discover a unique perspective of your own.
