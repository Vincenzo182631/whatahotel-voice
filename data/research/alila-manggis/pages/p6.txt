### A Hidden Gem with a Few Rough Edges

Alila Manggis is one of those properties that feels genuinely personal rather than corporate — with only a handful of rooms, you get a sense of intimacy that's hard to find at bigger resorts. The staff were outstanding throughout our stay, attentive without ever being intrusive, and clearly proud of the property.

The pool area is a real highlight, and a great spot to spend an afternoon. Worth noting: the beach out front is rocky rather than sandy, so this isn't a swimming beach — more a scenic backdrop than a place to take a dip.

Two things worth flagging for future guests. First, there's road construction currently underway along the beachfront side of the hotel, which did detract a bit from the peace and quiet we were hoping for — hopefully that wraps up soon. Second, the rooms themselves are looking a little dated and could use a refresh; the bones are good, but some updating would go a long way.

None of that took away from the overall experience, though. Between the setting, the pool, and especially the staff, this was a great stay and one I'd recommend — just go in with realistic expectations on the room décor and check on the construction status before you book.

### Stunning property

3 of us were having girls trip early August and stayed for 3-4 nights. I took the opportunity to try the lower ground and upper floor and here are my observations:

Lower ground:
Fab exit to green grass and straight to pool. The terrace sitting is private with brick walls separated each room. Even though we face the swimming pool you really cannot see what's inside the room with glass reflection.

Upper floor:
Slight upscale amenities including coffee pod (which is not utilised during my stay) and gorgeous day bed facing the sea. The drawback is there is less privacy at balcony because its only separated by cloth (not visible) but you can hear next doors talking. At one point 2 rooms next to me was talking on phone for 1.5 hours in the balcony which disturb all the peacefulness.

The property itself is stunning and has this relaxing vibe with superb staff. I reduced the star rating because there was construction facing the beach and it was damn noisey starting from 8.30 to 7.30 pm and we were lucky to have 1 day when they moved further up. This came as a surprise with no pre warning from the hotel prior to stay, and all the guests I spoke to have similar disatisfaction. I have been to other hotels where they pre warned in the hotel website snd given this hotel is much further awayfrom the main centre and the purpose is for relaxation, the excavators noise level are so disturbing all day long.

Otherwise we will return in future when no more construction work.
