## Alila Moments

Shaped by land and craft, by culture and care, Alila Moments reveal a different way of seeing. These personalized experiences offered at our Alila properties draw you closer to the world around you, and your place within it. These are our Moments, crafted just for you.

### Flavors of East Bali—A Culinary Encounter

Amidst the serene beauty of Alila Manggis, set forth on a culinary journey into the soul of Balinese tradition. In the tranquil Organic Garden, discover the banana tree’s vital role in local culture—its tender stem for dishes, sweet fruit for daily meals, and leaves as eco-friendly wraps for sacred offerings. Guided by expert chefs, stroll through lush rice paddies, exploring the harmony of nature and culture. Learn to craft authentic dishes using time-honored techniques, from grinding spices to wrapping food in banana leaves. This isn’t just a cooking class—it’s an intimate connection to Bali’s roots, where nature, tradition and artistry blend seamlessly.

**Garden Harvest**

Wander through the resort's lush organic garden, discovering a variety of fresh, locally sourced vegetables. Learn about the sustainable practices that nurture these ingredients, including the unique banana tree vegetable (Jukut Ares), which adds a distinctive flavor to your dishes.​​

**Flavors Come Alive**

Master the art of using the Ulekan (Balinese mortar and pestle) to create Bumbu Bali (curry paste) and Balinese chop garlic, onion and chili (Sambel Mbe). Immerse yourself in Balinese culinary traditions with this hands-on experience.​​

**Art in the Kitchen**

Refine your skills in preparing Jukut Ares (Banana Stem Soup), Pepes Ayam and Banan Fritter. It’s a perfect combination of dishes that seamlessly blend with the beautiful simplicity of East Bali. ​​

**A Feast by the Sea**

Enjoy a leisurely lunch at Organic Garden, savoring the fruits of your culinary journey and celebrating the vibrant flavors of your freshly prepared dishes.​​

**Duration:** 4hours

**Recommended Dress:** Resort casual

**Additional Details:** Guests will participate in on hands-on cooking with Chef at the garden, starting with picking out the ingredients to cooking them together using the traditional firewood method.

**Price:** IDR 1,750,000++ per person

### Caves & Crystals – A Sacred Journey to Goa Lawah and the Salt Fields

This is a journey of duality—of earth and sea, spirit and labor. Discover the enchanting Goa Lawah, Bali’s sacred Bat Cave, just minutes from Alila Manggis. This mystical site blends nature and spirituality, with a temple guarding a cavern alive with thousands of bats. During the ceremony you'll witness as the air hums with their wings, mingling with fragrant incense and the rhythmic chants of priests. Then continue to Kusamba’s coast, where sea salt is still harvested by hand in sunlit rituals passed down through generations. Walk the shimmering pans, speak with salt farmers, and witness the elemental process unfold. Along the way, you’ll discover that Bali’s soul is as much in its sacred sites as in its simplest, most enduring traditions.

**Arrival and Blessings** Depart Alila Manggis and begin your journey with a scenic drive to Goa Lawah Temple, one of Bali’s most revered spiritual sites.

**Enter the Cave Temple** Explore the Bat Cave sanctuary, observing the intricate stonework and vibrant offerings that connect Bali’s present to its past.

**To the Edge of the Sea** Travel to Kusamba, where salt farmers work in harmony with tide and sun, using ancestral techniques to produce Bali’s famed sea salt.

**Witness the Process** Walk through the salt pans, learn the steps from seawater to crystal, and engage with the people who carry on this elemental craft.

**Duration:** 4 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Resort wear

**Additional Details:** Woman on monthly period are not allowed to enter the temple. On some special days in Bali, some areas are closed due to the ceremony in the temple.

**Price:** IDR 1,500,000++ per person

### Mountain to Sea—Cycling Through a Living Tapestry

Embark on an unforgettable cycling journey from Bali’s highlands to the coast—an ever-changing ride through culture, agriculture and raw beauty. Begin in the cool air of Selat, pedaling past rice terraces, snake fruit plantations and traditional villages. The rhythm of rural life unfolds around you with each turn of the wheel: farmers at work, children waving from roadside shrines and the scent of earth and rain in the air. Descend toward the coast, where your reward awaits—a serene white-sand beach and a fresh-catch lunch prepared just for you. This is not just a ride; it’s a moving meditation through the soul of East Bali.

**The Highland Start**

Begin in the mountain village of Selat, where cooler air and expansive views set the tone for your descent.

**Ride the Ribbon of Life**

Cycle through landscapes stitched with rice fields, salak (snake fruit) farms and small villages, immersed in everyday Balinese life.

**Coastal Descent**

Continue toward the sea as the terrain gently shifts from inland green to coastal blue, with rest stops along the way.

**Beachside Reward**

Arrive at a peaceful white-sand beach where a picnic lunch awaits—local flavors served with a view and a sense of earned stillness.

**Duration:** 6 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Sportswear

**Additional Details:** Like another cycling, the guest will go to the village where they go through the Snake Fruit farm and finish up at the Pasir Putih Beach to have lunch.

**Price:** IDR 2,000,000++ per person

### Roots of Tenganan – A Journey Through Bali Aga Culture

Step back in time with a journey into the protected world of Tenganan, one of Bali’s oldest and most spiritually rich villages. Cycle through emerald rice fields before entering this Bali Aga settlement, where customs remain untouched by the outside world. Wander among stone walls and woven offerings, speak with master ikat weavers and taste wild honey harvested from sacred groves. Learn about local beliefs, spiritual restrictions and community rituals that shape daily life. This experience is not just a visit—it’s a rare cultural immersion, offering a deeper understanding of a Bali that few ever get to see.

**Ride into the Past** Begin your journey with a guided bike ride through lush rice terraces, soaking in East Bali’s vibrant landscapes.

**The Gate to Tenganan**

Arrive at the ancient village of Tenganan, home of the Bali Aga people, and receive a respectful introduction to its customs and spiritual protocols.

**Woven Histories**

Meet master artisans who create Geringsing ikat, one of the world’s rarest textiles, dyed and woven with symbolic meaning.

**Taste of the Sacred**

Sample wild forest honey and other traditional products tied to ritual and place—each crafted with reverence for the land.

**Departure with Understanding**

Leave with a sense of cultural reverence and connection, having stepped into one of Bali’s most sacred and enduring ways of life.

**Duration:** 3 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Resort wear, modest dress due to visit the temple. Balinese Sarong will be provided by Alila Manggis.

**Additional Details:** Woman on monthly period are not allowed to enter the temple.

**Price:** IDR 1,150,000++ per person
