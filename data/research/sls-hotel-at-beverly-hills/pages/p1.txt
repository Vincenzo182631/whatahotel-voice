# an indulgent wonderland in beverly hills

Make your entrance on an actual red carpet. Revel in lavish interiors by the venerable Phillippe Starck. Shop Rodeo Drive, play in West Hollywood, dine poolside at LADiDa Rooftop. Get beauty sleep in plush rooms and suites. Rise early with the LA sunshine. Welcome to SLS Beverly Hills, an iconic and indulgent wonderland where guests receive the star treatment from the moment they arrive.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Ultra-modern design meets classic elegance in 297 luxurious rooms and suites. It’s everything you’d expect from an iconic Beverly Hills hotel, and so much more.

## SAVOR & SIP

SLS Beverly Hills’ culinary experiences feature fresh Californian fare with seasonal menus inspired by local harvests as well as distant flavors. Captivating cocktails perfected with equal parts curiosity and craftsmanship. Savor the destination, and our culinary imagination, from the comfort of your room or one of our distinct outdoor settings as you discover what makes Los Angeles one of the most exciting cities for epicurean adventure.
