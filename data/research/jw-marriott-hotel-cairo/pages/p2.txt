## Accommodations

Experience refined Cairo accommodation at JW Marriott Hotel Cairo, featuring 440 luxurious guest rooms and hotel suites designed for comfort and elegance. Enjoy stunning views of the golf course, resort pool or private beach, along with deluxe marble bathrooms, soundproof windows and 24-hour room service. ### Luxurious Retreat with Golf View

Indulge in the ultimate luxury experience in our expansive 266-square-meter Royal retreat, featuring a king-sized bed, a bathroom equipped with a jacuzzi for golf views, a fully-equipped bar and kitchen, an eight-seat dining table, a comfortable living room with a TV, and panoramic golf course views throughout.

## Presidential Suite

Make yourself at home in our duplex suite bedroom, outfitted with soft bedding to ensure your ultimate comfort. These stylish, mood-enhancing, split-level suite provides a living room and two balconies offering stunning views of our resort's golf course. This suite features large bathtub, shower and two sinks as well as upscale amenities.

## Executive Suite

Elevate your stay by booking an executive room or suite and experience our exquisite Executive Lounge. Also, unwind during our vibrant Happy Hour, featuring local alcoholic drinks and a light dinner.
