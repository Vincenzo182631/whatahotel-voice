# City Exploration and Golf in Cairo

## Experiences

Designed so you can relax and indulge, JW Marriott Cairo is home to many leisure experiences, from massages and a spa in Cairo to golf opportunities. Mandara Spa will soothe your senses, as the fully equipped Health Center, sports courts and indoor swimming pool will keep you feeling healthy. Soak up the sun by the outdoor swimming pool or ‘The Beach’ - Cairo’s dynamic water park, with sandy shores...See More and a wave pool. Younger guests will enjoy fun activities at the Kids Club with the Family by JW Program. Both business and leisure travelers always return to this unique and welcoming hotel near City Stars Mall in Cairo.See Less

### Airport Shuttle Service

As a valued guest of our hotel, we are pleased to offer a complimentary round-trip airport shuttle to ensure a seamless start and end to your stay. Our dedicated shuttle buses operate on a scheduled basis, running between the local airport and our hotel lobby. Reservations are required 48 hours in advance.

The Beach

## All Is Well in the World

Experience the ultimate family fun at our man-made beach, complete with an exhilarating aqua park designed for children. Relax on the sandy shores while your little ones splash and play in the thrilling water attractions, creating unforgettable memories in a safe and entertaining environment.

### Your Gateway to the Grand Egyptian Museum - GEM

The Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM), located just 40 minutes away from our hotel with no traffic, is a must-visit for anyone interested in ancient history. This brand-new museum opens daily from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Experience the rich heritage of Egypt in this stunning architectural marvel, just a short drive from your accommodation. Don't miss the grand opening ceremony on November 1st. Enjoy a comfortable...See More stay with easy access to this must-visit destination, making your trip to Egypt unforgettable.See Less

## Mirage City Golf Club

Mirage City Golf Club has presented Egypt's most prestigious golfing experience for over twenty years, hosting a number of world famous golf tournaments, winning a number of awards and attracting enthusiasts from all levels of play.The Club's award winning, Peter Harradine designed 18-hole championship and 9-hole executive courses, are the ideal place to improve an existing game or enjoy a competitive round with friends. Enclosed within beautifully landscaped gardens, and serviced by an elegant clubhouse, pro-shop and team of professional staff.

## Mandara Spa

Experience ultimate relaxation at Mandara Bliss, where healing energies are carefully directed to the areas of your body that need it most. Choose from a wide variety of soothing services, each designed to release tension and promote tranquility. Begin your journey with a calming ritual of your choice, then enjoy peaceful reflection time while savoring a selection of thoughtfully curated refreshments, leaving you renewed in both body and mind.

### JW Garden

We believe that a connection to the natural environment is a necessary part of our guests experience. JW Garden is our source of fresh ingredients where it guides explorations of the landscape, striving to honor the natural environment.

## The Pool

The JW Marriott Cairo offers guests an exceptional luxury escape, complete with our action-manmade beach and the serene Health Center. Days spent outside are inspired by the stunning swimming pool at the JW Marriott Cairo. Enjoy the New Cairo heat on your terms by taking a dip in our outside pool. Regardless of what you choose, happiness will be found around every corner.

### Family by JW

Whether your child is a budding foodie, an abstract finger painter or just loves to explore, JW Marriott Hotel® Cairo offers enriching experiences designed to bring you and your kids together for some fun-filled creative adventures thoughtfully curated to leave the whole family feeling inspired and connected throughout your journey.

### Executive Lounge

Enjoy exclusive Executive Lounge access with all-day snacks and drinks, plus a buffet breakfast from 6:30 am to 11:00 am. Unwind during our vibrant happy hour from 6:00 pm to 8:00 pm with a light dinner and local alcoholic drinks. Whether catching up with colleagues indoors or outdoors, or taking time for personal reflection, our lounge also offers a quiet library and office facilities for your convenience.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Hotel Cairo have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Hotel Cairo offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Sauna, Steam, Jacuzzi, Indoor & Outdoor pools, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Cairo have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Cairo has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Indoor Pool at Health Center (Indoor)Outdoor Cabana Pool (Outdoor)Outdoor Pool at Health Center (Outdoor)

Does JW Marriott Hotel Cairo have an on-site spa?

Yes, Mandara Spa is located on-site at JW Marriott Hotel Cairo. Click here to book a service now.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Cairo offer golf course access nearby or at the hotel?

Yes, hotel guests have access to Mirage City Golf Club.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Hotel Cairo?

Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM), Al Azhar Mosque & University, Cairo Opera House, Cairo Tower, The Citadel and more are nearby JW Marriott Hotel Cairo.
