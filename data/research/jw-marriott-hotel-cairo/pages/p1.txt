# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Cairo

## Where Luxury Meets Unforgettable Experiences

Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Cairo, where 5-star luxury and exceptional hotel hospitality come together in the heart of New Cairo, Egypt. Conveniently located near Cairo International Airport and Al Manara International Conference Center, our luxury hotel in Cairo is ideal for both business and leisure travelers. Discover 445 elegantly appointed rooms and suites, thoughtfully designed with premium...See More amenities to ensure a comfortable stay. Savor diverse culinary experiences across our restaurants and lounges, featuring Italian, Asian, American, Oriental and international flavors. Enjoy a round at Mirage City Golf Club, rejuvenate at Mandara Spa, or relax on a stunning man-made beach. Families can enjoy kids’ water activities to make memorable moments. Committed to responsible hospitality, we support sustainability through initiatives such as LED lighting and solar energy. With shopping and entertainment destinations nearby, our hotel in Cairo, Egypt, offers the perfect blend of luxury, comfort and convenience.See Less

### Meet & Assist

The Meet & Assist service at Cairo International Airport is provided exclusively for foreign travelers under the supervision of the Egyptian Tourism Police Authority, with reservations required at least 48 hours in advance and a fee of USD 35 per person. Guests may be requested to wait until fellow passengers on the same flight have completed immigration procedures. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Cairo?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Cairo is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Cairo allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Cairo.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Cairo?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Cairo. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Cairo, with rates of $1.25 per hour, and $4.25 per day. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Cairo have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Cairo has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Cairo booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Cairo?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Cairo is Cairo International Airport (CAI). CAI is located approximately 3.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Cairo have an airport shuttle to Cairo International Airport (CAI)?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Cairo has an airport shuttle to Cairo International Airport (CAI).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Cairo have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Cairo has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Cairo have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Cairo has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
