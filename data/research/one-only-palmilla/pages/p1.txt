#### Pet Policy at One&Only Palmilla

- Cats and dogs are welcome.
- A maximum of one pet is permitted per room booked.
- Dogs must weigh less than 40 pounds.
- Do not leave your pet unattended.
- Pets are to be kept on leash at all times.
- Pets are permitted in designated areas of our pools, beach and restaurants. Pets are not permitted inside the Spa or KidsOnly (not applicable to Guide or Service dogs). No animals are permitted inside the swimming pools.
- Dog walking, curated cuisine and pampering services are available; inquire for more details.

#### Pet Fees

- Daily pet fee is USD 75; please connect with our team for a comprehensive list of amenities, services and for our full pet policy.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Useful details you need to know before you travel to One&Only Palmilla

#### What are the passport, visa and vaccination requirements for Mexico?

All visitors to Mexico must present a passport valid for at least 6 months from the date of travel. Visa requirements for entering Mexico vary significantly from country to country. Click here for more information.

#### Time Zone

Los Cabos is located within the Mountain Standard Time Zone, -7 GMT. As Mexico no longer participates in Daylight Savings Time, from March through October, Los Cabos aligns with Pacific Standard Time, which is -8 GMT.

#### What is the local currency in Mexico?

The Peso is the official currency of Mexico. All payments must be paid by credit card, One&Only Palmilla does not accept cash payments.

#### Which credit cards are accepted at One&Only Palmilla?

- American Express
- Visa
- Mastercard
- Union Pay
- Discover

#### What languages are spoken in Mexico?

Staff members are fluent in both English and Spanish.

#### Where is the nearest ATM from One&Only Palmilla?

For guest convenience an ATM is located at the Porte Cochere, bills are dispensed in pesos.

#### What internet services are available at One&Only Palmilla?

Complimentary high-speed WiFi service is available throughout the resort.

#### What is the address of One&Only Palmilla?

Kilometro 7.5 Carretera Transpeninsular, 23400 San Jose del Cabo, B.C.S., Mexico

#### What is the weather like in Mexico?

This part of Mexico enjoys sunshine most of the year, and the weather in Los Cabos is very comfortable, with most days filled with warm sunshine and low humidity. Average highs range from the mid-70s in the winter months to the mid-90s in the summer months, when humidity can also be higher.

#### What are the check-in and check-out times at One&Only Palmilla?

Check-in is at 3pm and check-out is at 12pm.

#### What is the electrical voltage at One&Only Palmilla?

Electricity is supplied at 120 volts. Adaptors are available.
