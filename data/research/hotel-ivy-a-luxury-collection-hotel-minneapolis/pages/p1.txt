# Welcome to Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis

## Experience our luxury hotel in Minneapolis, MN

One of the city's most enduring landmarks provides the backdrop for an unforgettable stay at Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, a premier luxury downtown Minneapolis hotel. Connected to the Skyway system,...See More our downtown Minneapolis hotel offers seamless access to the city's top attractions, dining, and entertainment. Tradition and modernity blend in our 136 rooms and suites, each featuring high-speed internet, original artwork, fine linens, and limestone bathrooms. Unwind at Anda Spa or recharge at Anda Fitness Center. Savor New American cuisine at Breva or descend into Masa & Agave, our vibrant basement cantina. Whether you're hosting a private event with custom catering or enjoying in-room dining, every detail is designed for comfort and elegance. As a pet-friendly hotel in Minneapolis travelers trust, we welcome all guests, including four-legged companions. Rethink refinement in the Twin Cities at Hotel Ivy.See Less

The Ivy Tower was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1986
Even before the blueprints were brought to fruition, it was clear that Minneapolis’s Ivy Tower was destined to be different. In both form and function, the tower contrasted greatly from the skyscrapers going up throughout the country.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis?

The check-in time at Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis?

Off-site valet parking is available at Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis. The daily valet fee is $60.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis booking.

What is the closest airport near Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis?

The closest airport near Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis is Minneapolis-St Paul International Airport (MSP). MSP is located approximately 8.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Hotel Ivy, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Minneapolis does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
