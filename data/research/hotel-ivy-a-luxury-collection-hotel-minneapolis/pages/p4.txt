### Explore Minneapolis

Located in Minneapolis next to the Convention Center, Hotel Ivy is close to all central attractions in and around the city.

## Historic Ivy Tower

The Ivy Tower was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1986. The Ziggurat style is identified as a terraced pyramid of successively receding stories.

## Masa & Agave

Named 2012 City Pages Best Bartender of the Twin Cities, he represented Minneapolis nationally in the 2012 GQ/Bombay Sapphire Most Imaginative Bartender, and in 2014 named Charlie Awards Outstanding Bartender.

The Minnesota Convention Center

Connected to Hotel Ivy with climate-controlled skyways. With 480,000 square feed the Convention Center can handle any event from a small meeting to a large convention or trade shows.

U.S. Bank Stadium

Opened in July of 2016, U.S. Bank Stadium is the breathtaking home of the Minnesota Vikings football team. This brand-new stadium is located near our hotel in downtown Minneapolis.

Target Field

Home of the Minnesota Twins. Make your way with ease to the stadium, which is located near our hotel in downtown Minneapolis. Boasting capacity for up to 39,500 people, Target Field also plays host to an array of special events and concerts throughout the year.

Shopping

A historic shopping destination, Nicollet Mall has been downtown Minneapolis's primary retail district since the beginning of the 20th century. Today, visiting Nicollet Mall is one of the most popular things to do in Minneapolis.

Hennepin Theater Trust

Hotel Ivy, Minneapolis partners with the Hennepin Theatre Trust to provide access to the thriving Hennepin Avenue cultural district in downtown Minneapolis. Open year-round, Broadway's best are on display to present a rich mix of live performances and create inspiring arts education experiences.

The Minnesota Orchestra

The Grammy Award-winning Minnesota Orchestra ranks among the top symphonic ensembles in the United States, with a distinguished history of acclaimed performances in Minneapolis and around the world. Recipient of the 2014 Grammy for Best Orchestral Performance.
