## CANADA'S _iconic_ CASTLE IN THE CITY

Fairmont Hotel Vancouver has been the pinnacle of elegance since its debut in 1939, when a visit by the British royal couple sealed its reputation as one of the most iconic, 5-star luxury hotels in Vancouver. Journey through the iconic moments of the storied past within the renovated grandeur of the present.

### Step into a world where contemporary convenience meets iconic luxury

Our prime downtown Vancouver location places you in the centre of the city’s biggest attractions, including the Vancouver Art Gallery, Granville Island, Stanley Park Seawall, and Robson Street shopping. Fairmont Hotel Vancouver was named on Condé Nast Traveler Magazine’s Gold List, the only Canadian hotel to receive this prestigious honour in 2024, as well as a 4-star rating on both the 2025 and 2026 Forbes Travel Guides.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fairmont Hotel Vancouver pet friendly?

Fairmont Hotel Vancouver is pet friendly, and even has resident Canine Ambassadors. The pet fee of $50.00 per night plus taxes (to a maximum of $100) applies to all pets and will be added to your guestroom for deep cleaning services. Guests are liable for any damages and/or repairs other than normal wear and tear that may occur. Any noise complaints that result in refunds to other guests will be charged to the guest room with the pet. A $75 removal fee will be applied to your guest room should your dog be removed due to excessive noise. Due to health regulations, pets are not allowed in the hotel restaurant, on the Health Club pool deck, or within the Fairmont Gold Lounge.

Animals must not be left unattended in the guest room. Service dogs are always welcome and exempt of charges.

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Hotel Vancouver?

Yes, Fairmont Hotel Vancouver offers accessible guest rooms and facilities. Additional accessibility items available upon request include:

- Toilet seat extensions to raise the height of the toilet seat
- Crutches
- Bed Pans
- Heating pads or ice packs
- TDD on site (please advise the hotel in advance)
- Closed captioning for televisions
- Fire alarm lamp

Does Fairmont Hotel Vancouver have a swimming pool?

Yes, the indoor swimming pool at our luxury hotel is open daily from 06:00am to 10:30pm. Please note that there is no hot tub on property.

Does Fairmont Hotel Vancouver have a gym/fitness center?

The Fitness Center at Fairmont Hotel Vancouver features state-of-the-art exercise equipment, a sauna, and an indoor swimming pool. The Indoor Swimming Pool is open daily from 6 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. The Fitness Centre and change rooms are open 24 hours a day. The sauna is open from 6 am to 11:00 pm.

Does Fairmont Hotel Vancouver have a spa?

Although Fairmont Hotel Vancouver does not currently have an on-site spa, please stay tuned as a new wellness experience is coming later in 2025.

What is the closest airport to Fairmont Hotel Vancouver?

Vancouver International Airport (YVR) is about 20 minutes from downtown Vancouver. It is easily accessible by car, taxi or a 25 minute SkyTrain ride.
