## The Hotel

**The Lowell Story**

**Hotel Services**

**Location**

**The Neighborhood**

The Lowell Hotel, a distinguished luxury hotel located in the heart of Manhattan, stands 17 stories tall amidst a renowned residential area, mere moments from the esteemed fashion boutiques of Madison Avenue and just steps away from Fifth Avenue and the expansive tranquility of Central Park. Founded in 1927, The Lowell embodies the enduring sophistication and character of the Upper East Side. Additionally, it houses the acclaimed Majorelle restaurant and Jacques Bar, popular among both guests and local patrons, now also serving an exclusive Lily of the Valley Afternoon Tea in partnership with Dior Maison.

Guests are greeted by the warmth of a familiar smile, meticulous attention to detail, and unobtrusive service as they navigate through elegantly designed classical spaces. The hotel features rare applewood-burning fireplaces, alongside landscaped terraces that offer breathtaking views of the city, all within sunlit rooms and suites that reflect the hotel’s unique aesthetic, providing the refined comforts akin to those of a private residence.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What sets the Lowell apart from other luxury hotels in New York?

The Lowell stands apart from other luxury hotels in New York through its rare combination of intimacy, heritage, and personalised service. With just 74 rooms and suites, it offers a far more private and residential experience than many larger Manhattan luxury hotels.

As a family-owned luxury hotel in New York that has been independently operated since 1927, The Lowell is one of the few remaining hotels in the city with a long-standing family legacy. This commitment to hospitality is reflected in the attentive, discreet service that guests return for year after year. The hotel is also known for its spacious accommodations, many of which feature wood-burning fireplaces and private terraces—amenities that are exceptionally rare in New York City luxury hotels.

### What room should I book at The Lowell?

The best room at The Lowell depends on your stay. Choose a Lowell Room or Junior Suite for a luxurious city escape, a Lowell One Bedroom Suite for extra space or extended stays, a Two Bedroom Suite for families, or one of our Specialty Suites for the ultimate luxury experience. For an unforgettable stay, request a suite with one of The Lowell's iconic wood-burning fireplaces or a private terrace. Our Reservations Team is happy to recommend the perfect accommodation.

### Is The Lowell family owned?

The Lowell is one of the few remaining independently and family-owned luxury hotels in Manhattan. Since opening in 1927, the hotel has remained independent, allowing it to deliver a highly personalized guest experience shaped by its heritage rather than the standards of a large hotel brand.

With just 47 suites and 27 rooms, The Lowell is renowned for its discreet, intuitive service, long-tenured team, and intimate residential feel. Independent ownership gives the hotel the freedom to focus on exceptional hospitality, timeless elegance, and meaningful guest relationships—qualities that have earned the loyalty of discerning travelers from around the world for generations.

### Is The Lowell pet friendly?

The Lowell is proud to be one of New York City's premier pet-friendly hotels welcoming well-behaved pets, making it an ideal choice for guests travelling with their four-legged companions. Located just moments from Central Park, The Lowell offers easy access to one of the city's most beloved green spaces, perfect for scenic morning walks, playtime, and daily exercise.

Every pet stay includes thoughtful amenities such as a comfortable pet bed and welcome treats. Our Guest Services Team can assist with dog walking, while our Concierge is happy to arrange grooming appointments, and recommend nearby pet-friendly parks, cafés, and activities. Your pet can even enjoy a specially curated in-room dining menu, with customized meals and snacks prepared by our culinary team upon request.

A one-time cleaning fee of $200 per stay applies, and certain restrictions and policies may apply. For specific pet requirements or to discuss your companion's needs before arrival, please contact our Concierge or Reservations team.

### Does The Lowell host private events?

Yes. Majorelle and The Club Room have hosted everything from high-profile events and intimate rehearsal dinners to special celebrations. The Events team can help create a memorable experience tailored to your occasion.
