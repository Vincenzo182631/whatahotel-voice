## Neighbourhood

Nestled among charming historic townhouses on East 63rd Street, The Lowell Hotel is perfectly positioned between the renowned Madison Avenue and Park Avenue, placing it right in the heart of Manhattan's luxury scene. Guests can indulge in high-end shopping just steps away, with prestigious brands like Hermes, Chanel, and Celine nearby. A short walk leads to the luxury flagship stores along Fifth Avenue, and iconic shopping destinations like Bergdorf Goodman and Bloomingdale’s are also within easy reach.

Just a quick stroll from the hotel, Central Park awaits, providing immediate access to its famous attractions such as the zoo, Wollman skating rink, and Sheep’s Meadow. The Lowell is surrounded by the cultural treasures of Museum Mile, including The MET, Guggenheim, MOMA, and the Frick Collection. Located in the vibrant Upper East Side, The Lowell offers guests a true taste of neighborhood life while ensuring easy access to the city's most famous landmarks.

The dedicated Concierge team at The Lowell is ready to assist guests with reservations and Broadway ticket bookings, ensuring a seamless and enjoyable stay.
