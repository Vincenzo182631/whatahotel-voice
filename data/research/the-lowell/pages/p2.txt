A chic refuge in a lively city, The Lowell's luxury accommodation in NYC invites guests to make the hotel their home away from home. Inspired by designer Michael Smith’s timeless residential style, 47 individually-appointed suites and 27 deluxe rooms are graced with the coveted luxuries of a private New York apartment from original wood-burning fireplaces and gourmet kitchens to beautiful terraces with iconic views of Manhattan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are some of the amenities at The Lowell?

The Lowell is the epitome of luxury in Manhattan. A true New York hotel, the property offers guests DDC28 toileteries designed by the co-owner, excellent room service. Some rooms have wood burning fireplaces and terraces and the hotel has a concierge service with access to highly exclusive spots in New York City.

### Do all rooms have a terrace?

We have a limited number of rooms and suites with a terrace. We advise you call our Reservations Team directly to book a room with a terrace.

### Do all rooms have a fireplace?

The Lowell is the only hotel in New York City with wood burning fireplaces. A true luxury element, however is limited to certain room categories so we advise in case you need a fireplace guaranteed with your room type and booking, contact our Reservations Team directly!

### Do all bathrooms have a bathtub?

Not all bathrooms offer a bathtub so to ensure your needs are met, contact our Reservations Team at time of booking to let them know your preferences of room type.
