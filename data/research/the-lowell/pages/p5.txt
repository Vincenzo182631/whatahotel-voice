The Lowell Hotel is home to Majorelle, Jacques Bar, The Club Room and the much loved, Pembroke Room, all offering a serene getaway from the bustle of the city. We invite you to explore our food and beverage offerings. Be it day or night, there is something for everyone.

Indulge in the elegant and sophisticated atmosphere of Majorelle, where you can savor French-Moroccan cuisine and also enjoy our much awaited

Lily of the Valley Tea, an exquisite Afternoon Tea experience launched in partnership with Dior Maison

For a more casual yet equally delightful experience, head to Jacques Bar, where you can enjoy classic cocktails and small plates in a cozy and inviting setting.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What cuisine does Majorelle offer?

Majorelle offers French-Mediterranean cuisine with Moroccan influences, combining classic French technique with seasonal ingredients and flavors inspired by the Mediterranean and North Africa. The menu includes both refined contemporary dishes and signature classics such as Grilled Dover Sole and Poulet Grand-Mère.

### We want to book a dinner for a special occasion.    Should we book Jacques Bar or Majorelle?

For a special occasion dinner, we recommend Majorelle. Our signature restaurant offers an elegant setting, seasonal French-inspired cuisine, and a refined atmosphere that's ideal for dates and celebrations.

If you're looking for a more intimate yet relaxed evening, Jacques Bar is perfect for cocktails and nibbles before or after dinner, or a relaxed celebration in a sophisticated setting.

### What are the most popular cocktails at Jacques Bar?

If you’re visiting Jacques Bar at The Lowell in New York, the cocktail menu is worth exploring. The bar leans into elegant, modern cocktails with fresh, fruity and slightly unexpected flavours.

Marrakesh Express:

Tito’s vodka, pineapple and St-Germain. This is a great introduction to the menu — refreshing, fruity and floral without being overly sweet.

Lily of the Valley:

Grey Goose, St-Germain, lime, pineapple and cucumber. Fresh and light, this is a particularly good choice if you prefer crisp, refreshing cocktails.

Moroccan Orange:

Mezcal, orgeat, lemon and orange. A more distinctive option, combining smoky mezcal with bright citrus and a subtle nuttiness from the orgeat.
