## About The Lowell

The Lowell is a New York legacy and a landmark luxury hotel, located near Central Park and all the wonderful shops of Madison Avenue.

The hotel rises 17-floors above an iconic residential neighborhood, a stone’s throw from the celebrated fashion houses of Madison Avenue and footsteps from Fifth Avenue and the vast oasis of Central Park.

Established in 1927, The Lowell, New York, one of the few remaining family owned hotels in the city, reflects the timeless elegance and soul of the Upper East Side in New York. The warmth of a familiar smile, attention to detail and discreet service welcome discerning guests as they move through chic classical spaces. Wood-burning fireplaces—a rare find in New York—and landscaped terraces with sweeping city views are enjoyed in sun-filled rooms and suites fashioned in the hotel’s distinctive style with the refined comforts of a luxury NYC apartment.

Guests come to escape the bustle of the city over the perfect martini at Jacques Bar, and seasonal cuisine at Majorelle, a gem among Upper East Side restaurants. The Club Room at The Lowell offers a sophisticated setting to unwind, open exclusively to guests of the hotel, while the reimagined Pembroke Room upholds tradition with its beloved breakfast and weekend brunch.

The Lowell is excited to collaborate with Dior Maison on the Lily of the Valley Afternoon Tea served at Majorelle. A hotel known as a destination for tea, we are excited to form this new collaboration and share a unique and memorable experience with guests.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is The Lowell located?

The Lowell is located at 28 East 63rd Street, New York; in the heart of Manhattan's prestigious Upper East Side. Situated on a quiet, tree-lined residential street between Madison Avenue and Park Avenue, the hotel offers a peaceful getaway while remaining close to some of New York City's most iconic attractions.

Guests are only steps from Madison Avenue's renowned luxury boutiques and fashion houses, and a 5 minute walk from Central Park. The hotel's prime Upper East Side location also provides convenient access to Midtown Manhattan, Fifth Avenue, world-class museums, fine dining, and business districts.

For travellers seeking a luxury hotel near Madison Avenue NYC or a hotel near Central Park on the Upper East Side, The Lowell combines an exceptional location with the charm and tranquillity of one of New York's most desirable neighbourhoods.

### What is The Lily of the Valley Afternoon Tea?

The Lily of the Valley Afternoon Tea at The Lowell is an elegant afternoon tea experience served at Majorelle, the hotel's acclaimed restaurant. Created in partnership with Dior Maison, the tea is inspired by the lily of the valley flower, one of Christian Dior's favorite blooms and a symbol closely associated with the House of Dior.

Recognized as one of the most distinctive afternoon tea experiences in New York City, the Lily of the Valley Afternoon Tea combines refined culinary offerings with beautifully curated Dior Maison table settings and presentation. The experience reflects both The Lowell's timeless sophistication and Dior Maison's celebrated design aesthetic.

The tea is available to hotel guests and outside visitors, although reservations are highly recommended due to its popularity. It has also received recognition from Condé Nast Traveller, further establishing it as one of the best afternoon teas in New York.

### Is The Lowell family friendly?

The Lowell is a family-friendly luxury hotel on Manhattan's Upper East Side, welcoming guests of all ages. Families enjoy our spacious guest rooms and suites, many of which can be connected and feature kitchenettes, providing additional space and flexibility.

Located just a short walk from Central Park, the Central Park Zoo, world-renowned museums, and family-friendly attractions, The Lowell is an ideal base for exploring New York City. After a day of sightseeing, the hotel's peaceful atmosphere offers a relaxing retreat away from the bustle of the city.

Cribs and other family amenities are available upon request. Whether you're traveling with young children or teenagers, The Lowell combines personalized service, spacious accommodations, and an exceptional Upper East Side location for a comfortable and memorable family stay.

### What wellness services does The Lowell offer?

The Lowell offers guests access to exceptional wellness experiences through its partnership with the renowned Dior Spa New York, an exclusive wellness destination located at the corner of Madison Avenue and 57th street. As the only Dior Spa in New York City, it provides a distinctive blend of luxury, innovation, and personalized care.

The spa features a curated menu of bespoke face and body treatments inspired by Dior's haute couture philosophy, combining advanced skincare expertise with a holistic approach to wellbeing. Guests can enjoy tailored experiences designed to promote relaxation, rejuvenation, and overall wellness in an elegant and intimate setting.

Appointments are available for both hotel guests and outside visitors, making the Dior Spa an ideal complement to a luxury New York City stay or a wellness-focused day on the Upper East Side.

The Lowell also offers a newly renovated Fitness Center, exclusively available to in-house guests. Equipped with state-of-the-art cardio and strength-training equipment, the facility provides a private and sophisticated environment for exercise and wellbeing during your stay.
