FAQs

**Does Anantara Spa at Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas offer after-hours treatments?**

Yes, after-hours treatments can be arranged with an additional supplement.

**Are pets allowed at Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas?**

While we do love our furry friends, we are not equipped to deal with pets at our luxury resort.

**Does Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas offer an airport shuttle service?**

Yes, we do offer an airport transfer service for a fee. Please get in touch to confirm your details.

**How far is Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas from the airport?**

Our resort is approximately 35 minutes from the international airport by car, depending on traffic.

**Can Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas arrange scuba diving excursions?**

Scuba diving can be organised with our nearby preferred dive centre.

**How many villas are there at Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas?**

There are 30 villas in total, located either on the beach or the hilltop, with a unique perspective of the secluded coastline and sparkling Indian Ocean.

Ten Ocean View Pool Villas are situated on the hilltop offering panoramic views over the surrounding natural habitat.

Eight Premier Ocean View Pool Villas offer total privacy and holiday sanctuary exclusivity with sweeping island and ocean views.

Linked easily through garden walkways, the nine Premier Beach Pool Villas are ideal for families and enjoy direct access to the soft white sands of Anse Louis Beach.

Three Peninsula Ocean View Pool Villas are perfectly located on the edge of the peninsula, offering the experience of ultimate privacy and open views of the Indian Ocean.

All Premier and Peninsula villas enjoy exclusive added benefits for stays of three nights or more.

**What are the check-in and check-out times at Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas?**

Check-in at Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas is at 2.00 pm and check-out at 11.00 am. For your comfort, we highly recommend pre-booking guaranteed early check-in for early arrivals and guaranteed late check-out for late departures (rate advised at time of booking).

**Where is Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas located on Mahe Island?**

Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas is located on the West Coast Road, Anse Louis, Mahe Island, Seychelles.
