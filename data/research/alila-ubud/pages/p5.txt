## Alila Moments

Shaped by land and craft, by culture and care, Alila Moments reveal a different way of seeing. These personalized experiences offered at our Alila properties draw you closer to the world around you, and your place within it. These are our Moments, crafted just for you.

### A Journey Through Purification and Prayer

Immerse yourself in the spiritual traditions of Bali with a visit to the Brahmin compound of a Balinese high priest in the quiet surroundings of Ubud. The experience begins with the Puja Mantra ritual, a centuries-old ceremony that offers a rare moment of stillness and clarity. During the Melukat blessing, the priest pours blessed water over you—a simple yet powerful act meant to clear the mind and restore balance. A guided meditation follows, helping you reconnect with yourself in a calm, reflective space. Finally, you’ll receive a Tri Datu bracelet, a traditional symbol of protection, before ending the day at Pura Gunung Kawi Sebatu Temple. As mantras rise and water flows, something within shifts—quietly, profoundly and forever.

**The Journey Begins**

Depart from the Resort to explore the serene sanctuary of the Balinese high priest, Pedanda, in Ubud, a place of deep spiritual significance.

**Ceremonial Grounding**

Observe the ancient Puja Mantra ritual, a ceremony performed by the high priest and designed to bring peace, clarity and spiritual renewal to all present.

**Waters of Renewal**

Receive the Melukat blessing, a purification ritual believed to cleanse both mind and body, removing negative influences and recharging spiritual energy.

**Mediation for the Soul**

Engage in a guided meditation session tailored to your individual needs, helping you rebalance your body, center your chakras and re-energize your soul.

**Symbol of Protection**

A simple combination of three colors—red, white, black—weave together to create a Tri Datu, a symbol of protection from bad energy that’s worn around the wrist.

**A Path That Lasts a Lifetime**

Conclude your journey with visiting the Pura Gunung Kawi Sebatu Temple to gain further insight into the Balinese spiritual Journey.

**Duration:** 2 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Resort Wear, Balinese Dress will provided by Alila Ubud

**Additional Details:** Women having their menstrual cycle are not permitted to join this activity as we are visiting the Brahmin compound and the ceremony takes place in the house temple. We apologize for this inconvenience, but respect local practices.

**Price:** IDR 950,000++ per person

### Wheels Through Time: A Cycling Journey into Ubud’s Heart

Set out on a cycling journey that opens the window to the soul of Bali, where village life unfolds with quiet beauty. Starting after breakfast, you'll ride through rice terraces and morning markets, passing farmers at work and families beginning their day. With running commentary from your Leisure Concierge, each stop—whether at a temple, a bustling local market or a family compound—adds depth to your understanding of Balinese traditions. Share coffee and handmade snacks with locals, and learn how culture shapes daily life in ways both simple and profound. As you return through the serene landscape of stepped rice paddies, you may consider ways to integrate the Balinese lifestyle into your every day—practices that include a profound respect for nature and community.

**A Gentle Start**

Begin the gentle cycling tour with a scenic start from the resort, setting out on a journey through Alila Ubud's main entrance as the farmers and Balinese people begin their morning routines.

**Into the Green**

Continue cycling through the breathtaking rice terraces and hear fascinating commentary from the Leisure Concierge on the significance of local temples and the Balinese way of life, enriching the journey with cultural context.

**The Colors of the Land**

Stop at the traditional Payangan market to immerse in local culture. Explore vibrant stalls proffering spices and fresh fruits and engage with the local merchants.

**Life Along the Road**

Visit a local family compound, sharing traditional snacks and Balinese coffee while gaining insight into traditional Balinese agricultural practices and village life. The family will explain the significance of their traditional houses.

**Return in Rhythm**

Conclude the tour by taking in the stunning views of the surrounding greenery, capturing the essence of Ubud’s natural beauty on this unforgettable adventure.

**Duration:** 2.5 - 3 hours

**Recommended Dress**: Sportswear and trainers

**Additional Details:** This cycling trek will take guests through a paved path and rice fields. Some experience with cycling is required since the track is not always as flat as the main road.

**Price:** IDR 500,000++ per person

### Roots of Bali—A Day in the Life of a Balinese Farmer

Step into the rhythm of rural Bali with a guided walk through Alila Ubud’s emerald rice fields—a journey that reveals the island’s deep-rooted connection to land and tradition. Learn about the centuries-old Subak irrigation system, witness farmers at work, and try your hand at planting rice with a local guide. After a humble field-style breakfast, enjoy a nourishing Balinese lunch under the forest canopy, then unwind with a soothing foot massage at Spa Alila. More than a walk, it’s physical, joyful, and profoundly humbling—a moment that shifts your view of food, labor and what it means to be rooted.

**The Flow of Life**

Learn about the Subak irrigation system with our Leisure Concierge as you walk through the verdant rice fields and witness the morning activities of farmers.

**The Farmer's First Meal**

Inspired by the farmers’ morning breakfast in the rice fields, delight in a simple, yet flavorful Balinese breakfast before you begin the day in the rice fields.

**Planting With Purpose**

Learn the technique of planting, and all the stories and philosophies behind this ancient practice, from how to walk in the fields to how to optimize the planting of each grain.

**A Living Legacy**

Make something memorable as we invite you to plant a tree, a reminder that you were part of this commitment to the preservation of the rice fields here at Alila Ubud.

**A Meal of Reflection**

After a day of planting, relax at the picnic area as a Balinese lunch sets the stage for a moment under the forest canopy, served with a handpicked coconut from the farmer.

**A Restorative Ritual to Close the Day**

For the final step, indulge in an Alila spa treatment that’s inspired from the farmer's wife. It includes a foot massage to sooth your feet after a day of planting in the fields.

**Duration:** Half Day - 5 Hours

**Recommended Dress**: Resort or sport wear, as long as you are comfortable enough to walk through the rice fields

**Additional Details:** Balinese light breakfast and lunch included in a designated area. Guest with any open cuts on their feet are not recommended to join, as there will be barefoot walks in the muddy rice fields.

**Price:** IDR 1,450,000++ per person

### Made by Hand: Crafting Silver with a Balinese Artisan

Learn time-honored techniques and discover the quiet beauty of craft through fire, focus and form in a hands-on silver-making experience guided by local artisans. In this intimate workshop, you’ll design and shape your own silver ring, learning time-honored techniques that bring your piece to life. Each step—filing, shaping, polishing—connects you more deeply to the island’s rich creative heritage. A visit to a traditional silversmith factory reveals the artistry behind every handcrafted piece, offering inspiration and insight. By day’s end, you’ll leave not just with a beautiful ring, but with a lasting connection to Bali—one forged through creativity, tradition and the joy of making something truly your own.

**Meet the Maker**

Depart to the local silversmith and prepare to unleash your creativity in a traditional workshop, forging a unique silver piece under expert guidance—crafting the perfect keepsake or a meaningful gift.

**Craft in Action**

Experience the artistry of silver making with a tour of a local silversmith factory, witnessing the traditional craftsmanship and dedication involved.

**The Beauty of Creation**

Learn essential silver jewelry techniques from skilled artisans, ensuring your creation is both elegant and individual.

**Something to Cherish**

Leave with a distinctive piece of silver jewelry, a lasting memento of your creative journey and the beauty of Balinese craftsmanship.

**Duration:** 3 hours

**Recommended Dress**: Casual resort wear

**Additional Details:** Guests will meet the silversmith teacher and learn how to make the silver on their own.

**Price:** IDR 1,350,000++ per person
