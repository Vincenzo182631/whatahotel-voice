# Drink & Dine

Dine on the vivid flavors of authentic Balinese cuisine or savor Western farm-to-table tasting menus amidst towering coconut trees.

## Plantation Restaurant

Delight in the refined flavors of Plantation Restaurant, where curated Asian specialties celebrate seasonal ingredients, thoughtfully sourced from local farmers or hand-harvested from our organic garden. Embark on a journey through the Nusantara, from the rich traditions of our delectable Indonesian Rijstaffel to beloved Asian favorites such as the fragrant Thai Green Curry. Dine beneath an airy Balinese wantilan-style pavilion, framed by soaring coconut pillars and a traditional alang-alang roof, as the Ayung River valley provides a captivating backdrop to an unforgettable evening

## Cabana Lounge

An intimate social space for daytime relaxation turned sophisticated boîte come evening. The Cabana Lounge is an intimate social space for daytime relaxation, while mood lighting on the two-story-high Karangasem Terracotta Brick features wall that inspires an air of sophistication, perfect for enjoying evening cocktails. Guests can choose from a newly composed cocktail menu serving up an enviable selection of creations featuring cold-pressed juices, local ingredient infusions and playful presentations.
