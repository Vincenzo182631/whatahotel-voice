# Accommodations

Check-In3:00 PM

Overlooking the rolling hills of the Ayung River Valley, the 56 rooms and 18 Villas of Alila Ubud offer a secluded retreat, designed to resemble a Balinese village. Revel in individual garden terraces in all of the lower-floor rooms, and spacious balconies with jungle views in the high-floor rooms. At 2,584 square feet, the Two Bedroom Villa is the resort’s largest, with a private pool, outdoor tubs, and a separate dining area.
