## A Hillside Sanctuary Above the Ayung Valley

Perched above the sacred Ayung River, Alila Ubud offers a secluded hillside retreat surrounded by jungle canopy and calming stillness. Blending contemporary design with traditional Balinese architecture, the resort’s open-air terraces, tranquil courtyards and lush gardens invite a deep connection to nature—framed by uninterrupted views of the valley below.

## Accommodations

Overlooking the rolling hills of the Ayung River Valley, Alila Ubud’s 56 rooms and 18 villas offer a secluded retreat, designed to resemble a Balinese village. Revel in individual garden terraces in the lower-floor rooms and, on the higher floors, spacious balconies with jungle views. At 240 square metres, our Two Bedroom Villa is the resort’s largest and features a private pool, outdoor tubs and a separate dining area.

## Discover Alila Ubud

Innovative Sustainability

Our approach to sustainability is twofold: We seek to minimise our environmental impact while nurturing economic opportunity within the region. Alila Ubud takes great pride in recruiting most of our team members from neighboring banjars. We also purchase all our produce locally and procure most of our Alila Living products, including soaps and shampoos, from village co-ops supported by the resort. Sourcing locally not only benefits our surrounding communities economically; it also keeps transport-related greenhouse gas emissions to a minimum.

Outdoor Pool

Swim laps or lounge at our 25-metre infinity pool, nestled among the hotel property’s lush greenery and surrounded by luxurious sunbeds.

Green Scene

Explore the lush natural attractions of Bali’s central foothills, awash in peaceful shades of green. Discover the terraced rice fields of Tegalalang, trek through deep jungle to reach the summit of Mount Batur, and marvel at the 9th-century cave of Goa Gajah, a UNESCO World Heritage Site with a serene garden.

Bali’s Cultural Heart

Just 15 minutes from the resort stands Ubud, a city that has reigned as the center of Balinese arts and culture for more than a century. Soak up Ubud’s one-of-a-kind artistic energy when you visit artists’ workshops and galleries or browse notable Balinese paintings and sculptures at Museum Puri Lukisan, the island’s oldest art museum.

### Featured review

My stay at Alila Ubud was absolutely unforgettable. The hotel itself is stunning—completely surrounded by nature and the peaceful jungle of Ubud. My room was beautiful, always perfectly clean, and smelled fresh every day. One of my favorite parts was the outdoor bathtub and rainfall shower. Being able to shower or relax in the tub while surrounded by nature was such a special experience. Breakfast every morning was incredible. The food was amazing, but what truly made it special was the women working at breakfast service. I was traveling solo on my first solo trip, and they made me feel so welcomed. Some of them honestly felt like mothers to me. They would talk with me, remember my name, remember the meals I liked, and share stories so I wouldn’t feel alone while eating. That kindness made my experience so much more meaningful. The infinity pool overlooking the jungle was breathtaking. Swimming while looking out over the trees and nature was better than I could have ever expected. This stay exceeded everything I imagined. I will definitely be coming back.

Cris B,
