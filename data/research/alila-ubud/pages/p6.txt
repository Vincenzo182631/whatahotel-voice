### Beautiful jungle setting and lovely staff, but room for improvement

Surrounded by lush jungle and plenty of monkeys, which are very much part of the experience!

The staff are lovely, friendly and always willing to help, from reception to the restaurants.

The hotel is around 30 minutes from central Ubud, although the journey can take longer depending on traffic. The complimentary shuttle service is very convenient and makes getting into town easy.

That said, I would have expected a little more from the overall experience, particularly in terms of activities, such as complimentary daily yoga classes, and a wider variety of dining options.

A definite highlight was the breakfast, especially the coffee!

The rooms, however, felt a little basic, and the bed could certainly have been more comfortable. I think they would benefit from some updating and refreshing, while of course preserving the traditional style and charm that suit the beautiful surroundings.

Overall, a lovely stay in a spectacular setting, with wonderful staff, but a few improvements would really elevate the experience.
