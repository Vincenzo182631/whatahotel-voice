Experience the grandeur of a bygone era at The Biltmore Los Angeles. A unique blend of historical elegance and modern luxury, the hotel has counted presidents, dignitaries, members of royalty, and celebrities among its distinguished guests since 1923. With remarkable interiors steeped in history at every corner, experience for yourself a living legend that continues to inspire.

## INIMITABLE HERITAGE

The Biltmore is more than just a hotel; it’s a landmark that has witnessed the evolution of Los Angeles. From President John F. Kennedy’s campaign speech to Al Capone’s stay and hosting The Beatles at the height of Beatlemania, every corner of the hotel tells a story of its rich and illustrious past.

## INDULGENT STAYS

Whether the script calls for business or leisure, The Biltmore Los Angeles promises a truly memorable experience, elevated by bespoke service and personal touches. Luxuriate in marble baths stocked with premium deluxe bathroom amenities and sink into plush beds for a dreamy night’s sleep.

## ARCHITECTURAL JEWEL

A prime example of Beaux Arts architecture with Renaissance Revival touches, the hotel’s opulent interiors, which feature breathtaking frescoes by Italian American artist Giovanni Smeraldi, crystal chandeliers, and ornately carved marble fountains and columns, are a sight to behold.

## GLAMOROUS SPACES

Walk through the same gilded halls where the Academy Awards were conceived, and where Hollywood’s finest such as Charlie Chaplin and Clark Gable once frequented the hotel’s dance halls.

## CLUB LOUNGE

Upgrade your experience at The Biltmore Los Angeles with exclusive access to our elegant Club Lounge. The Club Lounge offers a tranquil space to relax, work, or socialize. Enjoy complimentary continental breakfast, all-day refreshments, and evening hors d'oeuvres in a sophisticated setting.

## PREMIER ADDRESS

The hotel is nestled in the heart of Los Angeles with excellent views of Pershing Square, not far from Hollywood and the historic core of downtown LA. Landmarks such as the newly opened US Bank Tower, which houses the highest observation deck in California, and the Los Angeles Public Library, are located nearby.

## MEETINGS AND EVENTS

Whether you’re looking to tie the knot in style or hosting a global conference The Biltmore Los Angeles’ amazing venues are poised to turn your dream event into reality. Five grand ballrooms, a dozen elegant meeting rooms, and flexible breakout spaces are complemented by first-class banqueting options and a dedicated events team.

## HEALTH AND WELLNESS

Immerse yourself in an oasis of rejuvenation at The Biltmore Los Angeles. A 24-hour fitness center and a classic indoor pool offer serene spaces for guests to invigorate and unwind.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the check-in/check-out time at The Biltmore Los Angeles?

Check-in time is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is at 11:00 AM. Guests may request early check-in or late check-out based on availability.

Does The Biltmore Los Angeles provide airport transportation?

We do not currently offer airport transfer or shuttle bus service.

How do I get to The Biltmore Los Angeles from the nearest Airport ?

The Biltmore Los Angeles is 18 miles from the Los Angeles International Airport.

Public shuttle (60 minutes)

Car (45 minutes)

Does The Biltmore Los Angeles provide currency exchange services?

We regret to inform you that The Biltmore Los Angeles does not provide currency exchange services on-site. However, our concierge team can assist you in locating nearby currency exchange facilities or ATMs for your convenience. Please feel free to reach out to them for assistance with any currency-related inquiries during your stay.

Does The Biltmore Los Angeles have on-site parking?

Self-parking is available for our hotel guests in our on-site parking garage for an additional $55 (inclusive of tax) with unlimited in & out privileges per night, per vehicle. Valet parking is also available for $71.50.

What type of room can I book at The Biltmore Los Angeles?

The Biltmore Los Angeles offers a unique variety of room types:

\- Deluxe Room: reflecting the unique heritage and history of our hotel in Los Angeles

\- Executive Room: Spacious rooms with features reflecting Hollywood history

\- Club Room: the upmost comfort and opulence, taking you back to that old Hollywood glamour

\- Studio Suite: spacious and classically furnished suites with a large living area

\- Millennium Suite: suite featuring a separate bedroom with a mock fireplace and living space with a large couch

\- Music Suite: unique experience with views of Pershing Square and a baby grand piano with an antique music stand to hold fancy dinners for up to 10

\- Presidential Suite: the last word in luxury that has hosted 9 US Presidents, includes a grand piano, formal dining room and spacious living room

\- Accessible Deluxe Room: reflecting the unique heritage and history of the hotel

\- Accessible Executive Room: spacious room including one of the most comfortable hotel beds

\- Accessible Club Room: the utmost in comfort and elegance designed with your needs in mind

What kind of Breakfast is served at The Biltmore Los Angeles?

Guests staying at the Biltmore Los Angeles can enjoy breakfast at Smeraldi's. For our guests on the go, we also offer carry-out dining options at our Rendezvous Court Café.

Does The Biltmore Los Angeles have a swimming pool?

The Biltmore Los Angeles has an indoor swimming pool open 6AM - 10PM daily.

The beautiful saltwater pool, inspired by the city of Pompeii, still features the original blue tiles and brass railings (reminiscent of a Turkish hammam) from the roaring twenties.

🎬 Our pool was featured in notable films including Cruel Intentions, Bugsy, The Fan, and Alias

Does The Biltmore Los Angeles have a Club Lounge?

Yes, the Biltmore Club Lounge is located on the 10th floor and is open to club level guests from 6:30 AM to 10:00 PM.

What is the pet policy at The Biltmore Hotel Los Angeles?

Pets up to 50 lbs are welcome. A non-refundable pet fee of $100 per stay per pet including emotional support pets. Maximum two pets per room. No other pets are permitted. For the safety and comfort of our staff and guests, pets may not be left unattended in your room. Fees would apply if damages are incurred.

Are there European power sockets in the room?

Please note that our rooms do not have European plug points/sockets. Guests can however reach out to the Reception for adapters if required.

Does The Biltmore Los Angeles have meeting room facilities?

The Biltmore Los Angeles offers 70,000 square feet (6,503 sq. m) of flexible meeting and event space, presenting an unmatched blend of luxurious suites, spectacular breakout rooms and five glamorous ballrooms. Guests can have your choice of the ideal setting for anything from private soirees and productive corporate sessions to high-level meetings or a perfect celebrity tea party in the heart of “City of Angels”.

What is the easiest way to get around LA from The Biltmore Hotel?

The Biltmore Los Angeles is set against the bold and bright backdrop of downtown Los Angeles where the art and cultural scene is booming. Whether traveling by foot or by car, key attractions aren’t too far away.
