### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is parking available at The Biltmore Mayfair?

Please note that parking spaces at our hotel are extremely limited. Complimentary parking is provided exclusively for guests who have booked our Signature Suites, subject to availability. For guests booking other room types, parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis and is subject to availability on the day at an additional charge. Please contact our concierge team for more information and assistance regarding parking arrangements prior to arrival: Concierge.Biltmore@biltmoremayfair.com.

What is the check-in/check-out time at The Biltmore Mayfair?

Check-in time is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is at 12:00 PM. Guests may request early check-in or late check-out based on availability.

Does The Biltmore Mayfair provide airport transportation?

Yes, we organize transportation services to and from the airport for our guests at an additional charge. Please contact our concierge team to arrange airport transfers in advance.

What are the distances from The Biltmore Mayfair to London's airports?

The Biltmore Mayfair is conveniently located for travel to all of London's major airports. The approximate distances are as follows:

- Heathrow Airport: Approximately 15 miles away.
- Gatwick Airport: Approximately 30 miles away.
- London City Airport: Approximately 9 miles away.
- Stansted Airport: Approximately 39 miles away.
- Luton Airport: Approximately 34 miles away.

Please note that travel times may vary depending on traffic conditions and mode of transportation. For assistance with airport transportation arrangements, please contact our concierge team.

Does The Biltmore Mayfair provide currency exchange services?

We regret to inform you that The Biltmore Mayfair does not provide currency exchange services on-site. However, our concierge team can assist you in locating nearby currency exchange facilities or ATMs for your convenience. Please feel free to reach out to them for assistance with any currency-related inquiries during your stay.

Do you allow pets at the hotel?

No, we do not allow pets in any area of the hotel. However, we are happy to welcome service animals.

Has The Biltmore Mayfair been recognized for excellence in its rooms or events?

Yes, we are proud to share that our hotel has won for Best Luxury Extended-Stay Hotel, Best Luxury Banquet/Event Hotel in United Kingdom and Best Luxury Hotel in Northern Europe at the 2025 World Luxury Hotel Awards.

Does your hotel’s dining and bar outlets received any notable awards?

Yes. Our culinary and bar experiences have earned distinguished international recognition.

Grill 88 has been awarded Best Luxury Grill in Europe, Best Wine Selection in Northern Europe, and Best Luxury Hotel Restaurant in Northern Europe, honouring its exceptional craftsmanship and premium dining standards.
