# The Biltmore Al Madinah Hotel

Set in an unrivalled location just steps from Al Masjid al Nabawi, The Biltmore Al Madinah Hotel offers an exceptional stay defined by elegance, comfort and attentive service. With inspiring Haram views, refined accommodations and thoughtfully curated experiences, the hotel welcomes guests seeking serenity, convenience and elevated hospitality in the holy city of Madinah.

## SPIRITUAL PROXIMITY WITH TIMELESS LUXURY

The Biltmore Al Madinah Hotel enjoys a prestigious position directly facing Al Masjid an Nabawi, offering guests immediate access to the Prophet’s Mosque, including proximity to the ladies’ entrance. Designed for comfort and ease, the hotel features efficient lift access on every floor, ensuring smooth movement for guests at all times.

With panoramic views, elegant interiors and personalized service, the hotel creates a refined sanctuary for pilgrims, families and business travelers alike, blending spiritual proximity with timeless luxury.

## A PREMIER ADDRESS

The Biltmore Al Madinah Hotel enjoys an unrivalled setting at the very heart of the holy city, just steps from Al Masjid an Nabawi. With direct proximity to the Prophet’s Mosque and its revered surroundings, the hotel places guests within moments of Madinah’s most significant spiritual landmarks, including Jannat Al Baqi and the historic sites that define the city’s rich Islamic heritage. From this exceptional address, guests are invited to experience refined luxury alongside profound cultural and spiritual connection.

## BESPOKE SERVICE

Our attentive and personalised service reflects a deep commitment to care, comfort and discretion, ensuring every guest’s needs are thoughtfully anticipated. From seamless assistance to meaningful local guidance, our team is dedicated to supporting a serene and enriching stay, allowing guests to focus on reflection, connection and peace of mind throughout their time in Madinah.

## TOP NOTCH AMENITIES

An exceptional range of premium facilities defines the experience at The Biltmore Al Madinah Hotel. Thoughtfully designed dining venues, graceful public spaces and dedicated fitness facilities come together to create a refined environment offering comfort, convenience and tranquility within the sacred heart of Madinah.

## MEETINGS AND EVENTS

Elegant and versatile event spaces at The Biltmore Al Madinah Hotel provide an ideal setting for meetings, conferences and special occasions. With flexible layouts accommodating up to 500 guests, each event is supported by attentive service in a refined environment at the heart of Madinah.
