## WHERE HERITAGE MEETS MODERNITY

Housed in a 32-floor glass skyscraper and fronted by the historic, Soviet-era IMELI building, The Biltmore Hotel Tbilisi pays homage to the heritage of its location, whilst injecting a breath of fresh air through contemporary design and modern amenities. Glittering interiors echo the nostalgia of a bygone era, resonating the glamour and prestige of the social elite.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is parking available at The Biltmore Tbilisi ?

Self-parking is available free of charge exclusively for our hotel guests in the underground parking area. Please note that valet service is currently unavailable.

What is the check-in/check-out time at The Biltmore Tbilisi ?

Check-in time is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is at 12:00 PM. Guests may request early check-in or late check-out based on availability.

Does The Biltmore Tbilisi provide airport transportation ?

Airport transfer services are available for an additional fee, with prior reservation and prepayment required. Please note that we do not currently offer shuttle bus service.

How do I get to The Biltmore Tbilisi from the nearest Airport ?

An alternative to a pre-ordered transfer or taxi is public bus number 337, which takes guests from the airport to Rustaveli Avenue. The bus stop is just 200 meters from the hotel, with a travel time of approximately 50 minutes.

Does The Biltmore Tbilisi provide currency exchange services ?

On-site currency exchange services are available at The Biltmore Tbilisi, along with a Tera Bank branch in the hotel lobby, operating 24/7.
