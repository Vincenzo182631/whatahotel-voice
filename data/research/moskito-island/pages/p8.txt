Set on Moskito Island in the British Virgin Islands, The Branson Beach Estate at Moskito Island is an ultra-private, multi-villa estate offering a fully exclusive-use Caribbean retreat. The experience is centred around space, privacy and elevated island living, with standalone villas connected by walkways and positioned to maximise sea views. A strong fit for high-end groups seeking a fully serviced private island stay, complete with access to a private beach cove and shared island facilities within a low-density setting.

##### Review

Moskito Island is very close to Necker Island, it is ultra-exclusive, and consists of very modern private estates. It has a more contemporary style, super luxury, with dramatic infinity pools, and secluded beaches. You can rent an entire estate for complete seclusion, and each one has a totally different style from the others. Also, every estate has its own dedicated team (such as private chefs and butlers). It’s perfect for those who want absolute privacy.

### Room    Categories

##### The Branson Beach Estate - Exclusive Use

Sleeping a total of 22 guests across 11 rooms, the impressive three-villa estate boasts breathtaking private sea views. Each elevated villa has an immense master suite and further guest rooms, most of which are standalone and connect by wooden walkways. Book the whole estate for exclusive use or enjoy a tailor-made villa experience with individual stays.

Base Occupancy:  2 Adults

Max Occupancy:
16 Adults

or 15 Adults

and 1 Child

##### Beach Villa

All of the rooms at Beach Villa are elevated and connected to the neighbouring Mangrove Villa by wooden walkways. The main villa features a spacious lounge area with TV and a fully equipped kitchen with two breakfast bars, a full-size fridge, separate temperature-controlled wine fridge and an additional mini-bar. Lunch or dinner can be served at the dining table, which comfortably seats 8, or dine al-fresco by the infinity pool. Beneath Gecko Villa is a further shaded dining area. Beach Villa is fully air conditioned with WiFi available throughout, and you can stream your own music wherever you are.

Base Occupancy:  6 Adults

Max Occupancy:
6 Adults

or 5 Adults

and 1 Child

##### Mangrove Villa

All of the rooms at Mangrove Villa are elevated and connected to the neighbouring Beach Villa by wooden walkways. On the ground floor of the main villa is a large lounge, fully-equipped kitchen, two breakfast bars and dining table, all looking out onto a deck which leads directly onto the infinity pool and hot tub. There’s also a full-size fridge, separate temperature-controlled wine fridge and additional drinks fridge in the kitchen. Lunch or dinner can be served inside in the dining area, which comfortably seats 8, or by the front of the infinity pool with views out to sea. Mangrove Villa is also fully air conditioned with WiFi available throughout, and you can stream your own music wherever you are.

Base Occupancy:  6 Adults

Max Occupancy:
6 Adults

or 5 Adults

##### Headland Villa

This exceptional villa is arranged with three bedrooms and the living space on the ground floor, whilst the Great Room can be found on the upper level. The shared living space includes a TV and kitchen, and looks out on to the wrap around infinity pool. The Great Room features a signature corner bar, dining table for up to 26 guests (as well as a dining table on the deck which seats up to 22 guests), games areas and a fully-equipped kitchen, designed for showcase meals, celebrations of every kind and parties to remember
