ENQUIRE ABOUT THIS PROPERTY

A private island resort in the British Virgin Islands, Peter Island blends restored luxury with a strong sense of natural seclusion. Accommodation is spread across beaches and hillside settings, supported by spa, dining and yacht access. The philosophy is flexible and guest-led, with space for both activity and complete relaxation. A strong fit for clients seeking private island exclusivity without over-formal structure.

##### Review

Just a short boat ride from Tortola, Peter Island is stunning! Largely undeveloped there are lots of secluded coves with a backdrop of lush verdant hills. Deadman’s Bay is a long sweeping beach, perfect for a relaxing afternoon. The Sunset Loop was a real highlight, the hotel set you up with wine and cheese on the clifftop while enjoying the gorgeous sunset views across the neighbouring islands!

### Room    Categories

##### Beachfront Rooms w/private outdoor Hot Tub

Peter Island’s eight new Beachfront Rooms offer 590 sq. ft. of luxury with private outdoor hot tubs, direct beach access, and sweeping bay views. Set in four single-level buildings with just two rooms each, they ensure privacy and tranquility. Each room features a spacious patio, elegantly designed bathroom with soaker tub, walk-in shower, and dual vanities. Located steps from the sand, the Resort Lodge, restaurant, and infinity pool, these rooms provide a serene beachfront escape with effortless access to the island’s amenities.

##### Beachfront Junior Suite

Each 650 sq. ft. Beach Front Junior Suite is located in one of five two-story buildings, with two suites upstairs and two downstairs. Private entrances offer a seamless transition from the vibrant outdoors to a tranquil retreat, and connecting doors are available for families or friends traveling together. Each suite includes a private patio or balcony overlooking lush tropical gardens and the soothing sounds of the sea. Interiors feature a king-size bed, seating area, and a luxurious bathroom with a walk-in wet room, soaker tub, two-person glass shower, and dual vanities.

##### Beachfront Rooms

Peter Island’s 20 newly introduced Beach Front Rooms offer 405 sq. ft. of comfort and style on the shores of Deadman’s Beach. Housed in five two-story buildings with four rooms each, they provide private balconies or patios surrounded by lush tropical gardens, palms, and sea grapes. Guests enjoy sweeping views of powder-white sand, tranquil waters, and sailboats in the bay. Bathrooms feature a soaker tub, walk-in glass shower, and dual vanities. Positioned directly on the beach and near the Resort Lodge, restaurant, and infinity pool, these rooms combine effortless beach access with comfort and convenience.

##### Beachfront 2-bedroom Villa w/private Outdoor HotTub

Tucked along the beach and surrounded by lush palms and tropical gardens, these two 1,350 sq. ft. villas offer sweeping views of the sand and bay. Each villa features an open floor plan connecting living and dining areas with a kitchenette, flanked by two bedrooms. The master suite includes a private deck, while the guest suite has a separate entrance. Both feature ensuite bathrooms with outdoor showers, soaker tubs, walk-in glass showers, dual vanities, and walk-in closets. A full-length deck leads to a private hot tub on the beach, with direct access to the sand and sea.

##### Hawks Nest 3-Bedroom Villa

Hawk’s Nest exemplifies Caribbean luxury with high ceilings, expansive windows, and airy interiors overlooking Deadman’s Bay and the yacht-filled harbor. The villa’s three spacious bedrooms feature two-person Jacuzzi baths, while additional poolside quarters provide two more bedrooms and baths, comfortably accommodating six to ten guests. Hawk’s Nest includes a fully equipped kitchen, wet bar, living and dining area with 52-inch TV, Wi-Fi, and a private sundeck with dipping pool. Located near the resort’s lobby, restaurant, and beach, the villa also offers an outdoor grill and optional staff, ensuring a seamless, unforgettable vacation experience.

##### Falcons Nest 6-Bedroom Villa

Perched on a mountain with sweeping views of White Bay Beach and Norman Island, this 22,000 sq. ft. villa estate offers unparalleled luxury and privacy. The gated property includes a main house and two pod buildings, featuring an infinity pool, tranquil waterfall, and interiors with indigenous stone, wooden beams, and hand-troweled stucco. Outdoor spaces include a wraparound veranda, cabana, wet bar, and zero-entry pool, all showcasing 180-degree vistas. The main villa offers two master suites, a two-story great room, two kitchens, dining and media rooms, spa, and fitness center. The pod villas each feature two master suites with private entrances, patios, and stunning views.

### Food &    Beverage

##### The Drake Steak

The Drake Steakhouse offers an exceptional dining experience, combining classic American steakhouse elegance with Caribbean flair. Guests begin the day with a continental breakfast on the terrace, complemented by à la carte options, followed by a Caribbean-inspired lunch by the pool. Evenings feature dinner under the moonlight, with an extensive wine list, cocktails, and premium steak and seafood selections.

##### The Drunken Pelican

Casual, oceanfront, open air restaurant in the Yacht Club. Enjoy small bites, artfully crafted cocktails, and a selection of beers, wines and spirits. Open for lunch and dinner daily.
