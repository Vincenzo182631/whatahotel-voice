Moskito Island

Reviewed by
Jordi Lippe-McGraw
Why book?
Michelin-worthy meals, palatial private properties, and a dedicated staff with make-your-dreams-come-true attitudes let you experience a billionaire’s vacation.
Set the scene
It’s not unusual to be lying in a sun chair in a private infinity pool, overlooking the crystal blue Caribbean, as a staff member approaches you with a floating glass of Champagne you didn’t order. Meanwhile, a private chef is preparing a Michelin-worthy meal in your private kitchen, and a house manager is prepping another bottle of Champagne and chocolate-covered strawberries to be delivered to your room after dinner. The only guests in sight are those in your immediate party—that is, unless you decide to venture to the communal Manchioneel Beach (complete with oceanfront loungers, a floating trampoline, and bartenders at the ready), or the Beach House, a community recreational area where you can play a round of tennis and mingle with estate owners (including
Richard Branson
himself).
The backstory
While everyone knows about billionaire Richard Branson’s original private retreat, Necker Island, the Virgin founder purchased another exclusive playground just two miles away called Moskito Island. But instead of using it for himself and his high-powered pals, the mogul built a home and sold nine other plots for others to build their dream beach houses. While most are only inhabited by the estate owners themselves, three of the remarkable properties—The Branson Estate, The Oasis Estate, and The Point Estate—became available for rent in October 2021.
The rooms
Since Moskito Island comprises private estate rentals versus a single hotel, there’s a variety of architectural styles to choose from. The Branson Estate is the epitome of barefoot luxury, designed with extended family vacations in mind. It features two villas with private pools, outdoor bathrooms, and the main two-story Headland House, connected by elevated wooden walkways. Meanwhile, the nine-room, all-glass Oasis Estate, perched on the island's highest point, is the ultimate James Bond–esque party pad. Nearly every glass wall opens to the Caribbean breezes, and guests can take advantage of the multiple bars (including a swim-up one), a movie and billiards room, and a rooftop with 360-degree views. But the crown jewel has to be the 812-square-foot master guest room surrounded by glass. Lastly, the eight-bedroom Point Estate is another family-focused pad with a travel-inspired design (for example, a map-like sculpture on the ceiling). Adults will love the infinity pool, while kids won’t be able to get enough of the cozy bunk bed room with a partial glass ceiling revealing the pool above. It’s also the only property with direct access to the communal Manchioneel Beach.
Food and drink
One of the incredible things about Moskito is that there is no set menu. Instead, the house manager will reach out to learn about guests’ food preferences before arrival. From there, a chef will create a personalized menu for all three meals. Want Italian one night and Mexican another? They’ve got you covered. Interested in a healthy retreat? The dedicated chef will whip up a vegan feast. The culinary team has even been known to fly in specific ingredients to suit a guest’s taste. And one thing is for sure: Booze is always on hand, and Champagne is always flowing.
The neighborhood/area
Moskito is situated in the heart of the British Virgin Islands. And while there’s plenty to keep you busy on the island itself, the team is happy to arrange a snorkeling excursion along the shores of Peter Island, lunch at the famous
Soggy Dollar Beach Club
, or a visit to the legendary floating bar, Willy T.
The service
While, yes, guests experience the best linens, views, food, etc., it’s the service that makes Moskito stand out. Each estate has its own house manager whose job it is to ensure each guest’s needs are satisfied. They will ensure someone is available to make you a latte at 6 a.m. if you want and will arrange for surprises throughout the stay, like a family-style dinner set up in the infinity pool (yes, really). And should you need something in the middle of the night, there’s a phone in every room that connects to someone ready to help at all hours.
For families
Families will particularly love Moskito Island, as a full buyout is a prerequisite. That means total privacy, no bothering other island guests if little ones want to play in the pool at 7 a.m., and a staff dedicated to pampering even the youngest travelers. Movie nights, s’mores, and scavenger hunts are all possible to keep kids entertained, and babysitting services are available should the adults want some downtime.
Anything left to mention?
Be sure to check out the grass tennis courts, complete with stadium seating. (You can even set up a mini-tournament with staff and other guests.) Plus, the private hiking trails on the island have some of the best views of the Caribbean—and nearby Necker Island—you’ll ever see.
