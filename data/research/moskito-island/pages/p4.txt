ENQUIRE ABOUT THIS PROPERTY

Set in the British Virgin Islands, Necker Island is a 74-acre private island retreat offering an ultra-exclusive, all-inclusive Caribbean experience. The stay is highly private and experience-led, with accommodation available for sole-use groups or selected individual booking weeks. A standout option for clients seeking total seclusion, with a strong focus on shared island living, curated activities and a fully serviced, private-island format.

##### Review

For me Necker Island was an experience I'll never forget! It is a perfect mix of ultra-luxury and a rustic relaxed vibe. The Bali Rooms are so unique and original: you will literally sleep surrounded by nature. ##### Great House Master Suite

Located on the top floor of the Great House, this spacious suite boasts 180-degree ocean views from three private terraces. Guests can unwind in the outdoor hot tub or enjoy both indoor and outdoor bathing options, with a bathtub, outdoor shower, and a large en-suite bathroom.
The suite is equipped with a TV, kitchenette, and integrated indoor and outdoor sound system for added comfort and convenience. Accommodation includes a king-size bed, a double pull-out sofa bed, and a single sofa bed, making it an ideal choice for couples, families, or small groups.
