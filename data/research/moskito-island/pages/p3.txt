# The villas

## Bliss, Bali-style

Spread across 1,765 square metres (19,000 square feet), the estate features Headland House, Mangrove Villa and Beach Villa, offering a total of 11 bedrooms for up to 22 guests.

**Headland House**

Perched on an outcrop at the northeast tip of Moskito Island, this luxurious villa sits at the very heart of The Branson Beach Estate.

- Sleeps up to six guests
- One master suite and two double bedrooms
- Sea views from all bedrooms
- Wraparound infinity pool

**Beach Villa**

Beach Villa takes up an enviable sheltered position just above a sandy beach with views out to the Caribbean Sea and up to Headland House.

- Sleeps up to eight guests
- One master suite and three standalone bedrooms
- Infinity pool
- Direct access to private sandy beach

**Mangrove Villa**

Nestled among mangrove trees and connected to Beach Villa and Headland House via wooden walkways, Mangrove Villa faces east towards Necker Island and the island of Virgin Gorda.

- Sleeps up to eight guests
- One master suite and three standalone guest houses
- Infinity pool
- Sea views and views out to Necker Island
