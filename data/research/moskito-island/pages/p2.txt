# Discover the estate

## Unspoiled seclusion

Surrounded by turquoise Caribbean waters and commanding spectacular views, The Branson Beach Estate offers the perfect balance of that ‘home away from home’ feeling combined with areas that seamlessly blend indoor and outdoor living, dining and play areas.

## Staying at The Branson Beach Estate

The estate’s three villas - Headland House, Mangrove Villa and Beach Villa - sleep 6, 8 and 8 guests in 3, 4 and 4 bedrooms respectively, or a total of 22 guests across all 11 bedrooms. They can be booked individually, or all three together for exclusive use, all year round.
