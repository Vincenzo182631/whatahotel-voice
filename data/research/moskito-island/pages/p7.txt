Nestled in a secluded corner of the British Virgin Islands, Rosewood Little Dix Bay is a coastal retreat that blends effortlessly with its natural surroundings. Sheltered by verdant mountain peaks and framed by a crescent-shaped bay, it offers guests a serene escape in complete harmony with nature.Visitors can spend their days hiking to panoramic lookouts, relaxing beneath a sun shelter by the bay, or swimming among turtles in the calm reef shallows. Evenings are spent gathered fireside at the farm, where island stories passed down through generations bring the spirit of the Caribbean to life.

### Food &    Beverage

##### Sugar Mill

Sugar Mill offers vibrant Caribbean cuisine in an authentic beachfront setting. Guests can enjoy beautifully prepared small plates, expertly paired with craft cocktails, refreshing sangrias, and a curated selection of exclusive wines, creating a dining experience that celebrates the flavors of the islands.

##### Pavilion

Pavilion celebrates the culinary heritage of the British Virgin Islands, reflecting centuries of maritime trade that have shaped the region’s flavors. Guests can enjoy shared plates and slow-cooked comfort dishes made with the freshest local ingredients, including premium meats, seafood, and island-grown produce.

Blending Caribbean fine dining with a sense of place, Pavilion offers an immersive experience where every dish highlights the history, culture, and bounty of the islands, creating a memorable dining journey for all.
