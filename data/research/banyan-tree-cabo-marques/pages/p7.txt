# In-Villa Barbecue

## Taste the mastery of our barbeque chef in your private villa

Enjoy a delicious barbecue in your villa, where your very own personal chef will pamper you from beginning to end. More than just a dinner, this is an experience not to be missed.

As the ultimate expression of private luxury, our bespoke Acapulco in-villa barbecue service transforms your personal timber terrace into an exclusive, open-air restaurant. Without leaving the comfort of your infinity pool, you are in for a show before a sizzling grill with a personal chef who carefully prepares premium cuts of meat and fresh catches of the day.

The menu is tailored beforehand to match your exact palate preferences, accompanied by artisanal sides and regional pairings curated by our sommelier. It represents a deeply intimate culinary journey that allows you to dine under the stars completely free from the distractions of public spaces.
