# Oceanfront Pool Villa - KING

## Cliffside Oceanfront Serenity in Acapulco

Perched above the Pacific Ocean in Cabo Marqués, Acapulco, this luxury oceanfront pool villa offers immersive, front-row sea views from a dramatic cliffside setting. Designed for couples seeking exclusivity, it features a king-size bed oriented toward the horizon, expansive verandas, and a private heated infinity pool seemingly suspended above the water.

The spa-inspired bathroom enhances the sense of serenity. An elevated oceanfront retreat defined by privacy, design, and breathtaking Pacific panoramas.
