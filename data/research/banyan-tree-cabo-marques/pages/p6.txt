# DINING

### Banyan Tree Cabo Marqués

## SAVOUR AUTHENTIC THAI FLAVOURS AND DIVERSE CULINARY WONDERS

Banyan Tree Cabo Marques Resort features a thoughtfully curated selection of restaurants and bars that promise an unforgettable gastronomic journey. Enjoy three signature restaurants, a stylish bar, private in-villa dining, and our iconic Destination Dining experiences—all designed to satisfy the most discerning palates.

At the heart of our culinary offering is Saffron, our acclaimed Thai restaurant, where authentic recipes and aromatic ingredients come together in a setting of extraordinary natural beauty. For guests seeking European flavours, Cello presents the essence of Northern Italian cuisine, with seasonal dishes and traditional techniques served in a warm, welcoming atmosphere.

Whether you're craving authentic Thai dishes, fusion and seafood specialties, or spicy Mexican favourites, we cater to every palate with unmatched finesse.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

Expand All

##### How can I make restaurant reservations in advance

Guests may secure restaurant reservations ahead of their stay by contacting their Resort Host at GSC-Cab@banyantree.com

##### Do you welcome external guests at your restaurants and bars?

Yes. We warmly welcome external guests to enjoy our restaurants and bars. To ensure the best experience, we kindly recommend making a reservation in advance through OpenTable for any of the following venues:

• Saffron

• Cello

##### Is there a dress code for the restaurants and bars?

Yes. Banyan Tree Cabo Marqués has a dress code policy for its restaurants and bars:

- During the day, at Cello, Las Rocas Bar & Grill, and Las Vistas Bar & Lounge, resort casual attire is recommended. This includes comfortable and relaxed clothing appropriate for daytime dining or drinks.
- In the evening, at Saffron and Cello, elegant casual attire is suggested.

##### Can you accommodate guests with food allergies or specific dining requirements?

Yes, please inform our staff of any allergies or dietary restrictions when making your reservation or upon arrival.

##### Is breakfast provided as part of the villa accommodation rate?

Depending on your booking package, breakfast may be included. Please check your booking confirmation for details.

##### Do you offer private dining experiences?

Yes, we offer a curated selection of **private dining experiences** designed to create unforgettable and personalized moments.

- **In-villa BBQs** – Enjoy a private barbecue prepared especially for you in the comfort of your villa, ideal for a relaxed yet exclusive dining experience.

- **Destination dining experiences** – Romantic options such as **“Candle and Romance” by the Reflections Pool** provide an intimate setting with a beautifully arranged table, refined cuisine, and a magical atmosphere.

- **Floating breakfast in-villa** – Begin your morning with a touch of luxury as a floating breakfast is delivered to your villa’s private pool. A beautifully arranged tray, adorned with vibrant colors and exquisite details, allows you to enjoy a serene and indulgent start to the day.

\

The **destination dining** and **floating breakfast** experiences are especially ideal for **couples**, offering personalized culinary moments in exceptional and romantic settings.

##### Is in-villa dining available at the resort?

**Yes, in-villa dining is available 24 hours a day for your convenience (hours of service are subject to change).** Guests may enjoy a variety of dishes in the privacy and comfort of their villa at any time, whether for breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a late-night meal.

##### Are pets allowed in the restaurants?

Yes. Pets are welcome in our restaurants that offer terrace seating.

##### Does the resort offer non-smoking areas in the restaurants?

Smoking is not allowed in any of the hotel's facilities. We appreciate your understanding.
