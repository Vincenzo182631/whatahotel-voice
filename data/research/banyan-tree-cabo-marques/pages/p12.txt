# Resort Activities

Make the most out of your resort stay with special experiences for the whole family. Pamper yourself with specially crafted bath relaxation rituals. Spoil your kids with a tepee within your own villa - cookies and milk included. Try Mexico’s finest tequila and mezcal as you learn about its history, or discover your culinary talents in our cooking classes.

## Tequila and Mezcal Tasting

Try Mexico's finest tequila and mezcal and learn about its history, manufacturing, ageing and flavours in this one-of-a-kind experience.

## Master Cooking Classes

Spend time with our celebrated chef and take a tour through the intricacies of each region's diverse spices. Learn how to best unleash the flavours of each dish.

### Birthday Surprise

The perfect set up for a birthday celebration!

### Revitalize Your Senses

Soak in the frangrant infusions of fruits as you taste delicious chocolate truffles. Luxuriate in the warmth of the sun while discovering a stimulating sense of wellness.

### Glamping for Kids

The perfect set up for a birthday celebration!

### Pampering Day

An excellent present for a mom or a bride-to-be. Soak in a hot tub with rose petals, and delight your senses with unique essences and a taste of delicious macaroons.

### "Sun Salutation" Yoga Session

Energize your body, mind and spirit in a sequence of yoga postures performed as one continuous exercise. The programme starts off slow and builds up to a fast flow. This graceful sequence is also known as "Sun Salutation Yoga". Available for small groups. Indoors and outdoor options available.

### In-Villa BBQ

Let our chef cook up a barbeque in the comfort of your villa with an excellent menu of your choice.
