## OUR SPA MENU

Discover a curated menu of Asian-inspired therapies enhanced with Mexican elements. Each treatment is designed to restore harmony, nurture the body, and awaken deep relaxation.

SPA & WELLBEING MENU Book now

### Full Body Massages

From soothing Balinese to deep tissue therapies, choose from an indulgent selection of massages. Guided by skilled therapists, each treatment melts away tension and nurtures both body and mind.

### Body Treatments

Nourish your skin with scrubs and conditioners infused with natural elements. Enjoy healing rituals like the Aloe Lavender Healer, Honey Avocado Healer, Ginger Lemon Cleanser, or Turmeric Honey Cleanser.

### Banyan Signatures

Experience Thai and Balinese massages enhanced with Mexican ingredients like honey, cacao, and lavender. Rooted in ancient traditions, each treatment restores balance and grounds you in the richness of nature.

## Wellbeing Activities

Our curated seasonal experiences awaken something deeper within, allowing you to disconnect from the noise and reconnect with your inner being.

### Sunrise Flow Yoga

Awaken your body and mind through guided yoga at daybreak. These mindful sessions help you move with ease, find balance, and set a positive tone for the day ahead.

### Cacao Ceremony

A sacred monthly ritual honouring ancestral traditions. Pure cacao guides participants to connect inward, foster healing, and embrace a profound sense of community and spiritual renewal

### Aromatherapy Workshop

Design your own pouch with seeds and essential oils. A creative ritual of intention that encourages slowing down, breathing deeply, and letting go of daily distractions.
