## A World of Experiences, Within and Beyond

Set along Acapulco’s dramatic coastline, Banyan Tree Cabo Marqués offers a world of curated experiences for connection and discovery. From intimate moments within the resort to adventures beyond, including golf and iconic sights like La Quebrada, each stay is enriched by seasonal experiences, from our Thai Festival to renowned tennis and padel events across the destination.
