# Facilities

## Banyan Tree Cabo Marqués

## A Feature-Rich Destination

Banyan Tree Cabo Marqués features a wide range of luxurious facilities and amenities designed for an unforgettable experience. From an infinity pool that presents stunning sunset views to a private golf course near the grounds, our resort in Acapulco offers the best of resort lifestyle. Whatever the occasion or event, we are committed to making your stay with us truly memorable.

### Reflections Pool

Our infinity swimming pool showcases the best sunsets of Acapulco, and is the ideal spot to sunbathe while enjoying light snacks.

### Las Rocas Infinity Pool

Take a dip in our infinity pool, set in an intimate corner of Cabo Marqués along the coastline, where you can enjoy fresh seafood and prime cuts prepared on the grill, accompanied by the sound of waves breaking on the rocks.

### Meeting Rooms

Cabo Marques has two well-equipped rooms and three outdoor venues for business meetings and corporate functions. Visit our Meetings page to find out more.

### Restaurants and Bars

Banyan Tree Cabo Marqués offers a fantastic array of superb dining experiences. Visit our Dining page to find out more.

### Gym

Embrace a healthy lifestyle in our gym, equipped with modern fitness amenities and surrounded by views of nature to inspire your workout.

### Banyan Tree Spa

Subtle scents of herbs, spices and aromatic oils entice you the moment you step through the door of our award-winning Banyan Tree Spa. A dedicated Spa Concierge leads you to a tranquil private therapy pavilion where you can sip herbal tea while being treated to a soothing foot bath. Banyan Tree Spa is an intimate retreat that blends romance, relaxation and exotic refinement. Enter a true Sanctuary for the Senses and experience the essence of Asian wellness philosophy.

### Banyan Tree Gallery

Take little piece of Banyan Tree home with you. Browse through our delightful collection of craft items, casual wear and exclusive spa products to relive your Banyan Tree experience in the privacy of your own home.
