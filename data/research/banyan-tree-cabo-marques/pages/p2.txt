#### Frequently Asked Questions

Expand All

##### Are villas equipped with a private pool, and is heating available upon request?

Yes, all our villas include a private pool, maintained at a comfortable temperature, with heating available upon request.

##### Are children welcome at the resort?

Yes. Children are welcome as long as they are accompanied by an adult at all times. Please note that the resort does not offer dedicated children’s facilities.

##### Are baby cots available at the resort?

Yes. Baby cots are available **upon request and subject to availability**, free of charge. Please let our team know in advance by contacting: GSC-Cab@banyantree.com

##### Are pets allowed in the resort?

Yes, Banyan Tree Cabo Marqués welcomes dogs and cats. One pet is permitted per villa, while additional pets are subject to prior approval. A fee of USD 80 per pet, per stay applies and includes a pet bed and water bowls. During check-in, the resort’s pet policy will be shared with you for your review and acceptance.

For more information, please contact: GSC-Cab@banyantree.com

##### Are there any villas suited for families?

Yes. We offer two spacious, family-friendly two-bedroom pool villas designed for comfort, privacy, and unforgettable ocean views.

Our **Ocean View Two-Bedroom Family Pool Villa – King + Twin** is ideal for families with children, featuring two separate pavilions with a king-size bed in one bedroom and twin beds in the other, a shared sundeck, and a heated private pool overlooking the Pacific Ocean.

We also offer the **Ocean View Two-Bedroom Pool Villa – Queen + King**, perfect for families or groups of up to four guests. This villa features two elegantly designed pavilions with a king-size bed and a queen-size bed, a shared sundeck, and a heated private pool, all set above natural boulders with panoramic ocean views.

Both villas provide generous space, privacy, and a luxurious setting ideal for families seeking comfort, flexibility, and breathtaking scenery.

##### Do you allow smoking in the villas or resort facilities?

No. We do not offer smoking villas, and smoking is not permitted in any resort facilities. We appreciate your understanding.

##### How can I recover an item I may have left at the hotel?

Yes. Please contact us at GSC-Cab@banyantree.com, and we will make every effort to locate and return your item.

##### Early Check-in & Late Check-out

1. All guests arriving before 3:00 PM will be accommodated based on Villa availability. Subject to additional charge.
2. Late check-out from 12:00 pm to 6:00 pm will be charged 50% of the Best Available Rate of the day.
3. Late check-out from 6:00 pm will be charged 100% of the Best Available Rate of the day.

##### What is the Cancellation Policy?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival and 1 night of confirmed accommodation charges after that.
