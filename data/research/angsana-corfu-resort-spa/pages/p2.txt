# Accommodation

## Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa

## Far-reaching sea views, designed to delight the senses

Morning light spills across the Ionian Sea, green hills rise beyond the windows, and the quiet beauty of Corfu settles into every room. Choose from 159 elegant rooms and suites or 37 private pool villas, each combining generous space with Greek philoxenia and the intuitive warmth of Asian hospitality.

Woodland retreats look towards the hills, while sea-facing rooms, and suites open onto sweeping Mediterranean panoramas. For greater privacy, villas bring together Corfiot residential character, garden, and pools beneath the open sky.

##### What are the benefits of booking directly?

All guests who book directly through our website benefit from the best available offers and enjoy the following complimentary amenities:

- Welcome drink & local sweets upon arrival
- Use of the fitness club, including hammam, jacuzzi, sauna and indoor pool
- Participation in group training programs, subject to availability (registration required at check-in)
- Daily transfer to and from the Emerald Beach Club, during the period of operation
- Participation in Daily Eclectic Experiences, subject to availability (reservation required at check-in).
- Free Wi-Fi access throughout the resort
- Complimentary parking facilities

Guests should also consider joining our recognition programme withBanyan to enjoy exclusive rewards when booking directly.

##### Early arrival / late departure / early departure policy

- Check-in time: 3:00 pm • Check-out time: 11:00 am.
- Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and upon request.
- Late check-out from 11:00 am to 6:00 pm will be charged 50% of the Best Available Rate of the day. Late check-out after 6:00 pm will be charged 100% of the Best Available Rate of the day.
- **If you choose to depart earlier than your confirmed departure date, the total amount for all booked room nights will be charged. Any remaining nights will be released for resale.**
- In the event of a no show the total rate of the confirmed room nights for the entire length of stay shall be chargeable. Reservation for subsequent nights will be released for resale.

##### What services are included when booking a villa?

- Exclusive access to the V-Lounge
- Dedicated breakfast area for villa guests\*
- Dedicated Eclectic Villa Living team to curate unique experiences for you
- Complimentary participation in group training classes or in-house experiences such as yoga, meditation and more\*

\\* During certain periods, the dedicated breakfast area and in-house experiences may not be available.

##### Are there tea and coffee making facilities in the rooms?

Yes, each room is equipped with a kettle and a Nespresso coffee machine.

##### Are there any facilities for the children and is there a baby sitting service?

Rangers’ Club is a special space for our young visitors, dedicated for ages 4 to 11 years old. The kids club features its own swimming pool, sandbox and games.

Additionally, there is a baby sitting service upon request and availability. Please contact us for more details.

##### What is the maximum room capacity?

The maximum capacity is indicated on the page of each room type. Please note that the number of persons is for adults or children and cannot be exceeded. If in doubt, don't hesitate to contact us and we will get back to you with the best available option and price.

##### What is the Pet Policy?

01. Please note that only dogs and cats are allowed in the Hotel.
02. Pets must comply with local law requirements. The import certificate for the animal’s health must be presented upon arrival. If this document is not presented, the hotel is entitled to prohibit your access and cancellation will apply.
03. Pets allowed must be up to 10 kg.
04. Only one pet will be allowed per room. For more than one, approval will be needed.
05. Your pet must always be controlled.
06. The pet must remain on a collar and leash while transiting public areas of the hotel (the only exception is when inside the room). Pets are not allowed access to restaurants, bars, spa, swimming pool, gym or other areas that our collaborators indicate at the time (excluding guide dogs).
07. Please inform the reception when you have plans to leave the property and leave your pet alone in the room. If you require a caregiver or any special services for your pet, please contact the resort host in advance for appropriate arrangements.
08. Pets must not swim or bathe in private pools, tubs, plunge pools or the beach.
09. The pet must be trained to make their needs outside. We sincerely request that you use special bags to clean your pet's needs in established areas.
10. If your pet gets restless and/or barks, we kindly request that you take control to avoid interrupting the tranquillity of other guests.
11. When an associate of the Housekeeping cleans the room, we carefully request that you be present, put the pet in your conveyor box, or take the pet for a walk around the hotel.
12. You need to be present to take care of your pet while a member of our team of collaborators is providing service to your room.
13. Guests will be responsible for any damage caused to the property and/or for injuries to the hotel's collaborators by the actions of their pet.
14. Guests must agree to indemnify and de-armor the Resort, its owners and operators from all legal liability and damages suffered that were caused by their pet.
15. Angsana Corfu reserves the right to charge to the guest's account the full cost for any damages caused by their pet during their stay at Angsana Corfu.
16. Angsana Corfu is surrounded by a diversity of wildlife. The company is not responsible in the case the pet contracts an illness, suffers an accident or dies.
17. If during your stay, your pet requires medical attention or shampoo, haircut or other services, feel free to contact our Resort Hosts who will be able to locate the best options in Corfu to meet your requirements.
18. Pet fee is 100€ for the disinfection of the room, during your stay.
