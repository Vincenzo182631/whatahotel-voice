# Experiences

## Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa

## Discover Corfu’s Cultural Offerings and Activities

Corfu unfolds through olive-clad hills, historic towns, and a coastline scattered with quiet bays. Beyond the resort’s olive oil tastings, guided hikes, yoga and creative workshops, guests can follow the island towards cultural landmarks, mountain villages and some of the most beautiful beaches in Corfu, Greece.

Find things to do on Corfu Island for every rhythm, including family adventures, sea kayaking, private yacht journeys and day trips arranged by the resort team. Each experience opens another path into the landscapes, flavours and stories of the Ionian Sea.
