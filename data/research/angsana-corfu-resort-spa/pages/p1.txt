# Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa

> #### Corfu, Greece

## Dive into Corfu’s eclectic spirit

Perched on a verdant hillside above the crystal-clear Ionian Sea, Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa is Banyan Group’s first property in Europe and an idyllic five-star retreat in Greece. Esteemed among a selection of Corfu hotels, it pairs authentic Greek hospitality with Asian influences across elegant rooms, spacious suites, and Mediterranean villas with private pools.

A sweeping infinity pool, seafront dining, a private beach, completed by a restorative spa experiences set the rhythm for days beside the water. Among luxury hotels in Corfu, Angsana embraces the island through sea views, warm hospitality, and moments that linger long after the journey ends.

## Dazzling spaces and panoramas that capture the beauty of Corfu

The breathtaking 1,000 sqm infinity pool, honoured with the prestigious Prix Villegiature 2022 for the Best Hotel Pool in the World, is a standout feature among luxury hotels in Corfu. This captivating and Instagram-worthy spot seamlessly blends with the sea, creating the illusion of swimming all the way to the horizon. For those seeking utmost privacy, Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa presents an exclusive collection of 37 private pool villas, where guests can enjoy romantic dinners under the stars, rejuvenating in-villa massages or enhance their stay with our eclectic villa experiences.

## A meeting of timeless glamour and rich heritage

A destination rich in intrigue and allure, Corfu has captivated conquerors, royalty and writers alike. It is home to a plethora of historical sites including the Old Town of Corfu, the majestic Achilleion Palace and the ancient ruins of the Temple of Artemis. These landmarks offer visitors a glimpse into the island's fascinating past. Angsana Corfu is a premier luxury resort in a stunning Mediterranean location, offering easy access to the island's must-see attractions, sandy beaches and Venetian fortresses. The resort is also conveniently located near Corfu International Airport, with direct flights from Dubai, Tel Aviv, other Greek islands and numerous European cities.

##### Are there any nearby attractions or landmarks that guests can explore during their stay at Angsana Corfu?

Corfu is home to several historic sites, including the UNESCO-recognised Old Town of Corfu, the majestic Achilleion Palace, and the ancient ruins of the Temple of Artemis. All are located just a few minutes' drive from the resort. To find interesting things to do in Corfu, check out this guide we have prepared: https://www.angsana.com/greece/corfu/experiences/101-things-to-do

##### What is the best way to get to Angsana Corfu from the nearest airport?

The best way to get to Angsana Corfu from the nearest airport (CFU) is to arrange your transfer with us directly. We will organize a private taxi or minivan to pick you up from the airport or the harbour. If you have your own vehicle, kindly contact us and we will be more than happy to advise you on how to get to the hotel.

##### Does Angsana Corfu offer shuttle service to and from the nearest airport or harbour?

Our team can arrange a paid shuttle service from the airport or harbour to the hotel, while some of our offers may include complimentary airport transfer. For advance bookings, please contact us at +30 26614 42110.

##### Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Angsana Corfu offers accommodation with and without breakfast, depending on guests' preference during the booking. An additional supplement for the breakfast buffet costs 30€ per person per day and can be paid at the property.
