# Spa & Wellbeing

## Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa

## Restorative Spa Treatments Fusing Timeless Asian Rituals and Natural Ionian Elixirs

Our Angsana Corfu Spa treatments fuse time-honoured rituals from Asia with organic, locally sourced ingredients such as flowers, herbs and fruits, some of which are grown in the hotel’s rooftop garden. Each treatment is tailored by our expert therapists to your needs, preferences and mood, ensuring every experience is unique and personal. Treatment rooms offer exceptional views, with four featuring exclusive steam rooms and baths for guests to enjoy before or after their treatments.

Indulge in our unique Rain Mist massage, inspired by waterfall treatments in Thailand – an invigorating rain shower combined with moisturising body treatments that awaken your senses and hydrate your skin. Try our signature Thai massage, which uses delicate stretching, palming and thumbing techniques improve flexibility and soothe tired muscles.

For a truly transformative experience, our signature Angsana Senses massage works on your body’s key pressure points to strengthen inner "qui" or energy, using Euphoria massage oil to promote concentration and rejuvenation. Every massage and treatment begins with a restorative foot rub.

## An Authentic Asian Spa Experience

An award-winning Mediterranean sanctuary that blends the warmth and charm of Greek philoxenia with the traditions and techniques of Asian wellbeing, providing an experience to delight all your senses. A fusion of approaches connects East and West through calming treatments that promise unparalleled relaxation, with a focus on organic, natural ingredients.

## A Complete Fitness Studio

Meet your fitness goals and make the most of your island escape in our state-of-the-art gym, 25-metre indoor heated pool and yoga deck. Find the perfect place to focus on your personal wellbeing with our complimentary classes that are available for you to discover in our gym, where you can truly immerse yourself in the Angsana Corfu experience.

## Modernised Beauty & Hair Salon

Providing the pampering beauty experience you deserve, our in-house hair salon is ready to make you look and feel your best for magical moments under the Corfiot sky. Our services include haircuts and styling for him and her, manicures and pedicures, hair treatments, make-up, waxing and more.

### Terms & Conditions

- We highly recommend scheduling your appointments at least 1–2 days in advance to ensure your preferred time and treatment or service are available.
- Please check in at the Spa Reception at least 15 minutes prior to your appointment.
- We accept cash, debit cards, personal cheques, Visa, MasterCard and American Express.
- Angsana Spa is available to guests aged 16 years and above.
- Please advise us of any health conditions, allergies or injuries that could affect your treatment or service when making your Spa reservation.
- Prices for services and products are subject to change on our website and menus without prior notice.
- For more information and advance reservations, please call us at +30 26610 22900 or email spa-corfu@angsana.com.
