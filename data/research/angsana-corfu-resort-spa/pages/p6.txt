# Dining

## Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa

## In-style dining with flavours from around the world

Angsana Corfu places culinary discovery at the heart of the island experience. Each Corfu restaurant carries a different flavour, with Greek and Corfiot specialities joined by North Asian cuisine, sushi, and international favourites.

Meals are savoured against sweeping Ionian views and the mountainous Corfiot landscape. The setting changes from elevated terraces to the beach and rooftop, yet the spirit remains warm and Mediterranean throughout.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

Expand All

##### Is it a good idea for guests to have their dinner reservations in advance?

Yes, It is recommended for guests to book their dining in advance or upon arrival in one of our restaurants, especially in the peak season June to September. You can book your table on the spot or through or digital concierge app.

##### Do you offer half-board, full-board or all-inclusive meal plans?

We do offer half and full board on request and guests can enquire about this when making their booking or on arrival.

We do not currently offer all inclusive packages.

##### Are there water activities available on the beach?

Yes, there is a water sports station at Angsana Corfu's Emerald Beach, which operates during the summer season, depending on weather conditions. In addition, guests can pre-book a daily excursion by private yacht to one of Corfu's secluded beaches or a nearby island.

##### How to get to the Emerald Beach and what is the sunbeds policy?

Please note that during the operation hours/dates of the Emerald Beach Bar there is a complimentary shuttle to/from the beach, approximately every 15 minutes. The distance to the beach is about 3 minutes.

Sunbeds and towels are available for the resort guests without extra charge.

##### I want to schedule an event / birthday party / proposal / bachelor / business occasion, who should i contact?

- For any kind of Social Events you can send your request directly to events-corfu@angsana.com
- If you wish to enquire about Business Events, please email here salesmanager-corfu@angsana.com

##### I am not staying at the hotel, can i visit the pool, gym or one of the restaurants / lounges?

Guests not staying at the resort must purchase a day pass to use the pool, gym or beach.

However, access to the restaurants, bars and lounges is free for everyone.
