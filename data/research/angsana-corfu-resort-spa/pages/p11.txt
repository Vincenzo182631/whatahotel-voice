### Outdoor Infinity Pool

Angsana Corfu's outdoor infinity pool encapsulates the heart of the resort's lifestyle. With far reaching sea views and blue skies mingling together into an awe-inspiring landscape of blue, the pool reminds every visitor that summers are made to be lived poolside. Comfy loungers, sunbeds and umbrellas surround the pool, while the Sunrise Pool Bar offers cocktails and healthy snacks both poolside and waterside on a wooden deck across the waters.

READ MORE

### Heated Indoor Pool

Our 25 m indoor pool is located on the same level as the Spa and Gym, overlooking an infinity of blue waters and distant Grecian mountains. It offers perfect bliss on autumn and winter days, a hideaway sanctuary of comfort and style for guests to unwind in peace. The poolside Juice Bar makes for a perfect meeting spot after your morning swim.

### Private Beach

Experience an exclusive seaside escape at our private Emerald Beach. Shimmering turquoise waters meet luxury comfort and the most inviting energy from our lively beach bar. Enjoy exotic refreshments, mouthwatering snacks, and a wide selection of water- sports, for the most exciting beach adventures.

**Beach Bar hours**: 11:00 - 18:00

**Dinner** **hours**: 19:00 - 23:00

Transfer provided from/to the beach every 30 minutes from 10:00 to midnight, during the operation season.

### Fitness Club

Meet your fitness goals and make the most of your island escape in our state-of-the-art gym, or on our outdoor yoga deck and find the perfect place to focus on your personal wellbeing. Complimentary group classes and personal training sessions are available for you to discover in our gym where you can truly immerse yourself in the Angsana Corfu experience.

Contact our Spa to find more about the daily training program.

**Open daily**: 07:00 - 22:00

### Angsana Gallery

Take home memorabilia to remind you of the little touches that made your visit in Corfu and at the Angsana Corfu resort unique. With a collection of handicrafts and resort apparel, you'll find something to cherish long after you've returned home.

A special space for our young visitors, dedicated for 4 - 11 year olds sharing the most exciting all day adventures. Complete with its own kids’ swimming pool, sandbox, all day games and arts and crafts led by an expert team of professional educators, children can truly enjoy their holidays with special moments created just for them.
