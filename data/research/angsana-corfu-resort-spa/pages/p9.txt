# Ionian Luxury Pool Villas

> #### _Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa_

Set high above the Ionian Sea, the Ionian Luxury Pool Villas at Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa offer a refined take on luxury villa living in Corfu. Designed for travelers who value privacy, space and understated elegance, each villa combines contemporary architecture with sweeping sea views, private pools and seamless access to the resort’s curated experiences.

Whether you are planning a family villa holiday in Corfu, a romantic escape, or a longer stay with friends, these villas provide the comfort of a private residence with the services of a world-class resort — all within easy reach of Corfu’s cultural landmarks, beaches and mountain villages.

## Private Pool Living Above the Ionian Sea

Set into the hillside and overlooking endless shades of blue, each villa features its own private pool and expansive outdoor terrace. Floor-to-ceiling windows invite natural light indoors, while seamless indoor-outdoor living spaces allow guests to move effortlessly between sun-drenched decks and serene interiors.

Relax in your own sanctuary, take a refreshing dip in the pool and unwind in the luxurious surroundings of your private Corfiot villa. Enjoy romantic dinners under the stars, rejuvenating in-villa massages, or enhance your stay with our eclectic villa living experiences.

## Exclusive Benefits When Booking Direct

Guests who book the Ionian Luxury Pool Villas directly through our website enjoy exclusive privileges designed to enhance their stay, including:

- Participation in daily Eclectic Experiences and group fitness sessions (subject to availability)
- Exclusive access to the V-Lounge
- Dedicated breakfast area for villa guests (seasonal)
- Access to the fitness club, hammam, jacuzzi, sauna and heated indoor pool
- Dedicated Eclectic Villa Living team to curate unique experiences for you

## Your Private Paradise by the Sea

Nestled amongst the hills surrounding the resort, there are 37 beautifully decorated villas, each with a private pool and sweeping views of the sparkling Ionian Sea and mainland Greece. Spacious living areas, elegant bedrooms and generous terraces make these villas an ideal choice for those seeking family villas in Corfu without compromising on style or privacy.

##### What types of villas are available at Angsana Corfu Resort?

Our collection of luxury Corfu villas includes:

**One-Bedroom Ionian Seaview Pool Villa**

Perfect for couples and solo travellers, relax in your own space with private pool access and sweeping sea views — ideal for honeymooners or romantic escapes.

**Two-Bedroom Ionian Seaview Pool Villa**

These family-friendly villas in Corfu are designed for comfort and connection, with separate bedrooms, generous indoor areas, and your own plunge pool overlooking tranquil gardens and sea.

**Three-Bedroom Ionian Seaview Pool Villa**

Perfect for families and small groups, enjoy expansive indoor and outdoor living spaces, private pools, and panoramic Ionian views — a standout choice for a memorable holiday in Corfu.

**Four-Bedroom Ionian Seaview Pool Villa**

For larger groups or multigenerational stays, this villa provides luxury and space, with multiple rooms, abundant outdoor terraces, and private swimming pools perfect for family gatherings.

##### What resort privileges and benefits are included when booking a private pool villa at Angsana Corfu?

Guests staying in the Ionian Luxury Pool Villas enjoy the privacy of a private residence alongside full access to the five-star facilities and services of Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa. Villa stays include:

- Exclusive access to the V-Lounge
- Dedicated breakfast area for villa guests\*
- Dedicated Eclectic Villa Living team to curate unique experiences for you
- Complimentary participation in group training classes or in-house experiences\* such as guided hiking, yoga, and more
- Access to the fitness club, hammam, jacuzzi, sauna and heated indoor pool
- Daily transfers to and from Emerald Beach Club during the period of operation
- Complimentary Wi-Fi throughout the resort and free parking
- Welcome drink and local sweets upon arrival
- Daily housekeeping

\\* During certain periods, the dedicated breakfast area and in-house experiences may not be available.

##### Why choose a luxury villa in Angsana Corfu?

Our private pool villas offer exclusivity,

Choosing a luxury villa in Corfu means embracing a slower rhythm of travel — one defined by space, privacy and authenticity. At Angsana Corfu, this philosophy is elevated through thoughtful design, personalised service and an exceptional natural setting overlooking the Ionian Sea.

Here, private villa living becomes more than accommodation; it becomes an experience shaped around comfort, connection and a true sense of place.

##### What experiences can be arranged for villa guests?

Villa guests can enjoy a range of personalized Eclectic Villa Living Experiences such as private yoga sessions, cooking classes, spa therapies and more, in the comfort of their villa.

In addition, our dedicated Villa Living team can arrange unique Luxury Yacht Experiences to the nearby islands and Corfu's secluded beaches and most beautiful spots.

##### Can I arrange private events or celebrations in the villa?

Yes, our team can help you organize private events, celebrations, or even intimate dinners within your private villa. Please contact our Guest Relations Manager ( grm-corfu@angsana.com) to make arrangements for your special occasion.

##### Can I request a personal chef for my villa?

Yes, we offer the option to hire a personal chef to prepare meals in the privacy of your villa, such as The Corfiot Family Table experience. This can be arranged for special occasions or extended stays.

##### Are the villa pools heated?

Some of our 2-Bedroom villas feature heated pools, providing added comfort during cooler months. Please inquire at the time of booking for availability.
