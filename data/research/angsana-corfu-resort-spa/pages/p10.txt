# 5 Best Beaches in Corfu

## Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa

## Explore the 5 Best Beaches in Corfu

Corfu Island, nestled in the crystal-clear waters of the Ionian Sea, is renowned for its stunning beaches that beckon travelers from around the world. From secluded coves to vibrant coastal stretches, Corfu offers a diverse array of beach experiences. Let's embark on a journey to explore some of the best beaches this captivating Greek island has to offer.

## Paleokastritsa Beach

Nestled amidst dramatic cliffs and lush greenery, Paleokastritsa Beach is a true gem. Its turquoise waters and soft golden sand create a picturesque setting. Visitors can enjoy swimming, snorkeling, and exploring the hidden caves dotting the coastline. With stunning vistas at every turn, Paleokastritsa Beach is a must-visit for nature lovers and beach enthusiasts alike.

## Canal d'Amour

Located on the northern coast of Corfu, Canal d'Amour is a beach of captivating beauty. Its unique geological formations, featuring narrow channels and stunning rock formations, create an otherworldly atmosphere. Legend has it that couples who swim through the canal will experience eternal love. Whether you're seeking romance or simply want to witness nature's artistry, Canal d'Amour is an unforgettable destination.

## Agios Gordios Beach

Nestled in a charming coastal village, Agios Gordios Beach is a beloved destination for families and couples alike. This long sandy beach offers ample space for sunbathing, building sandcastles, and taking leisurely walks along the shore. Surrounded by verdant cliffs, it provides a serene and idyllic escape. Don't miss the opportunity to witness breathtaking sunsets painting the sky in a kaleidoscope of colors.

## Issos Beach

Issos Beach, located on the southwestern coast of Corfu, is a true paradise for beachgoers. With its vast stretch of golden sand and crystal-clear turquoise waters, it offers a breathtaking setting that captivates visitors. Nestled between rolling sand dunes and a picturesque lake, Issos Beach boasts a unique and untouched natural beauty. The shallow and calm waters make it ideal for families with children, while water sports enthusiasts can indulge in thrilling activities such as windsurfing and kiteboarding. With sun loungers and umbrellas available for relaxation, as well as beachfront tavernas offering delicious local cuisine, Issos Beach invites you to soak up the sun, unwind, and create unforgettable memories on the shores of Corfu.

## Porto Timoni

Porto Timoni is not your typical beach; it exudes exotic beauty and leaves a lasting impression, especially when viewed from above. Located near the village of Afionas, this double bay is best reached by boat or sea canoe from neighboring beaches. Alternatively, you need to walk for 20-30 minutes via hiking trail and under the scorching sun. The crystal clear waters of Porto Timoni are jaw-dropping and the entire landscape brings you in mind that you are located somewhere exotic like Thailand.
