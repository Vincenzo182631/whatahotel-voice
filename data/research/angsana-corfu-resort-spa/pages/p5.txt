# Ionian Seaview One-Bedroom Pool Villa

## The perfect romantic getaway

Relax in your own personal sanctuary, take a refreshing dip in the pool, and unwind in the luxurious surroundings. Enjoy romantic dinners under the stars, rejuvenating spa treatments, and personalized services that will make your stay truly special.

Featuring far reaching sea views, the one-bedroom villa includes a delightful private pool set into the idyllic countryside. The villa is available either as a one level or a two level maisonette, comprising an elegantly decorated master bedroom with an ensuite marble bathroom, a separate living room and a family kitchenette (equipment upon request) with a dining table.

## Delightful private pool set into idyllic countryside

Combining the feel of an authentic Corfu home with the privacy, exclusivity and services of a 5 star hotel, this villa is the perfect summer escape for couples but families as well. Our private pool villas provide a haven of comfort and fun for all.

The interior, living space of the One-Bedroom Pool Villa is 97 sqm / 1044 sqft. Exterior spaces and pool count 87 sqm / 936 sqft.

**Maximum room capacity**: 3 Adults or 2 Adults & 2 Children
