# Ionian Seaview Junior Suite

## The perfect escape with glittering views of the sea

The Ionian Seaview Junior Suite offers spacious, contemporary and elegant open-plan interiors inspired by the avant-garde Corfu island lifestyle and Far East philosophy, set against the sapphire expanse of the Ionian Sea and the picturesque Benitses Bay.

A futon king-size bed sets the mood for a relaxing and laid back bedroom ambiance. Rich wooden furnishings smartly transform this open-plan suite into a contemporary apartment, with wooden dividers stylishly separating the bedroom from the living room. (Kindly note that the balconies may have different layouts).

Book now

In the marble ensuite bathroom, stylish glass walls set off a luxurious shower space. An extra deep soaking bathtub turns this intimate space into a romantic playground for relaxation, ideal for young-at heart couples. Refined decor inspired by local architecture and Eastern influences create the perfect Zen space for shedding the stress away, recharging your energy and enjoying intimate moments of fun.

The interior, living space plus the balcony/exterior space of the Ionian Seaview Junior Suite is 69 sqm / 742 sqft (balcony spaces count around 12 sqm / 129 sqft).

**Room capacity:** 3 Adults or 2 Adults & 1 Child
