# Retreats

> #### _Angsana Corfu Resort & Spa_

## Reimagine Your Retreat: Discover Angsana Corfu

Sunlight, sea air, and Corfu’s unhurried landscapes forge a natural setting for renewal. Angsana Corfu is where one can host individual and corporate wellbeing retreats in Corfu. With yoga on the panoramic rooftop deck, guided movement, restorative spa rituals, and hikes through the island’s green interior - every activity encourages mindfulness.

Programmes can also reach beyond the resort through private boat journeys, local cuisine, and experiences rooted in island culture. Each retreat leaves room to reconnect with nature, with others and with a steadier rhythm of your own.

## TESTIMONIALS

Epic yoga retreat experience!
Hands down the most supportive and effective production for a yoga retreat ever. At every turn, I felt the amazing support on the ground level for the yoga retreat I co-organized with the help of the Angsana team. The facilities, the staff, the lodging, the exceptional food, the activities and itinerary crafted just for us was a 5 star luxury experience with the warmth and personability of a boutique hotel. Looking forward to having another epic experience next year!

_**Rina - October 2024**_

#### The Perfect Setting for a Retreat

Angsana Corfu was the perfect setting for our Enneagram & Kundalini Yoga Breathwork Retreat. The team's professionalism, warmth, and support were deeply impressive, providing an ideal, peaceful, and beautifully designed environment for personal transformation. The stunning views and well-maintained spaces created a perfect backdrop for reflection. The staff were incredibly friendly, accommodating, and genuinely invested in our success, handling all logistics seamlessly. I highly recommend Angsana Corfu to fellow retreat leaders and am already looking forward to returning in 2026!

_Lynn R. - May 2025_

#### Heaven in Corfu

Angsana was amazing. I went for a yoga retreat with friends and, from the moment we arrived, we were treated like royalty. Every experience was offered with so much intention. The food…amazing. The room…amazing…

The Spa was divine, from the design to the staff and therapists behind the service. Easy access to the beach with a shuttle that runs every 20 minutes, and on time. The infinity pool was dreamy, with a full view of the sea and staff that ensures you are taken care of. The fitness centre and yoga rooms, pristine and so relaxing. If you like hiking, there is a beautiful and challenging hiking path right outside of the hotel gates with even more amazing views. Thank you so much Angsana, for the stellar treatment and experience!

_Sarah A. - October 2024_

#### Luxury Yoga Retreat was a huge success

Group yoga retreat was a huge success! Every staff member went above and beyond to ensure that both group and individual needs were met. Very much enjoyed Greek hospitality and seeing part of Greece that isn't as well-known to many tourists. Much to see and do locally, as well as plenty of amenities on-site for a comfortable stay. Rooms were spacious, well-appointed and clean. Restaurant had multiple dining options. Both pool and beach access were lovely (although pool loungers were in high demand) Convenient shuttle to the beach. Excellent spa experience. Definitely looking forward to returning!!
