# Dining

Inspired Dining at Grand Hyatt Changsha

At Grand Hyatt Changsha, treat your taste buds to decadent dining experiences that embody international cuisine. An abundance of local and worldly culinary experiences await at three destination restaurants. Guests can savor Hunan’s legendary spicy cuisine and specialty Peking duck at Chinese restaurant – Xiangjiang 36, and enjoy western food at Grand Café.

## Grand Café

Grand Café is know as a lively interactive all-day dining restaurant in town. The restaurant features an open kitchen that allows patrons to see chefs in the live kitchen preparing dishes to each diner's preferences, and serving the freshest produce for each meal period. Along with the live kitchens, gourmands are offered a broad selections of hot and cold dishes that include seafood, grilled food, barbecued items, noodles, dim sum, delicious desserts, fruits, drinks, beverages and more. Selections include scrumptious Hunan countryside cuisine, Asian flavors as well as Western cuisine.

Highlights:
Winter Season
Grand Café is now serving limited winter dishes featuring “Budda jumps over the wall” & and “Sheep of Ningxia” sure to be enjoyed by both locals and travelers.

## Xiangjiang 36

Grand Hyatt Changsha’s signature Chinese restaurant, namely Xiangjiang 36, shines a dazzling spotlight on the bold flavors of authentic Hunan cuisine and its regional specialties, prepared by brilliant local chefs. Xiangjiang 36 also specializes in creative, sumptuous regional cuisines like Cantonese, Sichuan, Zhejiang and Hangzhou, and is also renowned for its signature Peking duck prepared by a master from Beijing.
Xiangjiang 36 welcomes guests into the colorful theater of a Chinese kitchen. Chefs can be seen in live-cooking action at three show kitchens, providing a thrilling backdrop to the dining experience. Guests can watch the chefs preparing steaming bamboo baskets of dim sum and hand-pulling fresh noodles in the steam and noodle kitchen like a live showcase, and creating exquisite sweets in the dessert kitchen.
