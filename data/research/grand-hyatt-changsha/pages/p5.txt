### Expectations exceeded

My recent stay at the Grand Hyatt Changsha was exceptional, particularly given the high-pressure environment of the Labor Day holiday. The hotel managed to balance luxury and efficiency perfectly. The communication was top-tier from the start. I received an email prior to arrival acknowledging the high occupancy rate and proactively explaining upgrade availability. While no suites were open, I deeply appreciated the transparency and the effort to provide the best available options. The staff was incredibly attentive throughout my stay, ensuring my Globalist benefits such as early check in and late check out wwre offered. The dining experience was a major highlight. Being able to sample local Changsha delicacies like Sugar Oil Baba (糖油粑粑), Scallion Pancakes (葱油饼), Crawfish (小龙虾), and Stinky Tofu (长沙臭豆腐) within the hotel was fantastic. The flavors were authentic, and having them in a five-star setting provided total peace of mind regarding food hygiene. A special shout-out to Executive Chef Jerry Zhang. His passion is evident; I saw him everywhere—from managing the breakfast rush at Grand Cafe to overseeing evening canapés in the Grand Lounge, and right back in the kitchen for dinner service. His energy and friendliness really elevate the guest experience. Location & Final Thoughts: the location is unbeatable for exploring the city. Between the proactive service, the dedication of the culinary team, and the perfect placement in the heart of Changsha, this is easily one of the best Hyatt properties in the region. I look forward to returning!

### A great ending to our Hunan trip

Grand Hyatt Changsha was the last hotel stay of our trip. We had great stays in all the hotels in Zhangjiajie, Wulingyuan and Phoenix ancient town. Grand Hyatt Changsha was the cherry on top of the cake.

It was really spacious and comfortable. My aunt could not stop marvelling at the space and the functionality of the room. We unfortunately got a smoking room as I forgot to specify during booking. The faint cigarette smell was only detected in the bathroom, which did not bother us that much. We had 9 rooms altogether, so we were split into different levels.

Though the hotel was fully booked during our stay, the breakfast area was large enough such that we did not have to queue for food nor did we feel claustrophobic. The staff were very attentive and cleared the dishes very quickly. It was a very comfortable dining experience.

We had dinner at the Chinese restaurant at level 5. Unfortunately that meal was mediocre at best and did not live up to expectation. The service staff were exemplary.

Nonetheless, Grand Hyatt Changsha left a very positive impression and we will not hesitate to return again.

### A Severe Lack of Communication Skills Unbecoming of a Global Brand's Reputation

I visited the Grand Hyatt Changsha for a two-night stay to attend a business meeting. From the moment I checked in, the hotel staff had difficulty communicating in English. They couldn't understand my questions on their own and had to call another employee over just to continue the conversation.

Since it was late when I checked in, I tried to order dinner through room service. However, the room service staff also had severe difficulty communicating in English. They ended up having to send an employee to my room just to take the order. (The staff member who visited also had trouble with English, and I had to painstakingly place the order by pointing at items on the menu.)

After a long delay, I called room service again to check on the status, but communication was not possible. In the end, I had to cancel the order after waiting for about two hours. I then tried calling the front desk to report this issue, but again, communication in English was impossible, so I couldn't even file a complaint.

For any foreigners planning to stay at the Grand Hyatt Changsha, I'd advise you to be prepared for communication challenges in English and have a translation app ready.

On a positive note, the hotel's facilities are nice.

Spent 7 nights at Grand Hyatt Changsha in a corner city view suite (upgrade certificate used) on the 61st floor, the hotel's highest floor. Grand Hyatt Changsha comes highly recommended.

### Room for Improvement on Cleanliness and Service at Grand Hyatt Changsha

The check-in process was satisfactory; I was assigned to the 61st floor, the highest room available at the Grand Hyatt Changsha. However, upon entering the room, I noticed stains and dust on the bedside table, sink, bathroom, and dressing room floor windows. Additionally, there was a piece of iron shaped like the letter "R" on the carpet, which I am certain was neither from the hotel nor myself, but likely from the previous occupant. When I brought this to the attention of room service, it took over thirty minutes for someone to respond. When I requested to have the cleaning crew re-clean the room, they offered to "upgrade" me to a different room type on the 57th floor, which I accepted. However, when I entered the new room, I found it was virtually the same except for the placement of the cupboards and sofas. Moreover, the Dyson hairdryer and smart toilet in the first room had been replaced with a generic hairdryer and an old-fashioned toilet. Finally, when I collected my luggage while checking out, the hotel staff invited me to leave a positive review of the Grand Hyatt Changsha on Trip.com, where I booked my room, in exchange for a set of L'Occitane toiletries. While it is understandable to seek positive reviews, I suggest that the most sustainable way for Grand Hyatt Changsha to achieve this is by improving the hotel's hygiene and room service.
