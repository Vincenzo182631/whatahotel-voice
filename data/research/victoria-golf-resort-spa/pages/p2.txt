Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about our resort. We’re here to help you plan the perfect stay.

Where is Victoria Resort Golf & Spa, Managed by Accor located?

Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor is located in Vilamoura’ s most exquisite neighborhood, just minutes from the marina and long stretches of beach, close to the main golf courses.

How do I get to Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor?

Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor is a 30-minute drive from Faro International Airport, and two and a half hours from Lisbon and Seville International Airports, and we can arrange a limousine transfer service. You can also take a two-and-a-half-hour train journey from Lisbon to Tunes, followed by a 20-minute drive to the resort. Buses also operate from Faro International Airport to Vilamoura Marina.

Does Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor have valet parking?

The Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor valet parking service has no charge.

What leisure facilities are there at Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor?

Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor boasts four swimming pools. There’s a tennis court and fitness center and a kids club. Relaxation and wellness journeys await at the Victoria Spa.

Where is the nearest golf course to Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor?

Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor is located near the 6 main golf courses in Vilamoura.

Are there wheelchair - acessible rooms?

Yes, we have wheelchair – accessible rooms.

Is Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor family friendly?

Yes, Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor is family – friendly.

Are pets allowed at Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor?

Yes, pets are allowed at Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor.
