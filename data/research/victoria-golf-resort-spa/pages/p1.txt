# An Exciting New Chapter Begins

Victoria Golf Resort & Spa, Managed by Accor, will temporarily close from 15 October 2026 as the resort undergoes an extensive transformation ahead of its reopening as Fairmont Vilamoura, the first Fairmont hotel in Portugal.

The new resort will feature reimagined spaces, enhanced facilities and new experiences inspired by the spirit of the Algarve, alongside Fairmont’s renowned hospitality.

For further information regarding existing reservations or the temporary closure, our team remains at your entire disposal.

We look forward to welcoming you back in 2028to experience Fairmont Vilamoura.
