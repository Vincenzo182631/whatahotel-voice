## The Ultimate All-Inclusive Getaway

At Paws Up, every detail is thoughtfully handled so you can focus on the moments, not the logistics. Recently recognized as the #8 best resort in the world by readers of Conde Nast Traveler, Paws Up offers everything from private accommodations,to award-winning dining, to your own private SUV to drive throughout your stay.

Why Paws Up Montana?

## The Wild You Need

There are places you visit, and places that stay with you for years to come.

At Paws Up Montana, we recognize that true luxury is found in the wild. Here, open skies replace crowded schedules. Campfires spark meaningful conversations. Adventure leads to unforgettable memories. And every detail is designed to help you slow down, reconnect, and feel fully present.

Spread across 37,000 acres of pristine Montana wilderness, Paws Up offers the freedom to explore, discover, and unwind without ever leaving the property. Whether you’re horseback riding through rolling hills, fly-fishing blue-ribbon waters, sharing stories under a sky full of stars, or enjoying a chef-prepared meal after a day outdoors, every moment feels both extraordinary and deeply personal.

This is where adventure meets refinement. Where families grow closer, couples reconnect, and lifelong memories are made. It’s where the wilderness isn’t something you simply observe—it’s something you feel: inspired, connected, and completely alive.
