## The Paws Up Experience

Step into the extraordinary. Put on your boots, expand your horizons, and discover a place where you don’t have to be anything other than present. Out here, where luxury lodgings meet endless adventure, you’ll find everything you didn’t know you were looking for. Paws Up Montana—The Wild You Need.

### Accommodations

From luxury historic homes and safari-style tents to hidden woodland hauses, Paws Up offers 64 unique lodgings that feel unforgettable and completely yours. Each stay combines comfort, privacy, and exceptional service, set against the untamed beauty of Montana.

### ADVENTURES

With more activities than any other ranch resort in Montana, Paws Up invites you to explore the wild at your own pace, your own way. Whether you’re venturing into the backcountry, honing new skills, or stepping out of your comfort zone, our expert guides make sure every experience is rooted in authenticity and connection to the land.

Every meal at Paws Up is a celebration of Montana’s bounty—crafted with seasonal, sustainable ingredients and meticulous care. Dine indoors or al fresco, where crackling fires and mountain views elevate every bite.
