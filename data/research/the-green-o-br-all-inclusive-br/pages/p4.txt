# Green Haus

Scroll Next

## See What Blossoms Here

Part woodland bunker, part modern museum, the Green Haus’ unique indoor/outdoor design invites guests to seamlessly roam from luxury to wilderness and back again. This enchanting haus features a pitched ceiling for unparalleled views of the stars.

Floor-to-ceiling windows flood the space with natural light by day while framing the surrounding forest from nearly every angle. Carefully curated furnishings, warm natural materials, and contemporary design elements create an atmosphere that feels both sophisticated and inviting. Step outside onto your private deck to enjoy the fresh mountain air, watch local wildlife wander through the landscape, or simply savor the peaceful sounds of nature. Whether you’re enjoying a quiet morning coffee, unwinding after a day of adventure, or settling in for a restful night, every moment feels deeply restorative.
