Feel Pampered at Our Day Spa in O‘ahu
Spa
Immerse yourself in a world of wellness and nourishment for your mind, body and soul with an authentic wellness experience at The Ritz Carlton Oʻahu, Turtle Bay. The products used at our O'ahu spa feature pure, sustainable, and natural ingredients, presenting the most significant and therapeutic elements that Hawaii has to offer. Explore the potency of nature through "laʻau lapaʻau," medicinal native Hawaiian plants, gathered from our healing gardens, and enhance overall health, vitality, and relaxation within the tranquil ambiance of our serene healing gardens.
Curated Immersions
Ocean of Renewal
Discover profound renewal through this exclusive wellness immersion inspired by the restorative rhythms of Hawaiʻi: Kai Ola, Ocean of Renewal. Your journey begins with the soothing resonance of a crystal sound bowl healing beside the ocean, followed by a replenishing foot soak infused with enriching organic botanicals. Unwind further with a customized chakra-balancing massage in our signature Oceanside Hale, then savor a locally crafted wellness beverage and wholesome refreshment while overlooking the endless blue of the Pacific. To reserve this Immersion, please contact our Nalu Spa or Concierge team.

Mālama Mahina
Embark on a restorative wellness journey inspired by the connection between land, sea, and moon. Begin with a mindful forest bathing walk through tropical pathways, where the sights, sounds, and scents of nature awaken the senses and cultivate a sense of grounding. As you move from the serenity of the landscape to the ocean's edge, your journey continues with a private oceanfront yoga practice and guided meditation inspired by the current lunar phase, creating space for reflection, release, intention, and renewal. To reserve this Immersion, please contact our Nalu Spa or Concierge team.

Spa Bookings
The spa is located on the resort pool level. The primary entrance to the spa is located on the lobby level next to the fitness center. Please arrive 15 minutes prior to your appointment time to complete a guest intake form and prepare for your services.
Fitness Bookings
The Ritz-Carlton O'ahu, Turtle Bay Fitness offers a variety of classes at different times throughout the week. We kindly request that you arrive 5 minutes before the start time of any class to ensure a smooth check-in and to be ready to begin on time.
Cancellation/ Late Arrival
We kindly ask for 24-hour notice if you need to change or cancel your spa reservation to avoid a 100% charge for the service. Late arrivals may result in reduced treatment times. Additionally, an additional 22% service charge and Hawaii tax will be added to all services.
