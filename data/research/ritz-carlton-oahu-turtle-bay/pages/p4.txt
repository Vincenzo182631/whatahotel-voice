Savor the North Shore
Dining
Experience world-class cuisine at The Ritz-Carlton O'ahu, Turtle Bay, where our dining venues redefine restaurants in Oahu with picturesque ocean vistas and locally inspired menus. From fresh island creations to globally influenced dishes, our North Shore, Oahu restaurants deliver unforgettable culinary journeys that celebrate Hawai'i's diverse flavors amid the awe-inspiring natural beauty of the North Shore.

Signature Dining Experiences
The Beach Club
Nestled on the pristine sands of Kuilima Cove, The Beach Club offers an idyllic escape to soak up the sunshine. Sink into the plush comfort of our cabanas or daybeds while enjoying drinks and light bites from Beach House by Roy Yamaguchi.

Private Beach Picnic
Celebrate life’s special moments with a private picnic on the beach. Delight in delectable cuisine crafted by our culinary experts while basking in the breathtaking views of the North Shore’s pristine shoreline and famed surf breaks.

A Private Oceanside Dinner Experience
Savor by the Sea
Perched on the oceanside cliffs of Turtle Bay, perfectly aligned with the setting sun, this candlelit dining experience sets the stage for an unforgettable evening of tranquil island elegance. With the horizon as your backdrop and the ocean as your soundtrack, enjoy a private, multi-course menu inspired by island ingredients and seasonal flavors. Uninterrupted sunset views, attentive service, and thoughtful personalized touches create an intimate celebration that lingers long after the evening ends.
