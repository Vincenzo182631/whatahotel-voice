Pools & Beaches
Set on over 1,100 oceanfront acres, moments of bliss and thrilling adventures transpire along the North Shore coast. Find your happy place at our terraced pool deck and soak in the sun or head out to one of our seven beaches from which you can surf, swim, snorkel, or simply relax on a sandy beach.
Main Resort Pool
This expansive pool deck includes a large family pool, hot tub, and ample room for fun-filled activities. For added relaxation, unwind in a spacious cabana or settle into a cozy daybed.

Adult Pool
This infinity-style pool in our Oahu resort spa features a hot tub, in-water loungers, and endless ocean views. Indulge in a private cabana, where you can sip on our famed Mai Tais and enjoy all-day lounging.

Nearby Attractions
The iconic North Shore of O’ahu is among Hawai’i’s most coveted locales. Sun-drenched beaches make up eleven miles of the area’s 17 mile coastline, where jaw-dropping surf and 360-degree views set the stage for every activity. Roughly a one-hour drive from Honolulu and Waikiki, our North Shore hotel near Waimea Valley and other famous natural attractions gives way to the most beautiful stretch of scenery on the island of O’ahu.

Yes. The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay offers a fitness center available for a fee to hotel guests during their stay.
