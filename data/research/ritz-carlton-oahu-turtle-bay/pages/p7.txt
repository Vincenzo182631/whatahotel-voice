Elevate Your Stay
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Club redefines the concept of elite hospitality, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities, and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination. Let our O'ahu hotel with a Club Lounge take your getaway to the next level.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay at our Oahu hotel with a Club Lounge by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.

In Good Taste
Daily Culinary Presentations
Five times each day, guests who choose our O'ahu hotel with Club Lounge enjoy complimentary food and beverage presentations. These daily offerings — breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea, hors d’oeuvres, and cordials — start with Hawaiian coffee, fruit plates, and locally-sourced breakfast items. In between sun-filled adventures savor freshly made sandwiches and salads. Cap off the day with refreshing cocktails and prime views of the sunset.
