Alaia
Our signature restaurant located on the lobby level highlights the island's abundant bounty with locally inspired creations. At our Beachfront O'ahu restaurants, enjoy a variety of modern dishes prepared with the freshest North Shore ingredients.
Explore
Off The Lip
Talk story with old and new friends at our relaxing O'ahu, Hawai'i bar located in our stunning oceanfront lobby. Delight in savory bites and deliciously crafted cocktails. Witness the North Shore's magical sunsets while you sip and stay awhile.
Explore
Sunset
Pull up a chair where the sun meets the surf at Sunset Pool Bar, located at the Main Resort Pool deck. Watch surfers flock to catch a wave as the sun sets below the horizon and enjoy live music entertainment daily from our waterfront restaurant in Oahu.
Explore
Ho‘olana
Aloha Kakahiaka! Start your day at our lobby coffee shop with a freshly brewed cup of coffee and beautiful ocean vistas. Enjoy oven-fresh baked goods, delicious smoothies, or grab a bite to holoholo (go cruising).
Explore
Beach House by Roy Yamaguchi
Located at Kuilima Cove, savor welcoming, family-favorite dishes created by Hawai‘i’s famed chef Roy Yamaguchi, inspired by memories of beachside gatherings. Visit this oceanfront restaurant in Oahu for incredible menus and beachfront views.
Explore
Lei Lei's Bar & Grill
A local favorite, Lei Lei's Bar & Grill is celebrated for its hearty dishes, relaxed atmosphere on the Fazio Golf Course, and lively conversations with beloved host, Uncle Ian.
Explore
Paniolo, A Hawaiian Cowboy Lūʻau
Set against the ocean backdrop at our horse Stables, celebrate Hawai‘i’s rich ranching and cowboy heritage at our weekly dinner show. Enjoy a locally inspired buffet, cultural music and dance, and captivating tales of the Paniolo way of life.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 35.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay, with rates of $48.00 per day, and $336.00 per week. Valet parking is available for $48.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay is Daniel K. Inouye International Airport (HNL). HNL is located approximately 37.5 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay have an airport shuttle to Daniel K. Inouye International Airport (HNL)?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay has an airport shuttle to Daniel K. Inouye International Airport (HNL).
Does The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton O‘ahu, Turtle Bay offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
