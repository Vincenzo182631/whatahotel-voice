# Find Your Zen

Nobu Hotel Palo Alto embodies the Japanese philosophy of Shiawase — a celebration of happiness and well-being—to infuse moments of holistic wellness, fitness, and relaxation into every detail of your time with us.

## Fitness Center

Take your routine on the road in a private sanctuary for wellness enthusiasts located on our second floor. Discover an elevated, 24/7 workout experience in our fitness center featuring innovative Technogym equipment, including free weights, treadmills, ellipticals, and cycle bikes.

## Watercourse Way Bath House Spa

Watercourse Way Bath House Spa, is an award-winning wellness destination inspired by the natural hot springs of Tassajara just five-minutes walking distance from the hotel. Experience the serenity of one of their private hot tub rooms with steam or sauna facilities, treat yourself to a relaxing massage, or reserve a luxurious treatment.
