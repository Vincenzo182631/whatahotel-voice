# Explore Palo Alto

Stay in the heart of Downtown Palo Alto, a sun-soaked Silicon Valley city that shines with rich culture, natural beauty, and creative energy. While this tech hub is unparalleled in its spirit of innovation, this Northern Californian city is far from business as usual with plenty of luxury and leisure within strolling distance.

Seamless Serenity

Nobu Hotel Palo Alto is a place of perfect balance where work and life feel equally luxurious. Our interior—created by Montalba Architects—is defined by neutral hues, natural materials, and minimalist style. Each space is designed to seamlessly integrate indoor and outdoor areas for guests to enjoy the city’s incredible weather. The exterior’s sleek wood façade is inspired by our Northern California home, complemented by a custom mural of El Palo Alto.

Our hotel’s design centerpiece is our garden, a lush sanctuary connected to Nobu Palo Alto that merges curated craftsmanship with traditional Japanese elements to create an unparalleled urban oasis.

## Discover Downtown Palo Alto

Downtown Palo Alto embodies small-town charm with its walkable streets and tree-lined avenues, dotted with open-air cafés, farmers’ markets, theatres, bookstores, and abundant shopping and dining options. Lush nature and green spaces are also nearby, from the sprawling grounds of Stanford University’s prestigious campus to the stunning landscape of the Filoli Historic House & Gardens, and undisturbed natural habitats of Baylands Nature Preserve—ideal for joggers, cyclists, and birdwatchers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

- What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 4:00 pm and check-out is at noon. We’ll always do our best to accommodate early check-in and late check-out requests.

- Is Nobu Hotel Palo Alto pet-friendly?

Unfortunately, we do not allow pets at Nobu Hotel Palo Alto.

- Is parking available?

Yes, we’re pleased to offer valet parking at a $55 daily rate. Many local parking options are also available nearby. Click HERE for additional parking options.

- How far is Nobu Hotel Palo Alto from the nearest airport?

The nearest airport to Palo Alto is the San Jose International Airport, just 17 miles away. Other nearby airports include the San Francisco International Airport (20.5 miles), and the San Francisco Bay Oakland International Airport (27.6 miles).

- Is Nobu Hotel Palo Alto family-friendly?

Absolutely. Children under 12 years of age stay free with an accompanying adult and cribs are available upon request at no additional charge.
