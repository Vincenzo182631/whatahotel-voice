# Dine Nobu-Style in Palo Alto

An iconic menu of signature dishes and local specialties crafted by world-renowned Chef Nobu Matsuhisa. A design-forward atmosphere rooted in an unmistakable sense of place. Thoughtful, attentive service infused with Omotenashi, the Japanese spirit of hospitality. Dining is at the heart of the Nobu Hotel Palo Alto experience.

Book Nobu Palo Alto

Contact Us

## Nobu Palo Alto

The Nobu Palo Alto menu is defined by the inspired flavors and innovative dishes mastered by Chef Nobu Matsuhisa. Enjoy signature dishes in the dining room or at the exclusive sushi bar—including Black Cod Miso and Yellowtail Sashimi—alongside offerings unique to Palo Alto, including California-inspired plates and exclusive sakes.

Book a Table View Menus Learn More

## Nobu Lobby Bar

The laid-back and lively Lobby Bar is the perfect prelude to an exceptional dining experience or conclusion to a night well-spent. This lounge-style space offers a vast selection of rice wines and favorite Nobu cocktails alongside the full Nobu Palo Alto dinner menu. The special Tanoshi Hour menu is available daily during snack hours, 3:00 pm to 5:00 pm.

Learn More

## Nobu Brunch

Join us for brunch and enjoy chef-crafted specials like our Cured Scottish Salmon Shokupan Benedict and Uni & Caviar Chirashi with freshly grated Botarga. Dine al fresco in our Japanese-inspired garden, restaurant patio, or dining room.

Saturday & Sunday

11:00 am – 2:00 pm

## Afternoon Tea

Zen ambiance is paired with Ikaati Tea in our Japanese-inspired garden. Enjoy light bites including Blinis with Crème Fraiche, Egg Salad Dry Miso & Chives Lobster Toast, Shokupan Milk Bread, Lobster Salad, Madeleines, Profiteroles Pâte De Fruit, and seasonal fruit.

Saturday & Sunday

12:00 PM – 3:30 PM
