## EXPLORE

24/7 Fitness Center

Discover a fitness hub that will accommodate your training routine how and when you want it. Our 24-hour fitness center will keep you on top of your game with high-tech cardio and strengthening equipment, a customized fitness plan and a personal trainer available on-site.

Dining

Grand Hyatt Manila offers several exciting dining experiences, including three unique concept restaurants, a timeless bar and casual dining options that will ignite your palate.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

Grand Club

Located on the 57th floor of one of the highest skyscrapers in Manila, the Grand Club provides unparalleled city views of the metro. Guests with exclusive suite and club floor access can enjoy a wide range of amenities, from all-day refreshments to complimentary one-hour use of the boardroom.

Swimming Pool

Luxuriate in an urban oasis on the 6th floor of Grand Hyatt Manila. Step outdoors for a dip in the heated pool amidst the vibrant city of Taguig. The pool is open daily from 6:00 AM–8:00 PM.

Illume Spa

Experience expertly choreographed spa sessions to rekindle your wellbeing. The Illume Spa offers relaxing treatments that blend culture and indigenous traditions with the latest science and technology—all conducted in private treatment rooms with ensuite bathrooms. The spa is open daily from 11:00 AM–11:00 PM

## EXPLORE MANILA

The capital of the Philippines encompasses 12 towns in Manila Bay, divided by the Pasig River. Manila features a mélange of historical sites and contemporary cultural delights highlighted by a historic walled city called Intramuros, containing Fort Santiago, San Agustín Church, Plaza de Roma and contemporary art galleries. The city offers guests a range of experiences, including shops, parks, museums, churches and nightlife destinations.
