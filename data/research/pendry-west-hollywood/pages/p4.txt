# Welcome to Pendry Mexico City

## Introducing Pendry Mexico City

Some cities set the bar. And some redefine it.

Introducing Pendry Mexico City, an all-new destination from Pendry Hotels & Resorts, perched in the epicenter of Mexico City’s unparalleled landscape of culture, contemporary style and inimitable intrigue. This is a city where wandering is welcome. Where art is found in each angle and every element. And where the warmth and spirit of Mexico’s defining personality is richly on display, day to night.

Set in the Roma Norte District, Pendry Mexico City will bring its signature hospitality and design to the globally revered destination with 114 guestrooms and suites alongside 20 private Pendry Residences, designed in partnership with local architects, Central de Arquitectura and IZ Arquitectos, with interiors by AVROKO. Beyond the guestroom, guests and residents will explore multiple signature food & beverage locales, including a morning-to-evening neighborhood café, a classic Bar Pendry, a chef-driven destination restaurant with a rooftop terrace and lounge. When leisure is a priority, Pendry Mexico City caters to it in style with a rooftop pool and sundeck, a world-class Spa Pendry and a state-of-the-art fitness center. And when business is on the agenda, both indoor and outdoor meeting and special event spaces feature the brand’s signature event planning design and services.

All designed to not just embody the unique and stunning beauty of Mexico City. But to celebrate it.

Welcome to Pendry Mexico City. Coming in 2027.
