# Welcome to Pendry Barbados

## Introducing Pendry Barbados

Introducing Pendry Barbados. The first international destination from Pendry Hotels & Resorts, this island oasis reflects a new chapter for Pendry’s signature sensibilities, bringing contemporary, comfortable luxury to a stunning Caribbean setting in St. Peter, on the country’s coveted west coast. This is where a blanket of white sand, the sea’s kaleidoscope of azure blues and the exquisite beauty of Barbados’ warm, welcoming culture surround you at every step. Pendry Barbados will feature 80 newly designed beachfront guestrooms, a private marina, multiple signature dining concepts, Spa Pendry, Paintbox Kids’ Club and more, all alongside the debut of Pendry Residences Barbados.

An intimate island resort where adventure inspires, where nature envelops and where the best of Pendry is sure to impress.
