# Welcome to Pendry Punta Mita

## Introducing Pendry Punta Mita

A distinctly discerning new getaway destination capturing the natural essence of Mexico’s stunning shoreline through a contemporary, awe-inspiring lens. Within, 125 guestrooms will sit alongside six signature dining concepts, a world-class Spa Pendry, outdoor and beach activities, a children’s program, 32 Pendry Residences and more, all perched on a lush, 30-acre landscape steps from one of the best surf breaks in Riviera Nayarit. A contemporary coastal haven layered with comfort and style, set along the water’s edge where soft sands meet the inimitable Mexican sea. All our favorite things in one unforgettable place.

Welcome to Punta Mita.
