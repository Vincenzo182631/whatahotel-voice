Explore Indonesia’s Attractions From Central Jakarta
Experiences
As Indonesia’s capital city, Jakarta welcomes visitors with an exciting array of luxury shopping, dining and entertainment. Yet the magic of this enigmatic city can be traced back to its days as an outpost for the spice trade, when travelers from around the world passed through its streets. Today, visitors continue to arrive at this cosmopolitan destination where colonial influences from Portugal, Spain, England and the Netherlands are seamlessly integrated into the rich legacy of regional and indigenous traditions.
Signature Experiences
The Spice Trail
Discover the art of crafting Jamu, Indonesia’s time-honored herbal tonic made from fresh treasured local ingredients such as kunyit (tumeric) for its warming properties and kencur (sand ginger) for its energizing qualities. Guided by our jamu-making experts, you will create their own revitalizing elixir to enjoy poolside and take home a keepsake Jamu bottle.
Healing Through Heritage
Designed to release tension, improve circulation and restore harmony, embark on a restorative wellness ritual inspired by Indonesia’s rich spice heritage. Handcraft your own personalized body scrub and indulge in a Balinese massage experience.
Echoes of Batavia
Journey back in time to Batavia, once the administrative heart of the Dutch East Indies where colonial elegance continues to echo through picturesque colonial architecture. Don traditional attire as you journey through the renowned museums of the city’s colonial, artistic, cultural and economic heritage.
Threads of Heritage
Step into an immersive experience at Jakarta’s Textile Museum, where the timeless artistry of Batik, one of the nation’s most iconic textiles, comes to life. Journey through the museum, learn the cultural identity behind each motif, before creating your own piece.
Tea Trails of Puncak
Venture into the lush highlands of Puncak and immerse yourself in the tranquil beauty of Gunung Mas Tea Plantation. Explore the tea-making process, from leaf to teacup, and finish the day with a thoughtfully curated set dinner at Lobo.
Coffee Trails of Jakarta
Embark on a walking tour of Jakarta’s coffee heritage, through the charming neighborhoods of Cikini and Jatinegara. This experience offers a sensory journey from traditional manual brewing techniques to curated cupping sessions.
