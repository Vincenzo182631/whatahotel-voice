Dining To Delight the Senses
Dining
The Ritz-Carlton Jakarta, Mega Kuningan invites you to savor exceptional dining at Asia Restaurant, Lobo, and Ozone. At Asia, watch chefs bring Pan-Asian and Western flavors to life in an open kitchen, while Lobo offers a refined indoor and outdoor setting where global cuisines are thoughtfully crafted. At our Jakarta cocktail bar, Ozone, unwind with refreshing beverages and light bites, complemented by Golfzon, a state-of-the-art indoor golf simulator that offers an exclusive leisure experience in the heart of the city.
Lobo & Juno x Banquet of Hoshena
First time in Indonesia, explore the epitome of culinary excellence as Lobo & Juno x Banquet of Hoshena brings you an exceptional 3D dining experience. Indulge in a culinary adventure that defies the limits of your imagination.

Experience elevated dining at Asia Restaurant, where bold flavors and remarkable variety come together in an immersive culinary journey. Home to one of the city’s largest buffet selections, our Jakarta hotel restaurant offers lavish lunch and dinner buffets, as well as our award-winning weekend brunch, each featuring an exceptional gourmet spread.

Asia Restaurant Highlights
The Grand Weekend Brunch
Embark every weekend on a culinary journey with our exquisite buffet, featuring a delectable array of seafood, our new prime meats selection, and a variety of fine cheeses. Indulge in a tantalizing assortment of expertly prepared dishes crafted by our talented chefs at our luxury restaurant in Jakarta.
Surf & Turf
Indulge in the perfect harmony of land and sea with our Surf and Turf special, featuring expertly prepared cuts of succulent steak paired with the finest fresh seafood for an elevated dining experience.
Asia Restaurant Signatures
Celebrate the essence of Indonesian cuisine with our signature Wagyu Bakso and Nasi Goreng, thoughtfully crafted by our culinary team with premium ingredients and authentic flavors. A refined interpretation of timeless favorites, offering an elevated dining experience inspired by Indonesia’s rich culinary heritage.
Lobo Highlights
Weekend Brunch
Every Saturday and Sunday, indulge in an abundant semi-buffet brunch showcasing fresh seafood on ice, soulful signatures, and chef-curated specialties, complemented by elegant pass-arounds including our signature paella.
Artisan Luncheon
Elevate your midday dining with our Artisan Luncheon, offering a refined four-course Western or Indonesian set menu, available Monday through Friday.
Deconstructed Afternoon Tea
Reimagine afternoon tea at Lobo, where you are invited to curate your own experience by selecting from an array of thoughtfully crafted sweet and savory creations.
Ozone Highlights
Sports Bar with Golfzon
Experience the energy of a vibrant sports bar with the excitement of Golfzon. Enjoy a virtual golf experience with friends, paired with refreshing beverages, signature cocktails, and elevated bites.
Happy Hour
Unwind with a curated selection of cocktails and bites in the dynamic and vibrant setting of Ozone. Available daily from 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM, enjoy an elevated experience crafted for moments of connection and indulgence.
Ladies Night
Gather and experience Ladies Night at Ozone, featuring curated drinks and an inviting ambiance designed for memorable moments. Join us every Thursday, Friday & Saturday from 6:30 PM – 8:00 PM.
Dining Experiences
Discover fine dining beyond breakfast, lunch, and dinner with chef-led experiences, wine tastings, private dining, brunches, and other special events at the hotel.
