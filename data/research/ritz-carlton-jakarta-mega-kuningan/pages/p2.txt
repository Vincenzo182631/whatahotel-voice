An Expansive and Luxurious Retreat in Jakarta
Suites & Rooms
Indulge in the ultimate experience at The Ritz-Carlton Jakarta, Mega Kuningan, where spacious, impeccably furnished accommodations set the tone for a luxury getaway. Our Jakarta luxury accommodation offers city views, with the exception of The Grand Spa Villa, which provides a private soaking tub and access to the pool.
The Club Upgrade
Recharge and luxuriate in our Grand Room with instant access to The Club Lounge, offering inspiring culinary presentations on the daily.
The Ritz-Carlton
Club Experience
Overlooking the skyline of Jakarta business district from the 26th floor, The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge elevates the luxury hotel experience by offering comfort and exclusivity with personalized service and attention to detail which is the signature of The Ritz-Carlton. The Club Lounge features meeting rooms, personalized business services, and continuous culinary offerings along with many other exclusive privileges.

Club Lounge Tradition
At The Ritz-Carlton, dress code is an important tradition. We highly appreciate if the guests are dressed in smart casual attire at all times. Note: Club Lounge access is not applicable for room upgrades.
Exclusive Privileges
Experience many exclusive benefits tailored to enhance your stay with us such as personalized check-in and check-out at the Club Lounge or in-room, continuous food and beverage offerings throughout the day, complimentary meeting room usage at the Club Lounge for 2 (two) hours per day, complimentary 2 (two) pieces of pressing per day, workstations with complimentary WI-FI available throughout, and more.
A Sanctuary Above the City
Designed as a tranquil escape above the city, the Club Lounge provides a calm and comfortable setting for guests to unwind, work, or reconnect throughout the day. For the third guest enjoying Club Lounge Privileges, a supplement of IDR 1,000,000 ++ per person per night will be applied for adults; and IDR 500,000 ++ per person per night for children from 5 to 12 years old.
