## KYOYAMATO – Kyoto’s famed Michelin Two Stars Japanese Restaurant

▪ Kyoyamato is closed on Wednesdays, though this may vary depending on reservations.　▪ Please note that Kyoyamato operates independently and is not eligible for World of Hyatt points.　▪ For exclusive experiences available to hotel guests, kindly inquire with the concierge.　▪ Breakfast served at the hotel is available daily without closures.

Nestled alongside Park Hyatt Kyoto and thoughtfully integrated into the guest experience, Kyoyamato is a prestigious ryotei with a history dating back to 1877. This storied culinary institution continues to preserve and celebrate the essence of Kyoto’s seasonal cuisine and hospitality. Set within a group of heritage buildings designated as cultural assets by Kyoto City, the property offers sweeping views of traditional view of Kyoto city with the iconic Yasaka Pagoda. Guests are invited to experience the artistry of Japanese hospitality through seasonal décor and heartfelt service “Omotenashi”. Savor the elegance and refinement of its Michelin two-star cuisine—an exquisite expression of traditional Japanese culinary arts.
