# Luxury Hotel Rooms & Suites \| Park Hyatt Kyoto

# Accommodations

Check-In3:00 PM

Park Hyatt Kyoto is comprised of 70 rooms, including 9 suites, designed to ensure you spend timeless moments at our luxury guesthouse. Kyoto's craftsmanship is evident in every corner of our rooms and suites including the interior crafted from fragrant tamo wood, contemporary art created by local artists, and the Japanese gardens on display throughout the property.
World of Hyatt Globalist members or guests staying in a suite can enjoy champagne to their heart's content at The Living Room on the first floor daily between 5pm and 6pm. Begin an unforgettable evening while sipping bubbles amidst the flickering lights of the fireplace.
