Absolutely unforgettable zen experience in amazing Higashiyama location! From the moment you enter the driveway, instantly you know you’re somewhere special. Kudos to all the Park Hyatt Kyoto staff who do so much to ensure an amazing stay!

### Leaving Japan on a high note!

Park Hyatt is a great gem in the Gion district. Situated in a charming and picturesque area it was a wonderful stay for the last leg of our Japan visit. It is a bit farther from the main parts of Kyoto where restaurants were open later for dinner, but everything was a quick cab ride away. For lunch there are some great hidden restaurants to explore which were walkable from the hotel. There were also some great cultural sites within walking distance of the hotel. The hotel itself was fantastic, with very comfortable and modern rooms and great amenities. Also loved the great views as we made our way to and from our room, but what stands out the most is the impeccable and friendly service. Ending our Japan visit at the PH was perfect as it gave us the opportunity to unwind, and relax before heading home. Highly recommend staying here!
