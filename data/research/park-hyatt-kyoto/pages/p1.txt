## A Luxury Guesthouse Located in the Heart of Higashiyama

Nestled along the storied cobblestones of Ninenzaka in Higashiyama, Park Hyatt Kyoto is a luxury guesthouse where tradition and modern elegance harmonize. Set on historic temple grounds, the hotel offers sweeping views of Yasaka Pagoda and the timeless machiya townscape, with 70 guestrooms and nine suites embodying Kyoto’s quiet sophistication. Guests may savor Michelin two-star kaiseki cuisine and tea ceremonies at the seventh-generation Kyoyamato, enjoy contemporary dining and cocktails at the Signature Restaurant & Bar, or discover curated cultural journeys such as Zen meditation and private temple access. A serene Spa & Fitness renews body and spirit, while weddings and events are elevated into unforgettable celebrations inspired by Kyoto’s cultural grace.

## Accommodations

## Discover Park Hyatt Kyoto

Champagne Hour

World of Hyatt Globalist members as well as guests staying in one of the hotel’s nine suites can enjoy free-flowing Champagne in The Living Room on the 1st floor daily from 5–6 p.m. Begin an unforgettable evening while sipping bubbles amid the flickering lights of the fireplace.

Wellness

The Spa and Fitness at Park Hyatt Kyoto is a tranquil oasis that is available for use exclusively by hotel guests. The private treatment room with relaxation lounge soothes mental and physical fatigue, giving way to a calm and relaxed state. Revitalize your body and soul while spending precious moments relaxing in a space inspired by Kyoto.

About Park Hyatt Kyoto

Park Hyatt Kyoto is a luxury guesthouse for discerning global travelers who are seeking a deeply cultural experience. Laying claim to a landmark location within Kyoto's largest preserved traditional building district, the hotel offers awe inspiring views of Kyoto's cityscape and a guest experience that blends authentic Kyoto culture with the elegance of Park Hyatt.

Experiences

At Park Hyatt Kyoto, we believe that luxury is personal. Entrust our knowledgeable concierge team to curate a personalized experience to make your stay a memorable one. Our tailor-made experiences offer a unique window into Kyoto's history and traditional culture, wellness experiences that heal the mind and body, as well as exclusive activities only available to guests of Park Hyatt Kyoto. Be inspired and discover your own personal Kyoto.

Wander Ichinen-zaka, a traditional street by the hotel’s main entrance, or Ninen-zaka, for picturesque photos and souvenirs.
