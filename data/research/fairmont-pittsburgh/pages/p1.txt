## Your _luxury_ destination in the heart of Downtown Pittsburgh

Located at the heart of the city's business, theater, and retail hub, and within walking distance to Market Square, Fairmont Pittsburgh offers distinctive guest services and accommodations in a luxury setting. With our newly refreshed guestrooms and suites, breathtaking views of PNC Park, the restored downtown, and the Pittsburgh skyline, our guests have an immediate sense of being at the center of it all.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fairmont Pittsburgh pet-friendly?

Fairmont Pittsburgh welcomes pets of all sizes for a nightly fee of $60. Pets must remain on a leash at all times and may not be left unattended in guest rooms. Any damage caused by pets will be the guest's responsibility.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Pittbsurgh?

Check-In time is 4:00 p.m. and Check-Out time is noon.

Is parking available at Fairmont Pittsburgh?

Valet parking is available for an additional charge. Guests may also take advantage of our on-site self-parking garage, available at an additional fee.

Is there a concierge service at Fairmont Pittsburgh?

The Guest Services/Concierge is available from 7:00 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. in the Main Lobby of the hotel. Contact them directly at pgh.concierge@fairmont.com. The Front Desk is available 24/7.

Photography at Fairmont Pittsburgh

To ensure an exceptional experience for all guests and to preserve the ambiance of our hotel, Fairmont Pittsburgh has established the following photography guidelines.

**Engagement Photography**

Engagement photography sessions are not permitted on hotel property.

**Wedding Photography**

Wedding, elopement, and vow renewal photography are exclusively reserved for couples celebrating their event at Fairmont Pittsburgh. Photography for weddings or ceremonies not hosted at the hotel is not permitted.

**Personal Photography**

Guests are welcome to take casual personal photos throughout the hotel during their stay. We invite you to share your Fairmont Pittsburgh experience by tagging @FairmontPittsburgh and @Fl2pgh on social media.

**Commercial Photography & Filming**

All commercial photography, videography, content creation, and media productions require prior approval from Fairmont Pittsburgh. Requests must be submitted in advance and are subject to availability and hotel approval.

For questions or to inquire about photography or filming permissions, please contact the Public Relations team at _PGHMarketing@Fairmont.com_.
