### Dining at Every Level

Savour seasonal dining beneath the hotel’s soaring atrium at Alluvial Restaurant, discover curated wines and classic cocktails at Bluestone Wine Lounge, enjoy contemporary Italian cuisine at Ronnie’s or indulge in the cherished ritual of Afternoon Tea.

### Club InterContinental

Designed for guests seeking elevated comfort and personalised attention, the club lounge offers exclusive daily breakfast, indulgent afternoon tea, evening canapés, all day refreshments and bespoke service in an elegant setting. A sophisticated ambience where every detail is thoughtfully considered.

Learn more

### Local Attractions & Experiences

Positioned on prestigious Collins Street offers seamless access to the city’s most popular experiences and celebrated attractions within easy reach. Wander Melbourne’s renowned laneways and luxury boutiques, immerse in the surrounds of art at National Gallery of Victoria or attend sporting and entertainment events at MCG and Marvel Stadium.

Learn more

### Signature Suites

Showcasing the grandeur of the hotel’s heritage and the sophistication of contemporary luxury, our Signature Suites offer an exceptional residential experience with expansive living spaces, bespoke furnishings and elevated comforts. Thoughtfully designed for the discerning traveller, each suite delivers a truly memorable Melbourne experience.

Learn more

### Heated Indoor Pool & Hot Tub with Skyline Views

Overlooking Melbourne’s iconic skyline, our heated indoor pool and hot tub offer a refined retreat above the city. Complemented by a Scandinavian-inspired sauna and a light-filled fitness centre, this elevated space invites guests to relax, recharge and maintain their wellness routine while enjoying sweeping urban views.

## FAQs for InterContinental Melbourne

Does InterContinental Melbourne have a pool?

InterContinental Melbourne has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

Swim and soak in our heated indoor. With sweeping views through the floor-to-ceiling windows over the CBD and out to the bay, our rooftop pool is the perfect place to relax. Pool and gym hours are 06:30 until 22:30 daily.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Melbourne?

At InterContinental Melbourne, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 50 AUD and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 80 AUD .

Valet parking is $80.00 /vehicle/night, multiple entry. Height limit for vehicles is 2 meters, guests are able to leave their cars up to 8PM after check-out, subject to availability; Self-parking is $50 /vehicle/night, at 15 William Street, single entry.

Parking fee: 50 AUD per day

In and out privileges

Parking for registered guests only

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Melbourne?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Melbourne.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Melbourne for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Melbourne have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Melbourne has 2 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Melbourne?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Melbourne?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Melbourne.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Melbourne?

Check-in at InterContinental Melbourne is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Melbourne offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Melbourne offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Melbourne from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Melbourne is conveniently located near Melbourne International Airport Tullamarine (MEL), approximately 12.43 miles away (20 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Melbourne?

InterContinental Melbourne is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Stroll the Royal Botanical Gardens
- Explore Melbourne Cricket Ground
- Visit the Koalas at Melbourne Zoo
- Shop in Grandeur at Emporium Melbourne
- Arts Centre Melbourne
- Marvel Stadium

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Melbourne puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
