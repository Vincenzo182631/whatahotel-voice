# InterContinental MelbourneDining

Savour Melbourne's diverse culinary spirit at InterContinental Melbourne The Rialto. From vibrant restaurants to a sophisticated bar, enjoy local, seasonal produce crafted with flair - an authentic taste of the city's rich cultural fusion.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

1. Dining at Every Level

### Dining at Every Level

Savour seasonal dining beneath the hotel’s soaring atrium at Alluvial Restaurant, discover curated wines and classic cocktails at Bluestone Wine Lounge, enjoy contemporary Italian cuisine at Ronnie’s or indulge in the cherished ritual of Afternoon Tea.

## Alluvial

Just a few steps away from Melbourne's iconic Flinders Lane, you'll find our Alluvial Restaurant. The beautiful light filled atrium space is perfect for a luxuriously lazy lunch, a business meeting or a sophisticated evening meal under the stars. Sample modern cuisine with the best local produce, catering to breakfast, and a la carte style lunches and dinners. Melbourne's electric fine dining scene offers a startling array of the world's greatest cuisines.

## Ronnie's

Helmed by chef Matthew Butcher, who cut his culinary chops under industry heavyweights Gordon Ramsay, Shannon Bennett, and Ryan Clift, Ronnie's named after Butcher's father is inspired by leisurely dinners around the table. Rustic chic, the restaurant is most known for its pasta and wine pairings. Share plates of Pasta Fusilli with Spicy Alla Vodka and Whipped Ricotta, Grandma's Bolognese gnocchi, or Risotto Milanese with scallops.
