# InterContinental MelbourneDestination

With a prestigious Collins Street address in Melbourne's city centre, our hotel puts you in the heart of the best shopping and dining in Melbourne. Wind your way through laneways or hop on a tram and explore Australia’s arts and sports capital.

1. Local Attractions & Experiences

### Local Attractions & Experiences

Positioned on prestigious Collins Street offers seamless access to the city’s most popular experiences and celebrated attractions within easy reach. Wander Melbourne’s renowned laneways and luxury boutiques, immerse in the surrounds of art at National Gallery of Victoria or attend sporting and entertainment events at MCG and Marvel Stadium.

Learn more

## Things to do

### Stroll the Royal Botanical Gardens

Melbourne’s stunning inner-city oasis, the Royal Botanical Gardens, is home to over 10,000 plant species. Wander past tranquil lakes and majestic trees, relax on the expansive lawns and delight in the stunning kaleidoscope of flowers.

Learn more

### Explore Melbourne Cricket Ground

Brunton Avenue, Richmond, VIC 3002

Cricket is Australia’s largest, oldest, and most popular sport, and the premier place to observe it is the Melbourne Cricket Ground. The famed sporting venue has hosted the World Cup, Olympic Games and ICC Cricket World Cup. It has also been host to blockbuster concerts and was the site of a Catholic Mass when Pope John Paul II visited Melbourne in the late 1980s. Also home to the Australian Football League, known as the AFL and has regular football games from March to September.

Learn more

### Visit the Koalas at Melbourne Zoo

Elliott Avenue, Parkville, VIC 3052

Walkable from city centre, the colourful Melbourne Zoo houses over 250 species from around the world. Wander the lush Asian and African rainforests, the Australian bush to see native wildlife including koalas, kangaroos, wombats, and emus. Continue the journey under water for a glimpse of Victoria’s own iconic little penguins and Australian fur seals.

Learn more

### Shop in Grandeur at Emporium Melbourne

287 Lonsdale Street, Melbourne, VIC 3000

Shopping takes on new heights — eight stories — at Emporium Melbourne, the city’s premier shopping experience. Opened in 2014, the Emporium is noted for its designer flagship stores and grand 1920s architecture. Savour the aromas and tastes from the stunning cafe court.

Learn more

### Arts Centre Melbourne

100 St Kilda Road, Southbank, VIC 3004

Our concierge will happily procure tickets to Australia’s largest performing arts venue, and even arrange a backstage tour. The Centre’s high-calibre professional presentations include classical concerts, live theatre, ballet, opera, comedy, circuses and magic shows.

Learn more

### Marvel Stadium

740 Bourke Street, Docklands, VIC 3008

Marvel Stadium is a multi-purpose venue located in the Docklands. The Stadium is designed to cater to major sporting, entertainment, corporate or casual events - celebrate in true Melbourne style.

Learn more
