### Weekend away and. Birthday celebration

3 days ago

We arrived on Friday and were delighted to receive an upgrade to a one-bedroom suite. We had originally booked a Club Room Suite and were also redeeming one of our Ambassador Weekend Night benefits to celebrate my partner’s special occasion.

The reception team were welcoming and helpful, and the concierge service was excellent\\. Unfortunately, the positive first impression quickly faded once we entered our room, Room 112 Fitzroy room.

The room felt dingy and dated, with tired looking furniture, stained sofas, and a work desk with badly bubbled surfaces that looked as though it needed replacing. While we appreciated the jacuzzi bathtub, the overall quality of the room and amenities fell well below what we would expect from an InterContinental property. The bathrobes felt cheap, and the slippers were particularly disappointing for a five-star hotel.

We also decided to enjoy the minibar champagne and wine to celebrate my partner’s special occasion. However, finding only one glass of champagne provided in the room was disappointing, especially when the 360 ml bottle of Mumm champagne was charged at $95. It felt like we were being charged a premium without receiving the level of service expected to match it.

The common areas were equally disappointing. The hallway leading to the bedrooms had damaged wall skirting and appeared poorly maintained\\. It was concerning that these areas did not seem to have been properly cleaned throughout our weekend stay. Even the bucket used for the complimentary sparkling wine was left in the hallway for the entire duration of our visit.

Unfortunately, the sauna and swimming pool were also under construction, meaning we could not enjoy these facilities during our stay.

The breakfast was probably the most satisfying part of our experience, although it was fairly standard and nothing particularly memorable compared with other five-star hotels in Sydney and Melbourne. The Club Lounge food was mediocre, too. Some staff members were attentive, friendly, and professional, while others were less welcoming.

Overall, it is such a shame to see a beautiful hotel with so much potential let down by poor maintenance, tired furnishings, and inconsistent service. We expected a more luxurious and polished experience, particularly as a platinum elite ambassador member celebrating a special occasion.

Sadly, the hotel did not live up to the standards we associate with the InterContinental brand. With better upkeep, attention to detail, and more consistent service, this property could offer a much more memorable guest experience.

ProsLocation, recognition for ihg rewards club members

ConsSleep Quality, Room Cleanliness, Amenities

No, I do not recommend this hotel.
