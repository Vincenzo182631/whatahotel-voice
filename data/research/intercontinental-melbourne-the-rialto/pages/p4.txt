### Club InterContinental

Designed for guests seeking elevated comfort and personalised attention, the club lounge offers exclusive daily breakfast, indulgent afternoon tea, evening canapés, all day refreshments and bespoke service in an elegant setting. A sophisticated ambience where every detail is thoughtfully considered.

### Heated Indoor Pool & Hot Tub with Skyline Views

Overlooking Melbourne’s iconic skyline, our heated indoor pool and hot tub offer a refined retreat above the city. Complemented by a Scandinavian-inspired sauna and a light-filled fitness centre, this elevated space invites guests to relax, recharge and maintain their wellness routine while enjoying sweeping urban views.
