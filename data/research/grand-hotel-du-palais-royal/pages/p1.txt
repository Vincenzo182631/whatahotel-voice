# **Grand Hotel du Palais Royal,**   a 5-star hotel in the heart of Paris

* * *

Thanks to its prime location, at once historic and cultural, the Grand Hotel du Palais Royal can boast a number of unique advantages right in the heart of Paris.

Only a stone’s throw from the Palais Royal and its romantic formal gardens, next door to the Louvre and the Tuileries Gardens, it is nothing less than a haven of peace in a bustling capital city.

## Rooms & Suites

* * *

Particularly bright, with soft colours and well thought-out comfort, the 59 rooms and suites of the hotel offer the splendour of the Palais Royal.

Some rooms have a balcony, allowing a more direct approach to the Parisian atmosphere.

## Café 52

* * *

The Grand Hôtel du Palais Royal is opening the Café 52 restaurant, which offers a selection of organic, balanced and seasonal dishes throughout the day, in an intimate and warm atmosphere. Maxime Raab's menu is geared towards well-being,respecting the seasonality of the products.

The vast terrace on the confidential and quiet Place de Valois is an invitation to unwind.

The bar, an intimate setting with warm candle light, invites its guests and Parisians to discover healthy cocktails, vegetal lattes and finger food with a bold signature.

Every Sunday from 12:30 to 2:30 pm, the Grand Hôtel du Palais Royal offers a healthy, gourmet champagne brunch.

## Spa & fitness

The Holidermie Spa offers expert facial and body treatments combined with exclusive techniques and carefully formulated cosmetics.

The hot tub, the Star Hammam and the fitness area are all places of well-being where relaxation and serenity are combined to allow time to stand still.

For more information about the treatments, please feel free to contact the concierge directly: +33 1 42 96 72 21

## Events

The Café 52, its restaurant and its bar, and the Signature Suites can provide the facilities to host custom events. Symposiums, meetings, press conferences, private events (lunches, dinners, cocktail parties) or other events can be planned on request.
