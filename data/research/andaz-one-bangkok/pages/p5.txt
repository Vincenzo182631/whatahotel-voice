- ### Outstanding

We made a last-minute booking for a two-night stay at Andaz One Bangkok, and I must say that the experience exceeded our expectations in several ways.
From the moment we arrived, we received a warm welcome from the bell team, followed by a smooth and efficient check-in. As a Hyatt Lifetime Globalist, I decided to use a Suite Upgrade Award to secure a room with a great view, which made the stay even more enjoyable.
The room was beautifully designed, immaculate, and clearly well maintained. A special mention goes to the housekeeping team for their attention to detail and the excellent standard of cleanliness.
Breakfast was another highlight, with an impressive variety of high-quality products and premium offerings. The selection was generous, and the overall quality made it a particularly enjoyable start to the day.
We also spent some time enjoying drinks at the rooftop bar, which turned out to be another pleasant surprise. The atmosphere and overall experience were well worth discovering. The gym facilities were equally appreciated and added another positive dimension to our stay.
Having experienced many Hyatt properties over the years, I particularly appreciate hotels that manage to combine thoughtful design, quality facilities, and genuine hospitality. Andaz One Bangkok certainly left a very positive impression.
What began as a last-minute booking turned into a memorable stay, and we will happily return in the near future. A property I would certainly recommend to fellow travellers looking for a stylish and enjoyable stay in Bangkok.
