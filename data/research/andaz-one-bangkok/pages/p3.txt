## Andaz Terrace

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

​​Andaz Terrace offers intimate indoor seating and a relaxing, spacious open-air terrace that feels as cozy and familiar as home, only with panoramic Bangkok skyline views. Whether lingering here over breakfast or a laidback Asian-inspired lunch, catching up with friends over course-by-course afternoon tea, unwinding with coffee and pastries, or enjoying Friday night drinks and live musics, it’s the perfect place to escape above the buzz and bask in the glow of the golden hour.  ​

## Jǐng

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

Jǐng means “view” in Chinese, a nod to the lush greenery of the neighborhood that accompanies every meal in our Chinese restaurant, where modern décor and the theater of an open kitchen create a stylish dining experience. Jǐng is led by our Chinese chefs, whose native understanding of authentic Chinese flavors is reflected in every creation. The menu showcases Cantonese and Sichuan cuisine, dim sum, fresh seafood and more. Private dining rooms are also available for exclusive occasions and celebrations.

## Piscari​

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

Piscari, a rooftop dining and bar destination on top of Andaz One Bangkok, brings to life the playfulness and energy of the Mediterranean coast in both its whimsical décor and menu from French chef Marc Vasseur who brings extensive experience from Michelin-acclaimed restaurants. Savor Mediterranean-inspired delights straight from the “Gourmet Library.” Beyond its lively flavors, Piscari sets the scene for celebrations big and small — with private dining rooms, party spaces, and live DJ beats that pulse with the city lights and sunset hues. Craving something cozier? Slip away into a hidden speakeasy for a more mellow, intimate vibe.
