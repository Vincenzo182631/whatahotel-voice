# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Valet parking is complimentary for hotel guests and those visiting the hotel.

Free

### Self-Parking

Andaz One Bangkok provides complimentary parking spaces for hotel guests and those visiting the hotel. The hotel is located within One Bangkok. The
recommended parking spots are at "The Storeys" zone (yellow zone), B2 – B3
floor, pillars A-D, 1-10.

Free

## Transportation

Getting here

Suvarnabhumi International Airport (BKK)31 kilometers31 kilometers

Andaz One Bangkok is located approximately 31 KM from Suvarnabhumi Airport, easily accessible via the Chaloem Maha Nakhon Expressway. Travel time is about 40 minutes.

Transportation Options

Hotel Limousine

Travel with ease in our BMW 5 Series or Mercedes-Benz Vito. Should you have any booking requests or enquiries, our hotel team would be pleased to assist.

Taxi

Guests can choose either a normal metered taxi or transportation offered by third-party providers such as Grab.

Bangkok Airport Rail Link

From Suvarnabhumi Airport, take the Airport Rail Link and get off at Phaya Thai station. Transfer to the MRT at Phetchaburi station and get off at Lumphini station.

Don Mueang International Airport (DMK)25 kilometers25 kilometers

Andaz One Bangkok is located approximately 25 KM from Don Mueang International Airport, easily accessible via the Uttaraphimuk Elevated Tollway (Don Mueang Tollway). Travel time is about 40 minutes.

Transportation Options

Hotel Limousine

Travel with ease in our BMW 5 Series or Mercedes-Benz Vito. Should you have any booking requests or enquiries, our hotel team would be pleased to assist.

Taxi

Guests can choose either a normal metered taxi or transportation offered by third-party providers such as Grab.

Getting around

Subway: Lumphini StationTHB 45.0045.00 Thai baht

Andaz One Bangkok is located just a few steps from Lumphini MRT station on the Blue Line. From Lumphini MRT station, take exit 1 and walk through One Bangkok to our hotel.

Shuttle: One Bangkok Shuttle Bus Free

One Bangkok offers a complimentary shuttle bus service between One Bangkok and the Ploenchit BTS station. The pick-up and drop-off area is on Level G - "The Storeys" zone.
