Fouquet’s Paris is synonymous with writers, movie stars, and glamorous gala dinners: a figurehead of Parisian elegance since 1899, where a legendary brasserie and refined hotel rub shoulders under one roof. Here on the Champs-Élysées, Jacques Garcia dreamt up a Palace designed with a family ho...me feel that strikes the balance between the spectacular and the intimate: a symphony of velvet, mahogany and crystal harmonize woven through with refined baroque salons and understated, elegant rooms. From its legendary brasserie to the historic bar and rooftop garden overlooking the capital, Fouquet’s Paris is a seamless patchwork of exquisite experiences. At the spa, let it all go in 750 m² dedicated to wellness, complete with pool, gym, sauna, hammam and ice room. A stay at Fouquet’s Paris offers up a little slice of luxury with a French twist, and the chance to immerse yourself in a vibrant celebration of culture, elegance and soul.See more

### What are the check-in and check-out times? What should I expect upon arrival?

**Check-in:**

Check-in is from 3:00 PM. Upon arrival at Hôtel Fouquet’s Paris, please present your ID and the credit card used during booking. Your card will not be charged at this time according to the chosen pricing policy. Should you arrive earlier, we’ll be happy to store your luggage until your room is ready.

**Check-out:**

Check-out is at 12:00 PM. If you wish to request a late check-out, please contact the front desk. This service is subject to availability.

### Can I request a late check-out?

Yes, late check-out is available upon request and subject to availability. Please contact the front desk to arrange it.

### What is the hotel’s cancellation policy?

The cancellation policy depends on the pricing policy chosen by the guest.

### Are pets allowed at Fouquet’s Paris?

Pets are welcome at our luxury hotel in Paris, subject to specific conditions. Dogs must be kept on a leash in public areas and should not be left unattended in the room. For safety reasons, our staff will not service rooms if your pet is alone inside. Additional fees may apply. Pets are not allowed in restaurants, bars, the spa, or the fitness center, except for certified assistance dogs.

### Are cribs or extra beds available?

Yes, cribs are available upon request at no extra charge. Extra beds can be arranged in certain room and suite categories, subject to availability and an additional fee. Please contact our reservations team for more information.

### Do you offer connecting or adjacent rooms?

Yes, Fouquet’s Paris offers connecting room options. Please contact our reservations team at contact-fouquets@groupebarriere.com to check availability and options.

### What dining options are available at Fouquet’s Paris?

Our luxury hotel in Paris offers several exceptional restaurant and bar experiences:

Brasserie Fouquet’s Paris: A legendary Paris restaurant wich revisits French brasserie classics with creativity and finesse.

Joy Restaurant: A verdant hideaway, ideal for a business or a romantic dinners in a serene garden setting.

Joy Bar: An elegant venue for cocktails, wines and digestifs in a cosy atmosphere.

L’Escadrille Bar: Iconic bar on the Champs-Élysées, with a cozy and elegant atmosphere.

Room Service: Available 24/7, offering a refined menu.

Please consult our website or contact the concierge for opening hours and reservations.

### Is room service available 24/7?

Yes, room service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
