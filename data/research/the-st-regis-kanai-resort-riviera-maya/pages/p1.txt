# Welcome to The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya

## Experience the beauty of the Riviera Maya

Discover an exceptional address at The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya, in Kanai, a resort in Mexico near Cancun and Playa del Carmen. Built on a natural mangrove reserve and inspired by the Sian Ka’an Reserve, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, our Riviera Maya resort hotel offers sweeping ocean views from a 620-acre setting on Mexico’s Yucatan Peninsula. The Pleiades constellation inspired our avant-garde architecture, floating above the mangrove canopy. Each of our 124 rooms, suites, and residences features dramatic views, bespoke furniture, and a private terrace or plunge pool. Indulge in curated culinary journeys, relax at the St. Regis Spa, and enjoy signature Butler Service for an unparalleled stay at this luxury resort in Playa del Carmen, Mexico.

### Sustainability

At The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya, we honor the inherent beauty of our natural surroundings through an architectural design that minimizes environmental impact and intertwines harmoniously with the mangrove ecosystem. Our dedication to sustainability is reflected through an initiative, led by an expert biologist, which encompasses environmental conservation, responsible waste management, energy efficiency, ecological education, and a strong plastic reduction policy. Our collective efforts aim towards a new standard in environmentally conscious luxury.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 11.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya is Cancun International Airport (CUN). CUN is located approximately 28.7 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
