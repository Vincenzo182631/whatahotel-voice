## Spa Specials

Celestial Journey - 2hr 50 minutes. Experience a celestial connection. Immerse yourself into a journey of relaxation with our Mayan - infused rituals: Under Sky Bathing, Celestial Energy, Secret Garden.

### Celestial Energy

Inspired by Mayan cosmology, a mystical notion of time and creation linked to the firmament. A journey full of aromas and colors designed to connect with the light of Ixchel (the moon) and the energy of the Motz (Pleiades) to restore your natural cycle and unwind your soul.

### Secret Garden

Honoring the traditional Mayan healers’ knowledge on herbs, this ritual will walk you through our secret garden where you will be able to choose from and personalize your own wellbeing recipe.

### Under Sky Bathing

The perfect under sky bathing ritual. Combining hydrating bath salts, herbs and flowers from the Mayan botanic culture and a head and neck massage, makes a relaxing experience to indulge your senses.

### Xunan Kab

A full body treatment inspired on the sacred honey that Mayan offered to their deities during rituals or ceremonies. A soothing and moisturizing treatment that combines honey and coconut ingredients, simply a sweet Mayan legacy.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya offers a 8:00 AM-7:00 PM fitness center available for a fee to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Family Pool (Outdoor)Serenity Pool (Outdoor)Main Pool (Outdoor)Whirlpool

Does The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya have an on-site spa?

Yes, The St. Regis Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya?

3D Museum of Wonders, Xplor Park, Museo Frida Kahlo Playa Del Carmen, Dolphins Beach, Parque La Ceiba and more are nearby The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya.
