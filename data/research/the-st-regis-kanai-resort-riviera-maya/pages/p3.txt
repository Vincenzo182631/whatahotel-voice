## Dining

When deciding where to eat in Riviera Maya, our hotel, The St. Regis Kanai Resort, offers a range of unique culinary destinations and bespoke offerings across its 5 distinctive restaurants. From International, French, and Mediterranean refined cuisines to the enticing Latin and Mexican flavors enhanced with local touches, epicurean discovery awaits.

An Extended Expression of Taste at Kanai

Beyond the boundaries of our resort, extraordinary dining experiences unfold at The Riviera Maya EDITION Kanai: where modern Mexican expression and effortless sophistication converge. Discover an inspired extension of the Kanai experience—designed for those who seek flavor without limits.

## Signature Ritual

Experience the Astor tradition of our Afternoon Tea at The Library with our select blends and finest pastries.

### A Reimaged Classic

Delight in one of our inviting rituals, the Bloody Mary which debuted in 1934 at The St. Regis New York. At our bars and restaurants in Riviera Maya, savor our iconic Kanai Mary made with exquisite local spices and ingredients.

### St. Regis Bar

This intimate venue features unparalleled views of the night sky, offers the Kanai Mary. It also features an expansive mural inspired by Maya astronomer-priests who looked to the heavens for guidance.

### The Library

The perfect back drop to experience St. Regis’s signature ritual of afternoon tea and enjoy a tasting of Mexican chocolate.

### Chaya

A beachfront restaurant in Riviera Maya featuring a menu that blends local ingredients with culinary traditions rooted in the Eastern Mediterranean.

## Exceptional Mixology

Redefine your mixology expectations with the exquisite options available to delight at Toro Bar.
