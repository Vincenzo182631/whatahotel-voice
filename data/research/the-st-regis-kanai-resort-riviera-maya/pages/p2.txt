# Quintana Roo's Bespoke Service

## Accommodations

The St. Regis Kanai Resort, Riviera Maya offers breathtaking views from each of its 124 guestrooms, 19 suites, and the Presidential Suite, promising an exceptional stay defined by exquisite comfort and unparalleled service.

### A Commitment to Excellence

Guests of all room categories will also have access to the St. Regis Signature Butler Service, a signature offering for more than a century.

### The Most Glamorous Getaway

Design elements in our Riviera Maya suites draw inspiration from ancient Mayan artistic manifestations. Rich materials evoking the surrounding elements are found in hanging mirrors, ceramic light pendants, and intricate walnut wood-carved headboards.
