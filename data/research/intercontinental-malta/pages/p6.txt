## FAQs for InterContinental Malta

Does InterContinental Malta have a pool?

InterContinental Malta has 1 indoor and 2 outdoor pools.

Indoor Heated Pool

SKYBEACH Outdoor Rooftop Swimming Pool

Narcis Outdoor Swimming Pool

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Malta?

At InterContinental Malta, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 17 EUR .

Parking fee: 17 EUR per day

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Bus, Trailer, Truck

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Malta?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Malta.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Malta for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Malta have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Malta has 6 restaurants.

Is there free breakfast available at InterContinental Malta?

No, InterContinental Malta does not offer complimentary breakfast.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Malta?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Malta.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Malta?

Check-in at InterContinental Malta is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Malta offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Malta offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Malta from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Malta is conveniently located near MALTA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (MLA), approximately 7.46 miles away (12 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Malta?

InterContinental Malta is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Republic Street in Valletta - 4.97 miles away (8 kilometers)
- The Three Cities - 6.71 miles away (10.8 kilometers)
- Diving - 14.23 miles away (22.9 kilometers)
- Harbour Cruise - 12.43 miles away (20 kilometers)
- Marsaxlokk - 8.26 miles away (13.3 kilometers)
- Ħaġar Qim and Mnajdra Temple Complex - 10.94 miles away (17.6 kilometers)
- Blue Grotto - 10.56 miles away (17 kilometers)
- Gozo and Comino - 23.86 miles away (38.4 kilometers)
- Hypogeum of Ħal-Saflieni
- Casa Rocca Piccola - 4.72 miles away (7.6 kilometers)
- St John’s Co-Cathedral
- Mdina - 8.7 miles away (14 kilometers)
- Upper Barrakka Gardens
- San Anton Gardens - 4.6 miles away (7.4 kilometers)
- Luqa Airport, Malta (MLA)
- St. George's Bay/St. Julian's
- Blue Lagoon
- Valetta
