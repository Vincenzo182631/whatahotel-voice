### Outstanding Service!

13 hours ago

We thoroughly enjoyed our short stay at the InterContinental Malta and will especially remember the truly outstanding service provided by the entire team, from the front desk to the Club Lounge staff, who consistently went the extra mile. The recognition of my Elite status, the flexibility of an early check-in and late check-out through the Club Lounge, as well as the thoughtful welcome card and gift in our room, were much appreciated. Our daughter was also treated with great warmth and attention. The pool, steam room, sauna and private beach area were further highlights. The hotel is also conveniently connected to the airport, with the TD2 bus being an easy alternative to a taxi or Bolt.

However, there is room for improvement. The hotel is showing its age, with some furniture and fixtures visibly worn or damaged. The rooms are spacious but rather dark, with insufficient lighting, limited power sockets, no USB ports and no bedside master switch. Additional towel hooks, a proper coffee machine and warmer duvets would also improve comfort. Some areas had an unusual smell, and our garden-view room felt quite cold despite the air conditioning being switched off.

Overall, we had a very enjoyable stay, largely thanks to the exceptional hospitality of the team. With some investment in room comfort and refurbishment, this hotel could offer an even more outstanding experience.

### Expected More from 5\* IHG

a day ago

This was a big birthday celebration visit for our family - 6 people in 3 rooms booked for 7 nights through Trailfinders following their recommendation for the hotel.
We had occasional issues with housekeeping - in one room the bottom sheet was left unmade and one room was not serviced at all on the last day. I reported the first issue to reception the following day and was given a drinks voucher. It was not explained that this voucher was only valid at one onsite bar and I experienced significant embarrassment at presenting the voucher twice (at 2 different bars) and being declined twice. The voucher was returned to reception when I checked out unused. The offer of the voucher ended up having the opposite effect to that intended , hence my review.
Our visit also coincided in part with the Motorola conference and a large influx of business guests - the only outside pool area was closed on 20.10.26 for their evening reception. This was advertised earlier in the day to be closed from 3.30pm but in fact our some of party was asked to leave before 3.00pm from this area. This is not the way to treat non-business guests - if you wanted the pool closed at 3pm then advertise this time rather asking guests to leave early because it suits staff. Other guests were also very unhappy to be asked to leave.
Hotel staff were all very pleasant but ultimately did not make our stay a 5\* experience

### IHG Malta for Night Life

5 days ago

The IHG hotel in St. Julian’s, Malta, offers a modern style and a convenient location, with numerous nightclubs and entertainment options in the surrounding area. It is also a good base for visiting Mdina Fortress and other attractions around Malta.

We stayed in a lovely junior suite with two balconies, which was a real highlight of our stay. The extra outdoor space made the room even more enjoyable.

However, the hotel’s ultra-modern design may not appeal to everyone. The entrance and initial impression upon arrival were not quite what we expected, and some guests may find the overall style a little too contemporary.

Overall, the hotel has plenty to offer, especially for travelers who appreciate modern accommodations, spacious suites, and a lively location. With some improvements to the arrival experience and more consistent front desk service, the stay could be even better.
