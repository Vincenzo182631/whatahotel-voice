## Al Fresco

Escape to Al Fresco, where a lush garden oasis and the rich flavours of Maltese cuisine await you. Our talented chefs use the freshest ingredients to craft dishes that celebrate local Maltese cuisine. From the traditional slow-cooked snails to pan-fried rabbit and a unique grill-your-own skewers experience, every meal is a journey through the tastes of Malta. Don’t forget to indulge in our exquisite Maltese desserts and a perfectly infused gin to complete your Maltese culinary experience.
