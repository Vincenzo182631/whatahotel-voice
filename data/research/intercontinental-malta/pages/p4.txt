### Ultimate Relaxation at Our Luxury Spa

Experience ultimate relaxation at our luxury spa on site. Indulge in bespoke treatments designed to soothe the body, calm the mind, and restore inner harmony. This tranquil haven perfectly balances beauty, comfort, and refined wellness for your rejuvenating escape in Malta.

### Refined Relaxation in Malta

Experience unparalleled wellness at our luxurious hotel and spa in Malta. Indulge in bespoke treatments at the tranquil hotel spa in Malta. Unwind in our hotel in Malta with indoor pool, the serene Indoor Oasis, or enjoy two outdoor pools, including the SkyBeach Infinity Pool. Your Maltese retreat promises ultimate comfort and harmony.

### Wellness & Fitness at Cynergi Health & Fitness

At Cynergi Health & Fitness Centre, wellness begins with movement. In a space designed for energy, focus, and renewal it welcomes guests to become their personal best. After your workout in Malta’s biggest fitness centre, complement your fitness journey with spa treatments

### Unwind Your Way: 3 Pools, Views & Pure Escape

Unwind in refined relaxation with our 3 pools, 2 outdoor, 1 indoor. Ascend to the Skybeach Infinity Pool for panoramic rooftop views, or bask in the sun at the lagoon-style Poolside Paradise. For year-round tranquility, retreat to our serene Indoor Oasis. A world of luxury awaits at our Malta hotel.

### Sustainability

#### Sustainability Certification

- Our hotel has a third-party sustainability certification.

**Certifications: Green Key (FEE)**

Our hotel is certified with Green Key (FEE), a globally recognised eco-label run by the Foundation for Environmental Education that is awarded to tourism and hospitality facilities that demonstrate outstanding commitment to environmental sustainability. Certified hotels must have implemented a range of measures across multiple categories including energy (e.g. energy-saving lightbulbs), water (e.g. water-saving taps, showers, and toilets) and waste (food waste and single-use plastic reduction schemes).
