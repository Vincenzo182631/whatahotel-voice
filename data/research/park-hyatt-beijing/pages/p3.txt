## China Grill

Last orders: 15 minutes before closing time

Visitors dine beneath a dramatic Chinese “lantern” at the building’s apex, featuring a soaring glass pyramid ceiling and glass walls, and surrounded by trees and water features. Specialising in premium meat, fish and seafood, the restaurant includes a semi-private dining area on a mezzanine level overlooking the main dining space; open kitchen. On the top floor, guests can enjoy a more unique and intimate space in a semi-private dining area which overlooks the main dining area on the ground floor. Kids can also enjoy a specially prepared menu of tasty and nutritiously balanced dishes, and surprise amenities for a unique dining experience.
Location: Level 66
Seating: 158 seats

## Dining Room

Last orders: 30 minutes before closing time

Dining Room – The Sky-High Chinese Restaurant at Park Hyatt Beijing, located on the 63rd floor with panoramic views of the city. In tune with the seasons, Dining Room regularly introduces seasonal tasting menus and invites guest chefs from Hyatt hotels in different cities across China to bring authentic regional flavors to Beijing diners. At lunch and dinner, the restaurant serves a refined selection of refreshing Cantonese and Chaozhou-style dishes. Beyond dining, Dining Room also offers an ideal setting for a tranquil afternoon tea experience.
Location: Level 63
Seating: 100 seats
