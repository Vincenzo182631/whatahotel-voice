Navigation Guidance for International Guests

For seamless navigation during your stay in Chinese Mainland, we recommend using local mapping applications such as Gaode Map or Baidu Map. International mapping apps may not be accurate in Chinese Mainland.

Getting around

Limousine Service

Park Hyatt Beijing limousine services are available for half-day and full-day private tours on request.

Taxi

Taxis operate 24 hours. Charges vary depending on the final destination, with the basic charge for a taxi of CNY 13/3 kilometers. The hotel offers a taxi card that features bilingual address information that can be presented to taxi drivers in order to ensure guests arrive at the correct destinations. Our concierge will be pleased to assist if you need further help.
