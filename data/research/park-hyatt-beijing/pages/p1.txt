## Unlock Your Personal Journey in the Heart of Beijing CBD

At Park Hyatt Beijing, you’ll quickly discover that luxury is personal. With uncommon experiences at every turn and tailored service that exceeds expectations, you’ll feel right at home. From world-class dining options to the tranquil pool, every moment is meant to be savored.

## Discover Park Hyatt Beijing

Fitness Center

Located on the 59th floor, the 24-hour modern fitness facilities are designed to meet all your needs, from strength training to cardio workouts. The most captivating feature is the breathtaking views: as you exercise by the windows, you can take in sweeping vistas of the Beijing CBD and its iconic landmarks. After your workout, you may unwind with a few laps in the adjacent 25‑meter heated indoor “Sky Pool” – the perfect place to rejuvenate during your business travels. For more information or to make a booking, please call +86 10 8567 1145.

Spa

### China Grill

Visitors dine beneath a dramatic Chinese “lantern” at the building’s apex, featuring a soaring glass pyramid ceiling and glass walls, and surrounded by trees and water features. Specialising in premium meat, fish and seafood, the restaurant includes a semi-private dining area on a mezzanine level overlooking the main dining space; open kitchen. On the top floor, guests can enjoy a more unique and intimate space in a semi-private dining area which overlooks the main dining area on the ground floor. Kids can also enjoy a specially prepared menu of tasty and nutritiously balanced dishes, and surprise amenities for a unique dining experience.
Location: Level 66
Seating: 158 seats
