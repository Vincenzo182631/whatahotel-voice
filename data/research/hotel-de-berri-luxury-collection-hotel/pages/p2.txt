# Enjoy 5-Star Luxury in Our Paris Suites

## Accommodations

Each of Hôtel de Berri’s 75 luxury hotel rooms and 35 spacious suites tells a unique story — a tribute to the golden age of Parisian interiors and the visionary spirit of Elsa Schiaparelli. Designed by...See More Philippe Renaud, the décor blends neoclassical elegance, surrealist details, curated antiques, and contemporary artworks to create immersive Paris accommodation unlike any other. Rich materials, couture-inspired palettes, and generous living spaces reveal a refined and deeply Parisian universe where fantasy meets sophistication steps from the Champs-Élysées.See Less

### The Parisian Suite

Inspired by the legendary residence of Elsa Schiaparelli, The Parisian Suite is Hôtel de Berri’s signature masterpiece. Spanning 115sqm / 1237sqft, this exceptional Parisian suite blends neoclassical elegance, couture-inspired details, and curated artworks to create a one-of-a-kind luxury experience in the heart of Paris. Featuring panoramic views over the hotel’s private garden, a dining room for six, a spacious salon, marble bathroom, and walk-in dressing room, the suite offers an immersive stay steps from the Champs-Élysées.
