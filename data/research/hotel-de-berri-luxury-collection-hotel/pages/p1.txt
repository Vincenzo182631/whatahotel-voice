## Paris as a Living Gallery

Located steps from the Champs-Élysées and Paris’s iconic Golden Triangle, Hôtel de Berri places guests in the heart of the capital’s art, fashion, and cultural scene. Surrounded by luxury boutiques, renowned galleries, museums, and celebrated Parisian avenues, the hotel offers an elegant and immersive stay in one of the most prestigious neighborhoods in central Paris.

Discover Paris

## Historical Experience - A Legacy of Art, Fashion & Parisian Heritage

Hidden just off the Champs-Élysées, Hôtel de Berri stands on a historic Parisian address once home to legendary couturière Elsa Schiaparelli. Today, this five-star Luxury Collection hotel celebrates the artistic and cultural spirit of Paris through bold interiors, curated artworks, and refined decorative influences imagined by designer Philippe Renaud. Blending couture heritage with contemporary elegance, Hôtel de Berri invites guests into a unique world where fashion, design, and history converge in the heart of Paris.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris?

The check-in time at Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 15.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €60.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris?

On-site self parking is available at Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris. The daily self parking fee is €50.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris booking.

What is the closest airport near Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris?

The closest airport near Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris is Paris/Orly Airport (ORY). ORY is located approximately 10.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris have an airport shuttle to Paris/Orly Airport (ORY)?

Yes, Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris has an airport shuttle to Paris/Orly Airport (ORY).

Does Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris have an airport shuttle to Charles de Gaulle International Airport (CDG)?

Yes, Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris has an airport shuttle to Charles de Gaulle International Airport (CDG).

Does Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
