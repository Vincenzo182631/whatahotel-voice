# W Lounge

The Place That Brings the Pulse of the City to W Rome’s Doorstep

See the Menu Opens a new window Book Your Table Opens a new window

## W Lounge, a Magnet for Animated Conversation, Good Drinks and Upbeat Music

W Rome's any-hours destination, fueled by live music, DJ sets, and cocktail culture. Celebrating individuality and diversity, W Lounge is where guests can roll with the moment. It is the upbeat music that defines the mood, but it's guests who decide the flow, whether it be mixing in the crush zone, hanging out in the more private lounge, or mingling in the Giardino Clandestino.

## Cocktail Experience

Signature cocktails take you on a journey through time. Our drink list, “The Time Journey,” spans from the very first spark that ignited the universe to the glimmering visions of the future, featuring inventive twists on classic flavors. W Lounge delivers on its promise, from aperitivo to after-dinner, offering elevated cocktails in a relaxed yet curated ambiance, to the rhythm of music, making it the perfect place to unwind, sip, and savor the moment.

## Aperitivo Eterno

With a Sicilian touch, our Aperitivo Eterno celebrates urban and market food - from the old Roman times and until today - to the beats of our resident DJ and sips of our signature cocktails. Neverending pleasure!

Every day, 6PM - 9PM

## Happy Hour

Who said fun is just for weekends?

On weekdays, join us for happy hour to unwind after a working day or kick start your evening with W Lounge’s energy. And if you’re a Marriott Bonvoy Member you get your drink at a special price, on selected drinks

Mon - Fri, 4PM - 6PM

## A Space With No Boundaries

At W Lounge, boundaries dissolve, creating a seamless experience that transcends space and time.

In winter, the energy extends to ' **See and Be Seen**', the lobby hotspot where warmth and vibrancy converge.

Come summer, the action shifts to the open-air courtyard, **Giardino Clandestino** where, amidst shared dishes and glimpses of roaming entertainment, live performances become the backdrop to your mingling moments.

## W Lounge on the Roof

Reopening mid-May: As temperatures rise and the good season arrives, W Lounge expands its boundaries to the hotel's 6th-floor rooftop, offering a sublime sunset and after-dinner bar experience. Enjoy al fresco aperitivos and signature cocktails by the scenic pool of the Wet Deck, enhancing the vibrant atmosphere as night falls over the city. The perfect backdrop for socializing, with breathtaking views and a sophisticated ambiance that encapsulates the essence of Roman summer nights.

## Giano Restaurant

When we claim that time and space have no boundaries, we truly mean it.

W Lounge is not just a pre-dinner or post-dinner spot, but it seamlessly fits into every moment in between. Its thoughtfully crafted drinks are ready to enhance your Sicilian dinner experience at Giano Restaurant, while Giano's delectable cuisine can be savored in tasty bites right at W Lounge when you are in the mood for a more laid-back dining atmosphere.
