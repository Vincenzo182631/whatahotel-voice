## LIVE ETERNAL

Classic Roman craftsmanship does a double take, as **old and new** mix and match through an inspired edit of frescoes, chrome and contemporary design at **W Rome**. The duality of Italy tempts the city's finest out to play, where **food**, **music** and **more** is a lesson on how **Italians do it better**.

Tapped into the pulse of the city, our resident DJs capture the local energy, while chefs Ciccio Sultano and Adriano Rausa lead an inventive team to concoct crowd-pleasing Italian flavors that fuel the senses.

###### Celebrating Sicily with Ciccio Sultano

### Ciccio Sultano

The soul of **Giano Restaurant** is Ciccio Sultano, a chef synonymous with the evolution of the **Sicilian culinary tradition**, one of the most eclectic, hybrid and intense in the world. Ciccio's approach may sound traditional but it's not.

Deeply rooted in history, it in fact betrays tradition to create the most relevant of cuisines: food with soul.
