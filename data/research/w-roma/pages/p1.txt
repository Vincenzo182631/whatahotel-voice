# Welcome to W Rome

## Live Eternal at our 5 star hotel in Rome

Set within a historic palazzo, W Rome brings a bold interpretation of luxury to the heart of the Eternal City, where past and present collide in a way that is distinctly Roman and unmistakably W. More than a place to stay, it is a platform shaped by energy, creativity, and connection. Home to 148 rooms and suites, each space balances expressive design with a sense of calm. Dining reflects Italy’s creativity,...See More from Giano Restaurant by Chef Ciccio Sultano to Citrico Rooftop – Pizza e Cocktail. At the center of the social scene, W Lounge and Giardino Clandestino bring together locals and guests over aperitivo and music. ​Deeply rooted in Rome’s creative pulse, W Rome is more than a hotel, it is a platform for community and contemporary expression. You don’t just visit the city here, you live it. You Live Eternal.See Less

Breaking Boundaries

### Unique Design

The design reflects Rome’s multi-layered history, creating a space where past and present exist in constant dialogue. Inside W Rome, the Eternal City is reinterpreted through a contemporary lens, where design speaks through detail and intention. Inspired by Will Meyer and Gray Davis, elements such as repeating arches, layered marble, and references to iconic landmarks like the Pantheon and Palazzo della Civiltà Italiana shape a narrative that feels both familiar and unexpected. Across the hotel, materials and textures unfold like a collage, from hammered and polished marble.

## Future Forward

At W Rome, sustainability is part of how we move through the city, not a statement, but a daily practice. From responsible operations to community-led initiatives, every detail is designed to create a more conscious way of experiencing Rome. Recognized with Green Key International certification, the hotel continues to evolve through thoughtful choices, supporting local communities, creative voices, and a more responsible future.​

### About Our Commitment

Sustainability is a priority to us both within and beyond our property: from the guestrooms to all our dining outlets, we've implemented various initiatives to minimize our ecological footprint and enrich our guest experience. Our commitment extends to sourcing practices that support environmental conservation. Moreover, we prioritize social sustainability by actively contributing to community...See More initiatives.See Less

Our Initiatives

Pillars of our on-property commitment to sustainability:

- Energy and water conservation
- Waste minimization
- Locally-sourced food products
- Green certification for coffee and tea
- Reduced use of plastic
- Indoor environmental quality
- Sustainable meeting practices
- Promotion of green transportation
- Employee awareness and engagement
- Internal Green Committee

Partners For Sustainability

We have activated different partnerships promoting environmental and social responsibility both at a local and international level:

- Clean The World
- Fondazione Bioparco di Roma
- Retake
- Comunità Sant’Egidio: Pasti per Strada, Ecosolidarietà – abiti usati

Our Goals

The objective is to achieve significant reductions in environmental impact. Specifically, the following targets have been established:

- CARBON: implementing strategies to cut carbon in half by 2030 and be Net-zero by no later than 2050.
- WATER: aim to reduce water intensity by 15% by 2025, thereby minimizing the amount of water used in our operations.
- WASTE: aim to reduce waste to landfill by 45% and food waste by 50% by 2025.

Thank you for joining us in our commitment to sustainability. Together, we can make a difference for our planet and future generations.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Rome?

The check-in time at W Rome is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Rome allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Rome. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 30.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W Rome?

Off-site valet parking is available at W Rome. The daily valet fee is €65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Rome have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Rome has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Rome booking.

What is the closest airport near W Rome?

The closest airport near W Rome is Ciampino – G.B. Pastine International Airport (CIA). CIA is located approximately 28.1 KM from the hotel.

Does W Rome have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Rome does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
