## Experiences

Shaped by the city’s creative pulse, experiences here unfold through curated events, cultural collaborations, movement-led moments, and music-driven nights, bringing guests and locals together in a shared, ever-evolving atmosphere. Set in the heart of the city, just moments from Rome’s iconic landmarks, it becomes a gateway to explore beyond the expected, connecting you to the people, energy, and culture shaping Rome today.​

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Rome have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Rome offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Rome have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Rome has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Rome?

Pantheon, Orto Botaninco, Sant'Andrea de Scaphis - Art Gallery, CHEZ DÉDÉ Store, Palazzo Zuccari and more are nearby W Rome.
