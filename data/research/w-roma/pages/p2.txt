## Roman Heritage, Reimagined

Behind the façades of two 19th-century palazzos, W Rome's guestrooms offer a contemporary take on the Eternal City's design heritage. From Standard rooms to more spacious accommodation, interiors bring together refined materials, sculptural furnishings and unexpected details. Inspired by Italian design icons Carlo Scarpa and Ettore Sottsass, each space creates a dialogue between past and present, with a distinctly Roman character.

### Penthouse Suite

Take suite living to new heights in W Rome’s 241 sqm Penthouse Suite. Set on the top floor, expansive living spaces bring together contemporary design, refined materials and the timeless character of the Eternal City. Designed for both relaxation and entertaining, the suite opens onto an extensive private terrace with sweeping views across Rome’s rooftops.

View Virtual Tour

### Supreme Suite

Located on the fifth floor, the Supreme Suite combines distinctive artworks, contemporary Italian furnishings and a spacious living area with details inspired by Rome’s heritage. An expansive private terrace offers outdoor living and dining spaces overlooking the city’s iconic skyline.

View Virtual Tour

### W Suite

More space, more possibilities. W Suites blend expressive design, contemporary comfort and the character of the historic palazzo. Separate living areas offer room to unwind, entertain and settle in, while select suites feature private outdoor spaces or additional bathrooms. With the option to connect to adjoining rooms, they are ideal for families, friends and anyone looking to experience Rome their own way.

View Virtual Tour

### Urban Suite

A junior suite with a distinctly W attitude. Urban Suite blends Rome’s creative energy with classic architectural details and contemporary design. Leather headboards, theatrical drapes and curated artworks bring personality to the space, while a sofa and seating area invite you to settle in. Select suites extend outdoors with a private garden or terrace, offering a quiet escape from the city's vibrant streets.

View Virtual Tour

Do the rooms at W Rome have a work space?

No, the rooms at W Rome do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Rome?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 8 at W Rome. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Rome?

Some rooms at W Rome permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

No, rooms at W Rome do not have a fridge.
