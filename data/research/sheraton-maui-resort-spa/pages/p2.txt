### Teppan-yaki Dan

Teppan-yaki Dan is where showmanship meets the plate. At Maui’s only teppanyaki restaurant, amazement is sure to set in as our talented chefs twirl their knives and create volcanoes of fire while serving up garlic fried rice, lobster, steak, teriyaki chicken, and more. Delight in contemporary Japanese cuisine grilled to perfection right before your eyes.

Book Your Dinner

### Maui Nui Luau

Discover the remarkable stories, history, and culture of the Valley Isle through the songs and dances of the Pacific at the Maui Nui Luau. Situated beachfront on award-winning Kaanapali Beach, enjoy the best in Hawaiian & Polynesian song and dance, including the spectacular fire knife dance. Enjoy an all-you-can-eat buffet, Hawaiian arts and games, and experience an authentic Hawaiian imu ceremony.

Book Your Luau

### The Spa at Black Rock

Enjoy an infusion of health brought by our soothing treatments, using natural products that draw on island plants and Polynesian essential oils like kukui coconut or mango pomegranate seed. Make the massage yours and choose from a variety of relaxing options like Hawaiian Lomi Lomi massage, Pu‘u Keka‘a Hot Stone, and more.

Book Your Massage

Frequently Asked Questions

Are rooms and suites pet‑friendly?

No, Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa only permits service animals in guestrooms and suites.

Do rooms have ocean views?

Over 80% of the rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa offer ocean views.

Do rooms have private balconies or terraces?

Yes, every room at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa has a balcony, terrace, or lanai.

Are rooms and suites family‑friendly?

Yes, many of our room and suite offerings are family-friendly, featuring pull-out beds or rollaway beds, connecting rooms, and our Ohana Suite features a Murphy bed to sleep up to 5 people in a single suite.

Do you offer connecting rooms or family suites?

Yes, the resort offers interconnected rooms and suites. The Ohana Suite is Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa's premier family suite, featuring either a king or double beds with both a pull-out bed and a Murphy bed to sleep up to 5 people.

Can I request a specific room or suite location?

Guests can request their preferred room or suite location, and we will do our best to accommodate the request based on availability.

Are in‑room safes or secure storage available?

Yes, rooms include an in‑room safe.

Are bathrooms equipped with a bathtub, walk‑in shower, or both?

Most bathrooms at the Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa feature a combined bathtub and shower. Select suites offer separate walk-in showers and bathtubs.

What suite options are available?

The resort offers a wide variety of suites, including ocean view, oceanfront, deluxe, and our presidential suite, The Ali‘i Suite.

What is a lanai?

A lanai is a Hawaiian-style private balcony or terrace, featured across most of our room categories.

Do you offer suites with separate living spaces?

Yes, all the suites at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have separate living spaces.

Do you offer rooms with views of Black Rock?

Yes, many rooms feature views of Puu Kekaa (Black Rock). Guests can request a view of Black Rock, and our team will do our best to accommodate the request, subject to availability.

How many wings does the resort have?

Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa has six wings: Anuenue, Lahaina, Hoku, Nalu, Moana, and Ohana.

Can I book packages with resort, spa, or dining credits?

Yes, Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa offers packages such as resort credit promotions and bundled packages. Visit our offers for more information.

What channels or streaming services are available on TVs?

In-room TVs include cable and Netflix for our guests.

Do you provide allergy‑friendly rooms or bedding?

Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa can provide allergy-friendly bedding upon request.

Do accessible rooms include roll‑in showers or grab bars?

Yes, select accessible rooms feature roll‑in showers and grab bars. Please note in your reservation that you require these accommodations, and our team will place you in an appropriate accessible room.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 5 at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

Some rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have hair dryers.
