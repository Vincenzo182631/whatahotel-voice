# Kaanapali Resort and Spa: A Haven for Rejuvenation

## Experiences

Situated near Kaanapali Beach and just steps from Black Rock Beach, the adventure begins at our Maui resort hotel with a variety of activities, including The Spa at Black Rock, offering massages, body...See More treatments, and skin care. While staying in Maui, relax at our lagoon-style pool, taste fresh island cuisine, enjoy Native Hawaiian cultural activities, shop at Whalers Village, and play on the white-sand beaches. From sunrise yoga to kayaking to surf lessons, our Kaanapali Beach hotel spa resort offers activities for the entire family.See Less

## Resort Activity Information

Maximize your time at our Kaanapali resort with pickleball courts, a fitness center and activities on-site. View our weekly activity schedule information, festive season calendars, pool cabana reservations, dining menus and more.

### Pool & Beach

Relaxation awaits in Maui at our Kaanapali Beach hotel spa and oceanfront 142-yard lagoon-style pool (the length of a football field!), meandering through lushly landscaped gardens, just steps from the sand with views of Black Rock and Kaanapali Beach. ## Ocean Activities

Pu‘u Keka‘a (Black Rock), fronting Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa, is one of the top snorkeling destinations on Maui and is home to an array of ocean activities, like kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding, outrigger canoe adventures and more. Guests at our hotel near Black Rock Beach in Maui can see tropical marine life, vibrant coral reefs and the beautiful honu (green sea turtle) in the waters right in front of the resort. Choose from a range of equipment for rent such as paddle boards, surfboards and snorkeling gear, or book family-friendly things to do in Kaanapali, like surf lessons, canoeing and kayaking. For assistance with booking your island activities stop by our Concierge Desk in the lobby. Contact us at (808) 661-0031 ext. 38135, or email: smc@aqualani.com

## Cultural Offerings

Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa offers guests an array of opportunities to connect to local culture through authentic, curated Kaanapali activities. The experiences rotate monthly, and include offerings like koi fish feeding, lei making, ukulele lessons, hula dancing, and more.

## Maui Nui Luau

Discover the remarkable stories, history, and culture of the Valley Isle and Kaanapali through the songs and dances of Maui and the Pacific at the Maui Nui Luau. Situated beachfront on award-winning Kaanapali Beach, the Maui Nui Luau shares the music, dance, and history of the islands right here at the foot of the famous cliff diving point, Puu Kekaa. World-renowned Polynesian entertainment producer Tihati Productions presents the best in Hawaiian & Polynesian song and dance, including the spectacular fire knife dance. Enjoy an all-you-can-eat luau buffet, Hawaiian arts, crafts, and games, and experience an authentic Hawaiian imu ceremony.

Learn More

## The Kuleana Series

The Kuleana Series is a first-of-its-kind interactive augmented reality experience featuring 11 leading cultural educators and scientific experts, spanning topics from the Meaning of Aloha to Endemic Species of Hawaii to Solar Astronomy on Haleakala. The Kuleana Series gives resort guests exclusive access to some of the island’s most sought-after voices via interactive plaques that come to life with a QR code and cellphone, creating a lifelike experience as the image begins to move and speak.

### The Nightly Cliff Dive Ritual

As the sun begins its slow dive into the ocean, torch lighting signals the nightly ritual of lele kawa (cliff diving). Mauiʼs last ruling chief, Kahekili (Thunderer), a master of cliff jumping, loved to dive into the ocean from the Leina a ka ʻuhane (leap of the soul) at Puu Kekaa (Black Rock). Because it was a portal where spirits on their journey to the afterlife would leap into eternity, his feats...See More proved the great power of his mana (divine energy) to all. Every day since our hotel near Black Rock Beach in Maui opened in 1963, we celebrate Kahekiliʼs courageous journey between the physical and spiritual realm with a sunset cliff diving ritual from the very Leina he jumped off of.See Less

## Whale Season (December – April)

With one of the best vantage points in Kaanapali for viewing whales, our hotel near Black Rock Beach, Maui, is the perfect location for guests to observe humpbacks in their natural habitat across Maui Nui Basin, where their population is among the highest. Relax on your lanai for morning coffee or sunset sips while gazing out at ocean views spotted with humpback splashes.

## Sunset S’mores Roasting

Roast s'mores at our oceanfront fire pits while taking in views of a picturesque Kaanapali sunset. S'mores kits are delivered to your guestroom with all the fixings and can be complemented by your favorite beverages for a relaxing evening spent with loved ones. Enhance your s'mores experience with our Local Style S'mores kit that includes fresh tropical fruit, macadamia nuts, and coconut flakes.

The Kaanapali Beach hotel spa resort features Raw Elements reef-safe sunscreen dispensers throughout the resort, filtered water stations and keepsake water bottles and more.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book excursions or activities through the resort?

Yes, Maui excursions and activities can be booked through our on-site concierge, who can be contacted at (808) 661-0031 ext. 38135, or email: smc@aqualani.com

What time is the resort’s cliff dive ceremony?

Sheraton Maui’s nightly cliff dive ceremony takes place every evening at sunset, and has been a tradition at our Maui resort since first opening in 1963. This evening tradition is a free experience best enjoyed from our Kaanapali Beach restaurant Cliff Dive Bar & Grill, with live music, hula dancer, and narration of the story of Pu‘u Keka‘a (Black Rock) and Kahekili, the last chief of Maui, who the dive honors. Please note that the dive does not take place during inclement weather conditions.

Does Sheraton Maui have a luau?

Maui Nui Luau takes place three nights a week on our resort’s oceanfront lawn, steps from Kaanapali Beach. For more info, visit sheratonmauiluau.com.

Are there any swimming pools or a beach at the resort?

Sheraton Maui is just steps from world-famous Kaanapali Beach. Our Maui resort also features a 142-yard lagoon-style pool. Guests can explore several large swimming areas, interconnected by lava-rock waterways, a small waterslide, and a shallow children's area for safe splashing.

Does the resort have a spa or wellness center?

The Spa at Black Rock is open daily from 9 AM to 5 PM and features an array of Maui spa treatments including massages, skin care, body treatments and more. For more info, visit blackrockspa.com. The resort also offers daily yoga classes, pickleball and tennis courts.

What amenities and activities are available at Sheraton Maui Resort and Spa?

Our oceanfront resort in Maui offers daily cultural activities for guests to enjoy, like lei making, hula dancing, ukulele lessons, and more. We also have s’mores roasting, koi fish feeding, Maui Nui Luau, ocean activities like snorkeling and paddle boarding, and wellness activities like yoga, pickleball, and tennis. Guests can enjoy the Kaanapali Trolley between resorts and Whalers Village, filtered water stations, and reef-safe sunscreen dispensers throughout the property.
