Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sheraton Maui have in-room dining?

In addition to an array of on-site dining options, our Maui resort does offer in-room dining daily from 6:30 – 10:30 AM and 5:30 – 9 PM. In-room dining selections are served in sustainable to-go packaging.

Is there a dress code for the restaurants at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

At our poolside restaurants, there is no specific dress code. At Teppan-yaki Dan, Coral Reef, and The Sandbar, shoes and shirts are required.

Do the restaurants at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa offer vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes, each restaurant and bar has vegetarian or vegan options.

Do the restaurants at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa offer gluten-free options?

Yes, each restaurant and bar has gluten-free options.

Do the restaurants at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa accommodate food allergies?

Yes, our restaurants are able to accommodate food allergies. Please notify your server of your allergies and our chefs can work to modify the meal to your needs.

Are the restaurants at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa open to non-hotel guests?

Yes, the resort's restaurants are open to non-hotel guests.

Do the restaurants at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have happy hours?

Yes, Cliff Dive Bar & Grill, Mai Tai Bar, and The Sandbar have happy hours. Check with the resort for current happy hour times.

Can I charge food or drinks to my room at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

Yes, guests of the resort are able to charge food and drinks to their rooms.

Is parking available for the restaurants at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

Yes, both self-parking and valet parking are available for guests of Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa's restaurants. Valet parking is validated at a reduced cost for restaurant guests.

Can the restaurants at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa host a private dinner or event?

Yes, the resort's restaurants can accommodate private dinners and events. Please contact the resort in advance to make arrangements for a private experience.

What bars does Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have on property?

Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa has The Sandbar, Cliff Dive Bar & Grill, and Mai Tai Bar.

Do the restaurants at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have live entertainment?

We host live music at the Cliff Dive Bar & Grill pool deck each evening before sunset.
