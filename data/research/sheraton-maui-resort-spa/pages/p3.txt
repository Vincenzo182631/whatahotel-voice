# Moana at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa

Cliffside Coastal Charm & Bliss

Opens a new windowOpens a new window

## Moana Oceanfront Suites & Premium Rooms

Atop Pu'u Keka'a (Black Rock), Moana at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa floats above the ocean like a majestic wa'a (canoe). Front-row seats to take in the beauty of Maui are yours from our Moana Oceanfront Suites & Premium Rooms. These spacious rooms offer unparalleled ocean and sunset views with the privacy of an exclusive retreat.

## Moana Oceanfront Suites

Our largest rooms in Moana, the one-bedroom oceanfront suites feature a private wraparound balcony with stunning views of Kaanapali coastline, Black Rock’s coral reef with all its tropical marine life, and our neighboring islands, Lanai and Molokai.

## Moana Oceanfront Suites

The Oceanfront Suites feature a king bed and two sliding glass doors in your bedroom for stunning ocean and West Maui views with easy access to your private, wraparound lanai.

## Moana Oceanfront Suites

The living room in these cliffside suites offers ample space for relaxation, a desk, pullout sofa, flat-screen TV, and a wet bar.

## Moana Oceanfront Suites

The suite's primary bathroom features a glass-enclosed walk-in shower, full-size bathtub, double sinks, and deluxe bath amenities. A half bath is also available just off the main entrance.

## Moana Ocean Front Extended Lanai

Soak up the sun on your own extended lanai. Our unique ground floor rooms feature an additional 144 square feet of outdoor space with two loungers to relax and take in the view and enjoy the best of indoor-outdoor living. Tranquility is right outside your door.

## Moana Ocean Front Guestrooms

With a private oceanfront balcony above Black Rock, these rooms are larger than our typical guestrooms and have expansive views of the Pacific Ocean and neighboring islands, Lanai and Molokai. Enjoy the resort’s private Moana location for even more relaxation during your Hawaiian vacation.

### Reserve Your Moana Oceanfront Suite or Premium Room

With the most breathtaking seascapes in all of Kaanapali, Moana at Sheraton Maui will leave you with a renewed connection to oneself and the ocean’s timeless rhythm. Plan your next visit and stay in our Moana Oceanfront accommodations.
