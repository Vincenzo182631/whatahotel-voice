# Welcome to Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa

## Discover the Beauty of Kaanapali Beach at our Resort on Maui

Nestled along the breathtaking shores of Kaanapali Beach, Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa is where an exceptional location meets deep intention. While our Kaanapali Beach resort's nightly lele kawa, the cliff...See More diving ritual, has gained legendary status, it represents just one facet of the immersive Hawaiian experience that awaits you. Feel the spirit of aloha infuse the grounds of our Kaanapali Beach hotel with well-appointed accommodations, a large lagoon-style pool, and soothing treatments at The Spa at Black Rock. Embark on exhilarating ocean adventures, from snorkeling amidst vibrant coral reefs to sailing on catamarans that slice through crystal-clear waters. Discover wide open spaces on our 23-acre resort in Maui for soulful reflection and a deeper connection to nature. Over 80% of our Kaanapali hotel rooms offer ocean views, the ideal vantage point for whale watching and the sun setting into the Pacific. Immerse yourself in Maui's profound beauty, where the Kaanapali resort lifestyle was born and perfected.See Less

## Hawaii’s Best Resort on the World’s Favorite Beach

Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa is an award‑winning, oceanfront Ka‘anapali Beach resort, perfectly positioned where iconic Pu‘u Keka‘a (Black Rock) meets the Pacific. Set on 23 beachfront acres, the resort...See More fronts a protected cove known as one of the best snorkeling spots in Maui, with frequent encounters with honu (Hawaiian green sea turtles) and seasonal humpback whale sightings offshore. Consistently ranked among the best hotels and resorts in Hawaii, Sheraton Maui has earned top honors including #1 Best Hotel in Ka‘anapali (U.S. News & World Report 2026, Gold Badge), delivering an unmatched blend of natural beauty, world‑class beachfront, and authentic island hospitality.See Less

### Moana Oceanfront Guestrooms and Suites

Atop Puu Kekaa (Black Rock), Moana at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa floats above the ocean like a majestic waa (canoe). With the most breathtaking seascapes in all of Kaanapali, Moana at Sheraton Maui will leave you with a renewed connection to oneself and the ocean’s timeless rhythm.

## The Nightly Lele Kawa (Cliff Diving) Ritual

A tradition since the resort first opened in 1963 that honors the last chief of Maui, Kahekili, who used to leap from Puu Kekaa’s sacred promontory to show his spiritual strength, uniting his warriors. Learn the history and cultural significance of this dive every evening at sunset.

## The Kuleana Series

The Kuleana Series is a first-of-its-kind interactive augmented reality experience featuring 11 leading cultural educators and scientific experts, spanning topics from the Meaning of Aloha to Endemic Species of Hawaii to Solar Astronomy on Haleakala. The Kuleana Series gives resort guests exclusive access to some of the island’s most sought-after voices via interactive plaques that come to life with a QR code and cellphone, creating a lifelike experience as the image begins to move and speak.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa built?

Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa originally opened in January 1963, becoming the first resort within the Kaanapali Beach Resort.

Does Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa offer late check‑out?

Late checkout is based on availability. Guests may request late checkout during their stay, provided they give a minimum of 24 hours' notice prior to departure. Should it not be available, luggage storage and access to showers in fitness areas are provided.

Does the Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa offer kamaaina rates?

Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa offers kamaaina discounts, which require a valid Hawaiʻi state ID upon check-in. You can book this rate using code Z43.

Does Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have a fitness center?

Yes, Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa has an on‑site fitness center for guest use.

Does the Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have a spa?

Yes, the resort features the Spa at Black Rock, offering massages, body treatments, facials, and oceanfront services.

Does Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have a pool?

Yes, Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa has a 142‑yard lagoon‑style pool with waterfalls, connected swim areas, a waterslide, and nearby cabana rentals.

What kind of family‑friendly activities does Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa offer?

Families can enjoy kid‑friendly swimming areas, a waterslide, cultural activities like lei making and ukulele lessons, koi fish feeding, snorkeling, and a game room.

Is there an on‑site laundry room at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

There is an on-site laundry room at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa, located on the 7th floor of the Nalu building. The machines are coin and card-operated. Detergent is available for purchase in the laundry room with coins or at our gift shop, Lamonts.

Where are the water stations located at the Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

Filtered water refill stations are available throughout the resort, supporting Sheraton Maui’s sustainability efforts. Stations are located near the lobby, pool areas, and main walkways.

Where are the ice machines located at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

Ice machines are available for guest use and are located on guestroom floors.

Is Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa directly on the beach?

Yes, the resort is located oceanfront on Kaanapali Beach, right beside Puu Kekaa (Black Rock).

What beach activities are available at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

Guests can enjoy a wide range of ocean and beach activities, including snorkeling at Black Rock (top site on Maui), kayaking, stand‑up paddleboarding, outrigger canoeing, surf lessons and equipment rentals, whale‑watching (seasonal), catamaran sails, and our nightly Cliff Dive Ritual.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa. Self parking is available for $39.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $49.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa is Kapalua Airport - Maui (JHM). JHM is located approximately 3.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have an airport shuttle to Kapalua Airport - Maui (JHM)?

No, Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa does not have an airport shuttle to Kapalua Airport - Maui.

Does Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
