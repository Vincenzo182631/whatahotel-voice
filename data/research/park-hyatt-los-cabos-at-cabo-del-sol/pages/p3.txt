# Restaurants & Bars

Dining at Park Hyatt Cabo del Sol is a curated journey celebrating bold Baja flavors, authentic Mexican cuisine and fresh coastal fare. From refined, ingredient-driven dishes to relaxed oceanfront settings, each restaurant offers a distinct experience rooted in local ingredients, thoughtful craftsmanship and a strong sense of place.

## Mesa Madre

At Mesa Madre, dining is a celebration of authentic Mexican cuisine inspired by the traditions of the abuela at the heart of every kitchen. Mornings feature comforting, flavorful dishes, while evenings showcase regional classics reimagined with refined technique. Each dish honors heritage, local ingredients, and the joy of gathering.

## Costamar Beach Club

Costamar is a beachfront beach club in Los Cabos offering a quintessential toes-in-the-sand Baja experience with a relaxed yet refined atmosphere. Overlooking the Sea of Cortez, the restaurant features Coastal Californian cuisine inspired by the peninsula, with fresh seafood, seasonal ingredients, and sustainable flavors, complemented by signature cocktails, curated wines, and cabana service.

## Silán

Silán is an evening destination for Middle Eastern cuisine in Los Cabos, drawing inspiration from North Africa to the Levant. Blending traditional techniques with Baja’s seasonal ingredients, the menu offers dishes that are both rooted and unexpected. A refined bar program and thoughtfully curated wine cellar highlight Old World depth and balance, creating an atmosphere ideal for intimate dining and relaxed conversation. Please note: Silán Menu currently being offered nightly at Mesa Madre.

## Datil Coffee Shop

Dátil is a specialty coffee shop in Los Cabos where the ritual of coffee meets the craft of baking. Guests can enjoy freshly baked pastries, artisanal chocolates, and delicate confections alongside thoughtfully brewed coffee and whole-leaf teas. Designed as a relaxed café setting, it is the perfect place to pause, recharge, and savor simple, everyday indulgences.

## Pool Bar and Pool Decks

The Pool Bar at Park Hyatt Cabo del Sol is a vibrant poolside destination in Los Cabos, offering relaxed service by day and an elegant lounge atmosphere by night. Guests can enjoy small plates and traditional antojitos alongside an extensive cocktail program and a curated wine-by-the-glass selection—perfect for casual daytime refreshment or evening socializing.
