# Luxury Hotel in Los Cabos Mexico \| Park Hyatt Cabo Del Sol

## Experience a Luxury Coastal Sanctuary in Los Cabos

Nestled in the private Cabo del Sol community, Park Hyatt Cabo del Sol is a coastal sanctuary shaped by the desert, sea and the Baja’s heritage. This refined resort offers 163 ocean-view rooms and suites, 19 villas and residences, Los Cabos’ only luxury resort beach club, stunning pools, a wellness center with a full-service spa and fitness facilities, and 35,000 square feet of event space. Guests can restore their senses through intentional design, stillness and storytelling while enjoying diverse culinary experiences ranging from traditional Mexican cuisine at Mesa Madre to Middle Eastern dishes at Silán, coastal California fare at Costamar Beach Club or artisanal offerings at Dátil Coffee Shop. Enjoy preferred tee-times at the Desert Course and proximity to Anima Village, a boutique luxury shopping and cultural hub.
