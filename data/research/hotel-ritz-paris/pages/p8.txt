# Hôtel de Crillon, A Rosewood Hotel

If Paris is a city of hearts waiting to be discovered, the Crillon’s beats on Place de la Concorde. Renovated in 2017 by Tristan Auer, it now plays a melody in a romantic key, with warm, inviting charm.
Famous figures have left their mark throughout its softly carpeted halls. Marie-Antoinette is said to have taken piano lessons here, and Leonard Bernstein visited so often they named a suite in his honour. To slip inside, is to take your place in a story that’s still being written; in which each character will shape their own legend of Hôtel de Crillon. From bubbles of calm in which to lose yourself — a spa where you can let go and the perfect pool for elegant aquatics — to exquisite culinary delights, and on to Bar Les Ambassadeurs, the Crillon takes you on a journey of budding memories and spontaneous delight.
