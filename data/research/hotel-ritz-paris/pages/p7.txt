# Le Meurice

Le Meurice opened in 1835 under the arches of Rue de Rivoli, opposite the Jardins des Tuileries, and the gardens have found their way into the rooms; the painted garden in the custom Gournay wallpaper mirrors the real one. The Salon Pompadour — where Picasso celebrated his marriage to Olga Khokhlova on 12 July 1918, with Jean Cocteau as witness — has retained its 1907 appearance. Dalí was a guest at the hotel for one month a year for three decades, staying in the former royal suite of Alphonse XIII. Both a suite and the central restaurant, beneath its glass dome, now bear his name.

## What we like

### The panorama

The sensation, from the outdoor salon of the Belle Étoile Penthouse Suite, of having been dropped right into the middle of the Tuileries.

### The moment

When it is cloudy and rainy, enjoy breakfast in bed with Cédric Grolet's pastries.

### The flavour

Alain Ducasse’s lightly cooked Noirmoutier sea bream, accompanied by a glass of Billecart-Salmon.
