## Our Rajasthan resort is a royal palace of well-being

A beautiful 14th-century fort sensitively converted to become a Six Senses sanctuary of well-being. Originally owned by a Rajasthani Royal Family, it faces the Chauth ka Barwara Mandir temple. The significant conservation effort incorporates two palaces and two temples within the walled fort. The design of Six Senses resort in Rajasthan reinterprets the gracious and regal ambiance of a bygone era dating back 700 years.

## Rewilding the forest to the east and reviving the Barwara lake

The historic fort has been sensitively preserved and converted, and enjoys a sensational view of the lake and temple. The landscaping integrates purely endemic plants and water features. The property is working with the local community to rewild the forests to the east and revive the sacred lake to its former glory.

## Our Journal

Since the 1300s, Fort Barwara, along with neighboring Chauth ka Barwara temple, has been home to many legends. To understand the ethos of our heritage property is to understand the folk symbols and stories surrounding it, even in its most modern avatar as our Six Senses hotel.

### Walk on the wild side

Six Senses Fort Barwara is rewilding to remove invasive plants, replant endemic flora, and bring back birdlife, jackals, and leopards, vital for rebalancing the ecology.
