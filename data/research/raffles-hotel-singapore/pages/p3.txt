## A refined sanctuary dedicated to your holistic wellbeing

A tranquil hideaway in the gardens, Raffles Spa promises to take guests and local visitors on a journey of rejuvenation, with rituals inspired by the wellness philosophies of Asia and an array of soothing, therapeutic body and facial treatments. Relaxation is enhanced by hydrothermal experiences, while fitness is supported with personal training, yoga, Pilates and a 25m outdoor pool. To promote 360 wellbeing, transformative holistic healing modalities are also offered, including soulful meditation, sound therapy and reiki.

## Our philosophy

Achieving a balance between exertion and relaxation is the key to inner tranquillity and rejuvenation. Our thoughtful and knowledgeable therapists and practitioners will guide you to rediscover mental, physical and spiritual harmony, leaving you feeling enlivened and transformed. Inspired by the healing energies of gemstones, Raffles Spa signature treatments help restore this balance. Our traditional treatments and Asian-inspired rituals are expertly personalised to ensure that your sensory escape is everything you hoped for, and more. Escape. Indulge. Transform.

## Mat Pilates at The Lawn with Space2B

Set within the tranquil greenery of Raffles Lawn, this midweek session offers a gentle recalibration through movement and stillness.

Each session begins with Mat Pilates, guiding you through slow, breath-led movements that quietly strengthen and build core stability with mindful precision. The session concludes with an Alchemy Crystal Sound Bath, designed to relax the body and ease the nervous system into rest.

Held every Wednesday evening, this one-hour experience provides a grounding pause amidst the week, where strength and stillness come together in quiet harmony.

In collaboration with Space2B, each session is thoughtfully led and open to all levels.

Available from June 2026 to December 2026.

**Details**

Day and Time: Every Wednesday, 6:30pm - 7:30pm

Venue: The Lawn, Raffles Hotel Singapore.

Limited spaces available. Advance booking is recommended.

## Frequently asked questions

### What treatments are available at Raffles Spa?

Raffles Spa at Raffles Hotel Singapore offers a comprehensive range of luxury treatments, including bespoke facials, therapeutic body massages, and specialized hand and foot care. Located within the Raffles Arcade, the spa features seven treatment suites named after gemstones and focuses on a wellness philosophy of "Escape, Indulge and Transform".

### How can I enhance my relaxation experience?

Indulge in cutting-edge skincare with Omorovicza products, designed to hydrate, repair, and rejuvenate for radiant skin. Experience the hydrothermal facilities at Raffles Spa Singapore, featuring a vitality pool with Peruvian Pink Salt, a Herbal Sauna Room with calming aromas, an Aromatherapy Steam Room with pure salt vapour, and an Ice Fountain for cooling relaxation.

### What is the Gem Suite Experience?

The Gem Suite Experience at Raffles Spa Singapore is a private, luxurious spa journey where you and your companion can customise a 90‑minute treatment and enjoy exclusive use of the ensuite hydrothermal facilities — including an aromatic steam room, UV sauna and vitality pool — followed by a 60‑minute Raffles Afternoon Tea with champagne for a truly elevated and intimate wellness experience.

### Are there fitness options at Raffles Spa?

Yes, the 24-hour fitness centre features Technogym equipment, personal training, and a 25m outdoor pool for your fitness needs.

### What holistic healing modalities are offered?

Explore various holistic healing modalities offered include soulful meditation, sound therapy (such as crystal bowl sound healing), reiki, craniosacral therapy, lymphatic drainage, basic and hatha yoga, and tantric awakening for complete mind–body wellness.
