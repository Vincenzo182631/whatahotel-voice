# Rooms &    Signature Suites

Santa Monica Proper Hotel

Our 262 guest rooms and suites were designed by Kelly Wearstler and boast views of the surrounding landscape, many with terraces. Featuring mid-century European-inspired furnishings curvaceous settees and wing chairs, complemented by geometric wall sconces. Enjoy a restful sleep on Proper beds dressed with crisp Italian linens and hypo-allergenic pillows and well-appointed bathrooms equipped with walk-in travertine showers, marble vanities, and premium Aesop bath products. Beyond the rooms, guests enjoy access to our state-of-the-art fitness center and ability to book private Recovery Suites or access to the Proper Club Terrace, with saunas, cold plunges, and red-light therapy options.

## FAQ

What types of rooms and suites are available?

Santa Monica Proper offers a diverse room lineup on the Rooms & Suites page, including Deluxe King and Double Queen rooms, Landmark Garden and Balcony Terrace rooms, suites like the Proper One‑Bedroom, Proper Two‑Bedroom, Family Suite (sleeps up to six), and specialized spaces such as Performance Wellness rooms.

What views do guests get from the rooms?

Rooms feature a range of views including city, garden, pool, and landmark outdoor scenery. Select rooms like the Garden and Balcony Terrace offer private patios and outdoor space. Explore specific room categories here.

How many guests can each room type fit?

Room capacities vary: Double Queen and Family Suites accommodate 4 to 6 guests, while Deluxe and Premier King rooms are ideal for two. Suites offer flexible layouts suitable for families or groups.

Are there ADA-accessible room options?

Yes. Many room types-including Premier King, Premier Balcony King, Garden Terrace, and Landmark Terrace-offer ADA-accessible features like roll-in showers and accessible layouts. These can be viewed on the Rooms page or requested via Guest Services.

Is the hotel pet-friendly?

Yes. Santa Monica Proper is pet-friendly and welcomes dogs. Designated pet-friendly accommodations are listed on the Amenities page.

What in-room amenities can I expect?

All accommodations include luxury travertine bathrooms, high-end linens, and wellness-focused options like the Performance Wellness rooms. Full amenity details are listed under each room type on the Rooms page.

Can I request connecting rooms or special setup?

Santa Monica Proper offers flexible room configurations, including connecting rooms and multi-bedroom suites. Contact Reservations or Guest Services to arrange adjoining setups or group bookings.

How do I choose the right room for my needs?

Use filters on the Rooms page to compare bed type, view, capacity, ADA access, and wellness features. For help choosing the best option, reach out to Reservations.
