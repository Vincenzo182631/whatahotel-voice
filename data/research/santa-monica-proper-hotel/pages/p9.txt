# Rooftop Pool

Santa Monica Proper Hotel

Proper boasts the Westside’s exclusive rooftop pool, offering breathtaking views of the surf, coastline, mountains, and cityscape. Whether you prefer lounging poolside, relaxing in cabanas or private alcoves, gathering at the circular bar with a fireplace, or dining at our award-winning Mediterranean-inspired restaurant, Calabra, every moment promises a memorable escape.

## Pool Details

Heated, expansive, and family-friendly, our rooftop pool is designed for restorative downtime. Relax on loungers with essentials on hand: plush towels, SPF, cooling aloe, and more. Hydration comes naturally with fresh-pressed juice shots and market fruit.

Pool Hours: 8am – Sundown

## Alcoves & Private Spaces

Daybeds, alcoves, and private suites offer space to unwind with friends or family, complete with full food and beverage service from Calabra and elevated touches like Aesop sunscreen and chilled fruit skewers.

## Poolside Dining

Calabra’s rooftop menu brings together California freshness and Mediterranean ease, mezze spreads, seasonal produce, turmeric tonics, and light dishes designed for grazing and gathering. Stay a while.

## FAQ

What makes the rooftop pool at Santa Monica Proper unique?

It’s the only rooftop pool on the Westside of Santa Monica, featuring panoramic views of the Pacific Ocean, coastline, mountains, and city skyline—complete with a sophisticated Balearic-style setting and poolside bar.

Who has access to the rooftop pool—guests, members, or locals?

Hotel guests and Proper Club members have full access. Non-guests may also enjoy the pool via day passes, subject to availability.

What are the rooftop pool hours?

The pool is open daily from 12 PM to 5 PM, with dining service provided by Calabra.

Are cabanas or lounge chairs available for reservation?

Yes—guests can reserve cabanas, lounge chairs, and poolside seating through the ResortPass system or by contacting Guest Services.

Is food and beverage service available poolside?

Absolutely—poolside food and drink service is provided by Calabra rooftop restaurant and bar, offering Mediterranean-inspired cuisine and cocktails.

Can non-hotel guests access the pool with a day pass or membership?

Yes—non-guests can access the rooftop pool with a day pass (available via ResortPass) or through a Proper Club membership, subject to availability. Learn more on the Amenities page

Are there rooftop wellness offerings like yoga or fitness classes?

Yes—rooftop yoga and movement classes are offered seasonally as part of wellness programming for guests and members, often scheduled on the rooftop terrace.

How do I reserve a cabana or book poolside amenities?

Reservations for cabanas, day passes, and poolside services can be made via ResortPass, the Proper Club portal, or by contacting Guest Services directly.
