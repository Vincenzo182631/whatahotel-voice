## **Santa Monica’s Luxury Wellness Hotel**

Designed by Kelly Wearstler, our spaces flow with light, layered textures, and sculptural details across 262 rooms and suites. Mornings might start with rooftop yoga, a beach run, a workout in our expansive fitness center, or breakfast in Palma, our art-filled lobby lounge. Days unfold with immersive treatments at Surya Spa, red light therapy and contrast therapy in our Recovery Suite, where sauna and cold plunge rituals work in tandem alongside the Ammortal Chamber to support recovery, restoration, and long-term vitality.

Poolside afternoons at Calabra follow naturally. Evenings are shaped by chef-driven, seed oil–free dining, craft cocktails, curated music, and rooftop gatherings under the stars. From science-backed wellness and holistic spa experiences to culturally driven programming and quintessential California design, Santa Monica Proper offers a luxury wellness hotel experience in the heart of the city.

## Rooms & Suites

With interiors by Kelly Wearstler, every room channels the rhythm of the coast-curved windows, sun-warmed textures, and garden terraces made for golden hour.

Signature Proper beds, premium amenities, and intuitive controls for lighting and shades meet a wellness-forward experience, including newly introduced Wellness Rooms featuring Eight Sleep technology, air and water purification, and curated sleep rituals.

## Eat & Drink

At Calabra, panoramic Pacific views meet coastal Mediterranean plates and biodynamic wines. All-day dining at Palma brings together artful interiors, seasonal produce, boutique wines, and handcrafted cocktails, all steps from the lobby.

Proper’s approach to dining honors sustainability, seasonality, and local sourcing, from Santa Monica Farmers Market fruits to seed oil-free cooking and responsibly sourced seafood.

## Wellness

Santa Monica Proper’s wellness offerings seamlessly integrate into everyday moments, inviting you to move, recharge, and recover with intention.

Discover a hotel that is equal parts coastal sanctuary and innovative wellness lab.

- **Daily movement classes** ranging from Pilates and Yoga to Tai Chi and Breathwork

- **Full-service Recovery Suites** featuring cold plunges, contrast therapy, and red light treatments

- **State-of-the-art Fitness Center** with free weights, treadmills, multiple reformers, and self-operated VO₂ Max testing

- Award-winning Ayurvedic therapies at **Surya Spa**

Explore Wellness

## Gatherings

Santa Monica Proper offers a series of Kelly Wearstler-designed flexible spaces, including four meeting rooms, a ballroom, courtyards and terraces, and the area’s only rooftop pool-deck-restaurant Calabra, with panoramic ocean views. Proper’s events team offers multi-layered personalization, with market-driven catering menus for indoor and outdoor meetings, weddings, special events, and private dining. Anything less wouldn’t be Proper.
