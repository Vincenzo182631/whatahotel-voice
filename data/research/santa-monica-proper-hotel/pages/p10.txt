## FAQ

##### Is Santa Monica Proper Hotel pet-friendly?

Yes, Santa Monica Proper welcomes dogs (up to two per room, each under 45 lbs) for a $100 non-refundable fee per stay. Learn more about pet-friendly policies on our Amenities page.

##### Who designed Santa Monica Proper Hotel?

The hotel was designed by acclaimed interior designer Kelly Wearstler, who blended coastal-modern aesthetics with layered textures. Her design touches can be seen throughout the Rooms & Suites and Photo Gallery.

##### What wellness offerings are available at Santa Monica Proper Hotel?

Wellness at Santa Monica Proper includes Surya Spa’s Ayurvedic treatments, a 3,000 sq ft fitness center, rooftop pool, daily movement classes, and a full Recovery Suite with advanced wellness tools.

##### What restaurants are on-site at Santa Monica Proper Hotel?

The hotel features two signature dining venues: Palma, an all-day lobby lounge, and Calabra, a rooftop Mediterranean restaurant and bar. Explore all options on the Restaurants & Bars page.

##### What fitness amenities are available at Santa Monica Proper Hotel?

Guests have access to a fully equipped fitness center with cardio and strength equipment, reformers, and 24/7 access.

##### Where is Santa Monica Proper Hotel located?

The hotel is located at 700 Wilshire Boulevard in Santa Monica, just blocks from the beach, the Pier, and Third Street Promenade. See Getting Here for directions.

##### What makes Santa Monica Proper Hotel unique?

Santa Monica Proper combines a restored 1920s landmark with bold design, offers the Westside’s only rooftop pool deck, and provides immersive wellness experiences in a refined coastal setting. Learn more on the About, Amenities, and Restaurants pages.
