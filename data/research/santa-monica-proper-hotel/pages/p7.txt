Santa Monica Proper Hotel

## **Movement, Recovery, and Rest, Woven into Every Stay**

At Santa Monica Proper, wellness isn’t just an amenity, it’s the essence of our hotel experience. Recognized among the leading wellness hotels in Los Angeles, we bring together movement, recovery, Ayurvedic healing, science-backed customized protocols, and seed-oil-free dining. Whether you’re seeking a wellness **hotel in Santa Monica,** or a contemporary retreat by the coast, each offering is designed to meet you where you are and elevate you from there.

## FAQ

What wellness experiences are offered at Santa Monica Proper?

Guests can enjoy yoga and fitness programming (HIIT, bootcamps), Ayurvedic treatments at Surya Spa, and access to our Recovery Suites , featuring the Ammortal Chamber, Theralight 360 red light bed, infrared sauna, and cold plunge.

What amenities are included in the fitness center and recovery studio?

The 2,500 sq ft fitness studio includes Technogym and Peloton equipment. The Recovery Suites offer cold plunge pools, dry sauna, red-light therapy, compression boots, and access to the Ammortal Chamber.

Can I book private wellness therapies or recovery treatments?

Absolutely. Guests can book private therapies including Ayurvedic spa treatments, massages, and sessions in the Ammortal Chamber.

What in-room wellness amenities are offered in rooms or suites?

Wellness Rooms feature curated sleep-enhancing design, air purification, lighting controls, recovery gear (e.g. yoga mats, rollers), and premium wellness toiletries.

Are there partnership programs with local wellness experts or retreats?

Yes. Proper Club members have access to local fitness studios (e.g. Barry’s, BODYROK, Sweat Yoga), wellness thought leadership panels, and discounts on Ayurvedic treatments at Surya Spa.

Is wellness programming available for both hotel guests and locals?

Guests and Proper Club members can participate in classes, recovery sessions, and spa treatments.

How do I book a wellness class or treatment during my stay?

Guests can reserve classes by contacting Guest Services or Reservations directly or by visiting our Fitness Class schedule on our website.
