# Fitness

Santa Monica Proper Hotel

Maintaining health and wellness is key at Santa Monica Proper. Our newly expanded 2,500-square-foot fitness center is thoughtfully designed to elevate your wellness journey. Located just below our Recovery Suite and across from Surya Spa, the space is equipped with state-of-the-art strength, cardio, and functional equipment.

Open 24/7, it features expert programming, personal training, and complimentary yoga and bootcamp classes. VO2 Max testing, once reserved for elite athletes, is now available to guests, offering clinical insight into cardiovascular fitness and performance. Pair it with a session in our Ammortal Chamber for advanced recovery and longevity.

## Fitness Studio

The Proper fitness studio offers three expansive, naturally lit spaces with floor-to-ceiling glass windows, equipped with state-of-the-art strength training, weight, and cardio equipment. Enjoy wellness experiences like a cold plunge, sauna and direct access to outdoor classes such as yoga and bootcamp. Each space is reservable for private use, with options for group sessions and personalized training available upon request.

## FAQ

What fitness facilities are available at Santa Monica Proper?

The hotel features a 24/7, 2,500 sq ft fitness center equipped with Peloton bikes, Woodway treadmills, Watt bikes, Technogym elliptical/stair climbers, Concept II rowers/ski ergs, reformer Pilates machines, free weights, and functional-training equipment.

Are group classes like yoga or boot camp offered at Santa Monica Proper?

Yes-guests can join on-site fitness classes, including yoga, HIIT, and bootcamp-style training. Additionally, Proper Club members can attend classes at partner studios like Barry’s, BodyRok, Sweat Yoga, and Studio Elevate.

What recovery or wellness amenities are available?

The Recovery Suites, Hyperion and Soma, include a variety of healing modalities, encompassing cold plunge, dry sauna, red-light therapy, compression therapy, and private showers. Hyperion’s experience is more about regeneration, while Soma’s is more performance-based.

Is the fitness center open 24 hours a day?

Yes-the fitness center is open 24 hours daily, providing uninterrupted access to all equipment and facilities.

Can I book personal training or one-on-one wellness sessions?

Absolutely-guests and Proper Club members can book private training, recovery treatments (like the Ammortal Chamber), Ayurvedic therapies, and personalized sessions through Guest Services or the fitness/club staff.

How do I reserve a fitness class, training session, or recovery treatment?

Reservations can be made via the Proper Club portal, by contacting Guest Services, or directly coordinating with fitness and spa teams.

Is the fitness or recovery center open to non-hotel guests?

Yes-non-guests can access the Recovery Suites via booked treatments, and the Proper Club membership offers full facility, class, and recovery access, including pool, spa, and workspaces.

Where can I find class schedules or a list of available equipment?

The latest class schedules, equipment inventory, and availability details are provided through the Proper Club portal or available upon request through the fitness desk or Guest Services.
