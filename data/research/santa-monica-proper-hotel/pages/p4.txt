Each experience is supported by personalized health insights, fresh seed-oil-free dining, and cultural programming from Proper Presents, including live music and panel conversations.

## FAQ

See All

##### What are Proper Retreats?

Proper Retreats are curated, multi-day immersive wellness experiences hosted at Santa Monica Proper. Each retreat blends movement, recovery, mindfulness, nourishment, and community into a thoughtfully structured program designed to reset, restore, and elevate performance.

For upcoming dates and themes, email **smp.retreats@properhotel.com**.

##### What’s included in a Proper Retreat?

Each retreat is unique, but typically includes:

-Guided movement sessions (yoga, strength, breathwork, mobility)

-Recovery experiences (sauna, cold plunge, red light therapy, mineral bath)

-Access to Surya Spa offerings or curated treatments

-Expert-led workshops or wellness discussions

-Curated culinary programming

-A structured itinerary with intentional downtime

For detailed inclusions for a specific retreat, email **smp.retreats@properhotel.com**.

##### Where do retreat experiences take place?

Programming is fully integrated across the property, including:

-Rooftop and indoor movement spaces

-The Recovery Suite

-Surya Spa

-Private event spaces

-Select outdoor Santa Monica settings

##### How long are Proper Retreats?

Most retreats are 3-day intensives, but they can be extended into longer multi-day experiences. Availability is limited to preserve the intimacy of each cohort.

##### Can retreats be customized for private groups or corporate wellness?

Yes. Santa Monica Proper offers private retreat buyouts and custom wellness programming for brands, executive teams, and curated groups.

To discuss private retreats or partnership opportunities, contact **smp.retreats@properhotel.com**.

##### How do I reserve a spot?

Retreats are released in limited quantities and may sell out in advance. Early inquiry is recommended.

To join an upcoming retreat or request more information, please email **smp.retreats@properhotel.com**.
