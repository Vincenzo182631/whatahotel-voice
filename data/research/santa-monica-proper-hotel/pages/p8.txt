# Surya Spa

Santa Monica Proper Hotel

- Reserve Now

Experience our 3,000-square-foot flagship Proper x Surya Spa, a collaboration with internationally acclaimed Ayurvedic doctor, chef, and herbalist Martha Soffer, recognized by Goop, Elle and Refinery29. Centered around age-old Indian wellness principles from the leaders of modern Ayurveda, Surya offers therapeutic massage, steam and oil treatments, cooking, yoga, meditation, personalized Dosha Yoga consultations, and cleansing Panchakarma programs. Be nurtured and supported in our serene Santa Monica setting, with treatments available across six rooms, outdoor patios, and lounges – or inside the comfort of your Proper hotel room. Surya’s customized culinary offerings are available both in our on-site restaurants and in-room dining.

At check-in, receive your exclusive redeemable card, offering a Proper 15% discount on soothing treatments at our award-winning Surya Spa, ensuring your stay in Santa Monica is a journey of rejuvenation and relaxation.

## Surya Spa Wellness Offerings

See All

Surya Sound Bath

_Inquire Now_

The deeply healing and restorative sounds of Surya’s crystal sound baths are profoundly relaxing and rejuvenating. Based on ancient healing techniques, the bowls’ gentle vibrations harmonize your body, mind, and spirit, literally balancing our brain’s hemispheres, nurturing ease and inner peace, even providing relief to chronic pain and headaches, while reducing stress, improving sleep, and increasing a lasting sense of well-being, calmness, and happiness.

Surya Lullaby Sound Bath

_(60 minutes, $375)_

Surya’s same healing Sound Bath, though in this case, performed at the end of your day, when you (and perhaps your partner) are tucked into bed, and ready for sleep. With the lights low, the sound bath musician melts away your worries and concerns, sending you into a deep and restorative rest, and with the final mantra, turns off the lights, tip-toes out, and gently locks the door behind them.

Surya Dosha Yoga

_(60 minutes, $350)_

Surya Dosha Yoga is a simple and practical form of Ayurvedic yoga, designed to help balance our innate constitutions, our Ayurvedic _doshas_, by either calming us, cooling us, or energizing us, as we might need. Each session is guided by the best instructors in Los Angeles, who will customize your flow to balance whatever is needed, leaving you in a restored and revitalized state.

Surya's Ayurvedic Astrology

_(90 minutes, $485)_

Vedic Astrology, known as Jyotish, or the Wisdom of Light, is a practice that predates western astrology, and dives deeply into the planetary patterns present at the time of our birth, gleaning valuable clues that help us understand our life’s journey. In an in-depth and personal session, your _Jyotishi_ will carefully analyze and interpret the patterns unique to your chart and life, and review both the challenges and advantages in which you currently find yourself, in order to optimize your full potential. Each session offers practical measures to overcome any areas of difficulty, personally and inter-personally, and provides the confidence needed manifest our true destiny, creating success, happiness, and harmony on all levels.

Transcendental Meditation Instruction

_(3-4 days, an hour a day, $910)_

In partnership with the David Lynch Foundation and Santa Monica’s local Transcendental Meditation center, this 3 or 4 day experience takes only an hour a day, and has immediate, positive effects. TM training is a simple and natural mental technique that gives proven benefits for your mind, body and emotions, with a process carefully personalized for you by a certified TM teacher, and taught one-on-one. The TM technique is an effortless and evidence-based meditation technique you can do anywhere, and receive benefits from as soon as you begin. Though the first session is one-on-one, if couples, families, or groups wish to learn together, the following days’ instruction are perfectly suited for that experience. TM is remarkable easy, with no prior experience is required, and the results are profound and lasting.

Surya Dosha Yoga Nidra

_(60 minutes, $350)_

In this traditional and effortless yoga experience, through guided meditations, visualization, and breath and body awareness, you will fall into a welcome state of relaxation, and an even blissful state of being. Give yourself the rewarding gift of finding the deeper sense of connection with body and mind, a journey of discovery that gently guides you to your deepest self.

Surya Breath-Work

_(60 minutes, $350)_

Surya’s breath-work practice, designed to help us expand our awareness and consciousness, beings with a reconnection to our current intentions, and what challenges we may be facing. A simple two-part breath gently activates what Ayurveda calls our “energetic body,” and helps move out of our mind, body, and spirit whatever we don’t need. This active breath-work fills your system with energy and gives you direct access to the healing potential of your own being. As emotions clear, we discover a space of stillness, vibrancy, and peace, allowing us to build a deeper relationship with all that we are. Each session ends, if desired, with space for discussion, meditative reflection, and guidance for creative, on-going practice. As the buddhist master Thich Nhat Hanh liked to say, breath is the bridge which connects life to consciousness, and in this session, you will experience that awakening your own life, too.

Surya Grandmaster Healing Tai-Chai & Chi Gong

_(60 minutes, $350)_

Grandmaster Ten “Kancho” Hirakawa teaches you the moves of a core Tai Chi “kata” that you can practice on your own, as well as a series of Chi Gong movements that revitalize organs, mind, and breath. Tai Chi, centuries old, is recognized even by western medicine as a way to decrease stress, anxiety, and depression, and to improve mood, energy, balance, strength and agility. The remarkable situation of being able to offer private lessons by a Grandmaster is a jewel in Proper’s wellness crown!

Surya's Ayurvedic Cooking Class

_(2 hours, starting at $350)_

In this unique and practical experience, you will be paired with one of Surya’s master chefs as you dive into Ayurvedic cooking essentials, recipes, tools, and techniques, all personalized to your unique constitution and any current imbalances. You’ll enjoy one of Surya’s famous and delicious Ayurvedic meals, and have all the tools you need to add this easy and balancing style of nutritious dining to your on-the-go repertoire. Group pricing also available if you’d like to bring your friends.

Remote Energetic Healing Session with Internationally Renowned Howard Wills

_(60 minutes, $550)_

For 50 years, Howard Wills has worked with people all over the world and witnessed marvelous, miraculous transformations. His life-transforming gatherings and private sessions help people permanently release obstacles that block them from living as they can – expansive, healthy and joyful lives filled with love, happiness and well-being. As Howard himself says, “when we practice the art of forgiveness and positive, non-judgmental thinking and behavior, we start feeling good, happy and free. When we start feeling good, happy and free, complete well-being in all areas of our lives will follow.” Howard’s work can positively influence all aspects of your life, from personal health concerns, to family concerns, to relationships, to issues at work at beyond. And guests often report that sessions with Howard have immediate, wide-reaching, and long-lasting positive effects.

## FAQ

What type of spa is Surya Spa at Santa Monica Proper?

Surya Spa is a 3,000 sq ft flagship Ayurvedic wellness sanctuary created in collaboration with Dr. Martha Soffer and styled by Kelly Wearstler. It combines traditional healing rituals with a modern California sensibility.

What treatments and wellness experiences are offered at Surya Spa?

The spa offers a rich lineup including Surya Sound Bath, Surya Lullaby Sound Bath, Dosha Yoga, four-handed Abhyanga massage, steam therapies, personalized oil treatments, meditation guidance, cooking classes, and full-scale Panchakarma retreats. Learn more about offerings on the Amenities page.

What facilities are available at the spa?

Surya Spa features six private treatment rooms, a meditation room, herbal-infused steam rooms, fountain patios, and an intimate herbal oil-making station-all immersed in a tranquil indoor-outdoor setting.

Who can use Surya Spa?

Services are available to both hotel guests and non-guests, with appointments required and spa packages available even without an overnight stay.

What are the spa’s hours and location?

Located at 700 Wilshire Blvd, Surya Spa is open daily from 8 AM–8 PM. Treatments can be booked by phone or email.

Are extended wellness programs available?

Yes-Surya Spa offers 3–28 day Panchakarma wellness retreats, cooking and meditation classes, and private Ayurveda consultations for in-depth healing journeys.

Can I stay at the hotel while attending a spa treatment?

Absolutely-you can book a Surya Spa + hotel package using the “SURYA” rate code, which includes treatment access and lodging.

How do I make a spa reservation or learn more?

Book appointments online at Surya Spa, call (310) 459‑7715, or email info@suryaspa.com.
