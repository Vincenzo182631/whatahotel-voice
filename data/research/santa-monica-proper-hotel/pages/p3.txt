# Proper Sleep Room

Santa Monica Proper Hotel

## SLEEP DEEPLY. WAKE RESTORED.

The Proper Sleep Room is a deeply restorative hospitality experience designed to optimize rest and renewal. Pairing Eight Sleep’s best-in-class sleep technology with Proper’s signature Kelly Wearstler design, each room is designed to support the body’s natural circadian rhythm and create the conditions for a deeper, more restorative night’s sleep.

A continuation of Proper’s longstanding wellness ethos, the experience brings together innovative sleep technology, bespoke wellness amenities, and elevated design to transform the way you rest, recover, and wake.

## The Bed

Proper rest begins here. Experience the innovative Eight Sleep system with individualized climate controls, layered with crisp Bellino Fine Linens, hypoallergenic pillows, and custom-designed throws to create an ideal environment for deep and restorative sleep.

## The Bath

Luxurious bathrooms invite a sense of calm with spacious travertine walk-in showers, marble vanities, and premium Aesop bath amenities. Filterbaby water filtration ensures every part of your bathing ritual is pure and refreshing.

## Technology

Stay connected and in control with premium WiFi, Apple TV, and bedside controls for blackout shades and lighting. The Proper TV app makes it easy to order in-room dining, request additional amenities, or schedule treatments at the nearby Surya Spa.

## FAQ

1\. What are Proper Sleep Rooms designed for?

These rooms are curated to support restoration, recovery, and relaxation, with features like sleep-enhancing design, purifier-equipped air, and soothing layouts.

2\. What in-room wellness amenities are included?

Expect offerings such as adjustable lighting, premium air filtration, wellness toiletries, and dedicated space for light stretching or meditation.

3\. How do Proper Sleep Rooms differ from standard rooms?

They go beyond typical comforts—featuring health-forward design, curated wellness products, and layouts that promote mental and physical restoration.

4\. Can guests book in-room wellness treatments?

Yes—guests may arrange in-room massage, meditation sessions, or wellness consultations via Guest Services or at check-in.

5\. Are wellness features available in other parts of the hotel?

Yes, these rooms complement broader offerings like the on-site fitness studio and rooftop movement classes and the Surya Spa.

6\. Can I book Proper Sleep Rooms online?

Absolutely—select the room directly from the Rooms page during the booking process.

7\. Are Proper Sleep Rooms accessible or pet-friendly?

Designated accessible rooms are available, and select rooms may be pet-friendly with advance request. Please inquire with Reservations.

8\. How do I upgrade or request wellness add-ons for my room?

Guests can upgrade to a Proper Sleep Room or add amenities like aromatherapy options, wellness kits, or treatment bookings via Reservations or Guest Services.
