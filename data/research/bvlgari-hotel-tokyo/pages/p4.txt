# Exclusive Hotel in Downtown Tokyo

### Overview

Occupying floors 40 through 45 of an ultra-skyscraper in the Yaesu District, Bvlgari Hotel Tokyo is home to 98 impeccably designed Rooms and Suites, and such iconic Bvlgari destinations as Il Ristorante - Niko Romito, Sushi Hōseki,The Bvlgari Bar, the fireplace Lounge and Bvlgari Dolci. Representing the 8th exquisite gem in the Bvlgari universe, it expands the Bvlgari name in Tokyo, enriching the Maison’s distinctive experience in one of Asia’s most electrifying cities.

Brochure

### Services

To ensure a seamless luxury experience from start to finish, the Hotel offers packing and unpacking services tailored to each guest’s unique needs. In addition, arrangements can be made for barber services, a tailor or, thanks to a special partnership with Maison Berluti, a state-of-the-art shoeshine.
