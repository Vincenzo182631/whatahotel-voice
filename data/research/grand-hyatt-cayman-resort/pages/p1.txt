## Grand Cayman in its Element

Grand Hyatt Grand Cayman Resort & Spa is an invitation into the power and wonder of the Caribbean, shaped by the elements. Ideally situated on the southern tip of Seven Mile Beach and just 10 minutes from Owen Roberts International Airport, the resort offers imaginative and dynamic experiences that nurture a personal connection with what makes Grand Cayman iconic. Whether swimming, snorkeling, paddle boarding or simply soaking in cerulean waters of the Caribbean, opportunities for adventure and relaxation abound. Indulge in elemental spa treatments, lounge at one of our oceanfront pools or savor exceptional cuisine at one of our distinctive dining venues. Captivating art, architecture and design frame every moment while imaginative dining thrills the senses. To stay with us is to be moved by the current of island life, and to let yourself be amazed by the wonders of the Caribbean.

## EXPLORE

Grand Club — An Atmosphere of Effortless Calm

Exclusive to Grand Club guests, enjoy daily breakfast in an elegant indoor lounge, complemented by an oceanfront terrace with refined dining and lounge seating. Each evening, return for cocktail hour featuring curated beverages and a selection of hors d’oeuvres.

Camp Hyatt

Calling all young explorers! Welcome to Camp Hyatt—a playful and engaging experience for children ages 4–12. With a thoughtfully planned selection of daily activities, campers can enjoy marine-themed adventures, arts and crafts, and a variety of supervised games and recreation. Family-friendly resort activities include kid’s yoga, sandcastle showdown and seasonal family arts and crafts. Half day: $80 USD, 9 a.m.–12 p.m. Full day: $160 USD, 9 a.m.–4 p.m.

MERA SPA

Allow us to pamper you, Cayman style, in our serene, luxurious spa with 14 indoor and outdoor rooms and a host of treatments, massages and experiences to choose from.

Pools

Our oceanfront pools are thoughtfully designed for a variety of moments—playful for family fun, restorative calm in the spa’s vitality pool and refined relaxation at the main pool with handcrafted cocktails served from our swim-up bar.

Fitness Center

Don’t miss a workout while enjoying your seaside escape. Our modern fitness center has you covered with the latest cardio and weight training equipment, plus plenty of space for yoga and more.

Invite your dog to join in the beach vacation fun and keep everyone’s tails wagging. All pets must be on leash in public areas. Pets cannot be left unattended in guest rooms.
