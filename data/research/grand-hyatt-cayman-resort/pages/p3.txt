# Dining

Grand Hyatt Grand Cayman Resort & Spa provides diverse dining options from morning to night. Start with coffee and bomboloni at Breadkind, enjoy coastal Mexican cuisine at Lo Tide or indulge in ceviches at Hi Tide at sunset. For dinner, Salt + Cinder offers globally inspired dishes in an open-kitchen setting, while the Lobby Lounge serves cocktails, fine wines and tapas in an elegant atmosphere. Each dining venue at our Grand Cayman resort aims to create a memorable culinary outing.

## Salt + Cinder

Salt + Cinder is a globally inspired restaurant that celebrates the art of cooking through the primal elements of water and fire. Our menu brings bold flavors from across the world, accented with a vibrant touch of Caribbean flair. Our beverage program is an experience in itself, featuring curated cocktails and a diverse wine collection.

## Breadkind Market

Breadkind is our casual Italian café, serving fresh, flavorful fare from morning to evening — perfect for dining in or on the go. Start your day with barista-made coffee and home-baked pastries. Midday offers vibrant salads, hearty bowls, and artisan sandwiches. As the sun sets, enjoy Gelato Hour with house-made gelatos, drizzled with island rum or finished with champagne.

## Lo Tide Bar & Restaurant

Framed by lush tropical flora and sweeping views of the turquoise Caribbean Sea, Lo Tide is the resort’s vibrant outdoor restaurant and bar. The culinary experience celebrates the bright, sun-kissed flavors of coastal Mexico, with fresh, colorful dishes crafted for relaxed poolside dining in Grand Cayman. As evening falls, the space glows as a picture-perfect venue to watch the sun dip below the horizon.

## Hi Tide

Perched above Lo Tide, Hi Tide is a terrace lounge designed for savoring the famed Cayman sunsets. This stylish cevichería serves a vibrant selection of distinctive ceviches, bringing together bold and fresh flavors. Complementing the menu, our agave-inspired cocktails are the perfect match for the shifting hues of the evening sky.

### Hours

            5:00 PM - 10:00 PM

## Lobby Lounge

The Lobby Lounge is the perfect place to unwind with thoughtfully curated cocktails inspired by modern takes on timeless classics — drinks that outlast eras. Our wine program features a distinguished selection of organic, biodynamic, and sustainable labels, paired with elevated bar-style tapas designed to complement every sip.
