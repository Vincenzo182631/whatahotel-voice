# Hotel Policies

Pet Policy

Unparalleled oceanfront luxury awaits you and your furry companion. Dogs and cats weighing under 70 pounds and a maximum number of two pets per reservation are permitted complimentary during your stay. We welcome all guests traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not considered as pets and therefore are not subject to this Pet Policy. We kindly ask you to inform the hotel at least three days in advance of your arrival date if you are planning to bring your pet, so we may ensure their utmost comfort. For more information on out pet policy, please contact the hotel directly.

Pet access to our restaurants is limited to Lo Tide. Dogs and cats must weigh under 70 pounds and a maximum number of two pets per reservation are permitted.

Resort Fee

A daily resort fee of $85.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

- Welcome drink upon arrival

- Daily instructor-led wellness classes
- Valet parking with daily in/out privileges
- Enhanced high-speed internet
- Daily family-friendly resort activities (Example: kids face painting, entertainer by the pool, family handprints, sunset paintings, movie night)
- Access to outdoor games lawn
- Nonmotorized water sports equipment (kayak, standing paddle board, floating raft, water tricycle)
- Snorkeling gear rental
- Bicycle rental
- Weekly food and beverage masterclass (limited availability)
- Local cultural experiences (activities will vary and may include: Cayman marine life educational sessions for children, custom souvenir making, sessions with local craftsman, thatch art and coasters, local flora educational sessions)
