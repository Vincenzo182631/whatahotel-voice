# Dining

Indulge in fine‑dining experiences with dazzling city views at Grand Hyatt Shanghai. Seven remarkable restaurants and bars across upper‑floor venues take you on a fantastic gourmet journey and delight your taste buds amid stylish and relaxing settings. Cloud 9 on the 87th floor combines Western‑style dining with sky‑high lounging space with full‑scale city views, recognized as a top‑notch global event venue. Club Jin Mao on the 86th floor serves innovative Huaiyang and Shanghainese fusion dishes for business banquets and family gatherings. Canton on the 55th floor presents elaborate Cantonese cuisine. ON 56 features artistic decor alongside 270‑degree city views; Kobachi offers authentic Japanese cuisine. The Atrium on the 33rd‑floor atrium is perfect for afternoon tea with soothing live‑band performances. Pastry Boutique on the 1st floor provides handmade desserts ideal for gifts.

## Patio

Patio at the bottom of the 56th floor hotel atrium is an ideal venue for afternoon tea and evening cocktails. Guests may enjoy pleasant time with live piano performances.

## Club Jin Mao

Perched on the cloud‑high 86th floor of Jin Mao Tower, exclusive private dining rooms deliver a tranquil retreat with sweeping views over both banks of the Huangpu River. It presents high‑end private cloud‑top banquets and wins favour among global guests with personalised attentive service. It serves classic Huaiyang and Shanghainese cuisines with innovative Shanghai‑style flavours.

## Canton

Canton sits on the 55th floor with a modern‑style setting serving chef‑crafted authentic Cantonese cuisine. It has three distinct dining zones with exquisite bespoke designs for diverse occasions. The main dining hall features two Chinese‑pattern etched gilded glass screens with granite, timber and brass materials integrated harmoniously. Terrace seats overlook the double‑height lobby with stunning panoramic views of Shanghai. Six private rooms of different sizes with city views cater to business banquets, birthday parties and family dinners. The largest private room holds up to 40 guests. Its interior design perfectly combines Chinese motifs with Western‑style design elements.
