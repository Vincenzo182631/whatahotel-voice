## Welcome to Grand Hyatt Shanghai

Occupying floors 54 to 87 of the iconic Jin Mao Tower in the Lujiazui Financial District, the hotel features an octagonal pagoda design embodying exquisite oriental luxury. Perched high above the city, it commands panoramic views of the timeless Bund scenery and the glittering Huangpu River. Boasting 548 guest rooms, the hotel’s soaring 33-storey central atrium creates an architectural masterpiece. Our collection of seven distinctive restaurants and bars serves refined Chinese and international cuisines, blending culinary craftsmanship with artistic charm for a feast for both palate and senses.

## ROOMS & SUITES

A total of 548 rooms and suites occupy the 58th to 85th floors of the Jin Mao Tower. These elegant rooms are among the most spacious and refined in the city, featuring allusions to art deco heritage combined with the essence of classical Chinese style. In addition, our rooms are equipped with all the practical and technological essentials of the modern age.

Business Center

Located on lobby level 54, the Business Center at Grand Hyatt Shanghai provides a full range of office equipment and confidential secretarial services, such as translation, printing and parcel wrapping. Business cards can also be printed and delivered. The Business Center also offers private work areas for rental on an hourly or daily basis, as well as a conference room that can seat up to 10 people.
