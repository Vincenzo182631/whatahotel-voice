# Aspen's Only Five-Star Hotel

The city of Aspen at our front door, Aspen Mountain at our back door. And in between, a gathering place for the brave and the bright at The Little Nell. We're proud to be the only Five-Star, Five-Diamond hotel in Aspen — hospitality is our passion. From our kitchens to our cellars, from the Concierges in our lobby to the guides on our mountain, The Little Nell has spent nearly four decades earning its place as Aspen's favorite address.

Now, that same standard arrives in New York City, as The Nell New York opens Fall 2027 as the first-ever hotel within Rockefeller Center, continuing a legacy of firsts. Learn more about getting to The Little Nell, our team, our distinguished awards, and the rich history of the hotel below.
