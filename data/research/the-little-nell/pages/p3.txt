## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you dine at The Little Nell without staying at the hotel?

Yes. Element 47, Ajax Tavern, and The Wine Bar are all open to the public. Reservations are recommended, particularly during winter season, and can be made through Resy or by contacting the hotel directly.

### How many restaurants and bars are at The Little Nell?

The Little Nell is home to two restaurants — Element 47 and Ajax Tavern — two bars, and two speakeasies, plus in-room dining available around the clock. All are located at the base of Aspen Mountain in the heart of downtown Aspen.

### Which restaurant is best for a special occasion versus a casual meal?

Element 47 is The Little Nell's signature fine dining restaurant, recognized by the Michelin Guide and home to one of the world's most celebrated wine cellars — ideal for a special occasion or a long, considered dinner. For something more relaxed, Ajax Tavern's slopeside patio is a natural gathering spot for lunch or après ski.

### Do you need a reservation to eat at The Little Nell?

Reservations are strongly recommended, especially during winter and peak season. Both Element 47 and Ajax Tavern can be booked through Resy.

### Is the Michelin-recommended restaurant open to non-guests?

Yes. Element 47 is recommended in the Michelin Guide and welcomes all diners, not just hotel guests. Reservations are recommended and available through Resy.

### Is there somewhere to eat right off the slopes at The Little Nell?

Ajax Tavern sits directly slopeside at the base of Aspen Mountain, making it one of the most natural stops after a morning on the hill. The sun-soaked patio is a local institution, and the Ajax Wagyu double cheeseburger has earned a following of its own.
