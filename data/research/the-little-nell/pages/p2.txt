# Stay at Aspen's Only Luxury 5-star Hotel

Experience warm luxury at the base of Aspen Mountain and at the center of town. The Little Nell is Aspen's only Five-Star, Five-Diamond, ski-in/ski-out hotel. Rooms, suites, and private residences are each shaped by warm materials and considered detail, held together by a team that anticipates what's next.

For nearly four decades, we have been one of Aspen’s most beloved hotels. Wine and dine at two restaurants, two bars, and two speakeasies; restore at the Spa at The Little Nell; wake up steps from the Silver Queen Gondola. Ski concierge, in-room dining, heated pool, and complimentary transportation take care of the rest. The mountain is right outside. Everything else is right here.

### Residences at The Little Nell

Just steps from The Little Nell at the base of Aspen Mountain, the Residences offer a more private retreat — with amenities of their own, including dedicated rooftop pools, plus full access to The Little Nell's spa, restaurants, and signature five-star service. Available to book by the night, they range from refined guest rooms to spacious multi-bedroom residences with full living areas and kitchens — a natural fit for extended visits, families, and anyone who wants a little more room to settle in, with ski-in/ski-out access to legendary Aspen Mountain.
