# Settle into a sense of harmony

Where Wellness Unfolds Naturally

At The Little Nell, wellness isn’t something you schedule—it’s something you settle into. A mountain reset, woven through every part of your stay, from restorative treatments at our Aspen spa to nourishing cuisine, thoughtful fitness, and time spent out in the alpine. Grounded in ritual and rejuvenation, we aim to offer transformative travel that enhances well-being. Fresh air, mountain days, Aspen, as it should be. Whether you’re here for a quiet reset or something more immersive—a wellness retreat shaped by high-alpine air and unhurried moments—every detail is designed to bring you back to what matters most: connection, clarity, and the ease of feeling entirely at home.

### The Spa At The Little Nell

Welcome to Aspen's only ski-in/ski-out, slopeside spa. This summer, The Spa at The Little Nell welcomes world-renowned facialist Georgia Louise, the celebrityesthetician of choice. Enjoy her Lift + Sculpt facials, Hollywood EGF treatments, and advanced therapies, performed by Georgia during her in-residence visits or by our elite aestheticians trained in her methods. Her award-winning products are also available on-property. Appointments can be booked through The Spa at The Little Nell and fill quickly.

Learn More About the Spa

### Fitness Center

Located adjacent to The Spa at The Little Nell, our state-of-the-art fitness center is designed for movement at your own pace. Featuring Woodway treadmills, EMS training, Peloton bikes, Technogym equipment, and dedicated space for functional training, it's a place to reset, recharge, and stay in motion. From personalized training sessions to in-room Peloton deliveries, every detail is designed to support how you move-on your schedule, in your way.
