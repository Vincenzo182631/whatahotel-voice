##### Room Features

Step into unparalleled luxury with our Avenue and Hazelton Signature Suites, designed for special occasions and first-class entertaining. With over 1,236 square feet of space, revel in breathtaking panoramic views from the expansive rotunda living room, savour a gourmet meal in the separate dining area, and unwind in the plush king-size bed. With a walk-in wardrobe, luxury furnishings, and exquisite amenities, our suites are lavish escapes in the heart of Yorkville. Need more space? We offer interconnecting rooms for added convenience.
