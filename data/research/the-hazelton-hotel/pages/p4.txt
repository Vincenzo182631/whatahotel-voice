##### Room Features

Indulge in opulence in the Bellair Suite, the epitome of luxury in Toronto. With a spacious 1,860 square feet, indulge in stunning views of Yorkville from your very own private walk-out balcony. Perfect for entertaining, the suite features an open-concept living room, a chic dining area for up to 10 guests, a convenient pantry, and a private bar. Our flawlessly appointed suite is equipped with state-of-the-art amenities, guaranteed to exceed your every expectation. An interconnecting room available for added convenience.
