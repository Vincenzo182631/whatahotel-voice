##### Room Features

With a separate bedroom and living room, the Luxury Executive Suite offers extra space to relax and unwind. Located at the front of the hotel, the suite features a private balcony overlooking Yorkville Avenue, along with a generous granite bathroom with heated floors, a deep soaking tub, rainfall shower and double vanity.
