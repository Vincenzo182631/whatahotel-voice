##### Room Features

Ideal for stays with family or friends, the Luxury Two Queen Room overlooks Yorkville Avenue and offers plenty of space with two queen beds, a comfortable seating area, walk-in wardrobe and generous granite bathroom. Accommodating up to four guests, interconnecting room options are also available.
