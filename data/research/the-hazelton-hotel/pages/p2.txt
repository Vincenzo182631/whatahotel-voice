## Luxury Accommodations In Downtown Toronto

Distinctive design, generous proportions and a sense of residential ease define the rooms and suites at The Hazelton. Rich textures, and original artwork are paired with Galaxy Green granite bathrooms, deep soaking tubs and rainfall showers, while Juliet or walk-out balconies bring fresh air into every room. Custom furnishings and considered details reflect the hotel’s unmistakable character, complemented by the highly personalized service of a Forbes Five-Star, AAA Five Diamond and Two MICHELIN Key hotel in the heart of Yorkville.
