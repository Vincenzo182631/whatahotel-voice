Ideal for stays with family or friends, the Luxury Two Queen Room is tucked away at the back of the hotel and overlooks a laneway; offering plenty of space with two queen beds, a comfortable seating area, walk-in wardrobe and generous granite bathroom. Accommodating up to four guests, interconnecting room options are also available.
