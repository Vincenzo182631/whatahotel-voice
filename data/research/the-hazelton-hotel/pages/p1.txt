# The Hazelton Hotel is Canada's only Forbes Five-Star independent boutique hotel, AAA Five Diamond and Two MICHELIN Key

The Hazelton Hotel features 62 beautifully designed rooms and 15 exceptional suites, with Juliet or walk-out balconies and elegant furnishings. Beyond the spacious and elegant accommodations, discover ONE Restaurant with its wrap-around patio; The Spa by Valmont, with an indoor saltwater pool, steam rooms, fitness centre and bespoke treatments; elegant event spaces; and the private 25-seat Norman Jewison Cinema, complete with DCP capabilities.

Consistently recognized in the Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards and ranked #1 in Canada on Tripadvisor, The Hazelton has become an icon in Yorkville and a hotel leader on the global stage.

## FAQ

#### What are the timings for check-in and check-out?

Check-in time is 4:00 pm and check-out time is 12:00 noon. Should you arrive early and your room is not yet ready, our front desk would be happy to store your luggage and enjoy our facilities. On a case-to case basis, we can offer a late check-out subject to availability until 1:00pm. Late check-outs between 1:00 pm to 6:00pm will be charged at 50% of the existing room rate subject to taxes. A late check out past 6:00 pm will be charged a full night rate. If you would like to request an early check-in or late check-out, please contact our reservations team at reservations@thehazeltonhotel.com.

#### Is Wi-Fi available at the hotel?

Yes, WiFi is available in the hotel rooms and is free of charge.

#### What is the smoking policy of the hotel?

Smoking (including electronic cigarettes) is not permitted in our bedrooms or public areas of the hotel. Smoking is only permitted in the designated smoking areas. A charge may be made where smoking has caused damage.

#### Is the hotel pet friendly?

The Hazelton Hotel believes that pets are an extension of your family, and we are delighted to welcome cats and dogs as valued guests. An in-room pet fee of CAD $150 applies every 7 days, for up to 3 pets (maximum 50 lbs each). Service dogs are always welcome and are exempt from this fee.

Learn more about our Pet Policy

#### Is it possible to store luggage at the hotel?

The Hazelton Hotel offers a luggage room available to all guests free of charge on the day of check-in or check-out.

#### How much is Valet Parking, and what are the hours of operation?

Valet parking operates 24/7 and costs $30 for up to 2 hours; $5 per half hour up to $69. Overnight valet parking is $69 with unlimited in-and-out privileges. All vehicles must be under 6.6 feet (2 meters). All other vehicles will be required to self-park offsite.

#### Can you arrange for airport transfers?

For any bookings or reservations, please feel welcome to get in touch with our wonderful Les Clefs d’Or Concierge at concierge@thehazeltonhotel.com

#### Do you offer in-room dining? What are the hours?

Yes! ONE restaurant offers 24-hour in-room dining

#### What are the timings of the Spa and Fitness center?

The Spa by Valmont is open from Wednesday to Saturday 9am to 8pm and Sunday to Tuesday 10 am to 7pm. Guests can enjoy access to our fully equipped gym 24 hours, 7 days a week. To make a reservation or for any queries, please email spa@thehazeltonhotel.com or call (416) 963-6300 963-6300).

#### Are extra beds and baby cribs available?

Roll-away beds are subject to availability and incur an additional charge of $50.00 per night. A limit of one roll-away bed per room applies, subject to room configuration. Baby cribs can be booked free of charge, subject to availability.

#### Do you have a loyalty program?

The Hazelton Hotel is a member of The Leading Hotels of the World and offers the Leaders Club loyalty program. Leaders Club members can enjoy a number of perks like free room upgrades, continental breakfast for two, in-room Wi-Fi and exclusive offers and experiences. To learn more and join The Leaders Club, please visit: https://www.lhw.com/leaders-club-hub?hotel=6891

#### How do I find out about special room rates and packages?

Sign up for our newsletter to keep up-to-date for special offers!
