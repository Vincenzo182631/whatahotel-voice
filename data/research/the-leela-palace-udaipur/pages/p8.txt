# FAQs - The Leela Palace Udaipur

Find answers to your frequently asked questions

### 1\. How many rooms does The Leela Palace Udaipur have?

The Leela Palace Udaipur is a stunning getaway featuring the grandeur of Rajasthani heritage, comprising 88 lavish suites and grand villas. Each luxurious room in the palace hotel reflects exclusivity with regal aesthetics and contemporary comforts. Precisely, guests can select a room with a view overlooking the serene Lake Pichola. Staying amidst luxury in the plush rooms of The Leela Palace Udaipur is an experience engulfed with elegance and sophistication.

### 2\. What are the check-in and check-out time at The Leela Palace Udaipur?

The Leela Palace Udaipur welcomes guests at a mid-day check-in time of 14:00 hrs and check-out comfortably at 12:00 hrs. The palace property is open for exploring its exquisite amenities and lush surroundings after check-in time. In case of an early check-in or check-out, it is advisable to discuss it with the reception staff to confirm the possibilities.

### 3\. What are the names of the restaurants in The Leela Palace Udaipur?

Dining at a 5-star hotel is an enthralling experience, especially when you have impressive restaurants with a striking aura and a variety of delectable cuisines – Sheesh Mahal, The Dining Room, and The Library Bar are the admired restaurants at The Leela Palace Udaipur:

- **Sheesh Mahal:** Ideal for a romantic dinner under the star-studded skies, this is an Indian fine-dining restaurant serving authentic recipes from the royal kitchens.
- **The Dining Room:** An all-day fine-dining restaurant best known for its global cuisine and a collection of vintage wines. Dine peacefully, admiring the outer courtyard and Lake Pichola.
- **The Library Bar:** A superb bar for everyone in love with spirits, single malts, cognacs from around the world, vintage wines, and specially crafted artisanal cocktails. All this can be enjoyed under a classy bar decorated with fine art and dark wood panels.

### 4\. How much does it cost to have dinner at The Leela Palace Udaipur?

An average dining cost for two guests at The Leela Palace Udaipur is INR 6000 plus taxes. However, the pricing may differ depending on the offers, if any are applied during the final billing. It is advisable to communicate with the hotel for a precise dining cost.

### 5\. What are the quintessential experiences offered at The Leela Palace Udaipur?

Booking a stay at The Leela Palace Udaipur brings amazing experiences and treasured memories of opulent surroundings, admirable architecture, candlelight dinners, stunning views of Lake Pichola, a serene palace garden, and the evening cultural ritual at the courtyard.

### 6\. Is The Leela Palace Udaipur easily accessible from the airport and other transportation hubs?

Yes, The Leela Palace Udaipur is accessible from the airport and prime transportation hubs. A 45-minute drive from the Maharana Pratap Airport, Udaipur to the palace hotel, and a 20-minute drive from the Udaipur railway station. Guests will be driven to the destination in our fleet of luxury sedans, followed by a serene boat ride.

### 7\. How much does a wedding cost at The Leela Palace Udaipur?

Weddings at The Leela Palace Udaipur portray a larger-than-life experience with themed-based décor and personalization for each family. Hence, the wedding cost at the Palace Hotel varies based on the factors required for the wedding. For a precise quotation, share your details on sales.udaipur@theleela.com.

### 8\. How can I request a quote or book an event at The Leela Palace Udaipur?

Booking an event or requesting a quotation at The Leela Palace Udaipur follows a standard procedure:

-    Visit the official website showcasing The Leela Palace Udaipur

-     Scroll down to the Weddings, Celebrations, and Meetings section on the website

-    Complete the inquiry form with relevant details related to the event, capacity, and specifications, if any.

-    Submit the host contact details, including the name, email address, and phone number.

-    On submission of the inquiry form, the hotel staff will contact the host to discuss the event details further.

-    The event team at the hotel can also be directly contacted via phone or email to get a personalized quote.

### 9\. Does The Leela Palace Udaipur have a swimming pool?

Yes, The Leela Palace Udaipur has a temperature-controlled swimming pool that is an amazing luxury amenity for the guests to use as seasons change. The swimming pool area is a stunning one at the palace hotel overlooking the scenic view of Lake Pichola.

### 10\. What makes the spa experience at The Leela Palace Udaipur unique?

What really sets the spa experience at The Leela Palace Udaipur apart is the ambiance, providing a sense of calm with tranquil landscape views of Lake Pichola. Even the semi-private pool is a unique amenity to unwind after the therapy.

### 11\. What other luxury services does The Leela Palace Udaipur provide?

The Leela Palace Udaipur goes above and beyond when it comes to luxury services, such as butler service, a fleet of luxury sedans for pick and drop from the airport, laundry assistance, packing for the trip, and rejuvenating spa treatment.

Thoughtfully curated experiences such as destination dining set by Lake Pichola, and romantic dinners under the stars with personalised menus and royal-style service. Evenings come alive with signature cultural rituals, including traditional Rajasthani folk music and dance performances, ceremonial lamp lighting, and spiritual aartis at the palace temple.

### 12\. What dining options are available at The Leela Palace Udaipur?

The Leela Palace Udaipur offers a remarkable array of dining options that truly cater to a range of tastes. The exquisite restaurants at the palace hotel serve traditional Indian dishes with an impressive menu of global cuisines as well:

- **Sheesh Mahal**\- Time-honoured recipes mastered in royal kitchens create culinary masterpieces like the Dal-e-Sheesh and the Raan-e-Sheesh.
- **The Dining Room** \- Offering you the finest delicacies from around the world, our all-day fine dining restaurant in Udaipur overlooks the outer courtyard and Lake Pichola.
- **The Library Bar** \- Beloved by whisky lovers, The Library Bar boasts a vast collection of single malts and cognacs from around the world, including Aberfeldy, Glenfiddich, Craigellachie, and Remy Martin. For those fond of other spirits, the bar is also home to vintage wines and specially crafted artisanal cocktails. Pair your drinks with exquisite accompaniments prepared by our expert chefs.

### 13\. Are special experiences like boat rides or cultural evenings offered?

The Leela Palace Udaipur does offer some special experiences, like boat rides on the picturesque Lake Pichola, especially at sunset. There are beautiful cultural evenings, featuring traditional music, dance, and an evening cultural ritual in the courtyard to experience the local vibe.

### 14\. How many guests can the wedding venues at The Leela Palace Udaipur accommodate?

Guests that can fit into the wedding venues at The Leela Palace Udaipur are roughly 250 to 300 guests. However, the exact number of guests to be accommodated can vary depending on the wedding décor and setup. If you are planning an elaborate or unique event, The Leela Palace has some flexibility to cater to your needs.

### 15\. What is the best time of year to stay at The Leela Palace Udaipur?

There is truly no single “best” time to stay at The Leela Palace Udaipur, because each season brings its own distinct charm and a completely different experience. Some seasons bring clear skies, cool evenings, perfect for outdoor dining and cultural exploration, while another transforms the city into a lush, romantic landscape with misty lake views. Even the summer days offer tranquil surroundings, golden sunsets, and a sense of exclusive serenity within the palace.

### 16\. Do rooms come with private balconies or plunge pools?

Yes, The Leela Palace Udaipur has a selection of rooms that come with their very own private plunge pools, and most of our rooms feature balconies that offer stunning views of Lake Pichola.

### 17\. Do palace hotels have conference and banquet facilities?

Yes, The Leela Palace Udaipur does offer conference and banquet facilities. We own well-equipped venues that can accommodate various events, from corporate meetings to grand celebrations. It is a versatile setting for any occasion and can cater to different sizes and styles of gatherings.

### 18\. Does the hotel organize guided tours in Udaipur?

Yes, The Leela Palace Udaipur organizes guided tours with bespoke itineraries based on the guests interests and schedules. The guided tour includes excursions to stunning forts, magnificent temples, vibrant markets, and bustling villages. To book your experience, please call on +91 (294) 670 1203 \| +91 95870 30693 or email us at fnb.tlpu@theleela.com or dm.tlpu@theleela.com.

### 19\. Is in-room dining available 24x7?

Yes, an in-room dining facility is available 24x7 at the hotel. This means guests can satisfy their late-night cravings or enjoy a cozy meal in the room without time restrictions.

### 20\. Are cultural performances or folk shows organized for guests?

Yes, cultural performances and folk shows are organized for guests at The Leela Palace Udaipur. The diverse art, music, and folk performances are curated beautifully with soulful music, energetic dances, and vibrant costumes. As the dancers take centre stage, the vivid hues of the Thar Desert come alive through traditional tunes, spirited performances, and striking costumes.

The art of Pichwai painting, along with the crafts of pottery and bangle making, also form a part of the Rajasthani rendezvous.  To book your experience, please call  +91 (294) 670 1203 \| +91 95870 30693 or email us at dm.tlpu@theleela.com.
