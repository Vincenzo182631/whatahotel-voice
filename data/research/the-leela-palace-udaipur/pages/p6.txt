# Exploring Udaipur

Think azure lakes, lush greens, and rolling ranges – all part of the same backdrop. These are just some of the key attributes that the grand city of Udaipur proudly boasts. Often referred to as ‘The Venice of the East’ or ‘The city of lakes’, this city seems almost magical with a plethora of dreamy palaces, aesthetic gardens, glistening lakes, and majestic temples. As for the heroic past held within its borders, Udaipur symbolizes the chivalry, bravery, and pride of the people of Mewar.
