## The Leela Palace Udaipur

Set against the tranquil waters of Lake Pichola, The Leela Palace Udaipur embodies the romance and grandeur of Rajasthan’s royal heritage. A true luxury hotel in Udaipur, it captures the soul of regal living with every architectural detail and gesture of hospitality. Arrive by boat to an opulent façade adorned with chhatris and jharokhas, and step into a world where every detail whispers elegance. Walk through a doorway of rose petals, explore halls rich with intricate silver accents, and lose yourself in the quiet charm of the Guava Garden, where marble pathways wind through fragrant orchards.

As the sun dips below the Aravallis, dine under lantern-lit terraces, with the lake shimmering in the twilight. Recognised among the finest 5-star hotels in Udaipur, it offers experiences that feel both intimate and grand. And for those seeking an even more rarefied retreat, Arq By The Leela, Villas at Pichola offers an ultra-luxury villa experience unlike any other - where brass-inlaid wooden floors, Thikri mirrorwork, and rare lapis stones create a sanctuary of timeless beauty.

From serene mornings by the lake to nights filled with starlit splendour, The Leela Palace Udaipur is an invitation to live the legacy of royalty.
