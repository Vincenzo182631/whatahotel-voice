Aujasya by The Leela brings together Indian wisdom, preventive wellbeing, longevity science and The Leela’s philosophy of personalised hospitality into one integrated approach to living well.

It is not wellness added to hospitality. It is a way of reimagining hospitality through the lens of wellbeing.

From the first moment of arrival to the rituals carried home, every interaction is considered as an opportunity to restore, strengthen, centre and transform.

Rooted in the Sanskrit word Ojas, the essence of vital energy that sustains life force, Aujasya reflects a distinctly Indian understanding of wellbeing as a state of balance between vitality, restoration, strength and purpose.

## The Trinity of Balance

At the heart of Aujasya is the **Trinity of Balance**: **Saumya, Shakti and Shanti.**

Rooted in Indian wisdom, these three complementary qualities reflect different dimensions of wellbeing. They are not sequential stages or fixed states, but qualities that can be cultivated according to an individual’s needs, rhythm and wellbeing journey.

Together, they form the philosophical foundation of Aujasya and give the experience its distinctly Indian soul.

### Saumya - Restore & Replenish

_Saumya speaks to gentleness, harmony and the ability to restore._

It creates space to slow down, receive, replenish and return to oneself. Through considered therapies, mindful nourishment and moments of renewal, Saumya nurtures a softer and more restorative relationship with wellbeing.

### Shakti - Strength & Vitality

_Shakti represents energy, strength and the capacity to act._

It is the active expression of wellbeing, building resilience, vitality and capability through conscious movement, strength, recovery and practices that support both physical and mental performance.

### Shanti - Peace & Inner Balance

_Shanti represents peace, stillness and inner equilibrium._

It is the state in which wellbeing becomes more integrated and sustainable, encouraging greater presence, perspective and ease through breathwork, mindfulness, restorative practices and reflection.

## Four Pillars of Wellbeing

The Trinity of Balance defines **what Aujasya stands for**, while four functional pillars define **how Aujasya is experienced**.

These pillars translate the philosophy into tangible, personalised experiences that guests can engage with during their stay and carry into everyday life.

### Nutrition

Purposeful nourishment that brings together functional nutrition, Indian wisdom and contemporary thinking around long-term wellbeing.

### Strength & Recovery

Movement, resilience and restorative practices designed to support strength, mobility, recovery and sustained vitality.

### Sleep & Regeneration

Experiences designed to support rest, restoration and the body’s natural ability to recover and regenerate.

### Purpose & Presence

Mindfulness, reflection and practices that encourage greater clarity, awareness and a more conscious relationship with everyday life.

#### The Aujasya Experience

_**A holistic approach to personalised wellbeing**_

Aujasya is not a product or treatment menu. It is a personalised continuum of wellbeing designed to begin during the stay and extend beyond it.

Its evolving ecosystem brings together:

- Personalised Nutrition combining functional nutrition with traditional wisdom
- Integrative Wellness Therapies through The Spa
- Longevity Programmes focused on preventive wellbeing, performance and healthy ageing
- Wellness Retreats led by in-house and visiting specialists
- The Aujasya Apothecary with curated botanical formulations and wellness products
- Home Care Rituals that extend the experience beyond the hotel
- Continuum of Care enabling ongoing connection with Aujasya experts

Together, these create a seamless progression from restoration to transformation to sustained wellbeing.

#### Sampoorna

_**Purposeful nourishment**_

Aujasya by The Leela Sampoorna is the culinary expression of the Aujasya philosophy, bringing together purposeful nutrition and the pleasure of exceptional dining.

Rooted in Indian wellness traditions while informed by contemporary nutritional thinking, Sampoorna approaches food as an essential part of long-term wellbeing.

Thoughtfully curated menus, including High Protein, Anti-Inflammatory and Detox programmes, are designed around individual outcomes while remaining seasonal, flavour-led and deeply enjoyable.

#### Aujasya by The Leela Signature Ritual

_**A personalised journey back to balance**_

The 90-minute Aujasya by The Leela Signature Ritual is a deeply restorative expression of the Aujasya philosophy.

Beginning with a personalised consultation, the experience is tailored to the individual and draws inspiration from India’s healing traditions.

Rhythmic, synchronised movements, considered touch and warm herb-infused oils work together to release tension, support circulation and encourage profound relaxation and renewal.

The ritual brings together Saumya, Shakti and Shanti, creating a deeply personal experience designed to restore the body, settle the mind and guide guests back towards their natural rhythm.

#### Aujasya Retreats

_**Immersive journeys in wellbeing**_

Aujasya by The Leela Retreats offer immersive, personalised journeys designed to help guests reconnect with their physical, mental and emotional wellbeing.

Each retreat brings together the Trinity of Balance and the four pillars of Aujasya through a curated combination of movement, nutrition, restorative therapies, reflection and specialist-led practices.

Experiences may include yoga, pranayama, meditation, Mudgar, Traatak, sound healing, signature therapies and outcome-led nutrition.

More than a temporary escape, each retreat is designed to create a meaningful shift in how guests feel, function and live, encouraging practices that can continue beyond the stay.
