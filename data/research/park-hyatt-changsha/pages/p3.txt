## Lilan Restaurant

Lilan（L Floor） offers a range of options throughout the day, starting with a tasty breakfast of cooked-to-order dishes and live cooking stations specializing in Asian and local flavors. A la carte lunch and dinner menus present authentic Hunan cuisine, prepared with locally sourced ingredients, paired with fine wines and handcrafted beverages. Come afternoon, indulge in a charming afternoon tea with stunning views. Lilan also includes eight exclusive private dining rooms seating up to 20 guests, ideal for family dinners and special occasions, where you can delight in the culinary theater of the chef preparing signature dishes right before you.
