# Luxury Hotel at Changsha IFS \| Park Hyatt Changsha

## Experience a Sophisticated Retreat Above Bustling Changsha City

Experience a luxurious Mansion perched high above the bustling Changsha cityscape
Occupying the top floors of Changsha IFS Tower 2, within one of the city’s most iconic shopping and commercial complexes, Park Hyatt Changsha is a stylish and sophisticated residence that harmoniously combines stunning views, cultural heritage and curated aesthetics with impeccable service. It offers exceptional dining experience’s and showcasing the integration of traditional Xiang elements and exquisite artworks.

## Discover Park Hyatt Changsha

Fitness Center

Our 24-hour fitness center is equipped with a range of advanced cardio and weight-training equipment plus a steam room, sauna, yoga studio and lounge. With stunning city views and natural light, it provides a complete wellness hub for physical rejuvenation and inner balance.

Garden Pavilion

Spacious and filled with daylight, our Garden Pavilion provides the ideal setting for exceptional events, including a show kitchen, pre-function welcome area and a stunning 898-square-meter lawn and garden, perfect for outdoor wedding ceremonies or larger tailored events.

Pool

Refresh your mind and body with a leisurely swim in our 25-meter indoor pool, a haven of serenity in Changsha. Floor-to-ceiling windows fill the space with natural light during the day and offer glittering city views at night.

Open daily 7 a.m.–10 p.m.

### Lilan Restaurant

Lilan（L Floor） offers a range of options throughout the day, starting with a tasty breakfast of cooked-to-order dishes and live cooking stations specializing in Asian and local flavors. A la carte lunch and dinner menus present authentic Hunan cuisine, prepared with locally sourced ingredients, paired with fine wines and handcrafted beverages. Come afternoon, indulge in a charming afternoon tea with stunning views. Lilan also includes eight exclusive private dining rooms seating up to 20 guests, ideal for family dinners and special occasions, where you can delight in the culinary theater of the chef preparing signature dishes right before you.
