### Unforgettable 15th Anniversary Celebration at Kimp

a day ago

• Dinner at Dean's Raw Bar: We had an incredible anniversary dinner at Dean's right inside the hotel. Sitting at the Raw Bar was a stellar experience—the food was completely unbeatable and the atmosphere was fantastic. Chef Jason personally made our dining experience incredibly memorable with his exceptional culinary craft.
• Outstanding Service from Brennon: A huge shoutout to Brennon, who took care of us as our waiter for both our anniversary dinner and our morning breakfasts. He was friendly, professional, and highly accommodating, providing the kind of dedicated service that truly elevates a stay.
• Beautiful Courtyard Breakfasts: Starting our mornings in the hotel courtyard was a true highlight. It is a peaceful, beautiful outdoor space that is perfect for a relaxing breakfast.
• Massages at Plaza San Antonio Next Door: For a little extra relaxation, we walked right next door to the Plaza for massages. The treatments were phenomenal, and we highly recommend adding a spa visit there to any stay here since it is so convenient.
Overall: The Kimpton Santo delivers on every level for a romantic trip or special occasion. Between the top-tier dining at Dean's, the tranquil morning courtyard, and the fantastic team like Chef Jason and Brennon, it gave us everything we wanted for our anniversary. We will definitely be back!
