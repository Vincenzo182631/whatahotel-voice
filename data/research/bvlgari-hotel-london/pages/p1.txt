## Discover our Rooms and Suites

### Deluxe Rooms

Deluxe Rooms are an oasis of calm comfort and understated luxury incorporating exquisite fabrics, fine woods and unique detailing. Exceptionally spacious compared to other rooms in the same category, Deluxe Rooms are a minimum of 43 square metres and boast either a luxurious king or two queen sized beds as well as generous closet space and large bathrooms with a black Marquina marble bathtub and separate shower. All Deluxe Rooms feature a Nespresso coffee machine and complimentary wireless Internet.
