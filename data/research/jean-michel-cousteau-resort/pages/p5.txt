# Location

The Jean-Michel Cousteau Resort is located on the northern Fijian island of Vanua Levu, with Savusavu, the closest larger town to the resort, home to Savusavu Airport (SVU).

* * *

### Getting Here

International flights to Fiji arrive at Nadi International Airport (NAN).

From Nadi, you take a domestic flight to Savusavu (SVU).

There are three options:

- **Fiji Link (operated by Fiji Airways) – regular scheduled flights**
- **Pacific Island Air – charter flights (reservations@pacificislandair.com)**
- **Island Hoppers – charter flights (info@helicopters.com.fj)**

Once you arrive at Savusavu Airport, a Jean-Michel Cousteau Resort Representative will transfer you to the resort, just a 20-minute drive away.

Alternatively, you may fly from Nadi to Labasa (LBS). From Labasa, the resort can arrange a scenic 90-minute transfer (additional cost, payable directly to the resort).

Major airline partners connecting you to Fiji include:

Fiji Airways \| Qantas \| Virgin Australia \| Jetstar \| Air New Zealand
