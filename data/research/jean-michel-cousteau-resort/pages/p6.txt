# A Legacy of Eco-Luxury

Set amidst extraordinary natural beauty, our resort seamlessly blends sustainability with refined comfort, creating a sanctuary where luxury and environmental stewardship exist in harmony. Inspired by the wisdom of Fiji’s indigenous communities, our journey began with Jean-Michel Cousteau, a visionary who redefined what Eco-Luxury could mean.

## Resort Amenities

At Jean-Michel Cousteau Resort, every detail is thoughtfully designed to immerse you in the beauty and rhythm of Fiji. Our resort amenities will elevate your island escape, from exceptional dining and restorative spa rituals to wellness spaces that inspire movement, vitality, and mindful living.

## Location

The Jean-Michel Cousteau Resort is located on the northern Fijian island of Vanua Levu, with Savusavu, the closest larger town to the resort, home to Savusavu Airport (SVU).
