# Dining

## Rooted in the Land

Discover one of Bali's most distinctive dining destinations, where locally sourced ingredients, seasonal harvests, and Indonesian culinary traditions come together in the heart of the rainforest.

From relaxed lunches and handcrafted botanical cocktails to immersive destination dining experiences, every meal celebrates the richness of place through thoughtful craftsmanship, responsible sourcing, and a deep respect for nature.

## Open Kitchen

Dining at Buahan is rooted in the belief that every meal should tell the story of the land.

Open Kitchen is Buahan's signature restaurant, celebrating locally sourced ingredients, seasonal harvests, and the richness of Indonesian culinary traditions.

Open daily from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM

## The Botanist Bar

Using herbs, flowers, spices, and tropical ingredients sourced from our gardens and nearby producers, each creation celebrates local flavours, thoughtful craftsmanship, and the beauty of slow afternoons overlooking the rainforest.

The Botanist Bar is a destination for handcrafted cocktails, locally roasted coffee, and botanical refreshments inspired by Indonesia's rich natural larder.

Open daily from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM

## Signature Experiences

### Jungle Cooking

Discover the flavours of Bali through a hands-on cooking experience at the Dukuh Asri Regenerative Centre. Guided by our chef, prepare traditional Balinese dishes using locally sourced ingredients and time-honoured techniques, before gathering around the table to enjoy what you have created.

### Jungle Affair

Escape into the rainforest for one of Bali's most intimate romantic dining experiences. Set within a private pavilion surrounded by nature, enjoy a candlelit multi-course dinner inspired by Indonesian flavours—perfect for anniversaries, honeymoon celebrations, or a romantic proposal.

## Timbungan Lunch

Gather over an authentic Balinese lunch inspired by the island's communal dining traditions. This experience brings together locally sourced ingredients and regional specialities, creating a memorable group dining experience with panoramic views of lush highlands, just north of Ubud.

##### Open to Visiting Guests

Whether you're staying at Buahan or exploring the highlands north of Ubud, our restaurants, bar, and signature dining experiences welcome both in-house and visiting guests by reservation.

##### Dietary Requirements

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and other dietary requirements can be accommodated with advance notice. Please inform our Escape Hosts when making your reservation.

##### Special Occasion

Whether you're planning a romantic proposal, anniversary, honeymoon dinner, birthday celebration, or gathering with family and friends, our Escape Hosts can help personalise your dining experience with thoughtful touches to make the occasion truly memorable.

##### Reservations

To ensure the best possible experience, we recommend making your reservation at least 24 hours in advance. Our Escape Hosts will be delighted to assist with availability, dietary requirements, and personalised arrangements.

##### Location

Located in Buahan village, approximately 30 minutes north of central Ubud, our open-air dining venues overlook Bali's lush rainforest and river valley, offering a peaceful dining experience immersed in nature.
