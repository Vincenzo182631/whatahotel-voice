This is a place where time moves differently. A place to disconnect from the ordinary and reconnect with what matters most.

Tucked within the lush highlands north of Ubud, this adults-only retreat invites you to rediscover the beauty of doing less.

Here, the boundaries dissolve, giving way to uninterrupted views, open-air living, and a deeper connection with nature.

More than a stay, Buahan is a shift in perspective.

Guided by a philosophy of "No Walls, No Doors", the resort blends thoughtful design, local culture, and regenerative hospitality to create meaningful experiences rooted in place.

## An invitation into a new way of being

#### Frequently Asked Questions

Expand All

##### What is unique about Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape?

Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape is an adults-only luxury retreat in Bali guided by a "No Walls, No Doors" philosophy. With only 16 open-air private 'bale's, immersive nature experiences, farm-to-table dining, and meaningful connections to local culture, the resort offers a distinctive way to experience Bali.

##### Where is Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape located?

Buahan is located in the lush highlands north of Ubud in Payangan, Gianyar, surrounded by tropical forests and views of Bali's seven peaks.

##### What does "No Walls, No Doors" mean?

"No Walls, No Doors" is Banyan Tree Escape design philosophy, inviting guests to experience a deeper connection with nature through open-air living spaces, uninterrupted views, and a seamless relationship with the surrounding environment.

##### Is Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape an adults-only resort?

Yes. Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape is an adults-only retreat welcoming guests aged 18 and above. Designed to foster tranquillity, privacy, and meaningful connection with nature, the resort offers an immersive environment tailored for rest, exploration, and reconnection.

##### Who is Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape best suited for?

Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape is ideal for travellers seeking immersive nature, open-air living, cultural discovery, wellness experiences, and meaningful connection. Guests who appreciate privacy, tranquillity, and a slower pace of travel often find Buahan especially rewarding.

##### Is Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape suitable for honeymooners or couples?

Yes. With only 16 private 'bale's, adults-only accommodation, destination dining experiences, and immersive natural surroundings, Buahan is ideal for couples seeking privacy, romance, and meaningful connection.

##### What dining experiences are available at Buahan?

Guests can enjoy farm-to-table dining at Open Kitchen, botanical-inspired beverages at The Botanist Bar, destination dining experiences, and seasonal menus inspired by Bali's local harvests.

##### What wellness experiences are available?

Wellness experiences include yoga, sound healing, meditation, Soul Freedom Journey, Insight Journey, spa treatments, and nature-based activities designed to restore balance and wellbeing.

##### How far is Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape from Ubud?

Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape is located approximately 40 minutes north of central Ubud, offering a secluded retreat surrounded by nature while remaining within easy reach of Bali's cultural heart.

##### How far is Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape from Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), and does Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape provide airport transfers?

Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape is located approximately 90 kilometres from Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS). Travel time approximately 2 hours depending on traffic conditions.

Private airport transfers can be arranged by our team for a seamless journey between Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) and Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape. Guests are encouraged to contact the resort in advance to arrange transportation.

##### What is the best time of year to visit Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape?

Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape offers a unique experience throughout the year. The dry season from April to October is ideal for outdoor activities and clear mountain views, while the green season from November to March brings lush landscapes, dramatic skies, and a deeper immersion in Buahan rainforest environment.

##### What should I pack for my stay at Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape?

We recommend comfortable resort wear, activewear for outdoor experiences, walking shoes with good grip, swimwear, light layers for cooler evenings, and sun protection. As Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape is immersed in nature, guests may also wish to bring preferred insect repellent, although amenities are available at the resort.

##### Is Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape accessible for guests with limited mobility?

Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape is situated within a natural rainforest setting characterised by sloping terrain, pathways, and stairs that connect different areas of the resort. While our team will always do its best to assist, guests with mobility concerns are encouraged to contact us before booking so we can recommend the most suitable accommodation and experiences.

##### Is Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape safe and secure despite its open-air design?

Yes. While Buahan embraces a ""No Walls, No Doors"" philosophy that encourages a closer connection with nature, guest safety and comfort remain a top priority. The resort operates with 24-hour security, dedicated hosts, and carefully designed pathways and facilities. Our team is always available to assist guests, ensuring a secure and comfortable stay while preserving the immersive experience that makes Buahan unique."

##### What is Buahan known for?

Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape, is best known for its secluded rainforest setting north of Ubud, Bali, and its pioneering “No Walls, No Doors” design concept. Featuring 16 open-air private pool balés immersed in the surrounding forest, this adults-only retreat offers nature-led wellness, locally rooted dining, and immersive cultural experiences for a quiet, intimate Balinese getaway.

##### How many days should you spend in Buahan?

A three- to four-night stay is recommended to experience Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape, at an unhurried pace. This allows enough time to enjoy a private pool balé, Toja Spa, Open Kitchen, and the Botanist Bar, alongside nature walks, cultural discovery, and wellness activities. Longer stays offer greater opportunity for personalized journeys, deeper rest, and exploring the surrounding community.

##### Is Buahan a good destination for a wellness retreat?

Yes, Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape, is an ideal destination for a wellness retreat, thanks to its secluded rainforest setting and adults-only atmosphere. Its open-air Toja Spa offers treatments inspired by traditional Balinese healing, native botanicals, therapeutic touch, and sound. Guests can also participate in yoga, meditation, sound healing, and personalized wellness programs supported by nourishing, plant-forward dining.

##### What nature experiences can you enjoy in Buahan?

At Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape, guests can explore the surrounding rainforest, river valleys, rice terraces, and local villages through guided walks, cycling, waterfall journeys, and seasonal nature observation. Highlights include birdwatching, discovering native plants, learning about local agriculture, and observing the valley’s fireflies, with each journey crafted to encourage a respectful, meaningful connection with the landscape.

##### What local flavours and culinary experiences can you enjoy in Buahan?

Culinary experiences at Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape, celebrate seasonal produce, Balinese food heritage, and local growers. Open Kitchen offers plant-forward menus and regional specialties, while Botanist Bar serves botanical cocktails made with local ingredients. Guests can also experience immersive Jungle Cooking, traditional Timbungan dining, and private meals tied directly to the surrounding landscape.
