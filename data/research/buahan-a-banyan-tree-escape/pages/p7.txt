# Location

## Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape

Set within Bali’s lush northern highlands, Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape brings about a quieter perspective of the island, one enhanced by rainforest, river valleys, and traditional villages. While immersed in nature, the resort remains within reach of some of the island’s most culturally significant landmarks and scenic landscapes.

As a luxury resort near Ubud, Buahan creates a balance between seclusion and discovery, allowing guests to experience both deep tranquillity and meaningful exploration throughout their stay.

Transport

- Ngurah Rai International Airport

48 km \| 2 hours drive
Ngurah Rai International Airport is located in the south of Bali. The one and only airport in Bali

Points of interest

- Ubud Center

25 km \| 45 minutes
The cultural heart of Bali, Ubud Center offers a blend of traditional art markets, Balinese temples, and vibrant cafés. Here, you can explore local crafts, experience traditional dance performances, and immerse yourself in Bali’s spiritual and artistic heritage.

- Kintamani

45 km \| 1 hour 30 minutes
Set against the backdrop of Mount Batur and its tranquil lake, Kintamani is renowned for its breathtaking volcanic landscapes. Enjoy scenic hikes, discover hidden temples, or relax in natural hot springs while taking in panoramic views of Bali’s rugged mountains.

- Tjampuhan Waterfall

20 km \| 15 minutes
Located inside the resort, Tjampuhan Waterfall offers a refreshing retreat within lush greenery. The cascading water and serene surroundings make it ideal for a quiet escape into nature, perfect for those looking to unwind and connect with Bali’s natural beauty.

- Tegalalang Rice Terraces

30 km \| 50 minutes
The iconic Tegalalang Rice Terraces are famed for their stunning, layered green fields. This UNESCO-listed site showcases traditional Balinese irrigation techniques and offers a peaceful, scenic environment where you can admire local farming culture and breathtaking views.
