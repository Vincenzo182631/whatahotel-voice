## No Walls. No Doors. Just Nature.

Inspired by the traditional Balinese _bale_ — an open pavilion where communities gather — these 16 private accommodations reinterpret this philosophy through immersive open-air living and a seamless connection with nature.

Guided by our "No Walls, No Doors" philosophy, each _bale_ has been thoughtfully positioned to frame a distinct perspective of Buahan's surrounding landscape, from the rainforest and rivers to rice terraces, valleys, waterfalls, and the seven peaks.

Designed exclusively for adults, every _bale_ offers the rare luxury of privacy, tranquillity, and space to simply be.

## Stay Your Way

Every _bale_ offers a different way to experience Buahan. Each has been thoughtfully positioned to immerse you in a different expression of Buahan—from iconic mountain panoramas and secluded waterfalls to peaceful valleys and lush rainforest.

Discover the _bale_ that feels most like you.

## The Art of Open-Air Living

### Your Private Retreat

Each 160-square-metre _bale_ has been thoughtfully designed with a private pool, open-air living spaces, and uninterrupted views of the surrounding landscape.

### Locally Sourced Materials

Inspired by Balinese architecture and craftsmanship, each _bale_ has been constructed using locally sourced and reclaimed materials, including resilient Ulin wood given new life within the resort.

### Nature, Unfiltered

From drifting mist and starlit skies to fireflies and the sounds of the rainforest, nature becomes part of everyday life at Buahan.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

Expand All

##### What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM, while check-out is at 12:00 PM.

##### Can I request early check-in or late check-out?

Early arrivals are subject to availability. For guests wishing to guarantee immediate access to their bale upon arrival, we recommend reserving the night prior. Late check-out until 6:00 PM is available for 50% of the nightly rate, subject to availability. Departures beyond 6:00 PM will incur a full night's accommodation charge.

##### How many guests can stay in each bale?

Each _bale_ is designed for a maximum of two adults. Extra beds and additional guests cannot be accommodated.

##### What should I expect from staying in an open-air bale?

Located in Bali's lush highlands north of Ubud, Buahan is immersed in a living ecosystem of rainforest, rivers, and rice terraces. Guests can expect changing weather conditions, natural sounds, and occasional encounters with local wildlife and insects that are part of the surrounding environment. Our team takes thoughtful measures to ensure comfort while preserving the authenticity of the landscape.
