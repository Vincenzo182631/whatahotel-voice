## Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape

## Elevate Your Experience

Thoughtfully integrated into the surrounding landscape, each space at Buahan is designed to encourage relaxation, reflection, and a deeper connection with nature throughout your stay. Discover an array of exceptional facilities and services that cater to your every need.

### Toja Spa

Toja Spa, meaning "water" in Indonesian, is an open-air spa concept that enhances your connection with nature. Enjoy personalized scrub and bath experiences using the natural surroundings. Natural botanicals, flowing water, and open-air treatment spaces all harmonise to create an atmosphere that feels grounding, restorative, and deeply connected to Bali’s healing traditions.

Explore More

### Outdoor Swimming Pool

Nestled within lush, tropical surroundings. Offering panoramic views of the jungle and nearby mountains, the pool provides a serene space to relax and immerse yourself in nature, enhancing the resort’s tranquil, open-air experience. From refreshing swims in early morning beneath drifting mist to contemplative afternoons overlooking the rainforest canopy, the setting invites moments of calm at every hour of the day.

### Bamboo Pavilion

The Bamboo Pavilion at Banyan Tree Escape is a versatile space set amidst nature. It serves as a serene venue for yoga sessions and can be transformed for romantic dinners, offering a unique blend of tranquility and intimacy in a lush, tropical setting. Cocooned by jungle greenery and soft natural light, the pavilion offers a peaceful atmosphere for meaningful gatherings, mindful practices, and intimate celebrations alike.
