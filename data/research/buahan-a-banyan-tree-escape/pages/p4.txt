# Spa & Wellbeing

## Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape

## Rooted in Nature

Nature has always been our greatest healer. At Buahan, wellbeing is shaped by rainforest, flowing rivers, and Bali's enduring healing traditions. Every treatment, ritual, and mindful practice invites you to slow down, reconnect, and return to yourself.

## Toja Spa

Named after the Indonesian word for water, Toja Spa draws inspiration from one of nature's most restorative elements.

Here, the sounds of flowing rivers, the scent of tropical botanicals, and time-honoured Balinese healing traditions come together to create deeply restorative experiences.

## Healing, Naturally

## Signature Spa Treatments

### Raindrop Therapy

Inspired by the rainforest surrounding Buahan, Raindrop Therapy is a signature wellness ritual combining ten therapeutic essential oils, Vitaflex foot stimulation, energy balancing, and Himalayan sound healing to restore balance and deep relaxation.

### Sejoli

Curated for couples, Sejoli begins with a heart-centred meditation, followed by nourishing body rituals, a Balinese massage, and a fragrant herbal flower bath—creating a meaningful journey of connection and shared wellbeing.

### Buahan Bliss

Inspired by Buahan's native botanicals and Balinese healing traditions, Buahan Bliss is a signature body ritual featuring a locally crafted body scrub, stimulating massage, and detoxifying herbal wrap to renew both body and mind.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

Expand All

##### Can I visit Toja Spa without staying at Buahan, a Banyan Tree Escape?

Toja Spa is exclusively available for in-house guests, allowing every wellbeing journey to unfold in complete privacy and at a slower pace. The most meaningful way to experience Toja Spa is as part of a stay at Buahan, where open-air living, nourishing cuisine, and nature-inspired rituals come together as one holistic wellbeing journey.

##### Do I need to book my spa treatment in advance?

Yes. We recommend reserving your preferred treatment before arrival to ensure your preferred date and time are available during your stay.

##### Can the spa treatment be personalised?

Yes. Before your treatment begins, our therapists will tailor pressure, focus areas, and wellbeing preferences to create a personalised spa experience.

##### Which spa treatment is best for couples?

Sejoli is our signature couples' wellbeing journey, featuring a heart-centred meditation, Balinese massage, and a fragrant herbal flower bath—perfect for honeymoons, anniversaries, or simply reconnecting together.

##### Are wellness activities included in my stay?

Many of Buahan's daily wellbeing experiences, including yoga and meditation sessions, are complimentary for in-house guests. Selected specialised experiences or private sessions may incur an additional charge.
