# Dine at Bishop's LodgeElevated culinary arts

## Cuisine That Celebrates Storied, Southwestern Ingredients

Inhale the earthy scent of peppers charring on the wood-fired grill and delight in the bright tang of a craft mezcal cocktail at The Bar. Textured and multi-layered, the cuisine at Bishop's Lodge will be sure to send you home with memories to cherish for years to come. The dining experience at Bishop's Lodge invites you to slow down and engage all of your senses. Learn more about our restaurant experiences in Santa Fe.

## A Narrative of Nourishment

## SkyFire

Nestled at the base of the Sangre de Cristo mountains, SkyFire draws inspiration from the diverse, rich ingredients and textures found in Mexico, and infuses them with unexpected modern techniques. The transformative element of fire is present throughout the bar, main dining room and terrace where piñon or cedar wood logs are burned to evoke a scent which roots you to our grounding atmosphere, and the wood-fired grill imparts complexity to bright, vibrant dishes.

- Dine in a fireside space with resplendent views of our lush mountainscape

- Partake in a multi-sensory experience with layered flavors

- Ingredients from artisanal purveyors

## SkyFire Signature Offerings

-

### Chef’s Tasting Menu: Private Dining Experience

Enjoy a three-course family-style feast inspired by Northern New Mexico’s vibrant flavors. Featuring seasonal ingredients and chef-driven creations, the menu evolves with the land and pairs beautifully with curated wines.

  - **Inquire**

-

### Bishop's Hour at SkyFire

Light up your senses where scintillating tastes and sounds meet. Join us on weekdays from 2-5PM for Bishop's Hour – our take on the traditional happy hour. Embrace the rugged mountainside views beside a traditional adobe fireplace in this one-of-a-kind dining experience.

  - 2PM - 5PM Monday-Friday
  - Enjoy drink specials and exclusive pricing on select menu items. Additional discounts do not apply.

-

### Canine Cuisine at SkyFire

Dishes created in collaboration with Deborah Davis, author of The Art of Canine Cuisine. Each recipe is thoughtfully crafted for dogs and veterinarian-approved.

  - 11AM - 10PM daily through In-Room Dining
  - The Art of Canine Cuisine cookbook can be purchased in Two Dogs Cafe

## Additional Dining Venues

-

### The Bar

The atelier of SkyFire, this cozy fire-lit space and adjacent patio features innovative craft cocktails and elevated Southwestern bar fare. With a welcoming vibe and expansive views, you will want to settle in and stay a while.

  - Bar & Lounge food \| 11AM – 10PM daily
  - Bar & Lounge drink \| 11AM – 11PM daily
  - Bishop’s Hour \| 2-5PM Monday-Friday

-

### Two Dogs Cafe

This bright, cozy cafe offers lush mountain views, a calming atmosphere and features inspiring local artwork. Savor warm pastries, hearty sandwiches, craft coffees, fresh salads and cold-pressed juices to nourish you for an exhilarating day of adventure and discovery.

  - 6:30AM – 2PM daily

-

### Poolside Dining

Cool off with a classic margarita or a crisp local brew, paired with fresh ceviches, bison burgers and Southwest-inspired tacos from The Prickly Pear, our vibrant new poolside food truck. Simply place your order with your server, then sit back and soak in the high desert sunshine.

  - Our poolside bar is now open for the season. Daily from 11AM - 5PM, indulge in expertly prepared food and handcrafted beverages served by our dedicated poolside team
  - Outside of these hours, food and drinks are available through our In-Room Dining service – ordering instructions are provided at the pool

## Savor the Spirit and Flavors of the Southwest

Join us for an array of thoughtfully crafted food and beverage experiences inspired by the rich heritage and bold flavors of Santa Fe and the surrounding Southwest.

-

### Wines of New Mexico

Discover New Mexico’s rich winemaking heritage through a guided tasting that explores its unique high-desert terroir, distinctive varietals and nearly 400 years of tradition.

-

### Chocolate Immersion

Savor an exquisite chocolate journey at Bishop’s Lodge in collaboration with local Kakawa Chocolate House and its renowned purveyor, Bonnie Bennett.

-

### Botanical Gin Making

Discover the essence of gin through a hands-on exploration of its botanicals, engaging your senses and crafting a personalized bottle to take home.
