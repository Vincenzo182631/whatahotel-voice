# Luxury Santa Fe Accommodations

## Accommodations that echo the essence of legendary Santa Fe

The luxury room accommodations at Bishop’s Lodge were crafted with reverence for the rich textures and subtle colors of New Mexico’s Sangre de Cristo Mountains, and the talented local artisans in Santa Fe. Choose from four styles of accommodations: Guestrooms, Suites, Casitas, or the 12-room Bunkhouse. Each invites you to reconnect with your loved ones and a slow, intentional way of life lived in harmony with the land.

## Guestrooms

Cozy sanctuaries that are accented by private terraces, traditional kiva fireplaces, and woven Navajo rugs. Each accommodation celebrates local artisans by showcasing traditional Santa Fe artwork, artifacts and textiles, and are located within minutes by foot from the main amenities of the resort.

##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is Bishop’s Lodge located?** Bishop’s Lodge is located at 1297 Bishop’s Lodge Road in Santa Fe, New Mexico, nestled in the foothills of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains and bordering 1.7 million acres of national forest.

**How far is Bishop’s Lodge from the airport?** Bishop’s Lodge is approximately 20 minutes from Santa Fe Regional Airport (SAF) and 75 minutes from Albuquerque International Sunport (ABQ). Airport pickup and private car service can be arranged in advance through the Lodge Host for an additional fee.

**Does Bishop’s Lodge have a pool?** Yes. Bishop’s Lodge has an outdoor pool and hot tub available to hotel guests, surrounded by the high-desert landscape of the Sangre de Cristo foothills.

**What experiences and activities are available at Bishop’s Lodge?** Bishop’s Lodge offers a wide range of experiences rooted in adventure, creativity and the culture of Northern New Mexico. Guests can explore horseback riding, hiking, fly-fishing and rafting alongside art classes, culinary workshops and regional cultural experiences, with seasonal adventures including skiing and snowshoeing in winter.

**Is Bishop’s Lodge a good fit for couples?** Yes. Bishop’s Lodge is an ideal setting for a romantic Santa Fe escape, with mountain scenery, destination dining and restorative wellness experiences at Stream Dance Spa. Couples can also choose intimate accommodations featuring traditional kiva fireplaces and private terraces or secluded, adults-only Kiva Suites.

**Is Bishop’s Lodge family-friendly?** Yes. Bishop’s Lodge welcomes guests of all ages, with family-friendly accommodations, children’s menus and cribs and highchairs available upon request. Hourly babysitting can also be arranged through the Lodge Host, while custom itineraries make it easy to plan experiences for the whole family.

**Does Bishop’s Lodge offer transportation to downtown Santa Fe?** Yes. Complimentary shuttle service to the Santa Fe downtown plaza is available on a first come, first served basis. Guests may also borrow a complimentary Mercedes-Benz test vehicle for up to four hours to explore Santa Fe and the surrounding area, subject to availability.

**Does Bishop’s Lodge have a spa?** Yes. Stream Dance, Wellbeing by Auberge offers a distinctive approach to wellness inspired by Santa Fe’s high-desert setting. Guests can enjoy restorative massages, facials and body treatments alongside movement, mindfulness and locally inspired healing experiences.
