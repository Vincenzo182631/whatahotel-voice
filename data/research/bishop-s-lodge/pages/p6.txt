## Movement at Bishop's Lodge

-

### Weekly Fitness Calendar

Greet the day with an invigorating vinyasa flow before exploring Santa Fe National Forest, connect with your loved ones with a Lodge Trail Hike or test your skills with. High Noon Archery. Take a mindful moment at the beginning of each practice to take deep breaths of fresh mountain air and set intentions.

-

### Private Yoga Classes

Yoga is the physical, mental and spiritual practice of asanas, pranayama and meditation. This enables the flow of vital energy, to rejuvenate the body, strengthen awareness and find clarity. Whether you are new to yoga or a seasoned practitioner, our yoga teacher will tailor this 60-minute experience to your needs. Choose between Restorative, Hatha or Ashtanga.

## Wellbeing Experiences

-

### Watercolors of New Mexico

Unleash your creativity with a local artist who will guide you in capturing the radiant desert skies, rolling vistas and delicate flora of New Mexico. Whether you're refining your technique or picking up a brush for the first time, this immersive session will leave you with a beautiful masterpiece, a deeper connection to the landscape and the inspiration to continue your artistic journey.

-

### Canyon Road Walking Tour

Discover the artistic heart of Santa Fe on an immersive two-hour walking tour of Canyon Road, the city’s premier art destination. Led by local experts, this experience takes you through a winding half-mile of world-renowned galleries, where you'll explore diverse artistic styles, from traditional Southwestern paintings to contemporary sculptures.

-

### Santa Fe National Forest Trail Rides

For adventurous riders seeking a more challenging experience than our typical trail rides, this two-hour horseback journey leads you through the rugged beauty of Bishop’s Lodge and into the landscapes of the Santa Fe National Forest. Guided by an expert wrangler, traverse varied terrain – from forested trails and river crossings to sprawling vistas.
