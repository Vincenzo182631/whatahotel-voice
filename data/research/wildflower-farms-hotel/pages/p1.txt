# A Two Michelin Key Hotel

Blanketed with meadows of native flowers and wild woodlands, Wildflower Farms sways in rhythm with the seasons to embody Hudson Valley ease. Set on 140 arcadian acres in Gardiner, New York, just 90 minutes from New York City, a slow-rippling river gently embraces free-standing cabins framing clear views of the mighty Shawangunk Ridge. Warm air, bright skies and the melodies of Mother Nature beckon stillness and contemplation, while forested footpaths and towering cliffs call to an adventurer's spirit.
