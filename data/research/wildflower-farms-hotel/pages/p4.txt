Wellbeing at Wildflower Farms draws from the power of nature and its seasonal cycles to create experiences that nurture through simplicity. Thistle, An Auberge Spa, combines age-old remedies and therapeutic bodywork with the essence of slow wellness and the healing powers of nature. Curated wellness journeys are designed to calm the mind and enliven the senses, inspiring a deeper connection with nature. ## Thistle, Wellbeing by Auberge

## Treatments

A nurturing space inspired by aromatic flowers and the changing of the seasons, Thistle offers a revolving treatment menu honoring the seasons and offering the harvest’s best. Local hand-blended products invite the incredible healing power of the natural world. Combining three distinct elements, therapies at Thistle integrate to offer true restoration and rejuvenation. An apothecary utilizes hand-harvested, small-batch oils, ointments and scrubs alongside floral remedies. Immersive outdoor treatments serve to re-wild the human spirit, restoring us to our natural selves.
