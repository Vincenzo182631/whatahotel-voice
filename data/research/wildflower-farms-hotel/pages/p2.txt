##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is Wildflower Farms located?** Wildflower Farms is located at 2702 Main Street in Gardiner, New York 12525, at the base of the Shawangunk Ridge in the Hudson Valley.

**How do I get to Wildflower Farms from the nearest airport?** Wildflower Farms is 18 miles from Stewart International Airport (SWF), 85 miles from LaGuardia Airport (LGA), 91 miles from JFK International Airport (JFK), and 95 miles from Newark International Airport (EWR).

**Is Wildflower Farms a good fit for couples?** Yes. Wildflower Farms is well suited to couples, with private freestanding cabins and suites, fireplaces, cedar hot tubs, spa treatments and Hudson Valley dining.

**When is the best time to visit Wildflower Farms?** Wildflower Farms welcomes guests year-round, with seasonal farm, wellness and outdoor experiences shaping each Hudson Valley season.

**Does Wildflower Farms have a pool?** Wildflower Farms has an outdoor pool and Thistle Spa’s indoor saltwater pool, plus two outdoor meadow hot tubs.

**Is Wildflower Farms pet-friendly?** Yes. We can accommodate up to two dogs with a one-time pet fee. Leashed dogs are welcome in dogs in outdoor public areas, including The Great Porch. Service animals are welcome without a fee. Guests should contact us directly to confirm other pet policies.

**Does Wildflower Farms have a spa?** Yes. Thistle, an Auberge Spa, offers nature-inspired treatments, an indoor saltwater pool, hot tubs, dry sauna, wellness programming and six treatment rooms.

**What experiences and activities are available at Wildflower Farms?** Guests can enjoy farm walks, forest foraging, cooking and mixology workshops, trail running, cycling, fly fishing, tennis, pickleball and seasonal arts experiences.
