## Ocean & Lagoons

Set along the shores of Nusa Dua, The Laguna Bali is shaped by seven lagoons that flow through lush tropical gardens, creating a tranquil landscape that extends naturally to the beachfront.

## Balinese Cultural

At The Laguna Bali, a stay is more than a retreat. It is an invitation to discover the island’s soul. Here, each moment becomes a gateway to Bali’s cultural richness, natural beauty, and timeless traditions.

## Epicurean Moments

At The Laguna Bali, the essence of the island is woven into the rhythm of each day. Traditional Jamu offers a gentle introduction to Bali’s herbal heritage, while weekly performances bring storytelling to life. Through a Balinese blessing ceremony, guests can share in a tradition that has long held a meaningful place in the island’s cultural landscape.

## Sustainability

At The Laguna Bali, we believe true luxury lies in harmony—between nature, humanity, and tradition. Inspired by the Balinese philosophy of Tri Hita Karana, we honor the divine (Parhyangan), nurture relationships (Pawongan), and respect nature (Palemahan). Our commitment extends globally, aligning with the UN’s Sustainable Development Goals to reduce inequality, combat climate change, and protect our planet. By preserving Bali’s beauty, we create exceptional experiences that celebrate local traditions and global sustainability. Welcome to a journey of balance, beauty, and purpose.

Our relaxed restaurants and bars offer both indoor and outdoor seating, surrounded by lush greenery, with stunning views of the vast Indian Ocean for idyllic Bali dining. ### Epicurean Expedition

Take part in an evocative epicurean journey and discover the authentic taste of Bali during your stay at our Bali beach resort. Join our expert chef on a visit to Kedonganan Fish Market, the island’s largest source of freshly caught seafood with a scenic view of fishermen’s boats.

## Journey of Balinese Taste

As an island nation, the local fish markets are a fascinating place to visit in Indonesia. Accompanied by our chef, explore Kedonganan fish market and enjoy an opportunity to engage with the local Balinese. This expedition culminates in a sumptuous Bali dining feast of oysters, lobsters and premium fish, specially prepared by our award-winning chef, adding exotic flavor to your Bali vacation.
