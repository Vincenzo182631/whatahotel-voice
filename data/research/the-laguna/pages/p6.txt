# Indigenous Bali

A destination escapade with The Laguna Bali

Opens a new windowOpens a new window

## HOTELS THAT DEFINE THE DESTINATION ™

At The Laguna Bali, a stay is more than a retreat. It is an invitation to discover the island’s soul. Here, each moment becomes a gateway to Bali’s cultural richness, natural beauty, and timeless traditions. Through our Indigenous Bali experiences, we curate immersive journeys that celebrate the island’s heritage, awaken the senses, and create stories to be cherished long after you depart.

## Cultural Landmarks

Discover sacred temples, ancient shrines, and time-honoured rituals that reveal Bali’s spiritual heartbeat. Each visit offers a deeper connection to the island’s traditions, artistry, and enduring heritage.

## Natural Wonders

From lush jungles to pristine coastlines, immerse yourself in landscapes shaped by centuries of harmony between nature and culture. Every vista is an invitation to pause, breathe, and marvel.

## Heritage Crafts & Countryside Beauty

Experience the timeless beauty of Bali’s artisan villages and terraced fields, where skilled hands and centuries-old techniques create treasures for both the eye and the soul.

## Wildlife & Botanical Sanctuaries

Step into serene havens where native flora and fauna flourish. Explore tranquil gardens, forest pathways, and conservation areas that celebrate Bali’s rich biodiversity.

### Discover Rare Moments at The Laguna Bali

Each quarter, The Laguna Bali unveils a curated series of exclusive experiences and bespoke offerings that celebrate the spirit of Indigenous Bali. From wellness rituals rooted in ancient traditions to inspired culinary creations and cultural encounters, every highlight is designed to immerse you in the island’s authentic essence.

Explore the full collection of seasonal experiences through the link below.

## Regency-Inspired Jamu Ritual

Join us at 6:00 PM daily in de Balé Lounge & Bar for our signature Jamu Ritual. Crafted by our expert mixologists, each serving revives this treasured wellness tradition, offering a refreshing taste of Bali’s cultural heritage.

## Signature Cocktails & Mocktails

Savour handcrafted creations at de Balé Lounge & Bar, where each cocktail and mocktail is inspired by the island’s traditions and natural bounty. Every sip captures the spirit of Bali in a refined and refreshing way.

## Resort Activities

Curated each quarter, our special activities invite guests of all ages to discover immersive cultural experiences. Thoughtfully designed to engage adults and inspire younger guests, each moment offers a deeper connection to the spirit of Bali.

## Journey to Renewal

Embark on a wellness journey that restores balance and harmony. Designed to nurture both body and mind, each experience leaves you feeling refreshed, revitalised, and deeply at peace.
