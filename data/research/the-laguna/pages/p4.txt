# Unwind at our luxurious Bali spa hotel

## Experiences

The Laguna, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Nusa Dua, Bali places the very best of the area well within reach. Activities near our Bali spa hotel range from majestic temples and museum-housed Indonesian...See More arts and artifacts to shopping at the Bali Collection and unique natural landmarks around the scenic peninsula. Dreamy beachfront settings make Bali an idyllic place for people who love to feel a tropical breeze. Those looking for adventure can partake in a variety of water sports, including swimming and snorkeling. Steps from our hotel and fringed by lush foliage and palm trees, a secluded beach invites guests to bask in the world-famous beauty and sunshine of Nusa Dua. Bali International Convention Center is also within walking distance of the resort. When the temperature rises, retreat to our hotel cabanas, known as bale bengong, to unwind in the cool shade with a beachside Bali massage or spa treatment. Nearby, Kulkul Beach House offers light snacks and refreshing beverages.See Less

Local Area & Activity Highlights

### Snorkeling

The best places for snorkeling in Bali are readily accessible; you only need to hit the seaside, don a mask, a snorkel and fins, and head into the water to enjoy beautiful underwater gardens teeming with tropical marine life.

### Waterparks

Home to some of the most notable waterparks, Bali boasts Waterbom Bali in central Kuta as its first and largest. This premier waterpark offers a variety of thrilling slides, relaxing pools, and family-friendly attractions, making it a top destination for both locals and tourists.

### Balinese Dance Shows

Like any form of storytelling, the best theatre in Bali can transport you to other worlds, take you on fantastic journeys, or simply showcase the many different facets of exotic culture and traditions through dance and drama.

### Surfing

There are many great surfing spots around the resort with waves for both beginners and advanced surfers.

### Ancient Temples

Ancient Bali temples dot the highlands and coasts. Several of them have become the island’s most iconic landmarks. They feature magnificent centuries-old architecture.

Yes. The Laguna, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Nusa Dua, Bali offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Dual adjustable pulley, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
