# Welcome to the Tides Inn

Deepen your connection to nature at the Tides Inn, which sits on a beautiful stretch of water that winds gracefully toward the Chesapeake Bay. Home to Blue Herons and Ospreys, Blue Crabs and oysters, the Tides Inn is a place where memories have been created for generations.

ROOMS AND SUITES

### Water View Rooms

Water View guest rooms are aptly named as all feature views of our beautiful shoreline, beach or marina. These rooms feature either a king bed or two queen beds. Some offer private balconies and cozy seating areas in addition to a full range of amenities.

Tides of History Speaker Series

Held in an intimate setting overlooking the water, each session offers meaningful conversation, fresh perspectives and the opportunity to explore topics ranging from local history and environmental stewardship to art, culture and culinary traditions.

Perfect for guests who seek enriching experiences alongside relaxation, the speaker series invites you to learn, reflect and connect in a setting as memorable as the ideas shared.

Explore Our Speaker Series

By Land

Historical Garden Tour

Step back in time and explore the agricultural traditions that shaped the Chesapeake Bay region. Led by Farmer Patrick Stickney, this guided experience takes you through the Tides Inn’s Demonstration Plot, where heritage crops illustrate how farming has evolved over the past 250 years.

CHESAPEAKE CONSERVATION

## The Tides Inn Waterfront Restoration Project

The Tides Inn completed a shoreline restoration project in 2022. The $3.6 million projectincludes a Shoreline Trail along Carter’s Creek, an expanded beach, a new oyster reef and hands-on educational programming.

Salt & Meadow

Seasonal Coastal Cuisine

Salt & Meadow is a dining experience that unites and balances the bounty of the Chesapeake Bay estuary with the farms of Virginia’s Northern Neck. The waterways here enjoy an abundance of seafood, and a vast array of herbs, vegetables and fruit thrive year-round in the region’s rich soil.

Seasonal Coastal Cuisine

## Dive Deeper: Only At The Tides

Rooted in the rhythms of the Chesapeake and shaped by generations of tradition, experiences at the Tides Inn invite you to take your time — on the water, at the table and in good company. Whether you’re hauling oyster cages with our resident ecologist or simply following the tide wherever it leads, what you find here has a way of staying with you long after you leave.
