# Accommodations

-

Our guest rooms and suites reflect the quiet elegance of the Chesapeake region — timeless in style, generous in comfort, and thoughtfully refreshed for today. From private terraces overlooking the river to inviting spaces designed for families to gather, each accommodation balances classic Tidewater character with modern ease.

Mornings unfold with soft shoreline light. Evenings wind down to the gentle rhythm of the marina. Whether you are returning for a long-held family tradition or discovering the shore for the first time, your room is more than a place to stay — it is your own waterfront retreat, designed for rest, reconnection, and the simple luxury of unhurried time.

## Adjoining Rooms & Suites

Adjoining rooms offer the ideal balance of togetherness and privacy -perfect for multi-generational families or small groups traveling together. These thoughtfully connected two- and three-bedroom rooms and suites feature a variety of bed and balcony configurations, with generous layouts spanning 600 to 2,000 square feet- designed for a more personalized experience. To learn more and secure your stay, please contact our reservation specialists by calling (888) 465-7706.
