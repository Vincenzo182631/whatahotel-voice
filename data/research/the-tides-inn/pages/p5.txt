# Experiences

-

Rooted in the rhythms of the Chesapeake and shaped by generations of tradition, experiences at the Tides Inn invite you to slow down, explore and connect more deeply—to nature, to craft and to one another. From hands-on discoveries to time-honored coastal pursuits, each moment is thoughtfully designed to awaken curiosity and create lasting memories along the water’s edge.

# Signature Experiences

Our Signature Experiences invite you to explore Virginia’s Chesapeake region—from Carter’s Creek and the Rappahannock River to the nearby Bay itself. From on-the-water adventures to farm-driven culinary workshops, each is thoughtfully rooted in place and uniquely ours.

### Watermen's Experience

This work-hard, learn-fast immersive experience gives you a glimpse into a working waterman’s life and the oystering culture of the Chesapeake Bay. As you haul oyster cages up from the depths below, you'll explore the rhythms of modern oyster aquaculture with our resident ecologist. Consider this your vacation-based, waterfront science lab.

### Chesapeake Gold—An Oyster Experience

Eastern oysters are a keystone species of the Chesapeake Bay that help to purify the water. During this small-group outing, visit an oyster farm, pull up oyster cages and help sort the catch. Back on shore, practice grading oysters for size and quality and learn how to grill them—before digging in yourself.

Explore

### Beautiful Swimmers - A Blue Crab Experience

Alongside our resident ecologist, Will, you’ll board a boat and head into Carter’s Creek. Out on the water, you’ll learn about the history of crabbing and the crab itself. You’ll get hands-on as you learn how to set and pull crab pots and evaluate which crabs can be captured and which must be thrown back.

Explore

### Mighty Rappahannock

A treasured local captain and raconteur, Captain Bull will be your guide and historian for the afternoon. Cruise aboard the Lil’ Tip-Sea and along the way he will share the history of the Rappahannock River over the last 400 years, including early native inhabitants, European exploration and Captain John Smith.

# Experiences

### Tides 250

As part of America’s 250th anniversary, the Tides Inn has launched Tides 250, a series of programs exploring the story of the Chesapeake Bay region from 1776 to today.

Explore

### Life On the Water

Embrace the spirit of the Chesapeake Bay with hands-on water adventures - paddle beyond the shoreline by kayak, cruise along Carter's Creek, and follow the tide wherever it leads. Then return to the dock as the sky ignites at sunset, a front-row seat to nature’s grand finale at the Tides Inn.

Explore

### Seaplane Tours

Take to the skies with Coastal Seaplane Experiences, from scenic tours of the Chesapeake Bay region to customized charter flights to unique destinations. Climb aboard at the Tides Inn Beach and enjoy a smooth take-off and landing in the calm waters of Carter’s Creek.
