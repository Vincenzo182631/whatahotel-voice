# Dining

You don’t need to leave the hotel to find some of the best restaurants in Bogotá. While in Colombia, savor the taste of Japanese cuisine at Ushin Japanese & Grill, where umami flavors pair with stellar city views for an elegant culinary experience no guest should miss. Our chefs at Capitalino Restaurant craft casual eats for breakfast, lunch and dinner, blending international tastes with Colombian traditions. Ilustre Bar is where to unwind in the evening, share small plates and sip creative cocktails with friends or colleagues. If you’re craving an expertly balanced cup of Colombian coffee, head to Aldeana Market and grab some baked goods on your way out the door.

## Aldeana Market

Grab On-The-Go Snacks and Drinks
Looking for something to eat on the go? Stop by Aldeana Market for baked goods, snacks and coffee you can grab on your way out the door to explore the vibrant city of Bogota. If you haven’t tried local Colombian coffee before, take a minute to savor the fresh Arabica beans and complex coffee flavors from Aldeana. Whether you’re looking for last-minute snacks or sweets to enjoy at your leisure, Grand Hyatt Bogota has them all.

## Ilustre Bar

Sip Craft Cocktails in a Welcoming Atmosphere
At the end of the day, enjoy drinks with friends or colleagues at Ilustre Bar, located in the lobby. Craft cocktails are the main attraction, yet you’ll also find cold draft beers, wine by the glass and snacks on the menu. Ilustre Bar stays open until midnight, providing a welcoming social space to swing by when you return to the hotel after visiting one of the local Bogota restaurants.

## Ushin Japanese & Grill

Experience the best views of the city and the mountains of Colombia while dining at Ushin Japanese & Grill. Savor flavorful Japanese dishes and grilled meats cooked over Binchotan Charcoal. Sip on perfectly paired wine, savor craft cocktails and enjoy a lively atmosphere at our Bogota restaurant located on the 18th floor of the hotel. e-mail: ushin@hyatt.com

## Capitalino Restaurant

Craving something more casual than dinner and wine at Ushin Japanese & Grill? Visit Capitalino Restaurant at Grand Hyatt Bogota, where international flavors mingle with the familiar. Feast on plates of zesty meats and well-seasoned veggies. If you haven’t tasted many Colombian specialties yet, the dishes on our menu will give you a delicious crash course in local culinary tradition. Stop by for breakfast, lunch or dinner. Dine on the outdoor terrace and enjoy refreshing mountain breezes.
