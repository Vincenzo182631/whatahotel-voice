## Explore Colombia’s Capital City From Our Luxury Hotel in Bogotá

Stay at Grand Hyatt Bogotá and experience unmatched luxury, comfort, and panoramic views of the city, the Andes Mountains, and lush gardens. Our spacious 41 m² guestrooms—the largest standard rooms in Bogotá—feature floor-to-ceiling windows and an ideal location near El Dorado Airport for a superior stay.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Wake up in Bogotá in an open, airy room perched above one of the town’s business hot spots. The floor-to-ceiling windows in our luxury hotel rooms and suites overlook lush gardens, the multicolored lights of the metropolis or Colombia’s cloud-topped Andes Mountains.

## EXPLORE

Fitness Center

Stick to your fitness goals while visiting Bogotá. Our fitness center is open 24 hours a day and offers free weights, strength-training gear and cardio equipment. Take in the view of the city and the Andes Mountains while using our state-of-the-art equipment. Head to the hotel’s indoor pool to cool off or relax in the hot tub after a good workout.

Grand Club

When you visit our hotel in Bogotá, elevate your stay by reserving a Club Room or a luxury suite and receive exclusive access to our comfortable Grand Club lounge. The 13th-floor lounge boasts striking views of the city and the Andes Mountains. Guests enjoy continental breakfast, refreshments throughout the day, and cocktails and hors d’oeuvres served at sunset.

Spa

Zaitania, which in Muisca means “the beginning of the world,” is a sanctuary within our luxury hotel, in the middle of the capital city of Bogotá. Our spa was created to help guests rejuvenate their bodies, energize their souls and reconnect with the best version of themselves. Recognizing water as a therapeutic agent, Zaitania is the largest urban spa in Colombia. It features a semi-Olympic indoor pool and 11 treatment rooms, including two Vichy showers and three rooms dedicated exclusively to couples.

Indoor Pool

After a day spent walking through the cobblestone streets of La Candelaria and exploring the vibrant city of Bogotá, Colombia, our hotel’s semi-Olympic indoor pool is the perfect place to kick back and relax. Swim laps for some low-impact exercise, head to the hot

tub for the luxury of a long soak or lounge poolside with a good book. Open 7 a.m.–8 p.m., daily.

### Discover Bogotá from Our Exclusive Helipad

Step into one of the most remarkable experiences at Grand Hyatt Bogotá. Our helipad combines exclusivity, height, and breathtaking scenery to create unforgettable moments above the city. Overlooking Bogotá’s skyline and the majestic Andes Mountains, this extraordinary venue becomes the stage for unique events, panoramic sunsets, and elevated experiences designed to surprise and inspire.

### Experience Colombia´s capital city

Visit La Candelaria, the capital’s cultural and historical center, when you stay at Grand Hyatt Bogotá. Visit Bolívar Square, a plaza that faces the Palace of Justice, and walk down cobblestone paths to the pre-Colombian gold collection at the Gold Museum.

### Pets Are Welcome

Will your dog be accompanying you on your tour of La Candelaria? Our luxury hotel in Bogota welcomes well-trained pets to snuggle up in the sunshine through the large windows of your room with its magnificent views of the city and mountains. You are welcome to walk your dog on a leash in the lobby, corridors and designated gazebos. However, we ask that you keep your dog out of the Ushin Japanese & Grill and the Spa.

#### Pet Fees

- Dogs size XXS to XL Up to six nights (Cleaning fee): **$100** _/ STAY_

- Dogs size XXS to XL 7 to 30 nights (Cleaning fee): **$200** _/ STAY_

- Dogs up to size 3XL Up to six nights (Cleaning fee): **$150** _/ STAY_

- Dogs up to size 3XL 7 to 30 nights (Cleaning fee): **$300** _/ STAY_

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  22kilograms

- Combined pets weight limit: 30kilograms

- Maximum number of pets is 2.
