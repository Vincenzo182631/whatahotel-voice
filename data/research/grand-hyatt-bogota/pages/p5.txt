### Beaware of questionable “tax” practices

Here’s a stronger version that keeps the tone firm while avoiding potentially risky accusations stated as facts:

I recently stayed at the Grand Hyatt Bogotá and unfortunately had a very disappointing experience regarding billing transparency and customer handling.

The hotel charged my credit card after checkout for taxes that, according to my reservation confirmation, were already included and prepaid. What made the situation worse was that the charge was processed without my authorization beforehand, and I had to proactively contact the hotel to ask for a receipt and clarification.

During our conversation, the hotel acknowledged they failed to properly explain the issue at check-in, yet continued insisting on the additional charge despite the written confirmation clearly stating that room charges and taxes were included in the original payment. Instead of resolving the matter professionally before charging my card, the burden was placed entirely on me to dispute and investigate the situation afterward.

I also checked directly with airport customs officials regarding hotel IVA practices in Colombia and was informed that tourists, diplomats, and generally non-residents paying with foreign credit cards are typically exempt from these taxes. In this case, the taxes appeared to have already been prepaid through the reservation, yet the hotel attempted to charge them again separately afterward.

Additionally, the receipt sent by email did not even clearly identify the charge as IVA, but under a different description, which made the entire process feel extremely unclear and questionable. The lack of transparency surrounding the charge raises serious concerns about the hotel’s billing practices and where exactly these additional charges are being allocated.

Because of this situation, the matter was appropriately reported, and my financial institution ultimately blocked the charge.

I understand hotels must comply with local regulations, but guests should never be surprised with post-stay charges that contradict their booking documentation. Transparency and proper communication are essential, especially from a luxury hotel brand.

The property itself is nice, but this experience seriously impacted my trust and overall impression. I hope management improves their billing practices and communication with international guests in the future.
