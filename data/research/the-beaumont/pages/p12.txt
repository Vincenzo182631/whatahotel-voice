# Premier Junior Suites

The Premier Junior Suite ranges in size from 44 to 53 sq. m (475 to 570 sq. ft), each boasting its own unique charm. Some feature elegant separate dressing areas, while all offer captivating views of Brown Hart Gardens or the iconic Mayfair rooftops. With their generous hallways and sophisticated design, these suites evoke the ambiance of a private period apartment in the heart of London, blending timeless elegance with modern luxury.
