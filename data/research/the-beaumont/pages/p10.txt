# Mayfair Suites

# Mayfair Suites

Measuring an expansive 81 to 87 sq. m (870 to 940 sq. ft), these are the largest of our one-bedroom suites. Each has its own unique layout, most overlook Brown Hart Gardens or the rooftops of Mayfair. Silks, mohair velvets, bronze detailing and subtle geometric patterns create a calm, relaxing environment for business or pleasure. Each features an extensive library of specially selected reading material.

Two of the Mayfair Suites can be connected to a Superior Grand Room to include two bedrooms.
