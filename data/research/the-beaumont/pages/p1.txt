## The Beaumont Mayfair Named One of The World's Greatest Hotels

We are delighted to share that we have been awarded the Condé Nast Traveler Triple Crown Award, a brand-new celebration of the world’s greatest hotels, highlighting The Beaumont Mayfair’s inclusion in the Hot List, Gold List, and as a Readers’ Choice Award winner.

Our commitment to delivering exceptional service is at the heart of everything we do and we are honoured to have been recognised, however this remarkable achievement would not be possible without the continued support of our clients, and we extend our sincere thanks to all who have made The Beaumont Mayfair their London home.
