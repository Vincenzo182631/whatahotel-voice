# Premier Junior Suite with Terrace

# Premier Junior Suite with Terrace

The Premier Junior Suite with Terrace ranges in size from 44 to 55 sq. m (475 to 570 sq. ft) and features unique elements such as a separate dressing area and a private outdoor terrace. Each suite offers a distinct view, overlooking either Brown Hart Gardens or Providence Court. Quiet and private, the Premier Junior Suite with Terraces are decorated in a serene palette of soft creams, ochre and browns, in keeping with the Art Deco style of the hotel. With masses of wardrobe space and a spacious sitting area with a large sofa and desk, it makes the perfect place for a longer stay.
