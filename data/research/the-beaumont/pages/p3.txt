# Deluxe Junior Suite

# Deluxe Junior Suite

Exuding elegance and generous proportions, the Deluxe Junior Suite at The Beaumont Mayfair offers a refined retreat, ranging from 38 to 43 m² (410 to 440 sq. ft). Thoughtfully designed for both comfort and sophistication, each suite features a stylish seating area, a dedicated dressing space, a lavish marble bathroom, and a private entrance hallway, ensuring a seamless blend of luxury and privacy.

For families or larger groups, The Junior Suite Wing provides an exclusive residential-style experience. This private enclave includes a Deluxe Junior Suite, a Premier Junior Suite, and a Superior Grand Room, all set within a discreet, separate corridor-offering unparalleled comfort and seclusion in the heart of Mayfair.
