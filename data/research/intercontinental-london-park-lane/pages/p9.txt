### More like a solid 4\* hotel rather than a 5\*

2 days ago

Feels more like a solid 4\* hotel. Location is good. Lobby atmosphere is non-existent and doesn’t feel very welcoming. No lobby vibe, no where to sit and the bar and dinner areas are not exactly atmospheric either. 2 lifts out of service, so we were waiting around for a while to catch a lift on a few occasions. Weirdly pales in comparison to other Intercontinental hotels we’ve stayed in that have 4\* ratings, nevermind 5\*.

We paid extra for a Mayfair room. Initially assigned the wrong room but was rectified. Anniversary message for my wife wasn’t in the room as promised and discussed before arriving, so that surprise was basically ruined. The bathroom was extremely small. Not even enough space for one human to navigate which was super frustrating. Random hair on bed when we checked in, didn’t even bother flagging as we were late for dinner at that point. Toilet was extremely hard to flush, had to push super hard and hold. Carpet was flat as pancake, not plush at all which again is something you’d expect from a 4\* hotel.

Front desk staff were great, answered questions promptly and in a very friendly manner.

Breakfast staff missed the mark when we sat down and no one advised us the timing that breakfast would be closing, so they started packing up at a different time than we expected. No warning before packing away so it caught us off guard.

Overall stay was fine but not for what we had paid for. If we wanted just a regular hotel stay we would have opted for many of the 4\* properties nearby for a much reduced rate. We didn’t really feel like it was a special stay for us, which partly we should have looked into the vibe of the hotel before booking but assumed it would be similar if not better to other Intercontinental properties.

ProsRoom Cleanliness, Location

ConsSleep Quality, Amenities, recognition for ihg rewards club members

No, I do not recommend this hotel.
