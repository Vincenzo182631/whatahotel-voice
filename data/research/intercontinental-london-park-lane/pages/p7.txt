## FAQs for InterContinental London Park Lane

Does InterContinental London Park Lane have a pool?

No, InterContinental London Park Lane does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental London Park Lane?

At InterContinental London Park Lane, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 65 GBP .

Car parking facilities are offered for a fee of £65 per vehicle per night, with secure underground parking. Please note that the hotel is within the Central London Congestion Charge Zone - £15.00 per day. Tesla / Universal chargers available.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental London Park Lane?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental London Park Lane.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental London Park Lane for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental London Park Lane have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental London Park Lane has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental London Park Lane?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental London Park Lane.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental London Park Lane?

Check-in at InterContinental London Park Lane is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental London Park Lane offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental London Park Lane offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental London Park Lane from the nearest airport?

InterContinental London Park Lane is conveniently located near London Heathrow Airport (LHR), approximately 18 miles away (28.97 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
