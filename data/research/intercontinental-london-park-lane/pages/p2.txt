# InterContinental London Park LaneRooms & suites

Luxuriate in one of the most sought-after locations in London. Tastefully designed rooms and suites are designed to create a warm ambiance of relaxation and indulgence in the heart of vibrant Mayfair. Our 449 rooms, including 75 expansive suites, offer the ultimate luxury. All rooms feature exquisite décor and products, along with quintessential English views over Buckingham Palace, the Royal Parks, and many iconic landmarks.

### Cosy

Cosy Rooms feature elegant natural interiors with a tranquil inner courtyard location, making it the ideal accommodation for concentration or relaxation. The room also offers sleek wooden furnishings and a well-appointed bathtub to further enhance your stay. Available with either king-size or twin beds.

### Classic

Each Classic Room is a residential-like sanctuary with a serene natural colour palette, celebrating the neighbouring Royal Parks. The cityscape views set the scene for our beautiful room. Thoughtful details such as USB charging ports add a touch of modern comfort.

### Premium

Expansive windows of Premium Rooms showcase views of the vibrant cityscape of bustling London and the neighbouring Royal Park. The elegantly appointed bathroom combines practicality and style with sleek wooden furnishings featuring Byredo toiletries.

### Mayfair Cosy

The Mayfair Cosy Rooms offer luxurious interiors, ideal for concentration or relaxation. Combined with sumptuous king-size beds, marble bathrooms with a large walk-in shower, vanity box, and mood lighting controls, these rooms are here to offer the most comfortable home away from home for discerning travellers.

### Mayfair Classic

Offering expansive views and luxurious interiors, our newly refurbished Mayfair Classic Rooms are stunningly decorated with sumptuous carpets, an embroidered headboard and cast bronze details. Size starting from 22sqm.

### Mayfair Premium

The newly refurbished Mayfair Premium Rooms feature a natural colour palette with a soulful tone, combined with wood, leather, pure wool and marble. Stay in one of these accommodations for a wonderful, tranquil getaway from the bustling city of London. Accessible rooms are available.

## Suites

1. Junior Suites

2. One Bedroom Suites

3. Hyde Park Suites

### Junior Suites

The Junior Suites, with their neo-classical design, offer 35sqm of comfortable living space. These accommodations boast a plush king-size bed featuring a padded and pleated headboard, topped with soft furnishings of leather, rich embroidered silk and satin fabrics.

### One Bedroom Suites

Our One Bedroom Suites start at 40sqm, featuring spacious living areas and additional interconnecting master bedrooms. These contemporary suites provide a versatile accommodation option, ideal for both business and leisure stays in London.

### Hyde Park Suites

Hyde Park Suites provide 50sqm of prime London living, featuring spacious living areas and interconnected master bedrooms ideal for families or groups. These contemporary suites offer a versatile accommodation option for all types of travel in the capital, complemented by expansive views over the Royal Parks.

## Mayfair Floor Suites

1. Mayfair Junior Suites

2. Mayfair One Bedroom Suites

3. Mayfair Hyde Park Suites

4. Mayfair Prestige Suites

### Mayfair Junior Suites

Exquisitely designed, our Mayfair Junior Suites are among the most luxurious accommodations in London. They feature stunning bedroom views of the city’s landmarks and parks, as well as a sumptuous living area with a sofa, dining space, and a marble bath with a walk-in shower.

### Mayfair One Bedroom Suites

Mayfair One Bedroom Suites provide utmost comfort and privacy, with a separate bedroom and exquisite furnishings. Spanning over 40sqm of space, every corner of the suite exudes timeless elegance, combining classic designs with fine craftsmanship.

### Mayfair Hyde Park Suites

Commanding tranquil and precious views over Hyde Park, enjoy living and dining areas with a separate bedroom and immense wardrobe space. The luxurious marble bathroom has a bath and a walk-in shower for ultimate relaxation and comfort.

### Mayfair Prestige Suites

Our Mayfair Prestige Suites feature an additional bedroom, complemented by a spacious walk-in closet and a dedicated make-up area. The luxurious marble bathroom includes a bidet, alongside a separate powder room for added convenience. Guests also enjoy complimentary access to Club InterContinental, offering exclusive amenities and services.

### London Suite

**A modern architectural expression**

A luxury penthouse suite in the heart of Mayfair, London Suite is a spectacular duplex designed for residential-style living. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame panoramic views of the Royal Parks and Wellington Arch, while bespoke interiors, a floating staircase, and contemporary design create a sophisticated retreat for the modern traveller.

**Quiet grandeur in the heart of London**

Framing uninterrupted views across the Royal Parks and the iconic Wellington Arch, the Palace Suite is an exquisite London retreat for entertaining or unwinding. Elegant living space, dining for six, and a beautifully appointed master bedroom with a seated dressing area offer a truly elevated experience.

### Apsley Suite

**Where Design and Discerning Taste Meet**

Gracefully styled with views over Apsley House, the home of the Duke of Wellington, this luxury hotel suite offers refined, contemporary living with captivating views towards Knightsbridge and Hyde Park. Vibrant blue accents bring a modern energy to the soft, elegant design. A private dining area, a fully equipped kitchen, and the option to connect with up to three additional guest rooms create one of the finest suite hotels in London for families or gatherings.
