# InterContinental London Park LaneDestination

Located in the heart of Mayfair, InterContinental London Park Lane offers stunning views of Hyde Park and easy access to iconic landmarks, including Buckingham Palace and Piccadilly. Luxury shopping, world-class dining and vibrant culture just steps away.

Curated Experiences

### Inspired By Our Royal Heritage

Located on the former site of 145 Piccadilly, the childhood home of Queen Elizabeth II, our hotel offers a regal experience. Enjoy a stay in our Mayfair suites, indulge in an afternoon tea fit for royalty, or savour tipples inspired by the royal family's esteemed history. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in every aspect of your visit. Come and immerse yourself in the grandeur of our hotel and celebrate the royal legacy that has shaped our history.

## Things to do

### Enjoy the Royal Parks with a Picnic Hamper

The Green Park, London, SW1A 1BW

Retreat to the Royal Parks with a classic British hamper. Our exclusive guest picnic includes charcuterie, British cheeses, sandwiches, smoked cheddar and pea quiche, sausage rolls, pork pies, vegetable crudités with hummus, and a selection of salads. Classic Battenberg, chocolate brownies, and cheesecake will end your excursion on a delightfully sweet note.

Learn more

### Horseback Ride Through Hyde Park

Hyde Park, Westminster, London, W1K1AB

Enjoy horseback riding in Hyde Park. Gallop across historic paths that were once royal hunting grounds, or meander slowly, enjoying the famed park’s 350 acres of lush greenery, rose gardens, and memorials, including the Diana Memorial Fountain.

### Natural History Museum

Cromwell Rd, London, London, SW7 5BD

The Natural History Museum is sure to impress even the most jaded of children. This ornate museum is home to more than 70 million specimens including insects, fossils and rocks. The Dinosaur gallery is one of the most popular exhibits in the museum. Children will also enjoy the interactive, hands-on educational tools, gallery trails and art activities.

Learn more

### Victoria and Albert Museum

Cromwell Road, London, SW7 2RL

The Victoria and Albert Museum (or V & A as it is known) was originally founded in 1852 to inspire and educate British designers and manufacturers. Today it houses over four million items spanning centuries and hundreds of cultures from around the globe.

Learn more

### London Eye

Riverside Bldg, County Hall, Westminster, London, SE1 7PB

At 135 meters tall, the London Eye is the world's tallest observation wheel with 40 kilometers panoramic views on a clear day. The concierge team will be delighted to arrange fast-track tickets to avoid extensive queues.

Learn more

### Kew Gardens and Richmond Park

Brentford Gate, Kew, Richmond, London, Surrey TW9 3AB

Three hundred acres of botanical delights grace Kew Gardens which lies on the south bank of the River Thames. Kew Gardens remain one of the world's premier public gardens and is well worth discovering.

Learn more

### Big Bus Tour

110 Buckingham Palace Road, London, SW1W 9SA

Explore London on a double-decker bus tour, allowing you to select the attractions and places of interest that appeal to you, whilst affording time to discover at your leisure. Advance purchase tickets are available at the concierge desk for your convenience.

Learn more

### Wimbledon Stadium Tour

Church Road, Wimbledon, London, SW19 5AE

The Wimbledon London Tour gives you VIP access to restricted areas where even tournament ticket holders are forbidden to enter and is led by specially trained Blue Badge Guides who bring to life the colourful history of the world's most famous tennis club.

Learn more

### London Zoo

ZSL London Zoo, Regent's Park, London, NW1 4RY

London Zoo in Regent's Park is one of the world's most famous zoos and home to over 12,000 animals.

Learn more

### Buckingham Palace Tour

Buckingham Palace, Berkshire, London, SW1A 1AA

Embark on an exclusive journey into the epitome of regal opulence with an exclusive Buckingham Palace tour, where the grandeur of British heritage awaits your admiration. Nestled in the heart of London, this iconic palace opens its doors to reveal a world of exquisite art, magnificent state rooms, and historic treasures that narrate centuries of royal history.

Learn more

### Hyde Park

Hyde Park, Westminster, London, W1K1AB

A visit to Hyde Park from the InterContinental London Park Lane is a must for nature lovers and history enthusiasts alike. Enjoy horseback riding across the park's historic paths, once royal hunting grounds, or take a leisurely ride through its 350 acres of lush greenery. Marvel at the stunning rose gardens and explore notable memorials, including the Diana Memorial Fountain. This enchanting experience promises unforgettable memories in the heart of London.

Learn more

### Mayfair Shopping

Shopping in Mayfair is a luxury experience like no other. Just steps from the hotel, you’ll find an array of high-end boutiques and designer stores, offering the latest in fashion, jewellery, and accessories. Stroll down Bond Street and Savile Row, where the finest British craftsmanship and global luxury brands await. Whether you’re looking for bespoke tailoring, unique jewellery, or the latest runway trends, Mayfair offers a sophisticated shopping adventure right at your doorstep.

Learn more

### Royal Academy of Arts

Burlington House, Piccadilly, London, W1J 0BD

A visit to the Royal Academy of Arts is a must for art enthusiasts. Less than a mile from the hotel, the Royal Academy hosts the world's largest open-submission contemporary art exhibition, the Summer Exhibition. This impressive show offers a delightful element of surprise, presenting a diverse cross-section of contemporary art every summer. It's a must-see for anyone looking to explore the vibrant art scene in London.

Learn more

### London's West End

Embark on a dazzling adventure to London's West End, the epicentre of world-class theatre, where you can experience everything from blockbuster musicals to gripping dramas. Stroll through the lively streets adorned with historic theatres and bright marquees, soaking in the electric atmosphere. A night in the West End offers a perfect blend of culture, excitement, and unforgettable performances, making it an essential part of your London visit.

Learn more

### Tower of London

The Tower of London stands as a resplendent emblem of England's opulent history, commanding a regal presence over the River Thames. With a legacy steeped in centuries of royal intrigue and dramatic events, this formidable fortress serves as a repository for the Crown Jewels and reverberates with accounts of imprisonment and execution, affording visitors a captivating glimpse into the nation's storied past.

Learn more

### Changing of the Guard

Indulge in the opulence of British tradition with the Changing of the Guard ceremony at Buckingham Palace. As the iconic red-clad guards meticulously swap shifts amidst the resplendent backdrop of the palace's ornate facade, visitors are transported to a world of regal pomp and pageantry, bearing witness to a timeless spectacle that has enraptured audiences for generations. This luxurious experience is a must-see for those seeking to immerse themselves in the grandeur of British culture.

Learn more

## Soak In Your Surroundings

### Royal Parks Picnic

Enjoy a delightful picnic in London’s Royal Parks, including Hyde Park and Kensington Gardens, just steps from InterContinental London Park Lane. Relax in lush surroundings, take in scenic views, and savour curated picnic baskets for a truly memorable outdoor experience.

Learn more

### Natural History Museum

Explore the wonders of the Natural History Museum, one of London’s most iconic institutions. Discover fascinating exhibits on dinosaurs, wildlife, and Earth’s history in this architectural masterpiece located in South Kensington, a must-visit for science and nature lovers.

Learn more

### Victoria and Albert Museum

Immerse yourself in art, design, and history at the Victoria and Albert Museum. Located in South Kensington, this world-renowned museum offers an impressive collection of fashion, sculptures, and decorative arts spanning centuries, perfect for cultural enthusiasts.

Learn more

### London Eye

Soar above the city on the London Eye, offering breathtaking 360-degree views of London’s skyline. This iconic Ferris wheel provides a unique perspective of landmarks like Big Ben, the Thames, and Buckingham Palace, making it a must-do experience for visitors.

Learn more

### Kew Gardens and Richmond Park

Visit Kew Gardens, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and Richmond Park, London’s largest royal park. Both offer stunning landscapes, vibrant botanical displays, and wildlife, making them perfect for nature lovers seeking a peaceful escape from the city’s hustle.

Learn more

### Big Bus Tour

Explore London’s iconic landmarks on the Big Bus Tour, a hop-on, hop-off sightseeing adventure. Visit famous sites like the Tower of London, Buckingham Palace, and the Houses of Parliament, all while enjoying informative commentary and panoramic city views.

Learn more

Destination

### Wimbledon

Visit Wimbledon, the world’s most prestigious tennis tournament, held annually in South West London. Explore the grounds, visit the Wimbledon Lawn Tennis Museum, and catch thrilling live matches during the summer for an unforgettable sports experience.

Learn More
