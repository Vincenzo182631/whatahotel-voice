# InterContinental London Park LaneClub InterContinental

Experience another level of world-class service that truly delights in our Club InterContinental lounge where unforgettable culinary delicacies await. Special highlights include our new afternoon tea with the ultimate selection of sandwiches, freshly baked English scones with a selection of the finest conserves and clotted cream. Exquisite cakes created by our pastry chef provide the finale to this truly decadent event.

Glide into the evening with your favourite tipple, or one of our bespoke creations accompanied by a tantalising choice of hot and cold canapés.

And unlike anywhere else, you will enjoy the most spectacular view looking out over the Royal Parks and monuments and even further afield to Buckingham Palace.

### It's In The Details

Our Club InterContinental Lounge welcomes Club guests daily between 6:30 AM and 10:30 PM.

Club InterContinental aims beyond the obvious. We have put extra thought into every small detail to make sure your stay feels very special, combining personal touches with exclusive privileges and signature moments.

### Club InterContinental Benefits

- A personalised welcome experience, including a complimentary drink and private check-in and check-out.
- A complimentary breakfast for two people to be taken in the Club Lounge between 6:30 AM and 10:30 AM Monday to Friday and 6:30 AM and 11:00 AM Saturday and Sunday.
- Afternoon Tea for two people to be taken in the Club Lounge between 1:30 PM and 3:30 PM. Featuring seasonal sandwiches, scones with homemade preserves, an elegant selection of cakes and pastries and a pot of our uniquely blended No.1 Park Lane tea.
- Evening drinks, champagne, and canapés for two people between 5:00 PM and 7:00 PM.
- All-day refreshments.

## Green Key

At InterContinental Edinburgh The George, we’re proud to be certified by Green Key - a “standard of excellence” ecolabel in the field of environmental responsibility and sustainable operation within the tourism industry.

### Additional Services

- Club Lounge has areas for relaxation, light dining, drinks, and business discussions.
- Entertainment area featuring a plasma TV and wireless internet access.
- Shower facilities are ideal for early arrivals.
- Personalised Concierge and business services.
- Complimentary local calls (0203, 0207, 0208)
