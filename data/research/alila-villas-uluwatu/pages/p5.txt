## Alila Moments

Shaped by land and craft, by culture and care, Alila Moments reveal a different way of seeing. These personalized experiences offered at our Alila properties draw you closer to the world around you, and your place within it. These are our Moments, crafted just for you.

### The Spiritual Essence of Bali: A Journey of Ritual, Reflection and Wonder

This soulful experience fosters mindfulness, gratitude and creativity, leaving guests with lasting memories and a profound connection to Bali’s timeless beauty and vibrant traditions. The day begins with the Melukat ritual, a sacred water purification ceremony, followed by a Nasi Yasa breakfast that celebrates Bali’s colorful culinary heritage. Let your creativity blossom during a Sodan crafting workshop, where guests create intricate floral offerings that will then be presented at the stunning Uluwatu Temple. As the sun sets, gather for a traditional Kecak Dance performance—a vivid celebration of myth and movement that brings the island’s stories to life. From ritual to reflection, this is a day of renewal, beauty and spiritual connection unlike any other.

**Sacred Waters**

Participate in a Melukat ritual, a sacred spiritual cleansing at the resort’s temple. Experience the calming sounds of flowing water and whispered prayers that release negative energy and restore balance.

**A Taste of Tradition**

Enjoy a traditional Nasi Yasa breakfast, a fragrant meal that introduces you to Bali’s culinary heritage. The diverse, local flavors tell the story of the island’s traditions in every bite.

**Crafted with Meaning**

Weaving together coconut leaves and blossoms, guests learn to make a Balinese offering during a Sodan crafting workshop.

**Clifftop Connection**

Travel to the clifftop Uluwatu Temple, set 230 feet above the Indian Ocean. Here you can offer your handmade Sodan, saying your own personal prayer at this magical Hindu site.

**Legends at Sunset**

End the day with the mystical Kecak Dance performance at sunset. Firelight, rhythmic chanting, and dramatic storytelling bring Bali’s myths to life in vivid movement and costume.

**Duration:** 7-8 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Alila Villas Uluwatu will provide Balinese attire for the experience. The team will arrive to the guest's villa 15 minutes before the start of Alila Moments to assist with wearing the attire. Alternatively, guests may choose to wear their own polite shirt.

**Price:** Price per 2 pax at IDR 10.000.000 + 21 % tax & services

### The Beauty of Mindful Living—Sustainable Steps in Nature

In this enlightening experience, you'll explore the thoughtful design and sustainable practices that make Alila Uluwatu a pioneer in mindful, sustainable hospitality. Find your holistic bearings first in our property's Sustainability Lab, where we introduce innovative practices for zero waste living. Continue into the Organic Garden and learn how regenerative farming feeds both body and earth. Then, tap into ancient wisdom with a hands-on Jamu-making session, crafting your own herbal wellness tonic that Bali residents have consumed for generations for digestive health. Leave with empowering new ways to shepherd the island—and your own environment at home—towards a more sustainable future.

**An Innovative Start**

Visit the Sustainability Lab, the center of the eco-conscious practices for the resort, and discover the unique ways glass, plastics, food waste and other materials are transformed into new resources.

**Explore Nature's Bounty**

Tour the property’s Organic Garden, where farmers practice sustainable farming and other methods that will lead us to a zero-waste future.

**Elixirs of the Earth**

Craft your own Jamu—a traditional herbal remedy—using freshly picked ingredients, learning how wellness begins in the soil and ends in your hands.

### From Market to Village to Table: A Balinese Culinary Journey

Get to know the vibrant cuisine of Bali—and the chef within you—as you journey through a traditional Fishermen Village and local produce market, guided by our chef. Begin your day where the sea meets the soul of the island, meeting local fishermen who offer their freshest catch before it reaches distributors or the market, giving you the rare opportunity to select from the ocean’s bounty at its very source. Our chef, the Kelian Adat (traditional leader) of the Fishermen Village, will gently encourage you to engage with the fishermen and market vendors, ask questions, and explore the rich diversity of island-grown fruits, vegetables and seafood. Back at the property, learn time-honored techniques to prepare your handpicked ingredients. Then, gather to share a meal that is not only deeply Balinese—but a reflection of your own journey. There’s no better way to connect with the spirit of the island than through its food, its people and your own two hands.

**Coastal Traditions**

Visit a nearby Fishermen Village to witness Balinese aquaculture in action. At the fish market, choose the perfect catch for your culinary creation.

**Market Discovery**

Stroll through the bustling local produce market as Chef selects island-grown fruits, vegetables and spices—many of which you’ll taste for the first time.

**Cooking with Heart**

Return to the property for a hands-on cooking class, where Chef shares time-honored Balinese techniques and guides you as you prepare your feast.

**The Shared Table**

Gather around the table to enjoy the meal you created—an experience infused with Balinese flavors, local knowledge and the joy of shared discovery.

**Duration:** 7-8 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Casual attire

**Price:** Price per pax at IDR 2.250.000 + 21 % tax & services

### Sacred Waters: A Poetic Pilgrimage

Born from a creative collaboration between the team at Alila Villas Uluwatu and acclaimed poet Lang Leav, this experience invites guests to explore harmony through the lens of poetry and Balinese ritual.

Inspired by Tri Hita Karana, the Balinese belief in harmony between the divine, nature and humankind, the journey unfolds with a traditional Melukat purification ceremony at Suranadhi Temple. Flowing spring water and sacred chants offer space to let go—an act of cleansing both literal and symbolic.

Afterward, guests are welcomed into a tranquil organic garden, where a simple breakfast is served beneath the trees. In this setting of calm and connection, you’ll be invited to compose a poem—guided by prompts drawn from the day’s experience.

After time spent in reflection and creativity, the experience concludes with a relaxed coastal lunch at Alila’s vibrant sister property, Andaz Bali.

**Morning Ritual and Renewal**

Your Villa Host will arrive to help you dress in traditional Balinese attire, including a sarong and sash, before accompanying you to the lobby.

**Spiritual Cleansing at Suranadhi Temple**

Travel to Suranadhi Temple for a traditional Melukat purification ceremony. Led by a local priest, this sacred ritual of flowing water and prayer invites you to release, rebalance and begin anew.

**Organic Garden Gathering**

Following the ceremony, continue to a tranquil organic garden, where a light Balinese breakfast awaits beneath the trees. Here, take time to reflect on the morning’s journey in peaceful seclusion.

**Poetry in the Garden**

Guided by prompts inspired by the experience, you’ll compose your own poem in a notebook made from recycled paper by the Alila Sustainability Lab.

**Coastal Lunch at Andaz Bali**

Conclude your journey with a relaxed seaside lunch at Fisherman’s Club, the vibrant beachfront eatery at Alila’s sister property, Andaz Bali.

**Duration:** 9-10 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Alila Villas Uluwatu will provide Balinese attire for the experience. Our team will arrive 15 minutes before the start of Alila Moments to assist with wearing the attire. Alternatively, guests may choose to wear their own resort formal shirt.

**Additional Details:** Poet Lang Leav created this journey with us, inspired by her recent stay at Alila Uluwatu. This moment requires a minimum of two guests to organize.

**Price:** Price per 2 pax at IDR 13.000.000 + 21 % tax & services
