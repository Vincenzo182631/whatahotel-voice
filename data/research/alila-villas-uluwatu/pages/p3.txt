## Batique

An cocktail destination where Indonesian craftsmanship and contemporary bartending artistry converge. Guided by a philosophy of seasonality, provenance, and innovation, each cocktail is thoughtfully crafted using locally inspired ingredients, house-made infusions, and refined techniques that honour the diversity of the Indonesian archipelago. Complemented by a carefully curated collection of premium spirits and whiskies, Batique offers a sophisticated journey of discovery where every pour tells a story and every visit reveals a new perspective on Indonesia's evolving cocktail culture.
