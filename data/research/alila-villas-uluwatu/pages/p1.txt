## A Luxurious Melding of Balinese Sky and Surf, Set Atop a Cliff

Set on a clifftop plateau along the southern coastline on Bali’s Bukit Peninsula, Alila Villas Uluwatu serves breathtaking coastal vistas, with unending sky and ocean views from our eco-friendly resort. This private corner of paradise offers the perfect escape and personalized journey, whether you intend to surf Southern Bali’s swells, relax by the cliffside infinity pool, restore your body and mind in a yoga class or spa treatment, or visit sacred local temples. Our pool villas offer generous private space, incredible comfort and stunning views, and an open-concept design throughout the resort embraces the outdoors while offering undisturbed privacy.

#### Spa Alila

1 / 5

Spa Alila exists to restore the balance between mind and body, the spirit and nature. We take a traditional approach, engaging local spa therapists—the keepers of age-old traditions and concoctions—to share effective and soothing therapies with guests in a serene, contemporary setting. Time-honored Balinese practices inform every treatment and technique, providing a sense of connection to the community and harnessing the curative properties of the local traditions our spa technicians bring. From sayang massage techniques that use long, rhythmic strokes in individualized sequences to the medicinal herbs we use, every Alila Spa journey is sublime.

#### Cliff Edge Spa Cabana

2 / 5

Experience a truly relaxing spa treatment in the Cliff Edge Spa Cabana, perched on the side of the Uluwatu cliff with a breathtaking view of the Indian Ocean, accompanied by the chirping of birds and the sound of waves crashing below. The unwinding experience begins from the moment you walk through the path of lush vegetation along the cliff, overlooking the ocean’s soothing blue, before arriving at the Spa Cabana for your 2-hour treatment, which includes a foot wipe, a Balinese or Therapeutic Massage, sound healing and a relaxing eye ritual. Continue your zen experience snuggled on the sofa, savoring delectable healthy snacks as your body, mind and soul awaken to all the positive energy and benefits of this elevated spa indulgence.

## Discover Alila Villas Uluwatu

Innovative Sustainability

Designed by award-winning Singapore-based WOHA Architects to exist in harmony with its natural surroundings, Alila Villas Uluwatu embraces environmentally sustainable design principles and is the first resort in Indonesia to receive the highest level of certification from EarthCheck as a Best Practice Building Planning and Design (BPDS) development. At Alila Villas Uluwatu, we aim to respect and preserve the way of life and traditions of local communities and minimize our impact on the environment—spoiling guests without spoiling the planet.

Monthly Highlights

Embrace the exclusive monthly offerings at Alila Villas Uluwatu, where cultural experiences unfold in captivating ways—from immersive journeys to exquisite culinary delights. Rejuvenate with tranquil wellness retreats, all set within the vibrant luxury of Alila Villas Uluwatu.

Steeped in Culture

In every aspect of the Alila sensibility is a commitment to honoring and sharing the rich Balinese way of life. We find inspiration and create relationships in the communities we inhabit. More than an approach, this core philosophy touches every facet of the Alila experience. From design to Spa Alila, dining and Moments, we infuse local culture and traditions into every aspect of your stay.

A Taste of Bali

At every turn, there is an opportunity to taste centuries of culinary traditions in their modern form. The Warung, a welcoming open-air space, celebrates vibrant local flavors while globally inspired CIRE offers Pan-Asian and Mediterranean fare made with fresh seasonal and organic ingredients. Every menu features recipes crafted by local chefs to offer guests the very best epicurean experience, while connecting them to regional traditions. At Sunset Cabana Bar, cocktail hour takes new heights in this unique setting suspended over the cliff’s edge with mesmerizing ocean views.

We had a wonderful stay at Alila Villas Uluwatu. The views from the reception and restaurants are magical.
