Getting around

M2 Line

The M2 line of the Istanbul Metro is a 10-minute walk from the hotel.

Bus

There are also bus stops nearby. Bus No. 43 takes you directly to Taksim Square. You can also use Bus No. 30M, which runs along the Mecidiyeköy area.

Taxi

By taxi, you can reach Beşiktaş Ferry Station in just 9 minutes. Taksim Square is an 8-minute taxi ride away, and you can reach the historic district of Eminönü/Sultanahmet in about 13 minutes.

Cable Car

Additionally, there is a cable car from Maçka Park to Taksim, providing a scenic way to travel to the area.

Bus Station

Teşvikiye bus station provides access to the Bosporus, Dolmabahçe Palace, Istanbul Modern, Galata Tower, Topkapi Palace and Hagia Sophia.
