## Cipriani Restaurant

Within Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas, Cipriani Istanbul carries four generations of Italian hospitality into one of the city's most sophisticated dining rooms. Mahogany walls, marble floors and the brand's signature leather chairs create a setting of timeless elegance, completed by the iconic Cipriani aquarium — a detail present in every restaurant the family has opened around the world. The menu honors the enduring traditions of Venetian cuisine, from Carpaccio and Bellinis to the celebrated Pappardelle Cipriani. Elegant interiors and attentive service make Cipriani Istanbul a destination in its own right, drawing guests and residents of the city alike.

## The Lobby Lounge

The Lobby Lounge at Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas is a beautifully lit space of elegant calm, set beneath the hotel's glass atrium where natural light filters through the afternoon. Each day from 3:00 PM, an exceptional selection of loose-leaf teas from around the world, sweet and savory delights and signature cocktails are served to the gentle accompaniment of live harp and cello music — one of Nişantaşı's most pleasurable ways to spend an afternoon.

## Nusr-Et Macka Palas

Nusr-Et Maçka Palas brings the celebrated steakhouse experience to the heart of Nişantaşı, within Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas. World-renowned cuts are dry-aged and prepared with an exacting attention to craft, from the signature Nusr-Et Special prepared tableside and Golden Baklava to the iconic salt-seasoning ritual that has made Nusr-Et a global phenomenon. The setting is refined and contemporary, the energy distinctly its own — equally suited to a long, leisurely lunch or a memorable evening in the city.

## Kapicciiiinoo

Kapicciiiinoo, Nusret Gökçe's first coffee concept, is located within Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas in the heart of Nişantaşı. The café offers a menu ranging from artisanal coffees and matcha to carefully crafted Dolce desserts. Its signature creation, the Gold Kapicciiiinoo — prepared with gold foil and a recipe that has never been disclosed — has become a destination in its own right, drawing coffee enthusiasts and curious visitors to Nişantaşı from across the city.

## Roof Bar 805

Opening is dependent on special events and weather conditions.

Perched above the rooftops of Nişantaşı on the eighth floor of Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas, Roof Bar 805 offers one of the neighborhood’s most distinctive open-air settings. Views extend towards the Bosphorus, with the rooftops of Nişantaşı spread below and the city's skyline visible beyond. A carefully crafted selection of signature cocktails and seasonal drinks is served throughout the evening in a relaxed and refined atmosphere. Open during the summer months only, and subject to weather conditions and private event reservations.

## Kalimera Istanbul Restaurant

Within Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas, Kalimera Istanbul brings the flavors and spirit of the Aegean to Nişantaşı, rooted in Greek tradition and the simplicity of coastal cuisine. The setting evokes the whitewashed simplicity of the Greek islands, with interiors of white and blue that create an atmosphere of easy, sun-lit warmth. Seasonal ingredients sourced from Greece inform a menu built around fresh seafood, traditional mezedes and handmade Greek desserts — presented directly at the table in keeping with the spirit of genuine Greek hospitality. Breakfast is available from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM, with lunch and dinner served throughout the day.
