# Luxury Hotel in Nişantaşı, Istanbul \| Park Hyatt Istanbul Macka Palas

## A Historic Residence in the Heart of Nişantaşı

Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas occupies one of Istanbul's most storied addresses. Designed by Giulio Mongeri and completed in 1922, the Art Deco Maçka Palas building was conceived as a private residence of distinction — a character it has quietly retained ever since. Following an extensive restoration, its 90 luxury rooms and suites offer an intimate retreat at the heart of Nişantaşı, where the city's finest boutiques, restaurants and galleries are steps away. Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas's spaces carry the warmth of a 5-star private home, enriched by carefully curated works of art including photography by Ara Güler, Istanbul's most celebrated visual chronicler

### Cipriani Restaurant

Within Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas, Cipriani Istanbul carries four generations of Italian hospitality into one of the city's most sophisticated dining rooms. Mahogany walls, marble floors and the brand's signature leather chairs create a setting of timeless elegance, completed by the iconic Cipriani aquarium — a detail present in every restaurant the family has opened around the world. The menu honors the enduring traditions of Venetian cuisine, from Carpaccio and Bellinis to the celebrated Pappardelle Cipriani. Elegant interiors and attentive service make Cipriani Istanbul a destination in its own right, drawing guests and residents of the city alike.

## Things to Do

Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas sits at the heart of Nişantaşı, one of Istanbul's most exclusive neighborhoods, known for its high-end fashion, gastronomy and lifestyle. Dolmabahçe Palace, the Bosphorus waterfront and Maçka Park are within minutes, alongside the districts of Beşiktaş and Taksim. For those visiting on business, the Istanbul Congress Center and Lütfi Kırdar Convention Center are nearby. The hotel's concierges are well placed to arrange any experience the city has to offer.
