# Hotel Rooms in Nişantaşı, Istanbul \| Park Hyatt Istanbul Macka Palas

# Accommodations

Check-In3:00 PM

Checkout12:00 PM

Each of the 90 rooms and suites at Park Hyatt Istanbul – Maçka Palas reflects the building's Art Deco origins — defined by space, natural light and a residential sense of calm. French balconies frame views across the rooftops of Nişantaşı and, in select suites, towards the Bosphorus. Photography by Ara Güler runs throughout, lending each space a personal connection to the city. Select suites feature a private Turkish hammam, a steam room, and Dornbracht rainfall showers.

250 sq mWater View

Occupying a top-floor corner position, the Palazzo Suite is the hotel's most exceptional accommodation — 250 square meters of space arranged across a separate bedroom, living and dining areas, guest bathroom, work desk and minibar. The marble bathroom features a full Turkish hammam, steam room, deep soaking tub, Dornbracht rainfall shower and refrigerated cosmetics cabinet.
