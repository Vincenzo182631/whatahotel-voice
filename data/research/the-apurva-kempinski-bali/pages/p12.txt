**Directions from Airport**

From Ngurah Rai International Airport, proceed east towards the roundabout and onto Bali Mandara Toll Road, which will take you straight to Nusa Dua. Turn left to Bypass Ngurah Rai Street, and take a right turn into Pintas Siligita Selatan Street. Follow the road and turn right to Nusa Dua Selatan Street. The Apurva Kempinski Bali is located 2 km ahead. These simple Bali resort directions make the journey from touchdown to check-in a smooth, scenic one.

**Why stay in Nusa Dua**

For travellers weighing where to stay in Nusa Dua, this enclave is known for calm waters and five-star resorts set apart from Bali's busier hubs — seclusion paired with quick access to the airport, convention centres and golf courses. As a hotel near Ngurah Rai airport, The Apurva Kempinski Bali offers a short arrival transfer without compromising privacy.
