### Swimming Pool and Kids Pool

Set amidst tropical gardens with magnificent views of the Indian Ocean, our spectacular 60-metre swimming pool is the perfect place to swim laps or simply to take a refreshing dip at your leisure. A children’s pool is located adjacent to the main pool.

As a leading family pool resort in Bali, The Apurva Kempinski Bali is designed so parents and children can enjoy the water together without leaving anyone out. The main pool's generous 60-metre stretch gives adults space to swim uninterrupted, while the adjoining kids pool at our Nusa Dua hotel offers a shallower, gentler setting where younger guests can splash safely under supervision.

Sun loungers and daybeds line the pool deck, shaded by tropical planting and framed by uninterrupted ocean views, making it easy to see why guests consistently rate this among the best resort pools in Bali. Attentive poolside service, fresh towels and refreshing drinks are on hand throughout the day, so the only decision left is whether to swim, float or simply relax.

Families looking for extra excitement will find our resort's wider recreational offering just as rewarding as the pool itself. Guests searching for a waterslide hotel in Bali experience will find our team happy to recommend the full range of family activities available across the property, ensuring every member of the family, from toddlers to grandparents, has something to look forward to each day.

Whether you are here for a quiet morning swim, an afternoon of family play, or golden-hour views over the Indian Ocean, the Swimming Pool and Kids Pool remains one of the most loved spaces at The Apurva Kempinski Bali, Nusa Dua.
