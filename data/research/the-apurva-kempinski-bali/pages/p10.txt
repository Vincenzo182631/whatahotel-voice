# Experience Real Access

Rooted in over 4,500 years of global exchange, the Spice Route Voyage invites guests to traverse the historic waters of the Indonesian archipelago aboard a handcrafted schooner phinisi, retracing the paths that once connected kingdoms, continents, and cultures through the trade of spices such as clove, nutmeg, and pepper.

### What E.R.A Are You In?

At The Apurva Kempinski Bali, the Spice Route Voyage is reimagined as a defining destination experience transforming Indonesia’s ancient maritime legacy into a contemporary journey of discovery, culture, and refined luxury.

Rooted in over 4,500 years of global exchange, the experience invites guests to traverse the historic waters of the Indonesian archipelago aboard a handcrafted schooner phinisi, retracing the paths that once connected kingdoms, continents, and cultures through the trade of spices such as clove, nutmeg, and pepper.
