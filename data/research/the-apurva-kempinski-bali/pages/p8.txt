### Jalak Family Club

Inspired by the methodological approach of Montessori, in which education is based on self-directed activity and hands-on learning, Family VIP offers a wide range of activities responding to the diverse needs of children, teens and their families within the resort facilities.

Family Club will make The Apurva Kempinski Bali an even more perfect place to spend time with the family. Providing an inside and outside playground, in addition to the family pool, will leave nothing to be desired and will let children’s hearts leap up.

Until then, we provide alternative indoor playing space for our little VIPs where the usual activities already take place.
