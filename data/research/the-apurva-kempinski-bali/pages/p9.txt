# Electric Vehicle Experience

Explore the Nusa Dua area and adjacent attractions while contributing to reducing carbon emissions.

### Electric Vehicle Experience

The Apurva Kempinski Bali has been consistently pushing out initiatives that are centred on embedding a sustainable plan into its programmes and guest experiences. As a leading presence on Bali's hospitality scene, it is committed to working towards Indonesia's Sustainable Development Goals with a series of efforts, such as operating the 'Electric Vehicle Experience' together with Nissan LEAF. This partnership focuses on creating a more environmentally friendly option for travellers.

Launched in 2023, guests who stay at the resort can use Nissan's Kicks Hybrid and Nissan Leaf Electric to explore the Nusa Dua area and adjacent attractions while contributing to reducing carbon emissions. This EV shuttle Bali hotel service reflects our broader commitment to responsible tourism, giving guests an effortless way to discover Bali's coastline, cultural landmarks, and dining destinations without compromising on comfort or sustainability values.

Guests can book these experiences by contacting our concierge.

**A Sustainable Resort Bali Experience, Reimagined**

As a sustainable resort Bali, we believe eco-conscious travel should never mean compromising on luxury. The Electric Vehicle Experience is one of several initiatives at The Apurva Kempinski Bali designed to lower our environmental footprint while enhancing how guests explore Nusa Dua, from quiet, emission-free rides along coastal roads to convenient transfers between the resort and nearby attractions.

**Why Choose Our Electric Vehicle Experience in Nusa Dua**

Guests seeking an electric vehicle experience Nusa Dua can expect a quiet, comfortable ride paired with attentive service. Whether heading to nearby beaches, cultural sites, or dining venues, the Nissan Kicks Hybrid and Nissan Leaf Electric offer a smooth and sustainable alternative to conventional transport, reinforcing The Apurva Kempinski Bali's position as an eco friendly luxury resort Bali travellers can feel good about choosing.
