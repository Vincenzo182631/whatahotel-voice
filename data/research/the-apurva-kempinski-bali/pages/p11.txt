# Sustainability

Our vision is to become the Bali's leading luxury hotel brand in terms of sustainability.

### Kempinski Sustainability

The Apurva Kempinski Bali is committed to adopting sustainability into its foundation and operations, encompassing the Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) impacts. As a Sustainable Resort Bali travellers can rely on, the property weaves environmental stewardship, social responsibility and transparent governance into every stage of the guest experience, from arrival to departure.

Guided by Kempinski's global standards and rooted in Balinese values of harmony with nature, our approach to sustainable hospitality reflects a genuine, long-term commitment rather than a seasonal initiative. Every department, from culinary operations to housekeeping and landscaping, plays a role in reducing environmental impact while preserving the cultural richness that defines Nusa Dua and the wider island of Bali.

As an environmentally responsible resort, The Apurva Kempinski Bali continuously reviews its energy use, water management, waste reduction and sourcing practices, working towards measurable outcomes that support both the local ecosystem and the communities surrounding the resort. This ongoing effort forms part of a wider ambition: to make sustainable travel Bali not only accessible but the natural choice for discerning guests who value responsible luxury.
