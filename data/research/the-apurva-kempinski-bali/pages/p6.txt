### Cliff Lounge

With uninterrupted views of the Indian Ocean and situated at the 9th and 10th floor on cliff main building, the Cliﬀ Lounge oﬀers privacy and exclusivity for suite guests. Exclusive breakfast and all day free-flow refreshment are available. This intimate lounge features two ﬂoors; the upper level serves as a dedicated breakfast venue, while the lower level features a generous lounge area for leisure and business purpose, bar, infinity pool, and hydro jet pool.

Perched above the coastline, it stands as one of Bali's most exclusive lounge Bali resort spaces, purpose-built for guests seeking a private, elevated retreat within the resort.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**What is the Cliff Lounge at The Apurva Kempinski Bali?**

The Cliff Lounge is a two-floor, suite-exclusive lounge set against the cliff at The Apurva Kempinski Bali, offering breakfast, all-day refreshments, an infinity pool, and a hydro jet pool with personalised service.

**Who can access the Cliff Lounge?**

Cliff Lounge access is reserved exclusively for suite guests staying at The Apurva Kempinski Bali, offering a level of private lounge access typically associated with a luxury hotel's top-tier accommodation category.

**Where is the Cliff Lounge located?**

The Cliff Lounge is located on the 9th and 10th floors of the cliff main building at The Apurva Kempinski Bali in Nusa Dua, Bali, with uninterrupted views of the Indian Ocean.

**What are the Cliff Lounge operating hours?**

The Cliff Lounge operates daily from 06:30 to 21:00, with breakfast served from 06:30 to 12:00, afternoon tea from 15:00 to 17:00, and evening cocktails from 17:00 to 19:00.
