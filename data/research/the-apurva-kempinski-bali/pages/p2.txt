### Why stay with us?

At The Apurva Kempinski Bali, guests are invited to connect to authentic Balinese culture, or simply enjoy their privacy to the fullest and reconnect with their loved ones. Whatever your preference is, it is our pleasure of sharing with you the reasons why The Apurva Kempinski Bali should be your preferred destination. If you are wondering why choose Apurva Kempinski for your next holiday, the answer lies in the balance of cultural depth, five-star comfort and a cliff-top setting that few properties in Bali can match.

**1\. Full Facilities Open**

Whichever room you prefer from our collection of 475 well-appointed rooms, iconic suites and exclusive villas, a full experience at The Apurva Kempinski Bali is waiting for you. With seven dining outlets including Dim Sum and Hot Pot Restaurant - Bai Yun, an award-winning iconic spa and fitness centre, as well as versatile meetings and events spaces, our resort offers guests a wealth of options that make every stay unique. This breadth of facilities under one roof is one of the clearest reasons guests search for and choose the best 5 star resort in Bali.

**2\. Nations Rich Legacy**

From the majestic architectural design, to the myriad of experiences offered at the resort, every aspect of The Apurva Kempinski Bali is a homage to the nation’s culture and traditions. And once you enter the resort, you are part of it too.

Inspired by the island’s mother temple, Besakih, the Grand Staircase is a signature detail of 250 flights of stairs. Reflecting Bali’s iconic terraced paddy field, the cliff suites and its waterfall hill took inspiration from subak, Balinese irrigation system. At the Pendopo Lobby, towering Gebyok partitions stand, each piece beautifully crafted by local artisans. These are some of many traditional influences that can be seen at the resort, making it a unique space unlike any other resorts, and a defining reason The Apurva Kempinski Bali is regarded as the best resort in Bali for guests who want cultural depth alongside five-star comfort

**3\. Strategic Location**

With its cliff top location in the trendy Nusa Dua neighbourhood, The Apurva Kempinski Bali is perfectly located for business and leisure travellers alike. Just 15 minutes from the Ngurah Rai International Airport, the resort is close to upscale shopping and entertainment options, and the Bali International Convention Centre is a short drive away. This convenient, cliff-top address is a major reason The Apurva Kempinski Bali stands out as the top luxury hotel in Nusa Dua, giving guests easy access to the airport, business venues and Nusa Dua's finest beaches without compromising on privacy.

**4\. Culinary Journey**

Dining at The Apurva Kempinski Bali is an experience not to be missed. Dim sum and hot pot restaurant Bai Yun adds to the diversity of culinary offerings presented at the resort. Six other dining venues, including the award-winning Koral Restaurant are ready to welcome you, each with their own unique charms,reinforcing the resort's reputation as a top luxury hotel in Nusa Dua for food-driven travellers.

Our Wellness Experts will guide you through various complimentary wellness activities that are available daily. Whether you wish to train your body or simply exercise to feel fresh, our ocean-view fitness centre will help you stay fit, with an array of modern equipment.

We believe that your experience with us should be a story worth telling. Everyone at The Apurva Kempinski Bali is here to ensure that your stay with us is a memorable one, and it is reflected in the five-star luxury service offered. The Apurva Kempinski Bali is featured with 475 well-appointed guest rooms, striking suites and discreet villas in a quiet beachfront setting.
