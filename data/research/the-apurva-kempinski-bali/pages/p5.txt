### The Most attentive Host

At the heart of Kempinski is the remarkable people who look after each and every guest attentively and thoughtfully. It is this philosophy that shapes every signature guest experience Bali resort guests encounter at The Apurva Kempinski Bali, none more iconic than the Lady in Red.

**The most attentive host**

The Lady In Red is your personal dedicated concierge. She knows everything about the city and can offer personalised recommendations on what to see and do in Bali. For more than a decade every guest has been welcomed by our Lady in Red, providing thoughtful, individual and intuitive help and expertise. Dressed in striking red, this impeccable host has unrivalled knowledge about the hotel and the local area. Lady in Red Apurva Kempinski is instantly recognisable, not only for her striking uniform, but for the warmth, precision and local insight she brings to every interaction, from restaurant reservations to hidden temple visits few travellers ever discover on their own.

**A Signature Guest Experience in a Bali Resort**

The Lady in Red is one of the defining reasons The Apurva Kempinski Bali is considered a unique hotel experience Nusa Dua. Her presence transforms an ordinary stay into a curated journey, guiding guests toward the island's best-kept culinary spots, sunset viewpoints and cultural ceremonies, all tailored to individual preferences and pace. Whether guests are visiting Bali for the first time or returning to explore it more deeply, the Lady in Red designs each recommendation around what matters most to them.

**Storytelling at the Heart of Balinese Hospitality**

Beyond logistics and recommendations, the Lady in Red is a storytelling hotel Bali guests often mention long after checkout. She shares the legends, rituals and everyday life of Bali with genuine warmth, turning simple directions into memorable narratives. This dedication to storytelling reflects The Apurva Kempinski Bali's broader commitment to authentic, immersive hospitality, where every detail, from architecture to service, tells a piece of the island's story.

**Meet Your Lady in Red**

From the moment of arrival, guests are invited to connect with their Lady in Red to plan an itinerary shaped entirely around them. It is this dedicated, human touch, sustained for more than ten years, that continues to set The Apurva Kempinski Bali apart as a benchmark for personalised luxury hospitality in Bali.
