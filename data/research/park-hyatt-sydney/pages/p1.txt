# 5 Star Hotel in The Rocks \| Park Hyatt Sydney

## There's No Place Else We'd Rather Be

Located on the very edge of Sydney Harbour with front-row seats to the world-famous Opera House, our luxurious harbourside residence is unmistakably Australian.

As green and gold ferries bustle back and forth across the harbour, sit back on your private balcony and watch Sydney come to life. All this within arm’s reach of the charming cobblestone laneways of Sydney’s historic Rocks precinct.

Explore the magic of Sydney like nowhere else at Park Hyatt Sydney.

## Discover Park Hyatt Sydney

Gallery of Australian Art

Explore a gallery of Australian art in our rooms, corridors and throughout the hotel—all paying homage to our harbour city and aboriginal history.

The Spa

Escape from the bustling streets of Sydney and enjoy a peaceful experience at our day spa. Enjoy five suite-inspired treatment rooms, an intimate relaxation zone and two steam rooms.

Rooftop Pool

Our exclusive rooftop pool is open year-round, with sweeping views of Sydney Harbour. Note: The pool is heated during our autumn and winter months, and there is no lifeguard on duty. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.

Fitness Centre

Our fully equipped Fitness Centre features a range of cardiovascular and weight-training equipment. The gym overlooks Dawes Point Reserve and Sydney Harbour.

## Things To Do

Stay in a luxury hotel situated along Sydney Harbour and its surrounding local gems, like the Museum of Contemporary Art, the stunning Harbour Bridge and the Royal Botanic Gardens—all just a 15-minute walk or less from Park Hyatt Sydney. The Sydney Opera House is just 2 kilometres from our doorstep; see a show or snap selfies in front of its iconic architecture. Take the kids to any number of local beaches, from Bondi Beach to Bronte Beach to Freshwater Beach, or visit the Sydney Tower Eye skyscraper for bird’s-eye views of the city.
