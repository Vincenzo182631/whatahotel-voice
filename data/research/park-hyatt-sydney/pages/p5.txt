Hotel location is excellent and they are banking on that. Service has declined a lot. Like a lot of hotels they reduced services after Covid and never brought them back. Which is disappointing. Boutique hotels are doing more personalized services.
I had a situation with my room and felt like I was inconveniencing the staff even though it was a health hazard to stay in the room.
As a store owner nearby said when we were chatting. “Hyatt needs to send people there to make changes because people are staying elsewhere”
