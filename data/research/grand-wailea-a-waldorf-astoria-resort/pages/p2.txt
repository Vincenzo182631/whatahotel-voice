### Napua Suites

Set above the heart of the resort, Napua Suites offer an elevated island retreat on the resort’s private upper floors. Thoughtfully designed with natural Hawaiian elements, contemporary interiors and refined finishes, each suite captures the relaxed pace of island living with the comforts of a private club-level experience. Guests enjoy dedicated Napua Club privileges and the exceptional amenities of Grand Wailea, A Waldorf Astoria Resort.
