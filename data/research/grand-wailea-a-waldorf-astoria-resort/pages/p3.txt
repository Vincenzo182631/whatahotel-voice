# Dining & Drinks

Dining & Drinks

Discover inventive takes on traditional Hawaiian flavors for an unforgettable dining experience rooted in the essence of the ʻāina (land) we call home. Explore chef-created plates and tropical cocktails at our resort’s collection of indoor-outdoor restaurants and bars.

## Nobu Grand Wailea Maui

The spacious and stunning Nobu Grand Wailea features indoor and alfresco dining spaces complimented by striking murals and panoramic ocean views. Signature dishes include the iconic Black Cod Miso and Yellowtail Jalapeño, alongside Nobu’s acclaimed sushi known around the world.

**Open Daily:**

5 pm – 9 pm

Explore Nobu Grand Wailea MauiMake a Reservation

## Humuhumunukunukuāpuaʻa

Humuhumunukunukuāpuaʻa offers a dining experience in an unforgettable beachfront lagoon setting with stunning Pacific Ocean views and an aquarium bar top. Enjoy an island-inspired breakfast buffet in the morning and in the evening, indulge in fresh seafood, prime meats, innovative cocktails, and an artfully curated wine list while taking in the Maui sunset.

**Open Daily:**

Breakfast Buffet \| 7 am – 10 am

Bar \| 4 pm – 9:30 pm

Dinner \| 5 pm – 9 pm

_Dinner reservations recommended_

Explore HumuhumunukunukuāpuaʻaMake a Reservation

## Olivine

Our oceanfront culinary destination where the natural bounty of Maui is perfectly paired with the art of coastal Italian cuisine. Named after one of Maui’s primary minerals, Olivine is a celebration of earth’s renewal, featuring handmade pastas and seasonal dishes that infuse local Hawaiian ingredients with authentic Italian flavors.

**Open Daily:** Lunch \| 11 am – 3 pm

Intermezzo \| 3 – 5 pm

Dinner \| 5 – 9 pm

Explore OlivineMake a Reservation

## Botero Lounge

Enjoy specialty cocktails while enjoying live music by local artists amid nine larger-than-life sculptures by renowned late artist, Fernando Botero, the namesake of this stylish nightspot at the heart of our open-air lobby.

**Open Daily:**

Beverages \| 4 pm – 11 pm

Dining \| 4 pm – 10 pm

Live Music \| 6 pm – 9 pm

Explore Botero Lounge

## The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea

Immerse yourself in Hawaiian culture with an evening of interactive entertainment, hula lessons, and local dishes including poi, poke, and haupia. Enjoy lively performances overlooking Wailea Beach with Pacific views.

**Available on Select Dates**

Summer Hours \| 5:30 pm – 8:30 pm

## Grotto Bar

Explore our exclusive Grotto Bar, nestled among cascading waterfalls and intricate poolside caves. Swim up to the bar for light bites and cocktails, which you can enjoy surrounded by the cool, shadowed grotto.

**Open Daily: 10:30 am – 5 pm**

## Wailea Surf Haus

Make the most of every beach day by cooling off with a tropical cocktail and a light lunch at Wailea Surf Haus, our refreshing beach bar just moments from Wailea’s iconic shoreline.

**Open Daily: 11 am – 5 pm**
