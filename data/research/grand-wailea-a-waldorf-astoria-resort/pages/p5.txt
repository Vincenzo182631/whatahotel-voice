# Experiences

Experiences

Discover the timeless culture and tradition of our island home with experiences for you and the whole family. Join us for a spectacular lūʻau with ocean views and thrilling outrigger canoe tours along the stunning coast of Maui.

## Grand Lūʻau

Immerse yourself in Hawaiian culture with an evening of interactive entertainment, hula lessons, and local dishes including poi, poke, and haupia. Enjoy lively performances from the comfort of your own private table overlooking Wailea Beach.

**Hours:**

5:15 pm – 8 pm, available on select dates.

Make a Reservation

## Kilohana Cultural Resource Center

Discover Hawaiian history, culture, and traditions through our resort’s unique collection of experiences and activities. Learn to dance the hula and play the ukulele, explore Hawaiian craft techniques, and tour the resort with the help of our Cultural Ambassador.

## SCUBA

Discover DIVE Wailea, our SCUBA partner offering complimentary lessons to first-time divers (12 years and older) in Hawaiʻi’s only resort-designated SCUBA training pool. Novice and certified divers can join DIVE Wailea’s team of professional instructors for one of four exciting ocean dives.

Experience SCUBA

## Paddleboarding

Embark from Wailea Beach on an unforgettable journey across the tranquil waters of the Pacific Ocean on a stand-up paddleboard, available to rent from our on-resort partner Aqualani.

Rent a Paddleboard

## Outrigger Canoe Tours

Join us for an authentic Maui experience aboard a traditional outrigger canoe on a guided paddling journey down the coast of Wailea—Hawaiian for the “waters of lea,” the goddess of canoe builders. Take an unforgettable journey to discover the wonder of Wailea with each dip of your paddle in these storied, sparkling waters.
