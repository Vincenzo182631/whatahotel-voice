# Things to Do

Things to Do

Explore relaxing experiences and thrilling adventures for the whole family at our resort. Start the day with a stretch on Wailea Beach before lounging under your own private cabana or sliding into any of our refreshing pools.

## Experiences

Make the most of your stay in paradise with thrilling adventures as you paddle down the coast of Maui and immerse yourself in local culture.

Discover Experiences

## Pools

Pools

Cool off in our sparkling pools as you enjoy signature cocktails and savory lite bites. Take the whole family to Wailea Canyon Activity Pool for endless adventure or lounge at the tranquil Hibiscus Serenity Pool.

## Hibiscus Serenity Pool

Guests seeking a peaceful retreat can relax at our tranquil 4,850 sq. ft. Hibiscus Serenity Pool, featuring two hot tubs and private, luxurious cabanas and poolside casabellas. Named for our state flower, the Hibiscus Serenity Pool is represented by a giant hibiscus flower made from over 630,000 pieces of Mexican glass mosaic tiles inlaid at the bottom of the pool.

## Wailea Canyon Activity Pool

Plan the perfect family adventure at Wailea Canyon Activity Pool featuring slides, swings, and a sandy beach. Explore waterfalls and warm whirlpools before grabbing lite bites at the swim-up bar.

## Lava Tube Slide

Splash into a refreshing pool from the top of our Lava Tube Slide. Enjoy a 262 feet of slide and a 360° turn along an exhilarating three-story drop to the bottom.

## Tarzan Swing

Tap into your sense of adventure as you fly through the air over a crystal blue pool from a giant Tarzan-style rope swing at Wailea Canyon.

## Baby Beach

Kids can make a splash at Baby Beach in our child-friendly, zero-entry pool with an easy, gradual slope.
