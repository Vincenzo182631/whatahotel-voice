## Explore Grand Wailea

Explore Grand Wailea

Discover a retreat shaped by nature and culture at Grand Wailea. Fronted by Wailea Beach and surrounded by 40 acres of lush tropical landscape, our newly enhanced resort features redesigned accommodations, award-winning cuisine, Hawaiʻi’s largest private art collection, healing spa treatments and more.

## Rooms & Suites

Experience our enhanced guest rooms and suites, just steps from resort activities, restaurants, and shops. Our luxurious accommodations combine Hawaiian elements and motifs, modern interiors, and elegant finishings to create an authentic sense of place. Featuring generous layouts and private lānais, our guest rooms and suites start at 640 sq. ft. and offer lush tropical or ocean views. For additional space, upgrade to a suite, which includes a separate living area and plush bedroom.

Explore Rooms

## Unforgettable Dining Experiences

Savor the flavors of our island home through a range of imaginative culinary experiences, including unique dishes that infuse Japanese and Peruvian flavors by Chef Nobu Matsuhisa at Nobu Grand Wailea, stunning Pacific views at our award-winning seafood and steakhouse Humuhumunukunukuāpuaʻa, authentic Italian dishes infused with local Hawaiian ingredients at Olivine, American comfort foods and live music at Botero Lounge, and gourmet made-to-order and grab-and-go options at Loulu.

Discover Dining

## Things to Do

Start the morning with an energizing yoga session on Wailea Beach, or ground yourself in the energy of nature at Kilolani Spa. Lounge, soak, and enjoy at our serene Hibiscus Serenity Pool or, take the family and explore our Wailea Canyon Activity Pool complex of interconnected pools, slides, Tarzan swing, swim-up bar, and more.

Explore Things to Do

## Kilolani Spa at Grand Wailea

Honoring the Kilolani, the stargazers of ancient Hawaiʻi who looked to the skies to navigate their course, Kilolani Spa, included in _Vogue_‘s 2025 Global Spa Guide, reflects the tranquility, peace, and stillness of the sky in tune with the natural currents of the world. We invite you to tap into the timeless wisdom of Hawaiian culture, the grounding energy of nature, and the expansiveness of the celestial realm to chart your own course towards well-being.

Visit the Spa

## Maui Experiences

Immerse yourself in culture with one of our special, educational, shopping or dining experiences. Feast on the sights, sounds and flavors at our Grand Lūʻau or spend the day touring Wailea’s coast aboard an outrigger canoe or diving deep with our resort’s scuba program.

Discover Experiences
