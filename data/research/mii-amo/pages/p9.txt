Mii Amo
A desert escape for everything from facials to celestial manifesting.

Reviewed by

Why book?
Consider it the mothership of all spas. Surrounded by rusty red mountains and rock formations, the renowned and recently revamped terra-cotta-hued sanctuary is the ultimate spa destination for inner transformation and self-care.
Set the scene
Javelina and Mule deer roam freely across expansive lawns peppered with oak trees, while the oasis-like retreat blends seamlessly into Boynton Canyon adjacent to infamous Kachina Woman Rock and more than 300 miles of hiking trails. City slickers and business execs seeking time-outs from flooded email inboxes and lengthy office commutes check in for a reset, while mother-daughter duos and longtime girlfriends come to commiserate and celebrate birthdays and babymoons. Solo travelers and seasoned spa-goers book for a reboot amid life and job transitions. Running shoes,
Vuori workout gear
, and Patagonia puffers are de rigueur, along with the spa's signature white terry cloth robes that can be worn in lieu of clothing at any time or place.
The backstory
Conceived with 16 rooms in 2001,
Mii Amo
(a Native American term meaning “one's path or journey forward”), has just reopened after a two-year closure. Now, following a $40 million complete overhaul and expansion, the redesigned refuge owned by the Enchantment Group—and designed by co-owner and original architect Dana Tang of
Gluckman Tang
—features 23 rooms and suites; a two-story movement and fitness studio; a new signature restaurant and culinary garden; a refreshed outdoor swimming pool and deck; a sunken living room space with built-in sofas; a designated consultation wing; and a juice bar with smoothies, pressed juices, and wellness tonics. There's also a 3,700-square-foot separate space for Journey guests with a relaxation lounge, a plunge pool and garden, and a library-like lounge with a fireplace. The all-inclusive Journeys span three-, four-, seven-, and 10-night stays with taxes, gratuities, and fees; three meals daily; a welcome reception; and more. Arizona residents can take advantage of a special two-night option.
The rooms
Southwest modern meets minimalist chic. Accommodations include 17 Casita Kings and Double Queens (six double queens and 11 kings); two Junior Suites featuring king beds and a separate living room area; and four suites with one- and two-bedroom layouts ideal for traveling friends seeking separate sleeping spaces. I stayed in a Casita King room appointed with a spacious bathroom with a soaking tub, heated floor, and a Dyson hairdryer; bed topped with crisp, white linens and a Pendleton-style throw blanket; a pair of persimmon-hued armchairs; rope wall hangings; a stucco kiva-style gas fireplace; a cozy window reading nook; a writing desk; and an outdoor patio with two lounge chairs.
Food and drink
The antiquated notion of “spa food” doesn't exist here. In addition to in-room and poolside dining, the property has two main options: Hummingbird, the on-site eatery helmed by Executive Chef Beau Widener (of Gateway Canyons Resort and Spa and Woodstock Inn & Resort), and a juice bar with made-to-order smoothies. Breakfast consists of hearty burritos, overnight oats, and made-to-order egg white omelets, while lunch and dinner span fully-customizable offerings from roasted carrot hummus to Ora king salmon and Black Angus beef tenderloin. Wine and cocktails aren't off-limits either. For dessert post-nighttime spa treatment, order the bourbon pecan pie and have it delivered to the room. Don't miss the Chef's Garden Tour (offered Tuesdays and Fridays; advanced reservations required) replete with a garden overview, a family-style lunch showcasing veggies and herbs plucked directly from planter boxes, and a cooking demonstration by Chef Beau.
The spa
Whether it's an old-fashioned sports massage, a chakra balancing session for centering and clarity, or a sleep therapy session with an expert, there's literally something for everyone. The staff, therapists, and practitioners are all akin to spiritual wizards and healers. For my three-night journey, I custom-designed my itinerary with the help of a Journey concierge for a compilation of mind, body, and soul offerings. I opted for an astrology reading with Kymberley Maajii Griffin, a former biology teacher. During the session, she presented a meticulous account of my zodiac sign—pre-prepared based on my birthdate and location using special computer software—detailing my life phases, rising moons, and personality characteristics. Next, I ventured on a guided forest meditation session, or Spirit of Nature, with Swiss-born intuitive, Ashiko Rupp, and ventured to various pondering points to connect with nature including sitting up against a massive oak tree. I completed my spa experience with a Fall Gratitude massage, a full body massage-meets-reflection ritual commencing with a magnesium foot scrub and a clay application followed by a brief gratitude practice consisting of contemplating my proudest life achievements. At the end, I was gifted a pen, index card, and small crystal to continue the exercise at home.
The neighborhood/area
It's a secluded hideaway set within the Boynton Canyon vortex, known for spiritual and healing energy, and away from the hustle and bustle of downtown Sedona. The vibe is serene, calm, and quiet. There are no rowdy groups here, and with an age requirement of 16, there are no children allowed.
The service
Anything is at your beck and call per the concierge, from airport transfers and off-property
Pink Adventure Tours
of Sedona to dinner reservations and tennis lessons with a pro at sister property,
Enchantment Resort
. At
Trail House
at Enchantment, ask one of the experienced guides for recommendations for hikes based on length and skill level, or reserve a guided trail running session or mountain bike ride. Before dinner, be sure to remove the do-not-disturb door hanger from the door for a turn-down amenity spanning a nightly small gift (think a hummingbird glass ornament, a nod to the resort's symbol), or printed quote card placed on the bed. My favorite was an adage by Winston Churchill: “Courage is what it takes to stand up and speak; courage is also what it takes to sit down and listen.”
Eco effort
Join The Pipe
water refill stations are located throughout the resort, and plastic bottles for guest use have been swapped for reusable water bottles made of sugar cane. In the Chef's Garden, 90 percent of all food waste is composted, while ladybugs and marigolds are used as natural pest control.
Accessibility
Mii Amo is primarily one-story, and ADA-compliant featuring elevators and ramps.
Anything left to mention?
There are 80 weekly classes available including vortex lectures, gentle flow yoga, and mat Pilates. A favorite is the Labyrinth Walk, a walking meditation along a circular, stone-lined maze modeled after a Trojaborg labyrinth dating to the Bronze Age. Carve out time at least once during the stay for the morning ritual in the Crystal Grotto, a circular space with a massive skylight and a petrified stone fountain topped with a large crystal. Before entering, remove shoes and write a personal intention on a piece of paper and place it into a collection box. Lastly, don't leave without stopping by the Boutique. Expect to find a well-edited mix of design tomes, facial serums by
Babor
, ultra-soft pullovers by
Fundamental Coast
, candles by
Jo Malone
, and silk pillowcases by
Slip
.
