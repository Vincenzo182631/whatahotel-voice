## Superfood Additions

Açaí

High levels of antioxidants with an exceptional power fighting free radicals, fighting aging and longevity. Rich in minerals, vitamins and healthy fats.

Aloe Juice

Contains Vitamins, Minerals, Enzymes, Amino Acids, Polysaccharides and is useful for aiding in digestion.

Collagen

Vegan, plant based, boosting hair, nail, skin, joint, and gut health.

Chia Seeds

A nutrition powerhouse, high in fiber, antioxidants and well known for its healthy fats. Also high in calcium, iron and fiber.

Echinacea

Known for its immune boosting properties, may be helpful for colds, coughs and flu.

Flaxseed

One of the best sources of essential fatty acids, great for the brain, joints, acts as an antiinflammatory and heart health.

Maca

Helps balance stress and increases strength and stamina. Reduces anxiety, increases libido and helps immune system. High on minerals and may lower cholesterol.

Matcha

Full of antioxidants known to reduce cell damage and contains vitamins A, B, C, E and K. Beneficial for healthy skin cell turnover and collagen production. Contains caffeine for an energy boost.

PlantFusion Protein Powder

A plant-based, vegan protein powder that sustains energy, builds and repairs muscle, and is easy to digest. Includes peas, artichokes, algae and organic superfoods.

Spirulina

Contains great quantities of micronutrients, high in minerals, vitamin A, K, D and B. Known to remove toxins and heavy metals from the body.

Turmeric

Anti-Inflammation, Reduce Heartburn and Stomach Aches, Aides with Sleep
