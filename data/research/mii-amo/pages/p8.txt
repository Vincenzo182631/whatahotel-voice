Guests choose Mii amo for a wide range of reasons. For some, it is the perfect place to disconnect from the pressures of everyday life or simply to indulge in self-care. For others, it is an enclave for discovery, healing, inspiration, celebratory moments, or to foster deeper connections to oneself and others.

Establishing your intention is the first step in every Journey.

## Intentional Wellbeing

Co-creation is at the heart of the Mii amo experience.  Our compassionate team is dedicated to fostering personal connections and offering intuitive support to ensure that your Mii amo Journey is truly one-of-a-kind.

Your personal Journey Guide will work alongside you, intentionally creating a Journey tailored to your needs in the present moment. This partnership provides both the freedom to explore and the guidance to feel empowered as you uncover your own path.

Through authentic connections and mindful support, we co-create experiences that honor your unique needs, fostering growth, transformation, and deepening your Journey experience with care and intention.

Curious about what services your Journey Guide could recommend for your Journey? Explore the Journey Intentions below.

### Insight

This is an invitation to discover how your personal energy expresses itself. A meditation and energy reading lead to an in-depth exploration into duality and your innate intelligence that lives beyond it.

### Compassionate Communication

Going deep into heartfelt and authentic communication, this session is based on the work of Dr. Marshall Rosenberg and his belief that communication is the foundation for human connection.
