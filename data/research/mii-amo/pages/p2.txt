# Accommodations

-

Mii amo’s casitas feature private outdoor spaces with views of the raw beauty of the canyon. All accommodations include cozy indoor and/or outdoor fireplaces, plush beds with luxury linens, heated bathroom floors and Mii amo’s signature bath amenities. Our Luxury Suites feature separate living areas, a designated private massage room, an outdoor shower and a large private courtyard with fireplace and whirlpool.
