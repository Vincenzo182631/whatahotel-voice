## **THE POOL**

## The pool at Sunset Tower Hotel is tranquil and relaxed, with sweeping views of the city as a backdrop, a perfect place to sunbathe, lounge, have a drink, or enjoy a meal. Delicious market food is freshly grilled and served in a glamorous outdoor setting.

Reserved for registered hotel guests only.

Please note the pool at Sunset Tower has a strict “No Unaccompanied Minors” policy. Minors must always be supervised by responsible adults.

## **FITNESS CENTER**

## Sunset Tower Hotel features an expansive fitness facility open to hotel guests 24 hours a day.

**FITNESS CENTER FEATURES:**

· Three styles of treadmills, twin Peloton bikes, and an elliptical stair climber

· A wide selection of free weights, dumbbells, and advanced strength training equipment

· Pvolve low-impact fitness training platform

· Ample space for stretching and floor exercises

· Floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the neighborhood

* * *

## **PET AMENITIES**

## Sunset Tower is very pet friendly.

## We pamper guests’ pets with mini Sunset Tower beds, bowls, and treats. Adjoining the hotel is William S. Hart Park (bequeathed by the silent film actor), a haven for dogs to run around unleashed.

Please note there is a $100 non-refundable pet fee.
