## **ACCESSIBLE ROOMS**

Accessible Guest Rooms are available to book on our website. For more information and to best accommodate your request, you can

also email our Reservations Department by clicking here, or call 323.654.7100. You may also refer to our ADA Policy for further details by clicking here.

ACCESSIBLE ROOMS AT SUNSET TOWER HOTEL FEATURE:

• Thermostats and light switches at a lower unobstructed level

• Brail signage

• Closed Captioning available on televisions

• TTY device for telephone

• Flashing light and audible alert devices in room in the event of fire or other emergency

• Wheelchair compliant entryway and space in room (wheelchairs can roll freely around the room without interference from furniture).

• Bathtubs with bars and/or a roll in shower with shower seat

• Low eye hole viewer on entry doors

• Lower shelving, safe, minibar, closet bar and beds
