# Find Your Zen

Relish recuperative relaxation at the adults only Nobu Pool or be pampered with an immersive experience at our Six Senses Spa, rekindling your zest for life. Our daily complimentary classes , including yoga and Pilates, complement our world-class gym that features live DJs. What’s more, the resort’s tennis club, a favourite with stars of the game, is the place for a coaching session with a pro, lively mix-ins with other guests, or the chance to work up a sweat at well at the trendy outdoor gym. This is wellbeing, Nobu-style.

## Six Senses Spa

Six Senses Spa offers a holistic approach to wellness and a distinct Mediterranean healing experience in traditional treatment rooms, breezy canopied cabana rooms, or by the sea with stunning views. Explore rejuvenating treatments including massages, facials, body treatments, wellness therapies, and beauty services.

Learn More Contact Us

## Health & Fitness Training Facility

Discover a private health sanctuary featuring Technogym equipment and a private studio for spin, barre, and boxing classes. At our fitness facility, state-of-the-art design blends with natural elements—including a lush living wall—to create an uplifting environment for workouts and wellness.

Learn more

### Tennis Club

Nobu Hotel Marbella property provides the ultimate setting for tennis enthusiasts of all ages, offering professional coaching, advanced training for adults, and the chance to play friendly matches with other guests. The facilities boast 8 clay courts, 2 plexipave courts, and 4 Padel courts.

Learn more

### Nobu Pool

Spend carefree days under the Andalusian sun at Nobu Pool in the heart of our sophisticated resort. Order light fare from a special menu, featuring refreshing smoothies, cocktails, sushi rolls, seasonal fruit platters, and refined Nobu-style bites.
