# Dine Nobu Style in Marbella

An iconic menu of signature dishes and local specialties crafted by world-renowned Chef Nobu Matsuhisa. A design-forward atmosphere nestled steps from the beach on a picturesque coastline. Thoughtful, attentive service infused with Omotenashi, the Japanese spirit of hospitality. Dining is at the heart of the Nobu Hotel Marbella experience.

Book Nobu Restaurant Contact Us

## Nobu Marbella

Nobu Marbella is defined by the inspired flavors and innovative dishes mastered by Chef Nobu Matsuhisa. Enjoy signature dishes, including Black Cod Miso and Yellowtail Sashimi, and exclusive offerings that capture the essence of the Mediterranean, at the intimate sushi bar, in the minimalist elegance of the dining room, or on the Nobu Terrace overlooking La Plaza.

Learn more View Menu Book a table

## Nobu Marbella Beachside

Now open for lunch and dinner, Nobu Marbella Beachside invites guests to experience signature Nobu cuisine in a relaxed, beachfront setting. Overlooking the Mediterranean, the menu features iconic dishes, handcrafted sushi, and shareable plates, complemented by refreshing cocktails and uninterrupted sea views.

Learn more

## Nobu Bar

Inspired by the blooming Sakura flower, Nobu Bar offers signature Nobu dishes paired with a Japanese-inspired botanical cocktail menu. Its nature-inspired interior offers the perfect prelude to an exceptional dining experience or the conclusion to a night well spent in the buzz of La Plaza.

Learn more View Menus

## Nobu Pool

Refreshing fruit-infused sakes, refreshing smoothies, hand-crafted cocktails, and an exciting poolside menu await at Nobu Pool, the sophisticated, adults-only retreat that’s exclusively for our guests.

Lounge, swim, and relax to DJ tunes, and indulge with Nobu pool favourites like Yellowtail Quinoa with agave and mango dressing, sushi, sashimi, club sandwiches, and tempting vegetarian dishes, before a your dazzling evening of exceptional Nobu dining and partying at La Plaza.

View Menus

## In Room Dining

Order world-renowned cuisine, cocktails, and beyond in the comfort and privacy of your own space, delivered with the genuine, attentive service that defines the Nobu restaurant experience. Available 24 hours a day, every day.
