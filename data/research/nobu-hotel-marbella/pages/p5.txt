# Things to do in Marbella

Nobu Hotel Marbella is a destination for exceptional entertainment, iconic cuisine, and genuine indulgence. Located on the exclusive Golden Mile in Southern Spain, our hotel is steps from the sandy beaches and turquoise waters of the Mediterranean. Here, sunny afternoons embody laid-back luxury—but evenings are another story.

## Your Guide to The Golden Mile

Nobu Hotel is nestled on Marbella’s Golden Mile, a glamorous stretch of coastline dotted with golden beaches, tropical gardens, luxurious shopping and dining, beach bars, and one of the most stunning seafront promenades on the Costa del Sol. Guests will also enjoy access to La Plaza, the city’s fine dining and nightlife scene set along the Mediterranean Sea.

## The Art of Andalusian Living

Marbella offers a wealth of wonder beyond its beautiful beaches. Nearby Old Town, or Casco Antiguo, offers a taste of traditional Andalusian life in the heart of modern Marbella with its narrow cobblestone streets, white-washed buildings, and lively sidewalks cafés. Visit the cosmopolitan Puerto Banús Marina, one of the most luxurious marinas in the world, where luxury yachts bob in the water, framed by designer stores and upscale seaside restaurants.

## Frequently Asked Questions

- Is Nobu Hotel Marbella pet-friendly?

Yes, we warmly welcome pets. For more information on our pet-friendly policies, please reach out to us.

- Is there a spa at Nobu Hotel Marbella?

Yes. Discover the serene luxury of Six Senses Spa, offering everything from rejuvenating massages to bespoke wellness treatments, all designed to harmonize body and spirit.

- What times are check-in and check-out at Nobu Hotel Marbella?

Check-in is at 2:00 pm and check-out is flexible, tailored to your schedule for seamless transitions. Contactless check-in and check-out are available.

- Is there a pool at Nobu Hotel Marbella?

Absolutely. Our sparkling Nobu Pool is an adults-only oasis in the heart of our hotel.

A picturesque walkway along the coast, perfect for strolls with stunning sea views and eateries.

A historic square in the heart of Old Town, surrounded by orange trees and quaint cafés.

A landmark church in Old Town, known for its impressive architecture and bell tower.

An early Christian church ruins set in a peaceful garden, showcasing historical artifacts.

Renowned for its beautiful beaches, top-quality restaurants, and the famous Nikki Beach Club.

A historic fortress offering cultural events, concerts, and breathtaking views of the coastline.

A majestic mountain range providing hiking, biking trails, and stunning vistas over Marbella.
