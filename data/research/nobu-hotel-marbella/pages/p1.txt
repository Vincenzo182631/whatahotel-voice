# Iconic Nobu Style on Marbella’s Golden Mile

Uncover a refined level of understated luxury at our beachfront retreat, designed for entertainment, world-renowned cuisine, and indulgence. At Nobu Hotel Marbella, Costa del Sol sunshine and Andalusian charm meet Nobu’s signature Japanese aesthetic and iconic cuisine along Marbella’s glamorous Golden Mile.

An iconic menu of signature dishes and local specialties crafted by world-renowned Chef Nobu Matsuhisa. A design-forward atmosphere nestled steps from the beach on a picturesque coastline. Thoughtful, attentive service infused with Omotenashi, the Japanese spirit of hospitality. Dining is at the heart of the Nobu Hotel Marbella experience.

Dine With Us

Paradise for the senses. Nobu Hotel Marbella embodies the Japanese philosophy of Shiawase—a celebration of happiness and well-being—to infuse moments of holistic wellness, fitness, and relaxation into every detail of your time with us. Discover a seaside wellness oasis at Six Senses Spa, a sparkling seaside pool, a state-of-the-art training facility, and a prestigious tennis club.
