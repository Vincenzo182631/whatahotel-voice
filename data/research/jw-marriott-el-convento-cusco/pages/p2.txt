## Accommodations

Experience refined luxury in the heart of Cusco, Peru. Our spacious, newly renovated hotel rooms blend elegant contemporary comfort with the rich cultural heritage of Peru, featuring sophisticated décor inspired by the region’s history and artisanal traditions. Designed for both business and leisure travelers, each room offers thoughtful amenities and a tranquil atmosphere. ### Deluxe Rooms

Our renovated guest rooms offer plush accommodations, high-speed Wi-Fi and a supplementary oxygen-enriched system to relieve altitude sickness. In addition to thoughtful amenities, these rooms offer spectacular views of the cloister of the San Agustin Convent from the 16th century.
