### Historic Grandeur

Housed in a restored 16th-century convent in the heart of Cusco's historic center, our luxury hotel features two exhibition halls with ancient Peruvian artifacts.

## Exhibition Hall

Recognizing the historical significance of the Killke remains discovered on the property, the artifacts were carefully recovered and preserved within the hotel. Beyond revealing the earliest origins of Cusco, these findings helped substantiate important theories about the city's development. They demonstrated that, when the Incas established Cusco as their capital in the 12th century, they settled among existing cultures rather than in an uninhabited region, subsequently assimilating and building upon these earlier societies.

## Front Desk

Representing Inti, the revered Inca Sun God, this remarkable artwork welcomes guests in our historic hotel in Cusco's first entrance and reception area. Specially commissioned for the space, the spectacular Swarovski light installation, crafted in Austria, takes the form of a radiant sun measuring 3.20 meters in diameter and composed of more than 76,500 crystals.Inspired by one of the most powerful symbols of Inca culture, it pays tribute to Inti's central role in the Andean worldview, where the sun was revered as a source of life, prosperity, and divine power, a legacy that continues to inspire to this day.

Old Augustinian Convent located in one of the thousand-year-old cities such as Cusco, where history is evident in every corner. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott El Convento Cusco?

The check-in time at JW Marriott El Convento Cusco is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 10:00 am.

Does JW Marriott El Convento Cusco allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott El Convento Cusco.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott El Convento Cusco?

JW Marriott El Convento Cusco does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott El Convento Cusco have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott El Convento Cusco has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott El Convento Cusco booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott El Convento Cusco?

The closest airport near JW Marriott El Convento Cusco is Alejandro Velasco Astete International Airport (CUZ). CUZ is located approximately 3.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott El Convento Cusco have an airport shuttle to Alejandro Velasco Astete International Airport (CUZ)?

No, JW Marriott El Convento Cusco does not have an airport shuttle to Alejandro Velasco Astete International Airport.

Does JW Marriott El Convento Cusco have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott El Convento Cusco does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
