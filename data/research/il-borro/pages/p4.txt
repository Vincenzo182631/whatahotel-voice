#### The art of cooking

Borro has always been a popular destination for lovers of Tuscan culinary excellence, expressed by the cuisine of Osteria del Borro and our Executive Chef Andrea Campani. Our cooking classes, designed for adults and children, allow you to get in touch with the essence of Tuscan culinary culture made up of ingredients, recipes, scents and flavors that reflect the territory and its ancient customs, each time selecting the best local products for refined and seasonal recipes.

## Walks   on horseback

#### Among tuscan vineyards and hills

Il Borro offers lessons for those who love riding horses or for those who want to approach the world of horseback riding for the first time. Our horses are suitable for everyone, experienced or beginner, organizing lessons tailored to your level.

## Sports and nature

#### Experience sports in nature

For those who wish to spend a stay in the pursuit of outdoor sports, the estate has plenty of trails to explore, routes to bike, tennis, soccer and golf courses. And among the new offerings is a facility dedicated to calisthenics, the art of free-body training.

## The Artisans   de Il Borro

#### Tuscan art and tradition

The workshops that enliven the ancient medieval village open their doors to guests who wish to see local artisans at work: jewelry, textiles, clothing, shoes and painting. Craft workshops allow guests to actively participate in the creation of small handmade productions and teach age-old crafts.

## Itineraries and excursions

## Truffle hunting

A ‘unique experience in search of truffles, precious and noble fruit of our generous land. Available, guided experiences in Valdarno, Casentino, Cortona, Montepulciano and Tuoro with tasting and lunch.

## Tuscan city tours

Guided tours dedicated to the discovery of major Tuscan cities: Florence, San Gimignano, Volterra, Siena and the entire Chianti area.
