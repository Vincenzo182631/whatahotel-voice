# Welcome to a place like no other

#### The charm of a timeless place

In the heart of Tuscany lies Il Borro, a thousand-year-old hamlet surrounded by 1,100 organic acres. Restored to its authentic splendor through a meticulous restoration begun in 1993, it is now a guardian of tradition, culture and sustainability, and a spokesman for the most elegant Tuscan hospitality.

#### Wine, an expression   of the territory

The unique pedoclimatic conditions, mild winters, and excellent exposure of the vineyards have allowed over time to produce wines of great quality. In 1995, after a careful study of the terroir, new vines were planted and existing ones cared for according to the principles of organic farming. Since 2015 Il Borro has become an entirely organic farm and promoter of sustainability. Today the winery produces 12 fine labels, 10 of which are organic.

### Farm   organic

#### Biodiversity and sustainability

Dedication to hospitality perfectly complements the life of a state-of-the-art farm committed every day to giving back to the Earth what man consumes, in full sustainable philosophy. 1,100 hectares under organic management, including 85 hectares devoted to viticulture, 40 hectares of olive groves for the production of extra virgin olive oil, 3 hectares of seasonal vegetables, 272 hectares devoted to forage and arable crops, and the remaining land devoted to forestry.
