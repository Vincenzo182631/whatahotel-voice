# OLIVE HARVEST SEASON

### OLIVE HARVEST SEASON

Each year, with the arrival of autumn, Il Borro is filled with a new fragrance, that of freshly picked olives. It is the **time of pressing**, an ancient ritual that for centuries has marked the rhythm of the land and its community, a unique moment to celebrate nature and its fruits.

At Il Borro, this tradition becomes an experience to be savoured. **From October 27 to November 7**, immerse yourself in the most authentic side of Tuscany through olive harvesting, new olive oil tastings, artisan workshops and olive oil-based spa treatments. Breathe in the scents of the olive groves, admire the golden hues of autumn and embrace the magic of this enchanting season.

## OLIVE HARVEST

Experience the heart of Tuscan tradition by taking part in the **olive harvest** among the estate’s olive groves. Discover the secrets of olive oil production, breathe in the aroma of freshly pressed olive oil, and enjoy a **guided tasting of the new season’s extra virgin olive oil.**

A complete sensory experience that brings together craftsmanship, tradition and flavour, inviting you to immerse yourself in the most authentic side of Tuscany and take home the flavours of autumn.

book the experience

## OLIVE OIL MASSAGE

Let yourself be enveloped by the aromas of extra virgin olive oil, a precious elixir of longevity, with a **unique and revitalising massage.**

Each treatment is a personalised ritual. Entrust yourself to the expert hands of our therapists and let yourself be transported by the fragrances and essences of Tuscany, rediscovering the harmony between body and mind in an atmosphere of authentic wellbeing and rejuvenation.

book the experience

## The Art of Olive Oil: From Culinary Tradition to Table

At **Il Borro’s restaurants**, the new season’s olive oil enriches the dining experience with its intense aromas, enhancing the authentic flavours of Tuscan tradition.

Taking centre stage in a **dedicated cooking class**, Il Borro’s olive oil reveals its distinctive qualities and versatility through the preparation of seasonal recipes.

At the Borro Bar, olive oil becomes the signature ingredient of **Olive Lady**, an innovative cocktail that combines Il Borro Gin, infused with our estate-produced olive oil, with Cointreau and freshly squeezed lemon juice.

book the experience

## Artisan Workshops

In the heart of the medieval village, local artisans open the doors of their workshops, inviting you to experience authentic Tuscan traditions through hands-on activities inspired by the new season’s olive oil. Guided by skilled artisans, discover time-honoured techniques and create unique pieces that capture the essence of this land.

A timeless experience, leaving you with a treasured keepsake to take home.
