## Il Borro Relais & Châteaux

Il Borro is part of the Relais & Châteaux circuit and offers different types of accommodations and residences to offer its guests a unique experience in the authentic Tuscan atmosphere,in a true oasis of peace where time seems to stand still. A Il Borro every guest will be able to choose from different solutions: the exclusive Dimora Storica, Villa Casetta, the 38 elegant and refined suites within the medieval village and the 20 new suites “Aie del Borro”.

## I Borrigiani

Il Borro also offers agritourism formula solutions designed for medium- and long-term stays, 1.5km from the main facility and services. Since there is no shuttle service, we recommend being self-drive. Thanks to a skilful restoration work, respecting the classic Tuscan style, I Borrigiani composed of 3 farmhouses, represents a perfect combination of nature and exclusivity. By renting a farmhouse, ideal for families with children, you can feel at home in Tuscany.
