## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Chengdu located?

Fairmont Chengdu is located at No. 269 Tianfu Middle Avenue, Hi-Tech Zone, Chengdu, China.

What are some of the property amenities at Fairmont Chengdu?

Fairmont Chengdu offers 336 spacious and elegantly appointed guest rooms and suites, featuring exclusive Fairmont bathroom amenities. Four outlets to provide memorable dining experiences, including Spectrum all-day dining restaurant, Chang Yi Chinese restaurant, top floor MIKU restaurant, and multi-function spaces CUBE.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Chengdu?

The check-in time at Fairmont Chengdu is from 2pm and the check-out time is by 12pm (noon).

Does Fairmont Chengdu have a swimming pool?

Yes, the swimming pool at Fairmont Chengdu is on the 4th floor.

Does Fairmont Chengdu have a gym/fitness center?

Yes, the gym/fitness center is on the 4th floor.

How can I get to Fairmont Chengdu from the nearest airport?

You can take Metro Line 18 to Tianfu International Airport. You can also consult the concierge for information about airport transfer services.
