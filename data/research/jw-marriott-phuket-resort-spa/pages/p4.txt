Set within an elegant private beach canopy directly in front of JW Steakhouse Phuket, Whispers by the Sea pairs a candlelit ambiance with uninterrupted views and the gentle rhythm of the Andaman Sea. Begin with a glass of Champagne on arrival, followed by a bespoke five-course menu thoughtfully crafted by our culinary team.
