Reviewed by
Chelsea Bengier
Why book?
For a sprawling family-friendly resort along the Andaman Sea that features three pools, a turtle sanctuary, a kids club, nearly a dozen different dining areas, and unlimited on-site activities, from Muay Thai lessons and complimentary water sports to eye-popping fire shows and buzzy beachfront barbecues.
Set the scene
It’s easy to get lost on the 27-acre beachfront property, which has 3 pools and 11 restaurants and bars, all shared between guests at the 262-room JW Marriott resort and the adjacent Marriott Vacation Club. Blending Thai traditions with Western comforts, the vibe at The JW Marriott Phuket is casual and laid-back. During the daytime, young families lounge by the pool in breezy beach cover-ups and flip-flops. In the evening, dinner is a little dressier, with couples wearing colorful resort wear and linen Tommy Bahama shirts.
The backstory
The JW Marriott Phuket was a game-changer when it opened in 2002 because it was the brand’s very first hotel in Asia. Since then, it has undergone multiple renovations to maintain a modern, updated look. In December 2025, they finished a complete overhaul of the Andaman Grill, building a new bar and reimagining the design and menu to create a fine-dining atmosphere.
The rooms
Neutral island-inspired interiors can be seen across the 250 guest rooms and 11 suites. Think: teak furniture, marble bathrooms, and sunny terraces or balconies. Check the closet for a straw beach bag and sandals to use during your stay. Though most of the guest rooms face verdant gardens and ponds, if you want a water view, it’s worth upgrading to the oceanfront hot tub suites. (Hint: These suites are popular, so book far in advance.) For couples who prefer peace and quiet, consider the poolside access rooms on the secluded south side. The steps on your back terrace lead directly into a tranquil pool that’s shared among only 10 guest rooms. It looks like a jungle lagoon, with long vines hanging down and birds chirping above. The most luxurious option is the JW Royal Villa, a two-bedroom residence with its own beachfront entrance, full-size pool, and personal butler.
Food and drink
There are seven restaurants and four bars, spanning every kind of cuisine. Start your day off with the signature French toast from the JW Cafe, which serves breakfast until 11 a.m. Lunch is your pick of Japanese teppanyaki at Benihana, light bites and killer Thai mojitos at Rim Nam pool bar, or gourmet sandwiches, salads, and burgers at Siam Deli. Aspiring chefs should take a Thai cooking class at Ginja Taste restaurant. Chef Hong guides you around the on-site herb and vegetable gardens, teaching you each plant’s nutritious and medicinal uses. Then, you’ll don your aprons and whip up spicy papaya salad, wok-fried sea prawns, and panang chicken curry.
Dinner is definitely the highlight. On Wednesday and Saturday nights, there’s a lively beach barbecue and fire show at M Beach Club. Craving pasta? Reserve a window table to catch the sunset at Italian restaurant Cucina, where the seafood tagliatelle and truffle specials are standouts. Saving the best for last: The freshly refurbished Andaman Grill is an upscale adult-focused option (children eight and up are permitted). Many dishes on the tasting menu are cooked over an open fire, most notably the mouthwatering Wagyu tenderloin that is served with a salt box of nine flavors to choose from (mushroom, garlic, and Thai spice are favorites). Spoiler alert: At the end of the meal, the staff may surprise you with a vial of salt to take home.
The spa
The wellness center is deceiving because from the outside, it seems quite small. But there’s every machine imaginable within the full-stocked gym, and if you forgot your sneakers at home, they’ll lend you a new pair for your workout. The changing rooms even have a dry sauna, hot tub, and cold plunge pool. Tennis courts and a yoga/Muay Thai pavilion are hidden in the garden out front. The next-door spa has a beauty salon with massage chairs for mani-pedis and 16 treatment rooms. Outside suites are tucked away on a tree-canopied path, with giant metal doors that seem like they lead to Narnia. Behind them, you’ll find a room with cathedral ceilings, two massage beds, and dual outdoor showers. Inside suites are a splurge, featuring a deep-soaking tub, steam room, and a Thai-style outdoor pavilion that floats above a pond.
Spend a day getting pampered with the 3.5-hour spa retreat package. It includes a 30-minute nourishing milk bath or herbal steam, a half-hour aromatherapy mineral body scrub, a 90-minute massage, and an hour-long organic facial in one of the grand indoor suites.
The neighborhood/area
The JW Marriott fronts Mai Khao Beach, a calm strip of sand on Phuket’s northwest coast. Far from the rowdy backpacker bars in town, this uncrowded area is lined with refined resorts for those seeking some R&R. If you do want to venture out, there’s a shuttle that runs from the hotel to Phuket town, Central Floresta shopping mall, and Patong Beach. The airport is also a 15-minute drive, making for a smooth arrival and departure.
The service
Guests are always greeted with a smile, and the maintenance team is quick to fix any issues that may arise. Ask the concierge for boat tour recommendations and excursions to see nearby islands or to arrange private drivers for day trips. The most impressive is the landscaping crew, who meticulously maintain the gardens by hand so as not to disturb the guests. Save time to explore the property on a complimentary bike, sea kayak, and stand-up paddleboard. Or have the experienced water sports staff sail you around on a Hobie catamaran. Depending on the tide, the beach can be quite steep, but the team will carry the equipment for you and even swim you out beyond the shore when the waves are strong.
For families
The JW Marriott is ideal for those with tots in tow. Family terrace rooms have a low-slung Thai-style bed for little ones and are located on the ground floor, near the kid-friendly south pool. The resort’s main pool has a shallow play area with a water slide and fountains that are fun for the whole family. There are babysitting services and a kids club (open until 9 p.m., for children ages four and up) that’s packed with activities to entertain them while parents relax by the adults-only north pool or have a date night. Teens can enjoy the ping-pong table and giant chess board in the game room, which screens Friday movie nights.
Eco effort
Sea turtles lay their eggs on Mai Khao Beach, and their nesting grounds are protected by the Mai Khao Marine Turtle Foundation. The JW Marriott has an on-site turtle sanctuary and education center, in partnership with the conservation group. Guests can go at 11 a.m. to see the daily turtle feeding and learn more about the endangered animals.
Accessibility
Elevators, ramps, and two ADA-compliant guest rooms are in place, with a third accessible room in the works. The staff are happy to assist in making room arrangements and accommodating guests with mobility issues.
Anything left to mention?
The JW Journal, a newspaper bulletin of the resort’s weekly activities, is provided in each room. Make sure to read the fine print to find out what time the events run. Don’t miss the
White Lotus
-style sunset fire ritual in the reflection pool by reception on Mondays and Thursdays. Speaking of timing, all three pools open at 9 a.m., but guests often arrive at 7 a.m. (or earlier!) to throw a towel over a sunbed to secure their spot.
