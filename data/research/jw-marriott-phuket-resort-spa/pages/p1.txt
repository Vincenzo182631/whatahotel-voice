### Discover the Authentic Side of Phuket in Mai Khao

### Discover a Different Side of Phuket in Mai Khao

Unlike the vibrant nightlife and busy beaches of Patong, Mai Khao offers a quieter and more authentic side of Phuket. Located along Phuket's northwest coast, the destination is home to 17 kilometers of natural beachfront where guests can enjoy uninterrupted views of the Andaman Sea, peaceful sunrise walks, and a deeper connection to the island's natural beauty. Protected wetlands, coastal ecosystems, and proximity to Sirinath Marine National Park create a setting that feels worlds away from Phuket's more developed tourism districts. Just 15 minutes from Phuket International Airport, Mai Khao allows travelers to transition effortlessly from arrival to relaxation while experiencing one of the island's most distinctive coastal destinations.

### Experience Nature, Conservation, and Wellness in Mai Khao

Mai Khao is recognized as one of Phuket's most important conservation areas and is home to protected sea turtle nesting habitats that help preserve the island's marine ecosystem. Visitors can explore natural beaches, discover diverse coastal landscapes, and gain a greater appreciation for Phuket's environmental heritage. The destination's peaceful surroundings also make it ideal for wellness-focused travel. Mornings begin with walks along the shoreline, afternoons invite moments of restoration inspired by the rhythms of nature, and evenings unfold against the backdrop of spectacular Andaman Sea sunsets. Together, these experiences create a sense of balance that reflects Mai Khao's unique blend of well-being, sustainability, and connection to place.

### Experience the Best of Mai Khao at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa

Set within 11 hectares of tropical gardens, freshwater ponds, and preserved coastal wetlands, JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa offers an immersive gateway to Mai Khao's natural and cultural experiences. Located directly on Mai Khao Beach, the resort allows guests to experience the destination's tranquil character while enjoying thoughtful luxury inspired by its surroundings. Families can spend the day exploring the beach, relaxing by one of three swimming pools, and creating meaningful memories together in a setting designed for connection and discovery. Guests seeking wellness can restore mind, body, and spirit at THE SPA, while culinary journeys range from authentic Thai flavors at Ginja Taste to beachfront dining overlooking the Andaman Sea. Throughout the resort, locally inspired design details, including custom furnishings crafted with premium Jim Thompson Thai silk, further reflect the destination's rich sense of place. Together, these experiences make JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa one of the most rewarding ways to discover the authentic beauty of Mai Khao.
