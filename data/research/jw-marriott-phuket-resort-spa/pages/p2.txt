# Discover five-star hotel rooms in Phuket

## Accommodations

Unwind in luxury beachfront accommodation in Phuket, Thailand, including the exclusive JW Royal Villa. Each offers plush bedding, marble bathrooms, 24-hour room service, free Wi-Fi, and private balconies with views of tropical gardens or the Andaman Sea. Thoughtfully designed with subtle Thai elements, guest rooms feature premium Thai silk by Jim Thompson, woven into bespoke furnishings to reflect...See More the island’s cultural heritage through a refined, contemporary lens. Relax in premium rooms with pool access, the Jacuzzi Suite, or the Oceanfront Suite with a private pool for ultimate luxury. For the pinnacle of luxury, experience the JW Royal Villa, offering unparalleled space and privacy. Every stay is a tranquil retreat, ensuring your ultimate comfort.See Less

Grande Premium Pool Access

### Indulge in Direct Poolside Serenity

Settle into a haven of calm in our Grande Premium Pool Access Room, where your private terrace opens directly onto the resort’s tranquil, lagoon-style pool. With 63 sqm of warm, Thai-inspired design, this refined family room in Phuket, Thailand, invites you to pause and reconnect — whether you're enjoying quiet moments on a sun lounger or easing into the water just beyond your terrace. Every detail is crafted to restore your sense of well-being, ideal for unwinding after a day of discovery in Phuket.

## Deluxe Garden Sea View

Immerse yourself in tranquility in our Deluxe Garden Sea View Room. Enjoy partial sea views and lush garden scenery through floor-to-ceiling windows, or step onto your private balcony to fully embrace the natural surroundings. The cozy Sala area invites relaxation, offering a serene spot to unwind. This thoughtfully designed retreat blends comfort with the beauty of the outdoors.

## The Luxury Jacuzzi Suite

In the Luxury Jacuzzi Suite, relish the fresh sea air alongside heightened levels of comfort and privacy. Enjoy a hot cup of coffee and a hearty breakfast on your seafront balcony, before unwinding in the luxurious comfort of your own whirlpool tub. Listen to the waves as you drift off to sleep.

## The Luxury Oceanfront Pool Suite

Nestled within the resort's landscaped gardens, our Luxury Oceanfront Pool Suite is furnished with an 18 square meter private plunge pool and a shaded sundeck. The suite's open-floor concept, which opens onto a terrace, is designed for an indoor-outdoor tropical lifestyle.

## Premium Accommodation

Enhance your stay with our exquisite selection of premium beachfront hotel rooms in Phuket, from the Grande Pool Access rooms to our luxurious ocean-view suites, private pool suites, and the exclusive JW Villa. Indulge in exceptional comfort, privacy, and breathtaking views, all tailored to your preferences and your family’s needs for an unforgettable experience.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa is the The JW Royal Villa with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa have fridges?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
