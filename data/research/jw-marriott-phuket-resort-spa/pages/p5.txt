Marine Turtle Foundation

### Mai Khao Turtle Foundation – Phuket

Along the pristine sands of Mai Khao Beach—one of Phuket’s last remaining turtle nesting grounds—our foundation is dedicated to protecting endangered sea turtles and preserving their fragile habitat. Meet Timmy, a spirited blind green turtle, and Jimmy, a resilient turtle with a neck deformity. Learn their inspiring stories, discover the turtles’ vital role in Phuket’s marine ecosystem, and see how your support helps their recovery. Visits are complimentary; donations are welcome.

Discover Good Travel With Marriott

A Phuket 3-Day Itinerary for Families

### Family Play by the Andaman

Ease into a luxury family resort in Phuket with Kids Club by JW, open daily from 9 am to 9 pm with Day and Night Camp, plus a game zone with giant chess and movie nights. Families can move between three swimming pools, including the main pool with a kids pool and water slide, before settling into golden-hour views by the Andaman Sea.

### JW Garden to Table

Start at JW Cafe with signature French toast during the breakfast buffet from 7 to 11 am, then join the daily JW Garden tour from 2 to 3 pm to discover ingredients such as Anchan and Thai chili. At Cooking School by JW, authentic Thai cooking classes run daily from 10 am to 12:30 pm or 3 to 5:30 pm, bringing family activities in Phuket to the table.

### Mai Khao Beach Discoveries

On Mai Khao Beach, families can visit the on-site Mai Khao Marine Turtle Foundation, typically from Tuesday to Saturday, 11 am to 3 pm, for meaningful conservation learning and one of the most memorable family activities in Phuket. End the day on the beach swing, where serene Andaman Sea sunsets invite everyone to slow down and stay in the moment.
