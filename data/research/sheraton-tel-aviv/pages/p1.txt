### Dining

Enjoy a fine meal while watching the sunset either at the Surfside Seaview Restaurant and Manara Restaurant or relax at the poolside Deck 115 and in Lobby Lounge Bar with a sea view. You can enjoy a meal between 06:30 AM and Midnight in the different locations.

### The Palm Restaurant

Enjoy a daily breakfast buffet and a traditional Shabbat dinner overlooking the Mediterranean. Fresh flavors, generous selections, and panoramic sea views create a relaxed yet refined dining experience from morning to evening.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv?

The check-in time at Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv. Self parking is available at Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv, with rates of $14.00 per hour, $34.00 per day, and $238.00 per week.

Off-site parking is also available near Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv?

The closest airport near Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv is Ben Gurion Airport (TLV). TLV is located approximately 25.7 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv have a Club lounge?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv has Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
