## Experiences

Only 3 km from the Hotel, you will spot the White City of Tel Aviv, which is a major historical monument and is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Tel Aviv will surprise you! The city is Israel's center of business, commerce, fashion, culture, architecture, entertainment, nightlife, dining and fun; it sparkles with vigor and vitality nearly 24 hours a day, justifying its title as "the city that never sleeps." Our Tel Aviv hotel is located directly on the beach, with the beautiful Mediterranean Sea only a few steps from your room. Also, the hotel is located within walking distance of the main attractions and the city center. Enjoy our hotel facilities after a long day of exploring Tel Aviv, from a swimming pool to spa treatments, we cater for the whole family.

The Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv boasts a beautifully renovated pool area that promises a luxurious and relaxing experience for guests. The pool area is well-maintained and offers stunning views of the surrounding area, making it a perfect spot for both relaxation and Instagram-worthy photos. In the pool you will find the Sheraton Grand Pool Restaurant & Bar which offers Mediterranean cuisine and refreshing cocktails.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio & strength equipment including machines and dumbbell, Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv have a pool on-site?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv have an on-site spa?

Yes, SHERATON GRAND SPA - Opening March 2027 is located on-site at Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv?

Marina & Sports Center, Old City of Jaffa and more are nearby Sheraton Grand Tel Aviv.
