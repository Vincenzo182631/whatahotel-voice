# ROOMS AND SUITES

Milanese Hotel of Refined Character. Set within a historic building in Milan’s Porta Venezia district, Casa Cipriani Milano is an intimate hotel offering 15 individually designed accommodations - twelve rooms and three suites. Each one reflects the understated glamour of Italian design, with bespoke furnishings, fine materials, and Cipriani’s signature sense of comfort. Whether you're here to explore the city or escape from it, our hotel offers a sophisticated retreat steeped in heritage and hospitality.﻿
