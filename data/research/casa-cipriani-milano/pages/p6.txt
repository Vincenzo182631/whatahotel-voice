# LOCATION

Casa Cipriani Milano sits beautifully in Milan’s Corso Venezia area. This five-star boutique hotel is ideally positioned within walking distance of key city attractions: the Teatro alla Scala, Milan’s Opera House, and the iconic Duomo, are a 15-minute walk. Additionally, it’s nestled in the Quadrilatero della Moda, Milano’s high end shopping district, merely a 10-minute walk from the luxury retailers in the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II and a 20-minute walk from the artistic Brera District. With such central positioning, Casa Cipriani Milano not only provides luxury and comfort but also grants access to the cultural and aesthetic richness that the city of Milan has to offer.
