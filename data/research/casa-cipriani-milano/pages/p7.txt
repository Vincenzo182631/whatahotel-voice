# THE CLUB

A timeless home in historic Milanese settings, in front of the Indro Montanelli Gardens. Old-World glamour for an eclectic community to relax, dine and entertain in style.

* * *

* * *

### OPEN DAILY, 7AM–MIDNIGHT

THE PICKERING ROOM

The Pickering Room, a tribute to the history and the tradition of Cipriani, offers an elegant setting to savor breakfast, lunch, aperitifs, and dinner. The menu, including Italian classics, provides a versatile selection of salads, Venetian sandwiches, pizzas, and beloved Cipriani dishes. Accentuated by a “winter garden,” an inviting extension of the room itself, The Pickering Room ensures a convivial and welcoming atmosphere at all times.

### OPEN DAILY, 12PM–11:30PM

THE RESTAURANT

The Restaurant is a blend of tradition and modern elegance. With an interior adorned in wood and marble, it’s a bright and inviting space that extends to a generous terrace. Open daily for lunch and dinner, the menu showcases Cipriani specials including the iconic Carpaccio and the original Bellini, both invented by founder Giuseppe Cipriani.

### SUN–TUE 11AM–MIDNIGHT / WED–SAT 11AM–2AM

THE LIVING ROOM

The Living Room, situated on the top floor of the building, features a private and intimate setting, ideal for those seeking a place to unwind or entertain guests. Open both day and night it offers a light menu perfect for mid-day refreshments or after-dinner indulgences. From Tuesday to Saturday, the Living Room hosts live entertainment with DJ sets, becoming the perfect space for cocktails and music.

### SPA AND WELLNESS

## WELLNESS

The Spa is a peaceful retreat, an intimate and private environment designed for relaxation and quiet indulgence. A team of hand-picked therapists offer a range of signature massages, rituals and wellness experiences, from reflexology to stress recovery, alongside expert facials and body treatments in uniquely designed treatment rooms. Sauna facilities are available in both the men's and women's changing rooms, and a flotation pool completes the experience with a deep sense of weightless calm.
