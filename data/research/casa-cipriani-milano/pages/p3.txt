# WELLNESS

The Spa at Casa Cipriani Milano, on Corso Venezia and steps from Milan's fashion district, is a calm space dedicated to wellbeing, where advanced technology, luxurious beauty treatments and time-honoured practices meet in a balance of innovation and relaxation.

The Spa has six elegant treatment cabins, where skilled therapists provide relaxing massages, rejuvenating facials and rebalancing body treatments. Cabin treatments and the cryo chamber are available to external guests by appointment. Members and hotel guests have exclusive access to the wellness facilities, including the flotation pool, Finnish sauna and hammam, designed for deep relaxation.

Completing the offering, the private gym brings together state-of-the-art fitness technology, personalised training and reformer Pilates, reserved for members and hotel guests.

- ## AGE REQUIREMENT

Our spa facilities are exclusively accessible to guests aged 18 and above.

- ## ARRIVAL TIME

To ensure a seamless and relaxing experience, we kindly invite guests to arrive at least fifteen minutes before their scheduled appointment. This allows time to unwind before treatment. Please note that late arrivals may require a shortened treatment duration to accommodate subsequent appointments.

- ## CANCELLATION POLICY

Should you need to amend or cancel your reservation, we kindly request a minimum of 24 hours' notice. Changes received after this time will incur the full treatment fee.

- ## FACILITIES

Spa treatments are available to members, hotel guests and external visitors. Access to the wellness facilities, including the sauna, hammam, flotation pool and fitness center, is reserved exclusively for Casa Cipriani members and hotel guests. Members also benefit from preferred rates on Spa treatments, with the exception of Beauty Essentials.

- ## HEALTH AND WELL-BEING

For your comfort and safety, we kindly ask that you inform our team of any health / medical conditions, allergies, injuries or pregnancy prior to your treatment. This enables our therapists to personalise your experience with the utmost care.

- ## HOURS OF OPERATION

Spa 9AM- 9PM \| Fitness 7AM -10PM

Hotel guests have 24-hour access to the Fitness Center.

- ## SPA ETIQUETTE

To preserve the peaceful atmosphere of the Spa, we kindly ask guests to maintain quiet conversation and refrain from using mobile phones within the wellness areas. Smoking and consumption of alcohol are not permitted. Swimwear is required when using the facilities, and access to the wellness and fitness areas is restricted to guests aged 18 years and over.
