# CASA CIPRIANI MILANO

A true private members club in the modern sense, Casa Cipriani Milan brings together an eclectic community of those who enjoy life’s simple pleasures. From the first days of Harry’s Bar in Venice, there was always a crowd of people from all walks of life, merrily sipping Bellinis, chatting, and living the good life.

### THE CLUB \| DINING

The Restaurant is a blend of tradition and modern elegance. With an interior adorned i wood and marble, it’s a bright and inviting space that extends to a generous terrace. Open daily for lunch and dinner, the menu showcases the famed Cipriani specials including the iconic Carpaccio and the original Bellini, both invented by founder Giuseppe Cipriani.

### WELLNESS

The Casa Cipriani Spa is designed to be a peaceful retreat for playful relaxation and ultimate indulgence in the privacy of an intimate environment. The spa offers a range of signature massages and wellness experiences from a team of hand-picked therapists, including reflexology and stress recovery.
