# MEMBERSHIP

Casa Cipriani, a true private members club in the modern sense, brings together an eclectic community who shares life’s simple pleasures.

APPLY

* * *

* * *

- ## DRESS CODE

Our landmark building evokes the refined elegance of the Beaux-Arts period, and we ask that all members and guests respect the tradition of that era through appropriate attire. Members and guests who do not adhere to the below guidelines may be denied entry. This decision will be entirely at the discretion of our front desk team, and we ask that you ensure the dress code is conveyed to any guests you invite to the Club.

An overall elegant and polished appearance is expected at all times.

Permitted at all times:

o Clothing that is tailored, smart and well-presented in appearance.

o Jeans, in a solid color with no rips.

o Jackets are strongly suggested for gentlemen in the dining rooms.

o Smart, clean sneakers, as part of an overall smart attire.

o Elegant hats such as a fedora, beret, which should be removed in the dining rooms.

o Women's tailored shorts.

Not permitted at all times:

o Clothing that is overly casual, sporty or streetwear-inspired in appearance.

o Overly revealing attire.

o Ripped jeans or pants or shorts.

o Hooded sweatshirts.

o Athletic wear of any kind, including running shoes, after 12:00 PM.

o Baseball caps and beanies.

o Flip flops and casual sandals.

o Overly large visible logos.

Permitted until Noon:

o Athletic or gym wear.

o Men's shorts, as part of an overall smart attire.

Permitted until 6:00 PM:

o Men's short-sleeved shirts, including tee shirts and collared shirts. After 6:00PM, gentlemen are required to wear long sleeves in the Club. Blazers may be provided by our staff, if needed.

- ## PHOTOGRAPHY

Photography and videography on Club premises are strictly forbidden in all areas. Taking photos or posting on social media is also prohibited while on Club premises.

- ## GUEST POLICY

Club Members are allowed to bring up to three (3) guests with them at any one time to the Pickering Room, the Club Restaurant and the Living Room. Tables at the restaurants are guaranteed only to those with a confirmed reservation, which is always recommended to reduce any waiting time.

If a member does not arrive with their complete party the full name(s) of each person in their group must be given to the front desk team. This can be done onsite or prior to arrival by emailing theclub.milano@casacipriani.com.

HOTEL GUESTS

Guests staying in the Rooms & Suites at Casa Cipriani are allowed to bring up to four (4) guests per room, to the Pickering Room, the Club Restaurant and the Living Room, at any time during the period of their stay. This includes the occupants of the room.

Reservation is always recommended to reduce any waiting time.

Club Members and Hotel Guests may make arrangements with the Proprietor to bring additional guests.  This can be done by reservation prior to arrival by emailing theclub.milano@casacipriani.com.

Members must be present at the Club at the same time as their guest(s) and are not permitted to leave their guest(s) at the Club upon their departure

- ## AGE

All guests visiting the Club must be at least 18 years old, after 6pm.

- ## CHILDREN

Children are welcome at our restaurants daily until 6pm. After 6pm people under 18 years old are not permitted in any areas of the Club.

- ## PETS

Pets are not permitted at the Club Restaurant nor at the Living Room, unless medically required or with the explicit permission of the Proprietor. Small pets up to 10 kilos are welcome at the Pickering Room and Casa Cipriani Rooms & Suites. Members and/or their guests who do attend our premises with animals are always deemed to be personally liable for their conduct. In any case animals cannot stay on the Club’s furniture.

- ## LAPTOPS & HEADPHONES

Both laptops and headphones are permitted for use until 6pm daily in the club.

- ## BOOKING GUESTROOMS/SUITES FOR FRIENDS & FAMILY

The Casa Cipriani member is responsible for booking and paying for his friends and family. Members must provide a Credit Card authorization form completed to cover Room and Tax. The reservations team will book the rooms and suites in the name of the actual guest staying. There is no limit on the number of rooms/suites a member can reserve for his friends and family.
