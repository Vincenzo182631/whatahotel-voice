The iconic Majestic gazes out to sea on the Croisette in Cannes, offering up views of the Palais des Festivals’ famous steps. At almost one hundred years of age, this five-star temple to French elegance and refinement is known for its timeless charm and breathtaking views over the Mediterranea...n. The hotel’s rooms and suites unfold in an ode to pure peace and quiet, while the Beefbar Cannes and Ciro’s Cannes serve up delicious dishes that put the emphasis on refined cuisine. The private beach, heated outdoor pool and water sports activities provide the perfect setting for guests who like to get moving, while the Spa Le Majestic is brimming with bespoke holistic treatments to help you unwind, along with the hotel’s very own sauna, hammam and gym. Younger guests are given the VIP treatment at the Kid's Club, where fun, creative and educational activities give children aged 4 to 12 the opportunity to dive deep into the world of art and the silver screen.See more

### What are the expected arrival and departure times? What is the procedure to follow?

Arrival:
The check-in time is scheduled from 4:00 pm. Please go to the reception and provide your credit card and a photo ID. The credit card provided at check-in will not be charged at that time. We do our best to welcome you as soon as possible. If you arrive before 3:00 pm and your room is not ready yet, we will be happy to take care of your luggage.

Departure:
Check-out is at 11:00 am. If you wish to leave later, please contact the reception to make the request, subject to availability.

### Can I request a late check-out?

Yes, please contact the reception to make the request, subject to availability.

### What is the hotel's cancellation policy?

(1) Flexible Rate:

For any stay from May 11, 2026 to September 30, 2026 inclusive and May 10, 2027 to September 30, 2027:

1 night deposit is required at the time of booking confirmation. However, this offer can be canceled at no cost and modified (subject to availability of new dates and at the rate in effect for the new stay) up to 7 days before arrival (before 4:00 pm local time). In case of cancellation after this date, a penalty of 1 night will be applied. In case of no-show at the hotel or early departure, a penalty of 100% of the stay will be applied.

For any stay from October 1, 2026 to May 9, 2027 inclusive and October 1 to 31, 2027 inclusive:

The reservation must be guaranteed by credit card. This offer can be canceled and modified (subject to availability of new dates and at the rate in effect for the new stay) until the day before arrival (before 4:00 pm local time). In case of cancellation after this date, no-show at the hotel or early departure, a penalty of 100% of the stay will be applied.

(2) Best Available Rate:

The total prepayment of the stay is required at the time of booking confirmation. This offer cannot be canceled or modified free of charge. In case of cancellation, modification, no-show at the hotel or early departure, a penalty of 100% of the stay will be applied.

(2) Event Rate:

The total prepayment of the stay is required at the time of booking confirmation. This offer cannot be canceled or modified free of charge. In case of cancellation, modification, no-show at the hotel or early departure, a penalty of 100% of the stay will be applied.

### How to organize a transfer to or from Nice airport?

You can contact our concierge service before your arrival at the following address: majestic@cannesbarriere.com. Please provide your flight number, expected arrival and departure times, number of passengers, and amount of luggage.

### Does the hotel offer a valet service?

Yes, a valet service is available for guests of our 5-star hotel in Cannes. €75 from May to September and €55 per day for the rest of the year. Rate of €150 for oversized vehicles (height > 1.85m)

### Are pets allowed at the Majestic?

Pets are allowed in the hotel with a daily supplement of 70€. A security deposit of 250€ per stay

will be requested upon arrival at the Hotel to cover any damages caused by your pet.

We kindly ask you to always keep your dog on a leash in public areas and not to leave it unattended. Dogs are not allowed in restaurants, bars, spa, fitness center or cinema, except for assistance dogs (with certificate). For safety reasons, our team will not be able to clean your room if your dog is left unattended. Guests are responsible for any damage caused by their pet.

### Does the hotel provide baby cots or extra beds?

Yes, baby cots and extra beds are available upon request in eligible rooms (rooms accommodating 3 people or more) at 90€ per day (complimentary for children under 12 years old)

### Do you offer connecting or adjacent rooms?

Yes, please contact our booking team at majestic@cannesbarriere.com to find out about availability and options.

### What services are included in my stay? What services are available for an additional fee?

Included: Access to the sensory space of the Spa du Majestic, access to the Fitness 24/7, access to the outdoor heated pool at 27°C all year round, Wifi with a speed of 3MB (very high-speed package available at an additional cost), access to the Kid's Club for children aged 4 to 12 (lunch, evening, dinner or outing available at an additional cost), VAT and service.

With supplement: Tourist tax of 4.42€ per person per day to be paid upon departure, buffet breakfast at Fouquet's for 50€ per person (if not included in your offer), served from 7am to 10:30am, any additional service consumed on site.

To book an additional service, contact majestic@cannesbarriere.com.

### What dining options are available at the Majestic Cannes?

Our 5-star hotel in Cannes offers a variety of culinary experiences to satisfy all your desires, whether on the terrace, on the beach, or in the comfort of your room:

Beefbar Cannes: Treat yourself to a unique culinary experience by sampling exceptional meats and the finest seafood.

Ciro's Cannes: Discover the new identity of our private beach and restaurant, Ciro's Cannes, for a lunch with your feet in the sand or a romantic dinner with a view of the sea.

La Terrasse Piscine:Located by the pool, La Terrasse Piscine offers a sunny menu with gourmet snacks and tasty cocktails, perfect for a refreshing break.

The Majestic Bar:In a sumptuous setting with large armchairs and beams adorned with gold leaves, Bar Fouquet's is the must-visit meeting place on the Croisette.

Room Service: Enjoy exceptional room service, available to savor exceptional dishes without leaving the comfort of your room.

To find out the specific opening hours of each establishment and make a reservation, we invite you to consult the information available on our website or contact our concierge service.

### Is room service available 24 hours a day?

Our dishes are served 24/7.
Please note that our dishes are served with a 15% surcharge from 11:00 PM to 5:00 AM.

### What are the rules for accessing Ciro's Cannes private beach?

Access tothe private beach of Ciro's Cannes is exclusively reserved for hotel guests and restaurant patrons.

Open year-round (except during congresses, special events, and the hotel's annual closure).

In case of exceptional closure, alternative beaches

will be offered. Loungers rental available by reservation for an additional fee. As part of packages including the beach, mattresses must be reserved at the latest the day before. Loungers guaranteed until 12 p.m. on the scheduled day.

### Is Hotel Le Majestic Cannes suitable for organizing seminars and professional events?

Yes, the Hotel Le Majestic Cannes is an iconic venue for organizing your seminars and professional events on the French Riviera. Located a few steps from the Palais des Festivals and 25 minutes from Nice airport, the hotel offers:

14 modular meeting rooms, providing up to 400m² dedicated to events.

335 luxurious rooms and suites.

3 restaurants and 4 bars for your business meals and relaxation moments.

A private beach for exceptional events.

A Spa and an heated outdoor pool for the well-being of your participants.

Our team is here to help you organize custom group activities and team building, such as cruises to the Lérins Islands, tours of the historic center of Cannes, wine tastings at a vineyard, or workshops at the Fragonard perfumery in Grasse.
