# Rooftop Pool

## WET Deck

Five floors above the Atlantic, WET Deck is your all-day sun scene at W Fort Lauderdale—a bold rooftop escape for cocktails, bites, cabana lounging, and ocean-view revelry.

## Your Sun-Soaked Social Scene

Stretch out under the South Florida sun, claim your place above the beach, and settle into a scene made for long afternoons and golden-hour lingerings. From easy lounging to celebratory poolside moments, WET Deck brings together panoramic views, bold design, and the unmistakable energy of Fort Lauderdale.

### Poolside, Poured to Perfection

At WET Bar & Grill, the menu is part of the mood. Cool cocktails, flavorful bites, and laid-back poolside service keep the energy high from midday lounging to late-afternoon toasts. It’s the kind of place where one round turns into one more hour. Best enjoyed with signature VIP seating, and panoramic views of the Atlantic Ocean, your poolside lunch slides graciously to golden hour here.
