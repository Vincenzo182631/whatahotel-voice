# Welcome to W Fort Lauderdale

## The beachfront crown jewel of hotels in Fort Lauderdale

Turn up the current at W Fort Lauderdale, where bold design meets the electric energy of Fort Lauderdale Beach. Steps from the city’s most vibrant shopping, dining, and cultural scenes, this is where sun-seekers and scene-makers come to play. Take in sweeping ocean views from your private balcony, then rise above it all at WET Bar & Grill, five floors up and made for sun-drenched lounging and poolside...See More indulgence. Savor standout dining at Steak 954 from James Beard Award–winning restaurateur Stephen Starr, and let the night unfold in the Living Room, where craft cocktails and conversation set the vibe. Recharge at FIT or tap into seasonal wellness experiences, then shift seamlessly from business to pleasure in oceanfront studios designed to inspire. From beachfront “I do’s” to pre- and post-cruise celebrations near Port Everglades, every moment here is dialed up. Whatever brings you in, W Fort Lauderdale is where you amplify your stay.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Fort Lauderdale?

The check-in time at W Fort Lauderdale is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Fort Lauderdale allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Fort Lauderdale. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 45.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Fort Lauderdale?

On-site valet parking is available at W Fort Lauderdale. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Fort Lauderdale have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Fort Lauderdale has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Fort Lauderdale booking.

What is the closest airport near W Fort Lauderdale?

The closest airport near W Fort Lauderdale is Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL). FLL is located approximately 6.2 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Fort Lauderdale have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Fort Lauderdale does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
