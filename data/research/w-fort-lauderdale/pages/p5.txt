# Discover our hotel near Fort Lauderdale airport

## Discover

Welcome to your hotel playground on the beach. Ditch your shoes for sandals & revel in all that Fort Lauderdale Beach has to offer. With miles of sandy beaches and water sports for every level of adventurer, there is something for everyone. Blast away on a jet ski, or paddle board up to a dockside Las Olas Boulevard café along the Intracoastal Waterway. See the sights on a private yacht, catamaran, or Water Taxi while cruising past multi-million dollar mansions on the water. Scuba dive down deep and discover an underworld of flourishing reefs. Our hotel is located on the beach near Hugh Taylor Birch State Park, Las Olas Beach, & stands as the central point in Fort Lauderdale. Located near Port Everglades, stay with us before you board your cruise or join the WET Deck party when you dock. It is one of the most unmissable hotels near Fort Lauderdale Cruise Port​.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Fort Lauderdale have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Fort Lauderdale offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Fort Lauderdale have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Fort Lauderdale has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does W Fort Lauderdale have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY SPA is located on-site at W Fort Lauderdale. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Fort Lauderdale?

Top Golf & Emerald Hills Golf Course, Jungle Queen Riverboat Cruises, Stranahan House, Las Olas Boulevard, To the Moon and more are nearby W Fort Lauderdale.
