### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Fort Lauderdale have a work space?

No, the rooms at W Fort Lauderdale do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Fort Lauderdale?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 6 at W Fort Lauderdale. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Fort Lauderdale?

No, the rooms at W Fort Lauderdale do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at W Fort Lauderdale have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Fort Lauderdale have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at W Fort Lauderdale have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Fort Lauderdale have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Fort Lauderdale have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Fort Lauderdale have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Fort Lauderdale have in-room jettubs or whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Fort Lauderdale have jettubs or whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
