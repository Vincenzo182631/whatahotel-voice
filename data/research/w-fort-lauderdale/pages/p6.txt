## Sound Sessions at WET Deck

Turn up the vibe and dive into the rhythm. Join us poolside at WET Deck for Sound Sessions. A curated mix of live DJ sets, signature cocktails, and oceanfront energy. Whether you're lounging in a cabana or dancing under the sun, this is your soundtrack to elevated afternoons and golden-hour grooves.

## Sunrise Yoga at WET 5th Floor Courtyard

Start your day with intention—and an ocean breeze. Join us in the Courtyard for Sunrise Yoga, where movement meets mindfulness beneath the glow of the rising sun. Flow through a guided session led by expert instructors, surrounded by sea air, skyline views, and serene energy. All levels welcome. Just bring your mat, your breath, and your vibe.

## Sunday Funday: Rosé All Day at WET Deck

Sip, splash, and vibe all Sunday long. Join us at WET Deck for Rosé All Day, where the bubbles are flowing, the beats are bold, and the ocean views are everything. Lounge poolside with a glass of chilled rosé, soak up the sun, and toast to the ultimate weekend wind-down W style.

## Golden Hour at WET Deck

As the sun begins to set, the energy rises. Join us at WET Deck for Golden Hour, where ocean views, poolside beats, and handcrafted cocktails create the perfect sunset scene. Lounge in a cabana, sip something sparkling, and let the golden glow carry you into the night.

## Golden Hour at Living Room Bar

Step into the glow at Living Room Bar, where Golden Hour brings elevated sips and laid-back luxury. Unwind with signature cocktails, curated sounds, and a chic indoor-outdoor vibe that transitions seamlessly from day to night. It’s your golden moment, own it.
