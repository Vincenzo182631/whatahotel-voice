Experiential Dining on Fort Lauderdale Beach

### Brunch by the Beach

Start your day with brunch on Fort Lauderdale Beach, where ocean views and coastal breezes set the scene. From relaxed beachfront dining to vibrant, shareable favorites, brunch here is sunlit, social, and made to linger just steps from the shoreline.

### Poolside at WET Deck

Midday calls for cocktails and bites at WET Bar & Grill, a rooftop poolside restaurant overlooking Fort Lauderdale Beach. Enjoy fresh flavors, light snacks, and handcrafted drinks while lounging above the ocean—where beachfront dining meets elevated poolside energy.

### Golden Hour at Living Room

As the sun begins to set, head to Living Room and the ocean-view terrace for golden hour on Fort Lauderdale Beach. Sip craft cocktails, take in sweeping views, and settle into one of the most vibrant beachfront dining and nightlife scenes in the city

## In-Room Dining

After a day of sun-soaked lounging and nights that blur into early mornings, retreat to the comfort of your room with in-room dining at W Fort Lauderdale. From indulgent late-night bites to elevated classics, every dish is served with ease—so you can unwind, recharge, and savor the moment in your own space. Kick back with ocean views from your private balcony, order one more round, and let Fort Lauderdale Beach linger just a little longer.
