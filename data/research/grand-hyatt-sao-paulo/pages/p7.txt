Reviewed by
Ramsey Qubein
Why book?
Convenient to corporate offices in the business-heavy Berrini area, this mega hotel is purpose-built for groups, events, and the briefcase-toting traveler. Don’t let its size fool you though as service standards here reach as high as this skyscraper.
Set the scene
In a city where traffic is so bad that helicopters are a common alternative, business travelers will appreciate this location. The moment you enter the sunshine-filled marble lobby, it becomes clear that this hotel is true to its name. Everything is on a grand scale, and the mostly corporate or group crowd is busy tapping away on laptops or conducting deals in the lounge. The muffled buzz in the air is a United Nations of languages, and if you look closely enough, you’ll notice that the hotel does a sizable business among tourists and individual travelers, too.
The backstory
South America’s largest city sprawls as far as the eye can see with towers and highways, which explains how this part of town (not the most central) has become a hub for meetings. Gatherings can sleep, work, and dine in one place without ever needing to sit in traffic.
The city’s domestic Congonhas Airport is only a few miles away when it’s time to go, and a nearby metro station can expedite the journey to other parts of town.
The rooms
When you swing open your door, the sunlight immediately hits through enormous walls of windows. While there are no landmark sights in this area, the neighboring cityscape and gardens add an energy that weary road warriors can appreciate.
The rooms are done in Earth-toned palettes that calm the mind after a hectic drive from the airport. Beige, brown, and dark leather hues dominate with Hyatt’s signature Grand Bed, the room’s centerpiece. Grand Hyatt’s bread is buttered with business travelers, and the wide desks and plentiful power outlets around the room confirm that this outpost is a prime example of that. Be sure to book a room on a high floor facing the Ponte Estaiada Octavio Frias de Oliveira Bridge, which is the most interesting of the bunch here.
Guests with Grand Club access (anyone who has top-tier World of Hyatt status or books a Club room rate) have an extra feather in their cap. This 20th-floor lounge is among the best of its type in town, with an impressive breakfast spread (made-to-order egg dishes are on offer, be sure to try the egg and cheese-stuffed tapioca) and evening happy hour with an open bar and buffet of light dinner selections. Staff here are always at the ready during the day to prepare specialty coffees for those who choose to make the lounge an impromptu office.
Bathrooms stock Brazilian-made Granado toiletry products. All are spacious and bright, especially by the marble vanities, and feature mosaic-tiled shower stalls.
Food and drink
Dining here skips over the traditional Brazilian stalwart options of churrasceria or meat-heavy venues in favor of classic Italian menus and a lively sushi selection. Both restaurants do solid business, although Kinu Sushi Bar only opens for dinner. Guests rub shoulders with neighborhood executives looking for an express lunch with colleagues at Il Cittadino.
By the pool, guests can order up a cocktail, although the menu at Vena Cosmopolitan Bar in the lobby is more impressive, with the bartender making especially potent Caipirinhas.
The spa
Indoor and outdoor pools here provide extra options for entertainment, and the patio’s sunbeds see a lot of attention, especially on weekends when rates often drop here. Designers were clever enough to install a series of waterfalls that spill into the pool to cover up traffic noise from the pool’s location.
In the spa, guests can choose from an array of massage or beauty services or avail themselves of the fitness center with cardio equipment, punching bags, and free weights.
The neighborhood/area
This is not the kind of area where you can walk out the front door and explore interesting shops or cafes. Taxis or Ubers are necessary here, but the metro station is a short walk away. For those on an expense account, this hotel has everything you need to stay put and entertained.
The service
For a business-oriented property, the service here is especially doting, and guests in the Grand Club can expect name recognition. Catering to repeat guests at this hotel seems to be a staff specialty.
For families
You won’t find a lot of kids here, but few visit this city on vacation with family in tow. Young guests who do check in make a beeline for the pools. Parents will appreciate that each floor has connecting room options.
Eco effort
Management takes sustainability seriously. The pool is heated with steam exhaust, and recycling and food-waste reduction programs are in full swing.
Accessibility
Rooms for those with mobility needs are in place, and the fitness club, pool, spa, and public bathrooms also address accessibility concerns.
Anything left to mention?
There are a lot of little extras hiding in cupboards or around the corner. These include niceties like complimentary garment bags in closets and a pet park with space for four-legged guests to play with toys and water bowls that can be sent to the room.
