## Experience Luxury in Brazil at our Hotel in São Paulo

Nestled in São Paulo’s vibrant business district, our centrally located luxury hotel puts you close to many top restaurants, shopping centers and attractions. Enjoy supreme relaxation at our hotel’s on-site spa, or unwind in the indoor-outdoor pool. Whether you’re visiting for business or pleasure, Grand Hyatt São Paulo offers impeccable service and sweeping city views from your room.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Relax and recharge in a spacious hotel room overlooking São Paulo and enjoy modern amenities and locally inspired décor. Enhance your stay with a Grand Suite, which includes a separate living area, Grand Club® access and panoramic city views.

## EXPLORE

Grand Club Lounge

Located on the 20th floor, our exclusive Grand Club Lounge provides comfort and convenience for guests in our club and suite categories. Along with high ceilings and breathtaking city views, the lounge offers complimentary continental breakfast, Wi-Fi access, cocktails and appetizers.

Gastronomic Events

Enjoy the best of gastronomy at weekly dining events that infuse Brazilian flavors, including feijoada on Saturdays and brunch on Sundays that features delicious seafood, oysters, gourmet stations, live stations and breakfast items.

Amanary Spa

Find peace and tranquility in the heart of Brazil’s most populous city at Amanary Spa. Choose from a variety of soothing facials, therapeutic massages and invigorating body treatments to relax the mind and body. Our São Paulo hotel spa facility features seven treatment rooms, a sauna, a climatized indoor pool and a cardio and strength studio.

Indoor and Outdoor Pool

Enhance your experience in São Paulo with amenities like a climatized indoor and outdoor pool, plus bar service perfect for sunny days.

Fitness Center

Keep up your fitness routine with Technogym equipment, boxing and yoga classes, complemented by a sauna and climatized indoor pool. The fitness center also offers personal trainers, free parking and special discounts at the hotel’s restaurants and spa.

### Pets Are Welcome

Your canine companions are welcome at our pet-friendly São Paulo hotel. We provide a pet park with pet toys, a green area and ample space for your furry friends to run. Upon check-in, every guest's pet receives a hygiene mat, water bowl and food bowl.

#### Pet Fees
