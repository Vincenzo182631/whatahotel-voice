# Dining

In cosmopolitan São Paulo, gastronomy is a highlight. Our hotel’s restaurant options include Cittadino, with contemporary Italian cuisine, Kinu Sushi Bar, with authentic Japanese flavors, and the new Vena Cosmopolitan Bar, which takes center stage in the hotel’s new lobby offering both classic and signature drinks. To complete the experience, Cittadino Coffee Bar serves specialty coffees. With diverse dining options, the hotel delights every palate.

## Kinu Sushi bar

Located in the hotel lobby, this São Paulo Japanese restaurant and sushi bar offers exotic sashimi and dishes prepared live by our chefs. Kinu also has a variety of sake brands and cocktails that pair well with Japanese cuisine. In addition, you can enjoy delicious Omakase options.
