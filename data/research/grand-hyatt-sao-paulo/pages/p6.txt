Transportation Options

Airport Shuttle Service

We offer transportation service to and from Congonhas Airport. You’ll be charged R$ 640,00 for each way. Please request service at least 24 hours in advance by notifying us at +55 11 2838 3123 or via email at saogh-concierge@hyatt.com
