## Luxury Mountainside Vacation Rentals In Deer Valley

Escape to a spacious modern mountain condo at The Residences at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley. With breathtaking views of Jordanelle Reservoir, this is the perfect ski vacation rental home near Park City. Floor-to-ceiling windows, fully equipped kitchens, spa-inspired bathrooms and private terraces in most residences create a luxury alpine retreat. Enjoy elevated service, the comforts of home and exclusive access to the upscale amenities of Grand Hyatt Deer Valley.

## EXPLORE

Skiing at Deer Valley

Steps from The Residences at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley, be among the first to experience Deer Valley Resort’s expanded terrain. For the 2025/2026 ski season, enjoy over 100 new ski runs, the completion of 10 new chairlifts, the new Deer Valley East Village Gondola, and more than 3,700 skiable acres. Reserve lift tickets early to ensure access - Deer Valley Resort opens for the winter in December 2025.Enjoy seamless, top-tier ski gear rentals directly on-property when you stay with us. Our ski butler service includes personalized fittings, on-mountain support, and effortless returns—all designed for your convenience and comfort.

Sunspell Pool + Provisions

With panoramic views of Jordanelle Reservoir and the Wasatch Mountains, Sunspell sets the scene for winter days and lively après-ski evenings. Enjoy craft cocktails, live music, seasonal fare, fire features, and elevated mountain views from the inviting 7,000-square-foot terrace. Warm up after the slopes or settle in beneath the stars.

Residences Lounge

The lounge is assessable when you book at The Residences at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley. You can enjoy a private retreat reserved only for guests staying in our residences December 15, 2025 - March 31, 2026, with all-day refreshments and snacks.

Dining at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley

Grand Hyatt Deer Valley redefines Park City’s dining scene with innovative concepts. Savor the unexpected at:

-Remington Hall: Our signature restaurant offering breakfast, brunch and dinner with innovative takes on classics

-The Lounge at Remington Hall: Lunch and après-ski indulgences, complete with an adjacent sports room and specialty cocktail menu

-Hidden Ace: Tucked underground, where live performances meet a curated whiskey-forward cocktail menu and elevated bites

-The Living Room: A family après-ski event featuring our High Chocolate—a hot cocoa experience elevated to tea service perfection

-Double Blacks: A bustling morning coffee spot transformed into an inviting après-ski haven, with outdoor seating and cozy firepits

Agaci Spa + Wellness

Rooted in stillness, Agaci Spa + Wellness at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley is a sanctuary where the alpine landscape and ancient rituals meet in harmony. Every treatment is designed to reconnect you: to your body, to the moment, and to the world around you. Whether you're seeking a luxurious Park City massage, a tailored facial, or a holistic wellness escape, what awaits is anything but ordinary.

### WE ARE PET FRIENDLY

Your dogs are always welcome at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley for mountain adventures. Please notify the hotel before your stay of your furry friend’s arrival. You’ll enjoy the use of branded bowls, beds and much more, including a dedicated dog run on-property. Items are available for purchase. For the safety and comfort of others, guests are asked to keep dogs on a leash in all public areas. Individual pet weight limit: 75 Pounds Combined pets weight limit: 100 Pounds Maximum number of pets is 2. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
