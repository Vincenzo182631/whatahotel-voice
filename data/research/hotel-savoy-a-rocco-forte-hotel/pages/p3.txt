### Our Story

Welcome to Irene – a love letter to Tuscany named in honour of Lady Forte. Her gracious hospitality and generous table are the inspiration behind our elegant trattoria.

More Information

# Tuscan Restaurant in Florence

### Chef Fulvio Pierangelini’s menu is a delicate take on authentic Tuscan food. Guided by his seasonal approach, exceptional local ingredients are handpicked to create uncomplicated but delicious dishes. Spaghetti with tomatoes and basil. Florentine-style stuffed veal fillet. Handmade ravioli with black truffle. All accompanied by a carefully selected wine list of Super Tuscans and regional labels. This is Florentine dining at its most elegant.

### The Concept

Sit down and stay a while. Bring friends. Make new ones. At Irene, time stretches, glasses are refilled, and expertly prepared dishes arrive just when they should. Loro Piana fabrics, hand-blown Venetian chandeliers and Pietrasanta marble are reminiscent of a Florentine home. The terrace on Piazza della Repubblica offers front-row views of city life. A beautiful spot to pause and simply watch the day unfold a stone’s throw from the Duomo.
