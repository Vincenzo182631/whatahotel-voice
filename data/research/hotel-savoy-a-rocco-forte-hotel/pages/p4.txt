### A rule-breaker and trailblazer, Artemisia Gentileschi pioneered female empowerment and artistic expression in 17th century Florence. Today Artemisia is remembered as the leading female old master, amongst the likes of Caravaggio and Carracci, but we also remember her for her tenacious spirit. This is a bar for her, for the creative and the defiant.

### Menus

Venice regained

Main Menu

Behind the bar, Maestro Salvatore Calabrese and his team reinvent the aperitivo cocktails while Fulvio Pierangelini combines local ingredients with Artemisia's artistic flair. Vibrant female-produced spirits, super Tuscan wines, and twists on the classics - a menu confident and intriguing, inspired by our namesake.
