Hotel Savoy, A Rocco Forte Hotel
A central location, five-star service, and lovely recent renovation—not to mention unbeatable views—help keep the Savoy among the top picks in Florence.

Start us off with an overview.
A drink or dinner on the terrace of the Hotel Savoy, overlooking the promenading crowds in
Florence
's Piazza della Repubblica, has long been a pleasure—but with the sleek, new-look Irene restaurant that is truer than ever. The old Savoy hotel, one of Florence’s grandest, first opened its doors to guests in 1893. Sir Rocco Forte acquired the property in 1998 and carried out a top-to-toe renovation. The interiors have been re-imagined by Olga Polizzi, Director of Design for Rocco Forte Hotels - and Sir Rocco’s sister -, in her trademark chic yet lived-in style which highlights the best in Italian design and manufacturing.
Nice. What’s the crowd like?
Florence is primarily a leisure destination, so the hotel hosts the on-vacation crowds exploring the city and Tuscany for three or four nights. About 50% are from North America.
How's check-in?
The small lobby and quick walk to the elevators mean you'll be up in your sanctuary in no time.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
Of the 80 rooms, 30 are suites, and there are also plenty of connecting rooms for families. Choices range from classic doubles and executive options to an array of suites that differ in size and shape; the two-room Grand View suites on the fourth floor are the best (Presidential Suite aside), but most rooms have views of the Duomo or busy Piazza della Repubblica. The décor is smart yet homely with bleached oak floors, pretty wallpapers and beautiful Italian-made textiles of cushions and soft furnishings. Shelves hold books and flea market-sourced objects and tea and coffee trays come with SMEG kettles and Nespresso machines. Marble bathrooms are stocked with the in-house Irene Forte organic bath products produced by Sir Rocco’s daughter.
Are there any good restaurant, café, and drink options on site?
The ground-floor Irene restaurant and cosy new Artemisia Cocktail Bar re-opened in spring 2025 after a major re-fit. The look is brighter and more elegant, a fitting setting for noted chef Fulvio Pierangelini’s fresh, modern Tuscan cuisine which showcases seasonal, local produce. Choose a spot on the terrace overlooking buzzing Piazza della Repubblica or a cooler table inside for unfussy dishes such as vitello tonnato, pici with yellow tomatoes and lobster, and pan-fried sea bass with artichokes. The lunch menu includes an award-winning club sandwich.
Anything stand out about other services and features?
The subterranean gym is well-sized for an 80-room hotel, and comes stocked with Technogym equipment and a dedicated fitness studio. Thanks to its longstanding relationships, the hotel can arrange for an array of unique experiences in the city and throughout Tuscany, covering topics from food and wine to fashion, art, history, and perfume. Families enjoy the art treasure hunt through the Uffizi for a new perspective on the museum.
Bottom line: worth it, and why?
A central location, five-star service, lovely rooms, and a good restaurant—not to mention unbeatable views—help keep the Savoy among the top picks in Florence.
