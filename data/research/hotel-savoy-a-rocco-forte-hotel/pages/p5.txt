- Experiences

### The Most Beautiful Tuscan Road Trips

Guests are being offered the driving experience of a lifetime with a bespoke, individually tailored Ferrari road trip.

Book Now

### The Cooking Touch

Embrace the simple art of Italian cooking with three experiences that bring you closer to the soul of Tuscan gastronomy.

Book Now

### Perfume Experience

Create your own fragrance, discover exclusive essences and learn the secrets and techniques of artistic perfumery in this sensory journey with AquaFlor Firenze.

Book Now

### My Happy Florence

Turn the city into a classroom with this educational tour for kids. Start with a visit to Palazzo Davanzati, then head to Museo Galileo, a science museum that will particularly appeal to children with an interest in science and mathematics.

Book Now

### Florence City Bike Tour

Are you looking for an enjoyable way to discover the heart of Florence? Discover the magic of the ‘Cradle of the Renaissance’ at your leisure as you cycle down picturesque cobblestone alleys and through crowded piazzas.

Book Now

### Day trips from Florence

Follow the wine and art trail in the heart of Tuscany, where you will find great wines and a city brimming with art. Embark on a unique voyage, accompanied by Super Tuscans, exquisite DOCG wines and UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

Book Now

### In the Footsteps of Artemisia

Step into a world shaped by the bold spirit of Baroque trailblazer Artemisia Gentileschi. This cultural itinerary, designed by our guide Sara Papini, celebrates an extraordinary Italian artist who defied convention, claiming space in a male-dominated realm and transforming personal pain into a powerful visual language.

Book Now

### Medici Experience

This tour itinerary takes guests out of the television and into history to discover the Medici family, the inventors of the Renaissance. Guests at the Hotel Savoy can follow an original itinerary to rediscover the city, both inside and outside Florence.

Book Now

### Paint Florence with a local artist

Embrace the rich artistic history of Florence, a city that inspired Renaissance masters Botticelli and Michelangelo, through a private painting session with a local artist, Mario Minarini.

Book Now

### Firenze Romantica

Who says Paris is Europe's most romantic city? Firenze Romantica has love stories that will blow your mind. Take your loved one to Florence on a special romantic tour with Hotel Savoy and discover its enchanting history.

Book Now

### Behind the scenes at Scuola del Cuoio

Florence is world-renowned for its leather goods, crafted by the city's most skilled artisans at the historic Scuola del Cuoio. The family-run business uses traditional methods to manufacture its designs to exacting standards.

Book Now

### Florentine Mixology

Federico Galli, our expert Head Barman and one of the world's leading figures in Florentine mixology, will equip you with all the techniques and tools you need to make your favourite drink at home. With a particular focus on Italian liqueurs, you will learn how to make the perfect Negroni and discover its origins here in Florence.

Book Now

#### For more information and reservations, please call +39 05 52735836 or email concierge.savoy@roccofortehotels.com
