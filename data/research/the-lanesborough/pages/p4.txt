## Full & Joint Membership

Full and joint membership to The Lanesborough Club & Spa for individuals and partners offers full access to the gym and spa facilities, in addition to a number of perks and benefits.

Fitness & classes

- 6 complimentary guest passes

- Full access to all group exercise classes

- 4 complimentary personal training sessions

- 1 complimentary fitness and lifestyle assessment

- £150 credit for EMS session with Vive Fitness

- Locker hire available at £1,000 per annum including gym attire laundered

- Locker hire available at £500 per annum without laundered gym attire

Spa & treatments

- 3 complimentary spa treatments

- 20% treatment discount

Dining & Entertaining

- 15% dining discount at The Lanesborough Club & Spa Restaurant

- 15% dining discount at The Lanesborough Grill, The Library Bar, The Withdrawing Room, The Garden Room and Le Café LPM

- Private dining and event credit totalling £3,500 to redeem for private functions at The Lanesborough

Hotel & services

- Use of The Lanesborough concierge services

- Introductory £250 credit voucher to redeem against a stay at The Lanesborough

- 15% off rooms and suites at The Lanesborough and guaranteed next room category upgrade

- Early check-in and late check-out on stays at The Lanesborough

- Guaranteed room upgrade when booking at any Oetker Hotels property (subject to availability)

- Members are welcome to work from the Withdrawing Room & The Great Hall at The Lanesborough

- Priority invitations to our exclusive calendar of culinary, live entertainment and wine events

- 10% discount off The Lanesborough Art Programme

## Spaces to unwind and bring balance to busy lifestyles

Our state-of-the-art gym offers a spacious workout floor and two dedicated studios, equipped with the latest high-performance cardiovascular, functional, and weight-training machines. When it's time to relax, recharge in the spa, featuring a hydrotherapy pool, saunas, and steam rooms. For work and leisure, our members-only lounge provides a quiet space for productivity, while the Club & Spa Restaurant and its adjoining semi-private meeting room offer the perfect setting to enjoy nutritious meals and refreshing smoothies.
