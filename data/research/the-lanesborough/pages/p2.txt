## Our facilities

Set against rare opulence and an exquisite aesthetic, first class facilities are complimented by a world of wellbeing opportunities. A serene spa relaxation lounge gives way to four individual treatment rooms, as well as a luxurious double treatment suite complete with double shower and rasul. Our thermal suites, adjacent to distincly decorated male and female changing rooms, offer saunas, steam rooms and rain showers, leading to a mixed hydrotherapy pool with jets designed to massage muscles and minds.
