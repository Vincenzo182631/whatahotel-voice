Experience the most sumptuous and seamless of stays at Park Hyatt Saigon, one of the leading accommodations in Saigon; with 253 rooms including 27 suites. The French-colonial style rooms combine elegant, traditional comfort with the latest technology such as Nespresso machines and sensor lighting systems. For those who welcome additional space, the suites at Park Hyatt Saigon feature a separate living room, as well as a curated collection of oil paintings, antiques, lacquerware and handcrafted embroidery by established Vietnamese artists.
