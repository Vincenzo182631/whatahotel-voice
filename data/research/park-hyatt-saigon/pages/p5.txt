### Another Excellent Stay - My Third !!!

This was my third stay at the Park Hyatt Saigon, and it has become my hotel of choice whenever I visit the city. There is a reason I keep returning. The hotel feels ultra-luxurious from the moment you arrive, while still offering a calm and relaxing escape in the heart of Saigon. The location is perfect for exploring the city.

What stands out most is the staff. Everyone is genuinely welcoming, hospitable, and caring. Their warmth makes the hotel feel personal, not just luxurious, and it is a big part of why I keep coming back.

As a Globalist, I also appreciated the recognition. I received an upgrade to a garden room and was given late checkout, both of which made this stay even more enjoyable. Breakfast was another highlight, and I looked forward to it each morning.

After three visits, the Park Hyatt Saigon remains one of my favorite hotels. It is beautiful and refined, with wonderful service in an ideal location. I will happily return on my next visit and highly recommend it to anyone looking for a luxurious stay in Saigon.
