# Ho Chi Minh City Restaurants \| Park Hyatt Saigon

# Restaurants & Bars

An iconic journey handcrafted by a team of masters, dining at Park Hyatt Saigon is an experience in itself. Each restaurant and bar possesses its own distinctive identity, highlighting creativity, gastronomic excellence and world-class services.
Our signature restaurant, Square One, invites guests on a thrilling adventure of French and Vietnamese cuisine; while our all-day dining room, Opera, presents authentic Italian cuisine in a cosy trattoria-style ambiance. Park Lounge distinguishes itself by offering the most iconic afternoon tea in town and the Martini bar – 2 Lam Son showcases world-class cocktails with an inventive twist. For those with a sweet tooth, The Pastry Boutique stuns guests with an artful collection of French cakes and pastries while Chocolaterie welcomes guests to a haven of artisanal chocolate confections.
