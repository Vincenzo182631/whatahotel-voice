Discover urban luxury with a combination of sophisticated design, handcrafted details, and modern-day comforts at Park Hyatt Saigon. ## Discover Park Hyatt Saigon

Prepare for a More Sustainable Stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities have been discontinued or are only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

Park Hyatt Saigon’s Signature Experiences

At Park Hyatt Saigon, your stay becomes an artful journey of discovery, offering rare and intimate experiences that immerse you in the essence of Saigon’s heritage.​

From indulging in exquisite culinary delights to enjoying luxurious products and curated experiences, each moment is thoughtfully crafted to weave together a rich cultural tapestry that celebrates the vibrant history and traditions of the city.

Pool

After a busy day of business or pleasure, enjoy an invigorating swim in our 20-metre outdoor pool or relax under the tropical sun on our comfortable lounge chairs. Choose from a delicious selection of thirst-quenchers ranging from fruit juices to delightful cocktails.

The pool is open 6 a.m.–8 p.m. daily, and refreshments are served 10 a.m.–7 p.m. There is no lifeguard on duty, and children must be accompanied by an adult at all times.

Xuan Spa

Designed to inspire the senses and renew the spirit, Xuan Spa features three private treatment rooms and one double treatment room, each offering the latest high-quality equipment. Our spa fully embraces the Mekong Delta’s essences, such as fresh herbs, local grains and fruits. Come and be pampered.

Open 10 a.m.–10:30 p.m. daily.

Fitness Centre

Located on the 3rd floor, our fitness centre covers 170 sq m (1,830 sq ft) and features modern Life Fitness cardio equipment with personal LCD television screens and headphones. For body-building, you can enjoy the most advanced weight machines from Life Fitness. To complete your experience at Park Hyatt Saigon, the fitness centre also offers you amenities such as personal lockers, bottled water and towels during your exercise.​​

Open 24 hours.​​

Tel +84 28 3520 2356 \| spa.saiph@hyatt.com

## Things To Do

With Park Hyatt Saigon as your home base, you’ll be close to everything this historic city has to offer. Situated on Lam Son Square, the hotel is just a quick walk from Saigon Opera House, the Central Post Office and Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon. If shopping is what you’re after, luxury brands abound on Đồng Khởi Street and Diamond Plaza.
