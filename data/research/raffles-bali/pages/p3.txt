## Suspended between _sun_ and sea

Rumari is set between sun and sea at the highest point of our beautiful resort. Here culinary maestro Gaetan Biesuz creates authentic bold flavours, presenting progressive Indonesian cuisine in an organic, contemporary way. His 80/20 philosophy means that 80% of produce is sourced from the archipelago, while 20% is imported out of necessity. Our wine collection features over 250 labels, paying homage to the classics as well as selections from independent winemakers around the globe.

## An intimate chef’s table experience

The Rumari Counter offers an intimate dining experience for up to ten guests, where no menu is ever the same. Each evening, the chefs curate a surprise sequence of dishes inspired by the freshest seasonal produce, guided by Rumari’s 80/20 philosophy and enhanced with specially selected ingredients. Prepared and plated before your eyes, each creation invites direct interaction with the chefs and sommelier, shaping an elegant, personal, and memorable culinary journey.

From Wednesday to Sunday, 6.30pm to 10.00pm. At IDR 3,000,000++ per person, with optional beverage pairing available.

_Rumari Counter welcomes guests aged 12 years and above._

## Frequently asked questions

### What types of dishes can you find at Rumari?

Rumari at Raffles Bali serves progressive Indonesian cuisine, featuring dishes inspired by flavours from across the Indonesian archipelago. The menu includes seafood, premium meats, and seasonal ingredients, offering a refined dining experience in Bali that highlights both traditional recipes and contemporary presentation.

### What makes Rumari different from other fine dining restaurants in Bali?

Rumari offers a distinctive interpretation of Indonesian dining, guided by its 80/20 philosophy, where most ingredients are sourced from across the Indonesian archipelago. Combined with seasonal menus, and panoramic ocean views in Jimbaran, Rumari delivers a dining experience that is both contemporary and deeply rooted in Indonesian culinary heritage.

### What is the difference between Rumari and the Rumari Counter?

Rumari offers à la carte and degustation dining, while the Rumari Counter presents a surprise chef's table experience, where each menu is tailored daily according to seasonal ingredients and guests' dietary preferences.

### What is the Rumari Counter experience?

The Rumari Counter is an intimate chef's table experience in Bali, limited to ten guests per evening. Seated directly in front of the culinary team, guests enjoy a surprise tasting menu inspired by the freshest seasonal ingredients and Rumari's progressive approach to Indonesian cuisine.

### What is the dining atmosphere like at Rumari?

Rumari at Raffles Bali offers a dining setting with elevated ocean views and a calm, refined atmosphere. Located at one of the highest points of the resort, it provides a comfortable space for breakfast and dinner in Bali, with a setting that transitions naturally from daylight to evening.

### How does Rumari incorporate local ingredients?

Rumari at Raffles Bali follows an ingredient-led approach, sourcing a large portion of its produce from across Indonesia. Local spices, seafood, and seasonal ingredients are used to shape each dish, allowing guests to experience Indonesian flavours in a contemporary format while supporting regional producers and culinary traditions.

### Are there vegetarian options available at Rumari?

Yes, Rumari at Raffles Bali offers vegetarian options to suit different dietary preferences. The menu includes dishes prepared with seasonal vegetables and plant-based ingredients, and the culinary team can adjust selections based on individual requirements, ensuring a well-balanced dining experience in Bali.

### Does the Rumari Counter offer wine or beverage pairing?

Yes. Guests may enhance their dining journey with an optional beverage pairing selected by Rumari's sommelier. The pairings are designed to complement the flavours and ingredients of each course, drawing from Rumari's award-winning wine collection.

### How can I make a reservation at Rumari?

To make a reservation at Rumari at Raffles Bali, guests may contact the resort via email at Dining.Bali@raffles.com or call (62) 361 201 5800. Advance booking is recommended to secure your preferred time and seating for a restaurant experience in Bali.
