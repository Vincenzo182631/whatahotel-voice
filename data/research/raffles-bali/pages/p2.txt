## Frequently asked questions

### What types of wellness activities are offered?

Raffles Bali offers wellness activities in Bali including sound healing, reiki sessions, chakra balancing, and cultural rituals such as temple blessings and purification ceremonies. Complimentary daily wellness activities, including yoga, guided meditation, and breathwork, are also available. These experiences are designed to support rest and wellbeing while connecting guests with local traditions and the natural surroundings.

### How does the Soul Blessing Ceremony work?

The Soul Blessing Ceremony at Raffles Bali is a guided ritual led by a Balinese priest at the resort temple. The ceremony includes prayers, offerings, and holy water purification, allowing guests to take part in a traditional spiritual practice in Bali. It is intended to bring a sense of clarity, balance, and connection to local culture.

### What benefits does sound healing provide?

Sound healing at Raffles Bali uses vibration and frequency to support relaxation and mental clarity. Held in the resort’s natural cave setting, the session helps reduce stress, improve focus, and encourage better sleep. It is a popular wellness experience in Bali for guests seeking a calm and restorative moment during their stay.

### Can I purchase gift cards for spa services?

Yes, gift cards for spa services at Raffles Bali can be purchased online via giftcards.raffles.com. These can be used for spa treatments, wellness experiences, and personalised services, making them a thoughtful option for special occasions or gifts in Bali.

### What is the significance of the Fire Cleansing Ritual?

the Fire Cleansing Ritual is performed on the beach lawn / beach area as a ceremonial act of purification and transformation. The sacred fire symbolises the release of fears, limiting beliefs and stagnant energy, inviting personal renewal and a restored sense of inner balance.

### What are the age guidelines for spa treatments at Raffles Bali?

Spa treatments are available for guests aged 12 years and above. Guests under 18 are welcome in the presence of, or with written consent from, a parent or legal guardian, ensuring every experience is enjoyed in comfort and care.

### What is the required spa attire and etiquette?

Conceived as a sanctuary of absolute tranquility, Raffles Spa invites guests to unwind in quiet mindfulness. To preserve this peaceful ambience for all, we kindly ask that mobile phones and electronic devices remain silent and unused during your visit.

A plush robe, comfortable sandals, and a private locker are provided upon arrival. We recommend leaving jewellery and valuables in your villa's safe. For your comfort and discretion, single-use undergarments are provided for all treatments.

### What spa offers and wellness experiences are currently available at Raffles Bali?

Raffles Spa offers a range of wellness experiences, including massages, facials, body treatments, bathing rituals, wellness packages, and seasonal spa promotions. To explore our latest offers, limited-time experiences, and signature wellness journeys, please visit our Wellness and What's On pages.
