## An unchanged landscape

Raffles Bali opened in 2021 in a hidden corner of the island, a place steeped in local legend and defined by spiritual beliefs and customs that have remained unchanged for hundreds of years. Surrounded by lush tropical gardens, the beautifully designed resort is a harmonious blend of contemporary comforts and unspoilt nature.

## Frequently asked questions

### What amenities does Raffles Bali offer?

Raffles Bali offers amenities including private pool villas, dedicated butler service, dining venues, and wellness facilities. Guests can also enjoy daily scheduled wellness activities such as yoga and meditation, along with cultural experiences within the resort, creating a comfortable and well-supported stay in Bali.

### How can I book a stay at Raffles Bali?

To book a stay at Raffles Bali, guests may visit the official website or contact the resort directly via email at bali@raffles.com or by phone at (62) 361 201 5800. Booking directly provides access to available offers, preferred villa selection, personalised arrangements, and exclusive direct booking benefits.

### What activities are available near Raffles Bali?

Near Raffles Bali, guests can explore beaches, local temples, and cultural sites within driving distance. Within the resort, activities include wellness sessions, cultural experiences, and nature-based programmes, offering a balance of exploration and relaxation during your stay in Bali.

### Why choose Raffles Bali over other hotels?

Raffles Bali offers a private villa experience in Bali just minutes from the airport, set across 23 hectares with both lush greenery and ocean views. The resort features a heliport and unique nature-based programmes such as the Botanical Tour and Birds & Butterflies experience. Spacious villas, dedicated butler service, and a strong sense of comfort and privacy make it a compelling choice for a villa stay in Bali.

### When is the best time to visit Bali?

The best time to visit Bali is during the dry season from April to October, when conditions are generally more favourable for outdoor activities and beach visits. Bali remains a year-round destination, and at Raffles Bali, guests can enjoy indoor and nature-based experiences even during the rainy season, when the surrounding greenery is at its most vibrant.
