## Explore Raffles Bali

Our tropical paradise is an inspiring combination of relaxed, Balinese beach chic and contemporary luxury. Discover our exceptional facilities and services.

### Raffles Spa

Experience relaxation, rejuvenation and sensory restoration with the finest treatments in a nurturing setting.

### Weddings & events

From a big wedding to an intimate renewal of your vows, our pristine beaches offer an unforgettable location.

Discover

### Unique dining experiences

From romantic repasts in a secret cave to a seaside dining, our inspiring spaces and imaginative cuisine are designed to charm and delight.

Discover

### Raffles Butler

You may never realize they are there, but you will always miss them when you leave. Our intuitive butler will reach out well before your stay begins, ensuring we anticipate your every need.

-

### Botanical Tour

Home to over 100 plant species—including vibrant flowers, exotic fruits, aromatic herbs, and majestic palms—this tour invites you to explore the rich biodiversity that makes Raffles Bali truly one of a kind.

-

### Swimming Pool

Perched seaside with sweeping ocean views, our 25-metre infinity pool reveals Bali’s majestic Mount Agung on clear days. Open daily from 7.00am to 7.00pm, this tranquil oasis invites you to unwind in nature's beauty.

-

### Secluded Beach

A hidden sanctuary of soft sands and gentle waves, creating a tranquil retreat. Embrace serene moments surrounded by unspoiled natural beauty, and let each sunset feel like a private show.

-

### The Sanctuary

Open daily from 9.00am to 6.00pm, The Sanctuary is a tranquil outdoor oasis where spa rituals unfold amidst ocean whispers and lush greenery, inviting you into deep relaxation and renewal.

-

### Fitness Centre

Located on the ground floor beside Raffles Spa, this 24-hour space includes an outdoor area with daily complimentary wellness activities for our in-house guests.

-

### Tennis / Pickleball

Set up your game at our court, whether you are refining your strokes or simply enjoying a casual rally.

## Respectful luxury

Raffles Bali offers guests an experience that is simultaneously luxurious, authentic and responsible. In preserving precious Balinese cultural and natural heritage, and upholding a pathway to a greener future, we embrace many diverse initiatives. From growing our own produce and using local artisanal ceramics, to innovative carbon reduction methods and experiences that connect guests with our community, we are on a constant quest for harmony in respectful balance.
