Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara
Discover our Santa Barbara luxury hotel
Perched on a picturesque bluff between the Pacific and the Santa Ynez Mountains is our luxury beach hotel in Santa Barbara. Our newly renovated oceanfront escape blends classic hacienda style and natural beauty with a laid-back California spirit. From sunrise to sunset, whether you're exploring our beaches and trails or relaxing in our zero-edge pools and expansive spa, there’s something to savor in every moment and every season.

Marisella
Marisella blends Amalfi Coast tradition with the richness of California’s land and sea. Savor handmade pastas, wood-grilled seafood and expertly crafted martinis in a beautifully appointed Italian restaurant with a Santa Barbara​ oceanfront setting.
Explore
Lulio
Enjoy fresh, Mediterranean-inspired fare and refreshing cocktails at Lulio, where bold flavors, handcrafted design, and panoramic ocean views create an unforgettable dining experience in Santa Barbara infused with endless summer energy.
Explore
‘O’ Bar & Terrace
Just beyond the lobby, ‘O’ Bar & Terrace serves modern Latin cuisine, inventive cocktails, and premium agave-based spirits. Named for the Chumash word for water, ‘O’ features an open-air loggia with ocean views and dishes inspired by the coastal region.
Explore
Mission Market
Located in the resort’s upper village, this laid-back market serves light fare, curated homewares, and artisanal goods. Enjoy coffee with a house-made pastry in the morning, fresh sandwiches and salads for lunch or seasonal afternoon treats.
Explore
SunBar
SunBar offers a sunny dose of Californian charm with beautiful Pacific Ocean views. Adults and kids will enjoy a menu including shareable bites, handheld classics, light local wines, refreshing cocktails and beer at this Santa Barbara oceanfront bar.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara. The daily valet fee is $75.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara is Santa Barbara Municipal Airport (SBA). SBA is located approximately 7.1 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
