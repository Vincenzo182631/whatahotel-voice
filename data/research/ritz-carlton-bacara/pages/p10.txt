Powering Curiosity & Creativity
Ritz Kids
Guided by playfulness and a sense of wonder, Ritz Kids delights and engages your children to interact with their surroundings in a fresh and exciting way, while introducing them to the four important pillars of Ritz Kids discovery: Water, Land, Environmental Responsibility and Culture.
Learn More
Bike Adventures
Electric Bike Rentals
We are delighted to be the first hotel in Santa Barbara to officially offer guests 18 and older complimentary use of our fleet of pedal-assist bikes by BCycle. The bicycles have the ability to transport travelers up to 30 miles on a single charge at a maximum speed of 17 mph.
Sporting Activities
Tennis
The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara features four clay tennis courts adjacent to the beach that invite the Santa Barbara sunshine and coastal breeze in. The resort proudly features a world-class tennis program managed by Cliff Drysdale Tennis and offers court rentals, classes, clinics, and summer camp for kids.
Poolside Relaxation
Cabanas
Enhance your poolside experience with a luxury cabana. Set on 78 acres, the resort offers two-miles of natural beach, three zero-edged heated saline pools with private cabana rentals and watersports including surfing, kayaking and paddle boarding.

‘O’ Bar & Terrace
California
Just beyond the lobby, ‘O’ Bar & Terrace serves modern Latin cuisine, inventive cocktails, and premium agave-based spirits. Named for the Chumash word for water, ‘O’ features an open-air loggia with ocean views and dishes inspired by the coastal region.
