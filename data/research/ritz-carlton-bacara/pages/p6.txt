Savor Fresh California Cuisine
Dining
Each of The Ritz-Carlton Bacara’s restaurants offers a distinctive take on Santa Barbara’s vibrant coastal cuisine. From Modern Latin flavors and craft cocktails at ‘O’ Bar & Terrace to Mediterranean-inspired fare highlighting local produce and sustainable seafood at Lulio, every fine dining experience in Santa Barbara at this oceanfront resort showcases the inspired spirit of the California coast.
Winemaker For a Day
In partnership with McKinney Family Vineyards, step into the craft of winemaking with an immersive dining experience featuring guided tastings, hands‑on moments, and thoughtfully paired cuisine that celebrate the flavors, stories, and vineyards of California’s renowned wine country.

Coastal California Cuisine with Breathtaking Ocean Views
From intimate dinners at Lulio to private catered events on the Bluff, The Ritz-Carlton Bacara offers Santa Barbara oceanfront restaurants for a refined dining experience. Menus feature fresh, locally sourced ingredients complemented by thoughtfully curated wines. Whether it’s a poolside lunch, sunset cocktail, or celebratory dinner, an unforgettable culinary experience awaits at The Ritz-Carlton Bacara.
