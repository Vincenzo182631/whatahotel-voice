Unwind in Our Luxury Santa Barbara Oceanview Hotel Suites
Strech out in our family-friendly suites in Santa Barbara
Indulge in our two-bedroom ocean-view suites, where luxury meets comfort. With expansive terraces showcasing stunning ocean vistas, spacious common areas for gathering, and a convenient kitchenette, these elegant family-friendly suites in Santa Barbara offer the perfect blend of relaxation and sophistication for families or friends seeking an unforgettable getaway.
The Ritz-Carlton Suite
Retreat to our enchanting two-bedroom hotel suite, where elegance meets comfort in a spacious 2,000 sq. ft. haven. Perfect for families or friends, this luxury suite features two beautifully designed bedrooms, each adorned with a plush king-sized bed, offering a restful Santa Barbara sanctuary for relaxation. The inviting living area, enhanced by a cozy sofa bed, creates a warm gathering space for shared moments.
Panoramic Views
Step onto the charming balcony and bask in the soothing coastal breezes while enjoying breathtaking views. With stylish bathrooms and a thoughtfully separated living space, this luxury suite on Santa Barbara's oceanfront provides the perfect backdrop for unforgettable memories in a serene escape that captures the essence of coastal living.

The Channel Island Suite
Luxuriate in ultimate comfort in our 3,700 sq. ft. two-bedroom suite, perfect for families or groups of up to eight. Each bedroom features a plush king-sized bed, complemented by a cozy sofa bed in the separate living room, ensuring ample sleeping space. With three elegant bathrooms, a mini fridge, and a coffee/tea maker, this Santa Barbara luxury oceanfront accommodation​ is designed for both relaxation and convenience. Enjoy the added benefit of lounge access, along with complimentary wired and wireless internet, evening turndown service, and high-tech entertainment options. Thoughtfully designed with air conditioning and a privacy wall separating the living area from the bedrooms, this non-smoking suite creates a perfect retreat for unforgettable moments.
