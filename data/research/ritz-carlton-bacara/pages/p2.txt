Furry Companions are Welcome at Bacara
We are delighted to welcome dogs weighing 50 pounds or less in our one- and two-bedroom Santa Barbara suites. The resort assesses a $250 USD per stay fee and a $50 USD fee for each additional dog. For Specialty Suites – The Ritz-Carlton Suite and Channel Islands Suite there is a $600 USD per stay pet fee. The fee covers the cost of deep cleaning the room in preparation of the next guest's arrival. It is non-refundable and will be charged to your room prior to check-out. For more information on a deposit, agreement, and amenity information, please contact the Concierge desk.

Pet Policy
Dog Policy
No more than two dogs may occupy each guest room.
Dog owners are fully responsible for their pet's behavior. The Ritz-Carlton Bacara, Santa Barbara reserves the right to remove the dog from the hotel should the dog continue to bark or disturb our guests.
For the safety of your dog and our staff, your dog must be out of the room or kenneled during housekeeping services.
Room balconies should not take the place of a waste area. Violations of this policy will necessitate off-premises boarding.
We regret that dogs are not allowed in the resort's restaurants, fitness center, spa, pool areas or the club lounge. This exclusion does not apply to guide or service animals. Leashed dogs are allowed to dine on Lulio’s outdoor patio, 'O' Bar + Kitchen outdoor terrace and Caffe terrace only.
The dog owner shall be held responsible for any cleaning and repair costs incurred.
Dogs (aside from Service Animals) are not allowed at the Pool at any time.
