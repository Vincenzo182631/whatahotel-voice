Luxe Spa Packages for Ultimate Relaxation
Indulge in the ultimate relaxation experience with the new lineup of packages at our Santa Barbara luxury day spa​ near the beach.
Featured Treatments
Citrus Avocado Body Scrub
A refreshing, full body exfoliation of mineral salts blended with citrus, avocado, and safflower oils. An invigorating Swiss Shower removes the scrub, and the treatment completes with a full body lotion application leaving the skin smooth and glowing. 50 minutes
Golden Hour Collagen Massage
An ultra-luxurious anti-gravity gold and peptide infused massage experience that lifts, and contours skin for ageless radiance. Melt stress with a brightening gold body serum to hydrate, soften, and smooth. A contouring peptide-rich cream is therapeutically applied for a full body cashmere- soft finish. 50/100 Minutes
Elemis Expert Tech Sculpt + Firm Facial
Our ultimate treatment, this multi-faceted facial is customized to target your skin needs using all 6 BIOTEC technologies for a transformative facial experience like no other. Your complexion will be left revitalized, strengthened, and defined. Includes: Cryotherapy for skin sculpting. Ultrasonic Peel for glowing skin. Microcurrent to lift the skin.
Jetlagged Rejuvenation Ritual
Immerse in the soothing ambiance of The Bacara Spa as we transport you to a state of deep relaxation, helping you overcome the effects of jetlag and embrace a renewed sense of well-being.
Blissful Beginnings
Immerse yourselves in a world of relaxation and romance with this curated experience, featuring a massage and CBD pedicures at our Santa Barbara couple's spa.
Tranquil Tides Bacara Babymoon
This pampering experience includes a soothing prenatal massage for the mom-to-be and a calming facial. Enjoy this tranquil escape before the arrival of your little one, complete with personalized attention and relaxation in a serene setting.
Indulge in Exclusive New Treatments
Morpho Designer 50 Minute
A coastal body renewal designed to leave skin silky, the body sculpted, and the mind restored. This luxurious full-body ritual combines a showerless exfoliating scrub with an invigorating contouring massage. Using rhythmic, sculpting movements designed to stimulate circulation, encourage lymphatic flow, and promote a more refined silhouette.
Oligotherapy 50/100 Minute
Inspired by the power of the sea, this reminerlizing body ritual combines nutrient-rich marine ingredients with a lymphatic-style massage technique to encourage drainage, restore vitality and leave the body light, balanced, renewed. A full body mineral cocoon provides perfect hydration and detoxification. Awaken to a total body makeover.
Golden Hour Repair Facial 50 Minute
A results-driven treatment designed to visibly firm, lift, repair, and deeply hydrate the skin. Powered by G-P8XTMTechnology, peptides, and plant stem cells, this sculpting facial enhances elasticity, refines the look of fine lines, and restores radiance. The experience concludes with KNESKO’s Signature Collagen Mask for visible renewal and restorative balance.
A Setting To Inspire Your Fitness Journey
California is synonymous with health and wellness, and the resort fitness center encourages guests of all levels to continue, or begin, their journey here. The Fitness Center offers the latest in cardiovascular and strength training equipment, a complete line of free weights, instructor-led classes, four Har-Tru tennis courts, and more. Daily, from 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. Kindly note guests must be at least 16 years of age to enjoy our Fitness Center.
