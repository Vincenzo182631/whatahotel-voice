Welcome to Our Santa Barbara Hotel With Concierge
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
Welcome to our Santa Barbara hotel with a Club Lounge. The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities, and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.

Club Lounge Access
An experience that transcends the ordinary, offering guests a truly elevated stay. As a guest on the Club Level, you'll be welcomed into a world of refined luxury and exclusive privileges. This Santa Barbara Executive Club Lounge is a private, serene space reserved exclusively for Club Level guests to unwind and indulge. These benefits are available as a benefit to particular rooms and suites.

In Good Taste
Daily Culinary Presentations
We offer five food presentations daily: Breakfast, Light Lunch, Afternoon Tea, Hors d'oeuvres, & Desserts and Cordials. Additionally, the Santa Barbara Executive Club Lounge offers a vast array of beverages including everything from your morning coffee to fruit juices, and an open bar to enjoy at your leisure. We invite you to enjoy freshly baked cookies, seasonal fruit, and candy throughout the day.
