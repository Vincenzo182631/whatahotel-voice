# AlUla

Saudi Arabia

#### AlUla, Saudi Arabia d

### Our Home Inside A Living Museum

Welcome to our sustainable home of wonder in AlUla, a unique desert retreat where dramatic landscapes and ancient heritage inspire creative expression, meaningful human connection, and the pursuit of adventure. Set amidst AlUla’s breathtaking sandstone canyons and UNESCO World Heritage sites, our resort invites travelers to explore Saudi Arabia’s natural beauty, rich culture, and spirit of discovery through immersive experiences that blend luxury, sustainability, and local tradition.

### Our Villas

### Celestial Villa

Our Celestial Villas feature telescopes and host spacious areas for lounging and absorbing the dramatic views of burning sunsets amongst sandstone cliffs.

### Alcove Villa

Our Alcove Villas have close-up views of the sandstone canyons. Take in the expansive desert views from your private terrace overlooking a starlit sky.

### Canyon Villa

Nestled amongst an ancient oasis, our Canyon Villas are immersed in the natural wonders of the Ashar Valley, surrounded by sandstone cliffs and sprawling desert views.

### Art Villa

Our Celestial and Canyon Art Villas tap into our creative spirit with views of one of the Desert X installations, including Falling Stones Garden and Najma.

### Wellness Villa

Our Celestial and Alcove Wellness Villas highlight the power of well-being, with additional wellness amenities, such as yoga mats and private in-room massage sessions.

### Arabian Villa

Our Arabian Villas are our largest and most secluded accommodations, featuring pristine views of the sweeping Ashar Valley and unique indoor and outdoor spaces.
