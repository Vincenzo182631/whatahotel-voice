### Experiences at Our Habitas AlUla

The heart of the Our Habitas experience lies in the immersion into our world and the extraordinary places we call home. Alongside our private activities available for booking, we offer our guests a complimentary daily programming schedule centered around our six pillars: Art & Culture, Wellness, Adventure, Learning, Food & Beverage and Music.

### Explore Ancient Worlds

Our home in AlUla is set among an ancient desert landscape, where towering ochre sandstone cliffs and remnants of ancient civilizations come together. In this space of great natural and historical significance, we adventure out of our comfort zones, discovering a unique place in the world.

Hike our 8km Adventure Trail and historical treasure site, stargaze with a local expert under the clear night sky, ride on camelback across the desert or drive through narrow canyons on a guided buggies UTV tour.

Book Your Activity

### Discover a Living Museum

By tapping into our innate curiosity through art and culture, we learn to question ourselves and the world around us. In AlUla, thousands of years of history live on to tell epic stories and set the stage for today’s creations.

Explore Hegra, Saudi Arabia’s first UNESCO World Heritage Site, get a glimpse into the not-so-distant past on a guided tour of AlUla’s Old Town and immerse in interactive art installations commissioned for Desert X AlUla.

Book Your Activity

### Experience the Sounds of AlUla

Music is our universal language. No matter where we are in the world, it brings us together regardless of cultures, borders and backgrounds. At our home in AlUla, we have always championed musicians and their art, welcoming local and international talents with open arms.

Connect with powerful soundscapes during our Concert Series, where musicians experiment with rhythms and beats that evoke profound sensations and emotions, taking us on an inner journey from start to finish.
