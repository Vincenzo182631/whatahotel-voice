### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sheraton Bratislava Hotel have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Sheraton Bratislava Hotel offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Weight Machines, and Yoga mats, resistance bands and foam rollers., making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Sheraton Bratislava Hotel have an on-site spa?

Yes, Wellness is located on-site at Sheraton Bratislava Hotel.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Sheraton Bratislava Hotel?

St. Martin’s Cathedral, Slovak National Gallery (SNG), Slovak National Theatre (New Building), Bratislava Castle, Eurovea - promenade & shopping and more are nearby Sheraton Bratislava Hotel.
