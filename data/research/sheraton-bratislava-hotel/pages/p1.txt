# Welcome to Sheraton Bratislava Hotel

## Discover the city from our hotel in Bratislava center

Sheraton hotel in Bratislava, Slovakia, is located on the banks of the Danube River, as a part of the shopping boulevard featuring a promenade, entertainment, cultural and relaxing venues. Sheraton Bratislava Hotel is only a five-minute walk from the city center. Our hotel is the perfect point for exploring the city’s iconic destinations. All 209 of the hotel’s stylish and spacious guest rooms and suites offer Sheraton Signature Sleep Experience beds, complimentary high-speed internet access, and extensive bathrooms. Sheraton Fitness, open 24/7, offers top fitness equipment by LifeFitness. Both Port Wilson Lobby Lounge and Amber´s Bar are perfect for business breakfasts, gatherings with friends, or elegant pre or after-dinner drinks, snacks, premium coffees, tea, and a wide selection of wines. Eight elegant function rooms with more than 700 square meters of space welcome every event, from large-scale conferences to intimate family reunions with gracious hospitality.

Festive Wonder at Eurovea & Old Town

Experience holiday magic at Eurovea Square right at our doorstep (Nov 13 – Dec 23, 2026) and the Old Town markets (Nov 26, 2026 – Jan 3, 2027). Explore festive stalls, seasonal treats, and vibrant lights, then retreat to modern riverside comfort and complete relaxation at our wellness.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Bratislava Hotel?

The check-in time at Sheraton Bratislava Hotel is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Bratislava Hotel allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Sheraton Bratislava Hotel. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €135.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Bratislava Hotel?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Bratislava Hotel. Self parking is available at Sheraton Bratislava Hotel, with rates of €3.00 per hour, and €39.00 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near Sheraton Bratislava Hotel. The daily valet fee is €39.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Bratislava Hotel have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Bratislava Hotel has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Bratislava Hotel booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Bratislava Hotel?

The closest airport near Sheraton Bratislava Hotel is Bratislava Airport (BTS). BTS is located approximately 9.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Bratislava Hotel have an airport shuttle to Bratislava Airport (BTS)?

Yes, Sheraton Bratislava Hotel has an airport shuttle to Bratislava Airport (BTS).

Does Sheraton Bratislava Hotel have an airport shuttle to Vienna International Airport (VIE)?

Yes, Sheraton Bratislava Hotel has an airport shuttle to Vienna International Airport (VIE).

Does Sheraton Bratislava Hotel have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Bratislava Hotel has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does Sheraton Bratislava Hotel have a Club lounge?

Yes, Sheraton Bratislava Hotel has Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
