## HOW TO GET  _to Hotel Bel Ami ?_

### BY PUBLIC TRANSPORT

**From Gare du Nord:**

Approximate travel time by taxi or private transfer: 16 minutes

Approximate travel time by public transport: 15 minutes

Take Line 4 towards Bagneux and exit at Saint-Germain-des-Prés. The stop is a 3-minute walk from the hotel.

**From Gare Montparnasse:**

Approximate travel time by taxi or private transfer: 7 minutes

Approximate travel time by public transport: 12 minutes

Take Line 4 towards Porte de Clignancourt and exit at Saint-Germain-des-Prés. The stop is a 3-minute walk from the hotel.

**From Gare de Lyon:**

Approximate travel time by taxi or private transfer: 14 minutes

Approximate travel time by public transport: 15 minutes

Take Line 1 towards La Défense, exit at Châtelet. Then take Line 4 towards Bagneux and exit at Saint-Germain-des-Prés. The stop is a 3-minute walk from the hotel.

OR

**From Roissy Charles de Gaulle International Airport (CDG):**

Approximate travel time by taxi or private transfer: 55 minutes

Approximate travel time by public transport: 50 minutes. Take the RER B towards Robinson or Orsay-Ville, exit at Châtelet - Les Halles. Then take Line 4 towards Bagneux and exit at Saint-Germain-des-Prés. The stop is a 3-minute walk from the hotel.

**From Paris Orly International Airport:**

Approximate travel time by taxi or private transfer: 32 minutes

Approximate travel time by public transport: 40 minutes Take Line 14 towards Saint-Denis Pleyel, exit at Châtelet. Then take Line 4 towards Bagneux and exit at Saint-Germain-des-Prés. The stop is a 3-minute walk from the hotel.

### BY CAR

Thanks to our partnership with a nearby parking garage, it’s easy to visit Saint-Germain-des-Prés by car. You can park your vehicle safely nearby. For guests with electric vehicles, charging stations are available on-site (service charged).

**From Gare du Nord:** Approximate travel time by taxi or private transfer: 40 minutes

**From Gare Montparnasse:** Approximate travel time by taxi or private transfer: 10 minutes

**From Gare de Lyon:** Approximate travel time by taxi or private transfer: 20 minutes

**From Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG):** Approximate travel time by taxi or private transfer: 1h20

**From Orly Airport:** Approximate travel time by taxi or private transfer: 35 minutes

### PRIVATE TRANSFERS

Our team remains at your disposal and will be happy to provide you with our negotiated rates. Do not hesitate to contact us directly by email or phone.
