# HOTEL BEL AMI   _5-star hotel in Saint-Germain-des-Prés_

At 7 rue Saint-Benoît, in a 19th-century building that once housed the printing press of Bel-Ami in 1885, Hotel Bel Ami embodies the vibrant spirit of Paris’s 6th arrondissement through a contemporary vision of Parisian elegance. Art, design and the distinctive lifestyle of the Left Bank come together in an address deeply rooted in Saint-Germain-des-Prés.

In the heart of the neighborhood, the hotel is just steps from iconic landmarks such as Café de Flore, Les Deux Magots and Place Saint-Germain-des-Prés. A privileged setting from which to experience the cafés, galleries and adresses that have shaped Saint-Germain. Designed for comfort and relaxation, this 5-star hotel features 102 rooms, suites and apartments with refined amenities—elegant retreats after a day exploring the French capital.

Hotel Bel Ami reveals itself as a lively and welcoming Parisian residence, combining contemporary design with attentive 5-star service. From its distinctive interiors to the Clefs d’Or concierge, every detail contributes to a personal and effortless experience of Paris. A place where every corner whispers a story, and every stay becomes a new chapter in a Parisian novel.

## ROOMS, SUITES & APARTMENTS  _in Paris's 6th arrondissement_

Just steps from Place Saint-Germain-des-Prés, our 102 rooms, suites, and apartments offer a peaceful retreat in the heart of Paris’s Left Bank. Designed by architect Pascal Allaman as elegant, contemporary living spaces, they invite guests to experience a 5-star stay shaped by comfort, tranquility, and attentive service.

Natural oak, lacquered glass surfaces in subtle tones and clean graphic lines reflect a contemporary interpretation of Parisian elegance, inspired by the character of Saint-Germain-des-Prés and the history of the building.

Beyond the décor, the experience is enhanced by carefully selected amenities, premium bedding, modern comforts, and discreet, personalized Clefs d’Or concierge service. More than simply a place to stay, each room is conceived as a refined Parisian living space, offering a relaxed and personal way to experience Saint-Germain-des-Prés.
