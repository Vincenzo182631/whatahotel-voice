# Starvinskij Bar: a cocktail bar in Rome

- Hotel de Russie
- Dining
- Stravinskij Bar

### Behind the walls of Hotel de Russie lies a different side of Rome. Set around an intimate open-air piazzetta, the Stravinskij Bar is a timeless retreat where sophisticated cocktails, understated glamour and a uniquely Roman atmosphere come together effortlessly. Long cherished by Romans and travellers alike, it remains one of the city's most coveted destinations for aperitivo and evenings under the stars.

### Rome in a Sip

"Rome in a Sip" celebrates the Eternal City through a collection of cocktails inspired by its history, beauty and timeless allure. Created by Maestro Salvatore Calabrese and Mattia Capezzuoli, the menu captures the spirit of Rome, from imperial grandeur and Baroque splendour to the glamour of La Dolce Vita. One sip, one story, one city.
