### An oasis of beauty and calm in the centre of the Eternal City, Le Jardin is renowned as one of Rome's most exclusive restaurants.

#### Le Jardin

Famed for its Italian cuisine, the restaurant celebrates Mediterranean flavours under the guidance of Chef Fulvio Pierangelini.

The à la carte menu features local favourites, including Tonnarelli Cacio e Pepe, Spaghetti with fresh tomato and basil, and Lamb scottadito with wild chicory.

#### The Concept

Chef _Fulvio Pierangelini_, brings over 25 years of experience to Le Jardin. Described as a ‘food whisperer’, he is renowned for his ability to conjure magic out of the simplest and most familiar ingredients combining and presenting them in new and inventive ways whilst remaining true to Italian tradition.

Inspired by Mediterranean ingredients and aromas, his style is surprisingly simple yet deliciously effective.
