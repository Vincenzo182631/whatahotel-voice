## Nijinsky Suite with Terrace

An ode to a timeless Roman lifestyle

Following a complete renovation led by Olga Polizzi, our Director of Design, this exceptional suite pays tribute to the graceful poise of esteemed ballet dancer and choreographer, Vaslav Nijinsky, who was a frequent visitor to Hotel de Russie.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Le Jardin

From the elegant dining room, replete with stylish classically-inspired accents, to the verdant outdoor terrace at the foot of the Pincio, surrounded by mature pine and flowering citrus, Le Jardin offers stylish Italian cuisine in sublime surrounds in Rome.

Discover

### Stravinskij Bar

Our award-winning oasis is a favourite Roman meeting point. Sip exotic drinks in one of the city’s most exclusive modern gathering places.

Discover

### Aquazzura Bar

Tequila and tacos in Rome’s place-to-be. Tequila cocktails paired with small bites by Fulvio Pierangelini, served on Aquazzura Secret Garden tableware – a complement to the garden’s verdant backdrop.

Discover

## Highlights & Offers

### Irene Forte Spa

Inspired by the tranquillity found within our Secret Garden, Irene Forte Spa, Hotel de Russie reflects the beauty of the outdoors through organic textures, soft lighting, and muted hues.

### Rocco Forte Kids
