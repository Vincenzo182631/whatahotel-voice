## Frequently Asked Questions

### What type of resort is Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection is a 5-star luxury mountain resort under MGallery by Accor, set on a hillside in Hang Dong district, Chiang Mai. It blends contemporary Lanna architecture with 360-degree valley views, a mountain-view infinity pool, and 5-star hospitality — ideal for couples, honeymooners, families, and nature-seeking travellers.

### Where is Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection located, and how far is it from downtown Chiang Mai?

Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection is located at 192 Moo 2, Ban Pong, Hang Dong District, Chiang Mai 50230, Thailand. The resort is about a 30-minute drive from downtown Chiang Mai and roughly 25–30 minutes from Chiang Mai International Airport (CNX). The route is a gently ascending mountain road that is easily accessible and safe.

### What are the check-in and check-out times at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Check-in at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection starts from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in or late check-out can be requested through the reservations team, subject to room availability on the day; additional charges may apply depending on room type.

### What facilities and amenities does Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offer?

Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offers comprehensive facilities including a mountain-view infinity pool, mountain-view fitness centre, Veranda Spa, separate steam rooms for men and women, The Higher Room restaurant, Rabiang Cha restaurant, Mountain View Bar, Afternoon Tea service, complimentary parking, complimentary Wi-Fi throughout the resort, scheduled shuttle service to downtown Chiang Mai, and fully equipped meeting rooms and event spaces.

### What room types are available at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offers 9 room categories, from Valley Deluxe, Valley Deluxe Scenery, Plunge Pool Pavilion, Scenery Pavilion, Luxury Bedroom Suite, Pool Villa, up to the Presidential Pool Villa (over 400 sqm). All rooms feature contemporary Lanna design, a private balcony with mountain or garden views, a bathtub, and a Smart TV.

### What restaurants and bars are available at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offers several dining venues:

1. The Higher Room — the main restaurant serving breakfast buffet and contemporary Thai/international cuisine, open 6:30 AM–6:00 PM;
2. Rabiang Cha — a Lanna-style restaurant;
3. Mountain View Bar — cocktails and imported wines with mountain views;
4. Afternoon Tea Service — served with valley views. https://www.verandaresort.com/chiang-mai/restaurant-mountain-view-dining/

### Does Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection have a swimming pool and fitness centre?

Yes. Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection features a large infinity pool with 360-degree mountain views — perfect for both leisure and lap swimming — and fitness centre open daily 6:00 AM–9:00 PM with full cardio and weight equipment. The gym sits next to Veranda Spa and the main pool, and is complimentary for all in-house guests. https://www.verandaresort.com/chiang-mai/facilities/

### What services does Veranda Spa at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offer?

Veranda Spa at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offers natural wellness treatments including Thai massage, aromatherapy oil massage, facial and body treatments, and Detox / Healthy Retreat packages. Separate steam and shower rooms for men and women are also available. Advance booking is recommended to secure your preferred time slot. https://www.verandaresort.com/chiang-mai/facilities/
