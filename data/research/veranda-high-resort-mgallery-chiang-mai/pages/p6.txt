# Family Resort Chiang Mai    for Time That Matters

At Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai, days unfold among green hills, rice and tea terraces, and the cultural landscape of northern Thailand. Plant rice together, cycle through the countryside, discover local traditions, or simply retreat to the pool and watch the mountains change with the light. It’s a family resort in Chiang Mai for families who want their time together to feel a little more meaningful.

## Why Chiang Mai Feels Different

Set on a valley slope outside central Chiang Mai, Veranda High Resort gives families a quieter base from which to discover another side of Thailand. Days can move between the resort and the surrounding culture; from temples and countryside cycling to hands-on experiences that introduce younger travellers to local ways of life.

## The "Veranda Parent" Perk

While younger travellers find their own fun, adults can settle into a slower Chiang Mai rhythm, whether that means a treatment at Veranda Spa, sunrise yoga overlooking the valley, or an unhurried afternoon beside the infinity pool.

### Veranda Spa

Private spa cottages with sunken baths, steam showers and twin treatment beds create a peaceful retreat after a day of exploring.

### Sunrise Yoga

Tuesday & Saturday Mornings

Wake with the mountains and begin the day slowly with yoga overlooking the valley.

Friday & Sunday Mornings

### Fitness

Keep your routine going while the family enjoys their own adventures. The air-conditioned fitness centre is equipped with modern workout gear, giving you a comfortable space for an energising session or a little movement between slower resort days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai have a Kids’ Club?

Yes. The Kids’ Club is open daily from 9:00 AM–5:00 PM, with educational toys and a programme of indoor and outdoor activities.

### What activities can families enjoy at the resort?

Families can experience activities including rice planting, cycling, morning alms giving and cultural experiences. Other scheduled activities include Muay Thai, yoga and temple walks.

### Which accommodation is best for a family of four or five?

The Luxury 2-Bedroom Suite offers 136 sq.m. of space with two bedrooms and is a comfortable choice for most families travelling together.

### Does Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai have accommodation with a kitchen?

Yes. The Luxury Pool Villa features a fully equipped kitchen, making it particularly convenient for longer family stays and larger groups.

### How far is Veranda High Resort from Chiang Mai city?

Veranda High Resort is located in Hang Dong, outside central Chiang Mai, offering a quieter mountain setting while keeping the city accessible.

The resort is approximately 15 km from Chiang Mai International Airport.

### Does the resort provide a shuttle to Chiang Mai city?

Yes. A complimentary shuttle to Chiang Mai city centre operates twice daily for guests who book directly, based on the information provided. Check the latest shuttle schedule with the resort when planning your journey.

### Is Veranda High Resort suitable for a multigenerational family holiday?

Yes. The combination of multi-bedroom accommodation, a private pool villa, cultural activities, children’s facilities, dining and quieter adult experiences makes the resort particularly well suited to families travelling across generations.

### Can we stay with a third child in the same room?

Room occupancy depends on the room type, number of adults and ages of the children. Families travelling with three children should contact the resort before booking to confirm the most suitable accommodation and whether an extra bed is required.

### Is an extra bed available for a third child or additional adult?

Extra beds are available in selected accommodation types and are subject to room capacity, availability and additional charges. We recommend requesting any extra bedding in advance.
