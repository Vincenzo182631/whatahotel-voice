## General

### What room types are available at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offers 9 room categories, from Valley Deluxe, Valley Deluxe Scenery, Plunge Pool Pavilion, Scenery Pavilion, Luxury Bedroom Suite, Pool Villa, up to the Presidential Pool Villa (over 400 sqm). All rooms feature contemporary Lanna design, a private balcony with mountain or garden views, a bathtub, and a Smart TV.

### What in-room amenities are included at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Every room at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection includes air-conditioning, Smart TV with Netflix connectivity, a coffee machine, bathtub, rain shower, in-room safe, mini-bar fridge, hairdryer, MGallery-standard toiletries, complimentary Wi-Fi, a private balcony with mountain or garden views, and daily housekeeping service.

### Are there rooms with private pools at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Yes. Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offers several room types with private pools, including the Plunge Pool Pavilion (with a private plunge pool), Pool Villa, and Presidential Pool Villa. All are private villas with scenic views. — ideal for honeymoons and families seeking maximum privacy.

### What makes the Presidential Pool Villa at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection special?

The Presidential Pool Villa at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection is an over-400-sqm private villa featuring a private swimming pool, private garden, multiple bedrooms, a living room, a bathroom with bathtub, a terrace with panoramic valley views, and dedicated Villa Host service — ideal for larger families, special occasions, and ultra-luxury private getaways.

### How many guests can stay in one room at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection, and are extra beds available?

Standard rooms at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection accommodate 2 adults; Pool Villas and the Presidential Pool Villa accommodate more depending on villa size. Extra beds are available in selected room types (additional charge applies). Children under 12 stay free when sharing existing bedding with parents. Please advise the number of guests at the time of booking.

### What are the mountain-view rooms at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection like?

Almost all rooms at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offer mountain or lush garden views, with Valley Deluxe Scenery providing the clearest valley outlook. Every balcony faces nature, and sunset views can be enjoyed from both the rooms and the main pool.

### Are there accessible rooms for guests with disabilities at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Yes. Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection has selected rooms designed to accommodate wheelchair users and guests with special-access needs. Please specify requirements at the time of booking — such as ground-floor rooms or wheelchair ramps — so our team can prepare a suitable room and support you throughout your stay.

## Rooms & Villas

### What type of resort is Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection is a 5-star luxury mountain resort under MGallery by Accor, set on a hillside in Hang Dong district, Chiang Mai. It blends contemporary Lanna architecture with 360-degree valley views, a mountain-view infinity pool, and 5-star hospitality — ideal for couples, honeymooners, families, and nature-seeking travellers.

### Where is Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection located, and how far is it from downtown Chiang Mai?

Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection is located at 192 Moo 2, Ban Pong, Hang Dong District, Chiang Mai 50230, Thailand. The resort is about a 30-minute drive from downtown Chiang Mai and roughly 25–30 minutes from Chiang Mai International Airport (CNX). The route is a gently ascending mountain road that is easily accessible and safe.

### What are the check-in and check-out times at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Check-in at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection starts from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in or late check-out can be requested through the reservations team, subject to room availability on the day; additional charges may apply depending on room type.

### What facilities and amenities does Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offer?

Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offers comprehensive facilities including a mountain-view infinity pool, mountain-view fitness centre, Veranda Spa, separate steam rooms for men and women, The Higher Room restaurant, Rabiang Cha restaurant, Mountain View Bar, Afternoon Tea service, complimentary parking, complimentary Wi-Fi throughout the resort, scheduled shuttle service to downtown Chiang Mai, and fully equipped meeting rooms and event spaces.

### Does Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes. Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection provides complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the resort, including all guestrooms, public areas, restaurants, meeting rooms, and the pool area, with no device limit. Wi-Fi credentials are provided at Front Desk upon check-in.

### Is Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection open year-round?

Yes. Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection is open all year, across every Chiang Mai season — from the misty green rainy season, to the cool High Season (November–February), to the summer months when the mountain remains cooler than the city. ### Is parking available at Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection?

Yes. Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection offers complimentary parking for all guests, with security personnel on site. The parking area accommodates private cars, vans, and buses for meeting groups and wedding events.

### How do I get to Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection by myself?

To reach Veranda High Resort Chiang Mai – MGallery Collection independently: from downtown Chiang Mai, take the Kan Klong Chonprathan Road (Route 121) or the Chiang Mai–Hang Dong Road (Route 108) heading to Hang Dong — about 30 minutes; from CNX Airport, about 25–30 minutes. The route is a gently ascending mountain road, easily accessible, safe, and well covered by GPS.
