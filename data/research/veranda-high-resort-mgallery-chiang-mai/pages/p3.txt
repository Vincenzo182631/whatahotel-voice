## Rabiang Cha Restaurant

Located on the lower plains of the valley with views of rice and tea terraces. The restaurant is modern version of a traditional northern rice granary each private room has air conditioned comfort. Outdoor terrace dining is also available. the menu is a fusion of Lanna Thai cuisine with a modern touch.
