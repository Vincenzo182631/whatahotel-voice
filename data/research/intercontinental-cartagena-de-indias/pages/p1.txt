### Infinite Views

Our infinity pool overlooks the Caribbean Sea, offering a privileged view of Cartagena’s sunset colors. Between water and sky, the light slowly changes as the day draws to a close.

### Kanuu Restaurant

Kanuu’s eclectic menu and wide variety of drinks and cocktails reflect a culinary dance that moves to the coastal rhythm, intertwining Caribbean flavor with dynamic global sophistication. Our culinary team’s creativity paired with the freshest local ingredients make for dishes that will captivate your senses.

### Yoga sessions

The hotel offers guided practice to start the day with the body awake. Postures, breath, and a quiet close, led from beginning to end. As fitting for a first class as for years of practice. Arrange yours at reception and we'll have it ready.

### A curated welcome, just for you

Plenty to choose from. Boards of cheese and charcuterie, fruit in season, the sweets made only here — enyucado, tamarind — and pan de bono straight from the oven. Each selection is built by our chef, Juan Jiménez, around what comes in fresh that day. Set it up before you arrive or order it whenever. An added service, on request.

Learn more

### Sustainable Artistry by Indhy Luna Foundation

A special corner dedicated to Indhy Luna Foundation, supporting local communities and promoting sustainability. Each piece, crafted with natural, eco-friendly fibers, reflects creativity, care, and purpose, turning artisanal work into unique items with a positive impact.

## FAQs for InterContinental Cartagena de Indias

Does InterContinental Cartagena de Indias have a pool?

InterContinental Cartagena de Indias has 1 outdoor pool.

Sunset Pool Terrace

Our oceanfront infinity pool overlooks the Caribbean Sea, offering a relaxed, sun-filled setting with coastal breezes and panoramic views. A special poolside sharing menu invites guests to enjoy easy, social dining throughout the day.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Cartagena de Indias?

Complimentary parking is available for hotel guests in the shopping center, in a covered, protected area.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Cartagena de Indias?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Cartagena de Indias.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Cartagena de Indias for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Cartagena de Indias have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Cartagena de Indias has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Cartagena de Indias?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Cartagena de Indias.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Cartagena de Indias?

Check-in at InterContinental Cartagena de Indias is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Cartagena de Indias offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Cartagena de Indias offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Cartagena de Indias from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Cartagena de Indias is conveniently located near Rafael Nunez International airport (CTG), approximately 5.53 miles away (8.9 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Cartagena de Indias?

InterContinental Cartagena de Indias is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Walled Historic Center - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- Getsemani - 1.62 miles away (2.6 kilometers)
- Rosario and Barú Islands - 22.87 miles away (36.8 kilometers)
- Ábaco - 2.17 miles away (3.5 kilometers)
- Botanical Garden

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Cartagena de Indias puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
