# All-Inclusive Hotels in Playa del Carmen and Riviera Maya

Our **All-Fun Inclusive™** concept adds all the fun to an **all-inclusive** sustainable tourism lodging with more than 200 experiences.

The main benefit when staying at **Hoteles Xcaret** is that you get unlimited access to the parks by Grupo Xcaret, as well as ferry tickets by Xcaret Xailing from Cancun to Isla Mujeres, and from Playa del Carmen to Cozumel. The round-trip transportation to our parks and the airport is part of this unique concept.

All the properties at **Hoteles Xcaret** feature a **Culinary Group** formed by Mexican and international celebrity chefs that will delight you with premium culinary experiences.

The **All-Fun Inclusive™** concept has different details at each hotel and depending on the chosen category.

## Our Experiences

## OUR PARKS IN CANCUN AND THE RIVIERA MAYA

Your vacation will be memorable while enjoying the **best parks in Cancun** and Riviera Maya. Get the best of Mexico through its culture and nature at Xcaret, and have a fantastic day at a natural wonder in Xel-Há.

Also, you can glide in zip-lines at Xplor, be part of a great Mexican party at Xoxi, or put your senses to test at Xenses and live an unlimited nighttime adventure at Xplor Fuego.

Also, get to know the wonderful Isla Mujeres and all its charms with the Xcaret Xailing ferries.

### Xcaret

Mexico's Majestic Paradise has more than 50 natural and cultural attractions to make your day unforgettable: shows, underwater rivers and much more.

### Xel-Há

In the natural wonder of the Riviera Maya you will enjoy swimming, snorkeling and interacting with the marine life of an inlet, as well as unlimited food and drinks.

### Xplor

Live the adventure in the most visited zip-line park in the world, where you will glide on the highest zip-line in the Riviera Maya, swim and paddle in stalactite rivers and more.

### Xplor Fuego

Get excited with the only unlimited nighttime adventure, where you will fly in zip-lines, drive amphibious vehicles through caves, hammock splashing, among other activities.

### Xenses

Discover a fantastic world with the more than 50 fantastic scenarios of Xenses, in its two circuits of activities that will take you to live a more fun reality.

### Xoxi

A floating Mexican party awaits you at Xoxi. Board the trajinera that takes you on a tour among natural settings, in an atmosphere with live music, dinner, and open bar.

### Xenotes

Xenotes is the only tour in the Riviera Maya that takes you to visit the four types of cenotes that exist in nature: semi-open, open, ancient, and cavern. Have fun with activities such as assisted rappel, zip-lines, kayaking, exploration swimming, or cliff jumping. To replenish energy, a delicious picnic will be waiting for you. Enter the heart of the Mayan jungle.

### Xcaret Ferry

Discover Isla Mujeres and Cozumel, two fascinating Magical Towns where you'll find spectacular beaches, relaxed atmospheres, and important Maya vestiges. Sail aboard our comfortable ferries enjoying round trip transportation to the Cancun pier or the Playa del Carmen maritime terminal.

### Xcaret Catamaran

Fall in love with the Caribbean Sea aboard a modern catamaran that will take you to discover coral reefs and visit Isla Mujeres, one of the most iconic destinations in the area. Enjoy this experience that includes transportation to the departure pier, a snorkeling tour, premium drinks on board, a box lunch, free time to explore the island, and more.
