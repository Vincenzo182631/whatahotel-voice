# Hotel Xcaret Arte: Mexican art stays with you

In an environment where art and nature come together, Hotel Xcaret Arte redefines the experience of a **Riviera Maya all-inclusive hotel**. Here you don’t just enjoy luxury accommodations, but a sensory journey that combines signature cuisine, creative workshops, and unlimited access to the parks and experiences of Grupo Xcaret.

Live unforgettable moments in this **adults-only resort**, with spectacular views of the Caribbean and an atmosphere designed to inspire and awaken the senses.

## All-Fun Inclusive™ at Hotel Xcaret Arte: beyond the all-inclusive

Hoteles Xcaret unique All-Fun Inclusive™ concept elevates your stay to a whole new level. With much more than just accommodation and dining, we bring you a limitless exploration of Mexico’s culture, nature, and entertainment, where all you have to do is enjoy.

## Access to Xcaret theme parks

Experience the seven parks and tours from Grupo Xcaret: explore underground rivers in Xcaret Park, ziplines at Xplor, relax in Xel-Há, or discover the Xenotes Tour.

## Ferry & Catamaran Tours

Discover Isla Mujeres and Cozumel with Xcaret Xailing ferries and enjoy catamaran rides as well as the ATV experience, all included in your stay.

## Transportation

Transfers to and from the airport, as well as to all parks and off-property activities. Includes transportation to the Xcaret Xailing experience to visit Isla Mujeres and Cozumel.

## Gastronomy & Mixology

Hotel Xcaret Arte hosts renowned Michelin Stars chefs who will amaze you with unique creations. Complete the experience with signature cocktails at bars and rooftops overlooking the sea.

## Spa & Wellness

Find your perfect body & mind balance in one of the **top Riviera Maya spa hotels**. Muluk Spa at Hotel Xcaret Arte offers treatments inspired by ancestral traditions, private areas, and relaxation rituals in natural surroundings.

## Shows & Live Music

From artist performances to cultural happenings and themed nights, every evening is an invitation to enjoy art and entertainment.

## Artistic workshops

Immerse yourself in unique creative experiences: take pottery, painting, dance, or textile lessons guided by expert instructors.

## Events & celebrations in natural settings

Plan your wedding, honeymoon, or meetings in venues that combine nature and artistic architecture, with customized service to make long-lasting memories.

## Adults-only comfort & fun

Enjoy spaces designed for couples or friends, with rooftops, bars, workshops, and common areas that inspire and relax.

## A one-of-a-kind Riviera Maya luxury resort

Hotel Xcaret Arte is a living canvas made up of six Houses (Casas), each with its own personality and architecture specially designed for a tailor-made experience.

## Casa del diseño

Facing the Caribbean, celebrates homecraft talent with textile workshops and open spaces to inspire you with the scenery.

## Casa de los artistas

A private setting next to the beach and coves, with library and painting workshops, and the Kibi-Kibi restaurant led by Michelin star chef Roberto Solís.

VIEW CASA DE LOS ARTISTAS

## Casa de la Pirámide

River views and direct access to Xpiral; this House offers a pottery workshop and a rooftop with unique panoramic views of the jungle and the sea.

## Casa de la Música

Full of energy, with its Latin dance workshop and terraces perfect for sensory experiences. Enjoy live shows and music events every night.

## Casa de la Paz

A haven of serenity, with exclusive access to Muluk Spa and private terraces with volcanic stone tubs.

## Casa del Patrón

The hotel’s cultural and gastronomic hub, with convention centers, event spaces, and the vibrant Cafeteca.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Hotel Xcaret Arte

### What is the minimum age to stay?

This is an adults-only resort for guests aged 16 and over.

### What’s included in the All-Fun Inclusive™ experience?

Accommodation in luxury suites, signature dining, unlimited access to parks and tours, artistic workshops, spa (additional cost), and land and sea transportation.

### What’s included in the transportation options?

You’ll have unlimited transfers between the airport, hotel, parks, and ferries to Isla Mujeres and Cozumel.
