# Luxury & Nature at    La Casa de la Playa

**A boutique hotel** in front of the Caribbean Sea, showcasing Mexico in an intimate, sophisticated, relaxed and fun way never seen before. With only 63 **hotel suites,** the beauty and privacy of this adults only experience blends the best of a house with the best of a hotel.

## TAILOR-MADE EXPERIENCES

We are curators of unforgettable visits to the most iconic parks in Riviera Maya, where only guests from La Casa de la Playa have access to exclusive **hotel activities** such as a dinner in an impressive wine cellar and an adventure tour through recently discovered caverns. Come and enjoy an exciting concept created to please the most demanding visitors. Experience round-trip airport transportation in luxury vehicles. Please schedule this service at least 24 hours in advance of your stay to ensure availability.

## Suites

63 suites overlooking the Caribbean Sea, each with its own distinctive character, showcasing the finest of Mexican design. Every suite features a private pool and organic luxury amenities. Meticulous attention to detail, carefully curated interiors, and sophisticated color palettes create an exceptional atmosphere throughout your stay.

Immerse yourself in the luxury and tranquility of the exclusive suites at La Casa de la Playa, where refined comfort meets breathtaking views of the Caribbean Sea or the lush Mayan jungle. Each suite is thoughtfully designed to provide a serene, contemporary retreat, blending local craftsmanship with modern elegance for an unforgettable stay.
