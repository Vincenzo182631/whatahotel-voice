# HOTEL XCARET MEXICO:   FAMILY HOTEL IN RIVIERA MAYA

In December 2017, **Hotel Xcaret México** opened its doors to offer the best of Mexico included. It was born as a tribute to Mayan culture and to our country, its artisans, its **Mexican gastronomy,** its living wealth, and its cultural heritage.

We are characterized by our hospitality and excellence in our service, which have earned us several recognitions, among them the **AAA Five Diamond Award.** Each achievement is a commitment to Mexico and to you. We invite you to get to know México through our love for it.

## ALL-FUN INCLUSIVE™ FAMILY HOTEL

### Xcaret Parks

Hotel Xcaret México has revolutionized the Mexican hotel industry with the **All-Fun Inclusive™ resort** concept, offering unlimited access and round-trip transportation to our parks: Xcaret, Xel-Há, Xplor, Xplor Fuego, Xoxi, Xenses, and Xenotes.

### Xcaret Xailing

Marvelous experiences in the Caribbean are waiting for you at Xcaret Xailing. From Catamaran Tours to Isla Mujeres to ferry tickets to discover Cozumel Island from Playa del Carmen or Isla Mujeres from Cancun.

### Transportation

Round-trip transportation service from the Tulum and Cancun airports (restrictions apply), plus shuttle service to the parks by Grupo Xcaret are included in your reservation.

## Suites

In each of the 1,800 **suites** of Hotel Xcaret México you will enjoy a privileged natural landscape: jungle, river or sea, as well as the ALL-FUN INCLUSIVE™ concept, that distinguishes Hoteles Xcaret for being one of the most complete and disruptive in the world. Choose the room that best suits you and delight yourself with the activities and amenities of a **5-diamond hotel in the Riviera Maya**.

## GASTRONOMY

The renowned gastronomy at Hotel Xcaret México is reaching new heights with the opening of new restaurants led by award-winning chefs. Discover the latest dining options that will delight your palate.

Chefs Alejandro Ruiz, Miguel Bautista, and Luis Arzapalo make up the Culinary Collective at Hotel Xcaret México. Their culinary creations are part of our All-Fun Inclusive™ concept.

We are also thrilled to have Chef Carlos Gaytán, who leads the first restaurant at Hoteles Xcaret with a Michelin Star and a 5 Diamond Award.

## SPA & WELLNESS

You deserve to be pampered and we know how. Harmonize with the energy of the universe in **Muluk Spa**, a mystical sanctuary that fuses ancient techniques with the latest in products for your skin.

For you to continue in the total wellness path through fun **hotel activities,** we have 3 gyms, paddle and tennis courts, open space functional training circuit, yoga lessons, meditation sessions and non-motorized aquatic activities like paddle board and kayak.
