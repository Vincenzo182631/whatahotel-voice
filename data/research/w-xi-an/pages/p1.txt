# Welcome to W Xi'an

## W Xi'an

Rising from the edge of the storied Qujiangchi Lake, W Xi’an dares to disrupt the ancient rhythm of the city with a fresh beat of style, energy, and indulgence. Spanning over 100,000 square meters and crowned with a soaring 17-meter-high Living Room, this is where the unexpected comes to play.With 372 stylish guestrooms and suites from the 5th to 26th floor, every room features a spacious private balcony...See More — your personal perch to soak in panoramic views.Fuel your cravings at three dining destinations —— WEI, YEN Chinese Restaurant and WOOBAR®, each serving up bold flavors and vibrant vibes. Then, take the plunge in the indoor/outdoor WET®, push limits at FIT, and surrender to the scene-stealing AWAY® Spa for that glow-up moment.Craving a show-stopping backdrop? From lavish weddings to next-level launches, our Great Room, Mega Room, Studios, and Strategy spaces are anything but business as usual.W Xi’an: live it up lakeside, and let the city’s pulse match yours.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Xi'an?

The check-in time at W Xi'an is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Xi'an allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Xi'an. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 18.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W Xi'an?

On-site self parking is available at W Xi'an. Self parking is available at W Xi'an, with rates of ¥10.00 per hour, ¥240.00 per day, and ¥0.00 perstay.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near W Xi'an?

The closest airport near W Xi'an is Xianyang Airport (XIY). XIY is located approximately 54.7 KM from the hotel.

Does W Xi'an have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Xi'an has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
