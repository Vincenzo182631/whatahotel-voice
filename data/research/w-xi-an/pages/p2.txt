### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Xi'an have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Xi'an have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Xi'an?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 6 at W Xi'an. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Xi'an?

Some rooms at W Xi'an permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Xi'an have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Xi'an have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at W Xi'an have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Xi'an have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Xi'an have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Xi'an have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Xi'an have in-room jettubs?

Yes, some room types at W Xi'an have jettubs. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
