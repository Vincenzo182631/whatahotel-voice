The Longest Day in Chang’an Theme Block

3.5 KM

"The Longest Day in Chang’an" theme block is China's first immersive Tang Dynasty-themed experience. It recreates ancient Chang’an with vibrant street scenes and cultural activities. Visitors can dress in Hanfu,fully experiencing the dynasty’s grandeur.

+86 29-4001806888

Family & Children's Activities

Grand Tang Mall

4.6 KM

A must-go attraction at night in Xi’an, to experience the city’s remarkable culture in its prosperous Tang Dynasty. When night falls and the street is brightly illuminated, visitors swarm into the street to view impressive views and enjoy performances.

+86 29-85228412

Family & Children's Activities

Stele Forest - Beilin Museum

9.8 KM

Stele Forest Museum was built based on Xi’an Confucius Temple dating to 1087. As a paradise for Chinese calligraphy lovers, the museum is a key venue of stone steles and epitaphs spanning 2,000 years.

+86 29-87253331

Family & Children's Activities

The Museum of Qin Terra-cotta Warriors and Horses

36.2 KM

The Museum of Qin Terra-cotta Warriors and Horses is home to a collection of terracotta sculptures depicting the armies of Qin Shi Huang, the first Emperor of China.

+86 29-81399127

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Xi'an have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Xi'an offers a fitness center available for a fee to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment and Yoga Room & Pilates Room, suitable for maintaining a workout routine while traveling.

Does W Xi'an have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Xi'an has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

WET (Indoor)Outdoor Wading Pool (Open in Summer) (Outdoor)

Does W Xi'an have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY®SPA is located on-site at W Xi'an.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Xi'an?

SKP, Daming Palace, Giant Wild Goose Pagoda, Terra Cotta Warriors, Bell Tower & Drum Tower & Muslim Street and more are nearby W Xi'an.
