## The Iconic Grand Hyatt Hotel in Central Seoul

Grand Hyatt Seoul is the ideal place to discover the vibrant culture and beauty of South Korea. Our rooms and suites invoke a sense of luxury coupled with the unparalleled convenience of being minutes from downtown Seoul, Myeongdong, and Itaewon. This spectacular urban retreat is the perfect destination for business travel and leisure or family vacations, and is set against the stunning backdrop of Han River and Mount Namsan, making every moment extraordinary.

## EXPLORE

Business and Meeting Center

Our business and meeting center features four private rooms equipped with computers and ten meeting rooms that can accommodate 6–16 attendees, as well as services to enable business travelers to optimize their time. Located on the 2nd floor. Inquire at +82 2 799 8154.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

322 Sowol-ro

Take a walk down 322 Sowol-ro, our unique gourmet alley where you’ll find four exquisite restaurants, each with its own distinct personality and cuisine: Steak House, Teppan, Kauri and Tenkai.

Club Olympus

Club Olympus is an exclusive space for members and guests, offering a premier wellness experience. The center features a sauna, indoor pool, outdoor pool with a whirlpool, as well as squash and tennis courts. Additionally, the fitness facility is available 24 hours a day for your convenience.

Located on Garden Level

Swimming Pools

The indoor swimming pool at Grand Hyatt Seoul is located on the Garden Level. The nature-friendly interior, combined with the trees, creates an exotic atmosphere. Overlooking the Han River, the huge 35x14 m sized outdoor swimming pool provides the perfect retreat for leisurely swimming and sunbathing.

Located on Garden Level

Ice Rink

Our massive rink offers the warmest and most romantic ice-skating experience in the city, with panoramic views in a fairytale setting. Enjoy the breathtaking scenery this winter. Located on the garden level.

Please note that our Ice Rink will be open from December 5, 2026 - February 28, 2027.

The Spa

Experience The Spa’s nine independent treatment suites and the European botanical garden concept to discover total rejuvenation and renewed vitality. Located on the garden level.

## Explore Seoul

Discover the culture and beauty of South Korea from the ideal home base, Grand Hyatt Seoul. Our rooms combine the convenience of proximity to downtown Seoul, Myeongdong and Itaweon with a sense of quiet luxury. Though set against the stunning natural backdrop of the Han River and Namsan Mountain, we’re also close to bustling shopping districts as well as cultural attractions like Leeum Samsung Museum of Art, Bukchon Hanok Village and much more.
