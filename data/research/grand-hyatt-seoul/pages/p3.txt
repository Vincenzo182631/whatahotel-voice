## THE TERRACE KITCHEN

Breakfast is exclusively available to in-house guests, is served until 10:30 AM.

The Terrace Kitchen, Grand Hyatt Seoul’s vibrant new dining destination where the kitchen takes center stage. Inspired by lively bistro-style cooking, the restaurant brings guests into the action with an expansive open kitchen and live stations, where chefs prepare every dish fresh to order in an à la minute cooking style. The menu blends Korean, Japanese, Chinese, and Western influences, offering elevated comfort dishes, bold cross-cultural flavors, and seasonal creations made with premium ingredients. Paired with sweeping views of Hannam-dong and the Han River, The Terrace Kitchen transforms each meal into an engaging, sensory experience — where great food, dynamic energy, and warm interaction come together. A celebration of live cooking and global flavors, The Terrace Kitchen is the newest signature experience at Grand Hyatt Seoul. \* For Public Holidays operation hours may vary depending on the situation.
