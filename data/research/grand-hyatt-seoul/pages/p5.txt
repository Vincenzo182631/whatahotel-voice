### Grand Hyatt Seoul

I have not been to this hotel in about 10 years. Since my last visit, the hotel has undergone renovations and upgrades throughout the facility, both indoors and outdoors. As a long-time returning guest, I got an upgraded room and access to the lounge. The service was impeccable, and the restaurants and bar area were very enjoyable. I even enjoyed the nice fall weather and took a morning hike in Namsan Park, right next to the hotel. The hotel is in a very quiet, exclusive part of Seoul, yet only a short taxi ride from fun areas like Itaewon and Myeongdong. I will start staying at the Grand Hyatt from here on.

### Top-notch stay at the Grand Hyatt

Had an absolutely fantastic 2-night stay at the Grand Hyatt Seoul this October. I was worried that the location being a bit out of the central area of the city might be problematic but it ended up being an asset. It was so peaceful, with astounding views. The service was absolutely top-notch. And Uber taxis were not expensive so trips into the center were fairly cheap and easy. Being a Globalist with Hyatt, we had access to the lounge which had incredible breakfast and evening snacks spreads. I used a Globalist suite upgrade and the suite, while not super large, was very well-designed and had a wall of floor to ceiling windows all along one wall of both the bedroom and living room, which offered stunning views overlooking the city but which the blackout shades covered completely to allow a great night’s sleep. I would return in a heartbeat. And I have to give one more shout out to the team — every staff member I encountered, from the front desk, housekeeping, lounge and fitness center/spa was friendly, professional, and eager to please. They seemed to genuinely enjoy working there and serving guests. I’ve traveled most of the world and this was one of the best hotels I’ve experienced.

### Good views of Seoul from a high hotel.

The Grand Hyatt Seoul lived up to its reputation as one of the best hotels in Seoul with excellent service and rooms. The rooms had firm beds and unusually soft pillows. Generally I prefer firmer pillows. The views from the room are unmatched, due to the high location of the hotel in the Namsan Hills area. Unusually for Seoul, the toilets had no bidet attached.
The breakfast buffet had a large variety of choices and coffee was even served to the table though there is a giant Nespresso machine available to use.
One main drawback is the high location of the Hotel with steeper than usual roads leading to the hotel necessitating the use of the Hotel Shuttles and taxis.
One good point is that Airport Limousine Bus 6702 stops directly in front of the hotel entrance, making for easy access to Incheon airport.
