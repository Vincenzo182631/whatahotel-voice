## Modern charm in Chengdu's Business Center

Our hotel is situated on the modern Chengdu Financial City Zone in the heart of South Chengdu. The Twin Tower is nearby and we are close to the tranquil Jiaozi Park and the fashionable shopping mall 'in99'. Enjoy our relaxing spa, restaurants, bars, pool, and gym with stunning views. Your personal concierge will plan custom excursions and fulfill special requests.

## Rooms & Suites

Stay the night in a spacious room or suite with elegant luxury design and skyline views. Each features concierge cabinets, chic brass and walnut finishes, a fully stocked minibar, and a marble spa bathroom with a soaking tub and twin vanities.

Choose Your Stay

## Dining & Drinks

Dining & Drinks

Dine at Waldorf Astoria Chengdu. Our five restaurants and bars, from afternoon tea at Peacock Alley to traditional Sichuanese at Infinite Luck, reflect our commitment to culinary excellence.

Discover Dining & Drinks

## Brasserie 99

Dine at a European style bistro with western and Sichuanese cuisine. More than 100 dishes are inspired by famous Sichuan traditional home style cuisines, such as small hot pots and street foods.

## Infinite Luck

Infinite Luck is one of the city's finest Chinese restaurants, Chef Tony Yang's traditional Sichuanese menu is sure to impress. Four contemporary private dining rooms are available.

## Limited Edition Sip Bar

Explore a world of artisanal spirits, wines, and beers in a refined setting.

## Peacock Alley

Peacock Alley provides an enchanting ambience to enjoy light bites to the Waldorf afternoon tea, which includes the signature Red Velvet Cake, or simply mingle with business associates over a glass of champagne in the cocktail lounge. Embrace the elegant afternoon tea time to the sound of live piano.

## Limited Edition Grill

Styled by renowned designer Jeffrey Beers, this American Grill restaurant offers a casual yet attentive dining experience in a relaxed setting with a show kitchen, which allows guests to observe the chefs at work. Sit back and relax with picturesque views of the city skyline for weekend brunch, business lunch, and dinner.
