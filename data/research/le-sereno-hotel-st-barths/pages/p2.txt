## Exclusive St Barths Villas

The Le Sereno Villas are considered by many to be the finest luxury accommodations in St. Barths.

These luxury villas in St .Barths offer the best of both worlds: the privacy and the expansiveness of a private villa combined with the amenities and service of a five-star hotel plus the attention to detail, design and service for which Le Sereno is known for.

## Le Sereno Villa

Each of the three Villas offer more than 7,000 square feet of indoor and outdoor space in a half an acre of private landscaped garden. Ideal for an exclusive St. Barths villa rental, each private villa features three to four bedrooms, large living areas, fully equipped kitchen, expansive terraces, landscaped gardens, private parking, furnished poolside deck, and heated private pools overlooking the magnificent views over the Grand Cul-de Sac. Guests feel thoroughly at home and completely pampered in Le Sereno Villas.

## Facts & Figures

- Three master bedrooms with en suite bathrooms, a private library that can become a fourth private bedroom with its own private bathroom.
- Wood-paneled Library with en-suite bathroom. Media room, surround-sound large screen home theater. Library can be outfitted with two twins or one king size bed. (270 sq ft; 25m2).
- Fully equipped designer kitchen with aluminum Schiffini cabinets by Vico Magistretti and appliances by Bosch, Pando and Ilve Italia.
- Main dining room (seats 10 comfortably).
- Very large living room with own powder room.
- Private swimming pool with panoramic ocean view.
- Pool deck with panoramic view furnished for sunbathing and reading.
- Large Covered terrace with panoramic ocean view with dining table (seats 8 comfortably) and lounge. Includes equipped wet-bar, ice maker, refrigerator and BBQ with separate powder room.
- All bedrooms equipped with state-of-the-art audiovisual equipment (flat-screen TV, stereo and bathroom speakers) and all public areas have built-in Bose speakers linked to a central stereo controller.
- Generous closet space in all rooms.
