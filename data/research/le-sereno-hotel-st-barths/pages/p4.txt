Bringing new meaning to refined living and modern design, Il Sereno stands out as a luxury hotel in Lake Como, breathing fresh air into this bucket‑list destination. The award‑winning designer Patricia Urquiola has combined the natural ambiance of the surrounding environment with comfort and luxury, resulting in a design that embodies all spectrums of luxurious accommodation.

From the banks of Lake Como’s eastern shore, Sereno Hotels introduces its newest addition, Il Sereno Lago di Como. Known as one of the best hotels in Lake Como, this property embodies the same ethos as its sister property, Le Sereno in St. Barths. Stretching 450 feet of simple elegance along the pristine shoreline, Il Sereno offers exceptional service, world-renowned design, and an unmatched location. Step out of the ordinary and into a world of effortless relaxation and timeless beauty.
