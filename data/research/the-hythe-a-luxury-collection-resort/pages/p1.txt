# Welcome to The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail

## Experience our legendary Vail, CO hotel resort

The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail channels the exhilarating spirit of Vail with a finely curated alpine experience. From cultivated European, Swiss-inspired rituals to locally crafted details,...See More discover the definition of legendary hospitality at this rarefied, pet-friendly resort in Vail, CO. Here, you will experience the adventurous side of Vail, with easy access to the Eagle Bahn Gondola, Lionshead Village and Betty Ford Alpine Village. Our spacious accommodations are a tranquil retreat after a day of slope-side adventures. Luxe suites boast whirlpool jet bathtubs and two balconies showcasing spectacular mountain views. Our Vail, CO, family resort is home to four food and beverage concepts, Well & Being Spa featuring Vail's only Himalayan Salt Lounge, 29,000 square feet of unparalleled event space and a four-season pool. Set amid the majestic snow-covered Rocky Mountains and a convivial après-all-day atmosphere, The Hythe Resort is where old and new legends come to life in Vail, CO.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail?

The check-in time at The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail?

On-site valet parking is available at The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail booking.

What is the closest airport near The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail?

The closest airport near The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail is Eagle County Airport (EGE). EGE is located approximately 34.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
