### Daily Epicurean Moments

Enjoy our complimentary daily epicurean moments, including fireside s’mores and tastings of locally distilled 10th Mountain Whiskey. Our signature epicurean experiences are designed to celebrate the endless possibilities of our legendary mountain town and inspire you to discover your own unique connection to Vail.

### Complimentary Outdoor Yoga

Fresh air and flow with an all-levels yoga class on the Après Deck offered seasonally in the summer months. Start your day with an energizing and complimentary yoga class. Mats provided while supply lasts.

### Perfectly Paired Experiences

We’ve created custom-curated experiences for you to discover the best of Vail in summer: a Farmers’ Market excursion and exclusive golf access to guided hikes, rafting, biking and more. Your favorite summer memories begin at The Hythe.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort, Vail offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Stair Climber, Peloton Stationary Bikes, FitnessOnDemand, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
