Opened by actor William Holden in 1959 at the base of Mount Kenya, this equatorial property has over 100 acres of lawns, streams, and gardens. Rooms have hardwood floors, verandas, and fireplaces, and the hotel’s two restaurants have views of Mount Kenya.
