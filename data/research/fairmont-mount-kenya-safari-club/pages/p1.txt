## A _Timeless_ Encounter with Nature’s Rarest Wonders

At the foothills of majestic Mount Kenya, Fairmont Mount Kenya Safari Club offers more than a stay it’s a soulful return to nature. Within the Mount Kenya Wildlife Conservancy, witness firsthand the rewilding of the critically endangered Mountain Bongo and visit the on-site Wildlife Rescue Center. From horseback safaris to forest trails, every moment invites you to reconnect, reflect, and help leave a legacy of conservation wrapped in Fairmont’s iconic charm and quiet luxury.

### Step into Sustainability & Unparalleled Luxury

Fairmont Mount Kenya Safari Club is a retreat rich in heritage where Samburu and Kikuyu traditions meet rare wildlife and rewilding efforts. Overlooking Mount Kenya, our elegant rooms and suites offer a serene base to explore forest trails, the Wildlife Rescue Center, and vibrant cultural encounters. Here, every moment tells a story worth remembering.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Mount Kenya Safari Club located?

Fairmont Mount Kenya Safari Club is located in Nanyuki, Kenya, nestled in the lush foothills of Mount Kenya and just a few miles from the Equator. This luxurious retreat offers stunning views, elegant accommodations, and a variety of exciting outdoor activities in a serene natural setting.

What are some of the property amenities at Fairmont Mount Kenya Safari Club?

Our hotel offers an array of luxurious amenities, including elegantly furnished rooms, suites, and cottages with modern amenities, a pristine golf course, a spa, a heated outdoor pool, gourmet dining options, horseback riding, nature walks, and an animal orphanage.

Is there a concierge service at Fairmont Mount Kenya Safari Club?

We have exceptional concierge services. Our dedicated team is ready to assist with personalized travel arrangements, local excursions, restaurant reservations, and any special requests to ensure your stay is comfortable, enjoyable, and memorable.

Does Fairmont Mount Kenya Safari Club have a swimming pool?

Our hotel features a luxurious outdoor heated pool. Guests can relax and unwind while enjoying the stunning views of the surrounding landscapes. The heated pool provides comfort and leisure year-round, making it a perfect spot for relaxation and enjoyment during your 5-star stay.

How can I get to Fairmont Mount Kenya Safari Club from the nearest airport?

The nearest airport is Nanyuki Airstrip, approximately 15 km (9 mi) away, a convenient 30-minute drive. Additionally, the hotel has its own airstrip for private charters. For international travelers, Jomo Kenyatta International Airport in Nairobi is about 200 km (124 mi) away, a 3-hour drive.

Does Fairmont Mount Kenya Safari Club have a spa?

We have the luxurious Riuki spa offering a range of rejuvenating treatments. Guests can enjoy massages, facials, and body treatments in a tranquil setting, perfect for relaxation and wellness. The spa provides a serene escape amidst the stunning natural beauty of Mount Kenya.
