# The St. Regis Downtown Dubai Dining

## Dining

Reflecting the hotel’s cultural heritage, The St. Regis Downtown Dubai brings together restaurants and bars with distinct character. Overlooking the Dubai Water Canal, Ginori Terrace by Ginori 1735 serves St. Regis Afternoon Tea on signature porcelain. BASTA! combines the spirit of a Florentine steakhouse and Neapolitan pizzeria with modern Italian cuisine, while TABŪ presents bold contemporary Japanese...See More flavors on the mezzanine. The St. Regis Bar offers cocktails in an intimate setting, Poolside Bar pairs light meals with canal and skyline views, and The Champagne Bar offers an elegant setting for premium champagne and refined drinks. ## Ginori Terrace

Overlooking the Dubai Canal, Ginori Terrace by the iconic Ginori 1735 brings Italian artistry and playfulness to the Best Address in Downtown Dubai. A celebration of la dolce vita, this elegant yet vibrant destination offers breakfast, lunch and dinner on exquisite Ginori 1735 porcelain with décor from the brand’s Domus collection. From the storied St Regis Afternoon Tea ritual to exclusive Aperitivo evenings, every moment is a blend of luxury, design and culinary excellence.
