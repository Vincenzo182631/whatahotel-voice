## Accommodations

Drawing inspiration from the brand’s rich heritage, the classic aesthetic of the hotel exudes vibrance and comfort through its curated palette of elegant fabrics. Amongst its signature suites is the Royal Suite, boasting a size of 305 sqm and comprising a spacious living room and a comfortable dining area with a fully equipped kitchen, bar and dining table.
