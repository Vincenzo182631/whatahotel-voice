## Experiences

In the heart of Dubai’s financial downtown district, The St. Regis Downtown Dubai is set along the Dubai Water Canal on Marasi Drive, steps from Dubai Mall. Burj Khalifa is five minutes away, and Dubai International Airport is 15 minutes from the hotel. Guests value the prime location for business travel and city breaks, with easy access to Downtown Dubai’s landmarks. ## The St. Regis Spa

The St. Regis Spa, the first of its kind, features six treatment suites and offers bespoke signature treatments by trained expert therapists, including Flotation Pod therapy and a traditional Arabian Hammam. For an experience tailored to each guest, their wellness journey will include detox juices, teas, and coffee amenities. Every spa treatment is provided with an additional 30-minute sensory experience to relax in the en-suite steam room or sauna. For the ultimate sensorial escape, our spa also offers a range of mani-pedi services, ensuring that every detail of their wellness journey is nothing short of sublime.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Downtown Dubai have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Downtown Dubai offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Leg Extension,Adjustable Pully,Chest Bench,Vertical Traction, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Downtown Dubai have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Downtown Dubai has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Downtown Dubai have an on-site spa?

Yes, The St. Regis Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Downtown Dubai. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Downtown Dubai?

Dubai aquarium and underwater zoo, The Dubai fountain show, Burj Khalifa, Dubai Opera, Museum of the Future and more are nearby The St. Regis Downtown Dubai.
