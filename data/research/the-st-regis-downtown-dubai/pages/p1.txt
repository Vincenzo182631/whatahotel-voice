# Welcome to The St. Regis Downtown Dubai

## Live Exquisite at our 5-star Downtown Dubai hotel

The St. Regis Downtown Dubai is a 5-star Dubai hotel with 298 rooms and suites overlooking the Dubai Water Canal and Burj Khalifa. Just five minutes from Dubai Mall, Dubai Fountains, and Dubai Opera, and 15 minutes from Dubai International Financial Centre and Dubai World Trade Center, this luxury hotel in downtown Dubai and Business Bay is ideally placed for leisure stays and business travel. Our signature St. Regis Butler Service delivers round-the-clock care, while guests can enjoy Ginori Terrace, BASTA!, and TABŪ, with traditions including afternoon tea, our iconic Bloody Mary, and champagne sabering. Guests praise the attentive service, prime location, and scenic views. Unwind at the infinity pool or The St. Regis Spa with tailored treatments and traditional hammams.

Advisory - Travel to Middle East

We continue to closely monitor the current regional situation in the Middle East as it evolves. Our hotel remains open and operational and our teams are focused on supporting our guests at this time.

The St. Regis Traditions

### St. Regis Butler Service

The foundation of our brand, the St. Regis Butler service is aiming to tend to your every need and create a unique and unforgettable experience. A service beyond expectation available to all guests that will personally assist with packing sevices, beverage service, garment pressing or any other prompt assistance. For more information, please email SR.DXBDX.Butler@stregis.com.

### Afternoon Tea

Rituals are an integral part of the St. Regis Brand and every St. Regis hotel globally offers its own interpretation of the renowned Afternoon Tea. Sample the high tea experience with a selection of finger sandwiches, pastries, cakes and fresh scones.
Daily from 2pm to 6pm. For more information, please email SR.DXBDX.concierge@stregis.com.

### Evening Ritual

Dating back to the battles of Napoleon Bonaparte, who famously opened champagne with his saber in both victory and defeat, the art of sabrage is the signature ceremony of the St. Regis Evening Rituals series. Take part in a rich celebration of storied history with champagne sabering at St. Regis Bar. Daily at 7pm. For more information, please email SR.DXBDX.concierge@stregis.com.

### Marasi Mary

Locally inspired interpretation of the brand’s signature cocktail, Bloody Mary. Marasi Mary is imbued with the aromas of tomato, Arabic savory spices and a hint of watermelon. Refreshingly refined and light, Marasi Mary reflects the modern waterfront vibes of Dubai Downtown and the rich traditional flavors of Arabic heritage in one elegant glass. Served at The St. Regis Bar.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Downtown Dubai?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Downtown Dubai is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Downtown Dubai allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Downtown Dubai.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Downtown Dubai?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Downtown Dubai. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Downtown Dubai have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Downtown Dubai has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Downtown Dubai booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Downtown Dubai?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Downtown Dubai is Dubai International Airport (DXB). DXB is located approximately 15.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Downtown Dubai have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Downtown Dubai has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
