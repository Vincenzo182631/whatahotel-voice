Safety First

Kierland FlowRider™ is only accessible to guests of The Westin Kierland Resort & Spa and The Westin Kierland Villas.

Reservations required, only checked in registered guests will be able to register for a Kierland FlowRider™ session.

Riders must be at least 42″ tall to bodyboard (safety standard).

Riders must be at least 52″ tall to flowboard (safety standard).

All riders 17 and younger must have liability waiver form signed by a legal guardian.

All adult riders must sign a liability waiver form.

Please arrive 20 minutes prior to scheduled time for waiver review and to receive a wristband.

Limit one rider per lane at a time (Four riders minimum to run; Eight riders maximum per lane).

Hours of Operation

For hours of operation, please email FUN@kierlandresort.com.

Rates & Lessons

Ride rates are subject to change seasonally.

Please note only checked in registered guests will be able to register for a Kierland FlowRider session.

All rides must be cancelled by 6 p.m. the day prior to scheduled ride time or the full amount will be charged to the guest’s folio at checkout.

For Kierland FlowRider™ session rates and more information, please email FUN@kierlandresort.com.

### Kids Compass Quest

The Kierland Compass provides a fun way for families to explore the resort by embarking on a property-wide scavenger hunt to search Prospector Pete’s hidden treasure. ## Discover Scottsdale

A visit to Scottsdale offers endless opportunities for exploration. Book your stay at our Scottsdale spa resort.

Learn More

Jeep, Hummer or ATV Tours

Cover wild desert terrain in powerful recreational vehicles, see stunning scenery and learn all about Arizona. Tours last 4 to 5 hours, and pick up from main lobby is available.

The Grand Canyon Experience

Take a trip to Northern Arizona for one of the most magnificent views in the world. Customize your journey to include a tour upon arrival or even helicopter ride!

McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park

The McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park is not only full of activities and great recreational fun, but also a historical and educational experience for all ages. Enjoy train rides, an antique carousel, historical buildings and actual railroad cars.

Horseback Riding

Saddle up and see the West! Even novice riders will enjoy the views of the Sonoran Desert from a guided trail ride on horseback. Half-day and full-day rides are available.

Scottsdale Quarter Shopping

One of the area’s most unique shopping, dining and entertainment destinations. Inspired by quaint city districts, the Quarter offers a streetscape of cafes and stores ranging from high-tech to high-fashion including Apple, H&M, West Elm, Armani Exchange and Nike.

See More

Arizona Casino

Have a little gambling fun and catch a Vegas-style show! Enjoy 1,500 slot machines including 80 high-limit slots, live Blackjack, 50 poker tables and live fast-action Keno.

Desert Botanical Gardens

The Desert Botanical Garden hosts one of the world’s finest collections of desert plants. One of only 44 botanical gardens accredited by the American Association of Museums, this one-of-a-kind museum showcases 50 acres of beautiful outdoor exhibits.

Heard Museum

One of the Phoenix area’s first cultural attractions and the world’s finest destinations for learning about American Indian arts and cultures, the Heard Museum is a renowned for showcasing the history and art of the native tribes of the Southwest.

Taliesin West

Built in the late 1930s by legendary American architect Frank Lloyd Wright, this cluster of buildings is now a National Historic Landmark. Patterned after the flow and feeling of the desert surrounding it, this school was also the architect’s favorite building.

The Phoenix Zoo

Voted one of the nation’s “Top 5 Zoos for Kids,” The Phoenix Zoo offers everything from the “Arizona Trail” for a look at Arizona native species, a Safari Train, giraffe feeding, the “Wild about Animals Show,” Monkey Village and more!

Cracker Jax Family & Fun Sport Park

Kids of all ages can enjoy batting cages, Go-karts, bumper boats, miniature golf courses, double-tiered driving range, arcade and snack bar.

See More

On-Demand Shopping Shuttle Available

On-demand shopping shuttle to Kierland Commons and Scottsdale Quarter – easy, convenient access to top shopping, dining and entertainment.
