THE WESTIN KIERLAND RESORT & SPA

# Experience Scottsdale

### Discover The Sonoran Desert

Retreat to The Westin Kierland Resort & Spa and experience a refreshing environment designed with your well-being in mind. Located in Scottsdale, Arizona, near the Kierland Commons outdoor dining and shopping center, our Scottsdale retreat resort offers a stimulating escape in the Sonoran Desert. Lounge in the shade of private cabanas or bring the whole family to our sprawling Adventure Water Park, featuring thrilling aquatic activities for all ages. Play a round of golf at the award-winning Westin Kierland Golf Club; book rejuvenating treatments at Agave, The Arizona Spa & Salon; and enjoy a taste of Southwest at our bevy of on-site restaurants. After a day of fun in the sun, relax in recently reimagined guest rooms and suites featuring plush Heavenly® Beds and signature bath products. From check-in to checkout, enjoy a complete sense of freedom at our Scottsdale resort with a water park.

## INSPIRE ALL YOUR SENSES

Savor the delightful on-site dining options at our Scottsdale retreat resort, including Arizona-inspired cuisine, casual café fare, 350 whisky labels at the Scotch Library, and more. Shop at nearby Kierland Commons or Scottsdale Quarter, or catch spring training at one of several local stadiums. Go on a hiking or biking adventure at Scottsdale's McDowell Sonoran Preserve, Pinnacle Peak Park, and Cave Creek Regional Park. On Thursday through Saturday evenings, head back to Dreamweaver's Canyon where a bagpiper plays at sunset.

Learn More

## Adventure Water Park

Play or unwind all afternoon at our Adventure Water Park, which features the Kierland FlowRider flowboard simulator for surfing in the desert. Float down the lazy river, take a ride down a 110-foot waterslide, or lounge alongside the Aguamiel pool, open for adults only. Families at our Scottsdale resort with water park can enjoy plenty of space to relax in one-of-a-kind lounge areas and cabanas like Tommy Bahama Relaxation Reef, a private "island" within our lazy river.

Learn More

## Golf Your Way

Enjoy a round at The Westin Kierland Golf Club, where we provide an elevated golf experience across three distinctive nine-hole courses. Designed by renowned Scottsdale golf architect Scott Miller, this exceptional club is enhanced by spectacular scenery. Here, you can play around serene lakes, dry desert washes, and flowering Arizona flora and fauna.

Learn More

## Renew Body & Mind

At Agave, The Arizona Spa, you can book an array of services, including massages and facials. Many treatments also offer the moisturizing benefits of the Agave plant, which is indigenous to the Scottsdale area. In addition to custom body treatments, the spa also provides premium salon services that revive hair, skin and nails.
