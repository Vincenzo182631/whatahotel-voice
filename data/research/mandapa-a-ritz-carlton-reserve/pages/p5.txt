Spa
A destination for wellness and spirituality, Bali carries a long tradition of healing and inner reflection. Our Ubud resort spa draws on these practices to offer a modern, luxury retreat amid the lush rainforest. Balinese-inspired treatments feature Subtle Energies and locally sourced products, and unique opportunities include a session with a local Balinese healer. The riverfront spa is a complete wellness destination; in addition to its five treatment rooms, it features a yoga pavilion, meditation temple, vitality pool, 24-hour fitness center, saunas, and relaxation areas.

Living in Harmony
Spa Treatments
In Bali, the concept of living in harmony based on ancient science philosophies and Balinese healing tradition plays a very significant role. According to the ancient Sanskrit text – Upanishads, within these bodies, there are five different dimensions. Our physical body expands into more subtle layers of energy around our spiritual center. These five distinct bodies that surround our soul are called koshas or sheaths. Each kosha vibrates at different speeds, they interact and overlap with each other, ranging from gross to transcendental dimensions. This is the road map that ancient seers have left us to understand our journey back to wholeness so that we can break free from all bondage. Drawing inspiration from the rich local culture and incorporating Subtle Energies natural products, Mandapa Spa treatments blend authentic Balinese massage techniques and powerful ingredients to rejuvenate and replenish the body.

Balinese Blessing Rituals
Melukat Geni - Fire Blessing Ritual
Melukat Geni is a Balinese sacred healing fire ceremony originating from the ancient Vedic texts. It is a process of purifying unwanted beliefs, habits or thought patterns to be transformed into more positive energy. This fire-healing ritual is known to be effective in helping one let go of unwanted aspects in their lives and manifest new intentions and new life. The ritual begins with meditation around an altar of fire, accompanied by the ringing of a Genta (sacred hand bell) and divine chanting of Sanskrit mantras for health and abundance. Fruits, grains, lentils, and other foods are ceremoniously offered as you manifest and seek spiritual purity and harmony.

Traditional Balinese Healing Touch
Bali is known for its rich culture, traditions and holistic healing. The unique healing distilled from the depths of ancient cultural practices. We have hand-selected truly remarkable local healers to share their gifted talents that complement the mystical culture of Bali.
Ibu Ketut Mursi - Intuitive Blind Healer
120 minutes
A unique synergy of healing energy is shared by two dedicated practitioners: Ketut Mursi, a visually impaired healer, and Ketut Kis, a physically impaired healer. Together, they bring their strengths into Healing for Awareness (Eling) through the practice of Prana Kombi Massage. This therapy is designed to gently support the central nervous system, peripheral nerves, and upper nerve pathways, helping the body and mind return to balance. During each session, both healers will carry out a careful assessment, tuning into the vibrations they perceive. They analyze and respond to these subtle signals, working with the interconnected elements of energy, matter, light, and inner consciousness. Their combined approach creates a powerful harmony that encourages awareness, restores equilibrium, and nurtures deep inner healing.
Panca Mayakosha Healing
60/90 minutes
Each session begins with prayer and meditation to create a calm, peaceful space. This helps balance the body’s five elements and connect with the five layers of being that support self-awareness and inner understanding. During the session, the practitioner uses gentle hand placements to support energy flow and clear imbalances. Massage techniques are included based on the guest’s needs, helping to relax muscles, improve circulation, and ground the body. By combining energy work and massage, the session releases emotional, mental, and physical tension, guiding the body and mind back to balance, harmony, and overall wellbeing.
Pranic Acupuncture Healing
75/90 minutes
This therapeutic journey nurtures both the subtle energy system and the physical body. Each session begins with pranic healing to rebalance the aura and harmonize the body’s chi, allowing life force energy to flow more freely. Gentle acupuncture is then applied to release energy blockages along the body’s meridians by precisely placing fine needles, restoring balance and vitality. To complete the experience, the
practitioner incorporates a signature healing massage. With intuitive touch, this massage eases muscular tension, improves circulation, and fosters a sense of lightness.
Alternative Healing Energy
Inspired by the Balinese word Ubad, which means medicine, Ubud was originally known as a hub for energetic holistic healers. Mandapa offers a range of indigenous healing practices performed by experienced masters, focusing on a subtle emotional connection that transcends the physical approach.
Acupuncture
60 minutes
Acupuncture is a method that encourages the body to promote natural healing and enhance its functions. This ancient Chinese technique involves the insertion of thin needles at specific points on the skin to stimulate the flow of energy, known as Qi. It is believed that disruptions in the flow of energy can lead to imbalances and diseases.
Reiki
60 minutes
Reiki consists of two words: Rei, meaning God’s Wisdom or the Higher Power, and Ki, which refers to life force energy. Therefore, Reiki can be defined as spiritually guided life force energy. It promotes a radiant glow that flows through the body, mind, emotions, and soul, leading to benefits such as relaxation, a sense of peace, security, and overall well-being.
Hasta Samudrika & Wariga Reading
60 minutes
Experience a unique Balinese tradition that combines Hasta Samudrika (the ancient art of palm reading) with Wariga (Balinese numerology and life path guidance). Through the lines and shapes of your hands, the practitioner can reveal insights into your personality, health, and life tendencies. Wariga then complements this with guidance based on traditional calculations of time, cycles, and destiny. Together, these practices provide a holistic perspective, helping you discover your inner strengths, life direction, and harmony with the natural rhythms of the universe.

Prior to Arrival
How can I make a reservation?
Please reserve your treatment(s) in advance by contacting extension 6000 from your room or (62) 361 4792777 ext 6000 from outside, or by email to
rc.dpsub.spa.reception@ritzcarlton.com
When should I arrive?
Please arrive at least 15 minutes prior to your scheduled treatment time.
Should I shave and shower before a treatment?
Shaving is recommended but not necessary for men prior to a facial. If you do choose to shave prior to your facial, be sure to do so at least 8 hours prior to your scheduled appointment. Shaving is not recommended prior to any body treatments or hair removal services.
What should I wear during my treatment?
We ask that you disrobe completely for your body treatment(s). For your comfort, disposable underwear and a bathrobe are provided. Our therapists are trained to appropriately drape you for privacy.
What about my jewelry and valuables?
We advise you to remove all jewelry prior to your arrival at the Spa.
Who can guide me in choosing my treatments?
Our Spa receptionist will help you plan the perfect Spa experience, including the best order in which to enjoy your treatments.
Spa Policies
Is there a minimum age for treatments?
Guests must be at least 16 years of age to enjoy spa treatments. Minors aged below 16 may receive treatments accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.
What is the cancellation policy?
For cancellations or changes in appointments, a minimum 12-hour advanced notice is required in order to avoid being charged in full for any scheduled appointment, program or service not attended.
What if I am late for my appointment?
Arriving late will shorten your treatment. Please remember that your treatment must end on time to prevent further delays, regardless of how late you start.
What about payment for Spa services?
All major credit cards, cash with proper identification are accepted. Spa services can also be charged to your hotel bill. For your convenience, payment is accepted prior to treatments at the time of check-in. Please present discounts, gift vouchers and gift certificates at the time services are rendered. Discounts do not apply to spa packages, promotions, special events or holidays.
What about gratuities?
You may opt to leave additional gratuity for your therapist.
May I bring my cellphone?
Our Spa promotes an environment of tranquility and relaxation. Please respect other guests’ rights to privacy and peace. The use of cellular phones and electronic devices is prohibited in Mandapa Spa.
May I smoke or drink alcohol in the Spa?
Smoking and alcoholic beverages are not permitted in Mandapa Spa. We recommend that you refrain from consuming alcohol within eight hours of your treatment.
