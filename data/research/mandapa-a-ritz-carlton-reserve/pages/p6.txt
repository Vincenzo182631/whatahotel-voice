Discover our resort near Ayung River
Experiences
Visitors are drawn to Bali for different reasons. Some seek a retreat, while others desire to connect with a culture whose traditions live on. Woven through each experience is the ability to let go of what is known and surrender to whatever may be, whether happening at an Ubud temple, catching the sunrise from atop Mount Batur or enjoying a dance lesson in Bali. Our resort near Ayung River offers an array of activities and can arrange for visits to nearby attractions and points of interest.
Immersive Experiences
Discover Tranquil Journeys
Immerse yourself in the serene beauty of Mandapa, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, where curated experiences connect you to the heart of Ubud. From sacred river rituals and guided rice field walks to holistic wellness and authentic Balinese culture, each moment is thoughtfully designed to inspire harmony and renewal. Discover personalized journeys that reflect the spirit of Mandapa—where nature, tradition, and luxury come together in perfect balance.

Signature Experiences
Arak Masterclass
Discover the heritage of Balinese Arak in an immersive masterclass, followed by a 3-course dining experience at Ambar with each course thoughtfully paired to complement this traditional spirit.
Jagi Melukat
Embark on a spiritual journey by crafting Canang Sari, a palm-leaf offering with flowers and incense symbolizing gratitude in Balinese culture. Continue with Melukat, a sacred water purification ritual at Gunung Kawi Sebatu temple, guided by a priest. This experience is conducted in traditional attire provided by the resort.
Hasta Samudrika & Wariga Reading
Discover a unique Balinese tradition combining Hasta Samudrika (palm reading) and Wariga (numerology and life path guidance). Through the lines of your hands and traditional calculations of time and cycles, gain insight into your personality, strengths, and life direction—offering a deeper sense of balance and connection with the universe.
Echoes from the Past: an Ubud Historical Walk
This guided walking tour traces Ubud’s evolution from a 19th-century royal seat to today’s vibrant art destination. Begin with a tasting of locally grown Balinese coffee, then visit Puri Saren Agung, the historic royal palace. Conclude at Museum Puri Lukisan, Bali’s oldest art museum, founded in 1953.
UNESCO Cultural Heritage Excursion
Experience a heritage journey to two UNESCO-listed sites: Jatiluwih and Batukaru Temple. Explore Jatiluwih’s expansive rice terraces, where the ancient Subak irrigation system is still practiced at the foothills of Mount Batukaru. Continue to Batukaru Temple, an 8th-century sanctuary dedicated to Mahadewa, symbolizing the source of life.
Waterfall Chaser: Into Bali’s Hidden Cascades
Discover the hidden waterfalls of Munduk on a full-day trek through the highlands of northern Bali. Journey through clove and coffee plantations, lush जंगल, and flowing streams, with stops at three scenic waterfalls: Red Coral, Melanting, and the multi-tiered Labuan Kebo.
Ring of Fire: Land Rover Series Volcano Exploration
Embark on a journey into Bali’s “Ring of Fire” with a Land Rover adventure across Mount Batur’s black lava fields. Explore the western slopes and active crater, with views of lava from past eruptions and geothermal steam vents, while learning its spiritual significance. Conclude with a refreshing stop at Pelisan Waterfall, set amidst lush rainforest.

The journey includes a vintage Volkswagen ride, a rice field walk, and a visit to Gunung Kawi Sebatu, an 11th-century water temple complex.

Mandapa Spa

Mandapa Signatures Activities
Vintage Tour: Journey to Bali in 1930s
5 hours
Journey back to the 1930s in a corner of Keliki Village, to a period known as the ‘Renaissance period’ for Balinese art. This guided art class explores a unique, intricate art capturing rural village life, natural landscapes, and various myths and legends, drawn with fine black ink and painted with acrylic on miniature paper.
Keliki village is rich in culture and history, with its roots tracing back to the 9th century. Whisked away in a vintage Volkswagen 181, this experience also includes a walk through a rice field and a visit to the 11th-century Gunung Kawi Sebatu water temple complex, with rock-cut shrines, known as one of Bali's most significant archaeological sites.
Vintage Tour: Hidden Jewel
5 hours
Discover the hidden gems of Bali, including the unique linear architecture of Bali Aga Village, the impressive multi-tiered shrines of Kehen Temple, and the 'King's Cave springs' at Goa Raja waterfall, all curated by local guides. The journey begins on a vintage Volkswagen 181 to of the Bali Aga region, home to a group of indigenous people who have lived there since before the massive Majapahit Empire invasion in 1343 AD. The Pura Kehen Temple, a 13th-century coronation temple, sacred fire symbolism, and Chinese porcelain artifacts. The tour ends with a swim in a cave-like waterfall, cascading from a 20-meter vertical cliff.
Echoes from the Past: an Ubud Historical Walk
3 hours
This walking tour explores the transformation of Ubud from a 19th-century royal seat to today's modern art city. Begin this guided tour by savoring the flavors and stories behind Balinese-grown coffee beans. Next, visit Puri Saren Agung, the royal palace, symbol of Ubud's royal history. Finally, conclude the tour at the Puri Lukisan Museum, founded in 1953 by Ubud's prince Cokorda Gde Agung Sukawati and Dutch artist Rudolf Bonnet. This museum is the oldest art museum in Bali.
Mandapa Action & Adventure
Waterfall Chaser: Into Bali’s Hidden Cascades
8 hours
Discover the hidden waterfalls of Munduk on this full-day trekking expedition through the highlands of northern Bali. Traverse clove and coffee plantations, dense jungles, and streams. The journey includes visits to three beautiful waterfalls: the stunning single-stream Red Corral and Melanting waterfalls, as well as the multi-tiered Labuan Kebo waterfall.
Ring of Fire Expedition: Volcano Exploration with Land Rover Series
8 hours
Embark on a journey to the heart of the Ring of Fire, renowned for its active volcanoes and black lava fields. Experience a geographical marvel that combines breathtaking landscapes, adventure, and Balinese culture in a Land Rover jeep. This tour weaves together elements of geology, history, and spirituality. Explore the western slopes and the active crater area of Mount Batur, where you will see charred lava fields from the 2000 eruption and geothermal steam vents. Also learn about the mountain's spiritual significance in Balinese Hinduism, regarded as the home of the goddess Dewi Danu. Catch the sunrise by the lava field. The Land Rover will transport you to the highland slopes, followed by a guided trek through steam vents and sulfurous craters. On the way back, dust off and refreshed by visit Pelisan Waterfall, a waterfall that source by the sacreed spring and surounded by the rainforest.
Kampung Bali Cycling
4 hours
Experience the joy of riding on this guided bicycle tour through hidden trails, dirt paths, plantations, and villages, past cascading rice terraces with stunning views of distant volcanic peaks and lush valleys in the Taro district. Along the way, learning about the Subak system and the spiritual connection between Balinese Hinduism and the land. Taro village has a rich history, existing for over 2,000 years. It is one of Bali's earliest farming communities and is famously associated with Resi Markandya, a priest from Java, who helped establish the city and introduced Shivaism. For individuals aged 10 years to under 65 years old only.
Exclusive Ayung River Journey
3 hours
This white-water rafting adventure on the Ayung River, the longest river in Bali, will take you through breathtaking landscapes. Witness diverse flora and fauna, towering trees, and stunning waterfalls, all while enjoying intricate stone carvings of the epic tales of Ramayana and Mahabharata. The rafting concludes at the Mandapa river pier. Perfect for families, the journey to the rafting point begins in a classic Volkswagen. For individuals aged 5 years to under 65 years old only.
Spiritual & Cultural
Enchanting Love Journey
1 hour ritual with Priest, followed by dinner
Experience romance and togetherness with a Melukat, a Balinese sacred water purification ceremony held in the resort's private temple, Padmasana, led by a priest. In Balinese culture, a purification ritual prepares couples for their new journey together as a family. Following the ceremony, enjoy a private dining experience by the rice paddies. A professional photographer will capture your special moments throughout.
Jagi Melukat
4 hours
Embark on a transformative spiritual pilgrimage, learning to craft 'Canang Sari,' a miniature palm-leaf woven basket filled with vibrant flowers and incense, a gesture of gratitude to the gods ingrained in Balinese life. Followed by Melukat, a sacred water purification ritual at the ancient water temple, Gunung Kawi Sebatu, performed by a temple priest. Locals frequent this temple to seek strength and clarity. This experience is performed in local cultural attire, provided by the resort.
Educative Excursion
Desa Bali Cultural Walk
2 hours
Explore the rich heritage of Begawan Village in Payangan, as if you have been transported back in time. The local Balinese villages are known for their family temples, communal halls, and terraced homes, all set against a backdrop of rice fields and traditional irrigation canals. The island's Tri Hita Karana philosophy is deeply rooted in these communities, fostering a harmonious relationship among animals, humans, and the spiritual realm.
UNESCO Cultural Heritage Excursion
6 hours
Traverse fields of rice terraces with soaring mountains in the background as you discover the heritage of rice cultivation and the traditions of Subak farming with our local guide in Jatiluwih, a UNESCO Cultural Heritage Site since 2012, covering over 600 hectares of rice paddies. The heritage tour continues with a visit to Taman Ayun, a Hindu royal temple built in 1634 by the Mengwi Kingdom. Water plays a vital role here, integral to rituals and community life even today. The temple is part of the wider Subak system, a traditional network linking water temples, farmers, and rice fields, reflecting an intricate relationship between water, community, and spirituality in sustaining the surrounding landscape.

Local Art Session
2 hours
This guided Keliki-style painting session is designed for children aged 8 to 12. Conducted by a local artist from the Keliki village, participants will use water-based paints and paper to create landscapes that reflect the region's culture. This creative experience will encourage children to challenge their imagination and develop their artistic skills while learning about the traditional styles of Keliki, a distinguished form of art that uses ink and pigment on small paper to capture the island life and its myths and legends.
Little Explorer: Mandapa Camp Bonfire
2 hours
Join us for an exciting afternoon-to-evening adventure designed for little explorers and families. Experience a trek through the resort's private rice fields, where you will have the chance to spot wildlife and learn about the rice-growing culture. As the evening sets in, gather around a bonfire for storytelling while enjoying delicious BBQ treats, marshmallows, and warm cocoa.

Resort Activities
Subak Farming & Rice Pounding
A meaningful connection to Bali’s rich agricultural heritage. Guided by local farmers, the experience invites guests to explore the traditional Subak irrigation system and take part in hands-on rice pounding, celebrating the island’s deep relationship with nature, community, and culture.
Herbal Tea Making
Every sip is a poetry of flavor. Discover the art of herbal tea brews, fragrant cups infused with notes of botanical wonders from Bali. Explore the fascinating world of tea with our tea expert. Start the experience by picking fresh herbs and flowers from the garden, followed by an interactive session in the Library.
Sawah Walk
Sawah Walk invites guests to wander through lush rice fields while discovering the beauty of Bali’s natural landscape and local traditions. Guided by our team, the experience offers a peaceful journey through the heart of Ubud’s countryside.
UNESCO Sites
The Jatiluwih Rice Terraces located in the Tabanan region and are among the most striking examples of terraced agriculture in the world. Arguably Bali’s oldest and most complex example of the Subak rice terrace system of agriculture. Taman Ayun Temple is a Royal Temple of Mengwi Empire located in Mengwi Village and functions as a temple to pray to the god in manifestation (Wisnu) at the top of Mangu mount.
Ubud Village
For thousands of years, Ubud has been the spiritual and cultural hub of Hindu Bali. Witness these, as you walk around visiting Ubud Palace, Saraswati Temple’s Lotus Pond, selfie time at Pura Gunung Lebah at Ubud’s famous Dutch bridge, Ubud Market and local Art Galleries. Your Patih can also arrange private appointment with Ubud many artists.Ubud Monkey Forest is a nature reserve and Hindu temple complex.
Tirta Empul Temple
Experience Tirta Empul Temple Holy Spring. Built in 926 AD, this iconic temple is famous for its purifying holy water, which attracts devotees, particularly during full and new moons, who come for blessings. Appointments for this once-in-a-lifetime experience can be arranged through your personal butler.
