Unwind in a Tropical Paradise
Signature Villas
Mandapa, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve offers a magnificent haven concealed within the dense jungle of Ubud, where remote sanctuaries reside amidst the bountiful greenery, providing an atmosphere of serenity and calmness. The Reserve Two-Bedroom or The Mandapa River Estate presents an unparalleled level of luxury, allowing guests to unwind and immerse themselves in the tranquil melodies of the nearby riverbank. This villa serves as the perfect retreat for individuals yearning to rejuvenate their minds, body, and spirit, be it for a family vacation or a respite from the bustling rhythm of urban existence. The personalized attention of a dedicated Patih or butler service will cater to your needs, ensuring a personalized and unforgettable stay.
