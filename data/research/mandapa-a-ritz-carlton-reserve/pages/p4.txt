Dining
With dishes and decor celebrating the rich flavors and natural bounty of Ubud, fine dining at Mandapa, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve is a feast for all five senses. From the serene Ubud Pool Bar to the romantic Kubu restaurant, guests can savour Indonesian delicacies alongside international cuisine.

Some of the most honest conversations happen over food. This year's Luxury Dining Series, Across the Table, is an invitation to slow down and gather at some of the most remarkable tables across Asia.

Dining Beyond
Sounds of the Ayung River
Surprise your loved one with a romantic candlelight dinner under the star-studded moonlit sky, just a few steps away from the majestic Ayung River. Using premium produce, experience a six-course degustation menu that showcases the finest gourmet delights specially handcrafted by the meticulous culinary team of Mandapa. A dedicated assistant will be your personal host for the evening and take care of all details.
Grace by the Rice Field
Named after the owner’s daughter, Grace, The Gracefield deck allows you to dine privately with the breathtaking view of our rice terrace – an unforgettable setting that reflects the unique indigenous rice culture of Bali and Ubud. Indulge in a six-course degustation menu thoughtfully designed by our culinary team.
In-Villa Romance
Experience the best of Mandapa’s culinary adventure in the comfort of your villa’s private garden. Elegantly decorated with fresh flower petals and encircled by candles and lanterns that aglow, this dining experience is the perfect way to celebrate a special occasion in an intimate setting. A six-course degustation menu.
Mandapa Picnic
Spend a relaxing afternoon over a private picnic with your inner circle. Pamper yourself with freshly prepared light bites along with healthy juices. At Mandapa, you may choose between the riverside picnic, next to the Ayung River, or at our iconic Lumbung (rice barn) with stunning views of our rice terrace. Offsite picnic is also available; please contact our Patih to receive recommendations of the best picnic
Barbecue by the Rice Field
Set in the private deck by the rice field, the bespoke private dining experience features a bountiful buffet selection as well as a Grill “Live Station” by a private chef. Indulge in Balinese-spiced red snapper, jumbo tiger prawns, Australian lamb satay, Black Angus beef fillet steak and many more, and end on a high note with our homemade dessert selection.
The Cliff at Sawah
Located at the edge of the cliff with the best vantage points of the Ayung River, guests are invited to enjoy a relaxed and refined private dining experience. Whether it is a romantic dinner or an intimate group dinner, The Cliff at Sawah exudes a magical feel with its unique and peaceful setting. Although it is an open-air venue, guests will enjoy the cool breeze coming from the river.

Cooking Studio by Sawah Terrace
For food aficionados and aspiring chefs, Cooking Studio by Sawah Terrace is an experience to remember offering a personalized culinary journey. Together with our skilled chefs, you will prepare an exquisite menu while our team will share their kitchen secrets. You will create authentic dishes using seasonal ingredients and techniques from a range of styles of cuisine. Cooking Studio by Sawah Terrace includes the opportunity to enjoy the dishes you’ve created, soft drinks, water, and your choice of one local beer or a glass of Balinese wine, as well as a recipe booklet to take home. Tailor-made Chef’s Table experiences are available upon request, with prices subject to the menu and wine selection.
