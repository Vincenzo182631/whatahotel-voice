A Sanctuary to Relax One's Mind, Body, and Soul
Located in Bali's cultural and spiritual heart of Ubud where the lush jungle meets the Ayung River, Mandapa is an exclusive journey inside an indigenous Balinese village, providing a sensory journey to wellness and nature. Mandapa offers 35 suites, 25 private pool villas, four unique dining concepts, including exclusive dining beyond experiences within the Reserve, and a restorative Spa and fitness center. Designed as a sanctuary to relax one's mind, body, and soul, Mandapa offers individually tailored spiritual, well-being, and health programs as well as activities to suit travelers of all ages, such as Mandapa Camp, along with the personalized attention of a dedicated Patih or butler. Focusing on three core pillars—wellness, gastronomy, and sustainability—Mandapa creates a holistic and immersive experience for guests seeking renewal, culinary discovery, and a connection to the local environment.

Ubud’s Culture and Nature Awaits
Inspired by Balinese Culture
Mandapa features thoughtfully designed accommodations that blend Balinese heritage with contemporary elegance by Jeffrey Wilkes. Set within Ubud’s lush landscape, each space honors nature through sustainable materials. With personalized service from a dedicated Patih (butler), guests enjoy a refined and immersive stay inspired by culture, comfort, and tranquility.
Surrounded by the Beauty of Ubud
Set within Ubud’s lush valley, Mandapa’s signature villas open to sweeping views of tropical greenery and the gentle rhythm of the river. Days unfold slowly by private pools and shaded daybeds, where nature surrounds each moment. Thoughtfully designed, each villa offers a sense of space, privacy, and connection to Ubud’s serene beauty.

Sustainable Living and Cultural Stewardship
At Mandapa, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, sustainability is thoughtfully woven into the fabric of our riverside sanctuary. Rooted in the philosophy of Tri Hita Karana, our initiatives honor harmony between people, nature, and spirit—preserving Bali’s heritage while inspiring mindful, responsible living.
Community Footprints
We nurture future generations through regular English classes for elementary students in Kedewatan, Ubud—empowering young minds with language skills and confidence while fostering meaningful community connections.
Soap for Hope
Through our Soap for Hope initiative, discarded solid soap is carefully recycled and transformed into hygienic resources, reducing waste while supporting local communities with essential sanitation solutions.
Guardian of the River
Honoring the sacred Ayung River, Bali’s longest waterway, we lead regular river clean-up initiatives alongside the local community, preserving its natural beauty and sustaining the ecosystem surrounding Mandapa.
