# Sixty-One Lobby Bar

London or New York come alive in this new Lobby Bar.

Allow us to invite you to discover the vibrant soul of Bilbao at Bar Sixty-One, a space where every cocktail is a work of art and every jazz note paints unforgettable emotions. A corner of inspiration and sophistication designed for those who seek unique experiences.

- Drinks Menu

Sometimes, one seeks to hide to be seen. And our **Secret Lobby Bar** is the perfect place for that. An open secret where you can enjoy a drink or a cocktail late in the afternoon.

Its warm, elegant, and timeless atmosphere evokes the venues of London or NY in the 1920s and invites you to immerse yourself in the artist’s chic bohemian vibe. Live jazz concerts complete the experience.

At Bar Sixty-One, cocktails are not just drinks; they are an experience. Our carefully curated menu offers everything from timeless classics to innovative creations celebrating signature mixology. Each drink is designed to delight your palate and take you on a sensory journey. If you want to enjoy the best cocktails in Bilbao, this is the place to be.

### Live Jazz

On the **second Thursday of each mont** h, from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM, **Bar Sixty-One** transforms into a stage where live jazz comes to life. Talented musicians fill the air with magic, creating an intimate and evocative atmosphere that transports you to another time and place. If you love live jazz in Bilbao, this is your must-visit destination.

**Schedules and Location**

**· Live Jazz:** Second Thursday of each month, from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM.

**· General Hours:** Every day from 5:30 PM to 1:00 AM.

**· Lobby Bar of The Artist Hotel, Alameda Mazarredo 61.**
