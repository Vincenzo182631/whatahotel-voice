# Breakfast

The privilege of having breakfast with the best views of the city. Impeccable service at the table and accompanied by the best products.

- Menu
- Book

They say breakfast is the most important meal of the day. At **The Artist – Grand Hotel of Art**, it’s also the most impressive.

Having breakfast with one of the best urban views nationwide, Bilbao’s most artistic skyline, is to nourish the senses right from the start of the day.

The best 100% farm-to-table products, freshly squeezed juices, origin coffees, and teas, all served at the table, complete this unique morning experience.

### Information

With the best raw materials we create unique experiences around coffee, accompanied by butter croissants, a selection of jams and French butter.

We have the best products: gluten free, lactose free and vegan.

Schedule: Every day from 07:00h to 11:00h.
