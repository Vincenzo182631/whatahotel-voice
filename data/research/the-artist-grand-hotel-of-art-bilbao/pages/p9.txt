# The Rooftop

It is said that Frank Gehry himself, when he ascended to the hotel's rooftop and saw his own work, had to hold onto the railing.

- Breakfast
- Evening Menu
- Brunch

True or not, looking out over **The Rooftop by The Artist** at any time of the day is a sensory experience. Its privileged views of the Guggenheim Museum provide the backdrop for our guests’ breakfasts.

An exceptional place to also enjoy our exclusive brunch, afternoon gatherings, and nights of live music.

### Breakfast

Our executive chef, with deep local roots, began his training as a culinary artist at El Bulli (Ferrán Adriá). He has also worked in the kitchens of Mugaritz (Andoni Luis Aduriz) and has collaborated closely with the great Martín Berasategui.
