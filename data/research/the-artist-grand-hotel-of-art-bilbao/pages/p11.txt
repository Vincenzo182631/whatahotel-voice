## The Artist Bilbao \| Where Hospitality and Art Cohabit

A space where creativity engages in dialogue with design. Art intertwines with the hotel, transforming every corner into a sensory experience. A living museum in the heart of the city.

Javier Mariscal at The Artist \| An Unmistakable Stroke

The work of Javier Mariscal permeates every corner of The Artist Hotel, transforming it into a living canvas. His creativity and visual language have left an indelible mark on the collection, giving it a vibrant and unique identity.

To celebrate this connection, "Mariscal in Bilbao" was born, a special edition that compiles his contribution to the hotel, documenting iconic pieces, sketches, and the creative process behind each work.

Available for purchase at The Artist Hotel, the bookstores of the Guggenheim Museum, and the Museum of Fine Arts.
