# Welcome to The St. Regis Beijing

## Live Exquisite at The St. Regis Beijing

The St. Regis Beijing is located in the heart of Beijing’s quiet, diplomatic compound, only 400 m away from a city highway leading directly to Beijing Capital Airport or Railway South Station. It takes about 7 minutes to walk from the hotel to Jianguomen Subway Station, and Subway Line 2 will directly lead to worker’s stadium, Subway Line 1 will directly lead to Universal Beijing Resort Subway Station....See More Our luxury hotel in Beijing, China offers many of the legendary St. Regis traditions, including our personalized Butler Service, Evening Ritual, Afternoon Tea and the St. Regis Bloody Mary. After a stroll along Wangfujing, a famous shopping street, indulge yourself at our Beijing spa hotel in Iridium Spa, complete with a natural hot spring that originates 1,500 meters below the surface. Let us pamper you with additional luxury amenities at this hotel close to Tian’anmen Square, such as a stunning glass- walled swimming pool, a 6,000 sqm landscaped garden, three exceptional restaurants and two signature bars.See Less

## Making History

Since the opening in October of 1997, the award-winning hotel in Chaoyang, Beijing, China has offered global travelers an unrivaled dimension of luxury, sophistication and bespoke service.

### Celestial Court

Opulent Chinese design mixes with elegant cuisine at Celestial Court in Beijing. Serving traditional Cantonese favorites, as well as specialty dishes, this restaurant also offers five private rooms for an intimate fine dining experience.

### Garden Court

The Garden Court features a private room, alfresco dining and a show kitchen in our Beijing, China hotel. Sample Western and Asian dishes at the buffet, which includes a large seafood station, or try something from the sumptuous à la carte menu.

Explore

### Garden Lounge

The essence of the St. Regis brand is honored through the grand staircase, glittering chandeliers and gold, foil-mounted ceiling of the Garden Lounge. Listen to seductive, discreet music as you dine or partake in The St. Regis Afternoon Tea in Beijing.

Explore

### Press Club Bar

This restaurant in Beijing features live music and expertly prepared drinks in a cozy, intimate setting that exudes old-world ambience. The extensive cocktail menu includes our world-renowned St. Regis Bloody Mary, with a variation unique to each hotel.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Beijing?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Beijing is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Beijing allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Beijing.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Beijing?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Beijing. Self parking is available at The St. Regis Beijing, with rates of ¥12.00 per hour, ¥216.00 per day, and ¥2016.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Beijing have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Beijing has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Beijing booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Beijing?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Beijing is Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK). PEK is located approximately 25.6 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Beijing have an airport shuttle to Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK)?

Yes, The St. Regis Beijing has an airport shuttle to Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK).

Does The St. Regis Beijing have an airport shuttle to Beijing Daxing International Airport (PKX)?

Yes, The St. Regis Beijing has an airport shuttle to Beijing Daxing International Airport (PKX).

Does The St. Regis Beijing have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Beijing does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
