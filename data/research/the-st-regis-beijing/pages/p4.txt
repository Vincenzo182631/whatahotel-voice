## Experiences

The prestigious address in China’s most historic city, The St. Regis Beijing places you in the center of the capital’s diplomatic and commercial district. Within a 10-minute walk of our hotel close to Beijing Central Business District are most of the embassies in Beijing’s original diplomatic district, the eclectic, exciting and energetic Silk Market and scores of restaurants. ## Spa & Fitness

The Beijing luxury hotel also features a modern Athletic Center, complete with an aerobics studio, squash courts, strength-training equipment, cardio machines, and 35 flat screen TVs. The 25-meter indoor swimming pool provides a beautiful view of The St. Regis Beijing’s unique garden. Other facilities that cater to guests’ every need include a full-service hair salon and spa.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Beijing have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Beijing offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-11:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Beijing have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Beijing has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Swimming Pool (Indoor)Indoor Children's Pool (Indoor)Indoor Natural Hot Spring Pool (Indoor)Whirlpool

Does The St. Regis Beijing have an on-site spa?

Yes, Iridium Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Beijing.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Beijing?

Forbidden City, Tiananmen Square, Summer Palace, Houhai Bar Street, Wangfujing Street and more are nearby The St. Regis Beijing.
