# A DOWNTOWN DALLAS ICON  The Joule

In the heart of Dallas, we’re here to inspire the mind, nurture the body, and delight the eye. Whether you’re a Lone Star local or a visitor from the furthest corners, we can’t wait to welcome you to The Joule. Stop by for an espresso, check in for a spa day, or book a suite for your next sabbatical. For however long you’re here, you’re part of our inner circle.

## about the joule

A revitalized, neo-Gothic landmark in the center of downtown, The Joule is a 160-room urban resort with unique retail, acclaimed dining, an award-winning spa, and more than a century of stories.
