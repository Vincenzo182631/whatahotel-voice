# The Marble Bar

The Marble Bar is located at the informally elegant restaurant NOI at Nobis Hotel Copenhagen.

The Marble Bar is not just a place where you can get a drink - it’s a bar with personality and with modern and exclusive cocktails. The cocktails are carefully selected for you with a Scandinavian influence.

For us the perfect cocktail triggers your memory. It makes you think. We want to highlight forgotten classics and signature cocktails made for your senses. Moreover, we emphasize on seasonality and carefully curated spirits. Sit back and ease into our world of cocktails.

### FOR THE LOVE OF CAVIAR

Step into a world of opulence and extravagance and revel in the most luxurious dining experience at our Marble Bar. Here, we offer an unparalleled combination of the finest Rossini Caviar and a bottle of Nyetimber Cuvee that is sure to leave a lasting impression on your palate.
