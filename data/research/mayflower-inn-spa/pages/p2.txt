##### know before you stay

**Where is Mayflower Inn & Spa located?** Mayflower Inn & Spa is located at 118 Woodbury Road in Washington, Connecticut, in the Litchfield Hills, roughly two hours from New York City.

**How do I get to Mayflower Inn & Spa from the nearest airport?** Mayflower Inn & Spa is approximately 1 hour and 15 minutes from Bradley International Airport (BDL) near Hartford, CT and about 1 hour and 25 minutes from Westchester County Airport (HPN) near White Plains, NY; private car service is available.

**Is Mayflower Inn & Spa a good fit for couples?** Yes. It is an excellent choice for couples seeking a romantic Connecticut countryside escape, with garden dining, fireplaces, wellness experiences and secluded accommodations.

**Is Mayflower Inn & Spa pet-friendly?** Yes. Mayflower Inn & Spa is pet-friendly; guests should contact the hotel directly for current pet policies, eligible room types and applicable fees.

**When is the best time to visit Mayflower Inn & Spa?** Mayflower Inn & Spa is inviting in every season, from gardens and outdoor activities in spring through fall to fireside stays, wellness and nearby skiing in winter.

**Does Mayflower Inn & Spa have a pool?** Mayflower Inn & Spa has an outdoor pool and an indoor spa pool for hotel guests.

**What wellbeing treatments & experiences are available at Mayflower Inn & Spa?** Spa treatments, hydrotherapy, a sauna & steam room, an indoor pool, fitness studios and relaxation spaces are all available at The Retreat, Mayflower Inn & Spa's 20,000-square-foot holistic wellness destination. Enjoy customized bodywork, therapeutic treatments, and best-in-class facials by Biologique Recherche, designed to restore energy flow, release tension, and promote deep relaxation.
