Rooms
12
Watch elephants grazing at this camp that is the "epitome of rustic elegance." Set on the Savute River, hacienda-style tents on raised wooden platforms have viewing decks with hammocks and "a large four-poster bed draped with mosquito netting." Guests and "very knowledgeable guides" spot antelopes, lions, and leopards in addition to pachyderms. The restaurant overlooks the river.
