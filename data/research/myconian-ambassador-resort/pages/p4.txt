Bathed in dazzling sunlight and embraced by the Aegean, Mykonos has long been a destination for rejuvenation. A vacation here is the perfect opportunity to nurture your well-being, whether by maintaining your fitness routine or elevating it with revitalizing spa treatments.

> Our holistic approach to wellness is rooted in ancient principles that recognize physical activity as essential to mental health and emphasize the importance of balanced nutrition

In this spirit, we cultivate the perfect conditions for well-being: the Mediterranean diet, celebrated for its health benefits; a Thalassotherapy spa that draws on the sea’s restorative power; a range of relaxing and rejuvenating treatments; and comprehensive fitness options, including yoga and Pilates. Seasoned practitioners will discover that Mykonos’ idyllic setting enhances the restorative effects of their routines, while novices can establish lasting habits with a week of regular practice.

## SPa

Our Thalasso Spa offers expert therapies with award-winning products, restoring balance to body and soul in an oasis of rejuvenation.
