CHARTER A YACHT FOR A DAY

## Spend an unforgettable day at sea

Book a yacht charter from our launch at Platis Gialos for an unforgettable day at sea. Tailor your itinerary—beach-hop along Mykonos’ south coast, discover hidden coves, or visit ancient Delos, the mythological birthplace of Apollo, famed for its magnificent archaeological site with temples, mosaics, and a sacred theatre. Take a private guided tour before swimming and snorkelling in Rhenia’s pristine waters. Enjoy an onboard lunch, beach picnics, and sunset cocktails. Jet skis and water sports instruction for all ages can be arranged upon request.

Our fleet includes the Cruisers Yachts 560 Express Hardtop, Dellapasqua DC13 Elite, Primatist G41 Hardtop, Azimut 43, 46, 55, and 56 Flybridge, Princess 42 Flybridge, and Colvic 36 SunCruiser Flybridge.

PLAN A LONGER SEA VOYAGE

## Island-hop with a customized itinerary

Bespoke itineraries can include multi-day island-hopping adventures, using the Ambassador as your Cycladic base, or exploring neighbouring gems such as Tinos, Paros, Naxos, Syros, Folegandros, and the enchanting Santorini.

Spend an unforgettable full-moon night on board, anchoring off a secluded beach before greeting the sunrise with yoga on deck. Enjoy a breakfast picnic as you sail to Delos—the sacred island of Apollo—where you can wander its ancient ruins in solitude before the world awakens. Whether it’s a romantic escape, an adventure at sea, or a unique cultural experience, we strive to make every possibility a reality.

FLYING HIGH

## Enjoy a bird’s view of the Aegean

Take to the skies on a breathtaking helicopter ride and marvel at the endless blue expanse of the Aegean and the Cycladic islands beyond.

Fly over Mykonos, its sunbaked landscape dotted with whitewashed houses, then sweep across Delos and Rhenia before heading towards Tinos, Paros, and Naxos—each with its own unique character despite their proximity. From this unparalleled vantage point, you will witness the islands as few ever do. In under an hour, you will have seen the Cyclades from a privileged perspective few ever experience.

COOK WITH OUR CHEF

## Greek hospitality begins in the kitchen

Owner’s Tip: “The celebrated Myconian tradition of hospitality is never more personal than welcoming a guest into our kitchen. It’s one of my favourite parts of the hotel—I may even drop in to learn a trick or two”.

Join our chef, Ilias Maslaris, on his morning round to source the freshest organic vegetables from the market and exclusive suppliers, along with the day’s catch straight from the boats. Later, take part in a private Mediterranean cooking class and master a few local culinary secrets. That evening, savour the fruits of your labour with your partner or family, paired with a special Greek vintage selected by our sommelier -offered on the house.

FINE DINING

## Dine by candlelight under the stars

Celebrate your first evening with a candlelit dinner under the stars at Efisia, our restaurant nestled in a sheltered alcove of rough-hewn stone, protected from the evening breeze. True to our guiding philosophy, Efisia takes its name from the Greek physis (φύσις), meaning ‘nature.

Recognised as one of the best restaurants on Mykonos, it blends the refined tradition of Relais & Châteaux with an authentic passion for food. Our star chefs and their team blend modern techniques with a contemporary appreciation of traditional Mediterranean flavours, transforming the finest farm-to-table produce and the fishermen’s daily catch while honouring simplicity and seasonality. Choose from à la carte or indulge in a menu dégustation, perfectly paired with a curated wine selection. Bookings essential!

THE THALASSO SPA

## Begin your vacation with a reset

Ease into the restorative embrace of the Aegean with a revitalizing spa visit upon arrival. At the very least, indulge in an exfoliating scrub and hydrating massage to prepare your skin for the Myconian sun, saltwater, and ionized sea air. Steam rooms, Thalasso pools with varying temperatures and salinity, exclusive products, and expert therapists await.

Choose from a menu of spa classics and holistic therapies tailored to your needs. Our therapists work with three award-winning ranges: Elemis, the leading British spa and skincare brand renowned for anti-aging innovations; St. Barth, a coveted Caribbean line prized for its all-natural floral blends; and Augustinus Bader, an advanced skincare range beloved by specialists and beauty experts worldwide.

Balance body & mind

## Create your individual wellness journey

Enhance your Mykonos escape with a personalised fitness programme scheduled ahead of time. Our private yoga and Pilates instructors are at your disposal to help you maintain or elevate your fitness—just one week of regular practice can be transformative.

Seasoned practitioners already know the restorative power of yoga to bring body and mind into balance, but Mykonos’ idyllic setting enhances these benefits. Beginners can simply breathe, stretch, and follow our lead. Our state-of-the-art Pilates studio and private yoga sessions take place in extraordinary settings—secret sunrise or full moon yoga, and sunset sessions paired with meditation. Complete your wellness journey with a spa treatment for the ultimate 360-degree Ambassador experiencee.

SCUBA DIVING

## Explore the world beneath the surface

The crystal-clear waters of Mykonos offer exceptional diving and snorkelling opportunities, from vibrant marine life to two intriguing shipwrecks. With excellent visibility, dive sites are chosen based on weather conditions and experience levels. Advanced divers can explore Night Diving, NITROX-enriched air, and Deep Diving Specialties.

Highlights include Paradise Reef (2–30m) and Paradise Point (10–30m), where dramatic swim-throughs, ancient artifacts, and steep drop-offs await. The Anna II Wreck (18–35m), a sunken cement carrier, is accessible to PADI Advanced Open Water divers. Further afield, Dragonisi’s rocky caverns, blanketed in yellow anemones, offer stunning underwater photography. Expect to encounter octopuses, barracudas, groupers, moray eels, starfish, and more.

HORSE RIDING

## A special bond across the centuries

Even if you’re not an experienced rider, that’s all the more reason to discover the magical world of horses with our team of accomplished trainers. From the age of five, both children and adults can learn the fundamentals of horse riding, regardless of experience level. Training begins with an introduction to horse behaviour and stable etiquette, ensuring a strong foundation before mounting. Private one-on-one lessons take place in specially designed, fenced arenas for maximum safety. With a commitment to professionalism and care, all necessary equestrian equipment is provided for each session. Whether a beginner or an aspiring rider, the experience offers a unique opportunity to connect with these majestic animals

A private photo tour takes you through the island’s iconic whitewashed alleys, windmills, and hidden gems, offering expert guidance on lighting, composition, and storytelling. Whether you’re refining your photography skills or simply wish to be the subject of a stunning visual journey, this experience creates unforgettable keepsakes. A creative and immersive way to preserve the magic of Mykonos.
