## The big blues

There’s nothing more relaxing than a day adrift in reverie or immersed in a book on our broad daybeds, cared for by our attentive crew with fresh towels, irresistible iced smoothies, and anything that catches your fancy from the bar. You could do nothing at all—just watch palm shadows and parasols lean into the afternoon until sunset stills the ocean, bringing an incomparable sense of tranquillity and well-being. Many guests do just that, which is why we simply call it Stay.

> The beach below our hotel is beloved for its fine sand, crystal-clear waters, and charming boutiques and restaurants along the boardwalk. Glass-bottom boat tours and private yacht charters depart from our jetty. Nearby, the beaches of Psarou, Paranga, and Agia Anna are a short stroll away, as is the ultra-cool Scorpios promontory. Fresh beach towels are available at reception
