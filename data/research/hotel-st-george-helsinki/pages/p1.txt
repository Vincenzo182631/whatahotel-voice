# Welcome to Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™

Housed in a meticulously restored landmark structure directly across from beautiful Old Church Park in the very heart of Helsinki, Hotel St. George not only offers guests a stunningly contemporary property set within an historically rich stage, but a total revamp on the concept of what a hotel should be. Conceived for a demanding new breed of traveler, Hotel St. George challenges the world of hospitality...See More when it comes to the very idea of luxury, presenting an innovative synergy of holistic care for mind, heart, and body. Just one soaring pillar of inspiration comes from the hotel’s boldly realized art collection. Run in collaboration with such renowned institutions as the Helsinki Art Museum and the National Gallery, the program showcases a rare work by celebrated Chinese artist Ai Weiwei, whose large-scale sculpture of a dragon is one of many stirring pieces in the seven-floor property.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™?

The check-in time at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™. A non-refundable pet fee of €35.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™?

Off-site self parking is available near Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ at Aimo Park Erottaja.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ booking.

What is the closest airport near Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™?

The closest airport near Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ is Helsinki Airport (HEL). HEL is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
