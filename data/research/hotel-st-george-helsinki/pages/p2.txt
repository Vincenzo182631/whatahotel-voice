### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ have a work space?

No, the rooms at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™?

The largest occupancy room at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ is the Companion Twin with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™?

Some rooms at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ have fridges?

No, rooms at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Hotel St. George, Helsinki, a Member of Design Hotels™ have hair dryers.
