## Dining

At The St. Regis Macao, we invite guests on an indelible epicurean journey through exquisite fusions of Eastern and Western flavors at our restaurant and bar. Our dedicated culinary team delivers bespoke service across a range of sophisticated dining settings. Offering a welcoming retreat from the energy of Macau's Cotai Strip. Our signature restaurant, The Manor, showcases the finest sustainably...See More sourced ingredients from around the world. The Manor is the perfect venue to gather, celebrate and create memories during your stay in Macau. The St. Regis Bar, our acclaimed Macau cocktail bar, brings the renowned New York heritage synonymous with the St. Regis brand and a curated selection of artisan cocktails and beverages. Guests can also enjoy an expanded menu of culinary offerings in an elegant, contemporary setting. To help guests truly unwind, light and healthy refreshments are also available poolside at The St. Regis Pool Bar.See Less

## Maria do Leste (Mary of the East)

The St. Regis Macao Signature Cocktail, Maria do Leste, takes inspiration from the worldly voyages of the Portuguese sailors whose exploration established Macau as an important trading port. Join us at our Macau cocktail bar for a unique interpretation of the traditional Bloody Mary.
