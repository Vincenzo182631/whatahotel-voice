A dual-line telephone and complimentary wired and wireless High Speed Internet...See More Access ensures effortless connectivity throughout your stay. A tea and coffee maker, along with a well-stocked minibar, provides refreshments at any time of day. The St. Regis bed and pillow menu ensure exceptional comfort and a restful night's sleep. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Macao have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Macao have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The St. Regis Macao?

The largest occupancy room at The St. Regis Macao is the Presidential Suite with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The St. Regis Macao?

Yes, all rooms at The St. Regis Macao permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Macao have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Macao have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Macao have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Macao have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Macao have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Macao have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The St. Regis Macao have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The St. Regis Macao have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
