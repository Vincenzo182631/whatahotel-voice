# Welcome to The St. Regis Macao

## A luxury address on the Cotai Strip

The St. Regis Macao, one of the finest 5 star hotels in Macau Cotai Strip, is a center for entertainment and shopping that boasts more than 850 luxury stores and an array of dining options. Sample some of the finest sustainably sourced ingredients from around the world at The Manor restaurant, an ideal venue in which to gather, celebrate and make new memories at our Cotai Strip Macau hotel. Sip on...See More a Maria do Leste, our hotel's interpretation of the Bloody Mary at The St. Regis Bar. Find sanctuary at The Spa at The St. Regis Macao or outdoor pool. Host elegant meetings or social events from our Cotai hotel in Macau with 1,300 square metres of event space, including the Astor Ballroom. Step into one of 400 luxury hotel rooms or suites fitted with traditional Chinese elements, each boasting dramatic views and marble bathrooms with a bespoke amenity collection featuring best-in-class product lines from SACHAJUAN, Sodashi and REN skincare. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Macao?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Macao is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Regis Macao allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Macao.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Macao?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Macao. Self parking is available at The St. Regis Macao, with rates of P25.00 per hour, and P300.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Macao have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Macao has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Macao booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Macao?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Macao is Macau International Airport (MFM). MFM is located approximately 3.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Macao have an airport shuttle to Macau International Airport (MFM)?

No, The St. Regis Macao does not have an airport shuttle to Macau International Airport.

Does The St. Regis Macao have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Macao does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
