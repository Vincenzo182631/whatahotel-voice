## Experiences

Situated on the western bank of the Pearl River Delta, Macau is a Special Administrative Region (SAR) of China. The St. Regis Macao is perfectly situated within the prime center of entertainment on Cotai Strip. Our refined hotel near City of Dreams Macau is the unparalleled gateway for embracing Macau’s rich Portuguese and Chinese heritage, historic ruins, spiritual temples, and imposing fortresses,...See More which illustrate the evolution of Western and Chinese civilizations spanning more than four centuries. Stroll around the paved streets and small lanes of the Historic Center of Macau, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Ruins of St. Paul’s, and St. Augustine’s Square are must-visit spots for tourists. Guests can also peruse the quaint shops offering regional handcrafted items and historic Sino-Portuguese buildings in Senado Square. More history awaits at the Museum of Macau and the Maritime Museum, and festival days are punctuated by spectacular lion and dragon dances.See Less

## Swimming Pool

Our Macau hotel with a swimming pool​ near the Cotai Strip features an outdoor pool on the eighth floor with an all-day menu and full beverage service. We invite guests to enjoy a refreshing swim in our two heated pools, gracefully enclosed by blue-tile accent walls mirroring the serene pattern of each pool floor. Two spacious whirlpools add to the ambience of this tranquil haven. Our generous deck area features plentiful sun loungers, sofas, and patio tables shaded by oversized blue and white striped umbrellas. Private cabanas provide an elegant retreat enhanced by unrivaled service. The St. Regis Pool Bar offers a menu of light snacks and tantalizing cocktails. All children 1.4 meters in height and under are required to wear inflatable arm bands or life vests when using hotel swimming facilities. Both are provided on a complimentary basis by the hotel.

## Family Traditions

The Family Traditions at St. Regis program is an experience beyond expectation. With captivating activities and thoughtful, welcoming attention that are tailored for younger guests and their families, each St. Regis hotel and resort is a destination of distinction that celebrates the art of play. Families will delight in special welcome amenities and turndown rituals designed to showcase the uniqueness of each hotel or resort’s destination, including delectable treats, child-sized robes, slippers, and a step stool for the little ones. Throughout a family’s stay, the hotel team is able to recommend and arrange a selection of recreational activities and enriching experiences, dining options, childcare services and other conveniences for families and children of all ages.

## The St. Regis Macao Cup

Our philosophy of keeping time honoured traditions while providing exceptional experiences was what drove the inspiration behind creating a legacy around the pursuit of time. Hence, The St. Regis Macao Cup was conceived. The current holder of The St. Regis Cup is Nick Moloney who on 20 November 2015, set a new speed sailing record across a 42km (26 mile) route across the Pearl River Delta from south of Lamma Island in Hong Kong to a waypoint off Macau’s Hac Sa Beach in Coloane. The time taken to complete this course was 5 hours, 29 minutes and 43 seconds.

Arts & Culture

Macau’s rich Portuguese and Chinese heritage and surrounding architecture illustrates the evolution of Western and Chinese civilizations spanning more than four centuries. The Historic Centre of Macau transports you back in time, with its old paved streets and small lanes surrounded by a mix of Portuguese and Chinese heritage buildings. It was listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2005.

Recreation & Retail

Macau is a shoppers paradise for its free port status. From luxury goods to intricate Chinese handicrafts, Macau has lots to offer. Admire breathtaking panoramic views of Macau at the Macau Tower. Ascend from ground to a soaring 223-meter high in 60 seconds to the observation deck to soak in the sweeping views of this city. One of the popular retail destination for luxury goods is the Grand Canal Shoppes.

## Family Traditions - Culinary Journey

Delight in a curated cooking experience at our Cotai Strip, Macau hotel where our expert chefs will guide and teach you in a small group the fundamentals of crafting two signature dishes from The Manor. Donned in your own chef’s apron and toque, learn about the exquisite ingredients and cooking techniques used by The St. Regis Macao kitchens as you expand your appetite for knowledge in the culinary world of haute cuisine. When the class is completed, sit back and enjoy the delectable dish that you have created. Please make a reservation two days in advance to join this culinary journey.

## An Indulgent Ritual - Bloody Mary Making Class

Our Cotai Strip, Macau hotel is proud to offer and present the Art of Drinking experiences at The St. Regis Bar. Celebrate the signature St. Regis ritual by indulging in an iconic Bloody Mary with distinct flavors, join our mixologist and mix your own Bloody Mary cocktails. Available daily from 2:00-3:00 PM. Please make a reservation 24 hours in advance to join this experience.

## Cultural Confectionery Atelier

Experience the elegance of tradition at The St. Regis Macao with a delightful candy workshop featuring Yau Kei’s Dragon’s Beard Candy, established for over 40 years. Join us as Kenny Yuen, the second-generation master, demonstrates this intangible Cultural Heritage, allowing you to explore exquisite pulled sugar creations at the House of Astor.

MOP1,500 for two people / 90 minutes

Available daily from 3:00-5:30 PM.

Advanced reservations are required, subject to availability upon request.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Macao have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Macao offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-10:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio and Strength-Training Equipment, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Macao have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Macao has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Macao have an on-site spa?

Yes, The Spa at The St. Regis Macao is located on-site at The St. Regis Macao. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Macao?

Macao Giant Panda Pavilion and Pavilion of Rare Animals, Guia Fortress, Chapel of Our Lady of the Snows and Lighthouse, Taipa Houses, A-Ma Cultural Village, teamLab SuperNature and more are nearby The St. Regis Macao.
