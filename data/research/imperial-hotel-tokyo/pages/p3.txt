## Membership Privileges

Please be sure to provide your membership number if you wish to use any benefit or discount you have received. Guests need to state their intention to use a benefit or discount for it to be applied. In addition, guests are requested to provide the membership number in advance to the room reservation clerk or at check-in. If you are already staying at the hotel, please contact the front desk.

Should you be unable to provide your membership number by check-in time, you will not be eligible to receive discounts on accommodation charges, including with respect to consecutive nights after registering. Discounts will not apply to accommodation and any other charges incurred prior to registration.
