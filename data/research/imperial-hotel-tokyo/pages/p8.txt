# Accessibility Information

## Barrier-Free Rooms

We offer rooms that have been partially modified to make them more accessible for guests using wheelchairs.

Barrier-free rooms are general guest rooms that have been partially modified to make them more accessible and convenient for guests with disabilities, the elderly, and other wheelchair users. Priority will be given to guests using wheelchairs when reserving these rooms.

### Main Features of the Rooms

- 1.

The width of the entrance (82 cm) allows for smooth entry even when using a wheelchair.

- 2.

The bathroom access ramp is gently sloped for easy access by wheelchair users, and sufficient clearance to accommodate a wheelchair is provided under the TV stand and the washbasin.

- 3.

The wardrobes installed in the room are equipped with sliding doors.

| Size | 42 m² / 452 sq. ft. |
| Bed size | Twin: 120 x 205 cm / 47 x 81 in. (width x length) |
| Space between beds | 90 cm / 35 in. |
