## USER GUIDE

At Imperial Hotel Limousine Service, we offer a service dedicated to meeting the customer's every request to allow you to enjoy the same level of comfort as when using the Imperial Hotel.

Please feel free to order us to take you to business talks or to go sightseeing.

### Recommended Plans

- Take a short trip to discover Japanese craftsmanship in a chauffeur-driven vehicle from the Imperial Hotel

- Visit the heart of Japan's grape-cultivation and wine-making region in a chauffeur-driven vehicle from the Imperial Hotel

- Night Drive Plan

_Airport transfers_

Please leave airport pick-ups from Narita or Haneda airports to us. We can handle transfers in the middle of the night, or first thing in the morning. We have staff fluent in English to meet overseas VIP guests.

_Guest Collection Service_

Provide us with the address, specified date, and time and we will arrange for a chauffeur-driven vehicle to meet and bring your guest to the hotel or other destination on a one-off or regular basis.

_Book us for sightseeing_

We offer various sightseeing routes, depending on customers' wishes.

_How many adults can we pick up?_

We also offer minivans and people carriers (max. capacity: 9 people). These can transport a large number of people, as well as handling a large amount of luggage.
