# Access

The Imperial Hotel is a short distance from the shopping and theater districts of Ginza and Marunouchi. The hotel's location in Hibiya also provides outstanding access to Tokyo International Forum and to government agencies.

## Train Access

The times shown are rough guides, and the transfer and waiting times at stations are not included.

### Subway

| Hibiya Station | Tokyo Metro: Hibiya Line, Chiyoda Line Toei Subway: Toei Mita Line 3 minutes walk from Hibiya Station (Nearest exit: Exit A13) Access Route |
| Uchisaiwaicho Station | Toei Subway: Toei Mita Line 3 minutes walk from Uchisaiwaicho Station Exit A5 Access Route |
| Ginza Station | Tokyo Metro: Hibiya Line, Marunouchi Line, Ginza Line 12 minutes walk from Ginza Station Exit B2 Access Route |
| Yurakucho Station | Tokyo Metro: Yurakucho Line 7 minutes walk from the station Access Route |
| Shimbashi Station | Tokyo Metro: Ginza Line Toei Subway: Toei Asakusa Line 9 minutes walk from Shimbashi Station Exit 7 12 minutes walk from Shimbashi Station Exit A2 Access Route |

### JR

| Yurakucho Station | JR Yamanote Line or JR Keihin Tohoku Line 5 minutes on foot Access Route |
| Shimbashi Station | JR Yamanote Line or JR Keihin Tohoku Line or JR Yokosuka Line 7 minutes on foot Access Route |
| Tokyo Station | JR Yamanote Line or JR Keihin Tohoku Line or JR Chuo Line or JR Yokosuka Line or JR Keiyo Line 5 minutes by Taxi Access Route |
