# Facilities

Enjoy a comfortable stay with our array of facilities including a pool, saunas, a fitness center, and conference and meeting rooms.

- _Executive Services Floor_\\
\\
The Floor offers an array of business services from Meeting and Conference Rooms, individual workstations to a private Lounge.\\
\\

- _Music Room_\\
\\
Located on the Executive Services Floor (5th Floor, Main Building), the sound-proof Music Room is equipped with a Steinway grand piano for visiting musicians and singers, wishing to practice or rehearse.\\
\\

- _Fitness Center, Pool and Saunas_\\
\\
Exclusively for our guests, the Tokyo Imperial's Fitness Center features a useful variety of state-of-the-art exercise equipment.\\
\\

- _Beauty Care, Shiatsu_\\
\\
Esthetics experts share treatments and ingredients that relax muscles, calm stress and soothe over-worked minds.\\
\\

- _Baby Room_\\
\\
The highly-experienced certified staff members have received specialized education and experience-oriented childcare training. They affectionately care for the children of our guests.\\
\\

- _Tea Ceremony Room "TOKO-AN"_\\
\\
The Imperial offers tea-ceremony demonstrations in elegant tea-ceremony rooms.\\
\\

- _Hotel Accessibility_\\
\\
Our facilities are designed to accommodate guests with wheelchairs or other physical disabilities, ensuring a comfortable and secure experience.\\
\\

- _Medical Facilities_\\
\\
Introducing medical institutions such as internal medicine, dentistry, and orthopedics located in the Imperial Hotel Main Building and the Imperial Tower.\\
\\
