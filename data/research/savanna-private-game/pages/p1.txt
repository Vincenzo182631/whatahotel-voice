# Just a Little Different

## AT SAVANNA LODGE, EACH DAWN BEGINS WITH A NEW STORY.

An exclusive 5-star lodge, set within the internationally famous Sabi Sand Nature Reserve, part of the greater Kruger National Park Conservancy.

Our internationally acclaimed lodge offers a unique style of tented accommodation, blending the romance of classic safari with the refined elegance of modern luxury.

Where untamed beauty meets the warmth of a privately owned lodge - in the heart of the Sabi Sand Nature Reserve.

Savanna is a privately-owned retreat with just nine luxurious suites, ensuring an intimate, home-away-from-home experience. Here, the Big Five roam free, and every detail is designed around your comfort and connection to the wild.

Our specially designed game-viewing vehicles and highly trained guides and trackers offer close, unhurried encounters with an astonishing variety of wildlife in the African bushveld. The Big Five - lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino - are seen regularly during our game drives.

From the waterholes in front of the lodge to the nearby dams that draw elephants daily in the dry winter months, every moment at Savanna offers a connection to nature and the timeless spirit of the bush.

### REFINED SAFARI LIVING

## OUR SUITES

Our five-star luxury lodge offers nine exquisite suites, seven of which feature graceful canvas-topped roofs that blend the romance of a classic safari with the elegance of a bygone colonial era. Inside, modern comforts meet timeless style - from air-conditioned interiors and deep soaking tubs overlooking the Savannah, to décor that captures the essence of understated luxury.

Each suite opens onto a private patio with a heated plunge pool, outdoor shower, and uninterrupted views of the bush. Thoughtful touches include a minibar stocked with inviting treats, a coffee machine for slow morning rituals, and the soft whisper of the evening breeze as the sun sets over the Sabi Sand. More than accommodation, each is a sanctuary - a place to retreat, rejuvenate, and reconnect.

### Your Safari Story Awaits

## OUR SAFARI

In an area rich with dams and waterholes that draw herds of game, and traversing more than 10,000 hectares of pristine bushveld, a Savanna safari offers exceptional opportunities to witness Africa’s most iconic wildlife. The “Big Five” - lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino - are seen regularly, alongside cheetah, wild dog, hippo, crocodile, abundant plains game, and over 400 bird species.

From twice-daily game drives led by expert guides to lantern-lit dinners beneath a canopy of stars, every moment at Savanna Lodge is crafted to bring you closer - to the land, to its wildlife, and to each other. Our emphasis is, first and foremost, on the safari experience: unhurried, immersive, and alive with the rare and beautiful creatures of the Sabi Sand.

### The Lodge

Whether unwinding in the comfort of the lodge lounge, enjoying its spacious patio, or relaxing at the main pool with views over lush gardens and a floodlit waterhole, guests are often treated to unforgettable wildlife moments - from flocks of birds to elephants and antelope quenching their thirst.

### The Loft

A cosy loft for a quiet game of chess and a bird's-eye view of the waterhole. The adjoining library offers Wi-Fi and facilities to transfer and back up your safari photographs.

### Dining

We love to surprise and delight our guests in the evenings with varied dining experiences. From lantern- lit bush dinners to private candle-lit dinners under the stars on warm evenings. Meals can be a special time for guests to come together, or for private reconnection after an eventful day of Safari. No matter what the experience, dishes are always thoughtfully presented and paired with fine South African wines from our curated cellar.
