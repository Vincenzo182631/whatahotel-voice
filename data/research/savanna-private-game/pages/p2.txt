# Our Lodge

Our beautifully appointed suites nestle amongst indigenous bush and trees that provide privacy, quiet, and a haven for birds. Blending colonial charm with classic safari style, each features hand-crafted furniture, fine linens, and elegant artwork.

### ONE SAVANNA SUITE, THREE EXECUTIVE SUITES AND FOUR LUXURY SUITES

Overlooking waterholes and the Savannah beyond, each suite is purposefully nestled into the indigenous foliage creating a private sanctuary for our guests.

### SAVANNA SUITE

UP TO 4 ADULTS OR 2 ADULTS + 3 CHILDREN \| 1 SUITE

A private sanctuary under thatch, featuring two beautifully appointed en-suite bedrooms connected by an elegant lounge and generous covered patio. Enjoy exclusive access to a private vehicle, guide, and lounge amenities when the suite is booked as a complete package. The Savanna Suite's unique facilities are much in demand by families who would like to have their children under the same roof, or groups of friends wishing to spend quality time together.
