## Know Before You Go

What are the best things to do in Kauai?

Kauai offers a wide variety of experiences, from exploring the dramatic Nāpali Coast and Waimea Canyon to snorkeling, surfing, hiking, visiting waterfalls and relaxing on beautiful beaches. Travelers can also explore Kauai's botanical gardens, scenic drives and charming towns, or experience the island's culture through local food and traditions. Staying at Koʻa Kea in Poʻipu makes it easy to experience both the island's natural beauty and its many outdoor adventures.

Where should I stay to explore Kauai?

Poʻipu is an excellent home base for exploring Kauai, particularly for travelers who want easy access to beaches, outdoor activities, dining and attractions on the island's South Shore. Koʻa Kea is located directly on Poʻipu Beach, providing a convenient starting point for island adventures while offering an oceanfront retreat to return to at the end of the day.

What is the best way to explore Kauai?

A rental car is one of the best ways to explore Kauai because many of the island's beaches, scenic overlooks, hiking trails and attractions are spread throughout the island. Having your own vehicle gives you the flexibility to explore at your own pace and create your own itinerary. Koʻa Kea makes exploring the island even easier with Tesla vehicle rentals available to guests (limited-availability).

What is there to do in Poʻipu?

Poʻipu offers a variety of activities for visitors, including swimming, snorkeling, surfing, beach walks, shopping, dining and exploring the South Shore. Resort car rentals available at Koʻa Kea also give guests the flexibility to explore at their own pace.
