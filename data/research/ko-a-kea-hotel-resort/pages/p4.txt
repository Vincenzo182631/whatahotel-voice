## Kauai's Best Sushi

Fresh & Flavorful

Cultures converge at the Red Salt sushi bar through the islands’ freshest sashimi, nigiri, and rolls. Savor standalone classics like ono, hamachi and uni, or indulge in signature rolls like the decadent _Ali'i_ Surf & Turf featuring purple rice, lobster, wagyu and 24K gold flake.

## Beverages

Sip & Savor

Beyond Red Salt's specialty cocktails, sip on a curated selection of sake, Japanese beers and Japanese whiskey to complement your dishes. Taste your way through sakes ranging from delicate and floral to dry and full-bodied.
