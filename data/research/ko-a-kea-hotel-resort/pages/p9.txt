## FAQ's

Is Ko'a Kea Resort a good place for couples?

Yes. Ko'a Kea is an intimate oceanfront resort on Kauai's South Shore designed for couples seeking a romantic getaway. The resort offers oceanfront accommodations, fine dining, spa experiences and easy access to Poipu Beach.

What are the most romantic things to do at Ko'a Kea?

Couples can enjoy a private beach cabana dinner, relax together at The Spa at Ko'a Kea, dine at Red Salt, spend the day at Poipu Beach or unwind together by the oceanfront pool. The resort also offers romantic experiences and packages for special occasions.

What are the best rooms at Ko'a Kea for couples?

Ko'a Kea offers romantic oceanfront rooms and suites with private balconies, providing couples with a peaceful setting to enjoy views of the Pacific Ocean. Oceanfront accommodations are an especially fitting choice for honeymoons, anniversaries and romantic getaways.
