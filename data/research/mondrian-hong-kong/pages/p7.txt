## Green Key Certification

In November 2025, Mondrian Hong Kong was awarded the Green Key certification, one of the world’s leading environmental certifications for the tourism and hospitality industry. The certification recognises hotels that meet rigorous standards across more than 150 criteria in 13 areas, including energy and water conservation, waste management, environmental management, community engagement and sustainable operations.

To learn more about our environmental initiatives and the Green Key programme, please visit www.greenkey.global or contact Green Key at info@fee.global.

Valid until 31 December 2026
