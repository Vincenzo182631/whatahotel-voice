# a cultural canvas unfolds in hong kong

Mondrian Hong Kong marks the launch of the first Mondrian hotel in Greater China. Situated in the vibrant Tsim Sha Tsui district with quick access to key attractions and landmarks, featuring globally acclaimed restaurant and bar concepts, this hotel offers bold new perspectives to the region. Supported by amazing views, its global network, and progressive cultural programming, guests can experience a unique luxury lifestyle tailored specifically to this cosmopolitan city.

### STAY

The hotel’s 322 modern and sculptural rooms connect to the spirit of the city, reimagining heritage design motifs through a contemporary lens, against the stunning backdrop of Victoria Harbour and the Hong Kong skyline.
