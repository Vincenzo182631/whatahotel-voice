FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Why is Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel a good choice for a luxury stay?**

Set in central Bangkok, this luxury hotel combines accommodation with distinctive Thai-inspired design and gracious hospitality. Guests can enjoy tropical garden surroundings, pool and golf-course views, refined dining, Anantara Spa and VIVID IV Drip Bar, creating a sophisticated stay with a strong sense of place.

**Is Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel a good choice for business travellers?**

Yes. Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel offers a Grand Ballroom, flexible meeting rooms and 17 breakout rooms, all renovated in 2026, plus Kasara Executive Lounge access for suite and villa guests. Located on Ratchadamri Road beside Ratchadamri BTS Station, it connects directly to Bangkok's Sathorn, Sukhumvit and Siam business districts.

**What should guests know before arriving at the hotel?**

Check-in is from 3:00 pm and check-out is until 12:00 noon. Free on-site parking is available, cats and dogs up to 10kg are welcome with one pet per room, and all rooms are non-smoking, with smoking permitted only in designated areas.

PET POLICY

**What dining experiences can guests enjoy at the hotel?**

Guests at Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel can enjoy a variety of dining experiences, including Madison, Thai cuisine at Spice Market, Japanese dishes at Shintaro, Italian favourites at Biscotti, and Guilty. Mocha & Muffins serves coffee, light bites and pastries throughout the day.

**What wellness and fitness facilities are available at the hotel?**

Guests can enjoy the newly renovated Anantara Spa, alongside a Technogym-equipped fitness centre, outdoor rock climbing, private Muay Thai sessions and Pilates. Wellness facilities also include a swimming pool, steam rooms and whirlpools, providing varied options for relaxation, movement and fitness throughout the stay.

**Is Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel a good place to stay near Siam?**

Located on Ratchadamri Road, this luxury hotel is close to Ratchadamri BTS Station, making it easy to reach Siam and explore central Bangkok. Its location also puts guests near major shopping destinations such as Central World , offering convenient access to shopping, dining and city attractions without relying on Bangkok traffic.

**Is Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel a good choice for couples?**

Couples can enjoy a luxury stay with Thai-inspired surroundings, intimate dining and tailored wellness experiences. Private dining can be arranged in settings such as a candlelit lawn or private cabana, while bespoke spa treatments provide another opportunity to relax and spend quality time together.

**What types of rooms and suites are available at the hotel?**

The newly renovated hotel offers spacious rooms, suites and private villas with garden, city, pool or golf-course views. Options include the 220 sqm Garden Pool Villa with a private pool and the 198 sqm Two Bedroom Jim Thompson Explorer Suite, alongside other luxury accommodations for different stays.

**Is Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel a good choice for families?**

Families can enjoy a comfortable stay at Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel, with a Kids' Club offering activities for younger guests. Spacious accommodation options include the Two Bedroom Kasara Executive Suite, Two Bedroom Royal Suite and Anantara Garden Pool Villa, alongside a swimming pool and cribs.

**How far is Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel from the airport?**

Approximately 45 minutes from Suvarnnabhumi International Airport and 30 minutes from Don Mueang International Airport

**Can I park a car at Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel?**

Yes. Free parking is available on site.

**Is Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel a smoking-free hotel?**

At Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel, all rooms are non-smoking rooms. Smoking is only allowed in designated areas, including those at AQUA Restaurant and BQT Terrace.
