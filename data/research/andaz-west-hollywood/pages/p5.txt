### Great experience all-round

The hotel is on Sunset Blvd. right next to the Comedy Store. ( I saw a great show by the way). The atmosphere is very vibrant as well as the decor.
I had the Rockstar room, which is Rock themed, facing Sunset Blvd. and while there was street noise it was not overwhelming and that's why I chose that side of the hotel. It was a nightly show out there, fun. If your looking for quiet, I suggest the opposite side which faces the hillside mansions. There is a small refrigerator w/small freezer which is great to have.
There's a great pool and deck on the rooftop. Food and drinks up there as well.
