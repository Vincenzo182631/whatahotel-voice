# Unleash Your Inner Celebrity at Our West Hollywood Hotel on Sunset Blvd.

As the first hotel on the Sunset Strip, Andaz West Hollywood attracted legendary rockers in the mid-60s. It now serves as the best hotel for an eclectic gateway to Hollywood, Beverly Hills, Santa Monica and other fashionable California neighborhoods in the greater Los Angeles area. Elevate your ultimate LA experience where anything is possible at this iconic Sunset Boulevard property that has been reimagined for modern day.

## Rooms & Suites

With local art adorning the walls and Calcutta mosaics setting the stage in the bath, our personality-infused West Hollywood hotel rooms with great views offer a unique, comfortable home base as you explore Los Angeles. Upgrade to a suite or the Penthouse Suite for bonus benefits, such as a marble tub or a balcony overlooking West Hollywood.

## DINING

View All Dining

### Riot House Restaurant

In the spontaneous open-kitchen atmosphere of Riot House Restaurant. Here in West Hollywood on Sunset Boulevard, you’ll find colorful food and playful cookery that’s always fresh and relevant. In fact, menus change seasonally to ensure you have a unique, flavorful dining experience each time you stop in. The dress code is casual/business casual.

### The Sundeck

Relax on a lounge chair, put your feet up and enjoy the warm California sun as you drink and dine at the highest rooftop pool bar in Los Angeles. The Sundeck bar is open seasonally and has views of the Sunset Strip and Hollywood Hills. Cabana reservations are also available via the Book Experiences Tab.

### Riot House Market

Get a taste of the local flare at The Riot House Market on Sunset Boulevard, offering everything from grab-and-go items, made-to-order dishes, rejuvenating juices, freshly brewed Vittoria coffee, light snacks and more. We also have a nice selection of gluten-free, vegan and vegetarian options available.

### Riot House Bar

Once a hangout for hard-partying rockers of the 1970s, Riot House Bar now offers a vibrant and engaging space to drink, snack and socialize on Sunset Boulevard. Indulge in a cocktail after your meetings, sip California wines at sunset or enjoy a refreshing microbrew while taking in spectacular views of the Sunset Strip.
