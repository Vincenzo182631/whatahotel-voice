Andaz West Hollywood
A sleek option for the cool, understated rocker-type who doesn't mind being on the edge of the action.

How did it strike you on arrival?
Set in an unassuming building on Sunset Boulevard, the Andaz West Hollywood seems slightly removed from the action at first glance—but upon further inspection, you'll see that the cool rock 'n roll vibes here are just more understated than its Hollywood competitors. Rockers like Led Zeppelin and Guns 'n Roses crashed here back in the day (it wasn't called Riot House for nothin') and that history is commemorated through modern, framed prints and an all-around laid-back vibe.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
Standard rooms are slightly plain in terms of decor—think beige with some green and gray accents—whereas suites up the ante a bit. They're infused with pops of color and works of art from local artists. Luckily, even in the blander rooms, floor-to-ceiling windows with views of the Sunset Strip make up for the slight decor lapses.
How about the little things, like mini bar, or shower goodies. Any of that find its way into your suitcase?
There are complimentary beverages and snacks upon arrival. Oddly, some rooms, like the King Deluxe, have no bedside lighting. Bathrooms have walk-in showers and are stocked with Lather products.
Room service and food: Worth it?
The Riot House Restaurant pays homage to the hotel's rowdy past and serves Southern California fusion dishes, like a sous vide New York strip steak accompanied by lemons and a romesco sauce. The lobby's nightly happy hour takes place from 5 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. and comes with free snacks.
Anything stand out about other services and features? Whether it’s childcare, gyms, spas, even parking—whatever stuck with you.
The guests-only pool is located on the roof and has incredible panoramic views of the city. In a very (cool) un-L.A. move, the covered cabanas are first-come, first-serve. DJs spin pool-side on Saturdays.
Bottom line: worth it, and why?
A sleek option for the cool, understated rocker-type who doesn't mind being on the edge of the action.
