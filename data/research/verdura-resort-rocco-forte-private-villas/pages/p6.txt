## A Story Between Japan and Sicily

## Nagori

### Tuna and wasabi cannoli; Mazara prawn ravioli and shiitake; Sciacca scallops and yuzu. Dishes to be shared and passed around the table. A menu designed by Fulvio Pierangelini, who looks to Japan without compromising his own culinary identity.

Menu

### "Nagori is a Japanese term that means the end of a fruit, a vegetable or nature's gift season. It evokes the nostalgia of a season that has come to an end, leaving us wistful for its departure…"

Additional Information

We wish for everyone to feel comfortable, so whilst we do not operate a dress code, we kindly recommend that our guests are respectful of the environment we have created here at Verdura Resort and refrain from wearing sportswear, shorts and beach shoes in our restaurants. Our guests within the hotel usually opt for a smart-casual style.

Nagori is open for dinner from June to October. Availability may vary throughout the season. Our team will be pleased to recommend the best dining options available during your stay.
