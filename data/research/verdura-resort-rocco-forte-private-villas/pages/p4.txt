# Liola Restaurant: Seafront Sicilian Trattoria near Sciacca

- Verdura Resort
- Dining
- Liolà

### With its relaxed atmosphere and rustic charm, Liolà captures the essence of a traditional Sicilian trattoria. Complete with its own pizzeria and a spacious outdoor terrace, it offers breathtaking clifftop views across the Mediterranean Sea.

## Our Menu

A celebration of Sicilian tradition, the menu features locally sourced caponata, crispy arancini and wood-fired pizzas. For an authentic taste of the island, this is the place to be.

### Sicilian Night

10 August \| San Lorenzo Night

As the sun sets over the Mediterranean and the stars begin to shine, indulge in a feast of Sicilian flavours at Piazzetta Liolà. Discover the colours, aromas and traditions of the island through live cooking stations and authentic folk music under the open sky.

Additional Information

We wish for everyone to feel comfortable, so whilst we do not operate a dress code, we kindly recommend that our guests are respectful of the environment we have created here at Verdura Resort and refrain from wearing sportswear, shorts and beach shoes in our restaurants. Our guests within the hotel usually opt for a smart-casual style.

Liolà is open for lunch and/or dinner from March to November. Availability may vary throughout the season. Our team will be pleased to recommend the best dining options available during your stay.
