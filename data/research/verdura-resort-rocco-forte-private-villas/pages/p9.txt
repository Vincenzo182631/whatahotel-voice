### Scirocco Bar

Scirocco Bar is the resort’s evening spot for uniquely Sicilian cocktails and creative mixology, inspired by the fabled wind that journeys from North Africa to our enigmatic island.

More information

# Scirocco Bar: Sea Front Mediterranean Bar near Sciacca

- Verdura Resort
- Dining
- Scirocco Bar

### Theatrical mixology that blends island folklore with ingredients from the surrounding land and sea. A terrace made for enjoying a sundowner, or perhaps for stargazing with a nightcap.

### Handpainted walls recall the movement of the Scirocco wind and the shape of an idea before rules take hold. The creative vision of Paolo Moschino and Philip Vergeylen, given form by artist Phyllis Clair Smith.

### Freeflowing expression, unexpected and full of potential.

### Island Signatures

Venice regained

### The Menu

### Sicily’s story, told through cocktails. Our storied cocktails are paired with small plates by Fulvio Pierangelini. Unwind with an aperitivo as the sun dips behind the Mediterranean, the sounds of live music washing over you.
