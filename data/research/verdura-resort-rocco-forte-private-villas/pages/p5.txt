### ZAGARA

With a glamorous dining room and an atmospheric outdoor terrace that offers views out towards the sea, Zagara combines elegant design and the natural beauty of Verdura to stunning effect.

# Zagara: Candlelit Mediterranean Restaurant near Sciacca

- Verdura Resort
- Dining
- Zagara

### Named after the orange blossom that scents the estate, Zagara reflects the timeless charm of Sicily. Dine on the terrace as the sun sets or in our vibrant restaurant for a refined sense of occasion.

## The Menu

The menu by Fulvio Pierangelini and Emmanuele Riccobono celebrates nature's bounty. Catch of the day, served with homegrown braised fennel and citrus. Spaghettoni with shrimp from Sciacca. Ricotta ravioli with truffle. Seasonal ingredients are handpicked from our organic garden, including our fragrant Verdura olive oil. Each dish is complemented by an extensive wine list of carefully curated Sicilian and Italian labels.
