On Sicily’s unspoilt southern coast, Verdura Resort is a rare expression of privacy and place. Suites and sun-lit rooms open onto Mediterranean greens and blues. Eight restaurants and bars celebrate flavours from the resort's own farm. Championship golf, sports and curated experiences invite complete immersion, a connection felt deeply in the flagship spa through homegrown botanicals and the longevity wisdom from the blue zone of nearby Caltabellotta. A place where nature, culture and relaxation await.

## Rooms, Suites & Villas

Rooms that open onto sea views, suites with pools, villas immersed in fragrant gardens, each ensures that every stay feels like a place of your own. The sea-facing suites, redesigned by Paolo Moschino and Philip Vergeylen in 2026, bring coastal modernism and Sicilian craft together to create a refined Sicilian home, shaped entirely around you.

## Golf

Two championship courses and a nine-hole par-3, designed by Kyle Phillips, create a true links experience by the sea. Broad fairways, coastal winds and cinematic sunsets enhance every round.

discover

## Restaurants & Bars

Eight distinct restaurants and bars, each distinct, connect to the land via the resort's own farm. Menus by Fulvio Pierangelini travel from Italian classics to Japanese cuisine.

discover

### Ondina Bar

By the pool and close to the shore, Ondina captures the ease of summer days, with seafood salads, light dishes and chilled wines poolside, as the sea glimmers just beyond.

discover

### Liolà

Wood-fired pizzas and colourful majolica tiles capture the warmth of a family trattoria, making Liolà a natural gathering point. Enjoy cliff top views and convivial meals in a rustic setting.

discover

### Zagara

Set among lush foliage and bronze sculptures, Zagara overlooks the sea. A refined setting where Mediterranean dishes and Sicilian wines evolve from sunset into lantern-lit evenings.

discover

### L'Osteria

All-day and sea-facing, L'Osteria is where farm-grown produce and the day's catch from Sciacca inspire Italian favourites and daily specials, served from lunch through to late evening.

discover

### Scirocco Bar

A seductive cocktail bar named after the African wind that sweeps the coast. Distinctly Sicilian cocktails, crafted from homegrown botanicals, accompany sweeping sunset views from the terrace.

discover

### Nagori

Fulvio Pierangelini's most intimate restaurant is found within the ancient Saracen Tower. Japanese techniques elevate Mediterranean produce through seasonal menus that create a dialogue between two islands.
