## Classic Luxury Chicago City Center Hotels

Unlock the gem of downtown Chicago at The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago. The Gwen Story

## A Sculptural Masterpiece

The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, is a sculptural masterpiece, exuding the vintage glamour of Chicago’s 1930s prime with contemporary elegance. The iconic Art Deco-infused facade was meticulously crafted by pioneering sculptress Gwen Lux in the historic McGraw-Hill Building. As spectacular as this storied landmark looks from the city’s bustling streets, it’s all the more enchanting inside.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago?

The check-in time at The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 75.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago?

On-site valet parking is available at The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago. The daily valet fee is $84.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago booking.

What is the closest airport near The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago?

The closest airport near The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago is Chicago Midway International Airport (MDW). MDW is located approximately 12.1 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
