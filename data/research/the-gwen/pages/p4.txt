# Ignite your inner Explorer

## Experiences

Engage with the soul of Chicago at The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel. Located on Chicago's famed Michigan Avenue, our downtown hotel is within walking distance to luxury shopping at The Shops of North...See More Bridge and local restaurants on the Magnificent Mile. Enjoy the ultimate night out with great food and live concerts at the amazing House of Blues Chicago or spend a beautiful day exploring the many attractions on the Navy Pier. A tour of the downtown campus of Northwestern University will give you a peek of college life in The Windy City. Venture to all areas of Chicago with our hotel's easy access to the Red Line on the corner of Grand and State Streets. The Gwen, is the ideal destination to create unforgettable memories in downtown Chicago.See Less

Shop Til Your Drop at The Gwen

### The Shops at North Bridge

With direct, enclosed access from The Gwen, you can shop til you drop at this sprawling shopping center right in the heart of the Michigan Avenue district. Chicago’s most stylish shopping and dining destination featuring a flagship Nordstrom and more than 50 world-class retailers along with 20 restaurants. Whether you are looking to shop or enjoy a casual lunch, North Bridge is just steps away.

The Best of Breakfast Made Sleep-In Friendly

Each weekend from 10:00 am to 2:00 pm, join us at Upstairs at The Gwen for everyone’s favorite midday meal. A snooze-friendly occasion, brunching at The Gwen is complete with sweet and savory day-starting delights and breakfast cocktails. And when the weather is warm, upgrade your experience with a side of sunshine on our open-air terrace with cityscape views.

Sip and Stay Awhile

There are endless spots to “cheers” in Chicago, but happy hour is just a little sweeter at Upstairs at The Gwen, our chic rooftop terrace frequented by guests and locals alike. Sip on craft cocktails, house wine, draft beer, and a selection of light bites to pair — all at special prices. Monday through Thursday, from 4:00 pm – 5:30 pm.

Glamping

Enjoy a night under the stars with the city’s first urban glamping experience on the private terrace of the Gwen Lux Suite, perched on the 16th floor of the historic McGraw-Hill building. With over 1,000 square feet, guests enjoy picturesque views of Michigan Avenue and iconic architecture.

Tipsy Tea Party

Sip, savor and spill the tea each Saturday and Sunday at The Gwen's Tipsy Tea in Private Affairs, our boozy twist on traditional high tea. Enjoy light bites, à la carte brunch options, delicate desserts and more.

Make it a Moët Moment

Grab a split and enjoy a #MoëtMoment at the hotel’s onsite vending machine.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Equipped with two Peloton Bikes and a Tonal., Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago?

Museum of Ice Cream, Bally’s Casino Chicago, The Art Institute of Chicago, Willis Tower Skydeck, Field Museum and more are nearby The Gwen, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Michigan Avenue Chicago.
