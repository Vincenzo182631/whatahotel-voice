Why book?
A River North hotel that melds bygone
Chicago
with modern-day luxury, and a local destination for its seasonal activations and programming.
Set the scene
Dreamy Art Deco design, fun programming, and a prime location make up this River North hotel. Check into one of the 311 rooms or 34 suites, then check out the skyline views at rooftop bar and restaurant Upstairs at The Gwen. While here, spot a mix of friend groups, families, and business travelers enjoying the amenities and year-round programming: winter curling, seasonally inspired Tipsy Tea, and Wine Wednesdays (i.e. half-priced bottles and live jazz).
The backstory
The hotel opened in September 2015 as part of Marriott’s Luxury Collection hotels, a portfolio focused on locally inspired experiences and thoughtful service. The property is inspired by 1930s Chicago and the architectural promise it brought with it, incorporating Art Deco touches throughout its spaces—most notably in its façade, created in 1928 by pioneering local sculptor Gwen Lux.
The rooms
Guest rooms feature calming color palettes of ivory and greys alongside soft pinks and deep greens, with custom murals adorning upholstered headboards. Bathrooms house marble finishes and a spacious walk-in shower, with Molton Brown toiletries and Frette bathrobes. Those looking for a special stay can check out one of the property’s five Terrace Suites (each with a private view of Michigan Avenue)—or can request a fifth-floor room for convenient rooftop access.
Food and drink
Find seasonal, all-day menus at Upstairs at the Gwen (with favorites like the flatbreads or Lux Burger). If you’re traveling with friends, make a booking for the hotel’s Tipsy Tea, a weekend offering featuring colorful bites and sips. And if you’re with kids, they’ll enjoy the in-room sundae experience, a chance to build your own ice cream sundae with all the fixings.
The neighborhood/area
Centrally situated in Chicago’s historic McGraw-Hill Building on North Michigan Avenue, guests have front-row access to the Magnificent Mile and its restaurants, shops, and museums—while also being steps away from the river and lakefront activities.
The service
The concierge team here has 50 years of combined experience, so they are the go-tos for any questions on local Chicago and hard-to-snag tickets or reservations. That experience and service style has earned them recognition from Michelin and global hotel concierge association Les Clefs d’Or.
Accessibility
The Gwen is ADA compliant, providing accessible rooms equipped with features like bedside telephones and visual notifications on smoke detectors and fire alarms.
Anything left to mention?
Those looking for more time outdoors can glamp on a spacious private terrace when booking the Gwen Lux Suite, set on the building’s 16th floor. Complete with a Lotus Belle tent, those intimate environs make it ideal for a romantic retreat under the stars—but you also have access to the suite’s two bedrooms and large dining area, ensuring a great group getaway.
