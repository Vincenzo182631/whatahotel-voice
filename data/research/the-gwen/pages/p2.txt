## The Gwen Glamping Experience

For those seeking something special, high-style glamping on our open-air terrace is anything but ordinary. Indulge in an exclusive VIP experience with plenty of space to spread out in your multi-bedroom suite and glamping tent, plus special amenities and services including welcome bites, turndown treats, and more. The Gwen Glamping experience can accommodate up to six guests and is available for stays between May and September. To book your stay, please contact our sales department directly.
