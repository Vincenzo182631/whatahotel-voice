## Small Bites, Big Views and Perfected Cocktails

Offering seemingly endless skyline vistas, sunrise to sundown dining, and masterful mixology in a laid-back, twinkle-lit atmosphere, Upstairs at The Gwen is one of Chicago's best kept secrets. Sunny summer afternoons make our open-air terrace a natural choice, but Upstairs at The Gwen takes patio season well into the winter with warm drinks, fire pits, and curling.

### Dining at The Gwen

There’s service; then there’s hospitality — a long-held value at the heart of everything we do. The only thing to rival The Gwen’s dining options? The gorgeous spaces that house them.
