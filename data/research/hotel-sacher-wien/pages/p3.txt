# Frequently Asked Question

## Questions and Answers

We're pleased to provide you with answers to the most frequently asked questions. If we should've missed one in particular, don't hesitate to let us know at any point in time. Thank you very much.

### General Information about Hotel Sacher Vienna

What is the address of the Hotel Sacher Vienna?

The Hotel Sacher Vienna is located at the Philharmoniker Str. 4, 1010 Wien, Austria

How far is Hotel Sacher Vienna from the airport or main train station?

- Vienna Airport: approx. 30 minutes by car
- Vienna Main Train Station: approx. 15 minutes

We are also happy to arrange a private transfer for you – stylish and stress-free.

Does Hotel Sacher Vienna offer an airport shuttle service?

We are pleased to offer you our transfer services from the airport or train station for a fee. To book your transfer please contact us at **portier.wien@sacher.com**

Does Hotel Sacher Vienna offer valet parking?

Yes, of course. For maximum comfort, we offer a professional valet parking service. Our porter team will safely park your vehicle. This service is available for € 45 per night.

Is the hotel Sacher Vienna wheelchair accessible?

Hotel Sacher Vienna is fully accessible. With a ground-level entrance, wheelchair-friendly rooms and elevators throughout the hotel, we ensure a carefree stay for guests with limited mobility.

Is smoking allowed in Hotel Sacher Vienna?

Smoking is not permitted in any indoor areas, including rooms and suites of the Hotel Sacher Vienna. However, we are happy to reserve a room with a private balcony where smoking is allowed. Outdoor smoking areas are also available.

What time is the check-in at Hotel Sacher Vienna?

You can check in at Hotel Sacher Vienna starting from 3:00 PM.

What time is the check-out at Hotel Sacher Vienna?

You can enjoy your stay at Hotel Sacher Vienna until 12:00 PM on your departure day, as that is the check-out time.

Does Hotel Sacher Vienna have a swimming pool?

Unfortunately, we don’t have a swimming pool on site.

Does Hotel Sacher Vienna offer a concierge service?

Yes, our experienced concierge service is available around the clock, offering discretion and expertise – whether for cultural recommendations, personal requests or special arrangements.

What services does the Hotel Sacher Vienna's concierge provide?

Our concierge team is happy to organize the following for you, among other things:

- Tickets for opera, theater, and concerts
- Private tours, museum visits, and excursions
- Luxury rides, limousine transfers, and airport transfers
- recommendations for restaurants and sights
- Personalized experiences for unforgettable moments in Vienna

What attractions are located nearby Hotel Sacher Vienna?

Hotel Sacher Vienna is situated in the heart of the city – just steps away from the world-famous Vienna State Opera, the Albertina Museum and the magnificent St. Stephen’s Cathedral. The finest sights of Vienna are easily accessible on foot.

How is Hotel Sacher Vienna connected to public transportation?

Hotel Sacher Vienna boasts **excellent public transport accessibility** due to its prime central location. Guests are within steps of **Karlsplatz/Oper**, a major hub for **U-Bahn (subway) lines U1 and U4**. A short walk also connects you to **Stephansplatz**, offering access to the **U3 line**. Additionally, numerous **tram lines** and **bus stops** are conveniently located close to the hotel, ensuring easy access to all of Vienna’s main attractions and other parts of the city.

Does Hotel Sacher Vienna Offer WiFi?

You’ll be able to stay connected throughout your stay at Hotel Sacher Vienna, as **Wi-Fi is available in all areas and is completely free of charge.** So whether you’re in your room, the lobby, or other common areas, you can easily access the internet without any extra cost.

Does Hotel Sacher Vienna offer tours or local experiences?

Yes, upon request we organize:

- Horse-drawn carriage rides through the Old Town
- Danube river cruises
- Customized city tours

Each tour is carefully tailored to your interests.

Are guided tours of the historic Hotel Sacher Vienna available?

Yes, we are happy to organize exclusive private tours of our property upon request – for guests who wish to experience the unique spirit and history of the Sacher up close.

Is medical assistance available in case of an emergency?

Yes, absolutely. Should medical assistance be required, we will discreetly and promptly arrange for a doctor visit or ensure professional care. Your safety and well-being are our top priorities.

What is the history of Hotel Sacher Vienna?

Founded in 1876 by Eduard Sacher, the son of the creator of the Original Sacher-Torte, Hotel Sacher soon became the top address for Viennese high society. Under the renowned Anna Sacher, it evolved into a meeting place for nobility, artists and businessmen.

Since 1934, the hotel has been carefully managed by the Gürtler family – privately owned and committed to tradition, excellence and authenticity.

Is there a dress code at Hotel Sacher Vienna?

There is no formal dress code. However, we kindly ask guests to dress in a manner appropriate to the elegant atmosphere of our hotel. In our restaurants such as the Rote Bar and Grüne Bar, we recommend smart casual to elegant attire.

Does Hotel Sacher Vienna have an app?

Hotel Sacher Vienna does not have its own standalone app. As part of The Leading Hotels of the World, guests can use The Leading Hotels of the World (LHW) app to explore the hotel, manage reservations, and discover services and offers. The app is available free on iOS and Android. Once in the app, simply search for “Hotel Sacher Vienna”.

Is Hotel Sacher Vienna recognized as one of the best hotels in the world?

Yes. Hotel Sacher Vienna has been named among The 50 Best Hotels for two consecutive years and ranks No. 42 worldwide in 2026, as the only hotel in Austria on the list. The hotel is also included in LA LISTE’s Top 100 Hotels worldwide, with a score of 98.5/100, and has been awarded three MICHELIN Keys, the highest distinction in the MICHELIN Key selection.

### Reservations & Booking

How can I make a reservation at Hotel Sacher Vienna?

We are very happy to welcome you at the Hotel Sacher Vienna. You can book directly through our website, through Leading Hotels of the World, by phone or via email at reservierung.wien@sacher.com. Our team is always available for special requests or arrangements.

Is a deposit required at Hotel Sacher Vienna?

A deposit is required exclusively for **prepaid bookings** or **during special periods with deviating cancellation conditions**. In all other cases, no advance payment is necessary. This information is clearly indicated during the booking process.

What payment methods are accepted at Hotel Sacher Vienna?

We accept all major credit cards, cash and EC/debit cards.

How can I modify or cancel an existing reservation at Hotel Sacher Vienna?

Bookings made via our website can be managed online. For other reservations, please contact our team at reservierung.wien@sacher.com anytime.

Does Hotel Sacher Vienna offer exclusive deals or seasonal packages?

Yes, Hotel Sacher Vienna regularly offers carefully curated packages. Anything you desire from seasonal specials to romantic experiences. More information can be found in **our special offers page.**

Does Hotel Sacher Vienna offer accessible rooms and suites?

For our guests with special mobility needs, we have a spacious, barrier-free Junior Suite available. Please inform us in advance so that we can prepare your stay in the best possible way.

Do children stay free of charge at Hotel Sacher Vienna?

Children are very welcome in Hotel Sacher Vienna. When staying in a Junior Suite or higher category (subject to room size), children stay free on an extra bed. Please mention this request when booking. We also offer our “Petit Sacher” package, a child-friendly all-round service.

Children up to 16 years stay without charge in the same room as their parents. Additional beds are available from the Junior Suite upwards.

Are baby cots or extra beds available at Hotel Sacher Vienna?

Do rooms at Hotel Sacher Vienna have air conditioning?
