## A Symbol of Viennese Elegance Since 1876

### Where Culture, History and Hospitality Come Together

At Hotel Sacher Vienna, a traditional 5-star superior luxury hotel near the Vienna State Opera and the Albertina, culture, history and hospitality come together in the heart of the city. Since its opening in 1876, Hotel Sacher Vienna has been an integral part of Vienna’s cultural and social life. As one of Austria’s most renowned hotel addresses, the hotel welcomes guests from over 100 nations as well as prominent figures from the worlds of art and culture, business, politics and society.

Today, Hotel Sacher Vienna combines timeless Viennese elegance with luxurious rooms and suites, finest culinary experiences, the world-famous Original Sacher-Torte, the Sacher Boutique Spa and personal concierge service. To this day, the history of this luxury hotel is shaped by family tradition, passion and a spirit of innovation.

* * *

**Hotel Sacher Vienna ranked No. 42 in The 50 Best Hotels 2026**

In the year of our 150th anniversary, Hotel Sacher Vienna has once again been named among The 50 Best Hotels, ranking No. 42 worldwide in 2026. For the second consecutive year, we are part of this international ranking and remain the only hotel in Austria on the list. This recognition is a special tribute to our entire Sacher team and reflects our commitment to genuine hospitality, personal service and the highest standards of quality.

## FAQs About Hotel Sacher Vienna

### Helpful Answers for Your Stay

What is the address of the Hotel Sacher Vienna?

The Hotel Sacher Vienna is located at the Philharmoniker Str. 4, 1010 Wien, Austria

Does Hotel Sacher Vienna offer a concierge service?

Yes, our experienced concierge service is available around the clock, offering discretion and expertise – whether for cultural recommendations, personal requests or special arrangements.

How is Hotel Sacher Vienna connected to public transportation?

Hotel Sacher Vienna boasts **excellent public transport accessibility** due to its prime central location. Guests are within steps of **Karlsplatz/Oper**, a major hub for **U-Bahn (subway) lines U1 and U4**. A short walk also connects you to **Stephansplatz**, offering access to the **U3 line**. Additionally, numerous **tram lines** and **bus stops** are conveniently located close to the hotel, ensuring easy access to all of Vienna’s main attractions and other parts of the city.

What are the opening hours of the restaurants at Hotel Sacher Vienna?

Our dining venues welcome you at the following times:

**Restaurant Rote Bar**: Monday to Sunday: 12 pm to 12 am

**Restaurant Grüne Bar**: Thursday to Saturday: 6 pm to 12 am

**Blaue Bar**:

- Sunday to Wednesday: 12 pm to 12 am
- Thursday to Saturday: 12 pm to 1 am

**Café Bel Étage**: Monday to Sunday: 8 am to 10 pm

**Café Sacher Vienna**: Monday to Sunday: 7 am to 11 pm

**Breakfast Buffe** t: daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM

**Salon Sacher**: Wednesday – Saturday: 3 pm to 1 am

Is Hotel Sacher Vienna recognized as one of the best hotels in the world?

Yes. Hotel Sacher Vienna has been named among The 50 Best Hotels for two consecutive years and ranks No. 42 worldwide in 2026, as the only hotel in Austria on the list.
