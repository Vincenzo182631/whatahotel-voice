# SacherConfiserieVienna

## The heart of the Sacher world Experience the magic

Step into a world of timeless indulgence, where every creation carries the legacy of Vienna’s most iconic hotel. From the world-famous Original Sacher-Torte to the handcrafted Sacher bellboy chocolate caps, our Confiserie invites you to savor a taste of Austrian tradition — perfected since 1832.

### The Original Sacher-Torte

At the Sacher Confiserie in Vienna, the Original Sacher-Torte is a timeless expression of Viennese pastry craftsmanship. Meticulously handcrafted in 34 steps and presented in its iconic design, it represents a unique balance of tradition and indulgence.

Whether as a thoughtful gift or a refined finale to a special meal, the Original Sacher-Torte brings the unmistakable taste of Sacher to every occasion.

## FAQs

Where is the Sacher Confiserie located in Vienna city center?

The Sacher Confiserie is located in the heart of Vienna’s 1st District at **Kärntner Straße 38**, directly inside the Hotel Sacher Wien and next to Café Bel Étage. It is situated just steps away from the **Vienna State Opera** and the **Albertina Museum**, easily accessible via public transport (U1, U2, U4 Karlsplatz).

What are the opening hours of the Sacher Confiserie?

The Sacher Confiserie is open **daily from Monday to Sunday**. Our opening hours extend until **10:00 PM**, providing a convenient option for late-night gift shopping or a sweet treat after an evening at the opera.

Is the Sacher shop open on Sundays and public holidays?

**Yes**, unlike most retail shops in Vienna, the Sacher Confiserie remains **open every Sunday and on all public holidays**. This makes it the premier destination for high-quality Viennese souvenirs when other stores are closed.

Is there a long queue or waiting time at the Confiserie?

Unlike the Café Sacher, the Confiserie offers a **quick and convenient shopping experience without long waiting times**. You do not need a reservation to enter; you can browse our selection of chocolates and cakes in a relaxed atmosphere and purchase items immediately.

What products can I buy at the Sacher Confiserie?

Beyond the world-famous **Original Sacher-Torte**, we offer an exclusive selection of premium Viennese specialties:

- **Original Sacher Würfel:** Bite-sized versions of the famous Original Sacher-Torte.
- **Handcrafted Pralines:** Artisanal chocolates and truffles.
- **Sacher Bellboy Chocolate Caps:** Fine nougat and premium chocolate treats.
- **Sacher Delicacies:** Signature coffee blends, premium teas, hot chocolate, and curated gift sets with wine or sparkling wine.

Can I buy a whole Original Sacher-Torte to take away?

Yes, the **Original Sacher-Torte is available for purchase on-site every day**. It comes in various sizes, presented in our iconic wooden box, and is ready to be taken away immediately—perfect for a last-minute gift.

Is Original Sacher-Torte available by the slice to go?

Yes, we offer **individual portions** of the Original Sacher-Torte. These are ideal for travelers who want a spontaneous taste of Vienna’s most famous cake without needing a cafe reservation.

Can I take an Original Sacher-Torte on a plane?

**Absolutely.** The Original Sacher-Torte is the ultimate **travel-friendly souvenir**. It is packaged in a sturdy wooden box and stays fresh for several days without refrigeration, making it easy to carry in your **hand luggage or suitcase** on international flights.

Does the Confiserie offer international shipping?

Yes. If you prefer not to carry your purchases, many of our products—including the Original Sacher-Torte—can be **shipped worldwide** via our online shop.

Can I pre-order Sacher products for boutique pickup?

Yes, you can pre-order selected items and collect them at your preferred time ( **Click & Collect**). We highly recommend pre-ordering during peak seasons such as Christmas, Easter, or Mother’s Day.
