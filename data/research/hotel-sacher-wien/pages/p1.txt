# CaféSacherWien

## Breakfast, Coffee House and Viennese Cuisine

### Café Sacher Wien

At Café Sacher Wien, Viennese coffeehouse culture has been celebrated for generations. Here, authentic Austrian tradition meets timeless elegance, accompanied by the aroma of freshly brewed Original Sacher Coffee and a slice of Original Sacher-Torte. Whether you are visiting Hotel Sacher Vienna for breakfast, a light lunch, a coffee, or a relaxed dinner before or after the opera – the historic Café Sacher Wien is one of the most renowned coffe houses in Vienna and offers a stylish retreat in Vienna city centre at any time of day.

Classic coffee specialties and traditional Austrian dishes are served with genuine warmth, in keeping with the spirit of traditional Viennese coffeehouse culture. A place to linger, indulge and observe. As a Pet-friendly hotel and cafe, Cafe Sacher Wein is open to guests from around the world and to their four-legged companions as well.

## Breakfast at Café Sacher Wien

A delightful start to the morning

Start your day with breakfast at the historic Café Sacher Wien. From Anna Sacher’s Breakfast and the Sacher Vital Breakfast to morning favourites such as Egg Benedict or Egg Avocado, a varied selection awaits for a delightful start to the day in the heart of Vienna. Enjoy our breakfast daily from 7 am to 11 am.

## **Viennese coffee house culture**

Coffee, Viennese pastries and Original Sacher-Torte

Whether a Sacher Melange, a black coffee or the Original Anna Sacher Coffee, Café Sacher Wien celebrates Viennese coffee house culture at its finest. Alongside our coffees, we serve the Original Sacher-Torte, traditional Viennese apple and curd cheese strudel, as well as selected creations from the Sacher Patisserie.

## Culinary delights at Café Sacher Wien

Viennese cuisine throughout the day

At lunch and dinner, Café Sacher Wien invites you to discover the flavours of classic Viennese cuisine. Enjoy favourites such as Wiener Schnitzel, creamy veal goulash and Original Sacher Sausages, alongside lighter dishes and a selection of vegetarian and vegan options.

## Breakfast at Café Sacher Wien

A delightful start to the morning

Start your day with breakfast at the historic Café Sacher Wien. From Anna Sacher’s Breakfast and the Sacher Vital Breakfast to morning favourites such as Egg Benedict or Egg Avocado, a varied selection awaits for a delightful start to the day in the heart of Vienna. Enjoy our breakfast daily from 7 am to 11 am.

## **Viennese coffee house culture**

Coffee, Viennese pastries and Original Sacher-Torte

Whether a Sacher Melange, a black coffee or the Original Anna Sacher Coffee, Café Sacher Wien celebrates Viennese coffee house culture at its finest. Alongside our coffees, we serve the Original Sacher-Torte, traditional Viennese apple and curd cheese strudel, as well as selected creations from the Sacher Patisserie.

## Culinary delights at Café Sacher Wien

Viennese cuisine throughout the day

At lunch and dinner, Café Sacher Wien invites you to discover the flavours of classic Viennese cuisine. Enjoy favourites such as Wiener Schnitzel, creamy veal goulash and Original Sacher Sausages, alongside lighter dishes and a selection of vegetarian and vegan options.
