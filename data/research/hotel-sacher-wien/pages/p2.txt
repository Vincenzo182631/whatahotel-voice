## CONTACT AND ARRIVAL IN VIENNA AND SALZBURG

## ARRIVING AT HOTEL SACHER VIENNA

The Hotel Sacher Wien in the heart of the city is easily reached via all major forms of transportation. If you are arriving by car, just follow the signs towards “Wien Zentrum/Stadtmitte.”

The hotel is easily accessible from the train stations and the Vienna International Airport (VIE) via S-Bahn, City Airport Train (CAT), cab, or our exclusive limousine service. Take the subway to “Karlsplatz/Oper” or the tram to “Kärntner Ring/Oper” – both are just a short walk from the hotel.

## ARRIVING AT HOTEL SACHER SALZBURG

The Hotel Sacher Salzburg is centrally located and easily accessible by car or public transportation. City buses and cabs as well as our exclusive limousine service will take you comfortably and quickly to Hotel Sacher Salzburg from both the Salzburg Airport and the main train station (Salzburg Hauptbahnhof).
