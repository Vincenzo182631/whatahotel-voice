# Hotel Sacher the Legendary 5-Star Hotels in Austria

**Hotel Sacher** truly embodies its fin-de-siècle grandeur while offering quintessential luxury through contemporary elegance and state-of-the-art amenities. It’s a prime example of a **first-class luxury hotel**. Once the rendezvous for high society, including the renowned artist Gustav Klimt, Hotel Sacher has remained a cultural cornerstone throughout its illustrious history, providing the ultimate 5-star hotel experience.
