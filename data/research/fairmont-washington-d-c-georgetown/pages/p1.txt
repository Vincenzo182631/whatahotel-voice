# _service_ DETAILS AT FAIRMONT WASHINGTON D.C., GEORGETOWN

At Fairmont Washington D.C, Georgetown, we take care of everything to make you feel at home and part of the community. We welcome all of our guests with a friendly greeting, personalized service, and genuine care. We look forward to assisting you during your stay.

## Guest information

### Check-in/Check-out

Check-in time is 4:00 PM.

Check-out time is noon.

Late check-out or early check-in is by request only and subject to availability. Additional fees may apply. Please inquire with the front desk for details.

### Parking

Paid valet parking with unlimited in and out privileges is available at Fairmont Washington, D.C., Georgetown. Please note that all prices are in U.S. Dollars (USD).

- $20.00 for the first 2 hours
- $30.00 for 2-3 hours
- $35.00 for all day (3-10 hours) hours
- $70.00 for 10+ hours/overnight

On-Street metered parking is available. Self-parking garages are available close by to the Hotel at 2440 M Street NW and 2300 Street NW, although are not open 24 hours per day.

### Accessibility

The hotel has accessible rooms and assistive devices available for guests who require them. Our Fairmont Rooms, Fairmont Gold Rooms and Deluxe Rooms feature many accessible amenities and may be requested at the time of reservation.

### Business Services

Fairmont Washington, D.C., Georgetown is pleased to offer a variety of business services available to in-house guests, 24 hours a day. For the following services, please visit the Front Desk:

- Paper Shredder
- Printing and Photocopies
- Fax Machine
- Couriers and Mailing

### Balance Gym

We are pleased to offer complimentary in-residence membership to Balance Gym-Foggy Bottom for all hotel guests Located on our Mezzanine level, it offers an extensive menu of facilities and services which go far beyond what you will find in a typical hotel fitness center.

These features include an indoor salt water pool, a steam room, sauna and whirlpool, free weights, cardio equipment, weight machines, stetching areas, and locker facilities.

Services include Personal Training and CrossFit plus Yoga, Spin, Balance BootCamp, Pilates, Barre, Zumba and more. Visit our Balance Gym page for more information.

### Fairmont Fit

With our available Fairmont Fit, you don't need to travel with your workout gear. We will have premium brand exercise shirt, shorts, and running shoes ready in your room upon arrival.

Be sure to contact us prior to your arrival or ask to have your gear preferences added to your guest profile upon arrival at the hotel.

### Pet Policy

Pets of all sizes are welcome at Fairmont Washington, D.C., Georgetown, as long as they are leashed at all times. Pets must not be left unattended in the room. Any damage incurred to the room by the pet is the responsibility of the guest.

For individuals who are not able to travel with their pet, the hotel offers the services of Georgie, Fairmont Canine Ambassador. Georgie joyfully spends her days meeting, greeting and making guests feel welcome. Come evening, Georgie will head home with her Dad, Mark Huntley, our Regional Vice President and General Manager.

### Business Center

We offer a variety of meeting rooms and large spaces that can accomodate private small gatherings or large conferences, ideal for corporate travelers and event organizers.

### Internet

Browsing speed wi-fi is available complimentary to ALL - Accor Live Limitless members. Daily internet access fee is $14.95 plus applicable tax for non-members. Preimum internet access is available for $25.39.

## Guest services

### Concierge

Concierge services are located at the Concierge Desk in the Main Lobby. We would be pleased to assist you with recommending and finding dining reservations, transportation, professional tour guides, personal shopping services, theater and sports tickets, plus directions to major attractions and hidden gems in the area.

### Dry Cleaning & Laundry Service

Fairmont Washington, D.C., Georgetown provides same-day dry-cleaning and laundry service when articles are received prior to 9:00 AM.

- Laundry/Valet pick-up is always from your room, please do not drop off elsewhere.
- Regular Service: Items received by 8:30 AM are returned the same day after 6:00 PM
- Rush Service: Additional 30% charge will be applied for all express services.
- Items picked up for express pressing will be returned within one hour (pick up between 8:30 AM and 5:00 PM).
- Express Dry Cleaning and Laundry items received before 11:00 PM will be returned the following day after 11 AM This service is available 7 days a week.
- Overnight laundry service is not available.

Please note that there many be a reduction in hours of operation based upon occupancy.

### Housekeeping

We offer daily housekeeping services during your stay. Turn down service is provided to our Signauture Suite and Fairmont Gold guest automatically and all other guest rooms upon request.

### Shoe Shine

We are pleased to offer overnight shoe shine service upon request.

## Make the most of your stay

### Bike Rental

We are pleased to provide a limited number of bicycles for use by our guests during their stay. Plesae inquire at the Front Desk. Bicycles are subject to availablity.

### Turndown Service

Turn down service is provided to our Signauture Suite and Fairmont Gold guest automatically and all other guest rooms upon request.

### Loyalty Benefits

We are proud to offer the benefits of ALL - Accor Live Limitless to members of the program. Benefits vary by status level. Please inquire with Reservations or the Front Desk to learn how to enroll or what benefits you are entitled to as a loyal guest.
