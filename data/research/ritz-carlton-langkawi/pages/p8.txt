The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi
A jungle-shrouded masterpiece that boasts cinematic sunsets, elemental design, and a world-class spa jutting out over the Andaman Sea.

Reviewed by
Jonnie Bayfield
Why book?
There is nowhere else in Langkawi that quite manages to combine the natural awe of the landscape with architectural dare, pure resort comfort, and the best kind of experiential travel. This is superbrand-level luxury, with an aesthetic firmly rooted in place and space.
Set the scene
No question, The Ritz-Carlton is among the best in the world for good reason. This Langkawi resort is where the yacht crowd and honeymooners head when they want their jungle with a side of air-con and turn-down service. It’s design-forward and high-end, yet manages to maintain a wholesomeness. Its vast footprint sprawls across rainforest and a pristine, private bay within a UNESCO Global Geopark, yet the constant shroud of trees, curving lanes, and chic eateries somehow renders it intimate, cozy even. This stay manages the hard task of keeping everyone happy—with an ace kids club (overlooked by dusky leaf monkeys), a hammam-equipped spa, an overwater adults-only pool with killer sunsets, and a range of innovative in-house activities, all underscored by the hypnotic din of abundant wildlife.
The backstory
Opened in 2017 as part of Ritz-Carlton’s expansion into Southeast Asia, this was one of the brand’s boldest statements. Designed by Philippe Villeroux of Kuala Lumpur–based Tropical Area Architects, the resort reflects local kampong village life while framing nature in a contemporary context. Aimed at reinventing the “legend of Langkawi,” the hotel balances Malay cultural motifs with an architectural audacity that feels entirely its own. There’s drama everywhere at the Ritz-Carlton, with canopy-height structures of dark wood housing everything from boutiques to restaurants and even the lobby. It’s as close as you’ll come on this island to a break from reality.
The rooms
Even the most economical rooms are on point, continuing that mythic, sultry vibe that’s consistent across the resort. Black, charcoal-like timber lines almost every structure. Inside, rooms are fresh and spacious, with oversized tubs, private terraces, and the option of jungle or sea views. The Rainforest Junior Suites come with separate living rooms, while villas—from rainforest to beachfront—amp up the privacy. The Kenari Villa (two-bedroom, with a 92-square-meter pool) is built for families or groups, but it’s the overwater villas, with private infinity pools that seem to spill out into the Andaman, that are the jaw-dropping, grid-stopping showpieces.
Food and drink
Breakfast at Langkawi Kitchen is comprehensive and high-quality—a parade of Malay, Chinese, and Indian flavors prepared in open kitchens, all served in a canopy-hugging, longhouse-inspired setting. Come evening, it switches to Malaysian classics with rainforest views from every seat. For fine dining, Hai Yan serves modern Chinese seafood at the water’s edge—the island’s only Chinese fine-dining restaurant—worth donning linen for, not only for its antique-inspired interiors but also delicacies such as the highly prized Empurau fish, whose sweet, berry-fed flavor attracts customers from far and wide. For barefoot afternoons, The Beach Grill (its food is far better than its name suggests) delivers fresh, locally caught seafood cooked to order, while Horizon—an adults-only, super chic poolside bar—mixes signature Hornbill cocktails and Southeast Asian tapas to the beat of a live DJ or jazz band.
The spa
Unmissable. The Ritz-Carlton Spa floats out over the crashing waves, with its six pod-like pavilions inspired by traditional Malay fishing traps, or ‘bubus’. Therapy rooms look out over the open ocean, as therapists deploy local oils, herbs, and spices via an imaginative roster of treatments. Signature experiences include the Urut Melayu massage, or more indulgent multi-sensory therapies such as Earth & Fire, which includes body mask, wrap, and exfoliation. The Ritz-Carlton also boasts the island’s only hammam, as well as a spacious relaxation deck to amplify the sense of elemental escape, with waves lapping away just beneath your feet.
The neighborhood
Tucked away in a private bay near Teluk Nibung, you won’t want or need to leave—but if you do, it’s only a 10-minute walk to the SkyCab cable car and Temurun Waterfall, or 25 minutes to Cenang Beach. On-site, there’s a cultural art trail showcasing local creators and a slate of “Ritzadventure” excursions that weave through rainforest and mangroves to hidden beaches.
The service
Signature Ritz-Carlton polish—consistent, unobtrusive, and intuitive. They know what you want before you do. This is the kind of service where housekeeping take it upon themselves to replace your dog-eared boarding pass bookmark with a shiny new one. Even the golf cart service (crucial in a resort this big) is on point, with a seamless online ordering platform and a bounty of staff ready to ferry you around, even at peak times. There’s also a genius Ritz-Venture & SeaVenture program offering a range of kayak excursions and jungle treks to secret beaches.
For families
There’s a comprehensive Ritz Kids program built around four pillars—nature, exploration, culture, and environment—with eco-workshops, nature walks, and babysitting available. The Eco Discovery Center, designed like a treehouse, has a terrarium and benthic tank, while the sea cucumber nursery lets children help regenerate one of Langkawi’s most vital marine species. Family villas add room to spread out, and the kids’ pool is thoughtfully zoned away from the adults-only areas.
Eco effort
From its nature-first architecture to its partnerships with local NGOs, the resort’s sustainability efforts are baked into the operation. There are Mangrove restoration projects, an in-house sea cucumber conservation program, and not a single-use straw in sight.
Accessibility
Fully accessible villas are available, as is step-free access throughout and buggies on demand.
Anything left to mention?
The monkeys are adorable from a distance and mischievous up close. Keep your fruit bowl covered and balcony doors closed—or risk bartering half a banana for your MacBook.
