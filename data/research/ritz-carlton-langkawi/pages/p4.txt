Savor the Best of Langkawi Dining
Dining
Enjoy fine dining in Langkawi at Hai Yan, our Chinese restaurant, which pairs authentic cuisine with ocean views, curated wines and private dining. Or sample dishes from Malaysia, China, and India at Langkawi Kitchen. Our other Langkawi restaurants include Horizon Lounge and, for seafood and cocktails, our beach bar in Langkawi, The Beach Grill.
Horizon and The Beach Grill Enhancement
Horizon will be temporarily closed for enhancements from 25 August to 17 October 2026, followed by The Beach Grill from 18 October to 3 December 2026. Alternative pool access is available.

Frequently Asked Questions
What restaurants does The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi have on property?
The on-property restaurants at The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi are:
Langkawi Kitchen
The Beach Grill
Hai Yan
Horizon Lounge & Bar
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi offer room service?
Yes, room service is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi.
What cuisine is offered at The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi?
The cuisines offered at The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi are:
Chinese at Hai Yan
Asian at Langkawi Kitchen, Horizon Lounge & Bar
International at The Beach Grill

Does The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi offer breakfast?
Yes, complimentary breakfast is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi to hotel guests during their stay.
