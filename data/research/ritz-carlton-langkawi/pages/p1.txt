Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi
Stay at our five-star luxury Langkawi resort
Set in a tranquil and private bay, on expansive grounds that are home to a 10-million-year-old rainforest and pristine beaches, our luxury hotel in Langkawi ushers travelers into a pocket of the world where indulgence and nature intersect. Built in harmony with the verdant surroundings and inspired by local architecture and indigenous art, residences at this beachfront resort on Langkawi Island provide complete privacy. Floor-to-ceiling windows allow you to observe wildlife up close, while the majestic beach and overwater villas at our hotel near Langkawi International Airport boast panoramic ocean views and sunsets. Langkawi Kitchen
Surrounded by lush rainforest, Langkawi Kitchen offers a theatrical dining experience inspired by Borneo longhouses. Three live kitchens celebrate Malaysian flavours, featuring signature dishes including rendang, sambal prawns and Lamb Rogan Josh.
Explore
The Beach Grill
Located by the main pool and facing the beach, The Beach Grill & Bar is a beach restaurant in Langkawi inspired by the Malay ‘Bubu’ fish trap, with four distinctive conical structures woven from the spirit of bamboo and rattan.
Explore
Hai Yan
Perched above giant rocks, this Chinese restaurant offers stunning overwater decks and panoramic views. Experience luxury dining in Langkawi with signature Wok Fried King White Prawn and Empurau, crafted with finesse.

Horizon Lounge & Bar
An adults-only overwater beach bar in Langkawi overlooking the Andaman Sea, serving sunset cocktails and signature Massaman and Roasted Duck Spring Rolls to DJ rhythms, with an infinity pool and rainforest setting.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi is Langkawi International Airport (LGK). LGK is located approximately 10.2 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
