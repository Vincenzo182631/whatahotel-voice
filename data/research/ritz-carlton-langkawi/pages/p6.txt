Experience enchantment at our beachfront hotel in Langkawi
Experiences
As you enter The Ritz-Carlton, Langkawi for the first time, the Walkway of Wishes stands ready to greet you. Simply write your wish on a stone and place it in the fountain; filled with water from the Lake of the Pregnant Maiden (Dayang Bunting), it is said to have the power to make wishes come true. This is perhaps the first indication that your experience at this luxury Langkawi hotel near the beach will be full of new discoveries. Whether you wish to spend your days relaxing in your accommodation or heading out to explore from our hotel near Pantai Cenang Beach to hike through the rainforest in search of waterfalls, your future is yours to write. Choose from a variety of activities at our including dance, jewelry making, and textile printing. Or consider jet skiing along a volcanic lake and learning wildlife photography. The hotel concierge is also available to assist in crafting an itinerary customized to your interests.
Signature Experiences
Sunrise Kayak & Picnic Escape
Immerse in a private guided serene kayak journey at sunrise. Explore calm waters, hear tales of Langkawi’s legends, and arrive at a secluded beach for a memorable morning picnic.
Elemental Wellness Journey
A multi‑sensory spa journey through four elemental sanctuaries—Water, Earth, Fire, and Air. Rooted in local ingredients and intimate rituals, each space gently restores, balances, and awakens a different dimension of well‑being, embraced by the ancient rainforest. Let the Isle of Legends reveal itself.
Sampan Dining
Sit alongside the boat, where the sampan itself becomes the dining table. Locally sourced and responsibly crafted recipes, stories of coastal traditions, and life by the sea unfold through an immersive, chef-led experience set along the secluded shores of the Andaman Sea.
Mandi Embun
Step into the rainforest for a gentle, scenic hike filled with natural discoveries. Collect rainforest dew, then experience a cleansing ritual and sound‑bath meditation by the shore, set against sweeping ocean views.
Dayang Mastura
Set sail aboard Dayang Mastura, a private catamaran where refined cruising meets natural wonder. Glide across turquoise waters to secluded beaches and tranquil coves, enjoying curated comfort, serenity, and an intimate connection to Langkawi’s archipelago. Indulge in a bespoke picnic by the resort’s private chef, inspired by the island’s rich culinary heritage.
The Hereafter Project
This guided experience uncovers the region’s natural history — from human heritage to biodiversity — before inviting guests to give back through mangrove tree planting, fostering a deeper connection to local ecosystems and conservation efforts.
Art of Driftwood
Create a personal keepsake in this hands‑on driftwood workshop, transforming ocean‑touched pieces shaped by wind and waves. This eco‑conscious experience inspires creativity while deepening your connection to Langkawi’s coastal environment and natural rhythms.
Events Calendar
Check out the Events Calendar at our Langkawi beach resort so that you can plan to make the most of your trip. From exciting bar takeovers, culinary collaborations, environmentally-focused events, and more, there's something for everyone in Langkawi.

A Spa Sanctuary
Reflecting the Rituals of Malaysia
Offering six overwater spa pavilions, The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Langkawi offers an ethereal experience in physical form. Even the treatment rooms at our tropical beachfront resort spa in Langkawi appear to float on a wave of relaxation. Discover a comprehensive menu of spa services, informed by the healing and beauty rituals of Malaysia’s shamans and midwives. The Mandi Bunga Inspired Ritual, for example, takes its cue from the bathing rituals of Malay royal houses. But instead of dispelling bad energy, this treatment eradicates stress and tension with a combination of massage and fragrant oils, herbs, and flowers. Additional services at this resort spa include therapeutic rituals for the face and body, as well as a selection of treatments developed especially for gentlemen. Of its six treatment rooms, three can accommodate couples. The spa also offers a spacious deck and the island’s first hammam.
Featured Spa Treatments
Mandi Bunga Inspired Ritual
This purifying and restorative full body experience takes inspiration from the Mandi Bunga bathing which originated from Malay royal houses, performed to dispel bad energy. Harnessing the benefits of traditional herbs rich in essential oils and therapeutic flowers to bring about a sense of calm and relaxation. Warm aromatic oils are gently poured over the body and combined with a rhythmical massage which incorporates long warming strokes to soothe tense, aching muscles, rebalance body and mind and bring about an overall sense of wellbeing.
Includes
: Mandi Bunga foot ritual, oil pouring, rhythmical massage, scalp massage.

90 minutes
Mystical Ocean and Earth Ritual
Discover Langkawi’s most mystical Island in this mineralizing body experience.
Joining in synergy the rich natural treasures of the rainforest and ocean to nurture and nourish the body and mind. Aching muscles are soothed while, giving nourishment and regeneration to tired dull lifeless skin, with this relaxing hot stone massage and marine mud wrap experience. Blending century old techniques with indigenous essential oils and heated stones to release tension and induce a deep state of relaxation.
Includes
: Full body exfoliation, marine body wrap, scalp massage, full body massage.

120 minutes
Lulur Hammam Experience
Lulur is a Javanese word for “coating of the skin”.
A centuries old treatment performed in the palaces of Central Java. A practice that is honored in this body smoothing treatment. Completed in a gentle steam environment, this traditional ritual deeply cleanses and regenerates skin, while the full body foam massage and nourishing marine mud maintain healthy skin and wellbeing. This ancient therapy finishes with a refreshing and a deeply relaxing scalp treatment designed to melt your stresses away.
Durations:
120 minutes
Frequently Asked Questions
Spa Policies
Is there a minimum age for treatments?
Guests must be at least 18 years of age to enjoy spa treatments. Minors aged 16-17 may receive manicures and pedicures unaccompanied by a parent or legal guardian. Minors aged 15 and younger may receive manicures and pedicures only when accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.
What is the cancellation policy?
If you must reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify the Spa at least 12 hours before your scheduled appointment to avoid being charged full of the service fee. Cancellations of packages or private parties require a 24-hour notice. A credit card number is required at the time you book your treatments.
What if I am late for my appointment?
If you must reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify the Spa at least 12 hours before your scheduled appointment to avoid being charged 50 percent of the service fee. Cancellations of packages or private parties require a 24-hour notice. A credit card number is required at the time you book your treatments.
What about payment for Spa services?
All major credit cards, cash with proper identification are accepted. Spa services can also be charged to your hotel bill. For your convenience, payment is accepted prior to treatments at the time of check-in. Please present discounts, gift vouchers and gift certificates at the time services are rendered. Discounts do not apply to spa packages, promotions, special events or holidays.
What about gratuities?
You may opt to leave additional gratuity for your therapist.
Health Questions
What if I have special health considerations?
Please notify our Spa reservation department before booking your treatments if you have high blood pressure, allergies, any other physical ailments or disabilities, or if you are pregnant. If you have any concern at all, please let us know. If at any time during your services you experience discomfort, please alert your therapist immediately.
What if I am pregnant?
While there are a few services that should be avoided during pregnancy, there are many beneficial treatments that can be enjoyed. Please notify our Spa reservation agents if you are pregnant when you make your appointments so that they can offer guidance.
Prior to Arrival
How can I make a reservation?
Reservations can be made by calling +62 361 849 8988 or email at
reservations.langkawi@ritzcarlton.com
. We encourage you to reserve treatments at least two days in advance.
When should I arrive?
For your enhanced comfort and relaxation, we suggest that you arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled treatment.
Should I shave before a treatment?
Shaving is recommended but not necessary for men prior to a facial. If you do choose to shave prior to your facial, be sure to do so at least 8 hours prior to your scheduled appointment. Shaving is not recommended prior to any body treatments or hair removal services.
Should I wax, shave, laser or use the sauna or steam room before a body scrub or peel?
To achieve the utmost satisfaction and comfort from your service, we ask that you wait at least 8 hours before and after the Spa service to wash, shave or use the sauna or steam room; and wait at least 24 hours before and after a laser treatment. In addition, we recommend that you limit your sun exposure before and after the facial or body peel treatment.
What should I wear during my treatment?
Most body treatments are enjoyed without clothing. However, please wear whatever is comfortable for you. During all treatments, the body is fully draped, except for the area being worked on. Robes and slippers are provided. Lockers are available for your personal items.
What about my jewelry and valuables?
If you are staying in the resort, please leave valuable items in the safe in your guestroom. Otherwise, lockers are available for your personal items in the spa facility.
Who can guide me in choosing my treatments?
Our Spa reception Ladies will help you plan the perfect Spa experience, including the best order in which to enjoy your treatments.
Upon Arrival
What about my comfort?
This is your time and you should delight in the experience to the fullest. Whether it's the room temperature, the amount of massage pressure or the volume of the music, please share your preferences with us. We request that cell phones and pagers be turned off in the spa.

Community Footprints - Sea Cucumber Nursery
Sustainability & the Environment
The people of Langkawi have long used sea cucumbers, known locally as 'gamat' for medicinal purposes, but overexploitation is causing an adverse effect on the marine ecosystem which includes a decrease in the number of sea cucumbers, water quality and ecological diversity. Led by our marine biologist, the resort's sea cucumber nursery provides a hands-on experience that will support the sustainability of these unique creatures while while understanding what they are and the important roles they play towards the ecosystem and humans. Built by the Ladies & Gentlemen, the sea cucumber nursery is located near the entrance of the resort's private beach. Guests at our Langkawi beachfront resort will learn about this unsung hero, the asexual reproduction technique used to repopulate their numbers, and will also have a chance to release the adult sea cucumber into the open sea through a kayak session with our marine biologist. Activities
Mystical Malaysian Kites
Let your kids get creative as they paint the wings of a mystical Malay kite in a rainbow of colors with patterns that endow the creature with magical powers. A favorite toy of Rajah Ahmad, the eldest son of a Sultan who used to rule Malacca in the 15th century, the beautifully decorated kite will look stunning when hanging above your kids’ bed back home, sparking their imagination as they picture his adventurous flight across the world back to Langkawi.
Duration: 2 hours
Nature Discovery
Let your kids discover the wonders of nature and get creative with whatever beautiful objects they may find. Divided into small groups, armed with baskets, bag barns & magnifying glasses and encouraged to find colorful leaves, interestingly shaped branches, smooth pebbles, beautiful flowers and other nature’s treasures, they will explore the rainforest behind the Ritz Kids club, trying to collect as many wondrous items as possible. Returning indoors, each of them will decorate with the found treasures a photo frame. With an inserted snapshot of your child enjoying the activity - an adorable souvenir you are sure to proudly display on your nightstand.
Duration: 1.5 hours
Langkawi Treasure Hunt
Nothing gets your kids as excited as a treasure hunt adventure. Dressed as pirates with an eye patch, head covered with a bandana and a terrifying scar painted across the cheek, two gangs will compete against each other, hoping to discover a treasure box hidden on a secret beach. Following an encrypted map and searching with fellow pirates for well concealed clues, your kids will learn to work towards a shared goal, rely on one another and sharpen their observation skills while having the time of their lives.
Duration 1.5 hours
