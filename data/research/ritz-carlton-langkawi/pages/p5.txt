Spa
The Ritz-Carlton Spa offers signature treatments and rejuvenating experiences, including the island's only Hammam facility. Relax with our global spa collection and indulge in locally-inspired Langkawi spa treatments which were thoughtfully created to draw upon the rich cultural elements and history of the island.
Let Us Make the World Revolve Around You
Unforgettable Experiences Await at The Ritz-Carlton Spa
Paying homage to the breathtaking surroundings, our Langkawi massage spa invites you to reconnect with nature and tune into the infinite abundance of the lush rainforest and the soothing energy of the sea.
Signature Treatments Inspired by Legends
Mandi Bunga-Inspired Ritual
This purifying and restorative full body experience is inspired by the Mandi Bunga bathing which originated in the houses of Malay royalty, which was traditionally performed to dispel 'buang sial' or bad energy. Essential oils, fragrant flowers and a rhytmic massage soothe tense muscles, rebalance body and mind and bring about an overall sense of wellbeing.
Mystical Ocean and Earth Ritual
Discover Malaysia's most legendary island in this revitalizing body experience. The synergy of the rich, natural treasures of the rainforest and ocean will nourish your body and mind. Aching muscles are soothed and dull skin is regenerated, with this relaxing hot stone massage and marine mud wrap. Using ancient techniques, indigenous essential oils and heated stones, a deep state of relaxation is induced.
Lulur Hammam Experience
Lulur is the Javanese word for 'coating of the skin' This centuries old treatment was performed in the palaces of Java, cleansing and regenerating skin while the full body foam massage and nourishing marine mud maintain healthy skin and wellbeing. Finished with a relaxing scalp treatment, aromatic oils poured over the body, and a steam room session.
