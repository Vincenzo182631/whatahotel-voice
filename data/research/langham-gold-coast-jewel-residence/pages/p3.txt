# PALM COURT

Home to the iconic Langham Afternoon Tea, Palm Court celebrates over 161 years of timeless tradition. Set within a grand coastal setting overlooking the Gold Coast, indulge in handcrafted sweet and savoury creations, signature warm scones, premium teas and sparkling pours. Every Afternoon Tea is a contemporary interpretation of the experience first served at The Langham, London in 1865, where one of the world's most enduring dining traditions began.

For those whose cravings arrive a little closer to lunchtime, Palm Court also offers an elegant à la carte menu celebrating the finest Australian produce, ensuring the experience is just as memorable beyond tea time.
