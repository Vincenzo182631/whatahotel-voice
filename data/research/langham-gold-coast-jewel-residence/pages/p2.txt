A unique and tantalising contemporary Cantonese affair. T’ang Court embraces the essence of the Southern Chinese region and its surroundings, and adorns your plate with the finest local produce, presenting elevated culinary masterpieces paired with a bespoke wine list. T'ang Court is proudly a nod to a dynasty that shaped a nation.
