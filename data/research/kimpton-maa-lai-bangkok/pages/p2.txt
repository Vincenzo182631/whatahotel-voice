Step into Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok and feel the rhythm of Bangkok’s beat in our playful spirit and innovative design. The electric vibe of our lifestyle luxury hotel, boasting 360 rooms and 97 serviced residences, captures the essence of Bangkok’s urban chic city centre.

From pet-friendly perks and cutting-edge amenities to a Fitness Center and luxurious amaranth spa, we've curated everything you need for an unforgettable stay. Seven globally inspired restaurants await your discerning palate, while our infinity pool lures you like a siren.

Embrace serenity in our lush garden oasis and when you feel the pull of the city, venture out to explore nearby attractions. Charming and charismatic, our hotel beckons you to settle in and stay awhile.

### Holistic Wellness

Our wellness offerings bring your best self forward. Stay fit and fabulous in our fitness centre, invigorate your body, spirit, and mind with indulgent treatments at amaranth spa, or relax at our infinity pool. Our holistic wellness focus ensures you feel refreshed, revitalised, and ready to embrace the electrifying energy of Bangkok.

Learn more

### Pet Friendly Privileges

Bring your pet for a tail-wagging good time with Kimpton Pets programme. Our motto is: Happy Pets, No Regrets! Whether your friend is furry, feathery, or scaly, we provide everything from pet menus to special amenities, and even pet sitter services to ensure you and your pet have a comfortable Kimpton experience.

Learn more

### Distinctive Dynamic Designs

Bangkok’s authentic spirit thrives in the contemporary luxury of our hotel’s distinctive design. Blending modern, linear architecture with organic materials and traditional Thai artisanship, we showcase the local culture amidst a tapestry of textures and visual delights.

### Eat + Drink

International inspiration meets rich local flavours at our seven unique restaurants and bars – sure to please your palate! Start with morning coffee in our lobby lounge, then visit our neighbourhood-inspired eatery for farm-to-plate delights. Have a drink at our cocktail bar and end the night on our rooftop terrace with stunning city views.

Learn more

### Feel Bangkok’s Vibes

Surrounded by lush greenery and cosmopolitan charisma, our boutique hotel rests on the leafy landscape of Langsuan Road near Lumphini Park, landmark attractions and urban excitement. Feel Bangkok’s vibes as we infuse the city’s energy into every element of our design and services to make your stay unforgettable.

Learn more

## FAQs for Maa-Lai Bangkok

Does Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok have a pool?

Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Located on the third floor, overlooking the lush Langsuan oasis, there is no better place to cool down and unwind after a long day of exploring.

Pool information

How much is parking at Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok?

Complimentary parking is available at Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok for hotel guests.

Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok provides 250 car parking spaces.

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok.

Pet policy description. All your furry friends are invited to chill out at Kimpton because we love animals as much as you do. No matter their size, weight, or breed, they will be pampered and treated as little guests. The best part: all at no extra charge.

Pet weight limit: No weight limit per pet

NO\_LIMIT pets allowed

Pets allowed: All pets allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok has 6 restaurants.

When is breakfast at Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok?

Check-in at Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok is conveniently located near Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), approximately 17.52 miles away (28.2 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok?

Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Siam Paragon-1.06 miles away (1.7 kilometers)
- Central World-0.75 miles away (1.2 kilometers)
- Lumphini Park-0.19 miles away (0.3 kilometers)
- Erawan Shrine-0.81 miles away (1.3 kilometers)
- Central Embassy-1.18 miles away (1.9 kilometers)
- Temple Of The Emerald Buddha
- Wat Arun

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Maa-Lai Bangkok puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
