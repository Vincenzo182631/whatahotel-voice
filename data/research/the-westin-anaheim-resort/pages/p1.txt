# Welcome to The Westin Anaheim Resort

## Anaheim Hotel Within Walking Distance to Disneyland

A haven of wellness and renewal, The Westin Anaheim Resort is a AAA Four Diamond hotel located across from Disneyland Resort and next to the Anaheim Convention Center. As a premier Anaheim hotel by Disneyland,...See More you can enjoy beautifully designed accommodations featuring luxury amenities and Westin Heavenly Beds. Discover a true family-friendly hotel in Anaheim with dining options including Fleming's Steakhouse, Puesto Mexican Restaurant, Tangerine Room Restaurant, and RISE Rooftop Lounge, offering views of Disney California Adventure Park. A short drive from Los Angeles and San Diego, our hotel near the Anaheim Convention Center is close to Angel Stadium of Anaheim, the Honda Center, and nearby beaches. Host weddings, meetings, and social events at our resort in Anaheim, California. Enjoy amenities such as an outdoor pool with cabanas and a 24-hour Westin WORKOUT Fitness Studio.See Less

## Modern Orchard Design

As the first new-build Westin hotel in over twenty-five years in Southern California, The Westin Anaheim Resort brings a new level of luxury accommodations to one of the most popular destinations in California. Constructed to AAA Four-Diamond standards, arranged around a spacious central courtyard, and massing gradually up to eight stories, the hotel’s contemporary classical architecture with biophilia design connects guests to nature in modern, relaxed surroundings.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Anaheim Resort?

The check-in time at The Westin Anaheim Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Anaheim Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Anaheim Resort.

What are the parking options at The Westin Anaheim Resort?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Anaheim Resort. Self parking is available at The Westin Anaheim Resort, with rates of $25.00 per hour, and $50.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $60.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Anaheim Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Anaheim Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Anaheim Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Anaheim Resort?

The closest airport near The Westin Anaheim Resort is John Wayne Airport-Orange County (SNA). SNA is located approximately 11.7 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Anaheim Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Anaheim Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does The Westin Anaheim Resort have a Westin Club lounge?

Yes, The Westin Anaheim Resort has Westin Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Westin Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
