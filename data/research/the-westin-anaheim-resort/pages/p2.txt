# Welcome to The Westin Anaheim Resort

## Accommodations

Find a restorative respite in beautiful and spacious hotel accommodations. Recharge upon arrival while being greeted with a cascading waterfall in the driveway and an airy interior lobby. The hotel is...See More invigorated with touches of nature. Feel welcome by the personal and friendly hotel staff, who strive to create your memorable stay. Each guestroom is meticulously designed to capture elegance and luxury. Guestrooms feature technology panels to control lighting, privacy notices, room temperature, and service requests. Signature Westin guestroom amenities include Westin Heavenly® Rain Shower, Westin Heavenly® Bed Pillow Top mattress made with DualCoolTM Technology fiber, Westin Heavenly® bath amenities, a curated Sleep Well Menu, mini-refrigerators, in-room safes, individual coffee machines, and Westin robes and slippers.See Less

### Theme Park View Rooms

Elevate your stay in one of the signature Theme Park View guestrooms, which have spectacular views of Disney California Adventure® Park. The Theme Park View deluxe guestrooms, studios, and suites are the optimal choice to feel the energy of the theme parks and watch the Disneyland® Resort fireworks. Most of the Theme Park View rooms offer the option for a standing balcony, or larger balcony area with...See More a seating area for two. Allow in all the essential daylight through the full sliding, cascading glass doors. Choose between a single king bed or two queen beds guestroom, a larger king bed studio room, or a one-bedroom king suite with a separated living and dining area.See Less

## Suite Guest Accommodations

Ideal for all travelers desiring an open living and dining area, separate from the bedroom. All suite guest rooms are furnished with a king bed and pull-out sofa. Choose from either the King bed Balcony Suite, King bed Theme Park View Suite, or the King bed Theme Park View Suite with Balcony. Nespresso® coffee machines with complimentary Nespresso® coffee are just for the suites.

## Studio Guest Accommodations

Enjoy the extra space with studio guestrooms. All studio guest rooms are furnished with a King bed, and many feature a pull-out sofa to accommodate up to four guests in the room. Studio rooms are popular for combining with standard guest rooms and may be requested upon reserving the rooms.

Enjoy Room For Up To 8 Guests

## Connecting Guest Rooms

Several guest rooms are connected to another guest room, and some guest rooms connect to a studio or 1 bedroom suite. Connecting two rooms may be requested upon arriving at the hotel, but it is not guaranteed. To guarantee connecting rooms, guests may book the Guaranteed Connecting Rooms package online or call Reservations and ask for guaranteed connecting rooms.

## In Room Dining

Relax in the privacy of your own guestroom while having access to a delicious array of dining favorites. Start your day with free range eggs served any style or refresh with our avocado toast served on grilled artisan sourdough bread. Wind down with healthy Westin Sleep Well Menu selections such as the quinoa salmon bowl to enhance your rest and enrich your wellbeing. In room dining service is offered daily at 24 hours/day.

### Presidential Suite

Stay in ultimate luxury. The presidential suite is a 2,030 square foot, two-bedroom, two and a half bathroom suite with extended balcony and superb views of the Disneyland® Resort theme parks. Included with the Presidential Suite is Club Lounge access, evening turn-down service, a full walk-in closet and full kitchen. Sleeps a maximum of six guests. For inquiries, please call (877) 264-9786.
