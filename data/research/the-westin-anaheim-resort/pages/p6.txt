## Experience the Magic at Disneyland® Resort

Being directly across Katella Avenue from Disney California Adventure® Park, the hotel is perfectly situated within walking distance to the Disneyland® Resort, about 15-20 minutes for most Disney fans....See More You can feel the energy of the park as you see the Incredicoaster® carts rush by, and catch a glimpse of the Ferris wheel, Pixar Pal-A-Round. As a Disney Good Neighbor Hotel, The Westin Anaheim Resort is designated in the highest category level of the Good Neighbor hotel rating system, the Superior Hotel Category. Insider tip is to connect with the concierge services associates to help plan your day in and around the theme parks.See Less

## Purchase Your Park Tickets

To enter one of the parks, each guest is required to have a theme park reservation in addition to a valid ticket for the same park on the same date. It’s highly recommended to purchase and reserve theme park tickets in advance. The hotel concierge associates can assist with Disney theme park ticket purchases and park reservations on-site, per availability.

### Directions to Disneyland® Resort From The Westin Anaheim Resort

If walking, exit the front of the hotel and walk left on Katella Ave, walk right at Disneyland Dr. (crossing Katella Ave), walk left at Paradise Pier Dr. (crossing Disneyland Dr.), follow the green street signs to “Downtown Disney," and walk through _Downtown Disney®_ District to the entrance of the theme parks.

### Additional Transportation

Although the hotel is walking distance to the parks, there are other transportation options:

Private car service.

ART (Anaheim Resort Transportation). Line 9 picks-up at bus stop in front of the hotel, at the corner of Disneyland Dr. and Katella Ave. Tickets must be purchased with ART directly. Please visit www.rideart.org for ticket purchases, schedules, and more.

Uber/Lyft: Available for pick-up and drop-off from near Disneyland East entrance (Harbor Blvd). Uber and Lyft reservations must be made on own through the respective apps.

Two Disney Resort Parking Lots: please click below on “Know Before You Go” and search for parking and more details.
