# Dining at The Westin Anaheim Resort

## Dining

Start off the day with the breakfast buffet, power up with a hearty lunch, or rewind and relax for dinner at the hotel’s signature restaurant, Tangerine Room. This restaurant offers an ever-changing menu...See More that celebrates seasonal ingredients and local flavors. Need fuel to go? Grab a gourmet coffee, sandwich, sunscreen or other sundries to-go from Blossom Café and Market. For the perfect afternoon snack or light refreshment in the evening, Bar 1030 is a welcome respite in the expansive hotel lobby. Bar 1030’s versatile menu emphasizes fresh Californian fare and Crafted at Westin® cocktails curated with local flair. After a full and adventurous day, treat yourself to the elegant offerings at Fleming's Prime Steakhouse & Wine Bar or Puesto's elevated Mexican cuisine. For dining with a view or after-dinner drinks, RISE Rooftop Lounge is the perfect place to take in Disneyland® Resort fireworks and Anaheim cityscape. Plus, on weekends enjoy live entertainment at RISE.See Less

### RISE Rooftop Lounge

When the sun goes down, RISE Rooftop Lounge heats up with tantalizing libations and unique spirits poured by a highly creative team of mixologists. The spectacular views overlooking Disneyland® Resort and live weekend entertainment make RISE one of Anaheim’s premier rooftop bars, which is why it requires reservations. The cuisine, a fun eclectic mixture of California tapas and bites, is perfect for...See More sharing with groups or sampling yourself. Popular menu items include California style pizzas, sushi, fresh Michelada oysters, and shareable salads. Pair with a nice glass of wine or craft cocktail while you soak in Southern California’s year-round convivial weather. RISE Rooftop Lounge was awarded OpenTable Diners’ Choice 2023.See Less

## Private Dining

Plan your next private event at one of the participating onsite outlets: Tangerine Room, RISE Rooftop Lounge, Fleming's, Puesto or in the hotel meeting space. Enjoy specially curated culinary experiences from award winning chefs and banquet teams. Choose from two classic private dining rooms at Tangerine Room for 11-40 guests each. RISE Rooftop Lounge features VIP seating areas and is available for buy-outs up to 200 guests. Fleming's and Puesto offer both private dining and buy-out options. View the Events page for more details on the hotel’s various event room capacities.

## In Room Dining

Relax in the privacy of your own guestroom while having access to a delicious array of dining favorites. Start your day with free range eggs served any style or refresh with our avocado toast served on grilled artisan sourdough bread. Wind down with healthy Westin Sleep Well Menu selections such as the quinoa salmon bowl to enhance your rest and enrich your wellbeing. In room dining service is offered daily at 24 hours/day.
