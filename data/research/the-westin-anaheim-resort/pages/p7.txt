A luxury hotel within walking distance of the magic of Disneyland.

Why book?
The Westin Anaheim Resort is an AAA Four-Diamond resort just minutes away from Disneyland and mere steps from the Anaheim Convention Center, with a rooftop lounge that offers incredible views of Disneyland’s weekend fireworks.
Set the scene
From the moment you enter the lobby, the Westin Anaheim Resort boasts family-friendly amenities that make this a standout property for those seeking a luxurious stay near the magic of Disneyland. Along with families heading out to the parks early in the morning, the hotel hosts convention attendees and business professionals, as it’s adjacent to the Anaheim Convention Center and boasts over 47,000 square feet of its own meeting space.
The backstory
The Westin Anaheim Resort opened in June 2021, and in the years since has proved that wellness and play can go hand in hand. Just look at the hotel’s signature WestinWorkout Fitness Studio, which occupies nearly 3,000 square feet of space, a resort-style pool, and a rooftop bar that overlooks Disneyland.
The rooms
All the accommodations at the resort are serene and airy, with luxury touches like rainfall showers, a curated Sleep Well menu, and cozy robes and slippers. Rooms feature a cool gray and cream color palette that’s warmed up with ambient lighting throughout the space. Comfortable beds and plush pillows will have you thinking about skipping the park and just enjoying a lazy day at the resort instead. Some rooms have a sliding glass door that opens to either a Juliet balcony or a traditional balcony with a set of chairs. Even better, many of the rooms offer a theme park view, so you can be greeted in the morning by views of Disney California Adventure, and fall asleep at night to the lights flashing on the Incredicoaster and Pixar Pal-A-Round.
Food and drink
The resort features seven different restaurants, though one not to miss is Rise Rooftop Lounge. The menu features delightful dishes such as duck carnitas tacos, shareable pizzas, and truffle fries paired with panoramic views of Anaheim and the Disneyland Resort. The rooftop bar also hosts a social hour every Monday through Thursday from 5:30 to 7:00 p.m., when select drinks and snacks are slightly discounted. And don’t ignore Bella’s Splash Pool Bar for the all-important relaxation day in the middle of your theme park vacation. The French onion toastie is an adult take on a classic grilled cheese, utilizing herb-butter brioche with Gruyère and white cheddar cheeses, and French-style onions in the middle.
The neighborhood/area
The Westin Anaheim Resort is located right in the middle of the action of Anaheim’s main tourist district. There’s a lot of foot and vehicle traffic in this area. That being said, the resort feels a world away from the bustle on the street thanks to tranquil accommodations, high-end amenities, and top-notch service. Most guests stay here due to its proximity to Disneyland, which is about a 15- to 20-minute walk away.
The service
Service at the resort blends casual chats and attention to detail into one overall delightful experience. And because the resort is a Disneyland Good Neighbor Hotel, you can buy tickets and get helpful tips about your park day from Disney experts on site.
For families
Families will love this property, not only because it’s close to the theme parks, but also because it feels a world away. After getting your fill of rides and Dole Whips, this relaxing retreat offers a place to calm down, enjoy a meal at a more leisurely pace, and even catch some fireworks without leaving your room. At the concierge desk, kids can pick up a travel journal to write down their special memories from vacation.
Accessibility
The hotel offers a host of accessibility options, including ADA-compliant rooms, elevators, and accessible parking. Service animals are welcome with no additional fee.
Anything left to mention?
This is one of the further properties from Disneyland, so keep that in mind if you’re traveling with children who may not want to walk another 15-20 minutes back to the hotel after a long day at the parks. However, ride-shares are easily available to and from the resort.
