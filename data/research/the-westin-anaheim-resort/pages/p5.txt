# Welcome to The Westin Anaheim Resort

## Experiences

Located across from the Disneyland® Resort and adjacent to the Anaheim Convention Center, the hotel is close to popular Southern California attractions, making it the perfect starting point for a vacation...See More in Orange County. Catch a baseball game at Angel Stadium, cheer on the Anaheim Ducks ice hockey team at the Honda Center or enjoy a concert at the House of Blues Anaheim. Connect with the community and savor local flavors at the vibrant food hall in downtown Anaheim, The Anaheim Packing House, or at one of Anaheim’s popular craft breweries: Brewery X, Bottle Logic, Unsung and more. For those who want time in the surf and sand, the hotel is a short drive to popular beach cities such as Huntington Beach, Newport Beach, and Laguna Beach. For those who enjoy shopping, luxury retail center South Coast Plaza and The Outlets at Orange are both a short drive from the hotel. For those extending their vacation in Southern California, Anaheim is located between Los Angeles and San Diego.See Less

## Disneyland® Resort

Experience the magic at Disneyland® Resort, which is directly across the street from the hotel. Disney recommends purchasing theme park tickets and making park reservations in advance.

### Events at and near The Westin Anaheim Resort

Discover exciting experiences like on-site events at The Westin Anaheim Resort, and nearby events at the Disneyland® Resort, Anaheim Convention Center, Honda Center, Angel Stadium of Anaheim, surrounding beach communities, and more!

Learn More

### Anaheim Convention Center

The Westin Anaheim Resort is adjacent to the newly constructed North hall of the Anaheim Convention Center (ACC), which is the largest convention center on the West Coast. The LEED® certified 1.8 million-square-foot ACC is consistently ranked as one of the top meeting venues in the U.S. in terms of its size, attendance, overall building services and amenities. The Westin Anaheim Resort is the premier four-diamond hotel for those attending events at the ACC.

Learn More

## Sporting Events

The Westin Anaheim Resort is conveniently located a few miles down the street from Angel Stadium of Anaheim and the Honda Center. Experience the thrill of MLB baseball cheering on the local team. Honda Center hosts NHL and other professional sporting events, NCAA collegiate events, and amateur competitions. The hotel concierge associates are eager to assist in your game day planning. See you at the next sports event!

Explore Orange County Beaches

### Newport Beach

Beyond the radiant sun, sand and surf, discover the sophisticated charm of Newport Beach, California. From sunset cruises to surf lessons, wine tastings to harbor-front promenades, there is so much to do in this world famous city.

Learn More

### Huntington Beach

Surf City USA boasts 10 miles (16 kilometers) of wide open sandy beaches, making it a paradise for families, couples, dogs, and surfers. The endless summer weather offers relaxation, things to do, and oceanfront dining year-round. Some visitors' favorites include biking, beach bonfire pits, kayaking, and of course, the iconic Huntington Beach Pier.

Learn More

### Laguna Beach

The artist colony of Laguna Beach is one of Orange County’s hidden gems. The city’s topography is unique to other California coastal cities with its miles of coves and beaches where visitors can explore sea caves, tide pools, ocean side bluffs, natural tide pools and of course, sandy beaches, all within city limits.

Learn More

Bringing the Westin well-being experience to our youngest guests, the Westin Family program draws inspiration from nature, with an emphasis on wonder, exploration and accomplishment. Activities offered at the hotel include: Find Your Adventure Travel Journal, Local Activity Guide for self-guided exploration, The Westin Eat Well Menu for Kids.

## WestinWORKOUT®

Open 24/7, the 2,860 square feet WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio is outfitted with floor-to-ceiling views of the resort’s outdoor pool alongside state-of-the-art equipment including Peloton bikes, TRX functional training equipment, free weights and cardiovascular equipment. Post workout, or after a long day at the park, enjoy the complimentary use of Hyperice percussion massagers that will help relieve tension, unlock sore muscles, and prime your body and mind to be at their best.

## Live Music and Concerts

Anaheim and Orange County boast some of the best venues for the live music, performances, and concerts. The House of Blues Anaheim is walking distance from The Westin Anaheim Resort. Their bar and restaurant serves to enhance the top acts that the venue draws. The 2,200-seat concert hall hosts both up-and-coming acts and your favorites. Other top venues nearby for live music, performances and concerts include Honda Center, City National Grove of Anaheim, Segerstrom Center for the Arts, and The Ranch.

Learn More

Orange County is a Shopper's Paradise

### South Coast Plaza

South Coast Plaza is the premier shopping destination in Southern California, about a 30-minute drive from the hotel. Some of the iconic brands include Louis Vuitton, Versace, Mikimoto, Madewell, Hublot, Breitling, Tudor, John Varvatos, and Gentle Monster to name a few. We invite you to visit soon, and return often, as there is always something enticing to experience at South Coast Plaza.

Learn More

### Outlets at Orange

The closest outlet shopping to The Westin Anaheim Resort is the Outlets at Orange, Orange County's premiere outlet shopping destination. The outlets feature more than 120 name-brand outlets and value retailers at knockout prices plus great dining and entertainment options. This shopping center is the perfect destination for vacations whether you're traveling near or far.

Learn More

### Downtown Disney District

A dazzling Disney destination awaits! Get together with family and friends for exhilarating entertainment, unforgettable food, spirited sips, fanciful shopping and magical moments. The Downtown Disney District is located in the heart of the Disneyland Resort, so you can experience the enchantment of Disney—without a theme park ticket!

Learn More

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Anaheim Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Anaheim Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Two Peloton Bikes, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Anaheim Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Anaheim Resort has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Swimming Pool (Outdoor)Whirlpool

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Anaheim Resort?

Muzeo Museum and Cultural Center, Irvine Regional Park, Santa Ana Zoo, Anaheim Founders' Park, Arboretum and Botanical Garden at Cal State Fullerton and more are nearby The Westin Anaheim Resort.
