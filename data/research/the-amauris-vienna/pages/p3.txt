## The Amauris Philosophy

```
The Amauris is named after the butterfly genus Amauris, which includes the monarch butterfly. Metaphorically speaking, our guests are like the monarch butterfly.
One might think that butterflies are simply pretty, but they are much more than what meets the eye. They travel long distances, pollinate flowers, act as messengers of change in the environment, and are incredibly inspiring in their beauty.
```

## Relais & Châteaux

```
Founded in 1954, Relais & Châteaux today comprises an association of over 580 unique and independent hotels and restaurants. Hoteliers and chefs share a passion for their craft and strive to build authentic relationships with their guests.
From the French Provence and the vineyards of Napa Valley to the beaches of the Indian Ocean, Relais & Châteaux is present on five continents. The association invites you to experience the art of living of diverse cultures and to discover the fascinating history of humanity.
```
