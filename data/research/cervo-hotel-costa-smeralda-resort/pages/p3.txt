## Modern Design with Authentic Interiors

Fully refurbished in 2019, Il Portico Bar features a stylish terrace with comfortable, contemporary décor. The interiors also deserve special mention: built in the 1970s, they retain original floors made of local trachyte stone. The bar counter is hand-crafted from steel and silver, while the bathrooms are fitted with artisanal taps and decorated with Assuan marble.
