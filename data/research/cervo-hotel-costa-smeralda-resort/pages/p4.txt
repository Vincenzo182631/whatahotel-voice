## Pevero Golf Club

Set in an extraordinary natural landscape, Pevero Golf Club offers a truly unique golfing experience. Surrounded by breathtaking views and shaped by the rugged beauty of Sardinia’s coastline, this world-renowned course combines technical sophistication with the pure pleasure of nature — an unforgettable destination for golf lovers.

18-Hole Course

The 6,107-meter, par-72, 18-hole course — with handicaps of 36 for both ladies and gentlemen — is set amid a windswept landscape of sculpted granite formations, natural lakes, and the captivating fragrance of juniper, myrtle, and wild strawberry typical of the Mediterranean maquis. The Mistral — the famed northwesterly wind of the region — heightens the challenge of every shot, creating a thrilling experience.

Pevero Golf Club

Renowned for its unspoiled natural beauty, exclusive atmosphere, and unique technical design, Pevero Golf Club ranks among the most fascinating, memorable, and challenging courses in the world. Facilities include an expert putting and pitching green, a bunker, and a chipping green, as well as a driving range with 20 outdoor and five indoor bays — ideal for instruction and practice.

### Pevero Health Trail

Inspired by a love of nature and a desire to reveal a different side of the Costa Smeralda, the Health Trail paths offer an intense workout for runners or mountain bikers, as well as relaxing family walks. Along the trail, eleven exercise stations equipped with benches and fitness gear bear the names of local plant species. The route also features several evocative monuments of Sardinia’s rural...See More past.See Less

Costa Smeralda

A variety of paths lead visitors through ancient tracks and traditional dry-stone walls, guiding them to natural and historical landmarks. Perched above Grande Pevero Bay, the “Madonnina dello Speronello” commemorates the flood that struck the Tuscan village of Montalto di Castro. Another highlight, “Lu Rotu,” dating back to the early 20th century, was once a site for wheat processing.

Instagram Point

The “Stazzo di Monte Zoppu”, with its traditional stone animal enclosures, offers a fascinating glimpse into Sardinia’s rural heritage. At the summit of Monte Zoppu, photography lovers will find a spectacular Instagram Point—the perfect spot to capture the timeless beauty of the Costa Smeralda.

Monte Zoppu

The route is mostly straightforward, except for the stretch leading to the summit of Monte Zoppu, which is better suited for experienced walkers. This trail offers peaceful walks through Mediterranean scrubland, as well as opportunities for jogging or hiking with enchanting sea views. In the Grande Pevero area, part of the path is covered with a fine layer of white sand.

Pevero Health Trail

The Pevero Health Trail features five entrances, each with a nearby parking area and clearly marked with “START” on the map. You can begin your walk from: the Club House at the Pevero Golf Club car park; Grande Pevero Beach, using the entrance and parking area near Piccolo Pevero; Piccolo Romazzino, Hotel Romazzino, or Cala Liccia, with parking available just beyond the hotel in the Porto Liccia area.

Expert Route

Along the map and the trail itself, you will find arrow-shaped rustic signs and red-and-white painted stones marking the way. Pay attention to the icons that indicate the recommended use of each section of the trail. In addition to points of interest and designated fitness stations, the entire route is divided into its optimal activity types: mountain biking, walking, or expert trekking.

## Surroundings

Costa Smeralda and its surroundings offer endless opportunities for discovery, both on land and at sea. Embark on a journey full of surprises and breathtaking views.

Surroundings Highlights

La Maddalena

Designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1994, the La Maddalena Archipelago comprises seven major islands. Spend the day exploring pristine beaches and hidden coves accessible only by boat. Dive into crystal-clear waters teeming with fish, and end your day with a magical sunset that paints the horizon in tropical hues.

Arzachena

The town of Arzachena, located in the heart of the Costa Smeralda, boasts 88 kilometers of coastline dotted with charming coves and bays that have made Sardinia’s sea world-famous. Beyond its spectacular shoreline, the surrounding natural landscapes are equally captivating — from lush countryside to the Saloni ponds, where visitors can admire a variety of marsh birds in their natural habitat.

Stella Maris

The Church of Stella Maris, designed in the 1960s by renowned architect Busiri Vici, is a gem of Mediterranean modernism. Inside, visitors can admire remarkable works of art — including bronze doors by Minguzzi, a priceless De Martino pipe organ, and two ancient German crucifixes. Outside, the church rewards visitors with panoramic views over Porto Cervo, one of the most picturesque scenes on the coast.

Gola di Su Gorroppu

One of Sardinia’s most iconic natural landmarks, the Gola di Su Gorroppu gorge is steeped in legend. According to local tales, a strip of magical land exists within the canyon—those who walk across it are blessed with long life. While no one knows precisely where this mystical spot lies, the Ogliastra region proudly holds one of the deepest canyons in Europe, an awe-inspiring destination for hikers and nature lovers alike.

Golfo Aranci

Rising 344 meters above sea level, with 200 meters directly overlooking the sea, the Capo Figari promontory can be reached on foot through the picturesque Cala Moresca or by boat along the enchanting coast of Cala Greca. Its rugged cliffs and sweeping views make it one of the most breathtaking locations in Sardinia.

Isole di Li Nibani

Lying southeast of Porto Cervo, the Li Nibani Islands are part of a protected international marine park. The bay of Porto Li Nibani offers uninhabited, quiet coves where visitors can enjoy rare tranquility and stunning vistas of the Costa Smeralda. Nearby, Monte Zoppu rewards hikers and athletes with spectacular panoramic views across the coast.

Panoramica di Micalosu

A drive along the Micalosu panoramic road, scented with wild gorse, is an experience to remember. A scenic route connects Arzachena to the coast between Cannigione and La Conia, winding through rolling hills and offering sweeping views over the Gulf of Arzachena and the La Maddalena archipelago—a perfect afternoon escape.

Nuraghe

In Arzachena, just a few kilometers from the hotel, visitors can explore one of Sardinia’s most important Nuragic archaeological sites. These ancient stone towers, known as nuraghe, date back to around 1800 BC and represent the architectural genius of the Nuragic Civilization. Across Sardinia, over 7,000 nuraghe—some remarkably complex—bear silent witness to this ancient culture. Since 1997, they have been recognized by UNESCO as part of the island’s World Heritage.

Porto Cervo

Each year, thousands of yachts and sailboats anchor in Porto Cervo’s bay, drawn by its lively atmosphere and the allure of the Piazzetta delle Chiacchiere. Wander through the village’s charming alleys and admire the distinctive Costa Smeralda architecture. The hamlet of Arzachena took the name Porto Cervo—literally “Deer Port”—from the shape of its bay, which resembles a deer’s head.

Porto Rotondo

Located within the municipality of Olbia, Porto Rotondo is home to some of the most beautiful beaches in the Costa Smeralda, including Spiaggia dei Sassi, Punta Volpe, and Spiaggia Ira, whose fine white sand evokes tropical shores. Famed for its vibrant nightlife and elegant bars, Porto Rotondo is also perfect for a leisurely cocktail in the main Piazzetta, soaking in the glamor of Sardinia’s social scene.

Fungo di Arzachena

According to legend, Sardinia’s first inhabitants sought refuge beneath this natural rock formation in prehistoric times. During the Roman era, the area was known as Turubulus Maior, meaning “large mushroom.” Shaped over millennia by wind and rain, this remarkable granite formation—resembling a giant mushroom—has become a symbol of Arzachena.

Rally of Costa Smeralda

Since 2004, the Rally of Costa Smeralda has brought world-class drivers together each year between late May and early June. The thrilling event takes place on dirt tracks and winding roads that cut through the mountains of Porto Cervo and the surrounding Gallura region, marking the Italian stage of the World Rally Championship.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort?
