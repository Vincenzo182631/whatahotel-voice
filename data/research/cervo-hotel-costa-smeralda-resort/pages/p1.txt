## Exquisite and Creative

From timeless international classics to enticing exotic flavors — all inspired by fresh local ingredients from land and sea — discover a range of culinary experiences, each with its own captivating view of the Marina, blossoming gardens, and the breathtaking Sardinian panorama.

### Cervo Bar

Overlooking the Marina and the Piazzetta, Agorà — the Cervo hotel’s elegant bar and lounge — is the place to enjoy the chef’s specialties, light lunches, aperitifs, and after-dinner cocktails. Bartenders craft drinks to the sound of live piano music.

Nuna al Sole embodies a warm sense of community, connecting like-minded souls in a relaxed atmosphere. It is the perfect spot to socialize while enjoying delicious Mediterranean dishes prepared with the fresh ingredients. Nuna al Sole opens on April 1st.

## Breathtaking Views

The extraordinary stretch of northeastern Sardinian coastline known as the Costa Smeralda — or “the Emerald Coast” — remained largely undiscovered until the early 1960s, when a group of investors purchased 5,000 acres of this prized shoreline, home to fine white-sand beaches and coves lapped by the most transparent Mediterranean waters.

Learn More

## The Heart of Village Life

In 1964, construction of the village’s original core began under the creative direction of architect Luigi Vietti. The iconic Piazzetta—destined to become the heart of life in the Costa Smeralda—had already been paved, while Cervo Hotel, along with the first houses and the Marina of Porto Cervo, was taking shape as a defining element of the village.

## A Subtle Presence

The architecture unfolds in a palette of soft reds, pinks, yellows, and oranges. Vietti himself explained that, when designing Cervo Hotel, he sought to convey a subtle presence—one that blends seamlessly into the landscape and welcomes travelers arriving by sea. In the early years of the Costa Smeralda, the most common way to reach the destination was by sea in private boats. He chose to design sinuous forms and adorn them with warm colors that stand out against the blue of the sea and sky.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort?

The check-in time at Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 3.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort?

On-site parking is available at Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort?

The closest airport near Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort is Olbia Costa Smeralda Airport (OLB). OLB is located approximately 35.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
