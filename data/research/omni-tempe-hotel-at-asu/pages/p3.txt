## Tempe, Arizona

### Stay at our vibrant downtown Tempe hotel

Experience the best of Arizona at the AAA Four Diamond-rated Omni Tempe Hotel. Situated in Tempe’s newly revitalized downtown and located within a five-minute walk of Arizona State University's Tempe campus, our hotel is ideally situated. New guest rooms and suites are a cool contrast to the Sonoran desert landscape, blending modern comfort and amenities with Southwest style. Keep up with your workout routine in our 24-hour fitness center, fully equipped with Peloton bikes, StairClimbers and more. After a busy day, unwind on the expansive rooftop pool deck or have drinks at our rooftop lounge amid views of the city. You can also enjoy meals from our innovative restaurants, including a coffee shop and an American restaurant serving farm-fresh ingredients and craft cocktails.

Whether you’re here for adventure or relaxation, Omni Tempe is your launch point for exploring Arizona — from boutique shopping on Mill Avenue to hiking scenic trails at Papago Park and Tempe Butte. Take a stroll around Tempe Town Lake or soak in the energy of this walkable, vibrant city. With nearly 36,000 square feet of event space, we also offer an exceptional setting for weddings, events, and gatherings of all kinds.

## Traveler's Insights

### Does Omni Tempe hotel have rooms for ASU Move-in and ASU Family Weekend?

Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU has rooms available for guests attending ASU Family Weekend, ASU Move-in week, ASU Homecoming week, and ASU Graduation ceremony. Our hotel is located steps from ASU Tempe campus on University Drive and Mill Ave. Check out our ASU upcoming events and activities planned for our guests at the hotel.

### What is there to do in Tempe, AZ?

Tempe hosts many things to do including:

- Sun Devil Stadium
- Desert Botanical Gardens
- Phoenix Zoo
- Papago Park
- Tempe Beach Park

At Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU, you can sightsee at numerous parks, participate in special events, indulge in great food, and more. See a full list of things to do here.

### Does Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU have a pool?

Omni Tempe ASU at Hotel has one pool located on the rooftop of the hotel. The pool is open daily from 6:00 a.m. - 10:00 p.m., hotel keycard access required.

A limited amount of cabana and pool passes are available to non-overnight hotel guests and can be purchased through Resortpass.

### What are the property amenities at Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU?

Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU is home to world-class amenities including:

- A rooftop pool with private cabanas
- A fully-equipped fitness center with Peloton bikes
- 36,000 square feet of meeting and event space
- Four dining outlets - one full-service restaurants, a coffee shop, a rooftop bar and a pool bar & grill
- Valet parking with a nightly charge

### Does Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU offer wifi?

Yes, complimentary WiFi is available at Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU for overnight guests. Select Guest loyalty members always enjoy complimentary WiFi at our hotels and resorts.

### What is the best area to stay in when visiting Tempe?

Downtown Tempe is an ideal place to stay in the city to be near plenty of things to do, dining and entertainment options. Our hotel in Tempe, AZ is conveniently located downtown and near Arizona State University, perfect for your next getaway.

### How many days should I spend in Tempe?

Tempe is a great destination for a long weekend getaway. Travelers find that 2-3 days is the perfect amount of time to visit the city’s main attractions. Scottsdale and Phoenix are also a short drive away, and offer plenty of attractions to explore during your trip. Browse our 48-hour itinerary for more ideas.

### What is the best month to visit Tempe?

March-May and September-November are ideal times to visit Tempe. In spring and fall, enjoy mild temperatures perfect for outdoor activities like hiking and exploring nearby trails. During fall, you can also immerse yourself in the city’s college culture and attend a Sun Devils football game at Mountain America Stadium.

### Where is Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU located?

Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU is located in the heart of downtown Tempe, on the north part of Arizona State University campus. The physical address is 7 East University Drive, Tempe, Arizona 85281.

### What is the closest airport to Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU?

Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU is located near the following airport:

- Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX) is 4.1 miles from the hotel (approximately 9 minutes).

- Taxi service to/from PHX Airport: Approximately $23 one way

- Public transportation options include Tempe Street Car, Light Rail, and Bus Service.

### How much is parking at the hotel?

- Valet Parking Only - $39 Overnight per Vehicle

- 1-2 hours parking \| $15 per vehicle

- Daytime parking \| $25 per vehicle

- Self-Parking is Not Available. Guests seeking self-parking options should visit downtowntempe.com/get-around/parking.

### What time is check-in and check-out?

- Check-in is at4 PM
- Check-out is at 11 AM

Guests must be 18 years of age to rent a room.

### Is the hotel pet friendly?

Omni Tempe Hotel at ASU is a pet-friendly hotel. Guests must have a valid credit card on their reservation. The weight of the pet is not to exceed 25 lbs. A non-refundable cleaning fee of $150 will be charged per stay per room for the first pet, $50 per second pet. No more than 2 pets per room. If guest plans to have two or more animals in the room, they must contact the hotel directly to discuss. Only dogs are allowed. Extreme or wild animals (snakes, birds, etc.) are not authorized. In the event a dog begins to bark or is the cause of guest complaints, the guest will be asked to remove the pet. The pet must be placed in a carrier when Hotel Associates enter the room. The guest will be required to sign a document accepting complete financial responsibility for any damage caused by the pet. Registered Service animals traveling with are exempt from the pet policy.
