# Welcome to Le Méridien Munich

## Discover our contemporary hotel in Munich city center

Our premium hotel in Munich, Le Méridien Munich reflects the city’s vibrant art, culture, and rich heritage. Our Munich hotel in the city center, and directly opposite the Central Train Station, places...See More guests near medieval palaces, Gothic churches, beer gardens, Viktualienmarkt, shopping streets, green parks, and cultural gems. For business travelers and city explorers, this Munich hotel near the train station offers 381 stylish, light-filled rooms and suites, high-speed internet, sophisticated conference rooms, onsite parking, and a multilingual team. Unlock Art™ brings free admittance to the MUCA museum, while the serene spa, pool, and sauna offer a peaceful place to unwind. Guests often praise the convenient location, helpful service, comfortable beds, and rich breakfast. This business hotel in Munich also connects guests to Bavaria’s lakes, mountains, and countryside. For a memorable stay at a hotel in Munich Germany, this is the place to be.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Munich?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Munich is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Munich allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Munich. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 35.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €25.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Munich?

On-site parking is available at Le Méridien Munich. Self parking is available at Le Méridien Munich, with rates of €5.00 per hour, and €35.00 per day. Valet parking is available for €35.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Munich have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Munich has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Munich booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Munich?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Munich is Munich Airport (MUC). MUC is located approximately 35.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Munich have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Le Méridien Munich has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
