## Experiences

Le Méridien Munich warmly welcomes you to the Bavarian capital, a city full of art, architecture, nature, culture, and tradition. Relax in beer gardens and street cafés, stroll the English Garden or the...See More Isar River, and visit Eisbach, the unique surfers’ spot. Shopping reaches beyond the city center into Schwabing and Glockenbach. At our Munich hotel near Oktoberfest, you are close to Theresienwiese, Stachus, and Marienplatz, making it ideal for guest seeking hotels near Theresienwiese, Munich. Guests praise the central location for sightseeing and Oktoberfest, with Munich Central Station across the street for day trips to lakes, mountains, and Neuschwanstein Castle. ### A Munich Walk from Le Méridien

After a good night's rest in the LM Bed and a strengthening meal, you can start right by foot at the hotel and walk direction Stachus - the gate to the pedestrian zone. Along the famous Frauenkirche with its two towers, the town hall (the "Glockenspiel" takes place at 11:00 am!) and after the ascent of "Old Peter" church tower - best views guaranteed.

Walk from Viktualienmarkt!

## Afternoon in Munich

Take your time and watch the market's hustle and bustle before you head to Odeonsplatz and walk through English Garden to the Eisbach Wave - surfers all day and all year long - in the center of Munich. Now, you have the choice of art at Haus der Kunst or more walking, before the day ends in one of the two beer gardens close by: at the Chinese Tower or a small lake in the park, the "Seehaus beer garden".

Experience Art with Le Méridien Munich

## Visit MUCA as Part of Our UnlockArt™ Program

Through Le Méridien's Unlock Art™ program, our hotel guests enjoy complimentary access to Germany's first museum dedicated to urban and contemporary art: the MUCA - Museum of Urban and Contemporary Art. Explore fascinating rotating special exhibitions showcasing works from renowned local and internationally acclaimed artists. The MUCA is not only captivating on the inside but also a visual spectacle from the outside: Its facades are adorned with impressive large-scale artworks that will captivate you from afar.

## Munich’s Beer Festival

The world's largest public festival takes place from mid September until the beginning of October every year - a great spectacle that should definitely not be missing on your bucket list. Le Méridien Munich is walking distance from the fair grounds and already thrilled to celebrate the next Munich’s Beer Festival together with its guests. When will we welcome you?

### Walking Distance to Munich’s Beer Festival

As easy as it can be: within 10 minutes' walk from the hotel you reach the Theresienwiese festival grounds. Just follow the signs or paintings on the pavements - at Le Méridien Munich you stay just a stone's throw away from bavarian tradition, rollercoasters and beer tents.

### A Day at Munich’s Beer Festival

The outfits at Munich’s Beer Festival are as exciting as the fairground rides and stands; marvel at the world's largest beer festival. We suggest the Teufelsrad ("Devil's Wheel") fairground ride and Schichtl's Original Magical Variety Show. Enjoy Bavarian specialities such as the "Halbes Hendl" fried chicken or drink a litre or "Mass", of beer during a trip to the Munich’s Beer Festival.

### Back to Hotel after Munich’s Beer Festival

Admittedly, at Munich’s Beer Festival, you have the potential to get a bit lost. Good thing that Le Méridien Munich hotel is close to the festival grounds at Theresienwiese! Our Guest Relations Team shares a local tip: keep an eye on the St. Paul's church with its clearly perceptible tower on the right. Wherever you are at the festival - this is the direction back to Le Méridien Hotel.

### Festive Season in Munich

From Le Méridien Munich, the festive season is within one's reach no matter what you are looking for: Christmas shopping in the pedestrian zone, winter festival Tollwood at Theresienwiese - or do you prefer one of the less popular festive markets? Our Guest Relations Team is happy to share their insider tips.

### Winter Walks in Munich

From the hotel, walk to Stachus with its rink. Along the famous Frauenkirche to the town hall and its Christmas market, then enjoy a second breakfast: Weißwurst at Viktualienmarkt. Watch the market's hustle & bustle before walking to Odeonsplatz, through English Garden to Eisbach Wave: surfers all year long! Warm-up then with art at Haus der Kunst before ending at the Chinese Tower festive market.

### Christmas Shopping in Munich

From hotel Le Méridien, it's just a short walk to Stachus and the pedestrian zone. Here or at Maximilianstraße, shopping opportunities reach from sports to interior to luxury fashion. Festive markets for a mulled wine break are just around the corner. For individual small shops, Gärtnerplatz or Schwabing might be preferred. Just ask the local experts at Le Méridien Munich – they know the best spots.

## Autumn in Munich

Experience the autumn in Munich during your stay at Le Méridien Munich and let yourself be enchanted by the city's unique atmosphere. Situated in the heart of the city, our hotel offers the perfect starting...See More point to discover the many facets of a Munich autumn. Start your day with a leisurely breakfast and get in the right mood for your tour of Munich. Explore nearby attractions such as Marienplatz with its famous Glockenspiel, the magnificent Residenz, or the Viktualienmarkt, where you can sample regional specialties. A must-visit in the autumn is the English Garden, one of the largest urban parks in the world. Take a leisurely stroll and watch the city life unfold or enjoy a refreshing Mass of beer in the beer garden at the Chinese Tower.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Le Méridien Munich have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Le Méridien Munich offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Technogym Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Le Méridien Munich have a pool on-site?

Yes, Le Méridien Munich has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does Le Méridien Munich have an on-site spa?

Yes, Le SPA is located on-site at Le Méridien Munich.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Le Méridien Munich?

Hofbraeuhaus, English Garden (Englischer Garten), Olympic Park, Stadium + Tower, Hofgarten, Viktualienmarkt and more are nearby Le Méridien Munich.
