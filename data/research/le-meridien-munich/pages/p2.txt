# Modern Premium Hotel Rooms in Munich for Culture-Seekers

## Accommodations

Le Méridien Munich is the ideal accommodation for art lovers seeking a blend of style and comfort. Each of our 381 Munich hotel rooms and suites offers a serene retreat from city life, complete with upscale...See More furnishings and chic interiors. Warm colors and an expertly-designed lighting concept create the signature bright and inspiring Le Méridien atmosphere. Located in the heart of the Bavarian capital, our hotel is just steps from major cultural sites, allowing guests to explore the city’s rich heritage while staying productive with business commitments. The prime location and unique ambiance of Le Méridien Munich makes our modern hotel rooms in Munich perfect for both leisure and business stays.See Less
