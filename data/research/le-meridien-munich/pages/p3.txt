# Modern Munich Restaurants & Bars in the Heart of the City

## Dining

Le Méridien Munich warmly welcomes you to the Bavarian capital, a city full of art, architecture, nature, culture, and tradition. Relax in beer gardens and street cafés, stroll the English Garden or the...See More Isar River, and visit Eisbach, the unique surfers’ spot. Shopping reaches beyond the city center into Schwabing and Glockenbach. At our Munich hotel near Oktoberfest, you are close to Theresienwiese, Stachus, and Marienplatz, making it ideal for guest seeking hotels near Theresienwiese, Munich. Guests praise the central location for sightseeing and Oktoberfest, with Munich Central Station across the street for day trips to lakes, mountains, and Neuschwanstein Castle. Cozy Moments in the Heart of Munich

## Enjoy Time Together as the Weather Cools Outside

When fall and winter cast a special atmosphere over Munich, spending time together becomes all the more precious. At Restaurant Irmi, a warm, inviting retreat awaits you right in the heart of the city. Take a seat with friends at large wooden tables, discover modern Bavarian cuisine, and enjoy dishes that are perfect for tasting and sharing.

With freshly tapped beer, regional specialties, and good conversation, you can experience the cooler season at its best.

When It Gets Cold Outside

## A Wonderful Way To Start Fall and Winter Days

Outside, Munich shows its cool, fall-like or wintry side; inside, a relaxing start to the day awaits. Start your morning with a varied breakfast featuring hot and cold dishes, fresh pastries, and aromatic coffee specialties. Whether you’re heading out on a city tour, heading to a business meeting, or enjoying a day off in Munich: Take your time, let the morning begin in peace, and start your day well-fueled.

Bavarian Delights for Food Lovers

### Munich Restaurant by Heart

Irmi's kitchen philosophy is as simple as it is successful: local, honest, really good food. Bavarian "Brotzeit" in Family Style, light and hefty Munich dishes plus locally brewed - and freshly draught - Giesinger beer. Relaxed date or group dinner: sharing is caring at Irmi - at the latest when it comes to the famous "Kaiserschmarrn" for dessert.

From the First Coffee to the Last Drink

## Meeting Place: Bar Longitude 11°

Bar Longitude 11° lies between the city’s pulse and the tranquility of the moment. It’s a place where a cup of coffee becomes a brief respite, a drink turns into a long evening, and a chance encounter becomes a special urban moment. With aromatic coffee, select cocktails, and light bites, it’s a place for special encounters and long conversations.
