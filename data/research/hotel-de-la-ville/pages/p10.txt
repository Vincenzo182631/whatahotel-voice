## Every Hour. A New Rome.

From above the Spanish Steps and Trinità dei Monti, our live 360° panorama reveals the city in all its splendour – from the first glow of dawn to golden hour, sunset and starlit skies.

St. Peter’s dome, the Pincio and Villa Medici’s gardens rising above a sea of terracotta rooftops. A glimpse of the extraordinary perspective that awaits at Hotel de la Ville.

## Your Skyline Escape

The camera offers a glimpse. Staying here puts you at the heart of the panorama. Wake to sweeping views from the De La Ville Panoramic Penthouse, or watch Rome change with the light over drinks at Cielo – an unforgettable view of the Eternal City, experienced in person.

### Cielo Bar

Perfectly situated for a sparkling summer, our terrace bar Cielo offers Rome’s most magical, panoramic views across the historical skyline. Sip on cocktails under the open skies or starry nights.

### Cielo Restaurant

Step out onto the glamorous rooftop terrace and breathe deeply, looking out at the staggering skyline of the Eternal City unfolding before you.

### De La Ville Panoramic Penthouse

Across the seventh and eighth floors of our 18th-century palazzo, the penthouse unfolds as a private residence with six bedrooms, and terraces framing views of the Spanish Steps.
