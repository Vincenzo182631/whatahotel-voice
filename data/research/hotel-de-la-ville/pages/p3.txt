# Mosaico: An Italian Restaurant in Rome at Hotel de la Ville

- Hotel de la Ville
- Dining
- Mosaico

### Whether dining al fresco in the beautiful courtyard or in the vibrant dining room flanked by the stunning open kitchen, guests will be captured by the natural light lifting every corner of the venue making it a very bright space.

### **Breakfast**

Our breakfast buffet is a celebration of seasonal ingredients and local delicacies, from baskets of freshly baked bread and homemade cakes to vibrant salads, cheeses, and seasoned meats. Complementing this banquet, our à la carte menu offers a choice of hot and cold dishes, such as our must-try Carbonara scrambled eggs.

Additional Information

We wish for everyone to feel comfortable, so whilst we do not operate a dress code, we kindly recommend that our guests are respectful of the environment we have created here at Hotel de la Ville and refrain from wearing sportswear, shorts and beach shoes in our restaurants. Our guests within the hotel usually opt for a smart-casual style.
