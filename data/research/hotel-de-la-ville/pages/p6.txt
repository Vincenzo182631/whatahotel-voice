# Café Ginori: Café & Restaurant in Rome

- Hotel de la Ville
- Dining
- Café Ginori

### In partnership with Ginori 1735, Italy’s foremost porcelain and lifestyle brand, Café Ginori is ‘the’ place to meet, play, dine, shop, and be inspired. Drawing from Ginori’s handcrafted aesthetic, born from almost three centuries of effortlessly bridging design and daily life, our all day dining concept brings a dynamic twist to classic Italian cuisine.
