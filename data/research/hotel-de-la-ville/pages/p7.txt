### Located on the top floor of Hotel de la Ville, at one of Rome’s most coveted addresses, Cielo overlooks the Eternal City. Widely regarded as the city’s most glamorous rooftop restaurant, it offers an unforgettable sunset experience.

Venice regained

### The Menu

Renowned for his creative flair, Fulvio Pierangelini brings a new dining concept to Cielo, set against sweeping views over Rome’s rooftops.

The menu celebrates Mediterranean flavours, from refined seafood to seasonal ingredients, expressed through the Chef’s distinctive, elegant style.

Additional Information

We wish for everyone to feel comfortable, so whilst we do not operate a dress code, we kindly recommend that our guests are respectful of the environment we have created here at Hotel de la Ville and refrain from wearing sportswear, shorts and beach shoes in our restaurants. Our guests within the hotel usually opt for a smart-casual style.
