- Hotel de la Ville

### Perfectly situated for sunny days and sparkling nights, our terrace bar Cielo offers Rome’s most magical, panoramic views across the historical skyline.

### The Menus

Under open skies or star-filled nights, sip cocktails and discover menus shaped by the signature style of Chef Fulvio Pierangelini.

### Every Hour. A New Rome.

From Hotel de la Ville, our live 360° panorama reveals the city in all its splendour – from the first glow of dawn to golden hour, sunset and starlit skies. St. Peter’s dome, the Pincio and Villa Medici’s gardens rising above a sea of terracotta rooftops.

Additional Information

Please note that Cielo Bar operates on a first-come, first-served basis and does not accept reservations.

We wish for everyone to feel comfortable, so whilst we do not operate a dress code, we kindly recommend that our guests are respectful of the environment we have created here at Hotel de la Ville and refrain from wearing sportswear, shorts and beach shoes in our restaurants. Our guests within the hotel usually opt for a smart-casual style.
