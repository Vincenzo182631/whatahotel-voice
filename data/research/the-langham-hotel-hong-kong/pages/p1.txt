### THE FAMED T’ANG COURT

T'ang Court has been recognised by the coveted Michelin Guide since 2009 consistently.

With three Michelin stars under its belt, the restaurant reflects the classic timelessness of The Tang Dynasty. Amid lavish furnishings, a feast of authentic Cantonese culinary masterpieces awaits.

### THE HOME OF AFTERNOON TEA

A bright and elegant space, Palm Court is styled after its namesake at The Langham, London — where British Afternoon Tea was first served in 1865.

The restaurant pays tribute to its heritage with a special menu crafted by our culinary experts.

### ICONIC ART COLLECTION

The Langham, Hong Kong features a stunning art collection for art lovers to explore.

With artworks created by some of the most renowned Chinese artists, this impressive assembly of artistry is thoughtfully integrated into the hotel's public areas, from the lobby to the restaurants and bar. Showcasing a wide range of media and styles, the collection allows guests to discover and appreciate the creative talents on display.
