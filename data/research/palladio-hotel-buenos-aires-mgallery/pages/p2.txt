# Our rooms

The hotel boasts 113 spacious and elegant rooms with modern decoration and luxury materials. All rooms stand out for its superb luminosity and large double glazed windows, with private balcony and incredible view of the city. The luxurious bathrooms covered in marble have double sink and separate shower. All rooms feature complimentary WIFI, minibar, tea & expresso machine, In-room safe suitable for a laptop, desk and 24-hour room service.
