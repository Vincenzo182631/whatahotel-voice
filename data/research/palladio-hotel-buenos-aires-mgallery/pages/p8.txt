Palladio Hotel
A boutique hotel with personality in Recoleta, The Palladio's distinct personality draws all types of travelers.

Why book?
Palladio is a boutique hotel with personality in Recoleta, striking the balance between intimate service and sophistication, and drawing in travelers who want character without sacrificing reliability.
Set the scene
Just steps from Avenida Santa Fe's shopping and cafés, but on a quiet Recoleta side street that feels residential, Palladio Hotel has an excellent setup. The property attracts a mix of design-conscious couples, repeat visitors to Buenos Aires who've graduated from the big-box hotels, and families who appreciate the connecting rooms and rooftop pool. The scale feels personal: This isn't a lobby where you'll get lost in a sea of tour groups.
The backstory
Part of Accor's MGallery Collection, the brand is known for boutique hotels with individual character rather than cookie-cutter design. The Palladio leans into Buenos Aires's European heritage while maintaining the service standards you'd expect from the group, but with a boutique hotel vibe. Before it was the Palladio, the property was a private palace, and later the birthplace of local politician Nicolás Rodríguez Peña. It was transformed into a hotel nearly a century after it's origin by a French architect.
The rooms
The 113 rooms channel refined Buenos Aires style without going overboard. Expect quality linens, thoughtful lighting, and a color palette that skews warm and inviting. Large windows provide excellent natural light, and modern design combines with luxury materials to create a space you’ll want to return to at the end of the day. Bathrooms are well-appointed with separate fixtures where space allows. The hotel offers 20 connecting rooms, which makes it genuinely family-friendly.
Food and drink
Negresco Bistró serves Mediterranean cuisine with a seasonal menu and a great list of local Argentine wines in a modern, elegant room that handles breakfast, lunch, and dinner with polish. The attached bar draws neighborhood locals as well as hotel guests, which is always a sign that the hotel is doing something right.
The neighborhood/area
The property is in Recoleta, but closer to the hustle of Avenida Santa Fe than any quieter residential pockets. You're a two-minute walk from the Callao metro station, making the rest of the city easily accessible. Recoleta Cemetery is nearby, as are museums, bookshops, and the Sunday flea market. The location balances convenience with neighborhood charm. You can grab morning coffee at a corner café and be at the Obelisco in 20 minutes.
The service
Staff are genuinely helpful in all aspects. The concierge arranges private cars from the airports (Ezeiza is around 45 minutes away, Jorge Newbery is 25 minutes), secures reservations, and offers recommendations that feel personal.
For families
With 20 connecting rooms and a rooftop pool, this property feels family-friendly without sacrificing its overall elegance.
Accessibility
There are elevators to all floors and accessible room options available.
Eco effort
Beyond the expected nods to conservation—dual-flush systems, LED lighting, motion sensors—the Palladio surprises with thoughtful touches: a solar-paneled rooftop terrace, a vertical garden blooming with native species at the building's rear, and a quiet practice of donating retired linens to local foundations.
Anything left to mention?
The heated outdoor rooftop pool is a real asset; it’s small but well-maintained, with enough space to actually swim laps while overlooking the views across the neighborhood. There's also a spa for facials and treatments.
