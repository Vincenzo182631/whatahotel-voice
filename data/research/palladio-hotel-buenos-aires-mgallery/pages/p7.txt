# M Moments

## Exclusive treasures to collect

MGallery believes in creating memorable and meaningful experiences – fleeting moments that will last forever.

Live your exceptional M Moment in our 120 boutique hotels around the world, each a gallery of art, beauty, and unexpected stories.

MGallery: Where the Unexpected Awaits

**What is a M Moment?**

M Moments are unique experiences, inspired and radiated by each destination, conveying the spirit and essence of every place. Live and collect yours.

**How to book a M Moment?**

**Online booking**: simply add your M Moment as an additional experience after the selection of your room during your online booking.

**By phone**: you can also call the hotel for any information and book directly via phone if you want to enjoy this M Moment apart from your stay. ​
