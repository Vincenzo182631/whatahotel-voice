Fairmont Fujairah Beach Resort
One of the best beachfront options in Fujairah, attracting European tourists and Gulf residents on quick getaways
Chevron
Chevron
Photos
Save Story

Reviewed by
Laura Ratliff
How did it strike you on arrival?
This new hotel set along the Gulf of Oman looks like something you'd see in the Caribbean—a dusky-pink tower, a sparkling tiled pool, and of course, water views—and that's not a bad thing.
Nice. What’s the crowd like?
The hotel attracts a fair number of European guests on holiday, as well as travelers from the surrounding Gulf states.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
One-bedroom suites have great views of the sea and adjacent mountains, plus two balconies. The decor is modern, and mostly neutral-hued, with a few subtle Arabic influences—a patterned rug here, a throw pillow there.
We’re craving some deep, restorative sleep. They got us?
Beds are soft and fluffy and dressed in neat white duvets.
Please tell us the bathroom won’t let us down.
Bathtubs in the suite are deep, and the excellent design means you can soak and look out on the view.
Maybe the most important topic of all: Wi-Fi. What’s the word?
Wi-Fi is complimentary, but speed upgrades are available.
Anything stand out about other services and features? Whether it’s childcare, gyms, spas, even parking—whatever stuck with you.
The elaborately tiled pool has beautiful sunset views across the sea. Different restaurant choices include all-day dining, a tea lounge, a seafood restaurant, and a European-style bistro.
What was most memorable—or heartbreaking—about your stay?
The area surrounding the hotel is new, so there's not much to do in the immediate neighborhood, but the on-site amenities soften the blow.
Bottom line: worth it, and why?
One of the best beachfront options in Fujairah.
