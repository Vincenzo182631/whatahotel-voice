Step inside Fairmont Fujairah Beach Resort to be surrounded by a sophisticated design theme in which modern bohemian elegance meets arabesque-infused art deco. As Fujairah’s only art-inspired hotel, our luxury resort comprises 181 exceptional guest rooms and suites, all with serene views of the marina or sweeping across the Gulf of Oman.

Experience the epitome of modern luxury at Fairmont Fujairah Beach Resort, an idyllic 5-star hotel in the United Arab Emirates. ## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Fujairah Beach Resort located?

Our 5-star beach resort, Fairmont Fujairah is located on Mina Al Fajer, in Suwayfah, Al Fqait.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Fujairah Beach Resort?

Check in is at 3:00PM and check-out is at 12:00PM.

Does Fairmont Fujairah Beach Resort have a spa?

Yes, our beach resort offers a luxury spa for unwinding moments and relaxing rejuvenation during your stay at our hotel.

Does Fairmont Fujairah Beach Resort have a swimming pool?

Yes, our luxury resort has two spacious swimming pool, perfect for both morning workouts and lazy pool afternoons.

Does Fairmont Fujairah Beach Resort have a gym/fitness center?

Yes, there is a state-of-the-art gym with modern fitness facilities and gym equipment for a successful workout session.
