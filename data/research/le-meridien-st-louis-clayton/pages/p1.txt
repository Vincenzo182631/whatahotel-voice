### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton?

The check-in time at Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 80.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton?

On-site parking is available at Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton. Self parking is available at Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton, with rates of $35.00 per day, and $245.00 per week. Valet parking is available for $45.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton?

The closest airport near Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton is St. Louis Lambert International Airport (STL). STL is located approximately 9.6 Miles from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
