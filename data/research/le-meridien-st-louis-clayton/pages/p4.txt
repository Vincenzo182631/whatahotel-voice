### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bikes, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton have a pool on-site?

Yes, Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton?

Anheuser-Busch Brewery, Lumiere Casino, Central West End, Missouri Botanical Garden, Grant's Farm and more are nearby Le Méridien St. Louis Clayton.
