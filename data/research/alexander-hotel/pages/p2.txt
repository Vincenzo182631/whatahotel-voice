Alexander Hotel
A 26-suite hideaway that feels like it’s floating above Bosque de Chapultepec park, located inside one of Mexico City's most iconic skyscrapers.

Best for:
Groups, business executives, design-minded visitors who want skyline views
Location:
Lomas Virreyes, Mexico City
Can’t-miss feature:
The Alexandar Bar, with the only dedicated caviar program of its kind in the city
In a nutshell
: Set inside one of the city’s most iconic skyscrapers, Torre Virreyes, this 26-suite property feels like it’s floating above Bosque de Chapultepec park, complete with a Petrossian caviar bar, a Leading Hotels pedigree, and one of the best views in Mexico City. It’s unapologetically luxurious and international in sensibility, offering a private, design-forward base for the seasoned traveler.
Michaela Trimble
Michaela Trimble
Rooms
The Alexander Hotel has 26 accommodations, ranging from entry-level rooms to the expansive Alexander Suite. All have king beds and floor-to-ceiling windows that open either toward the skyline or the green expanse of Chapultepec. The interiors feature blue calcite marble in the bathrooms, dark ebony wood and leather accents in the main area, and in-room amenities including Bang & Olufsen speakers and Byredo toiletries. One quirk worth knowing: The bathrooms are glass-walled, but offer a heavy curtain for privacy if you want it. Book a corner suite for 180-degree panoramas, and request a Chapultepec-facing room for incredible park views.
Michaela Trimble
Food and drink
The Alexandar Bar is one of the hotel’s stand-out features. It’s helmed by chef Gabriela Ruiz (whose other restaurant, Carmela y Sal, is Michelin-recognized), and you can expect Petrossian Ossetra Imperial and Beluga caviar, niçoise salads, house-smoked salmon, truffled tagliolini, and a strong cocktail program led by mixologist Mica Rosseau. Guests don’t even need to leave the Torre Virreyes building to dine at a few other top restaurants in the area, including the aforementioned Carmela y Sal, which has one of the city’s best tasting menus.
Amenities and experiences
The wellness program features a Technogym-equipped fitness floor, personal trainers and a personal jogging coach on call, yoga on request, and a small but thoughtful spa. There’s also a lifestyle concierge to help book all sorts of experiences or answer any questions, from arranging a tour of Frida Kahlo's Casa Azul to coordinating a private driver to Teotihuacán. E-bikes are available for borrowing, and VIP travelers can access the helipad on the roof of Torre Virreyes.
What to do nearby
Lomas de Virreyes is a financial district and residential pocket of Mexico City, and isn’t necessarily a walking neighborhood. It’s mostly surrounded by embassies and law firms, not cafés or galleries. But it does offer guests access to the Chapultepec park directly across the street, which I enjoy walking in. It’s the largest urban park in Latin America and is home to the Museo Nacional de Antropología, Museo Tamayo, and Chapultepec Castle. Beyond this, the neighborhood of Polanco is a five-minute drive away, home to top restaurants like Pujol and Quintonil.
The backstory
Alexander opened in 2023 as the first hotel from Namron Hospitality Group, occupying several floors of Torre Virreyes, one of the final works of Teodoro González de León, the late Mexican modernist whose projects, like the Auditorio Nacional, shaped late-20th-century Mexico City (both literally and figuratively). The hotel itself was designed by Daniel Álvarez Fernández of Grupo Arquitectura, with interiors by Lorena Cherem and Linda Masri of Ambia Studio.
Insider tip
Order the Alexander Martini at the Alexander Bar, a gin-infused cocktail made with sugar kelp, dry vermouth, and olive juice, served with a Kumamoto oyster topped with Baeri caviar. It was a favorite of mine.
Final considerations:
Family-friendly:
Technically yes, but the aesthetic skews strongly toward adults
Accessibility:
All suites and common areas are wheelchair-accessible via glass elevators
Wellness:
Full gym, personal trainers, yoga, and a spa
Loyalty group/hotel brand:
The Leading Hotels of the World; operated by Namron Hospitality Group
