### Alexander Suite

#### A 180° Perspective.

A 180° Perspective. This 80.42 m² sanctuary reveals a stunning 180° view of Chapultepec Park through expansive windows. It features spacious living and dining areas, a king-size bed, and a luxurious bathroom with a deep soaking tub and rain shower.

### Ambassador Suite

#### Your Sophisticated Welcome.

Spanning 117.38 m², this suite features an open-plan aesthetic with distinct living and dining areas. Rest in the king-size bed, fitted with the softest Egyptian cotton. Indulge in the lavish bathroom, complete with a soaking tub and rain shower, all enhanced by privileged views of the city.

### Grand Suite

#### A Perspective Without Equal.

The Grand Suite, with a spacious area of 57.30 m², offers a magnificent view of Chapultepec Park through its floor-to-ceiling windows, a unique Alexander attribute unrivaled in Mexico. An exquisite King-size bed, a comfortable living area, a dining room, and a bathroom fit for royalty complete the impeccable experience in this suite.

### City Suite

#### An Unrivaled Perspective.

Spanning 55.20 m², this corner sanctuary reveals magnificent city views through its soaring windows. It features distinct living and dining areas, an elegant steel bar, a sumptuous king-size bed, and a dedicated luxury bathroom.

### Park Suite

#### Unmatched view.

Spanning 48.07 m², this corner sanctuary reveals magnificent park views through its soaring windows. It features distinct living and dining areas, an elegant steel bar, a sumptuous king-size bed, and a dedicated luxury bathroom.
