Nearby Shopping

## Shoppers Paradise

Ideally located for discerning shoppers, The Westin Edina Galleria offers unparalleled access to premier retail destinations. Directly connected to the upscale Galleria, guests can indulge in an exquisite selection of designer boutiques, luxury brands, and curated specialty shops. Just across the street, Southdale Center presents a dynamic mix of fashion, technology, and lifestyle stores, while the charming 50th & France district invites visitors to explore an array of stylish boutiques, artisan retailers, and inviting cafés. From high-end fashion to the latest innovations, an exceptional shopping experience awaits just moments from your stay.

## Stable House

Stable House is an intimate cocktail house inspired by the great sporting lodges of New England and the English countryside. Rich wood, worn leather, antique brass, and crystal decanters create a timeless setting where exceptional whiskey, classic cocktails, and meaningful conversation take center stage.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Edina Galleria have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Edina Galleria offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Yes, The Westin Edina Galleria has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Indoor Heated Pool (Indoor)Whirlpool
