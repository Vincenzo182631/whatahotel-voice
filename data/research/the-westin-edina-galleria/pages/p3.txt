Specials Available at Prelude

### Monday \| Cellar Unlocked 3:00 PM-11:00 PM

Begin the week with a rare pour. Each Monday, Prelude unveils an exclusive Wine of the Week, a reserve selection typically offered by the bottle, now available by the glass for a limited time. Enjoy 25% off all bottled wines as you explore something exceptional.

### Tuesday \| The Martini Hour

A timeless ritual, reimagined. From 7–9 PM, our signature Martini Cart arrives in the foyer beside The Study for an intimate tableside experience. Expect classic, expertly crafted martinis, elevated garnishes, and chef-curated stuffed olives designed to complement each pour.

### Wednesday \| Sommelier’s Table

Join our Sommelier for a midweek moment of discovery from 4-6 pm every Wednesday. Settle into Prelude for complimentary tastings of 2–3 featured wines, thoughtfully selected and available by the glass throughout the evening. A guided exploration for both the curious and the seasoned palate.

### Thursday \| Champagne Social 3:00 PM-11:00 PM

A weekly toast to the weekend ahead. Settle in at Prelude for special 4 oz pours of Taittinger Prelude, offered for $18 every Thursday evening.

### Thursday, Friday & Saturday \| Live Music

Meet us at Prelude Bar for crafted cocktails and bites featuring live music by local artists from 7 pm – 11 pm.

### Sunday \| Rosé All Day

End the week on a lighter note with Rosé All Day, featuring 25% off rosé by the glass and bottle. A leisurely ritual best paired with good company and an unhurried afternoon. 3:00 PM-11:00 PM.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What restaurants does The Westin Edina Galleria have on property?

The on-property restaurants at The Westin Edina Galleria are:

McCormick & Schmick's Seafood & SteaksIn-Room Dining

Does The Westin Edina Galleria offer room service?

Yes, room service is available at The Westin Edina Galleria.

What cuisine is offered at The Westin Edina Galleria?

The cuisines offered at The Westin Edina Galleria are:

American at In-Room DiningSeafood at McCormick & Schmick's Seafood & Steaks

Does The Westin Edina Galleria offer breakfast?

Yes, breakfast is available onsite at McCormick & Schmick's Seafood & Steaks for a fee. Breakfast is also available through in-room dining for a fee.
