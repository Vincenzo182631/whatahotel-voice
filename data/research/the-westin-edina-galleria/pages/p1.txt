### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Edina Galleria?

The check-in time at The Westin Edina Galleria is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Edina Galleria allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Edina Galleria. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $25.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Edina Galleria?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Edina Galleria. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near The Westin Edina Galleria. The daily valet fee is $30.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Edina Galleria have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Edina Galleria has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Edina Galleria booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Edina Galleria?

The closest airport near The Westin Edina Galleria is Minneapolis-St Paul International Airport (MSP). MSP is located approximately 9.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Edina Galleria have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Edina Galleria does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does The Westin Edina Galleria have a Executive Club lounge?

Yes, The Westin Edina Galleria has Executive Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Executive Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
