## A place to rediscover balance

A sanctuary between the city, sand, and sea, Six Senses The Palm, Dubai is where the pace softens and space opens up in ways you don’t expect. Wellness comes naturally, and a way of living flows with you, in harmony with the beauty of the Arabian Gulf. Here, escape feels effortless, balance comes into view, and a sense of community takes shape through moments of genuine connection. In a city that moves quickly, this is where you return to yourself.

Sustainability is embedded in daily operations rather than presented as a promise. Thoughtful design, efficient use of energy and water, waste reduction practices, and hands-on learning at Earth Lab invite guests to take part in meaningful actions that support Dubai’s wider sustainability goals.

## Accommodation

The resort’s 61 suites reflect the colors and textures of the Gulf, with soft tones, natural materials, and generous terraces that frame the water or skyline. Thoughtful comforts, sensory amenities and intuitive service create a space where guests can slow down and settle in with ease.

- View all accommodation

## Six Senses Residences The Palm, Dubai

Six Senses The Palm, Dubai offers 172 private residences designed for long-term well-being and contemporary living. Generous layouts and curated amenities create homes that feel connected to the sea and the city. Owners enjoy all resort facilities, dedicated services, exclusive spaces, and a lifestyle shaped by ease and community.

- Read more

## Destination

Set on Palm Jumeirah’s quieter West Crescent, Six Senses The Palm, Dubai is a beachfront resort offering open Arabian Gulf views and a calm sense of retreat, all while staying close to Dubai’s cultural districts, dining scene, and coastal experiences.
