### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Perry Lane Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Savannah have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Perry Lane Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Savannah offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Two Peloton Bikes, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Perry Lane Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Savannah have a pool on-site?

Yes, Perry Lane Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Savannah has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Perry Lane Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Savannah?

Old Fort Jackson, Juliette Gordon Low Birthplace, Jepson Center for the Arts, Savannah National Wildlife Refuge, Bonaventure Cemetery and more are nearby Perry Lane Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Savannah.
