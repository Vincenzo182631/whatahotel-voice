# Olive Harvest

### Discover Tuscany’s liquid gold

Share

- Share in WhatsApp
- Share in WeChat

As autumn unfolds across the Chianti hills, our estate celebrates the olive harvest — one of Tuscany’s oldest and most cherished traditions. This October, join us for an immersive journey through our olive groves to discover the craftsmanship behind exceptional extra virgin olive oil, take part in the harvest and savour the seasonal flavours of the Tuscan countryside.
