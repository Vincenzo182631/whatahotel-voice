# DINING AT COMO CASTELLO DEL NERO

There are two dining venues at COMO Castello Del Nero: Michelin-starred La Torre, and the 'al fresco' Pavilion, as well as a bar, La Taverna. Each dining venue uses carefully sourced produce from around the Tuscan region to produce nourishing and flavoursome dishes full of the spirit of place.

### Breakfast in Tuscany

Mornings at COMO Castello Del Nero are a feast for the senses. Sit down for ‘al fresco’ dining at Pavilion, and take in the panoramic view of the hills of Tuscany. Enjoy the flavours from a spread of viennoiserie, local delicacies, and COMO Shambhala dishes with an Italian twist, there’s plenty to choose from for your first meal of the day.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1How many restaurants and bars are there at COMO Castello Del Nero?

The estate features two distinctive dining venues — La Torre, the resort’s Michelin-starred restaurant, and Pavilion, an al fresco space overlooking the Tuscan hills — as well as an elegant bar. From refined fine dining to casual countryside cuisine, guests can savour authentic Italian flavours paired with panoramic views of Chianti’s vineyards.

#### 2What type of cuisine is served in the hotel restaurants?

Dining at COMO Castello Del Nero celebrates contemporary Italian and Tuscan cuisine, crafted from locally sourced, seasonal ingredients drawn from nearby farms and the estate’s own gardens. Each dish reflects the culinary heritage of the Chianti region, reinterpreted with modern refinement.

#### 3Are vegetarian and vegan options available?

Yes. All restaurants feature creative vegetarian and vegan menus, showcasing fresh regional produce and COMO’s signature commitment to well-being through cuisine.

#### 4Does COMO Castello Del Nero propose healthy food options?

Absolutely. Guests can enjoy COMO Shambhala Cuisine, a wellness-focused menu of nutrient-rich, organic, and balanced dishes designed to nourish both body and mind — an integral part of COMO’s holistic philosophy.

#### 5Does the hotel have a Michelin-starred restaurant?

Yes. COMO Castello Del Nero is home to La Torre, a Michelin-starred restaurant in Tuscany where Chef Giovanni Luca Di Pirro reimagines Tuscan gastronomy with contemporary finesse. Set within a 12th-century castle overlooking the Chianti hills, La Torre offers one of Tuscany’s most refined fine-dining experiences, celebrating local ingredients and seasonal creativity.

#### 6Can private dining be arranged in outdoor settings?

Yes. Guests can enjoy exclusive private dining experiences in the castle’s gardens, vineyards, or historic courtyards — each offering a romantic Tuscan ambiance and unforgettable sense of place.

#### 7Are wine tastings and pairings available on the estate?

Yes. COMO Castello Del Nero offers private wine tastings and sommelier-led pairing experiences, featuring the finest Chianti Classico and Super Tuscan wines. These curated sessions celebrate the region’s renowned viticultural heritage within the setting of a 12th-century estate.
