### About COMO Shambhala

Meaning 'peace' in Sanskrit, Shambhala alludes to the pursuit of balance. It defines the essence of the COMO Shambhala approach to holistic wellness, combining modern science with ancient practices to align mind, body and spirit.

Our wellness centre at COMO Castello del Nero was one of our larger retreats, offering a wide range of treatments, experiences, products and activities.

Complementing these services is our signature COMO Shambhala Kitchen, featuring healthful nutrition-optimised dishes using pure ingredients in nourishing combinations.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Does COMO Castello Del Nero have a spa?

Yes. COMO Castello Del Nero is home to a COMO Shambhala Retreat, an award-winning wellness sanctuary combining holistic therapies, natural products, and expert practitioners. Set within the castle’s historic estate, the spa offers a serene escape for deep relaxation and rejuvenation.

#### 2What wellness and spa experiences are available at COMO Castello Del Nero?

COMO Castello Del Nero is home to COMO Shambhala Retreat, a sanctuary for wellness and rejuvenation in Tuscany. Guests can enjoy signature massages, aromatherapy treatments, and thermal suites, or participate in yoga and meditation classes overlooking the countryside. The retreat’s philosophy combines Asian-inspired holistic therapies with the calm and rhythm of Tuscan life, creating a complete wellness journey for mind and body.

#### 3Are yoga and pilates sessions offered at the hotel?

Yes. Guests can take part in daily yoga and Pilates classes, held in peaceful indoor studios or outdoor spaces with panoramic Tuscan views. Private lessons are also available for tailored guidance and personal wellness goals.

#### 4Is there a fitness center in the hotel?

Yes. COMO Castello Del Nero features a state-of-the-art fitness center equipped with the latest Technogym equipment, complemented by personal training sessions and guided wellness programs designed around each guest’s needs.

#### 5What wellness facilities are available at COMO Castello Del Nero?

The COMO Shambhala Retreat includes a thermal suite with sauna, bio sauna, ice fountain, and emotional showers, as well as a heated pool with panoramic views. Guests can also unwind in vitality pools, relaxation lounges, and outdoor meditation spaces surrounded by olive groves.

#### 6Is COMO Castello Del Nero a good choice for a wellness-focused holiday in Chianti, Tuscany?

Absolutely. Recognised as one of the finest wellness retreats in Tuscany, COMO Castello Del Nero blends modern health expertise with timeless Italian tranquillity. It is the ideal destination for those seeking holistic rejuvenation amid the vineyards and hills of Chianti.
