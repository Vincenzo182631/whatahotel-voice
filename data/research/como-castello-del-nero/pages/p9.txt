**Green Initiatives at COMO Castello Del Nero**

### Celebrating Sustainability

At COMO Castello Del Nero, we blend luxury with a deep commitment to sustainability. From eco-friendly arrivals and energy-conscious guest rooms to locally sourced cuisine and community empowerment, every detail reflects our dedication to preserving the natural beauty of Tuscany, while supporting the people and traditions that make it special.

### GETTING HERE

COMO Castello Del Nero is a 30-minute drive from Aeroporto di Firenze-Peretola, Florence’s international airport. Pisa’s Galileo Galilei International Airport is approximately 75 minutes away. There is also a helipad at coordinates N 43,550347 - E 11,199162. We can arrange private transfers. For further information, please contact castellodelnero@comohotels.com.

BY CAR

Please email castellodelnero@comohotels.com for the clearest directions from the city you will be driving from.

BY TRAIN

Trains from Rome Termini to Florence’s Santa Maria Novella station take approximately 1.5 hours. Car transfers to COMO Castello Del Nero will take around 30 minutes from there.

### SEASONALITY IN TUSCANY

Temperatures range from a minimum of 3°C in December and January to slightly above 30°C in July and August, the warmest summer months.

## THE COMO EYE FOR DESIGN

Our 12th-century castle has modern interiors by Milan-based designer, Paola Navone in calming cool tones, which highlight carefully preserved Renaissance frescoes. Whitewashed walls and terracotta floors, high ceilings, soft furnishings and sunlight spilling through windows make every room an elegant, pared-back haven.
