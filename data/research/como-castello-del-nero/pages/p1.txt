# COMO Castello Del Nero

Home/Italy/COMO Castello Del Nero

Our Italian hotel is unique in Tuscany: a twelfth-century castle with Renaissance frescoes and contemporary interiors by Milanese designer Paola Navone. Set within a 740-acre historic estate in the heart of Chianti, COMO Castello Del Nero is joined by Podere San Filippo, our collection of elegant self-catering apartments, and Villa San Luigi, an exclusive five-bedroom villa, offering three distinct ways to experience the Tuscany.

Whether staying in the castle, the apartments or the villa, guests can immerse themselves in Tuscany's rich culinary heritage, restorative wellness experiences and timeless landscapes, all within easy reach of Florence.

COMO Castello Del Nero has been recognised with a One MICHELIN Key in both the 2024 and 2025 MICHELIN Guide Italy Key Hotel selection.

**Location**

### ABOVE TUSCANY'S ROLLING HILLS

COMO Castello Del Nero is situated in the world-famous Chianti wine region. By car, the resort is 30 minutes from Florence's airport, 75 minutes from Pisa's airport, and three hours from Rome's airport.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Where is COMO Castello Del Nero located in Tuscany?

COMO Castello Del Nero is set in the heart of Tuscany’s Chianti wine region, surrounded by vineyards, olive groves, and gently rolling hills. This historic 12th-century estate lies perfectly between Florence and Siena, offering an idyllic base to discover Tuscany’s art, history, and gastronomy — all while enjoying the tranquillity and refined luxury of the Tuscan countryside.

#### 2Is COMO Castello Del Nero close to Florence and Siena?

Yes. COMO Castello Del Nero enjoys an exceptional location in the heart of Tuscany, just 30 minutes from Florence and 40 minutes from Siena by car. Its setting allows guests to explore both cities with ease — whether for art-filled day trips, Chianti wine tastings, or immersive cultural excursions — all while returning to the privacy and luxury of a 12th-century estate surrounded by rolling vineyards and olive groves.

#### 3What makes COMO Castello Del Nero a romantic luxury retreat in Tuscany?

COMO Castello Del Nero is a romantic castle retreat in the heart of Tuscany, surrounded by rolling vineyards and olive groves. Couples can stay in heritage rooms and suites with frescoed ceilings, unwind with wellness rituals at COMO Shambhala Retreat, and dine at La Torre, the resort’s Michelin-starred restaurant. With panoramic views of the Chianti hills, it offers a timeless blend of Tuscan charm, culinary excellence, and contemporary elegance.

#### 4What wellness and spa experiences are available at COMO Castello Del Nero?

COMO Castello Del Nero is home to COMO Shambhala Retreat, a sanctuary for wellness and rejuvenation in Tuscany. Guests can enjoy signature massages, aromatherapy treatments, and thermal suites, or participate in yoga and meditation classes overlooking the countryside. The retreat’s philosophy combines Asian-inspired holistic therapies with the calm and rhythm of Tuscan life, creating a complete wellness journey for mind and body.

#### 5Is COMO Castello Del Nero suitable for families

COMO Castello Del Nero is a family-friendly castle hotel in the Chianti wine region of Tuscany, Italy. The hotel offers interconnecting suites, one- to three-bedroom apartments at Podere San Filippo, and the five-bedroom Villa San Luigi, making it ideal for multi-generational family vacations in Tuscany.

Families can enjoy child-friendly amenities and outdoor experiences in Tuscany, including countryside picnics, truffle-hunting adventures, and cycling through the Chianti hills. The resort also offers seasonal family packages that combine wellness, gourmet dining, and outdoor exploration, creating a memorable family holiday in Tuscany for both parents and children.

#### 6Is COMO Castello Del Nero committed to sustainability and eco-friendly practices?

Yes. COMO Castello Del Nero is dedicated to sustainable luxury in Tuscany, with a strong focus on farm-to-table practices across the estate. The resort produces its own olive oil, wine, and flour for internal use, and maintains a chef’s garden where seasonal vegetables are grown on site. Energy-efficient systems, eco-friendly amenities, and responsible sourcing are integrated throughout the property. By supporting organic farming, local producers, and waste-reduction initiatives, COMO Castello Del Nero reflects COMO’s commitment to preserving Tuscany’s natural beauty, agricultural traditions, and cultural heritage.

#### 7Does COMO Castello Del Nero offer vineyard tours and wine-tasting experiences in Tuscany?

Yes. COMO Castello Del Nero is an authentic Tuscan castle estate set in the heart of the Chianti wine region. The estate produces its own wines, with private vineyards and an on-site cellar, where guests can enjoy curated Brunello di Montalcino, Chianti, and Super Tuscan wine tastings. Guests may also arrange guided vineyard experiences, exploring the craftsmanship behind Tuscan winemaking. Back at the resort, Pavilion and La Torre offer refined cuisine thoughtfully paired with exceptional vintages, completing a sophisticated food-and-wine journey through Tuscany.
