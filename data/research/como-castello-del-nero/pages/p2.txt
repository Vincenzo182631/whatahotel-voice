# Accommodation

COMO Castello Del Nero features 50 elegant rooms and suites, where pared-back, cool-toned interiors by Paola Navone complement Renaissance frescoes, vaulted ceilings and traditional terracotta floors. The result is a refined blend of contemporary design and timeless Tuscan heritage.

The 740-acre estate also encompasses two distinctive accommodation options: Villa San Luigi, an exclusive five-bedroom private villa with its own pool, sweeping countryside views and bespoke private experiences tailored to each stay; and Podere San Filippo, a beautifully restored farmhouse offering 12 contemporary self-catering apartments for guests seeking greater independence, with the option to enjoy COMO Castello Del Nero's restaurants, wellness experiences and estate activities at an additional cost.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1What types of rooms and suites are available at COMO Castello Del Nero?

COMO Castello Del Nero offers a range of elegant rooms and suites housed within a restored 12th-century castle. Each accommodation blends historic Tuscan architecture with contemporary design by Paola Navone, featuring frescoed ceilings, terracotta floors, and sweeping countryside views. Guests can choose from intimate Heritage Rooms to expansive COMO Suites with panoramic vistas over vineyards and olive groves.

#### 2Are there rooms suitable for large families or groups?

Yes. COMO Castello Del Nero offers spacious interconnecting suites, multi-bedroom apartments at Podere San Filippo, and the private Villa San Luigi, making it ideal for families or groups seeking luxury stays in Tuscany. These accommodations provide generous living spaces, enhanced privacy, and full access to the resort’s amenities, creating a refined yet family-friendly retreat near Florence and Siena.

#### 3Are the rooms equipped with modern amenities such as air conditioning and Wi-Fi?

Yes. All rooms and suites are equipped with air conditioning, high-speed Wi-Fi, smart TVs, minibars, and luxurious marble bathrooms featuring COMO Shambhala amenities — offering modern comfort with timeless Tuscan charm.

#### 4Can extra bed or baby cot be provided for children?

Yes. The resort provides extra beds and baby cots upon request, making COMO Castello Del Nero a welcoming choice for families traveling with young children.
