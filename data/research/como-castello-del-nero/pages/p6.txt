# Tuscan Countryside Hot Air Ballooning

Share

- Share in WeChat

Experience the magic of Tuscany from a breathtaking perspective with a hot air ballooning adventure. Float serenely above the picturesque countryside, capturing spectacular views of the rolling hills and vineyards below.

Ground yourself after with a delightful breakfast paired with sparkling wine for the perfect blend of adventure and luxury.

Book Now mailto:castellodelnero@comohotels.com

### What's included

Hot air balloons lift off early in a field only a five-minute drive away from our estate, where the team from Idea Balloon will prepare your balloon and take you on a one-hour flight with aerial views of the rolling greenery and COMO Castello Del Nero itself. After flying, enjoy a locally sourced breakfast accompanied by spumante (sparkling wine).

**Duration:** Three and a half hours

**Prices:**

- €380 per adult
- €340 per child (age seven to 14 years old, minimum height of 1.35m)
- €2,100 for a private group of two to four adults
- €2,530 for a private group of five to six adults

This experience is charged during checkout. For more information, please contact our Guest Services Team.

### Terms and conditions

- Reservations are required at least two weeks in advance.
- Cancellations made within one week will incur a 25 per cent cancellation fee. For cancellations made within 24 hours prior to experience, the full amount will be charged.
- Experience may be cancelled or postponed depending on weather conditions.
