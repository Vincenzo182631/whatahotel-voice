# See The World In A New Light

Celebrate Christmas and New Year in the heart of Tuscany at COMO Castello Del Nero. From ice skating and artisanal family activities to fireside aperitivi, Tuscan dining and restorative wellness, experience the festive season within our 12th-century estate.

### CHRISTMAS AND NEW YEAR AT CASTELLO

Spend Christmas and New Year among Chianti’s rolling hills, with ice skating, family activities, Tuscan dining, fireside aperitivi and restorative wellness across the estate. Explore the full festive programme and shape each celebration around your stay.

### Terms and Conditions

• Additional charges apply depending on the dining experience selected

• Rates include government tax

### A Tuscan Christmas Eve

Gather for a Christmas Eve aperitivo at La Taverna, choosing from a curated selection of festive drinks and cocktails from the bar menu, before dinner at your restaurant of choice.

_Drinks and dinner are charged according to the respective menus. Restaurant reservations are recommended_

**December 25th 2026**

### Christmas Day At The Castello

Begin Christmas Day with a leisurely festive breakfast, followed by a Christmas aperitivo from 12.30pm and a celebratory lunch at La Torre from 1.00pm. As the afternoon unfolds, gather beside the fireplace for traditional board games and hot chocolate, embracing the warmth and joy of Christmas at the Castello.

EUR 200 per adult \| EUR 75 per child

Optional wine pairing: EUR 90 per person

**January 1st 2027**

### New Year's Day

Welcome 2027 with a leisurely New Year’s Day brunch, served until 12.30pm for resident guests, followed by a relaxed afternoon of seasonal traditions.

Gather around the fire at La Taverna for complimentary roasted chestnuts and marshmallows from 3.00pm, before raising a glass to the year ahead with a mixology experience at 5.30pm.

Mixology Experience: EUR 100 per person

**December 31st 2026, from 7.00pm**

### Countdown Party

Bid farewell to 2026 with an elegant evening of celebration at the Castello. Begin with a Champagne reception from 7.00pm, followed by a New Year's Eve Gala Dinner at La Torre at 8.00pm. As midnight approaches, continue the festivities with DJ entertainment at Sala delle Volte, before coming together for the countdown and a celebratory midnight toast.

## FESTIVE HIGHLIGHTS

Embrace the festive season throughout December with activities for all ages, from ice skating and board games to a festive photo booth. On selected days, younger guests can follow a Christmas treasure hunt around the Castello, while fireside aperitivi offer a relaxed moment to gather.

**December 23rd 2026, 4.00pm**

### TUSCAN OLIVE OIL AND BREAD RITUAL

Discover a cherished Tuscan tradition with a tasting of the estate's extra virgin olive oil, served with Tuscan bread in the welcoming atmosphere of La Taverna.

**December 27th to 30th 2026, 11.00am**

### Traditional Truffle Hunting

Embark on an exquisite truffle hunting experience this festive season amid the rolling hills of our Tuscan estate. Venture into the woods with an expert truffle hunter in search of these prized delicacies, before enjoying a four-course lunch highlighting the unique and luxurious flavours of truffles.

EUR 320 per adult (over 12) \| EUR 160 per child aged 6 to 11 (accompanied by an adult)

**December 28th 2026, 4.00pm**

### Tuscan Cooking Class

Celebrate the festive season with a hands-on Italian pasta-making experience. Guided by Executive Chef Giovanni Luca Di Pirro or his assistant, discover the art of making fresh pasta using traditional Italian techniques, before sitting down at Pavilion to savour the dishes you have prepared.

EUR 160 per adult (over 12) \| EUR 80 per child aged 9 to 11 (accompanied by an adult)

**December 23rd 2026, 3.00pm**
