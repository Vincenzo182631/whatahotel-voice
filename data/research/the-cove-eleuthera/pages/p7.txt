## Spa Treatments

Barefoot Bliss

Recharge circulation in the lower limbs and refine your skin with a sea salt scrub exfoliation from the knees down. Followed by a deeply relaxing lower leg massage and reflexology techniques.

60 min, $200

Family Connection Massage

Experience a delightful parent-child massage designed for kids aged 6 to 11. This heartwarming session provides a wonderful bonding opportunity, allowing families to connect and create lasting memories while enjoying the soothing benefits of massage together.

30 min, $120

Island Glow Facial

Using a combination of gentle exfoliators, serums, marine eye treatment and masks, this facial helps to transform your skin leaving it feeling purified, nourished and perfectly balanced.

60 min, $216

Island Stone Ritual

Our Hot Stone Massage can help with pain, flexibility, circulation, stress and sleep. The heat and massage work together to promote relaxation and healing.

60 min, $250 \| 90 min, $330

Men's Renewal Facial

Exclusively created for men’s specific skincare needs, this powerful treatment exfoliates the skin, allowing for a deeper cleanse.

60 min, $216

Mother Rest Massage

The specially designed botanical wellbeing ritual promotes feelings of relaxation and tranquility while hydrating your skin from head to toe. Nourishing oil is used to gently ease away stress and tension with a body, face and scalp massage. Feel cocooned, supported and restored on your journey to motherhood.

60 min, $200 \| add 30-minute rejuvenating facial, $310

Ocean Calm CBD Massage (Deep Tissue)

A powerful treatment combining firm deep tissue pressure with assisted stretching to release tension and improve flexibility. CBD oil blend — infused with turmeric, black pepper, and arnica — enhances muscle relief, reduces soreness, and promotes deep relaxation.

60 mins $250   /   90 mins $330

Ocean Dew Mineral Melt

Our Mineral Melt enhances deep sleep, is great for joint health and helps release chronic muscle tension, leaving you feeling refreshed and revitalized.

60 min, $250

Spa Manicure & Pedicure

Treatment including nail shape and cuticle care with essential oils and natural products from OPI. Hot towel and massage will leave the skin smooth and replenished. Polish of choice.

45 min - Mani \| 45 min - Pedi \| 90 min - Combo

Sun Salutation Body Wrap

Like pure sunlight, the power of pure citrus enlivens the spirit and invigorates the body. Stimulating circulation with a magnesium melt body polish, this treatment preps the skin to absorb moisture of rich shea butter. Includes a coconut kukui hair and scalp massage oil as well as transdermal magnesium gel help to reduce cramping and boost energy on your leg.

90 min, $330

Thai Renewal Massage

Using ancient traditional techniques, our therapists will stretch your body in yoga-like poses while pushing on the zen lines, similar to acupressure points, to help the body regain mobility and flexibility and help open up blockages in the energy pathways. Please wear comfortable workout attire.

60 min, $250 \| 90 min, $330

The Cove Signature Massage

A personalized massage complete head-to-toe relaxation experience incorporating scalp massage, face massage combining gentle and stimulating movements.

60 min, $250 \| 90 min, $330

Upper Body Island Unwind Massage (De-Stress)

Back and head massage that includes a soothing dry facial massage and targeted acupressure techniques using coconut kukui hair and scalp massage oil to enhance your relaxation and nourishment.

60 min, $200

Vital Sea Salt or Aromatic Coffee Scrub

Invigorating and detoxifying, this scrub incorporates local, natural ingredients and infuses coffee to activate circulation. In addition, the treatment eliminates dead skin cells, reveals brighter looking skin, reduces cellulite and imparts a youthful glow while tightening the skin.

60 min, $250

Intentional Aromatherapy

The Cove Spa Suites partners with Body Bliss, a company that believes aromatherapy’s true healing potential can be achieved only using the purest and most complete essential oils.

Before your treatment, our therapists customize each guest’s “scent journey” through Intentional Aromatherapy; they will ask you questions about your intentions, birthdate and other details to tailor a scent for your treatment.

60min $250 \|90min $330
