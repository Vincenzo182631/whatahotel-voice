# Private Dining Experiences

### Dinner on the beach

With the sand between your toes under the starlit sky, this is a beachfront dining experience unlike any other. For two people, you may enjoy a three-course dinner with drink pairings and a beach setup of candles, tiki torches and floral arrangements.

### Bonfire on the Beach

Relax in a private lounge area with a blazing bonfire while you enjoy s’mores, hot cocoa and the company of your friends and family.

### Private Chef

Enjoy the perfect dinner for two tailored to your tastes and prepared by your very own private chef.

### Private Bartender

Whether on the beach or in your luxurious accommodations, indulge in a private bartender to mix and serve up classic Caribbean cocktails.

_Prices are subject to 10% VAT and 15% service charge (18% for parties of 6 or more). For pricing and reservations, please contact our resort hosts_
