# Wild Swimming with Paul Asmuth

At The Cove Eleuthera, the ocean isn’t something you look at, it’s something you enter.

Join internationally acclaimed marathon swimmer and Olympic Coach Paul Asmuth for an exclusive open-water swimming residency available only at The Cove from **November 5-8.** Guided swims, personal coaching, ocean safety mastery and storytelling come together in an immersive program that blends adventure, wellness and raw beauty.

Due to the highly personalized nature of this experience, reservations are limited to just a handful of participants. To secure your spot, please reserve securely online using the link below or contact one of our reservations specialists by calling (866) 644-4452.

**Please Note: This year’s experience is at capacity.** If you are interested in a future open-water swimming program, please reach out directly to our Resort Hosts for more details.

Swim with a World Champion

Paul Asmuth is a seven-time World Professional Marathon Swimming Champion and former English Channel world record holder. He served as Open Water National Team Leader for USA Swimming and coached athletes at the Olympic Games in Beijing and London.

Over the course of his career, Paul has swum in waters across the globe — including Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, Egypt, England, France, Italy, México, Portugal, Singapore, Spain, and the United States — experiencing everything from tropical currents to historic channels and vast inland seas.

He is a member of both the International Swimming Hall of Fame and the International Marathon Swimming Hall of Fame, and author of _Marathon Swimming: The Sport of the Soul_.

But what makes swimming with Paul extraordinary isn’t only his accolades, but his reverence for the water. Decades in the open ocean have shaped his belief that swimming is not simply sport. It is presence. It is rhythm. It is conversation with something larger than yourself.

Wild Swimming Agenda

Our immersive Wild Swimming residency invites guests to experience Eleuthera’s crystalline coastline in a way few ever do: guided, intentional and deeply personal.

**About the Program**

- This program is designed for intermediate and advanced swimmers.
- Coaching will be personalized to the individual, from strong lap-swimmers wanting to explore open water (wild) swimming for the first time to competitive swimmers and triathletes looking to refine their times or techniques.
- Each swimmer will enjoy three hours of coaching, one hour per day. Each session will be limited to a maximum of two swimmers with similar strengths and goals.
- Guests have the option to open water swim from The Cove to the beautiful Gregory Town at a distance of just over 1 mile.
- Free-flowing conversations with the coach and fellow swimmers over the course of the three-day program are welcomed and encouraged.
- Non-swimming guests of swimmers are invited to participate in all program activities besides the one-hour daily coaching sessions.
