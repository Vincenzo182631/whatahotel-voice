Escape the frenzy this holiday season and rediscover the quiet luxury of time unfolding. **Blue Mind Season** at The Cove Eleuthera is a celebration of simplicity, serenity and the soothing rhythm of the sea.

Inspired by the calming effect of water and the art of letting go, you are invited to trade stresses of the season for ocean breezes, hectic schedules for barefoot sunsets and to-do lists for tropical tranquility.

Whether you’re sipping cocktails under swaying palms, enjoying an unhurried beachfront dinner, or finding stillness in a morning paddle, Blue Mind Season is your invitation to embrace joy without the noise — to celebrate connection, presence and peace.

No two holidays are the same. We invite you to tell us what you’d love, and we’ll make it yours — from a large festive gathering to a quiet morning ritual, every detail is yours to shape.

Let the holidays feel like a holiday again. Welcome to Blue Mind Season.

## A Reason to Gather

This Thanksgiving, The Cove brings a refreshing twist to tradition — one where gratitude flows as freely as the island breeze and every moment is designed to soothe. Join us for a sun-drenched celebration of togetherness, where the only thing you’ll need to prepare is your appetite.

Enjoy Caribbean-inspired barbecue on Thanksgiving Day — all without the stress, cleanup or chilly weather. Thanksgiving reimagined — peaceful, present, and perfectly relaxed. This is gratitude in its most relaxed form.

## Hanukkah Festivities

Whether you prefer a more intimate celebration with your travel companions or would rather join together as a community with other guests, The Cove is the perfect gathering place for your Hanukkah festivities. While in residence in our secluded sanctuary, there’s no hurry, just the quiet luxury of time unfolding.

## Caribbean Christmas

Christmas in the Bahamas has a rhythm all of its own. Here, the season arrives on ocean breezes rather than winter winds — days spent on the sand with the people who matter most, evenings that drift from golden hour into a sky full of stars. No agenda, no crowds, no noise — only the simple pleasure of being present, together, on an island that feels like it was made for moments like these. This holiday season, we invite you to trade white snow for white sand and pine trees for palm trees as you enjoy the simple delights of life in paradise.

## A New Year in Paradise

**New Year’s Eve**

Start your evening with a Caribbean-Style celebration featuring a Junkanoo Parade and Bahamian food stations. At midnight, ring in the New Year with the Annual Pineapple Drop on the pool deck.

**New Year’s Day**

We invite you to begin your New Year with intentionality, taking time to slow down, reflect and identify a resolution. In the afternoon, embrace life on island time as you immerse yourself in tropical activities in our private coves.

## Sustainable Seasonal Decor

In celebration of the festive season, our decorations reflect both the spirit of the holidays and our deep respect for the natural beauty of our island home. With a strong commitment to environmental stewardship, we craft our decor from thoughtfully gathered materials — including natural elements and ocean-washed treasures found along our nearby shores.

We consciously avoid synthetic, petroleum-based products and single-use items, recognizing their lasting impact on delicate ecosystems and our limited island resources. Instead, we embrace creativity rooted in sustainability, ensuring that our holiday celebrations remain in harmony with the environment we are privileged to call home.

## Bahamas Blue Mind Offer

Blue Mind Theory is the idea that being close to water can ease your mind into a meditative state. Marine biologist, Dr. Wallace Nichols, claims that being in or near water triggers a positive response in our brains.

**Embrace a “blue mind” this holiday season with this offer that includes:**

- Three-night stay in our new Beachfront King rooms or Junior Suites
- Picnic for two on Floating Sea Lounge
- Oceanscape Paint Set: Choosing from watercolor, acrylic or paint-by-number sets, unwind as you paint the turquoise oceanscape from your patio
- Blue Mind in-room amenity: Local seashell chocolates from Bootleg, relaxing bath salts with a Bahamian My Ocean Candle
