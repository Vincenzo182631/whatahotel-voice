### At Dromoland Castle

Intimate, intricate, inviting, The Cocktail Bar at Dromoland was once the O’Brien family’s wonderful library. A fantastical octagonal room with cosy window seats overlooking the Castle lawns, you can enjoy a casual meal or snack from The Gallery menu or simply sit back with a drink while soaking up the regal views (or the banter with our bar staff as you perch by the bar!) An extensive cocktail list is available alongside beers on tap and a superb selection of wine, Irish whiskey and gin.
