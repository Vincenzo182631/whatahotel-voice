### Gracious Castle living on a grand scale

Dramatic, romantic, and brimming with character, each individually designed Suite at Dromoland Castle is a world apart. Cross the threshold and you’ve entered a realm of absolute, utter relaxation. Tucked away within the original Castle walls, you’ll enjoy all the luxury and seclusion of an exclusive residence. With abundant room to stretch out in the sanctuary of your beautiful bedroom and separate areas designed for lounging, cosy conversations and private dining, you’re spoiled for space.

We’ve chosen every detail of the decor to create an atmosphere where time seems to stand still, allowing you to savour every perfect moment. Expect historically-inspired furnishings, shimmering chandeliers, sumptuously textured fabrics, and colour palettes inspired by the Castle’s storied past. Soaring windows look out over the estate’s soothing landscapes, illuminating the room with soft, natural light. Sleek, soothing bathrooms and deep, inviting beds beckon, coaxing you to linger just a little while longer. You’ve probably planned a packed itinerary, but we hope you pencil in some extra time to simply revel in the splendour of it all.
