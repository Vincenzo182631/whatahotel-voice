# About Us

Celtic Castles delivers an award-winning castle booking service, from its base in the heart of Yorkshire.

Celtic Castles was founded in 1998 by Company Director, Roger Masterson, who saw the website as an opportunity to share one of his great passions - castles! Joined by fellow Director, Julie Bilton, in 2003, Celtic Castles now employs a team of the world’s leading castle experts and features an ever-growing list of over 100 unique and diverse castles. Read what our customers say about us!

Whatever you want from a castle experience, we can help. Castle breaks for two, castle weddings, self-catering castle holidays, a private castle rental or corporate events of various sizes and requirements; we have extensive experience of them all. Just tell us what you're looking for and what's important to you, and we can recommend the best options.

## How Can Celtic Castles Help?

Whether it's a short break for two or a lavish celebration, whether you’re just exploring or ready to book, our free service can answer any questions you may have and guide you to the perfect castle.

#### What can Celtic Castles offer that you won't find elsewhere?

**Informed Experts** \- We regularly visit the castles we feature, so the knowledge we pass on to you is first-hand and up-to-date.

**Impartial Opinions** \- We're completely independent, so our passionate experts will listen to your requirements and offer the best impartial advice.

**Personal Service** \- We're available 7-days-a-week by email or phone, we'd also be delighted to arrange a video call to discuss your options further.

**No Additional Fees** \- We never add any fees or charges, so you benefit from our expertise, but pay the same price as booking the castle directly.
