### Your blissful escape into five-star decadence

An imaginative blend of classic Castle opulence and fresh contemporary design, each Deluxe bright and airy room is individually decorated and possesses its own delightful personality. There are graceful five-star touches everywhere you look, with welcoming seating areas where you can lounge and dine at ease, glamourous marble bathrooms, and a majestic King-sized bed. With inspiring views over the romantic gardens and tranquil Dromoland Lough, these luxurious rooms are a blissful retreat after long, lovely days of indulgence and adventure.

Our Deluxe Family rooms, furnished with two exquisitely comfortable Queen beds, provide plenty of space for up to two adults and two children sharing. It’s also possible to arrange for interconnecting rooms in this category: if you are travelling with a larger party, just speak to our reservations team about your options.
