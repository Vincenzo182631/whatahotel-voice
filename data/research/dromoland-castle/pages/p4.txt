### Your grand historic hideaway

Housed within the original Castle walls, Dromoland’s Staterooms have retained many of their  historical features: gracious arches, graceful windows, and elegant quirks of personality. Unique in size, space, and style, each has been designed to bring out the best of its individual character. Bespoke furniture, specially commissioned textiles, and decor inspired by our rich Irish heritage create an irresistible atmosphere of gentle grandeur.

What all our Staterooms have in common is a generous sense of space, with inviting areas intended for lounging, dining, and living your very best Castle life. Curl up in a cosy wingback armchair and gaze out over the dramatic scenery, watching as the colours of the landscape shift and deepen in the changing light. Pamper yourself in your exquisite marble bathroom. Order twenty-four hour room service and tuck yourself into your huge, welcoming bed for a midnight feast with all the trimmings. This is a place set apart for enjoyment and indulgence, drifting daydreams, and deep, restorative sleep.
