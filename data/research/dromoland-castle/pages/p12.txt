### At Dromoland Castle

Experience the indulgent tradition of Mrs White’s Afternoon Tea at Dromoland Castle. Enjoy a selection of finger sandwiches, warm scones with clotted cream and homemade jams, delicate pastries, and our signature tea brack, all served with fine loose leaf teas in sparkling silver teapots. Settle into a cosy nook by the fire and savour a Festive Afternoon Tea, or elevate the occasion with a Champagne Afternoon Tea featuring a glass of Charles Heidsieck Brut Reserve or Champagne Rosé Reserve
