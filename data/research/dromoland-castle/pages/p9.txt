### Aristocratic luxury for lovers of history

Original architectural features. Sumptuous textiles and gorgeous furnishings. Captivating design touches weaving tales of the Castle’s fascinating heritage. Every Viscount Stateroom is linked to a specific character or moment in Dromoland’s history. Space is plentiful here, and every inch of it is richly detailed and immersive—as if you’ve just stepped onto the set of a romantic historical drama—and designed to the highest standards of modern five-star luxury.

Once you close the door behind you, you’ll feel the outside world flicker and fade away. Surrounded by high ceilings and crystal chandeliers, grand arched windows and captivating views, profoundly comfortable beds and indulgent bathrooms, and your own intimate, inviting seating and dining area, you’re enveloped in a perfect bubble of peaceful privacy. Everything about these magnificent rooms has been deliberately chosen to transport you to another dimension of relaxation and delight.
