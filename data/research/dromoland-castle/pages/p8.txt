### Your Family Trip has just found its home

Welcome to your own private sanctuary within the heart of the castle—where history meets modern luxury. This exquisite two-bedroom suite, connected by an exclusive corridor, offers the perfect blend of seclusion and serenity, with breathtaking views over the shimmering Lough Dromoland. Ideal for a multi-generational getaway, this suite is designed for both comfort and style.

Each room has been thoughtfully adorned with a curated mix of antique treasures and playful patterns, creating an atmosphere that feels both timeless and delightfully fresh. Sunlight pours in through the windows, filling every corner with warmth and light.

The first suite bedroom is an inviting retreat for two, complete with a spacious living area, fully stocked bar, and cozy electric fire—ideal for enjoying a glass of something special while the castle’s ancient walls keep the evening chill at bay. The second bedroom features two king-sized beds, offering plenty of room to relax and unwind.

Together, these suites provide the perfect space for your family to create unforgettable memories in a setting that’s as magnificent as it is peaceful.
