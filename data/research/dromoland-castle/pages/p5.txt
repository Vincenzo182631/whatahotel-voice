### Generously proportioned Castle luxury

Handsome and striking, with a distinctly aristocratic air, our luxurious Viscount Deluxe rooms offer you an extra measure of comfort and space. Their prime position within the Castle walls lends them an authentic and engaging ambiance, with original architectural features shaping each room’s character. Indulgent furnishings, fine textiles, and thoughtfully selected details reflect the illustrious history of Dromoland, wrapping you in an environment of effortless elegance and perfect ease.

Open plan and bathed in natural light, these generous rooms feature a restful seating area and an intimate dining nook, inviting you to really settle in and surrender to the relaxing tempo of Castle life. Many rooms in this category also provide a snug pull-out sofa bed: ideal for families with children or friends travelling together. In the spacious marble bathroom, you’ll find a tranquil sanctuary of self care, and when it’s finally time to retire, there’s nothing more delicious than the sensation of drifting off to sleep in our divinely comfortable beds.
