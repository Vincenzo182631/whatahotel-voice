Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an
IN THE ANCIENT CITY OF XI’AN, A LUXURY HOTEL LOOKS AHEAD
Home to 13 imperial Chinese dynasties, Xian today is a mix of eras and influences, where the UNESCO World Heritage-protected Terracotta Army lives among a thriving tech culture. Honoring both sides of the city is The Ritz-Carlton, Xian, set within the central business district. Contemporary accommodations offer city views. Dining includes three restaurants and a rooftop bar. And 31,000 sq. ft. of meeting space can host meetings and celebrations.

Contemporary Luxury
Set within the city’s central business district, The Ritz-Carlton, Xi’an is the ideal base for experiencing this vibrant, ancient capital. Merging traditional elements with modern luxury, the hotel offers a collection of amenities that appeals to every type of traveler.
Sustainability & Wellness
Dedicated to environmental guardianship, The Ritz-Carlton Xi'an focuses on energy and water conservation, waste reduction, garbage sorting, recycling & reusing and sourcing sustainable ingredients. Our commitment to green practices ensures that your stay is not only luxurious but also in harmony with environmental sustainability. At the Ritz-Carlton Xi'an, indulge in opulence while contributing to a greener planet. In addition to dedication of environmental protection, The Ritz-Carlton Xi'an has also left its footprint in community service, reaching out to the underprivileged and the vulnerable group, spreading love and care.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an. Self parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an is Xianyang Airport (XIY). XIY is located approximately 36.4 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
