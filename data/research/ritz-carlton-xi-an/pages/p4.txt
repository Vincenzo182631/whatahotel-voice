Experiences
Xi’an today reflects its historical position at the crossroads of China. The city’s Ming-era walls remain, best explored by bicycle. Narrow lanes weave through the Muslim Quarter. Temples and cultural landmarks dot the cityscape. And cuisine, a mixture of flavors and spices, is influenced by the Silk Road.

The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an Spa
A Xi'an Spa Centered on Relaxation
Serenity has a dedicated space at The Ritz-Carlton, Xi’an. Spread across an entire floor, the hotel’s spa offers an array of treatments designed to complement one another and rejuvenate the body from head to toe. Couple’s can retreat to a suite with a private balcony, while all spa guests enjoy access to the zen garden, relaxation lounges, sauna, whirlpool and indoor pool.

Silk Serenity Spa Package
Duration: 120 Minutes
A journey across time, where East meets West in a warm and healing embrace. Flowing Swedish strokes, soft as silk, soothe and awaken every tired contour—like celestial dancers brushing through brocade skies. Infused with rare Silk Road oils: black pepper to warm, ginger to energize, sandalwood to calm. As aromatic warmth seeps into the skin, feel the rebirth of oasis travelers beneath desert stars.

Does The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

Does The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an have a pool on-site?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an have an on-site spa?
