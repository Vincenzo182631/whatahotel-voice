## The Restaurant

Steps from the historic Grand-Place, our newly reimagined restaurant transports you, momentarily, to Italy.

In our light-filled dining room, custom Adam Ellis artwork pays tribute to Italy’s theatrical heritage with iconic figures from the Commedia dell'Arte. Signature monochrome plates by visionary Fornasetti decorate the walls in individually painted frames that echo the Commedia’s theme.

## Signature Dishes

Meno è meglio - less is more. The uncomplicated joy of Spaghetti Pomodoro. Our traditional Lasagne with ragù. Ravioli alla Carbonara made with guanciale aged by Dierendonk. A menu showcasing seasonal ingredients from local suppliers, handpicked by our passionate chefs.

## Our Story

It starts with Fulvio Pierangelini, the godfather of Italian cooking.

Chef Fulvio’s vision centres on simplicity. His life’s work is focused on sourcing the best seasonal ingredients and elevating them in a profoundly simple dish.

His menu is brought to life by our team of chefs hailing from Italy’s diverse regions. Uncomplicated, homemade food, seasoned with stories.
