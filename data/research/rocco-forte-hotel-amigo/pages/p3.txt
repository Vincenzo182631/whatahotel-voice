# Diane von Furstenberg Suite

The Diane von Furstenberg Suite tells its story through detail. Books written and objects d'art signed by the designer sit alongside signature DVF wallpaper that makes an immediate statement. In the living room, iconic magazine covers rest on the coffee table; the guest book — signed by every visitor who has stayed here — waits nearby. In the walk-in wardrobe, two iconic DVF wrap dresses are yours to try on, with the option to arrange an appointment at the flagship boutique close by. The king bedroom offers a welcome retreat after a day of exploring, with Irene Forte Skincare products in the ensuite bathroom promising a blissfully relaxing close to the day.
