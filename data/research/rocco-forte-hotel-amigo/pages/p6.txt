# Fitness Centre

- Hotel Amigo
- Fitness Centre

## Redefine Your Strength

Enjoy the benefits of our modern Fitness Centre during your stay, available throughout the week from 6.00am to 11.00pm and at your request.

Contact us

### Offering options for both cardiovascular and resistance workouts, our Fitness Centre has a suite of state-of-the-art Technogym equipment and full set of free weights to help you maintain your fitness routine, as well as amenities such as chilled bottled water and towels.

## City Running Tour

Individual or group Running Tours are available upon request. Please contact the concierge for further information.

We’ve made running easy (and more interesting) with a running tour of Brussels’s sights. We have four different categories:

- 3.6km - Where sights include: _Grand-Place, Brussels's Parc, Church Notre Dame du Sablon._
- 6.3km - Where sights include: _Brussels's Parc, Royal Palace, European Quarter, Ambiorix Square._
- 8.7km - Where sights include: _Royal Palace, Mont des Arts, Arc of Triumph, Quartier du Sablon._
- 20.5km _\-_ Where sights include: _Mont des Arts, Royal Palace, Brussels's Parc, Léopold Parc, Arc of Triumph, Boulevard du Souverain, Bois de la Cambre, Justice Palace._
