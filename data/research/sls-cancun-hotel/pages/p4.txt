# WELLNESS

##### AT SLS CANCUN

There are endless ways to indulge in self-care at SLS Cancun. Ciel Spa offers a luxurious menu of body treatments that inspire relaxation, while the 24/7 Fitness Center provides state-of-the art equipment for every kind of workout.

### CIEL SPA AT SLS CANCUN

The mission is simple…to pamper your every need and create an exceptional relaxation experience that exceeds your expectations. Ciel Spa has everything needed for ultimate relaxation. Indulge in the relaxation lounge, massage suite, herbal steam rooms, spacious experience showers, custom massage beds, fitness center and much more.

# FITNESS CENTER

From cutting-edge cardio and muscle-toning equipment to free weights, spin bikes, personal training sessions, plus a schedule of yoga, pilates and salsa classes, the state-of-the-art gym will keep your body moving.

To complement an active and healthy lifestyle, schedule a private, personalized fitness session available from beginner to advanced levels.

**Open from 06:00 to 22:00hrs.**

### private BEACH & POOL

Take a lap in a luxurious infinity pool overlooking the Gulf of Mexico, and relax on our private beach. For ultimate indulgence, reserve a private cabana for up to 6 guests.

### PUERTO CANCUN GOLF COURSE

Discover a day on the course to unwind and indulge. SLS Cancun guests receive access to an exclusive 18-hole Tom Weiskopf-designed golf course. Prices are set and payment is collected at the Clubhouse; prices may vary.
