# RESTAURANTS & BARS

#### AT SLS CANCUN

Capturing the vibrancy of Mediterranean cuisine at CLEO Restaurant and indulge in light bites and cocktails under the Caribbean sun at Bungalow Pool & Beach Club.

## CLEO

More than a culinary experience, Cleo is a space where food, art and community intertwine to create memorable moments. CLEO features familiar flavors from Lebanon, Turkey, Syria. Grace and Morocco, giving it a modern take on classic Levantine cuisine.

EXPLORE CLEO

## BUNGALOW POOL & BEACH CLUB

With lively atmosphere and an eco-chic design inspired by the French Riviera, Bungalow Pool & Beach Club combines cocktails, food, and entertainment with the jaw-dropping beauty of an exclusive and exquisite beach.

EXPLORE BUNGALOW

### IN-ROOM dining

Lounge in the comfort of your suite with private, in-room dining. The menu is available to guests upon arrival.
