SLS Cancun
A touch of urban sophistication to the Cancún waterfront.

SLS Hotels & Resorts finally expanded into Mexico with the 2021 opening of this beachside tower, which brings a touch of urban sophistication to the Cancún waterfront. Italian designer Piero Lissoni tackled the interiors, which pair a vaguely nautical white-and-navy palette with low-slung, mid-century-inspired furnishings and bold wall coverings that draw on Maya motifs. The 45-room property anchors a development that includes a beach club, a 175-slip private marina, more than 100 boutiques, movie theaters, and an 18-hole golf course designed by Tom Weiskopf. At Leynia, chef Jose Icardi bolsters his Argentinian grill menu with the flavors of Japan and Mexico, yielding such inspired dishes as miso-glazed totoaba (a marine fish) with hoja santa, branzino a la plancha with smoked chimichurri, and salmon tartar with bonito flake tortilla and caviar.
