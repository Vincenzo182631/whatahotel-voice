Charlotte’s Foodie Neighborhoods

### Creative Cuisine in South End

Easily reached from the LYNX Blue Line, the South End is a historic area dating back to the gold rush in the 1800s. With an emphasis on culinary excellence, as well as new creations and daring flavor combinations, as well as craft beer production, the South End is a creative and constantly evolving community.

Discover South End

### Artistically Elevated Dining in NoDa

Named for North Davidson Street, the main thoroughfare here, NoDa is the cityʼs arts district with a thriving food scene. As eclectic and diverse as the art youʼll find here, you can go from specialty coffee to Southern staples to Italian classics as you explore.

Discover NoDa

### Endless Variety in Uptown

This is Charlotte's business district, and it has steadily expanded to offer as exciting a range of dining choices to equal the sports and entertainment found here. From elegant fine dining eateries to casual haunts where creative cocktails are the name of the game, Uptown is in a league of its own.

Discover Uptown
