## Accommodations

Modern Room Design

### Inspired by the City

Spacious and contemporary, our guest rooms are designed to both calm the senses and spark inspiration. Subtle gold accents pay tribute to Charlotte's glittering past as the heart of the Carolina Gold Rush, while rich green tones echo the city's abundance of parks and tree-lined streets. It's a space where thoughtful design meets local heritage—inviting you to relax, recharge, and connect with the spirit of the Queen City.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Charlotte have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Charlotte have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Charlotte?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 5 at The Westin Charlotte. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Charlotte?

No, the rooms at The Westin Charlotte do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at The Westin Charlotte have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Charlotte have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at The Westin Charlotte have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Charlotte have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Westin Charlotte have hair dryers?
