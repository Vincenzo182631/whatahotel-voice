### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Charlotte?

The check-in time at The Westin Charlotte is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Charlotte allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Charlotte. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Charlotte?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Charlotte. Self parking is available at The Westin Charlotte, with rates of $18.00 per hour, $35.00 per day, $245.00 per week, and $35.00 perstay. Valet parking is available for $50.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Charlotte have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Charlotte has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Charlotte booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Charlotte?

The closest airport near The Westin Charlotte is Charlotte Douglas International Airport (CLT). CLT is located approximately 7.3 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Charlotte have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Charlotte has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does The Westin Charlotte have a Westin Club lounge?

Yes, The Westin Charlotte has Westin Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Westin Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
