The Westin Charlotte
The modern, chic Westin Charlotte lives in a funky, instantly recognizable high-rise amid a sea of Uptown skyscrapers.

What's the vibe?
The modern, chic Westin Charlotte lives in a funky, instantly recognizable high-rise amid a sea of Uptown skyscrapers.
Is it part of a chain?
Westin is a part of Marriott, and it participates in Marriott's Bonvoy rewards and loyalty program.
Tell us about your room.
The room design isn't anything for a shelter magazine spread, but Westin is known more for its experience than its look, anyway. Ergonomic work spaces, those signature Heavenly beds, rain showers: gang's all here.
Is there a charge for Wi-Fi?
Yes, $12.95 a day.
How are the food and beverage options?
Breakfast (not included in the rate) is either buffet or a la carte, and both are rather pricey. Better yet, head to SoCo Cafe for Starbucks coffee and a croissant before heading out. JP Charlotte has all-day dining and SoCo Bar is on hand for post-work cocktails and light bites.
How did you find the service?
Staff is helpful and friendly.
What's the crowd like?
Wellness-minded worker bees who like their meetings with a side of matcha.
How's the location?
Everything is right here, including the Convention Center, making this a good choice for conventioneers and anyone attending events. Additionally, the hotel has 28 meeting and event venues of its own.
Is there anything you'd change?
The Wi-Fi fee—c'mon, guys.
Any other hotel features of note?
There's a well-equipped fitness center with an indoor pool and sundeck.
What's the bottom line?
The Westin enjoys a great location and nicely toes the line between chilled out and professional.
