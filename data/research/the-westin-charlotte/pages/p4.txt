# Wellness at The Westin Charlotte

## Wellness

Wellness at The Westin Charlotte begins with the fulfilling discoveries of whatʼs new and exciting right here in the vibrant heart of the city. Dive into racing history at the NASCAR Hall of Fame, enjoy...See More world-class performances at the Blumenthal Performing Arts Center, experience live sports and entertainment at the BoPlex, the Spectrum Center and Bank of America stadium. Or explore the unique South End and NoDa districts for shopping arts, and more. The beauty of Charlotte is that from day to day, from season to season, it is constantly transforming. Whether you're discovering landscaped green spaces, shopping opportunities, creative artsy vibes, interesting plates and pints, or on-field and on-stage performances, there is something new to experience everywhere you look. City Sights

### Explore the Arts

Whether in the more traditional setting of a museum or theater, or a more unexpected display in a green space or industrial area, opportunities to engage with the arts are all around you in Charlotte.

Learn More

### Experience the Thrill of Competition

If you're a sports fan, you're sure to be a fan of Charlotte. From teams to tracks, stadiums to stands, cars to clubs, there's something here for everyone to cheer for.

Learn More

### Feel the Music

There's always great live music happening in Charlotte. You can go from stadium tour to tiny hole-in-the-wall performance to outdoor festival. No matter the type of music you enjoy, you'll find it here.

Learn More

### Get Some Fresh Air

Charlotte offers an almost unlimited number of ways to get outside. White water. Hiking trails. Zip lines. Green spaces. Mountain biking, running, boating. This is a city ready for adventure.

### Enjoy Family Activities

Charlotte knows how to get kids and families excited. Whether it's a thrill ride, the perfect ice cream cone, a great playground, a night at the ballpark, or walking through history, there's so much here to be excited about.

### Upgrade Your Style

For shopping, Charlotte's got it. Funky neighborhoods with fashion boutiques and vintage shops. Outlet malls filled with favorite brands. Upscale malls anchored by high-end retailers. Unexpected and interesting pop-ups. Your next find is waiting.

WESTINWORKOUT® Fitness Studio

## Movement That Inspires

Newly renovated. New equipment. New fitness goals. Our WestinWorkout® Fitness Studio lets you workout in all your favorite ways. And challenge yourself by exploring new ways to move as well. Youʼll find treadmills, TRX, Peloton Bikes, and free weights. All in a light-filled invigorating atmosphere with inspiring views of Charlotte that fill the floor-to-ceiling windows.

Gear Lending Program

## Get Moving

Fitness is always an option with the Gear Lending Program at The Westin Charlotte. We have top-of-the-line fitness, strength training, and recovery equipment to use during your stay. So whether you want to use our WestinWorkout® Fitness Studio or explore the cityʼs running trails, like Little Sugar Creek Greenway, you always have the ability to get up there and get moving.

## RunWESTIN™ Guided Group Run

Join our Run Concierge every Wednesday at 7:30 AM for a complimentary guided run through Uptown Charlotte. Designed for all fitness levels, RunWESTIN™ helps guests stay active while discovering the city. Meet in the hotel lobby and start your day energized.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Charlotte have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Charlotte offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bikes, TRX Station, Recovery Kits, and Yoga mats., Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Charlotte?

Harvey B Gantt Center, Mint Museum of Art, NASCAR Hall of Fame, Charlotte Convention Center, Bank of America Stadium Home of the Carolina Panthers and more are nearby The Westin Charlotte.
