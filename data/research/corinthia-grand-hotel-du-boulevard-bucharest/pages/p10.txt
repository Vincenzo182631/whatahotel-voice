# Turks & Caicos

Set on the last remaining undeveloped beachfront parcel along one of the most sought-after stretches of Grace Bay, this exceptional new development is being led by Corinthia Real Estate Ventures (C-REV).

The project will comprise an ultra-luxury 50-suite Corinthia-branded hotel, complemented by an exclusive collection of fully serviced branded residences, including beachfront villas, casitas and condominiums.

The resort has been carefully conceived to maximise the potential of its outstanding beachfront setting, bringing together architecture, landscaping and the natural coastal environment. Alongside the hotel and residences, the development will offer an extensive range of amenities, including a spa and wellness centre, multiple swimming pools, a private beach club, signature restaurants, retail spaces, padel courts and a selection of curated lifestyle experiences.

The architectural vision draws inspiration from the calm, shallow waters of the Caicos Banks, with an emphasis on understated elegance, natural ventilation and a seamless relationship between indoor and outdoor living.

The project is progressing towards formal planning approval, with construction currently expected to commence in 2027.

The Turks & Caicos development represents C-REV’s entry into North America and a significant addition to Corinthia’s expanding international portfolio of luxury hotels, resorts and branded residences.
