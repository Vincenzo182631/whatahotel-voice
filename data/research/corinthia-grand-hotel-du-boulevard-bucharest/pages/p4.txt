# Verdi St George’s Bay Marina, Malta

Perched along the sparkling shores of the Mediterranean Sea, Verdi St George’s Bay Marina emerges as a relaxing destination for your travels. With panoramic vistas of St George’s Bay and mere strides away from the bustling heart of St. Julian’s, the Island’s vibrant epicentre, our hotel offers unparalleled convenience for immersing yourself in the myriad delights of this sun-drenched paradise.

## Hotel's general Information

### Rooms

- 200 rooms including 11 Suites

### Dining & Bars

- Restaurants: Vinotheque Bistro, Bayview Restaurant, Henry J. Bean’s American style Bar and Grill, Sole by Tarragon and Don Royale
- Cafe 24, SeaSalt

### Other

- Two pools and access to Lido
- Health Spa offering therapies and health programmes for vitality, beauty and well-being at the Corinthia St Geoege's Bay
- Health and Fitness centre with gymnasium, sauna and jacuzzi at the Corinthia St George's Bay
- Water sports including a private beach area, scuba diving, sailing and jet bikes
- Indoor and outdoor pools at the Corinthia St George's Bay
- Hairdressing and beauty parlour at the Corinthia St George's Bay
- The Duke's Club (open for special functions)
