# Corinthia Palace Hotel

This is Corinthia’s first hotel, inaugurated in 1968 by HRH the Duke of Edinburgh. The hotel remains the spiritual home of Corinthia, a boutique operation with 147 rooms located adjacent to the Presidential Palace and public botanical gardens.

The hotel started out as a restaurant, opened in 1962, in a century-old restored country villa. The hotel was constructed in the villa’s gardens some years later. Today, it remains the Grande Dame of Malta’s hotel industry with dining, meetings and spa facilities to complement its accommodation offering.
