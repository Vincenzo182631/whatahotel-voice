# Verdi Tunis Beach Resort

Among the finest hotels in Tunis, Verdi Tunis Beach Resort is a Mediterranean haven nestled along the untouched shores of the Côtes de Carthage, offering a serene escape where the azure waves meet the charm of history and culture. Here, the Mediterranean’s beauty becomes your everyday view, and tranquillity is more than a feeling—it’s a way of life.

Step outside and step into a world where ancient stories and vibrant traditions collide. From the awe-inspiring UNESCO-listed ruins of Carthage to the winding alleys of the Medina of Tunis, history feels alive at every turn. Discover the grandeur of Roman Baths, the charm of Sidi Bou Said’s blue-and-white vistas, the bustling energy of the Central Market, or the timeless elegance of the Municipal Theatre.

For those who prefer the pulse of the present, adventure is only moments away. Dive into Gammarth’s nightlife, explore the colourful souks, or experience the lively heartbeat of downtown Tunis. Each moment invites connection—to people, place, and the memories you’ll take with you.
