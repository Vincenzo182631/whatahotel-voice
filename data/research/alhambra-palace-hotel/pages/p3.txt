# New Year´s Eve 2026

## Gala Dinner

Celebrate New Year’s Eve 2026 at Hotel Alhambra Palace in Granada with an evening of fine dining and festive traditions.

Enjoy a welcome cocktail, a gala menu with wine pairings, and the traditional lucky grapes as you toast to the new year.

Explore the gala dinner menu

**Schedule**

From 8:30 pm to 1:00 am, with party bags, lucky grapes, Christmas sweets and chocolates included.

**Prices**

Adults: €365 per person

Children aged 2–12: €130 per child

Prices include 10% VAT.

The gala dinner is **exclusively for guests staying at the hotel**, accommodation is not included.
