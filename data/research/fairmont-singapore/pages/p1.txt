## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Singapore located?

Fairmont Singapore is located at 80 Bras Basah Road, Singapore 189560 and within walking distance to key attractions such as Gardens By the Bay, Merlion Park, and more.

What is the nearest MRT station to Fairmont Singapore?

The hotel is situated above two of the major stations (City Hall and Esplanade stations) in the Mass Rapid Transit (subway/metro) system.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Singapore?

The check-in time at Fairmont Singapore is 3:00 PM, and the check-out time is 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in and late check-out is subject to availability.

Does Fairmont Singapore have parking?

Parking facilities are available at Raffles City Shopping Centre's basement areas at prevailing car park fees. Surcharge may apply during certain periods such as during Singapore's National Day.

What attractions are near Fairmont Singapore?

Fairmont Singapore is located in the heart of Singapore's Civic District, close to Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Merlion Park, National Gallery Singapore, CHIJMES and Clarke Quay.

Is Fairmont Singapore halal?

Yes, we are proud to say that Fairmont Singapore has been awarded the 'Muslim-Friendly Hotel of the Year' at the Halal in Travel Awards 2024. Additionally, Fairmont Singapore features the halal-certified buffet restaurant, Asian Market Cafe.

Does Fairmont Singapore offer children's amenities?

A range of children amenities are available. These include baby crib and blanket, bed guard, bottle steriliser, bottle warmer, baby bathtub, kid’s dental kit and more. Kid's menus are also available at selected restaurants and within our in-room dining menu. Please contact us for more information.

Does Fairmont Singapore have a spa?

Located within Fairmont Singapore, the 50,000 sq. ft. Willow Stream Spa offers a variety of treatments, wellness services, fitness classes and lifestyle membership packages.

Does Fairmont Singapore have a swimming pool?

A landscaped pool area with 2 freeform outdoor pools is available at Level 8. The Eight, our poolside restaurant, is located beside the swimming pools.

Does Fairmont Singapore have a gym/fitness center?

Our Fitness Center includes a cardio theatre, a strength training area, a fitness studio, a wellness studio, six outdoor tennis courts (two artificial grass courts and four clay courts). The fitness center is open 24 hours, daily, and available for persons aged 18 years and above.
