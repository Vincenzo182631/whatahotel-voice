Reviewed by
Scott Campbell
One of my favorite things about stepping into this five-star hotel, which you’ll find in the same Marina Bay building as the Swissôtel The Stamford? Being greeted by Pakcik Ismail, the 90-year-old, top hat-wearing doorman. There are countless other reasons to base yourself here, ranging from the 12 restaurants and bars, the fantastic afternoon teas (recent themes include Peter Rabbit) and the impressive sustainability stats—the Fairmont has the world’s first hotel-based urban aquaponics farm. When it comes to the rooms (there are 778, with the smallest still offering a generous 484 square feet of space), personal highlights include the Le Labo amenities, the marshmallow-like armchairs next to the floor-to-ceiling windows, and thoughtfully designed working areas, while my favorite dining spot was JAAN By Kirk Westaway, which has bagged two Michelin stars. It’s helmed by British chef Kirk Westaway, famous for his innovative takes on British cuisine (cheddar pancake? Yes, please). William J. Haandrikman, managing director of the Fairmont Singapore, suggested the ANTI:DOTE’s bar for a sundowner, and I concur—this Art Deco–inspired venue, with its marble bar and swathes of red velvet, is seriously sexy. Just arrived? Kick jet lag to the curb with the Bright Reset cocktail, made with Cachaca rum, Roku gin, passionfruit Champagne cordial, and lemon balm.
