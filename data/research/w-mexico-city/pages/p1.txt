# Welcome to W Mexico City

## Embrace the vibrant essences of our hotel in Polanco

W Mexico City is located off Reforma Boulevard in the stylish Polanco neighborhood where trend-seekers meet to explore chic shops, a fast-paced cultural landscape and tempting nightlife. It features custom details that define an extraordinary experience. Stay at our Mexico City hotel and enjoy the sleek furnishings and signature touches in our 237 sparkling guest rooms and suites. Keep your Polanco...See More party going at the Living Room Bar that oozes daring décor and quenches the thirst of many with the most innovative signature cocktail collection in the city. Perfect for work and play, our hotel in Polanco, Mexico City offers everything you need for a spectacular event in fresh, innovative hotel spaces. Our nine meeting studios, totaling 7,700 square feet, can accommodate events for 10 to 340 guests.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Mexico City?

The check-in time at W Mexico City is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Mexico City allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Mexico City. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W Mexico City?

On-site parking is available at W Mexico City. Self parking is available for $5.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $5.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Mexico City have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Mexico City has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Mexico City booking.

What is the closest airport near W Mexico City?

The closest airport near W Mexico City is Benito Juarez International Airport Mexico City (MEX). MEX is located approximately 18.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Mexico City have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Mexico City has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
