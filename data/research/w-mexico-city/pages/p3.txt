Mexican

Quintonil

0.7 KM

Quintonil – Mexico City, 2012 – is the emblem for the gastronomical project headed by Alejandra Flores and Jorge Vallejo. The aim is to express Mexican flavors with a personal touch. Since 2015, it has been included in The World’s 50 Best Restaurants.

Latin American

Rosa Negra - Polanco

0.8 KM

Occupies a prime corner spot on Masaryk Avenue, one of the most important streets of Latin America. Rosa Negra is a space that exudes freedom, a tribute to Latin American cuisine. The eclectic menu includes gastronomic traditions from Peru, Argentina, etc

International

Blanco Castelar

0.9 KM

Decorated with traditional elements where you can find from quiet and chic spaces to enjoy a good meal, to more relaxed places to enjoy music and mixology. Contemporary menu that stands out for the use of national ingredients.

Greek

Mythos

0.9 KM

Estiatorio Mythos was born from the love for Greek cuisine of four Greeks who, living in Mexico, met to share with their friends and family the cuisine, traditions and culture of Greece.

Mexican

Cuerno

1.7 KM

Cuerno is the Monterrey restaurant-bar specializing in Northeast grill and grill haute cuisine, which uses the best ingredients and inputs in a handmade menu of starters, top quality cuts, fish, seafood, wines, beers and specialized mixology.

International

Ryoshi - Masaryk

1.9 KM

A cosmopolitan place with a new “hangout fine-dining” proposal better known as “Izakaya”. It is a typical Japanese bar/restaurant, which can be found in the most exclusive cities in the world. Japanese, oriental and international cuisine.

Italian

Rosetta

5.4 KM
