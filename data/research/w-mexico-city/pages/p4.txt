Pricing and Reservations

Spa prices are quoted in Mexican pesos and include taxes. Reservations are recommended, and a valid guarantee must be given at time of reservation. Cancellations at no charge can be made up to 24 hours prior to treatment. However, cancellations made within 24 hours of the reservation will incur a 50% charge. For cancellations within 4 hours of confirmed treatment time, a 100% charge will be incurred. For further information or spa appointments, please contact us by phone or via email and we will gladly assist you. Call us at 52 (55)  9138 1800
