## Two Bedroom Ocean Front Villa

1,800 sq ftOcean View

Nestled along Maui’s pristine coastline, this intimate second-floor ocean front villa offers a private retreat with 2,350 square feet of combined indoor and outdoor living space. Ideal for multigenerational stays and relaxed island escapes, it blends exclusivity with family-friendly comfort.

The villa features a king bed in the primary bedroom, two queen beds in the second bedroom, and a playful loft room designed with keiki (kids) in mind, offering two cozy loft beds for children. Three spa-inspired bathrooms and a gourmet Miele kitchen provide elevated convenience. The private outdoor space includes lounge seating, a jacuzzi, a Viking grill, and a dining area, creating a seamless connection between indoor elegance and open-air living.

Newly reimagined with natural textures and thoughtful design, the interiors reflect the calming tones of the ocean and the spirit of Hawaiian craftsmanship. Floor-to-ceiling windows offer views of the nearby shoreline, where the rhythm of the waves is never far from reach.

## Three Bedroom Garden View Villa

3,250 sq ftGarden View

Feel at home in this 3,250-square-foot garden view villa, where lush tropical surroundings and elevated design create a peaceful island retreat. Ideal for families or small groups, the villa offers three bedrooms with two king beds and two double beds, along with four bathrooms featuring rainfall showers. Two of the bathrooms include soaking tubs for added relaxation.

A gourmet Miele kitchen provides everything needed for effortless meals and entertaining. The private lanai invites outdoor living with a plunge pool, Viking grill, dining area, and lounge seating, all framed by swaying palms and partial ocean views.

The interiors have been thoughtfully refreshed with natural textures and warm finishes that reflect the beauty of Maui. Subtle design details and locally influenced artwork bring a sense of place, creating a welcoming atmosphere rooted in the rhythm of island life.
