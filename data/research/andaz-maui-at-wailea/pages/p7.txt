Andaz Maui at Wailea Resort
A sleek, design-forward Wailea resort with cascading infinity pools, elevated dining, and direct access to Mokapu Beach

In a nutshell
: A sleek, design-forward resort inspired by the Hawaiian beach house aesthetic, the Andaz Maui features a relaxed island atmosphere in a modern luxury package. Known for its tiered infinity pools, elevated dining, and coastal accommodations, this is a resort where guests can fully unwind.
Rooms
The Andaz Maui at Wailea Resort offers a range of accommodations, from modern guest rooms with clean lines and private lanais to expansive multi-bedroom villas designed for groups. Neutral color palettes and wood furnishings let the tropical location and Pacific Ocean views take center stage. Villas include full kitchens, large indoor-outdoor living spaces, private hot tubs, and playful extras like bunk bed rooms and game areas—making them ideal for all types of stays, whether you’re a multi-gen group with kids or a couple on your honeymoon.
Food and drink
The resort’s signature restaurant, Kaana Kitchen, offers a chef’s table experience centered around an open kitchen, alongside an à la carte menu with standouts like grilled octopus salad and buttered red crab topped with an onsen egg. For casual bites and cocktails, Lehua Lounge and Bumbye Bar provide laid-back poolside service. If you’re opting to splurge, dine at Morimoto Maui, where Chef Masaharu Morimoto’s signature ishiyaki buri bop is prepared tableside.
Amenities and experiences
Multi-tiered infinity pools cascade toward the ocean, creating one of Wailea’s most iconic pool scenes, complete with a 24-hour hot tub. The northern wing houses the Awili Spa and Salon, a fully equipped gym, and an adults-only pool featuring cabanas, submerged loungers, and dedicated poolside service. Expansive green spaces host everything from lawn games and luaus to weddings and corporate events, with additional conference rooms and a business center for those working remotely.
What to do nearby
Set along Mokapu Beach, the resort offers ocean activities like snorkeling and Hawaiian canoe experiences. The Shops at Wailea and nearby beaches are quick off-property destinations for retail therapy, additional dining options, and opportunities to connect more deeply with Maui beyond the resort.
The backstory
Formerly the Renaissance Wailea Beach Resort, this property underwent a $90 million transformation led by the Rockwell Group, introducing a modern Hawaiian beach house aesthetic and reimagined pool design when it became the Andaz Maui in 2013. A recent refresh unveiled updated guest rooms, suites, and villas.
Insider tip
Don't skip mochi waffles at the Kaana Kitchen breakfast buffet—especially with fresh whipped cream and chocolate chips. Not the healthiest start to the day, but arguably one of the happiest.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Yes, the hotel offers connecting rooms, family-friendly amenities, and babysitting referrals, though it leans more adult-oriented overall
Accessibility:
Yes, the property features ADA-accessible rooms, accessible pathways, and pool lifts
Wellness:
Yes, featuring treatments at the Awili Spa and Salon
