### Lovely spot, wait for the renovations

The location is nice — it has amazing views and a beach nearby. There’s a very nice road along the coast if you want to go for a walk. The stay was good, but it didn’t feel like a 5-star experience. Two pools were under reconstruction during my stay, and it was very dusty and loud during the day. The rooms are a bit outdated, but they are nice and clean. I really loved the main hall — you can see stunning sunsets from it. The hotel provided some complimentary experiences like lei making, coconut husking, etc., that I got to enjoy. But the options were quite limited, which may have been affected by some recent weather conditions, though I’m not sure.
