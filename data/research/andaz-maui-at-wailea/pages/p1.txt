# Escape to Our Oceanfront Resort in Wailea, Maui

Experience oceanfront ease at Andaz Maui at Wailea Resort, a 15-acre retreat in Wailea, Maui with direct access to Mokapu Beach. Savor farm-to-table dining, cascading infinity pools and thoughtfully designed rooms, suites and villas where the spirit of Maui comes to life through local culture, natural textures and tropical views, creating a stay that feels immersive, refined and island-inspired.

## Rooms, Suites & Villas

Immerse yourself in tropical elegance with our 320 accommodations, including two bedroom suites, beachfront villas with kitchens, and rooms with incredible views. Each space features a private lanai, offering breathtaking vistas of Maui's stunning landscapes.

### Thanksgiving Buffet

Celebrate Thanksgiving in Wailea with a festive Thanksgiving dinner buffet at Ka’ana Kitchen. On Thursday, November 26, 2026, enjoy an abundant feast of traditional holiday favorites and Hawaiian specialties, including roasted turkey with rosemary gravy, Hawaiian salt-crusted prime rib, Berkshire ham, Maui-caught fish, fresh seafood, island-inspired salads and sides, and an indulgent selection of desserts. A keiki corner features child-friendly favorites. Available from 4:00 to 8:00 p.m. Adults $195++ per person and $97++ for keiki ages 6–12.

Details + Reservations

### Ka’ana Kitchen

Experience the heart of Hawai'i at Ka’ana Kitchen. Here at our signature restaurant, we aim to embrace the diverse cultures and flavors of Hawai'i, weaving together traditional and modern practices with the freshest, local ingredients. Our mission is to support local farmers, artisans and fishermen by sourcing local ingredients creating an ever-evolving menu. Our open kitchen is the heart of our restaurant. We invite you to gather and share stories while we share the spirit of Aloha through every dish, where every bite tells a story of the heritage of Hawaii. You are more than just a guest when you step in to Ka'ana Kitchen, you are part of our extended 'ohana (family).

Details + Reservations

### Chef’s Table at Ka’ana Kitchen

Situated in the heart of Kaʻana Kitchen’s open-air styled kitchen, we invite you to immerse yourself in our exclusive dining experience, an evening to remember. Interact with our chefs as they thoughtfully curate a customized six-course menu of fresh, local ingredients sure to impress. Wine pairings per course available.

### Feast at Mōkapu Lu'au

The Feast at Mōkapu is Maui’s most exclusive luxury luʻau experience. Set among the iconic grassy lawn of Andaz Maui at Wailea Resort and the shores of Mokapu Beach, the beachfront feast invites guests to go on an intimate journey of Hawaiʻi’s Polynesian ancestors arriving to Maui and settling in ahupuaʻa (land division) while dancers and storytellers give insight and life to the Hawaiian values that focus on the love of, connection to and kuleana (responsibility) to the land. While enjoying interactive storytelling, Wailea resort guests can indulge in a three-course plating of traditional Hawaiian dishes expertly prepared by the culinary team. Handcrafted cocktails personalized to your liking will also be available during the lu’au to enhance this culinary experience.

Premium Seating Adults $363++/PP, Children (4-12yr) $200++/PP
Classic Seating: Adults $283++/PP, Children (4-12yr) $140++/PP, Complimentary for children 3 and under

### Morimoto Maui

Highly acclaimed celebrity chef Masaharu Morimoto, best known for “Iron Chef” and “Iron Chef America,” uses locally sourced, seasonal fresh ingredients to embark on a distinct culinary style that blends Western and Japanese traditions. Morimoto Maui brings a thoughtfully crafted lunch and dinner menu to a laid-back, beachfront setting. Savor sumptuous hand-rolled sushi and sashimi from Maui’s freshest local fish at the sushi bar and enjoy an authentic island experience at our renowned restaurant.

Details + Reservations

### Bumbye Beach Bar

Bumbye Beach Bar invites you to sit back and unwind. Set on the edge of our lagoon pool near Mokapu Beach, Bumbye is a place where morning slowly drifts into the day. Set your pace to “island time” and take a moment to relax. Later, as lunch arrives, enjoy island-inspired dining, including shareable bites and refreshing cocktails. Then, try dishes crafted with locally sourced ingredients and influenced by the diverse cultures that shape the Hawaiian experience in Wailea.

### Lehua Lounge

Named after Hawaii’s brilliant red lehua blossom, this elegant beachfront lounge is nestled among crystal clear waterfalls and features handcrafted cocktails, gorgeous views of Mokapu Beach and delectable small bites. Choose from an inspiring farm-to-table menu that includes artisan cocktails, hand-infused with fresh fruit juices. With vibrant scenery and a sophisticated Maui setting, Lehua Lounge provides the perfect place to watch the sunset, relax with friends and enjoy the laid-back island dining.

### Lawaiʻa Lounge

Named for the Hawaiian word for fisherman, Lawai‘a Lounge is an inviting evening lobby bar. Inspired by the traditions of those who return from the sea, the space offers handcrafted cocktails in a warm, open-air setting overlooking the beauty of Wailea. Guests can gather for sunset aperitifs before dinner or linger over post-dinner drinks on the lanai, surrounded by relaxed island elegance. With residential-style comfort and refined Maui ambiance, Lawai‘a Lounge provides the perfect place to meet, unwind and ease into the evening.

### Mokapu Market

Mokapu Market is the perfect stop before you embark on your island adventures. Enjoy the island vibe and discover a host of locally made light snacks, flatbreads, prepared foods and locally sourced, native fare perfect for an anytime snack or beachfront picnic. Cool off on those hot Maui days with a selection of handcrafted soft drinks, excellent beers and a large variety of exclusive wines.

### Room Service

Whether it is a fine bottle of wine while you watch a movie or a romantic breakfast in bed, you will find a complete menu offering a selection of beverages, snacks and delicious dishes made from our seasonal and regionally sourced produce for ultimate convenience—contactless delivery available.
