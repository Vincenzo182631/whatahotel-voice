## A landmark through the ages

Cambridge House is one of the rare noble townhouses to have withstood London’s ever-changing rhythm. The home has now been meticulously restored and reimagined by Jean-Louis Deniot, featuring 102 rooms including 24 suites of sublime grandeur. The centerpiece is six historic suites forming a magnificent 6,500 sq ft Piano Nobile — a stage for light, scale, and elegance.

## Wellbeing Without Restraint

A new era of wellbeing arrives in London with the opening of Baeden House. Rooted in Britain’s ancient healing traditions, shaped by the rituals of the Roman bathhouse, and guided by Auberge Collection’s philosophy of joy and pleasure, this is wellbeing without restraint. Set across three expansive floors, an unrivalled sanctuary unfolds. Featuring a swimming pool, bathhouse, treatment rooms, and state-of-the-art gyms and studios. At its heart, an exceptional Master-in-Residence programme brings each space vividly to life, offering an experience that is as transformative as it is indulgent.
