# Celebrate the Art of Savouring Life

## Experiences

Le Méridien Ile Maurice promises an experience that goes beyond its luxury accommodations and the natural relaxation offered by the sand & sea. Located near Grand Bay in the north of Mauritius, our beach...See More hotel offers an array of activities, including beach games, bocce ball, water-skiing, sailing, windsurfing and kayaking. Take a pedal boat out for a solo tour, or join an excursion organized by our hotel boathouse, ranging from glass-bottom boat tours on Grand Bay to the snorkeling and scuba diving for which Mauritius is famous. Our kids club will keep the little ones entertained whilst parents can enjoy the resort facilities. The Explore Spa & fitness centre features saunas, steam rooms, power body showers, a cold plunge pool, a Jacuzzi, massage rooms, aerobic facilities and a gymnasium with a range of exercise equipment. The spa's focus is on natural & organic ingredients with treatments based on the ancient mantras of Ayurveda. Explore Spa by Le Meridien in partnership with PhytomerSee Less

## Water Activities

Unlock island adventures on and beneath the waves. At sunrise, glide across the calm lagoon on a stand-up paddleboard, or feel the thrill of water-skiing as you skim past swaying palms. Hoist the sail on a HobbyCat and harness the trade winds for a playful cruise, then discover vibrant marine life on a guided snorkel tour. With premium equipment, expert instruction, and breathtaking views, every moment on our water is an invitation to explore and create your own slice of Mauritian magic. (Complimentary non-motorized watersports)

## Explore Spa

Step into a world of calm where island botanicals and skilled hands restore body and mind. Discover signature treatments that blend Phytomer’s ocean-inspired rituals with local ingredients, from coconut scrubs to lemongrass massages. Unwind in our steam grotto, find balance in guided yoga sessions, or simply drift between saunas and relaxation lounges. Here, every treatment is tailored to you, inviting deep renewal and a renewed sense of place—because true escape happens when you let go.

Thoughtfully designed, supervised, and certified by Worldwide Kids, it's a place where holiday memories come naturally.

## LE PETIT CLUB

At two, three and four, the world is full of things worth investigating. At Le Petit Club, little explorers can get hands-on, head outdoors, create something colourful, enjoy free play or simply slow down when it’s time for a rest. Open daily from 09:00 to 16:00, each day follows a gentle rhythm designed for children aged 2 to 4, with sensory and creative activities, healthy snacks, nap time and a weekly menu of wholesome dishes specially prepared by our chefs for little appetites. A dedicated team is there throughout the day, with comfort and safety at the heart of the experience. Small in size. Big on discovery.

### Fitness Facility

Keep your momentum with ocean-view workouts designed for every stride and stretch. Our state-of-the-art gym features cardio machines, free weights, and functional training zones bathed in natural light. Whether you’re chasing a personal best or just staying active, you’ll find the tools—and the motivation—to move your way.

### Tennis

Game, set, match—on our floodlit clay courts framed by tropical greenery. Whether you’re honing your serve with a private coach or enjoying a friendly rally at sunset, our courts offer gear, balls, and a competitive edge

### Game Area

Discover our playful corner—featuring billiards and a curated selection of board games for all ages. Whether you’re plotting strategy over chess or sinking the eight-ball, these lighthearted moments are designed for laughter, friendly rivalry, and quality time together.
