# Nirvana: The Essence of Romance

## Signature Suites

Step into a world made for whispered moments and shared sunsets. Nirvana invites you to leave the ordinary behind and lose yourselves in a serene adults-only retreat by the lagoon. Here, soft light dances...See More on calm waters, gentle breezes carry the scent of frangipane, and time seems to pause just for you. Whether you’re lingering over dawn coffee, drifting between sunlit loungers, or toasting the horizon in quiet bliss, every detail is crafted to let your romance unfold—naturally and effortlessly.See Less

### Your Adults-Only Escape

Discover 51 chic suites tucked away in our secluded Nirvana wing. Dive into the serene infinity pool, sip bespoke cocktails at the exclusive Nirvana bar, and savour refined Pan-Asian flavours at Jade restaurant—reserved just for Nirvana guests on breakfast. Here, every moment feels intimately yours, set against the timeless beauty of Mauritius.

## A Place To Reconnect

Set apart in its own wing, Nirvana is designed for couples looking for quiet moments together. With turquoise waters stretching out to the horizon and a beach of soft white sand, the atmosphere is calm,...See More private, and inviting. This haven brings together 51 suites, a dedicated reception, a pool bar, an infinity pool, and its own restaurant. Every space feels easy and welcoming, letting you slow down and enjoy the beauty around you.See Less

### Plunge Pool Hideaway

Sun-drenched and serene, our Plunge Pool Hideaway Suites on the ground floor open onto private courtyards anchored by a hidden plunge pool. Inside, contemporary living areas flow into opulent bathrooms with walk-in rain showers and freestanding tubs—your personal retreat steps from the lagoon.

### Romantic Allure

Intimacy meets ocean panoramas in our 16 Romantic Allure rooms. Perched on the first floor, each opens onto a glass-fronted balcony framing sunrise over the Indian Ocean. Inside, open-plan living and marbled bathrooms invite you to savor moments that linger long after the sun sets.

### Tranquil Bliss

The 18 Nirvana Tranquil Bliss rooms are a sanctuary of calm. Located on the first floor, they feature soothing décor, marble bathrooms with separate walk-in showers and soaking tubs, and balconies shaded by swaying palms. Here, every breath of sea breeze becomes part of your quiet escape.

## You're Welcome

From the moment you arrive, Nirvana feels different. A private reception sets the tone, followed by a bottle of wine, fresh fruit, and floral touches waiting in your room. It’s a welcome designed to make you feel instantly at ease—and just a little spoiled.

## Mornings & Sunsets, Nirvana Style

Start the day your way: an intimate breakfast at Jade—exclusively for Nirvana guests—or the comfort of breakfast served in your room. And when evening comes, the ritual is always the same: cocktails and canapés at sunset, every single day. Some traditions are just too good to skip.

## Stay Longer, Get More

With a minimum of five nights in a Plunge Pool Hideaway or Romantic Allure Suite, your stay comes with thoughtful extras: two green fees each at Mont Choisy Le Golf and 20% off selected spa treatments. Guests in the Plunge Pool Hideaway also enjoy a floating breakfast served right in their private pool—because some mornings deserve a little extra sparkle.
