## Accommodations

Situated in the heart of Pointe aux Piments in Mauritius, Le Méridien Ile Maurice boasts 261 rooms and suites with breathtaking views of the Indian Ocean. ### An Unparalleled Getaway

Wake to endless turquoise horizons—243 of our 261 rooms and suites offer private balconies or terraces framing the lagoon. Let palm fronds dance in the breeze as you sip your morning coffee, and watch candy-pink sunsets ripple across gentle waves. Inside, natural woods, soft linens and clean lines cocoon you in comfort—perfect for family chatter or serene solitude. Every corner here beckons you to unwind, reconnect and simply be.

Beachfront Family Suite

## Family Paradise

Step into our Beachfront Family Suite, where space and light ignite shared adventures. Your suite opens onto a terrace overlooking the lagoon—sea breezes blending with laughter. Thoughtful touches make life easy: a cozy lounge nook, clever storage solutions and kid-friendly amenities at every turn. More than a room, the Beachfront Family Suite is your canvas for crafting lasting memories together.

Adult Only

## Nirvana Sanctuary

Discover an adults-only sanctuary where every detail invites serenity. Your Nirvana room opens onto a private terrace—or a tucked-away plunge pool—overlooking the lagoon or lush tropical gardens. Mornings dawn with panoramic sunrise vistas; evenings unfold to gentle rhythms and a perfectly poured sundowner. Inside, elegant furnishings and soft textures set the stage for pure relaxation. With exclusive access to our tranquillity pool and lounge, Nirvana is your private retreat—designed entirely for your peace.
