# Welcome to Le Méridien Ile Maurice

## Explore the world in style at Le Méridien Ile Maurice

Born in an era of glamorous travel, Le Méridien believes everyone should explore the world in style. Set in Pointe aux Piments, Le Méridien Île Maurice effortlessly blends style and relaxation, positioning...See More it among the leading 5-star beach hotels in Mauritius. With 261 elegantly appointed rooms stretching along a kilometre-long beachfront with direct beach access, Mauritius’ premier family resort creates a haven where every age is welcomed through thoughtful, immersive experiences. Younger guests explore and connect in a joyful, yet safe environment, while parents can unwind and couples retreat to exclusive spaces, including the tranquil adults-only Nirvana wing. Family bonds are nurtured through curated activities and screen-free Memory Makers programmes, while authentic culinary and cultural discoveries deepen every guest's connection to the island. Renowned for its beautiful beach, exceptional service and relaxing resort atmosphere, this Mauritius hotel is a place where meaningful moments come naturally.See Less

## Nirvana - The Essence Of Romance

Step into a world made for whispered moments and shared sunsets. Nirvana invites you to leave the ordinary behind and lose yourselves in a serene adult-only retreat by the lagoon. Here, soft light dances on calm waters, gentle breezes carry the scent of frangipane, and time seems to pause just for you. Whether you’re lingering over dawn coffee, drifting between sunlit loungers, or toasting the horizon in quiet bliss, every detail is crafted to let your romance unfold—naturally and effortlessly.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Ile Maurice?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Ile Maurice is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Le Méridien Ile Maurice allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Le Méridien Ile Maurice.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Ile Maurice?

On-site self parking is available at Le Méridien Ile Maurice. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near Le Méridien Ile Maurice.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Ile Maurice have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Ile Maurice has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Ile Maurice booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Ile Maurice?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Ile Maurice is Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU). MRU is located approximately 60.4 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Ile Maurice have an airport shuttle to Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU)?

Yes, Le Méridien Ile Maurice has an airport shuttle to Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU).

Does Le Méridien Ile Maurice have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Le Méridien Ile Maurice does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
