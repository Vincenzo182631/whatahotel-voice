# Explore Vibrant Flavours of Mauritius and Beyond

## Dining

Savor in the delicacy of seafood at Waves, immerse yourself in the aromatic smells of our Indian restaurant, Kumin, stimulate your palate with Mauritian cuisine at Nomad and savor in the new Asian fine...See More dining epicenter at Jade. Our resort offers an idyllic setting combined with cuisine from some of the finest hotel restaurants in North Mauritius.See Less

## BBB - Beach Barbecue Bonding

Under a sky freckled with stars, gather on the shore for a communal barbecue where everyone selects their favourite fresh catches and local cuts to grill over glowing embers. Complement your feast with crisp salads, island-style sides, and the rhythmic pulse of a live Mauritian guitarist. As marshmallows toast and stories unfold around the fire, families come together in an evening of simple joy and shared discovery.

## Exceptional Dinners

Elevate your stay with a collection of unforgettable culinary experiences. From the theatrical flair of live Teppanyaki to an intimate meal on our wooden jetty or a relaxed feast in the sand under the stars, each evening is crafted to delight. Discover grilled seafood at its freshest, reimagined Creole classics, decadent desserts—and so much more. Let our chefs surprise you.

## Wine & Dining Journeys

Embark on a curated odyssey of taste and terroir. From crisp rosés at sunset to bold reds paired with fragrant house-made curries, our sommelier guides you through nuanced pairings that celebrate Mauritius’s unique palate. Sparkling bubbles set the stage for shared toasts, while carefully matched food and wine awaken curiosity and foster conversations that linger long after the last drop.

## Signature Mornings

Awaken to the promise of the island with two exclusive rituals: float into your day with a leisurely breakfast served on your overwater terrace, or join us for a table-set feast inspired by local flavours—pineapple-infused pastries, moringa omelettes, and freshly pressed juices. Each morning’s menu marries European finesse with Mauritian freshness, inviting you to pause, sip, and savour the light of a new dawn.

### Coffee Culture

Where conversation meets craftsmanship. In partnership with illy, our Coffee Culture invites you to savour perfectly brewed espressos and signature frappés infused with local flair. Whether you linger over a morning pick-me-up or gather for an afternoon pause, each cup promises a moment of connection and inspiration.

### Scoop

Rediscover the simple joy of island life with our gelato and sorbet offerings.. Each scoop is an invitation to pause, share a smile, and let vibrant flavours transport you straight to the heart of Mauritius.

### La Vie Rosé

Sunset, sand, and a glass of rosé—reimagined with a Mauritian twist. La Vie Rosé brings you elegant tables set at the water’s edge, a handpicked selection of wines, and delicate island bites. Reserved for adults, it’s the perfect pause: chic, relaxed, and tinted in hues of pink.
