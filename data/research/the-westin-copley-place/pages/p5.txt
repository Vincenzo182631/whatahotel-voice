### Embrace Boston from our convenient Back Bay

Nestled in one of downtown Boston's most celebrated neighborhoods, The Westin Copley Place, Boston provides modern accommodations and signature touches. Explore family-friendly attractions, world-renowned cultural institutions, popular retail centers and incredible dining destinations. Boston Symphony Hall, Fenway Park and the upscale Copley Place shopping mall are all within easy reach.

Back at the hotel, workout in our 24-hour WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio, an energizing space featuring custom state-of-the-art exercise equipment. In the evening, relax in our beautiful guest rooms and suites, featuring stylish decor, a plush Heavenly® Bed and spacious bathroom.

## Explore Boston

Find something for everyone in Boston. Shop on Newbury Street or at the high-end Copley Place mall and The Shops at Prudential Center, connected to our hotel. Hop in a pedicab and catch a game at Fenway Park and TD Garden. Visit one of our many local museums, including the Museum of Fine Arts, the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum and the Institute of Contemporary Art. And when hunger hits, everything from classic New England fare to trendy hot spots are within a 10-minute walk of our hotel.

## Historic & Iconic

Discover some of the most iconic Boston attractions just steps from our hotel, including Trinity Church, Boston Public Library, Boston Public Garden and the Boston Common. Beyond Back Bay, experience the many unique neighborhoods that make up this historic city, from the cozy Italian restaurants in the North End to the entertainment and nightlife of the Theater District.

## Planes, Trains & Automobiles

Our hotel is easily accessible from Boston Logan International Airport via the free MBTA shuttle, and we're within walking distance of the Back Bay Amtrak Station. If you're driving to the city, we're located right off the Massachusetts Turnpike, which runs into Interstate 93, connecting us to all points west, north and south.

## Sophisticated Accommodations

Retreat to our sophisticated guest rooms and rest on a signature Heavenly® Bed. Our standard rooms are the largest in Back Bay and many offer stimulating views of downtown or the Charles River. Our 143 suites are perfect for families and include the option to book connecting rooms. Upgrade to our Presidential Suite for your next stay with us.

## Family Getaway

Enrich your Boston experience with an array of family-friendly activities around town. Take a refreshing stroll along the Riverfront Esplanade, check out the New England Aquarium and visit the historic landmarks along the Freedom Trail. Explore the city by water with a Boston Duck Tour or a cruise down the Charles River.
