# Relax at our four-star Back Bay hotel rooms

## Accommodations

Step into luxury within steps of history at The Westin Copley Place, Boston – offering the largest guest rooms in Boston’s Back Bay area and one of the only AAA Four-Diamond hotels. Refreshed guest rooms and spacious suites feature modern appointments infused with thoughtful accents that capture Boston’s rich history. Enjoy a suite of amenities to Sleep Well from our signature Westin Heavenly® Bed...See More supporting a restorative rest to pampering Westin Heavenly® Bath products for a spa-inspired escape. Suites are designed to reflect residential layouts ideal for families or business travelers featuring flexible spaces to work, meet, and relax. Savor nourishing meals through our In Room Dining offerings or upgrade your experiences to The Westin Club featuring complimentary breakfast in the morning, and snacks and drinks in the evening. Experience a new level of comfort and thoughtful spaces designed to restore, relax, and rejuvenate in our accommodation in Back Bay, Boston.See Less

More Room for What Your Need Most

### Studio & One Bedroom Suites

More room makes all the difference. Studio Suites offer a range of various configurations equipped with a King bed or two Queen beds and can be expanded to an additional guest room ideal for family trips to Boston. Take in stunning scenes of the Charles River from River View Studio Suites or upgrade to a one-bedroom suite offering a separate living room to take in the best of Boston in style. Whether it’s catching up on work, keeping the kids entertained, or celebrating a special occasion, explore a wide range of spacious suites offering more room and all the comforts of home.

Gain Access to a Suite of Enhanced Benefits

## The Westin Club

The Westin Club provides guests with an extension of their own guest room through access to an exclusive space to enjoy throughout their stay in our Back Bay suites. Experience complimentary daily breakfast and take in the stunning sunrise across Copley Square paired with a custom cappuccino just the way you like it. After a day of exploration, return in the evening for lite snacks and beverages to enjoy on comfortable lounge seating. Enhance your stay and enjoy exclusive benefits through The Westin Club located on the 5th floor of the hotel, open 7 days a week for breakfast.

More Room for More Memories

## Westin Family

From Boston’s cobblestone streets to its historic red brick buildings, wonder is just around the corner with Westin Family. We believe all our guests should leave Boston feeling better than when they arrived – and kids are no exception. Our Westin Family program is designed to ignite wonder and spark curiosity in a city steeped in history. Enjoy savings on a second room available across a range of guest rooms and spacious suites offering more room for more memories

### Luxury Suites

Embracing the city’s historic charm through a lens of modern sophistication, luxury suites offer penthouse-inspired accommodations architected to highlight the sweeping views of the Back Bay neighborhood. Bespoke layouts with separate living, dining and sleeping spaces provide all the extra space to discover the best of Boston. Step into the Ambassador Suite appointed with residential-style furnishings...See More and a soothing color palette, capable of expanding to as large as 1400 square feet across a three-bedroom configuration. Extraordinary in both dimension and décor, the Presidential and Imperial Suite evokes an elevated nod to New England elegance. Each suite offers an expansive configuration featuring a separate living room, a contemporary dining room with seating up to eight guests and two spacious bedrooms with separate bathrooms. Discover the ultimate home-away-from-home experience through luxurious suites ideal for families, memorable celebrations or extended stays in Boston.See Less

Amenities & Services

### Westin Heavenly® Bed

Step into a cloud of comfort after a day of exploring Boston’s historic landmarks. Westin Heavenly® Beds are uniquely designed with a plush, pillow-top construction for restorative sleep no matter where your day’s travels took you. As your evening routine comes to an end, soothe the senses with our bedside Sleep Well Lavender Balm infused with relaxing oils designed for a seamless drift off into dreamland.

### Westin Heavenly® Bath

Transform your morning from a routine into a spa-inspired journey. Enjoy a pampering collection of Westin Heavenly® Bath offerings including White Tea Aloe bath amenities, luxurious extra-large bath linens and plush bathrobes appointed in every guest room ensuring guests begin – and end – every day refreshed.

### Heavenly® Dog Bed

The Westin Copley Place, Boston welcomes our four-legged guests through special amenities that speak to our Back Bay dog-friendly hotel. When your pooch is ready to put their paws down for the day, they'll love the comfort of a plush Heavenly® Dog Bed, special amenities and maybe a treat or two while you enjoy your own room service.

### Westin Eat Well

Whether it’s breakfast in bed, a romantic dinner for two or an impromptu afternoon snack, sip and savor from the comfort of your guest room in Downtown Boston. Dine over delicious dishes featuring local, quality ingredients with well-being in mind giving guests the freedom to choose their nutritional balance from sunrise to sunset.

### Boston Beckons

Our spacious guest room and stylish suites are filled with natural light from expansive windows. Take in stunning views of Downtown, Cambridge, Copley Square, Boston Harbor and plot your course from your own guest room.

### Boston Within
