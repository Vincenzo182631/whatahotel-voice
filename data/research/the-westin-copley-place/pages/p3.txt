Epicurean & Entertainment Experiences in Boston

### Live Music

Gather, unwind & enjoy cocktails paired with live entertainment by local musicians every Wednesday, Thursday and Friday at Bar 10. Groove to the vocals of award-winning singer Lisa Bello who infuses her Thursday performance with the soul of Boston or nod to the instrumental beats of Will Nelson’s Friday performance as he strums his guitar across a range of musical genres. Wednesday's entertainment changes weekly

### Cooking Class Series

Inspired by the season’s freshest ingredients, sharpen your culinary skills in Saturday cooking classes. Chef Roy Kaler—trained at Le Cordon Bleu and former Sur La Table chef—blends learning, cooking, and dining into a memorable experience. Each class hosts up to ten guests, ideal for couples, friends, or individuals. Private sessions available upon request.

### Grab & Go at Ingredients

Whether it’s a full meal to start your morning in Boston, a quick afternoon snack in between meetings or a late night craving after a day of exploration, enjoy a wide selection of offerings from our grab-and-go style market, Ingredients. From iced tea, coffee and fresh pastries to hearty salads and sweet treats to beer and wine, satisfy all of your cravings conveniently charged right to your guest room.

Distinctive Dining in Boston’s Back Bay

### Fuel Your Day

Nourish the body and feed the soul through fresh fare and nutritious offerings exemplifying a holistic, urban dining experience. Whether it’s a healthy start to the morning with a delicious breakfast at Bar 10, a quick tea and hearty salad at Ingredients for an afternoon pick-me-up, or an elevated evening over craft cocktails at Bar 10 before discovering the sizzling flavor of Brazil at Fogo de Chao, there’s something to suit every taste for every mood no matter the time of day.

Sweet Flavors Sourced From the Sixth Floor

## Bees In Residence

Committed to farm-to-table dining and sustainability efforts that leave our community a better place our Back Bay, Boston restaurant has many bees as full-time residents. Located on the sixth floor overlooking Copley Square, their sweet suite produces hundreds of gallons of honey every year carefully harvested and infused into our culinary offerings, dining outlets, and cocktail programs.

The Dining Destination

## Savor International Cuisines Around the Corner

Cuisines from around the globe are just around the corner. Unlock over 140 shopping and dining offerings at Copley Place Mall – conveniently connected to The Westin Copley Place, Boston through a secure walkway. Situated in the heart of Boston’s Back Bay neighborhood, savor an endless array of dining options all located a short walk, car ride, or subway ride away. Whether you seek to savor from the comfort of the hotel or venture into one of Boston’s many neighbors, enjoy flavors for every palate from classic New England lobster rolls to international cuisines from every corner of the world.
