# Welcome to The Westin Copley Place, Boston

## Find your balance at our hotel in Back Bay, Boston, MA

Step into an AAA Four-Diamond hotel in the celebrated Back Bay neighborhood of Boston, MA, which is a short stroll from fine dining destinations and upscale clothing stores at Copley Place shopping mall. Boston Symphony Hall, Fenway Park, and other downtown attractions are within easy reach of our hotel in Back Bay, Boston, MA. Explore with ease and return to family-friendly accommodations and amenities....See More Exercise at our 24-hour WestinWORKOUT Fitness Studio with Pelotons, eat at delicious on-site restaurants, and sip on handcrafted cocktails at our welcoming lobby bar. In the evening, relax in a beautifully appointed, pet-friendly hotel room or suite with stylish décor, a signature Westin Heavenly Bed, and a spacious bathroom. Our impressive 57,493 square feet of contemporary event space makes us a leading conference, wedding, and event hotel in Boston, MA. We look forward to meeting you, providing for you, and helping you relax at The Westin Copley Place, Boston.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Copley Place, Boston?

The check-in time at The Westin Copley Place, Boston is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Copley Place, Boston allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Copley Place, Boston. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Copley Place, Boston?

On-site valet parking is available at The Westin Copley Place, Boston. The daily valet fee is $70.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Copley Place, Boston have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Copley Place, Boston has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Copley Place, Boston booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Copley Place, Boston?

The closest airport near The Westin Copley Place, Boston is Boston Logan International Airport (BOS). BOS is located approximately 3.7 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Copley Place, Boston have an airport shuttle to Boston Logan International Airport (BOS)?

No, The Westin Copley Place, Boston does not have an airport shuttle to Boston Logan International Airport.

Does The Westin Copley Place, Boston have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Copley Place, Boston has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does The Westin Copley Place, Boston have a Westin Club lounge?

Yes, The Westin Copley Place, Boston has Westin Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Westin Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
