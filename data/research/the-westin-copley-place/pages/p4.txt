### The Best Address in Boston’s Back Bay

It’s not the things you take with you that define your journey, it’s the experiences you have and the memories you bring home like exploring Boston’s cityscape steeped in history. Situated in the heart of Back Bay along the Charles River with an MBTA subway station steps from the hotel, explore Boston’s legendary landmarks and abundant attractions from history and art to music and entertainment to dining and beyond all just minutes away. Need to be pointed in the right direction? Our award-winning Les Clefs d'Or Concierge Team is there to guide you on your journey every step of the way.

Discover the Best of Boston

### Fun for the Whole Family in Boston

Your family vacation filled with fun begins at The Westin Copley Place, Boston. Enrich your experience with an array of family-friendly activities around town. Take a refreshing stroll along the Riverfront Esplanade, check out the New England Aquarium and visit the historic landmarks along the Freedom Trail. Explore the city by water with a Boston Duck Tour or a cruise down the Charles River.

### Boston's Vibrant Music Scene

Immerse yourself in Boston's dynamic music scene with concerts happening all around the city. From the iconic Fenway Park to the state-of-the-art MGM Music Hall at Fenway, there's a venue for every music lover. Explore the diverse neighborhoods and discover live performances that make Boston a hub for music enthusiasts. Whether you're into rock, jazz, or pop, Boston's concert scene has something for everyone.

### Shopping for Every Style

Sip, shop and savor across Boston. Recognized as the city’s chicest shopping neighborhood packed with legendary stores and cuisines from around the globe, Newbury Street is a destination in and of itself with fashion and food for every taste. Take advantage of over 140+ shopping and dining opportunities at the high-end Copley Place Mall and The Shops at Prudential Center, conveniently connected to the hotel.

### Global Dining Is Just Around the Corner

Discover the vibrant culinary scene of Boston from casual local eateries to exceptional fine dining. Savor classic New England fare, international cuisines or trendy hot spots all within a 10-minute walk from our Back Bay hotel. Our in-the-know Clefs d'Or Concierge Service will ensure you eat like a local guiding you to the best restaurant and culinary experiences Boston has to offer.

### Walk Amongst History

Discover a city steeped in history, with new experiences around every corner. Visit Boston’s iconic attractions steps from our hotel, including Trinity Church, Boston Public Library and the Boston Common. Beyond Back Bay, experience the many unique neighborhoods that make up this historic city, from the cozy Italian restaurants in the North End to the entertainment and nightlife of the Theater District.

### Sports & Recreation

Outdoor adventures and opportunities for exploration can be found across the city of Boston. Hop in a pedicab and catch a game at Fenway Park or TD Garden. Take a refreshing stroll along the Riverfront Esplanade. Explore the city by water with a Boston Duck Tour or a cruise down the Charles River. Whether it’s a full-day excursion or an impromptu family activity, adventure is just around the corner.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Copley Place, Boston have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Copley Place, Boston offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bike and TRX Equipment, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Copley Place, Boston?

Boston Common, Chinatown Gate, Commonwealth Avenue Mall, Statler Park, Museum of Fine Arts and more are nearby The Westin Copley Place, Boston.
