# JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort

## The JW Garden

Rooted in Marriott family tradition, the JW Garden is inspired by JW’s founder J. Willard Marriott who took daily nature walks, along with his wife Alice, who grew fruits, vegetables and herbs for her legendary Sunday night dinners. Today, we grow fresh produce and herbs in our own JW Garden, which are used in recipes, cocktails, oils, and more. At the JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort we believe that a...See More connection to the natural environment is a necessary part of nourishing one’s soul. From sourcing fresh ingredients to guiding explorations of the landscape, we strive to honor the natural environment and bring the outside inside.See Less

## Be Nourished.

Embracing locally sourced and pollinator-friendly plants, the JW Garden is our pledge to sustainability at JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort, delivering fresh, garden-to-table ingredients for your delight.

### The Pollinator Garden

A welcoming space where nature, sustainability, and wellness flourish. How can you help? Take photos & tag #JWButterflyGarden & @JWMarriottAnaheim on Instagram or purchase our signature Matilda Margarita and take part in our 2025 donation campaign to the Xerces Society towards habitat restoration and protection of monarch butterflies and other critical pollinators.

## Meet Miles and Matilda

One of the most mysterious and remarkable migration in the natural world takes place each year. Matilda and Miles are two adventurous Monarch butterflies on a mission for an epic aerial journey home traveling 3,000 miles from Canada and United States to Mexico in their quest to find the perfect place to rest and lay eggs. Along the way, they rely on special plants—like milkweed—to survive. At JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort, our Pollinator Garden is a safe haven for butterflies like Matilda and Miles, helping them complete their journey and keep their species thriving.

### Succulent Garden: A Lesson in Resilience

Home to drought-resistant plants that store water to survive. These unique succulents thrive in the harshest conditions.

### Floral Garden: A Colorful Welcome

A vibrant display of seasonal flowers designed to attract butterflies, bees, and hummingbirds. Breathe in the fresh floral scents and watch pollinators in action as they collect nectar.

### Vegetation Garden: Seed-To-Table Freshness

A chef’s garden filled with fresh herbs, vegetables, and more used in resort dining. See sustainability in action with our seed-to-table experience at Tocca Ferro.

## Our Signature Ingredient

Grown in our garden and used in recipes throughout our hotel, our signature ingredient, Rosemary, signifying love and remembrance is known for its potential to enhance memory, boost mood, and provide anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits.
