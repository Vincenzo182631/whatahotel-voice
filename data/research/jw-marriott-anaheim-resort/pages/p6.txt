We’re just a short walk from its gates, making your visit the easiest and most convenient it can be. ### The JW Garden

Inspired by founder J. Willard Marriott’s love of nature and his wife Alice’s homegrown cooking, the JW Garden reflects a deep-rooted family tradition. At our resort we grow our own herbs and produce for use in dishes, drinks, and more - nurturing a connection to nature that feeds both body and soul. Our garden also serves as a thriving Pollinator Garden, featuring California native plants that support local wildlife and enhance the sensory experience with vibrant colors and delicate fragrances. Flowers bloom year-round, providing pollen and nectar for butterflies, bees, and other insects.

### Anaheim GardenWalk

Just steps from our doors and easy to reach via our private pathway, Anaheim GardenWalk is the city’s epicenter of outdoor dining and entertainment. Lining the pedestrian lanes of Disney Way and just a block from Disneyland, the restaurants, family-friendly venues, outdoor art, and unique shops of GardenWalk stand out in the city for their variety and creativity. Explore its wonders by day, maybe bowling a few strings at Bowlero, and come back after dark for some of the best nightlife, concerts, and dance clubs in the city, including the renowned House of Blues.

Explore Anaheim GardenWalk

Explore the Best of Anaheim

### Shopping

Lifestyle brands, designer brands, and local shops are all nearby, showcasing the best of Southern California. Head to South Coast Plaza, the largest shopping center on the west coast and the largest collection of designer fashion in the United States. Browse the outdoor shops at Irvine Spectrum Center, where a Ferris wheel lifts you above Nordstrom’s and other fine stores.

### Dining

When it comes to dining in Anaheim, our spectacular Tocca Ferro Italian Chophouse is in good company. True foodies can’t miss the Anaheim Packing District, where a mouthwatering bounty of international dining choices occupy the city’s historic merchant buildings. Anaheim is also part of the nation’s artisanal brewery craze, with ample ales to sample. There’s no end to our city’s culinary pleasures!

Explore Dining

### Live Entertainment

Tap into the rhythms of Anaheim at any of its lively music venues. Lose yourself to the strains of jazz at The House of Blues. Catch national and international musicians and comedians at the City National Grove theater. Go full spectacle when you see a local or national act at the Honda Center stadium. Or get into the thrill of Mission Escape Games, one of the best escape rooms in the country, and Flight Deck.

Upcoming Events

### Sports

Anaheim loves its sports teams, and you can join the locals as they cheer on their home team. Root for the National Hockey’s League’s Anaheim Ducks at the Honda Center or head to Angel Stadium as Major League Baseball’s Los Angeles Angels take on the visiting team.

Explore Events

### Culture

When you’re ready to explore beyond Disneyland, Anaheim will not disappoint. Our vibrant city illuminates, educates, and delights. Visit the Discovery Science Center, Santa Ana Zoo, Orange County Museum of Art, Muzeo Museum and Cultural Center, Billy Beez funhouse for kids, the Richard Nixon Presidential Library, and so much more.

### Beaches

The coastal vistas, gorgeous beaches, and cultural offerings of Southern California’s iconic coast are as close as 20 miles away, making for a rewarding and breathtaking half-day or full-day outing. Take in the stunning beauty of Huntington, Laguna, Manhattan, and Redondo beaches; Balboa and Corona del Mar in Newport Beach; and countless others.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort offers a fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bikes, Tonal Home Gym, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort?

Downtown Disney District, Orange County Zoo, Irvine Regional Park, Muzeo Museum and Cultural Center, Downtown Santa Ana Historic District and more are nearby JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort.
