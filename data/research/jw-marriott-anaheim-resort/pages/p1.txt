## Magic awaits at our luxury hotel near Disneyland® Resort

Experience travel with greater meaning at our new JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort. Located in Orange County, CA, our luxury Anaheim family resort is less than a 15-minute walk to Disneyland® Resort and the Anaheim Convention Center, and steps from Anaheim GardenWalk, placing you at the center of world-class entertainment and dining. Designed to uplift the soul and enrich the body, mind, and spirit, our...See More resort invites you to discover elevated experiences around every corner. Enjoy upscale social spaces and resort amenities such as our JW Garden, yoga and zen room, fitness center, and outdoor swimming pool. After savoring exemplary recipes at our signature restaurant, ascend to the rooftop bar for views of Anaheim and fireworks. Planning a luxury event? Our hotel in Anaheim offers more than 40,000 sq. ft. of flexible indoor and outdoor event space. From locally inspired dishes to our holistic approach to well-being, JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort provides expertly choreographed experiences to deepen your journey.See Less

## The JW Garden

The JW Garden at JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort, is rooted in the cherished tradition of J. Willard Marriott and his wife Alice. Known for their daily nature walks and Alice's garden that enhanced their legendary Sunday dinners, we continue this legacy today. Our JW Garden thrives with fresh produce and herbs, meticulously cultivated for use in our culinary creations, cocktails, and oils. We believe in nourishing the soul through a profound connection to nature, sourcing ingredients sustainably and inviting guests to explore and appreciate the natural surroundings. At JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort, we honor this ethos by seamlessly integrating the beauty of the outdoors into every guest experience.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort?

The check-in time at JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort. The daily valet fee is $68.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort?

The closest airport near JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort is John Wayne Airport-Orange County (SNA). SNA is located approximately 13.4 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
