Award-Wining Dining

### Tocca Ferro Italian Chophouse

Tocca Ferro is an award-winning Italian restaurant near Disneyland, Anaheim, named North America’s Best New Restaurant by the World Culinary Awards. Inspired by Southern California’s farming and equestrian heritage, the dining room blends rustic elegance with iron accents, weathered glass, and warm countryside textures. Led by Executive Chef Des, Tocca Ferro offers a refined, on-property dining experience...See More for hotel guests, rooted in a sustainable seed-to-table philosophy. Each dish is crafted from scratch, from hand-rolled pasta and dry-aged beef to a thoughtfully curated wine program. Tocca Ferro, meaning “touch iron,” pays homage to our agricultural roots and the life-giving mineral found in our fresh ingredients. Whether joining us for an intimate dinner or a memorable evening during your stay, we invite you to savor Italian cuisine shaped by tradition and care.See Less

### Welcome Chef Des

Raised in Southern California, Chef Des brings a warm, welcoming spirit and a passion for creating memorable dining experiences. She believes great food is about more than flavor, it's about bringing people together and making every guest feel at home. Her culinary style combines refined technique with bold, approachable flavors and a focus on fresh, high-quality ingredients. Inspired by the relationships built throughout her career, Chef Des fosters a kitchen culture rooted in creativity, collaboration, mentorship, and genuine hospitality. She is dedicated to creating meals that spark connection and leave guests feeling cared for, welcomed, and eager to return. For Chef Des, food is more than a profession, it's her love language, and every dish is crafted with heart.
