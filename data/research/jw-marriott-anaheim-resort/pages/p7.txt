Celebrate every season at JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort, where holidays feel truly enchanting. From Thanksgiving feasts and Friendsgiving fun to Christmas dining and rooftop New Year’s Eve celebrations, every moment is crafted with care. Steps from Disneyland® Resort, enjoy festive dining, rooftop toasts, and family getaways filled with joy, wonder, and memories to treasure all year.

### Lunar New Year

Stay at JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort, a Disneyland® Resort Good Neighbor Hotel, and enjoy easy access to Lunar New Year festivities at Disney California Adventure Park. From vibrant parades and cultural performances to festive foods and character meet-and-greets, our hotel offers the perfect location to experience it all.

### Food & Wine Festival

Discover bold flavors during the Disney California Adventure Food & Wine Festival. With marketplace booths, wine tastings, and live entertainment, it’s the perfect spring getaway. JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort offers comfort and convenience just minutes from Disneyland® Resort so you can savor every moment.

### Disneyland After Dark Series

Experience Disneyland After Dark, a series of themed, after-hours events with limited-capacity access, exclusive entertainment, rare character meet-and-greets, and specialty food. From Star Wars Nite to Pride Nite, JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort offers the perfect stay near Disneyland® Resort to enjoy these unforgettable evenings.

### Halloween at Disneyland® Resort

Celebrate spooky season with Haunted Mansion Holiday, where Jack Skellington brings The Nightmare Before Christmas to life, and Oogie Boogie Bash, Disney California Adventure’s after-hours Halloween party with trick-or-treating, parades, and characters. Stay at JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort for easy access to it all.

What holiday events does JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort offer?

Enjoy festive dining, rooftop celebrations, and serene moments throughout the resort—each designed to inspire connection and joy.

Is JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort near Disneyland?

Yes. As a Disneyland® Good Neighbor Hotel, we’re just a short walk from the parks and their holiday magic.

Are there holiday activities for kids?

Our Family by JW experiences bring loved ones together through creative crafts, seasonal treats, and joyful traditions.

Does Parkestry have holiday menus?

Yes. Tocca Ferro and Parkestry Rooftop Bar & Lounge feature seasonal menus and handcrafted holiday cocktails.
