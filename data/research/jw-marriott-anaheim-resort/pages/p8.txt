### Annual Magical Events at Disneyland® Resort

Disneyland® Resort hosts a range of annual events that captivate visitors year-round. Lunar New Year kicks off the year with Asian cultural celebrations, followed by the Disney California Adventure Food & Wine Festival in spring. Pixar Fest lights up summer with parades and shows featuring beloved Pixar characters. Halloween Time brings spooky fun with themed treats and Haunted Mansion Holiday, while the holiday season dazzles with Disney Festival of Holidays and festive fireworks. These events ensure there’s always something magical happening at Disneyland® Resort.

Is JW Marriott, Anaheim Resort within walking distance of Disneyland® Resort?

Yes! The resort is just a short walk from Disneyland Park, Disney California Adventure Park, and Downtown Disney District, making it easy for families to go back and forth throughout the day.

Does the hotel offer Disneyland® Resort packages or ticket sales?

As a Disneyland Good Neighbor Hotel, we offer special vacation packages and can assist with multi-day Disneyland Resort Park Hopper® tickets through our concierge or online booking.

Is there a shuttle to Disneyland® Resort?

While we do not offer a shuttle to Disneyland Resort, the parks are just a short walk away. Ask at the front desk for directions and we would be happy to assist.

How far is the hotel from Downtown Disney® District?

The JW Marriott Anaheim Resort is just a short walk from Downtown Disney District, where families can enjoy shopping, dining, and entertainment without park admission.

Do you offer family suites or connecting rooms?

Yes, we provide a variety of spacious suites and connecting room options designed for families, including our Family Bunkbed Suite. Cribs and rollaway beds are also available upon request.

Where is the nearest airport for Disneyland® Resort travelers?

The closest airport is John Wayne Airport (SNA), about 20 minutes away. Long Beach Airport (LGB) and Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) are also convenient options for families flying into Southern California.
