## Indulge in a memorable getaway at our luxury hotel in Aspen

Only a few steps from the base of Aspen Mountain's renowned slopes, the St. Regis Residence Club is defined by its premier amenities and personalized service. Situated in the heart of town, the residences are within walking distance of all the main attractions, including charming boutiques and world-class restaurants. In addition, guests can enjoy the resort's premier facilities, including an outdoor...See More heated pool and a hot tub open all year long. At our boutique hotel in Aspen, CO, the wellness sanctuary with RAKxa spa will leave you relaxed during your stay. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen?

The check-in time at St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 10:00 am.

Does St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen.

What are the parking options at St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen?

Off-site valet parking is available at St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen. The daily valet fee is $60.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen booking.

What is the closest airport near St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen?

The closest airport near St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen is Aspen-Pitkin County Airport (ASE). ASE is located approximately 3.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen have an airport shuttle to Aspen-Pitkin County Airport (ASE)?

Yes, St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen has an airport shuttle to Aspen-Pitkin County Airport (ASE).

Does St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, St. Regis Residence Club, Aspen has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
