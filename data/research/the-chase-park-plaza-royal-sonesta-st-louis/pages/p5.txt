## Pet-Friendly Hotel Amenities

When you and your pet check in, we’ll greet you with a PAWS Welcome Letter and:

- Complimentary dog treats
- Water dish, feeding bowl, & waterproof mat
- Cozy pet bed

PAWS leashes, bandanas, pet waste bags, and other pet supplies are available for purchase at our pet marketplace. In addition, there are designated pet walking and potty areas at select hotels where space allows.
