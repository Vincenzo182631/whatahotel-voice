Meet Fable

Innovative, pet-safe products now available at The Royal Sonesta Hotels.

# Pamper Your Pup With Fable

Your dog deserves to feel as welcome as you do. We’re proud to partner with Fable to enhance our PAWS program at The Royal Sonesta Hotel locations.\*

Products Your Pup Deserves

Pups are welcome at The Royal Sonesta hotels. To enhance our existing PAWS program, we’re partnering with Fable to bring their innovative, stylish, pet-safe products to participating locations. When you book at these hotels, you’ll be able to take advantage of unique Fable food and water bowls, waterproof mat, toys, and more both in-room and for purchase.

About Fable

Fable is a family-owned pet product company who designs with your dog in mind. After finding there are no existing health and safety protocols for pet products, they decided to design their own. Each product is innovative, stylish, and prioritizes the health and safety of your trusted companion first. That’s what made them an excellent choice for partnering to enhance our PAWS program at participating Royal Sonesta locations.
