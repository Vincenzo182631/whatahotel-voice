# Welcome to The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort

## Our hotel on Sanya Yalong Bay

Gazing upon glistening blue waters, lush green palms and gentle mountainsides, you will soon discover that you have found a little piece of paradise at The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort. At Hainan’s southernmost tip, Sanya is often referred to as the “Hawaii of the East.” A marina and a protected mangrove forest surround the hotel, immersing you in natural beauty. Enjoy a dip in one of the pools. Culinary experiences await you in each of our three restaurants and two bars, with authentic flavors and relaxing ambiences. The hotel's 396 rooms include 38 suites and 28 beach villas overlooking Yalong Bay. Iridium Spa offers distinctive elegance, delivering customized experiences. St. Regis Butlers provide flawless, personalized attention during your stay in Sanya, offering one point of contact to answer all of your questions and meet all of your needs.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort. Self parking is available at The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort, with rates of ¥5.00 per hour, ¥80.00 per day, ¥0.00 per week, and ¥0.00 perstay.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort is Sanya Phoenix International Airport (SYX). SYX is located approximately 35.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
