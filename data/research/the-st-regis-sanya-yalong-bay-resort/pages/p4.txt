### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Our Exercise Room equipment brand is “Life Fitness”, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Sunshade Family Pool (Outdoor)Transparent Swimming Pool (Outdoor)Whirlpool

Does The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort have an on-site spa?

Yes, Iridium Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort?

End of the Earth, Nantian Hot Springs, Hainan Yanoda Rain Forest Cultural Tourism Zone, Tianya Haijiao End of the Earth and Corner of the Sea, Yalong Bay and more are nearby The St. Regis Sanya Yalong Bay Resort.
