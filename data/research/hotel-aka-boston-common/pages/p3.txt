# AN ELEVATED STAY IN WEST PALM BEACH

## Whether you’re traveling for a quick business trip, a luxurious week-long getaway, or an extended monthly stay, our studios, 1 and 2 bedroom suites provide all the comforts of home.

The brand-new Hotel AKA West Palm brings luxurious modern hospitality and AKA’s signature style to beautiful West Palm Beach. Located in the heart of the central business district, this sleek downtown hotel features sophisticated studio, one bedroom and two bedroom suites, and residences that will elevate your stay in South Florida. Interiors integrate the soothing simplicity of the surrounding waterways and sparkling sunlight with clean lines and elements of wood, stone, and steel to create surfaces free of all excess. When you aren’t lounging or staying productive in your suite, you’ll enjoy a chic pool deck, outdoor lounge, Technogym fitness center with Excite Live line, exclusive conference space and amenities, and breathtaking water views.

AKA’s walkable location provides convenient access to the vibrant local neighborhood, including lively restaurants, nightlife, open-air shopping at CityPlace, eclectic bars and entertainment on Clematis Street, and cultural attractions such as The Kravis Center for the Performing Arts. At the same time, the nearby corporate finance corridor ensures that your travel schedule remains productive. Directly across the Intracoastal and connected by a short bridge lies the spectacular beach and luxury shopping and dining options of Palm Beach. Explore Hotel AKA West Palm and treat yourself to an inspiring stay.

Amenities

Hotel AKA West Palm offers an impeccable collection of amenities designed to enhance your stay, from the expansive pool and Technogym Fitness Center to the sophisticated lounge and terrace overlooking the Intracoastal Waterway. Stay productive during the day in our resident-only public area, treat your furry friend to five-star grooming at the AKA Pet Spa powered by D is for Dog, and sip and savor at The Blind Monk restaurant. Explore A. Market on Olive for gourmet treats, rejuvenate at Body+Beauty Lab, or explore the neighborhood with our complimentary Circuit Car. Experience the best with exquisite lifestyle amenities that will leave you feeling inspired.

Services

From complimentary Wi-Fi and valet laundry service to housekeeping, fresh grocery delivery, and more, the thoughtful services at Hotel AKA West Palm anticipate your every need.

Design

Hotel AKA West Palm is accessed through a portal-style entrance, creating tranquil continuity with the outdoors.  The grand scale of a water-inspired art piece infuses a sense calm as it guides you to the sleek lobby and beyond. Embodying the intelligence of design focused on elimination of excess, all hardware and functional elements have disappeared, while textural components such as dark wood grain and rippling brushed aluminum create definition. Throughout each space, the simplicity of geometry and light evokes a sensation of sanctuary.
