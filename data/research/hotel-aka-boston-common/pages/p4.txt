# Luxury Serviced Residences in London

## Discover a Refined West End Retreat

We are thrilled to re-open our doors to our loyal guests and new visitors following a dazzling renovation. AKA Marylebone invites you to enjoy an extended stay in one of London's most exclusive West End neighborhoods. Nestled between Hyde Park and Regent's Park, this sophisticated hideaway is housed in a historic 1920s property and combines the convenience of a luxury hotel with the privacy of a monthly apartment rental. Whether you're in town for meetings, working remote, seeking a tranquil space during medical treatment, or a temporary home away from home during a renovation, our elegant urban escape will enhance every aspect of your stay.

Choose between the property's newly redesigned studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom suites and discover inspiring spaces, designer furnishings, and plenty of thoughtful touches. Explore the beautiful Mews House, a private three-bedroom home suitable for larger groups. Perfect for entertaining, staying productive, or relaxing throughout the day, these residences feature convenient home-style comforts, such as fully-equipped kitchens, spa-like bathrooms, and in-suite laundry.

AKA Marylebone is ideally situated for those seeking a peaceful retreat with easy access to London's world-class medical facilities, business districts, and cultural landmarks. Guests in need of serene accommodations near London hospitals or insurance-approved serviced apartments during life's unexpected transitions will find comfort in our tailored approach to extended stays. And when you're not lounging in the comfort of your suite, you'll find many of London's finest cafés, restaurants, museums, and boutiques within easy walking distance.

Explore everything our property has to offer, and start planning your stay at AKA.

AMENITIES

From gourmet coffee and tea service to complimentary access to Third Space Marylebone - the neighborhood's sleek health club - the convenient amenities here are designed to facilitate your lifestyle and enrich your time in London.

SERVICES

From complimentary Wi-Fi and valet laundry service to housekeeping, business facilities, and more, AKA Marylebone offers everything you may require during your stay.

DESIGN

These distinctive serviced London residences are set within a historic 1920s property that has been revitalised to incorporate contemporary style while embracing the integrity of the original architecture and the spirit of the neighborhood.
