# AKA Sutton Place Residences

## Long Term Stays in New York City

AKA Sutton Place provides fully furnished serviced apartments perfect for extended stays in NYC. The property’s beautifully designed luxury apartments are located in Manhattan’s Sutton Place, a quiet, upscale neighborhood just steps from the commercial hub of Midtown and the city’s most renowned medical centers, including Memorial Sloan Kettering, Hospital for Special Surgery, and NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital. From our spacious one- and two-bedroom suites to our spectacular penthouses, AKA’s accommodations provide an elegant alternative to month-to-month apartment rentals. Discover personalized service, thoughtful amenities, and a host of unexpected touches that will ensure your stay is a memorable one.

Whatever your reason for staying with us, you’ll enjoy an elevated experience at AKA Sutton Place. Our residents include traveling executives, digital nomads, those relocating to the city, and anyone else seeking a sophisticated home away from home in New York. From the moment you check in to our stunning pre-war building, you’ll enjoy luxurious services, inspired details, and a prime location that will help you unwind or stay as productive as you wish.

AMENITIES

From our indoor heated pool and 24-hour Technogym fitness center to our complimentary business center equipped with Mac workstations, amenities at AKA Sutton Place help you stay connected — or allow you to disconnect entirely —  for a stay that’s perfectly suited to your lifestyle.

SERVICES

From complimentary Wi-Fi and valet laundry service to meticulous housekeeping, business facilities, on-site laundry and more, services at AKA Sutton Place offer everything you may need during your stay.

DESIGN

Formerly the Sutton Hotel, this elegant pre-war building and its 76 artfully designed, furnished apartments in Midtown have been fully revitalized with a gracious ambiance that is bold and vibrant, yet intimate and serene.
