# Luxury Furnished Residences in Washington DC

## A Sophisticated Setting for Extended Stays

AKA White House provides sophisticated accommodations perfect for extended stays in Washington, D.C. Whether you need a convenient base between meetings and appointments, a temporary home away from home while renovating, or a relaxing retreat as you explore area attractions, AKA’s designer spaces and thoughtful comforts will anticipate your every need. With everything from sleek bathrooms and fully equipped kitchens to spacious terraces and panoramic views, the one-bedroom, two-bedroom, and penthouse suites provide a refined alternative to hotels and apartment rentals and ensure an inspiring stay.

AKA White House puts you just two blocks from the nation’s most important residential address and close to many of the capital’s noteworthy commercial, governmental, and cultural institutions, including the World Bank, the Treasury Department, and the International Monetary Fund. In your downtime, take a picturesque stroll through Farragut Square or leafy Lafayette Square, before heading south to visit the iconic National Mall, home of the Washington Monument and Lincoln Memorial. When the sun goes down, sample the lively dining and nightlife of nearby Dupont Circle — known to be some of the city’s best.

AMENITIES

Whether you’re relaxing in the inviting lounge, working out in the 24-hour fitness center with state-of-the-art Technogym equipment, making use of the professional boardroom, or sipping a drink at the end of the day on our rooftop terrace, the amenities at AKA White House will inspire every aspect of your stay.

SERVICES

From complimentary Wi-Fi and same-day valet laundry service to meticulous housekeeping, business facilities, and more, AKA White House offers a range of thoughtful services to elevate your experience. Our Resident Services Team is on hand to take care of dinner reservations, event tickets, and anything else you might need.

DESIGN

AKA White House's design was led by renowned NYC firm Meyer Davis Studio, known for celebrated NYC restaurants Locanda Verde and Boqueria, as well as other noteworthy international projects.
