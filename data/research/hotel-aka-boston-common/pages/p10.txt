# Boston's Newest Luxury Hotel

## Elevated Style In A Vibrant Destination

Hotel AKA Back Bay is delighted to welcome you to a newly transformed retreat where timeless architecture meets contemporary sophistication, designed by the award-winning Selldorf Architects. Experience the reimagined a.lounge+library, an inspiring space designed for relaxation and connection, along with a state-of-the-art Technogym fitness center and exquisite French bistro dining at The Berkeley. Discover beautifully redesigned guestrooms and suites, where understated elegance meets modern comfort; each space blends warm, residential touches with sleek, contemporary design, offering a serene escape in the heart of the city.

Nestled in the heart of Back Bay, our hotel boasts a storied past as the Boston Police Department Headquarters and is deeply intertwined with the cosmopolitan culture of this vibrant neighborhood. Conveniently located steps from notable landmarks such as Newbury Street, the Prudential Center, and world-class education and business hubs, your stay at Hotel AKA Back Bay places you in the center of it all.

Reserve now to experience world-class hospitality, high-end accommodations, and thoughtful amenities.

Amenities

Hotel AKA Back Bay seamlessly blends thoughtful hotel luxuries with the comforts of home, providing intuitive services and amenities that elevate your stay. Discover a.lounge+library, an inviting space for gathering, whether for work or conversation, while The Berkeley, our indoor/outdoor French bistro, provides a sophisticated setting for dining. Stay active in the state-of-the-art Technogym fitness center and get inspired in our versatile meeting and event spaces. Every detail is crafted to enhance your stay in the heart of Back Bay.

Design

Hotel AKA Back Bay has collaborated with the award-winning Selldorf Architects to transform their first hotel in Boston. Annabelle Selldorf's designs reflect a cultural shift away from the traditional hotel experience, introducing a unique, residential aesthetic to Hotel AKA Back Bay

Guest Services

From a 24/7 Guest Services Team, to complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi, dry cleaning, valet parking, and more, the thoughtful services at Hotel AKA Back Bay anticipate your every need.
