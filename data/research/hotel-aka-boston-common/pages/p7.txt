# AKA Central Park Furnished Suites

Located just off iconic Fifth Avenue, AKA Central Park provides an elevated home away from home in the heart of Manhattan. Whatever brings you to the city, we invite you to enjoy a weekly, monthly, or extended stay in one of our furnished apartments, sleek studios, or expansive penthouses. Discover personalized service, thoughtful amenities, and sophisticated touches that will leave you feeling more enriched and invigorated than when you arrived.

Our luxury serviced residences are ideal for modern nomads, those relocating to the city, or anyone seeking a comfortable base during renovations or transitional periods. You’ll be steps from legendary Manhattan landmarks, world-class art institutions such as The Met and MoMA, and Fifth Avenue flagships including Bergdorf Goodman, Saks Fifth Ave, Louis Vuitton, Tiffany & Co, Cartier, Gucci, and more. When you’re not exploring the city, stay productive in the tranquility of your suite, prepare a meal in your fully equipped kitchen, or simply relax — whatever your lifestyle, you’re sure to be inspired.

Amenities

Our amenities enhance your lifestyle with efficiency and comfort — keep up with your exercise routine at a. fitness, get your morning caffeine jolt at a.cafe, screen films within the privacy of a.cinema, and enjoy complimentary use of our business center.

Services

From complimentary Wi-Fi and valet laundry service to housekeeping, fresh grocery delivery, and more, the thoughtful services at AKA Central Park anticipate your every need.

Design

Inspired by the iconic museums and architectural wonders just beyond our doors, we have proudly partnered with leading designers to provide AKA Central Park's furnished apartments with a signature blend of sophistication and functionality.
