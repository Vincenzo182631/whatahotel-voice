# AKA Times Square luxury furnished apartments

## Sophisticated Accommodations in the Heart of the Theater District

AKA Times Square provides an elevated home away from home in the heart of Manhattan. The property’s beautiful studios, one and two-bedroom suites, and premium penthouses provide an elegant alternative to weekly and month-to-month apartment rentals. Whatever brings you to New York City, you’ll enjoy personalized service, thoughtful amenities, and sophisticated touches that will enhance your stay.

Our luxury serviced residences are ideal for modern nomads, those relocating to the city, or anyone seeking a comfortable base during renovations or transitional periods. The ideal location is perfect for both work and leisure, just steps from Times Square, Broadway theaters, luxury Fifth Avenue shopping, and the corporate and commercial hub of Midtown Manhattan. Enjoy a stroll in nearby Bryant Park, browse the renowned museums of the Upper East Side, visit the restaurants and shops of Hudson Yards or simply unwind in your suite — however you spend your stay at AKA, it’s sure to be inspired.

AMENITIES

Whether you are traveling for business, pleasure, or a mix of both, you’ll enjoy an array of premium amenities designed to complement your stay. Maintain your fitness routine at our Technogym Fitness Center, or stay connected in a.13, our bi-level, lounge and workspace featuring floor-to-ceiling windows and stunning views of Times Square from 13 stories up. Located adjacent to our lobby is one of New York City’s top restaurants. Enjoy lunch and dinner at Hunt & Fish Club, one of the city’s best and most elegant steakhouses.

SERVICES

From complimentary Wi-Fi and in-suite laundry to housekeeping, personal training, and more, AKA Times Square offers everything you may require during your stay.

DESIGN

AKA Times Square has partnered with innovative designers and artists to create a unique atmosphere. From our artful lobby design to carefully curated video art and furniture selection, the AKA experience is both inspired and functional.
