# AKA Beverly Hills Hotel Residences

## Extended Stay Suites in the Golden Triangle

Experience a luxury retreat at AKA Beverly Hills, offering discerning travelers a home away from home in one of California's most glamorous neighborhoods. Live like the A-list in our luxury serviced residences, in a private pied-a-terre perfect for weekly and monthly extended stays in Los Angeles. Whether you're traveling for leisure, business, medical treatments, home renovation, relocating or anything in between - you'll enjoy an inspired stay.

Situated in the heart of the “Golden Triangle,” our refined hotel residence is just steps from the world-famous shops of Rodeo Drive, the Los Angeles Museum of Art, Cedars-Sinai Medical Center, and illustrious gourmet dining such as Il Pastaio, Spago, and Funke. Discover personalized service, thoughtful amenities, and a host of unexpected touches that will ensure your stay is a memorable one.

Our collection of spectacular suites - including fully-furnished one and two-bedroom suites, penthouses, and townhomes, have been designed with the most discerning traveler in mind. Relax and unwind in thoughtfully curated spaces, featuring bespoke furnishings, gourmet kitchens, and fireplaces and private balconies in select suites.  Outside your private retreat, stop by the elegant lobby and relax in the café and lounge area, visit our Technogym fitness center, or treat yourself to a luxury experience in our private cinema. There’s also a business center with a boardroom-style meeting space if you’re in town for business.

AMENITIES

From private film screenings in a.cinema to exclusive direct access to the legendary Spago restaurant, AKA Beverly Hills’ resident amenities are designed to enhance your lifestyle during your LA stay.

SERVICES

From valet parking and complimentary Wi-Fi, to making restaurant recommendations and more, the thoughtful resident services team at AKA Beverly Hills anticipates your every need.

DESIGN

Designed in collaboration with acclaimed firms Koning Eizenberg, Asfour Guzy Architects, and Pamela Burton and Company, spaces here encourage both comfort and creativity, offering an engaging and upscale atmosphere perfect for relaxing, hosting, or staying productive.
