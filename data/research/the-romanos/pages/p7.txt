## Mouratoglou Coaching Philosophy

The idyllic location with year-round sunshine means players of all ages and levels can benefit from an extended playing season and provides a one-of-a-kind destination for the international tennis community. Our coaches will introduce guests to the unique methodology behind Mouratoglou’s global success and help players develop their physical, technical and tactical skills with a choice of private or group lessons, tennis clinics and camps.

## Patrick Mouratoglou – Learn From the Best

Globally acclaimed French-Greek tennis coach and mentor Patrick Mouratoglou brings his passion and expertise to Costa Navarino. Aptly nicknamed “The Coach”, he is the gold standard on the international circuit.
After a successful start to his career with a number of high-level players, in 2012 he became the official coach of Serena Williams, holder of 23 Grand Slam singles titles and widely considered the greatest female tennis player of all time. The “Dynamic Duo” have now been together for nine years and continue to make some of the greatest history in tennis. Patrick Mouratoglou has also helped other international tennis stars achieve their goals, including Greece’s Stefanos Tsitsipas, Coco Gauff, Grigor Dimitrov.
