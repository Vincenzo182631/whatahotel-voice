Dine Around

## Your Dine Around Journey

Offering a rich variety of concepts to meet every craving of yours, the Dine Around proposal features amazing restaurants, each creating a unique dining atmosphere, while our acclaimed chefs thoughtfully prepare wholesome yet tempting dishes, to keep you fueled. With your Half Board meal plan you may choose among the participating venues, where you can pick the set menu at no extra charge or dine a la carte with a credit allowance.

### More Culinary Experiences

Enjoy authentic culinary moments by discovering the rich cultural heritage of the region; cookery lessons in local homes, private dining, wine-making and tasting classes, farm to table experiences and many more.

### Olive Oil Tasting

Join a unique olive oil tasting session and learn from local experts how to discern the subtle flavors of Messinia’s renowned extra virgin olive oil. You are in a region that produces excellent quality extra virgin olive oil. The soil, the variety of olives and the process followed from tree to bottle, yields high quality results that have earned this olive oil its worldwide reputation.

Book Now

### Costa Navarino Wine Experience

Wine selection is a personal affair. In Costa Navarino we invest in personalized service aiming at providing the most delicate gastronomic experience. Our wine experts will not only guide you through a unique tasting journey in the very best place where the life of wine begins, but also help you choose the perfect food pairing for each wine you try.

Book Now

### Authentic Village Cooking

Visit our picturesque village home and discover traditional Messinian cooking with the help of local women who will share authentic family recipes passed down from mother to daughter. You will learn how to prepare a typical full Messinian menu, using regional techniques and fresh ingredients, followed by lunch and the opportunity to savor the food prepared.

Book Now

## Relax at The Dunes Beach

Washed by the sparkling blue waters of the Ionian Sea, The Dunes Beach stretches for 1 km under the warm glow of the Messinian sun. This sandy, west-facing beach, with cozy sun loungers and umbrellas, offers the ideal setting to leisurely enjoy our special beach menu, along with cool beverages and refreshing cocktails.
