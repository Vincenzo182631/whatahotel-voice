### A Notable Yachting Cruise

Indulge in a Yachting experience along the mesmerizing Messinian coast. Your full-day yachting voyage will venture northwest to Pylos, Sfaktiria Islet, Voidokilia beach, and Proti Islet. Our experienced skipper will lead you to the hidden gems of historic crossroads, where ancient civilizations once thrived. Swim into the crystal-clear waters of secluded beaches, crafting indelible memories along the way.

### Private Tour of Ancient Messini

Embark on a private tour at the spectacular archaeological site of Ancient Messini. A unique experience unfolds through “The Messenian Hero Treasure Hunt” that will uncover the site's most captivating secrets. Established in 369 BC by the Theban general and statesman Epaminondas, Ancient Messene is renowned as one of the best-preserved Greek city-states, providing a glimpse into ancient history like no other.

### Private Authentic Village Cooking Class

A private Authentic Village Cooking and Dining experience. Immerse yourself in the charm of traditional village cooking with the help of local women who will share delightful flavours and recipes that have been handed down through generations, utilizing fresh, locally sourced ingredients and the finest extra virgin olive oil of the region.

### A Unique Tennis Experience

Experience world-class tennis, padel and pickleball on state-of-the-art Mouratoglou Tennis Center’s courts. Enjoy breathtaking views of the Ionian Sea while receiving expert personalized coaching to enhance your game.

### Rejuvenation at Anazoe Spa

Explore the hydrotherapy experience and discover a full range of signature therapies and indulging oleotherapy® treatments, based on health and beauty practices of ancient Greece, at the multi-awarded Anazoe Spa.

### World-class Golf Experience

Discover Greece’s award-winning four signature 18-hole golf courses, set in breathtaking surroundings. Choose from exceptional golf packages and challenge your skills at a place where year-round sunshine awaits.

## Ambassador Villas Ithomi & Sapientza

Offering exquisite location and absolute privacy, the beachfront Ambassador Villas Sapientza & Ithomi enjoy unobstructed Ionian Sea views and direct access to the golden The Dunes Beach. These villas introduce the essence of palatial living and feature a master bedroom with king-size bed, and a second bedroom with two twin beds, accompanied by very elegant marble en-suite bathrooms, thoughtfully designed to offer absolute space and ultimate luxury. The Villas also provide a gracious lounge and dining area, state-of-the-art home cinema system, as well as an expansive terrace and a 73m2 private infinity pool.

## Master Infinity Villas

Enjoying total privacy and incomparable views of the Ionian Sea from the wide floor to ceiling windows, these beautiful beachfront villas are decorated in earth cream and light brown colors, combined with a touch of blue. The Master Infinity villas provide absolute space and ultimate luxury and feature one master bedroom and marble en-suite bathroom with a vanity counter, separate bathtub and tropical shower, a second twin bedroom with zip & link beds, a spacious lounge and dining area with state-of-the-art home cinema and 2 plasma TV screens, as well as an exceptional terrace with a 48m2 private infinity pool and direct access to The Dunes Beach.

### Additional Villa Experiences

Explore unparallelled and unique experiences curated specifically for The Romanos Ionian Exclusive villa guests. Immerse yourself in personalized activities and services designed to enhance your stay and create lasting memories.

### Epicurean Expeditions Inspired by the Gods

This culinary feast revives the mythos of the twelve Greek Gods, with an exclusive 12-course blind tasting menu that draws back to the rich culinary heritage of Greece with a modern twist. From the bold flavors of Zeus to the delicate tastes of Aphrodite, every dish is a tribute to the unique flavors of Greece in a journey through time.

### Pirate's Islet Snorkeling Adventure

Embark on a family-friendly snorkeling expedition starting at Proti, an uninhabited islet steeped in wild allure, once favored by notorious pirates. Explore Monopetro, a protected bay with a unique entrance marked by a large stone, offering a secluded and picturesque destination.

### Little Explorers’ Campout

A kids’ camping extravaganza begins! The sandy area of the villa is transformed into a magical campsite, setting the stage for an adventure full of wonders. Our young guests enjoy a variety of exciting activities such as beachside treasure hunt, face painting, creative games, innovative workshops and more, after they can even lay back and gaze at the twinkling stars – all under the supervision of our experienced associates who ensure that every moment is filled with laughter and joy.

### Your Sand-Screening Night

A private movie night is about to begin!  As the sun gracefully sets, the outdoor area of your villa transforms into a mesmerizing cinema under the night sky. The gentle glow of poolside lights and the twinkle of stars above set the stage for a magical atmosphere. Choose your favorite movie from a selection of cinematic gems and enjoy a captivating and immersive movie-watching experience.

## A Scuba Diving Discovery

Immerse yourself in an exclusive adventure tailored for diving enthusiasts. Our PADI-certified team will guide you through the enchanting turquoise waters, revealing hidden underwater treasures and showcasing the vibrant Mediterranean marine life amidst the clear visibility of the Ionian Sea off Messinia's shores.
