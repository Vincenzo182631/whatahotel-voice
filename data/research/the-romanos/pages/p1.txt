A wealth of fresh, organically grown ingredients and a devotion to authentic gourmet cooking define the dining experience at Costa Navarino with more than 10 different dining options.

Start your day with freshly squeezed local orange juice as you take in an extensive buffet showcasing the bountiful produce of Messenia. As evening falls, return for a nightcap at our cocktail bar in Costa Navarino.

### Navarino Agora

Located at Navarino Bay, near The Romanos and The Westin Resort Costa Navarino, Navarino Agora is an open-air destination uniting gastronomy, shopping, culture, and lifestyle in a vibrant coastal setting. Inspired by the traditional Greek agora, it serves as a lively meeting point for guests and locals day and night. Dining ranges from traditional Greek flavors to international cuisine and street food,...See More alongside specialty cafés. Seasonal events, cultural experiences, wellness activities, family moments, and open-air cinema complete this contemporary expression of the Costa Navarino spirit.See Less

## Environmental Practices

At the heart of The Romanos Resort business philosophy lies a strong commitment to economic, environmental and social responsibility, aiming at sustainable tourism development in harmony with the natural environment and local communities of Messinia. The protection and preservation of the natural environment of Messinia is a key priority for the development of Costa Navarino.

Details

##### Energy & Water Conservation

- High-efficiency lighting in guestrooms
- High-efficiency lighting in public areas
- Occupancy sensors
- Water conserving fixtures
- Smart irrigation practices
- Regular preventive maintenance

##### Waste Minimization & Environmentally Responsible Purchasing

- Recycling
- Dumpster monitoring
- Organic waste composting
- Elimination of styrofoam packaging
- Environmentally preferred suppliers
- Environmentally preferred products
- Amenity management
- Alternatives to plastic bottled water
- Sustainable food and beverage options

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino?

The check-in time at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino?

On-site parking is available at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino have in-room Wi-Fi?

Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino booking.

What is the closest airport near The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino?

The closest airport near The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino is Kalamata International Airport (KLX). KLX is located approximately 45.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino have electric vehicle charging stations?
