### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino have a work space?

No, the rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino?

The largest occupancy room at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino is the Koroni with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino?

Yes, all rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino have fridges?

No, rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
