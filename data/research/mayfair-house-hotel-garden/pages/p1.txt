# Refreshingly Eccentric

###### Rooted in its artist narrative and anchored by its signature eccentric design, Mayfair House Hotel & Garden, a MICHELIN Key hotel, offers a one-of-a-kind guest experience that’s artfully layered and culturally rich, paired with warm, individualized service. Centered in Miami’s Coconut Grove neighborhood, it’s a lusher, cozier enclave that transcends South Beach, inviting you to escape to exceptional dining, shopping, parks, and attractions in a refreshingly current yet timeless setting.
