Suites & Rooms in Doha Inspired by Sheikh's Palace
Signature Accommodations
Discover the epitome of luxury at Sharq Village & Spa, a Ritz-Carlton Hotel, where our Premium Suites and Rooms redefine elegance. Immerse yourself in Qatari-inspired architecture, showcasing natural materials and exquisite craftsmanship. Surrounded by lush greenery, each space offers a serene escape, blending traditional charm with modern comforts. Experience a tranquil haven where sophistication meets nature, ensuring an unforgettable stay.
Royal Suite
Experience the epitome of luxury in the Royal Suite, an entire floor designed to make you feel like royalty in a Sheikh's palace. This exquisite retreat features two king bedrooms and two lavish bathrooms, complemented by a grand living room with a dining table for twelve. Step onto your private veranda with a traditional majlis or unwind in the exclusive indoor pool. The master suite boasts a Jacuzzi and a terrace overlooking the stunning Doha skyline, enveloping you in Qatari elegance and opulence.
