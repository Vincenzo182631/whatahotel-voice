Creative Cuisine & Beautiful Spaces
Dining
Experience an ever-evolving landscape of unique culinary adventures at Sharq Village & Spa, a Ritz-Carlton Hotel, where we reimagine the culture of dining to exceed every expectation. Whether you're a discerning traveler or a Doha resident, our diverse collection of restaurants and cafés offers something exceptional for every palate. From our relaxed poolside café serving light bites and refreshing beverages to refined dining experiences, such as the popular Friday brunch, featuring world-class cuisine, each of our venues reflects the highest standards of taste and hospitality. Indulge at our acclaimed fine dining Persian restaurant, where authentic flavors meet contemporary presentation in a setting of timeless elegance.

Offers and Experiences
Al Seef Pool Lounge - Lime Breeze Ice Cream
Enjoy our signature handcrafted Lime Breeze Ice Cream, made with fresh limes from our gardens and served from our charming ice cream bicycle at Al Seef Pool Lounge.
Al Liwan Restaurant - Turkish Table Brunch
Experience the flavors of Türkiye at our Turkish Table Brunch at Al Liwan Restaurant, featuring favorites like Simit and Sujuk—perfect for a warm, vibrant gathering with friends and family.
Al Jalsa Lounge & Café - Golden Cappuccino
Indulge in a Golden Cappuccino at Al Jalsa Lounge, rich and aromatic with a touch of elegance, perfectly paired with freshly baked pastries. A refined moment to relax, unwind, and savor a luxurious treat in a serene setting.

Dine Under the Stars
Dine under the stars with a private beachfront dinner in our elegant gazebos, where gentle sea breezes meet the glow of the Doha skyline. An intimate setting designed for unforgettable moments, perfect for celebrating special occasions or romantic evenings.
