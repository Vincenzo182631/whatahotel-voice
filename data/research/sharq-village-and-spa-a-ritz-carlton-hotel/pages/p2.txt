The Royal Villa | Features & Amenities
The Royal Suite
Oversized and immersed in natural light, the Royal Suite, that can be reserved separately, opens to an impressive majlis terrace with endless sea views. Additional features include a private bathroom with whilrpool fitted with 18K gold and mosaic tiles, a spacious study, dining room for up to 16 guests along with access to the Royal Villa facilities.
A Secluded Haven of Wellness
Step into your private paradise as you begin your Royal retreat in this two-story villa with 7 rooms and suites. The Royal Villa is accessible through a private driveway and includes an annex with a chef's kitchen, an indoor pool, private gym and spa room where treatments can be provided upon request, at extra cost.
Legendary Services
Treat yourself to an incomparable experience with our unrivaled services. Guests of the Royal Villa enjoy a private in-villa check-in, 24/7 butler service, a dedicated Cadillac club car and a daily in-villa breakfast for up to 8 guests. To unwind in the resort, a gazebo is reserved for you at the beach or pool throughout your stay.
Where Heritage meets Luxury
Extraordinary Stays
Experience luxury rooms, spacious suites, and exclusive private villas in Doha, thoughtfully designed to combine authentic Qatari heritage with modern comfort. Whether for a relaxing staycation, family holiday, or extended escape, every stay is elevated by exceptional service and timeless elegance.
Two Bedroom Suite
Designed for families and extended stays, the Two-Bedroom Suite offers spacious residential-style living inspired by the grandeur of a traditional Sheikh's palace. Featuring two beautifully appointed bedrooms, expansive living and dining areas, marble bathrooms, and private balconies, it delivers exceptional comfort, privacy, and an unforgettable luxury suite experience in Doha.
One Bedroom Suite
Discover elevated living in the One-Bedroom Suite, offering generous space for relaxation, work, and unforgettable moments. Complete with a separate living room, elegant bedroom, marble bathroom, and private balcony, this luxury suite in Doha blends timeless comfort with the refined hospitality of The Ritz-Carlton.
Deluxe Room
Experience refined comfort in the Deluxe Room, where authentic Qatari-inspired design meets contemporary luxury. Featuring elegant interiors, a marble bathroom, and a private balcony overlooking the resort's lush gardens or pools, every detail is thoughtfully crafted for a relaxing luxury stay in Doha.
Please note: all room sizes are approximate.
