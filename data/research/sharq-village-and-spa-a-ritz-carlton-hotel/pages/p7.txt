Experiences
Personalization is at the heart of The Ritz-Carlton, where you'll discover an enchanting world of indulgence at one of the finest hotels in Doha near Souq Waqif. Located in the heart of the city, our luxury beach resort offers a peaceful atmosphere, splendid views and unforgettable experiences. From our private beach to our magnificent infinity pool, the scene is set to make your stay nothing short of extraordinary. As a five-star hotel in Doha, we provide unparalleled access to the country's most treasured destinations. Discover Qatar from our beach hotel close to Doha airport and key attractions such as the Museum of Islamic Arts. We offer a range of activities for kids, multilingual concierge services to assist you with excursions and cultural experiences, as well as a selection of restaurants and bars to indulge.
Family Fun with
Ritz Kids™
Experience unforgettable family moments with the Ritz Kids® Program at Sharq Village & Spa, a Ritz-Carlton Hotel. Inspired by nature, culture, and creativity, our signature children's program offers arts and crafts, beach adventures, interactive games, and hands-on activities that encourage young explorers to learn through play while parents relax at our award-winning spa, private beach, or exceptional dining venues.

The Pool and Beach Experience
Relax along our private 400-metre soft sand beach or unwind beside three outdoor swimming pools overlooking the Doha skyline. Whether you're seeking peaceful moments by the water or family-friendly fun under the sun, our beachfront resort offers the perfect setting for an unforgettable day in Doha.
Pool & Beach Day Pass in Doha
Relax, Swim & Unwind
Escape to a day of seaside relaxation at Sharq Village & Spa, a Ritz-Carlton Hotel. Our Pool & Beach Day Pass includes access to a private 400-metre soft sand beach, three outdoor swimming pools, and family-friendly facilities, making it one of the best day pass experiences in Doha for couples, families, and friends.

Magnificent Pools
Discover three magnificent outdoor swimming pools, including a stunning infinity pool, all set within lush gardens inspired by traditional Qatari architecture. Whether you're swimming, unwinding, or spending time with family, every poolside moment is designed for relaxation.
Private Beach
Relax on our exclusive 400-metre private soft sand beach, where crystal-clear waters, panoramic Doha skyline views, and attentive service create one of the finest beachfront experiences in Doha.
Exciting Water Activities
Experience exciting water sports and beach activities along the Arabian Gulf, from paddleboarding and kayaking to beach volleyball and seasonal adventures. Perfect for families and outdoor enthusiasts seeking an active day by the sea.
Daily Scenography
Experience the Past Today
As the sun sets over Sharq Village & Spa, a Ritz-Carlton Hotel, an enchanting tradition unfolds in our main hotel lobby. Join us every evening at sunset for a captivating ceremony where our team of Ladies & Gentlemen, dressed in traditional attire, leads the lighting of the Bakhoor. Sip on authentic Arabic Coffee and indulge in sweet local dates and handmade Baklava as the lobby fills with the warm aroma. Our hosts move gracefully through the space, inviting you to join in this unique experience. Immerse yourself in the rich heritage of Qatar and create unforgettable memories with us. Join us at sunset in the main lobby for a journey through time and tradition.
Planning Your Trip
Let Us Share with You Qatar's Best Kept Secrets
Escape the buzz of the city in our tranquil beach getaway. Begin your journey with a cultural walk to the Museum of Islamic Arts or the National Museum of Qatar. Further during the day, experience local flavors and discover traditional trinkets at Souq Waqif. The starting point of the Doha Corniche, our luxury beach resort is an ideal starting point for your trip to the Arabian Peninsula.
