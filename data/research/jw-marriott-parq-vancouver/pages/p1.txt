# Welcome to JW Marriott Parq Vancouver

## Experience luxury at our Vancouver, BC waterfront hotel

Indulge in a luxury hotel experience unlike any other at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver. With Western Canada's majestic mountaintops as its backdrop, our Vancouver waterfront hotel boasts a distinct sense of place in British Columbia with beautifully designed accommodations and impeccable service. Anchored by the stylish neighborhoods of Gastown and Yaletown, our downtown Vancouver hotel, near Queen Elizabeth...See More Theatre, is at the epicenter of downtown Vancouver’s dynamic entertainment district. After exploring the city's attractions, relax in our hotel in Yaletown, Vancouver, Canada with a spa by JW, work out in the hotel fitness center, enjoy world-class gaming or sample innovative regional cuisine in our distinct hotel restaurants and lounges. In the evening, retreat to waterfront luxury accommodations with floor-to-ceiling windows and scenic city, ocean and mountain views at our luxury hotel in Vancouver. If visiting for business or social reasons, host your event in our 60,065 square feet of venue space.See Less

## At the Centre of Everything

Perfectly positioned between the waterfront and Vancouver’s most iconic venues, JW Marriott Parq Vancouver places guests at the heart of Downtown Vancouver’s Stadium District—steps from BC Place Stadium and Rogers Arena, the premier stages for major league sports and world class entertainment and concerts. From our elevated setting along False Creek, guests can move effortlessly into Yaletown’s chic dining and boutique culture or travel across the water to the artisan energy of Granville Island, enjoying a seamless connection to Vancouver’s most vibrant neighbourhoods—while always returning to a sense of calm at the centre of the city.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Parq Vancouver allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 15.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver. Self parking is available for $55.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Parq Vancouver has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Parq Vancouver booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Parq Vancouver?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Parq Vancouver is Vancouver International Airport (YVR). YVR is located approximately 13.7 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Parq Vancouver has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Parq Vancouver has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
