JW Marriott Parq Vancouver
One of two stylish lodging options anchoring the Parq Vancouver—a splashy urban resort

Why book?
For those who want to be right in the heart of it, it is attached to a casino after all, and right next to BC Place, the city’s sports stadium.
Set the scene
Located in
Vancouver
’s downtown core, you’re a hop, skip, and a jump from many of the major attractions, like sports games, concert halls, restaurants, and the casino. Popular amongst convention goe-ers, sports fans, and Marriott loyalists, but there’s a good mixed crowd with families and vacationing couples as well.
The backstory
Very much a Marriott luxury property. You’ll be collecting all the Bonvoy points here. It’s one of the city’s largest with over 280 rooms and 48 suites. Lots of thoughtful touches and custom experiences can be arranged with give it that elevated touch. It’s been open since 2017 and the building also houses
The Douglas
, another Marriott luxury property.
The rooms
Design wise, it’s a gentle palate throughout. Plenty of white, gray, and taupe. All the rooms are filled with natural light and generally speaking have great views of either downtown, False Creek or the mountains. Even the entry level rooms are spacious at nearly 400 square feet and are all appointed with work desks and separate seating areas. Bathroom wise, the robes are plush, the products are Molton Brown, and the showers are all rainfall.
Food and drink
Food and drink options abound at this hotel. There are 6 bars and restaurants on site and in the summer months a gelato bar. The marquee restaurant is The Victor, located on the sixth floor for prime waterfront views. It’s steakhouse with a Pacific Northwestern slant. Expect prime cuts at Wagyu and porter houses, but a little more seafood and a whole sushi menu. If you’re looking for something more casual head to Honey Salt, where it’s comfort food classics like chicken parmesan, Caesar salad, and mac & cheese.
The spa
Well appointed and offers all the usual face, body treatments as well as nail services. All the products are the organic line Eminence. Views from many of the rooms are of False Creek, which make it extra pleasant when it’s light out. There’s also a spacious gym and an outdoor soaking tub. It’s definitely a place a wellness minded traveler can joyfully lose an afternoon.
The neighborhood/area
Right in the heart of downtown, but on False Creek which gives it lovely views. You’re in prime proximity to all the major attractions. This isn’t a place for a traveler looking for a more quiet or low key experience. It’s much more for someone looking for a mainstream, comfortable, and familiar experience.
The service
Friendly, personable, but not exactly personal. This is a big corporately owned hotel and you feel that the moment you walk in. It’s often busy and bustling, but you’ll have everything you need. Just ask.
For families
They offer adjoining rooms if needed, kids are most welcome, but it doesn’t necessarily cater to them. Many of the dining facilities have kids menus and are happy to provide distractions like coloring books.
Eco effort
LEED gold certified.
Accessibility
Yes, the hotel carefully outlines just how accessible it is on the website as well. Restaurants, meeting spaces and many amenities are ADA friendly.
Anything left to mention?
A solid luxury Marriott that caters to the sports, casino, and convention crowd. The wellness facilities make it also a secretly great place for a traveler interested in that as well.
