# Restore. Reconnect. Rise Above.

## Spa by JW Vancouver

High above the city, Spa by JW Vancouver offers a luxury day spa experience on the 17th-floor wellness level of JW Marriott Parq Vancouver. Overlooking BC Place and False Creek, the spa features therapeutic massages, advanced facial treatments, wellness journeys, steam rooms, and recovery experiences designed for relaxation and renewal. From the open-air Aqua Lounge to the innovative Mind-Sync Harmonic...See More Sleep Lounger, guests can enjoy a complete wellness retreat in the heart of downtown Vancouver.See Less

## Your Journey. Your Pace.

Wellness is never one-size-fits-all. At Spa by JW Vancouver, every massage, facial, and wellness treatment is tailored to your needs, your pace, and your personal sense of restoration.Our Spa Specialists are here to help you discover the experience that's right for you, whether you're visiting for a quick escape or a full day of relaxation.

## A Complete Wellness Floor

Spa by JW Vancouver's 17th-floor wellness level offers more than treatment rooms. Designed as a destination for relaxation and recovery, this luxury spa space invites guests to slow down before returning to downtown Vancouver's energy below. Complement your massage, facial, or wellness treatment with time in the steam rooms, ease tired legs in the TheraBody Jet Boots, or experience deep relaxation in the Mind-Sync Harmonic Sleep Lounger. Whether you're visiting for an hour or spending the afternoon, each wellness experience can be enjoyed at your own pace.

Featured Treatments

### Wellness Journeys

For those seeking a deeper wellness experience, our Wellness Journeys offer immersive head-to-toe rituals. From the Organic Honey Detox — featuring raw honey from our resident JW Garden honeybees — to the Ayurveda-inspired Elemental Balancing and the Vancouver-rooted Spirit of the Mountains, each journey combines therapeutic spa treatments designed to restore body, mind, and spirit.

### Massages

Every massage at Spa by JW Vancouver begins with your input on pressure and intention. From the deeply grounding Reset to the stress-releasing Beyond Calm and the energizing Find Your Balance, each session is fully customized to your body's needs — leaving you restored, present, and quietly at ease.

### Facials

At Spa by JW Vancouver, our facial offerings range from the advanced Bespoke by Doctor Babor — customized with cosmeceutical precision — to the Rose Quartz Gua Sha and Eminence Organic Custom Facial. Each treatment is tailored to your specific skin concerns, delivering visibly radiant, balanced, and healthy-looking skin.

### Aqua Lounge

Perched on the rooftop of the 17th floor, the Aqua Lounge is an outdoor pavilion overlooking BC Place and False Creek. Ease into the hydrotherapy tub — open daily from 9AM to 9PM — where warm water jets release muscle tension and quiet the mind. Chaise lounges and open sky complete the experience. Open to spa guests, hotel guests, and day pass visitors.

### Fitness Centre

Your commitment to movement doesn't pause when you travel. Our state-of-the-art fitness centre is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, offering a full range of equipment for every kind of athlete — whether you rise before dawn or wind down past midnight.

### Mind-Sync Harmonic Sleep Lounger

A truly one-of-a-kind wellness innovation. The Mind-Sync Harmonic Sleep Lounger is a zero-gravity chair that uses Vibro-Acoustic sound wave therapy to guide you into deep restorative states — reducing stress, quieting anxiety, and restoring sleep patterns.

### Curated Offers, Thoughtfully Crafted

From seasonal rituals to packages designed for two, our exclusive offers are crafted to deepen your experience at Spa by JW Vancouver. Whether you are celebrating a moment or simply giving yourself permission to pause, there is always something waiting to be discovered.
