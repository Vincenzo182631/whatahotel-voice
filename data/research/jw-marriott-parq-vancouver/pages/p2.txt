Settle into elevated comfort at our luxury hotel in downtown Vancouver, where spacious guest rooms and suites blend contemporary design with a serene coastal-inspired aesthetic. Select accommodations showcase views of False Creek, while pet-friendly rooms welcome one dog per room (up to 15 kg). ## Sky Suite

Up high, the peaceful energy of the natural world lives in our Sky Suites. Wrapped in floor to ceiling windows, our two bedroom luxury suites are bathed in light on three sides. View the magnitude of the mountains, the rise of the city, and the beauty of the water from your expansive living and dining area. We let in the natural beauty of Vancouver’s mountains and water, complemented with amenities that feel like home. Each bedroom is paired with an ensuite bathroom enveloped by marble, while a powder room is reserved for convenience. Your personal media room occupies the heart of the suite, inviting you to screen a film or conduct business. In these inspired surroundings, you will experience understated luxury and quiet sophistication; where even the most well-traveled find inspiration and peace.

## Parq Villa

Distinctively elevated, the amenity-filled two-level Parq Villa is beyond compare. Follow a handcrafted staircase to the loft-style bath and bedroom, which enjoy a sweeping wall of floor-to-ceiling windows. This exquisite luxury hotel suite in downtown Vancouver features white oak wooden floors throughout and a sliding wall of glass that grants access to a 600-square-foot terrace with views of False Creek waterfront.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver is the Sky Suite with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have fridges?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
