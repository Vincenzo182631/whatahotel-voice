## Dining

Discover exceptional dining in downtown Vancouver, where eight distinctive restaurants and lounges invite guests to savour the flavours of the Pacific Northwest through the creative vision of Elizabeth Blau and Chef Kim Canteenwalla. For sweeping skyline views, visit D/6 Lounge, an indoor-outdoor rooftop destination with cocktails and light bites, or unwind at Centre Bar with expertly crafted cocktails...See More amid the vibrant energy of Parq Vancouver. Honey Salt offers a warm, farm-to-table approach to Canadian cuisine, while The Victor, Vancouver's premier rooftop steakhouse, pairs premium steaks with seasonally inspired seafood and an extensive wine program. Fresh herbs, fruits, and garnishes from the JW Garden enhance culinary offerings throughout the resort, reflecting a connection to nature at the heart of the dining experience. For added convenience, enjoy 24-hour in-room dining in your room or suite, or indulge in Passione Gelato for a sweet finish to an unforgettable culinary journey.See Less
