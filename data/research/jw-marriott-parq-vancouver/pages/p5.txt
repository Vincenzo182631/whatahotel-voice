## Healing, Restoration and Rejuvenation.

Located on the 17th floor of JW Marriott Parq Vancouver, the space includes a 4,240-square-foot fitness studio, an Aqua Lounge terrace with a whirlpool, a manicure and pedicure bar, and steam rooms. Treatments at our spa in Vancouver are holistic and natural, centred around four distinctive results and benefits: Calm, Indulge, Invigorate and Renew.

Learn More

### Purchase Spa Products

Enjoy The JW Treatment at home with a range of quality products from Eminence Organics. Luxuriate in your own time with self-care that will leave you feeling enriched, invigorated, and calm.

### Facial Treatments

Simplicity and clarity are among the defining elements of our facial treatments. Our specialties include therapies to stimulate cell renewal, recapture a youthful complexion, purify every pore or provide an overall deep cleanse. Visit with one of our skin care specialists for a facial treatment designed for your specific needs.

### Express Treatments

Knowing your time is valuable, we offer express spa treatments as a simple indulgence. Delight in a luxurious express chair massage enhanced with advance software for meditation and relaxation, or pamper yourself with an indulgent hand treatment.

### Manicure / Pedicure

Add the finishing touches to your hands and feet with our professional manicure and pedicure services.

### Body Treatments

Set your body on a journey with an infused bamboo exfoliation followed by a body wrap of Douglas blended gels, oils and lotion. Then relax as a Shiatsu foot massage encourages the release of toxins. Our full body treatment is completed with Thai compressions and stretches.

### Waxing

Waxing services are also available. Please check with our spa curator for detailed options.

### Fitness

Enjoy complimentary access to the modern, fully equipped hotel gym.

### Aqua Lounge

After visitors replenish their spirits in Spa by JW they are welcome to relax in the outdoor Aqua Lounge terrace where breathtaking views offer an intimate connection with Vancouver’s natural beauty. The Aqua Lounge encourages rest and rejuvenation with a relaxing hydrotherapy tub, yoga space and chaise lounges. Please note that this is not a standard swimming pool.

### JW Marriott X Flamingo Estate

JW Marriott and Flamingo Estate seamlessly entwines luxury hospitality and an exclusive journey exploring scent, sound, and taste. The moment a guest arrives at our hotel, they are met with an herbaceous and uplifting scent and a unique sound that fosters a profound connection to Mother Nature.

## JW Garden

JW Marriott hotels around the world grow fresh herbs and ingredients in the JW Garden, adding something truly local to the culinary experience. At JW Marriott Parq Vancouver, you will find house-grown herbs in in room dining dishes. We invite you to take a moment to see what is growing by visiting the JW Garden located in the Aqua Lounge during your stay.

## Elevated Gaming

Access to Parq Vancouver’s 72,000 square-foot world class gaming facility features the latest advancements in gaming in a contemporary and natural light-infused environment, as well as private salons for patrons who enjoy a more discreet gaming experience.

## BC Place Stadium

Rising along False Creek with its celebrated retractable roof and dramatic lighting displays, BC Place Stadium stands as Downtown Vancouver’s premier venue for major sporting events, global concerts, and internationally recognized showcases. Home to the BC Lions and Vancouver Whitecaps FC, this iconic stadium hosts everything from world class matches to large scale cultural celebrations within a state of the art environment designed for unforgettable moments. From our hotel near BC Place Stadium, Vancouver, guests can experience the energy of the Stadium District at its peak—while enjoying a refined sense of ease at the center of the city, where each event brings downtown and the waterfront to life

Learn More

### Science World at Telus World of Science

Inspire young minds with interactive, hands-on science displays and films for kids from toddlers to early teens. Accessible from along the seawall, Science World is located inside a geodesic dome and includes an OMNIMAX Theater.

### Kids Market at Granville Island

Explore the Kids Market located on Granville Island for an afternoon of indoor or outdoor play. Situated in a 100 year old factory, the Kids Market includes interactive areas and over 25 shops for toys, dress-up, games, books and more.

### Vancouver Aquarium

Canada’s largest aquarium and home to over 70,000 creatures, a visit to the Vancouver Aquarium in Stanley Park will immerse you in the region’s marine life.

### Whistler

From skiing to mountain biking, Whistler is the perfect place to explore your adventurous side. As one of North Americas largest ski resorts, Whistler offers a beautiful chalet-style pedestrian village great for a day outside of the city. Don’t miss the Peak to Peak Gondola ride for an unforgettable experience.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Parq Vancouver offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Weight Machines, and Yoga Mats & Yoga Balls, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Parq Vancouver has one pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott Parq Vancouver have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa by JW is located on-site at JW Marriott Parq Vancouver. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Parq Vancouver?

Queen Elizabeth Park, VanDusen Botanical Garden, CF Pacific Centre, Granville Island & Public Market, Historic Gastown & Steam Clock and more are nearby JW Marriott Parq Vancouver.
