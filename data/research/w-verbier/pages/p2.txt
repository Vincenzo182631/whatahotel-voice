Check in, slip into a W signature bathrobe...See More and cozy-up to your spacious hotel room's private fireplace. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Verbier have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Verbier have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Verbier?

The largest occupancy room at W Verbier is the Epic Family with a maximum occupancy of 8.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Verbier?

Some rooms at W Verbier permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Verbier have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Verbier have a Refrigerator (full size).

Do the rooms at W Verbier have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Verbier have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Verbier have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Verbier have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Verbier have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Verbier have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
