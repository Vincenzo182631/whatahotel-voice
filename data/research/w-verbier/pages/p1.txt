# Welcome to W Verbier

## Explore The Swiss Alps Year-Round At Our Hotel In Verbier

Perched at 1,531 meters in the Swiss Alps, W Verbier is a ski-in/ski-out lifestyle luxury hotel in the heart of Verbier Resort, with direct access to more than 400 km of skiing and hiking terrain across Verbier and the 4 Vallées. This standout Verbier luxury ski hotel blends contemporary urban design with authentic alpine charm across 123 stylish rooms and suites, each with a fireplace, Signature W...See More bed, and private balcony. For family stays or longer escapes, W Residences offer two- to three-bedroom accommodations with private kitchens and spacious living areas. Guests love the mountain views and attentive service, while Brasserie 1519 by Michelin-starred chef Sergi Arola, the W Living Room, and 1,300 sqm of wellness at Le Spa W Verbier bring dining, spa, and social energy together. With two pools, nine treatment rooms, a 24-hour FIT gym, 245 sqm of flexible event space, and Whatever/Whenever service, every stay feels effortless.See Less
