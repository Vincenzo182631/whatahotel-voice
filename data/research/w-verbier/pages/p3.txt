Enjoy your stay in Switzerland with epicurean cuisine and custom cocktails. Our restaurants showcase the creativity and expertise of the W Verbier culinary team. W Verbier offers a diverse range of restaurants and bars created to fulfill every mood and every moment. From a morning coffee in the first rays of sunshine to après-ski vibes set to the DJ sounds of W OFF PISTE, our venues welcome you from...See More sunrise to sunset.
