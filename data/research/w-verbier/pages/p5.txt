# A Perfect Spot for Thrill-Seekers

## Skiing

Bask in the beauty and ski bounty of Les 4 Vallées, comprising the Verbier, Nendaz, Veysonnaz, and Thyon. The largest ski domain on Swiss soil, it features an access point, the Medran Gondola, just steps from W Verbier Ski Resort. Perched at an altitude of 1,500 meters, the hotel offers ski-in/ski-out access. Soar upwards towards and discover 412 kilometers of glittering, groomed slopes boasting 92...See More lifts and endless ski and snowboard itineraries for all levels. 43% of the pistes are marked as blue, 29% as red, and 28% as black. There are also infinite freeriding trails. Some of the best off-piste terrain in the world, they are easily accessible from the secured pistes, and expert guides are readily available to take you on an unforgettable tour. Snowpark La Chaux offers countless freestyle runs, boxes, and rails, while a network of trails caters to cross-country skiers.See Less

### Sparkling Snow & Spectacular Slopes

Perched at an altitude of 1,500 meters, W Verbier is situated on a plateau that gets more than its share of sunshine and fabulous panoramic mountain views. From this perfect location, in the center of it all, it’s easy to hop on transportation to all of the area’s main attractions. Located at the end of the fabulous Rue de Médran, surrounding Place Blanche, the Médran Gondola, which connects you to...See More all of the world-class mountain sports, is just outside. The largest lift will take you to soaring heights for amazing skiing in Switzerland.See Less

### Stay at W Verbier and Enjoy True Ski-In / Ski-Out Access in the Heart of Verbier

Step directly from the hotel onto the slopes of the renowned 4 Vallées, one of the largest ski areas in Europe. Spend more time on the mountain with the ski lifts just steps away. After a day on the slopes, return easily to the comfort and atmosphere of W Verbier.

Whatever You Want To Do In The Mountains, Starts Here

## Mountain Concierge by Powder Extreme

In Verbier, the possibilities are endless on and off the slopes. To help you make the most of every moment, our Mountain Concierge is here to guide you, plan your activities, and take care of every detail. From ski days to unique alpine experiences, simply choose what inspires you and we’ll handle the rest so you can focus on enjoying the mountains. For your convenience, ski equipment can be rented directly from the hotel through our trusted partner Mountain Air, located just opposite the property. Once fitted, your equipment is stored on-site by our Mountain Concierge, allowing for a seamless, ski-in/ski-out experience without the hassle of carrying your gear.

### Winter Offerings

### Ski and Snowboard Lessons

Learn how to ski or snowboard and advance your technical skills. Group or private lesson, we can tailor the experience to fit your needs.

### Off-Piste Coaching

Ever wanted to learn how to ski powder? Verbier has some of the best off-piste terrain in the world! Enjoy your first experience with a fully qualified instructor to improve your freeride technique or just get away from the slopes for a day.

### Mountain Guides

Go off the beaten track. Experience ski touring and skiing on glacial terrain. Well, these are all options when you ski with a guide – from a one day experience to over-night trips in high mountain cabins, and of course Heli-skiing.

### Heli-Skiing

Experience the adrenaline of a helicopter ride to one of the remote peaks around the Verbier ski area. Ski untouched powder and experience unrivalled descents. And if you haven’t had enough, you can fly back up and do it all again.

### Scenic Helicopter Flights

Whether you ski or not, why not enjoy an amazing flight around the Valais area in a helicopter. Fly around the region’s signature mountains including Mont Blanc and the Matterhorn. You can even ski to the heli-pad at Croix de Coeur where your helicopter will pick you up.

### Paragliding

Take to the open skies on a paraglider solo or tandem. Thrill your senses, infuse your mind with pure adrenaline and escape your routine point of view with a paragliding flight in the Swiss Alps. The view is better from all the way up the mountains.

### Photography Lessons

Let the stunning beauty of the Alps inspire your inner photographer, we’ll take care of the rest.

### Alternative Activities

Available On Request: Snow-shoeing, Cross country skiing, Telemarking, Torch-lit descents.
