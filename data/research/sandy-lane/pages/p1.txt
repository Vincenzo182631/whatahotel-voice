## Overview

Legendary Cream-Colored Coralstone hotel reflecting Palladian architecture, set amid 300 mahogany trees and situated beachfront.

- Details
- Year Built: 1961
- Year Last Renovated: 2008
- Check in Time: 4:00 PM
- Check out Time: 12:00 PM
- Number of Floors: 2

- Sandy Lane Hotel GDS Codes
