Offers & Seasonal Experiences To Savor

### Weekend Brunch at Paris Bar

Savor a leisurely weekend hotel brunch near Central Park at Paris Bar, Saturdays and Sundays from 11 AM – 4 PM. Enjoy French-inspired fare from the raw bar to signature classics like croque monsieur and croque madame, along with fresh salads and savory plates in an elegant, Parisian-chic setting.

### Rooftop Dining at Le Jardin

Savor the city skyline at Le Jardin. Sip craft cocktails like our signature espresso martini and enjoy your meal as the sun sets. Our rooftop bar in New York is the perfect golden hour escape near Central Park, where elevated food and drinks meet unforgettable views.

### Seasonal Experiences

Celebrate life’s moments in style with seasonal experiences at Paris Bar and Le Jardin. From Valentine’s Day and Easter Brunch to Thanksgiving Dinner and New Year’s Eve, enjoy curated menus, festive pre-fixe packages, and inspired ambiance that make every holiday at Le Méridien New York, Central Park truly special.

In-Room Dining

## Enjoy Paris Bar Favorites From the Comfort of Your Room

Savor the flavors of Paris without leaving your room. Available daily from 7am to 10:30pm, our in-room dining menu brings elevated French cuisine from our restaurant in NYC straight to your door. Start your morning with a classic breakfast from 7 to 11:30am, unwind over a leisurely lunch from 12 to 4pm, or indulge in refined French fare for dinner from 4 to 10:30pm. Whether it’s a relaxed morning or a late-night craving, dining in is always a pleasure.

Private Events & Gatherings

## Host an Elevated Event at Le Jardin or Paris Bar

From rooftop soirées to refined dinners, Le Méridien New York, Central Park offers unforgettable settings for every occasion. Celebrate above the skyline at Le Jardin Rooftop, a Parisian-inspired venue with sweeping Central Park views, or host an elegant affair within the chic ambiance of Paris Bar. Both spaces offer flexible layouts suited for gatherings of various sizes, from intimate celebrations to full buyouts. Enjoy curated food and beverage packages crafted by Paris Bar’s kitchen, along with attentive service that ensures every detail is seamless. Whether planning a corporate event, private dinner, or cocktail reception, our venues provide the perfect blend of sophistication, flavor, and New York energy.
