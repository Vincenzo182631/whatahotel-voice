### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien New York, Central Park?

The check-in time at Le Méridien New York, Central Park is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Le Méridien New York, Central Park allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien New York, Central Park. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 100.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien New York, Central Park?

Off-site valet parking is available at Le Méridien New York, Central Park. The daily valet fee is $85.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien New York, Central Park have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien New York, Central Park has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien New York, Central Park booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien New York, Central Park?

The closest airport near Le Méridien New York, Central Park is LaGuardia Airport (LGA). LGA is located approximately 8.1 Miles from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien New York, Central Park have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Le Méridien New York, Central Park does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
