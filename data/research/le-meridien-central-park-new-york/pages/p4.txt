# Your Winter in New York Starts Here

From Thanksgiving tradition to festive performances, sparkling city streets, and unforgettable holiday moments, experience the best of New York's most magical season from our Central Park South address. Stay steps from iconic celebrations, seasonal attractions, and winter adventures that define the city this time of year.

## Seasonal Traditions, Just Beyond Our Doors

With Central Park, Carnegie Hall, Fifth Avenue, Rockefeller Center, and Broadway all within walking distance, Le Méridien offers effortless access to the experiences that make winter in New York unforgettable. Discover beloved holiday traditions, hidden seasonal gems, and new reasons to explore throughout the season.

## The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade

Celebrate Thanksgiving in the heart of Manhattan with convenient access to one of New York City's most beloved holiday traditions. After enjoying the excitement of the parade, continue the celebration at Le Méridien with seasonal dining experiences, including a Thanksgiving prix fixe dinner at Paris Bar and a holiday buffet at Le Jardin Rooftop, where festive menus are paired with skyline views above Central Park South.

## Ice Skating at Wollman Rink

Experience winter in the heart of Central Park at Wollman Rink. Surrounded by skyline views and the park's seasonal beauty, it's one of the city's most memorable cold-weather traditions for visitors of all ages.

## The Rockettes Christmas Spectacular

A New York holiday staple, the Christmas Spectacular at Radio City Music Hall brings dazzling choreography, classic music, and festive storytelling together in an unforgettable seasonal performance.

## Fifth Avenue Holiday Displays

More than a shopping destination, Fifth Avenue becomes an open-air gallery during the holidays. Stroll past legendary storefronts adorned with elaborate window displays, seasonal décor, and festive lights that draw visitors from around the world.

## Winter at Bryant Park

Discover one of Manhattan's most beloved winter destinations. Browse the Holiday Shops, skate beneath the city skyline, and warm up with seasonal treats at the Winter Village, all within a short walk of the hotel.

## Holiday Shopping at Columbus Circle

Find unique gifts and seasonal finds at The Shops at Columbus Circle, where luxury retailers, curated boutiques, and festive décor create a distinctly New York holiday shopping experience.

## Broadway This Winter

From long-running classics to the season's newest productions, Broadway shines throughout the winter months. Spend an evening immersed in world-class theater before returning to the comfort of Central Park South.

## Holiday Performances at Carnegie Hall

Celebrate the sounds of the season with renowned orchestras, celebrated artists, and festive concerts at Carnegie Hall. Located just moments away, it's one of New York's most treasured cultural experiences.

## Horse Drawn Carriage Rides

See Central Park from a timeless perspective aboard a horse-drawn carriage. Cozy blankets, scenic park views, and the city's winter atmosphere make for a quintessential New York experience.

## Rockefeller Center & The Tree

No holiday visit is complete without seeing Rockefeller Center's famous Christmas Tree. Pair the experience with ice skating, Top of the Rock views, and some of the city's most iconic seasonal scenery.

## New Year’s Eve at Le Meridien

Ring in the new year from the heart of Manhattan. Enjoy festive dining, seasonal cocktails, and celebratory experiences before stepping out to experience the energy of New York on one of its most exciting nights.
