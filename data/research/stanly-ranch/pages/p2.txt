##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is Stanly Ranch located?** Stanly Ranch is located at 200 Stanly Crossroad in Napa, California, on the southern edge of Napa Valley near the Napa River.

**How do I get to Stanly Ranch from the nearest airport?** Guests should choose the Bay Area airport that best matches their itinerary. Napa County Airport (APC) is 6 miles to the resort and approximately a 10 minute drive. Oakland International Airport (OAK) is 49 miles to the resort and approximately a 1 hour drive. Sacramento International Airport (SAC) is 61 miles to the resort and approximately a 1 hour and 10 minute drive. San Francisco Airport (SFO) is 87 miles to the resort and approximately a 1 hour and 20 minute drive.

**Is Stanly Ranch a good fit for couples?** Stanly Ranch is a strong fit for couples seeking a Napa Valley hotel with intimate dining, spa and wellbeing treatments, vineyard settings and wine country experiences.

**Is Stanly Ranch pet-friendly?** Yes, Stanly Ranch is a dog-friendly resort. To confirm eligible pets, fees and restrictions, please contact Stanly Ranch.

**When is the best time to visit Stanly Ranch?** Napa Valley is a year-round destination, with spring and fall popular for vineyard scenery and harvest activity; the best time for Stanly Ranch depends on preferred weather, wine events and availability.

**Does Stanly Ranch have a pool?** Yes. Stanly Ranch has three mountain-view pools, including a family pool and the spa pool at Halehouse.

**What is the wellbeing experience at Stanly Ranch?** Wellbeing at Stanly Ranch centers on Halehouse, Springhouse and Fieldhouse, with treatments and programs focused on sleep, hydration, movement, mindfulness, recovery and individualized goals.
