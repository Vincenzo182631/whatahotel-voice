##### Art Through Adan Weaving

The native Adan tree is a crucial material for islanders, who for generations have used it to weave tools and baskets. Try your hand at adan weaving while preserving this local craft.

##### Rare Encounters

Miyakojima’s native horses are a beloved symbol of the island, but fewer than 50 remain in the world. Join our efforts to ensure their survival as you connect with the land's natural history.

##### At the Edge of Miyako Blue

Step from the beach in front of the resort and into Miyako Blue.

SUP, kayaking, and snorkeling invite you to experience the sea at your own pace.
