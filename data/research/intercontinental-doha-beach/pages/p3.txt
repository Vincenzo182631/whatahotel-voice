### Club InterContinental Lounge

Available exclusively to guests staying in one of our Club rooms and suites, Club InterContinental offers complimentary breakfast, light refreshments, afternoon tea, and evening cocktails. Overlooking the stunning Doha skyline, the Club InterContinental Lounge is the perfect place to unwind after a long day.
