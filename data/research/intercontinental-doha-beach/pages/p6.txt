### Club InterContinental Lounge

Available exclusively to guests staying in one of our Club rooms and suites, Club InterContinental offers complimentary breakfast, light refreshments, afternoon tea, and evening cocktails. Overlooking the stunning Doha skyline, the Club InterContinental Lounge is the perfect place to unwind after a long day.

At InterContinental Doha Beach & Spa, we provide more than just an ideal meeting location. Listen to the sounds of the waves as you enjoy an outdoor meeting, specially curated for your team. Experience a unique meeting location which creates lasting memories for you and your team.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Doha Beach & Spa?

Check-in at InterContinental Doha Beach & Spa is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Doha Beach & Spa offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Doha Beach & Spa offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Doha Beach & Spa from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Doha Beach & Spa is conveniently located near Hamad International Airport (DOH), approximately 12.43 miles away (20 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Doha Beach & Spa?

InterContinental Doha Beach & Spa is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Doha Golf Club - 3.11 miles away (5 kilometers)
- Guided City Tour (3 hours)
- The Bay Club
- The InterContinental Doha Beach
- Doha Corniche - 4.35 miles away (7 kilometers)
- The Pearl - a walk at the Porto Arabia - 4.35 miles away (7 kilometers)
- North Forts: Al Thagab and Al Rekayat - 74.56 miles away (120 kilometers)
- Camel Race - Seasonal - 37.28 miles away (60 kilometers)
- Khor Al Adaid - 37.28 miles away (60 kilometers)
- Mesaieed Beach - 24.85 miles away (40 kilometers)
- Museum of Islamic Art - 4.35 miles away (7 kilometers)
- Pearl Diving
- Sheikh Faisal Bin Qassim Al Thani Museum - 12.43 miles away (20 kilometers)
- Katara Cultural Village - 0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Doha Beach & Spa puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
