# InterContinental Doha Beach & SpaDestination

Explore all Doha has to offer. Our prime location is just minutes from Lusail, downtown Doha, the Doha Exhibition and Convention Centre, City Center Mall, and Doha Golf Club. The Al Qassar Metro Station is also a short walk away.

## Things to do

### Doha Golf Club

Doha Golf Club, West Bay, Doha 13530

Doha Golf Club boasts an 18 hole Championship Course, a 9 hole Academy Course, an enormous driving range and putting green, eight artificial lakes, graceful landscaping and a beautiful clubhouse designed in old traditional Arabic style located at one of the highest points on the course, giving it a magnificent prominence. Doha Golf Club is home to the Qatar Masters - co-sanctioned by the European and Asian Tour. The course is a par 72 world class test of skill.

Learn more

### Guided City Tour (3 hours)

Experience the magnificent city of Doha and what it has to offer by visiting its traditional landmarks. Our guided city tour will cover the stunning Doha Corniche, Katara Cultural Village, Lusail City, Souq Waqif (old souq), Msheireb Museum, Camel Racing (seasonal), The Pearl Qatar (Marina Walk), Museum of Islamic Art, Qatar National Museum (opening soon), Al Bidda Park, Gold Souq, and Al Wakra Souq. Our Concierge team are happy to assist you in booking your guided city tour.

### The Bay Club

The Bay Club offers a sanctuary in the middle of Doha with a variety of health and leisure facilities ranging from the longest private beach, outdoor pool, children's pool and playground, health club, tennis courts, and Spa InterContinental. Top up your tan in our private beach or join adrenaline-pumping group exercises in our newly renovated fitness studio. You can also enjoy some exciting activities with our Water Sports activities by Blue Marine. Approach our Bay Club reception for more info.

### The InterContinental Doha Beach

Relax on the beach and enjoy a refreshing cocktail as the sun sets. Top up some tan by the beach or enjoy our adrenaline-pumping water sport activities. Over the summer, the temperature spikes up so we recommend spending time by our chilled pool.

### Doha Corniche

The Corniche is a beautiful place to relax as you walk, jog, or rollerblade and take in the sights of the stunning Doha skyline. Avoid going on weekends as it is very crowded and in the summer or winter when temperatures can become extreme. You can also choose to take a dhow ride for approximately QR 10 (USD 3) per person. Approach our Concierge desk for further information on the Doha Corniche.

### The Pearl - a walk at the Porto Arabia

Experience Doha’s Beverly Hills at The Pearl, an artificial island. You can find the best branded clothing and shops here. Overlooking the luxurious yacht while having a cup of tea. Every Friday, some shops are open, but in the evening, all shops are open.

Learn more

### North Forts: Al Thagab and Al Rekayat

Northern Qatar was a much sought-after location for nomadic people looking to settle down. Vestiges of early fishermen's villages, archaeological areas and magnifident forts can still be found throughout the area. Two of the most historically significant forts, "Al Thagab and "Al Rekayat stand just north of the well known Al Zubarah fort.

Learn more

### Camel Race - Seasonal

A unique opportunity to observe the traditional sport of camel racing, a local seasonal attraction. Visits only take place in the morning and can be arranged in advance through the concierge.

### Khor Al Adaid

Also known as the Inland Sea, an area of breathtaking beauty at the country's southernmost point. Accessible only by 4x4 vehicles. The backdrop of towering crescent-shaped sand dunes rising up from the water's edge is a magnificent sight.

### Mesaieed Beach

The country's most beautiful beaches are in this area, where the famous arc-shaped barchan sand dunes begin.

### Museum of Islamic Art

This museum displays a world-class collection of Islamic arts, dating back to the eighth century.

Learn more

### Pearl Diving

Get a glimpse of the pearl divers' way of life at a heritage village.

### Sheikh Faisal Bin Qassim Al Thani Museum

Sheikh Faisal welcomes small groups of visitors to view his collection of 20th-century artefacts, weapons, Bedouin jewellery, traditional doors, coins, manuscripts, fossils and books. Please arrange your visit through the concierge.

Learn more

### Katara Cultural Village

Katara reflects the heritage of Qatar through its traditional architecture including an amphitheatre, art and exhibitions. It also features different culinary outlets and an magnificant view over the Arabian Sea.

Learn more
