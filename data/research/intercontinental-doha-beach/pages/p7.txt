### 5 stars all the way

11 days ago

What an absolute delight it was to stay at the flagship InterContinental Hotel in Doha. This five-star property truly delivered on every aspect, providing an outstanding experience from beginning to end.

From the moment I arrived, I received a warm and friendly welcome at check-in and was delighted to be offered an upgrade to a room with spectacular views overlooking the pool and beach. It was a wonderful start to my stay and immediately set the tone for what proved to be a memorable experience.

The room itself was clean, spacious and comfortable, with all the necessary amenities thoughtfully provided. It was a real relief to find everything I needed already in place, allowing me to settle in and enjoy my stay without any concerns.

Another lovely touch was being offered a QAR 75 voucher to spend at one of the hotel's restaurants. This thoughtful gesture added extra value to the experience and made me feel even more welcome.

I was particularly impressed with the team at Coral Restaurant. Having informed the team of my gluten allergy during my stay, I was delighted with how well they accommodated my dietary requirements. Gluten-free options were arranged for me, allowing me to enjoy my meals with greater peace of mind and making the dining experience all the more enjoyable. The team's attentiveness and willingness to cater to my needs were greatly appreciated. It is this level of personal care and consideration that truly sets a five-star hotel apart and makes guests feel valued.

The housekeeping team also deserves special recognition. Due to my religious beliefs, having access to a bidet was important to me. As the room did not initially have one, I was genuinely grateful when the morning housekeeping team arranged to provide one. This thoughtful gesture made my stay considerably more comfortable and satisfying. It demonstrated a genuine willingness to accommodate my personal needs, something I greatly appreciated.

Another wonderful touch was the evening visits from Nagendra, who took the time to check that I had everything I needed and whether anything in the room required housekeeping attention. This proactive approach to guest care was a particularly welcome surprise. It is such a simple yet thoughtful gesture, but one that makes a real difference to the overall experience. In my experience, this level of personal attention is rare, and it really set the hotel apart from others I have stayed in. Nagendra deserves recognition for going the extra mile to ensure guests are comfortable and well looked after.

A special shout-out to Shanny and Paulina, who greeted me with a warm smile every morning and consistently made sure I was well looked after. Their genuine warmth, attentiveness and outstanding service made me feel truly valued as a guest. The VIP treatment was very much appreciated and added a personal touch that made my stay even more special. They are both a real credit to the hotel and absolutely deserve recognition for their exceptional hospitality.
From the beautiful beachfront setting and luxurious surroundings to the attentive staff and thoughtful gestures, InterContinental Doha delivered on every level. What impressed me most was not simply the five-star facilities, but the people who went out of their way to make the experience personal. Whether it was accommodating my dietary and religious requirements, providing additional room amenities, checking in each evening or greeting me with a smile every morning, the team consistently demonstrated what genuine hospitality should look like.

I thoroughly enjoyed my stay and would highly recommend InterContinental Doha to anyone visiting Qatar. I would happily return, and I sincerely hope Shanny, Paulina, Nagendra, the housekeeping team and everyone who contributed to my experience receive the recognition they deserve.

A truly memorable stay where five-star luxury was matched by five-star service. I look forward to returning!
