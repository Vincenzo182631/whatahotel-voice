Step into a world where lively vibes and endless fun come together. Often praised by guests for the rooftop pool and central location, our hotel near Amsterdam Central Station, W Amsterdam, keeps you close to the action while making it easy to recharge. ### Stay Beside Amsterdam’s Dam Square

Where historical Dutch architecture and cutting-edge creative design meet and meld. W Amsterdam sits at the epicenter of this energetic, powerfully progressive city. Marvel at the dazzling views from our home between the Royal Palace and famed Singel Canal. Explore top local landmarks like the Rijksmuseum and Anne Frank House a few steps away.

Wet Area Access for In-House Guests

Please know that if you are a guest staying in the hotel, the duration of the access to the Wet® Area is a maximum of 2 hours and please reserve this prior to you visit to the AWAY® Spa.

Wet Area Access for Non-resident Guests

The Bank that became a Spa. Step into the Vault and experience an oasis of Amsterdam luxury while enjoying the best spa facilities of the city.

The following AWAY® Spa facilities are available to non-resident guests booking a treatment lasting for a minimum of 2 hours: sauna, steam room, Fire bath, cold plunge pool, whirlpool, spa changing and relaxation areas.

Due to obvious time constraints, non-resident guests who have reserved a treatment of 2 hours or less, will be escorted directly to and from their treatment room and do not have access to WET® area, heat experience, pool or FIT®.

Children's Policy

Access to AWAY® Spa, including the changing facilities, WET area and FIT® are restricted to over 16’s only.
