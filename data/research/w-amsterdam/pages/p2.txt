### W Amsterdam Bank

Check-in to the former KAS Bank to enjoy ease in the city of Amsterdam, built in 1906. W Bank is one of two iconic buildings hosted by W Amsterdam and primarily located in the heart of the Dutch capital.

Over 100 Years of Heritage, Preserved

## Spend the Night, Save the Memory

Located in a former historic bank, W BANK blends heritage with modern luxury. Rooms and suites reflect the building's rich past and the spirit of the city, with views over canals or skyline. Enjoy fine dining at THE DUTCHESS, discover Dutch design at X-BANK, or unwind at the award-winning AWAY spa.

## W Bank Suites

Our 13 spacious suites provide an exceptional retreat in the city center. With elegant golden details and modern design, each suite offers a harmonious blend of comfort and style, creating a serene environment with stunning views and a lasting sense of luxury.

## Urban Escape

W Exchange pulses with culture and style. From creatively designed suites to intimate rooms with Royal Palace views, every detail inspires. Dine at MR PORTER, dance the night away at the W Lounge, or unwind at the rooftop WET Deck pool with stunning city views.

## W Exchange, A Creative Lifestyle

Cultivate culture at W Exchange where tradition is turned upside-down. Step inside the W Amsterdam Exchange building and rise to the rooftop, W Lounge. Experiences here come in the form of innovative design and large style-driven guest rooms. Wake up to a renewed Amsterdam where art, design and sounds fuel connections that create an extraordinary dynamic in this city of canals.

### W Insider

Get the clothes to steal the show at one of the coolest concept stores in town, X BANK. Since opening its doors in 2015, X BANK has been a destination for the best in Dutch art, fashion and design, and a meeting place for the local creative community. They display high-end established labels alongside emerging labels, making it an invaluable platform for new Dutch talent.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Amsterdam have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Amsterdam have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Amsterdam?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 3 at W Amsterdam. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Amsterdam?

Some rooms at W Amsterdam permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Amsterdam have fridges?

No, rooms at W Amsterdam do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W Amsterdam have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Amsterdam have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Amsterdam have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Amsterdam have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Amsterdam have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Amsterdam have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
