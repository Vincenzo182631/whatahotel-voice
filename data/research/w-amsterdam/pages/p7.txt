How did it strike you on arrival?
The W spans two buildings that face off across Spuistraat. The grander structure is a former bank, while an elevator inside the former telephone exchange whisks you to the clubby reception area.
Nice. What’s the crowd like?
Club-going jet-setters.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
The decor is determined by the building. Those inside the bank building are outfitted in warm tones and gold accents, while the rooms inside the former telephone exchange leans almost futuristic, with gray and bright red color schemes, shiny metallic accents, modern furnishings, and open bathroom plans.
How about the little things, like mini bar, shower goodies, and other amenities?
All rooms come with the Bliss toiletries available at all W locations. Some of the rooms inside the bank building have gold minibars in the shape of small bank vault, complete with copper-colored cocktail shakers.
Room service and food: Worth it?
The on-site restaurant, housed inside the bank building, lays claim to a rooftop lounge. It was recently awarded one Michelin star, serves French-Italian cuisine like delicate broccoli and truffle gnocchi, or succulent grilled lamb cutlets with figs.
Anything stand out about other services and features? Whatever stuck with you.
The slick rooftop lounge has a wood-burning fireplace. Come summer, guests head to the slim rooftop pool that runs the length of the building and overlooks Dam Square.
Bottom line: worth it, and why?
The central location right next to Dam Square is unbeatable. It's also a modern sanctuary within the nearly perfectly preserved 17th century city that is Amsterdam.
