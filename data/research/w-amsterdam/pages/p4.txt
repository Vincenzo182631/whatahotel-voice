### Cocktails with a Touch of History

Make an entrance in our Exchange building, the former Royal Dutch Post's communication center of Amsterdam. For over 100 years it has been thew center of connecting. Bold gold lines printed on the windows of the W Lounge and over 5km of slim copper pipes pay tribute to the telephone wires and electricity lines that once exchanged information on a daily basis in this very building. Since 2016, it became a stunning Amsterdam rooftop cocktail bar for those most interested in what's new and next in the world of mixology. Events hosted in this space are unmissable and
welcome all.
