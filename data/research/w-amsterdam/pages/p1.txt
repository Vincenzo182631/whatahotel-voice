# Welcome to W Amsterdam

## Discover the city from our luxury Spa hotel in Amsterdam

A stay at W Amsterdam is immersive. Often praised by guests for its central location and stylish design, with landmarks, bars, restaurants, and shops within walking distance, our spa hotel in Amsterdam channels the city's cool, creative spirit across two landmark buildings: a former telephone exchange and a bank. Historic Dutch architecture meets avant-garde design, with Dam Square and the Royal Palace...See More nearby, making it a standout choice for hotels in central Amsterdam. If you're seeking hotels near the train station, we're a prime choice. Our Amsterdam 5-star hotel offers 238 stylish guest rooms and premium suites, plus our Whatever/Whenever service, W Insiders, and W Happenings for insider access to Amsterdam nightlife, dining, shopping, and fashion. Dine at THE DUCHESS and MR PORTER, sip cocktails at rooftop W Lounge overlooking the Royal Palace, and unwind at WET Deck, our rooftop pool with city views. A unique blend of heritage and contemporary style awaits at our luxury canal hotel in Amsterdam.See Less

### Two Sides, One Scene

Twice the character, right in the city’s heart. W Amsterdam spans two iconic heritage buildings — the former KAS Bank and the Royal Dutch Post’s communications center — just steps from Dam Square. Here, the city’s rich history and vibrant energy converge. Whether you choose classic elegance or industrial edge, each space immerses you in Amsterdam’s creative core. More than a place to stay, it’s your gateway to the city’s past, present, and pulse.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Amsterdam?

The check-in time at W Amsterdam is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Amsterdam allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Amsterdam. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Amsterdam?

On-site valet parking is available at W Amsterdam. The daily valet fee is €95.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Amsterdam have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Amsterdam has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Amsterdam booking.

What is the closest airport near W Amsterdam?

The closest airport near W Amsterdam is Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS). AMS is located approximately 16.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Amsterdam have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Amsterdam does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
