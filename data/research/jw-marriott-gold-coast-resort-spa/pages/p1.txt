# Welcome to JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa

## Enjoy your family vacation at our Gold Coast resort

Unwind at a 5-star resort on the Gold Coast in the town of Surfers Paradise. JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa boasts luxurious rooms and Spa by JW, award-winning dining, and a covetable hotel location on Ferny Avenue. Attend events at Gold Coast Convention Centre, treat the family to Sea World Gold Coast, and stroll the beautiful beaches of Surfers Paradise. Our hotel rooms offer an ideal setting...See More to relax, complete with high-speed Wi-Fi, plush bedding, and marble bathrooms. Wake up feeling refreshed and ready for adventure in Surfers Paradise. This luxury resort on the Gold Coast offers a tropical saltwater lagoon, an outdoor freshwater pool with a slide, a fitness centre, and a Sports Court®. One-of-a-kind experiences at our hotel include Family by JW culinary cooking classes, JW Garden herb planting and guided tours, daily fish feeding, snorkelling in the saltwater lagoon, and much more. Leave your family vacation to Surfers Paradise richer than when you arrived at our spa resort on the Gold Coast.See Less

## Spa by JW

Welcome to Spa by JW, an experience that favours simplicity over complexity and clarity over mystery. Knowing your time is valuable, we offer express spa treatments as a simple indulgence. The inviting lounge at our spa resort on the Gold Coast is the perfect place to gather with a group or simply unwind and take a moment. Spa by JW delivers experiential retail and treatments that are tailored to your exact needs and focused on delivering four distinctive benefits — Calm, Indulge, Invigorate, and Renew. Whether you’re fatigued from a long journey or simply desire a well-deserved retreat, we provide a personalised spa experience that is as effortless as it is rewarding. Begin your journey with your desired results in mind.

## Saltwater Lagoon with Waterfall and Tropical Fish

Awarded Australia’s Best Hotel Pool 2021 and 2024, our Gold Coast resort's pools are designed with multi-generational travellers in mind. Pools of varying depths and activations including the white sandy beaches, swim-up grotto, and waterfalls unique to the saltwater lagoon, streaming with tropical fish and the fresh water pool with children’s slide. The water sports kiosk provides for-hire snorkels, flippers, and motorized aquatic scooters. Or relax and unwind in one of the Jacuzzis or poolside cabanas.

## JW Garden

The JW Garden is our on-property farm-to-table program creating a sustainable way for our chefs to elevate the resort menus with seasonal herbs, edible flowers, fruits, and vegetables. It honours a sense of environmental responsibility and encourages a better relationship with food. Experience the herb and vegetable harvesting at the JW Garden daily, or enjoy handcrafted beverages that deliver unique health benefits made using ingredients grown in the JW Garden. Delight your taste buds with a selection of dishes made using ingredients grown in the JW Garden, available in all our restaurants.

## Award Winning Design

Award winning Australian design studio, DBI, the original architects and interior designers behind the existing property, were engaged to reimagine the iconic resort and to elevate the property to JW Marriott’s high standards of modern luxury, drawing inspiration from the Gold Coast Hinterland. Originally built in 1993, JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa has become an iconic Gold Coast, Australia, resort hosting multiple renowned events and countless domestic and international guests.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa, with rates of $5.00 per hour, and $30.00 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa. The daily valet fee is $50.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa is Gold Coast Airport (OOL). OOL is located approximately 22.8 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
