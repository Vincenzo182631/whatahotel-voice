## Dining

The cuisine choices for guests are wide and varied across our five Gold Coast hotel restaurants and bars, making the perfect location for celebrating special occasions and creating joyful memories. Citrique’s award-winning dining menu is designed using local fresh produce for the ultimate ocean and paddock-to-plate dining, while Misono brings the unique art of Japanese teppanyaki, sushi maki, and whisky....See More Enjoy crafted cocktails and award-winning high tea in Chapter & Verse or relax poolside with vibrant street food and beverages from the Pool Pavilion. ## Executive Chef, Paul Smart

Leading the JW culinary team, Executive Chef Paul Smart’s culinary expertise, creativity, and connections help bring the resort's vision in the kitchen and across our six Gold Coast hotel restaurants. His international reputation in luxury venues, together with his commitment to sourcing the freshest local produce ensured we deliver something truly exceptional and like nothing else available along this stretch of the Gold Coast. Paul Smart joined JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort & Spa following two decades working for well-established and respected restaurants across the globe.
