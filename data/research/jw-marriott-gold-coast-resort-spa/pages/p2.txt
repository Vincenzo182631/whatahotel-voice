### Luxury Executive Suite

After a busy day exploring the Gold Coast, sink into one of the comfortable beds in your Executive Suite, featuring panoramic views of The Gold Coast Hinterland and Pacific Ocean. Located on the resort's top floors, the 93 square meter Executive Suites, boast a plush king bed, a separate lounge room, floor-to-ceiling windows, a dining table, flat-screen TV, and chrome cast, the perfect location to relax and unwind. With options for interconnecting double rooms, this accommodation on the Gold Coast is ideal for the perfect family holiday.

### A Getaway in Comfort

## Ocean King

Located on the northern shores of Surfers Paradise, this room frames views towards the Pacific Ocean. From your private balcony, sweeping ocean outlooks and views of the resort’s signature Lagoon Pool capture the coastal beauty of the destination.

## Hinterland King Room

Located on the northern shores of Surfers Paradise, this accommodation frames views on the Gold Coast Hinterland. Beyond the coastline, the region’s lush mountain ranges, greenery and sweeping river outlooks for a calmer perspective of the destination.
