## Currumbin Wildlife Hospital

As corporate sponsors of Currumbin Wildlife Hospital, the hospital team is delighted to offer JW Marriott Gold Coast guests a unique 10% discount on the CURRUMBIN WILDLIFE HOSPITAL VIP tour. Located on the grounds of Currumbin Wildlife Sanctuary, the Sanctuary was gifted to the National Trust of Australia (Queensland) in 1976. Currumbin Wildlife Hospital first opened its doors in 1989, when founder Alex Griffiths hired the first vet. Now one of the busiest wildlife hospitals in the world, Currumbin Wildlife Hospital’s mission is to treat, rehabilitate, and release sick, injured, and orphaned native wildlife. This service is provided free of charge to the community. Currumbin Wildlife Hospital relies on community donations to continue its vital work. It’s now one of the busiest wildlife hospitals in the world, treating more than 14,000 animals every year. Please ask our reservations team for further information when making your accommodation reservation.

## HOTA, Home of the Arts

In tropical parklands with the Surfers Paradise skyline as the backdrop, HOTA, Home of the Arts is a contemporary cultural precinct in the heart of the Gold Coast. HOTA is the Gold Coast’s home of live performances, art, lakeside strolls, stargazing, cinema-watching, new ideas, and culinary delights. JW Marriott guests can explore six centuries of world-famous portraits and rare manuscripts from literary legends in 'Writers Revealed' unprecedented exhibition. Offering a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to come face-to-face with some of the most well-known figures in English literature, the exhibition will feature literary giants such as William Shakespeare, Jane Austen, the Brontë sisters, William Blake, Virginia Woolf, Sir Arthur Conan Doyle, J.R.R. Tolkien, Lewis Carroll, Sir Kazuo Ishiguro and Zadie Smith.

Book Now

## Witches Falls Winery

Witches Falls Winery, nestled in Tamborine Mountain, South-East Queensland, is a must-visit for wine lovers. This family-owned winery offers exceptional quality wines in a welcoming, relaxed atmosphere. Surrounded by lush gardens and rainforests, Witches Falls offers a unique and authentic wine-tasting experience with friendly, knowledgeable, and passionate staff. Visitors to the winery can spend an afternoon tasting the diverse range of wines, soaking up the sunshine, and grazing on locally sourced gourmet produce. The intimate cellar door is the perfect place to relax and unwind while sipping on an outstanding collection of wines. The Witches Falls Cellar Door is open daily from 10 am and bookings are essential.

Learn More

## Beachcomber Charters

Beachcomber Charters offers exclusive guest transfers from the private marina located at JW Marriott. Let our crew take you on our 30-foot vessel ‘Beachcomber’ to explore the waterways of the Gold Coast. Our vessel offers full shade, a marine toilet on board, options for beach landings via our front ladder, a large ice-filled esky stocked with drinking water and soft drinks, and comfortable seating. ‘Beachcomber’ is perfect for groups of up to 12 guests to enjoy sightseeing, lunch on board, island picnic lunches, small group whale-watching (seasonal) or even catching some fish in the sheltered waters of the Gold Coast Broadwater. Our crew is happy to point out the local wildlife of dolphins, dugongs, and many bird species. Let ‘Beachcomber Charters collect you from the JW Marriott’s Private Marina for your choice of tour.
