# The Rooms & Suites

It's the spirit of a place where you immediately feel at home, where classic meets modern, and where attention to detail prevails over superfluous décor. Spacious, calm and luminous, the 47 rooms and suites are a haven of peace, offering a typically Parisian atmosphere. They are in tune with the surprises of the architecture. The layout evolves according to the volumes. The charm is undeniable. The Classique room transports you into a typically Parisian world, which extends to the Superior rooms, as well as the Deluxe, Junior Suites and Suites.

Comfort is omnipresent, palpable. The color scheme is soft and easy to take in. The view of the district's facades and rooftops gives you the confused feeling of owning and belonging to Paris. Here you can taste the salt of the city.
