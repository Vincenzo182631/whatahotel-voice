# Get away from it all!    Discover a place dedicated   to relaxation and well-being

‍

‍

Experience the ultimate in well-being and forget the hustle and bustle of the city. This is the promise of the Spa des Prés by Codage.

In the 20th century, the vaulted cellars of Pavillon Faubourg Saint-Germain were home to the Quod Libet, a mythical cabaret where Léo Ferré, among others, began his career.

The steps leading to this former temple of song are now classified as a historical monument, while its old stones now surround a bubble of serenity.

The magic of water and silence, the play of light on a blurred mirror undulating like the surface of the water, the atmosphere isolates the world for a journey of the senses.

‍

Our Spa includes :

- a deep pool with water jets
- a fitness room
- a meditation and yoga room

- two massage cabins
- a hammam

‍

The Spa des Près is open every day from 7:30 am to 9 pm.

For any request or reservation, please contact us at 01 42 61 01 51 or by mail : concierge@pfsg.fr
