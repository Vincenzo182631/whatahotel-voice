### Rive Gauche, the rebirth of a 5-star boutique hotel

# Pavillon Faubourg   Saint-Germain

Such quintessentially Parisian details. A café that feels like a literary salon. A chic and lively dining experience. Neighborhood life, between the Seine and the Café de Flore. A must-visit destination: the Pavillon Faubourg Saint-Germain. Haute couture decor. Personalized service. And the privilege of a spa where you can gently escape the hustle and bustle. Paris without compromise. Paris as the only promise.

‍

This is the promise of a new “Germanopratin” hotspot. Between the Seine and the Café de Flore, the “ Pavillon Faubourg Saint-Germain ” opened in April 2022. A venue offering three distinct experiences: at number 1, Les Parisiens, a chic bistro led by Chef Thibault Sombardier; at number 3, Le Bar James Joyce; and at number 5, the luxury couture hotel & spa.
