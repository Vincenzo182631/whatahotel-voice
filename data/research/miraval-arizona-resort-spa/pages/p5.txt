Miraval Arizona Resort & Spa
A screen-free wellness resort amidst the Santa Catalina Mountains with a myriad of classes and spa offerings.

Why book?
While the majority of Miraval is a screen-free zone, it’s hard to miss your phone when your days are filled with a myriad of classes, well-being activities, and stops at the spa.
Set the scene
The resort is spread across 400 acres of Sonoran Desert, stretching against the Santa Catalina mountains just north of Tucson. On-site, guests stroll around in robes while sipping smoothies, perhaps heading to a meditation class or an organic facial. What you won’t see are faces buried in phones, thumbs scrolling social media, or FaceTime calls on speakers; Miraval welcomes everyone checking in with a linen sleeping bag for storing digital devices, and the majority of the property is set up as a screen-free zone. The crowd skews toward women on girls trips or solo getaways, but there’s plenty here to keep anyone busy while caring for their physical, spiritual, and mental health.
It’s not unusual to see people sitting outside journaling or in quiet meditation. For something more active, there's the confidence-boosting Challenge Course, and mountain bikers and hikers are free to use the desert paths. In the distance, you can see the ranch that’s home to Miraval’s signature equine experiences. These are the type of activities that stay with you long after checkout. There is so much to see and do that it’s impossible to cram it all in. And yet that’s also the point; you can’t do it all, so do what speaks to you, even if that’s nothing other than savoring the phone-free silence.
The backstory
While now part of the Hyatt family, Miraval’s wellness-focused ethos began in the early ‘80s, when Bill O’Donnell Jr. purchased the former ranch. Having gone through his own struggles, he turned the property into a residential addiction treatment facility called Sierra Tucson, which quickly became one of the leading centers in the country. It was known for its pioneering approach to equine facilitated psychotherapy, among other things. After Sierra Tucson downsized and relocated in the mid-‘90s, O’Donnell decided to branch out beyond addiction, and thus Miraval was created. The resort celebrated 30 years in 2025.
The rooms
Accommodation options range from a standard room to multi-bedroom villas for the ultimate indulgence. For something a little more special than a suite but not quite as large as a villa, look at booking one of the Retreats. With at least 600 square feet of space, these come with a spacious indoor/outdoor shower along with a patio where a bubbling, private in-ground hot tub awaits. There’s really nothing quite like indulging in a soak during the sunrise or under the star-studded desert sky near a flickering outdoor fireplace. Inside, rooms boast soothing, neutral colors and unique touches like the easel and markers for coloring a mandala, or the singing bowl to help foster inner peace in the privacy of your quarters. Perhaps most important of all is the bed, which makes it impossible not to get a good night’s sleep.
Food and drink
With the exception of alcohol and zero-proof cocktails, all food and drinks are included in the nightly rate. In the Cactus Flower restaurant, breakfast and lunch are served buffet-style with a small selection of set items; dinner, however, requires a reservation and includes à la carte menu items that change seasonally. It’s easy to tailor meals to any nutritional needs while also not feeling deprived. At the end of the day, Miraval is all about a life in balance, so make sure to pay attention to the extensive dessert menu too.
There are a couple of other dining options, including the Copper Moon Bar & Lounge, ideal for an evening drink, and a small café that serves smoothies, sandwiches, and snacks. The poolside bar also has a fun cocktail list, along with a couple of munchies like frozen grapes and trail mix. Note, this isn’t a place where you can call room service for a burger at 2 a.m., so if you’re a snacker, pick up some items from the café during the day and stash them in your room's fridge for later.
The spa
Miraval’s Life in Balance Spa is one of the key reasons to book a stay at the property. While there are numerous other ways to fill the day besides spa treatments, the resort prides itself on offering a wide range of services that go beyond the typical Swedish massage.
Recently renovated, the spa has a soothing and minimalist design that provides ample indoor and outdoor space to relax in pre- and post-treatment, including a small library (you are in a digital-free zone, after all). And the treatments? Everything from a long acupuncture list to Ayurveda to water-based sessions in a private pool. Book Aqua Zen or Vasudhara to experience an in-water massage, Rasayana Shirodhara to learn about and pamper your dosha (Ayurveda energy types), and Harmonize to incorporate singing bowls to open your energy channels.
The neighborhood/area
Older homes and horse properties surround Miraval, which sits miles away from Interstate 10 and almost an hour from Tucson International Airport. There isn’t much to see past the entrance gates of the resort, so spend your time exploring Miraval’s expansive grounds instead.
The service
From the moment you step into the lobby, Miraval’s employees are there to guide you through every step of your stay, starting with a tour of the property to help you get acclimated. Experience planners are available in the lobby to help book reservations or offer suggestions; it is worth taking a few minutes to chat with them to ensure you’re not missing out on anything special.
Eco effort
Miraval operates on 100% renewable energy. Guests are given refillable water bottles to encourage hydration, and water stations are located all around the resort. Packaging for café items is biodegradable and compostable, including the straws. Food waste is gathered for compost, which is then used in the on-site garden.
Accessibility
The property is wheelchair-accessible and has several ADA-compliant room options.
Anything left to mention?
Guests are encouraged to check in as early as 11 a.m. on the day of arrival to enjoy lunch and get a jump start on all of the activities. Make sure to take advantage of that, but note that your room may not be ready until several hours later; plan ahead if you require swimsuits or hiking shoes for post-lunch fun.
