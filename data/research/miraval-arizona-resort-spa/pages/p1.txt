# Life in Balance

Your Path Begins in the Sonoran Sunshine

Welcome to Miraval Arizona, set across 400 acres of serene Sonoran Desert under nearly year-round sunshine.

Here, desert meets mountain in a landscape defined by balance and calm. Ancient cacti stand tall, flowering ocotillo and prickly pear add bursts of color, and mesquite and acacia trees offer cool shade creating a tranquil setting for reflection, renewal, and discovery.

Plan Your Miraval Journey﻿

"Here, we trade the rush for the rhythm, noise for stillness, and scrolling for presence. And in that presence, something shifts. We remember who we are, what matters, and how it feels to just be."

Mark Stebbings, General Manager
