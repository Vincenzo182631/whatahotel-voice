# Experiences

Miraval Arizona’s approach to wellness is immersive and experiential, with hundreds of possibilities to explore. And your journey will be one-of-a-kind, just like you.

We offer an extensive menu of daily workshops, classes, lectures, and activities, many of which are complimentary. Choose from hundreds of life-enriching options—from equine and outdoor adventure experiences to yoga, meditation, and breathwork.  And that’s just the beginning. What does a life in balance look like to you?

Distinctly Desert

## Experience Arizona

Miraval Arizona’s landscape is spectacular. Go hiking up Mt. Lemmon, try therapeutic drumming, spend an afternoon bonding with our equine friends at Purple Sage Ranch. And no matter, take time to experience the restorative stillness of the Sonoran Desert.
