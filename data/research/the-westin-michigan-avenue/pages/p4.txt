Hit the Trail

## Lakefront Trail

The stunning beauty of Chicago’s Lakefront Trail is just a couple of blocks from our front door. This 18-mile path is where the city meets the serene western shores of Lake Michigan. Start your day with a morning jog or go for an afternoon stroll; the trail connects the city’s four major waterfront parks – Lincoln, Grant, Burnham, and Jackson. It offers the perfect blend of urban energy and natural beauty.

Learn More

Lakeside Escape

## Oak Street Beach

Just steps from the hotel, Oak Street Beach offers a vibrant escape in the heart of the city. With its sandy shoreline, stunning lake views, and lively atmosphere, it’s the perfect spot to soak up the sun, enjoy a swim, or simply relax with the Chicago skyline as your backdrop. Whether you’re looking for a refreshing dip or a peaceful place to unwind, this iconic beach captures the best of summer in Chicago.

Learn More

### RunWestin

Start your day with an exciting run along Chicago’s stunning Lakefront Trail as part of our Westin Run Club, held every Tuesday. Just steps from our hotel, this scenic route offers breathtaking views of Lake Michigan, the city skyline, and some of Chicago’s most iconic sights. Whether you're a dedicated runner or simply looking for an active way to explore the city, our guided runs provide the perfect blend of fitness and inspiration. Lace up your sneakers and experience the energy of the Windy City with every stride. Contact the hotel for more information!

### Your Chicago Adventure Starts Here

Chicago is an exhilarating cultural center that you’ll want to immerse yourself in. Our location makes it easy. We’re steps from the Navy Pier, 3,300 feet of entertainment reaching into Lake Michigan. Shops of the Magnificent Mile and Water Tower Place are right outside our door. Dip your toes in the lake at Oak Street Beach, a few blocks north. Browse the galleries at the Museum of Contemporary Art and the Chicago History Museum. Catch a show at the scenic Theater on the Lake. We keep you energized and ready to explore it all.

Root for the Home Team

## Chicago Sports

Chicago boasts an enthusiastic and lively sports crowd, with some of the nation’s most iconic stadiums close to our hotel. Experience the excitement of cheering on the home team at Wrigley Field, home of the Chicago Cubs, just a short distance from our doorstep. In basketball, catch the jump ball for the men’s team at the United Center, and the women’s league at Wintrust Arena. Football and soccer fans can witness the action at Soldier Field and SeatGeek Stadium. Hockey enthusiasts can enjoy the puck drop at the United Center and skating showdowns at Allstate Arena.

Does The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, and Strength Equipment, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago have an on-site spa?
