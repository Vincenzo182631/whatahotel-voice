# Rooms & Suites Designed for Wellness

## Accommodations

Our spacious guestrooms and suites are designed to inspire. Light soothing palettes, splashes of color, and artwork depicting our cityscape evoke a deep sense of Chicago. Many rooms offer breathtaking...See More views of Lake Michigan while others overlook the exciting energy of Michigan Avenue. Refresh in our marble bathrooms and fall into our revitalizing take on sleep with our plush Heavenly® Bed and Sleep Well Lavender Balm. Suites offer more space, with a separate living area, while Executive Suites provide dramatic views and access to the members-only Club Lounge. For the ultimate wellness room, power up your fitness routine in one of our Peloton Suites.See Less

A Variety of Suite Options

### More Space. More Comfort.

When you need a little more room, choose one of our elegant, spacious suites. Our Studio Suite provides more floor space, ideal for a couple or a business traveler. Executive Suites feature a separate private bedroom and a living area for family travel or business travelers who might be spending more time in the room. Elevate your stay to our highest level in our Crown Suite, where generous living and dining areas and a separate bedroom provide the ultimate comfort.

We Bring the City Into Your Room

## Feel the Essence of Chicago

Our rooms and suites embody the spirit of Chicago and our spectacular location. Sand-hued palettes echo the beaches along Lake Michigan while splashes of blue reflect the lake itself. Colorful artwork portrays the iconic sights of the Windy City, be it the famous “L” train or the tree-dotted cityscape. The aesthetic seems pulled from the Design Museum of Chicago – modern lamps, fashionable carpets, and a bean-shaped sofa inspired by the famous mirrored sculpture in Millennium Park, Cloud Gate.

Your Window on Chicago

## Every Room With a View

Our views surprise and inspire. Many of our generously appointed rooms and suites offer spectacular panoramas of Lake Michigan, the Gold Coast district, and the Oak Street and North Avenue Beaches. Other rooms overlook North Michigan Avenue and the start of the city’s famed Magnificent Mile of shops, restaurants, and entertainment. The John Hancock Building rises 100 stories across the street, where daring visitors look down at the city through glass floors from the top floor. Every room has a view at the Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago, so your stay will be truly iconic – and truly Chicago.

### Pets Welcome

Just because you’re traveling, there’s no need to part with your pooch. Dogs are as welcome as their humans at the Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago. Receive a special canine treat upon check-in and a Heavenly® Dog Bed in your room. Then share your adventure in wellness with your best four-legged friend.
