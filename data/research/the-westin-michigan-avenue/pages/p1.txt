# Welcome to The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago

## Enjoy our hotel near Lake Michigan in Chicago

The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago is a premier Michigan Ave hotel in Chicago, ideally located within walking distance of Oak Street Beach, the 900 North Michigan Shops, the 360 CHICAGO Observation Deck,...See More and the city's most iconic attractions. As a hotel near Lake Michigan in Chicago visitors love, we offer easy access to the lakefront and breathtaking views. Our modern guest rooms and spacious suites feature sweeping lake vistas, the renowned Heavenly® Bed, and relaxing bath amenities. Our Story

### Over 60 Years of Hospitality

Since we welcomed our first guest in 1963, we’ve been proud to play an integral part in our neighborhood - even before the Chicago we know today began to bloom. Over the decades, skyscrapers appeared all around us, including the John Hancock Center, rising 100 stories over Lake Michigan. In 1974, we added our 27-story addition. Though both our hotel and Chicago have changed, one thing has stayed the same over the past 60 years: our commitment to our guests and our love for this neighborhood that has embraced us throughout the years.

Perfectly Poised for Exploration

### Explore the Cultural Riches of North Michigan Avenue

Overflowing with cultural institutions and fine restaurants and shops, Chicago enjoys a stunning lakeside setting. You can discover it with ease from our prime location in the coveted Gold Coast district, at the start of the famed Magnificent Mile of shops, restaurants, and entertainment. Stroll the Lakefront Trail and admire stunning views of the skyline. Take in the carnival-like atmosphere of Navy...See More Pier, reaching 3,300 feet into Lake Michigan. Catch a Cubs baseball game at historic Wrigley Field. Find yourself in Grant Park, where recharging among the profusion of green space is as invigorating as browsing the galleries at the world-renowned Art Institute of Chicago and marveling at the marine life of the Shedd Aquarium. We keep you energized and ready to explore it all.See Less

Lake Strolls and Prime Shopping at Your Feet

### Right Where You Want to Be

Enjoy one of the most desirable locations in Chicago, right on the shore of Lake Michigan, in the coveted Gold Coast district. Here on the quiet northern end of Michigan Avenue, you’ll be on the doorstep of some of the city’s finest shopping along the Magnificent Mile, and close to the landmarks you’ve come to see. Walk the Lakefront Trail to take in the waterfront splendor from Oak Street Beach or explore the high society residences along Astor Street. Right across from our hotel, ascend to the top of John Hancock Center to experience the thrill of the 360 Chicago Observation Deck.

Your Path to Wellness

## Lakefront Trail Access at Your Doorstep

With the 18-mile-long Lakefront Trail just a couple of blocks from our door, it’s easy to keep your body moving when you stay with us, whether you’re a walker or a runner. This scenic mixed-use trail traces the shore of Lake Michigan, connecting most of the city’s beaches and the city’s four major waterfront parks: Lincoln, Grant, Burnham, and Jackson. It’s a great way to immerse yourself in the sights and sounds of Chicago and a refreshing way to stay active.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago?

The check-in time at The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago?

Off-site valet parking is available at The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago. The daily valet fee is $83.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago?

The closest airport near The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago is Chicago Midway International Airport (MDW). MDW is located approximately 13.8 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago have a Club lounge?

Yes, The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago has Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
