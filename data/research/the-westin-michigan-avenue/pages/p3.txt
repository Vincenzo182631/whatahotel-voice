## Dining

Locally sourced products. A commitment to sustainability. A prime location at the doorstep of the upscale shops of Magnificent Mile and Water Tower Place. ### 909 North

With its classic Chicago restaurant ambience of dark wood, white linens, lush artwork, and soft light, 909 North enjoys a dedicated following of local Chicagoans and visitors alike. Warm and welcoming with a touch of mid-century charm, we have the spirit of a generations-old pub, yet we’re only a decade old. CHICAGO DINING WITH A NEIGHBORHOOD TOUCH

### Delaware Place Entrance

With our street entrance on Delaware Place and lots of local foot traffic daily, 909 North has become part of the culinary fabric of Chicago. We recommend reserving a table early to find out for yourself what Chicago’s connoisseurs are talking about.

### Unique Artwork

From lush romantic landscapes to colorful and playful caricatures of Chicago’s bar scene, the artwork at 909 North portrays the best of America and the Midwest. The artist of many of our pieces is a renowned local art teacher, and some of his students gladly donated their work for our walls.

### 909 North Lounge

After a full day exploring downtown, settle in to the warm embrace of 909 North Lounge. Our intimate setting exudes a pub-like atmosphere that evokes 1920s Chicago of old. It’s the perfect end to a rewarding day, or the perfect start to an incredible meal at 909 North.

### Craft Cocktails

The talented mixologists at 909 North are shaking and stirring refreshing elixirs daily. Expertly mixed and infused with local, natural ingredients, our inventive libations are inspired by our great city.

### Daily Specials

Our chef invents new and exciting Chicago-inspired dishes daily, dishes whose creativity and unique flavor profiles we’re eager to share. You’ll find drink specials at the bar, too, cocktails that are far from ordinary.

Good for Our Environment. Good for You.

## Food Sustainability

The Westin Michigan Avenue Chicago is committed to zero-waste food preparation and to supporting other sustainability-minded producers. When you dine with us, you’re savoring both their bounty and the dishes thoughtfully prepared in our made-from-scratch kitchen. We control our ingredients, and we ensure that what goes into every dish is procured from environmentally friendly sources. We honor the art of hand-trimming our meats and discarding nothing, using every last bit to add flavor and depth to burgers, soups, and sauces. We also keep our business local, reducing the food mileage of your meals. Our chef calls on generations-old Chicago bakeries to craft wholesome breads and fresh pastries every morning and builds community with family-owned farms so you’ll reap the freshest bounty – on your plate and in your cocktail.

## Our Eat Well Menu

At Westin, feeling good is at the center of great travel. We support your healthy lifestyle in everything we do. Our Eat Well menu helps you make mindful choices that keep you feeling energized and balanced. Whether you’re grabbing a quick breakfast to go from our Café à la Carte or joining us at 909 North for an elegant meal, we’ve curated our menu with nutritious, locally sourced sustainable ingredients. Best of all, our Eat Well menu is not only good for you, it’s mouthwateringly delicious, too.
