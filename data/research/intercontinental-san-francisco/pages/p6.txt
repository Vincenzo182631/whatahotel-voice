Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental San Francisco?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental San Francisco.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental San Francisco?

Check-in at InterContinental San Francisco is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental San Francisco offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental San Francisco offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental San Francisco from the nearest airport?

InterContinental San Francisco is conveniently located near San Francisco International Airport (SFO), approximately 13.7 miles away (22.05 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental San Francisco?

InterContinental San Francisco is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Union Square - 0.5 miles away (0.8 kilometers)
- Cable Car Museum - 1 miles away (1.61 kilometers)
- Xanadu Gallery
- China Town
- Alcatraz
- Coit Tower
- Fisherman’s Wharf - 1 miles away (1.61 kilometers)
- Painted Ladies - 2 miles away (3.22 kilometers)
- California Academy Of Science - 3.5 miles away (5.63 kilometers)
- De Young Museum - 3.5 miles away (5.63 kilometers)
- Exploratorium - 2 miles away (3.22 kilometers)
- Conservatory of Flowers - 3.5 miles away (5.63 kilometers)
- Twin Peaks - 4 miles away (6.44 kilometers)
- Golden Gate Bridge/Fort Point - 6 miles away (9.66 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental San Francisco puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
