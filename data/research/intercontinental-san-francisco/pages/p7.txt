### Disappointed Stay

6 days ago

Prior to our stay, I contacted the hotel to share that we were celebrating my wife's recent promotion. Andrew, the Night Operations Manager, kindly responded that our request would be noted and that the Guest Relations team would be informed to help make our stay memorable.

However, upon arrival, there was absolutely no acknowledgment of our special occasion, not even a simple congratulations. We were only informed that we had been assigned a higher floor in recognition of my IHG Platinum Elite status.

I even sent Andrew a polite follow-up email on the day of check-in, asking whether any special arrangements had been planned. Unfortunately, I never received a response, even after our stay had ended.

I fully understand that upgrades and special amenities are never guaranteed, and I was not expecting anything extravagant. A simple acknowledgment would have been more than enough.

What disappointed me most was the complete lack of follow-through after the hotel's initial assurance, followed by the unanswered email during our stay.

As an IHG Platinum Elite member, I expected better communication and attention to detail from an InterContinental property. This was supposed to be a memorable celebration for my wife, but unfortunately, the experience fell short of the expectations the hotel itself had created.

ProsAmenities, Location

ConsRecognition for IHG One Rewards Members

No, I do not recommend this hotel.
