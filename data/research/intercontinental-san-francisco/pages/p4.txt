### Club InterContinental

Enhance your stay with Club InterContinental. Our dedicated team is here to assist with whatever you need. You'll enjoy exclusive benefits including access to the private Club InterContinental lounge, which includes complimentary breakfast, evening drinks and canapes, and light refreshments throughout the day.

Learn more

### The Gym at InterContinental San Francisco

Whether you're traveling for business or leisure, our 6th-floor San Francisco hotel with a fitness center, is open 24/7 and fully equipped for a complete workout. Enjoy Peloton bikes, treadmills, a StairMaster, free weights, cable machines, a reformer, TRX suspension trainers, slam balls, and a virtual trainer.

Learn more

### World Class Concierge

Hours: Tuesday - Saturday, 8AM - 4PM

The concierge is here to make your stay exceptional. Whether you need restaurant reservations, tickets to local attractions, or personalized recommendations, assistance is just a call or visit away to help you enjoy the best of San Francisco.

Learn more

### Swim and Soak

Unwind on the sixth floor with a heated indoor lap pool and soothing Jacuzzi. Open daily from 6 AM to 10 PM, this peaceful retreat is the perfect way to start your day with a swim or end it in total relaxation. Hours: 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM

### Stay Green with Us

Our sustainability program champions environmental stewardship through 100% renewable energy, solar-powered waste management, and energy-efficient technologies. Water use is reduced by 30%, and 80% of waste is recycled or composted. With natural light, public transit access, and EV charging, we create a positive impact for guests and the planet.

Learn more
