# InterContinental San FranciscoRooms & suites

Our classic downtown San Francisco hotel rooms blend calming tones, modern furnishings, and warm wood details for a refined yet relaxed feel. Stay productive at the spacious work area, then unwind with skyline views through floor-to-ceiling windows or a movie on the 60-inch TV.

## Thoughtfully Designed Accommodations

Every room and suite at InterContinental San Francisco is designed to support both productivity and relaxation, offering a serene retreat above the city. Guests seeking an enhanced experience may enjoy access to **Club InterContinental**, offering personalized service and exclusive amenities in a private lounge setting.

- Spacious layouts with dedicated work areas
- Floor‑to‑ceiling windows with city or bay views
- Refined furnishings and contemporary design details

- Premium bath amenities and plush bedding
- High‑speed Wi‑Fi and in‑room dining available

### Classic & Premium Rooms

Our Classic and Premium Rooms offer a calm, contemporary environment with generous space to work or unwind. Large windows fill each room with natural light, while modern comforts and thoughtful details ensure a seamless stay in downtown San Francisco.

### Suites

Ideal for extended stays or hosting, the suites at InterContinental San Francisco provide expansive living areas, elevated privacy, and exceptional views. With separate spaces to relax, work, or entertain, each suite reflects the brand’s commitment to understated luxury and refined comfort.
