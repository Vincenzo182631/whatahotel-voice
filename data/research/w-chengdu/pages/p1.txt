# Welcome to W Chengdu

## Luxury Lifestyle Hotel in Chengdu

W Chengdu is located in the pulsating Gaoxin district. Soak up the pulse of the city and get Insider Access to the newest, eclectic lifestyle that beats through this cultural, cosmopolitan city. A never-ending flow of the like-minded entangled in music and interactive art will see the Living Room transform from day to night. Ignite your palate with an explosive interplay of Southeast Asian and Sichuan...See More spices at ZING. See and be seen at SPARK while you get lost in the euphoric beats of local emerging artists and reputable DJs. Fuel your body at FIT, ripple through to the infinity of lights at WET and get your glow on at AWAY SPA. Then reimagine witty interpretations of the endlessly playful Chengdu in one of the 297 rooms and suites. WHATEVER WHENEVER makes sure you have what you want, when you want it. Play hard, live easy. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Chengdu?

The check-in time at W Chengdu is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Chengdu allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Chengdu. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W Chengdu?

On-site self parking is available at W Chengdu. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Chengdu have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Chengdu has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Chengdu booking.

What is the closest airport near W Chengdu?

The closest airport near W Chengdu is Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport (CTU). CTU is located approximately 10.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Chengdu have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Chengdu has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does W Chengdu have a W lounge?

Yes, W Chengdu has W lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with W lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
