### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Chengdu have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Chengdu offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Pilates equipment, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Chengdu have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Chengdu has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does W Chengdu have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY® SPA is located on-site at W Chengdu.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Chengdu?

Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding, Du Fu's Thatched Cottage, U Fun Plaza, Tiexiang Temple Water Street, Jinli Ancient Street and more are nearby W Chengdu.
