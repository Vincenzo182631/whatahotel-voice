## A _reimagined_ ICON IN THE HEART OF LOS ANGELES

Ideally situated in Century City, our luxury hotel remakes the past through its heart-of-the-action location, inspired design, and breathtaking architecture. Since 1966, Fairmont Century Plaza has been the destination for countless presidential visits, monumental performances by musical legends, and major awards ceremonies in Los Angeles.
