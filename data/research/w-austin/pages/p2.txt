## Accommodations

Embrace luxury, liberated in our completely renovated and elevated guest rooms. Every room and suite is thoughtfully designed to maximize space, comfort, and style. Breeze around the room on hardwood floors, listen to your favorite music on the Marshall speaker, feel the ultimate calm as the blue-green wall and ceiling paint reflects off Lady Bird Lake, relive history through the Scott Newton prints,...See More snuggle in the Frette robes and relax in the 300-thread-count linens and goose duvet – there is no shortage of comfort amongst the design. Become your best self when you stay at W Austin.See Less

## Room Amenities & Services

Marshall speakers, Davines bath products, Frette robes, Nespresso machines, safety deposit boxes, umbrellas available, express checkout, full laundry service (charge), turndown service (upon request).

## Room Features

Hardwood floors, water carafes and filtered water dispensers (in the ice room) for sustainable refilling, occupancy monitored lighting and HVAC, city view rooms available, lake view rooms available, 100% non-smoking guestrooms, individual climate control.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Austin have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Austin have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Austin?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at W Austin. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Austin?

No, the rooms at W Austin do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at W Austin have fridges?

No, rooms at W Austin do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W Austin have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Austin have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Austin have hair dryers?
