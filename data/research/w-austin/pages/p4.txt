# Enjoy foodie delights amidst an upscale esthetic

## Dining

Enjoy all the dining outlets at W Austin for every part of your stay. Wake up with a latte and fresh pastry at 2nd & Roast Coffee Bar and wind down with a cocktail curated just for you in Blue Room. For every meal, meet up, and moment in between visit Serenade American Brasserie full service restaurant or Living Room Bar. If you're staying in, we're pleased to offer 24-hour In Room Dining.
