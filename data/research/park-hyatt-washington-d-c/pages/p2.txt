Relax and rejuvenate in one of 220 luxury rooms, including 134 suites at Park Hyatt Washington D.C. Our rooms blend American folk artistry and craftsmanship with a Zen spa vibe to create a modern, urban retreat. Bright, airy rooms, relaxing hues, rich textures and plush bedding welcome you to unwind. Suites offer separate living room areas and beautiful limestone bathrooms. Upgrade to the hotel’s Presidential Suite featuring 2,006 square feet of space to spread out.
