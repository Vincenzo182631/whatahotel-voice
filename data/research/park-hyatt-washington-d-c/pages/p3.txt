# Restaurants & Bars

Park Hyatt Washington D.C. is home to the award-winning Blue Duck Tavern. This neighborhood restaurant near Foggy Bottom serves carefully crafted seasonal cuisine in a warm, contemporary atmosphere. Blue Duck Tavern features an open-concept kitchen with a wood-burning oven and boasts an impressive wine cellar and curated tasting menu. The adjacent Blue Duck Lounge offers refreshing cocktails and small bites, while the exclusive Tea Cellar serves afternoon tea and features single-estate teas from remote regions.

## Tea Cellar

Discover more than 30 rare, single-estate teas from remote regions of China, Japan, Sri Lanka, and the Himalayas at our exclusive Tea Cellar. Learn the importance of water temperature and steeping time, and introduce your palate to the subtle nuances in flavor and taste. Tea is ordered by the freshly brewed pot and ranges from $8 to $300. Afternoon tea is available.

Chic and intimate, Blue Duck Lounge offers an upscale haven in Georgetown and Foggy Bottom. Sip cocktails or fine wines, complemented with seasonally crafted light fare from Blue Duck Tavern. Comfortable seating and Capitol Hill glass-enclosed booths flank the stylish marble and glass bar, providing an intimate lounge experience.
