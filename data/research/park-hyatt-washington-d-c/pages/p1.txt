# Luxury Hotels In Washington D.C. \| Park Hyatt Washington D.C.

## Find Rare Luxury at our West End Hotel in D.C.

Nestled in the West End near Washington Circle and the White House, our hotel welcomes you to Washington, D.C. Find first-class service and handcrafted Windsor-style furniture in the lobby. Unwind in a luxury presidential suite with a rain shower and soaking tub, or relax by our skylit saltwater pool. Dine at Blue Duck Tavern for exceptional wines and an open-concept culinary experience.

## Discover Park Hyatt Washington

Fitness Center

Featuring two Peloton bikes, high-tech cardio machines and strength-training equipment in an immaculate and inspiring setting, our 24-hour fitness center, located in our guest house, makes it easy and enjoyable to maintain your exercise routine while visiting our Washington D.C. hotel.

Environmental Sustainability

Our green hotel participates in an environmental sustainability program that focuses on climate change, waste reduction and energy and water efficiency. From organic produce to eco-certified cleaning products, we care for our guests and our piece of Washington, D.C.

Pool

After a full day in the West End, rest your senses in the tranquility of our hotel’s skylit saltwater pool with calming décor, natural light and a separate hot tub. This oasis is part of our guest house, situated near our spa room and fitness center.

### Pets Are Welcome

Explore the capital with your canine companion during your stay at our dog-friendly hotel in Washington D.C. A dog bed, water bowl and treats await your arrival, and we donate a portion of our pet fee to the Humane Rescue Alliance. Service animals are welcome and are not subject to this policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
