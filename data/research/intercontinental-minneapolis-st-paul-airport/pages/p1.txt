## FAQs for InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport

Does InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport have a pool?

No, InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport?

At InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 34 USD and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 57 USD .

Valet parking is available at the main entrance and self parking is available in the parking ramp adjacent to the hotel. Day and overnight options are available.
The hotel offers two hours of complimentary parking for guests of the restaurants

Parking fee: 34 USD per day

Vehicles allowed: Bus, Rv, Trailer, Truck

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport.

Does InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport offer free airport shuttle service?

Yes, InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport offers a complimentary airport shuttle.

Follow hotel shuttle directional signs in baggage claim area. Call hotel for terminal 2 pickup.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport?

Check-in at InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport is conveniently located near Minneapolis/St. Paul International Airport (MSP), approximately 0.01 miles away (0.02 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport?

InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Mall of America - 6 miles away (9.66 kilometers)
- Minnesota Zoo - 13 miles away (20.92 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Minneapolis - St. Paul Airport puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
