### Not worth the price

9 days ago

I am a frequent traveler, and I do not intend to return to this IHG property. I paid full price for a short notice room, arriving after midnight. When I walked into the room, it was 60°. I immediately turned the heat up and went about preparing for sleep. By the time I went to bed nearly an hour later, it had warmed to only 61°. It never exceeded that temperature until I simply turned the system off the next morning. I had to take the covers off the second bed to be warm enough to sleep. Also, the toilet paper holder didn’t actually hold the toilet paper roll at all. Unrolling with any amount of pressure caused the toilet paper roll to eject from the holder and fly across the floor. In the morning, I called down for a replacement iron as the one in my room did not work. It was only after a second call to the front desk, more than 30 minutes later, that one was delivered. Are these the end of the world? No. But for $300 for a single night and the prestige of the Intercontinental, I expected more. I have had far better experiences at small town Holiday Inn Express hotels.
