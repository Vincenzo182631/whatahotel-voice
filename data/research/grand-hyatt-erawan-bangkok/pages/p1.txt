## An Iconic Sanctuary in the Heart of Central Bangkok

Blessed with a privileged position in the capital’s key commercial and diplomatic district, next to Erawan Shrine, Grand Hyatt Erawan Bangkok, 5 star hotel Bangkok, offers a refined retreat from the city’s hustle bustle. Immerse yourself in the fascinating sights and delights of downtown Bangkok while luxuriating in the height of opulence and comfort.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Grand Hyatt Erawan Bangkok features contemporary, spacious rooms and suites with stunning views of downtown Bangkok. For the ultimate indulgence, stay in our 240-square-metre Erawan Suite, which offers a spacious living area, dining room, kitchen, gym, and a spa-like bathroom. For an elevated experience, VIP Fast Track and immigration service at the airport, 24-hour butler service, personalised assistance, and more are available with select suites and upon request.

i.sawan Residential Spa & Club

Located on the 5th floor, i.sawan is a resort-style retreat within the hotel, spanning 7,000 square metres. The retreat features residential spa cottages, treatment bungalows, a nail bar, hair salon, pool, fitness centre, movement studio, tennis court and squash court. A menu of award-winning, results-oriented treatments is inspired by Thailand’s ancient healing arts.

**2025 Thailand Tourism Outstanding Awards –  Hotel & Resort Spa**

Fitness Centre

Maintain your fitness routine while you travel at Grand Hyatt Erawan Bangkok’s Greenhouse Fitness Centre, located on the 5th floor. Take advantage of Technogym cardio equipment or free weights, and enjoy benefits like built-in TVs, filtered water and complimentary towels. Personal trainers are available on request.

Outdoor Pool

Surrounded by a teak sundeck and lush landscaping, our 25-metre outdoor pool is the perfect place to squeeze in some laps, cool off from the sun or simply relax on a lounger. You can then unwind in the hot tub, steam room, sauna and cold-plunge pool within the changing rooms.

Erawan Shrine

Our hotel overlooks the iconic Erawan Shrine, located just outside the hotel. The Erawan Shrine is situated among six Hindu deities enshrined in and around the intersection. Famous for bringing good fortune, the shrine attracts millions of visitors each year.

Tennis Court

Enjoy a game of tennis in the fresh air during your stay with us. Our outdoor tennis court in Bangkok, located on the 5th floor, is now available for booking. Tennis equipment is available for rent, ensuring you have everything needed for an excellent game. Please make your reservation in advance.

**Opening hours:** Everyday, 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM
