# Dining

With its nine distinctive bars and restaurants Grand Hyatt Erawan Bangkok is a gastronomic destination in the heart of Thailand's capital, showcasing an unrivaled variety of authentic local and global flavors. Whether private dining or an extravagant buffet, a light lunch or a leisurely afternoon tea, an authentic French croissant or Thai chocolate, Grand Hyatt Erawan Bangkok offers world-class dining experiences and exceptional service.

## Salvia

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

Location: Mezzanine Level

Salvia is an Italian neighborhood-inspired Osteria. Here, friends and families enjoy a relaxing time and shareable dishes. Menu includes artisanal Italian cold cuts and non-pasteurized cheeses, wood-fired Napolitana pizzas, handmade pastas, grapevine-grilled meats and decadent desserts. For the thirsty, Salvia has you covered with thoughtfully selected Italian wines, ice cold craft beers, Martinis, Negronis and fruity Italian sodas that can also be sipped around the bar counter. Private dining is available in two beautifully-furnished private rooms.
