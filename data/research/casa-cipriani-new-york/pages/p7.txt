# MEMBERSHIP

Casa Cipriani, a true private members club in the modern sense, brings together an eclectic community who shares life’s simple pleasures.

APPLY

* * *

* * *

- ## DRESS CODE

Our landmark building evokes the refined elegance of the Beaux-Arts period, and we ask that all members and guests respect the tradition of that era through appropriate attire. Members and guests who do not adhere to the guidelines below may be denied entry. This decision will be entirely at the discretion of our front desk team, and we ask that you ensure the dress code is conveyed to any guests you invite to the Club.

An overall elegant and polished appearance is expected at all times.

Always Permitted:

o Clothing that is tailored, smart, and well-presented in appearance.

o Jeans, without rips.

o Women's tailored shorts.

o Smart, formal sneakers of refined appearance, in leather or suede, that are clean, well-maintained, and presented in neutral tones with minimal or no visible branding.

o Elegant hats such as fedoras, berets, or sunhats.

Never Permitted:

o Clothing that is overly casual, sporty, or streetwear-inspired in appearance.

o Bold or overly large logos and graphics.

o Overly revealing attire. Members and guests may be asked to cover up at the discretion of our team.

o Hooded sweatshirts.

o Ripped jeans or pants.

o Casual pants such as track pants, joggers, cargo pants, and sweatpants.

o Baseball caps and beanies.

o Sneakers that are athletic or streetwear-inspired in appearance, distressed or unclean, brightly colored, or feature large branding.

o Flip-flops and casual sandals.

Permitted Until Noon:

o Athletic or exercise sneakers.

o Athletic or exercise attire.

o Men's shorts.

Permitted Until 5:00 PM:

o Men's short-sleeved shirts, including T-shirts and collared shirts. After 5:00 PM, gentlemen are required to wear long sleeves in the Club. Blazers may be provided by our staff, if needed.

Permitted at the Spa & Wellness on Floor 2M:

o Athletic or exercise attire and sneakers are always permitted while at the Spa & Wellness on Floor 2M. If you plan to visit the Club on Floor 5 afterward, members and guests must be in dress code.

- ## PHOTOGRAPHY

Photography and videography on Club premises are strictly forbidden in all areas. Taking photos or posting on social media is also prohibited while on Club premises.

- ## GUEST POLICY

Members are allowed to bring up to (3) guests at any one time. If a member does not arrive with their complete party the full name(s) of each person in their group must be given to the front desk team. This can be done onsite or prior to arrival by emailing theclub@casacipriani.com.

HOTEL GUESTS

Guests staying in the Rooms & Suites at Casa Cipriani are allowed to bring up to four (4) guests per room. This includes the occupants of the room.

Guests staying in the Verrazzano Suite, may bring up to six (6) guests including the occupants of the room.

Guests staying in the Roebling or Bartholdi Presidential Suites may bring up to eight (8) guests including the occupants of the room.

- ## AGE

All guests visiting the Club must be at least 21 years old, after 5:00 PM.

- ## CHILDREN

Children are welcome to enjoy breakfast and lunch daily from 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM in the Club Restaurant, only. After 5:00 PM children are not permitted in any areas of the 5th Floor or Rooftop.

- ## PETS

Pets are not permitted on Club premises unless medically required or with the explicit permission of the Proprietor. Members and/or their guests who do attend the Club with animals are always deemed to be personally liable for their conduct. Animals are not permitted on Club furniture.

- ## LAPTOPS & HEADPHONES

Both laptops and headphones are permitted for use until 6:00 PM daily in the club.

- ## BOOKING GUESTROOMS/SUITES FOR FRIENDS & FAMILY

The Casa Cipriani member is responsible for booking and paying for his friends and family. Member must provide a Credit Card authorization form completed to cover Room and Tax. The reservations team will book the rooms and suites in the name of the actual guest staying. There is no limit on the number of rooms/suites a member can reserve for his friends and family.
