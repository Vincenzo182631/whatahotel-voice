# ROOMS AND SUITES

An urban sanctuary within the Battery Maritime Building, Casa Cipriani New York offers guestrooms, Suites and Presidential Suites imbued with serenity, resting on the very tip of Lower Manhattan. Designed by Thierry Despont, our forty-seven uniquely decorated Guestrooms and Suites, many with private terraces, overlooking the East River, the Brooklyn Bridge and the Statue of Liberty.
