# LOCATION

Located on the water in Lower Manhattan in the iconic Battery Maritime Building, steps from Wall Street and the Financial District, Casa Cipriani resides in the historic 1909 Battery Maritime Building overlooking the majestic Statue of Liberty and New York Harbor. Nestled on the southern tip of Manhattan, it neighbors the charm of Lower Manhattan’s Downtown area. From the elegant rooms and suites, guests bask in dazzling views of the East River, Brooklyn Bridge, and Statue of Liberty. Its privileged location allows easy access to experience the culture, art, and shopping of the surrounding Downtown and Soho neighborhoods. Casa Cipriani’s magnificent waterfront location in the former Governors Island ferry terminal makes it the perfect launchpad to soak in the magic of our lower Manhattan hotel.
