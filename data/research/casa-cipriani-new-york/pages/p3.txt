# WELLNESS

Casa Cipriani Wellness features unique cast iron architectural details, and is furnished with state-of-the-art equipment.

The Spa, a peaceful retreat for playful relaxation and ultimate indulgence, in the privacy of an intimate environment, offers a range of signature massages and wellness experiences from a team of hand-picked therapists, including acupuncture, pain management, skincare and medical stress recovery. A curated program of personal training and dynamic movement classes can be enjoyed in our Fitness Center and post workout guest can relax in our eucalyptus steam baths, cryotherapy and traditional cedar saunas are available as part of the Casa Cipriani Wellness journey.

### SPA POLICIES

- ## ARRIVAL TIME

You are encouraged to arrive 30 minutes prior to your appointments to enjoy the exclusive facilities at the Spa at Casa Cipriani. Please understand that late arrival may result in a reduction of treatment time.

- ## CANCELLATION POLICY

Please contact the Spa at least 24 hours prior to all appointments to inform us of any cancellation or modification needs (with a live representative). If appointments are canceled within the 24-hour window, 100% of service will be charged to the payment on file that has guaranteed your reservations.

- ## CHILDREN

Age restriction: Guests must be 16 years or older to use the facilities.

Minors (16 to 18 years): Must be accompanied by an adult at all times.

- ## FACILITIES

Start your relaxation and healing in our Eucalyptus Steam Room or Cedar Sauna and prepare your body and mind for your treatment to follow.

- ## GRATUITY

Please be advised that a twenty percent (20%) service charge shall be applied to all treatments, the entirety of which shall be distributed to those persons performing and/or supporting your services.

- ## HEALTH AND WELL-BEING

Kindly advise of any health conditions, allergies, or injuries, which could affect your treatments while at the Spa at Casa Cipriani.

- ## HOURS OF OPERATION

Monday–Friday

Fitness Center: 6am - 9pm

Spa: 11am - 8pm

Saturday & Sunday

Fitness Center: 8am – 8pm

Spa: 11am - 8pm

Hotel Guests have key card activated 24-hour Fitness Center access and all wet areas close thirty minutes prior to the listed closing time.

- ## SPA ETIQUETTE

The Spa at Casa Cipriani welcomes you to enjoy our Living Room space or Treatment Lounge before your Wellness journey begins. Tea and water service is available for all guests of the Spa. Please note that our Treatment Lounge is a space of tranquility and we do not allow cell phone use.
