# THE CLUB

A timeless home in historic unique waterfront settings. Old-World glamour for an eclectic community to relax, dine and entertain in style.

* * *

* * *

### OPEN DAILY, 7AM - MIDNIGHT

THE RESTAURANT

Casa Cipriani’s formal restaurant is open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner every day. The elegant interior is inspired by the glamorous dining room of a French ocean liner of the thirties. The menu includes Harry’s Bar classics like the original carpaccio, baked tagliolini with ham, calf’s liver alla Veneziana, baby artichokes from the island of Torcello, and the famous vanilla meringue cake.

### OPEN DAILY, 7AM–MIDNIGHT

EAST TERRACE

As an extension of the restaurant, the East Terrace offers a refined outdoor all-day dining experience against panoramic views of the Brooklyn Bridge and the East River. At night, the atmosphere turns magic with the lights and the glow across the water. Spring and summer invite guests to relish the sunny days, while the heated ceilings during fall and winter ensure year-round enjoyment.

### TUESDAY–SATURDAY, 7PM–1AM

JAZZ CAFÉ

The Jazz Café at Casa Cipriani, with a rich décor featuring a gold-leaf ceiling and hand-painted lamps, is reminiscent of the warm atmosphere of a supper club of the '30s and '40s. It offers dinner with live music performances; a pianist and two different jazz bands alternate each evening with a variety of musical flavors throughout the week. The menu includes Cipriani cuisine specialties.

### OPEN DAILY, 7AM–MIDNIGHT

THE PICKERING ROOM

A beautiful story of trust and shared dreams led to the opening of Harry’s Bar in Venice in 1931, named after Harry Pickering, a frequent patron of the bar where patriarch Giuseppe Cipriani used to work. The Pickering Room is an all-day warm ambience, perfect for coffee in the morning while working or catching up with friends, and a fun, vibrant atmosphere in the evening for dinner, especially for larger groups.

### OPEN DAILY, TUESDAY–SATURDAY 7AM–2AM, SUNDAY-MONDAY 7AM-1AM

PROMENADE BAR

Located to the right, at the entrance of the 5th Floor Club, the Promenade Bar is where members can enjoy a welcome drink, an afternoon tea, or an evening aperitif with crispy zucchini or Venetian “tramezzini” with friends, and acclimate overlooking stunning water views. A gilt-edged mahogany counter and an ocean-liner mural distinguish the bar that leads to the warm and expansive Living Room space.

### OPEN DAILY, TUESDAY–SATURDAY 7AM–2AM, SUNDAY-MONDAY 7AM-1AM

THE LIVING ROOM

Filled with colorful couches, Art Deco furniture, and cozy seating with a central fireplace, the Living Room offers different experiences throughout the day, from crisp and bright mornings to warm and romantic evenings. Members are welcome whether to work, meet with friends or business associates, or enjoy a casual lunch or dinner, with lounge music and dimmed soft lighting. The Living Room menu includes pizzas fresh out of the oven, appetizers, and a selection of decadent desserts as well as a full bar. Seating is on a first-come, first-served basis.

### OPEN DAILY, TUESDAY–SATURDAY 7AM–2AM, SUNDAY–MONDAY 7AM–1AM

WEST TERRACE

As an extension of the Living Room, the West Terrace, with its views of the waterfront, the Statue of Liberty, and the city skyline, is the perfect ambience for a leisurely coffee, a delightful lunch on a sunny afternoon, or cocktails with the most beautiful sunsets. As the day transitions into night, the terrace transforms into a lively rooftop for after-dinner leisure. Featuring the Terrazza Bar for drinks and the Living Room's menu with a variety of choices, the comfortable seating is on a first-come, first-served basis.

## WELLNESS

Encompassing 15,000 square feet, our expansive health and wellness floor includes a fitness center, movement studio, steam rooms, saunas, cryotherapy, a full-service spa, integrative medical treatments and a wellness cafe. While the Wellness Center is exclusively available to the Casa Cipriani community, the Spa can be reserved by non-member guests.
