# CASA CIPRIANI NEW YORK

A true private members club in the modern sense, Casa Cipriani, located in lower Manhattan, brings together an eclectic community of those who enjoy life’s simple pleasures. From the first days of Harry’s Bar in Venice, there was always a crowd of people from all walks of life, merrily sipping Bellinis, chatting, and living the good life.
