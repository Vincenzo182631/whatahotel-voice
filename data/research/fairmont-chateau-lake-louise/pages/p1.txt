## The _historic_ jewel of the Canadian Rockies

Surrounded by soaring mountain peaks, the majestic Victoria Glacier, and a glistening emerald lake, Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise offers 5-star luxury amid unparalleled natural beauty. Explore scenic hiking trails or enjoy world-class skiing at one of three ski resorts. Indulge with a spa treatment before savoring seasonally inspired cuisine.

### Step into unparelleled beauty and refined elegance

Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise is a year-round luxury hotel ideal for solo travelers, couples, and families alike. Located in Alberta's Banff National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, we are proud to be recognized globally for progressive environmental stewardship and responsible tourism.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Does Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise have a spa?

Yes. Our 5-star resort is home to the famous Fairmont Spa that offers signature treatments including massage, body, face, and nail salon services. Visit our Spa page for more information.

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise?

Yes. Our hotel offers accessible accommodation at Lake Lousie with a selection of amenities that will make your stay truly special. Visit our Accessibility page for more information.

Does Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise have a gym/fitness centre?

Yes. You can stay fit while on vacation with our 24-hour aquatic pool and health club. Visit our Fitness & Movement page for more information.

Is parking available at Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise?

The hotel is connected to a partially covered parkade with plug-ins.

Parking is available to our overnight in-house guests at the following rates:

- Self-parking: $40 per day
- Valet Parking: $55 per day

What is the Resort Experience Fee?

Please note that there is an additional $45+tax CAD per room per night daily fee\*. During your stay at Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise, we invite you to take advantage of the benefits of staying in the heart of Banff National Park. Learn more here.
