# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach

## Experience refined luxury at our Marina Bay hotel

JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach expertly balances historic heritage with modern style, providing a luxury experience in the city center. Our luxury hotel near Singapore's Marina Bay is ideally situated within the Central Business District (CBD), near leading downtown attractions, including the National Stadium and National Gallery. Find respite in our immaculately appointed hotel accommodations...See More in the Marina Bay area, with elegant interiors, intuitive amenities, and sleek marble bathrooms. Our signature Spa by JW offers tailored wellness treatments. Delight your palate in our stylish restaurants, and sip creative cocktails in our bars. Connect with nature at the JW Garden. For an escape from the day's activities, pause for a moment at the Hushed Habitat or Sky Escape. Plan an unforgettable event in breathtaking venues, including our elegant, standalone Grand Ballroom with iconic interiors and a stunning Forest of Lights. Work, dine, relax, and explore with us at JW Marriott Singapore South Beach.See Less

The JW Garden

Inspired by Alice Marriott’s love of gardening, the JW Garden is a serene sanctuary that welcomes guests and grows herbs and ingredients as part of the culinary journey. The JW Garden celebrates the ecological and cultural diversity of Singapore with seating to invite moments of mindfulness. Highlighted by a set of two sculptural blue trellises, which support native climbing plants and edible vines, visit our JW Garden, located at the outdoor terrace of Beach Road Kitchen during your stay with us.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach, with rates of $2.40 per hour, $20.00 per day, $140.00 per week, and $0.00 perstay.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach is Singapore Changi Airport (SIN). SIN is located approximately 18.4 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
