# Explore Premier Accommodation in Singapore's Marina Bay

## Accommodations

Step into a private world of luxury and exclusivity when you stay in one of the 634 premier Marina Bay hotel suites at JW Marriott Hotel Singapore South Beach. Take in panoramic views of the Singapore skyline as you lounge in the comfort of our unique "floating" beds. Recharge your senses with a relaxing soak in your hotel suite's well-appointed marble bathroom. Pamper yourself further in our club...See More room and luxury suite accommodations in Marina Bay, Singapore, including 2-bedroom suite options. ### Designer Rooms

Fitted with modern amenities and smart technology that blends with your tech-savvy lifestyle, all Deluxe and Premier Marina Bay luxury suites in Singapore are elegantly styled with our signature ‘floating' beds and luxury bedding, designer furnishings, and marble bathrooms featuring state-of-the-art automatic toilets with electronic bidets and a separate rain shower. All rooms and suites are equipped with a Nespresso Coffee Machine.
