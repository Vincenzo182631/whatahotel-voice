### Spa Services

## Spa Facilities

Entering through an inviting reception furnished in a soothing palette of soft greys, blues and blush, guests are greeted at the Curator's Desk where they are introduced to spa facilities including a Relaxation Lounge, Sauna, Steam Room and Jacuzzi.

## Calm. Indulge. Invigorate. Renew.

Guests may select from full-service and express treatments designed designed around four key benefits, catering to specific needs at our Marina Bay spa in Singapore

Details

Developed in collaboration with JW Marriott brand partner and spa industry leader Aromatherapy Associates, all treatments are designed with four essential benefit states in mind:

##### Calm

Allows guests to reset when out-of-sync, offering a moment of serenity and a chance to slow down time to restore balance. Treatments include Stress Relief Massage, Soothing Facial and Tense Shoulders Express Treatment.

##### Indulge

Provides nourishment for the soul and a way for guests to pamper themselves through a range of therapeutic treatments that reward and rejuvenate. Treatments include Ultimate Aromatherapy Experience Massage, Anti-Ageing Facial and Relaxing Massage Express Treatment.

##### Invigorate

Enlivens the body and clarifies the mind, wiping away the stresses of everyday life and replacing it with energy and vitality. Treatments include Jet Let Cure Massage, Firming Facial and Signature Body Cocoon.

##### Renew

Purifies the mind and body so guests feel more centred, re-focusing and instilling a sense of spiritual rebirth. Treatments include Intense Muscle Release Massage, Deep Cleanse Facial and Signature Body Scrub.

Nearby Attractions

### Merlion Park

An iconic landmark and tourist attraction located in the Marina Bay area, the Merlion statue that is half-lion and half-mermaid represents Singapore''s original name, ''Singapura'' also known as ''lion city."

### Chinatown

A taxi can quickly take you to Chinatown, a historic neighborhood located within the Outram district in the center of Singapore.

### Little India

Find traditional restaurants, Hindu temples and shops in Little India, located near our well-appointed hotel near Suntec Convention & Exhibition Center, in Singapore's arts and cultural district.

Local Area & Activities

### Gardens by the Bay

The 101 hectare national garden and premier horticulture attraction is located in the Marina Bay Area, and features the iconic supertrees, flower dome and cloud forest; the perfect attraction to visit with families when in Singapore.

### Esplanade Theatres

Singapore's iconic performing arts theatre, Esplanade, situated near to the JW Marriott Singapore South Beach, was designed after the local Durian fruit.

### Singapore Zoo

It is one of the top attractions in Singapore and an award winning wildlife park that is home to 2,800 animals and over 300 species of mammals, birds and reptiles.

### Singapore Flyer

Situated near to the JW Marriott Singapore South Beach, it is Asia's largest observation wheel which stands at 165 meters and offers breathtaking views of the Marina Bay.

### Arab Street

Located just steps from our hotel, Arab Street and the neighborhood of Kampong Glam were once home to Muslim traders.
