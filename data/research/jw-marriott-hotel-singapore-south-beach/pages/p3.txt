## Revel in an Entertainment-Lifestyle Enclave at The NCO Club

Situated in the art and culture district, The NCO Club's storied history dates back to the 1950s. Today, the conserved heritage building houses four electric bar venues, Cool Cats, a live music lounge and cocktail bar; Stags' Room, featuring a private reserve of fine wines from around the world; and Madame Fan Bar, an elegant cocktail bar offering avant-garde drinks and modern sophistication.
