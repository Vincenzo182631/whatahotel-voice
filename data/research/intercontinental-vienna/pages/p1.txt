### Perfect Day in Vienna

Start your day with breakfast at Café Schwarzenberg, then explore top sights on a city tour including Schönbrunn Palace. Enjoy lunch at the vibrant Naschmarkt, then visit the Belvedere or the Strauss exhibition. Dine on traditional Austrian cuisine at Gmoakeller, enjoy a classical concert and end your evening with a nightcap at Intermezzo Bar.

Learn more

### Best View Over Vienna

One of the best views over the city. Club InterContinental guests enjoy a 180-degree panoramic view over downtown Vienna from the Club Lounge on the 12th floor of the hotel, in addition to many other Club Lounge benefits.

Learn more

### Green Oasis

Stadtpark, located opposite the main entrance of the hotel, is an oasis of calm and relaxation right in the city centre. It's also an ideal spot for a leisurely stroll or more energetic sporting activities. Its golden Johann Strauss monument is one of the world's most frequently photographed memorials.

Learn more

### Responsible Business

InterContinental Vienna is committed to fulfilling its environmental and social responsibilities. The hotel is certified with the Austrian Eco-Label and adheres to the strict requirements of Green Engage, IHG’s global CSR programme. We are also proud to hold the international Green Key certification, underlining our commitment to sustainability.

Learn more

### The Intermezzo Bar: A Legacy

With its opulent crystal chandelier and exquisite drinks, the legendary Intermezzo Bar is the perfect place to end your day in style. A selection of international and local bar food is available for a late-night bite.

## FAQs for InterContinental Vienna

Does InterContinental Vienna have a pool?

No, InterContinental Vienna does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental Vienna?

At InterContinental Vienna, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 49 EUR and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 49 EUR .

We offer 220 parking spaces in our underground garage. Parking charges €4.90 per hour or €49 per 24hours. Our garage is maintained by Parkhaus Elbl. EV charging available. Charges upon consumption of electricity and time.

Parking fee: 49 EUR per day

In and out privileges

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Vienna?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Vienna.

Pet policy description. Pet dogs are allowed in room. Only guide dogs are allowed in restaurant. There is no charge for guide dogs.

Pet fee per night: 25 EUR

Pet weight limit: 25

2 pets allowed

Pets allowed: All pets allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Vienna for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Vienna have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Vienna has 1 restaurant.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Vienna?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Vienna?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Vienna.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Vienna?

Check-in at InterContinental Vienna is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Vienna offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Vienna offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Vienna from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Vienna is conveniently located near Vienna International Airport (VIE), approximately 11.18 miles away (18 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Vienna?

InterContinental Vienna is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Hofburg Imperial Palace - 0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)
- Belvedere Palace - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- Vienna State Opera - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- Prater - 1.86 miles away (3 kilometers)
- MQ - Museum Quarter - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Riding Dinner - 0.87 miles away (1.4 kilometers)
- KunstHausWien - 1.86 miles away (3 kilometers)
- Stadtpark
- Heuriger

- Third Man Tour - 0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)
- KHM - Kunstkammer - 0.93 miles away (1.5 kilometers)
- Albertina - 0.93 miles away (1.5 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Vienna puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
