### Terrific stay in Vienna

2 days ago

This hotel was in the perfect place for our stay. Not knowing much about Vienna, I picked this hotel because it was on the same side of the city as a Praterstrasse, and it didn't seem far from both the Inner Stadt and Praterstrasse (where the event was). It was a wise choice! The hotel is just outside of the ringstrasse, so removed from crowds and right next to Stadt Park, and a train station. We were able to take the train to the event, and to take the ferry to Bratislava. We could have easily taken public transportation to the airport. The ambiance in the hotel is classy, the food is terrific, and the service is impeccable. I found the bed, couch and pillows a little uncomfortable, but after a few days I got used to them.
