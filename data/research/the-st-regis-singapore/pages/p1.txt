# Welcome to The St. Regis Singapore

## Experience luxury at our hotel near Orchard Road, Singapore

Following an extensive transformation, The St. Regis Singapore welcomes you to explore our brand new guest rooms and suites, dining venues and event spaces. Situated at the city’s best address - our luxury hotel in downtown Singapore​ is at the crossroads of the famed Orchard Road shopping belt and the Singapore Botanic Gardens, a World UNESCO Heritage Site, and the Embassy District. Allow our luxury Orchard Road, Singapore hotel be your home away from home. Reside in timeless elegance and peerless service synonymous with The St. Regis brand at our luxury hotel in Singapore's city centre . Here, the House of Astor’s longstanding traditions and rituals come to life through our signature Bloody Mary cocktail, seasonal afternoon teas, daily evening champagne sabrage and our 24-hour legendary St. Regis Butler Service.

### St. Regis Butler Service

Introduced at The St. Regis New York, The St. Regis Butler Service has remained a signature offering for more than a century. At The St. Regis Singapore, allow our dedicated butlers to take care of your every need from the moment you check in and enjoy an array of complimentary butler services.

Signature Services

### Beverage Service

Upon arrival at our luxury Orchard Road, Singapore hotel, allow a St. Regis butler to arrange a welcome beverage to your room, or coffee and tea service in the mornings between 6:00 AM and 11:00 AM.

### Unpacking & Packing Service

Allow a St. Regis butler to take care of the unpacking and storage of your luggage—so that you can simply relax and enjoy. Similarly, before you depart, simply contact the butler service desk to dispatch a butler to assist with gathering and folding your garments to prepare your luggage for the journey home.

### Garment Pressing

Once unpacked, simply let your St. Regis butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. This gracious service includes pressing of two garments per person, per day with a maximum of four garments.

### eButler

During your stay, whether you are inside or outside the hotel, St. Regis butler service is accessible at any hour via email. Simply send your request and allow the butler service desk to assist.

Hotel Policies

**Age Requirement**

Guests must be 18 years or older to check-in. Guests under the age of 18 years must be accompanied by an adult for the duration of stay.

**Check-In Verification**

All guests must be registered. Valid physical ID or passport is required for identity verification. Some rates may also require company identification for verification.

**Check-In & Check-Out Time**

Official check-in time is from 3:00 PM and check-out time by 12:00 PM, unless otherwise stated. Early arrival and late check-out is not guaranteed and subject to availability.

**Cash Advance**

Cash advance services are not available.

**Additional Occupants**

Additional room occupant is chargeable at S$80.00++ per person per night.

Extra bedding is not available for the following room categories: Astor Guest Room, Astor Grand Deluxe Double & Lady Astor Double.

**Legal Drinking Age**

Alcoholic beverage service is restricted to those 18 years or older with valid identification. In accordance with government regulations, alcoholic beverage service is available during permitted hours only.

**No Party Policy**

In accordance with maximum room occupants in view of fire safety regulations mandated by the local authorities, the Hotel has a strict No Party Policy.

**No Pets Policy**

Except for guide dogs, the Hotel maintains a strict No Pets Policy on premises.

**No Smoking Policy**

This is a smoke-free Hotel. Smoking is strictly prohibited on premises. A cleaning fee starting at S$350.00++ will be charged to any guest who violates the No Smoking Policy.

**Parking**

In-house guests enjoy one complimentary parking coupon per night stay at the Hotel or S$10.00 per car daily for valet parking.

**Room Occupants**

For the safety of all guests, please be advised of the maximum room occupancy of each of the Hotel’s room categories. A maximum of two guests is permitted to stay in an Astor Guest Room. A maximum of four guests is permitted to stay in rooms with double beds. The Hotel reserves the right to vacate from Hotel premises, non-registered guest(s) and/or any additional guest(s) exceeding the maximum occupancy allowed for that room type.

**Visitors Policy**

In the interest of the well-being and security of all guests, all visitors are required to register at the Front Desk and are requested to leave by 10:30 PM. Please be advised that a maximum of five persons including registered room guests and visitors is permitted in a guest room or suite at any one time in accordance to fire safety regulations. The Hotel reserves the right to vacate from Hotel premises, any additional visitor(s) exceeding the maximum persons allowed per room.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Singapore?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Singapore is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Singapore allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Singapore.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Singapore?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Singapore. Self parking is available for $4.00 per hour. Valet parking is available for $10.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Singapore have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Singapore has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Singapore booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Singapore?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Singapore is Singapore Changi Airport (SIN). SIN is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Singapore have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Singapore has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
