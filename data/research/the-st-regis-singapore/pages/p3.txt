## Cantonese Fine-Dining

A meal at Yan Ting is an experience to be treasured. Experience a carefully thought medley of Cantonese cuisine's finest. Be enthralled by delectable dim sum delights and savour divine flavors and refined creations to suit even the most discerning of palates at this Cantonese restaurant near Orchard Road in Singapore.

Details

Helmed by Executive Chinese Chef Chan Chung Shing, Yan Ting showcases exquisite yet hearty Cantonese fine dining that will allure palates to satisfy even the most discerning gastronomes, presenting cuisine that are faithful to the traditional cooking techniques of Cantonese cuisine characterised by the immense diversity of ingredients used and the philosophy of bringing out the natural flavours of the freshest ingredients.

The ideal place for joyous gatherings, celebrations and entertaining, Yan Ting has been artistically designed with contemporary architectural style and modern sophistication to create an intimate, graceful and tranquil atmosphere where diners will receive the uncompromising service standards of the St. Regis.

To complete this gastronomically seductive gourmet affair, we also offer semi-private and private dining rooms for your special occasions, as well as a comprehensive collection of old and new world wines, premium spirits and champagnes.

### Dim Sum Brunch

On weekends, Yan Ting presents a captivating a la carte buffet brunch that offers aficionados of Cantonese cuisine a selection of mouth-watering dim sum, savoury meat signatures, traditional seafood indulgences, and delectable desserts.
