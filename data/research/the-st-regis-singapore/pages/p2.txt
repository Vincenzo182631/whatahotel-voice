# Step into luxury in Singapore with our 1-bedroom suites

## Accommodations

Experience contemporary luxury living at The St. Regis in Singapore, where the design of all 299 hotel rooms and luxury hotel suites on Orchard Road, Singapore, reflects the hotel's proximity to the Singapore Botanic Gardens. Every guest room is furnished with a study desk, a 65-inch TV, a marble bathroom with double vanities, a standing shower, a separate bathtub and expansive views of the cityscape.

Check-in Instructions

The main guest under whose name the reservation is made must be present at check-in. All guests must register and provide a valid physical identity card or passport.

### Exquisite New Rooms

Our refreshed luxury accommodations in Singapore​ on Orchard Road reflect the hotel's proximity to the Singapore Botanic Gardens. The interiors infuse botanical references with modern design, where calming tones of blue and aqua, timber-wood flooring, and bespoke furnishings create a residential atmosphere.

## New Distinctive Suites on Orchard Road, Singapore

Our collection of luxury hotel suites near Marina Bay, Singapore, offer a spacious sanctuary for the most discerning travellers. Furnished with a king-sized bed and a spacious commodious living room, intelligent technological enhancements offer our guests a seamless balance of form and function. Marble bathrooms with freestanding bathtubs, twin vanities, a standing shower and residential bath amenities complete the setting, while views of the Singapore Botanic Gardens or Orchard Road provide a sense of place.

## Family Traditions

Especially curated for our younger guests aged 12 and under, the Family Traditions program presents thoughtful amenities such as plush bathrobes, cozy bedroom slippers, and bath essentials ensuring a seamless and comfortable stay for the whole family.

For an added touch of enchantment, families travelling with children can opt to have a teepee tent set up in their rooms, complete with a galaxy night light for a cozy retreat (additional charges apply).

## St. Regis Butler Service

Allow our dedicated butlers to take care of your every need from the moment you check in. Every stay is completed with complimentary butler services including packing and unpacking of your luggage; beverage service upon arrival and in the mornings; daily garment pressing of two pieces daily; shoe shine service; Butler Service Desk and E-butler Services.
