## Bvlgari Resort Bali

Bvlgari Resort Bali is an exclusive and intimate destination for guests seeking privacy and ultra-luxury service, combining the breathtaking beauty of Bali’s unspoilt nature with sophisticated contemporary design, born from the encounter between traditional Balinese forms and distinctive Italian style. The Resort offers unrivalled views across the Indian Ocean and is uniquely positioned atop a dramatic cliff, with a beach and beachside restaurant available exclusively to in-house guests and accessible only through the Resort’s inclined elevator.

### The BVLGARI Villa

Protected behind its own exclusive entrance, the Bvlgari Villa is an extraordinary 1,300-square-metre villa clad in stone that is truly one of a kind. The centerpiece of this two-storey jewel atop the cliff is a 20-metre heated swimming pool, which reaches toward the sapphire Indian Ocean and cascades into a second lower pool. Amid the verdant gardens and large lawn are intimate thatched-roof pavilions for outdoor living and relaxation as well plush sun beds and a meditation pergola framed by an extensive terrace.
