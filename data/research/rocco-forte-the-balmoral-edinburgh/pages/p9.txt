# Christmas at The Balmoral

Introducing the Season of Festive Spirit at The Balmoral. Come and join us for a decadent Afternoon Tea with flavours of nutmeg and clementine, toast to the New Year with Scottish cheer, and come together on Christmas Day at Brasserie Prince.

### Dine with us

### Brasserie Prince

Come together for a delicious festive feast on Christmas Day or join us on Hogmanay to bring in the New Year at Brasserie Prince.
