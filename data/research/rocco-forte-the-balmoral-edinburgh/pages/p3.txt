### Our Story

Settle in for a dram in SCOTCH, our world-class whisky bar. With over 500 unique varieties of Scottish whisky within the bar’s hand-crafted, oak-panelled whisky cabinet, selected from distilleries in the Highlands, Lowlands, Islands, Islay and Speyside, our collection is one of the largest in Edinburgh.

Settle in for a dram in SCOTCH, our world-class whisky bar.

Whether you know your peaty from your creamier, sherry-matured whiskies, or you’re just looking to taste whisky for the first time, our passionate and friendly Whisky Ambassadors can help you make your choice from the ever-changing selection, with its compelling big-hitter bottles, as well as rare and limited editions. Or they can guide you through a phenomenal tasting, sharing stories, tasting notes and passing along their love of “the water of life” with each sip.

### The Last Dram of the Year

Slàinte mhath

SCOTCH is closing its doors on Hogmanay, and inviting an intimate number of guests to experience an unforgettable toast to the past year. Guests will experience a curated selection of six exceptional whiskies from iconic Scottish Whisky brands.

Before raising one final dram to 2026, watching over the spectacular Edinburgh Castle firework display from a private event room on the second floor.

Priced at £300 per person.
