### Our Story

Let the bustle of Princes Street fade as you step into the glamorous environs of Bar Prince.

Our menu features a mix of classic and signature cocktails, Champagnes, Scotch, British gins, Old and New World wines and a decadent selection of dishes.

More Information

# Bar Prince: Cocktail Bar in Edinburgh

- The Balmoral Hotel
- Dining
- Bar Prince

Take a seat in one of the cornflower-blue leather chairs or perch at the bar, festooned with gleaming copper barware and fresh ingredients for the bespoke cocktail list.

Peruse our menus, with its mix of classic and signature cocktails, Champagnes, Scotch, British gins, Old and New World wines, and more. For those with an appetite, the bar has a delightfully decadent selection of dishes.

View Menu

### Connected Icons: Mythical Legends

As you relax in Bar Prince, nestled within The Balmoral, take your time to muse over our signature cocktails as we take you on a journey through Scottish folklore and legends.
