# Brasserie Prince: French Restaurant in Edinburgh

- The Balmoral Hotel
- Dining
- Brasserie Prince

Overseen by Executive Chef, Paul Hart, Brasserie Prince celebrates the very best of Scottish produce with classic French cooking. Serving seasonally inspired, classic brasserie dishes that harmonise quintessentially French flavours with local ingredients.
