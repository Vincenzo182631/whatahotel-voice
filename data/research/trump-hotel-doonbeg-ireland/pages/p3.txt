SPA MENU

Step inside White Horses, the five-star spa at Trump International Golf Links & Hotel Doonbeg, and leave the outside world behind as soothing sounds and calming surroundings awaken the senses. Inspired by 5,000 years of Irish history and mystery, White Horses is a sanctuary where nature’s healing touch restores both body and mind. Soft lighting, glowing candles, and serene interiors create an atmosphere of warmth, tranquillity, and complete relaxation.

Designed as the ultimate escape, the spa features five beautifully appointed treatment rooms offering a wide range of rejuvenating therapies tailored to every guest. Indulge in luxurious hand and foot rituals in the dedicated manicure and pedicure area, or unwind in the spa’s signature copper baths, now available as part of a range of our curated seaweed bathing ritual treatment collection. These deeply soothing, immersive seaweed bath experiences are designed to promote relaxation, balance, and restoration. For golfers seeking post-round recovery, the extensive spa menu includes restorative deep tissue massages and revitalising treatments designed to relax tired muscles and renew energy.

At White Horses Spa, exceptional care extends to every detail. The spa proudly uses high-quality natural and organic products, carefully selected to enhance your wellbeing and create a truly indulgent experience in the heart of County Clare.

Natura Bissé

Each and every one of their products are created with the understanding that every skin has its own voice. We have an open, innovative approach to skincare that fuses expert methodologies with cutting-edge technology, academic research, and traditional practices to discover effective new ways to nurture and beautify the skin. All our products are Halal certified and are never tested on animals.

Repêchage

A pioneering skincare brand inspired by the restorative power of the sea. Founded on the healing benefits of sustainably harvested seaweed, Repêchage blends nutrient-rich marine ingredients with advanced skincare science to hydrate, soothe, and revitalise the skin. Rich in vitamins, minerals, trace elements, and antioxidants, these ocean-derived formulations help restore balance, improve tone and texture, and reveal a healthy, radiant complexion. Celebrated worldwide by skincare professionals, Repêchage treatments deliver visible results while offering a deeply relaxing and rejuvenating experience, perfectly aligned with our coastal philosophy of wellness and renewal.

We are delighted to share that White Horses Spa has undergone an elegant refurbishment to further enhance your wellness experience. Thoughtfully designed with comfort and relaxation in mind, these updates elevate both the spa’s serene atmosphere and its restorative treatments.

We are pleased to introduce our new copper baths, now available as part of a range of our curated seaweed bathing ritual treatment collection. These deeply soothing, immersive seaweed bath experiences are designed to promote relaxation, balance, and restoration.

Our wet rooms, featuring brand-new hot and cold plunge pools, are currently undergoing the final stages of refurbishment and will reopen in mid-June, completing this exciting new chapter of renewal and relaxation.

In the meantime, we invite you to enjoy a renewed sanctuary of calm, where thoughtfully curated treatments, bathing rituals, and restorative experiences are designed to promote balance, relaxation, and overall wellbeing.

Our tranquil spa offers the ultimate escape, with five beautifully appointed treatment rooms dedicated to rejuvenation and relaxation. Guests can also indulge in luxurious hand and foot therapies in our dedicated manicure and pedicure area, or unwind in our serene relaxation room, where heated massage beds and therapeutic music create the perfect environment to rest, recharge, and restore.

Please note that the wet rooms are currently under refurbishment and are scheduled to reopen in mid-June. We apologise for any inconvenience this may cause.
