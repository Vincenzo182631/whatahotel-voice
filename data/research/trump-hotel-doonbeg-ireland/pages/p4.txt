# **GOLF**

## GOLF IRELAND

ICONIC _GOLF_ PROPERTIES

The world renowned 18-hole championship course at Trump International Golf Links & Hotel™ Ireland, Doonbeg, spans 1.5 miles of crescent shaped beach and century old sand dunes along Doughmore Bay. The County Clare, Ireland golf course was officially opened in 2002 and immediately earned the prestigious title of Golf Digest's Best New International Course. In 2010, Trump International Doonbeg was awarded European Golf Resort of the Year by the IAGTO.

Learn more about our golf membership opportunities.

As early as 1892, officers of the Scottish Black Watch Regiment considered Doonbeg an ideal setting for links golf Ireland. Since then Trump Ireland has been transformed by Dr. Martin Hawtree to one of the 'must play' golf courses on the international circuit.  He has allowed the character and soul of the land to flourish while enhancing the playability tenfold. Every hole has its own identity which will both reward and challenge each golfer. Learn more about our golf history.

**IMPORTANT INFORMATION - AMGEN IRISH OPEN**

In preparation for the 2026 Amgen Irish Open, event installations will be present across parts of the resort from 6 July to 30 September (daily, 9:00am – 6:00pm). The golf course will be closed from 24 August to 15 September.

In addition to golf, you can also embark on an array of outdoor adventures at Trump International Golf Links & Hotel™ Ireland, Doonbeg. Enjoy horse riding with options for a beach ride or a country adventure. Immerse yourself in falconry, try archery, and experience the thrill of our free onsite amenities. Families can unwind at the Junior Clubhouse, featuring a soft playroom and more.

Explore County Clare's wonders, from Doolin Cave to Aillwee Cave and Bunratty Castle. Engage in kayaking, fly fishing, and deep-sea fishing, or discover the unique ecosystem of the Burren. Plan a day trip to the Aran Islands and marvel at the Cliffs of Moher. Embrace surf culture at Doughmore Beach for an unforgettable Irish adventure.
