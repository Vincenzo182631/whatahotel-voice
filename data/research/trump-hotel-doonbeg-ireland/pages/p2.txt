# **TRUMP'S BAR AND RESTAURANT**

## FINE DINING IRELAND

DOONBEG _IRISH_

BAR & FOOD

+353 65 905 5633

doonbeg.reservations@trumphotels.com

RESERVE A TABLE

Looking out over the Atlantic Ocean and across the 18th green, with the majestic Cliffs of Moher in the distance, Trump’s Bar & Restaurant sits at the heart of Trump International Golf Links & Hotel Ireland.

This distinctive bar and restaurant in County Clare is renowned for its warm, atmospheric Irish charm, a welcoming space where locals, guests, and golfers come together to relax, dine, and share stories from their journeys along the Wild Atlantic Way.

Celebrating the finest local produce, the menu showcases a relaxed yet refined approach to dining, with comforting classics and coastal favourites at its core, from freshly prepared seafood dishes and traditional fish & chips to hearty Irish beef burgers, seasonal soups, and artisan sandwiches.

Discover pints of stout poured to perfection alongside a carefully curated selection of local craft ales and artisan gins, best enjoyed fireside or with uninterrupted views of the ever-changing Atlantic—creating a dining experience that is both authentic and unforgettable.
