# **ACCOMMODATIONS**

## 5 STAR ACCOMMODATION IRELAND

LUXURIOUSLY APPOINTED.

_EXQUISITELY DECORATED._

Trump Doonbeg boasts sweeping views of the breathtaking Irish landscape from the comfort of your room. As one of Europe's most distinguished five-star destinations, paired luxurious accomodations, superior amenities, and dedicated service. All of our suites and cottages include a fully equipped kitchen to provide you with the comforts of home.

Our 5 star hotel Ireland offers you the ability to savor the culture of the country whether, you’re out exploring or enjoying the many activities on property. Guests can count on old-world luxury and refinement in our guest rooms appointed with curated furnishings, art, and antiques.

Explore the stunning landscapes of County Clare through a diverse range of outdoor activities. Engage in horse riding, falconry, archery, caving, kayaking, fly fishing, and deep-sea fishing. Take in the awe-inspiring views of the Cliffs of Moher or enjoy surfing at Doughmore Beach.
