# The Crown Jewel of Martha's Vineyard

## Seven Miles Off the Coast of Massachusetts

The Harbor View Hotel located in Edgartown, on Martha’s Vineyard, captures the essence of the Great American Summer. Situated just 7 miles off the coast of Massachusetts, this luxury seaside resort offers guests the ultimate vacation oasis on Martha’s Vineyard, boasting the best location on the island.

Our iconic hotel is situated steps away from boutique shops, art galleries, beaches, and sunset cruises, offering top-tier dining and breathtaking views of Edgartown Harbor.  The right location = a beacon of luxury.

## Faithfully Restored. Beautifully Redesigned.

### A Luxury Vineyard Resort

The Harbor View Hotel has been restored and redesigned to become the ultimate resort on Martha’s Vineyard. Chaise lounges and canvas totes: History and tradition. Smart rooms and martinis: renovation and renaissance. With a storied legacy spanning over 130 years, the iconic Harbor View Hotel stands as the undisputed Crown Jewel of Martha’s Vineyard, offering a five-star experience with breathtaking views overlooking the majestic Edgartown Harbor.

## Living the Good Life Begins with

### A Fabulous Meal

Experience culinary excellence at the Harbor View Hotel with three distinctive dining destinations tailored to your every mood. Indulge in a gourmet journey at **Bettini Restaurant**, where the finest contemporary American cuisine is elevated by panoramic ocean vistas and a stylish bar ambiance. For a more casual affair, unwind at **Behind the View**, a vibrant cocktail lounge serving up a delectable array of elevated comfort fare, complemented by handcrafted cocktails and premium spirits meant for sharing. And when the sun beckons, bask in the al fresco delights of **Roxy Pool Bar**, offering a sun-soaked retreat complete with a full bar and a selection of Behind the View’s favorites. Relax amidst plush couches, flickering fire pits, and shaded umbrellas, all set against the backdrop of a picturesque bar and manicured gardens, creating an atmosphere of laid-back elegance.
