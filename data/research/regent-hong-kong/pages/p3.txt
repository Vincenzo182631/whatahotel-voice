## Rooms

Your private sanctuary, elevated above the noise. Beyond a castle-like door, enter a personal world of tranquillity, removed from the ordinary. Experience the sublime design of Chi Wing Lo, imbued with subtle details – each room and suite envisioned as a serene Personal Haven.

## Discover your Personal Haven

Intimate spaces designed to inspire indulgent moments and unique experiences. Find unexpected harmony and serenity amidst neutral tones and meticulously chosen design details.

## Oasis Bathroom

An innovative design by Chi Wing Lo reimagines the bath experience as an art form. A sliding door reveals a Framed View of the harbour from the immersive bath. A moongate serves as an enticing threshold leading to a unique bath and shower experience. Curated bath rituals with luxurious Perricone MD products enhance tranquillity.

## With Compliments

Regent sets a new benchmark for a seamless and inclusive stay. For an uplifting experience, we eliminate small surcharges by introducing unique and standard inclusions when you stay with us.
