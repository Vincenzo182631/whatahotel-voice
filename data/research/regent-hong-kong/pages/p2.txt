## Rooms & Suites

Luxury suites with harbour views await in the heart of Tsim Sha Tsui, where calm meets elegance at Regent Hong Kong. Behind a castle-like door, step into your personal world — a refined space elevated above the city’s energy.

Designed by Chi Wing Lo, each room and suite embodies serene sophistication through, rich textures and precise detail. Whether gazing out over Victoria Harbour or relaxing in tailored comfort, every stay reflects timeless luxury.

Choose Regent Hong Kong , a hotel with harbour view in Hong Kong,  and scroll down to reserve your luxury suite in Tsim Sha Tsui, where comfort and elegance await.

## Discover your Personal Haven

Intimate spaces designed to inspire indulgent moments and unique experiences. Find unexpected harmony and serenity amidst neutral tones and meticulously chosen design details.

## Oasis Bathroom

An innovative design by Chi Wing Lo reimagines the bath experience as an art form. A sliding door reveals a Framed View of the harbour from the immersive bath. A moongate serves as an enticing threshold leading to a unique bath and shower experience. Curated bath rituals with luxurious Perricone MD products enhance tranquillity.

## With Compliments

National Day Firework Spectacle

Experience the spectacular fireworks display over Victoria Harbour from the prime vantage point of Regent Hong Kong on Tuesday, 1 October 2024. Positioned as one of the most picturesque spots in the city, Regent Hong Kong offers a reimagined sanctuary by the waterfront, perfect for cherishing moments of togetherness with your loved ones.

When reserving a harbourview room or suite, you will be privy to an exclusive view of the fireworks display within this serene Personal Haven, while creating lasting memories with your loved ones.
