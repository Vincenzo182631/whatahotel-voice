# Harbourside

Buffet Hong Kong with Harbour Views

Step into Harbourside, where the energy of global food markets comes alive in the heart of Tsim Sha Tsui. This immersive dining destination offers a dynamic buffet in Hong Kong, highlighting the finest of Asian and Western flavours.

##### Harbourview Dining Hong Kong – From Sunrise to Sundown

Dine at the edge of Victoria Harbour in a serene, garden-inspired setting, with sweeping waterfront views. Whether for breakfast, lunch, or dinner, Harbourside presents a spectacular buffet lineup — from handcrafted sushi and live wok stations to hearty roasts and fresh seafood.

##### Buffet Delights in a Harbourview Setting

At Harbourside, every dish is crafted with care and served in a setting designed for comfort and connection. It’s more than just a hotel buffet in Hong Kong — it’s a celebration of global cuisine, local charm, and impeccable service.

**Attire**

Smart casual: Please refrain from wearing bathrobes, pool attire, guestroom slippers, and flip-flops.

Sleeved shirts are required while knee-length shorts are acceptable for gentlemen.
