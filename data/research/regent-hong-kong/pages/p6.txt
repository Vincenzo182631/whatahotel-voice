## Suite Collection

Each Regent Suite is a residential-style luxury retreat with a spacious living area and Oasis bathroom.

Select a breathtaking Harbourview or Seaview Suite and be privy to magnificent vistas, while basking in the luxury of space and elevated amenities, along with access to Regent Club, ​an exclusive private sanctuary with bespoke service on your terms.

## OASIS BATHROOM

The bath is reimagined and elevated to an art form within a tranquil oasis and Personal Haven for relaxation. With a luxurious and innovative design, this spacious private sanctuary is the perfect blend of calm and rejuvenation. An enticing menu of indulgent bath rituals is curated for a total immersion of the senses.

## WITH COMPLIMENTS

To enhance your Suite experience, a selection of services and refreshments are inclusive in your stay. With our compliments, enjoy laundry of three items per stay, a locally inspired welcome snack, selected beverages and local beer and pampering Day-to-Evening Ritual amenities.

## DISCOVER YOUR PERSONAL HAVEN

From your exclusive vantage point on the harbour, discover intimate spaces within your Suite, thoughtfully designed to inspire indulgent moments. Be it from the cocooning comfort of the windowfront daybed or the immersive Oasis Bath, soak up the mesmerising vista in the ultimate privacy and luxury.
