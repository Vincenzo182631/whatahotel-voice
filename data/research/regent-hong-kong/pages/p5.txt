## Signature Suites

A trio of one-of-a-kind, luxury residential retreats with private terraces and unrivalled harbourviews. Enjoy access to Regent Club, ​an exclusive private sanctuary with bespoke service on your terms.

View Brochure

## MESMERISING HARBOURVIEWS

Awaken to the first rays of sunlight glistening on Victoria Harbour as it comes to life. Then watch the enthralling framed views transform throughout the day. Whether gazing at the skyline from your private terrace or Oasis Bath, be mesmerised by architectural wonders and everchanging colours in a cinematic show that never ceases to amaze. \*Photo for reference only.​

## PERSONAL HAVENS

Feel a sense of serenity and awe, immerse in the majesty of Victoria Harbour from intimate Personal Havens in your luxurious residential style retreat. Whether lounging on the harbourview terrace or soaking in a lavish Oasis Bath for two, savour the splendour of Hong Kong from your exclusive vantage point. ​

## PRIVATE TERRACES

With sweeping views overlooking Victoria Harbour, your private terrace offers an unforgettable Hong Kong experience. From an early morning dip in the whirlpool as the sun rises over the harbour to a private dinner for small party as the skyline morphs into a dazzling “Symphony of Lights”, each moment is an entrancing sensory delight. ​
