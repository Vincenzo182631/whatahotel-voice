# Qura Bar

A Feast for the Senses with Rare Spirits and Majestic Views

Unforgettable evenings begin and end at Qura Bar with an eclectic sensory journey through a cosy lounge, a stunning bar and an intimate cigar haven. Blending the glamour of a bygone era with Art Deco and vintage accents, Qura Bar is home to a curated collection of exceptional drinks, rare spirits, cross-cultural-inspired culinary delights and electrifying harbour views.

**Opening Hours**

Sunday to Thursday – 5:30pm to 12:00am (last order for drinks 11:30pm, last order for food 10:30pm)

Friday & Saturday – 5:30pm to 1:00am (last order for drinks 00:30am, last order for food 11:30pm)

\*Operating hours are subject to change without prior notice.

**Attire**

Smart Casual: Please refrain from wearing exercise apparel, pool attire, flip-flops, sandals, and expanded plastic shoes. Sleeved shirts and closed-toed shoes are required, while knee-length shorts are acceptable for gentlemen.

**Reservations**

Reservations are open 2 months ahead of the current month. New reservations will open on the 1st of each month at 10:00am through our restaurant table booking link.

Reservations are available for up to 5 guests. For restaurant reservations for 6 guests or above, please contact us directly.

**Child Policy**

Children under 12 years old are not permitted. Guests under the age of 18 must be accompanied by an adult.

## Discover Salon De Qura. An Evolving Masterpiece.

Step into Salon de Qura, where Qura Bar unveils its exquisite new drinks menu. We invite you to the Museum Gallery of Taste, where each meticulously crafted beverage is a distinct experience, and every glass is an exhibit. Explore a curated collection of vibrant creations, designed to be savoured and appreciated for their captivating colour, refined aroma, and intriguing texture.

## The Art of Champagne Pairing

An evening of refined indulgence awaits at Qura Bar. Savour five exquisite courses thoughtfully paired with prestigious Delamotte and Salon Champagnes—from horseradish and apple cider granita to cardamom pear sorbet. Each pairing celebrates craft, terroir, and the art of champagne. Thursday, 12 November.

## Private Dining Experience

## Private Dining Experience

Qura Bar offers a bespoke private dining experience that transcends the traditional. With partial or full privatisation, guests embark on an immersive journey through distinctive spaces—from the elegant parlour and drawing room to the vibrant bar and sophisticated dining room. Ideal for intimate celebrations and product launches alike, indulge in rare spirits paired with cross-cultural-inspired culinary creations against electrifying Victoria Harbour Views within our Art Deco setting. A tailor-made experience where vintage glamour meets modern sophistication.
