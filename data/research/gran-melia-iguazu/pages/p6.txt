# Unbeatable experiences in Iguazú National Park

Iguazú National Park is located 17 km from the mouth of the Iguazú River. It forms the impressive natural border between Argentina, Brazil and Paraguay, generating a magnificent and unique sight. This natural reserve of 67 thousand hectares is one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World.

Nature’s greatest show

Gran Meliá Iguazú is the only hotel situated inside Iguazú National Park.
This natural treasure offers three circuits through 100% accessible paths for people with reduced mobility. The most famous trip is the Devil’s Throat trail: take the Rainforest Ecological train from outside the hotel, travel 82 meters towards the clouds, and feast your eyes on one of the world’s most spectacular sights. Entrance to Iguazu National Park is not included in the rate. Tickets must be purchased on the Iguazu National Park website. Hotel guests who wish to visit it have the possibility to pay it once and access it as many times as they want during their stay within opening hours.

### Under the Full Moon

This unique experience happens 5 nights every month, on full moon days, in the Iguazu National Park. You will reach the balcony of the majestic Garganta del Diablo (Devil's Throat) under the moonlight, to watch the water flowing down with the natural reflection of the full moon, while listening to the sound of the birds and nocturnal animals.

### A bird's eye view of the falls

Experience a panoramic flight over the mighty Iguazu Falls, starting the tour from Foz do Iguaçu and marveling at them from the heights. Take the best aerial shots as the helicopter flies over the marvelous falls and the jungle.

The Great Adventure captures the essence of the lguazú National Park. You will explore the jungle and enjoy the river aboard a boat to the foot of the stunning Iguazu Falls. A unique, memorable moment, seamlessly blending the magic of the jungle and the falls in a single trip.
