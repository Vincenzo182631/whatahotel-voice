### Yoga Classes

Balance your energy in the heart of the jungle. Connect your body, breath, and mind in a unique setting, with the energy of the jungle and the power of the waterfalls. Guided practice with a specialized instructor, available from Tuesday to Saturday from 8:00am to 9:00am. Exclusive activity for MeliáRewards members, with prior reservation and subject to change according to the season.

### Tango Show

Experience the true Argentine way of life through dance. Tango is the most representative symbol of cultural and declared an Intangible Cultural Heritage by UNESCO. Exclusive activity for Meliá Rewards members, with prior reservation and subject to change depending on the season.

### Art workshop

Turn the magic of Iguazu Falls into a unique work of art. This creative workshop invites you to observe and interpret the shapes and colors of nature, drawing inspiration from the jungle environment. Guided by a talented art teacher, you will experiment with acrylic paint and create your own work of art while connecting with the beauty of the place. Exclusive activity for MeliáRewards members, requires prior reservation and is subject to change according to the season.

### Mate: flavor, tradition, and identity

Savor the essence of this land with the secrets of mate, an infusion that beats in the Argentine soul. Guided by local experts, discover stories, rituals, and techniques of this cultural icon in Misiones. Exclusive activity for MeliáRewards members, requires prior booking, and is subject to seasonal changes.
