Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou
WHERE HISTORY AND MODERNITY ARE BORDERLESS
The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou is nestled in the heart of Gusu District. This hotel is located within the newly developed China Central Place Suzhou, adjacent to the historic Gusu ancient city. The luxury hotel seamlessly blends the essence of the Suzhou culture with modern sophistication, showcasing the elegant charm of Jiangnan water town. The hotel in Gusu District, Suzhou boasts 190 spacious and bright guestrooms and suites, five event and meeting venues, and distinctive dining venues, including Feng Pan Ting Lobby Lounge, Alkanna all-day dining restaurant, and Biao Xia Chinese restaurant, where guests can savor both local flavors and Western delicacies. The Ritz Carlton Club Lounge offers guests daily refreshments, along with serene views of the outdoor garden and exceptional services, ensuring a luxurious stay steeped in the unique ambiance of Jiangnan for the guests.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou. Self parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou is Sunan Shuofang International Airport (WUX). WUX is located approximately 37.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
