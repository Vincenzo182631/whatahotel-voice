Where History and Modernity Are Borderless
Experiences
The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou, offers easy access to the city's cultural treasures. From our hotel, stroll near the serene Lingering Garden and Humble Administrator’s Garden, both UNESCO sites, or explore Pingjiang Historic Street and Shantang Street, where ancient charm meets modern life. Visit the iconic Hanshan Temple and the architecturally stunning Suzhou Museum, designed by I. M. Pei. These celebrated landmarks, just moments away, offer a rich tapestry of history, culture, and natural beauty to inspire your stay.

Equipped with state-of-the-art machines, free weights, stretching areas, and weight stations, this space invites guests to embark on a journey of physical rejuvenation. The locker rooms are thoughtfully appointed with luxurious shower facilities, saunas, steam rooms, and premium amenities, ensuring a truly indulgent experience.

The Ritz-Carlton Suite
The Ritz-Carlton suite is located on the 18th floor of the hotel, with an area of 306 square meters, interpreting the art and luxury of space. The Ritz-Carlton suite features a spacious, boundless flat area, offering panoramic views of the lush ancient cityscape and Suzhou's iconic garden architecture. It ingeniously combines privacy and comfort, crafting an exclusive tranquil retreat for you. Whether it is for business negotiations or private gathering, it can be easily achieved, and make every stay a memorable and prestigious experience.

Signature Experiences
Private Cellar - Chinese Wine Gastronomy
Set in a hidden private wine cellar, this exclusive Chinese wine pairing feast offers a luxe secluded venue for intimate gatherings. Seasonal bespoke dishes pair with premium cellar Chinese wines and uncork chosen bottles. Discreet sommelier service blends fine wine and gourmet food for an elegant tranquil escape.
Rice Wine Odyssey
This rice wine feast blends exquisite Huaiyang cuisine with mellow aged rice wine, featuring tailor-made menus per guests’ preferences. Handcrafted rice wine welcome drinks are served upon arrival, with DIY rice wine cocktails available alongside elegant tea ceremonies. Curated rice wine accompanies each course, merging gourmet flavours and mixology for an immersive taste of gentle Jiangnan culinary charm.
Night Tour of Humble Administrator's Garden
Crafted around “Timeless Garden Splendor, Exclusive Jiangnan Night Views”, this private tour pairs Humble Administrator’s Garden’s millennia‑old world‑heritage legacy with The Ritz‑Carlton, Suzhou’s refined luxury. Bespoke VIP service affords guests serene nocturnal garden vistas. Merging classical Jiangnan‑garden charm with timeless brand elegance, it delivers an unrivaled night‑garden experience.
H+ Art Museum Exploration
Premium Art Experience explores Suzhou’s new landmark H+ Art Museum designed by Tadao Ando. Within walking distance, guests enjoy private guided tours, intangible heritage workshops and priority access to artist-in-residence salons. After the visit, taste exclusive artist-collaboration signature drinks and receive limited art welcome gifts for an immersive journey into poetic Jiangnan aesthetics.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou have a pool on-site?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.
