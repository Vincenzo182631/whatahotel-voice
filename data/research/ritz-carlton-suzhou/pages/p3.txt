Dining
At The Ritz-Carlton, Suzhou, every detail of your stay is crafted to inspire. The restaurants at our hotel include Chinese restaurant Biao Xia, specialty restaurant Alkanna, and Suzhou tea culture-inspired Chinese tea lounge Feng Pan Ting. Each experience celebrates the borderless blend of Suzhou’s rich heritage and modern sophistication.

Biao Xia
On the third floor, Biao Xia serves innovative Huaiyang and Jiangzhe cuisine. Named after the highest peak of the Violet Garden, this this Michelin Guide-selected restaurant in Suzhou combines classic flavors with modern techniques in an elegant atmosphere.
Alkanna
Located on the first floor, Alkanna offers international cuisine in a space that blends modern chic with timeless elegance, providing versatile dining experiences.
Feng Pan Ting
Experience Suzhou's thousand-year tea culture at this Chinese tea venue in Suzhou. Enjoy Pingtan rhythm and traditional tea ceremonies as you savor afternoon tea in a Jiangnan-inspired setting.
