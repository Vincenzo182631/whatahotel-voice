## Indulge in ten restaurants and bars

Discover culinary excellence and innovation across 10 distinct restaurants, lounges, and bars. Whether sharing casual drinks along the shoreline or enjoying a formal dinner overlooking the ocean, each exquisite setting is designed to complement our stunning island surroundings.

### Terra

Gaze across the ocean from a private treetop bamboo nest as you savor fine ingredients paired with premium Champagne.

### Li Long

Inspired by noble Shanghainese residences, Li Long pairs wood-fired Peking duck with fine teas overlooking the lagoon.

### Tasting Table

Savor international cuisines from open-theater kitchens at Tasting Table, open daily for breakfast, dinner, and scenic beachside dining.

### The Rock

Dine inside a limestone cave at The Rock, savoring exclusive wine pairing dinners around a 200-year-old Jarrah wood table.

### The Ledge by Dave Pynt

Created by Michelin-starred chef Dave Pynt, The Ledge brings Burnt Ends' famous modern Australian barbecue style to the Maldives.

### Glow

Discover seasonal Mediterranean dishes paired with fine wines at Glow, where fresh ingredients take center stage in a refined island setting.

### Peacock Alley

Unwind with ocean views, live music, signature barrel-aged cocktails, and tapas at Peacock Alley, our serene day-to-night lounge.

### Amber

Savor premium Champagnes, hand-crafted cocktails, and curated spuntini at Amber, our relaxed ocean-view Champagne bar.

### Nava

Relax at Nava pool and beach club, featuring live DJs, hand-crafted cocktails, and wood-fired pizzas in a vibrant beachside setting.

### Zuma

Inspired by vibrant izakayas, the globally-acclaimed Zuma brings contemporary Japanese cuisine, sushi, and robata to the Maldives.
