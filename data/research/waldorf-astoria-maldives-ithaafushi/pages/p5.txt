# Things to Do

Things to Do

Each day brings new adventures at Waldorf Astoria Maldives Ithaafuushi. Play on the water, relax in the sun, and go where your mood takes you with endless activities to discover.

## Pools & Cabanas

Surrounded by green foliage, four cabanas offer privacy for a day in the sun beside our oceanside pools. Enjoy a day package with two glasses of Champagne and oysters or choose from a specially crafted cabana menu.

## Daily Activities

Our daily recreation schedule is always packed with fun, relaxing activities to enrich your vacation. Choose from yoga, fitness training, sip and paint nights, and much more.

Slide 1 of 5, Tennis1/5

### Tennis

Beginners and experienced players alike are welcome for a game on the hard-surface tennis court. Enjoy a complimentary 30-minute session with our on-site tennis pro. Open daily from 7 am – 7 pm.

### Padel

Guests aged 6 and up can enjoy a fun-filled session on the padel court. Open daily from 7 am – 7 pm.

### Painting & Crafts

Indulge your creative side with Maldivian leaf origami, coconut painting, painting sessions, and more.

### Beach Games

Enjoy some friendly competition with beach football and beach volleyball. Looking to try something new? Challenge your loved ones to a game of petanque, similar to bocce ball.

### Evening Entertainment

When the sun sets, the fun is only getting started! Enjoy a white party paint and sip, movie nights under the stars, silent disco, live music, and much more.

Slide 1 of 5, Tennis1/5

## Kids & Family Activities

Discover endless fun and educational activities for kids, teens, and the whole family. Younger guests will delight in curated creative play at Stars Club, while our Kids Concierge Program provides personalized attention for children of all ages.

## Activities on the water

Discover thrilling ocean adventures set against the idyllic backdrop of the Indian Ocean. Catch world-class waves while surfing, dive beneath the surface for snorkeling, or embark on private sunset cruises and fishing excursions.

Slide 1 of 3, Water Sports1/3

### Water Sports

Discover exciting recreation and water sport activities at Waldorf Astoria Maldives Ithaafushi. Experience spectacular views of the resort and nearby reefs when you parasail above the sea or take the Seabob, our underwater scooter.

Explore Water Sports

### Ocean Excursions

Embark on a thrilling adventure as you cruise on luxury yachts and soak up the sun from stunning sandbanks. Discover secluded beaches and explore new underwater worlds with snorkeling and Scuba tours.

Explore Ocean Excursions

### Coral Conservation

Adopt a coral frame to help restore ocean beauty and support our vital conservation efforts. Using natural fragments gathered from surrounding reefs, our nursery nurtures these corals back to health before transplanting them into our flourishing Ocean Conservation Program.
