# Dining & Drinks

Dining & Drinks

Indulge your senses across 10 specialty restaurants, where culinary excellence meets modern innovation. From outdoor tables overlooking pristine beaches to formal venues boasting breathtaking views, our lounges and bars invite vibrant cocktail conversations from day to night.

## Terra

Dine in one of seven private bamboo nests suspended high above the island.  Terra delivers an unparalleled culinary journey featuring Miyazaki Wagyu beef, Alba white truffles, and premium pairings curated by the Maldives’ first resident Krug Ambassador.

Explore Terra

1/4

## Li Long

Savor signature Peking duck roasted in the Maldives’ first classic wood-fired oven. Inspired by Shanghainese noble residences,  Li Long  showcases modern Chinese cuisine, curated specialty teas, and a private lagoon-view dining room ideal for intimate family gatherings.

Explore Li Long

1/3

## Tasting Table

Discover a vibrant culinary journey at  Tasting Table, our restaurant featuring open-theater kitchens for breakfast and dinner. Savor an international menu spanning from Asia to Europe, enjoyed in our relaxing dining room, cozy indoor spaces, and beachside terrace.

Explore Tasting Table

1/3

## The Rock

Descend into a subterranean cellar crafted from Jordanian limestone. Centered around a 200-year-old Jarrah wood table,  The Rock  hosts intimate wine dinners for 12 guests, showcasing Old World classics, New World favorites, and curated pairing menus that celebrate renowned wine regions.

Explore The Rock

1/3

## The Ledge by Dave Pynt

Savor modern Australian barbecue created by Dave Pynt of Singapore’s Michelin-starred Burnt Ends. Enjoy a relaxed poolside lunch by day, and watch the space transform into an intimate night grill featuring premium cuts, fresh seafood, and custom cocktails.

Explore The Ledge

1/1

## Glow

Discover the vibrant flavors of the Mediterranean at  Glow, where fresh, seasonal ingredients take center stage. Savor thoughtfully crafted dishes, organic wine pairings, and refreshing handcrafted beverages in a relaxed, garden-to-table island setting.

Explore Glow

1/2

## Peacock Alley

Unwind to the sound of live music in our serene oceanfront lounge. Featuring handcrafted barrel-aged cocktails, fine wines, and signature tapas,  Peacock Alley  invites you to connect, relax, and share vibrant moments from day to night.

Explore Peacock Alley

1/2

## Nava

Indulge your senses at our vibrant poolside playground, where art, music, and culinary innovation unite. Relax in the welcoming ambiance of  Nava, our beach club featuring live DJs, handcrafted cocktails, wood-fired pizzas, and gourmet small bites.

Explore Nava

1/3

## Amber

Savor prestigious Champagnes and curated spuntini against a breathtaking Indian Ocean backdrop. As our signature overwater Champagne bar,  Amber  offers an exclusive selection of rare vintages, handcrafted sparkling cocktails, and light bites for the ultimate sunset toast.

Explore Amber

## Zuma

Experience contemporary Japanese dining at the globally acclaimed  Zuma. Inspired by vibrant, easygoing izakayas, the menu showcases authentic yet modern culinary artistry with signature robata, fresh sushi, and innovative kitchen creations in a stunning overwater setting.

Explore Zuma
