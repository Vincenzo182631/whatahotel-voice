# What not to miss

Discover what to taste and sip, where to slumber and venture, what experiences to enjoy, and who to connect with during your stay at Hotel d'Angleterre.

### Eat

Indulge in fresh smoked salmon in our Le 17 Restaurant. Delicately prepared, this exquisite dish showcases the finest flavours of the sea. Accompanied by impeccable service, this promises for an unforgettable dining experience.

Discover Le 17 Restaurant

### Drink

Enjoy the timeless sophistication of a classic martini. Crafted with precision and passion, we invite you to sip and savour your martini in our atmospheric Leopard Bar, with a backdrop of live music.

Discover The Leopard Bar

### Sleep

Our Bellevue Suite exudes timeless elegance with plush decor, luxurious furnishings, and an unbeatable view. Featuring bespoke amenities, this suite offers a perfect blend of comfort and sophistication.

Discover our Bellevue Suite

### Explore

Geneva is one of Europe's greenest cities, with 50 parks. Discover a green wilderness set beside glistening Lac Léman in the heart of the Swiss capital.

Join us on our Cultural Odyssey Experience, one of our sustainable experiences. Accompany Chef Alexandre Cardiga to Geneva Market to select fine ingredients, prepare a delightful meal, and enjoy it with expertly paired wines in our iconic Le 17 Restaurant.

Discover our experiences
