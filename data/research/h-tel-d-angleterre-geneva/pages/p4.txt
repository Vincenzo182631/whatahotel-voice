# DINE IN STYLE

A truly great meal demands breathtaking surroundings as well as exceptional cuisine. At Le 17, views across Lake Léman towards the distant mountains enhance dishes guided by the power of the grill and the quality of seasonal ingredients. Food and scenery come with service to match, attentive, discreet and professional. Executive Chef Alexandre Cardiga and his talented team lead a diverse culinary offering throughout the hotel, from signature plates to more relaxed dining moments, all centred on excellent ingredients and skilled preparation.

### LE 17 RESTAURANT

Panoramic views extend across Lake Léman towards Mont Blanc at Le 17 Restaurant, forming a striking backdrop to a dining experience shaped by fire and seasonality. From relaxed, ingredient-led lunches and vibrant dishes to evening plates defined by flame, the experience centres on exceptional produce, technical precision and the unmistakable depth of flavour that comes from the grill. A new expression of fire, place and elegance.

### CAREFULLY CURATED

Geneva is surrounded by vineyards. With an annual production of around 87 million litres, the canton is Switzerland’s third-largest wine producer, and an understanding of and enthusiasm for wine is part of our local heritage.

With over 225 bins in our cellar, Julien Meurillon, our Master Sommelier has created an elegant and stylish wine list that fully lives up to this tradition. Noted for its extraordinary range and breadth, our wine-loving diners certainly find their favourite bottles, but are equally delighted to be introduced to new discoveries from the list.
