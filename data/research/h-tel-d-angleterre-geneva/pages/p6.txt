### The Leopard Bar

Step into The Leopard Bar and discover one of the best cocktail spots in Geneva. With its vibrant atmosphere, creative cocktails, and live jazz sessions from top local bands, it's the perfect place to unwind. Our extensive drinks menu features fine wines, classic and innovative cocktails, and over 50 whiskies.

Pair your drink with our delicious bar menu, including fan favourites like The Leopard Bar steak sandwich and expertly crafted sushi rolls. Let our team guide you through the options for a truly memorable experience.

If you'd like to branch out after your visit to the bar, don't miss our Cigar Lounge, where you can enjoy a premium cigar menu and a stream of live music from The Leopard Bar.

## Step inside

The Leopard Bar is one of Geneva’s most famous spots, known for its vibrant atmosphere, expertly crafted cocktails, and live music. Located at Hotel d'Angleterre, it offers a perfect blend of classic charm and modern elegance. Whether you're enjoying a creative cocktail, fresh sushi, or simply soaking in the energy of the city, the Leopard Bar promises an unforgettable experience.
