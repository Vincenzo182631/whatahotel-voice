# Extraordinary experiences

Explore our collection of extraordinary experiences, exclusively curated with our treasured local partners.

### Timeless Swiss watch history

Take a special tour of the world-famous Patek Philippe Museum. The Collection focuses on watches from 1839 onwards, looking at the company’s 175-year heritage, including the Caliber 89, the most complicated watch ever created.

Available in French or in English, from Tuesday - Friday from 2 to 6pm and Saturday from 10am to 6pm. Subject to availability.

From CHF 630 for two guests, including a private guided tour and your limousine driver.

Enquire now

### Explore medieval Gruyères

Discover the medieval village of Gruyères, nestled in the picturesque Swiss landscape. Transportation to this enchanting village will be provided. After exploring its charming streets, indulge in a delicious cheese fondue or savour fresh raspberries paired with local double cream

For a longer trip, stop off at the Charlie Chaplin Museum in Vevey or the Cailler Chocolate Factory in Broc.

From CHF 1080 for two guests. Subject to availability.

Enquire now

### The perfect picnic

Your limousine driver will take you to the perfect place for your picnic. We will pack you a hamper with handcrafted sandwiches, fresh salads, seasonal fruits and cheese, and petits fours.

Add an additional indulgence with a bottle of champagne, caviar or foie gras.

From CHF 530 for two guests. Subject to availability.

Enquire now

### Tea on Lake Geneva

Step aboard a 'Bow Rider' boat for a private cruise on Lake Geneva. Indulge in a luxurious Afternoon Tea, complete with Champagne, before relaxing on the sun-drenched deck and dipping into the lake.

Available from June to September.

Subject to availability and weather conditions.

From CHF 1015 for two guests (additional guests are CHF 85; maximum six people)

Enquire now

## Sustainable experiences

### A culinary odyssey

Accompany our Executive Chef, Alexandre Cardiga, to the Geneva Market, where he will guide you through the process of selecting the finest ingredients. Then return to our kitchens, where Alexandre will share his secrets and help you to prepare a wonderful meal. Enjoy the fruits of your labour in our iconic Le 17 Restaurant, with beautifully paired wines chosen especially by our sommelier.

Available in French or in English, on Wednesdays and Saturdays.

CHF 990 for two guests, including a three-course lunch with paired wines. Subject to availability.

Discover more

### From lake to plate

Join a seasoned local Swiss fisherman on Lake Leman for a three-hour fishing tour, where you can learn the local fishing practices and land some fish.

Then take your catch back to Executive Chef Alexandre Cardiga, who will prepare a sublime dish for you to enjoy in Le 17 Restaurant overlooking the lake and the Jet d’Eau.

Subject to availability, from May to September, depending on weather conditions.

From CHF 990 for two guests.

Discover more

### Cigar masterclass

Visit the renowned Cigar Bank in Geneva, where you will be immersed in the world of cigars. You will find out what makes a truly fine cigar and learn about the different varieties.

Then, return to our stunning Cigar Lounge, where you will discover the art of lighting a cigar and taste three different cigars.

Available afternoons from Monday - Saturday, Subject to availability

CHF 180 per person. Subject to availability.

Enquire now

### Make your own watch

Become a watchmaker for a day and leave with a truly unique timepiece. In the heart of Geneva, enter the Initium Workshop, where you will learn the fundamentals of watchmaking. Then you can select the components, decide on the style and begin assembly of your watch.

Available from Monday to Saturday. Subject to availability.

Enquire now
