# A warm welcome

Presided over by charismatic General Manager Jérémie Laurent, the hotel offers a range of sumptuous, individually styled rooms and suites, all bedecked in exquisite art and elegant furnishings and complemented by marble-lined bathrooms. Elegantly poised at the edge of Lake Leman with magnificent views of the famous Jet d’Eau and Mont Blanc, this beautiful boutique hotel showcases British charm and character with the finest of Swiss hospitality.

1872

The Hotel d’Angleterre is the work of the celebrated architect Anthony Krafft, conceived and later acknowledged as a heritage site of the city of Geneva, offering 30 spacious rooms, most of which had a spectacular view of Lake Geneva and Mont Blanc.

1903

Princess Louise of Saxe stayed at Hotel d'Angleterre, and hired a French teacher Mr. Giron to tutor her children. They fell in love and she stayed in Switzerland. This resulted in the loss of her royal title. It is believed that the fifth child of the princess was conceived with Mr. Giron.

1910

In 1910 the hotel was extended by the addition of another wing, with the main entrance moved to the Rue de Monthoux. This included 70 bedrooms, a reading room, a smoking room, restaurant, terrace, elevator, electrical lighting, and rooms with bathrooms and steam heating.

1911

At this time, rooms at the hotel cost four Swiss Francs.

1960

Hotel d'Angleterre was acknowledged as one of the heritage sites of the city of Geneva.

1995

The hotel closed for 12 months for the most ambitious renovation, in which only the listed facade, the internal staircase and the floors were preserved. This gave birth to a new interior that combined a more contemporary charm, elegance and convenience with all the hotel’s traditional refinement.

2001

Hotel d'Angleterre was bought by The Red Carnation Hotel Collection.

2020

We were delighted to be voted No.2 in the Top 15 Hotels in Central Europe at the Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards.

2022

The Hotel d’Angleterre has celebrated 150 year anniversary of history and Swiss hospitality. Family owned property since its opening in 1872, Hotel d’Angleterre is one of the jewels of the Geneva hotels.

# The power of passion

"Originally built in 1872, Hotel d’Angleterre has proudly celebrated more than 150 years of refined Swiss hospitality. Over the decades, we have welcomed presidents, ministers, ambassadors and international artists — and today we continue to curate exceptional stays for discerning travellers seeking the very best of Geneva on the shores of Lake Geneva.

The hotel joined Red Carnation Hotels in 2001, adding a distinguished Swiss address to this renowned collection. I am immensely proud to work alongside team members who have been part of our story for over 30 years. At reception, it is not unusual to hear guests and colleagues greeting one another by their first names — a reflection of the genuine warmth and sense of belonging that define our hotel.

Le 17 restaurant brings an elegant touch, celebrated for its renowned Afternoon Tea and refined signature dishes crafted with the finest  locally sourced ingredients. Excellence is not an aspiration here — it is our standard.

One of my favourite moments is accompanying guests to the rooftop at sunset, where Geneva reveals itself in all its beauty and the Mont Blanc turns into a magnificient pink, before continuing the evening at the Leopard Bar — without question one of the true “places to be” in the city.

Our prime location, directly facing the iconic Jet d’Eau, places the very best of Geneva at your doorstep. A short boat ride connects you to the city centre, where you can admire the famous Flower Clock, wander through the Old Town, or visit the remarkable Patek Philippe Museum. We are equally proud to offer signature experiences, from an exclusive watchmaking lesson to create your own timepiece, to curated day trips to charming destinations such as Yvoire, Gruyères, and Lausanne."

**\- Jérémie Laurent, General Manager**
