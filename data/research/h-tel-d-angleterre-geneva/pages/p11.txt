Set on the picturesque banks of Geneva's Lake Leman, every room in this c. 1872 family-run boutique hotel offers views of the lake or Jet d'Eau, Switzerland’s largest fountain. Its 39 guest rooms and six suites are decked out with plush fabrics, colorful furnishings, and eclectic artwork. Enjoy smoked salmon with caviar and foie gras at the hotel’s lakeside Windows restaurant, followed by a guided whisky tasting—there are more than 50 kinds offered—by the fireplace at the cozy Leopard Bar Room. Remember to check out the tables, repurposed from vintage Louis Vuitton suitcases.
