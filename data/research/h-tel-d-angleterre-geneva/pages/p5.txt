# Afternoon Tea

At Hotel d'Angleterre we pay proper respect to the traditional ritual of Afternoon Tea. There's no better place to indulge in this much-loved pastime in the company of friends or family.

An array of tempting treats awaits you, including delicate finger sandwiches, freshly baked scones, and sweet homemade pastries, all accompanied by an extensive tea menu or a glass of Champagne to enhance the occasion.

### Traditional Afternoon Tea

Enjoy a selection of finger sandwiches; scones (plain, raisin and chocolate) with Gruyeres double cream and homemade jam; and delights such as fruit tartlets, Tiramisu, or a pear and pecan choux pastry.

Your tea stand will be accompanied by a choice of refreshing Betjeman and Barton teas all inspired by different regions of the world, from India and China to Great Britain and South Africa. To make it a truly luxurious affair, why not add a glass of Ruinart Champagne for an extra-special experience?

_To book, please use the calendar widget in the top right corner of the page._

### Afternoon Tea parties

Afternoon Tea is very popular for celebrations, especially for those hosting a baby shower, birthday or hen party. Where better to celebrate than opposite the graceful Jet d'Eau, in the magnificent surroundings of Hotel d’Angleterre?

If you are planning a special theme for your event, please let us know and we will be more than happy to assist you.

_To book, please use the calendar widget in the top right corner of the page._

## More tea please...

### Afternoon Tea at home

Enjoy our traditional Afternoon Tea from the comfort of your own home! Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options are available. Make your tea extra special by adding a bottle of Ruinart Champagne for CHF 128 or half a bottle for CHF 60.

**Price:** CHF 65 per person. Card payment is requested at the time of ordering.

**Collection** **:** Every day of the week. Allow 24-hours between reservation and collection.

**Delivery:** Saturdays and Sundays, between 11am and 6pm. Delivery in Geneva is charged at CHF 30.- and CHF 50.- in the Canton of Geneva.

To enquire, please send this order form to windows@dangleterrehotel.com or call us at +41 22 906 55 14

### Tuk Tuk Afternoon Tea

In partnership with Welo, explore the countryside and old town.

During the ride, you'll enjoy a delicious Afternoon Tea whilst you discover what Geneva has to offer.

**Price:** Please enquire about prices

**Duration**: 1 hour and 30 minutes

Suitable for groups of up to 4 people.

To enquire, please call +41 22 906 55 14 or email windows@dangleterrehotel.com
