### Parking

- The hotel has private parking for 42 cars.
There are 3 Electric vehicle changers available: 1. Universal, 2. Tesla, 3. Porsche

- Car parking available

- Daily valet parking fee: 47.7 EUR daily

- Valet parking in/out privileges

### EV charging

- 3 charging options available in 2 parking spaces. Tesla & Porche fast chargers with type 2 European standard connector (Menneke).
Additional wallbox connector where guest can use his own hose and single-phase schuko connection.

- Parking drop off point adjacent to entrance
