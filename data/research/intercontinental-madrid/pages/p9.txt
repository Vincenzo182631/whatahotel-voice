Reviewed by
Ramsey Qubein
Best for:
Business travelers and tourists looking for classic luxury
Location:
Smack in the middle of Paseo de la Castellana within walking distance of museums and high-end shopping in Salamanca
Can’t-miss feature:
A see-and-be-seen Sunday brunch
In a nutshell
: Classic elegance is the theme here with a focus on perfected, but not overly doting, service. Its dining scene is also a respected address in this fashionable part of town.
Although renovations are rumored, the repeat clientele appreciate the polished patina of this grande dame. The InterContinental opened in 1953 as Spain’s first international luxury hotel, having hosted celebrities from Ava Gardner and Charlton Heston to visiting heads of state with security detail in tow. Live music in the marble-lined lobby swoons well-dressed diners as they tuck into plates of house-made croquetas, fresh seafood, and sushi. An elaborate Sunday brunch draws Madrileño families, but remember this is Spain (don’t arrive before 2 p.m.). During warmer months, the alfresco interior patio offers quiet respite from the bustle outside and is popular for area business offices to entertain their clients.
The best people watching, however, is from the second-floor Club InterContinental lounge where complimentary meals and evening drinks flow freely overlooking the bustling sidewalks below. Rooms facing the main thoroughfare are the most interesting and worth the upgrade, especially if on the club level. For others, the day spa’s decadent gold and pearl facial treatment is money better spent.
Family friendly:
Not necessarily given the hushed elegance here, but the concierge stocks welcome goodies for those that do check in, including a kid-friendly guide to Madrid
