### Spetacular Royal Suite with Skyline Views

This stunning 400m² suite blends art, design, and technology. It features a spacious living room, a private dining room, a kitchenette, and a cozy bedroom with a large dressing room and a Philippe Starck-designed luxury bath. The 220m² terrace offers breathtaking views of the Madrid skyline.

### Elevate Your Health

Transform your fitness routine with our 24/7 Fitness Studio, featuring premium Technogym equipment. Personalize your workouts with tailored sessions or invigorating Pilates and yoga classes. After exercising, indulge in bespoke treatments, including relaxing massages, revitalizing facials, and our authentic Swedish sauna.

### Picasso Suite

Stay in our luxurious 155m² Signature Suite with stunning Paseo de la Castellana views. Enjoy a spacious living room, hydromassage bathtub, second bathroom, and kitchen with Nespresso. Access the InterContinental Club with private check-in, late check-out, buffet breakfast, canapés, drinks, and a private meeting room
