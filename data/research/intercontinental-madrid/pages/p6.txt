## FAQs for InterContinental Madrid

Does InterContinental Madrid have a pool?

No, InterContinental Madrid does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental Madrid?

At InterContinental Madrid, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 47.7 EUR and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 47.7 EUR .

The hotel has private parking for 42 cars.
There are 3 Electric vehicle changers available: 1. Universal, 2. Tesla, 3. Porsche

Parking fee: 47.7 EUR per day

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Madrid?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Madrid.

Pet policy description. Maximum 1 pet up to 45 kilos weight.
Pets are not allowed within public areas of the hotel, only on the path to and from the room.
Pets cannot be left unattended and must be removed from the room when serviced by the room attendant.

Pet fee per night: 40 EUR

Pet damage deposit: 250 EUR

Pet weight limit: 45

2 pets allowed

Pets allowed: Only dogs and cats allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Madrid for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Madrid have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Madrid has 1 restaurant.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Madrid?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Madrid?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Madrid.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Madrid?

Check-in at InterContinental Madrid is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Madrid offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Madrid offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Madrid from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Madrid is conveniently located near Adolfo Suarez Madrid - Barajas Airport (MAD), approximately 6.84 miles away (11 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Madrid?

InterContinental Madrid is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Tour Bernabéu - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Prado Museum - 1.8 miles away (2.9 kilometers)
- Faunia - 6.77 miles away (10.9 kilometers)
- Royal Palace - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- Sorolla Museum - 0.25 miles away (0.4 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Madrid puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
