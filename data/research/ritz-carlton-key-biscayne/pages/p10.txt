The Art of Island Living
Resort Shopping
At The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami, retail is thoughtfully transformed into an immersive experience of coastal luxury. Guests are invited to explore six distinct boutiques, each thoughtfully curated to embody the refined elegance and effortless sophistication of island living. From beachside essentials and elevated design to coveted designer pieces, indulgent skincare, and artisanal chocolate keepsakes, every detail is selected to inspire and designed to linger long after your stay.
Resort Retail
Coastal Boutique
Situated on the lobby level of The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami, Coastal Boutique is the resort's signature retail destination. Curated with sophistication and a sense of relaxed coastal living, the boutique showcases a selection of branded apparel and accessories for women, children, and men. Guests will find everything from elevated resort wear and chic designer pieces to lifestyle items that capture the essence of Key Biscayne. For our young travelers, Coastal Boutique also features thoughtfully curated children's gifts perfect for creating lasting memories beyond their stay.

Curated Collections
25 Years of Memories, Told Through Art
In celebration of 25 years on the island and the resort’s transformation, The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami has partnered with luxury travel brand TUMI and artist Louis-Nicolas Darbon to showcase a shared vision of art, design, and modern luxury. The collaboration features a limited-edition TUMI collection inspired by the resort’s serene oceanfront setting, displayed at Coastal Boutique.
Late Checkout
Displayed at both Coastal Boutique and the Spa Boutique, discover Late Checkout, an exclusive capsule collection capturing the carefree spirit of travel. Designed for both leisure and long nights, the collection features everything from classic crewnecks to cozy joggers. Each piece embodies relaxed sophistication and the joy of an endless getaway.
Kilometre Paris
This exclusive collection of embroidered tote bags celebrates the spirit of exploration, featuring hand-drawn designs inspired by the resort’s island charm.
