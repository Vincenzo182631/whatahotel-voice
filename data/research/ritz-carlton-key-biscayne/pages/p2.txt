Suites & Rooms
Our luxury accommodations in Key Biscayne, Miami reflect the coastal elegance of the island. Balconies with ocean, island, or resort views, along with spacious, light‑filled layouts, are signature features of every accommodation at The Ritz‑Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami. Residences offer full kitchens, dining rooms, and additional living spaces for an elevated stay.

The Club Level at the Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami
This exclusive lounge on the 9th floor features an endless array of beverage and culinary offerings throughout the day coupled with the finest personal service. The Club Lounge ensures that memories of your vacation are lasting and your business needs are fulfilled. Views from the lounge and adjacent Club Level guest rooms boast stunning panoramas of Biscayne Bay. From your Key Biscayne accommodation's balcony, toast the sun's descent with a glass of champagne or join us in the family entertainment with games, TV, and more.
