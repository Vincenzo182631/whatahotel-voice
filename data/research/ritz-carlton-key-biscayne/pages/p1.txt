Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami
Miami's Only Island Destination
Escape to a luxury Key Biscayne beachfront hotel that exudes the classic charm of South Florida. Located on an exclusive five-mile-long barrier island, The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami is just minutes from dynamic downtown yet offers an atmosphere of sequestered serenity. With elegant architecture inspired by the natural beauty, heritage, and relaxed spirit of the island, The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami is one of the finest resorts Florida has to offer.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami. The daily valet fee is $80.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami is Miami International Airport (MIA). MIA is located approximately 15.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
