The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay at our Key Biscayne, Miami, hotel with private Club Lounge access, dedicated Concierge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite at our Miami hotel with Club Lounge access*, plus exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
Reserve Now
Club Lounge Access
The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge is exclusively for the comfort and convenience of Club guests. Our Key Biscayne, Miami, hotel with club lounge offers access by selecting a room or suite with Club Lounge included, or by purchasing access separately after reservation confirmation. To provide the highest levels of service, we kindly restrict the use of the Club Lounge and the food and beverage presentations to registered Club guests only. Club-level accommodations allow for up to two adults and two children per room. Should you desire additional access, this may be arranged with the Concierge Team, or by clicking below.

In Good Taste
Daily Culinary Presentations
The Club Lounge features five complimentary food and beverage presentations throughout the day. Each reflects the destination through locally sourced ingredients, iconic dishes, and signature drinks. Glass candy jars offer delightful treats, including our Executive Pastry Chef’s cookies. Wine, Champagne, beer, spirits, and non‑alcoholic beverages is also available throughout the day.
Our Key Biscayne, Miami hotel with Club Lounge access provides continuous culinary offerings at the following times:
Breakfast: 7 a.m.–11 a.m.
Lunch: 12 p.m.–2:30 p.m.
Light Snack: 2:30 p.m.–4 p.m.
Hors D’Oeuvres: 5 p.m.–7:30 p.m.
Desserts and Cordials: 7:30 p.m. - 9:00 p.m.
