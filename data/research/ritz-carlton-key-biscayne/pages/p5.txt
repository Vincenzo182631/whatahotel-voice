Where Island Serenity Meets Miami Energy
Experiences
Key Biscayne remained largely undeveloped until 1947, when the Rickenbacker Causeway connected the island to Miami. Years later, Florida’s southernmost Atlantic barrier island is easily accessible yet still feels quietly tranquil and secluded. Set within this upscale enclave is The Ritz‑Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami. Approximately 25 minutes by car from Miami Beach, the resort sits near Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park, Jungle Island, and other top attractions.

Crandon Golf Course
Challenge your skills at one of Miami’s premier golf destinations. Voted one of the Top 100 Golf Resorts by Condé Nast Traveler, The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami is just minutes from Crandon Golf at Key Biscayne, a par‑72 championship layout designed by Robert von Hagge and Bruce Devlin and long regarded as one of Florida’s most beautiful and challenging courses. It formerly hosted the Royal Caribbean Classic on the PGA Tour Champions for 18 years. The course winds through mangroves and a subtropical lagoon, with seven saltwater lakes and abundant wildlife creating a distinctive test from the opening hole to the dramatic, water‑lined 18th.

Designed by a Sports Legend
Cliff Drysdale Racquet Garden
The Cliff Drysdale Racquet Garden is notable for several reasons: it’s the largest facility of its kind within a Ritz‑Carlton; it has been recognized by Tennis magazine as one of the 50 best in the country; and it is renowned for its game‑arranging services and its design by tennis legend and sports commentator Cliff Drysdale. In addition to six tennis courts, three padel courts, and four pickleball courts, the center offers clinics and lessons for players of all ages, including personalized one‑on‑one lessons.

Yes. The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami offers a complimentary, 7:00 AM-7:30 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
