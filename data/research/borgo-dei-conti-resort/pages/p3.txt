# Welcome to Borgo dei Conti Resort

Nestled in the verdant Umbrian countryside near Perugia, Italy, Borgo dei Conti Resort is a luxurious country retreat that marries noble medieval origins with modern hospitality. The resort provides an immersive experience combining the rich history of Umbria with contemporary luxury, owing to its meticulous restoration by the Spagnulo & Partners Architectural Studio. This restoration has successfully integrated local craftsmanship and Renaissance art elements, creating a unique blend of the past and present.

## **Borgo dei Conti Resort**

Accommodations at Borgo dei Conti Resort are nothing short of spectacular, featuring 40 meticulously renovated rooms and suites. Each room, including the Rossi Scotti Apartments, Conti Suite, Conte Suite, and more, offers its unique charm and contemporary luxury. Guests can expect period frescoes, coffered ceilings, and breathtaking views of the countryside or the resort's cloister, ensuring a stay that is both comfortable and historically enriching.

### **Experience Luxury and Culture at Borgo dei Conti Resort**

Guests at Borgo dei Conti Resort can indulge in an array of dining options, from the fine dining experience at Cedri Restaurant to casual meals at Osteria del Borgo, and a variety of drinks at the Lounge Bar. Additionally, the resort offers numerous activities such as outdoor cinema, romantic dinners, picnics in the olive grove, cooking classes, wine tasting, and truffle hunting. For those seeking relaxation, the luxurious spa in the villa's western wing provides both indoor and outdoor wellness experiences. The resort is ideally located for those looking to explore the cultural and historical richness of the region. Surrounded by Etruscan remnants, medieval villages, and charming hamlets, guests have numerous opportunities for cultural and outdoor pursuits. From exploring local museums to visiting farms and oil mills, Borgo dei Conti Resort offers a perfect base for discovering the treasures of Umbria.

**1\. WHAT ARE COOKIES?**

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your electronic device (PC, tablet, smartphone, etc.) while you browse a website and that provide certain information. Cookies are then sent back to the originating website during each subsequent visit, or they are sent to a different website that is able to recognize that specific cookie.

Cookies act as a memory for a website, allowing websites to recognize your device each time you visit that same site. Cookies also allow your browsing preferences to be stored, offering you a more functional experience on the websites and making the content as personalized as possible.

Web browsers allow you to exercise some control over cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies or block cookies from specific websites. Browsers can also help you delete cookies when you close the browser. However, you should keep in mind that this may mean that any opt-outs or preferences you have set on the Sites will be lost.
