## History and bullfighting passion

The bullring of Las Ventas has become one of Spain's most important bullfighting arenas. A beautiful round built in brick and decorated with tiles, which originally sheltered the cattle sold in Madrid. Discover its ins and outs in a personalized itinerary where you will learn interesting trivia about the art of bullfighting.

Take a personalized tour of the Plaza de Toros de Las Ventas with a bullfighting expert, with access to areas not accessible to the general public, such as the bullfighting gate, the chapel, the infirmary corridor, the alley and the bullring. Once you have heard various anecdotes lived in the bullring, as well as a masterclass explaining the art of bullfighting, you will visit the Museo Taurino (Bullfighting Museum). And of course, you will be able to step into the bullring where some of the most wonderful stories of Madrid have been lived. To end the experience, you will enjoy a traditional menu at La Tienta, the famous place where bullfighting figures have been celebrating their triumphs for more than 60 years.

Famous for the San Isidro Fair, today the Plaza de Las Ventas continues to be a cultural attraction for both locals and visitors to the city. And it is history that supports its popularity: it was the place where the first woman made a paseíllo, where Manolete dazzled with his skill and where Dominguín made modern bullfighting history, proclaiming himself the number one bullfighter.

Las Ventas was also the place where the writer Ernest Hemingway discovered bullfighting while working as a correspondent in the 1920s; as well as the setting where the likes of Ernesto Che Guevara, the Cuban revolution's leader, Ava Gardner, Gina Lollobrigida, John Wayne, Sofia Loren and filmmaker Orson Welles have been spotted.
