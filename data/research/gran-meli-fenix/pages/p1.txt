## Exclusivity and elegance in a hallmark of Madrid's hospitality

This hotel draws from Spanish culture and art, thanks to its location near El Prado, the National Archaeological Museum (MAN), the Reina Sofía, or the Thyssen Bornemisza. Luxury and Spanish character imbue its essence in a unique setting, surrounded by shops and businesses, next to Paseo de la Castellana or Calle Serrano.

A flagship hotel of Madrid's hospitality, located in the heart of the city's financial and commercial center. Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá represents Madrid's unstoppable pulse thanks to its proximity to the best cultural attractions, and combines tradition, haute cuisine, and an unbeatable wellness offering to elevate your stay in the capital. The combination of luxury and Spanish character makes it a true benchmark for hotels in central Madrid, and has led it to become part of the 'The Leading Hotels of the World' group.

### Madrid is culture: the Zarzuela Theater

Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá, Madrid, Spain

Located next to the Congreso de los Diputados, this is where zarzuelas became popular, famous for addressing social and political issues on stage with irony and good humor: a form of expression born in the Golden Age and designed to attract courtly audiences. Today, the Teatro de la Zarzuela hosts zarzuela, ballet, opera and concert performances.

### Seseña's handcrafted tailoring

Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá, Madrid, Spain

In the heart of Madrid, very close to Puerta del Sol, there is a special place where the most famous capes in the country have been made for the past 100 years: the Seseña workshop. Discover its essence with a guided tour by the founder's great-grandson. A unique experience where you can tour the workshop, learn about iconic collections and pieces, and discover the fascinating history of the family and the brand.

### Flamenco: art and performance

Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá, Madrid, Spain

The Corral de la Morería is one of flamenco's most special landmarks. Opened in 1956 and run by the second generation of the Rey Ávila family, it is considered the hotspot of some of the world's best shows and able to launch new talents to success. This experience allows you to enjoy a master class and a show along with the best gastronomy.

### Madrid is tradition

Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá, Madrid, Spain

La Guindalera, which was once considered Madrid's most modernist and European neighborhood, is home to the Las Ventas bullring. Originally a space dedicated to the rest of the cattle that were sold in Madrid, it eventually became the core of the bullfighting shows in Spain, as well as one of the most beautiful, built in brick and decorated with tiles.
