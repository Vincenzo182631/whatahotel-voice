# Madrid is culture: the Zarzuela Theater

## Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá, Madrid Spain

## Madrid's culture at its finest

The best zarzuela, lyric and ballet shows take place at the Teatro de la Zarzuela. Located near the Congreso de los Diputados, zarzuelas, with their humorous and ironic approach to social and political issues, became popular on its stage. A typical form of expression of the Golden Age aimed at entertaining the courtesan audience.

Designated an Asset of Cultural Interest of Madrid, the Zarzuela will be the space around which this whole experience will revolve. With the reception of the Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá as a meeting point, you will be taken to the theater and, of course, a professional of the sector will welcome you and share with you the history of this space inaugurated in 1856. From its origins to the anecdotes that have shaped its character and fame, as well as the most unknown details of the productions that have been staged here. This guide will be a member of the "Amigos de la Zarzuela" association, comprised of current and former zarzuela artists, ensuring their bond and passion for the venue. It is impossible to enjoy this wonderful theater without watching a real show, so you will have access to one of the performances currently on the bill. At the end of the show, a private car will take you back to the hotel.

- Duration: 120'

- Please note: reservation is required at least 48 hours in advance
- What is included?: Private transportation, masterclass and tickets for the show
