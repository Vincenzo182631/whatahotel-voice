# Flamenco: art and performance

## Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá, Madrid Spain

,regionOfInterest=(1000,666.5))

Connect with the destination during your stay at Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá, Madrid, Spain

## A cultural gem

The Corral de la Morería is the place with the most flamenco charm in the capital. One of the tablaos with the best flamenco shows in the world and run by the Rey Ávila family, it has been devoted to this Spanish art since 1956. Enjoy a master class and a show while savoring a delicious culinary proposal.

Upon arrival, one of the dancers will welcome you, show you every corner of this iconic flamenco venue in the capital and tell you the history that shapes it. This will be followed by a 60-minute master class in which you will learn the four cornerstones of flamenco: arms, castanets, clapping and tapping. In the evening, chef David García will serve a gastronomic proposal in the restaurant's private dining room. There, you can enjoy a tasting menu paired with 9 wine references. The evening's finale comes after dessert and right in front of the stage, at one of the best tables in the room, you will be able to enjoy a flamenco dance and singing show.

- Duration: 1 hour class / 120 1 hour and 20 minutes dinner and show.
- Number of people: Up to 8 people.
- Please note: The class will take place in the morning, the day before or the same day of the show.
- What is included?: Private flamenco class with a professional dancer, Michelin-star gastronomic experience and flamenco show.

,regionOfInterest=(1000,666.5))

Visiting this temple, where flamenco coexists with a Michelin-starred restaurant, is the most authentic way to get to know flamenco, one of the cornerstones of Spanish culture. Thus, gastronomy, singing and dancing go hand in hand night after night, attracting locals and tourists alike who cannot help but fall under its spell.

No wonder that in past decades it was often frequented by the likes of Ava Gardner, Marlene Dietrich, Ingrid Bergman, Pablo Picasso, John F. Kennedy and Maria Callas. The New York Times named this flamenco cathedral one of the "1000 places to see before you die".
