# Madrid is... picture perfect

## Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá, Madrid Spain

## A wonderful setting for your trip

Tour Madrid with a professional photographer and take the best snapshots in the capital's most emblematic settings. An exclusive experience where you will learn good tips to take the best pictures with your own cell phone. Explore Madrid in this fun yet educational way.

* * *

Your route will begin together with the photographer in the charming reception of the Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá. Subsequently, you can visit some of the most special and representative areas of Madrid: starting with the Teatro Real, the Sabatini Gardens with their white marble fountains and sculptures, the recently renovated Plaza de España and the spectacular Debod Temple. During the two hours of the experience, the photographer will reveal his own secrets to achieve the best poses and tips, showing how to use their mobile phone camera, allowing you to replicate these same techniques on your next trips.

- Duration: 120'
- Number of people: Please check.
- Please note: Delivery of a selection of 50 photographs within 72 hours for the guest to select 15. Editing of 5 selected photographs. During the 72 hours after selecting the 15 photos and the 5 units to edit, the photographer will send the final session in high and low definition options.
- What is included?: Private transportation to the Palace area, photography masterclass, professional photoshoot and drink at Café de Oriente

,regionOfInterest=(1000,666.5)),regionOfInterest=(1000,666.5))

As a finishing touch, the photoshoot will come to an end with a drink at the Café de Oriente, a café right in the heart of Madrid where some of the memorable gatherings of the Generation of '27 took place.

Here you will be able to see the pictures for the first time and choose your favorite ones before returning to the Hotel Fénix Gran Meliá by private transportation.
