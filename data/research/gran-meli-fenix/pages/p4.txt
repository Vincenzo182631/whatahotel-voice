From Tokyo to Madrid: refined Japanese cuisine

Inspired by the original Zuma in London's Knightsbridge - a concept by Rainer Beacher - Zuma brings modern Japanese cuisine to the elegant district of Salamanca. Diners can enjoy the best signature dishes and cocktails in our elegant dining area or outside on our terrace open all year round, while our private dining room is perfect for intimate get-togethers or business meetings.

## Gastronomic proposal

,regionOfInterest=(2150,1433.5))

### Fine Japanese cuisine and cocktails

Our highly experienced chefs offer a sumptuous selection of fish and vegetable-based sushi and maki rolls, perfect for sharing garnished with our famous spicy wasabi sauce. However, there is more to Zuma than just sushi – we also offer a wide selection of seasonal and signature dishes including beef tartare and fried tofu, while large groups can enjoy our special tasting menu – perfect for special occasions or business lunches.

,regionOfInterest=(2150,1433.5))

### Light bites

In a hurry or simply in the mood for something lighter? Discover our casual bar menu, featuring delicacies such as tempura, maki, and marinated cod — ideal for sharing or savoring solo. If there's room for something sweet, don’t miss our luscious cheesecake or signature chocolate truffles. Complement the experience with a Kurasshikku cocktail — a refined Japanese take on timeless classics.

,regionOfInterest=(1181,1771.5))

### Afterwork cocktails and DJ

End your working day in style with Zuma’s vibrant DJ sessions, adding an energetic vibe to your dining experience every night of the week. Take the party to the next level on weekends, with live percussion performances creating the perfect captivating atmosphere. Our skilled bartenders offer a wide range of Kurasshikku cocktails, representing a Japanese twist on classic favorites, not forgetting our special alcohol-free range.

### The restaurant

Indoor dining area
