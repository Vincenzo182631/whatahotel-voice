# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self-Parking

Andaz Xiamen houses an underground self-parking area shared with The MixC Xiamen mall. Self-parking is available at the hotel 24 hours a day. Specific car parking enquiries may be directed to your Andaz Host.
