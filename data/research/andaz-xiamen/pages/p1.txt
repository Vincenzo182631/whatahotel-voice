# Refresh Your Inspiration Through Newly Defined Amoy Touches

Created by JID, which was founded by the late celebrated Indonesian designer Jaya Ibrahim and inspired by Xiamen’s concept “Garden On The Sea,” Andaz Xiamen is a modern Nanyang-style mansion that carries the local culture as a famous hometown of overseas Chinese, while weaving in the sights, sounds and tastes of its surroundings to create a unique environment and local neighborhood-inspired experiences for guests.

## Rooms & Suites

Andaz Xiamen is home to 291 spacious and stylish rooms, including 20 suites, inspired by the local Nanyang culture. These private, thoughtfully designed spaces feature natural textures and materials such as rattan-backed armchairs, woven bamboo wall paneling and timber louvers, which create a relaxed environment where you can enjoy being you.

### No. 101 Chinese Restaurant

Named after the hotel’s address, No. 101 tropical Nanyang style design infused with “Garden On The Sea”, features local Minnan cuisine with a twist, newly defined Xiamen cuisine, as well as Hyatt’s signature Beijing duck. The vibrant all-day restaurant houses a series of open kitchens showcasing live cooking. Indulge in a semi-buffet breakfast, lunch and dinner.

### Andaz Lounge

Andaz Lounge is the skylit heart of Andaz Xiamen and a convivial central gathering space. It serves up a menu of light snacks and an elevated afternoon tea experience where you can select sweet and savory treats from a pass-around service, while tea and coffee masters work side-by-side to brew pour-over coffee and Fujian tea.
