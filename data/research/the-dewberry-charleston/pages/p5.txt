## Honoring the history of Charleston’s landmark L. Mendel Rivers Federal Building (circa 1964), The Dewberry is a mid-century modern landmark _rescued, restored,_ and _reimagined_ by visionary owner John Dewberry.

Nearly every element in this iconic Charleston hotel carries an artisan’s touch — from hand-gilded cranes in the Swanston Room to custom botanical armoires, Vermont-selected marble, vintage Danish furnishings, and a bespoke Woka brass chandelier. These details, woven together with warm service, great food and drink, and luxurious spaces, create an experience that more than stands apart, it stands alone.

- A Legendary Design Collaboration

A Charleston landmark, experience, and state of mind, The Dewberry was reimagined over eight years by a team of renowned architects and designers who shared Dewberry’s vision to present Charleston from a uniquely personal perspective. The result is a thoughtful juxtaposition of past and present that redefines what’s possible when historic preservation and imagination are paramount.

- Southern Reimagined™

Deeply embedded into every design detail, point of service, event, and moment of the guest experience, our philosophy, Southern Reimagined™ is one of joyful connection deeply rooted in and inspired by Charleston’s astonishing natural beauty and our fresh reimagining of the multitude of ways it’s reflected and expressed — through art, music, literature, cherished foodways, reinvention, things made by hand, and the kind of warmth and care that reminds us what gifts we can be to each other.
