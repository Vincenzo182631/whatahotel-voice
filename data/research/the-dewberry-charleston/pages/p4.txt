### A Page Out Of History

As part of our exclusive partnership, hotel guests will have an opportunity to secure a privately guided introduction to The Charleston Library Society. Celebrating its 275th year, it remains the oldest cultural institution in the South. The guided tour, hosted by Special Collections Librarian, Lisa Hayes, includes a visit through the Igoe Shakespeare Library, which is housed in a part of the library reserved for members by special appointment.
