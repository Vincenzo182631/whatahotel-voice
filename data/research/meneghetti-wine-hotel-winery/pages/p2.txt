## Botanicals of Istria

### Our signature rituals are shaped by the land that surrounds us. Cold-pressed olive oil from the estate, antioxidant-rich grape extracts from our vineyards, and herbs such as lavender, sage and immortelle, gathered with care, form the foundation of each treatment, supporting a philosophy of longevity rooted in nature.     Here, nature is not a concept, but a presence. To work with it requires respect, restraint and mindfulness. Each ritual unfolds as a quiet dialogue with the elements, where healing begins with awareness and deepens in stillness. At Meneghetti Spa, restoration is guided by the belief that what is grown with integrity carries a certain purity of energy, one that nurtures both body and mind over time.
