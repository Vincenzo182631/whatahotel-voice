Meneghetti Wine Hotel & Winery, Istria
The best of land and sea come together in this luxurious Istrian paradise.

In a nutshell
: Here, you don’t have to choose between vineyards, olive groves, and the sparkling Adriatic Sea—Meneghetti sits where rolling green hills dip down to meet the rocky coast, and embodies the beauty that earned Istria the nickname “the Tuscany of Croatia.”
Katie Henwood
Katie Henwood
Rooms
The first three words I heard when I arrived at Meneghetti were “Welcome to Paradise." We had just made our way up the gravel driveway leading to the picturesque stone farmhouse, majestically lined with giant pines; inside, the lobby is filled with carefully curated antiques juxtaposed with pops of modern art; outside, shaded wooden tables tuck into the leafy courtyard, inviting guests to slow down and stay a while. When the hotel’s namesake glass of wine was poured and handed to me at check in, I immediately understood the staff’s opening line.
The hotel has a total of 59 rooms and suites, residences, villas, and houses for guests to choose from, including a few options in the original farmhouse, or stancija. In the newer buildings—built in the same style as the original house—some accommodations feature a private pool and garden area, which is the perfect space for a small family, bridal suite, or honeymoon escape. My room had two floors, with an expansive living and dining space downstairs (plus a half bath) and a bedroom and full bathroom with a soaking tub upstairs, plus a private garden with lounge chairs and endless views over the vineyard.
Katie Henwood
Katie Henwood
Food and drink
Meneghetti is first and foremost a wine hotel, which means you should spend some time at the tasting room (this is open to anyone, not just guests—there were many locals hanging out with their friends and children during my visit). The Wine Lounge offers a taste of both the wines and olive oils that are made on-site, with winery tours available for those who want to go a bit deeper into the inner workings of the wine-making process.
My husband and I booked a wine and olive oil tasting at sunset, as the elevated Wine Lounge has beautiful views across the vineyards and beyond during golden hour. Once we finished, we headed back to the main area of the hotel for dinner at the Michelin-recommended Meneghetti Restaurant & Bar (the courtyard I mentioned earlier is especially romantic after dark). Everything was delicious, but the standout dish was the risotto with egg yolk and local truffles—we ordered it two nights in a row and handed the waiter back an exceptionally clean plate both times. Breakfast is also served à la carte in the courtyard each morning, though note that the risotto is unfortunately not on the menu that early in the day.
If weather permits, be sure to dine at Oliveto Restaurant & Lounge Bar, which sits beneath a shady grove of trees and overlooks the pool and vineyard. Our sunny afternoon lunch featured fresh seafood and bright, flavorful pasta, paired with a crisp white wine from the hotel’s winery and a late-summer breeze shaking the trees overhead. Again,
paradise
.
Katie Henwood
Katie Henwood
Amenities and experiences
Surrounded by lush green vineyards and olive groves, it’s hard to believe the coast is just minutes away. But every hour or so, a shuttle brings guests from the main property up a dirt road and past a sheep farm to the hotel’s private beach club. Spend the day sunbathing in a chaise lounge or cabana, swimming in the unbelievably blue Adriatic Sea, enjoying food and drinks from the well-stocked beach bar and bopping along to a live DJ set. Ask the front desk to reserve a lounger for the day—it’s free of charge for hotel guests.
Back on property, the outdoor pool sits peacefully at the edge of the vineyard next to two large oaks (one of which has a storybook-like swing hanging from it, which the youngest guests adored). Take a stroll to the back of the property to the expansive hotel garden and greenhouse, where you’ll see the produce and herbs that will likely end up on your plate come dinner time. Inside the main building there are two distinct areas of the spa—on one side, a beautiful structure with floor to ceiling windows houses the family pool, open to all ages; on the other, an adults-only pool and tranquility zone are tucked away out of sight, with a sauna and cold plunge in a private area outside.
What to do nearby
On our way to the hotel we stopped in Rovinj, a colorful seaside village with strong Venetian influence and a historic city center—only 25 minutes away by car, it makes for the perfect day trip. However, with Meneghetti's on-site winery and tasting room, driving range, tennis court, indoor and outdoor pools, plus a beach club down the road and bikes you can take out exploring, we were content to remain on property for the rest of our brief two-night stay. For guests staying longer, the hotel can arrange activities including truffle hunts, seasonal wine harvest experiences, and tours of nearby towns and historical sites. They even offer a helicopter wine tour that flies guests between three participating local vineyards for the ultimate Istrian experience.
Katie Henwood
Katie Henwood
Insider tip
Istria is often referred to as the Tuscany of Croatia, and as someone who
recently got married in the real Tuscany
, I can see why it’s called that—the rolling hills, green patchwork olive groves, and vineyards that go on for miles and miles are reminiscent of their Italian neighbors. At Meneghetti, couples getting married here can choose from multiple locations around the property to create a unique, custom experience tailored to their vision for their special day, with an expert team to help with every step of the planning process. The best part? Driving down the grand driveway at dusk in the signature gold Bentley with your new spouse. The car is included; the
Just Married
sign is optional (though encouraged!).
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Yes, including a beautiful indoor family pool
Accessibility:
Elevators in the main building provide room access, and certain residences are designed for accessibility; golf carts are also available to get around the property
Wellness:
Adults-only tranquility zone; spa with treatment rooms, sauna, and cold plunge; plus an indoor and outdoor gym, clay tennis court, and golf range
Hotel brand:
Relais & Châteaux
