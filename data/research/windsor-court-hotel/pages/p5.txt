### How does the spa at The Windsor Court compare to other luxury hotel spas in New Orleans?

The Windsor Court Spa is one of the top luxury spas in New Orleans, but it is deliberately a different kind of spa than most. Where others lean into scale and spectacle, The Windsor Court Spa offers intimate sanctuary: low-lit retreat on the hotel’s fourth floor with a discreet, residential calm you feel the moment the doors softly close. The treatment menu offers signature facials and rituals built on Augustinus Bader, 111Skin, and Lola’s Apothecary and every visit includes a plush Italian robe, steam room and infrared sauna, relaxation lounges, and a glass of champagne (if you’d like one).

### What type of spa treatments are offered at The Windsor Court Spa?

The Windsor Court Spa menu spans Facial Rituals, a Massage Collection, Wellness Rituals for the body, Nail Treatments, and Private Fitness sessions. Facials range from fully customized treatments to advanced technology rituals; massages run from Swedish and Deep Tissue to warm-stone, CBD, assisted stretching, and prenatal; and body rituals include full-body treatments built on Augustinus Bader’s TFC8® technology. Advanced treatments are built around the Sapphire 3 IonixLight, the only one of its kind in New Orleans. Personalized aromatherapy is included with every massage, and enhancements can be added to nearly any service. Feel free to tell us how you want to feel afterward, and we will point you to the right one.

### What are the spa hours at The Windsor Court?

The spa is open daily, with standard hours from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We recommend booking ahead, particularly on weekends and during festival weeks, so your preferred time and therapist are waiting for you.

### How much do spa treatments cost at The Windsor Court?

Massages at The Windsor Court Spa start at $200, and Facial Rituals range from $210 for a fully customized facial to $890 for the Ultimate Sapphire, the spa’s most comprehensive treatment. Body rituals and nail treatments are priced separately, and enhancements, from a dermaplane or an Augustinus Bader scalp treatment to cupping therapy and 111SKIN eye masks, run $30 to $100 and can be added to most services without extending your appointment time. Build the afternoon that suits you; there is no wrong way to indulge.

### Can non-hotel guests visit The Windsor Court Spa?

Yes, and locals are some of our most loyal regulars. Non-hotel guests with confirmed spa reservations have access to the steam room, sauna, and relaxation lounges, with the hotel’s rooftop saltwater pool available during spa hours. Non-hotel guests can also book afternoon tea at Le Salon to turn a treatment into a half-day or full-day escape. Consider it a standing invitation.

### Does The Windsor Court Spa accommodate bridal parties?

With pleasure. The Windsor Court Spa team plans the day end to end: coordinated group scheduling, full-day packages with facials, manicures, and pedicures, and private treatment-room buyouts for the party that wants the spa to themselves. Bring everyone and we will make it feel like the celebration it is.

### Do you need to book spa treatments in advance?

We strongly recommend reserving your treatment at The Windsor Court Spa ahead of time, especially for weekend visits, and festival weeks such as Jazz Fest, Mardi Gras, and the Sugar Bowl. Same-day appointments do open up with therapist availability, so it is always worth a call.

### What product lines does The Windsor Court Spa use?

The Windsor Court Spa works with a considered roster of the world’s most acclaimed skincare: Augustinus Bader, which anchors our Method Augustinus Bader treatments for face and body; 111Skin, behind the Celestial Black Diamond; Manucurist, the vegan, plant-based nail line from Paris, for nail care; and seasonal collections from Lola’s Apothecary. Our therapists are glad to walk you through exactly what touches your skin, you need only ask.
