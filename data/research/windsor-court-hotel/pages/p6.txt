## The Windsor Court Grand Lobby

Ten Colorado blue spruce trees donning more than 3,000 ornaments and a myriad of wreaths and garland trimming adorn our lobby during the holiday season. Our majestic centerpiece is a 19.5 foot tree boasting more than 25,000 mini white lights and a classic toy train circling the base.

## Indulge in Holiday Revelry

Our Chefs prepare yearly holiday menus for Thanksgiving, Hanukkah, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Years Eve. Additionally, experience our Reveillon menus, a beloved New Orleans holiday tradition.
