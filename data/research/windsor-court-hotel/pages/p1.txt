### What makes The Windsor Court different from other luxury hotels in New Orleans?

The Windsor Court is a luxury sanctuary in the heart of downtown New Orleans, offering a quieter, more residential alternative to the city’s more energetic hotels. We are, in spirit, a British soul in a New Orleans body, combining timeless elegance with the warmth, generosity, and joie de vivre that define our city. Arrival sets the tone: guests come in through a private walled courtyard, a rare pocket of seclusion and security in the middle of downtown, and are met by anticipatory service that has earned AAA Four Diamond and Forbes Travel Guide Four-Star recognition year after year. Much of that care comes from a long-tenured team, the familiar faces our guests come to know by name and greet again on every return. Guests enjoy some of the city’s most spacious accommodations, a museum-quality collection of British and European art, a rooftop saltwater pool, exceptional dining, and a location just one block from the French Quarter. More than anything, what sets us apart is how we host our guests, each treated with a level of care that feels both personal and poetic.

### What kind of experience can guests expect at The Windsor Court?

Guests come to The Windsor Court for luxury that feels deeply personal rather than performative. Our atmosphere is elegant without ever feeling intimidating, with thoughtful service that anticipates needs before they’re spoken. Whether you’re enjoying afternoon tea, relaxing by the rooftop pool, celebrating a special occasion, or simply returning after a day exploring New Orleans, every experience is designed to feel effortless, comfortable, and memorable. We believe true luxury isn’t about spectacle but about feeling genuinely cared for.

### Where is The Windsor Court located and how close is it to the French Quarter?

The Windsor Court is located at 300 Gravier Street in New Orleans’ Central Business District, one block from the French Quarter, the Mississippi River, and within walking distance of the Arts/Warehouse District. Guests enjoy immediate access to the city’s celebrated dining, music, shopping, and cultural attractions while returning each evening to a quiet sanctuary behind vine wrapped walls with flickering gas lanterns.

### Who is The Windsor Court best suited for?

The Windsor Court welcomes travelers who appreciate thoughtful luxury, genuine hospitality, and a sense of place. Our hotel is equally well suited for romantic getaways, family vacations, milestone celebrations, business travel, and multigenerational gatherings. Spacious accommodations, personalized service, and an atmosphere that feels refined yet welcoming allow every guest to experience New Orleans in a way that feels uniquely their own. Whether you’re visiting for the first time or returning for the tenth, you’ll always be welcomed home at The Windsor Court.

### What amenities are available to guests staying at The Windsor Court?

Guests can enjoy a wide range of amenities throughout the property. Highlights include a rooftop saltwater pool, a luxury spa, fitness center, afternoon tea service in Le Salon, award-winning dining at The Grill Room, and classic cocktails with nightly live music in The Polo Club. Guests staying on the Club Level, which occupies floors 19 through 22, enjoy the added comfort of a private lounge, while the seasonal rooftop sun deck and pool bar offer a relaxed perch above the city, and a museum-quality art collection is displayed throughout the property. In addition, personalized concierge services are available and four-legged companions are warmly welcomed and supported by attentive service that reflects The Windsor Court’s commitment to tailored indulgence.

### When is the best time to stay at The Windsor Court?

Every season offers something memorable at The Windsor Court. Spring brings festivals and beautiful weather and fall offers cultural events and comfortable temperatures for exploring the city. During the holidays, the hotel transforms with festive decor (including a 19.5-foot live tree), seasonal dining, and cherished traditions such as an annual tree lighting and festive holiday afternoon teas. Summer provides a quieter pace and exceptional value, making it an ideal time to spread out and enjoy the spacious suites and our rooftop pool. Whenever you choose to visit, our concierge team can help you experience the city like a local.

### Is The Windsor Court a good choice for families, couples, or special occasions?

Yes! The Windsor Court was designed to celebrate life’s most meaningful moments, whether you are planning a romantic weekend, a family vacation, an anniversary, a babymoon, a wedding, or simply time in a beautiful place. Our spacious suites, personalized service, award-winning dining, and luxurious atmosphere make the hotel equally welcoming for couples, families, and multigenerational gatherings. We believe every stay deserves a sense of occasion and we are honored to help create memories that you will remember forever.
