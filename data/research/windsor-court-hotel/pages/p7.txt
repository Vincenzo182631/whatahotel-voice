# Happy travels with Pampered Pets

The Windsor Court warmly welcomes your four-legged companions for a comfortable, customized stay.

We will provide your pets with the five-star standards our hotel is known for, creating stays that feel as memorable for them as they do for their humans.

Instead of a traditional pet fee, we implement a Hospitality charge of $150 per stay.

This provides for a more comprehensive suite of concierge-led services, such as:

- Pre-arrival intake, capturing each dog’s profile and preferences for a truly bespoke stay
- Amenity kit, featuring a toy and treats tailored to each dog’s preferences
- A dental kit and personalized welcome note
- Use of The Windsor Court’s bowls and bedding, plus seasonal surprises
- A pet food menu available for in-room dining
- Access to a designated pet relief area for added convenience during your stay

## Some of our Pampered Pets

Pet Policies:

Please advise us of your pet’s name and breed prior to arrival.

A non-refundable $150 pet comfort amenity per stay, for up to two pets per room, will be applied. This amenity includes all pet perks listed above, an area walking map, and extra post-departure care and preparation of the accommodations for future guests.

Additional charges may apply should inadvertent damages occur.

Dogs are prohibited from first and second floor restaurants and lounges. Pets are welcome to join us on the second floor seating area outside of The Polo Club.

Dogs must be on a leash at all times in public areas and should not be left unattended.
