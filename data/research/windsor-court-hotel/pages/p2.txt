### How large are the rooms and suites at The Windsor Court?

The Windsor Court is known for having the largest guest rooms and suites among luxury hotels in New Orleans. Guest Rooms begin at 400 square feet, Deluxe Suites at 600, Premium Suites at 800, the Two-Bedroom Corner Suite at 1,250, and the James J. Coleman, Jr. Presidential Suite spans a full 2,780 square feet. Most of our accommodations are true suites rather than single hotel rooms, with a proper entry foyer, living room, and bedroom divided by real walls and doors, a rarity among downtown New Orleans hotels, and exactly why our suites feel more like private residences than hotel rooms.

### Are the rooms at The Windsor Court really as large as advertised?

Yes, and it’s often the first thing guests remark on. The entry-level Guest Room, at 400 square feet, is among the largest standard rooms of any New Orleans luxury hotel; the Deluxe and Premium Suites outsize most competitor suites in the same price range; and the 2,780-square-foot Presidential Suite is one of the largest in the city. Spaciousness is in the architecture.

### Is The Windsor Court a boutique hotel?

No, not by room count. However, with 314 guest rooms and suites, The Windsor Court offers the amenities of a grand hotel while maintaining the warmth and personalized service of a much smaller property. Quiet by design and personal by nature, we occupy a space few hotels do: grand enough to impress, small enough in spirit to know you by name.

### What is the Club Level at The Windsor Court?

Club Level at The Windsor Court occupies floors 19 through 22 and includes a dedicated concierge and exclusive access to the Club Level Lounge, which serves a generous daily breakfast spread, traditional afternoon tea, evening cocktails and hors d’oeuvres, and nightly desserts and cordials. It is the hotel’s most elevated way to stay in every sense.

### Does The Windsor Court have a presidential suite?

Yes. The James J. Coleman Presidential Suite is a Club Level accommodation: a 2,780-square-foot top-floor residence, recently reimagined in a $2 million renovation. Expect English-garden-inspired interiors, multiple bedrooms, a formal dining room, private terraces, and panoramic views across the Mississippi River and the French Quarter. The finishing details are just as considered: a fireplace, an original Canaletto painting, a Sonos sound system, a Miele espresso machine, and a private gym. It’s designed for milestone celebrations, executive travel, and guests seeking an unforgettable residential experience in the heart of New Orleans.

### What are the best rooms at The Windsor Court for families?

Families have real options at The Windsor Court. The Club Level Two-Bedroom Corner Suite is a single-key, 1,250-square-foot suite with a four-poster king in one bedroom, two queens in the other, two full baths, a balcony, and a large wet bar. Many Deluxe and Premium Suites connect to adjoining Guest Rooms or neighboring suites to create multi-bedroom layouts for larger groups. And because our suites have true separation, living rooms divided from bedrooms by real doors, with sofa beds in the parlor, parents can put children to sleep behind a closed door and still enjoy the evening. Tell our reservations team who’s traveling with you, and we’ll map the right configuration for everyone, including your four-legged companions.

### Are ADA-accessible rooms available at The Windsor Court?

Yes. The Windsor Court offers ADA-accessible rooms with enlarged door openings, roll-in showers, grab bars in the shower and tub, and visual notifications for the hotel alarm system, door, and phone. Tell our reservations team what you need and we will match you to the room that fits you best.

### How quiet are the rooms at The Windsor Court?

One of the things guests appreciate most about The Windsor Court is how peaceful it feels. We sit in the heart of a living, musical city, so a passing note of New Orleans now and then comes with the territory; it is part of the pleasure of being here. Set just beyond the energy of the French Quarter, the hotel keeps a quieter setting while remaining within walking distance of the city’s most iconic attractions, and double-paned windows and thoughtfully designed guest rooms further enhance that sense of calm, creating a true retreat at the end of a full New Orleans day.
