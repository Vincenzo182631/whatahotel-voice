# About COMO Parrot Cay

COMO Parrot Cay is one of the smaller cays in the Turks and Caicos, located 925 kilometres southeast of Miami in an archipelago of eight principal islands with numerous hidden coves, pristine beaches and reefs. Being this far from everywhere else means the island is ultra-private, and there is room for every family to find their own stretch of sand.

### GETTING HERE

The primary airport on the Turks and Caicos Islands is Providenciales International Airport (PLS), where most of our guests arrive. The private transfer from this airport to COMO Parrot Cay will take approximately 50 minutes in total. This includes a 15-minute drive to Leeward Marina, followed by a scenic 35-minute boat transfer. **Green initiatives at COMO Parrot Cay**

### Championing Sustainability

At COMO Parrot Cay, sustainability is at the heart of everything we do. We are actively working to preserve the natural beauty of the Turks and Caicos through the restoration and preservation of our native land and seascapes. We carefully monitor our environmental impacts, engage with neighbouring communities, and remain committed to continuous improvement year after year.

### SEASONALITY IN THE CARRIBEAN

The Caribbean climate is often described as ‘perpetual June’. There is very light pollution, which means a full moon can light up the landscape. In an average year, the islands enjoy 350 days of sunshine.

## THE COMO EYE FOR DESIGN

COMO Parrot Cay was designed by British interior decorator Keith Hobbs, combining between a relaxed beach house aesthetic with a soothing contemporary style. Whitewashed interiors are flooded with daily light that illuminates soft cotton-covered chairs and teak furnishings.
