COMO Parrot Cay has complimentary cruiser bikes available on a first come basis at the bicycle barn along the Main Beach pathway. Explore the many biking trails that lead through the tropical forests, coconut and banana plantations. Bikes are available for men and women, and are a great way to explore the island at a pace that suits you.
