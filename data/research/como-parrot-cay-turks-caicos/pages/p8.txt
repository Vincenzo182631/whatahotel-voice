Boats can be charted from our resort to explore the tranquil waters surrounding Parrot Cay. These boats can be loaded with kayaks, stand-up paddles and snorkelling equipment, or just a picnic lunch and bottle of wine. Sunset cruises are great during the summer months, and whale watching for migrating humpbacks is a great option for a day trip in the winter months. The boats can be used to explore local sites too, like the Iguana Sanctuary.
