# Wellness at COMO Parrot Cay

Home/Turks and Caicos/COMO Parrot Cay/Wellness

Set across the island from the villas and suites, COMO Shamabhala has a calming spirit with treatment pavilions overlooking the North Caicos Channel, alongside a light-filled yoga pavilion, Pilates studio and Jacuzzi garden. A programme of daily wellness activities and holistic therapies makes wellbeing an integral part of island life, as much as time spent exploring its beaches and waters.

### About COMO Shambhala

Meaning 'peace' in Sanskrit, Shambhala alludes to the pursuit of balance. It defines the essence of the COMO Shambhala approach to holistic wellness, combining modern science with ancient practices to align mind, body and spirit.

Our wellness centre at COMO Parrot Cay was one of our first COMO Shambhala Retreats; it remains iconic, offering a wide range of treatments, experiences, products and activities.

Complementing these services is our signature COMO Shambhala Cuisine, featuring healthful nutrition-optimised dishes using pure ingredients in nourishing combinations.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Does COMO Parrot Cay have a spa?

Yes — COMO Parrot Cay is home to the award-winning COMO Shambhala, a luxury spa in Turks and Caicos renowned for its holistic wellness therapies and tranquil setting. Surrounded by tropical gardens and ocean views, it offers massages, body treatments, and yoga designed to restore balance, relaxation, and vitality — the essence of Caribbean wellness luxury.

#### 2What treatments and therapies are available at COMO Parrot Cay?

COMO Shambhala offers a curated range of massages, facials, and body therapies, all using natural COMO Shambhala products. Treatments are designed to restore balance, vitality, and deep relaxation, blending traditional healing with modern wellness expertise.

#### 3Are yoga and pilates sessions offered at the resort?

Yes — guests can join daily yoga and Pilates classes led by expert instructors in open-air oceanfront pavilions. Private sessions are also available, offering personalised guidance in a peaceful Caribbean setting.

#### 4Is there a fitness center in the resort?

Yes. The resort features a fully equipped fitness centre with cardio and strength-training equipment, as well as personal training and holistic wellness programmes to support an active, balanced lifestyle.

#### 5Is COMO Parrot Caya good choice for a wellness-focused holiday in Turks and Caicos?

Absolutely. COMO Parrot Cay is a luxury wellness retreat in the Caribbean, renowned for its holistic approach to well-being and award-winning COMO Shambhala Spa. Surrounded by the natural beauty of Turks and Caicos, the resort offers yoga, spa therapies, and wellness cuisine, creating a serene sanctuary where luxury and mindfulness meet.
