# COMO       Parrot Cay

Home/Turks and Caicos/COMO Parrot Cay

Set across 1,000 unspoilt acres in the Turks and Caicos, COMO Parrot Cay is a secluded private island retreat edged by four miles of white sand. Light-drenched beach villas and private residences offer space to retreat or come together, with days spent between the sea, island adventures and the restorative calm of COMO Shambhala.

View Virtual Tour

**Location**

### IN THE NORTHERN CARIBBEAN

Our private island is a 50-minute transfer from Providenciales airport: a 15-minute drive followed by a scenic 35-minute boat ride through crystal-clear waters.

### accommodation

## AT THE WATER'S EDGE

Every beach house, villa and multi-room Residence has its own private pool and direct access to our iconic white sand beach. Step outside and the day begins: sand underfoot, water close by, and room for the whole family to spread out or find a quiet corner of their own.

### Experiences

## DISCOVER THE ISLAND AND BEYOND

Sail or kayak the clear waters around Parrot Cay, explore the island's forested trails or venture onto neighbouring North Caicos. From diving and fishing to tennis and padel, there are countless ways to spend your time.

### Mangrove Kayaking

Tucked in the heart of our private island is a beautiful secret - our mangrove forest. Join us for a meditative kayak into these pristine waterways, where our expert guides will introduce you to the diverse wildlife - including turtles, herons, and egrets - which call these waters home.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Where is COMO Parrot Cay located in the Turks and Caicos?

COMO Parrot Cay is a private-island resort in the Turks and Caicos Islands, surrounded by turquoise waters and white-sand beaches. The island sits about 50 minutes from Providenciales, offering a secluded Caribbean setting just a short transfer from the main island.

#### 2How do you get to COMO Parrot Cay from Providenciales International Airport?

After landing at Providenciales International Airport (PLS), guests take a 15-minute drive to Leeward Marina, followed by a 35-minute scenic boat transfer to the island. COMO’s team arranges every detail, ensuring a smooth, seamless arrival to this private-island paradise in Turks and Caicos.

#### 3Does COMO Parrot Cay offer all-inclusive packages?

Yes — while COMO Parrot Cay is not traditionally an all-inclusive resort, it offers the Island Inclusive Getaway, a full-board package that includes daily dining, premium round-trip transfers, and selected wellness and water activities. This flexible plan lets guests enjoy an all-inclusive private-island experience in Turks and Caicos while maintaining COMO’s signature personalized approach.

#### 4What makes COMO Parrot Cay one of the Caribbean’s most sought-after private-island retreats in Turks and Caicos?

COMO Parrot Cay is an award-winning luxury private-island resort in Turks and Caicos, celebrated as one of the Caribbean’s most exclusive retreats. Known for its seclusion, five-star service, and holistic wellness philosophy, the resort offers romantic escapes for couples, family-friendly beachfront villas, and wellness experiences at the renowned COMO Shambhala Retreat. Set on over 1,000 acres with a mile of pristine beach, COMO Parrot Cay captures the essence of Caribbean barefoot luxury — tranquil, refined, and unmistakably COMO.

#### 5What makes COMO Parrot Cay a romantic getaway for couples?

COMO Parrot Cay is a romantic luxury private-island resort in Turks and Caicos, perfect for couples seeking a secluded Caribbean getaway. The resort features oceanfront villas with private pools, personalized butler service, and the holistic COMO Shambhala Retreat. Couples can enjoy candlelit beachfront dining, tranquil wellness experiences, and pristine white-sand beaches — creating an unforgettable honeymoon or anniversary escape in the Caribbean.

#### 6Is COMO Parrot Cay suitable for families traveling with children?

Yes — COMO Parrot Cay is one of the leading family-friendly resorts in Turks and Caicos, combining luxury, privacy, and personalized service on a private island. Families can stay in spacious beach houses and villas ideal for multi-generational travel, while children enjoy Play by COMO, the resort’s nature-inspired kids’ programme. Parents can relax at the COMO Shambhala Retreat or on the island’s tranquil beaches, making it an ideal Caribbean family getaway.

#### 7What private villa options and exclusive services are available at COMO Parrot Cay?

COMO Parrot Cay offers oceanfront villas and private beach houses with infinity pools, personal butlers, and direct beach access. Guests seeking privacy can book entire residences or estate homes, ideal for couples, families, or groups. Each accommodation blends Caribbean light and contemporary design, complemented by 24-hour concierge service, wellness dining, and access to a full range of spa and water-sport experiences.

#### 8Is COMO Parrot Cay committed to sustainability and eco-friendly practices?

Yes — COMO Parrot Cay is an EarthCheck Silver-certified private-island resort in Turks and Caicos, dedicated to sustainable luxury. The resort reduces waste through composting and recycling, reuses materials like crushed glass in construction, and supports marine conservation and native landscaping, offering an eco-conscious Caribbean experience without compromise on comfort.
