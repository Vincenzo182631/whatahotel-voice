# SEE THE WORLD IN A NEW LIGHT

Home/Turks and Caicos/COMO Parrot Cay/Activities/Festive

The festive spirit takes shape through barefoot days, island adventures, starlit nights and shared moments. Whether you are drawn to lively feasts and live music or prefer to end the year with yoga and nature-guided wellness, our festive programme is designed for connection and celebration.

_The schedule is subject to change._

## FESTIVE FEASTS

Gather with loved ones over dining experiences — barbecues beneath the stars, themed dinners reimagined with island flavours, and live music and DJ sets that carry the evening well into the night.

## COUNTDOWN PARTY

Raise a glass to 2027 with cocktails and artisanal canapés at COMO Beach Club, before an elegant New Year's Eve dinner at Lotus Restaurant. ## Family Activities

This festive season at COMO Parrot Cay brings moments of wonder for every age. Younger guests can enjoy imaginative activities with Play by COMO, from water adventures to beach games.

1

### Play by COMO

At COMO Parrot Cay, our Play by COMO team has crafted a fun-filled festive schedule for younger guests. From creative craft sessions to beach games, gentle yoga and cosy movie nights, it’s a season to explore, imagine and enjoy the magic of the islands.

2

### Mangrove Kayak Tour

Explore the island’s serene mangrove waterways on a guided kayak tour that reveals the quieter side of Parrot Cay. Glide through calm channels, learn about the local ecosystem and spot birdlife as you soak in the stillness of nature. A peaceful festive adventure for all ages.

Dates: December 28th and 31st, 10.00am to 3.00pm

_Reservations required._

On December 26th and 30th,  explore the mangroves with our General Manager, who will share his personal experience and knowledge.

3

### Island Soccer Match

Join us for a spirited festive match as guests and our COMO team take to the pitch together in a friendly game filled with light-hearted competition, island camaraderie and plenty of cheer.

Date: January 2nd, 3.00pm to 4.00pm

4

### Communal Movie Nights

Settle in under star-lit skies for an evening of favourite films and gentle island breeze for movie nights with family and friends for relaxed moments at the end of a sunlit day.

Dates: December 24th, 25th, 26th, 29th and January 2nd

_Reservations required._
