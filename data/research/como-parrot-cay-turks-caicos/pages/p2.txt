# Dining at COMO Parrot Cay

COMO Parrot Cay’s menus draw from the Caribbean’s seafood larder, so what arrives at the table is shaped by the waters around you. Our relaxed poolside Lotus restaurant is an easy place for the whole family to gather, straight from the beach. It serves light Caribbean-inspired dishes during the day, and contemporary Asian cuisine at night. Terrace restaurant is more formal and specialises in modern Italian cuisine, for an evening with a little more occasion.

### Kosher Package

COMO Parrot Cay offers a fully Kosher experience year-round at our private island resort. In partnership with the Chabad Rabbi of Turks and Caicos, we kosher our kitchens and collaborate closely with our chefs to develop menus with carefully sourced ingredients to meet the highest standards of Kashrut. You will also have a private chef in residence for the entirety of your stay. The Kosher Package is available for any accommodation that has a full kitchen.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1How many restaurants and bars are there at COMO Parrot Cay?

COMO Parrot Cay offers two main restaurants — Lotus, which serves Caribbean-inspired fare by day and Asian cuisine in the evening, and Terrace, featuring modern Italian dishes. Guests can also enjoy relaxed dining and cocktails at the COMO Beach Club, along with a dedicated bar offering stunning views of the Caribbean Sea

#### 2What type of cuisine is served in the resort restaurants?

Guests can enjoy a blend of Asian- and Italian-inspired dishes, alongside fresh seafood and light Caribbean fare. Each menu showcases locally sourced ingredients and COMO’s signature focus on clean, flavourful cuisine.

#### 3Are vegetarian and vegan options available?

Yes — COMO Parrot Cay offers a wide variety of vegetarian and vegan dishes, thoughtfully designed to suit different dietary preferences and lifestyles.

#### 4Does COMO Parrot Cay propose healthy food options?

Absolutely. The resort features COMO Shambhala Cuisine, offering nutrient-rich, organic, and wellness-inspired dishes designed to support balanced, mindful living.

#### 5Are children’s menus available at the restaurants?

Yes — children’s menus are available at all dining venues, featuring nourishing and delicious meals thoughtfully created for younger guests.
