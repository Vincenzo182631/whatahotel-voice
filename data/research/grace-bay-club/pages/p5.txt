# Resorts

## Grace Bay Club

Discover the timeless luxury of the iconic Grace Bay Club. Where the world’s most beautiful beach meets pristine, crystal waters. Where you’ll find exceptional amenities, innovative dining and unsurpassed service. Where beauty and sophistication blend seamlessly. A true original, Grace Bay Club is the escape for those who want it all.

VISIT GRACE BAY CLUB

## Rock House

Embodying the charm and romance of Capri and the South of France, Rock House offers a truly private and soulful escape featuring lush native landscape and pristine ocean views. Relax amongst the ancient trees and limestone cliffs that surround this secluded and exclusive setting while enjoying a full complement of resort activities.

VISIT ROCK HOUSE

## Point Grace

An intimate boutique hotel inspired by West Indian architecture, this idyllic retreat is situated on the pristine twelve-mile Grace Bay beach. This is a picturesque cottage village filled with romance and luxurious Caribbean charm. From languid moments by the pool to delicious outdoor dining, Point Grace is a calming oasis for the privileged few.

Visit Point Grace

## West Bay Club

Envelop yourself in the beachside serenity of West Bay Club. Our luxurious oceanfront resort provides a relaxed and intimate boutique setting for a family vacation getaway, as well as a serene adult-only pool that beckons couples seeking some R&R. From sunning to sailing to snorkeling, you’ll enjoy the quintessential Turks and Caicos beach experience.

VISIT WEST BAY CLUB

## SOUTH BANK

South Bank is the Newest Iconic Oceanfront Resort and marina on the southern shores of Providenciales, offering a unique blend of luxury and adventure. Spanning 31 acres of stunning oceanfront, South Bank features 92 waterfront accommodations with contemporary design and unparalleled amenities, including the island’s first ocean swimming lagoon. This extraordinary destination presents a rare lifestyle opportunity for a spirited community devoted to relaxation, boating, water sports, and exploration. South Bank is not just a resort—it’s a playground for adventurous souls seeking a sophisticated sanctuary by the sea.

Visit South Bank

## Private Villa Collection

At the luxuriously Private Villa Collection, you’ll bask in exclusive residential living, bespoke service, privacy, and every convenience including a discreet complement of staff. Overlooking the ocean, all are steps from the beach and far from the ordinary. With full access to resort amenities, these exquisite residences are the perfect setting for your special occasions and multi-generational celebrations. Here, the island is truly yours.

Visit The Private Villa Collection
