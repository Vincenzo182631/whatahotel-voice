# Welcome to JW Marriott Nashville

## Experience Unparalleled Luxury at a Downtown Nashville Hotel

JW Marriott Nashville brings an elevated sense of calm and intention to the heart of Music City, offering a stay shaped by warmth, balance, and thoughtful design. As a five-star Nashville hotel, the property provides a natural retreat steps from the energy of downtown. Travelers enjoy an immersive connection to the city’s culture, with our address placing you close to celebrated dining, creative spaces,...See More and entertainment, making us an ideal hotel near Broadway Nashville, TN. From here, guests experience the best of the area while benefiting from refined comforts that encourage restoration. The hotel’s location also positions it among leading hotels in downtown Nashville, creating seamless access to attractions in The Gulch and beyond. Families and companions appreciate the ease of staying together in one of the premier pet-friendly hotels in downtown Nashville, where every detail reflects a commitment to well‑being and genuine hospitality.See Less

## 34th Floor Steakhouse

A place to see and be seen, Bourbon Steak by Micheal Mina is a destination steakhouse for both guests and locals alike. Bourbon Steak is celebrated for its innovative twists on classic steakhouse fare, stunning and unparalleled views of the downtown skyline, and its extensive collection of vintages from around the world.

## Exquisite Panoramic Dining Experience

The Bourbon Sky Lounge, a literal extension of Bourbon Steak, offers unobstructed panoramic views of downtown Nashville by way of glass walls that open and seemingly disappear from view and usher in fresh, cool breezes.

Learn More

## The Crown Jewel

Bourbon Sky is the crown jewel of the JW Marriott Nashville and true to its name, features terrace seating that extends past the building’s edge which gives one a “floating on air” feeling, unlike anything else the city has to offer. Artfully-crafted cocktails and thoughtfully designed indoor and outdoor spaces make this lounge a signature Nashville experience.

### Rooftop Pool Deck

Relax and soak up the sun on our tropical rooftop pool deck in the heart of Music City. Facing Southwest for optimal sun exposure, our 12,000 square foot - newly reimagined - pool deck is one the largest hotel pool areas in Nashville and features a freshwater swimming pool (open year-round and heated in winter months), posh private cabanas and daybeds, and Mediterranean-inspired food with cold refreshing craft cocktails served from the dedicated Cabana Club Bar and Grill (open seasonally and weather permitting). We look forward to welcoming you soon.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Nashville?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Nashville is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Nashville allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Nashville. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Nashville?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Nashville. The daily valet fee is $66.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Nashville have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Nashville has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Nashville booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Nashville?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Nashville is Nashville International Airport (BNA). BNA is located approximately 8.2 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Nashville have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Nashville has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
