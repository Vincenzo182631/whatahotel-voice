Poolside Events

Enjoy poolside celebrations at Cabana Club this summer for holidays including Memorial Day, Independence Day and Labor Day. Our poolside bar and grill offers a wide array of seasonally inspired menu items, along with cocktails and gourmet, fresh fruit, alcoholic and non-alcoholic popsicles. Hotel Art Tours

Explore exquisite modern artwork showcased throughout our luxury hotel on a guided tour led by our knowledgeable staff. These exclusive and complimentary tours are offered to guests as well as the general public. To request a tour, please contact concierge services by calling (629) 208-4820. Each tour highlights sculptures, modern art, theater, film, and our very own hand-crafted work.

Seasonal Tasting Series

Expand your palate and discover new seasonal sips, from natural wines, local craft brews, or smooth whiskeys and bourbons. Our seasonal tasting series delve deep into our beverage program with informative discussions led by industry experts and complemented by themed light bites and spreads. Soak in the skyline views from our tropical rooftop pool deck during the summer, or cozy up in our elegant bourbon bar.

Nashville Attractions

##### Schermerhorn Symphony Center

One Symphony Place, Nashville

Home of the Nashville Symphony, the neoclassical Schermerhorn Symphony Center is among the finest acoustical venues in the world.

##### Tennessee Performing Arts Center

505 Deaderick St., Nashville

TPAC for short, the Tennessee Performing Arts Center is home to the Nashville Ballet, Nashville Opera, and Nashville Repertory Theatre, and is the Nashville stop for touring Broadway productions.

##### Music City Center

201 Fifth Avenue South, Nashville

Spanning more than two million square feet in downtown Nashville, Music City Center is a premier convention center featuring more than 100 pieces of public art.

##### Grand Ole Opry

2804 Opryland Dr, Nashville

Called “country’s most famous stage,” the Grand Ole Opry showcases a dynamic line-up of new stars, superstars, and country music legends. In addition to headline entertainment almost nightly, don’t miss three different backstage tours.

##### The Ryman Auditorium

116 Fifth Avenue North, Nashville

Known as “the Mother Church of Country Music,” the historic Ryman Auditorium is a treasured landmark and the birthplace of bluegrass. Don’t miss the chance to record a song in the Ryman Recording Studio or take a self-guided or guided backstage tour.

##### The Mall At Green Hills

2126 Abbott Martin Rd., Nashville

With more than 100 stores ranging from Tiffany & Co. to Nordstrom, the sprawling Mall at Green Hills is Nashville’s epicenter of fashion.

##### Lower Broadway

200 To 400 Block Of Broadway

Known as Lower Broad, this eclectic section of Broadway is home to fun restaurants and retailers, including Boot Country, the Boot Barn, and Tequila Cowboy.

##### Imogene + Willie

2601 12th Ave S, Nashville

A converted Nashville gas station is home to this retail hotspot for denim lovers. These vintage-soft beauties are made right onsite.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Nashville have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Nashville offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Nashville have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Nashville has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott Nashville have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa by JW is located on-site at JW Marriott Nashville. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Nashville?

Stompin Grounds Line Dancing, Johnny Cash Museum, Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park, Cheekwood Estate & Gardens, Nissan Stadium and more are nearby JW Marriott Nashville.
