# InterContinental Koh Samui ResortDestination

Overlooking the coast, InterContinental Koh Samui Resort offers views of dramatic sunsets and the forested islands of Ang Thong Marine Park. Koh Samui's coast is ringed with sandy beaches, and Lat Wanon and Namuang waterfalls are a short drive away.

## Things to do

### Nathon Town

The harbour of Nathon offers pleasant walks along the old Chinese shop houses in Middle Street. There are both Chinese and Thai temples in the town. Stroll down one of the piers and you can witness everyday life on board a local fishing boat.

### Chaweng Walking Street

Head to the main tourist destination of Eastern Koh Samui for a nightlife shopping, street food and beach party, The beach is full of interesting things to do along with a lively night market.

### Ang Thong National Marine Park

Regarded as the inspiration behind Alex Garland’s novel The Beach, Ang Thong National Marine Park is a unique archipelago of 42 luscious islands. The marine park is a protected area of more than 100 square kilometers with soaring limestone mountains, exotic jungle, and white sand beaches and is home to a rich variety of exotic wildlife and sea creatures. Enjoy snorkeling, hiking, sea kayaking, diving, sailing and simply relaxing in this truly idyllic sightseeing spot in Samui.

### Koh Tan

Koh Tan is only a few hundred meters off of Taling Nam Bay. It is a relatively small, and mostly deserted island, renowned for its exquisite corals and exotic marine life. Giant clams, fan corals and a wide variety of underwater sea life live in and flourish in the protected reefs of this crystal -clear snorkeling haven.

### Koh Phangan

A 45-minute ferry ride from Samui will take you to the shores of the beautiful beaches famous for full moon parties. Koh Phangan is the island hub of lunar parties, but you can also find quiet and tranquil spots to explore. Get ready to dance and drink the night away on Sunrise Beach and enjoy an authentic full moon party experience complete with colorful cocktail buckets and amazing fire shows.

### Grandfather and Grandmother Rocks

Add this unique tourist attraction to your list of things to see and do in Samui and explore these truly interesting rock formations with beautiful ocean views. There are lots of shops and stalls nearby where you can pick up a refreshing a scoop of coconut ice-cream or some of the island’s specialty coconut caramels, kalamae

### Big Buddha

On everyone's to see and do list in Samui is this towering and impressive golden statue. Explore this colorful temple compound by the sea with all of its intricate and unique architectural elements. During the evening you can hear the monks chanting. Get a blessing from a monk while you’re there. Visit during Songkran for a real insider experience.

### Bohput

On the northern part of the island you’ll find the beach and nightlife more low-key and relaxed. Check out Fisherman's Village -- a popular walking street that comes alive at night. At the end of the street pull up a beanbag on the sand and relax with a beer or cocktail while the kids run around and play.

### Lamai

Home to a bustling open-air night bazaar of street food, souvenirs and local crafts, spend an evening exploring this popular coastal town just south of Chaweng. Grab a relaxing massage on the beach before you head to one of the beachfront restaurants for dinner. Also in the area is the famous and quirky attraction, Hin Ta & Hin Yai the Grandfather and Grandmother rocks.

### Central Festival Samui (Chaweng)

Central Festival Samui is divided into four main areas: Bird Cage, Chaweng Port, Beach Town Market, and Fisherman Village. Its main entrance is found in Chaweng Port. The large outdoor terrace features numerous bars and coffee shops selling everything from wine to authentic Thai coffee. Bird Cage is wholly dedicated to fashion shops and is found in the mall’s central area. You’ll find over 20 retail outlets selling local and international name brands in fashion.

Open daily from 11am to 11pm.

### Dolphin Trip

Only 25 minutes by speed boat from our hotel to the Khanon area. There are four varieties of Dolphin species inhabitation these waters, most notably the Indo-Pacific Humpback Dolphin. Blood flow beneath their skin is the contributing factor in the adult dolphin's unique pink hue. These dolphin's are wonderful acrobats and you will know doubt witness this first hand. Join us on an amazing day tour to see and experience the wonderful and rare pink dolphin's.

### Canopy Adventure Zip-line

Feel like Tarzan in Samui as you sail through the canopy of the tropical rainforest. Go on a beautiful and adventurous scenic drive and walk that comes complete with a secret waterfall and swimming hole. Get ready for a proper island adventure that’s great for couples and families. Experience an activity that is definitely off the beaten track.
