## FAQs for InterContinental Koh Samui Resort

Does InterContinental Koh Samui Resort have a pool?

InterContinental Koh Samui Resort has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Rest on the edge of our elevated and peaceful infinity pool floating above the panoramic sea view of The Gulf of Thailand.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Koh Samui Resort?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Koh Samui Resort for hotel guests.

The InterContinental Koh Samui Resort offers both valet and self-parking in our adjacent garage facility. Self-parking may be accessed at the entrance of the resort. Parking is complimentary for guests.

Parking fee: 0 THB per day

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Rv

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Koh Samui Resort?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Koh Samui Resort.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Koh Samui Resort for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Koh Samui Resort have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Koh Samui Resort has 2 restaurants.

Is there free breakfast available at InterContinental Koh Samui Resort?

No, InterContinental Koh Samui Resort does not offer complimentary breakfast.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Koh Samui Resort?

Yes, InterContinental Koh Samui Resort offers a complimentary local area shuttle.

Check our Transportation page for more details.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Koh Samui Resort?

Check-in at InterContinental Koh Samui Resort is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Koh Samui Resort offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Koh Samui Resort offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Koh Samui Resort from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Koh Samui Resort is conveniently located near Koh Samui International Airport (USM), approximately 18.39 miles away (29.6 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Koh Samui Resort?

InterContinental Koh Samui Resort is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Nathon Town - 6.84 miles away (11 kilometers)
- Chaweng Walking Street - 16.16 miles away (26 kilometers)
- Ang Thong National Marine Park
- Koh Tan
- Koh Phangan
- Grandfather and Grandmother Rocks
- Big Buddha
- Bohput
- Lamai
- Central Festival Samui (Chaweng)
- Dolphin Trip
- Canopy Adventure Zip-line

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Koh Samui Resort puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
