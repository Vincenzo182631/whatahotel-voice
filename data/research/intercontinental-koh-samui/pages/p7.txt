### Island Oasis with Plenty of Activities to do

3 months ago

Your trip to the resort may take longer from the airport, but it’ll be worth it. Nested along the beach, this resort offers plenty of things to do and see. Staff are super attentive and accomodating to your dietary needs. There are 6 pools at different viewpoints, bar pool service at the main beach and plenty of food options when you get hungry, Breakfast offers a comprehensive selection to settle your sweet and savoury cravings. There are two restaurants plus an open bar experience. The activities room offers a pool room and air hockey, with books too, Near beach there’s a decent size gym with air con. And guests get complimentary reef shoes, kayaking and paddle boarding. There’s also a ping pong table. For those that like adventure, the resorts own speedboat offers great value trips. The marine Anthong National Park is superb and not to be missed with a hike, picturesque views of islands, a yummy lunch and snorkeling. Your crew are well trained and knowledgeable too. If you feel like heading out, there’s even a shuttle service which is good. But if you want more activities then you can hire a private driver or explore online for more things to do outside of the resort. We also enjoyed walking outside the resort to discover the streets further. The beach can also be walked further up, just remember it gets rocky as you get further up. We saw little fish, puffer fish, plenty of crabs in the water further up. The water is usually lukewarm in parts so check out pools if you want cooler options. The sunset views are exceptional and during off season, the storms in sky are cool for those who like watching lightning from the mainland of Thailand. Overall, one of the best resorts we’ve stayed at.

ProsSleep Quality, Room Cleanliness, Location, Amenities, recognition for ihg rewards club members

ConsConnects you to the destination

Yes, I recommend this hotel.

### A perfect stay

3 months ago

Our stay had that X factor where everything just came together to make it special. We have gone to some effort in recent years to obtain Diamond Ambassador status, and this stay proved it was worthwhile doing so. We were upgraded from an already nice room, to a really great room.
Every staff member we interacted with looked after us so well, without exception they were all friendly and helpful.
The facilities were impressive (six pools!) and the club lounge was simply outstanding. Views from the lounge, the bar, restaurant and many other parts of the property were spectacular.
We understand there isn't as much outside the resort on this side of the island, compared with over the eastern coast of Koh Samui, but it was so nice inside the grounds and our room that we did not feel the need to venture outside the resort (apart from a special trip on the hotel boat). The quiet location was a major factor in choosing to stay here, we don't like noise and are far too old for night clubs! Waking up was to the sounds of birds instead.
Oh, and housekeeping was outstanding - it is surprising how uncommon this is in many hotels we have stayed in (someone forgets a face washer or toilet roll, a minor issue but inconvenient nonetheless), here it was consistently faultless. They even went the extra mile with some impressive towel art for us.
An outstanding stay we will remember fondly.
