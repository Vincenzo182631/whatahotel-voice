### Baan Thai Spa by HARNN

Discover an award-winning spa that captures the essence of Samui, perched on a cliff overlooking the stunning Gulf of Thailand. Floating above the coconut trees, experience luxurious treatments that blend Thai therapies using the highest quality products with luscious and deep aromas. Experience an exclusive spa where you feel at home.

Learn more

### Two Bedroom Family Villas with Kids Benefits

Experience luxury for families and a group of friends in our Cliffside residence with lush tropical island views or the private ocean-facing terrace for incredible sunsets.
Guests booking two-bedroom family villa accommodation will enjoy the special welcome amenity for children, dining with parents, spa treatments for kids, and more.

Learn more

### Club InterContinental® Benefits

Treat yourself to a Club InterContinental® luxury experience with attractive benefits , A celebration of exclusivity and style, through luxurious accommodation, personalized services and invited to privilege access to Club Lounge offers guests an elevated level of hospitality from the daily afternoon tea and digestif cocktails.
