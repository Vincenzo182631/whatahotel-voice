# InterContinental Koh Samui ResortDining

Stirring views of the ocean are the backdrop for a range of enticing dining options at InterContinental Koh Samui Resort. An in-room/villa dining menu is also available 24 hours a day, with a wide selection of dishes to choose from.

1. Air Bar

### Air Bar

Experience this incredible sunset location featured in The New York Times bestseller, "1,000 Places to See Before You Die". Drink signature cocktails as you watch the Samui sunset with the calm smooth sounds of live saxophone and guitar. It’s the island’s go-to destination bar for pre-dinner drinks and sunset celebrations.
