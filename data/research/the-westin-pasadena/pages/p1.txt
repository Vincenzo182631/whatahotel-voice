# Welcome to The Westin Pasadena

## Find your balance at our Old Town Pasadena hotel

Designed to refresh your mind, body, and spirit, The Westin Pasadena is the only hotel in Old Town Pasadena, CA with a rooftop pool, offering sweeping views of Historic Old Town, Pasadena City Hall, and the San Gabriel Mountains. Relax on our patio and enjoy fresh dining throughout the day at this hotel in Pasadena, CA. Conveniently located near Old Town Pasadena, the Pasadena Convention Center, Civic Auditorium, Rose Bowl Stadium, everything you need is within reach at our pet-friendly hotel in Pasadena, CA. For meetings and events, the hotel offers 23,000 square feet of flexible space across 29 venues, including the 5,200 square foot Fountain Ballroom. Unwind in our guestrooms and suites featuring the Westin Heavenly® Bed, with many offering patio, courtyard, or mountain views. Recharge in the WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio or relax at the heated rooftop pool. Savor seasonal cuisine at Ventanas, our award winning indoor and outdoor restaurant. Travel well, sleep well, and eat well at The Westin Pasadena.

Our pool and pool deck will be closed while we complete essential repairs and enhancements. We apologize for any inconvenience and truly appreciate your patience as we work to create an even better experience for your future enjoyment.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Pasadena?

The check-in time at The Westin Pasadena is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Pasadena allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Pasadena. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Pasadena?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Pasadena. Self parking is available at The Westin Pasadena, with rates of $8.00 per hour, and $19.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $28.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Pasadena have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Pasadena has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Pasadena booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Pasadena?

The closest airport near The Westin Pasadena is Hollywood Burbank Airport (BUR). BUR is located approximately 16.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Pasadena have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Pasadena has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
