Home Classes

We want our guests to leave Curtain Bluff with more than just the memories. Bring back a new skill, a unique hobby or perhaps a few sore muscles with our revolving series of aficionado Classes led by experts right here on the property

Visit Curtain Bluff’s newly opened adults only wellness area which is a space adjacent to the spa dedicated to rejuvenation, relaxation and exercise for the body, mind and spirit. Unwind during daily yoga classes with expert trainer Girlette on the expansive yoga deck, with the ocean in front of you the lapping waves as your sound track.

Spend the day at the adults only pool where you can enjoy comfortable day beds, beverage service and serene views of Montserrat. Not to be missed is the breathtaking sunset to close out the day from this serene spot.

For active couples, work out together in the hotel’s gym or take part in joint tennis lessons lead by the resorts’ two resident pros, Dillo and Sleepy. To explore and re-energize, hikes surrounding the hotel take you from the sea level to the mountains tops with breathtaking views all around. Finally, experience ultimate relaxation with daily wellness treatments at the resorts’ award winning spa where you can unwind and sink into the laidback luxury lifestyle best found at Curtain Bluff.

## Gardening

From hibiscus arnottianus to bougainvillea peruviana, see what’s in bloom on a tour of our gardens and back-of-house nurseries. Led by our gardener, Smooth, tours leave from the lobby every Wednesday at 10:30 AM.

## Aqua    Aerobics

This low-impact workout is an excellent way to build strength while minimizing stress on your muscles and joints. Our experienced aqua aerobics instructor Samantha holds hour-long classes in the swimming pool on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays.

## Yoga

Start your day with sun salutations and rejuvenating breath on our peaceful open-air deck. Resident yoga expert Girlette leads practice at 8:00 AM every Monday, Wednesday and Thursday.

## Pilates

Engage your core and work every major muscle group while taking in the sea views. Invigorating Pilates classes are held every Tuesday, Friday and Saturday at 8:00 AM.

## Power Walk

Join our Puddy for a power walk through the village every Tuesday and Thursday morning. It’s a perfect way to jump-start your day and see a bit of the local scenery.

Available add-ons to the room rate

## Tennis    Lessons

Resident tennis pros Dillo and Clifton are trained to teach aptitudes up to 5.5 (nearly a pro) and available daily from 7:00 AM to challenge you in a match, pair guests together for a game, or give your backhand some individual attention. Their enthusiasm and gentle manner are why many of our guests call our tennis program the best in the region. Private lessons are $90 per hour, $45 per half hour.

## Private Yoga,   Pilates, and    Meditation

Build your practice and work on your personal goals for strength, flexibility and focus in a private lesson. Girlette is certified by the Mathatitu Yoga Ashram in India and has completed her 200-hour yoga teacher training program.

## Caribbean    Cooking

Experience the mouthwatering flavors of locally sourced ingredients like lobster, snapper, wahoo and mahi mahi in a hands-on cooking class led by executive chef Christophe and executive pastry chef Maurine. Classes cost $120 and are offered on Tuesday mornings. Lunch and a specially selected bottle of wine from our cellar are included.

## Wine    Tasting

Curtain Bluff boasts one of the finest wine cellars in the Caribbean, with over 25,000 expertly sourced bottles. Treat your palate to one of our private tastings, which take place in the cellar on Thursday afternoons for $80 per person.
