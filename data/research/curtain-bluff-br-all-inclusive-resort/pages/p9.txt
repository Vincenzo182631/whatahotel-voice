Curtain Bluff has earned its reputation as one of the best values in the Caribbean, with more than a dozen exciting activities fully included in your room rate. Hit the court for a set of tennis, discover the incredible sea life while snorkeling on Cade’s reef, skim across the bay on a Hobie Cat, or take an invigorating class from our rotating lineup of wellness instructors. You may also choose to upgrade your stay by adding additional services like a rejuvenating massage or a private tennis lesson with one of our resident instructors.

Activities included in the room rate:

- Tennis courts, rackets and balls
- Swimming pool with two 75-foot lap lanes
- Waterskiing (speedboat, equipment and instruction)
- Beach toys (Hobie Cats, paddle-boats, kayaks and Windsurfers)
- Snorkel equipment (fins, mask and snorkel) and reef trips
- Beach chairs, hammocks and sun floats
- Fully equipped fitness center
- Squash court, rackets and balls
- Water aerobics, yoga, and Pilates classes
- Bocce and shuffleboard courts
- Computer rooms (one for children, one for adults)
- Cee Bee Kids Club and playground
- A Creative Camp and tennis clinics for kids (dates may vary)
- Pickleball

Additional services (not included in the room rate) :

- Spa and beauty salon treatments
- Tennis and squash lessons
- Caribbean-cooking and wine-appreciation classes
- Tennis shop, spa shop and gift shop
- Off-property island excursions
