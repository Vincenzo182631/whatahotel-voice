## Ground Floor Junior Suites

Entry level room $$$$$

Located just a stone’s throw from the water on the resort’s surf beach are our 17 ground floor junior suites. The serene interiors are outfitted in soft blues and greens that mirror the adjacent water, which glitters through the floor-to-ceiling glass doors. Grand marble and white-tile bathrooms feature double sinks, expansive soaking tubs and large, open-walled showers.  Our ground floor junior suites contain one king bed, which can be converted to two twin beds. Twin beds available upon request and subject to availability.  Maximum occupancy is four persons.

Roomy bathrooms feature double sinks, large tubs, and open-walled rainforest-style showers. Our junior suites contain one king bed, which can be converted to two twin beds. Entry level room $$$$$

Housed in a Caribbean-style villa, our nine ground floor deluxe rooms open directly onto our surf beach and are just a short walk down a shaded pathway from the bar and restaurant.
