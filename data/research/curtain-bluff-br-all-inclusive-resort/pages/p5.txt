Home Dining & Drinks

Imaginative, diverse and simply delicious, the food is one of the biggest reasons our loyal guests return to Curtain Bluff year after year. It’s been praised by the likes of Harper’s Bazaar, Forbes, Fodors and Gourmet Magazine, and with all meals included in the room rate, it truly sets us apart from our competitors in value.

Breakfast and lunch are served daily in the Seagrape Restaurant, and dinner is served nightly in the Tamarind Restaurant. Seagrape is open 5 nights per week for dinner. Reservations are required. Afternoon tea is served in the library overlooking the gardens. Breakfast and lunch are served buffet, dinner is served a la carte, with reservations required for all dinners.

### The Sea Grape

At the far end of the bay beach, right on the sand and tickled by near-constant sea breezes, is the Sea Grape. This open-air establishment serves up an expansive buffet breakfast, and daily lunch offering (including our weekly beach barbecue accompanied by a steel-drum band). Bento box service is available to your cabana on the beach or by the pool from 12pm to 4 pm. Seagrape offers dinner several nights per week for adults, age 13 and over. Dinner fare is an inventive blend of Italian and Antiguan influences with dishes like yellowtail tuna carpaccio, beetroot gnocchi and fresh-caught pan-seared wahoo filet. In the evenings, smart shorts and collared shirts are recommended for gentlemen. For ladies, denim shorts are not permitted.

### The Tamarind Restaurant

In the evening, dinner tables awash in candlelight are set with a rotating five-course menu including salads, locally caught fish, outstanding meats, and so many desserts they have their own menu. After the meal, diners filter onto the terrace for dancing to live music and stargazing under the moonlight. Daytime service is casual attire. At dinner, slacks (no jeans) and a collared shirt with dress shoes are required for gentlemen. For ladies, denim shorts are not permitted.

### The Wine Cellar

## Meet The Team

Auslando has been a staff here at Curtain Bluff for some 9 years and part of the wine team for 7 years. Auslando is a Wset certificate holder and a seasoned industry vet who is very accomplished and knowledgeable in his field.

To the far right is newcomer Patrick. After a long tenure as Sandals Antigua’s head Sommelier, Patrick bring a wealth of experience to the team. His vast knowledge has been a welcome addition. He’s also a Wset Certificate holder

Not pictured is assistant Sommelier Javid. Big Javid as he’s affectionately known brings over two decades of knowledge and experience to the team which dates back to intense training under late owner Howard. His uncanny ability to understand one\`s palate and make choice recommendations makes him a true pillar and a most valuable asset. He is also a key contributor to our weekly wine tasting event.

And lastly, To the left is Head Sommelier Glouster.  Glouster is in his second tenure here which spans over 25 years. One of the original “Wine Crew” of late owner Howard Hulford, he is now responsible for leading the team and running the cellar in every aspect namely  sourcing, purchasing and  cellar and wine list management.

His wine journey has taken him to France, Spain, Hungary, just to name a few regions. He has also served in that capacity at other prestigious resort and hotels both In Antigua and Europe where he’s still based.

We look forward to hosting you with one of our many tasting, help with wine pairing or help you make that informed choice.
