Close your eyes, inhale deeply and sink into the tranquility of the exceptional Spa at Curtain Bluff.

1/ 8

‹›

‹›

Relaxing and intimate, our 5,000-square-foot spa sits apart from the rest of the resort just feet from the sea. Its four treatment rooms boast spectacular views over the water to distant Montserrat, Cades Reef, St. Kitts, Nevis, and Redonda. Before your treatment, unwind on the terrace sipping tea brewed from fresh herbs picked from the spa’s garden. Afterwards, allow your mind to wander and your body to decompress in our luxurious cliffside soaking tub over-indulgent refreshments.

Our menu of spa treatments include:

- Massage techniques including Swedish, Shiatsu and deep tissue
- Body polishes
- Herbal aromatherapy wraps
- Couples massages
- Specialty massages
- Facials, manicures, pedicures and waxing
- Wellness and fitness services
