CEE BEE Kids Camp

Home Activities CEE BEE Kids Camp

For all bookings the Cee Bee Kids Camp will accept between the ages of 3 and 10 years in resort in order to operate. The camp operates 5 days per week, usually closed Thursdays and Sundays. Over peak holidays, the camp operates 6 days per week, closing on Sundays.

Evening dinner and movie sessions are available to book at a fee over peak holiday periods.

Planning the perfect family vacation is a delicate balancing act. On one side of the scale, there’s the too-rigid itinerary that leaves kids feeling dragged along on mom and dad’s stuffy trip. On the other side, there’s the circus-like amusement park experience that leaves parents feeling anything but recharged. Curtain Bluff strikes the perfect balance between both sides, with ample activities to keep kids entertained yet within sight of the nearest hammock (which is conveniently serviced by a friendly beach concierge).

Headquartered in the shade of the palms on our calm-water beach, Cee Bee Kids Camp is home base for our younger guests. A daily activity lineup runs the gamut from crafting to crab races. For the older children and not part of the camp schedule, active pursuits like Hobie Cat sailing, snorkeling, waterskiing and court sports keep even the most energetic youngsters occupied all day. For the little ones, there’s a shaded sandbox with full jungle gym, while our game room offers a break from the sun with board games, computer games, and a wide-screen TV showing children’s favorites.

In the evening, enjoy family-friendly early dinners on the Tamarind Terrace at 6:00 PM. Later on, childcare staffers are available for hire to look after kids back in the room, freeing up parents for leisurely dinners and dancing to our nightly live bands under the stars.
