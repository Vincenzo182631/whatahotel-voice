## One Bedroom Bluff Suite

Mid level room $$$$$

Overlooking our surf beach from their perch atop the bluff are our five one- and two-bedroom bluff suites. These light and airy rooms are split across three spacious levels outfitted with natural wood and sea-grass furniture. The three-walled living room offers the best of indoor-outdoor living, opening onto a garden patio with relaxing daybed where you’ll enjoy dozing off to the sound of the waves. This room is not fully enclosed, and is open-air on the side with the garden patio. Upstairs, you’ll find a breakfast nook and dining terrace, beyond which is the bedroom with an ensuite bath and adjoining oceanfront patio.

**Child Policy**: Children under age 6 are not allowed in the one bedroom bluff suite.

Features & Amenities

- Free WiFi
- One or two bedroom options
- Personal refrigerator
- Hair dryer
- Room service
- Amazing views
- One bedroom 1,759 sq. ft. / two bedroom 2,756 sq. ft.
