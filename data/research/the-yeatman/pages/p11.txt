# Food

◆

The Yeatman aims to be the destination of choice, not only for the wine lover, but also for the gourmand and epicure.

In the classic comfort of The Yeatman restaurant, guests experience inspired cuisine with the accent on fresh local produce and on the imaginative pairing of food and wine. The Yeatman Gastronomic Restaurant at The Yeatman holds two Michelin Stars which were awarded in November 2016.

Please note that we do not recommend The Yeatman Gastronomic Restaurant for children under the age of 12 years old. The menu in The Yeatman Gastronomic Restaurant is a  tasting menu and there is no children’s menu available. In our experience, younger children prefer our more informal restaurant The Orangerie, which has a bespoke children's menu available.

Dick's Bar & Bistro serves light dishes and sandwiches, or even main dishes for lunch with its wide choice of cocktails, wines and Ports.

The pool lounge provides a relaxed contemporary ambience in which to enjoy a healthy light meal or gourmet snack while taking in the spectacular panorama of the city.

A number of private dining options are available. A romantic al fresco meal can be served on a guest's own private terrace. An exclusive private dining room evoking the atmosphere of a wine cellar provides a memorable setting for an intimate dinner or reception.

Smoking is not allowed in the public areas of the hotel.
