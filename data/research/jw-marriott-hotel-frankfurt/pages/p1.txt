# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt

## Discover our urban retreat with holistic wellbeing in mind

Among Frankfurt luxury hotels, JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt is a serene haven where urban energy is balanced by a natural and uplifting atmosphere. Featuring 218 spacious rooms and suites with spa-inspired bathrooms, our Frankfurt city hotel has been mindfully redesigned for modern living, with spectacular skyline views from higher floors. A place to come together and be present, our family hotel in...See More Frankfurt offers personalized experiences focused on holistic wellbeing. Richly positioned for business and leisure, the hotel sits in Frankfurt’s financial district with direct access to MyZeil shopping center, smooth connections to public transport, major attractions and the main riverbank, and Frankfurt International Airport around 20 minutes away by car. Guests benefit from downtime at Harmony Spa, complimentary entry to Fitness First, and lively dining at Latin American-inspired GRETA OTO Frankfurt.See Less

Premium Accommodation

## Distinctly Elevated

Venture beyond expectation with our collection of premium suites, where guests enjoy luxurious amenities, such as delicious chocolates, on arrival and complimentary minibar. Located on the building’s highest floors, each space features ceiling-high windows, strategically placed to maximize on spectacular views across Mainhattan. Presenting a variety of set-ups, from the open-plan Junior Suite to the exceptional Presidential Suites, every detail is mindfully styled with holistic living and positive energy in mind. Light-filled and airy, the interiors feature natural materials, organic shapes and splashes of color that effortlessly harmonize to create a welcome haven, sitting high above the city.

Guestrooms & Suites

## Urban Retreat

A sanctuary placed high above the thriving city, each of our 218 rooms and suites offer a natural, residential space that encourages connection. Considered to be amongst the city’s most generously sized accommodation, every floorplan is styled to reflect both the principles of mindfulness and convenience of modern living.

Experience the Mainhattan skyline to the fullest from floor-to-ceiling windows, situated a minimum of 80-metres above ground level. Intentional design makes holistic wellbeing central, blending warmth, luxury and insight through the combination of organic textiles, botanical plants, contemporary technology and original artworks. Crafting a haven for relaxation, the bed features custom curvaceous headboard that serves to cocoon and protect. Softly lit and elegantly appointed, bathrooms are reminiscent of a spa environment where neutral tones and chrome fixtures create a serene and uplifting setting, primed to replenish the spirit.

Destination

## Discover Frankfurt

Stay at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt and discover the wonders of this innovative, modern city. Supremely located, the hotel is in the heart of Main metropolis, allowing easy access to a kaleidoscope of experiences and activities. Just a short stroll from premium shopping destination, Goethestraße, it is ideal for those seeking retail time nearby, while New Old Town is only minutes away, as is the financial district and River Main. Clustered within the city is an ensemble of museums for the culturally inquisitive, while Alter Oper and Oper Frankfurt will appeal to those who adore music. Renowned as the meeting point for international travelers, Frankfurt Airport is only 20 minutes’ drive from the hotel and is well-linked to the main train station.

## Stay Active

A city retreat designed for holistic wellbeing and invigoration of mind and body, JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt has partnered with Fitness First to offer guests complimentary direct access to its select training facility. Reached via the sixth floor, the studio houses an extensive suite of gym equipment, alongside a 20-meter indoor pool with relaxation area. In addition, guests benefit from a motivating schedule of over 60 classes every week, ranging from high intensity sessions with HYROX and Les Mills, to low impact Zumba and balancing yoga.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt. One pet is allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of €50.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt, with rates of €5.50 per hour, and €45.00 per day. Valet parking is available for €45.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt is Frankfurt Airport (FRA). FRA is located approximately 21.3 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
