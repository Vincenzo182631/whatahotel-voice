Greta Oto Frankfurt

## Signature Amazonian Experience

A harmonious blend of grace, beauty and vitality, GRETA OTO Frankfurt is the city’s destination for delicious South American cuisine. Located on the first floor of JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt, the captivating restaurant is accessed via a sweeping staircase that ascends from GRETA OTO Bar. Step into a stylish space, where every detail, from recurring butterfly motifs to fluidly shaped banquette seating, is curated to reflect the bounty of the Amazon.

Crafted to delight diners with tantalizing flavors and rich traditions, skilled chefs concoct enticing dishes using modern techniques and international influences. Made for sharing, each choice showcases the restaurant’s ethos for convivial gastronomy that piques curiosity and nurtures the love of food.

Greta Oto Bar

## Inventive Mixology

Indulge intrigue at the GRETA OTO Bar, where a Hispanic-influenced menu of cocktails provides an exciting taste sensation. Located in the hotel lobby, this convivial setting offers the freedom to come together and connect over signature drinks crafted by talented mixologists.
