# Spacious guestrooms and Frankfurt suites

## Accommodations

Subtle shapes, soft materials and luxurious furnishings welcome guests as they enter one of the 218 beautifully renovated rooms and suites at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt. As one of the finest options for Frankfurt accommodation, each space offers either sweeping views of the city or the River Main, designed with wellbeing and deep comfort in mind. The materials are rich and textural, with lighting...See More and elegant fittings engineered for a custom experience. Every detail has been tailored to soothe mind, body and soul, from cocooning curved headboards and cushioned furnishings that soften the sounds of the city, to en-suite bathrooms styled with the serenity of a high-end spa.See Less

Design Story

## Heightened Connections

Thoughtfully renovated, JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt’s guestrooms and suites bring high-end comfort to the Main metropolis. With holistic wellbeing in mind, soft materials, natural tones and curvaceous furniture all combine to present a home for mind, body and soul. Every detail of the interiors is tailored towards positive energy and adaptive simplicity. Decorated with original artworks, created exclusively by Hartwig Ebersbach, there’s also a focus on the use of luxurious natural materials, like fine woods, valuable textiles and bespoke fittings.

Amenities

## Quality Lifestyle

Supremely spacious, our portfolio of guestrooms and suites starts at 33m2, making these spaces some of the city’s most generously sized accommodation. Towering high above the buzz of Frankfurt, each setting combines exceptional views with a luxurious backdrop, furnished with premium amenities, including Nespresso coffee machine.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt have a desk for working.

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt?
