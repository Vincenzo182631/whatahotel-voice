Natural Connection

## Empowered Wellbeing

Come together at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt, where the dynamic essence of Germany’s financial capital is translated into a mindful space that nurtures wellbeing. Focused on cultivating a sense of balance, the hotel is designed to harness the power of simplicity with integrated green elements and relaxation nooks, where meditative soundtracks can be accessed via QR code, providing an anchor to the natural world.

Harmony Spa

## City Center Wellbeing

A space to recenter and find inner balance, Harmony Spa, located on the sixth floor brings a range of expert treatments to Frankfurt’s bustling metropolis. Take time to unwind in the separate male and female saunas and select from a diverse menu of therapies, using premium products by Sothys, designed to rejuvenate body and soul.

Holistic Wellbeing

### Focus on Mind, Body and Spirit

Leaning into balancing lifestyle with wellbeing, JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt has partnered with Fitness First enabling guests to revitalize and nourish body and soul. Whether you’re looking to swim laps in the 20m indoor pool, or relax in the steam bath and sauna, the host of facilities ensures complete rest and rejuvenation.

Experience Frankfurt

### Go Shopping

Explore the city’s lively retail scene with JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt as your gateway. Just minutes away, Goethestraße offers luxury boutiques, while MyZeil, directly connected to the hotel, impresses with modern architecture and an expansive selection of stores. With both destinations close by, guests can indulge in world-class shopping while staying at the heart of the metropolis.

### Cultural Hub

A rich tapestry of over 39 cultural institutions awaits at Frankfurt’s Museum Embankment, a short stroll from the hotel. Immerse yourself in masterpieces at Städel and Schirn Kunsthalle Frankfurt, uncover ancient art at Liebieghaus, or step into the life of the literary legend at Goethe-Haus. Nearby, the Museum für Moderne Kunst and Senckenberg Museum offer even more to discover for art and history enthusiasts.

### City Exploration

Discover Frankfurt’s varied landscape, where contemporary skyscrapers stand alongside iconic historic landmarks. With JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt as your base, wander through New Old Town’s cobbled streets, sample local delicacies at Kleinmarkthalle, or unwind in the lush Palmengarten. Flowing through the city, the picturesque River Main provides a natural draw, where great views create magical moments.

JW Market

## Feed Your Soul

An adaptable space where guests can connect with each other and all that Frankfurt has to offer, JW Market brings together the convenience of a store, with a welcoming café concept. Purchase regional produce, such as our honey, apple wine and local vintages and enjoy a menu of drinks, snacks and light meals, available to-go or while seated overlooking the JW Garden.

### Feed Your Wanderlust

JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt is ideally located for immersive discovery in this city of many guises. Frankfurt is rich in art, culture, nature and retail opportunities, all within easy walking distance from the hotel.

Serene Moments

### Savor Time

Be present in the here and now, away from the bustle of the city and find calm and relaxation in the hotel’s serene spaces. Carve out respite, find time for self-reflection and embrace bliss and tranquility.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-11:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Physio Area, Power Plate, Hammer Strength Training Center, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt have an on-site spa?

Yes, Harmony Spa is located on-site at JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt?

Palm Tree Garden, Frankfurt Zoological Garden, Riverside Museums, Goethe House, Römer - City Hall and more are nearby JW Marriott Hotel Frankfurt.
