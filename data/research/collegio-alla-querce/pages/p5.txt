# La Gamella

The restaurant at the heart of this much loved 100 year old institution thoughtfully merges the raw beauty of the Tuscan countryside with effortless Italian style. A place for all seasons, the restaurant uses locally sourced ingredients, local wines and local heritage to honor our unique proximity to the Tuscan countryside while presenting a menu of regional Italian dishes.

## Il Pranzo Della Domenica

From October 11th, Il Pranzo della Domenica brings family and friends together at La Gamella for a generous Italian lunch, inspired by the tradition of long Sundays spent around the table. The experience unfolds around the open kitchen, with a selection of antipasti, seasonal dishes and live cooking stations, alongside a generous buffet arranged across the kitchen island.
