This holiday season, revel in the company of acclaimed chefs, designers, musicians, and makers. At the world's most inspiring destinations, Auberge Collection makes an art of one-of-a-kind festive moments. From November through January, the festive season comes to life across Collegio alla Querce. Il Gran Circolo brings evenings of classic games and entertainment to the historic Il Teatro, while festive Afternoon Tea in Conservatorio, live music, culinary experiences and intimate winter rituals fill the season with moments to gather and celebrate, culminating in Christmas and a spectacular New Year’s Eve overlooking Florence.

# Il Gran Circolo

Inspired by the tradition of Italy’s historic social and gaming salons, Il Gran Circolo transforms Il Teatro into a spirited setting for evenings of play and entertainment. Blackjack and roulette with professional croupiers unfold alongside live music, festive cocktails and bespoke Collegio alla Querce playing cards created in collaboration with Dal Negro. With exclusive prizes to be won, guests are invited to gather around the tables and play simply for the pleasure of the game.

### Christmas Eve Dinner

Celebrate Christmas Eve at La Gamella with a four-course menu inspired by the flavours and traditions of an Italian Christmas. A festive evening designed to bring family and friends together around the table, in the warm setting of Collegio alla Querce.

-

### Christmas Day Brunch

Christmas Day is made for gathering. At La Gamella, a generous festive brunch unfolds around the open kitchen island, with live cooking stations, seasonal favourites and the warmth of Italian conviviality.

-

### New Year’s Eve

As the final evening of the year unfolds, La Gamella sets the scene for a memorable New Year’s Eve, beginning with Champagne and a celebratory tasting menu accompanied by live entertainment. At midnight, fireworks illuminate the gardens and the Florence skyline, before the celebration moves to Il Teatro for an after-party of classic games, music and cocktails into the early hours.

### Christmas Gospel Evenings

The evening begins with a festive welcome in Conservatorio before guests take their seats in the historic Il Teatro for a live Gospel performance. Christmas classics and soulful Gospel arrangements fill the theatre, bringing the spirit of the season to life through music, warmth and togetherness.

-

### Four Hands Dinner with Yoji Tokuyoshi \| December 11th

The second chapter of our Four Hands Dinner series welcomes Chef Yoji Tokuyoshi to La Gamella for a one-night culinary collaboration with Chef Nicola Zamperetti. Together, the two chefs explore the dialogue between Italian ingredients and Japanese sensibility through a menu shaped by seasonality, precision and creativity, bringing their distinct culinary perspectives together for one special evening.

-

### Festive Afternoon Tea \| Monday to Saturday

Beneath the vaulted ceilings of Conservatorio, Collegio alla Querce’s signature Afternoon Tea takes on a festive expression in collaboration with LILU Matcha, bringing together Tuscan flavours and the ritual of ceremonial matcha. An invitation to slow down, gather and savour an unexpected meeting of traditions.

-

### The Winter Pour

Guided by the Bar Bertelli team, guests discover the art of preparing Vin Brûlé, from selecting the wine to finding the perfect balance of spices, citrus and botanicals. The experience concludes around the table, enjoying their creation alongside regional cheeses, cured meats and seasonal bites.

-

### The Gingerbread Workshop

Guided by our Pastry Chef, this intimate workshop invites guests of all ages to create and decorate their own gingerbread, combining festive creativity with the joy of making something by hand. A playful seasonal tradition to enjoy together at Collegio alla Querce.

-

### The 10PM Ritual

A tasting of artisanal Panettone is thoughtfully paired with a selection of Tuscan dessert wines and digestifs, creating a simple yet distinctive way to end the evening. Set within the historic surroundings of Bar Bertelli, it is a moment for conversation, connection and the pleasure of an unhurried night at Collegio.
