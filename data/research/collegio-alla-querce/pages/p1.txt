## Stay in Harmonious Design

Realizing the vision of owner Analjit Singh, Collegio alla Querce feels like a relaxed yet refined country house within Firenze. The Renaissance-era villa has been transformed over time into a boarding school before its latest reimagination into an 83-room hotel featuring 34 suites and the Florentine chic 2,250-square-foot signature Penthouse la Quercia, set within the private Palazzo Moderno.

## A Fresh Perspective on Time-honored Culinary Traditions

A captivating dining destination, Collegio alla Querce celebrates old-world Italian allure with comfortable modern flair in style and experience. A journey through Italy, the varied dining venues include a destination restaurant, a glass-enclosed garden room for all day dining, a sophisticated bar and cigar lounge set in the former academic admissions office, and an outdoor pool bar offering lunch and cocktails nestled amongst the old growth olives and soaring oak trees.

## Terrazza della Piscina

Stroll through ascending terrazza gardens to find the garden Il Bosco and pool bar where aperitif-style cocktails, bright and seasonal produce-driven dishes are served under vine-covered trellis and the shade of the Tuscan trees. Il Bosco Restaurant is open seasonally through September 30. The Pool remains open daily from 9 AM to 7 PM through October 15. Seasonal opening and closing dates are subject to change.
