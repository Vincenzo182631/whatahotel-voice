# Unwind at the Pool

Stroll through the ascending terraced gardens to discover Il Bosco and the Pool Bar, a tranquil retreat nestled among the shade of Tuscan trees. Here, aperitif-style cocktails and seasonally inspired cuisine are served beneath pergolas, creating an atmosphere that is both relaxed and refined. Surrounded by greenery and sweeping garden views, it is the perfect setting to unwind and embrace the timeless charm of a Tuscan afternoon.

- Il Bosco Restaurant is open seasonally through September 30. The Pool remains open daily from 9 AM to 7 PM through October 15. Seasonal opening and closing dates are subject to change.

## Vivoli: A Poolside Gelato Ritual

Founded in 1929, Vivoli is one of Florence’s most historic gelaterias. At Collegio alla Querce a bespoke gelato cart brings this local tradition to the poolside. Guests can discover Vivoli’s signature ritual, where gelato is finished with a pour of warm espresso. Younger guests are invited to take part in a hands-on moment, decorating their own gelato with the guidance of our Chef.

## Il Bosco: the Poolside Restaurant and Bar

Il Bosco is a relaxed poolside retreat set among terraced gardens and olive trees, where lunch is savoured naturally under the Tuscan sun. The menu is light and considered, centred on seasonal produce, beautifully composed salads, grilled fish and vegetables, and refined pizzas and pastas, paired with chilled wines and aperitif-style cocktails. An easy, unhurried rhythm defines the daytime experience.
