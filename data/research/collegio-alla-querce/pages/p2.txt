In harmonious design, Collegio alla Querce, Auberge Collection’s 83 guest rooms and suites marry the old and new worlds, combining high Renaissance art with modern Italian design. Rich colors, locally sourced materials and contemporary furnishings will complement hand-selected antiques and walls of thoughtfully curated, decade-spanning artwork. Soaring windows look over manicured Baroque gardens, spectacular hilltop views of Tuscany and the historic terracotta tiled city of Firenze. Personal wine cellars, trunk closets for fully unpacking, marble tubs and invisible technology all create a true sense of la bella vita.

##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is Collegia alla Querce located?** Collegio alla Querce sits on a hillside about 15 minutes north of Florence’s historic center, connected by a complimentary shuttle running from 9:30 AM to 9:30 PM.

**How far is the hotel from the airport?** We are 15 minutes by car from Aeroporto di Firenze – Peretola. Private transportation can be arranged through your itinerary designer or taxis are available upon exiting the airport.

**Is Collegio alla Querce good for couples?** Yes. The combination of frescoed suites, terraced gardens overlooking Florence, and sunset views of the Duomo creates a setting that is hard to match in Tuscany. The property has the intimacy of a private hillside estate while sitting 15 minutes from one of the world’s great cities. Well suited to honeymoons, anniversaries, or any trip where two guests want to disappear a little.

**Is parking available at Collegio alla Querce?** Yes. Collegio alla Querce offers on-site parking for €60 per night.

**Is there a pool or spa?** The outdoor pool sits in Collegio alla Querce’s terraced gardens with views over Florence, open seasonally from April 15 through September 30. The Aelia Spa operates year-round, offering bespoke wellness rituals in treatment rooms with garden terrace views.

**What dining options are at Collegio alla Querce?** Four distinct venues: La Gamella is the signature restaurant, serving Tuscan and Mediterranean cuisine. Bar Bertelli occupies the former headmaster’s office and serves evening cocktails. Il Bosco opens seasonally with al fresco poolside dining and aperitif cocktails. The Conservatorio is an all-day lounge for lighter moments.

**What experiences can I arrange through the hotel?** Amongst others, pasta-making classes, after-hours access to the Accademia Gallery, private gallery tours, and Chianti countryside excursions are all on the menu. On property, the gardens host yoga sessions and sound-bathing experiences.
