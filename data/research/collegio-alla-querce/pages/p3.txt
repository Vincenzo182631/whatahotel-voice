# Paying homage to local heritage in buzzing social spacesDine at Collegio alla Querce

## A fresh perspective on time honored culinary traditions

A captivating dining destination, Collegio alla Querce celebrates old-world Italian allure with comfortable modern flair in style and experience. A journey through Italy, the varied dining venues include a destination restaurant, a glass-enclosed garden room for all day dining, a sophisticated bar and cigar lounge set in the former academic admissions office, and an outdoor pool bar offering lunch and cocktails nestled amongst the old growth olives and soaring oak trees.

## La Gamella

The stunning signature restaurant at the heart of Collegio alla Querce, La Gamella merges the raw beauty of the Tuscan countryside with effortless Italian style. A culinary haven in Florence that celebrates meticulous attention to flavor, the restaurant merges an artful approach to seafood-driven cuisine combined with Tuscan country comforts. A place for all seasons, the restaurant uses locally sourced ingredients and wines to honor its unique proximity to the Tuscan countryside while presenting a menu of inspired regional Italian dishes.

### Bar Bertelli

Once the office of the headmaster, Bar Bertelli's cocktail program draws on the Collegio alla Querce’s classroom and history museum roots, exploring science and chemistry to craft masterful cocktails. Here you’ll find global bar bites with flavors spanning Asia to Mexico, inspired by the school's history books that lauded tales of famous explorers who discovered exotic ingredients from faraway lands. In the evenings, enjoy hand-rolled Italian cigars in the cigar room and entertainment that beckons the curious with mystical performances by mentalists and magicians.

-

### Conservatorio

Filled with natural light and greenery, Il Conservatorio offers a relaxed yet refined setting for coffee, tea, and light seasonal fare throughout the day. From morning through afternoon, the space invites unhurried moments, whether pausing for a quiet coffee, enjoying handcrafted pastries. With its calm, garden-inspired atmosphere, Il Conservatorio is designed as a place to slow down and recharge between moments of the day.

-

### The Chef's Table

Anchoring La Gamella is an iconic Italian kitchen placed for all to see. Guests feel like they’ve stepped into Nonna’s kitchen as they explore this convivial space where they can graze on Italian breakfast pastries and small bites from the counter throughout the day while chefs prepare pasta and pastry dough in a daily aperitivo ritual. For those looking to hone their culinary skills, the Chef’s Table offers a calendar of Italian cooking classes and intimate dining experiences with visiting chefs.

-

### Il Bosco

Il Bosco is a relaxed poolside retreat set among terraced gardens and olive trees, where lunch savoured naturally under the Tuscan sun. The menu is light and considered, centred on seasonal produce, beautifully composed salads, grilled fish and vegetables, and refined pizzas and pastas, paired with chilled wines and aperitif-style cocktails. An easy, unhurried rhythm defines the daytime experience.

  - Il Bosco Restaurant is open seasonally through September 30. The Pool remains open daily from 9 AM to 7 PM through October 15. Seasonal opening and closing dates are subject to change.

## About Chef Nicola Zamperetti

As Executive Chef at Collegio alla Querce, Chef Nicola Zamperetti brings a deep-rooted passion for Italian cuisine and a commitment to celebrating the flavors of Tuscany through a modern lens. With a career shaped by prestigious kitchens across Italy and beyond, Chef Nicola combines classical training with contemporary techniques to create refined, seasonal menus. Deeply respectful of local culinary traditions, he is dedicated to honoring and reinterpreting Tuscan cuisine—highlighting its authenticity while giving it a fresh, modern twist. His culinary philosophy centers on quality and sustainability, carefully selecting each ingredient with dedication and care—crafting unforgettable dining experiences that capture the cultural richness of the region.
