## Aelia Exclusively Partners with Furtuna Skin

-

Rooted in the rich biodiversity of La Furtuna Farm in Sicily, Furtuna Skin brings the power of wild-harvested botanicals and extra virgin olive oil to the wellness experience at Aelia, an Auberge Spa.

-

Everything Furtuna Skin produces, from extra virgin olive oil to botanicals, is hand-harvested on site. The process is truly organic, starting with the natural spring water that feeds everything on the farm.

-

From therapeutic treatments inspired by Tuscan traditions to post-treatment olive oil tastings with a custom ingestible olive oil elixir, LXR12, bottled exclusively by Furtuna Skin for Collegio alla Querce, every aspect of Aelia is dedicated to relaxation in the pursuit of living well.

## Mindful Movement at Aelia, an Auberge Spa

-

### La Palestra & Volta Dell'Angelo

From mat pilates and yoga in our one-of-a-kind movement studio, Volta Dell'Angelo, to modern equipment like Technogym bikes and treadmills in La Palestra embark on your wellness journey with well-appointed facilities set against striking old-world architecture. Select from our daily classes and private sessions led by certified experts who blend traditional and innovative approaches to mindful movement.

-

### Terrazza della Piscina

Stroll through ascending terrazza gardens to find the garden Il Bosco and pool bar where aperitif-style cocktails, bright and seasonal produce-driven dishes are served under vine-covered trellis and the shade of the Tuscan trees.
