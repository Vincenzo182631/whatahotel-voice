## Details

Welcome to Connecticut Ave, Wine & Liquors ! We are located just 1 block north of historic Dupont Circle at the corner of Connecticut Ave. & Q street. We are metro accessible, Dupont Station (Q Street side). Since 1983 we have been serving the Dupont Circle - Washington D.C. area offering the most exclusive selection of wine, spirits & beer available. Our inventory is always increasing to accommodate an ever-growing customer base. Great teamwork with our distributors and outstanding patronage from our customers over the years has given us the opportunity to offer you a great selection at competitive prices.
