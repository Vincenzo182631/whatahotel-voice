# More Rewards

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
|   | Introducing DISCOVERY Dollars (D$) - our exclusive rewards currency available from day one. Effortless to earn, and yours to spend however you choose.  **What are D$?** Upon joining Doyle DISCOVERY, you'll start receiving a percentage back on all eligible purchases, ranging from 4% to 7% in D$ every time you visit one of our hotels\*. D$1 amounts to USD 1, so the value is clear. And keeping track of your balance is simple and easy on the all-new DISCOVERY app and website. **How do D$ work?** As a member, you'll automatically start earning from day one on stays and purchases in any of our hotels\*. At check-out, simply put your D$ towards your bill to help pay for your treats and indulgences. It’s that simple! **What can you spend D$ on?** We believe in celebrating life and delighting in every invaluable moment. That’s why your D$ are yours to spend however you like — so you can choose how you want to relish your precious time. Spend D$ across our hotels, from rooms to dining, spa, golf and Experiences. Or save up for your next dream getaway. Treat yourself to an opulent room upgrade, go for that decadent vintage at dinner, get the massage and facial or explore your location with a curated Experience … there are countless ways to spend your D$. |
