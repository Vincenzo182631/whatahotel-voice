# Luxury 5 Star Hotel in Washington, D.C.

With a thrilling landmark position in the center of the historic Dupont Circle neighborhood and a short stroll from the city's most iconic monuments, The Dupont Circle Hotel is perfectly situated to enjoy all that Washington D.C. has to offer.

Following a transformative refurbishment in collaboration with world-renowned designers Martin Brudnizki and Clodagh, The Dupont Circle is a luxury hotel that seamlessly weaves contemporary comfort and style with a mid-century aesthetic that pays homage to the building’s beginnings.

Designed by Martin Brudnizki,
The Pembroke restaurant is an established dining destination in Washington D.C. for locals and visitors alike. Bathed in natural light, the tranquil décor paired with an abundance of greenery, fresh color palette, and expansive outdoor terrace offer guests unsurpassed elegance in a relaxed and alfresco setting.

Directly across the foyer and harkening back to a bygone era of classic tailoring, and crisp dry martinis is
Doyle Bar – a glamorous hub for a cosmopolitan city. Featuring sweeping views of Dupont Circle alongside warm wood paneling, soft jewel-toned banquettes and vibrant artwork, the bar effortlessly nods to the cocktail culture of the 1950s and 60s. This beautiful Washington, D.C. bar also boasts an impressive collection of Irish whiskeys – a nod to its proud heritage as part of the family-owned Doyle Collection.

The epitome of a luxury hotel, The Dupont Circle offers spacious
guest rooms and
suites across nine floors that seamlessly blend art-forward design with modern comforts. On the Penthouse Level, an exclusive floor composed of 12 individually-designed suites, guests are invited to indulge in the ultimate city stay. Suites with private, furnished terraces offer incomparable panoramic city views, stretching from the Washington Monument to the National Cathedral.

The crown jewel of the ninth floor, the Grand Penthouse, designed by Irish-born and internationally acclaimed Clodagh, is one of Washington D.C.’s largest suites and features five private heated terraces, a dining area, an additional half bath for entertaining, and an elegantly appointed lounge and large bedroom.

**\\_\\_\\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_**

The Dupont Circle is owned and managed by The Doyle Collection, an Irish family-owned luxury hotel group with hotels in Dublin, London, Washington DC and Cork.
