## Details

Doyle & Co Coffee Bar brings a taste of Ireland to Dupont Circle, from expertly pulled Dublin Roasters espresso beverages to beloved Irish favorites like Barry’s Tea. Pair your cup with freshly made sandwiches, pastries, and sweet treats. Just steps from The Dupont Circle metro, take your pick-me-up to go or savor your morning in style. Ease into the day in Doyle’s chic lounge or soak in the sunshine on our wildflower-inspired terrace overlooking the park.
