## Details

Do you want to make changes in your life or relationship? Tessa Suppes, MA, LPC can help you become your best self, compassionately and with expertise. Tessa Suppes is a trauma trained Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC) with 12 years of experience working with individuals, couples, and families in the greater DC area.

Tessa’s calm demeanor coupled with her expertise provides an ideal environment to help you reach your goals in therapy. Prior to private practice, Tessa provided intensive home-based interventions for at-risk families under severe stress or with children and/or adolescents who were at-risk for an out of home placement. She also provided short-term intensive services for adolescents and their families in a structured and supportive outpatient environment with the goal of stabilizing the adolescent. Before becoming a therapist Tessa had a successful business career in high tech sales and a long track record of achievement in athletics—honored in the Athletic Hall of Fame at Principia College.
