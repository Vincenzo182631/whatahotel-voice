## Why August Is One of the Best Times to Visit Jackson Hole

There is something special about August in Jackson Hole. The days are long, the Tetons are at their most vibrant, and the valley settles into an easy rhythm of outdoor adventure, live music, and evenings spent under wide western skies. For families looking to squeeze in one final getaway before the return to school and routines, August offers the perfect opportunity to experience the best of the Tetons.

## Rodeo Nights & Western Traditions

Few experiences capture the spirit of Jackson Hole quite like the rodeo.

Held throughout August, the Jackson Hole Rodeo remains a favorite for visitors of all ages. From barrel racing to bull riding, it is an authentic western tradition that has entertained families for generations. Our Concierge Team can assist with securing tickets and planning the perfect rodeo evening.

## Live Music Beneath the Mountains

Summer concerts at Snow King Mountain bring nationally touring artists to Jackson throughout August. Spend the day exploring the Tetons, enjoy dinner at Corsa, then head into town for an evening of live music beneath the Wyoming sky. Ask our team about available concert tickets during your stay.

## Adventure Around Every Corner

August is ideal for experiencing the region’s most iconic outdoor adventures. Wildlife safaris, paddleboarding on alpine lakes, scenic tram rides, hiking, biking, and whitewater rafting are all at their peak this time of year. With Grand Teton National Park just minutes away, every day offers a new way to explore.
