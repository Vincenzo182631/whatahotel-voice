## Spa & Fitness

The Caldera House Spa & Athletic Club is an ideal place to rejuvenate after a day spent navigating the wild terrain of Jackson Hole. Guests have 24-hour access to our fitness studio, equipped with Peloton bikes and yoga corner, as well as our heated outdoor infinity plunge and cedar dry sauna. A number of massages and therapies are available in our treatment room or in-suite upon request.

We recommend contacting the hotel as soon as possible so we can book a spa treatment for you prior to your stay.
