**The Year of the Horse: Ride Into the Heart of the Tetons**

2026 is the Year of the Horse, and there is no better place on earth to honor it than Jackson Hole. In Chinese tradition, the Horse symbolizes freedom, adventure, and untamed spirit. In Wyoming, those same qualities carved a civilization out of wilderness. At Caldera House, we’re inviting you to celebrate this auspicious year the way it was meant to be lived: in the saddle, under open skies, with the Tetons at your back.

The Year of the Horse calls for movement, courage, and a touch of wildness. Answer it with a custom trail ride through the Gros Ventre wilderness, led by a seasoned local wrangler who knows these mountains like the back of his hand. Wind through golden aspen groves, cross sagebrush flats, and crest overlooks that feel like they belong in another century. The horse beneath you isn’t just transportation. It’s a connection to something older and truer than the modern world. Want to go deeper? Our concierge can arrange a multi-hour backcountry ride with a riverside picnic curated by our chef, a feast worthy of the year’s most spirited symbol.

Come evening, the celebration continues at the Jackson Hole Rodeo, a tradition since 1890 and, in the Year of the Horse, practically a sacred rite. Watch bronc riders cling on under neon lights with the Tetons silhouetted against the summer sky. Bull riding, barrel racing, and roping unfold in a blur of dust and thunder that would make any Horse year proud. We’ll arrange VIP tickets, transport, and a little something for your flask.

The Horse also rewards those who explore beyond the saddle. Visit a working ranch for a cutting horse demonstration and witness firsthand the extraordinary intelligence and partnership between horse and rider that defines the American West. Or take to the Snake River on a fly fishing drift boat, a quieter kind of freedom that the Horse energy of 2026 also honors.

Back at the House, the Year of the Horse meets the Caldera standard: rugged meets refined. Trade your boots for a robe, book a massage at our in-suite spa, and recount the day’s triumphs over small-batch bourbon in the Alpine Lounge. Gather your group for a chef-prepared fireside dinner in your private suite. This is Jackson Hole the Caldera way: wild at heart, impeccable at its core.
