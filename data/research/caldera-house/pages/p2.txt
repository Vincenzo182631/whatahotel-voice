## Mudroom Sports Shop

Tucked into the ground level of Caldera House, Mudroom is a specialty mountain shop built on a passion for the mountains, exceptional equipment, and personal service. Founded by Jackson Hole local legend Gov Carrigan and brought to life by his team of seasoned mountain professionals, Mudroom offers a carefully curated collection of premium skis, mountain apparel, and accessories. Every piece has earned its place, selected by people who know these mountains and love the sport. Their philosophy is simple: great service starts with listening and getting every detail just right.

## Gov's Winter Checklist

After more than three decades in Jackson Hole, Gov knows a thing or two about making the most of winter. From trusted mountain essentials to a few personal favorites, the founder of Mudroom shares his must-haves for a great season on and off the mountain. Consider it a little local knowledge from someone who's spent a lifetime living it.

- Mountain Studio Outerwear Kit — Thoughtful design, exceptional performance. Ready for whatever the mountain brings.
- POC BC MIPS Helmet & Fovea Goggles — Protection, comfort, and clear vision when it counts.
- Hestra Leather Fall Line Gloves or Mitts — Warm hands make for longer, happier ski days.
- Falke Ski Socks — Never underestimate a great pair of ski socks.
- ZipFit Liners & Proper Boot Fitting — It all starts with a great-fitting ski boot.
- The Right Ski, Tuned to Perfection — Match the ski to the day's conditions.
- Vuarnet Mineral Glass Sunglasses — Timeless style. A mountain classic.
- Amundsen Boiled Wool Sweater — Old-school mountain heritage that never goes out of style.
- Supergoop! Skincare — Take care of your skin. The mountain sun is no joke.
- Hestra Wakayama Gloves or Mitts — Great leather, classic character, and everyday warmth.
- MONO Après-Ski Boots — Comfortable, stylish, and just right for life after skiing.
