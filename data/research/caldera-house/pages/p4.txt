## Corsa

Corsa was created to capture the rustic and laid-back vibe of Jackson Hole, while nodding to authentic trattorias in the Italian Alps. Utilizing the highest quality fresh and local produce, Corsa's menu reflects a vision that honors traditional Italian dishes to deliver classics with a modern spin.

Corsa is currently closed for the off-season. We look forward to welcoming you back this winter. Online reservations open November 1.

## Southcable Café

Tucked within Caldera House, Southcable Café is our most laid-back space—open daily for coffee, breakfast, and casual bites to take along on your adventures.

## In-Suite Dining

Each equipped with its own chef’s kitchen and spacious dining room, our suites serve as excellent spaces to host a private dinner. Should you desire, you can arrange for a Corsa chef to prepare a meal in-suite.

## Alpine Members Lounge

With its mountain-facing outdoor patio and club-like interior, our Member’s Lounge is an ideal place for any meal or social gathering. In the winter, alpine club members and hotel guests can enjoy the offerings of Corsa from this exclusive space with a lively après scene. Outside of ski season, the lounge is open to all to rent as an event venue.
