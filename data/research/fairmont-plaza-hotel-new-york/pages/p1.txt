## THE _iconic_ CASTLE IN THE HEART OF NEW YORK CITY

Steeped in French Renaissance charm, The Plaza stands as a masterpiece of elegance at the corner of Fifth Avenue and Central Park South. Since opening in 1907, our luxury hotel has become a landmark – representing the pinnacle of New York City hospitality and welcoming the world’s most distinguished guests.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Plaza?

Check-in time is 3 p.m. Check-out time is 12 noon.

Does The Plaza have a swimming pool?

Despite Kevin McCallister’s famous cannonball, The Plaza does not currently house a swimming pool. The Plaza does offer an array of other relaxing experiences, however, including beautifying services from Blandi Salon.

Is there a concierge service at The Plaza?

The Concierge is available daily from 7 a.m. to 11 p.m. in the hotel lobby. They would be pleased to assist guests with an array of needs and special requests including dinner reservations, transportation, theater tickets, directions to major attractions and more.

Is The Plaza pet-friendly?

Yes, we were the first hotel in New York to permit pets! Inspired by the Fairmont culture of Canine Ambassadors, The Plaza offers our furry guests a bowl for food and water and a doggy bed to be comfortable and accommodated in true Plaza fashion.

Is parking available at The Plaza?

The hotel's parking facility is off-site. Valet parking is available at a price of $110 per night with no in-and-out privileges. A surcharge of $15 will be applied to any oversized vehicles.

Does The Plaza have a gym/fitness center?

Our fitness center is located on the 4th floor. Open daily, 24 hours.
