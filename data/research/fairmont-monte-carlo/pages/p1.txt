## Your gateway to the _splendor_ of the Mediterranean

A symbol of modernity in the Principality, our luxury hotel in Monaco is a perfect balance between the heritage of a historic destination and a contemporary lifestyle lived to the fullest. Blending a mix of urban pleasures, the Mediterranean way of life and the latest international trends, the Fairmont Monte Carlo encapsulates a state of being that is present in every moment.

### Step into the glamour of Monaco and the pulse of Fairmont

The Fairmont Monte Carlo, a landmark in the heart of the Principality, boasts daring architecture and has hosted memorable events for 50 years. Centrally located in Monaco, it offers sea view or quiet garden view rooms, recently renovated with modern amenities. Home to Nobu, Nikki Beach, and Amù, the hotel is a fusion of diverse and unique experiences.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Monte Carlo located?

The Fairmont Monte Carlo is situated in the heart of Monte Carlo, near the Casino plaza, offering stunning views of the Mediterranean and close proximity to major attractions. Our address is 12 Av. des Spélugues, 98000.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Monte Carlo?

Check-in is from 3:00 pm, and check-out is by 12:00 pm.

Does Fairmont Monte Carlo have a swimming pool?

Yes, our 4-star Monte Carlo hotel features two swimming pools for your enjoyment and relaxation.

Does Fairmont Monte Carlo have a spa?

Yes, we offer a luxurious spa with a range of treatments to help you unwind and rejuvenate during your 4-star stay at Fairmont Monte Carlo.

Does Fairmont Monte Carlo have a gym/fitness center?

Yes, our luxury hotel in Monaco includes a fully equipped gym and fitness center for your workout needs.

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Monte Carlo?

We offer accessible accommodations designed to ensure a comfortable stay for guests with mobility needs. Please contact us to discuss specific requirements.

Do you offer a kids club ?

Yes, all hotel guests can enjoy the Kids Club free of charge from July 1 to August 31, upon registration.
