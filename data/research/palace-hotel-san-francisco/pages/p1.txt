The Palace Hotel

## A San Francisco Landmark

A timeless icon where original architecture and authentic elements pair with classically inspired, modern décor. Originally established in 1875, the Palace was San Francisco’s first premier luxury hotel and the largest in the world. Throughout her lifetime as a landmark of the city, the Palace Hotel has witnessed history and evolved with the times. With the introduction of the new Palace in 1909, the award-winning 1989 restoration, and the reveal of our 2015 renovation, this luxury hotel in downtown San Francisco remains a first in time and place.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Palace Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Francisco?

Does Palace Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Francisco allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Palace Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Francisco. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Palace Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Francisco?

On-site valet parking is available at Palace Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Francisco. The daily valet fee is $89.00 per day.
