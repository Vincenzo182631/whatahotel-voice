# Indulge in Our Downtown San Francisco Hotel Rooms

## Accommodations

Enter your downtown hotel room in San Francisco with a sense of wonder. Turn your monogrammed, brass doorknob and swing open your solid oak door, a signature feature of our hotel since 1909. As you cross...See More the threshold, notice the rich hues and intricate textiles of your room's carpet and decor, which pay tribute to California Impressionism. Run your fingers across lush Frette linens and your leather headboard. Take in the hotel room's 11-foot ceilings with crown molding and view the city from your large window with elegant drapes. Every one of our San Francisco hotel suites and rooms offers full or partial views of downtown. Inspect your marble bathroom, which offers bright tiles, a backlit mirror, and in some suites, a jacuzzi-style bathtub. Slip into a bathrobe before you return to your pillowtop mattress.See Less

## Enhance Your Stay

Enjoy the luxury of guaranteed early check-in or late check-out, treat yourself to a hotel suite in downtown San Francisco​ enhanced relaxation and ensure your group stays close with connecting room upgrades. From the moment you arrive to the time you depart, we’ve designed every detail to elevate your San Francisco retreat.
