# Restaurants in Chicago

## Dine on Michigan Avenue

In Chicago, every culinary experience tells a story. Our story starts here, at our Chicago Loop restaurants and distinct dining destinations. A signature French brasserie welcomes hotel guests and locals alike for all-day dining in a lively, convivial space reminiscent of a bygone era of revelry and culture, while an intimate lobby bar beckons you for quiet conversation, hand-crafted drinks and demure hospitality.

Bar Pendry

Cocktail Bar in The Loop

A Pendry signature. Bar Pendry is a reimagination of the classic hotel bar, tailored carefully to Chicago’s distinct sense of style. Its rich, moody space is anchored by a central fireplace that welcomes guests for a Sunday afternoon boozy tea, evening cocktails, an elevated menu of small bites and late-night libations.

- Discover Bar Pendry

Château Carbide

Rooftop Bar in Chicago

Meet us on the rooftop for another season atop Chicago’s stunning skyline at Château Carbide, a city-inspired cocktail lounge where hotel guests and locals alike can take in the city’s summer splendor with crisp Aperol Spritz, icy signature cocktails, a menu of small bites and favorites, live DJs every weekend and incredible views.

- Discover Château Carbide

Venteux

French Brasserie on Michigan Avenue

Where a distinct, Parisian-inspired setting comingles with the sparkling charm of Chicago’s Magnificent Mile. Venteux (“windy” in French) is a French brasserie, café, and oyster & champagne bar with a modern take on Nouvelle French cuisine for brunch, dinner and happy hour. Expect classic French favorites alongside signature, Chicago-inspired novelties. In one of the city’s most stunning spaces. Only at Pendry.

- Discover Venteux

The Library

Intimate Hotel Lounge

A tucked away nook for cocktails and conversation. The Library is adjacent to the hotel’s lobby and restaurant, with a streetside view of Michigan Avenue. Unwind in an intimate lounge-like setting where a large conference table and cozy seating creates the perfect gathering place that awaits you for all-day play.

In-Room Dining

Room Service at Pendry Chicago

At your service. Day or night. Pendry’s signature In-Room Dining features a specially crafted menu of small bites, entrées, beverages and sweets, specific to each hotel and its local culinary influences, all carefully delivered to your guestroom to satisfy any craving with convenience and comfort top of mind.

## Pendry Chicago Dining FAQs

### How many restaurants and bars are located at Pendry Chicago?

Pendry Chicago offers 3 restaurants and bars on the Magnificent Mile. Dining destinations include Venteux, a modern French brasserie and café, Bar Pendry, a stylish cocktail lounge, and Château Carbide, the hotel’s rooftop cocktail bar.

### Does Pendry Chicago offer in-room dining?

Yes, Pendry Chicago offers 24-hours in-room dining, allowing guests to enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner and late-night fare in their guestroom or suite at our luxury hotel downtown Chicago.

### Do any restaurants at Pendry Chicago offer outdoor dining or scenic views?

Yes, Château Carbide, Pendry Chicago’s rooftop bar, offers cocktails and light bites with sweeping skyline views from the top of the historic Carbide & Carbon Building, providing a lively setting for rooftop drinks in Chicago. Venteux also offers seasonal outdoor terrace seating along Michigan Avenue, where guests can enjoy French-inspired dining with a view.﻿

### Where can I enjoy cocktails and lite bites at Pendry Chicago?

At Pendry Chicago, Bar Pendry provides a refined, contemporary setting with a menu featuring craft cocktails, wines, and a selection of elevated small plates, making it an ideal spot for both casual gatherings and pre- or post-dinner drinks.

### Does Pendry Chicago feature a rooftop bar or lounge?

Yes, Château Carbide, is Pendry Chicago’s rooftop lounge and bar, offering craft cocktails, light bites, and an outdoor terrace with sweeping city views. The outdoor rooftop area is seasonal, while indoor seating at Château Carbide is available year-round.

### Do Pendry Chicago’s restaurants accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes, we gladly accommodate dietary restrictions and special dietary needs whenever possible.﻿
