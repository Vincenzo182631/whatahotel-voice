AIRE Ancient Baths Chicago, located just 2 miles away, offers a unique relaxation oasis with thermal baths, aroma therapy steam rooms, and wellness treatments. Pendry Chicago now features a limited number of special Tonal Wellness Rooms, perfect for on-demand fitness. Guestrooms are equipped with Tonal home gym with bench and accessories, Bala weights, bangles and more.
