# Chicago Accommodations

## Luxury Hotel Rooms & Suites in Chicago

A contemporary stay in illustrious Art Deco style. Set within the iconic Carbide & Carbon Building in the Loop neighborhood, Pendry Chicago welcomes guests to discover a blend of modern luxury and sophisticated comfort. At our boutique hotel in downtown Chicago, experience one of our 364 luxury guestrooms and suites and discover the best of the Windy City from our prime Michigan Avenue location.

## Rooms

A great hotel starts here. Where the comforts of home meet the style and luxuries of an iconic architectural landmark. Pendry Chicago’s guestrooms blend design, comfort and unparalleled detail in one of the city’s most striking downtown settings.

## Pendry Chicago Accommodations FAQs

### What views do rooms at Pendry Chicago offer?

Pendry Chicago guestrooms and suites offer views of the city, the Chicago River, Michigan Avenue or Millennium Park, depending on the room or suite selected.

### Is Pendry Chicago family friendly?

Yes. Pendry Chicago welcomes families with room and suite options for a range of group sizes. Queen Queen Guestrooms accommodate up to 4 guests, and Suites are also available. Select connecting guestrooms and suites are available on request, subject to availability. The hotel also welcomes dogs and cats for an additional fee.

### Which Pendry Chicago accommodations are best for a romantic getaway?

The One Bedroom Suites are among the most popular choices for couples, ideal for anniversaries, honeymoons and other special occasions. Each One Bedroom Suite features a spacious sitting area and sweeping views of the city or the Chicago River.

### Is Pendry Chicago a good hotel for business travelers?

Yes. Pendry Chicago is located in the Loop, steps from Michigan Avenue and Chicago’s financial district, with the Clark/Lake and Washington/Wabash ‘L’ stations each about a 10-minute walk away. The hotel also offers more than 13,000 square feet of meeting and event space for gatherings of any size.

### Is Pendry Chicago pet friendly?

Yes. Pendry Chicago welcomes dogs and cats for an additional fee, a portion of which supports PAWS Chicago.

### Are connecting rooms available at Pendry Chicago?

Yes. Pendry Chicago offers select connecting guestrooms and suites. Since connecting accommodations are limited and subject to availability, they should be requested at the time of reservation rather than at check-in.

### Which Pendry Chicago room type is best for a longer stay?

For longer stays, Pendry Chicago’s suites are a popular choice. Suites offer additional living space with separate sitting areas, providing more room to relax or work during an extended stay.

### How many guests can each Pendry Chicago room type accommodate?

Pendry Chicago offers guestrooms and suites across a range of group sizes.

- King Guestrooms accommodate up to 2 guests.
- Queen Queen Guestrooms accommodate up to 4 guests.
- Suites include Studio Suites, One Bedroom Suites, Two Bedroom Suites and Specialty Suites, and can accommodate up to 2 to 5 guests, depending on the suite configuration, number of bedrooms and available sleeper sofa bed options.

### What are check-in and check-out times at Pendry Chicago?

Check-in at Pendry Chicago begins at 4:00pm and check-out is at 12:00pm. Early check-in and late departure may be available on request and are subject to availability. Guests should contact the hotel in advance to request either.

### Does Pendry Chicago offer accessible accommodations?

Yes. Pendry Chicago offers ADA-accessible guestrooms and suites featuring lowered deadbolts, viewports, thermostats and beds, roll-in showers or bathtub grab rails, and sensory notifications with audible and visual emergency alarms. Guests can view accessible room options or contact the hotel directly to request accessible accommodations.
