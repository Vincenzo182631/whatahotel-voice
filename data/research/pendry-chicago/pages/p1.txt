## Steeped In History, Anchored In Style. Discover The Chicago Loop Neighborhood.

Set within the iconic 1929 Art Deco Carbide & Carbon Building in Chicago’s Loop, just steps from Michigan Avenue, Pendry Chicago is a luxury boutique hotel in downtown Chicago that pays homage to both the past and the present. With modern interiors, signature culinary concepts and inspired gathering spaces, the hotel embodies the spirit of the Windy City and offers a prime location for discovering the best of Chicago.
