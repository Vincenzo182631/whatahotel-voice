### Tiexiang Temple

Tiexiang Temple in Chengdu Hi-Tech Zone dates back to the Ming Dynasty. The surrounding 'Tiexiang Temple Water Street' combines Western Sichuan architecture with modern commerce, creating a cultural hub showcasing Tianfu Culture.

### Chengdu SKP

Located in Chengdu’s Jiaozi Avenue Business District near Metro Line 18’s Jincheng Plaza East Station, SKP spans 190 mu (12.7 hectares) with 344,000㎡ of floor space. Housing 1,000+ luxury brands, it offers high-end retail in a unique park-like setting. Its five-level sunken structure is Asia’s largest underground commercial complex.

### Chengdu Global Center

Chengdu Global Center, one of the world's largest freestanding buildings, integrates shopping, entertainment, hotels, exhibitions, and offices. Featuring an indoor ‘Ocean Paradise’ and contemporary architectural design, it is a landmark complex in Chengdu.

### Chengdu Century City Convention Exhibition Center

Located in Chengdu Hi-Tech Zone, the Chengdu Century City New International Convention & Exhibition Center is a multifunctional complex integrating exhibitions, conferences, hotels, and commercial services. Opened in 2004, it became Western China's first UFI-certified venue in 2006, demonstrating international recognition of its facilities.

### Guixi Ecological Park

The hotel neighbors Guixi Ecological Park, a 93-hectare key site in Chengdu's Ring Ecological Zone. Built on 'ecology-first' principles, its lakes, woodlands and trails create a multifunctional urban oasis. It is often called "Chengdu’s version of Central Park."
