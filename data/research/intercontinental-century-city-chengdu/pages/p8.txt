# InterContinental Century City ChengduDestination

InterContinental Century City Chengdu stands out as an exciting new landmark in the
capital of Sichuan province. From museums and temples to bustling pedestrian street Chunxi Road, our hotel is convenient to top cultural and architectural destinations.

1. Tiexiang Temple

2. Chengdu SKP

3. Chengdu Global Center

4. Chengdu Century City Convention Exhibition Center

5. Guixi Ecological Park

### Tiexiang Temple

Tiexiang Temple in Chengdu Hi-Tech Zone dates back to the Ming Dynasty. The surrounding 'Tiexiang Temple Water Street' combines Western Sichuan architecture with modern commerce, creating a cultural hub showcasing Tianfu Culture.

### Chengdu SKP

Located in Chengdu’s Jiaozi Avenue Business District near Metro Line 18’s Jincheng Plaza East Station, SKP spans 190 mu (12.7 hectares) with 344,000㎡ of floor space. Housing 1,000+ luxury brands, it offers high-end retail in a unique park-like setting. Its five-level sunken structure is Asia’s largest underground commercial complex.

### Chengdu Global Center

Chengdu Global Center, one of the world's largest freestanding buildings, integrates shopping, entertainment, hotels, exhibitions, and offices. Featuring an indoor ‘Ocean Paradise’ and contemporary architectural design, it is a landmark complex in Chengdu.

### Chengdu Century City Convention Exhibition Center

Located in Chengdu Hi-Tech Zone, the Chengdu Century City New International Convention & Exhibition Center is a multifunctional complex integrating exhibitions, conferences, hotels, and commercial services. Opened in 2004, it became Western China's first UFI-certified venue in 2006, demonstrating international recognition of its facilities.

### Guixi Ecological Park

The hotel neighbors Guixi Ecological Park, a 93-hectare key site in Chengdu's Ring Ecological Zone. Built on 'ecology-first' principles, its lakes, woodlands and trails create a multifunctional urban oasis. It is often called "Chengdu’s version of Central Park."

### Dufu's Thatched Cottage

37 Qinghua Road, Qingyang District, Chengdu, Sichuan 610000

Du Fu's Thatched Cottage is located in the West of Chengdu by the side of the Huanhua Stream. It was Du Fu's former residence, it is a precious cultural heritage of Du Fu, the greatest realistic poet in Chinese history.

### Temple of Marquis Wu

231 Wuhouci Street, Wuhou District,, Chengdu, Sichuan 610000

WuHou temple was built in honor of Zhuge Liang, the Prime Minister of Shu Kingdom. The temple is noteworty for commemorating the monarch and his subjects together.

### Dujiangyan Irrigation System

Dujiangyan Irrigation Project is the oldest and greatest irrigation project with still function in the world. It constructed by prefect Li Bing in Shu Prefecture in 277B.C

### Qingcheng Mountain

Qingcheng Town, Du Jiangyan, Si Chuan 61000

Mt. Qingcheng boasts beautiful peaks with dense areenery all year around. It is a well-known summer resort in Sichuan. As a famous Taoist mountain, it has some 70 Taoist temples and 38 Taoist remains.

### Panda Research Center

1375 Panda Road, Chenghua District, Chengdu, Sichuan 610000

Take a short ride to futou Hill and visit thefanciful beasts in a nursery and in large pens.

### SanXingDui Museum

No. 133, Xi'an Road, Deyang City, Si Chuan 610000

Sanxingdui Museum located in the northwest of Guanghan City, Sichuan Province, Sanxingdui is a national key cultural relic protection unit. It is an ancient city, ancient country and ancient Shu cultural site with the largest scope, the longest duration and the richest cultural connotation found in the southwest. It is known as one of the greatest archaeological discoveries of the 20th century and the source of the Yangtze River civilization.

### Mount Emei

Emei City, Emei, Si Chuan 610000

Mt. Emei Scenic area includes Leshan Giant Buddha ，and it is located in Emeishan City of Sichuan Province with the Giant Buddha sitting at the confluence of Minjiang，Dadu，and Qingyi rivers.

### Jiuzhaigou Valley

Zhangzha Town, Jiuzhaigou County, Aba Ti, Aba, Si Chuan 610000

Jiuzhaigou, located in Jiuzhaigou County, Aba Tibetan and Qiang Autonomous Prefecture, Sichuan Province, is the first nature reserve in China with the main purpose of protecting natural scenery.
