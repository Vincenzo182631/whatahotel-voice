### We May Never Book Here Again

3 months ago

One of the most terrible InterContinental i've ever stayed.
Not just because of the accommodation itself, mainly because of the convention center inside the hotel.

This IHG hotel has a gigantic convention center, which means loads of the guests or people around were mainly for business purpose, having meetings.

Our company also had a 1-day meeting inside the convention center, but :
1\. the meeting room we booked is terribly maintained, looks like no refurbishment after it's opened and ugly stains on the wall. That day was 40℃ however the AC barely functioning during the day, the lowest temp setting didn't work and the meeting room was like a steaming pot. Hotel staff moved two giant noisy fans into the meeting room and it terribly affected the overall experience of the meeting. we had to bear the noise and hot temp at the same time.

2\. a small meeting room like we used, doesn't have a proper projector. The sales person suggested us to use an authorised 3rd party vendor to install the LED screen.
Which means a 120sqm meeting room has a giant unnecessary LED display at the front and it continuously creating warmth during the day. made the overall experience worse than ever.

3\. the hotel side emphasized that the convention center is not just running under the InterContinental, they were more like having a partnership. If so, i will suggest you remove the convention center from your stupid official homepage and don't mention that.

4\. the convention center is outside of the hotel and in between of two different IHG hotels, strangers come and go, passing by the room door. No feeling of privacy

5\. we had meeting participants from national wide China and none of them wanted give high credit to the meeting room infrastructure.

6\. My colleagues shifted the room mode to DO NOT Interrupt, but the hotel staff kept bothering her even called her during the meeting just to check if they could get into the room for cleaning.

7\. the light pollution in the room was terrible, the alarm and toilet have stupid light on whole night.

### Excellent warm service from all staff...

4 months ago

Firstly, starting from the warm welcome and efficient check-in at the front desk, to the guest relationship and concierge teams, and the friendly housekeeping staff with always a smile, a big thank you for making my family's stay a memorable one. I think the human touch is the most important aspect of any hotel visit.

Love the extensive and amazing main lobby with its traditional architecture, teahouse, and the lovely chirping of the song birds. The daily traditional musical instrumental performance adds to the overall ambiance. Oh... the two pandas sitting at the table is so cute too!

However, I have to say this Intercontinental room is looking a bit dated and worn out - the walls, fixtures, furniture, carpets, etc. The beds are comfy enough, things still work well and the housekeeping did a great job. The consumables and toiletries are of good quality, as expected of the Intercontinental brand.

Last but not least, my 10/10 appreciation to Ms Tiny from the guest relationship team. She answered and fulfilled all my travel questions and room requests quickly and cheerfully.
