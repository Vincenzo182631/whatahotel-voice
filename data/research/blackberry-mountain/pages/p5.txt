# The Lodge Pool

The Lodge Pool at Blackberry Mountain is open year-round and heated to the perfect temperature for guests to relax and take in the property’s sweeping views in every season. The pool’s sleek infinity-edge design looks out over an unobstructed view of the Great Smoky Mountains. Lounge chairs and umbrellas are arranged on the pool deck for guests to enjoy poolside comfort, and a natural canopy created by thoughtful shaping of trees offers a sustainable shaded area that blends perfectly with the beauty of the surrounding environment. Lodge Pool Service from 12:00pm to 2:30pm service is included in your stay, midday meals and service from 2:30pm to 5:30pm is available for a supplemental fee.
