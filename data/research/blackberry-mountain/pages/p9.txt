## Hiking

5,200 acres offer endless opportunity for exploration. Whether you choose to stroll a trail mid-Mountain or traverse into the Great Smoky Mountains, trekking experiences abound at Blackberry Mountain. You will find that time in the woods encourages a deeper connection with your surroundings and yourself, discovering the natural beauty of our land at every turn as you explore.

If you really want to learn about what you are surrounded by in our woods, set off on a hike with one of our trail guides, and let them draw you into nature with stories of the land and the plants that cover the Mountain.

_A daily hike to breakfast is included in our_ _Hub schedule_ _._

- All ages, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

#### On-Site Hiking

A chance to hike is waiting at every turn on the Mountain, with a trail system spanning from the sun-drenched, rocky ridge tops at the Mountain’s crest to the cool, damp creek bottoms and hollows at lower-Mountain. You may even discover an enchanting art installation along the way. Test your endurance and climb to the rocky overlooks of the Cat’s Paw Ridge, or take in a more relaxed, contemplative experience along the Double Branch Nature Trail. Along the way learn about the local plants and animals that inhabit the mountainside with our well-versed, knowledgeable team of guides.

- 1-2 Hour Guided Hike
- 2-3 Hour Hike
- Half-Day Guided Hike
- Full-Day Guided Hike

#### Off-Site Hiking

From perches and private trails of the Mountain, one can see almost the entire northwestern side of the Great Smoky Mountains National Park. Its lush natural beauty begs to be discovered. Venture off-site into the Smokies in search of waterfalls, hidden hollows and rocky mountaintop vistas on our half or full-day off-site excursions.

- Half-Day Off Property Guided Hike
- Full-Day Off Property Guided Hike
