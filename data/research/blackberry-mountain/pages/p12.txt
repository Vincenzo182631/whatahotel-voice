## Curated Elemental Sound Journeys

Our sound experts have carefully curated private, elevated soundbathing experiences to assist in restoring harmony to mind, body and spirit. See each elemental description to determine which journey may be right for you.

#### EARTH

This grounding sound experience is meant to restore a sense of strength and stability. With attention to the details of physical support and focused vibrational energies, the body is able to recall a feeling of landing, of coming home.

- Focus: Body
- Complement: Crystal Grid and Sandbags
- 50 minutes

#### AIR

The intention of this sound experience is to cultivate clarity and thoughtful focus. While the body is both physically and vibrationally elevated by aerial silk and sound, the mind and spirit can find the ability to still. Leave with present moment gratitude and a sense of lightness and ease.

- Focus: Mind
- Complement: Aerial Silks
- 50 minutes

#### ALCHEMY

The unique harmonic energy that emanates from the Crystal Tones Alchemy Singing Bowls is created by combining the highest-quality quartz crystal with gold, platinum and precious gems and minerals. In this elevated experience, specific tones, colors and alchemies can bring a balancing effect to the energetic and physical systems of the body. Leave this experience feeling awakened and aligned.

- Focus: Energy Body
- Complement: Crystal Tones Alchemy Singing Bowls
- 50 minutes

#### WATER

This sound journey is intended to reawaken our innate sense of flow, ease and connection. Soothing sounds coupled with moments of inspiring vibrations allow space for shifts in perspective, creativity and even healing. Leave this experience feeling both centered and refreshed.

- Focus: Spirit
- Complement: Structured Water
- 50 minutes

#### FIRE

This passionate sound journey encourages transformation and expression. Harmonious yet cathartic contrasts of sound inspire vitality and movement in body, mind and spirit. Leave this soundbath feeling revitalized and invigorated.

- Focus: Body, Mind, Spirit
- Complement: Candlelight
- 50 minutes
