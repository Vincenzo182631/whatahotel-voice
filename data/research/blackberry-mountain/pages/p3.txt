# Art Studio

WHEEL THROWN POTTERY

Experience the feel of clay in your hands as your imagination takes shape on the wheel. Explore the techniques and tools of the craft in an experience remembered for a lifetime. Discover the art of wheel thrown pottery with instruction for all skill levels. All work will be fired by our team upon completion.

- 2 HOUR WHEEL THROWN POTTERY
- 4 HOUR WHEEL THROWN POTTERY
- 8 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

HAND-BUILDING CERAMICS

Be inspired by the shape and beauty of the Mountain as you learn the process of hand-building ceramics. Explore and create with age-old techniques including pinch forming, coil building and slab construction while enjoying the meditative personal discovery of working in clay.

- 2 HOUR HAND-BUILDING CERAMICS
- 4 HOUR HAND-BUILDING CERAMICS
- 4 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

TILE MAKING

Explore the world of design and pattern as you make your own hand-crafted ceramic tiles. From repeating patterns to intricate line work, tile making offers an opportunity to be both the designer and the creator. Let the Mountain scenery inspire you as you learn the techniques of using various sized tile presses, stamp impressions and surface embellishments to create your unique tile pattern.

- 2 HOUR TILE MAKING
- 4 HOUR TILE MAKING
- 4 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

RAKU FIRING

Our three-hour Raku Firing session covers the unique history of Raku and its post-secondary firing method. Choose a readied bisque piece to glaze, learn brush decorating technique, load it into the kiln and leave with your finished pieces!

- 3 HOUR RAKU FIRING SESSION
- WEATHER PERMITTING
- 12 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

ACRYLIC PAINTING

The Mountain offers inspiration at every turn. From quiet wooded streams to open panoramic views, stretch your imagination and creativity through painting on canvas. Informative instruction will be centered on composition, color, texture and design.

- 2 HOUR STUDIO ACRYLIC PAINTING
- 2 HOUR OUTDOOR ACRYLIC PAINTING
- 4 HOUR STUDIO ACRYLIC PAINTING
- 4 HOUR OUTDOOR ACRYLIC PAINTING
- 4 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

WATERCOLOR PAINTING

The nature of watercolor offers great versatility for guests to explore their surroundings and the wonders that Blackberry Mountain offers. While exploring a variety of watercolor brush techniques, you will be introduced to view the natural landscape through the use of a series of opaque and translucent brush strokes to create a light, elegant painting. Guests are encouraged to explore composition, color, texture and design through guided instruction.

- 2 HOUR STUDIO WATERCOLOR PAINTING
- 2 HOUR OUTDOOR WATERCOLOR PAINTING
- 4 HOUR STUDIO WATERCOLOR PAINTING
- 4 HOUR OUTDOOR WATERCOLOR PAINTING
- 4 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

TRAILSIDE PAINTING

Capture the spirit and essence of the Blackberry Mountain landscape in a Trailside Painting class. Guests will leave behind the four walls of the BMTN Art Studio to immerse themselves into our mountainous topography, icorporating natural light, color and movement onto their canvas.

- 2 HOUR TRAILSIDE PAINTING
- 4 HOUR TRAILSIDE PAINTING
- 2 HOUR TRAILSIDE SKETCHING
- 4 HOUR TRAILSIDE SKETCHING
- 4 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

SKETCHING

Vast panoramic views. The fine, intricate veins of a maple leaf. From the big picture to the small details, the beauty of the Mountain can be seen in infinite ways. Join us on a journey through sketching our beautiful landscape and the elements that comprise it in this signature class. Capture the sights and visions of your stay, and discover a skill that can travel with you throughout the world.

- 2 HOUR STUDIO SKETCHING
- 2 HOUR OUTDOOR SKETCHING
- 4 HOUR STUDIO SKETCHING
- 4 HOUR OUTDOOR SKETCHING
- 4 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

TRAILSIDE SKETCHING

From graphite to page, explore a variety of intentional markings to capture the natural beauty of the Appalachian landscape on Blackberry Mountain. Guests will identify local vegetation of the East TN region and transpose them onto their sketchbooks that they will each take away from the class to continue to be inspired by the world around them.

- 2 HOUR TRAILSIDE PAINTING
- 4 HOUR TRAILSIDE PAINTING
- 2 HOUR TRAILSIDE SKETCHING
- 4 HOUR TRAILSIDE SKETCHING
- 4 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

BASKET MAKING

Create your own handwoven basket for collecting mountain treasures. Using natural materials of flat and round reed, craft traditional baskets to use for years to come as you remember your journey on the Mountain.

- 2 HOUR BASKET MAKING
- 4 HOUR BASKET MAKING
- 8 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

TEXTILE ARTS

Celebrate the history of the foothills region and discover the traditional art form of textile work during your stay at the Mountain. Try your hand at the captivating and detailed work of embroidery, cross-stitch, crochet or knitting. Create your one-of-a-kind textile piece inspired by the land, scenery and natural texture that abounds.

- 2 HOUR KNITTING
- 4 HOUR KNITTING
- 2 HOUR CROCHET
- 4 HOUR CROCHET
- 2 HOUR CROSS-STITCHING
- 4 HOUR CROSS-STITCHING
- 2 HOUR EMBROIDERY
- 4 HOUR EMBROIDERY
- 8 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

Meditative Marbling: The Art of Suminagashi

Discover the ancient art of 'floating ink' in this tranquil, guided workshop. Using traditional sumi ink and delicate washi paper, you will learn to harmonize the natural movement of water with intentional brushwork. This meditative practice invites you to embrace the beauty of the present moment, resulting in a series of unique, marbled prints to take home—a tangible memory of your time at the Mountain.

- 2 HOUR SUMINAGASHI
- 16 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

MANDALA STONE PAINTING

Purposefully hand-selected from the Mountain, these river stones embark on a meditative journey. Participants of the class will be immersed in the celebration of intricate geometric elements and beautiful colors, with finished pieces ranging from simple to elaborately designed compositions. The art of creating the mandala is, in and of itself, a form of meditation: a soothing ritual that permits guests to express their creativity and rediscover their sense of inner calm.

- 2 HOUR MANDALA STONE PAINTING
- 4 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

EXPERIENTIAL WATERCOLOR

Guests will be encouraged through guided instruction to explore the intentionality of watercolor brushwork that blends and moves throughout an ever-evolving composition. The dance of balancing both pigment and water in the same brushstroke orchestrates the variety of subtle and intricate elements that enhance the visual experience. Resulting in meaningful work, guests will depart from this session with an understanding of color, pigment wash techniques, and designing a variety of successful compositions through their creative painting experience.

- 2 HOUR EXPERIENTIAL WATERCOLOR
- 4 years and up, minors must be accompanied by an adult.

CURATED ART EXPERIENCE

Elevate your gathering with a bespoke artistic experience designed for groups—whether corporate retreats, community getaways or elegant family celebrations. Within the serene setting of the Mountain, guests are invited to explore a thoughtfully arranged collection of art stations, featuring refined activities such as watercolor painting, mandala stone design, everlasting floral bouquet creation and artisan card making. Each element is curated to inspire creativity, connection and a sense of elevated calm. Guests depart not only with beautiful, handcrafted pieces but with enriched bonds and lasting memories.

- 2-3 HOUR CURATED ART EXPERIENCE
- MINIMUM OF 8 GUESTS
