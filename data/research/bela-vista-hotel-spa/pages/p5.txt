# Luxury Hotel Experiences.

At Bela Vista, time slows down. These are not activities, but extensions of the land and sea: the ocean, the terroir, the wine, the act of cooking. Each one invites you to discover slowly, with sharpened senses. From boat to vineyard, from kitchen to clay, everything comes from the same idea of

living the south with authenticity.

Seeallexperiences

Seeallexperiences

## Private Boat Tour

Discover the Algarve coast aboard a private yacht with total comfort and privacy. An unforgettable journey at sea, perfect for admiring caves, cliffs and crystal-clear waters that make the Algarve so unique.

## Group Boat Tour

The stunning caves of Ponta da Piedade in Lagos are one

of the Algarve’s true wonders. Explore incredible rock

formations that resemble famous figures such as the

Titanic and the Cathedral, on a guided tour departing

from Lagos Marina, about 30 minutes from the hotel,

revealing the secrets and history of this extraordinary

coastline.

## Group Kayak Tour

Depart from Lagos Marina on a catamaran to the starting point of your kayak adventure. With the support of a certified guide, you will glide over the water exploring striking caves and rock formations. A serene and eco-friendly experience surrounded by the natural sounds of the sea.

## Fishing with the Chef

An intimate and memorable experience: embark on a fishing trip with Chef João Oliveira. A journey that blends the thrill of the sea with culinary artistry, ending with a fresh meal prepared to your taste.

Please note: this experience is subject to sea and weather conditions.

## HIkes

Walk the scenic trails of Praia do João d’Arens. Access is

made on foot along cliffside paths, making it quieter and

perfect for those who enjoy exploring hidden corners.

## Oyster Experience

Discover the fascinating world of oysters with a guided visit to local farms, where you can learn about production and sustainability. Enjoy a tasting paired with regional wines, a true celebration of the sea and the authentic flavours of our coast.

Please note: this experience is subject to sea and weather conditions.

## Bike tour

Explore the Algarve by bike along the famous Seven Hanging Valleys Trail. Around 2 hours and 30 minutes, 12 km of breathtaking landscapes with stops at beaches and viewpoints.

## Wine Tastings

Quinta dos Sentidos is a charming family estate where

tradition and nature intertwine. The visit includes

vineyards, olive groves, orchards, gardens and the lake,

followed by wine and olive oil tasting.

## Wine Classes

To appreciate wine is an art. Under the guidance of our Maître D’ and sommelier, deepen your knowledge of local wines, grape varieties and food pairings in a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere.

Please check availability at reception.

## Private Tours

Discover the Algarve through exclusive tailor-made tours. In the East, explore Faro, Olhão, Tavira and Silves with a stop at the stunning Ria Formosa. In the West, visit Silves, Carvoeiro, Lagos and Sagres with their dramatic beaches and cliffs. Both tours include luxury transport and a dedicated

guide for 7 to 10 hours.

## Pottery Making

Join local artisans for a creative clay workshop every

Wednesday from 16:00 to 18:00. For two participants,

the experience includes materials, photo documentation,

firing, water and delivery of finished pieces to the hotel

on Saturday. Shape your own work in the first hour and

in the second, paint and glaze two pre-fired pieces from

the atelier.

## Cooking Classes

Discover the secrets of Portuguese gastronomy in a hands-on cooking class with Chef João Oliveira and his team. Using fresh local ingredients, prepare three traditional Algarve dishes in the Michelin-starred Vista kitchen. Duration approx. two hours and subject to availability.

## Frequently  Asked Questions

### What experiences are available at Bela Vista Hotel & Spa?

Bela Vista offers a wide range of tailor-made experiences in the Algarve, including gastronomic adventures, cultural workshops, nature activities, wellness-oriented experiences, and private sea excursions. Each experience is carefully curated to reflect the authentic traditions, landscapes and flavors of southern Portugal.

### Does Bela Vista organize private or curated experiences?

Yes, Bela Vista specializes in private and curated experiences, designed in collaboration with local artisans, fishermen, producers and experts. Experiences can be fully personalized according to guests’ interests, duration of stay and seasonality, ensuring a unique and meaningful discovery of the Algarve.

### Is Bela Vista a good base to explore the Algarve?

Absolutely. Located in Portimão, Bela Vista is an ideal base to explore the coastline, countryside and cultural heritage of the Algarve, from sea caves and beaches to mountains, vineyards and traditional villages.

### What is the “Fishing with the Chef” experience?

Guests can join Executive Chef João Oliveira for an early-morning sport fishing experience at sea, discovering Algarve fishing traditions. The catch of the day is later prepared in a traditional cataplana, showcasing authentic regional flavors, and served at Bistrô by Vista. This experience combines sea, gastronomy and storytelling, and is subject to weather conditions and availability.

### Are there cooking and wine experiences available?

Yes. Bela Vista offers hands-on cooking classes led by Chef João Oliveira & his team, where guests learn the secrets of Portuguese cuisine using local ingredients. Wine classes, guided by the Maître d’ & sommelier, focus on Algarve and Portuguese wines, tasting techniques and food pairings.

### What is the oysters experience?

The Oysters Experience includes a guided visit to local oyster farms, learning about sustainability and farming methods, followed by a tasting of fresh oysters paired with regional wines. A refined and authentic way to connect with the Algarve’s maritime heritage.

### What cultural experiences are offered?

Guests can participate in pottery-making workshops guided by local Algarve artisans, learning traditional techniques and creating a personalized piece to take home. These experiences highlight regional craftsmanship and ancestral know-how.

### What nature-based experiences are available?

Nature lovers can enjoy hiking and biking experiences across the Algarve, from coastal paths to inland trails, discovering flora, fauna, cliffs, beaches and villages. These experiences are suitable for all levels and can be adapted to guests’ preferences.

### What is the honeybee experience?

The Honeybee Experience takes guests to Monchique, where they discover the world of bees and sustainable beekeeping. The experience concludes with a tasting of locally produced honey, reflecting the purity and biodiversity of the region.

### Is there an olive oil experience?

Yes. Guests can visit olive groves and production sites, learning about the olive oil-making process from harvest to extraction. The experience ends with a tasting of award-winning Algarve olive oils, offering a true sensory journey.

### Are there private boat or yacht experiences?

Yes. Bela Vista offers private yacht tours to explore the iconic Algarve caves, crystal-clear waters and dramatic rock formations. Guests can swim, relax and admire the coastline in complete privacy.

### Is Bela Vista suitable for golf lovers?

The Algarve is one of Europe’s top golf destinations, and Bela Vista assists guests with tee time reservations on prestigious nearby courses, suitable for both beginners and experienced players.
