## Frequently  Asked Questions

### What wellness facilities are available at Bela Vista?

Bela Vista Hotel & Spa features the Spa by L’Occitane, a 400 m² luxury wellness space designed for relaxation and holistic well-being in the Algarve. The spa includes five treatment rooms (two dedicated to couples), a sauna, a steam bath, and a wet relaxation area with a plunge pool. Guests also benefit from direct access to a fully equipped gym with ocean views, offering a serene and energizing wellness environment.

### What type of treatments are offered at the spa?

The Spa by L’Occitane offers a curated selection of signature facial and body treatments inspired by Provence and tailored to individual needs. Highlights include the Immortelle Divine Secret facial, a premium anti-aging experience, and the Almond Detoxifying Balance body massage, designed to stimulate circulation and eliminate toxins. Classic relaxing massages, personalized rituals, and wellness consultations complete the experience.

### Is the spa accessible to non-hotel guests?

Yes, the Spa by L’Occitane at Bela Vista is accessible to non-hotel guests, subject to availability and prior reservation. This makes the spa a sought-after wellness destination in Portimão, ideal for locals or visitors seeking a refined spa experience in the Algarve, even without staying overnight at the hotel.

### Can I book wellness packages or retreats?

Bela Vista offers customized wellness packages in Algarve region combining accommodation, spa treatments, and access to wellness facilities. Guests can also request bespoke wellness retreats, including yoga, Pilates sessions, detox-oriented stays, and personal training, tailored to relaxation, rejuvenation, or mindful escapes. These packages are particularly popular for off-season and shoulder-season wellness travel.

### Is Bela Vista suitable for a wellness-focused stay?

Absolutely. Bela Vista Hotel & Spa is ideal for travelers seeking a wellness-focused luxury stay in Portugal. With its Spa by L’Occitane, highly trained therapists, personalized treatments, wellness activities, and peaceful oceanfront setting, the hotel is perfectly suited for relaxation, rejuvenation, and holistic well-being, whether for a short escape or an extended retreat.

### What makes the Spa by L’Occitane experience unique?

What truly sets the Spa by L’Occitane in Algarve apart is the ongoing annual training provided by the brand, ensuring therapists continuously refine their techniques, gestures, and sensory expertise. Combined with personalized advice, exceptional human touch, and premium products, the spa delivers a highly differentiated wellness experience rooted in authenticity, expertise, and emotional well-being.

### Does the spa offer a boutique or retail space?

Yes, the spa features a dedicated L’Occitane boutique with a separate street-level entrance. Guests can purchase iconic products such as Almond Body Oil, Immortelle Divine face cream, and the Cocooning Relaxing Pillow Mist, allowing them to extend the wellness experience beyond their stay at Bela Vista.
