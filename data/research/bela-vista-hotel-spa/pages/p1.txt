## A house

The horizon begins in your gaze

and lingers in your memory.

### with a soul

### overlooking the sea

Bela Vista Hotel & Spa is a 38-room, 5-star hotel on the clifftop at Praia da Rocha, in Portimão, Algarve. It has welcomed guests since 1934, in a palace built as a private villa in 1918. It is a Relais & Châteaux member and holds Two MICHELIN Keys from the MICHELIN Guide.

## High on the cliffs of Praia da Rocha, Hotel Bela Vista preserves the spirit of a house that has truly mastered the art of welcoming. Between century-old walls and a sea that changes shade     by the hour, life unfolds at the pace of things done with time and enjoyed fully.

More\\
\\
More

There are 38 rooms, a team that remembers every name, and a story whispered through stained glass and aged wood.

The hotel occupies three houses above the beach. The Palacete is the original 1918 villa, "Vila Nossa Senhora das Dores", built for the canning industrialist António Júdice de Magalhães Barros; Casa Azul faces the Atlantic; Casa Jardim opens onto the garden. Together they hold 38 rooms and

suites. The hotel has operated on this site since 1934. Its restaurant, Vista, holds 1 MICHELIN Star, and its spa is a Spa by L'Occitane.

" Here, silence has texture, light settles slowly, and the sea is an everyday neighbor. "

Rooms

## every room tells    its own story

“A place designed for rest that feels like the very best dream.”

In the Palacete, antique colors and oil-painted details preserve the soul of the house. Golden tones and painted ceilings embrace winter days and late afternoons.

In the Blue House, everything feels vibrant and full of light, echoing the sea that pours in through the windows.

The Garden House opens onto calm and palm trees, with its feet almost touching the Atlantic.

## Frequently  Asked Questions

### Is Bela Vista Hotel & Spa a luxury hotel in the Algarve?

Yes. Bela Vista Hotel & Spa is a 5-star hotel in Praia da Rocha, Portimão, in the Algarve, Portugal. It has 38 rooms and suites across three houses, is a Relais & Châteaux member (since 2013), and holds Two MICHELIN Keys. Its restaurant, Vista, holds 1 MICHELIN Star.

### Where is Bela Vista Hotel & Spa located?

Bela Vista Hotel & Spa is located in Portimão, in the Algarve region of southern Portugal, overlooking Praia da Rocha. Its clifftop location offers direct sea views while remaining close to beaches, cultural sites, and the Algarve’s most beautiful landscapes.

### Is Bela Vista a Relais & Châteaux property?

Yes, Bela Vista Hotel & Spa is an official Relais & Châteaux hotel in Portugal, recognized for its excellence in hospitality, gastronomy, authenticity, and sense of place.

### When did Bela Vista Hotel & Spa open?

The building opened as a private villa, "Vila Nossa Senhora das Dores", in 1918. It has operated as a hotel since 1934. CNN Travel describes Bela Vista as the first hotel to open on the Algarve, in 1934. The Palacete is that original 1918 building and is still in use.

### What third-party recognition does Bela Vista Hotel & Spa hold?

The hotel is a Relais & Châteaux member (since 2013) and holds Two MICHELIN Keys, the Boa Cama Boa Mesa Chave de Prata (Silver Key, first awarded 2025), and a TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice 2026 award. It was named Luxury Boutique Hotel for Portugal at the World Luxury Hotel Awards in 2014, and CNN Travel listed it in "Europe's 20 most beautiful hotels" in 2016. Its restaurant, Vista, holds 1 MICHELIN Star, awarded 2017.

‍

### What makes Bela Vista unique compared to other 5-star hotels in Portugal?

Bela Vista is a 38-room hotel rather than a resort, set in a 1918 palace on the Praia da Rocha clifftop and operating as a hotel since 1934. It is a Relais & Châteaux member with Two MICHELIN Keys, and it has a 1-MICHELIN-Star restaurant, Vista, on site.

### Is Bela Vista suitable for couples / romantic getaways?

Bela Vista is an adults-oriented hotel with sea-view rooms, a Spa by L'Occitane, and a 1-MICHELIN-Star restaurant on site, and it is a common choice for couples, honeymoons and anniversaries. The hotel is not recommended for children.

### When is the best time to visit Bela Vista Hotel & Spa?

The best time to visit Bela Vista is from April to October, with spring and autumn being ideal for wellness, gastronomy, and quieter luxury stays in the Algarve.

### Is Bela Vista Hotel & Spa pet-friendly?

No, Bela Vista Hotel & Spa is not a pet-friendly hotel and does not allow animals on the property, including guest rooms and common areas. This policy helps maintain high standards of comfort, cleanliness, and tranquility for all guests staying in Praia da Rocha, Portimão, in the Algarve region of Portugal. Guests travelling with pets are advised to look for pet-friendly accommodation options nearby.
