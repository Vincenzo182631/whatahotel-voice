Dating from 1918, the Palacete is the living heart of Bela Vista. The original structure has remained intact, preserved only with what was necessary for it to keep breathing in the present. The style blends Portuguese heritage with oriental echoes in warm tones, deep blues and golden details.

Nothing is accidental and nothing repeats itself. The old speaks with the modern as if it had always been that way.

The rooms at Casa Jardim were designed to blend into the landscape rather than impose on it. The walls alternate between shades of blue and green, colours that breathe the same air as the sea and the pine forest. The wardrobes and details are inspired by the old fishermen’s houses, simple and honest, where everything has a purpose and a charm of its own.

Facing the Atlantic, Casa Azul is where the sea enters through the window. Everything here is light, reflecting the movement of the water outside. It was conceived as an extension of the horizon: simple, open, fresh. It is the sunniest part of the hotel. Here you can sit facing the sea and let the wind move slowly around you.

A dive into haute cuisine

Vista is not just a restaurant. It is a marine organism that the guest enters not to observe, but to belong to. Fish and seafood arrive daily from Algarve ports. The rest comes from precision and imagination.

‍

The dining room does not fully separate from the bar or the kitchen because everything happens in continuity, like an ecosystem where the ocean sets the tempo. The guest becomes part of the landscape, surrounded by blue reflections, corals and air bubbles, immersed in the vibration of a place

that breathes the sea.

"Dinner is a calm immersion where space, light and the subtle sound of the Atlantic intertwine."

Chef João Oliveira

Executive Chef of Vista Restaurant in Portimão, awarded 1 Michelin Star since 2017.
