# Between the city charm    and the tranquility of the sea

Portimão lies in the far southwest of Europe, where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Mediterranean spirit. In one of the city’s most emblematic places, Praia da Rocha, Bela Vista Hotel & Spa rises on the cliff with a privileged and breathtaking view over the ocean.

Location

### Feel like you were expected all along.

Then it is time to let the Atlantic do the rest.

## The Algarve region is known for its fantastic climate and for landscapes, traditions and gastronomy found nowhere else. The beaches of Portimão stretch for kilometres of golden sand. A         true paradise for an unforgettable holiday, to be enjoyed both on land and at sea.

## The elegance of the surroundings

Just a short distance from the hotel are the city centre and the picturesque villages of Alvor and Ferragudo, pearls of the Algarve’s rural and maritime heritage. A brief drive takes you to Monchique, the highest point in the region. Only a few more kilometres separate you from the historic towns of

Silves, Lagos and Sagres, the southwestern tip of continental Europe.
