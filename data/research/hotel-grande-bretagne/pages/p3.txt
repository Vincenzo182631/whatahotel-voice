## Afternoon Tea at the Winter Garden

A custom carried from India to Britain in the early 17th century. The 1,500 different kinds of tea prove the dedication of the English and its intrinsic presence in British culture. Since its inception, the Winter Garden tea serving and ritual became a landmark among tea consumers and connoisseurs. Perceived as the most opulent tea experience, accompanied by the finest selection of sweet and savory delicacies, the Afternoon Tea of the historic hotel is exclusively curated by its talented Pastry & Executive chefs.

### American Breakfast Buffet

As the sun rises in the morning, the extravagant American Breakfast Buffet indulges every culinary whim. Choose from a selection of cold and hot dishes, freshly baked pastries, fresh-squeezed juices, and a wide selection of teas and fresh-brewed coffee.

### Mediterranean Lunch

Enjoy the cooling breeze while sitting down to a delightful lunch. Under a deep blue sky, taste creations based on the Mediterranean culinary tradition and the application of modern haute cuisine techniques. Premium fresh raw materials and inexhaustible inspiration are combined to compose a selection of mouthwatering dishes.

### Idyllic Dinner

Dinner is perfect when the city lights come on and the cosmopolitan aura becomes apparent. A praise to the new Mediterranean cuisine, with emphasis on quality raw materials and excellent technique. The expanded wine list, reflects the major wine producing regions of the world and offers a wide variety of excellent choices from the head sommelier, recommending the ideal combination for dinner.

The Team

The GB Roof Garden team is comprised of the talented Chef Asterios Koustoudis, who is awarded with a Michelin star and is the Executive Chef Hotel Grande Bretagne and King George Hotel. He believes in simple cuisine, with premium quality fresh ingredients and advanced cooking technique, in order to achieve clean flavors and exquisite tastes. Chef De Cuisine, Giannis Rokanas is a highly qualified chef with an extensive experience in fine dining. Finally, Head Sommelier Evangelos Psofidis, who has been working for Hotel Grande Bretagne since 2006, has obtained multiple certifications on wine tasting. Our talented Executive Chef Asterios Koustoudis and his highly experienced team received one Toque d'Or award for GB Roof Garden Restaurant.
