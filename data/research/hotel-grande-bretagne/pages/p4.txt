## Discover Athens on Foot

Our Concierge team has prepared a unique 90 minutes walking tour for the guests of Hotel Grande Bretagne. Discover hidden gems in the heart of Athens, all within walking distance from the hotel. Cost is €35 per person, for 4 min.- 8 max. persons. Contact the GB Concierge for further details.

Athens Panorama City Tour

This tour will give you a unique perspective of the history of ancient and modern Athens and an opportunity to observe the striking contrasts that make Athens such a fascinating city, by revealing stories that are not included in any tourist guide.

The Panorama of Athens tour, begins with a visit at the Acropolis complex. Our expert guide will give you a tour of the Propylea, the Temple of Wingless Nike, the Temple of Erechtheion and the landmark of Athens, the Parthenon temple.

On your way down and by the slope of Acropolis you'll explore the ancient theatre of Dionysos, and the Odeon of Herodes Atticus. A 60 minutes tour, to the spectacular Acropolis museum, is highly recommended.

The tour drive continues to the Temple of Olympian Zeus, the biggest temple of mainland Europe and the Hadrian’s Arch,  located in front of the temple.

Next short stop is the Panathenaic Stadium, all made by white marble. This marvelous stadium can fit 64.000 spectators and was used for the first Olympics of the modern era, in 1896.

The tour continues in-front of the House of Greek Parliament and the tomb of the Unknown Soldier, in order to watch the change of the Guards.

Departing from the Parliament and the tomb of the Unknown Soldier, the tour drives you in-front of the Athens Catholic Cathedral of St. Paul Areopagites and in-front of the neoclassical trilogy of Athens (the Academy of Athens, the old University of Athens and Old National Library).

Last stop is Lycabettus Hill, the highest location of Athens city (278 meters), in order to enjoy the spectacular view of Attica Region, from the mountains in the north, to the Saronic Golf, in the south.

### Sightseeing Beyond Athens

### Sounion

The drive along the coastal road goes past the countless private coves and lovely beaches of Glyfada and Varkiza. From the comfort of the luxury motorcoach, bask in the splendid views of the Saronic Gulf while heading toward the tip of the Attiki peninsula to Cape Sounion. Here on a clifftop, overhanging the deep green Aegean Sea stands the tour’s ultimate destination—the spectacular Temple of Poseidon, built in the Doric style in 5th century BC as a place of worship to the god of the sea.

### Hydra, Poros, and Aegina

Sail for the picturesque island of Hydra, where if you have time at leisure, you can enjoy some strolling, shopping or swimming. In the early afternoon sail for the island of Aegina, passing through the narrow strait separating the Peloponnesian coast from the island of Poros. Upon arrival in Aegina you can either join the excursion to the Temple of Aphaia or walk around the scenic main town.

Enjoy some lunch, served on board and proceed with sailing to Poros for short visit, before embarking for the sail back to Piraeus Port.

### Delphi

The tour includes a visit to the majestic monastery of St. Lukas and Arachova, a beautiful village that maintains its traditional architecture. The visit to Delphi usually lasts for approximately three hours, so that there is enough time to walk around the archaeological site, the museum of Delphi and the streets of the villages.

### Argolis, Mycenae & Epidaurus

After traversing the canal of Corinth, which is uniting the Ionian and the Aegean Sea since 1893, you arrive at the ancient site of Mycenaes site, where a detailed guided tour is organized. The next stop is the Quaint of Nafplion, the previous capital of Greece, before Athens. Enjoy a tasteful lunch and possibly some of the best fried asparagus you have ever tasted. After departing from Nafplion, you head towards the famous Ancient Theater of Asklepios at Epidaurus, a UNESCO World Heritage Center and you can attend a guided tour of Epidavros, before heading back to Athens.

## Ancient Attractions

Witness to Athens’ singular mix of old and new, Hotel Grande Bretagne is a gateway to the vivid sights and sounds that capture the unmatched spirit of Europe’s oldest capital. Located at the corner of the impressive Syntagma Square, the hotel offers uninterrupted views of the Acropolis, while the ritual changing of the Evzone guard in front of the Parliament building occurs virtually on its doorstep. In addition to such celebrated attractions as the Parthenon, the Temple of Poseidon, the National Archeological Museum, and Cape Sounion, we have a number of special nearby places to explore.

### Sightseeing in Athens

### Lycabettus Hill

Approximately 300 meters high, this favorite spot among locals and visitors alike boasts superb views and a sense of tranquility amidst the sprawling Athenian cityscape. Intrepid travelers may ascend the hill on foot. The funicular provides an easier alternative.

### Thission

One of the most intact temples of ancient Greece is located at one end of the Ancient Agora. First built in 450 BC, the Pnyx, the hill on the west side of the Acropolis, was the meeting and debating venue for the Athenians. Nowadays, Thission is one of the priority choices for an evening walk along its cobblestones and numerous elegant restaurants & bars that set the scene for the perfect city experience.

### Ancient Agora

The most famous Agora in Greece is placed right inside the renowned Stoa of Attalos, at the foot of the Acropolis. The spiritual sense of the place where ancient Athenians used to have their everyday commercial and social activities will fill you with a deep desire to buy rare artifacts and miniature models of how the Acropolis and the Agora looked during ancient times.

### Zappeion

Zappeion is built between the temple of Olympian Zeus - the largest ancient temple in Greece which was dedicated to the Olympian god Zeus and was being built since the 6th century until 700 years later- and The National Gardens. The Zappeion was once the first building in the world to be constructed in order to serve Olympic needs and is today, one of the most beautiful walks in the heart of Athens.

### The First Olympic Stadium of Athens

Also known as Panathenaic Stadium, is situated right opposite the National Gardens’ entrance on Vassileos Konstantinou Avenue. It was built in 330BC and it was the venue to host the Zappas Olympics in 1870 and 1875 as well as the first Modern Olympic Games of 1896. The sparkling marble can still convey the feeling of greatness of the 80.000 Athenians that were and still are able to watch some of the greatest eve

### Temple of Zeus

Just 700 meters south of Syntagma Square, the temple's foundations date back to the tyrant Pisistratus in 515 BC. Its graceful columns influenced the development of Corinthian style.

### The Acropolis

The New Acropolis Museum was established in 2009 and is named to be one of the highest-profile cultural projects undertaken in Europe for the last decade. Visit its 226.000 sq. meters space, and become proudly part of the city’s greatest and most historic museum.

Tranquility and Outdoors

##### The National Gardens

Once named “The Royal Gardens” are located right behind the Parliament. Standing at the very entrance of The Garden, one can immediately feel the magnitude of the place which is surrounded by extremely high palm-tress. Hosting a large number of walkways, small lakes, and rich vegetation, this peaceful place will give you the absolute sense of tranquillity even though in the heart of the city centre.

##### Lycabettus Hill

The Lycabettus Hill is located approximately 300 meters high. This favourite spot among locals and visitors alike boasts superb views and a sense of tranquility amidst the sprawling Athenian landscape. Intrepid travelers may ascend the hill on foot. The funicular is an easier alternative.

Family Activities in Athens

##### The Museum of Greek Children’s Art

The Museum of Greek Children’s Art is a pioneering Museum, one of the very few worldwide that exhibits exclusively drawings and three-dimensional artworks created by children 5 to 14 years old.

##### The Benaki Museum Childhood, Toys and Games Exhibition

The collection includes 20,000 toys, games and childhood items, from antiquity to 1970, originating not only from Greece and the broader Greek world but from Europe, America, Africa and the East as well.

##### Stavros Niarchos Foundation Cultural Center

The Stavros Niarchos Park is ideal for children, who can play undisturbed in its playgrounds, but also for all visitors, who will be able to enjoy their leisure walk, exercise or attend concerts, exhibitions, and a multitude of other events.

##### Athens Happy Train Athens Sightseeing

The Athens Happy Train is a toy-like street train that starts its sightseeing tour from Syntagma Square, near the hotel, and Monastiraki Square and follows an amazing route through the historical center of the city, the old neighborhoods and the hill of Acropolis.

##### Cultural Center Hellenic Cosmos The Virtual Reality Theater - Tholos

The interactive shows at the Tholos are a unique experience of immersion into the virtual world. The virtual tours include interactive tours to ancient Greek sites and culture.

##### City Sightseeing Bus: A Tour Through History & Culture

Each tour with the sightseeing buses takes approximately 90 minutes and has a commentary in eleven languages that traces the history and culture of Athens. You can Hop On Hop Off from any one of the 27 stops as many times as you wish.
