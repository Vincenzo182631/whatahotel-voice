## Blissful Living on the  Shores of Grace Bay

Whether you select a suite with infinite Caribbean Ocean views or mature meticulously landscaped gardens, you will discover a level of style and comfort that sets the standard for luxury resorts in Turks and Caicos.

All suites at The Palms Turks and Caicos feature king-sized beds with custom hand-tufted bedding, beautiful bathrooms with quality bathing products, high-speed wi-fi, cable television, DVD/CD player, coffee and espresso maker, signature robes and an electronic safe. Many suites also boast an in-suite washer/dryer and a full kitchen, complete with modern appliances. When you choose to stay at The Palms Turks and Caicos, you can rest assured your accommodations will enrich your experience on Providenciales.
