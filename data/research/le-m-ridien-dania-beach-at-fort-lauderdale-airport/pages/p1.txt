## Inspire innovation and productivity at our Dania Beach hotel

Embrace an all-encompassing sanctuary for modern luxury at Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport in Florida. Plan each day's adventures in a sleek, minimalist room or suite with plush bedding and scenic skyline views before exploring the amenities of our hotel near the Port Everglades Cruise Port. Guests can take a dip in our heated outdoor pool, work out in our fitness center and yoga room, and enjoy the convenience of staying at a hotel in Dania Beach, Florida. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport?

On-site self parking is available at Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport. The daily self parking fee is $32.10 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport is Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL). FLL is located approximately 2.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport have an airport shuttle to Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL)?

Yes, Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport has an airport shuttle to Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL).

Does Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Le Méridien Dania Beach at Fort Lauderdale Airport does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
