#### Pet Policy at One&Only Mandarina

- Pets must weigh less than 50 pounds

- We do not allow pets in our Treehouses

- Pets are to be kept on leash at all times

- Do not leave your pet unattended

- Pets are not permitted in our restaurants, spa, fitness centre or pool areas including cabanas and daybeds – this does not apply to guide or service dogs

- Pets are welcome on our beach area

#### Pet Fees

- USD 100 charge per pet, per day will be applied to your villa account. An additional pet deposit of USD 3,000 is required for stays in our Private Homes.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Useful details you need to know before you travel to One&Only Mandarina

#### What are the passport, visa and vaccination requirements for Mexico?

All visitors to Mexico must present a passport valid for at least 6 months from the date of travel. Visa requirements for entering Mexico vary significantly from country to country. Click here for more information.

#### What is the time zone in Mexico?

Nayarit, Mexico is located within the US Central Time. It is five hours behind Greenwich Mean Time (GMT - 5).

#### What is the local currency in Mexico?

The peso is the official currency of Mexico. As a result of a change in Mexican regulations, One&Only Mandarina does not accept settlement of any bills in US dollars. Instead, all accounts need to be settled by credit card. One&Only Mandarina does not accept cash payments.

#### Which credit cards are accepted at One&Only Mandarina?

- American Express
- Visa
- Mastercard
- Union Pay
- Discover

#### Where is the nearest ATM from One&Only Mandarina?

For guest convenience an ATM is located at Alma, bills are dispensed in pesos.

#### What internet services are available at One&Only Mandarina?

Complimentary high-speed WiFi service is available throughout the resort.

#### What languages are spoken in Mexico?

Staff members are fluent in both English and Spanish.

#### What is the address of One&Only Mandarina?

Carretera Federal Libre 200 Tepic-Puerto Vallarta, El Monteón, Municipio de Compostela, Nayarit, C.P. 63724, Mexico

#### What is the weather like in Mexico?

The sun shines almost every day in Riviera Nayarit, with average temperatures in the low 80s. High season is from October to April while July and August are hotter with higher humidity.

#### What are the check-in and check-out times at One&Only Mandarina?

Check-in is at 3pm and check-out is at 12pm.

#### What is the electrical voltage at One&Only Mandarina?

Electricity is supplied at 120 volts. Adaptors are available upon request.
