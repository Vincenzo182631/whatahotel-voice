Experiences
The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul offers a wealth of destination activities that cater to every taste and preference. Explore the city's rich history and cultural heritage from our Istanbul hotel with a spa, indulge in luxury shopping and dining, or enjoy local sights. Take a leisurely stroll along the Bosphorus and admire iconic landmarks, or immerse yourself in vibrant street life and markets. Visit nearby museums and art galleries, or enjoy a private yacht cruise for a unique perspective. Our Istanbul hotel with a pool features a state-of-the-art gym, luxurious spa treatments, and Turkish Hammam, offering a perfect escape from the city’s hustle. With endless options for exploration and relaxation, The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul ensures every stay is unforgettable.

From the Hagia Sophia to the Grand Bazaar, Discover Istanbul’s Attractions
Istanbul has long stood at the intersection between East and West, and at the heart of it all is The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul. Soaring above the city near the banks of the Bosphorus, this five-star hotel is just moments away from the Hagia Sophia, Grand Bazaar and Blue Mosque. Yet Istanbul pulses with new experiences as well. Whether you seek modern art or artisan crafts, this ancient city is layered with experiences awaiting your discovery.
Arts and Culture
Istanbul Modern Museum
The Istanbul Museum of Modern Art is the first private museum in Türkiye to exhibit modern and contemporary art collections and was founded in 2004.
Contact: 90 212 334-7300
Miniatürk
Miniatürk exhibits 105 miniature models of the most prominent monumental works reflecting the heritage of Türkiye.
Contact: 90 212 222-2882
Ortaköy
Surely one of Istanbul’s loveliest seaside suburbs, Ortaköy, with its narrow cobbled streets, fine food and spectacular location under the Bosphorus Bridge- the first bridge to connect the European side of Istanbul to Asia- makes for a lovely day trip. Each Sunday, a large open air arts and craft market takes.
Distance: 10 minutes
Pera Museum
Contact: 90 212 334-9900
Rahmi M. Koç Museum
The Rahmi M. Koç Museum is the first major museum in Türkiye dedicated to the history of transportation, industry and communications.
Contact: 90 212 334-7300
Sakıp Sabancı Museum
The historical mansion and new gallery annex are now a museum hosting national and foreign exhibitions.
Contact: 90 212 277-2200
The Archaeological Museum
The Archaeological Museum proudly displays more than 60,000 Greco-Roman, Byzantine, Ottoman and Anatolian antiquities.
Discover Where East Meets West
Before it was Istanbul, the city on the Bosphorus was known as Byzantium, and then Constantinople, the heart of the Ottoman Empire. Uncovering this city that bridges Europe and Asia offers unlimited opportunities that The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul can arrange through private and guided tours to its most revered cultural and historical attractions.

Guest Activities
There is no one way to experience Istanbul. Some may be here to tour art museums and search for antiquities, while others may wish to spend their time tasting their way through Turkish culinary traditions. Whatever your interests, the Ladies and Gentlemen at The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul have crafted walking tours and itineraries to fulfill every traveler’s curiosity. It would be our pleasure to assist your needs, please directly contact with us (
rc.istrz.concierge@ritzcarlton.com
) for more details about the tours in the city.
Full Day Old City Tour
From the soaring domes of Hagia Sophia and the exquisite tiles of the Blue Mosque to the historic Hippodrome Square and the atmospheric Underground Cistern, this specially curated private tour invites you to explore the most fascinating sights of Istanbul’s Old City. Step into a city that has been the capital of four great empires over the last two thousand years, the Eastern Roman, Byzantine, short-lived Latin, and Ottoman, and let a professional guide bring its imperial landmarks and hidden stories vividly to life.
Designed as the ultimate experience for first-time visitors, the tour allows you to tailor the program to your interests and enjoy plenty of time to ask questions. Alongside these iconic monuments, immerse yourself in the bustling energy of the Grand Bazaar, the city’s lively streets, and its centuries-old traditions.
Highlights: Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, Hippodrome Square, Underground Cistern, Grand Bazaar
Duration: 6–7 hours
Distance from hotel to Old City: 20–25 minutes by car
Istanbulite
Discover Istanbul’s hidden corners on a private guided tour through its most charming and authentic neighborhoods.
Step beyond the city’s main landmarks and experience Istanbul like a local. Your professional guide will tailor the route to your interests, leading you through vibrant districts such as Beşiktaş, Kuzguncuk, Üsküdar, Çengelköy, Balat, Karaköy, and Çukurcuma, where the city’s true spirit comes alive. Stroll along colorful streets, visit artisan shops, historic mosques, bustling markets, and cozy cafés, while uncovering hidden stories and learning about Istanbul’s rich and diverse cultures.
Duration: 4–6 hours (flexible)
Includes: Private vehicle & licensed English-speaking guide
Optional: Stop for local coffee, tea, or a Bosphorus-view lunch
Notes: Choose from curated routes such as Balat-Fener, Karaköy-Beyoğlu-Çukurcuma, Nişantaşı-Beşiktaş, Kadıköy-Moda, or Üsküdar-Kuzguncuk-Çengelköy for a fully personalized experience.
TasteIST
Cross the iconic Bosphorus Bridge, connecting Europe and Asia, and ascend to Camlica Hill, the highest point in Istanbul, for breathtaking panoramic views of the Bosphorus, the Princes’ Islands, and the sprawling cityscape. Visit the Grand Camlica Mosque, Istanbul’s newest and largest mosque, completed in 2019, and marvel at its stunning architecture.
Continue to the charming Çengelköy district, home to an 800-year-old Monumental Plane Tree, one of the few remaining trees planted by the Ottoman Empire in newly conquered lands. Enjoy its shade while overlooking the Bosphorus. Delight in a taste of homemade chocolate before exploring Kucuksu Palace, the Ottoman summer and hunting retreat later used as a state guesthouse, featured in films such as James Bond: The World Is Not Enough and Bollywood’s Ek Tha Tiger.
Drive along the Bosphorus shoreline to Goksu River and visit Anadolu Hisarı, a medieval fortress built by Sultan Beyazıd I to aid in the siege of Constantinople. In Kanlıca, famous for its sweet yoghurt topped with caster sugar, enjoy a tasting in a café overlooking the Bosphorus while admiring historic wooden waterfront mansions.
The tour concludes at the Khedive’s Palace (Hidiv Kasrı), a 1907 residence of Khedive Abbas II of Egypt and Sudan, showcasing Art Nouveau architecture inspired by Italian Renaissance villas with neo-classical Ottoman details. Along the way, savor traditional Turkish treats including Simit (Turkish bagel), stuffed mussels, homemade chocolate, sweet yoghurt, and Turkish tea or coffee.
Duration: 4–5 hours
Notes: Please inform the concierge of any food allergies or intolerances, as The Ritz-Carlton Istanbul cannot assume responsibility for issues arising from undisclosed dietary restrictions. Private transportation with Mercedes-Benz vehicles (Vito or Sprinter, depending on group size) and a professional driver is available upon request.
Shop’n Roll
Lose yourself in the vibrant sights, scents, and sounds of Istanbul’s bustling bazaars on this guided shopping tour. Begin at the exotic Egyptian Bazaar, where colorful spices, nuts, herbs, and sweets create a feast for the senses, then wander through the Grand Bazaar, one of the world’s largest covered markets, with over 4,000 stalls offering everything from carpets and ceramics to jewelry and textiles. Along the way, watch skilled artisans at work, from carpet weaving to calligraphy and pottery, learn the art of haggling from your expert guide, and uncover hidden corners to find unique souvenirs, all while enjoying a safe and personalized experience in the heart of Istanbul.
Ottoman Splendors
Step into the glorious legacy of the Ottoman Empire, which ruled over Eurasia for seven centuries, and explore its monumental landmarks and rich cultural heritage. Wander through the opulent Topkapı Palace, admire the grandeur of Süleymaniye Mosque and its surrounding neighborhood, and stroll the colorful Spice Bazaar. Experience traditional Turkish coffee houses and sweet shops, while discovering the multicultural fabric of Istanbul, shaped by Greeks, Armenians, Jews, Venetians, Balkans, and Turks.
Highlights & Places Visited: Topkapı Palace Museum, Süleymaniye Mosque and complex, Spice Bazaar, traditional Turkish coffee house, traditional Turkish sweet and delight shops
Duration: 6–7 hours
Private Bosphorus Yacht Cruise
Lined with grand palaces, historic mansions, and lush gardens, the Bosphorus is one of Istanbul’s most enchanting sights, best experienced by boat. This private cruise offers a unique perspective on the city’s history and culture, as you navigate both the European and Asian shores, passing charming fishing villages, magnificent waterfront estates, and vibrant neighborhoods. Perfectly blending history, nature, and luxury, the tour allows you to relax and soak in the beauty of Istanbul from the water.
Highlights: Private yacht embarkation from Kabataş İskelesi, 2+ hour cruise along the Bosphorus, European and Asian shores, disembark at Kabataş or a preferred Bosphorus seafood restaurant
Upon request: Snacks and alcoholic beverages can be served on board for an extra cost. Tea, coffee, and water are complimentary
A Spa Retreat Built on the Healing Traditions of Istanbul
Spa at The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul strives to take guests on a healing journey, starting from within. The spa’s centerpiece is a hammam, modeled after the 16th-century Turkish baths of Roxelana and offering separate areas for ladies and gentlemen as well as a couple’s suite. The menu also features results-driven treatments including facials, massages, and body treatments. In the summer, guests enjoy a breathtaking infinity pool; in addition, there is access to an indoor pool under a ceiling of twinkling lights, a soaking tub (28°C), a sauna, a steam room, and a fitness center.

Frequently Asked Questions
At The Ritz-Carlton Istanbul, we have the delight of offering the truly exclusive Charme d’Orient treatments and products. These highly efficacious treatments have the suitability for all skins, specifically those concerned with premature aging and environmental stress.
For your convenience, we have compiled answers to common questions you may find helpful in choosing the types of treatment that best meet your individual needs or desires.
Is there a minimum age for enjoying spa services?
Minors may use the swimming pool when accompanied by their parents or a legal guardian between 9 a.m. – 5 p.m.
The age for the fitness center is 18
Minors of all ages may not use spa facilities (e.g., lockers, whirlpools, saunas)
Minors aged 18 and younger may receive manicures and pedicures when accompanied by a legal guardian
No Treatments on minors can be given in guest rooms or private rooms even with a legal guardian present
Massage is for those aged 18 and over
Facials for those aged 18 and over
What if I am late for my appointment?
Arriving late will simply limit the time for your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest is not delayed. The full value of the treatment will be applied.
What is the cancellation policy?
Should you wish to change or cancel your spa reservation please do provide us with a minimum of 6 hours’ notice to avoid a 100% cancellation charge.
For all cancellations taking place 8 hours or earlier, prior to the scheduled treatment, no cancellation fee is applied.
50% of the total treatment cost is to be charged as cancellation fee, if the treatment is cancelled between 6-8 hours before the scheduled time
The total treatment cost is to be charged as cancellation fee if the treatment is cancelled less than 6 hours before the scheduled time.
What about payment for spa services?
We accept all major credit cards and cash. If you are a guest of the hotel you may also charge Spa Services to your room.
What about gratuities?
Gratuities are not included in the spa treatment prices. They are a matter of personal discretion.
What is VAT?
20% VAT is included in the spa treatment prices.
