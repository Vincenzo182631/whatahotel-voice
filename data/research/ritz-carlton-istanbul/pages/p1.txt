Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul
Enhance your Turkey vacation at our luxury Istanbul hotel
The Ritz-Carlton is a luxury hotel in Istanbul, located in the prestigious Dolmabahçe area overlooking the Bosphorus, with many of the city's cultural attractions within walking distance. This Istanbul hotel in Turkey is close to Istanbul Airport, just 40 minutes by car. The hotel offers 239 rooms and suites. The meeting facilities consist of five meeting rooms, one boardroom and a ballroom that can be divided into three sections. Dining facilities are Atölye Restaurant for Anatolian Kitchen, Nobu Istanbul for Japanese-Peruvian Cuisine, Limoré, which offers a menu rooted in the traditions of Italian cuisine, Bleu Lounge for all-day dining and cocktails, and The Roof for an urban oasis, a stylish, sleek sunbathing terrace, massage rooms and an infinity pool. This hotel on the Bosphorus also offers a full-service spa with a Turkish Hammam, a couple's Hammam Suite, an indoor swimming pool and fitness center, enhancing its reputation as a leading spa hotel in Istanbul for guests seeking relaxation and wellness.

Istanbul, Turkey
Overlooking the Bosphorus, The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul combines European elegance with Turkish heritage. Guests can enjoy authentic moments, from sipping Turkish coffee in the lobby to savoring Anatolian flavors at Atölye or world-renowned Japanese-Peruvian cuisine at Nobu. The five-star hotel offers newly redesigned rooms and suites, a variety of spa experiences, nearly 26,000 square feet of event space, and three restaurants and bars, all with stunning Bosphorus views.

Experience Our Nobu Suite
The Nobu Suite at The Ritz-Carlton Istanbul is the epitome of luxury, offering a unique blend of Japanese design. The suite features a stunning ofuro tub on the terrace, inviting guests to soak in the warm water while taking in the magnificent views of the Bosphorus. The ofuro tub is a traditional Japanese soaking tub that is an essential part of Japanese culture, representing purification and relaxation. The Nobu Suite provides guests with an authentic Japanese experience, allowing them to immerse themselves in the rich cultural heritage of Japan while enjoying the modern amenities of a world-class hotel. Whether you're looking for a romantic getaway or a rejuvenating escape, the Nobu Suite is the perfect choice for those who appreciate the finer things in life.

The City Is Within Your Reach
Emerging as a symbol of contemporary luxury and rich tradition, The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul is an invitation to experience the best of what Istanbul has to offer. Sustainability
The Ladies and Gentlemen of The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul are recognized for their refined hospitality, guided by respect, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Genuine and inclusive interactions regardless of background, culture, or origin form the foundation of our service culture. Deeply connected to the city we call home, we honor our social and environmental responsibilities through thoughtful actions that support our community. For us, Community Footprints creating meaningful impact through collective care begin with a sincere smile.
Linen Program
As part of our commitment to sustainable luxury, we offer a mindful linen and towel program. Towels are replaced only when placed on the floor, bed, or chair, or when visibly soiled. Bed linens are elegantly refreshed in every 3 days, upon request, after departure, or as needed. This thoughtful approach allows us to reduce water and energy use while maintaining the highest standards of comfort and care.
Every Action Counts
At The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul, we believe small steps can create meaningful change. From reducing single-use plastics and recycling responsibly to providing electric vehicle charging, our sustainable practices help protect the environment. By making thoughtful choices, we ensure energy and resources are used responsibly, leaving a positive impact for the future.
A Stronger, More Inclusive Future
Creating positive change takes intention and teamwork. At The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul, we collaborate with local organizations to direct our efforts where they can make the greatest difference. From collecting plastic caps to support mobility programs, preparing care packages for children and families, to donating food and essentials for animals in need, every small action contributes to a brighter community.
Your Stay, Your Impact: Get Involved
Enhance your experience at The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul by participating in meaningful social and environmental initiatives that support the local community. Guests can join in and make a positive impact with activities such as:
Plastic Cap Collection for TOFD: Donate plastic bottle caps to support mobility aids for individuals with spinal cord injuries through the Turkish Spinal Cord Paralytics Association (TOFD).
Donations for Children and Families with KAÇUV: Help assemble essential items and care packages for children and families supported by The Hope Foundation for Children with Cancer (KAÇUV) each year.
Support Local Animal Shelters: Provide food, toys, and supplies for local animal shelters to help care for animals in need.
Impact Hour: Participate in a curated conversation with a local social impact leader to learn more about community challenges and how your stay can make a difference.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 15.0 kgs.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul, with rates of €17.00 per hour, and €17.00 per day.
Off-site parking is also available near The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul. The daily valet fee is €17.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul is Istanbul Airport (ISL). ISL is located approximately 38.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Istanbul has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
