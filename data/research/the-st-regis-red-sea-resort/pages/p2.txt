# Exquisite Villas

## Accommodations

The St. Regis Red Sea Resort presents a collection of 90 private villas, comprising beachfront Dune Villas and overwater Coral Villas, each featuring a private pool and expansive outdoor living spaces. Accommodations range from one- to four-bedroom villas, thoughtfully designed to accommodate every style of stay, from solo escapes and romantic retreats to family holidays and gatherings. Inspired by the natural beauty of Ummahat Island, every villa is crafted with sustainability in mind, blending seamlessly into the island's landscape to create a harmonious connection with its surroundings while offering contemporary comfort, exceptional privacy, and timeless sophistication. Every stay is elevated by the signature St. Regis Butler Service, where anticipatory service, personalized amenities, and thoughtful attention to every detail create a truly bespoke guest experience.

Seamless Transfers Included

Premium sedan and speedboat transfers are included in your room rate. To confirm your arrangements and explore elevated transportation options, please contact reservations.redsea@stregis.com

Featured Stay

### Presidential Dune Villa

Luxuriate in an extraordinary beachfront destination, where natural island rhythms captivate. Imitating the free movement of the shoreline, the Presidential Dune Villa’s curved architecture creates a spacious 349m2 floorplan that seamlessly integrates with a vast 270m2 outdoor sundeck and private pool. Arranged around a statement living and dining space, the accommodation flows across two wings, encompassing a kitchen, butler’s pantry, study, media room, powder room, staff quarters and four en-suite bedrooms, including a master suite with walk-in closet and refreshing outdoor shower.

## The Astor Villas

Defined by its exceptional sea facing position, the 229m2 Astor Villa features curvaceous architectural shapes that reflect the island’s innate sense of freedom. Following a wave-like composition, the accommodation comprises of a master suite with walk-in closet and bathroom featuring an open-air shower. There are two further bedrooms, each with en-suite bathroom, alongside a media room, kitchenette, study, powder room and staff quarters. Indoor and outside spaces seamlessly merge, creating a natural flow between the central living and dining room and the spacious terrace with private pool.

Exquisite Beachfront Accomodation

## Two-Bedroom Coral Villa

Floating above pristine waters, each 158m2 Two-bedroom Coral Villa is an exclusive retreat that shares an independent synergy with the natural setting. Inspired by the neighboring coral reef, curved lines, spiral shapes and flowing spaces appear throughout the villa’s architecture and present an immersive backdrop for complete respite. Exceptional sea views dominate from every aspect, as the accommodation follows a circular design that features two bedrooms, both with en-suite bathroom, alongside a living and outdoor dining area, powder room and spacious sundeck with private pool.
