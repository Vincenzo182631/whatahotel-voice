# Welcome to The St. Regis Red Sea Resort

## Luxury Red Sea beach resort on a private island

Located on a pristine private island, The St. Regis Red Sea Resort presents exquisite living in the Ummahat archipelago. Fashioned from one of the world’s little-known natural treasures, this luxury Red Sea resort in Saudi Arabia artfully blends the brand’s visionary spirit, avant-garde style, and bespoke service with a progressive new benchmark for sustainable development. Accessed from the coast of Saudi Arabia via speedboat or seaplane, this Red Sea private island resort occupies a secluded position within the pioneering Red Sea project. A year-round destination, the island offers a serene setting for escapes and family stays. Across 90 overwater and beachfront villas, each with a spacious footprint and private pool, guests praise the tranquil setting, while every stay at this Red Sea beach resort is elevated by signature rituals, exceptional dining, a Children’s Club, and the iconic St. Regis Butler service.

Exquisite Design

### Drawn From Nature

Celebrating an extraordinary natural habitat, The St. Regis Red Sea Resort brings visionary design and flawless luxury to a treasured archipelago. Individual architecture by Kengo Kuma reflects the organic shapes of the landscape, where undulating curves have a kinetic quality that echo gentle waves and sand swept dunes. Seamlessly integrating indoor and outside spaces, distinctive bespoke interiors feature rich finishes and elemental textures that imitate the harmonious movement of the island winds. Introducing a new benchmark in responsible travel, sustainable materials have been carefully chosen for their indigenous credentials, resistance to the local climate and authentic connection with the destination.

## Luxurious Wellness

A sanctuary for the extraordinary, The St. Regis Spa celebrates all that is natural and sensorial through bespoke treatments and immersive activities. Embracing the environmental virtues of the Red Sea, with the cultural legacy of Saudi Arabia and trusted heritage of the St. Regis brand, the spa provides a journey of extraordinary indulgence and sophistication. Integral to each treatment is the stimulation of the five senses, bolstered by the use of sustainably sourced and precious ingredients, supported by the latest technologies. Partnering with leading skin care experts, AMRA our skilled team of therapists are internationally trained to provide an opulent wellness experience like no other. The spa also benefits from cold plunge and tranquil vitality pools, just steps from the Yoga Pavilion. Divided between male and female, the relaxation areas include a jacuzzi for women, while men can enjoy dipping into the warm and cold plunge pools.

The Locale

### Discover a Rare Treasure Trove

A previously untrodden area of outstanding natural beauty, the Ummahat archipelago is ideally located for those seeking a range of recreational pursuits. Home to the fourth largest barrier reef, teeming with sea life, it is a spectacular diving setting. Explore further afield and discover a host of inland must-sees, from Al Ula, the ancient Arabic oasis in Medina, to the culturally significant fishing village of Umluj.

## Family Bonding in Luxury

At The St. Regis Red Sea Resort, family time becomes an opportunity to reconnect, explore, and create lasting memories together. Within the privacy of our family-friendly villas, guests enjoy a refined sanctuary where comfort, space, and thoughtful design allow every generation to unwind with ease.

Younger guests can immerse themselves in creative moments at the children’s club, from boat making and coloring to pasta jewelry crafting and bookmark design. Meanwhile, teens and adults can enjoy tennis, paddle, water sports, pool games, and relaxed movie nights under the stars.

From playful beach adventures to quiet evenings shared as a family, each experience is thoughtfully designed to bring loved ones closer together, creating moments of joy, discovery, and connection in one of the Red Sea’s most extraordinary island settings.

Sustainability

## A Renewing Approach

Conceived as part of Red Sea Global’s regenerative tourism vision, The St. Regis Red Sea Resort aims to go beyond traditional sustainability practices to positively impact the surrounding environment. Developed in accordance with LEED Platinum certification standards, the resort’s architecture reflects a commitment to creating energy-efficient spaces that reduce environmental impact and enhance indoor air quality.

This philosophy extends across the island, where over 190,000 mangroves have been planted with the support of Red Sea Global, helping to restore and enrich the coastal ecosystem. While coral propagation programs are expected to launch soon through RSG’s wider efforts within the AMAALA destination, the resort currently supports marine conservation by promoting the use of coral-friendly sunscreens among guests and staff.

The location’s naturally low light pollution also offers excellent conditions for stargazing, making it a unique setting to reconnect with the rhythms of nature.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Red Sea Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Red Sea Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Red Sea Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Red Sea Resort.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Red Sea Resort?

The St. Regis Red Sea Resort does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Red Sea Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Red Sea Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Red Sea Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Red Sea Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Red Sea Resort is Red Sea International Airport (RSI). RSI is located approximately 33.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Red Sea Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Red Sea Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
