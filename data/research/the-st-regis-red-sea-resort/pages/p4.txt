# A Sanctuary for the Extraordinary

## Spa

Offering serenity and relaxation, The St. Regis Spa is an exceptional sensorial retreat that celebrates its unique private island setting, the cultural ancestry of Saudia Arabia and enduring legacy of The St. Regis Brand. Honoring nature, the menu of indulgent treatments has been thoughtfully designed as a tribute to Ummahat Island’s extraordinary natural environment.

Perfectly Tailored Treatments

## Caroline’s Collection

Innately connected to the St. Regis brand, Caroline Astor defined luxury, opulence and taste to become an icon within the upper echelons of New York society. Her legacy lives on through a thoughtfully curated selection of spa treatments that are unique to St. Regis Spa. These luxuriously immersive experiences are crafted from the very best ingredients, that are entirely decadent and specialized. Each collection is tailored to individual requirements and encapsulates Caroline’s innovative and refined spirit.

Immersive Journeys

## Wellness by AMRA

At the forefront of skincare evolution, AMRA brings luxurious and pioneering therapies to The St. Regis Spa. Encouraging a holistic sense of wellbeing, each journey is individualized, tapping into mindfulness and specific skincare needs to promote a captivating experience. Rooted in sustainability and active formulations AMRA redefines spa treatments.

Massage

### St. Regis Signature Journey

An entirely personalized two-hour massage treatment, the St. Regis Signature Journey is a collaboration between therapist and guest to decide the massage that most suits individual needs. A choice of seven full body massages is offered, including Aroma, Relaxation, Energizing, Deep Tissue, Chakra Balancing or Pre/Post Mother to Be. The Prescribed Facial follows and targets specific skincare requirements, with an indulgent Foot or Scalp Massage completing the experience. Designed to release any remaining tension, it is a luxurious way to culminate the wellbeing practice.

Personal Wellness

### Gold Hot Stone Massage

A unique treatment using pure gold, the Gold Hot Stone Massage is a sensorial ritual where the body is bathed in 24kt Gold Body Oil, and precious hot stones are used to perform a soothing massage. Prized drops of gold are then smoothed over the skin to leave it illuminated and feeling supple and hydrated.

### Yoga Pavilion

Surrounded by the natural habitat of Ummahat archipelago, the Yoga Pavilion is an open-air platform from which to practice yoga and meditation. Combining the health benefits of connecting with nature, while working through postural exercises that balance mind, body and breath, the Pavilion’s unique design presents a holistic experience.

### Fitness

Hosting an array of cardiovascular, strengthening and elliptical equipment, including lap pool, the fitness suite is open 24/7. Ideal for those wishing to fine tune workout schedules, there is also a menu of personalized training sessions run by experts. Designed for all abilities, the classes target individual health goals, from HIIT, Conditional Training, Rehabilitation and much more.

### Luxurious Wellness

An unrivalled sanctuary dedicated to holistic wellness and ultimate relaxation, The St. Regis Spa Red Sea embraces the unique qualities of the destination in a world-first concept.
