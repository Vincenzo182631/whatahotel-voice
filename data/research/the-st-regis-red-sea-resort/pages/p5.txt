# The St. Regis Red Sea Resort Activities

## Experiences

The St. Regis Red Sea Resort offers a thoughtfully curated collection of experiences designed to inspire adventure, relaxation, and meaningful connection with one of the world's most extraordinary island destinations. Explore vibrant coral reefs through world-class diving, snorkeling, and watersports, restore balance at The St. Regis Spa, or enjoy enriching family moments at Little Treasures Kids Club. Guests can also discover the beauty of the surrounding islands, embrace bespoke wellness experiences, and participate in signature St. Regis rituals, all thoughtfully tailored by the St. Regis Butler Service to create memorable moments throughout every stay.

Travel to Ummahat Island

## Arrive in Style

Offering ultimate privacy and unrivalled exclusivity, Ummahat Island is an extraordinary natural habitat accessed from the coast of Saudi Arabia, via Red Sea International Airport. Guests can pre-arrange travel to the resort, choosing between a 25-minute seaplane transfer or a short car journey to the airport’s marina, where a 30-minute voyage by speed boat or private charter will transport you to our unique island retreat.

Family Experiences

## A Treasure Trove of Fun for Every Age

Choreographing captivating activities that resonate across generations, Family Traditions at The St. Regis Red Sea Resort is where the art of play is celebrated, and lifelong memories are made. Curated to ensure little ones feel extra special, our range of signature services includes bespoke amenities, daily activities and specialized menus. Providing every child with an inviting space to explore Ummahat Island, the welcoming Kids’ Club is where childcare professionals orchestrate imaginative moments and independent discoveries.

## An Icon of Sophistication on the Shores of the Red Sea

At The St. Regis Red Sea Resort, design becomes a dialogue between heritage and modernity. Inspired by the timeless beauty of Saudi Arabia’s natural landscapes, the resort’s architecture seamlessly blends contemporary elegance with regional artistry. From grand arrival to serene private spaces, interiors are adorned with rich textures, intricate patterns, and a refined color palette that evokes the desert, sea, and sky. Every detail, from bespoke furnishings to curated artworks, reflects a commitment to craftsmanship and cultural authenticity. The result is an immersive sanctuary where luxury is both seen and felt, and every space tells a story of place, prestige, and purpose.

The St. Regis Spa Red Sea

### Precious Indulgence

An unrivalled sanctuary dedicated to holistic wellness and ultimate relaxation, The St. Regis Spa Red Sea embraces the unique qualities of the destination in a world-first concept. Celebrating the essence of the Red Sea, guests can select from an exclusive menu of treatments, designed to utilize prized materials within a sustainable context, combining bespoke therapies with cutting-edge technology. Emphasizing the value of physical health, activities like olfactory yoga provide the foundation for building meaningful strength, whilst benefitting from the beautiful Ummahat Island setting.

Learn More

Leisure Facilities

### Sustainable Watersports

Red Sea aficionados, WAMA, have created a collection of invigorating activities that immerse guests in the brilliance of the locality. Gentle breezes make the island a fantastic location for a variety of sailing pursuits, including windsurfing, e-foiling, catamarans and more. If seeking more calming endeavors, kayaking and paddleboarding are as mindful as they are thrilling.

### Premier Diving

Located on the neighboring island of Nujuma, Galaxea diving center welcomes those looking to explore the Red Sea’s underwater world. Home to a thriving coral reef, this authentic marine landscape is where divers of all levels can enjoy everything from conservation and heritage dives, through to snorkeling excursions and PADI courses.

### Get Adventurous

Combining thrills with the protection of this rare ecosystem, adventure experts, Akun have developed a host of outdoor explorative opportunities for all the family. From conservation tours led by local experts, to hikes that navigate the rock formations of the Southern Dunes, there is a plethora of engaging activities to enjoy.

### Beachside Pool

Advantageously positioned adjacent to the beach, the oversized free-form pool is a haven for relaxation. Crowned by numerous palm trees and lush vegetation, it is sympathetically designed to complement the location, whilst providing prime leisure time for all generations.

### Tennis and Paddle Courts

Get energetic and enjoy a game of tennis or padel at our two specially designed open-air courts. Located adjacent to the gym, both are fully equipped to ensure competitive fun.

Fitness Center and Lap Pool

## Commitment to Sustainable Luxury

At The St. Regis Red Sea Resort, sustainability is integral to our identity. Built to LEED Platinum standards and powered entirely by solar energy, our resort honors the pristine environment of Saudi Arabia’s Red Sea archipelago. We work closely with conservation partners to support initiatives such as coral propagation, turtle nesting protection, and mangrove restoration. Every guest stay is designed to respect and preserve this extraordinary marine ecosystem, blending responsibility with refined indulgence.
