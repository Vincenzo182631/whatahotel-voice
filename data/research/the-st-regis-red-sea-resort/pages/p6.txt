# Harmony in Nature

Where sustainable luxury meets serene design

Opens a new windowOpens a new window

## Visionary Island Architecture

Located on the exclusive Ummahat archipelago, The St. Regis Red Sea Resort is a Kengo Kuma-designed sanctuary of minimalist elegance. Dune and Coral villas offer secluded indoor-outdoor living in harmony with nature. Rooted in sustainability, the resort features tranquil spa pavilions and refined open-air dining, all crafted to honor the Red Sea’s pristine marine environment.

## A Legacy of Elegance, Rooted in Nature

Thoughtfully designed to reflect and respect their natural surroundings, each space across the resort blends sustainable materials with a refined, organic aesthetic. From villas that flow seamlessly into the landscape to dining venues inspired by sea and sand, architecture and nature exist in perfect harmony, creating an atmosphere of understated elegance and deep connection.

## The St. Regis Spa Red Sea

Inspired by the island’s thriving marine ecosystem, the spa features immersive interiors with mother-of-pearl, shell accents, wave-like seating, and jellyfish-inspired chandeliers. This connection to the sea extends to a menu of indulgent treatments, where every element is designed to reflect and celebrate the serene, natural beauty of the Red Sea setting.

## Island Elegance

With seamless indoor-outdoor flow, treatment rooms feature calming marine tones, organic textures, and oversized windows framing beach views. Select suites offer private terraces, inviting guests to relax in the island’s natural splendor and gentle Red Sea breeze, creating a serene connection between wellness and the surrounding landscape.

## Holistic Connection

Nestled in the Ummahat archipelago’s rich ecosystem, the open-air Yoga pavilion offers a serene escape to reconnect and center. This minimalist, elevated structure harmonizes with the horizon and surroundings, creating a peaceful space where every breath and movement is deeply inspired by nature’s balance and beauty.

## Honoring the Landscape

Creating an engaging dialogue between interior and exterior, the resort’s style reflects private island living, where soft sand beaches and marine biology influence the ingenious design.

## Visionary Spirit

Thoughtfully styled to exist in balance with the island environment, The St. Regis Red Sea Resort is a refined blend of nature and architecture, envisaged by legendary architect Kengo Kuma. A remarkable feat of engineering and creative vision, the resort uses a light touch approach with renewable materials and practices to honor the destination and its outstanding ecological credentials.
