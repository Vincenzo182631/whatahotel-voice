Signature Rituals

## Coral Mary

An innate part of the St. Regis’ timeless rituals, our locally inspired signature Bloody Mary encapsulates the flavors and scents of this glamorous hideaway. The Coral Mary, served in handmade glassware, is a tangy libation created as an homage to the thriving reef that surrounds the island, the majestic desert sands, as well as the beauty of both sea and sky. Its vibrant coral hue combines with desert sand inspired, dried black lime, ubiquitous to every Saudi home, to add flavor that’s both acidic and savory. Salinity associated with the sea comes from specially picked algae giving the beverage a complexity in line with its heritage. Spices and herbs add body and heat to the mix, while a celery VOM cloud crowns the drink, replacing the traditional salt rim to give the Coral Mary a unique twist to an age-old favorite.
