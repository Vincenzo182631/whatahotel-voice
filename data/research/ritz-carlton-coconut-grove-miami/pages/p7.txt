Holidays at The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami
Seasons in the Grove
Embrace the festive spirit in Coconut Grove with an immersive holiday experience that beckons both guests and locals alike. As December unfolds, forge new and cherished traditions with loved ones against the backdrop of this vibrant community. The air is filled with joy as Coconut Grove transforms into a holiday wonderland, adorned with glittering lights, coastal-themed ornaments, and the centerpiece – a resplendent Christmas tree gracing the community's most scenic spots, creating an atmosphere of wonder and enchantment. Spending the holidays in Coconut Grove promises a magical and unforgettable experience for all.
