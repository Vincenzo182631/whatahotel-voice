Experiences
Between Biscayne Bay and downtown Miami, Coconut Grove has been a hub for artists and travelers since it was first settled in 1825. Today, the upscale neighborhood continues to attract the creative and curious with its warm climate, beautiful coastline, and mix of cultural experiences. Exploring the neighborhood surrounding our luxury hotel near the University of Miami, you will come across sidewalk cafes, high-end boutiques, sailing, or art festivals. And while the hotel offers its own four-acre escape, there are lush green spaces to be found at the Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden and the Italian villa-inspired Vizcaya Museum and Gardens. Nearby you'll find the Government Center; Brickell Avenue, home to large corporate offices; and the nightlife, arts scene, and dining of South Beach. To help plan your visit and secure coveted festival tickets, reservations, and other experiences, please contact the hotel concierge.

Crandon Golf
Play Crandon Golf at Key Biscayne and enjoy a day on a breathtaking barrier island surrounded by tropical beauty. Designed by Von Hagge, Crandon Golf is regarded as one of Florida’s most stunning—and most challenging—par‑72 courses. From the opening dogleg par 5, which demands a drive over water and mangroves, to the final par 5 overlooking the sparkling expanse of Biscayne Bay, Crandon Golf at Key Biscayne stands as Miami’s signature golfing destination.

Secluded Yet Inviting
Surrounded by water, mangrove forests and lush tropical foliage, Crandon Golf at Key Biscayne is only minutes away from downtown Miami, Miami Beach and Miami International Airport. Nestled away in an island paradise that lies between the Atlantic Ocean and Biscayne Bay, Crandon Golf is an explosion of color and light.
Stunning Vistas
Beauty, however, isn’t the only draw here. Owned and operated by Miami-Dade Park and Recreation, this public, 18-hole, championship-designed course, features friendly and professional staff, golf professionals on site for instruction, a full service restaurant and bar, a lighted driving range, practice chipping and putting greens, Titleist club rentals, GPS equipped electric golf carts, and a renovated Pro Shop.
Challenging Beauty
Designed by Von Hagge, Crandon Golf at Key Biscayne is considered one of the most beautiful and difficult par 72 courses in Florida. From the first hole, a dog leg par 5 that requires a drive over water and mangroves, to the last, another par 5 with views of beautiful Biscayne Bay, Crandon Golf at Key Biscayne is Miami’s signature golf course.

Vizcaya Museum and Gardens
Our hotel is located near Vizcaya Museum Gardens, Miami, a National Historic Landmark — a beautiful retreat in the heart of Miami, built by agricultural industrialist James Deering, who wintered on the property from 1916–1925. Today, the extraordinary European-inspired estate includes a Main House filled with art and furnishings, ten acres of gardens on Biscayne Bay, a hardwood hammock, and a historic village.
Learn More
Dinner Key Marina
Named for its beauty as a picnic spot while traveling by boat along Miami’s coastal waterways, Dinner Key Marina remains a popular spot for boat slips and fishing charters and is walking distance from our hotel near Biscayne Bay, Miami.
Learn More
The Barnacle State Historic Site
Home to Ralph Middleton Munroe, one of Coconut Grove’s most influential pioneers, the Barnacle is the oldest home in Miami-Dade County still in its original location. It is now operated as a state park with tours at scheduled times.
Regatta Park
The larger area surrounding Dinner Key Marina, Regatta Park is a popular viewing point for sailing regattas and watching the sun move over Biscayne Bay. The park also hosts a variety of events every year including the Sailing World Cup and the Coconut Grove Arts Festival.
Crandon Park and Beach
A minutes' drive from our hotel near the University of Miami, this park offers a variety of Miami sun-filled activities. Crandon Park features two beach areas on the ocean and Biscayne Bay side of Key Biscayne, tennis courts, golf facilities, nature center and marina.
