Dining
Dining at The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami celebrates the city’s rich history and coastal influences. The hotel’s garden restaurant showcases land‑to‑sea cuisine with premium steaks, fresh seafood, and a lavish brunch. Tropical drinks and light bites are offered poolside, while a classic cocktail bar captures Miami’s timeless elegance and storied past.

Isabelle's Coconut Grove
Isabelle’s Coconut Grove is named in honor of Isabella Peacock, one of the neighborhood’s earliest settlers. The restaurant features a warm, inviting palette of rich stained woods, burnished brass, cognac leather, and touches of white accented by deep greens—creating a setting that feels both timeless and modern. Adjacent to the dining room, a trellised outdoor garden terrace offers an oasis-like experience with oversized planters, lush landscaping, natural wood tables, and white‑and‑taupe woven chairs with plush cushions. This open‑air sanctuary has become one of Coconut Grove’s most sought-after destinations for dining, weekend brunch, private events, and social gatherings.
