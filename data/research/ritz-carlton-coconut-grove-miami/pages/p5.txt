Frequently Asked Questions
At our luxury spa near Coral Gables, your comfort is our highest priority. As you prepare for your treatment, we want you to feel completely relaxed and at ease, which is why we’re here to answer any questions you may have. For your convenience, we’ve gathered responses to the most common inquiries about our Miami spa to help you enjoy a seamless and restorative experience.
Prior to Arrival
Who can guide me in choosing my treatments?
Our spa therapists will help you plan the perfect spa experience, including the best order for your treatments.
What if I have special health considerations?
Please notify our Spa Reservation Department before booking your treatments if you have high blood pressure, allergies, any other physical ailments or disabilities, or if you are pregnant. If you have any concern at all, please let us know. If at any time during your services you experience discomfort, please alert your therapist immediately.
What should I wear during my treatment?
Most body treatments are enjoyed without clothing; however, please wear whatever is comfortable for you. During all treatments, the body is fully draped, except for the area being worked on.
Should I shave before a treatment?
Shaving is recommended but not necessary for men prior to a facial. If you do choose to shave prior to your facial, be sure to do so at least two (2) hours prior to your scheduled appointment. Shaving is not recommended prior to any body treatments or hair removal services.
Upon Arrival
What about my comfort?
This is your time and you should delight in the experience to the fullest. Whether it is the room temperature, the amount of massage pressure or the volume of the music, please notify us of any discomforts or preferences. We request that cell phones and pagers be turned off when having a spa treatment.
What about my jewelry and valuables?
If you are staying in the hotel, please leave valuable items in the safe in your guest room.
Health Questions
What if I am pregnant?
While there are a few services that should be avoided during pregnancy, there are many beneficial treatments that can be enjoyed. Please notify our Spa Desk Agents if you are pregnant when you make your appointments so that they can offer guidance.
Spa Policies
What about gratuities?
Gratuity is inclusive on all spa packages. A 20% service charge will be added for each spa service you receive.
What if I am late for my appointment?
Arriving late will simply limit the time for your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest will not be delayed. The full value of your treatment will be applied.
What about payment for Spa services?
We accept all major credit cards. You may also charge spa services to your hotel bill. For your convenience, payment is also accepted prior to your treatments, upon request.

An appointment is required at Boutique Spa at The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami.
