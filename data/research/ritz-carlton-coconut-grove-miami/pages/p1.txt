Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami
Indulge at our elegant Coconut Grove, Miami, hotel
The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami, brings to life the independent and artistic spirit of Miami’s original neighborhood through genuine hospitality with a bohemian flair. This luxury hotel in Coconut Grove, Florida, is an urban retreat that provides access to a lively community brimming with activity and culture and the privacy and comfort of a boutique hideaway.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami. The daily valet fee is $60.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami is Miami International Airport (MIA). MIA is located approximately 7.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Coconut Grove, Miami has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
