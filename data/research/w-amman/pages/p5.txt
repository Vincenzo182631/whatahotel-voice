### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Amman have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Amman offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Weight Machines, and Yoga Mats, Headsets, Jumping Ropes, Punching Bag, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Amman have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Amman has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does W Amman have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY® Spa is located on-site at W Amman. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Amman?

Roman Theatre, Citadel, Rainbow Street, The Boulevard, Carakale Brewery and more are nearby W Amman.
