Getting around

Transportation Options

Conveniently located in the heart of downtown Vancouver, discover a variety of ways to explore. Many of Vancouver’s most iconic attractions are just a short walk away, while the SkyTrain, bus network and SeaBus provide quick connections to neighbourhoods across the region. Rideshare services such as Uber and Lyft, along with local taxis, are readily available, and nearby bicycle and scooter rentals make it easy to enjoy the city’s scenic routes. For travel to and from YVR Airport, the hotel is a short walk from Vancouver City Centre stop on the Canada Line Skytrain Rapid Transit System, offering an efficient connection directly to the terminal. Taxis and rideshares are also easily accessible. Our concierge team is happy to assist with any transportation needs.
