# Luxury Downtown Vancouver Hotel \| Hyatt Vancouver Downtown Alberni

## A Luxury Downtown Vancouver Hotel on Alberni Street

Welcome to Hyatt Vancouver Downtown Alberni, a luxury hotel in downtown Vancouver’s most prestigious shopping and cultural district. Steps from boutiques, acclaimed restaurants, world-class galleries and Vancouver’s iconic Seawall, the hotel offers spacious rooms and suites with floor-to-ceiling windows framing city and mountain views. Unwind at the luxurious, full-service Orrin Spa, then savour Michelin-recommended Italian dining at Carlino. With an outdoor pool, refined event spaces and the distinction of a AAA Five Diamond rating, Hyatt Vancouver Downtown Alberni offers an elevated stay in the heart of the city.

## Rooms & Suites

Discover elevated comfort in stylish, spacious rooms and suites designed for a refined city stay. Each accommodation features floor-to-ceiling windows, soothing earth-toned interiors and marble bathrooms appointed with deep soaking tubs and separate glass-enclosed showers, creating a serene retreat above the energy of downtown.

Parking

Hyatt Vancouver Downtown Alberni offers valet parking, with access via 1129 Alberni Street, Vancouver, BC V6E 1A5, Canada. In/out privileges are included with overnight valet service. Rates are subject to change. Please confirm directly with the hotel prior to arrival.

We can accommodate oversize vehicles:

Ramp clearance: 12 feet 10 inches / 3.91 metres

Port Cochere Estate & Public Parking clearance: 7 feet 6 inches / 2.28 metres

P1 clearance: 6 feet 8 inches / 2 metres

6 a.m. – 5:59 p.m.: CA$18 for the first hour, CA$9 for every additional hour

6 p.m. – 2 a.m.: CA$23

Overnight parking: CA$56
