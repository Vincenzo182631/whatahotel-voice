# Dining

Taste creative, approachable chef-curated menus for every meal (and in-room dining), from hearty, healthy breakfasts to locally inspired Northern Italian classics, sourced from regional ingredients. This elegant downtown Vancouver restaurant is the perfect spot for happy hour or an evening of fine dining. Visit the juice bar for creative, fresh combinations of cold-pressed fruit and vegetables for a daily pick-me-up.

## Carlino

Discover our chef’s signature blend of Northern Italian culinary traditions, drawing on the regions of Veneto and Trentino, cooked with British Columbia’s seasonal bounty of local ingredients from the forest, mountains and coastal waters nearby.
