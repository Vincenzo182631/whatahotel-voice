### Seven ecosystems. One unforgettable legacy.

_&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve is more than a Big Five safari. It’s a conservation success story decades in the making. Once a patchwork of farmland, this land was carefully restored into 29,866 hectares (73,800 acres) of thriving wilderness. Today, cheetah sprint across open grassland and black rhino browse dense Tamboti thicket. There is a quiet power in seeing a place return to its former glory.

### Above the wild. Deep within it.

Set into the cliff face above the valley, Phinda Rock Lodge offers Phinda at its most intimate. Seemingly suspended over the landscape, the lodge feels deeply personal, with a rare vantage point on a wilderness shaped by time and care. Luxury speaks softly here. It lives in the pause after a game drive, the hush below your deck and a rooftop dinner beneath stars that need no stage.

Luxury in the wild

### Suites shaped by stone and stillness.

Each of the lodge’s six romantic Suites is constructed from contoured stone, with solid wooden shutters and doors that open onto a private deck and plunge pool. Spacious ensuite bathrooms bring generous comfort, while four Suites feature outdoor showers for an open-air moment between adventures.

- EXPERIENCES

### Phinda, from every angle.

From Phinda Rock Lodge, each day can be as adventurous or as unhurried as you choose. Track rhino on foot, follow Big Five paths through seven distinct habitats, join a conservation experience or travel towards the coast for ocean safaris and seasonal turtle nesting. Then return to the cliffside calm of your Suite, where the day has space to settle.
