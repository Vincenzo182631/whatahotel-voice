### Seven ecosystems. One unforgettable legacy.

_&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve is more than a Big Five safari. It’s a conservation success story decades in the making. Once a patchwork of farmland, this land was carefully restored into 29,866 hectares (73,800 acres) of thriving wilderness. Today, cheetah sprint across open grassland and black rhino browse dense Tamboti thicket. There is a quiet power in seeing a place return to its former glory.

### Where life comes naturally.

Held between rare sand forest and a wildlife-rich wetland, Phinda Vlei offers something few places still can: intimacy without intrusion. Generous in space and softened by natural textures, its quiet elegance invites you to pause. From timber screens to the shaded pool sala, every detail draws you gently back to the present.

Luxury in the wild

- ACCOMMODATION

### Privacy framed by the wild.

Crafted for quiet moments and deep rest, each of Phinda Vlei’s six suites balances refined comfort with uninterrupted views. Inside, woven textures and timber warmth create a grounding space. Outside, a private pool and open-air shower draw you closer to the rhythm of the reserve. From your deck, you might watch antelope grazing or a lone elephant slipping silently past. Here, the vlei gently draws wildlife throughout the day, making the lodge itself part of the safari.
