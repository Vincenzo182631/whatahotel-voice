#### Big Five Safari

### Your journey at a glance

#### Embark on a grand tour of South Africa, from the nation’s capital to its most famous game reserves and the magnificent city of Cape Town.

Set out in search of the Big Five in the greater Kruger National Park and benefit from the famous reserve’s wealth of wildlife. Track rarer wildlife like the elegant cheetah and endangered black rhino at &Beyond Phinda Private Game Reserve, set within easy reach of the Indian Ocean coastline and the famous Greater St Lucia Wetland Park, the reserve is known for its abundant wildlife, diversity of habitats and wide range of safari activities. Your holiday in South Africa ends at the southern tip of the continent, in picturesque Cape Town, where two oceans meet. Discover the views from magnificent Table Mountain, uncover the city’s colourful history and taste some of the world’s finest vintages in the scenic Winelands.

1 night Johannesburg \| 2 nights Kruger National Park \| 2 nights Phinda Private Game Reserve \| 3 nights Cape Town

### A taste of what to expect… Day 1:

#### Johannesburg

- Upon arrival at OR Tambo International Airport, you will be welcomed by a member from our &Beyond team and transferred to your Johannesburg Hotel.
- Stay at a Johannesburg Hotel, a comfortable hotel located within the vicinity of the bustling airport.
- Dinner can be enjoyed at an array of restaurants dotted around the hotel, whilst a cheery bar provides a relaxing setting for you to unwind with a few drinks.

### Day 2-4:

#### Greater Kruger National Park

- After a delicious breakfast you will be transfered from your hotel to OR Tambo International Airport for your flight to Ngala Private Game Reserve set inside the Kruger National Park.
- Embark on captivating game drives and bush walks, exploring the natural environment and seeking out the Big Five.
- Hone your skills on a photographic safari with a specialised guide, discover the art of tracking and sample wild fruit.
- At an additional cost, choose to experience the romance of Africa under a blanket of stars in the luxurious Ngala Treehouse

### Day 4-6:

#### Phinda Private Game Reserve

- You will be transferred to the airstrip for your domestic flight to Kruger Mpumalanga International Airport, where you will connect with your flight to &Beyond Phinda Private Game Reserve.
- Immerse yourself in thrilling game drives and bush walks with expert rangers who will guide you through &Beyond Phinda’s seven unique habitats.
- Choose to go on private Specialist Safaris such as a birding, photographic, or walking safaris (available at an additional cost)
- Also at an additional cost, choose from a variety of experiences including a Zulu village tour, a Maputaland beach adventure, night turtle drives (seasonal), scuba safari, an ocean safari, or a pangolin conservation experience.

### Day 6-9:

#### Cape Town

- Connect with your domestic flight from &Beyond Private Game Reserve to Cape Town International Airport via Kruger Mpumalanga International Airport.
- Upon arrival at you will be warmly welcomed by a member from our &Beyond team who will drive you to your hotel.
- As you drive toward the city, your view from afar will be Table Mountain, one of the most spiritual places on earth, with its heart, two oceans and the beautiful city of Cape Town at its feet.
- Explore famous landmarks including Table Mountain and Cape Point.
- Discover the lush flora and fauna of Kirstenbosch National Botanical Gardens.
- Shop at the world renowned V&A Waterfront, the ideal spot for retail therapy, and relax on the glorious beaches along Cape Town’s sun-kissed coastline.
- Indulge in a full day in the spectacular Cape Winelands. The estates visited will vary according to your preferences, but the tour includes the region’s major wine producing areas and visits some beautiful wine estates for tastings of high quality vintages.
- The next day, wave a fond farewell as your getaway comes to an end.
- You will be collected and driven to Cape Town International Airport for your flight onward.

### Discover South Africa

### Why visit Johannesburg?

#### A diverse cultural melting pot, this City of Gold (or iGoli as it is sometimes locally known) offers everything from traditional markets to exclusive boutiques in upmarket malls, theatres, museums, world-class restaurants and more.

Read More

### Why visit the Kruger National Park?

**The world-renowned Kruger National Park is South Africa's largest wildlife sanctuary with nearly 2 million ha (4.9 million acres) of unrivalled wilderness and wildlife land, and home to, not only the Big Five, but more species of large mammals than any other African game reserve.**

Read More

### Why visit Phinda Private Game Reserve?

**Showcasing one of the continent’s finest game viewing experiences, &Beyond Phinda is home to Africa’s Big Five (lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo and rhino), as well as 436 bird species. The reserve is particularly well known for close-up sightings of the elegant yet elusive cheetah, as well as the rare black rhino.**

Read More
