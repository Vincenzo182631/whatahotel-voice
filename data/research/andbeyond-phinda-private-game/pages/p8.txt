### Seven ecosystems. One unforgettable legacy.

_&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve is more than a Big Five safari. It’s a conservation success story decades in the making. Once a patchwork of farmland, this land was carefully restored into 29,866 hectares (73,800 acres) of thriving wilderness. Today, cheetah sprint across open grassland and black rhino browse dense Tamboti thicket. There is a quiet power in seeing a place return to its former glory.

### Beneath the canopy, the world softens.

Set within one of Africa’s last remaining dry sand forests, Phinda Forest Lodge offers a safari unlike any other. This is not a landscape you pass through. It’s one you become part of. Around you, the forest lives and breathes. Birdsong carries through the treetop. Small antelope move unseen beneath it. Light filters softly between branches. Every moment feels held, as though the outside world has gently fallen away.

—

Luxury in the wild

- ACCOMMODATION

### On the forest floor.

Zulu-zen inspired Suites rise lightly on stilts, woven into the trees rather than placed upon the land. Each one dissolves the boundary between inside and out. Walls of glass open to layered green, while elevated walkways wind quietly through the forest. Space is generous, but never excessive. Every detail is considered, so nothing distracts from where you are.

### More than one world, waiting beyond the trees.

While the forest draws you inward, Phinda opens outward into something far wider. Beyond the canopy lies a reserve of remarkable diversity, where no two days feel the same. Track rhino on foot. Follow predators across open grassland. Step into conservation efforts that shape the future of this landscape. Or trade the bush for ocean air, where seasonal turtle nesting and marine safaris reveal another side of the wild.
