### Seven ecosystems. One unforgettable legacy.

_&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve is more than a Big Five safari. It’s a conservation success story decades in the making. Once a patchwork of farmland, this land was carefully restored into 29,866 hectares (73,800 acres) of thriving wilderness. Today, cheetah sprint across open grassland and black rhino browse dense Tamboti thicket. There is a quiet power in seeing a place return to its former glory.

### A safari home shaped around you.

Cradled on the edge of dappled forest in the north of Phinda, Phinda Homestead is not simply a private safari villa. It’s a deeply personal home in the wild, shaped by the land around it and the people who care for it. Every day moves at your own pace, held by a dedicated ranger, tracker, butler, chef and host.

—

Luxury in the wild

- ACCOMMODATION

### Space for everyone to settle in.

With four ensuite Suites and an additional Children’s Suite, Phinda Homestead accommodates up to 10 guests. The design is contemporary but homely. Zulu craft influences, Nguni textures and aloe-inspired orange accents root the interiors in Phinda’s landscape and culture.

- EXPERIENCES

### Safari, on your own terms.

At Phinda Homestead, the day does not have to follow a lodge schedule. Set out on a private game drive with the whole family, explore by e-bike or travel towards the coast for ocean safaris, scuba adventures and seasonal turtle nesting. Whether you are travelling across generations or gathering for a milestone, Phinda offers space for everyone to experience the wild in their own way.
