### Your journey at a glance

#### Step into an adventure that reveals the true heartbeat of Phinda.

Over seven days, explore thriving wildlife habitats, meet the conservationists restoring endangered species and engage with our partner communities who share this landscape. Guided by a specialist ranger and your dedicated Wild Impact guide, you will learn new skills, deepen your understanding of conservation science and experience Phinda’s wildlife with a renewed sense of meaning.

You will stay at the breathtaking _&Beyond_ Phinda Zuka Lodge, a sole-use lodge in the southern reaches of the reserve, which offers its own private vehicle and guide, butler, and chef. These dates have been carefully selected around the best climates of the year, to ensure that you are able to make the most of your adventure.

_A seat on this Small Group Journey includes a Wild Impact donation._

**6 nights _&BEYOND_ **Phinda Private Game Reserve****

### Day 1

#### Arrival \| _&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve

**_&Beyond_ Phinda Zuka Lodge**

Arrive at OR Tambo International Airport, where an _&Beyond_ representative will meet you and assist with your flight to Phinda. Your guide will welcome you at the airstrip and transfer you to Phinda Zuka Lodge, your exclusive base for the week. Settle in and enjoy an afternoon game drive across the Munywana Conservancy, an area known for its thriving rhino populations, elusive cats and beautifully varied landscapes.

### Days 2 to 6

#### _&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve

**_&Beyond_ Phinda Zuka Lodge**

Discover Phinda through a mix of immersive experiences. Cycle through the sand forest on e-bikes, join your ranger for tracking sessions, take part in bird-ringing and spend meaningful time with community projects supported by Wild Impact. Swipe through for a glimpse of how these days will unfold.

### Day 2

#### ALL IN THE DETAILS

Begin with a morning game drive before switching to e-bikes for a scenic ride through sand forest. Learn track-and-sign techniques with your guide as you interpret fresh spoor and read the subtle clues that reveal wildlife activity.

### Day 3

#### LIFE UNDER THE SKIES

Take part in a hands-on bird-ringing session to net, measure, tag and release birds. As evening falls, settle into a lantern-lit sleepout with fireside dining and the sounds of the bush surrounding you.

### Day 4

#### BUDDING BUSH PRO

Walk from your sleepout back to the lodge, using your growing field skills to read tracks, listen for bird calls and interpret wildlife movements. Later, enjoy an unhurried afternoon game drive followed by a bush gin tasting.

### Day 5

#### ENRICH YOUR EXPERIENCE

Visit nearby community projects supported by Wild Impact. Spend time with local educators, explore a school environment and learn how community-led initiatives strengthen the region.

### Day 6

#### ACTING FOR THE FUTURE

Join the conservation team for an introduction to telemetry and wildlife monitoring. Take part in key wildlife conservation initiatives, and see how research guides the long-term management of the reserve.

### Day 7

#### Departure \| _&BEYOND_ Phinda Private Game Reserve

**_&Beyond_ Phinda Zuka Lodge**

Savour a final breakfast before travelling to the airstrip for your return flight. One last aerial view of the landscape offers a fitting farewell to a journey shaped by learning, purpose and discovery.

### What makes this journey unforgettable

#### A rare look behind the scenes of an award-winning reserve.

### Conservation moments that stay with you

Join the conservation team as they monitor endangered wildlife and witness signature procedures such as rhino dehorning or ear-notching. Gain insight into how real-time data shapes protection strategies.

### A living mosaic of habitats

Experience Phinda’s Seven Worlds of Wonder, from sand forest to river corridors and open grasslands. Each game drive reveals a different facet of a reserve known for cheetah, black and white rhino and the Big Five.

### Skills you will carry home

Practise track-and-sign identification, take part in bird ringing and sharpen your awareness on an interactive walking safari. Each activity is guided by experts who share their knowledge and care in the field.

### Community stories with real impact

Spend time with educators and community leaders who drive local change. Visit nearby schools and projects supported by Wild Impact, and discover how tourism strengthens resilience beyond the reserve.

### Recognised for what matters

Every story speaks to something deeper: connection, care, and the kind of impact that lingers long after the journey ends.
