### Where the wild gets personal.

#### A rare tapestry of habitats. A living legacy of impact.

_&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve is no ordinary Big Five safari destination. This is where cheetahs sprint through grasslands once farmed, and black rhinos browse dense Tamboti thickets. It’s a place where the land tells a story of restoration, and every encounter feels sacred.

Spanning 29,866 hectares (73,800 acres) of rare biodiversity across seven distinct habitats, Phinda is one of Africa’s most diverse private reserves. Set within the pioneering Munywana Conservancy in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, and a short drive from the unspoiled beaches and coral reefs of the Indian Ocean, it offers more than extraordinary wildlife. But meaning. Because here, every stay helps protect what matters most.

### FAQ: what to know about _&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve

### Is _&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve in a malaria area?

**Phinda is located in a low-risk malaria area in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. While precautions are generally not required, travellers should consult their healthcare provider before travel.**

### How big is _&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve?

**Phinda spans approximately 29 866 hectares (73 800 acres) of protected wilderness, encompassing seven distinct ecosystems, from rare sand forest and savanna to wetland and woodland.**

### Why is _&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve known as the ‘Seven Worlds of Wonder’?

**Phinda is called the ‘Seven Worlds of Wonder’ because it contains seven unique habitats within one reserve. This incredible ecological diversity supports a wide range of wildlife and landscapes within a compact area, offering extraordinary variety in game viewing.**

### What makes _&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve unique compared to other reserves?

**Phinda stands out for its biodiversity, exclusive game viewing, and close proximity to the Indian Ocean. It combines luxury lodges, expert guiding, and pioneering conservation work, all in a region where Big Five safari meets coastal adventure.**

### What kind of wildlife can I expect to see at _&Beyond_ Phinda Private Game Reserve?

**Phinda is home to the Big Five, along with cheetah, hyena, giraffe, nyala, zebra and more than 400 bird species. It's also one of the best places in South Africa to see rare species like black rhino and pangolin.**
