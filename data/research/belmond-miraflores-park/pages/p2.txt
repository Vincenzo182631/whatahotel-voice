## Lima's oceanfront tower of creativity

A contemporary hotel overlooking the Pacific Ocean, Miraflores Park is the gateway to cosmopolitan Lima situated within a six-mile stretch of parks and boardwalks. With renowned Peruvian cuisine and panoramic views, it has an artistic spirit as vibrant as the city itself.

- 87 rooms and suites
- 2 restaurants and 1 bar
- Rooftop pool
- Spa and fitness center
- 20km from Lima International Airport

## Accommodation

Our rooms and suites offer breathtaking panoramic views over the Pacific Ocean. A place of solace in the bustling city, unwind from the comfort of your own private hideaway, with some suites offering a terrace or plunge pool.

Miraflores Park first opened in 1996, rapidly becoming the finest Lima luxury hotel and embodiment of the city’s cosmopolitan edge and a place of sophistication within the chic residential area of Miraflores.

Over the years we have become a global destination for exhibitions, glamorous events and diplomatic meetings. World leaders and international luminaries from Paul McCartney to Claudia Schiffer rank among former illustrious guests drawn to the hotel’s modern elegance.
