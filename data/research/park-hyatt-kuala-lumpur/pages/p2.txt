Park Hyatt Kuala Lumpur houses 252 spacious guestrooms, including 27 suites, offering uninterrupted views of the city from levels 100 to 112. The elegant interiors seamlessly blend contemporary style and sophistication with design accents and materials, such as batik, songket and wood-carved motifs, that are an inherent part of Malaysia’s cultural identity. An array of luxury lifestyle amenities provides thoughtful touches to elevate your stay.
