## Merdeka Grill

Merdeka Grill offers a modern grill experience on Level 75, where premium meats, sustainable seafood, and locally sourced produce take center stage, complemented by a curated beverage program. Lunch highlights include a three-course set menu and à la carte grill selections. Dinner unfolds course by course, with signature dishes prepared or finished tableside — from wood-roasted salmon to chateaubriand and vanilla ice cream topped with Oscietra caviar.

## Champagne Brunch

Elevate your weekends at Park Hyatt Kuala Lumpur's Merdeka Grill. Immerse yourself in a world of refined indulgence, where the sparkle of free-flowing Champagne carries each moment to new heights. Savor the ocean’s finest, featuring a seafood bar, freshly prepared salmon tartare, an assortment of salads, charcuterie and cheeses, and a decadent spread of chocolate-forward desserts. Live music drifts through the air, completing an unforgettable symphony of indulgence.

The experience begins at MYR 368+ per person, including free-flow non-alcoholic beverages. For those seeking to further elevate their afternoon, our add-on alcoholic beverage package starts from MYR 198+ per person.

## Cacao Mixology & Chocolate

Savor the artful blend of mixology and chocolate at Cacao Mixology & Chocolate, Kuala Lumpur’s highest bar, where the richness of chocolate is expressed through artisanal cocktails and curated desserts. Exquisite creations include Chef Holger’s signature chocolate, alongside regional aged spirits, and thoughtfully crafted chocolate pairings. The smooth rhythms of Bossa Nova and panoramic skyline views set the mood for an experience that is both indulgent and quietly sophisticated.

Signature offerings include live, locally sourced fish prepared to order and an elegant afternoon tea experience that delights with handcrafted pastries served from a dessert trolley.
