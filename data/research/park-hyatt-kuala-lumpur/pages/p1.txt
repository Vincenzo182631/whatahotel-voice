# Luxury Kuala Lumpur Hotel \| Park Hyatt Kuala Lumpur

## Unparalleled Luxury Amidst Kuala Lumpur City Skyline

Set in the country’s most prominent historic site and occupying the top floors of Menara Merdeka Maybank​, the tallest skyscraper in Asia Pacific, Park Hyatt Kuala Lumpur provides unrivalled views from the city’s highest point. Revel in a refined and distinctive experience inspired by Malaysia’s rich cultural heritage and the artful tapestry of Kuala Lumpur’s past and present, imbued with a sense of tranquility.

## Discover Park Hyatt Kuala Lumpur

Fitness Center

Keep up your workout routine in our 24-hour, modern fitness center, featuring Technogym cardio and strength training equipment, yoga, a movement studio for group fitness classes and personal training.

A More Sustainable Stay

Park Hyatt Kuala Lumpur embraces the high-performance, energy-optimizing features that make Merdeka 118 an industry benchmark for sustainability. In addition to 100% solar powered hot water, we’re committed to adopting sustainable initiatives in our daily operations, food sourcing and energy, water and waste management. As part of this, certain room amenities are discontinued or available only on request. Learn what essentials you may need to bring for a more comfortable stay.

Sky Infinity Pool

Illuminated by natural light through floor-to-ceiling windows, our 30-meter infinity pool on Level 99 invites you to relax and recharge. Whether you’re enjoying a swim or lounging in comfort on the pool deck with a drink in hand, a stunning city view frames every refreshing moment.​

​Hours: 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily​

The Spa

Retreat to our spa sanctuary above the urban bustle, featuring six treatment rooms with en suite bathrooms for singles and couples, plus two aesthetic rooms. Indulge in rejuvenating massages, facials and body treatments—your senses soothed by an atmosphere of calm and serenity. Our holistic wellness hideaway also features separate male and female hydrothermal zones with Himalayan salt chambers, aromatherapy steam rooms, experience showers and vitality pools.​

​Hours: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily​

### Merdeka Grill

Merdeka Grill offers a modern grill experience on Level 75, where premium meats, sustainable seafood, and locally sourced produce take center stage, complemented by a curated beverage program. Lunch highlights include a three-course set menu and à la carte grill selections. Dinner unfolds course by course, with signature dishes prepared or finished tableside — from wood-roasted salmon to chateaubriand and vanilla ice cream topped with Oscietra caviar.
