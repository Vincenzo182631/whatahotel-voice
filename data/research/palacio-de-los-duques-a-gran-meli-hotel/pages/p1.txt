## Art and tradition in the heart of Madrid de los Austrias

This 19th-century palace, converted into a hotel, is housed in an Isabeline-style building in the very heart of Madrid. Palacio de los Duques is a canvas by a Sevillian artist, where his brushstrokes bring us closer to luxury, tradition, tranquility, and an authentic, traditional Madrid.

This hotel, inspired by the work of Velázquez, is located in a 19th-century Isabeline palace between Plaza de Oriente and Gran Vía, in Madrid de los Austrias. Palacio de los Duques Gran Meliá - a member of The Leading Hotels of the World - reflects the highest quality standards in an environment of art and tradition, with its contemporary design, historic gardens, privileged rooftop views, and the fine local cuisine at its restaurant.

### The Orchestra and Choir of the Teatro Real

Palacio de los Duques Gran Meliá, Madrid, Spain

The Palacio de los Duques Gran Meliá is just a couple of minutes walk from the Teatro Real. A pleasant walk before you enjoy a truly magical experience: witnessing, in complete privacy and behind closed doors, one of the rehearsals performed by the Orquesta Titular del Teatro Real or by the Coro Titular del Teatro Real, conducted by the Argentinian José Luis Basso.
