## Gastronomic proposal

,regionOfInterest=(1476.5,984.5))

### Traditional taste, enchanced experience

Discover Madrid's gastronomic heritage at Jardín de los Duques, a restaurant that celebrates the charm of traditional Madrid cuisine while enhancing and elevating it with a creative and refined touch. Experience an atmosphere that pays homage to the city’s traditional cuisine, where every dish celebrates the city's vast culinary legacy.

### Private dining room

Enjoy an exclusive dining experience in an intimate and elegant setting at Jardín de los Duques. Our private lounge, hosting up to 20 guests, offers the perfect setting for special events, business meetings or family celebrations.

,regionOfInterest=(2150,1433.5))

### Heritage dining: culinary excellence in a 19th-century palace

Set in the former stables of a 19th century Isabelline palace, our restaurant - inspired by the rich history of the Madrid of the Habsburgs - offers a combination of traditional recipes and innovative touches for a unique dining experience.
