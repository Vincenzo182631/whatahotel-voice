# Vermouth time

## Palacio de los Duques Gran Meliá, Madrid Spain

,regionOfInterest=(1100,733))

Connect with the destination during your stay at Palacio de los Duques Gran Meliá, Madrid, Spain

## Tasting, aperitif, and masterclass at Viva Madrid

Enjoy one of the best moments of the day to experience in good company. Viva Madrid proposes the classic vermouth on tap, as well as the best bottled options. A tavern just a few steps from the Plaza de Santa Ana, an iconic enclave in the center of Madrid which has taken on a new life thanks to Diego Cabrera, its owner and one of the best cocktail makers.

After a short transfer in a private vehicle from the Palacio de los Duques Gran Meliá hotel to Viva Madrid, you will enjoy an exclusive vermouth masterclass where you will learn about the history of this drink as well as the culture it has established in Spain. Afterwards, you will be able to taste three different vermouths so that guests can recognize different variations of their elaboration. A unique vermouth made exclusively for Viva Madrid will also be part of the tasting route. Two appetizers will complement the visit, as well as a bottle of Viva Madrid by Meliá Experiences vermouth as a gift.

- Duration: 120 min.
- No. of people: To be consulted.
- Please note: Book 48 hours in advance.
- What does it include?: Master class, tasting, pairing with snack and bottle of vermouth by Viva Madrid.

,regionOfInterest=(1771.5,1207.5))

Vermouth is an essential part of Spain's gastronomic tradition, especially in Madrid. The city and its casticismo have always been linked to the consumption of this fortified and spiced wine at aperitif time.

Formerly reserved for parents and grandparents, in recent years vermouth has undergone a revolution in Spain, a renaissance by the hand of new generations who have sought to restore the value of a drink that has always been able to gather us at the table or the bar.
