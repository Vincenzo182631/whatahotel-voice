# Capturing Madrid

## Palacio de los Duques Gran Meliá, Madrid Spain

,regionOfInterest=(1000,666.5))

Connect with the destination during your stay at Palacio de los Duques Gran Meliá, Madrid, Spain

## Images to remember

Discover the streets of Madrid accompanied by a professional photographer. A spectacular city, which is a perfect setting for taking the best snapshots. With the most emblematic corners of Madrid as a backdrop, you will have the opportunity to have pictures taken and even learn the techniques to capture them with your own cell phone.

* * *

The tour will begin in the charming lobby of the Palacio de los Duques Gran Meliá hotel. The photographer will take you through some of the most special and iconic areas of Madrid, all of them very close to the hotel. Starting with the Teatro Real and continuing through the Sabatini Gardens, with its fountains and white marble sculptures, the recently renovated Plaza de España, and the spectacular Temple of Debod. During these two hours, the photographer will reveal all their secrets to strike the best poses and photography tricks. All this with a cell phone as a means to capture every moment, which will allow you to replicate the techniques in future trips.

- Duration: 120
- No. of people: Up to 4 people
- Please note: Delivery of a selection of 50 photographs within 72 hours, for the guest to choose 15. Edition of 5 selected photographs. Within 72h after the selection of 15 photos and 5 photos to be edited, the final session will be sent by the photographer in high and low definition options.
- What does it include?: Photography masterclass, professional photoshoot, and a drink at the Café de Oriente

,regionOfInterest=(1000,666.5)),regionOfInterest=(1000,666.5))

To top it all off, the photoshoot will end with a drink at the Café de Oriente, a location with exceptional history in the center of Madrid, and the place where some of the memorable gatherings of the Generation of '27 took place.

It will be here where a first review of all the captured photographs will be made, as well as a first approach of your references.
