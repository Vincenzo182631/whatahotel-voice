## Behind-the-scenes experience

Take a leisurely stroll from the Gran Meliá Palacio de los Duques hotel to the Teatro Real for a magical experience. Behind closed doors and with complete privacy, you will be able to witness one of the rehearsals conducted by the Orquesta Titular del Teatro Real or by the Coro Titular del Teatro Real, directed by Argentinian José Luis Basso. A unique experience not to be missed.

One of the official guides of the Teatro Real will welcome you in the hall of the building to welcome you to such a unique space with a catering by the three Michelin star chef, Ramón Freixa. Afterwards, you will be able to visit some of the unique and private places that make up the theater. Once the tour is over, you will take a seat in the impressive stalls to enjoy the orchestra or choir rehearsal. As a gift and souvenir to take home, you will receive a 25cm poster printed on fineart giclée paper and wrapped in a rigid tube with the photograph or poster of your choice.

It was Fernando VII’s idea to build the Teatro Real de Madrid. He created it with the idea of replicating and surpassing the best theaters in Europe. The place chosen was in the Plaza de Oriente and its construction took 33 years to complete, a milestone that was achieved under the reign of Isabel II.

Nowadays, because of its popularity, the Teatro Real has become the leading national opera, committed to artistic excellence and technological innovation, as well as an outstanding program of great international projection.
