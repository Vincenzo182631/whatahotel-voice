# Reviews of Palacio de los Duques Gran Meliá

This 19th-century palace, converted into a hotel, is housed in an Isabeline-style building in the very heart of Madrid. Palacio de los Duques is a canvas by a Sevillian artist, where his brushstrokes bring us closer to luxury, tradition, tranquility, and an authentic, traditional Madrid.

“Wonderful Stay in Madrid with Excellent RedLevel Service!

We stayed here for a few nights in summer while exploring Madrid and had a fantastic experience. The hotel was beautiful, comfortable, and in a great location for exploring Madrid.

We opted for the RedLevel service, which made our stay even more enjoyable. The personalized attention and extra touches really added to the experience. Breakfast buffet was adequate/average but à la carte options were outstanding. The staff were welcoming, professional, and attentive throughout our stay.

We did encounter an issue with our room, but what really impressed us was how quickly and professionally the staff handled it. They addressed our concerns promptly and generously offered complimentary amenities as a gesture of goodwill. Their response really demonstrated their commitment to guest satisfaction.

Overall, a wonderful hotel with excellent hospitality. I would highly recommend the RedLevel experience and would happily stay here again!”
