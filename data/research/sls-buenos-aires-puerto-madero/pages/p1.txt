# MODERN & MARVELOUS IN BUENOS AIRES

Make your entrance alongside the illuminated figures of Eugenio Cuttica’s lobby installation. Lose yourself in the verdant landscaping of Enzo Enea. Be tempted by the allure of open flame cooking at Leynia. Indulge all your senses at Ciel Spa. Recharge with room service in one of 58 exceptional suites. Welcome to SLS Puerto Madero hotel in Buenos Aires, an immersive luxury experience on the city’s coast, bringing SLS’ signature ambiance to its first hotel in South America.

* * *

## LOUNGE IN LUXURY

Designed by world-renowned Italian designer and architect Piero Lissoni, each of the 58 contemporary suites serves as a haven from the mundane.
