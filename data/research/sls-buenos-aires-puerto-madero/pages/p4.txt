# WELLNESS

##### AT SLS PUERTO MADERO

Enjoy relaxation rooms, treatment suites with custom massage beds, two saunas, walk-in showers, and a south-facing pool with spacious sundeck, The fitness center features state-of-the-art weight-lifting and cardio equipment to meet all fitness needs.

### CIEL SPA
