Designed by world-renowned architect Richard Rogers, Fairmont Ambassador Seoul is a 5-star hotel that boasts unparalleled proximity to The Hyundai Seoul, the capital’s largest luxury department store, and effortless connection to the city’s metro network.

### Fairmont Seoul Official Character 'Sudari'

\- Front Desk (0:00AM - 0:00PM)

Price: KRW 42,000

Sudari is the newest member of the Fairmont Seoul family, inspired by the endangered otters living along the Han River beside the hotel.

The character was created to reflect the harmony between people and nature, rooted in the belief that a thriving city and a healthy environment can coexist.

Sudari’s name is an affectionate term for “otter” and also represents a bridge, symbolizing connection and understanding.

Through its warm and gentle presence, Sudari shares a message of environmental respect and thoughtful coexistence.

We invite you to join us in welcoming Sudari as a meaningful symbol of the values Fairmont Seoul holds close.

#### Fairmont Ambassador Seoul Awarded One MICHELIN Key

Fairmont Ambassador Seoul has been officially recognized with **One MICHELIN Key**, the Michelin Guide’s inaugural global hotel distinction celebrating the world’s most exceptional accommodations.

Evaluated across comprehensive criteria such as design, service, personality, and overall guest experience, this prestigious recognition affirms Fairmont Ambassador Seoul’s dedication to refined elegance and genuine hospitality.

We are honored to receive this distinction and remain committed to offering our guests unforgettable moments and an exceptional stay experience that embodies the spirit of Fairmont.

#### Spectrum - A Celebration of Global Flavors

Experience one of Seoul’s premier all-day dining destinations at Spectrum, where culinary traditions from around the world come together in an elegant and vibrant setting. Featuring a diverse selection of international cuisines, interactive live cooking stations, and seasonal specialties crafted with the finest ingredients, Spectrum offers a dynamic dining experience for every occasion.

Whether gathering with family, celebrating a special moment, or enjoying a leisurely meal, guests can explore an ever-evolving array of flavors—from fresh seafood and premium grilled selections to authentic regional dishes and exquisite desserts. Complemented by attentive service and a sophisticated atmosphere, Spectrum invites you to discover a world of exceptional taste in the heart of Fairmont Ambassador Seoul.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Ambassador Seoul located?

Fairmont Ambassador Seoul is located at 108 Yeoui-daero, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Ambassador Seoul?

The check-in time at Fairmont Ambassador Seoul is from 15:00, and the check-out time is at 11:00.

What is the cancellation policy at Fairmont Ambassador Seoul?

Cancellation is available until 18:00 on the day before check-in.

Does Fairmont Ambassador Seoul have a spa?

Yes, ESPA at Fairmont offers a selection of luxury treatments.

What is the closest airpot to Fairmont Ambassador Seoul?

Using Seoul's busy metro system, the Gimpo International Airport is only 20 minutes away from our hotel, while the Incheon International Airport is 60 minutes away from us.

Does Fairmont Ambassador Seoul have an airport shuttle?

Yes, we offer an airport shuttle service. Please contact us for arrangement.
