# InterContinental Paris - Le GrandDining

InterContinental Paris Le Grand proposes distinct areas dedicated to gastronomy, each offering a choice of cuisine and styles, where guests can share the vibrancy of Parisian society.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

1. La Verrière

2. Café de la Paix

### La Verrière

La Verrière is a refined 800 sqm winter garden, bathed in natural light beneath an impressive glass and metal atrium. A serene retreat in the heart of Paris, it is ideal for coffee, light dining or an aperitif. As evening falls, the space reveals a more intimate atmosphere under the warm glow of its iconic chandelier.

Learn more

### Café de la Paix

Located on Place de l’Opéra, Café de la Paix has been a symbol of Parisian life since 1862. Today, this elegant restaurant led by Chef Laurent André celebrates the great classics of French gastronomy, offering an exceptional dining experience overlooking the Opera Garnier.

## Café de la Paix

Both spectator and player, totally involved in the everyday life of Paris and the Parisians since 1862, the Café de la Paix is a place of legend, proposing one of the best tables in the capital, where friend and stranger mingle with visitors from the world over in a pleasantly convivial and soothing ambience. Our chef, Laurent André, proposes updated classic recipes, carefully and personally selecting only the freshest and best seasonal produce.
