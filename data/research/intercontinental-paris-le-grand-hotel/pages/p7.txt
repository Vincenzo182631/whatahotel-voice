### Formal Complaint – Unacceptable Room Cleanliness a

7 days ago

I am writing to express my extreme disappointment and dissatisfaction with my recent stay at InterContinental Paris Le Grand, from 12 to 16 October 2026, in Room 1129.

As a five-star luxury hotel, I had high expectations regarding cleanliness, hygiene, comfort, and overall service standards. Unfortunately, my experience was far below the standards expected from a hotel of your reputation.

Upon entering the room, I was shocked by the poor level of cleanliness. Even more concerning, I discovered what appeared to be bed bugs on the bed, as shown in the attached photographs. This is completely unacceptable, particularly for a hotel that prides itself on providing a five-star luxury experience.

I immediately raised this serious concern with the hotel manager, who assured me that the room would undergo a deep cleaning. However, despite this assurance, the bed bug issue remained unresolved, making the situation even more disappointing and distressing.

I find it extremely concerning that such a serious hygiene issue was not properly addressed, even after bringing it directly to management’s attention. Guests should never have to worry about the cleanliness and safety of their accommodation, especially when paying for a luxury hotel experience.

This experience has significantly damaged my confidence in the InterContinental brand. The condition of the room and the failure to resolve my complaint were entirely inconsistent with the standards expected of a five-star establishment.

I would appreciate a formal explanation of how this situation occurred, why it was not adequately resolved following my complaint, and what corrective measures will be taken.

Furthermore, considering the seriousness of this matter, the inconvenience caused, and the failure to provide an acceptable resolution during my stay, I am requesting a full refund of my accommodation charges as appropriate compensation for this unacceptable experience.

I expect this complaint to be treated with the seriousness it deserves and look forward to receiving a prompt response and satisfactory resolution.
