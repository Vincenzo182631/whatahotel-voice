### Green Key

Green Key certified since 2010, we are committed to reducing the environmental impact of our activities and preserving the environment through our ongoing sustainability efforts.

Learn more

### Algotherm Dermo Ocean Spa

The Spa offers a refined wellness experience in the elegant atmosphere of a Parisian apartment. Featuring four single treatment rooms and one double suite, it is a serene retreat where expert therapists deliver exceptional treatments by Algotherm, a renowned French skincare brand combining advanced technology and expert manual techniques.

Learn more

### La Verrière

La Verrière is a refined 800 sqm winter garden, bathed in natural light beneath an impressive glass and metal atrium. A serene retreat in the heart of Paris, it is ideal for coffee, light dining or an aperitif. As evening falls, the space reveals a more intimate atmosphere under the warm glow of its iconic chandelier.

Learn more

### Café de la Paix

Located on Place de l’Opéra, Café de la Paix has been a symbol of Parisian life since 1862. Today, this elegant restaurant led by Chef Laurent André celebrates the great classics of French gastronomy, offering an exceptional dining experience overlooking the Opera Garnier.

Learn more

### Discover Our Signature Suites

The five Signature Suites offer a refined vision of luxury, comfort and Parisian living. Designed by Pierre-Yves Rochon, they blend contemporary elegance with ultimate comfort and feature panoramic views over the rooftops of the Opera Garnier or the Eiffel Tower. True havens above the City of Light.

## FAQs for InterContinental Paris - Le Grand

Does InterContinental Paris - Le Grand have a pool?

No, InterContinental Paris - Le Grand does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental Paris - Le Grand?

At InterContinental Paris - Le Grand, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 55 EUR .

The hotel has 12 parking spaces, costing €55 per night.

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Paris - Le Grand?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Paris - Le Grand.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Paris - Le Grand for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Paris - Le Grand have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Paris - Le Grand has 2 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Paris - Le Grand?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Paris - Le Grand?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Paris - Le Grand.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Paris - Le Grand?

Check-in at InterContinental Paris - Le Grand is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is InterContinental Paris - Le Grand from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Paris - Le Grand is conveniently located near Paris Orly (ORY), approximately 15.53 miles away (25 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Paris - Le Grand?

InterContinental Paris - Le Grand is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Tuileries Gardens
- Arc de Triomphe
- Chateau de Versailles
- Musee d’Orsay
- Musee du Louvre
- Opera Garnier
- Sainte Chapelle
- Tour Eiffel
- Musee Rodin

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Paris - Le Grand puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
