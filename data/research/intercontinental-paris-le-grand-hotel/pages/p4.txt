### Green Key

Green Key certified since 2010, we are committed to reducing the environmental impact of our activities and preserving the environment through our ongoing sustainability efforts.

Learn more

### Algotherm Dermo Ocean Spa

The Spa offers a refined wellness experience in the elegant atmosphere of a Parisian apartment. Featuring four single treatment rooms and one double suite, it is a serene retreat where expert therapists deliver exceptional treatments by Algotherm, a renowned French skincare brand combining advanced technology and expert manual techniques.

Learn more

### Exclusive luxury at the Club Lounge

The Club Lounge embodies calm and comfort at the heart of the hotel. This refined space offers an elevated guest experience combining personalised service with exclusive privileges. Open daily, it serves breakfast, afternoon tea and an evening reception with a selection of refined canapés, creating a peaceful retreat throughout the day.

Learn more
