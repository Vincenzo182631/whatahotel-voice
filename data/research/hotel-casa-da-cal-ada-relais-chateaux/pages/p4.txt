CASA DA CALÇADA

#### A MAGICAL PLACE

A centuries-old property. An enchanted past that blends with the history of Amarante and Portugal. A visionary place, where conspiracies were made in the name of the republic, where the future was reinvented, where the local community and the country were served, and where wines and dreams were created and fermented. A house that gained fame as a hotel and that reopens its doors. Casa da Calçada is back, as is its charm and exclusivity. Welcome to this magical house in the heart of Amarante, whose spirit flows to the rhythm of the waters of the Tâmega, from which it learned that everything changes and that's a good thing!
