Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou
Experience the Art of Luxury in the heart of Guangzhou.
Located in the center of Pearl River New City, The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou is a luxury destination in the Southern China hub. Distinctive services and facilities include 350 guest rooms, with the Club Lounge and suites and guest rooms newly renovated, as well as five restaurants and lounges, including the signature Cantonese restaurant, Lai Heen, which has retained its Michelin star consecutively since the inaugural Michelin Guide Guangzhou in 2018. An extensive health club and The Ritz-Carlton Spa rejuvenate body and mind, while extensive catering and meeting facilities span over 3,300 square meters, including a large pillarless Grand Ballroom that measures 1,209 square meters. The Residences at The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou features 90 luxury serviced apartments of one to four bedrooms in size.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou, with rates of ¥16 per hour, ¥192 per day, and ¥1344 per week.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou is Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN). CAN is located approximately 45.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement.
