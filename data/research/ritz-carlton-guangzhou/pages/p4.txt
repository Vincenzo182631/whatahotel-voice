Escape Worldly Cares at This Guangzhou Spa
Spa
From the moment you set foot inside The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Guangzhou, the bustle of the city simply vanishes. Here you will discover a retreat where soothing, organic tones and sumptuous finishes invite you to relax and rebalance. Explore signature treatments designed to restore the body, mind and spirit. Soothe your aching muscles with a therapeutic massage, or impart new radiance to your skin with a personalized facial treatment. Encompassing more than 43,000 square feet on an entire floor of the hotel, the spa includes a fitness center and a heated, outdoor swimming pool.

Featured Treatments
Pearl River Stone Massage
A moment of pure indulgence to soothe both body and mind. A relaxing aromatherapy massage with powerful essential oils and warm river-smoothed stones eases tension and liberates energy flow, while the acupressure massage that follows brings an overwhelming sense of wellbeing and calm. 120 minutes.
Custom Facial
Purify, hydrate, brighten and smooth your complexion with this radiance-restoring facial that is tailored to your individual needs. 60 or 90 minutes.
The Men’s Massage
The ultimate male escape. Unwind in style with this rebalancing and intensely relaxing treatment that includes a full body massage using heated stones, along with a facial massage and a soothing scalp massage. 90 minutes.
Naturally Nurtured Massage
Nurture and nourish body and skin when you need it most with this gentle, restorative and entirely tailored massage. Ideal for expectant mothers after the first trimester. 60 or 90 minutes.
Prior to Arrival
When can I reserve a massage or treatment?
Spa treatments and services are available daily from 10 a.m. to 11 p.m.
How do I make a reservation?
Reservations can be made through the Spa reception desk by telephone at (86 20) 3813 6668 or in person on level 4 of the hotel.
Where can I keep my valuables?
Lockers for personal items are available for all guests of The Ritz-Carlton Spa, although hotel guests are advised to leave their valuables in the safe located in their guest rooms for additional security.
Spa Services
What if I have special health considerations?
Please notify your Spa Reservation Agent of high blood pressure, allergies, physical ailments, disabilities or pregnancy when making your reservation. In the case of pregnancy specifically, many treatments and services may still be enjoyed. Alcohol consumption should be avoided before, during and after treatments as well as prior to and during the use of sauna, steam room and soaking tub facilities.
Are bathrobes provided?
Disposable undergarments, bathrobes and slippers are provided in pre-treatment lounges for ladies and gentlemen. During treatments, guests’ bodies are fully draped except for the area receiving attention.
Spa Policies
Should I be aware of additional fees or gratuities?
All prices listed are inclusive of taxes and any other additional charges. For more information or reservations, please call (86 20) 3813 6668 or email

Fitness Center
Open seven days a week. Yoga Monday & Wednesday at 9 a.m. and Saturday at 10 a.m.
Fitness Orientation
A 60-minute introductory session consisting of a guided tour of the Fitness Center’s facilities and services combined with instructions on the safe usage of each machine and live demonstrations of exercises.
Private Personal Training
Arrange a private 60-minute session with a professional personal trainer who will provide guidance and insights into your fitness goals and practices.
Additional Wellness Amenities
Additional wellness amenities include a 25x10 meter outdoor heated swimming pool, soaking tub, poolside cabanas and sun lounges.
