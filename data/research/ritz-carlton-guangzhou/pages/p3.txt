Authentic, Michelin-Starred Cantonese Cuisine
Lai Heen, the signature Cantonese restaurant at The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou, earned its Michelin star since the inaugural Michelin Guide Guangzhou 2018. Considered among the quality Cantonese restaurants in Guangzhou, Lai Heen offers fine Cantonese dining experiences in a traditionally-styled atmosphere. Beyond the main dining room, take refuge in eight semi-private and six private dining rooms. Enjoy contemporary inspirations alongside classic dishes prepared under the watchful supervision of Executive Chinese Chef Aiden Huang and expertly paired course-by-course with fine teas and wines. Every dish is full of surprises and surpasses expectations. Browsing through the menu opens you up to a treasure trove of rare and premium culinary ingredients. Combined with consummate cooking skills, strict selection process and constant pursuit of unique ingredients, Lai Heen honors the cultural heritage of Guangzhou.

Italian Flavors in the Heart of Guangzhou
LIMONI
Focusing on elegant simplicity and the unimpeachable authenticity of natural flavors, LIMONI offers an enlivening and casual environment in which to enjoy authentic Italian cuisine. Marvel at the culinary team’s prowess as they work in the restaurant’s lively open kitchen, feed your imagination with the thrilling potential of an extensive wine cellar, and then begin the celebration of the senses that infuses every meal at LIMONI.

FOODS
A reincarnation of New York's original brasserie, FOODS offers flavorful Asian and Western culinary traditions in a rustic, urban setting. Breakfast, lunch and dinner take center stage as six live-action cooking stations feature a mélange of vibrant colors and fresh flavors. Each day, the Ladies and Gentlemen of FOODS’ culinary team present an innovative collection of dishes that fill the restaurant with heady aromas and tantalizing hints at the delicacies which lie in store for intrepid diners.

Afternoon Tea and After-hours Cocktails
Pearl Lounge
Bright, sunlit spaces and rich furnishings lend an air of European sophistication to the Pearl Lounge at The Ritz-Carlton, Guangzhou. Join friends for a signature Afternoon Tea experience accompanied by live music during the day, or arrange an after-dinner social rendezvous. In addition to its innovative Tea Bar, which features more than 100 different tea varietals, the lounge offers delicate, inspired creations by the hotel’s pastry team.

The Churchill Bar
Lose yourself in the effortless, wood-paneled style of The Churchill Bar. Featuring expertly- mixed drinks, wines, beers, fine cognacs and extensive collection of single malt whiskeys, the well-stocked bar has something to offer guests of all appetites. Meanwhile, a selection of flavorful imported cigars; hand-made chocolates; a Snooker table and live entertainment by a pianist-vocalist duo all set the stage for an exciting night in town.
