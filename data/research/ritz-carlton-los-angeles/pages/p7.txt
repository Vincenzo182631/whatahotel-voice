Elevate Your Stay
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.

A Private Retreat
Club Lounge Access
Begin your stay at our luxury downtown Los Angeles hotel with access to our Club Lounge overlooking panoramic views of the city skyline and exclusive concierge services. For the rest of your stay, consider the Club Lounge your private sanctuary. For guests who reserve a stay with The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge access, we welcome up to two guests per room to enjoy the Club Lounge facilities. An additional charge per person per day will be applied to your account for any additional guests.

In Good Taste
Daily Culinary Presentations
At our hotel with Club Lounge access in Los Angeles, it is our pleasure to offer complimentary food and beverage presentations five times each day. These daily offerings – breakfast, lunch, snacks, hors d’oeuvres and cordials – tell the destination’s story through locally sourced ingredients, iconic dishes and signature drinks. Meal presentations are accompanied by crafted coffees or cocktails in the comfort of self-service or assisted by the Club Lounge hosts.
