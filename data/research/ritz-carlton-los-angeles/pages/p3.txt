Exquisite Cuisine against Skyline Views
Dining
Embark on a culinary journey. Dining at our downtown Los Angeles hotel offers something for every moment—whether you’re savoring crafted cocktails, enjoying a relaxed poolside bite, or indulging in elevated dishes inspired by Latin American flavors and traditions. Cap it all with sweeping skyline views at our rooftop cocktail bar, a standout destination in downtown Los Angeles.
A Culinary Journey Down the Pan American Highway
Sendero
Perched on the hotel’s 24th floor with sweeping views of the city, Sendero invites guests on a culinary journey inspired by the Pan American highway. This Latin American restaurant in downtown Los Angeles showcases a range of distinct dining experiences, highlighting the diverse flavors found across the region—from the seafood rich Baja coastline and the grasslands of Argentina to the vibrant dishes of major metropolitan centers.

Culinary Experiences
A Signature Tequila Experience
Discover the spirit of Los Angeles through our house-infused tequila, crafted for the perfect sip. Whether you're enjoying it paired with dinner or exploring the flavors at our exclusive Agave Library, this experience is designed to elevate your stay.
Chocolate Master Classes
Learn the art of chocolate sculpting from our award-winning pastry chef and master chocolatier Francois Behuet. Get a sneak peek at how Chef Francois creates incredible chocolate showpieces for holidays and award shows. For guests seeking an even more personalized experience, private chocolate classes are also available upon request.
