Stay near top attractions at our hotel near Crypto.com Arena
Experiences
Set in the heart of downtown, our five star Los Angeles hotel stands at the center of the vibrant L.A. LIVE district, where the city’s most iconic moments unfold. Championship games, sold-out concerts, and Hollywood’s biggest celebrations happen just steps away. Cheer on your favorite team with pro basketball and hockey at Crypto.com Arena within walking distance, and Dodger Stadium and the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum only minutes from our doors. Immerse yourself in L.A.’s cultural landscape at nearby attractions like the Museum of Contemporary Art and the soon to open Lucas Museum of Narrative Art. And for a true taste of Los Angeles glamour, experience major award shows right outside— from the GRAMMYs and Emmys to the Oscars, making their L.A. LIVE debut in 2029.
