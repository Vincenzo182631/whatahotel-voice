Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles
Experience our 5-star hotel in Los Angeles, CA
Set within vibrant downtown surroundings, The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles effortlessly embodies the timeless appeal of a legendary city. As a downtown Los Angeles luxury hotel located at L.A. LIVE - home to world-class sports and extraordinary live entertainment - our property features a captivating atmosphere shaped by dynamic surroundings. Discover a singular sense of place through luxurious accommodations, dramatic downtown L.A. skyline views, relaxing spa services and imaginative cuisine, all enhanced by unparalleled local access. At our luxury L.A. LIVE hotel, the free spirit of southern California combines with the discerning sophistication of Los Angeles.

Los Angeles Joie de Vivre
The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles celebrates a modern energy while paying homage to the lavish spirit of the city’s Golden Age. The hotel is a tribute to Los Angeles’s Art Deco heritage, featuring references to the cinema, fashion, and culture of the 1920s interwoven with contemporary touches and personalized service.
Modern Art Déco Design
In the lobby, hand-painted, gilded wallpaper shows a whimsical scene originally created for fashion designer Jeanne Lanvin’s 1920’s Parisian home. Custom geometric grillage pieces inspired by metalwork artist Edgar Brandt line guestroom corridors, and etched mirror headboards emulating artwork from glass-maker Lalique adorn every guestroom of our downtown LA luxury hotel.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles. The daily valet fee is $71.50 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles is Hollywood Burbank Airport (BUR). BUR is located approximately 16.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Los Angeles offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
