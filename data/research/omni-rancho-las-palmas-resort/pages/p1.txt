## Rancho Mirage, California

### A resort retreat near Palm Springs, California

Set amid a mountainous backdrop surrounded by palms, Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort & Spa is an idyllic resort in Palm Springs, CA — a setting for countless activities and memorable moments in the Coachella Valley. Guest rooms and suites at our Palm Springs resort are bright and welcoming, thoughtfully inspired by Spanish Colonial design with French doors that open out to patios or balconies. Spend leisurely days playing tennis or making a splash in the crystalline pools or waterpark. Relax with a soothing massage in our resort spa or tee off on one of three palm-fringed golf courses with views of the San Jacinto and San Bernardino mountains from our California resort. After a day enjoying our resort activities, dine in one of six inviting restaurants.

## Traveler's Insights

### What are the property amenities at Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort & Spa?

Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort & Spa is home to world-class amenities including:

- Three different kinds of pools (including adults-only) and a waterpark
- A full-service spa
- 27 holes of golf (three nine-hole courses)
- Tennis and pickleball courts
- A fully-equipped fitness center
- 50,000 square feet of meeting and event space
- Six dining outlets - three full-service restaurants, two pool bars and a café
- Family-friendly resort activities
- Self-parking and valet parking with a nightly charge

### What is the best area to stay in when visiting Palm Springs?

Rancho Mirage, near Palm Springs is the ideal location for your next trip to Southern California. Our Palm Springs resort is surrounded by numerous things to do in the area. Experience the annual Palm Desert Food & Wine™, Indian Canyons, Aerial Tramway and more when you visit our resort near Palm Springs, CA.

### WHERE IS OMNI RANCHO LAS PALMAS RESORT & SPA LOCATED?

Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort & Spa is located in Rancho Mirage and sits on 240 lush acres in the heart of the Coachella Valley near Palm Springs, California. The physical address is 41000 Bob Hope Drive, Rancho Mirage, California, 92270

### WHAT IS THE CLOSEST AIRPORT TO OMNI RANCHO LAS PALMAS RESORT & SPA?

Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort & Spa is near the following airports:

Palm Springs International Airport (PSP) is approximately 10 miles (23 minutes) away.

Ontario International Airport (ONT) is about 75 miles (1 hour and 20 minutes) away.

Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) is about 130 miles (2 hours and 30 minutes) away.

### How much is parking at Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort & Spa?

- Self-parking is $30, plus tax per night/vehicle

- Valet parking is $40, plus tax per night/vehicle

- 2 Tesla Charging Stations + 2 Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Stations. Please see valet attendant or Front Desk for assistance.

### WHAT TIME IS CHECK-IN AND CHECK-OUT?

- Check-in is at 4:00 p.m.
- Check-out is at 11:00 a.m.
- Guests must be 18 years of age to book a room.

### IS THE RESORT PET FRIENDLY?

Yes, Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort & Spa is pet friendly in specific buildings. Pets must be less than 50 pounds and a $150 charge per pet, per stay will be applied. Pet friendly amenities are included. Learn more about our pet policy.
