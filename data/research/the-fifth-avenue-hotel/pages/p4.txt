## About Flâneur Hospitality

The spirit of the flâneur—an urban wayfarer and observer, who believes that to be present in the moment is the ultimate luxury—is the essence of everything we do at The Fifth.

## The Mansion and The Tower

The Fifth Avenue Hotel is built on the former estate of Mrs. Charlotte Goodridge, a legendary Gilded Age hostess whose home was the epicenter of culture and discourse in 19th-century New York City. The mansion then housed a fine art gallery before being reimagined by legendary architectural firm McKim, Mead & White as the stately Second National Bank.

Today, following a 10-year restoration by Flâneur Hospitality, the original brick and limestone Renaissance-style palazzo is complemented by a shimmering 24-story modern glass tower. The two impressive edifices embody the marriage of classic, sumptuous luxury and sophisticated innovation that help to create an unsurpassed guest experience at The Fifth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### About The Fifth

What is The Fifth Avenue Hotel?

The Fifth Avenue Hotel is an independent luxury hotel located in Manhattan’s NoMad neighborhood. Combining a restored Gilded Age mansion with a contemporary tower, the hotel offers a refined New York City experience defined by exceptional design, personalized hospitality, and acclaimed dining.

What makes The Fifth Avenue Hotel unique?

The Fifth Avenue Hotel seamlessly blends historic architecture with contemporary luxury. The property’s restored Gilded Age mansion, modern tower, richly appointed interiors, and highly personalized service create a distinctive stay that reflects the creativity and energy of New York City.

What neighborhood is The Fifth Avenue Hotel in?

The hotel is located in NoMad, one of Manhattan’s most vibrant neighborhoods known for its historic architecture, award-winning restaurants, boutique shopping, cultural attractions, and convenient access to Midtown, Chelsea, and Downtown Manhattan.

Where is The Fifth Avenue Hotel located?

The Fifth Avenue Hotel is located at **1 West 28th Street, New York, NY 10001**, at the corner of Fifth Avenue and West 28th Street in Manhattan’s NoMad (North of Madison Square Park) neighborhood. The hotel is within walking distance of Madison Square Park, the Flatiron Building, Empire State Building, Fifth Avenue shopping, Eataly, Koreatown, and numerous restaurants, galleries, and cultural attractions.
