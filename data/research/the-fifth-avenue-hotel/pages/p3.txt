## Frequently Asked Questions

Does the hotel have a cocktail bar?

Yes. Guests can enjoy handcrafted cocktails, premium wines, and seasonal beverages in sophisticated bar and lounge spaces designed for both casual gatherings and special occasions.

Is Café Carmellini open to non-hotel guests?

Yes. Café Carmellini welcomes both hotel guests and visitors. Reservations are recommended.

Is private dining available?

Yes. Private dining experiences may be arranged for celebrations, business dinners, and intimate gatherings.

What dining experiences are available at The Fifth Avenue Hotel?

The Fifth Avenue Hotel is home to **Café Carmellini**, the acclaimed restaurant from Chef Andrew Carmellini, offering refined French and Italian-inspired cuisine with global influences in an elegant setting.
