# THE FIFTH AVENUE HOTEL

Before it was ours, this Fifth Avenue mansion had worn a few different guises. It was a home, a gallery, a bank, a local fixture.

Today, the restored 1907 landmark stands beside a contemporary glass tower, with Martin Brudnizki bringing both to life in rich palettes and worldly curiosities. Pae White’s Bugs & Drugs tapestry invites wonder into the lobby. Photographs by William Klein, Gordon Parks, and Melvin Sokolsky adorn the walls. Cabinets of collectibles call for a closer look.

Magical luxury lives in the details: a butler by text, a Maxi Bar already stocked, a house car waiting below, while Andrew Carmellini gives the address its culinary point of view. Historic and modern. Intimate and imaginative. Very much New York.
