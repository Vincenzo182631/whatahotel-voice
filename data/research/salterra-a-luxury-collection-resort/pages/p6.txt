## Festive at Salterra

There is a different rhythm to the holidays here — salt on the breeze, light turning gold over the water, days that unhurry themselves. From the first candlelit dinner to the last fireworks over the water, the festive season at Salterra is built around the moments that matter — cookie decorating with little ones, seafood feasts pulled from island waters, quiet mornings on the sand, and evenings that stretch late beneath the stars. This is the holidays, reimagined on South Caicos: warm, unhurried, and unmistakably of the island.

### Every Tradition, Reimagined by the Sea.

Explore Salterra

When is the festive season at Salterra?

Festive '26–'27 runs from December 19, 2026 through January 2, 2027, with signature celebrations taking place December 24 through January 1.

Is there a minimum-stay requirement?

A minimum stay of seven nights applies for arrivals between December 26 and January 2.

How do booking and payment work?

A credit card is required to reserve, with no deposit taken at booking. Full payment is charged 90 days before arrival.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations are penalty-free up to 90 days before arrival. Within 90 days, a 99% penalty applies. No refunds are given for no-shows, late arrivals, early departures, or unused portions of a stay.

What is included in festive dining?

The Salterra Epicurean Experience is $475 per adult and $150 per young adult (ages 12–17) per night; children 11 and under are complimentary. It includes all scheduled festive programming and special dining events.

How do I make a reservation?

Reach out to our team to begin planning your festive stay.
