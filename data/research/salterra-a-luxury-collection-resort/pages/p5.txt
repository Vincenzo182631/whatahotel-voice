The South Caicos Salinas

### Immersive Island Discovery

Once the heart of South Caicos’ salt industry, the Salinas now stand as a quiet testament to the island’s past—and a thriving natural ecosystem. These shallow, sunlit pools once produced some of the world’s finest sea salt and today attract migratory birds from across the region. Flamingos, herons, and egrets can often be seen wading through the still waters, adding color and grace to this storied...See More landscape. In the dry season, the Salinas begin to evaporate, revealing crystalized salt that can be gently raked by hand—just as it was for centuries. A place where history and nature exist in quiet harmony.See Less

With four stunning swimming pools nestled across the property, Salterra invites guests to unwind in style beneath the Caribbean sun.
