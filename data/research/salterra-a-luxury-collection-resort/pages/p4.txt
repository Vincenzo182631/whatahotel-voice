Aquathermal Experience

## The Aquathermal Treatment Experience

Aquathermal therapy heals and soothes the body while putting the mind at ease. Featuring a salt sauna and steam room, the aquathermal circuit is an immersive, experiential journey within the spa—an unhurried passage through heat, mineral, and steam that draws on the same elemental connection that has shaped South Caicos for generations. Day passes provide access to the spa pool, hot tub, steam room, and sauna, each pass $50 per guest. Aquathermal Treatment Experience from $50++, subject to applicable taxes and service charge.

Signature Ritual

### The Salterra Salt Bar & Body Ritual

A 120-minute journey inspired by the healing properties of salt and the traditions of the island. After a personalized consultation, the experience unfolds through an essential oil awakening, dry lymphatic body brushing, and a natural salt exfoliation, before a bespoke massage using handcrafted healing salt blends formulated to invigorate, nurture, warm, or soothe. Linger afterward in the salt sauna, steam room, and outdoor relaxation jacuzzi, where heat, mineral, and water work in quiet concert.
