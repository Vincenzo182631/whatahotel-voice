# Turks and Caicos Oceanfront Luxury

## Accommodations

Graced with sweeping views of the ocean, every guestroom and suite at Salterra is a celebration of space, serenity, and coastal beauty. The resort features 100 luxurious accommodations, including 52 expansive...See More one- and two-bedroom suites and two grand Governor’s Suites, each designed to elevate the art of island living. All rooms offer generous balconies and ocean-facing views—while ground-floor guestrooms feature private lanais that open directly to the beach landscape. With thoughtful touches such as in-room filtered water dispensers, Salterra invites guests to experience effortless refinement at every turn.See Less

### Seamless Accessibility

Salterra is committed to inclusive comfort, offering beach wheelchairs, a sloped-entry lagoon pool, and a thoughtfully designed selection of rooms with roll-in showers, accessible bathtubs, and lowered fixtures to ensure ease and accessibility throughout your stay.

Oceanfront Living in Turks and Caicos

## Expansive Suites

At Salterra, our one- and two-bedroom hotel suites in South Caicos are serene retreats designed for effortless living. Each opens to sweeping ocean views and generous outdoor space, with interiors curated for comfort and connection. Enjoy spacious living rooms, elegant dining areas, and bathrooms featuring soaking tubs and rain showers. Perfect for couples or families, these suites offer space not as a luxury, but as a way of being.

## Exclusive Penthouse Suites

Framed by floor-to-ceiling windows and wraparound balconies, our two Penthouse Suites sit atop the resort with panoramic ocean views and 2,800 square feet of elevated living. Featuring three bedrooms, a full kitchen, expansive indoor spaces, and dedicated butler service, each suite is a private sanctuary of refined luxury.
