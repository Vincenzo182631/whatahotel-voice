# Welcome to Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos

## Find the ultimate haven at our South Caicos resort

Discover a world where senses are heightened at Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos. Expand your capacity to live in the moment during your stay at this luxury resort in Turks and...See More Caicos. At Salterra, remote tranquility gives way to a new palette of sensations amid breathtaking settings: clear, turquoise waters meet white sand beaches, rising into jagged bluffs covered in unique tropical flora and fauna. Immerse yourself in our signature South Caicos resort experiences - rare, captivating, and curated by a personalised concierge to reveal the vivid colours of the island.See Less

## Festive Season at Salterra

This December, the island slows and the celebrations begin. Spend the festive season on South Caicos with candlelit dinners by the water, traditions made for family, and two nights of fireworks over the sea. Discover Christmas and New Year's at Salterra.

## From the City to the Sea, Nonstop

BermudAir's new nonstop lands you directly at South Caicos International Airport (XSC) — no connection through Miami or Providenciales, and no ferry. Salterra is a ten-minute drive from the runway, so you can leave the New York area in the morning and be in the water the same afternoon. Twice weekly on Sundays and Thursdays from Newark Liberty (EWR), December 20, 2026 through April 25, 2027 — three and a half hours, gate to sand.

### Sustainability at Salterra

At Salterra, the natural environment is both our greatest luxury and our deepest responsibility. From solar-powered energy and composting initiatives to the elimination of single-use plastics, every effort reflects a deep commitment to the land and sea.Through the South Caicos Coral Reef Consortium, we proudly invest in the restoration and protection of the vibrant marine world that surrounds us.

With two convenient nonstop options, getting to Salterra is now easier than ever.

An Escape Shaped by Stillness

### Getting to Salterra

The most seamless route to Salterra is American Airlines’ nonstop flight from Miami (MIA) to South Caicos (XSC), available November through August. Beginning December 20, BermudAir will also offer nonstop service from Newark (EWR) to South Caicos (XSC) twice weekly, on Thursdays and Sundays. The airport is just minutes from the resort, with transfers that can be arranged with our concierge team. Guests may also fly into Providenciales (PLS) and connect via a short scenic flight. Please note: Providenciales is a separate island, not accessible to South Caicos by car.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos?

The check-in time at Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $120.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos?

On-site parking is available at Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos booking.

What is the closest airport near Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos?

The closest airport near Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos is South Caicos Airport (XSC). XSC is located approximately 2.5 Miles from the hotel.

Does Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos have an airport shuttle to South Caicos Airport (XSC)?

Yes, Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos has an airport shuttle to South Caicos Airport (XSC).

Does Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Salterra, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, South Caicos does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
