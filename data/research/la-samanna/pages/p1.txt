# Location

Baie Longue, Terres Basses, 97064 St Martin

Serene, sun-drenched and sophisticated, Saint Martin is one of the Caribbean’s best islands. Part of the French West Indies, its blend of French, Dutch and Caribbean cultures creates a character unlike anywhere else.

Immerse yourself in island life at La Samanna on the white sands of Baie Longue. From here, you are ideally located to explore local highlights like Fort Louis and the Marigot market.

- 15 min drive from Princess Juliana International Airport (SXM)
- 20 min drive from Marigot
- 20 min ferry from Blowing Point, Anguilla
- 40 min drive from Philipsburg, Sint Maarten
