La Samanna, A Belmond Hotel
With impeccable restaurants, complimentary water sports, and villas fit for royalty, Belmond is doing Baie Longue right.

In a nutshell
: For toes-in-the-sand luxury with impeccable service, foodie-approved eateries, Caribbean-cool design, and views that will have all your friends and family seeing green, La Samanna can’t be beat. Set along the white sands of Baie Longue, known for being one of the most beautiful beaches in all of St. Martin, guests are never far from the startlingly turquoise waters.
Hannah Chubb
Hannah Chubb
Rooms
There are 76 rooms and suites on the property, plus 8 independent villas, all of which boast bright and airy interiors, oversized bathrooms, sumptuous beds and linens, Caribbean artwork, Roucou Ritual toiletries, and private terraces or balconies for taking in the sounds of the waves. Several rooms come with personal plunge pools, while others open directly onto the sand, so you can go from air-conditioned bed to beach bed with just a few barefoot steps.
Hannah Chubb
My family and I stayed in one of the property’s six four-bedroom villas (there are two three-bedroom villas available, as well) for three nights, and felt like absolute royalty from the moment we walked—sorry, were guided by our personal butler—through the front door. The villas line a cul-de-sac overlooking the ocean that sits about a 3-minute walk or 30-second golf cart ride from the hotel lobby, so while they feel slightly more removed, you’re still a hop, skip, and a jump from the beach, pools, and restaurants. This privacy is the main draw for many villa guests, and the double-level mansions have everything a group could need to vacation more than comfortably, including a fully stocked kitchen (with a pre-filled fridge), a ginormous backyard pool, and en suite bathrooms in every bedroom. Take it from me: Every little villa feature is top-of-the-line—from the DeLonghi coffee maker to the marble-topped outdoor dining table—and worth the splurge.

Hannah Chubb
Food and drink
If you’re staying on the property for a week or less, there’s really no reason to leave for meals (and, frankly, you won’t want to). With three main restaurants—one of which changes in concept between lunch and dinner—plus a fabulous daily breakfast and top-notch room service, you’ll never get bored of the on-site eats.
Corail is where a complimentary breakfast is served each morning, complete with a killer buffet featuring tropical fruits aplenty (including fresh, straw-pierced coconuts), an omelet bar, and pastries so good you’ll be reminded that you are, in fact, on the French side of the island. There are also à la carte options, and the Old Bay shrimp toast and caramelized banana French toast are both standouts. For lunch, you have two options: Baie Longue Bar, for Caribbean-inspired snacks and sips overlooking the sea, or Laplaj, down on the beach, which serves elevated small plates to scarf down before heading back to your spot in the sand—always get the side of fries.
Come evening, Laplaj turns into La Spiaggia, a trattoria-style dinner spot that feels like it was plucked off the Amalfi Coast, with a menu bursting with fresh Italian fare (the meatballs were some of the best I’ve ever had) and a selection of effervescent cocktails (Negroni Fizz, anyone?). But the star of the show is L’Oursin, the upscale French restaurant just off the lobby, where each table comes with a view of the sailboat-spotted sea. The sea urchin appetizer is popular for those with an affinity for shellfish, as are the shells baked “en cocotte” (my personal favorite starter), and mains range from a prime rib on the bone to a spiced lobster tail.
Amenities and experiences
You’ll find most guests sprawled out by one of the two pools or soaking up the sun on beach loungers, attendants at the ready to grab them another piña colada or move their fringed umbrellas when the sun shifts in the sky. But anyone hiding from the rays will likely be partaking in self-care activities, perhaps getting a treatment at the ultra-zen spa, participating in one of the daily complimentary wellness classes (from Pilates to chakra-healing meditation), or rallying with a friend or instructor on one of the three tennis courts.
Guests who prefer imbibing to detoxing can also book cigar-rolling workshops, wine and rum tastings, and even personalized private dinners in Le Cave, which is the oldest wine cellar in the region and down the stairs from L’Oursin. If you’re visiting for a special occasion—proposal, milestone birthday, honeymoon—you’ll want to make note of this swanky private space.

What to do nearby
It’s all about the beach in St. Martin, and La Samanna offers plenty of ways to get out on the water. Walk down to the sand from the lobby, and you’ll bump into the Watersports Center, where you can take out complimentary kayaks, Hobie Cat sailboats, paddleboards, and snorkeling gear to explore Baie Longue, or try something with a motor for an additional fee (my husband and I couldn’t resist taking a WaveRunner out for a 30-minute spin, which cost $100). For something further out, you can book a number of boat tours, including a two-hour snorkeling excursion along a protected natural reserve, or a private charter to the neighboring islands of St. Barts and Anguilla—both easy day trips.
The backstory
In the early 1970s, American businessman James Frankel set out to find a vacation home in St. Martin for his family. When he stumbled upon the beauty of Baie Longue, he was hooked, and bought 55 acres of beachfront property. He soon learned that the land he’d purchased was zoned for commercial use, and quickly pivoted to build a hotel that would pay homage to his original intentions: a family-friendly getaway named after his three daughters. (“Sammana” is a blend of Samantha, Anouk, and Nathalie.) Belmond took ownership of the hotel in 1996, taking on thoughtful renovations and upgrades that led to the top-of-the-line resort you see today.
Insider tip
Don’t count out tennis just because the island is known for its high temperatures. All three grass courts have stadium-style lighting, so you can play after dark when things get cooler. One more reason to splurge on cute new athleisure!
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Yes, littles are catered to with kids' menus, babysitting services, specialized programming, and more
Accessibility:
Three guest rooms have been adapted for guests with physical disabilities
Wellness:
Yes, with complimentary wellness classes, a full spa, and fitness classes
