# Kimpton CottonwoodLocation

Kimpton Cottonwood Hotel is set in the revitalized Blackstone District, just a short drive to downtown. Here you can check out stylish restaurants, cafes and bars along Farnam Street, or take one of our bike cruisers to explore nearby museums and parks.

## The highlights

1. Location

### Location

The Cottonwood Hotel is a prominent presence in Omaha’s revitalized Blackstone District, the city’s oldest and coolest neighborhood. From our front doors, you’re a walk away to some of the best restaurants, nightlife and shopping in town. When you explore, you’ll discover rich history on every street corner – and in the Cottonwood’s every nook.

## Things to do

### Joslyn Art Museum

2200 Dodge Street, Omaha, NE 68102

The Joslyn Museum was a gift to the people of Omaha from Sarah Joslyn in memory of her husband, George.

The Museum’s original 1931 building is one of the finest examples of Art Deco architecture in the nation. The Walter and Suzanne Scott Pavilion, a 58,000-square-foot addition built in 1994, was designed by renowned British architect Norman Foster, Lord Norman Foster of Thames Bank, as his first U.S. commission.
