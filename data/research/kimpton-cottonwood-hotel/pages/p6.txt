### Location

The Cottonwood Hotel is a prominent presence in Omaha’s revitalized Blackstone District, the city’s oldest and coolest neighborhood. From our front doors, you’re a walk away to some of the best restaurants, nightlife and shopping in town. When you explore, you’ll discover rich history on every street corner – and in the Cottonwood’s every nook.

Learn more

### Pool

Get a refreshing cool-down, soak in some rays on our sun shelf, and enjoy poolside grilling and DJ sets. The expansive deck is complete with loungers, rentable cabanas, shaded seating areas for cooling off on summer nights and a relaxing hot tub. The Cottonwood Pool Club is open seasonally from early-May thru mid-September.

Learn more

### The Committee Chophouse

At The Committee Chophouse, we blend playful contemporary touches with a premier Omaha steakhouse atmosphere, where our tender, aged beef takes center stage. Be sure to explore our interactive dessert menu, offering a creative and immersive way to indulge your sweet tooth.

Learn more

### Orleans Room

Located in the heart of The Blackstone District, Orleans Room offers a captivating dining experience where guests enjoy cuisine surrounded by an impressive backdrop of locally curated artwork. Rotating seasons play host to ever-changing, locally sourced ingredients that deliver a meticulously crafted menu that is creative, fresh, yet simplistic.

Learn more

### Weddings

At the Kimpton Cottonwood Hotel, our wedding venues make way for your visions, whether intimate or extravagant. With our customized culinary experiences and rich, beautiful backdrops, you’ll love every moment – and your guests will never forget your special day.

Learn more

Does Kimpton Cottonwood have a pool?

How much is parking at Kimpton Cottonwood?

At Kimpton Cottonwood, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 37 USD .

Valet Parking offered to overnight at $37, event at $15, and restaurant/lounge guests at $10.

When is breakfast at Kimpton Cottonwood?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 7:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Cottonwood?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Cottonwood.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Cottonwood?

Check-in at Kimpton Cottonwood is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Cottonwood offer free Wi-Fi?

How far is Kimpton Cottonwood from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Cottonwood is conveniently located near Eppley Airfield (OMA), approximately 11 miles away (17.7 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
