### The Cottonwood Room

The Cottonwood Room is a timeless cocktail bar, offering a highly curated mixology experience in an intimate and welcoming setting. Located in the lower level of The Cottonwood Hotel, guests will enjoy an elevated speakeasy feel with their old friends and new acquaintances.

## Restaurant + bar highlights

### The Committee Chophouse

At The Committee Chophouse, we blend playful contemporary touches with a premier Omaha steakhouse atmosphere, where our tender, aged beef takes center stage. Be sure to explore our interactive dessert menu, offering a creative and immersive way to indulge your sweet tooth.

Learn more

### Orleans Room

Located in the heart of The Blackstone District, Orleans Room offers a captivating dining experience where guests enjoy cuisine surrounded by an impressive backdrop of locally curated artwork. Rotating seasons play host to ever-changing, locally sourced ingredients that deliver a meticulously crafted menu that is creative, fresh, yet simplistic.
