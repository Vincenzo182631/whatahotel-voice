# Miami Beach Restaurants

## Luxury Beyond Imagination

The dining experiences at The Setai exude elegance, setting them apart from other Miami Beach restaurants. Jaya offers the finest culinary creations from South Asia showcasing Chinese, Indian, Thai and Balinese fare, while the newly opened Japón provides an immersive experience featuring contemporary Japanese cuisine complemented by a captivating cocktail experience with an Asian twist at Jo's Bar and Courtyard. Additionally, Ocean Grill presents Mediterranean-Italian cuisine, complimented by a selection of premium grilled meats and fresh seafood in a serene oceanfront setting.

## Jaya

Helmed by visionary Executive Chef Vijayudu Veena, Jaya is a sophisticated celebration of South Asian gastronomies masterfully presented in an immersive courtyard ambiance.

### Dress Code Policy

_Please note, a casual elegant dress code is in place at Jaya. To ensure a sophisticated, comfortable environment for all, we ask that guests avoid sportswear, flip-flops, beachwear and exceedingly revealing clothing. Men are encouraged to wear a blazer and a button-down shirt, and avoid open-toed shoes and shorts. Entry to the restaurant based on adherence to the dress code is at management’s discretion. We recommend arranging bag storage prior to your visit._

### Executive Chef

#### Vijayudu Veena

Born in the city of Hyderabad, at the crossroads of North and South India, Executive Chef Vijayudu Veena pays homage to his culinary heritage with an array of signature Southeast Asian experiences at The Setai, Miami Beach. Veena attended and excelled at the acclaimed Institut Hotelier César Ritz in Le Bouveret, Switzerland where he achieved an advanced certificate and a bronze medal in culinary arts. Today as Executive Chef, Veena’s vision guides The Setai’s culinary drive to excellence and innovation.
