# Things to Do in Miami Beach

## Explore Miami and The Beach

From urban-coastal sophistication on the beach to cultural awakenings on the mainland, Miami Beach is a sprawling destination of thought-provoking expressions and intriguing things to do. In Miami Beach, time is best spent in avant-garde art museums and performance halls, strolling iconic streets, and exploring inspiring neighborhoods that will make you question all you thought you knew about the city.

## New World Center

500 17th Street, Miami Beach, FL 33139

(305) 680-5866

The New World Center is a Frank Gehry-designed concert hall and music pantheon in the heart of South Beach. Home of the New World Symphony and situated directly adjacent to Miami Beach SoundScape Park, this unique venue hosts prestigious live music performances and showcases the magnificent WallCast®concerts series for a perfect evening of masterful music to be enjoyed while picnicking outdoors.

- 0.5 Miles from The Setai Miami Beach
- 11 Minutes Walking
- 3 Minutes Driving

## Bass Museum of Art

2100 Collins Avenue, Miami Beach, FL 33139

(305) 673-7530

Still beaming from its ambitious $12-million-dollar renovation, The Bass is Miami Beach’s longtime contemporary art museum focusing on mid-career and established artists who reflect the spirit and international character of Miami Beach through influential permanent collections as well as fascinating traveling exhibits.

- 0.2 Miles from The Setai Miami Beach
- 3 Minutes Walking
- 2 Minutes Driving

## Ocean Drive

Ocean Drive, Miami Beach, FL.

A true South Beach icon and now a pedestrian-only thoroughfare, Ocean Drive continues to capture the imagination with its collection of pastel-and-neon Art Deco hotels, star-powered al fresco eateries, funky art galleries, happening all-night venues, and designer boutiques that make it the quintessential street to see and be seen.

- 2 Miles from The Setai Miami Beach
- 13 Minutes Driving

## Wynwood

266 NW 26th St, Miami, FL 33127

(786) 615-8828

Declared the “world’s coolest neighborhood,” Wynwood is a thriving cultural destination and global street art Mecca. This is the kaleidoscopic home of irreverent art spaces, inventive restaurants, breweries, funky night venues and shops, all revolving around the famous Wynwood Walls, a striking outdoor museum showcasing works by some of the world’s most renowned muralists.

- 6 Miles from The Setai Miami Beach
- 19 Minutes Driving

## The Design District

140 NE 39th St., Miami, FL 33137

(305) 722-7100 722 7100)

Dedicated to innovative fashion, design, architecture and elevated experiences, at Miami’s Design District, cutting-edge art is just part of everyday life. Home to luxe boutiques, upscale interior design and visual art spaces, catch an outdoor symphony concert in front of the striking Fly Eye Dome at Palm Court, peek inside the Institute of Contemporary Art, or just simply park your car… even parking garages here are magnificent works of art!

- 5.7 Miles from The Setai Miami Beach

## Calle Ocho

Little Havana Welcome Center 1442 SW 8th St. Miami, FL 33135

(305) 643-5500 643-5500)

Declared a “National Treasure” by the National Trust for Historic Preservation, Little Havana’s iconic Calle Ocho is the epicenter of Miami’s Cuban culture and home to a vibrant collection of authentic restaurants, popular café cubano window stands, vibrant street festivals, art galleries, bakeries, and fragrant cigar rolling shops. At night, the area transforms into a popular hub for live music acts and some serious salsa dancing.

- 6.7 Miles from The Setai Miami Beach
- 19 Minutes Driving

## Coral Gables

Coral Gables, FL, USA

Rightfully named “The City Beautiful,” historic Coral Gables is a gorgeously storied neighborhood of opulent European homes, elegant Spanish plazas, fountains and loggias, surrounded by towering banyan trees that line its wide and beautifully shaded avenues. Drive down verdant and scenic thoroughfares and then explore the downtown area and Miracle Mile shopping district. Coral Gables enjoys a robust cinema and theater scene, as well as some real world-class shopping, fine dining, and history museums.

- 14.2 Miles from The Setai Miami Beach
- 32 Minutes Driving

## Miami Beach Golf Club

2301 Alton Rd, Miami Beach, FL 33140

(305) 532-3350

Originally opened in 1923 and situated in glamorous Alton Road, Miami Beach Golf Club is considered the best public golf course in Miami. This 18-hole par 72 course is a historic treasure and a lush, scenic space to unwind in Miami Beach.
