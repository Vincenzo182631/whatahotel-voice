Japón

Welcome to Japón, an immersive journey into the heart of Japan’s rich culinary traditions and vibrant culture. Helmed by acclaimed chefs Vijayudu Veena and Iván Monzón, the menu reflects a masterful symphony of flavors, textures, and aesthetics that highlight the diversity and richness of contemporary Japanese cuisine, complemented by an extensive sake selection.

Japón has been named one of the 16 most beautiful restaurants in the world by the prestigious Prix Versailles.

Dress Code

Please note, a casual elegant dress code is in place at Japón. To ensure a sophisticated, comfortable environment for all, we ask that guests avoid sportswear, flip-flops, beachwear and exceedingly revealing clothing. Men are encouraged to wear a blazer and button-down shirt. Entry to restaurant based on adherence to dress code is at management’s discretion. Luggage storage is not offered in property. We recommend arranging bag storage prior to your visit.

## Miami To Kyoto—A Cocktail or Mocktail Omakase Experience

A journey from Miami to Kyoto, told through cocktails or mocktails. This immersive omakase unites Miami’s tropical vibrancy with Kyoto’s traditions, pairing bright citrus, herbs, and coastal freshness with botanicals, tea, fruit, and spices. ## Asian Night Bazaar

Asian Night Bazaar is a vibrant celebration of culture, cuisine, and entertainment, offering a truly unforgettable experience. Nestled in a lively al fresco courtyard, this nightly event combines the bold flavors of South Asian and contemporary Japanese cuisine, creating a unique culinary journey.
