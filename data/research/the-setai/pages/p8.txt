At Ocean Grill, indulge in the perfect blend of Mediterranean-Italian flavors while enjoying stunning ocean views. Whether you are savoring a fresh seafood dish, classic fresh pasta, or a Mediterranean-inspired entrée, each bite is a symphony of tastes. Dress Code

Please note, a casual elegant dress code is in place during dinner at Ocean Grill. To ensure a sophisticated, comfortable environment for all, we ask that guests avoid sportswear, flip-flops, beachwear and exceedingly revealing clothing. Men are encouraged to wear a blazer and a button-down shirt, and avoid open-toed shoes and shorts. Entry to the restaurant based on adherence to the dress code is at management’s discretion. Luggage storage is not offered in property. We recommend arranging bag storage prior to your visit.
