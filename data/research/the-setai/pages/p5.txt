Jaya

Meaning ‘victory’ in Sanskrit, Jaya was conceived as a celebration of culinary diversity that exalts regional specialties from across South Asia. Experience traditional tandoor oven delights alongside wok-crafted curries, indulge in Jaya's signature Peking Duck, and take in live entertainment nightly.

Dress Code

Please note, a casual elegant dress code is in place at Jaya. To ensure a sophisticated, comfortable environment for all, we ask that guests avoid sportswear, flip-flops, beachwear and exceedingly revealing clothing. Men are encouraged to wear a blazer and a button-down shirt. Elegant sandals and shorts are welcome during brunch. Entry to the restaurant based on adherence to the dress code is at management’s discretion. Luggage storage is not offered in property. We recommend arranging bag storage prior to your visit.

## Asian Night Bazaar

Asian Night Bazaar is a vibrant celebration of culture, cuisine, and entertainment, offering a truly unforgettable experience. Nestled in a lively al fresco courtyard, this nightly event combines the bold flavors of South Asian and contemporary Japanese cuisine, creating a unique culinary journey. Adding to the allure, Asian Night Bazaar features mesmerizing live performances by aerialists and contortionists, captivating the audience with their artistry and grace. The atmosphere is elevated by the energetic beats of our resident DJ, ensuring every evening is a dynamic fusion of taste, sights, and sounds.
