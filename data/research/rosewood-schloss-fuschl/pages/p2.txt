### Farm-fresh Fare

Fresh, local flavors from our fisherman, Gerhard, crafted by Chef Julian.

-

**BEEKEEPER PAUL**

Our own bee colony produces exquisite honey, served at breakfast and available in our boutique—nature’s purest sweetness, crafted with care from hive to table.

-

**FISHERMAN GERHARD**

Our fisherman Gerhard has worked the waters of Lake Fuschl since the 1980s, providing delicately smoked, lake-to-table fish—fresh, authentic, and rich in tradition.

-

**WALTER GRÜLL KAVIAR**

Walter Grüll has crafted fine Austrian caviar since 1981, including rare albino sturgeon roe—sustainably sourced and renowned for its purity, elegance, and subtle depth.
