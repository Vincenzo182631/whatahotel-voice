# Experiences Endless Adventure

Experience the Pacific Northwest at its finest. Fish for Chinook amidst the swirling channels and rapids of the Discovery Islands. Hike through ancient, old-growth rainforests and watch Grizzly bears in their natural habitat. Or take to the alpine and appreciate the views of the fjords and surrounding mountains. From gentle walks to thrilling helicopter rides, there are adventures for everyone looking to discover the best of British Columbia.

We proudly support our local community and indigenous partners, curating the best knowledge and expertise for our guests.
