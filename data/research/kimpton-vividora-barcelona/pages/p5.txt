# Kimpton Vividora HotelAmenities

## The highlights

1. We take care of your pet

2. Enjoy our Fitness Room

3. Discover our Wellness Room

### We take care of your pet

At Kimpton Vividora, pets are truly welcome. Enjoy a special in-room welcome kit with a cozy bed, toys, and tasty treats—so your furry companion feels just as comfortable and pampered as you do from the moment you arrive.

### Enjoy our Fitness Room

Discover the Kimpton Vividora Fitness Room, a modern gym fully equipped with Technogym. Designed for performance and comfort, it features state-of-the-art cardio and strength equipment, perfect for energizing workouts and staying on track with your training while in the city.

### Discover our Wellness Room

Discover the Kimpton Vividora Wellness Room, curated by Signature Spa. A serene urban retreat featuring organic products and indulgent luxury treatments, designed to restore balance, elevate the senses, and deliver a refined, holistic wellness experience in the heart of Barcelona.
