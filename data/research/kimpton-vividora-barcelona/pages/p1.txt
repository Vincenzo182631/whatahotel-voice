Tucked away on a pedestrian street in Barcelona’s city center, our lifestyle design hotel is conveniently 20 minutes away from El Prat airport and 10 minutes from both the cruise port and Sants train station. Enjoy sightseeing plans to Sagrada Familia and La Boqueria, but also experience authentic Barcelona by walking the streets of the city.

Kimpton Vividora, the first Kimpton in Spain, is located in the Gothic Quarter and offers a relaxing escape as well as a vibrant meeting point for locals and travelers. The hotel sits right next to Las Ramblas and is within walking distance of the Barceloneta beach. An ideal location with other nearby attractions that include the city’s shopping epicenter and the Gothic Cathedral.

The accommodations accentuate the Mediterranean and contemporary design combined with luxurious comforts, superlative service and standout amenities. Join us at our evening social hour, discover the city with our complimentary bikes and longboards and taste local gastronomy at our Fauna restaurant, Got Coffee & Cocktails or Terraza de Vivi. Don't leave your furry friend at home, we're a pet-friendly hotel.

### We take care of your pet

At Kimpton Vividora, pets are truly welcome. Enjoy a special in-room welcome kit with a cozy bed, toys, and tasty treats—so your furry companion feels just as comfortable and pampered as you do from the moment you arrive.

### Social Hour everyday

Join our Social Hour every day on the ground floor. Enjoy complimentary drinks in a relaxed, vibrant setting—an effortless way to unwind, connect, and experience Kimpton Vividora’s social spirit at the heart of the hotel.

### The best spot for your wedding

The best spot for your wedding is Terraza de Vivi. An intimate rooftop setting with stunning city views, stylish design, and a vibrant atmosphere—perfect for unforgettable celebrations, sunset ceremonies, and elegant receptions in the heart of Barcelona.

### Arrive in style with our Transfer Service

Arrive in style with our premium Transfer Service. Enjoy seamless, comfortable transportation designed for effortless travel, whether from the airport or around the city, ensuring a refined, stress-free arrival that sets the tone for your stay at Kimpton Vividora.

### Enjoy our Fitness Room

Discover the Kimpton Vividora Fitness Room, a modern gym fully equipped with Technogym. Designed for performance and comfort, it features state-of-the-art cardio and strength equipment, perfect for energizing workouts and staying on track with your training while in the city.

## FAQs for Vividora Hotel

Does Kimpton Vividora Hotel have a pool?

Kimpton Vividora Hotel has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Adjacent to Terraza de Vivi, our terrace and bar, you can opt for a refreshing dip or simply unwind poolside with a cocktail in-hand while marveling at sweeping views of the city from your cushy, shaded chaise lounge.

Pool information

How much is parking at Kimpton Vividora Hotel?

Parking is available for hotel guests at Saba Car Park on Plaza de Catalunya with an additional cost. Please contact reception for more information about the prices.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Vividora Hotel?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Vividora Hotel.

Pet policy description. We're pet lovers! No matter their size, weight or breed and with no extra charge. If your pet fits in the lift, they'll check in right before you. Let us know in advance, we'll make sure the room is set to impress.

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Vividora Hotel for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Vividora Hotel have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Vividora Hotel has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Vividora Hotel?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Vividora Hotel.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Vividora Hotel?

Check-in at Kimpton Vividora Hotel is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Vividora Hotel offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Vividora Hotel offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Vividora Hotel from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Vividora Hotel is conveniently located near Barcelona El Prat Josep Tarradellas Airport (BCN), approximately 9.94 miles away (16 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
