## Restaurant + bar highlights

### Rooftop Brunch

Experience the vibrant Vividora style at Terraza de Vivi's brunch. Indulge in a delicious variety of dishes, bottomless mimosas and Bloody Marys, and stunning 360º views of Barcelona. Available daily from 12 to 4 PM.

Learn more

### Private Dining

Our chef Jordi Delfa and his team use local and seasonal ingredients to create Mediterranean dishes with Catalan roots that dazzle both on the palate and on the plate. Paired with natural wines and adapted to any special needs or intolerances, our group menus offer both flavor and authenticity to elevate any event.

Learn more

### Fauna Restaurant

Fauna, the dining room of Casa de Vivi, offers Mediterranean flavours with Catalan roots in a warm and relaxed atmosphere. You can have a good time with your closest friends and chat while tasting traditional cuisine with an innovative touch by chef Jordi Delfa.
