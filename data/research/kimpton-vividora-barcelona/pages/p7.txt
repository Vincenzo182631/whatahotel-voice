### Wonderful Stay at Kimpton Vividora Barcelona!

4 days ago

We had a great stay at Kimpton Vividora Barcelona! The location was perfect for exploring the city, with pretty much everything we wanted to see and do within easy walking distance.

The hotel itself was beautiful. We really liked the room design, and everything felt comfortable and thoughtfully put together. Breakfast was also excellent, with a great selection of food and attentive service each morning.

What really stood out to us, though, was the staff. Everyone was friendly and welcoming, and the front desk team was especially helpful and on top of things. On the morning of our departure, we had to leave early to catch a train before breakfast service started. The staff made sure we had a taxi booked and even prepared breakfast to go so we wouldn't leave hungry. It was such a thoughtful gesture and something we really appreciated.

Overall, we really enjoyed our time here. Between the location, beautiful rooms, great breakfast, and genuinely helpful staff, we'd definitely recommend Kimpton Vividora to anyone visiting Barcelona. We'd happily stay here again!

### Great location, disappointing service

6 days ago

I stayed at Kimpton Vividora in Barcelona for 6 nights, I am a Platinum member with IHG as well and was very disappointed with my stay for multiple reasons, I will start with some of the Pros before I discuss the Cons:

\- Location was great, in the Gothic Quarter, a lot of things around walking distance, quite centrally located
\- We were welcomed with a nice message and a bottle of sparkling wine in our room as my wife and I were celebrating our anniversary
\- The concierge was extremely helpful and friendly with helping us plan on things to do during our stay

Cons:
\- Some of the staff were very rude, I do not like calling staff out but considering I am staying at Kimpton, a brand I have really enjoyed for multiple years, I expect top tier service and what we got was nothing close to that. The doorman was rude, some staff at breakfast were rude, I was planning on extending our stay but after just a couple of these experiences, I did not feel welcome and wanted to leave as soon as I could
\- Fitness center was underwhelming
\- Breakfast was underwhelming
\- Be prepared to hear doors slamming in the morning from other guests or housekeeping
