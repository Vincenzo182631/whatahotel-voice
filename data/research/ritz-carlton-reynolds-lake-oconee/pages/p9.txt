Embrace luxury getaways in Georgia
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with the signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination. Elevate your Georgia stay with our Georgia luxury lake resort.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.

In-Room Amenities
A Club stay ensures that additional amenities are available to you in your room or suite. Along with complimentary shoeshine and pressing of two garments delivered directly to your door, you will now enjoy an expanded selection of premium in room amenities upon request, including a high end steamer, digital luggage scale, international plug adaptor, Dyson Airwrap Multi Styler, and wellness essentials such as a yoga mat, foam roller, and resistance bands. These additions are designed to help you feel perfectly prepared for an evening out or your next adventure.
