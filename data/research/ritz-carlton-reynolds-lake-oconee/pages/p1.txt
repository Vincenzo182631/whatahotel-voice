Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee
Enjoy a stay at our Lake Oconee luxury resort in Georgia
As close to nature’s perfection as you can get, yet not far from Atlanta, The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee is one of the most luxurious Lake Oconee resorts in Georgia—a place where lake house-like charm, world-class accommodations, and highly personalized service create an unforgettable experience. A stay at our luxury resort in Georgia opens the door to spectacular golf experiences on five championship courses, a relaxing day of fishing or boating on Lake Oconee, a world-class spa, or dining lakeside as the stars fill the sky. It is where multiple generations of families gather to make vacation memories. Where Atlanta city-dwellers trade city noise for nature’s tranquil melodies. Where golfers discover 90 reasons to pack their lucky golf shoes. And where couples realize that wedding dreams really do come true. Our luxury resort near Atlanta, Georgia, is a place where memories are made that will stay with you forever.
Cashless Resort
The resort is exclusively cashless; guests must present a credit card at check-in and may charge purchases from the spa, restaurants, and bars to their room throughout their stay. Major credit cards are accepted.

Reserve a Fireside Guestroom for the ultimate seasonal comfort, complete with views of golden foliage and your own private fire pit. With curated fall activities, lakeside dining, and serene spa treatments, discover why autumn is the coziest season at the lake.

Cashless Resort
The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee is an exclusively cashless resort. Guests must present a credit card at check-in and may charge purchases from the spa, restaurants, and bars to their room throughout their stay. Major credit cards are accepted. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 175.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee. The daily valet fee is $40.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee is Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL). ATL is located approximately 75.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
