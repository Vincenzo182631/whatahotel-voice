Experience Luxury at Our Hotel Near Lake Oconee
Experiences
Just outside of Atlanta, Georgia, the town of Greensboro tells of southern history and tradition, its downtown filled with antebellum homes, churches and antiques stores. 20 miles away, our luxury lakefront resort in Georgia near Lake Oconee offers yet another side of the story; a tranquil retreat surrounded by natural beauty. A true vacation destination, this Georgia resort encourages guests to disconnect from the outside world and reconnect with family, friends and themselves. Five championship golf courses offer 90 holes of play. In the summer, the lake hosts kayaking, standup paddleboarding and other watersports, while winter features ice skating and fireside s’mores.
Immersions by The Ritz-Carlton
Elevate your stay with curated immersions that showcase the beauty of Lake Oconee. Master the art of mixology in a hands-on class, delight in a tranquil sunset cruise across shimmering waters, and embark on a lake-to-table fishing adventure where your fresh catch becomes a culinary masterpiece. Every moment is designed to inspire connection and discovery.

Pools and Beach
Infinity Pool
Your retreat awaits, featuring endless views of Lake Oconee. From high-top bar tables to lounge chairs outfitted with plush terry covers, every detail is designed to create a poolside sanctuary. Available to guests ages 12 and older from May 21 through Labor Day Weekend. Open to all guests for the remainder of the year.
Family Pool
Revel in the stunning lake views, experience the utter joy of childhood as you splash in the pool, and marvel at the memories being made. With interactive features like geysers and splash pads, the Family Pool is so much more than just a pool, it is a destination for endless fun. Open seasonally from April 1 to October 15 each year.
Indoor Spa Pool
Discover soothing serenity at the Indoor Spa Pool, the perfect place to swim laps or simply relax next to the poolside fireplace after receiving our signature Stress Relief Massage. No matter what your idea of a dreamy day by the pool entails, bring it to life at our family resort in Georgia.
Splash Shack
Don’t let hunger slow down your fun in the sun. Featuring family favorites like nachos, wraps, and salads, plus frozen cocktails, wine, and beer on tap, the Splash Shack is the perfect place to refuel when swimming at the Family Pool.
The Beach at Lake Oconee
Nothing says summer fun is quite like a day at the beach. Discover endless opportunities for adventure and relaxation at our private beach, where lounge chairs and umbrellas await. Enjoy food and beverage service from The Sand Bar (open seasonally), plus towels, sunscreen, and just about everything else you might need from The Beach Hut (open seasonally).
Sand Bar
Between dips in the lake and sun-tanning on the beach, refresh with local beers, frozen cocktails and grab-and-go snacks. Open seasonally.
Pools & Beach
Poolside Cabanas
Turn your day into a VIP experience at our resort in Georgia with an exclusive Poolside Cabana at the Infinity Pool or Family Pool, unlocking access to premium seating and amenities. Upgrade your experience with a variety of package options. Infinity Pool Cabanas are available to guests ages 12 and older from May 21 through Labor Day Weekend. Open to all guests for the remainder of the year. Family Pool Cabanas are available for all ages seasonally from April 1 to October 15 every year. Reservations are required.

Recreational Activities
Water Sports
Young Harris Water Sports Boat Rentals
Meet us at the South Docks for some family fun and adventure on Lake Oconee. Young Harris Water Sports is proud to offer a wide variety of motorized watercraft and water sport equipment including Jet Skis, Premium Tritoons, Ski Boats, Wake Boats and a Double-Decker Tritoon with a slide. In addition to facilitating all action sports on the water, Young Harris Water Sports also offers professionally guided Fishing Excursions.
Take in the beauty of the lake by reserving a seat on a Sunrise or Sunset Tritoon Cruise or join an instructor on a state-of-the-art Wake Surf Boat to learn the newest activity in watersports: Wake Surfing.
Whether you are looking for a relaxing tritoon ride or some adventure tubing, Young Harris Water Sports will be happy to assist with all your needs. Advanced reservations recommended, walk-ups avavlible daily based on watercraft availability and captained services available for an additional fee. Visit
lakeoconeeboatrentals.com
for additional information and to secure your reservation.
Non-motorized Lake Activities
Daily, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
Reservations are not required, included in Resort Fee
Join us daily for non-motorized lake activities that are sure to keep you entertained for hours at our resort. Our stand up paddle boards are sturdy and fun to maneuver around Lake Oconee. They are also a great way to strengthen your core muscles while on vacation. Jump in a single or tandem kayak and paddle leisurely around Lake Oconee - you just may see Bald Eagles, Great Blue Herons or Striped Bass jumping out of the water.
Land Experiences
S’mores By The Lake
Gather under the stars for a complimentary s’mores celebration each evening. Enjoy the glow of the campfire and indulge in our favorite tradition in The Backyard. The campfire is lit nightly, outside of Amore del Lago restaurant, weather permitting. Complimentary for registered hotel guests.
Hiking or Running Trails
Featuring the beautiful scenery of Georgia's Lake Country, trails around the resort are fully paved with moderate to extreme elevation changes. Explore miles of trails at the lakeside resort by picking up a running map from our concierges to plan out the perfect distance for tomorrow’s run.
Bicycle Rentals
Explore the area with the family on adult mountain bikes and children's bikes, with or without training wheels. Cruise around the lakeside resort on eight miles of trails, offering a variety of hills and curves for all levels. Complimentary bike rentals within the Resort Fee.
E-Bike Guided Tours & Rentals
Discover the scenic beauty of Lake Oconee along the surrounding trails on premium pedal-assisted e-bikes. Join us for a guided tour or rent an e-bike to explore at your leisure. Whether you are exploring lakeside paths or winding forest trails, our e-bikes offer a smooth and scenic way to experience the resort .
The Photography Concierge
Let our dedicated team of on-site professionals help capture the perfect picture with a breathtaking backdrop for your family portrait or special occasion. All guests (excluding corporate groups) receive a complimentary photo session and a $50 print credit during their stay. For additional information, stop by The Photography Concierge desk located on the Lobby level next to the Front Desk or call 239.449.6125.
Fishing
Fishing
Daily, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
Reservations are not required, included in Resort Fee
Cast your line and unwind. Perfect for lazy summer days on your lake vacation, fishing from the bank, dock, or The Pointe is the ideal way to relax. Catch bream, sunfish, catfish, and bass with our convenient fishing rentals, including a pole, tackle, and bait.
Guided Fishing Services
Reserve your fishing adventure and enjoy a day on the waters of Lake Oconee with our expert guides. Fishing Excursions for 1 to 4 guests include 4-hour professional guide services, boat rental; possibility of catching Striper/Hybrid Bass, Large Mouth Bass, Crappie or Catfish (depending on season and lake conditions). For reservations or other information, please contact Resort Concierge at 706.467.7130.
Tennis
Tennis
Spend an energetic day playing tennis at the Reynolds Lake Club Tennis Center. There are two stadium-size and eight championship-size, lighted and tournament-ready courts. Eight of the courts are Har-Tru Hydro Courts and two are traditional hard courts. Private instruction and a state-of-the-art Playmate Ball machine are available when you need a partner. The Lake Club Tennis Center offers complimentary demo racquets and a full-service pro shop for all your tennis needs.
Reservations are required.
Contact the Resort Concierge for hours, pricing and reservations at 706.467.7130 or Tennis Shop at 706.467.1607.

Ritz Kids Clubhouse
Ritz Kids Clubhouse
Home to a truly unique aquarium, an interactive play floor, and wall-to-wall views of Lake Oconee, the Clubhouse is designed to surprise and delight our youngest guests. Kids of nearly every age can enjoy a virtual play experience that ignites imaginations. Exciting nature programs featuring animal encounters, scavenger hunts, and nature hikes. Daily rotating Ritz Kids Lake Adventure programs that foster a love and appreciation for the region’s natural beauty. An array of arcade games and seasonal activities inspired by the great outdoors.
Ritz Kids Lake Adventures
Imagination and curiosity flourish when participating in a full or half-day Ritz Kids Lake Adventures. Designed for kids ages five to 12, Ritz Kids Lake Adventures introduces campers to the outdoors through tent-building and fishing techniques; teaches them about local wildlife and nature and encourages friendly competition through sports. Each day of the week features a different themed experience.
Full-Day Adventure: 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., including lunch
Half-Day Adventure: 9 a.m. to 12 p.m. or 1 to 4 p.m.
For additional information and assistance making reservations, please contact our Concierge at 706.467.7130. Reservations are required for Ritz Kids Lake Adventures at least 48 hours in advance. The program is limited to participants aged five to 12 years.
Children's Services
In addition to our Ritz Kids program, we offer junior guests and families a variety of special services and amenities.
VIK Check-in
Located adjacent to the front desk, your vacation begins at our Very Important Kids registration desk, where children can sign the guest book and receive a fun coloring book.
Linger Longer Express
All aboard The Linger Longer Express. Explore scenic areas of the resort with a complimentary ten-minute ride in the one-of-a-kind, eco-efficient locomotive departing at 11 a.m. and 3 p.m. from the Front Drive.
In-Room Camping
Bring the feel of our backyard inside and let your little ones experience an exploration unlike any other with a night of In-Room Camping. This experience includes one premium tent per room, complete with a plush sleeping bag and standard pillow, battery-operated flashlight, children’s book, stuffed animal and Ritz-Carlton tent delivery and set up.
This experience has limited availability and reservations are required. Please call 706.467.7130 for assistance securing your reservation.
Children's Dining
We offer special menus for guests ages 2 to 12 in all of our restaurants; children under 2 dine free. We are also happy to puree foods for babies to your specifications. If you have any concerns regarding food allergies, please alert your server prior to ordering.
