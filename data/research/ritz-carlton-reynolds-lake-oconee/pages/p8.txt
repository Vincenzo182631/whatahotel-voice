Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee
Warm Wishes and Winter Wonders
From delicious treats and train rides to family dinners to celebrate the season, The Ritz-Carlton Reynolds, Lake Oconee provides guests with a variety of festive experiences to create memories that last a lifetime. Surprises are in store for both children and adults as the resort decks the halls, ensuring each moment is merry and bright.
Lighting at the Lake
Kick off the holiday season with the resort’s annual Lighting at the Lake with an evening of live entertainment, sweet treats, and holiday fun! Listen to live musical entertainment and sing along to holiday classics as we await Santa’s surprise arrival. Once the big man in red arrives, he will cue hundreds of thousands of lights to illuminate the resort. Enjoy post-lighting fun by taking photos with Santa and experiencing The Winter Forest, complete with a 5,000-square-foot ice rink. Arrive early and explore the Mistletoe Market, featuring local vendors, from 4 to 8 p.m.

Autumn Awaits
Spooky Sweet Treats
Step into a world of sweetness and creativity at The Ritz Kids Clubhouse, where our talented sugar wizards will guide you through the magical art of candy crafting. Construct delightful creations using a variety of tasty treats and techniques. It is a hands-on experience that blends fun, flavor, and festive flair. Reservations required.
Reserve Now
Pumpkin Carving
Kick off your spooky afternoon in the No Wake Zone with a festive Pumpkin Carving session. Choose from spooky, silly, or sweet designs and carve your own pumpkin masterpiece to take home. Reservations required.
Learn More
Fall Festive S'mores
Every evening from October 16 to 31, from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m., gather around the glowing campfire in The Backyard for a cozy autumn tradition. Enjoy roasting marshmallows and crafting your perfect s’more with a variety of seasonal flavors. Whether you are with family, friends, or simply soaking in the crisp fall air, it is the perfect way to end your day with sweet treats and warm memories.

Holiday Festivities
Thanksgiving Brunch
Celebrate Thanksgiving with family and friends in The Ritz-Carlton Ballroom. Enjoy Chef’s Citrus-Brined & Fennel Seed-Rubbed Heritage Turkey, Bourbon-Peach Glazed Ham, Holiday Roasted Vegetables, an Omelet Station, Chilled Seafood Display, Harvest Salads, Local Cheese & Charcuterie, and decadent desserts like Cranberry-Pear Frangipane Tartlets and Apple Pie Pithiviers. Reservations required.
Reserve Now
Fall Festival
Celebrate autumn at the Fall Festival with a festive food menu, live music, hay bale obstacle course, axe throwing, cider, fall crafts, baby pumpkin painting, a petting zoo, and sweet caramel apples. Enjoy cookie decorating and Build-A-Buddy plush creations, all included with your purchase. This festive gathering is the perfect way to welcome fall in a luxurious lakeside setting. Reservations required.
Explore More
Breakfast with Santa
Children of all ages will delight in our Breakfast with Santa, where your child's wish list can be shared with him personally. Young guests will enjoy a breakfast buffet featuring Fruit Smoothies, Fluffy Pancakes and Brioche French Toast with an array of toppings, Made-to-Order Omelets, Applewood Smoked Bacon, and other elf-approved breakfast items. Reservations required.
Reserve Now
Elf Special Delivery
Create a touch of holiday magic with a special surprise delivered by Santa's elves. While away, return to find a festive gift waiting in the guestroom. Children will receive a book from Santa, holiday trinkets, and an assortment of sweet treats. Reservations required.
Reserve Now
Elf Tuck-Ins
Receive a special visit from Santa’s Elves and hear a bedtime story from them as they deliver a personalized bag filled with hand-selected items from Santa. Children will receive a Santa-autographed book, holiday trinkets, and an assortment of cookies and milk to complete the magical evening. Reservations required.
Reserve Now
Gingerbread Workshop
Get in the holiday spirit with Mrs. Claus and her merry elves while building a personalized Gingerbread House. Each creation begins with a pre-built structure and includes a festive assortment of candy and icing, plus a box for easy transport home. Indulge in cookies, hot chocolate, and apple cider while creating delightful memories. Reservations required.
Reserve Now
Christmastime Dinner
Enjoy the Christmas holiday gathered around the table with friends and family at Linger Longer Steakhouse, Amore del Lago, or Gaby’s by the Lake. Special holiday menu items will be available at each restaurant. Reservations are required for Linger Longer Steakhouse and Amore del Lago. Gaby’s by the Lake is available for walk-ins only.
Discover More
Annual New Year's Day Polar Plunge
Kick off 2027 with a splash at our annual Polar Plunge. On the morning of January 1st, brave the crisp waters of Lake Oconee, whether to ease in with a toe dip or dive off the dock in full celebration. After the plunge, warm up with steaming coffee and decadent hot chocolate. Dress in festive, funny, or themed costumes for a chance to win exciting prizes.

The Winter Forest
Step into The Winter Forest, where adventures and memories await. Guests can glide across the 5,000 square-foot ice rink, holding hands and laughing as they take in views of Lake Oconee. Find respite in the Ice Lounge with plush sofas, creative drinks, and twinkle lights. We will be serving up warm classics such as hot cocoa, apple cider, and hot toddies. Creative cocktails will also make an appearance, adding a festive twist to your evening. Don’t forget to snap and share those magical #RCMemories.
