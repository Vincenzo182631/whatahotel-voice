## Level Up Your Stay

Enrich your San Francisco experience in the best downtown hotel suites at The Westin St. Francis on Union Square. Each spacious suite offers thoughtful design, engaging city views and all the essentials for a comfortable stay.

## Amenities to Match your Expectations

Each luxury suite is thoughtfully designed with making the most of your stay in San Francisco, including breathtaking views, relaxing and restorative slumbers on the Westin Heavenly Bed, wine from Chateau Montelena customized to your preference, private check-in and check-out, and a personal driver or car rental upon request.

### Complimentary Amenities & Services

For all Luxury Suites, the below complimentary amenities and services are included:\*

- Pre-arrival Concierge experience
- Luxury Car Rental or Driver on demand\*\*
- 24-Hour on-site contact
- Private Check-In and Check-Out
- Bottle of Wine from Chateau Montelena upon arrival
- Custom mini-bar selection from in-house menu
- Westin Club Lounge Access

\*Discounts don’t apply

\\*\\* Up to three hours of Driver runs per day. Up to 300 miles per day included

## Windsor Suite

Enter your grand marble foyer to an airy living space revealing panoramic city and bay views, including Union Square, SOMA and the Bay Bridge. This 1,900-square-foot suite is suited for royalty with Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II and His Royal Highness Prince Philip having resided here on their historic visit to San Francisco. Lush furniture rich in jewel tone upholstery add a distinct touch to this suite with abundant seating, a warm wood dining table that seats eight, and a large HDTV and sound system. A marble bar area and separate half bath make hosting easy. The main bedroom is entirely separate from the living area, and has a king bed, a second flat-panel HDTV with its own living room, a large walk-in closet, and a large bathroom with separate walk in shower and marble bathtub. An optional connecting king guestroom adds another bedroom and bathroom to this spacious suite.
