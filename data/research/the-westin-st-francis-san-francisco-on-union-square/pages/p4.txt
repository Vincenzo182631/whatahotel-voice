About Chateau Montelena

Established in 1882 by California entrepreneur Alfred Tubbs, the modern-day Chateau Montelena Winery has been owned and operated by the Barrett family since 1972. In 1976, Chateau Montelena put California at the forefront of the wine world when a who’s-who of the French wine and food establishment rated the 1973 Chateau Montelena Chardonnay above all other wines in a blind tasting, convinced that this top-ranking white wine was one of their own. These results proved that Chateau Montelena could produce some of the world’s finest wines, and that California’s wine industry had come of age. Decades later, Chateau Montelena is still committed to producing world-class wines, with the Barrett family still at the helm.

## Mark Quitney, Executive Chef

Chef Quitney, known as “Chef Q,” was raised in Petaluma, California, where his love of cooking began at a young age. After training at the Culinary Institute of America in Hyde Park, he launched a 30-year career leading culinary teams in luxury hotels, resorts, and community kitchens across the United States and the Caribbean. Along the way, his talent and leadership earned him national recognition, including multiple culinary medals at the Louisiana Seafood Cookoff and Wine & Food Festival as well as the title of Best Chef of Louisiana from the American Culinary Federation. He was also honored as Marriott’s Executive Chef of the Year. Now returning to the Bay Area, he brings his creativity, global perspective, and passion for hospitality to The Westin St.

### Jean - Francois Houdre, Executive Pastry Chef

Born in the Southwest region of France into a family business of Boulanger-Pâtissier, Jean François was inspired to become a Pâtissier by his father. At the age of 21, Jean François began working in Southern California for the Vie de France Corporation. ## Erwing Gramajo II, Executive Sous Chef

Erwing Gramajo II has spent nearly two decades honing his culinary skills in luxury hotels, with experience spanning banquets, fine dining and large-scale kitchen operations. He launched his career at the Renaissance Hotel in Hollywood as the Kitchen Supervisor and then went on to become a cook at Gordon Ramsay’s highly acclaimed London Hotel in West Hollywood. Over the years, Chef Gramajo has advanced through leadership positions at several Marriott properties, including Manhattan Beach Marriott, Boston Copley Marriott and JW Marriott Union Square. Most recently, he served as an Executive Sous Chef and Operations Support specialist for Marriott International, providing guidance to properties including The Ritz-Carlton, St. Louis, JW Marriott Anaheim, The Ritz-Carlton, Maui, Sheraton Grand Seattle and Gaylord National. With Chefs Quitney and Gramajo at the helm, The Westin St. Francis continues to elevate its culinary offerings in its restaurants in downtown San Francisco​ while honoring its rich history as a San Francisco landmark.
