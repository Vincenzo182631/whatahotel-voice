RunWESTIN® at The Westin St. Francis. Stay fit and take in the sights with our signature RunWESTIN® program, designed for guests who want to keep up their wellness routine while exploring the city. ## Cardio Room

Our cardio room is equipped with Life Fitness treadmills, bicycles, and motion trainers. Individual viewing screens and digital music are offered on each piece of cardio equipment. Our weight floor is equipped with dual-pulley machines, free-weight equipment, and 5- to 50-pound dumbbells.

### Area & Activities

Experience the rich, colorful allure of the city at our hotel near Yerba Buena Gardens in San Francisco​. Nestled in the heart of the action on Union Square, The Westin St. Francis San Francisco has long served as the hub of social, theatrical and business life since its opening in 1904. Escape to a vibrant destination that is modern with a “proud past”. This is a celebrated oasis of living history...See More and legend, where, just beyond our front door, cable cars pull to a convenient stop – waiting to take you to the nearby Theatre District, upscale shopping, dozens of superb restaurants, world-class galleries and the famed San Francisco Museum of Modern Art. From Fisherman's Wharf to Nob Hill, our hotel offers easy access to a wealth of local treasures.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin St. Francis San Francisco on Union Square have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin St. Francis San Francisco on Union Square offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bikes, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin St. Francis San Francisco on Union Square?

Museum of Modern Art, Aquarium of the Bay, California Academy of Sciences, Yerba Buena Gardens, Lombard Street and more are nearby The Westin St. Francis San Francisco on Union Square.
