# Relax in our Hotel Suites in San Francisco

## Accommodations

Enjoy our accommodations in downtown San Francisco when you stay with us at the timeless yet modern, The Westin St. Francis San Francisco on Union Square. Select from the charming and classic rooms in the Landmark Building and the residential suites in our Tower Building. All of our hotel suites in San Francisco feature the Westin Heavenly® Bed for a serene night’s sleep in downtown San Francisco. Additional signature amenities include 250-thread-count sheets and duvets, an Enseo SMART TV system with personal streaming capabilities, a refrigerator, and private-label Westin-brand bath and beauty products to enhance your visit. Stay side-by-side with family and friends near Union Square and reserve your connecting hotel rooms for a $50 daily fee. For more information, please call us or email SFOUWReservationsTeam@marriott.com.

## Empower Your Well-Being

Recharge in our elegant hotel rooms in San Francisco​ near Union Square, many featuring chandeliers, soaring ceilings and decorative moldings that reflect the landmark building. All rooms offer Heavenly® Beds, marble bathrooms, Enseo SMART TV systems, ample desks and Wi-Fi. Upgrade to a suite to enjoy a large living area perfect for impromptu business meetings or entertaining. Luxury Suites

### Bayview

Dynamic views of Union Square, East Bay and South Bay are yours from this 800-square-foot corner suite of the Tower Building’s 31st floor.

### Bridgeview

San Francisco’s cosmopolitan culture is the focus of this two-bedroom luxury suite adorned with custom furnishings. Its premium corner location offers spacious living and dining areas with large windows on either side, showcasing signature 31st floor views of Alcatraz, the Golden Gate Bridge, the North Bay, and East Bay.

### Windsor

This 1,900-square-foot suite is suited for royalty with lush furniture, a large living, dining, and marble bar area, and panoramic city and bay views.

### Pacific

Maximize your time and your place in San Francisco in this modern one-bedroom floor plan with large, north-facing bay windows with engaging views of Alcatraz, the Golden Gate Bridge, and the North Bay.

### Union Square

Engage with the city in this, our most popular hospitality suite overlooking Union Square, the Financial District, Bay Bridge, and the San Francisco Bay.

### Golden Gate

Inspired entertainment happens in this spacious suite on the 31st floor with exhilarating views of Twin Peaks and of course the famed Golden Gate Bridge.

Specialty Suites

## Chairman's Suite

This sumptuous & stylish suite is a gem of a hospitality suite. Located in our Landmark Building, it has 750 sq. ft. & 2 levels of space. The first level features carved wood paneling & leaded glass windows that open onto Union Square, as well as a living room, dining room, half bath and kitchen area with a full refrigerator. The second level has 3 bedrooms (third bedroom at an additional fee).

## State Suite

This open 750-square-foot suite is elegant and sophisticated, and a popular suite with a wonderful view of Union Square and the San Francisco skyline. (2nd bedroom at an additional fee)

## MacArthur Suite

This hotel suite in San Francisco​ near Union Square features two bedrooms and a spacious parlor that's perfect for entertaining. The living area has a large dining area with a full dining room table.
