## Reconnect with nature at our sustainable hotel in Khao Lak

The JW Garden is a signature element of the JW Marriott experience and our sustainable hotel in Khao Lak, Thailand. This rooted approach to enlivening one’s spirit enables you to appreciate sustainability and feel rejuvenated by all that we harvest. Our sustainable tourism hotel in Khao Lak elevates this concept into a full farm model, an anchor to our commitment to a sustainable future, alongside a vast wilderness park that transports you to the very heart of nature.

### Curated Experiences Found Nowhere Else in Thailand

Discover an array of enriching and meaningful experiences during your visit to the JW Garden at our sustainable hotel in Khao Lak. Our daily tours showcase the three key pillars of the JW Garden: Sustainable Organic Produce, Education and Conservation, and Holistic Wellbeing, whilst our classes, workshops and excursions will transport you into the heart of nature.

### Explore Thai Culinary Heritage and Zero-waste Practices

Discover Thailand's rich culinary heritage and explore sustainable practices through a hands-on cooking class that doubles up as a cultural immersion session at JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa. Embodying farm-to-table and zero-waste philosophy at every step, this experience highlights the importance of sustainable sourcing, mindful consumption, and healthy eating, while fostering an appreciation for the local ecology among guests.

### Tropical Agriculture And Farmyard Friends

Supplying the resort with fresh organic produce, our working farm invites all guests to discover the wonders of horticulture and learn how we cultivate over 200 varieties of plants, fruits, vegetables, leaves and herbs. Meet a few furry and feathered friends along the way, including our two water buffalo, a herd of goats, and a flock of chickens and ducks supplying a hotel with free-range eggs.

### Environment School and Reforestation Project

Join our environmentalist for an impactful class on mangrove conservation and be introduced to our hero species, the Nipa Palm. Learn of its connection to the local community and the importance in maintaining a vital habitat for local wildlife. Take part in our Reforestation Project and help to nurture the coastal ecosystem and protect our shorelines with this once-in-a-lifetime experience at our sustainable tourism hotel in Khao Lak.

### Wilderness Experience and Nature Discovery Tours

The tranquility of the JW Garden offers the perfect setting to fully re-connect with nature. Freshwater lakes surrounded by tropical marshes and scrubland are home to a plethora of over 100 species of wild birds, reptiles, fish and colorful insects. Our wilderness park offers a truly unmissable opportunity to throw yourself into nature, enjoy total serenity with your loved ones.
