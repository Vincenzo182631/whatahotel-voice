# Welcome to JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa

## Experience a luxury stay at our Khao Lak resort in Thailand

Connect with the natural beauty of Khao Lak, Thailand, at JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa. From the moment you arrive at our Khao Lak beach resort, a sense of presence unfolds amidst sun, sea, sand, tropical greenery, granite mountains and nearby waterfalls. Located in peaceful Phang Nga, our luxury Khao Lak beachfront resort offers a refined retreat where local character meets understated luxury...See More and thoughtful design. Be inspired by the beauty of Thailand with the pristine white sand beach in front of our resort meeting the azure waters of the Andaman Sea. Our lagoon pool invites moments of relaxation and connection, while the JW Garden offers a deeper sense of place, where ingredients are grown with care and thoughtfully woven into culinary and wellness experiences. Indulge your senses across our collection of restaurants and bars or nourish your well-being at Quan Spa. Artful indoor and outdoor venues, set within nature, create a meaningful setting for gatherings, events and weddings in Khao Lak.See Less

Inspired by our founders – J. Enjoy wild encounters at the Shark Nursery

## Bamboo Shark Nursery

Native to the Andaman Sea, Bamboo Sharks are listed as a “Near Threatened” species due to their popularity among collectors and exotic diners, as well as overfishing and the destruction of their natural habitat. Join the Bamboo Shark Nursery & Conservation Center to help breed and nurture bamboo sharks before releasing them back into the wild to restore the balance of the marine ecosystem.

## Experience a World of Flavors at Khao Lak's Culinary Haven

Embark on a journey of flavor where diverse cuisines and thoughtful culinary expressions come together in a setting designed for connection and discovery. From refined dining to relaxed, casual moments, our collection of restaurants and bars caters to every taste and occasion. At Drift Beach Bar & Grill, Mediterranean dishes with a subtle Middle Eastern influence are prepared over fire, complemented by a selection of inspired cocktails crafted to reflect the rhythm of the sea. Olive presents authentic Italian classics, elevated through a curated selection of mono-cultivar olive oils and a refined wine collection. Our Thai and Japanese restaurants bring regional flavors to life through immersive dining experiences shaped by tradition and season. For all-day ease, Sala, the Deli, and Waterfront offer a variety of nourishing options, while Infinity and Aquamarine invite you to unwind with refreshments from within the water. As the day settles, the Beach Bar becomes a natural gathering place to pause, reconnect, and take in the horizon.

Learn More

## Sustainable Luxury at JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa

At JW Marriott Khao Lak, sustainability shapes every stay. Set along Thailand’s Andaman coast, the resort features a 27-acre JW Garden, responsible sourcing, and circular culinary practices. Enjoy farm-to-table dining, hands-on workshops, and immersive experiences that connect you to nature, community, and purpose while supporting the local environment.

## Unrivaled Beachfront Setting

Relax with your loved ones and reconnect with the world on the golden shores of Khuk Khak Beach, overlooking the Andaman Sea. This serene stretch of coast sets the stage for unforgettable tropical adventures. Leave footprints in the soft sand and splash in the shallow sea during the day, then hold hands and watch in wonder as the sun sets, casting a kaleidoscope of colors across the evening sky. The secluded sandy beach leads invitingly to the crystal clear waters, allowing you to reconnect with the irresistible beauty of nature in this serene tropical paradise.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa is Phuket International Airport (HKT). HKT is located approximately 83.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa have an airport shuttle to Phuket International Airport (HKT)?

Yes, JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa has an airport shuttle to Phuket International Airport (HKT).

Does JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
