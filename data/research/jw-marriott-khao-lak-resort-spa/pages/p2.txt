# Explore our luxurious Khao Lak accommodation

## Accommodations

Unwind in the serene luxury of our elegant rooms, suites, and villas, inspired by the principles of contemporary Thai design. Each crafted and fully refreshed space offers a haven of high-end tranquility. Our signature pool access rooms let couples and families alike to slip effortlessly into almost 2.5 kilometers of Southeast Asia's longest lagoon pool. Every pool-view room and luxury suite feature...See More a private balcony that serves as the perfect platform for capturing the stunning vistas of our beautiful beachfront resort in Khao Lak. Our newly opened, luxury beachfront villas in Khao Lak offer the ultimate in luxurious beachfront living with individual pools, Jacuzzis, and spacious accommodations for up to six guests. Step out from your room, suite, or villa onto the golden sands of Khuk Khak beach and into the shimmering waters of the Andaman Sea. All of our five-star accommodations has been designed for guests who desire privacy, luxury, and comfort in an exclusive beachfront setting.See Less

JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa

Signature Pool Access Rooms

### Glide Effortlessly from Your Private Terrace into Our Stunning Lagoon Pool

Experience the luxury of time and convenience when you stay in one of our signature Pool Access rooms, your private gateway to South East Asia’s longest swimming pool. Each Pool Access room features a terrace with direct access to the spectacular pool that meanders lazily through the entire resort, covering a distance of almost 2.5 kilometers. With floor-to-ceiling windows, the Khao Lak hotel rooms...See More are bathed in an abundance of natural light that brings to life the stunning décor and interior design with contemporary Thai-style furnishings and authentic local fabrics. Step gracefully down into the pool and start exploring the winding tropical waterways and large open lagoons where you can discover our two swim-up bars, aqua-playground, and a plethora of hidden, serene spaces. Look out for the JW Boat loaded with fresh, Ice-cold coconuts as it cruises daily around the lagoon, before returning home to watch reflections of the sun setting on the water from your private terrace.See Less

### Luxury Suites

For mindful and discerning guests seeking the luxury of space and comfort, our five-star beach resort offers an enticing selection of suites for couples and families alike. Our exclusive Khao Lak beachfront hotel suites have been inspired by the colors and vibrancy of Southern Thailand, with each one featuring distinctive, high-quality Thai-style furnishings, creating your perfect home away from home. Our Duplex Suites, measuring 85 sqm, boast separate spaces for relaxing and entertaining, with the master bedroom located on the upper floor, complete with a floating interior balcony.

### The Ultimate Khao Lak Beachfront Villa Experience

Our exclusive collection of beachfront Khao Lake villas represent the ultimate luxury experience in Thailand. Just three residences occupy the most enviable beachfront location on the entire property. Each villa enjoys stunning ocean views, private pools and separate living, sleeping and dining quarters. In both the one and two bedroom villas you will discover a level of grandeur, opulence and exclusivity...See More rarely experienced in Khao Lak with each villa featuring the finest contemporary Thai furnishings and curated amenities. Your personal villa butler is on hand around the clock to elevate your stay and help you to create lasting memories. Private dining experiences are always available, offering crafted menus featuring local produce, herbs and spices from our JW Gardens that bring the farm-to-table experience even closer to home. For a truly curated and bespoke Khao Lak experience, our beachfront villas offer the perfect platform for our team to tailor make the holiday of a lifetime.See Less
