# The Ultimate Culinary Destination in Khao Lak

## Dining

Feed your mind and nourish your soul with our award-winning selection of fine dining and casual al fresco Khao Lak beach restaurants where fresh, seasonal ingredients from the JW Garden are picked and integrated into every menu. Embark on a sophisticated culinary journey through some of the world's most exciting cuisines. Our signature restaurant Drift is proud to showcase Mediterranean and Middle...See More Eastern mezze (appetizers) and an eclectic mix of Asian and Western dishes prepared on the Parilla (open-fire grill) or in the charcoal grill oven. Olive's impressive selection of crafted authentic Italian classic dishes and hand-selected wines transport you effortlessly to the heart of Chef Nona's kitchen, whilst our specialty Thai and Japanese restaurants feature a variety of regional flavors and exciting opportunities for experiential dining at this stunning Khao Lak beach resort.See Less

Olive Italian Restaurant

### Elevated Italian Dining on the Shores of the Andaman Sea

Every mouth-watering dish at our Italian restaurant in Khao Lok, Olive, reflects the passion, experience, and commitment of Chef de Cuisine Vincenzo Sorrentino. This elegant restaurant, with its air-conditioned and al fresco seating options, offers an impressive selection of fine cuisine based upon traditional recipes that use authentic ingredients from every region in Italy. Naturally, the restaurant’s...See More wine cellar features old and new world wines that complement perfectly every dish. The open kitchen sets the scene for truly memorable dining experiences with the beachfront deck providing a stunning venue for sunset cocktails and aperitifs. The restaurant also boasts an ever-growing collection of monocultivar olive oil. Each oil is made solely from a single variety of olive harvested during Italy’s autumn season, with every drop bringing an added and very distinctive flavor. Chef Enzo is pleased to host private degustation classes featuring quality Italian ingredients and wines.See Less

## Authentic and Inspiring Thai Cuisine Cooked from the Heart

Inspired by the Lanna heritage of Northern Thailand, the most beautiful and elegant Khao Lak beach restaurant on the Andaman Sea, and home to authentic and exciting regional Thai cuisine, Ta Krai is a must to experience on any visit to Khao Lak. Our talented chefs champion fresh, seasonal produce that can be traced from farm to table, and many from the JW herb garden located at the restaurant itself. Step inside and immediately you are fully immersed in rich Thai culture, from the Khan Tok seating area to the elegant authentic décor, but most importantly the inviting, intense and exciting smells emanating from the kitchen. For the ultimate Thai dining experience, join us at Ta Krai where the organic local ingredients elevate classic dishes to something truly special.

## The Art of Japanese Cuisine Showcased in Khao Lak

Theatrical, elegant, and precise, Sakura embraces the ancient culinary traditions of Japan whilst showcasing the finest ingredients, served simply and perfectly every time. In a playful nod to modern Tokyo and the cherry blossoms that are the restaurant's namesake, diners can experience the excitement of a live teppanyaki grill, watch virtuoso knife smiths turn fish into art at the sushi bar, or dine under the stars on the poolside terrace. Complimenting the refined dishes is an extensive sake menu, ensuring a truly authentic dining experience and one that would not be out of place in the land of the rising sun.

### Our Chocolate Boutique & Atelier

Discover the art of chocolate-making in collaboration with Belgian chocolatier Duc de Praslin. Featuring locally grown Phang Nga cacao, our Chocolate Boutique & Atelier invites guests to explore the journey from cacao cultivation and harvesting to fermentation, roasting, and handcrafted chocolate. Through guided tastings and hands-on chocolate-making workshops, guests can learn to temper chocolate, prepare truffle fillings, and create their own sweet masterpieces. A distinctive culinary experience connecting local growers, Belgian craftsmanship, and the flavors of Phang Nga.
