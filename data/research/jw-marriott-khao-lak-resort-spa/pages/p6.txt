## Experience Good Travel with Marriott Bonvoy

Good Travel with Marriott Bonvoy is a program that offers meaningful travel, allowing you the opportunity to create a positive impact as you explore and build deeper connections in local communities when you stay in our hotels.

With over 100 curated experiences, the program focuses on three pillars – Environmental Protection, Community Engagement, and Marine Conservation.

Discover how you can contribute meaningfully to Marine Conservation with us at JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort & Spa and JW Marriott Khao Lak Resort Suites.

###### Bamboo Shark Nursery: Why It Matters

### Bamboo Sharks Decline Affects the Andaman Sea Ecosystem

Bamboo sharks are one of the native shark species from the Andaman Sea but their dwindling numbers have put them under the International Union for Conservation of Nature’s “Near Threatened” list.

As a predatory fish for the shallow reefs in Phuket and Khao Lak, bamboo sharks help maintain the balance of the food chain and serve as an indicator for the reef’s health. Due to their popularity among collectors and exotic diners, as well as overfishing and the destruction of their natural habitat, they are now classified as potentially endangered.

###### Bamboo Shark Nursery: The Experience

### Release Bamboo Sharks into the Sea

In partnership with Oceans For All Foundation, we invite guests to our Bamboo Shark Nursery & Conservation Center to help preserve the Andaman Sea’s underwater ecosystem.

Guests can participate in the following activities:

- Join a guided tour at an interactive education center for an introduction around the life cycle of bamboo sharks.
- Meet our resident marine biologist and shark expert at the aquarium nursery to learn more about their work and how they nurture sharks at different life stages, from egg incubation to the time of their release.
- Release baby bamboo sharks back into the wild – only six-month old (and above) bamboo sharks that are big enough will be released to ensure a higher chance of survival

###### Bamboo Shark Nursery: The Impact

### Restoring the balance of the Andaman Sea’s Marine Ecosystem

The experience offers guests a hands-on and educational experience to help promote the Andaman Sea’s bamboo shark population while learning about how a balanced ecosystem keeps our oceans healthy.

As sharks are a key predator in the ocean, the drastic decline of their population affects the entire marine ecosystem, which includes endangering coral reefs and other marine species. Breeding and hatching bamboo shark eggs in aquariums and nurseries rather than in the open sea help protect baby bamboo sharks from other predators during their vulnerable age. Once they reach a certain size, the release of juvenile sharks into the wild will have a high chance of survival in order to help balance the food chain again.  With the help of our guests, the Bamboo Shark Nursery & Conservation Center aims to help restore the Bamboo Shark population in the wild and population balance in the Andaman Sea’s marine ecosystem.
