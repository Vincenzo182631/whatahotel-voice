### Amazing Resort!

12 days ago

Amazing Resort, Beautiful Staff and an Unforgettable Stay!

This was our first time visiting Thailand and staying at a resort-style hotel, and what an experience it was!

Check-in was easy, especially given our late arrival from Australia.

We stayed in a pool access room, which was spacious, extremely clean and had everything we needed to completely relax and unwind. The lagoon pool was cleaned daily and was perfect for a quick dip. Swimming hours were 7am–7pm, although a quiet dip outside these hours near your room was fine.

The buffet breakfast was absolutely 5-star, with an incredible selection catering to both Western and non-Western diets. Everything was fresh, including the tiny baguettes that tasted like they’d come straight from a French boulangerie!

The beach, pools and hotel grounds were immaculate and constantly maintained. Being able to relax on a beach chair, dip into the pool and then wander into what felt like a heated ocean was something I’ll never forget. Sunset drinks were a highlight, along with the incredible dance and fire shows (bring some cash to tip the performers!).

It’s also a short walk along the beach to the nearest 7-Eleven and several local restaurants, although the hotel’s food was fantastic and catered to pretty much every craving we had.

But what really made this place special was the staff. Every single person was genuinely kind, welcoming and always smiling. A special mention to Joke from the Pine Bar, whose amazing memory, attention to detail and genuine passion for looking after guests really left an impression on us.

If there was one downside, it was seeing some guests not showing the staff the respect and appreciation they deserve. The Thai people have such a beautiful culture and spirit, and these staff work incredibly hard to make everyone’s holiday special. A little kindness goes a long way!

Overall, an unforgettable stay at a beautiful resort with even more beautiful people. Would absolutely return!
