### Curated Offers & Experiences

Indulge in exceptional moments crafted exclusively for you. Whether you are a guest of the hotel or visiting for the day, discover bespoke experiences designed to delight. From exquisite dining, Incredible Occasions to unique collaborations with distinguished partners, each experience promises something truly memorable.

Learn more

### Princess 78 Yacht

Step aboard W.J. Lip, InterContinental Phuket Resort’s exclusive 78-foot luxury Princess yacht, one of world’s most luxurious yacht builders. Embark on a marine adventure where legendary service meets the pristine beauty of the Andaman sea. Delight in gourmet dining curated by our resort’s chefs, with every dish crafted to perfection.

Learn more

### hom Restaurant

hom is a culinary destination rooted in fermentation, local land, and the stories of Thailand. Led by Chef Marcelo and a world-class team. Recognised as one of The 50 Best Discovery and MICHELIN Guide 2026 recommended venues for fine dining in Phuket.

Learn more

### Jaras Dining

Discover Jaras, a MICHELIN Guide 2025, where sustainable gastronomy meets culinary artistry. Embrace ethical dining practices while savoring bold flavours as you delight in our all-new habitat-conscious & ecologically-pure 9-course tasting menu. Experience Southern Thai cuisine, featuring dishes crafted with WWF® Thailand endorsed invasive species.

Learn more

### Tengoku

Take pleasure in watching the customary preparation of teppanyaki in an open kitchen. Imbibe the skilled teppanyaki chef’s focus on perfection and immerse in the tantalizing aromas and the energy of our cooks whilst they grill and fry your tasting menu choices before you.

Learn more

## FAQs for InterContinental Phuket Resort

Does InterContinental Phuket Resort have a pool?

InterContinental Phuket Resort has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

The five outdoor pools at InterContinental Phuket Resort allow guests to soak up Phuket sunshine and enjoy the cool ocean breeze or mountain fresh air. Five pools, including Beach Pool, Club Pool, Infinity Pool, Lagoon Pool, and Serene Pool.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Phuket Resort?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Phuket Resort for hotel guests.

InterContinental Phuket Resort provides convenient indoor car park areas and outdoor car park areas with EV charging stations.

Parking fee: 0 THB per day

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Phuket Resort?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Phuket Resort.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Phuket Resort for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Phuket Resort have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Phuket Resort has 7 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Phuket Resort?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Phuket Resort.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Phuket Resort?

Check-in at InterContinental Phuket Resort is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Phuket Resort offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Phuket Resort offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Phuket Resort from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Phuket Resort is conveniently located near Phuket International Airport (HKT), approximately 15.53 miles away (25 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Phuket Resort?

InterContinental Phuket Resort is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Phuket Old Town - 15.53 miles away (25 kilometers)
- Promthep Cape - 18.64 miles away (30 kilometers)
- Wat Chalong - 15.53 miles away (25 kilometers)
- Patong - 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)
- Phi Phi Island - 46.6 miles away (75 kilometers)
- Sunday walking street market (Lard Yai) - 14.29 miles away (23 kilometers)
- Rawai Sea Gypsies Fish Village - 19.88 miles away (32 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Phuket Resort puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
