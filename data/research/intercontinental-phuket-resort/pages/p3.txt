# InterContinental Phuket ResortDining

Embark on culinary journeys inspired by the tastes and textures of Thailand and around the world. Enjoy exquisite dishes served in breathtaking settings at our 7 outlets: Jaras, Tengoku, Pinto, Pine Beach Bar, 333 at the Beach, Devas' Lounge, and hom.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

1. hom Restaurant

2. Jaras Dining

3. Tengoku

### hom Restaurant

hom is a culinary destination rooted in fermentation, local land, and the stories of Thailand. Led by Chef Marcelo and a world-class team. Recognised as one of The 50 Best Discovery and MICHELIN Guide 2026 recommended venues for fine dining in Phuket.

Learn more

### Jaras Dining

Discover Jaras, a MICHELIN Guide 2025, where sustainable gastronomy meets culinary artistry. Embrace ethical dining practices while savoring bold flavours as you delight in our all-new habitat-conscious & ecologically-pure 9-course tasting menu. Experience Southern Thai cuisine, featuring dishes crafted with WWF® Thailand endorsed invasive species.

Learn more

### Tengoku

Take pleasure in watching the customary preparation of teppanyaki in an open kitchen. Imbibe the skilled teppanyaki chef’s focus on perfection and immerse in the tantalizing aromas and the energy of our cooks whilst they grill and fry your tasting menu choices before you.

Learn more
