In an elegant blend of rich and earthy tones and contemporary design, all our rooms, suites and villas feature an opulent bathroom, premium Byredo amenities and ample space. Bathe in the natural light from our floor-to-ceiling windows or relax in your spacious balcony or deck, overlooking lush tropical views of our pools, Kamala mountain of beachfront.
