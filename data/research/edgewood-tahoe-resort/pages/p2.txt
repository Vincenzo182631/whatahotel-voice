## Welcome to a New Level of Luxury  _The Villa Suites at Edgewood_

With a front-row seat to Lake Tahoe’s show-stopping splendor, enveloped in the warm opulence that Edgewood Tahoe Resort is known for, the Villa Suites at Edgewood Tahoe represent the next evolution of Lake Tahoe luxury.

Guests will find ample space to host family gatherings, wedding parties or multi-generation memory-making vacations at the Villa Suites — all while experiencing one of the most stunning scenic backdrops that Lake Tahoe has to offer.

Adjacent to the main lodge and fronting the 8th and 9th fairways, the Villa Suites are in the middle of all that Edgewood has to offer, while retaining the seclusion of a private piece of Edgewood. Take virtual tour with our 3D models.
