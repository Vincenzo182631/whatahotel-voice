# The Bistro

Savor a deliciously hand-crafted meal at the Bistro, where every dish is complemented by panoramic views of Edgewood Tahoe’s illustrious lakeside landscape. A feast for all the senses, The Bistro embodies casual yet contemporary dining through its blend of the finest, curated seasonal fare and thoughtfully selected wines or crafted cocktails.

Book Now

### Casual Sophistication _With Lakeside Views_

Designed to emulate the natural beauty of its surroundings, The Bistro exudes warmth and elegance. Every detail, from the use of organic textures and warm tones to its open layout that seamlessly flows into the outdoor patio space, invites guests to take in the breathtaking scenery. Inside, the intricate rope-accented ceiling, rustic stone tiling, open kitchen design, and abundance of natural light offer the perfect balance of refinement and ease to complement each dish.

Book Now

### Crafted Cocktails, _Elevated Moments_

The Bistro Bar captures all the effortless luxury of Edgewood Tahoe within the casual setting of The Bistro. Each cocktail, pour of wine, and bar bite is artfully crafted using only the finest and freshest ingredients, calling for every guest to slow down and savor each moment.

Bistro Bar Menu

### New American _Seasonal Fare_

From the first glimmer of yellow light across the turquoise waters of Lake Tahoe to the deep oranges and purples of the late-afternoon sunset, guests will find The Bistro open and ready to serve its distinct take on new-American cuisine. Offering breakfast, lunch, dinner and dessert, each dish is crafted with fresh, seasonal ingredients to keep you nourished throughout the day.

### A Private Setting _For Every Occasion_

The Bistro serves as the ideal setting for intimate gatherings and private celebrations, offering endless elegance and stunning views. The private dining room offers 442 square feet of open, airy space that accommodates around 30 guests, and features two sets of doors that lead right out into the Bistro patio. Room rental starts at $3,000 plus food and beverage. For smaller gatherings, the exclusive 418 square foot boardroom accommodates 12 guests and features a stunning solid wood boardroom table, a fireplace, a private bathroom, and patio access. Room rental starts at $2,000 plus food and beverage.
