## A place just    beyond compare

Nestled on the shores of Lake Tahoe, North America’s largest alpine lake, and bounded by the majestic peaks of the Sierra, you’ll find Edgewood Tahoe Resort®, a one-of-a-kind boutique resort where luxury reaches new heights every day.

Be it gourmet dining, a soothing day at the spa, legendary golf, world-class skiing, easy access to Tahoe’s fabled outdoor activities, or simply sitting back and basking in the wonder of it all, the experiences here are unequaled and the memories everlasting.

Discover the storied history of the Edgewood property that began 150 years ago on the shores of Lake Tahoe.

Inspired by the rustic elegance seen in the great lodges of the American West, we’re a place where blissful amenities and Nature’s grandest gifts converge.

Three acclaimed restaurants on the shore of Lake Tahoe - The Edge (fine dining & tasting menus), The Bistro (seasonal lakeside cuisine), and Brooks' Bar & Deck (Golf Digest's "Best Golf Pub"). Forbes 4-Star resort. Reserve your table today.

Edgewood Tahoe is Tahoe's only lakefront golf resort, and home to the American Century Celebrity Championship. Here, you're always on course with awe.

Award-winning 8,500 sq ft spa at Lake Tahoe. Book massages, facials, couples retreats, and our signature Oxygen Bar - designed for Tahoe's high altitude. Open to lodge guests and spa-day visitors. Call 775-588-3486.

## To cherish  _& protect_

Beyond our mission of being a world-class, one-of-a-kind luxury resort lies something infinitely more

important, our responsibility to safeguard the precious environment that surrounds us.

Every decision we make and action we take must align with our calling to be stewards of the land, guardians

of the Lake, and sincere, difference-making corporate citizens of the communities in which we operate.

And our credo is a simple one:

To continually minimize our environmental footprint and ensure that this pristine area is just as pristine

for those who come after us, as it was for those who came before us.
