## forbes travel guide  _4-Star Rated Lodge_  _and Spa_

Founded by Mobil and Simon & Schuster in 1958, Forbes Travel Guide is the oldest travel
guide in the United States. Forbes ratings are given by anonymous, paid staff members
based on an objective and independent process. With an emphasis on service, Forbes
Travel Guide awards stars for hotels, restaurants, and spas that deliver - above all else - an
exemplary customer experience.

We are proud and humbled to announce that Forbes Travel Guide has chosen to award
both our boutique Spa and mountain-modern Lodge with a prestigious 4-star rating.
Indicative of upscale quality and extraordinary comfort, this accolade reflects our steadfast
dedication to providing our guests with world-class service and a memorable luxury
experience that no other destination offers. We want to show our appreciation to Forbes
for extending this recognition and, most of all, we would like to thank our guests for their
continued support and patronage.

## The Peak Into  _Refined Luxury_

Guests can experience the peak of Edgewood Tahoe's elegance with a stay at the Villa Suites, where the serene beauty of the landscape and unmatched service only add to its splendor. Sat between the main lodge and the 8th and 9th fairways, these expansive two to five bedroom suites provide stunning views of the Sierras as well as the ideal proximity to resort amenities and year-round activities. Each Villa Suite boasts an expansive layout that is perfect for hosting and an exceptional selection of amenities, including a welcome bottle of bubbles, locally inspired snacks, a personal concierge, a sunrise pastry basket, a s’mores kit, and complimentary valet parking.

## A Taste of  _Culinary Excellence_

Dining at Edgewood Tahoe is defined by a team of chefs dedicated to their craft and setting a new standard of flavor. Each restaurant shares a commitment to creating exceptional cuisine while offering its own distinctive atmosphere. Guests can savor artisanal pub fare and creative cocktails in the relaxed atmosphere of Brooks’ Bar & Deck, enjoy a laid-back meal in the effortless elegance of The Bistro, or experience the fusion of seasonal flavors and sophisticated atmosphere at The Edge Restaurant & Lounge. Begin your morning with a perfectly brewed coffee or smoothie from Uplift Café, or indulge in the refined flavors of classic comfort foods served on the go at Fairway Grille.
