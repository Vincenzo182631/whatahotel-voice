# Tea with an Edge

## Afternoon Tea, Reimagined

Expect thoughtfully curated teas and alpine-inspired infusions paired with a visually driven selection of sweet and savory bites, plus scones each reflecting the spirit and seasonality of Tahoe. This is not your classic tea service. It’s a relaxed, experience-forward gathering meant to be shared, photographed, and remembered.

Included in your experience is Edgewood Tahoe’s exclusive tea blend, Essence of The Edge, presented in a commemorative tea tin.

Reimagined for the shores of Lake Tahoe, Tea with an Edge offers a fresh, modern take on traditional afternoon tea, elevated yet inviting, refined without feeling formal.
