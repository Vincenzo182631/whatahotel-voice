# The Edge Restaurant & Lounge

It all comes together here. The lake. The sky. The artistry of a perfect meal in a perfect place.

This is The Edge, where culinary craft is pushed to new heights and the warm embrace of mountain hospitality surrounds you.

### A Pinnacle _Experience_

At The Edge, flavors inspired by the surrounding mountain peaks and alpine meadows are transformed with meticulous care. Our adventurous culinary team pushes the boundaries of rustic mountain cuisine, blending time-honored techniques with modern artistry. In the hands of our visionary chefs, prized seasonal ingredients become dishes that captivate both the palate and the imagination.

Guests are invited to explore a menu that harmonizes the seasons with innovative culinary methods—tableside presentations, multi-course tasting experiences, and thoughtfully crafted cocktails. The Edge also features an exceptional wine collection of more than 1,000 labels from around the world, including rare estate vintages and boutique California wineries, all curated by our expert sommeliers.

Designed with a contemporary sensibility, The Edge Restaurant & Lounge immerses guests in a culinary journey that celebrates the spirit of Tahoe. Warm, welcoming, and sophisticated, it offers an atmosphere where families and food enthusiasts alike can savor a story of nature, heritage, and elevated dining. Each visit is an invitation to embark on a curated experience that evolves with the seasons and showcases the extraordinary.

Discover a pinnacle of taste and craftsmanship at Edgewood Tahoe. Join us for your next celebration and experience the newly reimagined Edge.

### Tea With An Edge _Afternoon Tea Experience_

**A Tradition Begins Here**

Reimagined for the shores of Lake Tahoe, Tea with an Edge offers a fresh, modern take on traditional afternoon tea; elevated yet inviting, refined without feeling formal. Priced at $95 All-inclusive per person, the experience blends artful presentation, seasonal flavors, and a touch of the unexpected, all set against Edgewood’s iconic lakefront backdrop.

Guests can savor a curated selection of sweet and savory bites, from smoked salmon with dill crème fraîche and capers, cucumber with lemon cream cheese, and truffle egg salad, to indulgent sweets like cherry pistachio madeleines, lavender blackberry petit fours, and chocolate devil’s food cake. Freshly baked cranberry white chocolate and blueberry scones round out the experience, paired with thoughtfully selected teas and alpine-inspired infusions.

Designed to be as social as it is scenic, this is not your classic tea service; it’s a relaxed, experience-forward gathering meant to be shared, photographed, and remembered. Perfect for celebrations, getaways, or simply an elevated daytime indulgence—afternoon tea, redefined.

### Unwind at _The Edge Lounge_

The Edge Lounge invites you to unwind in an elevated yet effortlessly welcoming atmosphere. A more relaxed dress code sets the tone for polished comfort, while an exclusive lounge menu (featuring select items not offered in the main dining room) creates the perfect place for pre-dinner handcrafted drinks, light fare, or a leisurely nightcap.

Seating is offered on a first-come basis, allowing you to step in whenever the moment feels right.

On Thursday evenings, live music fills the space with warmth, enhancing your time spent against the lake’s serene backdrop. Linger, connect, and savor the unmistakable beauty of an evening at Edgewood Tahoe.

### Dress Code & _Dining Etiquette at The Edge_

**Dress Code & Guest Guidelines – The Edge Restaurant and Lounge**

To preserve the elevated atmosphere of The Edge, we kindly request adherence to the following dress standards:

**Main Dining Room**

Attire should be business casual to semi-formal. Jackets and ties are encouraged but not required. Please refrain from wearing T-shirts, ball caps, athletic shoes or attire, flip-flops, shorts, or distressed clothing of any material. Guests dressed outside of these guidelines may be offered seating in the lounge. Exceptions may be made for children under 12 or for medical reasons.

**Lounge**

While more relaxed than the dining room, the lounge still maintains a refined environment. Dressy casual to cocktail attire is appropriate and recommended. Casual wear, including gym attire, beach or swimwear, and distressed garments, is not permitted. Lounge seating is limited and not guaranteed. Reservations can be made.

For more casual dining, we recommend The Bistro or Brooks’ Bar.

**Additional Information**

Well-behaved children are welcome at The Edge and should be included in your reservation count. Please note that dining at The Edge is a leisurely experience; we appreciate young guests who can remain patient throughout the meal. For the comfort of all patrons, disruptive behavior and the use of electronic devices at the table are not permitted. Due to space constraints, we are unable to accommodate strollers, highchairs, or booster seats.

Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a $50 per person fee.

Thank you for helping us maintain the distinguished setting of The Edge.
