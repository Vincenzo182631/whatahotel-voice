# Celebrate New Year’s

at Edgewood Tahoe

Plan your stay at Edgewood Tahoe around the upcoming New Year to take part in the season’s most unforgettable, grand celebration. The Edgewood Black & White Ball opens the festivities with an unrivaled elegance that carries through the evening, allowing tradition, craft, and celebration to converge.

From a classic countdown at Midnight in the Lodge to a timeless experience at Brooks After Dark, you can easily honor the turn of the year in signature Edgewood Tahoe style.

## Savor The Final Evening  _of The Year_

End the year by savoring unique, seasonal flavors in thoughtfully curated surroundings. Whether you choose to dine in the inviting, festive atmosphere of the Bistro at Midnight, the timeless style of Brooks After Dark, or the intimate culinary artistry of Midnight on the Edge, each dining experience offers a distinctive take on how to honor this once-a-year celebration.

### Celebrate The New Year _With Your Family_

Edgewood Tahoe makes it easy to make this New Year’s Eve a family-friendly celebration, fully equipped with immersive activities and festive treats.

The Lodge Family zone will act as the base for these activities, welcoming families with snacks and swag. Young guests can get creative with magic-themed crafts, try out their singing voices at the neon karaoke party, and witness a special sleight of hand magic put on by Alex Ramon before gathering together for the East Coast countdown at 9:00 pm.

Following the countdown, the celebration will continue with hosted karaoke, glowing swag, and retro arcade rentals, creating a fun atmosphere for the whole family.

Discover The Midnight Magic Celebration

### Celebrate _At The Clubhouse_

As the evening unfolds in bursts of laughter-filled excitement and joy, gather at the Clubhouse for even more entertainment.

Sway along to the upbeat tune of a three-piece band, catch an Alex Ramon sleight of hand magic, and capture the evening in the 360 Photo Booth before welcoming the new year with a lively DJ set and dancing into the night.

Join Us At The Clubhouse

### Culinary Artistry _At The Edge_

The Edge Restaurant and Lounge blends the seasoned artistry of our culinary team with a refined setting that provides the ideal backdrop for unforgettable meals, moments, and memories. Welcome the new year with a 5-course prix fixe menu that has been curated to guide guests through a thoughtful flavor journey.

Dine With Us This NYE

### Toast To Midnight _At The Bistro_

Join us at The Bistro for a festive evening of lighthearted celebration and classic cuisine that everyone can enjoy. Select an offering from each course of the prix fixe menu, beginning with an amuse bouche, followed by three courses, and concluding with a decadent dessert.

Spend Your Evening At The Bistro
