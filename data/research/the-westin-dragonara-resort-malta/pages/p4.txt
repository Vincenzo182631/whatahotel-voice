# Experiences at The Westin Dragonara Resort, Malta

## Experiences

The Maltese Islands' clear blue Mediterranean Sea is ideal for scuba diving and is highly accessible by relatively every area of the Islands. All three Islands offer unique diving experiences with reefs,...See More caves and wrecks to make the dive one of the most interesting ones in the Mediterranean. Divewise, an award-winning dive centre, is located within The Resort and is one of the biggest diving centres on the island. They offer year-round PADI diving courses amongst a number of other services. Enjoy our hotel facilities after a long day of exploring the area, from swimming pools and a fitness center to spa treatments, we cater for the whole family.See Less

## Westin Family Kids Club

Discover a world of wonder designed just for kids! Our Kids Club celebrates how children see and learn from the world through first-hand experiences. Open daily from 9am–5pm for ages 3–12, the Westin Family Kids Club offers engaging activities that expand young horizons and reveal hidden wonders at every turn.

Please note: Parents are not permitted to stay during club hours, allowing children to fully immerse themselves in the experience. Additional charges apply. Children under 3 are not permitted. Let your little ones explore, create, and grow while you enjoy some well-deserved relaxation time!

### Hotel Activity Highlights

### Dragonara Casino

Malta's premier gaming destination, the Dragonara casino is housed in a magnificent 19th-century palace, built by the Marquis Scicluna, with a surrounding colonnaded veranda that abuts directly onto the sea.

### WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio

Stay focused on fitness while traveling. The Westin Dragonara Resort offers a fully equipped, air-conditioned WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio and a heated indoor pool, free for guests. Minimum age is 16 to use the Fitness Studio.

### ​ Divewise Malta

The Divewise Malta is located on the grounds of The Westin Dragonara Resort, which caters to both recreational and technical divers. The diving school is an excellent choice for your diving needs including equipment and organized boat trips.

### The Beauty Clinic

Enjoy our rejuvenating facial treatments and holistic massages - Aromatherapy, Stone therapy, candle massage, body scrub / wrap, and Couple’s massage. A complete indulgence of peace and serenity for the mind and body. In-room spa treatments are also available, when booked 48 hours prior to the desired time.

### Local Area & Activity Highlights

### Explore the Maltese Islands

The Westin Dragonara Resort Hotel is situated in the heart of the Island of Malta, on a private 74,000m² peninsula in St. Julian’s, one of Malta’s most vibrant towns. The Maltese Islands have been described as one big open-air museum where you can discover 7,000 years of history in a relatively short time thanks to the short distances between all the attractions. Delve into the Islands' mysterious prehistory, retrace the footsteps of St Paul or see where the Knights of St John defended Christendom.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Dragonara Resort, Malta have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Dragonara Resort, Malta offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-11:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Indoor cycling bikes; TRX;, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Dragonara Resort, Malta have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Dragonara Resort, Malta has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Indoor Pool (Indoor)Bay View Pool (Heated) (Outdoor)Reef Club Pool (Closed from November to April) (Outdoor)Whirlpool
