# The Westin Dragonara Resort Facilities

## Westin Family Kids Club

At our Kids Club, every day is a chance for young explorers to discover, create, and connect. We believe children experience the world in their own unique way; through curiosity, play, and hands-on adventure. That’s why we’ve created a vibrant, safe, and inspiring space where imagination leads the way.

From creative workshops and interactive games to outdoor fun and new friendships, our carefully designed activities encourage children to learn through experience while having unforgettable moments. Whether they’re uncovering hidden talents, exploring new interests, or simply enjoying time to play, every moment is filled with excitement and discovery.

Our dedicated team ensures each child feels welcomed, engaged, and free to be themselves, giving parents peace of mind and a little time to relax. Because here, every child’s journey is just as important as the destination.

## Creative & Educational Fun

The Westin Kids Club offers a dynamic calendar of activities designed to keep every day fresh, exciting, and full of surprises. Rather than a fixed schedule, our programme evolves regularly to reflect new ideas, seasonal themes, and the interests of our young guests.

Children can enjoy a variety of creative and educational experiences, from arts and crafts sessions that spark imagination to interactive activities that encourage curiosity and learning through play. Each experience is thoughtfully planned to balance fun with development, helping children build confidence, express themselves, and discover new skills.

With something different to look forward to each day, every visit becomes a unique adventure filled with creativity, exploration, and joy.

### Childcare Information

Please note that our Kids Club is designed as a supervised activity space and does not provide babysitting services. However, should you require dedicated childcare, our Concierge team will be happy to assist in arranging trusted, independent babysitting services upon request, ensuring you have complete peace of mind during your stay.
