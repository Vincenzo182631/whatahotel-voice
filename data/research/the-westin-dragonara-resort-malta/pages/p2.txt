# Spacious rooms with views of The Mediterranean Sea

## Accommodations

The Westin Dragonara Resort boasts Malta's largest standard hotel rooms, family suites and its widest choice of room types. Most of our 413 recently renovated suites and hotel rooms in Malta offer spectacular views of the St. Julian's coastline and Mediterranean Sea from at least one private balcony or terrace. Rooms offer every comfort, and the standard amenities listed below are available in all rooms and suites unless otherwise noted. The resort features 66 brand new Luxury Bay Suites ideal for group or family vacations in St. Julian's, Malta. Situated on a private annex, these suites are adjacent to the main building. Some highlights of our Luxury Bay Suites include a fully equipped kitchenette, spacious en-suite with walk-in rain shower and free-standing bathtub, mood lighting, sofa bed, 2 private terraces and a guest bathroom.

### Luxury Bay Suites

Be transported to a world of luxury and style. Inspired by the soft hues of Maltese limestone and turquoise of the Mediterranean Sea, the newly-designed Luxury Bay Suites have been thoughtfully crafted to enrich contemporary lifestyles, blending versatile design with elegant living.

## Presidential Penthouse Suite

Located in an exclusive top floor setting, the One, Two and Three -Bedroom Presidential Penthouse Suites have been designed with a modern residential aesthetic that blends natural beauty and local styling. Textiles and furnishings, including handpicked artworks by renowned local artist Pawl Carbonaro have been thoughtfully sourced to bring harmony to the neutral color palette, providing guests with an enriching backdrop for both business and leisure.
