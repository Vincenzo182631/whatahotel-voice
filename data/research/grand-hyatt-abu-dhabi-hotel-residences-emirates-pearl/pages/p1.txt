## 1 King Club Access With Balcony

452 sq ftView Varies

​​​This 42 sq m guestroom is designed around light and space, with floor-to-ceiling windows leading onto a private balcony overlooking the Arabian Gulf or the Presidential Palace. Guests enjoy exclusive Grand Club access, including complimentary breakfast served at Sahha, all-day tea and coffee, and evening cocktails, beverages, appetizers and canapés from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
