### AN ARTIST'S IMPRESSION

The chandeliers in the Yarra Rooms have a quirky secret. Look closely — they’re an artist’s impression of the iconic Flinder Street Station, just upside down!

The round domes of the chandeliers are inspired by the station's distinct Victorian domes, which can be seen from the Yarra Rooms.

The Langham, Melbourne acknowledges the Wurundjeri Woi Wurrung people of the Kulin Nation as the Traditional Owners of the land on which we stand, and pay respect to their Elders past, present and emerging.
