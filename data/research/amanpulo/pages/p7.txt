**Calming Bamboo Treatment**

A deeply restorative treatment combining a 45-minute warm bamboo back massage with a 30-minute coconut scalp massage. The back massage uses heated bamboo techniques to release muscle tension, improve circulation and ease tightness across the back and shoulders. This is followed by a nourishing coconut oil scalp massage designed to soothe the nervous system, hydrate the scalp and promote deep relaxation.

**PADI Dive Centre**

Pamalican Island’s surrounding waters have been a self-declared marine sanctuary for over 30 years. Pristine reefs and clear waters are home to an array of corals, reef fish and marine life ranging from tiny shrimps to large sea turtles. Amanpulo's PADI Dive Centre offers personally guided dive tours and SCUBA diving courses all in a professional and safe atmosphere. Dive sites are no more than a 10-minute boat ride away.

**Manamoc Island Hike**

Take a relaxing boat ride to the nearby island of Manamoc and set off on a scenic trek up Mount English, culminating at the island’s highest point. From the summit, enjoy breathtaking vistas that stretch across the island. The full excursion lasts around four hours.
