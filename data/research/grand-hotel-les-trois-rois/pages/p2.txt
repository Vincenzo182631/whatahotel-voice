### Your Home Away from Home

Our rooms and suites mix old-school charm with a fresh, modern twist — think classic elegance, historic details, and standout design all under one roof. Located in the historic main building at Blumenrain 8 and the newly redesigned head building at Blumenrain 2, they combine the best of both worlds. Whether you're gazing out at the Rhine or soaking up the vibes of Basel’s Old Town, every room feels like a little slice of home. Expect top-tier comfort, high-end finishes, and just the right amount of storybook magic. Perfect for travelers who appreciate good design — and love a place with a story to tell.
