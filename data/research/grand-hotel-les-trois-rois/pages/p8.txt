## Seijaku – Place of Serenity

Scroll down

###

### Place of Serenity

High above the rooftops of the city, in a building designed by the renowned architects Herzog & de Meuron, lies a sanctuary far removed from everyday life: Seijaku is more than a place of retreat – it is an immersive experience of Japanese mindfulness culture. Everything here centres around Yasuragi, Ki, and Ichigo-Ichie – harmony, energy, and the art of living in the moment. Gentle warmth, soothing steam, and an atmosphere of complete stillness guide you toward renewed balance and deep regeneration.
