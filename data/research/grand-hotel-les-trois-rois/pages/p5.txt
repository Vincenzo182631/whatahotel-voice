Basel and the three-country region offer an exceptional variety of cultural experiences throughout the year – from world-class concerts and inspiring exhibitions to prestigious sporting events. Enjoy your stay in this unique destination where cultures come together.

Grand Hotel Les Trois Rois is at your service to assist you in discovering and selecting the most remarkable events and activities.
