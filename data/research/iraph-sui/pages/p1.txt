# Welcome to IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa

## An exquisite stay at our Miyakojima hotel

Retreat to beachfront luxury on Irabu Island at IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel in Miyako Okinawa. Located on Irabu Island, southwest of Okinawa, our luxury Miyako Island hotel is surrounded by lush...See More forests, an alluring sea and the rich culture of Miyakojima City. After exploring the islands, return to our hotel restaurant and dine on gourmet dishes, featuring wagyu, pork, fresh fish and vegetables. Spend your day at our on-site spa that incorporates the sand, sun and waters of Miyakojima and Okinawa in its healing body treatments. Take a dip in our sparkling pool, work out in the fitness center or gather for drinks with friends in our lobby bar. Our luxury hotel in Miyakojima boasts 58 guestrooms and suites, featuring balconies or terraces, thoughtful amenities and sea views. Select accommodations at this Okinawa luxury resort offer private pools and whirlpools for a soothing soak. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa?

The check-in time at IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 30.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa?

On-site parking is available at IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa booking.

What is the closest airport near IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa?

The closest airport near IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa is Shimojishima Airport (SHI). SHI is located approximately 4.9 KM from the hotel.

Does IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa have an airport shuttle to Shimojishima Airport (SHI)?

Yes, IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa has an airport shuttle to Shimojishima Airport (SHI).

Does IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa have an airport shuttle to Miyako Airport (MMY)?

Yes, IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa has an airport shuttle to Miyako Airport (MMY).

Does IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, IRAPH SUI, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Miyako Okinawa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
