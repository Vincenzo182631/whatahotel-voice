## ENJOY TURQUOISE WATERS FROM OUR LUXURY RESORT AND CASINO

Make memories that last a lifetime at our luxury resort in Nassau, Bahamas. Delight in every moment of your stay, from authentic Bahamian service to pristine waters and sugar-sand beaches. Unwind and retreat to stunning guest rooms, luxury suites and spacious villas. Our kid-friendly beachfront resort features eight pools, water sports like kayaking and paddleboarding, a unique wildlife sanctuary and a Gamezone Arcade in the kid’s club. Adults can find the ultimate relaxation by reserving a poolside cabana or booking a spa treatment at ESPA, the resort’s full-service spa. Stay active at Jack Nicklaus’ Royal Blue golf course or test your luck at one of the largest casinos in the Bahamas. Spend the day with the family at Baha Bay Waterpark, where you can speed down 10 waterslides, float the action river, surf the FlowRider or lounge by the 500,000-gallon lagoon wave pool. Grand Hyatt Baha Mar offers guests unlimited resort activities, premier shopping, and 45 dining outlets featuring cuisine from around the world, including Kosher options, sustainable seafood, beachside food trucks and poolside grills.

## ROOMS & SUITES

All guest rooms, suites and villas include plush beds and floor-to-ceiling windows with incredible views of the ocean, island, or fountain show. For an elevated Bahamas experience, book The Reserve, our luxury section of the resort featuring a private check-in desk, lounge and exclusive butler service (available on 2-bedroom residences in the East Tower and 2 bedroom poolside villas only).

## EXPLORE

Outdoor Pools

Enjoy eight exquisite pools, each with its own distinct Bahamian design and character. During your stay, experience them all, laze on a daybed or an in-pool chaise, or cozy up in a cabana—for an extra dose of exclusivity and privacy.

The Reserve at Grand Hyatt Baha Mar

Choose from a collection of elevated rooms, suites and residences in The Reserve at Grand Hyatt Baha Mar designed for guests seeking the highest expression of luxury. Reserve guests also enjoy a separate private entrance with dedicated check-in area, VIP lounge and upgraded amenities.

Resort Activities

At Grand Hyatt Baha Mar you can enjoy a diverse range of experiences, from wildlife encounters and world-class golf to an exciting casino, soothing spas, stunning pools, sports activities, and a thrilling water park.

Baha Bay Waterpark

Inspired by the natural beauty of the B\[1\] ahamas, Baha Bay is designed to perfectly fuse relaxing island chill with exhilarating fun at our family-friendly resort. The luxe waterpark is directly adjacent to Baha Mar and set on 15 lush beachfront acres, with an array of spectacular experiences and tantalizing cuisine. However, you like to play in the sun, it awaits you at Baha Bay. Baha Mar resort guests enjoy exclusive and unlimited access to Baha Bay waterpark.

Baha Mar Casino

Baha Mar Casino features 100,000 square feet of world-class gaming excitement and live entertainment. The largest in the Caribbean, our spectacular casino in Nassau, Bahamas, offers the latest slot machines and table games. The space is enhanced by ocean views and floor-to-ceiling windows, creating a next-generation casino experience.

Royal Blue Golf Course

Royal Blue at Baha Mar is a Jack Nicklaus Signature Golf Course with rolling fairways, moonscapes, native landscapes and striking views from every green and fairway. The par 72, 18-hole golf course features dramatic vistas of the turquoise ocean on the front nine and forested panoramas on the second half.

ESPA Baha Mar

The ultimate spa experience unfolds with 30,000 square feet of total tranquility with ESPA at Baha Mar. Our resort spa’s 24 private rooms offer a wide range of luxurious treatments and therapies flawlessly designed for total relaxation. Whether you’re looking to break a sweat with a quick treadmill run or center your body and mind with some yoga, Baha Mar will help keep your workout routine on track.

### Marcus at Baha Mar Fish + Chop House

Chef Marcus Samuelsson has brought his signature style and cuisine to Marcus at Baha Mar Fish + Chop House. This innovative chophouse and seafood restaurant is a celebration of Chef Marcus Samuelsson’s groundbreaking culinary legacy and passion for food, flavors and fun. Inspired by both Bahamian culture and his travels throughout the world, the six-time James Beard Award winning Chef has crafted a restaurant that is vibrant and delicious. With a menu featuring locally and sustainably caught seafood, fresh produce and premium dry-aged cuts, Marcus at Baha Mar is the premier destination for guests looking to get a taste of the best the island has to offer. Location: Grand Hyatt Baha Mar - Beachfront near the Pier

### Pets Are Welcome

We’re happy to welcome your traveling canine companions to our Nassau Bahamas luxury resort. To participate in the pet program, please inform the hotel you’ll have a pet (or pets) staying with you at least three days before your arrival. Dogs must be housebroken. Maximum of two dogs per room.

#### Pet Fees

- 1-6 nights: **$250**

- 7 nights or more: **$300**

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  50Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 75Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.

### Resort Fee  Policy

A daily resort fee of $76.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience.

Future Changes: Bookings on and beyond January 01, 2027 - A daily resort fee of $78.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.
