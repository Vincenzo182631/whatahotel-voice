# Hotel Policies

Resort Fee

A daily resort fee of $76.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience.

- Daily access to Baha Bay Luxury Beachfront Waterpark for all registered guests
- Non-motorized water sports
- Single & Double Kayaks
- Stand Up Paddle Boards
- Water hammocks
- Water mats
- ESPA fitness center access
- Unlimited local calls
- 2 branded refillable bottles
- Sunscreen at all Hyatt towel huts
- Hyatt beach and pool setup and seating with umbrellas
- Daily Flamingo Ambassador Parade
- Aviary Sanctuary hands on experience
- Basketball, Bocce Ball, Croquet courts

Future Changes: Bookings on and beyond January 01, 2027 - A daily resort fee of $78.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

- Daily access to Baha Bay Luxury Beachfront Waterpark for all registered guests
- Non-motorized water sports
- Single & Double Kayaks
- Stand Up Paddle Boards
- Water hammocks
- Water mats
- ESPA fitness center access
- 2 branded refillable bottles
- Hyatt beach and pool setup and seating with umbrellas
- Daily Flamingo Ambassador Parade
- Basketball, Bocce Ball, Croquet courts

Service Charge

A daily Service Charge of $21.78 (tax included) will be added to your rate for all room nights.

This Service Charge is distributed fully to Resort personnel as a gratuity. Guests may provide a voluntary tip to Resort personnel in addition to the Service Charge. Such charges may be subject to taxes in effect at the time of your stay.

Personal Packages

Due to exorbitant costs of freight, brokerage fees, customs duty and VAT associated with shipping to The Bahamas, we strongly advise that guests do not send personal packages to Grand Hyatt Baha Mar ahead of their stay. Guests are advised that all costs associated with shipping to the resort will be charged to the card on file.

Pet Policy

We are happy to welcome your traveling canine companions. To participate in the pet program, please inform the hotel you’ll have a pet (or pets) staying with you at least three days before your arrival.

**Additional Terms**:

- Damages to the room will be billed on a case-by-case basis.
- Owner will provide a cell phone number to locate guest in event of an emergency. Under extreme circumstances, dog may be moved to a kennel.
- Dogs must be settled by 9:30 p.m.
- We regret that pets are not allowed at the golf course or in the food and beverage outlets, health club or pool areas of the hotel. Service animals are an exception to this policy.
- Dogs must be walked in designated areas.
- Any noise disturbance must be curtailed to ensure the comfort of other guests.
- Room attendants are not allowed to enter guest rooms with pets, unless the occupant is present. For your convenience, please call housekeeping to arrange a suitable time to have your room serviced.

**Fees**:

To ensure a comfortable visit for all, we ask that you adhere to the following guidelines:

- A one-time mandatory cleaning fee of $250 for a 1–6 night stay.
- A one-time mandatory cleaning fee of $300 for a stay of 7 nights or more.
- In addition, a credit card must be presented at the time of check-in to guarantee against any damages or soiled carpets/soft goods in the room.

**Weight Limit**:

Only dogs that are housebroken and do not exceed 50 pounds or two dogs under a combined weight of 75 pounds are permitted. Two dogs per guest room or suite.

Credit Card Authorization Information

Why does my credit card have to be authorized for $200 per night? Since our hotel is a cashless resort, we place an authorization hold on your credit card at check-in for $200 per night for incidentals, so you are able to charge items to your room. Should your bill exceed the amount held during your stay, the system will automatically block the additional amount on your credit card.

Hurricane No-Risk Guarantee

In the event that a hurricane watch or warning is issued within seven  days of your scheduled departure by the United States National Hurricane Center (NOAA) for New Providence, The Bahamas or your place of residence, you may contact your travel professional who booked your trip or the Grand Hyatt Baha Mar reservations team prior to your scheduled departure to cancel your stay and receive a full refund, or reschedule your hotel room reservation and avoid the imposition of no show penalties, or any other hotel cancellation or change fees.

Early Departures

If guests are on vacation at Baha Mar and wish to depart early due to the threat of a hurricane, a full refund will be issued for the remaining nights.

Terms & Conditions

Rescheduled accommodations will be in an equivalent room category to the one originally booked. Accommodation of same room category is not guaranteed and is subject to availability. Other expenses, including but not limited to, pre-arrival bookings such as hotel transportation, kids club, etc., will be refunded. Airline ticket holders are subject to airlines’ policies and guidelines. This policy does not apply to group bookings. Group booking cancellations will be held in accordance with the group sales contract.

This policy is for individual travel booked directly with Hyatt; it does not apply to bookings via third parties, tour operators or resellers. Bookings made through these entities must contact their original booking source for information on the hurricane policy for their reservation.
