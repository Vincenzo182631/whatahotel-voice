# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Valet parking is available at our hotel and includes in-and-out privileges. Payment must be made via debit or credit card only—no cash or charges to your room. Restaurants do not validate for valet parking.

### Self Parking

Complimentary self-parking is available. The parking lot is located beside the convention center.

## Transportation

Getting here

Nassau Lynden Pindling International Airport (NAS)6 miles6 miles

Nassau Lynden Pindling International Airport (NAS) is now part of Mobile Passport Control (MPC). Baha Mar guests can now download and use the Customs and Border Protection Mobile Passport Control app to move quickly through U.S. Customs and Border Protection checkpoints when returning to the U.S. from The Bahamas.

To learn more please visit: https://www.cbp.gov/travel/us-citizens/mobile-passport-control

Transportation Options

Private Transportation

Paid private transportation to and from the airport is available. Contact concierge.nasgh@hyatt.com prior to arrival for arrangements or use The Bahamas Ride mobile app. Book your ride online in a minute at https://bahamar.com/transportation/

Private Transportation

Taxi

Taxis are available on arrival. The cost is $25-40 each way. Please note the cost varies based on the number of people and the amount of luggage. Note that taxis vary in condition, and many do not take credit cards. Both U.S. dollars and Bahamian dollars are accepted.

Airport Fast Track

The Bahamas also offers Fast Track service at Lynden Pindling International Airport. For more information about Fast Track services to Lynden Pindling International Airport please visit: http://www.bahamasairportfasttrack.com.
