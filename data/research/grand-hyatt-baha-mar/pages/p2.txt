Indulge in the ultimate Bahamas escape from our elegantly appointed guest rooms, suites, and villas each designed for comfort and immersed in breathtaking natural beauty. Enjoy plush bedding, refined finishes, and floor to ceiling views of the island or fountain show. For a truly exceptional stay, book The Reserve at Grand Hyatt Baha Mar, our exclusive luxury enclave within the resort. Reserve guests enjoy a dedicated private check in experience, access to a VIP guest lounge, and the unmatched convenience of butler service (available with 2-bedroom residences in the East Tower and 2-bedroom poolside villas).

- ### The Reserve at Grand Hyatt Baha Mar

Discover king or queen/queen fountain view rooms, one- or two-bedroom fountain view or ocean view residences and suites and one- or two-bedroom poolside villas.

- ### FEATURES

Guests of The Reserve at Grand Hyatt Baha Mar enjoy an exclusive private entrance, check-in experience, separate lobby, and VIP Lounge with breakfast each morning, light refreshments throughout the day, and hors d’oeuvres in the evening.

There is also a private conference room for meetings, personalized concierge and butler services (available on two-bedroom residences and villas) and spa-inspired bath amenities, plush robes, and slippers in every room.
