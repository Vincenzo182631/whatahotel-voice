### Disappointing Experience at a Resort We've Loved for Years

I've visited this resort once or twice a year for several years, and unfortunately, this was by far the most disappointing experience we've had. I personally organized this trip for eight people, confidently recommending the property based on our previous visits and the level of service we've come to expect. Unfortunately, the experience fell significantly short of those expectations.

Dining was consistently frustrating. Across nearly every restaurant, we encountered extensive shortages of wine, often with four or five bottles from the wine list unavailable. What should have been a simple dinner experience repeatedly turned into 30–40 minutes of waiting while staff searched for available selections, frequently leaving our table with nothing but water. Eventually, we gave up trying to order bottles and settled for individual glasses.

Our first evening at Marcus was particularly surprising. Despite having a reservation for eight, we were told the restaurant had ordered only 11 filets for the entire evening. Combined with the lack of available wines, it was an unusual and disappointing start to the trip.

Our experience at Shuang Ba was equally frustrating, with disappointing service and lengthy delays.

Baha Beach Club was another major disappointment. We reserved space for eight guests, yet clean towels were unavailable until approximately 1:00 p.m. Guests were left searching other areas of the resort for something as basic as a towel. After a rainy morning, a towel attendant then asked us to collect the soaking-wet towels that had been sitting outside in the rain and carry them to the towel return ourselves. For a premium beach club where guests are paying for reserved seating and service, this was an incredibly disappointing level of hospitality.

The billing issues continued after checkout. We received multiple $55 no-show notifications for restaurant reservations we attended, despite the dinners appearing on our resort folio. We also discovered an additional $98 charge associated with Katsuya, despite signing only one check. These discrepancies have added another layer of frustration to an already disappointing stay.

I completely understand that October is a slower season, and I'm certainly willing to extend grace for an occasional service issue. However, the consistent lack of inventory, limited menu availability, extended delays, and shortage of basic amenities felt less like isolated inconveniences and more like an overall decline in operations.

What was most disappointing was having personally encouraged seven other people to book this trip based on how highly I've spoken of the resort over the years. Instead, I repeatedly found myself apologizing for experiences that simply did not reflect what we had come to expect.

This resort has always been somewhere I've genuinely enjoyed returning to, which makes writing this review especially disappointing. The property itself remains beautiful, and I know firsthand how exceptional the experience can be when everything is operating as it should.

Unfortunately, this visit did not come close to that standard. For a resort positioned and priced as a luxury destination, guests should not have to lower their expectations for basic amenities, restaurant availability, or attentive service simply because they're visiting during a slower month.

I sincerely hope management takes a closer look at these operational issues. I would love to return with the confidence I've had in years past, but after this experience, I'd have a difficult time recommending the resort to others at its current price point.

### Incredible Stay From Start to Finish-We Can’t Wait to Return!

We had an absolutely incredible stay at Grand Hyatt Baha Mar from start to finish. From the warm welcome at reception to the friendly and attentive service we received from the majority of the staff throughout the resort, we genuinely felt well taken care of the entire time.

The food deserves its own mention because we honestly did not have a bad meal anywhere. Everything was fresh, hot, delicious and consistently impressive. The croissants at Madeleine rival some of the best we’ve had in France! Costa was another highlight, the food was incredibly tasty, and dining over the koi pond makes for such a unique and beautiful atmosphere.

Dinner at Marcus was outstanding and it’s easy to understand why it is consistently rated as one of the top restaurants on property. The service was attentive and professional, our server was extremely knowledgeable, and the food was a 10/10 in both quality and portion size. They even surprised us with a lovely birthday dessert at the end of the meal, which was a really nice touch.

We also spent time at the beach club, which was incredibly relaxing, and the food from 25 North was spectacular. Another surprise highlight was lunch at Vibe BBQ by Michael Samuelsson, one of the newer spots at the resort. It was SO good! The staff there were especially friendly, always smiling and genuinely seemed appreciative to have us there. Brisket sandwich + jerk chicken meal = A+

Beyond the restaurants, the entire resort is stunning from the luxurious and lush green grounds to the beautiful soft sand beach. And there is so much to do. Even the lazy river was an absolute blast!

Most importantly, we didn’t encounter a single issue during our stay. Everything came together for an amazing getaway, and we left already talking about coming back. Next time, I think we’ll be bringing our kids with us because they would absolutely love Baha Mar.

Thank you to everyone at Grand Hyatt Baha Mar for such a memorable stay. We can’t wait to return!

### Would not spend my money there again

Before our arrival, I emailed to request that our king bed be made up with two separate duvets. I was told to remind reception when we arrived, which I did. However, the bed was not prepared as requested.

Two hours later, I called reception and asked for this to be done before we returned from dinner. When we came back and wanted to go to sleep, it still hadn’t been done. I called reception again and, after another long wait, someone finally came and arranged it. I appreciated his help.

For the next three to four days, however, the duvets were simply placed on top of each other rather than arranged separately. Eventually, I stayed in the room to speak to the room attendant. When I explained how I wanted the bed made, she said she didn’t know how to do it. I asked her to bring a supervisor, but nothing happened. When we spoke again, she apologised and repeated that she didn’t know how.

In the end, I asked for four sheets, made the bed myself and showed her how it needed to be done. I also asked for someone from senior management to come so I could explain the arrangement and ensure it would be done correctly the following day. It took approximately two hours for a supervisor to arrive.

The supervisor apologised, and I also raised several housekeeping concerns. The bedroom floor did not appear to have been vacuumed, dust was left on surfaces, mirrors were only partially cleaned, and hair remained on the shower floor for around three days. The coffee supplies were not replenished consistently, and milk was sometimes provided and sometimes missing.

For the amount we paid, the standard of service was extremely disappointing. We experienced similarly poor service in the restaurants, despite the high prices. It often felt as though tips were expected, while very little care or attention was given to the service itself.

Having to make my own bed and repeatedly chase basic housekeeping during an expensive holiday was unacceptable. This experience has left me deeply disappointed with the hotel and has put me off returning to the Bahamas. Based on this stay, I would struggle to recommend either.Katsuya and Pizza Lab, At both restaurants, one person would seat us and ask about our order, leaving us with the impression that we were being looked after. We would then wait, only for another person to arrive and ask the same questions again. On one occasion, when we asked whether we could order, we were told, “No, I’m the main person. It wouldn’t happen without me.” The whole process was confusing, poorly coordinated and unnecessarily slow.
This particular issue did not happen at Marcus, where one lady provided excellent service. However, many of the other staff appeared to be standing around chatting rather than attending to guests.

Our restaurant bills totalled thousands, not merely hundreds, yet even basic details were overlooked. Steak was served without a steak knife, and lobster arrived without the tools needed to open it. We should not have had to chase essential cutlery and utensils while our food was already on the table. At these prices, attentive, coordinated service should be a basic expectation. Instead, we were repeatedly left waiting and asking for things that should have been provided without prompting.
To give credit where it is due, our experience at the Chuang BA, Leola, restaurant was very good. Tingum and Cigar bar too.
