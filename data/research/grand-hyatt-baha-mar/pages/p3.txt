# Dining

Discover more than 45 dining options at our luxury beachfront resort in Nassau, Bahamas, where there is something to please every palate, and kids 5 and under eat free. Our restaurants feature international cuisine, Kosher options, family-friendly eateries, and indulgent sweets shops. From Kosher fare at Cinkō, Café Madeleine, and Knosh to fresh Bahamian ingredients and sustainable seafood at Marcus at Baha Mar Fish + Chop House, guests can enjoy a wide range of flavors throughout their stay. Grab a bite from a nearby food truck when hunger strikes at the pool or beach, or indulge at one of our fine dining restaurants after a day of resort activities. Experience outdoor dining, local Bahamas cuisine, worldwide flavors, and bars and lounges with unique vibes, from casual to exhilarating, at Grand Hyatt Baha Mar.

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.
