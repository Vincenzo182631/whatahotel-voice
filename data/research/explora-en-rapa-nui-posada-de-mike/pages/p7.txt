What to Pack

Explora provides travelers with trekking pole and give them a bottle of water.

**Required for all activities:**

- A hat for the sun
- 1st layer: Upper and lower
- 2nd layer: Fleece and thin fleece
- 3rd layer: Waterproof jacket
- Short-and long sleeve t-shirts
- Hiking trousers and shorts.
- Hiking shoes
- Hiking socks (long)
- Thermal and windproof gloves
- Sunglasses
- Buff or protective neckwear
- Sunscreen

**Optional for all activities:**

- Swimsuit
- Sandals
- Camel back

Climate

Easter Island, or Rapa Nui, enjoys a mild, subtropical climate that remains pleasant throughout the year, making it a great destination no matter when you visit. The average annual temperature hovers around 70°F (21°C), with February being the warmest month, often reaching highs of 81°F (27°C).

Rain is scattered and brief, with an annual average of 45 inches (1,140 mm), but no distinct rainy season, so it’s always wise to be prepared for light showers. The island’s average humidity is about 76%, contributing to its lush green landscapes. Whether you’re visiting during the warmer months or the cooler periods, the gentle breezes and moderate temperatures make it comfortable year-round.

Wifi & Voltage

Please note that due to our remote locations, Wi-Fi access in rooms and common areas may vary in reliability.

The voltage in Easter Island is 220V.

Tips

Tips are voluntary. If you wish to leave any, you can do so at the hotel reception at the end of your stay. It will be distributed among all those who contributed to making your trip a unique experience. We suggest leaving USD 100 per day per room.

FAQs

- Is Explora Rapa Nui all-inclusive?

Yes. Every Explora stay on Easter Island is fully all-inclusive. Your rate covers all accommodations, unlimited guided Explorations, all meals and beverages including Chilean wines, spa facilities, national park entrance fees, and round-trip transfers between Mataveri International Airport and the lodge. There are no supplementary charges for activities or guided experiences during your stay

- What is included in an Explora Easter Island stay?

Your stay includes accommodations in one of 30 rooms (including suites with Pacific views), access to all guided Explorations — from hikes to the Rano Raraku quarry to coastal kayaking — all meals prepared by our culinary team, beverages including wine, spa and pool access, national park entrance fees, and airport transfers. The only extras are personal purchases and optional spa treatments beyond the included access.

- How Many Nights Do You Recommend Staying at Explora?

We recommend a minimum of four nights to have an exploration experience that allows you to deeply discover Easter Island, although we are sure that when discovering its mysteries, you will want to extend your stay. Five nights are a good alternative to explore all its corners and enjoy the local culture.

- Can I Change The Type of Bed In My Room?

At the time of booking, you must select a room with either a king-size bed or two double beds. In Rapa Nui, if needed, you may request a bed change, subject to availability.

- Do The Rooms Come with Air Conditioning?

Yes, all rooms are equipped with air conditioning.

- Is It Possible To Scuba Dive In Rapa Nui?

Our exploration programs in Easter Island offer various scuba diving options for both beginners and experienced divers. These activities come at an additional cost and can be arranged through our reservations team or directly on-site.

\*Explora is not responsible for services provided by third-party operators.

- What is the best hotel on Easter Island?

Explora Rapa Nui has been named among the best resorts in South America by the Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards and recognized as the World’s Leading Expedition Company. Located outside Hanga Roa with sweeping Pacific Ocean views, the lodge offers an all-inclusive experience focused on deep exploration of Rapa Nui culture, archaeology, and natural landscapes — a different category from conventional hotels on the island.

- How do I get to Easter Island?

Easter Island (Rapa Nui) is served by Mataveri International Airport (IPC) in Hanga Roa. LATAM Airlines operates regular flights from Santiago, Chile (approximately 5 hours) and seasonal flights from Lima, Peru. Once you land, Explora coordinates round-trip airport transfers to and from the lodge as part of your all-inclusive rate. There are no connecting flights required — just one direct route from Santiago.

- What kinds of explorations are available from the lodge?

Explora Rapa Nui offers more than 30 guided Explorations, all included in your stay. Options include hiking to the Rano Raraku moai quarry, biking to ancient ahu platforms, kayaking and stand-up paddleboarding along the volcanic coastline, snorkeling, diving, and cultural immersions with locally trained guides. Every exploration is customized daily based on your interests and fitness level.

- Are alcoholic beverages included at Explora Rapa Nui? A: Yes.

Yes. The all-inclusive rate at Explora Rapa Nui covers all beverages, including Chilean wines, beer, and spirits, served throughout the day in the dining room and common areas. There is no separate bar tab or beverage charge during your stay. Premium or specialty selections beyond the standard offering may carry an additional cost — contact reservations for specifics prior to arrival.

- What is not included in an Explora Rapa Nui stay?

The all-inclusive rate covers accommodations, all guided Explorations, all meals and beverages, spa facility access, national park entrance fees, and round-trip airport transfers. Items not included are personal purchases, gratuities, and optional spa treatments beyond general facility access. There are no hidden activity charges — every guided Exploration offered by Explora is included in the base rate.

- How many rooms does Explora Rapa Nui have?

Explora Rapa Nui has 30 rooms in total: 4 suites (including the Raa Suite at 44 m²/474 ft² with ocean and garden views) and 26 Varúa rooms (32 m²/345 ft²). All rooms include jetted bathtubs, twin or king bed configurations, and non-smoking policy. Room size reflects the lodge’s design philosophy: essential luxury and deep rest rather than maximalist features.
