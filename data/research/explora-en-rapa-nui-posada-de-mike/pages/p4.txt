## The World´s most remote inhabited island. Yours to explore.

Some 2,300 miles west of South America (3,700 km), discover enigmatic Easter Island with its enormous moai statues from our intimate lodge, Explora Rapa Nui. With a unique countryside location outside the village of Hanga Roa, our modern lodge offers countless opportunities for authentic immersion in Rapa Nui culture and traditions, as well as guided Explorations that include hikes to the RanoRaraku moai quarry, bike rides to ancient settlements and coastal adventures by kayak.

Explora Rapa Nui comprises 4 suites and 26 Varúa rooms, each appointed with natural wood interiors, ocean or garden views, and jetted bathtubs. On-site amenities include a full-service spa, outdoor pool, hot tub, and sauna, alongside an award-winning dining program featuring Polynesian and Chilean cuisine with Chilean wine service. The all-inclusive rate encompasses all

guided Explorations, all meals and beverages, spa facility access, national park entrance fees, and round-trip transfers from Mataveri International Airport (IPC).

What’s Included in Your Stay

Explora Easter Island lodge offers an all-inclusive experience, allowing you to immerse yourself deeply in the territory without any distractions.

Stay in a Uniquely Remote Easter Island Lodge

Go deeper into the most remote inhabited place on the planet with a stay at our luxuriously all-inclusive lodge featuring award-winning architecture and a countryside location beyond the main town of Hanga Roa. Our 30 contemporary rooms offer Pacific Ocean views as well as a tranquil, restful retreat after days exploring Rapa Nui Island.

Discover In-Depth Island Explorations

You’re invited to choose from 30 guided Explorations, included as part of your stay at Explora Rapa Nui. Led by our own expertly trained guides, these experiences and Explora-only adventures include hiking, biking, snorkeling, diving, stand-up paddle boarding (SUP) and cultural immersions.Every Exploration is also customized daily to match your interests and fitness levels, promising a personalized and unforgettable discovery of mystical Easter Island.

Our Explorations

Enjoy Elevated Rapa Nui Cuisine

At Explora Rapa Nui, an authentic, included culinary experience complete with Chilean wines offers another opportunity to experience the culture of the easternmost outpost of the Polynesian Islands. Join us in the dining room surrounded by ocean views to discover dishes that bring Easter Island to life with a menu showcasing its flavors, deep culinary traditions and diverse native ingredients.

Why Travel with Explora

**Award-Winning Lodges**

Recognized for their stunning architecture, low-impact design and essential luxuries, our intimate lodges offer an ideal base for Explorations – and retreat.

**Custom-Made Explorations**

Every expert-led excursion we lead is custom made for you and your skill level, designed to bring you closer to nature in a most authentic way.

**Passionate Expertise**

Our long-established presence in South America, deep connections in each unique territory and guides trained at our own exclusive school define our inspiring Explorations.

**All-Inclusive Experience**

Your stay, guided Explorations, culinary program designed by an award-winning chef, transfers and more are all included to provide a seamless experience for you.

**Commitment to Conservation**

We’re passionate about preserving the pristine environments we explore and invite our guests to join us in sustainable initiatives, including our Conservation Reserves.
