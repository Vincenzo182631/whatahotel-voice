Easter Island

How to get to Easter Island from Santiago Chile

**From Santiago, Chile,** you’ll need to connect to a flight to Easter Island (Rapa Nui), which takes approximately 5 hours.

**Upon arrival at Mataveri Airport,** our team will pick you up in a van and transfer you to Explora, a short 15-minute journey. _Included in programs with transportation. See the location on the map here._

**For a private transfer,** there is an additional fee. Please contact your reservation agent or email reserve@explora.com before your trip to inquire about availability.

**Please note:** If you experience a flight change less than 24 hours before check-in and require a private transfer, additional costs may apply.

Check-in & Check-out

**Check-in**: After 13:00. We recommend arriving at noon for an afternoon exploration.

**Check-out:** 10:00

We want you to make the most of your stay. That’s why we offer the option of a half-day exploration on your departure day before heading to the airport (if your flight schedule allows). In this case, you’ll need to check out at 07:00 before exploring, and we’ll store your luggage.

\*Please note: Lunch service is not included on your departure day and is available at an additional charge per person.

If you’d like to request a late check-out for your convenience, please consult with our team for availability and rates.
