Experience the Remote Beauty of Our Easter Island Lodge

Perched on rugged grasslands with sweeping views to the ocean, the 30-room Explora Rapa Nui offers a unique and immersive experience outside Hanga Roa. It’s contemporary design by award-winning architect José Cruz Ovalleseamlessly blends into the island’s volcanic landscape. Discover sleek rooms featuring natural wood materials and ocean views, a comprehensive spa with a sauna, and a stunning outdoor pool and hot tub surrounded by wild Rapa Nui landscapes.

Explora’s lodge enjoy a privileged and secluded location on Easter Island, surrounded by trees, grasslands and far-reaching views over the vast, blue Pacific Ocean. Located just a short distance from the main town of Hanga Roa and central to trailheads and archeological sites, Explora Rapa Nui is the perfect base for exploring the island’s ancient mysteries and natural wonders.

Contemporary Architecture

The architecture of Explora Rapa Nui, designed by José Cruz Ovalle, creates a unique spatial experience that connects the energy of the island with the vastness of the ocean. Interconnected volumes, reminiscent of the arrangement of the moai statues, generate a sense of community and belonging, while natural materials and the integration ofdistinctive woods as well as shadows and light echo the landscapes of a mesmerizing volcanic island.

FAQs

- What room types are available at Explora Rapa Nui?

Explora Rapa Nui offers two room categories: the Raa Suite and the Varúa Room. The

property has 4 suites and 26 Varúa rooms, for a total of 30 rooms. Both room types featurenatural wood interiors, jetted bathtubs, and views to the Pacific Ocean or lodge gardens. Twin or king bed configurations are available in all rooms, and cribs can be arranged on request.

- What is the Raa Suite at Explora Rapa Nui?

The Raa Suite is the premium room category at Explora Rapa Nui, at 44 m² / 474 ft². Each suite features views to the gardens and the Pacific Ocean, a king or twin bed configuration, a jetted bathtub, heating, and a wake-up call service. The suite name draws from the Rapa Nui word for the sun. There are 4 Raa Suites on the property.

- What is a Varúa Room at Explora Rapa Nui?

The Varúa Room is the standard room category at Explora Rapa Nui, at 32 m² / 345 ft². Each room features views to the gardens and ocean, natural wood interiors, a jetted bathtub, heating, and a wake-up call service. Twin or king configurations are available. There are 26 Varúa rooms on the property. ‘Varúa’ is a Polynesian word meaning spirit or soul.

- How big are the rooms at Explora Rapa Nui?

Raa Suites measure 44 m² / 474 ft². Varúa Rooms measure 32 m² / 345 ft². Both room types are designed to provide what Explora describes as essential luxury — sufficient space for deep rest and privacy after full days of

- Do the rooms at Explora Rapa Nui have ocean views?

All rooms at Explora Rapa Nui have views to the gardens and the Pacific Ocean. The property sits on elevated grassland outside Hanga Roa, with the lodge oriented to take advantage of the island’s coastal landscape. Room views vary by specific location within the property — guests seeking a particular view orientation can request this at the time of booking.

- Who designed Explora Rapa Nui?

Explora Rapa Nui was designed by Chilean architect José Cruz Ovalle. The architecture draws from the landscape and cultural heritage of Rapa Nui — the interconnected volumes of the building are described as echoing the arrangement of the island’s moai statues, while the use of natural wood and the interplay of shadow and light reflect the volcanic terrain. The design has received international architectural recognition.

- Does Explora Rapa Nui have a pool?

Yes. Explora Rapa Nui has an outdoor swimming pool and hot tub, situated within the lodge grounds with views across the island’s grasslands toward the Pacific Ocean. The pool area is accessible to all guests as part of the all-inclusive stay. Pool hours and specific operating details can be confirmed with the property directly prior to arrival.

- Does Explora Rapa Nui have a spa, and what treatments are available?

Yes. Explora Rapa Nui has a full-service spa — Hare Vaka — offering massage and body treatments, including options inspired by Polynesian and Rapa Nui wellness traditions. Basic spa facility access is included in the all-inclusive rate. Individual spa treatments are bookable separately and carry an additional charge. Appointments can be arranged prior to arrival or at the lodge.

- Does Explora Rapa Nui have a sauna?

Yes. Explora Rapa Nui includes a sauna as part of the on-property wellness facilities. The sauna is available to all guests as part of the all-inclusive rate, alongside the outdoor pool, hot tub, and spa. Specific hours of operation can be confirmed with the property.

- What are the views like at Explora Rapa Nui?

Explora Rapa Nui occupies elevated grassland on the outskirts of Hanga Roa, with sweeping views across the island’s volcanic landscape toward the Pacific Ocean. The lodge’s orientation and room placement are designed to maximize connection to the surrounding terrain. From the property, guests can see the coastline, open grasslands, and the wide Pacific horizon. The island’s archaeological sites and trailheads are accessible directly from the lodge.
