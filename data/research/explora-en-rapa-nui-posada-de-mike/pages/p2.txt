Experience Authentic Rapa Nui Cuisine

At Explora Rapa Nui, enjoy sweeping ocean views from our dining room and an exciting culinary journey through the island’s rich heritage and traditions. Our menu features local ingredients like sweet potatoes, taro, fresh Pacific seafood and the island’s bounty of vibrant tropical fruits, such as the famous Rapa Nui pineapple. Your entire dining experience is included, complete with an assortment of Chilean wines served at both our bar and dining room.

EASTER ISLAND

All-Inclusive Dining

Your stay at Explora Rapa Nui includes every dining experience during your stay, including breakfast, lunch and dinner,as well as all beverages, wine and an open bar. Meals and snacks enjoyed during full-day Explorations are also included.
