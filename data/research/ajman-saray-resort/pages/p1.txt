# Welcome to Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman

## Discover an enchanting beachside resort on Ajman's coastline

Ajman Saray, is the first beachside Luxury Collection Resort in the Middle East, set along a private beach in Ajman. As one of the finest 5-star hotels in Ajman, it blends coastal luxury with inspiring...See More holiday moments for global travelers. Our Ajman resort features 205 spacious guest rooms and suites with Arabian Gulf and Creek views, a private beach with soft white sand, two swimming pools, a gym, Zihn Spa, and a Kids Club. Guests praise the clean beach, spacious rooms, attentive service, and relaxing atmosphere, making it an ideal Ajman beach hotel in the UAE for family-friendly beachfront stays, couples' retreats, and short-stay escapes from Dubai and Sharjah. Five unique onsite dining concepts offer a tantalizing selection of epicurean experiences including Mediterranean, Middle Eastern, grills, and international flavors, while elegant event spaces set the stage for conferences, lifestyle celebrations, and special occasions with personalized service.See Less

### Sustainability Initiatives

Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort was built with intent to be an eco‐friendly facility and targeted to achieve LEED Silver Certification. This intent was carried forward in developing effective design and management strategies to minimize the impact on the surrounding environment during construction and operational phase of the project.

Details

##### Join Us on Our Journey to Green Key Certification

**Main Eco-Friendly Features:**

High Energy performance through highly insulated building envelope and high performance low e‐glazing

Optimized performance through efficient water cooled centrifugal chillers and VFDs in secondary pumping system, cooling tower fans and FAHUs

Potable water reduction through selection of water efficient fixtures

Energy efficient lighting fixtures

10% of materials contain recycle content

20% of the materials used in construction were sourced regionally

More than 85% of construction waste diverted from landfill

Adhesives, sealants, paints and coatings contain low or no Volatile Organic Content (VOC)

57% reduction in potable water consumption for irrigation due to efficient landscaping, use of native species and drip irrigation systems

In addition to the above, our day to day operations is committed to taking small steps to minimize water and energy consumption and reduce waste. These include:

Heart of House recycling of plastic, cans and paper. Printers in administration offices are programmed for double-sided printing.

CFL lighting in the heart of the house to illuminate corridors.

Building management system to control indoor air quality.

Automatic system for energy management.

Heat Recovery Systems are used to control emissions and avoid wasting energy.

Implemented effective energy and water monitoring system to optimized building operations needs

Participating in Earth Hour to promote energy conservation and raise awareness.

Introducing water dispensers in restaurants to minimize the use of plastic bottles.

Implementing the use of sugarcane straws in our outlets as a sustainable alternative.

Incorporating Byredo products in shower amenities to promote eco-friendly practices.

Spearheading a comprehensive zero waste initiative: Introducing food wastage monitoring system to reduce food wastage.

Participating in beach cleaning activities to preserve the coastal ecosystem.

Encouraging guests to walk to nearby destinations, promoting sustainable transportation practices and reducing carbon emissions.

Minimize waste and pollution by reducing, re-using, repairing, re-filling and recycling the resources consumed by our business, whenever appropriate. ##### Sustainability Initiatives & Activities

##### **Green Program:** is an environmental program where stay-over guests will be able to decide if they would like to have their room linen and terry changed on a daily basis, or not. Guests are informed of this initiative upon check-in and may participate by not placing the Green Program card on their bed which informs the Housekeeping Attendant to not change the bed linen. Similarly, guests are informed that if they wish to reuse their towels, they can communicate this to us by putting the towels back in the racks.

##### About Green Key Global

The Green Key certificate is a leading standard of excellence in the field of environmental responsibility and sustainable operation within the tourism industry. This prestigious certificate represents a commitment by businesses that their tourism establishments adhere to the strict criteria stipulated by the Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE). A Green Key stands for an establishment’s promise to its guests that by opting to stay with such an establishment, they are helping to make a difference on an environmental and sustainability level. The high environmental standards expected of these establishments are maintained through rigorous documentation and frequent audits.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman?

The check-in time at Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman.

What are the parking options at Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman?

On-site parking is available at Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman booking.

What is the closest airport near Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman?

The closest airport near Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman is Sharjah International Airport (SHJ). SHJ is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Ajman Saray, a Luxury Collection Resort, Ajman have an airport shuttle to Sharjah International Airport (SHJ)?
