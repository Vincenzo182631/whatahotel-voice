## Experiences

Engage with Argentina the authentic way. Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires is walking distance from museums, parks, fine dining restaurants and sailing on the Rio de la Plata. Discover...See More the historic Plaza San Martín, just steps from the hotel, or immerse yourself in the vibrant Puerto Madero, savoring incomparable Argentine cuisine at renowned parrillas beneath the iconic Puente de la Mujer in Buenos Aires. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-8:30 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), State-of-the-art cardiovascular and strength equipment., Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have a pool on-site?

Yes, Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires?

Puente de la Mujer, Casa Rosada, Plaza Dorrego, Edificio La Prensa, Parroquia San Nicolas de Bari and more are nearby Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires.
