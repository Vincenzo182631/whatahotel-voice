Best for:
Couples seeking old-world prestige without pretense, business travelers, anyone prioritizing European elegance over modern trends
Location:
Near Retiro Train Station and the Rio de la Plata waterfront, positioned close to the city's historic center with Teatro Colon and the Obelisk a short walk away
Can't-miss feature:
Guests in any room category get complimentary pressing of two garment pieces. The St. Regis Suite, which occupies an entire floor and has hosted presidents and royalty, features Louis XV French decor and European antiques.
In a nutshell:
Park Tower understands its audience: travelers who equate luxury with history, not novelty. The suites are generous, the dining focuses on Argentinian beef, and the location positions you at the center of the city’s heartbeat. It's the kind of place where the concierge knows how to navigate the city, not just the hotel.
Park Tower is positioned for travelers seeking European elegance and historical prestige in Argentina’s sophisticated capital. The 116 suites and rooms are appointed in French Louis XV and English styles with authentic European and Argentine antiques—think crown molding, throw rugs, wooden armoires, and lots of paintings in gilded frames. The legendary St. Regis Suite occupies the entire 23rd floor and has hosted presidents and royalty. All suites feature walk-in closets, mini kitchens, marble bathrooms, and pillowtop mattresses.
Breakfast is served at the St. Regis Restaurant and come dinnertime, the menu focuses on local fare, like Patagonian trout, Argentinian beef, and a variety of pasta dishes. The Lobby Lounge is a great place for a quiet moment with a coffee, craft cocktail, or glass of Malbec. Located a stone’s throw from the train station and at the nexus of historic neighborhoods—Recoleta, San Telmo, Puerto Madero—the hotel provides prime positioning for experiencing the city's rhythm. Private car service, concierge guidance, event spaces, and pool access (at the adjacent Sheraton) round out the amenities.
Family friendly:
Yes—there’s a kids club with a ping-pong table, foosball, and arts and crafts at the sister property. However, Park tower skews adult and business-oriented.
