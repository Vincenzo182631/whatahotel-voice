### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires?

The largest occupancy room at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires is the St Regis with a maximum occupancy of 8.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires?

Yes, all rooms at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have hair dryers.
