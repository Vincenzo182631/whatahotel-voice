# Welcome to Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires

## Immerse yourself in the charm of downtown Buenos Aires

Culture, art & history unite at our luxury hotel in downtown Buenos Aires. From the clock tower at Plaza San Martín to the Rio de la Plata coastline, discover a city attuned to authentic experiences. Explore...See More the vibrant hues & storied streets of La Boca, capturing the artistic spirit of its iconic zinc shacks along the Riachuelo River or the Puerto Madero dockside with its redbrick buildings, refined steakhouses & the iconic Puente de la Mujer suspension bridge. Step off the ferry from Montevideo and unwind with our luxury hotel services. Indulge in curated amenities, including two pools & a fitness center, before savoring exquisite dining with unparalleled views of Alem Street. Make Torre Monumental the backdrop to meetings & weddings in venues like our hotels private terrace. Our suites include walk-in closets, kitchens & separate living areas. Refresh yourself in a marble bathroom & sleep soundly on a pillowtop mattress. Authentic moments await you at our business hotel in downtown Buenos Aires.See Less

### St. Regis Suite

Occupying the entire 23rd floor of Park Tower, our vast 380-square-meter St. Regis Suite is fit for a king. And, in fact, it has been the home away from home for many presidents, royalty, and renowned artists. Sumptuous Louis XV French-style décor, fine English furniture, and authentic, precious antiques from both Europe and Buenos Aires attest to the general opulence.

### Terrace Suite

Decorated in distinguished English style, both our 160-square-meter Senator Suite and 173-square-meter Governor Suite offer breathtaking views of Buenos Aires from their personal terraces.

### Corner Suite

Magnificent river or city views are just one of the memorable aspects of our 70-square-meter Corner Suites. Furnished in a sophisticated German style, the calming light-colored walls are complemented by refined wood furniture.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires?

The check-in time at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires.

What are the parking options at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires?

On-site parking is available at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires. Self parking is available at Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires, with rates of $7.00 per hour, $40.00 per day, and $280.00 per week. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires booking.

What is the closest airport near Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires?

The closest airport near Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires is Buenos Aires/Jorge Newbery (AEP). AEP is located approximately 8.7 KM from the hotel.

Does Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires have a Lobby lounge?

Yes, Park Tower, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buenos Aires has Lobby lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Lobby lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
