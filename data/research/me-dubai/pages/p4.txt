### Endless Cocktail Possibilities

Salmon Guru has a refreshing cocktail for even the most indecisive. Check out our bold, strong range, or dare to try one of our sour specials. Looking for something different? Browse our alternative selection. If not, play safe and enjoy our classic untouchable range. Driving or not feeling like drinking tonight? Our selection of alcohol-free cocktails is bound to please.

### Flavours That Travel Far

Our liquid love flows, but our selection of solids is equally impressive. Our generous selection of light bites is defined by a unique combination of ingredients from around the world, perfect for globetrotting foodies. Described by some as ‘a frenetic festival of flavour’, we have something for everyone. Check out the best of the land, sea and garden, with favourites like nigiri, falafel and gyozas among our popular crowd-pleasers.

### The ‘Old School Funny’ cocktail

Aged in Lustau barrels for 5 years, this popular, bold cocktail with Italian flair is a bound to please. Made with red vermouth and Campari, with a dash of Bombay Sapphire gin and topped off with Tawny Port and Amontillado sherry, this cocktail transports you instantly to a sunny Italian terrace. Want to carry on the old school fun later on? No problem, this favourite is also available to take away in a large cocktail flask.
