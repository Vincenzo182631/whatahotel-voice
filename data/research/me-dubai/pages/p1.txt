## Sinuosity and contemporary design in the first hotel designed by Zaha Hadid

Located in the heart of Dubai's iconic Burj Khalifa district, this hotel is an attractive architectural gem that blends seamlessly with the spectacular Opus building. Designed by the visionary architect Zaha Hadid, ME Dubai embodies her innovative approach, where the fluidity of space converges with the elegant geometry of nature. A gastronomic epicentre offering a variety of culinary and entertainment experiences.

An ambitious commitment to sustainability, art, and culture, where technology, personality, and the lifestyle of a unique destination converge. The rooms, exuding a rich sensory experience, feature two distinct themes. One is more avant-garde and cosmopolitan, inspired by the city's nightlife, while the other is warmer and more evocative, reminiscent of the vast desert surrounding Dubai.
