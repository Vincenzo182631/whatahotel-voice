### Exclusive benefits

If you book a ME+ suite, the superior level of service and facilities offered by ME+ at the ME Dubai provides exclusive benefits throughout your stay. Such as a Cocktail Experience in one of our signature restaurants, private check-in and a direct line with our Aura team for any reservations you would like to make inside or outside the hotel, à la carte breakfast included in your stay (per person and day) and the possibility of an early check-in at 12 midday and a late check-out until 4pm (subject to availability).

### Personalised service

The ME+ suites provide a personalised service that will make your stay at ME Dubai a unique experience. It offers VIP welcome amenities, priority reservations for our restaurants, spa and wellness services, and a turndown service every evening and bath preparation (on request).

### Unique experiences

Enjoy a private session with our Aura team and select one of the experiences that we have designed specially for ME+ guests at the ME Dubai. A workshop with our mixologist to make signature cocktails, a workshop with our chefs to prepare delicious desserts, a guided tour with our Aura team to learn about the architecture of the ME Dubai, or a free 45-minute session with our fitness instructor. Activities require a reservation and are subject to availability.
