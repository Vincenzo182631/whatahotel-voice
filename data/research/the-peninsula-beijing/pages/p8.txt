Exercise In Style The Fitness Centre item 2

The Fitness Centre is fully equipped with a wide range of cardiovascular and strength-training machines, each fitted with personal television monitors to keep you entertained and up to date during your workout. Maintain your regular routine or take advantage of the Fitness Centre’s expert personal trainers, who will help you achieve maximum benefit from your workout. If you are more inclined to a dip, The Peninsula Beijing’s heated indoor swimming pool is an oasis of light and warmth all year-round.

Fitness Centre Membership: RMB 30,000 / Year / Person; RMB 50,000 / Year / Couple; RMB 60,000 / Year / Family, suitable for two adults and one kid under 16 years old.

Personal training: RMB 680 / Session

Swimming class: RMB 680 / Session

More detail about our membership and personal classes please contact The Fitness Centre.
