#### The Curated Stay:

## Exclusive Experiences for Guests

At The Peninsula Beijing, your stay is more than a moment of rest—it’s a gateway to curated experiences that celebrate culture, creativity, wellness, and refined living. Designed exclusively for our in-house guests, The Curated Stay invites you to discover a collection of enriching activities and offers that transform your time with us into something truly unforgettable.

Guests can explore the rich traditions of Huang Ting through tea ceremonies and handcraft workshops, and enjoy private access to rotating exhibitions in the hotel’s art gallery, showcasing a blend of contemporary and local artistry.

Complementing these cultural offerings are restorative wellness rituals designed to soothe the senses and promote balance. For those seeking movement, a curated library of on-demand fitness classes offers flexibility and energy at any time. Together, these experiences transform your stay into a journey of inspiration, reflection, and renewal—designed exclusively for you.

## Explore, Discover and Delight: The Peninsula Kid’s Passport Adventure

Our youngest guests are invited on a playful journey through the hotel’s most iconic spaces. With interactive questions and guided exploration, every moment becomes a memory.

Available daily from 10:00 am to 6:00 pm

24 hours advance reservations required

## Timeless Journeys on Two Wheels: Explore Beijing with Peninsula Elegance

Glide through the storied streets of Beijing on bespoke Peninsula bicycles—designed for comfort, style, and effortless exploration. From the winding hutongs of the old town to the grandeur of imperial landmarks, each ride becomes a personal journey through history and heritage.

Complimentary for in-house guests daily between 8:00 am and 8:00 pm

1 hour advance booking required via Concierge

## Heritage Refined: Cultural Immersion at Huang Ting

Step into the soul of Chinese heritage with immersive cultural experiences. From tea rituals to artisan crafts, each session invites you to connect with tradition in a refined, modern way.

Available on every Friday from 4:00 pm to 5:00 pm

Limited to 20 guests per activity

## Savour the Season: Tastefully Curated at Jing Restaurant and Bar

Enjoy exclusive 3- and 4-course menus featuring the most beloved and seasonal dishes from our Michelin-starred Jing’s à la carte selection.

Available daily for lunch and dinner, simply mention your room number to indulge.

## Artfully Curated: Memorable Exhibitions at The Art Gallery

Enjoy exclusive access to our in-house gallery, where rotating exhibitions showcase a blend of contemporary vision and local artistry. Whether you're a seasoned collector or a curious observer, each visit offers a quiet moment of inspiration and reflection.

The Art Gallery is open to the public daily 24 hours at the third floor

Stay energised with weekly group classes and on-demand workouts. Whether you prefer yoga, Pilates, or a quiet stretch in your room, our fitness offerings are tailored to your pace and lifestyle.
