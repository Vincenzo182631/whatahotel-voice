# Shopping Arcade

## luxury retail redefined the peninsula shopping arcade

Nestled within Wangfujing Street’s alluring mélange of tradition and fashion, The Peninsula Beijing is the very definition of an extraordinary landmark, which has blossomed over time. More than 25 years have passed and The Peninsula Arcade has grown to three levels, housing over 30 time-honoured brands under one roof. Unravel the essence of bespoke shopping at The Peninsula Beijing.

### Location

No. 8 Goldfish Lane, Wangfujing, Beijing – The Peninsula Beijing

### OPENING HOURS

11:00 am to 9:30 pm
