# BEING THERE: An Invitation to Cross Cultural Artistic Discovery

Share

- share on Facebook
- share on Twitter
- share on Pinterest
- share on Weibo

BEING THERE unfolds throughout The Peninsula Beijing's public spaces and 3F Gallery, drawing on visual memories shaped by travel, observation, and artistic exploration. Through depictions of landscapes, city scenes, and everyday life, the exhibition reflects on the connections between people, place, culture, and memory.

Guests are invited on a journey of visual discovery, experiencing the unique character of different places and cultures through vibrant colours and diverse artistic expressions.

The exhibition features acclaimed Australian artists Bernard Ollis OAM and Wendy Sharpe AM FRSN. Through their distinctive artistic approaches, the artists transform landscapes, cultural encounters, and lived experiences into compelling works, creating a dialogue between Eastern and Western artistic traditions.

_The BEING THERE art exhibition is open to the general public and hotel guests daily 24 hours at the Lobby and 3F Art Gallery of The Peninsula Beijing._
