# THE PENINSULA BEIJING

The award-winning Peninsula Beijing is nestled in the heart of the Wangfujing district, adjacent to the Forbidden City and Tiananmen Square, offering guests a deep connection to the city’s rich cultural heritage. With its iconic Chinese architecture and renowned Peninsula service, the hotel has set the standard for luxury in Beijing. It seamlessly integrates traditional craftsmanship, artistic elements, and cutting-edge Peninsula technology, redefining luxury with innovation. Upholding the 159-year legacy of Peninsula Hotels, it celebrates both the group’s heritage and the cultural richness of Beijing. Drawing on local resources, the hotel offers guests a more immersive and enriching experience, making each stay an unforgettable cultural journey.
