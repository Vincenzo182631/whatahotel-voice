Signature Experiences, Bespoke Services Luxury Fleet at The Peninsula Beijing item 2

The Peninsula Beijing offers guests an exceptional travel experience with a distinguished fleet of luxury vehicles. This includes six BMW 7 Series saloons. Each vehicle has been meticulously customised to ensure the highest standards of comfort, privacy, and sophistication, providing seamless and stylish transport across the city.

**BMW 7 Series**

Enjoy elegant and comfortable travel across Beijing in one of The Peninsula’s BMW 7 Series saloons. Our chauffeured service offers seamless airport transfers and flexible private hire options.

Beijing Capital International Airport:

- One-way: RMB 1,280
- Return: RMB 2,560

Beijing Daxing International Airport

- One-way: RMB 1,900
- Return: RMB 3,800

For private hire, the BMW is available at RMB 1,900 per hour, with a minimum rental period of four hours.

\\* Advance bookings are recommended to ensure availability. All prices are quoted in RMB and subject to a 6% surcharge.
