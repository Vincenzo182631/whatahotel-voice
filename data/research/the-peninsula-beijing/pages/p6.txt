THE PENINSULA Beijing Indoor Swimming Pool item 2

The crystal-clear water of The Peninsula Beijing’s indoor swimming pool provides an oasis of light and warmth all year round. Guests are invited to take a refreshing swim or a healthy lap in the 18-metre pool, which is heated to 28 degrees celsius year round ensuring the utmost comfort for guests. Otherwise, simply enjoy the tranquil ambiance as you lie back on one of the plush surrounding deck chairs.

Children under 12 years must be accompanied by an adult while swimming
