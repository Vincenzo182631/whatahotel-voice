The award-winning Peninsula Spa is a tranquil retreat to revitalise your body and mind. Indulge your desires with modern and traditional treatments inspired by Ayurvedic, European and Asian healing philosophies. Beautifully styled Spa suites for couples create an ambience of privacy and peace, and thermal bathing facilities provide additional relaxation benefits. A refined Oriental Tea Lounge is the perfect place to reflect on your personal Spa journey.

## Additional Information

#### Arrival Time

You are encouraged to arrive 30 to 60 minutes prior to your appointment to change, relax and enjoy the facilities of the spa. Please understand that late arrivals may result in a reduction of treatment time. We recommend that hotel guests arrive at the spa wearing the slippers and robe provided in the room, and advise that valuables are left in the guest room safe.

#### Thermal Suites

Prior to your treatment we encourage you to enjoy the range of heat and cooling experiences which cleanse the body and skin, relax and soothe aching muscles and prepare your body and mind for the treatment experience to follow. These facilities are separate for male and female guests, therefore the use of swimwear is optional and of personal preference. The facilities include:

Crystal Steam Rooms, Saunas, Ice Fountains,
Lifestyle Showers Relaxation Areas,
Changing Rooms.

#### Health Conditions

Kindly advise us of any health conditions, allergies or injuries that may affect your spa experience when making your reservation.

#### Pregnancy

The Spa has specially designed treatments for expectant women and nursing mothers. Please allow the spa team to guide you in selecting which treatments are most suitable for you during this special time.

#### Cancellation Policy

A 50% cancellation charge will be incurred for any reservation not cancelled at least 24 hours prior to appointment. Cancellation within 12 hours will incur a 100% charge.

#### Gift Certificates

Gift certificates are available at the spa –an ideal gift for someone special.
