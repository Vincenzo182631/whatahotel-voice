# Welcome to Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara

## Hotel in Nara; a Crossroad of Tradition and Modernity.

Nestled on the western edge of Nara Park, one of Japan’s most famous scenic spots, is Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara. The Yoshikien area, where our Nara luxury hotel is located, is surrounded...See More by World Heritage Sites such as Kasuga Taisha Shrine, Kofukuji Temple and Todaiji Temple, and has been protected by efforts to maintain the area’s lush greenery to ensure a scenic and serene environment. The name of our 5-star hotel in Nara, Japan, derives from the “purple trunk and green leaves (Shikan Suiyou)” – scenic vistas that have been cherished in Nara since ancient times. Just like the name, guests can indulge in the history and traditions of the city when staying at our heritage hotel in Nara, as well as the mystical flow of time that surrounds it.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara?

The check-in time at Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara.

What are the parking options at Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara?

On-site self parking is available at Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara. The daily self parking fee is ¥2200.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara booking.

What is the closest airport near Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara?

The closest airport near Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara is Osaka International Airport - Itami (ITM). ITM is located approximately 47.8 KM from the hotel.

Does Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Shisui, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nara has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
