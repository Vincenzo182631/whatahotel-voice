# InterContinental Jakarta Pondok IndahDestination

Discover the lively pulse of Jakarta from our prime Pondok Indah setting. Savor the city's dynamic offerings, from bustling markets and cultural landmarks to water parks, golf courses, and museums. Uncover the diverse fabric of this vibrant metropolis.

## Things to do

### Pondok Indah Water Park

Jl. Metro Pondok Indah, Kota Jakarta Selatan, Jakarta, 12310

Escape the ordinary and dive into the exhilarating world of Pondok Indah Water Park. Located in Jakarta, this water park offers aquatic adventures for the entire family. From thrilling water slides that promise an adrenaline rush to relaxing pools where you can unwind, this park defines a perfect day out. More than an ordinary water park; it's a destination where families create lasting memories.

Learn more

### Pondok Indah Mall

Jl. Metro Pondok Indah Blok III B, Pondok Pinang, Kebayoran Lama, Jakarta, 12310

Nestled in the heart of Jakarta, Pondok Indah Mall stands as a beacon of retail excellence. More than just a shopping haven, it's an immersive experience where luxury meets diversity. Stroll through its expansive corridors filled with high-end brands, exploring fashion, technology and lifestyle offerings. Savor culinary delights and lively entertainment venues. The best part is that it’s only steps from our hotel, making retail therapy easily accessible.

Learn more

### Pondok Indah Golf Course

Jl. Metro Pondok Indah, Pondok Pinang, Kebayoran Lama, Jakarta, 12310

Step into a world of refined leisure at the Pondok Indah Golf Course, an 18-hole world championship course nestled in the heart of Jakarta. Meticulously designed to offer a harmonious blend of challenge and tranquility, this golfing oasis is surrounded by lush greenery and beautifully landscaped grounds. Enjoy a golf retreat near our hotel and escape the urban hustle and bustle of the city.

Learn more

### Museum Layang Layang Indonesia

Jl. H. Damyati No.37, RT.6/RW.1, Cilandak Tim., Kec. Ps. Mingg, Jakarta, 12560

Uncover the rich cultural tapestry of Indonesia through the captivating lens of kites at the Museum Layang Layang Indonesia in Jakarta. This unique museum showcases an extensive collection of traditional and contemporary kites, offering a deep dive into the cultural and artistic significance of this age-old flying art form. Stroll the exhibits and discover the intricate craftsmanship, diverse designs, and the stories behind each kite.

### Jakarta Outer Ring Road

Jakarta Outer Ring Road, Jakarta

Navigating the bustling urban landscape of Jakarta is made seamless with the Jakarta Outer Ring Road. As a vital component of the city's transportation infrastructure, this modern expressway ensures efficient commuting and facilitates easy access to various attractions, and 45-minutes to CGK Jakarta International airport. Connecting key areas of Jakarta, the Outer Ring Road not only serves as a practical thoroughfare but also showcases the city's dynamic urban development.

### Lebak Bulus MRT Station

Jalan Lebak Bulus Raya No. 45,

Conveniently located just 10 minutes from our hotel, Lebak Bulus MRT Station serves as a gateway to seamless travel throughout Jakarta. This modern transportation hub offers swift and efficient connections, allowing guests to explore the city's vibrant attractions with ease. Whether you're heading to business meetings, cultural landmarks, or shopping districts, Lebak Bulus MRT Station provides a central and accessible starting point.
