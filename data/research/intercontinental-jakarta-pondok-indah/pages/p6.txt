Experience the epitome of luxury with Club InterContinental services. Enjoy exclusive access to our lounge, personalized service, meeting room and added amenities. ## FAQs for InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah

Does InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah have a pool?

InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah has 1 outdoor pool.

Swimming Pool

It is set amongst an intricately designed and lush garden area. With views over Pondok Indah, the pool area is the perfect place to unwind, relax and also an ideal venue for an evening gathering under the stars with a dedicated event space available.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah for hotel guests.

Indoor parking is available at the hotel, spanning across 3 basement floors, with one parking slot allocated per room. For added convenience, an elevator is connected directly to the main the lobby or function room floors.

Parking fee: 0 USD per day

In and out privileges

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah has 2 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:00 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah?

Check-in at InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah is from 2:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah is conveniently located near Soekarno-Hatta International Airport (CGK), approximately 21.69 miles away (34.9 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah?

InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Pondok Indah Water Park - 0.06 miles away (0.1 kilometers)
- Pondok Indah Mall
- Pondok Indah Golf Course
- Museum Layang Layang Indonesia
- Jakarta Outer Ring Road
- Lebak Bulus MRT Station

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
