### An exceptional stay filled with thoughtful gesture

a day ago

My family and I had an absolutely wonderful stay at InterContinental Jakarta Pondok Indah. From the very beginning, Ms. Gati from the Reservations team assisted me with my booking so kindly and professionally, making the entire reservation process smooth and perfect. Upon arrival, Ms. Charlene made my check-in seamless, while the concierge team warmly welcomed me and my family and assisted with all our luggage. Shortly after entering my room, I even received a personalized welcome call from Instant Service.

I was truly touched by the thoughtful birthday surprises prepared in both rooms, complete with balloons, birthday messages on the mirrors, complimentary cakes, pastries, and fresh fruits. To make it even more special, both rooms I booked for two nights were generously upgraded complimentary!

A special thank you to Herdian from Concierge for kindly helping me arrange a wheelchair for my dad, Zibran at Sugar & Spice for the wonderful breakfast experience and thoughtful refreshments to go, and Ms. Thea at The Lounge, who welcomed my family and me wholeheartedly, offered complimentary pastries, and even remembered my favorite chocolate cake.

The rooms were spotless and comfortable, the food was wonderful, and every staff member made me and my family feel genuinely cared for. What impressed me most was the seamless coordination, sincere hospitality, and thoughtful little gestures that made my entire stay so special.

Luxury is not just about beautiful rooms, but how a hotel makes you feel. The entire team made my (super late) birthday stay and family time truly memorable. Thank you for going above and beyond and making me feel so appreciated. I left with a happy heart and beautiful memories, and I cannot wait to return!

### Disappointing experience during my latest stay

2 days ago

I have always had a great experience staying at Intercon Pondok Indah but the most recent stay was a bit disappointing.
To start with, being a platinum member, my room upgrade was below expectations. The room I got was smaller than the rooms I previously had. It didn’t had the best of the layouts. When you open the door, there is a serious risk of bumping into the wall if you are not extra careful.
Second, I ordered same day laundry service and got it the next day and that too after I called them to get my laundry.
Third, On the last day when I came back from my office, my room wasn’t made. There as no DND and nothing I activated that should suggest that my room should not have been made.

Not the experience I usually get.
