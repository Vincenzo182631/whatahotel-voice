# Hotel Policies

### Hotel Policies

Destination Fee

A daily destination fee of $30.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

- One welcome drink per guest at check-in, max two drinks per occupied room.
- $20 daily food and beverage credit, valid after 11am CST and redeemable at Spoonful Provisions, Moda Fare, Tejas Lounge, room service or pool bar.  Excludes purchases at Ruth's Chris.
- Premium Internet Access
- (2) Bottles of water at Check in
- 10% Discount at Dasa Spa
- 10% Discount on Self Parking
- 20% Discount Old Town Trolley Tours
- 20% Discount on Admission to the San Antonio Zoo
- 10% Discount at Box St. All Day
- 10% Discount on House Made Gelato in Spoonful Provisions
- Pressing of up to two garments per stay

Pet Policy

Grand Hyatt San Antonio River Walk is a dog-friendly hotel. The availability of dog-friendly rooms is limited, and a minimum of 48 hours reservation notice is required by calling +1 210 224 1234. Dog tags are required.

**FEES:**

Up to 5 nights: $100 per stay

More than 5 nights: $200 per stay

**WEIGHT LIMITS:** Only dogs weighing 50 pounds and under are permitted. Two dogs may be allowed in a room, provided the combined weight does not exceed 75 pounds.

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Firearm Policy

The safety and security of our guests and associates is of utmost importance to Hyatt. This policy is designed for the protection of our guests and associates and pertains to the presence of firearms on hotel premises.

Hyatt recognizes that guests or visitors may legally possess firearms for a variety of legitimate purposes. This policy is intended to create a safe environment for all guests and visitors by providing appropriate guidance over the custody of firearms on our premises.

Hotel premises are private property. Guests or visitors of a Hyatt hotel who lawfully are permitted to possess a firearm may bring such firearm onto hotel property for storage purposes only. Any guest or visitor who is in possession of a firearm on the hotel premises is personally responsible for abiding by all applicable federal, state and local laws with respect to firearms, in the jurisdiction where the hotel is located and ensuring that the firearm is:

- Unloaded
- Secured in a locked, hard-sided firearm container provided by the guest
- Securely safeguarded at all times in a guest room (or personal vehicle), except when transporting the firearm into or out of the hotel

Guests who fail to abide by this policy may be asked to leave the hotel premises. A blanket exception to the restrictions described in this policy statement applies only to law enforcement officers and designated military personnel, who are on duty and required to carry firearms in the performance of their duties. No exemption to this policy is allowed for private persons, even those licensed and permitted to carry a firearm, and/or where applicable, to conceal-carry a firearm.

Questions concerning this policy, or its implementation, may be addressed to Hyatt’s Global Security department.
