Best for:
Honeymooners, couples celebrating anniversaries, and multi-generational families—all of whom might have a penchant for golf—looking for privacy and timeless elegance
Location:
Overlooking Castle Harbour on the most exclusive end of the main island of Bermuda, nestled in an upscale residential enclave; intimate and away from the crowds, it’s a true retreat
Can’t-miss feature:
Exclusive access to Bermuda’s largest private pink-sand beach and a lush private golf course with sweeping ocean views
In a nutshell:
Rosewood Bermuda is a private paradise with timeless British charm. It's all whitewashed, stately grandeur, perched on a cliff of lush Bermudian greenery that overlooks the turquoise waters of Castle Harbour. The estate extends into a golf course famous for its undulating fairways and down to the island's largest private blush-pink beach. Universally recognized as the most exclusive private property on the main island, it seamlessly pairs classic country-club charm with a refined coastal retreat, where top-tier service is always the bottom line.

Rooms
Rosewood Bermuda’s biggest draw is, cheekily enough, that it is the biggest. Across 240 acres, its 68 rooms and 20 suites feature the largest average room size on the island—and dreamy ones at that. Beds with crisp white Italian linens face doors that open out to large furnished balconies overlooking an always-cerulean sea by day, and offering a cozy spot to soak in the melodic sound of tree frogs at night. Spa-like bathrooms with deep soaking tubs, modern tiling, and large windows that flood the space with twinkling ocean sunlight add charming coastal flair. Royal One-Bedroom King Suites feature open living rooms and fireplaces, while other king ocean, poolside cabana, and harbor-view rooms come fitted with large terraces. Each room offers attention to detail—the Rosewood standard—and the first of many things the team does for you is offer a signature Dark ‘n’ Stormy paired with a local rum cake, rooting you in Bermuda.
Thirteen event spaces provide al fresco entertainment for casual, formal, or buyout events. The utter privacy of the property enhances the singular feeling of being somewhere special with your travel group.

Food and drink
The archipelago itself serves as the inspiration for the gastro-scene at Rosewood Bermuda, where daily catches, coastal ingredients, and island-grown plants fill each plate. It’s a totally immersive profile of the area, presented seamlessly across seven distinct dining locations. Mirador, the Spanish word for “lookout point,” is the property’s oceanfront Mexican-inspired venue and a true can’t-miss experience. The restuarant's partnership with Patrón Tequila means guests can enjoy signature cocktails crafted from a unique tequila barrel profile unavailable anywhere else on Earth. Beyond the drinks, it offers true refinement in a relaxed setting—the complete embodiment of island living. At night, the spot transforms into a vibrant social hub where guests soak in the ocean views alongside fresh Mexican-influenced seafood bites.
Only guests and members get access to the Beach Club Restaurant, the daytime social epicenter, where you can expect fresh oceanfront options like seafood, sushi, and seasonal barbecue, all on a private beach. For classic Italian fare, Sul Verde’s lunch and dinner menu offers a vibrant change of pace, alongside the Grille Room for pub classics.
The open-air Conservatory Bar is the perfect place to let time drift by over afternoon tea or cocktails, watching the sky turn from a brilliant blue to cotton-candy pink amidst mosaic tile and palm trees. But to know Bermuda is to know that rum is the name of the game here. Tucker’s Bar serves up a mean Rum Swizzle and is conveniently located next to Island Brasserie—the elegant dining choice for fresh seafood, premium cuts, and an equally indulgent setting for a decadent bite. Inside, the room is adorned with an 80-foot-long collection of canvas murals, originally commissioned in 1965 by Pan Am founder Juan Trippe for the exclusive Sky Club in Manhattan’s Pan Am Building. “Come for the swizzle, stay for the swagger” was a directive I did not need to be told twice.
Amenities and experiences
Full-service luxury means endless amenities, and the private offerings at Rosewood Bermuda will keep you bopping around the property in search of the next great activity. The estate features a pristine, jaw-dropping championship golf course and driving range, a private pink-sand beach, four stunning swimming pools (including an adults-only pool), tennis and racquet sports courts, and a beach club fitted out for water sports like complimentary snorkel cruises, paddleboarding, kayaking, and sailing. You can also sign up for a guided hidden gems tour, a coral garden snorkel, or a curated rum tasting.
Rosewood Bermuda also intentionally makes Bermudian culture the cornerstone of the guest experience, highlighted by an exclusive cultural partnership with the
Masterworks Museum of Bermuda Art
alongside bespoke island exploration excursions. Traditional Gombey troupes (a native Bermudian art form combining dance and music) also bring the property to life; you can witness this legendary, multi-generational Bermudian tradition firsthand during weekly performances on the Croquet Lawn. It’s a mesmerizing blend of colorful masquerade, acrobatic dance, and rhythmic drumming. The team also offers guided tours of historic forts, centuries-old churches, and artist-in-residence programs that dive deep into the rich history of Bermuda.

What to do nearby
A complimentary resort shuttle delivers you anywhere you need to go across the 240-acre estate—offering enough private amenities to keep you captivated for a lifetime. However, an itch to explore the local scene is entirely valid, and a scenic 20-minute taxi drive will bring you straight to Hamilton, the buzzy harbor heart of the main island, or to any of the island's powdery
beaches
. The property offers a curated three-day itinerary called “
Unveil Bermuda
,” where they round up the best bits for you—each a tailored experience rooted deeply in local heritage and island identity. Or when in doubt, rent a boat or book an
ocean excursion
.
The backstory
Reopened in 2018 after being acquired by Rosewood Hotels, this luxury property, originally built in 2009, has been refreshed, revitalized, and redesigned with the freshest of concepts and the prettiest of pennies. While it's a newer build, it still possesses a traditional British-colonial aesthetic authentic to the island. Its crisp whiteness and marble interiors effortlessly juxtapose Bermuda’s tropical canvas, creating a picturesque country-club experience with a lofty ocean breeze. Everyone on the island knows Rosewood and its exclusive location—it is universally recognized as the premier luxury destination on Bermuda, complete with a touch of cheeky British charm.
Insider tip
There’s a real sense of pride Bermudians possess for their island, seen in everything from how pristine the roads are and how brilliantly bright their infrastructure is, to the genuine kindness each person exudes. “It’s a Bermuda-ful day” slips off the tongue in an extremely unironic, painstakingly easy way here. Take a ride with the property’s team to Hamilton and talk their ears off—zipping between the island’s green enclaves among mopeds and bright pastel houses on a panoramic ocean drive—before heading to
Mamma Mia
for a legendary fish sandwich,
1609
for a sunset margarita, or a secret snorkel spot to see hogfish around a shipwreck.
Final considerations:
Family-Friendly:
Yes; there is a dedicated pool and a kids club, making it incredibly easy to find space for little ones.
Accessibility:
Contemporary design across the common areas and rooms provides wheelchair access and foundational accessibility features. Full details can be found
here
.
Wellness:
A state-of-the-art fitness facility and curated treatments at Sense Spa are available, each of which integrates the island’s rich botanicals and natural environment.
