### Wholesome Pleasures

A blend of locally sourced grilled vegetables and greens, drizzled with the finest local organic oil. This dish celebrates wholesome eating, offering rich natural flavours and guilt-free indulgence, packed with nutrients.

### Grill and Stone

Masterfully grilled Menorcan Angus Sirloin and Red Veal Chop are some of the favourites on the menu. Others prefer an authentic stone oven-baked pizza, crisp to perfection with fresh, local ingredients. Pair these with a carefully curated selection of local and international wines, meticulously chosen to complement our flavours. A natural gastronomic experience, crafted with care and expertise.
