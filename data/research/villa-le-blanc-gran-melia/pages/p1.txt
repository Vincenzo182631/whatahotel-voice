## The first grand luxury hotel in Menorca, a white stone refuge facing the Mediterranean

Located on the seafront, close to the best cultural spots and crystal-clear coves, this hotel in Menorca promises to take maximum care of every detail to bring together surroundings, relaxation, gastronomy, and exceptional experiences in the same enclave. A magnet that draws all attention to the authenticity of the island, its culture, and its beauty.

Located right on the beach of Santo Tomás, Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá stands as the first grand luxury hotel in Menorca. Conceived as a prototype hotel in terms of efficiency and environmental responsibility, it is inspired by the island's vernacular architecture for its exquisite interior design, where light floods the spaces through large arches. A window open to the Mediterranean that invites you to enjoy the tranquillity and sophistication of Menorcan culture, and whose garden reaches the very doors of the sea, integrating into the natural surroundings of the island's south coast. This hotel is a member of "The Leading Hotels of the World".

### Kids club

At Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá, there is a tailor-made place for the little ones: an exceptional games area, decorated in natural colours and based on alternative educational methods, where children can let their imagination run wild. It offers natural light and is divided into several small corners: a bed with comfortable cushions for resting, a reading area, a mat with dynamic games, activities that encourage concentration, attention and movement. Children under the age of 5 years must be accompanied by an adult. (Check opening days/times with the hotel).
