# Menorca, unseen and talayotic

## Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá, Menorca - Santo Tomás Spain

,regionOfInterest=(2732,1820))

Connect with the destination during your stay at Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá, Menorca - Santo Tomás, Spain

## An ancestral history amidst secret landscapes

Experience an introduction to the Menorcan identity adapted to every profile. As part of the “El arte del buen vivir” collection at Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá, this is a refined and unconventional guide to the island. From meetings with local producers and vineyards to natural landscapes and historical sites, the experience is adapted to offer an authentic look at the past and present of Menorca.

Menorca is revealed through a unique combination of culture, landscape, and tradition. From prehistoric remains to scenes of local life, the island offers multiple ways to be explored. Between talayots, rural estates, hidden coves, or marés quarries, each tour is configured in a personalised way. Connect with a legacy recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage site, integrated into a natural environment of great beauty.

- Duration: 6 hours.
- Number of people: Please consult the hotel.
- Things to keep in mind: The experience is fully customisable according to preferences. The pick-up time at the hotel must be chosen. The transfer has vehicles with a maximum of 8 seats, with a cost of 740€ (VAT included) each.
- What is included? Private transfer (round trip), guided tour, and transport in a Land Rover Defender. Itinerary adapted with extra details upon request.

,regionOfInterest=(2016,1512))

This experience takes you to discover Menorca through tailor-made itineraries, travelling across the island from north to south. On board a Land Rover Defender, explore settings ranging from cliffs and virgin coves to rural landscapes and spaces linked to local tradition.

Accompanied by a professional guide, you will be able to enter private estates, visit local producers, discover natural sites, or explore historical remains, always with the flexibility to adapt the route to your interests, at a leisurely pace and in connection with the Mediterranean lifestyle.

Designed to contemplate Menorca from a more personal perspective, this experience proposes a journey as intimate as it is enriching. Whether through its nature, its culture, or its traditions, connect with the most authentic essence of the island and enjoy an experience designed to your measure, difficult to forget.
