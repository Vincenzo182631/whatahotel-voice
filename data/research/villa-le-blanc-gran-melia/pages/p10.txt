# The artist's inspiration

## Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá, Menorca - Santo Tomás Spain

,regionOfInterest=(1920,1080))

Connect with the destination during your stay at Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá, Menorca - Santo Tomás, Spain

## Traditional sailing and contemporary art

Navigate through crystal-clear waters while watching the sunrise from a traditional Menorcan llaüt. Visit the Isla del Rey with a guided tour of the Hauser & Wirth gallery and its surroundings, admiring works by artists such as the sculptor Eduardo Chillida. Feel the harmonious connection between the Menorca community and the island's natural landscape.

Thanks to its privileged geographical location, Menorca boasts majestic landscapes, environments of pure serenity, and the untouched beauty of its crystal-clear waters. Delve into its unparalleled charm with an experience that is part of “El arte del buen vivir", a collection by Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá for those who value authenticity and refinement. Navigate the waters of the Mediterranean on a journey to Isla del Rey, where contemporary art is showcased in the prestigious Hauser & Wirth gallery.

- Duration: 4 hours.
- Number of people: Up to 5 participants.
- Things to consider: Book at least 3 days in advance. Subject to availability and weather conditions.
- What is included? Private round-trip transfer, boat trip on a llaüt, and museum visit.

,regionOfInterest=(1941,1296.5))

Using a private transfer, begin the activity at the distinguished natural port of Mahón. After admiring the unique physiognomy of the port, set course for the Isla del Rey on a traditional local boat, letting the slow rhythm of the sea guide the journey.

Accompanied by an expert guide, experience a unique visit to the Hauser & Wirth gallery and discover artistic proposals from all over the world. In addition, you have access to the small permanent exhibition dedicated to the sculptor Eduardo Chillida, in recognition of his extraordinary reception.

Next, visit the old hospital on the Isla del Rey, an 18th-century building that has been carefully restored and is of great heritage value. With your private transfer back to the hotel, an elegant experience that combines art, history, and Mediterranean lifestyle comes to an end.
