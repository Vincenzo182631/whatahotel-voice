# The pleasure of llaüt sailing

## Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá, Menorca - Santo Tomás Spain

## The beauty of the Mediterranean from the sea

Fall in love with Menorca’s turquoise waters aboard a Menorcan llaüt. An exclusive and very special experience where luxury and the local culture take centre stage thanks to Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá and its collection "The art of good living". A unique opportunity to admire the beauty of the island in a breathtaking natural setting with a thrilling itinerary that you can enjoy together with delicious culinary options.

Picture yourself sailing at 11 knots aboard a traditional boat that used to be a fishing boat, a benchmark in the world of sailing. Book a llaüt exclusively for you and enjoy activities such as snorkelling and paddle surfing in the middle of the sea, stopping at some of the best beaches in the south of the island. Cala Mitjana, Cala Macarella, Cala Macarelleta, Cales Coves... each one more special and each one more beautiful. Menorca hides endless magical and beautiful places ready to surprise you.

- Duration: Half or full day.
- No. of people: please check.
- Please note: Book at least 48 hours in advance, subject to availability.
- What does it include?: Llaüt ride with skipper and equipment for water activities.

,regionOfInterest=(2732,4096))

With a tour designed to suit your preferences, you will enjoy a one-of-a-kind experience sailing along the coast of the island in a luxurious Menorcan llaüt. A classic and elegant boat with the quality, comfort and exclusivity you deserve; a true gem for lovers of traditional boats, featuring different spaces for your use and enjoyment.

Book our exclusive llaüts (Marco Polo I and Marco Polo II) with different features to suit your needs. Sail the sea setting your own itinerary with a duration of half or full day, and visit Menorca’s wonderful and hidden spots.

Relax under the sun's rays on the large sun deck at the bow and enjoy activities such as snorkelling and paddle surfing in beautiful coves such as Cales Coves, Cala Mitjana, Cala Macarelleta and Cala Macarella. It will be the perfect moment to enjoy nature and connect with it.

Finally, surrender to the magical blue colour of the Mediterranean Sea while you enjoy a picnic on board to satisfy your 5 senses. Our Executive Chef will offer you a curated and succulent gastronomic proposal of gourmet products, wines and local cheeses to savour aboard the llaüt.
