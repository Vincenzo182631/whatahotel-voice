## Villa Le Blanc Gran Meliá, Menorca - Santo Tomás Spain

## An experience designed to your liking

Taste typical Menorcan products accompanied by the best masters, take part in their elaboration, and discover the island’s most emblematic workshops. Delve into the history and savoir faire of many generations, in an enriching experience with plenty to choose from. An immersive journey through the senses.

Become the protagonist of this experience where we will return to the beginning, to the product itself before it has been worked on and to the professions of yesteryear that remain an intrinsic part of Menorcan culture.

Participate in an experience where you can discover first-hand the island’s signature products together with their true artists and masters.

Discover more about their elaboration, the traditional techniques used or the tools that, albeit sometimes reinvented, remain the basis of the typical creations.

An exclusive and personalised route suited to cater to your tastes, where you can choose to immerse yourself in the tradition of an artisan cheese-making, learning about its production and enjoying local delicacies, visit a Menorcan wine and vermouth cellar with an exquisite tasting included, enjoy a tasting of the island's artisan beers or take part in an oil or saffron tasting.

Unique and iconic places where you can learn more about how products are manufactured and even help make them with your own hands. You will also be able to taste some of them, admiring the great variety of local produce of the best quality that the island of Menorca has to offer.

A fantastic way to get to know the local culture and gastronomy, and do it the way it used to be done: with time, dedication and effort.
