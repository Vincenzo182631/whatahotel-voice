# About Miraval Austin

Austin, Texas is unlike anywhere else—and so is Miraval Austin.

We’ve taken the cool, creative character of this vibrant city and embedded it deeply within our one-of-a-kind, 360-degree approach to wellness. In every corner of our peaceful property, there’s no doubt you’re at a Miraval Resort—and no question you’re in Austin.

## Mindfully Miraval

Welcome to Miraval Resorts. Our luxurious wellness destinations are designed to help you create a life in balance through mindfulness. With pioneering programs, exclusive spa treatments, nourishing cuisine, and world-class wellness specialists, Miraval Resorts wants you to live your best life—whatever that means to you.

## How It All Began

In 2003, The Crossings opened as a spiritual oasis for curious, health-conscious people and businesses. This little corner of the Hill Country quickly evolved into a retreat where bright minds and deep souls could convene, share ideas, and learn about and from each other. In 2019, the Crossings was reborn as Miraval’s second wellness resort. We like to think that the spirit of the original vision lives on at Miraval Austin.

## Our Hill Country Haven

Nature is nurturing. At Miraval Austin, we’re surrounded by it. Flowering bluebonnets blanket rolling hills, juniper trees bloom with bright berries, and Cypress Creek babbles through our farm. Guests can wander along meandering trails that hug the hills, while intentional architecture makes our natural surroundings easy to appreciate from within the built environment.

And because we’re located in the Balcones Canyonlands Preserve, this pristine natural environment is guaranteed by law to stay that way. Of Miraval Austin’s 220-plus acres, 170 will never be developed. That means endangered birds like the golden-cheeked warbler, which nest here (and only here) each year, can continue to make our oasis their own.

## The Spirit of Austin at Miraval

Miraval Austin is tucked away in the rolling hills of the Balcones Canyonlands Preserve—but you can feel the buoyant energy of Austin throughout the resort. Of course, that’s intentional. From our architects to our designers to our chefs, our team consciously chose each detail in alignment not just with Miraval’s mission, but with the vibrant culture of Texas’ creative capital.
