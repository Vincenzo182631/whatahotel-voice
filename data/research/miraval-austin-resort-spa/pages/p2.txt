# Life in Balance Spa

Our award-winning Miraval Life in Balance Spa draws on its nurturing setting within the Balcones Canyonlands Preserve. We lend a personal approach to wellbeing, where humans take care of humans through energy and physical touch.

## Before you plan your spa visit

Spa appointments fill up quickly, and last-minute spa availability cannot be guaranteed. 4-6 week advance reservations are strongly recommended.
