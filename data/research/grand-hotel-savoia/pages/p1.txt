## In Italy’s moststylish Alpine destination

### GATEWAY TO THE DOLOMITES

Sophia Loren posing for paparazzi at the 1956 Winter Olympics; David Niven in _The Pink Panther_; the roar of vintage cars around hairpin bends… A few flashbulb moments from Cortina d’Ampezzo’s glamorous past. But this is a place where the golden age of travel never ended, and the town continues to make history. For over a century, Grand Hotel Savoia has enjoyed a ringside seat, offering the inside track to one of Italy’s most beautiful mountain destinations.

## Your home from home inCORTINA D'AMPEZZO

### OUR BEDROOMS

At the Grand Hotel Savoia, we like to make guests feel as cosy and looked after as possible. Rooms are inspired by the traditional mountain chalet – snug and wood-lined with the sense of adventure just outside. The Grand Hotel Savoia may date back to the early 20th century but its 132 refurbished rooms and suites are designed with a clear sense of contemporary luxury. We have a variety of rooms for all occasions – from family breaks to romantic getaways and outdoor adventures. In each, the warm elegance of parquet flooring is combined with luxurious fabrics and fittings, mixed with individual details and views out over the town and mountainscape. Hi-tech facilities will keep you connected while the warm, characterful design gives a unique sense of place.

## Aprés ski at theSPECTACULAR

### DINE IN STYLE

A timeless setting in the Dolomites, temples of the most traditional Italian hospitality, places of consecration of quality raw materials, the restaurants and bars of the Grand Hotel Savoia will charm you with their impeccable style and culinary excellence.

The 1224 Terrazza is the exclusive open-air lounge where you can regenerate after a day on the slopes, enjoying the breathtaking view of Cortina and the Ampezzo Valley comforted by one of the best Italian coffees or a hot chocolate made with an exclusive selection of cocoa. The evening can continue to discover the refined cuisine offered by the Savoy restaurant and end at the 1224 Lounge Bar, where all the senses are tempted by refined cocktails and the rarefied atmosphere of the piano bar.

## Rejuvenate at theSENSATIONAL

### SPA IN STYLE

A sanctuary dedicated to wellbeing, relaxation and the balance of body and mind, the Savoia SPA is the ideal place for complete rejuvenation, allowing you to indulge in precious moments of total relaxation.

A delicate balance combines the natural surroundings, with the energy of the Dolomites, and treatments based on the skilful use of water, steam and heat. Pamper yourself with a range of exclusive beauty and wellness treatments designed to rejuvenate and restore perfect harmony.

Savoia SPA has chosen Comfort Zone products for their conscious and sustainable approach to skin and the environment.

Savoia SPA is the perfect retreat after a busy day spent on the ski slopes or exploring Cortina and the Dolomites.

## Discover theEXTRAORDINARY

## In the heart ofthe Dolomites

### CORTINA D'AMPEZZO

With its awe-inspiring peaks, and mountains considered to be the most beautiful in the world, Cortina is famed as the Queen of the Dolomites.

The Valle d’Ampezzo attracts visitors all year round. In the cold season, a pure white blanket of snow makes Cortina perfect for skiing and winter sports; while during the rest of the year, the mountain and the valley offers both sunshine and breath-taking landscapes. Explore Cortina and discover its luxury, boutique shops, countless leisure activities and unmissable selection of fine food and wine.
