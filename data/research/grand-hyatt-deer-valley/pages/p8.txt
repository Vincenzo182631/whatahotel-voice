## Parking

### Valet Parking

Valet parking only; self-parking is not available. Restaurant guests may receive complimentary valet parking with validation for designated time periods, which vary by restaurant and dining occasion. Please see below for current valet parking rates and restaurant validation options. For oversized vehicles or trailers, please contact the Front Desk team for assistance. Restaurant guests must obtain validation to receive applicable parking rates.

The daily price will increase to $55 effective January 1, 2027. Restaurant parking, currently complimentary, will cost $10 from January 1 through March 31, 2027. Complimentary parking will resume on April 1, 2027.

Getting around

Property Shuttle

We provide a complimentary shuttle for hotel guests daily to and from Park City's Main Street. The shuttle operates from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. During periods of lower occupancy, shuttle service may operate on an on-demand basis. Please contact the front desk for the current shuttle schedule during your stay.
