## Elevated Excellence At A Deer Valley Resort

As the flagship hotel of Deer Valley East Village, we are your basecamp for a grand adventure near Park City. Enjoy slopeside access to world-class skiing and effortless entry to Jordanelle Reservoir for fishing, paddleboarding and family fun. Our slopeside resort boasts breathtaking views, an onsite spa and an expansive pool for supreme relaxation and an escape from the ordinary.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Embrace modern mountain elegance, where contemporary design meets rustic charm. Our luxury hotel rooms, suites and residences are thoughtfully designed to showcase the stunning natural beauty of the Rocky Mountains surrounding Deer Valley, from the generous windows to the colors and textures. Lavish perks include fireplaces, kitchenettes and balcony views of Jordanelle Reservoir.

## EXPLORE

Meetings & Events: Designed to Impress

Grand Hyatt Deer Valley is your premier venue for corporate and social gatherings of any size, from intimate celebrations to large-scale conferences and product launches.

- Built for Large Events: 30,000 square feet of versatile space, including a garage-style entrance for large displays, stages or vehicles.
- Turnkey Solutions: State-of-the-art audiovisual support, bespoke digital signage and expert event planning services.
- Flexible for any Occasion: Seamless transitions from breakout sessions to elegant outdoor receptions.
- Dedicated Entrances: Private access and escalators for convenience and exclusivity.

Whether hosting a board meeting or a grand celebration, we provide the perfect setting to elevate your event.

Salute to Service

As a qualifying active duty, Reserve, National Guard, retired military, a disabled veteran, or actively serving Department of Defense civilian, enjoy a preferred rate in one of our Salute to Service rooms. In addition, we are pleased to provide a dedicated concierge lounge for Salute to Service guests, discounted Deer Valley Resort ski day passes, discount on the resort fee, and 20% off food and beverage (excludes alcohol).

Book a Residence at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley

Welcome to elevated mountain living at The Residences at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley. Offering an exclusive retreat beyond traditional hotel rooms, these private sanctuaries feature expansive layouts, full gourmet kitchens, spacious living areas with fireplaces and private balconies with breathtaking mountain or reservoir views. Enjoy the comforts of home with the impeccable service of a world-class resort, plus ski access to Deer Valley’s expanded terrain, an exclusive private lounge, après-ski experiences and premium amenities designed for extended stays and unforgettable escapes.

Family Activities

Settle into the winter side of Deer Valley, where family stories unfold against snow-covered peaks and cozy mountain surroundings. Days are filled with snowy adventures, scenic outings, and time spent together by the fire, while thoughtful in-room touches bring moments of surprise and delight. Indoors, the Nexus Lounge and bright gathering spaces invite connection through games, crafts, and movie nights that turn time together into something lasting.

Skiing at Deer Valley

This past winter, our Deer Valley hotel unveiled the largest ski expansion in North American history. With 4,300 acres of ski terrain, 202 runs and 31 lifts—including the East Village Express, a gondola linking the Village to Park Peak—the mountain experience has more than doubled in size. At our slopeside resort, guests can carve their way down the 4.8-mile Green Monster, explore five miles of terrain from valley to summit and enjoy reliable powder thanks to 300 inches of annual snowfall and a state-of-the-art automated snowmaking system. From new lift access to reimagined villages, Deer Valley’s Expanded Excellence initiative promises an alpine adventure.

### Sushi by Scratch Restaurants: Park City

Sushi by Scratch Restaurants brings Chef Phillip Frankland Lee’s signature omakase experience to Grand Hyatt Deer Valley in an unexpected and intimate setting. Seatings are offered at 5:00 PM, 7:15 PM, and 9:30 PM, Wednesday through Sunday. Reservations are strongly recommended.

## Deer Valley East Village

PreviousNext

- Grand Hyatt Deer Valley is the flagship hotel of Deer Valley East Village, the most ambitious alpine development ever undertaken in North America. Thoughtfully designed from the ground up, East Village is where world class skiing, elevated living, and all season adventure come together in a way the industry has never seen before.

At full build out, this modern mountain village will include more than 1,000 private residences, from custom estate homes to luxury ski in ski out condominiums. It will also offer 250,000 square feet of curated retail and dining, along with 68,000 square feet dedicated to wellness and recreation. Just steps from our doors, Deer Valley Resort is undergoing the largest terrain expansion in modern ski history, adding 3,700 new acres, 16 new lifts, and more than 130 new trails across four expansive peaks.

As the premier hotel at the base of this historic evolution, Grand Hyatt Deer Valley offers unmatched access to the newest chapter in Deer Valley Resort’s legacy—a visionary alpine destination redefining what it means to stay, ski, and explore in the Wasatch.

### We Are Pet Friendly

Your dogs are welcome to come along for mountain adventures at our slopeside resort. Please notify the hotel of your furry friend’s arrival before your stay. You’ll enjoy the use of branded bowls, beds and much more, including a dedicated dog run on-property. Items are available for purchase. For the safety and comfort of others, guests are asked to keep dogs on a leash in all public areas. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

#### Pet Fees

- 1-7 nights: **$150** _/ STAY_

- 7-30 nights (Additional Cleaning Fee): **$150** _/ STAY_

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  75Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 100Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.

### Resort Fee

A daily resort fee of $40.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.
