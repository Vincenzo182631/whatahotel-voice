Experience the ultimate mountain getaway in our Deer Valley hotel rooms and suites. Choose from 381 spacious accommodations, including 26 premium suites with private balconies, kitchenettes and cozy fireplaces. Each hotel room is crafted for relaxation, featuring oversize layouts, separate living areas with sleeper sofas and spa-like bathrooms. Inspired by modern mountain design, our rooms offer large windows with exclusive breathtaking views of Jordanelle Reservoir and the surrounding mountains for the ideal Park City hotel room stay.
