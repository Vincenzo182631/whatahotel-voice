# About Agaci Spa + Wellness Experience

### A New Chapter of Wellness in Deer Valley

Rooted in the restorative beauty of the Wasatch Mountains, Agaci Spa + Wellness at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley is an elevated sanctuary designed for stillness, restoration and connection. Now expanded, Agaci offers newly unveiled spaces, thoughtful amenities, immersive wellness experiences and personalized treatments inspired by nature and time-honored healing traditions. From restorative massage and results-driven skincare to couples' rituals and mountain recovery, every experience is designed to bring body and mind back into balance.

**Hours:** Monday–Sunday \| 9:00 AM–6:00 PM

**Location:** First Floor, North Side of Grand Hyatt Deer Valley

**Phone:**(435) 731-4552

**Email:** AGACI.Spa@hyatt.com

## RESTORE. RECONNECT. RENEW.

### Signature Massage Therapies

Slow down and surrender to treatments designed around your individual needs. Agaci's massage therapies range from deeply calming experiences to targeted recovery treatments for tired muscles after a day in the mountains.

### Facial Rituals

Personalized to your skin’s unique needs, Agaci’s facial rituals pair advanced skincare with restorative care to hydrate, replenish and renew. From customized treatments to collagen-focused, sculpting and revitalizing experiences, each facial is thoughtfully designed to restore balance and reveal healthy, radiant skin.

### Couples Experiences

Reconnect through treatments created to be shared. Agaci's couples' experiences pair personalized therapies with moments of intentional stillness, from side-by-side massage to the signature Mountain Soul Ritual featuring a calming foot soak, body polish and synchronized massage.

## BEYOND THE TREATMENT ROOM

### Sanctuary Lounge

**Co-Ed Relaxation Lounge**

A peaceful retreat designed for quiet reflection before or after your treatment. Settle into a moment of stillness, sip warm tea and embrace the calm surroundings as your wellness journey begins—or lingers a little longer.

### Ember Oasis Sauna

**Dry Heat Sauna**

Inspired by the comforting glow of embers, the Ember Oasis Sauna surrounds the body in soothing dry heat, helping ease muscle tension, support circulation and encourage deep relaxation. Unwind before your treatment or return afterward for a restorative moment of warmth and renewal.

### Cascade Ritual Showers

**Experience Showers**

Complete your thermal journey with a multisensory water experience designed to awaken the senses and restore balance. Contrasting temperatures and soothing rainfall provide an invigorating complement to the warmth of the sauna, leaving the body feeling refreshed and renewed

## WELLNESS THROUGH MOVEMENT

### Resort Fitness

Keep movement at the center of your stay at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley with our state-of-the-art fitness center. Whether preparing for a day on the mountain, maintaining your routine while traveling or complementing your spa experience, the fitness center provides a dedicated space to move, strengthen and recharge.

Pair movement with an Agaci recovery experience for a more complete approach to mountain wellness.

### Outdoor Heated Pool & Hot Tubs

Step outside and let the mountains set the pace. Grand Hyatt Deer Valley's year-round heated outdoor pool and hot tubs offer an inviting place to unwind surrounded by the alpine landscape. Start your morning with a refreshing swim or settle into the warmth after a day exploring Deer Valley for an effortless extension of your wellness experience.

### BE STILL. BE CONNECTED. BE ONE.

Agaci is more than a place to receive treatment. It is an invitation to slow down, reconnect, and return to yourself.

Whether you're joining us for a restorative massage, a shared couple's ritual, post-mountain recovery or an entire day devoted to wellness, discover a sanctuary rooted in the balance between earth, self and sky.
