# Dining

Savor the unexpected at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley. Our culinary and cocktail destinations redefine Park City’s dining scene with bold partnerships and elevated experiences. At Hidden Ace, speakeasy-inspired cocktails are crafted in collaboration with Franky Kurt Maldonado—recognized by North America’s 50 Best Bars—bringing world-class mixology to the mountains. Sunspell Pool + Provisions features signature drinks developed with Salt Lake City’s legendary Water Witch, a James Beard Award nominee and local institution. From refined American classics at Remington Hall to family-friendly après in the Living Room, every venue celebrates Utah’s vibrant flavors with creativity and flair.

## Sushi by Scratch Restaurants: Park City

Please note: Sushi by Scratch Restaurants: Park City is open exclusively to guests ages 21 and over.

Sushi by Scratch Restaurants brings Chef Phillip Frankland Lee’s signature omakase experience to Grand Hyatt Deer Valley in an unexpected and intimate setting. Tucked discreetly within the resort, this chef-driven counter offers a limited number of coveted seats, creating a highly personal journey through new-wave nigiri and thoughtfully composed bites from both land and sea. Guests are invited to slow down, settle in, and let the chefs guide the experience through an artful, free-form interpretation of the traditional sushi counter, punctuated by playful surprises and elevated takes on familiar favorites. Seatings are offered at 5:00 PM, 7:15 PM, and 9:30 PM, Wednesday through Sunday. Reservations are strongly recommended.

## The Living Room

Enjoy an afternoon of indulgence, elegance and alpine warmth with our High Chocolate experience. Step into the refined embrace of our Living Room, where afternoons unfold in an atmosphere of effortless luxury. High Chocolate is more than a treat—it’s a ritual, a moment of indulgence framed by the crackling warmth of our oversized, cozy fireplace, the soft melodies of live piano music, and breathtaking soaring views of the terrace and the snow-capped Uinta Mountains beyond.

Reservations required at least 24 hours in advance.

## Double Blacks

The perfect start and end to your day! Nestled next to the ski lockers with outdoor seating and cozy fire pits, Double Blacks transforms from a bustling morning coffee spot to an inviting après-ski haven. Enjoy hot chocolates, including Buttercream or Mexican, spiced ciders, and indulgent bites like Truffle Mac n Cheese, Croque Monsieur, and S’mores. Whether warming up or winding down, Double Blacks is your go-to destination for comfort and flavor.
