# Hotel Policies

Ski Boot Policy

For the comfort and safety of all guests, we recommend avoiding wearing ski boots in the hotel's public spaces whenever possible. Ski boots can be slippery on certain indoor surfaces, so we encourage guests to use caution when transitioning between outdoor and indoor areas during the winter months.

Resort Fee

A daily resort fee of $40.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

- Premium WIFI
- Nespresso & Tea in room
- 24 HR Fitness Center & Classes (seasonal)
- Hydration Stations
- (2) reusable water bottles
- Ski & Bike storage
- Board/Family Games and Nexus Lounge Game Room access
- Seasonal non-alcoholic Beverage at check-in
- Local & Domestic Calls (no access fee)
- Scheduled shuttle to main street Park City
- Seasonal Discounted tickets to Utah  Olympic Park
- Local/National Newspapers
- Lobby entertainment- seasonal

Pet Policy

We are delighted to welcome your dog to Grand Hyatt Deer Valley. Please inform us in advance of your pet’s arrival. During your stay, we provide branded bowls and beds, with items also available for purchase, plus access to a dedicated dog run on property.

For the comfort and safety of all guests, dogs should be settled and silent by 9 p.m. Dogs are not allowed in our dining outlets or spa or on the pool deck, except for service animals, and must be on a leash in all other public spaces. Please note that housekeeping will not enter your room if your dog is present, even if the dog is in a crate or cage.

**Cost / Fee Breakdown**

The pet fee is $150. This fee applies for any length of stay up to seven nights and is nonrefundable. For stays longer than seven nights and up to 30 nights, an additional $150 cleaning fee will be charged (for a total of $300).

**Restrictions Breakdown**

- Guests are not charged a pet fee for service animals.
- A cleaning or damage fee will still be charged if a service animal damages the room.
- Service animals are allowed in any room type on any floor.
- Weight limit: 75 lbs
- Two dogs may be allowed in a guestroom, provided the combined weight of both dogs does not exceed 100 lbs.
- Dogs should be settled and silent by 9 p.m.
- Dogs are not allowed in our dining outlets, spa or pool deck, and must be on a leash in all other public spaces.
- Housekeeping will not enter the room to provide service if the dog is in the room, even if the dog is in a crate or cage.

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Rollaway Bed Policy

Rollaway beds are available in select guest rooms only, as they cannot be accommodated in all room types due to space and safety requirements. A fee of $50 per night, plus applicable taxes, applies to each rollaway bed. As availability is limited, we recommend contacting the hotel directly prior to your arrival to confirm whether a rollaway bed can be accommodated for your reservation.

Package Handling Policy

Any items shipped or delivered to the hotel will be associated with a fee and charged to your guest room. To retrieve a delivered package, please visit the Front Desk located in the lobby. For assistance with shipping, please contact the Concierge Desk, and we would be delighted to help facilitate your shipment.

Incoming Package Handling Fees:

- Envelope (~1 lb): complimentary
- Small (0–10 lbs): $10.00
- Medium (11–25 lbs): $15.00
- Large (25–50 lbs): $20.00
- Golf Bag – Large (25–50 lbs): $45.00
- Ski Bag – Large (25–50 lbs): $45.00
- Oversize Bags and Packages (50+ lbs): $50.00

Outgoing Shipping: Prices will reflect to all outgoing shipments, in addition to the cost of shipping. Shipping rates will vary based on the size, weight, and destination of the package.

To ensure correct shipping, storage and delivery to a guest, please address as follows:

Grand Hyatt Deer Valley

Attn: GUEST NAME / ARRIVAL DATE

1702 Glencoe Mountain Way Park City, UT 84060

Shipments are held for a limited number of days. If the package has not been picked up after 30 days, the package will be returned to sender, who will be responsible for all additional shipping fees. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.
