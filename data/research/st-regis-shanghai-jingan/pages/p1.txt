# Welcome to The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan

## Luxury Downtown Hotel \| The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan

The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan is situated on West Beijing Road in the heart of Jingan District, Shanghai’s cultural and business center. The elegant interiors at this luxury downtown Shanghai hotel create an oasis of calm, blending traditional English and Chinese palatial styles with elements of Chinese art and culture, such as calligraphy and handcrafted gold foil paintings. Adding to its visual appeal, the Jing'an Shanghai hotel’s public spaces showcase five glass sculptures by world-renowned artist Dale Chihuly. The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan features 491 exquisitely designed guest rooms, six distinctive restaurants and bars, the St. Regis brand’s signature Spa, and a fitness center. Its 1,400-square-meter Astor Ballroom includes a 90-square-meter LED wall projection system, offering guests an unparalleled experience during their stay at this 5-star hotel in Jing'an, Shanghai.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan. Self parking is available at The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan, with rates of ¥20.00 per hour, ¥160.00 per day, and ¥1120.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan is Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA). SHA is located approximately 16.2 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan have an Executive lounge?

Yes, The St. Regis Shanghai Jingan has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services.
