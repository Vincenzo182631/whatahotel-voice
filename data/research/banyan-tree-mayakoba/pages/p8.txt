## Banyan Tree Mayakoba

## Experience Beachfront Bliss in Riviera Maya

### Luxury. Nature. Culture. Connection.

Welcome to your dream getaway in Riviera Maya, where the Caribbean Sea meets lush mangroves, and natural beauty seamlessly blends with refined luxury. Our beachfront resort offers an elevated experience for travelers searching for relaxation, adventure, and unforgettable moments in a tropical paradise.

We invite you to explore our thoughtfully curated facilities and experiences that make our resort truly exceptional. Enjoy oceanfront dining, exquisite destination weddings, and eco-boat tours through the Mayakoba lagoon. Whether you are unwinding at the spa, participating in daily wellness activities, or discovering the resort on a complimentary bicycle, every detail is designed to inspire connection, comfort, and exploration.

Let your journey begin!

## Luxury Accommodation

Banyan Tree Mayakoba invites you to rest in intimate, private villas and suites with a timeless design that blends harmoniously with our natural surroundings. Each one of our villas and suites features a private pool and spacious outdoor areas where you can enjoy absolute privacy and comfort. More than just a resort, we have created a true sanctuary to delight your senses in the heart of the Riviera Maya.

When it’s time to cool off, take a dip in one of our inviting pools, featuring family-friendly areas, peaceful adults-only zones, and a swim-up bar where you can enjoy your favorite drink without ever leaving the water.

## Things To Do For Families

Visiting Banyan Tree Mayakoba means embarking on a magical getaway surrounded by natural, cultural, and archaeological wonders. Here, explorers of all ages will find an incredible array of activities to delight the senses and strengthen their connections with each other.

### Complimentary Bicycles

Discover the natural beauty of Mayakoba. Ride through lush jungle trails, along scenic canals, and toward the beach or your next dining spot—enjoying a fun, active, and eco-friendly way to explore the resort at your own pace.

### Ecotour

Discover Mayakoba’s beauty on a complimentary ecotour through scenic canals. Glide past lush landscapes, while spotting local wildlife like birds, turtles, and crocodiles. Tours depart daily from the Main Lobby.

### Kid's Club

The Kid’s Club, located at our Sands Beach Club, is the most fun-filled corner of Banyan Tree Mayakoba. Daily activities guarantee adventures and discovery for our youngest guests under expert supervision.

### Nature Trail

Mayakoba’s three-mile nature trail is ideal for walking, jogging, or cycling through lush tropical landscapes. The path winds through mangroves and lagoons, with rest areas and bird-watching towers offering peaceful spots.

### Cooking Classes

Join our hands-on classes and explore Thai, Italian, Mexican, and Pastry & Desserts cuisine. Led by expert chefs, each session uses fresh ingredients and traditional techniques for a fun, flavorful experience.

## El Camaleón Golf Course

Designed by Greg Norman, El Camaleón offers a challenging 18-hole layout through lush greenery, freshwater canals, mangroves, and seaside views. Host to 16 PGA TOUR events, two LIV Golf dates, LPGA 2025 and other prestigious competitions, it’s a must-play course for golf enthusiasts.
