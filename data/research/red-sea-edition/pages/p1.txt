# Red Sea

Nestled on Shura Island within the visionary Red Sea Project in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, The Red Sea EDITION is where elemental beauty meets iconic sophistication. An extraordinary destination designed for connection – to unspoiled nature, modern hospitality and vibrant culture. Here, gracious, personalized service and immersive experiences re-imagine the possibilities for a balanced luxury stay. International and local taste makers will find inspiration everywhere from the buzzing Lobby and signature restaurant to the innovative Spa and electric Jiwa Beach Club. From tranquil mornings that restore the body to spirited evenings that inspire the senses, The Red Sea EDITION comes alive through an elegant balance of serenity and energy.

VIEW ROOMS & SUITES

## HOME AWAY FROM HOME

Retreat to a calm, tactile haven inspired by the softness of sand dunes. The 240 guest rooms and 53 suites at The Red Sea EDITION invite relaxation and reflection with soft lighting, natural oak and neutral tones. Custom furnishings, lightweight textiles, coral stone flooring and spacious baths provide a residential feel of elegant comfort. Panoramic windows, plunge pools and outdoor seating areas make the most of the expansive views.

## SENSORIAL DINING ON THE RED SEA

At the epicenter of Shura Island’s lively social scene, The Red Sea EDITION dining experience is an energizing expression of a health-forward lifestyle at any hour. During the day, indulge in sweet and savory treats from the snacks menu and a curated selection of tea-based cocktails. Be revitalized by refreshing afternoons pool or beach-side at JIWA Beach Club and Jiwa Terrace.

VIEW SPA & FITNESS

## WELCOME TO SOCIAL WELLNESS

At The Red Sea EDITION, health and fitness transcends trends to usher in a new era of social wellness. High energy workouts, dynamic training and next-gen spa rituals engage the island’s natural beauty, live music, and transformative mind and body optimization for a seamless fusion of energy, entertainment and recovery. RE\_SET and calibrate to regain clarity. Heal the physique and RE\_COVER energy. RE\_ENERGIZE and evolve to a better self.

From snow therapies and infrared saunas in the Recovery Dome to open water swims, martial arts and Aquafit board classes, The Spa and Gym at The Red Sea EDITION are dedicated to on-the-pulse programs that detox, harmonize, and boost physical and mental performance. Each element of the experience is sensorially enriched with transportive scents, nature-inspired interiors and music that sets the rhythm of the moment, enhanced by nutrient-rich menus and social spaces to reconnect with friends.

Omorovicza signature rituals are performed in the five private treatment rooms at The Spa at The Red Sea EDITION and luxury amenities include a beauty salon, private outdoor pool, and indoor/outdoor hot and cold plunge pools.

Unlock. Release. Soar. Repeat.

## WHERE THE WORLD MEETS THE RED SEA

Revolutionary fitness retreats. Ceremonies of a lifetime. The Red Sea EDITION plays host to region’s most exceptional gatherings. Each of the versatile spaces at The Red Sea EDITION seamlessly connect to the island’s natural beauty, adding authentic exclusivity to events of all kinds. Bespoke planning, elevated catering and integrated AV services are available whether the need is for an intimate boardroom, a luxurious ballroom or blissful beachfront.
