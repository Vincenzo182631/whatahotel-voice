# Getting Here

- From Red Sea International Airport (RSI)
- From King Abdulaziz International Airport (JED)
- From Prince Abdul Mohsin Bin Abdulaziz International Airport (YNB)

## From Red Sea International Airport (RSI)

## PRIVATE TRANSFER

Transportation from Red Sea International Airport (RSI) to The Red Sea EDITION is included in the room rate. For any alternate transfer routes or special transportation requests (such as from Yanbu or other locations), additional charges will apply. Contact reservations.redsea@editionhotels.com to secure your booking. The property can only be accessed via shuttle transportation from RSI Airport with a premium sedan as the default option. Airport shuttle service fee: 167 SAR (one way)

## From King Abdulaziz International Airport (JED)

## PRIVATE TRANSFER

The hotel team can coordinate a private transfer from JED Airport, offering a scenic approximately six-hour journey through the stunning Saudi Arabian desert. Head South and take the ramp to Yanbu Highway, keep left and continue on Route 5/Route 55M till The Red Sea Project exit.

## From Prince Abdul Mohsin Bin Abdulaziz International Airport (YNB)

## PRIVATE TRANSFER

The hotel team can coordinate a private transfer from YNB Airport, offering a 3-hour and 30-minute journey along the Red Sea coastline with breathtaking views of the Arabian landscape. Head East to Route 5/Route 55M till The Red Sea Project exit.
