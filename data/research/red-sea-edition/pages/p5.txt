# Central

## Central

A nod to New York City’s landmark Grand Central Station, Central invites guests to come and go as they please, enjoying a culinary crossroads of American classics and comfort food crafted with local ingredients and international artistry. The perfect balance of nourishing and indulgent, Central offers a refined three‑meal experience where breakfast is served whenever the mood strikes, pizza is a delicious experiment and grilling goes well beyond meat. Social by nature, the restaurant’s open kitchen and communal tables welcome individuals and groups to refuel and connect over flavorful plates and elevated handcrafted beverages in a lively, always-bustling environment.
