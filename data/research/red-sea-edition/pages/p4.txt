# Lobby

## Lobby

An elegant open-air space reminiscent of a wind-worn canyon enlivened by lush greenery, The Lobby at The Red Sea EDITION is the ultimate place to gather on Shura Island. The grand entrance evolves throughout the day—from a serene morning sanctuary with stunning ocean views to an intimate, candlelit lounge pulsating with music that sets the tempo for the night ahead.

A decadent selection of artisanal pastries, revitalizing tea infusions, fruit juices and zero cocktails tempt guests throughout the day. The Aperi-Tea service reinvents the classic presentation of tea and cake with a curated selection of tea-based cocktails and nutritious small plates that feel both indulgent and refined. Weekends usher in the Cocktail Reception featuring the Mixology Trolley and live music. The Lobby at The Red Sea EDITION is an energizing expression of a health-forward lifestyle at any hour.
