### W Lounge Details

## Design

Connect and experience the El Dorado legend through your senses. Bogota´s famous graffiti joins the golden threads to defy your imagination, taking you to the Guatavita lake where golden dust drops and the mythical story took place.

## Cocktails

Take a seat and enjoy where it all started, back in the days where the cocktail culture was just an upcoming trend in Colombia, our W Lounge was already crafting the best cocktails in town. Since then, our Bar program only got stronger and trendier. Curating local flavors and ingredients from all over the country, each cocktail connects you with our culture and history. Be prepared to enhance your senses and live the legend at our cocktail bar in Usaquen, Bogota.
