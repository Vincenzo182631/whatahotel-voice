# Play Your Way Through Bogota

## Experiences

Action awaits at W Bogota. Located in Colombia's capital, our hotel offers insider access to some of the most illuminating and coveted spots in South America. Shop your favorite retailers at Unicentro or stroll the streets of Usaquen, our business district with colonial-style buildings and local jewelry and crafts. Usaquen is a hotspot for restaurants, nightlife and nearby neighborhoods such as Parque...See More 93, Zona T and Zona G. Glimpse the past at Museo del Oro, our museum with artifacts and relics dating to the pre-Colombian era. Channel your inner creative at Museo Botero, where you find original pieces from the founder Fernando Botero as well as works from famous artists such as Picasso, Leger and Renoir. For more daring works, take a graffiti tour through the streets of La Candelaria. Bogota blends bold murals and historic architecture with modern restaurants and energetic entertainment.​See Less

## AWAY® Spa Details

Recharge in our 300-square-meter AWAY® Spa, our hydrotherapy spa situated adjacent to FIT at our Usaquen, Bogota, hotel. Inspired by the Colombian legend of El Dorado, where a chieftain would cover himself with gold and alight a raft to the center of Lake Guatavita, the spa’s modern décor welcomes guests with a gilded mix of abstract mosaics, emerald green hues, sculptural ceilings, and floral motifs. This enchanting fusion unites with west-facing city skyline views from both the reception and four treatment rooms, including a couple’s room.

More Information

Settle into your private space and select from a menu of signature massage, body, hand and feet, and facial therapies. Performed with local botanical spa products—crafted exclusively for the hotel from powerful plant extracts—each therapy is expressly tailored to you. Once your preferences are defined, our spa therapists will blend the prescribed essential oils in a shaker, creating a cocktail for the skin. Holistic spa packages are also available.

Prepare for your treatment or energize afterward with a soak in the nine-by-three-meter hydrotherapy pool. Wind down in the separate male and female steam rooms—or fuel your escape with one of our refreshing signature beverages.

Locker rooms are available for personal storage.

## FIT Gym Details

Work up a sweat at our hotel with a FIT gym in Usaquen, Bogota. From top Technogym cardio and muscle toning equipment and free weights to personal trainers, we have all you need to keep up your routine while away.

More Information

Your visit to Colombia's capital doesn't mean having to scrimp on your fitness routine. Overlooking Bogota’s skyline through oversized windows from its fourth-floor perch, FIT is an ultra-luxury fitness center offering hotel guests access to state-of-the-art aerobic and anaerobic equipment. Get moving on Technogym cardio machines with individual televisions and complimentary disposable headphones before cross-training with strength machines and free weights.

After your workout or busy day in Bogota, relax in the separate men and women’s steam rooms or in the lounge area, which—along with locker rooms and showers—is shared with the spa. And don't forget to help yourself to complimentary water, towels and apples.

Access to the fitness center is complimentary to in-house guests of W Bogota. Monthly membership, including access to the gym, steam room, and hydrotherapy pool is available for a fee. Please contact the hotel for more information.

Guests must be at least 17 years old to use the gym.

Hotel Activity Highlights

### In-Room Vinyl Menu

Put your spin on the scene with a spectacular selection of vinyl records and cocktails inspired and designed to elevate your senses, boost your room experience, and create the perfect mood. W Bogota’s In-Room Vinyl Menu - made in partnership with Sony Music is available in all rooms, featuring a curated list of 14 artists and cocktails inspired by each artist/genre.

### Living Room Sessions

Every Thursday, W Lounge at W Bogota becomes the stage for Living Room Sessions — a weekly celebration of Colombia’s freshest talent. From soulful singer-songwriters and electrifying DJs to genre-bending bands, it’s more than a concert — it’s a movement. Sip signature cocktails, savor gourmet bites, and immerse yourself in Bogotá’s creative rhythm.

### Coffee or Beer Tasting Experience

Explore our local craft brews, guided by experts who'll unravel the flavors, aromas, and brewing intricacies that make each one unique. Alternatively, embark on a coffee tasting journey, delving into various beans, roasting techniques, and brewing methods to uncover the rich tapestry of flavors that distinguish each cup – an experience that promises to awaken our senses and delight your palates.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Bogota have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Bogota offers a complimentary, 5:30 AM-10:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Bogota have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Bogota has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.
