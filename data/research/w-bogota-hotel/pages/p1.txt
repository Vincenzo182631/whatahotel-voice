# Welcome to W Bogota

## Embrace our unmistakable style in our Bogota Hotel

If you're looking for a stay in Colombia's capital, our luxury hotel in Bogotá is the ideal choice. Centrally located near embassies, restaurants, and boutiques in the exclusive Usaquén neighborhood, the hotel offers a vibrant destination close to premier shopping and entertainment options. Recharge at the state-of-the-art FIT gym or indulge in relaxation at AWAY® Spa. Celebrate every day at Jairo...See More Restaurant at W Bogota and every evening at W Lounge, our stylish signature bar. For meetings and special events, take advantage of more than 425 square meters of fully connected and dynamic event space.Experience the modern, visionary design of our 168 guest rooms, including 33 suites and the spectacular Extreme Wow Suite. Inspired by the legend of El Dorado, these spaces blend cutting-edge technology with the iconic W bed. Whatever/Whenever® service takes care of everything else. Plus, as one of the leading hotels in Usaquén, the property offers convenient access to El Dorado International Airport.See Less

## WIRED Business Center

Complete out-of-the-office work and communication in our WIRED Business Center, which offers a range of top technology and office services. Connect in our self-service WIRED Business Center. Situated on the third floor near the W Lounge, it offers two workstations, a printer, fax machine, High Speed Internet Access, and packing and shipping services—plus complimentary office supplies. And of course we’re here if there’s anything else you need.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Bogota?

The check-in time at W Bogota is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Bogota allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Bogota. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Bogota?

On-site parking is available at W Bogota. Self parking is available for $18.00 per day. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Bogota have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Bogota has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Bogota booking.

What is the closest airport near W Bogota?

The closest airport near W Bogota is El Dorado International Airport Bogota (BOG). BOG is located approximately 18.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Bogota have an airport shuttle to El Dorado International Airport Bogota (BOG)?

Yes, W Bogota has an airport shuttle to El Dorado International Airport Bogota (BOG).

Does W Bogota have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Bogota does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
