# The Best of Seoul: City Exploration and Ultimate Wellness

## Experiences

Stay with us at The Westin Josun Seoul, a hotel near Bukchon Hanok Village in Seoul, perfectly situated for exploring the city's historic landmarks. After a day of discovery, retreat to our City Athletic...See More Club for complete rejuvenation. Reenergize at our sun-lit pool and state-of-the-art fitness center, or unwind completely with our relaxing sauna, V-Spa, and signature 'On-gi' scrub program.See Less

## A Sun-lit Oasis for Total Renewal

Escape the city's vibrant energy and step into pure tranquility at The Westin Josun Seoul. Bathed in natural sunlight from a magnificent glass ceiling, our 25-meter indoor pool within the City Athletic Club is a sanctuary for your complete renewal. Experience the perfect harmony of eco-luxury design and world-class service as you dive into a deeper sense of well-being.

## City Athletic Club ‘Sauna’

Our Myeongdong hotel with a pool, spa and sauna is elegantly designed to transport you into a serene world of relaxation.

## City Athletic Club 'On-Gi' Program

Discover 'On-gi', our premium Korean body scrub program at the City Athletic Club. Blending traditional bathing rituals with modern wellness concepts, this personalized treatment uses warm, delicate touches to melt away tension and purify both body and mind.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Josun Seoul have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Josun Seoul offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Josun Seoul have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Josun Seoul has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The Westin Josun Seoul have an on-site spa?

Yes, V SPA is located on-site at The Westin Josun Seoul.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Josun Seoul?

Deoksu Palace, Gyeongbok Palace, Biwon Royal Secret Garden, National Folk Museum of Korea, Daehak-ro University Street - Korea's Off Broadway and more are nearby The Westin Josun Seoul.
