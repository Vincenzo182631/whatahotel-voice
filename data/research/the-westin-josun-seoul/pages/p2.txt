Do the rooms at The Westin Josun Seoul have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Josun Seoul have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Josun Seoul?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at The Westin Josun Seoul. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Josun Seoul?

Some rooms at The Westin Josun Seoul permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Westin Josun Seoul have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Josun Seoul have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at The Westin Josun Seoul have coffee/tea makers?

Do the rooms at The Westin Josun Seoul have hair dryers?
