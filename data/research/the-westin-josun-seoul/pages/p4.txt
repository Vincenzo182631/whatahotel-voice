## Dining

Savor a classic yet modern atmosphere infused with the hotel’s 100 years of history at each of our fine dining restaurants in Myeongdong. Enjoy an unforgettable dining experience, from sushi or Italian cuisine to an elegant afternoon tea, all served at The Westin Josun Seoul.

\[Aria Breakfast Operating Hours\]

Breakfast at Aria is served from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM, with last admission at 9:30 AM.On weekends and public holidays, wait times may be longer due to higher demand.Seated guests are allotted a 1-hour dining time limit.
