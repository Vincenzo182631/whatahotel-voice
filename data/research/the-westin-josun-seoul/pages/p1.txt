### Award-Winning Legacy in the Heart of Seoul

The Westin Josun Seoul has been awarded the 2026 TripAdvisor Travelers' Choice Award, recognized for delivering refined service, timeless elegance, and elevated comfort since 1914. Perfectly located in the heart of Seoul, near City Hall, Myeongdong, and Gwanghwamun, our \[luxury hotel in Seoul, Korea\], offers seamless access to the city's top attractions, shopping, and business hubs.

### Relax & Recharge With Urban Wellness

Relax and recharge at the City Athletic Club, open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM (closed on the first Monday of every month). Please note that the pool has dedicated maintenance breaks from 11:30 AM to 12:30 PM and 6:00 PM to 7:00 PM. Additionally, facility access is limited to one visit per day during weekends, public holidays, the summer peak season, and year-end holidays.

### Savor a Chef-Crafted Culinary Experience

Explore Seoul’s dynamic dining scene without leaving the hotel. Savor premium buffet selections at Aria, elegant Cantonese dishes at Hong Yuan, authentic omakase at Sushi Cho, contemporary Italian at Rubrica, and fine American dining with curated wine pairings at The Ninth Gate. The Lounge & Bar also offers an open-concept space for a relaxed drink or afternoon tea.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Josun Seoul?

The check-in time at The Westin Josun Seoul is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Josun Seoul allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Josun Seoul.

What are the parking options at The Westin Josun Seoul?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Josun Seoul. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for ₩30000.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Josun Seoul have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Josun Seoul has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Josun Seoul booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Josun Seoul?

The closest airport near The Westin Josun Seoul is Gimpo International Airport (GMP). GMP is located approximately 25.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Josun Seoul have an airport shuttle to Incheon International Airport (ICN)?

Yes, The Westin Josun Seoul has an airport shuttle to Incheon International Airport (ICN).

Does The Westin Josun Seoul have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Josun Seoul has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does The Westin Josun Seoul have an Executive lounge?

Yes, The Westin Josun Seoul has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
