Spa
The Ritz-Carlton Spa in Astana invites you to discover a personalized wellness journey crafted just for you. Experience a blend of traditional and modern techniques with a range of massages and treatments tailored to your individual needs. Nestled in the heart of Astana, our spa is a sanctuary of serenity, combining natural elements with expert therapeutic care. Indulge in the rejuvenating power of locally inspired rituals or explore advanced skincare treatments featuring renowned brands like ESPA and Valmont. Whether you prefer a single session or a curated package, The Ritz-Carlton Spa in Astana offers an exclusive escape into relaxation and renewal.

Featured Treatments
Traditional Body Bliss
Escape the fast pace of the city and find tranquility in a pampering treatment designed for two, performed side by side in our peaceful and luxurious couple’s suite. This serene experience invites you to reconnect and restore harmony to both mind and body. Enjoy a 120-minute journey that includes a full body exfoliation, an aromatherapy bath, and a soothing full body hot stone massage.
Summit of the Cervin
Experience the resolutely lifting and firming expert treatment. Valmont highlights its technical expertise in five steps, as a tribute to the 5th highest peak in the Swiss Alps. The results leave the skin lifted and smoothed and the face sculpted and plumped. Includes Valmont cleanse & tone ritual, exfoliation, lifting and sculpting massage, Regenerating Mask Treatment 90 minutes or 70 minutes.
Couples Retreat
Escape the futuristic pace of the city and unwind and reconnect, with this pampering treatment performed side by-side with a loved one in our peaceful and luxurious couple’s suite. This serene experience invites total calm as the mind and body return to harmony. Includes Full Body exfoliation, aromatherapy bath, full body hot stone massage 120 minutes or 90 minutes.
Spa Membership
The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Astana introduces an exclusive collection of memberships, inspired by the brilliance of precious gems — Emerald, Ruby, Jade, and Blue Diamond. Each tier offers elevated privileges, from preferred pricing on ESPA treatments to unlimited access to the state-of-the-art fitness center with Technogym equipment. Members are also invited to enjoy exclusive wellness experiences and events, discovering a refined journey toward balance, beauty, and well-being.

Frequently Asked Questions
How can I make a reservation?
Reservations can be made by calling the spa or by requesting a service online. We encourage you to reserve treatments at least one week in advance.
Who can guide me in choosing my treatments?
Our spa reservation department will help you plan the perfect spa experience, including the best order in which to enjoy your treatments.
Do I need to be a hotel guest to have a spa treatment?
Spa treatments may be booked by hotel guests or external guests.
Complimentary valet parking is included.
What time shall I arrive?
To enhance your experience, please arrive 30 minutes before your scheduled treatment. We will gladly provide a bathrobe, towels, slippers as well as toiletries.
What should I not bring to The Spa & Wellness Center?
It is not advisable to leave valuables unattended in public areas of The Ritz-Carlton Spa. We provide locker services for your convenience.
Cell phones and electronic devices must be turned off or on silent.
How many treatment rooms are available at The Ritz-Carlton Spa?
There are five treatment rooms plus one couple suite.
Can massages be performed during pregnancy?
Yes, massages can be performed from the fourth month of pregnancy.
Are the saunas and steam rooms separated?
Yes, our saunas and steam rooms are separately accessible for ladies and gentlemen.
Is there a minimum age for SPA treatments and using the Gym?
Guests must be at least 18 years of age to experience a SPA treatment and use Gym facilities.
Guests must be at least 14 years of age to experience facials.
Guests must be at least 18 years of age to experience massage and body treatments.
