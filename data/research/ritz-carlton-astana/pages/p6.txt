In Astana, a Vibrant City Awaits Discovery
Experiences
One of the first things that catches your eye the second you land in Astana is its unique architecture, whether it’s Norman Foster’s futuristic designs like the tent-shaped Khan Shatyr Entertainment Center or the magnificent domes of the Central Mosque. This unusual blend perfectly reflects the spirit of a young, vibrant capital. Our Astana hotel is conveniently located in the city center near many attractions, including Baiterek Tower, Astana Opera, and Nurzhol Boulevard. The hotel concierge can assist with crafting an itinerary that includes popular things to do in Astana as well as hidden gems of the city. For those seeking luxury shopping experiences, Talan Gallery offers a selection of high-end brands, while Mega Silkway provides an array of shops and boutiques catering to various tastes and preferences.

Featured Treatments
Traditional Body Bliss
Inspired by the city of Astana, this traditional body experience combines body exfoliation, a body wrap, and a full body massage to restore equilibrium, peace of mind, and provide a sense of deep relaxation. Integrating a tailor-made aromatherapy massage, this treatment leaves you with a renewed sense of well-being, feeling cleansed, revitalized, and deeply relaxed.
Summit of the Cervin
Using a combination of light pressure movements, Valmont's Peaks of Firmness treatment makes skin firmer and smoother, while the renowned Valmont Regenerating mask delivers deep nourishment. Using an incredible lifting and firming massage, the skin is stimulated and conditioned.
Couple Retreat
Escape the futuristic pace of the city, unwind, and reconnect, with this pampering treatment performed side by side with a loved one in our peaceful and luxurious couple’s suite. This serene experience invites total calm as mind and body return to harmony. Includes : Skin brush, exfoliating body polish, Body and head massage, aromatherapy bath for two.
Spa Membership
The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Astana introduces an exclusive collection of memberships, inspired by the brilliance of precious gems — Emerald, Ruby, Jade, and Blue Diamond. Each tier offers elevated privileges, from preferred pricing on ESPA treatments to unlimited access to the state-of-the-art fitness center with Technogym equipment. Members are also invited to enjoy exclusive wellness experiences and events, discovering a refined journey toward balance, beauty, and well-being.

Frequently Asked Questions
How can I make a reservation?
Reservations can be made by calling +7 7172 734 000 or by requesting a service online. We encourage you to reserve treatments at least one week in advance.
Who can guide me in choosing my treatments?
Our spa reservation department will help you plan the perfect spa experience, including the best order in which to enjoy your treatments.
Do I need to be a hotel guest to have a spa treatment?
Spa treatments may be booked by hotel guests or external guests.
Complimentary valet parking is included.
What time shall I arrive?
To enhance your experience, please arrive 30 minutes before your scheduled treatment. We will gladly provide a bathrobe, towels, slippers as well as toiletries.
What should I not bring to The Spa & Wellness Center?
It is not advisable to leave valuables unattended in public areas of The Ritz-Carlton Spa. We provide locker services for your convenience.
Cell phones and electronic devices must be turned off or on silent.
How many treatment rooms are available at The Ritz-Carlton Spa?
There are five treatment rooms plus one couple suite.
Can massages be performed during pregnancy?
Yes, massages can be performed from the fourth month of pregnancy.
Are the saunas and steam rooms separated?
Yes, our saunas and steam rooms are separately accessible for ladies and gentlemen.
Is there a minimum age for SPA treatments and using the Gym?
Guests must be at least 18 years of age to experience a SPA treatment and use Gym facilities.
Guests must be at least 14 years of age to experience facials.
Guests must be at least 18 years of age to experience massage and body treatments.
Ritz Kids
At The Ritz-Carlton, Astana, kids are encouraged to experience the city and its sights in their own way. In a playful manner, our young guests are introduced to the Ritz-Kids four pillars—Water, Land, Environmental Responsibility and Culture — through activities that are both exciting and educational. With diverse collection of activities, kids are engaged in storytelling, art and even food.
