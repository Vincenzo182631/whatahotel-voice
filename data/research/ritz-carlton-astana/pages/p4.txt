Where Dining & Design Come Together
Dining
Excite your palate with an array of local and seasonal dishes at our restaurants in Astana. Enjoy Italian-inspired cuisine, Kazakh flavors, and expertly crafted seasonal specialties, authentically prepared by our culinary artists. Experience fine dining in Astana at Mökki, savor seasonal dishes at Mökki Terrace, or relax with cocktails, afternoon tea, and lively evening gatherings at the Өzen Lobby Lounge.
Mökki Restaurant
Mökki Restaurant embodies elegance, offering a perfect combination of traditional Italian cuisine with a touch of modernity, carefully prepared by our Italian Chef. Commencing with a delectable breakfast and an expansive lunch selection, culminating in an exquisite dinner designed to elevate culinary experiences to new heights. In addition to culinary offerings, Mökki is a place of constant activity, designed to foster connections and enjoyment among guests. From delightful brunch gatherings to master classes and guest chef visits from all over the world. And with an extensive wine list curated by our sommelier, you can be sure to find the perfect pairing for your meal.
