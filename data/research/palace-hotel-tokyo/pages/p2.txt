# rooms & suites

## rooms & suites

Blissful to wake-up in, delightful to retreat to and irresistibly cozy in every way, our guestrooms range in size from an ample 45 square meters (485 square feet) to a generous 255 square meters (2,744 square feet), with every single one offering breathtaking views of the city’s most glorious gardens and the surrounding skyline.

Highlights of our luxury accommodations include spacious balconies and terraces, minibars so sleek you can shake, stir and sip without ever leaving your room, and open-style bathrooms with separate tubs and showers in which to soak, lather and repeat – complete with bath amenities by Bamford.
