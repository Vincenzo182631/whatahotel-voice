# FIND YOUR PERFECT ESCAPE

Dive into thrilling experiences at our 141-acre water park, relax on five miles of pristine beaches, and explore marine habitats with over 50,000 aquatic animals. Enjoy family-friendly pools, world-class dining, and luxurious spa treatments. Whether you're here for the excitement or tranquility, Atlantis offers something for everyone.

Paradise is brimming with adventures, explorations, and discoveries, offering unique experiences that make each visit more thrilling than the last. Dive into exclusive excursions and tours that showcase the beauty and excitement of this incredible destination. Whether you're seeking adventure or relaxation, every moment here promises unforgettable memories.

AQUAZEAL YACHT CHARTERS

AQUAZEAL can facilitate charter yachts ranging from 60 to 240 feet, accommodating up to 12 guests at a time, with well-seasoned and experienced captains and crew that provide exceptional service.

**To book a luxury yacht charter with AQUAZEAL call 242-603-4515 Ext. 46493 or email the office at** **bahamas@aquazeal.com**

NON-MOTORIZED WATER SPORTS

Looking for fun water activities for the whole family? Visit South Paradise Lagoon Beach for snorkeling, paddle boats, and other water sports. Paddleboards, water bikes, kayaks, masks and snorkels are available for rent.

**To Book, visit the Blue Adventures desk or call: 242.363.3000, EXT 55319 or 69511**
