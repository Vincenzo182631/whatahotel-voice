The Largest Waterscape in The Bahamas

A WORLD OF AQUAVENTURE

Splash into 20 million gallons of fun at Aquaventure, the biggest water park in The Bahamas. With 141 acres of high-speed slides, a rapid river, and sparking resort pools, every twist and turn offers something unique. Whether you're a thrill-seeker ready for heart-pounding slides or simply looking to unwind under the tropical sun.

Every stay at Atlantis Paradise Island comes with unlimited access to Aquaventure.

Hours subject to change, sunlight permitting.

LEAP OF FAITH\\
\\
The Leap of Faith offers a daring and adventurous almost-vertical drop from the top of the iconic Mayan Temple. This 60-foot body slide propels riders at tremendous speed through a clear acrylic tunnel submerged in a lagoon filled with amazing marine life. The Leap of Faith height requirement is 48" for all participants.\\
\\
Learn More CHALLENGER SLIDE\\
\\
Challenge a friend to a high-speed splashdown! Guests can race each other down the twin high-speed Challenger slides of the Mayan Temple, and then check their speeds on the clock at the bottom to see who won! The Challenger Slide height requirement is 48" for all participants.\\
\\
Learn More THE SURGE\\
\\
Starting at the top level of the 120-foot Power Tower, this inner tube ride features a camel back drop into a cavern, then quickly twists and turns downwards into the white-water rapids. The Surge height requirement is 48" for all participants.\\
\\
Learn More THE RAPIDS RIVER\\
\\
Surrounding the Power Tower is the Rapids River, the longest river in the Bahamas.\\
\\
Through the river's mile-long meandering loop are wave surges, elevations, and drops of up to 7 feet, including a section with powerful white-water rapids, all pushing riders through the scenic landscape.\\
\\

The Coral Pool

Located at The Coral Pool, the private Coral Cabanas offer families or friends a shady and relaxing place right next to the sparkling blue and white poolscape. Enjoy fun water activities and a swim-up bar.

Accommodates up to 6 people.

The Reserve at Baths Colonnade Cabanas\\
\\
Exclusively for guests of The Cove.\\
\\
The Reserve at Baths Colonnade is a refined poolside sanctuary created exclusively for registered guests of The Cove. Designed as an elevated extension of The Cove experience, this private retreat offers enhanced privacy, elegantly appointed cabanas, and dedicated service in a beautifully sun kissed setting just moments from Cove Beach.\\
\\
Guests are welcomed with a hosted, escorted arrival and invited to settle into a thoughtfully curated experience that unfolds throughout the day. Each cabana includes premium stocked coolers and elevated amenities allowing for effortless transitions between poolside relaxation and the shoreline. Signature touches, such as a welcome beverage and chilled towel ritual, set the tone for a day of comfort and ease.\\
\\
Culinary tasting moments, seasonal fruit, and Champagne service enhance the experience from midday through golden hour, culminating in a sunset sparkling wine offering. Every detail is designed to feel intentional yet relaxed—offering a polished, indulgent retreat that reflects the spirit, style, and sophistication of The Cove.\\
\\
_Non-refundable within 3 days of arrival._\\
\\

Mayan Temple

Elevated above the Mayan Temple Pool, and only steps away from the Mayan Temple and its thrilling slides, these cabanas are in the center of the action. Let your Cabana Host take care of the details while you take The-Leap-Of-Faith.

Accommodates up to 8 people.

The Current

Quiet, secluded, and private cabanas located on The Current Riverbend offering a small bathing area. Reserve your cabana and get lost in the ambiance while spending the day ensconced in comfort.

Accommodates up to 10 people.

Poseidon

Four family-friendly cabanas are conveniently located overlooking the new Poseidon's Playzone water-play fort and Ripples pool. These cabanas are the perfect, shady home base for families to enjoy the day exploring.

Accommodates up to 6 people.
