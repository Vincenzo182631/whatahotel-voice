## Experience _timeless_ elegance at Fairmont Le Château Frontenac

Enjoy stunning views of the St. Lawrence River and Old Québec, a UNESCO World Heritage site, while savoring locally focused cuisine and cocktails at one of our four restaurants. From the European-style charm of our 5-star guest rooms to restorative treatments in the spa, our landmark luxury hotel promises an unforgettable stay that will inspire you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Le Château Frontenac?

Check-in is from 4:00 p.m. and check-out is until noon.

How can I get to Fairmont Le Château Frontenac from the nearest airport?

Taxi services are available from the airport at a flat rate of $41.40 (day price) and $47.60 (night price - between 11:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m.) (\*prices may vary) Private transfer can be arranged with the concierge team. It is possible to drive from the YQB Airport to the hotel (approx. 20 minutes).

Is there a concierge service at Fairmont Le Château Frontenac?

Yes, the Concierge is located in the main lobby. The concierge team will happily arrange city tours, limousine transportation or restaurant reservations. Our Concierge team can be contacted by email at LCF.concierge@fairmont.com.

Is parking available at Fairmont Le Château Frontenac?

Yes, valet parking is $55 CAD per night, including in and out privileges. For over-sized vehicles (6’2 and higher) it’s $65 per night. Electric vehicle charging service : $30 additional charge.

Does Fairmont Le Château Frontenac have a swimming pool?

Yes, the hotel has a heated indoor pool with a spectacular view of Old Quebec. It also has a jacuzzi, a hammam and a sauna you can use to relax.

Where is Fairmont Le Château Frontenac located?

The hotel is ideally located within the walls of Old Québec, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Our address is 1 Rue des Carrières, Québec, QC G1R 4P5, Canada.
