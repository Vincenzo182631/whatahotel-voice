Why book?
Fairmont Le Château Frontenac is quintessentially Quebec City; the château-esque property is considered the most photographed hotel in the world, but step inside its gilded entrance and you’ll find world-class luxury and hospitality that extends from check-in at the original 18th-century lobby through to the award-winning restaurants and bars.
Set the scene
The historic property is perched within the walls of the old city, overlooking all of Vieux Quebec (a.k.a. Old Quebec) and the Saint Lawrence River below. Its convenient location is within walking distance to the city’s best bars, restaurants, and attractions, but its position within the Upper Town (essentially sitting upon a cliff) means that the hotel offers a quiet atmosphere come nighttime, affording travelers a good night’s sleep. The striking castle-like property draws all kinds of visitors, but don’t be surprised to find elegant wedding parties dotting the lobby—especially on weekends.
The backstory
The Château Frontenac is an essential part of the Canadian tourism industry. The property first opened its doors in 1893 after being commissioned by the Canadian Pacific Railway. Since, it has served as a stopping point for train travelers as well as an inspiration for many of the railway hotels that would open across Canada in the years that followed. Despite the later decline in trans-Canada train travel, the Château Frontenac continued to thrive as a destination hotel and Grand Dame of Quebec City thanks to its grand château-esque architecture, commitment to modern hospitality, and nods to the history of Quebec City and Canadian tourism as a whole.
The rooms
There are more than 600 rooms and suites at the Château Frontenac, and while each room follows a similarly cozy, muted color scheme and style, there are certain hidden (and obvious) Easter eggs that make each stay a little different. The property is over a hundred years old and the historic architecture extends into the rooms. You might find yourself in a corner room with vaulted ceilings or a more contemporary room with a window perch. Certain suites pay homage to relevant Canadian public figures (like the Trudeau & Trudeau Suite) as well as former hotel guests (like the Hitchcock Suite or the Suite Grace de Monaco), each of which boasts authentic artifacts and portraits of the room’s namesake.
Food and drink
The Château Frontenac boasts four restaurants and bars throughout the property with the standout being a tie between the tasting menu at Champlain and the afternoon tea service at Place Dufferin. Helmed by Chef Gabriel Molleur-Langevin, grabbing a table at Champlain is not always easy, and you’ll definitely want to book ahead. It’s also worth arriving early to grab a cocktail at the adjacent 1608 bar which is designed to look like a clock paused in time (and boasts an excellent dry martini). Weekend afternoon tea at Place Dufferin is also well worth making time for, but you’ll want to have a light breakfast as the traditional towering tea spread, complete with homemade scones, sandwiches, and sweets, is incredibly filling. Go ahead and order a glass of Champagne along with your tea and linger over the live music and beautiful views of the Saint Lawrence River below.
The neighborhood/area
Perched right at the edge of the Dufferin Terrace, the Château Frontenac is one of the best properties you could book if you want to be able to walk to the best restaurants and attractions in Vieux Quebec. The stylish, small plate Le Clan is quite literally right around the corner, while the charming cobblestone streets that Quebec City is famous for are steps from the hotel entrance.
The service
The Château Frontenac offers comprehensive hospitality that, while not white glove, will provide travelers with everything they could possibly need for a comfortable stay in Quebec City. Travelers looking for a bespoke experience should book Fairmont Gold which includes access to a private check-in, lounge area, and a Les Clefs d’Or concierge team who are known for making major requests happen—from helicopter tours to private shopping experiences in and around Quebec City.
Eco effort
The Fairmont brand is committed to eliminating single-use plastic, using efficient waste management, and partnering with major sustainable partners like the World Wildlife Fund and Sustainable Travel International, and that extends to the Château Frontenac and other historic railway properties across Canada as well.
Accessibility
The hotel includes a number of accessible guest rooms and suites, as well as accessible on-site parking and ramps, and doorways that provide 32" of clear width in the spa, swimming pool, and ballroom. Service animals are also welcome.
Anything left to mention?
Be sure to pack a swimsuit and make time for a dip in the indoor pool. There’s a somewhat hidden outdoor terrace area just beyond the pool with a small bar and lounge chairs that overlook some of the prettiest parts of Vieux Quebec, perfect for those visiting during the summertime.
