# Luxury Rooms and Accommodations at The Dunlin

## Check into modern coastal life

Experience the charm of Southern living in one of our 74 guest rooms and suites. With stylish and whimsical interiors by acclaimed designer Amanda Lindroth, South Carolina’s No. 1 Resort welcomes with a peaceful feel and a cottage-inspired aesthetic, featuring white-washed rooms and honey oak floors with mint green accents and textural linen fabrics. Outside, generous porches are the perfect place for morning coffee or a nightcap, offering a sublime oasis in the marshlands with expansive riverfront views.

## Guest Rooms

Cool breezes blow through The Dunlin’s inviting guest rooms, beckoning guests to sit back on their furnished porch to relax, listening to the whistles of Carolina wren. Measuring over 490 square feet, each room is decked with the essentials of Lowcountry living and a spacious sensibility, amplified by whitewashed tones and breezy linens. Accessible rooms available.

##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is The Dunlin located?** The Dunlin is located at 6000 Kiawah River Drive, Johns Island, South Carolina 29455, about 20 miles and a 40 minute drive from downtown Charleston.

**How far is the hotel from the airport?** Charleston International Airport is located 30 miles/45 minutes from The Dunlin. The Charleston Executive Airport is 16 minutes away from The Dunlin.

**Is The Dunlin a good fit for couples?** The Dunlin suits couples with river-view porches, Aster spa treatments, Southern dining, sunset pool time and quiet Lowcountry experiences.

**Is The Dunlin family-friendly?** The Dunlin welcomes families with connecting rooms and suites, larger living and dining spaces, farm experiences, outdoor adventures and family-oriented dining.

**When is the best time to visit The Dunlin?** The Dunlin welcomes guests year-round; the best time depends on preferred Lowcountry weather, wildlife, water activities and seasonal travel demand.

**Does The Dunlin have a pool?** The Dunlin has The Cove, a riverfront pool club featuring a heated pool, hot tub, poolside dining and cabanas.

**What experiences and activities are available at The Dunlin?** Guests can kayak, paddleboard, fly fish, take boat excursions, take an art class, play mahjong, bike, explore the farm, birdwatch and enjoy Lowcountry culinary experiences.
