# Connecting through the stories of coastal cuisineDine at The Dunlin

## A joyful celebration of soulful Southern cooking

With cool breezes and expansive views across the glittering Kiawah River just beyond the table's edge, dining at The Dunlin harkens back to life's simple pleasures. Fresh seafood from the waters just beyond accompanies produce and dairy grown from neighboring farms, creating the ideal menu for sunny days spent poolside, blustery fall days, or lingering dinners beneath the stars. Full of soul and infused with the comforts for which Southern food is known, this is elevated home-style cooking at its best.

## Linnette's

## A contemporary river camp with beautiful and comforting food

Linnette’s, the signature restaurant, pays homage to the heritage of Johns Island with familiar and comforting coastal cuisine prepared with a modern edge. Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, imagine a seafood-forward menu spotlighting the catch of the day from our local fishermen and produce that changes with the seasons. Cooked in our open kitchen, the wood-fired dishes merge charm and sophistication for a progressive approach to longtime favorite dishes from South Carolina.

### Willet Room

Relax to the gentle hum of the river, or mingle for some good conversation at The Dunlin's Lobby Bar. Open throughout the day, we invite you to pop in for a perfect iced tea or refreshing cocktail served with a side of Southern charm. This is where to sit if you'd like a light lunch, to try a rare whiskey, or to linger in the early evening with close friends over wood fire-grilled oysters and a Charleston Swizzle.

## Epicurean Experiences

## Coastal Culinary Journeys

Celebrate the cuisine that defines Lowcountry living. Engage with the traditions and heritage of South Carolina and life on the river via pop-up farm dinners, riverside oyster roasts, and outdoor movie nights with Southern snacks and local beer. Our community will join hotel guests for whole hog BBQs and snow cones from our summertime cart. Sharing craft stories and experimenting with customary flavors, Epicurean Experiences at The Dunlin offer unique insight into the heart of the Lowcountry.
