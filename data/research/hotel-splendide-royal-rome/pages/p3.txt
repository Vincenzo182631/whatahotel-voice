# Close to the Spanish Steps

#### 5 star hotel in Rome near Spanish Steps

Set between Via Veneto and the beautiful gardens of Villa Borghese, Hotel Splendide Royal, **5 star hotel in Rome near Spanish Steps**, is a converted 19th-century monastery. Each room of Hotel Splendide Royal, **5 star hotel in Rome near Spanish Steps**, is equipped with satellite TV with pay-per-view channels; some suites have a private terrace overlooking Villa Borghese.

Great care was taken and an enormous investment made in the renovation. The reception rooms on the ground floor are welcoming and beautifully appointed. The spacious rooms and suites in this **5 star hotel in Rome near Spanish Steps** feature Carrara marble bathrooms, antique furnishings, elegant fabrics and double-pane glass windows. About one third of the rooms have large walk-out terraces, most of which face the beautiful Borghese Gardens.

One of the most highly prized features of the hotel is its panoramic rooftop restaurant, Mirabelle. The restaurant features Mediterranean cuisine and offers dining on an outside terrace with a splendid view of the gardens. There is a bar adjacent to the restaurant where guests can relax after a hectic day of touring and shopping in the Eternal City. There are several private function rooms for small group meetings or private dining.

Hotel Splendide Royal Roma is the place you are looking for, if you're searching for **5 star Rome hotel Spanish Steps**.

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your electronic device (PC, tablet, smartphone, etc.) while you browse a website and that provide certain information. Web browsers allow you to exercise some control over cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies or block cookies from specific websites.
