## Inside the SPA Experience

### Luxury Spa Amenities

The SPA at Inn by the Sea features separate men’s and women’s relaxation sanctuaries, private steam rooms, experience showers, and your own private locker appointed with a plush robe and spa sandals. Complimentary beverages and thoughtful amenities help you unwind while you enjoy your treatment.

### Marine-Based Ingredients

Inspired by the natural beauty of Maine’s coastline, our spa treatments feature carefully selected marine-based skincare products rich in rejuvenating seaweed extracts, marine peptides, nutraceutical extracts, and pure essential oils. Combining time-honored traditions with modern skincare innovations, these ingredients help nourish, hydrate, and revitalize the skin.

### Massage, Facials & Body Treatments

From relaxing massages and rejuvenating facials to exfoliating body scrubs, The SPA at Inn by the Sea offers treatments designed to refresh the body and restore balance. Whether you're planning a spa day near Portland or adding a little indulgence to your coastal getaway, find the perfect way to unwind.

## What You Should Know Before Your Visit to The Spa at Inn by the Sea

_Your most common questions, answered in one place._

### How can I make an appointment at The Spa at Inn by the Sea?

We strongly recommend scheduling spa appointments before your arrival. Appointments may be booked online by calling 207-799-3144 ext. 811. All reservations are subject to availability and must be guaranteed with a credit card.

### What if I need to cancel my spa appointment?

Treatment times are reserved especially for you. We kindly request that cancellations or changes be made at least24 hours in advance. No-shows or same-day cancellations will be charged the full service fee. Group reservations are subject to a minimum 14-day cancellation policy as outlined in group contracts.

### What if I have special health considerations?

Please notify our Spa Concierge if you have high blood pressure, allergies, physical ailments, special needs, recent surgeries or medical procedures, or if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. This information may also be completed on your Personal Care Guide before arrival. Health considerations may affect your choice of services.

### How early should I arrive for my appointment?

We kindly ask that you arrive at least 30 minutes before your scheduled appointment to allow time for a relaxed check-in and enjoyment of spa amenities. To preserve the tranquil environment, please silence your cell phone upon arrival. A valid photo ID is required at check-in.

_Please note: Late arrival may result in a shortened service to avoid delaying the next guest._

### What if I am late to my appointment?

As a courtesy to other guests, late arrivals will receive only the remaining available appointment time and will be charged the full service fee.

### Can someone help me choose a treatment?

Absolutely. Our Spa Concierge is happy to help you plan the perfect spa experience tailored to your needs. Please share any medical concerns or therapist preferences during your reservation consultation.

### Is parking available?

The hotel offers complimentary valet and self-parking on-site.

### What should I wear?

Please disrobe to your comfort level. Undergarments are not required but may be worn if you prefer. Our licensed therapists are trained in professional draping techniques to ensure your comfort and privacy at all times.

### Should I shave before a treatment?

Shaving is not recommended before body exfoliation or hair removal services. Gentlemen are asked to shave at least 2 hours before a scheduled facial.

### Is there a minimum age requirement?

Minimum age for services is 16 accompanied by guest of 18 years or older. Spa services and amenities are reserved for guests 18 years of age and older.

### What is the cell phone policy?

In consideration of all guests, we ask that cell phones be turned off before entering The Spa.

### What types of payment does The Spa accept?

The Spa operates as a cashless environment and accepts all major credit cards.

### Is there a service charge?

A mandatory 22% service surcharge is added to all à la carte services and day spa packages. This surcharge is distributed to the spa staff who served you. Gratuities are optional and left at your discretion. All gratuities are retained in full by the service provider(s). Prices and services are subject to change without notice.

### Where can I keep my valuables during my treatment?

We kindly request that guests do not bring valuables into the spa or leave them in the lockers. The Spa is not responsible for loss or theft of personal items, including jewelry.

### Does The Spa have lockers?

Yes. For the comfort and convenience of all guests, only personal-sized bags are permitted in Spa lockers.

### Can I bring a group to The Spa?

Group spa reservations are booked separately from hotel meetings and event packages. If you are hosting an on-property wedding, meeting, or event and wish to include spa services, please coordinate through your Sales Manager, who will work with our Spa Group Coordinator to arrange your experience. To arrange group spa services, please contact spa@innbythesea.com.
