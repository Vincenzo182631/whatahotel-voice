Reviewed by
Alexandra Hall
Give us an overview.
Sprawling, sustainable, and stylish, this Cape Elizabeth property a 15-minute drive from downtown
Portland, Maine,
seamlessly blends topnotch service with laid-back chic. And it leans into its five-acre spot on Crescent Beach State Park with environmental programs, long-term commitments to restore the land it around it, and green design throughout. That means breathtaking landscaping and stunning marches double as a habitat for local wildlife as much as it does 7EV chargers, green energy (100% of the property’s clean electricity comes from solar), and composting programs for guests. And the Inn doesn’t merely accept dogs, it celebrates them; with kinds of spaces and treats for pooches, it’s one of the most canine-friendly high-end properties in New England.
Tell us about your room. What made it stand out?
Our (dog-friendly) beach suite offered privacy, soothing views of Sandy Crescent Beach, a private outdoor patio, a fully equipped kitchen, gas burning fireplace, steam shower and an extra-large bathtub, and a king-sized bedroom. To suites like these, the property delivers full lobster bakes, along with the rest of the menu from Sea Glass, its in-house restaurant.
The property has also recently added impressive newly designed garden suites and loft suites in the main house—many have two floors and dramatic views of the Atlantic, private decks, equipped kitchenettes, and bathroom suites with deep soaking tubs. Oversized windows and French doors fill each with plenty of light—the better relax and sink back into sterling blue and deep coral cushions, in patterns that manage to both energize and soothe.
Is there a charge for Wi-Fi?
Nope. Wi-Fi is free.
Drinking and dining—what are we looking at?
Sea Glass focuses on contemporary American cuisine but leans on local seafood dishes like Bangs Island mussels with green curry and grilled naan, salads laced with Atlantic Sea Farms Seaweed vinaigrette, and miniature lobster tacos with avocado crema that are easily the cutest (and some of the tastiest) specimens around.
And how was the service?
Swift, knowledgable, and personable yet professional.
Any other hotel features worth noting?
Its pristine lawn, dotted with Adirondack chairs, offers views of the coast, and its poolside patio is the perfect place to spend a lazy afternoon, glass of wine in-hand. Meanwhile, the LEED Silver-awarded spa is one of the most high-tech yet cosseting in the area: massage tables come with surround sound; and facials like the “Crescent Beach Custom” use sea-based enzymes and antioxidants to strengthen and repair skin.
Bottom line: Worth it? Why?
Undoubtedly. The property offers a rare combination of an incredible natural environment, topnotch hospitality, and urbane sensibility. And the beachside setting and overall dedication to relaxed but refined vacationing is unlike anything else you'll find in the Portland, Maine, area.
