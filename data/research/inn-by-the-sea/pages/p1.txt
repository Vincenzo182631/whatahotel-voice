# Oceanfront Luxury Resort in Maine

## Welcome to Fall in Maine

Inn by the Sea is located on a mile of sandy beach on the coast of Maine. Inn by the Sea's Maine Hotel offers sixty-two guestrooms and one or two-bedroom suites with private exterior entrances, making it the best resort in Maine. The Inn provides a sanctuary Maine Resort beach hotel for couples or family travelers who want to escape to the coast of Maine.

## Your Luxurious Maine Resort

Inn by the Sea is the perfect coastal escape, offering a blend of relaxation and refined comfort just steps from the beach. Spend your days enjoying the oceanfront setting, unwinding at our award-winning spa, or simply taking in the natural beauty of Maine’s shoreline. With thoughtfully designed accommodations—including pet-friendly options—every stay is tailored for comfort and ease. Discover why Inn by the Sea is a premier destination for a classic Maine beach getaway!
