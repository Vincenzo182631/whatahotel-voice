# Restaurants

### Enjoy our chefs’ International and Mediterranean Cuisine specially prepared from the finest local ingredients.

### Art Restaurant & Bar

### Every day from 7 AM to 10:30 AM, a high level of service is presented in our Art Restaurant & Bar. Enjoy a unique selection at the breakfast buffet and savor homemade delicacies and carefully selected ingredients..      If you are looking for a fine dining a la carte experience, Art Restaurant & Bar offers a variety of dishes prepared in a traditional way, using home-style recipes as well as international specialties.

### Peninsula Bar & Bistro

### Peninsula Bar & Bistro is situated just in front of the hotel, overlooking the Old Town of Šibenik.       The seaside swimming pool is surrounded by a large grassy area, making it ideal for relaxation. Here, you can enjoy refreshing cocktails and a casual bistro-style lunch while being caressed by the summer breeze.      Operates seasonally (weather permitting).

### Yacht Club Restaurant

### Yacht Club Restaurant is located by our majestic infinity pool. As well as the Pool Bar, it offers a variety of healthy meals and our rejuvenating signature cocktails. During the summer, guests can dine at the Yacht Club restaurant in the D-Resort Šibenik, with the offers of the freshest and most delicious seafood dishes.

# Bars

### Five well-placed bars offer a diverse and lovely ambience as well as live music performances.

### Lobby bar, Art Bar, Roof Bar by Moët & Chandon, Pool Bar, and Peninsula Grill & Bar are ideal spots for relaxation during the day or for spending a pleasant evening.

### Roof Bar

### If you are looking for an exclusive venue beneath the starry sky, our Roof Bar by Moët & Chandon is the perfect place for you. It serves you great music, a glass of champagne, and gourmet bites. Visit the Roof Bar by Moët & Chandon over the weekend and experience its pleasant vibe and extravagant ambience while enjoying the magnificent view of Šibenik.     Operates seasonally (weather permitting).

### Pool bar

### Pool Bar The Pool Bar is located just next to the infinity pool and it serves a wide selection of beverages and snacks during lunchtime. It is a perfect, sunlit spot for those who prefer spending time in the water or relaxing on a lounger with a refreshing drink in their hand.      Operates seasonally (weather permitting).

### Lobby bar

### The Lobby Bar is located near the hotel entrance. It is characterized by its intimate atmosphere and pleasant shades while offering fresh juices, healthy snacks, and smoothies. It is a great spot for an afternoon energy boost during a hot, summer day.

### Art Bar

### Positioned on the highest floor, Art Bar is a venue worthwhile your time, because it offers a glance at the nearby scenery. After dinner, you can enjoy our signature cocktails or aged liquor and cigars at the Art Bar Lounge while observing Šibenik’s panoramic view.
