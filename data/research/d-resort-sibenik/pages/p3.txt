## Primošten Beaches

### Primošten beaches

### Beaches with turquoise-colored, crystal-clear water overlooking the high seas of the Adriatic, Velika Raduča and Mala Raduča are true coastal gems. Both are located in the town of Primošten, widely famous for its stunning historical town center situated on a small, beautiful peninsula.

### Features:      The beaches offer all sorts of activities for their visitors. Bars & restaurants alongside the watersports, jet ski, and paddleboard rentals make them a worthwhile place for our guests who are looking to enjoy the Adriatic Sea at its best

## Beach Bar Shark Žaborić

### Beach Bar Shark Žaborić

### Beach Club Shark is the perfect spot for all people who want to enjoy a pleasant time with cold drinks and snacks while spending time dipping in the sea and absorbing the sun. Located in the small town of Žaborić, which is not so far away from our hotel, Beach Club Shark is an excellent and attractive place to visit.

### Features:     The place is very famous for its entertainment, which takes place during the afternoon and early evening hours. Apart from the wide choice of food and beverages, the place also provides its visitors with free comfy sunbeds. Watersports, jet skis, and paddleboard rental are also available.
