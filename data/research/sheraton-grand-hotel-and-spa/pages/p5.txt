# Experiences Sheraton Grand Hotel

## Experiences

Edinburgh is a city full of experiences, attractions and surprises. At Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, we provide the perfect base for you to explore everything the city has to offer. With close proximity...See More to Edinburgh Castle, you're just steps away from this historical icon. Whether you're travelling with friends, family, or on a solo trip, there are multiple things to do here. Spend time exploring the Royal Mile's luxury and boutique shops, or immerse yourself in architecture around the city's narrow, winding streets. For Harry Potter fans, you can't miss a trip to Diagon Alley - or Victoria Street for the muggles. As a spa hotel in Edinburgh with parking, we offer plenty of experiences on-site too. Strengthen your body and calm your mind in our state-of-the-art fitness studio, 19m indoor swimming pool with infinity edge and Edinburgh’s only thermal spa with rooftop Hydropool. Hotel guests enjoy complimentary access to our indoor swimming pool, poolside sauna and gym.See Less

### Area & Activities

Edinburgh is a spectacular city that just begs to be discovered. Steeped in history and heritage, emitting blends of old and new Scottish culture, and boasting an incredible array of sights and attractions, Edinburgh truly is a place like no other. The Sheraton Grand is located in the heart of the city, and so is in prime position for you to access the amazing sights and embark upon your adventurous...See More visit of the Scottish capital. You will be just a stone’s throw away from the iconic Edinburgh Castle, the World Heritage Site Carlton Hill, the Scottish National Gallery, St Giles Cathedral, Real Mary King’s Close, and Holyrood Palace.See Less

## Edinburgh Festival

Perfectly positioned opposite Edinburgh’s iconic Usher Hall and the Lyceum, the hotel places you at the heart of the city’s cultural scene. During the famous Edinburgh Festival season, soak up the buzz as international artists and performers bring the city to life. From the Edinburgh International Festival and Edinburgh Fringe to Edinburgh Military Tattoo, our central location offers effortless access across Edinburgh’s most celebrated cultural events.

Capture Edinburgh's Beauty at These Iconic Photo Opportunities

The Vennel

Enjoy a different perspective of Edinburgh Castle from The Vennel, 10 minutes from the hotel.

Dean Village

Just 10 minutes from the hotel lies Dean Village, a picturesque, tranquil oasis by the Water of Leith.

Circus Lane

Edinburgh presents another fantastic photo opportunity in the form of Circus Lane in Stockbridge, which is especially beautiful in spring and summer. There is so much to explore in this suburb, from local boutique shops, to independent cafes and restaurants.

## Edinburgh Farmer's Market

The Edinburgh Farmer's Market takes place every Saturday from 9am to 2pm on Castle Terrace, just a short 3 minute walk away from the hotel. Head there for a selection of fresh Scottish produce from local suppliers. If visiting the city on a Sunday, don't miss Stockbridge Market from 10am to 5pm for local art, fresh produce and unique keepsakes.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh offers a complimentary, 6:30 AM-10:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Resistance Bands, TRX, Battle Ropes, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
