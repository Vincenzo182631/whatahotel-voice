# Welcome to Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh

## Experience our luxury hotel in Edinburgh

Edinburgh’s UNESCO World Heritage city centre is best explored on foot, and our hotel is ideally located to discover its outstanding history and architectural beauty. Our 5-star luxury spa hotel in Edinburgh...See More has all the amenities you need. Wake up refreshed in our dreamily comfortable Sheraton Signature Sleep Experience™ beds and enjoy inspiring views of Edinburgh Castle from our Castle View Rooms & Suites. Spend your day exploring the Royal Mile or browsing the shops on George Street before escaping to Scotland's most renowned urban thermal spa experience, One Spa. Unwind in our iconic rooftop Hydropool to start your journey to wellbeing and rejuvenation. Find inner calm in our Thermal Suite, an unrivalled range of uplifting water, salt and heat experiences designed to help you rebalance and refresh your body and mind. Our Club Rooms include evening drinks and canapés in our Club Lounge, for that something extra special.See Less

Voted “Best Spa in Scotland” 2023 and 2024 by the Good Spa Guide and achieved Five Bubble Luxury Status. Discover a world of complete relaxation at Scotland’s most renowned urban thermal spa experience, featuring our iconic rooftop Hydropool and Thermal Suite, with a range of calming salt, water and heat experiences. Remember, complimentary use of our 19m indoor swimming pool, poolside sauna and state-of-the-art fitness centre are included with every guestroom reservation.

Sustainability at One Spa

Pools feature heat savers and blankets, enhancing efficiency. The Hydropool has a 30% more efficient Plate Heat Exchanger. Water is saved through fixtures, smart irrigation, and pumps with inverter drives, and Spirovent ensures clean hot water systems. The pool's filtration improves with AFM® Activated Filter Media, doubling sand filter efficiency and minimizing water and chemical usage.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh?

The check-in time at Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh. Self parking is available at Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh, with rates of £5.95 per hour, and £25.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh?

The closest airport near Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh is Edinburgh Airport (EDI). EDI is located approximately 6.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh have a Sheraton Club lounge?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Hotel & Spa, Edinburgh has Sheraton Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Sheraton Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
