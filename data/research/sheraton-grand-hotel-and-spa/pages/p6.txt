## Sheraton Club Edinburgh Membership

We invite you to become part of our exclusive community at Sheraton Club Edinburgh, the most coveted space for working, collaborating and relaxing. Realise your full potential in our Club Lounge. Experience a flexible, functional, design-led space, which is warm and inviting, complete with modern essentials, floor to ceiling windows and skylights which flood the space with natural light. Superior Wi-Fi and a private meeting space for up to 6 people make it a productive environment to encourage personal and professional growth.

**Club Lounge Access**

Exclusive access to the Club Lounge is available Monday to Friday, from 7am to 5pm, including a complimentary breakfast with extensive hot and cold options. Throughout the day, members can enjoy unlimited specialty tea, coffee, soft drinks, snacks and free high‑speed Wi‑Fi. Members may bring eight monthly guest passes per month, valid 11am to 5pm and benefit from up to nine hours of complimentary parking on weekdays.

**Dining**

Members receive 20% off the Club Lounge lunch menu and 20% off food and drink in One Square for bookings of up to six people. Please note that Sheraton Club Edinburgh benefits are available Monday to Friday only, apart from 20% discount in One Square which is available 7 days a week (blackout dates do apply). All benefits are subject to terms and conditions.

**Exclusive Extras**

Complimentary use of our meeting room for up to two hours per day, accommodating up to six people, ideal for meetings or private work.
