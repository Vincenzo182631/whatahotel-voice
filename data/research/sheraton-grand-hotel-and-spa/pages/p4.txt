## Cocktails at One Square

At One Square, every cocktail begins with a sense of place. The Four Corners of Scotland brings together flavours from across the country, celebrating local produce, traditional ingredients and the very best Scottish spirits. Inspired by Scotland's diverse regions, each drink captures the character of its corner, from crisp coastal notes to rich Highland warmth, blending heritage with modern creativity. The result is a menu that feels both refined and full of personality.

## Sustainability at One Square

0% of our waste goes to landfill. Around two thirds is recycled and the rest goes to an Energy from Waste scheme. Plastic straws were removed in February 2018, we offer guests a biodegradable alternative. Our kitchen is gas free with induction only cooking, individual hood control, salamander grills, and a heat recovery dishwasher. We choose environmentally preferred suppliers & products. We work with the best local suppliers to create our menus. We choose to partner with suppliers based on their sustainable food production so that we can offer responsibly-sourced meals in our restaurants.
