# Palazzo Parigi Hotel & Grand Spa Milan

## Check out Milan’s most luxurious 5-star rooms and suites here at Palazzo Parigi Hotel & Grand Spa.

Offering five star luxury accommodation in Milan city centre, our retreat is the epitome of elegance, with original artworks and rich, luxurious fabrics. Choose from one of the 54 Rooms and 41 Suites, in either a Milanese or Parisian style. The former are more contemporary with cream furnishings and natural wooden fittings while our Parisian rooms are designed with a classically chic feel. Terraces and balconies come with every bedroom, many with unmissable city views of the Duomo, the Sforzesco Castle or the Porta Nuova skyline.
