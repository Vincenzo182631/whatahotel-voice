# Grand Spa & Fitness

## Your oasis of wellness and calm in the center of Milan

The largest and loveliest spa in the city, immerse yourself in five star tranquility. Indulge your senses in a haven designed for both pampering and complete relaxation. Offering one-of-a-kind treatments and boasting some of the best spa facilities in Milan, the Grand Spa of Palazzo Parigi Hotel is the perfect tonic away from the fast city pace.
