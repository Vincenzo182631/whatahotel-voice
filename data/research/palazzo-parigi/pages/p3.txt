# Tastes and Flavours in one of the best luxury restaurants in Milan

## A luxury culinary experience

We take great pride in making sure our food and drink matches the exquisite quality of the elegant surroundings. Devour authentic Italian cuisine in the magnificent Gastronomic Restaurant of Palazzo Parigi Hotel & Grand Spa, gather with friends in the fashionable Caffé Parigi, treat yourself to a decadent Afternoon Tea or enjoy al fresco dining in the lush green of our outdoor garden.

### Gastronomic Restaurant

Here, inside one of Milan’s best restaurants, find a fusion of exceptional taste and the finest cuisine, delivered with show-stopping drama through a unique culinary catwalk.

### Bistrot Lounge “Caffé Parigi”

Effortlessly stylish and flooded with natural light, our chic café and bar draws a lively local crowd.

### Palazzo Parigi Private Garden

Adorned with elegant statues and ornaments, find our large private garden that dates back to the 18th century, shaded by ancient trees and surrounded by colorful plants and flowers.

### In-Room Dining

Whether you are craving a morning Cappuccino or a glass of Italian wine, our In Room Dining service delivers a special experience to your room or suite 24 hour a day.
