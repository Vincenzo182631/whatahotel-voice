The sweeping staircase, Murano chandeliers and marble pillars in the lobby of Milan’s newest luxury hotel may have a lot of operatic elegance but upstairs, rooms by architect-owner Paola Giambelli and Paris based designer Pierre-Yves Rochon are soothing nests done up with neutral-colored silk damask, white duvet covered beds with thickly padded headboards, crystal table lamps and terrazzo or parquet floors.
