# All Suites

All of our accommodations are spacious, newly reimagined suites, inspired by our Bauhaus roots to invite a deeper connection with nature. As you step into your suite, you'll immediately feel a sense of home, with every design element carefully curated to harmonize with the breathtaking landscapes that surround us. Our 98 guest suites feature floor-to-ceiling windows and contemporary furnishings. Every suite is thoughtfully designed with living, working, and sleeping areas to accommodate everyone from business travelers to families.

* * *

### In-Suite Amenities

- High-speed wireless Internet
- Nespresso coffee makers with pods replenished daily
- Bottled water
- In-room safe
- Private patio or balcony—available in most guest suites
- Room service available daily 7:00 am - 9:00 pm.
- No elevators
- 55' LCD televisions with 1080P HD
- Multi-line VoIP telephones
- Large work desk with lamp
- Clothing steamer
- Queen size pull-out sleeper sofa
- Pillow-top mattresses
- Hypoallergenic down pillows & comforters
- Print from your guest suite to the front desk
- Wet bar with mini-refrigerator and microwave
- Sets of four: plates, silverware, drinking glasses, wine glasses & mugs
- Lather bath amenities
- Bathrobes

* * *

### Services Available on Request

- Connecting guest suites—subject to availability
- ADA guest suites—junior suite, 1 bedroom suite, 1 bedroom corner and 2 bedroom suite-- subject to availability
- Humidifier
- Down alternative pillows & comforters
- Rollaway bed— additional fee of $50/night
- Crib—please note that we do not provide crib bedding
- Space heater
- Toiletries
