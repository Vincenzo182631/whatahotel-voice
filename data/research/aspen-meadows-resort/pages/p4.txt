# Crafted with Intention

#### Igniting Aspen's culinary scene

In a simplistic, approachable style, local Colorado products shine in every dish. Think local farmers, foragers, and business owners with a focus on the Rocky Mountains, and westward food sourcing – fewer, better ingredients. Whether you're sharing plates that encourage conversation or indulging in a multi-course meal, our menu is designed to adapt to your needs, occasion, and timing. Aspen’s newest dining experience is a journey through flavors, and connection in a vibrant, welcoming atmosphere.

Here, the art of conversation meets the art of cuisine — come for the food or the company.

## Staff Carousel

### Executive Chef

Justin Kalaluhi

With more than 25 years of experience in culinary operations, Chef Justin Kalaluhi brings a wealth of knowledge in menu innovation, team leadership and operational efficiency. He joins Aspen Meadows from The Lodge at Torrey Pines in San Diego, where he served as executive sous chef. Previously, he spent several years as chef de cuisine at the Fairmont Orchid on Hawaii’s Big Island and held various leadership positions across top resorts in Hawaii and Vail/Beaver Creek. Kalaluhi holds a culinary arts degree from Kauai Community College and enjoys mountain biking, shooting sports, hiking, and snowboarding.

### Executive Pastry Chef

Sara Figueiredo

Executive Pastry Chef Sara Figueiredos'  journey to become Executive Pastry Chef has taken her around the world to Singapore, Thailand and beyond — places she pulls inspiration from to create delicious pastries & desserts. After earning a Bachelor’s Degree from Johnson & Wales, Chef Sara worked at Harvest in Boston’s Harvard Square, followed by Italian-focused stints at Babbo and Eataly and a move to San Francisco where she worked at Michelin-starred Lazy Bear. A big promotion to Executive Pastry Chef at Craftsman and Wolves allowed her to develop a team of like-minded bakers. Chef Sara relocated to Colorado in 2021 and is West End Social's Executive Pastry Chef.
