The Bayer Center

The Resnick Center for Herbert Bayer Studies (The Bayer Center) is a new exhibition space and center for the study of art located on the Aspen Institute’s campus. Named after Herbert Bayer (1900–1985)—an influential modern artist and designer who worked at the Bauhaus before relocating to Aspen in 1946—the Bayer Center honors his interdisciplinary perspective by promoting an understanding of art and design through exhibitions and public programming

Lost Forest

_Lost Forest, located at Elk Camp Snowmass, is only open during the summer._

Tucked away in the natural beauty of a Snowmass experience is an adventure of an extraordinary kind: Lost Forest. It's an immersive world complete with biking and hiking trails, a zipline, ropes course, climbing wall, and high-speed alpine coaster.

**Canopy Run Zipline Tours**

Clip in, hang on, and take the ride of your life as you soar through the trees and meadows of Snowmass. With seven ziplines, three sky bridges, and platforms mounted in the treetops, this guided experience lasts a minimum of three hours.

**Treeline Trial Challenge Course**

Combining rappels, ziplines, bridges, and ladders for a thrilling obstacle course through the trees, learn to trust your instincts, conquer your fears, and surprise yourself. This high ropes course offers five different paths at five different difficulties, and takes you 40 feet into the air. Along the way, you’ll tackle all sorts of physical and mental feats, all under a guide’s watchful eye and a constant belay system.

**Breathtaker Alpine Coaster**

Wind through more than a mile of forest on an elevated track at speeds of up to 28mph. Guests at least 54 inches (137cm) tall can round out their summer trip by chasing thrills at Elk Camp on Snowmass.

**Rugged Ascent Climbing Wall**

Reach new heights on the custom climbing wall with auto-belay that lets you challenge yourself safely on dozens of routes of varying difficulty. Whether you’re a beginner or a dedicated wall rat, this is the perfect place to diversify your mountain getaway.
