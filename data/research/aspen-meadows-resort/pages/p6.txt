# Bold Simplicity on a Flavorful Canvas

Discover a culinary narrative crafted with intention, where locally sourced ingredients from Colorado's Rocky Mountains and beyond are made into masterpieces. Our commitment to simplicity and quality ensures that each dish becomes a canvas for the vibrant flavors of the region.

Our newly reimagined restaurant features 270-degree views of Aspen’s mountains and an approachable menu designed for every occasion, highlighting shareable plates that encourage conversation. There is both an openness and warmth to the redesign which appeals to guests and the community, which plays off the stunning scenery of Aspen’s ski mountains on offer. With an unobstructed, stunning view, the large outdoor deck is a perfect place for après drinks and dining. And, to appeal to all types of dining experiences, the same menu will be served in the dining room, at the bar and outdoors. Seasonal, live entertainment will also be a staple on the expansive patio.
