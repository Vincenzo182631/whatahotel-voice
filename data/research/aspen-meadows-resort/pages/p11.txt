# Your Guide to Autumn Adventures

As the vibrant colors of fall paint the West End, Aspen Meadows becomes a haven for autumn adventurers. With crisp air and golden landscapes, the season brings fresh opportunities for exploration. Stroll through picturesque trails, take in the changing foliage on art walk tours, or enjoy a game of tennis or pickleball surrounded by stunning scenery. Whether you’re here for iconic fall events or simply to savor the season, we’ve curated the perfect resort experiences to enjoy during your autumn escape.

Reserve Your Stay

### Art at the Meadows

Herbert Bayer’s influence across our campus creates a seamless blend of Aspen’s natural beauty and unique art. Fall is the ideal time to explore this vibrant art collection in the crisp mountain air. Discover the stories behind the sculpture and land art throughout the Aspen Meadows campus with a new self-guided tour on the Bloomberg Connects app. Just scan the QR codes located next to each artwork to learn about the rich history and design legacy of this one-of-a-kind landscape designed by Herbert Bayer. Experience the harmonious relationship between nature, mind, and body within this all-encompassing Bauhaus environment.

### Autumn Trail Adventures

This fall, invigorate yourself in Aspen’s fresh mountain air and experience the thrill of the outdoors. With an array of bike paths and hiking trails, our property is a haven for those who love to stay active. Discover the stunning fall scenery on the Meadows Trail or take on the scenic views of the Rio Grande Trail.

### Après at West End Social

At West End Social, embrace the cozy fall vibes with après daily from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM. Enjoy stunning 270-degree mountain views on our outdoor patio, perfect for sipping your favorite drinks in the crisp autumn air.

### Padel by Brisas

We are proud to partner with Brisas at our Racquet Sports Center. Drawing inspiration from the landscape of Aspen, the center has been enhanced to be a place for community to socialize and connect featuring a padel court, pétanque court, backgammon tables, clothing shack and lounge. Attend a clinic to learn from our pro instructors or reserve a court for a casual game with friends.

### Nature and Wildlife Scavenger Hunts

Rent a Polaroid camera from the front desk and gather your family for a fall adventure across our 40-acre campus. Our seasonal scavenger hunts invite you to explore the vibrant autumn scenery, from bird watching and rock balancing to appreciating the crisp air and colorful foliage.

Take in the beauty of the Roaring Fork River and enjoy sunsets over the mountains. For younger adventurers, our wildlife scavenger hunt offers a fun way to discover fall shapes, colors, and textures. Little explorers who complete the hunt will receive a sweet reward from our front desk team.

### The Resnick Center for Herbert Bayer Studies

Bayer's modernist influence is showcased throughout our campus, with The Bayer Center honoring his legacy through curated exhibitions and offering a dedicated space for the continued study of art. Discover the latest exhibit, "Double Take: Recurrent Dialogues in the Art of Herbert Bayer." Showcasing the evolution of several key motifs in Bayer’s vast output and spanning seven decades, from 1920s Germany to 1980s Southern California, this exhibition traces Bayer’s fascination with a set of iconographic elements that regularly appear in his work in different mediums including paintings, drawings, collages, and photographs

**Open complimentary to the public Tuesday - Sunday from 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM.**

### Complimentary E-Bike Rentals

Set amid 40 scenic acres with easy access to Aspen's trails, town and mountain views, Aspen Meadows invites guests to explore at their own pace. Complimentary e-bike rentals are available daily throughout the summer and fall, making it simple to ride into town for shopping, stop by Aspen Tap for a bite or head toward Maroon Bells with a bike pass.

To check out an e-bike, guests may stop by the Health Center, where the front desk team will provide a waiver, helmet, lock, and everything needed to get on the road. Learn More.

### Picnics by Meadows Café

Make the most of the mountain air and open skies with our picnics now available at Meadows Café. Whether you’re lounging on the lawn in Anderson Park or embarking on a hike on the Rio Grande Trail, we’ve packed everything you need for the perfect picnic set amongst the beautiful sights of the season. Your ideal picnic spot awaits.Picnics start at 31.00 per guest. Please fill out our simple inquiry form to reserve your picnic pickup time.

### In-Suite Wellness Offerings

Realign your body and mind with convenient wellness opportunities without ever leaving your suite. Yoga, meditation, breath-work, stretching and sleep. Let our Three Sages app playlists help you relax and unwind through restorative wellness classes.

Our new wellness kits are available to rent, including everything you need for your wellness journey. From yoga mats, balance blocks, and resistance bands to Hypervolt percussion massage devices, and Normatec recovery suits, these rentals are available from the front desk for mindful moments in your suite.

### Aspen Saturday Market

Immerse yourself in the vibrant local culture by spending your Saturday morning at the Aspen Saturday Market. From June 6th to October 3rd, 2026, stroll through a lively market brimming with Colorado-grown ingredients, artisan products, and unique finds. Experience the essence of Aspen and discover a community gathering that's as rich in flavor as it is in character. Open 8:30 AM - 2:00 PM and 9:00 AM - 2:00 PM after Labor Day.

### Heated Lap Pool & Hot Tub

At our Health Center, take in breathtaking mountain views from our hot tub, available on a first-come basis. Relax and breathe in the fresh air for a refreshing moment of wellness amidst nature’s beauty.

For those looking to stay active, our heated saline lap pool is perfect for a fall dip and offers an ideal workout setting. The pool can be reserved in advance for your convenience.

### Complimentary Shuttles & Parking

Arrive with ease at Aspen Meadows Resort with complimentary on-site parking or a convenient shuttle service to and from Aspen/Pitkin County Airport.

During your stay, take advantage of our eco-friendly electric shuttle buses, running every half hour from 7:00 AM to 10:30 PM, to downtown Aspen. Start your holiday shopping early and explore the charming shops in town, all just a quick ride away.
