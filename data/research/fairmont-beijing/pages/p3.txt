Located in the heart of Beijing’s Central Business District, this all-glass, rose gold-colored Fairmont houses 222 sun-drenched rooms and suites, decorated with local accents and bathrooms with heated floors. All guest services have been streamlined into one central number, the Royal Service, so you only need to touch one button to find an attendant—speaking remarkably good English—at your disposal.
