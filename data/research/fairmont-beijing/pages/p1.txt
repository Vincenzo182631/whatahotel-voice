## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Beijing located?

Located in the central business district of Beijing, our address is No.8 Yong An Dong Li, Jian Guo Men Wai Avenue, Chaoyang District, Beijing, China. The hotel is within walking distance to the Line 1 subway and is approximately 30km from the Beijing Capital International Airport.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Beijing?

Check-in is from 2:00pm and check-out is by 12:00 noon.

How can I get to Fairmont Beijing from the nearest airport?

Beijing Capital International Airport is approximately 30km or 45 minutes away from Fairmont Beijing. Beijing Daxing International Airport is approximately 60km or 90 minutes away from the hotel. You can reach the Fairmont Beijing via our hotel limousine pick-up service, airport bus, taxi or subways.

Does Fairmont Beijing have a gym/fitness center?

Covering approximately 2000 sqm, our fitness center includes a sauna steam, Jacuzzis, a yoga studio, and an indoor temperature-controlled 20-meter pool to allow our guests to stay healthy and fit every day of their stay. Spa treatments are customized to help our guests unwind and revitalize their energy.
