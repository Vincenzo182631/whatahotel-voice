## 5-Star Luxury Hotel Rooms in Inverness

At Ness Walk Hotel, no two rooms are alike. Our high-end hotel rooms span four distinct types, each named after the trees that define our extraordinary grounds.

Laurel Rooms sit on the upper floors and feature large floor-to-ceiling windows with views across the grounds or to the River Ness. Many include a separate bath, shower, and WC in an elegant marble bathroom. Next, the Cedar Rooms open directly onto private terraces with garden and river views, making them ideal for guests who want the Highlands close at hand. Finally, our Oak Rooms sit on the first floor and offer uninterrupted courtyard views towards the River Ness through wide, light-filled windows.

Furthermore, every room at Ness Walk Hotel includes 5-star en-suite bathrooms stocked with sustainable toiletries from Arran Sense of Scotland. Because these are luxury hotel rooms in Scotland built for guests who expect the very best.

## Signature 5-Star Suites: Luxury Accommodation in the Scottish Highlands

For the ultimate luxury accommodation in the Highlands of Scotland, the Rowan and Wellingtonia Suites at Ness Walk Hotel stand apart.

The Wellingtonia Suite spans 90 square metres and is widely regarded as one of the finest hotel suites in Scotland. It features a private lounge, a marble bathroom with a freestanding bath and rainfall walk-in shower, twin basins, and a super-king bed dressed in Egyptian cotton. The Rowan Suite is equally impressive. South-facing and light-drenched, it includes its own lounge, a handcrafted king-size bed, and an Illy coffee machine for relaxed Highland mornings.

Both suites are a popular choice for wedding accommodation in Inverness. They also suit any stay that calls for something truly special. Because at Ness Walk Hotel, the suites offer far more than extra space; they deliver a complete Highland experience.

## Ness Walk Accommodation Frequently Asked Questions

**What types of luxury accommodation does Ness Walk Hotel offer?**

Ness Walk Hotel offers 47 individually designed luxury hotel rooms across several categories. Each room features bespoke furnishings, premium Scottish toiletries, and modern amenities including Smart TVs and complimentary WiFi. Ness Walk’s 5-star hotel rooms in Inverness are designed to provide the finest luxury accommodation Scotland has to offer.

**Does Ness Walk Hotel have accessible hotel rooms?**

Yes, Ness Walk Hotel provides wheelchair accessible hotel rooms designed to meet the needs of all guests. This includes features such as level access, adapted bathrooms, and thoughtful design considerations. Please contact our reservations team to discuss your accessibility requirements so we can provide the most suitable luxury hotel room for your stay.

**What luxury amenities are included in the hotel rooms?**

All luxury hotel rooms at Ness Walk Hotel include premium Scottish toiletries from the Isle of Skye Candle Company, Nespresso coffee machines, Smart TVs with streaming capabilities, complimentary high-speed WiFi, and spacious bathrooms with walk-in rainfall showers or freestanding baths. These high-end hotel rooms blend contemporary comfort with Highland character.

**Can Ness Walk Hotel accommodate wedding guests?**

Yes, Ness Walk Hotel is one of Scotland’s premier wedding venues with accommodation, offering 47 luxury rooms perfect for wedding parties and guests. Whether you’re hosting an intimate celebration or a larger gathering, our combination of elegant event spaces and luxury Highland accommodation guarantees your wedding guests enjoy a memorable stay in the heart of Inverness.

**Do Ness Walk Hotel rooms have views of the River Ness?**

Many of Ness Walk Hotel’s luxury rooms feature beautiful views of the River Ness and the surrounding Highlands. While room views vary by category and availability, river-facing rooms offer particularly stunning vistas. If a specific view is important to your stay, please mention this when booking, and our team will accommodate your preference wherever possible.
