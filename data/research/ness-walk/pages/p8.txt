# Heavenly Afternoon Tea

## Sense & Sensibility Afternoon Tea Inverness at Ness Walk Hotel

A little sense. A little sensibility. And a very good reason to make for Ness Walk. This September & October, Ness Walk is joining PoB Hotels in celebrating the release of Universal Pictures’ Sense & Sensibility with our themed Heavenly Afternoon Tea menu. the setting is ready. The story is yours to write.

Join us for the afternoon tea Inverness locals rate as one of the best. Ness Walk Hotel sits on the banks of the River Ness, and here, a modern twist meets a British classic in our elegant 5-star setting. So settle by the roaring fire in our lounge or take a table in Torrish, one of the finest restaurants in Inverness. Then lose an afternoon to seasonal flavours from our brigade of chefs. And if you’d like to raise the tone, we offer a Champagne afternoon tea too.

Wherever you dine, views stretch across our verdant gardens and the River Ness. Every element on the stand is freshly baked, and we present each treat with precision, colour and texture. In short, it’s the picture-perfect centrepiece for an indulgent afternoon at Ness Walk Hotel, Inverness.

## The Best Afternoon Tea in Inverness

Our Heavenly Afternoon Tea offers savoury and sweet indulgences alongside fresh scones, finger sandwiches and cakes. Start with delicate options like our chanterelle quiche and smoked salmon roulade. Next, work through the finger sandwiches. Roasted chicken and tarragon mayonnaise on onion bread is a favourite, while smoked cheddar wither rocket pesto on tomato bread comes a close second. Brackla Farm egg mayonnaise makes it all the more special.

Then, move to fluffy buttermilk and wholemeal & raisin scones for two classic flavours. Finally, a decadent collection of sweet treats ends your heavenly afternoon in style.

We serve our afternoon tea with an extensive Loch Ness speciality tea collection and coffee menu. Prices start at £33 per guest. Additionally, we serve daily from 12:30pm to 4:30pm for **both residents and non-residents**. To book online, scroll down to the calendar. Alternatively, call us on 01463 215215.

## Ness Walk Hotel Heavenly Afternoon Tea - Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to be a hotel guest to book afternoon tea at Ness Walk?**

No, Heavenly Afternoon Tea is available to both residents and non-residents. Anyone can book and enjoy afternoon tea at Ness Walk Hotel. We offer parking for non-residents subject to availability.

**Can you accommodate dietary requirements for afternoon tea?**

Yes, we offer gluten-free afternoon tea and vegan afternoon tea, both priced at £33 per person. Our chefs source the finest local produce to create these specialist menus. Please inform us of any other dietary requirements or allergies when booking and our team will ensure suitable alternatives are prepared.

**What times is afternoon tea served?**

Heavenly Afternoon Tea at Ness Walk Hotel is served daily from 12:30pm to 4:30pm. You can enjoy your afternoon tea in our lounge by the roaring fire, on the terrace for alfresco dining during warmer months, or in Torrish Restaurant with views across the River Ness and gardens.

**Can I purchase afternoon tea as a gift?**

Yes, Ness Walk Hotel afternoon tea gift vouchers are available to purchase online. These make perfect gifts for birthdays, anniversaries, or special occasions. The vouchers can be redeemed for our Heavenly Afternoon Tea experience at Ness Walk Hotel in Inverness.

**What's included in the champagne afternoon tea option?**

The champagne afternoon tea includes everything from our Heavenly Afternoon Tea menu – savoury treats like langoustine profiterole and duck parfait doughnut, elegant finger sandwiches, fluffy scones (buttermilk and chocolate chip), and a selection of sweet treats – plus a glass of chilled Guy De Chassey Grand Cru Champagne. This option is priced at £49 per person.

**Where can I find the best afternoon tea in Inverness?**

Heavenly Afternoon Tea at Ness Walk Hotel is regularly cited among the best afternoon tea Scotland has to offer, served daily by the River Ness with a stand of fresh sandwiches, scones and patisserie, plus a Loch Ness speciality tea collection.

**Can I book afternoon tea as a gift?**

Afternoon Tea Gift Vouchers are available to purchase online, and they make a beautifully elegant present for birthdays, anniversaries or simply spoiling someone special.

**Where is Ness Walk Hotel?**

Ness Walk Hotel is set on the banks of the River Ness in Inverness, a short walk from the city centre. Non-resident parking is available for afternoon tea guests, subject to availability.
