# Ness Walk Hotel – 5 Star Luxury Riverside Hotel

## The best of luxury 5-star hotels in Inverness, Scotland

Ness Walk Hotel sits on the leafy banks of the River Ness, just minutes from Inverness city centre. This lovingly restored Grade B listed building retains its most cherished period features. However, it also embraces contemporary art and modern design with equal confidence. The result is a seamless blend of past and present, and a genuine retreat in the heart of the Scottish Highlands. So, it’s easy to see why Ness Walk is rated among the finest 5-star luxury hotels in Scotland.

Your stay begins with a glass of champagne on arrival. Rooms and suites also come with indulgent freestanding baths, and select rooms include their own outdoor firepit. A complimentary cooked-to-order breakfast is also included every morning. And in the evening, award-winning fine dining, afternoon teas, and tasting menus are all on offer. Our concierge team arranges private chauffeurs, too, as well as Highland experience tours and airport transfers. EV charging is also available on site.

Book directly through the Ness Walk Hotel website or by phone for the best guaranteed rates. There are no booking fees, and free cancellation is included as standard.

## An award-winning hotel with heart

Ness Walk Hotel holds the Conde Nast Johansens Best for Romance Award for UK and Ireland. We’ve also received the Highlands and Islands Thistle Award for Best Hotel Experience, a TripAdvisor Travellers Choice Award, and the Green Tourism Gold Award. Most recently, Ness Walk was named the most romantic hotel in the UK. Recognition this consistent does not happen by accident.

The hotel sits in a beautifully secluded spot, surrounded by a dramatic canopy of ancient trees. So, by staying here, you’re secluded but still right in the heart of Inverness city centre. Because of this, few luxury hotels in Inverness, Scotland, offer the same balance of peace and convenience. Loch Ness is just a 10-minute drive away, too, and the wider Scottish Highlands are on your doorstep.

All in all, Ness Walk is one of the most luxurious hotels Scotland has to offer because it brings together iconic design, historic character, and warm Highland hospitality. Winter brings its own appeal too, with crackling log fires and frost-touched landscapes that feel truly magical. Whether you’re planning a destination wedding, a romantic escape, or simply the finest luxury accommodation in Scotland, Ness Walk is the place to visit.

## Sumptuous rooms and suites: 5-star hotels in Inverness, Scotland

No two rooms at Ness Walk Hotel are the same, and each room and suite takes inspiration from the colours and textures of the Highland seasons. So, you’ll find soft tones, natural materials, and thoughtful details running throughout. Freestanding baths and exceptional amenities also come as standard, and warm Scottish hospitality makes every space feel genuinely welcoming.

Guests consistently describe Ness Walk Hotel as some of the best accommodation in Scotland. Because each room is peaceful, beautifully designed, and full of character, it’s the kind of place that stays with you long after you have checked out.

## Where luxury meets the Scottish Highlands

Location is one of the things that sets Ness Walk Hotel apart. Step outside and you find towering trees, riverside paths, and the unmistakable beauty of the Scottish Highlands. A short walk brings you into the heart of Inverness. So guests get both the city and the countryside, without compromise.

Beyond the hotel, the Highlands open up. Loch Ness, Culloden Battlefield, Loch Affric, and many Scottish hidden gems are all within easy reach of the hotel. Our concierge team is also available to handle the details for you, whether that is a whisky tasting itinerary, a private chauffeur, or a bespoke Highland experience tour.
