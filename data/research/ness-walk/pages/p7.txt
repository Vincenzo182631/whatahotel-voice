# Tasting Menu Experience

## Seasonal Tasting Menu

Our Tasting Menu Experience is one of the first in Inverness, showcasing the very best of seasonal and local food from Scotland. Helmed by our talented Chef Gerard Chouet, we’re bringing a touch of culinary magic to the Highlands. Embark on a tasting journey, where every plate tells a story. This is the perfect opportunity for those seeking the discovery of new flavours and local, seasonal ingredients to feel their tastebuds sing. Not only are we one of the best restaurants in Scotland, we are rooted in place and pride our delivery on the best of everything. So enjoy 5 star Scottish hospitality and restaurant service at Ness Walk, alongside a memorable dining experience with carefully planned pairing wines and creative menus.

## Inspired by the Highlands

Chef Gerard’s varied career has taken him from the food-obsessed region of Burgundy through some of Europe’s most renowned kitchens. He spent a decade at the iconic Andrew Fairlie at Gleneagles, and today he brings an infectious passion for Scotland’s rich natural larder to Torrish. Also an avid wild forager, Gerard has a unique ability to uncover the Highlands’ hidden treasures, drawing inspiration from the landscapes and seasons.

Our chefs transform ingredients like foraged wild garlic and Aberdeenshire lamb into dishes that capture the essence of the region. So this is an unpretentious celebration of Scotland’s bounty, creating a considered experience in the heart of Inverness.
