## Torrish: The Best Restaurant in Inverness for Scottish Cuisine

Ness Walk Hotel’s Torrish Restaurant takes its name from the elegant salmon fly used on the River Ness. It is, consequently, a name rooted in the landscape and spirit of the Scottish Highlands. Set within Ness Walk Hotel’s original drawing room, the restaurant features 19th-century detailing, high corniced ceilings, and sweeping river views. As a result, it offers one of the most beautiful and distinctive restaurant settings in Scotland for couples, individuals, and corporate travellers.

However, Torrish is not a stuffy fine dining experience. Instead, it’s relaxed, warm, and entirely without pretence. There are no dress codes and no unnecessary formality. And our team believes in outstanding Scottish cuisine backed by genuinely attentive service. This is why guests consistently rate Torrish among the best restaurants in Inverness, Scotland.

Head Chef Gerard Chouet leads the kitchen. His career spans some of Europe’s most celebrated restaurants, including a decade at the iconic Andrew Fairlie at Gleneagles. Furthermore, Gerard is an avid wild forager who draws inspiration directly from the Highland landscape. Therefore, dishes like foraged wild garlic, Aberdeenshire lamb, and Scottish-caught fish reflect the seasons and the land around us. Some ingredients also grow right here in the grounds of Ness Walk Hotel. It genuinely does not get fresher than that, and you can sample it all with a Torrish tasting menu experience.

## The Torrish Bar: Cocktails, Whisky and Terrace Dining

Before or after dinner, the Torrish Bar offers one of the most stylish spots for Inverness dining and drinks. Adorned with brass finishes, large mirrors, and luxurious velvets and leathers, it’s a place of warmth and sophistication. Sink into an inviting snug and choose from an extensive selection of cocktails, Scotch whiskies, craft gins, fine wines, and soft drinks.

Moreover, the Terrace Bar extends the experience outdoors. With garden and River Ness views from your table, it’s perfect for alfresco dining in the warmer months. The terrace menu also celebrates the best of the local area, from gourmet sandwiches and house-made crisps to locally sourced chargrilled steaks. No reservation is required for the bar, so feel free to drop in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What dining options are available at Ness Walk Hotel?**

Ness Walk Hotel offers several dining experiences, including its award-winning restaurant Torrish, a cocktail and whisky bar, terrace dining overlooking the River Ness, afternoon tea, and private dining for special occasions.

**Is afternoon tea available at Ness Walk Hotel?**

Yes. Afternoon tea is served at Ness Walk Hotel and includes freshly prepared savouries, cakes, and sandwiches made by the hotel’s pastry team. It is available to both hotel guests and non-residents.

**Does Ness Walk Hotel have outdoor or terrace dining?**

Yes. The Terrace Bar at Ness Walk Hotel offers outdoor dining with views of the nearby River Ness, particularly during the warmer months.

**Is there parking available for diners?**

Limited on-site parking is available at Ness Walk Hotel. Non-residents visiting for afternoon tea may use the car park subject to availability, while evening parking is reserved for blue badge holders. The hotel also has 11 electric vehicle charging points.

**Do you need to be staying at Ness Walk Hotel to dine there?**

No. Dining at Ness Walk Hotel is open to both hotel guests and visitors looking for restaurants in Inverness.

**Can Ness Walk Hotel accommodate special occasions or celebrations?**

Yes. Ness Walk Hotel offers private dining options for occasions such as birthdays, anniversaries, and celebrations, with seasonal menus and curated wine selections available for events.

**Do hotel guests have access to in-room dining?**

Yes, room service is available to guests staying at Ness Walk Hotel, allowing meals to be enjoyed in the comfort of their room or suite.

**Can you combine dining with a stay at Ness Walk Hotel?**

Yes. Many guests choose to combine dining experiences such as dinner or afternoon tea with an overnight stay at Ness Walk Hotel, making it a convenient option for visitors exploring Inverness or the surrounding Highlands.

**What drinks are available when dining at Ness Walk Hotel?**

Guests dining at Ness Walk Hotel can enjoy a wide range of drinks including cocktails, Scotch whisky, craft gin, wines from international wine regions, and non-alcoholic options.
