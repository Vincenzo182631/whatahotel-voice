# Ness Walk FAQs

## Got a question? We’ve got the answer!

Are you planning a visit to Ness Walk? Perhaps it’s your first time in Scotland, our you just need to know something about our hotel. Our FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions) may help you. However if you still have a question, please feel free to contact us online or give us a call on 01463 215215

**Where is Ness Walk Hotel located?**

Ness Walk is located at 12 Ness Walk, IV3 5SQ.

**Are you near shops and restaurants?**

Ness Walk is in the perfect location to take advantage of the nearby city centre shops and restaurants alongside our own restaurant Torrish.

**How far is it into the city centre?**

We are only a safe 10 minute walk along the riverside into town. Maps and directions are available at reception with concierge.

**How do I contact the hotel?**

The hotel can be contacted by calling 01463 215215 or emailing welcome@nesswalk.com.

**How do I make a booking?**

Bookings can be made online at www.nesswalk.com or by calling our reservation department on 01463 215215.

**How do I check my booking details?**

You will be able to check your booking details on the confirmation email sent to you after the reservation has been made.

**How do I amend or cancel my booking?**

Bookings can be amended or cancelled by calling our reservations department on 01463 215215 or online here.

**How do I make a group booking?**

Group bookings can be made by calling us directly on 01463 257107.

**Can I turn up without a booking?**

Bookings must be made in advance.

**What time can I check in?**

Check-in is available from 3pm however we can offer early check-in at £20 per hour.

**What time can I check out?**

Check-out is by 11am however we can offer later check-out at £20 per hour.

**What time is breakfast?**

Mon – Fri 6.30am until 10:30am / Saturday & Sunday 7am – 11am.

**What time is the restaurant open for dinner?**

Our dinner service begins at 5pm with last seating at 9.30pm.

**Do I need to pre-book dinner?**

It is advisable to make a reservation for dinner prior to ensure that you are able to dine at your preferred time.

**Do you cater for those with special dietary requirements?**

We would be happy to cater to special dietary requirements and adapt our offering accordingly, just let us our waiting staff know of your intolerances.

**When is the hotel reception open?**

Our reception is manned 24/7

**I am arriving very late at night, will there be someone to help me check-in?**

Of course. A member of our team will be available to check you in upon your late arrival.

**What star rating does the hotel have?**

Ness Walk has an official 5 star rating from VisitScotland.

**Is the hotel wheelchair accessible?**

Ness Walk is fully accessible with a level entrance to all public areas and lift access to rooms on the 1st and 2nd floor.

**Is the hotel pet-friendly?**

We do not allow any pets at Ness Walk.

**Is wi-fi included in the cost of my room?**

High speed WiFi is available on a complimentary basis.

**Can I bring children?**

Our restaurant Torrish is child-friendly and has a fantastic menu to choose from. We don’t however have family rooms/interconnecting rooms available at Ness Walk.

**What room types are available?**

Ness Walk offers 5 room types named after trees that can be found on the property; Laurel, Cedar, Oak, Rowan Suite & Wellingtonia Suite.

**Do the rooms have en-suite bathrooms?**

All rooms at Ness Walk offer en-suite bathrooms

**What in-room facilities are there?**

The rooms at Ness Walk are packed with all the amenities you would expect from a 5-star hotel.

**Do you provide housekeeping services?**

We offer a full housekeeping service including daily servicing of your room along with an additional evening turn-down service. Our preference is to carry this out at a convenient time when you are out of your room, however, if you do not wish for us to enter your room, you will be invited to use your Do-Not-Disturb sign to notify us.

**Do you provide room service?**

We provide a 24hr room service offering.

**Is it possible to get married at Ness Walk?**

We are able to offer wedding ceremonies at Ness Walk for exclusive use weddings only. Should you wish to host your reception for up to 60 guests without booking the whole venue, we can recommend ceremony locations nearby with beautiful views across the River Ness.

**Can I smoke on my room balcony?**

It is forbidden by Scottish Law to smoke on the premises outwith the designated smoking areas clearly signed.

**Does the hotel have car parking facilities?**

Ness Walk has car parking available for residents at £20 per car per day. This can be added at time of booking by our reservations team.

**Is there EV charging facilities on site?**

We have 9 overnight chargers, each 7kw as well as two superchargers – offering 22kW or 50kW depending on the output.

**How do I get to the hotel from the airport?**

Ness Walk is 10 miles away from Inverness Airport. You can get to the hotel by our transfer service or by taxi.

**How do I get to the hotel from the train station?**

The hotel is a short taxi ride from Inverness Train Station.

**Do you offer transfers?**

We offer transfers to and from the airport and to local golf courses at a set fee. Please contact us for more details and to make a booking.

**Does the hotel have a swimming pool and spa?**

We do not, however our sister hotel the Kingsmills Hotel offers a gym, swimming pool, steam room and sauna available to guests of Ness Walk on a complimentary basis. The Kingsmills Hotel also has a spa that can be bookable prior to arrival.

**I think I Ieft something with you after my stay – who should I contact for lost property?**

Contact us directly on 01463 215215 and we will be able to assist with locating this item.

**Do you offer laundry services?**

We offer a chargeable dry cleaning service. This is available Monday – Friday and will be delivered back to the hotel the same evening.

**What are the cancellation terms for a bedroom reservation?**

Bookings made on our Friends of Ness Walk and Best Available Rate are subject to free cancellation up until 6pm two days prior to arrival. For special offers, please do check the terms relating to the specific offer.

**Does the hotel provide in-room safe boxes?**

We provide **In-Room Safes — Please Read Before Use**

You are responsible for selecting and retaining your access code. If you forget your code, please contact reception immediately; however, as opening the safe may require specialist assistance, **we cannot guarantee it will be opened within any particular timeframe.**

**Do not store passports, travel documents, or any time-critical items unless you can allow sufficient time to seek assistance.** The hotel accepts no liability for any consequential losses, including missed flights, connections, or associated costs arising from any delay in opening the safe, whether or not we have been made aware of your time constraints.

The hotel is not liable for loss of or damage to items stored in the safe, except where caused by our negligence.

We recommend that guests hold travel insurance covering the full value of stored items and any travel disruption. Nothing in this notice excludes liability that cannot be restricted by law, including under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
