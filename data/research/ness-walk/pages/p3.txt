# 5 Star Hotel Suites, Inverness

## The Wellingtonia and Rowan – Luxury hotel suites in Inverness, Scotland

At Ness Walk Hotel, Inverness, we believe luxury should feel effortless. So, our two exquisite luxury hotel suites, the Wellingtonia and the Rowan, offer a unique interpretation of Highland hospitality. Located on the top floor of our 5-star hotel in Inverness, they capture the spirit of Scotland with warmth and elegance. Both are designed for guests wanting space to unwind and time to savour the finer details. As such, you should expect bespoke furnishings, high-quality appliances, and a sense of calm flowing through every room. From morning coffees by the window to evenings spent watching the shimmering River Ness, this is luxury accommodation in Inverness at its most refined.

## The Wellingtonia Suite

Our Wellingtonia Suite is the signature experience of Ness Walk Hotel and a true definition of a five-star hotel suite. At 90 square metres, it’s one of the most spacious suites in Inverness, and it’s designed to feel like a private residence within our hotel. Large windows line the walls and flood the space with natural light, and you’ll find sweeping views across our grounds. You’ll also enjoy the canopy of the 200-year-old Wellingtonia tree from which the suite takes its name.

Inside, contemporary design meets timeless comfort, and every element has been carefully handpicked. This includes bespoke furnishings and a soft neutral palette that echoes the tones of the Highland landscape. In addition, the open-plan lounge offers a tranquil retreat where you can truly relax. A separate dining area provides an intimate setting for private meals, while a large writing desk and media space give the suite a functional appeal. The bedroom also features a super-king-size bed dressed in the finest Egyptian cotton. And plush pillows, layered throws, and gentle lighting create an atmosphere made for long, unhurried mornings.

And when it’s time to unwind, the marble-finished bathroom is your very own private spa. Enjoy a deep soak in the freestanding bath, or refresh in the walk-in rainfall shower. Twin basins, soft robes, and indulgent, sustainable toiletries from Arran Sense of Scotland complete the experience.

So, from its refined décor to its serene atmosphere, the Wellingtonia Suite reflects everything that makes Ness Walk Hotel one of the best 5-star hotels in Inverness. Understated opulence, impeccable, award-winning service, and true Scottish warmth in the heart of The Highlands.

## The Rowan Suite

Equally elegant yet distinct in personality, the Rowan Suite offers another expression of luxury hotel suites in Scotland. At 53 square metres, this beautifully proportioned open-plan space is designed around light and flow.

South-facing windows fill the room with sunshine, while remote-controlled blinds soften the evening glow. Here, every detail has purpose. As a result, the design blends contemporary textures like polished wood, marble, and soft fabrics, with subtle Highland touches. The lounge is equally inviting and intimate, and ideal for relaxing after a day exploring Inverness or enjoying an aperitif before dinner in Torrish Restaurant. Alternatively, adjacent to the suite’s lounge, a dining area provides a perfect spot for private breakfasts or room-service dinners.

The Rowan’s king-size bed is a centrepiece of comfort, with a handcrafted headboard, luxurious linen, and a deep mattress. All of this comes together to deliver restful nights and peaceful mornings. The marble bathroom mirrors the elegance of the suite, featuring a bath, shower, and modern fittings. Thoughtful extras like complimentary mineral water and an Illy coffee machine guarantee a personal stay. And much like its namesake, the rowan tree, this suite embodies balance with light and shadow, comfort and sophistication, and privacy and connection.

All in all, The Rowan Suite at Ness Walk Hotel is a natural choice for guests seeking a luxury hotel suite in Inverness that combines personality with polish.

## Ness Walk Hotel Suites - Frequently Asked Questions

**What suites are available at Ness Walk Hotel?**

Ness Walk Hotel offers two luxury suites, the Wellingtonia Suite and the Rowan Suite. Both are located on the top floor of the hotel and provide spacious, high-end accommodation designed for comfort, privacy, and five-star hospitality.

**How large are the suites at Ness Walk Hotel?**

The Wellingtonia Suite measures approximately 90 square metres, making it one of the most spacious suites in Inverness. The Rowan Suite is approximately 53 square metres and offers an open-plan layout designed around light and flow.

**What type of beds are in Ness Walk Hotel suites?**

The Wellingtonia Suite features a super-king-size bed dressed in Egyptian cotton linens. The Rowan Suite includes a king-size bed with luxurious bedding designed for comfort and restful sleep.

**Do the suites include a separate living or dining area?**

Yes. Both suites at Ness Walk Hotel include lounge spaces for relaxing. Each suite also has a dining area suitable for private breakfasts or in-room dining.

**What bathroom features do Ness Walk Hotel suites have?**

The Wellingtonia Suite includes a marble bathroom with a freestanding bath, walk-in rainfall shower, and twin basins. The Rowan Suite also features a marble bathroom with both a bath and a shower, along with modern fittings and luxury toiletries from Arran Sense of Scotland.

**Do Ness Walk Hotel suites offer views?**

Yes, the Wellingtonia Suite features large windows overlooking the hotel grounds and the historic Wellingtonia tree. From the suites, guests can also enjoy the surroundings close to the banks of the River Ness.

**Are in-room refreshments provided in Ness Walk Hotel suites?**

Yes. The Rowan Suite includes complimentary mineral water and an Illy coffee machine. Both suites are designed with high-quality appliances and thoughtful in-room comforts.
