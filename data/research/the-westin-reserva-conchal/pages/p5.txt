# Holiday Events at The Westin Reserva Conchal All-Inclusive Golf Resort Spa

## LUMINA Where Light Lives

Welcome to LUMINA at The Westin Reserva Conchal. Moments glowing with laughter, the ocean whispering at sunset, memories made barefoot in the sand. Lumina is more than a celebration, it's the warmth of togetherness by the shore.
The Westin Reserva Conchal will transform into a heaven of festivity and joy, where there will be something for everyone to savor and enjoy. Immerse yourself in tranquil surroundings, indulge in nourishing spa treatments and taste delectable cuisine at our more than ten restaurants and bars. Let us create a magic opportunity for your family to reconnect, laugh and share the magic of the season. Join us for special holiday dinners and celebrations, exploring Costa Rica's biodiversity. We believe that holidays are not just about giving gifts, they are about giving yourself and your family the gift of personal growth and inspiration. Join us and let us get inspired for the festive season and the year ahead.

### The Journey Begins Here

Embrace the present, release the past, and trust in what the future holds. As we welcome 2027, we step into a new chapter, embracing change, celebrating growth, and evolving while staying true to who we are. Guided by our new motto, "Let the light of inspiration shine," we invite you to welcome the possibilities of what lies ahead. This New Year's Eve is more than a celebration; it marks the beginning of a new journey. A moment to leave behind what no longer serves us, embrace new perspectives, and welcome the year ahead with renewed energy and optimism. Join us as we raise a glass to new beginnings, new inspiration, and the light that guides us into 2027!

### Christmas Dinner

December 24th 6PM at Mitra Market. Celebrate the spirit of the season with an evening designed to bring loved ones together. Enjoy a festive character show, a lively wine market, and a delightful Christmas dinner featuring an array of flavors for every palate. Create lasting memories with family and friends as the magic of the holidays comes to life, complete with a very special visit from Santa.

### New Year's Dinner

December 31st 6PM at Mitra Market. Gather for a memorable final evening of the year and indulge in a festive dining experience complemented by live music, celebratory moments, and the warmth of togetherness. Raise a glass to the year gone by and the possibilities that await.

### LUMINA: New Year's Eve Celebration

December 31st 6PM at Mitra Market. Where light lives. Join us for an unforgettable evening as we welcome 2027 with hope, inspiration, and well-being. Guided by the spirit of LUMINA, celebrate new beginnings, meaningful connections, and the light that shines ahead. We look forward to sharing this magical night with you at The Westin Reserva Conchal.

Our Festive Pillars Guide Us, Always With Your Well-Being at the Heart

### Eat Well

Savor meaningful moments through experiences designed to nourish and inspire. From locally inspired flavors to hands-on culinary classes, discover new ways to Eat Well while connecting with Costa Rica’s vibrant culture and wellness-focused lifestyle.

### Move Well

Move with intention and discover the energy of Costa Rica. From sunrise yoga and scenic bike rides to beach walks and active recreation, our wellness experiences are designed to help you stay energized, find balance, and reconnect with both nature and yourself through movement.

### Sleep Well

True well-being begins with restorative rest. Embrace moments of calm, relaxation, and renewal as you reconnect with yourself and enjoy the comforts designed to help you Sleep Well throughout your stay.

### Westin Family

Immerse your loved ones in festive moments created for all ages. With nature-inspired adventures, creative workshops, and thoughtful programming, every experience encourages connection, discovery, and unforgettable holiday memories together.

### Heavenly Spa

Reawaken your senses with treatments designed to restore balance and serenity. Surrounded by nature and guided by expert hands, each experience invites deep relaxation, renewal, and a radiant sense of well-being throughout the festive season.

### Special Festive Events

Celebrate the season’s most meaningful moments with style and warmth. From heartwarming Christmas gatherings and elegant family dinners to vibrant New Year’s countdowns with live entertainment, every detail is crafted to spark joy, connection, and unforgettable memories under the LUMINA glow.
