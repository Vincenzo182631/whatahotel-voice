## Accommodations

Recharge and refresh during your stay at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa. Located near Playa Conchal, our all-inclusive hotel's airy villas, guest rooms and suites sit on...See More 2,400 acres of lush coastal property in Costa Rica. Relax in thoughtful accommodations designed to help you feel completely balanced. Enjoy dazzling views of the Playa Conchal beaches or the dynamic Costa Rican landscape from your suite's private balcony or terrace. Sleep soundly on our plush signature mattresses with down pillows and crisp cotton linens. Our Westin White Tea Heavenly™ bath amenities and waterfall showerheads give you a spa-like experience in the privacy of your own room. Your all-inclusive package includes 24-hour room service and a well-stocked minibar of soda, bottled water, liquor and beer. Stay connected with high-speed internet access and a flat screen LCD TV. Breathe easy - all of our hotel rooms and suites in Guanacaste, Costa Rica, are smoke free.See Less

## Bunk Bed Family Jr. Suite

Welcome to our Bunk Bed Family Jr. Suite, the perfect accommodation for your next family getaway in Guanacaste, Costa Rica! Relax in a spacious suite with a king-size bed, bunk bed, and sofa bed. Everyone in the family will have plenty of room to spread out and relax, with a cozy sitting area and all the amenities you need for a comfortable stay. All rooms are located on the second floor for added privacy

Learn More

Take a break and relax in our modern Bunk Bed Family Guest Room, designed with your family in mind. Our spacious Junior suite includes a comfortable king-size Heavenly® Bed for the parents and a bunk bed for the kids (age 12 and under). Plus, a sofa bed to accommodate an additional guest. The marble bathroom boasts a refreshing rainforest shower and Westin White Tea Heavenly™ bath amenities, as well as child-sized bathrobes, slippers, and a children's bath amenity kit.

Start your day off right with a freshly brewed cup of delicious Costa Rican coffee, made in the convenience of your own room with our in-room coffee maker. Everyone in the family will have plenty of room to spread out and relax, with a cozy sitting area and all the amenities you need for a comfortable stay. The suite is equipped with high-speed internet, a flat-screen TV, and a refreshment center stocked with kid-friendly drinks and snacks.

As a guest, you'll also have exclusive access to the Family Club Lounge and Family Concierge. The Family Club Lounge serves daily breakfast and an evening happy hour, and provides private check-in and checkout, daily turndown service, video games on request, and a Welcome Kids package.

## Family Club Lounge

A private check-in area ensures seamless check-ins for your group

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa?

The largest occupancy room at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa is the Family Jr St with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa?

Some rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa have fridges?

No, rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.

Can I request connecting rooms at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa?

Yes, connecting rooms can be requested at The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa, but they are subject to availability at check-in. Contact the resort in advance to add the request to your reservation.
