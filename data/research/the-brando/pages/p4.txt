### ACCOMMODATIONS

## Every villa is a cocoon,   a private space on Tetiaroa

Spread across the Onetahi motu and set back just off the beach are 35 private villas and one private residence, each a retreat within this sanctuary called Tetiaroa. There’s a flow to these spaces, where doors fold open to create breezeways to the outdoors, and transitions between rooms are subtle, open to entirely different spaces to live in. Welcome home.

## Sustainably Built

The Brando’s villas are constructed to conserve resources, limit waste, and tread gently on the natural environment. This pioneering approach has led The Brando to become the first resort globally to receive the prestigious LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design) Platinum Certification.

## Privacy Redefined

Separate and serene, each lodging serves as a solitary haven, a world apart within the resort’s embrace. The villas, cradled by foliage, offer versatile areas for sleep, serenity, literary escape, or quiet observation of nature’s march.

## Quiet Stays

The architecture of each accommodation marries traditional materials with modern design, featuring state-of-the-art insulation and ventilation systems. In the silence of a villa, with windows closed to the outside world, guests find themselves enveloped in a sanctuary of solitude, enjoying some of the most restorative sleep imaginable.

## One Bedroom Villa

### Overview

A thatched hut stands just off the main path, marking the start of an elevated walkway from the communal to the secluded.

There, crossing the threshold, door closed behind, the beach and ocean unveil themselves, and the foliage offers a sheltering presence. This space, rich with countless spots for repose, affords a retreat where solitude is shared only with those nearest to heart.

## Three Bedroom Villa

### Overview

At the secluded edge of Onetahi motu, a villa spacious enough to accommodate families or groups stands, surrounded by numerous alcoves for seclusion, rest, and personal time.

Embedded amidst palms and stretching across sands, the villa provides an experience akin to a personal resort. The daily rhythm flows from collective poolside leisure to solitary wanderings, reuniting as the sun dips for evening libations, embodying the Tetiaroa way of life.

## The Atoll

Tetiaroa Atoll is a unique sanctuary where natural splendor, Polynesian heritage, and refined hospitality converge.
