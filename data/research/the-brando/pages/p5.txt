## Kids Fly, Stay, Dine & Play Free Offer

Create unforgettable family memories on our private island paradise.

Children under 12 years old enjoy complimentary flights, accommodation, dining, and resort activities when traveling with their parents.

From shared flights to gourmet meals and exciting excursions, your little explorers experience the magic of Tetiaroa, entirely on us.

## The Ultimate Family Escape to Paradise

At The Brando, we believe the most precious moments are those shared with family. Our Kids Fly, Stay, Dine & Play Free offer transforms your family vacation into an extraordinary journey where young explorers can discover the wonders of Polynesian culture, pristine nature, and endless adventure. All while parents enjoy the peace of mind that comes with world-class luxury and sustainability.

From snorkeling in crystal-clear lagoons to learning traditional Polynesian crafts, from paddleboarding at sunset to stargazing on untouched beaches, your children will create memories that last a lifetime. And with everything included, you can focus on what truly matters: quality time together in one of the world’s most spectacular settings.

## How to benefit from this offer

**Complimentary flights, accommodation, and all meals** for children under 12, including shared Air Tetiaroa flights, villa stay for up to two children, and daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner at Beachcomber Café or Bob’s Bar.

**Resort activities included** when booked by parents—guided excursions, water sports, cultural experiences, and more (excluding diving excursions, reef quest and whale-watching experiences).

Available on both **All-Inclusive** and **Villa & Breakfast** packages, and **combinable with Early Bird and extended stay offers.**

Book for travel dates from **February 12, 2026 to March 31, 2028.**

**Upon confirmation of your reservation, our reservations team will reflect the applicable offer on the proforma invoice that will be sent to you.**

**Selling Dates:** January 15, 2026 to March 31, 2028

**Travel Dates:** February 12, 2026 to March 31, 2028

**Eligibility:**

Children must be under 12 years of age at time of travel.

Maximum of two children per villa.

Minimum stay of 3 consecutive nights required.

**Applicable Rates:**

Available on All-Inclusive and Villa & Breakfast rate plans.

Can be combined with Early Bird and Stay 4 Pay 3, Stay 5 Pay 4 offers.

**What’s Included for Children:**

_Flights:_ Complimentary shared flight to/from Tetiaroa via Air Tetiaroa (charter flights excluded).

_Accommodation:_ Free villa accommodation for the duration of your stay.

_Dining:_ Daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner at Beachcomber Café or Bob’s Bar (3-course menu, mineral water included).

_Activities:_ Complimentary participation in resort activities booked by parents (excludes diving excursions, reef quest and whale-watching experiences).

**Booking Requirements:**

This offer is not retroactive and is valid for new bookings only.

Existing bookings cannot be cancelled and rebooked under the same name and travel dates to benefit from this offer.

Subject to availability at time of booking.

**Air Tetiaroa:** Flight availability is subject to Air Tetiaroa’s schedule and capacity.

Charter flights are excluded from this offer.

The Christmas and New Year’s Eve festive dinner supplements shall apply to children.
