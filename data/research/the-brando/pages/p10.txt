## Fly Free

Are you dreaming of a tropical getaway in the mesmerizing atoll of Tetiaroa in French Polynesia? We have a special offer that will make your trip even more exciting. The Fly Free Offer allows you to fly to the enchanting atoll of Tetiaroa without any additional cost. Take advantage of this unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty and warm hospitality of this exotic destination.

## How to benefit from this offer

- To enjoy our exclusive offer, use the code “ **FLYFREE**” during the booking process.
- Book for a **minimum stay of 3 nights.**
- Our offer is applicable to all room types, allowing you to choose the accommodation that suits your preferences. Only valid for our All-Inclusive package.

**Terms & Conditions**

_Eligible Partners: Our offer is open to all eligible partners, allowing everyone to take advantage of our services._

_Eligible Markets: We extend our services to all eligible markets, ensuring a wide reach for travelers worldwide._

_Code Offer: To enjoy our exclusive offer, use the code “FLYFREE” during the booking process before March 31st, 2027._

Travel dates:

April 1st, 2026 to March 31st, 2027

The Fly Free offer applies only to flights between Papeete (Tahiti) and Tetiaroa.

_Applicable Room Types and Packages: Our offer is applicable to all room types, allowing you to choose the accommodation that suits your preferences. Only valid for our All-Inclusive package._

_Minimum Stay: A minimum stay of 3 nights is required to qualify for this offer._

_Inter-Island Round Trip Flights: For your convenience, we provide round trip flights between Tahiti and Tetiaroa, allowing you to explore and experience the beauty of both islands._

_Free Air Tetiaroa Flights: As part of our offer, we are pleased to provide free airfare for specific passenger counts based on the type of villa or residence you book:_

_One-Bedroom Villa & One-Bedroom Family Villa: Free flights for 2 passengers_

_Two-Bedroom Villa: Free flights for 4 passengers_

_Three-Bedroom Villa & Teremoana Residence: Free flights for 6 passengers_

_New Bookings and Availability: Please note that this offer is not retroactive and is only valid for new bookings. Availability is subject to a limited inventory, and the offer may not be available if the desired dates or accommodation are fully booked._

_Contract Terms: This offer is applicable to the 2026-2027 rates and is subject to the other terms outlined in the contract. We encourage you to review the contract thoroughly before making your booking._

_Commission Calculation: Commission amounts will be calculated based on the rates after the applicable discounts have been applied._
