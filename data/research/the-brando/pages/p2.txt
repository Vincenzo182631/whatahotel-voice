## Long Stay

Settle into serenity with our _Long Stay Offer_ and let the rhythms of Tetiaroa slow time down.

When you stay 7 consecutive nights or more, enjoy an exceptional **30% discount** on your villa rate.

This is your invitation to fully immerse yourself in the island’s beauty, culture, and luxurious comfort—all while enjoying outstanding value.

## How to benefit from this offer

- Stay a minimum of **7 consecutive nights** in the **same villa category**.
- Select the **Long Stay** offer during the booking process.
- Our offer is applicable to **all room types**, allowing you to choose the accommodation that suits your preferences. Only valid for our **All-Inclusive** package.
