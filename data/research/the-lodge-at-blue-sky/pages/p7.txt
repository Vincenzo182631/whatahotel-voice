## A cliffside wellness sanctuary dedicated to physical and energetic alignment

## The Edge Spa

## Treatments

Built on a dramatic precipice, the Edge Spa invites guests to enjoy a variety of indulgent treatments and mindfulness pursuits. Water is a key component of this sanctuary's design: The five luxurious treatment rooms overlook Alexander Creek while the soothing murmur of the brook encourages relaxation and flow. Treatments are locally inspired and feature products that utilize natural, bloom-to-bottle ingredients. Mindfulness experiences include indoor and outdoor yoga, family yoga, guided meditation, and vertical vinyasa: aerial yoga which uses large swaths of silk suspended from tree branches in a secluded forest.

## Edge Sanctuary

Tucked into a secluded aspen grove that is situated along the tranquil banks of Alexander Creek, the new Edge Sanctuary is a hidden haven that is dedicated to energetic and vibrational healing. Our resident energy healers hold all of their treatments in this space which include intuitive energy journeys, sacred plant ceremonies, healing sound baths, heart-opening tea ceremonies, chakra balancing treatments and master numerology readings. Listen to the murmur of the creek – and the crackle of the wood-burning fire in the colder months – as you relax in this cozy glamping tent and receive profound healing on a physical, emotional and energetic level.
