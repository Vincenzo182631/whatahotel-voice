# Discover 4,000 acres of mountain serenity just minutes from Park City

## Reconnect with Yourself and the Land

Just beyond Park City, The Lodge at Blue Sky spans 4,000 private acres of untamed wilderness. Modern, organic design blends into the landscape, where cliffside accommodations overlook creeks and sunlit peaks. Fill your days with horseback rides, spa rituals inspired by nature and farm-driven meals rooted in the rhythm of the land. Here, luxury lives in stillness, space and the freedom to explore.
