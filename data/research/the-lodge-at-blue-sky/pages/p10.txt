# The Auberge Concert Series

Experience live music in the heart of the Wasatch Mountains with Blue Sky’s concert series. Set across 4,000 acres of open landscape, the series brings acclaimed artists to the ranch for intimate performances that pair great music with the spirit of the American West. Each evening offers a unique way to enjoy Blue Sky.

On November 21, multi-platinum country star HARDY takes the stage at The Lodge at Blue Sky, Auberge Collection, for an intimate evening of music in one of Utah's most exclusive concert settings. Known for headlining amphitheaters and arenas, HARDY will bring his signature blend of country and Southern rock to a rare, small-scale performance, offering guests the opportunity to experience one of country music's most in-demand artists from just a few feet away.
