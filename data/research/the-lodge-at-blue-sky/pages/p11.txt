# The Class: Blue Sky Residency

As the landscape quiets and the air turns crisp, Blue Sky becomes a space for recalibration. Led by Founding Teacher Jaycee Gossett, the practice of The Class is nurtured by the vast terrain and early winter stillness that surrounds. Daily experiences center on grounding practices, steady breathwork and intentional movement that brings the body back into balance, offering a reset before the close of the year.

## Meet Your Teacher: Jaycee Gossett

Jaycee’s Classes create space to discover strengths, explore freedom and reconnect to spirit. She brings her love of music and dance to The Class and encourages her students to move, feel and live with full expression. Her Classes are challenging, soulful, liberating and welcoming.

Before joining The Class, Jaycee traveled around the world studying movement, competing in ballroom dance and working in television, where she hosted and produced on The Travel Channel, Z Living TV and Amazon Prime. She is a founding Teacher of The Class and Chief Experience Officer.
