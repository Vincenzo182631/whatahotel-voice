##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is The Lodge at Blue Sky located?** The Lodge at Blue Sky is located at 27649 Old Lincoln Highway in Wanship, Utah, just outside Park City. Set on 4,000 private acres between the Wasatch and Uinta mountain ranges, the resort offers a secluded mountain retreat within easy reach of Park City's skiing, dining and cultural attractions.

**How far is The Lodge at Blue Sky from the airport?** The Lodge at Blue Sky is approximately 43 miles, or a 40-minute drive, from Salt Lake City International Airport (SLC). Private airport transfers can be arranged year-round through the resort for an additional fee.

**When is the best time to visit The Lodge at Blue Sky?** The Lodge at Blue Sky is a year-round destination, with each season offering a different way to experience Utah's mountain landscape. Spring through fall is ideal for horseback riding, hiking, fly-fishing and sporting pursuits, while winter brings skiing, snowshoeing and other snow-filled adventures in and around Park City.

**What dining experiences are available at The Lodge at Blue Sky?** Dining at Blue Sky ranges from refined mountain cuisine at Yuta and casual fare at the Tavern to open-fire cooking at Guy Ritchie's WildKitchen. Guests can also enjoy whiskey tastings, distillery tours and locally inspired dining at the on-property High West Distillery.

**What experiences and activities are available at The Lodge at Blue Sky?** Blue Sky offers an extensive collection of outdoor adventures and immersive experiences across its 4,000-acre estate. Guests can enjoy horseback riding, fly-fishing, hiking, mountain biking and sporting clays alongside farm experiences at Gracie's Farm, wellness programming and seasonal culinary adventures. Winter brings additional opportunities for skiing, snowshoeing and cross-country exploration.

**What is the Auberge Concert Series at The Lodge at Blue Sky?** The Auberge Concert Series brings celebrated musicians to Blue Sky for intimate live performances in a spectacular mountain setting. Select events feature exclusive concert packages and curated itineraries combining music with the resort's signature experiences.

**What ski amenities and services does The Lodge at Blue Sky offer?** Blue Sky offers a private Ski Lounge at Park City Mountain Resort, providing a convenient home base for days on the slopes. The resort can also arrange transportation, equipment rentals and personalized ski experiences for a seamless mountain adventure.
