# Summer Together with Auberge

InquireInquire

Summer at Blue Sky is an invitation to slow down, reconnect and share in the spirit of the land. Gather with family and friends for a morning on horseback through alpine meadows and sagebrush hills, where quiet moments, expansive views and Western tradition come together.

## Tavern Breakfast Ride with Barbour

In partnership with Barbour, The Lodge at Blue Sky introduces a heritage-driven equestrian experience that blends timeless British fieldwear with the spirit of the American West. Guests begin by selecting a jacket from the Barbour Borrow Closet before setting out on horseback across Blue Sky’s expansive alpine terrain. Ride through sagebrush hills and open meadows en route to The Tavern at Blue Sky, an original 1800s cabin set within a pristine high-alpine field, where a Western-inspired breakfast awaits. Pause to take in the stillness of the landscape before a leisurely ride back through the mountains.
