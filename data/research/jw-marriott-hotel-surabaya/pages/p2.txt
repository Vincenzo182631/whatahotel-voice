### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya is the Executive lounge access with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya?

Yes, all rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have fridges?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
