# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya

## Stay at our luxury 5-star hotel in Surabaya, Indonesia

Perfectly located near Tunjungan Plaza Mall in the Surabaya, Indonesia city center, JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya is a luxurious, 5-star hotel in Surabaya City for business and family vacations in a vibrant destination. Experience the height of 5-star luxury in one of our 407 beautifully hotel-appointed rooms, including 22 extended-stay apartments. Stay in the heart of Surabaya’s shopping and business...See More district, minutes from heritage landmark of Kota Lama Surabaya, Tugu Pahlawan and Sunan Ampel Mosque. Savor delectable Japanese, Chinese, and Indonesian cuisines and the city’s newest steakhouse in our four distinctive restaurants. Gather in versatile meeting and event space with custom catering and expert planning. Family-friendly features at our Surabaya hotel include a pool with panoramic views and the JW Terrace by the Pool venue for movie nights. With ample self-parking and a 24-hour gym and massage service, JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya provides comfort, relaxation, and style for your visit to Indonesia.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for Rp100000.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya is Surabaya Juanda International Airport (SUB). SUB is located approximately 35.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have an airport shuttle to Surabaya Juanda International Airport (SUB)?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya has an airport shuttle to Surabaya Juanda International Airport (SUB).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Surabaya has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
