# Welcome to the City and Our Luxury Hotel in Geneva

## Experiences

Conveniently located close to the most recognized Swiss factories, international organizations including the UN, OMS, OMC, OMPI, diplomatic missions and consulates, and museums, Hotel President Wilson...See More is the ideal choice for both leisure and business travelers. Crossing the Mont-Blanc Bridge towards the left bank, the Jardin Anglais (English Garden) awaits, as well as the famous Flower Clock—a reflection of Geneva’s expertise in watchmaking. Steps away from our Lake Geneva spa hotel is the National Monument, which commemorates Geneva joining the rest of Switzerland in 1815. And on the right bank is the Brunswick Monument, containing the tomb of Charles II, Duke of Brunswick. Home to over 40 public and private museums and numerous art galleries, Geneva beckons to be explored. For seasoned aficionados and curious ingénues, the Art and History Museum, Patek Philippe Museum, International Red Cross and Red Crescent Museum will pique various interests ranging from archaeology to arts.See Less

## Outdoor Heated Swimming Pool

Overlooking Lake Geneva and the majestic Alps and Mont Blanc beckoning from the distance, our outdoor heated swimming pool and adjacent Terrace offer moments of unforgettable relaxation in a piece of heaven.

## Rive Droite Swimming Pool

Enjoy a leisurely swim or simply relax on a deck chair at the hotel’s heated outdoor swimming pool. Located on the first-floor terrace, our soothing swimming pool overlooks Lake Geneva and the panoramic Alps and Mont Blanc range across the distance. Guests enjoy a light menu of snacks and refreshing drinks from the Summerhouse. Hotel guests enjoy complimentary access during summertime, while visitors may access the pool for a fee of 75 CHF/weekdays or 115 CHF/weekends and public holidays, upon availability. Access to the swimming pool is free for children younger than 5, children from 6 to 11 benefit from a rate of 45 CHF/day or 70 CHF/weekend and public holidays. Fees are per person and include the use of a sun bed, a towel, a welcome bag and access to a locker. We kindly ask that guests under 16 be accompanied by an adult at all times. The swimming pool is only open during summertime from May to September. Please contact the hotel in order to know the exact opening dates.

Highlights Around Lake Geneva

### Best of Geneva

A Lake Geneva cruise around the famous Jet d'Eau, a visit at Palace of Nations or St. Pierre Cathedral or a shopping spree in Geneva Old Town or on Rue du Rhône, Geneva provides numerous stunning venues to discover.

### Perfect Alternatives

Nearby you will find the CERN (European Organization for Nuclear Research), the Floral Clock at Jardin Anglais, the Mont-Salève, the Bains des Pâquis or the Maison Cailler Chocolate Factory among many others…

### Gastronomy

The culinary offer in Geneva is very vast and constantly developing, with cuisines from all around the world. Several Michelin star restaurants with renowned Chefs have also opened in the region, among which our hotel with the Bayview by Michel Roth restaurant (1 Michelin star).

### Activities

All year round, Geneva offers you an unrivalled range of activities, with a lot of water sports and lake cruises, mountains hiking, but also extreme sports for the adventurers, without forgetting the winter sports in the poshest ski resorts in the world, easily reachable by train or by car. In summer, don’t miss the various festivals with open air concerts, food booths, open-air cinemas and other animations that are happening in the city.

### International Geneva

As an economic and financial center, Geneva distinguishes itself with its various international organizations, diplomatic missions and non-governmental organizations. Renowned as a capital of peace, it is also the European headquarter of the United Nations and houses the famous International Red Cross and Red Crescent Museum.

### Indigenous

Cradle of fine watchmaking, Geneva will make you dream with its luxurious brands and unique pieces. Recount the Swiss watchmaking history and discover the emblematic places of the city such as the flower clock, the famous Jet d’eau, and the Patek Philippe Museum.

### Shopping

Real paradise for shopping, Geneva is renowned for its famous Rue du Rhône, accommodating the most luxurious brands of fine jewelry, watchmaking and haute couture.

### Culture

Endowed with a rich cultural heritage, Calvin’s city owns countless treasures such as the St-Pierre Cathedral and the Reformers Wall. In addition to its unique museums, you can visit the CERN, the biggest physics laboratory in the world and its impressive Globe of Science and Innovation, housing the “Universe and particles” exhibition.

Regarding entertainment, the city attracts every year tourists from all around the world with the International Motor show, the International Fine Watchmaking Exhibition (SIHH) and the European Business Aviation Convention & Exhibition (EBACE).

All year round, Geneva also welcomes numerous music festivals and offers a vast programming of shows, concerts, plays, ballets and exhibitions.
