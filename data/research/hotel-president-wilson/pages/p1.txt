# Welcome to Hotel President Wilson, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Geneva

## Experience the Genevan lifestyle at our Lake Geneva hotel

Facing Lake Geneva and minutes from the parks and boutiques of rue du Rhône, our iconic 5-star hotel in Geneva, Switzerland, is one of the city's most contemporary addresses, framed by Mont Blanc. Our...See More luxury hotel on Lake Geneva features 204 guest rooms and 22 suites with elegant furnishings, Bang & Olufsen LCD screens, and distinctive city or lake views. Discover Spa La Mer, the first spa in Switzerland to offer the brand's premium skincare treatments, plus the indulgence of a private Spa Suite. Dining spans the seasonal Mediterranean setting of Rive Droite by Michel Roth, the Michelin-starred Bayview Restaurant by Michel Roth, and the rich Lebanese flavors of Arabesque. Guests often highlight the waterfront location and calm atmosphere at this Lake Geneva hotel. As one of the premier 5-star hotels in Geneva, Switzerland, we also welcome trained pets.See Less

### President Wilson Luxury Hotel History

Designed by architect and hotelier Marcel Jacquignon, Hotel President opened its doors for the first time on May 18, 1962. It was bought by Swissair and Nestlé in 1980, then later acquired by the Tamman family in 1989. In 1992, the family renovated and expanded the hotel with a 190 million CHF investment that included an additional wing of 80 guestrooms and suites, a conference center, underground...See More parking, and a terrace with an outdoor pool. The hotel reopened in 1996 as the new Hotel President Wilson, in honor of the 28th President of the United States of America, Woodrow Wilson—founder and relentless defender of the League of Nations. The Hotel President Wilson became part of Starwood Hotels and Resorts and a member of the Luxury Collection in 2000. From 2007 to 2010, it underwent a 40 million CHF renovation process covering public areas and characterized by three main themes conceptualized by South-Korean artist Jae-Hyo Lee: water, surrounding landscape and light.See Less

The Luxury Collection Concierge

Once a guest has arrived at Hotel President Wilson, the concierge team stands ready to be of service seven days a week, 24 hours a day, dedicated to ensuring that each guest is able to unlock the best of Geneva.

Each Luxury Collection Concierge is a bilingual, certified destination expert—a number of our concierges proudly wear the “Les Сlef d’or,” the gilt insignia of the celebrated international concierge society—eager to help create an unforgettable stay.

From dinner reservations to floral arrangements and local area information and tickets, no assignment is too small. We are also pleased to coordinate private limousine services and shipping services.

When it comes to local area information, our concierge is the premier authority, whether guests are looking for sightseeing recommendations or nearby excursions. Our Concierge team highly recommends visits to the Art and History Museum, International Red Cross Museum, Natural History Museum, plus the old town with the Cathedral and Town Hall, hidden lanes and flower-filled squares. Outside of the city are the prestigious Swiss vineyards, superior ski resorts of the Swiss Alps, and these picturesque locations in all of Switzerland: Gruyere, Fribourg, Lugano, Lucerne, and more.

The Luxury Collection Concierge will be happy to assist in making reservations and arrangements for all activities listed. After normal business hours, our front desk is available to attend to all requests.

Your Daily News

Luxury Collection’s cooperation with NewspaperDirect allows guests to receive their usual daily newspaper in their rooms on each day of their trip. Guests can choose from over 1,000 daily newspapers/magazines during their stay.

While the world has grown smaller, receiving same-day editions of a familiar newspaper from home still provides a welcome surprise and still represents a truly indulgent and appreciated luxury.

The Luxury Collection has partnered with NewspaperDirect, a global leader in multi-channel publishing and distribution, to provide its guests with this service. Guests of Hotel President Wilson can choose from a vast range of over 600 newspapers and magazines from 73 countries and published in over 39 languages, and have their favorite delivered to their guest room throughout their stay.
