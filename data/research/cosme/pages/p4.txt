## Elios Spa

Named after the Greek God of the Sun, Elios Spa pays tribute to the extraordinary setting in which it is nestled, drawing on the warmth and appeasing energy of Paros, a land gifted with natural resources, plants and herbs that cure the soul and body. Embark on a personalized wellness journey that embraces the slow-paced Cycladic way of life with signature treatments and rituals tailored to individual needs. Featuring two treatment rooms and a relaxation area with indoor jacuzzi, Elios Spa is an oasis that lends itself to physical rejuvenation, as well as mental and emotional regeneration.

## Stargazing Platform

Paros’ inky black skies showcase glittering constellations, meteor showers, planets, the Milky Way, moon and endless other celestial objects that will take your breath away. The quiet bay of Agioi Anargyroi, where Cosme is found, is home to some of the clearest skies in Greece and a perfect combination of elements that create ideal seeing conditions for stargazing experiences that are second to none.

## The Empiria Lounge

The Empiria Lounge sits at the heart of Parikia, just steps away from Paros’ port. Whether guests have just arrived on the enchanting island of Paros or are awaiting their departure, there stands the perfect rest point, where guests are welcome to securely store their luggage and discover the charming streets of Paros’ capital or find a few moments of relaxation in the elegant indoor space and inviting outdoor area. Open daily from 9am to 9pm, Empiria Lounge’s amenities and complimentary offerings include: Coffee, beverages, and refreshments, local treats, Wi-Fi and secure luggage storage space.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Cosme, a Luxury Collection Resort, Paros have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Cosme, a Luxury Collection Resort, Paros offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Cosme, a Luxury Collection Resort, Paros have a pool on-site?

Yes, Cosme, a Luxury Collection Resort, Paros has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does Cosme, a Luxury Collection Resort, Paros have an on-site spa?

Yes, Elios Spa is located on-site at Cosme, a Luxury Collection Resort, Paros. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Cosme, a Luxury Collection Resort, Paros?

Ekatontapyliani Church, The Frankish Castle, Ancient Marble Quarries, Naoussa Town, Kolymbithres Beach and more are nearby Cosme, a Luxury Collection Resort, Paros.
