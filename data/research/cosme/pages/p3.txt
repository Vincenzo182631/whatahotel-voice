## Dining

Celebrated Greek chef Yiannis Kioroglou is leading the culinary journey at Cosme, bringing his passion for ‘Medite-grecian’ gastronomy to the hotel’s stunning outlets. Having joined Cosme from La Petite...See More Maison in Cannes and La Guerite in Saint Barth and with a background in Europe’s most favorite dining spots, Yiannis remains faithful to his Greek roots and breathes Greek ‘philoxenia’ in all his culinary creations.See Less

### Savor Traditional Parian Recipes Reimagined

Try the gouna, an age-old Parian recipe with mackerel, reimagined by chef Yiannis Kioroglou for Parostia Restaurant with aubergine purée, melon and pickled celery.

### Parostia Restaurant

Parostia sits right on the beachfront and takes its name from the stone oven of traditional Parian houses. Vibrant and joyful, Parostià Restaurant acts as the ultimate lunch and dinner spot right next to Cosme's private beach, showcasing the generosity and laidback, yet rich epicurean traditions of Paros, Greece and the Mediterranean Sea. During the day, life is buzzing under the stripped shadows produced...See More by pergola straws, and during the night, soft lights invite guests to relax and enjoy the Medite-grecian way of life.See Less

## Circe Bar

Circe Bar is named after mythological enchantress and goddess Circe, who was renowned for her vast knowledge of potions and herbs. Located right next to the action, Circe Bar is adjacent to the half-moon pool and a few steps away from Parostià Restaurant. A beautiful lounge crowns the bar with sensational sunset views over the bay of Naoussa.
