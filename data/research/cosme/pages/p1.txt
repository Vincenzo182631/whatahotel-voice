And then there was Cosme. ## Cycladic Architecture

Design details throughout reference the style and beauty of Paros - all buildings are clean-lined and made with local stone showcasing harmonious architectural compositions inspired by the island’s bright fishing villages and indigenous houses. Even the hotel’s half-moon pool is an ode to the destination, reflecting the shape of the bay, where Cosme stands. Designed as a natural extension of Naoussa, meandering pathways throughout the property create an intimate village-style feel, reminiscent of the vibrant harbor town itself, encouraging guests to revel in chance encounters and serendipitous moments of discovery.

Celebrated Greek chef Yiannis Kioroglou is leading the culinary journey at Cosme, bringing his passion for ‘Medite-grecian’ cuisine to the hotel’s stunning outlets. Having joined Cosme from La Petite Maison in Cannes and La Guerite in Saint Barth and with a background in Europe’s most favorite dining spots, Yiannis remains faithful to his Greek roots and breathes Greek ‘philoxenia’ in all his culinary creations.

## Half-Moon Infinity Pool

Cosme’s iconic infinity pool is designed in a half-moon shape, mirroring the bay of Naoussa, where Cosme stands. Seeming as a naturally-shaped balcony over the sea, guests relaxing by the pool appreciate panoramic views towards the harbor town, the sea and the small fishing port with the traditional caiques, as the water blends with the Aegean.

Free to Imagine

## In the Name of Cosme

Cosme takes its name from the Greek word "cosmos," being the first compound of the world “cosmopolitan,” or else citizen of the world. Sitting at the heart of the Cyclades, within the Aegean Sea, Paros is an island with a cosmopolitan personality; an international trade hub since ancient times, where merchants from around the world would meet to exchange goods, stories, traditions.
