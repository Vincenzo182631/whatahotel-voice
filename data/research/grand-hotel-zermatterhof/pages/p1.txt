# The Grand Hotel Zermatterhof

The Grand Hotel Zermatterhof lies at the very heart of Zermatt and is host to royalty, Hollywood celebrities and mountaineers. Steeped in history with views of the Matterhorn, the hotel continues welcoming guests from around the world who enjoy experiencing personal service, timeless surroundings and the very best nature has to offer.

# _146_ _years_   _of history_

### 1829

**A Precious Good**

Zermatt is a remote village in the mountains, whose citizens live off the land and fight hunger and hardship.

### 1865

**The mountain of mountains**

After the incredible Matterhorn ascent with father and son Taugwalder, the mountain village becomes a world famous destination for tourists.

### 1874

**For a secure future**

Local vicar Josef Ruden encourages his fellow citizens not to sell the best plots of land in the village to foreign prospectors.

**A family business**

The families of Zermatt build the Hotel Zermatt as a community project in order not to inflict huge debt on the local community. Every burgher (citizen) young and old has to contribute to the ambitious construction project.

### 1876 to 1879

**Built on solid ground**

The services rendered during construction are converted to share capital for the families, which will render a dividend to the burghers once the hotel becomes profitable.

### 1891

**A steep ascent**

Once the Brig-Visp-Zermatt and the Gornergrat railways are built, the rich and titled travel to Zermatt and the golden age of mountaineering begins.

### 1880

**In good society**

The Hotel Zermatt is initally leased by Alexander Seiler. As of 1920 the Burgergemeinde (burgher council) runs the hotel itself come rain or shine.

**Discreet at the hightest level**

Over the years guests from the world of politics, industry, art and showbusiness such as Walt Disney appreciate the absolute discretion the safe haven Zermatterhof offers.

### From 1927

**The White Miracle**

Zermatt welcomes guests for its first winter season. Local boy Martin Julen is a waiter at the Zermatterhof and wins the Swiss Alpine Ski championship in 1951.

**Bound to tradition**

Every year on Corpus Christi day, the most important religious day of the year, the Zermatterhof prepares a traditional reception with local wine to the community.

**With engaged foresight**

The world class team of the Zermatterhof puts its heart and soul into caring for its inernational clientele. Its two Lipizzan horses collect the Zermatterhof guests at the train station.

**To care and preserve**

The Burgergemeinde Zermatt and its burghers pledge to preserve and develop the collective achievement of their ancestors for the future together with the Matterhorn Group.

# Your arrival

### Arrival by car

Zermatt is car – free . There is a shuttle train from Täsch, where you park your car, to Zermatt every 20 minutes. Collection from Zermatt station by electric bus can be arranged. The hotel is 10 minutes’ walk from the station.

View map

### Pickup by horse-drawn carriage

Gaetana, Herceg and Krabbe our Lipizzan horses would be delighted to pick you up from Zermatt station between 1pm and 5pm. Advanced booking recommended.
