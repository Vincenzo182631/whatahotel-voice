# Rooms

Every one of our 28 rooms are individually designed while sharing eclectic black and white touches, Savoir beds, unique artworks, thoughtful surprises and delicious treats. A chic, monochrome design runs throughout the hotel, complemented by mahogany-lined walls, open fireplaces and smart leather furniture, lending everything a cosy, contemporary look and feel. Lighthearted prints by René Gruau, drawings by French caricaturist Sem, and intriguing objets such as antique globes and marble busts add a touch of style and whimsy to the surroundings.

### Interleading rooms

**Interleading rooms** feature an external door that links multiple rooms, allowing access to all rooms booked as interleading.

We also offer **family wings**, which connect several rooms with internal doors.

These rooms can accommodate four to eight people, depending on availability, and must be booked directly through our reservations team. To check availability, please email us.
