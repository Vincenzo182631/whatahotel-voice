# What not to miss

Discover what to taste and sip, where to slumber and venture, what experiences to enjoy, and who to connect with during your stay at Hotel 41.

### Eat

Indulge in our eggs benedict at Hotel 41; savour in poached eggs atop crispy English muffins, draped in velvety hollandaise sauce, and accompanied by Canadian bacon. This iconic breakfast dish is a must-try, setting the standard for luxury dining experiences.

### Drink

Enjoy an exquisite passionfruit martini in our Executive Lounge at Hotel 41. Open to our in-house guests only, this is the perfect place to enjoy a well-earned cocktail after exploring all that London has to offer.

Our Conservatory Suite at Hotel 41 is a luxurious retreat boasting exquisite design and unparalleled comfort. With its spacious layout, this suite offers a serene sanctuary in the heart of London. Relax in style and enjoy panoramic views of London's skyline.
