# Dining & drinks

Hotel 41’s refined lounge is the perfect place to kick-back and relax, sip a glass of chilled champagne and recount the day’s events, or order a meal and dine in supreme comfort, waited on by the hotel’s bevy of butler-like staff. For more dining options, The Rubens adjoins Hotel 41 which is connected via a concealed door. The Rubens offers two gourmet restaurants and a happening bar without the need for Hotel 41 guests to venture outside.

### Our Executive Lounge

Our refined Executive Lounge is the heart of the hotel. This is where guests are first welcomed, where they are encouraged to relax and unwind by the fire, and perhaps indulge in an expertly mixed cocktail, a classic British comfort dish, or a light bite.

### Complimentary treats

Guests of Hotel 41 enjoy complimentary refreshments and generous hospitality, including the hotel’s unique ‘plunder the pantry’ concept, whereby a selection of sweet and savoury treats is laid out daily until late, alongside a ‘help yourself’ ice cream fridge.
