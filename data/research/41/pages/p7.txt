# Our location

Hotel 41 is the perfect base for exploring many of London's best known attractions. Take a short walk from the hotel and you'll find Buckingham Palace, The Royal Mews and The Queen's Gallery. The River Thames, Houses of Parliament, Big Ben and The London Eye are also within walking distance. The nearest tube station is Victoria, which will enable you to reach London's attractions easily. Trafalgar Square, Oxford Street, Piccadilly Circus and The West End are only a short tube ride away.

### Within easy reach

Valet parking is available at the nearby secure car park with a charge of £50 for up to four hours or £70 per 24-hours, available daily with our concierge team from 8am to 8pm. Should the value of your car exceed £300,000 please contact us directly.

Central London operates a congestion charge zone between the hours of 7am to 10pm daily, excluding Christmas Day. The current daily charge when entering the zone is £15. There is also a £12.50 ULEZ charge.
