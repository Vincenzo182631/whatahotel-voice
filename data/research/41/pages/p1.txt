Offering luxury rooms and Wi-Fi in public areas, 41 Hotel London is 2 km from Coca-Cola London Eye Riverside Observation Wheel and 25 minutes' walk from the scientifically spellbinding Science Museum. Located in the vicinity of Westminster, the hotel overlooks the courtyard and invites guests to have a drink at a lounge bar.

The 28 rooms at this property are equipped with comforts such as air conditioning, as well as an iPod dock and high-speed internet. They come with attached bathrooms. The marble bathrooms feature hair dryers and bathrobes.

The property provides access to a cinema for guests' enjoyment.

Staying in this London hotel you can choose from 4 restaurants to have dinner at. Located within easy reach of the 41 London, the restaurant Bag O' Nails offers a British menu.

This 5-star hotel in Central London with an array of dining experiences that will tantalize your taste buds, a mere 1.3 km from Mayfair. The 41 is 6 minutes on foot from Apollo Victoria Theatre and in close proximity to Victoria underground station. Nature lovers will enjoy the vicinity of The Mound, located about metres away. The nearby cultural experience includes the famous British Museum, which is 2.6 km away. The smoke-free 41 Hotel provides a place to stay 3 km from the Marylebone train station.
