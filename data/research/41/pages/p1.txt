Hotel 41

# An intimate hideaway

Just steps from Buckingham Palace, Hotel 41 evokes the charm of a private members’ club. With a prestigious Forbes Five-Star rating, this sophisticated enclave features 28 exquisite rooms and suites tailored for discerning travellers, just a five-minute walk from Victoria Station. At the heart of the hotel, a historic lounge and dining room - exclusively for in-house guests - offers a refined retreat, while the warm and attentive team delivers a uniquely personalised service, where no request is too much trouble. Hotel 41 is a proud part of The Red Carnation Hotel Collection.

### BEAUTY IN EVERY DETAIL

Individually styled in a black and white theme inspired by French cartoonist SEM, all guest rooms boast mahogany panelling, sumptuous bedding and marble bathrooms, while orchids, scented candles and polished chrome and brass abound.

### Plunder the pantry

With the relaxed and discrete feel of an exclusive private members' club, enjoy an informal and personalised dining and drinks experience around-the-clock. Our stunning mahogany Executive Lounge awaits, where guests of Hotel 41 enjoy round-the-clock refreshments and generous hospitality, including the hotel’s unique ‘plunder the pantry’ concept, whereby a selection of sweet and savoury treats is laid out daily until late, alongside a ‘help yourself’ ice cream fridge.
