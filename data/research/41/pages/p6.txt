# Meet our team

The extraordinary characters at Hotel 41 passionately care about each and every guest experience. Our warm and welcoming team provides the perfect introduction to our capital city, and every one of us is deeply committed to giving the very best in all we do.

Discover Career Opportunities

### Malcolm Hendry

#### Managing Director

malcolm41@41hotel.com

The epitome of a ‘people person’, Malcolm Hendry is driven by knowing that the guests in all of the hotels he oversees are enjoying their stays. After completing a degree in Hospitality and Business Management, Malcolm was Assistant Manager, then Deputy Manager of Speyside’s Craigellachie Hotel. By 2004, Malcolm was General Manager of The Rubens at the Palace, Hotel 41 and bbar, and Red Carnation’s first Scottish property, 100 Princes Street.

### Adéle Coetzee

#### Resident Manager

acoetzee@41hotel.com

As Resident Manager, Adéle is here to assist with all your special and last-minute surprises such as proposals, anniversary, or birthday celebrations, babysitting services and pet-sitting services. Adéle would also be happy to confirm existing reservations or make enquires on your behalf for your stay.

### Ben Kelliher

#### Executive Chef

bkelliher@rubenshotel.com

Cultivating his skills at renowned London establishments, including The Chesterfield Mayfair, Ben Kelliher's love for food flourished. Currently at the helm of The Rubens at the Palace and Hotel 41, he and his team are dedicated to sourcing fresh, local, and seasonal produce. His food is a testament to his culinary prowess, and under his guidance, guests are treated to extraordinary flavours that linger fondly in their memories.

### Angus Rose-Bristow

#### Sales Manager

arosebristow@rchmail.com

Angus Rose-Bristow is responsible for developing strategic partnerships, growing corporate business, and fostering long-term client relationships. He works closely with clients to understand their needs and create tailored solutions, and his focus is on driving commercial success while ensuring every interaction reflects the warmth, service excellence, and attention to detail for which Red Carnation Hotels is renowned.

### Marlen Obertelli

#### Assistant Resident Manager

mobertelli@41hotel.com

Marlen will be happy to assist with recommending and securing the best tables for you in some of London’s top venues. With a wealth of contacts that she has acquired over years, Marlen provides expert advice on the best attractions across the city and popular excursions further afield. Marlen is constantly on the lookout for new shows, gallery exhibitions and pop-up events to ensure that you enjoy the best London has to offer.

### Sergio Gimeno

### Kristina Veselyte
