# Executive Lounge

Our stunning mahogany Executive Lounge awaits, truly the beating heart of Hotel 41. Our refined lounge is the perfect place to kick-back and relax, sip a glass of chilled champagne and recount the day’s events, or order a meal and dine in supreme comfort. For more dining options The Rubens at the Palace adjoins Hotel 41 which is connected via a concealed door. The Rubens offers two gourmet restaurants and a happening bar without the need for Hotel 41 guests to venture outside.
