Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert
Within the sanctuary of Wadi Khadeja, The Ritz Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert offers the chance to experience romance and relaxation, adventure and natural beauty. Located in a 1,235-acre gated private Nature Reserve, this luxury hotel is an intimate resort offering 109 private villas with terraces and pools amongst the natural desert landscape. Located in Ras Al Khaimah, the resort offers guests the opportunity to enjoy the Equestrian center, Adventure center, Falconry school, or, alternatively, the extensive Spa and Wellness offerings that include The Rainforest, a rejuvenating hydrothermal experience enhancing mind, body and skin. Complimentary guest access to its sister resort’s beach elevates the traditional Bedouin desert experience with a luxurious touch.
Complimentary Vitality Pool Access
We are pleased to offer our guests complimentary access to our Vitality Pool as part of their stay. Please note that access is subject to availability and is available to guests aged 16 and above

Desert Luxury in Ras Al Khaimah
Just north of Dubai, The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert invites guests to immerse themselves in nature and culture within a 1,235-acre protected reserve. The resort’s Experience Concierge curates unforgettable moments, from desert treks on camel or horseback to private archery lessons, stargazing under clear desert skies, and engaging activities at Ritz Kids designed to inspire young explorers. Luxurious villas offer serene privacy, blending traditional Bedouin design with modern comfort.
Discover
Desert Discovery
Awaken your inner explorer at the Adventure Center. Experience the thrill of falconry, a cherished Emirati tradition, or test your aim with guided archery sessions. These immersive activities offer a deeper connection to the desert’s spirit and culture in a safe, engaging environment.
Al Wadi Equestrian Centre
Al Wadi Equestrian Centre is a third-party operator who manages the Equestrian Centre and Equestrian Activities independently from the hotel. All inquiries and reservations can be made directly with the Al Wadi Equestrian Team on their dedicated contact details.
Adventures for Young Explorers
Ritz Kids invites young explorers to discover the wonders of the desert through engaging, educational activities. From nature walks and wildlife discovery to creative crafts and cultural storytelling, each experience is designed to spark curiosity and connect children with the environment around them.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 27.0 kgs.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert is Ras Al Khaimah International Airport (RKT). RKT is located approximately 22.9 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
