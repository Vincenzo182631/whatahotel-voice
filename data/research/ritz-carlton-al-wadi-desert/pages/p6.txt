Roots of Ras Al Khaimah
An immersive gastronomic experience designed to connect guests with the natural richness, agricultural heritage, and sustainable spirit of Ras Al Khaimah. This is a journey of discovery through local farming, seasonal ingredients, storytelling, and conscious dining, culminating in an interactive culinary class and a five-course dinner served at Farmhouse by Syrco.
Bedouin Heritage Dining Journey
This Bedouin-inspired dining experience embodies our commitment to authentic cultural immersion and refined luxury. By celebrating local flavors, traditions, and sustainable sourcing, it reinforces our position as a destination rooted in heritage.
Desert Awakening Ritual
This mindfulness experience is both minimalist and profound, inviting calm through intentional simplicity. Practiced in the dunes, it reflects a form of luxury defined not by excess, but by nature’s purity and an extraordinary sense of place.
Starlight Meditation Journey
Rooted in the desert’s nomadic spirit, this private night-time experience combines guided stargazing and a meditation session beneath the open skies of Al Wadi, inspired by Bedouin storytelling and local rituals.
Wildlife Dune Odyssey
This active desert exploration invites guests to move in harmony with the land’s natural rhythm. A mindful trek across the dunes encourages deeper engagement with the surrounding landscape and wildlife, offering moments of play, spaciousness, and discovery.
Al Wadi Ascent Horizons
Floating gracefully above golden dunes and tranquil oases, your ascent reveals a breathtaking aerial tableau of the desert’s timeless majesty. This serene journey offers a rare perspective that transforms the landscape into an unforgettable moment of quiet wonder.
Summit to Stillness
Guests soar across the world’s largest zip line at Jebel Jais, for an exhilarating encounter with the UAE’s highest peak. Returning to the resort’s refined comfort, they discover a harmonious balance of adventure and luxury that transforms the journey into a lasting memory.
Gulf Pearl Immersion
Rooted in heritage and elevated by luxury, The Pearl Farm experience invites guests into the timeless artistry of the region’s pearling legacy. It is an immersive journey that reveals one of Ras Al Khaimah’s most captivating natural wonders.
Healing Water Oasis in Al Wadi Desert – The Rainforest
Experience The Rainforest, a blend of European hydrothermal culture and Middle Eastern bathing rituals, in the Arabian desert. With 16 stations, each enhancing relaxation, guests follow a mapped sequence, cooling off naturally or with the Ice Igloo. Relaxation on a lounger completes the journey. Ladies-only, spa-rty, and private sessions are available.
Guests must be 16 years old or older. Prior Reservation is required, Kindly contact us at +971 7 2067777 ext. 5705/5706 or rc.rktrw.spa@ritzcarlton.com.

Under the guidance of the Experience Concierge, guests are introduced to falconry, archery, fishing, desert biking, horseback riding, stargazing and other activities via one of the experienced rangers at the Nature Reserve.

Falconry
Witness the majestic beauty of the falcon in an interactive Falcon and Owl show daily, one hour before sunset. Suitable for all ages, the experience explains the importance of falconry in Bedouin culture and history. The show also features the desert eagle owl, barn owl and Harris hawk. Alternatively, join the team for private Falconry lessons to improve your skills with these featured marvels of the sky.

An Invitation to Connect to Nature
Nature is many things: immersive, resilient, rejuvenating and healing. Through tailored, locally inspired activities, guests are encouraged to connect with their surroundings and discover the beauty of Al Wadi Nature Reserve. To arrange for any of the below activities, please reserve in advance due to limited availability by contacting the Experience Concierge at +971 7 206 7777 ext. 5656.
Stargazing
The vast desert sky is a beautiful opportunity to experience the serenity and learn about the solar system. Astronomy experts will help you spot stars, constellations, visible planets and galaxies.
Desert Exploration
This early morning experience invites guests to feed the resident Oryx and gazelles in the Al Wadi Desert Nature Reserve. Also, get a chance to learn about Nature Reserve wildlife from expert guides and take photographs in this unique setting.
Bird-Watching
Using spotter scopes, binoculars and identification
books, guests can spot migratory and resident birds throughout the resort. The guided adventure, done via bicycle, is the ideal way to introduce young guests to nature.
Advisory - Travel to Middle East
We continue to closely monitor the current regional situation in the Middle East as it evolves. Our hotel remains open and operational and our teams are focused on supporting our guests at this time.

Yes. The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
