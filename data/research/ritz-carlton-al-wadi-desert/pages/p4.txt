Frequently Asked Questions
What restaurants does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert have on property?
The on-property restaurants at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert are:
Zuma
Farmhouse by Syrco
Kaheela
Destination Dining
Paddock Cafe
Oasis Pool Bar
Kan Zaman - Seasonal
Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert offer room service?
Yes, room service is available at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert.
What cuisine is offered at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert?
The cuisines offered at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert are:
Bar-B-Q at Kan Zaman - Seasonal
Coffee House at Paddock Cafe
Japanese at Zuma
International at Kaheela
Other at Farmhouse by Syrco, Destination Dining, Oasis Pool Bar

Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert offer breakfast?
Yes, complimentary breakfast is available at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert to hotel guests during their stay.
Free Buffet Breakfast is available at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert. during the following hours: Monday-Sunday 7:00 - 11:00 AM.
Free Grab n go Breakfast is available at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert. during the following hours: Monday-Sunday 7:00 - 11:00 AM.
Free Hot Breakfast is available at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Wadi Desert. during the following hours: Monday-Sunday 7:00 - 11:00 AM.
Breakfast is also available onsite at Kaheela and Paddock Cafe for a fee.
