Ras Al Khaimah Villas With Private Pools
Villas
Luxury in Ras Al Khaimah reaches new heights at our all-villa resort, where timeless Bedouin architecture meets refined modern design. Each secluded villa is a haven of tranquility, featuring a private temperature-controlled pool, expansive sun deck with sweeping desert views, and elegantly appointed interiors. Floor-to-ceiling windows flood the space with natural light, while spacious lounges and indulgent bathrooms offer the perfect blend of comfort and sophistication. Whether you're seeking solitude or a romantic escape, your private oasis awaits.
Signature Villas
The Signature Villas with private pools feature lavish and isolated accommodations nestled in the Al Wadi Nature Reserve. Private gated entry and your own villa parking allow guests to truly indulge in a getaway experience. Complemented by the legendary Ritz-Carlton experience and private butlers guests can relax in their two-bedroom accommodation offering a separate maid's room, fitness studio, butler pantry, and terrace with desert views that are the epitome of luxury.
