Waldorf Astoria Chicago
Glossy glamour is the calling card of this downtown high-rise.

Why book?
For all of the comforts of a luxury hotel with a personalized, boutique feel. Excellent dining options, warm hospitality, and a fantastic location in one of
Chicago
’s most iconic neighborhoods, footsteps away from premier shopping, dining, and nightlife.
Set the scene
You'll feel instantly transported to a French chateau upon arrival to this Gold Coast retreat, with its regal purple awnings and old-world motor court (credit celebrated architect Lucien Lagrange). That entryway is all the more eye-catching around the holidays, when a 25-foot tree and a canopy of string lighting greet guests driving up. But with its sleek charm and upscale amenities, the hotel evokes a fairytale kind of feel year-round. Dedicated service, alongside a notable spa, restaurant, and cocktail lounge, all draw an array of travelers checking in for an elevated escape, from families to couples seeking romantic retreat.
The backstory
The hotel was originally built on land owned by Bernard Heerey, who donated proceeds of the sale to Chicago youth organizations (onsite cocktail lounge Bernard’s is named in his honor). The property is currently owned by Chicago-based Mansueto Office.
The rooms
A warm white palette modernizes the rooms, while a whimsical, floral headboard from designer Ashley Woodson Bailey serves as a centerpiece. Depending on the length of your stay, try to cushion in some time to chill—you’ll want to cozy up with the Boca Terry robes and take advantage of the Aesop hair and body care (and get a good night’s rest with Frette bedding). If you’re looking to experience the property in big ways, consider one of their six residential suites, each boasting a fireplace, terrace, marble bathroom, and floor-to-ceiling windows.
Food and drink
Visit Brass Tack on the third floor for breakfast, lunch, and dinner (and weekend brunch). Chef Eric Damidot and team serve modern American-meets-French brasserie fare, with special attention paid to their sourcing (with relationships at Creekstone Farms for their black angus beef; Heritage Berkshire for their Sakura pork). Post-dinner, head to Bernard’s, the second floor’s intimate craft cocktail and Champagne speakeasy that’s become a local favorite.
The spa
The award-winning Waldorf Astoria Chicago Spa & Health Club offers a range of signature services, including massages, facials, and body treatments. Industry-leading skincare brands like Biologique Recherché, Knesko, and Balmain stock their shelves and inspire offerings (such as their Biologique Recherché Facials). If you book, come early to enjoy amenities like the eucalyptus steam rooms, dry sauna, jacuzzis, and outdoor spa terrace.
The neighborhood/area
The Gold Coast is home to several popular, nightlife-geared dining options—consider a reservation at any of them a good excuse to shop the neighborhood earlier in the day (it’s one of the city’s best for luxury brands like Veronica Beard or Le Labo).
The service
The staff here is friendly and attentive, and a Personal Concierge team helps to accommodate special requests and touches before, during, and after stays (e.g. a courtesy car for pick-ups and drop-offs; assistance with shipping goods and luggage).
For families
Parents will appreciate options for strollers and an in-room pack-n-play, and kids will enjoy thoughtful touches like teddy bears upon arrival or an in-room tent set-up.
Eco effort
The hotel prioritizes locally sourced ingredients throughout its dining venues, uses recycling systems, and encourages water conservation methods.
Accessibility
The hotel’s accessible rooms and suites are spacious and comply with ADA regulations—guests can expect step-free access throughout the suite, upper floors accessible by elevator, and accessible parking.
Anything left to mention?
Try to visit for the property’s annual holiday ceremony, when the Gold Coast’s largest tree is illuminated with 14,000 twinkling lights—an event benefitting Ann & Robert H. Lurie Children's Hospital. Later in the winter, catch Brass Tack’s special menus for Restaurant Week or Valentine’s Day.
