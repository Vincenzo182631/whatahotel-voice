## Supreme Suite

Pops of colour bring this 72sqm corner suite to life, framed by floor-to-ceiling windows. Inspired by Melbourne’s street art-lined laneways, our Supreme Suite features a separate lounge, oversized king bed, rain shower, and bathtub for ultimate comfort.

## Urban Suite

Settle into our 65sqm Urban Suite, with a separate lounge, spacious king bed, double vanity, rain shower, and bathtub. Take in sweeping city views, enjoy the curated W MixBar, or indulge in our diverse in-room dining options.

## Studio Suite

Relax in our 60sqm Studio Suite, complete with a living area, large king bed, double vanity, and bathtub. Floor-to-ceiling windows showcase city views, while artwork inspired by Melbourne’s landmarks and vibrant history adds character to the space.
