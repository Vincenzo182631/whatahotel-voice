# Bars & Restaurants at W Melbourne

## Dining

Discover four unique bars and restaurants in Melbourne CBD, perfect from AM to PM. Enjoy all-day dining at Belasco with a buffet breakfast and classic Italian dishes served from an open theatre kitchen. Experience Warabi, our two chef-hatted contemporary Japanese dining. Finally, unwind at Curious, a hidden cocktail bar with award-winning design, curated drinks, and live music.
