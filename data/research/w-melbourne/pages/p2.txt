# Stay With Us at W Melbourne

## Accommodations

With 265 hotel rooms and 29 expansive suites near Flinders Lane, W Melbourne sets the scene for stays that blur the line between luxury and lifestyle. Inspired by the tailors of Flinders Lane and the laneway culture they helped shape, our suites channel the energy of a high-fashion boutique; rich in colour, layered in texture, and made to be seen. Designed with entertaining in mind, each space features flexible layouts that separate social from sleep, alongside statement details like marble accents, oversized bathrooms, and floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Melbourne’s iconic laneways.
