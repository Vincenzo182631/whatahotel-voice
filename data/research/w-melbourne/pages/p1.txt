# Welcome to W Melbourne

## Discover our hotel in Melbourne, Australia

Located on the iconic Flinders Lane in the heart of Melbourne’s city centre, this luxury lifestyle hotel draws design inspiration from the laneways it sits between. Extending the laneway culture inside, 294 bold rooms and suites peel back the layers and revel in the city’s creative history. With 830 square metres of ultra-modern event space, it sets the stage for standout events. Discover four unique...See More bars and restaurants, from all-day dining at Belasco to Japanese fine dining omakase at our two chef-hatted restaurant Warabi. Unwind at Curious, a hidden cocktail bar with award-winning design, curated drinks and live music. On Level 14, break a sweat in the FIT gym or dip and sip poolside at WET, all with sweeping skyline views of Melbourne CBD.See Less

Recognised as one of Australia's Best City Hotels, W Melbourne stands apart for its considered design, dynamic dining and bar experiences, and service delivered with confidence and intention.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Melbourne?

The check-in time at W Melbourne is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Melbourne allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Melbourne. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 18.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W Melbourne?

On-site valet parking is available at W Melbourne. The daily valet fee is $80.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Melbourne have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Melbourne has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Melbourne booking.

What is the closest airport near W Melbourne?

The closest airport near W Melbourne is Melbourne Airport - Australia (MEL). MEL is located approximately 24.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Melbourne have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Melbourne has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
