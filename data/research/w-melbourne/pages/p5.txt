# Discover the Best of Melbourne

## Experiences

In the heart of Flinders Lane, our Melbourne hotel places you moments from the city's laneway culture, after-dark energy and iconic attractions. Step out to designer boutiques, hidden bars and Melbourne's ever-evolving dining scene, or hop on a tram to Marvel Stadium, the MCG and Rod Laver Arena with ease. Stay connected to the city's rhythm from the moment you arrive.

Relax and Restore

## WET

Soak up Melbourne's skyline poolside at our WET deck. Access is available to guests 24/7 and transforms to an adult-only space (ages 16+) from 7pm daily. Enhance your relaxation with our poolside dining and beverage options.

Relax and Restore

## FIT Gym

Tune into your workout with our state-of-the-art, fully equipped FIT gym, featuring floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Melbourne CBD. Stay on top of your fitness routine from the comfort of your own room with a fully equipped portable Technogym Workout Bench and yoga mats are available upon request.

Relax and Restore

## Steam Room

Unwind in our steam room on Level 14, a calm escape above the city. A simple ritual to reset, refresh and restore between moments in Melbourne. Available from 6am-10pm daily.

### W Happenings

Dive into the epicentre of Melbourne’s stylish scene with four bars and restaurants, and an expansive wellness floor with our 24/7 pool and fitness centre. Enjoy access to bespoke programming, events, and bold food and drink experiences during your stay.

### Festival Season

Summer brings a packed calendar of festivals, cultural celebrations and city-wide events. From film, arts and music festivals to New Year celebrations and the summer of tennis, Melbourne comes alive with experiences that draw locals and visitors alike.

Discover What's On

### Open Air

Longer days and warmer nights bring Melbourne outdoors. Rooftop gatherings, outdoor screenings, riverside activations and open-air events pop up across the city. From sunrise to after dark, there are countless ways to experience the city's vibrant atmosphere.

Explore More

### Stadium Stays

From first serve to final siren, and sold-out stadium shows, W Melbourne keeps you close to the action. Walk to Marvel Stadium, or take a quick tram ride to the MCG, Rod Laver Arena and Melbourne Park for the city’s biggest sporting and musical moments.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Melbourne have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Melbourne offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio and Strength Training Equipment and Free Weights, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Melbourne have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Melbourne has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Melbourne?

Melbourne Convention Exhibition Centre, Marvel Stadium, Royal Botanic Gardens, National Gallery of Victoria, Melbourne Cricket Ground and more are nearby W Melbourne.
