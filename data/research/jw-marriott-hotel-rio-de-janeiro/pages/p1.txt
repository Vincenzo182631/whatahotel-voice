# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro

## Enjoy a stay at our 5-star Rio de Janeiro, Copacabana hotel

Experience a blend of luxury and comfort at JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro. Conveniently located in front of the beach and near main business districts and airports, our beachfront hotel in Rio de Janeiro features elegant rooms and suites, upscale amenities, a variety of dining options and flexible event venues. Start your day with an authentic Brazilian breakfast at our luxury hotel in Rio de Janeiro....See More After a long day, visit the Moonlounge Rooftop Bar and indulge in international street food prepared with fresh and local ingredients. Reconnect with your body in our rooftop fitness center, overlooking the tranquil beauty of Copacabana Beach. Flexible event spaces are available for your business or social gatherings. During your Rio de Janeiro vacation, make sure to visit Rio's most iconic attractions: Christ the Redeemer, Copacabana Beach, Tijuca Forest, and SugarLoaf Mountain. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro. The daily valet fee is $17.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro is Santos Dumont Airport (SDU). SDU is located approximately 8.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro have an airport shuttle to Santos Dumont Airport (SDU)?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro does not have an airport shuttle to Santos Dumont Airport.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Rio de Janeiro has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
