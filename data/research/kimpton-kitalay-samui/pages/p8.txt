# Kimpton Kitalay SamuiLocation

Kimpton Kitalay Samui offers direct access to the beautiful white-sand Choengmon Beach, overlooking the Gulf of Thailand. In addition to enjoying the resort's amenities, guests can explore the thriving gallery scene on the island or take tours of local attractions such as an independent rum distillery, a Thai micro farm, or an authentic fishing village. With its stunning location and range of activities, Kimpton Kitalay Samui is an ideal destination for those seeking a luxurious and culturally enriching beach vacation.
