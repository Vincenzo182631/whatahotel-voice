### One of the Best Hotels in Ko Samui

14 days ago

One of the most comfortable hotels I have stayed in the IHG group. This is a beautifully designed hotel . The rooms and huge balconies with twin day beds and ceiling fans allowed you to enjoy the outdoors minus the searing heat in Ko Samui.
There is large pool just below each block, this is in addition to the huge main pool. We enjoyed the gym, sauna and steam rooms conveniently located just behind the block we stayed in (block 4). The evening canapes and drinks was a pleasant surprise to us. Breakfast was a great with a super wide selection of local and international cuisine with live egg , noodle and pancake /waffle station. Plus you can order ala carte from the menu too - this over simply an over indulgence. The beach front seafood dinner with the fire show was a highlight of the trip - we were literally sitting just a few feet from the performance (yelps!). The beach was in a secluded and sheltered bay perfect to swim and kids to play in water. We will be back again. Kudos to all staff.

Q: Reason For Your Visit?

A: Relaxing and super comfortable room
