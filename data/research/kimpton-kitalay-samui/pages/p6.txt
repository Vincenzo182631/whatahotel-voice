### The Villas

Step into a timeless escape where serenity meets sophistication, and every moment is artfully curated. Discover the perfect setting to begin a new chapter in your life’s extraordinary journey. In our villas, you will be enclosed in your own luxurious cocoon, where ideas flourish and inspiration thrives.

Learn more

### Restaurants and Bars

Kimpton Kitalay Samui turns island dining into the epicentre of every visitor’s experience. From the signature FishHouse beachside restaurant, SHADES Pool Bar, Boho Thai Lifestyle Café and SHIO Japanese Dining & Sushi Bar, the quality drinking and dining experiences are the resort's calling card.

Learn more

### Destination Weddings

For couples looking for in-villa vows, or the pleasure of saying "I do" with sand between their toes, our specialised wedding experts can make your every wish a reality. Dance the night away while the ocean waves lap the shore and moonlight glints.

Learn more

### Pool

Extending the length of our resort, seamlessly merging into the waters of Choeng Mon, our pool comes with ocean views and welcomes water and lounging lovers of all ages. Grab a cocktail poolside at Shades Pool Bar or refresh your palate from our signature menu at FishHouse, just steps away.

## FAQs for Kitalay Samui

Does Kimpton Kitalay Samui have a pool?

Kimpton Kitalay Samui has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Seafront Olympic-sized swimming pool that is available to all guests of our resort.

Pool information

How much is parking at Kimpton Kitalay Samui?

Complimentary parking is available at Kimpton Kitalay Samui for hotel guests.

Open air self and valet parking available.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Kitalay Samui?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Kitalay Samui.

Pet policy description. At Kimpton Kitalay Samui, we welcome every member of your family, including your pet. Our doors are always open for cats, dogs and beyond. No matter what your pets' size, weight or breed, their stay is complimentary.
Max number of pets per room allowed: 2

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Kitalay Samui for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Kitalay Samui have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Kitalay Samui has 3 restaurants.

Is there free breakfast available at Kimpton Kitalay Samui?

Breakfast is free with your stay at the Kimpton Kitalay Samui.

When is breakfast at Kimpton Kitalay Samui?

Breakfast at Kimpton Kitalay Samui is available from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM. On weekends, breakfast is available from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Kitalay Samui?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Kitalay Samui.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Kitalay Samui?

Check-in at Kimpton Kitalay Samui is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Kitalay Samui offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Kitalay Samui offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Kitalay Samui from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Kitalay Samui is conveniently located near Samui International Airport (USM), approximately 2.98 miles away (4.8 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
