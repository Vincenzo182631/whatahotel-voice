# Kimpton Kitalay SamuiRooms Made for You

Accommodation at Kimpton Kitalay Samui is characterised by a strong sense of pattern and weave, especially pronounced within the villas, where hints of a nautical personality slowly reveal themselves, alluding to this once being a chief fisherman’s home. Ranging from 58 square metres to 628 square metres, our 138 rooms, suites and villas offer open plan layouts with large terraces, many with direct access to a swimming pool and sea views.
