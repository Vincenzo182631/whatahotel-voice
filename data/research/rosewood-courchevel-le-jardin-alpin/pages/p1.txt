JULY 22, 2025 – Rosewood Hotels & Resorts® is pleased to announce plans for Rosewood Courchevel Le Jardin Alpin, the brand’s first property in the French Alps, set to open in December 2025. Located in a prime spot in Courchevel 1850, one of the world’s most sought-after ski destinations, the property signals Rosewood’s continued evolution into highly experiential, destination-led hospitality, while expanding its footprint across Europe.

Situated at the pinnacle of Courchevel 1850, Rosewood Courchevel Le Jardin Alpin, will offer ski-in ski-out access and an optimum blend of adventure and refinement. This exclusive mountain retreat will feature 51 guest rooms and suites, integrated retail and ski rental spaces, a spacious spa, an all-day dining concept with a terrace, a kids club, bar lounge and cigar room. With the highest suite inventory in Courchevel’s exclusive Le Jardin Alpin, the property will set a new standard for contemporary alpine experiences.

The former site is currently undergoing a complete transformation led by renowned French designer Tristan Auer, reflecting a new vision of contemporary alpine luxury. Designed to seamlessly blend into the natural grandeur of the French Alps, the property will embody the brand’s A Sense of Place® philosophy while ushering in a new expression of mountain hospitality.

> “Rosewood Courchevel Le Jardin Alpin represents a defining milestone for the brand as our inaugural venture into alpine hospitality, situated in one of the world’s most revered ski destinations. This opening affirms Rosewood’s position at the vanguard of destination-led hospitality, offering our guests an opportunity for discovery in the most coveted address of Le Jardin Alpin.”
>
> Radha Arora, President of Rosewood Hotels & Resorts

As Rosewood’s second property in continental France, alongside the established Hôtel de Crillon, A Rosewood Hotel in Paris, this property in the French Alps underscores the brand’s commitment to thoughtful expansion across Europe’s most iconic locales. The new resort continues Rosewood’s tradition of curating immersive experiences that resonate with both place and purpose.

## About Rosewood Courchevel Le Jardin Alpin

Perched high in the heart of Courchevel 1850’s prestigious Jardin Alpin, Rosewood Courchevel is a meticulously crafted, ski-in, ski-out chalet that fuels adventure and conviviality. The interiors and 51 rooms, including three signature houses, envisioned by acclaimed designer Tristan Auer, are an intimate retreat brimming with mirrored installations and organic textures. Set against the sweeping slopes is SALTO, the escape’s gastronomic heart that transforms in atmosphere and taste throughout the day. At lunchtime, its panoramic terrace welcomes guests with Alpine classics, including Fondue Savoyarde; come après ski, vintage champagnes flow. With personal ski concierges that curate guided explorations and rejuvenating treatments at Asaya Spa, Rosewood Courchevel offers a refined balance of adventure and restoration.
