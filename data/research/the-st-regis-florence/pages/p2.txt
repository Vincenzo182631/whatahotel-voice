# Unwind in the best Florence luxury hotel rooms and suites

## Accommodations

Exquisitely styled and lavishly appointed, our accommodations in Florence, Italy define refined luxury. Timeless elegance and personalized hospitality fill our spacious Florence hotel rooms and family suites. The St. Regis Florence features 80 luxuriously appointed guest rooms and 19 suites. Reflecting the city's rich artistic heritage, all of our Florence luxury suites feature individually hand-carved...See More gold leaf plaques and are dressed in one of three different color palettes - Medici, Florentine and Renaissance - with each design concept offering a unique selection of custom-designed furniture, paintings, frescoes and crystal chandeliers to create a sophisticated sense of relaxed opulence. In honor of Florence and its traditions, every guest room and suite bears the name of noteworthy Italian artists, benefactors and nobility.See Less

### In-Room Dining

Catering to all appetites, authentic Italian cuisine is available in the privacy of your room or suite. The flavors of our in-room dining introduce palates to an enchanting Tuscan menu. In the morning, a traditional continental breakfast offers a bounty of à la carte items. For lunch or dinner, Chef Gentian Sheshi selects top restaurant specialties for the in-room dining menu.

### Delights Delivered

Elevate the in-room experience. Send a meaningful amenity gift during or in advance of an upcoming stay through the thoughtfully executed Delights Delivered Program. From a fresh bouquet of flowers to a personalized cake for a special celebration, deliver a memorable amenity to be enjoyed within the comforts of our rooms and suites.

### Room With a View

Get inspired for your next stay at The St. Regis Florence. The art of Renaissance and the art of hospitality combined in a masterpiece of exclusive living in most of our rooms with breathtaking views of the Arno River and the city's legendary art treasures. Our elegant Arno View rooms offer guests a full immersion in the beauty of Florence.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Florence have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Florence have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The St. Regis Florence?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 3 at The St. Regis Florence. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The St. Regis Florence?

Some rooms at The St. Regis Florence permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Florence have fridges?

No, rooms at The St. Regis Florence do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Florence have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Florence have a Tea maker.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Florence have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Florence have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The St. Regis Florence have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The St. Regis Florence have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
