## The Fresco Technique

Through a revolutionary technique in the history of art – the art of the fresco, based on water-based pigments on fresh plaster – Florentine artists became the world’s foremost exponents and experts. Standing the test of time over hundreds of years, the frescoes in Florence are considered one of the city’s greatest artistic signatures, and are best experienced up close.

## Immerse Yourself in the Cradle of Frescoes

Prestigiously located in Piazza Ognissanti, The St. Regis Florence is a stone’s throw from a Botticelli fresco and is the perfect stay for exploring these ‘jewels’ of Italy’s renaissance capital. Journey through the city on The St. Regis Florence bespoke experience with an expert art-historian guide, who will share hidden details including fascinating insights from a specialist fresco restorer.
