# Crafting Memorable Moments in Florence

## Unwind to the cradle of the Renaissance

Florence, Italy's Renaissance Capital, offers a journey through time and beauty. Imagine starting your day with a stroll across the iconic Ponte Vecchio, the ancient bridge lined with charming jewelry shops. As you wander through the narrow cobblestone streets, the scent of freshly baked pastries from local bakeries fills the air. Enjoy an immersive culinary experience at Café Ginori or escape to a sanctuary of indulgence and relaxation at our Spa Suites, and end each day with elevated finest Tuscan cuisine by Chef Gentian Shehi and a well-crafted drink at our Winter Garden Bar.

## Café Ginori at The St. Regis Florence

Experience a sensory journey that is both bold and refined, audacious and sophisticated, playful and poised. At Café Ginori, each visit is an opportunity to savor not just the flavors of Italy, but the rich heritage and exceptional craftsmanship that define the Ginori collections.

Embark on a culinary journey of indulgence—savor an enchanting afternoon tea ritual, a chic light lunch, or exquisite coffee paired with fresh, artisanal pastries. Let the allure of the experience captivate your senses.

Celebrate the day elevating the Tea Ritual to a moment of joy at Café Ginori. A unique twist on the St. Regis Afternoon Tea Ritual, enhanced by the presence of our Tea Sommelier and the grace of the Ginori collection of porcelain.

Match your fragrance with exquisite sweet and savory sweet delicacies curated by our Pastry Chefs.

## The Art of Mixology

Nestled below regal arches and the iconic fresco, the Winter Garden Bar invites guests to enjoy the most glamorous experiences. The perfect setting to indulge in an Italian Aperitivo and well-crafted mixology, while the transition from day to evening is celebrated with the St. Regis Evening Ritual.

## Signature Cocktails

Discover the unique cocktail list signed by the creative flair of our Bar Manager, Lorenzo Bellieri. Inspired by the pioneers of Art Nouveau, each cocktail is crafted with a curated selection of ingredients from all over the world and presented with spectacular flair.
