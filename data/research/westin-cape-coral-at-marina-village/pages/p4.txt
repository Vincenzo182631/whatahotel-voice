# Experience our relaxing spa in Cape Coral

## Experiences

With an ideal location within a vibrant resort community near Fort Myers, The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village is the perfect place for an inspired vacation. While our Cape Coral hotel with a pool offers a dynamic array of on-site activities, there are plenty of things to do nearby as well. Enjoy the waterways of Cape Coral with offerings from the adjacent Tarpon Point Marina, including fishing charters, boat and kayak rentals, and guided local tours. Beyond the river lies the scenic Gulf of Mexico, dotted with small tropical islands, active rookeries, and sports fishing galore. Discover Florida's pristine beaches from our hotel near Fort Myers Beach, including world-renowned Sanibel Island, or hit the links at the seven area golf courses. Our hotel is also less than an hour away from Seminole Immokalee Casino.

### Local Area & Activity Highlights

Day Trips

Nearby Islands & Fort Myers

Sanibel Island

In addition to being near Fort Myers, our hotel is less than an hour's drive from Sanibel Island. If you're staying on the mainland in Cape Coral, these picturesque locale is a can't-miss attraction.

Sanibel Island, and its smaller sister Captiva Island, take pride in their wide variety of shells, which bring people from across the world to go "shelling." Home to more than 300 shell species, Sanibel Island offers gently sloped beaches and clear blue waters. Nature lovers can also spend some time exploring and bird watching at the J.N. "Ding" Darling National Wildlife Refuge, which is part of the largest undeveloped mangrove ecosystem in the United States.

If you're planning a quick trip to Sanibel Island from Cape Coral or Fort Myers, you don't have to worry about bringing beach and baby equipment with you. Island Rental Services delivers and picks up a wide variety of items to rent, including coolers, umbrellas, towels, beach wagons and toys, strollers, beach cabanas, and even bocce ball sets.

Of course, there's more to do on Sanibel Island than simply sitting in the sun. Stroll along covered boardwalks through unique stores and galleries, and pick up a souvenir or two. There's plenty of parking available, and many shops are handicap-accessible. The Mad Hatter, The Sandbar, and Sweet Melissa's Cafe are among the island's best restaurants, and offer both indoor seating and quaint outdoor patios.

If you'd like more information about Sanibel Island, please don't hesitate to contact a member of our hotel staff.

Captiva Island

Captiva Island, sister to larger Sanibel Island, is less than an hour's drive from The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village. Journey to Captiva Island from Sanibel by crossing a small bridge at Turner Beach. The five-mile beach is a Florida favorite for fishing, boating and diving, but for those who prefer to stroll along the water, more than 300 species of shells live on the shores of Captiva, making it the perfect place to pick up "shelling" as a new hobby. The island's east-west orientation allows Captiva to collect abundant, beautiful sea shells from the Gulf of Mexico on its shores.

Be sure to stop by a few of the island's serene beaches and nature preserves, including:

Lighthouse Park Beach

Lovers Key State Park

Sanibel Causeway Beach

Bonita Beach Park

Boca Grande Lighthouse

Lynn Hall Memorial Park

Denor-Wiggins State Recreation Area

The area is widely known for its beautiful wildlife. Visitors regularly report seeing dolphins, blue herons and a variety of colorful bird species throughout the island. Tarpon Bay Explorers offer guided canoe and kayak tours through the J.N. "Ding" Darling National Wildlife Refuge and charter SeaLife & Nature Cruises throughout the day and at sunset. If you're just on Captiva for the day, it's the perfect way to maximize your time on the island.

For those interested in shopping, travel along Captiva Drive and enjoy scenic views of the water before reaching downtown Captiva, which is a quaint, village-like shopping area that is worth the trip. Find antiques, clothes, pottery, furniture, beach chic decor and more in the shops clustered around Captiva Drive and Andy Rosse lane. Worked up an appetite? Stop in at Captiva Island's famous Key Lime Bistro for a slice of their key lime pie.

##### Fort Myers Beaches

A favorite destination for locals and vacationers alike, Fort Myers Beach attracts beach and vacation lovers at all times of the year.  Its white, sandy beaches along the Gulf of Mexico make it a top vacation spot for visitors who like a calm and relaxing environment.  The angle of the ocean floor tips towards the horizon so there are no sudden drop-offs or big waves.  The Fort Myers beaches are perfect for soaking up some Florida sun and enjoying the breathtaking sunsets known around the world.

### Arts & Culture

Just like Ft. Myers, Cape Coral has its own history to boast: our Cape Coral Historical Museum and the Imaginarium Museum are fascinating destinations for history buffs and children alike. Between concerts and musical / arts performances at the Broadway Palm Theatre or Barbara Mann Performing Arts Hall, Cape Coral has plenty of cultural activities to choose from.

### Edison & Ford Winter Estates

Located a short drive away in Fort Myers, Edison & Ford Winter estates offer visitors the opportunity to explore the winter retreats of Thomas Edison and Henry Ford. Explore 20 acres of botanical landscaping, Edison's laboratory, a museum filled with inventions, artifacts and special exhibitions, and an outdoor café.

### Naples Zoo

The Naples Zoo features over 70 species and boasts beautiful exhibits and tropical gardens. Delight at seeing many of your favorite animals like lions, kangaroos, monkeys, and bears. You'll also discover feature exhibits and an array of more rarely seen creatures like the fosas of Madagascar or an Asian deer.

### Shell Factory & Nature Park

Very large collection of shells, coral, specimen shells and sea products in a 60,000-ft. space. Additions to the Nature Park include new habitats and a 7,000-sq.-ft. aviary.

### Imaginarium Museum

Feed stingrays, see live animal demonstrations and experience hands-on exhibits like Dino Discovery, Build Your Own Coaster, Sporty Science Virtual Arena, Hurricane Experience, Tiny Town, Make It Move, Caloosahatchee Experience, IMAG TV Studio and more.

FastTrax Amusement Park

FastTrax Amusement Park is an indoor/outdoor facility with a Family Fun Arcade. FastTrax offers an amazing assortment of games, rides and attractions which are sure to make your experience a memorable occasion for all ages.

Topgolf Fort Myers

A premier entertainment destination in Fort Myers, Topgolf offers year-round fun. Swing with easy in the climate-controlled hitting bays. This high-tech driving range also features a chic lounge to enjoy cocktails and games with friends and colleagues.

Sports & Recreational Activities

##### Spring Training

Spring training for the Boston Red Sox and the Minnesota Twins takes place in Fort Myers each year from February-March. The Red Sox will play their spring training home games at JetBlue Park, while the Minnesota Twins will call Hammond Stadium their spring training home. Both JetBlue Park and Hammond Stadium are conveniently located near Southwest Florida International Airport and just 30 minutes from The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village.

The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village boasts a waterfront location on Tarpon Point Marina and offers sweeping views of the Caloosahatchee River, San Carlos Bay and the Gulf of Mexico. Central to the most popular Cape Coral attractions and just 18 miles from Southwest Florida International Airport (RSW), our Cape Coral hotel is ideal for your spring training accommodations. In addition to offering traditional rooms, we also offer 1, 2, and 3 bedrooms suites making our resort ideal for both family getaways and spring training vacations alike.

##### Boston Red Sox

JetBlue Park at Fenway South, which opened in 2012, is the new home for Boston Red Sox Spring Training. An environmentally-sustainable complex, Fenway South provides state-of-the-art training facilities including the main ballpark, JetBlue Park, and six practice fields. Located near Southwest Florida International Airport (RSW), JetBlue Park at Fenway South is approximately 30 minutes from The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village.

##### Minnesota Twins

Hammond Stadium at the Century Link Sports Complex is home of the Minnesota Twins Spring Training. The sports complex features five full fields, two soccer fields, a softball complex and an eight-acre lake for public fishing. In close proximity to Southwest Florida International Airport, Hammond Stadium at the Century Link Sports Complex is just 30 minutes from The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village.

## Southwest Florida Boating & Fishing

No matter what time of the year you visit, boating and fishing in Southwest Florida is phenomenal. Fort Myers also hosts major boat shows each year, including the Fort Myers Spring Boat Show and the Fort Myers Boat Show.

Boating & Fishing Locations

Located in Cape Coral, the marina is Lee County's largest clean, deep-water Marina and just a few miles from the best cruising grounds in Florida. The marina offers a variety of nature and sightseeing tours, water taxi to Fort Myers Beach, rentals, boating lessons, and more.
