# Welcome to The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village

## Experience Southwest Florida at Our Waterfront Resort

Experience Coastal Bliss at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village. Discover one of the most inviting resorts near Fort Myers, where well-being comes easily. Nestled in a picturesque waterfront...See More community, our Southwest Florida resort offers dream-home comforts, on-demand activities, and a deep connection to nature. Every room features stunning sunrise-to-sunset views of the marina and surrounding preserve. Enjoy kayaking through mangroves, dolphin cruises, or simply relaxing by the pool. With full kitchens, private balconies, and spacious suites, our accommodations feel like your own coastal retreat. Dine at Marker 92, unwind at Esterra Spa, or explore the Promenade Shops. Whether you're planning a getaway or a gathering, our hotel near Fort Myers invites you to embrace the rhythms of the water and feel your best—every moment of your stay.See Less

## Pet-Friendly Hotels Near Fort Myers

There's no need to leave your dog behind on your Fort Myers, FL visit. The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village is pleased to be a pet-friendly hotel; we welcome pets to join us and enjoy our Westin Heavenly Pet Treatment. At check-in you will be required to sign a release waiver and there will be a $50 per room per night ($150 per room per stay maximum) pet fee applied to your folio at check-out.

More Details

Pet Policy

The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village welcomes pets with a $50 per room per night ($150 per room per stay maximum).

Owners must sign a waiver at check-in and are responsible for any damage or additional cleaning required.

Dogs must be leashed at all times and are not permitted at the resort pool. Pets are permitted at outdoor dining establishments.

Dogs must not be left unattended in your room, unless in a secure crate.

A Heavenly Dog Bed, floormat and food/water bowls are available upon request.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village?

The check-in time at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village. Self parking is available for $25.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $32.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village?

The closest airport near The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village is Southwest Florida International Airport (RSW). RSW is located approximately 15.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
