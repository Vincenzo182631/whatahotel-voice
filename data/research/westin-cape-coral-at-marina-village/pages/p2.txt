# Discover our accommodations in Cape Coral

## Accommodations

Experience true relaxation in the spacious, intuitively designed hotel rooms and suites at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village. Located on the water, our accommodations offer stunning views...See More from private balconies - perfect for enjoying southwest Florida's famed sunsets. All our Cape Coral hotel suites and rooms are comfortably appointed with the iconic Westin Heavenly® Bed, featuring 10 layers of plush bedding, as well as spacious bathrooms furnished with Westin bath amenities. We want everyone to breathe easily while they're in Cape Coral, which is why we are pleased to offer smoke-free guest rooms at our hotel.See Less

### Your Waterfront Escape Awaits

Experience Coastal Bliss in spacious condo-style suites designed for effortless waterfront living. Every guest room features a private screened-in patio overlooking the coastal waters, with captivating sunrise or sunset views that inspire relaxation, renewal, and a deeper connection to the water.

## Traditional Double Queen

Begin each day with the gentle rhythms of the waterfront and sunrise views over the preserve and waterways. Thoughtfully designed for comfort and connection, this inviting retreat features two Westin Heavenly® Queen Beds, a spacious bath, workspace, mini refrigerator, dry bar, coffee maker, and private screened-in patio. A welcoming space where coastal bliss comes easy.

## Traditional King Room

Watch the marina come alive by day and unwind with breathtaking sunset views from your screened-in patio each evening. This polished waterfront retreat features a Westin Heavenly® King Bed, spacious bath, workstation, mini refrigerator, and coffee maker. Surrounded by the beauty of blue space wellness, it's the perfect place to relax, recharge, and feel your best.

## One Bedroom King Suite

Originally designed for resort living, this spacious suite feels more like a dream vacation home than a traditional hotel room. Wake to sunrise views over the preserve and waterways, then enjoy the comfort of a full kitchen, dining area, separate living room, and private king bedroom. Complete with a walk-in closet, washer and dryer, and screened-in patio, every detail is designed for effortless coastal bliss.

## One Bedroom Double Queen Suite

Blending the comforts of home with the amenities of a resort, this expansive suite welcomes guests into a uniquely personal waterfront experience. Enjoy marina and waterway sunsets from your screened-in patio, along with a full kitchen, dining area, living room, and private bedroom with two Westin Heavenly® Queen Beds. Residential-style comforts make it easy to settle in, spread out, and live well.

## Two Bedroom Suite

Gather, reconnect, and experience the best of coastal living in a spacious suite designed for families and extended stays. Overlooking the marina and waterways, this residence-style retreat features a full kitchen, dining room, living room, and two private bedroom suites, each with its own bath and walk-in closet. With sunset views from the screened-in patio and room for everyone to relax, well-being comes naturally here.

## Two Bedroom Marina Suite

Perched at the apex of the resort, this premier residence offers panoramic views spanning the marina, preserve, and surrounding waterways from sunrise to sunset. Designed for elevated coastal living, the suite features two king bedrooms with en-suite baths, a full kitchen, dining room, living area, private den, and screened-in patio. Spacious, intuitive, and deeply restorative, it is the ultimate expression of Coastal Bliss.

### Discover Cape Coral

Discover the beauty of Southwest Florida from a waterfront resort surrounded by endless opportunities to explore. Cruise through scenic waterways, visit nearby beaches, enjoy dolphin encounters, savor waterfront dining, and uncover the charm of Cape Coral's vibrant coastal community. Your adventure begins at The Westin Cape Coral, where every stay offers new ways to experience Coastal Bliss.

### Long Term Stay

Settle into resort-style living designed for extended escapes. Originally built as luxury waterfront residences, our spacious condo-style suites feature full kitchens, living and dining areas, and private screened-in patios. Whether you're working remotely, relocating, or simply staying longer, enjoy the comforts of home paired with resort amenities, marina views, and the relaxing rhythms of Coastal Bliss.

### Dock & Stay

Arrive by land or sea and experience Coastal Bliss from the water. Dock your boat at nearby Tarpon Point Marina and enjoy spacious waterfront accommodations, resort amenities, waterfront dining, and unforgettable Southwest Florida sunsets. Whether you're planning a weekend escape or a longer adventure, The Westin Cape Coral is your perfect port of call for relaxation, recreation, and marina-front living.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village have a work space?

No, the rooms at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village?

The largest occupancy room at The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village is the 2 Bedroom Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.
