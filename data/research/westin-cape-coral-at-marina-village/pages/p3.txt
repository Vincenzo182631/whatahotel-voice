# Satisfy cravings in our waterfront Cape Coral restaurants

## Dining

Experience Chef Victoria Wenning’s coastal-inspired menus infused with fresh sustainable seafood and exceptional produce to create flavorful, craveable dishes. The Westin Cape Coral Resort at Marina Village...See More offers two welcoming dining destinations, sure to please every appetite. Feel the warm gulf breezes as you savor your meal and sip your favorite beverage in laid-back Florida style at The Nauti Mermaid Dockside Bar and Grill in Cape Coral, FL. This waterfront seafood restaurant offers a full menu in a relaxed atmosphere, with captivating views of the marina and the sparkling waters of the Gulf of Mexico. Meanwhile, Marker 92 Waterfront Bar & Bistro features fresh seafood, premium steaks, enticing salads, and delectable desserts. Both restaurants are family-friendly and offer children’s menus - as well as daily Happy Hour specials for the adults. Those looking to enjoy their meal from the comfort of their room or suite can order our room service served until 11:00 PM.See Less

## Executive Chef Victoria Wenning

Executive Chef Victoria Wenning joined The Westin Cape Coral Resort in 2018, bringing a wellness-driven, locally-inspired culinary approach to southwest Florida’s premier waterfront hotel. A New Jersey native who relocated to Florida in 2004, she is a graduate of The Culinary Institute of America. Bringing more than a decade of seafood-focused culinary experience to our waterfront restaurant in Cape Coral, she has been a driving force in the evolution of the hotel’s popular seafood offerings, in which guests are treated to local favorites expertly cooked up by the hotel’s culinary team. Chef Victoria strives to showcase the hotel’s “Eat Well” by Westin menu items by introducing guests to new, unexpected flavors with vibrant plating presentations. It’s Chef Victoria’s goal to continuously surprise and delight both new and returning guests with elevated, revolving menus that emphasize the best of Florida’s seasonal bounty.

### The Westin Eat Well Menu For Kids

Since eating well isn’t just for adults, Westin has teamed up with the experts at SuperChefs™ to offer the Westin Eat Well Menu for Kids. Comprised of doctors, dentists, chefs, dieticians and other experts, SuperChefs is dedicated to educating kids on the importance—and the fun—of eating smart. Each of our special menu offerings is as delicious as it is nutritious, making kids and parents happy.

### Westin Fresh By The Juicery

Fuel your day and enhance your wellness with Westin Fresh by The Juicery, an assortment of nourishing, healthy juices and smoothies. Curated in partnership with the experts at The Juicery, each Westin Fresh blend packs a punch of revitalizing goodness. In keeping with our commitment to well-being, Westin offers a selection of wholesome juices, smoothies, and other breakfast options to start your day.

### The Westin Sleep Well Menu

Whether you’re adjusting to a new time zone or recovering from your travels, restful sleep is the key to rejuvenation. Eating the right foods can have a dramatic effect on the quality of your sleep, easing you into a night of sound sleep and ultimately boosting your well-being. Recommended by the experts at SuperFoodsRx™, eat smart and sleep well with our curated selection of sleep-enhancing superfoods.
