## Traveler's Insights

### What is the best area to stay in when visiting Nashville?

Downtown Nashville is an ideal location to stay, putting guests in the heart of Music City and near dining, shopping and more. Omni Nashville Hotel is located downtown and puts you just steps away from top attractions including the Country Music Hall of Fame, Ryman Auditorium, the Johnny Cash Museum and more.

### What are the property amenities at Omni Nashville Hotel?

Omni Nashville Hotel has the following amenities:

A full-service Mokara Spa

A heated rooftop pool

On-site dining at 5 restaurants

A fully equipped fitness center

80,000 square feet of meeting and event space

Self-parking and valet parking with a nightly charge

### Where is Omni Nashville Hotel located?

Omni Nashville Hotel is located in downtown Nashville on Fifth Avenue, between Demonbreun and Korean Veterans Boulevard and connected to the Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum. The physical address is 250 Rep. John Lewis Way South, Nashville, Tennessee 37203.

### What is the closest airport to Omni Nashville Hotel?

Omni Nashville Hotel is located 8 miles (approximately 20 minutes) from Nashville International Airport (BNA).

### How much is parking at Omni Nashville Hotel?

Omni Nashville Hotel offers valet parking, and self-parking options are available nearby. Overnight Valet is between $60 - $75 and the daily valet parking rate is between $32- $42 for up to 4 hours, and over 4 hours is $60. Guests leaving their vehicle with valet after 6 PM will incur an additional $40 charge.

Self-parking is available at the Music City Center garage adjacent to the hotel for $35+ tax per day without daily in and out privileges. The garage entrance is on Sixth Avenue between Korean Veterans Boulevard and Demonbreun.

### What time is check-in and check-out?

- Check-in is at 4 PM
- Check-out is at 11 AM
- 2 PM late check-out may be available for $100; after 4 PM, the full nightly rate applies. Guests will be subject to an early departure fee of one night's room + tax if they shorten their stay.

### Is the hotel pet friendly?

Yes, Omni Nashville Hotel is a pet friendly hotel for pets under 25 pounds. There is a one-time non-refundable pet fee of $125 per reservation. Service animals accompanying guests with disabilities are always welcome and will not be required to pay the one-time pet charge (no documentation necessary). Read more of our hotel policies.
