# hotel le fontanelle

## HOTEL LE FONTANELLE

##### ★★★★★

5-star accommodation, offering discreet elegance deep in the heart of Chianti. A manor house where hospitality comes naturally and needs are effortlessly met - ready to cater to couples, families, groups of colleagues and friends.
For guest free use: panoramic swimming pool, mountain bikes, pétanque, heated indoor swimming pool, sauna, turkish bath, Jacuzzi and relaxing area.

## THE HEALTH CLUB

Discover a peaceful oasis nestled within rolling Chianti hills. The Health Club includes an indoor heated pool, relaxation area, fitness room, sauna, Turkish bath, jacuzzi and private massage room. Access is free and reserved for guests at Hotel Le Fontanelle.
