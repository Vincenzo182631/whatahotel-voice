## What is a luxury African safari?

For us, luxury on safari is all about providing access to places most people will never reach, accompanied by guides who have spent their lives speaking the language of the wild. Our camps operate in private concessions or remote wilderness areas, accommodate between six and 18 guests, and offer game drives in custom vehicles limited to six guests, which means a more intimate and personal safari experience for you. Meals are prepared by dedicated chefs from fresh produce grown on site or purchased from surrounding communities where possible – the same ones you can visit on village excursions. Other activities include canoeing, hot air ballooning, helicopter flights, and mokoro and boat safaris. Combine all this with our ability to create a full-service itinerary complete with light-aircraft transfers, city hotel stays and beach getaways and you can enjoy a seamless trip to Africa, where your every need is taken care of.

The heart of our conservation hospitality story begins here with our first mobile operation. The most sought-after activity is a trek to see mountain gorillas.

## How much does an African safari cost?

A Wilderness safari typically ranges from USD 800 to USD 4,000+ per person per night, fully inclusive. That figure covers accommodation, all meals and most drinks at camp, twice-daily game activities with your guide, and all park and conservation fees.

The variation in price reflects three things: the remoteness of the camp, the season, and whether you travel in a shared or private vehicle. Fly-in camps in private concessions – where no other operator brings guests – command a premium because that exclusivity is real and the wildlife sightings reflect it. Shoulder season travel in May, June, or November cuts nightly rates by 15-30% at most of our camps without meaningfully compromising game viewing.

Our Travel Designers regularly combine one flagship camp with a second property in the same ecosystem at a lower nightly rate, extending your time on the ground without extending your budget. The conversation we start with is not how much you want to spend – it is how much time you have and what you most want to see.
