## FAQs

### General

01

#### How do you get to Sossusvlei?

Sossusvlei is easily accessible by road from Swakopmund or Windhoek. However, the best and quickest way to reach this remote part of Namibia is by light aircraft with Wilderness Air, where you will get to appreciate the true beauty of the desert from above.

#### Where is Sossusvlei located in Namibia?

Sossusvlei, famous for its towering sand dunes, is located in south-west Namibia, in the Namib-Naukluft National Park, the largest conservation site in Namibia.

#### How do you pronounce Sossusvlei?

Sossusvlei is sometimes written Sossus Vlei and when trying to pronounce the word it is best broken down into two parts. While the Sossus part is easy for most people to pronounce, the vlei section is said with an f, essentially pronounced ‘flay’.

#### How was Sossusvlei formed?

A vlei is a small shallow pool or marsh. While now dry, the pools of the Sossusvlei area were formed when the Tsauchab River flooded many years ago. On rare occasions, during very heavy rains, water flows in the river, and settles on the desert landscape of Sossusvlei.

### What you need to know

02

#### What is Little Kulala?

Little Kulala is an 11-chalet luxury camp on Wilderness's private Kulala Wilderness Reserve bordering Namib-Naukluft National Park — home to the iconic Sossusvlei dunes. It is the premium Sossusvlei option, offering Star Beds on the private deck of each chalet (sleeping under the Namib's extraordinary night sky), a more intimate 22-guest maximum, and direct private access to the dune fields via the reserve's own airstrip. Little Kulala is widely regarded as the finest base for Sossusvlei.

#### What are the Star Beds at Little Kulala?

Each Little Kulala chalet has a private sala sleeping platform — the Star Bed — a bed positioned under open sky on an elevated deck. Guests can elect to sleep outside under the Namib's unpolluted night sky, with stars from horizon to horizon at an altitude and clarity rarely available elsewhere. The Star Bed can be rolled under cover if wind or rain arrives. This is one of the most distinctive sleeping experiences in African safari accommodation.

#### How is Little Kulala different from Kulala Desert Lodge?

Little Kulala accommodates a maximum of 22 guests in 11 chalets — half the capacity of Kulala Desert Lodge. The camp is architecturally more refined, with private plunge pools for each chalet and the signature star beds. The price point is significantly higher. Both camps access the identical dune landscape and private reserve; the difference is entirely in camp intimacy and level of luxury. Little Kulala suits honeymoons, milestone celebrations, and travellers for whom camp design matters.

#### Is Little Kulala good for photography?

Sossusvlei is one of the world's great photography destinations — the ochre dunes, white clay pan of Deadvlei, and dead camel thorn trees are iconic images. Little Kulala's private airstrip access ensures guests arrive at the dunes for the first light of sunrise, before the public gates open and other visitors arrive. The combination of exclusive access, dramatic landscape, and a minimum of other tourists makes Little Kulala one of Africa's premier photography bases.

#### Can you do a hot air balloon flight from Little Kulala?

Yes — hot air balloon flights over the Namib Desert operate at dawn from near Sesriem, approximately 45 minutes from camp. The flights offer extraordinary aerial views of the dune sea and, on clear mornings, the Atlantic coast. Balloon flights must be booked well in advance and are subject to weather; they are one of Namibia's most memorable experiences. The camp can arrange bookings and logistics.
