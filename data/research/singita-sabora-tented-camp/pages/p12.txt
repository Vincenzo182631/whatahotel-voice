## Putting our purpose in motion

Everything we do is guided by an enduring purpose to protect and preserve large areas of African wilderness for future generations.

Our annual purpose-led events, held in collaboration with regional NGO partners, weave adventure with impact. They’re an invitation to move through wild places with intention — deepening your connection to nature and expanding our positive impact on the ecosystems we call home and the communities we share them with.

\\
\\
**Serengeti Girls Run** \\
\\
Grumeti Reserve, Tanzania\\
\\
This flexible, multi-stage run across the Serengeti plains helps raise funds for empowerment programmes for girls in rural Tanzania.\\
\\
Find out more \\
\\
**Wagora Bike Ride** \\
\\
Grumeti Reserve, Tanzania\\
\\
This five-day journey includes a 150-kilometre cycle across the Serengeti over three days, in support of the region’s anti-poaching scouts.\\
\\
Find out more \\
\\
**Ubumwe Women's Trail** \\
\\
Volcanoes National Park, Rwanda\\
\\
This multi-day event features a trail run, gorilla trek, and guided hike, raising funds for women’s inclusion, leadership, and economic empowerment.\\
\\
Find out more
