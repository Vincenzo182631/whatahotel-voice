### Outdoor Swimming Pool

Treat yourself to the experience of immersing in the swimming pool looking out over the city skyline. Hotel guests also enjoy access to the Fitness Centre, Yoga & Zumba Classes, Table Tennis, Indoor Mini Golf, Sauna & Hot Tub on the 7th floor.

### Fitness Center

In-house guests can enjoy access to a 24-hour, fully equipped gym located on the 71st floor of the hotel. Whether you prefer a quick workout before starting your day or a more extensive exercise session after a long day, the gym offers state-of-the-art facilities and a stunning view of the city skyline.
