## FAQs for InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72

Does InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 have a pool?

InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

The hotel's pool is located on the 7th floor of the building, providing an exclusive and serene escape from the hustle and bustle of the city.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 for hotel guests.

The hotel offers parking for both cars and motorbikes in the B1 basement, accessible from Pham Hung Street. Additionally, there is a dedicated parking area for electric vehicles on the 1st floor, accessible from Nguyen Quoc Tri Street.

Parking fee: 0 USD per day

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 has 5 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:00 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72?

Check-in at InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 is conveniently located near Noi Bai International Airport (HAN), approximately 15.53 miles away (25 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72?

InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Vietnam Museum of Ethnology - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- The Museum of Vietnamese Women - 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)
- Ho Chi Minh Complex - 4.97 miles away (8 kilometers)
- Tran Quoc Pagoda - 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)
- Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre - 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
