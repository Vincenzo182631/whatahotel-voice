# InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72Dining

Savour an exciting range of cuisines while enjoying panoramic views of Hanoi. Located on the 62nd floor of the Landmark72 building, InterContinental Hanoi Landmark72 offers a wide variety of exquisite dining experiences in its five restaurants and bars.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

### Club InterContinental

Discover elevated hospitality, thoughtfully personalised services and an inspiring culinary journey throughout the day at our exclusive Club InterContinental Lounge on the 71st floor, the largest in Southeast Asia. Lounge access is reserved for guests booking Club InterContinental rooms, suites, and InterContinental Royal Ambassadors.

### Sunday Familicious Lunch

Gather the family and make your way to 3 Spoons for the fabulous Familicious Lunch! Celebrate family get-togethers with fun-filled activities for the little ones such as cookie decorating, balloon twisting and colouring. Parents will be able to enjoy a variety of traditional and authentic delicacies from different countries.

## 3 Spoons

With panoramic views of Hanoi skyline, 3 Spoons offers a vibrant dining experience, featuring a curated selection of global cuisines. Beyond the extensive breakfast buffet, guests can witness chef's artistry firsthand. Savor authentic Vietnamese dishes, fresh seafood and Korean BBQ from dedicated Japanese and Korean kitchens. For European flavors, enjoy fragrant Italian breads, pastas and freshly made pizzas, creating a diverse culinary journey in an unforgettable setting.

## Q Bar

Inspired by the beauty of O Quy Ho Pass in Northern Vietnam, Q Bar is the best sleek speakeasy bar in Hanoi offering innovative and selective cocktails while enjoying rhythmic lounge music amongst a nocturnal scene of city skyline views. The bar aims to create a cocktail culture in Hanoi and enhances this goal through its advanced equipment for cocktail creation to ensure that excitement is added to all senses of cocktail lovers.
