## Delfinos Italian Chophouse

Savor the comforting flavors of Italy and enjoy indoor and outdoor dining at Delfinos Italian Chophouse. Certain to satisfy any appetite, we feature a selection of regional Italian favorites, Certified Black Angus meat selections from the Butcher’s block, fresh seafood, and an extensive wine list.

Sunday - Thursday: 5:00 PM - 9:00 PM

Friday - Saturday: 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Phone: 386.246.5650

Delfinos Italian Chophouse is open to club members and registered resort guests only. Reservations are highly recommended.

## Celebrate In Style

Hosting an intimate gathering or special celebration soon? Reserve the Private Dining Room above Delfinos Italian Chophouse for your next occasion! This hidden spot features a chic and cozy atmosphere and can accommodate up to 24 guests.

For information on reserving the Private Dining Room, please email Beth Giovacchini at Beth.Giovacchini@hammockbeach.com.
