Oceanview Three-Bedroom Condominiums

## Elevated Stays with Space to Unwind

Located in the Ocean Tower, these expansive three-bedroom residences offer relaxing coastal views from a private open-air balcony. Inside, you'll find coastal-inspired interiors, a fully equipped kitchen, and comfortable living and dining areas designed for gathering. With modern amenities and thoughtful touches throughout, these accommodations are ideal for families or friends seeking space, comfort, and connection by the sea.

## Resort View 3-Bedroom

These spacious three-bedroom condominiums overlook the beautifully landscaped resort grounds. Inside, you’ll find refined interiors with tile flooring, a fully equipped gourmet kitchen, and inviting living and dining areas ideal for gathering. The master suite features a soaking garden tub and walk-in shower, while two additional bedrooms include private baths for added comfort. A private balcony, in-room laundry, in-room safe, WiFi, and flat-screen TVs complete the experience—perfect for families or groups looking to stretch out and settle in.

## Ocean View 3-Bedroom

Wake up to Atlantic views from this spacious three-bedroom condominium in our Ocean Towers. Thoughtfully designed for comfort and connection, the open-concept layout includes a full gourmet kitchen, dining area for six to eight, and an inviting living space with fireplace. Each bedroom includes an en-suite bath, while the primary suite features a garden tub and walk-in shower for a spa-like retreat. Additional highlights include a private oceanview balcony, laundry room, in-room safe, flat-screen TVs, and WiFi—an ideal retreat for those seeking space and serenity by the sea.
