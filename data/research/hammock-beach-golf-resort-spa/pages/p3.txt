Boutique Comfort by the Sea & the Green

## The Lodge At Hammock Beach

On the grounds of Hammock Beach Golf Resort & Spa sits an ocean-front boutique hotel: The Lodge at Hammock Beach.  The lodge features 20 well-appointed guest rooms with views of the coast and The Ocean Course.  The Lodge is a short walk to the amenities and also houses the award-winning Atlantic Grille.

The Lodge is pet-friendly. Learn more about our Pet Policy here.

## Ocean View 2 Queens

Tucked inside The Lodge at Hammock Beach Golf Resort & Spa, these ocean-view rooms offer a relaxed coastal stay just steps from the sand. Gaze out over the Atlantic, pool, or golf course from your private balcony. Inside, enjoy two plush queen beds, a marble entryway, granite vanity, and décor inspired by the calming tones of the shoreline—perfect for a serene beach escape.

## Ocean View King

Enjoy comfort and quiet sophistication in an Ocean View King room at The Lodge. Each space offers scenic views of the pool, coastline, or golf course, plus a private balcony for taking in the breeze. Featuring a king bed, marble entry, coastal-inspired décor, and a sleek bathroom with a granite vanity, this room is ideal for couples or solo travelers seeking peace by the sea.

## ADA Ocean View King

Designed with accessibility and relaxation in mind, the Ocean View King ADA room at The Lodge offers a welcoming retreat by the sea. Take in sweeping views of the pool, coastline, or golf course from your private balcony, then step inside to find a serene space styled with coastal accents. The room features a plush king bed and an accessible bathroom with a granite vanity, combining thoughtful design with modern comfort.

## Ocean Front King

For those who want to wake up to the waves, the Ocean Front King rooms at The Lodge deliver direct Atlantic views from a private balcony. Located closest to the shoreline, these guest favorites feature a king bed, marble entry, and breezy décor that echoes the beach below. With the Atlantic Grille and Ocean Course Pro Shop just steps away, convenience and coastal charm come together in every detail.

## Ocean View Suite

The Ocean View Suite at The Lodge blends space, comfort, and sweeping views. This one-bedroom retreat includes a king bedroom, a separate sitting area for relaxing or entertaining, and two private balconies overlooking the ocean, pool, and golf course. Featuring premium bedding and a soothing coastal color palette, the suite offers an inviting atmosphere for extended stays or special occasions.
