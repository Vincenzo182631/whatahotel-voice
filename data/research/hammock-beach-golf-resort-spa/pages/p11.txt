## Lobby Bar

Ease into the afternoon or unwind in the evening with a signature cocktail, craft beer, or premium wine in our bright, welcoming lobby. On select evenings and weekends, enjoy live entertainment from local artists.

## Lobby Bar

- Wednesday - Thursday: 3:00 PM - 9:00 PM
- Friday & Saturday: 2:00 PM - 11:00 PM

Lobby Bar is open to club members and registered resort guests only.
