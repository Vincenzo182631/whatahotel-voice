## Beach Brew

More than just your morning fix - Beach Brew is a modern coffee haven where craft and flavor take center stage. Featuring artisan blends from Kahwa Coffee Roasters, cold brew and nitro on tap, and a curated selection of specialty sandwiches and fresh grab-and-go fare, it's your daily ritual, reimagined. Located in the Main Lobby, Beach Brew invites you to savor the familiar comforts of your favorite coffee stop - elevated.
