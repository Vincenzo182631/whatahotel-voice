Versatile Stays in the Heart of the Resort

## Main Tower Accommodations

Enjoy comfort and style in the Main Tower, where one-bedroom suites feature coastal décor, queen sleeper sofas, and convenient kitchenettes—perfect for couples or solo travelers. For families or larger groups, spacious three- and four-bedroom condominiums include full kitchens, dining areas, private balconies, and scenic views of the golf course or ocean. With close proximity to resort amenities like pools, golf, the spa, and more, these accommodations offer a seamless blend of space, convenience, and relaxation.
