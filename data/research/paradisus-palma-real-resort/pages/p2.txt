Why book?
Located in a turquoise oasis, Paradisus Palma Real has undoubtedly made the most of its serene surroundings.
Set the scene
A prestigious, high-end locale on the unspoiled beauty of Bavaro Beach, Paradisus Hotel has an innate understanding of what makes Punta Cana so atmospheric. You can taste the salty breeze when you walk outside, feel the sun melting into your skin, and almost touch the Caribbean Sea beaming in the distance as you enter the lobby, which, unlike most resorts in the area, boasts a bountiful view of the ocean. From there, walk through gardens dotted with emerald shrubs, listen to the morning choir—Vervain Hummingbirds singing their eternally upbeat tune—and make your way to your room, where you can set down your overpacked luggage, let out a sigh of relief, and gaze out at an ocean so blue it doesn’t even look real.
The backstory
This wildly popular hotel was built back in 2010 with a less aesthetically pleasing look, but it underwent renovation in 2022 to become a glorious staple of Punta Cana. A cool, spry, bright space for couples and honeymooners, it’s an obvious standout amidst Paradisius’s four hotels in the area.
The rooms
A palette so eye-squintingly white it dazzles in the sun, the exterior of Paradisus is a colonial-meets-tropical delight. Glimmering pool water reflects off lofty white pillars, Spanish tiles, and cobblestone pathways leading to hidden gardens. But when it comes to the rooms, I’ve seen more color in black-and-white movies. The suites are muted spaces defined by neutral tones—incredibly elegant yet incredibly empty. Something to brighten those sleek marble floors, wooden bed frames, and gray curtains, carpets, and bed sheets could add some personality to the space.
Food and drink
Expect a lavish dining scene in the à la carte restaurants, albeit one that comes at a price. While breakfast and lunch are first come, first serve, the mood becomes much more classy at the reservation-only dinner spots. As sunsets fade into the night, visitors trade their damp, sandy beachwear for floral dresses and white linen suits, and chefs bring their international cuisine to the table, giving traditional dishes a Caribbean twist. One night, I snagged a table by the water’s edge at Sante and sipped a glass of Cab Sauv as the sun set. Later, I devoured a plate of lobster ravioli loaded with rich white wine sauce. The dish was so good it nearly distracted me from the spectacular stars appearing over the crashing waves at crepuscule.
The spa
Much like the vibe of Punta Cana, the spa is zen, hushed, surrounded by sylvan nature, and almost completely secluded. The team of specialists emphasizes fluid shiatsu techniques over deep tissue rubs—treatments and decor seem to be inspired by Southeast Asian spas. I left my Thai massage feeling like a weight had been lifted off my shoulders, without the soreness or initial stiffness you might expect from a resort massage. Afterward, I dipped into a soothing hot tub at the spa’s entrance, nestled under glowing candles.
The neighborhood
The hotel is tucked behind a renowned golf course, miles away from civilization. It’s perfectly situated for visitors who are solely here to soak up rays of sun and unerring solitude. You won’t be interrupted by honking cars or obnoxious phone calls here, instead, you’ll only hear subtle splashes of pool water, waves lapping against the sand, birds chirping in symphonic unison, palm trees slow dancing in the breeze, wine glasses tinkling and echoing off parchment white walls—a 40 piece orchestra of vacation sounds creating a naturally poetic tune.
Accessibility
All rooms are handicap accessible.
