## Fitness Center

Keep your fitness goals on track throughout your getaway with a modern on-property fitness center, inspired wellness classes, state-of-the-art exercise equipment and outdoor activities curated to awaken your best self.

FITNESS CLASSES AND ACTIVITIES

Fitness Classes

Whether you need help getting started, pushing beyond a plateau or finding a new challenge, Revive Spa Fitness Center offers various levels of instruction including personal training and group classes available in-room and at the fitness center.

In-Room Fitness

The Joffrey Ballet and JW Marriott present Behind the Barre, an inspiring and fitness-focused video series that celebrates a shared passion for well-being and exceptional training. Join Ashley Wheater, the Artistic Director of The Joffrey Ballet, as he and two Joffrey dancers inspire wellbeing, balance, energy and renewal.

Personal Training

Our experts will design a new program or modify your existing one, helping you make the most of the time you spend in the gym.

50 minutes: $75

Yoga and Mat Pilates

Expert-led programs are offered one-on-one or in a private group setting and are suitable for all levels.

50 minutes (yoga): $75

Tennis

Three premier hard, lighted courts are available at the Tennis Center, which features a pro shop, seasonal daily clinics, group and private lessons, matching services, ball machine rentals and same-day racquet stringing.

Outdoor Fitness

Arizona offers the perfect climate for outdoor recreation; in the summer, be sure to get outside early before the temperature soars. The area around the resort offers natural beauty, which can be explored and experienced with a variety of activities.

Biking

What better way to explore the beauty of our scenic Sonoran Desert setting than during an enjoyable bicycle ride. To rent a bike, visit the Revive Spa or call (480) 293-3700.

Group Classes

Enjoy the community of wellness. The fitness center offers a daily schedule of group classes including yoga, spinning, Pilates and aquatics.

Walking and Jogging

Walking and jogging maps are available at the front desk and feature trails of various lengths and difficulties.

## Sedona Cove – Your Luxury Retreat

Sedona Cove, our exclusive pool experience, includes upgraded and enhanced food and beverage offerings, VIP seating and service for an incomparable day, poolside.

Book Now

Learn More

##### Cabanas

The private Sedona Cove Cabanas feature cushioned chaise lounges and sectional seating, a cooling misting fan, complimentary bottled water, refrigerator, towel service, digital safe, TV with DirectTV, complimentary Wi-Fi and a dedicated attendant.

##### Daybeds and Chaise Lounge Chairs

Daybeds and chaise lounge chairs can be reserved in advance or the day of rental by our Sedona Cove Concierge located just past the Welcome Center within the Sedona Cove pool area. Prices vary by season.

##### Contact Us

Reservations for our exclusive pool experience may be made online or through The Club at Desert Ridge, the resort front office or by contacting the recreation department up to three months in advance of arrival by emailing PHXDR\_recreation@marriott.com.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Splash Canyon Waterslides, 42" and 48" height requirement (Outdoor)Sky Island, adult pool experience, reservations required (Outdoor)Canyon Creek Lazy River (Outdoor)WhirlpoolLazy RiverWater Slide

Does JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa have an on-site spa?

Yes, Revive Spa is located on-site at JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa. Click here to book a service now.

Does JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa offer golf course access nearby or at the hotel?

Yes, hotel guests have access to Wildfire Golf Club. Click here to learn more.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa?

MIM-The Musical Instrument Museum, Talking Stick Resort Arena, Arizona State University - Sun Devils, Chase Field, Desert Botanical Gardens and more are nearby JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa.
