# JW Marriott Desert Ridge Resort & Spa Waterpark

## AquaRidge WaterPark

The 140,000-square-foot AquaRidge WaterPark at JW Marriott Desert Ridge Resort & Spa is full of water-filled adventures for all ages, featuring a lazy river, slides, an adult-only island, and more. Our Phoenix hotel with a waterslide is exciting for all ages.

## AquaNights Summer

As the sun sets, JW Marriott Desert Ridge transforms into a vibrant oasis of poolside nightlife and unforgettable summer experiences. Dive into AquaNights, where the lazy river and water slides stay open late every night, adding fun to your night under the stars. Every Friday, the energy peaks with a dazzling drone show and bubble bash and dance party. Our waterpark hotel in Phoenix offers these experiences all summer long, from May 22 to August 8.

## Water Fun For All Ages

At AquaRidge, within JW Marriott Desert Ridge, a premier waterslide hotel in Phoenix, AZ, there's something for everyone to bask in the sun and revel in the pools. The vast complex features the winding Canyon Creek Lazy River, two lively splash pads, inviting fire pits, and the centerpiece, Wildfire Grove. Wildfire Grove includes our main pool with a captivating waterfall tower and an activity pool. For exclusivity, Sedona Cove offers a gracefully curved pool with VIP cabanas and lounge chairs. Enjoy the Sedona Springs whirlpool’s soothing warmth amid stunning mountain views. For an adults-only retreat, Sky Island awaits those 21 and over.

Adult Only Exclusive Pool Experience

### Sky Island

The 21+ adults-only Sky Island sets the stage for serenity and relaxation. Guests are greeted with a glass of champagne or sparkling water as well as thoughtful touches throughout the day with the reservation only exclusive experience. Choose a bungalow, daybed or lounge chair for your tranquil day at Sky Island.

Water Adventure

### Gullywasher

A favorite of the Canyon Creek Water Slides at this Phoenix hotel with waterslide is the dual-person tube ride featuring thrilling loops, high-banking turns, and a heart-racing vertical wall plunge. Minimum height of 42”.

### Little Eddy

This slide features an enclosed tube with three loops and a longer ride time, guaranteed to be a favorite for all ages. Minimum height of 42”.

### The Drop

For the more adventurous, this slide begins with a fast loop followed by an exhilarating high-speed, free-fall drop finish. Minimum height of 48”.

### Splash Island

Little ones are sure to love splashing through the pop jets and boulders with water spilling out with views of the water slides.

### Canyon Creek Lazy River

Guests can relax and float on tubes down this winding lazy river surrounding Splash Island.
