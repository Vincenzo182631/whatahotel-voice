# Welcome to JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa

## Experience A Luxury Resort In Phoenix, AZ

Experience a transformative stay at JW Marriott® Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa, a AAA 4-Diamond luxury resort in Phoenix, AZ. Set against the stunning backdrop of the Sonoran Desert, our Phoenix resort offers the perfect escape for family vacations, romantic getaways, business meetings or special events. Guests can enjoy five sparkling pools, a relaxing lazy river, water slides, private poolside...See More cabanas, and 17 pickleball courts with a dedicated pro shop. As a premier Phoenix, AZ, spa resort, we offer two championship golf courses, PGA instruction, and professional club fittings. Unwind at our full-service luxury spa and experience exceptional dining, personalized service, and stunning desert views. Whether you're searching for a family-friendly resort in Phoenix with a lazy river, or a luxury desert retreat for relaxation, JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa delivers an unforgettable Arizona getaway.See Less
