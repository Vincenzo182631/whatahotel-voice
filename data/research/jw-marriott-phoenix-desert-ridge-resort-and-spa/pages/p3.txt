Twenty6

## Igniting Connections

Casually elegant with jaw-dropping views, Twenty6 is recognized as one of America’s great bourbon bars. Savor private labels, specialty and barrel aged craft cocktails, the signature JW Marriott wine program featuring 20 domestic and international wines by the glass. Pair your libations with a selection of inventive small plates and charcuterie while enjoying the panoramic vistas from our outdoor patio, where you and your friends can enjoy live music Friday and Saturday nights from 6 to 10pm. Twenty6 lounge is located on the bottom level of the JW Marriott Desert Ridge Resort & Spa. Complimentary self-parking is available on-site and valet parking is available for a fee. For more information about Twenty6, please call 480-293-2875.

Meritage

### An Urban Tavern Evolution

Featuring American fare, creative cocktails and Arizona craft beers and microbrews, Meritage is a fun, upscale gathering spot. An outdoor patio and lawn offers a relaxing respite for happy hour and post-golf bites. For resort guests, club members and the Desert Ridge community, we are also a favorite neighborhood gathering spot providing a casual vibe within a contemporary, industrial setting.

### Events at Meritage

Ignite the appetite for the exceptional. For large party functions and private events, reach us by calling 480-293-3681 or email at mayra.oharris@marriott.com. Our professional planners will help coordinate everything needed for an exceptionally memorable event.

### Poolside Rentals

Looking to enjoy our exclusive Griffin Bay Pool? Ask our poolside concierge about renting one of our twelve Cabana Cottages or Chaise Lounge Chairs for the day. Check out our upscale Lazy River Day Suites for an exclusive indoor and outdoor cabana experience with your own private bathroom.

### Events by Just a Splash

Poolside parties and special events are a breeze at Just a Splash. Our Restaurant Sales Coordinator is here to answer all of your event questions about creating an unforgettable special event. Please contact Lexa Estrada at 480-293-2875 or email Lexa.Estrada@marriott.com and leave the details to us.

Spa Bistro

## Nourishment for Body & Soul

Complement your rejuvenating spa experience with a menu focused on your wellbeing at the Revive Spa Bistro. With a healthy selection of salads, sandwiches and fruit smoothies, enjoy both indoor and al fresco dining in a tranquil setting with remarkable views of the lush resort grounds. Beer, wine and crafted cocktails are also available.

Just a Splash Bar & Grill

## Poolside Eats & Treats

Dive into refreshing appetizers, salads and casual poolside lunch fare with featured favorites like Green Chili Burgers, Sonoran Hot Dogs and Street Tacos. Just a Splash features a full bar to enjoy your favorite beverage with your meal or snack, including signature drinks like the Kokopelli Colada––a perfect way to kick-off weekend relaxation or to catch a game on our poolside TVs. And the littles won’t be able to resist our Dirty Worm––an Oreo cookie milkshake seasoned with gummy worms.

Restaurant and bar hours vary seasonally. Please contact us at 480-293-3798. Located at the main pool, Just A Splash Bar & Grill accepts all major credit cards, cash and room charges.

If you're craving extra privacy, enjoy your meal in one of our cabanas, which includes a TV and Wi-Fi.
