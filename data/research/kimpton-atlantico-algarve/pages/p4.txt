### A Stay Made for You and Your Pet

Bring your four-legged companion along and enjoy an Algarve escape together. At Kimpton Atlântico Algarve, pets are welcome to share in the experience, from relaxed mornings to coastal walks and sunny days by the Atlantic. Because the best holidays are the ones you can enjoy together.

Learn more

### Heated Pool Retreat by the Atlantic

Enjoy moments of relaxation all year round in our heated pool, designed for a soothing escape whatever the season. Surrounded by a tranquil setting and the Algarve’s natural beauty, it is the perfect place to unwind, reconnect and embrace a slower pace during your stay at Kimpton Atlântico Algarve.

### Stay Active, Anytime

Keep your wellness routine going during your stay with our 24/7 gym, equipped to support every type of training. Whether you prefer an early morning workout or a late-night session, our team is here to help you make the most of your holiday while staying energised, balanced and feeling your best.

### Holistic Wellness & Spa Sanctuary

Rebalance mind and body in our tranquil Wellness Hub, featuring a hydrotherapy circuit, sauna, steam room, and indulgent treatments. From sunrise yoga to soothing massages, every moment is designed for ultimate relaxation and self-care in style.

Learn more

### Relaxing Poolside Moments

Unwind by the pool in an atmosphere designed for calm and comfort. Between peaceful surroundings, warm sunshine and tranquil waters, every moment invites you to slow down and simply enjoy the rhythm of the Algarve.

#### Pet amenities

- The bond between humans and their pets is stronger than ever, we are stepping up to ensure that every member of the family feels welcome and pampered. Understanding the importance of this connection, we are proud to offer an array of amenities specifically designed for our furry guests. From water bowls to plush beds, our commitment to your pet's comfort is unparalleled. We recognize that pets are more than just animals; they are cherished members of the family. Traveling with pets can often be stressful for both the animals and their owners. Our goal is to create a welcoming environment that eases this transition. By providing essential amenities like water bowls, food bowls, comfortable beds, and engaging toys, we strive to make your pet feel at home, no matter where they are. This thoughtful attention to detail ensures that every moment spent in our hotel is enjoyable for pets, allowing owners to relax and focus on their own experience. we aim to create a memorable experience for both you and your furry companions, making your stay truly exceptional. Hydration and nutrition are paramount for any pet's well-being. Our hotels provide high-quality, stylish water and food bowls that not only serve a practical purpose but also complement the luxurious environment. These bowls are designed with safety in mind, featuring non-slip bases to prevent spills and messes. We ensure that fresh water is always available, and our staff is attentive to refill these bowls regularly, allowing your pet to stay hydrated during their stay. Every pet deserves a good night's sleep, and that’s why we offer plush, comfortable beds tailored for our four-legged guests. These beds come in various sizes to accommodate pets of all breeds, ensuring that each animal has a cozy spot to rest. Made from high-quality materials, our beds are designed to provide orthopedic support, catering to pets of all ages, particularly those who may have joint issues or require extra comfort. Whether your pet prefers curling up or stretching out, they’ll find a perfect resting place in our rooms. Keeping pets entertained during their stay is also a priority for us. We provide a selection of engaging toys that are both fun and safe for your furry friend. From chew toys to interactive puzzles, our collection is curated to stimulate your pet’s mind and keep them active. These toys are designed to promote healthy play and interaction, whether your pet enjoys solo activities or playtime with their owners. Our goal is to ensure that your pet remains happy and occupied, providing a sense of normalcy while away from home.Traveling together has never been easier or more enjoyable, and we look forward to welcoming you and your pets for a delightful getaway.
For information about our pet amenities and hotel policies, please contact our reservations team. They are available to assist you with any questions you may have regarding our offerings for furry guests, including details about our pet-friendly accommodations, amenities, and any specific guidelines. You can reach them via phone or email, and they will be happy to provide you with all the necessary information to ensure a wonderful stay for you and your pet. We look forward to welcoming you!
