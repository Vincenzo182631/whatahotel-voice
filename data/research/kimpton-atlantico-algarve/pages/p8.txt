# Kimpton Atlântico AlgarveLocation

The Algarve is a treasure trove in shades of blue and green. With its stunning coastline, beautiful year-round climate, and unique landscape, Algarve is home to many natural attractions, outdoor experiences, and—of course—beaches. In addition to golfing, beaching, hiking, and exploring, there is plenty more to do and experience in the Algarve.
Kimpton Atlântico Algarve is conveniently located near the Albufeira city centre, where visitors will find shopping, dining, and local charm. The hotel is also central to the most popular attractions in Southern Portugal, from action-packed racing tracks and amusement parks to insightful animal sanctuaries and world-class golf courses. Discover some of our favourite things nearby and plan for your stay in Algarve to be one you'll remember.

### Steps from São Rafael Beach

Wake up to the soothing sounds of the ocean and enjoy direct access to the stunning São Rafael Beach. A golden-sand retreat just moments away, perfect for sun-soaked days, refreshing dips, and unforgettable coastal sunsets. Your front-row seat to Algarve’s natural beauty.

## Things to do

### Museu Municipal de Arqueologia

Praça da República 1, Albufeira, Albufeira 8200-109

The Municipal Archaeological Museum contains the usual mix of archaeological exhibits found in the Algarve: Roman, Visigothic and Moorish and also hosts temporary exhibitions in the rooms on its upper floor

Learn more

### Albufeira Adventure Park

Estrada de Santa Eulália, 215, Albufeira, Faro 8200 - 381

This leisure park offers outdoor tree climbing and paintballing activities in a pine forest. Perfect for a family outing, a team building event, a birthday or a stag party in Albufeira.

Learn more

### Electric Karting Albufeira Marina

Albufeira Marina - Underground Car Park, Passeio do Ouro, R. Marina de Albufeira, Albufeira, Faro 8200-371

Indoor Karting with a suberb 400m track is known for its speed and adrenaline. With 12 bends and a straight, drivers have plenty of overtaking opportunities! Your courage will pay off as soon as you pass our two hairpin bends! Additional services: lockers, parking, toilets, air conditioning and we provide the racing suits! Our karts are 200cc with 4-stroke engines! We also have 160cc karts for youngsters and F1 hopefuls!
