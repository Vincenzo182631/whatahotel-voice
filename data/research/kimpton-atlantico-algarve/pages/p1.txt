Welcome to Kimpton Atlântico Algarve, where the warmth of the Algarve sun meets the endless blue of the Atlantic Ocean. Set along the coast of São Rafael Beach, our coastal retreat captures the spirit of southern Portugal through natural beauty, authentic flavours and moments to enjoy at your own pace.

Here, every day invites you to slow down. Begin your morning surrounded by Mediterranean gardens, take a swim in our heated pool, relax beneath the Algarve sunshine or admire the changing colours of the ocean throughout the day.

Our culinary experiences celebrate the richness of the region, bringing together local ingredients, seasonal flavours and Portuguese traditions across our restaurants and bars — from relaxed daytime moments to memorable dinners overlooking the Atlantic.

At Wellness Hub Atlântico, discover a space dedicated to balance and wellbeing, featuring a vitality pool, sauna, Turkish bath, personalised treatments and spaces designed to help you relax, restore and reconnect.

Between the Algarve coastline, regional flavours and moments of tranquillity, Kimpton Atlântico Algarve invites you to experience a stay shaped by sunshine, serenity and authenticity.

### A Space to Meet, Connect and Inspire

Bring your team together in a setting designed for meaningful meetings, corporate events and private gatherings. With versatile event spaces, attentive service and the Atlantic as a backdrop, Kimpton Atlântico Algarve offers an inspiring environment to connect, collaborate and create memorable moments.

### Seasonal Flavours, Rooted in Tradition

Discover the flavours of Portugal through dishes inspired by local traditions and the changing seasons. From fresh regional ingredients to familiar recipes with a contemporary touch, every plate celebrates the richness of the Algarve and the ingredients at their best.

### A Stay Made for You and Your Pet

Bring your four-legged companion along and enjoy an Algarve escape together. At Kimpton Atlântico Algarve, pets are welcome to share in the experience, from relaxed mornings to coastal walks and sunny days by the Atlantic. Because the best holidays are the ones you can enjoy together.

Learn more

### Heated Pool Retreat by the Atlantic

Enjoy moments of relaxation all year round in our heated pool, designed for a soothing escape whatever the season. Surrounded by a tranquil setting and the Algarve’s natural beauty, it is the perfect place to unwind, reconnect and embrace a slower pace during your stay at Kimpton Atlântico Algarve.

Keep your wellness routine going during your stay with our 24/7 gym, equipped to support every type of training. Whether you prefer an early morning workout or a late-night session, our team is here to help you make the most of your holiday while staying energised, balanced and feeling your best.

Garage Parking: Available at an additional cost of €5 per night, per car. This secure option offers protection from the elements.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Atlântico Algarve?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Atlântico Algarve.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Atlântico Algarve?

Check-in at Kimpton Atlântico Algarve is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Atlântico Algarve offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Atlântico Algarve offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Atlântico Algarve from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Atlântico Algarve is conveniently located near Faro International Airport - Gago Coutinho (FAO), approximately 17.4 miles away (28 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Atlântico Algarve?

Kimpton Atlântico Algarve is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Museu Municipal de Arqueologia
- Albufeira Adventure Park - 5.16 miles away (8.3 kilometers)
- Electric Karting Albufeira Marina - 1.12 miles away (1.8 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Atlântico Algarve puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
