### Great Hotel - But lacked management oversight

9 days ago

A lovely hotel that is an oasis of calm away from the tacky and unpleasant atmosphere of Albufeira. Stay for a break to rest and recharge by the pool, the beach is sandy but sea a bit rocky and lots of seaweed so not ideal for swimming.
This should be a great hotel if management were more present talking to guests and focusing on the detail.
Lunch and evening food offering (menu) is very limited with no specials or events even though they advertise a regular BBQ evening. Whilst we were the Mares was closed in the evening further reducing choice. Visited old town once for dinner and hope never to have to visit Albufeira town again!!
The pools don’t appear to be heated despite the hundreds of solar panel on the hotel roof.
It really is a nice hotel and probably one of the best in the area with some attention to detail it should be even better.

ProsSleep Quality, Room Cleanliness, Amenities

Yes, I recommend this hotel.

### Very enjoyable stay

15 days ago

This was my 2nd and I will return soon. As a solo traveller I found the hotel team generally very friendly and helpful. I was fortunate to have a room upgrade with a sea view which was much appreciated. A few tweaks needed at reception to make the welcome even better but overall a very good stay. The rooms are very clean and with good quality linens, etc. I would recommend that guests have a car so they can see more of the surrounding areas. The pool area was not busy with sun loungers available but I suspect in the summer it may be a lot busier. Overall a very enjoyable stay.
