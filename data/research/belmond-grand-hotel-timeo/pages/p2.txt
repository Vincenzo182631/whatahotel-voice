## The front row to Sicily’s eternal beauty

Welcoming artists and explorers for over 150 years, Grand Hotel Timeo combines ageless indulgence with spectacular Sicilian views. Located next to Taormina's Greek Theatre and with Mount Etna in sight, we embrace Sicilian culture through Michelin-starred dining and experiences rooted in the region.

- 67 rooms, 2 Signature Suites and 21 rooms in the newly renovated Villa Timeo
- Dior Spa Grand Hotel Timeo
- Pool and gym
- Michelin-starred dining at Otto Geleng
- 1 hour drive from Catania Airport
- Complimentary shuttle to Lido Villeggiatura at Villa Sant’Andrea
- Ristorante Timeo, Timeo Grill, Bar Timeo

Read The Story. Opens in a new window

Set on one of the most prized spots in Taormina, Grand Hotel Timeo’s roots wind back across 150 years.

Our story began in 1850 as a humble guesthouse for the art scene andpainters who were drawn to Taormina by the scenic views. German artist Otto Geleng stayed with us to witness the wonders of the Greek Theatre, and his paintings from our terrace captured the world’s imagination. The first hotel to be built in Taormina, we harness the history and mystique of nearby Mount Etna and Greek theatre.
