# Romantic Big Sur Restaurant with a View \| Alila Ventana Big Sur

# Drink & Dine

Savor the flavors of California’s Central Coast at Ventana Big Sur. Reflecting the region’s bounty, our romantic restaurants offer memorable dining with flair. Dining is included with your stay. Dine in The Sur House, our elegant and rustic restaurant with breathtaking views of the coast or let us prepare meals to enjoy in your room, poolside or out among the many Big Sur vistas on the property. For exceptional private dining, join us at The Glass House for a prix fixe menu. (Additional fee)

## The Glass House

Space available upon request for an additional fee. Please email SJCAL.RFP@ventanabigsur.com to learn more and reserve. \| Please note that reservations for this experience must be made at least 48 hours in advance.

Our exquisite Glass House and its breathtaking ocean views create a private dining experience like no other. Perched on the edge of the continent, your party will enjoy a prix fixe menu and make memories to last a lifetime at this exceptional and romantic restaurant. Modifications are available by request.

## The Sur House

The Sur House, high above the Pacific where the forest, mountains, and sea converge, offers an inclusive dining model for our resort guests. All meals are included with your stay, including breakfast on the day of departure. Upgraded options such as alcoholic beverages are available for separate purchase. Ocean views from the terrace area are as breathtaking as our Big Sur-inspired cuisine, which features fresh ingredients from our expansive organic garden.
