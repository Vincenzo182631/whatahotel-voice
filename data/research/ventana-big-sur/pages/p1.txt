## Find Harmony & Unmatched Beauty in the Majesty of Big Sur

Alila Ventana Big Sur is a luxury resort on the California coast, overlooking the Pacific Ocean, surrounded by redwood forest, and tailored to adults. Set along the iconic Big Sur coastline, the resort offers scenic hiking trails, immersive outdoor experiences, and wellness treatments. Dining is included with every stay.Located near Pfeiffer Beach, Bixby Bridge and Point Sur Lighthouse, the resort provides access to iconic Big Sur attractions. A destination for peaceful, nature-driven experiences designed for relaxation, privacy and elevated coastal luxury.

Coldstream Bliss: A Big Sur Ritual

Hike through Big Sur's majestic redwoods to a secluded forest spot for a cold plunge that awakens your senses, leaving you feeling refreshed, energized and deeply alive.

**Duration:** 2-2.5 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Closed-toe shoes and a bathing suit underneath appropriate athletic attire is ideal so guests can easily change in a outdoor setting. Bring any warm layers of clothes you would like for after the experience; we'll keep them safe. Pack a filled water bottle.

**Price:** $89 per person during renovation/low season, $109 per person fall & spring, $129 per person summer

**Book Now**

**Additional Details:** Also included in this experience is: A designated bag with towel; a surf poncho; transportation to and from the experience; hot tea.

## Discover Alila Ventana Big Sur

Fitness Center

Expand your approach to exercise at Ventana Big Sur. Our welcoming fitness center is open 24 hours a day, with cardio machines, free weights and more, but that’s just the start. Complimentary yoga, guided walking meditations, hiking on spectacular Big Sur trails and two luxurious outdoor pools provide exhilarating experiences you won’t find anywhere but our inspiring piece of the California coast.

Sustainable Tourism

We are proudly preserving the Big Sur landscape and commit to be the epitome of an eco-friendly California luxury resort. Our initiatives include property-wide recycling, native landscaping, electric vehicle charging stations and on-property housing for more than 40 employees, which reduces emissions. As a member of the Monterey Bay Aquarium’s Seafood Watch program, we buy and serve sustainable seafood. We also use ingredients and eggs from our organic garden, maintained through composting.

Pools

Our Big Sur luxury hotel offers two spectacular outdoor pools, each with its own experience. Meadow Pool delivers expansive views of the ocean and redwood forest, as well as private cabanas and an infinity-edge hot tub. Meanwhile, our secluded Mountain Pool is perched high atop the resort, surrounded by towering redwoods, with adjacent Japanese hot baths offering a serene communal bath experience.

## Things To Do

Experience the bountiful beauty surrounding Alila Ventana Big Sur, whether you stay on the resort property or venture beyond to hike at stunning Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park, get a feel for local flavors and art at Nepenthe or, depending on the season, view migrating whales along the Pacific coastline. The Henry Miller Memorial Library is less than 2 miles away from on Highway 1, and if you want to go a bit farther, Carmel-by-the-Sea and Monterey are just over 30 miles north of our hotel.
