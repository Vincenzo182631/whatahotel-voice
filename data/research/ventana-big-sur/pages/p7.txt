# Hotel Policies

Resort Bookings

**As part of your stay, we are pleased to include the following dining and amenities:**

- Dinner service on your arrival day
- Breakfast service on your departure day
- Breakfast, lunch & dinner service on all other days during your stay
- Gratuities for your included meals. All additional gratuities are at your discretion and are 100% distributed to colleagues
- In-room minibar & a branded refillable water bottle for your use while on property
- Selected programming & experiences
- Non-alcoholic sodas, waters, teas, coffees.
- A welcome beverage, celebrating local Californian wines
- Complimentary valet parking

**Additional dining and amenities can be added to your stay for a supplemental fee, these include:**

- Alcoholic beverages & mocktails
- Breakfast & Lunch service on your arrival day
- Lunch & Dinner service on your departure day
- Selected premium programming & experiences
- Spa experiences & treatments
- The Glass House tasting menu experience
- Please note: In room dining incurs a $10 delivery fee

**Occupancy**: Alila Ventana Big Sur is designed for those seeking a peaceful escape. To preserve this atmosphere, our accommodations are limited to a maximum of two guests per room.

**Check in time**: Check-in time is at 4pm. Due to the size and nature of our resort, we strongly encourage all guests to arrive on or after 4pm to avoid waiting for their room. We have limited ability to accommodate early arrivals and late departures.

**Travel Conditions**:For the most up-to-date information on the road conditions on the way to Alila Ventana Big Sur, please refer to the CalTrans District 5 website. Safe travels!

**A Note for Families:** Tailored to adults, Alila Ventana Big Sur is designed around tranquil, nature immersive experiences and does not offer amenities, programming, or an environment typically suited to guests traveling with children.

**Optional Enhancements**

_Please note that spa services, retail, premium experiences, upgraded private dining and alcoholic beverages available for a supplementary fee._

Pet Policy

We’re delighted to welcome and pamper your four-legged companions. To ensure a seamless dining experience for all, dogs are invited to join you for breakfast, lunch, and dinner outside on The Sur House Terrace, weather permitting.  A dog bed and water bowl will be provided in your room, and our culinary team creates gourmet, dog-appropriate meals for an additional fee.

We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

**Fees**

$150/stay with a maximum of 2 dogs
