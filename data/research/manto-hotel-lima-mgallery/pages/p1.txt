## Description

Manto Hotel Lima - MGallery Collection

Boutique hotels with personality for a remarkable travel experience

Discover an authentic experience in the heart of Lima. Manto Hotel Lima MGallery Collection is a boutique hotel located in San Isidro, one of the city's most elegant districts, ideal for both business travelers and those looking to explore Lima's culture from a unique perspective.

inspired by the figure of the tapadas limeñas-iconic women from colonial Lima-Manto offers an immersive stay that combines contemporary design with historical references. The hotel features 64 boutique rooms equipped with all modern comforts, perfect for unwinding after exploring the financial district or visiting historical landmarks like Huaca Huallamarca, El Olivar Forest, or the Lima Golf Club.

Guests can enjoy creative local cuisine and signature cocktails at Saya Terraza & Bistró, set against a charming San Isidro view. Key amenities include high-speed Wi-Fi, a rooftop restaurant and bar, private parking, 24/7 reception, and meeting space.

> At Manto Hotel Lima, every guest becomes part of a story. We are inspired by the cultural legacy of the city, and we strive to make every stay as unique as Lima itself. We are committed to offering warm, attentive service with a strong local character.

Adrian Tarazona, Hotel Management
