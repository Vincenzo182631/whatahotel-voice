At JW Marriott Venice Resort & Spa, your furry friends are warmly welcomed. Our pet-friendly services ensure your pets enjoy as much luxury as you do. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Venice Resort & Spa have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Venice Resort & Spa have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Venice Resort & Spa?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Venice Resort & Spa is the Villa Rose with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Venice Resort & Spa?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Venice Resort & Spa permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
