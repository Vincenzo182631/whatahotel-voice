# The Lodge, Destination Spa at Woodloch, a Poconos Spa Resort

**One Resort, Many Paths.**

## Start Your Journey at Our Poconos Resort.

The Lodge, Destination Spa at Woodloch embraces a philosophy of personal awakening. As so many of us get swept up in the whirlwind of life, our all-inclusive spa resort in Poconos, Pennsylvania gives you an opportunity to shift from your everyday routine and refocus on yourself. Here, you are able to reconnect to creative and stress-relieving outlets that will continue to benefit you long after your time with us.

**At The Lodge at Woodloch**

## Choice is Cultivated.

Whether choosing a day packed with activities or simply relaxing, countless options await during your Pennsylvania vacation at our Poconos resort.

We encourage you to take a path less known and discover new boundaries. Within the challenge, you will find a deeper understanding of yourself and the world around you.

Try something new, explore unique paths, and challenge your status quo. With over 50 unique classes and offerings per day, there are endless things to do at our resort in the Poconos.

Experience exciting and educational events at The Lodge at Woodloch, featuring inspirational and motivational guest speakers, available to elevate your consciousness and personal awareness. Diverse opportunities are offered to discover a greater understanding of life, love, self, spirituality, health, music, healing, the environment and our world.

## All-Inclusive Rates

Our Poconos all-inclusive resort packages are designed to help you concentrate on what matters most - your wellness journey.

All packages at our Pennsylvania resort include lavish accommodations, three consecutive artfully-crafted gourmet meals per night of stay, stimulating speakers and workshops, and experiences for the mind, body, and soul. Guests are welcome to avail themselves of inspiring creative arts, scheduled group fitness and mind/body classes, cooking and baking demonstrations, scheduled group outdoor adventure excursions, and the use of health and fitness amenities (such as our indoor pool, Hydromassage WaterWalls, indoor and outdoor whirlpools, steam rooms, saunas, Snow Room, Tyrolean Bucket Shower, Himalayan Salt Sauna, and a state-of-the-art 3,000 square foot CardioWeight Studio).

**Refresh & Rejuvenate at our**

## Award-Winning Poconos Spa

Feel your worldly burdens melt away as you refresh and rejuvenate at The Lodge at Woodloch, a destination spa in the Poconos with treatments and services customized to meet your unique wellness goals.

The Lodge at Woodloch is a sanctuary for the senses. Whether relaxing in the co-ed Whisper Lounge, enjoying the Aqua Garden's Hydromassage WaterWalls or activity pool with floor to ceiling windows surrounded by nature, you can choose to be as active or relaxed as you wish. Our luxury adults-only spa resort in the Poconos offers a unique selection of expert body treatments, massages, facials, and salon services designed to create heightened levels of relaxation and well-being.

**Artfully-Crafted, Locally Grown**

## Poconos Dining

Our spa cuisine features hand-picked ingredients from our on-property garden and local farms along with healthy cooking techniques that will delight…deliciously.

TREE Restaurant and Bar - elevated amongst the arbor canopy with glimpses of the glistening private lake - offers a diverse menu that is anything but the ordinary. With a focus on starting with only the best ingredients, the Chef team hand selects naturally raised meats, fish, and organic, locally grown vegetables. With three functioning gardens on property, we ensure the fresh flavors that only nature can create are the highlight at our Poconos restaurant.
