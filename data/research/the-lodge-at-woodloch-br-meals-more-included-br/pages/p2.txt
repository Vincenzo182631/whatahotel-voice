# ABOUT OUR POCONOS SPA RESORT

## The Lodge at Woodloch - a special Poconos resort with a meaningful purpose.

Since our beginnings, The Lodge, Destination Spa at Woodloch has remained committed to a number of traditions, but by far the most important one is the way we treat our guests - “it’s as if they are company in our own home”. This is what sets our Poconos spa resort apart from other places. We work to be more than just a place with accommodations and activities; we strive to be a place to stay in the Poconos where you feel like you're part of our family.

Our team members are the cornerstone of our success. Their dedication, genuine care, and commitment to excellence, day in and day out, are the essential ingredients guests at The Lodge at Woodloch comment on year after year!

_"From the 'welcome back' when you drive through the gates, to the bellmen, front desk, every server at the restaurant, and the spa team, you feel welcomed, valued, and like family. They never say no. You want something not on the menu? They make it happen. You want to stay the whole day after you check out? No problem."_

\- TripAdvisor Review

_"The staff at Woodloch is so friendly, efficient, and caring that you just feel like you are the only client the hotel has. They remember your names. I remember no one's name!"_

\- Trip Advisor Review
