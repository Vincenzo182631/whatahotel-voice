# Primiera Bar & Restaurant

On Via Veneto, within the elegance of the Baglioni Hotel Regina, a new address reinvents Italian and Roman culinary tradition. In Italian card games, the primiera is the winning hand: not a stroke of luck, but balance, harmony and precision.

## Simple, refined, made to be shared

Primiera is a project by the de Rosa family, with generations of experience in hospitality and restaurants, built around traditional cooking with unexpected pairings. Alongside these and the other dishes on the menu, a wine list of Italian, French and international labels.

Dinner Menu Lunch Menu

## Aperitivo and beyond, with a contemporary twist

At the bar, Roman and Italian classics shift shape again, becoming a snack menu of quicker, playful plates. Flavours meant for sharing, over a glass of wine or premium cocktails.
