# A stay with a new contemporary charm

The rooms and suites of the Baglioni Hotel Regina have been reimagined to welcome all the light of Rome and of Via Veneto: soft, pale tones on the walls, Rubelli curtains, furniture designed and made to measure by Molteni&C, the clean lines of the new lamps and fine natural materials come together in contemporary, comfortable interiors, with flexible spaces ideal for family stays. In the most elegant heart of the Eternal City, just a short walk from Villa Borghese.
