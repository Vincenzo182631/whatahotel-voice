## Spa Etiquette

- Aura Spa is open daily from 9:00 am to 8:00 pm. The Fitness Centre is open from 5:00 am to 11:00 pm.
- For guests of Baglioni Hotel Regina who have booked a Spa treatment, access to the wet area is included in their stay. For guests who have not booked a treatment, access is available at a daily rate of €35 per person. Advance booking is recommended.
- To ensure the best possible experience, we recommend booking your treatment at least 24 hours in advance. All prices are per person, and all 50- and 80-minute treatments may also be enjoyed as a couple.
- We kindly ask you to arrive 10–15 minutes before your scheduled treatment and to inform us of any health conditions, allergies or pregnancy at the time of booking.
