## Property Overview

In recognition of the kindness shown to Queen Margherita when she was in residence, the hotel's owner was granted permission to add "Regina" to the hotel name.

Situated on the famous Via Veneto, Baglioni Hotel Regina is in walking distance of the Borghese Gardens and Museum, the Spanish Steps, and Via Condotti, with its renowned shops. Housed in a magnificent, Liberty-style building, the hotel was once the home of Queen Margherita of Savoy. The charm and simplicity of the period façade combines with the refinement of the internal furnishings where the contemporary plays with Italian and Roman tradition. The stunning Roman Penthouse Suite, together with a large selection of Signature Suites, is the perfect choice for the most demanding traveler, offering exclusive benefits, such as butler service, a dedicated concierge, and limousine transfers. Primiera Restaurant blends unexpected flavor pairings, carefully crafted to be enjoyed in warm, fully renovated interiors with a contemporary feel. The Aura Spa is a metropolitan oasis where guests can experience an extensive range of beauty treatments.
