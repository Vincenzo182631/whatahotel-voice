## Step Inside Baglioni Hotel Regina

A witness to more than a century of Roman life, Baglioni Hotel Regina welcomes you to elegant, newly renovated rooms and suites — thoughtfully reimagined with refined tones, furnishings and materials, capturing the light of Rome while offering you exceptional comfort. And with the new restaurant Primiera, where the contemporary plays with Italian and Roman tradition.

## Rooms and Suites, a Renewed Splendour

More light and spaces redesigned around a broader idea of comfort — ideal for families too. Pale tones on the walls, furnishings designed to measure by Molteni&C, Rubelli curtains and new lamps with clean, essential lines elevate the comfort of every stay. Marble, alabaster, fine woods and boiserie compose contemporary interiors, in dialogue with the nineteenth-century proportions of the palazzo, the allure of Via Veneto and the enduring legend of la Dolce Vita.

## An Address We Are Proud Of

Our location is not precious only for its proximity to the gardens of Villa Borghese and the Galleria Borghese, Piazza di Spagna, Via dei Condotti and the Trevi Fountain. It is precious because this is where, among cafés open until dawn, film stars and the flashes of the paparazzi, an image of Italy was born — the one Fellini's La Dolce Vita made famous around the world. On those nights, the most glamorous in Rome, the Regina was here, just a short walk from the iconic Harry's Bar.
