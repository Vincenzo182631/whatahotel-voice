### Teotihuacán Pyramids

Discover the timeless wonders of Teotihuacán. Stroll the Avenue of the Dead connecting iconic landmarks—the Temple of Quetzalcoatl, Pyramid of the Sun, and Pyramid of the Moon—or admire its grandeur from above with a hot air balloon ride.

### Alameda Central

To absorb the beauty from this neighborhood you need to walk along it, to admire the old constructions, the wooden doors, the balconies, and how it blends with the daily movement of today. The Park was built in North America. Chestnut trees & willows come alive with water fountains, sculptures and benches to sit down and admire the culture.

### Chapultepec Castle

Discover Mexico history and culture at the emblematic castle, that once housed Mexican Emperor Maximilian I & Empress Carlota.

### MUNAL

With a permanent collection of Mexican art from XVI to XX, including pieces of José María Velasco & Saturnino Herrán. The building is also a marvel, the ancient “Palacio de Comunicaciones” design by Italian Silvio Contri, combining elements of neoclassic and renaissance.

### Angel of Independence

Walking along the City’s main avenue is an opportunity to explore its history. The Angel of Independence, built by Porfirio Diaz’s presidency, celebrates the centennial of the Independence.
