## Accommodations

The St. Regis Mexico City offers guests perfectly appointed rooms and suites offering privacy and distinctive style, ideal for relaxing and unwinding. Relax in an atmosphere created by warm grey tones, refined textiles, and plush carpeting. Oversized windows allow guests to marvel at the breathtaking Mexico City skyline and provide ample natural light. Cultivate your experience with Smart technology that includes a TBC system for master control of ambient lighting, air conditioning and an option for privacy. Each of our spacious rooms and Mexico City suites is both pet and family friendly. Anticipate exquisite comfort at our luxury hotel in Mexico City.
