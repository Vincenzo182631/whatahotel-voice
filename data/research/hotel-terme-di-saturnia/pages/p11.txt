## Holidays with your furry friends

At Terme di Saturnia Natural SPA & Golf Resort you can enjoy your wellness break or holidays and **share moments of relaxation and wellbeing with your beloved paw friends**. In the Resort, pets are VIPs: Very Important Pets! A **doggy basket and bowl** are available in room for naps and meals; an **exclusive “pet-friendly” area is set up on the lawn of the Spring**, with allocated spaces including **bed, blanket, and a water bowl** for the VIPs. You can bring your furry friend at the Bar Drink & Food Library and the Trattoria la Stellata, as well as take walks together along the pedestrian paths of the Golf course.

_It is recommended to communicate the presence of your VIPet when booking your stay._
