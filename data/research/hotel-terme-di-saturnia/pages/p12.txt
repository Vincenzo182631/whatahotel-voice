# THE CLUB OF TERME DI SATURNIA

## Your private ultimate SPA experience

Anytime you feel like escaping the world enter Il Club with your yearly membership or a day visit. Step into the serene Spa surroundings to be soothed by the flow of water and luxury wellbeing experiences. Rebalance your body and mind in the therapeutic Natural Hot Spring, and enjoy exclusive relaxing venues and benefits and access to our Spa, Golf and restaurants.

Make our Natural Spa therapies an intrinsic part of your ongoing preventative healthcare, it can transform your life. Immerse yourself in the rolling Tuscan hills playing our GEO-Certified golf course, the instructors and practice facilities are superb. Enjoy a day with friends, as Club member you can invite them with a special rate

The Club is open every day from 9.30 am to 7.00 pm.

Pets are not allowed.

_Due to limited availability of daily entrances, online booking is required_.

## BATHING UNDER THE STARS

It’s time to relax and be embraced by the natural enchantment of the Hot Spring at night. Every Friday evening, the magical experience of **Bathing under the Stars** is possible with **_NOTTURNO CLUB_: the single or couple evening entrance to the Club, to access the natural Hot Spring pool from 8.30 to 11.30 pm**.
