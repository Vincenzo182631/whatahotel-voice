# TERME DI SATURNIA NATURAL DESTINATION

## A haven for regeneration and relaxation

**Located in the heart of Tuscany** and less than 2 hours from Rome, **Terme di Saturnia is one of the best and renowned international spa destination**, where you get the chance to escape from the stress of daily life and immerse in a transformative experience luxury and relaxation.

A place of magnetic energy, ideal for those in search of psycho-physical balance, harmony between mind and body, health, and beauty, enhanced by the art of hospitality, expertise and passion of our Team, capable of transforming both a stay at the **5\* Resort** and a day visit into an authentic regenerating experience.

## ROOMS & SUITES

### A 5\* resort bathed in Saturnia's special light and energy.

Surrounded by rolling hills, vineyards, and olive groves, our Resort is the perfect location to escape from the stress of daily life and immerse in a world of radiant wellbeing. The hotel would be also the key to unlock your active wellbeing with plenty of complimentary facilities and daily activities for our Guests. Besides being at the center of Tuscan Maremma, a land that will spellbind with its charming villages, historical sites, cultural attractions, wineries, and local gastronomic excellences.

## A CULINARY JOURNEY

### Local tradition, innovation, prime quality, freshness.

Dine around to the rhythms of nature. A culinary experience that will delight your senses. The Resort features a fine-dining restaurant, a typical Tuscan “trattoria” and bar overlooking the Natural Spring: each showcasing a unique ambiance and a menu that celebrates the flavors of the region, the prime quality ingredients of Italy.

Our Executive Chef Salvatore Quarto aims at bringing straightforward flavours to the plate and to the palate therefore building memories and evoking emotions.

## GOLF

### Tee off into the wide beauty of the Maremma countryside.

You’ll be playing one of the few GEO-certified courses in Italy. Terme di Saturnia Golf is a championship, Par 72, 18-hole course, designed to merge with the surrounding hills, carefully conserving natural habitats. Professionals relish the many challenges; beginners soon discover the pleasures of golf in these inspirational surroundings.

# SATURNIA DAY SPA

### A day of relaxation and well-being immersed in regenerating thermal waters

Explore **SATURNIA DAY SPA**: with various DAY SPA formulas, you can access an experience of total regeneration through the ancient ritual of balneotherapy in the outdoor thermal pools, with hydro-massages and vascular paths.

Besides water, the primordial element of birth and rebirth, your organism will reconnect with its natural well-being through the beauty of the surrounding land; the uncontaminated air will expand your breath and mind; and the warmth of the sauna ritual will give you purified energy.
