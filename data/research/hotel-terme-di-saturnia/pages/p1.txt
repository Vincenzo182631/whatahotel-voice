# RESORT

## An icon of hospitality and well-being since 1919

Terme di Saturnia Resort is nestled amidst 120 hectares of unspoilt nature and includes a hotel with 124 rooms and suites, a pluri-awarded spa, a GEO-certified 18-hole golf course, and two restaurants.

At the centre of the experience, a millenary and immensely potent Hot Spring that feeds the natural pool from which spring water flows, all the thermal pools, whirlpools and vascular paths.

A thermal water unique in the world for its extraordinary antioxidant, myorelaxant and transformative power.

A haven of relaxation and well-being. A legendary thermal destination in the heart of the Tuscan Maremma.

# RESORT

## Dal 1919 un'icona di ospitalità e benessere

Il Resort di Terme di Saturnia è immerso in 120 ettari di natura incontaminata e comprende un hotel con 124 camere e suite, una pluripremiata spa, un golf 18 buche con certificazione GEO, ristoranti, bar.

Al centro dell’esperienza, una millenaria e potentissima Sorgente Termale che alimenta una vasca naturale da cui sgorga l’acqua sorgiva, tutte le piscine termali, idromassaggi e percorsi vascolari. Un’acqua termale unica al mondo per il suo straordinario potere antiossidante, miorilassante e trasformativo.

Un rifugio dal caos, dalla frenesia. Un abbraccio terapeutico della natura. Una leggendaria destinazione termale nel cuore della Maremma Toscana.

###### RELAXATION ROOMS

Indulge in the precious, regenerating silence: we have restored a typical Tuscan countryside Casale into an intimate, reserved, air-conditioned space for relaxation.
In the Hay Room you can breathe in the sweet woody fragrance of yellow hay, a natural myorelaxant. In the Cocoon Room there are soft circular beds where you can snuggle up wrapped in light curtains. The Silence Room allows you to enter a state of deep relaxation and meditation.

# GOING GREEN

### OUR COMMITMENT TO THE ENVIRONMENT

We have begun an articulated process of green regeneration and elimination of plastic: already many enclosures and consumables have been replaced, in preference to others made from recycled and environmentally sustainable materials.

In January 2023, 104 cypress trees were planted along the access path of Terme di Saturnia, one for each year of the Resort’s history, to make our contribution to the preservation of the environment and to counteract soil degradation.

Our golf course is GEO certified.

- The drainage system is connected to ponds for the recovery and recycling of rainwater.
- It has an internal purifier for recycling white water.
- Selected grass essences save 30% water consumption and are resistant to fungal diseases and weeds.
- No insecticides or pesticides are used. Fertilisers that do not pollute groundwater are selected and used. A weekly treatment with thermal water, thanks to the rich quantities of sulphur and other minerals, on the foliar part of the turf prevents or cures any disease.

### HOW TO REACH US

Our concierge is at your disposal to assist you in booking transfer service to/from airports or train stations.

By car – from the North

The A1 Milan-Bologna-Florence motorway, exit at Florence Impruneta. Then follow the Siena-Grosseto-Scansano-Montemerano-Saturnia superstrada. A1 Milan-Parma motorway, A 15 Parma-La Spezia, A 12 La Spezia-Livorno-Rosignano-Grosseto. From the Grosseto EST exit, follow the Scansano-Montemerano-Saturnia road.

By car - from the south

A12 Rome-Civitavecchia motorway, then the Aurelia SS1 main road to Montalto di Castro; at the junction of Vulci head towards Manciano-Saturnia.

By air - scheduled flights

“Leonardo da Vinci” airport – Fiumicino (RM), 150 km from Saturnia. “Galileo Galilei” airport, Pisa (PI), 220 km from Saturnia.

By air – private flights

Grosseto airport – helicopters can land here.

By train

- Grosseto station: 60 km from Saturnia
- Orbetello station: 55 km from Saturnia
- Montalto di Castro station: 50 km from Saturnia
- Capalbio station: 45 km from Saturnia
- Albinia station: 45 km from Saturnia
