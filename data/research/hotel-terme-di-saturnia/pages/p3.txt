# MEDICAL & SPA

## Since 1919, our values, your well-being

**The Heritage** \| Terme di Saturnia has been an exclusive Thermal Spa for well over 3,000 years. The Spa harnesses the miraculous properties of a Hot Spring, active for thousands of years and well-known by the Romans. Enriched by fossil clays and minerals, after a 40-year journey underground, the Hot Spring water emerges at a perfect temperature of 37.5°C, rich with the power and energy that make it one of Italy’s ultimate natural destinations, perfect for self-care and therapeutic treatments.

**The Method** \| The proficiency and expertise acquired in well over a century are today providing SPA treatments, which have garnered high levels of medical, technical, and aesthetic characteristics. Moreover, thanks to the hyper-fermentation found In Saturnia Bioplankton®, the biogenic treasure which matures in the thermal waters of Terme di Saturnia, Active Beauty, our exclusive cosmetic line was born.

### SPRING

Legend says that one day the god Saturn, angry and tired of the constant wars between men, hurled a thunderbolt at the Earth that generated a volcanic crater from which flowed a warm, magical water that brought calm and well-being to mankind. Today, that crater is the Source of the Resort.

### ROMAN BATH

A place dedicated to the care of the body and the spirit, this balanced space, for the exclusive use of Resort guests, offers an intense detoxification and purification experience between sauna and Turkish bath.

### Hot spring pools

This oasis extends over 20,000 square meters dedicated to well-being in the heart of Maremma: at the Terme di Saturnia, a place with millennial traditions of a hot springs spa and well-being. The various DAY SPA formulas allow access to the Hot Spring Pools for total regeneration experiences through the ancient ritual of balneotherapy, with whirlpools and the kneipp trails. In addition to water, the primordial element of birth and rebirth, the body will reconnect to its natural well-being through the beauty of the surrounding countryside, the breath and the mind will expand in the uncontaminated air, and the warmth of the sauna ritual will purify your energy.

### Casale

The senses will feed on relaxation indulging in the precious and regenerating silence of the rooms of the Casale. In the Hay Room you can breathe in the sweet woody fragrance of yellow hay, to dream of the sunny fields typical of the Maremma countryside. In the Cocoon Room there are soft circular beds where you can unwind wrapped in light curtains. The Silence Room allows you to enter a state of relaxation and meditation that lets your deepest thoughts emerge.
