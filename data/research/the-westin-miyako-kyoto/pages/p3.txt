## Global & Local Flavors

Upon renovation, one of our newly added restaurants in Higashiyama, Kyoto, includes RAQOU, the hotel’s smart-casual all day dining restaurant that overlooks the picturesque natural surroundings of Higashiyama and the city of Kyoto. The restaurant serves both Japanese and international favorites in a buffet style, along with a live kitchen.

See More

### Seasonal Recommendations - Afternoon Tea

Tea Lounge MAYFAIR offers afternoon tea menus that highlight a different theme each season. You can choose your favorite beverage from a selection of JING TEA and coffee.
Please enjoy a moment of elegance with the distinguished service of a hotel lounge.

See More

### Beautiful Morning - Breakfast

All Day Dining RAQOU is undoubtfully most beautiful in the morning with the pleasant and bright light filling the room through the floor-to-ceiling windows. A vast array of high-quality Japanese and Western cuisine is offered, and you also can enjoy Westin Eat Well Menu, a selection of nutritious low-calorie dishes, handcrafted by our Chef with guests’ well-being in mind.

See More

### Special Day - Dominique Bouchet Kyoto

This French restaurant in Higashiyama, Kyoto, serves the classics of modern French cuisine and the exquisitely flavorful sauces created by Michelin-Starred French chef Dominique Bouchet. The fine-dining restaurant pays tribute to the rich cultural heritage of Japan as well as the distinctive beauty of each of Kyoto’s four seasons.
