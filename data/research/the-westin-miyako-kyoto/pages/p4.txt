Beside Nature

## Wild Bird Sanctuary & Trail

Guests who enjoy bird watching can wander the trail near our Kyoto spa hotel, which ascends toward the Ohyakuinari Shrine and Jizo relics, and enjoy the peace and tranquility of a serene green forest that is a natural habitat for over 50 species of wild birds.

Spa Experience

### Kyoto Keage Onsen

In the South Wing of our luxury Kyoto spa hotel near Higashiyama, a luxurious, newly completed day spa complex with extraordinary views invites guests to experience the restorative essence of the Westin “Feel Well” ethos and discover complete well-being and relaxation. The complex features semi-outdoor traditional Japanese onsen or natural underground hot spring baths, framed by peaceful seasonal views of the gardens leading up to Mount Kacho, as well as the sounds of waterfalls and birdsong.

### Signature Treatment

This is an energizing treatment to gently awaken the senses at the beginning of a new day. Our Kyoto spa therapist will select a personally tailored combination of relaxation and healing therapies, such as massage and meditation, designed to steadily increase your latent energy and concentration levels.

### Awaken Spa Ritual

Our half-day program of gentle spiritual and physical activation together with mental stimulation will restore your energy balance and leave you full of vigor and vitality throughout the day. Recommended for people who tire easily, or tend to slow down later in the day, or are prone to illness.

### Sunset Spa Ritual

The program starts with a post-lunch therapeutic stroll through the forest, followed by more intense forms of physical stimulation to heighten the senses, refresh the muscles and relax the mind. As evening begins to fall, calmly bring your mind and body into alignment and enjoy a full body treatment that gives the day a sense of closure.

## A Tranquil Oasis

Envelop yourself in soothing, healing aromas while the long, gentle massaging strokes relieve your fatigue and stimulate your senses, providing restorative benefits to the autonomic nervous system that lay the foundations for a deep and relaxing sleep. This also serves as the perfect digital detox program to wipe away the mental stresses associated with mobile phones and computers that can cause eyestrain and impact on quality of sleep.
