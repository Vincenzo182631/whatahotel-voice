## Panoptis Escape Restaurant

From farm-to table- Mediterranean salads to melt-in-the-mouth fresh fish, the dining experience at Panoptis Escape is an immersive journey celebrating nature’s finest offerings. Locally sourced ingredients shine in Mediterranean dishes with a contemporary twist, enjoyed against sweeping sea views.

## Camares Greek Traditional

This poolside restaurant at the Royal Myconian reimagines the taverna with sophistication, blending a festive atmosphere with live music and a modern take on Greek traditions, including lively plate-smashing nights—all while offering a dining experience that’s both relaxed and refined.

## Sishu Sushi Bar

While enjoying the gentle breeze that caresses this open-air bar with breathtaking views across the bay, savour the art of sushi that is honoured here with an array of traditional classics, as well as imaginative fusion dishes prepared from the freshest catch hauled in at first light.

## Celebrities Mediterranean Seafood

Celebrating the region’s vibrant flavours and subtle ingredients, a menu of Mediterranean cuisine includes signature dishes like sea bass with black risotto and Aegean lobster, while vegetarian options and elegant presentation complete the sophisticated dining experience.

## Cabbanes Greek Traditional Elevated

A culinary adventure through the Mediterranean, inspired by Italy and France, yet ultimately a declaration of love for Greek cuisine—complemented by rare Greek vintages, international wines, and breathtaking views across the moonlit Aegean.

## Pavilion Fine Dining

Presenting a Relais & Châteaux fine dining experience at Utopia with sea views under the Mykonian night sky, a refined menu crafted from seasonal ingredients is paired with an exceptional wine list, creating an unforgettable experience in a sophisticated setting.

## Panorama Mediterranean Cuisine

Indulge in imaginative interpretations of authentic Italian cuisine or elaborate Mediterranean gourmet creations crafted from local ingredients while overlooking the impressive pool and Aegean Sea. As dusk settles, Avaton’s illuminated poolside setting enhances this elegant, flavour-filled dining experience.
