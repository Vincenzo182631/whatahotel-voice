## THE POOL TERRACE

Bask in golden Mykonos sunshine and feel the balmy sea breeze caress your skin as you enjoy the sublime serenity and sweeping views of our pool terrace. Luxe umbrella-shaded sunbeds are perfect for relaxing between refreshing dips in our stunning infinity pool, and the Pool Club bar will keep you going with a fantastic selection of drinks and light bites throughout the day. After dark, the pool terrace transforms into a nightlife hotspot, with artful lighting design making the pool sparkle as if filled with stars and the Pool Club shining with a sophisticated bar culture.

> Escape into the sublime and indulge in sun-kissed relaxation – by the pool or at our private spot on Elia Beach – with luxury sunbeds, umbrellas for shade, and a fab selection of drinks and snacks from our bars.

## ELIA BEACH

Feel the warm sand between your toes and let the sound of the surf lift your spirit at our private spot on Elia Beach. Thoughtfully arranged for guests of the Myconian Collection, this beautiful stretch of golden sand sets the scene for relaxed seaside moments, with comfortable sunbeds and umbrellas, fresh beach towels, and a bar serving chilled drinks, fresh smoothies and light snacks. Access to the beach is arranged through the reception shortly before arrival, ensuring a smooth and well-paced experience, and is available at a nominal charge, inclusive of selected amenities, while guests of the Grand Majestic Villa (two- and five-bedroom) enjoy complimentary use. Elia also offers a variety of leisure opportunities, from water sports to nearby bars and restaurants, while a complimentary shuttle service to and from Utopia makes every visit effortlessly enjoyable.

\*Complimentary use applies to guests of Panoptis Escape, the Apocalypsis Villa at Myconian Avaton, as well as the Agios Sostis and Super Paradise Villas within the Myconian Villa Collection.
