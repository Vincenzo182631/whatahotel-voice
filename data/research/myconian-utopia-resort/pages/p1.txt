## Brand Background

Myconian Collection is a premier, family-owned hotel group with a prominent and historic presence in Mykonos, Greece. Rooted deeply in local legacy, the group features a curated portfolio of 15 distinct five-star luxury hotels and resorts spread across the island’s most coveted destinations. Their mission is to provide an unparalleled standard of authentic Greek hospitality merged with world-class luxury, offering exceptional accommodation options, gastronomic excellence, state-of-the-art wellness facilities, and spectacular venues for unforgettable private events.
