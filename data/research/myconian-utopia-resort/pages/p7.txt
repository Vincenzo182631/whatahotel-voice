On the southeast coast, Platis Gialos boasts golden sands and crystal clear waters, perfect for swimming and water sports. Surrounded by luxury hotels, seaside tavernas, and vibrant beach bars, it's a lively hub with easy access by boat or on foot to nearby beaches like Psarou, Agia Anna and Paranga, Paradise, and Super Paradise.

## Ambassador

Presiding elegantly over Platis Gialos, the Ambassador offers timeless elegance with a tranquil pool deck, superb fine dining, serene spa facilities that include Thalassotherapy pools, and views that capture the essence of Mykonos.
