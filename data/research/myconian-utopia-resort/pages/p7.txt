YACHT DAY TRIPS

## WONDERS, ADVENTURES AND ROMANCE

Enjoy a day at sea and discover a whole new way of falling in love with Mykonos. Beach hop along the south coast’s famed cosmopolitan beaches or sail to remote hidden coves to swim in utter privacy. Sail to Delos for a private tour of its extraordinary UNESCO World Heritage Site, and maybe stop to snorkel in the crystal clear waters of nearby Rhenia. Sip cocktails as you sail into the sunset. The surf’s the limit when it comes to customising your trip, and our concierge team can help you create the perfect bespoke experience.

Choose from a selection of luxury power boats and yachts. Jet skis and other exciting options are available upon request.

BESPOKE CHARTER GETAWAYS

## SAIL INTO YOUR SUMMER OF LUXURY

Embark on a bespoke getaway and immerse yourself in the glamour of the yachting lifestyle. From romantic weekends away sipping champagne under the full moon to island-hopping around the Cyclades over several days, our luxury yacht charters are an ideal way to experience the true spirit of the Aegean Sea and discover top island destinations in the Cyclades, including Folegandros, Naxos, Paros, Syros, Tinos, and the magical Santorini. Yachts come fully crewed and cruises can be complemented with a range of activities and experiences. Our concierge team is at hand to help tailor your itinerary to your needs and requirements.

Choose from a selection of luxury power boats and yachts. Jet skis and other exciting options are available upon request.

HELICOPTER TOURS

## TAKE TO THE SKIES

Soar above Mykonos and enjoy a bird’s eye view of the island with a helicopter tour. Take in the stunning views of sunswept hills dotted with picturesque sugarcube houses and marvel at the glimmering expanse of the Aegean Sea, its blue hues vibrant as ever under the summer sun. From Mykonos, fly west towards the sacred island of Delos and then out over the open sea to enjoy a unique and exclusive perspective of the Cycladic islands of Naxos, Paros, and Tinos. The duration of each flight is approximately 60’.

PRIVATE COOKING CLASS

## GET HANDS ON WITH THE FLAVOURS OF MEDITERRANEAN SUMMERS

Discover the secrets at the heart of the Myconian Collection’s culinary culture with this exclusive cooking class and market visit experience. Join Executive Chef Nikos Moroglou on his morning visits to the local market and our exclusive suppliers and experience the process of selecting the freshest organic vegetables and herbs and the best of the day’s catch, straight off the boats. The experience resumes again later in the afternoon, with a step-by-step private cooking class where you’ll learn how to transform those fresh ingredients into a delicious Greek meal that you’ll then enjoy with a complimentary Greek vintage specially selected by our sommelier to complement your creation.

MYCONIAN GASTRONOMY

## SAVOUR THE GOOD LIFE

Celebrate the good life and the island’s culinary heritage with a delicious journey through Greek gastronomy at the Pavilion Relais & Châteaux restaurant. Breathtaking views of Elia Beach and the Aegean Sea set the scene for a sublime Mykonos fine dining experience that celebrates the culinary heritage of Greece and the Cyclades. Crafted with an emphasis on the unique flavours of seasonal and local ingredients, each locally inspired dish tells a story of Greek heritage, inviting you to explore a world of authentic Greek flavours that are perfectly complemented by our sommelier’s masterful pairings from our selection of local and international vintages.

SPA TREATMENTS

## RELAX, INDULGE AND PAMPER YOURSELF

Unwind and indulge with our curated selection of luxurious Mykonos spa experiences. Immerse yourself in serenity and relaxation at Utopia’s Sanctuary Spa and spas across the Myconian Collection, and discover an exquisite range of treatments, rituals, and experiences designed to pamper and delight: relaxing massages, soothing thalassotherapy, cutting edge spa treatments, and beauty rituals that leave you feeling positively radiant. Designed to work in natural synergy with the body and mind for luxuriously relaxing and rejuvenating results, our spa treatments and experiences are carried out by attentive and experienced spa specialists, using an exclusive selection of products from leading international skincare lines.

WELLNESS ACTIVITIES

## BALANCE BODY AND MIND

Balance body, mind, and spirit with our selection of wellness activities. Maintain your fitness routine with a visit to our gym, enjoy a workout session in the sunshine with a private trainer, or book a private Pilates session with one of our qualified instructors. Mykonos is a great place to practise mindfulness and reconnect with yourself, and we offer a range of options for you to elevate your sense of wellbeing, including wellness coaching, yoga classes for all levels, and exclusive Mykonos yoga retreats. Private yoga sessions allow you to make the most of the island’s outstanding natural beauty, while exclusive sunset sessions on the uninhabited island of Rhenia and full-moon yoga and meditation experiences on the sacred island of Delos are ideal ways to enjoy a truly unforgettable wellness experience.

DIVING AND SNORKELLING

## DISCOVER AN UNDERWATER WONDERLAND

Discover a different side of Mykonos with a day of snorkelling or scuba diving. The island’s crystal clear waters, sheltered bays and warm sun create ideal conditions for exploring its underwater wonderland. Visibility is fantastic and the island’s vibrant reefs are teeming with a rich variety of marine life, while two shipwrecks provide fantastic opportunities to mix things up. There are plenty of sites to choose from depending on weather conditions, your preferences, and your level of experience, while advanced divers can also opt for night diving, enriched air (nitrox) and deep diving, adventure dives, and other speciality experiences.

Water sports

## READY, SET, GET SPLASHING!

Spend more time in the water, have fun in the sun, and fuel your sense of adventure with our fantastic selection of water activities and exhilarating water sports. With a variety of beaches that enjoy different wind and water conditions on any given day, Mykonos is a veritable playground for all kinds of water sports. Choose SUP or canoeing to take in the great views of the coast, go oldschool with windsurfing or water skiing, and get that rush of adrenaline with jet skiing, flyboarding, or wakeboarding. Tube, crazy sofa, and banana boat rides – or even a glass-bottom boat cruise – are great ways to share the fun with friends!

HORSE RIDING

## EXPLORE THE ISLAND ON HORSEBACK

Slow down and connect with the island’s more traditional rhythms with an unforgettable horse riding experience. Taking your experience and preferences into consideration, you’ll be matched with a suitable horse to ensure a pleasant and safe ride under the supervision of a professional riding instructor. From beginners’ level introductory lessons and gentle walks through the fields to lively canters along the beach, you’ll enjoy a wonderful horseback experience specially tailored to you.

PHOTO TOUR

## CAPTURE THE MAGIC OF MYKONOS

Explore, experience and capture the magic of Mykonos with a private photo tour of our beautiful island. Matched with a local professional photographer, you’ll visit some of the best scenic locations and top attractions in Mykonos and enjoy the opportunity to learn useful tips and tricks to elevate your photo game. You can be the star of the shoot, going home with a beautiful set of professional shots of yourself in Mykonos, or step behind the camera and put your new skills to use capturing the island’s magic – and making magical memories in the process.

Enjoy our curated selection of some of the best things to do in Mykonos. Sail around the island, uncover the secrets of the ancient world, explore the Cyclades, and discover the many thrilling ways to enjoy the island’s crystal clear waters.
