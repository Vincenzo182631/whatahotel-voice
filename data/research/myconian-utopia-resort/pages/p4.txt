Agrari Bay, nestled on Mykonos’s serene southern coast, offers a tranquil escape
with its crystal-clear waters sheltered from the breeze, golden sands, and rugged natural beauty. Less crowded than neighbouring beaches, it’s a peaceful haven for relaxation, embraced by gentle hills and the shimmering Aegean - perfect for those seeking an exclusive, unspoiled retreat.

## Sunrise

Celebrating the luxury of simple pleasures luxury in one of the most idyllic settings on Mykonos, Sunrise sits with its feet in the sands of Agrari, exuding barefoot luxury around a charming pool deck, a taverna under the trees, and an exclusive beach club.
