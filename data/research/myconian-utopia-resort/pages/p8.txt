- Ornos Beach

## O Restaurant

Embark on a culinary journey with a creative fusion of Mediterranean classics, crafted from seasonal produce and the day’s freshest catch, ‘its head on the plate and tail in the sea,’ complemented by a refined selection of international wines.

- Ornos Beach

## Kove

This rooftop restaurant looking out over Ornos redefines “Greekology”, using local and organic ingredients to bring to life the traditions of Greek and Cycladic cuisine, blending centuries-old knowledge with contemporary culinary trends into an unparalleled dining experience.

- Agrari Beach

## Almiriki

Over long lunches that segue into late dinners, experience Greek taverna culture at its best, seated in the shade of a pine grove with your feet in the sand, sharing mezzes and Mediterranean specialties crafted from sun-ripened local produce.

- Elia Beach

## Panoptis Escape Restaurant

From farm-to table- Mediterranean salads to melt-in-the-mouth fresh fish, the dining experience at Panoptis Escape is an immersive journey celebrating nature’s finest offerings. Locally sourced ingredients shine in Mediterranean dishes with a contemporary twist, enjoyed against sweeping sea views.

- Elia Beach

## Camares Greek Traditional

This poolside restaurant at the Royal Myconian reimagines the taverna with sophistication, blending a festive atmosphere with live music and a modern take on Greek traditions, including lively plate-smashing nights—all while offering a dining experience that’s both relaxed and refined.

- Mykonos Town

## Epico Mykonos

Set on the scenic deck at Myconian Deos, Epico blends 1960s-inspired glamour with Mediterranean fine dining. Elegant stone-clad interiors flow onto a terrace with panoramic views of Mykonos Town and the Aegean, creating an unforgettable atmosphere of relaxed sophistication.

- Elia Beach

## Sishu Sushi Bar

While enjoying the gentle breeze that caresses this open-air bar with breathtaking views across the bay, savour the art of sushi that is honoured here with an array of traditional classics, as well as imaginative fusion dishes prepared from the freshest catch hauled in at first light.

- Elia Beach

## Celebrities Mediterranean Seafood

Celebrating the region’s vibrant flavours and subtle ingredients, a menu of Mediterranean cuisine includes signature dishes like sea bass with black risotto and Aegean lobster, while vegetarian options and elegant presentation complete the sophisticated dining experience.

- Elia Beach

## Cabbanes Greek Traditional Elevated

A culinary adventure through the Mediterranean, inspired by Italy and France, yet ultimately a declaration of love for Greek cuisine—complemented by rare Greek vintages, international wines, and breathtaking views across the moonlit Aegean.

- Elia Beach

## Pavilion Fine Dining

Presenting a Relais & Châteaux fine dining experience at Utopia with sea views under the Mykonian night sky, a refined menu crafted from seasonal ingredients is paired with an exceptional wine list, creating an unforgettable experience in a sophisticated setting.

- Mykonos Town

## Noa Traditional Greek Cuisine

Farm-to-table ingredients shine in creative dishes that redefine Mediterranean haute cuisine with a blend of traditional flavours and contemporary techniques, served in the elegant setting of Kyma, with full-height windows framing striking views of Mykonos Town and the Aegean

- Mykonos Town

## Kinjo Japanese Restobar

In a refined setting overlooking Mykonos Town, this Japanese Restobar delivers a sensory dining experience that fuses Japanese precision with both Greek and Japanese ingredients, offering an exquisite array of delicacies with meticulous craftsmanship and superior service.

- Mykonos Town

## Baos Fine Dining

Exceptional fine dining on Mykonos with views over the iconic windmills of Little Venice at Korali Relais & Châteaux, transforming regional flavours with innovative techniques – including molecular gastronomy and fusion cuisine – to celebrate contemporary Mediterranean cuisine

- Elia Beach

## Panorama Mediterranean Cuisine

Indulge in imaginative interpretations of authentic Italian cuisine or elaborate Mediterranean gourmet creations crafted from local ingredients while overlooking the impressive pool and Aegean Sea. As dusk settles, Avaton’s illuminated poolside setting enhances this elegant, flavour-filled dining experience.

- Platis Gialos

## Efisia Fine Dining

As one of the top tables on the island, this Relais & Châteaux culinary experience offers a gourmet menu in a luxurious ambiance by candlelight, sheltered from the evening breeze in a stone alcove under the stars.
