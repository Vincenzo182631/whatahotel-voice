## Epico Mykonos

Set on the scenic deck at Myconian Deos, Epico blends 1960s-inspired glamour with Mediterranean fine dining. Elegant stone-clad interiors flow onto a terrace with panoramic views of Mykonos Town and the Aegean, creating an unforgettable atmosphere of relaxed sophistication.

## Noa Traditional Greek Cuisine

Farm-to-table ingredients shine in creative dishes that redefine Mediterranean haute cuisine with a blend of traditional flavours and contemporary techniques, served in the elegant setting of Kyma, with full-height windows framing striking views of Mykonos Town and the Aegean

## Kinjo Japanese Restobar

In a refined setting overlooking Mykonos Town, this Japanese Restobar delivers a sensory dining experience that fuses Japanese precision with both Greek and Japanese ingredients, offering an exquisite array of delicacies with meticulous craftsmanship and superior service.

## Baos Fine Dining

Exceptional fine dining on Mykonos with views over the iconic windmills of Little Venice at Korali Relais & Châteaux, transforming regional flavours with innovative techniques – including molecular gastronomy and fusion cuisine – to celebrate contemporary Mediterranean cuisine
