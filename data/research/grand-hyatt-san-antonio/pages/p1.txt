## Explore San Antonio’s Historic River Walk from Our Downtown Hotel

Grand Hyatt San Antonio River Walk delivers a modern hotel experience rooted in the city’s rich heritage, perfectly positioned between the world-famous River Walk and the Henry B. González Convention Center. Enjoy refined accommodations in the heart of downtown with effortless access to the Alamodome and Frost Bank Center for concerts and major events. Enhance your stay with our elevated Club Lounge, explore San Antonio’s newest dining scene, and unwind at our expansive fitness and recreation deck featuring a lap pool, sun deck, and seasonal pool bar.

## ROOMS & SUITES

After exploring the city’s many attractions, Grand Hyatt San Antonio River Walk offers an upscale—and updated—retreat, with rich décor and modern Southwestern accents for an authentic ambience. Our family-friendly luxury hotel suites offer separate living areas and city views of downtown San Antonio.

### Pets Are Welcome

Grand Hyatt San Antonio River Walk is a dog-friendly hotel. The availability of dog-friendly rooms is limited, and a minimum of 48 hours reservation notice is required by calling +1 210 224 1234. Dog tags are required. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

#### Pet Fees

- Up to 5 nights: **$100** _/ STAY_

- More than 5 nights: **$200** _/ STAY_

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  50Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 75Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.

### Destination Fee

A daily destination fee of $30.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.
