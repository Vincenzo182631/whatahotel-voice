## An Extraordinary Suite Designed for Grand Living

Discover an exceptional retreat where expansive spaces create a sense of refined residential living. Thoughtfully designed for both private moments and gracious entertaining, the Presidential Suite offers an impressive setting for discerning guests seeking comfort, sophistication and a truly distinguished stay in Kuala Lumpur.

### Studio Suite

Elevate your stay in the Studio Suite, featuring a spacious bedroom paired with a dedicated dining area. Designed for sophisticated living, this suite blends comfort and style, making it ideal for both business and leisure travelers who appreciate a thoughtfully curated environment for relaxation, work, and entertaining.

### Junior Suite

The Junior Suite offers a refined, spacious retreat designed for the design-conscious traveler. Featuring a unique layout, plush bedding, and a cozy daybed, it balances elegance and functionality. Perfect for unwinding, the suite creates a serene haven where comfort meets thoughtful design in the heart of Kuala Lumpur.

### One-Bedroom Suite

Relax in the comfort of the One-Bedroom Suite, thoughtfully designed with plush bedding, a separate living room, and a dedicated work area. Combining privacy and elegance, it offers a versatile space for business, leisure, or quiet reflection, ensuring an elevated and memorable stay in the heart of the city.

### Two-Bedroom Suite

Perfect for families or groups, the Two-Bedroom Suite features two bedrooms—King and Twin—each with ensuite bathrooms, plus a spacious living area. Combining comfort, privacy, and style, this suite provides a serene home-away-from-home, allowing guests to relax, connect, and enjoy quality time together in the city center.

### VIP Suite

The VIP Suite is tailored for business travelers seeking comfort and elegance. Featuring a separate living room, dining room, and work area, the suite’s bedroom is a cocoon of relaxation. Entertain guests or unwind in style with plush seating, contemporary design, and thoughtful amenities that elevate every moment of your stay.

### Chairman Suite

The Chairman Suite exudes classic elegance, featuring a luxurious bedroom with spacious ensuite, a grand living room, and private dining area. Ideal for hosting or relaxing, the suite offers ample seating, stylish décor, and thoughtful layouts, providing a refined sanctuary that elevates your experience in Bukit Bintang.
