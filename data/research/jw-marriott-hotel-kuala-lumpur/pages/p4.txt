# Curated Restaurant in Kuala Lumpur

## Dining

Centred in the Home of the Tastemakers, The Starhill Dining celebrates culinary sophistication at its most elegant with a diverse spread of gastronomic creations, bringing to diners fresh, seasonal and globally inspired dining experiences served by our collection of captivating dining and entertainment concepts.

### Private Events at the Starhill Dining

The Starhill Dining houses the city’s most sought- after private event spaces with three exceptional venues designed to cater for events of all sizes. Connect and entertain with custom-crafted events to delight any palate. Let our Tastemakers collaborate with you for unique event ideas and a catering experience sure to impress.

### Private Dining Room

From intimate events to lavish affairs; no matter the occasion, the Private Dining Room, a private space that can accommodate up to 30 persons, will cater to your event, all in its dynamic location.

### Shook! 118

Shook! 118 sets the stage for an unforgettable event, be it intimate gatherings, product launches or company dinners; and can be separated from the rest of the restaurant with a capacity for 206 seated.

### Club Chinois

Club Chinois brings together an event venue in the heart of Bukit Bintang with a touch of Chinese heritage. This private space can be adapted for enchanting celebrations and lavish parties, accommodating up to 60 persons.

## A Shanghainese Culinary Symphony

A celebrated restaurant in Kuala Lumpur’s Chinese fine dining scene for two decades now, Shanghai restaurant is evocative of its namesake region through its décor of modern Chinese glamour, paired with decadent Shanghainese dishes.  Discover authentic Shanghainese cuisine and artistry, elevated with an exquisite selection of quality Chinese wines and teas.  A refined dining experience awaits.
