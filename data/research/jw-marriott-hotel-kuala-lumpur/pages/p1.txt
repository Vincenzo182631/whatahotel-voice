# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur

## Enjoy comfort at our luxury hotel in Kuala Lumpur

JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur offers enriching experiences in the heart of the city. Located on Jalan Bukit Bintang in Kuala Lumpur’s central business and entertainment district, this luxury hotel features 738 rooms designed for comfort and style, creating space to focus on mind, body, and spirit. Known as The Happening Hotel, it offers poolside rooms and spacious suites, plus 18 versatile venues...See More for meetings, conferences, and social events. Savor innovative dining at Shook! or traditional flavors at Luk Yu Tea House. Recharge with a swim in the pool, a workout in the fitness center, or a soothing visit to the sauna. Ideal for business and leisure, this family-friendly hotel in Kuala Lumpur city center offers a memorable stay. Reserve your experience at JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur and discover Bukit Bintang’s vibrant energy. JW Marriott Kuala Lumpur is operated by Star Hill Hotel Sdn Bhd (Company No: 199501034724 (363926-V)).See Less

At The Starhill – Home of the Tastemakers, modern luxury unfolds in an intimate, thoughtfully curated setting. Discover world-renowned maisons such as Louis Vuitton and Rolex alongside Asia’s first Balmain boutique, complemented by refined international and Asian dining. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur, with rates of RM10.00 per day, and RM0.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur is Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL). KUL is located approximately 70.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Kuala Lumpur has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
