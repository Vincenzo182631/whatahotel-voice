### Luxury, Warm Hospitality and a Wonderful Family Ex

a day ago

We had a truly wonderful family vacation at InterContinental Bali Resort. The property is beautiful, peaceful and extremely well maintained. Luxury seems to ooze from every corner of the resort, yet what impressed us most was the warmth and friendliness of the people.

From the Front Desk team who welcomed us and were always accommodating, to the Housekeeping team who kept our room absolutely immaculate, the service throughout our stay was exceptional. Everyone we interacted with was friendly, attentive and genuinely seemed committed to making our family feel comfortable.

The dining experience deserves a special mention. We thoroughly enjoyed Chowk for Indian cuisine and Bella Cucina for Italian, with Bella Cucina offering handmade pastas and classic Italian dishes in an elegant yet relaxed setting. Bella Cucina is described by the resort as refined yet welcoming, which certainly matched our experience.

Sunset Beach Bar & Grill was another highlight. Sitting by Jimbaran Bay, enjoying great food and drinks with the ocean in front of you and the sun setting is a wonderful experience. The beachfront restaurant is particularly known for its open-air setting and spectacular sunset views across Jimbaran Bay.

The variety and quality of food across the resort were excellent, making it easy for a family with different preferences to find something to enjoy. The resort itself offers a broad mix of Indonesian, Balinese, Mediterranean, Japanese and international cuisine across its venues.

What really makes this resort special, however, is the combination of luxury and genuine Balinese hospitality. It never feels pretentious. It is quiet and relaxing, while still being very family-friendly and accommodating.

We left with some wonderful family memories and would highly recommend InterContinental Bali Resort for families looking for a luxurious, peaceful and memorable Bali vacation.

We would happily return.
