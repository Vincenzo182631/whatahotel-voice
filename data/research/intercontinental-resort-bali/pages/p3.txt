## KO Restaurant

Our signature Japanese restaurant, award winning KO offers a stylish dining experience infusing Balinese architecture with Japanese gardens and aesthetics. Serving dinner only, KO features three distinct dining concepts, Teppanyaki, an interactive experience where chefs prepare grilled Japanese specialties, The Restaurant for fresh sushi, sashimi and tempura, and The Cocktail Lounge featuring an extensive menu of sake based cocktails, Japanese beers, and lighter Japanese fare.

## Bella Cucina

Dine al fresco under the stars, next to tropical gardens illuminated with lanterns, or reserve the private indoor glass conservatory with chandeliers and gold plated cutlery. Bella Cucina, our signature restaurant, offers a Mediterranean seasonal menu of delicious and lively open kitchen concepts. Two, three, and four course tasting menus are featured. Start with the Mushroom and Truffle Veloute, Herb Crusted Lamb Loin creamy polenta, edamame, confit shallot, kalamata olive juice, and Tiramisu.

## Sunset Beach Bar & Grill

Escape amidst the swaying coconut palms and white sandy beaches of Jimbaran Bay to savor drinks, dine, and witness breathtaking crimson streaked sunsets. Our newly revamped Beach Bar and Grill offers delectable menus, including fresh salads, succulent Ribeye grilled meats, and fish such as Mahi Mahi and Barramundi. You can also enjoy signature cocktails and be entertained by live music performances from Monday to Saturday. On Sundays, groove to the beats of a live DJ.

## Taman Gita

A pendulous bamboo lighting installation reminiscent of a set of Balinese wind chimes sets the stage for breakfast at Taman Gita. Settle into sleek banquettes and alcoves adorned with Balinese artefacts, before indulging in the extensive international breakfast buffet. Take time to peruse the storyboard wall display of musicians playing gamelan instruments, a tribute to the restaurant's namesake, Taman Gita, which means "heavenly garden of music".
