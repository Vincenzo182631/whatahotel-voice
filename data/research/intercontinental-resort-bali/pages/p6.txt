### Club InterContinental Lounge

Enter a world of understated elegance at Club InterContinental, where privacy, exclusivity and intuitive service define the art of refined living. From tranquil moments in secluded surroundings to thoughtfully curated indulgences, every experience unfolds with effortless sophistication, creating a sanctuary of timeless luxury in Bali.

Learn more

### Convenient beachfront location

Nestled within the exclusive Jimbaran Bay on Bali’s southern coast, the resort is conveniently located near local attractions as well as the island's famous temples and destinations. It’s also only a 15-minute drive from Ngurah Rai International Airport, ensuring easy access to and from the resort.

Learn more

### Premium Rooms, Suites & Elegant Villas

Boasts a luxurious collection of 425 beautifully appointed rooms, suites, and private villas, distributed across six distinct wings that elegantly blend modern convenience with traditional Balinese architectural elements.

Learn more

### Take a culinary journey

Indonesian, local Balinese, Mediterranean, and Japanese are part of the InterContinental Bali Resort dining experience. Choose a four-course tasting menu at Bella Cucina, or opt for regional Balinese specialties at Jimbaran Gardens.

Learn more

### Elevate your meeting and event

Located in Bali’s upmarket resort destination of Jimbaran Bay, InterContinental Bali Resort provides the perfect balance between business and leisure. The beachfront resort boasts extensive facilities and modern audio-visual technologies in authentic Balinese-style pavilion buildings within its own dedicated wing.

## FAQs for InterContinental Bali Resort

Does InterContinental Bali Resort have a pool?

InterContinental Bali Resort has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Treat yourself to a refreshing, decadent pool each day. Choose between our stunning Nirvana pool, the largest, overlooking Jimbaran Bay. Or, opt for the Balinese Bath, Fountain, ‘Fun’ (family) or Club pool, exclusive to InterContinental Club guests.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Bali Resort?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Bali Resort for hotel guests.

Resort Parking Area is available for the Resort guest only.

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Bali Resort?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Bali Resort.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Bali Resort for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Bali Resort have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Bali Resort has 5 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Bali Resort?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Bali Resort.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Bali Resort?

Check-in at InterContinental Bali Resort is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Bali Resort offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Bali Resort offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Bali Resort from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Bali Resort is conveniently located near Ngurah Rai Airport (Denpasar) (DPS), approximately 4.35 miles away (7 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Bali Resort?

InterContinental Bali Resort is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Padang Padang Beach - 7.58 miles away (12.2 kilometers)
- Bingin Beach - 7.77 miles away (12.5 kilometers)
- Balangan Beach - 5.78 miles away (9.3 kilometers)
- Jimbaran Traditional Market
- Jenggala Ceramic - 0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)
- Jimbaran Beach - 1.86 miles away (3 kilometers)
- Kecak Dance, Uluwatu Temple
- Ubud
- Uluwatu Temple
- Garuda Wisnu Kencana - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- Bukit Peninsula - 3.73 miles away (6 kilometers)
- Jimbaran Bay
- Visit Bali’s Sacred Temples
- Step onto the White Sands of Bukit Peninsula
- Explore Ubud, Bali’s Cultural Centre

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Bali Resort puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
