# Exclusive Experiences

## _Beach Village_ Exclusive Experiences

A private enclave within the legendary Hotel del Coronado, your stay at Beach Village includes a personal concierge to take care of every detail, with exclusive experiences and amenities at every turn. Discover new flavors, embark on new adventures, explore hidden talents, or savor a perfect moment of peace.

Your Beach Village concierge is available at 619.522.8809 or Concierge\_BeachVillage@Hilton.com.

Reserve A Cabana

### Secluded Pools

Find your poolside haven at the Beach Village Oval Pool or one of the secluded plunge pools and jacuzzis throughout the lush grounds. The Oval Pool offers luxe chaise lounges, plush private cabanas, and intimate cabanettes with Breezes poolside bistro for coastal fare delivered to your chair.

Pools are open from 8am to 8pm daily. To reserve a private cabana or cabanette, please call the Beach Village concierge at 619.522.8809 or re serve online.

### Del Beach Access

Relax on the sand in your complimentary Del Beach sand chairs and umbrellas included with your Beach Village stay. For a more luxurious experience, reserve plush Del Beach daybeds and half-moon cabanettes.

### Personalized Service

Beach Village offers dedicated personal concierge service throughout your stay, a private welcome experience, gated access, priority reservations across the resort, and complimentary use of beach chairs and an umbrella.

Guests may also request personal shopping, party planning, VIP tours at local attractions and more.

Call the Beach Village concierge at 619.522.8809.

### Priority Dining

Beach Village guests enjoy priority seating at our dining outlets.
