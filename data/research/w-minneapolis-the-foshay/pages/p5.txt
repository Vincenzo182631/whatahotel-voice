# Explore Downtown Minneapolis in Style

## Experiences

Welcome to Minneapolis, MN, the epicenter for entertainment and music culture in the Midwest. Located on Marquette Avenue, near the city’s main thoroughfare, W Minneapolis – The Foshay is within walking distance or a quick train ride from all the major activities and attractions. Step out the front door of our hotel and find yourself near attractions like US Bank Stadium, the Minneapolis Convention Center, Target Field and Target Center. Take a stroll down Nicolette Mall, a shopping hot spot and the best dining the city has to offer. Still looking for things to do? Our team will connect you with insider events and places to experience the city like a native. If you're looking for a hotel that matches the high energy and easy hospitality of downtown Minneapolis, MN, look no further than W Minneapolis - The Foshay.

### Downtown Views

Unlock the view! Discover captivating exhibits, dive into Foshay Tower's rich history, and soak in stunning 360° city views from our 31st-floor observation deck. Ideal for history buffs, architecture lovers, and thrill-seekers! Open daily 9 AM - 9 PM.

### Nicollet Mall

Just one block from our property is the heart of downtown Minneapolis' shopping and entertainment mecca. Lined with boutique shopping and epic eateries, Nicolett offers nightlife at your doorstep.

### Walker Art Center

Acting as the unofficial bookend to Nicollet, this Minneapolis institution is one of the most visited modern and contemporary museums in the country. Across the street is the Minneapolis Sculpture garden featuring 40 works of contemporary art including the city's iconic "Spoonbridge and Cherry".

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Minneapolis - The Foshay have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Minneapolis - The Foshay offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Minneapolis - The Foshay?

Valleyfair, Target Center, Xcel Energy Center, Orchestra Hall, Walker Art Center and Sculpture Garden and more are nearby W Minneapolis - The Foshay.
