## Accommodations

With fabulous views, ample space and high-speed internet access, we wouldn't be surprised if you never left W Minneapolis - The Foshay. Each of our hotel rooms and suites draws inspiration from the vibrant downtown Minneapolis surroundings and features sparkling views, a W signature pillow-top bed and luxurious amenities. Welcome home to your sleek downtown hotel suite.

### Penthouse I King Suite

Complete with panoramic views of the city, our Penthouse I King Suite offers you a respite from a busy day or the perfect setting to host your own happy hour. There's no other suite like this in the city.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Minneapolis - The Foshay have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Minneapolis - The Foshay have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Minneapolis - The Foshay?

The largest occupancy room at W Minneapolis - The Foshay is the Penthouse I with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Minneapolis - The Foshay?

Some rooms at W Minneapolis - The Foshay permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Minneapolis - The Foshay have fridges?

No, rooms at W Minneapolis - The Foshay do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W Minneapolis - The Foshay have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Minneapolis - The Foshay have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Minneapolis - The Foshay have hair dryers?
