## In-Room Dining

Downtown Minneapolis dining in the comfort of your own sweet retreat at W Minneapolis - The Foshay. Experience culinary bliss catered by award-winning Manny's Steakhouse without wandering far. In-Room Dining is available for breakfast, lunch, and dinner daily.

## Great Steak, No Bull

Indulge in USDA-Certified, dry-aged, center-cut beef and a 300-bottle wine list at Manny's Steakhouse, our celebrated downtown Minneapolis restaurant, ranked as one of "The Top Steakhouses in the World."

## Toast Your Minneapolis Getaway

Keep it fun during your visit to downtown Minneapolis at our signature hotel lobby bar, The Living Room. Mix, sip and play in our inviting space that sets the social pulse of Minneapolis with handcrafted cocktails and a chef-curated menu.

## Luxe and Chic Cocktail Culture

Step back in time and head up to the 27th floor to get a taste of what life was like in the 1920’s. Originally Wilbur B. Foshay’s private full floor office and retreat has been restored to its original glory and converted to a speakeasy in the sky offering visitors a full bar, hideaway lounges and 360° views. Enjoy a cocktail in the clouds.
