# Welcome to W Minneapolis - The Foshay

## Dare to indulge at our luxury hotel in Minneapolis

Discover a stylish retreat in the heart of the city at W Minneapolis - The Foshay, a hotel in downtown Minneapolis that blends historic character with modern luxury. Inspired by the landmark Foshay Tower, our guest rooms and suites feature art deco details, contemporary furnishings, and innovative amenities designed for comfort and convenience. As a luxury hotel in downtown Minneapolis, we place you...See More moments from Target Field, U.S. Bank Stadium, Orchestra Hall, the Minneapolis Convention Center, and other top attractions. Enjoy exceptional dining, handcrafted cocktails, and panoramic city views from the iconic 30th Floor Observation Deck. Whether you're planning a business trip, wedding, or weekend getaway, our versatile event spaces set the stage for memorable occasions. As a welcoming pet-friendly hotel in downtown Minneapolis, we invite you and your furry companion to experience elevated service, vibrant social spaces, and the unmistakable energy of downtown Minneapolis.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Minneapolis - The Foshay?

The check-in time at W Minneapolis - The Foshay is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Minneapolis - The Foshay allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Minneapolis - The Foshay. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room.

What are the parking options at W Minneapolis - The Foshay?

On-site valet parking is available at W Minneapolis - The Foshay. The daily valet fee is $60.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Minneapolis - The Foshay have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Minneapolis - The Foshay has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Minneapolis - The Foshay booking.

What is the closest airport near W Minneapolis - The Foshay?

The closest airport near W Minneapolis - The Foshay is Minneapolis-St Paul International Airport (MSP). MSP is located approximately 12.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Minneapolis - The Foshay have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Minneapolis - The Foshay does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
