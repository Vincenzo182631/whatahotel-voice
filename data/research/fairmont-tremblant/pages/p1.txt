## Eastern-Canada's _landmark_ ski-in ski-out hotel

Spectacular by nature, Tremblant’s iconic ski-in ski-out hotel offers a year-round alpine escape. Nestled at the base of the majestic Tremblant peaks, Fairmont Tremblant blends relaxation and adventure. It's the perfect destination for couples, families, and solo travelers. Prepare for the ultimate alpine experience and create unforgettable memories.
