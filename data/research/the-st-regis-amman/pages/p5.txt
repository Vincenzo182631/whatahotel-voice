### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Amman have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Amman offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Amman have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Amman has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Amman have an on-site spa?

Yes, The St. Regis Amman has multiple spas on-site for hotel guests:

St. Regis SpaHair Inc Salon

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Amman?

Roman Theatre, Dead Sea, Citadel, Mount Nebo, Rainbow Street and more are nearby The St. Regis Amman.
