# Book Your Stay at One of Our Luxury Hotel Rooms

## Accommodations

Indulge in our spacious hotel rooms and suites at The St. Regis Amman, where luxury meets thoughtful design. Each accommodation features elegant touches that reflect the hotel’s commitment to refined comfort. Enjoy breathtaking views of the cityscape from your private balcony, creating a serene backdrop for your stay. Experience restful nights with premium Frette linens and the exclusive St. Regis Signature Bed, ensuring you wake up refreshed. Every room includes a beautifully decorated marble bathroom, providing an oasis of relaxation, while complimentary Wi-Fi keeps you seamlessly connected throughout the hotel. For an elevated experience, take advantage of the renowned St. Regis butler service, offering personalized assistance tailored to your needs. Whether in Amman for business or leisure, every moment is marked by luxury and unforgettable experiences at The St. Regis Amman.

Honoring the namesake of The St. Regis brand, The St. Regis Suite extends in two separate areas highlighting the additional privacy and discreet comfort and individuality of the suite. The beautiful full marble bathroom is part of the master bedroom.

## Deluxe Room

Floor to ceiling windows overlooking the city, earthy tones and signature details of rose and orange colors link you back to the picturesque landscapes of Wadi Rum. Allow the St. Regis Butlers to provide you with uncompromising and exquisite service to enliven 55th Avenue's memories.

## Royal Suite

Enjoy the captivating skyline view of Amman from the comfort of your Royal Suite, where elegance meets sophisticated design, from the customized chandelier to specially crafted artwork, paired with quintessential services provided by the St. Regis Butler Service.

## John Jacob Astor Suite

Luxuriate and enjoy the comfort, elegance and spacious ambiance of the 252 m2 John Jacob Astor Suite. Experience the quintessential of design from elegant canopy king size bed to the shimmering chandelier and the exquisite skyline view.
