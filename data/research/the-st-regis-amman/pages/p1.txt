# Welcome to The St. Regis Amman

## Amman luxury hotels

Welcome to The St. Regis Amman, set in the prestigious 5th Circle and recognized among the finest luxury hotels in Amman, Jordan. Each of the 258 rooms and suites is defined by timeless décor, city views, and the hallmark St. Regis Butler Service — whether you arrive for a refined city escape or as an executive requiring state-of-the-art meeting facilities and personalized event support. Dine at Mercado for an international breakfast buffet, Tamara for Levantine specialties, Segreta for brasserie classics, and Zenith for rooftop grills and cocktails, then unwind at the St. Regis Bar or Tea Lounge. Restore at our spa hotel in Amman, where curated treatments and a Thermal Suite with sauna, steam room, and jacuzzi await. This Amman hotel in Jordan is a 5-star hotel in Amman built for those who expect nothing less.

Advisory - Travel to Middle East

We continue to closely monitor the current regional situation in the Middle East as it evolves. Our hotel remains open and operational and our teams are focused on supporting our guests at this time.

### Signature Butler Services

### Packing and Unpacking Services

Upon arrival, entrust your luggage to a St. Regis Butler, expertly unpacking, organizing, and storing your belongings with meticulous care. For departure, a dedicated butler ensures you leave in elegance, seamlessly gathering and folding your garments.

### Garment Pressing

Further entrust your garments to the discerning hands of a St. Regis Butler. Promptly arranged with a call, our Butler will pick up and return your pressed garments with exceptional precision. As a testament to our commitment, enjoy complimentary pressing for up to two garments.

### Shoeshine Elegance

Step into luxury with the refined Shoeshine service. Expert butlers ensure a polished finish, showcasing an unwavering dedication to impeccable attention. Elevate your footwear for any occasion with the signature touch of excellence.

### Beverage Service

Experience the art of refined indulgence at The St. Regis Amman. At your request, our Butler arranges coffee or tea service upon your arrival, as well as ensures an exquisite start to your day with a wake-up call including the weather forecast.

### E-Butler

Through a live WhatsApp Chat, our E-Butler awaits your needs. Elevate every moment with St. Regis Butler Excellence, accessible through the QR code in your room TV and Key Card Insert.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Amman?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Amman is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Amman allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Amman.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Amman?

On-site valet parking is available at The St. Regis Amman. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Amman have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Amman has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Amman booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Amman?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Amman is Queen Alia International Airport (AMM). AMM is located approximately 35.2 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Amman have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Amman does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
