For the best of Jordan, Amman restaurants, savor an exquisite culinary journey at The St. Regis Amman, featuring world-class dining options. Start at the Tea Lounge, a St. Regis tradition offering fine teas and refreshments, perfect for unwinding after exploring Amman's iconic sights. The St. Regis Bar is a stylish destination for business and leisure, making it one of the city's premier gathering...See More spots.
