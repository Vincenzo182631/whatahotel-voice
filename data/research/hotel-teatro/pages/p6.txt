# Explore LoDo, Right Outside Our Door

## Explore The Heartbeat of Denver

Welcome to Lower Downtown, Denver’s oldest and most vibrant neighborhood. From the historic brick-lined streets of Larimer Square to the artistry of the performing arts center or the music notes coming from the Denver Center for Performing Arts, Hotel Teatro is your gateway to the very best of the city. Step outside our doors and immerse yourself in a district where Victorian architecture houses a modern, energetic scene of world-class dining, arts, and entertainment
