## THE NICKEL

Voted Denver’s Best Hotel Restaurant, **The Nickel** offers a dining experience unlike any other in the heart of Downtown Denver’s Theatre District. Our menus feature the highest quality ingredients from local farmers, ranchers, distillers, and craft producers, delivering classic American favorites with a creative twist. Blending rustic charm with industrial detail, our lively dining room is a hidden gem where each dish feels both warm and familiar.
