# Our Hotel

Denver's Original Boutique Hotel, with Room for Your Dog

## Welcome to Hotel Teatro

Housed in a restored 1911 landmark, Hotel Teatro brings together Denver history, modern comfort, and an AAA Four Diamond experience in the heart of downtown. We’re also proud to stand out among pet friendly hotels in Denver, Colorado, welcoming dogs. Whether your plans include the theater, the Colorado Convention Center, or an afternoon exploring the city, you’ll have a memorable place to return to each night during your Denver getaway.

Our Story

## A Timeless Denver Legacy

Rooted in over a century of Denver history, Hotel Teatro has long been a landmark of timeless style and gracious hospitality. Our story continues with thoughtful updates that honor our past while embracing the needs of today’s traveler.

Each guest room has been refreshed with bespoke oak wardrobes and refined, comfortable furnishings that reflect our historic character through a modern lens. The result is a space where classic elegance meets contemporary comfort, true to our roots yet ready for the future.

You’ll still find all the signature touches that make Hotel Teatro feel like home, including a complimentary welcome drink, local car service, and in-room dining from The Nickel.

Perfectly situated in downtown Denver, steps from the Denver Center for the Performing Arts, the Colorado Convention Center, and Larimer Square, Hotel Teatro remains a living piece of the city’s story where history and modern luxury meet.

## Destination Amenity Fee

A daily Destination Amenity Fee of $30 plus tax (per room, per night) is posted to each guest room in order to provide guests with a unique selection of amenities and services throughout their stay.

- $15 daily credit toward bites, appetizers, and entrees from the dinner menu in The Nickel
- Evening storytelling and wine ritual in The Study
- Curated discounts at the Colorado Athletic Club, the Denver Center for the Performing Arts, The Oxford Club & Spa, and Dog Savvy
- Custom vintage cruiser bicycles available for use throughout your stay
- 24/7 access to our fitness center featuring a Peloton bike
- Two branded water bottles provided in your room
- Wi-Fi throughout the hotel
