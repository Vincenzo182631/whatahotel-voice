Hotel Teatro

## Where Denver's Story Meets Yours

Set inside the beautifully restored Denver Tramway Building, originally constructed in 1911, Hotel Teatro offers a stay with more character than most hotels in Denver, Colorado. Denver’s original boutique hotel sits across from the Denver Performing Arts Complex and within an easy walk of Larimer Square, LoDo, and the city’s best dining and entertainment. Return from a day downtown to thoughtful service, award-winning dining at The Nickel, and the comfort of an AAA Four Diamond experience.
