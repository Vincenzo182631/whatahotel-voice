Electrifying the city’s culinary and nightlife scenes, W Sydney brings four dining and bar venues across three exciting levels. BTWN blends the finest Modern Australian ingredients with European-inspired cuisine, where craftsmanship meets attitude to create an urban playground made for food lovers. The only Australian outpost for 2am: dessertbar, the imaginative collaboration with Singaporean dessert...See More visionary Janice Wong, pushes the boundaries between sweet and savoury. Living Room embraces its Asiatown edge with Sake, Soju, and Japanese-inspired small bites, 29/30 is Sydney’s two-storey rooftop bar, where signature cocktails and casual Australian bites are served alongside sweeping views of Darling Harbour.See Less

### A METROPOLITAN MASHUP OF TEXTURES AND TASTE

Located on level three of W Sydney, BTWN restaurant evolves and transforms as the day unfolds. Linger longer over an expansive breakfast experience with continental and hot selections served across a series of pods, including Bean 2 Bar with its specialty brew selection catering to aficionados seeking a taste of Sydney's coffee love affair. Long lunches become launchpads for fun-filled evenings in...See More Darling Harbour with an à la carte menu designed for sharing, from small bites to substantial dishes, all driven by a sustainable ethos. Discover our modern Sydney restaurant, where a metropolitan mashup of textures and taste does everything but stay in its lane.See Less

29/30 Rooftop Bar

## Revel at a Higher Level

W Sydney’s two-storey rooftop bar, 29/30 is a spectacular sky-high experience in Darling Harbour, Sydney where cocktail alchemy meets poolside cabanas, and a brilliant mix of music takes evening experiences to new heights. The menu features a selection of moreish bites, thoughtfully paired with handcrafted signature cocktails. Sit by the floor-to-ceiling windows inside the bar and take in the striking décor and coral-inspired lighting that evokes an underwater world or watch as the sun sets over Darling Harbour in a semi-private open-air cabana.

Private Dining

## Intimate Affairs

Celebrate special occasions with a private dining or bar experience at W Sydney. Slip behind a screen at the far side of BTWN restaurant to enjoy the private dining room for up to 50 guests. Secure Living Room’s secret space overlooking Darling Harbour on the edge of the main bar area for up to 30 guests.
