# Stay in Luxury Hotel Rooms in Sydney

## Accommodations

W Sydney’s rooms, suites and studios echo the curves and lines of the hotel’s exceptional architecture, with no two rooms alike, embracing the asymmetry with a sleek, modern décor and expansive views over Darling Harbour or the city through floor-to-ceiling windows. The interior palette of our luxury hotel rooms is inspired by the surrounding marine environment, from nautical whites and navy blues...See More of bespoke furnishings to subtle recessed lighting recalling the sails of yachts in the Darling Harbour marina below. Gradated carpets suggest an ocean wave, continuing into the deep blue of the monolithic bathrooms. In contrast, a burst of inflorescent red and pink waratah flowers punctuates the walk-in wardrobe. Guiding the way to each guest room is a distinctive light constellation along the moodily lit corridor ceiling, designed by artist Stanislava Pinchuk, a renegade creative who takes inspiration for her curated piece from Sydney’s night skies.See Less

Sky-High Sophistication

### Penthouse I

The 202 sqm ultra-luxe Penthouse I on Level 27 channels the essence of the HMAS Onslow submarine. A catwalk bar leads to a spacious living and dining area, offering stunning Darling Harbour vistas. The bedroom boasts a unique crescent shape, an adjoining walk-in robe, and a second circular bathtub overlooking the harbour. Equipped with a Hi-Fi music system and five independent zones to stream music, each suite also features a private cellar stocked with premium Champagnes and wines, alongside a curated spirit bar.

Submerge in Splendour

## Penthouse II

W Sydney’s five upper-level Penthouse II, with notes of the submarine motif, offer floor-to-ceiling views of Darling Harbour. Ranging in size from 112sqm to 125sqm, these one-bedroom standout luxury suite accommodations in Sydney feature a bar, dining, and lounge space, a walk-in robe, and a second soaking tub overlooking the harbour below. Each suite is equipped with a Hi-Fi music system with independent zones to stream music, and a cellar stocked with premium Champagnes and wines, alongside a curated spirit bar.

Residential Luxury, Redefined

### Studios

W Sydney Studios redefine the concept of luxury urban accommodation. Residential in design, the fully furnished Studios offer separate living, dining and bedroom areas in Sydney. Business, leisure and long stay guests will experience luxury design, full gourmet kitchens and access to all of the facilities of a 5-star hotel. Each Studio echos the curves of the hotel’s exceptional architecture, creating a variety of sizes to suit all needs, and views of the city or Darling Harbour through oversized windows for a stellar outlook.

### P.A.W. Pets Are Welcome

Pets Are Welcome (P.A.W.) at W Sydney, offering pet-friendly Sydney hotel rooms alongside a variety of bespoke amenities including clean-up bags, a pet bed, food and water bowls, and a ‘Pet In Residence’ door hanger. Please note the room rate will incur an additional daily fee of 200 AUD. A limit of one pet per room applies, pets are not allowed in public areas including restaurants, bars, and pool...See More areas, and cannot be left unattended in the accommodation room.See Less

Extra Touches

### W Mixbar

Ease into the evening with a custom cocktail from the sparkling chrome W Mixbar delivering an echo of Sydney’s art deco heritage design into your room or suite. The W Mixbar is stocked with spirits and mixers sourced in partnership with the finest local producers including ready-to-pour cocktails by the supremely creative mixologists behind cult Sydney bar Maybe Sammy.

### WHATEVER⁄WHENEVER Service

Whatever you want. Whenever you want it (as long as it’s legal). Just ask. No wish is too small and no ask too big for W’s signature service philosophy: Whatever/Whenever. For off-the-beaten-path neighbourhood hotspots and hidden gems, turn to your W Insider – W’s locally connected guest experience creator – for the best Sydney has to offer.

### In-Room Dining

Whether it’s a relaxed start to the day with breakfast in bed or satisfying a late-night craving, W Sydney's in-room dining menu is available throughout the day and late into the evening.
