# Welcome to W Sydney

## Stay ahead of the curve at our 5-star hotel in Sydney

Rising over Darling Harbour, W Sydney instantly captures the eye with the undulating lines of its futuristic exterior and striking interiors that pay homage to the luxury hotel’s NSW locale. Daring and original, a sleek retreat where bold design meets the larrikin spirit, seamlessly melding Sydney and Australia’s cultural touchstones to create an all-encompassing dining and social destination. This hotel transforms as day turns to night, from the evocative future-noir allure of the Living Room bar and kinetic energy of BTWN restaurant to AWAY Spa, a refreshing sanctuary amid its concrete city surrounds, and the panoramic harbour views from WET Deck and double story rooftop bar, 29/30. Within each of our hotel rooms, suites, and studios, marine chic design interiors channel the hotel’s Darling Harbour location and Sydney’s stunning coastlines. W Sydney is ahead of the curve, making room for renegades to eat, drink, play, and stay in inimitable style.

From the moment of arrival, embark on a luxury design-led journey, each layer of this hotel in Darling Harbour, Sydney, sharing a story that is as unique as it is enrapturing. Set On Darling Harbour

## Waterfront and Centre

A vibrant hub on the harbour, W Sydney boasts a waterfront setting in the heart of Sydney’s lively entertainment, retail, and dining districts. Stay in a skyline-defining landmark offering harbour or city views from each guestroom and venue or explore the ever-evolving Darling Harbour and adjacent Darling Quarter and Barangaroo precincts. Leading to Sydney’s icons, follow the scenic waterfront walking trail that winds its way from neighbouring Pyrmont and the Barangaroo headland, towards The Rocks, Circular Quay, Sydney Opera House and Royal Botanic Garden Sydney. Visit our hotel in Darling Harbour near Sydney's CBD, for business. We're steps away from the city’s commercial area and the Sydney International Convention Centre (ICC).

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Sydney?

The check-in time at W Sydney is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Sydney allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Sydney. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room.

What are the parking options at W Sydney?

On-site valet parking is available at W Sydney. The daily valet fee is $100.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Sydney have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Sydney has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Sydney booking.

What is the closest airport near W Sydney?

The closest airport near W Sydney is Sydney Airport (SYD). SYD is located approximately 14.3 KM from the hotel.

Does W Sydney have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Sydney has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
