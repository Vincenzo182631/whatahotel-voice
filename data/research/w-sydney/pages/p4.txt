### Vivid Sydney

Vivid Sydney, a festival of light, sound and ideas held in Sydney’s winter months of May and June, lights up the city. Vivid’s waterfront Light Walk spans Circular Quay to the promenade outside W Sydney, and beyond. Enjoy street food and fine dining experiences at Vivid Food events, catch Vivid Live shows at iconic venues including the Sydney Opera House, and seek out Vivid Ideas talks and workshops across town.

Step Into the Light

### Sydney Marathon

Taking place each August, Sydney Marathon is the city's premier running event, sending elite athletes and everyday runners through closed harbour city streets. The course runs from North Sydney across the Harbour Bridge and through the CBD, finishing beneath the Opera House sails. Cheer runners on Macquarie Street, join the Runners Village buzz, and celebrate at the harborside after-party by Circular Quay.

Discover More

### Sydney Festival

Taking place each January at an eclectic mix of venues across the city, Sydney Festival is a three-week program of live performances. The annual event spans the disciplines of theatre, music, dance and visual art, and includes several specially commissioned new Australian works. Secure tickets to shows from local and internationally renowned artists, take in public art and join in immersive dance parties.
