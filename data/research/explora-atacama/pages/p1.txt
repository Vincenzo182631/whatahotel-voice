Step Inside Our Atacama Desert Lodge

Situated on a pristine 42-acre (17-hectare) desert estate near San Pedro de Atacama, the 50-room Explora Atacama offers an elevated experience in the middle of the world’s highest and driest desert. Designed to match this spectacular destination and immerse you fully in it, the lodge offers an award-winning architecture, inviting rooms for rest and rejuvenation, a tranquil spa and multiple pools, and a world-class Observatory for taking in dazzling Atacama night skies.

Prime Desert Location

Explora Atacama boasts a perchon the beautiful Ayllu de Larache, surrounded by the sand dunes and high-elevation parklands of enchanting San Pedro de Atacama, the Atacama’s most famous oasis. The lodge also affords easy access to the many wonders of the desert and the high-elevation Altiplano, including the nearby Altiplanic Lagoons and ancient Pukará de Quitor archeological site.

Architecture Inspired by Nature

Designed by famed Chilean architect José Cruz Ovalle, ouraward-winning lodge embodies a commitment to sustainabilityand immersion in nature, creating an unforgettable experience for guests who want to authentically connect with Atacama’s desert landscapes. Soaring spaces, sweeping views and locally sourced furnishings define the grounds and interiors, while common areas, such as the dining room and lounge spaces, invite opportunities to enjoy relaxing, socializing, or quiet contemplation.

Scenic Spa, Pools & More

Surrounded by tall pampas grass and Atacama flora, our four interconnected pools are the perfect place to relax and soak in the quiet of the desert. Each pool offers a different experience, including a pool warmed to 77–82°F (25–28° C). Nearbyin a traditional adobe house, visit our spa with a range of treatments as well as a sauna, steam baths and outdoor hot tubs.

World-Class Observatory

San Pedro de Atacama is world-renowned for its clear skies and incredible stargazing. The exceptional clarity of its sky has attracted cutting-edge observatories, including ALMA with its groundbreaking astronomical discoveries. Seethese dazzling night skies from our very own onsite Observatory, with guided star tours and Explorations that reveal the Milky Way, planets and endless constellations.
