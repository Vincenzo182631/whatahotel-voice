What to Pack

Explora provides travelers with trekking poles, helmets and riding gaiters for horseback rides and give them a bottle of water.

**Required for all activities:**

- A hat for the sun
- 1st layer: T-shirts and thermal underwear
- 2nd layer: Fleece
- 3rd layer: Warm jacket
- 4th layer: Waterproof jacket
- Long- and short sleeve t-shirts
- Thin fleece
- Hiking trousers and shorts
- Hiking shoes
- Hiking socks (long).
- Thermal and windproof gloves.
- Sunglasses
- Sunscreen
- Buff or protective neckwear
- Small backpack (25 liters)
- Bottle of water

**Optional for all activities:**

- Swimsuit
- Sandals
- Camel back

Climate

Situated in one of the driest regions on Earth, San Pedro de Atacama is defined by its extreme aridity and striking landscapes. Here, the climate is remarkably stable, with almost no rainfall throughout the year. Days are typically warm, while nights can be cold, with significant temperature drops after sunset.

The desert’s unique environment supports a surprising diversity of life, with flora and fauna adapted to thrive in these harsh conditions. San Pedro de Atacama is also renowned for its crystal-clear skies, making it one of the best places in the world for stargazing, offering unparalleled views of the night sky.

Wifi & Voltage

Please note that due to our remote locations, Wi-Fi access in rooms and common areas may vary in reliability.

The voltage in Atacama is 220V.

Tips

Tips are voluntary. If you wish to leave any, you can do so at the hotel reception at the end of your stay. It will be distributed among all those who contributed to making your trip a unique experience. We suggest leaving USD 100 per day per room.

FAQs

#### Logistics & Planning

- What’s the best time of year to visit the Atacama?

The Atacama Desert is a year-round destination, with each season offering unique conditions that shape the travel experience. In the summer months (December to February), temperatures during the day are warm and pleasant, but this is also when there’s a slightly higher chance of rain due to the so-called Altiplanic Winter—short, sporadic showers that can bring dramatic skies and rare desert blooms. In contrast, winter (June to August) brings colder nights, often with temperatures dropping below freezing, especially at higher altitudes, while daytime remains sunny and dry. Spring and fall offer a balance of mild weather and fewer crowds. Regardless of when you visit, the Atacama’s striking landscapes, starry skies, and otherworldly beauty make it an unforgettable destination any time of year.

- How many days should I spend in the Atacama Desert?

To truly connect with the vastness and diversity of the Atacama Desert, we recommend staying for at least five nights. This allows enough time to explore a variety of landscapes—from salt flats and geysers to canyons and lunar-like valleys—without feeling rushed. With six or more nights, you’ll have the opportunity to go deeper into the territory, reaching the high plateau, visiting remote lagoons, or even ascending one of the region’s iconic volcanoes. A longer stay also means you can enjoy the pace of exploration while taking in the desert’s silence, colors, and night skies at a more immersive rhythm.

- Is credit card acceptance widespread or should I bring cash?

Credit cards are widely accepted at Explora and in most shops, restaurants, and services in downtown San Pedro de Atacama. However, it’s a good idea to carry some cash for small purchases at local markets, artisan stands, or rural communities, where card payment may not be available. Cash is also useful for tipping staff at the Explora lodge, should you wish to do so. That said, there’s no need to worry about paying for additional services during your stay—everything at the lodge is included as part of the all-inclusive experience.

#### Climate & Food

- How extreme is the temperature difference between day and night in Explora Atacama Lodge?

During winter, the temperatures in Explora’s Atacama Desert Lodge fluctuate between -10°C (14°F) at night and 20°C (68°F) in daytime. During summer, the temperatures fluctuate between 10°C (50°F) at night and 30°C (86°F) in daytime.

- Are there vegetarian/vegan food options available?

Yes! There are vegetarian/vegan food options available in every Explora destination. If you have any dietary preferences or restrictions, please contact our reserves team before your arrival.

- Do I need to make restaurant reservations in advance?

Theres is no need to make restaurant reservations in advance if you are staying in any Explora Lodge, our travelers are always considered in every meal, it is part of the all-inclusive service we offer.

#### Activities & Experiences

- What are the must-see attractions in the Atacama Desert?

Explora has plenty of “must-see” attractions that you certainly cannot miss, however, it will depend on your preferences and experience, as you can choose from almost 50 guided explorations included in your stay, from a visit to the observatory to see a wonderful starry night to a panoramic view from the peaks of different mountains.

- What is the best way to experience stargazing in the Atacama?

The Atacama Desert is world-renowned for its clear skies and minimal light pollution, making it one of the best places on Earth for stargazing. At Explora, you can take this experience to the next level with access to our private observatory, designed specifically for astronomical observation. Accompanied by expert guides, you’ll have the chance to view constellations, planets, and deep-sky objects through high-quality telescopes—an awe-inspiring experience that reveals the full beauty of the desert’s night skies.

- Can I visit Bolivia’s Salar de Uyuni from the Explora Atacama Lodge?

Yes, it is possible to visit Bolivia’s Salar de Uyuni from Explora Atacama through a special overland journey designed by Explora. However, this experience must be booked separately and confirmed prior to your arrival at the lodge. Due to the logistics involved, it is not possible to arrange this trip once you are already at the lodge. We recommend planning ahead to ensure availability and a seamless travel experience across the border into Bolivia.
