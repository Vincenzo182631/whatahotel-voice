Experience the Authentic Flavors of the Desert

At Explora Atacama, your journey through this vibrant desert is inclusive of an exquisite culinary experience. Our menu blends tradition with innovation, with a focus on local flavors and ingredients in every dish. Enjoy an exciting dining experience, complemented by a selection of fine Chilean wines, available at both our bar and dining room.

Atacama

All-Inclusive Dining

Your stay at Explora Atacama includes every dining experience during your stay, including breakfast, lunch and dinner, as well as all beverages, wine and an open bar. Meals and snacks enjoyed during full-day Explorations are also included.

Your stay at Explora Atacama includes every dining experience during your stay, including breakfast, lunch and dinner, as well as all beverages, wine and an open bar. Meals and snacks enjoyed during full-day Explorations are also included.

Exceptional Chilean Wines

Your dining experience at Explora Atacama includes an impressive selection of wines reflecting Chile’s famed viticulture, including Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and Carmenère. Enjoy tastings paired with beautiful meals in the dining room or at the bar by a fire, immersing in unique flavor profiles surrounded by views of the landscapes that inspired them.
