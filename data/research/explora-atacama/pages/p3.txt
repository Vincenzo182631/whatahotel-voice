## Welcome to Explora Atacama

Experience the best of Chile’s surreal desert landscape at Explora Atacama, our multi-award-winning lodge near the oasis town of San Pedro de Atacama. Surrounded by the wild beauty of the vast Atacama Desert — salt flats, volcanoes, geysers, windswept dunes and more — discover a contemporary retreat with every comfort in mind. Enjoy a unique culinary experience, four pools and a spa, and stargazing from our onsite Observatory. Then venture out on horseback from Explora’s own stables, bike or hike the Altiplano with views of the Valley of the Moon, and soak in the natural hot springs, exclusively located in Explora’s PuritamaConservation Reserve.

Stay in a Stunning Oasis

With an awe-inspiring architecture to match its vast desert surrounds, Explora Atacama is a true oasis amidst the rugged landscapes of the Atacama Desert. At our 50-room lodgenear the historic town of San Pedro de Atacama, discover an elevated stay beyond compare, including all meals, spa facilities witha sauna and four pools, and our own onsite observatory and horseback riding stables.

View Lodge

Embark on Desert Explorations

You’re invited to choose from nearly 50 guided Explorations at Explora Atacama, all included as part of your stay. Led by our own expertly trained guides, these experiences and Explora-only adventures include horseback riding, biking, hiking and high-elevation climbs across the soul-stirring geology of the otherworldly Atacama.

Every Exploration is also customized to match your interests and fitness levels, ensuring an unforgettable adventure made just for you.

Explore Our Puritama Conservation Reserve

Encompassing a unique ecoregion deep in an Andean canyon of the Atacama Desert, Explora’s private Puritama Conservation Reserve offers an important river oasis and sanctuary for endangered wildlife. Guest staying at Explora Atacama enjoy privileged access to Explora-only Explorations, including the opportunity to swim in its naturally occurring, mineral-rich hot springs.
