### SUSTAINABILITY AT W MALDIVES

Discover how we’re making waves for a greener future at our Maldives resort. Our bold commitment to sustainability ensures that every escape leaves a positive impact on our environment and local communities.

SMARTER RESOURCES, BRIGHTER FUTURE

Our Sustainable Management Plan outlines strategies to minimize negative environmental impacts, conserve natural resources, and champion social responsibility. Through waste reduction, energy efficiency, water conservation, carbon emissions cuts, biodiversity protection, and educational initiatives, we set ambitious goals to lead responsible hospitality in the Maldives.

PEOPLE POWERED, COMMUNITY DRIVEN

We believe real change begins with people. Our plan empowers talents and engages stakeholders by fostering communication, encouraging active participation in green initiatives, and building strong partnerships with local communities. Together, we create a ripple effect that reaches far beyond our shores.

PROGRESS IN MOTION

To ensure progress, we set measurable KPIs and publish annual sustainability reports showcasing achievements and future commitments. Our plan is reviewed and refined each year, so our journey toward environmental excellence continues to evolve with purpose and accountability.

WASTE LESS, LIVE MORE

W Maldives is dedicated to reducing waste in every facet of operations. By phasing out single-use items, championing recycling and composting, and implementing robust food waste management, we actively lower our footprint while inspiring a culture of mindful consumption across our Maldives island resort.

CONSCIOUS SOURCING, LOCAL FLAVORS

At our Maldives island hotel, we prioritize locally sourced, seasonal, and sustainably certified products to promote ethical supply chains and reduce emissions from transportation. By collaborating with trusted suppliers and Maldivian producers, we strengthen community economies and ensure the freshest, most responsible ingredients and materials.

PROTECTING PARADISE

Our vibrant coral reefs and rich marine life are treasures we fiercely protect. Partnering with environmental experts, we support reef conservation, safeguard native habitats, and prevent invasive species. Our commitment preserves the unique biodiversity of the Maldives, ensuring it thrives for generations to come.

### Traveling to the Maldives

Get in the know before traveling to the Maldives and explore the unscripted beauty in true W style. A bold and unscripted escape awaits at our Maldives island hotel. Feel free to connect with us at reservations.wmaldives@whotels.com if you need further information.

Whatever/Whenever® Service

We’re always here for you. Anything, just ask!​ A luxury speedboat ride to a private beach with a sunset dinner for two? Wed your way on a castaway island? Lessons in Dhivehi? Our Whatever/Whenever® team will deliver whatever you want, whenever you want it.​

Seaplane Transfers

Your escape begins on arrival at Velana International Airport. Our Airport Ambassadors will assist with seaplane check-in and escort you to the terminal. Mandatory return seaplane transfers are charged separately at USD 730 net per adult and USD 365 net per child (aged 2–11), per person, for a round trip to and from Velana International Airport.

External Speedboat Policy

For the comfort, safety, and consistency of the guest experience, all transfer and marine excursions are managed exclusively by the resort. In accordance with resort security guidelines and operational standards, outsourced external speedboat services are not permitted for arrivals, departures, or excursions. Guests are invited to connect with the resort for approved transfer and excursion options.

Time Zone & Language

W Maldives is in the same time zone as Male’, which is GMT +5 hours. Dhivehi is the official language of the Maldives however English is spoken by all talents at W Maldives.

Climate

The climate is warm and tropical throughout the year with cooling sea breezes. Daily temperatures vary between 26°C / 80°F and 32°C / 90°F. Even during the "wind directions" months (southwest from April to October and northeast from December to March), there are seldom more than a few days of rain at a time.​

Drone Policy

Due to safety and privacy concerns for our guests, W Maldives prohibits the operation of unmanned aerial systems or drones, on or above the island by the general public — including model aircraft by recreational users and hobbyists. This prohibition includes drones used for filming or videotaping, as well as any drone use by media or journalists operating above or within the resort area boundaries.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Maldives?

The check-in time at W Maldives is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Maldives allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at W Maldives.

What are the parking options at W Maldives?

W Maldives does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Maldives have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Maldives has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Maldives booking.

What is the closest airport near W Maldives?

The closest airport near W Maldives is Velana International Airport (MLE). MLE is located approximately 57.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Maldives have an airport shuttle to Velana International Airport (MLE)?

Yes, W Maldives has an airport shuttle to Velana International Airport (MLE).

Does W Maldives have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Maldives does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
