On the Shoreline

## Beach Villas

Our Maldives beach villas channel the raw beauty of the island’s lush mangroves, reimagined with a sleek, modern twist. Textured finishes, natural tones, and open spaces create a vibe that balances calm and thrill — your own private basecamp just steps from the sand. Whether you’re soaking in the serenity or sliding into the pool, this is where barefoot luxury meets untamed energy. Beach days just got an upgrade.

Above It All

## Overwater Villas

Inspired by the hypnotic patterns of majestic whale sharks, our Maldives luxury overwater villas are a bold blend of sleek lines, fluid forms, and modern design made for the ultimate lagoon life. Think sun-drenched decks, indoor-outdoor flow, and private pools that make every plunge feel iconic. Whether you're chasing the sunrise, vibing to the sunset, or floating in between — this is your front-row seat to the reef, wrapped in style that hits different.

Some rooms at W Maldives permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
