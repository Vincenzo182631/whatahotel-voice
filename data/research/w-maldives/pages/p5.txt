Festive Experiences

### Glow up the Season

Gather as twilight falls and the season officially begins with a radiant tree lighting by the sea. Expect sparkling lights, uplifting beats, seasonal sips, and that unmistakable magical moments. It is the perfect time to pause, toast, and step into nights filled with celebration.

### Tides & Tinsel

Celebrate Christmas Eve with a spectacular buffet spread on the sand, where the aroma of the grill mingles with ocean air. Indulge in premium roasts, festive specialties, fresh seafood, and decadent desserts, all served in a warm, glowing beachside setting. With live cooking stations and a lively atmosphere, this is Christmas Eve done the island way.

### Splash With Santa

Christmas Day turns playful at WET Deck with a sunshine-soaked celebration for the whole family. Expect lively games, creative kids’ activities, cheerful music, and a grand Santa appearance complete with exciting gifts. It is a joyful, high energy afternoon filled with laughter, poolside treats, and unforgettable holiday moments.

### Whispers of Umami

Surrender to the chef’s creativity with a refined omakase experience where every course arrives as a surprise. Meticulously crafted dishes showcase pristine ingredients and contemporary techniques, paired with premium sake selections designed to elevate every bite.

### Silk Road Supper Club

Join us for a night of vibrant culinary journey across Asia. From aromatic street-food classics to refined signature dishes, each station invites you to explore something new. Lively, colorful, and endlessly satisfying, this is comfort food elevated to new and distinctive level.

### Negroni Afterglow

Discover the many personalities of the iconic Negroni in one stylish tasting flight. From bold and bitter to bright and aromatic, this guided tasting is perfect for cocktail lovers to deepen their appreciation while soaking in the sunset views at SIP.

### Champagne Seas

Dive into a luxurious dining experience celebrating the finest treasures of the sea. Expertly crafted dishes highlight pristine seafood prepared with the freshest ingredients, each course elevated by thoughtfully selected premium Champagne pairings. This is an evening of pure indulgence set against the rhythm of the ocean.

### The Velvet Revelry

Step beyond the ordinary New Year’s celebration and into The Velvet Revelry, an opulent speakeasy soirée hidden behind red velvet curtains. From golden hour to midnight, expect immersive entertainment, decadent dining, and sultry festive energy. When the countdown begins, raise your glass beneath the stars and toast to a new year in unforgettable style.

### Poolside Reset, No Regrets

Kick off the year with a splash at WET Deck’s New Year’s Day Pool Party. Dive into a laid-back BBQ feast, unwind with flavorful shisha, and refresh with recovery drinks crafted to revive your vibe. Need a little extra? Poolside massages keep the good times flowing and the relaxation going strong — the perfect reset after last night’s celebration.
