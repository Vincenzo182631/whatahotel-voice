# Taste Paradise at W Maldives' Restaurants

## Dining

Abandon the expected and dive into bold Flavors across three vibrant Maldives restaurants and two bar concepts. Go barefoot for lunch in the heart of the island and savor delicious local bites at KITCHEN, then switch it up with an overwater seafood dinner at FISH—where the Indian Ocean is both your view and your menu. When night falls, FIRE brings the heat with the best from the grill and smoker, all...See More served under the stars. Dialling up the romance made easy with a private island romantic dinner at Gaathafushi. At the overwater sunset bar, SIP, savor the unique Nikkei cuisine along with refreshing signature cocktails. Craving for some social time? WET Deck is the noon-to-moon social hub of the island. From glow-up breakfasts to after-dark indulgence on the Maldives beachfront, each fine dining experience is a mood—and every bite captures the rhythm of the island.See Less
