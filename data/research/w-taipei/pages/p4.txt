# Taipei's Hottest Spot for Culture & Entertainment

## Experiences

Disappear from the everyday and escape into the fantastic sights, scents and sounds of W Taipei, where energy, light, creativity and art whisk you to where mixing and mingling are always in vogue. Feel the sensational vibe of one of the most exciting cities around the world - Taipei. From our perch above the hip Uni department store and City Hall MRT stop, W Taipei is just minutes from Taipei 101,...See More one of the world's tallest building with an 89th floor observatory. Right in the heart of the vibrant Xinyi district, you never have to go far to explore the hottest retail therapy in town—this is where the most premier malls hold court, all connected by footbridges to make it easier for you to shop around. Also, our Xinyi, Taipei spa hotel is near the hub of the most scintillating nightlife, check out all the scenes to be seen at, a super-hip rendition of the immensely sociable nature of Taipei locals.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Taipei have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Taipei offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Weight Machines, and Yoga Mat, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Taipei have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Taipei has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does W Taipei have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY® Spa is located on-site at W Taipei. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Taipei?

TAIPEI 101 Observatory, Taipei Arena, National Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall, Taipei 101, National Dr. Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall and more are nearby W Taipei.
