## Accommodations

Step into style at W Taipei. Our rooms and suites are decked out with Zodiac-inspired animal sculptures, stainless steel map installations and other worldly artwork. Each of our 405 rooms are decorated and outfitted for a personal, energetic feel.
Take it easy in a contemporary setting that complements your iconic window views of Taipei 101. Our hotel accommodations include guilt-free initiatives such...See More as our Green Program for linens and towels. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Taipei have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Taipei have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Taipei?

The largest occupancy room at W Taipei is the Extreme WOW Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Taipei?

Some rooms at W Taipei permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Taipei have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Taipei have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at W Taipei have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Taipei have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Taipei have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Taipei have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Taipei have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Taipei have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
