# Spa & Salon at The Del

## _Spa & Salon_ Let the Ocean Be Your Guide.

Since 1888, Coronado Island has been a wellness destination with its revitalizing ocean air, temperate climate, and serene environment to promote health and well-being. Today, Spa & Salon at The Del continues this legacy with treatments that blend traditional wellness practices with modern spa techniques using natural ingredients derived from the land and sea.

Enjoy exclusive access to our relaxation lounges, spa terrace, and ocean-view spa pool when you reserve a massage, facial, or body treatment. Guests of the Spa & Salon at The Del also receive complimentary self-parking.

## Massages

**The Destination**

Being in touch with the natural world reconnects us with our natural selves, resulting in a physical, emotional, intellectual, and spiritual homecoming. Harness the power of the Earth and the Sea with rituals designed to improve sleep, reduce stress, and ease discomfort.

**Ocean Essence Ritual**

Harnessing the power of wild, organic, hand-harvested seaweed, this treatment stimulates blood flow, detoxifies and hydrates the skin, and helps with cellulite and muscle fatigue. A finely ground walnut shell body buff is polished over the body with a blend of almond oil, seaweed, and ginger extracts. A warm seaweed body mask is then applied. While wrapped, enjoy a marine eye treatment and scalp massage. After a shower, nourish the skin with an application of nutrient-packed seaweed body oil enriched with rosehip and primrose.

100 Minutes

**Mindful Dreams Rest Ritual**

The Sleep Ritual offers a unique and heightened sensory experience of pure relaxation. Soothing, flowing massage techniques combined with mindful breathwork and the power of rose quartz crystals help invite rest and induce deeper sleep. This journey begins with an organic foot ritual, your feet enveloped in seaweed leaves to detoxify, increase circulation, and reduce fluid retention. This light-to-medium pressure full-body massage also includes a beautifully relaxing face and scalp massage. Feel your whole body soften as deep rest descends, enveloped in the aromas of lavender and evening primrose.

100 Minutes

**Balancing Ritual**

This beautiful balancing spa ritual begins with mindful breathwork and uses unique massage techniques to bring alignment, balance, and a deeper mind-body connection. Lymphatic drainage techniques and Gua Sha help stimulate blood circulation and aid in reducing fluid retention and swelling throughout the body. Cold stone massage promotes circulation and acts as a powerful decongestant. An oil blend of jojoba, sweet almond, and geranium brings a balancing and toning effect to the skin, while antioxidant-rich sweet orange oil leaves the skin feeling rejuvenated. This unique and purposeful massage experience will bring alignment and balance to the body.

100 Minutes

**Voyager Aromatherapy**

This therapeutic aromatherapy massage balances your skin, body, and energy to bring a sense of equilibrium and calm. This ultimate luxury treatment rejuvenates your body by deeply infusing it with an organic seaweed oil of your choice to renew damaged skin and improve tone and elasticity.

50 Minutes \| 80 Minutes

50 Minutes \| 80 Minutes

**Relaxation**

This full-body massage incorporates slow, rhythmical light pressure massage techniques, helping to alleviate muscular tension while having a meditative, deeply relaxing effect.

50 Minutes \| 80 Minutes

**Muscle Recovery**

Flowing, rhythmical, and firm, this massage will assist in releasing overworked muscles from their tense and tightened position while also improving circulation and restoring balance.

50 Minutes \| 80 Minutes

**Table Thai**

This classic Thai massage combines pressure point stimulation over loose-fitting clothes to encourage the balance of energy flow throughout the body by integrating yoga-like stretches to provide flexibility and increased range of motion.

80 Minutes

**Beach Feet Reflexology**

Following a gentle walnut and seaweed foot scrub, pressure points on the feet are massaged to help with stress, fatigue, pain, and sleep difficulties.

80 Minutes

50 Minutes

**TROPICAL REFRESHER**

Travel to a tropical shore without ever leaving the spa. Juicy yuzu, pineapple, papaya, and Kakadu plum unite to reveal a vibrant and smooth complexion.

50 Minutes

Finishing with a Marshmallow mask, skin will be deeply hydrated and have a radiant appearance.

50 Minutes

## Body Treatments

**Being Well Therapies**

Blend the calming essence of the sea with the grounding qualities of the earth.

**STIMULATING SEAWEED AND LAVENDER BODY BUFF**

A wonderfully fragrant mix of lavender oil, seaweed, and sugar, this double-action treatment gently removes dead skin cells and heals with an application of seaweed extract and lavender essential oil. It will remove dull skin and refine skin's texture.

50 Minutes

**GROUND WELLBEING BODY POLISH**

Choosing from either an uplifting or balancing body scrub blend, this invigorating, all-over body exfoliation refines skin texture and increases luster. Improving circulation and aiding the absorption of the potent essential oil application, this treatment will leave you feeling replenished and grounded in your body.

50 Minutes

**REVITALIZING BODY WRAP**

Combining a skin-softening rice mask and organic seaweed, this treatment lifts the senses to indulge the body and the mind. A dry lymphatic body brush increases blood circulation and is deeply detoxifying. With a refreshing and uplifting massage with a fragrant mix of peppermint, lime, and eucalyptus, this treatment provides an overall feeling of well-being.

80 minutes

**DETOXIFYING SEAWEED LEAF WRAP**

Unlike any other seaweed treatment, this truly amazing detoxifying therapy uses real organic seaweed leaves to scrub and wrap your body. Beginning with a Seaweed Body Buff, this treatment detoxifies, firms, and softens the skin. The body is then wrapped in seaweed leaves, and the treatment concludes with a massage using seaweed, aloe vera, lime, mandarin, and plum seeds to nourish and soften the skin. This treatment tones and firms your skin and helps with the signs of aging and cellulite.

80 Minutes

**Mom To Be**

Relieve pain, improve sleep, and induce a profound sense of calm with these deeply comforting treatments for the second and third trimesters of pregnancy.

**PRENATAL MASSAGE**

Treatment soothes nerves and relieves strained back and leg muscles in expectant mothers. Jojoba and cacay seed oils boost collagen and skin's elasticity to strengthen the skin and minimize stretch marks.

50 \| 80 Minutes

**PRECIOUS MOMENTS BODY THERAPY**

This organic treatment begins with a cocooning mask for the bump, and a stretch mark-minimizing oil will help support connective tissue and help prevent stretch marks. Your feet will be enveloped in seaweed leaves designed to boost circulation and improve lymphatic drainage, helping to lighten legs and reduce fluid retention. Mom will also enjoy a beautifully relaxing face, neck, hand, and arm massage.

50 Minutes

**PRENATAL VOYAGER JOURNEY**

Nourishing Mama Oil is used to gently ease stress and tension with a head-to-toe body exfoliation, followed by a face, scalp and full body massage. Melt away the aches and pains associated with pregnancy and feel comfortably cocooned, supported, and restored.

80 Minutes

## More Information

**GIFT CARDS**

Spa at The Del accepts Hilton Gift Cards, Spa & Salon at The Del Gift Cards, and Spafinder Gift Cards _(Spafinder Gift Cards cannot be combined with other discounts, promotions or offers)_.

**ARRIVAL**

Please arrive 30 minutes prior to your spa appointment so that we may show you to your locker and allow you time to change into a robe and slippers. Your appointment is reserved exclusively for you. In order to honor each guest's experience, late arrivals will receive only the remaining time of the appointment.

**PARKING**

Spa guests receive complimentary self-parking at the hotel.

**CANCELLATIONS**

Should you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please notify us 24 hours prior to your service to avoid being charged in full. Group reservations are subject to the terms of the contract.

**AGE REQUIREMENTS**

Young adults 14 and older are welcome to receive spa services. A parent must be on-site and provide consent for anyone 17 or younger. Services are scheduled with a same-gender provider, and teens must wear their own bathing suit.

**CLOTHING OPTIONS**

For spa services, bring a bathing suit to enjoy the vanishing-edge pool on the outdoor co-ed spa terrace. Bathing suits are optional in the men’s and women’s lounge areas.

**SECURING VALUABLES**

We recommend you leave valuables at home or in your guest room safe. Spa at The Del is not responsible for lost or stolen items.

**SERVICE CHARGE**

For your convenience, menu prices are inclusive of a 20% service charge. All prices and services at the Spa & Salon are subject to change without notification.
