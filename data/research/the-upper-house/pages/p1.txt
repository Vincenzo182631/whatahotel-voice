WELCOME TO

UPPER HOUSE

HONG KONG

Journey upward to a haven high above the city lights.

Upper House is a design-led luxury hotel in Admiralty, conceived by architect André Fu as a tranquil, residential retreat high above Pacific Place, where natural limestone, warm timber and an understated palette carry through a peaceful sky-retreat.

EXPLORE OUR HOUSE

At Upper House, each team member brings their own ideas and personalities to create unforgettable experiences for our guests. We can't wait to welcome you along on the upward journey that our House is named for.

Simon McHendry

General Manager, Upper House Hong Kong

YOUR STAY

Generous rooms, thoughtful service, and expansive city views.

Our House sits directly above this interchange and above Pacific Place mall, with Hong Kong Park at the door and every room opening to sweeping views of Victoria Harbour or the city.

For a first luxury stay, Admiralty offers a quieter, design-led alternative for travellers who value space and connectivity over waterfront proximity.
