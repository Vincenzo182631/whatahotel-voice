Reviewed by
Ed Peters
Start us off with an overview: what were your first impressions?
One of the city’s most refined addresses, Upper House Hong Kong brings together warm, intuitive hospitality with minimalist design. Designed by Hong Kong wunderkind Andre Fu, the hotel’s serene interiors balance understated Japanese influences with extraordinary harbor, city, and mountain views.
Got it. So tell us about your room.
Our Upper Suite feels more like a private apartment than a hotel room—spacious, light-filled, and impossibly calm. Wrap-around windows frame sweeping views of Victoria Harbour and the surrounding hills. Thoughtful touches—cushioned window seats, a hippo-sized tub, a Jacob Jensen alarm clock, and a backgammon board—round out the experience.
Say we're famished. What are our options?
Up on the 49th floor, Salisterra brings a taste of the Mediterranean to the Hong Kong skyline. Under the direction of House Culinary Advisor Chef Ricardo Chaneton, the menu takes cues from the coasts of Italy, France, and Spain, balancing comforting flavors with refined presentations. Add in arresting views, sun-dappled interiors, and a laid-back energy, and you’ve got one of the most inviting dining rooms in town.
After dinner, head to the Green Room next door, where panoramic views set the scene for cocktails that are simple yet sophisticated. The salon’s weekly Moonlight series brings together Hong Kong’s creative crowd for drinks, conversation, and genre-hopping DJ performances.
How did you find service overall?
All guests are whisked upstairs; check-in happens seamlessly in-room, where the how-tos are courteously explained. That a maintenance worker paused in his task to wish me "good morning" and open the door to The Lawn, a tucked-away garden oasis serving up light bites and drinks, is the best service indicator.
Imagine the other guests are part of a tribe. How would you describe them?
Design devotees, creative professionals, and global nomads who value understatement over flash.
What about the neighborhood? Anything we should know about this hotel's location?
Upper House Hong Kong sits atop Pacific Place mall, one of the city’s premier shopping and dining complexes, with easy access to the MTR. Hong Kong Park and its lush aviary and tea museum are just next door, while trams to The Peak or downtown are a short stroll away. Staying here gives you a genuine sense of the city.
What's the one thing you'd change?
Balconies are an impossibility, but one can dream. We also wouldn’t argue with a pool or a full-service spa, partly because we know Upper House would execute it remarkably well.
Anything else worth calling out?
Rather than a conventional spa, Upper House Hong Kong takes a personal approach to wellness. Guests can book in-room treatments or join one of the hotel’s rotating wellness residencies, which range from sound baths and guided meditation to yoga and longevity-focused therapies.
Sum it up for us: Worth it, and why?
Refined yet refreshingly unpretentious, Upper House Hong Kong offers a masterclass in modern hospitality. It’s one of those rare city hotels that feels both in the heart of the action and cocooned in its own luxurious little world.
