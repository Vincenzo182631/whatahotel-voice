Upper House Shanghai is a design-led luxury hotel in the Dazhongli neighbourhood, in the heart of Jing'an. Thoughtfully designed rooms offer a refined, residential-style retreat for design-conscious travellers, couples and business guests seeking quiet luxury in the heart of Shanghai.
