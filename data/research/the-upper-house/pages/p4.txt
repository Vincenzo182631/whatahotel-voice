CHENGDU

Discover the contrasts of the city amongst the hallowed stone walls of our House.

Upper House Chengdu is built around a preserved heritage courtyard and highly tailored design, set within Taikoo Li, Chengdu's central retail and heritage district.

EXPLORE OUR HOUSE

Chengdu is an extraordinary place where the past and present live side by side. It’s a sense of place that has inspired how we see wellness, gastronomy and art – an influence we hope you encounter during your stay.

CHRISTIAN KRONAST

General Manager, Upper House Chengdu

YOUR STAY

Upper House is designed on an intimate scale, with just 100 rooms and 42 serviced apartments, in gentle contrast to the several hundred rooms found in many larger luxury hotels across Chengdu.

Every space is generously proportioned, considerately served and thoughtfully appointed, with a design language drawn from Chengdu's own culture rather than any standardised international template. It is a stay shaped by this particular place, an experience larger, uniform hotels rarely offer.
