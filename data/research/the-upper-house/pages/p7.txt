## THE HOUSE COLLECTIVE JOINS THE SET COLLECTION AS MEMBER HOTELS

Upper House has joined The Set Collection as the first partner to sit alongside its prominent founding member hotels. This exciting collaboration will broaden our network beyond Asia.

## About The Set Collection

The Set Collection comprises of some of the world’s best independent hotels and resorts at the most oustanding addresses. Its founding members - Hotel Cafe Royal in London, Conservatorium in Amsterdam, Lutetia in Paris and Mamilla hotel in Jerusalem offer both livable design rooted in the destinations and tailored individual experiences - something that we deeply value and resonate with as a brand.
