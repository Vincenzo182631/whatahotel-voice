## Live like royalty off the Maui shoreline

We’re a private, gated community across from the Grand Wailea, A Waldorf Astoria Resort. Offering spacious villas, our property blends residential living with resort amenities, including a sparkling pool with sandy beach entry, waterfalls, and a children’s pool. Guests also enjoy access to amenities at Grand Wailea, A Waldorf Astoria Resort.
