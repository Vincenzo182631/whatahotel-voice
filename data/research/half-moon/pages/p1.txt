# Welcome Back Home

Set on 400 acres of land of wood and water, with two miles of private beachfront, Half Moon is Jamaica’s highest-rated resort, a biophilic sanctuary where space and privacy are our intention and your luxury. Individually appointed accommodations, most with sweeping sea views, invite guests to discover where they truly belong.

At Half Moon, elevated experiences await: the Caribbean’s largest spa oasis, a championship golf course, and award-winning dining. With distinctive restaurants, immersive wellness programmes, elevated activities, and experiences that inspire, your days are yours to design. Half Moon offers an escape that transcends the ordinary, where time slows, space expands, and every experience is shaped by the moments that matter.

### Now Open

Framed by the glistening Caribbean Sea to the north and the lush hillsides to the south, guests may opt for _Eclipse at Half Moon_ or the newly reimagined, _Villas at Half Moon_.
