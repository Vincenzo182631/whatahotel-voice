# Delmare

Eclipse

Elegantly and dramatically appointed with local artwork, this award-winning seafood restaurant with Italian influences overlooks the pristine white sand beach of Eclipse at Half Moon. Featuring the freshest catches, including Red Snapper, Grouper, and Shrimp, the restaurant seamlessly blends fine dining with one of the Caribbean’s most breathtaking backdrops.

Evening Dress Etiquette: Smart casual – collared shirt, dress or casual top, long trousers, or Bermuda shorts. Torn jeans, flip-flops, T-shirts, cutoffs, and cargo shorts are not permitted.
