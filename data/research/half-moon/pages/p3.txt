# Founders Cove

Historically a playground for the world’s elite, Founders Cove was established in 1954, during an era widely noted for its gentility and grace. With a limited number of exclusive “cottages” – ten, to be precise – this coveted destination quickly became the “it” place for white glove service, palatial amenities and a sundrenched subtropical setting preferred by kings and queens, power-brokers and captains of industry. Our collection of rooms and suites feature the newly remodeled Hibiscus neighbourhood, where the prestige ocean junior suites are mere steps away from the Caribbean Sea.
