# Hayward's

Eclipse

Offering both views of the Caribbean Sea and the Great House Grand Lawn, this open-kitchen restaurant offers prepared-on-the-spot dishes for breakfast and lunch\* in an alfresco setting. Jamaican-inspired, yet worldly in its influence, it features a variety of dining areas.

Dress etiquette: Smart casual - collared shirt, dress or casual, long trousers and Bermuda shorts; no torn jeans, flip-flops, t-shirts, cutoffs, casual or cargo shorts allowed.

#### **HOURS**

Breakfast (A la carte) Mon – Thu \| 7:00 AM -10:30 AM

Breakfast (Buffet) Fri – Sun \| 7:00 AM -10:30 AM

Dinner (A la carte) Mon – Sun \*closed Fri \| 5:30 PM – 10:00 PM
