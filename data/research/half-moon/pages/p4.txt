# Welcome Home

Half Moon features three distinctive accommodation experiences. Framed by the glistening Caribbean Sea to the north and the lush hillsides to the south, guests may opt for _Eclipse at Half Moon_ or the newly renovated _Villas at Half Moon_. There are 210 elegantly appointed rooms and suites, and 19 villas with easy access to resort amenities, along two miles of beachfront.

Nestled between the glistening Caribbean Sea and lush southern hillsides, Eclipse at Half Moon stands as one of the Caribbean's most luxurious new additions. This exquisite property boasts 57 spacious accommodations, two restaurants, three bars, a market café, the renowned Fern Tree - a Salamander Spa, a stunning infinity-edge pool, and a private beachfront with a natural swimming cove.

Learn more about Eclipse

Villas at Half Moon are your home away from home, just 15 minutes from Montego Bay’s Sangster International Airport. Each villa offers spacious, private accommodations with a cook, housekeeper, and butler. Enjoy a private pool, outdoor living space, and  to 7 bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms.

Learn more about Villas

Historically a playground for the world’s elite, Founders Cove was established in 1954, during an era widely noted for its gentility and grace. This exclusive destination, with just ten luxurious "cottages," quickly became renowned for its impeccable service, opulent amenities, and stunning subtropical surroundings. Today, our newly remodeled Hibiscus resort offers a collection of prestigious ocean junior suites, just steps from the Caribbean Sea, continuing our tradition of gentility and grace.

### Newly Renovated Villas

Our villas are located just 15 minutes away from Montego Bay’s Sangster International Airport (MBJ), and they offer the perfect combination of space and privacy. Our villas are self-catering and fully staffed with a cook, housekeeper and butler. Each one features a private pool, outdoor living space, 5-, 6- or 7 bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms, and living and dining rooms.
