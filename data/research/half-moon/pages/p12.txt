## Traditional Hatha Yoga Iyengar Style

Hatha includes most yoga styles. This ancient system includes the practice of asanas (steady and comfortable yoga postures) and pranayama (breathing). We welcome peace within the mind and body, preparing the body for deeper spiritual practices. This - combined with lyengar's intense focus on the subtleties of each posture - allows closer attention to musculoskeletal alignment within each asana.
