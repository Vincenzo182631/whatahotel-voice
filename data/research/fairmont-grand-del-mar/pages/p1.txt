## A soulful Sanctuary Where Finding Yourself Comes _Naturally_

Experience magic at every turn as you walk amidst a grove of eucalyptus trees and marvel over a waterfall cascading through ancient volcanic rock. At Fairmont Grand Del Mar, located on 400 acres alongside Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve near Torrey Pines and Carmel Valley, you will be swept away by nature’s majestic beauty.

Bask in the San Diego sunshine and enjoy our five-star setting at Fairmont Grand Del Mar. Explore two dozen weekly outdoor activities including hiking, horseback trailrides, yoga, meditation and archery.  Hit the links on our 18-hole private Tom Fazio-designed golf course, then embrace well-being at our five-star spa. Lounge with a cool drink by one of four secluded palm-lined pools and dine on local artisan fare at six different venues, including San Diego’s only three-Michelin starred restaurant, Addison by William Bradley.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Grand Del Mar?

Yes, our hotel offers accessible accommodations to our guests. Please contact us for further details prior to your stay.

What are some of the property amenities at Fairmont Grand Del Mar?

Our 5-star hotel's general amenities include six restaurants, car rental service available upon request from the concierge, concierge services, gift shops, a full-service spa, laundry service, valet service, luggage hold and golf bag storage, room service, and an in-room safe.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Grand Del Mar?

Check-in is at 4:00 pm and check-out is at 11:00 am. Pacific Standard Time.

Where is Fairmont Grand Del Mar located?

Fairmont Grand Del Mar is located at 5300 Grand Del Mar Court, San Diego, CA 92130 in the Del Mar area.

Does Fairmont Grand Del Mar have a swimming pool?

Yes, there are four pools at Fairmont Grand Del Mar.
