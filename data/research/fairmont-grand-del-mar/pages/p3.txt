Why book?
Fairmont Grand Del Mar pairs Mediterranean-inspired elegance with spacious resort amenities. Set adjacent to the Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve just off the coast of the Pacific, it offers refined on-site dining—including San Diego’s only Michelin-starred restaurant—championship golf, and a serene spa destination, all with attentive service and ample space to unwind.
Set the scene
Surrounded by the Los Peñasquitos Canyon and tucked away from the crowds of beachgoers, this resort feels like a separate enclave itself. Guests skew more towards affluent—couples celebrating anniversaries, golf and spa enthusiasts, and families enjoying multiple days of pampering and onsite activities rather than quick city stops.
The backstory
Part of Fairmont’s portfolio of luxury properties that blend distinctive design and local character, Grand Del Mar channels Californian-meets-Mediterranean architecture with craftsmanship and elegance. Grand Del Mar has long been recognized as one of California’s premier resort destinations, particularly by golf aficionados.
The rooms
Guest rooms and suites here feel spacious and tranquil, with many offering private balconies overlooking lush landscape and the golf course. Larger suites and villas are ideal for families or extended stays, with some courtyard views offering a quieter, more secluded feel.
Food and drink
Addison is notable as Southern California’s only three-star Michelin restaurant, offering an exquisite tasting menu perfect for special evenings. Amaya offers refined California-Mediterranean cuisine, casual poolside fare, and inventive cocktails during happy hour.
The spa
The Forbes Five-Star–rated Fairmont Spa & Wellness features a 21,000-square-foot wellness facility with massages, hydrotherapy, aromatherapy, and restorative lounges before and after treatments. Other amenities include saunas, whirlpools, steam rooms, and an adults-only relaxation pool.
The neighborhood/area
While not beachfront, the resort is close to Del Mar’s beautiful beaches, boutique shopping, and world-class surf breaks if you’re up for light day trips. Nearby, you can explore Torrey Pines State Reserve trails, Del Mar racetrack events, and the vibrant One Paseo culinary district.
The service
Service here is polished and thoughtful, matching Fairmont’s global reputation. Concierge can arrange golf tee times, spa experiences, private excursions, and dining reservations, as well as custom experiences like outdoor concerts at the Grand Social space or wellness activities on the lawn.
Accessibility
Accessible guest rooms and public areas are offered, with wheelchair accessible rooms specially adapted for physically challenged guests. Additionally, housekeeping can provide guests with raised toilet seats and shower chairs during their stay.
Anything left to mention?
The hotel’s Grand Social seasonal outdoor events offer lawn games, live music, and craft cocktails during the summer months. Guests can also reserve a personal cabana and private fire pit for an even more memorable evening.
