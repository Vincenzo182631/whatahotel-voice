## A Collection of Suites that Inspire

Choose one of our stylish suites and add a subtle touch of luxury to your stay.

### Paderewski Suite

Paderewski Suite is an exceptional and vivid place where history and elegance meet. Named after famous composer and Prime Minister of Poland, Ignacy J. Paderewski – the first owner of the hotel, the suite consists of a spacious bedroom, living room and dining area designed with a light color scheme, two grey marbled bathrooms and the balcony offering unforgettable view to the most fashionable street...See More in Warsaw – the Royal Route. All guests staying with us in this unique suite receive a vast array of benefits: a welcome amenity, a complimentary minibar upon arrival, Mercedes Limousine transportation from/to the Warsaw Chopin Airport and a complimentary second treatment in Bristol spa.See Less

## Junior Suite

Discover luxury and comfort in our Junior Suites, featuring a stylish living room and spacious bedroom, arranged over a maximum of 46 m2. Adorned with timeless Art Deco style and modern design, they offer stunning views to our tranquil green patio. Indulge in unlimited access to our Bristol spa facilities and a complimentary welcome amenity for the perfect relaxing stay.

Explore

## Bristol Suite

Our opulent Bristol Suites offer tranquil views of our luxuriant courtyard or the enchanting park through large arched windows. With up to 54 m2 of spacious layout, these suites comprise a separate bedroom and living room, providing the perfect balance of comfort and privacy. Guests staying in Bristol Suite receive a warm welcome amenity and a taste of our famous Tort Bristol patisserie.

Explore

### Studio Suite

Indulge in luxury with our Studio Suites, featuring a timeless Art Deco decor with modern design elements. Enjoy spacious accommodations of approximately 59 m2, including a comfortable sleeping area, stylish living room with a 4-seater dining table, and luxurious bathrooms with a walk-in shower and relaxing bathtub. With a warm welcome amenity, a complimentary welcome drink, and a unique view of the historical Royal Route, our Studio Suites offer the perfect combination of elegance and comfort.

### Grand Suite

Our Grand Suites embody the timeless Art Deco style of our hotel, featuring elegant and modern decor and offering trademark views of the Royal Route, a cosy courtyard or the lush park through large arched windows. These spacious suites offer up to 54 m2 of luxurious living space, ensuring a truly relaxing retreat. As part of the experience, guests staying in Grand Suite receive a welcome amenity and enjoy a Mercedes limousine transfer to Warsaw Chopin Airport (one-way).

Explore

## Glorietta Suite

This unique two-bedroom corner suite embodies the quintessence of contemporary luxury and ultimate comfort. Treat the family to a special time together by staying in the heart of Warsaw in one of our most spacious suites, which consists of two separate bedrooms with walk-in closet, elegant living room with dining table and sitting area, two marble bathrooms and additional guest toilet. Guests staying in our unique Glorietta Suite will enjoy an array of additional benefits inclusive of a welcome amenity, a complimentary minibar upon arrival and a complimentary Mercedes Limousine transportation from/to the Warsaw Chopin Airport.
