Family Activities & Amenities

Food & Beverage

Children’s menu is available in Column Bar & Restaurant, Café Bristol, and through room service.

Highchairs are available in our restaurant and Café Bristol, and these can also be delivered to hotel room through the 24-hour room service.

Drawing kit.

Rooms

We offer interconnecting rooms and suites to accommodate all family members, featuring queen beds (160 cm x 200 cm), king beds (180 cm x 200 cm) or twin beds (120 cm x 200 cm).

Four our youngest guests, complimentary cribs are available for children up to three years old. Please note that guests must provide their own blankets, bumpers and pillows for cribs.

Additional rollaway beds can be arranged for an additional fee of PLN 350 per night per bed, plus tax. These are available upon request in Executive rooms and suites.

Children up to 12 years old may share an adult’s room for no additional fee, where no additional bedding is required.

We also offer special accommodation packages for families.

For added convenience, a bottle warmer is available for families with infants.

Other Services

Our Concierge team has prepared a special Welcome City Guide with Warsaw’s top recommendations for children, including child-friendly attractions, museums and shows, with available discounts, a list of kids’ shops and shopping centers.

Assistance with arranging the tickets to city attractions.

Recommended hotel transportation service including child’s seats.

Immediate medical care available round the clock.

A children’s hospital located a five-minute drive from the hotel.

A PS3 console and a wide range of games available to rent.

Parking

We have limited parking spaces.
