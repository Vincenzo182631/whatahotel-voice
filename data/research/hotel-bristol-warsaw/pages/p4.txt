### Timeless Décor

Café Bristol exudes with history and charm. Its cream walls feature elegant wainscoting, while the striking cream, maroon, and black checkerboard floor is the most distinctive design element. The space is adorned with sinuous Art Deco-style sofas, marble-top tables, and wooden chairs, complemented by a wall of towering street-facing windows.

### Sweet Masterpieces

Brass accents and polished dark wood form a marble-top, mirror-backed bar spliced by crystal showcases, housing our pastry chef’s famous, freshly baked creations. Numerous delicacies—from the signature millefeuille with raspberries to the prestigious Tort Bristol—are celebrated as the city’s premier desserts.

### Heritage

For generations, this legendary Café has been a cherished gathering place where the most meaningful moments have unfolded. Its walls have witnessed the brightness and warmth of our guests’ shared memories and continue to enchant with a unique atmosphere.

## Tort Bristol

The story of Tort Bristol began in 1901, when the first guests were welcomed to the legendary Hotel Bristol along Warsaw’s historic Royal Route. For more than a century, the hotel has stood at the center...See More of the city’s cultural and social life, hosting distinguished visitors, celebrations, and cherished moments. Throughout the years, Café Bristol has earned a reputation for exceptional pastry craftsmanship, delighting guests with creations inspired by tradition, artistry, and the finest ingredients. Its treasured recipes, developed by pastry chefs and passed down through the decades, have become an integral part of the hotel’s heritage. Tort Bristol is a tribute to this legacy, bringing together flavors that reflect the history of Hotel Bristol. The cake features layers of Polish plums, hazelnut cream with a delicate crunch, light sponge cake, and a smooth chocolate finish, creating a timeless dessert that celebrates over 125 years of hospitality and culinary excellence.See Less

## Tort Bristol by Jakub Józef Orliński

To celebrate the 125th anniversary of Hotel Bristol, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Warsaw, the legendary Tort Bristol returns in a special limited edition created in collaboration with Jakub Józef Orliński,...See More the internationally acclaimed countertenor and proud Warsaw native. Inspired by the hotel’s rich artistic and musical heritage, this unique partnership blends tradition with contemporary creativity, offering a fresh interpretation of one of the hotel’s most beloved culinary icons. Known for his extraordinary talent, charisma, and passion for artistic expression, Orliński brings a modern perspective to the classic Tort Bristol while honoring its historic roots. The result is a refined and memorable dessert experience that reflects both the elegance of Hotel Bristol and the vibrant spirit of today’s Warsaw. Available exclusively at Café Bristol, this anniversary edition invites guests to discover a unique meeting of music, culture, and craftsmanship in every slice.See Less
