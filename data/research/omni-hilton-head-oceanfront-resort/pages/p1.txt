## Hilton Head, South Carolina

### An idyllic oceanfront resort on Hilton Head Island

Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront Resort embodies the essence of its location, overlooking the Atlantic Ocean, a beautiful beach and three championship golf courses. As part of the prestigious Palmetto Dunes Oceanfront Resort Community, it’s a standout destination among Hilton Head resorts. With access to 11 miles of lagoon trails, tennis courts, private beach access, sparkling swimming pools and wellness areas, guests can fill their days with enriching experiences. Charming guest accommodations are decorated with a neutral palette augmented by ocean views and splashes of color, creating a unique setting among hotels and resorts in Hilton Head. Dining venues are a blend of fine and casual, with creative chefs serving everything from prime steaks and seafood to Stance coffee and snacks. Located on the #1 ranked US island by Conde Nast Traveler, Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront Resort is the premier Hilton Head beach resort, making it the ultimate destination for island getaways and celebratory events.

## Traveler's Insights

### What are the property amenities at Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront Resort?

Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront Resort is home to world-class amenities including:

- Adult, family and splash pools
- Private beach access
- A full-service spa
- 54 holes of championship golf
- Full-sized tennis and pickleball courts
- A fully-equipped fitness center
- 14,000 square feet of meeting and event space
- Five dining outlets - two full-service restaurants, a grab-and-go market and two pool bars
- Family-friendly resort activities and kids camp
- Complimentary self-parking and valet parking with a nightly charge

### DOES OMNI HILTON HEAD OCEANFRONT RESORT HAVE A POOL?

Yes, Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront Resort has an adult pool, two family pools and a kids’ splash pad.

### WHERE IS OMNI HILTON HEAD OCEANFRONT RESORT LOCATED?

Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront Resort is ideally located on Hilton Head Island, just off the coast of South Carolina. Easily accessible by both air and land, the area is a thriving and popular resort destination, yet an easy commute from several major cities. The charm of Savannah, Georgia, is within 45 minutes of our resort, Beaufort is 30 minutes away and Charleston is just two hours north. The physical address is 23 Ocean Lane, Hilton Head, South Carolina 29928.

### WHAT IS THE CLOSEST AIRPORT TO OMNI HILTON HEAD OCEANFRONT RESORT?

Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront Resort is located near the following airports:

- About 6 miles from Hilton Head Airport (HHH)

- About 42 miles from Savannah/Hilton Head International Airport (SAV)

### How much is parking at Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront Resort?

- Self-parking is available at no additional cost.

- Valet parking is available for $30

- A gate fee is required for all commercial vehicles.

### DOES THE RESORT OFFER TRANSPORTATION SERVICES?

Yes, Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront Resort offers a variety of transportation services including a charged taxi service to and from Savanah/Hilton Head Airport and complimentary shuttle service to Palmetto Dunes and Shelter Cove. Learn more.

### WHAT TIME IS CHECK-IN AND CHECK-OUT?

- Check-in is at 4 PM
- Check-out is at 11 AM.
- Guests must be 21+ to check-in. Photo ID is required at check-in.

### IS THE RESORT PET FRIENDLY?

Yes, Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront Resort is a pet friendly resort for dogs. There is a one-time non-refundable pet fee of $200 per reservation for up to one dog. Service animals accompanying guests with disabilities are always welcome and will not be required to pay the one-time pet charge (no documentation necessary). Read more of our resort policies.
