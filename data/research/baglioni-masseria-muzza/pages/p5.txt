## Spa Etiquette

Aura Spa is open daily from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Guests of Baglioni Masseria Muzza Resort & Spa enjoy complimentary access to The Water Journey as part of their stay.
To ensure a seamless and personalized experience, we recommend booking in advance.
Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled treatment and inform us of any health conditions, allergies, or pregnancy when making your reservation.
Access to the spa and fitness center is restricted to guests over the age of 18. Minors may access these areas only if they are accompanied by an adult who has signed a liability waiver.
