# TASTEFUL MOMENTS IN THE HEART OF SALENTO

At Baglioni Masseria Muzza, Puglian and Mediterranean traditions come to life through experiences designed for every moment of the day. From the refined creations of Ristorante Mirto to aperitifs at Sunset Bar, cocktails and light dishes by the pool, and a rich calendar of seasonal events, every moment is an invitation to savour the quiet beauty of the Salento countryside.

## A NEW SEASON OF GASTRONOMIC EVENTS

Discover the true essence of Puglia at Baglioni Masseria Muzza, with a rich calendar of gastronomic experiences. From cooking classes to dedicated menus, wine tastings, sunset aperitifs and evenings with traditional music and local flavours, each event celebrates the region’s ingredients and the authentic Mediterranean spirit

Enjoy unforgettable moments in the enchanting setting of our resort, surrounded by the colours and scents of Salento’s gentle hills.
