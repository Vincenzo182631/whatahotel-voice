## The Enduring Charm of Authentic Puglia

Located near Otranto, and surrounded by verdant olive groves and the Sea, Baglioni Masseria Muzza Resort & Spa, is a historic property nestled deep in the Salento countryside.

## Aura Spa & Wellness Center

Treat yourself to serene moments at Aura Spa where you can schedule massages, beauty rituals & wellness treatments. Plus, take advantage of the modern equipment in our Fitness Center.

## Refined Suites and Rooms

Whitewashed walls and exposed wooden beams define the understated elegance of the resort’s 41 rooms and suites. Spacious and comfortable, each accommodation is designed to offer a relaxing stay immersed in the timeless charm of Salento.

## Deluxe Suite

Bright and refined, featuring a double bed and futon, a bathroom with hydromassage shower and chromotherapy, and a large private entrance patio. An intimate space of comfort and harmony.

## Loved This Gem in Puglia!

The property is beautiful and peaceful. The staff were amazing - friendly, attentive, and very helpful. The room we stayed in was lovely, the bed and pillows were very comfortable. The pool area had stunning views of the countryside, and the pool itself was excellent….a very relaxing atmosphere. We loved every second of our stay here. I would definitely stay here again if I’m ever back in the area!
