Getting Around

### Off Road Vehicles

With an off-road vehicle, you can tour the entire coastline in under three hours. Koh Samui’s main road, which is numbered 4169 and known locally as the “Ring Road,” takes you over hills and through forests to the stunning landscapes on the northern and eastern shores. Whatever/Whenever® will be thrilled to get you started on your adventure with a map and recommendations to the island’s main attractions.

### Ferry Service

Ferries departing from Surat Thani every 30 minutes will get you to the island within 90 minutes. From Koh Samui’s Na Thon Pier, it’s easy to catch a taxi or Songthaew to the hotel, although expect several stops on the latter.

### Taxis And Songthaews

Taxis and songthaews—mini-buses converted from pick-ups—will get you to the hotel in true Thai fashion. When taking a cab, make sure to use one that is licensed. We recommend that you only take a songthaew if you are traveling light, as there is often not much room for several big pieces of luggage. Make sure you have agreed upon a price with the driver before committing to the journey.

## Pet Escape at W Koh Samui

Bring your furry companion along for the getaway. At W Koh Samui, pets are warmly welcomed as part of the family. Our pet-friendly resort invites you and your four-legged friend to relax, explore, and enjoy island living together.

To ensure a comfortable stay for all guests, we welcome up to 1 pet per villa with a maximum weight of 18 kg.

Details

Welcome to the first W in Thailand. Remixing Koh Samui’s iconic natural beauty with landmark architecture and signature W features and amenities, the resort dramatically reinvents island living.

Stop by anytime. A sublime architectural tribute to the majestic Maenam Beach on the island's spectacular northern coast, this visionary resort is the revelation of world-renowned MAPS Design Studio, with interiors created by the award-winning firm P49 Deesign. Harmonizing with the natural environs, the resort features plush public spaces straddling the crystal blue Gulf of Thailand and 74 villas with intimate private pools, top technology, and intuitive Thai touches. Uniting sparkling sands with championship kite and windsurfing waters, the resort sets the stage for play and relaxation by day, while signature restaurants, sensuous bars, and exhilarating events light up the night in true full-moon style.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Koh Samui?

The check-in time at W Koh Samui is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Koh Samui allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Koh Samui. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W Koh Samui?

On-site parking is available at W Koh Samui. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Koh Samui have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Koh Samui has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Koh Samui booking.

What is the closest airport near W Koh Samui?

The closest airport near W Koh Samui is Koh Samui Airport (USM). USM is located approximately 9.4 KM from the hotel.

Does W Koh Samui have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Koh Samui does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
