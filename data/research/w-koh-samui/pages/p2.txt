## Accommodations

Come on in. Each of our 74 luxury pool villas in Koh Samui features a private patio and pool, sweeping sea and island views, custom furniture, state-of-the-art technology and oversized bathrooms with exclusive MOMO sink-side six-bath amenities—all curated by award-winning designer Carl Almeida from P49 Design. Help yourself to W MixBar, Sweet Spot, complimentary ice cream and beverages, High-Speed...See More Internet Access and complimentary beach bags with W flip-flops, water and more at our luxury resort in Koh Samui. Or take it easy in Thailand on your villa's king-sized signature W pillow top beds with fluffy goose-down comforters, 350-thread-count Egyptian cotton sheets and a W pillow menu. ### Grand Residence Pool Villa

The possibilities are endless in the private paradise of our one-of-a-kind GRAND RESIDENCE POOL VILLA, a two-story, four-bedroom luxury pool villa in Koh Samui. Behold stunning beach views from an expansive balcony comprising an infinity pool, loungers and a sun bed. On the same level are the furnished outdoor spaces of the two guest bedrooms, while the dining room and both master bedrooms have separate balconies.
