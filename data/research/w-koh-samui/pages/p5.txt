Make the season yours at W Koh Samui with Made the List, where festive traditions get a W twist. Set against the stunning Gulf of Thailand, discover extraordinary dining, vibrant nightlife, and island-inspired celebrations. From the grand festive tree lighting and exotic festive market to indulgent Christmas feasts at The Kitchen Table and contemporary Japanese dining at NAMU, every moment brings a...See More new reason to celebrate. Savor sunset beach BBQs, sip expertly mixed cocktails, and feel the energy at WOOBAR. As the year draws to a close, turn up the glamour with an opulent New Year’s Eve dinner, an international guest DJ, dazzling fireworks, and bubbly to toast to what’s next.
