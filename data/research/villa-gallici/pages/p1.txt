# An exquisite haven of peace

_"The Villa Gallici embodies luxury and the fine art of Italian hospitality style. Located in the most popular area of ​​Aix-en-Provence, this Provencal mansion is a real invitation to travel, enjoy serenity and relaxation for an exceptional stay"_

## A baroque and sophisticated universe

Immerse yourself in a world of magic, where East meets West, and where Provence brings to mind an Italian-style dream. A real haven of peace, where everything has been done to ensure that you have an unforgettable stay.

Book your stay

The hotel _La Villa Gallici_ welcomes you to an elegant 18th century residence. This **5-star Relais & Châteaux** hotel offers top-of-the-range facilities combined with service bordering on excellence. The country home opens out on to a garden filled with the sweet fragrances of Provence, with cypress trees sheltering the outdoor swimming pool. On the patio, the plane trees offer welcoming shade when the restaurant's dining room is dressed in its most beautiful finery.

The spirit of Villa Gallici, a Provençal residence dating from the 18th century, is embodied through a refined baroque decoration which make this bastide a real place of enchantment.

Enjoy a unique experience at _La Villa Gallici_, an enchanting and poetic site located just a few minutes' walk from the centre of Aix-en-Provence.

## Hotel restaurant in Aix-en-Provence

La Villa Gallici invites you to dine at its **gourmet restaurant** in Aix-en-Provence. In the shade of the plane trees, enjoy the seasonal cuisine inspired by the tastes of Provence. The Mediterranean flavours are inventively brought together in the meticulously prepared dishes by the **Chef Christophe GAVOT.**

A short walk from the town centre of Aix-en-Provence, enjoy a delicious treat at the hotel's restaurant. The terrace's enchanting setting, the delicate tableware, the gentle light of the glimmering candles and service as discreet as it is efficient, help to ensure you unique experience.
