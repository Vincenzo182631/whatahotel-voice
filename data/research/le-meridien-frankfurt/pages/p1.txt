# Welcome to Le Méridien Frankfurt

## Explore the city from one of the best hotels in Frankfurt

Located next to the main train station, Le Méridien Frankfurt is a hotel made for city trips and business visits. Blending historic architecture with modern design after its 2025 renovation, this hotel...See More in Frankfurt offers easy access to the city center, fairgrounds, restaurants, shops, and sightseeing routes. As a Frankfurt business hotel in the city center guests can rely on, it features 220 elegant rooms in the Legacy wing and 80 fully refurbished Palais rooms with high ceilings and newly designed interiors. Guests praise our Frankfurt hotel's central location, friendly service, and varied breakfast. Enjoy BBQ and smoked specialties at The Legacy Bar & Grill, then explore Frankfurt's art scene through Unlock Art™, with exclusive access to SCHIRN.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Frankfurt?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Frankfurt is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Frankfurt allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Frankfurt. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 25.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Frankfurt?

On-site self parking is available at Le Méridien Frankfurt. Self parking is available at Le Méridien Frankfurt, with rates of €4.00 per hour, and €40.00 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near Le Méridien Frankfurt. The daily valet fee is €40.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Frankfurt have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Frankfurt has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Frankfurt booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Frankfurt?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Frankfurt is Frankfurt Airport (FRA). FRA is located approximately 12.7 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Frankfurt have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Le Méridien Frankfurt has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
