## Running Along the River Main

The river Main is only a short walk or quick run from Le Méridien Frankfurt and the perfect running + jogging path in the city. Just leave the hotel on your left hand side and go straight until you reach the Main. If you prefer to run on the city side or the Sachsenhausen embankment is your choice - both sides are predestined for a run at any time of the day.

Museum Embankment

### Cultural Hot Spot

With 26 museums, the Museum Embankment in Frankfurt is one of the most important and diverse museum locations in Europe. It offers everything from Old Masters and modern art to film, photography, architecture, and design. Beyond the museums, the riverbanks of the Main are perfect for strolling. Thanks to the proximity of Le Méridien Frankfurt to the Main riverbanks, many museums are just a few minutes' walk away.

## Frankfurt Fairs

Some of Europe's most important fairs take place in Frankfurt. Within walking distance to the fairgrounds and within easy reach to the main station and Frankfurt Airport Le Méridien Frankfurt is the perfect spot for your fair and exhibition stays.

Within Walking Distance of the Fair

### Messe Frankfurt

Messe Frankfurt offers more than 50 internationally significant trade fairs such as IAA, Bookfair or the Ambiente fair. The fairground itself is the third largest exhibition ground in the world and is located right in the city center. Le Méridien Frankfurt is within easy reach. It's just a 15 minutes walk or a short ride by public transportation (two stops by suburban train, one stop by underground train or three stops by tram). It's only a 5 minutes drive by taxi to the different entrances of Messe Frankfurt. Frankfurt's main station with its Deutsche Bahn long-distance connections, is in the direct vicinity of the hotel. From there, it's only a 20-minute ride to the airport. Arrive right where you want to start your visit.
