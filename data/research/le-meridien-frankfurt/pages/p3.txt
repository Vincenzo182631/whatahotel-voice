### The Legacy Restaurant

The Legacy Bar & Grill Restaurant next to Frankfurt's main station at Wiesenhüttenplatz in the heart of the hippest districts of Frankfurt. BBQ and specialties from the smoker. Vegetarians and vegans are welcome, too! Reserve a table to make sure you'll have a smoky evening!

### Best Ribs in Town

We proudly present our smoked specialties: Juicy spare ribs St. Louis Cut and tender pulled pork. Smoked for a minimum of eight hours in spicy flavor of 100% hickory wood and rested 24 hours in a barbecue marinade to develop their delicate taste. Spare ribs receive final touch inside the Fire Magic Smoker, heated with wood charcoal. It has been imported from the US, a rarity in Europe.

### The Legacy Bar

Into the night: Where your evening starts. Or ends. Or both. Enjoy cocktail classics, longdrinks of best quality, smokey cocktails and a well selected beer and wine selection. Best bar food is served directly from the Legacy kitchen. Your host, Miguel and his team guarantee you are in the best of company. And best of location to move on or to stay.

## Cocktail Classics

Signature Drinks Mixologist Creations. Whatever your favorite drink is: The bar team is thrilling to mix, stir or shake it for you. Try one of the smokey cocktails like Rob Roy Smokey, Negroni or Skye and Sea. They are prepared with a smoking gun - the preparation is spectacular!

### Breakfast Room

What about breakfast? Our huge breakfast buffet is served daily in the breakfast room Cloud from 6:30 am until 10:30 am. Fresh pastry, an omelette from the live cooking station, cereals, juices and coffee specialties - the variety will set your heart aflutter. That's for sure.

## But First, Coffee

Grab your daily cup of finest illy coffee at Le Méridien breakfast or use your coffee voucher in the discovery envelope on your bed for an illy coffee specialty at Le Méridien Hub during the day. Our Master Barista will kindly help you to decide which one you'll love.

## Which Breakfast Type Are You?

Healthy, sweet, and substantial food—no matter what type of breakfast you prefer, we have something for everyone. Enjoy cereals and granola, a live cooking station with omelettes, fried and scrambled eggs, an Asian corner, and fresh vegetables like avocados, to name just a few options. There's no better way to start the day!
