### Beautiful property, pity to drink and food.

The stay itself was fantastic, and the views are absolutely first-class. It’s definitely worth experiencing! However, as a frequent traveler, I noticed a couple of areas that could be improved.

First, the breakfast didn’t quite meet expectations. The selection was quite limited; there were very few à la carte options, and it really lacked local Hangzhou culinary specialties.

Second, the rooftop bar has amazing views and a great live band if you enjoy a lively atmosphere, but the drinks unfortunately ruined the vibe. As a discerning drinker, the cocktails were nowhere near the high standard you'd expect from a luxury hotel. They were a major letdown.

Overall, it's a beautiful property, but the food and beverage side definitely needs to step up to match the excellent rooms.
