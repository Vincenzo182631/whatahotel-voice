# Upscale Rooms with Views of the Qiantang River \| Park Hyatt Hangzhou

# Accommodations

Check-In3:00 PM

Checkout12:00 PM

When visiting Park Hyatt Hangzhou, you'll savor a ‘Luxury is Personal’ ambience in each of our 241 guestrooms including 14 lavish suites. Planned by renowned designer Yabu Pushelberg, our rooms feature high ceilings and iconic views of Qiantang River or the Qianjiang New Central Business District. The luxurious bathrooms are outfitted with twin vanities, rain showers, deep-soaking bathtubs, and a separate automatic washlet. Each room includes a walk-in closet and custom-made furniture and art, making your visit to Park Hyatt Hangzhou an effortless experience in luxury.
