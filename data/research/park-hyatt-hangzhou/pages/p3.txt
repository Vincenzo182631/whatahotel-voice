# Restaurants & Bars

From lively rooftop dining with romantic views to cozy homemade fare, Park Hyatt Hangzhou offers a world of dining experiences from one central location.

The Living Room serves all-day refreshments and comfort food, featuring Chinese and Western fare, in a family-friendly atmosphere.
The Dining Room offers Hangzhou, Zhejiang and Cantonese cuisine, elevating classic dishes with creative interpretations.
The iconic Forty8 offers two distinct dining opportunities, plus two bars and a live entertainment destination with outdoor terraces and private dining lounges.
Park Hyatt Hangzhou offers the city’s ultimate dining and drinking experiences at Hangzhou’s most elevated entertainment venues.

## Living Room

Located on level 37, Living Room wraps around the lobby embracing 270-degree panoramic views of the city and Qiantang River. Guests can relax in the serene, naturally-lit social space with five-meter-high windows. Meanwhile, indulge in our signature afternoon tea experience, complete with refined individually served savouries and dessert trolley.

## Dining Room

Located on level 37, Dining Room serves authentic local Zhejiang and Cantonese cuisines crafted by master chefs using seasonal ingredients. Filled with natural light and eye-catching city views, the pastel-hued restaurant is decorated with large glass lanterns and delicate silk panels hand-embroidered by Suzhou artisans. Private dining rooms are also available.

## Forty8 Bar

Forty8 Bar encompasses a standalone a cocktail bar, a whisky bar and a music room with a stage for live music, plus a large outdoor garden terrace featuring three private semi-alfresco dining pavilions. The Ledge is a highly sought-after spot for a romantic rendezvous or proposal, featuring a glass-floored architectural feature extending over the edge of the building. Forty8 Bar also open onto a large outdoor garden terrace with lounge seating, a movie screen and views of the Hangzhou skyline.

## Forty8 Restaurant

Located on level 48, Forty8 Restaurant is styled like a vibrant gourmet marketplace with open Kitchens specializing in fresh seafood, grilled meats, and regional Chinese and Western cuisines. Four private dining rooms with excellent views. Forty8 Restaurant offers variety of course craft lunch with complimentary dessert buffet on weekdays. Celebrate joyous weekend with all you can eat buffet trolley service of freshly cooked gourmet delights delivered to your table, paired with free flow of Perrier jouët grand brut n.v, France champagne.
