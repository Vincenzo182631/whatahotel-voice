Experiences
Breathtaking natural beauty, the opportunity to make a spiritual connection or the chance to escape the everyday draw travelers from around the world to Nusa Dua, Bali. At The Ritz-Carlton, Bali, guests can experience all three. Set along the white-sand beachfront, this Bali beach hotel overlooks the azure waters of the Indian Ocean. This is a place where days can be filled with sunrise yoga, surf lessons, or a peek into a local seaweed farm. Nusa Dua's lush volcanic mountains and verdant rice paddies provide shelter for beautiful ancient temples including Uluwatu, where sunset hosts a traditional Kecak dance each day. Resort guests head out on cultural excursions, but they are also encouraged to discover why Bali is a destination where people seek peace and quiet.
Moments of Meaningful Connection and Tradition
Cultural and Spiritual Experiences
At The Ritz-Carlton, Bali, every experience is thoughtfully curated to reflect refined elegance, cultural authenticity, holistic wellness, and meaningful family connection. It is an immersive journey where the richness of Balinese traditions is seamlessly woven with elevated resort experiences, creating moments of timeless beauty and unforgettable discovery.

Signature Experiences
Journey into Bali’s Living Traditions
Guests are invited to explore a beautifully preserved traditional Balinese home compound, the guide will explain the significance of each structure—the family temple, the northern building for ceremonies, the southern building for daily life, and the eastern building for guests.
The Art of Batik
The guests are invited to explore the batik gallery, learning about techniques like dyeing, printing, brushing, and the intricate canting method. We also observed the traditional weaving process using a manual loom.
Soul Purification
Traditionally referred to as "Melukat," derived from the word ‘Lukat’ which means to purify in Balinese, the soul purification practice is centered on holy water. This spiritual activity is often performed when a Balinese native is about to enter a new phase in their life and the holy water is used to purify and cleanse the body, mind, and soul.
Beyond the Bar: A Sundown Mixology Class
Guests are invited to explore local spirits infused with Indonesia’s rich array of spices and herbs, crafting unique cocktails and recipes they can recreate and enjoy at home.
Guided Wellness Program
Begin each day with a revitalizing morning stretch by the beach, featuring guided yoga, meditation, and light cardio sessions set to the rhythm of the ocean. For a unique sense of movement and flow, enjoy a guided aerial yoga sessions at the Yoga Studio.
Market to Table
The "Market to Table" experience at The Ritz-Carlton, Bali, is a culinary program where guests shop for fresh ingredients at a local market with a chef, then return to the resort to learn how to cook authentic Balinese dishes. Led by Bejana’s experienced chef, the activity culminates in enjoying the meal created during the class, often at a picturesque location overlooking the ocean.
A Sunset Discovery
Embark on a guided cultural excursion curated by our concierge team to explore the majestic Uluwatu Temple, one of Bali’s most revered sea temples perched dramatically on a cliff overlooking the Indian Ocean. As the sun begins to set, witness the enchanting Kecak Dance performance—a mesmerizing blend of rhythm, chanting, and storytelling that brings ancient Balinese legends to life.

Soul Purification
The Ritz-Carlton, Bali is now offering guests the chance to participate in an authentic Soul Purification Ritual. Traditionally referred to as "Melukat," derived from the word ‘Lukat’ which means to purify in Balinese, the soul purification practice is centered on holy water. This spiritual activity is often performed when a Balinese native is about to enter a new phase in their life, is recovering from sickness, or celebrating either one of the “Rites of Passage,” such as marriage or childbirth, and the holy water is used to purify and cleanse the body, mind, and soul.

Soul Purification Details
At The Ritz-Carlton, Bali, the Soul Purification Ritual takes place on the secluded white beaches of the resort in the early morning, when Surya, or God of Sun, is believed to rise. Balinese Hindus believe that the blessings from Surya and Baruna, the God of Ocean, are required for a clean and fresh new beginning, which is why this is performed on the beach.
The spiritual journey begins with a Balinese Priest offering a devotion to both of the Gods, followed by a ‘Tepung Tawar’ ritual, cleansing of the guest’s hands, feet, face, and mouth three times each with the holy water, to symbolically cleanse the body, mind, and soul, and concluded by a prayer. Completing the journey, a piece of three-colored string called Benang Tridatu is tied around the guest’s wrist and worn until it falls off – symbolizing their quest for balance, harmony, and understanding. Guests will be accompanied by a “Sutri”, the priest’s assistant who will lead through entire steps of the ritual and provide consultation on Balinese culture.
Starts 7 a.m. – 7:30 a.m.
Price: IDR 2.500.000 excluding 21% government tax and service charge
Includes full Melukat ceremony, and morning snack.

Ritz Kids®
Endangered Sea Turtle Preservation Program
Each year nesting sea turtles flock to the pristine beaches of the Ritz-Carlton, Bali Resort to lay their eggs. As part of the Community Footprints program, the resort partners with the Bali Sea Turtle Society to protect the hatching sea turtles. A fence is erected around the nest and as the turtles hatch they are transferred to a “temporary home” where they are guarded from predators until big enough to safely enter the sea. Guests are invited to join the turtle release and enjoy that wonderful moment as these delicate creatures splash their way into the nourishing waters of the Indian Ocean. Children lead the way and identify themselves as ambassadors of the environment. Back at the resort’s Ritz Kids Club, turtle-themed decorations generate excitement for the hatching season and teach young guests about the importance of preserving the local environment. We kindly request you to check the availability of this program, due to its seasonal nature.

NYEPI
