Embrace true luxury at our Bali accommodations in Nusa Dua
Suites & Rooms
The Ritz-Carlton, Bali is an oasis of exclusivity overlooking the ocean in Nusa Dua, Bali. With an exclusive offering of hotel rooms and villas, luxury infuses every detail of this beachfront resort. Featuring spacious layouts, Balinese décor, and outdoor spaces with garden views, our suites are ideal for group and family holidays. Villas include private pools where guests can experience the floating breakfast.
