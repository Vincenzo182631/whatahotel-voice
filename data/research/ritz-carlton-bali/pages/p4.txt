Indonesian & Oceanfront Dining in Nusa Dua, Bali
Dining
Experience exceptional oceanfront dining at The Ritz-Carlton, Bali, with each restaurant offering breathtaking sea views from both indoor and open-air settings. Mornings begin at Senses with a generous breakfast made for lingering, while The Beach Grill carries the day into lunch and dinner with relaxed oceanfront dining. In the afternoon, The Ritz-Carlton Lounge & Bar invites a quieter pause over afternoon tea and handcrafted drinks. Along the resort’s shores, Missoni Resort Club brings a vibrant Italian spirit to the coast through an exclusive design collaboration. As evening falls, Bejana by Petty Elliott sets the scene for dinner, celebrating the rich flavors of Indonesia from its dramatic cliff-top setting.

Dining Experiences
Uncelebrated Celebrations
Because it’s never too late to celebrate your special occasions, whether it is anniversaries, birthdays, or other life’s milestone, we are delighted to craft an intimate beach dinner at our beach.
Private Barefoot Barbecue by the Beach
There is nothing like having a private chef to grill some succulent seafood and tender meat for you and your loved ones in this stunning private beach barbecue setting. Relax and enjoy the cool ocean breeze with the toe-in-the-sand sensation.
Glamping Family Barbecue
Laid back and relax with your family with glamorous camping by the beach, illuminated with a bonfire. Let the dedicated Chef prepare some delicious grilling that can be enjoyed while listening to the sound of gentle waves.
Balinese Buffet Night
Delight in a tantalizing array of Balinese delicacies traditionally crafted to preserve authentic flavors. Available every Saturday at Bejana Indonesian Restaurant.
Cooking Class
Discover exotic flavors of Balinese cuisine with Chef Made Karyasa, who conducts these aromatic, engaging sessions in an open-air kitchen. Learn the essential cooking techniques to enhance the taste of the local dishes through spices, seasonings, and herbs.
Afternoon Tea
Enjoy a delectable classic English afternoon tea and local Indonesian treats with a premium tea or coffee selection for a leisurely afternoon at The Ritz-Carlton Lounge and Bar.
Breakfast Is Elevated
Breakfast is elevated at this casual beachfront restaurant in Nusa Dua, where morning meals feature local and international favorites. A la carte delicacies include homemade pastries, tropical fruit, classic waffles, a choice of eggs prepared to perfection, and regional specialties. Diners can sit inside or on the patio, and a private dining room is available for events.
