Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Bali
Discover our beachfront 5-star resort in Nusa Dua
Offering 313 spacious and sumptuously appointed suites and villas, 100 sqm onward, in an elegant tropical ambiance, The Ritz-Carlton, Bali is a Nusa Dua luxury resort benefiting from a stunning clifftop and beachside setting. With tranquil views over the azure waters of the Indian Ocean, guests at this luxury hotel in Nusa Dua, Bali, can indulge in an idyllic romantic, family, or business interlude. There are five stunning dining venues, an indulgent and exotic marine-inspired Spa, a glamourous beachfront chapel, fun and recreational activities at Ritz Kids, and extensive meeting and celebratory event venues for a stay that makes memories that last a lifetime. Complimentary Wi-Fi, access to Ritz Kids (Kids Club) and a daily schedule of recreational activities coupled with refreshments is served every hour by the pool at this luxury resort in Nusa Dua, Bali.

Community Footprints
The Balinese philosophy of Tri Hita Karana embraces the harmonious relationships between humans and God, humans and other humans and humans with nature. This is the core of the Community Footprints program at The Ritz-Carlton, Bali and at the heart of how the Ladies and Gentlemen interact with guests and the community. This beachfront hotel in Nusa Dua, Bali, is committed to assisting those in need in the Sawangan, Nusa Dua area of Bali through programs that leverage the skills and time of our Ladies and Gentlemen.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Bali?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Bali is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bali allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Bali.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Bali?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Bali. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bali have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Bali has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Bali booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Bali?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Bali is I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS). DPS is located approximately 16.8 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bali have an airport shuttle to I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS)?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Bali has an airport shuttle to I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Bali have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Bali does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
