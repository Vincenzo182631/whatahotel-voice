# Bespoke Accommodation

## Accommodations

An ideal getaway awaits you at Cairo's best address, where our iconic butler service will elevate your stay, overlooking sweeping views of the Nile River or Old Cairo from our signature 5-star hotel rooms, serviced apartments and luxury hotel suites in Cairo.

### City Luxury Apartment

The 321 m2 three-bedroom City Apartment has been thoughtfully designed with spacious living and dining areas, separate office, and fully equipped kitchen, ideal for extended stays. Rooms combine residential comfort with arabesque details, from oriental rugs and decorative sconces to rich wood fittings. Furnished with flat-screen TVs in each room, modern technology complements the destination’s historic significance, where views across Old Cairo add immersive authenticity.

### Capital Apartment

Lending themselves to extended stays, and with a choice of one-, two- and three-bedroom options, the Capital Apartments bring luxurious living to Cairo. Sized between 133-300 m2, they have been designed to offer partial views across the Nile River. Each hotel apartment is exquisitely furnished with living and dining areas, including discreet office space, as well as a fully equipped kitchen and walk-in closets. Thoroughly indulgent, some of the bathrooms house a bathtub where compelling views of the Nile River are captured.

### St. Regis Apartment

Two- and three-bedroom serviced St. Regis apartments bring refined living to those seeking exceptional accommodation for an extended stay. Arabesque detailing and rich materials add residential flair to each footprint. Varying in size, the 154 m2 to 214 m2 hotel apartments include living and dining areas, with an office desk, kitchenette, and walk-in closet. In the ensuite bathroom, the judiciously placed bathtub makes the most of the unparalleled views across the river.

### Sky Apartment

Embodying the spirit of Cairo, where floor-to-ceiling windows present compelling views across the skyline, the two- and three-bedroom Sky Apartments range from 154 m2 to 212 m2, offering ideal family accommodation. Exceptionally furnished living and dining areas bring relaxation to the fore, while a useful corner office ensures connectivity. Meal preparation is seamless in the well-equipped and spacious kitchen, while the St. Regis Butler is on hand for any additional needs.

## St. Regis Suite

Entirely individual, the 94 m2 St. Regis Suite brings exceptional panoramic views across the Nile River from vantage points of the bedroom, living room and dining area. Styled with floor-to-ceiling windows the luxury hotel suite is furnished to embrace the landscape, with a comfortable chaise longue in the bedroom, primed for relaxation. A handcrafted mosaic mural adds to the splendor of the bathroom, where a flat screen TV has been positioned, allowing guests to luxuriate in the bathtub and unwind.

## Astor Suite

The 75-88 m2 Astor Suite presents a seamless lifestyle with a spacious floorplan and indulgent views across the Nile River and hotel swimming pool. The comfortable bedroom with a signature St. Regis King-size bed is dressed with a walk-in closet, while the living and dining areas have an impressive Nile River aspect. Every detail has been thoughtfully tailored, down to the Orient-inspired materials, useful workspace, custom-created bathroom mosaic, and exemplary butler service.

## Metropolitan Suite

The Metropolitan Suite has a corner aspect and at 66 m2 offers understated living with a partial view of the Nile River. This hotel suite is elegantly furnished where the bedroom is styled with signature St. Regis King-size bed, while the separate living area adds to the sense of privacy and relaxation. Styled to create a haven away from the city, materials have been chosen for their local significance, where rich wood furniture, oriental rugs, and inlaid mother-of-pearl showcase the expertise of regional artisans.

## Discover The St. Regis Cairo, in 360°

Journey to the heart of Cairo, where timeless heritage meets contemporary elegance on the banks of the Nile. The St. Regis Cairo unveils a world shaped by history, refined by craftsmanship, and animated by the rhythm of the city.

Drift through expansive suites and signature serviced and fully furnished apartments, where panoramic views frame the Nile’s gentle flow and Cairo’s storied skyline. Wander through refined dining destinations, curated rituals, and intimate spaces where every detail speaks of bespoke luxury and discreet sophistication.

Now, through our 360° virtual experience, you are invited to explore this iconic address from every perspective. Trace the curve of the Nile at sunrise, step into interiors layered with artistry and soul.
