The city’s premier choice for classic cuisine reinterpreted, J&G Steakhouse Cairo serves the world’s finest cuts of meat and highest quality fish, each expertly prepared and grilled to perfection. Dark walls, mirror detailing, and crystal curtains create an alluring backdrop, where exquisite Nile views are maximized by vast floor-to-ceiling windows.

Drawing inspiration from the mythical winged horse of Chinese legend, Tianma offers a bold reimagining of contemporary Asian dining in the heart of Cairo. Honoring time-honored traditions while embracing a modern culinary spirit, the restaurant presents a thoughtfully curated menu where every dish is designed to intrigue and delight. ### La Zisa

Refined and relaxed in equal measure, La Zisa Italian restaurant combines authentic Sicilian-influenced cuisine, with exclusive Nile River views. Elegantly designed to create a light-filled space, continental details like mosaic flooring, tiled arches, and bistro-style seating are offset by Italianate touches and the expansive Nile River. Open throughout the day, guests can enjoy an excellent menu...See More of tailored classics, from delicious buffet breakfast to à la carte options served at lunch and dinner. Bringing a refined café experience to the shores of the Nile, Il Café Bar is a 24-hour venue serving fine tea, expertly made coffee, and light meals, adjacent to La Zisa restaurant.See Less

### Sirocco

Elevated on the hotel’s 7th floor, poolside Sirocco is defined by the Saharan breeze that offers respite from city living. Open from morning to night, the venue has transformational quality where daytime lounging beside the mosaic-tiled pool is accompanied by fresh juices, cocktails, ice cream, and light bites. As night falls, the space transitions into a glamorous evening lounge, enhanced by the warm glow of traditional lanterns. Distinctive Egyptian creations and crafted drinks are served al fresco at the deck overlooking the Nile, whilst individual cabanas are curtained by flowing drapes.

### The St. Regis Bar & Water Garden

Extending the legacy of New York’s renowned King Cole Bar, The St. Regis Bar & Water Garden bring a custom pink sun mural and Nile views to each celebration. Serving an exclusive selection of Champagne, vintage wine, and crafted cocktails, both venues are also the setting for signature rituals. Tastemakers gather to savor the exotic, hibiscus-infused rendition of the St. Regis Bloody Mary, alongside sunset Champagne sabering. Served against a bespoke floral masterpiece, is where sweet treats are enriched with a touch of gold.

### The Astor Library

Located on the fifth floor, The Astor Library is a sophisticated sanctuary for book lovers, where guests can relax quietly with a drink, as if in their own home. Appointed with an antique rug and traditional pieces of furniture, it is lined with dark wood shelving, wrapped in red leather, and beautifully stitched. Throughout this gathering place, there is a carefully curated selection of artefacts and literature available to browse and enjoy.
