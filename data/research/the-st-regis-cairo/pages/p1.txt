# Welcome to The St. Regis Cairo

## Enjoy luxury at our family-friendly Nile view hotel in Cairo

The St. Regis Cairo draws guests to the heart of Egypt's timeless metropolis, weaving a story of luxury, local heritage, and outstanding views of the Nile and Old Cairo. Our riverfront 5-star hotel in Cairo rises 36 stories, designed by renowned architect Michael Graves and standing among the leading luxury and Nile view hotels in Cairo. For leisurely stays and family getaways, 360 lavishly appointed...See More family-friendly rooms, suites, and upscale apartments are joined by signature rituals and seven thoughtfully crafted restaurants and bars in this family hotel in Cairo. Guests praise the attentive service, and the signature St. Regis Butler Service is available with our compliments any time, day or night, anticipating every need from unpacking to the finest details throughout your stay.See Less

### Overview

The St. Regis Cairo offers 360 impeccably crafted rooms, suites, and serviced apartments, exceptionally furnished to facilitate every stay. Ideally positioned with views across the Nile River and Old Cairo, all accommodation combines the impeccable St. Regis Butler service with effortless luxury. ### Luxury Accommodations

The St. Regis Cairo has a lavishly appointed 232 rooms, 50 suites, and 78 serviced apartments with floor-to-ceiling windows, offering panoramic Nile River views and authentic views of Old Cairo with great attention to detail and a design that evokes the feeling of a residential home. All furnished with a unique collection of pieces and our wardrobes are visually enticing for those who are impressed by the unexpected; the story behind it is inspired by the Nile, which is accentuated with lilies, lotus flowers, birds, and luscious greenery that have all been nurtured by our mighty storied river.

### Cultivating an Epicurean Journey

The St. Regis Cairo presents an elevated lifestyle, where an eclectic selection of restaurants and bars bring sophisticated gastronomy to refined tastemakers.

## The Address for Refined Tastemaking

A premier location for exquisitely crafted cuisines, The St. Regis Cairo introduces a curated list of restaurants and bars to the city. Seven individual concepts offer diverse dining experiences that transcend culture and tradition, forming a unique culinary journey. Designed to entice discerning gastronomes, the choice extends from flavorful Italian fare at La Zisa, to sophisticated prime cuts at J&G Steakhouse Cairo and exceptional Asian dishes at Tianma. Paying tribute to the legacy of the St. Regis Brand, The St. Regis Bar & Water Garden is where engaging daily rituals are celebrated.

## J&G Steakhouse Cairo

The city’s premier choice for classic cuisine reinterpreted, J&G Steakhouse Cairo serves the world’s finest cuts of meat and highest quality fish, each expertly prepared and grilled to perfection. Dark walls, mirror detailing, and crystal curtains create an alluring backdrop, where exquisite Nile views are maximized by vast floor-to-ceiling windows.

Presenting the apex of exclusivity, J&G Steakhouse Cairo’s private dining room hosts up to 12 guests. Located inside the restaurant on the third floor, the richly furnished wood-paneled space provides an unparalleled setting for intimate dining occasions.

Learn More

## Tianma

Drawing inspiration from mythical winged horse of Chinese legend, Tianma offers a bold reimagining of contemporary Asian dining in the heart of Cairo. Honoring time-honored traditions while embracing a modern culinary spirit, the restaurant presents a thoughtfully curated menu where every dish is designed to intrigue and delight. From vibrant spices to delicate flavors, each dish is crafted to evoke a sense of discovery. Set against a striking backdrop of oversized bells, curated artwork, and immersive music, Tianma creates a dining experience that is as dynamic and captivating as the culture that inspired it.

Learn More

## La Zisa

Refined and relaxed in equal measure, La Zisa Italian restaurant combines authentic Sicilian-influenced cuisine, with exclusive Nile River views. Elegantly designed to create a light-filled space, continental details like mosaic flooring, tiled arches, and bistro-style seating are offset by Italianate touches and the expansive Nile River. Open throughout the day, guests can enjoy an excellent menu of tailored classics, from delicious buffet breakfast to à la carte options served at lunch and dinner. Bringing a refined café experience to the shores of the Nile, Il Café Bar is a 24-hour venue serving fine tea, expertly made coffee, and light meals, adjacent to La Zisa restaurant.

Learn More

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Cairo?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Cairo is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Cairo allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Cairo.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Cairo?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Cairo. Self parking is available at The St. Regis Cairo, with rates of $2.10 per hour, $9.00 per day, and $63.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Cairo have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Cairo has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Cairo booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Cairo?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Cairo is Cairo International Airport (CAI). CAI is located approximately 24.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Cairo have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Cairo has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
