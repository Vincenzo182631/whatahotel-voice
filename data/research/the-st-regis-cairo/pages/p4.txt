### Iridium Spa

Luxuriate in a sanctuary dedicated to exquisite living at Iridium Spa at The St. Regis Cairo, where the essence of the St. Regis brand is brought to life. Designed to flow across two floors, every interaction is enhanced by culture, bespoke service, and understated exclusivity. Positioned on the sixth floor, the luxury spa is styled with neutral tones and soft lighting to craft an inviting sense of...See More purity. The St. Regis signature scent, Caroline’s Four Hundred, with its subtle bouquet of tropical palms and garden roses pervades. In the lounge, Spa Experts welcome each guest with a refreshing beverage, before offering a consultation based on individual requirements. Eight dedicated treatment suites are the backdrop for a menu of therapies, whilst a vitality pool, ice room, light therapy, adventure showers, sauna, and steam room encourage holistic relaxation. For those looking to elevate the experience, the Iridium Spa suites provide ultimate privacy, ideal for couples and small groups.See Less

### Outdoor Pools & Jacuzzi

Indulgent and designed for peak relaxation, the outdoor swimming pool has been intricately crafted with mosaic-patterning and acts as a shimmering centerpiece, framed by teak loungers, plush sunbeds, and private cabanas. An ideal backdrop for all the family, the setting is enhanced by a heated jacuzzi and dedicated children’s pool. Open throughout the day, poolside dining comes courtesy of Sirocco. Whether choosing to dine at a sun lounger or in the alfresco restaurant, a choice menu of refreshing drinks and authentic dishes is complemented by flawless service.

### Indoor Pool

Vaulted ceilings and artful mosaics provide a pristine environment for the indoor pool. Floor-to-ceiling windows fill the space with natural light and offer dramatic views across Cairo. Plush sunbeds, decorated with scatter cushions, offer prime relaxation, while the pool, with its intricate aqua marine tiling, is ideal for a refreshing swim.

### Amulet Children's Club

Designed to bring enjoyment and enchantment to children’s playtime, the Amulet Children’s Club has a host of activities, including foosball, PlayStation, crafts, and more. There’s also a babysitting service for when parents require child-free moments.

## Astor Library

Located on the fifth floor, The Astor Library is a sophisticated sanctuary for book lovers, where guests can relax quietly with a drink, as if in their own home. Appointed with an antique rug and traditional pieces of furniture, it is lined with dark wood shelving, wrapped in red leather, and beautifully stitched. Throughout this gathering place, there is a carefully curated selection of artefacts and literature available to browse and enjoy.

## St. Regis Butler Service

As the hallmark of the St. Regis experience, the signature St. Regis Butler Service is offered to all hotel guests. A visible representation of the brand’s renowned level of anticipatory service and flawless attention to details. Personal butlers allow each guest to savor the greatest luxury of all, the gift of time.

## Private Dining

The St. Regis Cairo brings exceptional dining to the privacy of a guest’s accommodation with a thoughtfully crafted culinary menu. Catering to a wide range of preferences, the best of the hotel’s food and beverage options can be requested, throughout the day and night, served by personal butler.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Cairo have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Cairo offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Cairo have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Cairo has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Indoor Pool (Indoor)Outdoor Pool (Outdoor)Kids Pool (Outdoor)

Does The St. Regis Cairo have an on-site spa?

Yes, Iridium Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Cairo. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Cairo?
