## The only all-suite and villa oceanfront _oasis_ in Hawai'i

Experience Maui like never before at Fairmont Kea Lani, Hawai‘i’s only all-suite and villa oceanfront resort. Fresh off an extensive transformation, this tropical paradise redefines luxury with the island’s most spacious 5-star accommodations, unrivaled culinary offerings and immersive experiences that embody true aloha.

### Embark on a journey into the heart of Maui

Indulge in Fairmont Kea Lani's distinct collection of award-winning dining venues, discover bliss with the comprehensive spa and wellness program, or embrace poolside luxury with three sprawling swimming pools. All within steps of the sunny shores and azure waters of Polo Beach in Wailea, Maui.
