Fairmont Kea Lani
A spacious resort that offers family-friendly amenities, elevated dining, and oceanfront relaxation with thoughtful perks for locals.

Best for:

Can’t-miss feature:
The swim-up bar at AMA Bar & Grill, where submerged seats let you sip on tropical cocktails—which can be served in a fresh pineapple—without leaving the pool
In a nutshell
: This resort, aptly named Kea Lani, which means “heavenly white,” boasts gorgeous, palace-like architecture that's inspired by the white plumeria flower. Unlike most Wailea resorts, Fairmont Kea Lani is entirely made up of suites and villas, which is something I’ve found makes a noticeable difference if you value space, especially compared to the size of more standard hotel-style rooms at nearby resorts. Locals enjoy added perks, including special rates on accommodations and spa treatments, waived resort fees, pool access with applicable passes, and 25% off on dining, including at signature restaurant Ko.
Rooms
The property exclusively offers one-bedroom suites and multi-story villas—the only resort in Hawaii to make that claim—all featuring coastal-inspired design and stunning ocean views. One-bedroom suites start at 860 square feet and include separate living areas and oversized bathrooms, while villas offer full kitchens and private courtyards with plunge pools. I’ve found it to be an ideal setup for travelers who want space to spread out without sacrificing luxury.
Food and drink
The four main dining venues are AMA Bar & Grill for poolside and dine-in service with an impressive seafood platter; Nick’s Fishmarket for fresh seafood and a signature strawberry flambé dessert; the reimagined Ko Restaurant, where Hawaiian-inspired cuisine takes center stage; and Pilina, a lobby lounge and sushi bar. Locals receive 25% off across all outlets.
Amenities and experiences
A lagoon-style pool complex caters to all ages, with waterfalls, hidden hot tubs, a waterslide, and a shallow wading pool for kids, while a quieter, adults-only pool offers a more relaxed escape. The on-site cultural center, Hale Kukuna, provides insight into Hawaiian history through artifacts, instruments, and hands-on classes like lei making and ukulele lessons. The Hawaii Royal Chapel, complete with a romantic gazebo, offers a picturesque setting for weddings.
What to do nearby
The resort overlooks Polo Beach, with easy access via a short path to the waterfront, where ocean activities abound. A quick walk south leads to The Shops at Wailea for luxury shopping and dining, including casual bites at Waikiki Brewing Company and elevated yet approachable New American cuisine at Aurum Maui. Don’t miss a stop at Lappert’s Ice Cream for a sweet treat or flavor-infused drip coffee—the Tiki Toffee being a personal favorite of mine.
Insider tip
You don’t need to be an overnight guest to enjoy the resort amenities, with day passes for the pool available through services like
ResortPass
. While you’re there, order the Tidal Wave, a shareable rum punch served in a commemorative sand pail.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Yes, the large suites and villas offer plenty of space for families to spread out, and there are resort activities for all ages
Accessibility:
Yes, the property features ADA-compliant suites and villas, although they recommend you contact them directly for any specific needs ahead of your arrival
Wellness:
Yes, there are excellent treatments at the Fairmont Spa
