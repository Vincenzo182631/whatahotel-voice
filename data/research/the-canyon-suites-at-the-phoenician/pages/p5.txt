# Villas at Canyon Suites

## Elevate your stay in our luxury hotel villas in Scottsdale​

Nestled in the foothills of majestic Camelback Mountain, our new Canyon Villas in Scottsdale exemplify unparalleled luxury and contemporary sophistication. ### Ultimate Desert Playground

The Villas at Canyon Suites offer eight free-standing, single-level accommodations with two, three and four-bedroom configurations, each showcasing iconic valley and golf views. Designed with contemporary Southwest decor, the luxury hotel villas in Scottsdale​ feature gas fireplaces in the living rooms, floor-to-ceiling stacking glass doors and outdoor patio fire tables with seating. Each villa includes...See More a full kitchen, dining table, wet bar, mini bar and a washer/dryer combination for added convenience. ## Two Bedroom Villas

The 2,460 sq ft single-level, 2-bedroom / 3 bath Villas immerse you in refined elegance, with soaring 12-foot ceilings and expansive windows framing breathtaking resort views. Savor vivid Scottsdale Desert sunsets from a private terrace adorned with plush seating and a glowing fire table. The open layout features sleek tile flooring, a gourmet kitchen with stone island, dining table for eight, a sectional sofa, and a dramatic gas fireplace wall. The primary king suite offers a lavish en-suite with grand walk-in closet, dual vanities, glass-enclosed shower, freestanding soaking tub, and private water closet. A stylish guest bath with shower, closet, and washer/dryer enhances convenience. The second guestroom presents two queen beds, a flat-screen TV, wet bar, secluded patio, and an en-suite with dual vanities, soaking tub, shower, and water closet — ideal for family or guests. Accommodates up to 8 guests.

## Three Bedroom Villas

The 3,200 sq ft 3-bedroom / 4 bath, single-level Villas embody elevated desert living, blending modern lines with warm comfort. Soaring ceilings and sweeping windows bathe the interiors in natural light while framing panoramic vistas. A vast terrace with plush seating, fire table, private hot tub, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow sets the stage for memorable evenings. Inside, the open design features a chef’s kitchen with striking stone island, dining table for eight, and a spacious living room with a sectional sleeper, sleek TV, and statement fireplace. The primary king retreat offers a sanctuary of indulgence with a spa-inspired en-suite, walk-in closet, dual vanities, glass shower, stand-alone tub, and water closet. Two additional guestrooms provide versatility: one with two queen beds, a wet bar, patio access, and en-suite; the other with a luxurious king suite, wet bar, secluded patio and full bath. Thoughtful touches include a powder room and washer/dryer. Accommodates up to 11 guests.

## Four Bedroom Villas

The 3,450 sq ft 4-bedroom / 4 bath, single-level Villas offer the pinnacle of desert luxury, with soaring ceilings and expansive windows framing sweeping resort and mountain views. An oversized terrace with plush seating, fire table, private hot tub and seamless indoor-outdoor living captures the essence of Sonoran sunsets. The open layout of these Scottsdale villa rentals includes refined tile flooring, a chef’s kitchen with stone island, dining for eight and a spacious living room with a sectional sleeper, sleek TV and dramatic fireplace. The primary king retreat features spa-style en-suite with, dual vanities, glass shower, freestanding soaking tub and private water closet. A second king guestroom also enjoys a private en-suite and patio. Two double-queen guestrooms provide versatility: one with its own en-suite and patio access, the other with a stylish bath nearby. Designed for both gathering and privacy, the Villa accommodates up to 13 guests in unrivaled comfort.

Villas Amenities

### Villas Pool and Cabanas

The Villas offer private access to an exclusive pool and spa with four cabanas, chaise lounges, fire-pit seating, dining tables, and full dining service. Surrounded by luxurious amenities and breathtaking views of Camelback Mountain, guests at our Scottsdale villa rentals also enjoy access to all Canyon Suites and Phoenician pools.

### Chef-Inspired Kitchens

The Villas boast gourmet kitchens with premium appliances, stocked cookware, and an espresso machine. A grand stone island and dining table for eight create the perfect setting for culinary artistry. Enhance your stay at our luxury Scottsdale resort and villas​ with a private chef or mixology services, plus complimentary breakfast daily.

### Private Villa Golf Carts

Villas guests enjoy the convenience of a private golf cart, offering effortless transportation throughout the resort grounds. Each Villa includes designated cart parking, ensuring easy access for exploring amenities, dining, and activities with comfort and style.
