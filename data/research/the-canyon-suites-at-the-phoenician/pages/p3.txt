## Dining

Two dedicated venues offering unique dining experiences make The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale a captivating culinary destination for breakfast and lunch. At the...See More center of it all is the Canyon Lounge, an inviting social gathering spot in the main lobby offering a welcoming ambiance of togetherness; join us at breakfast, or stop by for evening cocktails accompanied by small plates. For some poolside splendor amidst the warm sunshine, relax and enjoy the Canyon Grill, featuring a lunch menu of fresh salads, specially prepared entrees, curated beverages and fresh smoothies. For additional options, The Phoenician offers eight excellent restaurants and bars, including a sleek steakhouse, luxury Afternoon Tea in Scottsdale​ as well as a wide array of culinary events throughout each season.See Less

### J&G Steakhouse

The award-winning, fine dining Scottsdale restaurant with a view features new seasonal food and drink menus. Inspired by the dramatic desert backdrop just beyond J&G’s famed floor-to-ceiling window walls, the stylish interior features striking architectural details to match. With glowing fire pits, modern seating and the addition of ceiling fans, the scenic outdoor patio serves as a year-round alfresco...See More dining destination.See Less

### Chef Jacques Qualin

Acclaimed as a “master of his art,” Jacques Qualin has served as J&G's executive chef since its doors opened in 2008, bringing with him a variety of international experience developed at some of the world's finest dining establishments.

### Refined Menu

Menu items such as the Wagyu Tomahawk Ribeye, Lamb T-Bone, and Alaskan King Crab Legs are matched by innovative twists on a variety of cocktail classics, such as the Brandy Apple Fashioned at this steakhouse in Scottsdale, AZ.

### Mowry & Cotton

Presenting a relaxed setting with a social vibe, Mowry & Cotton features Modern American cuisine prepared using the timeless cooking techniques of fire, coal and smoke.

### Specialty Menu

Using simple, but bold ingredients, Mowry & Cotton creates menus filled with flavor. Whether Huevos Rancheros for breakfast, a Citrus Salmon & Chickpea Bowl for lunch, or Citrus Herb Game Hen for dinner, guests are sure to enjoy the variety of regionally inspired dishes.

### Inspiring Vistas
