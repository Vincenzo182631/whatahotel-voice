# Welcome to The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale

## Discover our exclusive luxury resort in Scottsdale

The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale blends approachable luxury with modern sophistication. The contemporary design at our boutique resort in Scottsdale, Arizona,...See More reflects the rich tones of the Sonoran Desert. Enjoy refined accommodations with elegant furnishings and outdoor patios or terraces with daybeds, or unwind in a private cabana at the infinity pool in this 5-star hotel in Scottsdale, Arizona. Discover the new Canyon Villas, offering an elevated residential-style retreat within this exclusive enclave. Begin your day with a complimentary executive breakfast in the Canyon Lounge, which also features boutique wines and crafted cocktails in the late afternoon and evening for purchase. Our luxury resort in Scottsdale, Arizona, sits at the base of Camelback Mountain, with easy access to hiking, biking and horseback riding trails. Additional amenities include multiple pools, a spa and fitness center, golf, and a variety of fine and casual dining options.See Less

## Landmark of Luxury, Reimagined

The Canyon Suites has completed a thoughtful renewal of its guest rooms and suites, inspired by the natural beauty and spirit of the Sonoran Desert. As part of The Phoenician’s Landmark of Luxury story, the refreshed accommodations blend contemporary desert elegance with the personalized service and exclusivity that define the Canyon Suites experience.

## Treasured Fall

Fall settles gently over the Sonoran Desert, bringing crisp mornings, golden light, and perfect days for exploring Arizona’s most captivating landscape. At The Canyon Suites, sun-drenched afternoons invite relaxation beside sparkling pools, while cooler temperatures create ideal conditions for championship golf, scenic walks through lush resort grounds, and outdoor dining beneath palm-framed skies.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale?

The check-in time at The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale?

On-site parking is available at The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale booking.

What is the closest airport near The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale?

The closest airport near The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale is Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX). PHX is located approximately 9.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale have electric vehicle charging stations?
