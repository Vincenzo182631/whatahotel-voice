# Experience our Scottsdale spa resort

## Experiences

Scottsdale, Arizona, is a vibrant destination with a wealth of exciting activities, attractions and events, as well as an alluring nightlife and world-class shopping. Set in the beautiful Sonoran Desert,...See More bordered by Phoenix to the west, the city is bursting with unforgettable adventures that will captivate your spirit - and our luxury Scottsdale resort with hiking nearby places many of them at your doorstep. Explore attractions close to our Scottsdale resort and spa including Old Town Scottsdale and the Phoenix Zoo. Enjoy hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding and desert eco-tours under idyllic blue skies, or tap into nature on scenic trails with breathtaking views. Go river rafting and kayaking, visit the famed Grand Canyon or take to the tranquil skies with an unforgettable hot air balloon rideSee Less

## Treasured Fall

Fall settles gently over the Sonoran Desert, bringing crisp mornings, golden light, and perfect days for exploring Arizona’s most captivating landscape. At The Canyon Suites, sun-drenched afternoons invite relaxation beside sparkling pools, while cooler temperatures create ideal conditions for championship golf, scenic walks through lush resort grounds, and outdoor dining beneath palm-framed skies. Seasonal menus showcase the flavors of the harvest, and restorative spa experiences offer a tranquil retreat after sunrise hikes on nearby Camelback Mountain. As vibrant desert sunsets paint the horizon in rich amber and rose hues, guests are invited to savor the distinctive elegance and natural beauty of an unforgettable Arizona autumn.

## The Phoenician Golf Course

To complement the exceptional course at our luxury resort near Camelback Mountain is the world-class Phoenician Golf Shop, pristine practice facilities, instruction from PGA-trained professionals and the 19th Hole dining venue.

### Accolades

Named “North America’s Leading Golf Resort” by the World Travel Awards, our Scottsdale hotel with a golf course has been ranked among the “Top 75 Golf Resorts in America” by Golf Digest.

### Golf Experience Package

Our stunning 18-hole golf course with iconic views of the Valley and beyond features emerald fairways shared with stately saguaros and desert terrain. This course displays water features, re-contoured fairways and a cart path rebuilt for smoother navigation. All this can be enjoyed while experiencing a relaxing stay at our AAA Five Diamond resort.

### Caddie Services

Enhance your game at The Phoenician by adding a caddie to your next round. The Phoenician has partnered with CADDIEMASTER to enhance the overall golf experience by offering caddies, forecaddies and special caddie packages that will make your round even more memorable. Available November - April.

### The 19th Hole

Curb your thirst or hunger with a visit to our golf course grill and patio, featuring light grab-and-go items, as well as refreshing alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages.

### Local Area & Activities Highlights

### Desert Off-Road Excursion

Be picked up in a 4x4 off-road vehicle by your cowboy guide and experience the beauty of the Sonoran Desert. This tour is perfect for groups that are interested not only in an adventure but also the cultural background and history of the region.

### Desert Evening Under the Stars

An evening of excellent cuisine, entertainment, and dancing will leave you with a memorable experience. Candle lanterns, string trio, mesquite trees, and giant saguaros set the atmosphere for this desert evening under the stars. Capacity: 10 - 600+

### Desert Botanical Garden

The world's finest collection of arid-land plants from deserts around the world. The Garden has more than 50,000 plants throughout its five thematic trails, which illustrate tropics such as conservation, desert living, plants, and desert wildflowers.

### Horseback Riding

Hop in the saddle and ride into the heart of the majestic the Sonoran Desert. We offer trails over hilly terrain that dip down into arroyos and climb up steep embankments. Or ride on the old cavalry, outlaw and Indian trails and laugh along with the tall tales told by our cowboy wranglers.

### Flatwater River Float

Raft through the desert with our professionally trained guides. They will take you for a cruise down the beautiful Rio Verde or Salt River, surrounded by rough and rugged desert mountains. The float offers mild ripples combined with spectacular desert scenery and on occasion, a glimpse of Arizona wildlife along the way.

### Kayaking

Flowing from the mountains in Northern Arizona, the Salt River is captured by four dams and released for use by Phoenicians and farmers year-round. While kayaking on this Class 1 river you are surrounded by spectacular, unobstructed 70-mile views into four different mountain ranges.

See More

### The Dolly Steamboat

Arizona’s World Class Cruise on Canyon Lake. Travel up through the historic Apache Trail in the Tonto National Forest. Featuring scenic nature, twilight dinner and astronomy cruises.

### My Arizona Guide

A custom, private tour company offering Road Trips, Night Trips, Native Culture Tours, Photo Trips, Birding, Desert Hiking, Mountain Hiking on Camelback Mountain, AZ, and more. Half-day, full-day, multi-day, and overnight programs available.

### Special VIP Phoenician Package

Sedona and Jerome Wine Tasting Tour. Guests leave from the resort for a 1.5 hour drive to beautiful Sedona and embark on an invigorating 2-hour hike at Red Rock State Park. The afternoon is spent enjoying a full picnic lunch (with choice of options) overlooking the majestic scenery. Next, it’s on to the historic mining town of Jerome. Guests visit 2 tasting rooms and sample wines from Arizona’s Caduceus Cellars.

### Wild Bunch Desert Guides

A boutique mountain biking and hiking company. Working with guests to ensure trail selections match their needs.

### Detours American West

Professionally guided tours of Arizona and the West. Daily tours showcase the Grand Canyon, Sedona, the Apache Trail, Tombstone and Phoenix/Scottsdale city highlights.

### Hot Air Expeditions

Special VIP packages. Daily flights year-round, featuring a gourmet Champagne breakfast.
