Treetops Lodge & Estate Activities

Browse our list of sightseeing and active adventure recommendations. Like all things at Treetops all adventures can be tailored for your personal itinerary.

### ACTIVITIES INCLUDED IN YOUR STAY

#### WALKS AND HIKES

Explore the estate unguided using our trail maps. Please see more information and maps below.

Duration: All day/ as long as you want

Suitable: All ages

Grade: Easy to Difficult

#### MOUNTAIN BIKING

Our fleet of dual suspension mountain bikes will ensure you have a comfortable ride exploring the trails in the Treetops valley.

Duration: All day/ as long as you want subject to availability

Suitable: All ages

Grade: Medium

#### KAYAKING

Enjoy a gentle kayak on Treetops’ Lake Reflection. You will view waterfowl, rising trout and animals roaming in the fields.

Duration: All day/ as long as you want subject to availability

Suitable: All ages, children must be supervised by an adult.

GLOW WORMS

Just a few minutes from the main lodge explore the caverns of Glow worms. Take a look and wonder at the New Zealand glow worm in its natural habitat.

Duration: 30 minutes

Suitable: All ages available from 9pm onwards

SIGNATURE EXPERIENCES

Talk to our reservations team about prices and availability. Weather in the Horohoro and greater Rotorua area can vary. All activities are subject to suitable weather conditions, our team can arrange help arrange the best times to have these experiences while you stay once you arrive. Please do identify any other special interests that you may have, so that we can help you achieve the holiday of your dreams.

FLY FISHING

Our guides will take you to the best spots in the region to ensure a fly fishing experience you will cherish for the rest of your life.

Packages include all equipment and licenses required.

Duration: Half day or Full day adventures

Suitable: Novice to Expert

FLY FISHING BASICS

Learn everything you need to know about the skillful art of fly fishing. With all the equipment you will need and the perfection locations on property, your guide will take you through all the basics in a fun filled lesson.

Duration: 1.5 hours

Suitable: All ages

HELI FISHING

For a truly unique experience try our wilderness Heli fishing allowing access into wilderness terrain boasting unspoilt fishing not easily accessible by any other means.

Duration: Full day Adventure

Suitable: Novice to Expert

GUIDED WALK

Take a walk to the lodge’s own private waterfall. Accessed by a light trek through fern forest with your guide, opening up to the spectacular Bridal Veil Falls where you are close enough to feel the mist on your face.

Duration: 2 hours

Suitable: All ages

Grade: Medium

ARCHERY

Make your way through the forest undergrowth with compound bow in hand watching out for fun targets hidden amongst the bush. A great activity for the whole family.

Duration: 1 hour

Suitable: All ages

PRIVATE ESTATE TOUR

Join our guide on a private tour of the property. Opportunities abound to view and photograph majestic stag, water buffalo, sheep, goats, and a variety of birdlife.

Duration: 2 hours

Suitable: All ages

CLAY BIRD SHOOTING

Take aim on our clay bird target range, and with a little training from our expert guide you’ll be blowing them out of the sky in no time.

Duration: 30 minutes per person

Suitable: Ages 13 and over

**Estate and Lodge** \\
\\
Set in Rotorua’s untouched heartland, Treetops is New Zealand’s privately owned 2,500-acre nature reserve, featuring four waterfalls and lakes, seven trout streams, and over 70 km (35 miles) of private hiking and adventure trails. Much of the estate remains undeveloped, preserving pristine rainforest, ancient trees, and rich native habitat and New Zealand birdlife.\\
