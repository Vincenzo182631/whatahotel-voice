## A LUXURY BEACHFRONT WATERPARK

Inspired by the natural beauty of The Bahamas, Baha Bay is designed to perfectly fuse relaxing island chill with exhilarating fun for the entire family. The water park is directly adjacent to Baha Mar and set on 15 lush beachfront acres. With an array of spectacular experiences and tantalizing cuisine, however you like to play in the sun, it awaits you at Baha Bay.

## DINING AT BAHA BAY

Discover dining options at Baha Bay Water Park, from laid back bites to world class fare. There is something for everyone to enjoy.

VIEW BAHA BAY RESTAURANTS

## FUN FOR THE KIDS

## TURTLE BEACH

Splash, slide and play all day in a place built just for our smallest guests.The kids can race their friends down slides that are just their size, and splash around in shallow pools.

## STINGRAY COVE

Speed down twisty slides, get drenched by the tipping bucket and splash through the battle zone in this kids’ area designed for maximum adventure.

* * *

## POOLSIDE EXPERIENCES

### THE RIVER

Exhale and enjoy the island breeze as the exhilarating river current takes you on a journey surrounded by lush tropical foliage.

### BAHA BAY LAGOON

Wade out into the water from our luxurious beach area and ride the tides in our 500,000 gallon wave pool.

### THE SURF

Experience the thrill of surfing with no lessons required, on this fun, state-of-the-art FlowCurl surf simulator.

## WATERSLIDES

### HAMMERHEAD

High speeds and huge thrills await. This multi-person raft slide rockets riders through tunnels, then up and around the walls of massive water saucers.

### CYCLONE RUSH

Hold on tight! You’ll ride out the storm on a multi-person raft that speeds around a huge swirling bowl, then up and back down a nearly vertical wall.

### PIRATES PLUNGE

This high-action body slide takes you through back-to-back turns and 360-degree loops before a final freefall plunge into the pool.

## EXTREME SLIDES

### DUELING RIPTIDE COASTER

Rocket through tunnels, spin around saucers and dive down steep slopes—this high-speed water coaster is a one of a kind blast.

### THUNDERBALL

This extreme freefall slide is only for our bravest riders. A super- fast, straight-down plummet delivers an unforgettable rush.

### DEVIL’S BACKBONE

Feel the floor drop out from under you before blasting through looping and twisting tunnels that will keep you screaming until the end.
