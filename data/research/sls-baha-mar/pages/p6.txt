SLS Baha Mar
The SLS at Baha Mar feels grown-up—without sacrificing the beachfront fun you flew to the Caribbean for in the first place.

Reviewed by
Lauren Dana Ellman
Best for:
Friend groups, couples, celebratory trips
Location:
Inside the larger Baha Mar complex in Nassau, The Bahamas
Can’t-miss feature:
The Baha Bay Water Park, with its 20,000-square-foot wave pool and plenty of loungers to relax on
In a nutshell
: For a Caribbean trip that feels like a scene—restaurants, pool, and nightlife all built in—there’s very little reason to leave SLS Baha Mar once you arrive. It’s the most high-energy of the hotels in the wider Baha Mar resort complex, with a crowd that skews mostly adult, giving it a polished, elevated spring break feel.
Hannah Chubb
Hannah Chubb
Rooms
Rooms lean into SLS’s signature bold, fashion-forward style, with patterned walls and tropical prints that feel more dressed-up than the usual beachy whites and woods. It works, especially with the amount of natural light coming through floor-to-ceiling windows and balconies in every room, which keeps things from feeling too heavy. Entry-level rooms are on the smaller side but still feel substantial, with enough personality to avoid the generic resort look.
For groups, it’s worth booking a suite. The added space makes a real difference, with separate living areas, multiple bathrooms, and, in some cases, full kitchens that make longer stays more comfortable.
Food and drink
The lobby, anchored by the Monkey Bar, sets that tone upon arrival. I kept circling back in the early evening for a drink; it’s easily the best place to people-watch as the night takes shape. Guests pass through on their way to dinner, then drift back later, louder and looser, until the whole space tips into a full see-and-be-seen scene.
As far as food goes, you could easily stay a few days here without repeating a meal—and that’s just within SLS. Across the Baha Mar complex, there are more than 40 dining options, which adds real range if you’re staying longer. Carna is the standout—subtly flashy, like the hotel itself, it’s a contemporary Italian steakhouse with consistently strong cuts and a room that’s worth getting dressed up for. Katsuya is louder and more scene-driven, built around familiar crowd-pleasers like spicy tuna crispy rice, miso black cod, and jalapeno-topped yellowtail sashimi. Cleo pulls double duty, shifting from a standard breakfast buffet to a much livelier dinner setting. Breakfast is well done, but dinner is the highlight, with meze, grilled meats, and local seafood like spiny lobster and snapper. By the beach, 25° North keeps things breezy and casual, with a menu spanning salads, sandwiches, pizzas, tacos, and sushi.
For drinks, SkyBar is the place to start or end the night. Set on the hotel’s 20th floor, the open-air space looks out over the property, with a menu of delicious signature cocktails like the Sky Punch—rum, coconut cream, and pineapple juice—the hotel’s take on a Bahamian classic. And that’s just the start of the culinary lineup; there’s enough here that you don’t need to plan much in advance.
Hannah Chubb
Hannah Chubb
Amenities and experiences
The 30,000-square-foot Espa is shared across the entire Baha Mar complex. Quiet, ocean-facing, and pared back (compared to the rest of the property), it offers a calming reprieve from the bustle. Treatments—including massages, facials, and body therapies—are well executed, especially the Seashell Serenity massage that uses warmed tiger clam shells, adding a distinctly local touch to the experience.
By day, your stay revolves around the pool. The adults-only Privilege Pool is the center of it all, with music, drinks, and a steady crowd that builds through the afternoon. It’s busy but controlled, without tipping into chaos, and is where I spent most of my time. Rows of loungers and cushioned cabanas frame the space, making it easy to settle in for a few hours without moving.
The wider resort adds even more range, from a high-tech Game Zone with a mix of newer arcade-style games and nostalgic favorites to the John McEnroe Tennis Center, which offers multiple courts and a more active counterpoint to the pool and beach scene.
It’s also worth making time for the wildlife sanctuary, where you can get surprisingly close to sea turtles, stingrays, nurse sharks, and native birds. It’s one of the property's more distinctive features, adding a quieter, more educational layer that appeals to all ages.
What to do nearby
While technically in Nassau, this is a self-contained resort, and most guests treat it that way. If you do venture out, you’re a short drive from Cable Beach and about 15 minutes from downtown Nassau, but most stays here revolve entirely around the Baha Mar complex.
Hannah Chubb
Hannah Chubb
The backstory
Baha Mar opened in phases starting in 2017, designed as a self-contained resort complex with multiple hotels, restaurants, and shared amenities. Within it, SLS is positioned as the most design-forward and nightlife-driven of the three hotels, alongside Grand Hyatt and Rosewood.
Insider tip
Part of the appeal of staying at SLS is the access you’ll have to the rest of Baha Mar. You’re not confined to one hotel—you can move easily between pools, the spa, casino, golf course, and Baha Bay, which makes the stay feel more dynamic.
At Baha Bay, the Beach Club sits along the edge of the water park but feels like its own separate space. It’s one of the better setups on the property, especially if you book a daybed or cabana, which gives you some privacy without fully stepping out of the atmosphere.
I didn’t have high expectations for the water park—I’m not much of a slide person, and it’s easy to assume it’s mostly for families—but I ended up really liking it. The 20,000-square-foot wave pool is a highlight, and the surrounding loungers make it simple to settle in. It’s surprisingly easy to spend a morning, afternoon, or even a full day here.
Hannah Chubb
Hannah Chubb
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Yes, although this is the most adult-friendly and nightlife-focused property in the Baha Mar complex. They have two- and three-bedroom suites with kitchens and laundry, which are great for those with little ones, and while at night Monkey Bar is very scene-y for adults, during the day, it's a gathering spot for all ages
Accessibility:
SLS offers accessible rooms and step-free access across many of its main public spaces, including restaurants, pools, and shared facilities.
Wellness:
There’s a 30,000-square-foot Espa that’s shared across the entire Baha Mar complex.
