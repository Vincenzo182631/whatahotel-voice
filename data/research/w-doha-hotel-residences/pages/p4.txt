# Excite your Palate as you Discover Fine Dining in Doha

## Dining

While Doha offers many excellent eateries, you don’t have to step outside the hotel to amplify your dining experience. Some of the most exclusive restaurants in the city are right here at W Doha. With three Michelin stars and international acclaim, you’ll experience the hottest two culinary fine-dining concepts in Doha from celebrity chef Jean-Georges Vongerichten. For fine dining in Doha, Market and...See More Spice Market by Jean-Georges are some of the most highly rated in the city. Guests at our hotel can also experience authentic Italian fare from the renowned La Spiga, Doha by Paper Moon. At this Italian restaurant in Doha, authentic dishes are expertly prepared using some of the finest, fresh ingredients. Additionally, enjoy the unique flavors at our sushi restaurant in Doha, adding another layer to your dining experience. Our dining experiences also extend to other cuisines, with Latin American fare, local delicacies, and light bites available from our other restaurants and bars in Doha.See Less
