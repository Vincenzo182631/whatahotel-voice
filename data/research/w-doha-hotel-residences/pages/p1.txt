# Welcome to W Doha

## Embrace the 5-star lifestyle at our Doha luxury hotel

Welcome to W Doha, where lifestyle luxury meets bold design in the heart of West Bay, just minutes from Hamad International Airport. This Doha 5-star hotel offers effortless access to the Corniche, Katara Cultural Village, the beach, and the city’s most vibrant attractions. Relax in stylish rooms and suites featuring modern design, cutting edge technology, and signature W touches. Enjoy world class...See More wellness at the award winning Sisley Paris Spa, energizing workouts at FIT, and sun soaked moments by the WET pool. Savor Doha’s top dining destinations, from Michelin starred creations by Chef Jean Georges at Market and Spice Market, to authentic Italian flavors at La Spiga by Papermoon, and contemporary Peruvian cuisine at COYA Doha. Traveling for business or events? Our Doha luxury hotel offers nearly 1,000 sqm of versatile meeting and event space near the DECC, perfect for conferences, celebrations, and creative gatherings. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Doha?

The check-in time at W Doha is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Doha allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Doha. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 8.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of ر.ق270.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Doha?

On-site parking is available at W Doha. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Doha have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Doha has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Doha booking.

What is the closest airport near W Doha?

The closest airport near W Doha is Hamad International Airport (DOH). DOH is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Doha have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Doha has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
