Why book?
It’s all about the eclectic energy at the W Doha. Located in the West Bay area, the hotel attempts to bring some youthful energy to the hotel scene. The rooms are popping with color, the cocktails are perfect for sipping pool-side and the tunes are pumping at one of the hotel’s nightclubs. This is a fun place to stay.
Set the scene
Just a short walk from the beach, the neon lit entrance to the hotel sets the scene for the vibrant atmosphere found on the interior. It’s certainly a place to have fun, as well as relax, with bold statement decor, multiple restaurants and bars with music playing until late and a brightly lit rooftop pool and spa area.
The backstory
Designed by world-renowned architect Zaha Hadid, the building is a sculptural wonder, with its fluid lines and organic forms. Constructed in 2009, it was the first hotel of the W brand in the Middle East.
The rooms
The W Hotel has over 440 rooms and suites to choose from, each designed with modern curves, rounded frames, dim mood lighting and subtle touches of traditional Qatari patterns. The options are varied, with the standout Wow Suite offering a staggering 3200 square feet of space and floor to ceiling windows overlooking the Gulf.
Food and drink
Taking inspiration from around the world, the culinary team at W Doha provides a variety of experiences. The signature restaurant, The Spice Market, is host to three-star Michelin Chef Jean-Georges Vongerichten. Otherwise, you can indulge in Italian or Peruvian at the other refined establishments. The rooftop bar is where the playful cocktails are served up, right by the neon lit pool and lounges.
The spa
The Sisely Paris Spa brings all the wealth of knowledge and experience of the French cosmetic brand to Doha. There’s a wide range of treatments available, including anti-aging facials, couples massages, manicures and beauty services. The spa also features a number of relaxation areas, including a sauna, jacuzzi, steam room and outdoor pool with views across the cityscape.
The neighborhood/area
Located in the popular West Bay area, the hotel is perfectly located for work, rest and play. The City Center Doha Mall, Doha Exhibition and Convention Center and a variety of public beaches are all within walking distance.
The service
With all the help you can need, a stay at W Doha is pretty stress-free. The staff are incredibly attentive to all the details and make every guest and visitor feel welcome. From the expert mixologists at the bar to the well-trained staff in the spa treatment area, it’s impossible not to feel well looked after.
For families
The hotel offers spacious accommodation options and a range of fun activities for traveling families. The kids can enjoy the hotel's heated outdoor pool, lazy river, and water slides, or parents can take advantage of the on-site babysitting services at any time of the day.
Eco effort
As part of the global W Hotels brand, it’s accommodation that is committed to reducing their impact. They have implemented water conservation and energy efficiency initiatives across the operations, plus they support a few charitable causes that are important to their values.
Accessibility
The hotel offers a range of amenities and services to meet the needs of guests with disabilities, including wheelchair-accessible rooms and restrooms, Braille signage, visual alarm systems in all areas, assistive listening devices, and sign language interpreters.
