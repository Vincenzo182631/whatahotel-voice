## W Doha Residences

Domesticate as you luxuriate in every amenity you’ve ever dreamed of, from fully-integrated kitchens with modern appliances and complete laundry facilities to state-of-the-art home entertainment systems and magnificent bay windows. Experience stunning city skyline views or West Bay area panoramas. Choose your own spectacular backdrop. Enjoy private access via a separate entrance and reception area and W VIP privileges to all hotel facilities and services, including dedicated parking space. There’s never a dull moment with our fabulous in-room entertainment, featuring a 46-inch IP LCD TV, and Tivoli clock/alarm/radio. Take care of business at the oversized work desk with High Speed Internet Access and IP phone, with a separate cordless phone so you roam wherever you please.

## Embrace Luxury at W Residences

Claim your residence with the signature king-sized W bed—complete with a luscious pillowtop mattress, plush pillows, lavish linens, and goose-down duvet. Enjoy a film on the 42-inch LCD TV or indulge in breakfast in bed. Relax in the splendid bathtub or experience a revitalizing splash in the glass-enclosed rain shower in the spacious bathroom adorned with stone countertops. Post-pampering, envelop yourself in our signature W velour bathrobe. Benefit from available laundry/dry cleaning services. Your luxury, your terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I book for one month only?**

Yes, apartments are available for rent from one month and above.

**Do I need to pay a deposit?**

Yes, for 3-month contracts and above.

**What kind of payment methods do you accept?**

We accept cash, postdated cheques, advance bank transfers and credit cards.

**Can I share with a friend and make him pay with me?**

Yes, this is possible for the 2-bedroom apartment, as the contract will be under both your names.

**How many times do you clean the apartment?**

We fully clean the apartments 3 times per week.

**What floors are apartments located on?**

All the apartments are located on the 16th through 28th floors.

No, utilities are included.

**What if I want to leave early?**

Early departures are subject to contract terms.
