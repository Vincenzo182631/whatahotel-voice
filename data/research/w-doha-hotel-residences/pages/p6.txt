# Experiences and Activities at W Doha

## Experiences

Get local and explore Doha. From towering skyscrapers and sand dunes to ancient architecture and rock carvings, the city of Doha is where majesty meets metropolitan style, art meets archaeology, history meets haute couture and nature meets nightlife. Nestled by the Arabian Gulf and set against a desert landscape, our hotel with an outdoor pool invites you to begin your Arabian adventure on the main...See More promenade, the Corniche. Find yourself surrounded by a bustling city center, home to the Museum of Islamic Art, and postcard-perfect, palm-fringed beaches, where the waters are as warm as the city’s hospitality. Step outside our hotel spa in Doha to explore historical forts, beautiful palaces, and culturally rich museums, or marvel at the skyline from a sunset cruise around the bay. Race camels at Al-Shahaniya or view an unrivaled collection of Arabian thoroughbreds and racehorses at Al Shaqab.See Less

FAQ

**Is there a minimum age for treatments?**

Guests of Sisley Spa must be at least 18 years of age, however hair services are available for children.

**What if I am late for my appointment?**

Arriving late will unfortunately limit the time for your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest will not be delayed. The full value of your treatment will be applied.

**What is the cancellation policy?**

If you must reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify us 24 hours before your scheduled appointment to avoid being charged the full service fee.

**What about payment for Spa services?**

Sisley Spa accepts all major credit cards. You may also charge Spa services to your hotel bill. For your convenience, payment is also accepted prior to your treatments, upon request.

**What about gratuities?**

Additional gratuities are a matter of personal discretion, and should reflect your satisfaction with the services of Sisley Spa.

**What if I have special health considerations?**

Please notify our reservations agents when booking your treatments if you have any sort of medical condition of which we should be aware, such as high blood pressure, allergies, physical disabilities, pregnancy or ongoing chemotherapy treatments. If you have any concerns, please let us know. If at any time during your services you experience discomfort, please alert your therapist immediately.

**Should I shave before a treatment?**

Shaving is recommended but not necessary for men prior to a facial. If you do choose to shave prior to your facial, be sure to do so at least two hours prior to your scheduled appointment. Shaving is not recommended prior to any body treatments or hair removal services.

**What about my jewelry and valuables?**

If you are staying in the hotel, please leave valuable items in the safe in your guest room. Otherwise, lockers are available for your personal items in our facilities and a key will be given to you upon arrival. Please note Sisley Spa is not responsible for any loss of personal items stored in the lockers.

**Do I need to make a reservation for a treatment?**

Sisley Spa welcomes walk-in guests upon availability. However, as one of the best luxury spas in Doha, our services are in high demand hence we do recommend making your appointment at least 3 days in advance.

**When should I arrive?**

For your enhanced comfort and relaxation, we suggest that you arrive 30 minutes before your scheduled treatment. This will allow you to relax in our lounge and utilize facilities. Additionally, we proud ourselves on tailoring your treatments to you. By arriving early, you will be able to share more information with us and your therapist to make the most of your personal spa experience.

**What about my comfort and preferences?**

This is your time and you should delight in the experience to the fullest. Whether it's the room temperature, the amount of massage pressure or the volume of the music, please notify the therapist of any preferences you may have and our team will be delighted to adapt. We request that cell phones be turned off during your visit at Sisley Spa.

**Are Gift Certificates available from The Spa at Sharq Village?**

You may purchase gift certificates for monetary amounts or for select services and pamper someone with a visit to Sisley Spa

**How can I contact you for more details?**

For inquiries and more information, please call +974 4453 5555 or WhatsApp +974 3059 8071.

You may also contact us via email at w.dohwh.sisleyspa@whotels.com

Spa Policies

To enjoy your spa experience to its fullest potential, please familiarize yourself with the Spa Guidelines.

Guests are prohibited from bringing any outside food or alcohol into the spa. A guest appearing intoxicated will be denied access to the spa.

**Method of Payment**

We accept all major credit cards. You may also charge services to your hotel bill.

**Cancellation Policy**

If you must reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify the spa at least twenty-four (24) hours in advance of your appointment to avoid being charged the full-service fee.

**Personal Belongings/Valuables**

We cannot be responsible for any personal items and/or valuables brought into the spa or left in your locker. Please leave your valuables at home or in your room safe.

**Use of Electronic Devices** To protect the privacy of our guests and to preserve the guest experience, use of cell phones, electronic devices and cameras are prohibited in the spa. Cell phones and electronic devices must be turned off.

**Age Requirement**

You must be 18 years of age or older to enjoy the spa relaxation and locker areas. Massages and facials cannot be performed on anyone under the age of 18 years. Salon treatments are available for children 5 years of age or older. Parent must be present for the entire service and a locker will not be provided.

**Treatment Recommendations**

Shaving is not recommended prior to body treatments or hair removal services. Shaving is recommended, but not necessary for men prior to a facial. Your spa experience is your time, and you should delight in it to the fullest. If you experience discomfort due to room temperature, massage pressure, music volume or any other conditions, please notify your therapist immediately.

If you are celebrating any special occasions

Al Koot Fort

Deep in the heart of the city of Doha stands Al-Koot fort. Built in 1927 by Sheikh Abdullah bin Qassim Al-Thani, who governed Qatar between 1913 and 1949, the fort was created to protect the nearby Souq Waqif from would-be thieves.

Katara Cultural Village

An innovative interpretation of the region’s architectural heritage, this purpose-built development’s impressive theatres, galleries and performance venues stage a lively year-round programme of concerts, shows and exhibitions. Among Katara’s recreational attractions are a wide choice of dining options, including top class restaurants offering a variety of cuisines, and a spacious, well-maintained public beach w

Dune Bashing

A top place to visit while you visit Qatar is naturally the desert. The desert offers a challenging terrain for those seeking adventure in Qatar. There are a number of desert-centric activities including renting a dune buggy on the beach near as well as dune bashing, which remains a popular sport for both Qataris & expatriates.

Inland Sea

This breathtakingly beautiful area, surrounded by crescent-shaped sand dunes, is one of Qatar’s finest treasures and a prime tourism asset. A shallow tidal lake with a narrow outlet to the sea, the many-lobed Khor Al Adaid lies between Qatar and Saudi Arabia and has shores in each country.

Zubara Fort

Al-Zubarah fort serves as a pristine example of a typical Arab fort built using the traditional Qatari technique. The Coast Guard used the sturdy fort as a station until the mid 1980s when it was turned into a museum to display findings uncovered in the nearby Al-Zubarah archeological area.

Villaggio Mall

If shopping is your sport of choice, just head next door to one of the most well-known malls in Doha, the Villaggio Mall.

Kayaking Tours

Nestled along the coast near Al Khor there is a vast beautiful mangrove forest &amp; the best way to access it is by kayak. The area supports a lot of bird life and is a favourite destination for nature lovers. Suitable for families and those looking for a relaxed easy paddle time.

Museum of Islamic Arts

The Museum of Islamic Arts contains artifact, manuscripts and precious materials that showcase the historical culture, excellence and intellectual aspects of Islamic Culture.

Souq Waqif

Souq Waqif offers a variety of restaurants and shisha lounges as well as shops for souvenirs, handicrafts, spices and traditional garments.

The Pearl

A man-made island with residential area, hotels, marina, restaurants and retail shops.

Lusail City

Lusail City, just off the West bay lagoon, will be home to residential buildings, island resorts, a commercial district, marina and shopping facilities.

Aspire Zone

For sports fanatics, check out Aspire Zone (Doha Sports City), a 250-hectare international sporting complex just 20 minutes from the hotel.
