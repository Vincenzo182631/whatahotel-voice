## Meet the chef

Our Executive Chef at Hilton Cancun, Rubén Ruelas Gómez, brings heart and heritage to every dish. Inspired by his grandmother’s Oaxacan cooking, Chef Rubén’s journey led him from the U.S. to Le Cordon Bleu in Atlanta, and on to leading acclaimed culinary projects across Mexico. At Hilton Cancun, his signature style blends Mexican, Mediterranean, and Asian flavors, always with fresh, local ingredients. Chef Rubén is passionate about sustainability and community, and creates memorable dining experiences that celebrate culture and bring people together.
