### An Unforgettable Lake Tahoe Vacation

## Things to Do

Explore Olympic Valley’s diverse recreational opportunities! Enjoy ski-in/ski-out access to Palisades Tahoe ski resort, ideal for winter sports enthusiasts. Glide on our private ice rink and explore over six miles of Nordic trails.

Tee off at our championship golf course, unwind with a swim in outdoor pools, and embark on guided fly-fishing excursions. Discover scenic hiking and biking paths, while Lake Tahoe invites water enthusiasts with boating, rafting, and paddleboarding adventures. Your unforgettable experiences await in Olympic Valley and beyond.

### Skiing & Snowboarding

Everline Resort & Spa, a Lake Tahoe ski resort, is located in the heart of Olympic Valley with direct lift service to Palisades Tahoe - the site of the 1960 Winter Olympics.
