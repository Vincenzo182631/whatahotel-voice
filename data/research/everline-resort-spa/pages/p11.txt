### Bearshine Cafe Retail

Bearshine Retail is located in the Cafe. You can shop a wide selection of food, snacks, and sundries, including locally made chocolates and candy apples. Also be sure to check out our beautiful collection of home decor and candles.

### Double Diamond Jewelry

Double Diamond Jewelry has a wide variety of pieces from casual earrings to fine gems, crafted locally here in Lake Tahoe.

### Imprints

### Tahoe Style

### Gift Cards

Another way to shop at Everline Resort & Spa, Lake Tahoe, is through our gift cards. Gift cards can be used for most on-site charges and include:

- Spa Treatments, including massages, facials, and body treatments
- Front Desk charges to your guest room
- Restaurant purchases at Six Peaks Grille, Sandy's Pub, Cascades, and Bearshine Cafe
- Retail purchases at Bearshine Cafe, Everline Sports, Imprints, and Spa at Everline
- Recreational items including golf tee times, tennis, pickleball and bike rentals

Cards can be purchased with preloaded amounts of $20, $50, and $100. Additional custom amounts can also be purchased.Gift cards make great presents, or issue them to attendees as part of a group booking. Stop by the Front Desk or call +1(530) 412-7034412-7034.).
