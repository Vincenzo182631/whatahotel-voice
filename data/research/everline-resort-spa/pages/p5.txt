### Pub Fare, Draft Beers, Tasty Desserts

## Sandy's Pub

Immerse yourself in the rich local history of Olympic Valley as you dine surrounded by the stories past of the Poulsen family. Adorning the walls of our restaurant, ski pictures of Wayne and Sandy Poulsen echo the pioneering spirit that shaped this valley. Enjoy our carefully crafted menu, a tribute to their enduring influence.

From hearty pub fare to refreshing draft beers, every dish tells a story of tradition and passion. And for those with a sweet tooth, our decadent desserts promise a perfect conclusion to your dining experience.
