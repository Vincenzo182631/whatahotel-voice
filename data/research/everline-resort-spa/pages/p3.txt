### Discover an Array of Flavors

## Dining in Olympic Valley

Experience exceptional dining at Everline Resort & Spa in beautiful Lake Tahoe. Our on-site restaurants offer warm service and a diverse range of cuisine, from wood-fired pizza and pub fare to steakhouse and fresh seafood. Enjoy thoughtfully crafted menus featuring fresh, seasonal ingredients, all inspired by our stunning alpine surroundings.
