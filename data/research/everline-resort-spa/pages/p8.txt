Indulge in bold flavors at Six Peaks Grille, Everline’s signature steakhouse. Under the guidance of Chef Watkins, the menu showcases premium cuts, fresh seafood, and seasonal accompaniments, served alongside breathtaking views of the Sierra Mountains in a warm, modern atmosphere.

### Reservations

We are delighted to have the opportunity to serve you. To confirm your reservation, please note that a credit card is required to hold your booking. Cancellations or no-shows will incur a charge of $25/person.

- If your plans change, we kindly ask that you let us know 24 hours in advance.
- Please give us a a call if you anticipate arriving late, we offer a five-minute grace period.
