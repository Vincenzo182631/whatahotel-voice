### A Tahoe Breakfast

## Cascades

Savor the taste of Tahoe at Cascades with our exceptional breakfast buffet. Delight in traditional favorites from the local region, complemented by a Bloody Mary bar, mimosas, fresh juices, and expertly crafted coffee drinks made to order. Start your day on a delicious note amidst the stunning backdrop of Olympic Valley.

### HOURS OF OPERATION

**Opened Monday-Sunday \| 7AM-11AM**

Hours are subject due to seasonality and occupancy levels. Please call us at 530-581-6619 to check hours and availability.
