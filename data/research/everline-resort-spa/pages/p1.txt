### Experience a Mountainside Escape

# Your Elevated Lake Tahoe Resort

Escape to Everline Resort & Spa, Lake Tahoe, nestled in Olympic Valley at the base of Palisades Tahoe, home of the 1960 Winter Olympics. Inspired by Tahoe’s evergreen landscape and the historic railroad line that connected the region, Everline offers the perfect winter retreat with ski-in/ski-out, biking, nordic trails, ice skating, snowcat tours and endless alpine adventures. Call us at 800.404.8006 to make a reservation.
