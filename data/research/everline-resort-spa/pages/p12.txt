### Lake Tahoe Adventures

## Family-Friendly Adventures Await

Discover the beauty of Olympic Valley through our seasonal experiences thoughtfully crafted by our Recreation Team. With the exception of our pools, most activities are open to both resort guests and non-guests. Please note that offerings are subject to change based on weather conditions, and advance reservations may be required.

EVENTS CALENDAR BROWSE EXPERIENCES

### Hours of Operations

Recreation Office \| Mondays - Sundays \| 8AM-8PM

For more information, please call our Recreation Office at 530.584.4024.

### Summer Adventures

Splash into our Oasis Pool Complex with a heated lap pool, waterslide, and whirlpools. Explore the outdoors with guided hikes, bike rides, fly-fishing in private ponds, or paddleboarding on Lake Tahoe.

LEARN MORE

### Winter Adventures

Embark on a winter adventure with ski-in/ski-out access to Palisades-Tahoe, cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, sledding, ice skating, broomball, swimming, and more - all weather permitting.

LEARN MORE

### Area Attractions

The Olympic Valley-Lake Tahoe region offers a diverse array of events, concerts, and activities for the whole family to enjoy.
