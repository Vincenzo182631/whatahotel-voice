## Welcome to

# Rimrock Banff, Emblems Collection

Above the forest canopy, where mountain air runs clear and horizons stretch without end, Rimrock Banff, Emblems Collection rises from the slopes of Sulphur Mountain. As the first Emblems Collection hotel in North America, The Rimrock Banff is shaped by nature, a retreat surrounded by the wild expanse of Banff National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site where the story of the earth is written in peaks, rivers, and sky.

Here, every season reveals its beauty. Spring stirs rivers and meadows into bloom. Summer brings bright days and alpine lakes that glow turquoise. Autumn turns the valleys gold as larches flare in fleeting brilliance. Winter settles in silence, peaks crowned with snow and nights alive with starlight.

To stay at Rimrock Banff is to live within this rhythm, to step into landscapes that stir awe and invite connection. Days bring discovery on trails, summits, and lakeshores. Evenings invite reflection, where the stillness of the Rockies lingers long after the day has passed.

This is more than a destination. It is an immersion into a world elevated by nature, where the mountains shape memory, and every visit becomes part of Banff’s enduring story.
