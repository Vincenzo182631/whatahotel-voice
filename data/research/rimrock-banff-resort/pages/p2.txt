# Rimrock Banff, Emblems Collection

To stay at Rimrock Banff is to feel embraced by the mountains themselves. From the first glimpse of alpine horizons to the quiet stillness at day’s end, every moment is shaped by the landscape around you. Our collection of 320 guestrooms and suites offers more than comfort, they invite reflection, renewal, and the rare chance to slow down in a place where time feels different.
