#### Aurora Chasing

Don’t miss a moment of nature’s most magical light show! With our Aurora Wake-Up Call, you can sign up to be notified if the northern lights make an appearance during your stay. Our team will give you a call and wake you up, so you can step outside and witness the stunning aurora borealis under the clear, starry skies of the Rockies.
