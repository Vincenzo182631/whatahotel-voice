# Parker’s Table

### Dining Anchored in Place and Season

Parker’s Table is the signature dining experience at Rimrock Banff, a celebration of Canadian terroir and the true North. Rooted in the land, every element reflects a deep connection to place, season, and story. The menu evolves with nature, guided by what is fresh, local, and hyper seasonal, ensuring each visit offers something distinct and alive in the moment.

Ingredient driven storytelling is at the heart of Parker’s Table. Producers, growers, and artisans are not just sources but partners, their origins traced through thoughtfully curated maps and narratives that highlight the farms, fields, and waters behind each dish.

Whether you arrive for a celebration or a quiet evening, Parker’s Table welcomes you into an atmosphere of trust, connection, and intention. It is the root system of Rimrock’s culinary ecosystem, anchoring the hotel’s food and beverage philosophy in community, nature, and story, a place where every meal feels both timeless and uniquely of its moment.
