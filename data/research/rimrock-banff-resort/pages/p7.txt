# Discover our

# Nature-Immersed Resort

For over a century, this perch high in the Canadian Rockies has been a place where wonder meets stillness, where explorers, dreamers, and seekers of wellness have found renewal in the mountains.

Today, Rimrock Banff, Emblems Collection, continues that story. Nestled above the valley and just moments from Banff Upper Hot Springs and the Banff Gondola, our setting places you in the heart of the park’s most captivating experiences. From skiing powder dusted peaks to hiking sunlit trails, from mountain golf courses to quiet evenings under star filled skies, the Rockies shape every moment.

More than a resort, Rimrock Banff is an invitation to linger longer, to breathe deeper, and to discover what it means to be truly elevated by nature.
