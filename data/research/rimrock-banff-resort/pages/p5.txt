# Rimrock Market

### From Trailhead Coffee to Après Taps

Rimrock Market is where Banff adventures begin and end, a casual yet elevated hub where mornings begin with handcrafted Chronicle Coffee, fresh pastries, and signature grab and go bites. It is a warm welcome for guests and locals alike, including four legged friends, offering a cozy spot to fuel up before the day’s adventure.

As the day unfolds, Rimrock Market transforms into the après hub of the hotel. Craft beers and cocktails flow from the taps, signature plates and house made gelato make their rounds, and seasonal programming keeps the space lively, from happy hour to fireside Bombardino evenings.

Anchored by Chronicle Coffee and infused with the energy of Banff National Park, Rimrock Market is more than a café. It is the front porch of Rimrock Banff, a place to meet, refuel, and reconnect.
