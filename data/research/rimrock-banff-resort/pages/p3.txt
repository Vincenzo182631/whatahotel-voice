# Social

### The Social Hub of Rimrock Banff

Social is the beating heart of Rimrock Banff, a space alive with rhythm and energy that shifts with the day. In the quiet of the morning, it offers light, globally inspired flavors that awaken curiosity and invite pause before the adventures ahead. As the day unfolds, it transforms into an après hub, where the air hums with music, conversation, and the artistry of crafted drinks shared among friends. By night, Social shifts into tempo, where crafted drinks and music shape the atmosphere.

Social is a hub designed for connection and discovery. Here, a guest might slip in for a thoughtful drink alone or linger with others as the night builds. Every element is considered, from the curated sounds to the flavors on the plate and the pour in the glass, weaving together an atmosphere that feels both relaxed and electric.

Whether you arrive seeking calm or energy, solitude or company, you will find it here. It is Banff’s Social Heart, an ever changing canvas of taste, sound, and spirit.
