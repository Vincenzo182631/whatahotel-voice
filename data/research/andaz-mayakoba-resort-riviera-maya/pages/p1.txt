## Alila Moments

Feel the heartbeat of the Riviera Maya through our wellness resort's signature immersive encounters. An Alila Moment is more than a point in time; it is a gateway to presence, a bridge between Mayakoba’s spirit and your own. By weaving storytelling and ritual, we create experiences that transcend the temporary, becoming a permanent part of who you are.

Explore Alila Moments

Sacred Fire

Guided by the ancient Yucatecan Maya worldview, the sacred fire—Kili’ich K’áak’—serves as a celestial conduit where intentions are carried upward by flame, smoke and breath. In gentle observance of ceremonial protocols and lunar cycles, we offer Mejen K’áak,’ “the Little Fire.” While not a full rite, it is a curated encounter that honors the fire’s essence through the virtues of presence and personal prayer.

The experience is led by Alonso Waxal Ik “Ocho Viento,” a philosopher, writer and priest of the Yucatecan lineage, who translates the ancient language of fire as a messenger to the four horizons. Within the aromatic veil of copal, guests gather to request permission of the land, offering their intentions through sacred chant and reflective silence—leaving the circle transformed by the very light they helped to kindle.

**Location**: On property

**Additional cost** applies

**Dress code**: Resort casual

Ixchel Water Blessing

A sacred tribute to the divine feminine, the Ixchel water purification is a transformative and tender immersion into the Mayan worldview, celebrating water as the sacred, feminine source of all life, governed by the goddess Ixchel (patron of water, the moon and healing). Through a gentle ritual utilizing soft elements like water, clay vessels, flowers and the ancestral chants of the Mayan healer, guests are wrapped in a loving embrace, allowing them to feel nurtured and accepted as children of Mother Earth. This profound journey fosters deep personal purification, self-acceptance and energetic renewal, while connecting participants to the wisdom of the lunar cycles. By reflecting on nature's constant phases of rest, death and rebirth, guests gain resilience and understand that there is a perfect time for every stage of their own personal growth.

**Location**: On property

**Additional cost** applies

**Dress code**: Comfortable active wear

Winds of Renewal

Embark on a personal odyssey at our Mayakoba resort, encircled by the greenery of Riviera Maya. The journey begins in the hush of a private sanctuary, as shared intentions weave a collective spirit. Here, you compose a mandala of the earth before treading a secluded path, accompanied by ethereal melodies that draw the gaze inward.

The trail unfolds into the light of an open cenote for a Mayan water blessing - a communion with the source of all renewal. In the velvet silence that follows, you are led to a hidden cenote for a golden honey ritual and a transformative immersion. As the ceremony closes with gratitude to the four horizons, you emerge grounded in the remembrance that love is not sought, but embodied and eternally renewed.

**Location**: Off property

**Additional cost** applies

**Dress Code**: Comfortable activewear and swimwear required

**Active physical skills** may be required for this activity

Earth & Clay

Be refreshed at our luxury beach resort on the shores of Mayakoba, where the Earth’s bounty meets the sweetness of the soul. This ritual centers on the application of mineral-rich clay and honey, creating a cocoon of restoration that is amplified by the sun’s warmth and the echo of ancient instruments. As the ceremony reaches its climax, you are guided to the ocean for a ritual immersion. Physical and emotional burdens dissolve, making way for a profound sense of self-acceptance and a body grounded in the perfection of the natural world.

## Discover Alila Mayakoba

Spaces for Renewal

Connect with your inner self, with others and the world around you at Alila Mayakoba. From a fitness center outfitted with Peloton™ bikes to an authentic temescal steam lodge and a spa with treatments grounded in physical, mental and spiritual wellness, our spaces for renewal invite you to explore the many dimensions of wellbeing. Discover meditation and movement classes at our wellness center—or pause, stretch and reflect on your own time throughout the abundant tranquil spaces that seamlessly blend the indoors with nature’s splendor.

Ancestral Healing

Alila Mayakoba invites you to embark on a profound journey inspired by the wisdom of the land and its people. Alongside traditional healers and spiritual guides, we offer destination cenote immersions, guided ceremonies, energy cleansings and more ancestral practices deeply rooted in Mayan spirituality and the breathtaking natural wonders of the Yucatán.

Festive Season

At Alila Mayakoba, the holiday season unfolds in harmony with the rhythms of the Riviera Maya. Surrounded by mangroves, lagoons, and the Caribbean Sea, days invite a slower pace, while evenings come alive with special dining, culinary classes, wellness rituals, and celebratory experiences that bring loved ones together.

Sand Meets Sea

Spend a sun-soaked day at our Beach Club, our resort’s restaurant on the shores of Riviera Maya, where flavorful dishes, inventive cocktails, music and sea breezes set the tone for a sparkling vacation. Let us craft a coastal moment just for you, whether it’s snorkeling through coral reefs, private dining toes-in-sand or sailing on the blue waves of Mayakoba.

Culinary Journeys

From local markets to our Chef’s Garden, we source fresh, seasonal ingredients that honor the heritage of the land and are prepared using thoughtful, sustainable techniques. Embark on a sensorial journey through our creative dining venues, take part in hands-on workshops and uncover the rich tapestry of Mayan flavors that continue to shape the culinary soul of the Riviera Maya.

Beyond the Walls

Crafted with local historians, artisans and wellness experts, our Alila Moments are gateways to captivating experiences. Set out from our luxury resort in Riviera Maya and walk among villages and ancient ruins. Venture into the jungle and connect with the historical, cultural, creative and spiritual roots of Mexico through storytelling, hands-on engagement and deep destination immersion.
