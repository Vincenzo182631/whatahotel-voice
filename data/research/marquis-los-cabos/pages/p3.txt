You can't go wrong with any room you book at the Marquis Los Cabos—in fact, it has only suites, which means ample space for relaxing and recharging. Better still, all come outfitted with a hot tub, a furnished balcony, and views of the Sea of Cortez. Expect the requisite slew of restaurants and bars at this adults-only all-inclusive, but it's the extracurriculars that really stand out: Sign up for sport fishing or art walks, or dance the night away at weekly themed parties.
