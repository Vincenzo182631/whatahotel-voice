## Marquis Los Cabos: Luxury Resort in Cabo San Lucas

A luxury adults-only experience in Los Cabos, Mexico, Marquis Los Cabos Resort & Spa includes 209 hotel suites with stunning sea views and 25 enchanting Casitas located just a few steps from the ocean, three beachfront pools, the exquisite Spa Marquis, and five distinctive dining options created by Executive Chef Claudio Hotter. We are dedicated to surpassing our guests’ expectations as a member of The Leading Hotels of the World.

## The Art of Marquis Los Cabos

Your stay can become a treasure hunt by locating all the art pieces on display throughout the resort.

**THE ANGELS, THE INSPIRATION BEHIND MARQUIS**

_“The Angels are flying overlooking a new Paradise. As they approach, they can see Marquis Los Cabos, and something catches their attention, so they land. They set their minds; they have found serenity and decide that this is the place to live, shedding their wings, and as humans dedicated to the comfort of Marquis guests.”_

If the angels have found their serene paradise at Marquis Los Cabos, we are confident that you will.

**THE HARMONY OF THE MARQUIS' MANDALAS**

Hidden in plain sight is a series of mandalas by visual artist VENA2, who made each piece giving significance to the composition's geometry and the numerology behind its elements. For the artist, mandalas are the path to a personal encounter and detonate the subconscious stimulus of well-being.

**LATIN AMERICAN COLLECTION**

We proudly display pieces by world-known Latin American artists throughout the resort as part of our private collection.

**JORGE MARÍN**

_Mexico, 1963_

He is one of the greatest exponents of contemporary figurative art in Mexico. Bronze is his signature element since this material makes it possible to give the anatomical details and textures the perfection of living matter.

_"Art is an indispensable tool to generate more reflective and peaceful societies."_

_A black and white image of a lady at Marquis Los Cabos_

**ANA MERCEDES HOYOS**

_Colombia, 1942_

Considered one of the most important female artists of the 20th century, Ana Mercedes Hoyos has strong Pop Art influences; her creations carry vivid colors that usually mix fruit with everyday life.

_"The awareness of our indigenous past, the conquest by the Spaniards, and the arrival of slaves from Africa strengthen my identity."_

Sustainability

### Embrace It Initiative

Embrace IT is a fund created by the Government of California Sur. This fund encourages sustainable projects and initiatives around the state. Scan the QR to contribute.

### Sea Turtle Protection

Each summer, turtles born in Los Cabos return to create a nest. Our team works diligently with the Sea Turtle Protection Network staff to ensure turtle mothers and babies are always protected.

### Energy-Saving Schedules

The resort has designed programs to make our electrical consumption more efficient according to seasonal needs, successfully reducing our impact on the country's electrical network.

### WTTC Hotel Sustainability Basics Distinction

At Marquis Los Cabos, we are proud to hold the WTTC Standard granted by Hotel Sustainability Basics. This recognition reflects our ongoing efforts to operate responsibly, care for the environment, and provide meaningful travel experiences that respect our destination and community.

### Green Key

We are proud to hold the **international Green Key certification**, one of the leading sustainability certifications in the global tourism and hospitality industry.

### Led Illumination

Our suites use only LED lighting, significantly reducing our electrical consumption in guest areas.

### Recycling Cooking Oil

Cooking oil from all our restaurants is collected by a certified recycling company and used to create biodiesel fuel.

### Recycled-Plastic Bottles

All water bottles provided at the resort are made with 100% recycled plastic, preventing us from being part of the raw material consumption chain.

### Water Desalination System

The resort has a system that converts saltwater for use in hygiene purposes, meeting 100% of our needs and making Marquis Los Cabos a fully self-sufficient property.

### Sewage Treatment System

The resort has two treatment plants to treat and improve the quality of our disposed water, which is utilized for activities that do not require potable water.

Useful Information

Where Is Marquis Los Cabos Located?

Marquis Los Cabos' prime location on the Touristic Corridor offers you the best of both Cabos. We are located 15 minutes’ drive away from San Jose del Cabo downtown and 20 minutes’ drive away from Cabo San Lucas downtown.

What Is Included in The All-Inclusive Plan?

4 Dinning Options, 1 Bar, 1 Snacks & Coffee Bar, Tennis Court, Fitness Area Premium Liquor Brands, 24/7 Suite Service, Coffee Capsules for your Nespresso Machine, Turn Down Service, Minibar refreshed daily, 2 Infinity Pools & 1 Central Pool, Resort Entertainment, Parking, Molton Brown Toiletries, Hydromassage bathtub, Sofa Bed, Electronic Safe, Wi-Fi

Does the Resort Have Daily Activities and Evening Entertainment?

Our Entertainment team hosts a weekly Activities program to add a fun take to your relaxation escape. You can find more information on our Resort Activities page.

What Is the Age Required to Enter the Resort?

The hotel is an adults-only property. All guests must be above eighteen (18) years old to check-in and enjoy the facilities. NO EXCEPTIONS.

Does Marquis Los Cabos Offer a Day Pass?

No, Day Passes are not available as the resort wants to keep an intimate and relaxed atmosphere for all our guests.

Can I Drink Tap Water?

All major hotels and restaurants have purified water for your safety and convenience. Bottled water is available in tourist areas. We do not recommend drinking directly from the tap. Always drink bottled or purified water.

Is the Beach of The Resort Swimmable?

No, the waves are very strong. A swimmable beach can be found approximately 7 minutes’ walk away.

Does the Staff Speaks English?

Yes, English is widely spoken in Los Cabos. Our hotel team is fluent in both Spanish and English.

Is Wi-Fi only Available in The Guest Rooms?

Complimentary Wi-Fi is offered throughout the property including suites, public spaces, and pool areas.

Do I Need an Adapter for My Electronic Appliances?

The standard voltage is 127 V and the frequency is 60 Hz. Most of the US electric appliances can be used in Los Cabos as the standard voltage is fairly similar (120 V). We strongly recommend checking the label on your appliance.

How Much Does the Parking Cost?

The hotel provides complimentary parking if you travel with a car or if you rent one.

How Many Pools Does the Resort Have?

We have two Infinity Pools and one Central Pool. Each of our twenty-five Casitas has its own Plunge Pool. Each of the five Master Suites has its own Plunge Pool. The Spa also has one large Plunge Pool.

Does the Resort Offer Vegetarian Options?

Yes, we have vegetarian, gluten-free, and vegan options. If you are any food restrictions, please let us know.

Can the Lounge Chairs Be Reserved?

We have a wide number of lounge chairs and there is no reservation needed.

What Credit Cards Can Be Used on The Resort?

American · Express · Mastercard · Visa · Discover

You must present the credit card used for the reservation upon checking-in. The property does not accept third-party credit cards to settle a guest's bill.

Do I Need to Book at Any of Your Restaurants?

Yes, Sakke, Pergola, and Canto del Mar have booking tables available at 6:00 pm and 8:00 pm.

Is There Any Fine Dining Option at Marquis Los Cabos?

Yes, Canto Del Mar, is a four-course menu that showcases the best contemporary French cuisine with the local Los Cabos flavors. There is an additional fee per person for dining in this restaurant.

What Time Does the Bar Close?

1:00 am

Are Weddings Allowed at The Resort?

Yes, we have had the pleasure of hosting a lot of weddings, as there are a lot of venues at the resort. You can find more info here and If you are interested in a quote, you can write us at weddings@marquisloscabos.com

What Are the Pool Hours?

Pools are open from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm.

What Services Does the Spa Include?

Spa Marquis has a wide variety of treatments from Manicures, Pedicures, Hair Styling, Polish, Shampoo & Blow Dry, Waxing, Body Scrubs, Body Massages, masks, Hydrotherapy Circuits, and exclusive treatments with Natura Bissé. Spa treatments are not included in the All-Inclusive Plan.

Is It True that Whales Be Seen from The Resort?

Yes, all our suites front face the ocean. In winter you can see lots of whales playing in the morning.

Does It Include Unlimited Beverages and Food?

Yes, the All-Inclusive Plan includes 6 different menu options and premium liquor brands.

Is There a Pool Bar?

No, but the pools are crowded with many waiters, so you will never be short of a drink.

Does the Gym Have Free Weights?

Yes, the gym has free weights and machines.

Does Marquis Offer Timeshare?

No, the resort doesn't offer any kind of time-share so our guests can have a more relaxed and intimate vibe at the resort.

Is Los Cabos Safe to Visit?

Yes, according to the World Travel and Tourism Council, Los Cabos, it's one of the safest destinations for a vacation in terms of health and sanitation. Los Cabos is also considered one of the safest destinations in terms of crime in Mexico.

What Should I Pack for Los Cabos?

The weather in Los Cabos is enjoyable all year round. We recommend packing shorts, swimsuits, tennis, casual outfits, hats, and sunglasses during the day. At night, pack a sweater or light jacket.

Does Marquis Los Cabos Offer Any Tours?

We do not arrange the offsite tours directly; however, the resort has an Activities Desk ( toursanstransfers@marquisloscabos.com) that will provide you with all the necessary information. All the offsite tours have different prices that you should verify with the concierge.

How Far Away Is the Airport from The Resort?

Los Cabos Airport (SJD) is a 35-minute drive from the resort.

How Do I Get to The Resort from The Airport?

Most of our guests request round-trip transportation. Feel free to email us at toursandtransfers@marquisloscabos.com for rates, and we will coordinate your transportation. Car rental is also available for an additional charge.

Is Marquis Reforma Some Way Related to Marquis Los Cabos?

Yes, Marquis Hotels begins its story in Mexico City, on August 26th, 1991, Marquis Reforma is unveiled. After some years, on April 6th, 2003, Marquis Los Cabos is unveiled.

Do I Have to Convert My Dollars to Mexican Pesos?

No, Los Cabos is a destination where most establishments receive payments in US Dollars. Marquis Los Cabos receives US Dollars as well.
