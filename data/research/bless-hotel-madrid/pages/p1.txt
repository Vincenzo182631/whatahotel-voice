Enjoy unique dining experiences, exclusive spaces, and an atmosphere that blends elegance and innovation. Designer rooms and suites, a rooftop pool with privileged views, a spa, a fitness center, and a carefully curated leisure offering make BLESS Madrid a destination in its own right.

A haven of elegance and delicacy, where each space, from our rooftop with exclusive views, to our lounges decorated with impeccable style, are designed to inspire and captivate. A place where luxury is experienced naturally and each detail invites you to enjoy an unforgettable experience.

At BLESS Madrid, gastronomy is converted into a sublime experience that enriches and transforms each moment. We combine the best of local and international cuisine. Discover the culinary art in a luxury, sophisticated atmosphere in each one of our gastronomic spaces: Picos Pardos Sky Lounge, Pinzelada Lounge, Blessed Breakfast and SLVJ.

More information

Discover a space for well-being in our Wellness space: be captivated by the exclusive treatments of Beldon Wellness in the Spa, revitalise yourself at the The Gym, delight your senses with our exquisite BLESSed Breakfast, and enjoy absolute serenity on the Wellbeing Terrace Villa, equipped with a jacuzzi and the innovative Hogo Beds, designed to care for your rest and well-being.
