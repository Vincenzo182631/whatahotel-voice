### Coastal Abundance

Rosewood Kauri Cliffs nurtures relationships with premier local suppliers, ensuring each ingredient is a reflection of the region's essence, and that every dish attains the highest level of quality and taste.

-

**ZEALONG TEA**

Grown in the Waikato on New Zealand’s only commercial tea estate, Zealong crafts award-winning certified organic teas that reflect the purity of their surroundings. A refined expression of terroir, from leaf to cup.

-

**THE TAIPA SALT PIG**

Handmade salt, kissed by New Zealand sunshine, crafted sustainably on Rosewood Kauri Cliffs’ Pink Beach. A taste of pristine coastal luxury.

-

**Leigh Fish**

Leigh Fish is based in the small fishing village of Leigh on New Zealand's northeastern coast, providing a picturesque and unspoilt gateway to some of the finest seafood on Earth. Using low impact, sustainable fishing methods ensures the highest quality wild-caught fish.
