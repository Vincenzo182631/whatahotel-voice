### FIFTH AVENUE AT ITS FINEST

Fifth Avenue is the artery of bustling New York City, and we're lucky to call this prestigious avenue home.

Arguably the most famous address in the world,

Fifth Avenue lets you access nearly every neighborhood in Manhattan with ease.

### THE GOLDEN CROWN

When Gwathmey Siegel — the original architects of The Langham, New York, Fifth Avenue — first drew up the building plans, they aimed to make a mark on the skyline.

Looking at our breathtaking architecture and gleaming golden crown, we're proud to say that dream is now a reality.

Loading...

### ALEX KATZ GALLERY

Upon entering The Langham, New York, Fifth Avenue, you'll notice two massive oil paintings behind the front desk.

Both are by famed New York painter Alex Katz, and are the first in what has become a collection of 10 original artworks displayed throughout the hotel.

### SITE OF DREAMY WEDDINGS

Our event space, The Gallery, has hosted many epic events — from a wedding depicting the four seasons to a party celebrating the return of the Grammys to New York City.
