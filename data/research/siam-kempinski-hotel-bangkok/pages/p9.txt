### Kempinski for Kids

Children stay comfortably and are entertained at Siam Kempinski Hotel Bangkok

With incredible on-site amenities and services, there is truly something for your little ones to enjoy.

- Kempinski Kids Club, located on the ground floor in the Royal Wing, welcomes little guests aged up to 12 years and is open daily from 9:00 to 18:00. Children under three should be accompanied by a parent, guardian or babysitter. For more information, click here.
- Children's menu is available in our restaurants and for in-room dining service.
- Child-sized robes and slippers, baby bath and toiletries.
- Babysitting services can be arranged with surcharges.

**Children’s information**

- Children under five years may share the existing bed, or a baby cot may be requested at no extra charge.
- Generous breakfast buffet for children without any charge (up to five years).
- Children between 6 and 12 years benefit from a 50% discount on the breakfast charge.
- An additional charge of THB 1,545++ per child between 6 and 12 years per night for an extra bed, inclusive of breakfast. Based on the room type selected, we can arrange comfortable bedding on the existing sofa.
- Children above 13 years are chargeable at THB 3,090++ per child per night for an extra bed, inclusive of breakfast. Based on the room type selected, we can arrange comfortable bedding on the existing sofa.

Please contact our Guest Services or Kids Club Specialists for more information by calling +662 162 9000.
