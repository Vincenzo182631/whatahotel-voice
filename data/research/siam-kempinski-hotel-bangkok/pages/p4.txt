### Garden and Pools

#### Enjoy your vacation and relax at our pool or garden, a haven in the heart of Bangkok

Kempinski has a resort atmosphere in the city of frenetic Bangkok. Recognised as a true oasis in the heart of downtown, Kempinski has extensive landscaped gardens with multiple saltwater swimming pools, including one for children. It’s the ideal spot to sunbathe or relax under the shade of the trees whilst listening to the birds chattering.

The swimming pools are open daily from 07:00 to 19:00, with lifeguards on duty throughout operating hours.
