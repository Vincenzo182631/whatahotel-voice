### Kempinski Kids Club

#### A fun array of educational activities keeps our little guests occupied and amused.

The exclusive club occupies 275 sq m on the ground floor and has a contemporary yet safe and cosy vibe. The floor-to-ceiling windows flood the space with natural light and allow for spectacular views across the landscaped gardens and swimming pool. Our indoor playground offers fun and educational activities, interactive games, and arts and crafts to keep our little guests occupied and amused.

The Kempinski Kid’s Club welcomes children up to 12 years. Children under three should be accompanied by a parent, guardian or babysitter.

Open daily from 9:00 to 18:00 hrs.
