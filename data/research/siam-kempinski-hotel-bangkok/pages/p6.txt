### Premier Family Room

This elegant family haven merges two Premier Rooms (king and twin), creating a thoughtfully designed space for comfort and connection. Both rooms offer cozy living areas, desks, and private balconies with lush garden and pool views. Enjoy marble bathrooms with bathtubs/walk-in showers, abundant daylight, and refined interiors. A perfect Bangkok retreat to relax and reconnect.

Maximum occupancy:

6 adults, or 5 adults and 1 child under 12 years old, or 4 adults and 2 children under 12 years old.
