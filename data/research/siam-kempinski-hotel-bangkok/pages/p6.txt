### Bringing Your Pet? We’re Delighted to Welcome You Both

At Siam Kempinski Hotel Bangkok, cherished pets are part of the family — and we’re delighted to extend our warm hospitality to them too. While we have designated room types and areas to ensure the comfort of all guests, furry companions are most welcome and will be looked after with care and comfort.

**Pet Stay Essentials**

- Two pet per room are welcome.
- Weight limit: up to 25 lbs (11 kg)
- Available exclusively in our Garden Wing accommodation:

        • Premier Room

        • Executive Suite

        • Garden Suite

        • Terrace Suite
- Fees: A pet stay fee of THB 1,500++ per night applies.
- Pets must be leashed or in a carrier when outside the guest room.
- For the comfort of all guests, pets are not permitted in restaurants, public areas or the hotel gardens.

**A Thoughtful Stay for Everyone**

To ensure a pleasant experience for all, we kindly ask pet owners to sign our Pet Agreement Form at check-in. It outlines a few simple guidelines regarding safety, cleanliness and shared spaces, which are designed to protect your pet, your peace of mind and the enjoyment of fellow guests.

In-room dining is available throughout the day, allowing guests travelling with pets to enjoy a relaxing meal in the privacy of their room – at their own pace and in their pet’s company.

We also ask that pets be supervised at all times and that owners ensure their companions are quiet, healthy and up to date on vaccinations in line with Thai regulations.

If you’re planning to bring your pet along, we’re happy to assist with preparations. Simply let us know in advance so we may ready your room with care.
