### Room Description

Located in the high-rise building ‘Royal Wing’ high floors, Executive Balcony Room offers peaceful moments after restless sightseeing in the city, with its elegant and comfortable bedroom, living and working areas. Adjacent to the bedroom lies a marble bathroom with bathtub and separate shower. Overlooking from your balcony, relax with calming blue skyline and by sipping your favourite drinks.

Maximum occupancy:

3 adults and 1 child, or 2 adults and 2 children under 12 years old.
