### Executive Lounge

#### **A Haven of Exclusive Comfort**

Open daily from 7:00 to 21:00

The Executive Lounge at Siam Kempinski Hotel Bangkok offers an unparalleled experience of luxury, privacy and exclusivity. Located on floor 17 of the high-rise ‘Royal Wing I’ building, this serene retreat is thoughtfully reserved for guests staying in Executive Rooms, Cabana Rooms, Suites (excluding Family Suites and Two-Bedroom Family Suites) and Duplex Cabanas.

The Executive Lounge provides a comprehensive range of exceptional services designed to enhance your stay. Guests can enjoy personalised check-in and check-out services, secretarial support and exclusive access to private meeting rooms and workstations. Throughout the day, a delightful array of culinary offerings is available, including:

Breakfast: 7:00 to 11:00

Afternoon Tea: 14:00 to 16:00

Evening Cocktails: 17:00 to 19:00

Light meals, snacks and an exquisite selection of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages are served to ensure a memorable experience.

Our dedicated team at the Executive Lounge is here to welcome you warmly and provide seamless, personalised service and thoughtful touches to ensure an unforgettable experience.
