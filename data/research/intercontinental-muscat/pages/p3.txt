## Trader Vics for French Polynesian Fare

Since 2000, Trader Vics has been a top dining destination in Muscat, set by Qurum Beach with views of the Sea of Oman. Created by Vic Bergeron, it is known for its tropical interiors, exotic cocktails, and French Polynesian allure. A global favorite, it is perfect for romantic dinners, beach holidays, and resort guests. Do not miss the signature Crispy Duck, a highlight of this iconic restaurant vibrant experience.

## Authentic Italian at Trattoria Tomato

Bask in the scent of basil and baked crust with poolside alfresco dining and an authentic menu at Trattoria Tomato, the premier Italian restaurant at our beach hotel in Muscat. Enjoy homemade pasta, particularly the Lasagna, or one of the many crispy sourdough pizzas. Fresh seafood and rustic family recipes deliver a taste of Italy under Oman open sky. Whether on a romantic escape or beach retreat, Tomato is a warm, flavorsome stop on your InterContinental Muscat holiday.

## Takara for a Genuine Taste of Japan

Welcome to Takara, a treasured Japanese restaurant that transports guests to the sights, tastes, and experiences of Tokyo with delicate sushi, sizzling Teppanyaki, and a curated sake collection. A renowned gem among Japanese restaurants in Muscat, this elegant dining spot offers more than just cuisine, it is a multilayered cultural journey. Located at InterContinental Muscat, near the Sea of Oman, it is ideal for discerning guests seeking premium dining during their Oman seaside escape.

## Aromatic Arabic at Mynah’s Edge

Dine under the stars at Mynahs Edge, where the bold flavors of authentic Arabic cuisine unfold beside the shimmering Sea of Oman. Savor charcoal grilled prawns, tender lamb chops, and spiced beef kebabs, all paired with vibrant dips. End with a refreshing fruit platter scented with orange blossom and mint. This open air dining lounge captures the soul of Oman, making it perfect for beach weddings, spa escapes, and romantic evenings.

## Al Ghazal, an Original English Pub

Situated inside InterContinental Muscat, just steps from the Palm Beach Club, Al Ghazal is your classic British local, where British charm meets coastal Muscat flair. This traditional English pub surprises with comfort food classics, from Fish and Chips and Shepherds Pie to Boerewors and Mash, served with perfectly poured pints or your drink of choice. Whether it is a casual night out or a cheerful break after a long day, Al Ghazal feels like your favorite lively local pub, just by the sea.
