### Hotel from hell

7 days ago

I booked stays for October 15–16 and 18–19, clearly confirmed by email. On October 16, I discovered the hotel had incorrectly booked my second stay for October 17–18. Staff assured me this was corrected.

Upon returning on October 18, reception insisted I had cancelled my reservation, spending 30 minutes questioning me about refunded points. Despite the hotel's error and my Ambassador status, I was denied a suite and blamed on availability.

On October 19, we returned at 4:30 PM to find our room uncleaned. Management dismissed this as another "oversight" and offered another room while ours was cleaned. I declined. During cleaning, while my wife was showering, another attendant attempted to unlock our door.

The repeated errors, accusations, lack of accountability and disregard for guest privacy were unacceptable. This is an appalling standard of service, particularly for an Ambassador member.

No, I do not recommend this hotel.
