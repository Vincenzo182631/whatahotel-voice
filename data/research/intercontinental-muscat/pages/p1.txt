## FAQs for InterContinental Muscat

Does InterContinental Muscat have a pool?

InterContinental Muscat has 2 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Unwind at the outdoor glistening pool or amidst the lush gardens; with a favored outdoor bar serving light snacks and a broad selection of ice-cold beverages daily from 12:00 to 17:30 pm, creating the ideal spot for unwinding under the Omani sun.

Lap Pool

The 25-meter lap pool at InterContinental Muscat offers dedicated swimming lanes in a calm beachfront setting. Separate from the leisure pool, it is ideal for fitness training, endurance workouts, and uninterrupted lap swimming.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Muscat?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Muscat for hotel guests.

InterContinental Muscat offers hotel valet parking and 300 free spaces for in-house and dine-in guests. Our EV charging stations offer added convenience for guests with sustainable travel in mind, with charging available at a standard service rate.

Parking fee: 0 OMR per day

Vehicles allowed: Bus, Truck

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Muscat?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Muscat.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Muscat for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Muscat have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Muscat has 7 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Muscat?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Muscat.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Muscat?

Check-in at InterContinental Muscat is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Muscat offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Muscat offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Muscat from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Muscat is conveniently located near Muscat International Airport (MCT), approximately 13.05 miles away (21 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Muscat?

InterContinental Muscat is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Jebel Shams - 149.44 miles away (240.5 kilometers)
- Muscat City Tour - 7.39 miles away (11.9 kilometers)
- Nakhal Rustaq - 74.56 miles away (120 kilometers)
- Jabreen Castle - 108.74 miles away (175 kilometers)
- The Royal Opera House
- Barter at Mutrah Souk
- Visit Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque
- Bait Al Zubair and Al Alam Palace

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Muscat puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
