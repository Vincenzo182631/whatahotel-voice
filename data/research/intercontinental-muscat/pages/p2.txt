### Experience Elevated Luxury in our Royal Suite

Experience exceptional comfort in our 250 sq. meter Royal Suite, featuring a spacious living room, two elegantly appointed bedrooms, and stunning sea facing views. With a king size master bedroom, twin bedroom, and connection to a Junior Suite, it offers the perfect setting for families or groups seeking space, privacy, and refined luxury.

Designed for comfort and accessibility, our Accessible Room features a spacious wheelchair-friendly layout, step free shower, grab rails, accessible bathroom amenities, visual emergency alerts, and in-room assistance systems, ensuring a safe, comfortable, and seamless stay for every guest.
