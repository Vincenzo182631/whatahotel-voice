## Club Lounge

Gather with colleagues or relax with friends and family over some light snacks and sweeping views, with exclusive access to a space that is all your own. The Sheraton Club Lounge lets you stay connected in comfort with privileges like complimentary breakfast, all-day snacks and beverages, business center services, and free high-speed Wi-Fi.

### Amiri Family Suite

Experience Sheraton's hospitality in our renovated Amiri Suite, linked to a twin Grand Deluxe Room. With 143 sqm of luxury, this suite offers tranquil views of Dubai's skyline, ideal for extended stays by corporate executives on project assignments or relocations. It features a spacious living room, dining area, fully equipped kitchen, washing machine, microwave, storage, guest bathroom, flat screen TV, and a sofa bed. The master bedroom provides a king-size bed with Sheraton's Signature Sleep Experience, leading to a bathroom with Gilchrist & Soames amenities.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai?

The largest occupancy room at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai is the 3 Bedroom Family Suite with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai?

Yes, all rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have in-room whirlpools?
