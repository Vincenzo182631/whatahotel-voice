# Welcome to Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai

## For a 5-star business hotel in Dubai, look no further

Step into a world of warm welcome at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates, a 5-star hotel Dubai travelers choose for direct access to Mall of the Emirates, seamless Metro connections, and a prime Sheikh Zayed Road address. Our Dubai city hotel is designed for business trips, city breaks, and family stays, with bright rooms and suites, soft finishes, and light wood tones. As one of the standout hotels near Mall of Emirates Dubai, we pair world-class shopping with easy access to the city. Guests praise the attentive service, cleanliness, and comfortable rooms, while families value the spacious feel and mall-connected setting. Savor authentic Turkish cuisine, catch live sports, and take in 360° skyline views where Japanese elegance meets Peruvian vibrance. Among Dubai family hotels, Sheraton stands out with Sheraton Fitness, pampering spa treatments, and refreshing rooftop outdoor pools.

Advisory - Travel to Middle East

We continue to closely monitor the current regional situation in the Middle East as it evolves. Our hotel remains open and operational and our teams are focused on supporting our guests at this time.

### Mall of the Emirates: Direct Connection

Mall of the Emirates is the world’s first shopping resort, in the heart of Dubai. It features about 630+ brands, in an area of 233,467 sqm. It includes department stores, fashion and lifestyle. It also has sports, electronics, home furnishing and the largest Carrefour in the city. The mall is the perfect destination for families. It offers fun attractions and activities such as VOX Cinemas and Ski Dubai. On top of that, with more than 100 dining and café selections, the mall has something for everyone.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai?

The check-in time at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai?

The closest airport near Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai is Dubai International Airport (DXB). DXB is located approximately 31.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai have a Sheraton Club lounge?

Yes, Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai has Sheraton Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Sheraton Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
