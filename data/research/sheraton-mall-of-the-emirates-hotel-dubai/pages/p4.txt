## Dining

Sheraton Mall of the Emirates Hotel, Dubai brings warm Arabic hospitality to every table. With four restaurants and bars in Dubai, enjoy dining throughout your stay, from brunch and coffee to sports screenings, rooftop drinks, and distinctive evening meals. Spartan Sports Bar is a lively spot to unwind over gastropub fare, craft hops, and shisha after work or a day in the city. Besh Turkish Kitchen serves authentic Turkish flavors, while OAnjo on the 24th floor pairs Japanese elegance with Peruvian vibrance and sweeping skyline views. At &More by Sheraton, fresh coffee and all-day dining suit quick breaks and casual meetings. Guests often praise the breakfast and attentive service, and our restaurant in Dubai lineup is made even more convenient by direct Mall of the Emirates access.
