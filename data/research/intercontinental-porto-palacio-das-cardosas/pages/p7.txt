### Fabulous experience in Porto InterContinental Hote

há 7 dias

My wife and I thoroughly enjoyed our stay at the InterContinental Porto. All the staff members were friendly, attentive, and highly professional, which made us feel welcome throughout our visit.

As Platinum Elite IHG members, we had hoped to receive a room upgrade, but we were informed that the hotel was fully booked. Nevertheless, we were very pleased with our room, which was spacious and beautifully decorated in a modern style. The bathroom featured both a bathtub and a separate shower, while the striking red marble vanity countertop with delicate white veining added a distinctive and romantic touch. The room also included a small sitting area that opened onto a balcony overlooking the statue, providing a lovely place to relax.

We especially enjoyed spending time in the lounge on the main floor each evening after returning from our full-day tours. It was a wonderful place to unwind and relax. Breakfast was another highlight of our stay, with an excellent selection of delicious food each morning.

The only minor disappointment was the wait for coffee at breakfast. We would have loved to start each morning with a nice cup of coffee, but we often had to wait 10 to 15 minutes to be served. Eventually, I asked the staff for a coffee pot so we could serve ourselves rather than wait for another refill. A more efficient coffee service would have made the breakfast experience even better.

We didn't have dinner at the hotel, as there were so many wonderful restaurants nearby that we wanted to explore. However, based on our overall experience, I have no doubt that the hotel's restaurant offers a dining experience worthy of the property's high standards.

Overall, we had a wonderful stay at the InterContinental Porto. The spacious accommodations, elegant decor, welcoming staff, and relaxing atmosphere made our visit memorable. We would happily recommend this hotel to anyone planning a visit to Porto and would gladly consider staying here again on a future trip.

The hotel itself is stunning, and our room was beautiful, elegant, comfortable, and thoughtfully appointed—a wonderful place to relax after a day of exploring.

The dining experiences were equally impressive. Breakfast was a highlight, with a wonderful selection and excellent quality to start each day. Dinner was delicious, and the bar was a fantastic spot to unwind and enjoy a drink in a beautiful atmosphere. Every experience felt special and lived up to the hotel's high standards.

Their hospitality made a lasting impression on us.

We couldn't have asked for a better hotel for such a special occasion. Between the beautiful accommodations, outstanding food and drinks, unbeatable location, and exceptional service, the InterContinental Porto exceeded our expectations.
