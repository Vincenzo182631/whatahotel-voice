# Luxury Hotel in Guangzhou, China \| Park Hyatt Guangzhou \| Hyatt

## Gateway to Guangzhou’s rich cultural tapestry

Set on the uppermost floors of the Yingkai Square Tower, steps from the Pearl River embankment, Park Hyatt Guangzhou rises 286 metres above Guangzhou’s central business district. From this elevation, views across Zhujiang New Town extend toward the Canton Tower and along the river, tracing the contours of the city below. Interiors by Super Potato are composed with restraint, guided by tactile materials, and light, with subtle references to Lingnan architecture expressed through layered spaces and generous proportions.

## Discover Park Hyatt Guangzhou

Kids at the Park

At Park Hyatt Guangzhou, families are well taken care of with special amenities and services designed for children. From baby cribs to kids bathrobes and slippers, we provide everything needed for a comfortable stay. Our kids menus at all venues offer delicious options, ensuring dining is a delight for the whole family.

Welcome to the Park

Whether you’re seeking cultural experiences, culinary adventures or simply immersion in the local scene, our bimonthly “Welcome to the Park” brochure is your guide to unlocking the best of what the city has to offer.

Rising Above Yingkai Square

Occupying the top 14 floors of the R&F Yingkai Square skyscraper, Park Hyatt Guangzhou graces one of the city’s tallest buildings. Designed by the renowned Chicago-based architecture firm Goettsch Partners, this towering landmark rises 296 meters into the sky. Its striking profile, characterized by delicately sculpted corners and graceful vertical stripes, emanates a captivating sense of perpetual motion.

Lingnan Mansion in the Sky

Envisioned by Tokyo-based design firm Super Potato, Park Hyatt Guangzhou’s interior embodies timeless elegance and refined luxury. Drawing inspiration from the rich cultural heritage of the Lingnan region, every element of the hotel’s design tells a story, weaving together tradition and modernity to create a harmonious tapestry that captivates imagination. Materials such as rich woods, sumptuous fabrics and intricate detailing adorn the spaces, inviting guests to engage their senses.

Art at the Park

Incorporating sculptures and installations inspired by the legendary dragons of nearby Baiyun Mountain, Park Hyatt Guangzhou celebrates the city’s rich cultural heritage and mythical allure. Japanese artist Tadashi Kawamata’s masterful creations, crafted from reclaimed wood, evoke the majestic spirit of these mythical creatures, adding a touch of enchantment and reverence to the hotel’s ambiance.

Elevating Every Experience

As guardians of Jay Pritzker’s vision for the Park Hyatt brand, we uphold the highest standards of hospitality, weaving a tapestry of personalized experiences and bespoke journeys that epitomize luxury. Our concierge team, distinguished by the iconic golden keys, serve as master storytellers, unveiling the soul of the city with every personalized recommendation.

The Spa

Bathed in natural light high above the city, The Spa at Park Hyatt Guangzhou is a sanctuary of warmth and calm. Earthy walls, soft wooden tones, and modern art create a setting where time slows and the senses are soothed.

#### Canton Tower View

1 / 4

Rooms and suites with Canton Tower view feature the most captivating vistas. Facing south, guests are treated to an unobstructed view of the iconic 604-meter-high Canton Tower (1,981 feet) across the Pearl River. These rooms also overlook Huacheng Square, Guangzhou’s largest square, and the scenic Haixinsha Island. As night falls, the skyline dazzles with the spectacular light show on Liede Bridge and Pazhou’s central business district.

#### Pearl River View

2 / 4

Rooms and suites with Pearl River view offer an expansive view either south or west. Enjoy unobstructed vistas stretching to the horizon, encompassing the lush grounds of the U.S. Consulate below and views upstream along the Pearl River. Ersha Island and numerous bridges crossing the river add to the scenic panorama, while the bustling traffic on Guangzhou Bridge provides a dynamic contrast. These rooms offer the most spectacular sunsets, particularly those that face west.

#### Skyline View

3 / 4

Facing east, rooms and suites with Skyline view overlook the verdant expanse of Zhujiang New Town’s green lung. Surrounded by a cluster of towering high-rises — including the Guangzhou International Finance Center and China’s third-tallest building, the Chow Tai Fook Finance Center, rising 530 meters (1,738 feet) — these rooms offer sleek urban views. Ideal for early risers, guests can enjoy the tranquil beauty of the sunrise each morning.

#### City View

4 / 4

Facing north, rooms and suites with City view present a breathtaking perspective. Guests are treated to a glimpse of Baiyun Mountain and a panoramic sweep of the northern section of Zhujiang New Town’s central axis. This view captures the grandeur of several towering high-rises, including the neo-futuristic Pearl River Tower and the Yuexiu Financial Tower, each standing over 300 meters tall (984 feet).

## Worthy Pursuits

Located in the heart of Zhujiang New Town, Park Hyatt Guangzhou places the city’s cultural landmarks, riverside walks, museums, shopping areas and family attractions within easy reach. Canton Tower, stands across the Pearl River within walking distance and offers panoramic views from its observation decks. Nearby, Huacheng Square, Guangzhou Opera House, Guangdong Museum, and the riverside promenade offer an introduction to Guangzhou’s architecture, art, and urban life. Guests may also explore the Pearl River by evening cruise, or discover the city’s wider cultural landscape through museums, galleries, temples, markets, and historic neighbourhoods.
