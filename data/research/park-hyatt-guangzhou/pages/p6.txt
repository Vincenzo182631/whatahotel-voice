# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self-Parking

For guests arriving by car, self-parking is available in the basement of Yingkai Square Tower, accessible from Zhujiang West 4th Street with direct elevator access to the hotel. For those needing luggage assistance, it is recommended to use the main entrance to unload luggage before parking. The car park is independently operated, and parking spaces are available on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to actual availability. For guests with electric vehicles, 24-hour charging stations are available across the street at R&F Center.

Getting here

Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN)40 kilometers40 kilometers

Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport is the city’s primary international gateway, offering extensive domestic and global connections. With three terminals and five runways, it is among the world’s busiest airports and serves as the main hub of China Southern Airlines.

Chauffeured Transportation

Park Hyatt Guangzhou offers chauffeured transportation with a selection of sedans, minivans, and electric vehicles. All vehicles are equipped with wireless internet access, charging cables, and complimentary refreshments. Pricing is all-inclusive, covering road tolls, parking fees and value-added tax. Navigation Guidance for International Guests

For seamless navigation during your stay in Chinese Mainland, we recommend using local mapping applications such as Gaode Map or Baidu Map. International mapping apps may not be accurate in Chinese Mainland.

Getting around

Public Transportation

Directly connected to Zhujiang New Town metro station, served by lines 3 and 5, and conveniently located within walking distance to Huacheng Boulevard metro station, served by the APM line, Park Hyatt Guangzhou provides seamless access to Guangzhou’s extensive public transportation network.

Pedestrian Network

Yingkai Square is seamlessly integrated into Zhujiang New Town’s extensive underground pedestrian network, offering guests easy access to an array of landmark destinations within walking distance. From myriad retail offerings at GT Land Spring Plaza and IFC Mall to the seven-story experience of K11 Art Mall and the cultural immersion of Guangzhou Opera House, guests can explore their vibrant surroundings with unparalleled convenience.

Canton Fair Shuttle Service

During the Spring and Autumn Canton Fair, Park Hyatt Guangzhou offers a dedicated shuttle service to and from the Canton Fair Complex. With six departures in the morning (starting at 8:30 AM, with the last at 10:20 AM) and six return trips in the afternoon (starting at 4:45 PM, with the last at 6:30 PM), this service ensures convenient transportation for fair attendees. Guests are encouraged to check with the Concierge for the latest schedule and specific days of operation.
