# Restaurants & Bars

Start your day at radii Restaurant, where a gourmet breakfast buffet and à la carte specialties highlight the finest local ingredients. For relaxed all-day dining, The Lounge & Garden offers vibrant, seasonal dishes perfect for casual meetings or light bites. Indulge in an elegant high tea, set against a relaxed outdoor setting surrounded by greenery. As the evening unfolds, unwind at radii Bar, where expertly crafted cocktails, fine wines, and premium spirits create the perfect setting for a pre-dinner drink or a nightcap. Prefer to dine in? Our in-room dining service is available until late, ensuring a seamless culinary experience at any hour. Whether it’s a leisurely breakfast, a refined weekend treat, or a gathering with friends, Park Hyatt Melbourne promises a truly memorable dining experience.

## The Lounge & Garden

Whatever the occasion, enjoy a relaxed yet refined dining experience at Park Hyatt Melbourne.
