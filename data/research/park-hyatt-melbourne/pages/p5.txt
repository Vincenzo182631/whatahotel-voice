### A Relaxing Stay with a Beautiful Cathedral View

We stayed in a Deluxe King Room with a lovely view of St Patrick’s Cathedral. The room was spacious and comfortable, and we really enjoyed waking up to the beautiful cathedral view.
The hotel is in a great location, right next to the park, which was perfect for a relaxing morning walk. It’s peaceful and quiet, yet still within easy reach of the city centre.
The staff were friendly and helpful, especially at the front desk, and we felt very welcome throughout our stay.
