# Luxury Hotel & Accommodation \| Park Hyatt Melbourne

## Luxury Hotel in Melbourne

Overlooking St. Patrick’s Cathedral, Fitzroy Gardens, and the cosmopolitan mix of Victorian and modern architecture, Park Hyatt Melbourne offers exclusive, luxury accommodation in the heart of the city. Our beautifully appointed 5 star hotel is centrally located in Melbourne’s CBD, offering the city’s largest 245 guestrooms and suites. A warm ambience, exceptional levels of personalised service and luxurious amenities with exquisite surrounds - provides guests a private haven in the heart of the city.

### Dogs Are Welcome

At Park Hyatt Melbourne, we also extend our signature hospitality to guests on four legs. Our dog-friendly offering invites your canine companion to enjoy the same level of comfort and care as you. From serene garden strolls to sunlit naps to in-room indulgence, every detail is designed with your pooch in mind. Each dog enjoys a plush bed, food and water bowls, a welcome treat and exclusive access to our in-room dining menu for Very Important Pooches. You may even spot Charlie, our resident pup and unofficial ambassador, always ready with a wag and a warm “hello.” View our Hotel Policies page for more information.

## Things To Do

Situated in Melbourne’s central business district, Park Hyatt Melbourne is close to the area’s top attractions. Just two minutes from our doorstep, explore Fitzroy Gardens, a 26-hectare park that features a conservatory, picturesque statues and fountains, various cottages, a book shop and more. Visit Art Centre Melbourne, the city’s landmark performing arts center, or hike the trails at Main Yara Trail Kew, 5 kilometres from the hotel.
