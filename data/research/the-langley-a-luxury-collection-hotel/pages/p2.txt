# Accommodations at The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire

## Accommodations

Withdraw to one of forty-one exquisitely appointed rooms and suites, designed by Dennis Irvine studio. Dignified roll-top baths steal the show and expansive suites with grand windows frame the picturesque...See More grounds and shimmering lake. All rooms and suites in our Buckinghamshire accommodation marry modern tastes with rich history and uncompromising luxury. Baroque fireplaces and ornate doorways have been painstakingly re-established in the main house. In the Grade II listed outbuilding, the Brew House, characterful rooms reflect the playful nature of former owners who brewed their own ferments.See Less

### Duke of Marlborough Suite

Overlooking the grounds and the lake, the Duke of Marlborough Suite comprises a prestigious adjoining living room, three beautifully presented bedrooms, and Athenian marble bathrooms. The former room of the third Duke of Marlborough has been transformed to a luxurious signature suite that promises an immersive and memorable experience in the heart of Buckinghamshire.

## Families

Relax into our expansive connecting rooms and suites, perfect for families including children's robes, slippers and bathroom amenities. Baby sitting services can be arranged on request with our Concierge.

## Dogs at The Langley

With acres of tree-lined tracks and footways, the Langley Park and the surrounding area present countless opportunities to explore and wander from the beaten path. So, naturally, we’re more than happy to accommodate four-legged companions in our beautiful Terrace Rooms, located in the Brew House, and throughout the grounds. Regrettably though, we’re not able to welcome dogs into the Main House. A surcharge of £30 per dog, per night is applied.
