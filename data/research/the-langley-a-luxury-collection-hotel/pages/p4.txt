## Ladies Thermal Area

Blissful privacy and discreet comfort await in your private sanctuary. Melt into a pool of bliss and enjoy exclusive use of our ladies-only thermal suite. Immerse yourself in the rose quartz steam room and samarium, float in the vitality pool or relax on a heated lounger, where paradise is no doubt found.

## Spa Treatments

Engage each and every sense with our collection of precise, results-driven treatments. Our spacious treatment rooms, including a double treatment suite is complete with a private changing room and offer a comfortable setting for individual and couples. An entirely unique experience awaits you, curated by our expert therapists.

## Country Pursuits

Discover quintessentially English activities in the countryside, from falconry on an English estate, to horse riding in Windsor Great Park and learning how to play polo. Our expert partners can create a bespoke itinerary to fit individuals, groups and families. Contact our Concierge team for more details.

### Langley Park & Grounds

Discover the historic former royal hunting grounds of the third Duke of Marlborough. Twelve hectares of park and woodland, where Lancelot ‘Capability’ Brown was architect to uninterrupted landscapes over England’s home counties.
