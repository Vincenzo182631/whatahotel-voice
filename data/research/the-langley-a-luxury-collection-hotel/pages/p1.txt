# Welcome to The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire

## Discover 5-star timeless luxury at The Langley

Surround yourself with incomparable style at The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire. Beautifully situated in the former manor home of the third Duke of Marlborough on 150 acres of formal...See More gardens and parklands originally designed by Lancelot ‘Capability’ Brown, this hotel in Buckinghamshire offers an exclusive luxury retreat in the heart of Iver's countryside. Relax in our sophisticated rooms and suites, each meticulously restored with modern furnishings amidst historic features. Our opulent spa hotel in Buckinghamshire offers holistic wellness treatments, along with an indoor and outdoor pool, a thermal area, fitness programs, and healthful dining selections.See Less

### Cedar

Cedar at The Langley captures the spirit of European cuisine, featuring many influences from Spain, France and the best of Italian flavours at its heart. All ingredients in our fine-dining restaurant in Buckinghamshire are sourced from local suppliers.

### Churchill Bar

Hideaway in this intimate retreat featuring light bites, sandwiches or our Chef's tempting creations. The welcoming setting offers a curated selection of fine spirits, cognac and cocktails, alongside a rare choice of cigars stored in a custom humidor.

### Drawing Room

Experience an authentic Afternoon Tea in Buckinghamshire in the classic decor of our restaurant. Appointed with historic features, original artwork and contemporary furnishings, this is the perfect spot to view our landscaped gardens and lake.

### A Historical & Architectural Masterpiece

## Steeped in History

Sensitively restored, the Palladian-style mansion house and adjoining Grade-II listed Brew House offer 41 sumptuous guestrooms and suites. The Grade II-listed gardens were re-designed in the 18th century by Lancelot ‘Capability’ Brown.

Learn More

## Escape to the Countryside

All things slow and natural beauty bounds in the heart of the Buckinghamshire countryside. Look out to the picuresque surroundings of Langley Park, all found within a short car or train journey from central London.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire?

The check-in time at The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire.

What are the parking options at The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire?

On-site self parking is available at The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire booking.

What is the closest airport near The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire?

The closest airport near The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire is Heathrow Airport (LHR). LHR is located approximately 12.2 KM from the hotel.

Does The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Langley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Buckinghamshire has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
