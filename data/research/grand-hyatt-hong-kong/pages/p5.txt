### Outstanding Hotel with Classic Hong Kong Elegance

The Grand Hyatt Hong Kong remains an outstanding hotel, combining classic Hong Kong elegance with exceptional management under General Manager Mark Holmes.

The service and attention to detail are impeccable and, in many ways, surpass those of other five-star Hong Kong hotels where I have stayed, including the Rosewood, Peninsula, Ritz-Carlton, and Four Seasons. From the moment you enter the lobby, you immediately understand what makes this hotel so special. It is beautifully designed, exceptionally elegant, and offers some of the most spectacular close-up views of Victoria Harbour from many of its rooms.

The hotel’s restaurants are also excellent, particularly Grissini and Grand Café. Grand Café is even better than before—the breakfast wontons are now made with large, fresh shrimp, and the Hainanese chicken rice is exceptional.

The Grand Hyatt Hong Kong is truly a must-stay hotel. Be sure to enjoy its outstanding restaurants while you are there as well.
