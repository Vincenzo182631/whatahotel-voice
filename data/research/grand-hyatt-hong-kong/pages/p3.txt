The restaurant’s convivial, unpretentious ambience and its passionate and personable service also contribute to a memorable dining experience. (Opening Hours: 11:30 AM / 12:00 PM – 12:00 AM)

One of the notable Selected Restaurants recommended by MICHELIN Guide Hong Kong & Macau 2025. (Opening Hours: 12:00 PM – 12:00 AM)

Located on the 11th floor tucked away from the hustle and bustle of the city, Waterfall Bar is an all-day alfresco poolside lounge featuring specialty coffees, home crafted iced teas, refreshing cocktails, wines and snacks all day.

The evenings reveal a selection of signature cocktails crafted by our hotel mixologist, accompanied by a dynamic whiskey selection for guests to enjoy in a relaxing ambience while being surrounded by an artfully landscaped oasis. (Opening Hours: 9:00 AM - 11:00 PM / 12:00 AM)
