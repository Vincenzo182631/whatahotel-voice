## EXPLORE

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

Caring for the planet is a natural extension of Hyatt’s purpose – to care for people so they can be their best. At Grand Hyatt Hong Kong, we are taking steps to help make a difference.

Muslim-Friendly Travel

With the globally-recognized Crescent Rating of 5 signifying a certified standard level of Muslim-friendly hospitality, Grand Hyatt Hong Kong is fully equipped to cater to Muslim travelers, offering a range of services upon request to ensure a seamless and hassle-free stay. With an extensive selection of renowned mosques and an abundance of Halal-friendly certified restaurants including the hotel’s all-day dining restaurant, Grand Café, Muslim travelers can enjoy an exceptional experience in Hong Kong.​​

Grand Club

Grand Club provides a luxurious lounge exclusively for suite and club floor guests. It is a perfect location to unwind with a sumptuous menu that changes throughout the day, from exquisite breakfast selections to light refreshments, all-day Champagne and evening cocktails—all under the dramatic panorama of Victoria Harbour through floor-to-ceiling windows.

Outdoor Pool

Located on the 11th floor of our hotel, the 50-metre heated swimming pool commands spectacular views of the Central skyline. Guests can relax on the poolside sun loungers while enjoying refreshments. Hours are daily from sunrise to sunset. Access to the pool is complimentary for registered hotel guests and fitness centre members.

Fitness Center

Our Fitness Centre has an extensive range of Life Fitness exercise equipment and classes, including complimentary active wear rental services for guests. It has been designed to create a multitude of functional training spaces for free weights, strength training, abdominal training and stretching.

Plateau Spa

Located on the 11th floor of Grand Hyatt Hong Kong, the Plateau Spa is a self-contained resort within our hotel, offering privacy, seclusion and an escape from the vibrant city. Celebrate the wellbeing of yourself and loved ones with award winning, result oriented treatments conducted in private treatment rooms with en suite bathrooms. Hours are daily 10 a.m.–10 p.m.
