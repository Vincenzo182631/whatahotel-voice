# Pet Friendly Hotel in NYC

We don't simply allow pets at The Greenwich Hotel, they are some of our most beloved guests

## At The Greenwich Hotel, we welcome well-behaved dogs with the same thoughtful hospitality we extend to every guest.

Spacious accommodations, a quiet residential neighborhood, and easy access to some of Manhattan’s best waterfront parks make Tribeca one of the city’s most enjoyable places to stay with your four-legged companion.

While many visitors assume Central Park is the only destination for dogs in New York, locals know that Lower Manhattan offers a more relaxed experience. From morning walks along the Hudson River to neighborhood cafés and tree-lined streets, you’ll find plenty of opportunities to explore together just steps from the hotel.

Whether you’re visiting for a weekend getaway or an extended stay, The Greenwich Hotel provides a luxurious home base for both you and your pet.

## Explore Tribeca    with Your Dog

**The Tribeca neighborhood is known as one of the most dog-friendly neighborhoods in all of Manhattan.**

Dogs visiting will be in good company when venturing out for a walk, as our tree lined and quiet cobblestone streets in the neighborhood have attracted many families with pets to choose Tribeca as their home. Hudson River park, a 5 minute walk from the hotel, conveniently features a four mile path with the city’s most beautiful views, including the Statue of Liberty and the downtown skyline. One of it’s highlights for dogs being just one block west from our doorstep, Pier 25 & 26 and the Tribeca Dog Park, which features an off leash area with water features and climbing structures.

**Nearby highlights include:**

- Hudson River Park
- Tribeca Dog Run
- Rockefeller Park
- Battery Park Esplanade
- The Hudson River Greenway

Whether you’re heading out for a quick morning walk or spending an afternoon exploring downtown Manhattan, you’ll find plenty of pet-friendly spaces within easy walking distance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are dogs and cats allowed at The Greenwich Hotel?

Yes. Well-behaved dogs and cats are welcome. Please contact the hotel prior to arrival for current pet policies.

### Are there places to walk my dog nearby?

Absolutely. Hudson River Park, Pier 25 Dog Run, and several neighborhood parks are all within walking distance.

### Can the concierge help arrange dog walking?

Yes. Our concierge can recommend trusted local professionals for dog walking, pet sitting, and other pet services.

### Are there pet supply stores nearby?

Yes. Several neighborhood pet boutiques and supply stores are located within Tribeca and the surrounding downtown neighborhoods.

### Is there an additional fee or size limitation?

While there is no additional fee or size limitation to pets welcome at the hotel, a waiver is required to be signed upon check in by their responsible owners.

### Where in the hotel are pets welcome?

In addition to their accommodation, pets are welcome to enjoy the hotel’s Drawing Room, Courtyard, and Lobby areas on leash.

### Can I leave my pet alone in the room?

For the comfort and safety of your pet, pets should not be left unattended in guest rooms. If you need to step out without your pet, our concierge team is happy to help recommend local pet care services.
