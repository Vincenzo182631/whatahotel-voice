## Frequently Asked Questions

Restaurants and Bars

Do I need a reservation for the restaurants at Eau Resort & Spa?

Yes. Reservations are recommended, especially for dinner and peak dining hours, to ensure availability at our Palm Beach restaurants.

What types of dining experiences are available at the resort?

The resort offers a range of dining options, including fine dining, oceanfront restaurants, casual daytime dining, cafés, and bars.

Are there oceanfront restaurants at Eau Resort & Spa?

Yes. Select restaurants offer oceanfront dining in Palm Beach, with views of the Atlantic and open-air settings.

Which restaurants are best for dinner or special occasions?

Nobu and Angle are ideal for dinner and special occasions, offering refined menus and elevated dining experiences.

Are there casual dining options available during the day?

Yes. Breeze Ocean Kitchen, Stir, Savor, and Oceana offer casual dining for breakfast, lunch, and daytime experiences.

Can non-hotel guests dine at the restaurants?

Yes. Non-hotel guests are welcome at our Palm Beach restaurants, though reservations are recommended and availability may vary.
