The epitome of modern luxury, located just off the Magnificent Mile

Why book?
Check in here for modern luxury in Millennium Park, with all of the onsite draws—an acclaimed restaurant, private lounge, and full-service spa.
Set the scene
The property overlooks Lake Michigan, Millennium Park, and
Chicago
’s sprawling skyline, giving it an edge when it comes to location. There are 686 guest rooms, all of sleek, sophisticated design, and a recent renovation of the property’s Fairmont Gold guest rooms and suites yielded fresh, reimagined spaces. That renovation extended to the 15th floor’s Fairmont Gold lounge, where Gold guests can retreat for breakfast and all-day snacks, concierge services, and stunning city views. Chef Richard Sandoval’s Toro restaurant offers Latin American flavors throughout the day, and guests can visit Leaf Spa to unwind with a massage, facial, or body treatment. Expect to see a mix of business travelers and families in town looking to explore, plus those staying onsite for a number of special events hosted year-round (from weddings to galas).
The backstory
The hotel opened in December 1987 and today is owned by international hospitality group Accor, overseeing more than 45 hotel brands across more than 100 countries.
The rooms
Rooms are spacious and simplified, with neutral hues, bespoke furnishings, and layered lighting. Bathrooms are outfitted with marble finishes, rainfall showers, Le Labo amenities, plush robes, and professional-grade hairdryers. Book a Deluxe Cityscape or Signature Parkview room for prime views of Millennium Park or Lake Michigan—or enjoy those sights from the Fairmont Gold Lounge by booking in with one of the property’s Farimont Gold rooms or suites.
Food and drink
Toro is Chef Richard Sandoval’s interpretation of pan-Latin cuisine, featuring dishes like ceviche and empanadas, along with breakfast and brunch favorites like tres leches pancakes and Shrimp & Corn Enchiladas (plus a cocktail menu featuring South American spirits). Guests can also head to the lobby bar for breakfast, coffee, or evening drinks.
The spa
Leaf Spa features a range of nature-inspired, holistic treatments that draw inspiration from the seasons. Post-treatment, guests can enjoy the steam room, meditation room, or soaking tub.
The neighborhood/area
Close to shops, landmarks, museums, and the lake, the area is one of the best for exploring some of the city’s iconic sites.
The service
A knowledgeable Guest Services team is at the ready to assist with a range of requests, from coordinating transportation to helping with itineraries.
Eco effort
The property is committed to eliminating single-use plastics—efforts that helped to earn them a 4 Green Keys Rating by Green Key Global.
Accessibility
The hotel adheres to ADA standards, offering mobility-accessible rooms with roll-in showers and accessible entry doors, hearing-accessible rooms with visual alarms and TDD access, and height-adjusted room features—along with features throughout the property, including step-free entrances, wide doorways, and accessible routes.
