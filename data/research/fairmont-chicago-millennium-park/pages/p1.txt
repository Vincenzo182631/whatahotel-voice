## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Chicago?

Check-in is at 4 pm. Check-out is at 11 am.

What is the closest airport to Fairmont Chicago?

The closest airports to our luxury hotel in Chicago are the Chicago O'Hare International Airport (18 miles northwest) and the Chicago Midway International Airport (12 miles southwest).

Does Fairmont Chicago have a spa?

Yes! Leaf Spa, our luxury spa in Chicago awaits with unwinding experiences and a variety of spa treatments. It is located on level B1 of our hotel.

Is parking available at Fairmont Chicago?

Yes! Fairmont Chicago offers parking for guests. Please see our rates below:

- 0-3 hours: $32
- 3 - 6 hours: $55
- 6 - 24 hours: $88
- Overnight: $88
- Toro Chicago & Leaf Spa (up to 4 hours): $26
- Special Events: $45

Sunday – Thursday 6 am – 12 am: No overnight service

Friday - Saturday: 24 hour continuous service

Is Fairmont Chicago pet-friendly?

Yes! Dogs and cats under 40 pounds are permitted but cannot be left unattended and must be on a leash. There is a $150 non-refundable room cleaning fee for short-term stays. Service dogs are always welcome and exempt from charges.

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Chicago?

Fairmont Chicago strives to meet the needs of its guests with disabilities. Please visit our Accessibility page for more information.
