## Frequently Asked Questions

Looking for more information? You may find the answer here.

Do I need a reservation for Solara?

Reservations at Solara are not required or accepted. You’re welcome to call the restaurant directly at (954) 602-6000 to check availability.

What are Solara's hours?

Solara is open daily from 11:00 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight). Whether you’re looking for a poolside cocktail at noon or a late-night drink after dinner, Solara is the spot. For the most up-to-date hours, please call the resort at (954) 602-6000.

Does Solara serve food, or is it just a bar?

Solara serves both cocktails as well as full lunch and bar food menus. Lunch runs from 11:00 AM to 3:30 PM, bar menu from 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM, and specialty cocktails are available from 11:00 AM to midnight.

What kind of cocktails does Solara serve?

Solara’s cocktail program is built around the flavors and spirit of South Florida: tropical, fresh, and coastal. Our specialty drinks menu features artfully crafted cocktails using fresh ingredients, citrus-forward profiles, and Caribbean-inspired spirits. The bar team rotates seasonal specials to reflect what’s fresh and local. Whether you prefer something frozen, sparkling, spirit-forward, or alcohol-free, Solara’s menu is designed to match the mood of a warm South Florida afternoon or evening.

Does Solara have vegetarian or vegan-friendly options?

Yes, several items on Solara’s menu are vegetarian or easily adaptable. If you have specific dietary needs, please ask your server for further accommodations.

Can I sit at Solara without using the resort pool?

Yes, Solara is open to any guest or visitor. You don’t need pool access to sit, eat, or drink here. Resort guests, day visitors, and outside diners are all welcome. Please note that pool access is reserved for resort guests.
