## Frequently Asked Questions

Looking for more information? You may find the answer here.

Do I need a reservation for Diplomat Prime?

Reservations at Diplomat Prime are not required, but are strongly recommended, especially on weekends. Reserve online through OpenTable or by calling the restaurant directly at (954) 602-8331. Walk-ins are welcome based on availability.

Does Diplomat Prime have a dress code?

Yes, we invite you to experience Diplomat Prime in an atmosphere of modern elegance. Guests are to dress in relaxed professional or resort casual attire. Please refrain from wearing any hats, flip-flops, athletic shorts, tank tops, and sportswear.

What are Diplomat Prime's hours?

Diplomat Prime is open for dinner Sunday through Thursday from 5:30 PM to 10:00 PM, and Friday through Saturday from 5:30 PM to 10:30 PM. Hours are subject to change, so please call ahead or book via OpenTable.

Is Diplomat Prime inside the Diplomat Beach Resort? Do I need to be a hotel guest?

Diplomat Prime is located inside the Signia by Hilton Diplomat Beach Resort at 3555 South Ocean Drive, Hollywood, Florida, but the restaurant is open to both hotel guests and outside visitors. You do not need to be staying at the resort to dine. Complimentary valet parking is available for all guests dining at Diplomat Prime.

Does Diplomat Prime have vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free options?

While Diplomat Prime’s menu is focused on prime cuts and fresh seafood, the kitchen can accommodate dietary restrictions and preferences. Guests with vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free needs are encouraged to contact the restaurant in advance at (954) 602-8331 or inform their server upon arrival so the team can suggest or prepare suitable options.

Where is Diplomat Prime located?

Diplomat Prime is located within the Signia by Hilton Diplomat Beach Resort at 3555 South Ocean Drive, Hollywood, Florida, which is conveniently situated between Miami and Fort Lauderdale. We’re approximately 20 minutes from Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport and 35 minutes from Miami International Airport.
