## Where Sun & Flavor Meet

Escape to Playa, where bold Latin flavors, tropical cocktails, and sweeping Atlantic views come together just steps from the sand. Discover beachfront favorites like Caribbean coconut shrimp, fresh ceviche del mar, and a smoky parillada, then toast to the coast with a signature frozen cocktail as sun-soaked afternoons turn into golden hour. Your oceanside table awaits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Looking for more information? You may find the answer here.

What kind of restaurant is Playa Beach Bar & Grill?

Playa Beach Bar & Grill is a Latin-inspired oceanside restaurant at the Signia by Hilton Diplomat Beach Resort in Hollywood, Florida. The restaurant draws on Caribbean and coastal Latin flavors, serving beachfront bites, fresh seafood, and signature tropical cocktails in a relaxed, open-air setting. It’s the resort’s go-to spot for casual lunch and dinner with direct views of the Atlantic Ocean.

Do I need a reservation for Playa Beachfront Bar & Grill?

Reservations at Playa are not required, but you are welcome to reserve online with OpenTable.

What is the restaurant’s dress code?

Playa embraces a relaxed, beach casual atmosphere. Think sundresses, shorts, and resort wear. There is no formal dress code, and the beachfront open-air setting welcomes you straight from the beach or the pool to the terrace.

What are Playa Beach Bar & Grill's hours?

Playa Beach Bar & Grill is open daily from 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM, serving lunch, dinner, and everything in between. Playa is open seven days a week, making it a convenient option for your stay.

Does Playa Beach Bar & Grill have outdoor seating with ocean views?

Yes. Outdoor, oceanfront dining is the Playa experience. The restaurant features an open terrace with direct views of the Atlantic, making it one of the few true beachfront dining options in Hollywood, Florida. It’s an ideal setting for a sunset dinner or a long, leisurely lunch.

Is Playa Beach Bar & Grill a good choice for families with kids?

Absolutely. Playa is one of the most family-friendly dining options at the Diplomat Beach Resort, with a dedicated kids’ menu, a casual beachside atmosphere, and all-day hours that work well for families on vacation. The relaxed, open-air setting means little ones have room to breathe, and the Latin-inspired menu offers approachable flavors alongside more adventurous options for adults.

Is there a happy hour at Playa Beach Bar & Grill?

Yes, Playa Beach Bar & Grill offers a happy hour featuring tropical cocktails and coastal bites. The beachfront bar is a popular gathering spot for resort guests looking to catch the sunset with a drink in hand. For current happy hour times and specials, please contact the restaurant directly at (954) 602-6000 or ask at the resort’s front desk upon arrival.

Is Playa Beach Bar & Grill open to the public, or only for hotel guests?

You don’t need to be staying at the Signia by Hilton Diplomat Beach Resort to enjoy a meal or cocktail at the beachfront terrace. Playa Beach Bar & Grill is open to both hotel guests and outside visitors. Parking is available at the resort, with valet service offered for dining guests.
