## Savor Your Morning

Start your day at Palmea Kitchen, where fresh Florida ingredients, Mediterranean flavors, and elevated American classics set the tone for a memorable breakfast experience. Enjoy favorites like orange blossom-crusted French toast, signature shakshuka, and avocado tartine, alongside a generous breakfast buffet crafted with care. Soak in the sunshine on our terrace or ease into the morning indoors as the day unfolds at your own pace.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Looking for more information? You may find the answer here.

What kind of restaurant is Palmea Kitchen?

Palmea Kitchen is the breakfast and brunch restaurant at the Signia by Hilton Diplomat Beach Resort in Hollywood, Florida. This restaurant blends seasonal Floridian produce with Mediterranean influences and American classics, served in a bright, breezy setting from sunrise to midday. It’s the resort’s signature morning dining destination, whether that’s a quick start to the day or a leisurely weekend brunch.

Do I need a reservation for Playa Beachfront Bar & Grill?

Reservations at Playa are not required, but are recommended during weekends and peak season months (December through April). Reserve online via OpenTable or by calling the restaurant directly at (954) 602-8750. Walk-ins are welcome based on availability.

What are Palmea Kitchen's hours?

Palmea Kitchen is open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM, and on weekends (Saturday and Sunday) from 7:00 AM to 12:00 PM, perfect for a relaxed Saturday or Sunday brunch. Hours are subject to change; guests can confirm by calling (954) 602-8750.

Does Palmea Kitchen serve a breakfast buffet?

Yes, Palmea Kitchen offers The Palm Buffet, a full breakfast buffet priced at $42 per person. The buffet includes freshly brewed coffee, Florida orange juice, made-to-order omelets, applewood smoked bacon, pork and chicken sausage, pancakes or waffles, smoked salmon, charcuterie, assorted cheeses, house-baked breads and pastries, fresh fruit, yogurts, oatmeal, a full cereal selection, and gluten-free options. It’s a favorite hotel breakfast buffet in the Fort Lauderdale and Hollywood area.

Does Palmea Kitchen have vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes, Palmea Kitchen offers vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options which are clearly marked on the menu. The full breakfast buffet also includes plant-based and gluten-free selections.

Is Palmea Kitchen family friendly?

Yes, Palmea Kitchen welcomes outside guests. You do not need to be staying at the Signia by Hilton Diplomat Beach Resort to enjoy breakfast or brunch. The restaurant is located within the resort at 3555 South Ocean Drive, Hollywood, Florida. Valet and self-parking options are available for dining guests.
