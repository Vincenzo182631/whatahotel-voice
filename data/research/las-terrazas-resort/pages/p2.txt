## Questions before you book

The answers guests look for most before confirming their stay.

### How can I change or cancel my reservation?

Reservations at the refundable rate can be cancelled with a full refund up to 31 days before arrival. Within 31 days, the reservation is non-refundable. We strongly recommend purchasing travel insurance to protect your investment. To make changes, contact us directly.

### How do I get to the resort from the airport?

Fly into Belize City (BZE), then take a 20-minute domestic flight to San Pedro on Ambergris Caye. Our van driver meets you at the airstrip and takes you to the marina, where our private boat brings you to the resort in about 10 minutes. We handle all transfer arrangements.

### Is the beach swimmable?

Yes. Las Terrazas has one of the largest stretches of private white sand beach on Ambergris Caye. Sargassum (seaweed) can appear seasonally throughout the Caribbean, but our team maintains the beach daily and the island has invested in removal equipment to minimize impact.

### Do I need a rental car on the island?

No rental cars on Ambergris Caye — the island runs on golf carts. We can arrange a golf cart rental for you through the resort. Most guests find the resort itself has everything they need within walking distance.

### What's in the kitchen?

Every residence includes a full kitchen or kitchenette with essentials for daily cooking, plus easy access to on-site dining if you prefer not to cook.

### Is there daily housekeeping?

Yes. Daily housekeeping is included, with fresh towels, tidying, and restocking to keep your residence comfortable throughout your stay.

### Can I book multiple rooms for a group?

Absolutely. We regularly host families, reunions, and small groups in multiple residences. Contact us and our team will coordinate room combinations and availability.

### What's the check-in/check-out time?

Standard check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. If you need flexibility, ask our team and we will do our best based on availability.

### Is WiFi free?

Yes. Complimentary high-speed WiFi is available across guest residences and main resort areas.
