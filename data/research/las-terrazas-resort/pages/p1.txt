## Quick answers

Here are the questions guests ask most before booking.

### How do I get to Las Terrazas?

Fly into Belize City (BZE), then take a 15-minute flight or 90-minute water taxi to San Pedro. We're a short walk or golf cart ride from the airport.

### Is it family-friendly?

Yes. Our residences have full kitchens, and the calm private beach is perfect for kids. We offer family experiences like snorkeling and beach games.

### Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. We can arrange water taxi or flight transfers from Belize City. Book during checkout or contact us in advance.

### What is your cancellation policy?

(Non-refundable)

This rate is fully prepaid at the time of booking and is non-refundable. Date changes and cancellations are not available once confirmed. Includes our Best Price Guarantee: if you find a lower rate for the same room and dates on any other website before check-in, we will refund the difference.

Flexible Rate (Refundable)

A deposit is collected at booking. Cancel at no cost up to 30 days before your arrival for a full refund to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. After the deadline, your deposit becomes non-refundable. The remaining balance is charged at check-in.

### Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is not automatically included, but you can add it during booking or purchase on-site. Every residence has a full kitchen if you prefer to prepare your own.

### Are pets allowed?

We do not allow pets at this time, with the exception of service animals. Please contact us if you have specific needs.

### What is the best time to visit Belize?

The best weather is usually from late November to April, with sunny days and lower humidity. Green season (May to October) brings warmer weather and occasional rain, but also fewer crowds.

### How far is Las Terrazas from Belize International Airport?

From Belize City International Airport (BZE), you can take a short local flight to San Pedro (about 15 minutes) or a water taxi route that typically takes around 90 minutes including transfer time.

### Is snorkeling good near Las Terrazas?

Yes. The reef is close to Ambergris Caye, with clear water and popular snorkel spots such as Hol Chan Marine Reserve and Shark Ray Alley available via short boat tours.

### How far is the reef from the resort?

The Belize Barrier Reef is just offshore from Ambergris Caye, and many reef snorkeling and diving sites are reachable in a short boat ride.

### Are there restaurants near Las Terrazas?

Yes. You will find a variety of nearby dining options in and around San Pedro, including local Belizean food, seafood, and international cuisine.

### Does the resort have Wi-Fi?

Yes, Wi-Fi is available on property. Speed and stability are generally good for regular browsing, messaging, and email, though they may vary slightly depending on weather and island network conditions.

### Do I need to bring cash or can I use cards?

Most restaurants and tour operators accept major credit cards, but carrying some Belize dollars or US dollars is recommended for small shops, tips, and local vendors.

### Can the resort help book tours and activities?

Yes, our team can assist with booking snorkeling, diving, fishing, mainland excursions, and other experiences based on availability and weather.

### What family amenities are available for young kids?

Our residences have full kitchens, and we can arrange cribs, high chairs, and pack-and-plays on request. The private beach has calm, shallow water that's ideal for younger swimmers, and the main pool has a gradual entry. Let us know your kids' ages when you book and we'll have everything ready.

### How do I get around San Pedro from the resort?

Common transportation options include golf carts, bicycles, and water taxis. Our team can help arrange rentals and local transport recommendations.

### Is airport transfer assistance available?

Yes. We can guide you on the best transfer option from BZE to San Pedro and help coordinate local flight or water taxi logistics.

### Are there diving options for beginners and certified divers?

Yes. Belize offers excellent dive opportunities for both beginners and certified divers, with guided trips to reef sites and training courses available through local operators.
