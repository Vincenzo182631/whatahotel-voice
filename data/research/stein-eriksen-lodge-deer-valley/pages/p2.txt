## Frequently Asked Questions

##### What types of accommodations are available at Stein Eriksen Lodge?

Stein Eriksen Lodge offers luxury guest rooms and suites, designed for comfort, privacy, and alpine elegance. Each accommodation option provides access to the lodge’s full range of resort amenities and services.

##### What is the difference between rooms, suites, and residences at Stein Eriksen Lodge?

Guest rooms provide refined comfort and convenience; suites offer additional space and enhanced amenities. Each option maintains the lodge’s signature level of luxury and service.

##### Are accommodations suitable for families or larger groups?

Yes, Stein Eriksen Lodge offers accommodations well suited for families and larger groups. The resort provides flexible layouts that allow guests to stay together while enjoying privacy and comfort.

##### Do accommodations include mountain views, balconies, or fireplaces?

Many accommodations at Stein Eriksen Lodge feature mountain views, private balconies, and fireplaces. Specific amenities vary by room type and availability, allowing guests to select the experience that best fits their stay.

##### What amenities are included with each stay?

Accommodations at Stein Eriksen Lodge include thoughtfully selected in-room amenities designed for relaxation and convenience. These typically include Molten Brown toiletries, luxury linens, well-appointed bathrooms, and modern comforts expected of a Forbes 5-Star resort.

##### Are accommodations ski-in / ski-out?

Most guests use the ski locker area, making ski-in / ski-out access quick and easy. This also allows guests to move easily between their lodging, the slopes of Deer Valley Resort, as well as spa and dining.
