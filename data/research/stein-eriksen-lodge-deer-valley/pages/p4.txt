# Utah's Finest Five-Star Spa

Rooted in Norwegian tradition and refined with modern luxury, Utah’s Forbes Five-Star spa invites you into a world of relaxation, restoration, and renewal. Each treatment is thoughtfully inspired by Nordic wellness practices, creating a deeply restorative experience for body and mind.

Our experienced therapists will ensure you leave with an overall feeling of elevated health and well-being.

We look forward to welcoming you into a sanctuary of warmth, tranquility, and care, where every detail has been thoughtfully prepared for your comfort.

To book a treatment, please click below or call (435) 645-6475 876-4395).

Advanced reservations are highly recommended.

### Spa Hours

**Spa Hours:**  Winter 9:00 am to 7:00 pm   •   Summer 9:00 am to 6:00 pm

**Spa Services:**  9:30 am to 5:30 pm

**Pool Area:** 7:00 am to 9:00 pm

**Fitness Center:** 5:00 am to 11:00 pm with room key

### Spa Amenities Included with Select Spa Treatments\*

- Men’s and women’s relaxation lounges and locker rooms
- Cardio and weights fitness center
- Heated outdoor pools and hot tubs
- Indoor hot and cold plunge pools
- Steam rooms and dry sauna
- Spa boutique featuring local products and gifts

_\*access is included with spa treatments of $100 or more_
