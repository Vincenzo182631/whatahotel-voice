## Frequently Asked Questions

##### What dining options are available at Stein Eriksen Lodge?

Stein Eriksen Lodge offers three restaurants and a coffee shop. Guests can enjoy casual dining as well as Forbes five-star dining. Restaurants offer breakfast, lunch, après and dinner. Stein Eriksen Lodge also offer 25 hour in-room dining.

##### Does Stein Eriksen Lodge offer après-ski dining?

Yes, Stein Eriksen Lodge is known for its après-ski scene, offering inviting spaces to relax after a day on the slopes. Guests can enjoy cocktails, small plates, and mountain views in a lively yet sophisticated alpine atmosphere at Troll Hallen Lounge.

##### Are there restaurants with mountain or slope-side views?

Several dining venues at Stein Eriksen Lodge feature scenic mountain and slope-side views. These settings enhance the dining experience, whether enjoying breakfast, après-ski, or an evening meal.

##### Are family-friendly dining options available?

Yes, Stein Eriksen Lodge offers family-friendly dining options suitable for guests of all ages. Menus and settings are designed to accommodate families while maintaining the lodge’s elevated culinary standards. Plus, Champions Club offers a relaxed atmosphere with complimentary arcade games for the while family.

##### Is in-room dining available at Stein Eriksen Lodge?

Yes, Stein Eriksen Lodge offers 24-hour in-room dining, allowing guests to enjoy lodge cuisine in the comfort and privacy of their accommodations. Availability and menus may vary by season and time of day.
