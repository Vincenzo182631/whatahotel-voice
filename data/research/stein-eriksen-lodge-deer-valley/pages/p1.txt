### A Premier Year-Round Resort

Nestled amid the alpine serenity of Deer Valley and identified worldwide as a preeminent luxury hotel, Stein Eriksen Lodge is an authentic year-round European lodge offering an elegant blend of contemporary sophistication and nostalgic grace. Inspired by the Olympic legend and his Norwegian heritage, this traditional ski lodge was the first luxury property in Park City. Adorned with oversized leather chairs, crackling fireplaces, and a welcoming ambiance, the Lodge provides guests the ultimate in service, comfort, and location.

Being Utah’s longest-running Forbes Five-Star hotel and spa, guests experience direct to lift mid-mountain access, award-winning dining, indulgent spa treatments, and entertaining après, making Stein Eriksen Lodge a premier year-round resort. Here, you are sure to experience only the finest.

## Frequently Asked Questions

##### Where is Stein Eriksen Lodge located?

Stein Eriksen Lodge is located mid-mountain at Deer Valley Resort in Park City, Utah, offering direct access to world-renowned skiing and alpine experiences. Its elevated setting provides sweeping mountain views while remaining just minutes from Historic Main Street Park City.

##### Does Stein Eriksen Lodge offer hiking and mountain biking access?

Yes, Stein Eriksen Lodge offers true direct access to lift-served hiking and biking at Deer Valley Resort, allowing guests to step directly from the lodge onto the trails. This seamless access makes it one of the most desirable luxury destinations in Park City.

##### Does Stein Eriksen Lodge offer mountain views, fireplaces and hot tubs?

Yes, many of the rooms at Stein Eriksen Lodge offer mountain views. At least 80 percent of rooms offer a fireplace, and all our rooms have large soaking tubs. Many rooms also offer a hot tub on the deck.

##### What activities are available near Stein Eriksen Lodge throughout the year?

Guests at Stein Eriksen Lodge enjoy immediate access to skiing and snowboarding in winter, and hiking, mountain biking, golf, and outdoor recreation in warmer months. The lodge’s location also makes it easy to explore Park City’s dining, shopping, and cultural events year-round.

##### How far is Stein Eriksen Lodge from Historic Main Street Park City?

Stein Eriksen Lodge is approximately five minutes from Historic Main Street Park City. Guests can easily enjoy boutique shopping, art galleries, dining, and nightlife while staying in a peaceful alpine setting.

##### What types of accommodation are available at Stein Eriksen Lodge?

Stein Eriksen Lodge offers a range of luxury accommodations. Each option is thoughtfully designed to provide comfort, privacy, and mountain-inspired elegance.
