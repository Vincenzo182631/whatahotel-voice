- **Las Hojas Spa Bungalow**

### Breathe fresh air and listen to the harmony of hot spring waterfalls as you contemplate the secrets of the surrounding rainforest.

You will enter a heavenly river canyon surrounded by several hot springs and creeks. Each spring and creek presents a different temperature and therapeutic experience to enhance your physical and mental wellbeing.

The water in our artesian, hydrothermal pools emerges naturally from volcanic magma thousands of feet below carrying minerals up from the various layers deep in the earth’s crust.

These primary hot springs were exposed as the once mighty Arenal River cut its way through the valley over thousands of years. As the river cut deeper the forest enveloped these springs and created this magical place that boasts more shades of green than almost any place on earth. The light breeze and the spectacular view of the river complete the ambience. Please come join us at Las Hojas Spa Bungalow for a naturally remarkable Spa Experience.
