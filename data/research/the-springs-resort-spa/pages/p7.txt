# Hot Springs Day Pass

## Relaxation and Adventure Await

We are offering a Special Price for a Two Day Pass to the Hot Springs, Pools and our Five Restaurants and Six Bars. You may arrive anytime after 8:00 AM in the morning and stay until we close at 10:00 PM. Of course, with the Two Day Pass you can go back and forth to the property as much as you like during the two days.

For an additional fee, we offer a guided tour of our Animal Sanctuary for wildlife rescued by the Costa Rican Environmental Ministry. This tour departs from the 5th floor reception. Please consult our reception for tour times. We currently care for an assortment of wildlife including jungle cats, monkeys, sloths, and more!

\*During the peak seasons of Christmas, President’s Day Week, and the week before and after Easter, Day Pass is not available. Prices vary by season and pass type. Click here to see live availability and current prices.

## **Hot Springs Day Pass**

For those who would like to visit just for the day, we offer a two-day hot springs day pass. Guests may arrive any time after 8:00 a.m. and enjoy the facilities until 10:00 p.m. on each day. With the two-day pass, guests may enter and exit the property freely during the valid period.

Prices and pass type vary by season. Click Book Now below to see live availability and current rates.

**IMPORTANT INFORMATION**

WHAT TO BRING FOR YOUR TOUR OR HOT SPRINGS DAY PASS

PLEASE REMEMBER TO BRING VERY COMFORTABLE CLOTHES (SPORT CLOTHES, SYNTHETIC) TENNIS SHOES OR HIKING SHOES; WATER SHOES FOR TOURS IN THE RIVER LIKE TUBING OR KAYAKING, RAIN JACKETS, REPELLENT AND SUNBLOCK; AND A CHANGE OF CLOTHES IN CASE IT RAINS. WE OFFER WATER SHOES FOR RENT.

FOR TUBING PLEASE WEAR A LONG SLEEVE WATER SHIRT TO AVOID CHAFING YOUR ARMS.

BRING LONG PANTS AND SNEAKERS FOR HORSEBACK RIDING TOURS, YOUR SHOES MIGHT GET WET BECAUSE WE MAY BE CROSSING THE ARENAL RIVER ON HORSEBACK.

OUR HOT SPRINGS WATER IS RICH WITH MINERALS AND IT MIGHT STAIN YOUR BATHING SUIT OR THE ROCKS MIGHT SNAG IT, SO WE RECOMMEND WEARING A DARK COLOR NON-EXPENSIVE BATHING SUIT TO ENJOY OUR HOT SPRINGS.

WE HAVE LOCKERS, CHANGING ROOMS AND TOWELS AVAILABLE FOR YOU, THEY ARE FREE OF CHARGE.
