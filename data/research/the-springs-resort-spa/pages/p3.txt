These 2,562 sq. ft., Beautifully Finished Suites feature Spectacular Views, 3 Bedrooms, 3 Baths plus a Large Living Room and Balcony with 8-Person Jacuzzi. Master Bedroom has a King-Size Bed, Second Bedroom Two Queen-Size Beds, Third Bedroom a Queen-Size and a Twin Bed. The Balcony has Sofa Seating, Dining Table, Rocking Chairs, Lounge Chairs. ## DESCRIPTION

This oversized suite offers three bedrooms, a shared spacious living area and an enormous balcony showcasing some of the best panoramic views of the Arenal Volcano and San Carlos River Valley. On the balcony, you will also find a state-of-the-art, huge, 6-Person, Multi-Jet Jacuzzi, a Dining Table for 6, Lounge Chairs and Rocking Chairs. The master bedroom features a king bed with a picturesque view of the volcano. The second and third bedrooms have full baths but DO NOT have a view.
