The Springs Resort & Spa
With over two dozen hot springs, uninterrupted views of the Arenal Volcano, and an on-site adventure center, The Springs is the ultimate Costa Rican getaway.

Reviewed by
Katie Henwood
Best for:
Families, groups, and couples looking for a blend of adventure and relaxation
Location:
La Fortuna, Costa Rica, directly north of the Arenal Volcano
Can’t-miss feature:
There are over two dozen hot springs, pools, and waterfalls spread across the 65 acre property—some tucked away in the jungle, others connected to a swim-up bar. The choice is yours.
In a nutshell
: With views of the Arenal Volcano from every room, an on-property adventure center for all ages, and unmatched service and hospitality, The Springs is your one-stop destination for an unforgettable Costa Rican vacation for the whole family.
Katie Henwood
Katie Henwood
Rooms
The resort offers a range of room types, from honeymoon suites to private villas, and can host over 400 guests when fully booked. Despite being at capacity during our visit (it was peak spring break time), the hotel never felt crowded—the guest rooms are spread across the property and easily accessible to the main building on foot or by golf cart. One of the best features? Our three-bed, three-bath suite came with a private hot tub on the balcony that had clear views of the Arenal Volcano during the day, and a sky full of stars at night; it was the perfect place to soak after a packed day of activities.
Everything at the hotel is built on-site—there are warehouses on the property for woodworking, welding, natural stonework, and more—and that craftsmanship and care is noticed in every guest room and across the property. It’s no wonder that a few episodes of
The Bachelor
and
Keeping Up With the Kardashians
were filmed here.
Katie Henwood
Food and drink
With five restaurants and six bars on property, there is something for everyone in your group, from traditional Costa Rican cuisine to sushi, tacos, and pizza. After checking in, change into your bathing suit and head straight to the main pool which features a swim-up bar that perfectly frames the volcano—nothing will taste as good as that first piña colada after a long travel day.
For a truly special culinary experience, book a table at fine dining establishment Las Ventanas and let food and beverage manager Erick Orozco take you on a journey through Costa Rican culinary highlights paired with some of the rarest bottles of wine from his travels around the world. Ask for The Springs Wine Journey when reserving your table—it will be the best meal of your trip.
Katie Henwood
Katie Henwood
Amenities and experiences
I could spend an entire day pool hopping around the property, each water feature more unique than the last. I recommend starting with the main pool, surrounded by 12 hot springs of varying temperatures, then heading down the hill to discover 7 more hot springs with a water slide that’s likely to have just as many adults in line as kids (don’t be fooled by the seemingly gentle slope of the slide—you’ll gain enough speed to make quite a splash at the bottom before heading back to the top for another go).
Down at Club Rio I found the most secluded hot springs. Follow the footpath that winds above the river to discover even more natural pools with few, if any, other guests, and enjoy a soak with only the sound of the jungle and the river running gently below.
What to do nearby
At The Springs, you don’t have to step foot off the property to have an adventure. The hotel’s activity center, called Club Rio, is located one mile from the main lobby with shuttles running back and forth every half hour. Most excursions center around the Arenal River, which winds through the middle of all the action—think river tubing that's more like bumper cars, and horseback riding across water deep enough to get your boots wet. When it gets dark, go on the guided night walk tour through the jungle to see tree frogs, scorpions, and two-toed sloths that are more active after the sun goes down.
Off-property excursions can also be arranged, such as ATV tours, zip-lining, and guided hikes of the volcano itself. La Fortuna has a town square surrounded by restaurants and shops, though we never felt the need to leave the property during our stay.
Insider tip
Flower lovers can pop into La Casita de las Flores—the resort’s on-site floral studio—at any time to see fresh arrangements being made daily with great artistic care and detail. Surround yourself with the colors and scents of the tropical plants, plus learn about the secrets of the local awapuhi plant from the florists themselves.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
This is the perfect place to vacation with a multigenerational family or group of friends of all ages.
Accessibility:
The property offers several accessibility features for guests with mobility needs—the main public areas are reachable via paved pathways, and complimentary golf cart service is available throughout the property 24/7. Accessible rooms are available upon request.
Wellness:
The Sanctuary Spa offers all traditional services, and the hot springs around the property allow you to soak in the mineral water and connect deeply to nature in the heart of the jungles.
