# The Finest Accommodations in Costa Rica

### Luxurious Guest Rooms, Suites and Villas at The Springs Resort & Spa

Each Guest Room, Suite, and Villa at The Springs Resort & Spa includes a Private Terrace and/or Balcony to enjoy our famous, picturesque view of the Volcano. The rooms are equipped with Hammocks, Rocking Chairs, Minibars and Luxurious Marbled Bathrooms with Jacuzzi Tubs. Relax in bed with the best Latin American Internet Cable Television and Smart TV’s which offer Apple TV and connectivity to Netflix and other streaming services. You can play music over our Smart TV’s or through a surround sound system. Each room has an individual high-speed internet connection as well as connections in most public areas. Our oversized guest rooms vary in size from a minimum of 720 sq. ft. for Vista and Alta Vista categories to 2,562 sq. ft. for our Three Bedroom Premier Suite.
