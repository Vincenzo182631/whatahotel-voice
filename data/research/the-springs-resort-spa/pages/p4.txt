### Dining & Cocktails

The Springs Resort & Spa presents a varied selection of dining experiences sure to please even the most particular palate. Whether you crave a hamburger served poolside at Tree Tops Grill, or a gourmet meal served in our fine dining restaurant, Las Ventanas, we are sure to satisfy your culinary desires. Our award-winning chef, José Rojas, endeavors to embrace the true flavors of the country with organic fruits and vegetables grown specifically for us on neighboring farms, and his menus incorporate the best local offerings of seafood found bountifully on both the Pacific and Caribbean coasts of the country.

Our restaurants offer custom designed versions of traditional Costa Rican and Latin cuisine created with unique methods of tropical fusion. Tres Cascadas restaurant offers a hearty, Latin breakfast buffet as well as lunch and dinner, all served with a spectacular view of the Arenal Volcano. Ginger Sushi is rated one of the best sushi restaurants in the country with its tropically adjusted versions of traditional Japanese favorites. Lunch at Tree Tops Grill and Rancho Club Rio includes burgers, quesadillas, tacos and home-style “Casado” dishes. Our elegant, fine dining experience, Las Ventanas, is perfect for special celebrations with its elaborate dishes and great wine pairings suggested by our sommelier.

Throughout the property you are sure to find yourself a unique cocktail experience! Grab a drink in our ever popular La Laguna swim up bar while enjoying a breathtaking view of the Arenal Volcano. Our rustic, palm thatch jungle bar, Los Perdidos, is also a “must visit” stop while enjoying the hot springs and water slide at Perdido Springs. After your Club Rio adventure activities don’t forget to relax with a drink and enjoy the scenic views of the flowing Arenal River at Rancho Club Rio. The fantastic view and sophisticated elegance of the Heliconia Bar offers the perfect ambience for pre-dinner cocktails.
