The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.

Club Lounge Access
Begin your stay with us in our exclusive Club Lounge with breathtaking city view, where you’ll be greeted by our Club Concierge and enjoy a dedicated check-in services exclusively for our Club guests. Club guests will receive a personalized welcome amenity accompanied by a handwritten card placed in their room prior to arrival.

At Your Service
Dedicated Concierge
Guests staying at The Ritz-Carlton Club Level enjoy extra touches of luxury, including personalized in-room or Club Lounge check-in and check-out, and complimentary drop-off service within 3km between 10am to 7pm. The Ritz-Carlton Club features luxuriously appointed rooms and suites. The focal point of The Ritz-Carlton Club is a spacious lounge area furnished with library, water feature, two meeting rooms, and one elegant board room. The non-smoking Club Lounge is the focus of quiet, refined activity, offering an ambiance appreciated with exceptional views of the Central Business District and garden.

In Good Taste
Daily Culinary Presentations
Five times each day, the Club Lounge offers complimentary food and beverage presentations. These daily offerings — Breakfast, Light Lunch, Afternoon Tea, Hors D’oeuvres and Cocktails and Desserts and Cordials — tell the destination’s story through locally sourced ingredients, iconic Asian and Italian dishes by our chefs and signature drinks.
