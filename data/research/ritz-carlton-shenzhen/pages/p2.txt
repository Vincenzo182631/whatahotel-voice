The Exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Level
Guests staying at The Ritz-Carlton Club Level enjoy extra touches of luxury. These include personalized in-room or Club Lounge check-in and check-out, a dedicated Concierge to provide customized and personalized service, complimentary limousine service from 10 a.m. – 7 p.m. within 3 km in Central Business District area, Club Lounge access with an array of delicacies, and more unparalleled privileges. Only guests registered on the Club level can access the floor through the use of a special elevator key, ensuring a sense of personal security. The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level provides guests with the highest personal service and features five food and beverage presentations daily.

Exceptional Club Lounge Service
The Ritz-Carlton Club features luxuriously appointed rooms and suites. The focal point of The Ritz-Carlton Club is a spacious lounge area, furnished with a fireplace, library, water feature, two meeting rooms, and one elegant board room. The non-smoking Club Lounge is the focus of quiet, refined activity and the ambiance of The Club can be appreciated along with exceptional Central Business District and garden views.
