Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen
New Oasis of Urban Life
Nestled amidst the bustling urban landscape of the city, The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen shines as a beacon of elegance and refinement. Having undergone a renovation, its spacious guestrooms feature iconic city elements inspired by mountains, coastlines, shells and ripples. The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen enlivens the Elegant Oasis through consistent, personalized service, embodying the Greater Bay culture and urban life that influence the new smart city. Here, immerse yourself in the city's vibrant culture while enjoying the hotel's unique blend of luxury and sophistication, offering a new micro-vacation lifestyle experience that is both inspiring and memorable.

Destination & Activities
Things To Do, Places To Discover
In only two decades, Shenzhen has transformed from a fishing village into an international metropolis. As an economic leader in Guangdong, China and with facilities such as the nearby Shenzhen Convention and Exhibition Center, luxury shopping, fine dining and cultural attractions; the city welcomes global visitors. Centrally located in the Futian District, The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen is a perfect base for exploring this extraordinary destination.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen. Self parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen is Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport (SZX). SZX is located approximately 27.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen have an airport shuttle to Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport (SZX)?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen has an airport shuttle to Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport (SZX).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen have an airport shuttle to Hong Kong International Airport (HKG)?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen has an airport shuttle to Hong Kong International Airport (HKG).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Shenzhen offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
