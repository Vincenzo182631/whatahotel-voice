### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W New York - Times Square have a work space?

No, the rooms at W New York - Times Square do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W New York - Times Square?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at W New York - Times Square. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W New York - Times Square?

Some rooms at W New York - Times Square permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W New York - Times Square have fridges?

No, rooms at W New York - Times Square do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W New York - Times Square have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at W New York - Times Square do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W New York - Times Square have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W New York - Times Square have hair dryers.
