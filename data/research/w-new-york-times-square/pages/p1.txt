### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W New York - Times Square?

The check-in time at W New York - Times Square is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W New York - Times Square allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W New York - Times Square. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at W New York - Times Square?

Off-site valet parking is available at W New York - Times Square. The daily valet fee is $100.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W New York - Times Square have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W New York - Times Square has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W New York - Times Square booking.

What is the closest airport near W New York - Times Square?

The closest airport near W New York - Times Square is LaGuardia Airport (LGA). LGA is located approximately 9.7 Miles from the hotel.

Does W New York - Times Square have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W New York - Times Square does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
