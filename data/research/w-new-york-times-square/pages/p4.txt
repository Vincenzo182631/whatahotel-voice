Local Area & Activity Highlights

### Broadway Theater

Our hotel is located near Broadway, just steps from some of the most famous theaters in the world, catching a Broadway show is an essential thing to do during your getaway to W New York - Times Square.

Shows and Tickets

### Shopping

Explore all of the shopping neighborhoods, home to renowned retailers and high fashion as well as the cutting–edge local NYC boutiques. Just ask our W Insider where to go and they are happy to help.

### Music Venues

Mix. Play. Remix. From world-class concerts to underground shows, New York City has it all. Hit up all of the great music venues from our hotel near Times Square, New York.

More Local Area & Activities

### Carnegie Hall

Amplify your Big Apple experience with a visit to Carnegie Hall, the legendary New York City performing arts venue. W New York - Times Square is situated near this one-of-a-kind venue, just minutes away, connecting hotel guests to the greatest musical talent in the world. Showcasing performers of all types and talents, Carnegie Hall brings the power of music to three main stages.

Learn More

### Madison Square Garden

Stay fabulous at our hotel near Madison Square Garden. Amplify your visit to New York City by experiencing an exciting sports game, concert, or other major event at The World’s Most Famous Arena®. Our Times Square hotel is mere minutes from the action.

Learn More

### Radio City Music Hall

Originally constructed between 1929 and 1940, Radio City Music Hall is an iconic NYC attraction. Home of the world-famous Rockettes, plus an array of special events and concerts throughout the year, Radio City is beloved by both tourists and locals.

Learn More

### Museum of Modern Art

With the world’s finest collection of modern and contemporary art, MoMa is an essential thing to do for art buffs visiting NYC. Explore the world-class collection, which is located just minutes away from our hotel.

Learn More

### Observation Decks

Take in New York City from a whole new perspective at one of the many observation decks within walking distance from our doors. Head a few blocks south to take in the classic city view from the top of the Empire State Building, look 100 stories down from the glass floor at The Edge, or get lost in one of the multi-sensory immersive experiences being offered at SUMMIT One Vanderbilt.

### Lincoln Center for Performing Arts

Lincoln Center for the Performing Arts is the world's leading performing arts complex, renowned for its diverse and creative events. Our hotel is located near the Lincoln Center, the home of the New York City Ballet and the New York City Opera.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W New York - Times Square have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W New York - Times Square offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Punching Bags, Medicine Balls, Yoga Mats, Peloton Bikes, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W New York - Times Square?

Museum of Illusions, Metropolitan Museum of Art, Empire State Building, Madison Square Garden, Javits Center and more are nearby W New York - Times Square.
