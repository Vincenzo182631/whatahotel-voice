With gleaming elegance, the 108 guests-rooms located on the six floors of the building have been redesigned for maximum comfort and highlight the hotel’s connection with its urban landscape. Featuring a distinguished international identity, they offer guests an exclusive, personalized and engaging experience. The room design also emphasizes typical Milanese architectural details, whilst the neutral color palette creates a unique connection taking inspiration from the Duomo Cathedral, the city’s main landmark.
Renowned as the unique gems of Park Hyatt Milano, its Terrace Suites, Montenapoleone Suite and Duomo Suite stand out for their exclusive and evocative panoramic terraces overlooking the city skyline. The Brera Suite, located on the second floor of the hotel, boast unparallel living quarters and ample areas that overlook the glass dome that adorns La Cupola Restaurant.
