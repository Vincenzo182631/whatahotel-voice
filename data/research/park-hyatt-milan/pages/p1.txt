# Luxury 5-Star Hotel in Milan Near Duomo \| Park Hyatt Milan

## Luxury Five-Star Hotel in Milan Near Duomo

At Park Hyatt Milano, luxury unfolds through intimacy with Milan’s soul. Tucked discreetly beside the grand Piazza del Duomo, our 5-star haven offers a deeply immersive connection to the city's refined rhythm. Begin your morning with a cappuccino where locals linger in sunlit piazzas, feel the quiet elegance of Via della Spiga as you browse ateliers steeped in Italian craftsmanship, or let the notes of a Verdi aria guide you through a night at Teatro alla Scala —as if Milan whispers its secrets only to those who stay with us.

### Pellico 3 Milano

The gastronomic restaurant Pellico 3 Milano is the ideal location to enjoy a culinary experience in a bright and welcoming setting. Cuisine and seasonality go hand in hand, with meticulous attention in terms of research and selection of ingredients, strictly linked to the roots of the Mediterranean cuisine and our Italian tradition of craftsmanship.

### Pets Are Welcome

We’re delighted to welcome your pets during your stay at Park Hyatt Milan. To welcome you and your pet to the fullest, please contact us before you arrive. Dogs are accepted only in the rooms and only in categories from DELUXE upward, but no pets are allowed in the food and beverage outlets (Pellico 3 Milano, La Cupola and Mio Lab) or in the spa.
