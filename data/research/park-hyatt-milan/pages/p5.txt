### A few days stay in Milan

We had a wonderful stay at Park Hyatt Milan!The staff were fantastic and made the experience even better. The hotel is in an incredible location, right in the heart of Milan, yet it feels tucked away and understated. You might not even realize it’s there if you weren’t looking for it, which adds to its charm. The bed was extremely comfortable, and the bathrooms were impressively spacious and well appointed. Everything about our stay felt comfortable and luxurious without being over the top. We would absolutely stay here again and highly recommend it to anyone visiting Milan!

### Hyatt Milan a bit disappointing.

The hotel is beautiful and well appointed. We were put in a handicapped room (not by request) and the set up was less than optimal for us. Bathroom too large and bedroom too small. Other handicap amenities really didn't work well for us. Some of the staff was outstanding, we found others a bit arrogant and dismissive. I was ignored for over 15 minutes when asking for a check in the restaurant (that wasn't particularly busy at the time). I got their attention when I got up to walk out.

### Steps from the Galleria, built for a quiet stay

At the Park Hyatt Milan, we found a polished, highly convenient luxury base for exploring the city. The check-in was seamless, our room was ready at noon, and our loyalty was acknowledged. The room was clean and thoughtfully decorated, with a separate tub and shower, bath salts, and quiet double-glazed windows (despite us being assigned to a first-floor location). The real advantage is location: Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II shopping arcade is steps from the entrance, while the Duomo and Starbucks Reserve Roastery are all an easy walk away.
