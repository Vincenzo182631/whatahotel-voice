# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Park Hyatt Milan is pleased to offer indoor parking at an off-site parking garage for guests and their visitors. Our 24-hour parking garage offers valet service: Guests may leave the car in front of the hotel. The valet service can take at least 20 minutes for car collection at the hotel. Additionally, we offer car wash services on request, priced starting from €95.

### Congestion Charge

Milan City Authorities introduced a daily congestion charge of €7.50 per vehicle traveling within the city center Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m.–7:30 p.m. No access is permitted to Euro 3 and 4 class diesel vehicles. For your convenience, the daily fee of €7.50 will be charged to your hotel bill if you’re using our recommended parking garage. If you are parking in a different location, please provide the concierge with your license plate, and we will add the daily congestion fee of €7.50 per vehicle to your hotel bill.

Important Notice: Limited Traffic Zone in the Fashion District

Please be advised that, effective May 12, 2025, the City of Milan has introduced a Limited Traffic Zone (ZTL) within the renowned Quadrilatero della Moda area.

This regulation will be enforced 24 hours a day, and applies to the perimeter enclosed by Via Manzoni, Via Senato, Via San Damiano, Corso Monforte, Via Cino del Duca and the pedestrian area of Corso Vittorio Emanuele.

We kindly encourage you to consult the City of Milan’s official website for further details. For any assistance or guidance you may require during your stay, please do not hesitate to contact our Concierge Team, who will be delighted to assist you with navigating these new regulations.
