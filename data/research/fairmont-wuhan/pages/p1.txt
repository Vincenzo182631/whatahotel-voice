## A Landmark of Rare _refinement_ in the heart of Wuhan City

Fairmont Wuhan is a luxury hotel located in the city's brand-new central business district. Fairmont Wuhan is the landmark of rare refinement and the ideal place to explore the city and conducting business. The hotel offers 318 lavish guest rooms and suites, a spa, 3 distinctive restaurants and one lounge, complete with a total of 2,008 sqm of extraordinary meeting & conference space.
