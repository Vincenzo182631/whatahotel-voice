## Our Story

Set on a tree-lined avenue in central Phnom Penh, Hotel Le Royal has always been the city’s most prestigious hotel. Built in 1929, it reopened as Raffles Hotel Le Royal in 1997, following sensitive extension and renovation. Today we avowedly honour our historic past while embracing the city’s dynamic future.

## Cambodian flavours gifted by royal decree

Many of the succulent dishes offered in Restaurant Le Royal are crafted from recipes entrusted to us by Cambodia’s Royal Palace. These take their place alongside modern Khmer cuisine, creating a gastronomic fusion that is timeless yet completely of its time.

### What dining options are available at Raffles Hotel Le Royal?

### How can I book a room at Raffles Hotel Le Royal?

### What makes Raffles Hotel Le Royal unique?

The hotel combines rich history with modern luxury, offering personalized service and a stunning ambiance.

### When is the best time to visit Phnom Penh?

The ideal time to visit is from November to February when the weather is cooler and more pleasant for exploring the city.

Choosing Raffles means indulging in luxury, experiencing exceptional service, and immersing yourself in the vibrant culture of Phnom Penh.
