## Our philosophy

At Raffles Hotel Le Royal we create an environment that nurtures wellbeing holistically, through harmonious design, serene rituals, nutrition for pleasure and the world's best wellness experiences. Everything that touches the senses is consciously crafted to take care of your physical and emotional wellbeing. Expect a sumptuous sojourn, with spa journeys tailor-made to suit your needs, state-of-the-art fitness facilities and a serene outdoor lap pool. We partner with expert local practitioners and world-class wellness and beauty brands, and our products are sourced with environmental sustainability in mind, reflecting our social responsibilities.

### Signature Khmer Massage

Inspired by Asian therapies, the traditional Khmer therapeutic massage, also known as chad ta shai, is a vigorous, firm pressure point massage.

### Le Royal Spa Experience

Treatment begins with a mineral salt scrub to exfoliate and nourish dry skin followed by an authentic Khmer massage to help restore energy levels.

-

### Anti-Ageing Facial

A powerful customised multi-vitamin treatment to firm, smooth and revive using products designed to fight premature ageing.
