### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp is the Signature Tented Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp?

Yes, all rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp have fridges?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp have hair dryers.

Can I request connecting rooms at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp?

No, connecting rooms are not available at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp.
