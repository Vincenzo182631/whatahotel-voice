# Dining at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp

## Enjoy Food and Drinks Offered at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp

At This Hotel

## Mavuno – all day dining

International

Savor menus inspired by nature’s rhythms, crafted with locally sourced ingredients, heritage recipes, and mindful preparation. Every dish is designed to nourish the body, awaken the senses, and celebrate a true sense of place.

## Ember Bar and Lounge

International

Unwind in a space where nature’s calm meets timeless elegance. The Bar Lounge invites you to reconnect over signature cocktails and curated wines, inspired by local botanicals and landscape. Gather by firelight or under the African sky - and feel present.

## JW garden

Vegetarian

Experience the JW Garden, a living expression of freshness and sustainability. Herbs, vegetables and edible flowers are harvested daily to inspire seasonal menus and infusions, creating vibrant flavors in a sanctuary where guests reconnect with nature.
