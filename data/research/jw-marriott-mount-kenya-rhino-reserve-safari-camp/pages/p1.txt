# Welcome to JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp

## Immersed in Nature's Quiet Majesty

Cradled seamlessly within the renowned Solio Conservancy, between the slopes of Mount Kenya and the peaks of the Aberdare Ranges, this Safari Camp provides guests with a deep connection to nature, where thoughtfully curated experiences and spaces provide a haven for private reflection, the perfect retreat for mind, body and soul.This exclusive sanctuary is internationally recognised for its successful...See More rhino breeding programme, offering sightings of both white and endangered black rhinos, alongside other indigenous wildlife including leopards, cheetahs, plains game and bird life. Guests can enjoy luxurious tented suites with private plunge pools, tranquil wellness spaces including the signature Spa by JW, and curated dining venues. An exclusive array of unforgettable adventures from night game drives to visits to a private rhino orphanage, this graceful sanctuary invites you to reconnect with yourself, the wilderness, and the enduring conservation legacy of this remarkable land.See Less

Concierge Service

Before arrival, enjoy a personal call with our General or Camp Manager to understand what matters most to you. From preferences to special moments, we tailor every detail - shaping a safari experience that feels deeply personal and entirely your own.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of being a Marriott Bonvoy Member at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp?

Marriott Bonvoy Members staying at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp are eligible for additional benefits only available at resorts in the All-Inclusive by Marriott Bonvoy Portfolio. You can refer to official benefits here.

What is included in the per night room rate at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp?

The nightly room rate at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp typically includes: room, meals, snacks, non-alcoholic & alcoholic beverages, entertainment, non-motorized water sports, kids’ activities, taxes, service fees, and gratuities.

What additional amenities and services are available for purchase at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp?

JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp may offer spa treatments, motorized water sports, laundry, transportation, tours, premium items, and services for an additional fee.

Are there any guest age restrictions at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp?

JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp is a Family-Friendly resort and is open to guests of all ages. The guest count of adults and children should be updated during the booking process to ensure appropriate pricing.

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp is 12:00 pm and the check-out time is 10:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp is Nanyuki Airport (NYK). NYK is located approximately 34.5 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp have an airport shuttle to Nanyuki Airport (NYK)?

Yes, JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp has an airport shuttle to Nanyuki Airport (NYK).

Does JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Mount Kenya Rhino Reserve Safari Camp does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
