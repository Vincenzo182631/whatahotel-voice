# Gran Meliá Palacio de Isora

Avenida Los Océanos, s/n, Alcalá, Tenerife - Guia de Isora\| Show map

4.4 (5869 reviews)

## A climate of eternal spring at the foot of Teide

On the shores of the Atlantic Ocean and at the foot of Teide, a magical experience awaits you amidst the natural magnetism of Tenerife. A five-star resort that captures the island's natural beauty and boasts the longest infinity pool in all of Europe.

Perfectly integrated into an exceptional natural setting on the beachfront, Gran Meliá Palacio de Isora is one of the most exclusive resorts in Tenerife. An oasis of tranquility thanks to its saltwater infinity pool, its 2000 m2 spa, and suites with the most exclusive RedLevel distinction. It also stands out for its gastronomic concept at the Dos Naifes by Jorge Peñate restaurant, awarded a Sol Repsol 2025. Chefs Jorge Peñate and Luis Martín have created a unique proposal in the Canary Islands that champions marine biodiversity and local seafood.
