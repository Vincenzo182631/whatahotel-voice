## Marine wildlife & ocean encounters

Set sail along Tenerife’s dramatic coastline aboard a private Bavaria 46 Cruiser, where volcanic cliffs, crystal-clear waters and marine wildlife create an unforgettable Atlantic escape. Guided by an expert captain and cetacean specialist, this exclusive experience combines sailing, whale watching and moments of complete relaxation in one of the world’s most privileged ocean settings.

The experience begins aboard a private Bavaria 46 cruiser, sailing along the Atlantic coast of Tenerife in the company of an experienced captain and a guide specialising in marine wildlife. During the trip, guests will have the chance to spot dolphins and pilot whales in their natural habitat, whilst always adhering to respectful conservation practices. The tour also includes time to anchor in a secluded cove, where you can enjoy paddleboarding or snorkelling at a leisurely pace. Weather permitting, part of the route can be covered under sail, creating a more peaceful and authentic connection with the ocean. On board, a selection of Iberian cured meats, Canarian cheeses, fresh fruit, cava and wine is served throughout the experience.

- **Duration**: 4 or 6 hours.
- **Number of people**: Minimum 2 guests. Up to 8 guests.
- **Please note**: Subject to weather condition.
- **What’s included?**: Private Bavaria 46 Cruiser experience with captain and expert cetacean guide, marine wildlife observation, stop in a secluded cove for swimming, paddleboarding and snorkelling, curated selection of beverages and light gastronomy served on board, plus private cabin and bathroom with shower.

,regionOfInterest=(3000,2000))

There is something deeply special about experiencing Tenerife from the sea. Away from everyday distractions, the Atlantic creates a rare sense of calm and perspective. Time slows naturally as the coastline unfolds beside the yacht, volcanic cliffs rise dramatically from the water and the sound of the ocean replaces everything else. This experience is designed not only to showcase the beauty of Tenerife, but also to create space for genuine connection with nature and the present moment itself. Whether enjoying wine on deck, swimming in a hidden cove or watching dolphins appear beside the boat, every part of the journey feels intimate, effortless and deeply connected to the surroundings.

The vastness of the Atlantic and the untouched beauty of the coastline create a powerful feeling of freedom and emotional disconnection from routine, allowing guests to fully switch off and experience the island from a completely different perspective. Ideal for couples, families or small groups seeking privacy and authenticity, Sailing Across the Atlantic transforms a day at sea into one of those experiences that remains long after returning to shore.
