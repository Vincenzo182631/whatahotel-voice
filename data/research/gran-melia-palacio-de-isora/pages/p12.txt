## Through lava fields, vineyards and coastal landscapes

Beyond Tenerife’s coastline lies a landscape shaped by lava, wind and centuries of history. This private experience through the Malpaís de Rasca reveals one of the island’s lesser-known natural settings, where volcanic terrain, Atlantic views and local traditions come together in a journey designed for guests seeking a more authentic and grounded connection with Tenerife.

* * *

Guided by local experts, guests explore the striking landscapes of the Malpaís de Rasca, walking through lava fields, volcanic cones and rugged coastal paths overlooking the Atlantic. Along the route, the experience uncovers traces of Tenerife’s past, from ancient Guanche remains to traditional agricultural landscapes that continue to define the region today. The journey also includes lunch at a local restaurant before continuing to Vilaflor, home to one of the island’s highest-altitude wineries. There, guests enjoy a tasting of volcanic wines paired with Canarian specialities, offering a deeper connection to the flavours, traditions and natural character that define Tenerife.

- **Duration**: 7–8 hours including lunch (+ approximately 40 minutes return journey from the hotel). Shorter versions are also available.
- **Number of people**: 2-5 guests.
- **Please note**: The experience may vary depending on weather conditions.
- **What’s included?**: Private guided experience through the Malpaís de Rasca in English or Spanish, exploration of volcanic landscapes and historical areas, lunch at a local restaurant, visit to a winery in Vilaflor and wine tasting accompanied by Canarian specialities.

,regionOfInterest=(800,532.5))

This experience reveals a quieter, more authentic side of Tenerife, far removed from the crowded viewpoints and the usual tourist routes. Walking through the volcanic landscapes of the Malpaís de Rasca offers a direct connection with the island’s natural character, where fields of black lava, coastal cliffs and sweeping views of the Atlantic create a setting that feels wild and spectacular. The silence of the landscape, the texture of the volcanic terrain and the vastness of the coastline create a unique perspective that naturally draws one’s attention away from everyday distractions.

The local lunch and volcanic wine tasting add another dimension to the journey, offering a more personal connection with Tenerife through its flavours and traditions. Seated amongst vineyards and mountainous landscapes, tasting wines shaped by the volcanic soil and dishes rooted in local culture, guests can experience the island in a way that is both immersive and deeply connected to the destination itself. Combining nature, gastronomy and local heritage, ‘Beyond the Beaten Track’ is designed for guests seeking a more meaningful way to experience Tenerife through its landscapes, flavours and cultural identity.
