From the sea to your table

Located in the heart of Gran Meliá Palacio de Isora, Dos Naifes - Corte Atlántico focuses its innovative proposal on Atlantic cuisine, a concept with the ocean as its main source of inspiration, respecting marine biodiversity and paying tribute to the local fishing culture. A journey where freshness, traceability and sustainability are the protagonists, offering the diner a gastronomic immersion that is enjoyed even more while watching the sunset over the Atlantic. The restaurant, within the exclusive RedLevel space, has been awarded a 2025 Repsol Sol.

### Our chefs

Together with Luis Martín, Jorge Peñate leads the Dos Naifes - Corte Atlántico dining project at Gran Meliá Palacio de Isora. After more than eight years of work together, the two have established themselves as experts in Atlantic cuisine in the Canary Islands with their first Repsol Sol for Dos Naifes - Corte Atlántico. The award recognizes their career as one of the most unique in Spain.

### Atlantic-style cuisine

Dos Naifes - Corte Atlántico celebrates the Atlantic Ocean with seafood prepared with contemporary techniques that raise flavors to new levels. The chefs work with local fishermen from protected areas such as La Graciosa and trusted farmers. Cuisine that is thrilling visitors with its taste, but also with its commitment to sustainability and Canary Islands identity.
