## Celestial evenings above the volcanic landscape

As night falls over Mount Teide, Tenerife reveals one of its most extraordinary natural spectacles. Beneath exceptionally clear skies, this private astronomical experience invites guests to discover constellations, planets and distant galaxies in complete tranquillity. Surrounded by volcanic landscapes and silence, the evening offers a rare opportunity to experience the island from an entirely different perspective after sunset.

The experience begins at dusk, when the changing light transforms the volcanic landscape surrounding Mount Teide into shades of gold, pink and deep purple. Visitors explore the night sky through professional long-range telescopes, which reveal constellations, planets and distant celestial formations with extraordinary clarity. The evening also includes access to a planetarium experience, offering a deeper insight into astronomy, the universe and the importance of Mount Teide as one of the world’s leading sites for astronomical observation.

- **Duration**: 4-6 hours.
- **Number of people**: 2-6 guests.
- **Please note**: The experience is subject to weather conditions.
- **What’s included?**: Private astronomical experience at Mount Teide with accredited Starlight guide, exclusive planetarium access and professional stargazing using long-range telescopes beneath one of the clearest skies in the world.

,regionOfInterest=(2016,1512))

There is a unique sense of stillness that can only be experienced beneath a sky free from artificial light. High up on the coast, surrounded by volcanic landscapes and silence, Mount Teide becomes a place where time seems to stand still and the immensity of the universe is suddenly perceived with astonishing closeness. This experience creates a space to completely disconnect from the daily routine and reconnect with a more tranquil and reflective side of the journey. The darkness of the national park, the fresh mountain air and the clarity of the night sky create an atmosphere that naturally draws attention away from everyday distractions and directs it towards the vastness of the landscape stretching out above.

As the planets, constellations and distant galaxies come into view through the telescopes, the experience offers a different perspective. The silence of the landscape and the vastness of the sky create a unique, immersive atmosphere, far removed from everyday life. An elegant, intimate and unforgettable experience, The Sky’s The Limit reveals a side of Tenerife rarely experienced during the day. Guests leave not only with memories of extraordinary skies, but with a renewed sense of calm, wonder and connection with the natural world.
