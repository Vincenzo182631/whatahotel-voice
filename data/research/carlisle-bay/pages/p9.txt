# OTTIMO!

A feast of authentic Italian dining in a less formal setting, poolside under the shade. Ottimo! is often the first choice for lunch or a casual dinner with the family. The wood-fired oven produces the most sublime pizzas while the menu also includes antipasti, crispy salads and gelato to cool off. Ottimo! looks after the younger guests too with its Little Ottimo! menu.

This is a holiday dining experience that appeals to everyone. You will have the chance to spend time together and enjoy really good food simply prepared. This is what Ottimo! delivers.
