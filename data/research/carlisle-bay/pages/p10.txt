# The Jetty Grill

Arrive at the Jetty Grill and take in the beautiful views overlooking Carlisle Bay's jetty. Listen to the Caribbean Sea lapping up on to the horseshoe bay, The Jetty Grill offers the best of authentic Caribbean dishes. Join us on Fire Pit Fridays to listen to local singers & songwriters from 7.30-9pm. Kindly note The Jetty Grill is for Adults Only (13 years or older).
