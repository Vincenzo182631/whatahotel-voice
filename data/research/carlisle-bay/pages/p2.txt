# Suites

The 87 beautifully appointed suites at Carlisle Bay offer a relaxed sense of Caribbean luxury, with generous living spaces and sweeping views of the ocean or tropical gardens through floor-to-ceiling windows.

Thoughtful design, bespoke furniture, hand-carved details and locally sourced artwork bring Antigua’s heritage and natural beauty indoors, creating a calm and comfortable retreat for couples and families alike.
