# Indigo on the Beach

Indigo on the Beach allows you to dine or enjoy a refreshing drink without ever leaving those stunning ocean views. The open-sided room and terrace with wooden floors merge with the sand and sea. Stroll in whenever you feel like a snack or a much-needed chilled drink, bare feet are fine with us. Come evening, dinner is served as the sun sets over the ocean. Indigo on the Beach offers the flexibility and variety that is just what your vacation calls for.
