# East

Entering through tall, carved Indonesian doors the dark wood and red interior, East provides a memorable atmosphere and gastronomic experience. Japanese, Thai and Indonesian dishes all featuring on the menu, all expertly created by our international culinary team. Choices include freshly-caught seafood, duck, beef, chicken, lamb and vegetarian options, all prepared with the tantalising flavours of the East.

This is a sophisticated and stylish dining experience and perfect for celebrating a special time together. East is also an ideal venue for a wedding breakfast or a family occasion.
