# CARA Spa - Resort in Antigua

Restore inner harmony and balance by allowing CARA Spa at Carlisle Bay to take you on a journey, tuning into the elements, to reconnect with body, mind and soul.

CARA Spa encompasses a more natural and therapeutic way of living; in addition to our luxurious facial treatments and deeply restorative massage therapies, we have a variety of holistic oﬀerings, regular yoga classes and intensive health retreats available.

CARA Spa is proud to partner with GROUND Wellbeing.

We're delighted to announce a new partnership with GROUND Wellbeing, an acclaimed Irish wellness brand. At the heart of Carlisle Bay’s wellness philosophy lies an understanding that true wellbeing extends beyond the physical—it’s a state of harmony between body, mind, and spirit. In partnership with GROUND Wellbeing, CARA Spa now invites guests to embark on an intuitive journey of self-care through a series of rituals designed to support sleep, stress relief, hormonal balance, and skin health.
