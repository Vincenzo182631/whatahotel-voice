Dining at Carlisle Bay is an intricate and important part of this luxury resort experience. Choosing from an abundance of fresh local produce the culinary team create menus to entice and delight. Preparation and cooking of food is subtle yet sophisticated. The dishes that are produced are bursting with flavour and taste without being fussy.

Choices are numerous and varied from formal, casual, intimate to family dining – you choose, and we will deliver in each unique setting. Daytime menus are perfectly suited to a Caribbean climate with delicious salads, juicy tropical fruits and chilled gelato among the tempting offerings. Evenings bring a varied choice with relaxed Italian food, Asian fine dining and the most succulent grilled meats and local seafood to satisfy every longing. We also offer options of authentic Antiguan cuisine, allowing you to immerse yourself through food in this wonderful, rich culture.

**Lounges and bars at Carlisle Bay**

If you simply wish to enjoy a fruit punch, a cocktail or an ice-cold beer you can choose from our selection of bars and lounges or order to wherever you are. Sit back and people-watch from the comfort of the Pavilion Bar or opt for privacy and intimacy in the more sophisticated setting of Coconut Grove. Wherever you are, the sounds, sights and scents of the island and the ocean will always be there.
