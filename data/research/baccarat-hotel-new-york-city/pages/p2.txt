### Do you have connecting rooms?

Yes, we do. Please see our Rooms page here.

### Is there housekeeping service daily?

Yes, there is.

### Do you have rooms with views?

Yes, we do.

### Do you have rooms with a balcony or terrace?

No, we do not.

### Do you have rooms with two beds?

Yes, we do. Please see our Rooms page here.

### Do you have rooms that have a bathtub?

Yes, we do have a limited number of rooms with bathtubs.

### Is the bathroom private from the rest of the hotel room?

Yes, it is.

### Do you offer allergy-free rooms?

No, we do not.

### Does the hotel have housekeeping service?

Yes, it does.

### Can I cast from my device to the TV in rooms?

Yes, you may.

### Are there minibars in the rooms?

Yes, there are.

### Are there ice machines, or can I get ice delivered to my room?

Ice can be delivered through in-room dining.

### Do the rooms have safes? Are they large enough to accommodate a laptop?

For your convenience, each guest room is equipped with an electronic safe suitable for storing valuables, including most laptop computers. To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Baccarat Hotel New York shall not be liable for any loss of or damage to any guest property. Guests are responsible for safeguarding their personal belongings. If you require assistance with the in-room safe, please do not hesitate to contact our team of hosts by dialing "0" from your guest room telephone.

### Is wifi available? How much is wifi?

The hotel offers complimentary wi-fi for all guests.

### How can I request a rollaway bed or crib?

Rollaway beds may be requested by contacting nyhosts@baccarathotels.com.
