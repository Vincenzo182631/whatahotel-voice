# Wellness

Wellness at Baccarat takes many forms. Start the day in the fitness studio, spend the afternoon at La Piscine, or book a treatment at Spa de La Mer. However you choose to recharge, each experience invites you to slow down, reconnect, and embrace the art of living well.

## Spa de La Mer

Spa de La Mer is the first dedicated La Mer spa in the United States, offering bespoke treatments designed to restore radiance and bring the senses back into balance. Each experience is tailored to support deep relaxation and visible renewal.

## Strength & Focus

A bright, light-filled fitness studio offers a quiet retreat above the energy of Manhattan. Featuring Technogym and Woodway equipment alongside dedicated spaces for yoga and stretching, the experience is designed to support your wellness routine at every pace.

Explore

## La Piscine

Beneath a dramatic barrel-vaulted ceiling, La Piscine offers a luminous retreat in the heart of the city. Heated waters, Riviera-inspired cabanas, and white daybeds invite moments of relaxation, while the iconic checkerboard floor brings a touch of playful elegance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

View All FAQ

### Do you have a fitness center? What equipment does it offer? What are its hours?

Yes, our fitness room offers top-tier equipment by Olympic supplier Technogym and professional sports supplier Woodway. Also containing a separate freestyle room for yoga and Pilates, the space is continually replenished with amenities and is open 24 hours.

### Do you offer personal training or group fitness classes?

We offer personal Training. Reservations are required and can be requested by emailing spadelamer@baccarathotels.com

### Do you have a pool? Is it heated? What are its hours?

The hotel offers a heated indoor swimming pool, La Piscine, which is open from 7am to 9pm and located on -2 floor.

### Does the hotel pool have any other guidelines?

**La Piscine Guidelines**

To ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all guests, we kindly ask that the following guidelines be observed:

- Guests use the pool and surrounding facilities at their own risk.
- To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Baccarat Hotel New York shall not be liable for any accident, injury, or loss of or damage to personal property, arising from use of the pool and surrounding facilities.
- Guests under 14 years of age must be accompanied and actively supervised by an adult.
- No running, diving, jumping, rough play, or other unsafe behavior is permitted.
- Floors may be wet and slippery. Please use caution at all times.
- Appropriate swim attire is required. Street clothing, excessively revealing attire, or garments not intended for swimming are not permitted.
- Headsets or headphones are required when listening to personal audio. Outside speakers are not permitted.
- To protect the privacy of our guests, flash photography and the filming or photographing of other guests or hotel associates is prohibited without prior consent.
- Glass containers and outside food or beverages are not permitted in the pool area.
- Guests should refrain from using La Piscine while under the influence of alcohol or any substance that may impair judgment or physical ability.
- In the event of an emergency, please notify the lifeguard immediately.

Thank you for your cooperation in helping us maintain a safe and tranquil environment for all guests.

### Do you have a spa? What are its hours?

Yes, the hotel offers Spa de Le Mer, open Monday through Thursday, 9am to 8pm, and Friday through Sunday, from 9am to 9pm. Reservations in advance are highly encouraged.

### Can I visit the pool if I’m not a hotel guest?

Our pool is for hotel guests only.

### Do you offer laundry or dry cleaning services?

Yes, we offer laundry or dry cleaning services for our hotel guests.
