# Welcome to The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes

## Reserve your stay at our 5-star hotel in Cairo

The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes is a haven of wellness and tranquility set within a prestigious private community in New Cairo, Egypt. This 5-star retreat features a 9,000-square-foot...See More spa and a 27-hole championship golf course designed by Sir Nick Faldo and Brian Curley. Choose from thoughtfully appointed guest rooms and suites offering serene views of lush fairways. Each space is designed for relaxation and renewal, complete with the signature Heavenly® Bed and Heavenly® Bath. Savor Mediterranean-inspired cuisine at our all-day dining restaurant or unwind at The Dunes Bar with an exceptional wine selection. The resort also offers two adaptable function rooms ideal for meetings and social gatherings. At The Westin Cairo, our signature wellness programs inspire you to eat well, sleep well, move well, and feel well—so you can be at your best while discovering Egypt’s capital.See Less

## Exceptional Golf Experience

Set amidst stunning landscaped surrounds, The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa Katameya Dunes features a 27-hole golf course designed by the legendary Sir Nick Faldo. Configured into two championship golf courses, it promises an enjoyable and unique challenge for both beginners as well as the more skilled player.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes?

The check-in time at The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes.

What are the parking options at The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes. Self parking is available at The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes, with rates of $2.00 per hour, $0.00 per day, $0.00 per week, and $0.00 perstay. Valet parking is available for $4.00 perstay.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes?

The closest airport near The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes is Cairo International Airport (CAI). CAI is located approximately 26.1 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes have an airport shuttle to Cairo International Airport (CAI)?

Yes, The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes has an airport shuttle to Cairo International Airport (CAI).

Does The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
