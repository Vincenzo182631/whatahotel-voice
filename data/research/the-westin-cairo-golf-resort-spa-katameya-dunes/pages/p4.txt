Our resort places you close to key destinations...See More such as Cairo Festival City, the American University in Cairo, and Point 90 Mall. ## Westin Swimming Pools

The Swimming Pool is at the heart of the outdoor luxury that The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa Katameya Dunes envisions. Whether it’s a lazy day spent basking in the sun or an afternoon swim with the kids, our pool is the ultimate spot to revive, play and relax.

### New Cairo

A short drive from Cairo International Airport, New Cairo is an upscale district, which includes the Fifth Settlement, Katameya and Rehab City. The area is relatively new but has emerged as one of the most desirable neighborhoods in the Greater Cairo area, because of its quiet location, wide streets, gardens and close proximity to schools, universities and shops.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
