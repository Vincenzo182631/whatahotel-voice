## Cairo Restaurants at the Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa

When it comes to dining in Cairo, The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes offers a variety of cuisines and experiences to suit every taste. Discover Paloma Restaurant, Maré Social, and Dunes...See More Bar—favorites among New Cairo locals. Each venue celebrates fresh, seasonal ingredients and authentic gourmet cooking, defining the resort’s exceptional culinary experience. Maré Social provides an inviting atmosphere both indoors and outdoors, overlooking the golf course—ideal for relaxed gatherings. Whether savoring Mediterranean flavors, enjoying a crafted cocktail, or sharing a meal with friends, every experience reflects our Eat Well philosophy.
