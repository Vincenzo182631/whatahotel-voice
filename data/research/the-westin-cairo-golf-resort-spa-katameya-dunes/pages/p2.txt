## Enjoy a Restful Sleep in Our Hotel Suites in Cairo

Feel at ease in the distinctive surroundings of The Westin Cairo Golf Resort & Spa, Katameya Dunes, where luxury rooms suit every type of traveler. Our 177 tastefully furnished rooms and suites blend modern...See More elegance with a calming atmosphere. Each refreshed accommodation in New Cairo offers signature wellness amenities, featuring the Westin Heavenly® Bed and a Heavenly® Shower-inspired walk-in shower.
