### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Dallas?

The check-in time at W Dallas is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Dallas allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Dallas. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 75.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $175.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Dallas?

Off-site valet parking is available at W Dallas. The daily valet fee is $70.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Dallas have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Dallas has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Dallas booking.

What is the closest airport near W Dallas?

The closest airport near W Dallas is Dallas Love Field (DAL). DAL is located approximately 7.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Dallas have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Dallas does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
