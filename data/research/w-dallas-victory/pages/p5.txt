# Relax & Rejuvenate at Our Luxurious Dallas, TX Spa

## Spa

Welcome to AWAY® Spa — your Uptown Dallas sanctuary rooted in wellness and big Texas energy. Our luxury Dallas, TX day spa offers guests a rejuvenating and detoxifying spa experience, from custom massages to results-driven skincare and holistic body therapies. Hit pause, revitalize your glow, and emerge ready for what’s next.

### Luxuriate in a Personalized Spa Day

From soothing relaxation massages to invigorating facials, each treatment is tailored to your unique needs, ensuring a personalized and unforgettable experience. Your booked treatment includes full access to the to the pool, fitness center, steam shower, and dry sauna, and complimentary valet parking.

Book Your Spa Appointment

## Discover Wellness

Whether you're in need of pampering ahead of a night out or revitalization from the night before, AWAY spa provides guests a variety of customized treatments and relaxing amenities. Located on the 16th floor with stunning views of downtown and Victory Park, our hotel spa in Dallas is your relaxing urban escape.

Learn More

## Therapeutic Treatments

At AWAY Spa Dallas, treatments are tailored with intention—each one crafted to go beyond the expected and meet individual needs. From our Reset Massage that promotes better sleep to the Rewind Facial designed for firming and restoring your skin, the approach is elevated, not overdone. Enhancements like CBD oil, collagen masks, and hot stones offer targeted relief and refreshment.

## Spa Offers

Treat yourself to pure relaxation at our luxury spa in Dallas. Wednesday through Friday, first-time guests can enjoy exclusive pricing on select treatments. Book now and discover why AWAY Spa at W Dallas is a favorite destination for elevated spa services and wellness in Uptown Dallas, TX.

Learn More

## Contrast Therapy

Experience modern contrast therapy before or after your spa treatment at AWAY Spa. Move between the dry heat of our sauna and refreshing cold showers to reset your body and mind. This recharging ritual boosts circulation, eases tension, and leaves you feeling grounded and ready for what’s next.

W Dallas Spa Amenities

### WET Deck Pool & Fitness Center

Spa guests enjoy year-round access to the rooftop WET Deck and fitness center. Sip a cocktail poolside while you soak in the views of downtown Dallas.

### Sauna, Steam Shower & Lounge

Detoxify and indulge in self-care in our dry sauna, steam shower, and relaxation lounge with beverages and snacks. Robes and slippers are provided for ultimate relaxation.

### Complimentary Valet Parking

Finding parking isn’t an issue before your spa treatment. Bonus: Marriott Bonvoy members earn points on spa treatments — making your next vacation (or staycation) that much closer.
