# Suites at W Dallas

## Premium Accommodations

W Dallas keeps it bold, sleek, and unapologetically Victory Park. Our suites rise above Uptown Dallas with floor-to-ceiling windows, Texas-rooted design, and Whatever/Whenever service ready when you are. Whether you are here to celebrate, decompress, or simply take up more space, there is a suite built for exactly that.

### Penthouse

The W Dallas Penthouse delivers a front-row seat to the city, with a private furnished terrace overlooking American Airlines Center and the Dallas skyline. A bold living space with wet bar sets the tone for hosting, while floor-to-ceiling windows make every moment a scene. Spacious, high-design, and unapologetically W.
