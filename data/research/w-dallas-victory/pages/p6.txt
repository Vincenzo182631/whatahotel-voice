# Amplify Your Time in Downtown Dallas

## Experiences

W Dallas stands in Victory Park, one of the city’s prime hubs for entertainment, dining, culture and much more. The American Airlines Center—home of the Mavericks and the Stars, as well as major A list concerts—is across the street from our front door. Steps away, you’ll find the Perot Museum, Katy Trail, the Arts District and more–so much is within walking distance, you’ll be surprised how little you have to Uber. For activities near and far, regardless of distance, our W Insider team can help guide, book and curate the ultimate Dallas experience. All you have to do is ask.

## The Epicenter of Entertainment

W Dallas is next door neighbors with the American Airlines Center, which is one of the city’s most popular venues. From the roar of Mavericks fans rallying behind a clutch three-pointer to the thunderous cheers when the Stars skate to victory, the arena crackles with energy and excitement. On top of sports, concerts by A-list stars are regularly scheduled throughout the year, while comedians bring the laughs and fun-filled family shows ignite wonder for all ages. All you need to do is walk out our front door, and you’re there.

## Sound Minds

At W Dallas, sound is a signature. Spin into vinyl sets at the Living Room, where curated tracks fuel the moment, or head sky-high for rooftop afternoons with local DJs and sun-struck beats by the pool. Beyond the hotel, Dallas hums with music—from indie shows to iconic acts at the city’s myriad sound-focused destinations like House of Blues and Echo Lounge. Whether you’re at the bar, lounging poolside or venue-bound, the rhythm plays on. It’s loud, proud and unmistakably Dallas.

Here, indulgence meets intention in a contemporary space designed to refresh the senses while quieting the mind. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Dallas have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Dallas offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Dallas have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Dallas has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does W Dallas have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY Spa is located on-site at W Dallas. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Dallas?

Dallas Arboretum and Botanical Garden, American Airlines Center, Dallas Market Center, Dos Equis Pavilion, Katy Trail and more are nearby W Dallas.
