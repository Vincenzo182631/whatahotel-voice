# Seize the Day in Downtown Dallas

## Dining

W Dallas dining is bold, vibrant, and distinctly Dallas. From morning espresso to late-night cocktails, elevated dinners at Villa Azur to shareable bites in Living Room, every hour has its flavor. Whether you are starting the day, meeting for lunch, or settling in for the night, there is something here for every taste.

## Fork N' Film

Set within W Dallas, guests are invited into a cinematic evening of chef-driven menus, curated cocktails, and cult-favorite films brought to life one scene at a time. Equal parts dinner party and movie night, Fork N’ Film is designed for date nights, group outings, and anyone who believes film is better with a perfectly paired bite.

### Living Room

From morning to night, Living Room sets the tone. Start with espresso and pastries at the café, then transition into bespoke cocktails and curated sound as the day unfolds. An elevated lounge in the heart of Victory Park, it moves seamlessly from focused mornings to electric evenings. Come early. Stay late.

## Living Room Café

Savor expertly crafted espresso and matcha alongside fresh pastries and thoughtfully prepared light bites. Living Room Café offers a refined yet relaxed setting designed to energize your morning and inspire your afternoon.

## Distinctive by Design

Double-dyed cowhide walls, sculptural furnishings, and a perpetual chandelier create an environment that is both bold and immersive. The aesthetic balances sophistication with ease, inviting conversation, creativity, and visibility in equal measure.

## Happenings

From vinyl-spun DJ sets to game-day energy and seasonal activations, Living Room evolves with the hour. Programming is curated to reflect Dallas culture, creating moments that feel spontaneous yet thoughtfully executed.

### Villa Azur

Villa Azur at W Dallas is where great food meets great energy. The menu is rooted in familiar favorites, refined with thoughtful technique and premium ingredients. Clean flavors, seasonal touches, and subtle twists elevate each dish without overcomplicating it. ## Elevated Eats. Electric Atmosphere.

Bold energy. Refined execution. Villa Azur delivers elevated dining where familiar favorites meet thoughtful technique and premium ingredients. Clean flavors, seasonal touches, and a room built for the kind of nights worth remembering.
