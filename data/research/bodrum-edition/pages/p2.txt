# Rooms

Warm wood finishes and airy fabrics lend a beach house feel to the generous, 5-star guest rooms at The Bodrum EDITION. Every resort guest room features signature linens, custom-designed furniture and ergonomic work areas. Get ready for a day exploring Turkey in the luxury marble bathrooms with deep soaking tubs, monsoon rain showerheads, and custom Le Labo bath products. Oversized windows frame the garden and the breathtaking Aegean Sea, inviting utter relaxation.

# Villa

For those seeking a true refuge in Turkey, our private 4-bedroom villa offers complete tranquility. Spread over three floors, the 5-star villa features balconies and terraces overlooking its large private pool and sauna. The villa is spacious with a large kitchen and separate en suite facilities for each bedroom.
