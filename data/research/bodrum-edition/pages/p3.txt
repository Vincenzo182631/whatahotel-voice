# DAHA Mehmet Akdağ

**DAHA Mehmet Akdağ**

RESERVE

Led by Chef Mehmet Akdağ, DAHA offers an authentic Turkish dining experience at The Bodrum EDITION. Inspired by Anatolia, the menu blends regional ingredients, fire cooking, and refined techniques, marrying heritage with modern Turkish gastronomy.

#### Hours     Dropdown

- SEASONALLY CLOSED

**Morena**

RESERVE

The sun-loving Daylight Restaurant “MORENA” is fun, unapologetically free, relaxed, stylish, and vibrant, just like the spices of the Bazaars where sharing is caring. MORENA is a lively beachside restaurant that excites by offering diverse global flavors.

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY 11:00 AM – 6:00 PM

**Inari Kujira**

RESERVE

An innovative collaboration with Istanbul’s finest Asian Fusion concept, Inari Kujira, transforming the relaxed all-day dining menu at MORENA into an elegant Japanese-inspired dinner menu as “Pop-up Inari Kujira” that celebrates the freshness and diversity of Turkish produce.

#### Hours     Dropdown

- SEASONALLY CLOSED

**Beach Club**

Perfectly perched on Bodrum’s white sand beach, Beach Club is the epitome of elegant relaxation. The minimal yet elevated space plays host to exclusive weekly beach parties, bringing the world’s hottest DJs and entertainers to what has become the region’s ultimate summer destination. Soak up sun, culture and stunning views of the Aegean Sea from VIP cabanas flanking the infinity pool or from reserved beach beds at the water’s edge.

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY
- Hotel guest 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Day use guest 11:30 AM – 6:00 PM
- Beach Bar Drinks 10:00 AM – 12:00 AM
- Beach Bar Dinner 6:00 PM – 12:00 AM

**The Trattoria by Stefano Ciotti**

RESERVE

Since its opening, The Trattoria has been The Bodrum EDITION vibrant spot to enjoy fine dining. Starting in the 2024 season, The Trattoria is partnering up with Michelin-Starred Chef Stefano Ciotti, who is taking the helm to showcase the flavors of Italian cuisine. At the The Trattoria, cocktails are creatively elevated by both local and Italian flavors. More than a culinary destination, The Trattoria inspires as a cultural hot spot, hosting live experiences with renowned artists from the music, art and fashion worlds.

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY
- Dinner 7:00 PM – 12:00 AM

**KITCHEN BY OSMAN SEZENER**

RESERVE

Our locally inspired KITCHEN Restaurant has been awarded One Michelin Star for the third consecutive year. Kitchen by Osman Sezener explores the flavors of the Turkish coast for a true taste of community. Our renowned chef, Osman Sezener, immerses diners in the vibrancy of contemporary Aegean and Anatolian cuisine.

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY
- Breakfast 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM
- Dinner 7:00 PM – 12:00 AM
