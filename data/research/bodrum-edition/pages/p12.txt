# Services & Amenities

Discover the 5-star amenities, superb design and exceptional location of The Bodrum EDITION. This luxury resort welcomes visitors to Turkey with exquisite hotel details revealing elegance and tranquility.

### For further information

Please contact Concierge at +(0252) 311 31 31 or concierge.bod@editionhotels.com

GUEST SERVICES

- 110 unique luxury hotel guest rooms
- Suites feature private plunge pools and terraces
- Four-bedroom private villa with swimming pool and sauna
- Michelin-starred signature Restaurant KITCHEN by Osman Sezener
- Signature restaurant The Trattoria by Michelin-starred Chef Stefano Ciotti
- Infinity pool overlooking the Aegean Sea
- 12,900 square feet of indoor and outdoor hotel meeting and event space with custom catering and natural light
- Customized banquet menus for private events, luncheons, dinner parties and business meetings
- 24-hour Room Service
- Complimentary high-speed internet access throughout the hotel
- Concierge Services
- Complimentary use of the 24-hour gym
- Safety Deposit Boxes
- Multi-lingual staff
- Twice daily housekeeping
- Valet parking available

EXECUTIVE SERVICES

- Executive Boardroom, Meeting Rooms and Private Event Spaces
- Smart boards and electronic flip charts available
- Video Conferencing
- Complimentary high-speed WiFi Internet access throughout the hotel
- State-of-the-art audio/visual equipment
- Executive car services available
- Executive butler services available

GYM

- Gym features state-of-the-art fitness equipment, including cardiovascular machines
- Reservation required
- Free-weights
- State-of-the-art strength-training, weight and cardio equipment
- Personal training available
- Indoor cycling sessions available
- Pilates Room for group or personalized training sessions
- Yoga Terrace
- Complimentary water and towels

SPA

- Luxury spa offers massages, therapy baths, plunge pool sessions and other healing and beauty treatments
- Full-service spa with 8 treatment rooms and suites
- Turkish Hammam, salt treatment room, and sauna
- Vitality Lounge
- Quiet Zone

RESTAURANTS AND BARS

- The Trattoria restaurant offers the flavors of Italian cuisine
- Morena is a casual restaurant offering international fare and tempting bar specialties by the infinity pool, overlooking Yalikavak Beach
- KITCHEN by Osman Sezener tempts patrons with Mediterranean and Turkish dishes with its Michelin star
