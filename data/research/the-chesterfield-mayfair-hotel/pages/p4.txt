# Stanley's Rooftop Bar

Stanley’s is a relaxed rooftop bar above Mayfair, created in honour of Stanley Tollman, whose lifelong passion for hospitality, cocktails, and delicious food inspired this space. He believed in the simple pleasures of sharing a drink, a meal, and good conversation.

For our family, naming the bar after him feels like a natural way to recognise those values and his rich legacy. We welcome you to enjoy Stanley’s as he would have, with warmth, generosity, and good company.

### COSY EVENINGS AT STANLEY’S

Make the most of the season at Stanley’s with seasonal signature cocktails, comforting bar favourites and dishes designed to share, all served with sweeping views across Mayfair.

Perfect for cooler evenings, our covered rooftop is heated, with cosy blankets on hand so you can settle in whatever the weather. On selected evenings, live music from 5pm to 10pm performed by guitarist and singer Martha Goddard adds to the atmosphere, making Stanley’s the perfect spot for after-work drinks and relaxed evenings with friends.

### After Dark at Stanley’s: Cigars

As we approach the colder months and the roof will need to remain closed more frequently, we will no longer offer tobacco sales at Stanleys until the return of the warmer weather in 2027.

Guests are still welcome to bring their own tobacco and smoke at Stanleys when weather conditions allow the roof to be open between 8 and 11pm, subject to a minimum spend of £50.
