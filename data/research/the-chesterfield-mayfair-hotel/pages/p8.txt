# What not to miss

Discover what to taste and sip, where to slumber and venture, what experiences to enjoy, and who to connect with during your stay at The Chesterfield Mayfair.

General Manager

### Eat

Indulge in our signature Dover Sole at Butlers Restaurant. Delicately prepared, this exquisite dish showcases the finest flavours of the sea. Accompanied by impeccable service, this promises for an unforgettable dining experience.

### Drink

Indulge in the timeless sophistication of a Smoking Old Fashioned. Crafted with precision and passion, we invite you to sip and savour your cocktail in our atmospheric hotel bar, Sem's of Mayfair.

Our Chesterfield Suite exudes timeless elegance with its plush decor and luxurious furnishings. Featuring bespoke amenities, this suite offers a perfect blend of comfort and sophistication.

Indulge in the splendour of the Royal Academy of Arts Museum. Just a leisurely stroll away from The Chesterfield Mayfair, this is the perfect place to immerse yourself in London's cultural scene.
