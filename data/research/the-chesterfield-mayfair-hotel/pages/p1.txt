# Classic British charm

This summer, enter our polished marble lobby and immediately experience the warm and personal service that characterises The Chesterfield Mayfair. As a proud part of The Red Carnation Hotel Collection, our hotel on Mayfair's Charles Street offers a serene sanctuary amidst the bustling city life. With a prime central London location, just a brief walk from Bond Street, Hyde Park, and iconic landmarks such as The Royal Academy and Buckingham Palace, The Chesterfield Mayfair truly embodies the vibrant essence of our historic city.

Our location ensures guests have easy access to the very best that London has to offer, from upscale shopping on Bond Street to cultural excursions at nearby museums and galleries.
