# Dining & drinks

Visitors and guests alike are treated to creative cuisine at Butlers Restaurant, classic cocktails in Sem's of Mayfair, stylish rooftop drinks at Stanley's, whimsical Afternoon Tea in the Conservatory, and intimate private soirées in The Library. Dining at The Chesterfield Mayfair is aways inspired and always delicious, showcasing organic, seasonal British ingredients.

Rooftop bar

### Stanley's

Stanley’s is a relaxed rooftop bar above Mayfair, created in honour of Stanley Tollman, whose lifelong passion for hospitality, cocktails, and delicious food inspired this space.

Breakfast in Bed & Beyond

### In-Room Dining

Enjoy the comfort of dining in your room at The Chesterfield Mayfair. Our All-Day Dining menu is available from 11:00am to 11:00pm, offering a selection of light bites, classic dishes and indulgent desserts delivered directly to your door.

A Night Menu is available overnight for late arrivals or evening cravings.

Guests may also enjoy room service breakfast by completing the breakfast card provided in their room and placing it outside their door before retiring. Cards are collected by the Night Managers during the early hours to ensure breakfast is delivered at your preferred time the following morning.

### Christmas Parties in Mayfair

Host your festive celebration at The Chesterfield Mayfair, with private dining rooms available for exclusive use and festive packages from £130 per person. Enjoy a three-course seasonal menu, sommelier-selected wine, homemade mince pies, luxury Christmas crackers and personalised menus and place cards.

Make the occasion your own with canapés on arrival, a sparkling wine reception or after-dinner drinks, while our experienced events team takes care of every detail. Larger party bookings can also enjoy exclusive rewards, from Afternoon Tea for four to an overnight stay in a Junior Suite.
