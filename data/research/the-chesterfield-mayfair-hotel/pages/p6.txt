# Sem's of Mayfair

Nestled on tranquil Mayfair Street, just moments from Shepherd's Market and Berkeley Square, Sem's of Mayfair is an enchanting day-to-night escape. The bar was named after the artist Georges Goursat; better known by his pen name, "Sem", whose playful illustrations adorn the bar's walls.

Sem's of Mayfair offers an irresistible blend of innovative cocktails from Bar Manager Alejandro Peralta Santos and delectable small dishes. As twilight descends, immerse yourself in the seductive rhythm of live musical performances, including a jazz quartet, and a live singer and pianist. Delve into distinctive bar experiences such as our Rum Tasting, adding an extra layer of allure to your night out.

### Menus

At Sem's of Mayfair, we pride ourselves on serving timeless classics with a modern twist, where each sip takes you on a journey of refined taste and sophistication. Whether you're joining us for a cocktail, or sampling from our all-day dining menu of light bites and classic dishes, Sem's of Mayfair invites you to indulge in delicious flavours and share memorable moments in a welcoming atmosphere.
