### The St. Regis Spa

Embark on a journey towards tranquility at our Spa. Start at the Celebration Bar, a consultation space with an array of offerings, from light bites and premium tea to beauty care. Experience the Caroline Astor Collection's unique treatments and unwind in the heated pool, steam room, salon, or at a dedicated 'Silence Room', each designed to deepen your sense of serenity.

### Fitness Center

Seamlessly integrate wellness into your stay at our fitness center, equipped with state-of-art facilities, including a BMI scale and personal trainers. A multifunction room accommodates both group & personal training sessions, while floor-to-ceiling windows offer natural light & inspiring views during your workout. Open 24 hours, our fitness center ensures your health remains a top priority any time of the day.

### Swimming Pool

Step into a serene paradise that offers a peaceful escape from capital's hustle. With its bohemian style, featuring with Batik-inspired art, this space invites relaxation. The ambience is enhanced with a gentle mist, creating a tranquil oasis complete with a kids' pool, whirlpool, and a pool bar where classic drinks and signature cocktails await.

Exceptional Service Around the Clock

## The St. Regis Butler Experience

The St. Regis Butler Experience is the epitome of our renowned personalized luxury, ensuring that every aspect of your stay is imbued with unparalleled care. From addressing any special request before your arrival to a seamless check-in and check-out process, our butlers are there to serve. With top-tier services including packing and unpacking, pressing, bedding services, and a nightly turndown routine, our butlers will make you feel effortlessly at home.

For the Little Ones

## Family-Friendly Activities

The St. Regis Jakarta offers bespoke activites designed with the entire family in mind, including the little ones. Our selection of amenities includes themed bathrobes, playful tents, and a variety of toys, making every stay both engaging and comfortable. During the weekends, discover kid-friendly activities, offering everything from coloring to watching movies. At The St. Regis Jakarta, every family member is well cared for and thoroughly entertained throughout their stay.

Yes. The St. Regis Jakarta offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
