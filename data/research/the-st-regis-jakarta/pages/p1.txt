# Welcome to The St. Regis Jakarta

## Elevate your experience at this luxury hotel in Jakarta

The St. Regis Jakarta reflects the soul of Indonesia and celebrates the country's cultural achievements. Set within the region's Golden Triangle, our 5-star hotel in Jakarta is designed for the moment and built to last for generations. Find our luxury hotel in Jakarta, Indonesia, near the city's three main business districts. Our Jakarta hotel rethinks urban comfort with The St. Regis Spa, a two-story...See More facility with six treatment rooms, a sauna, a steam room and a whirlpool. Lounge under the palm trees beside our pool or linger at The Pool Bar. Attend a personal training session or group class in our hotel fitness center before a meal in one of six restaurants and bars. Book one of our event venues or ballrooms, including Astor Court, our coveted outdoor space, for a meeting or wedding in Jakarta, Indonesia. Delight in 5-star amenities as you recharge in a room or suite with chandeliers and our signature 24-hour butler service. Let us transport you to luxury at The St. Regis Jakarta.See Less

## Solène

Bonjour!

Inspired by soleil, French for “sun”, Solène brings the warmth and allure of Southern France flavors to Jakarta. Rooted in cuisine du soleil, our chefs craft refined dishes inspired by the effortless charm of the French Riviera, featuring seafood, seasonal produce and quality ingredients. Step into French Riviera-inspired interiors for intimate dinners, celebrations and gatherings.

Legacy of Taste, Luxury of Choice

## Bel Étage

Our all-day, upscale dining venue, Bel Étage, marries culinary exellence with gracious dining, satisfying every palate with flavors from around the globe. The essence of Bel Étage is in its thoughtful details, from the historical charm of a function antique oven to the exclusivity of personalized meal options. Every guest's preferences are carefully documented, allowing us to serve them exactly the way they like it at each visit. Set against a backdrop of minimalist copper and earthy hues that radiate both warmth and sophistication. Bel Étage presents a tableau-sourced ingredients. The culinary experience at Bel Étage is an ode to diversity and quality, serving bullet, and a la carte menus that celebrate a rich tapestry of taste and traditions.

Learn More

A Previlege and a Pleasure

## The St. Regis Bar

Welcome to The St. Regis Bar, the city's quintessential destination for discerning patrons seeking artistry and a dynamic ambiance in their toast to life. Delve into a curated selection of exquisite wines from around the globe in our award-winning vault, find bespoke flavors in the tasting and malt rooms, or revel in privacy within our exclusive dining areas. Savor our New York-inspired & bespoke cocktails, where each sip offers a unique story. Celebrate the art of refined living in an atmosphere that's both unique and timeless, starting with our captivating mural that narrates Javanese spiritualism, music, and communal harmony, illuminated by the grandeur of a chandelier centerpiece, and serenaded by the soothing sounds of live, upbeat Jazz performed by an international four-piece band led by a female vocalist that rotates quarterly. Distinguished as the number one bar in Jakarta, The St. Regis Bar is more than a place.

The Finer Things

## Rosé Gourmand Deli

Step into Rosé, a charming Gourmand Deli that artfully blends the comfort of a neighborhood coffee spot with a fine deli and grocer. Start your day with our artisanal coffees, high-teas, or homemade smoothies, and savor the afternoon with a selection of craft beers and fine wines. Dedicated to delivering an experience of quality and indulgence, Rosé also offers an array of cold cuts, cheeses, and ready-to-eat, homemade delights. Whether you're seeking a quiet moment over a drink, or in search of the perfect gathering place for connoisseurs and friends alike, Rosé welcomes you with its soft blush design that soothe the senses.

A Symphony of Culture and Grandeur

## The Drawing Room

The Drawing Room serves as a sanctuary where the rituals of arrival and departure are transformed into moments of indulgence, just steps away from The Great Hall’s breathtaking visual ceiling display - a tribute to both Indonesian culture and St. Regis’ legacy of music and performance. Experience timeless traditions shared through generations including delectable afternoon tea and the weekend's ceremonial Sabrage, all while being serenaded by a solo pianist in the afternoon. From gourmet delights to fresh botanicals, The Drawing Room is designed to evoke an elevated sense of residential comfort.

### Impeccable Service

Embark on an immersive odyssey with The St. Regis Butler Experience, a coveted hallmark of our hospitality. Our Butlers attend to your every need even before your arrival, ensuring you feel right at home as you step into our doors. Indulge in unparalleled, around-the-clock assistance, from turn-down services to packing, and relish the personalized care that defines your stay with us.

### Weekend Brunch

Weekend Brunch at Bel Étage is a celebration of gourmet splendor. Dive into a lavish seafood selection, accompanied by a rich parade of antipasti, à la minute signature mains, and scrumptious desserts. We mark the occasion with a noon Sabrage and share the champagne with our distinguished guests. With an unlimited selection of cocktails, wines, brews, and champagne, there's a toast for every taste.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Jakarta?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Jakarta is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Jakarta allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Jakarta.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Jakarta?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Jakarta. Self parking is available at The St. Regis Jakarta, with rates of Rp5000.00 per hour, Rp120000.00 per day, Rp840000.00 per week, and Rp0.00 perstay. Valet parking is available for Rp200000.00 per hour.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Jakarta have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Jakarta has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Jakarta booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Jakarta?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Jakarta is Halim Perdanakusuma International Airport (HLP). HLP is located approximately 13.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Jakarta have an airport shuttle to Jakarta Soekarno-Hatta International Airport (CGK)?

Yes, The St. Regis Jakarta has an airport shuttle to Jakarta Soekarno-Hatta International Airport (CGK).

Does The St. Regis Jakarta have an airport shuttle to Halim Perdanakusuma International Airport (HLP)?

Yes, The St. Regis Jakarta has an airport shuttle to Halim Perdanakusuma International Airport (HLP).

Does The St. Regis Jakarta have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Jakarta does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
