Weekend Brunch at Bel Étage

Bel Étage

### Weekend Brunch

Meaning 'noble floor', Bel Étage treats gourmands to delectable global tastes. Indulge in multicultural fare and iconic Weekend Brunch, featuring an exceptional array of seafood, signature mains, and desserts.

### Pool Bar

For a refreshing change of scene, visit our Pool Bar where classic drinks and signature cocktails are served in an inviting, eccentric outdoor space.

The St. Regis Bar

### Exemplary Cocktails

The St. Regis Bar epitomizes refined indulgence with a theatrical flair, offering an immersive gateway to the finer things in life. Start with an exquisite drinking experience in the heart of the city, featuring New York-inspired signature cocktails like the Batavia Mary—a local twist on the classic Bloody Mary.

### Violet Hour

Begin the evening with the Violet Hour, a celebrated ritual where bartenders serve complimentary martinis from a rowing bar cart. This tradition marks the transition from the day's hustle to a serene evening, offering guests a moment of refined relaxation with every sip of their drink.

### Award-Winning Wine Selections

Master the art of fine wine at our award-winning wine vault, featuring an inspiring collection from the world's most renowned wine-producing countries. Each selections is meticulously curated, showcasing rare and celebrated options ideal for discerning connoisseurs with a refined palate.

Rosé Gourmand Deli

### Refined Indulgence

Whether for a swift bite or a leisurely pause, Rosé Gourmand Deli blends casual sophistication with culinary delight. Offering an exceptional range of artisanal coffees, high-teas, and smoothies, alongside an array of craft beers and wines. Rosé caters to the connoiseur. Browse our selection of ready-to-eat items from cold cuts and fine cheeses to nutritious bites and fresh homemade confection.

The Drawing Room

### Afternoon Tea

The Drawing Room is where the heart of the hotel turns into a stage for indulgence. Every detail from the elegant afternoon tea service, offering a spectrum of light, hearty delights and desserts. The afternoon tea is thoughtfully orchestred to evoke the warmth and refined comfort of a luxurious residence, ensuring each visit feels like home.

### Immersive Experiences

As the day winds down, find solace in The Drawing Room, where an exclusive soundtrack and the mesmerizing dance of Kinetic Light Chandeliers create an enchanting evening retreat, while weekends come alive with the celebrated traditions of a Sabrage.
