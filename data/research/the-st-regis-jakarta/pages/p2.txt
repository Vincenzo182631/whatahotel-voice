A Sanctuary Above the City

### Presidential Suite

Ascend to a world where opulence and tranquility merge at The St. Regis Jakarta’s Presidential Suite. A palette of soft pastels and soothing grey hues welcomes you, setting a scene of understated royalty and tasteful elegance. Spanning 187 sqm, this grand suite unveils a majestic living area, an elegant bedroom, and a private study, each fitted with advanced technology for an effortless stay. From the captivating city views by day to a serene retreat by night, every aspect of this suite is designed with discernment in mind. With a dedicated 24-hour butler service, every need is cared for.

Where Elegance Resides

## Empire Suite

Step into the Empire Suite at The St. Regis Jakarta, a sanctuary of timeless elegance where home meets unparalleled luxury.
This suite, complete with a separate living room, dining space and dressing area, radiates warmth with its bronze undertone and enriched in royal blue accents. Every detail in this 154 sqm space is a testament to meticulous craftsmanship with a seamless fusion of contemporary design and local artistry. Relish every moment with exclusive access to our 24-hour dedicated St. Regis butler service, ensuring that every detail of your stay is flawlessly curated. Here, distinguished living is an art, with elegance and comfort uniting to enhance your experience.

Elevate Your Stay

## Caroline Astor Suite

Experience a stay that feels home yet transcends ordinary luxury at the Caroline Astor Suite. The spacious suite features a distinct living room, bedroom, and dressing area, adorned in calming ivory hues, complemented by bronze accents and royal blue. Delight in cultural richness, from signature tastes of our in-house bar to locally-designed furnishings exclusive to The St. Regis Jakarta. Named after a figure pivotal to the St. Regis legacy and celebrated for her love of polished luxury, the suite further elevates your stay with our cutting-edge systems and iconic butler service.
