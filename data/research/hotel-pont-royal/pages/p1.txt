# ExperienceExperience the Hotel Pont Royal

Nestled on the Left Bank, steps away from the literary cafés and art galleries that shaped the legend of the neighbourhood, Hôtel Pont Royal offers more than a stay — it's an immersion into the Paris that inspired Hemingway, Sartre and Simone de Beauvoir. Its exceptional location, between the Seine and Boulevard Saint-Germain, makes it the perfect base to explore world-class museums, historic bookshops and the most iconic terraces in the city.

## The art of the parisian stay

Wrapped in warm wood tones and an intimate atmosphere, Hôtel Pont Royal unveils 75 light-filled rooms and suites, each designed as a refined retreat in the heart of Saint-Germain-des-Prés. From your room with Parisian rooftop views, let the soul of the City of Light wash over you — iconic monuments, Haussmann rooftops and a rare sense of stillness, all within sight.

## Gastronomy and exceptional flavors

At Hôtel Pont Royal, gastronomy is the natural extension of an exceptional stay. From the gastronomic cuisine of L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon to the craft cocktails of Bar F-Sharp, each space embodies a distinct identity. An invitation to savour Paris through the senses, in one of the most vibrant neighbourhoods of the capital.

# Les réponses à vos questions

- ### Why stay in Saint-Germain-des-Prés in Paris?

Saint-Germain-des-Prés is one of the most iconic neighborhoods on Paris’s Left Bank. It offers convenient access to the capital’s major cultural attractions, while retaining a quiet, residential atmosphere that is hard to find in the more touristy areas. Art galleries, historic bookstores, literary cafés, and Haussmann-style facades come together to create an authentically Parisian living environment.

Discover the neighborhood

- ### How can I reserve a table at the hotel's restaurant?

We recommend booking your dining experience in advance, particularly for the Atelier de Joël Robuchon, renowned for its fine cuisine and its friendly “kitchen-centric” atmosphere. Our staff will be glad to make the reservation on your behalf to ensure you get the best table possible. The hotel also features the Bar F-Sharp, where you can enjoy lunch, dinner, or extend your evening with a signature cocktail.

Discover our restaurants

- ### Does the Hôtel Pont Royal offer rooms with views of the Eiffel Tower?

The Hôtel Pont Royal offers rooms and suites with unobstructed views of the Paris skyline from the Left Bank. Depending on their location and orientation, certain room categories offer a view of the Eiffel Tower. They are among the most sought-after experiences for travelers seeking to combine 5-star comfort with an iconic panorama.

Explore our rooms

- ### Why choose Saint-Germain-des-Prés for a night of jazz in Paris?

Saint-Germain-des-Prés is one of the Parisian neighborhoods most naturally associated with jazz music and the cultural nightlife scene. Its artistic history, its cozy and cosmopolitan atmosphere, and its Left Bank spirit create a setting that is perfectly suited for an evening of music. Jazz finds a natural home here, free from artificiality, making it the go-to destination for an elegant evening of this kind in Paris.

Discover our jazz nights

- ### What are the booking conditions for extended stays?

The Hôtel Pont Royal regularly offers special rates for stays of more than 3 nights. These offers often include flexible cancellation policies, providing valuable flexibility when planning a trip in Paris. We strongly recommend booking in advance to secure the rooms best suited to your needs and to take advantage of the best availability.

Explore our offers

- ### Is the Hôtel Pont Royal suitable for families with children?

Hotel Pont Royal’s flagship family accommodation is the 2-Bedroom Panoramic Terrace Suite, a luxurious 65m²apartment designed to accommodate up to 5 guests. The Junior Suite and the Junior Suite with Eiffel Tower View, each approximately 30 m², are also designed for families traveling with children. Each suite features a dedicated living area equipped with a sofa bed, carefully selected to provide a restful night’s sleep for two children under the age of 12.

Explore our suites
