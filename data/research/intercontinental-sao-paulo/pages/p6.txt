## FAQs for InterContinental Sao Paulo

Does InterContinental Sao Paulo have a pool?

InterContinental Sao Paulo has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Enjoy our serene pool area every day from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM — the perfect, peaceful pause to unwind and soak up São Paulo’s sunshine.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Sao Paulo?

At InterContinental Sao Paulo, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 75 BRL .

Private parking lot is available in the hotel entrance. Valet parking is available for guests and non-guests.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Sao Paulo?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Sao Paulo.

Pet policy description. Dogs or cats up to 33 lbs are allowed, always accompanied by their guardians.

Pet fee per night: 180 BRL

Pet weight limit: No weight limit per pet

2 pets allowed

Pets allowed: Only dogs and cats allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Sao Paulo for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Sao Paulo have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Sao Paulo has 1 restaurant.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Sao Paulo?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:00 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Sao Paulo?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Sao Paulo.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Sao Paulo?

Check-in at InterContinental Sao Paulo is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Sao Paulo offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Sao Paulo offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Sao Paulo from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Sao Paulo is conveniently located near Guarulhos International Airport (GRU), approximately 21.75 miles away (35 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Sao Paulo?

InterContinental Sao Paulo is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- São Paulo Art Museum - Masp
- Ibirapuera Park
- Municipal Market
- Liberdade Street
- Football Museum - Pacaembu Stadium
- Sala São Paulo
- SESC Paulista - 0.68 miles away (1.1 kilometers)
- MASP Museum
- Cidade São Paulo Mall
- Trianon Park

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Sao Paulo puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
