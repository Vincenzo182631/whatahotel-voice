### Pet Friendly Hotel São Paulo

We believe pets are part of the family! Enjoy a warm welcome for your furry companions, with amenities designed to make their stay comfortable. Explore nearby parks and outdoor spaces together, and relax knowing your pet is welcome to join you on your travels. Experience a luxurious getaway where everyone feels at home.

### Exclusive Club Lounge Access

Our exclusive Club Lounge offers a sophisticated retreat with personalized services, complimentary breakfast, and evening cocktails. Enjoy a refined atmosphere with breathtaking city views, perfect for business meetings and unwinding in comfort.

Learn more

### Les Clefs d’Or Concierge Excellence

Our dedicated concierge team, including one Les Clefs d’Or member, is here to elevate your stay with expert recommendations and personalized service, ensuring you experience the best of São Paulo.

### 24-hour Gym

Maintain your wellness routine in our fully equipped gym, open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Whether it’s an early morning workout or a late-night session, enjoy the convenience and comfort of a space designed to keep you energized throughout your stay.

Learn more

### Improvements in Progress

As part of our ongoing commitment to enhancing your stay, we are currently improving our pool area. During this time, the pool will be temporarily closed. We appreciate your patience and understanding as we work to create an even more enjoyable experience for your future visits.

Learn more
