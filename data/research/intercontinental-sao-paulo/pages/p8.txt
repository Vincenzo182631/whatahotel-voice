Welcome to InterContinental São Paulo, where the heart of this historic city awaits your exploration. From culture to iconic landmarks and hidden gems, we're here to help you create unforgettable memories during your stay.

1. Prime Location in São Paulo

### Prime Location in São Paulo

InterContinental São Paulo boasts a prime location in the heart of the city, just steps from Paulista Avenue. Surrounded by top restaurants, luxury shopping, cultural landmarks, and business hubs, the hotel offers easy access to everything São Paulo has to offer, making it the perfect choice for both business and leisure travelers.

## Things to do

### São Paulo Art Museum - Masp

Av. Paulista, 1578, São Paulo, SP

The Masp museum is very famous in São Paulo mainly because of its historical importance. The building is a city's postcard where an interesting exhibition can be found and appreciated by all. On Sundays, under the building you will get delighted with an open air market where people sell valued household antiquities where you can stroll around, buy stunning objects and have a great meal nearby. Moreover, you will love to walk inside Trianon Park, just across the street. We hope you enjoy it!

Learn more

### Ibirapuera Park

Av. Pedro Álvares Cabral, s/n, São Paulo, SP 04094050

Ibirapuera Park is one of São Paulo’s most iconic and expansive green spaces, offering a perfect blend of nature, culture, and leisure. This sprawling 1.6 square-kilometer park features beautiful open lawns, serene lakes, and vast tree-lined paths, making it a hub for relaxation. The park is also home to several cultural institutions, including the Museum of Modern Art, the Afro-Brazilian Museum, and the Oca Pavilion, offering a rich array of art, exhibitions, and events.

Learn more

### Municipal Market

Rua da Cantareira, 306, São Paulo, SP

The Municipal market is a very unique experience where you will be able to see and buy all different fruits, cereals, meats and food worlwide. This historical place was built between 1928 and 1933. If you happen to visit it, try a very traditional mortadela sandwich. It is really worthy it!

Learn more

### Liberdade Street

Rua da Liberdade, s/n, São Paulo, SP

São Paulo has the largest Japanese community outside Japan. The Liberdade Street is an open market where you can observe and live the Japanese culture wherever you look at. This is a great place where you can buy typical Japanese clothes, souvenirs and taste some very typical food.

### Football Museum - Pacaembu Stadium

Praça Charles Miller, S/N - Estádio do P, São Paulo, SP

This great museum is dedicated to the footbal sport and not to a team specifically. The Pacaembu stadium is a historical patrimony. If you enjoy this sport, you will love the timeline connecting the football and its history. It is worth joining a group to watch a live match.

Praça Júlio Prestes, nº 16, São Paulo, SP 01218020

Sala São Paulo is a place apreciated by those who love musical concerts and architeture. The building is a beautiful historical landmark in São Paulo city.

### MASP Museum

Av. Paulista, 1578, São Paulo SP

The MASP (São Paulo Museum of Art) is famous for its collection of Western art, including pieces by renowned artists such as Van Gogh, Rembrandt, and Picasso, as well as a significant selection of Brazilian art. The museum also hosts temporary exhibitions, cultural events, and educational programs, making it a hub for all art enthusiasts. Its design and collection embody a fusion of contemporary vision with historical significance, making MASP a must-visit destination in the heart of São Paulo.

### Cidade São Paulo Mall

Av. Paulista 1230, Bela Vista, São Paulo, SP

The shopping mall at Cidade São Paulo offers a premium retail experience in a prime location. Featuring a curated selection of high-end boutiques, international brands, and local retailers, it caters to a wide range of tastes and styles. With a variety of dining options, as well as entertainment choices, the shopping mall at Cidade São Paulo is a top destination for both shopping enthusiasts and those seeking a leisurely day out in the city.

### Trianon Park

Rua Peixoto Gomide, 949 - Cerqueira Cesar, São Paulo, SP

Trianon Park is a serene urban oasis located along the iconic Paulista Avenue in São Paulo. This lush park offers a peaceful retreat amidst the bustling city, featuring dense foliage, tall trees, and well-maintained pathways. The park is also home to a diverse range of native plants and wildlife, creating a natural sanctuary within the city. Situated near many cultural landmarks, Trianon Park is an ideal spot to unwind while enjoying the vibrant atmosphere of São Paulo.
