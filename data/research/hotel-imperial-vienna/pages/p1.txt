# Welcome to Hotel Imperial, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Vienna

## HISTORY, TRADITION, LUXURY AND ROMANCE IN PERFECT HARMONY

Home to such iconic classical composers as Mozart, Beethoven, Schubert and Strauss, Vienna boasts more than 50 theaters and opera houses. The Vienna waltz originated here and its centuries-old spirit still...See More permeates the city's dancehalls. The expert concierge and butlers at our Vienna 5-star hotel attend to all wishes and desires before they are expressed, such as having a hand-ironed newspaper delivered to your bedside with morning tea. Tastefully furnished with precious, authentic antiques, walls cocooned in silk, and bathrooms made of marble, our 79 rooms and 59 suites blend the timeless elegance of 19th Century Vienna with state-of-the-art facilities and amenities. Voted as ‘One Of The 25 Most Iconic Hotels To Visit On Earth In 2024’ by Elle Décor and being recognized on Condé Nast Traveler’s 2024 Gold List, our renowned central Vienna hotel awaits with a matchless experience.See Less

### Imperial Torte

### Pets at Hotel Imperial

We permit one dog (36kg/80lbs) per room and provide the following amenities for their comfort: a dog bowl, bed and mat, and a Pet Welcome Kit. Guests are required to sign a liability waiver at check-in and a one time fee of € 50 and € 20 for each additional night for the cleaning fee will apply. Please note that we do not permit pets in the hotel's meeting rooms and in the restaurant Opus.

### Delight All the Senses

Catering to your individual wishes is our top priority, whether at the Café Imperial Wien, the exclusive Restaurant OPUS, or in our elegant banquet rooms, magnificent halls or suites. The magnificent Imperial Bar is the hotel’s centerpiece. Today concerts and lavish celebrations are held where horse-drawn carriages once entered the palace in imperial times.

### Café Imperial Wien

## We Care About the Environment

Our goal is to protect our environment and act sustainably wherever possible. The Austrian Ministry for Environment recognized our green actions by awarding us with the Austrian Eco Label. Environmental activities focus on efficient energy saving, waste prevention, eco-friendly buying, and ecological cleaning. Thus water-saving modules in the fittings are used, heat recycling from air-conditioning is practised, eco-friendly refill packages are bought while only fair-trade coffee and meals from predominantly regional products with a focus on sustainable cultivation are served. Furthermore, we encourage our guests and employees to take part in this important cause every day. For example, we offer all guests who arrive at our hotel by train an additional bottle of water while various digital services to reduce paper consumption are available. Last but not least, we are always striving to introduce new processes to become more sustainable every day.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Hotel Imperial, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Vienna?

The check-in time at Hotel Imperial, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Vienna is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Hotel Imperial, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Vienna allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Hotel Imperial, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Vienna. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 36.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €50.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Hotel Imperial, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Vienna?

Off-site self parking is available near Hotel Imperial, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Vienna at Contipark Tiefgarage Kärntnerringgarage.

Does Hotel Imperial, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Vienna have in-room Wi-Fi?

What is the closest airport near Hotel Imperial, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Vienna?

The closest airport near Hotel Imperial, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Vienna is Vienna International Airport (VIE). VIE is located approximately 16.0 KM from the hotel.
