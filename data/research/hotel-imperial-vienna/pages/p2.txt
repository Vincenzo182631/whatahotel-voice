### Signature Suites

For over 150 years, royalty and dignitaries have stayed in suites adorned with antiques, marble baths, silk walls, and chandeliers. Each room reflects 19th‑century grandeur while offering today’s luxury.

Explore Suites

### The Royal Suite

Today’s Royal Suites were once the private apartments of Prince Phillipp of Wuerttemberg. Crystal chandeliers gleam from seven metre stucco ceilings, imbuing the rooms with aristocratic flair. With double doors and inlaid parquet flooring, they provide an ideal setting for functions, banquets or presentations. The two symmetrical suites can be joined with adjacent rooms, creating 460 m².
