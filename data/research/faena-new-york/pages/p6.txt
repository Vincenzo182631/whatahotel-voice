## New York

### Check-In/ Check-Out

Check-in begins after 3:00 PM / Check-out by 12:00 PM

### Reservation General

- A valid credit card is required to guarantee all reservations. An authorization of one night’s room and tax will be placed on the credit card at the time of confirmation. A refundable deposit of one night’s room and tax will be applied to all suite bookings, as well as all bookings of 5 nights or more.
- Valid government-issued photo identification will be required at check-in
- Must be 21 years or older for room bookings and stay
- Children under the age of 12 years are granted a complimentary stay in the existing bedding configuration. Rollaway beds are available for certain room types at an extra cost
- To cover incidentals, a $250/per night hold is placed on guest credit card
- Early arrival/late departure obtainable based on availability. Guaranteed early arrival/late departure available for additional fee, dependent on date and room type.

### Cancellation Policy

Cancelations may be cancelled 3 days prior to arrival to avoid a 1 night penalty. Seasonal and rate specific exceptions may apply.

Early departures: fee of one room night plus tax issued to departures of one-night prior.

### Property General

Faena New York is a non-smoking property. Any room that has been used for smoking is subject to an additional smoking fee.

Faena New York welcomes canine travelers up to 25 lbs. Kindly note that our pet policy is limited to dogs only. A non-refundable $250 pet fee will apply. Pet guidelines may be updated in accordance with local government ordinances.

### Photography

In keeping with the intimate, artistic atmosphere of Faena New York, and to preserve the privacy and comfort of our guests, **photography, videography, and audio recording are not permitted** within _La Boca,_ _The Living Room and public spaces_.

This policy includes, but is not limited to:

- Taking photos or recordings of guests or team members
- Social media filming, live streaming, or content creation
- Use of professional or commercial equipment, including cameras, lighting, tripods or stands, or recording devices

Our team is happy to assist with any questions. Thank you for your understanding and for helping us maintain the experience and ambiance of our spaces.

### Health & Safety Guidelines

At Faena, the safety and well-being of our guests and team members remains our top priority. With your security, safety, and health in mind, we have enhanced our health and safety protocols to adhere to the recommendations and guidelines set by the City of New York.

### Valet Parking

- Valet parking is available at Faena New York for $120 per vehicle, per 24-hour period, plus applicable taxes. This rate applies to all vehicle types.
- Kindly note that valet parking does not include in-and-out privileges. Electric vehicle charging is available upon request and is included in the rate, with adaptors available for Tesla and all other EV models.
- To retrieve your vehicle, please contact our Guest Experience team with your valet ticket number. Kindly allow approximately 20 minutes for vehicle retrieval.
- For guests visiting our restaurants and lounges, valet parking will be processed upon arrival. A member of our team will escort you to the front desk to complete payment. For vehicle retrieval, please return to the Ambassador team for assistance.
- Valet parking is located at: 515 W 18th Street

## Miami Beach

Check-in begins after 4:00 PM / Check-out by 12:00 PM

- One room night and tax deposit apply at time of reservation with a valid credit card guarantee policy. Deposit is subject to change based on rate type and blackout dates.
- Credit card used to make the room reservation is required at check-in
- Valid government issued photo identification will be required at check-in
- Must be 18 years or older for room bookings and stay
- Children under the age of 12 years granted complimentary stay in the existing bedding configuration. Rollaway beds available for certain room types at extra cost
- To cover incidentals, a $250/per night hold is placed on guest credit card
- Early arrival/late departure obtainable based on availability. Guaranteed early arrival/late departure available for additional fee dependent on date and room type.

### Cancelation Policy

Cancelations may be made 7 days prior to arrival or deposit is forfeited. Seasonal and rate specific exceptions may apply.

Early departures are subject to fee: fee of one room night plus tax issued to departures of one-night prior,  50% of remaining room balance plus tax balance issued to departures of two or more nights prior

### Property General

Faena Hotel Miami Beach is a non-smoking property. Any room that has been used for smoking is subject to an additional smoking fee.

Faena Hotel Miami Beach is a pet friendly property and welcome all four legged furry travelers up to 25 lbs. A pet fee of $250 non refundable deposit + a $25 per night fee will be incurred during the stay. Pet admission is subject to change based on local government ordinances.

Valet service available for all vehicles. $68 ++ per vehicle for overnight valet.

### Resort Fee

Resort Fee Inclusions:

- $65.00 + 14 % tax
- Complimentary Access to Tierra Santa Healing House wet spa
- Complimentary Access to our Oceanfront Fitness center in Tierra Santa Healing House
- Complimentary Chaise lounges, umbrella and towel service at Faena pool & beach
- Digital newspapers via iPad
- Unlimited local calls
- In-Room and property-wide WiFi
- Welcome beverage
- Access to house car upon first come availability

### Photography

To preserve the privacy and serenity of our shared spaces, professional photography and filming are not allowed in public areas unless expressly approved in advance.

### House Car Program

Hotel guests of Faena Miami Beach are invited to explore the sights of Miami Beach with Faena’s house car, available on a first-come, first-served basis.

### Health & Safety Guidelines

At Faena, the safety and wellbeing of our guests and team members remains our top priority. With your security, safety and health in mind, we have enhanced our health and safety protocols to adhere to the recommendations and guidelines set by the City of Miami Beach.
