# Tierra Santa Healing House

Set across the entire underground level of Faena New York, Tierra Santa Healing House is a 17,000-square-foot sanctuary dedicated to the art of living well. Rooted in ancient South American healing traditions and infused with a spirit of soulful transformation, this wellness destination brings together time-honored rituals with advanced, results-driven technologies.

The space features a full suite of thermal experiences, including a hammam, sauna, infrared sauna, and steam room, alongside curated retail offerings. From immersive bodywork to high-performance skincare, Tierra Santa Healing House offers a journey designed to restore, rebalance, and renew.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Tierra Santa Healing House open to non-hotel guests?

Yes, spa treatments and Thermal Sanctuary access are available to non-hotel guests by reservation. The fitness center is exclusively for hotel guest use. Please contact the spa team at +1 332 286 0170 or email SpaNewYork@faena.com to arrange your visit.

### What is included in the Thermal Sanctuary?

The Thermal Sanctuary includes access to a traditional hammam, sauna, infrared sauna, steam room, and restorative pools, each designed around the transformative power of water, heat, and ritual. Guests of the hotel and non-hotel guests receiving spa treatments enjoy complimentary access to the Thermal Sanctuary by reservation.

### How do I book a treatment at Tierra Santa?

Treatments can be booked online or by calling +1 332 286 0170. Hotel guests may dial 5070 from their in-room phone or select the spa option on their in-room iPad. Advance reservations are strongly recommended. The spa is open daily from 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.

### How large is Tierra Santa Healing House?

Tierra Santa spans 17,000 square feet across the entire underground level of Faena New York, making it one of the largest hotel spa facilities in New York City. This sanctuary to wellness includes a full suite of thermal experiences and curated retail offerings.

### Does Tierra Santa offer couples treatments?

Yes. Each of the treatments on our Shared Rituals menu is thoughtfully designed for two guests, with each experience inspired by South American healing traditions. A dedicated couples treatment room is available for deeper connection and presence. Advance booking is recommended.

### What healing traditions does Tierra Santa draw from?

The treatments and rituals at Tierra Santa are rooted in ancient South American healing traditions, blending time-honored healing with advanced wellness technologies. The spa was conceived by Alan Faena as an oasis of holistic therapies designed to awaken consciousness and restore well-being.
