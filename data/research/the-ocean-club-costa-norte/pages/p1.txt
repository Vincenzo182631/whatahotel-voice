# Welcome to The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte

## The quintessential beach resort in Sosua, Dominican Republic

Every detail of The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte is inspired by the beauty and allure of the ocean. Perched beside a sweeping private beach on Costa Norte, this beach resort in Sosua,...See More Dominican Republic, celebrates its surroundings at every moment. Appreciate the sprawling dimensions of your hotel suite, which is pet-friendly and boasts a private terrace or breezy balcony. Unwind at our hotel near Cabarete, Dominican Republic in one of our three pools with an infinity pool. Discover culinary delicacies at several of our distinct restaurants and bars with views of the sparkling water. Complete your getaway at our Ocean Club Spa by ESPA, a playground for wellness treatments that balance nature and tradition with empowering spa services. At The Ocean Club, transformative experiences enrich and elevate your stay at our luxury resort near Cabarete. Please note that tap water is not drinkable—use bottled water. Only guests present at check-in may access rooms; others must buy a day pass.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte?

The check-in time at The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 10.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte?

On-site parking is available at The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte booking.

What is the closest airport near The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte?

The closest airport near The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte is Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic (POP). POP is located approximately 8.2 KM from the hotel.

Does The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte have an airport shuttle to Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic (POP)?

Yes, The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte has an airport shuttle to Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic (POP).

Does The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte have an airport shuttle to Las Americas International Airport (SDQ)?

Yes, The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte has an airport shuttle to Las Americas International Airport (SDQ).

Does The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Ocean Club, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Norte does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
