# Indulge in New England Flavors

## Marsh Tavern

A favorite destination for Manchester locals and hotel visitors since 1769, the fabled Marsh Tavern offers drinks and lite bites.

**Open Daily**

- Breakfast: 7:30 AM - 10:00 AM
- Lunch: 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM
- Dinner: 5:00 PM - 9:00 PM

## Falcon Bar

Falcon Bar is the perfect spot to mingle with friends & review the day’s exploits. Sit at our richly decorated bar, or enjoy your drinks outside around our fire pit while listening to live music.

**Open Thursday - Saturday**

Dinner- 5:00 to 10:00 Thursday to Saturday

## Dormy Grill

Cocktails with a view. Relax on the deck of our golf clubhouse for a cocktail, hot dog, or light snack accented by spectacular views of the ninth hole and Manchester’s Green Mountains.

**Address**: 108 Union St Fork, Manchester Center, VT 05255

#### Reopening for the season on May 22nd.   11:00 to 6:00 Monday to Sunday
