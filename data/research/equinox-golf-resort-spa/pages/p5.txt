# The Orvis Inn

Discover the timeless allure of Charles Orvis Inn, a 19th-century gem located adjacent to the Main Inn just steps from the resort's diverse offering of activities and amenities. Spacious suites, a cozy library, a fireplace sitting room, a New England-style porch, and a breathtaking setting. Your retreat to history and tranquility awaits.
