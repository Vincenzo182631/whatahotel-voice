# Dormy Grill at The Equinox Golf Clubhouse

Relax after a round at Dormy Grill, where classic clubhouse fare meets scenic mountain views. Enjoy fresh salads, hearty sandwiches, and seasonal favorites—perfect for a laid-back lunch or post-golf unwind. Located just off the 1st hole green, Dormy Grill is the ideal spot to toast a great game or simply soak in the Vermont charm.
