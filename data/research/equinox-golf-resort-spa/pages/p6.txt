## Meadow House

Nestled in a quiet meadow overlooking the Green Mountains and a peaceful reflecting lily pond, Meadow House —the resort’s newest addition—offers a private retreat on the edge of Manchester Village. This free-standing home combines the comforts of a luxury residence with the amenities of a world-class resort—making it the ideal home base in any season. With five beautifully appointed bedrooms, including two king suites, two king rooms, and one twin/twin room, Meadow House accommodates up to 12 guests in refined comfort. Inside, two private parlors, a spacious living room with a fireplace, and a full kitchen with dining area provide inviting spaces to relax and gather, while the expansive deck, screened-in patios, and multiple balconies offer stunning mountain and pond views.

Guests of Meadow House enjoy full access to the resort’s exceptional amenities, including the spa, golf course, tennis courts, heated indoor pool, outdoor hot tub, steam room, sauna, and state-of-the-art fitness center with daily classes. Whether hosting a family getaway, friends’ retreat, or intimate celebration, Meadow House offers the perfect blend of seclusion, sophistication, and scenic Vermont charm.

Total space:3,600 square feet

## Townhomes

Our modern 3-bedroom, 3-bath townhomes feature a spacious bi-level layout with a living room and fireplace, dining area, and full kitchen. Enjoy ample outdoor space, including a private balcony, a patio with mountain views, and separate bedroom key card access at entry points.

The townhome comfortably sleeps 6-8 guests. The lower level includes a Primary King Bedroom with an en-suite bath and private patio overlooking the mountains, plus a separate Queen Bedroom with its own en-suite bath. The upper level offers an open-concept living area, dining space, full kitchen, an additional King Bedroom, a full bathroom, and a private deck.

Total space: 1,077 square feet

## Dormy House

Overlooking the first tee of the golf course, The Dormy House is a spacious, bi-level retreat offering all the comforts of home with the amenities of a world-class resort. This free-standing private home features four beautifully appointed bedrooms—two queen rooms, one king room, and one twin trundle room—along with two full bathrooms and one half bath, including two Jacuzzi tubs. A full kitchen, dining area, and inviting living room with a fireplace create the perfect setting for gathering and relaxation, while the thoughtful layout comfortably accommodates up to nine guests.

Step outside to a private patio with outdoor furniture and a cozy fire pit surrounded by Adirondack chairs—an ideal spot to enjoy the scenic golf course and mountain views. Guests enjoy full access to the resort’s exceptional amenities, including complimentary Wi-Fi, the spa, golf course, tennis courts, heated indoor pool, outdoor hot tub, steam room, sauna, and a state-of-the-art fitness center with daily classes such as yoga, Pilates, and water aerobics. Whether you’re planning a family getaway, a golf weekend, or a peaceful Vermont escape, The Dormy House provides the perfect balance of privacy, comfort, and resort luxury.

Total space: 1,868 square feet
