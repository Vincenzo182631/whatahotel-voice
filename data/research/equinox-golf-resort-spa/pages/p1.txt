### The Equinox Golf Resort & Spa

# A Classic Vermont Getaway

Set in the heart of Manchester, Vermont, The Equinox Golf Resort & Spa is a historic four- season destination that blends adventure with relaxation. Originally established in 1769 as Marsh Tavern, Vermont’s oldest hotel and a gathering place for Ethan Allen’s Green Mountain Boys, the property has grown into one of New England’s most iconic resorts.

Surrounded by the scenic Green Mountains, the resort features elegant accommodations, a championship golf course, a full-service spa, and year-round outdoor activities. From hiking and skiing to shopping and dining in Manchester Village, The Equinox offers the perfect escape for couples, families, and groups where timeless New England charm meets modern comfort.
