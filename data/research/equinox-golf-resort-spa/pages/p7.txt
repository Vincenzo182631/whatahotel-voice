Reflexology is the art and science of touch with the intention of establishing harmony in the recipient’s body. Using pinpoint compression on reflexes, energy pathways are opened and relaxed allowing better flow of blood, nerves, lymph to all parts of the body, including the brain.These sessions promote relaxation, better sleep, and move the mind and body toward better health and wellbeing. Allow time before your session to review your health status. Guests with numerous ear piercings are not candidates for ear reflexology. You will remain fully dressed except for your footwear. _Please note, this is not massage._
