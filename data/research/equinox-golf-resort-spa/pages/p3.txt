# Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your reservation at The Equinox Golf Resort & Spa. If you have additional questions, call  855-501-0029.

- CHECK-IN & CHECK-OUT

Check in is at 4PM & Check-out is at 11AM.

Please note:The Mimimum age to check into The Equinox Resort is 21 Years Old. Guests must be 21 years of age with a valid photo ID. Photo identification must match the name on the reservation. Acceptable forms of identification include license, passport or proof-of-age card.

- DEPOSIT AND CANCELLATION POLICY

### House Default

**Standard Cancellation:** Cancellations made within 48 hours of arrival will forfeit 1 night's room rate plus tax (10%) and a $44 Resort Fee per night. December 23 – January 2: Cancellations must be made at least 14 days prior to arrival.

**Deposit:** The first night's room and tax will be charged at the time of booking.

### Vacation Rates

**Cancellation:** Cancellations must be made at least 14 days prior to arrival. Cancellations within 14 days will be charged 1 night's room and tax. September 4 – October 19 and December 23 – January 2: Cancellations must be made at least 30 days prior to arrival.

**Deposit:** 50% of the total room and tax will be charged at the time of booking to guarantee the reservation. December 23 – January 2: The first night's room and tax will be charged at the time of booking instead.

- HISTORIC PRESERVATION FUND

Please Note our Historic Preservation Fee for 2022 Reservations & Beyond of 4% which is collected to preserve and protect landscapes, buildings, and amenities that have played a meaningful role in The Equinox’s past while carefully integrating continuous improvements for the future. The proceeds from this fund will also have an important economic, environmental, and educational impact on the resort and surrounding communities. We want future generations to be able to experience and discover their own connections to The Equinox.

- COMMUNITY IMPACT FEE

To amplify our community impact, The Equinox Golf Resort & Spa has introduced a 1% Impact Fee on guests’ hotel stays. The fee directly benefits local causes that we are passionate about. This initiative aims to support several local organizations through annual donations.

- SMOKING POLICY

Inns at the Equinox is a non-smoking hotel for all guestrooms, hallways, stairwells and public spaces. A $250 room recovery fee will be charged to the registered guests for smoking in the guestroom.

- TAXES & FEES

9% Occupancy Tax + 1% Local Tax + $44 Per Night Resort Fee + 4% Historic Preservation Fund + 1% Community Impact Fee

- EQUINOX RESORT FEE INCLUSIONS: $44 PER NIGHT

At The Equinox, we want our guests to enjoy a quintessential Vermont getaway experience unlike any other. In order to help you to focus on your Vermont escape, we provide services & offerings that are truly unique to our property. The Equinox also offers complimentary Self-Parking.

Activities:

  - 24 Hour Access to the Equinox Fitness Center.
  - Access to The Spa at the Equinox which includes:
    - sauna and steam room
    - relaxation room
    - indoor heated pool and hot tub
  - Access to resort bikes for in town or trail riding.
  - Outdoor activies i.e.; cornhole, croquet and disc golf.
  - Unlimited use of golf putting green practice area.
  - Outdoor Tennis court usage for daily play.
  - Unlimited access to 850 acres of private hiking trails at the Equinox Preservation Trust.
  - Sleds & Kids Monster Trax Snow Shoes for winter fun.
  - Fire Pits.

Pampering

  - Morning coffee & tea
  - Bottled water in your guest room.

Business & Technology

  - Wireless Internet access in guest rooms, meeting & public spaces
  - 24-Hour Business Center access

- HOUSEKEEPING

Stayover Service: Full Service Stayover Housekeeping Service is available daily for resort guests.

Please contact Guest Services at the Front Desk by dialing ZERO from your guest room phone for additional towels or toiletries which can be picked up at the Front Desk or delivered contact-less outside of your guestroom door. They are open 24 hours.

- RESORT ACTIVITIES - RESERVATIONS REQUIRED

Reservations are required for Lunch & Dinner in Chop House as well all spa services, fitness classes & golf tee times. Requests are handled on a first come, first serve basis. Please contact us at 855-501-0029 to make reservations or get more information. Our goal is to provide all guests with the full Equinox Resort & Spa experience that keeps you coming back year after year.

- PETS ARE WELCOME

Pets are always welcome at The Equinox. We charge a nightly Pet Service Fee and there are certain restrictions. Guide dogs and other assistance dogs are allowed at no fee. Please call 855-501-0029 to inform us in advance of any pet staying with you. Weight limit of a dog shall be 80 lbs or less. Dogs over 80 lbs may be allowed at the hotels discretion. We charge $75 per night for one dog and $100 per night for two dogs (two dog maximum per room). Service dogs for those that are sight impaired are allowed at all times with no charge and no size limit. In accordance with the local health codes, (dogs are not allowed in the restaurants or the pool).

- WHAT HAPPENS IF THERE IS WEATHER DURING MY STAY?

If you added Sensible Weather™ coverage to your The Equinox Resort reservation, you’re protected with a weather guarantee. If weather impacts your stay, you’ll automatically receive reimbursement for qualifying weather — no forms, no claims, and no hassle. It’s the easiest way to enjoy weather protection for your vacation and book your trip with confidence.
