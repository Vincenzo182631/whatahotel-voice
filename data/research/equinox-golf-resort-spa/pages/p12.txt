# The Spa at The Equinox

### Relax and Refresh In the heart of the Manchester, VT

We are delighted to welcome you to The Spa at Equinox, a mountain oasis where you are invited to relax and recover after a day of hiking, biking, skiing, riding, or shopping. Our facility features separate men’s and women’s locker rooms with a sauna and steam room, a 24-hour fitness and yoga studio, a 25-yard indoor lap pool, and a beautiful outdoor whirlpool. Our highly skilled therapists and estheticians will focus on your unique body and skin needs to deliver an experience that is truly reminiscent of the surrounding landscape: majestic, serene, and sure to leave you feeling refreshed inside and out.

### Fitness Club Classes

Our 24-hour fitness center features state-of-the-art cardio machines, free weights, and a variety of body-sculpting equipment. After your workout, relax in our movement studio or soak in the outdoor hot tub.

For those who prefer guided sessions, we offer a wide range of group classes for guests 16 and older. From Hatha Yoga and Yoga Flow to Restorative Yoga and Core Yoga Flow, our yoga offerings help you stretch, strengthen, and connect with your body. Pilates classes—including Mat Pilates, Pilates on the Ball, and Pilates with Props—build core strength, stability, and flexibility.

Take your workout into the water with our aquatics classes, like Deep Water Aerobics, Aqua Boot Camp, Aqua Zumba, and Floating Sound Bath, all designed to provide low-impact, full-body conditioning. Dance and cardio enthusiasts can join Zumba or Tap4Fun, while outdoor lovers can enjoy Yoga Hikes, Nature Hikes, and other invigorating experiences.

Additional classes support joint health, mobility, and functional strength, including BARRE and ZENGA, blending Pilates, yoga, and mindful movement for total-body wellness. Classes are $10 for hotel guests and $20 for drop-ins.
