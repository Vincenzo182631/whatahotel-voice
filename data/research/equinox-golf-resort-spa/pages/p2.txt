# Vermont Accommodations

Discover a diverse range of accommodations at Equinox Resort. Experience timeless elegance in the Main Inn, rustic charm at the Orvis Inn, and the comforts of home in our Vacation Rentals. Whatever your preference, each option promises an enchanting escape amidst Vermont's natural beauty.

## Main Inn

Step into a world of refined sophistication at the heart of Equinox Resort. Graced with timeless elegance, this historic building offers luxurious accommodations and modern comforts. From its grand architecture to its warm and welcoming ambiance, the Main Inn embodies the essence of a quintessential New England escape, all while providing convenient access to the resort's world-class amenities and breathtaking natural surroundings.

## Orvis Inn

Escape to the enchanting Orvis Inn at Equinox Resort—a rustic Vermont retreat. Cozy rooms and suites with wood accents offer modern comforts, while thrilling outdoor activities like fly-fishing and hiking beckon. Share tales by the fireplace and create cherished memories while embracing the essence of Vermont's beauty and adventure.

## Vacation Rentals

Embrace the comforts of home in our beautifully appointed residences, ranging from the charming Meadow House to luxurious townhomes. Nestled amidst Vermont's natural beauty, each rental offers ample space, modern amenities, and breathtaking views. Whether you're seeking a romantic escape or a family adventure, these well-equipped rentals provide the perfect retreat to savor the tranquility of nature while enjoying access to the resort's world-class amenities.
