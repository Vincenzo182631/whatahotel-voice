### Manchester, VT’s Premier Cocktail Lounge & Bar

# Falcon Bar

Located just off the main lobby, Falcon Bar is Manchester, VT’s go-to spot for craft cocktails, live music, and lively conversation. Relax at our richly appointed bar or enjoy drinks outdoors around the fire pit while soaking in local tunes. Whether mingling with friends or unwinding after a day of adventure, Falcon Bar offers the perfect mix of flavor, ambiance, and Vermont hospitality.
