# Welcome to The Sky Residences at W Aspen

## Boutique hotel in Aspen, CO - The Sky Residences at W Aspen

Located at the base of the world-renowned Aspen Mountain, The Sky Residences at W Aspen is the first and only W Hotels Alpine Escape in North America. This luxury Aspen Mountain hotel offers mountainside access to coveted slopes and stunning panoramic views, paired with bold architecture and modern design. Guests seeking a premium hotel in Aspen, CO will enjoy year-round adventure, from skiing in winter...See More to paragliding, rock climbing, and rafting in summer. Our rooms and suites provide an elevated stay with the same refined feel, just steps from the mountain. For travelers looking for an exceptional hotel, the property sits in the heart of it all, just moments from high-end shopping, museums, galleries, and music venues. As a welcoming pet-friendly hotel in Aspen, it also ensures a comfortable stay for you and your furry companions, delivering a truly immersive Aspen experience.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Sky Residences at W Aspen?

The check-in time at The Sky Residences at W Aspen is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Sky Residences at W Aspen allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Sky Residences at W Aspen. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The Sky Residences at W Aspen?

On-site valet parking is available at The Sky Residences at W Aspen. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Sky Residences at W Aspen have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Sky Residences at W Aspen has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Sky Residences at W Aspen booking.

What is the closest airport near The Sky Residences at W Aspen?

The closest airport near The Sky Residences at W Aspen is Aspen-Pitkin County Airport (ASE). ASE is located approximately 4.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Sky Residences at W Aspen have an airport shuttle to Aspen-Pitkin County Airport (ASE)?

No, The Sky Residences at W Aspen does not have an airport shuttle to Aspen-Pitkin County Airport.

Does The Sky Residences at W Aspen have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Sky Residences at W Aspen has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
