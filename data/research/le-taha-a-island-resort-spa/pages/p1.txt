# Your Polynesian sanctuary

Experience the essence of French Polynesia in beautifully styled sanctuaries of peace and relaxation. Our 59 suites and villas are specially designed so guests feel completely at one with the tropical setting, with each space crafted using natural materials and offering direct access to the lagoon or the beach.

Our Overwater Suites offer a romantic setting above the lagoon's rippling waters; our Pool Beach Villas promise remarkable privacy amid lush nature; and our two Royal Villas represent the ultimate Polynesian escape. Whichever you choose, every detail invites you to embrace the gentle rhythm of island life.

In all our guest rooms, you'll find original artworks by Polynesian artists, with some pieces specially commissioned. Louis Devienne created all the artworks for the Overwater Suites and Beach Villas, while Haumani Stanley and Évrard Chaussoy created exclusive artworks for the two Royal Villas, reflecting the hotel’s Polynesian navigation theme.
