# Experience Polynesia with all your senses

Located on the Tautau motu, our hotel offers breathtaking views of the lush shores of Taha’a and the iconic silhouette of Bora Bora.

Every detail is designed to celebrate beauty, the art of living, and harmony with the lush nature of this preserved island in the South Pacific. As the first Relais & Châteaux establishment in French Polynesia, we honor the local culture, embodying authenticity, tranquility, and sophistication for a unique escape.

Love story in paradise

### Culture & Polynesian art

Cultural journey

Our Polynesian culture, rich and timeless, is the soul of our establishment. Here, discover a living art gallery, where each space is imbued with local artworks, as well as traditional activities and performances that celebrate our heritage.

### Activities

Taha'a in your own rhythm

Taha’a, known as the Vanilla Island, offers a wide range of activities combining culture, nature, and relaxation. Explore the pristine motus, uncover the secrets of pearl farming, and dive into the turquoise waters of the coral garden. We organize the experience that reflects who you are.

Get inspired

### Coral Garden

Pristine waters

You will get up close and personal with the teeming, colorful sea life. The most adventurous guests will try drift snorkeling and let the incoming tide carry them from the small reef at the edge of the islet right to the heart of the coral garden.

Discover More

Tāvai Spa

### A SENSORY JOURNEY

Facing our inner lagoon, where water lilies and lotus flowers float, the Tāvai Spa invites you to a moment of pure serenity. In this calm and natural setting, Polynesian massage reveals itself as an art de vivre, considered a crucial part of islanders' physical, psychological and spiritual harmony.

### LE BORA BORA

The Fiery

Beneath the gaze of Mount Otemanu, Le Bora Bora embodies what Polynesia holds most deeply and most vividly. Architecture and nature, tradition and modernity, luxury and authenticity: all coexist with a rare and effortless grace.

### LE NUKU HIVA

The Mystic

Far from the ordinary, Le Nuku Hiva invites the curious traveller to discover a mystical island of wild horses, ancient forests, and towering waterfalls, returning each evening to a hillside lodge of warmth and panoramic bay views.

### LE TIKEHAU

The Peaceful

Nestled on a private motu within a wild coconut grove, Le Tikehau opens onto a turquoise lagoon ringed by coral, where pink sand shores, infinite blues, and the silent sway of coconut palms offer the rare gift of disconnecting from the world and returning to the essential.

### VAHINE PRIVATE ISLAND

The Exclusive

On a private motu in the lagoon of Taha'a, Vahine Private Island offers nine bungalows and a royal villa cradled among a lush coconut grove, facing the open horizons of Bora Bora, Raiatea and Huahine. A confidential address where the unhurried rhythm of island life is preserved in its purest form.
