### An over rated five star hotel

20 days ago

This InterContinental falls well below the standard of modern international properties I have experienced in the US and Asia.

A large block of guest rooms faces a noisy street, and the rooftop pool is unreasonably small for a brand of this standing. Compounding the noise issue, the ground floor lacks a dedicated lounge for conference delegates. Conference gatherings spill out into shared public areas, leaving guests with virtually no quiet space to relax.

Staff training is clearly inadequate. Many team members come across as unfriendly and unengaged. Housekeeping staff are ineffective and do not acknowledge guests in hallways. At check-in, an unsmiling agent made an early-arriving guest wait more than three hours, as rooms were still unprepared even by noon. Ground-floor bar and restaurant staff were similarly inattentive, ignoring guests to chat in groups with kitchen staff. This dismissive attitude seems common among hospitality workers in Barcelona, with many employees showing little care for visitors.

My own stay also had service failures. The bed and pillows were overly soft. I requested a firm pillow in the morning, but it wasn't delivered after my room was serviced that afternoon. I had to contact housekeeping a second time and received no apology, only ridiculous excuses. To make matters worse, they didn't replace my towels during cleaning, forcing yet another call for fresh towels.

Overall, anyone expecting the consistent quality of InterContinental hotels in the US or Asia will be deeply disappointed.

ConsSleep Quality, Amenities

No, I do not recommend this hotel.
