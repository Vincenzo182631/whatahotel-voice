# InterContinental BarcelonaDestination

From renowned architectural and cultural landmarks to flavorful culinary traditions and a wide range of entertainment, InterContinental Barcelona is conveniently located in the heart of one of Europe’s most exciting and vibrant cities.

### Highlights of the Montjuïc District

Poble Espanyol: An open-air museum featuring Spanish architecture and cultural events.
MNAC: A museum showcasing paintings, sculptures, and decorative art.
Montjuïc Park: A spacious urban park with gardens, cultural sites, and sports facilities.

### Barcelona City

Barcelona is a beautiful city, known for its rich history, vibrant culture, and stunning architecture. From the Sagrada Familia to its beaches, there's so much to explore. While staying at InterContinental® Barcelona, our Concierge is ready to offer personalized tips to help you experience the best of the city.

## Things to do

### Casa Batlló

Passeig de Gracia, 43, Barcelona, Barcelona 08007

The Casa Batllo, a major landmark of the Modernista Style Barcelona, is much more than a Gaudi building and invites you discover a legend of the architecture and world design of all time

### Sagrada Familia

Mallorca Street, 401, Barcelona, Barcelona 08013

The Sagrada Familia is a large Roman Catholic church in Barcelona, designed by Catalan architect Antonio Gaudi. Construction of Sagrada Familia had commenced in 1882. Although incomplete, the church is a UNESCO World Heritage.

### Santa Maria del Mar

Plaça Santa Maria, 1, Barcelona, Barcelona 08003

The Basilica of Santa Maria del Mar in Barcelona, also known as the "cathedral of La Ribera", was designed by Berenguer de Montagut and is one of the most perfect examples of Gothic Style architecture.

### FCB Museum

Avenue Aristides Maillol, Barcelona, Barcelona 08028

The FCB Museum is currently considered the most modern and most visited football museum in the world. You will find the most relevant trophies and objects related to our club history,

### Pueblo Español

Avenue Francesc Ferrer i Guarida, 13, Barcelona, Barcelona 08038

Is an open-air architecural museum, constructed in 1929 and located on the mountain of Montjuïc, Barcelona,
Josep Puig i Cadafalch decided to creat a town containing architecture, style and culture of various locations from around Spain.

### National Museum Art of Catalonia (MNAC)

Palau Nacional, Parc de Montjuïc, Barcelona, Barcelona 08038

The Mnac embraces all the arts (sculpture, painting, objects d´art, drawing, engraving posters, photography and coinage) and has the tasks of explaining the general history of Catalan art from the Romanesque period to the mind-twentieth century.
