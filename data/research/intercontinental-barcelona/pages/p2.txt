# InterContinental BarcelonaRooms & suites

Classic Rooms offer more than just a place to rest — they’re a refined retreat where elegance meets comfort. Located on the hotel’s lower floors, these rooms are among the most spacious in the city, offering a serene escape designed for both productivity and relaxation.

With generous layouts, plush twin beds or a king-size bed, and smart technology seamlessly integrated into the design, each Classic Room surrounds you with quiet sophistication. The soothing color palette, soft lighting, and elegant finishes create an atmosphere of calm and exclusivity.

Every detail is carefully curated to ensure a sense of effortless luxury, from the high-thread-count linens to the contemporary furnishings. Whether you're starting a busy day or winding down after exploring the city, the Classic Room offers a space to pause, recharge, and indulge — a true sanctuary at the heart of the hotel.

Enhancing your stay, each room is thoughtfully equipped with climate-controlled air conditioning, a minibar, and an espresso maker for your refreshment. Unwind in the spacious bathroom featuring a separate bath, a walking rain shower, and a bidet, complemented by plush robes and slippers. Stay entertained with a large Smart TV, and enjoy 24-hour room service along with exclusive benefits through IHG One Rewards — all within a non-smoking environment designed for your utmost comfort.

### Presidential Suite: The Ultimate Luxury Escape

The Presidential Suite at InterContinental® Barcelona is the largest and most luxurious room, featuring a spacious living-dining area with a kitchen, two bedrooms, and refined décor. The main bedroom has a king-size bed and walk-in closet, while the second offers twin beds. Located on the 5th floor for a serene, luxury experience.
