### QUIRAT, our Michelin-starred restaurant

Discover haute cuisine at QUIRAT, a Michelin-starred and Sol Repsol restaurant at InterContinental Barcelona. Chef Víctor Torres celebrates seasonal and sustainable ingredients in elegant creations, paired with selected wines for a unique culinary journey.

Learn more

### Exclusive Sunday Brunch

Enjoy an exclusive brunch where haute cuisine blends with a delicious buffet and à la carte options. Let yourself be surrounded by the magic of live music while savoring unique flavors in an elegant atmosphere full of inspiration for an unforgettable day.

Learn more

### Meet our chef - Victor Torres

Víctor Torres, the youngest Spanish chef to receive a MICHELIN star, leads with passion and creativity at our restaurant QUIRAT. Backed by a team of experts, his goal is to capture the essence of each ingredient, offering signature cuisine that celebrates gastronomic excellence.

### Arrel

At Arrel, we invite you to experience a journey that celebrates the earth through its food. Each dish connects with nature and awakens your senses. We offer a lunch buffet, an executive menu during the week, and a varied dinner menu, highlighting local products according to the season.

Learn more

### Gebre, our specialty cocktail bar

Immerse yourself in a night of luxury at Gebre, where our bartenders craft exceptional drinks inspired by ancestral methods and the sue temporis concept, honoring the passage of time. Discover over 250 whiskey references and indulge in a refined atmosphere daily from 5 PM to 1 AM.

Learn more
