### QUIRAT, our Michelin-starred restaurant

Discover haute cuisine at QUIRAT, a Michelin-starred and Sol Repsol restaurant at InterContinental Barcelona. Chef Víctor Torres celebrates seasonal and sustainable ingredients in elegant creations, paired with selected wines for a unique culinary journey.

Learn more

### Highlights of the Montjuïc District

Poble Espanyol: An open-air museum featuring Spanish architecture and cultural events.
MNAC: A museum showcasing paintings, sculptures, and decorative art.
Montjuïc Park: A spacious urban park with gardens, cultural sites, and sports facilities.

### Discover InterContinental Spa

InterContinental Barcelona Spa is a restful haven that places special attention on beauty, the body, and the mind to find an inner balance. Our specialised therapists apply techniques borrowed from India and the East, where knowledge of mind and body has been cultivated for thousands of years.

Learn more

### Exclusive Sunday Brunch

Enjoy an exclusive brunch where haute cuisine blends with a delicious buffet and à la carte options. Let yourself be surrounded by the magic of live music while savoring unique flavors in an elegant atmosphere full of inspiration for an unforgettable day.

Learn more

### Our Green Key Certification

We are proud to announce that InterContinental® Barcelona has received the Green Key certification, an international award recognizing excellence in environmental responsibility within the tourism sector. This certification underscores our strong commitment to sustainability and eco-friendly practices.

## FAQs for InterContinental Barcelona

Does InterContinental Barcelona have a pool?

InterContinental Barcelona has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Our swimming pool will be closed from mid-February to the end of March. Our 173 Rooftop Terrace, is a unique space to marvel at the stunning panoramic views of the city while enjoying a bespoke cocktail and relaxing to the magical sound of the music.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Barcelona?

At InterContinental Barcelona, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 40 EUR .

Parking charges 40 euros per day.
Located under the hotel with direct access to the hotel lobby, it is accessible 24 hours a day.
Guests on parking-inclusive packages need to register their details at reception.

Parking fee: 40 EUR per day

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Barcelona?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Barcelona.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Barcelona for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Barcelona have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Barcelona has 3 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Barcelona?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Barcelona?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Barcelona.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Barcelona?

Check-in at InterContinental Barcelona is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Barcelona offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Barcelona offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Barcelona from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Barcelona is conveniently located near El Prat Barcelona International Airport (BCN), approximately 7.46 miles away (12 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Barcelona?

InterContinental Barcelona is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Casa Batlló - 2.3 miles away (3.7 kilometers)
- Sagrada Familia - 3.29 miles away (5.3 kilometers)
- Santa Maria del Mar - 2.55 miles away (4.1 kilometers)
- FCB Museum - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- Pueblo Español - 0.53 miles away (0.85 kilometers)
- National Museum Art of Catalonia (MNAC) - 0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Barcelona puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
