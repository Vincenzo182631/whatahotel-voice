You'll have a bird's-eye view of the runway comings and goings at the Fairmont Vancouver Airport, whose halls can be accessed directly through the airport's departures terminal. Though its spacious, muted gray rooms have floor-to-ceiling windows that look out on the tarmac, you'll never hear a plane take off or land, thanks to the excellent soundproofing. One feature we're upvoting here are the 'Quiet Zone' floors: these floors are designated silent during the day to allow tired travelers to rest. Signature restaurant Globe@YVR has towering stone fireplaces and serves West Coast specialties made in the show kitchen, though its the Carry on Cuisine program, which lets travelers take meals like smoked British Columbian salmon aboard their aircraft, that serves to impress. Among the other perks: A BMW cruise bike to explore the city between layovers; the Jetside Bar, where you can drink in views of the runway and North Shore mountains, along with your Chardonnay; and a citrusy pink grapefruit jet-lag recovery treatment at the in-house spa. You may just forget you're at the airport, at all—thank goodness for that.
