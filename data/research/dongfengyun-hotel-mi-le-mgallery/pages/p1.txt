## Description

DongFengYun Hotel Mi'Le - MGallery Collection

Boutique hotels with personality for a remarkable travel experience

This hotel is situated in Dongfengyun Art Village, a quaint hamlet comprised of vineyards, lavender fields, and post-modern architecture. The hotel is heavily the MGallery brand's unique artistic and cultural heritage, and the care it shows for nature and local artisans. Ingenious designs, brilliant and diverse dining experiences, and comprehensive state-of-the-art facilities all serve to enhance each guest's stay. Book direct at all.accor.com.

Winner of the Special Prize Exterior of Prix Versailles with its depiction of the Magic Castle in red brick.

Kunming Changshui International Airport is about 150 km from the hotel. Two travel options:

• By car/taxi: approx. 120‑min drive

• By high‑speed train: 40‑min ride from Kunming South Railway Station to Mile Station, then a 10‑min taxi to the hotel.

> 欢迎光临弥勒东风韵美憬阁酒店。作为总经理，我热情邀请您前来体验丰富的"红土"文化，享受 M moment，踏上一段难忘的艺术之旅。

Jack Guo, Hotel Management
