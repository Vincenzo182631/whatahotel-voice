# Map, Parking + Transportation

## Parking + Transportation

Located in the heart of Yaletown, one of the city’s most central and vibrant neighbourhoods, OPUS Vancouver is close to many of the city’s major attractions.

Being at the centre of it all means street parking can be difficult. We offer 24/7, secured valet service in the private garage under the hotel for an additional fee. Simply pull up to the front of the hotel, and our door staff will take care of your vehicle. Complimentary valet is also available with select special offers.

But you don’t need a car to get around town. We’re mere steps from the Yaletown-Roundhouse Canada Line with direct service to Vancouver International Airport, our Bike Fleet is at your disposal, and we’re a short walk away from the False Creek Water Taxis.
