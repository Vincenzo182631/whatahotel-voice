# Yaletown Boutique Hotel Experience

## Pet Friendly

Dogs are always welcome at OPUS Vancouver. In fact, we tend to treat our four-legged guests even better than our two-legged ones!

Pampered pooches can expect:

- Complimentary in-room “pup tents,” bowls, and locally sourced treats
- Fenced, off-leash dog park just 2 blocks away in Emery Barnes Park
- Open, off-leash dog park just 10 minutes away along the scenic Seawall

\*Pet fee of $50 (per stay) will be applied to guest room.
