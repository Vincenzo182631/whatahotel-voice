Set in the heart of Vancouver’s cosmopolitan Yaletown neighborhood—OPUS Vancouver is a boutique lifestyle hotel offering unparalleled luxury experiences.

Choose from 96 spacious rooms and suites, with vibrant colour palettes and sophisticated amenities, and revel in on-site dining, with house-made pizza and Italian fare at Capo and delectable cocktails at The Spritz.

Whether in town for business or leisure, you’ll discover intuitive service and elevated hotel features that make your time in Vancouver truly unforgettable.

## Rooms

Effortlessly stylish and always fresh, OPUS Vancouver defines the city’s boutique hotel experience, blending contemporary design with intuitive service. Our 96 guestrooms and suites are inspired by five lifestyle décor themes with vibrant colours, pop-deco art, and spa bathrooms.
