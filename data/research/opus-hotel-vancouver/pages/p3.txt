## Capo Restaurant

Steps from your room (and just off our lobby), CAPO is a neighbourhood Italian gem serving up comforting classics with a modern twist. Think crave-worthy pizzas, fresh pastas and sips worth lingering over - whether you're grabbing a quick bite or settling in for the evening. Laid-back yet refined, CAPO is where locals and guests come to eat, drink, and keep the good times rolling.

_Open 7 days per week 8am till late \| Happy Hour daily from 3pm - 5:30pm_

## The Spritz

The perfect complement to CAPO's straightforward pizzeria energy, The Spritz is a cozy, sophisticated cocktail bar nestled just off our lobby. The Spritz offers the classics, as well as original libations created by our world-class bartenders in homage to the romance and craftsmanship of Italy’s aperitivo culture.

_Open 7 days per week 11am till late \| Happy Hour daily from 3pm - 5:30pm_

## Room Service

Appetites don’t bow down to arbitrary concepts like time zones. OPUS Vancouver offers Room Service from our in-house restaurant, Capo. Partial to a particular libation? You may personalize your in-room bar with our BYOB (Build Your Own Bar) program. Just make a request with our Guest Service team, in advance or at any time during your stay.

_Available daily from:_

_Sunday - Thursday: 8:00 am - 9:30pm_

_Friday - Saturday: 8:00am - 10:30pm_

_Sidebar: Evidence strongly suggests room service arrived in Vancouver in the 1930s, thanks to special requests made by a certain Miss Katharine Hepburn_. _The more you know…_
