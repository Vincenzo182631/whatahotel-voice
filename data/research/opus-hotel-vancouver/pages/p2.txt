# Vancouver Boutique Hotel Rooms

- Rooms
- Suites

Each of our 96 rooms exemplifies our boutique luxury feel, with bright splashes of colour, luxe spa bathrooms with heated floors and deep soaking tubs, high-tech amenities, and pop art adorning the walls. You’ll find each room has a personality all its own, just like vibrant Yaletown; some additional features include step-out balconies, fireplaces, and stunning views of the city or garden courtyard.

- Spa bathroom with heated floors
- Nespresso coffee experience
- Philips 55” Smart TVs with Chromecast
- Frette® bathrobes
- Herman Miller ergonomic chairs
- Luxury amenities by TALLU & Co
- JBL Bluetooth speakers

Did we mention our Dyson Supersonic™ hair dryers in all Deluxe and Balcony Studio guestrooms? Go on, we know you want to try one.
