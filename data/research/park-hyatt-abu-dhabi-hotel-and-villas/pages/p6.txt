Getting around

Shuttle Service

We offer daily shuttle service to Yas Island, covering Yas Waterworld, Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World and SeaWorld. The bus departs from the hotel at 10:40 a.m., 12:25 p.m. and 2:30 p.m.
Departures from Yas Island operates on a first-come, first-served basis as follows: Ferrari World: 5 p.m., 6:50 p.m., 8:20 p.m.
Yas Waterworld: 5:10 p.m., 7 p.m., 8:50 p.m.
SeaWorld: 5:20 p.m., 7:10 p.m., 9 p.m.
