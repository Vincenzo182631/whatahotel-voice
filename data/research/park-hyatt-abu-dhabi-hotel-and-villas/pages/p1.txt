## Experience Unmatched Luxury at Park Hyatt Abu Dhabi Hotel in Saadiyat Island

Park Hyatt Abu Dhabi presents the best of both worlds, an uncommon journey from lounging around the most pristine shores of Saadiyat to adventures on the sun-kissed sands of the Arabian desert.

## Discover Park Hyatt Abu Dhabi

Fitness Centre

Overlooking the inviting outdoor pool and bathed in natural light, our fitness centre offers more than just a space to exercise—it’s a sanctuary for wellness and rejuvenation. Invigorate your senses with a swim in the 21-metre lap pool, enjoy the thrill of a game on our flood-lit tennis and padel courts, or elevate your fitness journey in our high-end gym. Outfitted with the latest equipment from Life Fitness and Technogym, every workout ensures you’ll leave feeling revitalised and balanced.

Open daily, 6 a.m.–10 p.m.

Camp Hyatt

Park Hyatt Abu Dhabi Hotel and Villas houses the popular Camp Hyatt, an exclusive facility for younger guests at the resort. Several fun and interactive indoor and outdoor recreational activities ensure children have an unforgettable stay at the resort. Camp Hyatt enables parents to take a break to enjoy the resort’s many offerings while feeling secure that their children are safe with our caring, skilled associates.

Open daily, 9:30 a.m.–6:30 p.m.

Pools

Set against the breathtaking backdrop of Saadiyat Beach and the Arabian Sea, this luxury hotel offers a sanctuary of refined elegance across five swimming pools. Surrounded by towering palm trees and lush gardens, our pools cater to every desire. Take a serene dip in the exclusive adults-only pool, make cherished memories in the family pool or delight in the charm of our child-friendly pool—all while basking in an atmosphere of timeless luxury and natural beauty.

Open daily, 6 a.m.–7 p.m.

Golf

Embark on a thrilling golfing journey at Saadiyat Beach Golf Course. Elevate your stay with a round of golf on this spectacular 18-hole championship course, designed by the legendary Gary Player. Set against the breathtaking backdrop of Saadiyat Island’s pristine beach, each hole offers a perfect blend of challenge and natural beauty, delivering an exhilarating experience for golfers of all levels.

Atarmia Spa

Situated in the heart of Saadiyat Island, Atarmia Spa unfolds as a serene oasis of unparalleled luxury. Immerse yourself in an exquisite selection of organic treatments, each designed to evoke deep relaxation and rejuvenation. Every detail is meticulously crafted to elevate your experience, ensuring each moment becomes a cherished memory.

Open daily, 11 a.m.–10 p.m.
