## Beach House

The Beach House restaurant offers an exceptional dining experience featuring authentic Italian cuisine influenced by recipes from the southern Italian coast. The distinctive gastronomic menu showcases a wide array of daily fresh seafood, served in a family-style setting and complemented by a curated selection of wines and cocktails. Nestled away from the bustling city, this restaurant provides an idyllic setting for both lunch and dinner, with serene views overlooking the pristine Saadiyat Beach.
