### Beautiful, great service, wonderful food, but very overwhelming perfumy

A beautiful hotel conveniently located near museums and a quick drive to downtown Abu Dhabi. Everything was lovely - the staff attentive, the food delish. And the spa was amazing. I had two complaints - one minor - room location was as far from the lobby as possible and felt like the basement. Room was nice, but journey to get there was long and there was no view. Would not recommend for anyone with mobility issues. To be fair, I did not ask to be moved or see if there was another option - I was jet lagged and just wanted to settle in. Second, and more importantly, the hotel has a signature scent that set off some sort of major allergy. If you are at all smell sensitive, I would not recommend this stay. It was such a shame because it meant that I could not enjoy the public areas without my nose running and a sneeze attack. But if you can handle that, and don't mind it, I truly recommend the space. It was beautiful.

### Unforgettable memories collected in this hotel

I would like to express my sincere appreciation to every member of the hotel team, from the security staff to the receptionists, restaurant team, chefs, managers, and everyone else who contributed to making our stay so special.
We had originally booked a standard room, but since we were staying for eight days, the receptionist kindly and generously offered us one of the best rooms in the hotel, with a beautiful sea view and a central location, allowing us to enjoy the very best experience.
Everything was thoughtfully designed with guests’ comfort in mind — convenient, fast and effortless. The room was equipped with every little detail you could possibly need, and all the amenities and personal care products were of excellent quality. From the moment you enter, you are welcomed with lovely little surprises. Even providing a beach bag in the room was such a thoughtful and impressive gesture. Everything was truly at the highest level.
The restaurant where we had our meals deserves special mention as well. The service was at a true high-end restaurant level rather than the usual buffet-style experience. The managers and waitstaff were incredibly warm, attentive and professional. And the food was simply amazing — delicious, fresh and extremely diverse. It was honestly impossible not to find something that suited your taste.
The pool and beach teams also made a great impression. They were constantly providing chilled water, fresh towels and assistance whenever needed, always making sure everything was comfortable for us. The beach itself was beautifully maintained and clean, and the sea was absolutely wonderful.
I would also like to highlight the concierge service. The concierge helped me quickly and efficiently find the medication I needed online from local pharmacies and responded incredibly fast and accurately. This level of care and responsiveness truly made a difference.
Overall, I have already recommended this hotel to people close to me who are planning to visit Abu Dhabi, and I will definitely be coming back here myself.
Thank you to the entire team for making our stay so memorable!
