# The Silo Hotel Spa

Book A Treatment Buy Gift Vouchers

Wellness

## A sanctuary in the city

An oasis of peace and relaxation offering a regenerative journey towards enhanced wellbeing. Benefit from the latest therapies, spa techniques, and signature treatments which can be personalised to meet your specific wellness requirements.

The Silo Hotel Spa also has a double-treatment room, fitness centre, sauna and a dedicated manicure and pedicure area. Our expert therapists use top local and international products including Ling and Black Pearl.

Spa Menu Spa Packages

## Everything you need to know

Our expert therapists ensure that you receive the perfect treatment for your individual skin type and requirements through an interactive consultation process.

The Silo Hotel Spa is open to non-resident guests, subject to availability. To learn more, please email spa@thesilohotel.com or phone +27 (0) 21 670 0510
