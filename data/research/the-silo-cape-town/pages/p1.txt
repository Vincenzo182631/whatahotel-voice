Why stay at The Silo Hotel in Cape Town?

The Silo Hotel offers an experience available nowhere else in Cape Town — a five-star sanctuary directly above the Zeitz Museum of Contemporary Art Africa, within the V&A Waterfront. Each suite features the hotel’s distinctive pillowed-glass windows, framing panoramic views of Table Mountain, the Atlantic Ocean and the city skyline. World-class dining, a rooftop pool and a spa, and curated access to Cape Town’s vibrant art and cultural scene make it the defining choice for discerning travellers. Whether visiting for leisure or business, it remains one of the most celebrated 5-star hotels in Cape Town.

Where is The Silo Hotel in Cape Town?

The Silo Hotel is located within the V&A Waterfront, Cape Town’s most celebrated waterfront precinct. Positioned within the converted grain silo above the Zeitz Museum of Contemporary Art Africa (MOCAA), it sits at the intersection of the city’s historic harbour and its contemporary cultural landscape. Table Mountain, the Cape Town city centre and the Atlantic Seaboard are all within easy reach. For guests seeking a luxury hotel in Cape Town with an unrivalled address, few properties come close.

How many rooms are in The Silo Hotel in Cape Town?

The Silo Hotel in Cape Town has 28 rooms and suites across six floors, each designed to honour the building’s extraordinary architectural heritage. The accommodation ranges from Luxury Suites to the spectacular two-bedroom Royal Suite, with every room featuring the hotel’s signature pillowed-glass windows and bespoke furnishings. Given its intimate scale, The Silo Hotel delivers a level of personalised service rarely found in Cape Town hotels of this calibre — making early reservation advisable, particularly during peak season.
