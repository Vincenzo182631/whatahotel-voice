# Explore Cape Town

Location

## Dramatic mountains and sandy beaches

Lying in the shadow of one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World, **Table Mountain**, Cape Town offers spectacular scenery, diverse cultures, great food and wine and a thriving arts and design scene.

Hike up **Lion’s Head**, or stroll around the city centre and take in the buzz and bustle of the street markets. Explore the **Victoria & Alfred Waterfront** – one of the trendiest spots to visit with boutique shops, great restaurants and the Two Oceans Aquarium. The **Zeitz Museum of Contemporary Art Africa** (MOCAA) houses Africa’s largest contemporary art collection and can be found directly beneath The Silo Hotel.

**Kirstenbosch Botanical Gardens** – discover the unique fynbos plant life of the Cape, with over 7,000 species growing at Kirstenbosch, many of which are rare and threatened.

**Table Mountain** – enjoy a scenic hike or take the cable car to the top and take in the dramatic views.

**Lion’s Head** – it’s a moderate to strenuous 45-minute hike to the top, and the views are certainly worth the effort.

**Robben Island** – visit the cell where Nobel Peace Prize Winner and the first democratically elected President, Nelson Mandela, was imprisoned for decades.
