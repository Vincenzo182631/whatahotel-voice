# Questions about The Silo Hotel

All of your frequently asked questions answered.

When did The Silo Hotel open?

The Silo Hotel opened on 1 March 2017 after 3 years of construction.

What is Zeitz MOCAA?

MOCAA stands for Museum of Contemporary Art Africa. Zeitz MOCAA is a contemporary art museum that can be compared to the Tate Modern in London, MoMA in New York, and Museo Nacional del Prado in Madrid.

Zeitz MOCAA is a not-for-profit partnership between the Victoria & Alfred Waterfront in Cape Town and Jochen Zeitz, whose collection forms part of the museum’s founding collection.

What are the opening times for Zeitz MOCAA?

Everyday (including public holidays):

10h00 – 85h00

Last entry: 17h30 pm

First Friday of the month:

Extended opening hours, 10h00 – 21h00

Last entry: 20h30 pm

How much does it cost to visit Zeitz MOCAA?

Single Day Entry: ZAR 190

Annual Membership: ZAR 270

Other membership options are available.

Free admissions to all under 18’s. Free admissions for all African citizens between 10h00 & 13h00 every Wednesday. Half price admissions for all on ‘Late Night Fridays’.

What is the relationship between The Silo Hotel and the Zeitz MOCAA?

The relationship is one of proximity only. The hotel and the museum are located within the historic grain silo building that was built in 1924. The Silo Hotel is in the grain elevator portion of the grain silo building occupying 6 floors above Zeitz MOCAA.

The hotel and the museum nevertheless enjoy a close relationship and guided tours of the museum can be arranged with the hotel concierge.

Who is the architect of the grain silo building in Cape Town?

The exterior of the building and the inside of the museum has been designed by Thomas Heatherwick, Founder and Principal of the London-based **Heatherwick Studio**. Heatherwick Studio was tasked with the transformation of the elevator house and the adjacent storage annex of 6 rows of 7 silos, while carefully retaining its cultural significance and creating a building that would be synonymous with the Cape Town skyline.

What is the V&A Waterfront?

The **V&A Waterfront** – known to everyone as the V&A – is the best place to stay in Cape Town and Africa’s most popular tourist attraction. It is within walking distance of the CTICC and an ideal location for major annual entertainment events and exhibitions.

Set on the edge of a historic working harbour and with the iconic Table Mountain as its backdrop, the V&A Waterfront offers local and international visitors a cosmopolitan mix of experiences.

Discover shops, restaurants, bars, hotels, cafes, galleries, exhibitions and entertainment, or embark on a visit to Robben Island. The prime positioning of the V&A Waterfront boasts sweeping views of the ocean, city bowl and mountain peaks.

When is the best time of year to visit Cape Town?

Cape Town enjoys a Mediterranean-type climate. Summer runs from October until April when daytime temperatures are usually over 22°C / 71.6°F. The hotter months are December, January, and February when temperatures can reach above 30°C / 86°F.

June, July, and August are the winter months which bring milder temperatures and some winter rainfall.

Cape Town is fast becoming a year-round destination with compelling reasons to visit including major sporting events, festivals, international conferences, and conventions and, of course, the perennial beauty and fascination of Table Mountain and the Cape Winelands.

What time is Check-In / Check-Out?

Check-in is at 2pm and check-out is at 11am.

Please speak to our reservations team if you require an early check-in or late check-out (subject to availability).
