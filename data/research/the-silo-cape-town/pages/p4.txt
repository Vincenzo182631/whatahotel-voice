# Experiences at The Silo Hotel

The experiences at The Silo Hotel is focused on the art, food and culture of Cape Town while celebrating the exquisite natural beauty of South Africa’s Mother City.

The Silo Hotel Experiences

All ExperiencesActivitiesExcursions

**Simple Sophisticated Dining** The Silo Hotel

Our dining experience introduces you to the cultural nuances of your destination and shares the refined artwork of culinary masters, revealing new identities of ingredients and tantalising your senses with flavour, texture, aroma, and colour.

Experience

**The V&A Waterfront** The Silo Hotel

The V&A Waterfront is a lively hub of world-class eateries and designer shops, talented street performers, family-friendly activities, and authentic arts & crafts from the Cape, all gathered together on the historic working harbour in the shadow of Table Mountain.

Discover

**Zeitz MOCAA** The Silo Hotel

Zeitz MOCAA is the world’s largest contemporary African art museum and it sets the stage for African artists of today and aspiring artists of tomorrow. It is located just below The Silo Hotel.

Explore

**Art at The Silo Hotel** The Silo Hotel

Meticulously selected by the owner of The Royal Portfolio and avid art enthusiast, Liz Biden, The Silo Hotel’s art collection houses some of Africa’s most talented, emerging, and eminent contemporary artists.

Explore

**Entertaining Little Travellers** The Silo Hotel

Travelling with your family brings a level of connectedness that transcends the generational gap. Your family is warmly welcomed at The Silo Hotel, where home-based activities and outdoor excursions encourage exploration of the beautiful Mother City.

Explore

**Fitness and Fresh Air** The Silo Hotel

Cape Town is the keep-fit capital of South Africa with the great, scenic outdoors offering some beautiful places to build up a sweat or relax during a round of golf. Discover hiking and biking tracks along the seaside and up mountain paths, all just a short distance from The Silo Hotel.

Get Active

**Theatre** The Silo Hotel

Cape Town is a festival for the arts in South Africa – there are schools dedicated to studying the art of performance and there are a handful of theatre houses throughout the city that have carved a name for themselves on the global performance scene.

Discover

**Celebrations** The Silo Hotel

The location of The Silo Hotel alone is reason enough to celebrate special milestones here. Table Mountain on one side and the ocean on the other – the views are dramatic and the hotel is picturesquely located to make the most of it all.

Dream Big

**Out and About in The City** The Silo Hotel

Discover the city! Smell the wild flowers and indigenous trees in our national botanical gardens, take the cable car to the top of Table Mountain, or embark on a guided historic tour of Robben Island.

Venture Out

**Sunshine and Surf** The Silo Hotel

Summer in Cape Town is all about the beachfront, whether you’re on the sand under a colourful umbrella, or enjoying the breeze from a beach bar. Surfing big waves or just basking with a book, the coastline of the Cape has something for everyone.

Dive In

**Relax and Rejuvenate the Senses** The Silo Hotel

Soak up some quiet time and realign your mind, body, and soul at The Silo Spa. Whether you’re looking for deeply nourishing skincare treatments, or you just want to switch off and be pampered, find it in our place of tranquillity in the city.

Sink In

**Cape Nature and Wildlife** The Silo Hotel

Cape Town’s biodiversity is on display both on land and at sea where marine life is just as cherished as the wildlife and birds in the fynbos mountains. Spending time in these naturally rich environments is one way to help value and conserve them.

Explore
