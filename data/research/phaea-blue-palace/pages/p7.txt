Step into Phāea Blue, where breathtaking views meet art, craftsmanship, and thoughtful design to create an atmosphere of warmth and timeless elegance. From the curated books lining the shelves to the handcrafted details throughout the lobby, every element invites you to immerse yourself in a world of refined simplicity and authentic Greek hospitality.

Phāea Blue is a serene retreat where modern luxury meets the island’s rich heritage, inviting you to immerse yourself in Crete’s unparalleled beauty and history.

Every detail at Phāea Blue is designed to create an intimate atmosphere that fosters genuine connections. In our rooms, indulge in the ultimate pampering with Apiceuticals, a brand rooted in a legacy of organic and ethical beekeeping since 1981, harnessing the power of honey, propolis, royal jelly, and beeswax. Aligned with our Plan Bee initiative, we’ve created a bee-friendly landscape featuring seasonal gardens, vibrant flowering plants, and ample water sources. This dedication reflects our mission to connect like-minded individuals through an experience that celebrates nature, sustainability, and thoughtful luxury.⁠

At Phāea Blue, every space is a celebration of art and craftsmanship, with curated details and locally inspired designs creating a uniquely immersive experience.
