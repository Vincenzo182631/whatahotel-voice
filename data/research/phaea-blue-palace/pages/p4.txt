Wellness

Escape from the stresses and strains of modern life and step into the tranquil world of the Phāea Blue Spa. Imagine being surrounded by beautifully curated gardens that frame the beauty of Aegean Sea, offering a sanctuary for yoga, meditation, and fitness enthusiasts. In this idyllic space , yoga and meditation become not just practices but profound connections with the natural world. These soul-soothing sessions are regular features on our daily activities calendar, inviting you to harmonize your inner self with the tranquil beauty of our surroundings.

Wellness Retreats

Escape the hustle and bustle of daily life and discover a sanctuary of peace and tranquility. Our Retreats invite individuals from all walks of life to embark on a journey of self-discovery, surrounded by the beauty of nature. Immerse yourself in a holistic experience designed to promote overall well-being and inner harmony. From yoga and meditation sessions to rejuvenating spa treatments, you'll be able to go on a wellness journey like no other.

Our visiting Wellness Specialists are here to guide you on a transformative journey of rejuvenation and self-exploration during your time at our Cretan Estate. Offering a range of services including soothing massages, energy healing, acupuncture, as well as holistic therapies and mindfulness workshops, these four experts provide an enriching experience tailored to nurture your overall well-being and inner harmony.

Fitness

An oasis of tranquility, where mind, body, and soul become one with nature's elements. In this intimate haven, bespoke rituals infused with sea vitality and mountain energy unfold in softly lit treatment rooms.
