## Discover Our Iconic Indian Wells Resort, Transformed.

Nestled in Greater Palm Springs, Grand Hyatt Indian Wells Resort & Villas offers world-class accommodations, luxury spa services, a golf course and outdoor recreational opportunities. Experience our epic water park featuring a lazy river, splash pad and dueling water slides that makes our family-friendly resort the ultimate year-round destination.

## VILLAS, SUITES & ROOMS

Grand Hyatt Indian Wells Resort & Villas offers 531 sophisticated and uniquely designed rooms, including 39 suites and 43 villas. Unwind in a spacious living area or retreat to a spa-inspired marble bathroom with separate shower and tub. Find tranquility on a private balcony or tackle the next day’s agenda in your workspace. Our spacious hotel rooms make it easy to relax, play and indulge in all our Indian Wells resort has to offer.

## EXPLORE

Resort Activities

Stay up to date on our Indian Wells resort by exploring our guide. Discover information about activities, fitness classes, spa specials, hours of operation, a resort map and more.

Visit the Gift Shop

Located next to Agua Serena Spa and villa registration, discover our expertly curated gift shop, where essentials meet exquisite finds. Explore a refined selection of stylish sunglasses, premium apparel, chic hats, sophisticated accessories for men, women, and children. Plus, uncover unique keepsakes and exclusive souvenirs, perfect for capturing the essence of your stay.

Celebrate the Season

Make the most of the holiday season with a desert getaway at Grand Hyatt Indian Wells Resort & Villas. Explore seasonal offers designed to make your stay even more memorable.

Wellness & Spa Services

Surround yourself in peace at our spa, where opportunities to nourish and restore mind and body abound. Agua Serena Spa offers facials, massages and body treatments, a yoga studio and classes, wellness consultations and a 24-hour fitness studio. Enjoy one of the many walking and biking trails throughout our Palm Springs resort or get in a game of tennis on our courts.

Cabanas

Experience complete relaxation with our signature cabana rentals. Our family-friendly cabanas offer exclusivity right at the center of all the action, while the cabanas located in the Oasis Pool, our tranquil adult-only pool offer a private space.

Oasis Pool

Relax in a private Palm Springs oasis away from it all. Cool off in our resort’s adults-only pool or relax in a poolside cabana. This pool is open to guests who are 18 and older.

HyTides Waterpark

Dive into some fun at our huge pool area, home to HyTides Plunge with some of the tallest waterslides in the desert. The three-story attraction features 30-foot dueling waterslides and a 450-foot lazy river and splash pad, complementing the resort’s seven expansive pools with a backdrop of majestic mountains and palm trees.

### Pets Are Welcome

Your dogs are welcome at Grand Hyatt Indian Wells Resort and Villas. Some restrictions apply. Please contact us for complete details. Service animals are exempt from the dog fee. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

#### Pet Fees

- 1 - 6 nights: **$175** _/ STAY_

- 7 nights or more (Additional fee for every six-night block): **$175**

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  75Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 100Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.

### Resort Fee

A daily resort fee of $45.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience.

Future Changes: Bookings on and beyond January 01, 2027 - A daily resort fee of $50.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.
