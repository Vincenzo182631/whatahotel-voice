# Dining

Grand Hyatt Indian Wells Resort & Villas in Greater Palm Springs offers an exceptional blend of laid-back and upscale dining experiences, crafted by expert chefs to delight any palate. Enjoy a culinary journey in a stunning resort setting.

PreviousNext

## Tía Carmen

Tía Carmen, by renowned chef Angelo Sosa, draws inspiration from Sosa’s aunt and pays homage to the beauty of the Southwest’s stories, land and farmers through an upscale dining experience. This unique Palm Springs restaurant is now open for breakfast and dinner. Hours of operation are subject to change.

## Carmocha

Follow Tía Carmen, known as “Carmocha” as she takes us on a journey across the globe and back to Indian Wells, exploring rich flavors, diverse cultures and delicious recipes. Carmocha’s menu specializes in an array of small plates from various regions around the world, and a variety of craft cocktails. Restaurant hours of operation are subject to change.

## Camino Springs Market

Conveniently located in our lobby, Camino Springs Market is an all-day oasis for on-the-go guests, featuring a selection of fresh coffee, teas, sandwiches, and treats infused with locally harvested fruits. It’s the perfect pick-me-up to an already perfect day. Hours of operation are subject to change.
