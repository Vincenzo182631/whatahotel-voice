### Very disappointed!

Been bringing our family to this Hyatt as a family tradition for the past 13 years, I never imagined I would be writing a review like this. We loved this hotel, looked forward to staying here every year, and raved about it to our friends and family. Unfortunately, this stay was so disappointing that we will not be returning.

Our experience was unacceptable! We were given an upgraded room when we checked in which was a pleasant surprise. It had a beautiful terrace and view of the pool. Not long after settling in I looked around the room where I noticed bugs/insects all over the ceiling edges. We were understandably uncomfortable and asked to be moved. Packed all our things moved to the second room. As we start to look around this room it also had bugs/insects in the corners, tons of dust on the lamp, dust bunnies on the walls, and dirt on the trim of the floor. At this point it is 10:00. Guest services sent o it someone to clean it and said if we wanted to move in the morning we could. We woke up in the morning in hopes to move because the condition of this room was horrible the door didn’t shut all the way air conditioning wasn’t working great, tape over the sliding door corner and a view of the parking lot. Here we are now moving again to another room… this is the 3rd time! Now in the room
before we move everything down from the 3rd floor to the 1st floor I do an inspection before we lug everything down while there were no bugs that we noticed, the cleanliness was still completely unacceptable. The room looked as though it had not been properly vacuumed or cleaned. There were sunflower seeds and other debris on the floor, significant dust throughout the room, and food stains on the curtains and floor. I called to have someone come in and vacuum… I am sooo done at this point. Like am I on candid camera this is insane! Someone did come out to vacuum but honestly the room still felt so dirty!

I was very apparent this wasn’t just an isolated issue with one room — every room we were moved into had noticeable cleanliness problems! This was not a minor inconvenience or one bad room. We had to move three times and encountered serious cleanliness issues in every room.

We also ordered poolside food service twice, and both orders were incorrect. Is anyone paying attention!

What makes this experience even more difficult to understand is that the hotel was reportedly at only about 20% occupancy during our stay. With the property nowhere near full capacity, there is simply no reasonable explanation for why multiple rooms would be presented to guests in this condition. At a hotel that positions itself as a high-end luxury property, cleanliness and attention to detail should be non-negotiable.
That should never happen at a 5-star luxury hotel!

I do want to make clear that the employees we interacted with were friendly and provided good customer service. Our frustration is not directed at the individual employees who tried to help us. The issue is the overall lack of cleanliness, quality control, and attention to detail that we experienced.

And to top it off! We are Hyatt card holders we have had the parking/resort fees waived as such. When checking out we’re smacked with a $139 fee. Unbelievable! You’d think they would have waived that this time after a terrible experience we had, but no!

After 13 years of loyalty, I am extremely disappointed! We have spent years telling people how much we love this hotel and encouraging others to come here. We genuinely looked forward to our stays. As we checked out my husband and I looked at each other and said we are done coming here!

I sincerely hope management takes this seriously!
