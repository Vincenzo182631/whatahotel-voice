## One Bedroom Villa

1,100 sq ftView Varies

Discover this 1,110-square-foot resort villa featuring one king bed in the bedroom, a fireplace in the expansive living space, a wet bar in the dining area and a grill on the private furnished patio. Step out to stunning views of the mountains in Greater Palm Springs and the resort golf course.

In-Room Amenities

- Luxurious villa, with indoor and outdoor privacy
- Private outdoor patio, BBQ grill with dining set and umbrella
- Separate bedroom with Sealy Posturepedic ® Luxury Pillowtop Mattress and upgraded luxury linen bedding
- Soaking tub and separate walk-in, marble shower, and luxury bath amenities
- Spacious living area with fireplace
- Dining area and wet bar with microwave, glassware, and silverware
- Two 65" flat-screen TVs
- Bluetooth equipped sound system
- Washer/Dryer and Ice Machine located just steps away from villa
- Shuttle service provided to and from villa.
- Select villas offer an outdoor spa, plunge pool, or fire pit (based upon availability and subject to change)
- Complimentary Valet Parking
- Complimentary Spa Access
