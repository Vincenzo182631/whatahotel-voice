## Two Bedroom Villa with Premium Outdoor Terrace

1,500 sq ftView Varies

Experience ultimate desert luxury in this 1,500-square-foot villa with two bedrooms, a cozy fireplace, and a wet bar. The space features two bedrooms, a king bedroom and a bedroom with two queen beds. With each outdoor terrace starting at 1,200 square feet of premium backyard space, guests are treated to breathtaking views, an elevated daybed, a hot tub, and a fire pit—perfect for starlit barbecues and relaxed evenings.

In-Room Amenities

- Luxurious villa with indoor and outdoor privacy
- Private outdoor patio with BBQ grill, dining set and umbrella
- Hyatt Luxury Pillowtop Mattresses and upgraded linens
- Soaking tub and separate marble walk-in shower
- Spacious living area with fireplace
- Dining area and wet bar with microwave, glassware and silverware
- Three 55-65” flat-screen TVs
- Hair dryer
- Robes
- Balmain bath amenities

- Shuttle service to and from villa
