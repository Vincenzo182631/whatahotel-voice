## Wymara Villas Sunset Cove Becomes the First

## Forbes Five‑Star Property in Turks and Caicos

Wymara Villas Sunset Cove has officially received the Forbes Travel Guide Five Star Award, a landmark achievement for both the Wymara brand and the Turks and Caicos luxury landscape. This historic recognition, the first Five‑Star designation ever awarded in Turks and Caicos, places Wymara Villas Sunset Cove among the world’s most exceptional private villa experiences and underscores the island’s rising profile as a global destination for modern, design‑driven hospitality.

The Forbes Five Star distinction is reserved for properties that deliver flawless service, intuitive guest experiences, and an atmosphere of effortless luxury. These qualities have defined Wymara Villas Sunset Cove since its debut. With its contemporary architecture, expansive indoor‑outdoor living, and private oceanfront setting, the Villas continue to elevate what discerning travelers expect from Turks and Caicos.

Wymara Resort simultaneously maintains its Forbes Four Star Award, strengthening the brand’s presence across the island and offering guests two distinct expressions of elevated Caribbean living, the intimacy and exclusivity of the Villas, and the contemporary, oceanfront energy of the Resort.

## Green Globe Certified

Wymara Villas + Beach Club is proud to announce that it has officially been awardedGreen Globe Certification, a globally recognized standard for sustainable tourism. Timed with Earth Month, this milestone underscores Wymara’s unwavering commitment to environmental responsibility, community support, and best-in-class sustainable practices — all while delivering luxury with a conscience, a hallmark of the Wymara experience.

The Villas ranked among the top-performing first-time applicants, establishing a strong foundation for ongoing advancement in climate-conscious hospitality. ## Sleek Design, Stunning Location

Here, an exquisite fusion of seclusion and indulgence awaits, allowing you to revel in pure bliss while relishing in the plethora of amenities that a resort offers.

Our villas stand as a true embodiment of magnificence and tranquility, nestled in one of the Caribbean's most breathtaking destinations. ### Villa Concierge Services at Your Command

### Ensuring you have all the assistance and information you need to make the most of your stay

At Wymara Villas + Beach Club, each visit guarantees a fresh and personalized experience, perfectly crafted to cater to our guests' unique preferences. Our dedicated Villa Experience Managers are here to fulfill any specific requests or adventurous endeavors that align with your desires, serving as your very own Personal Concierge throughout your stay. Extraordinary and unparalleled experiences are just a phone call or a simple text away.

Rest assured, your Villa Experience Manager has the expertise to organize a variety of activities, ranging from boat charters, island tours, and diving expeditions to indulgent spa services and an exquisite five-star dining experience, all conveniently arranged within the privacy of your villa.

Services include:

- Complimentary daily breakfast at Land + Sea from 7am to 9:30am
- Exclusive access to Sunset Cove Beach Club
- Private roundtrip airport transfer to and from PLS Airport (two SUVs per way) for our 4, 5 and 6 Bedroom Villas
- Unobstructed ocean views from every room
- On-site Villa Manager who will design your trip from reservation to departure
- Welcome refreshment and light bar set up upon arrival
- Access to Wymara Resort amenities
- On-Request Shuttle to transfer Guests between the resorts Wymara Villas and Wymara Resort on Grace Bay Beach. The shuttle will depart from The Sports Club and will be ready for departure within thirty minutes of your request. If you would like to reserve a transfer for a specific time, please notify us in advance, and we will ensure it is arranged accordingly.
- 24-hour on-site security
- Private over-the-water swim platform with access to the sea in our 4, 5 and 6 Bedroom Villas
- Courtyard waterfall plunge pool in our 4, 5 and 6 Bedroom Villas
- Private infinity pool
- Complimentary high-speed internet
- Twice-daily housekeeping

#### Chef Prepared In-Villa Cuisine

Our team of talented Chefs are devoted food experts, ready to craft every element of your gastronomic adventure within the seclusion and luxury of your 4, 5 or 6 Bedroom Villa. From relaxed Caribbean barbecues on the terrace to exquisite fine dining occasions with loved ones, or even light snacks by the poolside, your Villa Manager is capable of arranging any culinary experience you desire.

##### Kosher Events

For our guests staying in our luxurious 4 & 5 Bedroom Villas, we are delighted to provide personalized turnkey Kosherization services for your villa. In collaboration with Rabbi Berkowitz, the esteemed Chabad Rabbi on Providenciales Island, we ensure that your villa meets all the necessary Kosher requirements. Rabbi Berkowitz, working closely with our talented chefs, crafts tailor-made Kosher menus for your gatherings and oversees the Kosherization process of your villa. Rest assured, your special occasion, Jewish lifecycle event, or wedding will be handled with utmost care and expertise by our highly skilled team and Rabbi Berkowitz.

##### Private roundtrip airport transfer to and from PLS Airport

Arrive feeling completely rejuvenated and utterly at ease in the unparalleled comfort of our opulent luxury vehicles. We take immense pride in offering you personalized and private transfers, ensuring a seamless journey that leads you directly to the doorstep of your exquisite Wymara Villa. Our top-of-the-line vehicles are meticulously maintained and exude an air of sophistication, providing you with the utmost tranquility throughout your entire experience. With our unwavering commitment to delivering unparalleled service, you can rest assured that your arrival will be nothing short of extraordinary. So sit back, relax, and allow us to transport you to your destination in the epitome of style, comfort, and exclusivity.

###### Scheduled complimentary transportation to and from Wymara Resort

Getting from your Villa to Wymara Resort is a breeze - a speedy 6-8 minute journey covering 6km/2.5 miles via our convenient car service. Just reach out to your Villa Experience Manager and we'll happily provide complimentary transportation, allowing you to sit back and relax. Transportation services run from the Villas to the Resort hourly on the hour and from the Resort to the Villas hourly on the half hour.

### Sunset Cove Beach Club.

### Wellness by the sea

A visit to the villas offers access to the stunning Sunset Cove Beach Club, where tranquil shallow waters, a secluded beach, gracefully swaying palm trees, and a spacious lawn area invite relaxation. The Club proudly features a state-of-the-art 130-foot ocean pool, a first of its kind in the region. This remarkable amenity offers SUP Yoga classes in an unparalleled setting and enhances the contemporary Sports Club and wellness center, which includes basketball and pickleball courts, tennis facilities, premium Technogym fitness equipment, and a lap pool hosting Pool-Lates classes.

### Wellness

#### Elevate Your Well-Being at Wymara

Experience the ultimate wellness retreat at Wymara Villas + Beach Club, a meticulously crafted sanctuary where the therapeutic power of the surrounding azure ocean rejuvenates body, mind, and soul. Nestled along the pristine shores of Turtle Tail, this private enclave invites guests to reconnect through curated wellness rituals and immersive island serenity.

The111SKIN SPAfeaturesadvanced skin care treatments created by renouned surgeon, Dr Yannis Alexandrines, in soothing treatment rooms designed within a calm and modern environment.

Indulge in our exclusive, state-of-the-art Sunset Cove Beach Club, featuring cutting-edge Technogym cardio equipment, pampering Wymara Spa services, and a captivating open-air Game Room with billiards, ping pong, and vintage arcade games. Elevate your fitness journey with our lap pool, Pool-lates classes, basketball, bocce, and tennis and pickleball courts, designed for holistic movement and mindful play.

For deeper restoration, guests can enjoy in-villa massage treatments, SUP Yoga sessions on crystal-clear waters, and transformative sound therapy experiences—each tailored to harmonize energy and promote inner calm. Whether you're seeking active renewal or quiet reflection, every detail is designed to leave you feeling replenished, balanced, and inspired.

At Wymara, your well-being is our highest priority. From sunrise rituals to sunset stillness, we invite you to discover a wellness journey as breathtaking as the island itself.

##### Luxury With A Conscience

Our beautiful islands are renowned for their picture-perfect ocean and beaches, which continue to captivate new visitors and entice returning guests year after year. We fully recognize the significance of our environment and the vital role it plays in our interconnected existence. With this in mind, we have made it our mission to safeguard our home, including our magnificent reef - the third largest in the world - as well as the diverse marine life that calls it home. In doing so, we are actively contributing to the preservation of our planet.

As we deepen our understanding of sustainability and our impact on the environment, we are committed to implementing necessary changes to our practices and procedures. This ongoing evolution enables us to remain steadfast in our mission. We firmly believe in upholding integrity and taking responsibility for our actions. Therefore, we kindly request your collaboration in identifying areas where we can further improve and work together to achieve our shared goals.

The Villas have been honored as a Sustainability Leader by The Leading Hotels of the World. This recognition reflects our dedication to excellence in sustainable hospitality, particularly concerning community, culture, and environmental stewardship.
