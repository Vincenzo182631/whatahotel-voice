# Getting Here

## Getting to the Island

The Turks and Caicos Islands are privileged with excellent air service to the island of Providenciales. Access from many major U.S. airports allows connections from domestic and international feeder cities; the island is currently serviced by American Airlines, Delta, United, JetBlue and Southwest Airlines. Air Canada and WestJet offer service from Canada, while British Airways and Virgin Atlantic each have twice-weekly services from London Heathrow.

### Private Air Travel

Providenciales features two state of the art FBOs; Atlantic Aviation and Provo Air Center.

For further details: https://www.atlanticaviation.com/location/PLS

https://plsairport.com

### Arrival to the Island

Upon arrival to the island and the Providenciales Airport (PLS),  a representative will assist guests with luggage and escort them to a shared taxi service for transport to the resort.

### Airport Expedited Services

Streamline your journey with a VIP customs and security line, personalized assistance with paperwork, and expedited transport to Wymara Resort + Villas or your boarding gate. Say goodbye to long waits, overcrowded lines, and unnecessary stress.

﻿More Information

### Airport Transportation

The Resort is located approximately 10-14 minutes and the Villas 15-20 minutes from the airport. The shared taxi fee is currently $11.50 per person, $23.00 per couple for Resort guests and $15.00 per person, $30 per couple for Villa guests and should be paid directly to the taxi driver.

Rates are subject to change without notice.

### Private SUV Airport Transfers

A private SUV can be arranged prior to arrival through our Guest Services team. Each vehicle can accommodate one to six passengers for a one-way transfer at flat rate of $132.00 to the Resort and $165 to the Villas inclusive of taxes and gratuity.

Rates are subject to change without notice. 24 hours notice required for reservations and cancellations to avoid any penalties.
