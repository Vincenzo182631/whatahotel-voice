## Overlooking Iconic Grace Bay Beach

Island charm meets understated elegance at Wymara Resort. Take in stunning views of Grace Bay Beach in one of our oceanfront Studios or Suites. Ideally located in the heart of Turks and Caicos’ award-winning Grace Bay Beach, Wymara Resort combines the abundance of a high-end resort with the exclusivity of a boutique hotel. Whether retreating to your tranquil Studio or Suite, or basking in the luxurious comforts of the extensive Penthouse Suite, you’re in for the ultimate Caribbean escape.

Wymara Resort + Villas takes great pride in being affiliated with prestigious organizations such as Leading Hotels of the World, Virtuoso, Forbes Travel Guide, Signature, Fine Hotels + Resorts, Internova, and Ensemble.

#### The Destination Wedding Of Your Dreams

Wymara Resort + Villas is the ideal location for any type of destination wedding.  From intimate ceremonies to sizable events, our breathtaking Caribbean backdrops provide the perfect venues for your nuptials on Turks and Caicos.

Our Villas + Beach Club can host an exclusive wedding celebration for up to 35 people at our private Sunset Cove Beach and Club.  All catering and special events is handled on site by our expert team of culinary specialists and planners.

For larger affairs, Wymara Resort is available for complete wedding buyouts of up to 190 people, the perfect location to welcome family and friends for the party of a lifetime, from sun-up to sun-down.

## Ownership Opportunities

Step into the world of luxury Caribbean real estate with Wymara Resort, an exclusive opportunity to own a contemporary oceanfront condominium on iconic Grace Bay Beach, Turks and Caicos. Designed for seamless indoor-outdoor living, each private residence offers panoramic ocean views, sleek modern architecture, and direct access to the island’s most coveted coastline.

Our exquisitely designed and luxurious private villa residences are also available for purchase. The stunning oceanfront Villas, featuring one, four, five, and six bedrooms, are situated just a brief 2.5 mile drive from Wymara Resort, along the magnificent southern coastline of Providenciales in Turtle Tail. Owners benefit from the seclusion of a luxurious villa while enjoying immediate access to resort-like amenities and services, including direct communication with a dedicated villa butler available to cater to your every need.

As a Wymara homeowner, you’ll enjoy full access to the award-winning Wymara Resort + Villas amenities, including signing privileges at our signature restaurants, indulgent treatments at The Spa, exhilarating watersports including the Caribbean's first Ocean Pool, and curated beach experiences. Ownership also includes the option to participate in the resort’s rental program, offering effortless property management and income potential — ideal for international buyers or those seeking a flexible investment.

Whether you're looking for a second home in the Caribbean, a luxury villa for sale in Turks and Caicos, or a smart investment with five-star benefits, Wymara offers a rare blend of privacy, prestige, and performance.

###### Luxury with a Conscience

### Turks and Caicos' only Green Globe Certified Resort

At Wymara Resort + Villas, we understand the irresistible charm of our unspoiled ocean and beaches, which continuously captivate newcomers to our breathtaking islands and keep our valued guests returning year after year. As a community intrinsically connected to nature, it is our responsibility to safeguard our home, including our renowned reef (the third largest in the world), and marine life, while also contributing to the preservation of our planet.

As our understanding of sustainability and its connection to the environment grows, we remain steadfast in our determination to adapt our practices and procedures accordingly. Our unyielding dedication to the environment drives us to seek your collaboration in pinpointing areas where we can enhance our efforts, in order to realize our goal of creating a truly sustainable paradise.
