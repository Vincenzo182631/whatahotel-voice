### Wymara Villas Sunset Cove Earns Distinction

### as the First Forbes Five-Star Property in Turks and Caicos.

Wymara Villas Sunset Cove has proudly achieved the prestigious Forbes Travel Guide Five Star Award, setting a remarkable milestone for both the Wymara brand and the luxury landscape of Turks and Caicos. This landmark recognition is the first Five-Star designation in the region, positioning Wymara Villas among the world’s elite private villa experiences and underscoring the island’s allure as a premier global destination for contemporary hospitality.

The Forbes Five Star distinction recognizes properties that deliver unparalleled service and effortless luxury—qualities Wymara Villas has exemplified since its inception. With its modern design and stunning oceanfront setting, the Villas cater impeccably to the expectations of discerning travelers in Turks and Caicos.

Furthermore, Wymara Resort maintains its Forbes Four Star Award, further strengthening the brand’s footprint and offering guests two distinct experiences of Caribbean living: the exclusive Villas and the lively, oceanfront Resort.
