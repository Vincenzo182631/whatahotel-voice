# Our partners

At 100 Princes Street, we are proud to partner with established, long-standing businesses who share our family values of quality and integrity. Here, we introduce the long-established Scottish producers and suppliers who work with Chef Wilhelm Maree and his culinary team, as well as a select number of businesses who contributed to the design of our hotel, all of which share Red Carnation’s inherent family values.

### Eddie’s Seafood Market

Scotland's premier supplier of sashimi grade fish, live shellfish, smoked fish, and local seafood, freshness and sustainability are the main priority of husband and wife team, award-winning chef Campbell Mickel and Giselle, who run Eddie’s Seafood Market, and supply exceptional fish and seafood.

Discover more

### East Coast Cured

Husband and wife team, Steven and Susie Anderson, grew up together in Edinburgh. 20 years, a wedding and two children later, they now run an award-winning charcuterie business based in Leith. Championing the finest Scottish produce, sourcing meats from local farms, East Coast Cured supplies outstanding Scottish charcuterie.

Discover more

### Araminta Campbell

We are proud to showcase five tartans that are unique to the hotel, created by revered Scottish designer Araminta Campbell. Renowned for her intricate designs and unwavering commitment to sustainability, Araminta Campbell seamlessly fuses contemporary designs with centuries of Scottish tradition.

Discover more

### George Anderson & Sons

A family run business now in its fourth generation, George Anderson & Sons is one of Scotland’s largest independent distributors of fresh produce, and supplies 100 Princes Street with the highest quality fruit and vegetables.

Discover more

### Yester Farm Dairy

Hand-making everything on the family farm, ensuring the freshest possible produce for its customers, Yester Farm Dairies is run by husband and wife team Simon and Jackie McCreery, who ensure that their cows enjoy time on the East Lothian pastures, while also being fed a nutritionally balanced diet.

Discover more

### Belhaven Smokehouse

Owned and managed by two close, long-standing friends, the smoked salmon supplied to 100 Princes Street by Belhaven Smokehouse is dry cured for up to 48-hours using a varying mix of salt, sugar, spices and botanicals, which draw out moisture and infuse incredible flavour.

Discover more

### Edinburgh Butter Co.

Following traditional methods of production to capture the texture and flavour of how butter used to taste when time, effort and care went into producing it, the Edinburgh Butter Co. supplies the hotel with silky slow-churned butter that is deep and rich in flavour.

Discover more

### John Gilmour Butchers

A third-generation family business based in East Lothian and established in the 1940s, using the skills and experience built up over more than 80 years of trading, John Gilmour Butchers supplies 100 Princes Street with the finest British meats.

Discover more

### Croxford and Saunders

Upon entering the hotel, guests are immediately invited on an artistic voyage, marked by an array of artworks including bespoke pieces by the notable decorative arts specialists, Croxford and Saunders. Among these, a hand-painted mural, celebrating the daring spirit of Scottish explorers.

Discover more

### The Torridon

We are delighted to partner with The Torridon, one of Scotland’s most celebrated Highland retreats, to offer an exclusive twin-stay experience that showcases the country’s extraordinary diversity. Set amid the dramatic landscapes of Wester Ross, The Torridon is renowned for its deep connection to nature, warm hospitality and a spirit of adventure. Together, our partnership invites guests to experience the cultured elegance of the city alongside the wild beauty of the Highlands, creating a seamless journey that captures the very best of Scotland.
