# Our hotel

Inspired by the adventures and tales of Scottish explorers who once called the building their home away from home, 100 Princes Street is set within the historic former Royal Overseas League headquarters, nestled in Edinburgh’s cultural heart.

1867

With the expansion of Edinburgh Waverley station, Princes Street saw the establishment of several high-end hotels. One such entrepreneurial hotelier was Calixte Dejay, a French monsieur who took over 99-101 Princes Street in 1867 for his new luxurious lodge, Dejay’s Hotel.

1877

100 Princes Street passed into the hands of German immigrant Albert Thiem, who re-christened it the Windsor Hotel. Upon purchasing 99-101 Princes Street, the original four-storey Georgian townhouse was expanded into a seven-storey hotel, a design of famed architect Robert Paterson.

1929

Half a century later, the Royal Overseas League took up residence at 100 Princes Street. Opened by the Duke of York, Edinburgh’s first mixed club became popular with visitors from across the Commonwealth, including a number of prime ministers.

2020

The painstaking renovation and transformation of 100 Princes Street into a luxury townhouse hotel, and newest member of The Red Carnation Hotel Collection, begins.

2024

In April 2024, 100 Princes Street began a bright new chapter in the building’s storied history.
