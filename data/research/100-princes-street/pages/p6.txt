# Location & directions

An elegant haven of calm set amidst one of the city’s most lively thoroughfares, 100 Princes Street is centrally located in the cultural heart of the Scottish capital, directly facing Edinburgh Castle and just five minutes’ walk from the main train station, with its high-speed connections to the airport.

### Within easy reach

**Princes Street Gardens:** 1-minute walk

**Scottish National Gallery:** 5-minute walk

**Scott Monument:** 7-minute walk

**Edinburgh Waverley Train Station:** 10-minute walk

**Writers' Museum:** 10-minute walk

**Scottish National Portrait Gallery:** 11-minute walk

**Edinburgh Castle:** 12-minute walk

**National Museum of Scotland:** 14-minute walk

**Old Town:** 15-minute walk

### Getting here

Edinburgh Waverley Train Station is just a 10-minute walk from 100 Princes Street. We would be delighted to arrange a porter to collect your luggage from the station, so that you may make the short walk to our hotel unencumbered. Alternatively, taxis are readily available at the station’s main taxi rank; this is located to the south of the station on Market Street.

## Welcome to Edinburgh

Alive year-round and bursting at the seams with culture, from the lofty heights of the city’s iconic castle, to the cobbled streets of the Old Town, and bucolic surrounds of its public gardens, every corner of Edinburgh tells tales of antiquity, and whispers secrets of centuries past.
