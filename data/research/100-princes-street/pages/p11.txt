# What not to miss

Discover what to taste and sip, where to slumber and venture, what experiences to enjoy, and who to connect with during your stay at 100 Princes Street.

> "At 100 Princes Street, we're dedicated to offering authentic experiences. Join me as I introduce you to a curated selection of moments that embody our historic charm, impeccable service, and vibrant surroundings."

Resident Manager

### Eat

Enjoy a perfect blend of crispy, golden-battered fish and fluffy, seasoned chips. Served with a side of tangy tartar sauce and a hint of lemon, this classic dish is a delightful culinary experience that captures the essence of traditional comfort food at 100 Princes Street.

Discover The Wallace

### Drink

Don't miss the whisky selection at 100 Princes Street. Our team will guide you through tastings, offering rich, complex flavours that showcase Scotland's finest distilleries. It's a must-try experience for both connoisseurs and those new to the world of whisky.

Discover Ghillie's Pantry

### Sleep

The Archibald Suite offers unparalleled luxury with its elegant decor and stunning views of Edinburgh Castle. This spacious suite features a plush king-size bed, a cosy sitting area, and a lavish bathroom. Perfect for a memorable stay in the heart of the Scottish capital.

Discover The Archibald Suite

### Explore

Calton Hill is an iconic spot offers panoramic views of Edinburgh, the National Monument, and Nelson Monument. Perfect for a picturesque stroll, it's rich in history and provides stunning photo opportunities, especially at sunset.

Discover our neighbourhood

### Experience

Join Araminta Campbell and her talented team to to witness the artistry and dedication that goes into creating exquisite textiles. Engage with the weavers working on the looms, and gain an insight into their traditional techniques.

Discover our experiences

### Meet

Meet Rory McKinnon, our Food & Beverage Operations Manager. Rory strives to offer guests genuine, passionate and warm hospitality, with paramount importance placed on his attention to detail.
