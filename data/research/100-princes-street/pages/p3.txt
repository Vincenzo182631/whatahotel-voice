# Dining & drinks

Embark on a gastronomic journey like no other at 100 Princes Street. Delight in hearty fare and exquisite flavours in The Wallace, or enjoy an intimate private gathering at Ghillie's Pantry surrounded by a curated collection of rare whiskies. Our dining and drinking venues are reserved exclusively for residents, inviting you to relish moments of refined luxury and culinary mastery within our historic walls.

### The Wallace

Looking directly at Edinburgh Castle and helmed by the hotel’s talented chef, Willhelm Maree, the gastronomic heart and soul of 100 Princes Street is undoubtedly The Wallace.
