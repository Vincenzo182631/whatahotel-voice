# Travel FAQs: The Lodge at Primland, Auberge Collection

### _**Before You Decide**_

- **What kind of place is The Lodge at Primland?**

The Lodge at Prim land is a luxury Blue Ridge Mountains resort on 12,000 private acres above the Dan River Gorge in Virginia.

- **Where is The Lodge at Primland located?**

The Lodge at Primland is located in the Blue Ridge Mountains near Meadows of Dan, Virginia, about two hours north of Charlotte.

- **What makes The Lodge at Primland different from other Auberge Collection properties?**

Primland stands out for its 12,000-acre private mountain estate, The Highland Course, state-of-the-art Observatory, outdoor adventure and back-to-nature escapes, including its tree houses.

- **Is The Lodge at Primland a good fit for couples?**

Primland is well suited to couples, with mountain views, spa treatments, stargazing, golf, nature experiences and secluded accommodations.

- **Is The Lodge at Primland family-friendly?**

Primland welcomes families with rooms, suites, a six-bedroom residence, connecting options, children’s activities and outdoor adventures.

- **What kinds of accommodations are available at The Lodge at Primland?**

Primland offers 30 rooms, 23 suites and a six-bedroom luxury residence, plus cabins and tree houses in its private mountain landscape.

- **Is The Lodge at Primland pet-friendly?**

Pets are welcomed at Primland; guests should contact the resort directly to confirm current pet fees and restrictions.

- **When is the best time to visit The Lodge at Primland?**

Primland welcomes guests year-round, with seasonal mountain activities, golf, hiking, stargazing and holiday programming.

### _**Before You Arrive**_

- **How do I get to The Lodge at Primland from the nearest airport?**

Primland is about a two-hour drive north of Charlotte; guests should contact the resort to confirm current airport-transfer arrangements.

- **What is the check-in and check-out policy at The Lodge at Primland?**

Please contact us before arrival to confirm our current check-in and check-out times.

- **Is parking available at The Lodge at Primland?**

Please contact us before arrival to confirm our current parking terms, including options and any fees.

- **Does The Lodge at Primland charge a resort fee?**

Please contact us before booking to confirm our current resort-fee policy, including fees and inclusions.

- **What is the cancellation policy at The Lodge at Primland?**

Cancellation terms vary by reservation and rate; guests should review their booking confirmation or contact Primland.

- **Does The Lodge at Primland offer airport transportation service?**

Primland provides private car service and a complimentary resort shuttle; guests should contact the resort to confirm airport-transfer availability.

- **Is The Lodge at Primland wheelchair accessible, and what accessible accommodations are available?**

Primland offers accessible accommodations; guests should contact the resort directly to confirm suitable accessible rooms, mobility features and services.

### _**Once You’re Here**_

- **Does The Lodge at Primland have a pool?**

Primland has an indoor pool and hot tub overlooking the mountain grounds.

- **Does The Lodge at Primland have a gym or fitness center?**

Primland has a fitness studio with Peloton bikes, treadmills and free weights, plus yoga, meditation and fitness instruction.

- **Does The Lodge at Primland have a spa?**

Sage, Wellbeing by Auberge is Primland’s spa and wellbeing destination, with with nature-based treatments, an indoor pool, sauna and wellness programming.

- **What are the dining options at The Lodge at Primland?**

Dining includes Leatherflower, Laurel Bar, Stables Saloon, in-room dining, private dining and customized menus.

- **What is the wellbeing experience at The Lodge at Primland?**

Primland wellbeing combines spa treatments, weekly movement classes, hiking and outdoor experiences set within Virginia’s Blue Ridge Mountains.

- **What experiences and activities are available at The Lodge at Primland?**

Guests can enjoy golf at The Highland Course, hiking, the ropes course, stargazing at the Observatory and Primland Sporting Club adventures, from horseback riding and fly-fishing to sporting clays, guided RTV tours and more.

- **What can I do in the area around The Lodge at Primland?**

The Blue Ridge region offers mountain scenery, trails, wildlife, local culture and Virginia outdoor recreation.

### _**Weddings, Meetings & Events**_

- **What kinds of events can be hosted at The Lodge at Primland?**

Primland hosts weddings, celebrations, meetings, retreats and social events in flexible indoor and outdoor mountain venues.

- **Does The Lodge at Primland host weddings?**

Primland hosts wedding ceremonies and receptions, with dramatic mountain scenery, personalized menus and event coordination.

- **What venues at The Lodge at Primland are available for wedding ceremonies and receptions?**

Primland offers wedding ceremony and reception sites; the events team can recommend current venue configurations.

- **Can The Lodge at Primland be reserved for a full buyout?**

For full-resort buyouts, please contact our events team for availability and terms.

- **What meeting and conference spaces are available at The Lodge at Primland?**

Primland offers five meeting rooms, flexible indoor and outdoor event spaces, wireless internet and on-site event coordination.

- **Is The Lodge at Primland suitable for corporate retreats and executive offsites?**

Primland is suitable for corporate retreats and executive offsites, combining meeting space with golf, outdoor adventure and wellness.

- **What are the options for private and group dining at The Lodge at Primland?**

Private dining includes the Schlumberger Wine Cellar, outdoor dining and the Bellvine Room, with custom menus available.

- **What is the minimum and maximum group size The Lodge at Primland can accommodate?**

Primland accommodates a range of group sizes across five meeting rooms and outdoor spaces; the events team can advise on exact current capacities.

- **How far in advance should I book an event or group stay at The Lodge at Primland?**

Primland does not require a universal lead time for events or group stays; groups should contact the resort early, especially for weddings and peak dates.
