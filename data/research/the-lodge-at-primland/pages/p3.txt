# Dine at The Lodge at PrimlandSatisfying the finest of palates with sustainable authenticity

## Seasonal cuisine honoring nature’s bounty

Grounded in flavorful traditions of Virginia, Primland’s culinary offerings embrace storied epicurean concepts while modernizing for today’s palate. From refined dining in the Lodge at Leatherflower to a relaxed Southern supper at Stables Saloon, dining at Primland promises a gastronomic journey for all the senses.

## Leatherflower

## A bespoke journey through Virginia’s culinary traditions

Through expansive windows, Primland’s rolling fairways and majestic mountain views provide an idyllic setting for a culinary journey through the tastes of Appalachia. Inside, wooden beams, floral custom grasscloth wallcoverings and upholstered chairs and banquettes invite diners to an elegant dining experience that honors the region’s rich culinary heritage. As the morning sun gilds the verdant forest landscapes just beyond, fuel up on a classic grits bowl or Blue Ridge omelet. At night, the central fireplace glows while refined yet familiar service invites you to revel in the best of the land and sea.

- Breakfast \| 7-11AM

- Dinner \| 5-8:45PM

- To reserve a table, or inquire about wine pairings, email pri.prearrival@auberge.com

  - $108 per person, inclusive of service charge

## Dining Venues at The Lodge at Primland

Each restaurant reflects Primland’s farm-to-table ethos with highly experienced chefs curating dishes to comfort and inspire.

-

### Laurel Bar

With its outdoor terrace and well-stocked bar, this convivial dining spot is always full of camaraderie and good cheer. Open from late morning until late evening, it’s ideal for refueling between holes, recounting adventures from the trails and planning your next day’s itinerary with our signature Southern Old Fashioned in hand. A fresh view on clubhouse favorites with a nod to the bounty of Primland, the menu has something for everyone.

  - Breakfast \| 9-11AM daily

    Seasonal pastries and coffee available for dine-in or takeaway
  - Lunch \| 11AM – 5PM daily
  - Dinner

    Sunday – Thursday \| 5-10PM

    Friday – Saturday \| 5-11PM

-

### Schlumberger Wine Cellar

Plan your private get-together or attend a tasting in our Schlumberger Wine Cellar, located in The Lodge. This intimate and stunning space showcases the resort’s nearly 450 labels from over 18 countries and the world’s finest vineyards, including our sister property, Domaines Schlumberger in France’s Alsace region, with history that dates back to the 1600s. Seating up to 12 guests, the cellar is the perfect place for a celebratory dinner, group wine tasting, executive cocktail hour and more. A blend of Old and New world, the Schlumberger Wine Cellar has wine varietals to complement all of the exceptional fare offered at The Lodge at Primland.

  - Named for sister property – Domaines Schlumberger – in Alsace, France
  - Seats up to 12 guests
  - Private dinners available nightly for bookings
  - Reserve or inquire at mailto:pri.catering@auberge.com

-

### Stables Saloon

Climb the stairs to the secluded second floor of Primland’s former horse stables and discover traditional Southern food and music at its best. Available year-round for private parties and events, the rustic Stables Saloon regularly hosts live bluegrass music concerts and is part of the Crooked Road: Virginia’s Heritage Music Trail. Enjoy a Southern supper menu featuring family-style favorites in a laid-back atmosphere that will have you never wanting to leave.

  - Monday-Tuesday, Friday-Saturday \| 6-8PM
  - Served family-style
  - Adults $74 \| Children 12 and under $35
  - Seasonal live music
  - Reservations recommended \| Reserve

-

### Woodland Grill

Savor a dining experience crafted to complement your journey. Whether you're fueling up before a day at the Primland Sporting Club or unwinding after a morning along the Dan River, the Woodland Grill’s ready-to-go meals offer a perfect blend of comfort and flavor – ideal for enjoying in the heart of nature or on the way to your next adventure.

  - Wednesday - Sunday \| 11AM - 3PM

-

### Wandering Hearth

Nestled in the meadow beside Stables Saloon, the pizza truck invites guests to gather over handcrafted pizzas and refreshing beverages. Enjoy a leisurely outdoor meal surrounded by the beauty of the Blue Ridge Mountains.

  - Friday - Saturday \| 12-3PM
  - Weather permitting
  - Available Spring - October

-

### In-Room Dining

With their breathtaking views and abundance of space, choosing to dine in the comfort of your room, suite, cottage or treehouse is not the lazy option – in fact, it’s highly recommended. Enjoy breakfast in bed or out on your private deck with a range of options – from the grits bowls to omelets – served until 11AM. The extensive in-room dining menu also features a delicious selection of hot and cold appetizers, sandwiches, sides and snacks, not to mention indulgent desserts, available between 11AM and 11PM. Every dish is presented with as much care and finesse as The Lodge at Primland's restaurant fare, ensuring that in-room dining is just as memorable.

  - Breakfast \| 7-11AM daily
  - Lunch & Dinner

    Sunday – Thursday \| 11AM – 10PM

    Friday – Saturday \| 11AM – 11PM
  - To order, please dial extension 3977 (276.222.3977)
