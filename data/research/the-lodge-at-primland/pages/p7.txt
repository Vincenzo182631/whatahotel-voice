The Junior Mountaineers Kids Club ignites the innate curiosity and adventurous spirit of our youngest guests during their visit to Primland’s mountain oasis. Kids ages 6-12 embark on a journey through nature-based experiences ranging from pond-fishing to foraging and archery to horseback riding.

### Archery

Target practice is a fun way to engage body and mind in Primland’s spectacular outdoor setting. Try your hand at archery with one of our expert instructors. Archery equipment includes PSE and Bear recurve bows and Genesis compound bows with Easton Jazz aluminum arrows. Primland’s experienced guides will demonstrate the proper grip and stance.

  - 1 hour
  - Available year-round
  - Ages 6 and older
  - Inquire at pri.outdoor-activities@auberge.com

### Lodge Balcony Suite Two Queens

Located in the Lodge and boasting some of the property’s finest views, Lodge Balcony Suites feature separate living and dining areas and showcase the same smoky palette as the bedroom, with wood and stone touches referencing the mountains all around.

With their own 248-square-foot decks, Juliet balconies and separate living rooms, Pinnacle Suites offer sweeping views of the verdant valley below. Fairway Cottage Double Queen rooms offer a spacious bedroom, a wood-paneled bathroom with a free-standing soaking tub, a kitchenette and a living room with a gas fireplace all on one level. With two queen beds, deck and private putting green.

### Getting Here Is Simple

Primland’s vast property means it is serviced by two entrances, one in the north and one in the south. The estate’s helipad is located at the North Gate near the Meadows of Dan, which is also the closest entrance to the main Lodge with its restaurants and spa, and The Highland Golf Course. The South Gate near Claudville provides access to the estate’s sports shooting and trail riding areas.
