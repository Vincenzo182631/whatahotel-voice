## From lofty suites and treehouses to luxury residences at one with nature

Consummately private, elegantly appointed and all with exceptional views, Primland’s varied accommodation options reflect the 12,000-acre estate’s abundance of space. Discover the #1 resort in the South through our regal Lodge offering luxurious rooms and suites that overlook the golf course and the Blue Ridge Mountains rolling to the horizon. Or choose a suite in the Pinnacles or Fairway Cottages, ideal for booking multiple neighboring accommodations when traveling with family or friends. Quite possibly the most romantic spaces on the property are the designer Tree House Suites, perched on the lip of the Dan River Gorge and immersed fully in nature.

* * *

## The Lodge

Elegance and style meet premium comfort in the Lodge, which offers easy access to both Leatherflower and the 19th Pub, as well as the spa, fitness center, observatory, theater and golf course. Pause in the soaring Great Hall with its twin fireplaces and linger in the two-story wine room – but it won't be long before you choose to slip away to your own private Primland paradise. Here, in your Lodge Room, Jr. Suite, Lodge Suite or the sumptuous Celestial Suite, world-class amenities await, including deep soaking tubs, impeccable views, private balconies, sliding floor-to-ceiling windows and Frette sheets to make you feel right at home. Accessible rooms are available.

  ### Pinnacle

  Named after the famous Pinnacles of Dan, these chalet-like accommodations offer bird's-eye views of the valley below. Charming and generously proportioned abodes are stylish eyries with serene interiors and space to spare. Suites boast an inviting living area arranged around an open stone fireplace with expansive private decks, and Pinnacle Rooms also offer their own private balconies. Accessible rooms are available.

  View Pinnacle

-

  ### Fairway Cottages

  Located along the golf course with stunning jade and sapphire-hued mountain views, fairway accommodations offer hardwood floors, cathedral ceilings and cozy living areas with Tennessee fieldstone fireplaces. Wooden decks are ideal for alfresco dining, while spacious studios, lofts and suites are offered in east and west wings – catching the light at sunrise and sunset, respectively. Each fairway accommodation boasts a unique layout, sleeping from two to four guests.

  View Fairway Cottages

-

  ### Tree Houses

  Perched in the treetops high above the Dan River Gorge, Primland’s 12,000 thickly wooded acres are home to intimate Tree Houses with sweeping views. Designed in France by renowned treehouse architectural firm La Cabane Perchee – and built of aromatic red cedar – the 440-square-foot Tree Houses with expansive decks feel utterly remote, being at an elevation of 2,700 feet. Tree Houses suit up to two adults, who also receive a complimentary daily continental breakfast delivered to their door and a self-guided wine-tasting experience in partnership with Flowers Vineyards and Winery. Please note: Accessing the Tree Houses requires a four-wheel drive vehicle. For guests who do not have four-wheel drive, complimentary shuttle services are available.

##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is The Lodge at Primland located?** The Lodge at Primland is set on 12,000 private acres in Virginia’s Blue Ridge Mountains near Meadows of Dan, overlooking the Dan River Gorge and surrounded by expansive mountain scenery.

**How far is The Lodge at Primland from the airport?** The Lodge at Primland is approximately a two-hour drive north of Charlotte. Private car service can be arranged through the resort, and guests should contact the team in advance to confirm transportation options for their arrival.

**When is the best time to visit The Lodge at Primland?** The Lodge at Primland is a year-round mountain destination, with each season offering a different way to experience the Blue Ridge Mountains. Spring through fall brings golf, hiking, horseback riding, fly-fishing and other outdoor adventures, while cooler months invite stargazing, wellbeing experiences and time spent enjoying the quiet of the mountain landscape.

**What experiences and activities are available at The Lodge at Primland?** Primland offers an extensive collection of outdoor adventures across its 12,000-acre estate. Guests can hike, horseback ride, fly-fish, play golf, explore by RTV and challenge themselves on the aerial ropes course, alongside sporting pursuits, wellness programming and complimentary activities including guided nature walks, pickleball, croquet and disc golf.

**What is The Highland Course at The Lodge at Primland?** The Highland Course is Primland’s 18-hole championship golf course, set across the ridges and valleys of the Blue Ridge Mountains. Golfers can also refine their game through private instruction, clinics and multi-day lessons, with the course generally open from spring through fall.

**What is the Primland Sporting Club?** The Primland Sporting Club offers a collection of sporting pursuits across the resort’s expansive Blue Ridge Mountain estate. Guests can experience activities including sporting clays, archery and air rifle, with expert guides and instruction available for beginners and experienced participants alike.

**Is The Lodge at Primland a good fit for couples?** Yes. The Lodge at Primland is well suited to couples seeking a secluded Blue Ridge Mountains escape, with expansive mountain views, destination dining, restorative wellbeing experiences and outdoor adventures across the estate. Couples can also choose distinctive accommodations including private tree houses overlooking the surrounding landscape.
