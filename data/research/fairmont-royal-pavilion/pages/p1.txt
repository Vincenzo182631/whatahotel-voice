## YOUR _beachfront_ ESCAPE IN BARBADOS

Fairmont Royal Pavilion is a luxury beachfront hotel located on Barbados’ renowned Platinum Coast. The resort is designed for couples seeking a romantic getaway, honeymoon escape, or tranquil Caribbean retreat. All guestrooms and suites are located directly on the beach, offering uninterrupted ocean views and immediate access to soft white sands and warm turquoise waters. This rare all-beachfront setting makes the property one of the most distinctive luxury resorts in Barbados. Blending understated elegance with personalized service, Fairmont Royal Pavilion delivers an intimate island experience focused on relaxation, privacy, and elevated beachfront living.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Does Fairmont Royal Pavilion have a swimming pool?

Fairmont Royal Pavilion has an outdoor freshwater swimming pool and a heated jacuzzi.

Does Fairmont Royal Pavilion have a fitness center?

Yes, the hotel's Fitness Center is open from 6 am - 8 pm. Guests have full access via use of their room key.

What are some of the property amenities at Fairmont Royal Pavilion?

Some of the amenities available at Fairmont Royal Pavilion include:

- Platinum Fast Track Service – Airport Concierge (available on certain room categories & rates)
- Butler Service
- Non-motorized water sports
- Fitness Center
- Tennis court with flood lights for night play
- High-speed Wi-Fi

Is parking available at Fairmont Royal Pavilion?

Self-parking is available and is free of charge for guests of Fairmont Royal Pavilion.
