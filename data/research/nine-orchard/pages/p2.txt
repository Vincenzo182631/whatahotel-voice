## Discover the details

Offering a quiet retreat from the streets right outside of our doors, each of our residential-inspired hotel rooms abounds with considered details. Original moldings and large windows, which fill the space with natural light, are complemented by a collection of commissioned artworks from local artists and one-of-a-kind accents and furniture; custom-made Ojas speakers and amplifiers are programmed with four New York-inspired stations curated by Devon Turnbull and DJ Stretch Armstrong for our guests.
