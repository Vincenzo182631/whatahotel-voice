### Frequently Asked Questions

Useful details you need to know before you travel to One&Only Reethi Rah

#### What are the passport and visa requirements for visiting Maldives?

All visitors to the Maldives must present a passport valid for at least six months from the

date of travel. Visa requirements for entering Maldives vary significantly from country to

country. For more up-to-date information on visa requirements, please click here.

#### What is the weather like in Maldives?

The Maldives has a pleasant equatorial climate, with two main seasons. November to

April is light and fairly dry. May to October occasionally brings brief rain showers

followed by sunshine. Temperatures range from mid 20°C to mid 30°C (77°F to 95°F).

#### What is the local currency in Maldives?

Local currency is the Maldivian rufiyaa. Whilst all billings are made in US dollars, most

other foreign currencies can be exchanged at the resort. Please note, however, that the

Maldivian rufiyaa cannot be exchanged for any other currency.

#### What is the time zone in Maldives?

For convenience, Reethi Rah time has been set one hour ahead of Malé time, providing an extra hour of daylight at the end of your day; six (6) hours ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT +6).

#### What languages are spoken in Maldives?

The official languages are Dhivehi and English; the resort has multi-lingual staff as well.

#### What is the electrical voltage at One&Only Reethi Rah?

Electricity is supplied at 220 to 240 volts. Adaptors are available in the villas.

#### What internet services are available at One&Only Reethi Rah?

Complimentary WiFi access is available across the resort.

#### What are the check-in and check-out times at One&Only Reethi Rah?

Check-in is 2pm and check-out is 12pm.
