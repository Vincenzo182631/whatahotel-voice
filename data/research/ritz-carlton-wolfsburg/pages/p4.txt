Wolfsburg Experiences and Attractions
Destination and Activities
Set within Volkswagen’s Autostadt, this hotel places you at the heart of Wolfsburg’s remarkable transformation — where industrial heritage meets contemporary design. Nestled along the Mittellandkanal and at the crossroads of key East–West routes, the city was envisioned in the 1930s as a model town for the Volkswagen factory and its workforce. Yet Wolfsburg’s story reaches even further back: the historic Wolfsburg Castle, dating to 1300, stands as one of Northern Germany’s best-preserved medieval landmarks and remains a cultural focal point to this day. Together with the iconic Autostadt, the visionary Phaeno Science Center and the world’s largest car manufacturing plant, Wolfsburg continues to captivate millions — a city shaped by history, innovation and forward-looking spirit.

Enjoy the Best of Wolfsburg
Set within the Autostadt theme park and near the city center, this hotel is perfect for exploring every facet of Wolfsburg. At the Autostadt, car, culinary, art and design aficionados come together to explore impressive exhibits and events. The football club VFL Wolfsburg plays at the nearby VW arena. And the Renaissance-style Wolfsburg Castle houses a gallery and museum.

A World of Mobility, The Autostadt in the Heart of Wolfsburg
The Autostadt in Wolfsburg is a unique destination dedicated to mobility, innovation and design. As the home of the Volkswagen Group, it brings together iconic architecture, interactive exhibitions and immersive brand experiences. Surrounded by water, parkland and cultural spaces, the Autostadt invites visitors to explore the past, present and future of mobility while enjoying dining, events and moments of discovery in an inspiring setting.
Discover More
Enjoy the Autostadt
Discover the Autostadt Pavilions
The pavilions in the Autostadt are a highlight for anyone who values design, innovation, and remarkable architecture. Each pavilion welcomes guests with its own unique atmosphere and offers inspiring insights into brand worlds, interactive exhibits, and impressive installations. If you’re curious and eager to explore something new, the pavilions are a must-see during any visit.

Autostadt Fun and Inspiration
The exhibition LEVEL GREEN – The Concept of Sustainability examines various dimensions of sustainability and uses architecture to display the content in an interactive and playful way. Situated on approximately 1 000 m², the ‘experiential space Sustainability’ is an interplay between a walk-in sculptural installation, divided into six themed areas, and the 28 exhibits within it.
Discover More
Autostadt Car Tower Tours
The tower rides at the Autostadt offer a unique experience for visitors of all ages. On a guided tour, guests can explore the fully automated Car Towers up close, which store up to 800 vehicles. At 48 meters high, with the world’s fastest automated parking system, the towers even hold a Guinness World Record. Visitors also learn a lot about the technology, logistics, and fascinating operation of the towers.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-10:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Not Applicable, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg have a pool on-site?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg have an on-site spa?
Yes, Kraftwerk - Spa is located on-site at The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg.
