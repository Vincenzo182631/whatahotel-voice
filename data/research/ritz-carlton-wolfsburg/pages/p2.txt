The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg
The redesign of The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg by Elliott Barnes is a contemporary update of the original design concept created by Andrée Putman. Exquisite quality, perfect craftsmanship, an extensive art collection and personalized service by The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg staff make staying at this hotel a truly exceptional experience. A luxurious, comfortable atmosphere is instilled through a sense of warmth and security, without superfluity or opulence. Instead of an action-packed stage, the hotel offers an oasis of calm amidst the Autostadt attractions.

The Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge
The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining intimacy, comfort, and exclusivity with personalized service and attention to detail that are the hallmarks of The Ritz-Carlton. This oasis is often described as a hotel within a hotel with a relaxed lounge atmosphere. The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg features a Club Lounge for guests seeking a heightened level of luxury. Surrounded by the finest materials and hand-selected furniture, the Ladies and Gentlemen of the hotel pamper their guests all day long with culinary delicacies, refined cocktails, and personalized service. Our Club Lounge is perfectly located on the top floor with a view of the beautiful theme park of the Autostadt.
