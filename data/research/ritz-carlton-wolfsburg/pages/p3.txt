Experience Fine Dining in Wolfsburg
Dining
The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg invites you to embark on a culinary journey that delights all the senses. From relaxed dining to exceptional taste experiences, all of our culinary offerings focus on quality, creativity, and craftsmanship. At Terra, Head Chef Fabian Schröter reinterprets modern German cuisine using regional ingredients in a warm and inviting atmosphere. Aura offers a dynamic start to the day with fresh, seasonal breakfast variations, while the Lobby Lounge provides the perfect setting for afternoon tea, light dishes, or cocktails in a relaxed yet elegant environment. Each experience reflects genuine hospitality and a true passion for fine taste.

Afternoon Tea at the Lobby Lounge
Relax during an Afternoon Tea in the elegant ambiance of the Lobby Lounge and enjoy the imposing view of the power plant. Accompanied by your favorite tea, we will serve you freshly baked scones, English tea sandwiches and delicacies from the patisserie on a lovingly decorated étagère. We kindly ask for your understanding that from now on we can only offer afternoon tea for two hours from the date of reservation.

Afternoon Tea
Every Wednesday to Sunday from 2 PM to 5 PM, we will serve your favorite tea accompanied by freshly baked scones, English tea sandwiches, and delicious petit fours. 50,00 € for adults | 25,00 € for children. Reservations by e-mail at wolfsburg.reservation@ritzcarlton.com or under +49 5361 607000. We ask for your understanding that from now on we can only offer Afternoon Tea for two hours from reservation.
New Culinary Experience at Terra
Starting in April, we are introducing a new culinary experience at “Terra”: a fine dining menu with five, six, or seven courses. You may choose a perfectly paired wine accompaniment – naturally also available as a non-alcoholic option. Coffee specialties, tea, and water are included.
Brunch at Aura
As the year draws to a close and anticipation for the festive season grows, our restaurant “Aura” invites you to a truly special culinary experience. The Advent Brunch offers the perfect opportunity to enjoy the season of reflection in an elegant atmosphere. Indulge in winter-inspired flavors and refined delicacies, and savor a delicious start to the holiday season. 12:30 pm on 06.12.2026, 13.12.2026, 20.12.2026 and 25.12.2026.

Terra
Private Dining
Terra welcomes you with farm-to-table dining. The menu is determined by the season and the story of the ingredients provides the inspiration. The result is fresh, healthful cuisine, featuring vegetarian and vegan options as well as meat and fish specialties. For private occasions or confidential meetings in a secluded atmosphere up to 20 persons, we recommend our private dining room Compartment. Plan your event with us!
