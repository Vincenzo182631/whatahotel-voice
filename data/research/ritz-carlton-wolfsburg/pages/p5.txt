Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg
Festive Season
Celebrate the most wonderful time of the year at The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg. From festive brunches and exceptional dining experiences to seasonal Afternoon Tea, our charming Christmas Market and elegant New Year's Eve celebrations. Discover unforgettable moments filled with warmth, culinary excellence and holiday magic.
Autostadt Winter Wonderland (Winterzauber)
Just steps from the hotel, Autostadt's Winter Wonderland invites guests to enjoy a festive Christmas market, outdoor ice skating, seasonal treats and enchanting holiday experiences for all ages.

New Year's Eve Gala at Aura
December 31 | 6:30 PM – 9:30 PM Welcome 2027 with an elegant dinner buffet at Aura.
New Year's Eve Gala: €235 per person Includes dinner buffet, soft drinks, wine and beer. New Year's Eve Gala & Lobby Party: €330 per person. Includes dinner buffet, soft drinks, wine, and beer at Aura, plus soft drinks, wine, beer and selected long drinks at the Lobby Party from 9:00 PM to 3:00 AM.
New Year's Eve Menu at Terra
December 31 | From 6:00 PM Celebrate the final evening of the year with an exclusive seven-course menu by Chef Fabian Schröter and the Terra team. Enhance your experience with access to the Lobby Party. New Year's Eve Menu: €195 per person
Beverages charged upon consumption. New Year's Eve Menu & Lobby Party: €290 per person Soft drinks, wine, beer and selected long drinks available from 9:00 PM to 3:00 AM.
New Year's Eve Party in the Lobby
December 31 | From 9:00 PM Dance your way into 2027 at our exclusive Lobby Party with live music and festive entertainment. €145 per person. Includes soft drinks, wine, beer and selected long drinks from 9:00 PM to 3:00 AM.
Advent Brunch at Aura
November 29, December 6, 13 & 20 | From 12:30 PM. Celebrate the Advent season in an elegant setting with a festive brunch featuring seasonal specialties, live cooking stations, and live jazz music. Enjoy a warm holiday atmosphere accompanied by Champagne, selected long drinks and premium spirits. €189 per person | Children (6–11 years) €119
Christmas Brunch at Aura
December 25 | From 12:30 PM Enjoy a festive Christmas Day brunch with an extensive buffet of seasonal delicacies and refined holiday classics. A relaxed and elegant way to spend Christmas with loved ones. €189 per person | Children (6–11 years) €119
Christmas Dinner at Terra
December 24 | 6:00 PM – 9:30 PM. December 25 & 26 | 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM and 6:00 PM – 9:30 PM. Celebrate the festive season at Terra with an Christmas menu crafted by Chef Fabian Schröter and his team. Featuring regional specialties, whether you prefer meat, fish or a vegan option, each dish is thoughtfully prepared to capture the spirit of the holidays. 3-Course Menu €75 | 5-Course Menu €95
Seasonal Afternoon Tea
Thursday – Sunday | 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM. Indulge in a seasonal Afternoon Tea featuring a selection of sweet and savory delicacies, accompanied by premium teas. €50 per person | Kids Afternoon Tea €25
Goose To Go
Available throughout the festive season. Enjoy a perfectly prepared festive goose to take home, served with homemade red cabbage, potato dumplings, baked apples, glazed chestnuts and rich goose jus. €299 per goose | Includes all side dishes.
Christmas Market on the Park Terrace
November 1 – December 20 | 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM. Experience the magic of the season at our festive Christmas Market on the Park Terrace, featuring winter specialties, mulled wine, and a cozy holiday atmosphere. Exclusive bookings for groups of 25 to 60 guests are available. €60 per person. Includes festive music, Autostadt admission, outdoor heaters, and up to two hours of enjoyment.
