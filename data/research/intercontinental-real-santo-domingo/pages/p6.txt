## FAQs for InterContinental Real Santo Domingo

Does InterContinental Real Santo Domingo have a pool?

InterContinental Real Santo Domingo has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Infinity edge pool in L shape in the heart of the city, located on the 3rd floor of the hotel.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Real Santo Domingo?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Real Santo Domingo for hotel guests.

3 levels of parking. No parking charge for registered guests.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Real Santo Domingo?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Real Santo Domingo.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Real Santo Domingo for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Real Santo Domingo have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Real Santo Domingo has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Real Santo Domingo?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Real Santo Domingo.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Real Santo Domingo?

Check-in at InterContinental Real Santo Domingo is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Real Santo Domingo offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Real Santo Domingo offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Real Santo Domingo from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Real Santo Domingo is conveniently located near Las Americas International Airport (SDQ), approximately 19.88 miles away (32 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Real Santo Domingo?

InterContinental Real Santo Domingo is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- National Botanical Gardens - 3.29 miles away (5.3 kilometers)
- Colonial Zone - 3.11 miles away (5 kilometers)
- Three Eyes National Park - 8.7 miles away (14 kilometers)
- Malecón de Santo Domingo
- Parque Mirador Sur
- Faro de Colón

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Real Santo Domingo puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
