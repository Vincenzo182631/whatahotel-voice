# InterContinental Real Santo DomingoDestination

Step outside and immerse yourself in Piantini, the city’s trendiest district. From the Colonial City to Mirador Sur Park, discover Santo Domingo’s history, culture and modern pulse just moments from our doors.

## Featured Attractions

### Colonial City & Casa de Colon

**Colonial City, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic**

Experience the vibrant history of Santo Domingo at the Colonial City, a landmark showcasing the city's rich heritage. Wander through cobblestone streets and marvel at well-preserved colonial architecture. Don't miss Casa de Colon, offering insights into the life of Christopher Columbus with exhibits and artifacts. Explore several museums nestled within the area, each offering a unique perspective on Dominican culture and history.

Learn More

### Botanical Garden Dr. Rafael Maria Moscoso

**Av. República de Colombia, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic**

Immerse yourself in lush greenery and tranquility at the Botanical Garden in Santo Domingo. Spanning acres of meticulously landscaped grounds, this oasis showcases an impressive collection of native and exotic plant species. Stroll along shaded pathways, admire colorful blooms, and relax amidst natural beauty. With educational exhibits and themed gardens, the Botanical Garden offers a delightful escape from the hustle and bustle of the city.

Learn More

### Malecon de Santo Domingo

**Avenida George Washington, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic**

Experience the vibrant energy of Santo Domingo's waterfront promenade, Malecon de Santo Domingo. Stretching along the Caribbean coast, this iconic esplanade offers stunning views of the ocean and city skyline. Take a leisurely stroll, jog, or bike ride along the scenic pathway, dotted with parks, sculptures, and cafes. As the sun sets, the Malecon comes alive with street performers and a lively atmosphere, making it a favorite spot for locals and visitors alike.

### Parque Mirador Sur

**Av. Mirador Sur, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic**

Escape to nature at Parque Mirador Sur, a sprawling urban park offering recreational activities and breathtaking vistas. Spanning lush greenery and rolling hills, this expansive park provides an ideal setting for picnics, jogging, and outdoor sports. Explore winding trails, tranquil ponds, and shaded groves, perfect for relaxation and unwinding. With playgrounds and recreational facilities, Parque Mirador Sur is a popular destination for families.

Learn More

### Faro de Colon

**Av Mirador del Este, Santo Domingo Este 11604, Dominican Republic**

Discover a beacon of history at Faro de Colon, a monumental lighthouse overlooking the Caribbean Sea. Built in honor of Christopher Columbus, this iconic landmark offers panoramic views of the coast and city skyline. Explore the interior museum, featuring exhibits on maritime history and Columbus' voyages. Climb to the top for sweeping vistas, especially spectacular at sunset. Faro de Colon stands as a tribute to the explorer's legacy and a must-visit attraction in Santo Domingo.
