Elevate your stay in Santo Domingo with our upscale amenities. Rejuvenate at SPA InterContinental, take a dip in our rooftop infinity pool with stunning views, stay active in our gym, and savour delectable dining in our restaurants.

## Poolside Leisure, Perfectly Framed

Soak in sweeping city views from our outdoor pool—an inviting retreat where open skies, refined surroundings, and effortless comfort come together. Whether basking in the sun or unwinding at day’s end, every moment feels distinctly elevated

### Spa InterContinental

Rejuvenate at SPA InterContinental, where massages, facials, and body treatments await. With six treatment rooms, two Finnish saunas, two Turkish baths, a Jacuzzi, and an infinity pool boasting panoramic views, our luxury spa offers the ultimate escape.

## More Services & Facilities

### Fitness Center

Step into a world of opulent wellness with our state-of-the-art fitness equipment curated to elevate your workout routine. Our 24/7 fitness center shares a floor with our pool and spa, boasts unparalleled amenities, natural lighting, and panoramic views.

**Hours of Operation:** Open 24hrs.

### Outdoor Pool

Take a dip in our spectacular infinity-edge pool with stunning vistas or soak in a soothing Jacuzzi. Relax in a cabana with a beverage or light bite from Blu Pool Bar while you watch your kids swim and play in the sun.

**Hours of Operation:** 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM

### Complimentary Parking and Valet Parking

Enjoy complimentary parking at our hotel with three levels of convenient parking options and a capacity for 229 vehicles. Registered guests are exempt from any parking charges.

### Gift Shop

Discover a curated selection of treasures and essentials at our boutique-style gift shop. Find the perfect memento to commemorate your stay with us, a unique souvenir, or a practical necessity for your travels.

### Executive Lounge

The Executive Lounge at InterContinental Real Santo Domingo is your exclusive retreat! Enjoy breathtaking Caribbean Sea views, complimentary breakfast, and evening cocktails. Perfect for business and leisure travelers, it offers a serene atmosphere for relaxation or meetings. Experience luxury and personalized service during your stay!

### Golf

- ##### Las Lagunas National Golf Club

Located off-site

**Number of holes:** 18

The "Las Lagunas" National Golf Course has exceptional design features that make the most of the varied topography, with sparkling lakes and surrounded by mountains abounding in exuberant tropical vegetation.

##### Santo Domingo Country Club

Located off-site

**Number of holes:** 18

This golf course was created in 1946 by H.N. Hansard. Located in the most exclusive area of Santo Domingo, this private green receives external visitors during weekdays.

##### Naco Golf and Country Club

Located off-site

**Number of holes:** 18

It is a splendid golf residential community conceived on over 3,400,000 m², offering an exclusive lifestyle with villas, luxury apartments, a clubhouse, stores, restaurants and more. This residential host is one of the newest golf courses in the area.
