# Welcome to The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort

## Paradise awaits at our Bora Bora Resort

Escape to an unforgettable five-star luxury getaway on the water at The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort. Stay in the largest overwater villas in French Polynesia and the South Pacific, starting at 1,550 square feet, and revel in the turquoise blue waters visible from the glass-panel floors. For ultimate privacy, reserve a secluded beach villa surrounded by palm trees. No request is too small or unattainable for our Butlers. The Signature St. Regis Service is available 24 hours a day. Sail from our Bora Bora luxury resort through azure waters, or dive under to meet blacktip reef sharks and rays up close. After your island adventure, swim in our Lagoonarium, work out in the fitness center, or unwind with a spa treatment. Order a cocktail at the swim-up bar, or sample international and Polynesian-inspired cuisine at our four restaurants. Our luxury Bora Bora hotel offers an awe-inspiring backdrop for destination weddings, honeymoons, and corporate retreats.

Celebrate the Festive Season in Luxury

Experience the magic of winter at The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort & Spa this holiday season. Indulge in luxury, honor family traditions, and enjoy delightful festivities. With our full board option, you can relax and create precious memories with loved ones during an unforgettable beachside escape.

The Ultimate St. Regis Bora Bora Resort Experience

Offering an incomparable experience of bespoke luxury, this 1,200 square meters (13,000 square feet) private compound is enclosed on three sides by coral walls and opens up to its own private beach facing Bora Bora’s sparkling lagoon. Inside, an entrance lobby, 3 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms, a dining room and lounge with adjoining pantry, an office and a solarium surround a central open-sky courtyard with a pool tiled in light-reflecting white glass mosaic.

Exotic Accents

Sculpted columns of lustrous exotic wood line the internal walkways bordered by lush tropical vegetation. Textures blend indoors as Venetian stucco wall treatments, Italian marble, noble woods, woven fibers and rich fabrics subtly mix with fine Polynesian art accents.

The separate master bedroom pavilion features a whirlpool, sauna, and steam room as well as a private gazebo. ### Planning Your Journey to Bora Bora Starts Here

Traveling to Bora Bora, French Polynesia begins with a flight to Tahiti and a layover near the airport. Next, a flight from PPT to BOB Bora Bora Airport, Motu Mute Airport. There are some direct flights to PPT Faa'a International Airport.

Consider staying near Papeete, the heart of Tahiti and explore the famous Municipal Market. Bring cash or convert your currency to CFP franc (XPF) as many of the taxis and vendors on Tahiti do not take credit cards.

The final leg of your journey is a short scenic flight from PPT to BOB. Upon arrival, you will be welcomed and transferred by boat to The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort.

Summary: Your home airport > PPT (Tahiti) > BOB (Bora Bora) > Boat transfer to the resort.

For any questions, please contact our Pre-Arrival Concierge: borabora.econcierge@stregis.com

From Home to Tahiti

Flights to Tahiti depart from around the globe, including direct routes from the United States, Australia, New Zealand, Chile, and Japan.

The Islands of Tahiti are easily accessible by air from the United States, with a flight from Los Angeles to Tahiti taking just over 8 hours. Many airlines operate direct flights to Tahiti from the US mainland, including:

United Airlines and French Bee from San Francisco

Air Tahiti Nui and Air France from Los Angeles

From Tahiti to Bora Bora's Airport

The Tahiti-Faa’a International Airport is the only international airport in French Polynesia. From here, you can hop on connecting flights via Air Tahiti to explore the diverse regions that make up this pristine island nation.

Once you arrive at Tahiti-Faa’a International Airport, the domestic terminal for Air Tahiti is located right inside. Air Tahiti provides inter-island flights throughout French Polynesia to the other islands and atolls. For schedules and information, check with the airlines.

From Bora Bora’s Airport to the Resort

Bora Bora has a very small airport which basically consists of one partially open-air building on a small offshore islet.

Once you step off the airplane, a very short walk will lead you to the airport’s main building, where our Airport Greeter will be waiting for you at the St. Regis desk.

You will be assisted in retrieving your luggage, prior to embarking on the boat that will escort you to the St. Regis Bora Bora Resort. The transfer itself is a 20-minute boat ride.

Shuttle Service

The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort is located on a motu (islet) and boat transfers will be necessary from/to Bora Bora Domestic Airport or to reach Bora Bora main island (Vaitape).

**From/to Bora Bora Domestic Airport**

20 minutes, Shared airport transfer

XPF 8,600 – One way transportation

XPF 17,200 – Round trip transportation

**From/to St. Regis Base on the Main Island**

10 minutes, shared transfer

The Resort extends a once-a-day complimentary shuttle service to the main island and the village of Vaitape, except on Sundays and public holidays.

Reservations with the Concierge Desk are mandatory.

Additional shuttles to the main island are available subject to a charge.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort?

The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort is Bora Bora Airport (Motu Mute Airport) (BOB). BOB is located approximately 9.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort have an airport shuttle to Bora Bora Airport (Motu Mute Airport) (BOB)?

Yes, The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort has an airport shuttle to Bora Bora Airport (Motu Mute Airport) (BOB).

Does The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
