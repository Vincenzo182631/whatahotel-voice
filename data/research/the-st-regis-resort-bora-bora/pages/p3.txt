## Dining

Embark on a culinary journey at The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort, featuring four exceptional restaurants. Savor modern French Polynesian cuisine, flavorful Italian dishes, vibrant Asian flavors, and a variety of international delights, all crafted with fresh, island-sourced ingredients. Enhance your experience with drinks at the swim-up bar or the 727 Lounge Bar, offering breathtaking sunset views over Mount Otemanu. Celebrate special occasions with candlelight dinners in secluded settings such as the Spa Beach or Main Beach, or enjoy 24-hour in-villa dining. Dinner reservations for our Bora Bora beach restaurants are highly recommended. Please note that restaurant days and hours may vary; contact the resort for updates. For assistance, reach out to our E-Concierge at borabora.econcierge@stregis.com.

In-Room Dining

Not feeling like a sit-down dining experience tonight? No problem. At The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort, a full in-room dining menu is available 24 hours a day for your convenience.

What restaurants does The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort have on property?
