Polynesian Taurumi Massage

Discover the healing benefits of monoï oil at our spa. This traditional Tahitian beauty secret is used in full-body relaxation during the Polynesian Taurumi massage. This relaxing medium-pressure single or couples massage uses traditional Polynesian techniques and dancing forearm movements. The therapist uses her "mana", which means inner strength in Tahitian, to help your mind and body to relax.

Outdoor Candlelit Massage

Experience deep relaxation at sunset beneath a beachfront gazebo at our Bora Bora spa resort. Indulge in a candlelight outdoor massage, whether for personal rejuvenation or a shared moment with a loved one.

Balneotherapy

Add balneotherapy to your massage and enjoy an effective and pleasant treatment that promotes cellular oxygenation, vasodilation, and blood circulation stimulation. This choice also enhances the benefits of your subsequent treatment. Alternating between soothing and toning, bubbling water is released, gently brushing against and massaging the body.

Private Wellness Area

Before your massage or spa treatment, unwind in the private whirlpool and steam room of our couples’ suites.

## Fitness Club

Our Fitness Center is equipped with state-of-the-art strength-training equipment from Technogym, along with ergonomically advanced machines designed to meet your individual training needs.

### The Lagoonarium

Discover our private Lagoonarium in Bora Bora, a protected underwater sanctuary home to a vibrant diversity of tropical fish and coral, including the remarkable Napoleon fish. Accessible via several pontoons, it offers effortless snorkeling at your own pace. Three times a week, a marine specialist invites you to experience the Lagoonarium Discovery by Natura Ora.

## Family Experiences

As part of the St. Regis Family Traditions program, families are invited to share a curated selection of experiences while discovering the cultural richness of the destination together. We look forward to creating unforgettable moments for you and your loved ones.

Family Amenities & Services

At The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort, every moment becomes a treasured memory. Family Traditions offers captivating activities and thoughtful attention tailored to children of all ages and their families. Delight in welcome amenities and turndown rituals designed to reflect the uniqueness of our family-friendly destination in French Polynesia, including delectable treats, child-sized robes and slippers, as well as step stools for little ones. Throughout your stay, our team will be pleased to recommend and arrange recreational activities, enriching experiences, dining options, childcare services, and other personalized conveniences for your family.

##### Recreation

Open to guests of all ages, the Main Pool provides an ideal setting for families to spend time together. The beach is also accessible to guests of all ages. Our activities coordinator can arrange engaging experiences such as snorkeling, as well as complimentary use of non-motorized water equipment, including paddleboards and kayaks.

##### Dining

Guests may choose from four restaurants. Menus designed to appeal to all ages are available across all dining venues.

##### Nanny & Babysitting

Our Concierge maintains a list of qualified babysitters available to care for your children in the comfort of your guest room. Please contact us for assistance in arranging nanny or babysitting services prior to your arrival or during your stay. Our Concierge will be pleased to assist at any time.

##### Necessities & Provisions

Our resort provides a selection of family necessities, including cribs and child-sized bathrobes. Allow our St. Regis Butler Service to assist you in preparing these amenities prior to or during your stay. Please contact the E-Butler for any special arrangements.

## St. Regis Bora Bora Resort Art Exhibition

Alain Despert, renowned for his vibrant and expressive works inspired by the beauty of Bora Bora, rose to fame with his iconic Absolut Despert series — joining the ranks of Warhol and Haring. Although the artist has since passed away, his legacy lives on through his colorful pieces, beloved by collectors around the world. His art is showcased at The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort, in the Lagoon, Bamboo, Farniente and Te Pahu restaurants, as well as in the reception area. For more information, please contact our Concierge at bobxr.concierge@stregis.com or visit Despert.com.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Bora Bora Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
