# Cabins    in Montana

## All-Inclusive Amenities at The World's First Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star Ranch.

Nestled into mountains and meadows, The Ranch offers 31 distinct accommodations serve as a restful homebase for resort adventures. Whether you choose a lavish lodge suite, converted Historic Barn accommodation, spacious luxury log home or canvas glamping cabin, each is appointed with luxuries as lofty as our five ranch peaks. Resort amenities include twice-daily housekeeping services, beverages, snacks, robe, slippers, cuddledown linens, Frette pillows and duvets, wireless Internet access, long-distance landline calls, bathroom kits and curated decor.

Stay in the Historic Barn

### Original Homestead Barn Accommodations

Our original 19th Century barn was lovingly restored into three specialty accommodations that capture Montana’s historic splendor. Often billed as “rustic,” a barn was actually the original rural luxury. It cost more than a home and stored a ranch’s wealth, in the form of livestock, equipment and feed. Originally the homestead’s hay loft, horse stables and tack room, resort accommodations, Loft, Stables and Wrangler, can be booked separately or in concert to host groups of up to 13 guests.

Tour the Barn

Stay in a Private Cabin

### Luxury Log Cabins

Slip away to your private log cabin on the range, where the exceptional comforts of an all-inclusive luxury resort remain at your fingertips. From two-story log homes to a cozy romantic cabin, every luxury log cabin includes ensuite bathrooms, so couples and friends can enjoy private space within larger accommodations. Some of our luxurious log cabins include bunk rooms, ideal for multi-family sleepovers. Boasting exceptional views and trail access, spend your stress-free group getaway tucked into the heart of Montana’s Rock Creek Valley.

Tour Luxury Cabins

Stay in a Canvas Cabin

### Glamping Accommodations

While we can’t take credit for Montana’s breathtaking scenery, we love helping our guests revel in it while glamping on the banks of Rock Creek. Our hybrid glamping structures are hewn of rustic wood and canvas tents. These “canvas cabins,” are designed to be easily removed and have minimal impact on the environment, providing a highly sustainable way to travel. Meanwhile, our Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star resort services and amenities enrich the experience, making it the ultimate luxury glamping adventure.

Tour Canvas Cabins

Stay in the Lodge

### The Granite Lodge

The Lodge’s inviting Great Room and nearby suites are inspired by the great turn of the century railroad hotels and national park lodges. Nine one-of-a-kind accommodations exemplify the West’s iconic heritage with custom-made furniture, woven textiles, frontier antiques and lavish linens. Located on two levels, lodge rooms offer unparalleled access to the crackling fire, pool, jacuzzi, dining room, Silver Dollar Saloon and Spa, while being just steps away from trails that extend across our 6,600 acres.
