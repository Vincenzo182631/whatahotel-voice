Our Homes on the Range

### \# Luxury Cabins in Montana

View Rates & Availability

From two-story log homes to a cozy single-bed romantic cabin, our luxury log cabins are your private home on the range. At The Ranch you can enjoy a luxury cabin with all-inclusive dining, housekeeping, and Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star luxury amenities, ensuring a stress-free family vacation or group getaway to Montana.

Our luxury log cabins are designed in Western style and filled with antiques, textiles and art. Some cabins, like Sara Jane’s Cottage and Eagle’s Perch, include bunk rooms, ideal for multi-family sleepovers. Every cabin includes ensuite bathrooms, so couples and friends can enjoy private space within the accommodation.

## What’s Included With Your Five-Star Stay?

- Twice-daily housekeeping and in-room luxury amenities
- Daily scheduled activities on the property— see our full list of activities here.
- Farm-to-table Ranch cuisine: breakfast, lunch, social hour, and dinner. Coffee, tea, and pastries are also available throughout the day
- Evening entertainment in the Silver Dollar Saloon, including billiards, poker table, shuffleboard, karaoke, table tennis, private movie theater, board games, four lanes of bowling and outdoor s’mores roasting over a bonfire.
- Access to the fitness center, pool and hot tub.
- And more!

3 Guests Max,950 Sq Feet

Bluebird is a whimsical and well-appointed one-bedroom luxury cabin, ideal for a couple or small family. Whether you’re looking for a romantic vacation or unforgettable honeymoon in Montana, this accommodation offers privacy, space and some truly remarkable amenities.

The primary living space has a king canopy bed and twin pull-out. Its appealing Audubon décor is enhanced by an audiophile-quality sound system and hidden flat screen television. Relax in the quiet sitting area and enjoy the convenience of a small kitchenette. Enjoy a spa-like experience in your eight-person cedar sauna, large clawfoot bathtub, or rainshower.

A heated, screened porch opens the cabin to the sounds of Rock Creek all year long, or go a few steps further and delight in a morning fly fishing session.

### Bear House

12 Guests Max,3329 Sq Feet

Sit in a rocking chair and look over sweeping views of the Rock Creek Valley. The spacious landscape mirrors the spacious feeling inside this accommodation. Bear House is an accommodation you won’t find at other luxury resorts in Montana. Taking a bold cue from our state animal, the living spaces feature authentic ranch accents, like leather chairs, fresh flowers, a stone fireplace and traditional pottery.

This luxury cabin sleeps up to 12 people in six bedrooms, five with an ensuite bathroom, and one with a bathroom just outside the door. Upstairs is the largest suite, with a king bed. In addition, there are two more queen bedrooms. The lower level bedrooms are comprised of a queen and two twin beds/converted king, respectively, each with its own bathroom. In 2023, the garage was converted into a King bed executive suite with ensuite bathroom, including a bathtub and shower. This level also includes a full kitchen, dining room, sunroom, laundry room, wood-burning fireplace, and nighttime stargazing from the back deck hot tub. Rise and hike to Top of the World on hiking trails that pass right by the house and continue to two of The Ranch’s five peaks.

### Sara Jane’s Cottage

12 Guests Max,2500 Sq Feet

Sara Jane’s Cottage is a two-level luxury home inspired by Sara Jane, the daughter of manager Grover Bowles, who helped run the Ranch in the 1940s. It occupies a prime ranch location, across from the Granite Lodge and the swimming pool and with direct access to hiking trails.

On the main level is a spacious bedroom equipped with a king bed, an ensuite bath with a large two-person copper soaking tub and a private deck. The other main floor bedroom has a queen bed and bathroom. The main floor also features a full kitchen, dining area, and living room with a gas stove.

The lower level of the cottage features a bunkroom with two sets of bunks, each with a queen bed on the bottom and twin bed on the top, and a bathroom and sitting area. The fourth bedroom is an additional king suite with ensuite bath. The basement contains full laundry facilities.
