## Western adventure with all-inclusive amenities

The world's first Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star guest ranch

Secluded in one of Montana's most pristine valleys, The Ranch at Rock Creek's 19th-century homestead thrives in balance with its diverse eco-system.

Reserve Your Stay

The Ranch’s award-winning hospitality is grounded in its all-inclusive design, allowing guests to leave their wallets behind to explore five mountain peaks with care-free wonder. Find respite in nature and inspiration in the still serene, still legendary West with a true Montana ranch vacation experience.

### An Extra Night of Montana Magic, On Us

Valid January 13, 2027 - April 30, 2027

The adventure doesn’t have to end too soon. Reserve three or more nights at The Ranch and enjoy an additional complimentary night between January 13 and April 30, 2027. Spend your days snowmobiling, skiing, snowshoeing, or exploring the backcountry and your evenings gathered fireside. However you choose to spend your extra day, it’s our gift to you. Offer valid for new reservations of three or more consecutive nights arriving between January 13 and April 30, 2027. Complimentary night applies to all-inclusive room rate only and must be used in the same stay. Offer subject to availability and cannot be combined with group bookings, other promotions or applied to existing reservations.
