Lodges in Montana

### \# The Granite Lodge

View Rates & Availability

When you pass through our arch, the first thing you’ll see is the Granite Lodge, built and decorated in the style of the great National Park lodges. Step outside to the Aspen-lined courtyard and roast marshmallows over the bonfire. Follow the flagstone path to the Silver Dollar Saloon, Spa and Mercantile.

The Granite Lodge’s ranch accommodations weave luxury, style and authenticity into your Montana adventure. Our lodge rooms are appointed with custom-made furniture, woven textiles, frontier antiques and lavish linens. Design inspiration stems from iconic western themes, such as the great turn of the century railroad hotels and the classic guns that shaped the West.

### Elite Granite Lodge Rooms

2 Guests Max,397 Sq Feet

Elite Granite Lodge Rooms offer sweeping views of the Rock Creek Valley grasslands from their private balconies overlooking the lodge courtyard. Both accommodations offer king beds with ensuite bathrooms within a spacious wood-paneled suite. Springfield can be combined with neighboring Classic Lodge Room Colt to form a two-bedroom family suite within a private corridor. Similarly, Sharps can be combined with Classic Lodge Room Derringer to form a two-bedroom family suite.

### Premium Granite Lodge Rooms

4 Guests Max,396 Sq Feet

Premium Granite Lodge Rooms are spacious and provide exceptional access to the hub of The Ranch. Located on the lower level, Winchester and Remington have high ceilings, king beds and pull-out sofas, to accommodate three guests comfortably. Both offer private access from the lodge courtyard. Remington is ADA-compliant.

Located on the upper level, Appaloosa and Palomino are appointed with custom king canopy beds and offer views of the sapphire mountains behind the Granite Lodge. Mustang is the only lodge room featuring two queen beds, and it is located across from Palomino.

### Classic Granite Lodge Rooms

2 Guests Max,315 Sq Feet

Our Classic Granite Lodge Rooms are appointed with wood paneling and luxury linens. Derringer is situated in the corner of the upper level with a king bed and views of the Rock Creek valley. Derringer may be combined with Sharps to form a two-bedroom family suite with a private corridor.

On the opposite side of the lodge upper level, Colt features either a converted king bed or two twin beds. Colt may be combined with Springfield to form a two-bedroom family suite with private corridor.
