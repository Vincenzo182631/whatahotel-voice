## The Spa at Keswick Hall

Book Now

Mindfully designed to embrace both the wonders of nature and the latest advances in wellness, The Spa at Keswick Hall offers holistic spa experiences for body, face, hair and nails, as well as a refined curation of clean beauty products, apparel and gifts.

The Spa at Keswick Hall offers a selection of holistic spa experiences thoughtfully curated to support your mental, physical and emotional well-being. Deeply inspired by the nature around us, we have partnered with artisanal brands that hand-craft in small batches with passion and integrity. Cutting edge organic ingredients synergistically combine with advanced technologies to deliver optimal results in a highly individualized manner.

Nestled into the edge of woodland and featuring soft interior lighting and natural finishes, the spa’s design encourages tranquility and recovery. A serenity room offers weighted blankets, guided meditation tools and freshly steeped herbal teas. Eight beautifully appointed treatment rooms accommodate couples’ services, massages, body treatments and facials. Hair and nail services are available in our stylish salon and the spa boutique is stocked with a curated selection of clean beauty skin and body care, as well as swimwear, loungewear and gifts.
