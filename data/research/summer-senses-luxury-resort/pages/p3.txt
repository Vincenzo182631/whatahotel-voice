## THEENERGYOFTHEISLAND

Summer Senses is located near the authentic seaside village of Piso Livadi, a place known for its traditional character, relaxed rhythm, and waterfront tavernas shaped by everyday island life. Nearby, the hilltop village of Marpissa offers panoramic views and a glimpse into Paros’ timeless heritage. Just a two-minute walk from Punda Beach, with its soft sand and crystal-clear waters, the resort invites guests to embrace the essence of summer.

Perfectly positioned on an island defined by balance, Paros offers a Cycladic escape where refined comfort meets authenticity and ease. Hidden coves, sandy bays, and clear waters are complemented by a friendly, laid-back atmosphere, welcoming travellers of all ages.

Across the island, contrasts unfold naturally. Quiet hilltop villages sit above lively coastal towns, while animated beaches are balanced by secluded shores where calm prevails.

Visitors from around the world are drawn to Paros for its slower pace of life, far removed from the demands of the modern world, and immersed in a unique sense of peace.

From leisurely meals at family-run tavernas celebrating local produce to exceptional wind and kitesurfing conditions on the eastern coast, Paros offers rich flavours and rewarding adventures, complemented by scenic walking trails.

PAROS

NAOUSSA

Cycladic perfection

Picturesque cobblestoned alleyways, whitewashed balconies with bougainvilleas in full bloom, a vibrant little port filled with traditional fishing boats and dotted with restaurants and lively bars. Overlooking this lovely scene is a Venetian Castle, which stands guard on the authentic character of this island day and night, taking visitors on an irresistible journey with a sapphire-tinted Aegean Sea backdrop. Naoussa, the second-largest village in Paros, is situated on its northern shore and is one of the most sought-after destinations in the Cyclades.

PAROS

PARIKIA

The island’s capital

Discover the island’s shopping centre, where traditional and contemporary meet for an unforgettable experience. The whitewashed shades that are prevalent in all destinations of the Aegean Sea play a starring role here, as do the neoclassical manors which can be found amid its narrow Cycladic alleyways. The East and West meet harmoniously with a 13th century Venetian castle looking on from above, and the 6th century Byzantine church Panagia Ekatontapiliani as one of the most significant monuments in Paros.

PAROS

LEFKES

Where time stands still

It was built during the 15th century in the mountains of Paros, aiming to protect residents of coastal villages from pirate attacks. Until today, Lefkes is one of the most profoundly beautiful villages in the Cyclades, with its traditional character unmarred by the passage of many centuries: beloved island architecture, quaint windmills, whitewashed houses with shutters painted in blue peeking out under a covering of purple bougainvillea, and the timeless shade of ancient trees. Immerse yourself in the sound of cicadas as you stroll aimlessly through winding alleyways, each leading to a destination more timeless than the next.

PAROS

ANTIPAROS

Carefree with an Aegean Sea backdrop

Just across from Paros, Antiparos island has a life of its own beneath the Cycladic sun. It’s a small island of many faces: beaches of serene beauty, cosmopolitan bars and restaurants, as well as places of historical and archaeological intrigue. Take a walk in Kastro, the central residential area of the island, which presents a perfect example for the fortress-like homes that remained after Venetian rule. Tour the remarkable Cave of Antiparos, which holds findings from the Stone Age within, as well as Despotikó, the uninhabited islet with beautiful beaches and the ancient Temple of Apollon and Artemis, where the first ritualistic restaurant of the Aegean Sea was discovered, dating back to the 8th century BC.

PAROS

PISO LIVADI

A charming village

A walk through two small pine forests leads to the picture-perfect port of this small village, filled with traditional tavernas. During the summer, there are daily routes to neighbouring islands such as Antiparos, Naxos, Ios and the Koufonissia via small boats. Days go by at a leisurely pace at Piso Livadi, with visitors lounging on the golden sands of the village’s breathtaking beach, and enjoying sweeping sea views and delicious cocktails at nightfall. A visit to the church of Agios Georgios Thalassitis, which dates back to the 13th century, will introduce you to remarkably poignant paintings.

PAROS

MARPISSA

Authentic Aegean charm

A stone’s throw from Summer Senses Luxury Resort, Marpissa is a gorgeous village built amphitheatrically on the crest of a small hill. Filled with beautiful Cycladic houses hailing back to the 16th and 17th centuries and paved with cobblestoned alleyways, this timeless destination will charm even the most discerning of visitors. The authentic character of this village, castle-like and with interconnecting yards in front of each house, is revealed to visitors during the “Routes in Marpissa” festival which takes place here every August. Local residents showcase the traditions of their home by keeping memories of the past alive through museums and cultural activities.

PAROS

CHRISSI AKTI

Surfer’s paradise

The vast stretch of golden sand and clear, shallow waters which have earned a Blue Flag are brilliant focal features of the largest beach on Paros. Strong winds make it ideal for windsurfers, who coast over the waves with exhilaration, but there’s something for everyone here; it’s no coincidence that this beach is considered to be one of the most beautiful in the Mediterranean. Fully organised and dotted with bars and restaurants, it also features a fully equipped surf club for fans of the sport.

PAROS

KOLYMBITHRES BEACH

Wild natural beauty

50 shades of… blue are reflected in its turquoise waters, as the granite rocks, sculpted by wind and waves, cast their shade upon the sea. The otherworldly views of Kolymbithres Beach instantly capture the gazes of those who visit. This beach has been named after the natural pools which have been shaped over the centuries and look like the holy basins used for performing baptisms in the churches of Greece. You can find Kolymbithres Beach in Northwest Paros, 5km to the west of the bay of Naoussa and at a 10km distance from Parikia. It’s an organised beach, perfect for families with young children as its waters are shallow, warm and protected by the wind.

PAROS

FARAGGAS BEACH

Ultimate relaxation

Nestled beneath a small gulf on the north side of Paros and completely sheltered from the wind, Faraggas Beach is an up-and-coming destination, which has still kept its air of seclusion. Every year, it earns more and more admirers in search of complete and utter relaxation, with powdery sand, clear waters and protection from the occasional gust of wind. Only one beach bar-restaurant and water sport centre can be found in this quiet bay, making it perfect for families.

discover

PAROS

SANTA MARIA & MIKRI SANTA BEACH

An exotic Cycladic destination

Crystalline sapphire waters and soft, golden sand set the scene on one of the most popular beaches on Paros. Santa Maria, on the northeast side of the island, has a distance of 2 km from Naoussa and 15 km from Parikia, and seamlessly combines a cosmopolitan aura of fun with serene ambience. The tiny bays along its length are perfectly secluded; this is also a great surfing destination, easily reachable and completely organised with beach bars, restaurants and water sport facilities. Mikri Santa Maria is a more “low profile” beach, at a short distance from this one but just as stunning.

PAROS

PUNDA BEACH

Crystal clear waters

A mere heartbeat from Summer Senses, barely two minutes away on foot, is Punda Beach. Known for its crystal clear, deep blue waters, it’s a favourite in Paros; modern, cosmopolitan vibes and refreshing cocktails take centre stage at its two beach bars, with mesmerising Aegean Sea vistas sealing every moment in the memory of onlookers. Surrounded by shady eucalyptus and tamarisk trees, the pale sands of this beach are the ultimate destination for unforgettable summer moments.

PAROS

ISLAND TOURS & CRUISES

An explorer at heart?

A range of flexible sailing trips and mini cruises are available on Paros during which you can explore the amazing coastline and the nearby Cyclades. Sail to the secluded bays around the island with an experienced captain & crew to discover secret swim and diving spots! Take a mini-cruise to the southwestern side of Paros, where you will discover some tiny gems known as Panteronisia – small islands surrounded by crystalline waters and golden sea beds. Swim in the famous ‘Blue Lagoon’ before a stop off at the ‘Pirate’s Cave’ close to Trypiti beach for some mesmerising cave diving.

PAROS

WATER SPORTS

A water sports enthusiast?

Try your hand at windsurfing or kite surfing with experts, who will teach you the basics of these popular water sports. Learn how to identify the island winds and the techniques needed to harness them while enjoying all the excitement of the waves.

PAROS

WINE TASTING

A stroll through the vineyards of Paros

Raise your glass! Explore the island’s wine-making traditions by visiting a traditional vineyard as the owner takes you through the whole vinification process from the vine to the bottle. Sample some of the excellent wine varieties, accompanied by local cheeses and delicious delicacies in a lovely al fresco setting.

discover

PAROS

HIKING

Hiking adventures

Paros is a hiking lovers’ paradise, with a 35 km hiking trail network and a wide selection of routes. Marpissa is as an ideal destination for this activity. Follow the path which connects Punda Beach with Piso Livadi and Logaras to savour a 3km route by the sea, with refreshing breaks to take dips in crystal-clear waters. The most daring of adventurers can try the 6 km Punda Beach-Mesada-Tserdakia-Chrissi Akti (Golden Beach)-Drios route, which combines hiking with an exploration of the island’s best-kept secrets.

PAROS

FISHING WITH A KAIKI

In the seas of Paros

Awaken early and prepare for an unforgettable day in the company of local fishermen, who look forward to welcoming you to their traditional “caique” and sharing their years of experience in the art of fishing, whilst showing you hidden destinations on a wonderful journey. Follow them on a typical fishing trip, discover the secrets of their craft and become a fisherman for a day! The experience is completed with a meal of your catch of the day. Whether you opt for a traditional ”kakavia” soup or mouthwatering pan-fried fish, the flavours are sure to delight your palate.

YOGA LESSONS

A wellness devotee?

Connect with the positive energy of Paros in a reviving yoga lesson for all levels. Follow a class led by a professional teacher in serene beach locations where you can enjoy either sunrise or sunset asanas while inhaling the fresh aromas of the island. A personal yoga session can also be arranged on request.
