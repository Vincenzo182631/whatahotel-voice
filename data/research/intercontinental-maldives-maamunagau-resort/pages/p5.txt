# YOGA

RECONNECT BODY AND SOUL

Yoga is a mind and body practice with a 5,000-year history in ancient Indian philosophy. There are various styles of yoga which combine physical postures, breathing techniques and meditation as well as different types of yoga of many disciplines within the practice, all dedicated to the wellness of mind and body.

This is beautiful island of Maamunagau, your wellness journey starts and continues as you stretch, strengthen or meditate in the tranquility of our ocean-facing yoga pavilion and the different scenic points of the island. From Hatha Yoga to Arial Yoga, the endless views provides the perfect setting to unify the mind, body and spirit. Join us and let our skillful Yogi master takes you to through various yoga of your choice as you rejuvenate yourself throughout your stay.

WELLNESS CALENDER

## YOGA

An ancient art encompassing physical, mental and spiritual practices; yoga is the perfect way to unwind and recharge. Our island resort offers a dedicated yoga pavilion and several tranquil outdoor spaces; all designed to allow you to stretch, tone, strengthen or meditate to unify the mind, body and spirit. Allow our resident guru to introduce you this gentle yet effective wellness practice at any one of our scheduled sessions or continue your yoga journey through tailored private sessions.

## AERIAL YOGA

Aerial Yoga incorporates silk hammocks into a traditional yoga class to raise your attitude to a new whole attitude. Aerial Yoga is a fun, challenging workout routine combining aspects of Yoga, Pilates, Aerial Arts and Strength Training designed to increase strength, mobility, balance and flexibility.

Pricing subject to regular or private classes booked.

## FLOATING SOUND THERAPY

Drift into a quieter state with Floating Sound Therapy, a water-based session designed to release tension and settle the senses. As layers of sound move through the space, the body is invited to soften into the support of the water.

This is a slow, immersive practice for those craving calm at the end of the day. Let the sounds rise and fade around you, creating space for rest, release, and a feeling of complete ease.

## AQUA YOGA

Aqua Yoga brings movement into the water, allowing the body to stretch, lengthen, and open with a sense of natural lightness. The support of the water makes each posture feel more fluid, easing pressure while encouraging mobility and breath.

Ideal for guests seeking a low-impact practice, this session offers a refreshing way to move without force. Expect a feeling of openness, renewed energy, and a softer connection to the body.
