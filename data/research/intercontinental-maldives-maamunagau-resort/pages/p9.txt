# TEEN ESCAPADES AT MAAMUNAGAU

ESCAPE THE ORDINARY

At Maamunagau, all ages and sizes are welcome, and we aim to provide a memorable experience for kids, teens and adults alike. Recognising the slightly different requirements for a teen holiday, InterContinental Maldives welcomes teens to explore the Teens Offerings, with dedicated experiences and activities for teens only, including private classes or sessions and fun group gatherings and outings.

## CULINARY MASTERS

You don’t really know where to start or you have already created masterful dining experiences at home for your family and friends - our chef will pick you up right where you are at.

COOKING CLASSES “BASICS TO MASTERS"

From basic cooking ABC to sophisticated dishes or just something local, we got you. Book your cooking class experience for yourself or with your siblings or friends.

MOCKTAIL MASTER

Create your own aperitif cocktail, zero proof of course but just as delightful and chique.

ICE COFFEE & ICE TEA MASTER

It is hot out there, isn’t it? Our barista will be happy to assist cooling you down.

## WATER BENDERS

Finally, you are ready to dive deep into the ocean, to explore the secrets and magic that lies beneath the surface.

CATAMARAN CAPTAIN

Before you become the captain of your own sailing boat, start with the Catamaran certification right here and now.

DIVER CERTIFICATION

First things first, get your diver certification to be able to visit the various dive sites, all with something different to uncover.

WATERSPORTS PRO

5 days, 5 skills. Secure your water sports pass with this fun and rewarding compilation of motorized watersports.

## BLUE WATER ADVENTURES

Our island is blessed with a large lagoon which surrounds the entire island and is home to several snorkelling spots, thriving with diverse fish, black tip reef sharks, dolphins and turtles.

## GLOW & FIT

Immerse yourself in the ultimate wellness experience with the "Glow and Fit" program at Intercontinental Maldives.

KICK BOXING

To try it out or to turn that steam into fun, meet our personal trainer in the gym and give it a go. The session will be adapted to your age and level of experience.

YOUNG SPA

Experience the ultimate in pampering and wellness with a selection of luxurious spa treatments, each tailored to promote deep relaxation and rejuvenation.

## CULTURAL IMMERSION

At Intercontinental Maldives, we invite teens to embrace a world of discovery and understanding through our "Cultural Immersion" program.

LOCAL ARTS & CRAFTS

Maldivians love arts and crafts and many are talented artists who love to share their techniques and will show you how to create this locally inspired
decorations and souvenirs, made from sand, leaf and coconuts.

BODUBERU TRY OUTS

The rhythmic drums you hear for new arrivals or farewells from the island, are more than an arrival and departure ritual, but deeply rooted in the Maldivian culture.

## TEENS FOR NATURE

At Intercontinental Maldives, the "Teens for Nature" program offers a transformative journey that empowers young guests to become stewards of the environment, ensuring a sustainable future for generations to come

MANTA TEEN TALK TEA

You will definitely want to know everything manta ray before you depart from the island.

CLIMATE WARRIORS

Environmental awareness starts with education, and that doesn’t have to be too boring or set in a classroom.

## JUST TEENS GROUP ACTIVITIES

At Intercontinental Maldives, we invite teens to create lasting memories and forge friendships in an idyllic paradise where every moment is filled with fun, learning, and adventure.

AQUA AEROBIC

Before it is time to get ready for dinner, join our personal trainer in this fun Aqua Aerobic class – no worries, strictly teens only!

FISHING TRIP “TEENS ONLY”

Hop aboard our fishing vessel with your siblings or new found friends and find out who will catch the most fish or maybe the biggest one.

DANCE TOK

Ready to move, ready to show! Follow the dance moves of our instructor, inspired by the latest TikTok trends.

PEACE OF MIND

Take a breath, relax and let go. This ‘peace of mind’ meditation is meant to give you a moment. A moment for yourself to listen to your inner being,

TEEN BBQ

Enjoy a blissful evening at the beach with your friends and join this fun BBQ right at the Lighthouse beach.

GRILL & CHILL

The specially curated BBQ on a secluded island will easily become the highlight of your Maldives holidays.

MOVIE MANIA

A movie night at the beach! Is there anything more epic? Decide on a movie with your friends and enjoy an evening with popcorn and soft drinks, with
your feet in the sand.

POSTURE YOGA

All these hours sitting in a classroom, while your body is still growing and forming,

AERIAL YOGA

Aerial yoga does not just help you to practice your balance and core strength, but also give a fun upside down perspective on things. Give it a try.
