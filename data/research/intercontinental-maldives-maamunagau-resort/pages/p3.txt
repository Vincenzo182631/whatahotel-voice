# RECREATION

AN ISLAND DESTINATION LIKE NO OTHER.

The Maldives is an archipelago of 1200 islands grouped into 26 atolls dotted across 871 kilometres in the Indian Ocean and is one of the most dispersed countries in the world, where only 200 islands are inhabited by Maldivians, less than 140 islands are tourist resorts, and the remaining islands are left untouched or are partially submerged underwater.

The coral reef systems of the Maldives are the seventh largest in the world, and the fifth most divers ecosystem of the world’s reef areas. A paradise for underwater explorers, the Maldives reefs are exceptionally rich in marine life and home to 1,100 different marine species including, 15 species of sharks, 16 species of rays, 21 species of cetaceans (whales and dolphins), and 5 species of marine turtles. All of which are protected under Maldivian law.

Discover more about this unique island destination as you create your own adventure through a range of recreational and educational activities and join us as we play our part in preserving our island home for generations to come.

## PADDLE TENNIS

Discover the excitement of padel tennis at InterContinental Maldives Maamunagau Resort, where friendly competition meets laid-back island living. Perfect for couples, friends, and families alike, guests can enjoy complimentary access to the court, with rackets, balls, towels and water provided for a seamless experience.

## SWIM WITH NURSE SHARKS

Discover a guided snorkelling experience just moments from Maamunagau Island, where nurse sharks are often seen gliding through calm, crystal-clear waters. Learn about these gentle, bottom-dwelling sharks while observing them respectfully in their natural lagoon habitat.

LEARN MORE

## HANIFARU BAY

Jewel of Baa Atoll's UNESCO Reserve. Home to the world's largest manta ray gatherings. Visit during New Moons and Full Moons(June-November) for a plankton-rich spectacle with over 100 mantas and whale sharks

## HOUSE REEF SNORKELLING

Explore our vibrant house reef just steps from the beach, teeming with diverse coral species. Enjoy active marine life in the early morning and evening, while afternoons reveal the reef's bright colors.

## SANDBANK EXPERIENCES

Whisk your loved one off on a once-in-a-lifetime experience as you escape to a sandbank surrounded by the crystal clear Indian Ocean.

## PRIVTE YACHT EXPERIENCE

Escape beyond the shoreline on your own luxury yacht, where time slows and the horizon feels endless. With personalised service, fine pours, and chef-curated bites, the result is island living at its most exclusive.

LEARN MORE

## DOLPHIN CRUISE

Set sail on a dhoni cruise in search of playful dolphins. Witness their acrobatic displays or watch them gracefully surf near our boat in their natural habitat

## MAAMUNAGAU LAGOON

Witness the majestic reef manta rays during Northeast Monsoonal months (Dec-April), as they feed in Maamunagau Lagoon. This lagoon also serves as a vital nursery ground for juvenile mantas.

## TURTLE NESTING

Maamunagau Island is a haven for sea turtles. We protect their nests and witness the incredible journey of hatchlings to the ocean. These turtles, born on the same island, return years later to complete the circle of life

## DIVING

Dive into crystal-clear waters and explore stunning coral reefs, colorful fish, and the enchanting beauty of underwater ecosystems. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced diver, the resort offers expert guidance and tailored excursions, ensuring unforgettable experiences amidst breathtaking scenery. With every dive, you’ll encounter the Maldives’ rich biodiversity, making it a true haven for ocean enthusiasts.
