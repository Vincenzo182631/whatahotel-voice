# LUXURY FAMILY RESORT IN MALDIVES

BEST FAMILY-FRIENDLY RESORT MALDIVES

Discover the perfect blend of luxury, adventure, and serenity at InterContinental Maldives Maamunagau Resort. Whether you're exploring the captivating marine world of the Indian Ocean, bonding over family activities, or simply unwinding on the beach, our resort guarantees an unforgettable experience for every member of your family.

WHERE FAMILIES COME TOGETHER TO CREATE TREASURED MOMENTS AND UNFORGETTABLE ADVENTURES

## PLANET TREKKERS PROGRAMME

FOR OUR YOUNG EXPLORERS

Planet Trekkers is a youngster’s paradise for discovering the wonders of the Indian Ocean and island living. Through games, arts and crafts and mini expeditions, budding marine biologists and curious explorers from ages 4-12 can connect with the magic of the island while cultivating long lasting friendships.

## TEEN ESCAPADES AT MAAMUNAGAU

ESCAPE THE ORDINARY

At Maamunagau, all ages and sizes are welcome, and we aim to provide a memorable experience for kids, teens and adults alike. Recognising the slightly different requirements for a teen holiday, InterContinental Maldives welcomes teens to explore the Teens Offerings, with dedicated experiences and activities for teens only, including private classes or sessions and fun group gatherings and outings.

DISCOVER MORE

## MAKING MEMORIES

Family Accomodation

Our accommodations are designed with spacious luxury in mind, offering families a serene retreat. Choose from villas and residences featuring modern amenities, private pools, and direct beach access, ensuring everyone has their own slice of paradise.

Dining

Dining at our resort is a delightful journey of flavors catering to every palate. Our restaurants offer kid-friendly menus and accommodate special dietary needs, ensuring family meals are both enjoyable and accessible.

Family Activities

For families seeking excitement, our resort boasts a wealth of activities. Teens can dive into thrilling water sports like snorkeling and paddleboarding, explore lively coral reefs brimming with marine life, or join guided excursions such as dolphin watching and island hopping.

Cultural and Educational Experiences

Families can participate in workshops where they learn about traditional Maldivian arts and crafts such as coconut carving, local weaving techniques, or making traditional handicrafts. These hands-on activities offer insight into the cultural heritage of the Maldives.

Environmental Awareness

The resort promotes environmental awareness through activities like beach clean-ups, recycling workshops, and talks on sustainable practices. These initiatives educate families about the importance of conservation and encourage responsible tourism.

Spa and Wellness

At Intercontinental Maldives, spa and wellness experiences are designed to cater to the whole family, offering relaxation and rejuvenation in a serene island setting. Alongside traditional spa services, wellness activities such as yoga sessions on the beach or in dedicated yoga pavilions are offered. These sessions are designed to promote relaxation, flexibility, and mindfulness for guests of all ages.
