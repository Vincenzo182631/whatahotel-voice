### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have a work space?

No, the rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto?

The largest occupancy room at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto is the KYOTSUKIKOTO ONSEN with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto?

Some rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
