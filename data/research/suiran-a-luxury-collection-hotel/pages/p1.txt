# Welcome to Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto

## Discover relaxation at our luxury hotel in Kyoto

Immerse yourself in the unique, rich and beautiful history and culture of Japan at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto. Set along the peaceful Hozu River in the scenic Arashiyama Hills, our historic...See More hotel in Kyoto welcomes you to an authentic Japanese-style experience. A harmonious blend of a relaxing haven and the modern conveniences of a vibrant city, our luxury Kyoto retreat is perfectly poised to offer access to some of Japan's most important national treasures. With an intimate collection of 39 rooms, including six specialty rooms, this 5-star Kyoto wellness hotel is inspired by traditional Japanese touches and culture, offering a truly enriching and indigenous experience. ## Arashiyama Onsen

17 rooms out of 39 at this Kyoto luxury hotel offer an 'Onsen' hot spring bath in the room for guests' relaxation and wellness. Also, a Private Spa Room is available equipped with an open-air bath for guests who would love to enjoy a bigger bath.

### Kyo-Suiran

Constructed as a summer residence during the Meiji period, Kyo-Suiran evokes a bygone era while embracing modern Japan. Stop by our Kyoto fusion restaurant for washoku cuisine prepared in the French style, using the freshest local ingredients.

## Inspired Cuisine

Located in an over 100-year-old traditional Japanese villa, Kyo-Suiran, a Japanese restaurant near our Kyoto luxury hotel, offers modernly innovated Japanese cuisine inspired by French presentation. The villa was originally built in 1899 by Baron Shozo Kawasaki.

Learn More

## Kyo-Suiran

Nestled amid a majestic garden near our Kyoto hot springs hotel, Kyo-Suiran presents washoku cuisine crafted with innovative French preparations and fresh local ingredients in an iconic setting - a carefully restored, private residence of the Meiji Era.

Learn More

### Cafe Hassui

Located alongside the tranquil Hozu River, near our Kyoto 5-star hotel, a breathtaking view awaits from the over 100-year-old building. Shaded by a lush, natural canopy and a thatched straw roof, indoor and terrace seating areas are surrounded by panoramic views of the enchanting Arashiyama.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto?

The check-in time at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto.

What are the parking options at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto?

On-site self parking is available at Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto. The daily self parking fee is ¥3300.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto booking.

What is the closest airport near Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto?

The closest airport near Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto is Osaka International Airport - Itami (ITM). ITM is located approximately 48.8 KM from the hotel.

Does Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Suiran, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kyoto does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
