## A vibrant gem offering prime access to shopping and culture

Bisha Hotel Toronto, a distinguished member of The Luxury Collection, defines urban sophistication in the heart of the Entertainment District. This boutique luxury hotel offers an immersive experience...See More with 96 exquisitely designed rooms, including exclusive Kravitz Design suites. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame breathtaking skyline views, creating an atmosphere of refined indulgence. Culinary excellence awaits at KŌST, a rooftop oasis, and Akira Back, where Japanese-Korean fusion meets artistry. With a rooftop infinity pool, a sleek cocktail lounge, and personalized service, Bisha is Toronto’s premier destination for luxury, culture, and style.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto?

The check-in time at BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto.

What are the parking options at BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto?

On-site parking is available at BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto. Self parking is available for $67.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $67.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto?

The closest airport near BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto is Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport (YTZ). YTZ is located approximately 2.0 KM from the hotel.

Does BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto have an airport shuttle to Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport (YTZ)?

No, BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto does not have an airport shuttle to Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport.

Does BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
