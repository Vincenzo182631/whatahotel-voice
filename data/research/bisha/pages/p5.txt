BISHA, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Toronto
The Bisha is a solid pick for foodies, creative types, and travelers who like their luxury with a hit of personality.

Give us the overview for this one.
After an ownership change and joining Marriott’s Luxury Collection, the Bisha’s contemporary take on luxury is alive and well. This
Toronto hotel
doesn’t shy away from pushing a few boundaries; case in point, the fleet of suites envisioned by rocker (and designer) Lenny Kravitz. But before you get up to those hideaways, there's a warren of bold, decadent, and highly Instagram-worthy public spaces to enjoy, from the sexy lobby—all black marble and draped velvet—to the areas adorned with pieces from the hotel’s 3,000-piece collection, which includes a fair number of Warhol prints.
Sounds plush. What's the crowd like?
International jetsetters, low-key celebs and rock stars, art buffs, and travelers up for something different—plus, of course, their four-legged friends.
Visitors as soigné as the lobby would lead you to believe. So what about the rooms?
The Bisha's 96 well-sized rooms and suites are all ultra-hip, thanks to Deco armoires, patent leather couches, geometric carpets, vibrant artwork and photography, and playful bar carts that stand in for traditional mini-bars. All have custom-designed beds, oversized windows, and flat-screen TVs that swivel for easy viewing, while the bathrooms, which are swathed in marble, have heated floors. The 14 suites by Kravitz Design up the game with extra-swanky materials, terraces, or living rooms.
The most essential: Is there WiFi, and how is it?
The WiFi is free.
We've got WiFi, we've got Lenny Kravitz-designed spaces—but most importantly, what about the food?
The eponymous (and the first Canadian) restaurant from Michelin-starred chef Akira Back is worth at least one visit during your stay—you'll find excellent sushi, tempura, sashimi, and share plates. On the 44th floor, KOST is a casual Mexican restaurant done up in a shabby-chic beach house style. Cuddle up around the fireplace with a cocktail or fine whiskey in the dimly lit Mister C Bar, and grab coffee, salads, sandwiches, or pastries at French Made, the lobby cafe.
Anything else stand out inside the hotel? What about outside?
There's a large 24/7 gym on the third floor and a narrow infinity pool on the 44th floor. When you want to explore the city, be sure to ask the Bisha's so-called Experience Producers—AKA the hotel's plugged-in staff—for suggestions.
In the end, who is this one best for?
The Bisha is a solid pick for foodies, creative types, and travelers who like their luxury with a hint of personality.
