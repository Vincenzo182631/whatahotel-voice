The city at your doorstep

# Copenhagen,    curated

Discover an exciting array of events and experiences in, around and beyond d’Angleterre. With an ever-changing calendar of seasonal celebrations, cultural happenings and local discoveries, there is always something new to experience – both within the hotel and just beyond its doors.

From our iconic address in the heart of Copenhagen, explore the city through the eyes of those who know it best. Our Concierge Team is at your service to uncover hidden gems, local favourites and unexpected experiences, and their curated guide invites you to discover the many sides of the city we call home.
