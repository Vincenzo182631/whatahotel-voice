## FAQ

Learn more about your stayLearn more about your stay

01. Where is d'Angleterre located?

    d'Angleterre, the only 5- star superior hotel in Denmark, is located in the very heart of Copenhagen - on the city's most prominent square, Kongens Nytorv. From here, many well-known sights can be reached on foot making our luxury hotel the ideal base for exploring Copenhagen, with everything the city has to offer.

02. What is the address of d'Angleterre?

    The address of d'Angleterre is Kongens Nytorv 34,1050 Copenhagen - a prime location in the historic centre of the city. Our central location, and close proximity to public transportation, makes the journey to and from the hotel a smooth ride.

03. How long has d'Angleterre been open?

    d'Angleterre has a long and proud history, dating back to the middle of the 1700s. In 1755 the French servant Jean Marchal married the royal chef's daughter Maria Coppy and they founded a restaurant that later turned into a luxury hotel. This hotel is now known as d'Angleterre and has grazed Kongens Nytorv for more than 270 years. Our luxury hotel is synanons with Copenhagen, and the snow-white facade is a landmark of the city attracting prominent guests, royalties and celebrities, from Denmark and abroad.

04. What is the phone number of d'Angleterre?

    The phone number of d'Angleterre is +45 33120095. If you have any questions, our staff will be delighted to answer your call. We look forward to talking to you.

05. Does d'Angleterre serve alcohol?

    d'Angleterre has an extensive array of restaurant and bar outlets, and alcohol is served in all of them, including Marchal, Marchal Bar, The Upper Lobby, and Balthazar. Balthazar is our Champagne Bar in Copenhagen serving signature cocktails, gourmet snacks, and Champagne from over 42 different houses. Please note that alcohol is only served to people of legal age and that alcohol free options are always available.

06. Can you smoke inside d'Angleterre?

    Smoking is prohibited in all rooms of d'Angleterre, including public spaces like restaurant and bars.

07. Is room service available at d'Angleterre?

    To set the standard of a 5 star luxury hotel, room service is available at d’Angleterre around the clock. Our room service menu features everything your heart desires, from Champagne and snacks to main courses and sweet treats. If you are in need of anything that is not on the menu, let us know and we will be happy to assist you.

08. Does d'Angleterre provide baby cots or high chairs?

    At d’Angleterre, we love children and do everything in our power to make it a memorable stay for everyone – big and small. Therefore, we offer a wide selection of baby and children amenities at the hotel, for example a baby cot, high chair, baby bathtub, potty, changing mat, toys, and much more. Please let us know in advance, so we can create the perfect setup for your little one.

09. Is currency exchange available at d'Angleterre?

    We offer currency exchange at d’Angleterre. We are able to exchange Euros, British Pounds, US Dollars, and Swiss Francs for Danish Kroner.

10. Can I store my luggage before check-in or after check-out at d'Angleterre?

    The city is best explored without the added weight of your luggage. Therefore, d’Angleterre is happy to store your luggage before check-in or after check-out. Please rest assured that your belongings are safe with us.

11. Does d'Angleterre offer concierge services?

    d'Angleterre has a knowledgeable concierge team, always on hand to help you with restaurant reservations, event tickets, and local experiences. Their job is to provide thoughtful guidance and insider tips to help you make the most of your stay. The concierge team knows the city in detail and can uncover hidden gems and exceptional finds that allows them to craft tailored experiences, transforming a stay into something truly unforgettable.

12. Is d'Angleterre wheelchair accessible?

    d’Angleterre is a fully wheelchair accessible hotel in Copenhagen. The hotel consists of accessible rooms and public areas, and elevators run to all floors of the building.

13. Is there a mobile app or digital check-in at d'Angleterre?

    Digital check-in and check-out is made available to our guests ensuring a seamless experience at d’Angleterre. We also offer a digital compendium listing all amenities and services, available prior to and during the stay. For Leading members, we offer the use of the Leading Hotels of the World app.

14. What forms of payment are accepted at d'Angleterre?

    At d'Angleterre, we accept the following forms of payment. Card: Dankort, Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Diners, China UnionPay. Cash: DKK, NOK, SEK, USD, GBP, EUR, CHF (we cannot accept cash value of DKK 20.000,00 or above due to the Danish law regarding money laundering). We also accept bank transfer and Mobilepay.

15. Are there rooms with a balcony at d'Angleterre?

    A number of our luxury rooms and suites at d’Angleterre feature private balconies with views of Kongens Nytorv or central Copenhagen, allowing guests to enjoy the magical sight of the city's skyline. Several balconies are facing the very heart of the city, making it the perfect spot to watch the city unfold and spot famous attractions like Nyhavn, The Royal Danish Theater or The Marble Church. Please note that not all rooms have balconies.

16. Are there rooms with a private bathroom at d'Angleterre?

    Every room and suite at d’Angleterre features a luxurious private bathroom with high-end amenities.

17. Is there a restaurant at d'Angleterre?

    d'Angleterre's love of gastronomy and great taste spans centuries and is still an integral part of the hotel experience. d’Angleterre is home to the Michelin-starred restaurant Marchal, offering fine dining in elegant surroundings.

    When the weather allows, Marchal extends onto the terrace and sidewalk of Kongens Nytorv, offering guests a lively alfresco experience, ideal for immersing in Copenhagen’s buzzing lifestyle. Maison Is the hotel's pâtisserie, a joyful and pink universe, where guests can enjoy freshly baked cakes, pastries and other refined delicacies. Balthazar Champagne Bar is the hotel's most prominent bar. With its opulent golden décor, the bar offers a selection of more than 200 Champagnes, tapas-style dishes, caviar service, and live DJs on weekends. Further to this, food and drinks can also be enjoyed in Marchal Bar and in The Upper Lobby.

18. Is afternoon tea available at d'Angleterre?

    Artisanal afternoon tea is served in the graceful surroundings of the gorgeous Gallery Hall at d'Angleterre in central Copenhagen. Here you can indulge in the refined British tradition featuring a sparkling twist in cooperation with KRUG Champagne. Krug Afternoon Tea is served on Sundays from 3pm to 5pm, except selected dates. Reservation is required and can only be made online.

19. Are there any historical sites close to d'Angleterre?

    d’Angleterre is ideally situated in the heart of Copenhagen, in the historical part of the capital. Here, you are only steps away from one of the most iconic historical sites, Nyhavn. Due to the central location, many attractions can be reached on foot – for example Amalienborg Castle, The Marble Church, Christiansborg, Tivoli Gardens and pedestrian shopping street Strøget.

20. Is there a deposit required at check-in at d'Angleterre?

    Upon check-in at d'Angleterre, a hold will be placed on your credit card for the full amount of your accommodation (if not prepaid), plus an additional 15% to cover any incidental charges. Please note that a physical credit card with the name of the primary guest, along with matching photo identification, must be presented at check-in to complete registration.

21. Do I need to pay in advance or at check-in at d'Angleterre?

    A credit card is required to guarantee your booking at d'Angleterre; however, it will not be processed until your arrival at the hotel, when the physical card will be presented and verified.

22. What food and drink options are available at d'Angleterre?

    Several food and drink options are available at d'Angleterre. From uplifting breakfasts overlooking the buzzing Copenhagen city square to decadent dinners served with creativity and passion, every dish on the d'Angleterre menu is a work of art. Our food and drink outlets include our Michelin-starred restaurant Marchal, Marchal Bar, Balthazar Champagne Bar, Maison pâtisserie and The Upper Lobby.

23. Is there free Wi-Fi at d'Angleterre?

    Free Wi-Fi is provided at d'Angleterre, so you can browse freely during your luxury stay in Copenhagen. The code to the free Wi-Fi will be handed to you at check-in.

24. What is the cancellation policy at d'Angleterre?

    The cancellation policy at d'Angleterre varies, depending on the time of the stay. In high season, cancellations or modifications are free of charge up to 4 days prior to arrival. In Low Season, cancellations or modifications are free of charge up to 48 hours prior to arrival.

25. How far is d'Angleterre from the centre of Copenhagen?

    d’Angleterre is situated right in the heart of Copenhagen at one of the most central and prominent adresses of the city.

26. Is parking available at d'Angleterre?

    We are pleased to offer convenient parking of your vehicle at d'Angleterre, allowing you to relax from the very moment you arrive. Upon your arrival, our gracious Bellhosts will be waiting to welcome you, assist with your luggage, and carefully take charge of your vehicle and car keys — offering you a seamless transition from travel to tranquillity. If you wish to park in the city, several car parks are available in close proximity to the hotel.

27. What restaurants are close to d'Angleterre?

    Copenhagen is a true gastro destination, known for the famous New Nordic Food Manifesto that continues to shape the culinary landscape, emphasizing seasonality, ethics, health, sustainability, and quality. d'Angleterre is situated in the middle of the gastronomic capital with world-famous restaurants all around. Our Concierge team knows the restaurant scene in and out, and will be happy to help you secure a table at some of the best restaurants in Copenhagen - and indeed the world.

28. How can I get to d'Angleterre from the nearest airport?

    The airport nearest d'Angleterre is Copenhagen Airport and the fastest and easiest way to get to it, is by taking the metro. The metro runs directly to Copenhagen Airport in approximately 15 minutes. The metro stop nearest the hotel is called Kongens Nytorv and is only steps away from the hotel entrance.

29. Does d'Angleterre offer an airport shuttle service?

    The d'Angleterre team is happy to assist you in arranging private transportation with our hotel limousine partners or via taxi. Arrangements should be made by contacting the concierge at least 24 hours prior to arrival or via concierge@dangleterre.com.

30. What are the check-in and check-out times at d'Angleterre?

    Check-in at d'Angleterre is availbale from 3pm and check-out is at 12pm. If you have any specific wishes regarding check-in or check-out, please contact us and we will do everything in our power to accommodate these.

31. What are some of the room amenities at d'Angleterre?

    Amenities vary depending on which room category you are staying in. For our d'Angleterre Suites, room amenities include flowers, Champagne upon arrival plus a selection of welcome amenities.

32. What kind of breakfast is served at d'Angleterre?

    At d'Angleterre, breakfast is served in Marchal restaurant with a spectacular view over Kongens Nytorv, the central square of the city. Our thoughtfully curated breakfast buffet in Copenhagen offers a generous spread of seasonal fruits, freshly baked pastries, artisanal cheeses and warm selections. For a more tailored experience, choose from our à la carte menu, featuring refined morning classics, all served with freshly brewed coffee or a sparkling glass of Champagne.

33. Is d'Angleterre dog/pet friendly?

    d'Angleterre is a pet friendly hotel in Copenhagen and we believe that every member of your family should feel at home — and that includes your cherished four-legged companion. We are delighted to extend a warm welcome to dogs weighing up to 10 kg. For your pet’s well-being and in respect of other guests, we request that pets not be left unattended in the room.

    We provide the following pet-friendly amenities in your hotel room: A cosy dog bed, a bowl for water, and a bowl for food.

    Kindly note that dogs are not allowed in Marchal, our bar area, or Balthazar Champagne Bar, but are welcome in the Upper Lobby.

34. What is there to do at d'Angleterre?

    d'Angleterre consists of 92 luxury rooms and suites located on Kongens Nytorv (King’s New Square), a prime central location in the heart of the city. From the hotel, many major sights can be reached on foot, while entertainment and gourmet experiences also await in-house. d'Angleterre features a Michelin-starred restaurant with French-inspired cuisine, a Champagne bar with more than 200 varieties of bubbles, lavish function rooms, a pâtisserie offering refined sweets and pastries, and a private pool and spa managed by the world-renowned holistic spa & skincare concept Amazing Space.

35. Are there any cleaning services offered at d'Angleterre?

    We offer a variety of cleaning services at d'Angleterre. For example, daily Full-Service Cleaning, complete with tidying, dusting, vacuuming, and sanitizing of all surfaces. You have the option to choose linen change daily or as per standard, every 3rd day. Evening Turn-Down Service consists of the room being refreshed, lights adjusted, curtains drawn, fresh water provided, and bed prepared for sleep. We also offer Laundry/Dry cleaning service, available Monday till Friday, and Saturday till 13:00 (regular and express). Moreover, we offer in-house pressing service, 7 days a week. Regular service will be returned within 2 hours of ordering. Butler service is available upon request in advance.

36. Is there a dress code at d'Angleterre?

    The dress code in our Michelin-starred restaurant Marchal is smart casual. If in doubt, our restaurant team is happy to answer any questions you might have regarding attire.

37. What languages do the staff speak at d'Angleterre?

    d'Angleterre is an international luxury hotel in Copenhagen with many nationalities gathered under one roof. Our primary languages of service are English and Danish.

38. Is d'Angleterre child/family friendly?

    d’Angleterre is a family friendly hotel in Copenhagen, here at our luxury hotel we love children and we do everything in our power to make it a memorable stay for everyone – big and small. Therefore, we offer a wide selection of children and baby amenities, such as cots, highchairs, potty setup, changing mats and much more.
