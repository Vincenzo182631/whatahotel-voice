# Hotel Policies

Resort Fee

A daily resort fee of $55 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

- In-room Nespresso coffee program
- Leucadia Loop transportation service within 3 miles of property
- Weekly sound bath experience
- Print services
- Premium internet access
- Beach access with chairs, umbrellas and towel
- Pool towels and Sun Bum sunscreen
- Daily yoga class
- E-bikes
- 2 welcome beverages
- 2 welcome water bottles
- Unlimited local calls
- 24/7 fitness center
- Surfboard and bike storage
- Surfboard and wetsuit rentals
- In-room Bluetooth capability

Pet Policy

This pet-friendly hotel welcomes dogs. Fees are $150 per pet, per stay for up to two dogs per room. Dogs may not exceed 50 pounds each or 75 pounds combined. Additional charges may apply for excessive damage or cleaning. Service animals are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Smoking Policy

Alila Marea Beach Resort is a 100% smoke-free property. A cleaning fee of $500 per day will apply to any documented violations.

Cash-Free Hotel Policy

Please note that this hotel only accepts credit cards, debit cards and, where applicable, other contactless forms of payment. It is a cash-free environment.
