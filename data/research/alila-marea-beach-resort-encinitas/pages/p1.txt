## Beach Life and Rugged Beauty in Encinitas

Steps from sunlit sand, historic Highway 101 and the coastal town of Encinitas, Alila Marea Beach Resort blends seamlessly into the bluffs in an innovative expression of eco design. Tailored for adults seeking a refined coastal escape, this luxurious yet laid back hotel embodies the quintessential Southern California lifestyle, mixing relaxation, elevated experiences and endless adventures from South Ponto Beach and Batiquitos Lagoon to eclectic cafes and boutiques.

## Accommodations

Our 130 luxurious resort hotel rooms and suites are inspired by the natural elements of the Southern California coastline and showcase amazing beach views. Stone, wood and sophisticated décor blend style with a sustainable focus, creating havens of understated elegance, accented with pops of color and replete with modern amenities for your utmost comfort.

## Alila Moments

Shaped by land and craft, by culture and care, Alila Moments reveal a different way of seeing. These personalized experiences offered at our Alila properties draw you closer to the world around you, and your place within it. These are our Moments, crafted just for you.

Explore Alila Moments

Seaside Sun Salutations

Submit to the tranquility and power of the ocean as you cycle through your asanas with the gentle crashing of the waves as your soundtrack. An optional cold plunge ends the experience.

**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Recommended Dress:** Comfortable yoga or athletic attire is recommended. Guests may want to bring swimwear if they plan to participate in the cold plunge. Footwear should be suitable for a short walk to the beach.

**Price:** $145

**Inquire**

**Additional Details:** Yoga & Meditation class runs 50 minutes, with the ability to add on an optional Cold Plunge at Ponto Beach, which should last around 15 minutes. (Note: likely overlaps with or follows the yoga session, depending on guest participation)

Make Meaning from Shell Combing

Be a part of the solution as you help preserve the coast at Agua Hedionda Lagoon. Build oyster wattles, explore scenic trails and discover local biodiversity through a mind-cleansing communal conservation experience.

**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Recommended Dress:** Outdoor casual or comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes are recommended.

**Price:** $95 per person

**Inquire**

**Additional Details:** Guests should be prepared for light manual activity and walking on natural trails.

The Endless Wave

Ride along on an unparalleled surf adventure at our Encinitas hotel. Expert instructors, demo boards, legendary waters and hidden breaks await as you dive into surf culture and support the effort to keep our waters clean.

**Duration:** 3.5 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Swimwear suitable for surfing, plus comfortable clothes for wearing before and after the activity. Wetsuit, towel, sunscreen, and water will be provided, but feel free to bring any product you wish, as well.

**Price:** $550 per person

**Inquire**

**Additional Details:** Guests should be comfortable in ocean water and active movement. Surfboards, wetsuits and towels provided, along with transportation to and from the surf location. Surfing will be facilitated by trained surf instructor and board selection will be tailored to each guest's skill level. Surf break locations are selected based on real-time ocean conditions. Note that this experience is ideal for guests interested in surfing, ocean connection and surf culture and includes guided coaching discussions, hands-on surf instruction and reflective moments. A portion of the cost supports the Rob Machado Foundation, which promotes clean oceans and drinking water.

The Art of Tasting

Our wine tasting guides you on a sensory journey into the complex world of beverages. You'll discover the art of tasting beers, wines and spirits while gaining a new appreciation for mindful sipping. A simple tequila will never be the same again.

**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Recommended Dress:** Resort Casual

**Price:** $145 per person

**Inquire**

**Additional Details:** Guests must be 21+ with valid ID. Not recommended for those abstaining from alcohol. Small bites will be provided to complement tastings, which will take place either in the VAGA Bar or VAGA Private Dining Room.

## Discover Alila Marea Beach Resort Encinitas

Laidback Coastal Scene

Live the fabled California dream of sun, sand and surf at Alila Marea. Surf the best breaks in Southern California, hike the surrounding coastal wetland of Batiquitos Lagoon, and discover an eclectic mingling of sidewalk cafés, boutiques and art galleries amid the relaxed beach town vibes of Encinitas.

Focus on Sustainability

From harnessing the energy of the elements to minimizing our environmental footprint in collaboration with community partners, this eco-friendly California resort places sustainability at the forefront. Our green initiatives include solar power for all pool and water preheating, multiuse water vessels and partnering with local coffeehouse Lofty Coffee to eliminate coffee pods in rooms. Our resort car is a Tesla Model X, and electric vehicle charging stations are available.

Southern California Soul

Alila Marea combines understated elegance with coastal views from nearly every room. Designed to emulate the surrounding coastal bluff, beach and ocean, the resort is framed by natural stone, warm woods and clean lines for a tranquil retreat with a sublime backdrop. We maintain an atmosphere of tranquility with a maximum occupancy of two people per room to ensure the comfort of all guests.

Culture Abounds

Take in the beach-inspired artwork within our walls or discover more culture just beyond our doors. Large-scale pieces by our artist in residence, Aaron Chang, inspire contemplation, while surfboard wall panels by local board shaper Brian Szymanski infuse The Pocket with beachside energy. Venture to Encinitas’ main street to catch a show at La Paloma Theatre, a century-old Spanish Colonial Revival-style theater or explore the San Diego Botanic Garden.
