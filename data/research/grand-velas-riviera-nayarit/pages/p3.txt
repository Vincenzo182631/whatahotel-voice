# World Inspired Restaurants In Puerto Vallarta

Savor chef-inspired world cuisine at our innovative restaurants in Puerto Vallarta. Grand Velas has revolutionized the all-inclusive dining concept with gastronomic eateries that offer diverse global flavors with a creative spirit. Stroll along breezy corridors with colorful plants and koi ponds to beautifully designed restaurants where the flavors of Mexico, Asia, Italy and France come to life.
