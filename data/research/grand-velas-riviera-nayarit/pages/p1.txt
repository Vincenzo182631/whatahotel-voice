## Traveler’s Insights

- Which restaurants are included in the all-inclusive plan? Which restaurants are included in the all-inclusive plan?
All of our gourmet restaurants, bars, poolside swim up bar, and room service are offered in the all-inclusive package at Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit. Whether you are in the mood for a romantic beachfront dinner, creative Mexican, Italian fare, a cold beer while watching sports, or cocktails in the sun, you will enjoy a taste of paradise with our all-inclusive dining concept. Our all-inclusive plan also offers daily drinks and snacks in your suite's minibar.

- Is it safe to travel to Puerto Vallarta? Is it safe to travel to Puerto Vallarta?
Puerto Vallarta is a popular vacation destination and was named the friendliest city in the world by Condé Nast magazine. Puerto Vallarta is also one of the safest destinations in Mexico for international visitors as well as expats. And, according to the World Health Organization and local authorities, there are currently no travel bans to the Puerto Vallarta area.

- What activities can i do at the resort? What activities can i do at the resort?

Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit is the place to play in Puerto Vallarta. Enjoy swimming in the infinity pool and sandy beach on Banderas Bay. Hit the gym and take fitness classes, like yoga, Zumba and Pilates. Head to the Activity Desk for Puerto Vallarta adventures and tours. Our Activities Program offers snorkeling and kayaking, bike tours, and cooking classes. Guests can also enjoy beach volleyball, Bocce ball, tennis, ping pong, water aerobics, bingo, and dance classes. The resort also features evening entertainment, live music in the open-air amphitheater, fiestas, DJs, karaoke, and theme nights that celebrate the spirit of Puerto Vallarta. Designed for young vacationers, the Kids' Club offers daily beach and pool games, nature discovery and arts and crafts, and the Teens' Club offers a lounge for hanging out and playing video games and billiards.

- Does Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit accept children? Does Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit accept children?

Kids are welcome. Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit is a family-friendly resort with daily activities for kids and teens. The resort offers kid-friendly restaurants, a Kids' Club with pool and beach games and arts and crafts, and a Teens' Club with a video game lounge and karaoke. Our tour agent is happy to arrange snorkeling and scuba diving adventures, whale watching and dolphin encounters, and bay cruises for your family and kids. Check the Daily Activities schedule for fun recreation, from bike tours to scuba clinics and Spanish classes.

- Does the resort offer transportation from the Puerto Vallarta International Airport? Does the resort offer transportation from the Puerto Vallarta International Airport?
Yes, at an additional charge. Our resort offers transportation to and from the airport, as well as private VIP service. The service may be reserved with drinks and help with your luggage, and is perfect for families and groups of friends. Taxi service is also available. The approximate driving time from the Puerto Vallarta International Airport (PVR) is 20 minutes with normal traffic. The airport is just 11.8 km, or 7.3 miles, from the resort.
