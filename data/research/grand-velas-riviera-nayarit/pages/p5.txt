# An All-Inclusive Nuevo Vallarta Luxury Resort & Spa

Experience the essence of luxury and the natural beauty of Mexico's Pacific Coast at Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit. From the moment you enter our lobby, you will be greeted with a hibiscus infusion, scented towel and relaxing massage. Rejuvenate at the spa, savor global cuisine, dive into our infinity pool, lounge on the beach or play a round of championship golf. At Grand Velas, you are free to indulge at your own pace. Our "Beyond All Inclusive, Beyond All Compare" concept creates a sublime vacation experience with gorgeous ocean view suites, incredible dining and recreation. Play in paradise with our Activities Program, which sets up snorkeling, kayaking, water aerobics, pickleball, tennis, basketball, beach volleyball, and scuba diving classes, and enjoy live music in our beautiful amphitheater, with fiestas and theme nights that celebrate the culture of Puerto Vallarta. It's all here.

## Kids’ Club

We're full of surprises! To ensure more entertainment for your little ones, we have divided our Kids' Club into two categories: Kids' Club Garden, designed for children aged 4 and up; and Kids' Club Terrace, created for kids aged 6 to 12. Learn More

- Kids' Club Garden

A space specially created for children aged 4 and up, so they can have fun in a safe environment with their parents and our experienced staff. The oversized games will encourage cognitive development and boost their creativity—from Tic-Tac-Toe and Twister to Snakes and Ladders, Jenga, and Connect 4.They can also enjoy our horizontal climbing wall.

In the evenings, there will be an array of exciting activities, including slumber parties, story-telling, camping, and Cinema Pancho, among others.

- Kids' Club Terrace
Children aged 6 to 12 will have fun as never before during their vacation. Let them challenge their abilities with our hockey and soccer tables, table games, and TV programming suitable for their age. If you wish, you can also enjoy this amazing environment with your little ones!

#### Digital Detox

This new program inspires families and couples to power down, disconnect from the world and focus on enjoying time with one another. When guests arrive at the resort, the concierge is tasked with the all-important duty of "cleansing" their suite by removing the flat screen television from the room, replacing it with classic board games and then whisking away their personal electronic devices (phone, laptop, iPad, PSP, etc.) to a safe.
