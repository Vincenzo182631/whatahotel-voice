# Pools

Carefree days are spent drifting from the pool to your lounger, to a soundtrack of waves lapping on the shore and Balearic beats.

Our two outdoor pools, facing the sea and just steps from the beach, allow you the freedom to do what you like at a pace that suits you.

Our adults pool offers oversized day-beds and sun loungers where you can enjoy the lively vibe and island atmosphere.

Close to the Kid’s Club, our more tranquil family pool is a relaxing place for sunbathing and refreshing swims. It is the ideal spot for you to spend the day with your little ones.

Pool and beach areas have sun _chaise-lounges_ and bar full-service menu.

All loungers are complimentary for our guests.

Bali beds around the adults' pool require minimum f&b consumption.

**Main Pool:** 10.29 m x 19.49 m

**Family Pool:**  6.80 m x 27.16 m
