### Juice Bar

Ibizan-Inspired Refreshment: Fresh, Wholesome & Made-to-Order

Savour the vibrant flavours of Ibiza at our open-air Juice Bar, nestled beside the family pool. A tempting spot for something fresh and wholesome, with the sea breeze and views across Talamanca Bay as your backdrop.

Each day, we blend and press a colourful selection of local produce and natural ingredients to order. From refreshing juices and nutrient-rich smoothies to light, healthy bites, everything is made fresh for a delicious boost that feels as good as it tastes.

Whether you’re after a hydrating pick-me-up after a morning workout, a fresh blend between swims or a wholesome treat for the kids, simply choose your favourite and watch it come together before your eyes.

Take a moment, sip something fresh and let the easy rhythm of Ibiza Bay take over.

**Open daily from 8am to 6pm**
