## Wellbeing The Mediterranean Way

Fuel your Ibiza adventures with a touch of zen. Unwind, restore, and discover a haven of wellness at Nobu Hotel Ibiza Bay.

Inspired by the character and colors of Ibiza, wellbeing at Nobu Hotel Ibiza Bay brings together a mix of sophisticated luxury and Bohemian fun. Our range of holistic wellbeing and beauty experiences is designed to relax and revive the senses. Indulgence and serenity cohabit peacefully at Ibiza Bay Spa by Six Senses.

Uncover Ibiza’s Hidden Soul

Discover a range of exclusive skincare products and treatments by **Dr. Barbara Sturm**, the globally recognized aesthetics doctor and skincare brand founder. Exclusively available at Ibiza Bay. Also, explore **KLORIS, a CBD** wellness brand using organic and natural ingredients to create high-quality products, including face oils, balms, and bath products, all designed to soothe and nourish the skin.

At Ibiza Bay, everybody is welcome, including your beloved pup. Indulge your four-legged friend with Ibiza’s one and only **Dog Spaw**, created in partnership with Charlie & Co Naturals, a luxury range of 100% Natural dog grooming products for precious dogs who deserve the very best. Now your beloved pup can join you and enjoy the ultimate in luxury pampering too.

Wellness is all about experiences that nourish the soul and make you feel that little bit closer, to being your best self. With this in mind we are delighted to introduce our series of retreats especially designed to ensure the maximum wellnewss and wellbeing. In partnership with leading industry experts **Ibiza Hike Station**, we present the **Holistic Hikes Retreats**. Don’t miss these unforgettable hiking experiences that combine adventure with wellness and take you to some of the most beautiful and lesser-known spots on the island.
