## Prime Location

# Where Convenience Meets Luxury

Nobu Hotel Ibiza Bay is ideally positioned on the shores of Talamanca Bay. Away from the hustle and bustle, step from the hotel straight onto the soft sandy beach - or your yacht. With the beach promenade right outside your door, in a short walk you will be wandering through the trendy boutiques and restaurants of Marina Botafoch and old town.

SEE MAP HERE

Conveniently situated near key attractions, dining, and entertainment, at Nobu Hotel Ibiza Bay you will enjoy easy access to the vibrant pulse of the city, Formentera port, and the most stunning calas and beaches of the White Isle, ensuring a memorable and centrally located stay:

- **Dalt Vila and Ibiza Old Town**: 10 minutes by car
- **Airport**: 12 minutes by car
- **Port (Formentera)**: 6 minutes by car / 19 minutes walking
- **Marina Botafoc (shopping)**: 3 minutes by car / 15 minutes walking
- **Les Dalies (Hippy Market)**: 26 minutes by car
- **Platja en Bossa**: 14 minutes by car
- **Santa Eulària city**: 21 minutes by car
- **Sant Antoni city**: 24 minutes by car
- **Es Vedrà**: 29 minutes by car
- **Clubs:**
