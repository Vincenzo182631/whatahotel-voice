# The Almacen at Ibiza Bay

Shop up a storm at our Ibiza Bay boutique; every piece has been hand-selected to be exciting, fresh and the most coveted item in your home or wardrobe.  Discover local and international lifestyle brands including clothing, swimwear, ceramics, beauty products, and jewelry.

Bringing the finest Ibiza has to offer all under one roof; Almacen is perfect for those who don’t have the time to search the island looking for hidden gems. Our ethos is to feature brands that give back to the earth and community, are handmade by local artisans, or use ancient techniques in a modern way.
