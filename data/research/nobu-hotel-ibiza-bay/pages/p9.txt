Island Inspired Programmes and Activities

# Kid's Club

Little bohos can discover the art of jewellery making and basket weaving from local artisans at our Hippy Market, whilst those with inquisitive minds and a passion for the earth will love learning about the environment through Balearic crafts lessons using repurposed material from the beach like mermaid and pirate days, sandcastle creations and master chef sessions at Nobu and Chambao.

If the surge in electronic music has inspired your little one to become the next Carl Cox or David Guetta, then our DJ workshops are a must.

Mini sports lovers can push themselves to the limit with classic pool games, yoga sessions or paddleboarding in the bay.  Chefs in the making will love the pizza making in Chambao.

Our team of childminders is professionally trained and certified.

Babysitter service available. €30 per hour (one or two kids); €40 per hour (three kids).

**Ages**: Children 4 – 12

**Opening times**: 10am – 6pm

Email: kidsclub-ibizabay@nobuhotels.com\\
