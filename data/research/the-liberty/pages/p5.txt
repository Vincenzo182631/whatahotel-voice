## Luxury Boston Hotel near Soccer Championship

Stay at the center of it all as the World Championship arrives in Boston, one of 11 U.S. host cities. Built in 1851, The Liberty is a Boston landmark with luxury guest rooms and suites, plus world-class dining. Follow #WeAreBoston for citywide fan events, then make your way to the city's premier stadium with convenient public transit options via South Station. Celebrate with fellow fans at our lively Boston bar for soccer championship watch parties, cocktails, and post-game gatherings.

## The Liberty Bar

The Liberty Bar anchors the hotel lobby beneath its soaring 90-foot rotunda, drawing a Beacon Hill crowd night after night. Cocktails feature house-infused spirits, custom bitters, and fresh-muddled fruit as fans gather ahead of championship matches and citywide game day events.

### Your Way to the City's Premier Stadium

Here are a few common ways guests can get from The Liberty to the city's premier stadium:

- Train: Go to South Station, then take the Franklin/Foxboro Line when event-day service runs.
- Private ride: Taxi or rideshare from The Liberty to the city's premier stadium.
- Drive: Go by car and plan ahead for stadium-area parking and traffic patterns.

### What to Expect

Boston is set to host seven global soccer championship matches, including one quarter-final, with fan zones and citywide events throughout the competition. Follow #WeAreBoston for updates on local fan events and official city happenings.

A private terrace and expansive living and dining rooms make space for long mornings, late evenings, and gatherings around championship plans. From skyline sunsets to nights overlooking the Charles, the suite offers a rare perspective on the city during its biggest moment.

Are there public transportation options nearby?

Yes. Bus and subway options near The Liberty connect to South Station, where the Franklin/Foxboro Line departs with stadium service on event days.

How far is The Liberty from the stadium?

The stadium is approximately 29 miles from The Liberty. Travel times vary by traffic and route.

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Liberty?

Check-in at The Liberty is at 4:00 pm and check-out is at 11:00 am.

What is the closest airport near The Liberty?

Boston Logan International Airport (BOS) is the closest airport to The Liberty, located approximately 3.0 miles away from the hotel.
