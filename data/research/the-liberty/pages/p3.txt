### Private Dining

The Liberty Hotel can accommodate many different environments to suit your needs. From a sit-down dinner party at CLINK. to a VIP cocktail reception at The Liberty Bar, we can assist in making your event a distinctive and enjoyable Boston private dining experience.

## CLINK.

CLINK.’s private dining area, separated by unique detailing such as vestiges of the historic structure’s original brick wall and jail cell façades, lends an exclusive feel for personalized lunches and dinners. Our CLINK. management team works closely with each client to create a customized event; carefully planning every detail from the selection of the pre-fixe menu to the wine pairings.

## Liberty Bar

The Liberty Bar’s VIP and Horseshoe sections are available for private bookings. Make your Boston private dining experience unforgettable with bespoke service, all while being encased in our stunning 90-foot rotunda lobby.

##### Dress Code

- Thursday – Saturday: Casual Chic. Stylish attire is expected, so please no athletic wear (i.e. sweatpants, athletic shorts, trainers, tank tops).

##### Major Mondays

- Mondays just got major at The Liberty Hotel. Join us in The Liberty Bar every Monday from 6PM – 9PM for live blues music.

##### Gallery Night Tuesdays

- Every Tuesday from 6PM-8:30PM, The Liberty displays art from some of the most prominent local artists and galleries in the city. This weekly show reinforces The Liberty’s commitment to supporting the Boston art scene and our local community.

##### Whole Note Wednesdays

- Join us in The Liberty Bar for Acoustic Jazz every Wednesday from 6:30PM – 9:30PM. Admission is complimentary.

##### Beat Weekends

- Sounds from Boston’s top DJs at The Liberty Bar in the lobby, every Friday & Saturday night starting at 9PM. Complimentary admission, as always.

##### Fashionably LATE Thursdays

- Fashionably LATE brings Boston's fashion scene to life. On Thursdays, the expansive lobby turns into an interactive-style fashion show, featuring sounds from DJ Frank White and looks from local Boston designers.
