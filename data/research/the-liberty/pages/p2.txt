## Accommodations

Each of the rooms and suites at The Liberty, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Boston has been painstakingly renovated to maintain the historic nature of our landmark building. Inspired by its location as the...See More former Charles Street Jail, our accommodations feature playful nods to the hotel’s infamous past in the Beacon Hill neighborhood. Find the ultimate union of historic influence and contemporary design in our 298 hotel rooms. Between the original structure and the new tower, our rooms and suites are serene and sumptuous, design-forward and chic. During your stay in Boston, enjoy the well-appointed amenities and modern luxuries guests have come to expect. Many accommodations feature private balconies that boast enchanting views of Beacon Hill and The Charles River.See Less

### Ebersol Suite

This suite provides the quintessential 270-degree panoramic view of Boston from the State House in the East around to Charlestown in the North. The views can be enjoyed from any room in the suite or from the 300 square feet open-air patio.

Do the rooms at The Liberty, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Boston have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Liberty, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Boston have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Liberty, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Boston?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at The Liberty, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Boston. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Liberty, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Boston?

Some rooms at The Liberty, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Boston permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
