Elevate Your Stay
Discover luxury at our Club Lounge hotel in Portland, Oregon
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience. Combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail, our downtown Portland, Oregon, hotel with a Club Lounge offers everything The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities, and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay at our Downtown Portland, Oregon, hotel with Club Lounge access by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.
