Signature Suites
Experience the perfect blend of luxury and comfort in our one-bedroom and two-bedroom suites, perched high above the vibrant Rose City. Offering breathtaking views and spacious common areas ideal for gathering, these elegant retreats pay tribute to the rich history and natural beauty of Portland. Each specialty suite features access to The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge.
The Ritz-Carlton Suite
Inspired by the frozen beauty of Multnomah Falls, this luxurious 2,700 sq. ft. sanctuary offers a serene escape where guests can host, play, and unwind at their leisure. Floor-to-ceiling windows provide sweeping views 18 stories above downtown Portland, creating a perfect balance of comfort and breathtaking scenery.
