Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Portland
Discover an urban retreat at our hotel in Portland
A tribute to nature and the thrill of discovery, The Ritz-Carlton, Portland, offers a striking vantage point from which to explore the Rose City. Centrally located, this luxury hotel in Portland, Oregon, boasts views of the city skyline and the Cascade Range. The 19th floor of The Ritz-Carlton, Portland, is a haven for nurturing the mind, body, and spirit, featuring a rejuvenating spa inspired by the unfolding petals of a Portland rose. An infinity pool surrounded by floor-to-ceiling windows and a 4,000 sq. ft. fitness facility with equipment favored by professional athletes complete this restorative space. Savor the region’s bounty at Bellpine, our 20th floor restaurant, where panoramic views enhance the culinary journey through Pacific Northwest-Mexican cuisine. Or experience inventive mixology and Japanese-inspired cuisine at Meadowrue bar and lounge. Our luxury hotel in Downtown Portland, Oregon, takes visitors through the history, landscape, and spirit of one of America's most beautiful destinations.

Join us for a month-long celebration of culture & craftsmanship. Savor cuisine inspired by Chef Jocelyn Chacón's heritage at Bellpine, enjoy Golden Hour specials, & discover wines from Hispanic-owned & Hispanic-led Oregon wineries featured in the Meadowrue Winemakers Series. Sept. 15 - Oct. 15.

Memorable Meetings and Special Events in the Pacific Northwest
The Ritz-Carlton, Portland offers 17,900 square feet of distinctive event space, thoughtfully designed as a stunning backdrop for meetings and celebrations. Inspired by the snow-capped Cascade Mountains and the dramatic peak of Mount Hood, the design—Ascending Mount Hood—features floor-to-ceiling windows, crystalline lighting, and custom carpets that echo the region’s topography. The venue includes a grand ballroom with pre-function space, a junior ballroom, and three elegant breakout rooms—all enhanced by state-of-the-art technology and the legendary service of The Ritz-Carlton.

On-Site Photography Policy
Professional photography is not permitted in public areas of The Ritz-Carlton, Portland without prior approval. Requests must be submitted at least 72 business hours in advance. Professional photography includes any photography or videography using a professional camera, tripod, lighting, or other production equipment. Restrictions apply. For photography or videography requests, and for additional information regarding this policy, please contact our property directly.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Portland?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Portland is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Portland allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Portland. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $200.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Portland?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Portland. The daily valet fee is $69.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Portland have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Portland has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Portland booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Portland?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Portland is Portland International Airport (PDX). PDX is located approximately 12.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Portland have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Portland has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Portland have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Portland offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
