New American, Pacific Northwest Inspired
Dining
In the heart of Portland—where creative spirit meets world-class wine and cuisine—Bellpine rises above it all. Set on the 20th floor of The Ritz-Carlton, Portland, our signature Downtown Portland, Oregon, restaurant with a view of the city skyline offers a refined dining experience rooted in the bounty of the Pacific Northwest. Inspired by Oregon’s coastlines and landscapes, Bellpine’s design features curved forms, tidal textures, and shimmering light that echo the natural world. By night, the atmosphere shifts to one of celestial calm, with the city aglow beneath a canopy of stars. Our lobby lounge, Meadowrue, invites guests into a forest-inspired retreat, where twinkling lights and organic textures set the stage for Japanese-influenced small plates and masterfully crafted cocktails. For those seeking a more intimate experience, In-Room Dining brings the same culinary artistry to the comfort of your guestroom or suite.
