# Welcome to Le Méridien Hamburg

## Discover the Alster Lake near our Hamburg city center hotel

Discover Le Méridien Hamburg, the Design Hotel, and the city in completely new ways. Situated on the banks of the Outer Alster Lake in the trendy St. George district, the center is just a short trip away....See More Stay at our Hamburg hotel in the city center and choose from a variety of newly renovated guest rooms with modernly designed interiors. Whether you are visiting for business or leisure, our business hotel in Hamburg offers a prime location, contemporary accommodation and inspiring meeting facilities. Enjoy our on-site HERITAGE Restaurant and Bar and plan your next event in our meeting space with a spectacular view. Worried about leaving your pet behind? Our hotel is pet-friendly. Our Hamburg family hotel is also within easy reach of the main station and attractions like Hamburg's historic districts, trade fair grounds, the harbor, the UNESCO World Heritage sites Speicherstadt and Kontorhausviertel and the music halls. After a long day, take a dip in our indoor pool, work out at the gym or relax in our sauna.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Hamburg?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Hamburg is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Hamburg allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Hamburg. One pet is allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of €25.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Hamburg?

On-site parking is available at Le Méridien Hamburg. Self parking is available at Le Méridien Hamburg, with rates of €6.00 per hour, and €38.00 per day. Valet parking is available for €38.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Hamburg have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Hamburg has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Hamburg booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Hamburg?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Hamburg is Hamburg Airport (HAM). HAM is located approximately 12.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Hamburg have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Le Méridien Hamburg has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
