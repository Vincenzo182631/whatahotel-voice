# Hotel Suites in Hamburg

## Accommodations

Our Le Méridien hotel unlocks pure Hamburg: from the beginning of your arrival throughout your stay with us. Enter clear maritime design, see and feel vastness that inspires. Discover our premium Hamburg...See More accommodation and suites at our design hotel. The hotel is located in Hamburg‘s city center, right in the hip St. Georg district, with the Alster Lake on your doorstep. All 275 elegant rooms, including the 14 hotel suites in Hamburg, offer the exclusive Le Méridien bed and furnishings, complimentary Wi-Fi, LED TV, and high-speed internet access. Warm colors and straightforward design give a unique Hanseatic note to our hotel in the heart of Hamburg. Our Hamburg accommodation ensures a comfortable and stylish stay, catering to both business and leisure travelers. For those traveling with loved ones, our Hamburg hotel family room options provide ample space and comfort, making us an ideal choice for families visiting the city.See Less

## Pure Style on the Inside, Hamburg on the Doorstep

Wake up to unique views. Have breakfast above the Alster lake. Discover the city from one of its hippest quarters. Sink into the Victorian Bathtub. Fall asleep in your Le Méridien bed. Repeat.

Breathtaking Alster Views

## Prestige Lake Suite

Our Prestige Lake Suite offers 113m² of luxury and elegance at the highest level. With a unique spatial concept and a color scheme inspired by Hanseatic tradition, this suite brings the essence of the Hanseatic city directly to you. From the moment you step in, you'll be captivated by the breathtaking panoramic views over the expansive Alster Lake, perfectly complemented by carefully selected decor and design elements.
The open-plan living area features a generous sofa and a stylish dining area, spatially separated from the cozy bedroom. The spacious marble-inspired bathroom exudes timeless sophistication and also boasts a magnificent view of the Alster. An additional separate WC provides extra convenience. The private balcony invites you to enjoy the fresh Alster air and Nordic breeze – perhaps over a romantic breakfast.

Suites With a View

## Lake Suite

With a room size ranging from 55 m² to 61 m², our timeless and air-conditioned Lake Suites offer generous space for pure relaxation after a bustling day in Hamburg. Here, you'll find a harmonious blend of luxury and comfort: A curtain separates the bedroom from the inviting corner couch, where you can either unwind with the latest blockbuster or enjoy a spectacular view of the Alster. The mobile bar is the perfect gathering spot to enjoy refreshing drinks, while the spacious bathroom exudes timeless elegance with its high-quality materials. On the private balcony, you can fully immerse yourself in the fresh air. With the option to expand the Lake Suite with additional rooms, you can require a generous oasis of up to 210 m² with space for up to six guests.

Duplex Views of the Alster Lake

## Panorama Lake Suite

Experience modern luxury and timeless elegance with breathtaking views in our Panorama Lake Suite. Spread over two levels with an area of up to 74 m², this suite leaves nothing to be desired: Upon waking up, you'll be greeted by a sweeping view of the Alster, which will also capture your attention from the upper living area. The integrated sofa bed provides a comfortable sleeping space for our little guests, while the additional separate toilet makes the suite the ideal choice for a family vacation in the Hanseatic city

Panoramic Views

## Duplex Lake Suite

Experience exclusive Hanseatic flair on two levels, just for you! Our Duplex Lake Suite invites you to relax in a modern yet cozy atmosphere, spanning approximately 45 m². With its panoramic windows, the suite offers unrestricted views over Hamburg and the Alster. Two bathrooms in Victorian style with a bathtub and separate shower add an extra touch of luxury to the suite. In addition to the king-size bed, the integrated sofa easily transforms into a comfortable bed – a favorite and suitable option for our little guests.

Generous Living Space

## Urban Suite

Our spacious Urban Suites offer you a stylish retreat in Hamburg's trendy St. Georg district, which you can observe directly from your suite. With 69 m² of Hanseatic lifestyle, this suite category presents itself with sleek design and an inviting atmosphere. After a busy day, the Victorian-style bathroom invites you to relax and recharge. The unique Le Méridien bed offers you the utmost comfort. The integrated sofa easily converts into a comfortable bed, and some of the suites also offer connecting options with additional rooms for even more space – perfect for family getaways.

Useful Information

### Room Amenities

Our air-conditioned rooms feature modern amenities, including a bathroom with a bath tub and a additional shower, a cosmetic mirror, hairdryer, bathrobes, slippers, and Malin + Goetz toiletries. Complimentary Wi-Fi, a flat-screen TV, a minibar with a coffee machine as well as an ironing board with an iron are also provided. Many suites also include a private balcony or terrace.

### Pets Regulation

We warmly welcome pets as well! Your furry friend will receive their own dog bed (available in small or large sizes) and food bowls. Please be aware that there is a cleaning fee per pet per night (excluding assistance dogs), and only one pet per room is permitted.

### Parking

On-site parking is available for €6.00 per hour or €38.00 per day. Charging stations for electric vehicles are also available. Additionally, we offer valet service for an additional fee.
