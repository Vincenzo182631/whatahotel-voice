# Dine With a View at Heritage Restaurant Hamburg

## Dining

When Hamburg turns blue in the twilight hour, the day blurs into the first flickering lights of the Alster banks. The water becomes this broad, smooth surface and the night awakens. That is the best time...See More for dinner at HERITAGE, our Hamburg restaurant in Germany. Enjoy the panoramic view on the 7th floor and start these unique hours with a creative apéritif. Whether you're savoring a perfectly cooked steak at our steakhouse or enjoying the atmosphere at our Hamburg rooftop bar, every moment is special. Our bar in Hamburg offers mesmerizing lake views—perfect for a relaxing evening by the water. Let your evening be as sparkling as your drink and make the most of the unforgettable ambience.See Less

What’s On at HERITAGE

### Feierabend – October

Join us on 1 October as Hamburg's favorite afterwork party returns high above the city. Mark Bale delivers the soundtrack for an evening filled with great drinks, urban beats and golden autumn vibes, while the Alster and Hamburg skyline set the scene for a memorable night above the rooftops.

### HERITAGE – Halloween Special

On 31 October, HERITAGE Rooftop Bar goes spooky. Expect wickedly good drinks, driving beats and rooftop vibes with guaranteed goosebumps. Guests arriving in costume will receive a complimentary welcome drink, making this the perfect way to celebrate Halloween high above Hamburg.

Get Tickets

### HERITAGE – XMAS Special

Celebrate the festive season with us on 26 December at the HERITAGE Rooftop Bar. Enjoy great drinks, rooftop vibes and a soundtrack featuring the occasional Christmas classic, all set against the backdrop of Hamburg's winter skyline. Festive outfits are more than welcome.
