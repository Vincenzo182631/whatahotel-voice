# 2026 Artists-in-Residence

November 19, 2025

We’re pleased to announce dates for the 2026 artists-in-residence program with the First Nations Anangu artists from Ernabella Arts.

At Longitude 131°, our partnership with Ernabella Arts – a celebrated First Nations arts community in the Anangu Pitjantjatjara Yankunytjatjara (APY) Lands – is a cornerstone of our commitment to cultural connection and storytelling.

A highlight of this collaboration is our artist-in-residence program, which welcomes Ernabella artists to the lodge to paint with sweeping views of Uluru and Kata Tjuta. During these visits, guests are invited to observe the creative process, gaining rare insight into the artists’ practice as each vibrant piece comes to life.

The 2026 program dates are:

- February 16-19
- April 20-23
- June 1-4
- August 24-27
- November 2-5

Set against the rich cultural landscape of the Red Centre, this shared experience offers a meaningful encounter with the living traditions of the Anangu people – one of deep respect, artistic inspiration, and a sense of place.

Located in the Anangu Pitjantjatjara Yankunytjatjara (APY Lands) some 250km – or a 30-minute scenic flight – from Longitude 131°, Ernabella Arts Inc supplies the vibrant ceramics, canvases, tiles and spears (kulata) to the lodge which are seen in guest tents, in the Dune House bar and restaurant and Spa Kinara. Many artworks are also for sale in the boutique.

Proceeds from the artwork commissions and sales contribute to the community’s income, ensuring the sustainable economic growth and preservation of the culture for its residents.

Since 2016, Baillie Lodges has partnered with Ernabella Arts to provide $50,000 annually to support a dedicated mentor for the artists with the aim of establishing commercially viable, sustainable enterprise.

For bookings, contact Baillie Lodges on +61 2 9918 4355 or reserve@baillielodges.com.au.
