# Stay

Nested among ancient dunes, Longitude 131° is a gathering of sixteen tented pavilions where sanctuary and a profound sense of place arrive together. Floor-to-ceiling glass frames the ever-changing light of the desert, while custom furnishings and First Nations artworks layer each space with story and warmth. Step onto the balcony, unfurl a swag beside the fireplace, and let the outback sky settle over you. _Longitude 131° welcomes children ten years and over._
