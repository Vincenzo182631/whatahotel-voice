At the hotel

At Principe, excellence is expressed through authentic and intimate experiences. Discover the soul of our hospitality with private fine-vintage tastings, Michelin-starred cooking classes, and exclusive dinners by the sea. Every detail is designed to turn Tuscan flavors and landscapes into an everlasting memory, tailored just for you.

Yacht and sea

Discover the absolute freedom of the open sea. Our 24-meter Mazarin yacht features a flybridge and three cabins: two king-bed suites with private ensuite bathrooms, and a third twin-bed cabin with its own ensuite. Marina di Principe delivers an exclusive experience, offering all the comforts and amenities of a villa on the water.

Helicopter

Our land is a unique blend of sandy beaches, rolling hills, and majestic marble mountains. The breathtaking view from above offers unforgettable emotions. Don't miss the chance to fly over Tuscany and live a thrilling new experience.

Marble quarries

Forte dei Marmi’s most iconic experience. The world of marble is rooted in ancient history and, if you are brave enough to conquer your fear of heights, you will discover a fascinating universe of pure, white, and precious stone.

Nature and trekking

Principe Hotel has set a series of key goals regarding the environment and the world around us, with the clear intention of making its operations fully sustainable. We invite you to discover our splendid region, rich in history, nature and heritage.

Beyond Art

Why does our region hold such an irresistible charm? Because it is a place where luxury, nature, history, and art intertwine into a single, extraordinary experience. Every corner tells a story, every view evokes an emotion. Walking through the Tuscan hills, breathing air rich with the scent of the earth and centuries of tradition, taking in villages that seem suspended in time—all of this creates a sense of beauty that exceeds every expectation.

Guided tours of Tuscany’s most iconic villages, cooking classes in the heart of Lucca, or exclusive artistic experiences alongside local artists.

Shopping

We are delighted to announce a prestigious collaboration with two of Italy’s most renowned luxury outlets: The Mall Firenze, just minutes from Florence, and Fidenza Village, part of The Bicester Collection, near Parma. Thanks to this partnership, you can enjoy a unique shopping experience, exploring an exclusive selection of the most celebrated fashion, luxury, and lifestyle maison in an elegant and refined setting.

For kids & teen

We are a family-oriented resort designed to welcome guests of all ages. For our younger guests, we offer a thoughtfully curated selection of engaging activities designed to spark creativity, encourage fun, and create unforgettable memories. Highlights include private tennis and golf lessons, guided bike tours, children's pastry workshops, and many other tailor-made experiences, all organized with the utmost care for the whole family's enjoyment.
