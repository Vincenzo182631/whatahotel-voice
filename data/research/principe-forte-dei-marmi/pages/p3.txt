# Dining experience

Lux Lucis

Chef Valentino Cassanelli

One Michelin Star

One Michelin star

Restaurant

LUX LUCIS is home to the Michelin-starred cuisine of Chef Valentino Cassanelli.

Valentino’s dishes are a free expression of Italian cuisine, with an unrestrained creativity, drawing inspiration not only from his own life experiences, but also from the rich biodiversity present on the coast of Forte dei Marmi.

Principe Restaurant

Framed by a large window that blends into the elegant and luxuriant outdoor garden and the pool area, the Principe restaurant is a small oasis where you can savor moments of relaxation and true pleasure. The gastronomic proposal of the restaurant manages to satisfy every need with its international cuisine.

67 Sky Lounge Bar

Located on the panoramic rooftop of Principe Forte dei Marmi hotel, the 67 Sky Lounge offers an exclusive and dynamic environment overlooking the entire Versilia coast, with a view that will leave you breathless.

Dalmazia Beach

Restaurant

Framed by large verandas with an incomparable view of the beach and the open sea, at Dalmazia restaurant you will live an authentic experience made up of typical smells and flavors of Versilia and Forte dei Marmi, with a menu created by the starred chef Valentino Cassanelli.
