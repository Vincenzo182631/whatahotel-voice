# Art at Principe

Home

At Principe Forte dei Marmi, art is not merely decoration, it is part of the experience. Every space in the hotel is designed to embrace artistic expressions that interact with the architecture, the light, and the rhythm of a holiday. This is the inspiration behind Arte al Principe, a project dedicated to showcasing contemporary art, where elegance, luxury, and culture come together in natural harmony.

Contacts
E. info@principefortedeimarmi.com T. +39 0584 783636

The presence of the artworks integrates into the hotel's sensory experience, transforming every moment into something unique.

Romolo Del Deo

A dialogue between art, landscape, and time. A special collaboration with artist and sculptor Romolo Del Deo, who has transformed the setting of the Hotel Principe Forte dei Marmi into a space where contemporary creativity, nature, and contemplation meet.

**On the hotel’s panoramic rooftop, Romolo Del Deo’s works engage with the sea, the light and the Versilia horizon, inviting guests to slow, profound contemplation.**

A meeting of kindred visions, where excellence in hospitality meets enduring art.

Park Eun Sun

The heart of the "Arte al Principe" project is the collaboration with Park Eun-Sun, an internationally renowned Korean artist. His marble and granite sculptures, characterized by essential volumes and geometric harmonies, reflect a deep exploration of balance, time, and memory. The polished surfaces, the natural veins of the stone, and the contrasts between light and shadow create a beauty that dialogues perfectly with the contemporary elegance of the Principe.

**The works of Park Eun Sun displayed in the hotel are not merely aesthetic presences. They are invitations to contemplation, to silence, and to a relationship with the material. They represent a bridge between cultures, between tradition and modernity, between Versilia and the world.**

"Suffering generates beauty, and within the split and recomposed stone, harmony is rebuilt—the fruit of the creative act."

Deodato Arte

Throughout the year, Art at Principe\* program is enriched through partnerships with select artistic entities. Among these is the collaboration with the Deodato Arte gallery, which brings new visions, new artistic languages, and a curated selection of contemporary artists to the hotel—a dialogue that evolves over time, offering guests ever-changing opportunities for discovery.
