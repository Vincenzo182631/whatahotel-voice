# Welcome

Your luxurious intimate retreat in Forte dei Marmi

Pristine beach clubs and modern design meet in the charming beach town of Forte dei Marmi.

And nestled in its redolent landscape of palm and pine trees, you’ll find Principe Forte dei Marmi,

a gorgeous luxury hotel on the Tuscan Coast.

Dining experience

A taste experience, for every taste. This is the goal that Chef Cassanelli has given himself in his three restaurants, where it is possible to range from the tasting menu of his starred cuisine, to the classic international dishes loved by all types of customers, up to the classic traditional cuisine of Forte dei Marmi, to be enjoyed with feet in the sand admiring the magnificent coast of Versilia.
