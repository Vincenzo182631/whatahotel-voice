# dine & drink

**DESTINATION DINING**

Mondrian Cannes is a destination for the curious epicurean. Mr. Nakamoto elevates modern cuisine in an undeniably stylish setting while Hyde Beach, set directly on the Bay of Cannes, serves an Italian menu in a casual daytime and lively evening atmosphere.

An elevated menu of prized local and seasonal ingredients meets an array of intricate and intriguing Asian flavors at Mr. Nakamoto Cannes.

Discover MR. Nakamoto

Located on the sandy beach along the legendary boulevard de La Croisette, Hyde Beach Cannes offers a casual daytime and lively evening atmosphere. Experience the southern art de vivre with stunning sun-drenched colors and a touch of la Dolce Vita.

Discover HYDE BEACH CANNES
