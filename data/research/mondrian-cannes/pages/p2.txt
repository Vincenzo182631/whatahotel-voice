# ROOMS & SUITES

The curved cleaned lines of soft, aqua-hued flooring set a stage for modern design enlivened by a sea-side setting and tempered by sophistication in our rooms. Suites are infused with a dreamlike sensibility, inspired by the fresh calm of the Mediterranean.
