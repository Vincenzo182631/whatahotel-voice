## Elios Spa

Our first-class spa offers guests the opportunity to relax and unwind in the hands of experts. A wide range of therapeutic massages, beautifying face and body treatments ensure guests leave fully rejuvenated.

Book Now

Elios Spa Etiquette

Your guide to Elios Spa:

- The Spa and its environs are strictly no smoking areas.

- Please conduct all conversations at levels consistent with a relaxing environment

- The Spa is open to persons over the age of 16. Guests under 16 years of age must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian in order to receive treatments.

- Please leave all items of value in your suite. The Spa is not responsible for loss of or damage to a guest’s personal belongings.

- All guests will be required to complete and sign a health & lifestyle questionnaire upon arrival and prior to treatments.

- Please note that a 24-hour cancellation notice is required for all bookings and services as a courtesy to our other guests.

- Out of consideration for all Spa guests, late arrivals will not be given an extension of scheduled treatment times and the full cost of the treatment will be charged.

- Guests are advised to arrive at least 20 minutes prior to their first appointment to allow themselves ample time to check in and shower, as it is essential before any treatment.

- During treatments, please let your therapist know if you are comfortable or uncomfortable, too warm or too cold, or if their pressure is too light or too firm.

- If you have a particular injury or physical condition, please explain it to the therapist. They may suggest appropriate adjustments to the treatment for your comfort and benefit.

- Our Spa staff will clean all surfaces or objects that are frequently manipulated with products approved by the EPA only after each treatment.

- All our reception staff will use hand sanitizer or wash their hands with soap and water whenever necessary, or at least every 30 minutes and after each contact with guests.

- The manicure and pedicure stations and furniture will be cleaned and sanitized after each service.

- There will be minimum 15 minutes between each service to carry out the adequate sanitization process in our treatment suites.

### Kinship: Family Experiences Rooted in Culture and Connection

At Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, days unfold within the stone walls of our 400-year-old wine estate, where mythology, music, and heritage come to life. Our Kinship Program invites families to discover Santorini together through hands-on experiences that celebrate connection, creativity, and the island’s living traditions. Families can join the nearby Symposion Cultural Center for interactive...See More storytelling and instrument-making workshops or visit the Tomato Industrial Museum to explore Santorini’s famed cherry tomato through tastings, exhibits, and cooking sessions. From farm visits and cooking classes to gentle spa rituals for children and horseback rides through Megalochori’s vineyards, each experience is designed to bring families closer together. ### Discover Greece

Located 200 kilometers southeast of the mainland, the island covers 96-square-kilometers and attracts international tourists seeking its volcanic black-sandy beaches, sugar-cube Cycladic buildings, twisting stone alleyways with gregarious nightlife, blue-domed churches, and azure sea.

Santorini’s dramatic beauty was formed by a massive volcanic eruption that took place over three and a half thousand...See More years ago around 1645-1600 BC. Rumors abound that this eruption was the demise of the legendary lost city of Atlantis. Lost city or not, Santorini is steeped in rich history, and Vedema Resort has the advantage of being located near the airport and the port of Athinios—convenient to all area attractions.See Less

Learn More

- The ancient Minoan city Akrotiri: Dominated by a medieval fortress, this is one of the most inspiring sites in Greece.
- The Archaeological Museum: This impressive museum contains Hellenistic artifacts, Byzantine art, and remains of prehistoric times.
- Thira: Santorini’s most ancient city houses Dorian, Byzantine, and Roman remains.
- The Museum of Prehistoric Thira: This collection chronicles the island's history from the Late Neolithic to the Late Cycladic period.
- 18th Century monastery Profitis Illias: Located on the highest point in Santorini, the monastery contains both an enchanting church and folk museum.
- The Red Beach and the black sands of Perivolos: These beaches are renowned as some of the most breathtaking in the world.
- Thirasia: This nearby island with hot springs and impressive volcanic structures makes an unforgettable day trip.
- Closer to the resort, the beaches of Kamari and Perissa host diving centers and offer a bevy of water sports. These activities, horseback riding, and more can be arranged through The Luxury Collection Concierge. The island’s best shopping options are Fira's renowned designer stores located only a few miles from the resort.
- The currency is the Euro and the electrical system is European Standard 220 volt. Santorini's official language is Greek, however; English is prevalent, and French, German, and Spanish are spoken in large villages.

### Local Area & Activity Highlights

## Ancient Thira

A place full of history. Do not miss the chance to visit The Ancient City of Thira, open daily from 8:00-15:00 except Mondays. The ruins of the City transfer you thousands of years back in time, before the big eruption of the Volcano that destroyed half of the island.

### Hiking

While views from Thira town are enough to satisfy most travelers, even more spectacular panoramas await those who trek to small villages at higher elevations such as Imerovigli and Pyrgos. From cliff-top walks to volcanic vistas, the island of Santorini boasts some of the Mediterranean’s best hikes. Visitors can explore untouched landscapes in places where locals still embrace their traditions.

### Water Sports

Diving in the Mediterranean, experiencing the big blue, watching interesting animals, plants, lava formed bottoms, caves, drop offs; all in the clear waters surrounding Santorini and the Volcano Islands. An organized diving center, The Mediterranean Dive Club at Perissa Beach, where visitors can enjoy speedboat diving, snorkeling and PADI courses. Water sports vary from windsurfing , water-ski and many more.

### Horseback Riding

Start the day riding to the top! After your morning dash, visit Panagia Episkopi, the most important Byzantine monument on the island. A swim at Kamari and as soon as the sun begins to go down, a walk round Messa Gonia. As you stroll through the ruins of houses deserted by the villages after the earthquake of 1956 you can still see some traces of their daily lives here.
