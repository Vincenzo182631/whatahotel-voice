### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini?

The largest occupancy room at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini is the Nafsika Estate with a maximum occupancy of 10.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini?

Yes, all rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, Santorini have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
