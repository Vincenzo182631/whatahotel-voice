## An Italian Original in Munich

The timeless elegance of la Dolce Vita. Classic Italian dishes inspired by the swing of the seasons, meant for sharing with good company.

Our classic Italian dishes are profound in their simplicity - ingredients selected with the greatest respect for nature in a menu created by Fulvio Pierangelini, the Godfather of Italian cooking.

View Menu

Good conversation, time spent with friends, a lunch that extends effortlessly into dinner. We revere life’s pleasures. The restaurant's verdant dining room and terrace with views over the Old Botanical Garden, invite the relaxed glamour of Rome’s Le Jardin to our corner of Munich.

Surrounded by greenery, our plant-filled terrace is perfect for leisurely meals or a cocktail at aperitivo hour. Marble-topped tables, comfortable seating, and botanical accents create a space reminiscent of a sun-dappled piazza. Here, the gentle rhythm of a day well-spent unfolds.

Florio Bar is where the day slows down and the evening comes alive. Sink into deep, cushioned banquettes as aperitivo drifts into late-night cocktails, accompanied by a warmth that's unmistakably Italian. A spirited celebration of Il dolce far niente.

Additional Information

We wish for everyone to feel comfortable, so whilst we do not operate a dress code, we kindly recommend that our guests are respectful of the environment we have created here at The Charles Hotel and refrain from wearing sportswear, shorts and beach shoes in our restaurants. Our guests within the hotel usually opt for a smart-casual style.
