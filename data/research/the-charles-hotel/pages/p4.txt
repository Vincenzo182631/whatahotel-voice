# Circle Bar

Sink into an armchair, amid the soft glow of antique gold lamps and good company, with a coupette of Champagne or a signature cocktail – perhaps the _French 75_ or the _Flower Fizz_.

Paying homage to both Mauro Staccioli’s iconic _Ring_ sculpture outside our hotel and the circle of friends within, our aptly named bar gives a quiet nod to the Champagne bubble. In the sleek comfort of Paolo Moschino’s Art Deco backdrop, enjoy the buzz of lively conversation or pour over a vintage novel from our library.

### FINE DRINKING

Our Champagne list celebrates the world’s most talked-about wine region, showcasing both internationally renowned labels and boutique producers. Our team will guide you on a wine journey across vintage classics and exciting newcomers, paired with our selection of small plates, from Miso octopus skewers with chimichurri to Crispy artichoke with tartare sauce.

Additional Information

We wish for everyone to feel comfortable, so whilst we do not operate a dress code, we kindly recommend that our guests are respectful of the environment we have created here at The Charles Hotel and refrain from wearing sportswear, shorts and beach shoes in our restaurants. Our guests within the hotel usually opt for a smart-casual style.
