## Culinary spaces

### Robata grill

Fire, flavor and crafts

Seated around the robata (an open grill), guests can enjoy watching chefs bring the traditional Japanese charcoal grill to life. From meat to seafood and vegetables, every dish is infused with deep and rich flavors applying ancestral Japanese techniques to create a show of fire and elegance.

### Rooftop bar

Drinks with city views

Up above Lisbon, our ME Lisbon rooftop bar is the perfect place for sunset drinks and the seductive ambience of the night. The ideal opportunity to try hand-crafted cocktails and superior quality spirits while admiring the wonderful panoramic views of Lisbon. Visit Attiko and start relaxing, socializing and feeling the energy of the city from above.
