## Me By Melia Lisbon Hotel

ex. Me Lisbon By Meliá

Offering a steam room and a sauna, the elegant Me By Melia Lisbon Hotel is nestled just a 6-minute walk from the Portuguese Palacio Sotto Mayor and around 3 km from National Azulejo Museum. Located within 2 km of Rossio Square, this 5-star hotel offers views of the city and a heated pool.

The hotel lies in Avenidas Novas, which is a great choice for travellers interested in gourmet food and discovering the monuments of the city, within 20 minutes' walk of the high-end Amoreiras Shopping Center. Lisbon Zoo, 2.5 km away, is definitely a must-see for family tourists in Lisbon. Staying at the Me By Melia, you can also enjoy natural sights such as the public Parc Eduardo VII, which is 950 metres away. Humberto Delgado airport is 5 km from this accommodation and Rossio train station is approximately 20 minutes' walk away.

The air-conditioned rooms at the hotel feature air conditioning along with a minibar and an electric kettle. The rooms at Me By Melia Lisbon Hotel feature a modern-style decor. Also, the spacious bedrooms are furnished with a wardrobe. Providing a separate toilet, some bathrooms come with hair dryers and bathrobes.

Fismuler, an onsite restaurant, is open for dinner. There is a seafood restaurant serving Portuguese food approximately 100 metres away.
