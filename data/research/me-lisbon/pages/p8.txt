Here, art is not just observed, it's experienced: it forms part of your stay and awakens your senses. The hotel's spaces transform into a living, dynamic exhibition. Creative and immersive events by artists from the city's cultural scene, offering a unique and contemporary way to experience Lisbon.

### Fernanda Fragateiro

Wave 2024
Site-specific sculpture in painted steel and marble, part of the curatorial project by Guta Moura Guedes.
Composed of vertical painted steel modules, the piece traces a silent rhythm of interrupted waves, evoking the presence of the River Tejo in Lisbon. Its deep lilac/violet tone recalls the jacaranda bloom in May, adding an unexpected sensory layer that enhances its presence in space.

,regionOfInterest=(2150,1434))

### Jorge Silva

Sunny, 2024
Wall installation in coloured transparent acrylic, part of the curatorial project by Guta Moura Guedes.
Sunny evokes light, warmth and joy through a playful appropriation of a familiar social icon. Composed of enlarged sunglasses in coloured acrylic, the installation creates a pop-inspired gesture that reflects our gaze back through colour, reimagined with humour and lightness.

,regionOfInterest=(1121.5,1350))

### Renê Tavares

The painting 'The Celebration, 2024', created using a mixed technique on canvas, is part of the curatorial project by Guta Moura Guedes. The diptych evokes collective memory and the body as independent spaces. A pure assertion of identity and shared memory in multicultural Lisbon.

,regionOfInterest=(2925.5,4096))

### Joana Astolfi

Everything I Couldn´t Leave Behind, 2024
Architect, artist and designer, Astolfi draws inspiration from found objects and artifacts, shaping spaces as living, breathing entities. Her work weaves history and narrative together, infusing each project with new life while honoring the richness of the past - setting the stage for stories yet to unfold.

### Maria Ana Vasco Costa

The Portuguese artist and architect captivates with the language of ceramics through geometry, texture, and light. Her practice unfolds through site-specific installations and sculptural pieces that transform space into rhythmic compositions of form and shadow.

,regionOfInterest=(833.5,1250))

### Alberto Van Stockum

Discover more about Portuguese culture through the work of Spanish photographer and director Alberto Van Stockum, who presents a series inspired by Portugal’s iconic azulejos at ME Lisbon. By translating the intricate patterns onto the human face, Van Stockum explores the idea of cultural identity as a living mosaic, creating intimate contemporary works in the process.

,regionOfInterest=(1756.5,2634.5))

### Anna Westerlund

Anna Westerlund’s artworks at ME Lisbon reflect a life shaped by travel, culture and a constant urge to create.
With Swedish and Portuguese roots, she grew up between worlds, later deepening her artistic path through years of travel before discovering ceramics as her true language.
Her handmade pieces blend simplicity, texture and emotion, carrying memory and movement. At ME Lisbon, her work invites guests to pause, observe and feel.

,regionOfInterest=(2000,3000))

### Elsa Rebelo

At ME Lisbon, art doesn’t sit still; it lives with you.
Elsa Rebelo’s ceramic pieces transform rooms and suites into intimate, expressive spaces where form meets emotion. From the face vases that echo Lisbon’s landscapes to the Ophelias that bloom as a tribute to feminine strength, each creation carries a presence of its own.
Handcrafted, unique, and deeply human, these pieces invite guests into a quiet dialogue.

### Oficina Marques

A hybrid art gallery, studio and shop, it showcases unique, handcrafted works rooted in local culture and material exploration. Led by artists driven by the motto Tusa de Viver, the space blends ceramics, sculpture and found objects into compelling, one-of-a-kind pieces that bring imagination into everyday life.

,regionOfInterest=(2000,3000))

### Bordallo Pinheiro

Founded by the artist Rafael Bordallo Pinheiro, the brand skilfully captures the essence of Portuguese nature in its expressive ceramic pieces, which blend craftsmanship with contemporary design. As a tribute to culture and imagination, these creations add a distinctive artistic touch to the spaces at ME Lisbon.
