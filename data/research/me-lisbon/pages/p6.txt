### Spa for children

Children can use the KORPO Spa if they are accompanied by an adult and make a prior reservation. The minimum age for visitors is 6. Children aged 6 to 14 can enjoy the indoor pool, sensory shower, relaxation room and changing rooms from 9am to 1pm with a 60-minute limit. Access to the sauna and Turkish bath is prohibited for children under 14 years of age. Children with nappies should not enter any part of the spa.
