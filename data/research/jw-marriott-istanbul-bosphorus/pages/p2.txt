### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus is the 2 Bedroom Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus have fridges?

No, rooms at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus have hair dryers.
