# Welcome to JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus

## Book your stay at one of Istanbul's finest 5-star hotels

Located in eclectic Karaköy, JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus is a luxury hotel in Istanbul. This hotel in Istanbul blends Bohemian charm with modern comfort, nestled near boutiques, cafés, and markets. Conveniently situated next to Galataport and equidistant from Istanbul’s major airports, our Istanbul Karakoy hotel is ideal for both leisure and business. ### Octo

Octo Istanbul reimagines Türkiye’s rich heritage, bringing its culinary treasures to the table with a modern twist. Each dish reflects a perfect balance of history, culture, nature, and innovation.

Explore

### Sky Karaköy

Located on the roof with an outstanding 360-degree panorama of the city, strong bar and good music, Sky Karaköy is designed to metamorphose regular Istanbul nights into an illustrious journey. Enjoy our rooftop Istanbul restaurant from May to November.

Explore

### Tea Lounge

In addition to hosting social gatherings and business meetings, Tea Lounge offers a stylish atmosphere with brick walls dating back almost 200 years. Enjoy carefully selected tea and coffee options at our Bosphorus, Istanbul cafe.

Explore

### Istanbul Baking Company

Wholesome, organic, and high-quality baked goods, paired with indulgent drinks, are served in a warm and creative ambiance. Located on one of Karaköy’s liveliest streets, Istanbul Baking Company offers a peaceful Istanbul Coffee Shop experience.

Explore

### Galata Lounge

Located on the 9th floor and with a dominating view of the historical landmark Galata Tower and characteristic rooftop churches, Galata Lounge is the ultimate venue for inspired meetings and stylish events.

Explore

### Wine Cellar

Known as Istanbul's first hotel wine cellar, our Wine Cellar is a perfect venue to host a private dinner for up to eight people. A niche selection of local and international wines is offered in a contemporary atmosphere embodying brick walls.

Explore

### IOKI Nau

At İOKİ Nau Karaköy, enjoy Far Eastern flavors with exceptional service. As a premier Istanbul restaurant, we offer a carefully curated menu and cocktails, proudly introducing Nikkei Cuisine to Turkey through all our branches.

Explore

### Skull & Bones

Skull & Bones is a lively cocktail bar nestled on a very vibrant street of Karakoy offers an energizing experience that refreshes you from afternoon till midnight.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 8.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus?

Off-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus. The daily valet fee is €18.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus is Sabiha Gokcen International Airport (SAW). SAW is located approximately 45.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Istanbul Bosphorus does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
