Discover Our Luxury Hotel Suites in Doha
Suites & Rooms
Experience refined comfort at The Ritz-Carlton, Doha with thoughtfully designed hotel rooms and suites in Doha. The Deluxe Room offers a serene retreat with a plush king bed, living area, and a marble bathroom with an oversized bathtub, separate shower, and premium amenities. Enjoy complimentary high-speed wired and wireless internet, a mini fridge, and a coffee and tea maker, along with a makeup mirror, hairdryer, robe, slippers, and Diptyque bath amenities. For family accommodation in Doha, Qatar, spacious 1-bedroom suites include a separate living room, exclusive Club Lounge access, and city and sea views. Guests praise the spacious feel and comfort, while integrated technology and attentive details support both leisure and business travel.
Opulent Living in the Heart of Doha
Signature Suites
Step into a world of refined indulgence with our exclusive collection of spacious accommodations in Doha designed for discerning travelers. Set high above the Arabian Gulf, each suite offers sweeping panoramic views of Doha’s glittering skyline and the serene waterfront below. Whether for a short retreat or an extended stay, our Signature Suites promise an elevated sense of home, crafted for luxury, defined by privacy.

Elevated Luxury
Residential Living
Each of these exclusive suites offers an inspiring panorama that sweeps across the city skyline and stunning sea views, featuring unique layouts thoughtfully designed for entertaining, working, or unwinding, all complemented by a refined sense of elegance and style.
Junior Suite
Indulge in the lavish comforts of the Junior Suite, a modern masterpiece boasting French Oak flooring and luxurious furnishings. With 75 square meters of luxurious space, the suite features a separate bedroom and marble bathroom. Natural lighting and stunning views create a serene ambiance, while the living room offers the perfect spot to take in the beauty of the surroundings.
Executive Suite
Boasting 112 square meters of opulent space, the Executive Suite features a spacious living room with a working desk, perfect for business travelers, and a walk-out balcony. The contemporary bedroom and marble bathroom are adorned with quilted velvet and silk wall covering whilst high-end technology including 2 curve-screen TVs and a connectivity hub complement the residential feel.
Amiri Suite
Experience pure luxury in the newly modernized Amiri Suite, boasting a full kitchen, dining area, separate living room and a private balcony. Relax in the spacious bedroom with a walk-in wardrobe and a Tuscan marble bathroom with a separate shower and bathtub. The 152-square-meter suite offers a lavish makeup area, a guest bathroom and a flat-screen TV.
Elevate Your Stay With the Ritz-Carlton Club® Level
The Club Lounge at The Ritz-Carlton, Doha is the first of its kind in the Middle East region. Boasting privacy and elegance, it is designed around a modern residence interior concept. The space is elegantly sectioned into five areas: Warm Welcome, In Residence, Library, The Dining Room and The Kitchen.
Please note that access to The Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge is exclusively reserved to guests over 21 years old.
