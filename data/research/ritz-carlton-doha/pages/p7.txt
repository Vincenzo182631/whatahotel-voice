The Ritz-Carlton Boutique
The Ritz-Carlton, Doha, a destination renowned for its legendary hospitality and timeless stories, proudly has its own Boutique, an exquisite area ideally located in the heart of the hotel’s elegant lobby. Designed to reflect the refined spirit of The Ritz-Carlton and the rich cultural heritage of Qatar, this luxury retail salon invites guests to immerse themselves in a world of curated indulgence and bespoke craftsmanship. The Boutique promises more than just a shopping experience, it offers a sensory journey through an exclusive selection of collectables and lifestyle items.

Elevated Luxury
Delightful Choices for Ultimate Indulgence
Infused with elements that pay homage to Qatari artistry, the Boutique presents a distinctive blend of cultural elegance and modern luxury. Reflecting the prestige of The Ritz-Carlton name, from different accessories to luxurious lifestyle pieces, our curated selection captures the spirit of refined living. Each item was carefully chosen to serve as a lasting reminder of our guests' experience at The Ritz-Carlton, Doha.

Explore
Our Boutique is where unique taste meets everyday utility. Whether you are looking for a meaningful keepsake or a gift that captures the essence of your stay, you will discover an array of timeless treasures across apparel, travel essentials, and more.
Signature Ritz-Carlton T-shirts
Made to reflect the discreet elegance of The Ritz-Carlton, Doha, our signature T-shirts offer a polished approach to casual luxury. Tailored to suit both men and women, each piece is exceptionally soft, lightweight, and breathable, delivering comfort without compromising on style. Available in a color palette of navy, purple, grey, black, and white.
Effortlessly Stylish Tote Bags
Designed with both sustainability and elegance in mind, our exclusive tote bags offer a perfect balance of refined form and function. Available in soft, beach-inspired hues of light blue and light green and created with eco-friendly Azo-free dyes, with a large front pocket or a durable zipper, and padded cotton rope handles to ensure a comfortable carry.
Timeless Performer Cap
Available in a versatile selection of Black, White, Blue, and Khaki, these caps features breathable fabric with a self-fabric hook and loop closure, allowing for a customized fit. Discreet yet distinctive, they serve as a stylish nod to The Ritz-Carlton's tradition of graceful luxury.
Souvenirs for Little Guests
Meet our soft and cuddly Leo, The Ritz-Carlton Kid’s mascot and his cozy gold-toned blanket, the perfect duo. Make every bath time delightful with our themed floatable ducks featuring fun designs. From our beloved lion to playful bath companions, each item blends comfort, fun, and a touch of Ritz-Carlton charm.
Signature Notebooks
Created from high-quality recycled polypropylene material, ensuring durability while minimizing environmental impact. With a discreet pen-holding loop on the back and 100 pages of FSC®-certified recycled white lined paper, providing an eco-friendly canvas made to accompany you through your travels, reflections, and professional endeavors.
Tumblers & Cups
Experience every sip with our distinctive collection of triple-insulated mugs, tumblers, and stemless grapes cups, designed to keep drinks perfectly hot or cold for hours. Featuring spill-proof lids, sleek designs, and durable materials, they’re ideal for poolside lounging, daily walks or elegant evenings.
A Celebration Rooted in Nature: Torba Market
As part of its ongoing commitment to sustainability, The Ritz-Carlton, Doha is pleased to partner with Torba Market, a haven of sustainable agriculture where every ingredient is cultivated in harmony with the land. Together, we invite you to discover a curated selection to support your well-being journey beyond your stay.
