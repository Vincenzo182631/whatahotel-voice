Dining
Embark on a culinary journey at The Ritz-Carlton, Doha, where global flavors meet local inspiration. Our luxury restaurants in Doha each offer a distinct experience shaped by tradition and modern flair. B-Lounge brings vibrant ambiance and Asian fusion cuisine, while STK Doha is a modern steakhouse offering premium cuts in a dynamic setting. Sel & Miel pairs French charm with chic brasserie style, and Lagoon Restaurant serves international dishes in a family-friendly environment. Little guests can enjoy The Ritz Kids Club and The Ritz Kids Garden, while adults unwind at Flamingos Pool & Restaurant. Guests often praise the variety and quality of dining, whether visiting for fine dining, relaxed lunches, or casual sophistication in the heart of Doha.
Social Experiences
Gather with your loved ones to ease into the evenings at B-Lounge Doha, STK Doha or Habanos. Each outlet has curated an experience designed to celebrate togetherness and the joy of shared connections, with handcrafted drinks and special bites menus.
