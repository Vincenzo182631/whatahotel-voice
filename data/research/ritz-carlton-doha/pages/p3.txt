Elegant Stays with Doha Skyline
Signature Suites
Bringing in a new dawn of palatial hospitality in the beating heart of Qatar’s capital, The Ritz-Carlton, Doha’s masterful five-year renovation completes with the unveiling of the awe-inspiring Grand Amiri Suites. Each with a singular theme and design, they are a defining tribute to the city of Doha, its celebrated artists and artisans, and the inspiring surroundings. Perfectly suited for both family vacations and business trips, prepare to settle into a wondrous escape where each guest becomes part of the story.
Grand Amiri
Where Stories Begin
Enhance your Doha escape with our elegant suites offering stunning city & sea views. Blending elegance and functionality, these spacious accommodations provide separate sleeping and living areas, featuring a comfortable lounge, wide bathrooms and a working desk ideal for families or business travelers.
The Ivory Suite
Sea View Suite with European Flair
Creamy white hues dominate the color scheme of the Ivory Suite, a tribute to the thriving pearl trade with Europe that flourished from the 17th to 19th centuries. These calming, shimmering shades are accented with bold black and decadent gold, offering a contemporary feel with a touch of European flair.

The Quartz Suite
Where Natural Lighting Shimmers With Gold
Step into a mineral retreat at the Quartz Suite, where earthy tones of translucent quartz and black onyx meet metallic brass, bronze, and silver. The space is softly illuminated from dawn to dusk by subtle globe lights and Italian gold-accented walls, creating a serene, luxurious ambiance.

The Cobalt Suite
A Motorsports Enthusiast's Dream
Inspired by Qatar’s love for the thrill of cars and motorsports, the Cobalt Suite features an aerodynamic, masculine design that reinforces The Ritz-Carlton, Doha's role as a preferred hotel in global sports events. Luxurious leather wall panels echo high-end car interiors, while cobalt blue curtains and carpets pay tribute to race cars.

The Katara Suite
Pearl-inspired Suite with City View Terrace
Transporting guests back to Qatar’s humble beginnings, the third-floor Katara Suite is inspired by the region’s founding years. Named after ‘Catara’, the first and most ancient name given to the Qatar Peninsula, the suite reflects the movement of the sea and golden sands, paying tribute to the first settlers -pearl divers, and channeling the rich history and heritage.
