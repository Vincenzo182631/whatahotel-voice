Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Doha
A Secluded Luxury Hotel in Doha, Qatar for Treasured Moments
The Ritz-Carlton, Doha invites you to discover elevated hospitality at one of the leading luxury hotels in Doha, Qatar. Blending modern sophistication with rich local heritage, this landmark Doha 5-star resort offers spacious rooms and signature suites with sea, city, and skyline views, while the exclusive Club Lounge provides personalized service. Guests can enjoy award-winning restaurants and lounges, from innovative Pan-Asian fusion cuisine to a contemporary steakhouse, with many praising the variety and quality of dining. Designed for relaxation and recreation, this beach hotel in Doha also features indoor and outdoor facilities, including a semi-Olympic indoor pool, two tennis courts, a squash court, and a fully equipped fitness center. Wellness seekers can rejuvenate at The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Doha, or relax at the private beach island, where a peaceful resort atmosphere meets the city.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Doha?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Doha is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Doha allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Doha.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Doha?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Doha. Self parking is complimentary for guests.
Off-site parking is also available near The Ritz-Carlton, Doha. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Doha have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Doha has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Doha booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Doha?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Doha is Hamad International Airport (DOH). DOH is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Doha have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Doha has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
