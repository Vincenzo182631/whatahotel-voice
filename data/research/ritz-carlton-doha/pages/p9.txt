Elevate Your Stay
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.
Discover Club Rooms
An Indelible Experience
Club Lounge Access
The Ritz-Carlton, Doha Club Lounge, exclusively for guests aged 21 and above, offers refined culinary delights and personalized services throughout your stay. Located on the 23rd floor with panoramic views, enjoy dedicated check-in and check-out, along with complimentary refreshments and more exclusive benefits. Enhance any room or suite with Club Lounge access for a nominal fee upon check-in.

Dedicated Concierge
From dining reservations to excursion recommendations, our dedicated Club Lounge Concierge team elevates your experience, catering to you personally to ensure your stay is exceptional. The team is available from 7.00 am to 11.00 pm, daily for your individualized needs.

A Symphony of Tastes
Daily Culinary Presentations
Five times each day, our Club Lounge offers complimentary culinary experiences for your convenience. These daily offerings (breakfast, lunch, hors d'oeuvres, and pastry delights) tell the destination’s story through our locally sourced ingredients, iconic dishes, and classical drinks.

For Your Convenience
In-Room Amenities
A Club stay ensures that all your in-room needs are catered to. Your morning begins with a wake-up call service accompanied by your choice of freshly brewed coffee or fine tea.
