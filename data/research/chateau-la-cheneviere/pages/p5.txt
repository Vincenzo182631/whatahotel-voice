From **Paris** : take the A13 motorway via Caen, direction Cherbourg. At Bayeux, take the D6 towards Port-en-Bessin.

The Chenevière is 1 kilometer before the roundabout at Port-en-Bessin on the right at the bottom of a shaded driveway

En train : Gare de **Bayeux** (10km).

The hotel can arrange for transfers to and from Bayeux Train Station.

For more information about airport transfers, please contact us:

- Contact us by email: reservation@lacheneviere.com
