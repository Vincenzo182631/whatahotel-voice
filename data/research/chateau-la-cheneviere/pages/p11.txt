There's nothing bad about checking into an 18th-century Norman chateau—especially not when it comes on 30 acres, with a heated outdoor pool, apiary, and a restaurant supplied with ingredients from the home’s own vegetable garden. Like most things native to Normandy, though, Château La Chenevrière does harbor its own history: It was occupied by the Germans during World War II, and then the Americans after the D-Day landings before being fully restored in 1988.
