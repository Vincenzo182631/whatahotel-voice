# The park & ​​the gardens of the Château

## The park

## The park

La Chenevière is set in a park of 12 hectares of which 2 include the walled garden with a heated outdoor pool (open from mid-May until mid-September), a tennis court and bicycles.

## The Plants

## The Plants

The castle's rose gardens, fragrant flowerbeds and numerous **exotic trees** make this an ideal retreat for enjoying serene rest or invigorating moments.

Every year in September, Chenevière invites the best artisans and nurserymen in the country to **the Plant Festival**.

## The permaculture vegetable garden and the production of honey

## The permaculture vegetable garden and the production of honey

The kitchen garden at La Chenevière, cultivated using permaculture principles, supplies fresh herbs, fruits, and vegetables to the tables of both restaurants. The honey served at the breakfast buffet comes directly from the château's beehives and is also available for purchase.
