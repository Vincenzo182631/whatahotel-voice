# Chateau La Cheneviere

Welcome to Château **La Chenevière**, a five-star hotel located on the Normandy coast, between Bayeux and the D-Day landing beaches.

This former 18th-century manor house, nestled in a romantic park, welcomes you within its walls steeped in history. Completely restored in 1988, it has retained its **old-world charm** to offer you an exceptional stay today, marked by **luxury and serenity**.

Settle into one of its 30 spacious and comfortable rooms, and let us offer you castle life: discreet service, personalized welcome, high-end services.

At **La Chenevière**, finesse and elegance can also be appreciated on the plate: visit the Château restaurants for a **gastronomic journey**.

Our rooms

Spacious & refined

La Chenevière offers its guests spacious and refined rooms. The unique decoration of each of them harmoniously combines the charm of the old and modern comfort. The wood paneling and marble fireplaces contribute to the enchantment that only old residences can offer.

These pleasant rooms in pastel tones deliver, each in their own way, a feeling of fullness for a cozy atmosphere, an elegant and romantic haven.

Discover

Le Botaniste

Locally sourced

Together, Didier Robin, Chef Director and Hugo Genty, Head Chef at the Botaniste, have put together a refined, seasonal and inventive menu which takes guests on a gastronomic experience, made in Normandy. The creative and subtle flavours can be paired with wines from our cellar selected by our sommelier from among the best vintages.

Discover

Le Petit Jardin

Bucolic restaurant

In a romantic, rustic setting, Le Petit Jardin awaits you in La Chenevière’s old conservatory. The menu focuses on fresh, high-quality products, all locally sourced or from the château’s permaculture vegetable garden. Le Petit Jardin is open for lunch and dinner.

Discover

The garden

English charm

La Chenevière is nestled within a 12-hectare park, including 2 hectares of walled gardens. Here you'll find a heated outdoor swimming pool, open from mid-May to mid-September, an orangery, a tennis court, and bicycles available for guests. After strolling along the lime-lined avenue, you'll discover a magnificent English garden surrounding a former 18th-century manor house. Cedars of Lebanon, ginkgo biloba trees, and centuries-old sequoias stand alongside a rich collection of old roses, while wisteria and ivy elegantly adorn the walls of the former stables.

Discover

The swimming pool

Sport & relaxation

Sun loungers, parasols, palm trees… the Château's swimming pool welcomes you in a verdant setting. The outdoor pool, open and heated from mid-May to mid-September, has a small children's pool.

A true haven of peace within the park, the swimming pool offers privacy and tranquility sheltered by its high stone walls. It is adjacent to the restaurant Le Petit Jardin.
