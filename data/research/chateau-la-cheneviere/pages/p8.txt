# Le Petit Jardin, bistrot restaurant in Normandy

Le Petit Jardin is the bistrot restaurant located in the chateau's old conservatory, near the swimming pool at the end of the garden.

It is open for lunch and dinner from Thursday to Monday. The restaurant opened in 2019 and serves casual French cuisine with locally-sourced produces, as well as vegetables and herbs from La Cheneviere's own permaculture garden.

LE PETIT JARDIN'S MENU

## From the garden to your plate

At Le Petit Jardin we love vegetables, even in desserts like candied fennel for example or mushroom caramel.

The evening menu changes regularly depending on the harvest, and the lunch menu changes every two weeks, specifically on Fridays. Customers can choose between a starter and a main course or a main course and a dessert for €20, or a starter, a main course, and a dessert for €25. This set menu is available every lunchtime except Sundays and public holidays.

They always offer vegetarian plates, fish of the day and meat accompanied by vegetables of the moment.

On the evening menu of the bistro restaurant, we find the vegetables of the moment with always a few vegetarian and sometimes vegan options.

Aperitifs to share are available for lunch and dinner, with homemade pâté, pickled vegetables from our garden or our famous spring rolls with goat's cheese and honey from our own beehives.

To pair with your meal, our team suggests organic or natural wines, seasonal cocktails and mocktails, as well as a variety of iced teas.

In the summer, on Sunday lunchtimes, the menu changes and offers dishes to share

## Refined cuisine in the heart of a green setting

Guests can sit inside under the glass roof, near a magnificent bar decorated with plants, on rattan chairs with a view of the swimming pool\*. Or outside on the terrace, in a corner of the garden surrounded by old rose bushes and century-old trees.

We invite customers to go for a walk in the garden of Château la Chenevière, to discover the permaculture vegetable garden or the brand new flower garden.

The restaurant welcomes groups and can also be privatized for events such as birthdays, weddings or simply a small party with friends!

_\*which is only reserved for hotel guests._

The small garden is open for noon and evening from Thursday to Monday.
