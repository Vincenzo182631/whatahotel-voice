# Hotel in Normandy with swimming pool in a wooded park

## A heated swimming pool in Normandy

To access our heated outdoor swimming pool, you will have the pleasure of crossing our park and taking one of our tree-lined paths. This picturesque route will take you to the heart of our walled garden, just a few steps from our bistro Le Petit Jardin, where you can taste delicious dishes cooked with products from our vegetable garden. Once you have reached the swimming pool, you will discover an idyllic relaxation area, where you can relax on one of our deckchairs, sheltered under large parasols, while enjoying the view of the castle's old orangery and the majestic century-old trees that surround it. Our palm and citrus trees add a Mediterranean touch to the heart of Normandy.

Our swimming pool, 16 meters long, is maintained at a constant temperature of 26 degrees, creating an ideal environment for swimming. So that the whole family can fully enjoy this aquatic area, we have provided a children's pool at the end of the pool. The youngest ones will be able to have fun under the supervision of their parents.

The swimming pool is generally open from mid-May to mid-September, but due to the unpredictability of the Normandy weather during the seasons, it is best to contact our reception before booking. We will keep you informed of the exact opening and closing dates of our pool so you can plan your getaway.

## Enjoy cocktails by the pool

Just a few steps from our swimming pool, you will discover the charming conservatory with Le Petit Jardin's bar. This elegant structure is surrounded by a green setting. From inside the conservatory you will have a breathtaking view of the swimming pool, creating a calming atmosphere. Guests can relax while sipping a variety of drinks to quench their thirst. Our drinks menu includes various choices, from refreshing water to elaborate cocktails, including beers, wines, Champagnes and homemade iced teas.

If you prefer to combine your refreshment with a gourmet meal, the Petit Jardin welcomes you to its table from Thursday to Monday for lunch. You can enjoy delicious cuisine in this peaceful environment, while admiring the swimming pool.

## Relax with our soothing treatments

Enhance your relaxing day by combining the pool with the soothing experience of our massage sessions. Various types of full-body massages are available.
