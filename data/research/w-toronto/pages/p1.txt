# Welcome to W Toronto

## DISCOVER A LUXURY OASIS IN DOWNTOWN TORONTO

Discover a luxury urban oasis in downtown Toronto. Experience the city’s first W Hotel, a luxury lifestyle hotel in downtown Toronto, just steps from the iconic fashion and culture district of Yorkville. Designed as a luxury escape reflecting the locale, check into jewel-toned, theatre-inspired guest rooms and signature suites featuring city and courtyard views. Discover two bars & restaurants: Living...See More Room, the hotel’s signature lobby lounge and coffee bar, and TONO by Michelin-starred Chef Akira Back, a rooftop hideaway where Nikkei cuisine meets skyline views. With a lineup of live performances, Canada’s first Sound Suite recording studio, and always-on programming, expect magnetic energy from day to night. A curated collection of local artworks reflects the city’s diversity, from rooftop to guestrooms. Explore an elevated hotel in Toronto, Canada, where culture and creativity collide – at W Toronto, you’re always centre-stage.See Less

## Customise Your Stay at W Toronto

W Toronto places you in the heart of Yorkville, surrounded by culture, fashion and nightlife. Experience bold design, elevated dining, and vibrant social spaces that reflect the energy of the city. Explore what awaits and begin planning your visit.

### Taste & Explore

Fuel your stay, see and be seen at W Toronto’s culinary destination. Taste the perfect mix of local and global flavours.

### TONO by Akira Back

Experience TONO by Akira Back. Born from the vibrant interplay between Japanese precision and Peruvian passion, Nikkei cuisine is a bold blend of flavour, culture and storytelling. Expect unique pairings and raw artistry that honour two culinary worlds.

Explore

### W Café & Bar

An extension of Living Room, W Café & Bar is the perfect start to your day, featuring specialty coffees & teas by local favourite Barocco. Pair your morning beverage with locally sourced pastries. Dine in or grab & go at W Toronto’s Café & Bar.

Explore

### LIVING ROOM

Experience some of the most celebrated restaurants in downtown Toronto, including the Living Room’s menu featuring signature cocktails inspired by the hotel's artwork, dishes inspired by the city, and programming to take you from day to night.

Explore

### TOKI Listening Bar

TOKI is an intimate listening speakeasy rooted in a love of vinyl and the power of sound. Rare records, whisky‑forward cocktails, and refined craft mixology come together in an immersive space meant to be discovered, not announced.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Toronto?

The check-in time at W Toronto is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Toronto allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Toronto. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Toronto?

On-site parking is available at W Toronto. Self parking is available at W Toronto, with rates of $25.00 per hour, and $30.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $66.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Toronto have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Toronto has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Toronto booking.

What is the closest airport near W Toronto?

The closest airport near W Toronto is Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport (YTZ). YTZ is located approximately 7.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Toronto have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Toronto has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
