## The Yorkville Scene

Much like the city it calls home, our hotel in Yorkville, Toronto, is in an area that's a melting pot of style and culture, where high-brow boutiques share space with urban parks and minimalist architecture rubs elbows with Victorian rowhomes. Ground Zero for Toronto's buzziest events, Yorkville is flush with galleries, trendy eateries, museums, and some of the city's best people-watching. Spend the day browsing the shops on the stretch of Bloor Street known as ""Mink Mile"" or wandering Gallery Row on Hazelton Avenue — just be sure to save enough energy for patio- and rooftop-bar hopping come nightfall.

### W Sound Suite

Immerse yourself in W Hotels’ sound studio for artists, musicians, and novices alike. From light recordings to live streaming, content creation and acoustic performances, W Sound Suite is your spot to be inspired. Designed by Curves by Sean Brown, the suite features a lineup of iconic CD rugs, bespoke furnishings, and music-inspired décor. Go ahead – sing your heart out, improvise a new beat, unleash your creativity, and let the music play.

Must-Experience Hot Spots

### Yorkville Murals

Yorkville is quickly becoming a hotbed of thought-provoking public art, thanks in no small part to this annual, eight-day cultural event, which bills itself as "a celebration of contemporary muralism." Expect plenty in the way of activations: art installations, artist convos, panel discussions, film screenings and more.

### Toronto Pride

This month-long celebration of Toronto's diverse 2SLGBTQ+ community — think headliner performances, street fairs, pool parties, drag shows, and more — ends in a blowout of a festival weekend each June. Events are centered around Bloor Street and the nearby Church-Wellesley neighborhood, which holds the banner as Toronto's Gay Village.

### Pamper Yourself: Majesty’s Pleasure Spa

Where beauty meets self-care, your next mani-pedi, massage, or facial awaits at Majesty’s Pleasure, Yorkville’s premium spa & salon. Gather, indulge, and unwind with luxury products and delicious, rejuvenating mocktails. Want to book your next appointment? Let our Insider Team know.

Exploring Yorkville

### Shopping

Wander Bloor Street, the epicentre of Toronto fashion, and you'll pass Hermès, Chanel and Balenciaga storefronts, as well as Toronto’s trademark high-end department store, Holt Renfrew. At Yorkville Village, you'll find outposts of Canadian labels — some exclusive to Toronto — and global designers intermingling with trendy eateries, art installations and pop-up boutiques.

### Galleries

Whether you're moved by pop art, photography or contemporary work, the world-class galleries that dot Yorkville hold some of Canada’s biggest names in the art world and serve as Toronto's exhibition hub. Browse Taglialatella Gallery, W Toronto’s local art curator partner designing the artwork that adorns the hotel and guestrooms.

### Museums

Near the hotel, you’ll find the Royal Ontario Museum, its Daniel Libeskind-designed crystal set against the 1914 stone facade. A landmark housing over 13 million artworks, artifacts, and specimens. Nearby, explore the Bata Shoe Museum, highlighting footwear’s history and cultural impact. Continue to the Gardiner Museum, celebrating clay and ceramics.

## Royal Ontario Museum

Discover art, culture, and natural history at the Royal Ontario Museum, located just minutes from W Toronto. Home to 18 million artworks, cultural objects, and natural history specimens, the ROM offers galleries and exhibitions spanning cultures and time periods from around the world.

Plan your visit and explore current exhibitions, admission, and operating hours on the ROM website.

Learn More

## Bata Shoe Museum

Step into the fascinating world of footwear at the Bata Shoe Museum, an iconic cultural gem just moments from W Toronto. Exclusive to hotel guests, enjoy a special 2-for-1 admission offer and discover the art, culture, and history behind one of the world's most extraordinary collections of footwear. From ancient Egyptian sandals to contemporary designer shoes, explore captivating exhibitions that span more than 4,500 years. Guests are encouraged to contact the W Insider Team in advance to inquire about availability and reserve their tickets.

Hours are subject to change. Please visit the Bata Shoe Museum website for the latest operating hours and visitor information.

Casa Loma

A historic landmark and top attraction in Toronto, Canada’s very own castle is a must see. Originally constructed as a residence, the majestic mansion is now open for public browsing and exclusive events. Learn about its history, incredible gardens, and architecture.

CN Tower

The CN Tower, Canada’s most recognizable and celebrated attraction, is a must see. The world-famous landmark stands 1,815ft. tall and includes spectacular views with floor-to-ceiling panoramic walls. Enjoy EdgeWalk if you dare, 360 Restaurant with rotating floors and ongoing events.

The Distillery District

This pedestrian only, 19th-century square is home to a wide variety shops and sculptures. The quaint cobblestone streets, harkening back to the district’s days as a whiskey distillery, are now lined with an assortment of local restaurants, bars, boutiques, galleries, music venues, and more. Come December, the District transforms into a magical Christmas Village.

Scotiabank Arena

This renowned multi-purpose arena is recognized as Canada’s premier sport and entertainment venue. Immerse yourself into the Toronto spirit with the excitement of a hockey match or a basketball showdown.

Rogers Centre

Home to the MLB’s Toronto Blue Jays, Rogers Centre is Toronto’s premier stadium. Catch a ballgame or one of the many other live events hosted throughout the year.

Toronto Harbourfront

Take a tranquil stroll along the Toronto Harbourfront and soak in the stunning views of Lake Ontario. From waterfront parks to hip shops & restaurants, there is no shortage of things to do. Feeling adventurous? Take the ferry to the Toronto Islands for bayside beaches and the Centreville Amusement Park.

Toronto International Film Festival

Every September, Toronto hosts one of the largest and most prestigious film festivals in the world. With tickets available to the public, seize your opportunity to see premieres of Hollywood’s Oscar darlings and artful indie projects.

Rogers Stadium

Check out Toronto’s newest concert venue. Launching in the summer of 2025, Rogers Stadium will be hosting some of the hottest acts in music! Located at 123 Garratt Blvd Toronto, ON M3K.

Union Station

Located in the center of Downtown Toronto, this major intermodal transportation hub can take you anywhere you need to go. Union Station is easily accessed via the underground Path and is just steps away from major landmarks such as the CN Tower, Ripley’s Aquarium, Rogers Centre, Scotiabank Arena, Metro Toronto Convention Centre, and the Hockey Hall of Fame.

BMO Field

BMO Field is an open-air stadium at Toronto’s Exhibition Place.

## An Eye for Art

Our art curation is a love note to Toronto and the natural beauty, diversity, creativity, and community that bring it to life. The "W Monument" by Sid Lee is a dynamic image created by combining patterns found in crystal rocks with vibrant colors. The driveway and elevator landing feature bold pieces by artist Mikael Bandrup, who used bright colors and organically flowing shapes to create "Below the Surface" and "Hidden Gem." In the Living Room, Sage Barnes' "Self Care" encourages guests to pause and reflect, while in guest rooms, Julia Mercanti’s "I Miss You!" pillows bring to life whimsical wanderlust while wall art featuring song lyrics from Charles de Lint expresses hope and compassion. Up on the roof, Kirsten McCrae’s "Forms of Joyful Engagement" murals celebrate the stories and people of Yorkville’s past and present.
