# Explore Our Toronto Accommodation

## Stay at W Toronto

Our luxury hotel rooms and W hotel suites in downtown Toronto offer an open concept layout with elevated city and courtyard views. Perfect for work and play, stretch out in jewel-toned theater-inspired rooms complete with Mix Bar and the essentials to turn up or turn down during your stay. The hotel’s appreciation for the arts and fashion district extends to the rooms with Toronto-inspired artwork...See More and design elements. At W Toronto, you’re always center-stage.See Less

Plan Ahead, Stay Elevated

Elevate your stay before you arrive. Reserve welcome amenities, request early check-in or late check-out, secure dining reservations, enjoy in-room dining, and discover exclusive guest perks alongside the latest experiences and programming at W Toronto.

Featured Suite

### Penthouse I Suite

Live the Suite Life in our premium bi-level suites. Continuing W Toronto’s celebration of the city’s performance art scenes, the Penthouse I Suites offer a nod to Toronto glamour dripping in jewel-tones and theatrical design elements. This luxury accommodation in downtown Toronto features spacious living rooms complete with oversized Mix Bar perfect for entertaining, dramatic uplighting and plush velvet decor and spa-inspired bathrooms including vanity, deep soaker tubs and shower for two.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Toronto have a work space?

No, the rooms at W Toronto do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Toronto?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at W Toronto. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Toronto?

Some rooms at W Toronto permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Toronto have fridges?

No, rooms at W Toronto do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W Toronto have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Toronto have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Toronto have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Toronto have hair dryers.
