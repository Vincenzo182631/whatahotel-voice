### A Taste of Local

The best food in Canada is (no surprise) from Canada. Whether you’re ordering a late-night bite from in-room dining or Living Room, you’ll find ingredients from local suppliers including honey from Bee Local 416, fish and meat from Woodward Meats, produce from Muddy Crops and cheese from Brights Cheese & Butter.
