Located just a 5-minute walk from the hotel, this world-class landmark department store in Southwest China features a landscape park at ground level and houses over a thousand luxury brands underground. It creates a premium, fashion-forward multidimensional retail environment anchored by its iconic landmark—the "Tower of Life".
