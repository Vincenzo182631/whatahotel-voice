## FAQs for InterContinental Chengdu Global Center

Does InterContinental Chengdu Global Center have a pool?

InterContinental Chengdu Global Center has 1 outdoor pool.

Semi-outdoor heated swimming pool

1 semi-outdoor butterfly pool (533 square meters), 1 semi-outdoor infinity pool, and 2 semi-outdoor hot spring pools.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Chengdu Global Center?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Chengdu Global Center for hotel guests.

The parking lot is not managed by the hotel and is complimentary for in-house guests one car per room. Please register at Concierge Desk. Not applicable to group or conference guests. Parking fees: RMB8 for the first 2 hours, RMB4 per hour thereafter.

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Chengdu Global Center?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Chengdu Global Center.

Does InterContinental Chengdu Global Center offer free airport shuttle service?

InterContinental Chengdu Global Center does not offer free airport shuttle service.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Chengdu Global Center for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Chengdu Global Center have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Chengdu Global Center has 6 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Chengdu Global Center?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Chengdu Global Center.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Chengdu Global Center?

Check-in at InterContinental Chengdu Global Center is from 2:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Chengdu Global Center offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Chengdu Global Center offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Chengdu Global Center from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Chengdu Global Center is conveniently located near Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport (CTU), approximately 9.26 miles away (14.9 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Chengdu Global Center?

InterContinental Chengdu Global Center is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Panda Base - 16.16 miles away (26 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Chengdu Global Center puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
