### Poor conditions and service.

a day ago

Annual A/C maintenance was going on, but we were not notified before our arrival. The hotel should have known in advance and should have been able to notify their guests beforehand, or even at check-in, but they didn't. Staff could hardly speak English, and not even the room service or operation desks when you call. We were blatantly asked if we could speak mandarin. We were pretty much left to find our own ways across the vast property from the first moment we stepped into the door. The staff at the door did not bother to direct us or provide any assistance on to where we can find the check-in counter, or even after check-in, we were just pointed towards the general direction of the building we were supposed to go, and was somehow expected to magically maneuver our way through to our rooms.

The room was stuffy, especially when the A/C wasn't working. When called, we were delivered an ice bucket. And a simple "sorry".
