Grand Hyatt Kauai Resort & Spa
Flowing blue lagoons, white-hot Hawaiian glamour, and superb poolside service will make you say "mahalo" to this tropical paradise.

Past guests have described the Grand Hyatt on Kauai's sunny South Shore as “heaven on earth”—and we agree. Ringed by flourishing tropical gardens and flowing blue lagoons, this low-rise Keoneloa Bay resort has an open-air design that recalls the island glamour of the 1920s and ’30s, with whitewashed facades, wood-trimmed windowsills, and immaculate marble entryways at every turn. Look a little deeper, and you’ll be greeted by small sprinklings of luxury, like rain shower shower heads, fresh Hawaiian-print linens, and four-post mahogany beds, with windows wide enough to take in the expansive mountain or beachfront views.
At Stevenson’s Sushi & Spirits, where the 27-foot carved koa bar sets a gorgeous backdrop to the spot’s extensive selection of spirits and nibbles. But if you like a little sunshine with your moonshine, check out the Shipwreck Bar, where you can swim and swig through the saltwater lagoon. Luaus are held Sunday and Wednesday nights. Be sure to book an appointment at the Anara Spa, where the facials and full-body massages are taken under a
hale
, a thatched roof bungalow, and the scent of vanilla oil mingles with fragrant floral overgrowth. Or, simply float down a lava-rock lined lazy river pool past waterfalls.
