## Transportation

Getting here

Lihue Airport (LIH)15 miles15 miles

Transportation Options

Taxi Services

Taxis are available. Arrange in advance or call from an airport phone, located curbside at the baggage claim area.

Hired Drivers

Uber and Lyft are available.

Rental Car

All major companies are represented on Kauai. To pick up your car at the airport, proceed from the baggage claim area to the rent-a-car booths across the street from the terminal. An Avis counter is also available in the lobby of our resort.

Getting around

Hotel Shuttle

Our hotel offers nearby area (Koloa/Poipu) shuttle service upon request from 4:30–10 p.m.
