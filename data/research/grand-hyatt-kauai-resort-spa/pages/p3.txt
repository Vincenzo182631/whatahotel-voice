# Dining

Sweeping ocean views welcome you to the beachfront restaurants and bars at Grand Hyatt Kauai Resort & Spa. Where possible, menu items are sustainably sourced from responsible local vendors. For dinner, choose from a romantic open-air seafood and steak restaurant set over a lagoon, delicious sushi or burgers, salads, pasta and more. Start the day with a breakfast buffet, coffee and pastries. Enjoy lunch poolside and then take in an authentic evening Kauai luau on Poipu Beach. For a private dining experience, the resort offers a starlit beachside dinner for two. Or, dine on your lanai with In-Room dining.

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.
