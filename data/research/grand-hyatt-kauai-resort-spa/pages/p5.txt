### Not ready for guests: no beach access, no guest communication, no lounge

Not ready for guests, and nobody told us anything

Communication, access and setting expectations. The hotel failed on all three during our stay.

Power outages came with same-day notice. The Grand Club lounge opened late with zero communication. The tiki torches weren't lit the entire trip and no one said why. When we asked, employees had no clue what was going on.

There was no real beach access. A perfectly good spot in the middle of the property, with no exposed irrigation pipes, stayed closed. Instead the hotel insisted everyone use the public beach or the rocky access on the right, both far less safe. When guests went through the barrier to reach the beach they paid for, landscaping staff yelled at them.

The lagoon was closed the whole time, with noisy construction going on. The hotel also put up a fence that blocks the ocean view, and all sound of the ocean, from the entire outdoor space.

The pool was cold the first four days. There were no life jackets or floaties for toddlers, just cheap plastic ones you had to pay for. The poolside staff were rude and explained nothing about the order cards or where to wait for food. We sat at our table with our number and no one brought the food or told us you have to stand by the bar.

The food was lousy the entire stay. Every staff interaction was brief and rude, and nothing about the service felt welcoming. Our room had cockroaches.

The mismanagement at this hotel is astounding. I've never seen anything like it at a property this size. Hyatt needs to fly in a completely new management team to take over the recovery. Whoever is in charge now is so out of touch with the guest experience they shouldn't work in the hotel industry.

### Grand Hyatt Kauai Resort & Spa

Overall, we had a very good stay at the Grand Hyatt Kauai Resort & Spa. The resort is beautiful, clean, and very well maintained, and the staff throughout the property were friendly and welcoming.

That said, both the resort and our rooms felt somewhat worn and outdated. Considering the price charged for the rooms, I believe the property is due for a renovation and could use some updating.

I especially want to recognize the housekeeping staff. They did an excellent job, and Luzviminda Austria was exceptional. She was friendly, attentive, and took great care of our rooms. Employees like her truly make a difference in the guest experience.

My only real disappointment was our check-in experience. We arrived around 11:00 AM and completely understood that the official check-in time was 4:00 PM. We had three rooms, and the front desk employee told us they should be ready shortly. Based on that information, we had lunch and then waited in the lobby. Around 2:00 PM, we checked again and were once again told the rooms should be ready shortly.

At approximately 3:45 PM, after waiting for several hours, we checked again and were told that our rooms were ready. The employee apologized that no one had called or texted to let us know.

I wish the front desk staff had simply been upfront with us when we arrived and said that the rooms likely would not be available until the 4:00 PM check-in time. Had we known that, we would have opened our suitcases, changed into appropriate clothing, and spent the afternoon enjoying the pool and beach instead of waiting around the lobby expecting the rooms to be ready at any moment.

Overall, it was still a very enjoyable stay. With some renovations and a more communicative and personable approach at the front desk, the Grand Hyatt Kauai could provide an experience that better matches the premium price and the otherwise excellent level of service we received.
