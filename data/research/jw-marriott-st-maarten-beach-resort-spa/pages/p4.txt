Life by the Sea

## An Island State of Mind

Life along the water shapes each day, from sunrise yoga and water aerobics to evenings accompanied by live music near the pool. When the island calls, venture offshore to discover the Underwater Museum, a gallery of 300 reef sculptures set beneath St. Maarten’s coast.

Indulge the Senses

## Dining Under Caribbean Skies

Relax poolside at Carambola beneath open blue skies and enjoy seafood served fresh, light, and beautifully composed. Step into Philipsburg for a curated mix of French, Creole, Chinese, and Indian cuisine, ideal for a night that calls for variety. Then take the ferry to St. Barths for top-tier dining and luxury shopping, where impeccable service and an unhurried setting bring the evening to a close.

Wellness and Leisure

### Hibiscus Spa

Retreat to Hibiscus Spa for serene relaxation in 16 specialty treatment rooms, where massages ease travel-worn tension and facials cleanse, smooth, and hydrate, leaving you feeling grounded and your skin beautifully cared for.

### Oceanfront Pool Oasis

Cool off in our heated outdoor pool facing the sea, then glide over to the swim-up bar for a cocktail without ever leaving the water.

### The Pickleball Court

Enjoy pickleball, the fast, social racket sport that blends the best of tennis and ping-pong, set in an open-air setting designed for spirited play.

Culinary Arts & Mixology

## JW Culinary School

Step into JW Culinary School and settle at your station as ingredients are laid out, tools are set, and key techniques are demonstrated up close. Mixology takes you from building the drink to the final garnish, while culinary workshops focus on prep, searing and simmering, seasoning, and plating, with chefs guiding each step. In an intimate group, you taste as you go, compare notes, and pick up techniques you’d miss on your own. Private classes, team-building sessions, and themed culinary events are available upon request.

### Sail, Skydive, and Venture

Helm the America’s Cup 12 Metre, then unwind on a sunset sail to nearby cays. Jump tandem from 10,000 feet at up to 125 mph, then ferry to Anguilla’s Greg Norman course or St. Barths for luxury boutiques and fine dining.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa offers a complimentary, 8:00 AM-6:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa have an on-site spa?

Yes, Hibiscus Spa is located on-site at JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa?

Blue Bubbles Sports & Tours, St Barthélemy (St. Barths), Anguilla, Rainforest Adventure St Maarten, Loterie Farm and more are nearby JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa.
