## Discover a revitalizing beachfront hotel in St. Maarten

Welcome to the exquisite JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa, a luxurious haven of mindfulness, inspiration and nourishment. Situated on the captivating Dawn Beach, our luxury hotel in St. Maarten is a serene island retreat with a luxury spa, nestled amidst lush greenery and pristine white sandy beaches. It is the ideal destination for those in pursuit of unrivaled relaxation. The Art of Cooking

## The Art of Cooking

Observe and practice alongside seasoned chefs as Caribbean and international cuisine is prepared at the counter. Using professional-grade cookware and premium equipment, guests take part in an intimate setting limited to eight participants for culinary sessions and twelve for mixology. The experience closes around the table, where dishes are plated and shared in a convivial setting, carrying the skills and memories well beyond your stay.

Learn More

The Destination

## Across Land and Sea

Explore beneath the surface across more than 50 dive sites, including an underwater museum of 300 reef sculptures, then look down from 10,000 feet during a tandem parachute jump as the Caribbean stretches in every direction. On land, experience the island on horseback, bike through Philipsburg, hike Guana Bay, or cross the water by sail and ferry. Between excursions, discover historic landmarks, hands-on perfume workshops at Tijon, and a dining scene influenced by French, Creole, Chinese, and Indian traditions.

### Along the Shoreline

Overlooking the shoreline, the pool draws the eye toward wide sea views, with palm-lined seating and shaded loungers surrounding it. Guests enjoy heated water throughout the day with no set closing hours, along with towel service, a dedicated children’s area, and a swim-up bar for drinks without leaving the pool. From morning through early evening, guests swim, rest poolside, and enjoy live performances on the deck.

Carambola

## Seafood Dining

Set beneath open-air colonnades overlooking the shoreline, the restaurant welcomes guests throughout the day for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Mornings feature a continental breakfast buffet, while evenings focus on seafood-led dinner service. Signature dishes include the Lobster Roll, Grouper Tacos, Cedar Plank Salmon, and Seared Scallops. On Sundays, the setting shifts to a generous brunch with a raw seafood bar, roasted sirloin, jerk chicken, and bottomless champagne.

On the Water

## Sailing the Caribbean

Sail across open waters through the America’s Cup 12 Metre Challenge or during sunset sails and excursions to nearby cays. Each outing centers on time at sea, combining classic sailing with views that open from the shoreline to the wider Caribbean.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for $20.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa is Princess Juliana International Airport (SXM). SXM is located approximately 14.6 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
