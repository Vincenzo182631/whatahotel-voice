# Seaside Gourmet Experience

## Dining

Explore flavors shaped by the coast, from seafood classics like the Lobster Roll, Cedar Plank Salmon, and Seared Scallops at Carambola, to Mediterranean dishes at Liguria, where Wagyu Ribeye, Miso-Glazed Sea Bass, house-made pastas, and vegetarian specialties anchor the menu. Caribbean bites and tropical cocktails take center stage at Breeze, while the island’s broader culinary scene stretches from...See More Philipsburg’s global kitchens to Marigot’s waterfront tables and Grand Case’s celebrated Tuesday night street festival. The experience continues at the JW Culinary School, where hands-on cooking sessions turn local and international flavors into something guests can take with them beyond their stay.See Less

### Tropical & Mediterranean

Begin the morning with a continental breakfast by the water, then move into the day with Mediterranean plates at Liguria, where Tuna Crudo and focaccia finished with truffle butter arrive at an easy pace. As evening approaches, take a seat at the Opal Lobby Lounge, enjoy signature cocktails like the Gin-Z or Spicy Chile Margarita, and let conversation carry the night forward.

## Sunsets by the Water

Share an evening by the water with someone special as golden hour settles over the sea, casting warm light across open views at our St. Maarten luxury resort. Solo travelers can linger just as easily, taking in the Caribbean air and enjoying tropical cocktails at Breeze as the day fades into evening.

Caribbean Sounds

## Nightfall at Dawn Beach

Evenings begin on the Lower Pool Deck with live performances three nights a week, featuring Latin, Caribbean, steel pan, and French music. During the performances, guests gather with cocktails and poolside dishes from Liquids Pool Bar, enjoying the energy of the music by the water. As the night deepens, the experience continues at the Opal Lobby Lounge, where creative mixologists prepare cocktails in a more intimate setting, inviting conversation to linger well into the late hours.
