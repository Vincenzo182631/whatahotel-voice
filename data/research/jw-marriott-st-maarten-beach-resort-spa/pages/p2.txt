# Wake by the Sea

## Accommodations

Stay surrounded by salt air and the sounds of the Caribbean Sea at JW Marriott St. Maarten Beach Resort & Spa. Our guest rooms and suites feature private patios, balconies, open decks, and private plunge pools. Inside, modern interiors and luxurious bedding invite rest between sun-filled days along Dawn Beach. Meals arrive through in-room dining, keeping the pace entirely your own, with concierge support...See More available as plans come together. Time at the Hibiscus Spa and our Fitness Center completes a stay centered on relaxation, wellness, and spectacular ocean views.See Less

In-Room Dining

### The Luxury of Staying In

Welcome the morning sun with breakfast served in your guest room, accompanied by the sights and sounds of the ocean. Chef-crafted mains and late-night bites follow as the light shifts outside, kept entirely within the privacy of your space.

Learn More

Resort Life

## A World Set Apart

Slip into cool water at your private plunge pool and wash away the afternoon heat. The experience continues across the resort, with access to the Hibiscus Spa, a 24-hour fitness center, rooftop pickleball, and cabanas set directly along Dawn Beach.

Explore

Concierge Services

## Discovering the Island

As you arrive, the island begins to reveal itself. The concierge introduces guests to St. Maarten’s water adventures, land excursions, cultural landmarks, and culinary destinations, opening pathways across the island that unfold naturally over the course of your stay.

Learn More

## JW Culinary School

At the Chef’s Table

A short walk from your room brings you to the JW Culinary School, where seasoned chefs from the resort’s executive team and local culinary faculty welcome you inside for an intimate hands-on cooking session. Caribbean and international dishes come together step by step, turning technique, timing, and taste into an experience meant to be remembered long after your stay.
