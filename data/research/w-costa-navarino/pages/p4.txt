# Taste the Spirit of W Costa Navarino

## Dining

Savor the spirit of coastal living at W Costa Navarino, where dining becomes a journey through flavor and creativity. Each venue brings its own story to life, from authentic Messenian dishes to wholesome bites and bold grill experiences. Start your day at Platia, the vibrant breakfast square offering fresh coffee, baked bread, and local produce across the Coffee Shop, Bakery, Deli, and Cookery. Guests...See More love the rich breakfast variety and the choice of restaurants and bars across the resort. By the sea, Parelia celebrates Messenia’s essence with simple, generous sharing plates made with local ingredients. For something fresh, Green Pepper serves colorful, nourishing bowls throughout the day. As evening arrives, Piqantro brings Iberian-Messenian grill dishes cooked over olive wood, while Between and W Lounge keep the mood going with sunset spritzes, cocktails, and global flavors. Every Costa Navarino restaurant experience feels distinctly W.See Less

## The Philosophy of our Executive Chef

Inspired by the land and traditions of Messinia, Executive Chef Nikos Billis brings a fresh, contemporary lens to local cuisine. His culinary approach blends authenticity with creativity, highlighting seasonal ingredients, sustainable practices, and a true sense of place across every W Costa Navarino dining experience.

A Vibrant, Greek Inspired Beach Club

### Parelia

From “Parea” — togetherness — and “Paralia” — beach — Parelia is a celebration of Messenian cuisine, a living culinary tradition shaped by the land, the people, and the cultures that have passed through it. Under the direction of Chef Nikos Billis, each dish reflects his philosophy of locality, simplicity, and sustainability — flavors that are honest, vibrant, and deeply rooted in Messinia’s abundant...See More land and sea.See Less

### Piqantro, Navarino Hills Clubhouse

An olive-wood fired grill where Iberian inspiration meets Messenian ingredients in a bold celebration of flavor, fire, and connection. At Piqantro, culinary excellence flows seamlessly into unforgettable nights of music and celebration — a place where guests arrive for the view, stay for the flavors, and return for the energy. Small bites, premium cuts from around the world, and family-style dishes share the spotlight with curated sounds, creating a dining rhythm that blends local soul with global flair. More than a restaurant, Piqantro is an experience — vibrant, social, and made to remember.

## Wet Deck

The vibrant heart of W Costa Navarino, WET Deck captures the rhythm of the day — from laid-back mornings to golden-hour gatherings. Start your day with a refreshing swim or a morning smoothie by the pool, then let the atmosphere build with uplifting beats, handcrafted cocktails, and Mediterranean energy. As the sun dips over the Ionian Sea, the vibe shifts into effortless celebration — where music, connection, and style flow together, the W way.

## Green Pepper

Bright, fresh, and full of flavor, Green Pepper is the wellness hub of W Costa Navarino — where healthy living meets delicious food. Perfect for mindful mornings and leisurely brunches, it celebrates seasonal ingredients, colorful dishes, and nourishing combinations. From creative egg plates and vibrant poke bowls to crisp salads and smoothies, every bite is designed to refuel, recharge, and delight — naturally.
