### Suites & Villas Luxury Experience

Embark on a luxurious journey and explore unparalleled exclusivity. W Costa Navarino Suites and Villas offer a bespoke experience that delivers top-notch service, comfort, and style. Indulge in the Bespoke Villas and Suites experience exclusively crafted for guests reserving our Oasis I, Oasis II Villas, the Contemporary W Beachfront Suites with private pool, or our W Bayfront Suites with infinity Ionian Sea views.

## Oasis\*I Villas

Nestled along the captivating shoreline surrounds, the OASIS Villas offer an unparalleled experience of luxury and personalized services for the most selective of guests. Whether enjoying a private DJ session or savoring bespoke cocktails prepared by your own mixologist, each moment in the beachfront Villas is crafted to cater to the highest standards of discerning travelers. Step into the exquisite seafront two-bedroom villa or the three-bedroom sanctuary, each radiating a fusion of grace and refinement. Embrace the spacious interiors adorned with elegance, complemented by the tranquil landscape and a secluded pool just steps away.

## Oasis\*I Beachfront Infinity 3 Bedroom Villa

With a total space of 153 sqm, this beachfront Oasis I 3 Bedroom Villa offers unobstructed sea view, an extended terrace with an outdoor living and dining area with private infinity pool and direct access to the beach. One master bedroom with en-suite bathroom facilities, a second twin bedroom with zip & link beds and a private bathroom, a third bedroom with King bed and a private bathroom, a separate lounge and dining area. The villa also offers a fully equipped kitchen (available for use exclusively as part of a pre-booked private cooking experience), including a cooker hood, an oven, a dishwasher and a built-in fridge.

To book this Villa, there is minimum stay of 3 nights and the Villa accommodates a maximum of 6 guests.
