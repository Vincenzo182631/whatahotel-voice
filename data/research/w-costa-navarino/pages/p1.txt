W Costa Navarino marks the debut of the W Hotels brand in Greece. Set beachfront by the Ionian Sea in Navarino Bay, this Escape for adults and young adults (12+) opened in summer 2022, bringing fresh energy and a new perspective to the destination. The Destination

## Costa Navarino

Costa Navarino is the prime, sustainable destination in Greece’s southwest Peloponnese. One of the most unspoiled and breathtaking Mediterranean seaside landscapes, this area has been shaped by 4,500 years of history. Costa Navarino philosophy is driven by a genuine desire to promote Messinia, while protecting and preserving the region’s natural beauty and heritage.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Costa Navarino?

The check-in time at W Costa Navarino is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Costa Navarino allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Costa Navarino. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €50.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Costa Navarino?

On-site parking is available at W Costa Navarino. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Costa Navarino have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Costa Navarino has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Costa Navarino booking.

What is the closest airport near W Costa Navarino?

The closest airport near W Costa Navarino is Kalamata International Airport (KLX). KLX is located approximately 40.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Costa Navarino have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Costa Navarino has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
