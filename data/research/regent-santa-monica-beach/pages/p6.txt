# Experience Santa Monica

With golden beaches and colorful markets, luxury shopping, and oceanfront dining, Santa Monica is a place to indulge all the senses. Just minutes from the breathtaking coastline, world-class culture and entertainment await at iconic LA venues.

* * *

Plan your stay

You’ll be just steps from the Historical Santa Monica Pier and recognizable city landmarks. Relax on the sandy beach with stunning views while immersing yourself in the vibrant local culture and entertainment during your vacation or staycation.

-

### Surf Lessons

Just steps from our beach access, our surf lessons offer a fun and immersive way to experience Santa Monica. With expert instruction, most participants are catching waves by the end of the lesson—ideal for both beginners and seasoned surfers!

_To book your surf lesson, kindly connect with your Regent Experience Manager upon arrival._

-

### Westside Arts Scene

For art lovers, a visit to Bergamot Station is a must. This former train station is now home to a collection of galleries showcasing contemporary art. The Getty Center is another cultural highlight, offering not only remarkable art collections but also stunning architecture and panoramic views of Los Angeles.

-

### Third Street Promenade

Santa Monica’s characteristic relaxed luxury and joyous outdoor lifestyle come together on the Third Street Promenade. This pedestrian-only destination features upscale stores, a vibrant dining scene, and a picture- perfect weekly Farmers Market.

-

### Savor every moment

Enjoy the bounty of regional farmers, fishers, and artisanal producers transformed into unforgettable menus by the most talented chefs, served in a setting with sweeping views of the Pacific coastline and the Santa Monica Mountains.

Extending over 1,500 feet out into the Pacific Ocean, the Santa Monica Pier offers spectacular coastline views in an atmosphere of simple pleasures. Take a ride on the Ferris wheel for an even higher perspective and enjoy fresh seafood at one of the casual restaurants on the pier.

### Shopping in Santa Monica

For a day of retail therapy, head to Montana Avenue for a boutique shopping experience, or explore the eclectic Abbot Kinney Boulevard. Just a few blocks inland, you’ll find the pedestrian-friendly Third Street Promenade and Santa Monica Place, home to an exciting mix of high-end shops, local shops, nationwide brand stores, and a mix of casual dining options.

-

### Water Sports

Explore Marina del Rey’s variety of water activities, including yacht rentals, stand-up paddle boarding at Mother’s Beach, or a relaxing electric duffy boat ride through the marina’s tranquil waters.

_To learn more about all available water activities, kindly connect with your Regent Experience Manager upon arrival._
