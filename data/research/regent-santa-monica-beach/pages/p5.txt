## Frequently Asked Questions

How can I make a reservation or speak with someone at the spa?

To make a reservation or to reach the spa directly, call 310.899.4040, or email **guerlain.wellness@regenthotels.com**.

What are Guerlain Wellness Spa’s hours of operation?

Guerlain Wellness Spa at Regent Santa Monica Beach is open daily from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.

Where is Guerlain Wellness Spa located in Santa Monica?

Guerlain Wellness Spa is located on the fourth-floor lobby level of Regent Santa Monica Beach at 1700 Ocean Avenue, Santa Monica, CA 90401.

What are the details of parking for the spa?

Valet parking is available for Guerlain Wellness Spa guests, and is included with spa memberships. It’s $25 for the first 3 hours, $12 each additional hour, daily max $110.

What is Guerlain Wellness Spa’s arrival and cancellation policy?

We recommend arriving at least 30 minutes prior to your appointment to fully enjoy the spa facilities. Changes or cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance to avoid the full service fee. A 22% gratuity is added to each service; additional gratuities are at your discretion.

Does the Guerlain Wellness Spa offer gift certificates?

Yes! Give the treasured gift of Guerlain this season. Spa gift certificates can be used toward treatments, wellness experiences, spa memberships, or products from the Guerlain boutique, making them a perfect choice for birthdays, holidays, or special celebrations. Certificates are available in customizable amounts and can be purchased here or directly through the spa.

How large is the spa at Regent Santa Monica Beach?

Guerlain Wellness Spa spans 10,000 square feet, offering a spacious and tranquil wellness environment.

Is there an age requirement to visit the spa?

Guerlain Wellness Spa is an adult-oriented atmosphere reserved for use of those 18+ years of age. Minors are not permitted in the locker room or spa facilities.

What facilities are available?

Guerlain Wellness Spa features:

- 12 treatment rooms, including a VIP double suite
- Plunge pool, sauna and steam room
- Serene relaxation lounges and an all-gender relaxation lounge and champagne bar
- State-of-the-art fitness center
- Guerlain spa boutique

Do Spa guests have access to Azure pool?

Spa guests with a same-day treatment may enhance their experience by adding access to the Azure Pool through ResortPass. Conveniently located just across the hall from the Guerlain Wellness Spa, the Azure Pool offers the perfect place to relax before or after your treatment.

Is there a spa membership program?

Yes. Guerlain Wellness Spa Membership includes:

- One 60-minute massage or facial per month (services do not roll over)
- Two pool passes each month
- Valet parking
- 20% off treatments and retail products
- Daily facilities access from 5 a.m. to 10 p.m.

What distinguishes Guerlain Wellness Spa from other spas?

Guerlain Wellness Spa combines luxurious Parisian skincare expertise with a serene beachside setting, offering exclusive treatments, tailored wellness experiences, a focus on holistic relaxation and rejuvenation, and a full selection of Guerlain beauty and wellness products.

What is Guerlain known for?

Guerlain is world-renowned for luxury skincare, perfumes, and cosmetics—blending scientific innovation with heritage craftsmanship. At Guerlain Spas, wellness is treated as an art: an ultra-personalized journey for body, mind, and soul, enriched by over 80 years of pioneering expertise and signature techniques. Drawing on nature’s most precious ingredients, every treatment awakens the senses and delivers an unforgettable experience of beauty and well-being.

What exclusive treatments are unique to Regent Santa Monica Beach?

Regent Santa Monica Beach’s current signature spa treatments include:

- Crystal Vibes: A gemstone bed ritual with a crystal sound bath
- Solar Shape: A pre- and post-sun body smoothing and sculpting ritual
- Skin Perfection: HydraFacial-infused facial for deep cleansing and hydration
- Skin Surfing: Radio frequency facial that leaves the complexion luminous

Does the spa offer treatments for couples?

Yes. The Romantic Getaway at Guerlain Wellness Spa allows two guests to unwind together in a beautifully appointed treatment space. Options are 60 or 90 minutes, each designed for connection and relaxation.

Can I plan a spa day with my girlfriends?

Absolutely. The Among Friends experience at Guerlain Wellness Spa is perfect for sharing restorative time together, whether it’s a birthday, reunion, or celebration. Available in 60 or 90-minute sessions, with a tailored atmosphere for your group.

What is the Spa Soirée experience and how can I reserve it?

The Spa Soirée is a bespoke gathering designed for celebrations, bridal parties, birthdays, or cherished time with friends. Guests enjoy a private sanctuary where every detail is tailored, including curated treatments, light refreshments, and serene spaces to create an unforgettable experience.

To learn more or book your Spa Soirée, please email guerlainwellness@regenthotels.com

What makeup services are available?

The Spa at Regent Santa Monica Beach offers professional makeup application, including day, evening, and special occasion looks, using Guerlain’s exclusive line of cosmetics.

Are there any unique in-room wellness experiences available?

Yes. The Bath Butler Experience at Regent Santa Monica Beach provides a handwoven seagrass basket with sea salts and Guerlain fragrances for a personalized at-home or in-room bath ritual.

Who can use the spa fitness area?

The state-of-the-art fitness center is available to spa guests and hotel guests. To ensure a comfortable and enjoyable experience for everyone, we manage capacity thoughtfully so guests always have space to move freely and enjoy their workout. Personal training sessions may also be arranged by appointment.

What will I find at the Guerlain boutique?

The spa boutique offers exclusive Guerlain perfumes, skincare, and cosmetics, as well as personal consultations with our knowledgeable team, allowing guests to continue their wellness and beauty experience at home.

Is there a separate spa food & beverage menu?

Yes, Guerlain Wellness Spa has light refreshments available, including beverages and wellness snacks, depending on your treatment or experience.

What is Les Clos?

Les Clos is Guerlain Wellness Spa’ s exclusive, private champagne lounge, perfect for small events. Toast with chilled flutes in this exclusive tucked away sanctuary, which can be reserved solely for your group.
