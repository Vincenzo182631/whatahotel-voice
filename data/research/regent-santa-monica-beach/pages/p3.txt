culinary excellence, and personalized service.

From elegant modern indoor dining to sweeping terraces wrapped in Pacific Ocean views and a cozy bistro serving casual fare, an array of culinary experiences entice you to follow your muse and take delight in the moment.

Orla by michael mina

Award-winning celebrity chef and global restaurateur Michael Mina showcases cuisine inspired by his Greek and Egyptian upbringing at Orla by Michael Mina. Specialties including fresh-from-the-sea catches are complemented by artfully crafted cocktails. Tuck into buttery-soft leather banquettes and select from an decadent, sweet finish from dessert carts by Goodshop.

Breakfast (Monday through Friday): 6:30 AM – 11:00 AM

Azure Bar

Nestled along the pristine shores of Santa Monica Beach, where the sun kisses the Pacific Ocean each evening, lies Azure—a haven of relaxation and luxury. Bask in the sun and enjoy poolside cuisine and creative cocktails, all with breathtaking views of the Pacific Ocean as your backdrop. An imaginative blend of flavors and an elegant ambiance elevates every moment by the water at Azure.

**Hours:**

Service (Daily) \| 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM

Pool (Daily) \| 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM

## In-Room Dining

When the allure of your guestroom is too enticing to leave, sit back, relax, and allow us to enhance your stay with our gourmet in-room dining. Available 24 hours a day, Regent Santa Monica Beach offers a chef-crafted menu that blends familiar comfort foods with exquisite culinary delights for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and late-night dining. From a morning espresso enjoyed in bed to a nightcap and dessert as you take in the sunset from your balcony, our in-room dining promises unparalleled excellence at any hour.

**Hours:**

Offered 24 hours Daily

## Private Dining

With the Odyssey as its muse and a custom mosaic floor depicting fish and octopus, Orla’s Private Dining Rooms immerses diners in a space where ancient mythology meets contemporary Aegean cuisine. Driftwood sculptures by a local artist, emerald accents, and Roman-shaded windows overlooking the pool deck create a dramatic ambience.

For private dining inquiries, please contact our Events Team: _events.smb@regenthotels.com_
