## Dining

Rooted in locally sourced ingredients, dining at The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui reflects the island’s rich cultural influences through fresh, modern cuisine. From relaxed oceanfront settings to refined evening experiences, each menu celebrates seasonal flavors, local inspiration, and exceptional oceanfront dining in Maui.

As the sun sets over Namalu Bay, Sunset Hale at Cliff House invites you to embrace the tradition of pau hana. Gather with friends and unwind in Maui’s most iconic oceanfront setting, high above the Pacific, as another perfect day in paradise comes to a close.

### Elevated Private Dining

Whether celebrating a special occasion or simply gathering with those closest to you, private dining at The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui offers an elevated, intimate experience. From secluded oceanfront settings to sunset evenings on your lanai, each moment is thoughtfully curated to create a personalized and memorable dining experience.

## Cliff House Culinary Journey

Originally built in 1940, Cliff House is a historic waterfront venue perched above Namalu Bay with views of neighboring islands. Available for private dining and celebrations for 2–12 guests, this intimate setting hosts a four-course culinary journey paired with unforgettable Maui sunsets.

Book Now

## Signature Gatherings

Celebrate life’s special moments with a curated three-course dining experience at The Restaurant at Kapalua Bay. Available nightly for groups of 8–18 guests, Signature Gatherings offers a warm, elevated setting for birthdays, anniversaries, and meaningful celebrations shared around the table.

## Private Chef & Luau

Designed for up to 10 guests, our inresidence experiences bring the flavors and spirit of Hawaii to you. Enjoy a customized multi-course dinner prepared by a private chef, or host an intimate luau with live entertainment, traditional music, and a festive feast on your lanai — each tailored to your occasion.
