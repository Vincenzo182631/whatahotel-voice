The Resort at Kapalua Bay
An ultra-luxurious West Maui retreat with residential-style suites, elevated dining, and oceanfront experiences designed for both romantic getaways and group stays.

In a nutshell
: Consistently ranked among the best resorts in the world, this exclusive West Maui retreat is designed for those looking to splurge. It truly feels like a private residence rather than a hotel—something I haven’t experienced to the same degree anywhere else on the island. The ultra-luxurious, beachfront residential-style suites can accommodate groups of all sizes while offering effortless access to Kapalua Bay. Expect Hawaiian-inspired spa treatments, private chef experiences, multi-tier lagoon-style pools, and highly personalized concierge service.
Rooms
Accommodations feel more like private residences than hotel rooms, with elegant one- to four-bedroom suites starting at over 1,200 square feet. Each is outfitted with a full kitchen, spacious living and dining rooms, soaking tubs, in-unit laundry, and private lanais with additional seating and sweeping ocean views. Spa-style bathrooms with deep soaking tubs further elevate the experience, making these some of the most spacious luxury accommodations on Maui—ideal for extended stays or group travel.
Food and drink
The Restaurant at Kapalua Bay (formerly Cane & Canoe) serves seafood-forward cuisine for breakfast and dinner. Don’t miss the rich, buttery lobster agnolotti, a signature from executive chef Justin Purpura. Next door, The Hideaway is a cocktail bar offering American fare. After a 2026 refresh, it now features a revamped interior alongside a new menu, which includes a handcrafted mai tai selection that celebrates the iconic tiki drink. The Champagne Hale, a pop-up lounge, is a classy spot for light bites and—naturally—Champagne, complete with stunning ocean views and impeccable service.
Amenities and experiences
The resort’s lagoon-style pools span multiple tiers, featuring waterfalls, built-in bubbler loungers, and bridges connecting each level. A secluded adults-only infinity pool overlooks Kapalua Bay for a quieter escape. Cabanas provide shade, privacy curtains, and full poolside food and beverage service. For a deeper sense of relaxation, visit the spa for a traditional lomi-lomi massage in a private outdoor hale (home or hut), followed by a hydrotherapy experience.
What to do nearby
Kapalua Bay is just steps away, making it easy to spend a day in the sun and gentle surf. The resort offers beach rentals for kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding, snorkeling, and more. Across the bay, Merriman’s Kapalua is a go-to for an exceptional off-property meal. For a scenic outing, explore the nearby Kapalua Coastal Trail, an easy two-mile walk with dramatic cliffs, tide pools, and panoramic ocean views.
The backstory
This property has undergone several ownership changes, most recently transitioning from the Montage portfolio to a Marriott Bonvoy property in March 2026. Already in the midst of renovations, the resort is reportedly set to fully unveil its new identity as a St. Regis in 2027.
Insider tip
Keep an eye out for the iconic Pegge Hopper paintings that adorn the walls throughout the property. Her signature style pays tribute to the beauty and grace of Polynesian women, adding a meaningful artistic touch to the resort’s interiors.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Yes, and there is a kids club, though the overall atmosphere leans more adult-oriented
Accessibility:
Yes, the property features accessible rooms for people with mobility restrictions or hearing impairments, lifts/ramps to enter the pool, and elevators
Wellness:
There’s a strong wellness focus, with standout treatments at The Spa at Kapalua Bay
Loyalty group/hotel brand:
Marriott Bonvoy
