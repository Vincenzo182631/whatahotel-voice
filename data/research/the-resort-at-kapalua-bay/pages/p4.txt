# Embrace the Aloha of Maui

## Experiences

Kapalua invites you to slow down, explore, and make your stay your own. Spend the morning along the bay, find a new favorite local flavor, connect with Hawaiian culture, or make time for wellness, recreation, and adventure. From moments of discovery to days spent doing nothing at all, there are countless ways to experience the island. Follow the rhythm of the day and see where Maui takes you.

The Spirit of Kapalua

## A Distinct Sense of Place

On Maui’s northwest coast, Kapalua offers a relaxed pace without slowing down the adventure. In Hawaiian, Kapalua means “arms embracing the sea,” reflecting the connection between the land, ocean, and Hawaiian culture that shapes this place. Our Cultural Ambassadors honor that culture by sharing the history and traditions of Hawaii through workshops and meaningful moments during your stay.

### Kapalua Golf

Golf along Maui’s northwest coast on the award-winning Plantation Course, designed by Ben Crenshaw and Bill Coore. Take on dramatic elevation changes, ocean views, and challenging fairways, with exclusive resort rates and tee time access for our guests.

### Tennis & Pickleball at Kapalua

Located nearby at Kapalua, tennis and pickleball courts invite guests to play at their own pace. Whether enjoying a friendly match or guided instruction, the setting offers open air, natural surroundings, and an easy balance of activity and leisure.

### Children's Club

The Children’s Club offers a engaging space for creativity, play, and discovery. Curated activities encourage exploration and connection, giving younger guests a place to learn and unwind—while parents enjoy time to themselves.

### Kapalua Bay Beach

Just beyond the resort, Kapalua Bay offers calm, clear waters ideal for snorkeling and paddleboarding. Resort guests and residence owners enjoy complimentary loungers, umbrellas, and towel service, with water‑sport rentals available nearby through Aqualani.

## Shopping at The Resort

Mahana Market brings together resort wear, hats, shoes, poolside essentials, and gifts to bring a little aloha home. At the Spa Boutique, find spa-inspired beauty, active wear, accessories, and thoughtfully selected pieces to remember your time in Kapalua.

Inspiration for Your Stay

### Day 1

Begin your stay with a morning on the nearby Kapalua Coastal Trail, taking in ocean cliffs, hidden coves, and the coastline along the way. Spend the afternoon paddleboarding in Kapalua Bay near tropical fish and sea turtles, then sip a Mai Tai at The Pool Bar and watch the sunset over the water before settling into a relaxed evening at the resort with time to unwind.

### Day 2

A morning yoga class at The Spa offers a gentle start to the day, followed by a round on the award-winning Plantation Course. Take in ocean views of Maui and challenging fairways as you tee off in paradise. Back at our resort, enjoy dinner at The Restaurant, featuring fresh, island-inspired dishes and craft cocktails, with the horizon just beyond the table.

### Day 3

Make the early start worth it with sunrise at Haleakala National Park, where the first light reveals views across the island. Return to Kapalua and slow things down at the Sunset Pool with a good book, cocktails, and light bites. As evening arrives, head to Sunset Hale at Cliff House for a final toast and one last look out over the Pacific before heading home.

Does The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-6:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
