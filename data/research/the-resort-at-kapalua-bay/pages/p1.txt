# Welcome to The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui

## Luxury Beach Hotel with Wellness & Ocean Experiences

Set along the serene shores of Kapalua Bay, this distinguished Maui retreat offers an elegant balance of seclusion, space, and natural beauty. Residential‑style accommodations feature expansive living areas, private lanais, and refined design that blends island character with timeless luxury. Thoughtfully curated dining, bespoke wellness experiences, and ocean‑focused pursuits are delivered with intuitive, discreet service, creating an atmosphere where every stay feels effortless, unhurried, and distinctly refined.

One Day Only - Epicutis in Kapalua

On September 23, discover Epicutis, a science-driven approach to skin health and longevity, through two exclusive offerings. Resort guests and residence owners are invited to a complimentary masterclass at Cliff House, while all guests can reserve a 45-minute VIP facial experience. Space is limited.

## The Spa at Kapalua Bay

Immerse yourself in the restorative power of Namalu Bay at The Spa at Kapalua Bay. Inspired by the traditional ho’oponopono (balance) of the islands, our treatments utilize the island’s natural remedies—from volcanic clays to nutrient-rich oils—to create a signature experience tailored to your unique needs. We invite you to surrender to the rhythms of the ocean and emerge revitalized by the timeless wisdom of Hawaiian culture.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui?

The check-in time at The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui.

What are the parking options at The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui?

On-site parking is available at The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui. Self parking is available for $65.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui booking.

What is the closest airport near The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui?

The closest airport near The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui is Kahului/Maui (OGG). OGG is located approximately 33.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
