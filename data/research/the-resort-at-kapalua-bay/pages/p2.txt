# The Luxury of Feeling at Home

## Residences

Nestled along the shores of West Maui’s Kapalua Bay, our residential-style accommodations are more than a place to stay. Each residence features full kitchens, multiple bedrooms, spacious living areas, and private lanais with views of nearby beaches and bays. Perfect for extended stays, family travel, and group getaways with the comforts of home and the amenities of a luxury Maui resort.

Signature Amenities

### Framed by Kapalua

Garden, ocean, and oceanfront views bring a unique perspective of Kapalua into every residence. Private lanais and floor-to-ceiling windows frame tropical landscapes and the Pacific beyond, while natural light fills the interiors throughout the day. From the moment you arrive, the scenery leaves a lasting connection to Maui long after you return home, drawing you back to paradise again and again.

### Designed for Living

The Resort at Kapalua Bay, Maui’s residences offer expansive living and dining areas, full kitchens, ensuite bedrooms, marble bathrooms, hardwood floors, and in-residence laundry, reflecting the ease of a private island home. Wraparound lanais extend the living space outdoors, with select residences offering grills and private pools for open-air entertaining and gathering with friends and family.

### Resort Experience

Your stay unfolds with an effortless resort experience featuring six curated dining outlets, three pools— including the Sunset Pool with daybeds and a waterslide—an award-winning spa, and Keiki Club for younger guests. Steps away, Kapalua Bay invites snorkeling, paddleboarding, and scuba diving, alongside championship golf, tennis, pickleball, ziplining, scenic trails, and seasonal whale watching.

A Collection like No Other

### Signature Residences

We invite you to experience the luxury of our Signature Residences, five individually designed three- and four-bedroom retreats featuring expansive lanais, refined interiors, and oceanfront or lush garden views. Whether enjoying a private infinity pool on your lanai or being steps away from Kapalua Bay, your choice of Signature Residence shapes a personalized Maui stay with distinct amenities.

## Grand Residences

Elevated living meets island elegance in our Grand Residences, offering refined comfort in Kapalua Bay. These privately owned three- and four-bedroom accommodations feature wraparound lanais with garden, ocean, or oceanfront views, along with full gourmet kitchens, marble bathrooms, and ensuite bedrooms. Thoughtful details create a stay of ease, ideal for families and group getaways.

Each features a private lanai with panoramic ocean or garden views, full gourmet kitchens, and expansive living and dining areas. Our newly reimagined residences feel light and contemporary, while traditional options reflect a more timeless island aesthetic.
