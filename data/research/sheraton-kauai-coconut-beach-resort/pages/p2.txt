# Relax and unwind at our spacious suites in Kauai

## Accommodations

Newly reimagined beach front rooms in Kapaa, Kauai, have fantastic views of the Garden Island. With private lanais, you can take the time to relax and breathe in the cool ocean air. The Sheraton Kauai...See More Coconut Beach Resort rooms and suites are your sanctuary while on vacation. They are designed to offer you not only a comfortable night's sleep, but also a place where you can sit back, kick up your feet and enjoy a good book or an afternoon nap. There are over 300 rooms at the resort, most of which have a view of the ocean. Some rooms have sweeping panoramic views of the blue Pacific Ocean. From the moment you enter your Kapaa Kauai hotel room to the moment you depart the island, you will feel the warmth of Hawaiian hospitalitySee Less

## Oceanfront Suites

Sit back and take in the fresh ocean breeze from the balcony of our Ocean Front Suite. This suite accommodation in Kapaa Kauai offers a spacious living and dining area with a wet bar, one king bed in the master bedroom boasting ocean views, a glass-enclosed shower and a soaking tub perfect to unwind after a day at the beach.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort?

Some rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
