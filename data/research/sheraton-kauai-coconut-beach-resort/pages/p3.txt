## Dining

While staying at the Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort you will have access to the dining options at our Kapaa Kauai beach resort restaurants. Enjoy your morning at the First Light Coffee and Juice Bar, our on-site coffee house, serving a variety of custom coffee drinks, freshly pressed juices, nitro cold brew coffee as well as fresh pastries and sandwiches. Stop by the Daybreak Restaurant which has a full breakfast buffet in an open-air environment. The Crooked Surf features a menu of lighter fare and signature cocktails perfect for lunch or dinner. Moamoa Hawaiian Fish House offers a delicious seafood menu featuring upscale locally sourced Pacific Island cuisine with an open-air setting. And don't miss the celebrated Luau Ka Hikina where you can experience Hawaiian and Polynesian culture, an open bar, and a luau buffet on select nights each week

Easter Brunch at Daybreak Restaurant

Experience our Springtime Feast on Easter Sunday from 10:30am-2:30pm. Brunch Buffet featuring a seafood station!
