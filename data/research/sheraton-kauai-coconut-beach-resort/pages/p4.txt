# Explore Hawaii from our Kauai beachfront resort

## Experiences

Your vacation can be filled with adventures like hiking to waterfalls, kayaking, swimming in the bright blue Hawaiian waters, or you can sit back and relax, enjoying the warm tropical sun and cool ocean...See More breezes. Staying at our hotel near Waimea, Kauai​ puts you near all these wonderful activities. Rent bikes and explore the island. Take a kayak tour of the Wailua River. Relax with Yoga on the beach. Play 18 holes on Kauai's Wailua challenging golf course. Choose from a wide variety of activities to satisfy everyone from the bookworm to the thrillseekerSee Less

### Lei Making

Learn the art of Lei making and give the gift of Lei to a loved one or create a piece for yourself. During this lesson, you will learn about our talented Lei maker and the history and significance of the Lei.

Learn More

### Sunrise Yoga

Take in the best views for early morning sunrise on Kauai. Join us beachside for Sunrise Yoga and get ready for what the rest of your day has to offer.

Learn More

### Ukulele Lessons

Learn the basics of Ukulele playing while staying at our hotel near LIH airport. Enjoy a lesson with our talented Kumu while visiting Kauai. Learn basic chords and a simple tune to take home.

Learn More

### Hula Lessons

Learn about the history of Hula and the basic steps used to create a story through dance. Kumu Hula Maka and his 'Ohana love teaching the art of hula to young and old.

Learn More

### Oceanfront Infinity Pool

Unwind at our zero‑edge infinity pool, set directly along the beach with sweeping ocean views. Lounge with your favorite read, soak in the adjacent whirlpool, and let the rhythm of the waves create the ultimate Kauai escape—just steps from the sand.

### Hands-Free Convenience

Enjoy every moment at our oceanfront Kauai beach resort without worrying about your key card getting wet or lost. Our wristbands are waterproof, so whether you're lounging by the pool or exploring the beach, your key card stays with you.

### Coastal Cruising

Explore Kauai at your own pace with complimentary bike rentals included with your stay. Cruise along scenic paths, discover nearby beaches, and enjoy the freedom to explore just beyond the resort—on us.

### Play Poolside

Just steps from the pool deck, our Game Room offers a relaxed space to unwind between swims. Perfect for families and friendly competition, it’s an easy, fun escape without ever leaving the heart of the resort.

## Steps From the Beach

Discover effortless island living just steps from the resort. From iconic surf breaks to serene moments snorkeling, kayaking, or strolling the shoreline, Kauai’s most celebrated beach experiences unfold right outside your door.

## Kauai Mountain Range

If you grow tired of the sand and water, then you can turn inland. Our beachfront hotel near Princeville, Kauai​ is also close to the Sleeping Giant trailhead, Opaekaa Falls and the natural beauty of Wailua River State Park which includes the spectacular Wailua Falls.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Free Weights, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Zero Edge Infinity Pool (Outdoor)Whirlpool

What are some local attractions and things to do near Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort?

Smith Family Garden Luau, Hikinaakala Heiau, Kauai Backcountry Adventures, Kauai Photo Tours, Opaeka'a Falls- scenic overlook and more are nearby Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort.
