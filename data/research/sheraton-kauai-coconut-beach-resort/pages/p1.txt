# Welcome to Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort

## Discover a Hawaiian paradise at our Kauai resort

With unparalleled views of the Kauai mountains and ocean, as well as a breathtaking location on Garden Island, Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort boasts a transformative experience for your next getaway....See More We provide easy access to Kapaa's biggest hot spots, such as Smith's Fern Grotto Tour and Wailua Falls. Relax in this Kapaa, Hawaii resort with a beautiful, beachfront location and guest rooms featuring complimentary Wi-Fi, mini-refrigerators and private lanais, allowing you to soak up the Hawaiian sun. Guests at our beach resort in Kapaa, Kauai, experience new heights of relaxation in our infinity-edge pool, with separate areas for children and adults, surrounded by our oceanfront bar, lounge chairs and fire pits. Savor culinary delights and signature drinks at The Crooked Surf Bar and Restaurant, and Moa Moa Hawaiian Fish House, located in the warm aloha spirit of our gorgeous oceanfront hotel in Kapaa, Kauai, Hawaii. Treat yourself to a one-of-a-kind Hawaiian vacation at our Kauai Coconut Beach Resort.See Less

### Lei Greeting

Be welcomed with a traditional lei greeting, an elegant expression of aloha and a timeless symbol of Hawaiian hospitality. This meaningful gesture honors local culture while setting a warm, gracious tone for your Kauai stay.

### A Central Kauai Beachfront Stay

Our resort is set right on the beach and centrally located near Kapaʻa and Līhuʻe Airport, making it easy to settle in and explore the island at your own pace. On property, you’ll find experiences like Luau Ka Hikina, daily resort activities, and open spaces to relax and unwind. Spend your days enjoying the resort, take a drive north toward Princeville, or head south to discover the beauty of Poʻipū.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort?

The check-in time at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort?

On-site parking is available at Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort. Self parking is available for $20.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $30.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort?

The closest airport near Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort is Lihue Airport (LIH). LIH is located approximately 6.2 Miles from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Sheraton Kauai Coconut Beach Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
