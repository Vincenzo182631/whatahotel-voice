# Singita Journey Designers

## No longer considered just a luxury, travel has become a necessity. It holds the potential to be completely restorative – balancing body and mind, and recalibrating one’s sense of perspective.

### At Singita Journeys, we understand that every opportunity to experience a world beyond your own is precious. Whether it’s a remote sanctuary in which to reconnect with family or friends, an intimate hideaway for two, or a retreat to embrace the healing power of solitude, we appreciate the incalculable value of combining the perfect location with bespoke levels of comfort and personalised experiences, and allowing the trip of a lifetime to unfold.

## Expert advice & guidance

With their extensive experience of each reserve, proficiency in the intricacies of every property, and ability to align unique experiences to your personal tastes, our Journey Designers’ wealth of knowledge about travel in Africa enable them to streamline every aspect of your itinerary.

Whether it’s experiencing the Serengeti’s Great Migration from a hot-air balloon or exploring South Africa’s magnificent bushveld on foot; life-changing encounters with mountain gorillas in Rwanda’s misty mountains and forests or extraordinary sightings of black and white rhino in Zimbabwe’s remote southeast – each tailormade journey is based on their ability to bring your dreams to life.

## Personalised itineraries

Crafting customised itineraries that reflect individual wishes and interests, Singita Journey Designers follow a guest-centric approach and their planning extends well beyond working around availability. Having experienced every region, lodge, camp and activity for themselves, they harness their intimate knowledge of all of Singita’s offerings to create unforgettable African experiences that surpass expectations at every turn.

From the moment planning starts, all aspects of your journey

are moulded to match your tastes and interests, dreams & moods –

enjoying the privacy of a romantic rim-flow pool overlooking the vast Serengeti plains; falling asleep to the sounds of the bush on the most authentic safaris under canvas; experiencing the thrill of a front-row seat to Big Cat country in Sabi Sand; and savouring the exclusivity of fully-staffed villas surrounded by nothing but nature.

## Tailored services

Attention to detail, personalised care and an emphasis on a high-touch travel experience throughout are the hallmarks of the service. Dedicated to building strong personal relationships with our guests, we ensure that their wishes are not just understood and met, but also exceeded.

Detailed preferences such as private wine tastings, special celebrations and dinners, and connecting with favourite Field Guides, are seamlessly incorporated, managed and adapted to make the most of each unique itinerary. No stone is left unturned to make a dream holiday come to life and no requirement, query or need is lost during the planning process – ensuring a tailormade service every step of the way.
