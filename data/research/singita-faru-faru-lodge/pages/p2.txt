# Elela Four-Bedroom Camp

Yield to the freedom and flow of the Okavango

## An homage to the many cycles of nature, inspired by the Delta’s seasonality

Deep in the Okavango Delta, where space seems endless and time slower, the land is alive in natural motion and continual flow. This is where you’ll find Elela Four-Bedroom Camp, an embodiment of the region’s contrast. Ever-changing yet impermanent, wild and serene, private yet deeply connected.

Elela Four-Bedroom Camp comprises four private camps, arranged in a loose circle and connected by walkways, canopies, and communal spaces. The surrounding landscape is a constant presence here, both seen and felt always, in setting and design, facilitating a deeper connection to place.
