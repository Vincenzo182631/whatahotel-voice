## David Ráday \| Music Curator

Budapest-born David Ráday (aka Raasa) is the sound behind W Budapest’s cultural pulse. A DJ, art director, and former creative head of Sziget Festival, he has been shaping the city’s music and creative scene for over a decade.

Known for his eclectic sets blending house, minimal, disco, and indie dance, David brings an unmistakable energy to every performance. Beyond music, he is the co-founder of Hello Wood, an award-winning design studio celebrated for its festival stages and installations.

At W Budapest, David curates signature programs like THE WARM-UP and AFTER DARK, connecting locals and travelers through rhythm, design, and a shared love for sound.

### SOUND PROGRAMMING

Music is at the heart of W Budapest. Inspired by the city's vibrant creative scene, our signature programming moves effortlessly from sunset to after dark. Discover curated DJ sessions, cultural collaborations and global live performances that transform every visit into an unforgettable soundtrack.

### SUNSET SESSIONS

Golden hour starts here. Ease into the evening with laid-back DJ sets, signature cocktails and front-row views of the Hungarian State Opera House. As the city slows down, W Lounge and W Terrace become the perfect place to unwind before the night unfolds.

### THE WARM-UP

Where the night begins. As the energy builds, W Lounge transforms into the city's pre-night destination. Expect carefully curated DJ sets, elevated cocktails and a vibrant social atmosphere that brings together locals and travelers before the city's nightlife comes alive.

### AFTER DARK \| NIGHT SERIES

Where music becomes momentum. When the city sleeps, W comes alive. From W PRESENTS and international headliners to late-night DJ takeovers and immersive cultural programming, AFTER DARK celebrates Budapest's creative pulse through unforgettable nights of music, connection and discovery.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Budapest have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Budapest offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Stretching facilities, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Budapest have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Budapest has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does W Budapest have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY® Spa is located on-site at W Budapest. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Budapest?

Heroes' Square, Hungarian Parliament Building, Buda Castle, Széchenyi Bridge, Hungarian State Opera House and more are nearby W Budapest.
