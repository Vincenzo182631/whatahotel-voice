Riccardo Giraudi

## Food’s Art Director

At the beginning of his career, Giraudi became known as the "Beefboy" by working with exclusive meats and bringing previously unattainable high quality to his customers. His vision under the motto "food is the new fashion" made him an undeniable force in the industry, with 34+ restaurants worldwide. Le Petit Beefbar is a spinoff of his famous Beefbar concept, designed as an approachable introduction to Asian cuisine. Gastronomy à la Giraudi.

The menu is a celebration of indulgence, where Beefbar signatures meet everyday favorites with a twist. ### Live, Love, Lounge

With a cold cocktail in one hand and a soul-lifting bite in the other, join the W Lounge for some socialising. Local DJs provide the soundtrack of the moment to the backdrop of twisted turquoise ceramics and coral hues.

### Bar of 1001 Nights

Our bar is a destination in itself. Whether you're easing into dinner or winding down your day, sip on inventive cocktails inspired by Budapest’s creative spirit. Try the signature Beefbar Negroni or our twist on the Old Fashioned – each crafted to match the restaurant’s bold and playful energy.

### As We Speakeasy

Underground is where we level up. Enter Society25, our secluded speakeasy draped in a decor of secret cafés, once home to the city's intelligentsia. With eight signature cocktails, each representing an era of history, you'll taste delight in the present. Gatsby's opening gambit.
