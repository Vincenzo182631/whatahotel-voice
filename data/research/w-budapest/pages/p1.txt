Beyond the urban fairytale, an unmatched stay awaits at W Budapest, a luxury hotel set in the historic Drechsler Palace on UNESCO-listed Andrássy Avenue, opposite the Hungarian Opera House. With 151 stylish guest rooms, including 45 suites, this Budapest luxury hotel blends history with modern luxury, creating a vibe between ballet and beats, elegance and edge, established and avant-garde. ### The Spirit of an Era

W Budapest is a modern-day homage to the city's DNA and the building it's housed in – Drechsler Palace. Located on the most prominent avenue in town, Andrássy Avenue (a UNESCO heritage site), the hotel rubs shoulders with luxury boutiques and offers the best view of the iconic State Opera House. Inspired by café culture and social playgrounds, the design captures the spirit of the Belle Époque and the iconic sons & daughters of Budapest. From a flamboyant interior à la Zsa Zsa Gabor, to Houdini-like illusions in architecture. Discover new details hidden in plain sight with every gaze.

## Design With a Bold Duality

W Budapest reimagines Drechsler Palace with a design story that fuses past and present. Inspired by ballet, chess, and Hungary’s creative spirit, every space surprises with bold contrasts, layered textures, and playful illusions. The White Swan and Black Swan Penthouse Suite pay tribute to the building’s ballet heritage, with all-white and all-black designs inspired by Swan Lake. From grand spaces to hidden gems like AWAY Spa Budapest, design here is never static - it’s always evolving.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Budapest?

The check-in time at W Budapest is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Budapest allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Budapest. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of €100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Budapest?

On-site valet parking is available at W Budapest. The daily valet fee is €65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Budapest have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Budapest has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Budapest booking.

What is the closest airport near W Budapest?

The closest airport near W Budapest is Budapest Airport (BUD). BUD is located approximately 21.3 KM from the hotel.

Does W Budapest have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Budapest has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
