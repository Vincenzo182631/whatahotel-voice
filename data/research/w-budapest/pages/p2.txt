# Rooms & Suites

## Accommodations

Our luxury accommodation in Budapest boasts authentic tiles, elegant mesh details, and patterns that pay tribute to Budapest's energy. United through contrasts, the design narrative tells the tale of two cities, Buda and Pest, in a bold-meets-traditional interior worthy of a palace. Luxurious guest rooms and suites have plush bedding, and smart amenities.

HOTEL AWARDS 2026

W Budapest is proud to be awarded the Forbes Travel Guide Four-Star rating for 2026, alongside a MICHELIN Key Hotel distinction in the MICHELIN Guide Hungary 2025 and being named a Top 5 Hotel in Central & Southern Europe by the Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards 2025.

Featuring Stay

### White and Black Swan Penthouse Suites

Curtains up for your version of Swan Lake. Inspired by the world-famous ballet, these suites are emblems of extravaganza in all-white and all-black color themes. The design speaks Zsa Zsa glam and Houdini flair with details such as tiled arches, checkered mirror ceilings and bespoke artsy wallpapers adorning the hallways. Both suites feature an opulent bar and impeccable views of the iconic State Opera House across the street. Pirouettes of pure pleasure, guaranteed.

## Cozy Cabin Flair

The fifth floor has a unique wooden-centric style that highlights the Palace's original and restored roof design. Nestle yourself into the warm and cozy feeling of an overall timber theme with natural colours and light. A serene destination within the hotel that begs you to settle in. Come here to feel remote, yet in the lap of buzz.

## Historical Rooms

Neo-Renaissance vibes for the 21st-century traveller. Due to the monument protection, these rooms enjoy the remnants of iconic architecture. Arches lean on columns with carved-in sculptures and original ornament ceilings give you a farewell into a good night. Only four rooms carry these presents of the past for an unforgettable stay.

## The Crown of Categories

Soft pink hues and curved lines meld with a lush colour palette into a graceful ballet dance of design. A contemporary twist on Zsolnay-like tiles, and jewellery-inspired light fittings dot the "i" in interior. Enter the golden age of La Belle Époque through one of the 45 suites and revel in true fashion with joie de vivre.

### Black Swan Penthouse Suite

This take on the Presidential Suite is extreme, and wow for sure. Stretched out on 198 m², some highlights include a separate living room, a bar and a kitchen. While views outside transport you to the French Renaissance, inside, chequered interior details continue the narrative nod to the Budapest chess culture. In other words, this is the checkmate of luxury stays.

View Virtual Tour

### White Swan Penthouse Suite

Wallowing in all-white, this suite is a showstopper. It checks all the boxes of luxury comfort laid out on 122 m². The interior features a separate living room, two bathrooms and a full stocked bar. Soft pink hues on tiles, curved lines and impressive light installations gracefully reflect the Ballet Étoile aesthetic. An ensemble of grandeur delight.

View Virtual Tour

### W Suite

Torn between a beautiful interplay of black and white, all ten of these rooms represent the famous ballet Swan Lake and face the Hungarian Opera House. Next to arresting views of architecture, amenities include a king-sized bed, a 55-inch TV and a bathroom with an elegant tub as its centrepiece. Check into accessible vanity.

View Virtual Tour

### P.A.W. Pets Are Welcome

To make sure your furry friend enjoys the visit as much as you do, the room will be pet-ready upon your arrival. This includes food and water bowls, a floor mat and an extra cover to keep animal hair and dirt from your bed. Now that’s pawesome.

### In-Room Wellness

W RE(TREAT) brings you in-room yoga videos by our local connector, Annmaria Kaptay. Access the custom-made classes via your phone or TV and turn your room into a sanctuary of meditation. Unwind with soothing breathwork, and mindful or strength-building yoga.

### In-Room Dining

Do you have the cravings but feel like staying in? Enjoy restaurant-quality dishes from the comfort of your room, 24/7. Select from special in-room service menu and gratify your appetite.
