## Accommodations

Sleep soundly and awaken refreshed at The Westin Puntacana Resort & Club. Our oceanfront hotel offers guests a rejuvenating stay in the Dominican Republic with vitalizing wellness amenities and thoughtfully appointed hotel rooms and suites. Wake up to the sounds of paradise on your private balcony overlooking the pool and crystal-clear ocean. Take a deep breath, each of our hotel rooms and suites in Punta Cana are smoke-free.

Renovation alert

Please contemplate that the Hotel will be under renovation from September 1st to October 15th. We invite you to visit our alternative options at Puntacana Resort & Club.

## Tranquil Views for Inner Peace

Our oceanview hotel rooms and suites in Punta Cana offer peaceful, calming views of either the Caribbean Sea, lush greenery, or serene pools; transforming your stay into an immersive experience of natural beauty and tranquility.

Even while you’re away, maintaining your fitness routine is easy. Our WestinWORKOUT® Gear is available upon request, providing you with everything you need for an in-room workout.
