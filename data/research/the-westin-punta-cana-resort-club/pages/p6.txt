### Plan Your Festive Celebrations

How Do I Reserve Holiday Dining?

Reservations are required for Thanksgiving Dinner, the Beach BBQ, Christmas Eve Dinner and the New Year’s Eve Caribbean Gala Dinner. To reserve, please contact our Guest Services team at **gservice@puntacana.com**. Space is limited, so we recommend booking early.

Is There an Early Booking Rate for New Year’s Eve?

Yes. Reserve the New Year’s Eve Caribbean Gala Dinner before December 29 to enjoy the adult rate of US$130++ per person. Reservations made on or after December 29 are US$160++ per person. Children ages 4–12 are US$50++.

Are Beverages Included?

Beverages are not included at the Beach BBQ. The New Year’s Countdown on the Beach includes an open bar, and a sparkling wine special is available during the Caribbean Gala Dinner for US$10. For details on other events, please contact Guest Services.

What Does ++ Mean on Prices?

All prices are in U.S. dollars per person. The ++ indicates that applicable government taxes and service charges will be added to the listed price.
