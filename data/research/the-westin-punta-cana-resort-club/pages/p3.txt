# Savor the vivid flavors of Punta Cana

## Dining

With three of the best Punta Cana restaurants in the Dominican Republic, The Westin Puntacana Resort offers culinary experiences to suit your every mood. Our experienced culinary staff brings the color...See More and robust flavors of Dominican cuisine alive, including our ocean-fresh seafood, locally sourced coffee and vibrant spices. Recharge and refuel with nutritious and energizing dining options at The Westin Puntacana Resort. From a light beachside lunch at Brassa Grill & Bar to a wholesome Caribbean breakfast at Anani Restaurant, discover fresh, locally-sourced ingredients prepared to enhance your well-being.See Less

Oceanfront Dining

### Arena Beach Club

Set against the stunning backdrop of Punta Cana’s turquoise waters and vibrant coral reefs, Arena Beach Club combines the best of international cuisine with breathtaking ocean views. Whether you’re enjoying a lunch or a special sunset dining experience, our menu features a selection of dishes designed to satisfy a variety of tastes.
