# Golf at The Westin Puntacana Resort

## Golf

Perfect for golf enthusiasts and nature lovers alike, our seaside golf course promises an unforgettable experience, combining sport, relaxation, and stunning coastal views. Discover the ultimate coastal retreat at our seaside golf course.

## Corales Golf Club

Tom Fazio’s dramatic 18-hole championship golf course features wide fairways, dramatic bunkers, deep quarries, and tight lies characterize the inland holes. The six holes along the ocean will get your heart racing with forced carries over coral cliffs, crashing waves, and a persistent Caribbean breeze. Corales’ last three holes have been named the Devil’s Elbow; a name that will become obvious when you drop your final putt on the 18th green. Corales is home of the only PGA Tour event in the Dominican Republic, The Corales Puntacana Championship.

## La Cana Golf & Beach Club

P.B. Dye’s design is 27 holes of resort golfing at its finest. Each 9-hole layout is named after its surrounding residential community, once celebrated as "the number one course in the Caribbean" by Golf Magazine. Pot bunkers, moguls, lakes, and railroad ties protect many of the greens of this seaside masterpiece featuring 6 of the 12 ocean holes played right along the Caribbean Sea.

## Corales PuntaCana Championship

The first PGA TOUR Event held in the Dominican Republic. The seventh edition of the Corales Puntacana Championship was hosted from April 15-21, 2024 at Corales Golf Club, our exclusive 18-hole course with miles of natural cliffs and majestic coralina quarries; where more than 120 professional players will have the chance to earn points of the FedExCup.

Dining at the golf courses in Puntacana Resort is more than snack food on the go; it is delightful dishes with incredible views of the courses and the Caribbean Sea.

### The Grill

At La Cana Clubhouse, the Grill is the ideal destination for contemporary and soothing dishes, that brings a balance between local flavors with an international influence, and exciting beachfront lunch or dinner eatery.
