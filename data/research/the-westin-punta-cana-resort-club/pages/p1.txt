# Welcome to The Westin Puntacana Resort

## Experience the true Puntacana

The Westin Puntacana Resort is part of the 15,000-acre Puntacana Resort and Club, which boasts 3 miles of white-sand beaches. Nearby are two championship golf courses, a range of water activities, a shopping...See More village and world-class restaurants. Rejuvenate with a spa visit, energize at our WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio, or refresh by the open-air pool and enjoy wholesome cuisine at the adjacent grill. Gather in style at the unforgettable meeting and wedding facilities of our Punta Cana resort. All 200 guest rooms, including 16 suites, feature contemporary decor and private balconies with soothing views of lush gardens or the Caribbean Sea. Our hotel in Punta Cana near the beach is perfect for a couple's getaway. Renew your senses with the revitalizing Heavenly Shower and experience pure refreshment in the Heavenly Bed at our Puntacana resort in the Dominican Republic.See Less

## Our Resort's Address

Nestled within the tranquility and beauty of the renowned Puntacana Resort and Club, our Puntacana resort in the Dominican Republic offers exclusive, top-tier amenities, including two championship golf courses, a full-service spa, and exquisite dining options.

## How the Past Evolved to the Present

Puntacana Resort was founded in the early 1970s by visionary Frank Rainieri who saw the potential of the region's untouched beauty. He created a sustainable, low-density resort that harmonized with the landscape, protecting local ecosystems like coral reefs, mangroves, and forests. Today it is home to our beachfront hotel in Punta Cana, where you will find the perfect blend of relaxation and adventure.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Puntacana Resort?

The check-in time at The Westin Puntacana Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Puntacana Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Puntacana Resort. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room.

What are the parking options at The Westin Puntacana Resort?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Puntacana Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Puntacana Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Puntacana Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Puntacana Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Puntacana Resort?

The closest airport near The Westin Puntacana Resort is Punta Cana, Dominican Republic (PUJ). PUJ is located approximately 1.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Puntacana Resort have an airport shuttle to Punta Cana, Dominican Republic (PUJ)?

Yes, The Westin Puntacana Resort has an airport shuttle to Punta Cana, Dominican Republic (PUJ).

Does The Westin Puntacana Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Puntacana Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
