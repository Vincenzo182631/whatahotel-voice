# Bourbon Steak

Bourbon Steak

Bourbon Steak Orange County is Chef Michael Mina’s nod to the American steakhouse experience. Join us for a taste of classic Americana infused with world flavors and Chef Mina’s flair for the dramatic.

## Hours

Hours

5 – 8:30 pm Sunday and Tuesday to Thursday

5 – 9 pm Friday & Saturday

Bourbon Steak invites guests to relax and indulge in superb steak, seafood, and wine selections, plus a one-of-a-kind whiskey and cocktail experience.

Book a Table

## Cocktail Program

Explore the distinctions of classic 20th-century cocktails alongside modern-day interpretations. Our beverage program presents a playful juxtaposition of old-school cocktails and their new-school counterparts.

## Private Dining

A private dining experience, artfully prepared, provides the perfect respite and a tranquil connection point. Host your next gathering at our Southern California resort. Suitable for 12 to 30 guests, all of our private dining offerings include a prix fixe menu crafted by our executive chef and available at different price points.
