# AVEO Table + Bar

AVEO Table + Bar

Find yourself whisked away to an oceanview dining experience anchored by the California coast. A rotating seasonal menu showcases local seafood, prime filets, savory sandwiches, and fresh salads, with ingredients predominantly grown or caught in Southern California. At the expansive bar, take a seat with a specialty cocktail or local brew while admiring ocean-facing terrace views.
