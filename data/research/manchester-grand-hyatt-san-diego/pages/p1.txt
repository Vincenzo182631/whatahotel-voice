## Experience our Downtown Waterfront Hotel Near the San Diego Convention Center

Explore Southern California’s vibrant culture and natural beauty from our waterfront hotel in downtown San Diego. Unwind in spacious hotel rooms and family suites featuring stunning views and appointed with upscale amenities. Located steps from the city’s top attractions, like Petco Park and the San Diego Convention Center, our guests enjoy easy access to all things San Diego, including the region’s best beaches, the local restaurant scene, Seaport Village and various entertainment venues.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Our spacious hotel rooms and family suites feature incredible waterfront views and endless amenities, plus a convenient location near the San Diego Conference Center. Opt for one of our 1,050-square-foot Presidential Suites for stunning sunset views of the bay, a separate living space and upscale décor.

StayFit Fitness Center

Waterfront Meeting Space

Explore our hotel transformation

Experience the transformation of Manchester Grand Hyatt Sand Diego with refreshed meeting spaces, dining venues and our iconic rooftop pool. Hotel Dining

Experience the beautiful bounty of California when dining in our downtown hotel restaurants. Our seven dining options give you flexibility and variety. Our menus highlight the San Diego region's locally sourced ingredients, as well as local craft beers and world-renowned California wines. Room service delivery and grab-and-go meals are also available.

## EXPLORE SAN DIEGO

Our hotel is ideally located for exploring tons of exciting attractions in and around downtown San Diego. Business travelers will enjoy our convenient proximity to the San Diego Convention Center. History buffs won’t want to miss a tour of the famous USS Midway aircraft carrier, while families can enjoy the wealth of entertainment, shopping and dining options at Seaport Village. Don’t forget to highlight your San Diego visit with some cultural activities, like a show at Rady Shell or a classic film at Rooftop Cinema Club Embarcadero.
