### Good hotel with a few disappointments

We recently stayed at this hotel since it was suggested by Disney prior to a Disney cruise. While the hotel is large and full service, we found a few areas of disappointment. The mandatory $49/day resort fee did have lots of options for activities with a 10% discount and a $15 discount if you went to their rooftop bar. But for us, many of the activities didn't interest us or due to the excessive heat, we chose not to try. One activitiy that we really wanted to do, ended up not being available at all (Paiint the Bay no longer exists). We also don't really drink and don't love sushi. The $15 discount was in a noisy bar with very limited sushi menu. We went the first night and had no interest in returning on the other 2 nights we were there. We did enjoy the 2 "free" bottles of water each day! I would recommend that the hotel review the offerings for the required resort fee! The breakfast buffet was rather pricey too. We did enjoy the pool and the hotel is in a great locaton for exploring San Diego.
