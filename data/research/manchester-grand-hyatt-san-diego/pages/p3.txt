# Dining

Experience the beautiful bounty of California when dining in our downtown hotel restaurants, near the San Diego Convention Center. Whether you want a waterfront table just steps from the bay, private dining for an intimate event or a panoramic sunset view over the Pacific, our seven restaurant options give you flexibility and variety. Our menus highlight the region's locally sourced seafood and produce, as well as local craft beers and world-renowned California wines. Room service delivery and grab-and-go meals are also available.

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.
