# Welcome to JW Marriott Tampa Water Street

## Enjoy modern luxury at our hotel on Water Street, Tampa, FL

Discover a luxury hotel on Tampa's Riverwalk that offers just what you need, exactly when you need it. JW Marriott Tampa Water Street combines modern luxury with the sophisticated energy of Tampa's vibrant downtown district; the Tampa Riverwalk, Benchmark International Arena, and other attractions are right at your fingertips. Find wellness in the Spa by JW®, with its luxurious treatments and custom...See More massages. Relax and bask in the Florida sun at our resort-style pools, which feature private cabanas and scenic water views. Catch an inspiring sunset from our outdoor terrace or savor locally inspired dishes from six dining outlets, showcasing indoor and outdoor seating. Unwind in sophisticated lodging with stunning city and water views, contemporary décor, and elegant touches. Spark creativity or celebrate a special occasion in nearly 100,000 square feet of event space. From chef-crafted specialties to holistic wellness amenities to sleek, modern rooms, our downtown Tampa hotel is dedicated to your well-being.See Less

### Discover Our Destination

### Water Street Tampa

Sitting amid a coastal city bursting with life, culture and natural beauty, Water Street Tampa is a new kind of urban community that offers exceptional dining, shopping, wellness and entertainment for guests and locals alike.

### Tampa’s Riverwalk

The scenic downtown Tampa and Tampa Riverwalk continues to expand its offerings and boasts not-to-be-missed experiences for all to enjoy. The hotel's central location makes it easy for guests to access Sparkman Wharf, the Pirate Water Taxi, Tampa Museum of Art, Straz Center, and more.

### Ybor City

Tampa Bay's historical and cultural roots lie in Ybor City. Brick exteriors and converted cigar factories exude historical charm while award-winning dining lines the streets. Flock to Colombia for traditional Cuban fare and more.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Tampa Water Street?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Tampa Water Street is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Tampa Water Street allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Tampa Water Street.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Tampa Water Street?

Off-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Tampa Water Street. The daily valet fee is $55.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Tampa Water Street have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Tampa Water Street has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Tampa Water Street booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Tampa Water Street?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Tampa Water Street is Tampa International Airport (TPA). TPA is located approximately 7.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Tampa Water Street have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Tampa Water Street has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Tampa Water Street have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Tampa Water Street has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
