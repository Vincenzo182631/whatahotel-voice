## Discover the Tampa Neighborhood

Positioned in the heart of Tampa’s thriving Water Street district, JW Marriott Tampa Water Street places guests at the center of the city, near premier attractions such as Benchmark International Arena, Raymond James Stadium, the Florida Aquarium, and the Straz Center for the Performing Arts. Perfect for travelers looking to experience the best of Tampa, the hotel offers effortless access to major sporting events, concerts, and live performances, allowing guests to enjoy an exciting day or night out before returning to the comfort and sophistication of a luxury stay.

### The Guac Experience

When life gives you avocados, make guacamole with us at Lona. Groups of up to 8 can enjoy a 45-minute guacamole making experience, let by our culinary team. Each person who joins will also enjoy a complimentary Lona Margarita.

Reserve Your Spot

### JW Garden Pairing Experience

Guests are invited to bring the essence of the JW Garden to every sip and bite. Savor expertly crafted cocktails made tableside with fresh botanicals from our garden. Enjoy our selection of refreshing, garden-inspired cocktails, such as Sage Before Beauty and Pineapple Basil Tepache, paired with flavorful small plates such as Pomme Pavé and Shrimp Figueroa, each delicately finished with hand-picked garden herbs.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Tampa Water Street have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Tampa Water Street offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Tampa Water Street have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Tampa Water Street has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott Tampa Water Street have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa by JW® is located on-site at JW Marriott Tampa Water Street. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Tampa Water Street?

Yacht StarShip, Florida Aquarium, Tampa Riverwalk, Pirate Water Taxi, Port Tampa Bay and more are nearby JW Marriott Tampa Water Street.
