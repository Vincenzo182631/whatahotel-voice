## Destination Fee Inclusions

Our destination fee includes a daily $20 food and beverage credit, daily fitness/wellness classes, a daily beverage tasting, your choice of admission for two guests on a round-trip water taxi ride, one hour use of water sports OR one hour use of bike rentals, and enhanced daily Wi-Fi. The fee is currently $20 daily, excluding tax. Destination fee rates and inclusions are subject to change.
