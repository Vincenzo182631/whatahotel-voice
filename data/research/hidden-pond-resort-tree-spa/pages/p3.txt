## Elevate Your Experience

Make the most of your stay in our luxury hideaways with a selection of enhancements designed to elevate your experience. From floral arrangements and chocolate-covered strawberries to gourmet charcuterie boards and fine wine, order as many add-ons as you like! Please email stay@hiddenpondmaine.com to add onto your getaway.

Order a Stay Enhancement

Kids Camping Experience

$425

We’ll fashion a tent on your screened-in porch complete with two sleeping bags, a plush “fire pit”, and a backpack filled with essential camping needs. Must be ordered four days in advance and only available in two-bedroom cottages, for up to two children.

Golf Cart Rental

(4-6 seats)

$100-$130/day

Grab the family, and cruise around the resort grounds in style! Front desk concierge will confirm golf cart availability & driver requirements with you upon approval of your request.

Wag with Swag

$600

Complete your furry friend’s getaway with a luxurious custom dog bed, cozy Chappy Wrap dog blanket, specialty treat, and fun toy.

E-Bike

$60/day

Enjoy a scenic ride through our grounds or along the coastline on one of our E-Bikes. Perfect for exploring at your own pace and taking in the surrounding views. Front desk concierge will confirm E-Bike availability & driver requirements with you upon approval of your request. **Riders must be at least sixteen (16) years of age**.

Sleep Kit

$55

Unwind with a curated selection of sleep essentials for sweet dreams! Featuring a lavender aromatherapy inhaler, magnesium spray, face mask, ear plugs, and soothing sleep tea, this unique kit helps you relax and drift into peaceful slumber amongst your travels.

Weber Grill Provisioning

(Burger, Chicken, or Steak Dinner for Two)

$65, $80, $100

All meals have been seasoned and prepared carefully by our Chef. Choose from steak, chicken or burgers – all of which come with veggies from the farm, salad and a dessert. All kits are for two people. Instructions are included for how to cook properly.

\*Please note, all orders need 48 hours advance notice.\*

Bucket of Local Craft Beverages

$40

Crisp bucket of four local craft beers, hard ciders, or seltzers.

Bottle of Wine

$50

Your choice of red or white wine - Chardonnay, Pinot Grigio, Sauvignon Blanc or Pinot Noir, Cabernet Sauvignon, or Red Blend.

Maine Snack Basket

$35

Welcome to Maine! Munch on a variety of snacks for a taste of Maine.

Plate of Macarons

$30

Enjoy an assortment of sweet macarons in the comfort of your room.

La Vie Est Belle with Champagne

$265

Hand-dipped chocolate strawberries, whoopie pies, and a bottle of Champagne make for the sweetest stay.

Beach Lunch

$30

Make the most of your time on the coast with a freshly prepared to-go lunch, ready whenever your adventure begins. _**\*Please note, all orders need 24 hours advance notice.\***_

If You Give a Mouse a Cookie

$35

A plate of old-fashioned chocolate chip cookies and glass-bottled milk is the perfect way to unwind between a day of adventure and bedtime.

Seasonal Flower Arrangements

$145

Brighten up your space with a beautiful floral arrangement displayed in a glass vase, created from fresh, local flowers that highlight what’s in bloom each season.

\*Must be ordered four days in advance.

Chocolate Strawberries

$25

Celebrate a special occasion or treat yourself to a well-earned plate of hand-dipped chocolate-covered strawberries, just because.
