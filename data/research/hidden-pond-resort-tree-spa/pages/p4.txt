# Things to Do at Hidden Pond®

During your stay at Hidden Pond, do as much or as little as you’d like. Plan a day at the spa, kayak along the shore, go for a walk in the woods, or simply relax in your room with a good book. When you’re ready for a bite to eat, make your way to the Farm Bar at Earth at Hidden Pond for small plates and poolside drinks. Or hop on our free shuttle for lunch at our sister property, The Tides Beach Club. By the time you return, the nightly bonfire will be ready for some s’mores.

## Bath Soak Class

### Bath Soak Class

Pamper yourself with our self-care class and learn how to make and create a special one-of-a-kind bath soak. Step into a world of relaxation as you customize your own bath salts tailored to specific areas of the body you want to focus on. Whether it's soothing sore muscles, refreshing tired feet, or simply unwinding after a long day, this class offers a personalized touch to your self-care routine. ## Body Scrub Workshop

### Body Scrub Workshop

In this Class we will demonstrate how to make a few types of scrub textures specifically scrubs and emulsified sugar & salt scrubs. This interactive class will cover different scrub textures from salt scrubs to oil-based and sugar-based scrubs. You’ll also gain knowledge about exfoliation levels, skin-safe actives, preservation, and blending techniques for effective yet gentle results.You will learn how to work essential oils, and nourishing ingredients to craft scrubs that cleanse, detoxify, and rejuvenate the skin. This class is perfect for DIY lovers who want to have fun creating a special memory from Hidden Pond that will leave your skin fresh and smooth! Ages 12+
