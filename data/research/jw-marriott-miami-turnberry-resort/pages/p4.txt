# A Tropical Oasis, Minutes From Miami's Top Attractions

## Experiences

From world-class Golf and our 5-acre Tidal Cove Waterpark, to our expansive 3-story Spa & Wellness Center, discover activities & experiences for all ages.

## The Soffer Course

Home to LPGA and PGA events, it offers a risk and reward scenario at nearly every hole. Avoid the creeks that crisscross several fairways. Beware of lagoons that creep into many landing areas. Challenge yourself with our newly implemented Tif Eagle greens providing a consistent green speed. When you reach 18, prepare for a par four that tests your fortitude with a picturesque Peninsula Green sitting on a legendary finishing hole. The Soffer Course is a champion masterpiece.

## The Miller Course

Shorter than the Soffer Course, this shot-maker’s layout requires solid drives and pinpoint irons. Rolling fairways framed by mature ficus, banyan and olive trees put a premium on accuracy at every hole. Try not to be distracted by the pink flamingos nesting on Lake Julius – named for Julius Boros, US Open Champion and our first Director of Golf. The Miller Course is a strategic gem.

Golf Rules & Etiquette

Golf carts may only be operated by individuals who are at least 16 years of age and possess a valid driver’s license. No exceptions will be permitted.

Proper golf attire is required at all times while on the golf course and when using the driving range.

All juniors under the age of 16 must be accompanied and supervised by an adult at all times on both the golf course and driving range.

Juniors aged 16 and under are eligible for a 50% discount on the prevailing green fees throughout the day.

Individuals aged 17 and older are required to pay the standard adult green fee.

Club rentals are not available for children or junior golfers under any circumstances.

Play Guidelines & Etiquette

At Turnberry Isle Racquet Club, gameplay is enjoyed in the spirit of sportsmanship and respect. Please observe all guidelines and safety rules prior to court usage.

The Racquet Shoppe offers premium equipment, apparel, refreshments and more, for rent and purchase.

All participants must sign a safety waiver prior to entry.

All entrants assume gameplay at their own risk.

Proper racquet attire is required; shirts with sleeves, non-marking light-soled shoes only.

USAPA and Padel USA approved and non-approved equipment may be used on the courts. Low noise requirements. Dura Fast balls are not permitted.

A maximum of four balls may be used per court, except during approved lessons.

Non-participating guests & children must watch from the viewing area under adult supervision

Court Rules \| Pickleball And Padel

All players must sign in at the Racquet Shoppe prior to court entry.

When Open Play courts are at capacity and others are waiting, play may be limited to thirty (30) minutes per group. Players are welcome to rejoin the rotation as frequently as desired.

Pickleball Court #3 is reserved as a beginner-friendly and solo-player court. New players are encouraged to start here; it is also ideal for those without a partner.

When open courts are in use, waiting players may place their paddles next to the net of the game in progress. At the end of the game: if four paddles are down, all players must vacate the court for those waiting. If two paddles are down, the winning team may remain, or the waiting players may opt to play singles.

“Double-stacking” paddles—playing while placing a second paddle to hold a spot on another court—is not permitted.

Courts may not be held while others are awaiting to play. Please respect scheduled court times - arrive early, and finish on time.

Please return rental equipment to the Racquet Shoppe promptly after play.

## Don Soffer Exercise Trail

Open 24 hours a day, year-round. The Trail is free and open to the public. The 3.1 mile exercise path circles our JW Marriott Turnberry Miami Golf Course along Country Club Drive. The Trail consists of a 10-foot wide paved ADA accessible path, landscaped with an abundance of shade trees complete with ground lighting, benches, chilled water fountains, trash receptacles and crosswalks.
