## From Napkin Sketch to Miami Resort

A 55-year-old property, Turnberry was at the core of Don Soffer's vision of present-day Aventura - a city that was planned out on a cocktail napkin by Don himself. The Resort continues to be owned and managed by the Soffer family.

## A Timeless Tradition

The city of Aventura, Spanish for “adventure”, began with a prestigious country club, 12 miles north of Miami with more of Don Soffer's planned developments rising up around it. Fast-forward several decades and today, JW Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa is a world-class resort and the centerpiece of the vibrant community of Aventura.

## Celebrated Design

The standout interior design took inspiration from Miami’s art-deco history presenting clean, geometric lines coupled with classic designs and simple, sophisticated details. The materials and color palettes used throughout the recently renovated property come together to celebrate elegance and a strong sense of place, boasting a modern residential design that is true to the JW Marriott aesthetic.

## The Turnberry Legacy

For all its glitz and glamor, Turnberry traces its roots back to one humble origin — a stray cocktail napkin. Back in 1967, famed real estate developer Donald Soffer conceived a vision for an upscale residential area; and with help from his father, Harry, and a group of investors, he purchased 785 acres of South Florida swampland. Ownership changes, renovations, and expansions occurred in the decades since, and today — under the watchful eye of Donald's son, Jeffrey — the JW Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa occupies a spot on Travel + Leisure's "World's Best Resorts" list.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa. Self parking is available for $45.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $55.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa is Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL). FLL is located approximately 12.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
