## Accommodations

The guest rooms and suites at JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa are among the most spacious and elegant in South Florida. Each features a private furnished balcony or terrace affording stunning views of lush Florida foliage in Aventura, the ocean, two championship Turnberry Isle golf courses, or beautifully landscaped pools. Highlights of these soothing and contemporary retreats include custom...See More designer furnishings, floor-to-ceiling windows, and marble bathrooms.
