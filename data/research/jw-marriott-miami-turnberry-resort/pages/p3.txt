Restaurant Details

Director of Culinary, Brian Kenny

Brian brings over 25 years of luxury hospitality experience, with leadership roles at world-class properties such as The Ritz-Carlton, Buckhead and most recently the Fontainebleau Las Vegas, where he was part of the opening executive team for the 3,700-room resort.

In that role, he helped build and lead a team of more than 1,000 culinary professionals, oversaw multiple executive chefs, and drove the creation and development of thousands of recipes across 32 food and beverage outlets.Beyond his professional achievements, Brian enjoys spending time outdoors and is passionate about his hobbies of hunting and fishing.

RESTAURANT DETAILS

Chef Michael Mina

Chef Mina’s culinary vision led to founding his company, MINA Group, with partner Andre Agassi. He opened operations around the world, including the Michelin-starred MICHAEL MINA, a flagship restaurant in San Fran. Honored with numerous accolades, including James Beard Foundation “Who’s Who of Food & Beverage” inductee, Gayot Restaurateur of the Year, and more, Chef Mina continues to dazzle the culinary world.

Private Dining

Bourbon Steak invites guests to sample signature cuisine in a private venue, surrounded by striking mirrored glass walls overlooking the restaurant. Both options create a stylish, intimate space for up to 14 or 30 guests respectively. The Tasting Room is accented with high ceilings and a long, oak wood table, each space is complemented by a selection of over 850 wines to pair with premium cuts, and group dining menus.
