## Hotel Design

Redesigned by Didier Gomez, our trendy hotel features neighborhood craftsmanship. The interior design is inspired by jewelry, fashion, leatherwork and gold smithery.

## A Design Locally Inspired

Harrison Ford entrusted Didier Gomez to design his private residence. Saint Laurent, Kenzo and Dior picked him to shape their headquarters. Didier Gomez oversaw the renovation of Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel, breathing his innate sense of contrast into our boutique hotel. His goal was to sketch out the features of our space and dress it up in a contemporary, urban style while revealing the charisma of our historic, 18th-century mansion, once owned by the Duc de Noailles.

Past Meets Present

### The Grand Hôtel de Noailles

In the 18th century, the Grand Hôtel de Noailles, owned by the Duc de Noailles, graced the site of today's Renaissance Paris Vendôme. This grand building was the setting for the marriage of the Marquis La Fayette, one of France's most sought-after bachelors, to Marie Adrienne Françoise de Noailles in 1774. Queen Marie-Antoinette herself received Lafayette here upon his return from America.

### From Revolution to Restoration

During the French Revolution, the Grand Hôtel de Noailles was confiscated. It wasn't until the Restoration that the hotel was returned to the de Noailles family, who subsequently sold it. In 1830, the hotel and its gardens were demolished. Baron Haussmann's redevelopment of Paris later divided the property into five parts, marking the end of an era.

### A New Era

In 2005, Marriott International breathed new life into the historic site by transforming it into the Renaissance Paris Vendôme. Inspired by the greatest haute couture houses, designer Didier Gomez infused elegance and femininity into the hotel's renovations, unveiling a blend of historical charm and modern luxury.

### Water, Energy & Waste Management

At the Renaissance Paris Vendôme, we reduce our environmental footprint by offering bed linen and towel cleaning on request. The hotel is equipped with 100% LED light bulbs and works with specialist waste sorting companies.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel?

The check-in time at Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel.

What are the parking options at Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel?

On-site valet parking is available at Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel. The daily valet fee is €60.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel booking.
