# Discover Downtown Paris Lifestyle

## Accommodations

Set in a historic 18th-century mansion in the intimate Rue du Mont Thabor, our trendy 5 star boutique hotel offers 97 family friendly guest rooms. Comfortable, stylish and above all Parisian, our rooms...See More and luxury suites offer a touch of local flavor with references to our downtown neighborhood’s savoir faire. From the peaceful terrace of our Urban Patio to our iconic Parisian Corner Suite or our elegant Junior Suite, enjoy our surprising amenities, vibrant dishes from room service and amazing views of Paris and the amazing typical views on the Parisian roofs from the high floors of our Hotel.See Less
