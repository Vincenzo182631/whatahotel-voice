# Experiences at Renaissance Paris Vendome Hotel

## Experiences

Our boutique hotel near the Louvre in Paris is deeply steeped in the atmosphere of the 1st district of Paris, located near the city center. Here, fashion and culture live in harmony. This emblematic Parisian...See More neighborhood is also known as the shopping district and it places the major sights of the French capital at your doorstep. From the Opera Garnier and the iconic shops of Rue Saint-Honoré to the Louvre Museum and Place Vendome, where the city's most renowned jewelers are concentrated, our hotel with a balcony in Paris immerses you in the culture of downtown.See Less

### Water, Energy & Waste Management

At the Renaissance Paris Vendôme, we reduce our environmental footprint by offering bed linen and towel cleaning on request. The hotel is equipped with 100% LED light bulbs and works with specialist waste sorting companies.

### Biodiversity

We support local environmental projects such as protecting bees by sponsoring organic hives in the Mercantour National Park.

### Raising Awareness

We raise our guests' awareness of sustainable practices by suggesting eco-actions they can adopt. Our “Green Team” trains associates in environmentally friendly behaviour, to be applied in their daily lives too.

### The Fashion District

From the luxury shops of the Champs-Élysées to the small boutiques of Rue Saint-Honoré to department stores like Printemps and Galleries Lafayette, some of the best shops in Paris are steps away from our hotel.

### Evenings at Renaissance

Take time for your 5 to 9. Our Evenings at Renaissance are mixing guests and locals for an invitation to discover. Every week, we aim to showcase talents from the neighborhood in the hotel’s lobby, Le Studio. From pop-up exhibitions to tastings or new collection presentations, you are invited to pass by, to grab a drink, and immerse in a Parisian cocktail. A local fashion designer, an ethic jewelry...See More brand, a local creative chocolatier and much more await you at the Renaissance Paris Vendome.See Less

## Discover Local Talents

A perfume launch cocktail party, a presentation of a hats designer’s summer collection, a fashion pop-up exhibition varnishing… Evenings at Renaissance are all about discovery.

Local Area & Activity Highlights

### Place Vendome

Discover the iconic Place Vendome located at a few steps away from our hotel, home to prestigious jewelry shops such as Van Cleef & Arpels, Boucheron and Cartier.

Learn More

### Opera Garnier

Attend an opera or ballet at the Opera Garnier or simply visit this historic building, which is representative of the art and architectural style of the French Second Empire.

Learn More

### Palais Royal

Take a stroll in the Royal Palace Gardens, attend a play at the La Comédie-Française theatre and pose for a picture by one of the iconic columns designed by Buren. The Palais Royal is a 10-minute walk from our hotel near the city center.

Learn More
