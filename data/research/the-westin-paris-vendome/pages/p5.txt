There's a lot to like about this hotel in Paris's first arrondissement, from its spacious, high-ceilinged rooms, to its 24-hour room service policy, to the toothsome breakfast buffet that's laid out for guests each morning—no need to scout around on the streets for a fresh, flaky croissant when you stay here. But of course, there are some standouts: Lunch at the hotel's Le First restaurant is a must-do (it's worth it to sit out on the terrace, in the hotel's inner courtyard, where you can admire the mosaic-tiled fountain and surrounding columns and archways). The Six Senses spa offers an extensive menu of treatments, from a body scrub to an "Indian head massage," applied with warm coconut oil. Try to snag a Junior Suite for a panoramic view that stretches from the Louvre to the Eiffel Tower.
