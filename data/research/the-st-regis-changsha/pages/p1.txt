### Yan Ting

Yan Ting showcases luxury Cantonese cuisine with a modern presentation, accompanied by authentic Hunan dishes, offering a tranquil and pleasant ambience with two private rooms ideal for business and family gatherings.

Explore

### Social

Inviting open kitchen with an interactive combination of buffet and live cooking, allowing the kitchen to virtually perform a live buffet with fresh culinary offerings. A wide variety of food will be offered pass-around for an added entertaining service.

Explore

### Un

Un's five private rooms—three of which can accommodate live cooking—are sophisticated yet relaxing environments. The authentic flavors of Japanese gastronomy are showcased in an extensive menu of fresh sushi and sashimi.

Explore

### The Drawing Room

The lobby-level Drawing Room boasts an extensive selection of aromatic teas, gourmet coffees, and vintage Champagnes. The St. Regis Afternoon Tea celebrates traditions from the 1800s yet incorporates a modern interpretation with a local twist.

Explore

### Tea Lounge

The Tea Lounge offers a complete service with time-honored Chinese traditions, showcasing a wide variety of premium Chinese teas and light meals.

Explore

### The St. Regis Bar

Paying homage to the world-renowned King Cole Bar in New York City, The St. Regis Bar features premium wines, rare spirits, and handcrafted cocktails, including The St. Regis Bloody Mary. Bottles of Champagne are opened with flair by sabering.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Changsha?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Changsha is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Changsha allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Changsha.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Changsha?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Changsha. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Changsha have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Changsha has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Changsha booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Changsha?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Changsha is Changsha Huanghua International Airport (CSX). CSX is located approximately 18.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Changsha have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Changsha does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
