# Soap Service for Palace Suites

### Soap Service for Palace Suites

Çırağan Palace Kempinski Istanbul revives an old tradition dating back to the ancient water therapies of the Turkish bath.

Guests will enjoy 100% pure olive oil handmade soap, available in four different scents; mint-lemon-green tea, jasmine, orange flower, and rose-lavender. The unique experiential event, marked by the butler slicing the soap for guests - one bar crafted for immediate use, another delicately wrapped in a pouch to take home - ensures a lasting memory.

The service is provided by the butler team for guests staying in the private **palace suites** and the redesigned **Royal Ottoman Suite**, **Pasha Suite**, **Vali Suite**, and **Vezir Suite** located in the hotel section.
