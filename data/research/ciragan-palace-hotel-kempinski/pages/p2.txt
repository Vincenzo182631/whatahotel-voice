# Prepare your Stay

### Prepare your Stay

Check-in is at 14:00, check-out at 12:00

**Travellers' Information:**

Visitors from several countries need only a valid passport for visits of up to three months or can apply for a visa upon entry to Turkey. Please check if this applies in your case. Visit the Republic of Turkey Ministry of Foreign Affairs website before travelling to find out up-to-date information about visa information.

**Local Customs:**

Currency is Turkish lira – TRY, and the currency symbol is ₺. National language is Turkish.

Turkey has a rich cuisine and provides the most delicious tastes to its visitors. Istanbul is Turkey's icon city, significantly affecting the Turkish and world economy. Constantly on the move and thrillingly hip, Istanbul is one of the most exciting metropolitan areas in the world and was chosen as the European Capital of Culture in 2010. The economic and financial heart of Turkey, Istanbul is the biggest producer and importer of art, culture and entertainment. Amazingly, the city keeps expanding and now has over 18 million inhabitants.
