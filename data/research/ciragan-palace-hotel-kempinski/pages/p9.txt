# Special Decoration

As in many other areas, the Çırağan Palace has been a pioneer in interior decoration.

### Special Decoration

Decorative elements used in the Palace Suites were modelled after existing items at other Istanbul palaces. Many custom-made furniture pieces were inspired by the Topkapı Palace, Dolmabahçe Palace, Beylerbeyi Palace and Yıldız Palace. The design philosophy is rooted in a desire to connect the old Çırağan with the present Çırağan. Except for Sultan Suite, 10 of the Palace Suites are maisonette-style with featuring rich, custom-designed duplex suites, which exude a cozy and residential warmth, enhanced by the sophisticated style and soft colour palettes. Palace Suites are also decorated with great artworks and art pieces such as tiled vases, crystals, antique candlesticks, gorgeous paintings, and many others.
