# The New Hotel Lobby

### The New Hotel Lobby

Step into the stunning new lobby of Çırağan Palace Kempinski and prepare to be transported to a world of effortless luxury,  where timeless elegance meets authentic sophistication. Our iconic hotel's recent redesign has elevated the guest experience to new heights, offering a warm welcome and an unforgettable first impression. Impeccably blending contemporary design elements with the hotel's historical grandeur, the new lobby offers a seamless fusion of past and present.

The moment you enter the lobby, you will be greeted by a breathtaking view of the Bosphorus, the legendary waterway that has inspired artists, and travelers for centuries. In the designing process, inspiration has been taken from the natural beauty and rich cultural heritage of Istanbul to create an inviting and awe-inspiring space.  The redesigned lobby features a high ceiling adorned with a Murano-style grand chandelier, locally produced tile art, and an elegant sitting area that exudes opulence. The color scheme with rich Burgundy comes from the "Kaftan" which was a symbol of power. The hints of greens create a sophisticated ambiance and the large windows provide a stunning view of the Bosphorus Strait.
