# Transfer Services

### Transfer Services

The Concierge team would be delighted to assist with your travel arrangements by offering seamless private transfer services to and from both Istanbul Airport and Sabiha Gökçen International Airport.

To ensure a flawless journey upon your arrival or departure, the Concierge team invites you to contact them in advance to arrange your private luxury transfer.

**Price list:**

- **Business** (Mercedes E, BMW i5 - Electric vehicle or similar segment): € 140 + 20% VAT one way
- **Luxury** (Mercedes S Class - or similar segment): € 240 + 20% VAT one way
- **Maybach S 580**: € 610 + 20% VAT one way
- **Mercedes Vito** (between 1-6 people): € 140 + 20% VAT one way
- **Mercedes Sprinter Minivan** (between 1-9 people): € 210 + 20% VAT one way

**Please be informed:**

- Distance from Istanbul Airport to the hotel is around 45 kilometers (28 miles).
- Distance from Sabiha Gökçen Airport to the hotel is around 50 kilometers (31 miles).
- Cancellation or flight changes within the last 24 hours prior to flight arrival/hotel departure and no-shows are subject to 100% charge.

**Additional Meet & Greet service with Fast Track:**

- €200 + VAT (for up to 4 guests)
- €400 + VAT (for up to 8 guests)

_To be applied in addition to the vehicle transfer rates listed above._

Should you require any assistance upon arrival or need help locating your representative, please do not hesitate to contact the Concierge Desk at +90 212 326 46 46.
