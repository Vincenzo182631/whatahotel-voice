# A Journey into History

### Journey into the Depths of History and the Magnificent Past of Çırağan Palace

We invite you on a journey through the magnificent history of Çırağan Palace, an icon that

captivates those who visit and intrigues those who have yet to see it.

On this exclusive tour, narrated by a licensed professional tour guide, you will uncover the

unique stories of the only palace and hotel on the Bosphorus, with origins dating back to

the 17th century. As you trace of the history, you will delve into the glorious past of

Çırağan Palace.

At the end of this enchanting journey, you will be welcomed into an unforgettable historical

venue that you will leave an indelible mark on your memory.

Prepare to uncover the palace’s secrets and immerse yourself in the magical atmosphere of

a bygone era.

**Tour Details and Terms & Conditions:**

- Tours take place every Friday from 7:45 to 8:45.
- Pre-reservation is required to join the tour.
- The meeting point is the hotel lobby.
- Tours are conducted in English. Requests for other languages will depend on guide availability and may incur an additional fee.
- To make a pre-reservation, please contact our Lady in Red team in the Guest Relations department.
- Each tour requires a minimum of 4 participants and accommodates up to 10 guests.
- This tour is exclusively available to guests staying at the hotel.
- Taking photographs is not permitted in the historical area at the conclusion of the tour.
