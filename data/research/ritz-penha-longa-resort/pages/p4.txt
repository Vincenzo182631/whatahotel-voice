Michelin Star Restaurants in Portugal
Dining
Gastronomy is an expression of art, where each restaurant reveals a distinct identity and a memorable experience. From the award-winning creations at LAB by Sergi Arola and Midori to the aromas and flavours that journey across continents at Spices, through the contemporary interpretations of Arola and the authentic Italian cuisine of Penha Longa Mercatto, every moment is thoughtfully crafted. From relaxed poolside dining to signature cocktails at sunset, each experience invites guests to savour the extraordinary.
A Fresh New Look for B Lounge
Discover the newly renovated B Lounge, where contemporary design meets comfort. Featuring a refreshed décor, lighter interiors, and a warm, inviting atmosphere, it is the perfect setting to relax, connect, and unwind.

Sustainability
One of Penha Longa Resort’s greatest strengths is its exceptional gastronomy. Thanks to our extensive grounds and favorable climate, we grow fresh organic herbs and ingredients on-site. Under the leadership of Chef Vladimir Veiga, these ingredients are harvested and used in our restaurants, reflecting our commitment to sustainability and authentic dining experiences.
