Welcome to Penha Longa Resort
Set within Sintra’s rolling green hills, Penha Longa Resort is a reminder that true beauty withstands time. One of the leading 5-star hotels in Sintra, Portugal, our luxury resort is the only hotel within the Sintra-Cascais Natural Park, seamlessly weaving a 14th-century monastery and chapel into a contemporary retreat. For gastronomy-led escapes, savor seven dining experiences, including Michelin-starred LAB by Sergi Arola and Midori. For golf getaways and family breaks, our luxury hotel in Sintra, Portugal, includes a 27-hole championship course, kids’ club, playground, and multiple pools. Relax in peaceful surroundings with attentive service, just a short distance from local palaces and Lisbon, yet far from the pace of the city.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at Penha Longa Resort?
The check-in time at Penha Longa Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does Penha Longa Resort allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at Penha Longa Resort. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €125.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at Penha Longa Resort?
On-site parking is available at Penha Longa Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does Penha Longa Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, Penha Longa Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Penha Longa Resort booking.
What is the closest airport near Penha Longa Resort?
The closest airport near Penha Longa Resort is Lisbon Airport (LIS). LIS is located approximately 36.0 KM from the hotel.
Does Penha Longa Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, Penha Longa Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
