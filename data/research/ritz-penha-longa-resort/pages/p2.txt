In-Room Camping Package
Penha Longa Resort has created an “In-Room Camping” package to ensure that your children have a fun and memorable holiday. Specially designed for children ages 4 – 8, this package transports your little ones on an exciting adventure, providing all the amenities they need for an exciting in-room camping experience. The package includes a tent with a pillow, an activity book, and crayons. Prices: €79 per stay or €26 per day. *The tent is designed for play purposes only. * Package is subject to availability and advanced reservation is required. (maximum of 7 nights). For inquiries or reservations, please contact our Resort Activities department at (351) 21 924 90 11 or rc.lisrz.guest.recognition@penhalonga.com.

Sustainability
Our commitment to sustainability extends to our cleaning services and linen care. Cleaning service is offered twice daily, from 9:30 AM to 5:00 PM and again between 5:00 PM and 10:00 PM, ensuring your room remains comfortable and tidy. To minimize environmental impact, bed linens—including sheets, pillowcases, and duvet covers—are changed every other day, after checkout, upon request, or when visibly soiled. We also invite you to participate in our efforts by hanging any towels you wish to reuse on the rack or hook, while those left on the floor, chair, or bed will be replaced. These thoughtful practices help conserve water and energy while maintaining your comfort and convenience.
