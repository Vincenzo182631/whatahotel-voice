Say “I Do” in a Dream Sintra Wedding Venue
Weddings
Sintra’s lush green hills and storybook palaces set the scene for romance. At Penha Longa, our wedding experts help bring celebrations to life across Sintra wedding venues rooted in traditional elegance and easily tailored to modern tastes. Exchange vows in the 16th-century chapel or Sala dos Arcos, the former stables of the 14th-century monastery, then toast in the opulent Salão Nobre or the Coroa ballroom, once the royal family’s sleeping quarters. For outdoor weddings, our garden and lawn venues are framed by the natural beauty of the park. From bridal showers to rehearsal dinners, enjoy delicious catering options at these wedding venues in Sintra Portugal, creating memorable weddings with historic atmosphere.

Dream weddings don't just happen, they're planned. Marriott Certified Wedding Planners are trained to help. Historic Wedding Venues Offering Luxury and Elegance
With its unique location, historic venues, Sintra Mountains backdrop, and stunning gardens, Penha Longa Resort makes every wedding magical. From the elegance of a 14th-century monastery to a private chapel and lush landscapes, it offers a truly special setting. Penha Longa Catering also provides a curated selection of off-site venues to match your vision. Let us create the wedding of your dreams.

Chapel
Discover the Monastery at Penha Longa Resort. Located inside the XIV Monastery, it offers privacy with no noise or time limits. Inside, a 16th-century chapel showcases a majestic setting, while its gardens provide the perfect stage for ceremonies, cocktails, or dining.

Situated in the mysterious region of Sintra, Casa dos Penedos is a 20th Century palatial mansion designed by the renowned Portuguese architect, Raul Lino. This emblematic construction traces back to romances and mythical tales of the past.

Palácio da Fonte Nova
Discover the rich history and timeless charm of Palácio da Fonte Nova, dating back to 1743. Surrounded by natural beauty, its elegant architecture and gardens create the perfect setting for weddings, celebrations, and corporate events. Here, the past and present unite for unforgettable memories.

Ceremony and/or Reception
Salão Nobre
Accommodates up to 120 guests (cocktail setup) and up to 60 people (seated) in a remarkable 2700 sq. ft. room located with a 14th-century old palace, adjacent to the hotel. This room is on the ground floor next to the church and has dual entrances from both the garden and the Palace.
Sala Dos Arcos
One of the most emblematic rooms at Penha Longa, it is one of the most chosen for social events. Former stable of the Monastery, it kept its clerical classical image.
Sala Árabe
Unique at the Penha Longa complex, illustrates our Mudejar tradition. With strong decoration, is today a room chosen for social events and back up to the main meeting room Coroa.
Nuncio Garden
The garden dates back to the XVI century when it was used as a place for contemplation. Nowadays it is the place to create unforgettable events.
Coroa I
Also located within the palace and meaning crown, this fully restored 700 sqm ballroom was the old sleeping quarters of the Portuguese Royal Family when visiting Penha Longa. It retains a sense of grandeur with its imposing staircase, allowing up to 700 guests to enter the ballroom in style.
Forte da Cruz – Estoril
Glamorously located in the heart of Estoril and with exceptional sea views, Forte da Cruz is a truly enchanting venue. Ideal for all types of events, this charming beachfront castle can cater to up to 250 guests indoors and up to 500 on the magnificent terrace.
