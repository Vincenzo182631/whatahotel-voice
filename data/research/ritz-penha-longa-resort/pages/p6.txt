Unwind at Our Golf Courses in Sintra, Portugal
Golf
The 27-hole Sintra golf course at Penha Longa Resort, designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr., delivers a championship test framed by the tranquility of the Sintra-Cascais Natural Park. This Sintra golf resort offers two distinct circuits, Atlantic Championship and Monastery, plus practice facilities for sharpening your game. Take advantage of the variety and scenery across Sintra’s hills and enjoy five-star service at the Penha Longa Golf Club, founded in 1992, with more than 100 members and tailored activities from competitions to social events.
Book the Atlantic Championship and Monastery Golf Course
Experience the prestigious Atlantic Championship Golf Course and enjoy a thrilling round on this championship layout designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr.

The Atlantic Championship
The 18-hole Atlantic course designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr., opened in 1992 and staged the Portuguese Open two years later. Every hole is different and exciting, with Jones using the contours to great effect. The course has a superb flow and the views throughout are magnificent, especially away to the coastal towns of Cascais and Estoril and to the blue Atlantic ocean.

The Monastery Course
Opened in 1995 and also designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr., the Monastery Course perfectly complements its sister course. This 9-hole layout is less undulated than the Atlantic Championship, offering moderate challenges for all skill levels, with fast greens, narrow fairways, and stunning views of the surrounding National Park.

Sustainability
Partnership with Quinta do Pisão
Through
our partnership with Quinta do Pisão, we combat invasive species to
reduce wildfire risks and reintroduce key wildlife, including deer and
mountain horses. Witness nature's revival at Penha Longa Resort.
Wildlife Monitoring and Return of the Red Squirrel
Penha Longa Resort celebrates biodiversity with species like wild boars, foxes, and the return of the red squirrel after 500 years—a crucial player in forest regeneration.
Bird Nesting Program
Since 2022, 92 bird nest boxes have supported biodiversity at Penha Longa. With a 95% occupancy rate, they host species that naturally control pests and even assist pollinators like bumblebees.
Naturalized Areas
Natural growth areas on our golf course reduce water use and fuel consumption while boosting pollination, combining eco-consciousness with natural beauty.
Electric Buggies
With 50 electric buggies, including 15 powered by sustainable lithium batteries, Penha Longa is driving toward a greener future—less energy, lower emissions, and greater efficiency.
Weather Station Technology
Precision irrigation at Penha Longa is powered by cutting-edge meteorological data, ensuring top-quality greens with minimal environmental impact.
