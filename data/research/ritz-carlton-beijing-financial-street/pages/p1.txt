Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street
A contemporary design of glass and chrome defines the sparkling exterior, while inside, the hotel will exude elegance and warm hospitality in true Ritz-Carlton style. Two exceptional restaurants, a 1,500-square-meter/16,146-square-foot health club, and a choice of spacious meeting rooms will be among the Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street's many distinguishing features.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street. Self parking is complimentary for guests.
Off-site parking is also available near The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street is Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK). PEK is located approximately 35.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Beijing, Financial Street offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
