Suites & Rooms
Discover the comfort of ancient Chinese philosophies and retreat to luxury hotel suites that bridge the gap between exhilaration and relaxation. Suites featuring modern décor elevated with wood tones and thoughtful amenities allow the opportunity for a soothing rest in Beijing's Financial District.

The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing Financial Street
Club Level
The Ritz-Carlton Club Level features a selection of exclusive benefits:
Open 6:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. every day
Complimentary 1-hour pressing service upon arrival.
Business Center Service (Complimentary Black and White printing up to 20 pages)
Complimentary club shuttle within the Financial Street Area (Weekdays).
Complimentary 2 hours of reading service at Club Reading Area per stay.
Complimentary tea or coffee service in-room following your wake-up call.
Elevated Experience
The focal point of The Ritz-Carlton Club is a spacious lounge area, furnished to resemble a comfortable, homely living area and well stocked with books and a variety of international magazines and newspapers. A dedicated concierge is always on duty to meet any needs from hard-to-get restaurant reservations to assistance with changing airline flights. Only guests registered on Club level can access the floor through use of a special elevator key, ensuring a sense of personal security.
