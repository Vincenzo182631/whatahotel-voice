### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Residences Algarve?

The check-in time at W Residences Algarve is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Residences Algarve allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Residences Algarve. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 15.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €130.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Residences Algarve?

On-site self parking is available at W Residences Algarve. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Residences Algarve have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Residences Algarve has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Residences Algarve booking.

What is the closest airport near W Residences Algarve?

The closest airport near W Residences Algarve is Faro Airport (FAO). FAO is located approximately 45.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Residences Algarve have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Residences Algarve has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
