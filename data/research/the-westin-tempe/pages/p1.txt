### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Tempe?

The check-in time at The Westin Tempe is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Tempe allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Tempe. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 75.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Tempe?

On-site valet parking is available at The Westin Tempe. The daily valet fee is $49.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Tempe have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Tempe has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Tempe booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Tempe?

The closest airport near The Westin Tempe is Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX). PHX is located approximately 4.5 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Tempe have an airport shuttle to Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX)?

No, The Westin Tempe does not have an airport shuttle to Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport.

Does The Westin Tempe have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Tempe does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
