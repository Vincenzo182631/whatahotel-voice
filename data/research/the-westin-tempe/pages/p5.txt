# Things To Do

## Wellness

Whether you are in town for an ASU game, for business or for leisure, The Westin Tempe has plenty to do on-site and surrounding our incredible hotel. Positioned in the heart of Tempe, our hotel is within...See More walking distance or a short drive to everything the area has to offer. Check out our on-site events and activities during your stay and enjoy many unique opportunities to explore something new and exciting without ever having to leave the hotel.See Less

### Move Well

Experience a transformative journey to well-being with our signature wellness programs, designed to help you thrive no matter the challenges of travel. At The Westin Tempe, you can nourish your body, rest deeply, stay active, rejuvenate your senses, maintain productivity, and enjoy leisure time, surpassing the demands of your journey.

## Gear Lending

The Westin Tempe offers guests the WestinWORKOUT Gear Lending program, providing fitness accessories like Bala’s full-body equipment and Hyperice’s recovery devices.

## Bike Rentals

Explore the city in style with our complimentary bike rentals! Enjoy two hours of cycling adventure on us. Tempe boasts a bike-friendly environment, with bike lanes weaving through the town, making it the perfect destination for two-wheeled exploration.

## Local Trails

The Westin Tempe is conveniently situated near popular walking and hiking trails, such as “A Mountain” and Tempe Beach Park. Guests can also discover curated digital running routes using Strava, ensuring they stay active while immersing themselves in the city.

## Savor Wellness with Every Bite

At Terra Tempe Kitchen & Spirits, our Eat Well menu features locally inspired dishes made with fresh, wholesome ingredients to fuel your day. For added convenience, our in-room dining menu offers a variety of delicious and nutritious options, allowing you to savor flavorful meals in the comfort of your room.

Learn More

### Play Well

Play well at The Westin Tempe, where relaxation and excitement come together seamlessly. Lounge by our rooftop pool with breathtaking views, unwind in the hot tub, or enjoy crafted cocktails at the award-winning Skysill Rooftop Lounge. Enhance your experience with our curated lineup of events and entertainment, from tastings to seasonal happenings. And with Tempe’s vibrant downtown just steps away,...See More explore a dynamic mix of dining, shopping, and nightlife right outside the hotel.See Less

### Rooftop Bar

Breathtaking views, handcrafted cocktails, and elevated bites. Awarded The Best Rooftop Bar in Phoenix two years in a row, Skysill Rooftop Lounge is the ultimate destination for unforgettable sunsets, vibrant social events, and a one-of-a-kind rooftop experience.

### Rooftop Pool

Relax and unwind at our rooftop pool, featuring daybeds, lounge chairs, and a hot tub. Enjoy stunning views of downtown, Camelback Mountain, and ASU Stadium while soaking in the sun or taking a dip in the pool.

### Curated Experiences

Always hosting a vibrant lineup of entertainment and experiences, including fitness classes, tastings, and seasonal events, there's something for everyone to enjoy and Play Well.

## Terra Tempe Experiences

Our revolving menu of Sonoran dishes is rooted in seasonal ingredients from our own backyard. “Terra” meaning of the land, we find inspiration for our menus through provisions sourced through relationships with local farmers and artisans. Often, we bring these experiences to life with tastings, events, and more.

Relax and enjoy stunning sunsets overlooking the local area including downtown, Camelback Mountian, and ASU Stadium at our rooftop pool. You will appreciate the thoughtful design of our lounge area, or pull up a seat at our rooftop bar Skysill.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Tempe have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Tempe offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bike, Pilates Balls & Mats, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Tempe have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Tempe has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Tempe?

Papago Park, Tovrea Castle at Carraro Heights, Downtown Tempe, Tempe Beach Park, Butterfly Wonderland and more are nearby The Westin Tempe.
