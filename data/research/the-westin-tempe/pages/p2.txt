### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Tempe have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Tempe have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Tempe?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at The Westin Tempe. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Tempe?

No, the rooms at The Westin Tempe do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at The Westin Tempe have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Tempe have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at The Westin Tempe have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at The Westin Tempe do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Westin Tempe have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Tempe have hair dryers.
