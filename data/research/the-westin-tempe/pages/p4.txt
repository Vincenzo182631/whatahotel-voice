### Rooftop Revelry

Experience elevated entertainment and stunning sunset vistas at Skysill Rooftop Lounge. Perched atop The Westin Tempe, this vibrant venue offers an unforgettable atmosphere for rooftop revelry. Delight in panoramic views of the city skyline as the sun dips below the horizon, setting the stage for an evening of energetic parties and lively entertainment. Whether you’re dancing under the stars or sipping cocktails with friends, Skysill Rooftop Lounge promises an exhilarating experience high above the bustling streets of Tempe.

## Rooftop Pool

At The Westin Tempe’s rooftop pool, weekends come alive with exciting pool parties featuring a rotation of local and national DJs, along with the option for bottle service and delicious culinary offerings available for order. Surrounding the pool area are cabanas and daybeds, perfect for relaxation, intimate gatherings, or both. This vibrant social hub includes a dedicated bar known as Skysill Rooftop Lounge.

## Dining

Discover three exceptional outlets at The Westin Tempe. Ascend to Skysill Rooftop Lounge for stunning views and cocktails by the pool. Savor Southwestern cuisine with a twist at Terra Tempe Kitchen & Spirits. Need a caffeine boost? Head to Cup of Joe for Starbucks coffee and chic retail finds from brands like lululemon and TravisMathew.

## Let's Get Physical

Experience a transformative journey to well-being with our signature wellness programs, designed to help you thrive no matter the challenges of travel. At The Westin Tempe, you can nourish your body, rest deeply, stay active, rejuvenate your senses, maintain productivity, and enjoy leisure time, surpassing the demands of your journey.

## Gear Lending

The Westin Tempe offers guests the WestinWORKOUT Gear Lending program, providing fitness accessories like Bala’s full-body equipment and Hyperice’s recovery devices.

## Bicycles

Explore the city in style with our complimentary bike rentals! Enjoy three hours of cycling adventure on us. Tempe boasts a bike-friendly environment, with bike lanes weaving through the town, making it the perfect destination for two-wheeled exploration.

## Local Trails

The Westin Tempe is conveniently situated near popular walking and hiking trails, such as “A Mountain” and Tempe Beach Park. Guests can also discover curated digital running routes using Strava, ensuring they stay active while immersing themselves in the city.

### Stay Like a Local

Explore the bustling city of Tempe and all of its attractions to keep you entertained.

## Arts & Culture

The Downtown Tempe Authority (DTA) hosts a diverse range of events year-round, from art festivals to family-friendly gatherings and concerts. These events, including the Tempe Festival of the Arts and Oktoberfest, contribute to the vibrant atmosphere of Downtown Tempe, fostering community and providing memorable experiences for visitors and locals alike. With a packed calendar of entertainment, DTA ensures there’s always something exciting happening in the heart of Tempe.

## Scene & Sights

Located in downtown Tempe, The Westin is your gateway to Arizona's best. With easy access to Tempe's attractions and neighboring cities like Scottsdale and Phoenix, you'll discover a wealth of activities. Whether you're into arts, dining, shopping, or sports, our recommendations guarantee an unforgettable experience in Tempe.
