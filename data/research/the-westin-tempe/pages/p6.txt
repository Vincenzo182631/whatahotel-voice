## Prime Location Adjacent to ASU​

Located just steps away from Arizona State University, The Westin Tempe offers unparalleled convenience for visiting families and faculty. Enjoy easy access to campus events, lectures, games, and ceremonies without the hassle of long commutes.

## Rooftop Pool Overlooking ASU

Unwind at our rooftop pool, offering breathtaking views of downtown Tempe, Camelback Mountain, and ASU Stadium. Whether you're soaking up the sun or enjoying a sunset cocktail, our rooftop is the perfect retreat.

## Onsite Events & Activities

From live music and rooftop socials to seasonal tastings and game-day specials, The Westin Tempe offers events that make every stay unforgettable. Enjoy wellness activities, themed dining, and exclusive gatherings, all designed to elevate your experience. Whether you're here to relax or celebrate, there’s always something happening. ## Wellness and Fitness Amenities​

Maintain your fitness routine in our 24-hour WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio, equipped with state-of-the-art equipment, including Peloton bikes. We also offer a Gear Lending Program, providing on-demand workout essentials for your convenience.

## Culinary Delights at Terra Tempe

Indulge in Sonoran-inspired cuisine at our on-site restaurant, Terra Tempe. Our menu features seasonal ingredients sourced from local farmers and artisans, providing a true taste of the region. Inside Terra Tempe, The Arizona Room offers a stylish setting for ASU graduation parties, welcome receptions, and group celebrations.
