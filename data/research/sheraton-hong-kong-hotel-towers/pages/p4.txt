## Discover Hong Kong Icons: Avenue of Stars, Star Ferry & Clock Tower

Explore some of Hong Kong’s most celebrated attractions within walking distance of Sheraton Hong Kong Hotel & Towers. Walk along the Avenue of Stars, a tribute to the city’s legendary film industry, marvel at the historic Tsim Sha Tsui Clock Tower, and embark on a ride aboard the iconic Star Ferry—renowned for offering one of the world’s most memorable harbour crossings and breathtaking views of Victoria Harbour’s stunning skyline.

## Convenient Access to Hong Kong’s Premier Events & Entertainment Venues

Whether attending a concert, sporting event, exhibition, or international performance, Sheraton Hong Kong Hotel & Towers offers excellent connectivity to Hong Kong’s leading event destinations. Reach the Hong Kong Coliseum in just minutes, or enjoy easy access to Kai Tak Sports Park, the city’s newest world-class venue for major sporting events, live concerts, and large-scale entertainment experiences. With convenient transport links and a prime location in the heart of Tsim Sha Tsui, guests can seamlessly experience Hong Kong’s vibrant calendar of events and performances.

## Experience Authentic Hong Kong Through Its Vibrant Local Neighbourhoods

Venture beyond the tourist hotspots and immerse yourself in the city's vibrant local culture. Discover the vibrant atmosphere of Nathan Road, explore the energetic shopping streets of Mong Kok, and visit the ever-popular Temple Street Night Market after dark. Discover the waterfront charm of Tsim Sha Tsui East, where local life blends seamlessly with spectacular harbour views. From traditional cha chaan teng eateries and tempting street food to bustling markets and hidden neighbourhood gems, these dynamic districts offer an authentic glimpse into the energy, flavours, and everyday life of Hong Kong.

## Immerse Yourself in Hong Kong's Arts & Cultural Treasures

Sheraton Hong Kong Hotel & Towers provides convenient access to some of the city’s most celebrated cultural attractions. Explore the vibrant West Kowloon Cultural District, home to world-class museums, galleries, and performance venues. Discover inspiring exhibitions at the Hong Kong Museum of Art, enjoy captivating performances at the Hong Kong Cultural Centre, or experience the rich heritage of performing art at the renowned Xiqu Centre. From contemporary creativity to timeless traditions, these cultural landmarks offer an enriching journey through Hong Kong’s dynamic arts scene.
