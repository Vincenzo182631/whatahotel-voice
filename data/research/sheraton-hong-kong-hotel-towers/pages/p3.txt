## Cuts & Casks

Unveiling the new "Cuts & Casks" menu at Oyster & Wine Bar. Chef Oscar skillfully infuses the unique character of each whiskey into exceptional culinary creations.

Menu highlights include Laphroaig Marinated Smoked Beef Tongue, and Smoked Scallops Risotto that marries Laphroaig’s iconic peaty aromas with the seafood flavors. The Roasted Venison Loin is accompanied by a rich blackberry reduction crafted with fruit-forward Sullivans Cove whisky, where layers of sweet-tart fruit notes harmonize beautifully with the tender, delicate venison.

We invite you to savor this ultimate gastronomic journey with more whisky-infused dishes and desserts, against the mesmerizing backdrop of Victoria Harbour.
