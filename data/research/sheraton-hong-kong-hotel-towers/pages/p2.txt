## Proud to be Awarded CrescentRating Accreditation

Sheraton Hong Kong Hotel & Towers is proud to receive CrescentRating Level 3 accreditation, recognizing our commitment to serving the needs of Muslim travelers. Guests can enjoy in-room prayer amenities, including a Quran and prayer mat upon request, with Qibla direction provided in all guest rooms. The nearest mosque is conveniently located just 0.5 km from the hotel.

Halal dining options are available at selected restaurants, and our team is pleased to assist with dietary requirements and special requests, ensuring a comfortable, welcoming, and seamless stay in the heart of Hong Kong.
