### OUR HISTORICAL LOCATION

The Langham, Sydney resides on Kent Street in Millers Point, one of Sydney's longest streets, and sits equidistant to Barangaroo, Walsh Bay and 'The Rocks'

There's lots to do nearby with iconic sights, charming bars, restaurants, and cafes.

### THE OBSERVATORY SUITE AND OBSERVATORY BAR

Our Observatory Suite and Observatory Bar pay homage to the Sydney Observatory just opposite the hotel. Built in 1858, the Sydney Observatory is a historically significant star-gazing site and has some of the best views in Sydney.

Enjoy the 3-D Space Theatre or a night tour!

### ELEGANT ARCHITECTURE

The Langham, Sydney resides in a beautiful Irish Georgian-style building, epitomising an elegant period of Australian architectural history.

### OUR ICONIC POOL

A swim in our pool at The Day Spa by Chuan is unlike any other.

Look up for a beautiful starry ceiling — a painted replica of the Southern Hemisphere.

When the water is still, you can see reflections of the constellations.

Also, be sure to admire the wall mural of Bondi Beach in the 90s!

### OUR TENNIS COURT

Take a swing at our very own championship-size tennis court just opposite the hotel.

Given our neighbourhood setting, the court is always alive with guests and local residents alike.
