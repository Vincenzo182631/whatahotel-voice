##### SCRUB ISLAND OUTPOST

Located in Marina Village, the Outpost offers a deli menu, where you’ll find fresh fruit salad or a made-to-order sandwich. Open daily, the market features a wide assortment of provisions—including the important early morning coffee and baked goods—plus deli meats and cheeses, snacks, sweets, premium wines, and liquor. Everything you need for a picnic, to provision your boat, or stock your villa. Insider tip: The market also has a pizza oven!

**Hours:**

Daily: 7:15am – 8:00pm

##### Scrub Island Garden

Guests dining at Scrub Island are now enjoying fresh, all-organic herbs, fruits and vegetables harvested from the resort’s new garden. Our executive chef incorporates items from the garden in dishes served at our various dining outlets, including Cardamom & Co. Garden items that are not used by the chef are available for purchase in the market at the Outpost by overnight guests and island-hopping visitors. It doesn’t get fresher than this!

##### Picnic Lunch on North Beach

Discover a luxurious yet convenient beachside culinary delight at secluded our North Beach. Enjoy a variety of curated picnic options, perfect for a relaxing day by the sea. Indulge in gourmet meals and refreshing snacks while soaking in the serene, tropical ambiance.

Available daily from 11am – 4pm. Advance notice is requested.
