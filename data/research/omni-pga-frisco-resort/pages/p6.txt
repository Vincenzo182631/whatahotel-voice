# Trick Rider

A Texas-style supper club paying homage to traditions with a modern twist, specializing in signature steaks and seafood dishes. Enjoy a cocktail at the horseshoe-shaped bar, under the glimmer of Cinnamon, a Swarovski crystal horse chandelier.
