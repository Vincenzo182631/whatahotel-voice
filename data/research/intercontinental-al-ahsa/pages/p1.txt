## FAQs for InterContinental Al Ahsa

Does InterContinental Al Ahsa have a pool?

InterContinental Al Ahsa has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

Exclusive indoor pool available for both and men. Men's pool is temperature controlled and includes a kid's pool and has natural light whilst women's pool is temperature controlled with an artificial blue sky.

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Al Ahsa?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Al Ahsa.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Al Ahsa for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Al Ahsa have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Al Ahsa has 3 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Al Ahsa?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Al Ahsa?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Al Ahsa.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Al Ahsa?

Check-in at InterContinental Al Ahsa is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:01 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Al Ahsa offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Al Ahsa offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Al Ahsa from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Al Ahsa is conveniently located near Dammam - King Fahd International Airport (DMM), approximately 99.42 miles away (160 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Al Ahsa?

- Flavors Restaurant
