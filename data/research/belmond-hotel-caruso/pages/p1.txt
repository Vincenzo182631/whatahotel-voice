## The haven beyond sky and sea

Between the endless blue sea and sky, and offering the most stunning panoramas on the Amalfi Coast, sits Caruso, our 11th-century palace and the crown jewel of Ravello.

- 50 rooms and suites
- Fine dining at Il Pantaleone
- Access to Villa Margherita, a Signature Villa
- Casual poolside dining at Caruso Grill
- Iconic Amalfi Coast destination for private events and celebrations

## Stay in Ravello’s Haven

At Caruso, balconies overlook the Tyrrhenian Sea, and the interiors are airy and elegant. Every room and suite in this palace is unique, featuring intriguing historical details.
