# Relax and rejuvenate at our Longboat Key resort spa

## Spa

Discover a world-class luxury spa experience in Sarasota, designed to pamper, indulge, and revitalize. From the moment you enter our luxury spa in Longboat Key, with its Celebration Bar serving champagne and wellness shots, you begin your journey of relaxation and restoration. Centered around the healing power of water, and inspired by the neighboring Gulf of Mexico, our 20,000 square-foot spa features...See More indoor and outdoor vitality waters, an oceanfront relaxation room, steam and sauna areas, thermal experiences, beauty salon, and 14 treatment rooms including a suite and a Cabana, both featuring an infinity edge tub overlooking the sea. Each zone throughout the spa has been designed to evoke a feeling of profound tranquility, with soft light emanating from nature-inspired chandeliers, a calming palette of natural stone and wood, and the comforting presence of water to soothe the soul.See Less

## Thermal Experience

Healing through water (“salutem per aquam,” as the Romans called it) has been a cornerstone of wellness since ancient times. Water symbolizes life and renewal, and its potential to purify and relax is embraced by all cultures. The St. Regis Spa aims to harness and channel the power of hydrotherapy, and the rituals of contrast bathing, with a range of experiences that will leave you feeling revitalized and refreshed. Our state-of-the-art wellness spa in Longboat Key includes: Eucalyptus Steam Room, Finnish Sauna, Showers.

## Outdoor Vitality Waters

Our one-of-a-kind outdoor vitality pool has six specialized hydrotherapy pods designed to enhance your wellbeing through the therapeutic use of water. The infinity edge provides a wonderfully immersive feeling, making you feel at one with the Gulf of Mexico.

## Fitness Center

The cutting-edge fitness center at our Longboat Key resort spa features an array of cardio and strength training equipment designed by TechnoGym. Guests enjoy 24-hour access to this world-class facility. Our wellness program will expand outdoors, allowing guests to participate in classes curated by our fitness expert.

Fitness Classes

### Mindful Start

Mondays at 8:00 AM — Meditation & Gentle Yoga Flow: Start your week with guided meditation and a gentle yoga flow. This slow, nourishing class helps release tension, set intentions, and leave you feeling grounded and clear. Tuesdays at 8:00 AM — Hatha Yoga: A traditional practice focusing on alignment, breath, and steady movement. Build strength and stability while cultivating mental clarity and resilience.

### Strength & Energy

Wednesdays at 8:00 AM — Yoga Sculpt: A dynamic blend of yoga-inspired movement, strength training, and light cardio. Sculpt muscles, boost endurance, and leave feeling strong and energized. Fridays at 8:00 AM — Mat Pilates: Core-focused exercises to improve posture, stability, and mobility. Build deep strength and body awareness for better alignment and injury prevention.

### Restore & Flow

Thursdays at 8:00 AM — Yin Yoga: A deeply restorative practice with long-held poses to release tension and improve flexibility. Perfect for creating space, softness, and inner stillness. Sundays at 9:00 AM — Vinyasa Yoga: A fluid, breath-led flow linking movement and breath. Creative sequencing and mindful challenges leave you feeling open, energized, and connected.
