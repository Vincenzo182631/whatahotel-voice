# St. Regis Longboat Key Resort Dining

## Dining

Embark on an international journey of delectable flavors and time-honored culinary traditions at The St. Regis Longboat Key. Choose from seven distinctive restaurants on Longboat Key, Florida, featuring globally inspired cuisine ranging from coastal Italian classics to succulent steaks, Latin American flavors to Nikkei Cuisine. All our venues offer indoor/outdoor seating, upscale design, and a curated...See More collection of cocktails and wines sourced from around the world. The resort is also home to a coffee shop, a tiki bar on the beach, and our supremely elegant The St. Regis Bar, which boasts a magnificent hand-painted mural and a superb program of tailored entertainment. Private dining and bespoke menu planning for hosted functions are also available, as well daily in-room dining from 6am-midnight.See Less
