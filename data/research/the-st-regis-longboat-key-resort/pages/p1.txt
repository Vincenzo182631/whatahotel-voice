# Welcome to The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort

## Bespoke Beachside Luxury on Longboat Key

Where powdery sand meets shimmering surf, discover The St. Regis Longboat Key—a newly-opened, 18-acre coastal resort offering a premier beachside experience. This sophisticated resort on Longboat Key features legendary Butler service, where every detail is tailored for timeless luxury. Whether your waterside plans include relaxing on the private beach or in a poolside cabana, drifting through the winding...See More river, or snorkeling alongside wonderful wildlife in a 500,000-gallon saltwater lagoon, there’s an aquatic option just for you. After a sun-filled day, rejuvenate at our award-winning oceanfront hotel spa in Longboat Key, Sarasota, complete with bespoke wellness services and vitality pools. From signature Afternoon Tea, to the iconic buzz of The St. Regis Bar, taste chef-driven flavors across a myriad of offerings. And for life’s most memorable moments, celebrate in the Astor Ballroom or on a picturesque beachfront lawn. Enjoy sweeping views and stunning sunsets at The St. Regis Longboat Key.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 30.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $350.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort?

On-site valet parking is available at The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort. The daily valet fee is $55.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort is Sarasota-Bradenton International Airport (SRQ). SRQ is located approximately 9.8 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
