### Tailored for Tastemakers

The Members Club amenities include:

- Access to the resort’s “zero-drop” entrance family pool, two family whirlpools, the Winding River, the adult-only Vilebrequin-themed pool and whirlpool
- Preferred pricing on beach and poolside cabana and daybed rentals, watersports equipment such as kayaks, paddleboards, surfboards and skim boards
- Pool and Beach seating, featuring complimentary towels, lounge chairs, and umbrellas
- Preferred pricing for Under the Sea Lagoon Snorkel Experience
- State-of-the-art, 24-hour fitness center with weekly wellness classes
- Changing facilities with day lockers
- Preferred pricing on St. Regis Spa services and Spa Boutique purchases
- Exclusive pricing at each of the distinct restaurants, bars and coffee shop
- Early access to holiday and special event reservations
- Members-only portal and newsletter
