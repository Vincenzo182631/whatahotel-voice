# Discover our hotel with an aquarium in Longboat Key

## Under the Sea Lagoon

Dive into an unforgettable adventure at our hotel with an aquarium in Longboat Key. Led by expert marine aquarists, our Under the Sea Lagoon Experiences place you face-to-face with graceful rays, marvel at colorful tropical fish, and immerse yourself in a tranquil aquatic environment suitable for all swimming abilities. Along the way, your marine aquarist will reveal captivating facts about the local marine ecosystem and the importance of sustainable conservation efforts.

### Close Encounters

Set off on an aquatic adventure with an extraordinary Under the Sea Lagoon experience at our hotel with a lagoon in Longboat Key. Snorkel through vibrant coral reefs teeming with marine marvels, from graceful rays to rainbow-hued tropical fish.

Book Now

### Educational Moments

We celebrate every day at the Lagoon Viewing window, an educational moment hosted by one of our Resort Aquarists. Learn about the Under the Sea Lagoon’s ecosystem, the stingrays and all the different kinds of local fish.

### Tortoise Time

Back on dry land, meet our resident Aldabra tortoises, gentle giants who are one of the world’s largest species of tortoise and among the longest-lived animals on the planet.

LAGOON LUMINARIES' FUN FACTS

### Porcupine Puffer Fish

We have 3 puffer fish named Alvin, Simon, and Theodor, and after the Cartoon series from 80’s Alvin & the Chipmunks

### Southern Stingray

The stingrays can grow to a wing span of 4’-5’ and weigh up to 200 pounds and females are larger than males

### Queen Angel Fish

Mature Queen Angelfish can release as many as 10 million eggs in a single spawning season.

### Marine Environment

Experiences at our hotel with an aquarium in Longboat Key are led by an marine aquarist who will share fascinating insights about the local ecosystem, marine life, and sustainable practices.
