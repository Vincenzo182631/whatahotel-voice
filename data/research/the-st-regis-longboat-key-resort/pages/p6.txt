# The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort Activities

## Experiences

Experience the ultimate in relaxation and indulgence at The St. Regis Longboat Key resort, which is home to a unique collection of curated amenities, bespoke services, and marine marvels. Dotted with soaring palms and lush planting, the leafy central courtyard, known as “The Pools,” features an array of swimming pools, luxury loungers and personal cabanas offering tranquility and privacy. This exquisitely...See More landscaped haven is also home to our Winding River, perfect for a leisurely float, and an extraordinary Saltwater Lagoon experience, which allows guests to swim with schools of tropical fish and interact with our resident Aldabra giant tortoises. Just a few steps away from our adults-only pool at Longboat Key lies the resort’s private beach, where guests enjoy attentive service as they marvel at views of the Gulf of Mexico. Other exceptional facilities at our hotel near St Armands Circle include our signature The St. Regis Spa, a cutting-edge Fitness Center, and Children's Club.See Less

Resort Experiences

### Pools & Beach

Discover a range of swimming pool experiences at our hotel near St Armands Circle. From the family-friendly Resort Pool featuring a large Jacuzzi and our bespoke Winding River experience, to the adults-only Vilebrequin Pool & Cabana Club with its atmospheric cascading waterfall. Pool and beach access is exclusive to overnight resort guests. Public Day Passes are unavailable.

Learn More

### The Vilebrequin Pool & Cabana Club

Indulge in an exclusive adults-only pool and beachside escape featuring The St. Regis Longboat Key’s signature Aqua Butler service with a curated menu and St. Tropez–inspired cocktails. Relax in cabanas, enjoy playful beachfront activities, and immerse yourself in vibrant Vilebrequin décor showcased through colorful furnishings, butler uniforms, and games for a fully immersive experience.

Learn More

### Under the Sea Lagoon Snorkeling Adventure

Dive into the vibrant underwater world with exclusive Under the Sea Lagoon Experiences Led by expert marine aquarists, at our hotel near Selby Aquatic Center, Sarasota. Connect with friendly rays, discover tropical marine life, and relax in a tranquil setting suited for swimmers of all skill levels. Your marine aquarist will provide captivating insights into the local ecosystem, marine life, and sustainable practices.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Resort Swimming Pool (Outdoor)The Vilebrequin Pool & Cabana Club (Outdoor)Lazy River

Does The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort have an on-site spa?

Yes, The St. Regis Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort?

Mote Marine Laboratory & Aquarium, The John and Mable Ringling Museum of Art, Marie Selby Botanical Gardens Downtown Sarasota campus, Sarasota Art Museum, Marietta Museum of Art & Whimsy and more are nearby The St. Regis Longboat Key Resort.
