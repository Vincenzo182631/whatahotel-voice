# Lifestyle Hotel Bridging Modern Living and Senses of Local Culture in the Heart of Gangnam​

Andaz Seoul Gangnam is directly connected to the Apgujeong Metro Station in Seoul and is just a short walk from the affluent Apgujeong and Cheongdam neighborhoods where you can explore Korean-chic luxury fashion and beauty, art museums and culinary delights waiting to be discovered.

## Rooms & Suites

Inspired by the meaningful Bojagi, traditional Korean patchwork weaving art, the creative interiors, artwork and amenities in guestrooms are tightly knitted to acquaint guests with an elevated sense of aesthetics closely tied to the heart and soul of our city.

## DINING

Details & Reservations

### Jogakbo

Jogakbo was inspired by Apgujeong Gourmet Alley, comprising three unique interactive dining zones: Bites & Wine, Jogakbo Kitchen, and Meat & Co. Steakhouse. Each interactive kitchen offers a menu prepared by specialist chefs with distinctive cooking techniques and styles, inviting diners to view the gourmet ingredients of the day in each zone. This presents a creative way to enjoy intimate occasions or casual social gatherings, providing unforgettable experiences in an engaging and energetic atmosphere. Don't miss the opportunity to try our Seasonal Bingsu, crafted by our passionate pastry team.

### Meat & Co. Steakhouse

Meat & Co. Steakhouse is an American-style Steakhouse where classic dishes are prepared with a modern twist, featuring a selection of premium cuts of local Hanwoo and American beef skillfully grilled to perfection in the original Pira charcoal oven, accompanied by live jazz performances every Friday evening to complete your romantic night.

### Jogakbo Kitchen

Jogakbo Kitchen presents Seasonal Korean Dining, where our chefs thoughtfully select the finest seasonal ingredients. From a casual yet refined lunch to signature dinner courses and an exquisite selection of à la carte dishes, every plate offers a unique culinary journey through Korea’s rich seasonal bounty.

### Bites & Wine

Bites & Wine offers signature cocktails, beer, wine, and a diverse all-day dining menu. Enjoy a relaxing afternoon with our signature afternoon tea set or refreshing Bingsu, and immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of the Andaz Happy Hour in the evening. Complete your ultimate culinary experience with elegant French pâtisserie from world-renowned Pierre Hermé, featuring signature macarons, pound cakes, and artistic chocolates.

### A'+Z

Feel the stylish mood and sensational atmosphere of being in a gallery. Pair our Andaz blended coffee with homemade croissants, bagels and ham and cheese baguettes, or discover our celebration cakes and signature cookie boxes—the perfect gift for your loved ones.

### Room Service

Enjoy high-quality dining in the comfort of your guest room. Our 24-hour private dining service brings you exquisite meals prepared by our professional chefs, making your relaxation complete.
