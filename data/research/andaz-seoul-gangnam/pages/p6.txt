## Transportation

Getting here

Incheon International Airport (ICN)65 kilometers65 kilometers

Transportation Options

Taxi

Approximately 80 minutes

Limousine Bus

Terminal 1: Take Exit 4B to the bus platform on the 1st Floor of Terminal 4. Take Bus 6006 and get off at Apgujeong Station. Then you have a five-minute walk to Andaz Seoul Gangnam (approximately 50-60 minutes)

Terminal 2: Proceed to bus platform 14 on Floor B1. Take Bus 6006 and get off at Apgujeong Station. Then you have a five-minute walk to Andaz Seoul Gangnam (approximately 80 minutes)

Subway

Take the Airport Railway Line to Gongdeok Station and transfer to Line 6. At Yaksu Station, transfer to Line 3 and proceed to Apgujeong Station (approximately 95 minutes)

Gimpo International Airport (GMP)30 kilometers30 kilometers

Transportation Options

Taxi

Limousine Bus

International Terminal: Take Exit 6 on the 1st floor and then take Bus 6000. Get off at Banpo Station and take the Blue Bus No. 148. Get off at the Apgujeong Kookmin Bank Stop (approximately 70 minutes)

Domestic Terminal: Take Exit 4 on the 1st floor and then take Bus 6000. Get off at Banpo Station and take the Blue Bus No. 148. Get off at the Apgujeong Kookmin Bank Stop (approximately 70 minutes)
