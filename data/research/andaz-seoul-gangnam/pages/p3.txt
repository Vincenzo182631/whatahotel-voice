## Jogakbo

Jogakbo was inspired by Apgujeong Gourmet Alley, comprising three unique interactive dining zones: Bites & Wine, Jogakbo Kitchen, and Meat & Co. Steakhouse. Each interactive kitchen offers a menu prepared by specialist chefs with distinctive cooking techniques and styles, inviting diners to view the gourmet ingredients of the day in each zone. This presents a creative way to enjoy intimate occasions or casual social gatherings, providing unforgettable experiences in an engaging and energetic atmosphere. Don't miss the opportunity to try our Seasonal Bingsu, crafted by our passionate pastry team.

## Meat & Co. Steakhouse

Meat & Co. Steakhouse is an American-style Steakhouse where classic dishes are prepared with a modern twist, featuring a selection of premium cuts of local Hanwoo and American beef skillfully grilled to perfection in the original Pira charcoal oven, accompanied by live jazz performances every Friday evening to complete your romantic night.

## Bites & Wine

Bites & Wine offers signature cocktails, beer, wine, and a diverse all-day dining menu. Enjoy a relaxing afternoon with our signature afternoon tea set or refreshing Bingsu, and immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of the Andaz Happy Hour in the evening. Complete your ultimate culinary experience with elegant French pâtisserie from world-renowned Pierre Hermé, featuring signature macarons, pound cakes, and artistic chocolates.
