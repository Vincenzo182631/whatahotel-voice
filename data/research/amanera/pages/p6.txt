**Cigar Class with Juan Alberto**

Discover the rarefied world of premium cigars in a private masterclass led by Juan Alberto, Amanera’s Maestro del Cigarro. Drawing on the Dominican Republic’s unrivalled tobacco offering and his more-than-a-decade tenure with the world-renowned Arturo Fuente, this immersive experience offers expert guidance of cigar blending, rolling and pressing, ending with the enjoyment of a signature Amanera cigar.

**Humpback Whale Season**

With every new year comes the return of an extraordinary spectacle to Amanera’s waters. Thousands of humpback whales migrate in search of a mate, dotting the horizon with their graceful presence from mid-January to mid-March. Embark on a private, day-long boat tour to Samana to witness these rare, majestic creatures on their seasonal sojourn. A marine specialist joins the trip to offer insights along the way and snacks and refreshments are included.
