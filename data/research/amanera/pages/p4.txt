## Make a reservation

Restaurants are open to Aman guests and non-residents (subject to availability).

- Phone: +1 809 589 2888

- Email: amanera.res@aman.com

\\
**Rum masterclass**

Discover the true spirit of the Caribbean most celebrated rums with a masterclass led by Amanera’s rummeliers. The class takes guests on a tasting tour of the Dominican Republic’s top distilleries, pairing individual rums with hand-selected organic chocolate.

\\
**Private beach dinner**

Enjoy the starlit of dining on the sand, accompanied by the sound of waves and the flickering of a bonfire. Take a seat amidst candles and torches while attended by your personal waiter, serving a bespoke menu crafted for the occasion exclusively by the chef.

\\
**Chef's Table**

Embark on an intimate bespoke culinary journey inspired by cuisines close to our hearts. Our Chef’s Table is where gastronomic delights and culinary artistry converge in a symphony of flavours and creativity with Mediterranean, Peruvian and Dominican menus served in spellbinding locations.

\\
**Host an event**

Let Amanera provide the perfect setting for celebrating a milestone birthday, anniversary, incentive trip, or an intimate proposal.
