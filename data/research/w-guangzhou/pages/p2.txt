### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Guangzhou have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Guangzhou have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Guangzhou?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 3 at W Guangzhou. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Guangzhou?

Yes, all rooms at W Guangzhou permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Guangzhou have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Guangzhou have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at W Guangzhou have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Guangzhou have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Guangzhou have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Guangzhou have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Guangzhou have in-room jettubs?

Yes, some room types at W Guangzhou have jettubs. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
