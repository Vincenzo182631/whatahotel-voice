# Welcome to W Guangzhou

## Discover a luxury lifestyle hotel in Guangzhou

Located in the city’s bustling Pearl River New Town District, W Guangzhou weaves the city's vibrant threads into a dazzling tapestry of modern architecture, rich history, luxury shopping and captivating entertainment. This city center hotel is steps from ancient sites, as well as the Metro and Guangzhou East Railway Station. All-day dining restaurant The Kitchen Table offers authentic Chinese and international...See More cuisines to tempt every epicurean. After sampling those tantalizing tastes, discover signature sips in WOOBAR. W Lounge adds to the avant-garde mix, while FIT, WET and AWAY Spa fuse relaxation and rejuvenation. A magnetic intersection of heritage and innovation, our 317 hotel rooms, including 32 luxury suites, unite unique amenities, like our signature W Bed and indulgent jetted tubs, with top technology, glamorous décor and generous layouts. Our elevated, city center venues, supported by expert planners, take ordinary events and turn them into extraordinary productions.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Guangzhou?

The check-in time at W Guangzhou is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Guangzhou allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Guangzhou. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 18.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W Guangzhou?

On-site self parking is available at W Guangzhou. Self parking is available at W Guangzhou, with rates of ¥16.00 per hour, ¥128.00 per day, and ¥896.00 per week.

Off-site parking is also available near W Guangzhou.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Guangzhou have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Guangzhou has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Guangzhou booking.

What is the closest airport near W Guangzhou?

The closest airport near W Guangzhou is Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN). CAN is located approximately 36.9 KM from the hotel.

Does W Guangzhou have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Guangzhou has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does W Guangzhou have an Executive lounge?

Yes, W Guangzhou has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
