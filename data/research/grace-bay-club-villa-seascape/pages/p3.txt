# The Hotel Building

Experience the transformation of The Hotel Building at Grace Bay Club, the exclusive adults-only section of our flagship property. The redesign introduces 22 newly reimagined oceanfront suites by award-winning AD100 interior designer Young Huh, each reflecting a contemporary vision of Caribbean sophistication. Natural textures, soft tones, and materials inspired by the island’s serene landscape flow throughout the Grace Bay Junior Suite, Luxury One-Bedroom Suite, and Luxury Two-Bedroom Suite, every accommodation rebalanced to enhance light, proportion, and connection to the sea.

## Grace Bay Junior Suite

Elegant Junior Suites located in the adults-only Hotel Building (ages 16 and older) offer an intimate oceanfront retreat. Each spacious suite spans 1,080 square feet and features a king bed, oceanfront terrace, stylish wet bar with Nespresso and Cuisinart coffee machines, in-suite record player, and a luxurious bathroom with a walk-in shower and L’OCCITANE bathroom amenities.

Redefining personalized service in the Caribbean, our dedicated Personal Concierge team curates each stay to your individual preferences, ensuring a seamless and elevated experience from arrival to departure. Guests are welcomed with our signature espresso martini upon arrival, and daily American breakfast in the restaurant is included.

\*These suites are available exclusively on the Ground Level.
