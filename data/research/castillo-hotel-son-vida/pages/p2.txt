## Benefits of a Suite Life

Book one of our suites for your upcoming stay at Castillo Hotel Son Vida and unlock exclusive privileges. Enjoy a 100 EUR daily credit to spend in our restaurants and bars, and be welcomed with a bottle of cava and local chocolates upon arrival. Your stay includes round-trip airport transfers and a 30-minute welcome massage per person. You’ll also benefit from early check-in and late check-out, as well as priority access to the best tables in our restaurants, Balinese beds by the pool, and treatments at the Spa, subject to availability. Throughout your stay, our Luxury Collection team will provide you with dedicated and personalized service to ensure an exceptional experience.
