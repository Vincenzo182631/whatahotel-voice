# Holiday Events at Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel

## Live the Festive Season at Castillo Hotel Son Vida

Celebrate the season with unforgettable moments at Castillo Hotel Son Vida. From the Christmas Lights & Gospel Concert to festive workshops, afternoon teas and open-air cinema, every event brings joy and...See More elegance. Both Christmas Eve and Christmas Day shine with gourmet dining, while our spectacular New Year’s Eve Gala with fireworks marks the perfect finale to the year.See Less

Festive Experiences

### Christmas Lights Switch On and Gospel Concert

Celebrate the start of the festive season on the 4th of December as we switch on the magical Christmas lights at Castillo Hotel Son Vida. Enjoy the sparkling atmosphere with a stylish cocktail, then immerse yourself in the powerful voices of the Palma Gospel Singers. Their vibrant performance of soulful songs filled with hope, joy and compassion will make this an unforgettable evening.

### Open Air Cinema at the Balcony of Mallorca

Join us for an enchanting open-air festive cinema evening on the 13th of December. Enjoy a movie under the stars with a refined selection of hot cocktails, crafted to warm the spirit and elevate the festive mood. Let yourself be carried away by the magic of the season in an unforgettable pre-Christmas experience on our terrace.

### Festive Floral Workshops

Discover the magic of Christmas at Castillo Hotel Son Vida with a creative workshop led by Cardo Studio on the 17th of December. In a warm and elegant setting, the session captures the spirit of the season while you craft unique floral decorations to cherish. Share special moments with family or friends and take home not only your own creations, but also lasting memories of this joyful time of year.

### This Holiday Season, Experience the Magic of Castillo Hotel Son Vida

Surrounded by timeless elegance and festive charm, Castillo Hotel Son Vida invites you to celebrate the season with unforgettable moments in a truly magical setting.

### Festive Gastronomic Experiences

## Festive Christmas Eve Menu at the New Restaurant Es Balcó De Son Vida

Enjoy an exquisite Christmas Eve menu at Es Balcó de Son Vida, featuring festive creations such as charcoal-grilled red prawn with shellfish cream, roasted cauliflower and caviar, and turbot with piquara pepper pil-pil, fish fritter and seaweed. Accompanied by atmospheric live music and impressive views of Palma's sea of lights, the setting is as magical as the occasion itself. Reserve your place and celebrate this unforgettable evening with us in true festive style on the 24th of December.

## Grand Christmas Brunch at Es Balcó Restaurant

Celebrate Christmas Day (25th of December) with a festive brunch at Es Balcó de Son Vida, where an abundant selection of seasonal delights awaits. Enjoy premium charcuterie, artisan cheeses, fresh festive salads and a selection of traditional Christmas desserts and handcrafted pastries, carefully curated for a refined holiday experience. A serene atmosphere and the gentle melody of live music create the perfect setting for a truly memorable celebration.

## Christmas Afternoon Tea at Salón Real

Savour the spirit of the season with a festive Afternoon Tea in the elegant Salón Real on the 26th of December. Seasonal flavours, subtle Christmas décor and soft music create a refined atmosphere for an enchanting holiday experience.

## Grand New Year’s Eve Gala With Fireworks

Celebrate the most magical night of the year at our exclusive New Year’s Eve Gala in the new Es Balcó de Son Vida. Indulge in first-class delicacies while live music sets an elegant tone, then welcome 2027 with an open bar, DJ and a dazzling fireworks display from the terrace. As midnight passes, the celebration continues in Salón Real with late-night snacks and vibrant dancing, ensuring an unforgettable start to the new year.

### Explore All the City Has To Offer This Holiday Season

Celebrate the season as the city lights up with festive decorations, holiday markets, delicious treats, and special events for all ages. Discover the joy of exploring everything the city has to offer this holiday season.
