# Premium Rooms & Signature Suites

Premium Rooms & Suites at Castillo Hotel Son Vida elevate your escape with expansive layouts and breathtaking vistas. Meticulously designed with local touches, these sanctuaries offer unparalleled comfort and a transformative experience. Unveil yours.

## Premium Rooms

Generously proportioned layouts invite relaxation, while meticulously chosen design blends contemporary comfort with timeless Mallorcan charm. Step onto your private balcony or terrace and unveil breathtaking vistas, be it the shimmering Mediterranean or the verdant Mallorcan countryside.

## Suites

Embrace an unparalleled escape within our Suites. Expansive living quarters adorned with locally-inspired artistry create a sanctuary of refined elegance. Unwind in the sumptuous comfort of a separate bedroom and living area, each designed to elevate your Mallorca experience. Indulge on your private balcony or terrace, where breathtaking panoramas of the shimmering Mediterranean.

## Signature Suites

Within the storied walls of Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a collection of exquisite Signature Suites awaits. Each a masterpiece in its own right, these sanctuaries offer a transformative experience unlike any other, a gateway to a world of unparalleled luxury and transformative experiences.

## Enjoy A Suite Life

Enjoy the following benefits when booking one of our suites for your upcoming stay in Castillo Hotel Son Vida:

- 100 EUR daily credit in our restaurants and bars
- Bottle of cava and chocolates upon arrival
- Transfer from and to the airport
- Welcome 30-minute massage per person and per stay
- Early check-in and late check-out, subject to availability
- Preference to book the best tables in our restaurants, subject to availability
- Preference to book the Balinese beds by the pool, subject to availability
- Preference to book Spa treatments
- Dedicated, personalized attention by our Luxury Collection team

## Classic Suite

Step back in time within the Classic Suite at Castillo Hotel Son Vida. This inviting retreat whispers stories of Mallorca's rich heritage with its comfortable elegance and meticulously crafted design. Unfold a private balcony and inhale the island's captivating essence.

## Deluxe Suite

Immerse yourself in the harmonious blend of space and luxury within the Deluxe Suite at Castillo Hotel Son Vida. Expansive living areas flow seamlessly, designed meticulously to create a symphony of comfort and style. Unwind on your private balcony and soak in the unbeatable views over the Bay of Palma.

## Palma Suite

The Palma Suite unfurls a love letter to Mallorca from your private balcony, showcasing dazzling vistas of the Bay of Palma. Indulge in spacious serenity within this one-bedroom haven, meticulously designed for unforgettable moments.

## Royal Suite

Embrace the grandeur of a bygone era within the Royal Suite. Expansive living quarters, adorned with custom crimson and cream tones, exude an aristocratic air. Unwind in a separate bedroom and living area, each a testament to exquisite comfort.

## Villa Suite

The Villa Suite unveils the ultimate private haven, boasting four luxurious bedrooms and a private pool. Immerse yourself in the beauty of Mallorca's rolling hills from your expansive garden and pool, perfect for unforgettable gatherings.
