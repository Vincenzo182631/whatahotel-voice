# Welcome to Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca

## Majestic luxury hotels Mallorca

Set in the exclusive Son Vida neighbourhood, Castillo Hotel Son Vida is a luxury Mallorca hotel housed in a 13th-century castle overlooking the Bay of Palma. This adults-only retreat combines panoramic...See More views, refined rooms and suites, attentive service and a 900-square-metre spa, offering a distinctive way to experience the island beyond the summer.Just minutes from Palma, this resort in Mallorca, Spain, provides an ideal setting for couples and luxury leisure travellers seeking relaxation, wellness, golf, culture and gastronomy. Surrounded by nature and part of Arabella Golf Resort Mallorca, the hotel offers easy access to renowned golf courses and the island’s cultural and culinary highlights.With the expertise of our Luxury Collection Concierge, guests can discover a more authentic side of Mallorca through personalised recommendations and thoughtfully curated experiences. Here, heritage, wellbeing and Mediterranean living come together in every season.See Less

## Mallorca, Beyond the Summer

Discover a quieter, more authentic side of Mallorca, where mild Mediterranean days invite you to slow down, explore and reconnect. From culture and gastronomy to golf, nature and wellness, Castillo Hotel Son Vida places the island’s best experiences within easy reach, while offering a peaceful retreat above Palma to return to.

## Festive Season at Castillo Hotel Son Vida

Celebrate the festive season in the enchanting setting of Castillo Hotel Son Vida. From traditional celebrations and gourmet dining to exclusive spa experiences and our spectacular New Year’s Eve Gala, discover magical moments for an unforgettable holiday in Mallorca.

Hotel Activities

### Wellness & Spa

Take time to reset with restorative treatments, relaxing rituals and moments of wellbeing in our 900-square-metre Spa, designed for slowing down and reconnecting.

### Art & Culture at the Castle

Experience Mallorca’s creative spirit within the castle through art exhibitions, guided art tours and cultural activities that bring local stories and creativity closer to our guests.

### Golf at Your Pace

Enjoy the only European Tour Destination golf courses in the island during Mallorca's cooler months, when comfortable temperatures and quieter surroundings create ideal conditions for a round.

### Seasonal Dining

Discover Mediterranean flavours through seasonal produce and thoughtful cuisine, with dining experiences that invite you to take your time and enjoy the island from the table.

### Nature Around Son Vida

Step outside and discover Mallorca at a gentler pace, with hiking and cycling routes through the pine-scented surroundings of Son Vida and beyond.

### Slow Mornings

Wake up to panoramic views over Palma and ease into the day with a leisurely breakfast, a quiet coffee or simply more time to enjoy the comfort of your surroundings.

### Mallorca, Curated for You

Mallorca reveals its most memorable side when you know where to look. Our Luxury Collection Concierge brings the island to life through local knowledge, trusted connections and a genuine understanding of what makes each place special.
From a hidden restaurant in a quiet village to a private visit with a local artisan, a day exploring the island’s wine country or an unexpected cultural discovery, our Concierge can shape each experience around your interests.
It is Mallorca beyond the obvious, thoughtfully recommended, personally arranged and always with a local perspective.

## Golf in Mallorca’s Ideal Season

With mild temperatures, quieter fairways and four world-renowned courses, autumn and winter are made for golf. As part of Arabella Golf Resort Mallorca, Castillo Hotel Son Vida offers an exceptional base from which to experience some of the island’s most renowned courses, from championship golf to a relaxed round of Pitch & Putt.

Discover Our Golf Courses

GOLF COURSES AT ARABELLA GOLF RESORT MALLORCA

### Son Muntaner

A championship course shaped by Mediterranean landscapes, offering a challenging and rewarding round for experienced golfers.

### Son Vida

Mallorca’s oldest golf course and one of its most iconic, surrounded by the exclusive Son Vida neighbourhood and just moments from the hotel.

### Son Quint

A scenic course combining generous fairways, natural surroundings and views across the Mallorcan landscape.

### Palma Pitch & Putt

A relaxed way to enjoy the game, ideal for a quick round, improving your short game or introducing younger players to golf.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca?

The check-in time at Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 10.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca?

On-site parking is available at Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca booking.

What is the closest airport near Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca?

The closest airport near Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca is Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI). PMI is located approximately 15.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca have an airport shuttle to Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI)?

Yes, Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca has an airport shuttle to Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI).

Does Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Castillo Hotel Son Vida, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mallorca has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
