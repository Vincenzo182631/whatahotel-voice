### SA Font Pool Bar - Closed for the Season

Set across two sunlit levels, Sa Font Pool Bar is a summer sanctuary where sea breezes meet relaxed elegance. The menu features light yet flavourful creations, from a classic Caesar salad to a rich lobster roll and local delicacies, perfect for a midday pause or a leisurely afternoon by the pool.

Learn More

### Light Bites by the Pool

During the day, enjoy uninterrupted views of the pool and sea. Take shelter from the sun, cool off with a refreshing drink, and indulge in a light, Mediterranean-inspired menu perfect for long, lazy afternoons.

### The Grill Experience - Closed for the Season

As the sun sets, Sa Font Pool Bar transforms into Castillo Hotel Son Vida Grill, a seasonal restaurant open during the summer months. Savor freshly grilled meats, fish, and seasonal vegetables, paired with vibrant side dishes and local wines, in an atmosphere made for lingering.

## Dining With Views

Wherever you dine at Castillo Hotel Son Vida, the view is part of the experience. From sunrise to sunset, savour exquisite flavours paired with sweeping vistas of the Bay of Palma and the shimmering Mediterranean — a feast for the senses.

Avaliable 24/7

## Room Service

Experience the flavours of Castillo Hotel Son Vida without leaving your room. Our 24-hour service offers Mediterranean and international dishes, fine wines, and lighter options, all served with elegance. Whether it’s breakfast on your private terrace, an intimate dinner, or a late-night indulgence, every moment is yours to savour.

## The Essence of Mallorca Fresh From Our Garden

Carefully cultivated just steps from our kitchens, the organic garden at Arabella Golf Resort Mallorca provides the freshest herbs, vegetables, and fruits for our culinary creations. Each season brings new flavors, hand-harvested to inspire dishes that honor Mallorca’s rich gastronomic heritage. From garden to plate, experience the purity of ingredients grown with passion and served with care.
