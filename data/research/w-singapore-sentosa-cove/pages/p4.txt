### SKIRT by W Singapore – Sentosa Cove

SKIRT is a contemporary open-fire steakhouse in Singapore, known for exceptional Wagyu, expertly dry-aged beef and signature wood-fired dishes. Blending craftsmanship and provenance with the beauty of live fire, the kitchen uses the Josper Basque Grill and traditional binchotan to create deep, distinctive flavors. With whole-carcass sourcing from dedicated producers, SKIRT ensures quality, traceability and prized cuts. Set along the marina, this Sentosa restaurant offers a refined yet vibrant dining escape with premium steaks and beautifully grilled seafood.

Elevate your next dining experience aboard The Afterglow Voyage, an exclusive luxury catamaran journey departing from Sentosa Cove Marina. Against a backdrop of Singapore’s glittering skyline and surrounding island vistas, indulge in bespoke menus, refined food and beverage offerings, and personalised service, all complemented by the serenity of open waters.
