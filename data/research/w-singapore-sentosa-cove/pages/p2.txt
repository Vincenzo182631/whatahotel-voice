## Accommodations

Step into your ultimate escape at W Singapore - Sentosa Cove, where bold design meets elevated comfort in our reimagined suites and hotel rooms in Sentosa Island​. Ideal for luxe staycations or business travel, these refreshed spaces pulse with energy through vibrant microcosm-inspired décor, sleek wooden floors and plush carpets inspired by the marina and surrounding views. Amplify your experience...See More with advanced tech equipment and visionary amenities. Make a splash with eco-friendly filtered water taps in every room, offering unlimited on-demand water that seamlessly blends sustainability with sophistication. Brew your perfect cup with the in-room Nespresso® coffee machine, then unwind with next-level entertainment on a dazzling 65-inch smart TV. Recharge in style with a rainforest shower or separate bathtub featuring exclusive MOMO bath amenities. Wrap yourself in a plush robe and sink into W’s signature beds, choosing your preferred comfort from the pillow menu.See Less

### Penthouse

Throw open the doors with flourish and spread out. With a spacious 195 square metres, the Penthouse is perfectly suited for larger-than-life scene stealers. Kick back with a cocktail on the outdoor terrace, or soak up the sun in your own private plunge pool while taking in panoramic views of the sea, marina, and WET pool. More is more — amplify your stay in island paradise with a twin-bedded connecting room for limitless indulgence.
