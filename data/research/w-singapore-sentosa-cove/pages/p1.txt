## Luxury Island Resort Living at W Singapore - Sentosa Cove

Discover the new stay at W Singapore – Sentosa Cove: a fully reimagined luxury resort on Sentosa Island with striking sculptural design and vibrant colors. With 240 transformed rooms and suites inspired by a tropical microcosm, expect oversized beds, coral-orange sofas, private balconies, and tech-forward touches. Set along the marina waterfront, indulge at SKIRT, our signature open-fire restaurant,...See More soak in WET Deck's energy with its playful slide and live DJ sessions, shake up the night at WOOBAR, or linger over the iconic Sunday brunch at the kitchen table. Recharge at AWAY Spa's Contrast Zone — Magnesium Whirlpool, Infrared Sauna, Steam Room, Cold Plunge, and Vitality Pool — and go deeper with AiRe Wellness, our recovery partner offering Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy, Red Light Therapy, Body Composition Analysis, and expert coaching across five pillars: Recover, Move, Connect, Optimize, and Mindfulness & Sleep. ### Transformed Design: A Tropical Microcosm

The new design of our luxury hotel in Singapore is a celebration of transformation and lush growth, brought to life through an unexpected interplay of colours, textures, and forms. The overall aesthetic is delicately surreal—where nature’s abundance meets imaginative expression. Drawing inspiration from the infinite richness of the natural world, the design weaves together themes of tropical vibrancy and microcosmic detail, capturing the spirit of the hotel as a refined island escape. Organic shapes and untamed greenery echo the wild beauty of Sentosa’s flora and fauna.

### Plan and Prepare for your Stay

Photography, Videography and Drone Usage

Drone Usage

The use of drones is strictly prohibited within the property.

Photo and Video shoots in the Resort

Guests may shoot non-commercial photography or video for their own personal use (with no professional equipment, lighting, etc.) in their room, or in the resort but must respect the privacy and well-being of other guests in the area.

Professional photography/videography and photography/filming done with a professional camera will only be allowed if the Resort Management has given permission before the shoot. All photography/videography requests will be reviewed and approved based on occupancy, availability, and content.

Commercial photography or videography is not permitted at the Resort without permission from the Resort Management.

Our first priority is the experience of our guests so we have a strict policy against the use of any of our facilities for unauthorized professional or commercial photography or videography including but not limited to pre-nuptial shoots, engagement shoots, advertising shoots, model/fashion shoots, and film productions, etc.

Requests to use any area of the resort as a location for commercial photo shoots or filming as well as special occasion/wedding shoots should be directed to the Marketing Department. These photo/video shoots are subject to fees and a strict schedule which must be approved by the Resort Management.

Should any Talent of the Property observe a photoshoot that compromises the privacy or comfort of other guests, they reserve the right to ask the guest to cease the activity. The property team reserves the right to request proof of hotel or venue booking to verify guest status before granting or continuing permission for any shoot.

The Resort reserves the right to capture photographs and video recordings in all public areas of the property for marketing, promotional, and operational purposes. While the Resort will make reasonable efforts to avoid photographing or filming identifiable guests without their consent, guests acknowledge and agree that incidental inclusion of their image does not entitle them to any form of compensation, nor give rise to any claim, complaint, or legal action against the Resort.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Singapore - Sentosa Cove?

The check-in time at W Singapore - Sentosa Cove is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Singapore - Sentosa Cove allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at W Singapore - Sentosa Cove.

What are the parking options at W Singapore - Sentosa Cove?

On-site self parking is available at W Singapore - Sentosa Cove. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Singapore - Sentosa Cove have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Singapore - Sentosa Cove has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Singapore - Sentosa Cove booking.

What is the closest airport near W Singapore - Sentosa Cove?

The closest airport near W Singapore - Sentosa Cove is Singapore Changi Airport (SIN). SIN is located approximately 28.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Singapore - Sentosa Cove have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Singapore - Sentosa Cove has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
