### Things To Do and See

Immerse in the vibrant spirit of the Island and the attractions in Sentosa from our hotel near Universal Studios Singapore. Take in the views with the infamous Sentosa cable car ride, indulge at Quayside Isle, shop at VivoCity and amplify the excitement at MegaZip Adventure Park.

Local Area & Activities Highlights

### Universal Studios Singapore

Stay at our hotel near Universal Studios Singapore, Southeast Asia’s first and only Universal Studios theme park. Be transported by adrenaline-pumping rides, interactive shows and a wide variety of exciting attractions based on the blockbuster movies and television series you know and love so well.

### Singapore Oceanarium

Explore the wonders of the deep at Singapore Oceanarium at Sentosa. Walk through mesmerizing underwater tunnels, meet majestic sharks, graceful manta rays, and schools of colorful fish, and discover vibrant coral reefs. Interactive exhibits and up-close encounters with starfish, rays, and playful seahorses make every visit both immersive and unforgettable, perfect for families and curious minds alike.

### National Stadium

The National Stadium in Singapore is a state-of-the-art, multi-purpose venue that stands as a centrepiece of the Singapore Sports Hub. Known for its iconic dome and retractable roof, it hosts a wide array of events, from major sporting competitions and concerts to national celebrations. Our hotel near Universal Studios Singapore offers convenient access to the National Stadium; just a short 20-minute drive.

### Singapore Cable Car

Be captivated and enthralled by the setting sun as the night unfolds a picturesque island landscape with Singapore Cable Car, offering a stunning 360-degree view of the Southern precinct with its transparent enclosure.

### Mega Adventure - Singapore

A first for Singapore and a must for all visitors, MegaZip Adventure Park will give you a real high, literally! Featuring the most extreme zip wires in Asia, an aerial rope course and a free-fall simulator, it's an experience that you'll never forget. Depending on your fitness level, choose to try out the different courses at your own pace.

### VivoCity

VivoCity is an iconic retail, entertainment & lifestyle destination that constantly surprises and stimulates visitors with its vibrant mix of retail and entertainment concepts. It features wide, open spaces for waterfront relaxation and entertainment, and a full calendar of exciting festivals and events that will draw both locals and foreign visitors.

See More

### Beaches

As a hotel near Sentosa beaches, W Singapore – Sentosa Cove places you moments away from the island’s many attractions and seaside experiences. Ride roaring waves, master different water sports, be mesmerised by fireworks, lights and water displays, get up-close with friendly animals or simply stroll along in search of solitude. You'll find it all along the island’s 3.2km stretch of sandy white shores.

### Singapore’s Island Adventures

Live the vivacious life in Singapore with exhilarating island tours and adventures during your stay at W Singapore - Sentosa Cove. Simply call Whatever/Whenever and we will assist with your scuba dive booking, sailing trip or island hopping arrangements!

### Cycling In Singapore

Escape the city and explore Singapore on bikes. While getting around Singapore has always been easy with their well-planned transportation systems, the city’s clean air, advanced infrastructures and traffic systems make it the perfect environment for cycling.

### The Merlion

No visit to Sentosa Cove is complete without meeting its most famous, 37-metre tall resident, The Merlion.

### Skyline Luge Sentosa

Part go-cart, part toboggan, the Luge offers a truly unique outdoor experience suitable for all ages that is not found anywhere else in Singapore or Southeast Asia.

### Madame Tussauds

Madame Tussauds Singapore is a world-famous wax attraction where guests can step into immersive zones and come face-to-face with lifelike figures of global celebrities, sports icons, and historical leaders. Pose with your favorite stars, explore interactive exhibits, and discover stories of fame, culture, and history brought vividly to life through detailed craftsmanship and engaging experiences.
