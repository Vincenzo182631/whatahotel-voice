# Holiday Events at W Singapore - Sentosa Cove

## A Festive Escape Where You Made the List

Set against the vibrant backdrop of Sentosa Cove, discover a season filled with indulgent feasts, dazzling celebrations, festive stays and moments made for sharing. Whether you’re gathering with loved ones, toasting the year ahead or escaping for a little island indulgence, let the festivities unfold the W way.

### Holiday Spotlight

The season of sparkle is here, and W Singapore – Sentosa Cove is turning up the heat on festive celebrations. From joyous Christmas feasts filled with holiday cheer to a dazzling New Year’s Eve countdown in Black & Bronze, every event is a moment worth celebrating.

Festive Happenings

### Christmas Eve Dinner Buffet

Kick off the Festive celebrations with a spectacular spread, from a succulent whole roasted turkey to fresh oysters, lobster, and an exquisite selection of snow crab and scallops on the shell. Don’t miss our live stations and the carvery featuring whole roasted turkey and more. Elevate your evening with farmhouse cheeses by Maison Bordier, a perfect close to a night of indulgence.

Reserve Now

### Christmas Dinner Buffet

Round off Christmas with a dinner buffet that’s as magical as the day itself. Savor lobster, snow crab, scallops, and a host of festive classics. The honey-glazed ham and roasted turkey at the carving station promise to deliver that holiday warmth, while the decadent cheese selection by Maison Bordier ensures a luxurious finale to this unforgettable feast.

Reserve Now

### New Year's Eve Dining Celebrations

Indulge in an all-inclusive package featuring dinner, champagne, and cocktails at the kitchen table, followed by champagne and cocktails at WOOBAR during the New Year’s Eve Countdown Party. Raise a glass as we pour from the champagne tower and enjoy a complimentary bubble to toast the arrival of 2026.

Reserve Now

### Christmas Brunch Edition

Start your Christmas day with a brunch like no other. Dive into a lavish oyster bar, chilled seafood, and smoked delicacies from the sea, with signature live cooking stations. Our carvery steals the show with honey-glazed ham and roasted turkey, while the farmhouse cheese by Maison Bordier adds a refined touch to your brunch experience.

Reserve Now

### SKIRT Festive Specials

Christmas, fired up. This festive season, SKIRT turns up the heat with open-fire specialties and bold holiday flavours that hit different. Sink into our signature steakhouse experience — then let our Festive Specials steal the show: Winter Black Truffles from Périgord, Wagyu Tenderloin, Kingfish Ceviche, each kissed by flame and finished with a touch of seasonal magic. This is Christmas, the SKIRT way.

Reserve Now

### Eclipse: Black & Bronze Countdown Party

Step into ECLIPSE, our most dramatic New Year's Eve yet, where black and bronze set the stage for a night of electrifying energy, live music, and unstoppable grooves that carry you straight into 2027. Your all-inclusive package covers dinner, champagne, and cocktails at the kitchen table, followed by champagne and cocktails at WOOBAR as the countdown begins. One epic night. Zero excuses.

Reserve Now
