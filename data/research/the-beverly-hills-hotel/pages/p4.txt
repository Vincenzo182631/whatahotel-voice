# Dining

## From power lunches to poolside lounging

For every occasion and every mood, The Beverly Hills Hotel has the restaurant for you.

Contact us

Vibrant buzz

Polo Lounge

The most interesting room in Beverly Hills, still buzzing with hot names and hotter stories. Known as the epicentre of LA power dining, the Polo Lounge has been the favourite spot for generations of stars and Hollywood deal-makers.

Laid-back al fresco

The Cabana Cafe

Casual, fresh dining in a picture-perfect setting. Enjoy a relaxed meal in the LA sunshine while your view of the pool sparkles with sheer pleasure.

Warm and welcoming

The Fountain Coffee Room

The iconic diner has been serving Hollywood stars since 1949 and you still never know who you might find yourself eating a slice of apple pie next to.

What restaurants are available at The Beverly Hills Hotel?

We have the Polo Lounge, The Cabana Cafe, and The Fountain Coffee Room.

Tell me about the Polo Lounge.

The Polo Lounge is The Beverly Hills Hotel's most legendary restaurant. From power breakfasts to late-night martinis, it has been the meeting point for Hollywood’s most recognisable faces for decades. It's open daily.

Tell me about The Cabana Cafe.

The Cabana Cafe is The Beverly Hills Hotel's al fresco poolside restaurant. Its opening hours vary by season. In May to September, it's open from 9am to 5pm (Sunday to Thursday) or 6pm (Fridays and Saturdays). In October to May, it's open from 10am to 4pm every day.

What are The Fountain Coffee Room's hours?

The Fountain Coffee Room is open daily from 7am to 4pm.

What time is breakfast served and where?

Breakfast is served à la carte at The Polo Lounge from 7am to 11am, in the Fountain Coffee Room from 7am to 4pm, and in The Cabana Cafe from 9am to 11.30am.

Is breakfast à la carte or buffet?

Breakfast is served à la carte in all available outlets.

Is the Polo Lounge open every day?

Yes, the Polo Lounge is open daily from 7am to 11pm.

Does The Cabana Cafe have extended hours on weekends?

Opening hours at The Cabana Cafe vary by season. In May to September, it's open from 9am to 5pm (Sunday to Thursday) or 6pm (Fridays and Saturdays). In October to May, it's open from 10am to 4pm every day.

Where can I get a poolside drink during the day?

The Cabana Cafe bar serves drinks for poolside enjoyment.

Where do in-house guests have breakfast?

Breakfast for guests is served at The Polo Lounge, Fountain Coffee Room, The Cabana Cafe or through 24-hour in-room dining.

Is there a casual daytime dining option?

Yes, The Cabana Cafe offers relaxed daytime dining, adjacent to the pool, while the Fountain Coffee Room is a landmark 19-stool diner serving classic breakfast and lunch favourites until 4pm every day.
