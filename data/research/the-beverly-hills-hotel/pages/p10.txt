What to know

Doggie details

We welcome up to two fully house-trained dogs (max. 20kg / 45lbs) per room for a $150 pet fee per stay – this fee is waived for service animals. Please ensure that dogs are kept on a leash at all times whilst on hotel property, except when they are in their room, and are not left unattended.

While we love your pets, kindly note that you are responsible for any damages during their stay.
