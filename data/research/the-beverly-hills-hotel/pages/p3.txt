The Concept

The American dream

Our comforting, all-day dining menu features a selection of brunch items, salads, sandwiches and burgers as well as housemade ice creams, milkshakes and our famous Beverly Hills cakes.

The View

Take a front-row stool

This diner seats its customers at the gloriously nostalgic, curved bar. Grab a stool, relax and watch the ballet of calm and experienced service at work in front of you.

TAKE-AWAY

Sweetness to go

You can now order our famous cakes, cookies and brownies to enjoy at home. Customise your cake with decorations and a personal message to guarantee a showstopping centrepiece.

Private parties

Parties at The Fountain Coffee Room

Our Fountain Coffee Room is available after hours for your own private party. Be it a birthday, wedding, anniversary or any celebration you want, this relaxed and fun setting will serve up a treat for your guests. Suitable for up to 20 people.

Breakfast

Pull up a stool for our legendary breakfast at the Fountain Coffee Room. Think freshly-squeezed orange juice, smoothies and a stack of our delicious silver dollar pancakes.

Lunch

Our Fountain Coffee Room is home to delicious American diner classics, including burgers, sandwiches and shakes. Your spot at the curved counter awaits.

Cocktails

Cocktails so good, they shouldn't just be for brunch. Enjoy a selection of featured wines and our classic spin on daytime favourites – such as the FCR Bloody Mary with house-spiced tomato juice or a sparkling Bellini.
