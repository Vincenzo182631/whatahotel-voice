Our event spaces

Explore our event spaces

Every ballroom has a private foyer that creates an inviting space of its own, and can be decorated to suit your chosen style or theme.

The Crystal Ballroom

A glamorous Art Deco space with spectacular rose chandeliers and French doors overlooking the Crystal Garden and gazebo. Your arrival at the largest of our three ballrooms is via a grand staircase, which acts as an ideal photo opportunity. Are you ready for your close-up?

Explore The Crystal Ballroom

Rodeo Ballroom

Three rooms, one glorious location. The Rodeo Ballroom features a built-in stage and dance floor, and its signature chandeliers which have been maintained and restored to their original splendour. An adjacent pre-function area is enhanced by drapery fringe accents and a built-in-bar featuring natural stone.

Explore Rodeo Ballroom

Polo Private

A charming space amongst our wonderful gardens. Despite its total seclusion, Polo Private's patio is just behind the famous Polo Lounge, creating a warm, buzzing atmosphere at any hour.

Explore Polo Private
