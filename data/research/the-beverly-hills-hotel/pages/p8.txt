Frequently asked questions

What are the spa's opening hours?

The Beverly Hills Hotel Spa is open daily from 10am to 6pm.

Are the spa hours different on weekends?

The spa is open every day from 10am to 6pm. There is no change to the timings on weekends.

What facilities does the spa offer?

The Beverly Hills Hotel Spa offers signature body, face, and nail treatments. There are multiple treatment rooms, a duet suite, nail suite, women's and men's locker facilities which include steam showers.

Can I book spa treatments?

Yes, treatments are available daily at The Beverly Hills Hotel Spa; please contact us to arrange or reserve online.

What are the gym's opening hours?

The gym is open 24-hours a day with key card access for hotel guests only.

When is the pool open?

The pool is open for hotel guests daily from 7am to 10pm.

Can spa guests use the pool?

The Beverly Hills Hotel pool is reserved for hotel guests only.

Is the pool suitable for families?

The pool is available for all hotel guests to use. It's family friendly, with no specific time slots. Please note that all children under the age of 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Are there specific pool time slots?

No, there are no specific time slots. The Beverly Hills Hotel pool is family-friendly, and children are allowed at any time of day. Note that the pool is only open to guests staying in the hotel.

Can children use the pool?

Children must be accompanied by a parent or guardian until the age of 16.
