Where is The Beverly Hills Hotel located?

We're at 9641 Sunset Boulevard, CA 90210, the original 90210, on Sunset Boulevard.

How do I contact the reservations office?

You can reach our reservations office on +1 424 421 0060.

How far is the hotel from the airport?

Los Angeles International Airport is around 30-40 minutes away.

Is there a minimum size for a group booking?

Group bookings apply from 10 or more rooms.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

Yes, The Beverly Hills Hotel is pet-friendly. We permit up to two fully house-trained dogs (max 20kg/45lbs) for a $150 fee per stay, with a special welcome treat and dog bed provided. No fee applies to service dogs. We are unable to permit cats in the hotel.

What is the parking fee?

Parking is $70 per day.

What facilities does the hotel offer?

We have The Beverly Hills Hotel Spa, a 24-hour gym, family-friendly pool, the legendary Polo Lounge, Landmark Fountain Coffee Room and several other restaurants.

Can spa guests use the pool?

The Beverly Hills Hotel pool is reserved for hotel guests only.

What's close to the hotel?

We're a 5-minute drive from Rodeo Drive and Sunset Boulevard, and within easy reach of LA's business and entertainment centre and Century City.

Is the hotel well placed for shopping at Rodeo Drive?

Absolutely. Rodeo Drive is just a 5-minute drive away.

Is the hotel near the beach?

Some of the area's best beaches are within a 30-45 minute drive.

What makes The Beverly Hills Hotel distinctive?

For over a century, the 'Pink Palace' has been Hollywood's ultimate beacon of glamour, famous for its Polo Lounge, pool and spacious bungalows.
