Relax in Luxury Hotel Suites and Rooms in Abu Dhabi
Suites & Rooms
Accommodations at this hotel stretch beyond traditional expectations to include a collection of luxury hotel suites, rooms, residences, and villas in Abu Dhabi. Spacious layouts, outdoor spaces, and modern interiors are complemented by views of the beach, gardens, and Grand Mosque.
Redevelopment Announcement
A transformative redevelopment is underway with the deconstruction of Venetian Village, making space for a new collection of ultra-luxurious villas. While early construction may cause some disturbance, measures are in place to reduce impact. All resort facilities remain fully operational.

A Pet-Friendly Stay for You and Your Companion
At The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, we are pleased to welcome pets as part of your stay. Our pet-friendly signature villa accommodation inclusive of the Garden Suite, Garden House, Plunge Pool Villa, the Rabdan Villas are designed to ensure a comfortable experience for both guests and their furry companions.
Pets are only allowed in designated outdoor spaces and approved pet-friendly areas within the hotel. For the comfort of all guests, pets are not permitted in indoor public areas, restaurants, lounges, pool areas, or any location where food and beverages are served.
