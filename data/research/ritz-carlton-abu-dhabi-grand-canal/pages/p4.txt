Our Own Vertical Hydroponic Farm
The Vertical Farm is the latest addition to the Food & Beverage operations. Expecting to yield around 10 up to 15 kilograms of crops each day, the produce will be used in our restaurants and bars. It provides the freshest, most nutritious produce possible while reducing our carbon footprint by eliminating the need to transport produce from other locations. Moreover, our Vertical Farm does not use any pesticides, herbicides, or fungicides, making it a safe and healthy option for our guests.
