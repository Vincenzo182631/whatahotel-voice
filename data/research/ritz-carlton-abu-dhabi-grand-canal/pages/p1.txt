Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal
Welcome to a luxury resort in Abu Dhabi
Set along a tranquil waterfront, with uninterrupted views of the iconic Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal is a 5-star luxury hotel where timeless elegance meets serene resort living.Framed by canals, gardens, and a private beachfront, this distinctive resort hotel in Abu Dhabi offers refined hotel rooms and suites, along with private villas featuring secluded terraces and plunge pools — each designed for space, privacy, and quiet moments.Dining unfolds across eight venues, including three signature experiences, placing you among some of the finest restaurants in Abu Dhabi.Ideally positioned as a beach hotel in Abu Dhabi near the airport, the resort offers both convenience and escape — a place to reconnect, restore, and experience a true luxury resort in Abu Dhabi.

Green Initiatives
The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal, has implemented multiple initiatives across the property. Some of these initiatives speak to a unified drive that is guided by our Environmental Responsibility Strategy—as key projects—are positioned to increase our portfolio offering. We are presently making strides with the following key projects which lead our sustainability portfolio.

The Glass Bottle Project
The Glass Bottle Project implements our own in-house re-bottling system, with the aim to reduce plastic wastage and save 850, 000 plastic bottles from being thrown away, annually.
Save Our Amenities
Save Our Amenities is a new refillable bathroom amenity program to also reduce our carbon footprint, and to promote recyclables.
No to Styrofoam
No to Styrofoam is a seasoned campaign where our hotel does not allow the material for use or onsite of the property. By eliminating Styrofoam, we further support the eco-positive movement, tremendously.
The Vertical Farm
The Vertical Farm is a recent initiative that has been launched as of April 2023. It is an addition to our Food & Beverage operations that provides us with the freshest, most nutritious produce possible while reducing our carbon footprint by eliminating the need to transport produce from other locations. Moreover, our Vertical Farm does not use any pesticides, herbicides, or fungicides, making it a safe and healthy option for our guests.
'Gloves Come On!’ Initiative
The ‘Gloves Come On!’ initiative has a bold goal to save 1.4 million pairs of gloves annually—equal to 4 tons of plastic waste—with a glove that is washable over a period of months before being recycled back into the production process of where it originated.
Green Key Hotel
Certified Green Key Hotel! A prestigious recognition of our commitment to sustainable practices. From water conservation and energy efficiency to responsible waste management and local sourcing, we strive to minimize our environmental impact while exceeding your expectations for luxury.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal is Zayed International Airport (AUH). AUH is located approximately 24.6 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites.
