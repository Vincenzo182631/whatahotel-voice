Experiences
In Abu Dhabi, the concept of having it all is a tangible reality. Here, you can spend the day viewing masterpieces at the Louvre Abu Dhabi or lounging by the resort’s glittering, 17,222-sq-ft pool. You can pick up handcrafted textiles and jewelry, or update your wardrobe with luxury brands. You can examine the craftsmanship of the nearby Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque or treat your children to the creative projects of the Ritz Kids Program. From renowned attractions to on-property features, our hotel near Abu Dhabi National Exhibition Center is at the heart of it all.

Activities
Padel Courts
The recreational facilities of the resort have been enhanced with the introduction of five world-class padel courts. Enjoy a game with your friends or take advantage of a private coaching session as the state-of-the-art facility provides a certified professional academy by BELA-Fernando Belasteguin.
After an energetic match, relax at Dolce, the sports café, with healthy beverages and bites.
Football Pitch
The FIFA-standard field is fully covered by professional floodlighting, allowing for early morning and evening training sessions and matches. A separate seven-a-side pitch and goalkeeper practice extend the training opportunities for international and local teams. The superbly equipped sports campus offers the flexibility to accommodate other sports.
Pool and Beach Day Pass
Relax at one of the largest outdoor pools in Abu Dhabi and enjoy light fare from Al Fresco, our pool restaurant. Weekdays: AED 300 with AED 150 in F&B credit. Weekends: AED 400 with AED 200 in F&B credit. AED 50 per Child 6 to 11 Years Old. Pool and Beach Day Pass
Hotel Membership
Access to the hotel’s leisure facilities, including a Wellness Center, jogging tracks, and the 1,600m2 outdoor pool.
Wellness Center
Elevate your fitness routine in the luxurious surroundings of The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal's state-of-the-art Wellness Center. Open 24 hours a day, it is equipped with the latest in cardio and strength-training gear, including treadmills, elliptical, stationary bikes, and free weights. The spacious center is thoughtfully designed to accommodate a range of workout preferences.

Discover Abu Dhabi’s Wealth of Attractions
Located adjacent to the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and just 20 minutes from the Abu Dhabi International Airport, the hotel offers guests exceptional access to the city. Discover the love of Art at the Louve Abu Dhabi or visit the thrilling Ferrari World. The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal offers a gateway to Abu Dhabi’s legendary decadence and cultural surroundings. Explore it all from this five-star hotel, from shopping to desert safaris.

Ritz Kids Programs
With a compelling repertoire of creative activities, Ritz Kids engages the imaginations of the resort’s youngest guests. Families can select full- and half-day programming that emphasizes the natural wonders and cultural traditions of Abu Dhabi through arts-and-crafts projects, playtime, and more. For the enjoyment of our younger guests, and the convenience of their parents, the Ritz Kids Club is available to children between the ages of 4 and 12. The Ritz Kids Club offers an extensive program of supervised activities, both indoors and outdoors, and is a great opportunity for our young guests to intermingle with kids of their own age group. To ensure a safe and healthy environment at the hotel and to prevent inconvenience, we kindly ask our guests to abstain from using bicycles, tricycles, scooters, motor boards (hoverboards), Segways, and rollerblades on the hotel grounds and in the parking areas.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, Grand Canal offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
