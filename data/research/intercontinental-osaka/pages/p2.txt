# InterContinental OsakaRooms & suites

Our luxuriously appointed Classic room has been designed with a blend of contemporary elegance and elements from Japanese culture. The comfortable king bed is adorned with high thread count bed linens, whilst the spacious marble bathroom comes with separate bath and luxury rain shower experience. The room also features a walk-in wardrobe and ergonomic workdesk for your convenience.
