# InterContinental OsakaDining

From celebrated Specialty Restaurant Pierre, to daily dining at NOKA Roast & Grill, mouthwatering dining options are available throughout the day and night. The lounge 3-60 and adee Lounge & Bar a selection of beverages from aperitifs to nightcaps.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

### Pierre Restaurant

Recipient of a one-star rating in The Michelin Guide 2026, specialty restaurant Pierre offers one of the finest dining experiences in Osaka. Seasonal dishes are prepared with the freshest locally sourced produce using refined French cooking styles, and the restaurant boasts a selection of exceptional wines and Champagne.

Learn more

### NOKA Roast and Grill

Our signature restaurant NOKA Roast & Grill offers a bountiful daily buffet breakfast each morning and a salad and appetizer buffet with a la carte options for lunch and dinner, all featuring trademark roasted and grilled dishes prepared in an open kitchen.

Learn more

### adee Lounge and Bar

Indulge in an extensive array of gins and other spirits from around the world, and a delightful selection of original cocktails made with seasonal fruit, backdropped by a spectacular view of Osaka’s night-time cityscape. Along with fabulous drinks and light meals, enjoy live music performances every Friday and Saturday evening.

Learn more

### 3-60

3-60 is the perfect lounge venue for a friendly gathering, an after-work drink, business meeting, or our renowned afternoon tea featuring our own TWG Tea blend. 3-60 affords spectacular views amidst a setting adorned with fine artwork.

Learn more

### Stressed

An oasis far from the hustle and bustle of daily life, enjoy a selection of sweets bursting with novelty and playfulness in a quiet, tranquil space filled with light and greenery. Original InterContinental Osaka gift items on display provide a perfect memento of your stay.

Learn more
