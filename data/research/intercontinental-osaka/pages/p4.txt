### Club InterContinental

The exclusive Club InterContinental provides personalised hospitality from our dedicated concierge to ensure a relaxing stay. The Club InterContinental Lounge, located on the 28th floor, offers breakfast, afternoon tea and evening cocktails and canapes.

Learn more

### Fitness Gym

Our dedicated Spa and Fitness floor features a full fitness gym open 24/7 and complete with free weights, cardio exercise machines, and a private exercise room and stretching area. Training attire is available free of charge.
