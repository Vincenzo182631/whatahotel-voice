### Club InterContinental

The exclusive Club InterContinental provides personalised hospitality from our dedicated concierge to ensure a relaxing stay. The Club InterContinental Lounge, located on the 28th floor, offers breakfast, afternoon tea and evening cocktails and canapes.

Learn more

### Pierre Restaurant

Recipient of a one-star rating in The Michelin Guide 2026, specialty restaurant Pierre offers one of the finest dining experiences in Osaka. Seasonal dishes are prepared with the freshest locally sourced produce using refined French cooking styles, and the restaurant boasts a selection of exceptional wines and Champagne.

Learn more

### NOKA Roast and Grill

Our signature restaurant NOKA Roast & Grill offers a bountiful daily buffet breakfast each morning and a salad and appetizer buffet with a la carte options for lunch and dinner, all featuring trademark roasted and grilled dishes prepared in an open kitchen.

Learn more

### Residences

Our elegant one, two and three bedroom Residences offer the epitome of sophistication, comfort and convenience. Our multi room Residences include a fully equipped kitchen with microwave, espresso machine, refrigerator, freezer and dishwasher.

Learn more

### Pool

Our swimming pool is a beautiful 20-meter indoor lap pool with accompanying indoor jet bath and is available free of charge to all hotel guests.

## FAQs for InterContinental Osaka

Does InterContinental Osaka have a pool?

InterContinental Osaka has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

Our swimming pool is a beautiful 20 meter indoor lap pool with accompanying indoor jet bath and is available free of charge to all hotel guests.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Osaka?

We have 59 parking spaces, available at a fee of 6,000 yen per night. Valet parking is available with an additional cost of 2,000 yen.

Parking fee: 6000 JPY per day

In and out privileges

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Osaka?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Osaka.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Osaka for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Osaka have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Osaka has 5 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Osaka?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Osaka.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Osaka?

Check-in at InterContinental Osaka is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Osaka offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Osaka offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Osaka from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Osaka is conveniently located near Kansai International Airport (KIX), approximately 31.81 miles away (51.2 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Osaka?

InterContinental Osaka is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Osaka Castle - 3.85 miles away (6.2 kilometers)
- HEP Five Ferris Wheel - 0.5 miles away (0.8 kilometers)
- National Bunraku Theatre - 3.42 miles away (5.5 kilometers)
- Sumiyoshi Shrine - 6.84 miles away (11 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Osaka puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
