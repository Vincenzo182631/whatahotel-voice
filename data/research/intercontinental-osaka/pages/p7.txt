### An Excellent Stay at the InterContinental Osaka

17 hours ago

A wonderful stay at the InterContinental Osaka, five days (4+1, room 2421), in September, I enjoyed my time at this hotel.
The location is excellent, with easy access to train and subway. My room was spacious, spotlessly clean, and the air conditioning worked perfectly — something that is not always a given in Osaka!
What truly stood out, however, was the quality of the staff, professional, welcoming, and attentive. I would particularly like to thank Ayako Nishinaka , who went above and beyond to help me resolve a challenging IT issue. She took the time, found a solution, showing patience, commitment, and a real willingness to help. I think that this level of personal attention deserves special recognition.
Overall, an excellent five-star experience. My only minor disappointment was the food selection in the Club Lounge, which I felt could be improved to be more in line with the high standards of the hotel.
I have already booked this hotel for my next trip, and would happily recommend the InterContinental Osaka.

11 days ago

We had the best experience at Intercontinental Osaka. We stayed for 5 nights, and two nights in my mother unfortunately suffered from a medical emergency at about 8 PM. The hotel sent multiple personnel to check on my mother, and they helped us in calling for an ambulance. My Japanese is limited to basic conversation, but the housekeeping manager, Mr. Doi shinnosuke was with us the entire ride to the hospital to help us translate. He stayed with us while the EMS looked for an available hospital, and he stayed with us at the emergency room for almost three hours. He also went above and beyond and had gotten us water for the visit to the hospital. He called a tab for us, and arranged proper arrangement for our hotel room to accommodate my mother’s medical requirements. The service we received from Mr. Doi and his team was top notch, and dare I say, probably the best one could possibly receive from a hotel. I cannot express my gratitude for this hotel and its staff. They truly turned around what could’ve been a disaster and an earlier end to our vacation, to a comfortable stay which allowed us to continue our vacation.
If anyone is looking for a hotel in Osaka, I would recommend staying with intercontinental Osaka. If an opportunity presents itself in the future and we happen to be back in Osaka again, I would definitely stay at this hotel again.

### The Pinnacle of Service

15 days ago

Our stay at the InterContinental Osaka was truly exceptional and easily one of the best hotel experiences we have ever had.

The hotel itself is beautiful, elegant, peaceful, and incredibly well maintained. We stayed in a one-bedroom residence, and it was absolutely perfect for a longer stay. The room was spacious, comfortable, thoughtfully designed, and genuinely felt like a home away from home. The location in Umeda is also fantastic and made exploring Osaka incredibly convenient.

What made this stay truly unforgettable, though, was the staff.

From the very beginning, the level of hospitality was outstanding. Over the course of our stay, the team remembered us, learned our preferences, greeted us warmly each day, and took the time to have genuine conversations with us. We never felt like simply another room number or another hotel guest. We felt truly welcomed and cared for.

Breakfast became one of our favorite parts of every day, not only because the food and service were excellent, but because of the wonderful people working there. As our stay continued, we found ourselves getting to know several members of the team, and those interactions became some of our favorite memories from our entire trip to Japan.

Atsuko, in particular, made an extraordinary impression on us. Her kindness, warmth, sincerity, and genuine care went far beyond anything we could have expected. We are so grateful that staying at this hotel gave us the opportunity to meet her and form what we hope will be a lasting friendship. She is an incredible reflection of what true Japanese hospitality can be.

We would also especially like to recognize Yogesh from the breakfast team. His warmth, attentiveness, and friendly presence helped make our mornings something we genuinely looked forward to. The entire breakfast team was wonderful, and Yogesh was a memorable part of that experience.

Miho at the concierge desk also deserves special recognition. She was incredibly kind, helpful, and professional throughout our stay. Whether assisting us with plans, answering questions, or simply taking the time to speak with us, she made us feel genuinely cared for. Her warmth and personal attention added so much to our experience.

We also greatly appreciated how naturally welcoming the entire staff became throughout our stay. Whether it was a conversation over breakfast, a friendly greeting, a handwritten message on a takeaway coffee cup, or simply someone remembering a small detail from a previous conversation, there were so many thoughtful touches that made us feel incredibly special.

There is a difference between receiving excellent service and experiencing genuine hospitality, and the InterContinental Osaka gave us the latter. The professionalism is exactly what you would expect from a luxury hotel, but the warmth, sincerity, and personal connection are what set this property apart.

By the end of our stay, we were genuinely sad to leave. Osaka already holds a very special place in our hearts, and this hotel and its wonderful team are a huge part of the reason why.

We will be returning to Osaka in about five months, and there is absolutely no question where we will be staying. The InterContinental Osaka has become our home in Osaka.

Thank you to the entire team, and especially Atsuko, Miho, and Yogesh, for giving us an experience we will remember for a very long time. We cannot recommend this hotel highly enough.

An easy 5/5 — and if we could give more than five stars, we would. ️
