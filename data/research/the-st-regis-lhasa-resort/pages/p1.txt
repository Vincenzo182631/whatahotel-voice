# Welcome to The St. Regis Lhasa Resort

## Unlock a world-class experience at our resort hotel in Lhasa

Set amid the timeless splendor of the ancient Barkhor area, The St. Regis Lhasa Resort offers luxury accommodation in a legendary travel destination. Discover a rejuvenating respite overlooking the wondrous Xizang landscape and magnificent Potala Palace. Embrace effortless relaxation in stylishly appointed luxury hotel rooms and suites in Lhasa, Xizang, with contemporary essentials, luxury amenities, stunning views and motifs inspired by the nearby Sera Monastery. Ignite your senses with delectable Chinese cuisine and flavorful international fare in our restaurants. Explore attractions including Jokhang Temple, Barkhor Street and the legendary Potala Palace. Celebrate and succeed in elegant event space, masterfully designed for flawless meetings, elegant weddings and memorable social engagements. Punctuate a full day of work or sightseeing with a rejuvenating treatment at our resort spa. Situated at the legendary "Roof of the World" in Lhasa, China, our hotel is a scenic haven for your visit to Xizang.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Lhasa Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Lhasa Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Lhasa Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Lhasa Resort.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Lhasa Resort?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Lhasa Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Lhasa Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Lhasa Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Lhasa Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Lhasa Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Lhasa Resort is Lhasa Gonggar Airport (LXA). LXA is located approximately 60.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Lhasa Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Lhasa Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
