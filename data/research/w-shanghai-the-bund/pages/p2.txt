### Extra Bedding Policy

Rollaway beds and baby cots are subject to availability. Rollaway beds are included in extra adult charge or available for a fee separately. Baby cots are complimentary. Please submit request for extra bedding at the time of reservation.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Shanghai - The Bund?

The largest occupancy room at W Shanghai - The Bund is the Extreme WOW Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund?

Some rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Shanghai - The Bund have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Shanghai - The Bund have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
