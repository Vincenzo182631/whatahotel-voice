# Redefine Authentic Experiences In Shanghai

## Experiences

Where the city’s first cinema made its debut, W Shanghai—The Bund now steals the skyline show by exuding mesmerizing haipai, the uniquely local fusion of old with the new, the east with the west, reconfiguring colonial glamour into contemporary inspiration. Go with the flow in this city of constant motion – cruise along the iconic Huangpu River at sunset, or swept off your feet in a Shanghainese whirlwind...See More of marvels, from the centenarian Jade Buddha Temple to the post-modern Oriental Pearl TV Tower. Find time to slow down and revel in tales of Shanghai’s ethnic tapestries and dignified legacies – preferably while serenaded by the legendary Peace Hotel jazz band evoking Golden Age nostalgia.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Shanghai - The Bund have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Shanghai - The Bund offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Shanghai - The Bund have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Shanghai - The Bund has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

WET® Indoor (Food & Beverage not permitted) (Indoor)WET® Outdoor ( Closed from Nov 1, 2026) (Outdoor)

Does W Shanghai - The Bund have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY® Spa is located on-site at W Shanghai - The Bund. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Shanghai - The Bund?

The Stage, Shanghai Ocean Aquarium, Shanghai New International Expo Center, Oriental Pearl TV Tower, Shanghai Circus World and more are nearby W Shanghai - The Bund.
