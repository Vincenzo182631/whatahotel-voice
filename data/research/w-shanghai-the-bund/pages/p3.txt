## Dining

W Shanghai - The Bund is your choice for dining in style! Whether for casual get-togethers or business entertaining, our five restaurants and bars deliver a curated, diverse dining experience tailored to your preferences. The Kitchen Table weaves the modern character of a New York–style bistro bringing together global flavors while celebrating the best from the Josper oven; YEN Chinese restaurant,...See More serves contemporary local specialties with bold character; Liquid provides an elegant setting for relaxed drinks and sweeping views of the Pudong skyline; The outdoor WET® Bar is a vibrant spot for social gatherings; By day, the signature WOOBAR® is a lively social hub, by night, it transforms into a sophisticated bar celebrating the best in cocktail creations. Whether you seek quiet solitude, time with friends, or a taste of Shanghai’s dynamic social scene, W Shanghai – The Bund offers refined ambiance and unmistakable character.See Less
