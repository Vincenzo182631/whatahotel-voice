# Welcome to W Shanghai - The Bund

## W SHANGHAI - THE BUND

W Shanghai - The Bund is a luxury hotel with a river view located on the up-and-coming North Bund in glamorous Shanghai. Wake up in one of our 374 supremely chic hotel rooms and suites that fuse sophisticated Mandarin designs with playful twists. Innovative decor and 5,800 sqm of event space ensure your meetings are anything but ordinary with an iconic The Bund view. Taste the flavors at our five Shanghai...See More restaurants and bars, including The Kitchen Table, a modern New York City-style bistro; YEN celebrates contemporary Jiangnan and Cantonese cuisine mixed in with some Shanghainese favorites; LIQUID, a Golden Age-inspired cocktail bar; WOOBAR®, a playground that spotlights world-renowned DJs and crafted concoctions on The Bund and WET® Bar, a perfect place for cocktails, light fare and finger food. Unwind in spectacular style at our FIT gym, then pamper yourself at AWAY® Spa. Take a swim in our hotel's outdoor and indoor WET® pools with a playful WET® Deck that provides an iconic skyline view of Shanghai.See Less

## Electrify the Sky

Marvel at The Bund’s architectural mix, then revel in our alluring Shanghai dream where 1920s glam and futuristic flash play out. Where the city’s cinema made its debut, our hotel, with a captivating The Bund view in Shanghai, now steals the skyline show by exuding mesmerizing haipai, the local fusion of old with the new, the East with the West, reconfiguring glamour into contemporary inspiration.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Shanghai - The Bund?

The check-in time at W Shanghai - The Bund is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Shanghai - The Bund allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Shanghai - The Bund. with a maximum weight of 10.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W Shanghai - The Bund?

On-site self parking is available at W Shanghai - The Bund. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Shanghai - The Bund have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Shanghai - The Bund has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Shanghai - The Bund booking.

What is the closest airport near W Shanghai - The Bund?

The closest airport near W Shanghai - The Bund is Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA). SHA is located approximately 19.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Shanghai - The Bund have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Shanghai - The Bund has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
