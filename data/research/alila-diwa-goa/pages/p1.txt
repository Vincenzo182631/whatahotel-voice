## An Immersive Journey Through Goa’s South

Alila Diwa Goa is both a serene sanctuary and a starting point for deep discovery, designed to offer the space to unwind and inspire meaningful connection to the region’s lush surroundings and vibrant community. Embark through a land of ancient forts, colorful villas and rich traditions rooted in the colonial past, all amidst idyllic swaying palms, white sands and sparkling waters.

## Alila Moments

Shaped by land and craft, by culture and care, Alila Moments reveal a different way of seeing. These personalized experiences offered at our Alila properties draw you closer to the world around you, and your place within it. These are our Moments, crafted just for you.

Explore Alila Moments

The Spice Studio Session: Cooking with Chef

Let the aromas of Goa’s coast lead you to an interactive culinary session with the Chef at Spice Studio Restaurant. Begin with an introduction to locally sourced ingredients—fragrant spices, fresh seafood, and regional produce.  As you don your apron, learn traditional techniques rooted in both Hindu and Portuguese traditions.

As you’re perfecting in curries, the class is equal parts hands-on and heartwarming. End the experience by gathering around the table for a communal meal and stories shared by the chef himself.

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Recommended Dress:** Casual attire

**Price**: INR 4000/- Plus tax per couple, INR 2000/- Plus tax per person

**Inquire**

**Additional Details:** The Moment is done at our specialty Coastal Cuisine Restaurant, guided by the Chef, the guest is only required to choose the menu that will be prepared at the class and served to the guest at the table of their choice.

The Sounds of Healing

Tap into a form of relaxation you never knew possible through an immersive sound bath that gently vibrates your eternal waters and recalibrates your body's internal systems.

**Duration**: 60 minutes

**Recommended Preparation & Attire**: For your comfort, please arrive on an empty stomach or at least two hours after a meal. We suggest wearing loose, comfortable clothing to allow ease of movement and relaxation.

**Price**: INR 2500/- Plus tax per person – Minimum 2 persons to book.

**Inquire**

**Additional Details**:  Sound Therapy is a holistic wellness experience designed to support relaxation and well-being. It is not a substitute for medical or psychological treatment. Guests with health concerns are advised to consult their physician before booking.

Meet the Poder—The Art of Goan Breadmaking

Before day breaks fully, follow a winding village path to a neighborhood bakery where Goa’s oldest ritual begins breadmaking. The village’s beloved Poder's Family welcomes you into their world of dough and discipline. With flour-dusted hands, you’ll knead and shape your own pão as they share stories of Portuguese influence, community routines, and why bread remains at the center of Goan life. As your loaf bakes in the clay oven, sip chai and snack on local savory treats. It’s a moment that blends storytelling, scent, and skill—and leaves you carrying both bread and memories back to the resort.

**Duration**: 1 hour

**Recommended Dress:** Casual attire

**Price:** INR 600/- Plus tax per person

**Inquire**

Distilled by the Land—The Story of Feni

From fruit to fermentation, uncover the origins and identity of Goa’s most iconic spirit. Tour a working distillery, meet the maker and taste feni like a local—with stories in every sip.

**Duration:** 2 hours

**Recommended Dress**: Cool Casuals (you will wade in the shallow water)

**Price**: INR 2500/- Plus tax per person; transportation to the venue is an additional cost

**Inquire**

## Discover Alila Diwa Goa

Diwa Pool

For The Diwa Club Only - Designed as an enclave of​ exclusivity, the Diwa Club boasts its pool with an open-air ‘jetted tub’.​

Size of the pool: Length 34.29 m; Width 15.06 m; Depth 1.20 m.​

7 am to 8 pm​

Taste of Goa

Discover authentic preparations of southwest coastal cuisine, as well as contemporary plates with influences from across the globe. Celebrate the deep flavors and aromas of the kitchens of Kerala, Karnataka and Goa at Spice Studio, built around a majestic Banyan tree. Bistro, overlooking the pool at Diwa Club, draws on the cuisines of the Mediterranean and Middle East.

Coastal Wonder

Goa offers a one-of-a-kind mix of unspoiled nature and a commingling of cultures. Goa’s lanes still hark back to its colonial past, evident in the exquisite architecture, and in the soulful strains of Portuguese fado music that still carry on the sea breeze. Its white sand beaches are allures all their own and include the seemingly endless Gonsua Beach, just 500 meters away from the resort and easily accessible, thanks to our complimentary, daily beach shuttle service.

Swept in Greenery

High-pitched roofs, wide verandas and tranquil courtyards set the scene for a restorative stay. Contemporary Goan architecture combines with local materials, including red laterite stone and teak, for some of the largest rooms in Goa, all of which feature a balcony for taking in views of the pool, lush gardens or paddy fields.

Alila Mini-Theatre

Sit back, relax, and enjoy a movie at the Alila​ Mini-Theatre, where daily screenings offer a delightful escape into the​ world of cinema.​

Play Alila & Teen Zone

Find your family’s perfect balance between time spent together and quiet time as a couple, knowing your kids are having fun and in trusted hands.

**Play Alila** Alila Diwa Goa’s dedicated kids’ club, offers a range of activities and curated experiences, ideal for kids 12 and under. Play Alila also offers babysitting services, which must be booked in advance and confirmed based on availability.

**Teen Zone**

Adjacent to Play Alila is the Teen Zone, a chill-out space for teenagers and their families to relax over games of table tennis, foosball, carrom, chess, and scrabble.​

The Beach Experience

Take a leisurely walk, soak in the mesmerizing sunsets, relax your feet in the cool waters or enjoy water sports when you’re in the mood for some adventure at Gonsua Beach. Known for its clean and gorgeous white sands, Gonsua Beach is only 500 meters from the resort. We operate a free beach shuttle to and from the beach for all our guests daily, 7 a.m.–7 p.m.

Infinity Pool

At the heart of the resort is an endless infinity pool that​ extends out into the paddy terraces. The infinity pool is 35.30 m long,​ 15.30 m wide and 1.65 m deep.

7 am to 8 pm​
