### A beautiful & luxurious hotel

The Alila Diwa Goa is a lovely hotel… probably the nicest we’ve stayed in. The rooms are huge with luxurious & comfortable bed. The hotel has lovely grounds, huge swimming pool & really fabulous & attentive staff. They can’t do enough for you.

The only downside isn’t the Alila itself, it’s the surrounding area… there’s not a lot to see or do nearby. You need to get a taxi to anywhere. We stayed for 2 weeks & went out most evenings but the decent restaurants nearby only number a few & all Indian cuisine… which provided great food but it would have been nice to have some variety.

So we would advise staying here BUT maybe at the end of an extended holiday seeing other parts of Goa. Make this the luxurious & relaxing end to a holiday.
