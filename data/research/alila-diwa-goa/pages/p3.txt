## Spice Studio

Embark on a coastal odyssey inspired by the culinary traditions of Kerala, Karnataka and Goa at the homestyle coastal restaurant. Built around a majestic banyan tree, the earthy ambience of Spice Studio exudes an aura of authenticity and elegance. The menu is replete with vegetarian and non-vegetarian coastal delicacies, inspired by age-old preparation methods.
Chef Recommendation: Kundapura Kori kari, Muringakai Thenga Pal Curry, Kuri Kalu Bafat.

## Edge – Tropical Bar & Kitchen

Escape into the enriching ‘Susegaad’ luxury at the family-friendly tropical paradise with unforgettable views of the Infinity Pool and lush paddy fields. Edge exudes a vibrant yet relaxed ambiance with a live bar and a show kitchen. An ideal venue for lunch by the pool, the menu is designed for a comfortable and fun experience. It seamlessly transitions into a lovely sundowner spot with some tropical and enchanting drinks with some groovy edgy music for company.

## Courtyard Bar

Courtyard Bar is a speakeasy inspired retro-themed evening cocktail bar overlooking the courtyard with both indoor and outdoor seating. The menu offers hyperlocal drinks, cocktails and mocktails, crafted from spirits and mixers infused in-house with local spices and build-your-own gin and tonics. Conjuring the true flavors of fruits and botanicals, our unique cocktails and timeless classics make it perfect for an evening aperitif. There are also delicious virgin cocktails like the Dadima Cooler made with fresh mint, lime juice, sprite and pomegranate syrup.
Tip: The Manhattan with Kokum-infused Martini Rosso & Cherrywood Smoke is a crowd favorite.

## VIVO

Overlooking the pool and lawns, Vivo is an all-day diner that offers a relaxed ambience with both indoor and shaded open-air seating. The menu is a delicious mix of Oriental, Indian and Continental favorites with plenty of light, healthy choices to suit every palate, as well as a 24/7 a la carte menu, an extenive buffet breakfast for all the guests and an elaborate Sunday Brunch.
Vivo La Brunch, is the best Sunday Brunch in Goa with an access to the infinity pool, unlimited food and drinks, interactive food stations, live music, fun games and activities and much more.
