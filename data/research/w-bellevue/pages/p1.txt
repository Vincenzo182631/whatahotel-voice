# Welcome to W Bellevue

## Mix it up at our Bellevue, WA, hotel

Discover a magnetic remix of stunning nature, premier shopping, and bold flavors in the heart of downtown Bellevue, WA. Our luxury Bellevue, Washington hotel is located steps from local corporate headquarters and The Bellevue Collection shopping center, with Downtown Seattle, Woodinville wine country, top golf courses and the Pacific Northwest mountains and lakes in short driving distance. Expect striking design and curated interiors, blending contemporary chic with local artistry at our hotel in Bellevue. Showcasing sparkling city and lake views, our luxurious rooms and suites are designed with modern travelers in mind. Sip hand-craft cocktails at Living Room Bar or speakeasy, Civility & Unrest and savor farm-to-table dining at The Lakehouse, where fresh local ingredients shine. Recharge your body and mind at the fully equipped FIT fitness studio with Peloton bikes, boxing bags, stretching bar and everything in-between.

Experience luxury with a carefully curated stay

From popping bubbly to personalized in-room celebrations, guests can dive into a delightful experience infused with the lively spirit of Bellevue, Washington, available only at W Bellevue!

## P.A.W. - Pets Are Welcome

The pet-friendly W Bellevue welcomes you and your four-legged friend. Treat your pet to a restful nap on the W Pet Bed before enjoying a walk in one of the beautiful dog-friendly parks near our Bellevue hotel.

Learn More

W Bellevue Hotel is a pet friendly hotel. In the interest of other hotel guests, the customer agrees to prevent noise or any other disturbance by their pet. While we know our fur babies need a good nap, pets are not allowed to be left unattended at any time. The customer agrees to pay a one-time, non-refundable $100 fee that will be added to the hotel bill. Additional fees may apply if extra cleaning is needed.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Bellevue?

The check-in time at W Bellevue is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Bellevue allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Bellevue. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 60.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at W Bellevue?

On-site parking is available at W Bellevue. Self parking is available for $37.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $47.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Bellevue have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Bellevue has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Bellevue booking.

What is the closest airport near W Bellevue?

The closest airport near W Bellevue is Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA). SEA is located approximately 17.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Bellevue have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Bellevue has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
