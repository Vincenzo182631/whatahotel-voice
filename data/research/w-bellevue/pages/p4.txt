### Customized Workouts

If you prefer the inspiration of a customized workout, our W Insider can arrange for a personal trainer to come to you. From a private HIIT, yoga or barre class, we have partnerships with the top trainers in town. Just email winsider.bellevue@whotels.com 48 hours prior to your stay to arrange.

## FUEL Your Workout

Within a few miles of W Bellevue, you can explore other fitness-focused activities. Whether you’re looking for group fitness classes, a hike through the PNW wilderness, or a paddle board session on the lake, there are many options to keep you on your routine while on the go: TruFusion, OTF, REI Boathouse, and Rattlesnake Ledge.

## FUEL Your Body

Get the energy to look good, feel good, go longer, and stay later by fueling your body from the inside out at our downtown Bellevue hotel. Whether you’re looking for something to grab on the go or sit down and take your time, savor some healthy bites or sip a craft cocktail in the Living Room Bar, there are many healthy options within steps of W Bellevue: In-Room Dining, The Lakehouse, Lincoln Square Food Hall, and Pressed.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Bellevue have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Bellevue offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Treadmills, elliptical machines, and free weights, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Bellevue?

Chateau Ste Michelle, Enatai Beach Park, Snoqualmie Falls, Lumen Field (Home of the Seattle Seahawks & Seattle Sounders), T-Mobile Field (Home of Seattle Mariners Baseball) and more are nearby W Bellevue.
