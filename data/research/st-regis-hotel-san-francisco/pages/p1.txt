# Welcome to The St. Regis San Francisco

## Five-star hotel in the San Francisco Bay area

Welcome to The St. Regis San Francisco, a Forbes Five-Star sanctuary in the heart of SoMa. Here, timeless sophistication meets modern design, creating an experience where every detail is curated for distinction. From newly reimagined accommodations to anticipatory service, every element reflects a legacy of excellence. Discover a destination where culture, culinary artistry, and bespoke moments await.

### Suites & Rooms

### The Spa at The St. Regis San Francisco

The Spa at The St. Regis San Francisco offers a thoughtful approach to modern wellbeing. Spanning 9,000 square feet across two levels, the spa is a serene enclave featuring cedar saunas, eucalyptus steam rooms, a Himalayan Salt Room, and intimate relaxation lounges, complemented by access to the hotel’s indoor infinity pool. A curated collection of facials, massages, and body treatments balances refined indulgence with restorative intention. Each experience is delivered with the anticipatory care that defines St. Regis, inviting moments of restoration that feel seamless, personal, and unhurried.

### The Best Address

Modern sophistication and rich heritage decorate every St. Regis address. The St. Regis San Francisco offers an incomparable location for exploring the San Francisco Bay Area - from Michelin Star dining in SoMa and sporting events at Oracle Park to wine tasting in Napa Valley.

### An Artful Destination

## A Captivating San Francisco Art Collection

The discerning art collection at The St. Regis San Francisco features exquisite works by acclaimed local artists that have been carefully curated to exemplify timeless elegance and luxury. Throughout the hotel, the collection references “Old World“ charm in a contemporary “New World“ fashion.

## Unparalleled Access to Premier Museums

The St. Regis San Francisco is housed within the Williams Building - a 20th century landmark combining architectural bravura with modern refinement. The artful location is home to the Museum of the African Diaspora (MoAD) and is situated adjacent to the San Francisco Museum of Modern Art (SFMOMA), making this luxury San Francisco hotel the ideal destination for art aficionados and connoisseurs.

## Concierge Services

The Concierge at The St. Regis San Francisco is pleased to offer personalized assistance with special requests during your stay, including courier service, car rental, private car service, business center services, and dining and theatre reservations. We welcome you to contact us with your requests by phone at 415-284-4032 or by emailing sr.sfoxr.concierge@stregis.com.

## Luxury Car Service

While in residence at The St. Regis San Francisco, guests enjoy complimentary access to the luxury house car available select evenings. The courtesy car is available on a first-come, first-served basis for complimentary drop-offs within a 2-mile radius of the hotel.

## Delights Delivered

Elevate the in-room experience by sending a welcoming amenity during or in advance of an upcoming stay through the thoughtfully executed Delights Delivered Program. From a fresh bouquet of flowers to a personalized cake for a special celebration, deliver a personalized amenity to be enjoyed within the comforts of our rooms and suites. For more information, please contact the Concierge at 415-284-4032.

### 24-Hour Business Center

## Green Initiatives at The St. Regis San Francisco

With its various activations and partnerships, The St. Regis San Francisco is dedicated to ensuring a sustainable experience, from guestrooms to food and beverage initiatives.

### Awards

### San Francisco Green Business

Recognized by The City & County of San Francisco.

### California Green Lodging Program

Awarded a Participation Level certificate by the State of California Department of General Services.

### Certified Level

San Francisco Green Business Program.

Conde Nast Traveler – Triple Crown – 2026 Award

Guestroom Initiatives

- Reusing guestroom keys that are turned in at check out.
- Guests are asked to participate in our green housekeeping program to help reduce water usage. This includes not changing bed sheets or refreshing towels.
- Recycling bins in every guestroom.

Clean the World Partnership

Partners with Clean the World, a Global Hygiene Revolution to distribute recycled soap and hygiene products to children and families in countries with a high death rate due to acute respiratory infection and diarrheal diseases.

In a six-month period:

9,382 bars of soap distributed

1,844 lbs. of soap collected

1,844 lbs. of plastic collected

EcoLab Partnership

Partners with EcoLab to reduce water intensity by 15%, to reduce carbon intensity by 30%, and to reduce landfill waste by 45% by the end of 2025.

The cleaning products we use have chemicals made from plant derived natural resources, are biodegradable, have zero volatile organic compounds, and are certified by Green Seal and the U.S. Department of Agriculture Bio Preferred program."

Food and Beverage Initiatives

- Linen napkins and leather coasters are used instead of cocktail napkins.
- We offer paper or corn-plastic straws which are biodegradable and compostable.
- To go boxes are biodegradable and to go cups are made with paper products.
- Catered events use all chinaware and cloth napkins.
- Lights are turned off when meeting spaces are not in use.

Recology Partnership

- Partners with Recology to recycle and compost throughout the hotel.
- Recology implements recycling programs to recover recyclables, reduce landfill
- bound materials, return organic materials to farms as compost, and provide recycled raw materials (such as plastic or aluminum) to manufacturers.

Replate Partnership

Partners with Replate, a tech-enabled nonprofit that aims to reduce food waste and food insecurity by picking up the food surplus to donate meals to nonprofits serving communities who are experiencing food insecurity.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis San Francisco?

The check-in time at The St. Regis San Francisco is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Regis San Francisco allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis San Francisco. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 35.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $350.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis San Francisco?

On-site valet parking is available at The St. Regis San Francisco. The daily valet fee is $96.90 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis San Francisco have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis San Francisco has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis San Francisco booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis San Francisco?

The closest airport near The St. Regis San Francisco is San Francisco International Airport (SFO). SFO is located approximately 13.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis San Francisco have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis San Francisco has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
