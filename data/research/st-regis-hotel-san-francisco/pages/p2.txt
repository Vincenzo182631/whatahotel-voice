## Indulge in Relaxation in Your Luxury Suite

The St. Regis San Francisco provides an unforgettable experience and exquisite luxury in each of our spectacular San Francisco suites, ranging in size from a spacious 700 square feet to a lavish 3,200 square feet.

Details

##### Astor Suite:

These inviting corner suites are flawlessly furnished with fumed oak and limestone. Lounge on decadent furnishings and take in views of San Francisco through floor-to-ceiling windows. At over 700 square feet, no detail goes without thought. Guests may indulge in the King size bed, separate living room and rainfall showerhead.

##### Overlook Suite:

Overlook suites feature views of the Overlook Terrace and the Yerba Buena Gardens. The luxury suite living room provides lounge seating and a layered smoked glass desk. A half bathroom is located off of the living room, and the full bathroom includes extra long soaking tubs and rainfall showerheads. The luxuriously large bedroom has plush carpeting, a sumptuous king-size bed, and lounge chair and ottoman.

##### Metropolitan Suite:

Take in captivating views of San Francisco from the Metropolitan Suite, featuring marble window seats below floor-to-ceiling windows that surround the spacious living room area. On the other end of the room, gather around the dining area with seating from four to six. Elegant details, including a layered smoked glass desk and separate dressing area with three-way mirrors, add character to the already distinctive space.

##### 2-Bedroom Suite:

Create an oasis two bedroom corner suite with the Metropolitan Suite and an adjoining Deluxe Double Double. A comfortable welcoming atrium entryway provides privacy to both bedrooms.

##### St. Regis Suite:

The exclusive St. Regis Suites sit on the 19th and 20th floors and offer 1,500 square feet of refined living space. Each suite features views of the financial district's skyscrapers, the San Francisco peninsula's rolling hills, San Francisco Bay, and the distant ridgelines of the East Bay. Guests can indulge in the lavish living space with plush seating, dining table for up to six, and a layered smoked glass desk. The enormous limestone bathroom features an extra deep, extra long jetted bathtub, extended marble vanity, and extraordinary shower room with ceiling-mounted rainfall showerhead.

##### Presidential Suite:

The exclusive Presidential Suite is an exceptional example of modern luxury in San Francisco. The 3,200-square foot suite boats a living room, media room, library and office, dining room, butler's pantry, bedroom, dressing room, and one full bathroom with separate powder room. Each room features exquisite details, such as a butler's pantry wine cooler and a formal dining room that seats up to 10 people. Elegant bookshelves line the office walls, with a large desk and seating area, and the bedroom's three large windows offer views of the cityscape and Yerba Buena Gardens.

## Signature St. Regis Butler Service

St. Regis Butler Service has been a treasured hallmark of the St. Regis experience for over one hundred years. From a perfectly pressed suit ready for an important meeting, to a favorite book at hand for an evening read, to a thoughtful, last minute gift for a loved one, no request is too small or unattainable no matter what the hour of the day.

Details

**Signature St. Regis Butler Service (Available in Suites Only)**

##### St. Regis Butler Service Desk:

Guests who reserve suite accommodations at The St. Regis San Francisco have access to St. Regis Butler Service via the Butler Service Desk. Should you need something for your travels or just for your convenience, St. Regis Butler Service will be sure to have on hand or source your required item. Whatever the request, guests may dial for prompt service on their guest room phone.

##### Garment Pressing:

Once unpacked, simply let your St. Regis Butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. Two garments may be pressed complimentary per day. For all other wishes or requests, St. Regis Butler Service is pleased to assist.

##### eButler:

During your stay, whether you are inside or outside the hotel, St. Regis Butler Service is accessible at any hour via email. Simply send your request and allow the Butler Service Desk to assist.
