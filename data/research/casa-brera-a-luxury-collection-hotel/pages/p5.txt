### Teatro Alla Scala

The renowned opera house in Milan, Teatro alla Scala has lived many lives, but has always maintained its position as the world’s principal opera venue and a must-visit when the opera season debuts each year, on 7 December. Hosting familiar favorites and lesser-known works, its ‘loggione’ seating area ‘in the gods’ is considered of great cultural significance for its demanding challenge to performers.

### Antique Market

Every third Sunday of the month (bar August), Via Fiori Chiari, Via Madonnina and Via Formentini come to life as the streets of Brera are lined with stalls, where vendors sell a remarkable array of antiques. A bustling transformation that animates the neighborhood, it’s where jewelry, books and more can be marveled over.

### Grande Brera

An ambitious project that connects cultural institutions, Pinacoteca de Brera, Palazzo Citterio and Biblioteca Nazionale Braidense, Grande Brera brings together an exceptional collection of artworks. Spanning prehistoric to contemporary art, it is a culturally immersive haven that sits adjacent to the Botanical Gardens. The largest exhibition space in Milan, it is a celebration of art in all its glorious beauty.

### Art galleries

As Italy’s hotspot for art, Milan’s cosmopolitan attitude makes it the country’s undisputed artistic capital. The vast array of galleries means that there is something for every kind of culture seeker, from viewing age-old masterpieces, to exploring the thriving contemporary scene, visual arts span the length and breadth of this captivating city.
