## All-Italian Aesthetic

Patricia Urquiola takes guests on a journey through iconic Milanese creativity, where form and function have equal stakes. At Casa Brera, every detail is a nod to the city’s legacy for design and socializing, from geometric patterning and dome-shaped lamps to Cassina and Poliform furniture, complemented by custom terracotta vases. A haven for relaxation, the overall sense of indulgence extends to each en-suite bathroom, where floor-to-ceiling-streaked marble, glossy surfaces and sleek black fixtures weave a narrative between historic and modern, with unmistakable flair.

## Stylish Living

Crafted to maintain a strong bond with the fabric of the destination, each guestroom reflects the vibrancy and creativity of Brera. Intended for relaxation, accommodation combines intelligent design with integrated technology forming an elegant backdrop that inspires connection with all things Milan. Bespoke furnishings, like alcove seating, custom drinks table, and the minibar stocked with Italian treats, are a nod to the city’s appreciation for socializing, whilst the pink-streaked marbled en-suite bathroom is a haven for indulgence.

### Two Bedroom Studio

An inspired option for up to four guests traveling together, the Two Bedroom Studio is formed by connecting adjoining guestrooms to create a singular space with two stylish bedrooms, furnished with super king-size beds and twin marble-clad bathrooms. An indulgent retreat, here is where Milanese design sparks conversation and infuses the space with residential warmth and quiet luxury.

### Panoramic Two Bedroom Suite

Crafted to maximize space, light, and sophistication, the Panoramic Two Bedroom Suite reveals a coveted corner layout framing dynamic Brera views. Each detail speaks to modern Milan, from modular Poliform furniture to the bespoke glass-topped drinks bar. Two marble bathrooms, equipped with Etro products and exquisite finishes, offer an indulgent balance of style and serenity within this stylish city retreat.

### Milanese Two Bedroom Suite

An expansive terrace brings city views to al fresco moments, while bespoke furnishings and three marble-clad bathrooms, complete with Etro products, create a lavish and thoughtfully curated residence.
