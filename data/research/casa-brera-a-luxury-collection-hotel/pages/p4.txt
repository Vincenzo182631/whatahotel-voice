Inventive Cuisine

## Culinary Arts

Celebrating Milan’s passion for food, and its continued influence on local culture, Casa Brera is a magnet for those seeking authentic epicurean experiences in a distinctly sociable setting. A coveted choice for gatherings and celebrations, rooftop bar and restaurant, Etereo, offers a stylish backdrop, where exciting gastronomy plays a vital role in bringing people together for important culinary moments. Serving everything from breakfast to aperitivo and beyond, guests are taken on a journey that explores the many flavors of Milanese cuisine.

Signature Cocktails

## Ingenious Mixology

Taking an artful approach to mixology, Casa Brera is a coveted destination for meeting up over inventive cocktails. Influenced by Milan’s art, design and fashion heritage, drinks are skillfully made by talented professionals, who add inimitable theatricality and flair to every creation. At Etereo cocktails are a direct tribute to the metropolis: each drink is dedicated to a different neighborhood within the city and has been translated to offer a fully immersive destination.

Epicurean Moments

### Daily Epicurean Rituals

Quintessential Milan is showcased through daily signature moments at Etereo. Each evening at 5.00pm, guests can sample Casa Brera's custom alcohol-free version of the legendary Milanese cocktail, Negroni Sbagliato, accidentally created at the historic Bar Basso in 1972.

### Weekly Immersive Moments

Experience a taste of Milan every Wednesday evening at Etereo, where our Culinary Team is on hand to freshly prepare a pot of traditional Risotto Milanese and offer guests a taste of Casa Brera’s special made. Served at 6:30pm, this signature dish provides guests a glimpse into the rich culinary heritage of the city and is a highlight of the restaurant’s refined menu.

### The Art of Aperitivo

Aperitivo is a Milanese must-do. As a tribute to this social tradition Brera rooftop bar, Etereo, serves the classic evening ritual with an artful twist, meticulously cast from the shapes and colors that define the hotel’s interiors and the immersive gallery of artworks. Reflecting the hues and design elements, the cocktails and mocktails are a work of art and simply delicious.

### Sunday Brunch

Every Sunday, Etereo Rooftop invites guests to a contemporary brunch experience where great food, music, and panoramic city views come together. Enhanced by a curated DJ set, the atmosphere blends relaxed sophistication with vibrant energy, creating the perfect setting to enjoy Milan from above in an elegant and convivial way.

As the luxury hotel nearest to La Scala Milan, Casa Brera provides the ideal spot for those seeking a post-theatre epicurean experience. Positioned on the building's rooftop, Etereo is a glamorous setting where a menu of Mediterranean specialties is complemented by captivating views.
