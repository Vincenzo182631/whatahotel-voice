# Welcome to Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan

## Live the rhythm of Brera

Just steps from Teatro alla Scala, Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan captures the city’s spirit in the heart of Brera. Our luxury Milan hotel in the city center invites storied experiences and...See More social encounters amid Milan’s history, art, and culture. Celebrated designer Patricia Urquiola brings timeless elegance to 101 guestrooms and 15 suites, including the Milanese Suite. Crafted with sustainability and beauty in mind, each room showcases precious regional materials that honor the building’s story. Italian cuisine is reimagined across our venue, from all-day breakfast and a classic Milanese aperitivo to Etereo, the sky bar and restaurant where exceptional gastronomy meets a rooftop pool and panoramic views. Despite the central setting, guests often highlight the hotel’s quiet, refined atmosphere.See Less

Guestrooms & Suites

### Stay Entirely Milanese

Free-spirited artistry is skillfully translated into Casa Brera’s 101 guestrooms and 15 Milan hotel suites. Arranged for relaxation, each space fluently blends the opulent and the essential to create a homely retreat, steeped in Milanese flair. Set against the backdrop of Rationalist architecture, the considered mix-and-match aesthetic features hardwood, mirrored glass, and leather, complemented by...See More unique regional creativity, like terracotta vases, beveled edges, and pendant lighting. Sparking an ongoing dialogue with the neighborhood, art is celebrated through a curated selection of local and international artists. Fashioned from locally sourced streaked marble, each en-suite bathroom is a luxurious sanctuary, where glossy surfaces, sleek black fixtures and Etro products add a further touch of Italian sophistication.See Less

Suites

## Alluring Milanese Spirit

A showcase for all things Milan, Casa Brera’s collection of suites interlaces art, architecture, fashion and socializing into an elegant residential layout, expertly designed by award-winning Patricia Urquiola. Discover luxurious Milan accommodation with that inimitable Italian sense of style, where each space maximizes on the building’s unique Rationalist architecture and prime position overlooking the charming streets of vibrant Brera. Featuring a sociable open-plan set-up, both the Junior Suite and the Panoramic Suite, with its advantageous corner aspect, are equipped with custom sofa and high-tech technology. Whilst those seeking a more extravagant experience, can choose to stay in the Brera or Milanese Suites, furnished with separate master bedroom and living room, each opening onto a large terrace, fashioned with bespoke seating, lush planting and impressive views.

Learn More

Design

## A Conversation in Style

Brought to life by multi-award-winning designer, Patricia Urquiola, Casa Brera is a destination that is indelibly Milanese. In contrast to the rigor of Pietro Lingeri’s Rationalist architecture, the interiors are an interpretation of the city’s enlightened spirit and radiant energy, bringing a vibrant new dimension to the building. Showcasing an inspired combination of colors, tones and textures, the aesthetic opens a dialogue between the city’s dual souls, where minimalist and maximalist elements harmonize with ease. Every detail is a tribute to Italian creativity. From artworks inspired by fashion, theatre and design to Cassina and Poliform furniture, as well as locally sourced marble, each aspect adds an  important layer that references both cultural heritage and an unwavering commitment to socializing.

Learn More

### Architectural Legacy

Reflecting 20th-century Milan’s design language, Casa Brera’s exterior by Pietro Lingeri, crafts a Rationalist landmark within the neighborhood. In contrast to the outside, Patricia Urquiola’s vibrant interiors tell a different story, where lively colors, geometric patterns and a mix of aesthetics captivate and inspire. Quintessentially Milanese in style, this juxtaposition reflects the city’s innate duality.

### Fashion Capital

Milan’s status as a global leader in fashion and art is undisputed, with the city home to some of the world’s most celebrated creatives. Within walking distance of the renowned fashion district and upscale Via Montenapoleone, Casa Brera has infused the area’s legacy for timeless style and innovative trendsetting into every aspect of its identity, showcasing artistry from an all-Italian inventory of creators.

### Artistic Direction

Casa Brera’s art collection is a showcase for all things Milan. Presenting the city through a contemporary lens, pieces from legends such as Tim Walker and Carolina Mizrahi, sit side-by-side with local emerging talent, to offer a creative exploration of the multifaceted Milanese experience.

## Milan’s Artistic Soul

Placed at the heart of Milan’s historic center, just moments away from the legendary Teatro alla Scala, Brera is a vibrant neighborhood where the city’s creative scene thrives. Primed for exploration, its network of winding streets, lined with ancient palazzi, forms a charming backdrop, where an assortment of bottegas, galleries, studios, al fresco cafés and more, provides inspiration at every turn. Intrinsically connected to the locale, distinctive experiences unveil hidden Milan.

How To Reach Us by Car

How To Reach Casa Brera by Car

Arriving at Via Cusani, turn right onto Via Broletto. Please note that the no-access sign refers only to the far end of the street and does not affect your route. Take the first left onto Via dei Bossi, and continue to the end. Here, you will find Casa Brera’s automatic gate. To gain access, ring the bell marked, Casa Brera, located on the left hand side and make your way to Piazzetta M. Bossi n.2.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan?

The check-in time at Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 15.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €125.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan?

On-site valet parking is available at Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan. The daily valet fee is €85.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan booking.

What is the closest airport near Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan?

The closest airport near Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan is Linate Airport (LIN). LIN is located approximately 9.3 KM from the hotel.

Does Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Casa Brera, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Milan does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
