### Inside the Accommodations at Sugar Beach

Every room at Sugar Beach, A Viceroy Resort has a story to tell. From intimate guestrooms and beachfront bungalows to grand residences overlooking the azure waves, this modern rainforest escape offers a discerning twist on the finest accommodations in St. Lucia. Here, private plunge pools, custom kitchens, open-air terraces, and attentive service blend with an indelible sense of place that brings you closer to the stories that make this part of the Caribbean unique.

Ready to craft your own island story? These four breathtaking accommodations provide the perfect setting.

### Accommodations at a Glance

**For barefoot moments on the sand:** Luxury Beachfront Bungalows — intimate retreats with whitewashed adobe walls, thatched roofs, and private plunge pools, steps from the beach.

**For settling in like a local:** Residences — gracious Plantation-style vacation homes with full kitchens, comfortable living areas, and large sun terraces with private pools.

**For design-forward beach living with your family or group:** Four-Bedroom Beachfront Collection — three refined beachfront homes with custom kitchens, private pools, outdoor dining, and direct access to Glenconner Beach.

**For once-in-a-lifetime gatherings:** The Spice of Life Residence — an ultra-exclusive nine-bedroom residence featuring a museum-worthy art collection, private chef service, and space for up to 18 guests.

### The Luxury Beachfront Bungalows

**Why Guests Choose The Luxury Beachfront Bungalows:**

- Indoor/outdoor living
- Direct beach access
- Private outdoor terrace and plunge pool
- Views of the Caribbean Sea
- One, two, or four-bedroom configurations
- Range from 645 to 4,000 square feet

The gentle lapping of waves. The shimmer of sunlight on the water. The flutter of voile drapes dancing in the breeze. Far from typical, Viceroy’s Luxury Beachfront Bungalows offer serene one, two, and four-bedroom retreats, steps from sand and sea. Thatched roofs and whitewashed adobe walls nod to the island’s vernacular architecture, blending with the lush panorama of mountain, rainforest, and ocean that surrounds you.

Each Luxury Beachfront Bungalow boasts its own shaded terrace and private plunge pool overlooking the Caribbean Sea. Step through French doors for a taste of indoor/outdoor living at its finest: towering, pitch pine exposed-beam ceilings, concrete floors, and custom-crafted furnishings in light, airy tones. A stately four-poster king bed, appointed in Egyptian cotton linens, anchors the space.

As with all accommodations at Sugar Beach, A Viceroy Resort, guests enjoy seamless, personalized attention via a dedicated Host. From arranging a sunset sail to having your garments pressed and folded into bespoke drawers, your Host ensures every detail is anticipated.

### The Residences

**Why Guests Choose The Residences:**

- Fully equipped kitchen
- Infinity-edge pool
- Private patio, walled garden, and balcony terraces
- Choice of beach, oceanfront, or ocean view
- One to four-bedroom configurations
- Range from 1,200 to 3,300 square feet

The Residences at Sugar Beach, A Viceroy Resort, invite you to step through your own intimate door to the Caribbean Sea. These St. Lucia vacation rentals are elegantly designed in Plantation style, evoking the property’s past as an 18th-century sugar plantation. Richly layered interiors pair gleaming hardwoods with custom furnishings in shades of white and cream, while large windows emphasize the majesty of the UNESCO World Heritage-listed Pitons just outside.

A host of residential-inspired amenities ensure the comforts of home are always to hand: inviting living areas, in-suite laundry, and fully equipped custom kitchens with granite countertops and high-end appliances. In the spa-like en suite bathrooms, a freestanding soaking tub takes center stage, thoughtfully appointed with full-sized amenities by Corpus. Draw a bath and luxuriate in subtle, woodsy fragrances with top notes of pink lemon and orange blossom that evoke the verdant hills just outside your window. A blissful way to wile away an afternoon—and the perfect self-care complement to an in-room wellness session by The Class™.

Outside, your private walled garden offers a serene space for quiet contemplation. This is no ordinary space; rambling terraces, shaded lounge areas, and infinity-edge pools create boundless space for sun-dappled stillness. Here, every sensory detail is designed to ensure you never have to leave the seclusion of your Residence

### The Four Bedroom Beachfront Collection

**Why Guests Choose The Four Bedroom Beachfront Collection:**

- Outdoor kitchen and dining area
- 1,164-square-foot infinity pool
- Private patio, walled garden, and balcony terraces
- Game room with pool table
- Direct access to Glenconner Beach
- 4,000 square feet

There are St. Lucia luxury villas, and then there’s the Four Bedroom Beachfront Collection from Sugar Beach, A Viceroy Resort. Three of the most refined beachfront homes in the world, these elegant havens offer a masterclass in biophilic design. Towering ceilings, locally sourced materials, and abundant natural light invite the outdoors in. But the real showstoppers here are the 180-degree views through two stories of floor-to-ceiling windows.

At the heart of the home is a custom Italian kitchen outfitted with luxury Gaggenau and Miele appliances. The oversized island with black granite worktop offers the perfect spot to sip a morning Nespresso or late-afternoon rum punch—though it’s hard to linger too long indoors in a place this beautiful.

A large terrace with outdoor showers, a kitchen, and a dining area makes for a subtle yet irresistible invitation to dine, lounge, and live al fresco. And if you can tear yourself away from the sparkling, 1,164-square-foot infinity pool, the soft, white sand is mere steps away; each Four Bedroom Beachfront Collection enjoys private access to Glenconner Beach.

### Spice of Life Residence

**Why Guests Choose The Spice of Life Residence:**

- Private chef and 24-hour host team
- Main kitchen plus commercial-grade kitchen
- Outdoor pool and private beach access
- Dedicated on-property shuttle and driver
- Museum-worthy art collection
- 35,000 square feet

Where the Lucian rainforest meets the Piton Mountains, something extraordinary awaits: an ultra-exclusive, impeccably designed sanctuary with chef service, a 24-hour host team, a sprawling outdoor pool terrace, and space for up to 18 guests. Welcome to Spice of Life Residence. Designed by Botelho Wood Architects, the nine-bedroom residence blends locally sourced wood and stone with custom furnishings in soothing tones. The decor may be neutral, but the ambiance is anything but. Inspiration abounds, thanks to one of the most expansive private art collections in the Caribbean. Expect to find pieces by Damien Hirst, Yayoi Kusama, and Salvador Dali gracing the walls.

An inviting great room centers around a sprawling harvest table and several lounge spaces, framed by floor-to-ceiling windows and dazzling ocean views. Ideally suited for grand events or weddings, the residence features a second commercial-grade kitchen, a private chef, and customizable culinary offerings.

Outside, a series of spacious terraces offer ample space to lounge in the shade, soak up the sun, and bask in breathtaking Pitons views. An infinity-edge waterfall pool and private beach access put the delights of the Caribbean within easy reach. For adventures farther afield, a 24-hour host team is on hand, along with a dedicated on-property shuttle and driver, for a St. Lucia getaway that spares no degree of indulgence.

### Craft Your St. Lucia Story

Your next adventure is a story waiting to be told. From romantic rendezvous to grand celebrations, our elegant St. Lucia accommodations offer an unparalleled blend of luxury and natural beauty. Delve deeper into the breathtaking coastal accommodations at Sugar Beach, A Viceroy Resort, and discover the perfect setting for your own St. Lucia story today.
