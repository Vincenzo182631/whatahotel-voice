# 5 Star Luxury Resort In Los Cabos

## Viceroy Los Cabos Reimagined

Where the desert mountains of Baja rise from the Sea of Cortez, Viceroy Los Cabos sits at the edge of sea and sky — a private, playful escape, and a side of Cabo few travelers find. Golden dunes meet the sapphire Pacific in a single view; pathways wind across glassy reflecting pools to hidden corners where Baja's flavors, colors, sounds, and scents come alive. And just minutes from San José del Cabo, the true Mexico is close at hand: an old town of markets, art, and quiet streets. Shaped by the landscape, the local culture, and a deep sense of place, this is a modern Mexican oasis designed to eat, explore, and unwind.

### Wellness

Here, wellness isn't one more thing to do — it's what happens when you fall into Baja's rhythm. Embrace more than one pace: the rush of the Sea of Cortez and a long table at sunset, or the stillness of The Spa, the cold plunge, and sauna. Loud or calm, it's the same invitation — to be fully where you are.

### Meetings & Events

Gather where the Sea of Cortez meets open sky. Every space here — indoor or outdoor — is shaped by the same desert-and-ocean light that defines the resort. Board meetings, cocktail receptions, anniversary celebrations: tell us what the occasion calls for, and we'll design a setting to match.

Explore Meetings & Events

### Weddings

Say “I do” amidst the Baja blue. Choose the beach, a rooftop beneath open sky, or the calm of modern interiors — then celebrate with everyone who matters, from an intimate 50 to a full 250. A dedicated team shapes every detail of the ceremony and reception, so the day belongs entirely to the two of you.

## A Golf Trip For the Gents at Viceroy Los Cabos

On the doorstep of Baja’s most iconic bunkers, greens, and fairways, Viceroy Los Cabos is the ideal setting for an unforgettable golf-lover’s getaway. Set out from our oceanfront haven, and within a short drive, you’ll find yourself at iconic courses designed by household names like Jack Nicklaus and Greg Norman. Of course, the legendary location is only half the story; the villas of Viceroy Los Cabos are the other. Nature-inspired, modernist spaces with configurations for up to five bedrooms, these stylish casitas offer an inviting haven for your whole crew. You’ve never experienced Cabo quite like this.
