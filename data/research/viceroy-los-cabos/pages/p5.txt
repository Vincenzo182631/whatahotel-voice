## Golf

Los Cabos is home to some of the best golf experiences in Mexico, if not in the world. The following golf courses are located within 20 minutes of Viceroy Los Cabos and offer multiple levels of design, difficulty and amenities. For more information or to request/book tee times, please email concierge.vlc@viceroy.com(link sends email).

- Cabo del Sol Course

- Palmilla Golf Club

- Cabo Real Golf Course

- Puerto Los Cabos Golf Course

- Club Campestre San Jose

## Cabo del Sol Course

Located approx. 15 miles (20 minute) from the resort.

The Cabo Del Sol Course, formerly known as the Desert Course, is a stunning blend of desert and ocean landscapes in the Baja area. Since 2001, golfers have been captivated by its canyon-like arroyos and challenging layout. The course offers breathtaking views from all 18 holes, complemented by varying elevation changes and dramatic bunkering, making it an integral part of your Cabo Del Sol golf experience.

**HOURS OF OPERATION:**

Golf Course Opens at 7:30am

Pro Shop Open 7:00am – 6:00pm

Tuesdays Closed.

Learn More

## Palmilla Golf Club

Located approx. 3 miles (8 minutes) from the resort.

Palmilla Golf Club in Los Cabos, México is famous for its breathtaking scenery and world-class golf. It has been recognized as the “Grand Dame of Los Cabos Golf”. As the first Jack Nicklaus Signature design in Latin America, the masterfully conceived golf course is stunning and challenging.

As of January 1, 2025, the Arroyo and Mountain nines have transitioned to private access, leaving the spectacular Ocean Course as the sole public option. Golfers can still enjoy either 9 or 18 holes on the Ocean Course—with the 18-hole experience played as two rounds on the same nine.

## Cabo Real Golf Course

Located approx. 8 miles (16 minutes) from the resort.

Cabo Real features a multi-themed, target-style course designed by Robert Trent Jones, Jr. The recent reversal of the club’s nines has greatly enhanced the layout´s flow and drama. In its current configuration, Cabo Real’s front nine, completely refashioned by Jones from a pre-existing layout, provides a scenic, gentle test with broad fairways tumbling down to the Sea of Cortez.

## Puerto Los Cabos Golf Course

Located approx. 5 miles (14 minutes) from the resort.

Puerto Los Cabos is a 27-hole golf resort, featuring three distinct courses designed by two of the sport’s most legendary figures: Greg Norman and Jack Nicklaus. All three courses are championship layouts, perched above the Sea of Cortez and etched into rolling desert foothill and backdropped by the Sierra de la Laguna Mountains.

## Club Campestre San Jose

Located approx. 2 miles (8 minutes) from the resort.

Tucked in the rolling foothills of the Sierra de la Laguna Mountains, we find the Nicklaus Design golf course at Club Campestre. The layout’s verdant fairways, chiseled into sloping desert terrain, offer players fine views of the Sea of Cortez.

## Cabo Pulmo Scuba Dive

Cabo Pulmo National Park is one of the most wonderous dive sites in the world, known for its abundance of exotic marine life. Cabo Pulmo National Park is a 5 miles beach and sea stretch on the east cape region of Baja California Sur approximately 60 miles north of the Baja's epicenter. Reef & Dive site highlights include El Islote, El Bajo, Los Cantiles and a shipwreck, "El Colima" - a tuna boat sunk by a storm in 1939.

## Dolphin Swim & Ride Experience

Feel the thrill of swimming with dolphins in Cabo. Dolphin Swim & Ride Experience by Cabo Adventures is your chance to experience a dorsal pull that lets you feel the power of these majestic creatures.

## Luxury Sunset Sailing

Sit back and relax as you sip top-shelf wine aboard a luxury sailboat or catamaran. There are few things in life more peaceful than sailing as the sun sets into the deep blue waters of the Pacific.

## The Cabo San Lucas Marina

The Cabo San Lucas Marina is a hub of exciting activity, shopping and restaurants. This waterfront neighborhood is utterly charming and has a large array of boutiques, restaurants, and shops. From the Marina, you can admire the Yachts as they arrive, watch fishing boats slip in and out as they ply their trade, or simply enjoy the sand and ocean near the Malecon (boardwalk).

A variety of tour companies offer thrilling adventures such as helmet diving, parasailing and snuba (a technique that combines snorkeling and scuba diving). From the Marina you can also take water taxis, or charter private tours, to El Arco and Lover’s Beach.

## Snorkeling

Grab a snorkel and jump in for an unforgettable expedition of Los Cabos most impressive ocean attractions such as Land's End, the Arch, Lover's Beach, the sea lion colony and the gateway to the Pacific. Let your snorkeling adventure begin.
