## Desert resort escape in Saudi Arabia

We invite you on an exciting journey into seldom-experienced nature, wildlife, and culture. Located along the historic incense trade route against the backdrop of desert plains and the Hijaz Mountains, Six Senses Southern Dunes, The Red Sea resort pays homage to Nabataean architectural heritage and majestic desert surroundings while introducing our trademark crafted experiences and wellness approach. By setting new standards in sustainability and desert hospitality, we hope to write the next chapter in the region's story, as Saudi Arabia drops its pin on the global tourism map.

Dining

## Diverse culinary experiences

Diverse culinary delights are inspired by the historic incense route and married with our Eat With Six Senses philosophy and modern touches, served in unique locations across the resort.

- #### Bariya

  - All-day dining restaurant in a light-filled and cozy indoor and outdoor setting, serving regional and international cuisine with a focus on well-being.
- #### Merkaz

  - Brew bar and restaurant serving snacks and plant-based Arabic tapas during the day, and special themed menus for dinner, paired with locally inspired non-alcoholic cocktails.
- #### Cooking School & Chef's Table

  - Cooking school by day and private chef’s table by night to learn the secrets of our relatively undiscovered local gastronomy.
- #### Pool Bar & Grill

  - Poolside dining serving international comfort food and hot plates while your feet and drinks stay cool.
- #### Al Sarab

  - Signature restaurant serving authentic flavors from across Saudi Arabia, Morocco, Syria, Persia, and Lebanon.
- #### Gelato Pod

  - Some come just for the patisserie! Our freshly baked delicacies and complimentary ice cream and sorbet are available throughout the day.
- #### Destination & In-villa Dining

  - Destination dining offering unforgettable dining experiences at out-of-the-ordinary locations in and around the resort.

## Accommodation

Consistent with our sustainability standards, our guest rooms boast spectacular desert vistas and unique cultural charms. These new spaces are designed to connect inner privacy and seclusion with the allure of desert adventures outside. Surrounding our main Oasis complex is a collection of 36 guest rooms and suites, and a further 40 pool villas are dotted in strategic locations, with interiors reflecting the tones and textures of the sandy dunes and soft furnishings drawing from the rich colors and patterns of traditional Bedouin tribal dress.

## For those with an adventurous spirit

We are open all year-round, and our on-site attractions are diverse in the truest Six Senses tradition. We are delighted to welcome both adventure-seekers looking to experience the uniqueness of the Saudi Arabian desert and pleasure-seekers who want to relax in the tranquility and serenity of the Alnesai dunes. One thing is for sure: arrive open-minded and leave with your mind blown!

## Destination

Six Senses Southern Dunes, The Red Sea is one of two inland locations that, together with coastal and marine initiatives, form The Red Sea Development Project that spans 10,800 square miles / 28,000 square kilometers on Saudi Arabia’s west coast.
