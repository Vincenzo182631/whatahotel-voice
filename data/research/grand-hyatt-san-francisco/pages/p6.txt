# Hotel Policies

Destination Fee

A daily destination fee of $40.00 is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide the following services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

- 25% Daily Food & Beverage discount valid on property outlets (Please present the destination fee card provided at check-in for discount)
- 15% Discount off Blue & Gold Bay Cruise. See concierge for details.
- Exclusive discounts with Bay City Bike rental (availability daily; see Concierge for details).
- 10% Discount at nearby E&O Restaurant for all food and beverage items (excluding Happy Hour offerings)
- 10% off booking at Holey Moley Golf Club. Discount Code card provided at hotel
- 10% off Grayline tours. See concierge for details.
- California Academy of Science $5 off per ticket- Discount Card provided at hotel
- 10% Discount off any Big Bus City sightseeing tour. See concierge for details.
- Macy's (Union Square Location) Visitor Saving Pass - 10% off all regular, sale & clearance-priced merchandise, with a few exceptions
- San Francisco Keepsake Button - see concierge
- One shirt pressing per stay
- One In Room Movie per stay
- 20% off at The Parthenon- Mediterranean Wine Bar & Mezes (see concierge for details)

Pet Policy

We’re happy to welcome your traveling canine companions to our pet-friendly hotel in San Francisco’s Union Square. To participate in the pet program, inform the hotel at least three days before arrival that you’ll have a pet (or pets) staying with you. Only dogs weighing 50 pounds and under, or two dogs under a combined weight of 75 pounds, are permitted. Our knowledgeable staff can recommend pet-friendly parks and restaurants in the downtown area.

**Rates are as follows:**

Up to 6 nights: $150 per stay

Up to 30 nights: $300 per stay

31+ nights: Fee assessed at the hotel’s discretion

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Pets are not permitted in the restaurant, Grand Club, or Fitness Center EXCEPT SERVICE ANIMALS.

Package Handling Policy

Any items shipped or delivered to the hotel will be associated with a fee and charged to your guest room. To retrieve a delivered package, please visit the Bell Desk located in the lobby. For assistance with shipping, please contact the Concierge Desk and we are delighted to help facilitate your shipment.

Incoming Package Handling Fees:

• Letter/Envelope: $5

• Small Box (under 10 lbs): $15

• Medium Box (10–20 lbs): $20

• Large Box (20–50 lbs): $25

• Extra Large Box (over 50 lbs): $50

• Plastic Cases / Luggage / Golf Clubs: $80

• Crates / Pallets: $350

• Double Pallets: $650

Outgoing Shipping: A $10 handling fee applies to all outgoing shipments, in addition to the cost of shipping. Shipping rates will vary based on the size, weight, and destination of the package.

For additional questions or more information, please email us at sanfrancisco.grand@hyatt.com

Early Departure Fee

If there are any changes to the confirmed departure date after check-in, an early departure fee will apply equal to one night’s room and tax.
