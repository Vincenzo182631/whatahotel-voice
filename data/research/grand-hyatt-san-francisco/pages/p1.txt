## Enjoy the Best of San Francisco from our Union Square Hotel

Grand Hyatt San Francisco welcomes you with a sophisticated elegance and refined modern style. Located in the heart of the city on Union Square, our downtown high rise hotel is the perfect jumping-off point for exploring San Francisco’s top attractions, from premier shopping and Michelin Star dining to iconic sites like Golden Gate Theatre and Hayes Valley. After a day of discovery, retreat to your room where you can unwind and enjoy the spectacular views of the City by the Bay.

Environmental Responsibility

We’re deeply committed to sustainability, with initiatives focusing on carbon emissions, water use, waste and responsible sourcing—all tracked via Hyatt EcoTrack. We care for the planet, and in turn care for people so they can be their best.

36th-Floor Event Space

Book your next business gathering or social event at Grand Hyatt San Francisco, where our hotel’s breathtaking top-floor venue makes an ideal backdrop to any occasion.

In-Room Amenities

Elevate your stay in San Francisco’s iconic Union Square with an array of lush in-room amenities to suit every occasion. Choose from a selection of house-made items expertly curated by our culinary team, including savory treats like a decadent cheese and charcuterie board using local ingredients or sweets like a batch of freshly baked chocolate chip cookies with milk. Adults can indulge in our collection of alcoholic beverages, from delicious wines sourced from local vineyards to cans of your favorite beers. All items are available to preorder online for a fee and can be delivered to your hotel room upon your request.

StayFit Fitness Center

Keep up your workout routine at Grand Hyatt San Francisco’s 24-hour StayFit fitness center. Our high rise hotel’s 35th-floor facility features floor-to-ceiling windows with sweeping views of the City by the Bay to fuel your spirit as you recharge. We also offer a business card–size map highlighting scenic jogging routes, available from the concierge desk.

### Pets Are Welcome

We’re happy to welcome your traveling canine companions. Please inform the hotel at least three days before arrival that you’ll have a pet (or pets) staying with you. For those staying 30+ nights, a fee will be charged at the hotel’s discretion. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

#### Pet Fees

- Up to 6 nights: **$150** _/ STAY_

- Up to 30 nights: **$300** _/ STAY_

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  50Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 75Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.

### Destination Fee

A daily destination fee of $40.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay to provide services and amenities that enhance the guest experience.
