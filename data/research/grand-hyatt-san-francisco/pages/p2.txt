Unwind and rejuvenate in the welcoming comfort and upscale style of Grand Hyatt San Francisco, located on Union Square. With 669 rooms, including 30 luxury suites, our hotel blends sophisticated décor with residential-style amenities to help you feel at home. Floor-to-ceiling windows in each room offer unbeatable Union Square, bay or city views. Suites feature more space with a separate living area as well as hardwood floors for an elegant stay.
