### Nice hotel, but beware of the SCAM

This was a nice hotel and we enjoyed our stay. It’s in a good location, the rooms are clean and updated, service is friendly, etc.

But even when my experience is otherwise positive, as it was in this case, I always leave feeling utterly ripped off by the ridiculous destination fees.

This hotel charges $40 per night, which is nearly $50 including taxes. Unlike other hotels that at least offer a modest food and beverage credit, there is literally no tangible value tied to this fee.

The main “benefit” advertised for the destination fee is a 25% discount at the hotel bar and restaurant. Some simple math:

\- For a three nights stay, I paid $150 in destination fees
\- In order to recover this value, I would have needed to spend $600 at the hotel restaurant!

Destination fees are always a scam, but in this case, they are particularly egregious. Hotel managers who toe this line for Hyatt should be ashamed.

corbin766,
