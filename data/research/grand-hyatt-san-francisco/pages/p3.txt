# Dining

Grand Hyatt San Francisco offers a welcoming dining experience in the heart of Union Square. OneUP Restaurant and Lounge is our signature dining venue designed as a warm and comfortable gathering spot for both locals and guests. Enjoy breakfast before you head out to start your day or sip a relaxing wine or cocktail along with classic American bistro fare in the evening. Craving a sweet treat or a savory snack from the comfort of your bed? Order from a selection of in-room amenities for a fee, and have them delivered to your room.

## OneUP Restaurant

Start your day with our delicious American classics, served buffet-style for a satisfying breakfast experience. Enjoy made-to-order omelets, fluffy pancakes, fresh pastries, crispy bacon, savory sausage, and refreshing fruit. Prefer something lighter? Continental options are available too. Join us at OneUP and savor a breakfast that fuels your day!

## OneUP Lounge

Located on the Mezzanine Level, OneUP Lounge is the perfect spot to gather with friends, family, or colleagues. Enjoy fine wines, crafted cocktails, and savory bites in a stylish setting. With cozy seating, attentive service, and a refined bar, it's the ideal place to unwind. Join us and let the conversations flow as smoothly as our drinks!
