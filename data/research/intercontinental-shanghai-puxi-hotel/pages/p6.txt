Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An has 7 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An?

Check-in at InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Shanghai Jing'An is conveniently located near Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA), approximately 15.53 miles away (25 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
