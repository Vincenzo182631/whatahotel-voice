### Breeze Garden

Breeze Garden, the open-air garden lounge of our hotel, blends comfort and elegance seamlessly.
With its glass-panelled and semi-open design, it captures soft daylight by day and the city’s glittering skyline by night. The temperature-controlled space feels like an urban oasis.
Services include siphon-brewed tea, exquisite afternoon tea and party.
