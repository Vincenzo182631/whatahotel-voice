Do the rooms at Le Méridien Vienna have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien Vienna have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Le Méridien Vienna?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 3 at Le Méridien Vienna. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Le Méridien Vienna?

Some rooms at Le Méridien Vienna permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
