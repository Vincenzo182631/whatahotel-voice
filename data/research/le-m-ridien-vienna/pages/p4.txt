From the Ring, it is just a few minutes walk to the Museumsquartier,...See More a combination of high culture, contemporary art, and excellent bars and restaurants – an urban chill-out zone. Unique among the hotels in Vienna's city center, the muses – architecture, music, fashion, and dance – unfold their creativity here. ## Exclusive Fitness Club

Escape the everyday and rejuvenate both body and mind at the John Harris Executive Club, located directly at Le Méridien Vienna. Here, you'll find an exceptional fitness and wellness area that meets the highest standards. In addition to modern fitness facilities, you'll have access to a pool, whirlpool, and a first-class sauna landscape. You also have the option to book a personal trainer at an exclusive hourly rate of 70 EUR. For extensive jogging sessions, the picturesque Burggarten awaits right in front of the hotel.

## Ball Season in Vienna

Put on Your Best Dress - Polish Your Shoes - Take This Waltz

To all ball-goers and dance fans: Don't miss November 11, when the best time of the year begins in Vienna - the magical season of classic Viennese balls. Each year more than 450 balls take place in Vienna, starting in November and reaching its peak in January and February, during the season of Carnival. The Grand Finale: The Vienna Opera Ball at Vienna State Opera.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Le Méridien Vienna have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Le Méridien Vienna offers a complimentary, 6:30 AM-9:30 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Le Méridien Vienna have a pool on-site?

Yes, Le Méridien Vienna has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Indoor pool heated all year, in John Harris Executive Club (Indoor)Whirlpool
