## Le Méridien Vienna: an inspiring design hotel in Vienna

An individual design and modern hotel in Vienna. Le Méridien Vienna is style, not bivouac; it is art puzzle and gallery. Located in the heart of Vienna, the design hotel is the hub of the city’s artistic...See More jewels. They surprise and inspire. Spacious and newly designed guest rooms and suites make you happy; in roomy bathrooms, you revive. Your view falls on Vienna’s splendid Ringstrasse, the serene Schillerplatz, or the sun-filled courtyard. At last - vacation in Vienna, at Le Méridien hotel in the city’s epicenter. Develop your company at Le Méridien business hotel in Vienna. Celebrate the happiest day of your life at Le Méridien wedding hotel. You sleep regally and dine magnificently. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Vienna?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Vienna is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Vienna allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Vienna. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €25.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Vienna?

On-site parking is available at Le Méridien Vienna. Self parking is available at Le Méridien Vienna, with rates of €4.20 per hour, and €40.00 per day. Valet parking is available for €10.00 perstay.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Vienna have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Vienna has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Vienna booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Vienna?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Vienna is Vienna International Airport (VIE). VIE is located approximately 22.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Vienna have an airport shuttle to Vienna International Airport (VIE)?

Yes, Le Méridien Vienna has an airport shuttle to Vienna International Airport (VIE).

Does Le Méridien Vienna have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Le Méridien Vienna has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
