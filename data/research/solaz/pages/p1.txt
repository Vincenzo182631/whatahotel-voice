# Welcome to Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos

## Find your paradise at our Los Cabos beach resort

Nestled along one of the most exclusive stretches of coastline in Baja California Sur, Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos offers an unparalleled beachfront escape. Ideally located between San...See More José del Cabo and Cabo San Lucas, our resort provides easy access to local attractions, dining and the marina while immersing guests in the beauty of the Sea of Cortez. Our contemporary 5-star accommodations blend comfort and sophistication, featuring elegant interiors, premium bedding, marble finishes and custom furnishings. Many rooms and suites offer indoor-outdoor living with private terraces, outdoor showers, plunge pools and ocean views.Guests can unwind at the spa, enjoy exceptional dining, stay active in the 24-hour fitness center and relax in heated infinity pools overlooking the sea. Families are also welcomed with dedicated spaces for younger guests.Whether seeking a romantic getaway, wellness retreat or refined beach vacation, Solaz delivers an exceptional experience on the shores of Los Cabos.See Less

## Destination

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos?

The check-in time at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $120.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos?

On-site parking is available at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos booking.

What is the closest airport near Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos?

The closest airport near Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos is Los Cabos International Airport (SJD). SJD is located approximately 34.7 KM from the hotel.

Does Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
