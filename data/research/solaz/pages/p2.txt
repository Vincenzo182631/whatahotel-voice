Do the rooms at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos have a work space?

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos?

The largest occupancy room at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos is the Beachfront Residence with a maximum occupancy of 10.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos?

Some rooms at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Solaz, a Luxury Collection Resort, Los Cabos have fridges?
