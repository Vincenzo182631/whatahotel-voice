### Holiday Boutique Bazaar

### Lighting Ceremony

As twilight settles, the season comes alive. The ceremonial lighting of the tree marks a timeless holiday moment—sparkling, intimate, and enchanting—inviting all to share in the wonder of tradition.

### Holiday Specials

An extraordinary Christmas Eve, defined by elegance and flavor. Indulge in a refined dining experience where exceptional cuisine and storied traditions come together to create an unforgettable holiday night.

### Santa Claus Arrival

Christmas morning unfolds with joy and delight. The iconic arrival of Santa Claus brings smiles, wonder, and a sparkling festive spirit—an enchanting moment treasured by guests of all ages.

### Holiday Dinner

A captivating holiday table unfolds in a warm, radiant ambiance. An exquisite culinary journey designed to delight the senses, where festive elegance and exceptional flavors come together in a truly memorable Christmas celebration.

### Last Brunch of the Year

A celebratory farewell to the year. Carefully prepared grill selections, richly seasoned and masterfully executed, are paired with festive accompaniments and indulgent desserts—an intimate gathering to savor the season alongside those most cherished.

### New Year's Eve Dinner & Party

An iconic night of celebration and transformation. Live music, DJ rhythms, fireworks illuminating the sky, a champagne toast, golden open bar, and refined hors d'oeuvres set the stage for a luminous welcome to the New Year—where memories are made and joy feels boundless.

### Holiday Luminous Art-Lights of the North Pole

An enchanting artistic experience inspired by the radiant lights of the North Pole. Children create glowing artworks using luminous materials and winter hues, exploring creativity within an immersive atmosphere evoking stars, auroras, and magical winter landscapes.

### Kids’ S’mores Experience

A joyful hands-on activity where children safely toast marshmallows and create their own classic s'mores with chocolate and graham crackers. A guided experience that encourages creativity, togetherness, and sweet holiday memories to treasure.

### Gingerbread House Decoration

A cherished holiday tradition brought to life. Children design and decorate their own gingerbread houses with sweet, festive details, blending creativity and coordination while delighting in seasonal aromas, vibrant colors, and the timeless spirit of Christmas.

### Signature Holiday Experiences

## Ayuna Master Class

A transformative skincare journey led by an Ayuna expert. Begin with a grounding meditation, followed by a step-by-step exploration of the art of skincare for face and body. Delight in seasonal fruits, parfaits, and refreshing beverages.

Date: Thanksgiving Week

Duration: 90 to 120 minutes

Add a 60-minute facial for a complete experience of renewal

## Whispers of Nature

An immersive apothecary-inspired workshop where nature and self-healing converge. Through aromatherapy, mindful tastings, and hands-on creation of natural skincare, guests reconnect with their body's wisdom in an intimate, curated setting.

## The Intention Bracelet

A meditative creation ritual where intention takes form. Guests craft a personal mala bracelet, infusing each bead with meaning and mantra—an intimate talisman symbolizing balance, clarity, and conscious presence.

## New Year Ceremony

A sacred ritual of gratitude and release to consciously close the year. Through reflection, the transformative power of fire, and the warmth of ceremonial cacao, guests are invited to let go of what no longer serves them. The experience concludes with a quartz crystal, a luminous symbol of renewal, intention, and the transformation awaiting in the year ahead.
