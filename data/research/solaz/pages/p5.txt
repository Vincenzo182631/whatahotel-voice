## Rejuvenate Yourself

Named after a coastal lagoon located in the northwestern area of Baja California Sur and taking inspiration from the magnificent Sea of Cortez, Ojo de Liebre Spa has found a fabled selection of converted local ingredients into treatments curated specially to offer a transcendent well-being experience. Guests at our Los Cabos hotel spa​ are welcomed to immerse themselves in a world of stimulating experiences for the senses, while discovering the textures and exquisite aromas of this colorful oasis. The only Thalasso therapy wellness spa in Baja allows you to enjoy the seawater and fresh water pools, 12 treatment rooms, hot and ice-cold tubs, aromatherapy, chromotherapy light showers and our exclusive Cryotherapy chamber. With a full range of spa & salon services, arrive to a haven of relaxation and return rejuvenated.

## CaboNautas

Children can immerse themselves in the art of fun with endless things to do at our San Jose del Cabo hotel, while parents relish a spot of quiet time with an afternoon lounging in our cabanas. At the CaboNautas Kid’s Club, children are free to play and experience a space all on their own with a selection of enriching activities and toys to enjoy under the watchful attention of specialized personnel. They will also be able to participate in cooking classes and make pizza or cookies.

## 13\|19 Teens Club

This is the place for young and adventurous explorers aged between 13 -19 to find a sanctuary of free expression and enjoyment. Our facility offers a variety of activities and games for you to have amazing moments with your loved ones or meet new friends. Come and have a blast at our pool tables, foosball, and video game stations, or challenge yourself at our arcade basketball.

### Cabo Sport Complex

The Cabo Sport Complex has an approximate area of 5.3 hectares, in which there are buildings and spaces suitable for hosting any type of sporting, cultural or social event. It is a space designed to take advantage of large open spaces as The Los Cabos ATP.

## Gabinete del Barco Exhibit

El Gabinete “Del Barco” is the indigenous gallery featuring regional artifacts and open spaces in which to walk while enjoying the breathtaking natural scenery. Viewed through the eyes of Baja California explorer, Miguel del Barco themed exhibits display a suspended skeleton of a 13-meter-long grey whale, ancient maps of the Americas based on limited navigation discoveries, 360˚ virtual photography; video interviews with archeologists; and replicas of harpoons, knives, and other tools from the region.
