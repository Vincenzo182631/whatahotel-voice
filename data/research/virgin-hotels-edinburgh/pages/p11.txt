**London's Creative Epicentre**

Find our luxury boutique hotel in Shoreditch, near Liverpool Street Station and Old Street Station, an achingly hip hotspot in London’s eclectic East End. Hit the bustling neighbourhood markets to unearth vintage treasures and fistfuls of flowers. Seek out an arthouse masterpiece or cult classic at one of the tiny independent cinemas. Or just throw on your comfiest shoes and ponder the graffiti and murals splashed across the buildings and alleyways of Shoreditch and Brick Lane. Here, exploring is its own reward.
