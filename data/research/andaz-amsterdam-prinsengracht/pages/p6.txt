# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

As a hotel with parking in Amsterdam-Centrum, the hotel offers secure underground parking with limited spaces, available exclusively through valet parking and subject to availability.
Located within Amsterdam's UNESCO-listed Canal District, parking is limited throughout the neighborhood.
Electric vehicle charging is available on-site for all vehicle types, subject to availability. Please bring your own charging cable compatible with your vehicle.
Valet parking is available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
Vehicle height restriction: 1.9 m (6 ft)

Getting here

Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS)17.2 kilometers17.2 kilometers

We're happy to arrange private limousine transfers between Amsterdam Airport Schiphol and the hotel. The service includes:
● Personal meet and greet at the arrivals gate
● Luggage assistance
● Complimentary bottled water
● Complimentary Wi-Fi
● Newspaper
● Personal welcome on arrival at the hotel.
Transfers are available by appointment. Please contact the hotel to make arrangements.
