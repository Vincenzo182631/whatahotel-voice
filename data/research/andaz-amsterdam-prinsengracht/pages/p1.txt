# A creative lifestyle hotel on Amsterdam's iconic canals

Set within Amsterdam's former public library, Andaz Amsterdam Prinsengracht is a 5-star ​​luxury lifestyle hotel where bold design, local culture, and modern luxury come together. Designed by renowned Dutch designer Marcel Wanders, the hotel celebrates Amsterdam's heritage through imaginative interiors, one-of-a-kind artwork and considered touches that invite you to discover the city from a fresh perspective. ​​Located on the UNESCO-listed Prinsengracht canal, it's a boutique hotel in Amsterdam-Centrum where you can stay, connect and experience Amsterdam like a local.

## Rooms & Suites

Discover 122 stylish guestrooms, including five signature suites, where Marcel Wanders' singular interiors reinterpret Dutch heritage through a kaleidoscope of bold artwork, playful details and contemporary comfort. With views across the Prinsengracht canal, the Secret Garden or the observatorium-inspired atrium, every stay offers a unique perspective of Amsterdam.

### Festive Dining and Events at Andaz

Make this festive season one to remember at Andaz Amsterdam Prinsengracht. Whether you're planning a celebration, client event, family gathering or festive night out, discover seasonal experiences across the hotel. We have a range of experiences available, such as festive private dinners for groups and corporate celebrations, immersive experiences for unforgettable group gatherings, festive dining at Prins & Aap, and Christmas brunch and dinner at Renvy by Michelin-starred Chef Randy Karman. Gather colleagues, clients, friends and family for a festive experience designed to surprise, delight and inspire. Book your table or enquire today.

Details + Reservations

### Prins & Aap

Welcome to Prins & Aap, your local cocktail bar and restaurant where polarities connect in the best way possible all day, every day.

Nestled in the city centre of Amsterdam and overlooking the UNESCO Amsterdam Canal District, we invite you to experience the perfect blend of local flavours, cosy ambience, and Dutch design. From cocktails to lunch to dinner, Prins & Aap offers an unforgettable experience. With locally sourced ingredients and crafted high-end cocktails, each bite and sip narrates a story.

Join us to explore why Prins & Aap is more than a bar or a restaurant, its an extraordinary vibe that mirrors Amsterdam's diversity.

Details + Reservations

### Renvy

Chef-led restaurant by Randy Karman. Along the Prinsengracht canal, within Andaz Amsterdam, Renvy takes root. A restaurant shaped by heritage, Dutch memory, and the rhythm of the seasons. Guided by the vision of Michelin-starred chef Randy Karman, Renvy combines local ingredients and refined technique to create a dining experience that resonates. Here, nature leads and stories unfold. A place where taste becomes longing, and every seat is the envy of those still waiting. Renvy — rooted in heritage, shaped by nature

Details + Reservations

### Breakfast

Breakfast is served daily in The Garden Room, with an extensive buffet, a dedicated Dutch selection and freshly prepared à la carte dishes. From lighter choices to familiar morning favorites, the menu offers a considered start to the day in a calm setting with views across the Secret Garden.

### Vintage Saloonboat

Embark on an enchanting journey with our vintage saloon boat, awaiting your arrival right in front of the hotel. Choose between a captivating 60 or 90 minute cruise through scenic views with private dining. Please contact us for hours, pricing and further details.

### Room Service

Twenty-four-hour Room Service brings the hotel’s food and drink to your room or suite at any time of day. Begin with breakfast in bed, order something light between plans or settle in for a more elaborate dinner, with options ranging from snacks to four-course meals. A dedicated late-night menu keeps the experience going after the restaurants close.

### Daily Jenever Hour at Prins & Aap

Join us daily from 5:00 - 6:00 PM for our educational Jenever Hour - a fun introduction to one of the Netherlands’ most iconic spirits. Taste two different Jenevers while our team shares a brief history, interesting facts, and the story behind each one. Then, choose from our different drink options and styles, and enjoy unlimited refills until 6 PM.
The experience is free for in-house guests, and €32 per person for outside guests.
Come curious. Taste something new. Find your Jenever.

## Dog-Friendly Stays

At Andaz Amsterdam Prinsengracht, dogs are welcomed as part of the family. Through our Very Important Dog (VID) program, your four-legged companion will enjoy thoughtful amenities, including a Cloud7 dog bed and blanket, designer bowls and bottled mineral water, making every stay as comfortable for them as it is for you. Dogs must be housebroken.

# Bedtime Stories with PAN Amsterdam

To celebrate GLUE, we’re hosting the fourth edition of Bedtime Stories, our intimate art talk series.

This edition welcomes Swedish artist Alfhild Kulper, whose tapestries, sculptures and immersive installations use textile art to bring softness, organic forms and a new dimension to interior spaces.

During the evening, Margitte Verwoerdt, Art Advisor and PAN Amsterdam Head of Program & Partnerships, will sit down with Alfhild to explore her work and creative practice, as well as look ahead to the upcoming PAN Amsterdam art fair.
