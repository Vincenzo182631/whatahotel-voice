## Prinsengracht Suite

1,517 sq ftCanal View

The hotel's signature penthouse — the Prinsengracht Suite — is one of the most remarkable hotel suites in Amsterdam, pairing Amsterdam's largest hotel terrace along the Prinsengracht canal with expansive indoor and outdoor living space. Inside, sophisticated interiors include a king bedroom with a rotating television, spacious living and dining areas, a kitchenette and a private library/study. The oversized bathroom features Marcel Wanders' signature soap bar bathtub, a walk-in shower and an integrated TV mirror. For additional flexibility, the suite can be connected to a Twin Room to create a two-bedroom suite.

In-Room Amenities

  - Complimentary early check-in and late check-out upon availability
