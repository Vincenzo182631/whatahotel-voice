### My honeymoon experience fell flat.

I had been saving my World of Hyatt points specifically for our honeymoon, and after doing a lot of research and reading such positive reviews, I chose The Cape because I expected it to make this special trip feel memorable.

When making the reservation, I spoke with a Hyatt customer service agent and specifically asked that our honeymoon be noted on the reservation. I had a wonderful experience with Hyatt during the booking process, so I was surprised and disappointed that our honeymoon was never acknowledged by the hotel during our stay.

As a Discoverist member, I also hoped that, if available, we might receive an upgrade or at least a room on a higher floor—especially given that we were celebrating our honeymoon. I certainly understand that upgrades are based on availability, but combined with the lack of any acknowledgment of our special occasion, the overall experience felt underwhelming.

Another frustrating experience involved a bottle of champagne that friends had sent to our room through the hotel to celebrate our honeymoon. We wanted to bring the bottle to the rooftop so we could enjoy it outside rather than sitting in our room. The staff initially gave us a difficult time and attempted to charge us a corkage fee, even after we explained that the champagne had been delivered to us by the hotel. For a celebratory bottle sent as a honeymoon gift, the interaction felt unnecessarily rigid and took away from what should have been a simple, special moment.

The Cape is a beautiful property, but unfortunately, the level of hospitality did not match what I expected for a hotel of this caliber or for such an important trip. I saved my World of Hyatt points for a long time specifically to use them for our honeymoon, and I left wishing I had chosen a different Hyatt property for the occasion.

### The Cape! 5 stars all around!

This was our second time visiting The Cape, and second year in a row. Last year we had a great time, and this year we came with friends and enjoyed the 3 bedroom Penthouse for several of our 40th birthday celebrations. There was 7 of us staying in the 2 story Penthouse + 1 other room was booked for another couple. We thoroughly enjoyed our stay at the hotel. If you're planning visiting with family or close friends, the value of the Penthouse is great and should be worth considering. Lots of amenities are included with the Penthouse booking that are typically extra. The pool area is a great vibe. The view from every room at this hotel is amazing, and the staff and service is very attentive and welcoming.

A special thank you to Fernanda Hernandez from the concierge team. I've traveled around the world and have stayed at many 5 star hotels... I don't think I've ever dealt with someone so pleasant and thorough. She was very attentive to my request for bookings and suggestions for our group of 9 for several weeks before our arrival. I was in charge of all the restaurant reservations and activities for our group, and she did a terrific job arranging everything for me.

I would 100% come back to The Cape in the future, and would highly recommend this hotel. I think this hotel is mainly suited for adults (w/o kids) who are looking for a more lively atmosphere with the great pool area + rooftop bar.

Great location too! Not too far from the main town. Every room has a view, and there are surfers all day out in the water so it's nice to enjoy this unique experience as well.

Rooms were clean and quiet. The individual balconies of each suite is super nice and relaxing too.

The Cape is a great hotel and one of the stars of Cabo.

### An unforgettable stay with exceptional hospitality

Our stay at The Cape, a Thompson Hotel exceeded every expectation. From the moment we arrived until the day we checked out, the service was truly outstanding. Every member of the staff—from the front desk and housekeeping to the pool attendants, restaurant team, and room service—was warm, attentive, and genuinely happy to help. I wish I had written down everyone’s names because they all deserve recognition. Every request, no matter how small, was met with a smile and handled immediately.

The hotel itself is absolutely stunning. The architecture and design create a beautiful organic, earthy atmosphere that feels both luxurious and relaxing. Everything was impeccably clean and beautifully maintained. Our room was spacious, comfortable, spotless, and thoughtfully designed, with incredible views that made it difficult to leave the balcony.

The food throughout the property was exceptional. Every meal was fresh, beautifully presented, and of outstanding quality. We also enjoyed the spa, which was wonderful and added another level of relaxation to our stay.

One thing I especially appreciated was having both a beautiful saltwater pool and a traditional pool. While the ocean in front of the hotel is generally too rough for swimming, the saltwater pool more than made up for it and quickly became one of our favorite places to relax while still enjoying the incredible ocean views.

We were in Cabo for a wedding, so over four days we experienced nearly every part of the resort, and the level of service never wavered. It’s rare to find a hotel where every department consistently delivers such a high standard of hospitality.

Another bonus is the location. There are excellent restaurants within walking distance, including one with fantastic Mediterranean cuisine, live jazz, and live music, making it easy to enjoy the area without going far.

The Cape delivers everything you hope for in a luxury resort: beautiful accommodations, incredible food, breathtaking views, exceptional amenities, and, most importantly, a team of people who truly care about making your stay memorable. We can’t wait to return. Highly recommended.
