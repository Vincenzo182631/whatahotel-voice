# Boutique, Luxury Resort in Cabo San Lucas \| The Cape, a Thompson Hotel

## A Luxurious, Boutique Escape on the Shores of Cabo San Lucas

Along the striking coastline of Cabo San Lucas, The Cape is a luxury boutique hotel that redefines the resort experience with bold design, effortless style and panoramic views. Each of the 159 guest rooms, suites and villas—ranging from one to three bedrooms—offers uninterrupted vistas of El Arco and the shimmering Bahia Cabo San Lucas. From the saltwater pool carved into natural rock to the rooftop lounge with sweeping ocean views, the hotel blends rugged beauty with refined amenities. Guests can indulge in locally inspired treatments in open-air spa cabanas, enjoy two stunning pools including a lively infinity option with a swim-up bar and savor inventive cuisine at Manta, the signature restaurant led by acclaimed chef, Enrique Olvera.

## Rooms & Suites

Every room, suite and villa at our Cabo San Lucas hotel is designed to reflect the effortless beauty and laid-back energy of the coast. Enjoy sweeping ocean views from your private balcony, complete with a hanging daybed and space to savor a handcrafted cocktail. Inside, mid-century Latin American furnishings and custom sateen-woven linens add a refined, personal touch. For even more space and privacy, our one-, two- and three-bedroom villas feature full kitchens, plunge pools and all the comforts of an elevated Cabo escape.
