Tucked within lush tropical gardens along Fiji's coral coast, the Plantation Pool Bure invites you to slow down and savor the island's gentle rhythm.

With natural textures, Fijian artistry and open-air design, every detail connects you to the land and spirit of _Bula_. This is barefoot luxury made for the rest, renewal and togetherness.
