# Experience the magic of Waikiki

An epic beach destination

You don’t have to look far to see why Honolulu’s Waikiki Beach is considered one of the most iconic vacation destinations in the world. Home to spectacular white sand beaches, rich culture and a laid-back vibe, this iconic destination is waiting for you.

### Cirque du Soleil 'Auana

Voted the No. 4 best new attraction of 2026 by USA Today Readers’ Choice Awards, a new dawn of live entertainment rises in **Cirque du Soleil 'Auana** – a Hawai'i-inspired production featuring a cast of powerful acrobats, hilarious comedians, talented musicians and singers and profound hula dancers.

Only at the **OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beachcomber Hotel**.

There's a handful of reasons that guests love our sunrise yoga. It's the perfect way to start your day off right in Waikiki. Whether you follow it up with some fresh acai bowls, a dip in Waikiki Beach, or even crawling back into bed, we promise you'll love this class too.

Join us every Wednesday and Saturday at 7 am - 8 am on the pool deck.

ACTIVITIES & EXPERIENCES

### Soak up island vibes

A stay at Beachcomber puts you just steps from famous landmarks, bustling nightlife, and the energy of Waikiki. From world-class museums to stunning hiking trails, you’re invited to experience the best of Oahu.

Learn more

Discover Waikiki

Our complete Waikiki vacation guide

Discover the best places to go and the must-do activities in Waikiki.

Learn more

Waikiki Beach Walk

Embrace the spirit of Aloha at this vibrant shopping and entertainment destination. Packed with cool shops and dining, you’ll find unique souvenirs, taste fresh local flavors and enjoy live music just steps from our hotel.

Learn more

International Market Place

Visit Waikiki’s premier, open-air shopping, dining and entertainment destination to discover upscale retailers , dine under the stars on the Grand Lanai and visit the historic Banyan tree.

Learn more

Waikiki Aquarium

Discover underwater worlds and the mysteries of the Pacific Ocean through interpretive exhibits, live animal displays and fun, hands-on activities for the whole family at the award-winning Waikiki Aquarium.

Learn more

Terrace of Modern Art

TOMA – the Terrace of Modern Art – is our surf-art series featuring celebrated artists who draw their inspiration from the beach. It’s also free and open to the public.

Learn more

Diamond Head Crater

Visit one of Hawaii’s most impressive natural wonders. Take a hike up to the ancient Diamond Head volcanic crater summit and you’ll be rewarded with breathtaking panoramas of Oahu.

Learn more

Iolani Palace

Built in 1882 by the last king of Hawaii, King Kalakaua, Iolani Palace was the official residence of the Hawaiian monarcy. Step back in time and into the rooms where the king and queen entertained dignataries from around the world.
