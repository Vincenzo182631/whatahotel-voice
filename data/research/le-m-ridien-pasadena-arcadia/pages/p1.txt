# Welcome to Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia

## A Mid-Century Modern Hotel Near Pasadena, CA

Step into mid-century modern sophistication at Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia, where the creative-minded, the culture seekers, and those who live to savor the moment gather near Los Angeles and the greater...See More Southern California area. Set in the heart of the San Gabriel Valley, our design-forward Arcadia, CA hotel features 233 stylish hotel rooms and over 14,000 square feet of modern event space; we guide our guests through the nuances that make our city great. Discover chic equestrian-inspired interiors influenced by nearby Santa Anita Park Racetrack, curated global art installations, and a signature high tea and high balls program at Mélange, our on-site restaurant serving New American cuisine. Explore the LA County Arboretum, Westfield Santa Anita, and downtown Pasadena — all minutes away. Whatever brings you to the area, see and be seen at the new Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia?

On-site valet parking is available at Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia. The daily valet fee is $31.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia is Hollywood Burbank Airport (BUR). BUR is located approximately 22.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Le Méridien Pasadena Arcadia does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
