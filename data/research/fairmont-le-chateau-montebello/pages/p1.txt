## The _true_ Canadian experience

Fairmont Le Château Montebello, a five-star resort, is known for its rustic charm, unique architecture and beautiful natural surroundings. Described as the true Canadian experience, the world’s largest log castle is a welcoming resort destination with more than 40 unique activities and experiences available on-site.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Le Château Montebello located?

Our luxury hotel's address is 392 Notre Dame, Montebello, Quebec, Canada J0V 1L0.

Is Fairmont Le Château Montebello pet-friendly?

Yes, we welcome furry friends at our pet-friendly hotel.

Is parking available at Fairmont Le Château Montebello?

Outdoor self-parking for guests is included in the Resort Experience Fee. Outdoor self-parking for non-hotel guests is $25 per day plus taxes. Valet parking is available for $39 per day plus taxes. Charging stations are available for Teslas and other electric vehicles.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Le Château Montebello?

Check-in is from 4:00 pm and check-out is by 11:00 am.

Does Fairmont Le Château Montebello have a spa?

Yes, the hotel has a Fairmont Spa with a variety of unique spa treatments available.

Is there a concierge service at Fairmont Le Château Montebello?

Let our concierge team help you plan the most memorable getaway. To reserve activities, to make dinner reservations or to learn more about our different services, please contact our team at 819 423 6341 or directly via email.
