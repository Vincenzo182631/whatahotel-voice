Inspired by the châteaux of the Swiss Alps, this 1930 resort on the Ottawa River was built with 10,000 giant red cedar logs, all cut and set by hand. Midway between Montreal and Ottawa, the "beautiful resort is great for couples and families." A six-sided fireplace with a central chimney is the focal point of the three-story atrium. Rooms mix rich woodwork with custom fabrics and rustic accessories and have windows that open to let in the fresh air; classic country colors are accented by vibrant reds, greens, and mustard yellows. "It has fun activities inside and out year-round, and fits anyone’s idea of what a Canadian lodge ought to be." Aux Chantignoles uses regional ingredients and serves "very good food compared with other places in the area."
