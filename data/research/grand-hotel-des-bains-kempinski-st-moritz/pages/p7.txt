# The Oasis: Alpine Spa Pool Parties

Discover The Oasis, a series of exclusive Saturday pool parties at Alpine Spa, featuring refreshing cocktails, live DJ sets and access to the Day Spa.

### The Oasis at Alpine Spa in St. Moritz

This summer, Alpine Spa invites you to discover The Oasis, an exclusive poolside experience at Grand Hotel des Bains Kempinski St. Moritz.

Every Saturday, unwind in the elegant surroundings of our pool area, enjoy refreshing cocktails and groove to the sounds of DJ Nicolo Cappi. Your entrance also includes access to the Day Spa, creating the perfect setting for a relaxed summer afternoon in St. Moritz.
