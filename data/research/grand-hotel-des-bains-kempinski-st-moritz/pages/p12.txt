# Activity Programme

### Activity Programme

Join us for these exciting activities that showcase the beauty, culture, and adventure of our surroundings. Each day offers a new experience that will create unforgettable memories of your stay

## Winter Activities

### Ice Skating

#### Ludains Ice Arena

The Ludains Ice Arena offers guests the chance to enjoy open-air ice skating on an artificial rink, located just 10 to 15 minutes' walk from the hotel. During the winter months, an additional natural ice rink is prepared for a more authentic ice skating experience. Equipment can be hired on-site.

#### Olympic Ice Rink

Glide across the meticulously groomed rink in front of the Kulm Country Club, the site of the 1928 and 1948 Winter Olympic Games.

**Experience Ice Skating**

### Sledding, Tobogganing & Snowtubing

#### Preda to Bergün

Europe’s longest illuminated toboggan run is a short journey away. The winding and adventurous route along the Albula railway line is perfect for families and thrill-seekers alike. From St. Moritz, you can reach Preda by train in about 30 minutes. There, you collect your sled and walk to the starting point. The 5 km run ends in Bergün, where you can either return the sled or hop on the train back to Preda for another thrilling ride. Adventurous guests can take the chairlift to Darlux for a steep descent to Bergün.

#### Muottas Muragl

Just 15 minutes from the hotel by bus or car, this sled run features 20 thrilling curves. The 705-meter vertical drop over 4 km promises plenty of action for thrill-seekers.

**Experience the Thrill of Sledging**

#### Snowtubing Maloja

For a fun winter activity, try snowtubing in Maloja. Using an inflatable ring, you can glide down snow-covered slopes – a perfect adventure for families and friends.

### Carriage Rides in the Snow

#### Lej da Staz

Experience the beauty of St. Moritz from the comfort of a horse-drawn carriage. Departing directly from the hotel entrance, the ride takes you along Lake St. Moritz to the secluded mountain lake, Lej da Staz, located in a nature preservation area where cars are not allowed. The nearby Staz Kiosk offers casual alpine snacks and drinks by the frozen lake.

#### Val Roseg Valley

From the Pontresina train station (a 10-minute drive from the hotel), horse carriages begin their journey into Val Roseg. After a one-hour ride through scenic landscapes, you arrive at the restaurant Roseg Gletscher, where you can enjoy traditional Swiss cuisine. Guests can return either on foot or by carriage.

#### Val Fex Valley

In Sils Maria, just 15 minutes away by car, horse carriages are available to take guests through the idyllic Val Fex Valley. Routes vary in length from 30 minutes to an hour.

### Freeride & Ski Touring

Experience the ultimate off-piste adventure with a day of freeriding on Corviglia, Corvatsch, or Diavolezza. Led by a local guide, you'll learn essential avalanche safety and freeriding techniques while enjoying fantastic powder snow runs.

#### Heliski

Fly over snow-capped peaks and glaciers on a helicopter ride, followed by a breathtaking descent under the guidance of an experienced mountain ski guide.

**Take to the Skies with a Heliskiing Experience**

#### Glüna Plaina

Skiing under the full moon on Diavolezza is a special experience. As the moon illuminates the mountain, the slopes are bathed in a magical light. After dinner at the Diavolezza mountain restaurant, guests can descend the mountain, taking in the stunning night scenery.

### Olympia Bob Run Guest Ride

For a thrilling experience, join a ride on the 1722-meter natural ice track at speeds of up to 135 km/h. In just 75 seconds, you'll complete this adrenaline-pumping run, safely nestled between the pilot and brakeman. The price is CHF 269 per person, and participants must be at least 16 years old.

### Winter Hiking

Explore the beauty of Engadin with winter hikes on frozen lakes, scenic trails, or snow-blanketed forests. Flat, family-friendly paths from Maloja to St. Moritz are ideal for strollers, while the picturesque walk from Sils into Val Fex offers a romantic winter escape.

### Tandem Paragliding & Hang-Gliding

Take to the skies with a tandem paragliding or hang-gliding experience. No experience is required; simply meet at Corviglia top station and enjoy the ride. Flights run daily from 10:00 to 16:00, weather permitting.

### Sleddog

Just 75 km from St. Moritz in Arnoga, you can visit the Husky Village for an unforgettable dog-sledding experience. Under the guidance of expert instructors, you’ll ride your team of huskies through the snow.

### Ice Climbing

Take your first steps in ice climbing under the watchful eye of experienced guides. With crampons and an ice axe, you'll feel a sense of accomplishment as you scale the ice wall.

### Cresta Run

The famous Cresta Run in St. Moritz offers an adrenaline-packed toboggan experience on a natural ice track. This thrilling ride attracts adventurers from around the world.

### Skijoring

Skijoring combines skiing with the excitement of being pulled by a horse. Originating in Scandinavia, it requires strong skiing skills and offers a unique thrill.

### Fatbiking & Winter Night Biking

Explore the snowy trails of the Engadin valley with a fatbike. These wide-tired bikes provide control and safety on snow. Rent fatbikes from local outlets or join a guided tour to discover the region.

### Winter Canyoning

In this winter adventure, you'll hike, climb, and glide along ziplines past frozen waterfalls and ice-covered cliffs. Safely secured by expert guides, you'll experience the thrill of canyoning in a stunning winter landscape.

### Ice Bathing

Take the plunge into icy waters and experience the rush of ice bathing. Known to improve circulation and boost immunity, ice swimming is an invigorating challenge. Please consult a physician before participating.

### Ice Fishing

Lake Sils offers the perfect setting for ice fishing during the winter months. Enjoy the peaceful atmosphere and scenic views while trying your hand at this unique winter activity.

### Climbing & Bouldering

Serlas Park offers an excellent climbing and bouldering facility with breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains. Equipment is available for hire, making it suitable for climbers of all levels.

### Bowling

Enjoy an evening of bowling at one of the region's historic or modern alleys, including Gasthaus Spinas, Grand Hotel Kronenhof, and Serlas Parc. Perfect for families or groups, reservations are required.

### Panoramic Train Rides

#### Bernina Express

Travel along the UNESCO World Heritage route from St. Moritz to Valposchiavo and Tirano on the Bernina Express. This scenic train ride offers breathtaking views of glaciers and mountain landscapes.

#### Glacier Express

The Glacier Express takes you on a scenic journey from Zermatt to St. Moritz, passing through stunning Alpine landscapes, including the Rhine Gorge and Oberalp Pass. This eight-hour ride offers unparalleled panoramic views of Switzerland's natural beauty.

**Discover the Glacier Express**

### All-Weather Activities

#### Tennis, Squash & Paddle

The Tennis Centre is just a short walk from the hotel and offers state-of-the-art facilities for tennis, squash, and paddle. Equipment rental and coaching are available.

#### Climbing & Bouldering

Serlas Park provides 450 sqm of climbing walls and 120 sqm of bouldering space, perfect for climbers of all levels. Equipment can be hired on-site.

#### Bowling

For a fun and competitive evening, visit one of the local bowling alleys at Gasthaus Spinas, Grand Hotel Kronenhof, or Serlas Parc. Reservations are required.

#### Museums

Discover the cultural and artistic heritage of the Engadin region by visiting its many museums, including the Segantini Museum, the National Park Centre, Nietzsche-Haus, and Museum Alpin. Explore a variety of art, sculpture, and ethnographic exhibitions that reflect the rich history of the area.
