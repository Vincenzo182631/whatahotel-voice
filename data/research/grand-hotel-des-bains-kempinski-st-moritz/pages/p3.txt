# Roomservice

### Roomservice

Experience luxury and comfort with our 24-hour room service, delivering exquisite dishes and refreshing drinks directly to your room or suite. Start your day with a delightful breakfast while taking in stunning views of the Engadin, setting a perfect tone for your adventures in St. Moritz. Enjoy a diverse range of international flavours and seasonal specialities for lunch and dinner, crafted to please every taste.

Our room service is flexible and accommodating, allowing for custom orders beyond the menu. Whether you desire a fine wine, a vibrant cocktail, or a refreshing non-alcoholic drink, our extensive selection is sure to satisfy. Relax, unwind, and let us make your stay unforgettable with our dedicated service.

## Breakfast Options

### Continental Breakfast

- Bakery Selection: Freshly baked buns, buttery croissants, and delectable pastries.
- Accompaniments: Mountain raw honey, a variety of jams, and rich butter.
- Hot Beverages: A selection of rich coffee, decaffeinated options, a range of teas, herbal infusions, hot chocolate, and warm milk.
- Milk Choices: Whole milk, low-fat milk, almond milk, and soy milk.
- Juices: A refreshing array of grapefruit, pineapple, carrot, and freshly squeezed orange juice.

### Morning Power Breakfast

- Energising Drinks: Choose between a revitalising smoothie with turmeric, ginger, mango, banana, and oat milk, or a fruity blend of pineapple, cherry, coconut water, and maple syrup.
- Healthy Options: Enjoy a choice of muesli, homemade fitness bars, organic yoghurt with fresh fruits and cereals, crunchy granola with mixed berries, and spelt muesli.
- Bakery Items: A variety of whole grain bread, breakfast buns, and croissants.
- Condiments: Low sugar jam, mountain raw honey, and light cream cheese.
- Beverages: A full range of hot drinks as listed above.

### European Breakfast

- Gourmet Delights: A selection of regional dried meats, fine cheeses, smoked fish, and fresh fillets.
- Bread Basket: House-baked bread, toast, croissants, and assorted pastries.
- Condiments: Mountain raw honey, artisanal jams, and Swiss butter.
- Yoghurt & Eggs: Organic plain yoghurt with berries, and eggs prepared to your liking.
- Savory Sides: Crispy bacon, regional sausages, grilled tomatoes, and mushrooms.

### À La Carte Breakfast

Specialty Dishes: Enjoy a variety of gourmet breakfast items including rich, savoury, and sweet options.

## Lunch & Dinner Options

### Sandwiches

Gourmet Options: Indulge in a selection of hearty and flavourful sandwiches, served with crispy fries or a fresh salad on the side.

- Fresh & Vibrant: Choose from a variety of fresh salads featuring crisp greens, rich cheeses, and delightful dressings.

### Soups & Pasta
