## A Tranquil Perspective Above Kuala Lumpur

Perched high above the dynamic Bangsar district, Alila Bangsar offers a calm, contemporary retreat with sweeping views of the city. Just moments from Kuala Lumpur’s culinary hotspots, cultural enclaves and main transit lines, it’s an ideal base for both exploration and escape.

Occupying the 35th to 40th floors, all our rooms and suites offer luxurious amenities and uninterrupted views of the Kuala Lumpur skyline.

### Entier

Savor French plates in a serene communal dining setting on the 41st floor.

Entier, an old French word meaning “whole” or “entire,” is the inspiration behind the restaurant’s nose-to-tail approach. The menu reflects this philosophy with dishes that highlight specific parts of the animal or vegetable, or focus on signature ingredients served in their entirety.
