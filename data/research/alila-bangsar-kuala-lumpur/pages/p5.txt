Guided by thoughtful instruction, you'll build your own terrarium, layering soil, stones and handpicked plants to create a living piece of art. Add decorative elements and explore creative ways to make it uniquely yours, a reflection of your mood, story or intention. As the session closes, share your design and the meaning behind it with others, then take your terrarium home—a lasting reminder of calm, creativity and your connection to nature.

**A Green Welcome** Begin with a calming introduction to the art of terrarium-making and the mindfulness benefits of working with nature.

**Succulent Science** Learn about the unique traits of succulents and their symbolism in resilience, growth and simplicity.

**Hands-On Creation** Design your own terrarium using curated materials. Choose your layout, layer your soil and bring your miniature world to life.

**A Living Reminder** Take home your terrarium as a symbol of serenity and a ritual of presence you can return to long after the workshop ends.

### Crafting Heritage: A Journey Through Peranakan Beading

Step into the delicate world of Peranakan beadwork, where color, patience and cultural pride converge in every stitch. In this guided workshop, learn how Straits Chinese communities have used embroidery and beadwork to express identity, celebrate tradition and mark life’s most meaningful milestones. Choose your palette, thread your needle and bring a floral motif to life using time-honored techniques passed down through generations. This isn’t just decorative art—it’s living heritage in your hands. Leave with your own handmade creation and a deeper appreciation for the stories held in craft.

**A Cultural Introduction** Begin with an overview of Peranakan history, including the significance of beadwork in garments, weddings and rituals.

**The Language of Beads** Discover the hidden meanings behind traditional motifs and color choices.

**Hands-On Crafting** Follow guided techniques to begin beading your own floral pattern, stitching tradition into each detail.

**A Legacy to Keep**

Take home your beaded piece and a printed guide on Peranakan design—your own entry into this vibrant cultural lineage."

### Into the Fold: An Exploration of Malaysia Through Origami

Step into the meditative world of Malaysian origami, where every fold carries intention, and every shape tells a story. In this hands-on workshop, you’ll explore the cultural roots of paper art in Malaysia—from Malay ketupat weaves to Chinese-influenced decorative folds. Guided by an experienced instructor, create iconic shapes like the hibiscus (Malaysia’s national flower) while learning the symbolic meanings behind each design. This isn’t just paper folding—it’s storytelling through stillness, creativity and care. Leave with a collection of your own folded pieces, along with a deeper appreciation for what emerges when you slow down and shape something by hand.

**A Paper Welcome** Begin with a warm introduction and the history of Malaysian origami traditions across cultures and ceremonies.

**Meaning in the Fold** Learn the symbolism behind traditional shapes—like the hibiscus, ketupat and birds of peace.

**Hands-on Crafting** Practice folding with guided support as you craft your own set of origami pieces using curated specialty paper.

**Takeaway & Reflection**

Leave with your creations and a printed booklet explaining the cultural meaning behind each fold—a quiet memory you can unfold again and again.
