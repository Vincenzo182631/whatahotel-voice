### Wonderful experience

This hotel was one of the best I have stayed at. Rooms were spacious, laid out well, wonderful bathroom/shower/sinks, and all facilities were top notch in terms of quality and condition. Snacks and a good coffee machine were provided on all floors. Swimming pool was wonderful to have, even though length swimming was a bit constrained. Very good exercise room. And the breakfast was also first class. One eccentricity is that breakfast is in the lobby, reception is floor 41, and then you use separate elevators to go down to the sleeping rooms. But it all works well.
