### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown is Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL). ATL is located approximately 11.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Atlanta Downtown does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
