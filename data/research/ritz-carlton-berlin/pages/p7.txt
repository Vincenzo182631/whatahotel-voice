Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Berlin
Glorious Winter Season
Immerse yourself into a festive and glamorous ambiance and lock forward to memorable experiences during the most wonderful time of the year. Culinary delights await you throughout the hotel, such as the traditional goose, opulent brunches, and festive highlights on Christmas days and the turn of the year.
The Ritz-Carlton, Berlin Champagne Brunch - Festive Edition
In a festive winter atmosphere, we invite you to indulge in a selection of exceptional festive culinary highlights. Of course, you can also enjoy our Champagne Brunch classics, such as poached Canadian lobster and the tempting truffle pasta from the Parmesan cheese. Enjoy Louis Roederer champagne in the famous Ritz-Carlton style, which will accompany you throughout the brunch spectacle. Live music provides the perfect atmosphere.

12:30 PM - 1:00 PM | Aperitif
1:00 PM - 4:00 PM | Brunch

New Year's Eve Experiences
New Year's Eve at POTS
Ring in the New Year with elegance and indulgence. Join us at 6:00 PM for a champagne reception with Louis Roederer Cristal, followed by a masterfully composed six-course menu from the POTS team. Paired with select wines, the evening promises culinary highlights, refined moments and exclusive indulgence to welcome 2027 in style.

New Year's Eve Party at Bar & Lounge
Experience a night of elegance and glamour in the exclusive setting of The Curtain Club and the adjacent The Lounge, with live entertainment and access to the legendary Ritz-Carlton New Year’s Eve party. Culinary delights include a New Year’s Eve étagère, an oyster bar, and Louis Roederer Champagne in free-flow. From 9 PM onwards, additional exquisite gourmet stations await on the Beletage.

The Ritz-Carlton New Year's Eve Party
Celebrate New Year's Eve in glamorous 1920s style at The Ritz-Carlton, Berlin. From 9 PM, enjoy live entertainment, dazzling shows and dancing into the early hours. Indulge in gourmet stations with oysters, truffle pasta and more, alongside free-flowing Louis Roederer Champagne and cocktails - culminating in a festive midnight countdown in our grand ballroom.
