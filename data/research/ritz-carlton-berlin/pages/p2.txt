The Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge
The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining intimacy, comfort and exclusivity with the personalized service and attention to detail that are the hallmark of The Ritz-Carlton. This oasis is often referred to as a hotel within a hotel, in a well-appointed, relaxed lounge environment. The Ritz-Carlton, Berlin features a Club Lounge for guests seeking a heightened level of luxury. Surrounded by finest materials and hand selected furniture, the Ladies and Gentlemen of the hotel pamper their guests all day long with culinary delicacies, refined cocktails and personalized service. Our Club Lounge is perfectly located on the top floor with views of the iconic Tiergarten, Berlin's biggest park. Our lounge location allows our guests the opportunity to enjoy the seasonal views of the park: a haven for experiential travelers above the rooftops of Berlin!

The Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge
Services & Distinctive Amenities
Three different buffets entice visitors with culinary delicacies throughout the day. Sustainable, regional, and seasonal – that is the theme at The Ritz-Carlton, Berlin. We obtain fish, meat, and cheese from regional suppliers who work with organic fertilizing methods and practice humane animal husbandry. For breakfast, the kitchen team serves the hotel’s own honey produced by up to 400,000 honey bees during the summer months on the hotel roof. Club guests may enjoy complimentary tea and coffee delivered with their wake-up call. As a Club Level guest, you receive complimentary pressing of two garments per day.

The Ritz-Carlton Penthouse
With spacious 285 square meters and located on the 12th floor, The Ritz-Carlton Penthouse is the hotel’s largest and most lavishly appointed suite, presenting a truly elegant expression of luxury and panoramic views over Berlin. Thoughtfully curated down to the smallest detail, The Ritz-Carlton Penthouse features three bedrooms, a bathroom, a spacious living area with its own library and fully equipped kitchen, as well as a large walk-in closet and built-in fitness station.

The Pawfect Stay
Experience Berlin with Your Dog
With The Pawfect Stay Arrangement, we create the perfect stay for you and your four-legged friend. The arrangement includes a cozy dog bed with a blanket in two sizes, bowls, treats, wet wipes, doggy bags and a little cuddly plush lion. With Tiergarten park just a few steps away, the hotel is also located in a very dog-friendly area. With The Pawfect Concierge, a dog guide for Berlin, our concierge team has compiled important addresses for all eventualities, such as veterinarians, dog groomers, dog sitters and dog stores. Even in culinary terms, the dog stay is in no way inferior to the human stay at The Ritz-Carlton, Berlin.
