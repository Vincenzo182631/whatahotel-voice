Creative Dining Concepts in our Berlin Restaurants
Dining
Explore the hotel's refined places to eat in Berlin that guests can enjoy, from reinvented German cuisine at POTS to afternoon tea at The Lounge and cocktails with live music at The Curtain Club. In summer, the Secret Champagne Garden serves champagne and selected cocktails al fresco, while the hotel also offers an exclusive champagne selection by the glass with 28 varieties. Guests praise the high-quality dining and renowned bar experience, while celebrated offerings such as Champagne Brunch, high tea, and bespoke culinary events complete the dining scene.

In-Room Dining
In-Room Dining at The Ritz-Carlton, Berlin features an extensive selection of the hotel's cuisine and beverage options to enhance your stay. Cigar Club
An insider tip and an ideal retreat for cigar lovers. The Cigar Club welcomes epicures daily between 2.00 pm. - midnight to taste and enjoy exquisite, handmade cigars together with the Aficionado of the house, in a relaxed atmosphere. About 20 selected cigars will be on offer. For cigar lovers who want to enjoy the dignified atmosphere of the lounge with their own cigars, the club charges a cut fee.
