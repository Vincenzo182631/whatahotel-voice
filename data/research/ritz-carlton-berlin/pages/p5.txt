Brandenburg Gate
The Brandenburg Gate is Berlin's most famous landmark and a must-see for all visitors. A symbol of Berlin and German division during the Cold War, it is now a national symbol of peace and unity.
Potsdamer Platz
Berlin's vibrant Potsdamer Platz is the most striking example of the urban renewal and has been redeveloped as the new center of the city after the fall of the Wall.
Alexanderplatz & TV Tower
The famous Alexanderplatz is one of the liveliest places in the capital. The square and its attractions - such as the TV Tower, the World Clock, and the Fountain of Friendship between Nations - are popular with tourists from all around the world.
Museum Island
Museum Island is a unique ensemble of five museums in the center of Berlin. The entire complex is listed by as a Unesco World Heritage Site. The ensemble of historic buildings is among the most beautiful architecture that Berlin has to offer.
Tiergarten Park
Popular among locals and visitors alike, the Tiergarten is Berlin's largest and most frequented inner-city park. It is ideal for a stroll, outdoor activities or a picnic.
Remnants Of The Former Berlin Wall
Until 1989, the Berlin Wall marked the border between East Berlin and West of Berlin. Today only remnants of the concrete barrier remain. Mainly re-positioned, visitors may receive an impression of the city's division, but not far from Potsdamer Platz there is still a 200-meter-long original piece of the Berlin Wall.
