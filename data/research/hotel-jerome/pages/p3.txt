## 2026 Michelin Guide Recommended

## Prospect

Prospect at Hotel Jerome brings a fresh perspective to mountain dining with a menu that showcases bold, seasonal flavors rooted in local ingredients, an homage to the region’s natural rhythm and bounty. The Michelin Guide Recommended restaurant is further elevated by the addition of custom artwork from world-renowned photographer David Yarrow, whose dramatic Western portraits echo the spirit of the Rockies. The new seasonal à la carte offerings celebrate high-altitude ingredients, continuing Prospect’s tradition of inventive, ingredient-led cuisine in a setting that feels both timeless and unmistakably Aspen.

## Discover Bad Harriet

Tucked beneath the historic Aspen Times building, Bad Harriet is Hotel Jerome’s intimate cocktail bar, where inventive libations, refined small bites and a moody, subterranean setting come together for an unforgettable night in Aspen. Named after Harriet Wheeler, wife of Jerome B. Wheeler, the space pays tribute to bold and influential women throughout history while blending timeless glamour with a modern edge. Thoughtfully crafted cocktails, late-night energy and an ever-evolving menu make Bad Harriet a favorite for both locals and visitors alike.

- 7PM – 1AM Wednesday-Sunday

- Live DJ performances Thursday-Saturday from 10PM – 1AM

- Reservations recommended but not required

  ### J-Bar

  Cowboys and miners once rode their horses through its doors, 10th Mountain Division soldiers gathered here over pints after a day of training on the slopes, and the late Hunter S. Thompson turned it into his de facto office when he was running for sheriff. With its lively buzz and Old West vibe, the 19th century bar remains the town’s social hub, where ski bums rub elbows with socialites over craft beers, vintage-inspired cocktails and Aspen’s best burger. Make sure to take a peek at the original Chinoiserie Chippendale till, signed by every bartender to work at J-Bar over the years.

  - 11:45AM – 11PM Sunday-Wednesday

  - 11:45AM – 12AM Thursday-Saturday

## Felix Roasting Co.

Felix Roasting Co. has unveiled its first location outside of New York City at the historic Hotel Jerome. Founded by Matt Moinian and award-winning interior designer Ken Fulk, Felix is a contemporary coffee concept and roasting company from an eclectic team of visionaries set out to redefine coffee culture. The Aspen outpost brings the best of Felix to the Rockies, serving their signature hot and cold beverages, delicate bites and an extensive menu of specialty drinks, each meticulously infused with Felix’s house-made non-dairy milks, proprietary syrups and tonics and custom spice blends.
