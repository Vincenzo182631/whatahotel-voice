##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is Hotel Jerome located?** Hotel Jerome is located at 330 East Main Street in the heart of downtown Aspen, within walking distance of the city’s shops, galleries, restaurants and nightlife and just minutes from Aspen Mountain.

**How far is Hotel Jerome from the airport?** Hotel Jerome is approximately three miles, or 15 minutes, from Aspen/Pitkin County Airport (ASE). Complimentary airport transportation is available upon arrival by request, while private pickups can also be arranged in advance through the concierge for an additional fee. Eagle County Regional Airport (EGE) is the closest commercial alternative to Aspen/Pitkin County Airport (ASE) although Grand Junction Regional Airport (GJT) is located 125 miles north of Aspen along I-70, making the drive approximately two hours through Glenwood Canyon. Denver International Airport (DEN) is 220 miles away for an approximate 3.5-hour drive and offers both private and commercial flights. Rifle Garfield County Airport (RIL) is 61 miles away for private aviation. Montrose Regional Airport (MTJ) is 134 miles for private and commercial flights approximately 3 hours to Aspen, Colorado.

**Is Hotel Jerome a good fit for couples?** Yes. Hotel Jerome is an ideal setting for a romantic Aspen getaway, pairing refined rooms and suites with intimate dining, cocktails and restorative treatments at Yarrow Spa. Its downtown location also makes it easy for couples to experience Aspen’s celebrated dining, culture, nightlife and year-round mountain adventures.

**When is the best time to visit Hotel Jerome?** Hotel Jerome offers distinctly different experiences throughout the year. Winter is ideal for skiing, snow sports and Aspen’s lively après scene, while summer brings hiking, biking, outdoor adventure and a vibrant calendar of cultural events. Hotel Jerome typically closes for a period in spring, so guests should confirm seasonal operating dates when planning a stay.

**What experiences and activities are available at Hotel Jerome?** Hotel Jerome offers year-round experiences that showcase Aspen’s mountains, culture and adventurous spirit. Depending on the season, guests can enjoy skiing, hiking, horseback riding, rafting, fly-fishing, cycling and snowmobiling alongside culinary experiences, cultural programming and personalized adventures arranged through the hotel.

**Does Hotel Jerome have a spa?** Yes. Yarrow Spa is Hotel Jerome’s intimate wellness retreat, with massages, facials and restorative body treatments inspired by Aspen’s alpine environment. Treatments incorporate botanicals and natural elements found in the Rocky Mountains, with experiences designed to restore hydration, balance and vitality after days spent exploring the mountains.

**What ski amenities and services does Hotel Jerome offer?** Hotel Jerome makes it easy to experience all four Aspen Snowmass mountains, with an on-property ski concierge, ski storage and complimentary shuttle service. The concierge can also assist with lift tickets, ski school and rentals, while ski transfer services make it convenient to explore a different mountain each day.
