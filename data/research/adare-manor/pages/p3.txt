### Limerick’s First Michelin Star Restaurant

Experience a gracious evening of fine-dining by candle light our Michelin star Oak Room restaurant, where Head Chef Michael Tweedie’s seasonal menu has distilled the essence of Ireland’s best artisan ingredients, paired with our wine collection, lovingly curated by our sommeliers.

In the mornings, the magnificent Gallery offers a breakfast menu designed to give you an inspiring start to the day. Our Afternoon Tea in The Gallery is a refined indulgence, where our expert Executive Pastry Chef has created a divine menu of delightful confections, pastries, and hand-cut sandwiches, served with a selection of exquisite teas from around the world.

For freshly brewed coffee or light bites to enjoy alongside a glass of champagne or an aperitif before dinner, The Drawing Room is a luxurious setting overlooking the beautiful formal gardens. Connoisseurs of fine spirits and imaginative libations will find a warm welcome in The Tack Room, where a menu of savoury snacks and sharing platters are the perfect companion to a world-class whiskey collection and specialty cocktail list. Whatever you might be in the mood for, the talented and passionate culinary team at Adare Manor has perfected a menu to match.

What dining venues/restaurants are available at Adare Manor?

Adare Manor offers a collection of distinct dining experiences, including the Michelin-starred The Oak Room, contemporary Japanese cuisine at Ōku, the relaxed atmosphere of The Carriage House, and a variety of informal dining and lounge options throughout the estate. Each venue offers its own style, setting, and culinary focus. Explore our restaurants and reserve your table to experience the finest of dining at Adare Manor.

What kind of cuisine and dining experience does The Oak Room offer?

The Oak Room is Adare Manor’s Michelin-starred fine dining restaurant, offering an elegant tasting menu experience that celebrates seasonal Irish ingredients. The atmosphere is refined and intimate, with a focus on craftsmanship, presentation, and exceptional service. Reserve your table to enjoy an unforgettable Michelin-starred dining experience.

What is the dining style and atmosphere at The Carriage House?

The Carriage House offers relaxed elegance in a beautifully renovated setting just a short walk from the Manor House, with sweeping views across the estate. The venue features a restaurant, bar, and terrace, creating a welcoming destination throughout the day and evening. Guests can enjoy light lunches, relaxed dinners, or drinks in a lively and inviting atmosphere. Book your table and enjoy relaxed dining, drinks, or terrace views at The Carriage House.

Is there a dress code for dining at Adare Manor?

Dress codes vary slightly by venue. Smart casual attire is appropriate for most restaurants across the estate. For The Oak Room, guests are encouraged to dress in elegant or smart attire to reflect the refined atmosphere of the Michelin-starred dining experience. Sportswear, shorts, and casual footwear are generally discouraged in the evening, particularly in The Oak Room. We invite you to reserve your table and enjoy a refined dining experience at Adare Manor.

Children are very welcome at Adare Manor. The Carriage House and other casual dining venues are particularly suited to family dining, and children’s menu options are available.
