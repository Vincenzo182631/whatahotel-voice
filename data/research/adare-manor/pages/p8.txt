What are the gourmet experiences available at Adare Manor? What dining options are available?

Adare Manor offers curated gourmet stays including A Taste of Adare Manor, featuring a Michelin-starred dinner at The Oak Room; The Adare Manor Escape, combining luxury accommodation with dining at The Carriage House; The Afternoon Tea Retreat, celebrating the tradition of Champagne Afternoon Tea; and An Adare Manor Celebration, a multi-night experience with fine dining, champagne, and indulgent touches.
