What kinds of activities are available at Adare Manor?

Adare Manor offers a wide range of estate activities, from championship golf and outdoor pursuits to wellness experiences, indoor leisure, and cultural exploration. Guests can tailor their stay to be as active or as relaxed as they wish. In addition to experiences on the estate, there are also many wonderful activities available nearby. Our Concierge team would be delighted to recommend and arrange off-site experiences such as horse riding, local excursions, and other regional activities, helping guests explore the surrounding area during their stay.

Enquire Now

Are there family-friendly / children’s activities available?

Our estate offers a variety of family-friendly experiences designed for younger guests, alongside activities that families can enjoy together. Our team can recommend age-appropriate options and help plan a balanced itinerary.

Enquire Now

Can I explore the history and the estate architecture at Adare Manor?

Guests can step back in time through our self-guided architecture and garden audio tour of the Manor House. These immersive guides trace the history of Adare Manor, telling the story of the Earls of Dunraven and the generations who lived here, as well as the remarkable 30-year process of building the Manor House. Guests can also enjoy our History of Adare Manor film, shown in our private cinema, which brings the estate’s rich past to life through captivating storytelling and visuals. Dedicated versions of the audio tour are available for both adults and children aged 6–12, offering a captivating journey through the estate’s heritage.

Learn More

What outdoor / adventure-type activities are possible (e.g. sport, nature)?

Outdoor pursuits include golf, cycling, falconry, fishing, archery, axe throwing, gundogs, clay pigeon shooting, horse and carriage tour and estate walks, along with a range of seasonal and nature-based experiences. Activities take place across the estate’s parkland, woodland, and river surroundings.

Enquire Now

Is there fitness / wellness / leisure-type activity – for those not seeking “adventure”?

Guests can enjoy wellness experiences at the Spa, alongside fitness and leisure facilities at The Padel Club, which include swimming, gym and studio, and padel courts. Combined with relaxed estate walks, these options are ideal for restoration, light recreation, and gentle activity.

The Padel Club

Do I need to stay at the Manor to take part in these activities?

Most estate activities are reserved for guests staying at Adare Manor. Availability for non-resident visitors may vary depending on the activity and the season.

Contact Us

Are activities subject to extra cost or are they included in the stay?

Some activities are included as part of your stay, while others are offered at an additional charge. Our reservations or concierge team can advise on pricing and availability when planning your itinerary.

Enquire Now

Can I combine multiple activities during my stay?

Many guests enjoy combining several activities across their visit. Our team can help curate a personalised itinerary to suit your interests and schedule.

Enquire Now

Are there guided tours, or is it self-guided only?

Both options are available. Certain experiences are offered as guided tours, while guests may also enjoy self-guided exploration – including our architecture and garden audio tour – allowing you to discover the estate at your own pace.

Learn More

What if I’m interested in indoor leisure rather than outdoor or sport activities?

Adare Manor offers a variety of indoor leisure experiences, including spa and wellness treatments, the estate cinema, browsing the boutique, and visiting Harry Lowes Chocolate Shop. Guests can also enjoy relaxed lounges and dining spaces – ideal for a quieter, more leisurely pace.
