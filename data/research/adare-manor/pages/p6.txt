### We are proud to host the Ryder Cup 2027 in Adare Manor

The Golf Course at Adare Manor will reopen for the 2026 season for resort residents to play 18 holes from the 26th of May until the 18th of October 2026. Thereafter, the golf course will remain closed for the winter season. Please note that the golf course is closed every Monday, excluding bank holidays.

Masterfully designed, magnificently maintained, and perfectly set within the glorious estate of one of Europe’s most spectacular manor house hotels, The Golf Course at Adare Manor is ready to take its place among the best championship courses in the world.

Celebrated golf architect Tom Fazio has taken what was already an award-winning course and transformed it into a parkland paradise befitting a country in the top tier of global golfing destinations. The new course has been created in harmony with the outstanding natural beauty of its surroundings: lush, softly rolling terrain, mature tree scaping, and the sweeping banks of the River Maigue.

Each hole of The Golf Course at Adare Manor could be crowned the feature hole: each one has been entirely redesigned and reconstructed, with the very latest technology and course innovations, creating a challenging, characterful, and profoundly satisfying playing experience for golfers of every level. The final result is a course that is at once strategic and soulful, playable in all conditions, and future-proofed in preparation for premiere amateur and professional golf events. Cared for by an experienced, passionate team of devoted greenkeepers, the condition of this course and its velvet-smooth Pure Distinction bent grass greens is unrivalled.

### Where Champions Unwind: The Carriage House

Off the course, this commitment to perfection continues in the perfectly appointed clubhouse. Luxurious, stylish, with a modern jazz-age sensibility and every convenience and indulgence you could require, The Carriage House is a worthy counterpart to Europe’s finest new golf course. Taken together, The Golf Course at Adare Manor provides an unforgettable experience designed to inspire, challenge, and captivate the most demanding and spirited golf enthusiasts in the world.

What kind of golf course does Adare Manor offer?

The Golf Course at Adare Manor is an 18-hole championship-standard parkland course, set within the estate’s mature landscape along the banks of the River Maigue. Designed to be both strategic and scenic, it blends beautifully with its natural surroundings and offers a world-class playing experience. Explore our golf experiences and plan your visit to Adare Manor.

Golf Experiences

How long is the course and what is its par?

The course measures approximately 7,509 yards with a Par 72 from the championship tees, with multiple teeing options to accommodate golfers of varying ability levels. Discover more about the championship golf experience at Adare Manor.

The Golf Course

What makes the course special – design, layout or quality?

Every hole at Adare Manor has been redesigned and reconstructed to championship-level standards with the latest course technology and innovations. The widely acclaimed layout demands thoughtful shot placement, rewards bold play, and highlights the natural terrain – ensuring a memorable challenge for golfers of every level. Learn more about the golf course and experiences available at Adare Manor.

Golf Course

Is the golf course open to hotel guests / public or only club members?

The Golf Course at Adare Manor is part of the resort experience, and priority access is given to guests staying at Adare Manor – particularly through golf reservations and stay-and-play packages. While tee times for non-guests may be limited or subject to availability, staying at Adare Manor is the most reliable way to secure a round during your visit. Enquire Now.

Enquire Now

When is the golf season at Adare Manor?

The Golf Course at Adare Manor typically operates from late May through mid-October each year, offering prime playing conditions throughout the Irish golf season. Exact dates may vary from season to season to accommodate course works and ongoing improvements, ensuring the highest possible playing standards. We recommend contacting our reservations or golf team for exact dates.

Enquire Now

What facilities and services does the golf course offer (clubhouse, equipment hire, pro shop, etc.)?

The golf course offers a full range of world-class facilities designed to ensure a seamless experience from arrival to the final putt. Guests are welcomed at the dedicated golf bag drop near The Carriage House, where our golf team assists with equipment and introductions to caddies. Facilities include luxurious locker rooms with guest amenities, a fully stocked Golf Shop offering premium merchandise and Ryder Cup x Adare Manor collections, and extensive practice facilities including a driving range, short-game areas and a Pure Distinction bent grass putting green. Guests can also enjoy refreshments and light meals at the Halfway House or at The Carriage House Bar & Restaurant overlooking the course. Learn More about the Golf Experience at Adare Manor.

Golf Experience

Has the course hosted any major tournaments or events?

Adare Manor has hosted events like the Irish Open and the J.P. McManus Pro-Am, and it is the selected venue for the 2027 Ryder Cup, placing it among the most prestigious golf destinations in Europe. Learn More about the 2027 Ryder Cup.

2027 Ryder Cup

What is the atmosphere or style of the course – more for serious golfers or casual players too?

The course provides a championship challenge for experienced golfers while offering an enjoyable and welcoming atmosphere for players of many abilities. Each round begins with a warm welcome from the First Tee Starter, who shares course conditions, pin positions and helpful insights before play begins. Throughout the round, player assistants are on hand to support guests and ensure a smooth pace of play, while refreshments are available from the Halfway House and drinks buggy service. Explore more leisure experiences at Adare Manor.

Leisure Activities

How do I book a tee time – and are there restrictions or requirements (e.g. must stay at the hotel)?

Tee times can be arranged through the golf or reservations team at Adare Manor, with priority typically given to resort guests. On arrival, guests are welcomed by the golf team at the bag drop and guided through the experience – from the Golf Shop check-in and locker rooms to the practice facilities before heading to the first tee. Booking ahead is strongly recommended, particularly during peak season or around major events. Contact our team to plan your golf experience at Adare Manor.

Contact Us

What should I know about the course’s maintenance and playing conditions?

The course is meticulously maintained with championship-level agronomy, fast and true putting surfaces, and strategic fairway and green complexes. Throughout your round, our golf operations team ensures the course is presented to the highest standard, while dedicated staff are available to assist both on and off the course. At the end of your round, our team will care for your equipment and ensure everything is returned to your room or securely stored for your next game. Learn more about golfing at Adare Manor.

Learn More

When is the Ryder Cup taking place in Adare Manor?

Adare Manor will host the Ryder Cup from 13–19 September 2027, welcoming one of golf’s most prestigious international tournaments to the estate. Learn More about The Ryder Cup.

Learn More

Which holes are considered the signature holes at the Golf Course at Adare Manor?

While the entire course is designed to championship standard, golfers often highlight the closing stretch as especially memorable. In particular, the dramatic 15th and 18th holes are frequently considered signature moments, combining strategic design with striking views of the River Maigue and Manor House. These holes showcase the course’s bold green complexes, immaculate conditioning, and scenic setting – a fitting finish to a world-class round. Explore the championship golf experience at Adare Manor.

Explore

Are golf carts, clubs, or equipment available to hire?

Yes. GPS-equipped golf carts, premium club hire, and electric trolleys are available through the Pro Shop, along with a range of equipment from leading golf brands to support your round. Learn more about golf facilities and services at Adare Manor.

Learn More

Do I have to use a caddie when playing at Adare Manor?

All golfers are required to use our elite caddie programme to ensure a seamless experience on the course. Your caddie will meet you before your round, assist you during warm-up at the practice facilities and guide you throughout the course with expert knowledge of yardages, strategy and course conditions. To maintain a highly personalised service, double-bag caddies are not offered; however, a forecaddie may accompany two golfers sharing a drive cart. Discover more about golfing experiences at Adare Manor.

Discover

Can I play golf at Adare Manor if I’m not staying at the hotel?

Priority access to tee times is reserved for guests staying at Adare Manor. Limited availability may be offered to non-resident visitors depending on the season and course schedule, and advance enquiry with the golf reservations team is essential. Explore our stay and play packages at Adare Manor.
