### Welcome to The Spa at Adare Manor, a sanctuary of rest and relaxation.

The atmosphere surrounding The Spa at Adare Manor is one of tranquillity and peace. This secluded retreat is an oasis of calm for even the weariest traveller, where a spirit of heritage and elegance is evident throughout our treatment rooms. A truly unique experience, The Spa at Adare Manor is proud to be the first in Ireland to offer customised facial and body treatments featuring 111SPA/CLINIC.

The ultimate luxury experience, elevated with medically-inspired innovation and 111SKIN products. The Spa at Adare Manor also boasts private manicure and pedicure rooms, a relaxation pool for guests of The Spa only and a new relaxation room. We invite you to surrender to the full experience, including extraordinary hands-on treatments from expert therapists, each designed to leave you feeling indulged and renewed. Afterwards, when you emerge into the vast brilliance and serenity of our grounds, you will carry the restorative power of our spa experience with you.

The Spa at Adare Manor has five exquisitely appointed treatment rooms. Our availability can sometimes be as precious as the moments of serenity you seek. As such, we highly recommend advanced booking to secure your preferred time slot and treatment. By planning ahead and reserving your appointment in advance, you ensure not only your seamless access to our array of indulgent treatments but also contribute to a smoother experience for all our cherished guests..

What amenities does The Spa at Adare Manor offer?

The Spa at Adare Manor offers a range of luxury wellness amenities including a relaxation lounge and thermal facilities such as an exclusive heated relaxation pool and infrared sauna. Guests are encouraged to use the relaxation pool and infrared sauna prior to their spa treatments to promote total relaxation. Book your stay and reserve your treatment at The Spa at Adare Manor.

Book Now

What types of treatments are offered at The Spa at Adare Manor?

The Spa at Adare Manor offers a range of body and massage treatments designed to promote relaxation and overall wellbeing. These include aromatherapy massage, Swedish massage, deep tissue massage, hot stone massage, cryotherapy body tension release treatments, maternity treatments, detox body therapies, and restorative body treatments such as the Renewing Rose Body Envelopment and Ultimate Detox Reviver. Explore our full spa treatment menu.

Spa Brochure

Should I book The Spa at Adare Manor in advance?

Guests are strongly encouraged to book spa treatments in advance. Due to high demand and limited availability, it is recommended to reserve treatments at the time of booking your stay or at least four to six weeks prior to your visit. Plan ahead and reserve your spa treatment to ensure availability during your stay.

What skincare products are used in The Spa at Adare Manor?

The Spa at Adare Manor uses 111SKIN products within its treatments. These medically inspired skincare products are incorporated into customised facial and body therapies. Experience the benefits of 111SKIN by booking a facial or spa treatment at Adare Manor.

Enquire Now

How long should I allow for a spa visit?

The duration of a spa visit will depend on the treatment selected; however, guests are encouraged to allow sufficient time to enjoy both their treatment and the spa’s relaxation and hydrotherapy facilities. Browse our spa treatments and plan your relaxing visit to The Spa at Adare Manor.

Spa Brochure

What should I bring with me to visit The Spa at Adare Manor?

Guests are advised to bring swimwear if they wish to use the hydrotherapy facilities. Comfortable clothing is also recommended for before and after treatments. Reserve your spa treatment and prepare to unwind at The Spa at Adare Manor.

Enquire Now

Do I need to be a guest to book The Spa at Adare Manor?

The Spa at Adare Manor is reserved for hotel guests. Guests must be aged 16 or over to receive facials and 18 or over to receive massage treatments. Book your stay at Adare Manor to enjoy access to The Spa and its wellness experiences.

Book Now

Can I use the spa facilities without booking a treatment?

Spa facilities are reserved for guests who have a treatment booked. However, hotel guests have complimentary access to the leisure facilities located within The Padel Club. Reserve your spa treatment to enjoy access to the relaxation and hydrotherapy facilities.

What makes The Spa at Adare Manor unique compared to other luxury spas?

The Spa at Adare Manor is the first spa in Ireland to offer customised treatments using 111SKIN products. Its medically inspired approach to wellness and nature-focused philosophy distinguish it from other luxury spa experiences. Discover the unique wellness journey awaiting you and book your spa experience at Adare Manor.
