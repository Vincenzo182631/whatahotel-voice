## THE _iconic_ LANDMARK IN THE HEART OF BAKU

Enter a world of unparalleled luxury at Baku's most iconic address - Fairmont Baku, Flame Towers, your slice of opulence in Azerbaijan. Located at the Flame Towers overlooking the medieval Old City and Caspian Sea, our luxury 5-star hotel in Baku carves out the perfect setting for unforgettable moments with its 299 rooms and suites, and 19 serviced hotel apartments.

### A stunning sanctuary of rest, relaxation and pure luxury is yours

Our highly sought-after array of guest rooms, suites and serviced apartments designed to exceed 5-star standards. Bathed in natural light from floor-to-ceiling windows, our luxury hotel rooms immerse you in the spirit of Baku with sweeping views of the Caspian Sea or the incredible city.
