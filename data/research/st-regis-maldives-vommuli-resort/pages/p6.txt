## Stay Connected, to Tailor Your Bespoke Holiday

Your festive escape begins with you. Share your preferences and interests with our team, allowing us to tailor your stay around the experiences and indulgences that matter most to you. Contact us for more details.

Is The St.Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort suitable for multi-generational family festive holidays?

Yes. The resort is ideally suited for multi-generational family getaways, offering spacious two-bedroom and three-bedroom villa residences, family-friendly dining, children's experiences, marine adventures, watersports, wellness activities, and dedicated festive programming designed to be enjoyed together across all generations.

What makes a festive stay at The St.Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort unique?

Nestled within a secluded private island sanctuary in Dhaalu Atoll, far removed from the pace of everyday life, The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort offers an unparalleled setting of privacy, tranquillity, and breathtaking ocean vistas. Against this backdrop of natural beauty, an exclusively curated festive calendar unfolds through signature celebrations, immersive dining experiences, family traditions, and thoughtfully crafted island moments designed to elevate the season for our Luminaries.

What festive celebrations can guests expect at The St.Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort?

Our Luminaries can look forward to a thoughtfully curated festive programme featuring Christmas traditions, New Year's Eve celebrations, gala dinners, family activities, wellness experiences, and immersive island events. From the annual Tree Lighting Ceremony and festive dining journeys to signature St.Regis rituals and year-end celebrations, each occasion is designed to create meaningful moments throughout the holiday season.

How does the St. Regis Butler Service enhance my festive stay?

The legendary St.Regis Butler Service ensures every aspect of your island sanctuary escape is thoughtfully arranged with effortless ease. From personalized pre‑arrival planning and preferred villa arrangements to festive dining reservations, family activities, wellness experiences, and special celebrations, every detail is tailored to your preferences before and throughout your stay. As your journey draws to a close, the Butler Service continues to provide attentive assistance through departure arrangements, ensuring a seamless experience from arrival to farewell.

Can I reserve my festive stay online?

Yes. Guests may reserve our Festive Getaway package directly through this page or via our Offers page. Available for stays between 15 December 2026 and 15 January 2027, the package includes preferred privileges for those reserving in advance, thoughtfully designed to enhance the festive season within our island sanctuary.

How can I reserve my festive stay and secure preferred experiences in advance?

Our Reservations team is delighted to assist with advance arrangements, including preferred villa selections, festive dining reservations, wellness journeys, family activities, and special celebrations. Early reservations are recommended to ensure availability for preferred festive experiences throughout the season.

Connect with us at reservations.vommuli@stregis.com
