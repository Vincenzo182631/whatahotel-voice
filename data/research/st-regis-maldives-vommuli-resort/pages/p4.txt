# St. Regis Maldives Resort Dining

## Dining

Explore a delightful range of dining options at The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort. Modern Japanese Kaiseki-style cuisine awaits you at T•Pan, hidden away at the edge of the white sand beach. Our signature restaurant, Alba, offers dishes inspired by locally sourced ingredients. Visit Crust & Craft, our family-friendly pizzeria, and design your own pizza with traditional Italian and other delicious toppings. Relax with a sunset cocktail at The Whale Bar before wandering inland to dine at Cargo. Enjoy Eastern haute cuisine at Orientale, our Maldives oceanfront restaurant, set in an airy and elegant space. No matter your tastes, our restaurants and bars are sure to delight you during your stay.

Restaurant Attire

Guests are invited to dress in Resort Casual attire, reflecting the restaurant’s elegant setting. Tank tops, swimwear, cover-ups, and gym attire are kindly discouraged.

## Luxe Culinary Journey

Discover a world of gastronomic wonders at The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort. Our year is packed with a diverse array of culinary events, each promising an unforgettable experience. From the expertise of renowned guest chefs to unique dining adventures, we are set to tantalize your taste buds. Immerse yourself in an array of events that blend exquisite flavors with the breathtaking beauty of the Indian Ocean at our Maldives beachfront restaurants. Whether it is a special chef's table, an innovative mixology session, or a themed dining extravaganza, each event is a testament to our commitment to culinary excellence. Join us in our island paradise for these epicurean delights, where every meal becomes a moment to cherish.

### Exclusive Private Dining Experiences

Whether you want an elevated level of intimacy to complement your honeymoon experience, a delightful private setting to propose to your special someone or an extra touch of luxury added to your fine dining experience, our restaurants in the Maldives offer private dining arrangements that create timeless memories shared between you and your loved one. Please contact our St. Regis butler service to make arrangements.

Starlight Dinner

Relax under the stars as they fill the night sky with a romantic private dining experience set on one of Vommuli Island’s pristine white sand beaches, and surrounded with illuminating candlelight and festive lanterns all with your toes in the sand. With the soothing sounds of the waves crashing along the shoreline, enjoy one of the three beach barbecue style menus.

The Levant Night

For a truly majestic private beach dining experience, this Arabian Bedouin style setting is arranged with an exotic carpet, bedouin cushions, low tables and enchanting Arabic lanterns. Surrounded by the soothing sounds of paradise on Maldives Island, enjoy Arabic style fine dining with live barbecue menus that feature hot & cold Arabic Mezze with meats and/or seafood, and dessert to finish.

Decanter

Sample something unique at this underground wine cellar featuring a five-course private dining experience paired with an inspired selection of prestigious vintages. Our executive chef and head sommelier meticulously plan every meal, promising something special.

Sandbank Picnic

Escape to a private island sanctuary with your loved one and indulge under the brilliant sun on an island destination picnic: the ultimate romantic getaway. Time will freeze as you delight in each other's presence, accompanied by nothing other than a glass of sparkling Champagne and a sumptuous picnic set.

Floating Breakfast

Picture yourself in Maldivian paradise, where the horizon blends sea and sky. At St. Regis, awaken to a culinary masterpiece served on a floating tray in your private plunge pool. Savor gourmet delights, tropical juices, and ocean vistas with your loved one. It's not just a meal; it's an immersive symphony of opulence and nature, a memory that lingers.

## The Island Mary

Inspired by the distinctive flavors and pristine landscape of the Maldives, the Island Mary complements the classic ingredients of a traditional Bloody Mary with kala namak salt, a distinctive seasoning popular in South Asian cuisine. The Island Mary evokes sunsets on Vommuli Island's fine white-sand beach, overlooking the glistening Indian Ocean.
