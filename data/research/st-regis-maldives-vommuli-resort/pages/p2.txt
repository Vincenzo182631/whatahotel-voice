## Villas

Encounter a distinctly unique, distinctly bespoke experience at The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort. Designed to be ecologically friendly, we offer 77-inspired accommodations, including 44 overwater villas and 33 located on the beach. Enjoy an unrestricted views from your private terrace, whether staying in our overwater villas, beach villas, or spacious two- and three-bedroom villas overlooking the Indian Ocean or our island's lush tropical gardens. Relax and reinvigorate your spirit in your luxury, premium villa in the Maldives, featuring a state-of-the-art Bang & Olufsen BeoVision entertainment system and complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi. Curl up in your pillowtop bed and experience deep, restful sleep. Start your day on the beach in your rainforest shower or soak in your separate tub and let the signature bath amenities replenish you. We are pleased to provide a discreet St. Regis Butler who is dedicated to making your stay in the Maldives special and memorable.

### St. Regis Butler Service

St. Regis Butler Service is a treasured hallmark of the St. Regis experience and has been for over a century. During your Maldives family stay in our Two-bedroom Ocean Villa with a private pool, you will enjoy the attentive care of a personal butler, who is pleased to recommend customized recreational activities on the island, from beach yoga and sandbank picnics to deep-sea fishing and dolphin or sunset cruises, as well as thoughtful touches such as curated books for nighttime reading. A St. Regis Butler is accessible at any hour—simply send your request and allow the Butler Service Desk to assist. St. Regis Butler Service is dedicated to understanding and anticipating your unique preferences, fulfilling every detail, no matter how small, throughout your time with us in the Maldives.

St. Regis Butler Signature Services

Unpacking & Packing Services

Allow a St. Regis Butler to take care of the unpacking and storage of your luggage—so that you can relax and settle in for your stay. Similarly, before you depart, simply contact the butler service desk to dispatch a Butler to assist with gathering and folding your garments to prepare your luggage for the journey home.

Beverage Services

Allow a St. Regis Butler to arrange coffee or tea service in your room. And if you wish, a St. Regis Butler will arrive after your morning wake-up call to draw your shades, deliver your coffee or tea as well as the day’s newspaper and weather forecast.

Garment Pressing

Once unpacked, simply let your St. Regis Butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. This service includes pressing of two garments per person, per day.

No Drone Policy

Please note: there is a strict no drone policy at The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort due to the privacy and peace of all our distinguished guests.
