# Relax at Our Maldives Spa and Resort

## Iridium Spa

Our hotel's signature Iridium Spa promises a transformative journey in an oasis of tropically inspired sophistication. Alighting atop the scintillating waves of the Indian Ocean, our 1,850-square-meter sanctuary features plush furnishings set against white oak interiors. Each of the six treatment suites at our Maldives spa resort, including two Ayurvedic suites with private steam rooms, offers a refined luxury retreat in the Maldives. Sliding glass doors allow guests to admire the sea from their spa beds, while private terraces with soaking bathtubs ensure relaxation enhanced by fresh ocean breezes. En-suite showers & bathrooms provide unsurpassed privacy and convenience. Our luxury spa in the Maldives also pampers you with steam rooms, saunas, and cold-drench showers. Situated on our covered terrace, our Blue Hole Pool is one of the most expansive hydrotherapy pools in the Maldives. An array of whirlpool jets melt away tension while offering curative benefits for both the mind & body.

### Spa Services & Treatments

Offering a holistic approach to wellness and rejuvenation, our spa treatments are enhanced by Biologique Recherche products, hand selected for their therapeutic benefits. Each visit begins in our serene Iridium Room, where guests sip Iridium tea while watching vibrant marine life through glass floors. Our Ayurvedic specialist, acupuncturist and other expert staff curate tailored rituals to fulfill each individual’s wellness needs, from Balinese and Thai massages and herbal body scrubs to facials, manicures and pedicures. Finish your Maldives spa resort experience in the serene setting of our relaxation lounge with complimentary water, tea and refreshments. Contact your personal St. Regis Butler to make an appointment.

## Salon

The island salon located in the Vommuli House provides bespoke hair, makeup, manicure and pedicure. The in-house salon’s signature treatments ensure the ultimate pampering experiences.

Shirodhara

A perfect example of Ayurvedic principles in practice, comprising an Abhyanga massage followed by the shirodhara, which involves pouring a stream of warm oil onto the third eye chakra on the forehead, sending the recipient into a blissful state.

Caviar Journey

This ritual draws on the anti-aging properties of caviar extract to revitalize the skin. It concludes with a lavender-scented bath, accompanied by a glass of champagne and a serving of delicious caviar.

Iridium Signature Touch

Using a bespoke fusion of traditional Asian massage techniques, and a blend of natural oils tailored to each individual, the Iridium Signature Touch treatment offers the ultimate in deep relaxation.

Biologique Recherche Iridium Jet Lag Massage

Feel lighter and more energized after a long trip with this stimulating experience to boost circulation. This treatment features lavender oil, tsubaki oil, cottonseed oil and rosemary oil extract and includes a decongestant process that combines the wrapping of both legs and a lymphatic drainage massage. The extended session offers an additional head, shoulder and back massage.

Biologique Recherche Bespoke Massage

This relaxing treatment provides mood boosting benefits and helps to alleviate stress. This experience begins with a deep massage of the stomach and body, guaranteed to restore balance, promote muscle recovery and relieve tension from head to toe.

Lava Shell Massage

A body treatment using tiger-striped clam shells as a massage tool, filled with lava gel and activator liquid to produce heat that will gently relax and smooth away knots. Please refer to the Iridium Spa Menu for more information on all available treatments and services.

## Yoga Flow Series

An exceptional journey that fulfills both body and spirit. Alongside our talented Yoga specialists, slip away into paradise by unlocking mindfulness, energizing the chakras and reducing stress. Overlooking the mesmerizing waves of the Indian Ocean and white-sand beaches, experience healing and happiness from within. Treat the body to what it needs whether it’s basic asanas, yoga cleansing, or breath work. All sessions can be tailored to each guests’ comfort level. Wellness Packages are also offered in three-, five-, or seven-day time frames that combine meditation with varying asanas, mudras, pranayama and Yogic Kriyas to promote maximum healing, renewal, and recovery. With continued practice each day, the learnings of this ancient, sacred art of yoga therapy can be carried into daily life. Keep the body healthy and strong while recharging with unrestricted views of the Indian Ocean at the finest address at the Maldives.

### Acupuncture Healing & Wellness Journey

Keep the body in balance and promote healing from within with an Acupuncture Healing & Wellness Journey – a form of alternative therapy that has been used across cultures for thousands of years, now offered at our luxury Maldives spa.

Treatment options range from Cosmetic Acupuncture to Acupuncture for pain management. Balance the body’s systems with an Acupuncture option that targets excess puffiness and dull-looking skin, relieves pain and calms the mind, or keeps the body in balance and supports overall health and well-being. Treatments can be combined and customized to help guests overcome a variety of health issues.

There is no better place in the world to try and heal oneself with natural treatments than in the luxury of our private island paradise.

### Please Notify Us

When making your reservation, please notify our team of any mobility challenges, medical conditions, allergies, or physical ailments for us to give you the best possible experience. If you are pregnant or think you may be pregnant, we have specially designed spa treatments that provide maximum benefit for expectant mothers. Due to the changes to your body, we do not perform full body massages or body treatments on expectant mothers within their first trimester and some treatments should be avoided throughout. In addition, our Dive Centre follows PADI guidelines that recommend women avoid diving while pregnant or trying to become pregnant. For more information, please contact stregis.maldivesvommuli@stregis.com.
