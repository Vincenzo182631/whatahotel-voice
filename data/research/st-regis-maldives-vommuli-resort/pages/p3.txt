## A Modern Architectural Marvel at The John Jacob Astor Estate

The John Jacob Astor Estate at The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort is more than just a luxurious retreat—it's an architectural marvel. The estate's design not only honors the stunning surroundings but also tells a story of innovation and eco-consciousness, making every stay here an extraordinary experience.
