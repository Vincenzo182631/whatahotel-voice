# Welcome to The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort

## Cultivate Relaxation in the Maldives

Surrounded by verdant rainforest and white-sand beaches on a private island, The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort overlooks the alluring waves of the Indian Ocean. Explore the tropical beauty and rich marine life of this peaceful, eco-conscious setting. Our private lagoon, Iridium Spa, and outdoor infinity pool promise nonpareil relaxation. A large variety of water sports, diving, and excursions at our luxury resort in the Maldives can be arranged by our signature butler service. In addition, exquisite culinary fare awaits at our seven restaurants and bars. Boasting refined furnishings and island-inspired design, each of the 33 on-land and 44 over-water luxury private villas at our island resort in the Maldives promises picturesque ocean or garden views from private terraces and pools. Our legendary St. Regis Butlers deliver bespoke service day or night.

### Family Living, Exquisitely Composed

From cycling beneath palm‑lined paths to playful lagoon moments and curated island adventures, our resort celebrates the art of family connection. Here, days unfold with spirited ease, creating cherished chapters of togetherness shaped by the essence of Living Exquisite.

### Where the Reef Whispers Truth

With over 40 distinguished dive sites encircling the atoll, our Vommuli Dive & Watersports Centre opens a world of manta glides, reef gardens, and cerulean depths. Along our vibrant house reef, serene snorkeling reveals coral tapestries and hidden wonders, while overwater villas hover above this living canvas. From calm lagoon explorations to PADI‑certified dives.

### A Symphony of Culinary Masters

From ALBA’s sun‑lit Mediterranean elegance to T•Pan’s kaiseki artistry, from Orientale’s Far‑Eastern finesse to the Whale Bar’s sunset‑touched tapas, our chefs craft moments of gastronomic theatre. Each dish is a composition of precision, passion, and the island’s finest flavors.

### Sustainability With Natural Poise

Where white sands, sculpted island contours, and flourishing reef life meet, Vommuli emerges as a testament to thoughtful coexistence. Our landscape flows with the integrity of place—an island shaped by nature’s design and upheld through practices that honor its beauty, its ecology, and its enduring marine harmony.

### Wellness in Tranquil Reverie

Rejuvenation unfolds as a quiet choreography of breath, stillness, and ocean‑borne light — a place where time softens and the mind drifts into restorative calm. Each moment is shaped with intention, inviting the body to unburden and the spirit to find equilibrium. And for those seeking deeper renewal, Iridium Spa gently extends the journey with its overwater serenity and transformative therapies.

### A Legacy Beneath the Waves

Here, the ocean reveals its finest palette — crystalline turquoise swirled with coral shadows and sunlit shallows. The island’s curves and overwater silhouettes rise as though composed by the sea itself, forming a serene, living tableau that embodies the true soul of our shores, that flourishes alongside our reef‑restoration efforts in partnership with Reefscapers.

## Meet The Tastemaker

Discover a curated season of the renowned Tastemaker whose creativity, expertise, and craft shape extraordinary moments across the island. St. Regis Rituals

**Champagne Sabering**

As the sun sets over the Indian Ocean, The Whale Bar comes alive with the celebrated art of Champagne Sabering. Inspired by a tradition attributed to Napoleon Bonaparte, who famously opened bottles of champagne with a saber in both victory and defeat, this signature St. Regis ritual marks the transition from day to evening with ceremony, elegance, and a toast to life's finest moments.

**Afternoon Tea**

Inspired by the legacy of Caroline Astor and the celebrated gatherings of New York's Gilded Age, Afternoon Tea remains a timeless St. Regis tradition. Guests are invited to savor a refined selection of teas, delicate pastries, and meaningful conversation in an atmosphere of timeless elegance.

**Island Mary**

The Island Mary is The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort's signature interpretation of the iconic St. Regis Bloody Mary tradition. Infused with flavors inspired by the destination, this distinctive elixir captures the spirit of Vommuli Island while celebrating a ritual enjoyed at St. Regis hotels around the world.

St. Regis Butler Service

The hallmark of the St. Regis experience, the Butler Service ensures every stay unfolds with effortless ease. From pre-arrival arrangements and bespoke itineraries to dining reservations, restorative rituals, and special celebrations, every detail is thoughtfully curated to individual preferences.

For more than a century, St. Regis Butlers have anticipated guests' needs with discretion and precision. Whether arranging personalized experiences, unpacking on arrival, pressing garments, or attending to requests throughout the stay, they remain a dedicated presence, elevating every moment at the Maldives' Finest Address.

Life Beyond the Villa

Beyond the privacy of the villa, Vommuli Island invites guests to discover a world of experiences designed to inspire connection, exploration, and wellbeing. From ocean adventures and wellness pursuits to multigenerational activities and cultural discoveries, each moment reveals a new dimension of island living.

**Island Discovery**

With bicycles provided for every villa, guests can explore Vommuli Island at their leisure, uncovering tranquil beaches, shaded pathways, and breathtaking ocean vistas along the way.

**Rejuvenation & Tranquility at Iridium Spa**

Suspended above the turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean, Iridium Spa is an intimate sanctuary devoted to renewal and restoration. Within this inviting setting, refined treatments and sensory rituals are thoughtfully tailored to individual wellbeing, cultivating moments of meaningful pause and relaxation.

From the soothing embrace of the iconic Blue Hole Hydrotherapy Pool to tranquil spaces designed for reflection and repose, every detail is crafted to help guests unwind, reconnect, and transcend the rhythms of everyday life.

**Vommuli Dive & Watersports**

From vibrant snorkeling excursions and world-class diving to thrilling watersports, endless adventures await above and below the surface of the Indian Ocean.

**Children's Club**

A vibrant space designed for our youngest Luminaries, featuring engaging activities and enriching experiences that inspire creativity, curiosity, and discovery.

**Tennis & Padel**

Set amidst lush tropical surroundings, our tennis and padel courts offer opportunities for recreation, friendly competition, and shared moments together.

**The Boutique & Souvenir Store**

Discover a bespoke collection of resort treasures, artisan-crafted gifts, and elegant keepsakes inspired by Vommuli Island.

**Yogasala**

Surrounded by nature, Yogasala provides a serene sanctuary for mindfulness, movement, and renewal.

**ALBA Beach & Infinity Pool**

Relax beside the resort's main pool or along the shores of ALBA Beach, where panoramic ocean views and the gentle rhythm of island life set the scene.

**The Library**

An intimate retreat offering a peaceful setting to unwind with a curated collection of books and reading materials.

**Blue Hole Hydrotherapy Pool**

Perched above the ocean, the iconic Blue Hole Hydrotherapy Pool invites relaxation and restoration through the therapeutic power of water.

Together, these enriching experiences create unforgettable moments beyond the villa, shaping a stay defined by discovery, connection, and renewal.

Coral Garden and Reef Restoration

Beneath the turquoise waters surrounding Vommuli Island lies a thriving marine ecosystem. Through our partnership with Reefscapers since 2023, The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort is committed to protecting and nurturing local coral reefs. Guests are invited to discover this ongoing conservation journey, fostering a deeper connection with the ocean while contributing to the preservation of the Maldives' extraordinary underwater world.

For every bottle sold, $3 goes to our “Blue Fund”, supporting reef rejuvenation with local organization Reefscapers. In addition to this, complimentary Fen still water is provided in the gym and villas for indoor & outdoor use.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort?

The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort is Velana International Airport (MLE). MLE is located approximately 160.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
