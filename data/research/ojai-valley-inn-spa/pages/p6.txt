Experience a day in the life of an apiarist with our beekeeping and honey-tasting excursion. Beginning at the Discover Ojai Center, you will meet with our resident beekeeper for a brief introduction on our most vital pollinators, the European honeybee. Enjoy the beautiful Ojai vistas as we escort you to our bee yard in the heart of Mistletoe Meadows. Once you arrive, you will experience firsthand how it feels to be inside an apiary and learn about the extraordinary life of the honeybee. The excursion will conclude with a honey tasting of cold-pressed, raw honey in a beautiful and serene setting. Reservations are required.

Explore Ojai’s Wills-Rice Canyon trail, an easy/moderate 2-hour hike through three Ecosystems: Oak Meadow; Riparian Canyon including the “Enchanted Forest” and Chaparral. An experienced and knowledgeable Trail Guide will lead the group on the 4.6 mile loop with 580 feet of elevation. Group hikes include round-trip transportation from Spa Ojai to the trail head, bottled water, and a snack. Guests must be at least 10-years-old to participate.
