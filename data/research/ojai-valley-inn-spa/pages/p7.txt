ECO PARTNERSHIPS & RECOGNITION

## AUDUBON INTERNATIONAL GREEN HOSPITALITY PLATINUM CERTIFICATION

The Green Hospitality Program provides third-party verification that certified lodging facilities have met environmental best practice standards and have demonstrated a strong commitment to making improvements. Through a comprehensive method for assessing environmental measures undertaken, participating facilities can reduce environment-related costs and gain a marketing advantage. Areas of focus include Communication, Community, Waste & Materials, Water Conservation, Energy, and Chemicals & Air Quality. Ojai Valley Inn’s Platinum Certification covers every hospitality element from its array of locally sourced restaurants to its Forbes Five Star-rated Spa.

## Forbes VERIFIED™ Responsible Hospitality

We’re pleased to share that Ojai Valley Inn is the first hotel in the world to receive Responsible Hospitality VERIFIED™, an industry-leading sustainability standard for luxury hospitality. Responsible Hospitality is recognized by Forbes Travel Guide, the foremost authority on hospitality excellence, as the official sustainability verification for its global community of Star-Rated hotels. Its comprehensive assessment verifies the implementation of best practices to protect the environment and support the well-being of employees, guests, and the community. As the inaugural recipient, Ojai Valley Inn is deeply honored to receive this acknowledgment and pledges to continue redefining luxury hospitality by remaining dedicated to minimizing our impact.

## City of Ojai: Adopt-A-Trail Cleanup

Ojai Valley Inn is proud to partner with the City of Ojai in their extensive Adopt-A-Trail cleanup program, working together to preserve and maintain the local trails that our guests love. Through our collective efforts, we enhance the natural beauty that makes the Ojai Valley so special and promote responsible outdoor recreation.

## Ojai Valley Land Conservancy

The Inn supports and partners with the Ojai Valley Land Conservancy (OVLC) in their mission “to protect and restore the natural landscapes of the Ojai Valley forever.” By contributing to the preservation of vital habitats and open spaces, we ensure the long-term sustainability of our environment and the well-being of our community.

## Watershed Progressive

## ReCORK: Recycling

In collaboration with pioneering cork innovators, ReCORK, the Inn supports the recycling and repurposing of natural wine corks through North America’s largest natural cork recycling program. By collecting and redirecting corks from landfills, we reduce waste and contribute to sustainable manufacturing processes.

## Clean the World: Soap Recycling

Ojai Valley Inn proudly partners with Clean the World to recycle and distribute discarded soap bars. This global initiative not only helps protect the environment, but also improves hygiene and prevents disease in underserved communities—ultimately saving lives

WATER RESILIENCY

## Turf Reduction

Water conservation is a critical concern throughout the Ojai Valley. By minimizing the amount of turf throughout the resort’s grounds, we decrease water requirements and create more sustainable landscapes. Our commitment to turf reduction ensures a greener, water-efficient environment for all.

## Low-Flow & Drip System Irrigation

By utilizing advanced irrigation technologies, such as low-flow and drip systems, we optimize water distribution and minimize wastage at the Inn. These innovative systems deliver water precisely where it’s needed, conserving this precious resource.

## Stormwater Capture

Throughout the resort, we harness the power of nature by capturing and utilizing stormwater. This solution simultaneously takes advantage of local rainfall to replenish groundwater supplies and prevents stormwater from becoming polluted urban runoff, supporting a more sustainable water cycle.

NATURE & BIODIVERSITY

## Heritage Tree Stewardship

Ojai Valley Inn recognizes the importance and significance of legacy trees in our vital ecosystem. By celebrating, protecting, and caring for these remarkable trees, we preserve their beauty, provide habitat for wildlife, and contribute to cleaner air.

## Invasive Species Removal

Invasive plant species can threaten native ecosystems. Through the Inn’s proactive approach to removal, we promote the growth of indigenous plants and restore balance to our beautiful environment.

## Native Restoration

The Arundo Project is a local and statewide conservation initiative focused on restoring native vegetation, particularly in areas impacted by the invasive Arundo donax (giant reed). By replacing this non-native species with native plants, the project enhances biodiversity, fosters resilient ecosystems, and contributes to fire mitigation efforts on a large scale spanning multiple acres. At the Inn, we’ve already successfully removed Arundo donax from four acres of affected land.

## Drought-Tolerant Pollinator Gardens

Creating gardens that are both drought-tolerant and pollinator-friendly helps us conserve water at the Inn, while providing vital habitats for bees, butterflies, and other pollinating species throughout the resort. These gardens create complex worlds that support biodiversity and contribute to a thriving ecosystem, ensuring the future is filled with pollinators.

GREEN GOLF

## Celebrating the Natural Landscape

The original designs of our golf courses incorporate the existing natural landscape into the layout, preserving its integrity and minimizing disruption to local ecosystems. By celebrating nature, we create harmonious spaces for golfers and wildlife alike.

## Pollinator-Safe Fertilizer

To protect native pollinators, like the highly valued honeybee, we use fertilizers that are free from harmful chemicals. This impactful approach ensures our golf courses remain safe, pollinator-friendly environments for these essential species.

## State-of-the-Art Irrigation

Through advanced technology and meticulous planning, the Inn strikes a balance between maintaining high-quality greens and minimizing water consumption. One way we optimize water usage on our golf courses is by utilizing state-of-the-art irrigation systems.

EDIBLE ECOSYSTEM

## Sustainable Seafood

With science-based guidance from the Monterey Bay Aquarium’s Seafood Watch program, a global leader in the sustainable seafood movement, Ojai Valley Inn is committed to supporting sustainable fishing practices and offering responsibly sourced seafood options throughout our restaurants. By prioritizing sustainable seafood, we help protect the health of our oceans' ecosystems while providing guests with delicious, ocean-friendly dining experiences.

## Procuring Local Foods

The Inn’s restaurants prioritize purchasing fresh, seasonal produce directly from local farms and farmer’s markets to support our Ojai Valley economy and community. We also encourage organic farming practices and raise awareness through educational events.

## Herb Gardens & Fruit Trees

Our treasured 220 acres are home to organic herb gardens that showcase 65 diverse culinary and medicinal herbs. Our hills and orchards nourish a collection of 20 types of fruit trees, including our famed Pixie tangerines, that use integrated pest management (IPM) to protect biodiversity and create a safer environment for our pollinator friends.

## Food Waste Composting

Through our resort-wide food waste composting partnership, we actively reduce waste sent to landfills. By composting organic materials, we create nutrient-rich soil, promoting healthy plant growth and completing the sustainable food cycle

CLEAN INGREDIENTS

## Spa Ojai’s Eco Pledge

Our 30,000-square-foot Spa Ojai embraces natural and organic beauty and wellness practices. From skincare to body treatments, we prioritize products that are free from harmful chemicals, ensuring a rejuvenating and holistic wellness experience for our guests.

## Guestroom Shower Amenities Inspired by Nature

Here at the Inn, we strive to make every aspect of our guests' stay sustainable, right down to the shower amenities. Inspired by nature, each amenity is carefully selected to be paraben-free, sulfate-free, and cruelty-free, offering a truly eco-conscious experience.

## Single-Use Plastic Free Solutions

As part of our commitment to reducing plastic waste, we have implemented single-use plastic-free initiatives throughout the resort. By choosing sustainable alternatives, we minimize our environmental impact and contribute to a cleaner future.
