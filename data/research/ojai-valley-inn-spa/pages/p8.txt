Reviewed by
Krista Simmons
How did it strike you on arrival?
Time seems to slow down as you pull up to this sprawling Spanish revival hotel. Birds chirp from atop the towering oak trees, and the peacefully running fountains set the tone for a serenity that'll carry through your weekend.
What’s the crowd like?
Travelers are discerning, yet laid back luxury seekers that appreciate the finer things.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
The Fireplace Shangri-la room is your own personal retreat, complete with an oversized soaking tub (big enough for even a tall girl like myself to enjoy) and separate shower. Hand-painted Spanish tile, gold-auburn granite and cobalt blue accents give this room a modern Mediterranean look. But the real highlight is the stucco fireplace with seating, a perfect spot to tuck into a book and indulge in the rejuvenating spirit of Ojai.
We’re craving some deep, restorative sleep. They got us?
The bed is as plush as can be, and you'll feel like royalty wrapped in their 300-thread-count Egyptian organic cotton sheets.
How about the little things, like mini bar, or shower goodies. Any of that find its way into your suitcase?
If morals know no consequence, would it be considered bad form if we swiped some of the marine-based facial line from Osea from the spa? We sure hope not.
Please tell us the bathroom won’t let us down.
The bathroom is a sanctuary in and of itself, complete with an oversize soaking tub adorned with hand painted Spanish tiles, cozy robes made from sustainable materials, and Spa Ojai products with a soothing lavender scent.
Maybe the most important topic of all: Wi-Fi. What’s the word?
You're here to unplug, but should you need it, Wi-Fi is strong and reliable.
Room service: What do they have?
In-room dining amenities include a South of the Border snack tray, which comes with chips, epic guacamole, Coronas, and two Ojai pixie margaritas made with the region's famously delicious citrus fruit.
Staff: If you could award one a trophy, who gets it, and why?
The award for the most attentive staff goes to the spa!
Anything stand out about other services and features?
The pools are worth the visit alone—killer views, and plenty of space to hop around to keep the scenery fresh.
What was most heartbreaking about your stay?
That you have to leave.
Bottom line: worth it, and why?
This hotel is worth it for the wonderfully relaxing vibes, stellar service, and top notch spa experiences.
