# Activities

A

Activities

By Land, Sky or Sea

Those with curious spirits can set off on new adventures each day: discover the beauty and charm of the Datça Peninsula from all angles with activities at D Maris Bay.

01

03

SAILING

SCHOOL

Spend a relaxed day exploring hidden coves and nearby islands with a catamaran rental, available for up to 10 guests plus crew. For a more high-octane adventure, take a lesson with one of our expert instructors at the international sailing school aboard a catamaran sailing boat.

Watersports

Wakeboarding, sailing, surfing, kayaking, diving and waterskiing are all on offer at our specialist watersports centre, with expert instructors to show you the ropes.

TENNIS and PADEL

COURTS

Whether you want one-on-one coaching with a professional instructor, or a friendly game with family, you can work up a sweat with a few sets on our hard courts — or enjoy an energizing game on our brand-new padel court.

HELICOPTER

Soar above the rugged coastline and azure seas of the Datça Peninsula on a private helicopter tour, taking in luscious landscapes and olive groves.

RUNNING AND

Bike Tracks

Take in the beauty of the Datça Peninsula as you run or cycle through verdant forest, volcanic earth, past dark olive and pale almond trees, with the mesmerising turquoise coast on the horizon.
