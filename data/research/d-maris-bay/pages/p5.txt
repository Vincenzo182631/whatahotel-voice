# Dining

D

DINING TO DREAM OF

Restaurants & Bars

Breathe in the shimmering spectacle of the bay as you indulge in delicacies from around the world. Savour deliciously decadent flavours and traditional Turkish hospitality at six unique restaurants and seven sophisticated bars. It’s all yours for the tasting.

* * *

01

La Guérite

La Guérite brings the sophisticated essence of Cannes and St. Barths to D Maris Bay. Offering fresh seafood and attentive service, La Guérite promises a stylish experience at the heart of Mediterranean cuisine, complemented by a sea breeze. Enjoy experiences that match your rhythm with its happy hour concept while savoring the sunset.

Explore

* * *

02

Manos

Manos, with its rustic decor and mosaic flooring, delivers a warm tavern atmosphere, connecting you with the freshest seafood and an assortment of Greek mezes. Located on the Datça Peninsula, Manos enriches your experience with Greek cultural traditions and festive activities, leading you on a delectable Mediterranean journey as you groove to the rhythm of the music.

Explore

* * *

03

D Marıs Kitchen

Nested at The Bay Beach, D Maris Kitchen blends the essence of Aegean and Anatolian cuisines with a modern, refined touch. From local olive oil dishes to mezzes, from hearty stews to refined presentations, every plate reflects the traces of the past and a contemporary aesthetic.

Explore

* * *

04

Nusr-Et

Located at The Bay Beach, Nusr-Et creates a memorable culinary experience by serving tender steaks with magnificent theatrical touches in an engaging presentation.

Explore

* * *

05

Zuma

Zuma delivers authentic Japanese flavors in a modern twist through its Izakaya-style dining, providing a sophisticated touch. With its unique ambiance, Zuma offers a visual spectacle set against a breathtaking panoramic sea view.

Explore

* * *

06

Aurora Caprı

A legendary name from the heart of Capri, Aurora Capri brings over 130 years of Italian culinary heritage to the shores of D Maris Bay. Known for its iconic “Pizzallacqua®” and timeless flavors, this celebrated restaurant blends Campanian traditions with contemporary finesse. Curated by Mia D’Alessio and Chef Franco Aversa, Aurora Capri offers an elegant dining experience shaped by generations of passion, creativity, and Mediterranean charm.

Explore

* * *

07

DD SCOOP & DD PASTRY

Nibble on fresh, flaky croissants with a morning latte. Temptingly nestled near Bay Beach, DD Pastry and DD Scoop provide fresh coffee, smoothies and tasty treats made fresh each day to tide you over between ocean swims.

Explore
