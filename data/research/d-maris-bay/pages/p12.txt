ABOUT DOĞUŞ

OUR VISION

Doğuş Hospitality & Retail Group’s mission is to enrich modern life through flawless experiences. By bringing the most prestigious retail, hotels, food and beverage, travel and event management companies together under one family, we pride ourselves on providing world-class service in perfect synergy across all sectors.

OUR FOUNDER

Ayhan Şahenk, our late founder, was an inspiring pioneer in hospitality, with a vision underpinned by strong values. His focus was global and long-term, always with a view to benefit his country and its people.

Doğuş Group

Today, Doğuş Hospitality & Retail Group has a rich and unique ecosystem of 180+ restaurants and 17 hotels in 18 countries. We continue to strengthen our position in the global market through international partnerships, while creating new value for Turkey.

Doğuş Group Hospitality

D-HOTELS &

RESORTS

An unmatched collection of extraordinary hotels, D-Hotels & Resorts destinations are united by their timeless beauty, immersive cultural experiences and exquisite service. Scattered across Switzerland, Turkey and Spain, each storied property has its own distinct character, yet shares the same purpose: to create eternal, soul-affirming experiences.
