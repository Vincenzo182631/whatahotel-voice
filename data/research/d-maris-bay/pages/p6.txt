# BEACHES&POOLS

Whether swimming, sunbathing, partying or baydreaming… find your own rhythm at one of six private beaches and two shimmering pools.

Crystal Clear

Sea and Be Seen

Feel your senses enliven and spirits lift as you immerse yourself in calm, restorative waters — the sea is home to marine life including rare loggerhead sea turtles and monk seals. Discover the best beaches in Turkey here at D Maris Bay.

THE BAY

BEACH

This spacious, white-sand beach is a family-friendly paradise at the heart of the bay. Swim, splash and snorkel in crystal-clear waters, spotting turtles and fish, and keep kids entertained all day with the Kids Club just around the corner.

ZUMA

X

SILENCE

BEACH

With the horizon and a gentle breeze whispering through the pines, Zuma x Silence Beach is a place of wholehearted peace. This serene, adults-only beach, reserved for guests aged 18 and above, invites you to unwind without music or distractions, where lying on a floating daybed above the sea becomes a moment of pure stillness. This season, Zuma elevates the beachfront experience at Zuma x Silence Beach, bringing its signature contemporary Japanese flavors to this idyllic setting. Refined tastes meet the tranquility of the bay, offering an immersive culinary journey unlike any other.

Maris

Beach

This exclusive slice of peaceful, palm-dotted beach offers a beachside spa treatment room and private cabanas, one with its own luxurious Jacuzzi. A coveted floating daybed, suspended above the sea, makes for magical memories.

Manos Beach

An elegant and intimate golden beach set next to Manos, a traditional Greek taverna. Soak up the sun and refresh yourself in the sea before feasting alfresco on the freshest seafood and fine Greek fare.

Beach

Your own private paradise and a pristine shoreline all to yourself in a private cabana equipped with all the essentials at Hidden Beach. Discover personalized services and breathtaking beauty for an unmatched experience at the bay of your dreams.

LA GUERITE

La Guérite

BEACH

La Guérite Beach embodies the sophisticated, lively luxury of sister concepts in Cannes and St Barths. A private stretch of palm-fringed sand is speckled with raffia-shaded loungers and white canopied daybeds. Sip on bubbles between ocean swims, as tropical house music lilts through the air. Later, the beach pulses to life with sunset DJ sets and vibrant parties til late.
