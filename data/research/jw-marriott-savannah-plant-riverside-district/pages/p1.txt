# Welcome to JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District

## Iconic Moments Await

The AAA Four Diamond JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District is located in the heart of historic Savannah on the riverfront, making it a premier hotel in Savannah, GA, with countless attractions just steps away. Explore the downtown shopping district, visit Kessler Fine Jewelry, dine at one of the hotel's restaurants, reconnect with friends at our riverside bars, or embark on a walking tour....See More Once a power plant, Plant Riverside District now represents the beauty of progress. Whether you're planning a weekend getaway or a business meeting, the Savannah Plant Riverside District hotel offers a setting with timeless elegance. Our three unique buildings showcase chic architecture, romantic styling, and contemporary maritime details. Complete your stay with a relaxing massage at our spa before enjoying the vibrant nightlife nearby. Experience the charm and sophistication of a riverside hotel in Savannah, GA, in one unforgettable destination.See Less

Choose Your Experience

### Power Plant

The original power plant has been repurposed as a contemporary riverside retreat while retaining its unique architectural features. Step inside the expansive lobby, complete with a life-size, chrome-dipped dinosaur, and explore its many restaurants, art galleries and vibrant rooftop lounges, at one of the most unique hotels in Savannah, GA.

### Three Muses

Polished marble and classic European elegance define this intimate escape, adorned with whimsical art and glamorous decor. Discover unique Savannah treasures in the ground floor specialty shops, or indulge your social side and the most unique event space Savannah has to offer at the secret Myrtle & Rose Rooftop Bar.

### Atlantic

The Atlantic has a maritime concept, handsome and contemporary, inspired by a bespoke journey aboard a luxury yacht at sea. Catch live performances by your favorite artists at District Live powered by Live Nation. And experience the Salzburg Ballroom which takes guests from “sea to land” and features large-scaled velvet draperies, representing the sails of Salzburg ships arriving in Savannah in the 1730s.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District. The daily valet fee is $55.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District is Savannah/Hilton Head International Airport (SAV). SAV is located approximately 10.4 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Savannah Plant Riverside District does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
