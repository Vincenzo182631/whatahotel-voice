# Elevated Stays

## Rooms & Suites

Get away from it all with one of three distinct hotel experiences to express your unique style. No matter your choice, handsome and stately, romantic and elegant, or historic and chic we invite you to relax and stay a while at one of the best hotels in Savannah, GA. To curate your own elevated stay, contact our Luxury Reservation Coordinator at info@plantriverside.com with your confirmation code.

Reimagined History

## Power Plant

The bold character of a repurposed power plant is transformed into a contemporary riverside retreat. The Power Plant's hotel rooms retain historic architectural details paired with modern-industrial stylings perfect for relaxing and recharging.

Inspired Beauty

## Three Muses

Designed to reflect pure beauty, the guestrooms reveal soft and sophisticated colors and are adorned with magical realist art. The charming detailing and iconic views of the Savannah Riverfront make the Three Muses perfect for a romantic getaway.

Bespoke Luxury

## Atlantic

Handsome and stately, the Atlantic invites guests on a getaway inspired by a bespoke journey aboard a luxury yacht. Traditional maritime details define this contemporary masterpiece, setting the stage for a stay that’s both relaxing and luxurious.

Atlantic

### Executive Studio

Upgrade your stay in our luxurious executive suite featuring a living area with sofa bed, marble walk-in bathtub and floor-to-ceiling views of the Savannah Riverfront.
