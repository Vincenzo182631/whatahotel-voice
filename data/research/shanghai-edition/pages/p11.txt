# Spa

**Spa**

Share

The spa features six treatment rooms, along with a sauna changing area and a spacious relaxation zone. By integrating genuine Asian healing practices with advanced therapies and premium products, the SPA will provide guests with a lavish and soothing spa experience, upheld by exceptional standards of service and professionalism.

For general reservations, please call us at +86 21 5368 9999

**GYM**

Share

With state-of-the-art equipment, the Gym on level 7 combines wellness and holistic design. The 24-hour facility features a large outdoor terrace where guest can relax, practice tai chi, yoga, or cardio against a stunning urban backdrop.

Open 24 Hours

For general inquiries, please contact our team at

+86 21 5368 9988
