# Services & Amenities

The Shanghai EDITION is an authentic custom-made hotel concept. Uniquely of its place and of its time, it is unlike any other EDITION before it or to come.

### For further information

Phone +86 21 5368 9999 – Email: info.sha@editionhotels.com

Guest Services

- 145 unique rooms and suites, some with oversized furnished terraces
- One-of-a-kind Penthouse comprised of the entire 25th floor
- 2 signature restaurants
- Lobby Bar, Club Room, Punch Room and Roof bars
- Customised banquet menus for private events, luncheons, dinner parties and business meetings
- 24-hour Room Service
- Concierge Service
- Complimentary work station in the lobby area
- Complimentary high-speed, wireless Internet access throughout the hotel
- Complimentary use of the 24-hour fitness facilities
- Executive Meeting Room and Meeting Studios
- State-of-the-art audio and visual services available for private functions
- Multi-lingual staff
- Valet Parking

Executive Services

- Executive Boardrooms
- Meeting Rooms and Private Event Spaces Smart boards and electronic flip charts available
- Laptops, iPads, cell phones and fax machines available
- Video Conferencing
- Executive Car Services available
- Personal Assistant available
- 24-hour multi-lingual secretarial staff available
- State-of-the-art audio and visual equipment

Gym

- Outdoor terrace for relaxation, tai chi, yoga, and cardio
- Swimming Pool
- State-of-the-art gym equipment
- Accessible 24 hours a day
- Complimentary towels
- Personal training sessions available
- Headphones and refreshments

Spa

- 6 treatment rooms featuring 4 individual rooms and 2 VIP suites
- Designated changing rooms with sauna
- Relaxation area with full bar and retail boutique
