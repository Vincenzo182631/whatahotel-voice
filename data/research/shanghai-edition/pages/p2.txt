# Rooms

The Shanghai EDITION's 145 rooms invite a new era of laid-back, modern luxury with exceptional details and iconic Shanghai city and Bund views. Every room -- from the Deluxe Rooms to the more spacious Premium Rooms -- features the conveniences both travelers and revelers have come to expect and rely upon.

# Suites

Suites feature an open plan living and dining area, light-filled bedrooms, walk-in-wardrobes, large angled windows, which are signature to the postmodern tower. Guests are able to enjoy views of the historical Bund area of Shanghai Puxi, against the backdrop of Pudong’s modern skyscrapers.
