## Enjoy Montreal to the fullest with our luxurious suites!

Warwick Le Crystal – Montreal offers 112 refined suites and 19 signature suites designed for both connection and calm. Each spacious suite features bold, modern design, kitchenettes, and oversized bathrooms filled with light. Whether here for business or leisure, guests enjoy a serene retreat above the city, surrounded by the vibrant energy that defines Montreal’s dynamic spirit.

For an elevated experience, the Penthouse Suites at Warwick Le Crystal – Montreal offer expansive living spaces, exceptional comfort, and sweeping views from large private rooftop terraces. Located in the heart of downtown, these exclusive suites blend luxury, privacy, and style for an unforgettable stay.
