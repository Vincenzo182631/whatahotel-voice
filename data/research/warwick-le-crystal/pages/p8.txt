Warwick Le Crystal
Sink into the hot tub and soak up some best cityscape views in Montreal.

Why book?
Large rooms, a relaxing pool and hot tub area, and an enviable location in the middle of downtown
Montreal
make this one a no-brainer for business and leisure travelers alike.
Set the scene
With a super convenient location and 131 streamlined suites, this is a hotel that’s especially suited for those in search of some residential comfort, whether that’s families or business travelers.
The backstory
Warwick Hotels and Resorts is known for intimate, private-feeling hotels, and this is the brand’s first hotel in Canada. The cool, pared-back ethos is much more serene than some of Montreal’s comparable hotels, where buzzy hotel bars and in-your-face designs can feel a little overstimulating.
The rooms
The ambiance here is distinctly private and residential. Suites come equipped with a well-stocked minibar of beverages and treats, spacious sitting areas that are ideal for business travelers or families to spread out, and most come with kitchenettes. For even more room, book one of the multi-bedroom penthouse suites, some of which come with fireplaces. For the price point, and the location, it’s tough to find a better value proposition in this part of Montreal.
Food and drink
The hotel offers a limited continental breakfast (for a fee), and there is also an attached Thai restaurant, Siam. The elevated dishes are fresh and flavorful, but the real draw here is the seasonal terrace–thanks to its location just steps from the Bell Center, it’s a popular watering hole before or after concerts or hockey games.
The spa
The small yet lovely spa Éléments Maison de Beauté offers mostly facials, cosmetic laser treatments, waxing, pedicures and manicures.
The neighborhood/area
The Warwick sits right in the heart of downtown’s Golden Square Mile, just steps from shopping, nightlife, and the Bell Centre. It’s about halfway between the Lucien L’Allier and Guy-Concordia Metro stops.
The service
This is a hotel that caters largely to business travelers, so you can expect the service to be efficient–the valet swoops cars in and out of the loading area in the blink of an eye.
For families
The 19 multi-bedroom signature suites are a great choice for families, with kitchenettes, spacious living rooms, and multiple bathrooms so everyone can get ready uninterrupted.
Accessibility
Each floor is reachable by elevator, and accessible suites are available.
Anything left to mention?
Pack your bathing suit. There’s a fantastic rooftop pool with an outdoor sundeck area complete with hot tub–with some of the best cityscape views of any pool in Montreal.
