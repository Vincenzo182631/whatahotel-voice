#### Fitness Centre

Stay active in the hotel’s light-filled fitness centre, offering panoramic views of downtown Montreal. Open daily from 6:00 am to 11:00 pm, it features cardio machines, free weights, yoga balls, and personal TVs for a complete workout.

#### Valet Parking

Enjoy 24-hour valet service with in/out privileges and EV charging stations. Daily parking is $34 and overnight is $59, with 45 spaces available for guest convenience.
