# Warwick Le Crystal – Montreal

## Experience the dynamism and refinement of our boutique hotel in Montréal

Warwick Le Crystal – Montreal reflects the city’s culture and energy through a lens of modern luxury, defined by warmth and understated elegance. Comprising exclusively of suites, the hotel combines contemporary design with attentive, personalized service offering a refined, residential experience in the heart of the city.

Ideally located in downtown Montreal, steps from Mont Royal, the Bell Centre, and the city’s premier shopping, the hotel places guests at the center of the city’s rhythm.

- ### Is there a parking at Warwick Le Crystal - Montreal hotel ?

Safe indoor valet parking area priced at 59 CAD/night for hotel guests, 34 CAD for daytime usage by guests visiting the hotel.

Self parking is not availble.

The max headroom is 6.5 FT.

The parking is not bookable/subject to availability.

Electric vehicle charging station free of charge.

We don't accept the studded tires.
