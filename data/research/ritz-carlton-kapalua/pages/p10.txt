The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua
A luxury oceanfront resort in Kapalua that blends elevated dining, rich cultural programming, and outdoor adventure with a deep respect for its sacred surroundings

In a nutshell
: This oceanfront property in West Maui has a long-standing commitment to honoring Hawaiian culture—educating guests, fostering community engagement, and protecting the sacred land it occupies. A serene spa and multi-tiered pool create space for relaxation, while special event weekends transform the property into a lively hub for food, music, culture, and connection for both tourists and locals.
Rooms
Accommodations range from well-appointed guest rooms with private lanais and partial ocean views to more expansive suites and residential-style spaces. Ground-floor suites offer added amenities like firepits and direct lawn access. Higher-tier room options provide additional living space for families or longer stays, balancing classic resort comfort with flexibility across price points.
Food and drink
There are six on-property dining options, starting with breakfast at Ulana Terrace and grab-and-go items from Kai Café, moving to afternoons featuring poolside bites from Olu Café, and ending the night with cocktails at Alaloa Lounge. For elevated American cuisine, Banyan Tree delivers an immersive dining experience with interiors that transport you to an enchanting garden and curated tastings, like a weekly chef’s table and the monthly Kapalua Wine and Chef Series. For casual fare with a view, head to the Burger Shack overlooking D.T. Fleming Beach Park for truffle fries and a decadent milkshake—plus boozy versions for those 21 and over.
Amenities and experiences
The resort is a premier venue for events like the annual Maui Jazz & Blues Festival and Kapalua Wine & Food Festival, along with the ongoing Kapalua Concert Series and Tales of the Kapa Moe Luau. On any given week, guests can choose from more than 70 activities aimed at nourishing the mind, body, and soul—from yoga and fitness classes to sound healing and walking meditations. On-site boutiques and convenience stores add ease, while thoughtfully placed artifacts and local artwork deepen the connection to Hawaiian culture.
What to do nearby
Active travelers can take advantage of two championship golf courses and a driving range, on-property tennis and pickleball courts, and easy access to D.T. Fleming Beach Park for sunbathing, bodyboarding, or a beach run. Walk to Makaluapuna Point (also nicknamed as the Dragon’s Teeth Trail) for a casual coastal hike through dramatic lava rock formations that includes a meditative labyrinth. Zip-lining, ATV tours, and bike rentals are also available nearby.
The backstory
During construction in 1987, crews uncovered an ancient burial site containing the iwi (bones) of Native Hawaiians dating as far back as 850 C.E. The discovery led to a complete redesign of the project, shifting the hotel inland to preserve the sacred site and honor the ancestors who remain there. The property opened as the Ritz-Carlton Maui in the ’90s.
Insider tip
If you’re traveling with a companion and plan to spend most of your time on the property, spring for access to The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge at the Anuenue Room. This exclusive space includes a private lounge, dedicated concierge, open bar, and a buffet that refreshes five times a day.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Yes, there’s even a playground and dedicated kids activities
Accessibility:
Yes; the property features accessible rooms with features like bathroom grab rails and self-closing doors, as well as elevators throughout the common spaces and accessible access to the pools
Wellness:
Yes, there are plenty of classes and treatments at The Ritz-Carlton Spa Maui, Kapalua
Loyalty group/hotel brand:
Ritz-Carlton, loyalty group Marriott Bonvoy
