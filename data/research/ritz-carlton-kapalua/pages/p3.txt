45th Annual Kapalua Food & Wine Festival
Home to the annual Kapalua Wine & Food Festival, one of the nation’s most iconic culinary events. This extraordinary weekend brings together world-class vintners and acclaimed chefs, alongside Maui’s favorite restaurants and local purveyors. Enjoy an inspiring lineup of experiences, from intimate winemaker dinners and interactive tasting seminars to rare wine discoveries from around the globe. Sip, savor, and celebrate in the spirit of aloha as Kapalua transforms into a premier destination for food and wine enthusiasts.

Kapalua Wine and Chef Series
Enjoy an intimate evening of exceptional cuisine in a stunning Kapalua setting. Held the second Saturday of each month, featuring Michelin-starred chefs and renowned winemakers.
Chef's Table
Join us every Friday for Chef’s Table—an interactive dining experience featuring seasonal dishes by Banyan Tree’s culinary team, shared at a community table for up to 10 guests.
The Art of Mixology
Unlock the art of mixology with our expertly crafted signature cocktails, featuring locally inspired flavors. Engage in a hands-on session with our mixologists.
Keiki Milkshake Class
Join our milkshake masters for a sweet journey where your keiki can enjoy the art of ‘milkshake making’ and indulge in their creations. $35+, includes tasting.
Tales of The Kapa Moe Lū'au
Savor authentic Hawaiian flavors at Tales of the Kapa Moe Lu‘au, paired with culture and storytelling in an elegant open-air setting.
