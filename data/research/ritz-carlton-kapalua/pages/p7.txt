Kapalua Wine & Food Festival
Kapalua Wine & Food Festival is the longest-running and most prestigious festival of its kind in Hawaii. Every June, the world’s most renowned winemakers, top sommeliers, and celebrated chefs come to Kapalua for a weekend of exhibitions, wine tastings, and gala events.
Learn More
Whale Tales
Whale Tales is an annual four-day event community event on Maui that brings together marine experts with thousands of residents and visitors from around the world every February. World-renowned presenters join the Whale Trust research team on Maui to collaborate and share the latest findings on humpback whales and their environment.
Learn More

Local Community Initiatives
The resort’s Mauka to Makai Preservation of Place Project was established to benefit two local nonprofit organizations, each focused on preserving what makes Maui special.

The Pu’u Kukui Watershed is Hawaii’s largest private nature preserve and is home to rare and endangered flora and fauna.

Hui O Wa’a Kaulua

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua is Kapalua Airport - Maui (JHM). JHM is located approximately 3.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua have an airport shuttle to Kapalua Airport - Maui (JHM)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua does not have an airport shuttle to Kapalua Airport - Maui.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites.
