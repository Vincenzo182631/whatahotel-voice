Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua
Indulge in the Magic of Kapalua
Say "Mele Kalikimaka" and experience the magic of the holiday season at The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua. With a picturesque "winter" setting of dramatic ocean views, swaying palms, and warm island breezes, the resort has put a signature Hawaiian twist on classic holiday traditions. Enjoy festive celebrations, holiday lights, family friendly activities, and seasonal spa-liday treatments.
Holiday Cheer in Maui
Savor gourmet dining, live music, and curated events that blend festive cheer with aloha spirit.

Story Time with Santa and Mrs. Claus
Join Santa and Mrs. Claus for a cozy storytelling session at The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua. Families are invited to gather for festive tales, warm holiday cheer, and magical moments that spark imagination and joy.
Tales of The Kapa Moe Lū'au
Experience an unforgettable evening of culture, cuisine, and storytelling at Tales of the Kapa Moe Lu‘au, held at the Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua. Set in the elegant, open-air Aloha Garden Pavilion, Tales of the Kapa Moe Lu‘au showcases authentic Hawaiian chanting and hula, beautifully handmade costumes, an aerial performance, and a dramatic fire knife dance.
Reserve Now
Holiday Harvest Dinner at Banyan Tree
On December 25, celebrate the spirit of the season with an exquisite culinary experience at Banyan Tree, where coastal cuisine meets festive elegance. The Holiday Harvest Dinner is a specially curated prix-fixe menu, designed to showcase the bounty of Maui’s land and sea through a multi-course journey of flavors.
