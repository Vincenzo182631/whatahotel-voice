Experiences
Watching whales dance above the water. Sailing across the crystal clear Pacific Ocean. Soaring like a bird, ziplining between the trees. Tennis & Pickleball
Enjoy pickleball and tennis courts conveniently located on property, available to hotel guests on a first-come, first-served basis.
Golf
Discover world-class golf at Kapalua’s Plantation Course, where championship play meets breathtaking Maui views.
Exclusive Poolside & Beachfront Retreats
Discover a more elevated way to relax with our exclusive VIP experiences. Enjoy the comfort of poolside VIP Cabanas and Oasis Beds, or retreat to a private Casabella overlooking award-winning D.T. Fleming Beach. Each offers premium seating, attentive service, and food and beverage offerings designed to enhance your day in paradise.
Bike Riding
Explore the stunning landscapes and natural beauty of the Kapalua and grab a snack from Honolua Store with our stylish cruiser bikes. Check the bikes out from the valet desk.

Spend the day enjoying a variety of outdoor recreation, from friendly lawn games and cornhole to basketball, bocce ball, shuffleboard, and bucket golf. Equipment is available at the pool and outside of the Ambassadors Hale.

Explore Kapalua
ATV Rides
ATV rides in Kapalua offer an exciting way to explore the rugged and beautiful landscapes of West Maui. One of the best places to experience this adventure is with Maui Off Road Adventures.
Horseback Riding
At Ironwood Ranch, embark on guided horseback rides through lush valleys, ironwood forests, and pineapple fields. The trails offer spectacular panoramic views of Lanai and Molokai.
Hiking
Curated by nature, Kapalua’s scenic trails invite discovery at every turn. Traverse dramatic ridgelines, wander past secluded lakes, and follow breathtaking coastal pathways framed by sweeping ocean views. From Mahana Ridge to the shoreline trail connecting Kapalua Bay and D.T. Fleming Beach, unforgettable island experiences await.
Ziplining
Elevate your Maui adventure with an exhilarating zipline experience above the island's breathtaking landscapes. Glide over lush valleys and dramatic ridgelines while enjoying sweeping views that reveal West Maui's natural beauty from a truly extraordinary vantage point.
Helewai Eco Tours
Experience the beauty of Maui’s native forests and cultural heritage on a guided hike with HeleWai Eco Tours, exploring the Honolua Ridgeline and Puʻu Kukui Watershed Preserve.
Find Adventure in Maui
Designed as a destination for families, couples, and group retreats, our resort offers plenty of things to do in Kapalua for every guest. Ocean-view tennis, luxurious poolside cabanas, sound-healing meditation, surfing, and trail hikes are among the resort’s activities.

Ambassadors of the Environment
An exclusive partnership with Jean-Michel Cousteau’s Ocean Futures Society found at only six Ritz-Carlton hotels worldwide, Ambassadors of the Environment is a unique program that introduces guests to the local culture and environment. Through guided, family-friendly excursions, Maui becomes a living classroom where adults and children learn about the environment and leave with an appreciation and respect for nature.
