# Welcome to Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai

## Tap into the spirit of our 5-star Dubai luxury hotel

Elegantly towering above the azure Dubai Marina and the soaring cityscape, the iconic Grosvenor House Dubai, a Luxury Collection Hotel, is a destination unlike any other. Nestled in the renowned Dubai Marina, our Dubai family hotel is near the city center and within minutes of Dubai Internet City, Media City, golf clubs, malls, and more. As one of Dubai's premier business hotels, our sophisticated & modern property boasts two gyms, two outdoor pools, and a serene spa. These luxurious facilities near Dubai Marina unite with our exquisite dining destinations, modern meeting facilities & access to the private beach at Le Royal Méridien Beach Resort & Spa for an inimitable 5-star hotel experience. Fusing marble floors, natural hues, modern décor, and large windows overlooking turquoise waters, our 750 hotel rooms and suites are ideal for extended stays, providing serene escapes high above Dubai Marina, enhanced by 5-star details. Experience the epitome of a luxury Dubai hotel at Marriott Grosvenor House.

### An Iconic Symbol of Dubai

Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai, and its two iconic 45-story towers, Tower One and Tower Two, redefines the world of luxury. ## Level 5 Lounge

Level 5 is replete with the very best amenities for travelers staying in the Premier Rooms and Suites in Tower Two and Marriott Bonvoy members staying in Tower One.

Learn More

Level 5 is a privileged business and leisure lounge located on the fifth floor of Grosvenor House Tower Two. It offers the finest amenities for business and leisure travelers staying in our Premier Rooms in Tower Two, Superior Deluxe Suites in Tower One, and Marriott Bonvoy Elite Members (Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador) staying in both towers.

Offering a distinctive blend of business, leisure, and family amenities, Level 5 is the largest facility of its kind in the UAE and reinforces Grosvenor House Dubai’s reputation as the premier business and leisure property in the Emirates. Designed with sophistication in mind, Level 5 exudes refined elegance, furnished in understated browns and creams that complement the rich dark-wooden floors throughout. Guests can also enjoy breathtaking views of the Dubai Marina.

Exclusive to guests staying in Premier Rooms in Tower Two, Superior Deluxe Suites in Tower One, and Marriott Bonvoy Elite Members (Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador), Level 5 serves a fine selection of delicious fare throughout the day—from breakfast between 6 am and 11 am, to light bites and afternoon tea from 2 pm to 4 pm. Pre-dinner drinks and canapés are available between 6 pm and 8 pm. Additionally, a bespoke all-day à la carte food and beverage menu from our Main Kitchen is available upon request (at an extra cost), for those wishing to dine at their own leisure from morning until midnight.

For breakfast, guests can choose from a variety of fresh buffet items, including a wide range of freshly squeezed healthy juices and made-to-order omelettes prepared by the chef at the dedicated egg station.

Level 5 boasts three lounge areas, plus a separate bar lounge, family lounge, and a meeting room. Visitors can enjoy a range of business tools, including a selection of books and complimentary WiFi access. The private meeting room accommodates up to eight people, features floor-to-ceiling windows, and offers incredible views of Dubai Marina. The meeting room is available by advance booking.

A dedicated family lounge caters exclusively to younger guests, allowing families to relax in a comfortable, family-friendly environment.

**Rules and Regulations** Level 5 is open exclusively to guests staying in Premier Rooms in Tower Two, Superior Deluxe Suites in Tower One, and Marriott Bonvoy Elite Members (Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador).

Dress Code: Smart casual all day. Shorts are permitted until 18:00 hrs. During pre-dinner (6 pm to 8 pm), only long trousers and closed shoes are allowed.

Alcohol is served only to guests aged 21 and above.

Children aged 12 and above are welcome in the lounge.

Children under 12 are permitted only in the family lounge during pre-dinner drinks (6 pm to 8 pm).

Guests staying in Premier Rooms in Tower Two, Superior Deluxe Suites in Tower One, and Marriott Bonvoy Elite Members are entitled to bring one guest free of charge into the lounge. Any additional guests beyond the first will be charged for food and beverage consumed on a pay-per-use basis.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai?

The check-in time at Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai.

What are the parking options at Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai?

On-site parking is available at Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai booking.

What is the closest airport near Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai?

The closest airport near Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai is Dubai International Airport (DXB). DXB is located approximately 33.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai have a Level 5 lounge?

Yes, Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Dubai has Level 5 lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Level 5 lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
