# Welcome to Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini

## Shaped by nature, enhanced by style

Carved into Caldera cliffs, Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, overlooks the turquoise expanse of the Aegean Sea. Designed using Cycladic architecture, where curves and arches blend with a muted color...See More palette and light enhancing spaces, the hotel is a favored destination for honeymoons and romantic escapes. Linked by a network of cobbled pathways, Mystique’s 43 suites and villas, two pools, Elios Spa, fitness center, panoramic sea views across to the active volcano that has shaped this island. The dining options include Lure, a high-end gastronomical experience highlighting the rich epicurean heritage of Santorini and Charisma, for breakfast and lunch, inviting guests in a relaxing ambiance with the views of the infinity-edge pool and the midnight blue of the Aegean Sea. Mystique has been recognized as one of the “World’s Best Hotels” in Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards for two consecutive years and ranked #1 in “Intent to Recommend” among all Marriott Luxury Brand Hotels in the EMEA region.See Less

A favored destination for honeymoons and romantic escapes, Mystique emanates a carefree spirit, imparting an understated, exclusive feel, so reminiscent of the spectacular location.

## Suites & Villas with Natural, Bespoke Design

An exclusive hideaway in the natural world, Mystique perfectly lends itself to wider exploration, offering a tantalizing insight into the captivating island of Santorini and its unique traditions, romance and wild beauty. The design follows the emblematic Cycladic architecture, where gentle curves and sculpted arches blend with a muted color palette and light enhancing spaces.

Learn More

## Natural Materials and Contemporary Twist

Generating space and light into each guest room, the interiors are a combination of traditional regional charm and natural untamed materials with a contemporary twist and up-to-the-minute technology. Mystique's furniture are created by Frank Lefebvre, who is a nature-inspired designer. Each item showcases Lefebvre’s unique passion to breathe new life into natural materials, such as driftwood, and combine it with pebbles and lacquered wood to bring nature to the hotel’s interiors.

Learn More

### Breathtaking Location, Awe-Inspiring Views

## Overlooking the Sea

Carved into the rugged Caldera cliffs of Oia in Santorini, Mystique overlooks the turquoise expanse of the Aegean Sea that encircles the island of Santorini. Mystique's iconic location on the lower side of the Caldera offers unobstructed, infinity views to the Aegean Sea and the volcano, that are enjoyed from every spot of the hotel, including all restaurants and bars, the spa and fitness center among others.

## Captivating Romance

The unique Oia scenery imparts a tranquil and discreet quality to Mystique that overlooks the breathtaking Santorini panorama, making it an ideal setting for intimate, romantic escapes.

### Mediterranean Dining

Inspired dining options exalted by authentic, Greek-influenced cuisine are available whether guests choose to eat in the privacy of their own terrace or at one of Mystique’s restaurants. Most of the vegetables and herbs used to prepare the refined dishes at Mystique’s restaurants, are cultivated in the hotel’s organic farm in the village of Megalohori. ## Rich History

An immense volcanic eruption over three and a half thousand years ago, around 1645 BC, formed what are now Santorini’s beautiful trademarks: striking cliffs and dramatic coasts. Some say that this was the eruption that led to the demise of the lost city of Atlantis. True or not, Santorini is bathed in history, and Mystique has the distinct advantage of being right in the middle of it all.

## About Ownership

A series of serendipitous events led Kalia and Antonis Eliopoulos to hospitality, armed with a natural talent for piecing together a compelling narrative. It was the pull of Santorini, a mesmerizing island Antonis Eliopoulos had been visiting since childhood, that changed their destiny. On one of his countless trips here, he decided to buy a rustic vineyard, which turned into their first hotel in 1993 – Vedema, a Luxury Collection Resort, a unique village-like setting loved by locals and guests alike. Their passion for hospitality motivated them to take on the iconic Oía village, where they introduced in 2007 Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, a secluded hideaway carved into the rugged caldera cliffs and boasting glorious views to the infinite blue of the Aegean archipelago.

## Additional Properties

The duo knew that their third hotel on the volcanic island, Istoria, a Member of Design Hotels, had to be on the magical black sand beach of Perivolos, where they transformed a former palazzo of a legendary socialite to a serene retreat for sophisticated travelers. Wishing to expand their horizons, in 2018 they journeyed to the nearby island of Paros, for their fourth property, Parīlio, a Member of Design Hotels, opened in July 2019, where they crafted an artful statement amid a world of pine-fringed coves and white-washed historical villages.

## Awards & Recognitions

Every distinction we receive begins with the people who bring Mystique to life and the guests who trust us with their most meaningful moments. In 2025, Mystique ranked #1 in Intent to Recommend (ITR) among Marriott International Luxury Brand properties in EMEA, a reflection of heartfelt service and genuine connections that inspire guests to share their experience. Mystique was also recognized among the World’s Best Hotels in the Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards 2025, celebrating destinations that move travelers through care, emotion, and memory. These recognitions embody the essence of Mystique: intuitive hospitality, quiet excellence, and moments that stay with you long after you leave.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini?

The check-in time at Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini.

What are the parking options at Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini?

On-site valet parking is available at Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini. The daily valet fee is €15.00 per day.

Does Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. What is the closest airport near Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini?

The closest airport near Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Santorini is Santorini - Thira National Airport (JTR). JTR is located approximately 16.0 KM from the hotel.
