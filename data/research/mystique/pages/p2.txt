## Accommodations

Designed using Cycladic architecture, where gentle curves and sculpted arches blend with a muted color palette and light enhancing spaces, Mystique is an exclusive hideaway that offers gloriously open...See More horizons, in a stunning, legendary location. All of the stone-crafted, cave-style suites and villas boast secluded terraces that peer out onto the deep blue Aegean Sea and the quietly active volcano. Lovingly handcrafted from local wood and stone, effortlessly harnessing the elegance of simplicity, all suites are a wondrous exploration of free-spirited driftwood, linen textiles, and intriguing carved patterns on the walls.See Less

### Holistic Villa

Designed to be Mystique’s most spacious room, the Holistic Villa can host up to four persons in its airy interiors that feature a large living area, and two cave-style bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms, while the expansive private terrace features an infinity pool and a jacuzzi. Majestic villa located at the highest point of Mystique, with airy living and dining area, two bedrooms with the Luxury Collection beds and en-suite bathrooms with rain showers. Its vast terrace with hydromassage infinity pool, outdoor jacuzzi, sunbeds and open-air dining area offers captivating views to the Caldera.
