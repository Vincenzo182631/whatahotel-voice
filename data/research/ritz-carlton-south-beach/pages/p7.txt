Discover What Brings Miami to Life From This Beachfront Hotel
Experiences
Immerse yourself in the colorful history and vibrant cultures that draw visitors from around the world to South Beach, Miami. Located on famed Lincoln Road in the heart of the Art Deco Historic District, the local area offers a blend of oceanfront relaxation with cosmopolitan energy. Explore iconic attractions like the Vizcaya Museum and Gardens, The Wolfsonian, Miami City Ballet, and The Bass Museum. Discover nearby neighborhoods like Wynwood, the Design District, and Little Havana. Savor traditional Cuban cuisine, admire Art Deco architecture, shop luxury labels, or simply relax an dunwind on the Miami Beach sand.
Discover Miami with The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach
At The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach, thoughtfully curated experiences invite guests to connect with the destination both within the hotel and beyond. From refined wellness and coastal relaxation to engaging cultural moments throughout the city, every experience is designed with the elegance and care that define The Ritz-Carlton.

Born out of love in 1904, Krigler is a legendary fragrance house renowned for its mysteriously alluring and elite scents. From classic favorites to modern reinterpretations, Krigler continues its tradition of exceptional craftsmanship and olfactory artistry. Duchamp Jewelers
Duchamp Jewelers, located in the hotel lobby, is a boutique founded by Coralie and Patrick Duchamp, who share over 50 years of combined expertise.
