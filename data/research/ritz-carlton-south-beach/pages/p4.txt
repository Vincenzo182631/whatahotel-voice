The Ritz-Carlton Spa, South Beach
The Ritz-Carlton Spa, South Beach offers a serene sanctuary rooted in a mindful approach to wellness. Designed to inspire rest, relaxation, and rejuvenation, our spa in Miami features 11 tranquil treatment rooms, a co-ed relaxation lounge, separate male and female lounges, locker rooms, and steam and sauna facilities. Signature experiences include the Awakening Bamboo Massage and Resilience Facial, complemented by a full suite of salon services, including nail and hair treatments. A thoughtfully curated retail boutique allows guests to continue their wellness journey beyond the spa.

Spa Policies
Minimum Age
Guests at The Spa and Fitness Center must be at least 18 years of age.
Late Arrival
Arriving late will limit the time for your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so the next guest will not be delayed. The ful value of your treatment will be applied.
Cancellation
If you must reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify us 24 hours before your scheduled appointment to avoid being charged the full service fee.
Payment
We accept all major credit cards and traveler’s checks. You may also charge spa services to your hotel bill. For your convenience, payment is also accepted prior to your treatments, upon request.
Gratuities
With your permission a 20% service charge will be added for each spa or salon service you receive. If your services were satisfactory, please feel free to leave an additional gratuity that will be distributed to the Spa staff who served you during your visit.
