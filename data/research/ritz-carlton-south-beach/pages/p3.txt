Miami-Inspired Dining Experiences
Dining
An iconic destination in one of Miami Beach’s most legendary neighborhoods, The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach is home to some of the city's most sought-after Miami Beach restaurants. Whether guests are looking for a leisurely poolside brunch or dinner in a lively atmosphere, the resort offers a range of exceptional culinary options designed to delight every palate. Acclaimed Spanish-American chef, restaurateur, and humanitarian José Andrés brings his deep expertise in Mediterranean cuisine to the resort’s signature venues—Zaytinya, DiLido Beach Bar, and Lapidus Bar—each offering a distinct and memorable dining experience.
Festive Season at The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach
Celebrate the season at The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach, where festive traditions, exceptional José Andrés Group dining, oceanfront experiences, and seasonal spa offerings await. From cherished family moments to spirited evenings by the sea, discover our curated holiday programming.
