THE MAGNIFICENT MILE

Michigan Ave, Chicago, IL 60611

Sprawling 13 blocks, the Magnificent Mile is Chicago's most famous shopping area. Wander down the street, and discover a dizzying array of high-end shops, lavish restaurants, and iconic architecture. Whether you're planning to shop, sightsee or enjoy a good meal, the Mag Mile will not disappoint.

CHICAGO ARCHITECTURAL BOAT TOUR

111 E Wacker Dr, Chicago, IL 60601

Our city is famous for its incredible architecture, and there's no better way to explore these gems than with an architectural cruise on the Chicago River. Sit back and take in the picturesque sights, while your docent shares captivating stories about each building.

Loading...

THE MERCHANDISE MART

222 W Merchandise Mart Plaza, Chicago, IL 60654

Opened in 1930, Merchandise Mart is an icon that, at the time, took the title of the world's largest building. It has four million square feet of floor space, housing offices and home showrooms. Keep an eye out for "Art on theMart", where digital art displays will be projected onto the building.

THE SHOPS AT NORTH BRIDGE

520 North Michigan Avenue, Chicago, IL 60611

Helmed as the city's most stylish shopping destination, The Shops at North Bridge boasts an array of stores that'll excite any fashionista. Browse through big names like Nordstrom, MCM Worldwide, or Hugo Boss. And when you need a break, grab a bite at any of the chic restaurants nearby.
