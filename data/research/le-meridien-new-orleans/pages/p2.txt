### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Le Méridien New Orleans have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien New Orleans have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Le Méridien New Orleans?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at Le Méridien New Orleans. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Le Méridien New Orleans?

No, the rooms at Le Méridien New Orleans do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at Le Méridien New Orleans have fridges?

No, rooms at Le Méridien New Orleans do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at Le Méridien New Orleans have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien New Orleans have a Coffee/tea maker.
