# Welcome to Le Méridien New Orleans

## Embrace the modern luxury of our New Orleans hotel

Experience luxury at the recently renovated Le Méridien New Orleans, one of the hotels on Poydras Street, New Orleans, in the heart of downtown. Located in a quiet area, our New Orleans hotel near the...See More French Quarter and the renowned Arts District offers easy access to the city's best attractions. Enjoy modern design and upscale amenities at our boutique 4-star hotel, blending European heritage with contemporary style. As a rooftop pool hotel in New Orleans that travelers love, we invite you to relax poolside, stay active in our 24-hour fitness center, and savor all-day dining overlooking Lafayette Square. Hosting an event? Take advantage of our elegant venues, custom catering, and exceptional meeting services. Our newly renovated rooms and suites feature plush bedding, chic furnishings, and destination-inspired artwork. Whether you're here for business or leisure, our pet-friendly hotel in New Orleans ensures a memorable stay with comfort and convenience at every turn.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien New Orleans?

The check-in time at Le Méridien New Orleans is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Le Méridien New Orleans allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien New Orleans. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien New Orleans?

On-site valet parking is available at Le Méridien New Orleans. The daily valet fee is $52.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien New Orleans have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien New Orleans has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien New Orleans booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien New Orleans?

The closest airport near Le Méridien New Orleans is Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY). MSY is located approximately 15.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien New Orleans have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Le Méridien New Orleans does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
