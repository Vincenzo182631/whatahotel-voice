# ESPACIO SPA

## Massages

Whether your goal is to unwind, balance, or uplift, your massage will be expertly tailored to your desired intention and individual needs. We invite you to share details on your health considerations and treatment preferences so that we can create your perfect experience.

## Custom Services

ESPACIO Spa’s highly skilled therapists specialize in creating personalized treatments, using rare botanicals and oceanic elements to deliver a one-of-a-kind sensory experience tailored to your intention for renewal, balance, or indulgence.

## In-Room Services

Enjoy a luxurious treatment from the comfort of your suite. ESPACIO Spa offers in-room massage and wellness experiences, allowing you to relax in total privacy. Available for individuals, couples, or special occasions.
