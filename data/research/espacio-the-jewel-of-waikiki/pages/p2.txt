# Oceanfront Suites

## Endless Horizons, Private Sanctuary

Perched above the waves, ESPACIO’s Oceanfront Suites offer a rare blend of complete privacy, sweeping ocean views, and signature luxury. Occupying the uppermost floors, each suite is a full-floor residence, allowing you to experience the island’s beauty from an unmatched perspective. From sunrise over Diamond Head to vibrant Waikiki sunsets, every moment spent here is designed to be unforgettable.
