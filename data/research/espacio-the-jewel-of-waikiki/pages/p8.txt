### Memorable Meals

Each stay at ESPACIO comes with complimentary daily breakfast and a dining credit at our fine-dining restaurant, Mugen (available Tue-Sat only). Grocery service or in-suite dining is available to guests looking to dine in the privacy of their own suite. For a truly unique culinary experience, a private chef can be arranged with advance reservations to entertain in your suite.

### Heart of Waikiki

At the most coveted address on Kalakaua Avenue, ESPACIO places you steps from Waikiki Beach and the vibrant energy of Honolulu. Discover a sanctuary where oceanfront serenity and world-class shopping, dining, and culture are all within effortless reach.

### Rooftop Soiree

Celebrate with a bespoke rooftop soirée, crafted exclusively for you. Enjoy unobstructed ocean views, a private chef experience, gourmet catering options, butler service, curated décor, and access to the rooftop pool.

### Afternoon Tea

Savor an afternoon of elegance with ESPACIO’s reimagined tea experience. Delight in rare blends, and exquisite pastries, in a setting as breathtaking as the flavors themselves.
