Espacio The Jewel of Waikiki
An elegant oasis steps from one of the world’s most famous beaches.

Why book?
With only nine suites—each occupying its own floor—Espacio’s bespoke service (which includes an attentive butler and a house car with a driver) and location directly across from Waikiki’s popular stretch of beach makes it a standout in an area where space is limited and personalized service is rare.
Set the scene
Amidst the chaos of street performers, tour kiosks, and streams of people passing by with shopping bags, strollers, and surfboards in tow, Espacio’s small, unassuming entrance between retail shops on Kalakaua Avenue is practically hidden in plain sight. Fortunately, you don’t have to search for it; your private driver, arranged by the hotel, will fetch you from the airport in a sleek black SUV and deliver you to the front door like the VIP you pretty much certainly are if you're booking this exclusive—and pricey—property.
Upon my arrival, two team members were waiting curbside to greet me, quickly whisking me inside the sliding glass doors and up to the serene reception area where several more team members offered me a seat on the couch with a view of Kuhio Beach. I was treated to an oshibori (a Japanese hospitality tradition involving a moist towel to refresh the guest), a glass of Champagne, and island snacks. During my stay at Espacio, I never bumped into other guests, and that’s by design. There are only nine suites, and each one takes up an entire floor, so you’ll never see another guest in your hallway (though you may occasionally share the elevator), and time spent at the rooftop infinity pool is by reservation to ensure you have a private experience there, too.
The backstory
Espacio opened in September 2019, bringing a fresh, refined, and incomparable experience to this beachside destination, which was already overflowing with sprawling, often-crowded, and impersonal resorts. Although it’s managed by the Honolulu-based Aqua-Aston Hospitality group, which is a part of the larger Marriott Vacations Worldwide family, the
Espacio luxury brand
is distinct in its smaller-scale properties that are rooted in omotenashi, the Japanese approach to selfless hospitality with a focus on attention to detail and anticipating guests’ needs. The brand also has a handful of luxury properties in Japan (including one opening in October 2025 across from the 17th-century Nagoya Castle) and plans to introduce a “floating haven of luxury” soon in the form of a private yacht.
The rooms
Each spacious suite features multiple rooms (two-bedrooms and three-bedrooms are available), a large living area, a chef’s kitchen, and hot tubs on private balconies facing the beach. Dashes of color that reflect the room’s specific color theme (the Emerald Suite has subtle greens incorporated into throw pillows and bench cushions, for example) subtly brighten the otherwise muted palette. Italian marble and thoughtful features such as a private dry sauna, wooden stools in the shower, TOTO washlets, and deep soaking tubs add a sophisticated touch and a nod to Japanese style. Personalized butler service, in-room dining, a two-in-one laundry machine, and the 2,250-square-foot, residence-like suites ensure guests have high-level service, plus the luxury of peace and privacy (despite being so centrally located)
and
the comforts of home. The ninth-floor, three-bedroom Peridot Suite is the ultimate retreat for families looking for an elegant stay with plenty of space and ocean views.
Food and drink
Mugen, the intimate, award-winning on-site restaurant, delivers culinary magic, from divine breakfast options that include lemon ricotta pancakes to an exclusive afternoon tea (limited to 12 seats, only available on Saturdays) and elegant dinners featuring creative and colorful dishes showcasing ingredients from the islands, crafted by Chef Colin Sato and paired with wines by the in-house sommelier. For a romantic evening in, book a private in-suite kaiseki dinner by Chef Mamoru Tatemori. Guests of Espacio can now take advantage of the hotel’s new “Luxury Without Limits” program, the only culinary all-inclusive offering in Hawaii. It includes breakfast, in-suite and poolside dining during the day, a complimentary wine tasting, and a $300 dining credit for Mugen.
The spa
The spacious suites didn’t leave much room for the hotel’s spa, which is quite small for a luxury hotel. In-suite treatments are the way to go. Be sure to share your goals in advance; like everything at Espacio, the spa experience is tailored to your needs.
The neighborhood/area
This is a popular area along one of the busiest streets in Waikiki, so don’t expect the same serenity outside the hotel that you find inside. But such a buzzing neighborhood does offer a lot of convenience; you can walk across the street to Kuhio Beach for a swim, take a surf lesson at one of the famous breaks, do some shopping at the nearby International Marketplace, jog around Kapiolani Park, hike up Diamond Head, or grab a cocktail at Duke’s beach bar.
The service
Inspired by the Japanese concept of omotenashi, attentive service here is the priority, with a butler, concierge, and driver at the ready. With only nine suites, even if the hotel is full, the team can truly focus on providing top-notch, personalized service. Private chefs, wellness activities, and secluded rooftop experiences are all possibilities, and if you’ve got plans outside the neighborhood, the house car can drop you off or pick you up (anywhere from Kahala to downtown)—at no extra charge.
For families
Multi-room suites in such a convenient location—across from the beach and in close proximity to some of the area's best restaurants and attractions—make this a perfect place for families to feel at home.
Eco effort
Smart energy systems, waste reduction policies, refillable toiletries, reef-safe sunscreen, digital concierge services, and the elimination of plastic water bottles all help to reduce the property’s footprint. Mugen’s focus on locally sourced, seasonal ingredients and minimal waste also ensures a more sustainable dining experience.
Accessibility
Street-level automatic doors that lead directly to a large elevator set the tone for an accessible stay, and Espacio’s fifth-floor Coral Suite is ADA-friendly, offering a roomy layout with features that include lowered fixtures, TDD equipment, and a roll-in shower. The butler and team are available to assist if more specific needs arise.
Anything left to mention?
Views from the lanais in the lower floor suites are of the street, beach, and large banyan tree in front, and some street noise can make its way up to those floors. If you’re looking for more ocean and less noise, go for an upper-level suite. Also worth noting when it comes to views: The bedrooms at the back of the suites won’t have the beach or ocean views, so temper your expectations and plan to spend your time in the large living area, private lanai, and hot tub.
For a unique experience only offered at Espacio, book the Champagne and caviar service. The panoramic sunset views of Waikiki and the surf below from the rooftop pool deck are not to be missed.
