# Ocean View Suites

## Where Tranquility Meets Elegance

Perched just above the radiant heart of Waikiki, ESPACIO’s Ocean View Suites offer a private, full-floor sanctuary where the shimmering Pacific is your daily backdrop. With refined interiors and sweeping views, each suite invites you to relax, rejuvenate, and reconnect in effortless island luxury. From sunrise glows to twilight reflections, the Pacific is never out of sight—and serenity is never out of reach.
