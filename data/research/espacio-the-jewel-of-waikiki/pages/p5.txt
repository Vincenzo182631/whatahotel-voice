# Penthouse Diamond Suite

## The Pinnacle of Oceanfront Luxury

High above the sparkling shoreline of Waikiki, the Penthouse Diamond Suite at ESPACIO offers a world reserved for the few. Designed for those who demand nothing less than perfection, this full-floor sanctuary combines refined living with the breathtaking natural beauty of Hawaiʻi. From sunrise over Diamond Head to sunset over the Pacific, every moment in the Penthouse is framed by sweeping, unobstructed views.

### Oceanfront 2 Bedroom + Den

Top Floor

1-6 People

2 King Beds + Sofa Bed

The Penthouse Diamond Suite offers over 2,250 square feet of elegantly appointed living space, thoughtfully designed for relaxation, entertainment, and rejuvenation.

- Two spacious bedrooms each with king-size beds, plus a private den perfect for quiet reflection or remote work
- Three and a half bathrooms featuring deep soaking tubs, glass walk-in showers, and TOTO Washlets
- Expansive furnished balcony overlooking Waikiki Beach, complete with a private Jacuzzi® soaking tub and plush daybeds
- Gourmet chef’s kitchen equipped with premium European appliances and a 5-burner cooktop
- Private elevator and entryway opening directly into your suite
- Dry sauna within your private space for ultimate relaxation
- Smart Home technology controlling lighting, climate, and privacy settings seamlessly

Every corner of the Penthouse invites you to live effortlessly, wrapped in understated luxury.
