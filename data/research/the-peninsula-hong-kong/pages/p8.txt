This autumn, The Peninsula Hong Kong invites all guests to step into the enchanting universe of _CHARLIE AND THE CHOCOLATE FACTORY_. Inspired by the musical, the “Grande Dame” is bringing the story to life through limited-time experiences – from sky-high afternoon tea at Felix to upcoming stay offers with chocolate-led surprises.

## A Golden Dream of Wonder and Imagination

The Peninsula Hong Kong partners with Broadway musical _CHARLIE AND THE CHOCOLATE FACTORY_, debuting in Hong Kong this November, offering themed dining, activities and stay packages inspired by the enchanting story.

## CHARLIE AND THE CHOCOLATE FACTORY Afternoon Tea at Felix

High above Victoria Harbour, top-floor restaurant Felix sets the stage for an afternoon of pure imagination. Inspired by _CHARLIE AND THE CHOCOLATE FACTORY_, this limited-time afternoon tea transforms classic treats into playful, cocoa-kissed creations, inviting guests into a world where every bite sparks wonder.
