# Our Exceptional Culinary Team's Mastery at The Peninsula Hong Kong

Every dining experience at The Peninsula embodies the exceptional gastronomic artistry of our chefs. The members of our culinary team have developed their virtuosic skills in some of the world’s most acclaimed kitchens, and continue to hone their craft at The Peninsula. Their endlessly innovative and masterful cuisine has earned our restaurants some of the culinary world’s highest accolades – including multiple Michelin stars.

Chinese Cuisine Executive Chef

### Mr Lam Yuk Ming

Spring Moon’s Chinese Cuisine Executive Chef since 2019, Chef Lam emphasises authenticity and seasonality in his preparations, using only the freshest ingredients to create a delicate balance of tradition and innovation in his dishes. Prior joining The Peninsula Hong Kong, Chef Lam’s two-decade long career including significant positions at acclaimed Chinese restaurants throughout Asia, including the Michelin-starred Pearl Dragon in Macau.

Executive Sous Chef

### Mr Albin Gobil

First appointed as Gaddi’s Chef de Cuisine in 2019, Chef Gobil has been the restaurant’s Executive Sous Chef since 2023. Prior to joining The Peninsula Hong Kong, Chef Gobil worked for more than a decade at Michelin-starred establishments in London, where he developed his relentless dedication to culinary excellence. He is renowned for his “modern classic" style of cuisine, a less-is-more approach that allows carefully selected flavours to shine in each dish.

Executive Sous Chef

### Mr Terrence Chiu

Chef Chiu first joined The Peninsula Hong Kong in 2009 as Banqueting Chef, an esteemed post which he still occupies today. Working alongside Chef Boyer, he applies both creativity and impeccable technique to ensure culinary excellence at every hotel function.

Chef De Cuisine of Gaddi's

### Ms Anne-Sophie Nicolas

Appointed as Gaddi's new Chef de Cuisine in May 2024, Chef Nicolas is the first female to hold the position in the restaurant’s 70-year history. A rising culinary star, Chef Nicolas brings more than 15 years of experience in Michelin-starred restaurants to her new role. Her most recent position was as Chef de Cuisine at L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon in Hong Kong, where her outstanding leadership and dedication to excellence helped to create three Michelin-starred experiences.
