5 Star Luxurious Spa Treatments The Peninsula Spa item 2

A serene oasis where guests can pursue harmony of mind, body, and spirit, our expansive 14-room spa offers a sumptuous menu of therapeutic treatments and relaxing rituals. Our highly trained therapists perform treatments using some of the world’s most exclusive and naturally sourced product lines – including Margy’s Monte Carlo and Voya – in a soothingly designed environment featuring warm-toned marble, weathered wood, and pale woven textiles. Six deluxe treatment rooms have stunning views over Victoria Harbour; the singularly luxurious couples’ spa suite, boasting an enormous harbour-view whirlpool tub for two, offers a private “spa-within-a-spa” experience.

All spa guests are free to enjoy amenities such as men’s and women’s relaxation rooms, hammam-style steam rooms, saunas, and special aromatherapy experience showers.

## Additional Information

#### Preparing for Your Treatment

You are encouraged to arrive 60 minutes prior to your appointment in order to change, relax and enjoy the facilities at The Spa. Please be advised that late arrivals will result in a reduction of treatment time. We recommend that hotel guests arrive at The Spa wearing the robe and slippers provided in the room, and would advise that valuables are left in the safe in the room.

#### Spa Etiquette

The Peninsula Spa is a place of relaxation and tranquility. Please respect all Spa guests’ right to privacy, serenity and comfort. The Spa is a smoke-free zone, and the use of mobile phones, personal digital assistants and other personal electronic devices is not permitted. The Spa provides towels, slippers, shower caps and all amenities required for use during your visit. The minimum age is 16 for access to and treatments at The Peninsula Spa.

#### Cancellation Policy

Kindly note that a 100% cancellation charge will be incurred for any spa treatment cancelled within 24 hours prior to the appointment.

#### Gift Certificates

Gift certificates are available in denominations of HK$ 1,000 or HK$ 2,000. Gift certificates for specific treatments are also available to purchase.

#### Home Care

To continue your Spa regime and experience at home, all products used in our treatments are available for purchase from The Spa Reception.

#### Prices

All prices are quoted in Hong Kong dollars and are subject to a 10% service charge. Prices are subject to change at any time.
