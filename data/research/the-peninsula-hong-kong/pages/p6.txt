THE PENINSULA HONG KONG Fitness Centre item 2

State-of-the-art cardio and strength-training equipment, knowledgeable trainers, and sweeping Victoria Harbour views make our Fitness Centre an inspiring place to pursue fitness goals.

Our spectacular, recently renovated gym offers the latest ultra-modern equipment from Life Fitness, including an extensive range of resistance-training machines – as well as treadmills, elliptical trainers, and stationary bikes, all equipped with personal television screens, iPod docking stations, headphone jacks, and VIVO digital workout-tracking technology. Personal trainers are on hand to offer one-on-one coaching sessions and assist guests with customized fitness programmes.

Post-workout, guests can unwind in five-star locker room facilities that include separate hot whirlpools, cold plunge pools, saunas, and steam rooms for men and women.

Memberships to the Fitness Centre are available for local residents and guests who visit regularly. For membership details, please click here
