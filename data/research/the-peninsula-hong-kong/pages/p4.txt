# Spring Moon

Since opening in 1986, Spring Moon has presented a Michelin-starred expression of Cantonese cooking in Hong Kong, set against an Art Deco backdrop of stained glass and rich detail.

Chef Lam Yuk Ming draws on tradition with precision and depth. Dishes such as braised bird’s nest soup and the legendary wok-fried Kagoshima beef are complemented by artisanal teas and the signature XO Chilli Sauce.

MICHELIN One Star for ten consecutive years (2017–2026) and Black Pearl One Diamond for seven consecutive years (2020–2026).

#### The Finer Details

The extensive collection of porcelain on display throughout the restaurant consists of reproductions of Chinese items (Ming and Qing Dynasties) and 18th Century French Royal Family and Portuguese armorial pieces, together with Chinese export porcelain from the 18th, 19th and 20th Centuries.

#### The Chef’s Table at Spring Moon

Treat your tastebuds to a behind-the-scenes look at the orchestra of sights, sounds, smells and tastes in the Spring Moon kitchens. Guests can indulge in a specially designed menu with unique dishes served with seasonal ingredients, curated in traditional Cantonese cooking style.

#### A Spicy Secret

Spring Moon began producing its signature XO sauce in 1986 when the restaurant first opened its doors. It comprises chillies, dried scallops, dried shrimps, and spices. Spring Moon’s popular and highly sought-after XO sauce is set-apart by its unique preparation methods and the proportion of ingredients used. It is freshly made by hand using a closely guarded secret recipe.

#### Artisanal Craftsmanship

Newly unveiled this year are two exceptional zesty condiments – Sichuan Chilli Sauce and Chiu Chow Chilli Sauce. Faithful to an authentic Sichuanese recipe, the Sichuan Chilli Sauce incorporates Da Hong Pao Sichuan pepper, nutmeg, and Japanese Sakura shrimp. Meanwhile, the Chiu Chow Chilli Sauce comprises a deeply umami blend of chilli peppers, spices and salted fish. Both sauces are made by hand in-house at Spring Moon.

#### Dedicated To Tea

With a dedicated tea counter featuring over 25 selected Chinese teas, Spring Moon's versatile tea collection adds to its classic Cantonese culinary experience with expertly curated tea pairing by the restaurant’s professional tea masters. Additionally, the tea counter features The Peninsula's collection of over 200 miniature Chinese clay teapots, including designs inspired by oriental legends and elements of nature.
