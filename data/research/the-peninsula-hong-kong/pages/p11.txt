The Peninsula Hong Kong Superlative Luxury Transport at The Peninsula Hong Kong item 2

For more than half a century, The Peninsula Hong Kong has committed to providing incomparably luxurious transport options for its guests. The hotel’s fleet of exquisite custom vehicles ensure that, even before setting foot on the property, new arrivals can experience the impeccable elegance and opulent comfort that defines The Peninsula. Once settled in, guests can also rely on the fleet – which includes Rolls-Royce Extended Wheelbase (EWB) Phantoms and four newly added Bentley Bentayga Extended Wheelbase (EWB) Azure SUVs – to explore their destination in unparalleled style.

In addition to its automotive fleet, The Peninsula Hong Kong also offers guests the option of helicopter transport and flightseeing via the hotel's dedicated twin helipads. These Heliservices offer unsurpassed convenience, as well as breath-taking views over Hong Kong island and its spectacular surrounding landscapes.

**Rolls-Royce Phantoms**

The Peninsula Hong Kong has redefined luxury travel with the launch of its new bespoke Rolls-Royce Phantom Extended vehicles in April 2025. These masterpieces are dressed in the iconic Peninsula Green, featuring illuminated grilles, redesigned daytime running lights, and bold wheel designs. Inside, a state-of-the-art LED dashboard, seamless Bluetooth integration, and multi-function rear seats with massage and elevating footrests ensure sublime comfort. Every detail—from personalised treadplates to dark spice accents—embodies timeless sophistication, delivering unparalleled journeys for the world’s most discerning travellers.

**Bentayga Azures**

The Peninsula Hong Kong’s four Bentley Bentayga Extended Wheelbase (EWB) Azures are super SUVs whose striking design and expansive interiors offer unparalleled comfort to passengers. The vehicles feature custom bodywork in signature Peninsula green, with hushed self-closing extended rear doors, and opulent inner compartments with custom-quilted seat backs, veneered picnic tables, individual entertainment screens, a rear control panel, signature audio systems, and Bentley mood lighting. Bespoke extras include a rear-seat massage function, dual wireless chargers in the front and rear seats, and spacious leather-clad luggage compartments.

**Heliservices**

The Peninsula Hong Kong has a rich history in civil aviation, and in 1994, twin helipads were introduced following a significant renovation and tower expansion. The helipads offer guests unparalleled landscape and access to the city and exemplify the luxurious travel experience that defines The Peninsula. The inauguration ceremony of the helipads also marked the opening of the China Clipper Lounge, which pays tribute to the pioneering spirit and adventure of the "flying boat" era. Today, the helipads and Heliservices are integral to The Peninsula's legacy and commitment to excellence in luxury travel.
