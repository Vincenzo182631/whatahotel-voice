## Spring Moon

The Michelin-starred Spring Moon Cantonese restaurant has drawn fans of classic, refined Cantonese cuisine for decades. Chef Lam Yuk Ming’s magnificent array of authentic preparations is served in a timelessly elegant dining room graced by Art Deco stained-glass windows.
