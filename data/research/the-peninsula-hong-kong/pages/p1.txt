# The Peninsula Hong Kong

Building on over 98 years as a global beacon of hospitality, the legendary “Grande Dame of the Far East” continues to set hotel standards worldwide, offering an exceptional blend of the best Eastern and Western hospitality in an atmosphere of unmatched classical grandeur and timeless elegance. The Peninsula Hong Kong features a world-class fleet of bespoke Rolls-Royce Phantom Extended and Bentley Bentaygas, and remains the only hotel in Hong Kong with twin helipads, providing helicopter services that land directly at the property, ensuring unparalleled luxury from the moment of arrival. The hotel’s distinctive façade, inspired by 1920s European architecture and a spacious lobby, contributes to a refined atmosphere that extends throughout its accommodations, dining venues, and wellness facilities. Consistently recognised by internationally renowned publications and travel guides, The Peninsula Hong Kong has earned countless awards and accolades, cementing the hotel’s status as one of the world’s finest hotels and a symbol of enduring luxury and innovation.
