# Welcome to The US Grant, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Diego

## Explore top attractions from our San Diego Hotel

For more than a century, The US Grant has set the standard for sophisticated hospitality in Southern California. Located in the heart of the city, this distinguished hotel in downtown San Diego offers...See More convenient access to world-class dining, entertainment, cultural landmarks, and the scenic waterfront. Guests can explore nearby attractions including Balboa Park, the San Diego Zoo, Petco Park, and the vibrant Gaslamp Quarter before returning to elegant accommodations designed with contemporary comforts and historic charm. Recognized as a Gaslamp Quarter historic hotel, The US Grant preserves its rich architectural legacy while delivering thoughtful service and modern amenities. Enjoy exceptional dining at Grant Grill, handcrafted cocktails, and versatile event spaces suited for memorable gatherings. Whether traveling for business or leisure, discover the timeless elegance of a luxury hotel in downtown San Diego where heritage, style, and convenience come together.See Less

Honoring Tradition

## The Story of Our Lobby Turndown

The term “concierge” was first used in France around 1697 meaning doorkeeper and landlord’s representative. In medieval times, the concierge managed hundreds of workers within the castle to ensure that guests of the nobility were taken care of during their stay. In a time where candles and flame torches were the only source of light in the vast castles, the concierge became the “keeper of candles.” Our concierge team continues this historic tradition at our legendary property in our grand lobby every evening at dusk to celebrate the lighting of the candles.

## The Historic Grant Grill

Since 1951, Grant Grill and Lounge has been at the forefront of San Diego’s culinary scene. No matter what brings you to Grant Grill, a downtown San Diego restaurant, we guarantee a remarkable dining experience. From the sumptuous surroundings – including classic mahogany wood, fleur de lis banquettes, and white linen-topped tables – to the ever-evolving seasonal menus, Grant Grill carries a tradition of excellence since 1951. Grant Grill’s menu sets the standard for San Diego fine dining with approachable epicurean elegance, acutely focused on highlighting only the freshest of ingredients, prime meats and Pacific seafood. Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner menus elevate comfort cuisine and highlight the bounty of the Southern California region. Compliment your meal with Grant Grill’s extensive wine list and craft cocktail collection.

### A Historic Icon of San Diego

The US Grant opened its doors with great pomp and circumstance on October 15, 1910. After five years of construction in the heart of downtown San Diego, anticipation had reached a fever pitch, fueled by extensive press coverage and a remarkable investment of $1.9 million. Thousands of guests traveled from across the country to celebrate the debut of a hotel that would set a standard for luxury hospitality....See More This marked the beginning of an extraordinary journey spanning more than a century. From the transformation of downtown San Diego and the vibrant Jazz Age of the 1920s to the legendary Grant Grill and the hotel's rescue from demolition, The US Grant has remained a defining landmark. Over the decades, it has welcomed presidents, dignitaries, celebrities, and travelers from around the world. A symbol of resilience, progress, and timeless elegance, The US Grant continues to stand as one of San Diego's most iconic luxury hotels, honoring its heritage and inspiring generations to come.See Less

Yes, pets are welcome at The US Grant, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Diego. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 35.0 lbs.

Off-site valet parking is available at The US Grant, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Diego. The daily valet fee is $70.00 per day.
