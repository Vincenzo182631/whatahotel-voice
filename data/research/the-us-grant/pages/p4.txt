## A Day in the Life of a Grant Grill Chef

A Day in the Life of a Grant Grill Chef creates moments to savor through a memorable epicurean experience. Guided by one of our chefs, guests will enjoy a behind the scenes tour of culinary operations, exclusive tastings, cooking and more. Guests will have the opportunity to enjoy exclusive tastings from a scratch kitchen that changes daily while having an opportunity to participate in cooking and preparing a dish designed around the offering of the day. The tour ends with an introduction to Grant Grill’s exciting beverage program and a surprise wine or cocktail tasting before wrapping up with recipe to recreate their dish at home.

San Diego's Finest Attractions

### Balboa Park

Explore nearby Balboa Park that includes green spaces, several museums, theaters and the San Diego Zoo. At 1,200 acres, this area is often referred to as the cultural heart of San Diego.

### A Day on the Water

Welcome to San Diego and there’s no better way to enjoy the area than spending time on the water. Choose from sailing, kayaking, surfing, paddle boarding and more to create the perfect Southern California day.

### A Top Family Destination

Make memories that will last a lifetime. With 70 miles of beautiful beaches, world-class attractions like the San Diego Zoo, Safari Park, SeaWorld® San Diego, LEGOLAND, kid-friendly museums and hiking trails, you will see why families love staying with us in downtown San Diego.

### Golf

Golf enthusiasts will love the number of local golf courses, from the challenging 72 par course at Balboa Park, where Sam Snead holds the record, to the dramatic elevation changes at Steele Canyon Golf Club. Or test your skill on 54 holes of Ted Robinson-designed golf on 425 acres at Singing Hills Golf Resort.

### Naval History

The Navy has been a big part of the San Diego region since the Naval Base was established back in 1922 and then expanded during World War II. Take the day and explore the USS Midway or Star of India at the Maritime Museum to learn more about this fascinating part of our culture.

### The History & Art of The US Grant

The US Grant opened her doors in 1910 after five years of construction, totaling $1.9 million. Thousands of guests took part in the opening ceremonies of this hotel of untold luxury. Over 100 years later, it is now one of the favorite luxury hotels in San Diego featuring a $6.5 million collection of distinctive artworks, including stunning sculptures, ironwork, murals and paintings. Created by carefully selected, world-renowned artists from European, American and Native American backgrounds, the artwork heralds the storied past and modern renaissance of this cultural landmark.

Does The US Grant, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Diego have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The US Grant, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Diego offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The US Grant, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Diego?
