# Wellness Overview Ocean Air And Canyon Light

Nestled where canyon meets the sea, wellness at The Ranch Laguna Beach is rooted in the restorative rhythm of nature. Here, mornings begin with ocean air and canyon light, while days unfold through movement, mindfulness, and moments of quiet connection. From rejuvenating spa treatments and guided fitness experiences to scenic hikes, golf, and nourishing farm-to-table dining, each offering is thoughtfully designed to inspire balance from within. Surrounded by 87 acres of protected coastal beauty, The Ranch invites guests to slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect with what feels good.

## House of Hudson

House of Hudson Spa offers a deeply restorative escape inspired by the rhythms of the coast and canyon. Thoughtfully designed to nurture mind, body, and spirit, the spa invites guests to unwind through personalized treatments, holistic therapies, curated spa retail items available for purchase and calming spaces that encourage stillness and renewal. From soothing massages and revitalizing facials to moments of quiet reflection surrounded by nature, every experience is rooted in the laid-back luxury and natural serenity that define The Ranch Laguna Beach.

## Fitness

Guests are invited to move at their own pace with thoughtfully curated wellness experiences including an indoor fitness center includes cardio, movement, free weights and resistance equipment center, outdoor movement classes, yoga, guided hikes, and scenic coastal activities that energize both body and mind. From sunrise stretches beneath canyon skies to invigorating workouts steps from the Pacific, every experience is designed to inspire balance, vitality, and connection to the landscape that makes The Ranch a true hidden gem.
