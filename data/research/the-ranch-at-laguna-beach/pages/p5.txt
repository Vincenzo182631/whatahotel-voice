# Harvest Farm

Harvest Farm is the vibrant heart of The Ranch at Laguna Beach, unfolding in 16 neat, 50‑foot rows of vegetables, herbs, flowers, lettuces and root vegetables just beyond the golf course and canyon views. Thoughtfully tended in an organic, hands‑on way, the farm supplies our culinary team with just‑picked ingredients that shape daily menus, seasonal specials and inventive cocktails at Harvest restaurant and bar.

Harvest Farm is one of Southern California’s largest working farms integrated directly into a hotel experience, giving guests rare, behind‑the‑scenes access to where their food begins. Out here, you’ll find happy chickens scratching in the soil, compost piles transforming kitchen trimmings into rich earth, and chefs walking the paths to gather what will be on the plate that evening. Guests are invited to explore with our farm team on guided walks, discovering how this small but mighty patch of land nourishes the resort, supports the local ecosystem and brings an authentic farm‑to‑table story to life in the canyon.
