# Stay at The Ranch

Hand-Crafted California Luxury

Nestled across the park-like grounds, within the incomparable setting of Mother Nature's own magnum opus, our spacious Canyon Rooms, Creekside Studio Suites, Creekside One Bedroom Suites, Cottage Two Bedroom Suites and The Treehouse enjoy a seamless dance between casual elegance and the breathtaking 360-degree vistas. Our pristine 87-acre oasis makes The Ranch at Laguna Beach one of Southern California's top resorts and spas. This AAA Four Diamond-rated and Forbes recommended romantic getaway, nestled just a short walk from one of Southern California’s finest beaches, brings the best of coast and canyon rooms together in one uniquely Laguna experience.

## Resort Highlights

Located in the Aliso Canyon \| Access to Aliso Beach via walking trail or shuttle \| Outdoor Saline Pool & Hot Tub \| Beach Cafe \| Three Meal Signature Restaurant \| Spa & Fitness Center \| In-Room Dining \| 9-Hole Golf Course \| Check-in 4:00pm - Check-out: 12:00pm

## Canyon Room

410 Sq Ft

Our Canyon Rooms feature 410 square feet of total bliss. Nestled in nature, each private oasis offers a king bed, expansive views and plenty of indoor/outdoor space to enjoy. The Ranch Laguna Beach offers two standard rooms, each with a King bed, which are connected with a door in between, giving our guests more space (each room is 410 sq. ft.) and two bathrooms.
