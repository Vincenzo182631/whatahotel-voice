# Welcome Gifts

Know someone staying in Laguna Beach for the weekend? Be sure to send them a hotel welcome gift full of our local favorites to enjoy during their stay. These ready-to-eat gifts are a great way for visitors to immerse in the culture of Laguna and are perfect for a quick weekend stay no matter the reason for their visit! Please allow 24 hours to process your order.

To protect the quality and freshness of every item — and out of respect for our canyon setting — The Ranch does not pre-deliver food or beverage amenities to guest rooms before arrival. All amenities are delivered within the first hour of a guest checking in.

If you would like a specific delivery time after 4:00 p.m., please contact the Front Desk at 949.715.1373%20715-1373) or Frontdesk@ranchlb.com, and we will gladly arrange it for you. Please note that we cannot guarantee delivery times prior to our 4:00 p.m. check-in time.

_\+ Please note, listed price includes a 22% service fee and 7.75% sales tax._
