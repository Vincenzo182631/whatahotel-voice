# GolfA Local Legend

Our 9‑hole, GEO Certified® golf course at The Ranch at Laguna Beach is set in one of the most serene, naturally beautiful corners of Orange County. Nestled into the dramatic, lush walls of Aliso Canyon, it offers an ultra‑private round with stunning views and engaging elevation changes on every fairway.

Speedy greens and a commitment to proper tee box and hole placement keep the game fast, fun, and inviting. An off-the-beaten-path location means a quiet play experience that offers a rare chance to connect fully with nature. This is not your stuffy country club experience—you're in Southern California now. Our laid-back resort experience even includes three-clubs barefoot tournaments for novices, families, and avid golfers alike.

By proceeding, you agree to our Golf Terms and Conditions.

## The Barefoot Club

Local golfers, meet The Barefoot Club - our ongoing exclusive recognition program designed for our most local and loyal players. As a member, your golf experience comes with elevated perks, including discounted play rates, 14-day advance booking windows and 10% off non-golf retail items. The Barefoot Club is open to residents of Laguna Beach and Dana Point, as well as returning guests who have played 24 or more rounds in the past calendar year, with an annual-fee option available for those who do not meet the local or loyalty criteria.
