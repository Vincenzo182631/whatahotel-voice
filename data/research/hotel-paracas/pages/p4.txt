## Experiences

Golden dunes in an arid wonderland. Tranquil waters. Massive mysterious geoglyphs. That is what awaits you in the area around Pisco some 250 km south of Lima: an oasis of contrasts and contradicting landscapes...See More and a refuge of myths and legends. Paracas is the name of the bay, a Pre-Incan culture, a natural reserve, and a strong wind that occasionally produces sand storms. It is also the name of our seaside resort that was constructed as an oasis in the desert and is the perfect place to enjoy the marine life, culture, history, and adventure.See Less

## Activities Center

Within the grounds of our Paracas resort: Kayak, catamaran rides, paddle surfing, sand bikes, among others. Includes free activities for guests.

### Main Pool

Furnished with cushioned deck chairs and poolside cabins available for rental, the Main Pool area is the ideal place to relax. Located next to the Ballestas Restaurant, food and beverage service and pool butlers lend extra allure to this sunny spot.

### Koni Family Pool

Ideal for families with kids, the family Pool, is situated on the south side of the property near both the children’s playground and beach. An abundance of umbrellas ensures shade on warmer days. Kids of all ages enjoy splashing around against spectacular ocean views. Floaties, ball and other water toys keep kids having fun all day long.

## Watersports Desk

Our hotel with a pool in Paracas offers a range of non-motorized nautical activities. Lessons and boat use are available for a fee. Catamaran and kayak rental are complimentary. Reservation can be made in advance at the activities desk.

More Information

Reservations can be made in advance at the activities desk. Nautical sport availability is based on weather conditions and is restricted for children 5 and younger.

Watersport options include:

16 ft catamaran for a trip around the bay accompanied by a captain.

14 ft catamaran for a trip around the bay by yourself.

Kayaks (doubles and singles).

Paddleboards.

Additional rentals include:

Bicycles & Fat Bikes

## Koni Camp

Kids take advantage of Paracas’ stunning natural beauty and the resort’s myriad offerings through our Koni Camp, with organized outdoor and indoor adventures and activities: Treasure hunts, shell collection on the beach, and movie nights.

More Information

At our Koni Camp, the children's fun begins at 10:00 am with a brief tour of the hotel, after that they can meet and enjoy an array of activities either inside the club itself around the hotel grounds, or at the playground.

Activities and amenities include:

- Art and crafts
- Pool & Beach activities
- Social learning activities
- Pirate ship playground
- Treasure hunts
- Original Movies on Streaming

Children up to 12 years old are invited to have all meals provided at the Ballestas Restaurant or the Trattoria for $35 per child, per day. All activities are complimentary. Please note that children under 6 must be under the supervision of one adult at all times.

## Ballestas Islands

In Paracas Bay there are three important groups of islands where you can spot marine fauna in its fullest: Ballestas, San Gallan and Chincha.
Discover the Ballestas Islands, several small islands are home to peculiar and diverse coastal fauna: sea lions, spotting Humboldt penguins and a wide variety of guano birds. On the way to the islands you will be able to appreciate the magnificence of the “Candelabro” (the Candlestick), an enormous and mysterious geoglyph carved on the desert soil, making this a morning of archeological and natural history discovery.
