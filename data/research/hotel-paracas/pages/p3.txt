## Dining

## Be Epicurean

To "Be Epicurean" is to be a connoisseur of the arts, the refinements and the pleasures of life. The art of providing the highest quality of food and beverage is personified at The Luxury Collection. We invite you to take a culinary journey in one of the world’s most coveted food destinations, curated by celebrated chefs. For more information and reservations contact our Luxury Collection Concierge:
conciergeparacas@hotelparacas.com.pe.

Epicurean Experiences

Epicurean Moments

##### Daily

Indigenous, standalone culinary moments, that occur on property daily.

##### Weekly

These explorations occur once a week. and fall into the following categories: Mixology class / Cooking class / Tours / Tasting / Indigenous offering.

##### Epicurean Journey

Rare, one-of-a-kind indigenous culinary experiences that must be booked in advance.

These experiences go above and beyond other epicurean offerings and include guidance from operations and the executive chef.

Tasting Ocean to Table

##### Flavors of the Sea

Discover the exquisite taste of the best local seafood on a culinary journey that begins at sea and ends at your table. Through the best techniques of fusion cuisine and our sophisticated Josper grill cooking system, you can enjoy an elegant lunch in the gardens of the hotel in the best style of The Luxury Collection. This experience includes: 4 starters, 4 main courses, 3 side dishes and 4 desserts.

**Bar Lounge Garden**

From 13:00 hrs. to 16:00 hrs.

Minimum booking of 20 and a maximum of 100 persons.

Fan Shells Harvest

##### Treasures of the Sea

A unique experience. Be taken to the National Reserve of Paracas in our private vessel "Rayador" and be part of an authentic harvest of shells in the sea; followed by an exquisite and elegant tasting by of our best chefs, who will prepare tempting dishes with the six varieties of the product.

**Shell Farm (National Reserve of Paracas) - Chalana**

From 8:00 hrs. to 13:00 hrs.( 2-hour duration)

Includes 1 glass of sparkling wine, 1 glass of white wine and 1 bottle of mineral water. Minimum booking of 1 and a maximum of 8 persons.

\*Rate based on two persons.

Cooking Masterclass

##### Secrets of the Sea

Get to know all the secrets of our signature dish. Our executive chef will accompany you in preparing authentic Peruvian ceviche in a sustainable experience and using fresh products from local fishing and agriculture. We await you at our restaurant "Chalana", located in the private dock of the hotel.

**Chalana Restaurant**

From 10:00 hrs. to 12:00 hrs.

Includes ceviche class, 1 dish for each of the participants and1 glass of white wine. Minimum booking of 1 and a maximum of 32 persons.
