## Experience our Resort Hotel in Goa

Redefining luxury at the heart of Goa, Grand Hyatt Goa overlooks the visually stunning waters of the Bambolim Bay. This hotel in Goa spreads across 28 acres of colourful, tropical gardens and lush lawns that roll down to the water’s edge.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Grand Hyatt Goa is our award-winning luxury destination resort overlooking the calm waters of Bambolim Bay. Unwind in our expertly appointed rooms and suites and choose from a collection of more than 101 experiences offered at our resort.

### The Dining Room

This all-day-dining restaurant features five live kitchens, that embrace the idea of eating out of the pan, off the grill and out of the wok. Guests can enjoy a myriad of choices that include a taste of Asian, Indian, Italian and Middle Eastern cuisine, all prepared à la minute.
Guests can reserve a private dining area, a perfect gathering place for celebrating special occasions such as baby showers, birthdays, and holiday parties.
