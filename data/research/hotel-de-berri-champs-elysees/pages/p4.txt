## Parc Monceau

Located just a 10-minute walk from the hotel, le Parc Monceau was the whimsical fancy of the king’s cousin, The Duke of Chartres. In 1769, he asked Carmontelle to create a unique garden, mixing styles in curious juxtapositions, such as the lake surrounded by Roman-inspired columns or the Egyptian pyramid, both of which can still be seen today.

## Jacquemart-André museum

Our hotel also offers close proximity to some of Paris' lesser-known museum, such as Jacquemart-André museum built in 1876 owned by Edouard André and his wife Nélie Jacquemart, two eminent art collectors of the time, contemporaries of Princess Mathilde.

Yes. Hôtel de Berri Champs-Élysées, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Paris offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Free Weights, Rowing machine, treadmill, exercise bike and free weights, and Strength Equipment, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
