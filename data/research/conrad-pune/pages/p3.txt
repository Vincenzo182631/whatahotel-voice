## International Cuisine and Alfresco Dining

Coriander Kitchen at Conrad Pune offers a vibrant all-day dining experience and is celebrated as one of Pune’s most expansive brunch destinations. This contemporary multicuisine restaurant showcases flavours from around the world — including Indian, Asian, Mediterranean and international favourites — brought to life through interactive live kitchens.

Set within elegant interiors and complemented by alfresco seating overlooking a serene waterscape, Coriander Kitchen provides a refined yet welcoming setting for brunches, business lunches and social gatherings. Every dish reflects Conrad Pune’s signature elevated hospitality, refined presentation and exceptional quality.

Complete your experience at the Signature Dessert Library or enjoy handcrafted flavours from the iconic Tonda Ice Cream Machine. Perfect for celebrations and memorable moments, Coriander Kitchen invites you to savour global cuisine in an atmosphere of elegance and indulgence.

## Sunday Brunch

Indulge in lavish brunch at the Coriander Kitchen on weekends, and rejoice in a wonderful ambience,  vegetarian and non-vegetarian live stations, an ice-cream wheel, and an elaborate spread of palatable delicacies.

## Alfresco Dining

Revel in a gorgeous outdoor seating with an intriguing waterscape view at Coriander Kitchen.

## Operational Hours

**Breakfast**

Weekdays: 6:30AM to 10:30AM (Sat & Sun)

Weekends & Public Holidays: 6:30AM to 11:00AM

**Lunch**

Weekdays: 12:30PM to 3:30PM (Mon – Fri)

Weekends: 12:30PM to 4:00PM (Sat & Sun)

**Dinner**

All Days: 6:30PM to 11:30PM

Lunch buffet is available on all days.

**(Notes – Coriander Kitchen will be non-operational between 3.30 pm to 6.30 pm)**
