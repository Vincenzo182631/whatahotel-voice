## Spa Highlights

## Swimming Pool

A leisurely swim in Pune’s first outdoor temperature-controlled swimming pool, with an in-built whirlpool, awaits you at Conrad Pune. Laze away in the lap of luxury in the outdoor pool, and inhale the essence of nature coupled with a mesmerising view. The pool stretches a majestic 8 metres approximately 20 metres in length and has a depth of 4 feet. The pool extends to the Kabana, the western lounge. Conrad Pune also houses a kid’s pool. Splash away all your worries and revel in a cool pool day with your friends and family at Conrad Pune!
