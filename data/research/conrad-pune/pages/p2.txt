## Experiential Dining

Perched high above the city at Conrad Pune, Aldila is a sophisticated rooftop destination offering panoramic skyline views and an elevated experience of indulgence. Known for its refined ambience, curated cocktails, and globally inspired small plates, Aldila blends urban luxury with a breezy, relaxed charm. Whether it’s a golden hour sundowner or a starlit evening with curated music and conversations, Aldila sets the scene for unforgettable moments high above it all.

## Rooftop Lounge Bar and Rooftop Restaurant

Experience an unforgettable dining experience at Al Di La, one of the top Italian restaurants in Pune, located on the rooftop of a building. The restaurant is also a perfect rooftop cafe in Pune that offers a stunning city view. Al Di La is known for its sumptuous Italian delicacies, including pasta, tiramisu, and more. If you’re looking for an open terrace restaurant in Pune, Al Di La is the perfect choice for you. With its plush and majestic design, the restaurant is curated to meet futuristic aesthetics and is adorned with chandeliers from New York Hardware Store. The rooftop restaurant has an intimate and romantic overtone that enhances the grandeur of the place.

The rooftop bar and lounge area at Al Di La is the perfect place to unwind on weekends, with live DJ music, sundowners, and extravagant celebrations. This rooftop restaurant in Pune houses a selfie corner where you can capture priceless moments with friends and companions.

Explore the finest range of smoked cocktails, house-infused beers, a gin bar, exemplary whiskies and cutting-edge mixology from the finest in-house mixologists at the rooftop restaurant in the city. Make sure to visit Al Di La, the most beautiful rooftop restaurant in Pune that offers a euphony of Italian delicacies and a sensational city view of Pune.
