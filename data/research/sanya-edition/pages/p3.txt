# Xianhai by the Sea

**Xianhai by the Sea**

The custom-designed spaces feature teak interiors and rich upholstery beneath a shaded canopy. From afar, the restaurant resembles a collection of glowing lanterns skimming across the water. Guests can enjoy upscale à la carte dining indoors, alfresco, or in a private dining room.

RESERVATIONS

+86 (0) 898 8858 2219

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY
- Lunch 12:00 PM - 2:00 PM
- Dinner 5:30 PM - 10:00 PM

**BOHN**

Best of Hainan (BOHN) draws inspiration from Hainan Island’s abundant natural resources, offering diners traditional Hainanese fermented vinegar soup and a selection of classic dishes.

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM

**Beach Club at EDITION**

Perfectly perched between the resort’s private ocean and Beach Club pool, our Beach Club is the epitome of sophisticated summer libations and culinary experiences. The minimal yet elevated space plays host to exclusive events, bringing a new style of entertainment to what has become the ultimate summer destination. Soak up the sun, culture and stunning views of the Private Ocean from VIP cabanas flanking the infinity pool or from reserved beach beds at the water’s edge.

Beach Club at EDITION is a game-changer, uniting great design and innovation with personalized, enchanting, modern service as well as outstanding, one-of-a-kind dining and entertainment offerings.

#### Hours     Dropdown

- Daily 12:00 PM - 9:00 PM (Adults Only)

**Sky Bar at EDITION**

Located at the highest point of the hotel, Sky Bar features a sophisticated adults-only pool and relaxation area. The space is a relaxing hideaway where guests can enjoy afternoon tea during the day. At night, it transitions into a vibrant venue offering cocktails, appetizers, and DJ performances throughout the week.

RESERVATIONS

+86 (0) 898 8858 2227

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY
- Bar\|Adults Only 5:00 PM - 12:00 AM
- Afternoon Tea 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM

**The Jade Egret**

At ocean level, The Jade Egret offers modern European cuisine, pre-dinner cocktails, and small plates in a yacht-inspired space.

RESERVATIONS

+86 (0) 898 8858 2213

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY
- Dinner 5:30 PM - 10:00 PM

**Private Table at The Floating Deck**

Take an idyllic evening boat ride aboard a customized boat to the center of our Private Ocean. Guests can dine at a private table on an illuminated floating platform and enjoy a customized menu prepared for their special occasion in an intimate setting.

RESERVATIONS

+86 (0) 898 8835 9999

#### Hours     Dropdown

- By Reservation Only

**Beach Barbacoa**

Inspired by Bali’s renowned Jimbaran Bay, the Beach Barbacoa offers an enticing array of delicacies. The outdoor dining area, nestled amidst a lush landscape and shaded by majestic rain trees, invites guests to step barefoot onto soft sand and savor the ambiance. Colorful lights twinkle from the treetops in the distance, enhancing the setting with a magical glow. Live band performances and outdoor bonfires add to the enchanting atmosphere, creating a serene backdrop where time seems to stand still.

RESERVATIONS

+86 (0) 898 8858 2290

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY
- Dinner 5:30 PM - 10:00 PM

**Market at EDITION**

An all-day dining destination, Market at EDITION references Sanya’s history as a trading port at the southernmost tip of China along the ancient Maritime Silk Road. Its timeless design evokes an old Chinese warehouse, where guests gather for breakfast and enjoy the vibrant atmosphere of a traditional marketplace.

Market at EDITION features a series of food stations offering a fine selection of local and international cuisine, including a raw bar, an Asian noodle and dumpling station, a bakery, a hot kitchen with an open-fire grill, a wok station, a dim sum station, and a juice bar.

RESERVATIONS

+86 (0) 898 8858 2215

#### Hours     Dropdown

- OPEN DAILY
- Breakfast 7:00 AM - 10:30 AM
- Hot Pot Seafood Buffet 5:30 PM - 9:00 PM
- A La Carte Menu 11:00 AM - 10:00 PM
