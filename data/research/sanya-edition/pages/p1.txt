# A NEXT GENERATION URBAN RESORT

Inspired by the sea, The Sanya EDITION is an exceptional new destination on Hainan Island, just off the coast of southern China. Water, a source of vitality and serenity, defines the lush 50-acre EDITION property, which features the spectacular Private Ocean, a unique man-made ocean, along with two swimming pools.

All 501 guest rooms in the horseshoe-shaped hotel have ocean views. In addition, 12 villas are nestled into a terraced hillside with ocean views, private pools, and pavilion spaces.

Nearby attractions abound, such as luxury designer outlets. However, once guests cross the entrance into The Sanya EDITION, they become part of an experience that is otherworldly.

VIEW ROOMS & SUITES

## Home Away From Home

All 501 guest rooms in the horseshoe-shaped hotel have ocean views. The warm and inviting interiors exude a comfortable, simple sophistication, using light oak finishes and airy fabrics that evoke the beach. The design continues with limestone floors from Indonesia and a clean, light color palette. Bathtubs are positioned in the main room; surrounded by views, the space becomes even more luxurious and open.

VIEW DINING & ENTERTAINMENT

## A Dining Destination

Throughout the property, specialty restaurants celebrate culinary innovation and creative design. Market at EDITION references Sanya’s history as a trading port with a design evoking an old Chinese warehouse. The Jade Egret welcomes guests with an exquisite drink list and offers a selection of creative and fresh European cuisine with a hint of Asian flavors. Take an idyllic evening boat ride to Xianhai by the Sea, the hotel’s signature seafood restaurant, which specializes in Cantonese cuisine. Beach Barbacoa at EDITION captures the casual sophistication of beachside dining and the full-bodied flavors of Indonesian cuisine. The restaurant takes its culinary cues from the traditional market restaurants lining Sanya’s beachfront.

## Relaxation Vacation

Spanning over 2000 square meters on two levels with extensive lush gardens and a seamless connection to nature, The Spa at The Sanya EDITION reflects a sophisticated approach to beauty, wellness, and wellbeing equally attuned to modern living and local customs.
