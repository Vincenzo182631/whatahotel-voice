# Beach Club at EDITION

## Beach Club at EDITION

Perfectly perched between the resort’s private ocean and Beach Club pool, our Beach Club is the epitome of sophisticated summer libations and culinary experiences. The minimal yet elevated space plays host to exclusive events, bringing a new style of entertainment to what has become the ultimate summer destination. Soak up the sun, culture and stunning views of the Private Ocean from VIP cabanas flanking the infinity pool or from reserved beach beds at the water’s edge.

Beach Club at EDITION is a game-changer, uniting great design and innovation with personalized, enchanting, modern service as well as outstanding, one-of-a-kind dining and entertainment offerings.
