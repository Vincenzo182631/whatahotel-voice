# Rooms

All of the 501 guest rooms in the horseshoe-shaped hotel have ocean views. The warm and inviting interiors exude a comfortable, simple sophistication, using light oak finishes and airy fabrics that evoke the beach. Surrounded by views, bathtubs are positioned in the main room, making the space even more luxurious and open.
