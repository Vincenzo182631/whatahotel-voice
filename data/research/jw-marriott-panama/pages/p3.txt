# Enjoy Food and Drinks Offered at JW Marriott Panama

## Dining

Enjoy a new and unique culinary experience in Panama City, with extraordinary menus, in addition to relaxing in one of our magnificent rooms with ocean views.

### MASI

Fire + Ceviche + Terrace. MASI. A colorful and unique restaurant in Panama City, Panama, where flavors and textures are the main characters of this live ceviche bar experience, alongside a distinctive local touch.

### Azul Restaurant

Soak up the sunshine and stunning views of Panama City at Azul Restaurant. With a tropical cocktail in one hand and a healthy meal in the other, transcend to an exotic getaway at our poolside bar and American restaurant in Panama City.

### CAVA 15

Ambient lighting, leather armchairs and gold accented details radiate an inviting atmosphere for dinner at Cava 15. Sip on a cocktail and savor mouthwatering appetizers in our luxury cocktail bar in Panama City​.

### Executive Lounge

Offering soft drinks, coffee, and snacks. Appetizers served from 5:30pm to 8 pm. Breakfast will continue to be served in MASI.
