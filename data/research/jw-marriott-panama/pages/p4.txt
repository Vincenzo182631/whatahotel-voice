# Activities For Everyone

## Experiences

One of Central America’s most vibrant and diverse metropolises, Panama City is a mix of cosmopolitan style, Spanish Colonial charm and tropical beauty. Downtown Panama City is a bustling center of international commerce, shimmering skyscrapers, and urban chaos. Meanwhile, enchanting neighborhoods like Casco Antiguo, Costa del Este and Casco Viejo showcase the cobblestone streets, crumbled ruins and...See More historic architecture that trace our city’s lineage from the pre-Columbian era to the days of pirates and Spanish Conquistadors to the arrival of French and American prospectors, politicians, and engineers. From attractions such as the Panama Canal and the Panama Viejo Museum to beach trips and eco-adventures in lush rainforests, an array of unforgettable activities awaits at our luxury Panama City waterfront hotel.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Panama have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Panama offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-11:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Panama have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Panama has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Heated Pool (Outdoor)Outdoor Pool (Outdoor)Infinity Pool (Outdoor)

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Panama?

France Square, Museo de Panamá Viejo, Parque Omar, Paseo de Las Bóvedas, Plaza Simón Bolívar and more are nearby JW Marriott Panama.
