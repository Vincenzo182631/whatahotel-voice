Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman
Memories Made Along the Shore
As the year turns toward its close, The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman becomes a gathering place for warmth, wonder, and the quiet luxury of island time. Set along the powdered sands of Seven Mile Beach, the resort welcomes the season with a collection of experiences that honor both tradition and destination, from candlelit dinners beneath the stars to spa rituals drawn from the surrounding waters and gatherings that carry the ease and grace of Cayman itself.
Unforgettable Celebrations
Welcome the New Year along Seven Mile Beach at The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman. Move from elegant seated dinners to beachfront festivities, savoring exquisite cuisine, live music, and dancing before midnight fireworks illuminate the Caribbean sky over the water.

Festive Experiences
Traditional Thanksgiving Dinner
Gather with loved ones for a traditional Thanksgiving celebration at Cayman Islands Ballroom, featuring abountiful buffet complete with classical favorites including a savory roast turkey with expertly prepared trimmings and decadent desserts.
Reserve Now
Thanksgiving Dinner at Seven & In-Room Dining
Indulge in a four-course, prix fixe Thanksgiving menu prepared by our resident
culinarians. An ambiance of festive sophistication provides an ideal backdrop for
lasting memories this season.
Reserve Now
Pie Making
Discover the secrets of perfect pie dough with Executive Pastry Chef Camilla Testa in this engaging complimentary workshop at The Culinary Studio, and leave with a memorable, edible keepsake.
Reserve Now
Gingerbread House Workshop
Celebrate the holiday spirit at Rembro where creativity, joy, and a dash of imagination come together to create the perfect gingerbread house. This family-friendly activity transforms carefully placed sweet treats into a delicious, memorable keepsake.
Reserve Now
Gingerbread Man Tea
Savor holiday aromas, enjoy spirited music, and embrace festive cheer at Silver Palm Lounge, where Santa hosts a magical afternoon tea featuring scones, sandwiches, seasonal sweets, and fine teas.
Reserve Now
Christmas Feast To-go
Celebrate Christmas with a flawlessly prepared feast by our Ritz-Carlton chefs, available through In-Room Dining and served in the comfort of your residence or suite. Select from Beef Wellington, Roasted Turkey, Baked Ham, or a Vegetarian option, accompanied by indulgent sides and desserts.
Reserve Now
Christmas Day Brunch
Gather with friends and family in the Cayman Islands Ballroom for a grand holiday feast featuring traditional holiday creations, an impressive carving station, seafood bar, charcuterie station, and live-action culinary offerings for the perfect Christmas Day celebration.
Reserve Now
New Year's Eve Dinner at Seven
Mark the occasion with an elegant dinner at Seven, featuring Caribbean flavors, signature dishes, decadent desserts, and expertly crafted cocktails.
Reserve Now
New Year's Eve at Saint June
Bid farewell to 2026 as you watch the final sunset over Seven Mile Beach, with live music and South American flavors at Saint June.
Reserve Now
New Year's Eve Dinner at Blue by Eric Ripert
Ring in the New Year with a tasting menu at Blue by Eric Ripert, showcasing seasonal creations and exquisite flavors in the Caribbean’s only Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star setting.
Reserve Now
Ring in the New Year Beach Celebration
Dance into the night and the New Year enjoying the sounds of a live DJ on beautiful Seven Mile Beach. Watch the sky illuminated by the highly anticipated annual fireworks display at midnight.

Island Treasures and Conservation
Created by the legendary oceanographer and conservationist Jean-Michel Cousteau, Ambassadors of the Environment invites you to celebrate the holiday season through immersive nature experiences, including stargazing, night snorkeling, and other adventures inspired by Grand Cayman’s natural wonders.

Celebrate with Restorative Wellness
Revitalize body, mind, and spirit at the newly reimagined Ritz-Carlton Spa, Grand Cayman, where bespoke treatment, transformative therapies, and personalized guidance create a sanctuary of renewal.
Thermal Circuit and Vibroacoustic Meditation
Customized by our in-house wellness consultant to align with individual goals, this 90-minute ritual combines heat and cold therapy with sound-based meditation to calm the nervous system and promote deep rest.
Sunrise Floating Sound Bath
A morning ritual designed to align body and mind with the island’s natural rhythm. Guests float in a tranquil pool as the soothing tones of crystal bowls and chimes wash over them.
Sound Theraphy Integration, Myndstream
Myndstream soundscapes enhance treatments throughout the spa, designed to calm the nervous system and deepen relaxation. Wellness consultants extend the experience beyond the spa by curating personalized in-room soundscapes to support rest, recovery, and sleep.
111Skin Exosome Regeneration Facial
Featuring 111SKIN’s proprietary NAC Y2™ formula and exosome technology, this facial promotes cellular communication for a dramatic lifting and tightening effect.
