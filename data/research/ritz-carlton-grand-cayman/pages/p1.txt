Community Footprints
Community Footprints is The Ritz-Carlton's social and environmental volunteer program. In Grand Cayman, our Ladies and Gentlemen join monthly beach clean-ups with Plastic Free Cayman, lead mangrove clean-ups with guests, and prepare and deliver meals with Meals on Wheels on the last Monday of every month. In 2025, the resort hosted 17 mangrove clean-up events, removing more than 700 pounds of debris from local mangrove ecosystems while engaging both guests and Ladies and Gentlemen in conservation efforts.
Meals on Wheels
Meals on Wheels provides daily meals and wellness checks to seniors, individuals with disabilities, and those in need. The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman proudly supports this essential program by preparing, boxing, and delivering meals on the last Monday of every month. These efforts help enhance the well-being of the community’s most vulnerable members and reinforce the resort’s dedication to local outreach.
Humane Society
The Cayman Islands Humane Society rescues and rehomes abandoned and mistreated animals while promoting responsible pet ownership. The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman supports the shelter through volunteer dog walking, fundraising, donations, and advocacy for animal care and adoption.
Plastic Free Cayman
Plastic Free Cayman is a nonprofit working to reduce plastic pollution across the Cayman Islands. Our Ladies and Gentlemen join their monthly beach clean-ups and host additional resort-led clean-ups along Seven Mile Beach throughout the year.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 20.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman is Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM). GCM is located approximately 4.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman have an airport shuttle to Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman does not have an airport shuttle to Owen Roberts International Airport.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites.
