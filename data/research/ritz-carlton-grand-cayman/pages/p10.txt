Why book?
If you want quintessential Caribbean luxury on world-famous Seven Mile Beach, The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman delivers a premier escape with refined service, legendary dining, and an expansive resort that blends beach days with golf, watersports, and cultural experiences—all steps from blue water and soft sand.
Set the scene
Set right on Seven Mile Beach, the sprawling Ritz-Carlton property is a playground for global jetsetters, families with older kids, and even celebrated chefs during the hotel’s annual Cayman Cookout. Early-evening strollers in crisp linen and boat shoes, sundress-clad beachgoers, and golfers in polos make up the crowd you’ll find
The backstory
Opened in 2005 and expanded over the decades, The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman is one of the Caribbean’s most iconic luxury resorts. As part of the Ritz-Carlton brand, it blends classic resort grandeur with Caribbean flair. The property has repeatedly refreshed guest rooms, restaurants, and public spaces to stay contemporary while anchoring itself in Cayman’s culture and beach heritage.
The rooms
The resort's rooms embody midcentury British-Caribbean elegance, featuring open interiors, marble bathrooms, and private balconies with beach views. Guests celebrating special occasions or those just living the good life can choose the Ritz-Carlton Suite, which offers spacious living, ocean views, and can be further expanded. Club-level rooms provide access to a dedicated concierge lounge with complimentary food throughout the day.
Food and drink
The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman, is home to Blue by Eric Ripert, the Caribbean’s only Forbes Five-Star restaurant, as well as the only Caribbean restaurant with AAA Five Diamond level of recognition, where the famed chef has created a menu where local seafood and produce are the main focus. Saint June pairs oceanfront views with a coastal-inspired cuisine, while Taikun delivers an upscale take on sushi. The Silver Palm Bar has become a haven for rum lovers, with rare Caribbean pours and an evening “rum ritual” experience that celebrates island heritage.
The spa
The resort’s Forbes Travel Guide Triple Five-Star Spa is a standout, inspired by the Caribbean Sea and featuring ESPA and 111SKIN treatments, vibroacoustic wellness beds, steam rooms, and serene relaxation lounges. Signature rituals here blend marine botanicals and high-end product lines for a restorative, island-infused experience.
The neighborhood/area
If you can manage to get yourself away from Seven Mile Beach to explore the local neighborhood surrounding the resort, you'll find plenty of local restaurants and bars that cater to natives, expats, and travelers alike. The upscale vibe extends to chic beachfront cafés, refined cocktail lounges, boutique retailers, and galleries featuring local designers.
The service
True to the Ritz-Carlton name, the service here is polished and anticipatory—golf tee times, private boat charters, in-room dining requests, and restaurant reservations are handled with professionalism. Club Level guests enjoy a dedicated concierge team, elevating even mundane requests into bespoke experiences.
For families
The Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman, has plenty of ways to keep children entertained during their stay. Jean-Michel Cousteau’s Ambassadors of the Environment programs help introduce kids to marine ecology, while the resort's Starfish Cay waterpark keeps little ones entertained, and, of course, the resort offers reef snorkeling and boating options that will entertain all ages.
Eco effort
The property holds Green Key certification and runs initiatives such as rainwater collection for irrigation, reduction of single-use plastics, composting partnerships, and sustainable sourcing of food and beverages.
Accessibility
Public spaces, restaurants, fitness center, spa, pools, and guest rooms with accessibility features are available. Service animals are welcome, and routes throughout the resort are designed for ease of movement.
Anything left to mention?
The resort is host to one of the Caribbean’s most celebrated culinary events—The Cayman Cookout—which brings some of the most celebrated chefs right to the beach.
