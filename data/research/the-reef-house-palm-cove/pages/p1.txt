### Enjoy, it's all included

- **Escape Planning Specialist:** curate your personalised itinerary before you arrive, with our Luxury Concierge available throughout your stay.
- **Arrival Experience:** gin-infused welcome drink and refreshing cold towel on arrival.
- **Luxury Concierge:** porterage service and guided property tour.
- **Curated Arrival:** music, oil burners and a chilled bottle of Australian sparkling wine awaiting your arrival.
- **Sleep Easy Guide:** relaxation guide, sleep playlist, podcasts and wellbeing resources.
- **Pool Butler:** refreshing cold towels served daily at 11am.
- **Poolside Sorbet:** locally made tropical sorbet served daily at 3pm.
- **Brigadier's Lounge Honesty Bar**, a tradition originating from Brigadier Thomson's Officers Mess
- **Brigadier's Punch & Canapés:** served in the lounge each evening at twilight.
- **Our gift to you:** the Reef House History Book and water bottles to take home.
- **Rejuvenate:** in two pools, the tranquillity & heated mineral pool.

- **Every Escape Includes:** 28+ Signature Inclusions & Experiences, including Mixology and Wine Appreciation.
- **Botanical Drawing:** guided workshops inspired by the beauty of Tropical North Queensland.
- **Culture Connection:** expert talks on indigenous culture.
- **Handwritten Postcards:** send to loved ones via our complimentary Reef House mail service.
- **Vintage Photo Booth:** capture a timeless keepsake.
- **Wellness:** beachfront yoga, aqua aerobics, stretch & breath and personal training sessions\*.
- **Explore & Unwind:** with cruiser bikes, puzzles, chess sets, guest library, fishing rods and sketch pads.
- **Tranquil Palm Cove Beach:** 25 minutes, drive north from Cairns international airport.
- _\*Fees apply._
