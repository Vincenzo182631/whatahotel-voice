## The Reef House Sleep Easy benefits for all guests

We want our guests to relax, rejuvenate and maximise their enjoyment, and restful sleep is an important part of your Relaxation Escape. Our Sleep Easy Support Guide provides expert tips and tricks to help improve your sleep, benefiting you now and long after your stay with us.

Sleep Easy is a cohesive set of information aids designed to promote healthier and more fulfilling sleep. In addition to the Sleep Support Guide provided in-room on your arrival, our Sleep Easy suite includes a selection of blogs, videos and audio files that explain the importance of sleep, and outlines some routines and relaxation techniques. Sleep Easy joins the many signature inclusions offered at all The Reef House luxury boutique hotel.

## Sabine Christelli - Sleep & Rejuvenation

## Video introduction

We’re taking relaxation to a whole new level.

Sabine Christelli has a Graduate Research Certificate in sleep science and is currently doing her PhD in the same field. She has a Bachelor of Science with Honours in Immunology and Biochemistry, and as an internationally accredited yoga teacher has been teaching meditation, mindfulness, yoga, and ways to live healthier to people of all ages, from all walks of life for over 15 years.

Sabine loves to see people thrive and in helping thousands of people, plus being a sleep scientist, she has become an expert in the field of sleep, translating scientific discoveries and its jargon for the general public and making it practical, relevant, and applicable for anyone.

## Sleep Easy Group Sessions

## Sleep

This is an informative and practical class where you will learn how to harness more of the benefits of optimised sleep, and how you can start getting better sleep straight away.

## Meditation

Enjoy a guided meditation experience to support your nervous system to calm down and enter the rejuvenating flow state which enhances your entire wellbeing and deep restful sleep.

## Breathwork

Discover simple and scientifically validated techniques that can assist you in your ability to harmonise your nervous system so you can relax more, let go of stress and nourish a better night’s sleep.

## Journaling

This is a guided practise which is highly effective in enabling an inner sense of calm and stress-reduction, ideal for deeper sleep.

## Private Sessions

Guests who want to further work on developing healthy sleep patterns can book private 30-minute consultations with Sabine at an additional cost. This session covers light therapy, sleeping posture, the sleep science of temperature, chronotypes, sleep architecture, food and drink timing, and light exposure.
