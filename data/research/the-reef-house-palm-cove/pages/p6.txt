# Travelling Solo to the Reef House

### Why Palm Cove is the perfect destination for Solo Travellers

There is something so rewarding and empowering about embarking on a solo adventure. The taste of the unknown, the feeling of freedom and the opportunity to curate the holiday of your dreams without having to compromise.

Whether you are looking for moments of solitude to completely switch off, a journey of self-discovery to build character and confidence or a mix of both – the Reef House is your ideal destination with a relaxing yet social atmosphere, a multitude of activities exclusively for in-house guests, all the facilities you could possibly need and prime Palm Cove location directly across from the beach.

The small seaside village of Palm Cove is one of the safest destinations for solo travellers with its tranquil vibe, welcoming local community and laidback atmosphere where everything you need is in walking distance, not to mention the close proximity to two of the worlds natural wonders, the Great Barrier Reef and ancient Daintree Rainforest.

_Here are our the reasons why the Reef House Palm Cove is the ultimate spot for solo travellers._

## Every detail is taken care of

Our Escape Planning Specialists are here to assist with the pre-planning of your Palm Cove getaway and upon arrival our Luxury Concierge Cedric is here to cater for your every need.

### Your holiday, your way

One of the biggest joys of solo travel is the luxury of time and indulging in endless days spent doing whatever you please, however you please. No compromises needed.

### New opportunities

Travelling solo can open you up to opportunities that might otherwise be missed. The people you meet. The places you visit. New friendships forged over impromptu conversations.

## Mingle with like-minded travellers

Travelling solo doesn’t need to feel like you are alone. The Reef House provides the ideal atmosphere for meeting fellow travellers with a daily activity schedule offered exclusively to our in-house guests. We’ve had plenty of guests become lifelong friends after holidaying at the Reef House and continuing to return each year.

#### Nightly Punch & Canapés

At dusk, the lanterns are lit and guests are invited to come and enjoy complimentary   punch & canapés in the Brigadier’s Lounge, providing the perfect opportunity to chat to other guests and exchange travel tips.

### Wellness Activities

Take part in our group fitness classes including beachfront yoga beneath the palms, stretch and breathe classes held by the Tranquility Pool and aqua aerobics in our heated Mineral Pool.

### Mixology Classes

Learn something new in our fun and interactive ‘ Reef House Mixology‘ class where our experienced bartender will teach you how to create (and enjoy) two delicious cocktails.

#### Wine Appreciation

Join our Sommelier in a wine appreciation session where you will taste and discuss a variety of wines in a group setting and have any of your questions answered.

### Skincare Talk in the Day Spa

Sip mimosas and chat all things skincare with our Spa Director Isabelle who shares all her tips and tricks for taking care of your skin and the secrets of defying age.

## Find solitude & recharge your batteries

If you’d prefer to take some time out for self-reflection and some much needed rest, relaxation and rejuvenation, look no further.

#### Take time out in nature

Savour the tropical warmth on your skin, feel the sand between your toes and salt in your hair. It’s pretty amazing what a dose of nature can do for the soul. We highly recommend taking a day trip north to Mossman Gorge and the Daintree Rainforest where you will discover freshwater swimming holes, lush green rainforest, incredible wildlife sightings and pristine walking trails.

### Relax Poolside

Treasure the chance to slow down and spend an afternoon lounging by our adults only tranquility pool, heated mineral pool and jacuzzis. Our poolside butler service can cater to your every whim whether it’s a cocktail or cool towel, relaxed lunch from our poolside menu or some delicious local tropical sorbet served everyday at 3pm.

#### Disconnect to reconnect

Take some time away from the digital world and read that book you’ve been longing to find the time for, take in deep breaths to the soothing soundtrack of the ocean in our beachfront yoga classes or adopt a beach stroll ritual each evening at sunset.

### Explore Palm Cove by bike

Getting around Palm Cove is easy with our vintage cruiser bikes available for complimentary use throughout your stay. Pedal the palm tree lined esplanade as you soak in the magnificent vistas across the Coral Sea.

## A Foodie's Paradise

If you tend to plan your holidays around where and what you’re going to eat, then the Reef House will not disappoint with its extensive offerings that give you no reason to leave the hotel if thats what you wish. Of course if you’d like to explore, there are a multitude of restaurants and cafe available on Williams Esplanade too.

#### Breakfast with a view

Linger on a leisurely breakfast as you gaze across the Coral Sea and plan your day ahead, to do as little or as much as you like. Our extensive menu includes everything from Acai bowls to avo toast, pancakes to Eggs Benedict and everything in between.

#### Reef House Restaurant

Dining on the Reef House deck is a highlight for many of our guests, matching an award-winning menu with a carefully curated wine list, beautiful views and the soothing sounds sounds of the ocean, it doesn’t get much better.

### Casual Dining

Looking for something a little more casual? Our poolside cafe menu is a great option to enjoy a relaxed meal in an informal poolside setting. Or opt to relax in the comfort of your own accommodation with room service or private in-room dining.

#### Unique Dining Experiences

One of our goals at the Reef House is to treat you like a celebrity with our five star service and extensive offerings. Indulge with one of our unique dining experiences including our picnic experiences,  private dining or seafood platters. Discover more here.

#### Atherton Tablelands foodie day trip

Enjoy a day trip to the Atherton Tablelands aka the ‘Food Bowl’ of the North. Taste some of the regions best produce including cheese, wine, tropical fruit, coffee, gin and more as you soak up the unique landscapes. Our Escape Planners can assist in planning your itinerary or tour.

## Indulge in the finer things in life

Every holiday deserves some indulgence, and a solo holiday is certainly no exception.

#### Day Spa pampering

If it’s total relaxation you’re trying to achieve, then a visit to the award-winning Reef House Day Spa is an absolute must. Choose from an impressive range of treatments that can be tailored to your needs using the exclusive Phytomer and Waterlily products. Our talented spa therapists will leave you feeling as light as a feather and ready to enjoy the rest of your solo holiday in paradise. Discover our range of spa rituals, facials, massages and spa package here.

#### Touch of Reef House luxury

From the moment you arrive our team will be working hard to exceed your expectations with a number of luxury touches to enhance your stay. Start with a gin-infused welcome drink on arrival that will be sure to get you into holiday mode. Our concierge will then whisk you away to your room where our signature coconut and lime scent will be wafting through the air, music playing and our gift to you awaits – a bottle of French Champagne and local chocolates. The icing on the cake is the exceptional Appelles toiletries to enjoy during your stay.
