# Work, Live & Play in Paradise

## at The Reef House Adults Retreat Palm Cove

The Reef House Adults Retreat is the most iconic Colonial Style Beach House in Queensland. We are renowned for our personalised service in a truly relaxed setting offering tranquillity, ambience & privacy.

Palm Cove, where the rainforest meets the sea, is located 25 minutes from Cairns and is the ideal base for exploring the Great Barrier Reef and the Daintree Rainforest.

Don’t miss this incredible opportunity of a lifetime to work, live and play in one of Australia’s most diverse and beautiful destinations with year-round sunshine, pristine beaches, lush rainforest, unique wildlife and a fun, laidback lifestyle.

## Who are we looking for?

We are currently seeking genuine hospitality professionals to help us deliver the highest level of guest engagement by building and grooming relationships with every customer. We value one’s ability to be self-motivated, be energetic and have a strong understanding of a la carte traditional service etiquette required to work in a Luxury Boutique Hotel.
