# Wellness Escapes in Palm Cove

### Retreat to the Reef House for rest, relaxation and rejuvenation.

If you’ve been craving an opportunity to escape your ordinary world and truly reconnect with your mind, body and soul, the Reef House is the perfect destination for you.

Indulge in the art of slowing down with blissful spa treatments, deep breaths, indulgent moments and nourishing dining experiences. Feel your tensions unravel the moment you step inside the Reef House and do as little or as much as you like, from beachfront yoga to dining experiences, aqua aerobics to spa treatments and so much more.

## Our Signature Experiences & Inclusions

We’re pleased to offer a range of signature inclusions and experiences to enjoy throughout your stay that have been designed to maximise your wellbeing and leave you feeling refreshed and revitalised.

#### Relax

Recharge your batteries and lounge by our adults only tranquility pool, heated mineral pool and jacuzzis. Enjoy daily poolside butler service including cool towels at 11am and local tropical sorbet at 3pm.

### Move

Getting your blood flowing is one of the best ways to maintain your physical wellbeing. Try our beachfront yoga, poolside stretch and breathe classes and aqua aerobics.

### Explore

Collect a vintage cruiser bike from reception and pedal around our beautiful little village of Palm Cove. Why not try your luck fishing off the Palm Cove jetty with fishing rods available for our guests.

#### Discover

Gather in the Reef House Day Spa for an insightful skincare Q&A and complimentary mimosa. This is a great opportunity to learn about the importance of a skincare routine and get professional advice.

### Tradition

Experience some of the Reef House oldest traditions with our Brigadiers Honesty Bar and our punch & canapés served each evening at twilight, a great opportunity to catch up with fellow guests.

## Day Spa Experiences

Succumb to total bliss and let the skilled hands of our spa therapists work their magic at the award-winning Reef House Day Spa. All treatments can be tailored to suit your personal needs. Each element and experience has been carefully thought our to provide an intimate wellness journey that will leave you with a calm mind and reborn beauty.

### Nourish Your Body

Start each day with a delicious breakfast overlooking the Coral Sea which will set the tone for your day of indulgence and relaxation. Lunch can be enjoyed poolside or opt for a picturesque picnic. Dinner is always an affair to remember whether you choose our a la carte menu, 7-course degustation, seafood platter or private dining experience with personalised service.

## Connect with Nature

The sleepy town of Palm Cove is an idyllic destination to unwind with its golden beaches, swaying palms and relaxed atmosphere. There are an abundance of nature activities on our doorstep including hiking trails, amazing waterfall circuits and so much more. Don’t miss visiting the oldest living rainforest in the world and the Great Barrier Reef; one of the seven natural wonders.
