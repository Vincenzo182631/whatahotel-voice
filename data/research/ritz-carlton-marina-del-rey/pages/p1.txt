Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey
Escape to the Los Angeles waterfront.
Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey, where ocean breezes, refined coastal luxury, and legendary service create an unforgettable waterfront retreat. Our guest rooms and suites offer contemporary coastal-inspired design, and thoughtful amenities. Savor fresh, locally-sourced cuisine and handcrafted cocktails at Cast & Plow, our marina-front restaurant. Unwind by the heated saltwater pool, indulge in a personalized Sisley Paris spa treatment, or enjoy tennis, pickleball, and cycling along the scenic coastline. Our hotel places you moments from the very best of Southern California.
A Transformation is Underway
The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey is undergoing a transformative enhancement designed to elevate every aspect of the guest experience. From August 10 to January 10, we're refreshing our lobby and guestrooms. Our pool, whirlpool, tennis and pickleball courts, and Fitness Center remain open throughout.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 30.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey is Santa Monica Municipal Airport (SMO). SMO is located approximately 5.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey have an airport shuttle to Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey does not have an airport shuttle to Los Angeles International Airport.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey have an airport shuttle to Santa Monica Municipal Airport (SMO)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey does not have an airport shuttle to Santa Monica Municipal Airport.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Marina del Rey offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
