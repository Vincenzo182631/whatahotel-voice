### Festive Season

Trade winter weather for island warmth this festive season at Kimpton Seafire. Enjoy thoughtfully curated holiday experiences, exceptional dining, wellness rituals, and beachfront celebrations, all set against the backdrop of Grand Cayman's iconic Seven Mile Beach.

Learn more

### The SPA at Seafire

The Spa at Seafire invites you into a refreshed world of relaxation and renewal. From private wellness sanctuaries to transformative treatments and restorative rituals, every detail has been thoughtfully reimagined to help you reconnect, recharge, and feel your best.

Learn more

### Pools

Serenity with a capital sea. That’s how we describe our two oceanfront pools. Bring the whole family to Seafire Pool, an ample oasis with an all-day bar serving salads, ceviche, hand-crafted cocktails and more.

Learn more

### Library by the Sea

Settle into one of the Caribbean's most acclaimed cocktail destinations at Library by the Sea. Named among the Top 50 Bars in North America, this intimate lounge pairs inventive cocktails, rare spirits, and impeccable craftsmanship in a setting that's equal parts sophisticated and inviting. Perfect for a pre-dinner aperitif or a nightcap by the sea

Learn more

### 10 Years of Kimpton Seafire Resort + Spa

In 2016, Kimpton Seafire opened its doors on Seven Mile Beach and redefined modern luxury in the Cayman Islands. Ten years later, we’re celebrating the people, moments and memories that have made this place so much more than a resort.

Learn more

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Seafire Resort + Spa?

Check-in at Kimpton Seafire Resort + Spa is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is Kimpton Seafire Resort + Spa from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Seafire Resort + Spa is conveniently located near Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM), approximately 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Seafire Resort + Spa?

Kimpton Seafire Resort + Spa is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Camana Bay-3.54 miles away (5.7 kilometers)
- North Sound Golf Club-2.67 miles away (4.3 kilometers)
- Rum Point-29.39 miles away (47.3 kilometers)
- Stingray City
- Queen Elizabeth II Botanic Park-21.93 miles away (35.3 kilometers)
- Cayman Turtle Centre-3.6 miles away (5.8 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Seafire Resort + Spa puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
