## Our Philosophy

At Raffles Dubai we create an environment that nurtures wellbeing holistically, through harmonious design, serene rituals, nutrition for pleasure and the world's best wellness experiences. Everything that touches the senses is consciously crafted to take care of your physical and emotional wellbeing. Expect a sumptuous sojourn, with spa journeys tailor-made to suit your needs and a state-of-the-art gym with personal training. We partner with expert local practitioners and world-class wellness and beauty brands, and our products are sourced with environmental sustainability in mind, reflecting our social responsibilities.

-

### AROMA TOUCH

This aromatherapy massage is designed to alleviate stress and restore harmony to your body and mind. Using carefully selected essential oils, the treatment balances your skin, soothes your muscles, and calms your energy, leaving you with a deep sense of equilibrium and relaxation.

-

### GROUNDING RITUAL

Guided by the strength of earth and stone, this journey begins with a revitalizing exfoliation that awakens the skin. A mineral-rich Moor Mud wrap draws out impurities, before the flow of a restorative massage, clarifying facial, and nourishing scalp ritual bring body and spirit to a place of grounded clarity.

-

### THE SOUL OF ARABIA

This serene ritual invites you to slow down and return to yourself. Inspired by the calming traditions of Arabia, warm aromatic oils are blended with a soothing massage of intuitive movements that dissolve tension and restore balance. The experience concludes with gentle sound healing - a quiet resonance that restores deep inner stillness.

-

### ORGANIC HERBAL POULTICE MASSAGE

Warm, steamed bags infused with orange, clove, ginger, and Fucus Serratus are gently massaged over the body, releasing their rich, aromatic oils for a deeply soothing experience. This massage helps to alleviate muscle tension, improve circulation, and enhance overall relaxation, leaving your skin nourished and your body revitalized.

-

### MOM TO BE MASSAGE

Indulge in our soothing organic wellbeing ritual, specially crafted for expectant mothers. This treatment promotes deep relaxation and tranquility while providing your skin with essential hydration and nourishment, ensuring a sense of calm and rejuvenation throughout your pregnancy journey.

-

### GOLDEN BODY TREATMENT

A luxurious body polish designed to smooth and
illuminate the skin with immediate visible results. Infused with mineral-rich actives and hydrating oils, this treatment leaves the skin soft, radiant, and renewed - perfect before a special occasion or as a luminous reset.
