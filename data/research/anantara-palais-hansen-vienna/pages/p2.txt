Built by one of Vienna’s leading architects, Baron Theophil Edvard von Hansen, and an integral part of the city’s rich heritage, Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna is a newly renovated 5-star hotel in Vienna that impresses with its Neo-Renaissance grandeur and unrivalled luxe.

Enjoy a coveted location on the Ringstrasse, with the city’s leading museums, theatres and coffeehouses within walking distance, then return to Michelin-starred dining and spa unwinding at this luxury hotel in Vienna.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Where is Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel?**

Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel, a luxury hotel in Vienna, is located on the grand Ringstrasse that surrounds the city centre. Famous sights, including museums, palaces and theatres, are within walking distance.

**How do I get to Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel?**

Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel is a 25-minute drive from Vienna International Airport, with u-bahn and tram stations just down the road.

**Does Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel offer parking?**

A public parking garage is located underneath Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel with direct access to the lobby via a lift. A daily fee is applicable.

**What leisure facilities are there at Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel?**

Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel, a 5-star hotel in Vienna, offers immaculate wellness facilities, including a spa, indoor hydropool and gym.

**What dining options are there at Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel?**

There are three restaurants and bars at Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel, including our Michelin-starred restaurant, EDVARD with an extensive wine collection.

**What event facilities does Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel offer?**

Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel has dedicated event spaces with private street entrance, from a grand ballroom to meeting rooms catering for up to 265 guests.

**Are there wheelchair-accessible rooms at Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel?**

Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel is pleased to offer wheelchair-accessible accommodations.

**Are pets allowed at Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel?**

Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel, our luxury hotel in Vienna, welcomes pets with prior reservation. Please contact the hotel for further information and to book directly.
