# InterContinental ChangshaRooms & suites

InterContinental Changsha offers full-equiped luxury guest rooms with fantastic river views of Changsha’s Xiangjiang River. All rooms are contemporarily designed, elegantly furnished and styled with local cultural flair. Each has a digital entertainment system, luxurious marble bathtub and separate walk-in rainforest shower, ergonomic workspace and high-speed internet access.
