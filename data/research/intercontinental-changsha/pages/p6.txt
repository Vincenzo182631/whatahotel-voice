InterContinental Changsha sits prominently in the Beichen Delta of Kaifu District, where the Xiang River and Liuyang River converge. The hotel enjoys convenient access to Changsha Huanghua International Airport and is just four metro stops from Wuyi Square, the city's vibrant commercial heart. Nearby landmarks include Kaifu Temple and Yuelu Mountain, with the Three Halls and One Center cultural complex and Beichen Hui commercial hub within easy walking distance.

## FAQs for InterContinental Changsha

Does InterContinental Changsha have a pool?

InterContinental Changsha has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

The Fitness Center features a fully-equipped gym, heated indoor swimming pool with sky-view. All center facilities are state-of-the-art, and designed to make your stay even more enjoyable.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Changsha?

Staying guest please drive from the hotel entrance and park at Floor G or M.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Changsha?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Changsha.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Changsha for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Changsha have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Changsha has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Changsha?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Changsha.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Changsha?

Check-in at InterContinental Changsha is from 2:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Changsha offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Changsha offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Changsha from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Changsha is conveniently located near Changsha Huanghua International Airport (CSX), approximately 19.88 miles away (32 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Changsha?

InterContinental Changsha is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Kaifu Temple-1.93 miles away (3.1 kilometers)
- Orange Isle Scenic Spot-4.97 miles away (8 kilometers)
- Yuelu Academy-10.56 miles away (17 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Changsha puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
