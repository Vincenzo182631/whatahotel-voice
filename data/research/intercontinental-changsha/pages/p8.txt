## Things to do

### Kaifu Temple

No. 52, Kaifu Road, Kaifu District, Changsha, Hunan 410000

As a buddhist temple located in Kaifu District, Hunan Province in the People's Republic of China, Kaifu Temple includes Entrance, Hall of the Great Heroes, Assisted dnyana, Dining Room, etc. Kaifu Temple belongs to the Yangqi and Rinzai schools of buddhism. The temple is built within a 16,000 square metres area of land.

Learn more

### Orange Isle Scenic Spot

Orange Isle Scenic Spot, Yuelu District, Changsha, Changsha, Hunan 410006

Orange Isle Scenic Spot is located in the middle of the Xiang Jiang River. Stretching miles from south to north across the river, it’s facing to the Yuelu Mountain on the west and the Changsha City on the east. Orange Isle is an important tourist attraction of Changsha, a key national AAAAA (5A) place of interest which known as "The First Continent in China".

Learn more

### Yuelu Academy

Lu Shan Road(inside Hunan University), Yuelu District, Changsha, Hunan 410012

Yuelu Academy is one of the "Four Great Academies" in ancient China, founded in the Northern Song Dynasty. In 1903, it was renamed Hunan Higher Education School, in 1926 it was named Hunan University, and in 1937 it was renamed National Hunan University. At present, Yuelu Academy is a secondary college of Hunan University, where a thousand year long tradition of education, academic culture, and research continues.
