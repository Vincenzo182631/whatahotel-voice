## SKY27

Stylish. Dazzling. Unforgettable. Perched atop the InterContinental Changsha, SKY27 is the city’s most iconic rooftop bar. With a panoramic view of the Xiangjiang River and city skyline, guests often liken it to being in Manhattan, London, or Shanghai. Come for the cocktails, stay for the vibe—SKY27 is where Changsha’s nightlife reaches new heights.
