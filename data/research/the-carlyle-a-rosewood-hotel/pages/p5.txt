Reimagined New York classics and nostalgic throwback dishes, where every plate tells a story of seasonality and provenance, set amidst a rich collection of over 200 works by local artists in an iconic Manhattan setting.

To preserve the elegant atmosphere of Dowling's at The Carlyle, we encourage guests to dress for the occasion. We kindly ask that guests arrive in proper attire and refrain from wearing athletic or casual wear, such as hoodies, sweatpants, shorts, tank tops, baseball caps or athletic sneakers. Thank you for helping us preserve the distinctive magic of Dowling's at The Carlyle.

Illustrator Tea

### Afternoon Impressions

Dowling’s welcomes Izak Zenou as illustrator-in-residence throughout 2026. Known for his 20+ years imprinting the windows of the legendary Henri Bendel on Fifth Avenue, Zenou joins guests in the dining room, creating live sketches in real time. Each drawing is made in the moment as a keepsake of the experience.

Reserve your experience on select dates throughout the year.
