# Bemelmans Bar

An iconic New York hideaway, renowned for nightly piano, late-night jazz and murals by Ludwig Bemelmans.

Inquire

View Menu

360° TOUR

A favorite of Upper East Siders, this iconic New York hideaway features storied murals by Ludwig Bemelmans, nightly piano and late-night jazz. A place for everyone to be seen sipping a nightcap, drawing socialites, politicians, movie stars, and moguls to its classic timeless charm.

To preserve the elegant atmosphere of Bemelmans Bar, we encourage guests to dress for the occasion. We kindly ask that guests arrive in proper attire and refrain from wearing athletic or casual wear, such as hoodies, sweatpants, shorts, tank tops, baseball caps or athletic sneakers. Thank you for helping us preserve the distinctive magic of Bemelmans Bar.

**HOURS**

12 pm - 12 am (Sunday - Monday)

12 pm - 12:30 am (Tuesday - Thursday)

12 pm - 1 am (Friday - Saturday)

**CONTACT**+1 (212) 570-7120

### Dress Code

As one of the best-dressed rooms on the Upper East Side, we kindly ask guests to dress for the occasion. Athletic and casual leisure wear are better suited for other settings. At our discretion, we may suggest guests modify their attire or join us at another time.

### Live Music

A cover charge applies starting at 5:30 pm with the pianists opening set, followed by the jazz trio from 9 pm onward.

Tables: USD25\* - USD35\* per person

The Bar: USD20\* - USD25\* per person

\* _Depending on the artist_
