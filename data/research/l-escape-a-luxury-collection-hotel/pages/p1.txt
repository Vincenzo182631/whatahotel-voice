# Welcome to L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong

## Boutique Hotel in Myeong-dong Near Seoul City Hall & N Tower

Experience a luxury city retreat like no other at L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel Seoul, Myeongdong. With an exceptional location in the heart of the city, our boutique hotel in Seoul's Myeongdong​...See More neighborhood is near many leading attractions, including N Seoul Tower, Namdaemun Market, and Deoksu-gung Palace. Our hotel is a haven of Belle Époque-inspired elegance, where sumptuous interiors by Jacques Garcia recall the bygone glamor of 19th-century Paris. Sophisticated accommodation includes pet-friendly rooms and grand suites, each curated with plush beds, refined decor, and modern comforts. Choose from our collection of dining options, including Michelin-starred French cuisine and specialty Chinese fare, while the top floor houses a world-class cocktail bar. Relax with tailor-made treatments at our tranquil spa, before energizing with a workout at the 24/7 Fitness Center. Our luxury hotel in Myeongdong​ also features elevated events spaces for celebrations, corporate functions, and boutique weddings.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong?

The check-in time at L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong.

What are the parking options at L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong?

On-site parking is available at L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for ₩25000.00 per hour.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong booking.

What is the closest airport near L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong?

The closest airport near L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong is Gimpo International Airport (GMP). GMP is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.

Does L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong have a La Maison Boutique lounge?

Yes, L’Escape, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Myeongdong has La Maison Boutique lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with La Maison Boutique lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services.
