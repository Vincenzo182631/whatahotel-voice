## Mindful journeys through Bhutan’s hidden valleys

Tucked high in the Eastern Himalayas between India and China, Bhutan is one of the world’s most enigmatic destinations, where tradition is sacred, nature is revered, and happiness is a national priority. Life won’t look the same after you come to the Land of the Thunder Dragon. You won’t want it to. This place, sometimes referred to as "The Last Shangri-La," prioritizes Gross National Happiness over economic growth. Bhutan has chosen a path of low-volume, high-value tourism, preserving its pristine environment and rich cultural heritage. Snow-capped mountains, lush valleys, and centuries-old monasteries form a surreal landscape that feels untouched by time. Nature and spirituality are deeply intertwined, fluttering prayer flags dance in the mountain breeze, cliffside temples like Tiger’s Nest seem to defy gravity, and crimson-robed monks chant in dzongs that have stood for centuries. Join us to reawaken your senses, disconnect to reconnect with purpose, and experience life in a more meaningful rhythm.

Sustainability

## Preserving and protecting the world’s first carbon-negative country

As the world’s first carbon negative country, Bhutan is a vibrant tapestry of protected forest and abundant nature which is depicted in the ancient murals and carvings within Buddhist monasteries and temples. The uncompromising focus on the harmonious well-being of people and planet is evident from the verdant paddy-lined lower valleys up to the pristine snow-capped peaks, unclimbed except by the occasional yeti. We also feel a deep obligation to care for our environment, natural resources and communities

## Plan your journey with us

Six Senses Thimphu, Six Senses Punakha, Six Senses Gangtey, and Six Senses Paro sit high on hilltops like gemstones throughout the Kingdom’s western and central valleys, while Six Senses Bumthang is located near the Chamkar Chhu (river), calling you further east. Whether you stay at one lodge or all five, we will take care of all the planning for a bespoke “Khamsa” or “Royal Stroll” through the Kingdom. With a private guide, driver and vehicle, we’ll take you beyond the inspiring scenery and deep into the heart of each valley you explore. Come with us through the rich cultural heritage, local life, breathtaking scenery, and unique healing therapies, all wrapped up in warm-hearted hospitality.
