# Fitness Center, Pool and Saunas

Experience the joy of moving your body and the peace of relaxing without a care at the Imperial Hotel.

- **Facilities**
- **Hours**
- **Entrance Fees**

The Imperial Hotel Osaka Annex Building houses an elegant members-only fitness club.

Entering the lobby, guests are immersed in the club's open, resort-like interior with its abundance of natural light and greenery. The club boasts a total floor area of 4,400 m², one of the largest in Western Japan, and it offers advanced equipment as well as the hospitable services the Imperial Hotel has honed for over 120 years. We hope that you will enjoy exercising your body and relaxing comfortably at the Imperial Hotel.

- \*

This facility is open only to resident guests, fitness club membership holders and accompanying guests.

## Entrance Fees

### Entrance Fees (for guests staying at the hotel)

- | Use of pool, bath area, and sauna (one time) | 2,200 yen |

- | Use of pool, bath area, sauna, and sports facilities (one time) | 5,500 yen |

- Please note that although use fees cover unlimited use for an entire day, you will be charged an additional entrance fee each time you leave and re-enter the facilities.

- The Imperial Club International Members can enjoy facilities at a 50% discount off the regular price during their stays.

As this is a members-only fitness club, it can only be used by club members, guests accompanying members, and registered hotel guests.

Fees for use: Members (free of charge), guests accompanying members (11,000 yen), persons staying at the hotel (5,500 yen)

- The above prices include rental fees for clothing, shoes, and other items. Non-members are charged separate fees for golf and other private lessons.

- Fitness Center, Pool, and Saunas are only available for guests over the age of 18.

- The club can under no circumstances be used by persons with heart disease or an infectious disease, persons who have are prohibited to exercise or bathe in accordance with the instructions of a doctor, intoxicated persons, and persons with tattoos.

- Please note that the club will assume no responsibility for loss or theft inside club premises.

- The members lounge inside the club is nonsmoking.

- Taking food and beverages inside the facilities is strictly prohibited.

- Entering the club wearing the hotel's light kimono, gown, or slippers is strictly prohibited.

- Please refrain from using cell phones inside the club.

- Please return items to their designated storage locations after you have finished using them.

- Please put your bags inside of the lockers, and refrain from carrying them inside the facilities.

- Please be considerate and respectful of other guests.
