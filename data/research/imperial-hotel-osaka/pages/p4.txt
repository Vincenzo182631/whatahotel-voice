## Entertainment & Unique Experiences

## Sumo Studio Osaka

- 1

More than a sport, sumo is a living tradition rooted in Shinto rituals, embodying discipline and honor. This 90-minute live performance features retired professional wrestlers who bring this ancient tradition to life through authentic demonstration matches, expert commentary, and audience participation. Gain insight into the rituals, techniques, and history that have shaped one of Japan’s most iconic cultural traditions, and witness its remarkable power, skill and artistry up close.

## Japan Tatedo Association

- 2

Tatedo, the art of Samurai swordplay, is the traditional art of choreographed swordplay often displayed in Japanese films and theater. Far more than mere combat, it demands flawless timing, graceful movement, and a shared harmony between attacker and defender, creating a breathtaking and realistic experience. In this exclusive one-hour experience, you will master the fundamentals of handling a katana (Japanese sword), both striking and reacting, resulting in a cinematic sequence worthy of the silver screen. And after your visit, you can be sent a video recording of your performance as a perfect memento to show family and friends.

## Horie Art School

- 3

Horie Art School in Osaka is a creative hub where anime, manga, and character design are taught. Offering tailored guidance for all ages and flexible drop-in lessons, they make it easy to try out a class or focus on a specific technique. They specialize in pop culture and illustration, covering both traditional drawing and digital techniques. Guided by experienced instructors, drop-in and long-term students alike learn to draw expressive characters and bring their favorite fictional worlds to life.

## Japanese Culture & Traditions

## Chopsticks Making Osaka

- 5

Chopstick Making Osaka is a hands-on Japanese craft experience where participants shape and finish their own pair of wooden chopsticks. Held in a cozy, quiet local setting, the workshop emphasizes balance, precision, and attention to detail. Through the process of crafting chopsticks, guests gain insight into the philosophy, aesthetics, and craftsmanship behind an everyday object that plays a central role Japanese life.

## Creating your Katana Knife (BUB ACTIVITY CENTER OSAKA)

- 6

Slip on a traditional haori jacket and you’re ready to craft your own one-of-a-kind Japanese knife in this unique Osaka experience. Under the guidance of friendly, English-speaking master swordsmiths, you will learn the rich history of Japanese sword production while you practice sharpening the blade of your own sword, a wonderful memento of your experience. Complete your masterpiece by engraving your name or a custom design on the handle. After the workshop, unwind and savor the experience with complimentary Japanese tea and snacks.

## Crafting a Tin Sake Cup (BUB ACTIVITY CENTER OSAKA)

- 7

Experience the art of suzu (tin) metalworking. Under the guidance of friendly English-speaking staff, you will use traditional hand-hammering techniques to craft your own unique tin sake cup. After the workshop, enjoy water tasting to discover how drinking from a tin cup perfectly smoothens and enhances the flavor. This personalized experience lets you connect with the local culture and take home a beautiful, hand-packaged memory of Japan.

## Creating Bonsai (BUB ACTIVITY CENTER OSAKA)

- 8

Discover the history and philosophy of this internationally practiced Japanese art by creating your own bonsai in Osaka. Our English-speaking staff will help you to select a favorite pot and preserved tree branches and leaves to shape a unique masterpiece. This personalized workshop offers a meaningful way to chat with locals and connect with Japanese traditions. Made from real, dried natural materials, your customized dry bonsai requires no maintenance and can be safely flown home internationally.

## Tool Shop WADA

- 10

Tool shop WADA (Wada Shoten) in Sakai City offers top-quality Japanese cutlery, handcrafted by master artisans using centuries-old techniques. Sakai knives are world-renowned for their unmatched sharpness, durability, and precision cutting performance. At Wada Shoten, you can explore a wide selection of authentic traditional blades suitable for both professional chefs and those cooking at home. Beyond shopping, the store provides an exclusive knife-making workshop where you can forge your own blade. Experience the art of crafting the world’s sharpest cutlery firsthand and take home a truly unique Japanese souvenir.

Savor seasonal Japanese sweets designed to complement the rich, earthy flavors of the tea. This experience provides a peaceful escape from the bustling city, while offering deep insights into Japanese hospitality and culture.

## Mochi Making (Mochi Pounding Experience Osaka「Oh Mochi」)

- 12

Explore a fun and dynamic Japanese culinary tradition with a hands-on mochi-making experience in Osaka. Using traditional wooden mallets and mortars, you will pound steaming sticky rice into delicious, soft rice cakes. After the lively session, you can bite into your freshly made mochi served with popular toppings like sweet soy sauce and kinako powder. This memorable activity offers a delightful blend of authentic culture, teamwork, and local flavors.

## Naniwa Nipponbashi Theater

- 13

The "Geiko Dance", the pride of Naniwa Nipponbashi Theater, is not merely a performance, but a living tradition imbued with Japan's history and aesthetic spirit. Performed on a stage woven from soft light, every movement of the magnificent Geiko vividly reflects the charm and delicate sensibility of classic Japan. Whispering a timeless story that speaks to the viewer's heart with serene and profound emotion — this is the supreme moment bestowed by the Geiko Dance.

## Sakai Traditional Crafts Museum (Denshokan)

- 14

Culinary enthusiasts and connoisseurs of fine craftwork need look no further. At the Sakai Traditional Crafts Museum (Denshokan), you can take in the impressive knife-blade chandelier just inside the entrance, learn about the history and forging process of Sakai knife-crafting, shop for professional-grade blades and watch live sharpening demonstrations that bring this ancient craft to life. This is the definitive stop for anyone interested in high quality cooking knives and bladesmithing.

## Osaka Duck Tour

- 21

Experience Osaka’s cityscape and landmarks from a unique vantage point 3.7 meters above the ground, cruising through the city with the wind in your hair. The highlight of the tour is the thrilling splash-in, when the amphibious bus seamlessly transitions from land to water, a one-of-a-kind experience unique to this adventure. Once afloat, enjoy surprisingly tranquil views from the water, offering a fresh perspective on this vibrant metropolis.

## Aqua Liner

- 22

A classic cruise in the water city of Osaka. A sightseeing boat that takes you around Osaka’s famous landmarks and historical sites, such as Osaka Castle and Nakanoshima. One of its attractions is the view of the many bridges and the scenery that changes with the seasons, seen from just above the water’s surface. You can board and disembark at one of three piers, making it a convenient mode of transportation for sightseeing.
