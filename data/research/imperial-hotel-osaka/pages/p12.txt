## From Airport

### From Kansai International Airport

| Taxi | Approximately 60 minutes |
| Airport Limousine Bus | Take the bus bound for "Osaka Castle/Nippombashi" departing from Stop 10 (airport's 1F) and get off at "Imperial Hotel Osaka" (Approximately 100 minutes) Fares adult　1,800 yen、child　900 yen (One-way) Read more |
| Bus | From airport to Osaka city Take the bus bound for "Osaka Station" departing from Stop 5 (Terminal 1, 1F) or Stop 1(Terminal 2, 1F) and get off at "Herbis Osaka" (Approximately 60 minutes) (2-3 buses operate per hour) From Osaka Station to the hotel Approximately 15 minutes by shuttle bus Or take the JR Osaka Loop Line and get off at Sakuranomiya Station, about 5 minutes walk. - Complimentary shuttle service from JR Osaka Station |
| Train (JR) | Approximately 47 minutes by limited express Haruka from JR Kansai Airport Station to Osaka Station From Osaka Station to the hotel Approximately 15 minutes by shuttle bus Or take the JR Osaka Loop Line and get off at Sakuranomiya Station, about 5 minutes walk. - Complimentary shuttle service from JR Osaka Station |
| Train (Nankai Electric Railway) | From Nankai Electric Railway Kansai Airport Station to Namba Station: Approximately 40-minutes by Limited Express Rapi:t Approximately 50-minutes by airport express From Namba Station to Umeda Station Approximately 10 minutes on the Osaka Metro Midosuji Line (Approximately 5 minutes walk to JR Osaka Station) From Osaka Station to the hotel Approximately 15 minutes by shuttle bus Or take the JR Osaka Loop Line and get off at Sakuranomiya Station, about 5 minutes walk. - Complimentary shuttle service from JR Osaka Station |

### From Osaka International Airport (Itami Airport)

| Taxi | Approximately 30 minutes |
| Bus | From airport to Osaka city Take the bus bound for "Osaka Station" departing from Airport North Terminal Stop 4 or Airport South Terminal Stop 14, and get off at "Herbis Osaka" (Approximately 30 minutes) (Runs approximately every 30 minutes) From Osaka Station to the hotel Approximately 15 minutes by shuttle bus Or take the JR Osaka Loop Line and get off at Sakuranomiya Station, about 5 minutes walk. - Complimentary shuttle service from JR Osaka Station |

### From Kobe Airport

| Taxi | Approximately 60 minutes |
| Train | Approximately 18 minutes from Kobe Airport Station to Sannomiya Station by Kobe New Transit Port Liner Approximately 20 minutes by JR Kobe Line Rapid from Sannomiya Station to Osaka Station From Osaka Station to the hotel Approximately 15 minutes by shuttle bus Or take the JR Osaka Loop Line and get off at Sakuranomiya Station, about 5 minutes walk. - Complimentary shuttle service from JR Osaka Station |
