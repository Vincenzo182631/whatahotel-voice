# Facilities

Enjoy a comfortable stay with our array of facilities including a pool, saunas, a members-only fitness club, and conference and meeting rooms.

- _Business Center_\\
\\
Guests can use our Business Center, conference room, computers, and various types of business machines throughout their sojourn.\\
\\

- _Fitness Center, Pool and Saunas_\\
\\
This club boasts a total floor space of 4,400 m², one of the largest in Western Japan, and offers advanced equipment as well as the obliging services of the Imperial Hotel.\\
\\

- _Floor Plan_\\
\\
Please refer to our hotels floor map to find what you are looking for.\\
\\

- _Guide to Entrances_\\
\\
The hotel has four entrances. Entrances are provided for the different purposes of visiting the hotel, including staying at the hotel or attending a banquet.\\
\\

- _Hotel Accessibility_\\
\\
Our facilities are designed to accommodate guests with wheelchairs or other physical disabilities, ensuring a comfortable and secure experience.\\
\\

- _The Imperial Hotel Plaza_\\
\\
This shopping plaza lies along the bank of the Okawa River, part of Osaka Amenity Park.\\
\\
You can leisurely choose from a wide selection of high-quality items from around the world in a luxurious setting.\\
\\
