# Steigenberger Icon Frankfurter Hof

### Steigenberger Icon Frankfurter Hof

In the heart of the financial district and the historical city center, we have been welcoming guests in the tradition-steeped Steigenberger Icon Frankfurter Hof since 1876. The Maintower, Römer and Paulskirche are only a few steps away. The Central station can be reached in approx. a 13 minutes' walk.

### Immerse yourself in an exclusive ambiance unlike any other

Enjoy the sophisticated ambience and first-class service in one of our 267 hotel rooms and 36 suites. The upscale amenities of this 5-star hotel in Frankfurt include a flat-screen TV, air conditioning, complimentary minibar, desk and safe.

### Culinary highlights

The restaurants of the Steigenberger Icon Frankfurter Hof offer culinary highlights for every taste. Start your day in our stylish Restaurant Hofgarten with a freshly prepared breakfast or our sumptuous buffet. In our Brasserie Moderne OSCAR's your palate will be spoiled with regional and contemporary presented menus and snacks.

### Highlights

WiFi (free of charge) in the entire hotel, Restaurant Brasserie Moderne OSCAR's, Autorenbar, Restaurant Hofgarten, Ehrenhof terrace, Cigarrum, THE SPA, barber, fitness area, 17 meeting rooms and a total event space of 1.200 m².

### Pampering moments in the exclusive THE SPA

Look forward to free access to the exclusive "The Spa", which makes up a large part of our spa and wellness area. Treat yourself to a time-out in the style of "1001 Nights" in our luxury hotel in Frankfurt. On about 1,000 m² you have the opportunity to pamper your body, mind and soul.

Are you looking for an ideal meeting room? Our professional event team will be happy to advise you on our exclusive conference offers. With 17 conference rooms, whether salon or ballroom, the Steigenberger Icon Frankfurter Hof offers you the best conditions for holding your personal meetings and events.
