# The One Aldwych Pool

## Immerse yourself

Our biggest secret is our 18-metre / 59-foot lap pool, hidden under the hotel and seemingly a million miles from the bustle of the streets above. Thanks to an innovative mineral cleaning system, our pool is chlorine-free, for an underwater experience without the usual harsh chemicals. Drop in for a refreshing swim before or after a long day, or to unwind between treatments before a blissful session in the steam room or sauna.
