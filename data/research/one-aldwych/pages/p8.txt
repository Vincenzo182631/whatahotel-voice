# Our Neighbourhood

## London's most exciting neighbourhood

Ideally located in the heart of Covent Garden, we are just steps away from an abundance of extraordinary dining, museums and renowned shopping, with cornerstones of British culture within easy reach, and nearly all of the West End theatres within 15 minutes walk. Think of our resident team as the friends with all the best recommendations. Brunch, ballet, boutiques, cocktails, exhibitions and the little known corners of the city that we think you’ll love.

### Autumn in London

Endless attractions and hidden gems await as Autumn in the city offers unforgettable experiences for everyone. Step outside and explore some of our favourite things to do this season.

### Opera & Dance

With The Royal Ballet and English National Opera within a short walk of One Aldwych, guests looking for classic culture will be spoilt for choice.

More

### Galleries

London is home to an extraordinary number of world-class galleries with new and exciting exhibitions opening all the time. Perhaps take a stroll up The Strand to the National Gallery, wind through historical streets towards the British Museum or stop across the road at Somerset House.

More

### Shopping & Markets

Covent Garden has a unique and eclectic mix of leading luxury brands, high street favourites and independent makers and producers. There is also the famous market full of crafts and treasures in the iconic Market Building, complete with daily entertainment – watch out for the musicians and magicians!

More

### Sightseeing

We’re always happy to help you plan your day to get the most of visiting our capital city. There are must-see attractions close by, and plenty of famous spots to take in on your walks, drives or cycles. You can also explore the Thames by boat, or get a bird’s eye view from the London Eye!

More

### Family days out

Make falling in love with London a group activity and create lasting memories together while you explore our most exciting neighbourhood, Covent Garden. Discover galleries, museums, West End theatres and boutiques as a family, from our perfectly positioned doorstep.
