As London’s cultured independent hotel we own our location in Covent Garden. Yet on talking with local friends and neighbours, we found there are still things for us to discover; the exquisite selection of Japanese silk-screened papers at stationers Choosing Keeping, exceptional vegan dining in Neal’s Yard and London’s oldest wine bar.

Curious to know more, we have assembled a collection of voices from the community to share their knowledge and give you a sense of surprise and discovery as you get under the skin of London. Their guides will hold your hand as you go off the beaten track to live as a Londoner with One Aldwych as your residence.
