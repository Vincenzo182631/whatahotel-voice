# Inside One Aldwych

Our cultured, independent hotel at the beating heart of London life.

### History

Stepping into the lobby of our important Edwardian building is to step into a moment in history, dressed up in style. Built in 1905 as the home of the Morning Post, we like to think it still inspires spirited conversation and connection.
