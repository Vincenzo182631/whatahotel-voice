## FAQs

#### What is included in a luxury hotel room at One Aldwych?

Each room is designed as a calm retreat above the bustling city, with thoughtful details throughout. You’ll find beautifully considered interiors, a carefully curated, complimentary minibar with drinks, snacks and in-house filtered still and sparkling water and a Nespresso coffee machine 24-hour in-room dining, complimentary  high-speed WiFi and access to our Health Club with its pool, steam room, sauna, large gym and treatment rooms.

#### Does One Aldwych have hotel rooms with views of London landmarks?

Yes, all of our rooms look out onto the surrounding streets of Covent Garden surrounded by theatres, with glimpses of London’s familiar landmarks including the London Eye, The Shard and Waterloo Bridge.

#### How do I book a hotel room in London directly with One Aldwych?

You can book directly through our website or by contacting our Reservations Team by telephone or over email. Booking with us ensures access to our best rates and latest availability, along with any tailored offers we may have.

Contact us

#### Are there any special offers available for stays in London?

Yes, we offer a selection of thoughtfully curated seasonal offers and experiences, from prepaid rates to long stays, and a host of cultural moments. Our website is the best place to explore what’s currently available.

Explore our offers

#### What’s the difference between the suites and bedrooms at One Aldwych?

Within the art nouveau curves of our heritage building, all of our bedrooms and suites are individual. Our bedrooms are designed for comfort and ease, while our suites offer more space and additional features. Many include a separate living area, giving you room to relax, entertain or simply enjoy a slower pace during your stay.

#### Can I request an early check-in or late check-out?

Please let us know in advance if an early check-in or late check-out would be helpful with your travel plans and we will do our very best to accommodate you in line with availability on the day.

#### Is One Aldwych suitable for families or extended stays?

Yes, very much so. We welcome families with thoughtful touches for younger guests, and our rooms and suites are well suited to longer stays, offering flexible configurations and up to four interconnecting rooms for added space and comfort in the heart of the city. To make travel with young children easier, we are happy to provide highchairs, baby bathtubs and baby wash, cribs, bottle warmers and steriliser, changing pad and a sealed waste bin.

For more information, explore The Kids are on us offer

#### Are the rooms pet-friendly?

All well-behaved, small to medium-sized dogs who have passed their first birthday are invited to stay in our dedicated dog-friendly bedrooms and suites, provided they are fully house-trained. Please let us know at the time of booking so we can ensure everything is prepared for your pet’s arrival.

Find out more: Dog-friendly stays

#### What facilities do the suites include (e.g. separate lounge, terrace, view)?

Our suites are designed with space and comfort in mind. Many feature separate living areas, dressing rooms and separate guest cloakrooms, as well as generous bathrooms and views across Covent Garden. Each one is a place to settle in and feel at home. Individually designed, we have a number of interconnecting options with our suites to comfortably accommodate families and small groups.

#### Where is One Aldwych located in London and what are the nearby attractions?

We are located in Covent Garden, right at the centre of London’s cultural scene. From here, you’re just moments from the Royal Opera House, West End theatres, The National Gallery, Somerset House and some of the city’s best shopping and dining. Our friendly Concierge are on-hand to advise and tailor itineraries to suit all tastes.
