## We like to describe One Aldwych as the hotel for Londoners at heart, wherever they might be from.

#### Our Neighbourhood

Our home is at the centre of Covent Garden, London’s most exciting neighbourhood. For the curious minded, you have access to the finest entertainment and cultural adventures, positioned as we are on the doorstep to galleries, museums, West End theatres and the famous Royal Opera House.

Discover More

#### Our Recommendations

Consider our resident team as the friends with all the best recommendations. Brunch, ballet, boutiques, cocktails, exhibitions, and the little-known corners of the city that we think you’ll love.

Discover More

### Suites & Bedrooms

Designed to be more than just a place to lay your head, each bedroom and suite at One Aldwych embraces a contemporary, stylish character ready to host, cocoon and enchant. All with the sense of modern, independent Britishness that we like to champion.

Discover More

### Signature Suites

Each of our four Signature Suites has unique features and bespoke touches inspired by the space and surrounding views, as well as artwork from One Aldwych’s extensive private collection. If you admire a particular piece, there will be a story behind it that we will be all too happy to share!

Discover More

### Bedrooms

Our Superior and Deluxe Bedrooms are a celebration of warm, modern British design with a timeless appeal.

Discover More

### Suites

We have twelve beautiful and spacious suites each with their own defining characteristics and potential configurations to suit your personal set-up. If you are unsure about what will suit your needs best, please get in touch and we can advise on the perfect spot for you.

Discover More

## The  Curators

There is always more to be uncovered in London: little known gardens, best-kept secret boutiques, historic hidden streets, and those places that ‘if you know, you know’. Therefore, we have assembled a collection of voices from our community to share their knowledge and a sense of surprise and discovery to get you under the skin of London.
