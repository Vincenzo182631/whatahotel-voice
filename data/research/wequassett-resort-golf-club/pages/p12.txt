## A Court You’ll Love

## Game-Set-Match

Wequassett’s three pristine Har-Tru tennis courts and three Har-Tru pickleball courts set the stage for everything from spirited competition to easygoing rallies.  Join one of our lively clinics or refine your game with a private lesson led by our director or talented staff professionals. Prefer a more leisurely pace? Reserve a court and enjoy social play, where the focus is as much on connection as it is on the game.

###### Expert Instruction. Exceptional Play

## Adult Clinics

Director Rob Parker and his team offer a well-rounded selection of summer clinics designed for players of all levels—from beginners to advanced competitors. These sessions provide a blend of skill development, strategy, and enjoyable social interaction.

Players can choose from various specialized clinics tailored to different skill levels and interests.  Whether you’re seeking skill development, great workouts, or a vibrant social scene, Wequassett’s adult tennis clinics provide something for every player.

###### Fueling Passion, Inspiring Play

## Junior Clinics

Wequassett’s junior tennis program offers a fun, skill-building experience for players of all ages and levels, designed to spark a lasting love for the game. Summer clinics and camps blend playful instruction with strong fundamentals—from early racquet skills and coordination to technique, footwork, and strategy. As players progress, more advanced training and match play provide the perfect balance of development, confidence, and on-court enjoyment.

###### Director of Racquet Sports

## Rob Parker

With over 25 years of experience in the tennis industry, Rob is a highly accomplished Director of Tennis and Head Professional, known for his leadership, expertise, and commitment to delivering exceptional guest and member experiences. Formerly serving as the Director of Tennis at the Chatham Bars Inn in Chatham, MA, Rob was responsible for all aspects of tennis programming. Since 2020, he has designed and implemented a diverse range of programs, including private instruction, clinics, social events, and tournaments for both adults and juniors, while also managing staff, budgeting, and inventory for the Inn’s tennis shop.

Prior to his role at CBI, Rob was the Director of Racquet Sports at the Conanicut Yacht Club in Jamestown, RI, from 2013 to 2019, where he was responsible for developing and overseeing both tennis and platform tennis programs. He also managed the club’s racquet sports staff, budgets, and marketing efforts, ensuring top-tier experience for the club’s membership.

#### Rob continued

Earlier in his career, Rob served as the Director of Tennis at Rolling Hills Country Club in Wilton, CT, from 1999 to 2006, where he led a comprehensive tennis program and was instrumental in the renovation of the club’s tennis facilities. Rob’s experience also includes serving as a Staff Professional at the Four Seasons Racquet Club in Wilton, CT, and as Head Coach of Varsity Boys’ Tennis at Weston High School, where his team achieved remarkable success with a 68-5 record and multiple state and conference championships.

Rob holds several prestigious certifications, including RSPA Elite Professional, PTR Certified Professional, PPTA Certified Professional, and USRSA Certified Stringer. He is also a valued member of the Wilson Advisory Staff. Known for his dedication to excellence, Rob consistently strives to create an environment where players of all levels can thrive, while upholding the highest standards of professional service.

###### Assistant Director of Racquet Sports

## Sandy Palmer

Sandy Palmer, a seasoned tennis professional, has recently relocated to Cape Cod. She also works as a Tennis Professional in the winter months at the Mid Cape Athletic Club.

Her previous role as the Junior Development Director at Woburn Racquet Club spanned 16 years. Additionally, she served as the Assistant Tennis Coach for the Wakefield High School Boys Varsity team for 6 years. Sandy’s entrepreneurial spirit is evident in her creation and management of the highly successful Wakefield Recreation Tennis Program, which she grew from the ground up and ran for 12 years.

#### Sandy continued

Sandy played varsity tennis at Franklin Pierce College, where she was captain for 3 out of her 4 years on the team. Her dedication to the sport is evident in her impressive competitive achievements, including being a 4.5 USTA League Player and Champion, as well as claiming victory at the USTA New England Clay Court Slam and the 2023 Harwich Pickleball Funfest. Her professional certifications include PTR and PPR, underscoring her expertise and passion for tennis and pickleball.

## Q&A

#### Q: How does on reserve a court or book a lesson or clinic?

A: Contact Rob Parker at rparker@wequassett.com or 508.430.3061

#### Q: Is rental and demo equipment available and is there a fee?

A: Yes—rental and demo equipment are available for your convenience at no charge. There is a $7 rental fee.  We also offer a selection of WILSON racquets for purchase along with professional restringing services to keep your equipment performing at its best.

#### Q: Are the clinics open to the public?

A: Yes—when availability allows. If a clinic has open spots 24-hours prior to its scheduled start time, members of the public are welcome to reserve a spot.  Fees apply.

#### Q: Do you offer a tennis membership?

A: Yes—tennis memberships are available. To learn more, please contact Jena Barker, the Director of Membership Services, for details and availability- jbarker@wequassett.com.
