## FAQs

#### Which restaurants offer outdoor dining?

Several of Wequassett’s dining venues offer outdoor seating, allowing guests to enjoy waterfront views and fresh Cape Cod air. Outdoor dining is available at Outer Bar, The Verandahs, LiBAYtion, and The Sill, with availability varying seasonally and weather permitting.

#### Does Wequassett offer fine dining on Cape Cod?

Twenty-eight Atlantic offers a Forbes Five-Star dining experience showcasing seasonal New England cuisine, exceptional service, and panoramic waterfront views overlooking Pleasant Bay.

#### Are reservations recommended?

Reservations are recommended for twenty-eight Atlantic, particularly during weekends, holidays, and the busy summer season. Other dining venues typically operate on a first-come, first-served basis, though availability may vary throughout the year.

#### Are the dining venues open year-round?

Select dining venues operate seasonally, so hours and availability may vary throughout the year.
