## LiBAYtion serves summery drinks and lite bites overlooking Pleasant Bay.

A fun gathering place next to the lapping waves. LiBAYion is a canopied bar with a magic touch by the beach, pool and boats. Live entertainment nightly during the summer months creates and fun and lively vibe. LiBAYtion is the best of Cape Cod’s beachy, summer fun.

###### Food & Beverage

## Magical Cocktails Paired With Seafood Favorites

Enjoy lobster rolls and chowder along with specially-crafted cocktails.

## A Lively Canopied Bar on the Boardwalk

Sit under the soft shade of a canopy with drink in hand, while catching the ocean breeze. The light and playful atmosphere lends a touch of whimsy to the bar and beach.

## Live Entertainment & Soulful Music Overlooking Pleasant Bay

Step off a barstool and land directly on the beach. Jazz, rock, and blues music can be heard from the live performance nearby, making for a convivial atmosphere on the beach.
