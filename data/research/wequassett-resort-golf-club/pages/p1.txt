## Welcoming Generations, Creating Lifelong Memories

Welcome to Wequassett – Cape Cod’s most enchanting waterfront resort. Nestled along the glimmering shores of Pleasant Bay, our double Five-Star retreat invites you to unwind in timeless elegance. For over a century, Wequassett has been more than a resort. It’s a place where memories are passed down like treasured heirlooms. What began as a quiet coastal retreat in Harwich has grown into one of Cape Cod’s most iconic destinations, welcoming generations of families, friends, and guests who return year after year.

## Coastal Dining at Wequassett

Savor every moment at Wequassett, home to Cape Cod’s most celebrated dining experiences. From Forbes Five-Star cuisine to comforting seasonal favorites, each setting blends coastal charm with culinary artistry, inviting you to gather, indulge, and reconnect over unforgettable meals on Pleasant Bay.

#### twenty-eight Atlantic

Step into twenty-eight Atlantic, Cape Cod’s only Forbes Five-Star restaurant, where each detail is a celebration of refinement. Housed in an 18th-century sea captain’s home overlooking Pleasant Bay, this iconic setting is as breathtaking as the cuisine. Executive Chef James Hackney crafts modern New England dishes that spotlight the region’s freshest ingredients – think sweet lobster, delicate halibut, and seasonal produce layered with creativity and care. As candlelight dances across the wood-beamed ceilings and salt air drifts in from the bay, every course becomes a moment to indulge.

## FAQs

#### What makes Wequassett Resort unique among Cape Cod resorts?

Wequassett Resort & Golf Club is a historic waterfront resort in Harwich overlooking Pleasant Bay. With private cottages and villas, award-winning dining, and experiences for every generation, it offers one of the most distinctive stays on Cape Cod.

#### Is Wequassett Resort a family-friendly resort?

Wequassett Resort has welcomed families for generations, offering seasonal children’s programs, waterfront adventures, pools, and spacious accommodations designed for multi-generational stays.

#### What dining options are available at Wequassett Resort?

Guests can enjoy several restaurants and bars at the resort, including the Forbes Five-Star restaurant twenty-eight Atlantic, along with relaxed waterfront dining and seasonal culinary experiences.

#### Where is Wequassett Resort located on Cape Cod?

Wequassett Resort is located in Harwich on Pleasant Bay, one of the most scenic waterfront settings on Cape Cod. Guests are close to beaches, boating excursions, and the charming villages of the Lower Cape.

#### What activities can guests enjoy at Wequassett Resort?

Guests can enjoy a wide range of experiences including boating and kayaking on Pleasant Bay, family activities, resort pools, seasonal programs, and curated adventures across Cape Cod.
