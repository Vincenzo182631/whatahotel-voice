## James Hackney

Executive Chef at Wequassett

Accolades: Coastal New England Rising Stars Award

Experience: 15 years

Executive Chef James Hackney has helmed the culinary team at Wequassett for the past decade. He is credited with elevating the status of twenty-eight Atlantic to its coveted Forbes Five-Star status. Originally from Leicestershire, England, Hackney was inspired to begin a career in the culinary world by working at his parents’ Garden Hotel, a quaint inn residing in the English countryside. Prior to Wequassett, Chef Hackney served as Chef de Cuisine at Boston’s legendary L’Espalier and other notable restaurants such as Blantyre in Lenox, Massachusetts and Charlie Palmer’s Aquaterra in Palm Beach, Florida.

## FAQs

#### Where is twenty-eight Atlantic located?

The restaurant is located within Wequassett Resort in Harwich, Massachusetts, on the shores of Pleasant Bay.

#### Do I need a reservation for twenty-eight Atlantic?

Reservations are required for twenty-eight Atlantic and are strongly encouraged to be made in advance, particularly during weekends, holidays, and the busy summer season on Cape Cod.

#### Is twenty-eight Atlantic open to non-resort guests?

Both resort guests and local visitors are welcome to dine at twenty-eight Atlantic with a reservation.

#### What kind of cuisine does the restaurant serve?

The menu highlights seasonal New England ingredients with a focus on fresh seafood, refined coastal flavors, and thoughtfully curated wine pairings.

#### Is there a dress code at twenty-eight Atlantic?

Guests are encouraged to dress in polished resort or smart casual attire to complement the dining experience.
