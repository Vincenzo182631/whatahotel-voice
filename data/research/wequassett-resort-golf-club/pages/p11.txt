## Dive Right In

## A Relaxing Retreat

Oversized vases filled with lush flowers and greenery surround the blue ripples that dance inside our beautiful pool and jacuzzi. To enter full relaxation, a boardwalk of rich, Brazilian woods leads you to the area’s **Sister Parish** cabanas, where you can experience breathtaking views of the sun shining down on the sea.

If you are looking for a quiet retreat, explore our Adult Lap Pool located by our tennis courts.  This area is set back and hidden for adults looking for an escape.

## Morning to Evening Magic

Enjoy some of the most magnificent sunrises over Pleasant Bay from our pool deck.  The cotton candy skies always surprise and delight our guests in the morning hours.

At night, as the stars begin to paint the sky, head over to the nearby fire pits for an evening filled with laughter and great company in an oversized circular setting. During the Summer season, enjoy live music nightly at the Main Pool, as well as Dive-In Movie nights and S’mores on the beach.

## Cabanas

Take your luxury stay to the next level with one of our private cabanas. This oversized, poolside retreat offers spectacular views of Pleasant Bay, and comes with added amenities that will help you get lost in the hours and wonder of the day.

## Lap Pool

Set in a secluded corner of the resort, our adults-only heated lap pool offers a quiet escape dedicated to relaxation and wellness. Just steps from the racquet courts, this peaceful retreat invites guests to enjoy a leisurely swim, soak in the serene surroundings, or unwind between activities in an atmosphere of complete tranquility.
