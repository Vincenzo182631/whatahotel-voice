## Pet-Friendly Stays on Cape Cod

Wequassett welcomes your furry family members with open arms. Dogs enjoy plush beds, gourmet treats, and concierge services including grooming and playtime. Pets must weigh under 90 lbs, be crated when alone, and are welcome in all outdoor spaces except pools and dining areas, but must be on a leash.

A nightly fee of $100 applies during spring, fall, and winter; $200 from Memorial Day to September 30. Please book directly to ensure the perfect pet-friendly accommodation.

## FAQs

#### What types of accommodations are available on Cape Cod at the resort?

Guests can choose from a range of accommodations, including suites, private cottages, villas, and homes. Each offers a different layout and level of space, designed for everything from short stays to extended visits.

#### Are there pet-friendly accommodations available?

Select accommodations are pet-friendly, allowing guests to travel with their dogs while enjoying the same level of comfort and access to the resort.

#### Which accommodations are best for longer stays on Cape Cod?

Private cottages, villas, and homes are well suited to longer stays, offering additional space, privacy, and residential-style features for a more relaxed experience.

#### Where is the resort located on Cape Cod?

The resort is located in Harwich along Pleasant Bay, offering a quieter coastal setting with easy access to beaches, local towns, and Cape Cod attractions.

#### Can I reserve accommodations for a wedding or group stay?

Yes, room blocks can be arranged for weddings and group celebrations, allowing guests to stay together across suites, cottages, and private homes. The team can assist with coordinating availability and guest arrangements.
