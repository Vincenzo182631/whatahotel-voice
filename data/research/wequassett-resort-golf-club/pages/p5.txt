## Inspired by the local legend that Henry David Thoreau, one of New England’s most celebrated writers, would sneak pies cooling on his neighbors’ windowsills, The Sill is Wequassett’s newest addition to its distinctive collection of food and beverage experiences.

Beginning October 15th Open Thursday-Sunday 6am till 12pm

Closed November & December

Phone: (508) 430 - 3000

Email: dining@wequassett.com

2173 MA-28, Harwich, MA 02645

## Discover more at Wequassett

Designed by Taniya Nayak of Taniya Nayak Design, the modern, inviting space features a soft palette of whites and grays paired with wood tones and soft greens inspired by the essence of Cape Cod. The artisanal grab-and-go bakery and espresso program will focus on quality craftsmanship and fresh ingredients, tucked into a quaint nook alongside the signature Forbes Five-Star twenty-eight Atlantic restaurant and the tavern-style, intimate Thoreau’s. The picturesque outlet serves breakfast pastries and beverages such as coffee, tea, draft cold brews, and juices.
