## The Cape Villa Conference Center

Cape Villa Conference Rooms offer the personalized service, acclaimed cuisine and exclusivity of our Five-Star Resort while providing a private and efficient option for meetings or conferences. The Cape Villa facility can accommodate groups of ten to 160, depending on room configuration and your preference of seating arrangement. Complete audiovisual services are available upon request.

## Waterfront

Completed in the spring of 2014, this stunning new space offers one of the most intimate and beautiful waterfront settings on Cape Cod for private gatherings and resort guests and visitors. The centerpiece is a one-of-a kind, temperature-controlled tent that can accommodate a wide variety of functions, from cocktail receptions to gala dinners or traditional clambakes, all with panoramic water views. Surrounding the tent is a network of terraces, verandahs, gardens and promenades each with its own character and sense of place. A grand lawn and lush seasonal plantings roll into Pleasant Bay providing a truly exquisite setting for a memorable event celebrated on Cape Cod. The allure of this new locale is the flexibility to accommodate individual guests, small parties and grand affairs.

## Food for Thought

Wequassett Resort can provide a fine dining experience for your group. Enjoy breakfast, breaks, lunch and dinner in exquisite settings.

Chef James Hackney’s innovative approach to New England cuisine celebrates native seafood and fresh ingredients. His catered menu options are many, ranging from New England clambakes and theme buffets to cocktail receptions with hot and cold hors d’oeuvres, chef’s brunches or formal plated dinners. Any menu can be customized to suit your group’s particular tastes or needs.

The resort’s conference planners and friendly staff will attend to your every whim, assisting in all aspects of planning, tailoring and organizing your meeting or conference with the perfect setting, menu and atmosphere for your specific needs.
