# InterContinental Amman (Jordan)Rooms & Suites

Experience a blend of comfort and elegance in our classic 34 sqm room, available with either one king-sized bed or twin beds. The room is thoughtfully appointed with a sleek flat-screen TV, convenient coffee and tea making facilities, and amenities that are refreshed daily for your comfort. Stay connected with complimentary wireless Internet access, allowing you to enjoy a seamless blend of relaxation and modern convenience.
