In the Diplomatic Area, and not far from Old City and the Citadel, the InterContinental Amman provides contemporary comfort in this most ancient of settings. After 50 years, the property feels fresh and vital. Large public areas with seating provide impromptu meeting space, rooms are large with attention to detail, particularly in the bathrooms.
