Your Stay, Thoughtfully Curated
With our compliments, enjoy a gourmet breakfast at Estero restaurant, a wellness ritual kit, locally crafted art, and your choice of cultural immersion or guided meditation—each element curated to enrich your stay.
Design Inspiration
Where Divine and Organic Intertwine
Within each suite, the essence of Riviera Nayarit unfolds through thoughtful design. Soft tones recall the Huichol’s sacred desert pilgrimage, while stone details evoke the strength of the mountains, and wood screens filter light like the jungle canopy. Crafted baskets and woven textiles meet sleek pottery and Italian furnishings, a marriage of past and present that showcases artistry at every turn. The result is a crown jewel retreat where tradition and modern elegance coexist, offering a sense of arrival to a place both rare and restorative.
