Signature Things To Do in Riviera Nayarit
Relax, Explore & Indulge: Activities & Resort Amenities
In Riviera Nayarit, the extraordinary landscape reveals one of the world’s most exquisite corners, where dense jungle, volcanic cliffs, golden beaches, and mangroves meet. Here, nature thrives, from giant manta rays to honeybees, in a setting where the land is left to unfold freely. Amid this rare estate lies Siari, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, where every experience is designed to deepen connection with place. Guests may follow hidden trails to waterfalls, snorkel in conservation waters, discover the flavors of Nayarit cuisine, or embrace cultural traditions told through dance and song.
Há Yeka Wellness Center
Há Yeka evokes water as origin—the sacred source from which all life flows. More than a physical element, water is a spiritual thread offering clarity, balance, and renewal for body, mind, and spirit. A Riviera Nayarit luxury spa rooted in nature.
