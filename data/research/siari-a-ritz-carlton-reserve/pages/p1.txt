A Luxury Resort in Riviera Nayarit, Mexico where Tranquility Reigns
Nestled between the Sierra Madre Occidental and the Pacific Ocean, Siari Riviera Nayarit, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, is an intimate sanctuary in the untouched landscapes of Riviera Nayarit. Inspired by the traditions of the Wixárika (Huichol) people, the Reserve celebrates Mexico's rich heritage through thoughtful design, regional cuisine and immersive wellness. The journey begins along a scenic highway from Puerto Vallarta International Airport. Winding through breathtaking jungle vistas before arriving at a secluded retreat where nature, privacy and genuine hospitality exist in perfect harmony.

Ocean-View Rooms and Luxury Suites in Nayarit, Mexico
Rooms & Suites
Like rows in a grand theater, where each seat reveals a new perspective, the rooms, suites, villas, and penthouses at Siari, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, frame nature’s breathtaking performance. Every accommodation reflects thoughtful artistry, from golden-sand beaches and turquoise waters to black-rock mountains wrapped in emerald jungle. Guests are invited to embrace and savor the view they most desire, each offering a rare connection to place.

Oceanview Residences
A Sanctuary in the Truest Sense
Overlooking the Pacific from a dramatic cliffside, Siari’s 34 residences embody one of the most exquisite corners of the Riviera Nayarit. Created by Bernardi + Peschard Arquitectura, each four- and five-bedroom turnkey home is designed to harmonize with its surroundings, where open-air living spaces invite the landscape inside. As part of this rare estate, residents enjoy privileged access to curated amenities , from an 18-hole championship golf club to a private beachfront , ensuring a lifestyle that is both exclusive and deeply connected to place.

At Siari, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, the essence of adventure unfolds in one of the world’s most exquisite corners. Here, jungle paths lead to cascading waterfalls, hidden coves become sanctuaries for reflection, and the ocean reveals its grandeur as whales rise from the depths. When evening arrives, a sky awash in stars provides the backdrop for cultural performances that honor the traditions of the land. Renewal Rooted in Nature

At Siari, this philosophy inspires the spa, where every space is designed as an extension of the natural world. What are the check-in and check-out times at Siari Riviera Nayarit, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve?
The check-in time at Siari Riviera Nayarit, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does Siari Riviera Nayarit, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at Siari Riviera Nayarit, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at Siari Riviera Nayarit, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve?
On-site parking is available at Siari Riviera Nayarit, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
