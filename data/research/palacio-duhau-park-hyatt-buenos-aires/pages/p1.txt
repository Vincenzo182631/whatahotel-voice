## Experience a Luxury Landmark with Elegant Suites in Buenos Aires

Experience unmatched luxury at Palacio Duhau - Park Hyatt Buenos Aires. Nestled in Recoleta, our two-building complex blends aristocratic elegance with modern design. Enjoy spacious rooms and suites featuring exquisite architecture and premium amenities. Delight in world-class dining crafted by talented chefs, admire our private art collection, and relax in the serene gardens or our luxurious spa and wellness center for an unforgettable hotel stay.

## Discover Park Hyatt Buenos Aires

Paseo de las Artes Duhau

Enhancing the hotel’s contemporary décor, a private fine art collection adds cultural depth to Palacio Duhau - Park Hyatt Buenos Aires. Discover Paseo de las Artes Duhau, an underground art gallery showcasing dynamic, temporary exhibitions and creative expressions.

Florería del Duhau

Our flower boutique enchants guests with vibrant colors and captivating fragrances. This charming space creates stunning floral arrangements for the hotel’s public and private areas and welcomes visitors from the community.

Duhau Gardens

Find tranquility in our hotel’s serene gardens surrounded by ancient trees. Relax by the cascading fountain, enjoy a good book or stroll among blooming flowers. Savor evening tea on Los Salones del Piano Nobile’s balcony or indulge in an alfresco dinner at Gioia Cocina Botánica for a truly peaceful retreat.

Ahín Wellness & Spa

Discover ultimate relaxation at Ahín Spa, one of the city’s most exclusive luxury wellness retreats. Experience soothing body massages, private meditation sessions and curated wellness treatments. With a short-course swimming pool, a high-end gym and an expert team, Ahín Spa offers an unparalleled escape for rejuvenation and well-being.

## Things To Do

Situated in Buenos Aires’ historic Recoleta neighborhood, this luxury hotel is close to the area’s most loved museums, restaurants, parks and cultural landmarks. See a live performance at Colón Theater, the city’s most revered opera house, or tour Casa Rosada, Argentina’s national government office where the country’s president lives. For family-friendly outings, take the kids to Planetario Galileo Galilei or to MALBA, the Museum of Latin American Art of Buenos Aires.
