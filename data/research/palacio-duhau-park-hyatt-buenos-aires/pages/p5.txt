### Who wouldn't want to live in a palace?

Palacio Duhau is an oasis in the city -- the buildings are gorgeous, the facilities superb, and the patios between the Palace and the Tower are a true haven. We stayed in a quiet, spacious, one-bedroom suite in the Palace and felt like royalty. The bed was dreamy, the bathroom vast and perfect, and housekeeping outstanding. Breakfast on the Piano Nobile was divine, and we especially enjoyed dinner at Duhau Restaurant, which offered a variety of selections and a superb, attentive, professional staff. Highly recommended.

### Wonderful anniversary trip

Our stay at Park Hyatt Duhau was exceptional. From the moment we arrived, the staff were consistently professional, warm, and attentive. Service was impeccable without ever feeling overdone, and every interaction reflected a genuine level of care and hospitality.

The property itself is beautiful. The historic architecture, impressive grounds, and beautifully maintained gardens create a setting that feels both elegant and relaxed. Our room was equally impressive, with thoughtful design, excellent amenities, and a level of comfort that made it easy to settle in and enjoy the stay.

The food was outstanding. Every meal we had was beautifully prepared, with excellent ingredients, thoughtful presentation, and great attention to detail. It was clear that the culinary experience is an important part of the hotel.

What stood out most was the consistency. The property is beautiful, the food is excellent, and the service is exceptional, but it is the combination of all three that makes Park Hyatt Duhau such a memorable hotel. We were celebrating our 25th wedding anniversary, and we are very grateful to the entire team for making our stay so enjoyable. We would absolutely return to Buenos Aires and stay at Duhau again.
