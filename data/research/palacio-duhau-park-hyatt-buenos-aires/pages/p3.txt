# Restaurants & Bars

Discover refined culinary experiences, recommended by the Michelin Guide, at our three restaurants in Recoleta. Plant-based Italian cuisine, signature Argentine dishes, curated wine and cheese tastings, and the timeless ritual of afternoon tea reflect a philosophy rooted in authenticity and elegance. Each menu is artfully curated by our chefs, using locally sourced, seasonal fresh ingredients. These moments of flavor are best enjoyed on the expansive terraces overlooking the serene Duhau Garden.

## Los Salones del Piano Nobile

With large windows, oak floors, original frescoes and boiserie, the restaurant offers a distinguished atmosphere that evokes historical charm. These magnificent salons and their adjoining terraces provide the ideal ambiance to enjoy à la carte breakfast, elegant snacks, international cuisine, and our most celebrated experience: the traditional afternoon tea.
