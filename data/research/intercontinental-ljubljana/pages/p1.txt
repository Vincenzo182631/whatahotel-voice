### Gym with a View

InterContinental Ljubljana has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

Indulge yourself in the luxurious atmosphere at our indoor swimming pool located on the 18th floor of the prestigious Hotel InterContinental Ljubljana and enjoy the stunning views of the Tivoli Park.

InterContinental Ljubljana has 1 restaurant.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Ljubljana?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Ljubljana?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Ljubljana.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Ljubljana?

Check-in at InterContinental Ljubljana is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Ljubljana offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Ljubljana offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Ljubljana from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Ljubljana is conveniently located near Ljubljana Jože Pučnik Airport (LJU), approximately 16.16 miles away (26 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Ljubljana?

InterContinental Ljubljana is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Tivoli Park - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- Ljubljana Old Town - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- Ljubljana castle - 0.53 miles away (0.85 kilometers)
- National gallery - 0.33 miles away (0.53 kilometers)
- Botanical garden - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Ljubljana puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
