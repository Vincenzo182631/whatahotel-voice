## B-Restaurant and Bar

Nestled on the top of the InterContinental Ljubljana, spans the entire 20th floor of the building and includes a restaurant, an outdoor terrace, and a bar. The restaurant and bar have a spectacular 360 degree view overlooking the old town Ljubljana with the castle on one side and the Alps on the other. The menu is elegant, yet recognizable and approachable, celebrating the vast range of Slovenian produce and products.

## B - Bar

Right next door to the sophisticated B-restaurant, our rooftop B-bar is the ideal place to relax after a day of meetings or sightseeing. Settle back on the outdoor terrace with an expertly mixed cocktail or a glass of wine to enjoy dazzling views of the Old Town, the castle and the surrounding Alps.
