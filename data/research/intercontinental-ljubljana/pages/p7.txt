### Excellent restaurant, nice pool, average room

4 days ago

Food in the restaurant is excellent for dinner. The beef fillet was delicious & very good wine. The pool & gym are good. The bedroom was disappointing (beds 2x double were very small, pillows very hard, duvet too hot). No mirror in the bedroom making it impossible to blow dry hair in front of a mirror. Whilst there were mirrors in the bathroom & dressing area there were no plug sockets. The room was very bare & boring. No effort to make the room feel special in any way. Distinctly average which isn’t what you want when staying 5\*. For 5\* you want something special.

No, I do not recommend this hotel.

### Modern Luxury with Thoughtful Service

25 days ago

We had a wonderful stay at the InterContinental Ljubljana. Through American Express, we were upgraded to a beautiful room with a lovely view over the city, which made the stay feel especially comfortable from the start.

The hotel is modern, stylish, and very well maintained. Our room felt updated and thoughtfully designed, with all the conveniences you would expect from a true luxury hotel. The entire property has a polished, contemporary feel without being cold or impersonal.

One of the highlights was the rooftop breakfast area. The setting was beautiful, the breakfast was very well prepared, and the presentation was excellent. The staff throughout the hotel were consistently warm, pleasant, and welcoming, which added to the experience.

The location is also excellent. You are very close to the heart of Ljubljana and the beautiful riverfront area, so it is easy to enjoy the historic center and then return to a more modern, full-service hotel.

If you are looking for a luxury hotel in Ljubljana with modern rooms, excellent service, and a convenient location near the old city, we would highly recommend the InterContinental. We would definitely stay here again.
