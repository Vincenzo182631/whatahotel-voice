## A _beautiful_, heartfelt retreat in the English countryside

Welcome to Fairmont Windsor Park, a beautiful, luxury hotel in Windsor that blends iconic heritage with modern elegance. Staying at Fairmont Windsor Park is a chance to enjoy the charms of grand, country house luxury as well as an opportunity to benefit from state-of-the-art facilities. It is a destination for those who cherish the stories of yesterday as well as the advancements of today.

### The perfect blend of iconic heritage and modern elegance

Located on the edge of historic Windsor Great Park, our luxury spa hotel is surrounded by 40 acres of open green space and a tranquil lake. Fairmont Windsor Park is the ultimate escape from the city within easy reach of Heathrow Airport and Central London.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Windsor Park?

Check-in is at 3pm and check-out is at 11am. The spa facilitities are not accessible before check-in or after check-out.

Is Fairmont Windsor Park pet-friendly?

Yes, we can accommodate dogs and other pets in a selection of our rooms subject to availability.

What is the closest airpot to Fairmont Windsor Park?

Approximately 7 miles east of Fairmont Windsor Park, London Heathrow Airport is 20 minutes by car, or a one-hour bus ride from Terminal 5 to Coopers Hill Lane bus stop with a 19-minute walk.

Does Fairmont Windsor Park have a swimming pool?

Yes, Fairmont Spa & Wellness has a 25 metre Indoor Lap Pool and Courtyard Vitality Pool. For children's swim times, please speak to a member of our Spa & Wellness team.
