Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou
The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou is located in the volcanic region of Haikou city on the exotic Hainan Island of China. The 190-room, 16-villa and 15-suite hotel enjoy year-round tropical sunshine for perfect days on the legendary 350-acre Blackstone Course that weaves its way around the world-renowned Mission Hills Golf Club. Built on top of a bedrock of ancient lava from extinct volcanoes, the dramatic landscape features rocky outcrops with striking variations of jungle vegetation, expansive lakes and wetlands which can be viewed from every room. The luxury golf resort features 3,500 sqm of banquet facilities for conferences and celebrations. Spaces include a 1,350 sqm Grand Ballroom, two Junior Ballrooms (275 sqm each), seven Meeting Rooms and one Boardroom. With a manicured Fairway Lawn and vast expanses of greenery, The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou wedding specialists can create a memorable experience to perfection.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou. Self parking is complimentary for guests.
Off-site parking is also available near The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou is Haikou Meilan International Airport (HAK). HAK is located approximately 16.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Haikou offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites.
