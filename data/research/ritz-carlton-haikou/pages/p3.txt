Dining Experiences
Curated Italian Set Dinner for One
Professional Chef Leo selects the finest ingredients to create one innovative tasting menus, which aim to enhance the evening and bring a memorable dining experience for guests. Monday to Thursday, 5:30 p.m. – 9:30 p.m. Venue: 2nd Floor, Terra.
Dim Sum Buffet Lunch
Our experienced Chinese Executive Chef presents a delicious Dim Sum menu for you to feast on this extensive list of Cantonese Classics, Sichuan Specialties and Local Treats. Daily, 11:30 a.m. – 2 p.m. Venue: Tin Lung Heen 1st floor.
Family Set Dinner
Partake in authentic Chinese culinary traditions with a family style dinner specially prepared by professional culinary team, bringing together even the most subtle of flavors to create a culinary masterpiece. Gather your family and friends around the table, and enjoy delicious dishes and warm and engaging conversations at Tin Lung Heen. Daily, 5:30 p.m. – 9:30 p.m. Venue: Tin Lung Heen 1st Floor
All You Can Eat - Asian Voyage
Flair chefs have selected the finest ingredients to create an Asian gourmet All-You-Can-Eat menu highlighting authentic Asian regional cuisine. Let us take you on a culinary journey. Including an unlimited flow of soft drinks, beer, wine, and selected cocktails. Wed-Thu, 5:30 PM-9:00 PM
Sunset Tea
Enjoy the delightful Asian Tapas set, and flavored mojito selection; let us offer you a unique late-afternoon tea experience with the perfect sunset view. As night falls, the menu shifts its focus to whiskey and other fine spirits. Wed-Sun, 5:30 PM-7:30 PM.
The Ritz-Carlton Afternoon Tea Experience
Take part in the time honored tradition of afternoon tea with hand crafted sweet and savory delights from our Culinary team paired with only the finest selection of TWG teas.
Daily, 2 p.m. – 5 p.m. Venue: The Ritz-Carlton Bar & Lounge, 3rd floor.
Meet in Haikou
Savor Chef-Driven, Cantonese Cuisine in Haikou
Tin Lung Heen sets the stage for an elegant interpretation of Cantonese cuisine. The restaurant’s chef-driven menu changes daily to highlight traditional Cantonese delicacies, while bountiful dim sum offerings are available daily for lunch. Enjoy a memorable meal within the serene dining room, or reserve one of five private and outdoor dining rooms for a special occasion, celebration or meeting.
