Frequently Asked Questions
How can I make a reservation?
Reservations can be made by calling +86 898 6683 6888 Ext. 1226 or by requesting a service via email
rc.hakrz.spa@ritzcarlton.com
. We encourage you to reserve treatments at least 48-hour in advance.
When should I arrive?
For your enhanced comfort and relaxation, we suggest that you arrive 30 minutes before your scheduled treatment. This will allow you to relax in our lounges, saunas and steam rooms.
Should I shave before a treatment?
Shaving is recommended but not necessary for men prior to a facial. If you do choose to shave prior to your facial treatment, be sure to do so at least 8 hours prior to your scheduled appointment. Shaving is not recommended prior to any body treatments.
Should I wax, shave, laser or use the sauna or steam room before a body scrub or peel?
To achieve the utmost satisfaction and comfort from your service, we ask that you wait at least 8 hours before and after the Spa service to wash, shave or use the sauna or steam room; and wait at least 24 hours before and after a laser treatment. In addition, we recommend that you limit your sun exposure before and after the facial or body peel treatment.
What should I wear during my treatment?
Most body treatments are enjoyed without clothing. However, please wear whatever is comfortable for you. During all treatments, the body is fully draped, except for the area being worked on. Robes and slippers are provided. Lockers are available for your personal items.
What about my jewelry and valuables?
If you are staying in the hotel, please leave valuable items in the safe in your guestroom. Otherwise, lockers are available for your personal items in the spa facility.
Who can guide me in choosing my treatments?
Our Spa reservation agent will help you plan the perfect Spa experience, including the best order in which to enjoy your treatments.
Featured Treatments
Golf Massage
A deep massage that is ideal for relieving and reducing muscle tension, joint pain and stiffness. A unique technique using golf balls to help release trigger points and roll out sore muscles.
Agar Wood and Coconut Oil Ritual
The local Hainan ingredients of coconut and Agar wood work together to invoke a deep sense of relaxation, balancing and restoring well-being to comfort the body and mind.
Aloe Vera Sun Soother Body Wrap
With a choice of a 2 hour or 3 hour spa treatments, 1 hour relaxation facility usage, and a spa cuisine meal in the private couple lounge, side by side deluxe spa treatment beds and a lavish vitality pool for two.
Muscle Recovery Massage
Recharge your body with this powerful massage designed to alleviate deep-seated tension and muscular stress. Using deep movements and stretching techniques concentrated on specific areas of concern to help relieve common discomforts and sports related tension.
Custom Facial
Purify, hydrate, brighten and smooth your complexion with this radiance-restoring facial that is tailored to your individual needs.
Thai Massage
Ancient and authentic Thai massage is a compete healing system combining acupressure massage, gentle stretching and assisted yoga postures to elongate muscles and release joint tension. Awakening the body and mind, emerge with a renewed sense of freedom and ease of movement.
