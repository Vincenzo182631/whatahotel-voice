### Where Luxury Excites The Senses

The Venetian Hotel is our reimagining of a bustling Italian piazza.

An extravagant and spirited escape from the every day, guests will experience the electric rhythm of life by surrounding themselves with stunning frescos and sculptures, authentic gondolas, a lively casino, and more food, drink, and entertainment than we could fit in this paragraph.

### Where Luxury Comes To Play

The Palazzo Hotel is our palace of Italian indulgence, a destination within a destination.

This is where elegance resides and time slows down so life’s pleasures can be fully appreciated. Tucked away from prying eyes, the most discerning of guests can let loose, trade ritual for spontaneity, and marvel at everything while worrying about nothing.
