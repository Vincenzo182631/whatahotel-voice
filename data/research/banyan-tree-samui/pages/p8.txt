# Destination Dining

## A bespoke dining experience overlooking our private bay

Treat your partner to a romantic dinner on the shores of Koh Samui, at our main pool backdropped by coconut trees and the stunning bay or at the Infinity Point where you get a picturesque view of our resort and the natural wonders of the island.

In each Destination Dining experience, we prepare a private table adorned with romantic touches and filled with dishes made with fresh and local ingredients. Enjoy an expansive view of the Gulf of Thailand and a star-filled sky above. This private dining experience in Samui is surely one you’ll remember forever.
