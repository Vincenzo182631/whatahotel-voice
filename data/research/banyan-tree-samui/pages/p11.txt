# Facilities

## Banyan Tree Samui

## A destination for any occasion

With a wide range of facilities for business and pleasure, our Samui retreat offers the best of resort lifestyle. Whatever your purpose of travel, we will do our best to make your experience absolutely perfect.

More than just your typical private beach resort, Banyan Tree Samui incorporates the traditions and culture of Thailand into every aspect, from our accommodations to our spa to the experiences we facilitate.

### Banyan Tree Spa

With a suite of 10 spa treatment pavilions, your search for blissful respite can be found in the peace of Banyan Tree Spa Samui. Surrender yourself to the intuitive touch of our professionally-trained therapists and rejuvenate your mind, body and soul in a setting of tropical serenity.

Open Daily: 10:00 am - 9:00 pm

### The Rainforest

Combining the best of European spa and hydrothermal therapy with the essence of Asian wellness, The Rainforest is a complete hydrotherapy experience for those seeking refreshment in a sanctuary of serenity.

Open Daily: 10:00 am - 9:00 pm\*

\*Last booking at 5:00 pm

### Banyan Tree Gallery

Take home the little touches that make Banyan Tree unique. Browse our collection of handicrafts, resort apparel and signature spa amenities to find the perfect item to relive your Banyan Tree experience at home.

Open Daily: 8:00 am - 6:00 pm

### Beach

Our private beach delivers an ideal setting to simply relax and unwind amid the tropical surroundings or to explore the tranquil waters of our private bay with our complimentary activities on offer including kayaking, stand-up paddle boarding, sailing or snorkeling in the house reef located just steps away from the shoreline.

Open Daily: 8:00 am - 6:00 pm

### Fitness Centre

The fitness centre includes treadmills, elliptical and cycling machines and an extensive range of free weights and machines facilitating strength training.

Open Daily: 6:30 am - 9:00 pm

### Yoga Centre

Complimentary yoga, stretching and sun salutation classes are available under the tutelage of our in-house instructor. For those looking for a more personalised session, private classes are available.

Open Daily: 8:00 am - 6:00 pm

### Main Pool and Kids' Pool

The 50-metre long pool provides a relaxing setting overlooking the beachfront lawn and our private bay, while our spacious Kids' Pool, adjacent to our Kids' Club and beachfront lawn, delivers a perfect space for children.

Open Daily: 8:00 am - 6:30 pm

### Kids Club

Our Kids' Club is available for children ages four to 12 and features a variety of activities for entertainment including arts and crafts as well as games.

Babysitting services for children ages four to 12 are also available with advance notice.

Open Daily: 8:30 am - 6:00 pm

### Library and Games Room

The Library has an extensive collection of books, novels, newspapers, magazines and games for your selection.

Open Daily: 7:00 am - 11:00 pm

### Events Centre

Our Events Centre presents ideal venue options for hosting themed parties, meetings, weddings and other events.
