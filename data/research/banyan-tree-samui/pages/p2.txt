## Open-air living spaces flow naturally into an outdoor terrace and a spacious infinity pool

Our villas feature private pools, and come in a range of configurations, sizes and views. You can wake up to the gorgeous coast of Koh Samui in our ocean view villas or slip into blissful repose in a private living space surrounded by lush tropical greenery.

Whichever private pool villa you choose, our Koh Samui hotel is your ticket to a total reset. Relax on the comfortable sun lounger, delight in the tropical charm of our private hillside or cool off in the infinity pool. Each villa is carefully laid out to maximise comfort for you and your travel companions.

##### Maximum Occupancy Policy

**One-bedroom Villas**

Please note that our one-bedroom villas have a maximum occupancy of 3 persons with an additional charge in the amount of THB 7,500 per person per night.

**Two-bedroom Villas**

Please note that our two-bedroom villas have a maximum occupancy of 5 persons with an additional charge in the amount of THB 7,500 per person per night.

##### Early Arrival / Late Departure Policy

• Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability.
• Late check-out from 12:00 pm to 6:00 pm will be charged 50% of the Best Available Rate of the day.
• Late check-out after 6:00 pm will be charged 100% of the Best Available Rate of the day.

##### Cancellation Policy

Free cancellation period is subject to each individual offer.
