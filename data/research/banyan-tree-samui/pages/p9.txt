# Spa & Wellbeing

## Banyan Tree Samui

## Retreat to the tender touch of our highly-skilled therapists

The very best of spa treatments and therapies developed in award-winning Banyan Tree tradition awaits you in this secret hideaway featuring The Rainforest, a holistic hydrotherapy wellness experience designed to heal and soothe. With a suite of 10 spa treatment pavilions, your search for blissful respite can be found in the peace of Banyan Tree Spa Samui. Surrender yourself to the intuitive touch of our professionally-trained therapists and rejuvenate your mind, body and soul in a setting of tropical serenity.
