# Royal Banyan Ocean Pool Villa

## Delight in the tropical charm of our private hillside

Set like a jewel on the loft of the resort offering unforgettable ocean views from the generously-proportioned living area. Refresh in the cool waters of your infinity pool or soak in the warmth of the adjacent jet pool. Nestle on the sofa of your private sala while watching dawn break over the horizon and, come evening; enjoy a candle-lit dinner on your open terrace beneath a moonlit sky.

Positioned to capture expansive views across the Gulf of Thailand, this Samui ocean pool villa offers a refined blend of privacy and island luxury. Generous indoor and outdoor living spaces create a comfortable setting for couples or families seeking a polished stay experience. The villa’s infinity pool appears to merge with the ocean beyond, while large terraces provide space for dining, relaxation, and quiet moments overlooking the coastline. Elegant furnishings, natural textures, and warm lighting contribute to a welcoming atmosphere that reflects the tropical surroundings without compromising privacy.
