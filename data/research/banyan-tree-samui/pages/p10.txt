## Signature Spa Journeys

Nurture your natural state of well. Guided by our 8 Pillars of Wellbeing, our treatments - led by rigorously trained therapists - draw on ancient wisdom to support modern needs. Whether through traditional Asian therapies or immersive rituals that reconnect you with nature, our approach restores equilibrium, one intuitive session at a time.

## Spa Treatments

### Sense of Place - Sukothai Heritage

Immerse yourself in Thai heritage with a healing blend of fresh coconut, jasmine rice, turmeric, and tamarind. This scrub and wrap, paired with traditional massage techniques, promotes deep relaxation and flexibility.

### Rainforest Indulgence

This journey begins in The Rainforest with a Rainwalk and Salt Scrub, followed by a Rasul mud treatment, Swiss Shower, sauna, and ice rub-down. Complete your wellness ritual in the Vitality Pool, and with a tailored massage.

### Royal Banyan

A beloved signature, Royal Banyan unites Eastern and Western massage techniques to ease muscle tension and boost circulation. A warm herbal pouch steeped in sesame oil enhances this deeply restorative experience.

## Wellbeing Activities

## Wellbeing Activities

### Yoga Asana

Build strength and inner calm through yoga asanas. This breath-aligned sequence of postures brings balance, improves focus, and fosters physical and mental clarity in a serene natural setting.

### Introduction to Thai Boxing

Explore the cultural tradition of Muay Thai with dynamic classes. Learn punches, kicks, and knee techniques in a full-body workout guided by our expert instructor.

### Aromatherapy Workshop

Craft a personalised aromatherapy spray using curated essential oils. Choose your favourite scents to create a meaningful souvenir that brings calm to your daily routine.

### Conscious Grounding

Reconnect with the Earth through a mindful barefoot walk. This guided sensory practice raises awareness and fosters a deeper connection with the natural surroundings.

### Breathwork

Learn conscious breath control techniques to reduce stress, improve focus, and support emotional balance. A calming practice ideal for any moment of pause.

### Sense of Nature Walk

Join a guided nature walk through our tropical grounds. Spot native plants and wildlife, and learn how our resort integrates seamlessly into this lush environment.

### Tai Chi

Experience the flowing movements of Tai Chi. This graceful Chinese practice enhances coordination, promotes balance, and cultivates inner peace and strength.

### Duo Stretch

Deepen flexibility with assisted partner stretches. Support each other through synchronized movements that release tension and build body awareness.

### Explore Our Bay

Enjoy an active session with complimentary kayaks or stand-up paddleboards. This fun coastal workout lets you explore the bay and soak in the ocean breeze.

### Ocean Breath

Enhance your day with guided beachside breathing. Inhale the sea air rich in negative ions to awaken your senses and restore inner calm.
