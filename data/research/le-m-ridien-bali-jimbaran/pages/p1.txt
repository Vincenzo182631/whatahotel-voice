# Welcome to Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran

## Discover fascinating Bali from our Jimbaran resort

Explore Bali in style at Le Meridien Bali Jimbaran. This hotel in Jimbaran, Bali, is situated near the beach. Our resort is a short drive away from Ngurah Rai International Airport and attractions like...See More Garuda Wisnu Kencana Culture Park. All rooms feature stylish furnishings, cozy sofas, mini-refrigerators, a coffee machine, spacious desks and complimentary WiFi. Sip your morning coffee or recharge on your balcony while enjoying the ocean, lagoon, or garden views. Suites offer extensive living areas, while the penthouse and villa boast private pools. Pamper yourself at the Explore Spa or unwind at the restaurant or rooftop bar. Take the plunge in our Jimbaran resort’s 1,300-square-meter outdoor lagoon pool or work out in the 24-hour gym center, while the kids are entertained by activities at Family Kids Club. Those planning events in Bali will appreciate our Jimbaran resort's versatile event spaces, including the remarkable Bale Banjar ballroom with its 360-degree glass windows and breathtaking ocean views.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran?

On-site self parking is available at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran is I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS). DPS is located approximately 8.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have an airport shuttle to I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS)?

Yes, Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran has an airport shuttle to I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS).

Does Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
