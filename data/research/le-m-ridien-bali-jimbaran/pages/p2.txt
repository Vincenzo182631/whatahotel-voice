### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran?

The largest occupancy room at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran is the Avant Garde Suite with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran?

Some rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at Le Méridien Bali Jimbaran have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
