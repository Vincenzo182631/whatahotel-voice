FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**What is Anantara Mui Ne, and what makes it distinctive?**

Anantara Mui Ne Resort is a five-star beachfront resort on Mui Ne beach in southern Vietnam, part of Minor Hotels' Anantara brand. It opened in 2006, one of the first five-star resorts on this beach, and has 89 rooms, suites and private pool villas set in mature fruit-tree gardens around a lotus pond, with a wide private beach. Its restaurants and private dining are open to visitors who are not staying, and service is assessed twice a year by LQA (Leading Quality Assurance), an external quality-assurance company, against its luxury hospitality standards. Read more about the resort.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne

**What makes breakfast at Anantara Mui Ne stand out?**

Breakfast at Anantara Mui Ne is made in-house: the breads, croissants, pastries, nut butters, yogurts and kombucha are all prepared in the resort's kitchens, and juices are pressed fresh to order, including vegetable-based recipes. Sparkling wine is served. Breakfast is laid out at L'Anmien overlooking the lotus pond, where guests feed the fish each morning, and runs from 6.30am to 10.30am. Discover breakfast at Anantara Mui Ne.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/restaurants

**Can guests book private beach dining and cocktail hours?**

Yes. Designer Dining by Anantara is a private dinner on the beach with a dedicated chef and butler, with menus including a caviar dinner. The Sand Lounge hosts private group gatherings and cocktail hours with Vietnamese canapés and local refreshments. Private barbecues are cooked in pool villas from meat, seafood or vegetarian menus, and the Wine Room holds private tastings and degustations. All are reserved in advance and open to guests who are not staying. Explore Designer Dining.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/restaurants/designer-dining-by-anantara

**Can I visit Anantara Mui Ne without staying overnight?**

Yes. Visitors who are not staying are welcome at L'Anmien, Cham Lounge and Flow Bar, and can book Designer Dining, the Sand Lounge, private group dining and Wine Room tastings. Reservations: +84 252 374 1888 or muine@anantara.com. See all restaurants and bars.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/restaurants

**Do you cater to special dietary requirements?**

Yes. Every menu marks dietary options, and the kitchens cater for vegetarian diets and dietary preferences. Every restaurant has a children's menu, and private barbecues come with a vegetarian menu. Please tell us about any allergy or intolerance when booking; the kitchens will adapt dishes where they can, but cannot guarantee an allergen-free kitchen.

**Is there a dress code?**

The dress code in the resort's restaurants and bars is smart casual. Swimwear is welcome at the pool and at Flow Bar.

**What spa and wellness experiences does Anantara Mui Ne offer?**

Anantara Spa is open daily from 9am to 7pm, with two treatment suites each with a steam room and jacuzzi, two deluxe treatment rooms and a beauty salon. Signature treatments are the Anantara Signature (60 minutes) and the Sand Compress Massage (90 minutes), which uses heated sand compresses inspired by the Mui Ne dunes. The Botanical Wellness Bath Ritual is prepared with fresh local ingredients for guests to use in their own room. A resident yoga teacher takes private and group classes on the beachfront or in an air-conditioned studio. Explore Anantara Spa.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/spa-wellness

**Which excursions and cultural experiences can guests arrange?**

The resort arranges trips to the red and white sand dunes by jeep, quad bike or sandboard, with hot-air balloon sunrises available. The Phan Thiet Explorer visits the Po Shanu Cham towers, the Phu Hai River by wooden boat, a dragon fruit farm and Duc Thanh School. Farmer for a Day covers local farms and the Fairy Stream. The concierge also arranges Ke Ga Lighthouse, sea fishing, Ta Ku Mountain by cable car and kitesurfing lessons. Explore the experiences.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/experiences

**Can Anantara Mui Ne arrange golf?**

Yes. Sea Links Golf & Country Club, an 18-hole course with ocean views, is about five minutes' drive from the resort. NovaWorld Phan Thiet Golf Club, also 18 holes, is about 30 km away. The concierge books tee times and transport and can combine golf with other trips or spa treatments, subject to course availability. Arrange golf through the concierge.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/leisure

**When is the best time to visit Mui Ne?**

Mui Ne is a year-round beach destination, with the dry season generally running from November to April, offering sunny weather and favourable conditions for outdoor activities. This period is also particularly popular for kitesurfing, thanks to the seasonal winds. May to October typically brings more rainfall, although sunny days are still common. Anantara Mui Ne Resort welcomes guests throughout the year, whether for a short coastal escape from Ho Chi Minh City or a longer holiday exploring the region's beaches, sand dunes and cultural attractions.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/experiences

**Is there nightlife and dining outside the resort?**

Yes. Guests can explore a variety of local seafood restaurants, cafés and bars along Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street and the surrounding Mui Ne area, just a short taxi ride from the resort. The area offers a relaxed coastal atmosphere with opportunities to enjoy local Vietnamese cuisine and evening entertainment. For a quieter evening, guests can unwind at the resort's Cham Lounge, serving signature cocktails and light bites until midnight. Our Concierge team is happy to recommend local dining venues and assist with transportation arrangements.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/experiences

**What is the design style of the resort?**

Anantara Mui Ne is built in classic Indochine style from natural materials: dark timber, granite and dark-varnished parquet, with ceiling fans, garden terraces and softly lit interiors. Pool villas have covered al fresco bathrooms and sunken dining pavilions, and the beachfront villas have four-poster beds. The resort was refurbished in 2024. See the rooms and villas.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/rooms

**Can Anantara Mui Ne host a beachfront wedding, including planning, catering and accommodation?**

Yes. The beach hosts ceremonies, cocktails and banquets for up to 250 guests, and L'Anmien Ballroom holds up to 170 for cocktails or 100 for a seated banquet. A dedicated wedding planner handles invitations, décor, catering, music and wedding-party transport, and the 89 rooms, suites and villas accommodate guests on the same property. Couples can arrive for the ceremony by traditional round coracle boat. Explore beach weddings.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/weddings

**Do you cater to special dietary requirements?**

Yes, we cater to special dietary requirements at Anantara Mui Ne Resort. Whether you have specific dietary needs due to allergies, intolerances, or preferences, our team is happy to accommodate them. Please inform the resort in advance or notify the restaurant staff when dining. We will work to ensure that your dietary needs are met with delicious and suitable options.

**Is Anantara Mui Ne a sustainable resort?**

Anantara Mui Ne Resort is Green Growth 2050 Gold-certified, a hotel sustainability certification assessed by independent third-party auditors. Drinking water is served in glass bottles rather than single-use plastic. All hot water is heated by solar energy. Wastewater is treated in the resort's own plant, and the grey water is reused to irrigate the gardens. Kitchen food waste is composted on site, and guest soap is recycled; the resort has also hosted soap-recycling sessions with guests. Lighting is LED throughout and water flow regulators are fitted across the property. See our sustainability practices.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/sustainability

**What private pool villas are available at Anantara Mui Ne?**

Anantara Mui Ne in southern Vietnam has 19 private pool villas in six categories, each with its own pool, a covered al fresco bathroom, a sunken dining pavilion and butler service. One-bedroom villas are 90 sqm: the One Bedroom Pool Villa in a private garden courtyard and the One Bedroom Beach Front Pool Villa with direct beach access. Two-bedroom villas are 128 sqm and sleep five adults and two children: the Family, Duplex, Residence and Beach Front Pool Villas. Compare the pool villas.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/rooms

**Is Anantara Mui Ne suitable for a honeymoon or anniversary?**

Yes. For couples, the One Bedroom Beach Front Pool Villa has a four-poster bed facing the sunrise, a private pool, direct beach access and butler service. Designer Dining serves a private dinner on the beach with a dedicated chef and butler, including a caviar menu, and couples' treatments take place in spa suites with their own steam room and jacuzzi. The resort arranges room decoration, private dining and treatments for honeymoons, anniversaries and proposals before arrival. Plan a honeymoon or anniversary.

https://www.anantara.com/en/weddings/honeymoons

**Is Anantara Mui Ne good for families with children?**

Yes. The kids' club is open seven days a week with a dedicated attendant; activities include building sandcastles, feeding the fish in the lotus pond and picking fruit in the gardens. The resort has a separate children's pool, 0.5 m deep. Spice Spoons for Kids is a hands-on cooking class that ends with a certificate, and every restaurant has a children's menu. The Two Bedroom Family Pool Villa has a second bedroom opening off the master, and Deluxe Rooms can be booked as connecting rooms, including sets of three. Children under 12 stay free in existing bedding; breakfast for ages 3 to 11 is charged. See family stays.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/leisure

**What beds are in the rooms, and can I add a cot or an extra bed?**

Every bedroom at Anantara Mui Ne features a Hollywood twin bed configuration, allowing the bed to be arranged as either one king-size bed or two twin beds. Baby cots are available free of charge upon request. An extra bed may be arranged, subject to room availability, occupancy limits, the resort's prevailing policy and applicable charges. Guests are kindly requested to arrange baby cots or extra beds in advance when making their reservation.

**What restaurants and bars does Anantara Mui Ne have, and are outside diners welcome?**

L'Anmien is the resort's all-day dining restaurant, overlooking the lagoon and ocean beyond. It serves a daily breakfast buffet, à la carte lunches featuring international favourites and Vietnamese specialities, and themed dinners until 10.00 pm. Cham Lounge offers signature cocktails, premium beverages and light bites from 11.00 am until midnight, while Flow Bar serves refreshing drinks and casual poolside dining. The resort also offers private dining experiences, including Designer Dining by Anantara. Non-resident guests are welcome to enquire about dining options, subject to availability. For reservations and further information, please call +84 252 374 1888. Explore all restaurants and bars.

https://www.anantara.com/en/mui-ne/restaurants
