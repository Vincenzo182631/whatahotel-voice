Anantara Hoi An Resort boasts an enviable setting, less than a kilometre from the historic quarter of Hoi An, in lush gardens fronting the Thu Bon River. Boutique luxury at our Hoi An resort unfolds through charming garden and river-view rooms and suites, including our newly unveiled riverfront suites, alongside unforgettable dining, blissful spa journeys, and sunset river cruises.

Stroll along the promenade past small cafes, and into the charming UNESCO World Heritage Site. Explore its alleyways, discovering shrines, modern art galleries and teahouses. Take a day to visit the My Son ruins, local artisan villages and nearby An Bang Beach.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Do you offer transfer service and how much does it cost?**

We offer transfer services at the resort, including from and to Danang International Airport. Please check out our transportation price list here. All transfers must be booked in advance.

**What is the cancellation policy of Anantara Hoi An Resort?**

The cancellation policies vary according to the rate that you have booked. Our Best Flexible Rate allows free cancellation up to 7 days prior to arrival.

**What is the children’s policy at Anantara Hoi An Resort?**

Children aged 3 years and below sharing a room with at least one adult stay for free. A surcharge of USD 35++ per day will be applied for children from 4 to 11 years’ old including an extra bed and breakfast.

**What are the dining options available at Anantara Hoi An Resort?**

Anantara Hoi An Resort offers a variety of dining venues: Hoi An Riverside restaurant, Art Space bar and restaurant, Lanterns restaurant and Reflections pool bar. You can also experience in-room dining or our signature Designer Dining by Anantara experiences.

**Is early check-in or late check-out available?**

Standard check-in time is 14:00 and check-out time is 12:00 at Anantara Hoi An Resort. Early check-in and late check-out options are available on request and subject to availability. Additional fees may apply.

**How far is Anantara Hoi An Resort from the Ancient Town?**

Anantara Hoi An Resort is less than a kilometre’s walk from the historic quarter of Hoi An Ancient Town.

**How far is Anantara Hoi An Resort from the beach?**

The nearest beach is An Bang beach which is around 5 km from our resort. Complimentary bicycles are available for guest use.

**Is breakfast included for guests at the resort?**

Yes, you can enjoy complimentary daily breakfast during your stay at Anantara Hoi An Resort.

**What is Anantara Hoi An Resort’s pet policy?**

Pets are not allowed inside Anantara Hoi An Resort.

**Does Anantara Hoi An Resort have any combination packages with The Vietage?**

We offer an exclusive package for guests with two one-way train tickets aboard The Vietage and two nights at Anantara Hoi An Resort at a special rate. Please contact our team for more information.
