Set on a unique peninsula where the Kalu Ganga River lagoon meets the Indian Ocean, Anantara Kalutara Resort is one of the most distinctive luxury resorts in Sri Lanka, ideally located in Kalutara within easy reach of Colombo, just 90 minutes from the Bandaranaike International Airport Colombo and a short drive from Bentota. Inspired by the design philosophy of Geoffrey Bawa, the resort blends Sri Lankan architectural heritage with contemporary elegance. Surrounded by lush tropical gardens, tranquil lagoons, and the natural beauty of the coastline, it offers a refined sense of place rooted in the island’s culture.

Elegant rooms, suites, and private pool villas combine comfort, privacy, and understated luxury. Complemented by exceptional dining, Ayurvedic wellness experiences, and curated cultural journeys, the resort is suited to couples, families, and travellers seeking a tranquil retreat. Whether beginning or ending a journey through Sri Lanka, Anantara Kalutara offers a refined luxury escape where architecture, nature, and hospitality come together effortlessly.

Frequently Asked Questions

**Where is Anantara Kalutara Resort located?**

The resort is situated in the Western Province of Sri Lanka, approximately 45 kilometers from Colombo and 90 kilometers from Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA)

**How can I reach the resort from the airport?**

The resort is approximately a 1.5-hour drive via the Southern Express Highway from Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Colombo. For those preferring a more scenic route, a helicopter ride offers a swift 25-minute journey from BIA.

**Does Anantara Kalutara Resort offer airport transfers?**

Yes, the resort provides airport transfers (additional charges apply) with a required 48-hour advance booking. Guests can choose luxury chauffeur services or helicopter transfers.

**What leisure facilities are available at the resort?**

The resort features two expansive outdoor swimming pools, including a dedicated adults-only pool, a fully equipped modern gym, and an activity tower offering zip-lining, rock climbing, and abseiling. The renowned Anantara Spa provides Ayurvedic treatments and holistic wellness programs. Guests can also enjoy various water sports and explore 'Koratuwa,' the resort’s model farm garden.

**Are there dining options available on-site?**

Yes, Anantara Kalutara Resort offers a variety of dining experiences, including international all-day dining at Olu, Italian coastal cuisine at Acquolina, a fusion of Indian, Thai, and Chinese dishes at Spice Traders, and a sports-themed bar at Upper Deck.

**What are the check-in and check-out times?**

Check-in begins at 2:00 PM, and check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available at an additional cost.

**What makes Anantara Kalutara Resort unique?**

Anantara Kalutara Resort is uniquely located on a peninsula between the Kalu Ganga lagoon and the Indian Ocean, offering guests a rare combination of lagoon tranquility and oceanfront luxury. The resort’s Geoffrey Bawa-inspired architecture, tropical gardens and strong connection to nature create a distinctive sense of place on Sri Lanka’s west coast.

**Is the resort family-friendly?**

Yes, Anantara Kalutara Resort is designed with families in mind, featuring family accommodations, a dedicated kids' and teens' club, children's menus at restaurants, and babysitting services (available for an additional charge with 24-hour advance booking).

**Do you have accessible rooms for guests with disabilities?**

Yes, we offer accessible rooms designed for guests with disabilities, featuring:

•Wider doorways

•Roll-in showers

•Grab bars

•Emergency panic button access

•Other amenities to ensure a comfortable stay

Please contact us in advance for availability.

**Are pets allowed at Anantara Kalutara Resort?**

Yes, Cats and Dogs allowed at Anantara Kalutara Resort with an advance booking.

**Is Anantara Kalutara Resort on the beach or river?**

Anantara Kalutara Resort is positioned between both river and ocean. Guests enjoy access to the Indian Ocean alongside peaceful lagoon and river views created by the Kalu Ganga River surrounding the resort’s peninsula setting.

**Is Kalutara good for a luxury holiday in Sri Lanka?**

Yes. Kalutara offers a quieter and more authentic alternative to some of Sri Lanka’s busier beach destinations while remaining easily accessible from Colombo and Bentota. Anantara Kalutara Resort combines luxury accommodation, wellness, dining and cultural experiences within a serene tropical setting.

**Is Anantara Kalutara Resort designed by Geoffrey Bawa?**

Geoffrey Bawa began the design of Anantara Kalutara Resort in 1995 as one of his final architectural projects. Following his illness and passing, the development remained dormant before being completed by his protégé Channa Daswatte, preserving the spirit and philosophy of Bawa’s original Tropical Modernist vision.

**Is Anantara Kalutara Resort suitable for architecture lovers?**

Yes. Anantara Kalutara Resort is considered one of Sri Lanka’s most distinctive Bawa-inspired luxury resorts and is ideal for travellers interested in Geoffrey Bawa, Tropical Modernism and Sri Lankan architectural heritage.

**Is Anantara Kalutara Resort close to Bentota?**

Yes. Anantara Kalutara Resort is located along Sri Lanka’s west coast near Bentota, making it an ideal base for exploring nearby beaches, cultural sites and river experiences while enjoying a more secluded luxury resort environment.

**What is the best luxury resort near Colombo?**

Anantara Kalutara Resort is one of Sri Lanka’s leading luxury resorts near Colombo, offering a unique peninsula setting between the Kalu Ganga River and the Indian Ocean. Combining Geoffrey Bawa-inspired architecture, tropical gardens, wellness experiences and contemporary luxury, the resort provides an ideal escape within convenient reach of the city and airport.

**Is Anantara Kalutara Resort suitable for a weekend escape?**

Absolutely. Anantara Kalutara Resort is ideal for luxury weekend escapes from Colombo, offering peaceful river and ocean surroundings, spa experiences, dining, wellness and relaxation without requiring long travel times.

**Is Anantara Kalutara Resort a good first or last stop in Sri Lanka?**

Yes. The resort is perfectly positioned for travellers beginning or concluding a Sri Lanka itinerary. Its proximity to the airport allows guests to enjoy a luxury beach and lagoon experience without travelling deep into the island immediately after arrival or before departure.

**Are there villas suitable for families at Anantara Kalutara Resort?**

Yes. The resort offers spacious private pool villas, suites and connecting room options ideal for families and multi-generational travellers seeking additional space, comfort and privacy.
