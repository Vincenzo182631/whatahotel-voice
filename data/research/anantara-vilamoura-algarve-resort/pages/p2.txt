With only 25 suites spread over seven storeys, Anantara Chiang Mai Serviced Suites offers an exclusive base from which to take in the sights of the Lanna capital. Wander the ancient ruins and glittering temples for which the old city is known. Venture further, out into the cool mountains to visit artisan villages, hill tribes and tea plantations. Or float down the river in luxurious style with Anantara’s JAO Ping River Cruise. The suites themselves are spacious and fully equipped, ideal for business travellers, families, including those with pets, and long stays. Each consists of ensuite bedrooms, a separate living room, a kitchen and laundry. Guests benefit from complimentary minibar, daily housekeeping, as well as secure parking downstairs and a 24-hour service.

They also enjoy, in additional to the rooftop pool, full access to the facilities of Anantara Chiang Mai Resort, located just across the road and reached via a covered walkway. These include daily wellness activities, traditional Thai healing and IV drips at Anantara Spa, and immersions in the local culture with muay Thai and Spice Spoons cooking classes.

The dining, meanwhile, is unrivalled, centred around the magnificent 1921 House, formerly the British consulate and built over a century ago. Here, afternoon tea is served on the verandah each day, drinks in the secret service-inspired bar, and dinner in the sophisticated steakhouse and grill. The riverfront Bodhi Terrace, meanwhile, is the setting for leisurely breakfasts, as well as for lunch and dinner featuring regional Thai cuisines.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**What are the check-in and check-out times at Anantara Chiang Mai Serviced Suites?**

Check-in at Anantara Chiang Mai Serviced Suites is from 3.00 pm, and check-out is until 12.00 noon.

**How far is Anantara Chiang Mai Serviced Suites from the airport?**

Anantara Chiang Mai Serviced Suites is 4.5 km from Chiang Mai international airport. The resort is located on the bank of the Ping River which is one of the two main tributaries of the Chao Phraya River.

**Do we offer a shuttle bus to/from an airport?**

Shuttle bus is not available, however, we can arrange airport transfer for guests. Charges apply.

**Do we have a Kids Club?**

We do not have a Kids Club but we do offer activities available for children.
