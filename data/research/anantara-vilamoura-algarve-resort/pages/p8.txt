Among the finest beach resorts in Vietnam, surrounded by mountain on three sides and overlooking the Bay of Quy Nhon, Anantara Quy Nhon Villas offers beachfront basking and adventurous discoveries. Only 25 luxury pool villas dot seven acres of lush landscaping fronting a private cove.

Laze by the pool snacking on gourmet street eats served by your Villa Host. Luxuriate with a synchronised four-hand massage at Anantara Spa. Explore surrounding ruins, fisherman’s villages and uninhabited islands just off the coast.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**How much is the transfer to and from Anantara Quy Nhon Villas?**

We do offer chauffeured service at the resort. Click here to find out our transportation prices.

**Do all the villas at Anantara Quy Nhon Villas have ocean views?**

Guests can enjoy spectacular ocean views from all villas at Anantara Quy Nhon Villas.

**What is the cancellation policy of Anantara Quy Nhon Villas?**

The cancellation policies at Anantara Quy Nhon Villas vary according to the rate that you have booked. Our Best Flexible Rate allows free cancellation up to 7 days prior to arrival.

**What is the children’s policy at Anantara Quy Nhon Villas?**

Children aged 3 years and below sharing a room with at least one adult stay for free. A surcharge of USD 65++ per day will be applied for children from 4 to 11 years’ old including an extra bed and breakfast.

**How many dining options are there at Anantara Quy Nhon Villas?**

We offer a variety of world-class dining options at Anantara Quy Nhon Villas, including Sea.Fire.Salt. restaurant, the pool bar lounge and a variety of in-villa dining choices. Guests can also enjoy our signature Designer Dining By Anantara experiences on the beach or on your private villa deck.

**How far is Anantara Quy Nhon Villas from the city centre?**

Anantara Quy Nhon Villas is located in a secluded bay, approximately 12 km from Quy Nhon city centre.

**Is breakfast included during my stay at Anantara Quy Nhon Villas?**

Yes, you can enjoy complimentary daily breakfast during your stay at Anantara Quy Nhon Villas.

**What is the pet policy at Anantara Quy Nhon Villas?**

Pets are not allowed inside Anantara Quy Nhon Villas.

**What is the surcharge for a third person at Anantara Quy Nhon Villas?**

An additional charge of USD 100++ per day will be applied for a third adult including an extra bed and breakfast.

**Does Anantara Quy Nhon Villas have any combination packages with The Vietage?**

We offer a package for guests with two one-way train tickets aboard The Vietage and two nights at Anantara Quy Nhon Villasat a special rate. Please contact our team for more information.
