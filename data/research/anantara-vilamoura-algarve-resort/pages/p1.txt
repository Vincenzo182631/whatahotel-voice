Anantara Mui Ne Resort is a five-star beachfront resort on Mui Ne beach in Lam Dong Province, southern Vietnam, 200 km and about two and a half hours by road from Ho Chi Minh City. It was the first five-star resort to open on this beach and has 89 rooms, suites and private pool villas set in mature tropical gardens around a lotus pond, with a wide private beach.

The resort has 19 private pool villas in six categories, each with its own pool, a covered al fresco bathroom, a sunken dining pavilion and butler service.
