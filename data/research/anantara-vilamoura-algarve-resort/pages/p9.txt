Located on the banks of the Mae Ping River, Anantara Chiang Mai Resort opens doors to a world of discovery. Wander the ancient Lanna ruins and glittering temples for which the old city is known. Venture further, out into the cool mountains to visit artisan villages, hill tribes and tea plantations. Or float down the river in luxurious style with Anantara’s JAO Ping River Cruise.

Back at the resort, tranquil living awaits, with daily wellness activities, traditional Thai healing and IV drips at Anantara Spa, and immersions in the local culture with muay Thai and Spice Spoons cooking classes.

The dining, meanwhile, is unrivalled, centred around the magnificent 1921 House, formerly the British consulate and built over a century ago. Here, afternoon tea is served on the verandah each day, drinks in the secret service-inspired bar, and dinner in the sophisticated steakhouse and grill. Begin your day with international selections and live cooking stations for breakfast at our riverside restaurant, Bodhi Terrace. Ranked in the top 5 on TripAdvisor, guests can also indulge in authentic Thai cuisine from both the northern and southern regions of Thailand during lunch and dinner.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**What are the check-in and check-out times at Anantara Chiang Mai resort?**

Check-in at Anantara Chiang Mai resort is from 3.00 pm, and check-out is until 12.00 noon.

**How far is Anantara Chiang Mai resort from the airport?**

Anantara Chiang Mai resort is 4.5 km from Chiang Mai international airport. The resort is located on the bank of the Ping River which is one of the two main tributaries of the Chao Phraya River.

**Do we offer a shuttle bus to/from an airport?**

Shuttle bus is not available, however, we can arrange airport transfer for guests. Charges apply.

**Do we have any connecting rooms?**

We do not have connecting rooms but guests may request adjoining rooms.

**Can an extra bed be requested for Deluxe Room categories?**

We do not provide an extra bed in our Deluxe Room categories due to safety regulations. Extra beds can be requested in Kasara Suites.

**Do we have a Kids Club?**

We do not have a Kids Club but we do offer activities available for children.

**Can kids enjoy the spa?**

Kids above age 12 may enjoy the spa, under 12 only with parental supervision.

**Can pregnant guests go to the spa?**

We do not recommend the spa for pregnant guests.

**Sauna/Steam/Jacuzzi available?**

Steam/Sauna available (charges apply), no Jacuzzi.

**Do we have a salon?**

Our spa offers nail service, hair removal (waxing), but no hair service.
