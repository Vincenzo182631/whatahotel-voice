Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Layan Life by Anantara**

Layan Life by Anantara is a thoughtfully curated wellness and lifestyle experience set within the serene grounds of Anantara Layan Phuket Resort. Rooted in mindfulness and natural beauty, it offers a holistic journey through rejuvenating spa rituals, invigorating fitness sessions, nourishing cuisine and immersive experiences in nature.

Whether you seek balance, restoration or simply a moment of calm, Layan Life invites you to reconnect gently and meaningfully with yourself and the world around you.

**How far is Anantara Layan Phuket Resort from the airport?**

Just 30 minutes away, Anantara Layan Phuket is a luxury hotel close to the Phuket airport.

**What are the check-in and check-out times?**

Check-in at 3:00 pm, and check-out is until 12:00 noon.

**Is Anantara Layan Phuket Resort pet-friendly?**

We are pleased to welcome your four-legged companions to our luxury resort in Phuket. As a pet-friendly destination, we offer a warm and comfortable stay for both you and your cherished pets, ensuring every member of the family feels right at home.

**How far is Anantara Layan Phuket Resort from Phuket town?**

Anantara Layan is a luxury hotel near Phuket town, approximately 45 minutes away.

**Is Anantara Layan Phuket Resort a smoke-free hotel?**

Smoking inside rooms and public areas including restaurants are prohibited at this luxury hotel in Phuket. Smoking is only allowed at the designated area.

**Is Anantara Layan Phuket one of the best luxury resorts in Phuket?**

Yes. Anantara Layan Phuket Resort is recognised as one of the best luxury resorts in Phuket, located on the tranquil shores of Layan Beach on the island’s northwest coast. The resort offers a collection of elegant suites and private pool villas, complemented by personalised service, award-winning dining and world-class wellness experiences, creating a peaceful yet luxurious escape in Phuket.

**Is Anantara Layan Phuket one of the best wellness resorts or wellness retreats in Phuket?**

Yes. The resort is home to Layan Life by Anantara, a pioneering integrated wellness centre combining modern medical science with holistic healing traditions. Its personalised programmes support longevity, biohacking, fitness, stress recovery and holistic wellbeing, making it one of the best wellness resorts and wellness retreats in Phuket.

**How many restaurants do you have at Anantara Layan Phuket Resort?**

We have 5 restaurants, including Breeze, Zuma, Dara Cuisine Phuket, Rooftop and Age, some of the leading dining destinations in Phuket.

**What are longevity and biohacking and are they available at Anantara Layan Phuket Resort?**

Longevity focuses on enhancing long-term health, vitality and quality of life, while biohacking uses advanced diagnostics, technology and personalised lifestyle strategies to optimise the body’s performance and wellbeing. At Anantara Layan Phuket Resort, guests can experience tailored longevity and biohacking programmes at Layan Life by Anantara, where modern health and wellness approaches are combined with holistic therapies and personalized guidance.

**Why is Anantara Layan Phuket considered one of the most romantic resorts in Phuket?**

The resort offers a secluded beachfront setting, luxurious private pool villas and bespoke experiences designed for couples. Romantic highlights include candlelit beach dinners, sunset cruises, couple’s spa journeys and private stargazing experiences. These curated moments make the resort a popular choice for honeymoons, anniversaries and romantic escapes in Phuket.
