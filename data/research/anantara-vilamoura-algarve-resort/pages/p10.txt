Situated along 400 metres of private shoreline with sweeping views of the Arabian Gulf, Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort invites you to unwind in Thai hospitality with a choice of over water villas, beachfront sanctuaries or swim-out lagoon rooms.

Revel in distinctive dining at six acclaimed restaurants, including the piquant dishes at Mekong. Rebalance in the sanctuary of Anantara Spa with its remarkable hammam facilities for both women and men.

Days unfold under the sun, whether at the main pool or exploring the coast by paddleboard or kayak. Younger guests can join in creative play at the Tuk-Tuk Kids’ Club, while teens find their vibe at the Chill Teens’ Club. A complimentary shuttle connects you to nearby malls and attractions, taking your discovery further.

FAQs

**What makes Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort a luxury resort stay on Palm Jumeirah?**

A luxury stay combines beachfront accommodation, private pool villas, lagoon access and overwater villas with diverse dining, wellness and leisure experiences, creating a tranquil escape on Palm Jumeirah.

**Is Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort a family-friendly resort?**

Yes. Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort is ideal for family holidays, offering spacious family rooms, apartments and villas, swimming pools, beachfront relaxation and family-friendly experiences, with convenient access to Dubai’s popular attractions.

**Does Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort have overwater villas?**

Yes. We offer distinctive overwater villas with spacious interiors, private outdoor areas and Arabian Gulf views, providing greater privacy, comfort and exclusivity for couples and guests seeking a memorable stay.

**Does Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort offer villas with private pools?**

Yes. Our private pool villas provide additional privacy and outdoor space, making them well suited to couples, families and small groups seeking an exclusive stay while retaining access to the resort’s facilities.

**Why is Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort a romantic choice for a honeymoon in Dubai?**

Guests have direct access to a private beachfront on Palm Jumeirah, with Arabian Gulf views, beach and pool relaxation, and a secluded resort atmosphere while remaining close to Dubai’s attractions.

**Does Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort offer quiet or adults-focused areas?**

Yes. While Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort is a family-friendly destination, it also offers tranquil escapes designed especially for adults. The Lotus Lagoon View and Lotus Lagoon Access Rooms are centred around a dedicated adults-only lagoon for a more peaceful stay. Guests seeking greater privacy can also choose from the resort’s Beach Pool Villas or Dubai’s only Over Water Villas, offering a more secluded resort experience.

**What dining experiences are available at Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort?**

Guests can enjoy varied dining experiences, including international cuisine, Thai flavours, beachfront dining and relaxed resort settings. Our restaurants and bars offer options for romantic dinners, family meals and casual dining throughout the day.

**What wellness and spa experiences are available?**

Guests can unwind at Anantara Spa with treatments and wellness experiences designed for relaxation and wellbeing. Our beachfront setting, swimming facilities and peaceful atmosphere complement the spa, creating a restorative retreat on Palm Jumeirah.

**Why is the resort a good choice for couples and families?**

Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort suits both couples and families, offering private villas, beachfront dining and spa experiences alongside spacious accommodation, pools, beach access and leisure facilities for a well-rounded Palm Jumeirah stay.

**What can guests expect from a premium vacation at Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort?**

A premium vacation combines luxury accommodation with private villas, overwater stays, beachfront relaxation, swimming pools, dining and wellness facilities. Our Palm Jumeirah location provides a peaceful seaside setting while keeping Dubai’s attractions accessible.

**Where is Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort located?**

Anantara the Palm Dubai Resort is located on the East Crescent of Palm Jumeirah Dubai.

**How many restaurants are available in the resort?**

Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort consists of seven bars and restaurants. Mekong, Bushman's Restaurant & Bar, Mai Bar, Revo Café, The Beach House, The Lotus Lounge and Crescendo.

**Are the Residences and Apartments located in the resort?**

The Residences and Apartments are located within the same vicinity as the resort and are just a short walk from the main rooms and facilities.

**Does Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort offer airport transfer services?**

Yes, Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort offers airport transfer services, including both pick-up and drop-off options, with a selection of luxury vehicles available.

**How far is Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort from the airport?**

Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort is approximately 45- 50 minutes away from Dubai International Airport.

**What is the check in and check out time at Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort?**

Check-in begins at 3:00 pm for the resort and 4:00 pm for the residences. Check-out is at 12:00 pm for the resort and 11:00 am for the residences.

**Does Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort offer a free shuttle bus from airport?**

No, Anantara the Palm Dubai Resort does not offer a free shuttle bus from the airport.

**Is there free parking on site?**

Yes, Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort offers free parking space for all in house guests and those who have a confirmed restaurant reservation.

**Are the rooms at Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort smoking or non-smoking?**

Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort is a non-smoking resort; therefore, all rooms, villas and apartments are strictly non-smoking.

**Does Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort allow pets?**

Anantara The Palm Dubai Resort is not a pet friendly resort and pets are not allowed in the resort.
