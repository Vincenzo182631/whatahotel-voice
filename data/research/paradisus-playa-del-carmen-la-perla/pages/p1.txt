## An exotic treasure in the heart of the Riviera Maya

Welcome to a little corner of paradise. Located in an incredible mangrove forest very near huge beaches with fine, white sand, this all-inclusive luxury resort guarantees a truly unique stay. Rooms you will love with a sophisticated design and meticulous attention to even the tiniest details. Fun under the Caribbean sun in the pools and water parks for the whole family. A world of flavors in every dish. And a selection of activities that will enrich your spirit and introduce you to the splendor of Mexican culture.

Adventure, nature, relaxation... Paradisus Playa del Carmen opens the doors to everything you would like to experience during your stay in Riviera Maya. Beaches, coral reefs, crystal-clear cenote pools and inspiring Mayan ruins await you in nearby Playa del Carmen. Discover the exclusive services and benefits for Family Concierge guests designed for the whole family. Amazing and varied dining experiences in its restaurants and bars. Find the time to improve both your body and soul in the YHI Spa, a temple of well-being. Kids will also enjoy endless adventures and take part in exciting games and experiments that stimulate their curiosity and imagination.

Emma Uppal

10/17/26

5/5

> “We had a fantastic family vacation at Paradisus Playa Del Carmen. This was our first all-inclusive and the staff made it a special experience. The rooms were comfortable, well-stocked with snacks and abundant bottled waters (necessary!), cool temperature at night, extraordinarily comfortable beds, blackout curtains, and a jacuzzi on the porch. We paid extra for the family concierge service which provided us access to another lovely pool and a good restaurant where we ate breakfast every morning. They also left gifts and surprises for our kids (3 and 6) several times throughout the stay. My kids loved both the family pool with the pirate ship and the waterpark (seeing them conquer their fears was a highlight for us as well!). There are snacks and drinks available everywhere, including really great quesadillas and free gelato. The KidsDom program was really well-run; I had had difficulty finding information out about it before but my kids enjoyed spending time at the club every day, and we took full advantage of the extra activities they offered throughout the day outside of the club room (face painting, crafts, cookie decorating, pool party). The staff who run that program (I remember Edgar and Daniel, but they were all great) speak english, are fantastic with kids, good artists and have wonderful energy. My family also used the gym daily; it was beautiful and well-run. We especially enjoyed Nestor’s classes- HIIT made us sweat like crazy, and yoga in the gazebo was challenging and relaxing. I only visited the spa once, but the massage was truly wonderful (thanks Mirabel) and I wished I’d had time to enjoy the amenities more. The beach was ok, but there is a hut on the beach with good smoothies, and there are outside vendors there who you can purchase massages or take boat rides/SUP.
