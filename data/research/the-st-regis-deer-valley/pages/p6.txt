### Tips & Frequently Asked Questions

When does the festive season programming begin and end?

Our festive programming runs from Mid-December through January 2, with daily events including champagne sabrage, hot cocoa and cider, après ski, and s’mores. Select dates feature special celebrations such as the gingerbread house ribbon cutting, live reindeer visits, and visits with Santa and Mrs. Claus.

Do I need reservations for holiday dining experiences?

We recommend reserving in advance for our dining venues during the festive season. Please contact our concierge to arrange your table at RIME, La Stellina, Brasserie 7452, or any of our culinary destinations.

Is the gingerbread house display open to all guests?

Yes, our gingerbread house is open for all guests to enjoy throughout the festive season. Hot cocoa and cider are served beside it daily from 3:00 pm to 6:00 pm.

Are children’s activities included in my stay?

Most of our festive activities, including the ballroom bounce house, balloon artistry, cookie decorating, and visits with Santa and Mrs. Claus, are complimentary for hotel guests. Please consult our daily festive schedule for specific times and locations.

What ski services does the resort offer during the holidays?

The St. Regis Deer Valley offers true ski-in/ski-out access to Deer Valley Resort. Our dedicated Ski Valet team prepares your gear and ensures a seamless return. Complimentary yoga is offered each morning, and après ski is served in the Library each afternoon.

How can I arrange a butler service request during my stay?

Our St. Regis Butlers are available to anticipate your every need throughout your stay, from arranging festive activities to curating bespoke experiences. Please contact the Butler Service Desk.

Are the live reindeer visits held on specific dates?

Yes, our live reindeer visits take place on the Astor Terrace on select evenings, from 5:30 pm to 8:00 pm. Please consult our daily festive schedule for the most up-to-date information.

What is the Champagne Sabrage ritual and when does it take place?

Champagne sabrage is the timeless St. Regis tradition of opening Champagne with a saber. Each evening at 5:00 pm in the lobby, our Butlers perform this celebrated ritual, paired with exquisite caviar and Moët Champagne.

How do I book a holiday stay at The St. Regis Deer Valley?

We invite you to reserve your holiday stay through our website or by calling.
