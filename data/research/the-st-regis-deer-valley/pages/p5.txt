# Hotel near Jordanelle Reservoir for an unforgettable stay

## Experiences

Nestled in the lower Deer Valley of Park City our upscale hotel with outdoor pools is primely positioned for year-round adventure. When fresh powder graces the Wasatch Mountains, The St. Regis Deer Valley offers guests convenient ski-in, and ski-out access to the slopes, as well as a private ski beach. Winter in Park City also boasts the intriguing Sun Dance Film Festival and an array of cold-weather...See More activities including ice skating, snowshoeing, and horse-drawn sleigh rides. In the warmer months, marvel at the seasonless beauty of Park City as the snow is replaced with colorful mountain wildflowers. Come spring and summer, enjoy the temperate weather by hiking, biking, fly-fishing, golfing, and more. The St. Regis Deer Valley is located just minutes from the city's charming downtown area and 25 minutes from the famous Utah Olympic Park.See Less

## Premier Skiing Experience

Experience ultimate convenience at our premier ski-in/ski-out Park City hotel. Step out of your door and onto the slopes without the hassle of transporting your gear. Enjoy effortless access to world-class skiing, with quick returns to your cozy, well-appointed room for a seamless and relaxing mountain getaway at our Park City ski resort hotel.

## Park City Winter Activities

Discover refined adventure and quiet indulgence during the winter in Park City. Beyond the legendary ski slopes and snowshoe trails, festive celebrations and boutique shopping on historic Main Street; winter in Park City also means après-ski gatherings by glowing fireplaces, craft cocktails and locally sourced ingredients at over 100 restaurants in town.

## Scenic Drives

Embark on an unforgettable journey through Deer Valley's breathtaking landscapes. Traverse the stunning Mirror Lake Highway, a 30-mile stretch from Kamas to Mirror Lake. Discover the wonders of the Alpine Loop, a captivating 20-mile loop starting from Sundance Resort. Indulge in the scenic majesty of Guardsman Pass, a captivating route connecting Park City to Big Cottonwood Canyon. Stay at our hotel near Utah Olympic Park to immerse yourself in the beauty of nature and create lasting memories along these remarkable scenic byways.

### Utah Olympic Legacy Foundation

Our partnership with the Utah Olympic Legacy Foundation offers hotel guests elite-level bobsledding and biathlon experiences, with additional signature St. Regis enhancements.

Learn More

##### Bobsled With An Olympian

Experience the thrill of the 2002 Winter Games on the Bobsled track alongside an Olympic Silver Medalist, Valerie Fleming or Shauna Rohbock.For the first 45 minutes of the session, guests will hear Valerie or Shauna’s inspirational story and receive an exclusive Olympian-led tour of Utah Olympic Park’s bobsled track. Guests will also have an opportunity to visit and take photos at curve 12 of the track. After the safety demonstrations, visitors will take the ride of a lifetime! In less than mile, you will rip through 10 curves, pulling 3Gs and hitting speeds up to 70 mph. Your day on the track would not be complete without a medals ceremony of your own, an autographed photo from Shauna and Val, and a special souvenir from your time here at Utah Olympic Park.

##### Olympic Biathlon Experience

Discover why Biathlon is the most popular winter sport in Europe! As an “aspiring Olympian,” participants are coached by our professional staff as they learn to shoot with precision rifles that are comparable to equipment used during the Olympic Games. After a safety briefing, instructional range orientation, and time for practice, participants engage in a mini fun competition set up for the group. During the winter, you will either ski or snowshoe and in the summer, choose jogging or hiking. The biathlon course will raise heart rates and give participants a sense of authentic biathlon competition. Participants are challenged to hit all five targets.
