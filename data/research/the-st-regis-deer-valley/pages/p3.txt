# Dining at The St. Regis Deer Valley

## Dining

The St. Regis Deer Valley offers guests select Park City bars and restaurants serving cuisine as exquisite as the views. Savor fresh seafood or a prime cut steak at RIME at The St. Regis Deer Valley; a French comfort classic at Brasserie 7452; or an elegant cocktail at The St. Regis Bar & Lounge. Regardless of the season, experience Park City outdoor dining at the Terrace Café, located on our Mountain Terrace overlooking spectacular Deer Valley.

A Table Worth Discovering

From the mountain-modern cuisine of RIME to the legendary Bloody Mary ritual at The St. Regis Bar, savor five venues that celebrate flavor, craft, and the rituals that define St. Regis. Reserve your table.
