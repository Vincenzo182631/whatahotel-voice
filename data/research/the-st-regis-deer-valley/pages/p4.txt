## Relaxation Pools & Yoga

Begin with Daily Morning Yoga, available to all guests, then restore in the soothing warmth of our relaxation pools. Choose the ambiance of outdoor tubs or the privacy of our enclosed spa tub—rejuvenation amid mountain serenity.

## Wellness Collection

Increase your healing potential with ancient remedies of warming spice & Balinese massage to balance body, mind & soul. Indulge in our Deep Sleep Journey, for those who are longing to sleep well and awaken with a renewed sense of energy. A powerful body and mind switch-off to induce a serious state of relaxation for a comforting, peaceful night’s sleep. Working on all aspects of physical, mental, and emotional stress.

### Cancellation Policy

If you must reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify the Spa at least 24-hours in advance of your appointment to avoid being charged the full service fee. Any appointments rescheduled within the 24- hour window will incur a move fee of 50.00. Cancellations of packages or private parties require one week’s notice. Groups are subject to a minimum 30-day cancellation policy.

### Age Requirement

Guests must be 18 years of age or older to experience a massage, body treatment
or enjoy the Spa relaxation areas, locker areas and amenities.

### Pricing & Service Charge

Services are listed as starting prices and are subject to change. For your convenience, a 20% service charge will be added to each Spa service. This includes gratuities disbursed to the Spa staff who serve you during your visit. Additional gratuities may be offered at your discretion.
