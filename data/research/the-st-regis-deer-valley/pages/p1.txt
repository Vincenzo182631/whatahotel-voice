# Welcome to The St. Regis Deer Valley

## Transcend ordinary at our Park City ski resort in Utah

The St. Regis Deer Valley — a luxury ski resort above Park City. The only true ski-in/ski-out Deer Valley hotel, where a private funicular carries guests from the grand lobby to the slopes. Each of our 174 hotel rooms and residences opens to panoramic mountain views, with fireplaces, marble baths, and the attentive presence of your St. Regis Butler Service. The St. Regis Spa spans 14,000 square feet of restorative calm. Five dining venues, from the mountain-modern cuisine of RIME to the timeless rituals of The St. Regis Bar, await discovery. This is alpine luxury without compromise — where every detail is considered, and every moment is yours to savor.

Beyond the Slopes, a World of Ritual

Beyond the slopes, a world of ritual awaits. Discover the Bloody Mary at The St. Regis Bar, afternoon tea in the Grand Hall, and evening sabrage beneath the Wasatch stars. Every season at Deer Valley brings new reasons to linger.

## Deer Valley Ski Resort

Our Deer Valley resort revolutionized the ski industry by offering five-star service in a ski-only setting. The resort boasts 24 chairlifts, 122 runs, seven bowls, and 2,342 acres of pristine alpine terrain, with 300 inches of annual snowfall. Renowned for its top-tier ski school and children's center, Deer Valley delivers an exceptional experience for skiers of all levels.

### We Are a Pet Friendly Hotel

The St. Regis Deer Valley is pleased to welcome both you and your family pet. As part of our pet service, we are excited to offer a cozy St. Regis pet bed, ceramic pet bowls and daily turndown amenity so your companion can bask in the comforts of our Park City ski resort hospitality.

Pet Policy

Please notify Concierge prior to arrival so we are able to provide your pet a proper welcome. Please be advised that we charge a $150 non-refundable cleaning fee to your account at departure.

We require that all pets are on leash while in public areas of the grounds. We strongly recommend that you do not leave your pet unattended in your guest room. An unattended pet may cause a delay in daily services provided by our associates to your room. If your pet is unattended and a noise complaint is communicated to our Front Desk, your pet will likely be removed and brought to our Security Department. To arrange pet sitting and walking services please contact the Concierge. A Pet Waiver will need to be completed prior to your stay or immediately upon arrival.

We are delighted to be a pet friendly resort, and hope that you and your pet enjoy your stay at The St. Regis Deer Valley.

### Family Traditions

The St. Regis Deer Valley, a premier resort in Utah's Park City, is pleased to welcome both you and your family. Celebrate the art of play with enriching year-round family adventures that you will treasure for a lifetime.

Learn More

**Family's Delight**

Family Traditions at St. Regis provides an experience beyond expectation. With captivating activities and thoughtful, welcoming attention tailored for younger guests and their families, each St. Regis hotel and resort is a destination of distinction that celebrates the art of play and being together.

Families will delight in special welcome amenities and turndown rituals designed to showcase the uniqueness of each hotel or resort’s destination, including delectable treats, child-sized robes, slippers, and a step stool for the little ones. Throughout a family’s stay, the hotel team is able to recommend and arrange a selection of recreational activities and enriching experiences, dining options, childcare services and other conveniences for families and children of all ages.

The pursuit of fun, redefined at the best address.

**Family Experiences**

As part of the Family Traditions at St. Regis program, families can choose from a selection of activities to experience together while exploring the enriching culture of the destination. We look forward to creating indelible memories for your family to share.

**Bobsled Run** Few experiences can match the thrill of the Comet, where speeds can reach an exhilarating 70 mph. Our professional pilots take three passengers at a time down the length of Utah Olympic Park track where bobsledders raced to glory during the 2002 Winter Olympic Games. Guests are advised that riders must be at least 14 years of age, that a parent or guardian must be present to sign a waiver if a child is under 18 years of age and that proof of age may be requested.

**Family Snowmobile Ride** Families are invited to experience the exhilaration of snowmobiling through mountain meadows, drifted snow and picturesque glades. The Uinta and Wasatch Mountains provide unforgettable vistas in our own backyard. Wildlife sightings are not uncommon – guests may observe deer, moose, elk, fox and all manner of birds. Riders of all ages, except infants, are welcome, and licensed drivers may pilot their own guided, two-hour adventure.

These experiences are perfectly suited for a family of four (two adults and two children), but can always be customized. The Concierge is pleased to provide more details and to help make arrangements. To reserve an experience, it is recommended that guests contact the Concierge at least 24 hours in advance.

**Family Amenities & Services**

While in residence, discover an array of thoughtful services and amenities designed especially for children and families. Allow our dedicated team to provide a flawless experience and every convenience a family needs to enjoy cherished time together.

**Recreation** Open to guests of all ages, our heated, split-level, infinity-edge swimming pool and two Jacuzzis overlook the majestic Deer Hollow ski run. Guests under the age of 15 may use the pool when accompanied by an adult.

**Dining** RIME, our destination restaurant, provides a warm, relaxed atmosphere in which to enjoy gourmet pizza, fresh seafood and more. Reservations are recommended. The St. Regis Bar, a guest favorite for après-ski, offers a classically comfortable ambience.

**Nanny & Babysitting** Our Concierge team maintains a list of babysitters who are able to care for your children in the convenience of your guest room. Please contact us for assistance with reserving nanny and/or babysitting services prior to your stay. If needed during your stay at the hotel, the concierge will be pleased to assist.

**Necessities & Provisions** Our resort is supplied with a variety of family necessities such as wipes, diapers, cribs and rollaway beds. Allow St. Regis Butler Service to assist you with preparing these items for you during your stay or before your arrival at the hotel. Please contact the eButler for assistance.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Deer Valley?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Deer Valley is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Regis Deer Valley allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Deer Valley. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Deer Valley?

On-site valet parking is available at The St. Regis Deer Valley. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Deer Valley?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Deer Valley is Salt Lake City International Airport (SLC). SLC is located approximately 42.6 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Deer Valley have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Deer Valley has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
