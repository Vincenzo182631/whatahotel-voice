### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Osaka?

The check-in time at W Osaka is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Osaka allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Osaka. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of ¥14300.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Osaka?

On-site parking is available at W Osaka. Self parking is available for ¥1500.00 per hour. Valet parking is available for ¥5000.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Osaka have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Osaka has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Osaka booking.

What is the closest airport near W Osaka?

The closest airport near W Osaka is Osaka International Airport - Itami (ITM). ITM is located approximately 17.2 KM from the hotel.

Does W Osaka have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Osaka has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
