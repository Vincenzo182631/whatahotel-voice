## Breakfast

Start your morning in Osaka's Shinsaibashi, at our breakfast restaurant featuring healthy American and Japanese menus to help you feel refreshed and rejuvenated. Personalize and match your mood by selecting your brekky favorites from our à la carte menu, such as our signature Eggs Benedict.

## Afternoon Tea

Experience a fresh take on afternoon tea in Osaka, in the heart of Shinsaibashi along Midosuji Boulevard, where handcrafted pastries and sweets, refined savory bites, and specialty beverages are presented in themes inspired by fashion, culture, design, and culinary innovation. Blending the creativity of W Osaka with the sophistication of a luxury hotel setting, each edition transforms a classic social ritual into a vibrant and memorable dining experience.

## In-Room Dining

Get extra comfy and make yourself at home in a plush W Osaka robe and order in with our Osaka hotel in-room dining experience. Just because you stay in doesn't mean you can't enjoy healthy and indulgent meals. Offering Chef Specials from a variety of global flavors and iconic Japanese cuisine utilizing the best in local ingredients to meet your every guilty pleasure and satisfy any appetite.

## Wet® Deck and Bar

Embark on the ultimate poolside adventure at our vibrant WET Deck, nestled amidst lush greenery. From sunrise to sunset, it's the perfect spot to unwind and connect. Whether you're basking in the sun for a boost of vitamin D or indulging in enticing bites and refreshing drinks from our city center destination cocktail bar in Osaka, WET Bar, we've got everything to balance your wellness needs and rejuvenate your body. Join us for an unforgettable experience that celebrates the harmony of relaxation and revitalization.
