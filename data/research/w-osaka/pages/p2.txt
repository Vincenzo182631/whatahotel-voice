### PENTHOUSE

Prepare to indulge in the ultimate luxury in our expansive 200 sqm Penthouse suite. Perched atop the highest floor of the 27-storey hotel, this lavish retreat boasts soaring 4.5-meter-high ceilings, offering a sense of grandeur like no other. Wake up to sweeping panoramic views of Osaka from five interconnected rooms, including a spacious dining room, fully stocked bar, and open kitchen ideal for hosting private soirées and exclusive events. Unwind in the inviting living/social room, adorned with plush sofas and a state-of-the-art DJ booth, creating the perfect ambiance for entertaining.

## SUPREME SUITE

Kick back in supreme comfort and have the time of your life in superb luxury accommodation in Osaka city center, boasting an expansive living area, open kitchen, and six-seater dining room. Take up residency on the top two floors overlooking the glittering Osaka skyline, so you’ll be gifted with unparalleled views of Osaka’s happening downtown and with epic panoramas of the city’s skyline through inviting wall-to-wall windows.
