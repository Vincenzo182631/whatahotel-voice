# Shopping Arcade

### Shop at the Adlon

**Adlon To Go**

Located at the Pariser Platz with a wonderful view of the Brandenburg Gate, we offer great coffee, homemade sandwiches and fabulous cakes. We use organic local milk and also sell items produced small businesses in Berlin, such as the porcelain factory located in Prenzlauer Berg. We proudly support sustainability and local businesses.

Visit Adlon To Go and let us indulge you with our extraordinary coffee creations!

**Juwelier Leicht**

Experience the fascination of traditional craftsmanship. Discover the glorious beauty of living traditions in jewellery and watches from Leicht. Unique custom jewellery using your own design ideas can also be made on request.

**Krigler**

The scent of craftsmanship, elegance and intrigue. Discover the mysteriously enticing and elite fragrances from Krigler, a historically iconic perfume house conceived in 1904 in St Petersburg and born of founder Albert Krigler’s desire to create unique luxury fragrances handcrafted from the finest ingredients around the world.
