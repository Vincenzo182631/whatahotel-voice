# Roomservice

### Roomservice

Our room service offers you the opportunity to enjoy the hotel’s unparalleled comfort and first-class service directly in your room or suite and is available around the clock to fulfil your wishes.

Start your day with a rich breakfast while enjoying the breathtaking view of the Brandenburg Gate or the picturesque inner courtyards. Have an ideal start to discovering Berlin and be fortified for the day’s activities.

At lunchtime and in the evening, we will spoil you with a wide selection of first-class international dishes from our kitchen. You can also choose from our regularly changing seasonal offers.

Our room service goes one step further. Regardless of what is offered on the regular menu, we are also happy to take your individual orders and do our best to fulfil your wishes.

In addition to food, we offer a wide selection of drinks, including fine wines, refreshing cocktails and non-alcoholic options.

Relax, sit back and let our first-class room service pamper you. At Hotel Adlon Kempinski Berlin, your well-being is our top priority. We look forward to providing you with an unforgettable stay.

The team looks forward to your call at 1579.
