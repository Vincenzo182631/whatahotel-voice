# Adlon Flower Atelier & Boutique

### Your place for elegant flower arrangements

At our Atelier, you’ll find not only a selection of ready-made bouquets but also the opportunity to create your own personalised bouquet from our selection of fresh flowers.

Let us inspire you to create the perfect bridal bouquet for your wedding, a thoughtful gift for a wedding day, anniversary, Valentine's Day or for your room or suite during your stay.

Whether you wish to drop by spontaneously or order in advance, we will ensure you receive the perfect arrangement.

If you’re planning something special, we are delighted to design customised wreaths and floral decorations for any occasion. We are more than happy to discuss all the details with you in advance to ensure that your wishes are fulfilled.

Our team will also be happy to advise you on the suitable floral decorations for your event here at Hotel Adlon Kempinski.

Exclusive merchandise from the Hotel Adlon Kempinski Berlin also awaits you in our studio.

Visit us and let us inspire you. We look forward to making your floral wishes come true.
