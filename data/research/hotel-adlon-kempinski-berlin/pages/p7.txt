# City Tour

### The best way to discover Berlin

A sightseeing tour in Berlin offers a variety of ways to discover the fascinating history and impressive culture of this vibrant city.

**Hop-On, Hop-Off Buses**

These buses offer a flexible way to explore Berlin’s main sights. You can hop on and off at different stops and explore the city at your own pace.

**Boat Tours on the Spree**

The Spree flows through the heart of Berlin and provides a picturesque backdrop for a boat tour. Choose between relaxed cruises and informative guided tours that teach you about the city’s history.

**Trabi tours** are a unique and fun way to explore Berlin. The Trabant, also known as the ‘Trabi’, was an iconic car in the former GDR and is now seen as a symbol of German reunification.

**Trabi Safari**

On a Trabi Safari, you will get the pleasure of driving a Trabi yourself or riding along as a passenger. The tours are accompanied by a guide who drives in front and provides interesting information about the sights via an audio device. Explore the highlights of Berlin while driving through the streets in the iconic Trabi.

**Trabi XXL**

The Trabi XXL tour seats up to five people in an extended Trabi and is ideal for groups or families who want to explore the city together. A guide accompanies you and gives you information about the sights along the route.
