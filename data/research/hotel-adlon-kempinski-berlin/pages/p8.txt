# Bike Rental

### Discover the city

Here are some highlights to visit during your bike tour of the city:

**Reichstag building**

Right next to the Brandenburg Gate is the Reichstag building, the seat of the German Bundestag. Climb the glass dome and enjoy a breathtaking view of the city.

**Museum Island**

Cycle to the Museum Island, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Here, you will find an impressive collection of museums, including the Pergamon Museum, Neues Museum and Alte Nationalgalerie.

**Checkpoint Charlie**

Make a stop at Checkpoint Charlie, the famous border crossing between East and West Berlin during the Cold War. Learn more about the history and division of the city at this historic site.

**East Side Gallery**

Drive along the Spree River to the longest remaining section of the Berlin Wall, now known as the East Side Gallery. Admire the impressive artworks painted on the wall segments by artists from all over the world.

**Tiergarten**

Take a break from the hustle and bustle of the city and cycle to Tiergarten, Berlin’s largest park. Enjoy a relaxing ride along the green avenues and stop at Bellevue Palace, the official residence of the Federal President.

**Charlottenburg Palace**

Cycle to Charlottenburg and visit the magnificent palace. The Baroque architecture and beautiful gardens provide an impressive backdrop for a relaxing stroll.

\-\-\-

These are just a few of the many sights you can discover by bike in Berlin. Plan your own route and explore the diversity and beauty of this fascinating city on two wheels. For further excursion tips, our Ladies in Red and the concierges will be happy to help you.

Our tips for spring and summer: tours to the nearby lakes (Weißer See in the northeast or Lietzensee in the west) or along the two rivers Havel and Spree
