# Concierge Service

### We make it possible

The concierge team at Hotel Adlon Kempinski Berlin fulfils the wishes of all guests to make your stay unforgettable.

Whether you are travelling for business or pleasure, our concierges use their connections to make the impossible possible.

**Local Information**

Our concierge team will be happy to assist you with public transport and currency exchange, as well as provide you with information about Berlin and the surrounding areas.

**Restaurants & Bars**

The contacts of our concierges are as varied as Berlin itself. We will be happy to reserve a table at your favourite restaurant or recommend personal insider tips. More

**Limousine Transfer**

The concierge team will be happy to arrange a limousine for you from or to the airport or to any other destination. More

**Ticket Reservations**

Whether for city tours, entry to one of the 165 museums or 420 galleries, theatre, opera or concert performances, the Concierge Team will be happy to take care of your tickets.

**Sights**

Berlin has many scenic spots, such as the Reichstag, the Museum Island, the East Side Gallery or the Zoological Garden, which was founded over 150 years ago. We will help you find the right one for you. More

**Shopping**

The German capital offers a variety of shopping opportunities that will delight any shopaholic. From trendy fashion boutiques to exquisite luxury shops and traditional flea markets – everyone will find something suitable in Berlin. More
