# About COMO Metropolitan Singapore

Housed in COMO Orchard on Bideford Road, COMO Metropolitan Singapore marks many firsts. Our 5 Star hotel in Singapore is part of an integrated and immersive experience spread over 19 floors, bringing the best of COMO Group's hospitality, fashion, wellness and cuisine in one prime destination. Savour outstanding international dining concepts, enjoy access to curated collections from multi-label Club 21, and discover wellness from within at COMO Shambhala Singapore.

### SEASONALITY IN SINGAPORE

Temperatures in Singapore range from 31 to 33ºC (87.7 to 91.4°F) during the day, and 23 to 25ºC (73.4 to 77°F) during the night. While there is no distinct wet or dry season in Singapore, higher rainfall typically occurs from November to January. Singapore also has an average humidity of 84 per cent.

## EYE FOR DESIGN

Enter a world of contemporary elegance and purposeful design, brought to life by longtime COMO design partners Atelier Ikebuchi and Milan-based OTTO Studio headed up by Paola Navone, along with Norwegian digital artist Thomas Hilland and Modellus Novus, a New York City-based architecture firm. COMO Metropolitan Singapore is a testament to the fusion of form and function - each space confident in its modern simplicity, pale colour palette, and attention to detail.
