### Terms and Conditions

- Guests can walk-in (subject to availability), book online here or simply email our reservations team at metsin@comohotels.com
- Prevailing pre-deposit requirements apply for weekend reservations.
- Hotel key card must be presented to enjoy the dining discount
- Discount can only be applied to 1 bill and is valid for dine-ins only
- Not valid with other discounts, promotions, privileges, set menus, special event menus, event packages and/or other offers
- Not valid on eve of PH and PH, and other blackout dates like Easter Sunday, Mother's Day, etc
- AT feast is located at 16A Dempsey Rd, Singapore 247695

## Complimentary Delivery by Culina

Enhance your stay with access to Culina's curated selection of gourmet groceries, fine wines and premium pantry essentials.

As a guest of COMO Metropolitan Singapore, enjoy complimentary delivery on all orders placed via the Culina eShop\* with no minimum spend required.

Orders are delivered directly to the hotel, subject to Culina's standard delivery times and terms and conditions.

\*Email our Reservations team via metsin@comohotels.com for a dedicated promo code to use upon checkout.
