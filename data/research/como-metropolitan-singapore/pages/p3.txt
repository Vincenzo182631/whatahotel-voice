# Dining for our guests

COMO Orchard is home to COTE Singapore, a Korean Steakhouse from Simon Kim, who has garnered Michelin stars for his restaurants in New York and Miami. Guests can also enjoy the full gamut of popular classics from COMO Cuisine, and our healthy COMO Shambhala Kitchen. We celebrate another first with Cédric Grolet who brings his signature pastries to his first patisserie in Asia. Enjoy preferred access to these tables when you stay with us.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1How many restaurants and bars are there at COMO Metropolitan Singapore?

The hotel features multiple dining venues and bars, each reflecting COMO’s signature focus on culinary excellence and wellness-inspired cuisine. Guests can enjoy everything from modern Asian and European fine dining to casual contemporary fare, all within a refined and vibrant setting.

#### 2What type of cuisine is served in the hotel restaurants?

COMO Metropolitan Singapore offers a diverse dining experience, including COMO Cuisine for global and healthy dishes, and COTE, a refined Korean steakhouse. The menus highlight seasonal, locally sourced ingredients prepared with creativity and balance.

#### 3Are vegetarian and vegan options available?

Yes. All restaurants provide thoughtfully curated vegetarian and vegan options, incorporating plant-based ingredients and mindful preparation techniques for both wellness and taste.

#### 4Does COMO Metropolitan Singapore propose healthy food options?

Absolutely. Guests can enjoy the signature COMO Shambhala Cuisine, offering nutrient-rich, organic, and low-sodium meals designed to promote well-being, energy, and balance — perfect for health-conscious travelers.
