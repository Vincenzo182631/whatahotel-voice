### ABOUT COMO SHAMBHALA SINGAPORE

COMO Shambhala Singapore, the global wellness brand's urban flagship, occupies the literal and metaphorical ‘heart' of COMO Orchard: a fourth-floor, 9,000-sq ft haven for nurturing wellness from within.

The expert-led approach integrates gut health and body composition diagnostics, with functional fitness training, yoga, Pilates, hot and cold therapies and hands-on healing. Classes, one-day Wellness Packages and multi-day Wellness Paths encourage long-stay commitments.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Does COMO Metropolitan Singapore have a spa?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan Singapore features COMO Shambhala Singapore, a 9,000-sq-ft, purpose-designed wellness sanctuary inside the COMO Orchard building. The retreat offers expert-led holistic therapies, hot and cold thermal suites, movement studios, and a serene space for rest and renewal in the heart of Singapore’s city centre.

#### 2What treatments and therapies are available at COMO Metropolitan Singapore?

Guests can experience a comprehensive range of treatments including full-body massages, facials, body scrubs, and detox programmes. Many use natural COMO Shambhala products and are tailored to restore balance, improve sleep, support gut health, and promote functional fitness.

#### 3Are yoga sessions offered at the hotel?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan Singapore offers daily yoga classes, suitable for all levels. Guests can also book private sessions tailored to their personal fitness or relaxation goals.

#### 4Is there a fitness center in the hotel?

Yes. The hotel includes a state-of-the-art fitness center equipped with Technogym machines, weights, and cardio equipment, complemented by personal training sessions and guided wellness programs.

#### 5What wellness facilities are available at COMO Metropolitan Singapore?

COMO Shambhala Singapore includes private treatment rooms (one double, two singles), thermal suites (red-light therapy, contrast therapy, oxygen therapy), yoga and Pilates studios, and a 1,500-sq-ft fitness gym outfitted with premium kit. These combine to create a holistic wellness experience tailored to urban guests seeking restoration in Singapore.

#### 6Is COMO Metropolitan Singapore a good choice for a wellness-focused holiday in Singapore?

Absolutely. COMO Metropolitan Singapore is one of the finest wellness-focused luxury hotels in Asia, seamlessly merging urban sophistication with holistic well-being. It’s perfect for travelers seeking a revitalizing escape in the heart of the city, surrounded by COMO’s signature design, cuisine, and care.
