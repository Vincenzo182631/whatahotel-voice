# Accommodation

COMO Metropolitan Singapore features 156 rooms and suites--all equipped with SleepHub®, an innovative, neuroscience-led solution designed to help modern travellers achieve deeper, more restorative rest. Our spaces feature clean-lined, customised furnishings from Giorgetti, integrated with tech-forward touches for your comfort and convenience. Every detail is meticulously crafted to welcome you home to COMO, from a discreet kitchenette to an in-room card-protected delivery vault for services that you order to your room.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1What types of rooms and suites are available at COMO Metropolitan Singapore?

COMO Metropolitan Singapore offers a curated selection of spacious rooms and suites designed for both leisure and family travel along Orchard Road. Options include Metropolitan Rooms, Premier Suites, and the expansive COMO Suite, each featuring floor-to-ceiling windows, contemporary interiors, and serene city or skyline views. The variety of layouts makes the hotel ideal for couples, solo travelers, and families seeking comfort in the heart of Singapore.

#### 2Are there rooms suitable for large families or groups?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan Singapore offers spacious one- and two-bedroom suites, ideal for large families or groups. The COMO Suite features a residential-style layout with two bedrooms, separate living and dining areas, and generous space for privacy and comfort. It is one of Orchard Road’s most versatile suite options for multi-generational travel, longer stays, or guests seeking a premium family-friendly hotel experience in Singapore.

#### 3Are the rooms equipped with modern amenities such as air conditioning and Wi-Fi?

Yes. All accommodations are equipped with air conditioning, high-speed Wi-Fi, smart TVs, minibars, and luxurious bathrooms featuring COMO Shambhala bath products — ensuring both comfort and contemporary style.

#### 4Can extra bed or baby cot be provided for children?

Yes. Extra beds and baby cots can be arranged upon request, making COMO Metropolitan Singapore a family-friendly urban retreat suitable for travelers with young children.
