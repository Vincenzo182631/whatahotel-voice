Experience a luxury shopping experience with COMO’s dedicated service at Club 21. Spread over two floors, women’s wear and menswear sit alongside kids’ wear and jewellery collections. Discover exquisitely curated collections from a diverse line-up of brands that includes Alaïa, Amiri, Marine Serre, Simone Rocha, Thom Browne and Tom Wood, each showcased in dedicated spaces.
