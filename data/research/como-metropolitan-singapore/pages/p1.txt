# COMO Metropolitan Singapore

Home/Singapore/COMO Metropolitan Singapore

COMO Metropolitan Singapore is part of an integrated, immersive experience spread across 19 floors and all under one roof at COMO Orchard in the city's heart. Alongside our rooms and suites equipped with SleepHub® technology, you will find COMO Group’s signature brands: multi-label fashion by Club 21; an urban wellness space by COMO Shambhala; contemporary international dining by COMO Cuisine; a Michelin-starred gourmet concept by COTE Korean Steakhouse, as well as the world-renowned patisserie Cédric Grolet.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Where is COMO Metropolitan Singapore located in Singapore?

COMO Metropolitan Singapore is located at 30 Bideford Road, in the heart of the Orchard Road district. As part of the integrated COMO Orchard building, the hotel offers direct access to Singapore’s premier shopping, dining and cultural experiences — all within a short walk of the city’s most iconic attractions.

#### 2Does COMO Metropolitan Singapore offer direct access to Orchard Road’s shopping malls and nearby experiences?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan Singapore has direct, sheltered access to Orchard Road’s malls. The hotel is linked to Paragon Shopping Centre via a covered walkway and sits inside COMO Orchard, home to Club 21 retail. From Paragon, guests can easily reach Takashimaya at Ngee Ann City, ION Orchard, and Mandarin Gallery within minutes.

#### 3Does COMO Metropolitan Singapore offer wellness or holistic health programmes?

es. COMO Metropolitan Singapore offers a range of holistic wellness programmes designed to support sleep therapy, cleansing, detoxification, and overall well-being. These include the Holistic Health Programme developed with DA MedSuites, as well as the Cleanse and Sleep Dreams programmes curated by COMO Shambhala. Guests can access evidence-based therapies, restorative movement, mindful practices, and nutrition-led protocols that promote better sleep, deeper rest, and gentle detox — all within the hotel’s integrated wellness ecosystem at COMO Orchard.

#### 4Does COMO Metropolitan Singapore offer a rooftop infinity pool with city views?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan Singapore features a rooftop infinity pool that offers elevated views over Orchard Road. Guests can relax in the infinity-edge pool, enjoy comfortable loungers, and experience a serene urban escape in the heart of Singapore’s premier shopping district. The pool’s open-air setting makes it ideal for morning laps, quiet mid-day breaks, or sunset relaxation after a day exploring the city.

#### 5Does COMO Metropolitan Singapore have a sky bar or rooftop bar near Orchard Road?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan Singapore offers a stylish rooftop pool bar atop Level 19 of the COMO Orchard building, where guests can sip handcrafted cocktails and premium spirits while overlooking the city skyline. Open daily from 10:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., its open-air layout makes it one of Orchard Road’s standout elevated bars for sunset drinks, casual bites and an urban rooftop vibe.

#### 6Is COMO Metropolitan Singapore committed to sustainability and eco-friendly practices?

Yes. COMO Metropolitan Singapore is committed to being a sustainable, eco-friendly hotel in Singapore, with design and operations centered around responsible luxury. The property incorporates energy-efficient systems, smart water management, and eco-conscious materials throughout its spaces. It also supports local sourcing, reduces waste through mindful operational practices, and aligns with COMO Hotels’ global commitment to sustainable luxury hospitality.
