# Dining

The restaurants at Grand Hyatt Kochi Bolgatty share a flair for the dramatic, the special and the memorable. In between relaxing at the hotel, attending business meetings or exploring this historical city, enjoy an exceptional experience at any of the dining spots at our hotel.

## Colony Clubhouse & Grill

Colony weaves a fine fabric of global cuisines that lines your palate with authentic taste and showcases the finest form of art on your plate. Our barman's expertise brings a mesmerizing range of beverages to complement your food. Highlights: Homemade buratta, roasted heirloom tomatoes and passion fruit dressing • Cured salmon carpaccio, caper salsa, fennel leaves, tonnato sauce • Crab and mascarpone girasoli, lemon butter • Jamaican jerk spiced roast baby chicken, edamame beans barley risotto, roast jus • Pineapple tart tatin, rum and raisin ice cream

## Colony Bar

Indulge in the epitome of luxury at Colony Bar, nestled within the Grand Hyatt Kochi Bolgatty. With breathtaking waterfront views, our bar offers a refined atmosphere, harmonizing modern design with local charm. Savor meticulously crafted cocktails and a premium spirits selection, expertly served in an ambiance that exudes sophistication. Immerse yourself in plush surroundings, relishing impeccable service and a diverse menu that caters to both classic and adventurous tastes. Whether unwinding after a day of exploration or celebrating special moments, Colony Bar guarantees an unforgettable experience in the lap of luxury.

## Malabar Cafe

Drawing on the city’s culinary and cultural legacies, fired with Malabar spices, Malabar Cafe offers a relaxed and quintessentially South Indian experience. Relax at our modern café that features an all-day menu with Kerala region’s age-old treasured recipes crafted by our team of Chefs. Savour an endless breakfast menu and exquisitely flavoured interpretations of traditional Kerala dishes for lunch and dinner just the way it was made at home.
Highlights: Aatinkaal Bharani Charu • Vazhappoo Cutlet • Kerala Beef Fry • Koon Ulli Theeyel • Egg Porotta • The Local Market Thali • Murgh Lal Mirch • Keema Masala • Queen's Way Kuluki • Kochi Colada • Parippu Pradaman - Sweet Lentil Payasam

## Thai Soul

Drunch Market: Every Saturday

Inspired by the vibrant streets of Bangkok, Thai Soul is a casual poolside dining space. The aroma of fresh Thai herbs and flavours comes alive in the live kitchen, creating a multi-sensorial experience for our guests. Highlights: Som tum koong yang-raw papaya salad with grilled prawn • Tom yam koong-spicy prawn soup with kaffir lime, lemongrass, galangal

The outdoor pool also includes a children’s pool. We also have a beautiful indoor pool at the P level overlooking our lush gardens.

## Room Service

Relish gourmet food in the comfort of your bed at Grand Hyatt Kochi Bolgatty overlooking the panoramic Vembanad Lake and the cityscapes. The Malabar Cafe's hearty breakfast is our go-to when you wake up. Pan-Asian cuisines from Thai Soul and European delicacies from Colony Clubhouse & Grill are always on speed dial. Our welcoming Room Service team will be happy to assist you at any time.
