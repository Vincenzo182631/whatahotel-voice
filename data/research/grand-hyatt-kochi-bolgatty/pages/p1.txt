## EXPLORE

Fitness Centre

Maintain your fitness routine while at Grand Hyatt Kochi Bolgatty with our advanced Fitness Centre on M Floor. Open 24 hours a day, our centre offers the latest cardio and strength-training equipment. Plus, expert personal trainers are happy to assist. Equipment includes treadmills, exercise bikes, elliptical cross trainers, a cross-over machine, rowing machines, a stair-stepper, dumbbells, kettlebells, exercise balls, adjustable benches, weight training equipment and an ARKE kit.

Outdoor Pool

Spend your day soaking in the tropical sun from our stunning outdoor swimming pool surrounded by lush gardens overlooking Kochi’s serene backwaters. The outdoor pool also includes a children’s pool. Additionally, enjoy a beautiful indoor pool on the P level that overlooks our lush gardens.

Indoor & Outdoor Swimming Pool: 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM

The Houseboat

Set against the tranquil expanse of Vembanad Lake, the luxury houseboats at Grand Hyatt Kochi Bolgatty offer an immersive backwater experience that blends Kerala’s timeless waterways with the sophistication of contemporary luxury hospitality. Designed as floating extensions of the hotel’s signature warmth and elegance, the houseboats provide guests with a rare opportunity to experience Kochi from the water - slowly, intimately and meaningfully.

Villa

Ideal for families, long-stay guests, intimate celebrations, and luxury leisure travelers, the villas offer expansive living and dining spaces, private outdoor areas, personalized hospitality and an atmosphere of complete privacy and relaxation. The villa experience allows guests to enjoy the scale and amenities of a luxury urban resort while still feeling deeply personal and secluded.

Santata Spa

A sanctuary of wellbeing that marries traditional wisdom with modern luxury. The 11,000+ sq. ft. spa is home to seven treatment rooms, an indoor pool, hydrotherapy facilities and serene relaxation lounges.

Beyond treatments, wellness extends into beautifully landscaped outdoors - with a yoga lawn, exploration gardens, a spice garden, outdoor pool, children’s pool, beauty salon and even a private jetty for backwater escapes. Whether you seek a deep Ayurvedic ritual or a simple moment of calm, Santata offers a complete wellness retreat within the heart of Kochi.

Chinese Fishing Net

Set within the serene expanse of Grand Hyatt Kochi Bolgatty, the iconic Chinese fishing nets stand gracefully against the backdrop of the hotel’s striking waterfront facade. This timeless symbol of Kochi’s maritime heritage is seamlessly woven into the luxury and elegance of the resort, offering a rare blend of culture and contemporary hospitality.

As the evening sky unfolds in soft hues of amber and violet, the still waters reflect both the historic charm of the nets and the architectural beauty of the hotel, creating a setting that is both captivating and tranquil. It is an experience that brings together heritage, design, and the gentle rhythm of the backwaters in perfect harmony.

Grand Club Access

Experience Grand Club when you select one of our specialty private access club or suite rooms. Grand Club access includes complimentary breakfast, appetizers, beverages, evening refreshments, boardroom access and more. Take advantage of our luxury spaces and enjoy exclusive serene rooms and suites, many with picturesque Vembanad Lake views and Grand Club access—plus, enjoy cocktails and canapés on select brands from 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM. Contact the hotel about special offers for access to Grand Club.

Special Events

Plan your special days with us. Whether it’s a wedding by the lake, a large conference or a corporate meeting, we have the ideal space for you. With more than 30 indoor and outdoor venues for your event needs, look no further than Grand Hyatt Kochi Bolgatty.
