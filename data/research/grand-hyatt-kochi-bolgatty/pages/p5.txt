Grand Hyatt Kochi Bolgatty stands as an architectural masterpiece along the backwaters, blending luxury with traditional Kerala warmth.
The property delivers an exceptional stay experience through its striking waterfront views, Suites are impeccably appointed with modern smart-room technology and floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the Vembanad Lake. Service remains consistently polished and intuitive,

The Grand Club experience elevates a stay at Grand Hyatt Kochi Bolgatty to an entirely new level, offering an exquisite blend of privacy, personalized service, and refined hospitality.
Situated to provide stunning backwater views, the lounge delivers a sophisticated yet relaxed atmosphere. The dedicated check-in and check-out service ensures seamless arrivals and departures, while the culinary offerings—ranging from a gourmet breakfast spread to evening cocktails and curated canapés—are consistently top-tier.
