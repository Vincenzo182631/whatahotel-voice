## Stay at Our Beautifully Renovated Hotel in ATL, Georgia

Experience the perfect blend of luxury service and serene elegance at the beautifully renovated JW Marriott Atlanta, Buckhead hotel. Situated in the upscale Buckhead neighborhood, our hotel in Atlanta, Georgia, provides all you need to explore the city's allure. Find the hotel directly connected to Lenox Square® and just minutes from popular local attractions, such as the Georgia Aquarium, Atlanta...See More Zoo, Atlanta History Center, and High Museum of Art. Plan a special occasion in our redesigned event space that provides an ideal setting for impressive business meetings, private gatherings or weddings in Buckhead. Each tasteful venue offers state-of-the-art technology, custom catering and expert service. Enjoy premium amenities like a 24-hour fitness center and superb dining options before you retire to well-appointed rooms with entertainment systems and marble bathrooms. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead, with rates of $10.00 per hour, and $52.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $62.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead is Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL). ATL is located approximately 18.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
