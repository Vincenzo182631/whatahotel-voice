### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bikes, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead?

LEGO® Discovery Center Atlanta, Atlanta Botanical Garden, Atlanta History Center, Centennial Olympic Park, SkyView Ferris Wheel and more are nearby JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead.
