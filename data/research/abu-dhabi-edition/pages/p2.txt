# Rooms

The Abu Dhabi EDITION's 198 guestrooms are designed using a sophisticated neutral palette. Grey oak timber flooring with solid pale khaki rugs are featured in the main room with off-white marble flooring reserved for the bathrooms. The herringbone pattern on the wood floors draws inspiration from local basket weaving techniques. Each room features beautiful wood paneling and the windows are dressed in elegant off-white drapery. The guestroom balconies take advantage of the impressive marina views and feature comfortable outdoor furniture by Gandia Blasco.

# Suites

Each suite features a spacious bedroom with a separate living area. Our suites may also offer private balconies and optional connecting rooms. All guest rooms are adorned with photography by twentieth-century photographer Brett Weston. The collection displays Weston’s masterful skill at manipulating photos of sand dunes to create sinuous lines and sensuous forms which reference Abu Dhabi’s surrounding environment while remaining true to EDITION’s sophisticated aesthetic.
