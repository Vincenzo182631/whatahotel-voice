# Alba Terrace

**Alba Terrace**

RESERVE

Upon stepping foot into the Alba Terrace restaurant, your own story of visiting the Mediterranean will unfold as guests are greeted by a large brass-clad aperitif bar. As the day unfolds, small plates, light aperitivo, and Aperol Spritz encourage guests to relax and unwind. On the lush outdoor terrace, a series of intimate seating areas under pavilions or next to the reflection pool provide guests with the opportunity to dine al fresco, reminiscent of elegant Italian summers.

**Oak Room**

RESERVE VIEW WEBSITE

Oak Room is a modern steakhouse that encourages guests to indulge in the finest produce, free from formality and pretense, yet always thoughtful in its approach. Sample a robust wine selection and discover exceptional prime-grade cuts of meat.

**Library Bar**

RESERVE VIEW WEBSITE

Taking design cues from its name, the walls are furnished with artwork inspired by themes of language and the written word. Designed as a quieter, more intimate retreat from the bustle of Abu Dhabi, Library Bar offers a creative approach to mixology and an immersive evening of expertly crafted drinks, led by its award-winning Assistant Bar Manager & Head of Mixology, Ryan Remulta.

**Market at EDITION**

RESERVE

An imaginative, healthy lifestyle restaurant open for all-day breakfast and lunch, Market's relaxed and interactive style blurs the boundaries between traditional and al fresco dining. The international cuisine focuses on fresh ingredients, harmonizing nutrition with delicious flavors. Stop by for a juice, grab and go, or stay for a family meal in an informal setting enhanced by the theatre of live cooking.
