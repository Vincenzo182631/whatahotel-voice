# Welcome to The Westin Galleria Houston

## Unwind at our Premier Hotel in the Galleria Houston

Embark on a journey of sophistication at The Westin Galleria Houston, where modern elegance meets timeless comfort in the vibrant heart of Texas. Nestled amidst the iconic Galleria Mall, our prestigious Four-Diamond AAA hotel offers a gateway to luxury and convenience. Discover the pulse of Uptown Houston, moments from attractions, as you indulge in signature experiences. Savor the flavors of American cuisine at Daily Grill or unwind with a cocktail at our chic Lobby Bar. Elevate your wellness routine with workouts at our WestinWORKOUT Fitness Studio, boasting panoramic views, and relax by our refreshing outdoor pool. Enhance your stay at our family-friendly hotel in Houston, offering exceptional comfort for every traveler. Enjoy seamless access to premier shopping from our hotel near Galleria Mall Houston. Retreat each evening to spacious rooms and suites with Westin Heavenly Beds and deluxe amenities at our pet-friendly hotel in Houston.

Parking Advisory: Blue Garage Improvements

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Galleria Houston?

The check-in time at The Westin Galleria Houston is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Galleria Houston allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Galleria Houston. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The Westin Galleria Houston?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Galleria Houston. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for $58.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Galleria Houston have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Galleria Houston has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Galleria Houston booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Galleria Houston?

The closest airport near The Westin Galleria Houston is William P Hobby Airport (HOU). HOU is located approximately 17.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Galleria Houston have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Galleria Houston does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does The Westin Galleria Houston have a Club lounge?

Yes, The Westin Galleria Houston has Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
