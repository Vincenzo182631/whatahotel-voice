### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Galleria Houston have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Galleria Houston offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton, Weight Equipment, Cardio Equipment, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Galleria Houston have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Galleria Houston has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Galleria Houston?

The Galleria, iPic Movies, Pinstripes, Alley Theatre, Houston Museum of Natural Science and more are nearby The Westin Galleria Houston.
