## Welcome to Morocco's _finest_ beach resort

Discover the elegance of Fairmont Taghazout Bay, where the Atlantic meets luxury. Nestled on Morocco’s stunning coast, our 5-star resort offers breathtaking ocean views, a private beach, and a world-class spa. Enjoy gourmet dining, thrilling water sports, and cultural explorations, all in a serene, sophisticated setting.
