# Sightseeing

### Sightseeing

Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich is located directly in the centre of the city and is therefore the perfect starting point for your sightseeing tour of the metropolis. To make the planning of your stay easier, we have listed the most important attractions below.

- Marienplatz
- Odeonsplatz
- Hofbräuhaus
- Viktualienmarkt
- Bayerische Staatsoper
- Englischer Garten
- Olympiapark
- Deutsches Museum
- Allianzarena
- Königsplatz
- Nymphenburg Castle
- Residenz Munich
- Pinakothek der Moderne
- Bavaria Film Studio
- Frauenkirche Cathedral
- Hellabrunn Zoo
- Hofgarten

Detailed information on all sights can be found on https://www.munich.travel/en
