# KRIGLER boutique

### KRIGLER boutique

The wonderful history of the KRIGLER perfume house spans more than a century and half the world. Munich is a return to the Bavarian capital for the traditional brand, because this is exactly where a KRIGLER boutique was located at the beginning of the 20th century. The iconic fragrance “Elegant Swan 06”, for which founder Albert Krigler was inspired by a visit to Neuschwanstein Castle in 1906, also dates from this period. To this day, it is a reminder of the German roots of the house and the Krigler family.

One can't miss the KRIGLER boutique as you make your way into the hotel's opulent, famous lobby. Inside, Art Deco elements, gold and mirrors meet Bavarian reminiscences - alongside portraits of famous KRIGLER fans such as Audrey Hepburn and Jackie Kennedy, there are also pictures of Empress Sissi and Neuschwanstein Castle. Like all boutiques, this one was designed by Ben Krigler, who is not only a perfumer but also a trained architect.

Krigler proudly returns to its roots: creating personalised, tailor-made fragrances that combine exclusivity and sumptuous flacons like a sparkling jewel. In order to discover the nuances of your personality and capture the quintessence of your being, KRIGLER invites you to arrange a personal consultation with one of the master perfumers.
