# Bespoke Guest Service

### Bespoke Guest Service

At Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich, every stay is shaped around your individual preferences. Our renowned Ladies and Gentlemen in Red provide discreet, personalised assistance throughout your visit, ensuring every detail is thoughtfully arranged before and during your stay. From expert services and butler assistance to tailored suite preparation and exclusive access to the Bavarian State Opera, our dedicated team is committed to creating seamless and memorable experiences.

Whether you are visiting Munich for business, leisure, a special celebration, or a cultural escape, our experienced team is on hand to anticipate your needs and exceed expectations. From securing sought-after restaurant reservations and arranging private city tours to preparing your suite for a milestone occasion, we curate bespoke experiences that reflect the elegance and heritage of our hotel. Allow us to take care of every detail while you enjoy the very best of Munich.

### Ladies & Gentlemen in Red

For more than a decade, our iconic Ladies and Gentlemen in Red have been welcoming guests with warmth, expertise and impeccable attention to detail. Easily recognised by their distinctive red attire, they serve as your personal connection to the hotel and the city of Munich.

With unrivalled local knowledge and an extensive network of trusted partners, they can arrange restaurant reservations, private transfers, exclusive sightseeing experiences, last-minute theatre and opera tickets, luxury shopping appointments and personalised itineraries. Whether you seek hidden local gems or Munich’s most celebrated attractions, our Ladies and Gentlemen in Red are delighted to create a truly tailored experience.

### Butler & In-Suite Service

For guests seeking the highest level of comfort and convenience, our personalised butler service ensures an effortless stay. From unpacking and garment care to coordinating dining arrangements and special requests, your butler is available to assist with every detail.

Guests staying in selected suites may enjoy dedicated butler services designed around their individual preferences. Our team is pleased to arrange bespoke assistance prior to arrival or throughout your stay. Please contact our Guest Service Team to discuss your requirements.

### Suite Preparation & Personal Touches

Every stay can be personalised before your arrival. Whether you are celebrating an anniversary, birthday, honeymoon, or hosting an important business visit, our team can prepare your suite according to your wishes.

From floral arrangements and welcome amenities to personalised gifts, champagne, sweet treats, or business set-ups, we ensure every detail reflects the purpose of your visit. Simply share your preferences with our Guest Service Team, and we will create a thoughtful and memorable welcome.

### Access to the Bavarian State Opera & Munich Culture

As one of Munich’s most prestigious cultural institutions, the Bavarian State Opera offers world-class performances throughout the year. Our guest service team can assist with securing tickets, including sought-after performances whenever availability permits.

To complement your evening, we can arrange pre-opera dining reservations, transportation, and recommendations tailored to your interests. Beyond the opera, our team can provide access to Munich’s vibrant cultural scene, including museums, galleries, concerts, and seasonal events.

### Bespoke Munich Experiences

Discover Munich through carefully curated experiences designed exclusively for you. Enjoy private shopping appointments with luxury boutiques, personalised city tours led by expert guides, exclusive culinary experiences, and tailored excursions throughout Bavaria.

Whether you wish to explore Munich's historic landmarks, discover hidden neighbourhoods, experience local traditions, or plan a day trip to nearby castles and alpine destinations, our team will create an itinerary perfectly suited to your interests.

### FAQ

**What can the guest service team arrange?**

Our Ladies and Gentlemen in Red can assist with restaurant reservations, transportation, sightseeing tours, shopping appointments, cultural experiences, event tickets, special celebrations, and personalised itineraries throughout Munich and Bavaria.

**Is butler service available?**

Yes. Butler service is available for selected suite categories and can be tailored to your individual requirements. Please contact our Guest Service Team for details and availability. Butler service is complimentary for guests of the Theresien, Maximilian, and Ludwig Suites. For other suite categories, this service is available at an additional charge.

**Can the hotel arrange opera tickets?**

Yes. Our guest service team can assist with securing tickets for the Bavarian State Opera and other cultural events, subject to availability.

**Can you prepare a suite for an anniversary?**

Absolutely. We can arrange personalised suite preparations including flowers, champagne, amenities, decorations, gifts, and other special touches to celebrate anniversaries and memorable occasions.
