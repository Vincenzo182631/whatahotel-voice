# FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the address of Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich?**

Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich

Maximilianstrasse 17

80539 Munich

Germany

**What are the check-in and check-out times?**

- Check-in is available from 15:00 hrs on the day of arrival
- Check-out is required by 12:00 hrs (noon) on the day of departure

Early check-in and late check-out are available for an additional charge, which can be added during the online booking process.

**What transfer services does Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich offer?**

We offer an airport transfer to and from Munich Airport (MUC). This transfer service can be booked as an add-on for an additional fee during the online booking process. For more transfer options, please contact us at T +49 89 2125 2110 or via email at guestservice.vierjahreszeiten@kempinski.com.

**What parking facilities are available at Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich?**

Our guests have access to an underground parking garage, with applicable fees.

**What are the breakfast hours, and what does the breakfast offer include?**

The breakfast buffet is served Monday to Friday from 06:30 hrs to 10:30 hrs and on Weekends from 06:30 hrs to 11:00 hrs at Schwarzreiter Tagesbar and Schwarzreiter Restaurant. It features a rich selection of international and regional dishes, both hot and cold.

**Are pets welcome and are there special services for them?**

Dogs are always welcome at Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich! Your furry friends will be provided with a blanket and a feeding bowl. Please note that an additional fee per dog per stay applies, which can be added during the online booking process.

Please note that pets are not allowed in our Schwarzreiter Tagesbar during breakfast, the Jahreszeiten Bar, the fine dining Schwarzreiter Restaurant or the Kempinski The Spa. You may visit the Schwarzreiter Tagesbar for lunch and dinner or our Jahreszeiten Lobby with your four-legged friends.

**What are the cancellation policies at Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich?**

For enquiries about cancellation policies or bookings, please contact our reservations office at T +49 89 2125 2700 or via email at reservation.vierjahreszeiten@kempinski.com.

**Do the rooms offer Wi-Fi and other modern amenities?**

All rooms at Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich are equipped with complimentary Wi-Fi. Other amenities vary depending on the room category and availability.

**Are there smoking areas at Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich?**

Smoking areas are located outside the hotel building, on balconies, and in our exclusive Cigar Lounge. Smoking rooms are available in the Superior Rooms and Premier Rooms, subject to availability.

**What are the spa opening hours, and what facilities are available?**

- Spa Reception & Treatments: 07:00 hrs to 21:00 hrs
- Pool Area: 07:00 hrs to 21:00 hrs
- Sauna Area: 07:00 hrs to 21:00 hrs
- Fitness Centre: 07:00 hrs to 21:00 hrs

We welcome our little guests from 09:00 hrs to 16:00 hrs in the pool area.

Explore our exclusive spa offers and our Day Day Spa Spa options.

**Is there a laundry service?**

Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich offers a laundry service for all hotel guests. For further information, please reach out to us at T +49 89 2125 2110  or via email at guestservice.vierjahreszeiten@kempinski.com.

**What can you do in and around Munich?**

Munich offers a diverse range of events, cultural experiences and historic landmarks year-round. The surrounding area, with its idyllic lakes, picturesque castles and nearby Alps, is also worth exploring.

The exclusive location of Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich on Maximilianstrasse provides the perfect starting point to explore the city and experience its unique charm.

**Can I customise special requests or requirements for my stay?**

Before your stay at Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich, our Guest Service Team will get in touch with you to accommodate your wishes and requests. For further assistance, please reach out to us at T +49 89 2125 2110  or via email at guestservice.vierjahreszeiten@kempinski.com.

**Do you offer accessible rooms?**

Although our facilities are not fully accessible, we are committed to ensuring you have a

pleasant stay. We are happy to make individual arrangements as far as our building

constraints permit, so please let us know if you require any assistance. Please contact us

prior to your arrival to discuss your requirements and how we can best assist you.
