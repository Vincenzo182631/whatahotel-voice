# Location

### Your Journey to Us

The Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich enjoys a peerless position directly on Maximilianstraße, placing you in the beating heart of Munich’s historic Old Town. This exclusive location redefines urban convenience: as a luxury hotel in central Munich, the city's most celebrated cultural landmarks are just a few minutes' walk away. Whether you are exploring the upscale boutiques of Maximilianstraße, taking a short stroll to the historic Bavarian State Opera, enjoying the idyllic greenery of the English Garden, or watching the world go by at the vibrant Marienplatz—everything is within easy reach. This address offers the perfect base for guests who demand both elegance and effortless accessibility.

### Walking Distances from the Hotel

**Maximilianstraße Boutiques** — Munich’s most exclusive luxury shopping mile awaits you directly outside the hotel entrance.

**Bavarian State Opera** (~3–4 min foot) — A short stroll west along Maximilianstraße brings you straight to Max-Joseph-Platz and the historic Nationaltheater.

**Marienplatz & The Old Town** (~8 min foot) — Heading directly west leads you to Marienplatz, where you can admire the famous Glockenspiel and explore Munich's historic center.

**English Garden** (~10 min foot) — Walk just a short distance north to immerse yourself in one of the world's largest and most beautiful urban parks.

**The Pinakotheken & Kunstareal**(~15–20 min foot / ~10 min tram) — The world-renowned museum quarter, housing the Alte, Neue, and Pinakothek der Moderne, is a brief walk or a direct tram ride northwest of the hotel.

**Lenbachhaus** (~25 min foot) — This prestigious art museum at Königsplatz, famous for its "Blue Rider" collection, is quickly and conveniently accessible from the hotel.

**Olympic Park & BMW Welt** (~15 min drive) — The legendary 1972 Olympic grounds and the modern BMW Welt in the north of the city are excellently connected, making them ideal for a half-day excursion.

**Allianz Arena** (~20 min drive) — The architectural masterpiece and home of FC Bayern Munich can be reached from nearby Marienplatz via a direct, no-transfer U-Bahn ride.

### Nearby Sightseeing

**Luxury Shopping on Maximilianstraße**

The finest fashion and haute couture await you right outside the hotel. The Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich sits directly on Maximilianstraße, framing the city's legendary luxury shopping mile as your very own doorstep. From global fashion houses to exclusive local designers—first-class shopping is seamlessly integrated into your stay.

**The Bavarian State Opera**

World-class culture is just moments away. Thanks to a short, 3-minute walk to the Bavarian State Opera (Nationaltheater) and the neighboring Residenz palace, you can enjoy an evening of world-class opera or ballet and top off the night perfectly in the Old Town.

**Discover Marienplatz & the Old Town**

Experience the rich history of the Bavarian capital with a leisurely walk to Marienplatz. As the natural starting point for exploring the Old Town, you are perfectly positioned to view the breathtaking New Town Hall, climb the tower of St. Peter’s Church, or sample local delicacies at the bustling Viktualienmarkt.

**Stroll Through the English Garden**

Escape the urban bustle in just 10 minutes on foot. At the English Garden, you can watch the famous river surfers at the Eisbachwelle, enjoy a traditional Bavarian pretzel at the Chinese Tower beer garden, or simply stroll along the scenic streams and take in the idyllic nature.

**World-Class Art at the Pinakotheken**

Art lovers can reach Munich’s Kunstareal district in just 15 to 20 minutes on foot, or via a short tram ride directly from our doorstep. The Alte Pinakothek, Neue Pinakothek, and Pinakothek der Moderne offer masterpieces spanning from the Renaissance to contemporary art—an invaluable advantage for a multi-day cultural getaway.

**The Lenbachhaus at Königsplatz**

Only a quick U-Bahn ride or a relaxed 25-minute walk separates you from the municipal gallery at the Lenbachhaus. Admire the world's largest collection of the "Blue Rider" expressionist movement, featuring works by Wassily Kandinsky and Gabriele Münter, all set inside a magnificent Florentine-style villa.

**Experience Olympic Park & BMW Welt**

A trip to the north of Munich is an absolute must during your stay. Explore the world-famous sweeping canopy roofs, take in the panoramic city views from the Olympic Tower, or step into the futuristic pavilions of the neighboring BMW Welt.

**Direct Route to the Allianz Arena**

Our central location is also the ideal starting point for sports enthusiasts. The stadium of FC Bayern Munich, the Allianz Arena, is about 12 kilometers away and can be reached by car in 20 minutes. Alternatively, simply walk over to Marienplatz and take the U6 underground line straight to the stadium without having to change trains.

### Directions

**A9 - Nuremberg**

At the end of the Autobahn, continue straight onto Mittlerer Ring Ost. Stay on the Mittlerer Ring, drive through two underpasses, and turn onto Ifflandstraße at Tucherpark. Continue straight until you cross Prinzregentenstraße, then keep heading straight onto Sternstraße until you reach Maximilianstraße. Turn right.

**A8 - Salzburg**

At the Munich Süd junction, continue straight toward Ramersdorf. From the end of the Autobahn, go straight onto Rosenheimer Straße toward Stadtmitte (city center). Drive straight ahead until you reach the Altstadtring. At Isartor, turn right onto Thomas-Wimmer-Ring, then turn left onto Maximilianstraße.

**A95 - Garmisch**

Take the Mittlerer Ring exit for Pasing, and turn right at Landsbergerstraße toward Stadtmitte. Follow Schwanthalerstraße until you hit Sonnenstraße, then turn right onto the Altstadtring. Continue straight until Maximilianstraße, then turn left.

**A96 - Lindau**

Drive to the end of the Autobahn and take the Donnersberger Brücke exit toward Stadtmitte. Follow Schwanthalerstraße to Sonnenstraße, turn right onto the Altstadtring, and then continue straight until you reach Maximilianstraße. Turn left.

**A8 - Augsburg / Stuttgart**

Head toward Stadtmitte and continue straight for about 20 minutes (Verdistraße - Amalienburgstraße - Menzingerstraße - Notburgastraße - Arnulfstraße) directly onto Sonnenstraße. Turn left onto the Altstadtring and continue onto Franz-Joseph-Strauß-Ring, then turn right onto Maximilianstraße.
