The suites at Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich are designed with expansive proportions and distinct architectural character, offering an experience that transcends standard accommodations. As a premier accommodation within the property, the **Ludwig Suite** offers 220 sq m of masterfully designed living space. ### **A House in Residence Since 1858**

The value of a stay at Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich is inextricably linked to its living history. Founded in 1858 at the explicit wish of King Maximilian II, the property was envisioned from its inception as the most distinguished grand hotel in the city.

This royal provenance delivers an intrinsic, non-replicable value. It lives in the grand architectural proportions of the lobby, the historic murals that adorn the public spaces, and a 160-year continuity of welcome. To stay here is to reside within a landmark of Bavarian heritage, experiencing a tradition of hospitality that has hosted monarchs, artists, and global statesmen for generations.

### **Munich’s Most Considered Address**

Situated at Maximilianstraße 17, the hotel occupies a coveted position on Munich’s premier boulevard of haute couture, fine art, and architectural culture. This central location places the city's finest landmarks within short walking distance, minimizing transit times and maximizing the depth of the travel experience.

- **The Bavarian State Opera** **:** Located close to the hotel, offering immediate access to world-class performances.
- **The Residenz** **:** The former royal palace of the Bavarian monarchs, located a 3-minute walk from the property.
- **Marienplatz** **:** Munich’s central square, featuring the New Town Hall, located a 7-minute walk away.
- **The English Garden** **:** One of Europe’s largest urban parks, accessible within a 10-minute stroll.
