# PURITY Chocolate

### PURITY Chocolate

PURITY is Ian Baker's chocolate brand, which stands for handmade chocolate art and delicacies. Ian is an award-winning patissier and chocolatier, with over 23 years experience in the industry worldwide and 10 years in Munich. Ian has turned his love of chocolate and creative craftsmanship into a unique chocolate experience.

Ian and his brand stand for handmade chocolates and chocolate products with PURE flavour, style and design. He uses traditional techniques and innovative approaches to produce chocolate products of the highest quality. The unique flavour combinations and artistic designs make PURITY's handmade chocolates a real treat for the senses.

PURITY attaches great importance to the quality of its ingredients. Starting with sustainably produced cocoa and chocolate, homemade nut pastes, ripe fruits and the best spices, Ian Baker also uses only natural and additive-free colours.

PURITY offers a range of artisan chocolates and seasonal creations made from the world's finest raw materials and regional ingredients, which can be enjoyed in the lobby bar of the Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich, picked up from the lobby showcase or ordered online. Discover the seductive flavours and unmistakable sophistication of PURITY chocolates and indulge in an unforgettable taste experience.
