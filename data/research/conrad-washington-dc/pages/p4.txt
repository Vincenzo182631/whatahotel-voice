# **Dine**

Washington, DC has become a culinary destination and dining at the Conrad is more than a mere reservation—our fresh and innovative offerings allow our guests the opportunity to enjoy an exceptional experience.

Estuary, our signature restaurant located on the third floor next to the atrium, offers breakfast, lunch and dinner served in the lounge and main dining room overlooking the Park at CityCenterDC. We also offer in-room dining.

### **estuary**

Estuary celebrates contemporary American cuisine, embracing the rich bounty of the Chesapeake region.Chef Arturo brings his Mexican heritage into every dish, adding bold, flavorful twists to traditional Mid-Atlantic fare.
