## **Welcome** to dc

We are very pleased to welcome you to the Conrad Washington, DC!

With an art-forward approach to contemporary luxury, the Conrad invites you to experience DC through the lens of a local resident of the District and enjoy the culture and color that makes our nation’s capital one of the most incredible cities in the world. As a part of the Hilton family of hotels, we are committed to striving toward sustainability in travel and hospitality through our daily operations, as well as our LEED Gold Certified building. Please find more information about our sustainability story here.

Located in downtown DC at CityCenter , it is convenient to the most elevated shopping and dining options in DC. The Conrad is a few blocks from the Convention Center and a short walk to the White House and several DC attractions.

Please join us for breakfast, lunch and dinner in our signature restaurant, Estuary, or grab a cocktail at the Estuary Bar and enjoy views of the Park at CityCenterDC. During the warm season, enjoy unparalleled views of Downtown DC, the US Capitol, the Washington Monument on our rooftop, Summit at Conrad, combined with a relaxed atmosphere and forward-thinking offerings.

We look forward to welcoming you to the Conrad on your next visit to DC!
