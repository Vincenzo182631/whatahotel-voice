# **Sakura** Club

With our dedicated team of Ambassadors, curated culinary offerings and top tier service that extends beyond your stay, Sakura Club is an experience unlike any other – one where you can immerse yourself in exceptional care at every turn. Take a 360 tour.

### **Exclusive** Service

Our Sakura Club Ambassadors ensure that your experience goes beyond top tier service by personalizing your entire stay.

### **Culinary** Offerings

Crafted by our private Sakura Club chef, our culinary offerings include freshly made and convenient options for breakfast and dinner. Each day also brings a curated selection of beer and wine.

### **Personalized** Experience

As part of CityCenterDC, Conrad DC aligns with our luxury retail neighbors to offer our Sakura Club guests a truly immersive experience. Please ask your Sakura Club Ambassador for more information.

Guests staying in a Sakura Club room or suite enjoy complimentary access to the Sakura Club. Access is not available as a Hilton Honors upgrade or as part of the Hilton Honors Diamond member benefits.
