# Madi hiyaa

## a one-of-a-kind dining experience at Madi Hiyaa

Discover an unparalleled dining experience at Madi Hiyaa, an exclusive overwater restaurant nestled in the heart of the Maldives. Perched on our overwater jetty, this extraordinary culinary haven promises breathtaking sunset views, a serene sea saltwater pool, and a horizon that will captivate your senses.

**Japanese Cuisine with a Maldivian Twist**

Prepare to be enchanted as you savour authentic Japanese cuisine infused with a unique Maldivian twist. Our talented chefs skillfully blend locally sourced ingredients to create dishes that are both delicious and sustainable, a true testament to our commitment to preserving the beauty of the Maldives.

### Immerse Yourself in Nature

As you dine at Madi Hiyaa, relish the gentle caress of the ocean breeze, adding a touch of magic to your culinary journey. The restaurant over the water allows you to fully immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the Maldives, making every moment truly special.

### Sustainable Bamboo Architecture

Madi Hiyaa's sustainable architecture, meticulously crafted entirely from bamboo, sets the stage for a dining experience like no other. Every corner of our restaurant over the water showcases our unwavering dedication to sustainability, ensuring that your meal is not only memorable but also eco-conscious.
