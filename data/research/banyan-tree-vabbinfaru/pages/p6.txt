# Dining

## Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru

## Indulge in Local Flavours and International Culinary Delights

Dining in Maldives at Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru runs across four venues, linked by an exclusive dine-around concept that invites you to savour the flavours of the Maldives and beyond.

Our restaurants in Maldives span three cuisines. Indulge in the authentic tastes of Thailand at Saffron, recognised by the MICHELIN Guide Thailand 2024.

Discover the art of Japanese cuisine at Madi Hiyaa, an exclusive overwater restaurant built from sustainable bamboo. For a garden-to-table experience, Sangu Garden presents dishes crafted with fresh, locally sourced ingredients. For guests who prefer more familiar flavours, Naiboli Bar also serves an international Western menu.

As the day transitions to night, unwind at Naiboli Bar, our beach bar in the Maldives, with craft cocktails, beachfront views and live Bodu Beru drumming every Friday.

## Dine-Around Offer

Discover Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru’s Dine-Around Offer, featuring Thai signature cuisines at the Michelin-recognized Saffron, authentic Maldivian delicacies at Sangu Garden, and refined Japanese culinary artistry at award-winning Madi Hiyaa. Elevate your stay with all-inclusive package from exclusive dining offers, wellness treatment to Diving & Water sports activities.
