# An Invitation to an Exquisite Lifestyle

## Accommodations

Blending local design and international craftsmanship, our accommodation in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia includes 83 guestrooms, with 24 suites, offering a spacious retreat with captivating VIA Riyadh views. Decorated with a neutral palette, inviting modern interiors express contemporary luxury through custom furnishings and bespoke artwork inspired by the location’s rich heritage. High ceilings and glass curtain windows fill each generously sized layout and large en-suite with natural light, creating a calm haven with a chic aesthetic. Choose from a bespoke collection of suites for luxury living, including the 225m2 Royal Suite, thoughtfully considered and meticulously crafted for a comfortable sleep in peaceful surroundings. The hallmark of refined travel, the St. Regis Butler enhances every stay with personalized service that transcends the ordinary.

Unequalled Opulence

### The Royal Suite

Ushering in a new age of luxury to Riyadh’s glittering scene, the Royal Suite with signature St. Regis Butler service, is a rare city residence that exceeds expectation. Flowing across 225m2, the layout includes two-bedrooms with walk-in wardrobes, separate lounge, study, bar and dining area, where magnificent glass curtains enhance the open plan aesthetic with natural light and compelling VIA Riyadh views. Artfully representing a rich history and progressive future, the design places local pearl alongside bespoke artworks and sculptural pieces to reference the kingdom’s evolving culture.

Exclusive Environment

## John Jacob Astor Suite

Relax in the refined environs of the John Jacob Astor Suite where elegant décor creates a sumptuous experience. Defined by impressive Salmani architecture, the spacious 124m2 footprint, features expansive floor-to-ceiling windows that infuse the setting with natural light and exquisite city-scape views. Styled to reflect a chic residential aesthetic, the accommodation includes a richly appointed living room, where custom artworks and sculptural pieces add compelling points of interest. Dedicated to relaxation, the separate master bedroom is furnished with king-size bed, dressed with impeccable Frette linen, whilst the marble-clad en-suite bathroom offers luxurious sanctuary.

## The St. Regis Suite

A modern interpretation of Riyadh’s rich legacy, each The St. Regis Suite provides a meaningful backdrop for rest and sanctuary. Occupying 104m2 of sumptuous interiors, the accommodation is styled with muted tones, precious local materials and custom artworks, that add a distinctive focal point. Spacious and light filled, the living room is furnished with curved seating, that accommodates a custom crescent shaped desk. Smart design extends to the master bedroom, where a sliding wall reveals the marble-clad bathroom with standalone bathtub and separate rainfall shower.
