### An Outdoor Pool

Gracefully positioned above the city’s most luxurious district, The pool offers a serene escape with captivating views. This elevated outdoor haven invites guests to unwind in plush loungers, enjoy handcrafted refreshments, and bask in the golden glow of Riyadh’s sun.

### Panoramic Gym

Outfitted with the latest Technogym equipment, this private space with wonderful views of the city invites guests to pursue their fitness goals with intention. Every detail is designed to inspire balance and vitality within an atmosphere of quiet refinement.

### Thermal Experience Area

Where heat and harmony meet, embark on a curated journey of rejuvenation. The thermal suite features a steam room, dry sauna, Jacuzzi, and heated relaxation beds—each designed to soothe the senses, release tension, and elevate your wellness ritual in refined tranquility.

## Where Serenity Meets Signature Design

Step into a sanctuary where award-winning designer Kristina Zanic brings the story of “Lines in the Sand” to life. At The St. Regis Spa Riyadh, arched forms, rippled glass, and warm desert hues blend into a tranquil journey. Natural elements, soft lighting, and flowing lines echo the beauty of Saudi landscapes—offering guests a refined and restorative escape. Honored by the Interior Design Best of Year Awards 2024, this spa is where culture, design, and wellness meet in harmony.
