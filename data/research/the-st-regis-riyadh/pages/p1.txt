# Welcome to The St. Regis Riyadh

## Live exquisite at our five-star hotel in Riyadh

Cast from ancient Tuwaiq mountain stone, our five-star hotel in Riyadh embodies the capital's unique identity through an entirely modern aesthetic. As the debut St. Regis in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, the hotel introduces exceptional service and signature rituals to a new legacy of guests. Our business hotel in Riyadh’s diplomatic quarter is close to King Abdulaziz Historical Center, Al-Masmak Fortress, and is the only hotel within the rarefied vicinity of VIA Riyadh, where international designers, restaurants, and cinema come together. Enriched with premier accommodations, 83 guestrooms and suites blend subtle tradition and contemporary minimalism with modern comfort. A destination in itself, the offering includes two distinctive culinary experiences, choice event spaces, and the city’s largest wellness venue, the innovative St. Regis SPA.

Unique Design

### Celebrating the Exceptional

Inspired by La Dolce Vita, and its synergy with the prosperous Saudi-Arabian lifestyle, the property features interiors designed by esteemed studio, Kristina Zanic. Styled to embody the energy and glamor associated with life in Riyadh, the aesthetic exudes refined luxury through an all-neutral palette, offset by eclectic bursts of color and soft lighting. High ceilings and sweeping chandeliers reflect the innate sense of grandeur, whilst elegant angles and long lines serve to forge fresh dialogue between each thoughtfully curated space. Poised at the heart of the city’s social scene, the hotel places a contemporary lens over the kingdom’s rich heritage, to unveil a bold new approach that fosters authenticity and social connection. Emblematic of the rare and impeccable, The St. Regis Riyadh is the city’s best address for exquisite living.

The St. Regis Hallmark

### The St. Regis Butler Service

Allow the St. Regis Butler to craft indulgent moments through refined rituals and orchestrated experiences that go beyond expectation. Introducing this signature offering to Saudi Arabia, guests benefit from an unmatched commitment to excellence that is bespoke and discreet. Whether it’s packing and unpacking luggage, delivering beverages or garment pressing, your butler is on hand to personalize every aspect.

### Sabrage Evening Ritual

Celebrating the transition from day to night, the signature Sabrage Ritual takes place in the sophisticated environs of the grand Lobby, beneath the impressive spiral staircase. A time-honored ceremony, it is the ideal prelude to the evening ahead. Performed by a skilled St. Regis ambassador, with saber in hand, guests are invited to join in a toast and raise a glass to life’s most cherished moments.

### Afternoon Tea

Served in the relaxing environs of the Astor Library, our signature afternoon tea ritual, served in delicate bone china, includes a delectable selection of savory and sweet options, including pastries, cakes and freshly baked scones. Guests can also enjoy a range of specialty coffees, the ideal accompaniment for the range of decadent delicacies on offer.

The St. Regis Riyadh Spa

### The City’s Unrivaled Wellbeing Sanctuary

Featuring a generous 1,200m2 footprint, unmatched in the city, The St. Regis Spa Riyadh brings a unique wellness journey to the destination. Enriched with Saudi Arabian flair, the celebrated The St. Regis SPA concept is elevated by precious local elements that infuse the space with glamorous appeal. Alongside eight dedicated treatment rooms and a spa suite, the exceptional facilities include separate male and female thermal areas, with traditional sauna, herbal steam room, snow shower, ice fountain and heated lounges. Designed to soothe the senses, the relaxation room is furnished with warm fireplace and captivating chandelier. Innovative AMRA products are the foundation for world-class treatments, including bespoke creations like, The 400 Journey and international therapies, such as HydraFacial. An overarching focus on personalization sets the venue apart for its holistic approach that places the guest at the heart of the experience.

Via Riyadh

## A New Era of Luxury

A location for tastemakers, The St. Regis Riyadh makes its glittering debut at the region’s haven for superior leisure – the acclaimed VIA Riyadh. Combining world class dining, designer shopping, elevated accommodation and high-end entertainment, this prime destination is where luminaries gather to appreciate fine living. An inspired blend of Salmani architecture and urban style, VIA Riyadh has been designed to complement the age-old identity of the capital, preserving its unique heritage whilst ushering in a new age of exclusivity. Offering a select lifestyle, coveted international restaurants, including Sexy Fish, Scott’s and Raoul’s, sit alongside world-famous brands and local designers to carve out an entirely new proposition in splendor.

Edge of the World

## Unparalleled Natural Beauty

Enjoy captivating views from the pinnacle of the 800km-long Tuwaiq mountain range and take in the magnificence of Saudi Arabia’s timeless landscape. Located 100km from the center of Riyadh, this remote aspect is a draw for hikers, mountaineers and nature lovers looking for a one-of-a-kind destination that challenges and impresses, in equal measure. Journey to the summit at either sunrise or sunset and experience the best this spectacular setting has to offer, as the sky captivates with its fusion of red and orange hues.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Riyadh?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Riyadh is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Riyadh allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Riyadh.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Riyadh?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Riyadh. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Riyadh have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Riyadh has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Riyadh booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Riyadh?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Riyadh is King Khaled International Airport (RUH). RUH is located approximately 45.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Riyadh have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Riyadh has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
