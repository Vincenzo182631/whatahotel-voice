Nestled on the heights of Gustavia, overlooking the iconic Shell Beach, Fouquet’s Saint-Barth embodies an art of living where luxury and sensuality meet the gentle charm of the Caribbean. With just 16 suites and a 5-bedroom villa, this exceptional property is a true haven of intimacy and elega...nce. By day, enjoy highly-coveted complimentary beach chairs at our seaside partner, La Guérite Beach. In the evening, immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of the Maison and savor the finest cuts of meat in the world, along with Beefbar’s signature cuisine, at the heart of the property. To extend this moment of well-being, indulge in a timeless escape at the Spa, in exclusive partnership with Biologique Recherche.See more

### What are the check-in and check-out times? What should I expect upon arrival?

**Check-in:**

Check-in time is from 3:00 PM. Upon arrival at Fouquet's Saint-Barth, our team will greet you for a discreet and personalized welcome. Please present a valid ID and the credit card used for your booking. If your suite or villa is not yet ready, we’ll gladly take care of your luggage until it is.

**Check-out:**

Check-out is scheduled at 12:00 PM. For late check-out requests, please contact the reception. This service is subject to availability.

### Can I request a late check-out?

Late check-out may be granted upon request and subject to availability. Please contact our front desk to make the necessary arrangements.

### What is the hotel’s cancellation policy?

The cancellation policy varies depending on the stay period and the selected rate. Non-refundable rates are charged in full at the time of booking and are not eligible for any refund. In the event of a late cancellation or no-show, fees may apply in accordance with the conditions of the chosen rate.

### How can I arrange an airport transfer to or from Saint-Barth?

To arrange a private transfer, please contact our concierge at concierge-stbarth@fouquets.com

Kindly include your flight number, arrival and departure times, number of guests, and luggage details.

### Does the hotel offer valet parking?

Fouquet's Saint-Barth offers valet parking upon request. To plan this service in advance or get more details, please contact our concierge team.

### Are pets allowed at Fouquet's Saint-Barth?

Pets are welcome at our luxury hotel in Saint-Barth, subject to specific conditions. Pets must be kept on a leash in public areas and may not be left alone in the room. For safety reasons, our staff will not enter accommodations occupied by an unattended animal.

Additional fees may apply. Pets are not allowed in the restaurants, bars, spa, or fitness center, except for certified service animals.

### Are cribs or extra beds available?

Cribs are available free of charge upon request. Select suites and villas can accommodate an extra bed, subject to availability and a supplement. Please contact our reservations team for details.

### Do you offer connecting or adjacent rooms?

Some suites or villas may be combined to accommodate families or groups. Please contact us at reservations-stbarth@fouquets.com to explore the available options.

### What dining options are available at Fouquet's Saint-Barth?

Our 5-star hotel in Saint-Barth offers a range of unforgettable culinary experiences:

La Guérite Beach: from April through August 2026, we are delighted to partner with the iconic La Guérite Beach.

Guests of Fouquet’s Saint-Barth are invited to enjoy the ultimate seaside escape at St. Jean Beach, complete with complimentary beach chairs and seamless round-trip transportation or valet service.

Beefbar: A refined concept that combines exceptional meats (Wagyu, Black Angus, Kobe) with fresh seafood and plant-based creations in a sophisticated setting.

Beefbar's bar: Creative cocktails and reimagined street food are served all day with stunning views over the ocean.

Please consult our website or contact the concierge for opening hours or to make a reservation.

### Is room service available 24/7?

Our room service is available 24 hours a day. A refined in-room dining menu is available for all suites, villas, or private terraces. Please note that an additional charge may apply for orders placed between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM.
