# Luxurious Bangkok Hotel Rooms with Modern Amenities

## Rooms & Suites

The 176 rooms and 52 suites at The St. Regis Bangkok showcase unparalleled elegance, space, and luxury. Overlooking verdant golfing greens and regal skyscrapers, our family hotel rooms in downtown Bangkok provide a welcoming retreat from the city's non-stop energy. Enjoy a tranquil night's rest on a feather-soft mattress with 300-thread-count linens and down-filled pillows. Refresh your body and spirit...See More each morning under a rainforest shower, part of your marble-lined bathroom with a separate soaking tub. Treat yourself to luxury in-room amenities during your Bangkok stay, including an LED television, complimentary Wi-Fi, a workstation with a media hub, and a well-stocked minibar. While in residence, hotel guests enjoy discreet signature St. Regis Butler service, 24 hours a day.See Less

Featured Stay

### Caroline Astor Suite

Offering a contemporary haven for the discerning guest, our Caroline Astor Suites exude comfortable elegance, complete with spectacular views. Every element of the Caroline Astor Suite is designed to provide an extraordinary experience high above the city of Bangkok. With a corner location and panoramic views of the sweeping cityscape and verdant parkland through floor-to-ceiling windows, the distinguished 90-115 square meter suites offer a unique, residential-style retreat for relaxing or entertaining. Enjoy a touch of opulence with our luxury accommodation in downtown Bangkok.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Bangkok have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Bangkok have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The St. Regis Bangkok?

The largest occupancy room at The St. Regis Bangkok is the The Owner's Penthouse with a maximum occupancy of 8.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The St. Regis Bangkok?

Yes, all rooms at The St. Regis Bangkok permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
