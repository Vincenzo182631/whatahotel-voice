## Explore Bangkok by BTS & Beyond

Experience Bangkok from The St. Regis Bangkok, where an exceptional location puts the city’s shopping, parks, art, entertainment and neighborhoods within easy reach. Whether you are looking for things to do in Bangkok, luxury shopping, cultural attractions or local experiences, explore the city effortlessly by BTS Skytrain, taxi or boat.

## Taste Bangkok: Local Cuisine, Riverside & Creative Neighborhoods

Discover Bangkok through its food. From the bustling streets of Chinatown to historic shophouses, floating markets and sophisticated riverside destinations, Bangkok offers an extraordinary culinary journey. During your stay at The St. Regis Bangkok, explore the flavors and neighborhoods that make Thai food and Bangkok local cuisine an essential part of the city experience.

## Discover Thai Culture Through History

Experience the history and culture of Bangkok through magnificent temples, royal landmarks, museums and historic neighborhoods. From the spiritual heart of the old city to the traditional communities along the Chao Phraya River, discover the stories that have shaped Thailand.

### Bangkok's Must Try Eats

Although famous for its old world charm and eclectic hustle and bustle, Bangkok is also quite popular for its cuisine. Whether it be classic Thai favorites, fresh seafood, piping hot noodles or sweets, the Bangkok food scene has travelers covered. Delicious meals at real value can be found everywhere in Bangkok, from street corners to department stores to five star eateries. Here, we’ve compiled some of our favorites. Get out there and explore our wonderful city, but don’t forget to enjoy eating!

### Luxury Shopping & City Experiences

Explore Bangkok’s luxury lifestyle from Chidlom to Silom by BTS Skytrain. Discover Gaysorn Village and Central Chidlom, then Siam Paragon and CentralWorld for shopping, dining and entertainment. Continue to Phrom Phong’s Emporium, EmQuartier and EmSphere, explore Thong Lo’s vibrant cafés and restaurants, or discover One Bangkok and Central Dusit Park in Silom.

### Chinatown and Song Wat

Begin in Chinatown Bangkok, where Yaowarat Road comes alive with traditional Chinese-Thai cuisine, street food and local delicacies. Explore bustling food stalls and historic shophouses before discovering Song Wat, a riverside neighborhood where heritage architecture meets a new generation of cafés, restaurants and creative spaces.

### River City Bangkok

For a blend of art, culture and riverside dining, visit River City Bangkok. Explore art exhibitions, galleries and creative spaces before enjoying the atmosphere along the Chao Phraya River. Whether you are searching for the best local food in Bangkok, authentic Thai flavors or sophisticated riverside dining, let the city’s diverse culinary landscape become part of your stay.

### Temple of Dawn (Wat Arun)

Cross the Chao Phraya River to Wat Arun (Temple of Dawn), recognised for its distinctive riverside prang decorated with intricate ceramic and porcelain details. The temple offers one of Bangkok’s most memorable cultural and architectural experiences.

### Bangkok’s Green Escapes

Escape the energy of the city with a visit to Lumphini Park, one of Bangkok’s best-known urban parks, conveniently accessible from BTS Sala Daeng, or discover the tranquil landscapes of Benchakitti Forest Park, easily reached from BTS Asok or BTS Phrom Phong. Here, wetlands, elevated walkways and lush greenery create a refreshing contrast to the surrounding skyline.

### Talad Noi

Continue to Talad Noi, one of Bangkok’s most atmospheric historic neighborhoods. Wander through narrow lanes lined with traditional homes, colorful street art, old shophouses and hidden cafés, offering a fascinating glimpse into the city’s multicultural heritage.

### Temple of the Emerald Buddha

Begin your cultural journey at Temple of the Emerald Buddha and The Grand Palace, among Bangkok’s most important historic landmarks. Admire the intricate architecture and craftsmanship of the Grand Palace complex and visit Wat Phra Kaew, home to Thailand’s revered Emerald Buddha.

### Museum Siam

Explore the story of Thailand at Museum Siam, an interactive museum dedicated to Thai identity, culture and society. Its engaging exhibitions offer an accessible introduction to the evolution of Thailand and its people.

### Art, Culture & Entertainment

Discover Bangkok’s creative side at BACC near BTS National Stadium, or explore SEA LIFE Bangkok Ocean World beneath Siam Paragon from BTS Siam. Enjoy panoramic city views at King Power Mahanakhon near BTS Chong Nonsi, or visit the Erawan Shrine near BTS Chidlom, a spiritual landmark in the heart of the city.

### ICONSIAM & Asiatique The Riverfront

Experience the Chao Phraya River at ICONSIAM, Bangkok’s luxury riverside shopping destination. Discover leading international and Thai brands, acclaimed restaurants and cultural experiences overlooking the river. As the sun sets, continue to Asiatique The Riverfront, where shopping, dining and entertainment come together in a lively riverside setting.

### Wat Phra Chetuphon

Visit Wat Phra Chetuphon Wimon Mangkhalaram Rajwaramahawihan, also known as Wat Pho, one of Bangkok’s most historic temples. Famous for its monumental Reclining Buddha, the temple is also closely associated with traditional Thai massage and Thai knowledge.

### Jim Thompson House Museum

Step into the world of Thai silk and traditional architecture at the Jim Thompson House. Discover a collection of historic Thai houses surrounded by lush tropical gardens and learn about Jim Thompson’s legacy and his contribution to bringing Thai silk to an international audience.
