# Welcome to The St. Regis Bangkok

## Luxury 5-Star Hotel in Bangkok, Thailand with Butler Service

Experience the splendor of Thailand and the Siam area from The St. Regis Bangkok, our luxury hotel in downtown Bangkok. Find us near many of Bangkok's elite business institutions, superb local restaurants, boutique shops, and attractions like the Erawan Shrine, Siam Paragon, Central World, and Lumphini Park. Travel throughout Bangkok on the BTS Skytrain with direct access to Rajadamri Station. After you explore the Bangkok city center, pamper yourself with a 5-star spa treatment at Bangkok LIFE Longevity & Biohacking Hub, lounge on our hotel's 15th-floor outdoor pool bar, or sip a handcrafted cocktail in one of eight restaurants and bars. Then retreat to a luxury Siam hotel suite in Bangkok, Thailand, featuring multi-bedroom layouts, Thai-inspired décor, 5-star amenities, and our signature 24-hour St. Regis Butler service. Revel in unobstructed views of the greenery of The Royal Bangkok Sports Club and the skyline of Siam Bangkok from our luxury hotel.

## The Art of Sharing

In its first location outside Switzerland, IGNIV by Andreas Caminada has spread its wings & landed to create a new nest at The St. Regis Bangkok. Imagined by Andreas Caminada, the Swiss founding chef of IGNIV and head chef of the internationally acclaimed 3-Michelin-starred restaurant Schloss Schauenstein in Switzerland, IGNIV is all about a unique fine dining sharing experience, where exquisite lunch and dinner experience are composed with the connoisseur in mind and crafted with fresh, seasonal ingredients, bearing the whimsical signature of its creator, led by head chef Dominik Falk.

## Concierge Team

Led by our Chief Concierge, Ms. Kanda Chukachon, a member of Les Clefs d’Or and Golden Key Holder, our concierge team is always available with the knowledge and desire to meet your needs and exceed your expectations at our luxury 5-star hotel in Bangkok City.

Concierge Service

Our multilingual concierge team provides an array of services to accommodate business and leisure interests. From dinner reservations to floral arrangements, golf tee times to local area information and tickets, no assignment is too small for our knowledgeable concierges. We are also pleased to coordinate private limousine services, courier and message services.

Guests may also rely on our concierge staff to arrange for professional babysitting and childcare services with trusted providers.

Delights Delivered

Hotel Policies

Booking Policies

**Age Requirement Policy**

All guests of the hotel must be 18 years of age and above. Alternatively, guests below the age of 18 should be accompanied by adults. The hotel respectfully reserves the right to check identification.

**Connecting Room Policy**

The hotel is pleased to offer connecting room for your convenience. Please make your request as early as possible, to ensure availability.

**Extra Bedding Policy**

It is our pleasure to offer extra beds upon request for an additional fee. Complimentary cribs are available.

**Family Plan Policy**

Children age 11 and below sleep for free in the existing bedding of a room shared with a paying adult. Rollaways and child rollaways may incur extra charges. Child breakfast charge applies for children from 4 to 11 years of age. Adult breakfast charge applies for children 12 years of age and above.

**Room Policy**

The exact layout, furnishings and view from the guestroom may vary depending on the room or suite assigned upon check-in. It is our pleasure to accommodate any specific requests, subject to availability.

Check-In & Check-Out Policies

**Early Arrival Policy**

We are pleased to guarantee early arrival. To guarantee a room for early arrival before 3 pm, we request that guests provide one night’s room charge. Please make your request as early as possible, to ensure availability.

**Early Departure Policy**

We are pleased to accommodate early departures upon request. At this time, guests may change their departure date without a penalty if their rate-plan permits.

**ID Requirement**

Valid photo identification is required at check-in.

**Late Checkout Policy**

We are pleased to accommodate late check-outs upon request. In the case of a late check-out before 6 pm, an additional half-day rate will apply. For check-outs after 6 pm, a full-day rate will apply.

General Policies

**Alcohol Policy**

We are pleased to serve alcoholic beverages to guests over 20 years old (with valid identification).

**No Durian Policy**

Please note that durian fruit is not allowed in any hotel area. A fee of THB 8,000 will be assessed if in possession of a durian.

**Package Handling Policy**

We are happy to accept packages for arriving guests with a secured reservation- otherwise, packages cannot be accepted. Please note that for security purposes, contents must be checked and declared.

**Pet Policy**

While we greatly appreciate our guests’ four-legged companions, the hotel does not accept pets at this time.

**Pool Policy**

The hotel is pleased to extend the use of our pool exclusively to hotel guests. Visitors are not permitted in the pool area. Children of age 13 to 16 are welcome to use the Exercise Room and spa facilities when accompanied by an adult. Children of age 12 and under are not permitted to use the Exercise Room and spa facilities.

No diving and jumping is allowed. There is a pool attendant but the hotel does not provide lifeguards.

**Visa/Passport Policy**

We request that all non-local residents present passports and immigration entry cards upon arrival, according to Thai immigration laws.

Health & Well-Being Policies

Smoking is not permitted in all our guest rooms and suites.

Please note that as per Thai law, smoking is not permitted in restaurants, bars, meeting rooms, or public areas of the hotel.

A recovery fee will be charged to any guest who violates the smoking policy. This fee is necessary to cover the extensive cleaning processes required to restore the room to a smoke-free condition.

\- THB 20,000 per stay for Deluxe Room

\- THB 30,000 per stay for Grand Deluxe Room

\- THB 35,000 per stay for Metropolitan Suite

\- THB 50,000 per stay for One-Bedroom Suite

\- THB 75,000 per stay for Two-Bedroom Suite

\- THB 150,000 per stay for The Royal Suite and The Owner’s Penthouse

Payment Policies

**Check Policy**

We regret that checks are not accepted as a method of payment. However, we are pleased to accept cashier’s checks. Check cashing service is only available for traveler's checks. Valid identification is required. Other conditions may apply.

**Gift Certification Policy**

We are pleased to accept gift certificates and will do our best to accommodate guests’ requested dates for redemption. However, requested dates will be subject to availability at the time of booking, unless otherwise stated on the gift certificate.

**Payment Policy**

We are pleased to accept cash, credit cards, and bank transfers as forms of payment.

Credit card authorization forms are accepted. Please contact the hotel directly to make arrangements.

All guests booking a prepaid reservation with a credit card are required to present the credit card used for the payment at check-in for verification.

Other Services

Beverage Service

Upon arrival, allow a St. Regis Butler to arrange coffee or tea service in your room. And if you wish, a St. Regis Butler will arrive after your morning wake-up call to draw your shades, deliver your coffee or tea as well as the day’s newspaper and weather forecast.

Unpacking & Packing Services

Allow a St. Regis Butler to take care of the unpacking and storage of your luggage—so that you can simply relax and enjoy. Similarly, before you depart, simply contact the Butler Service Desk to dispatch a butler to assist with gathering and folding your garments to prepare your luggage for the journey home.

Garment Pressing

Once unpacked, simply let your St. Regis Butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. This gracious service includes pressing of two garments per person, per day.

For all other wishes or requests, St. Regis Butler Service is pleased to assist.

eButler

During your stay, whether you are inside or outside the hotel, St. Regis Butler Service is accessible at any hour via chat using Marriott Bonvoy application or email at Butler.bangkok@stregis.com. Simply send your request and allow our Butlers to assist.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Bangkok?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Bangkok is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Bangkok allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Bangkok.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Bangkok?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Bangkok. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Bangkok have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Bangkok has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Bangkok booking.
