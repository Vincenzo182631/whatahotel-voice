The Drawing Room & The St. Regis Bar

### Afternoon Tea Indulgence

Our timeless Afternoon Tea is a decadent delight. Overlooking the golf course’s verdant view, convene for sweet and savory bites, with TWG tea or coffee. The experience is offered every day from 2:30 PM to 4:00 PM in The Drawing Room & The St. Regis Bar. The tradition of afternoon tea dates back to the 18th century when the Duchess of Bedford of Great Britain established an afternoon snack which soon became a social get-together in middle and upper class households. This ritual was adopted by Mrs. Astor – the mother of the founder of The St. Regis.

## The St. Regis Bangkok

Discover gastronomic pleasures and fine dining in our eight restaurants and bars in downtown Bangkok. We serve exceptional culinary experiences, from extraordinary buffets and authentic Thai feasts to modern European sharing-style dining experiences. Along with signature rituals including Siam Mary and The St. Regis Afternoon Tea, our offerings cover a wide range of Bangkok dining options.

Spicy, Zesty, Sweet & Salty

## Siam Mary

The Bloody Mary is the signature cocktail of the St. Regis brand, with each hotel crafting its own local interpretation of the libation. Discover the Siam Mary: spicy, zesty, sweet and salty ingredients combine for undeniably Thai refreshment at The St. Regis Bangkok.

VIU

### Signature Sunday Brunch

Our new Sunday Brunch concept presents Mediterranean-inspired “At the Table” menu, featuring a fresh seafood tower - served directly to your table - consisting of French oysters, Alaskan king crab, Boston lobster claw and Argentinian red prawns, alongside artisanal Italian cold cuts and cheese, freshly made pizzas, and homemade pastas. Rotating unlimited main courses such as Boston grilled lobster, Wagyu delights and more, are freshly prepared and cooked à la minute. Explore all-you-can-eat fresh Japanese sashimi and sushi, Thai classics, handcrafted desserts and a tableside tiramisu special.
