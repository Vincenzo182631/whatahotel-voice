Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton Naples, Tiburón
Where Festive Moments Become Cherished Memories.
The Ritz-Carlton Naples, Tiburón invites families and friends to embrace the spirit of the season and create timeless holiday memories that will be cherished for years to come. Memories are brought to life through enchanting experiences from a whimsical Grand Christmas Buffet, to treasured favorites like Breakfast with Santa, Gingerbread House Decorating, and the ever-festive Ugly Sweater Party. Let forever memories unfold and new ones begin in unforgettable ways.
Featured Celebrations
Embrace the wonder of holidays in paradise at The Ritz-Carlton, Naples, Tiburón, where the spirit of the season shines brilliantly. From elegantly dressed spaces to festive touches and glittering lights, every moment is thoughtfully crafted to celebrate the joy of togetherness and inspire lasting holiday moments.

Festivities
A Season of Wonders
Tiburón Turkey Trot
Guests are invited to walk or run through the picturesque Tiburón neighborhood during the annual Turkey Trot, benefiting Children’s Miracle Network. Welcoming participants of all ages and fitness levels, the 5K follows Tiburón’s scenic pathways and concludes with refreshments. All entrants will receive a commemorative shirt and medal!
Reserve Now
Tiburón Thanksgiving Table
Overlooking the beautiful, lush grounds of Tiburón, join Chef Jared Young and his team of culinary experts, for a signature dining experience in our new Swaying Palm event venue. Enjoy Thanksgiving classics family-style as well as curated culinary presentations in intimate surroundings. Celebrate the bounty of the fall harvest with those closest to you!
Reserve Now
Tiburón Gingerbread Workshop
Create sweet memories with loved ones and celebrate the wonder of the holidays at our enchanting Gingerbread School. Under the guidance of our expert pastry chefs, guests of all ages will embrace the magic of the season as they craft their own gingerbread masterpieces, blending creativity, festive spirit, and just the right touch of holiday sweetness.
Reserve Now
Ugly Sweater Cocktail Social
Embrace the spirit of the season with an elegant evening of festive revelry on the picturesque Palmera patio. Gather friends, raise a glass to the holidays, and make a grand entrance on the red carpet showcasing your most unforgettable seasonal attire. The evening culminates with the crowning of the coveted “Best Ugly Holiday Sweater in Naples,” all amid an atmosphere of spirited celebration and holiday cheer.
Reserve Now
Twelve Days of Holiday Cocktails
Make every day leading up to Christmas a celebration at Palmera. Our festive countdown invites you to discover a new holiday-inspired cocktail each day, artfully crafted with exceptional ingredients and the flavors of the season. Sip, savor, and raise a glass as we toast to the warmth, joy, and timeless magic of the holidays.
Reserve Now
Tiburón Holiday Buffet (Christmas Dec. 25)
Embrace the magic of Christmas Day with an unforgettable celebration. Indulge in a premier Christmas Day culinary extravaganza showcasing thoughtfully curated holiday favorites, masterfully prepared by Executive Chef Jared Young and his team of talented culinarians. As the festivities unfold, a special appearance by Santa Claus adds an enchanting touch, inviting families to share in the wonder of the season.
Reserve Now
Down the Rabbit Hole to 2027
Venture down the rabbit hole this New Year’s Eve as The Ritz-Carlton Naples, Tiburón transforms into a world inspired by the wonder and whimsy of Alice in Wonderland. Discover exceptional cuisine, handcrafted cocktails, live entertainment, and unexpected delights throughout an immersive evening of celebration. At midnight, toast to new beginnings and welcome 2027 with champagne, dancing, and a touch of magic.
