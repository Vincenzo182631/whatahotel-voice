Stay and Play at Our Naples, FL, Golf Resort
Golf
The premier golf destination in Southwest Florida, The Ritz-Carlton Naples, Tiburón invites players to experience two championship golf courses designed by Greg Norman. Proudly designated as a Certified Audubon Cooperative Sanctuary, Tiburón Golf Club offers guests exclusive access to world-class play, personalized instruction, caddie concierge services, and tailored golf packages, creating the ideal luxury golf vacation. Whether perfecting your game, enjoying unlimited play, or planning a dedicated golf getaway in Florida, The Ritz-Carlton Naples, Tiburón delivers an unparalleled experience surrounded by the region’s natural beauty.

Tiburón Golf Club
Certified as an Audubon Cooperative Sanctuary, Tiburón’s two 18-hole championship golf courses designed by Greg Norman feature stacked sod wall and coquina shell waste bunkers, and no conventional rough. Host to PGA and LPGA events, Tiburón invites golfers to play year-round where the top professionals compete.

Golf Programs
Leave your clubs and extra baggage fees behind.
For your next round, reserve a set of new Callaway golf clubs with your tee time at Tiburón Golf Club, plus a six-ball pouch of Callaway golf balls, a golf glove, golf shoes and more. Exclusively for Ritz-Carlton guests, this "hassle free" offer is $40 per person and represents a $130 value. The program is not applicable to corporate/group rounds of golf.
Family Golf
Young golfers ages 15 and under can enjoy complimentary rounds of golf at Tiburón Golf Club year round. One child plays free after 3:00 p.m. for each paid adult round.
The Tiburón Golf Academy
Join PGA Lead Instructor Tom O’Brien and his team of instructors for a truly game-changing experience at Tiburón Golf Academy. The Tiburón Golf Academy offers the best instructional programs for adults, juniors, and corporate clients. Whether you are looking to sharpen your game with a one-on-one lesson, introduce someone to the game, or reserving a group clinic, we can structure our instructional program to meet your needs. Please call (239) 593-2201.
Caddie Concierge Program
Enhance your Naples golf vacation with a unique brand of personal attention with Tiburón Golf Club’s Caddie Concierge program. Extending the unparalleled tradition of service of Tiburón Golf Club and The Ritz-Carlton onto the green, this innovative program pairs each group with a knowledgeable professional attendant, providing a distinguished level of service usually reserved for private clubs. Available November through April.
For more information on the golf experience and tee times, please contact the golf department at (239) 254-3340.
The Vault
The Vault is a unique entertainment experience for members, hotel guests, and groups ranging in size from 8 to 36. The Vault provides an option to relax and watch a game, utilize a state of the art HD sports simulator, throw darts, or enjoy a few games of pool. This ultimate adult sports lounge is the perfect setting for a night with friends, or an afternoon watching your favorite sporting event. Call 239-593-2209 for more information.
Training for Optimal Performance
Tired of high scores and inconsistent play? Top Mental Game Coach Dr. Shannon Reece teaches golfers how to perform at their best using her proven mental formula. Her high-impact coaching programs help golfers of all levels eliminate interference and leverage their strengths to reach championship form. For details on private or group coaching or to reserve a session, please call (239) 431-6810 or click Learn More.

Frequently Asked Questions
Are guests able to make online reservations to Tiburón Golf Club?
Online reservations are accepted at Tiburón Golf Club.
Reserve a tee time online
, or call us at +1 239-2543340.
Do kids golf for free at Tiburón Golf Club?
Kids golf for free at Tiburón Golf Club.
Does Tiburón Golf Club have a clubhouse?
There is a clubhouse at Tiburón Golf Club.Enjoy a bite to eat or a cool refreshment at our clubhouse restaurant.
What is par at Tiburón Golf Club?
Tiburón Golf Club is a par-72, 18-hole course, with a total length of 7382 yards. To learn more about the specifications of Tiburón Golf Club, please call +1 239-2543340.
Does Tiburón Golf Club offer a driving range?
A driving range is located at Tiburón Golf Club. Amateur and experienced golfers alike can warm up or practice their shot ahead of their next round.
Does Tiburón Golf Club offer equipment rental?
Equipment rental is offered at Tiburón Golf Club.
Does Tiburón Golf Club offer golf school instruction?
Golf school instruction is offered at Tiburón Golf Club. Work with experts to improve your golf game.
Does Tiburón Golf Club provide lessons?
Lessons are offered at Tiburón Golf Club. Golfers in search of tips or tweaks to elevate their golf game can feel encouraged to do so.
Is there a children’s golf program available at Tiburón Golf Club?
There is not a children’s golf program available at Tiburón Golf Club.
What forms of payment does Tiburón Golf Club accept?
Yes, Tiburón Golf Club offers the following forms of payment: Cash,Credit Cards
Are spikes allowed at Tiburón Golf Club?
Spikes are permitted at Tiburón Golf Club.
Is there a putting green available at Tiburón Golf Club?
A putting green is available for guests at Tiburón Golf Club.
