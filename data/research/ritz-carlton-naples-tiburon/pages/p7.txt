A Legacy in Motion
A Sanctuary Reimagined
A new expression of nature framed elegance is emerging at The Ritz Carlton Naples, Tiburón. Envisioned by Parker Torres Design, the resort’s transformation brings a contemporary sensibility to its serene surroundings, weaving clean architectural lines with a palette of monochromatic earth tones and textured natural elements. Inspired by Naples’ tropical greenery and verdant lush surroundings, carved woods, organic patterns, and luminous finishes come together to create spaces that feel grounded, restorative, and effortlessly modern. Throughout the year, the renewed resort will debut a refined haven nestled among lush gardens and championship greens, thoughtfully crafted for family escapes, romantic retreats, and moments of meaningful connection.
Overlooking The Lush Botanicals
ROOMS & SUITES
Guest rooms and suites are being reimagined with a lighter, fresher aesthetic that reflects the region’s character. Elevated amenities, tailored lighting, and soft, tactile finishes foster a serene sense of ease, while private terraces frame lush green and fairway views. The result is a calm, contemporary retreat designed for rest.

BOLD FLAVORS & STYLES
Culinary enhancements at The Ritz-Carlton Naples, Tiburón reflect a deep connection to regional flavor and thoughtful technique. Fenrose begins the day with refined French breakfast traditions and meticulous egg dishes, complemented by Café Fenrose, an all day market designed for ease and flavor driven opulence. As evening arrives, Palmera becomes the resort’s vibrant core, offering coastal menus shaped by Gulf Coast harvests, sun-ripened citrus, heirloom tomatoes, fresh herbs, and local seafood. The Club Lounge elevates its offerings with refreshed furnishings and polished presentations, creating a space for lingering from morning to twilight. These elements come together to showcase the resort’s dedication to craftsmanship, culinary storytelling, and a vibrant reflection of Naples’ spirit.

MEETINGS AND SPECIAL GATHERINGS
Soon debuting in Naples, the Swaying Palm Ballroom introduces a fresh expression of coastal sophistication. Sweeping golf-course views, sculpted details, and organic textures reflect the surrounding landscape. Expansive windows fill 2,700 sq. ft. with natural light, while luminous finishes, refined lighting, and warm contemporary materials create a serene yet striking setting for memorable gatherings.
