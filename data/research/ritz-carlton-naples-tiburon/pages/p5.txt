Experiences
Nestled amid rolling greens and tropical palm trees stands The Ritz-Carlton Naples, Tiburón. An exceptional retreat just three miles away from the Gulf, guests can surrender to the serene atmosphere of a stately country club. Other amenities at our Naples hotel with a waterpark include two pools and unique dining experiences. Guests are also encouraged to use the hotel’s complimentary shuttle to access The Ritz-Carlton, Naples, located just 10 minutes away.

The Massage and Facial Center FAQ
For your convenience, we have compiled answers to common questions you may find helpful in choosing the types of treatment that best meet your individual needs or desires.
FAQ
Prior to Arrival
When should I arrive?
For your enhanced comfort and relaxation, we suggest that you arrive 5-10 minutes before your scheduled treatment. Arriving late will simply limit the time for your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure, and the full value of your treatment will be charged.
What should I wear during my treatment?
Most body treatments are enjoyed without clothing; however, you may wear whatever makes you comfortable. During all treatments, the body is fully draped, except for the area being worked on. Robes and slippers are provided before and after your treatment.
What about my jewelry and valuables?
If you are staying in the hotel, please leave valuable items in the safe in your guestroom. Otherwise, lockers are available for your personal items in the Massage and Fitness Center.
Should I shave before a treatment?
Shaving is not recommended prior to any body treatments or hair removal services.
Who can guide me in choosing my treatments?
Our fitness attendants can help you plan the perfect massage, including the best order for your treatments.
Massage and Fitness Center Policies
What is the cancellation policy?
Guests will have up to six hours before their scheduled treatment time to cancel / change without a cancellation fee. Guests that cancel within six hours of their reservation will be charged 50% of their total treatment price. Guests that do not arrive for their scheduled reservation and do not contact the spa by the end of the business day will be charged 100% of their total treatment reservation.
What about gratuities?
For your convenience, a 20% service charge will be added to each service you receive. You may choose to leave an additional gratuity at your discretion.
Is there a minimum age for treatments?
Guests must be 18 years of age or older to enjoy massage services.
What if I am late for my appointment?
Arriving late will simply limit the time for your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest will not be delayed. The full value of your treatment will be charged.
What about payment for spa services?
We accept all major credit cards. You may also charge massage services to your hotel bill. For your convenience, payment is also accepted prior to your treatments, upon request.
Are gift certificates available from the spa?
You may purchase gift certificates for a specific dollar amount to be used towards massage services. Gift certificates are available here.
Upon Arrival
What about my comfort?
This is your time, and you should delight in the experience to the fullest. Whether it's the room temperature, the amount of massage pressure or the volume of the music, please notify us of any discomforts or preferences. We request that cell phones and pagers be turned off in the Massage and Fitness Center.
What if I have gender preferences?
If you prefer a male or female therapist, please make your request when scheduling your appointment.
Health Questions
What if I am pregnant?
While there are a few services that should be avoided during pregnancy, there are many beneficial treatments that can be enjoyed. Please notify our fitness attendants if you are pregnant when you make your appointments so that they can offer guidance.
What if I have special health considerations?
Please notify our fitness attendants before booking your treatments if you have high blood pressure, allergies, any other physical ailments or disabilities, or if you are pregnant. If you have any concerns at all, please let us know. If at any time during your services you experience discomfort, please alert your therapist immediately.
Luxury Matches Designed by a Tennis Legend
Perfect Your Game Like a Pro
The Ritz-Carlton Naples, Tiburón is pleased to partner with world-renowned tennis coach, Patrick Mouratoglou on his first signature Tennis Center in Florida. Launching in early summer 2024, the exclusive Tennis Center will offer a variety of training camps, private lessons, leagues, and retreats, delivering a premium tennis experience for both resort guests and members. Tennis coaches trained in the proven Mouratoglou Methodology, which is based on the idea that every individual is unique and uses customization, adapting to each player based on their needs. This hyper-personalized approach to teaching, regardless of age or level, will ensure that all players will benefit from any program and improve as quickly as possible.

Beach Resort Activities
Our Naples, Florida, Golf resort features a host of recreational amenities and a vast array of activities for the active lifestyle, including golf, tennis, and cycling. Take advantage of the warm, tropical climate and lush green surroundings of this beautiful resort and its nearby sister property, The Ritz-Carlton, Naples, to discover an endless variety of activities to do in Naples. Spend the day exploring one of the most delicate and complex ecosystems in the country, perfecting your golf game, hitting the courts, or simply relaxing at the beach or by the pool – with so many unique and memorable Naples activities, you’ll enjoy an unforgettable experience. The Ritz-Carlton, Naples overlooks the Gulf of Mexico with amenities including a resort family pool, rejuvenating spa, an interactive children’s program, and eight unique dining experiences. Guests are encouraged to use the hotel’s shuttle to access The Ritz-Carlton, Naples, located just 10 minutes away.

Explore Downtown Naples Attractions Near Our Hotel
Surrounded by palm trees, blue waters and white sand beaches, it is no wonder Naples bears the name “Florida’s Last Paradise.” Situated along the Gulf of Mexico in South Florida, Naples is surrounded by protected wetlands. Renowned by golf enthusiasts, The Ritz-Carlton Naples, Tiburón's golf courses host two PGA and LPGA golf tournaments annually. Off the course, hotel visitors can shop in nearby downtown Naples, visit the Naples Zoo or explore Delnor Wiggins Pass State Park.
