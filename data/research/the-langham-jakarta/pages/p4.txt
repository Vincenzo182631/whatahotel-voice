##### Old Town Batavia

09:00 - 14:00

### OLD TOWN BATAVIA

Discover the charm of Kota Tua Batavia, Jakarta’s historic old town, on an elegant cultural journey. Stroll through cobblestone streets and visit the Jakarta History Museum, immersing yourself in the city’s rich heritage. Continue with a refined exploration of the area, including a visit to a traditional Wayang workshop, where master artisans create intricate puppets offering a glimpse into Indonesia’s timeless craftsmanship and performing arts.
