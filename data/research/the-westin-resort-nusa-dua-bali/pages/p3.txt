## Dining

Immerse yourself in the culinary culture of Bali at The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali. With six restaurants and bars on site, there is something for every guest. Our Nusa Dua, Bali resort's idyllic beachfront...See More setting enhances the outdoor restaurants, while the indoor venues offer modern, inviting atmospheres. At our bars, refreshing cocktails, freshly squeezed juices and tropical beverages keep you cool through a day in the Indonesian sun.
