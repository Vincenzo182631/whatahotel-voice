## 3 Pillars of Wellbeing

No matter what obstacles travel puts between you and your well-being, the signature wellness programs at our Nusa Dua, Bali spa hotel help you soar above it all. Here you can eat, sleep, move well, transcending the rigors of travel while you’re on the road. Stay well at Westin Hotels & Resorts®, a place where together, we can rise.

### Sleep Well

Seize the night, too. With our award-winning Heavenly® Bed, calming Sleep Well Lavender Balm Amenity, and a special menu packed with sleep-enhancing superfoods, you’ll get an incredible night’s rest, ensuring that you don’t just get up, you rise.

### Eat Well

Rise above the rigors of travel and keep up with your healthy eating habits while on the road. Explore all the ways you can eat well at Westin with a menu packed with thoughtful options to keep you fueled and focused on feeling your best.

### Move Well

Our unique fitness programs ensure your workout routine stays routine when you travel. From RunWESTIN™ local running maps to Gear Lending, all of our wellness programs keep you active and energized while you’re on the road.

### Wellness Ambassador

Hailing from the serene Himalayas, our Wellness Ambassador, Dr. Jitendra Pokhriyal spearheads resort's wellness programs rooted in traditional practices that were met with high guest satisfaction. His offerings include stress management, Reiki healing, food and nutrition consultations, daily yoga sessions, meditation, and Pranayama (breathing techniques). Whether guests are seasoned wellness enthusiasts or curious beginners, Dr. Jitendra’s holistic programs are designed to elevate their well-being on every level.

Choose wellness gear and equipment, quality swimwear and casual outfits to match the relaxed resort atmosphere, or find traditional Indonesian batik and handmade Balinese lace clothing for your next special occasion. ## Explore Beauty of Nusa Dua

Choose from traditional dance performances, temple visits, horseback rides, relaxing nature walks and more, or dive into Bali's vibrant underwater world. The Concierge at our beachfront hotel in Nusa Dua can help you plan day tours that make the most of your time and reveal the island's unique character. For any questions, preferences or ideas to enhance your stay, please reach out to our destination team. Contact us at destination.westinbali@westin.com.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali offers a complimentary, 7:00 AM-6:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Exercise Bike(s), Pilates Reformer & Pilates Reformer Tower, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Resort Pool (Outdoor)Water Slide

Does The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali have an on-site spa?

Yes, Heavenly Spa by Westin™ is located on-site at The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali?

Bali Safari and Marine Park, Beachwalk Shopping Center, Uluwatu Cliff and Temple, Bali International Convention Center BICC, Kuta Art Market and more are nearby The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali.
