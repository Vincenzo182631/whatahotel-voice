# Welcome to The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali

## Focus on your well-being in Bali, Indonesia

Find your balance on the white sands of Nusa Dua Beach. The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali is a contemporary five-star resort in Bali, Indonesia, designed for families on vacation and business travelers...See More alike. Located next to the Bali International Convention Centre, our 30 meeting rooms and event venues, spanning 10,500 square meters, make it easy to bring people together. When the day slows down, recharge at one of our six restaurants, from relaxed family dining to refined evenings out. Restore body and mind with a treatment at Heavenly Spa by Westin, let the little ones play at Westin Family Kids Club, or settle beside our sparkling freshwater pool with a drink in hand. Stay active with nearby watersports, two onsite tennis courts or the 24-hour WestinWORKOUT Fitness Studio. Each room and suite is a calm retreat, with a signature Westin Heavenly Bed, luxury amenities and a private balcony with garden, ocean or pool views. At The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali, leave feeling better than when you arrived.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali?

The check-in time at The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali.

What are the parking options at The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali?

The closest airport near The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali is I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS). DPS is located approximately 11.4 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali have an airport shuttle to I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS)?

Yes, The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali has an airport shuttle to I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport - Bali (DPS).

Does The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
