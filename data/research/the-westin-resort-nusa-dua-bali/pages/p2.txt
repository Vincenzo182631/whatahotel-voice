# Sleep Well at The Westin Bali

## Accommodations

Feel at home during your stay at The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali. Our refined hotel rooms and suites are thoughtfully designed to help you feel restored and fulfilled in Nusa Dua, Bali. Warm wood accents, serene earth tones and natural elements reflect the beauty of our idyllic surroundings, while oversized windows welcome soft natural light that gently lifts the spirit. Each standard room features a balcony overlooking the garden or pool. For a little more space, choose a terrace room with a private outdoor area, lounge chairs and direct access to our lagoon-style swimming pools. Wake refreshed after a restful night's sleep on our signature Heavenly® Bed, dressed in plush pillows and crisp cotton linens. Indulge in a spa-like escape in your own room with marble vanities, deep soaking tubs and invigorating bath amenities. Our spacious suites are ideal for families visiting Bali, with plenty of room to relax together.

Levy Fee For International Tourists Visiting Bali

Local authorities have announced the implementation of a levy fee of IDR150,000 per person for all International tourists visiting Bali.

### Ocean View Suite

Indulge in our luxurious Ocean View Suites. Just steps from the Indian Ocean and resort pool, each suite is named for one of six Balinese regions (Karangasem, Bangli, Klungkung, Panglipuran, Celuk and Tenganan) and reflects its own unique design. Bathed in natural light, the expansive layout includes a soothing living room with day bed, a welcoming space to gather with friends and family in Nusa Dua, Bali. A separate dining area is ideal for an intimate meal for four, while a well-equipped desk offers a dedicated place to work.
