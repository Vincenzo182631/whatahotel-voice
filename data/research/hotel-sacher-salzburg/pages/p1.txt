## A symbol of Salzburg hospitality since 1866

### Where history and culture come together

History, culture and hospitality come together at Hotel Sacher Salzburg, a family-owned 5-star superior luxury hotel located in the heart of Salzburg. Opened in 1866 as the “Österreichischer Hof” and renamed Hotel Sacher Salzburg in 2000, it is Salzburg’s only historic grand hotel. Set directly on the banks of the Salzach, the hotel offers a special view of the historic Old Town and Hohensalzburg Fortress. From the moment you arrive, you can feel the unique atmosphere of Salzburg and the hotel’s close connection to the city.

Today, Hotel Sacher Salzburg combines timeless Austrian elegance with luxurious rooms and suites, exceptional culinary experiences, Café Sacher Salzburg and personal service at the highest level. Shaped by generations of heartfelt hospitality, it is a place where historic charm meets modern comfort.

## Luxurious rooms and suites in Salzburg

## Elegant comfort with views of the Salzach and Old Town

Hotel Sacher Salzburg offers 110 individually designed rooms and suites that combine classic Austrian elegance with modern comfort. They were designed by co-owner Alexandra Winkler in collaboration with internationally renowned French star designer Pierre-Yves Rochon. Many rooms and suites offer views of the Salzach River, Salzburg’s UNESCO-listed Old Town or Hohensalzburg Fortress, creating a wonderful retreat in one of the city’s most renowned luxury hotels.

## Restaurants, café and bar at Hotel Sacher Salzburg

### Austrian cuisine, coffee house culture and the Original Sacher-Torte

From the finest Austrian cuisine to relaxed moments of indulgence by the Salzach, Hotel Sacher Salzburg is one of the city’s most elegant culinary addresses. Discover the modern Restaurant Sacher Grill, the traditional gourmet restaurant Zirbelzimmer, Café Sacher Salzburg and the famous Original Sacher-Torte, served in the heart of Salzburg. The Sacher Bar is one of the city’s best bars and invites guests to enjoy an aperitif, selected drinks or a refined moment after dinner in a special atmosphere.

## FAQs about Hotel Sacher Salzburg

### Everything you need to know for your stay

What is the address of the Hotel Sacher Salzburg?

The Hotel Sacher Salzburg is located at the Schwarzstraße 5-7, A-5020 Salzburg, Austria

Does the Hotel Sacher Salzburg have a concierge service?

Yes, our experienced concierge team is available around the clock with discretion and expertise – whether for cultural recommendations, personal requests or special arrangements.

How is the Hotel Sacher Salzburg connected to public transport?

The hotel is very well connected to the public transport.

Bus lines 1, 3, 5, and 6 stop at the nearby Makartplatz stop. The airport is also reachable in just 15–20 minutes

What are the opening hours of the restaurants at hotel Sacher Salzburg?

Our culinary venues welcome you at the following times:

- **Café Sacher Salzburg:** daily from 8:30 AM to 10:00 PM
- **Sacher Grill**: daily from 12:00 to 2:30 PM and 6:00 to 9:30 PM
- **Breakfast buffet:** daily from 7:00 to 10:30 AM
- **Zirbelzimmer:** Thursday to Sunday from 6:00 to 9:00 PM
- **Sacher Bar:** daily from 4:00 PM to midnight
