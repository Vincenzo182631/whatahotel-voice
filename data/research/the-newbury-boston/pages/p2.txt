# The Embodiment of Residential Comfort

Heritage architecture and residential comfort define each guestroom at The Newbury, the newest boutique hotel in Boston's Back Bay area. Intuitive interiors give way to calming, contemporary accommodations featuring original artwork by illustrator **Veronica Lawlor**, custom-designed furnishings by acclaimed interior designer **Alexandra Champalimaud**, handcrafted woods, and marble touches. Revel in layered luxury with bespoke amenities that include **Premium bath amenities selected specifically for the hotel**, luxurious robes and linens, in-room **Nespresso Vertuo** espresso-maker, and private access to The Library and Fitness Center, along with preferred seating at The Street Bar.
