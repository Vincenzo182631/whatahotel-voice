# Neighborhood

Discover the sophistication of the Back Bay from our premier location, where heritage and modernity converge in the shadows of Victorian brownstones and the Boston Public Garden, and surrounded by Newbury Street’s boundless boutiques and luxury brands, including **Tiffany’s** Boston flagship, located in our lobby, and neighboring **Burberry**, **Chanel**, and **Hermès.**
