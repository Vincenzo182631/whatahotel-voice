# Fireplace Suite Retreat

* * *

Luxuriate in the romance of the season with your own wood-burning fireplace, complete with the services of a Fireplace Butler.

Book This Offer

**Experience the magic of Boston’s Fireplace Season at The Newbury.**

From October 9 through April 10, select suites invite you to gather around your very own crackling hearth. Our Fireplace Butler will prepare and tend the fire to perfection.

Whether you’re escaping for a romantic weekend, savoring the charm of winter in the city, or simply indulging in the warmth of a private fireside evening, this retreat is Boston at its most enchanting.

Available exclusively in Fireplace Suites, October 9, 2026 – April 10, 2027.
