# Modern Boston Dining

At the crossroads of old-world skill and new world charm, The Newbury Boston tailors and refines culinary experiences to delight and amuse. Deeply rooted in craft, comfort and familiar luxury, we are Boston’s consummate gathering place.

#### **The Street Bar**

The Street Bar is the spirit of the Newbury and the ultimate expression of the inventive and established. For decades, The Street Bar has enjoyed a reputation as the preeminent social hub for discerning Bostonians and travelers alike.

#### **Contessa**

Major Food Group brings its acclaimed creative palette and flair to The Newbury's signature glass-enclosed four-season rooftop restaurant. Designed by Ken Fulk, the restaurant features sweeping views of the city, and the dramatic space is complemented by a menu of Italian cuisine.

#### **Afternoon Tea in The Street Bar**

A Boston tradition, reimagined. In the wood-paneled glow of The Street Bar - with crackling fireplaces and views of Newbury Street - Afternoon Tea becomes an experience you can’t miss. Curated by Major Food Group, the menu pairs exceptional teas with savory bites, freshly baked pastries, and delicate sweets, all served with the timeless hospitality of The Newbury Boston.

#### **The Library**

The curated mind and soul of this great residence, The Library at The Newbury is the hearth, an immersive haven to recalibrate, set the day’s tone, and savor an exclusive escape. Discreetly connected to the iconic Street Bar, The Library is an intimate salon space of well-crafted provisions and literary reads curated by the Boston Public Library. The Library is reserved exclusively for guests of The Newbury.
