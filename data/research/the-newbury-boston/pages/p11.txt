## The Boston Run: The Newbury x Tracksmith for Marathon Weekend

**The Pursuit of Excellence: A Boston Marathon Collab, The Newbury x Tracksmith**

As April lands in Boston, there’s a runner’s hum that starts at the finish line on Boylston Street and radiates through the Public Garden to The Newbury’s front door.

Marathon Weekend (the race is on Monday, April 20th, Patriots’ Day) in Boston is more than a race; it’s our city-wide legacy, triumphant, exciting, and full of inspiring stories of runners near and far, experienced and beginner.

For 2026, The Newbury Boston is proud to be at the heartbeat of the Boston Marathon’s magnetic energy. We have both a front-row seat to the weekend’s spirit and a luxurious retreat from the crowds, and with that we’re thrilled to announce our official partnership with **Tracksmith**, two Boston icons, one street. Tracksmith, an independent running brand founded in New England, offers apparel and community events based on their commitment to training and racing and dedication to a specialized craft, an obsession with quality, fabric, design,  function.

The connection between The Newbury and Tracksmith is organic, rooted in a shared appreciation for Boston’s history and a commitment to excellence in all its forms. While Tracksmith’s " Trackhouse" and The Newbury sit at different ends of Newbury Street, we are connected with a shared purpose: celebrating through performance, style, and our super strong Boston community.

**Spotlight on Stamata: Celebrating the Female Athlete**

The centerpiece of our collaboration is a special event hosted right here at the hotel. On **Saturday, April 18, 2026, we invite runners, locals, and spectators to join us for a Stamata Panel Event.**

The afternoon will feature an inspiring conversation with the elite female athletes of Team Stamata including: Stephanie Bruce, Elizabeth Chikotas, Maria Luevano-Salazar, and Katy Presley. The panel will delve into the intersection of sport and lifestyle, exploring the dedication it takes to compete at the highest level and the community that sustains it.

Before we settle in for the discussion, the day will kick off with a community shakeout run. This is a defining moment of the weekend—a chance to stretch your legs alongside fellow runners, find your rhythm, and experience the city’s pre-race magic before retreating to the refined comfort of the hotel.

**Your Marathon Hub**

Whether you’re a marathoner looking for a pre-race sanctuary or a spectator wanting to experience the historic weekend, The Newbury serves as your ultimate hub. We aren't just a backdrop to the race; we’re part of the fabric of the weekend.

From the quiet luxury of our suites to our vibrant gathering spaces where the running community will meet, we invite you to celebrate the spirit of Boston with us.
