Peru’s Colca Valley is a land of soaring canyons and open skies. It has been home to pre-Inca inhabitants since 600 AD, including the Collagua and Cabana tribes. At the heart of it all lies Las Casitas, a hidden sanctuary and the perfect place to forge a deeper connection with nature.

Winding trails lead you here through terraced hills, where moments of discovery are framed by soaring views. The Colca Canyon is found moments away; set off before dawn and witness the majesty of the Andean condor, rising on thermal winds and gliding across the Canyon’s depths.
