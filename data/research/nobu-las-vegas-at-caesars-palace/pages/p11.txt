#### The Nobu Penthouse

NOBU Hotel Las Vegas

Nestled in the pristine Nobu Hotel, the Penthouse is the largest of the Nobu suites, ranging from 2,200 - 4,350-square-feet. The Las Vegas suite features a curved staircase with a second-story terrace, a stone hearth wall and sleek Japanese-inspired décor. Luxury accommodations include one king bedroom with option for 4 additional bedrooms, a 90-inch flat screen, billiard table and a separate dining area. Guests may lavish in exclusive Nobu amenities and priority seating to Nobu restaurant.
For assistance with connecting rooms, events or additional suite information, call 855-855-9205. Penthouse events are required to occupy connecting rooms.

#### Nobu Villa

The three-bedroom, 10,300-square-foot Nobu Villa is both a celebrity and high roller favorite. The Japanese-inspired rooftop villa has a massive patio overlooking the Strip. It features a barbecue pit, full bar, fire and water fixture, and a secluded Zen garden with an Italian-made whirlpool. The indoors is just as impressive. With its sprawling formal dining room, spacious rooms and spa-like bathroom, guests can easily unwind and spend their entire trip in this lush villa.

## Hotel Amenities

#### Porsche House Car

Guests staying in a Nobu Hotel suite can enjoy complimentary transportation within a 10-mile radius of the resort in one of our exclusive Porsche house cars, subject to availability.

Each ride accommodates up to three guests (including passenger seat next to driver). House cars cannot accommodate large luggage. Service includes direct pick-up and drop-off only; staging and wait times are not available. Alternate vehicles will not be substituted if a Porsche house car is unavailable. Nobu Penthouse and Villa guests receive complimentary airport transfers via luxury Escalade.
