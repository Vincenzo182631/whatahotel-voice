## Immerse yourself in luxury

Take advantage of the exclusive fitness center and business lounge Nobu Hotel has to offer. Looking for more? As a part of Caesars Palace take advantage of dining experiences, shows and events that are also available.

#### Qua Spa and Baths

Envelop yourself in Qua’s sprawling, 50,000-square-foot spa grounds that include a Laconium Room and Crystal Body Art Room. Three Roman baths use the ancient healing powers of water, while the snowing Arctic Ice Room is the only one of its kind in the world.

#### Nobu Lounge

Enjoy the chic surroundings of Nobu Lounge at any time of day.  Plug-in and check your emails, relax, meet friends and enjoy all that Nobu has to offer. To set the tone, bowed columns of bamboo line the exterior of the restaurant and lounge to resemble the structure of the traditional Japanese ikebana basket used for the art of flower-arranging.
