## Questions & Answers

### What is Olympus?

Olympus is the newest and highest tier within Caesars Rewards®, positioned above Seven Stars® and designed to recognize Caesars Entertainment’s most loyal and engaged members with elevated benefits, exclusive access, and curated experiences.

### How do I qualify for Olympus?

The journey to Olympus is by invitation only and begins with 300,000 Tier Credits. Tier Credits earned by Caesars Rewards members throughout the calendar year through gaming, dining, entertainment, partner purchases, and hotel stays will be considered.

### What benefits does Olympus offer?

Olympus members receive exclusive privileges, including Reward Credits earned 40% faster, suite upgrades, an annual retreat benefit, access to the Olympus event series, and more.

### When does Olympus launch?

The road to Olympus begins now. Members have until December 31, 2026 to earn 300,000 Tier Credits and become eligible for consideration. Founding Members will be announced in January 2027 with benefits beginning February 1, 2027

### How is Olympus different from Seven Stars?

Olympus is a new tier above Seven Stars, created to provide members an even higher level of recognition and unique privileges. Members will earn Reward Credits 40% faster, enjoy suite upgrades, an enhanced annual retreat, as well as access to highly curated member-only events including the Founder's Party at Caesars Palace Las Vegas in March 2027.

### What does it mean to be an Olympus Founding Member?

Founding Members are the first to earn a place in Olympus, a distinction few will hold. They'll enjoy exclusive tier benefits beginning in February 2027 and be celebrated at the Founder's Party at Caesars Palace Las Vegas in March 2027

### Does online play count toward Olympus qualification?

Yes. All Tier Credits earned through wagering with Caesars Sportsbook, Caesars Palace Online Casino, Horseshoe Online Casino, and WSOP Online will count toward Olympus eligibility.

### Can members earn Olympus Tier Credits through non-gaming activity?

Yes. All Tier Credits earned through non-gaming activity, including hotel stays, dining, entertainment, and partner purchases, count toward the 300,000 Tier Credit threshold for Olympus, in addition to Tier Credits earned from gaming and online play.

### I have over 300,000 Tier Credits, why haven't I been invited to Olympus?

﻿Reaching 300,000 Tier Credits is the first step toward Olympus. As an invitation-only tier, earning the Tier Credit requirement does not guarantee membership. Founding Members selected for Olympus will be notified in January 2027.

### Will Olympus members receive a new Caesars Rewards card?

Yes. A new card, designed specifically for Olympus members, will be issued beginning February 1, 2027.
