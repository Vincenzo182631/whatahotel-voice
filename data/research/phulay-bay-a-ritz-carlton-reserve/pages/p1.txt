A World Away in Krabi, Thailand
Beyond the limestone mountains rising from Thailand’s Andaman Sea, virgin rainforests, ethereal beaches, and crystal clear waterfalls, a secluded paradise awaits in one of the world’s most spellbinding destinations. Embark on a voyage of discovery at Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve—a hidden retreat where the charm and natural splendor of Thailand blend with an aura of serenity and luxury. Discover our 5-star Krabi beach resort where you can wake up to yoga on the beach, spend the day hiking the majestic Dragon Crest Mountain and relax over dinner with ocean views.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve?
The check-in time at Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve.
What are the parking options at Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve?
On-site parking is available at Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve booking.
What is the closest airport near Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve?
The closest airport near Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve is Krabi International Airport (KBV). KBV is located approximately 40.0 KM from the hotel.
Does Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve have an airport shuttle to Phuket International Airport (HKT)?
Yes, Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve has an airport shuttle to Phuket International Airport (HKT).
Does Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve have an airport shuttle to Krabi International Airport (KBV)?
Yes, Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve has an airport shuttle to Krabi International Airport (KBV).
Does Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve have electric vehicle charging stations?
