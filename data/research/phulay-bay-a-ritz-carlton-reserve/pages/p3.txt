Dining Experiences
Discover a world of exceptional dining that transcends the traditional rhythm of breakfast, lunch, and dinner. At Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, culinary experiences are artfully curated—from intimate, chef-led journeys and bespoke private dining to refined lunch offerings and exclusive four-hands dinners, each thoughtfully designed to captivate the senses.
Dining Beyond
Discover the tastes of Thailand beyond traditional settings with Phulay Bay’s Executive Chef. Design your private dining adventure in Krabi, whether it is a family barbecue on the beach, a romantic candlelit dinner for two, or an exclusive Chef’s Table with a meticulously crafted menu.

Private Dining Experiences in Krabi
Sri Trang by the Sea
Indulge in authentic southern Thai cuisine in a private pavilion overlooking the sea, and savour local dishes made from only the finest ingredients, sourced from local markets each morning.
Candlelight Dinner at Sala Srichan
Bask in the soothing water features of the Candle Light Pavilion, the mystical melodies of Thai musicians and the illuminated walls adorned with thousands of candles. Be enchanted by this unsurpassed romantic setting for a once in a lifetime dining experience.
Barbecue on the Beach
Spend a relaxing evening on Jampoon Beach surrounded by the sea breeze and a sunset. Your own personal Chef will create a personalized menu as you dine surrounded by torches and Thai inspired decoration for a signature private dining experience in Krabi you cannot miss.
Reserve Picnic at Hong Island
Hong in Thai means “room” and the literal translation of the island’s Thai name is “the island with the room” due to the gorgeous lagoon located in its centre. Relax under the tropical sun with a delicious picnic basket of tropical fruits, pastries, and local Thai dishes or choose from a menu of international cuisines.
Lae Lay Under the Stars
Dine on the private rooftop of Lae Lay Restaurant overlooking the dramatic coastal scenery of Krabi, and indulge in a truly memorable experience amidst the sunset and twinkling stars.
