Experience Our Hotel Near Phuket, Thailand
Experiences

Begin your day strolling the beaches, taking a relaxing swim in our hotel near Phang Nga Bay's infinity pool, or hiking through the jungle scenery. Guests enjoy long-tail boat rides to Hong Island or drive out to Tiger Cave Temple. This luxury hotel near Phi Phi Islands also offers unique opportunities for guests to learn about the Thai language, Thai cooking, and Batik painting.

Phulay Bay Signature Experiences
Two Thousand Candle Dinner at Sala Srichan
Step into a romantic dreamscape at Sala Srichan, where the soft glow of 2,000 candles lights your evening. Surrounded by tranquil water, this private dining experience celebrates your love story. Enjoy a multi-course menu by our Executive Chef, paired with Champagne, as flickering flames and gentle ripples create an unforgettable night for special moments together.
Dragon Crest: Breath Above the Clouds
Awaken your spirit with a hike to Dragon Crest Mountain, one of Krabi’s viewpoints. This guided two-hour trek winds through lush rainforest as wildlife stirs. At the summit, take in sweeping views of forests, limestone cliffs and emerald seas. As light appears, enjoy mindful breathing session above the clouds, where nature, movement and stillness meet.
Balance of Five Elements
Embark on a wellness journey inspired by Ayurvedic wisdom and the five elements: earth, water, fire, air and space. This one-hour ritual features clay packs, breathwork, yoga and meditation to restore balance. Guided by practitioners, each stage nurtures body, mind and spirit, creating harmony and inner peace that lingers beyond the experience.
Krabi’s Real Touch
Discover the heart of Southern Thailand on this immersive journey into authentic Krabi. Travel by long-tail boat through mangroves to Khao Kanap Nam’s limestone cliffs, then cross to Koh Klang, a traditional fishing village. Explore by tuk-tuk, meet artisans, try batik painting, and hand-crafting. This four-hour adventure offers a deeper appreciation of Krabi’s culture beyond the tourist trail.
Mist & Morning: Nong Thalay Dawn Cruise
Experience a magical morning as the sun rises over Ban Nong Thalay, one of Krabi’s most beautiful lakes. Enjoy a private boat ride across tranquil waters, wrapped in morning mist and framed by distant mountains. Savor a traditional Thai breakfast with coffee and tea onboard, guided by a local boatman, in a serene journey that captures the essence of slow, soulful travel.
Echoes of Ao Luk
Explore Ao Luk’s tranquil landscapes of limestone cliffs, caves, forests, and hidden waterfalls. Glide by long-tail boat or kayak through mangroves to Phi Hua To Cave, where prehistoric paintings and seashells hint at the past. Continue to a secluded waterfall for mindful moments, and optionally visit Bang Thong Temple, famed for its 95-meter-high golden pagoda, completing this serene journey.
Andaman at Dusk: Cruise & Fishing
Sail into the golden Andaman sunset on a private voyage along Krabi’s coastline. Cruise past limestone karsts as the sun dips, surrounded by vibrant colors. Meet locals and try traditional squid-catching, experiencing coastal life firsthand. Immerse in the sea’s rhythm and evening serenity, creating lasting memories where nature, tranquility, and tradition converge.
Heritage Flavors of Krabi
Taste Southern Thailand’s culinary heritage on this multi-sensory adventure. Guided by a local expert, explore a bustling market, then visit Ao Thalane to learn traditional chili paste. Continue to a seaside home to craft Hor Mok, steamed Thai fish curry in banana leaves. This four-hour journey along Krabi’s tranquil coast leaves lasting flavors and unforgettable memories.

Featured Excursions
Hong Island
This limestone island formation is one of the most beautiful in Krabi. The small lagoon is the perfect spot to swim and snorkel, surrounded by a backdrop of limestone cliffs and lush greenery. For a truly unforgettable experience, take a private boat from our hotel near Hong Island, Krabi, and enjoy a relaxing picnic on the sandy shore. Daily long-tail boat to Hong Island: Departs: 9 a.m. Returns: 12:00 a.m.
Four Islands National Park Hopping
Explore the ancient limestone islands and stunning scenery of Krabi on a day boat trip. Visit breathtaking landmarks like See Island, a famous underwater spot for snorkeling, and Poda Island, where the soft white sand beach is surrounded by glistening blue waters.

Our Ladies and Gentlemen, together with representatives from the Sanctuary will guide you on this meaningful experience.

The Krabi Elephant House Sanctuary is a safe haven for Thai elephants, many of which have been rescued from years of labor in the logging or mass-tourism industries. These gentle, sociable, and highly intelligent animals are given the opportunity to live as they would in the wild. The sanctuary also advocates for elephants, educating locals and visitors about the importance of elephant conservation. During this three-hour experience, you will have the opportunity to:
Meet the herd's matriarch, Elephant Thong, and go for a forest walk with the herd
Prepare the herd's lunch, and feed and bathe them
Watch the elephants having a mud bath – and if desired, enjoy a rejuvenating mud spa yourself
Against the magnificent backdrop of Krabi's lush greenery and freshwater lagoons, you can observe and interact with Thai elephants as they roam freely in their peaceful natural habitat, as well as participate in elephant conservation activities.
A visit to the Sanctuary will provide you with new perspectives about these magnificent animals, learning about the importance of elephant conservation from the people who dedicate their lives to Thai elephant protection.

Points of Interest
Hong Island
Hong Islands, or ‘Koh Hong’ in Thai, is one of the islands protected within the area of ‘Than Bokkhorani National Park’. This particular island is popular for its white sandy beaches, coral reefs, flora and fauna as well as beautiful caves. Unlike some crowded touristy places, the Hong Island offers a quiet beach area, close to our resort near the Andaman Sea, for those who wish to unwind and get away from the hustle and bustle of city life.
Distance: 20 minutes by traditional long-tail boat.
Phang Nga Bay
The bay lies just northeast of Phuket and is one of the world’s most scenic wonders. The calm bay features hundreds of magnificent limestone Islands formed over millions of years of tidal movement. The bay was made famous when the movie ‘James Bond’ The Man with Golden Gun was filmed here in the 70’s and hosts some of the most beautiful islands in the world.
Distance: 45 minutes by speed boat
Sports & Entertainment
Diving & Snorkeling at Bamboo Island
One of Thailand’s biggest coral reefs is located in Bamboo Island. The reef has the size of four football pitches and the water varies from 2 to 6 meters. You can glide over one of the most diverse coral reefs in the world combining marine life and all the rainbow colors. Spend the day snorkeling, sunbathing and observing the parrot, puffer and clown fishes!
Distance: 1 hour by speed boat
Phi Phi Island
Phi Phi Island is one of the Andaman Sea’s most famous islands. From our hotel near the Phi Phi Islands, excursions include snorkeling at our secret island and swimming in the Lagoon at Phi Phi Ley, with additional snorkeling at Monkey Beach and Maya Bay. Guests have the option of a sightseeing stroll on Phi Phi Don, with its many small streets for local handicraft shopping.
Distance: 1 hour by speed boat
