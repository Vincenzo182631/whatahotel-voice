## FAQs for InterContinental Bahrain

Does InterContinental Bahrain have a pool?

InterContinental Bahrain has 2 outdoor pool.

Elements Pool and Lounge

A chic outdoor pool with scenery of nature to relax and unwind, while sipping a handcrafted cocktail from Elements Pool and Lounge.

Regency Health Club Pool

Set on the 10th floor of Regency Plaza, the outdoor pool at the health club offers a tranquil retreat above the city, where leisurely swims and unhurried afternoons come together.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Bahrain?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Bahrain for hotel guests.

InterContinental Bahrain provides car parking area for its guests just in front of the hotel as well as shaded parking spaces located at the Regency Plaza building.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Bahrain?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Bahrain.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Bahrain for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Bahrain have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Bahrain has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Bahrain?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Bahrain.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Bahrain?

Check-in at InterContinental Bahrain is from 2:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Bahrain offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Bahrain offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Bahrain from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Bahrain is conveniently located near Bahrain International Airport (BAH), approximately 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Bahrain?

InterContinental Bahrain is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Al Fateh Mosque - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Bahrain National Museum - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Qalat al-Bahrain - 1.86 miles away (3 kilometers)
- Siyadi House Muharraq - 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)
- Bahrain International Circuit
- Bahrain National Theatre - 3.11 miles away (5 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Bahrain puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
