### Parking

- InterContinental Bahrain provides car parking area for its guests just in front of the hotel as well as shaded parking spaces located at the Regency Plaza building.

- Car parking available

### EV charging

- A Mercedes Benz electric vehicle charger is situated in the hotel's front parking lot, marked by a blue parking space. This universal charger accommodates all electric vehicles. Please note that the charger’s usage is at the discretion of the vehicle.
