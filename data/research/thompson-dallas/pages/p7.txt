DART Light Rail

Multiple Dallas Area Rapid Transit (DART) light rail lines serve Downtown Dallas, with nearby stations including Akard Station and St. Paul Station, both within walking distance of Thompson Dallas. Guests staying at Thompson Dallas can use DART to reach key areas across Dallas, including Uptown, Deep Ellum, Dallas Love Field Airport via transfer, Fair Park, and Cotton Bowl Stadium. For access to Fair Park and Cotton Bowl Stadium, take the DART Green Line to Fair Park Station or MLK Jr. Station.

Getting around

DART Bus Service

Downtown Dallas is a central hub for DART bus routes, providing flexible connections throughout the city and toward Arlington. During major summer events, expanded service and special routes may be available to help guests travel between Downtown Dallas and the AT&T stadium area.

Trinity Railway Express (TRE)

The TRE commuter rail connects Downtown Dallas (Union Station) to Fort Worth, with stops in between. Guests can use TRE service as part of a broader transit route toward Arlington and AT&T Stadium, depending on schedules and connections.
