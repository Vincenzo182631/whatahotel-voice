# Luxury Dallas Hotel with Rooftop Pool \| Thompson Dallas, by Hyatt

## DESIGN-FORWARD STAYS IN THE HEART OF DOWNTOWN DALLAS

Since 1965, the First National Bank Tower has anchored the Main Street District with a modern, global vision of Dallas. Reborn as Thompson Dallas, its midcentury lines still cut a commanding figure against the skyline—a timeless symbol of precision, ambition and potential. Today, the site is home to 219 guest rooms and suites, offering a curated home base for travelers who seek a mix of art, culture and culinary excellence—with an instinct for the classics. Designed for leisure, business or both, Thompson Dallas creates compelling scenes from the ground-floor lobby, across restaurants and terraces, to the rooftop pool. From day to night, discover an award-winning culture hub at one of Dallas’ most coveted addressees.

## Rooms & Suites

After a day of exploring the hidden treasures and iconic gems of downtown Dallas, unwind in the residential comforts of any of our 219 rooms and suites. Clean lines, bespoke furnishings, integrated technology, city views and midcentury modern design create inviting spaces to order in, sleep in or just mix up a cocktail.

## Take a Look Around

Dining & Nightlife

The National offers several one-of-a-kind culinary experiences by both local and national award-winning chefs, including a two Michelin star award winner. Host a dinner party or enjoy late-night bites at our hotel’s signature restaurants in downtown Dallas.

Inside The National

A fixture of the Dallas skyline since 1965, The National has long been part of the story of downtown. Today, the landmark begins a new chapter as a richly layered destination where hospitality, art and Texas craftsmanship come together. Thompson Dallas calls The National home alongside Samuel Lynne Galleries, showcasing contemporary art, and Lucchese, carrying forward a legacy of handcrafted Western style. Step beyond your stay and discover more of Dallas culture, all under one roof.

Rooftop Pool & Cabanas

Relax at our resort-style rooftop pool on the 9th floor! Enjoy poolside food and drinks, or rent a private cabana with TVs and luxury lounge seating. Day passes are also available. Pool Hours: 8 am - 9 pm daily. Pool Bar Hours: 10 am - 7 pm daily.

Spa

Treat yourself to a dip in the plunge pools or book a couple's package for side-by-side care at our hotel's full-service spa. Complete your spa experience with a visit to the nail salon.

### Featured review

OUTSANDING \| The Thompson Dallas is an absolutely beautiful hotel. It actually feels like a resort with so many amenities. I had no idea what to expect as I only selected it because of the spa amenities and that it was a newer hotel (2020). I was totally surprised to see how beautiful and luxurious it was. We were ecstatic. The lobby looks like an art gallery and there was actually an art gallery in the hotel. Check in was quick and they were able to move things around to get us in our room a little earlier. Our room was very large with modern decor and CLEAN. The bathroom was spacious with a walk in shower and a separate toilet room. There were robes and slippers available for our use. The only drawback was not having a refrigerator other than that it was perfect. We dined at The Monarch (on the 49th floor) which was fantastic with views of the city. And to top it all off we were chauffeured in their courtesy car which was a Porsche which is not my everyday car 😊. I loved the hotel and would definitely recommend and stay here again.

### Pets are Welcome

We welcome dogs at no additional charge for stays less than 30 nights. For stays greater than 30 nights, a deep cleaning fee may be charged at the hotel's discretion. Dogs must remain in their assigned guest room. A signed waiver is required for all pets. These restrictions do not apply to service animals. Thompson Dallas does not charge a pet fee. Individual pets are restricted to 50 pounds. Maximum number of pets is 2. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
