# Restaurants & Bars

Discover downtown Dallas dining at Thompson Dallas, where four distinct restaurants and bars create a layered culinary experience for guests and locals alike. Start the day at Little Daisy, settle in for rooftop dining and cocktails at Catbird, savor wood-fired modern Italian at Monarch, and head to Kessaku for sushi, seafood, sake and city views from the 50th floor. From breakfast and happy hour to destination dinners and in-room dining, Thompson Dallas brings together some of the most compelling hotel restaurants and rooftop bars in downtown Dallas.

## Little Daisy

Little Daisy is a Franco-American bistro in downtown Dallas, where European flair meets American fare. Enjoy breakfast favorites, artisan daily plates, curated wines, and specialty cocktails, with live music on weekends. Private dining is available for celebrations, business dinners, and intimate gatherings in a refined, inviting setting.

## Catbird

Catbird is a jewel box restaurant and lounge with a rooftop terrace overlooking downtown Dallas. Its striking design and menu set the scene for happy hour, dinner, or late-night bites and cocktails. Live music plays Tuesdays and Wednesdays, with a DJ on Fridays and Saturdays. Private dining is available for celebrations and corporate gatherings.

## Monarch

Located on the 49th floor, Monarch is a wood-fired modern Italian restaurant imagined by two Michelin Star chefs, Danny Grant and team. Serving handmade pastas, steaks and fresh seafood, the overall approach to preparation is light, fresh and technique-driven. Celebrate with an upscale dining experience during your downtown Dallas stay. Monarch is independently managed by Maple Hospitality Group.

## Kessaku

Situated on the 50th floor above downtown Dallas, Kessaku is a cocktail and sake lounge serving the city’s freshest globally-sourced fish, seafood and sushi. Kessaku is independently managed by Maple Hospitality Group. Take in incredible views from this sophisticated spot above the city.
