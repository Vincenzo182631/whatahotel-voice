## Your _secluded_ retreat in Sentosa

Inspired by contemporary Singaporean design with touches of tradition felt throughout the interiors, your villa in Sentosa has been created as a secluded haven for indoor-outdoor living. Discover separate pavilions for day and night, while a private outdoor pool and terrace expand the living experience to encompass the lush and vibrant island landscape.

For guests travelling with family or a close circle of friends, interconnecting villas offer the perfect balance of togetherness and privacy – seamlessly combining shared experiences with the luxury of space.

Our story

## A haven of wellbeing

Immersed in an exquisite natural environment, Raffles Sentosa Singapore is the first all-villa property to open on Singapore's 'island in the city', and the second Raffles address in the country where Raffles was born. Landscaped into a forested hillside, the new ultra luxurious resort features 62 contemporary private pool villas surrounded by exuberant tropical gardens, with views out over the South China Sea.

Read More

Raffles Sentosa Spa

## A sanctuary of relaxation, blending traditional healing with contemporary wellness.

To step into our resort's natural haven is to be cocooned in holistic wellbeing at every turn. Housed in a heritage building, Raffles Spa is where serenity meets vitality in a canvas of wellbeing experiences encompassing mindfulness, massage, fitness, hydrotherapy and science-based skincare. Embark on a journey of self-care and rejuvenation in harmony with the rhythms of nature, guided by our expert practitioners.

## Frequently asked questions

### What amenities are available at Raffles Sentosa?

The resort offers private pool villas, wellness facilities, and exquisite dining options for a luxurious experience.

### How can I book a villa at Raffles Sentosa?

You can easily book your stay by visiting the Raffles website or contacting their reservations team directly.

### What dining options are available at Raffles Sentosa?

Enjoy a variety of authentic flavors, with dining experiences that reflect both local and international cuisines.

### What activities can I enjoy during my stay?

Engage in wellness activities, explore the lush surroundings, or relax by your private pool for a rejuvenating experience.

### When is the best time to visit Raffles Sentosa?

The ideal time to visit is during Singapore's dry season, typically from February to April, for pleasant weather and outdoor activities.
