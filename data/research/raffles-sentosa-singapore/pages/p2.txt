A healing sanctuary infused with island charm

To step into our resort's natural haven is to be immersed in holistic wellbeing at every turn, and to feel your senses gently reawakening away from urban stresses. Guiding you on a journey of rejuvenation, in harmony with the rhythms of nature, are the expert practitioners at our Raffles Sentosa Spa. Here, within a characterful heritage building, serenity meets vitality in a canvas of experiences to enhance your physical, mental and emotional wellbeing. Blending traditional healing with contemporary techniques, our holistic approach is based on movement, nourishment, restorative wellness and connection, encompassing yoga, mindfulness, massage, fitness, hydrotherapy and science-based skincare. A menu for life, gently nudging you back into lasting balance.

Away from the spa, the resort has convenient access to Tanjong Beach and two award-winning neighbouring golf courses.

## Our philosophy

At Raffles Sentosa Spa, we believe in empowering individuals to thrive at every stage of life with grace, strength and purpose. We want to inspire a world where vitality, balance and longevity are not just goals but a way of life, and we aim to provide the tools and knowledge to enhance wellbeing for the long term, well beyond the spa. Our spa sanctuary is a transformative space where ancient wisdom harmonises with modern science and innovation. Here, our holistic approach works its magic, unlocking vitality in body, mind and spirit, in the knowledge that longevity is about living well, not just living long.​ We partner with expert local practitioners and world-class wellness and beauty brands, and our products are sourced with environmental sustainability in mind, reflecting our social responsibilities.
