A Season of Extraordinary Moments
Embrace the spirit of the holidays through thoughtfully curated experiences that blend tradition and the natural beauty of Dorado Beach, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve. From intimate dining experiences and wellness rituals to festive gatherings and signature celebrations, every moment is crafted to inspire connection and wonder.

A Year of Meaningful Celebrations
Explore a thoughtfully curated collection of festive celebrations, culinary experiences, wellness rituals, and family traditions inspired by the beauty of Puerto Rico and the spirit of the season.

Festive Culinary Gatherings
Traditional Thanksgiving Dinner
Join us at COA this Thanksgiving for an exquisite celebration of tradition and taste. Our chefs present a family-style menu, thoughtfully curated to highlight the season’s finest ingredients, masterfully prepared in “Mi Brasa,” our signature wood-burning oven.
Christmas Dinner
Celebrate Christmas through an evening of exceptional cuisine, heartfelt traditions, and meaningful connection. Gather with family and friends for a thoughtfully curated dinner showcasing seasonal flavors, festive specialties, and the warmth of Puerto Rican hospitality. Set within the beauty of Dorado Beach, this memorable dining experience captures the spirit of the season.
New Year's Eve Celebration
Welcome the arrival of a new year through an evening of refined dining, thoughtful celebration, and meaningful connection. Savor a specially curated culinary journey inspired by the season, accompanied by live music and immersive moments that honor the spirit of the occasion. As midnight approaches, gather beneath the stars to toast new beginnings in the intimate setting of Dorado Beach, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve.
