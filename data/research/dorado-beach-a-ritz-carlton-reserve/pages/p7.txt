Golf in Dorado Beach, Puerto Rico
The resort’s two courses present players with both the joy and challenge of the sport. Designed to capture the beauty of nature, the Tournament Players Club courses offer 18 holes each and use native sand, man-made rivers and contoured bunkers to test even the most seasoned golfer.
East Course
A timeless masterpiece reimagined, the East Course reflects the vision of Robert Trent Jones Jr., who modernized his father’s iconic seaside design while preserving its storied character. Stretching over 7,200 yards, this classic championship course challenges players of all levels with its sweeping ocean vistas, native beach-sand bunkers, and impeccably smooth greens. Each hole is named for the trees that frame it, some still bearing the marks of errant shots from decades past, honoring a rich heritage that continues to define this legendary course by the sea.

West Course
Debuting on November 23, the West Course introduces a new chapter in the Dorado Beach golf legacy. This 18-hole, par-72 masterpiece features a parkland-style layout defined by tree-lined fairways, gently undulating terrain, and a thoughtful design that rewards both strategy and skill. Unlike the seaside East Course, the West offers a serene inland experience, with holes that shift in direction to create a dynamic interplay of wind and challenge.

Discover Dorado Beach
Land Experiences
Land carries the stories of those who stood there before. It invites exploration. It gives life and helps it grow. Experiencing the land in Puerto Rico includes strolling amid the cobbled streets and colorful houses of Old San Juan; trekking through a preserved cave that ends with a breathtaking view; and following the journey of Puerto Rican coffee from bean to cup.
Rockefeller Nature Trail
Winding its way through the resort's 50 acres, this 11-mile trail takes guests past the two courses, along the soft sandy beaches, and through tree-shaded forests where moist, cool air offers relief from the sun. Named after the resort's original developer Laurance S. Rockefeller, the trail can be explored on foot, by bike or with a golf cart.

Outdoor Adventures
Rockefeller Nature Trail
Winding its way through the resort’s 50 acres, this 11-mile trail takes guests past the two golf courses, along the soft-sand beaches and through tree-shaded forests where moist, cool air offers relief from the sun. Named after the resort’s original developer, Laurance S. Rockefeller, the trail can be explored on foot, by bike or with a golf cart.
Horseback Riding
Explore the private natural reserve of La Hacienda Esperanza, where a secluded beach and historic estate are among the discoveries.
Ziplining
The longest zipline in the Americas soars above Orocovis in Toroverde Adventure Park, offering both a thrilling ride and a breathtaking view.
Cave Exploration
A hike through Cueva Ventana passes preserved geological formations and leads to a limestone cliff overlooking a lush mountain valley.
Rainforest Experience
El Yunque Tropical Rainforest is home to more than 240 species of plants and animals, and a hike along its rustic trails is rewarded with a refreshing dip at La Mina Falls.
Exploring History
Puerto Rico is an island of influences, from the Taino to the Spanish, told through its architecture, artwork, archaeological sites and traditions.
Old San Juan
The city’s historic center begs exploration with colorful houses lining cobblestoned streets, galleries featuring local artists and centuries-old buildings overlooking the ocean.
Archaeological Adventure
The story of the native Taino people is partly told through ancient petroglyphs found near the Las Delicias waterfalls.
Cigar Making
The secrets behind the artistry and craftsmanship of hand-rolled cigars are revealed on this tour and sampling.
Dorado Beach's Naturalist Experience
The Dorado Beach's Naturalist Experience inspires guests to connect with the world around them in hopes that they will work towards preserving it for future generations. The program highlights Puerto Rico’s rich native culture and unique ecosystem through scheduled outdoor group experiences designed for children & adults. Schedule varies based on seasonality and weather considerations. Activities are based on availability only, reservations are required. Must be booked 48hrs in advance. Contact us: +1 787-278-7222.

Above the Water and Below
Sea Experiences
Start your journey into the intriguing depths and discover the beauty of Puerto Rico's coastal waters, a world both familiar and yet brimming with undiscovered wonders. Immerse yourself in the classic allure of a private yacht excursion, where modern luxury meets the timeless tranquility of the sea. Discover the beauty of vibrant coral reefs teeming with life or find a private sanctuary as you cast your line into the deep blue.
Set Sail
From the coastal cities of Puerto Rico, the sea and ocean beckon the curious, the adventurous and the free-spirited. To experience the water, you simply need to feel it gently bob beneath you, hear its soothing hum, breathe in its salty air or look out at its vastness.
Yacht Experience
Venture to surrounding islands including Culebra, Vieques, Icacos, Palomino or Palominito aboard a private yacht. Available vessels can host 10 to 12 guests.
Catamaran & Cay Excursion
Spend a day exploring the colorful cays surrounding Puerto Rico. Set out on a luxury catamaran to remote locations where crystal-clear waters are all yours to explore.
Rainforest Experience
Explore the enchanting paths of El Yunque Tropical Rainforest, home to more than 240 species of plants and animals. Hike through the rustic trails that lead you to La Mina Falls and dive into the clear refreshing waters.
Beyond the Surface
Within the depths of the ocean, an entire world awaits discovery. The diversity of Puerto Rico’s natural wonders extends to the water, where fish, coral and other marine life make their homes.

Bioluminescent Bay
Among Puerto Rico’s hidden treasures, its three bioluminescent bays emit a magical glow best seen from the seat of a kayak at night.
Scuba Diving
Puerto Rico’s cays are a diver’s dream, home to colorful marine life and breathtaking coral formations. Explore Isla del Desecheo or Cayo Diablo on these all-day excursions.
Fishing
Fishing is both an adventure and a retreat. Using state-of-the-art gear, guests of all skill levels can try their hand at inshore and offshore, tarpon and hook-and-cook excursions.
Water Parks and Pools
Water is a healer, a powerful force of nature, a necessity for life. At the resort, where the ocean sits just at its edge, it is a source of both thrilling adventure and quiet reflection. Experiences extend beyond the sea to include a family-friendly waterpark and two swimming pools.

Pools

Puerto Rico Outdoor Experiences
Coquí Experience
Search for Puerto Rico's beloved coquí frog in the Pterocarpus Forest, guided by naturalists who will share insights about their habitat. Based on availability. Reservations required. Must be booked 48hrs in advance.
Wildlife Scavenger Hunt
