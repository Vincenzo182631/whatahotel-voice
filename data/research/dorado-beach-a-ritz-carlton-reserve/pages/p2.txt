Suites & Rooms
Dorado Beach Villas, Suites and Residences feature signatures tied to Puerto Rico: the ocean just outside your door; the personal attention of an Embajador; outdoor living space; an open-air shower; and artwork capturing the colors of nature.
Suite guests enjoy a complimentary Honor Bar
When staying in our Suites, guests may enjoy a thoughtfully curated complimentary Honor Bar featuring a selection of premium beverages and snacks, included to enhance the comfort and convenience of your stay. Valid through October 31, 2026
