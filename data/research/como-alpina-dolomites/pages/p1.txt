# COMO Alpina Dolomites

Home/Italy/COMO Alpina Dolomites

COMO Alpina Dolomites is a modern architectural masterpiece, cradled by the sheer cliffs of a UNESCO Natural World Heritage site in northern Italy. Our location on South Tyrol's Alpe di Siusi one of Europe's largest, most beautiful high-altitude plateaus - offers guests unique direct access to an exceptional natural setting and a wide range of mountain experiences throughout the seasons. Our first alpine property has been awarded One MICHELIN Key in the 2026 MICHELIN Guide Italy hotel selection, marking its third consecutive year of recognition since 2024.

Please note: COMO Alpina Dolomites will remain open until October 25th before closing for a seasonal break. We look forward to welcoming you back on December 4th for the winter season in the Dolomites.

**Location**

### Crowning the Dolomites

Located on the Alpe di Siusi, COMO Alpina Dolomites gives you easy access to hiking, skiing and more in and around this UNESCO-protected valley in Italy's South Tyrol - part of the world-famous Val Gardena known for its winter sports.

### Accommodation

## Contemporary Alpine Comfort

Our 60 rooms combine soft colours with quality furnishings finessed by South Tyrol's traditional artisans. Each room offers sweeping views of the Dolomites range, its alpine meadows and jagged peaks.

## Discover the Dolomites

Explore the Alpe di Siusi, your year-round base for outdoor adventures. Hike breathtaking trails, enjoy horseback rides, and tackle high-adrenaline mountain biking in the wider Val Gardena. ### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Where is COMO Alpina Dolomites located in the Italy?

COMO Alpina Dolomites is a luxury hotel and wellness resort located on the Alpe di Siusi (Seiser Alm) in South Tyrol, northern Italy — part of the UNESCO World Heritage–listed Dolomites. Set at an altitude of 1,800 metres, the property offers panoramic views of mountain peaks and alpine meadows. It’s approximately 30 minutes from Bolzano and around two hours from Innsbruck or Verona airports, making it one of the most easily accessible hotels in the Dolomites for year-round alpine adventures and wellness

#### 2Does COMO Alpina Dolomites offer ski-in ski-out access?

Yes, COMO Alpina Dolomites offers direct ski-in ski-out access from our slopeside resort in Alpe di Siusi, within the Dolomiti Superski region of Val Gardena. Guests can step straight onto the Alpe di Siusi ski slopes, enjoying world-class winter sports and panoramic views of the Dolomites.

#### 3is COMO Alpina Dolomites a romantic hotel for couples in the Dolomites?

COMO Alpina Dolomites is an ideal romantic hotel in the Dolomites for couples seeking privacy, alpine elegance, and breathtaking views. With wellness suites, fine dining, and spa rituals inspired by the Alps, it’s a perfect romantic getaway in the Dolomites.

#### 4Is COMO Alpina Dolomites suitable for a family holiday in the Dolomites?

Yes — COMO Alpina Dolomites is recognised as a family-friendly resort in the Dolomites, offering spacious rooms and suites with flexible sleeping arrangements, a dedicated Play by COMO kids-club for children aged 4–12, and child-friendly dining menus including complimentary meals for under-5s and reduced rates for ages 6–12

#### 5Why do travellers choose COMO Alpina Dolomites for a luxury mountain getaway in the Dolomites?

Travellers choose COMO Alpina Dolomites for its location on the Alpe di Siusi (Seiser Alm) in South Tyrol’s UNESCO-listed Dolomites, offering direct ski-in/ski-out access and sweeping mountain views. The hotel combines modern alpine design, a COMO Shambhala wellness spa, and seasonal cuisine that celebrates local ingredients — making it one of the best luxury hotels in the Dolomites for relaxation and adventure.

#### 6Is COMO Alpina Dolomites open year-round for both summer and winter stays?

Yes. COMO Alpina Dolomites operates on a seasonal basis, welcoming guests during the winter season from December to March and the summer and autumn season from June to October. During winter, the hotel offers ski-in/ski-out access and alpine winter experiences, while the warmer months are ideal for guided hiking, cycling, and outdoor adventures in the Dolomites. This seasonal approach allows guests to experience the region at its best, combining alpine wellness, nature, and adventure during peak times of the year.

#### 7Is COMO Alpina Dolomites committed to sustainability and eco-friendly practices?

Yes. COMO Alpina Dolomites is a sustainable luxury hotel in the Dolomites, designed with eco-conscious materials and operated using energy-efficient heating and ventilation systems suited to its alpine environment. The resort emphasises responsible sourcing, including local and organic ingredients in its cuisine, and minimises its environmental footprint through water and waste management initiatives.
