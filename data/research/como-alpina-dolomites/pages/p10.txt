# DINING AT COMO ALPINA DOLOMITES

Cuisine at COMO Alpina Dolomites pays homage to the finest local, regional products, with a light, elegant COMO twist. You have the choice of the bright and casual Alpina Chalet Grill and Bar, the cosy and intimate Sassolungo, and the welcoming Lobby Lounge for casual bites and drinks. No meal is enjoyed without spectacular views of the mountains.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1How many restaurants and bars are there at COMO Alpina Dolomites?

The resort features three restaurants and a bar, offering elegant mountain dining and relaxed après-ski atmospheres, all with panoramic alpine views.

#### 2What type of cuisine is served in the hotel restaurants?

Guests can enjoy refined Italian, South Tyrolean, Mediterranean and international dishes, prepared with fresh local ingredients and a focus on seasonal flavors.

#### 3Are vegetarian and vegan options available?

Yes. COMO Alpina Dolomites offers a wide range of vegetarian and vegan dishes, thoughtfully crafted to meet all dietary preferences.

#### 4Does COMO Alpina Dolomites propose healthy food options?

Absolutely. The menus feature COMO Shambhala Cuisine, emphasizing nutrient-rich, organic, and wellness-focused meals for balanced nutrition.

#### 5Can private dining be arranged with mountain views?

Yes. Guests can enjoy exclusive private dining experiences with breathtaking mountain views, perfect for romantic evenings or special celebrations.

#### 6Are children’s menus available?

Yes — COMO offers child-friendly dining menus featuring healthy and delicious options tailored for younger guests. Children under five dine complimentary, while those aged six to twelve enjoy meals at a reduced rate.

#### 7Are wine tastings available featuring Italian and South Tyrolean wines?

Yes, COMO Alpina Dolomites hosts wine tastings and pairing experiences, showcasing the finest Italian and South Tyrolean wines curated by expert sommeliers.
