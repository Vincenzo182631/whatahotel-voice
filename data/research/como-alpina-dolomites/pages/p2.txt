# Accommodation

With meticulous design and functional furnishings, our 60 rooms offer a welcoming retreat. Large windows frame panoramic views, transforming each space into a private sanctuary.

This summer we completed the refurbishment of our Chalet Suites, designed to elevate both style and comfort while maintaining the welcoming spirit of the Alps. Relaxing shades of blue, grey and white evoke the purity of ice and sky, balanced by natural wood details that add warmth and intimacy. Guests can now enjoy larger two-bedroom suites or flexible connecting options, perfect for families or groups seeking both space and togetherness.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1What types of rooms and suites are available at COMO Alpina Dolomites?

COMO Alpina Dolomites offers 59 refined rooms and suites across the Main and Chalet Buildings, each designed with natural wood, soft alpine tones, and panoramic Dolomite views. Options include Mountain Rooms with balconies or garden access, Mountain Suites featuring private saunas, and spacious Chalet Suites with separate living areas. The signature COMO Suite accommodates up to six guests, combining luxury space and breathtaking scenery for the ultimate alpine stay.

#### 2Is breakfast included in the rate?

Yes — every stay at COMO Alpina Dolomites includes a daily gourmet breakfast. Guests can enjoy a farm-to-table buffet featuring local South Tyrolean produce, homemade pastries, fresh fruit, and healthy options from the COMO Shambhala Cuisine menu, thoughtfully crafted for wellness-focused travellers.

#### 3Do rooms offer views of the Dolomite mountains?

Almost all rooms and suites at COMO Alpina Dolomites offer stunning panoramic views of the Dolomites UNESCO World Heritage peaks. Guests can wake up to sunrise over the mountains or unwind at sunset from the comfort of their private balcony or large window views.

#### 4Are family suites or connecting rooms available?

Yes — COMO Alpina Dolomites offers family suites and interconnecting rooms, ideal for families or small groups visiting the Dolomites. These spacious accommodations feature multiple sleeping areas and private balconies overlooking the mountain landscape, allowing everyone to unwind together while enjoying the comfort and privacy of a luxury alpine retreat in the heart of the Dolomites.

#### 5Can extra bed or baby cot be provided for children?

Yes. Extra beds and baby cots can be arranged upon request, ensuring a comfortable stay for families with young children.

#### 6Are accessible rooms available for guests with mobility needs?

Yes — COMO Alpina Dolomites offers beautifully designed accessible rooms that maintain the property’s signature alpine elegance while meeting the highest standards of comfort and accessibility. These rooms feature step-free layouts, wide entrances, and accessible bathrooms without compromising luxury.
