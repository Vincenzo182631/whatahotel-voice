# Sellaronda Ski Adventure

### Guided Sellaronda ski circuit tour

Share

Experience the famous Sellaronda ski circuit, an impressive route that takes you across four magnificent mountain passes in the heart of the Dolomites.

Designed for confident skiers, this full-day tour offers the chance to explore untouched slopes without repeating a single lift ride. Led by an experienced local guide, you'll enjoy stunning panoramic views and the warmth of traditional mountain huts along the way.

Book now mailto:concierge.alpinadolomites@comohotels.com

### Experience Details

- **Location**: Sellaronda, Dolomites
- **Tour options:** Join a guided group tour offered as part of our complimentary weekly programme (no fixed days) or opt for a private guided experience tailored to individual preferences
- **Start time:** 8:30am
- **Duration:** Full day
- **Difficulty level:** suitable for advanced skiers; participants must be comfortable on black slopes
- **What to wear or bring:** ski gear, Dolomiti Superski lift pass, cash, sun cream, water
- **Inclusions:** professional guide
- **Price:** For pricing details please contact our Concierge team, ski equipment can be rented from the Outdoor Activities Centre

### Terms and Conditions

- Reservations for private tours are required at least 24 hours in advance.
- Group tours can be booked the day before the experience.
- Experience is non-refundable and non-amendable unless otherwise stated.
- Experience may be cancelled or postponed depending on weather conditions or unforeseen circumstances.
