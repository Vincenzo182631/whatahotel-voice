# Sella Ronda E-Bike Tour

### An iconic alpine cycling adventure

Embark on one of the most iconic e-bike adventures in the Alps with our Sellaronda e-bike tour.

This full-day experience takes you across four mountain passes, winding through rocky trails and breathtaking panoramas as you circle the majestic Sella Group.

With a professional guide by your side, you'll explore 58km of varied terrain, taking in some of the most spectacular views the Dolomites have to offer and enjoying scenic rest stops in traditional mountain huts.

Book now mailto:concierge.alpinadolomites@comohotels.com

### Experience Details

- **Location**: Sella Ronda, Dolomites
- **Tour options:** Signature private guided experience
- **Start time:** 8.30am or 9.00am
- **Duration:** Full day
- **Difficulty level:** Moderate, suitable for advanced bikers; participants must be confident riding on black slopes
- **What to wear or bring:** Sun hat, sun cream, water and weather-appropriate clothing
- **Inclusions:** Expert local guide
- **Price**: For pricing information, please contact our Concierge team via concierge.alpinadolomites@comohotels.com

### Terms and Conditions

- Advance booking is required and subject to availability
- A cancellation policy applies and will be communicated at the time of booking
- Experiences may be cancelled or postponed in the case of adverse weather conditions
- For pricing details please contact the concierge.
