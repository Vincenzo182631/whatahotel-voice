# Guided Mindful Hike

### Awaken your senses in the Dolomites

Share

- Share in WhatsApp
- Share in WeChat
- Share in Facebook
- Share via Email
- Share in Twitter

Start your morning with a peaceful guided hike through the alpine trails of the Alpe di Siusi to Bullaccia Hill.

This experience combines movement and mindfulness, encouraging you to slow down, embrace the beauty of your surroundings, and welcome the day with clarity and intention.

Along the way, your guide will lead gentle mindfulness practices designed to deepen your connection to nature and yourself.

Book now mailto:concierge.alpinadolomites@comohotels.com

### Experience Details

- **Location:** Alpe di Siusi, Dolomites
- **Tour option:** This experience is available once a week. Please contact the Concierge team for the schedule of the week
- **Start time:** 7.00am
- **Duration:** Approximately three hours
- **Difficulty level:** Suitable for active individuals with a mindful spirit
- **What to wear or bring:** Sun hat, sun cream, water and weather-appropriate clothing
- **Inclusions:** Professional hiking guide
- **Price:** For pricing information, please contact our Concierge team via concierge.alpinadolomites@comohotels.com

### Terms and Conditions

- Advance booking is suggested
- Experiences may be cancelled or postponed in the case of adverse weather conditions.
- Please contact the concierge for pricing details. This activity is part of our weekly wellness programme.
