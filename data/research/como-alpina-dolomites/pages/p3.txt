### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Does COMO Alpina Dolomites have a spa?

Yes — COMO Alpina Dolomites features a luxury COMO Shambhala Retreat spa, one of the most exclusive wellness sanctuaries in the Dolomites. Guests can enjoy holistic treatments, massages, and alpine-inspired therapies that promote relaxation and renewal. Designed in harmony with the surrounding mountains, the spa combines modern wellness with natural serenity, making it a leading destination for luxury spa experiences in the Dolomites.

#### 2What treatments and therapies are available at COMO Alpina Dolomites?

Guests can enjoy massages, facials, body rituals, and hydrotherapy experiences, all using natural COMO Shambhala products. Treatments draw on the region’s healing alpine ingredients, creating a deeply rejuvenating connection to the Dolomites’ natural environment.

#### 3Are yoga, Pilates, and meditation sessions offered at COMO Alpina Dolomites?

Yes — COMO Alpina Dolomites offers daily yoga, Pilates, and guided meditation sessions, suitable for all levels. These practices enhance balance, mindfulness, and inner calm, set against the tranquil backdrop of the Dolomite mountains.

#### 4Is COMO Alpina Dolomites a good choice for a wellness holiday in the Dolomites?

Yes — COMO Alpina Dolomites is widely regarded as one of the best wellness resorts in the Dolomites, blending luxury, nature, and holistic well-being. Through its signature COMO Shambhala philosophy, the resort offers a complete wellness experience — from spa rituals and mindful movement classes to nutritionally balanced cuisine — making it an ideal destination for a wellness-focused alpine escape.

#### 5Does COMO Alpina Dolomites offer spa and wellness packages for couples in Alpe di Siusi?

Yes — COMO Alpina Dolomites curates exclusive couples’ spa and wellness packages in Alpe di Siusi, combining side-by-side massages, private rituals, and romantic alpine experiences. Designed for connection and rejuvenation, these luxury spa treatments let couples unwind together while surrounded by the natural beauty of the Dolomites.
