# Snow Power Biking

### A winter workout

Share

Embark on an adventurous e-bike tour across the stunning Alpe di Siusi, tackling snowy trails on spiked winter tyres.

This experience is perfect for advanced bikers seeking a unique way to explore serene beauty of the Dolomites, away from the usual ski slopes.

Available as a private tour on request, options range from one to six-hour rides, allowing you to explore and enjoy the breathtaking landscapes at your own pace.

Book now mailto:concierge.alpinadolomites@comohotels.com

### Experience Details

- **Location:** Alpe di Siusi
- **Tour type:** Private guided tour
- **Start time:** From 9:30am
- **Duration:** One to six hours
- **Difficulty level: Hard,** advanced biking experience required
- **What to wear or bring:** Water, sun cream, appropriate sport clothing for winter, sturdy footwear
- **Inclusions:** E-bike, helmet and guide
- Price: For pricing information, please contact our Concierge team via concierge.alpinadolomites@comohotels.com

### Terms and Conditions

- Reservations are required at least 24 hours in advance.
- This guided private experience is limited to one guest maximum. An additional guide can be arranged for additional guests.
- Full payment is required at the time of booking.
- Cancellations must be made at least 10 days before the scheduled start of your experience. Experience is non-refundable and non-amendable unless otherwise stated.
- Experience may be cancelled or postponed depending on weather conditions.
- For pricing details please contact the concierge. E-bikes with spikes are available for rent in our in-house Ski Rental.
