# SEE THE WORLD IN A NEW LIGHT

Home/Italy/COMO Alpina Dolomites/Experiences/Festive

See the festive season in a new light at COMO Alpina Dolomites. Spend days exploring the snow-covered mountains, then gather for seasonal feasts, live music and Christmas traditions. Discover our Christmas Forest, make time for COMO Shambhala wellness and share celebrations with family and friends, from Christmas morning to the arrival of 2027.

View festive programme View festive programme

**Festive Mountain Escape**

### Your Christmas Stay

Celebrate Christmas in Alpe di Siusi, surrounded by the snow-capped peaks of the Dolomites. Enjoy festive dining, live music and days exploring the mountains, with daily breakfast, afternoon treats and access to our wellness facilities included in your stay.

### What's Included

- Inclusive of daily breakfast at Sassolungo
- Complimentary daily sweet pastries and savoury treats in our Lobby Lounge and Bar from 3.00pm to 5.00pm
- Festive dining and experiences throughout the festive period, available at an additional charge with advance booking
- Play by COMO festive programme with seasonal

activities for younger guests
- Access to the outdoor heated swimming pool
- Access to COMO Shambhala wellness facilities

### Terms and Conditions

- Rates include government tax.
- A minimum length of stay applies.
- Cancellations must be made at least 21 days prior to arrival. Cancellations made within 21 days of arrival will be charged in full.

## FESTIVE HIGHLIGHTS

Previous SlideNext Slide

**December 4th 2026 to January 2nd 2027**

### THE CHRISTMAS FOREST

Experience the enchantment of our very own winter wonderland on the terrace, surrounded by Christmas trees and filled with festive treats. Enjoy hot chocolate, mulled wine, apple juice, panettone, biscuits and marshmallows in a cosy Christmas atmosphere.

The Christmas Forest is open daily from 3.00pm to 5.00pm from 4 December to 2 January, with traditional live music every Advent Sunday from 4.00pm to 5.00pm.

**December 24th**

### Christmas Eve Dinner

Celebrate Christmas Eve with a delightful selection of culinary experiences across our restaurants. Savour a festive seafood buffet at Sassolungo Restaurant, enjoy an elegant à la carte menu at Trattoria dell’Alpe, or dine à la carte at Alpina Chalet Grill & Bar.

Throughout the evening, strolling festive music will add a touch of Christmas magic to the celebrations.

**December 25th**

### Christmas In The Dolomites

Christmas morning in the mountains begins with a special surprise for our younger guests, as presents await to add a little extra magic to the festive day.

In the evening, join us for a festive Christmas Aperitif at the Lobby Lounge & Bar, followed by a choice of dining experiences: an à la carte menu at Sassolungo Restaurant, a refined Gala Set Menu at Trattoria dell’Alpe, or an à la carte menu at Alpina Chalet Restaurant.

Strolling festive music throughout the evening will add to the magical Christmas atmosphere.

## New Year's Eve In The Dolomites

Welcome 2027 with an unforgettable New Year’s Eve celebration in the heart of the Dolomites. Begin the evening with a festive aperitif at the Lobby Lounge & Bar, followed by an exquisite gala dinner at Sassolungo Restaurant and Trattoria dell’Alpe. Guests may also enjoy an à la carte menu at Alpina Chalet Grill & Bar.

As midnight approaches, the celebrations continue with late-night bites, a spectacular fire and LED show, a photo booth, DJ sounds and magical entertainment throughout the evening, creating the perfect atmosphere to welcome the New Year in style.

### What's Included

- Inclusive of breakfast at Sassolungo
- Complimentary daily sweet pastries and savoury treats in our Lobby Lounge and Bar from 3.00pm to 5.00pm
- Festive dining and experiences throughout the festive period, available at an additional charge with advance booking
- Play by COMO festive programme with seasonal

activities for younger  guests
- Access to the outdoor heated swimming pool
- Access to COMO Shambhala wellness facilities

### Terms and Conditions

- Rates include government tax
- A minimum length of stay applies
- Cancellations must be made at least 21 days prior to arrival. Cancellations made within 21 days of arrival will be charged in full.

### SHAKEN OR STIRRED

Begin your New Year’s Eve celebrations with an elegant aperitif. Enjoy a drink in the Lobby Lounge, accompanied by the sounds of our DJ as you ease into the evening.

### NEW YEAR GALA DINNER

Celebrate the final evening of the year in style with an exquisite New Year’s Eve Gala Dinner at Sassolungo Restaurant and Trattoria dell’Alpe, where fine cuisine and a festive atmosphere set the stage for an unforgettable evening in the Dolomites.
