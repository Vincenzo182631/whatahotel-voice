## The New All Day Bar at the Lobby of King George Hotel

Nestled within the elegance of the King George Hotel lobby, George, The Lobby Bar redefines the art of all-day hospitality, offering a refined experience from morning espresso and brunch to a relaxed lunch. Each moment of the day is elevated by cosmopolitan charm and sophisticated design, inviting guests to linger and indulge. As evening falls, the bar becomes the vibrant centerpiece of the night. Signature cocktails, inspired by the six senses and crafted around a zero-waste philosophy, are prepared tableside in a captivating ritual featuring aromatic bitters and rare expressions of gin and tequila. Inventive bites perfectly complement each drink, transforming the evening into an unforgettable journey of taste, atmosphere, and style.

## Daily Epicurean Moment

Experience an authentic indigenous culinary moment at the MICHELIN-Starred Tudor Hall Restaurant, available daily at 5 p.m. Enjoy "Skotyri," a traditional cheese from Ios, one of the most beautiful islands in the Cyclades.
