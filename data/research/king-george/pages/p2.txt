# Book Your Escape to Our Luxury Rooms and Suites in Athens

## Accommodations

Individual and reflective of the property’s rich legacy, our Athens accommodation for guests spans 102 luxury hotel rooms and suites designed for comfort and enriched living. Select rooms feature a private...See More patio or balcony, while all offer oversized windows, hand-picked antiques, classic cornicing, hardwood floors and plush silk drapes. Settle in with The Luxury Collection Bed dressed in custom Frette linen for deep comfort and truly restful sleep. Modern technology, including flat-screen TV and USB sockets, supports an effortless stay. Service is attentive, with nightly turndown and a fully stocked refreshment center, with guests often highlighting the room comfort and high cleanliness standards. Each elegant room includes a marble-lined en-suite bathroom with a rainfall shower, and many offer a bathtub. Luxuriant bathrobes, slippers, and premium bath amenities complete the experience.See Less
