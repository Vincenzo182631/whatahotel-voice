# Welcome to King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens

## Embrace opulence at our luxury hotel in Athens, Greece

Among the finest of the luxury hotels in Athens, Greece, King George Hotel dates back to 1930 and sits in the heart of Syntagma Square. This central Athens hotel is perfectly placed for the city’s wonders-...See More the Agora, Plaka, Thission, and the Monastiraki Flea Market-along with upmarket boutiques, world-renowned museums, and the vibrant business district. Our elegant Athens hotel in Greece is also within easy reach of the Acropolis. Stay in one of the 102 rooms and suites of this Luxury Collection Hotel, including a Penthouse Suite with a private outdoor pool overlooking the fabled Acropolis. Dine at the Michelin Star-awarded Tudor Hall Restaurant for modern cuisine and incomparable landmark views. Discover our walkable location and outstanding personalized service, making this the ideal Athens 5-star hotel for luxury city breaks and executive stays.See Less

## Greek Artists

Our hotel houses a number of vivid paintings exclusively curated by renowned Greek artists such as Pavlos Samios and Kostis Georgiou or even the unique Golden Olive Trees by Constantinos Valaes.

### Art in The Lobby

The majestic lobby of this Luxury Collection hotel, evokes the most splendid feelings from the very first second; the Murano chandeliers, the bright marbles and the finest furnishing are just some of the elegant touch points.

Given that the refined hotel hosts not only permanent but temporary collections as well, one may admire up close a number of vivid paintings exclusively curated by renowned Greek...See More artists such as Pavlos Samios, Sofia Petropoulou, Sofia Maria Xenaki and Kostis Georgiou or even the unique Golden Olive Trees by Constantinos Vales, which elegantly pose throughout the lobby and finely immerse the sense of the most traditional tree of Greek history; the Olive Tree.

If you love art, we invite you to visit the city of Athens and at least the lobby of King George, Athens to browse around and let another modern side of Greece enchant you; an art collection experience not to be missed.See Less

A Collection of Artworks from Greek Artists

The Historic King George Hotel, located in the heart of Athens, is a proud Ambassador of the contemporary Greek art scene, by presenting to its cosmopolitan travelers, a rotating collection of artworks from Greek Artists.

Many well-known Greek painters are pleased that their works reach the public in a more indirect and perhaps deeper and more meaningful way- with their art being displayed in places where visitors move unsuspectingly. Browsing at their leisure, they discover works of art that touch their souls.  From there, visitors can also choose to buy through the artists' representative at the hotel.

Browse around the King George's lobby and let another modern side of Greece enchant you. This magestic lobby will then have achieved one of its goals: a new and vibrant way of communicating art.

## The Luxury Collection Concierge

Available 24 hours a day, seven days a week

Our Luxury Collection Concierge, Apostolos Papayiannakis, is a bilingual, certified destination expert proudly wearing the "Les Clefs d’Or," is eager to reveal the secret treasures of Athens.

Learn More

In addition to arranging full private tours of historical landmarks, such as the New Acropolis Museum, Archaeological Museum, Benaki Museum, and Byzantine Museum, Mr. Papayiannakis and his team are pleased to recommend and direct guests to a selection of rare treasures and sites, both in and out of the city. They are also available to assist with dinner reservations and shopping tours, and procure tickets to everything from concerts and plays to festivals, trains, airplanes, and ferries. The team can also arrange a tee time at any number of golf courses in the Glyfada area, approximately 20 minutes away.

Within the hotel, they are on hand to help with floral services, babysitting referrals, transportation services, and much more.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens?

The check-in time at King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens. One pet is allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of €200.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens?

King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens booking.

What is the closest airport near King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens?

The closest airport near King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens is Athens International Airport Eleftherios Venizelos (ATH). ATH is located approximately 35.0 KM from the hotel.

Does King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, King George, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
