Local Attractions

### Petronas Twin Towers

The Petronas Twin Towers are the world's tallest twin towers, boasting 88 storeys and breathtaking skyline views. Explore the world-class architecture and endless shopping experience in Kuala Lumpur.

### Merdeka Square

Lookig for things to do near Petronas Twin Towers? Merdeka Square is a must-visit destination in Kuala Lumpur that immerses you in rich culture, iconic landmarks, and is a historical site where Malaya declared its independence.

### Batu Caves

A 400 million-year-old limestone hill houses a century-old temple with shrines and statues. To reach the top, climb 272 steep steps for a breathtaking view. A significant pilgrimage site for Hindus and popular tourist destination.

More Local Attractions

### Botanical Garden

Visit the first large-scale recreational park in the city from our luxury hotel near Bukit Bintang, Kuala Lumpur​. Measuring 91.6 hectares, it is located in the heart of the city and established in 1888.

### Bukit Bintang

When you stay at our hotel next to Petronas Twin Towers, be sure to visit the bustling district lined with high-end shopping centres, open-air bars, and live music clubs on Changkat Street. Explore Jalan Alor, a vibrant strip famous for its street-side eateries serving local delicacies.

### Petaling Street

Petaling Street is the Chinatown of Kuala Lumpur - a traditional shopping street near other major attractions such as Kwai Chai Hong, Central Market and Sri Maha Mariamman temple.

### Chinatown Graffiti

Nestled in between the streets of Chinatown are gorgeous murals painted by local and visiting artists, definitely make for an artsy Instagram photo.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Kuala Lumpur have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Kuala Lumpur offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Kuala Lumpur have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Kuala Lumpur has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does W Kuala Lumpur have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY Spa is located on-site at W Kuala Lumpur. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Kuala Lumpur?

Petronas Twin Towers, The Exchange TRX, Suria KLCC Shopping Mall, Bukit Bintang, Kuala Lumpur Tower and more are nearby W Kuala Lumpur.
