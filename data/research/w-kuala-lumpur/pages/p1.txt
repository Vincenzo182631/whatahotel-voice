# Welcome to W Kuala Lumpur

## The leading city escape in Kuala Lumpur with iconic views

W Kuala Lumpur stands amidst the city's dynamic skyline, overlooking the iconic Petronas Twin Towers. Blending bold design with modern luxury, our vibrant rooms and suites feature signature W amenities for an unforgettable stay. Explore distinctive dining experiences at FLOCK, serving Pan-Asian grill creations, YEN, offering modern Cantonese cuisine, and HOJA, showcasing Pacific Coastal Cuisine inspired by Colombia, Peru and Chile. Complete the journey at HOJITA, where Latin American cocktails and regional distillates to life. Be seen at WET Deck, Kuala Lumpur's iconic rooftop destination, or experience the vibrant energy of the LIVING ROOM. Unwind and recharge at AWAY Spa, your urban sanctuary. Ideally located near the city's leading attractions, W Kuala Lumpur is Vibrant, Welcoming and Exclusive, inviting you to experience the city your way, Whatever/Whenever. IOI Business Hotel Sdn Bhd (CO. NO. 1011908-V) with respect to W Kuala Lumpur.

New Car Park Rates

Effective 1 December 2026, new car park rates apply: RM15 for the first hour and RM20 flat rate. Complimentary parking validation is available exclusively at the Front Desk and Concierge, with a guest room number required for every transaction.

### Rooms & Suites

All 150 rooms and suites at our 5-star hotel in Kuala Lumpur are embodied with traditional and modern elements of the Jungle City and captivating Kuala Lumpur skyline views.

### Experience Premium Suite Lifestyle: Penthouse Suites

Live it up in W Kuala Lumpur’s Premium Suites, where urban luxury meets vibrant energy. Perched on the 23rd floor, the Penthouse I and Penthouse II Suites feature distinctive jungle-city designs, expansive living spaces, and breathtaking views of the Petronas Twin Towers. These versatile suites transform into bespoke event spaces for intimate gatherings and celebrations. Experience the perfect blend of comfort, aesthetics, and exclusive amenities at our 5 star hotel in Kuala Lumpur​, your ideal base for creating lasting memories.

### Welcome to the Jungle

W Kuala Lumpur blends old and new, offering a sensory celebration of colors, aromas and sounds. A key design feature is the pixel—or dot—symbolizing Malaysia’s diverse cultures, woven throughout this luxury hotel in Kuala Lumpur's city center. Upon arrival, guests are welcomed by large traditional batik motifs, sleek metal accents, and bamboo-inspired chandeliers, representing the city’s lush plant life and evolving technology. The Welcome area evokes the jungle with LED lights mimicking raindrops and floor spotlights flowing like a river. The grand staircase, with its geometric steel-mirrored panels, creates a dynamic play of light and space, reflecting vibrant colors and native designs while guiding guests toward the spa and beyond.

### Explore Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur, born from humble roots where the Klang and Gombak Rivers meet, pulses with a dynamic energy that bridges past and present. Beneath its sunny tropical skies, the city thrives—a lush jungle interwoven with soaring skyscrapers and traditional Malaysian architecture. Here, nature and innovation intertwine, creating a tapestry where leafy canopies cradle modern marvels. Beyond its striking contrasts, Kuala Lumpur celebrates a vibrant cultural mosaic, offering a feast of flavors and a canvas for creativity. It’s a city where every corner tells a story, making it the ideal stage for the brand’s Malaysian debut— and you can explore it all from our luxury hotel in Kuala Lumpur near Petronas Twin Towers.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Kuala Lumpur?

The check-in time at W Kuala Lumpur is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Kuala Lumpur allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at W Kuala Lumpur.

What are the parking options at W Kuala Lumpur?

On-site parking is available at W Kuala Lumpur. Self parking is available at W Kuala Lumpur, with rates of RM5.00 per hour, RM0.00 per day, and RM0.00 perstay. Valet parking is available for RM40.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Kuala Lumpur have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Kuala Lumpur has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Kuala Lumpur booking.

What is the closest airport near W Kuala Lumpur?

The closest airport near W Kuala Lumpur is Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL). KUL is located approximately 68.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Kuala Lumpur have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Kuala Lumpur has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
