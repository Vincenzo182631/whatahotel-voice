## Dining

Energizing Kuala Lumpur’s dining scene, W Kuala Lumpur offers a diverse selection of Kuala Lumpur restaurants near Petronas towers​ for an unforgettable culinary journey. FLOCK, our vibrant all-day dining venue, features an open kitchen where expert chefs craft delicious Pan-Asian grills using organic produce and sustainable ingredients. LIVING ROOM, a chic all-day dessert bar, is the perfect spot...See More to relax and indulge. Head to WET DECK, our rooftop bar in Kuala Lumpur, for creative cocktails and stunning views of the iconic Petronas Twin Towers. ## Hoja and Hojita

Two new culinary concepts bringing the flavours, spirit and vibrant culture of Latin America’s Pacific coast to the heart of Kuala Lumpur. Rooted in the rich coastal traditions of the region and reimagined through a contemporary lens, HOJA and HOJITA invite guests into a world of bold flavours, spirited hospitality and effortless discovery.
