# Dining

Our downtown 7th and Pine location in Seattle creates an unmatched culinary experience. Savor succulent, seasoned meats and classic American fare at our upscale steakhouse, nosh on New York-style sandwiches and subs at our gourmet to-go deli, cook a variety of fresh ingredients in a flavorful simmering broth at the center of your table. Or gather your friends for champagne at The Elliott Bar. Guests can also enjoy in-room dining options for a more private experience.

## Qiao Lin Hotpot

Located off the Grand Hyatt Seattle lobby, Qiao Lin Hotpot offers an authentic Chongqing Hotpot experience that invites guests to engage in an interactive and communal dining style. Diners can cook a variety of fresh ingredients in a flavorful simmering broth, creating a personalized meal that's perfect for sharing. This immersive experience combines tradition with bold flavors, making it a must-try for anyone seeking an unforgettable dining experience. Whether you're with friends, family, or colleagues, Qiao Lin Hotpot is the ideal spot for a unique and delicious meal.
