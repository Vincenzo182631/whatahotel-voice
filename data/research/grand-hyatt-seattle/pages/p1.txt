## Experience the Refined Essence of the Pacific Northwest

Immerse yourself in the rich culture and natural beauty of Seattle from the sophisticated comfort at Grand Hyatt Seattle. Nestled in the heart of downtown Seattle on Pine Street, our luxury hotel offers effortless access to the city's most iconic landmarks, from the timeless charm of Pike Place Market to the historic Paramount Theatre. Just a mile from the Space Needle, guests can take a scenic stroll or ride the monorail to explore the city's vibrant energy. Back at the hotel, unwind in elegantly appointed rooms and suites, where sweeping views of downtown Seattle and Elliott Bay set the scene for relaxation. Savor elevated dining experiences, discover captivating local art, and host seamless gatherings in our meeting rooms and conference venues. Whether visiting for business or leisure, indulge in the refined comforts and unparalleled service that define the Pacific Northwest experience.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Relax and unwind in contemporary comfort. Our luxury Seattle hotel rooms and suites promise warmth and home-like charm with touches of grandeur, like in-room, deep-soaking hot tubs in Carrara marble bathrooms and floor-to-ceiling windows for dramatic views of the downtown skyline and the water of Puget Sound. Rest easy in one of our spacious suites or upgrade to a room with Grand Club access.

### Pets Are Welcome

Your four-legged family member deserves a luxury getaway too. Bring your furry friend along for a “paw-fect” stay at Grand Hyatt Seattle. Take them shopping or visit nearby dog parks and outdoor eateries in downtown Seattle. When it’s time for a nap, our pet-friendly hotel is waiting with a comfy bed and tasty treats. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
