# Zoetry Agua Punta Cana

**Zoetry Agua Punta Cana** is located on the beautiful, unspoiled beach of Uvero Alto. Zoetry Agua is a boutique sanctuary oasis with 96 luxury suites that embodies the local heritage, respects the environment and encourages cultural immersion to inspire your well-being. Upon arrival, you’re immediately enveloped by its natural beauty and abundant flora. Hard woods, palapa (cane-thatched) roofs, cathedral ceilings and natural stone touches showcase the resort’s eco-friendly architecture and are a nod to the Dominican Republic’s history. Caribbean and international flavors are used in the gourmet cuisine at the four a la carte restaurants as well as indigenous treatments at the Zoëtry Spa.

The **All-inclusive Zoetry Agua Punta Cana** has been a AAA Four Diamond award winner since 2012 and includes Zoëtry’s signature Endless Privileges®, where everything is included.

With Endless Privileges everything is included with our compliments for your stay - from unparalleled personal service to customized and unique experiences, exclusive amenities and authentic gourmet cuisine.

EXPERIENCES ON PROPERTY

An intimate getaway at Zoëtry artfully balances unrivaled luxury and personal well-being. Every part of a guest’s stay is designed to inspire and connect people to the distinctive culture, heritage, natural beauty and location of each boutique luxury resort. With a menu of unique experiences, authentic gourmet cuisine, restorative spa treatments and devoted personal service, your every need is thoughtfully anticipated.

Zoëtry Agua Punta Cana is a AAA Four Diamond award-winning, all suite sanctuary on the easternmost coast of the Dominican Republic in Uvero Alto. Experience an unforgettable and tranquil getaway that uniquely blends the region’s natural beauty, five-star luxury, eco-friendly practices, rich Taino Indian and Spanish Colonial traditions and the healing benefits of the sea and water.

Lulled by the soothing sound of the Caribbean Sea, you can easily lose track of time at Zoëtry® Agua Punta Cana. Under the shimmering Dominican Republic sun, there’s plenty to do and discover – from pampering at the Spa and long intimate walks on the uncrowded coastline to deep sea fishing and reveling in the rainforest’s natural beauty. Discover the array of inspiring activities both on and off property.

With its stunning 200 yard stretch of pristine beach along the shimmering Caribbean Sea, Zoëtry Agua Punta Cana offers many ways to experience enriching activities and the revitalizing power of water. From your arrival by helicopter\* and horseback ride on the beach to much-deserved spa treatments and exhilarating kite surfing and snorkeling, it’s the ideal place to relax on the gorgeous private beach, where powder-white sands meet turquoise waters.

### Explore the Destination

Perfectly located on the magnificent northeast coast of the Dominican Republic, Zoëtry Agua Punta Cana is surrounded by natural beauty and countless culturally enriching attractions. Explore the historic area of Santo Domingo or discover enormous starfish and limestone cliffs of the secluded Isla Saona. Immerse in activities on the water, at a nearby golf course or in a cultured day of exploring the region.
