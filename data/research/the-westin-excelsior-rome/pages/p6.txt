Amenities
Rooms
319
"A romantic dream," this 1906 hotel is just steps from Trevi Fountain and the Villa Borghese. The Grandluxe rooms offer extended space and "old-world charm," while the Via Veneto Suites are decorated in Biedermeier style with marble-bedecked living rooms and chandeliers. "Each floor’s lobby is like a museum, with gorgeous paintings, statues, and furniture." Feast on "a very good Mediterranean buffet" for lunch or dishes from the à la carte menu at Doney Restaurant, including risotto with wild asparagus, mint, and Morolo cheese. "Staff are amazing at remembering who you are."
