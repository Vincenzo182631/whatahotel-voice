# Elevate your stay. Discover our Signature Suites.

## Signature Suites

At The Westin Excelsior, Rome, every stay becomes a sensorial journey — where comfort meets beauty, and every detail is crafted to elevate your well-being. Drift into a world of plush serenity each night...See More and awaken refreshed, inspired, and ready to embrace the Eternal City. From refined design to breathtaking views, each of our central Rome suites tells its own story — none more iconic than the Villa La Cupola, one of Europe’s most spectacular and opulent suites. Discover more about our most iconic Signature Suites.See Less

### The Villa La Cupola Suite

The Westin Excelsior Rome unveils the renewed Villa La Cupola Suite, one of the most exclusive and spacious suites in Europe. Spanning over 740 square meters (7962sqft) across two floors, this extraordinary residence is a true palace within the Eternal City. Blending Italian craftsmanship, contemporary design, and cutting-edge technology, it offers a private, luxurious retreat with breathtaking panoramic...See More views of Rome.See Less

Book now

VILLA LA CUPOLA

## The Villa guestroom

The master bedroom is a refined sanctuary of elegance. Furnished with exquisite pieces and sophisticated finishes, it features Murano glass mirrors and lamps by Rubelli Casa and Donghia. A warm color palette—ranging...See More from dove gray to pearl white and gold—wraps the space in a luxurious and serene ambiance, highlighting the finest in Italian design and artisanal detail. Enhancing its exclusivity, the room also boasts a private balcony overlooking the prestigious Via Veneto, as well as an elegant walk-in closet that adds both functionality and style to this exceptional retreat.See Less

VILLA LA CUPOLA

## The Cupola living room

At the heart of the suite is the majestic domed living room, crowned by a frescoed cupola inspired by Renaissance and Baroque Rome. The space is furnished with pieces by Giorgetti and Rubelli, including the contemporary Domino Sofa and elegant coffee tables. A balance of neutral tones and vibrant accents creates a harmonious and stylish atmosphere, enhanced by a state-of-the-art Devialet audio system and scenographic lighting for an immersive experience.

VILLA LA CUPOLA

## The private workspace

The private studio offers a secluded space for work or relaxation, enriched by iconic design elements. The Rio Novo desk in canaletto walnut and leather by Rubelli Casa, the lime green HUG armchair by Giorgetti, and the olive green Utrecht armchair by Cassina (designed by Gerrit Rietveld) stand out as bold and elegant features. The room also includes a crystal and leather football table, “90° Minuto,” adding a playful and exclusive touch.

VILLA LA CUPOLA

## The wellness penthouse

On the upper floor, guests can indulge in a complete wellness experience featuring a magnificent Pompeian-style Jacuzzi pool, adorned with a mosaic floor, vaulted ceiling, and frescoes that evoke the ambiance of ancient Roman baths. Adjacent to the pool is a fully equipped fitness area, a sauna, and a steam bath, offering the ultimate in relaxation and rejuvenation. The suite also boasts an outstanding panoramic rooftop terrace, crowned by the frescoed dome that rises above the entire building.
