Delectable Dining

Breakfast at Café Doney

Start your morning with the flavours of Italian tradition at Doney Café. Enjoy a classic Italian breakfast with freshly baked cornetto and cappuccino, or choose from our à la carte selection, designed to accompany you throughout the day. In the heart of Via Veneto, Doney Café is the perfect place for a relaxed breakfast or a leisurely pause.

Exclusive Via Veneto Dining

A blend of Mediterranean flavours made with the finest local and seasonal ingredients. From Monday to Friday, a special Lunch menu offers a curated selection for a relaxed and enjoyable midday break. On Sundays, enjoy Doney’s Sunday Brunch with live stations, desserts and free-flow Italian sparkling wine. The à la carte menu is also available for lunch and dinner in the heart of Via Veneto.

Orvm Bar Cardinale Cocktail

The Cardinale was created at the Hotel Excelsior in 1950, when a cardinal suggested a new combination of ingredients to the bartender. The result quickly won over the other guests at the bar, and the cocktail was named “Cardinale” in honour of its unusual inspiration. Today, this cocktail is part of the history and tradition of Italian cocktail culture and continues to be served at Orvm Bar.
