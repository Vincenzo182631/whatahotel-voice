### Private Tour

Private walking tour with a licensed guide (min. 3 hrs): we will design the itinerary according to your preferences. Please let us know which sites would you like to visit and when and we will prepare a dedicated quotation including admission tickets (skip-the-line) and transportation. Kindly specify number of guests in your party and full name of participants.

### Private Golf Cart Tour

Golf Cart Tour with an English speaking driving guide (min. 3 hrs): we will design the itinerary according to your preferences. Perfect to start your stay in Rome and see the main highlights of the city and admire the fountains and squares driving through the narrow streets of the Eternal City. Kindly specify the number of guests in your party to receive a quotation.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Excelsior, Rome have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Excelsior, Rome offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Offers cardio and strength equipment, hand weights and sauna, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Excelsior, Rome have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Excelsior, Rome has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Excelsior, Rome?

St. Peter Church and Vatican Museums - Sistine Chapel, Colosseum, MAXXI, National Museum of 21st Century Arts, Spanish Steps, Palazzo Barberini and more are nearby The Westin Excelsior, Rome.
