# Welcome to The Westin Excelsior, Rome

## Discover our legendary classic hotel in Rome

Inaugurated in 1906, The Westin Excelsior Rome has stood for over a century as one of the capital’s most iconic luxury hotels, a timeless symbol of elegance, refinement, and impeccable service. Ideally...See More located on Via Veneto, our 5-star hotel in Rome enjoys a privileged setting between the Spanish Steps and the Borghese Gardens and Gallery, offering a perfect balance of historic charm and contemporary vibrancy. Its magnificent Belle Époque architecture, enhanced by expansive panoramic terraces and exquisitely appointed interiors, pays tribute to the enduring beauty of the Eternal City, creating an atmosphere that is both sophisticated and unforgettable. Our hotel in central Rome features 281 rooms and 35 exclusive Signature Suites, including the extraordinary Villa La Cupola, widely regarded as one of the most spectacular and opulent suites in Europe. Each space is thoughtfully designed to ensure the highest level of comfort, seamlessly blending classic luxury with modern amenities for an exceptional stay.See Less

### Our Respect for Tomorrow

In a social and environmental approach, The Westin Excelsior Rome has embarked on a continuous journey toward sustainability. We are actively working to reduce our environmental footprint through sustainable actions and eco-friendly practices embraced by our employees on a daily basis. Our efforts focus on reducing energy and water consumption, increasing responsible and local sourcing, minimizing...See More waste, and raising overall awareness. As proof of our commitment to sustainable development, we are proud to announce that The Westin Excelsior Rome has obtained the Green Key label since last year.See Less

### Social Commitment

The Westin Excelsior Rome is socially committed to the local community. Every month, we donate 40 meals to an organization that cares for the city’s homeless. We actively support local charitable associations and promote responsible reuse: decommissioned or forgotten items (after the claim period) are donated to charity.

### Environmental Care

The Westin Excelsior Rome is determined to continue its environmental commitment. We are constantly working on the reduction of our carbon footprint. We encourage virtuous behavior among staff and guests, raising daily awareness of more responsible management of energy resources, such as water and electricity. We are proud to actively contribute to environmental preservation by adopting trees.

### The Green Key Certificate

The Green Key certificate is a leading standard of excellence in the field of environmental responsibility and sustainable operation within the tourism industry. Recognized internationally, it rewards establishments that demonstrate a consistent and verifiable commitment to ecological and sustainable practices, promoting more responsible tourism through rigorous criteria.

## WHAT TO EXPECT

### Plan and Prepare for your Stay

Learn More

Property Information

General

**What time is the check-in and check-out time?**

Check-in starts from 3pm and check-out is at 12pm

**Do you accommodate early check-in/late check-out?**

We can only guarantee early check-ins for rooms reserved the prior night at full rate. We will do our best to accommodate late checkout requests, subject to availability and not guaranteed

**What is the hotel voltage?**

Hotel voltage is 220 V. Please make sure to check the voltage of your equipment before usage.

**Do you provide adaptors or converters?**

Our concierge may provide guests with adapters (subject to availability), but no converters are available. Please contact the concierge desk should need any

**Do you have safe deposit boxes?**

For your convenience, there is an electronic safe box in all rooms, with a person digit code. Safe deposit box is at your disposal also at front desk

**Do you have a business center?**

Our business center is at your complete disposal 24 hours a day, 7 days a week

**What does Internet cost?**

The entire hotel is equipped with complimentary low band WIFI access for all our guests. Premium WIFI is available at a daily charge of Euro 12 per 24 hours

**Which payment methods does the Hotel accept?**

We accept Credit Cards. Based on the law 2015, No. 898, approved on 22.12.2015 by Council of Minister, we remind you that we cannot accept payments in cash for amounts greater than or equal to 3.000€ for Italian guests and 15.000€ for foreign guests.

**Can I pay for a hotel room for someone else?**

You may pay a room for someone else only if the payment is performed at least 7 days prior to arrival. In order to pay for someone you will be asked to fill an online CC form that has to be sent via secure link or via wire transfer

**What is the Rome city tax?**

The Rome City Council introduced a City tax which is in addition to VAT currently 10%. City tax is payable by all guests at check-out and is applicable for a maximum of 10 consecutive nights. Rome City tax is €10.00 per person per night.

Rooms

**How much will be charged for an extra person?**

Maximum occupancy per room or suite is three guests

The extra person charge is 100€ + VAT + 10€ city tax per night

The extra person charge applies also if the third person sleeps in existing bed (king or twin) but we can also provide an extra bed on request. 4 people in the room are not allowed even if a SNA has been confirmed and a second room shall be booked. There is a supplement for reserved single room with double occupancy

**Do you have connecting rooms or Suites?**

Connecting rooms are only Grand Luxe rooms and they are reserved for families with small kids booking Grand Luxe. Availability is on request. Two- or Three-bedrooms Suites available on request . Please contact the hotel for further information

**Does your hotel provide cribs?**

Cribs are complimentary for kids up to 3 y.o. and are provided upon request for all rooms and Suites. Hotel provide blankets, bumpers, pillows upon signed waiver.

**Do you have hairdryer?**

All rooms are equipped with hairdryers.

**Do all your rooms have air conditioning?**

All rooms are equipped with air conditioning

**Do you provide guests with boiler, coffee makers or iron boards?**

1\. Iron/Iron board (can be requested via service express and delivered, upon availability, for the time to iron your garments);

2\. Water Boilers/Coffee makers: need to be requested in advance and will be delivered upon availability.

**Are pets allowed and at what costs?**

The hotel is delighted to welcome pets to the property. We permit one dog (5kg) per room and provide the following amenities for their comfort: a dog bowl, bed, mat and a Pet Welcome Kit.

**Do you have rooms with balconies or terraces?**

We have rooms with balconies and some rooms have terraces. Please, contact the hotel for requests as the availability is limited.

**Do you have accessible rooms/areas?**

We have accessible rooms. In order to book them, please contact the hotel . The bathroom of these rooms is fitted to be wheelchair-accessible . To enter and exit the hotel guests with a wheel chair use a side entrance with a ramp. To go to the Doney restaurant for breakfast lunch and dinner we place a portable ramp

Services

**What are the laundry service hours?**

Laundry is available to all guest from Monday to Saturday: 8.00am to 9.00pm; Sunday: 08:30am to 09:00pm.

**Does your hotel have a fitness center/Spa/pool?**

Our hotel is equipped with a WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio with Technogym facilities accessible 24/7 with room key and complimentary to all guests.

We have a small indoor pool, sauna and massage center

Transportation

**How do I get to the hotel from the train station?**

Termini Central train station is a 10min taxi from the Westin Excelsior. A taxi-drive is around EUR 15, depending on the amount of luggage, that is added as a fix rate to the fare.

**How do I get to the hotel from the airport?**

The hotel can be reached by taxi or public transport in about 50 minutes. Busses and the Leonardo Express direct train from the airport arrive at Termini Central Station, a 10 min taxi drive from the Hotel. The hotel does not provide Shuttle bus

**Do you offer a private transfer service?**

The Westin Excelsior Rome will gladly arrange a private car transfer from/to the hotel. For more information and pricing options, please contact our concierge

Dining

**What are the breakfast hours and costs?**

Our Doney restaurant is open for breakfast daily from 07.00am to 11.00 am

Buffet Breakfast costs 39€ per person.

Kids 0-3 eat free from Kids Menu

Children 3-12 pay 50% of Breakfast

Breakfast from 7.00 to 10.30 and W/E from 7.00 to 11.00

**How many dining possibilities does the hotel offer and what are the opening hours?** Restaurant Doney 7:00 AM - 10:30 PM

Doney Café 7:00 M - 10:30 PM

ORVM Bar 9:30 AM - 1:00 AM

Mobility & Assistance

Main entrance of the hotel is not accessible with a wheelchair but a side door to reach guest rooms. Ramps allow access to Front Office, Restaurant and Bar. Some guest rooms are especially accessible (please request at the moment of the reservation). Our indoor swimming pool does not have entrance facilities other than the vertical standard Swimming Pool Ladder.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Excelsior, Rome?

The check-in time at The Westin Excelsior, Rome is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Excelsior, Rome allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Excelsior, Rome. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 6.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at The Westin Excelsior, Rome?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Excelsior, Rome. Self parking is available for €55.00 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near The Westin Excelsior, Rome. The daily valet fee is €55.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Excelsior, Rome have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Excelsior, Rome has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Excelsior, Rome booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Excelsior, Rome?

The closest airport near The Westin Excelsior, Rome is Ciampino – G.B. Pastine International Airport (CIA). CIA is located approximately 27.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Excelsior, Rome have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Excelsior, Rome does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
