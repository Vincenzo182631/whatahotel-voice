### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Excelsior, Rome?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 3 at The Westin Excelsior, Rome. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome?

Some rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The Westin Excelsior, Rome have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The Westin Excelsior, Rome have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
