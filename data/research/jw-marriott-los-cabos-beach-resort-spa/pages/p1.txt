# Welcome to JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa

## Enjoy a romantic stay at our Los Cabos Beach Resort

Experience the essence of tropical luxury at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa. Located along the picturesque coastline of San José del Cabo, where the Sea of Cortez meets the Pacific Ocean, our resort offers the perfect setting to enjoy the sun with a touch of sophistication. Whether you're strolling along the beach, relaxing by our impeccable pools, or perfecting your golf game at the Puerto...See More Los Cabos club and its impressive 27-hole course, disconnecting from your daily routine is easy. Seek respite from the sun and enjoy serenity at our Jasha Spa and Salon. Our San Jose del Cabo luxury resort features six distinctive restaurants and bars, ideal for savoring moments from sunrise to sunset. When it's time to rest, find comfort in elegant rooms and suites, with luxury furnishings and inspiring ocean views. Whether you're seeking solitude, a family trip, or rekindling romance, create lasting memories at our Los Cabos resort.See Less

Pool Enhancements

Guests are invited to enjoy 10 of our 11 pools throughout their stay, along with our dining venues, bars, and resort amenities. Our Adults-Only Pool and Kaha Bar will undergo enhancements from September 1 through the end of October. We appreciate your understanding and look forward to welcoming you.

### Café des Artistes

The culinary jewel of Los Cabos, designed by chef Thierry Blouet, a master at fusing the best of Mexico with French elegance. A sophisticated space where every dish is a work of culinary art and each evening is accompanied by live piano music.

### Auka Deli

Auka Deli makes it easy to fuel up for your day in Los Cabos, thanks to a variety of fresh-baked pastries, gourmet coffee and other on-the-go specialties. This Los Cabos spot offers a new selection each morning with wholesome favorites.

### Niparaya JW Bar

The ideal place to start the afternoon and let the night flow with good vibes. At Niparaya JW Bar, mixology takes the spotlight with timeless classic cocktails and creative blends, complemented by a selection of light and delicious Asian-inspired cuisine.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa is Los Cabos International Airport (SJD). SJD is located approximately 18.5 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
