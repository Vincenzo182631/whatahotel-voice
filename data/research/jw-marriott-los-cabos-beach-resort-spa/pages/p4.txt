## Immerse Yourself

The Jasha Spa is inspired by natural healing ingredients found in the surrounding botanical garden. The spa features 12 treatment rooms, an outdoor garden and patio, vitality pools, an indoor pool, and private Jacuzzis. Our spa hotel in San Jose del Cabo will also house a modern version of the traditional temazcal, a Mexican sweat lodge used by ancient Aztecs as a therapeutic instrument to promote healing, health and well-being.

SPA DETAILS

Spa Facilities

Experience our full menu of spa services inside our unique and elegant facilities. Amazing indoor and outdoor pools, saunas, whirlpools, a steam room, treatment suites and an ancient Temazcal help you connect with your inner self for an overall greater well being. All pools, locker areas and saunas are separate for him and for her.

Directions and Parking

Our resort is located in Puerto Los Cabos and can be easily reached by following the main boulevard. Go past the security office towards the PLC Golf Course and make a right, in 150 meters you will see the JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa, on the ocean side. We offer complimentary on-site and valet parking.

### Unforgettable Activites

Guests always enjoy numerous activities and ecotourism experiences, such as ATVs, horseback riding, fishing, snorkeling and scuba diving, in addition to many others.

## The Aquarium of the World

Experience the only place in the world where six different types of whale species migrate to the warmer waters in the Sea of Cortez and Pacific Ocean. By the end of November, and through April, whales trek up to 6,000 miles in their migration rituals to give birth to their offspring. Whale-watching tours are exhilarating during this particular time of year, and famous for Jacques Cousteau calling it “The Aquarium of the World.”

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa offers a fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have a pool on-site?
