### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 3 at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa?

No, the rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have fridges?

No, rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at JW Marriott Los Cabos Beach Resort & Spa have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
