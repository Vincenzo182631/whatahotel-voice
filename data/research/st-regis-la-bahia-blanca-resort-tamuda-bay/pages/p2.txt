Family Traditions at St. Regis are all about creating lasting, bespoke family memories through curated experiences. At The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay, this is reflected in thoughtfully designed connecting accommodations ranging from 64m² to 90m², offering generous, flexible spaces ideal for extended stays. ### Unpacking and Packing

Relax into coastal living and allow your St. Regis Butler to take care of all the finer details. Our signature unpacking and packing service ensures all belongings are expertly presented and safely stored.

### Garment Pressing

A visible representation of The St. Regis brand’s commitment to excellence, your St. Regis Butler is a discreet presence that finesses daily routines. Take advantage of complimentary garment pressing, of up to two items daily.

### Tailored Service

A hallmark of flawless and intuitive hospitality, the St. Regis Butler is on hand to enrich each stay with elevated service. Providing an attentive and knowledgeable presence, each request is treated with commitment and personal attention.

## Signature Suites

Discover the art of refined hospitality at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay, where each suite is a sanctuary of elegance and elevated comfort, inspired by the Astor family’s legacy of luxurious retreats. Thoughtfully designed, the signature suites blend exquisite craftsmanship, luxurious materials, and curated Moroccan-inspired details. Expansive interiors open onto serene coastal vistas, while bespoke furnishings and signature St. Regis touches create an atmosphere of timeless sophistication and immersive, sensory luxury.
