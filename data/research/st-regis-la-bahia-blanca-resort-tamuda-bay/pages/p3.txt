# Welcome to the St. Regis La Bahia Blanca resort, Tamuda Bay

## Suites

Ideally set along the shores of Tamuda Bay, 20 minutes away from Tetouan Airport, the signature suites at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay offer a refined coastal retreat defined by Mediterranean views and timeless elegance. Named after the Astor family, the founders of the St. Regis brand, each suite reflects their legacy of refined living, blending the Astors’ luxurious lifestyle with the spirit of Moroccan coastal life. Every suite features a private terrace or balcony with sparkling sea views and interiors combining signature St. Regis design and local craftsmanship, enhanced by subtle touches of Chefchaouen blue. From the inviting Junior Suites to the spacious John Jacob Astor Suite, each space is designed for comfort and sophistication. The Royal Suite crowns the collection with panoramic sea views and expansive living areas, while the signature St. Regis Butler Service ensures a seamless, personalized stay.

### The Royal Suite

Occupying a privileged fourth floor setting, the Royal Suite has panoramic seaviews. Transcending the ordinary, the St. Regis Butler oversees every personalized detail.

## The Royal Suite

Step into the Royal Suite, an exquisite haven of luxury spanning an impressive 176 square meters (1,894 square feet), where opulence meets comfort. Within this regal retreat, a sumptuous king-sized bed awaits, promising restful slumber befitting royalty. The suite's expansive living and sitting areas provide an elegant setting for relaxation or private gatherings, enveloping you in an atmosphere of refined sophistication. Modern conveniences abound, with a discreetly placed mini fridge and microwave offering the ease of refreshments at your command. This Royal Suite is not merely a room, but a sanctuary, meticulously crafted to embody the very essence of royal living.

### Connecting Family

The Connecting Family rooms are thoughtfully designed to cater to families traveling together, seamlessly integrating a suite with an adjacent room to provide ample space and privacy. Both rooms offer breathtaking beachfront views, ensuring that each guest can enjoy the serene beauty of the ocean. The suite features a luxurious setting with a separate living area, while the connected room offers additional comfort and convenience. This configuration allows families to share quality time in a spacious, cohesive environment, while still having their own distinct areas. With direct access to the stunning beach vistas from both rooms, this arrangement is ideal for families seeking both togetherness and individual comfort in a picturesque coastal setting.
