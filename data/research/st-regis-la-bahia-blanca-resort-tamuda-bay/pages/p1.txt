The Quintessential Rituals

### The Caroline Astor Afternoon Tea

A regal tradition that was adopted by Caroline Astor, our sensory Afternoon Tea experience features meticulously selected teas and exquisite culinary delicacies, handcrafted to take you on a refined journey from London’s drawing rooms to Tetouan’s courtyards.

### The Exclusive Bahia Mary Cocktail

The Bloody Mary is the St. Regis signature cocktail, a timeless icon served in every Address. Inspired by this legacy, The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay has crafted its own interpretation, named “The Bahia Mary”, using locally sourced ingredients rooted in daily Moroccan cuisine, some of which are even grown on the premises in the Chef’s Potager.

### The Magical Evening Ritual

Inspired by Mrs. Astor and the legacy of St. Regis, we invite you each evening at sunset to witness the iconic champagne sabrage. This timeless ritual celebrates elegance, heritage, and the art of marking the transition from day to night at The House Of Celebration.

## The Legendary St. Regis Butler Service

First introduced at The St. Regis New York by John Jacob Astor IV, the St. Regis Butler Service has been a defining hallmark of the brand for over a century. At The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay, this legacy continues through dedicated butler service, offering intuitive, highly personalized attention and anticipating each guest’s preferences for a seamlessly exquisite stay.

Unpacking and Packing

Allow your St. Regis Butler to take care of the unpacking and storage of your luggage, or contact the Butler Service Desk to dispatch a butler for assistance with gathering and folding your garments before departure.

Beverage Service

Upon arrival, allow your St. Regis Butler to arrange coffee or tea service in your room. A Butler may also arrive following your morning wake-up call to open the curtains and deliver your coffee or tea.

Garment Pressing

Contact your St. Regis Butler for the prompt pickup and return of any garments that require pressing. Generally, the pressing of up to two garments is complimentary.

Butler Desk Service

All guests have access to the signature St. Regis Butler Service via the Butler Service Desk. Whatever the request, guests may dial the Butler Service from their guestroom phones for prompt assistance.

E-Butler Service

The St. Regis Butler Service is accessible at any hour via email and text message. For assistance, please send your request to the Butler Service Desk, where a Butler will be pleased to assist you.

Elevate the In-Room Experience

Send a meaningful amenity gift during or in advance of an upcoming stay through the thoughtfully curated Delights Delivered Program. From handcrafted  special amenities to personalized cakes for a joyous celebration, create memorable moments to be enjoyed in the comfort of our rooms and suites.

## The St. Regis Pet Experience

At The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay, every Luminary is welcome.

From tailored amenities to attentive service, our pet-friendly experience ensures your beloved companion enjoys a stay as comfortable and memorable as your own.

To ensure every detail is thoughtfully arranged, we invite you to contact us before your arrival.

Discover Our Pet Policy

We welcome well-behaved dogs and cats.

A non-refundable pet cleaning fee of 200 MAD per stay applies.

A maximum of 1 pet per guestroom is permitted.

Pets are permitted inside guestrooms only.

Pets are not allowed in restaurants and bars, including Baie Blanche Brasserie, Bahia Vista Beach Bar and Grill, and The St. Regis Bar.

Pets are not allowed at any area of the St. Regis Spa, Fitness Center, swimming pool, pool area, or private beach.

Pets must be leashed or securely contained in all resort common areas.

Housekeeping can service your room only if your pet is absent, supervised by you, or safely contained.

Guests are responsible for any damage or additional cleaning costs caused by their pet.

The resort reserves the right to request the removal of any pet displaying disruptive or unsafe behavior.

Service animals are welcome and are not subject to the hotel's pet policy.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay?

The check-in time at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of MK1500.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay?

The closest airport near The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay is Sania Ramel Airport (TTU). TTU is located approximately 23.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
