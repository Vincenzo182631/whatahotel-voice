# Activities and Experiences in Tamuda Bay

## Experiences

At the meeting point of the sparkling Mediterranean Sea and the majestic Rif Mountains, The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay offers an exceptional setting for a refined coastal escape. Just 20 minutes from Sania Ramel Airport in Tetouan, the resort invites guests to experience the art of bespoke leisure, from tranquil moments by the sea and elevated dining to rejuvenating treatments at...See More the St. Regis Spa. With its beachfront setting and thoughtfully curated experiences, every stay is an invitation to Live Exquisite.See Less

A Sensorial Journey

### The St. Regis Spa

A coveted and nurturing setting, The St. Regis Spa is a high-end sanctuary where modernity and tradition come together to create a truly bespoke wellness experience. Rooted in the heritage of Morocco and the legacy of St. Regis, the spa blends time-honoured rituals with contemporary therapies, offering personalized treatments designed to restore balance, vitality and a deep sense of relaxation. With serene surroundings and expert therapists, every moment is an invitation to unwind, recharge and embrace refined indulgence.

Explore

### The Moroccan Hammam Experience

Rooted in centuries-old Moroccan traditions, our Moroccan Hammam offers a deeply sensory journey of purification and renewal. A timeless expression of Moroccan wellness, the Hammam invites you to reconnect with the art of self-care in a setting of refined luxury.

### The Spa Indulgence for Two

Reconnect and unwind together in our Couple’s Treatment Room. Enjoy side-by-side massages and bespoke treatments in an intimate setting, surrounded by soft lighting, soothing aromas and refined touches designed for shared relaxation.

### The Relaxing Indoor Pools

Three pools, one exquisite escape. Overlooking the serene shores of Tamuda Bay, each offers a distinct temperature for refreshing dips or soothing moments of relaxation, surrounded by Mediterranean beauty.

### The Spa Shopping Experience

Take the essence of The St. Regis Spa home with you. Explore a curated selection of luxurious skincare, oils, creams and fragrances, designed to transform everyday rituals into moments of indulgence, relaxation and well-being, for yourself or for someone special.

### Fitness in Refined Balance

Stay active at your own rhythm in our 24/7 fitness center, thoughtfully designed to combine state-of-the-art equipment with a bright and sophisticated atmosphere. From invigorating workouts to mindful movement, each session helps you restore your energy.

### Exquisite Dining

At The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay, gastronomy is an expression of refined coastal living. The finest local ingredients meet Moroccan heritage, Mediterranean inspiration and sophisticated culinary artistry, creating an elevated dining experience rooted in the richness and elegance of the region.

## Baie Blanche Brasserie

Baie Blanche Brasserie blends the culinary artistry of France and Morocco. Featuring seasonal ingredients and fresh seafood, the menu is elevated by a curated wine selection served in an elegant setting and a sophisticated seaside ambiance.

Immersive Dining Experiences

## Bahia Vista Beach Bar & Grill

Summer comes to life at our Tiki-style beach bar and grill, Bahia Vista, where balanced health-forward cuisine is served with a side of sea breeze and sun-soaked shores. Delight in a creative gastronomic experience.

Immersive Dining Experiences

## The St. Regis Bar

Opening at sunset, our St. Regis Bar in Tamuda Bay serves timeless favorites, including the resort’s locally inspired signature Bahia Mary. Each evening is toasted with a seven o'clock signature Champagne Sabrage Ritual, performed by the head bartender.

### Afternoon Tea Ritual

A cherished St. Regis tradition, Afternoon Tea at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay invites you to savour exceptional teas and exquisite handcrafted delicacies. Inspired by London’s elegant drawing rooms and the timeless charm of Tetouan’s courtyards, each moment is steeped in refined indulgence.

Make Reservations

### Champagne Sabrage Ritual

Napoleon Bonaparte famously said of champagne, “In victory, one deserves it. In defeat, one needs it.” Each evening at sunset, The St. Regis celebrates this timeless tradition with its signature Champagne Sabrage Ritual, marking the transition from day to evening with elegance.

### Bahia Mary Signature Cocktail

Our signature take on the iconic Bloody Mary, The Bahia Mary brings a distinctly local touch to the classic cocktail. Crafted with locally sourced ingredients, fresh herbs and produce from the Chef’s Potager, it captures the vibrant flavours and spirit of Tamuda Bay.

## Curated Cooking Classes

Join our Culinary Team for a hands-on cooking experience designed for both adults and little chefs. Learn new techniques, explore local flavours and create delicious dishes together in a fun, engaging and memorable culinary journey. Classes can be booked through your Butler.

## A Romantic Dinner by the Sea

Set against the Mediterranean, enjoy an intimate dinner for two in a private setting overlooking the shimmering waters of Tamuda Bay. As the sun sets over the horizon, savour exquisite flavours, thoughtful service and an unforgettable evening designed for romance.

### A Sense of Place

From the warmth of Tamuda Bay’s only heated outdoor pool to the golden sands of our private beach, every moment invites you to slow down and savour the Mediterranean. Complete the experience at Moroccan Crea, our exclusive boutique celebrating Moroccan craftsmanship through contemporary fashion and beauty.

### The Heated Outdoor Pool

Enjoy Tamuda Bay’s only heated outdoor pool at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay. Surrounded by lush gardens and the Mediterranean, it offers an idyllic setting to unwind and swim, even during the winter months.

### By the Mediterranean

Soak up the beauty of Tamuda Bay from the beachfront of The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort. Golden sands, azure Mediterranean waters, and panoramic views set the scene for effortless days by the sea.

### Crafted in Morocco

At The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay, discover the exclusive Moroccan Crea boutique, featuring refined caftans, prêt-à-porter creations, and beauty essentials designed exclusively for the resort. Each piece celebrates Moroccan craftsmanship, contemporary elegance, and timeless artistry.

### A Journey Beyond Tamuda Bay

Beyond Tamuda Bay lies a region rich in contrasts, from the untamed landscapes of the Rif to centuries-old medinas and vibrant coastal cities. Venture from The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay to experience the heritage, culture and natural beauty of northern Morocco.

### The Adventure of Fahs Lamhar

Just beyond Tamuda Bay, Jbel Labyad mountain and Fahs Lamhar plateau invite you into the wild Rif, where herds of free-roaming horses wander across vast landscapes, with scenic trails and breathtaking views stretching from the mountains to the Mediterranean.

### The History of Tetouan

Just 20 minutes from The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay, Tetouan is the inspiration behind the resort’s refined architecture. Known as the “White Dove” of Morocco, its UNESCO-listed medina reflects centuries of Andalusian and Moroccan heritage through intricate zellige, carved wood, vibrant souks, and renowned craftsmanship.

### The Blue of Chefchaouen

From The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay, set out to discover Chefchaouen, the city that unfolds like a colourful dream in the heart of the Rif Mountains. Draped in shades of blue, the Blue Pearl welcomes you to wander its winding medina, discover its crafts and flavours, and venture into lush mountain landscapes.

### The Vibrance of Tangier

Within easy reach of The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay, Tangier offers a captivating journey into Northern Morocco. A meeting point of Mediterranean, Atlantic, and Moroccan influences, the city invites guests to wander through its whitewashed Kasbah, vibrant souks, artisan workshops, and storied cafés, with sweeping views of its legendary bay.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as All equipment, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay have an on-site spa?

Yes, The St. Regis Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay.
