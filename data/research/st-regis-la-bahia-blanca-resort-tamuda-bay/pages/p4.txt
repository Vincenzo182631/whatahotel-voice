# Coastal Flavors Reinvented

## Dining

Located on Morocco’s pristine Tamuda Bay shoreline, 20 minutes away from the historic city of Tetouan, The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca Resort, Tamuda Bay is placed at a cultural crossroads, where Mediterranean, French, and African traditions converge. An inspired gastronomic scene, our Tamuda Bay restaurants thoughtfully celebrate the region’s culinary heritage while crafting fusion-forward cuisine that is bold and innovative. Its exceptional seafront location enables the resort to serve fresh seafood, caught daily by local suppliers. European techniques and Moroccan spices collide, setting a new standard among Tamuda Bay restaurants with its sophisticated modern and coastal cuisine.

## Bahia Mary

In honor of the original Bloody Mary created by barman, Fernand Petiot in 1934, the Bahia Mary has been reinterpreted to feature regional spices, local harissa sauce, rosemary and black pepper. Our mixologists have curated a cocktail which is both light and distinctive, capturing the pleasure evoked by the bay’s exceptional sunsets.
