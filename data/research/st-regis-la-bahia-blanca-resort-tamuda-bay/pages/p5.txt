# Iridium Spa-Timeless Luxury & Rejuvenation

## Spa

Immerse yourself in tranquility at Iridium Spa, an exclusive retreat at The St. Regis La Bahia Blanca, Tamuda Bay. Rooted in the legacy of St. Regis, Iridium Spa offers a bespoke wellness experience, blending ancient rituals with modern therapies. Indulge in personalized massages, rejuvenating facials, and signature treatments designed to restore balance and vitality. With serene surroundings and expert therapists, each moment is an invitation to relax, recharge, and embrace true indulgence. Experience bespoke wellness, refined luxury, and the art of rejuvenation.

Spa Serenity-Relaxation, Beauty & Luxury Awaits

### Experience Tranquility at Every Corner of Iridium Spa

Begin your journey at Iridium spa with the stunning entrance, where the intricate Moroccan Zellige tiles and cascading fountain set the tone for tranquility. As you step inside, the breathtaking sea view adds an element of calm, creating the perfect introduction to a luxurious experience. Next, explore the spa hall, a grand space adorned with Moroccan roses & elegant lanterns. This serene hall exudes a sense of timeless beauty & grace, welcoming you intoa worls of relaxation & indulgence. Finally, unwind in the peaceful relaxation room, where plush seating and soothing amenities provide the ideal environment to rest before or after your treatments. With calming refreshments and tranquil music, the room is a sanctuary for your body and mind to recharge.

The Sanctuary-Tranquility Redefined

## Three Exquisite Pools With Breathtaking Sea Views

Revel in the sanctuary where three soothing pools overlook the peaceful shores of Tamuda Bay. Each pool offers a unique temperature setting, from a refreshing cool dip to a soothing warm embrace, providing a distinctive experience. Surrounded by serene landscapes and designed for pure relaxation, this exclusive retreat invites you to unwind in an atmosphere of luxury and tranquility. Whether seeking invigoration or deep relaxation, The Sanctuary is your personal oasis by the sea.

Moroccan Hammam-A Timeless Ritual

## Purify & Rejuvenate With an Authentic Moroccan Hammam

Step into a world of ancient rituals at Iridium Spa's Moroccan Hammam. Inspired by centuries-old traditions, this purifying experience begins with a warm steam bath to open pores, followed by an exfoliating Black Soap cleanse and a soothing Ghassoul clay wrap. Let expert hands guide you through a journey of renewal, leaving your skin refreshed, your body relaxed, and your mind at peace. A true sensory escape, the Hammam is the ultimate expression of Moroccan wellness, inviting you to embrace the art of self-care in a setting of distinctive luxury.

Couple's Treatment Room-Shared Bliss

### Relax & Reconnect in Our Couple's Room

Experience true harmony with your significant other in our Couple's Treatment Room. This intimate space offers side-by-side massages, facials, and custom treatments tailored to your needs. Enjoy the tranquility of the soft lighting, soothing aromas, and luxurious touches that create the perfect environment for relaxation & reconnection.

### Indulge in a Romantic Flower Bath Experience

Celebrate your love with our Flower Bath Treatment, an intimate & luxurious experience for newlyweds or brides-to-be. Immerse yourself in a warm, fragrant bath filled with delicate petals, enhancing your senses & inspiring serenity. Combined with a soothing massage, this treatment creates a tranquil moment to relax and unwind before the big day. A true celebration of Love and Luxury.

### Glow With Our Signature Facial Treatments

Experience radiant, glowing skin with our signature facial treatments. Tailored to your unique skin needs, each facial combines premium products & expert techniques to cleanse, exfoliate & nourish. Whether rejuvenating tired skin, targeting signs of aging, or achieving a deep cleanse, our facials leave you with refreshed, luminous skin and a renewed sense of vitality.

### Exclusive Products for Your Daily Indulgence

Discover a selection of luxurious skincare & wellness products at The Iridium Spa Reception. Crafted with care for your well-being, our range includes high-quality oils, creams and fragrances to extend your spa experience beyond the treatment room. Whether for yourself or as a gift, these premium products offer indulgence & relaxation at home, allowing you to extend your wellness journey.
