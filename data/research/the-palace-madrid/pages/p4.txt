### Madrid’s Treasured Address

Occupying a prime location in the UNESCO-accredited Landscape of Light district, The Palace sits alongside world-famous art galleries, celebrated natural spaces and enlightened institutions. Part of Madrid’s scene since 1912, it is woven into the very fabric of the destination, both shaping and shaped by the city’s spirited and historic scene.

Immersive Cultural Scene

## Golden Triangle of Art

Composed of Spain’s three most famous galleries - Prado National Museum, Thyssen-Bornemisza Museum, and Reina Sofía Museum - Madrid’s Golden Triangle of Art is home to an unrivalled collection of European masterpieces. Located adjacent to The Palace, each venue provides a unique journey through the history of Spanish art, with treasures from Italian and Flemish artists enriching the experience.

One-Night Stay With Private Art Tour

## Thyssen-Bornemisza National Museum Premium Experience Package

Combining the luxury of The Palace with one of Madrid’s finest cultural insights, enjoy an overnight stay for two, daily breakfast and exclusive access to the Thyssen-Bornemisza National Museum. Guests will receive a morning guided tour, where the collection’s treasures can be enjoyed pre-opening.

A Coveted Green Escape

## Natural Madrid

Revel in a unique urban setting where expansive green spaces meet city life. Within walking distance of The Palace, Parque del Retiro spans over 350-acres of natural landscape, where visitors can enjoy outdoor pursuits, such as boating and walking or running through the attractive parkland. Nearby, the Royal Botanical Garden contains a rare collection of plants, from classic to exotic species.

Exclusive Retail Experiences

## Shopping

With a distinctive selection of boutiques, department stores and independent names, Madrid offers some of Europe’s best outlets. Alongside on-property personal shopping, the city is home to Serrano Street, known as the Golden Mile, lined with exclusive designers. Occupying historic buildings, Galería Canalejas is the newest upscale lifestyle destination, combining retail, dining and entertainment.

Does The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have a fitness center on-site?

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
