La Cúpula Restaurant & Bar

### Gastronomic Destination

Celebrating the city’s enduring culinary heritage, La Cúpula showcases elevated cuisine using the finest ingredients and regional produce. Overseen by talented Executive Chef Nuno Matos, vibrant flavors and signature recipes offer epicureans a true insight into the distinctives tastes and dishes so representative of Madrid.

### Opulent Setting

Impressively striking, the crystal dome that commands the entirety of La Cúpula garners attention from the outset. Multi-colored glass combines with historic decorative finials to provide a backdrop that is both innovative and magnificent. An iconic location for entertainment, it continues to frame the hotel’s story.

### La Cúpula Bar

Beneath The Palace’s palatial crystal dome, the circular bar has been thoughtfully designed to encourage a genial atmosphere for guests and locals. Order from a menu of specially fashioned cocktails and tasty tapas dishes or choose from a list of over 300 wines featuring labels from renowned estates and emerging talents.

Iconic Encounters

### 27 Club

Where legacy meets creativity, 27 Club is distinctive for its eclectic clientele, excellent mixology and connection to the hotel’s evolving history. A social scene that has endured across generations, the bar captures the essence of its erudite and visionary influences through expertly curated artworks and reworked heritage pieces. Immortalized by Ernest Hemingway’s literary tribute of the club’s dry martini, its renown for serving the city’s finest cocktails still stands.
