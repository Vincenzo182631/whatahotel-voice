# Welcome to The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid

## Discover our 5-star hotel in Madrid, Spain

Facing Madrid's iconic Neptune Fountain, The Palace Hotel, Madrid, a Luxury Collection Hotel, has been one of the city's most celebrated landmarks since 1912. Located within UNESCO's Landscape of Light...See More and moments from the Prado, Thyssen-Bornemisza and Reina Sofía museums, the hotel combines over a century of history with contemporary luxury. Its beautifully restored stained-glass dome, elegant guestrooms and suites, renowned culinary experiences and legendary event spaces have welcomed artists, royalty, world leaders and discerning travellers for generations. Today, The Palace Hotel remains one of Madrid's most iconic luxury destinations, offering an authentic connection to the city's artistic, cultural and gastronomic heritage.See Less

A City Landmark

## Supremely Located

A living legend, The Palace is profoundly entwined with the city’s heritage, architecture and people. Embedded in the cultural fabric of the capital, the hotel stands within Madrid’s UNESCO accredited Landscape of Light district. Ideally placed, it is within easy walking distance of the most prestigious museums and galleries, part of the renowned Triangle of Art.

Learn More

Local Cultural Scene

### Landscape of Light

A historic site in central Madrid, The Palace has been part of the city’s story since 1912 and is the first fully restored Asset of Cultural Interest within The Landscape of Light. Designated UNESCO Worldwide Heritage status in 2021, this fascinating area is celebrated for bringing culture, nature and science together in an urban environment.

Learn More

### Golden Triangle of Art

At the heart of the Golden Triangle of Art, The Palace sits side-by-side with the city’s iconic museums, Prado, Reina Sofía and Thyssen-Bornemisza. Marked out for offering outstanding universal value, these world-famous institutions provide a unique cultural journey through the history of Spanish art from Renaissance to the modern day.

Learn More

### Barrio de Las Letras

The Palace sits adjacent to the vibrant Bohemian area, Barrio de las Letras. Long associated with a rich literary tradition, the neighborhood has attracted artists, writers and musicians seeking insight. Here is where the Golden Age of Spanish literature saw acclaimed creatives, including Miguel de Cervantes and Lope de Vega, find inspiration.

Learn More

### A Celebrated Address for Bright Minds

Home to the aristocracy, as well as Spanish and Hollywood royalty, such as The Three Tenors, Ava Gardner, Sofia Loren, Orson Welles and others, The Palace has always been an innate part of Madrid’s lively social scene. The epicenter for creatives and intellectuals, it stimulates the imagination and invites expression, in a refined setting infused with convivial and genuine warmth.

An Evolving Story

### Travel in Time

Shaped by historic, cultural and natural evolution, The Palace stands regally in the heart of Madrid. A key landmark that has time-honored resonance as an innovator, it has been meticulously restored, with its façade returned to its 1912 splendor. The association with glamor endures through the building’s ornate sculptural niches, decorative elements, museum quality cupula and majestic scale.

In Tune With Madrid’s Creative Spirit

### Meetings and Special Occasions

Long held as the city’s principal landmark for special celebrations, the hotel is the go-to for a host of extraordinary events, favored by families, Madrileños and the international elite. The environment for work or play, it offers a range of inspirational and elevated meeting spaces that can host up to 600 guests for a cocktail reception and 450 in a conference set up.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid?

The check-in time at The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid?

On-site self parking is available at The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid. Self parking is available at The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid, with rates of €10.00 per hour, €50.00 per day, and €350.00 per week.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid booking.

What is the closest airport near The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid?

The closest airport near The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid is Adolfo Suárez Madrid–Barajas Airport (MAD). MAD is located approximately 13.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Madrid does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
