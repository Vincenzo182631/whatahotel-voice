The Royal Suite

### Madrid’s Illustrious Crown

Embodying elegant design, The Royal Suite provides unrivaled accommodation across 158m2. The expansive one-bedroom suite, overlooking Neptuno Fountain, has been fashioned to incorporate grand dining room, library with bar and chef’s kitchen. Sculpted marble and wood add refinement, while bespoke fixtures, splashes of color and whimsical chandeliers enhance the ambience to reflect the storied history of this palatial setting.

### Distinguished Landmark

Set within Madrid’s UNESCO accredited Landscape of Light, celebrated for its rich cultural ties to nature, art and science, The Palace has long been favored by the international and local elite. Housed in the most majestic building, the 470 rooms and suites benefit from an array of spectacular city aspects, including views across Neptuno Fountain, Cortes Square and Congress of Deputies.

Enriched With Madrid’s Story

## Palace Suite

Culture and sophistication converge in the generously sized Palace Suite. Depicting the most enchanting aspects of the city, custom design showcases the hotel’s exceptional past while residential comfort is assured. Historical context remains with the original fireplace as a stylish centerpiece, while bespoke wallpaper draws on the destination’s affinity with the Surrealist movement.

Honoring Spain’s Creative Legacy

## Cervantes Suite

An homage to renowned Spanish writer, Miguel de Cervantes Saavedra, this suite is furnished with handcrafted tapestry that draws inspiration from neighboring Parque del Retiro. Gifted artisans have skillfully designed furniture, offset by artworks that reference the evolving artistic movements of the 20th century. Compelling views across Las Letras neighborhood add to the sense of comfort and sophistication.

Sophisticated City-Center Living

## Cortes Suite

Designed with individual bedroom and living space, the Cortes Suite embodies Madrid’s artistic and aristocratic heritage. Overlooking Cortes Square and the Congress of Deputies, it provides luxurious comfort in a setting that celebrates the property’s impressive lineage. Influenced by Madrid’s creative scene, lush fabrics, bespoke artworks and plush furnishings add to the sense of refinement.

A Modern Expression of Madrid’s Gilded Past

## Neptuno Suite

A luxuriously appointed suite with advantageous views across to the Neptuno Fountain, the suite features separate living and sleeping areas. Expertly blending noble ancestry with modern comforts, the interiors have been custom styled to reflect local artisanal excellence. Motifs and embellishments also pay tribute to the hotel’s rich artistic past and lively cultural traditions.

Premium Guestrooms & Suites

### Savor Luxurious Accommodation

Long favored by those visiting Madrid, The Palace has iconic status and is renowned for its splendidly designed premium rooms and suites. A combination of beauty and innovation, the refined choice exemplifies the richness and compelling narrative so integral to the hotel’s evolution. Rich fabrics, custom furnishings and fine artworks provide modern comfort and a sumptuous setting.

Majestic Accommodation

### Exceptional Outlook

Superbly located at the center of historic Madrid, many of the 470 guestrooms and suites at The Palace benefit from excellent views across storied landmarks. Whether it’s gazing out over the Neoclassical Neptuno Fountain or marveling at the impressive Parliament, the hotel offers an unmatched vantage point.

### Palatial Living

Making an indelible mark on Madrid since its opening in 1912, The Palace, with its ornate façade and magnificent internal glass dome, remains at the forefront of the city’s gentrified scene. Occupying a prime corner aspect, opulence extends throughout the accommodation, creating an enduring beacon of luxury.

### Artistic Pedigree

A magnet for the enlightened, erudite and exceptional, The Palace is synonymous with Madrid’s golden age, where art and culture reigned supreme. Carefully selected artworks nurture this legacy, with paintings, wall coverings and design details drawing inspiration from Surrealism, theatre, music and more.
