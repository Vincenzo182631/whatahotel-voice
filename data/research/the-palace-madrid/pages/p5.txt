## Festive Season for Corporate & Groups

This festive season, bring colleagues, clients and partners together in one of Madrid’s most iconic settings.

The Palace Hotel, Madrid invites companies and groups to celebrate Christmas with bespoke experiences combining elegance, tradition and exceptional gastronomy.
