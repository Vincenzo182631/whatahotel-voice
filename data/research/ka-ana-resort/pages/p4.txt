## Learn More About Our Spa

What unique "Mayan" treatments do you offer? What unique "Mayan" treatments do you offer? - Spa

The spa is best known for its Maya Abdominal Massage, a traditional healing technique focused on improving digestion and energy flow. They also incorporate regional ingredients like cacao, coffee, sugar, and local herbs into their scrubs and wraps to provide a truly Belizean wellness experience.

Can I have my treatment outdoors? Can I have my treatment outdoors? - Spa

Yes. While there are serene indoor treatment rooms, guests can also opt for a massage in various outdoor settings across the property. Many travelers choose to have their sessions under the jungle canopy to enjoy the natural sounds of the rainforest and the cooling Belizean breeze.

Do I need to be a guest at the resort to book a spa treatment? Do I need to be a guest at the resort to book a spa treatment? - Spa

While the spa primarily serves resort guests, outside visitors can often book treatments subject to availability. It is highly recommended to book at least 24 to 48 hours in advance, especially during the peak season (November–May), as the boutique nature of the resort means slots fill up quickly.

What other wellness activities are available? What other wellness activities are available? - Spa

Wellness at Ka’ana extends beyond the massage table. The resort offers:

Sunrise Yoga: Classes are held on a dedicated jungle platform or even at nearby Mayan temples.

Holistic Healing: Including Reiki sessions to balance energy.

Organic Nutrition: Many ingredients used in spa snacks and post-treatment teas (like their signature Kombucha) come directly from the on-site organic garden.

Does the spa offer treatments for specific adventure-related needs? Does the spa offer treatments for specific adventure-related needs? - Spa

Absolutely. Since many guests spend their days hiking ruins or cave tubing, the spa offers specialized treatments like cooling aloe and chamomile rituals for sunburns, as well as targeted therapies for mosquito bites and muscle recovery after a day of trekking.
