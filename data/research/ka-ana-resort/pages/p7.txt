## Maya Cooking Class in Clarita’s Kitchen

Food offers one of the most immediate ways to understand Cayo. In Ka’ana’s Maya Cooking Class, you step into Clarita’s Kitchen for a hands-on experience shaped by local ingredients, traditional techniques and the stories carried through Maya cooking. You do not simply watch a demonstration; you take part in the preparation and share the meal that follows.

## COOK, DON’T JUST WATCH

Cook alongside your host and learn through touch, aroma, heat and repetition. The pace gives you time to ask questions and understand why a technique or ingredient matters, while keeping the experience relaxed and enjoyable rather than formal.

Ask About the Cooking Class for COOK, DON’T JUST WATCH

## INGREDIENTS WITH CONTEXT

Corn, herbs, cacao, spices and other local ingredients open a window into Maya foodways and the wider culinary landscape of Belize. As you cook, those ingredients connect back to the garden and to everyday food traditions, giving you context you can carry into the rest of your stay.

Discover the Experience for INGREDIENTS WITH CONTEXT

## A CULTURAL EXPERIENCE THROUGH FOOD

Experience Maya culture through food as something living, not staged in the past. The class keeps the focus on cooking, hospitality and respectful storytelling, giving you a tangible way to understand continuity, adaptation and family tradition in Belize today.

Plan Your Cultural Experience for A CULTURAL EXPERIENCE THROUGH FOOD

## FOR COUPLES, FAMILIES AND SMALL GROUPS

Share the experience in the way that fits your trip. Cook as a couple, learn together as a family or reserve the class with a small private group. If you have dietary needs, let the team know before booking so the current menu can be reviewed with you.

## FROM GARDEN TO TABLE

Pair the class with time in Ka’ana’s garden to see where part of the food story begins. After cooking, the experience comes full circle at the table: the ingredients, techniques and conversation become a meal you helped create.
