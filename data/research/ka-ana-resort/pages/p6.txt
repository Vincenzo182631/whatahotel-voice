## Pontoon Jungle & Waterfall Experience

Discover the natural beauty of Belize’s Cayo District on a jungle pontoon and waterfall adventure surrounded by lush rainforest and panoramic views of the Maya Mountains. Swim in refreshing natural pools, float along the river by tube, and explore some of western Belize’s most scenic waterfalls. For guests seeking more adventure, climb the 130-foot Sandpaper Waterfall and enjoy optional cliff jumps and a rope swing. A freshly prepared BBQ lunch is served aboard the pontoon, with time to relax along a sandy riverbank surrounded by tropical forest and waterfalls.

Available to guests of Ka’ana Boutique Hotel in San Ignacio, this experience offers a memorable way to explore the rivers, waterfalls and rainforest of Belize’s Cayo District.

## Atm Cave (Actun Tunichil Muknal)

Located in the heart of the Belizean rainforest and the Tapir Mountain Nature Reserve, this cave was a sacred place to the Mayas of Belize. Consisting of a series of chambers, ending in a large Cathedral where ceremonies once took place.

Your adventure begins with a 45-minute trek that includes wading across 3 rivers to get you to the “Cave of the Stone Sepulcher”.

## Adventure Horseback Riding

Uncover the spirit of adventure in Belize with this immersive 1.5-hour guided horseback ride through the lush landscapes of the Maya Mountains. Journey beneath a vibrant jungle canopy alive with exotic medicinal plants and native birdlife as you make your way to a hidden treasure of the ancient world.

Your destination: an unexcavated Maya mound, an ancient pyramid that has remained untouched for centuries. Dismount and hike to the summit, where breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding jungle await a powerful and unforgettable connection to the rich legacy of the ancient Maya civilization.

Led by knowledgeable wranglers and paired with well-trained horses, this adventure offers the perfect balance of culture, nature, and exploration. Bring your camera and prepare to experience one of Belize’s most captivating hidden gems.

## Tikal Archeological Site

Journey into the heart of the Maya world with a visit to Tikal, one of the largest, most spectacular and archaeologically rich ancient cities ever uncovered. A UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1979, Tikal remains a bucket list destination for travelers seeking breathtaking history and unforgettable adventure and from Belize, it's just a convenient trip across the border.

Stand in awe of Tikal’s five great pyramids overlooking the vast Mayan ceremonial center where clay artifacts, vessels and stelae tell tales of a civilization entrenched in other-worldly practices and ceremonies.

## Mountain Pine Ridge Forest Reserve

Located in the highlands of the southern zone of western Belize, the Mountain Pine Ridge Forest Reserve offers cool temperatures and reveals splendid natural monuments.

Within its 300,000 acres of protected pine forests, you will find sites including Thousand Foot Falls, the largest fall of Belize and the Central American region, as well as the cave Rio Frio, a quarter-mile cave, to name a few.

## Xunantunich Archeological Site

Once the thriving home of ingenious Maya artisans, master builders, and early engineers whose creation still amaze archaeologists today, this remarkable site now invites modern day explorers to step into its timeless magic.

Perched atop a natural limestone ridge, Xunantunich "Maiden of the Rock" rises above lush valleys and rolling mountains,

offering breathtaking 360 degrees panoramic views that stretch all the way to neighboring Guatemala.

Its towering temples, atmospheric plazas, and captivating legends make it a must see destination for travelers seeking history, beauty, and adventure in one unforgettable experience.

## Caracol Archeological Site

Caracol temples ("snail ") represents the largest Mayan City in Belize and home to the largest man-made structure in the country. Venture deep into the jungles of the Maya Mountains, habitat to an array of wildlife such as tapirs, jaguars, monkeys, toucans, and scarlet macaws.

_Explore ancient courtyards, ball courts ,residential complexes and a complete astronomical observatory. Over 100 tombs have been uncovered to date. Your expert guide will lead you from small relics through partial structures building. Enjoy a  refreshing swim at Rio on Pools on your way back._
