# An Intimate Boutique Hotel in San Ignacio, Belize

Nestled on the edge of the lush wilderness in San Ignacio, Cayo, Ka’ana Boutique Hotel is a refined sanctuary where nature, culture, and comfort come together. Our name, Ka’ana, means “heavenly place” in the ancient Maya language—a reflection of the tranquility and sense of belonging we strive to create for every guest.

We believe in unhurried moments, genuine hospitality, and a deep respect for our surroundings. Our commitment to sustainability and community is woven into daily operations, from supporting local artisans and farmers to promoting responsible tourism that honors Belize’s cultural and natural heritage. Whether you are seeking adventure, cultural discovery, or quiet relaxation, Ka’ana Boutique Hotel is a place to slow down, reconnect, and experience Belize in a truly personal way.
