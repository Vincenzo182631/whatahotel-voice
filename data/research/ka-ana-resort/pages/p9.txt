## Xunantunich Archeological Site

Once the thriving home of ingenious Maya artisans, master builders, and early engineers whose creation still amaze archaeologists today, this remarkable site now invites modern day explorers to step into its timeless magic.

Perched atop a natural limestone ridge, Xunantunich "Maiden of the Rock" rises above lush valleys and rolling mountains,

offering breathtaking 360 degrees panoramic views that stretch all the way to neighboring Guatemala.

Its towering temples, atmospheric plazas, and captivating legends make it a must see destination for travelers seeking history, beauty, and adventure in one unforgettable experience.

## Caracol Archeological Site

Caracol temples ("snail ") represents the largest Mayan City in Belize and home to the largest man-made structure in the country. Venture deep into the jungles of the Maya Mountains, habitat to an array of wildlife such as tapirs, jaguars, monkeys, toucans, and scarlet macaws.

_Explore ancient courtyards, ball courts ,residential complexes and a complete astronomical observatory. Over 100 tombs have been uncovered to date. Your expert guide will lead you from small relics through partial structures building. Enjoy a  refreshing swim at Rio on Pools on your way back._

## Maya Cooking Class

Let us take you and your taste buds on a culinary journey!

Maya food is steeped in a thousand years of history and tradition.Maya cooks still use ancient methods to prepare flavorful meals for their families.

In this class, you’ll create classic dishes like tamales, corn tortillas, and caldo using the fogon, an authentic Maya stove, in our remake of a traditional kitchen.

Discuss techniques and of the Maya and then enjoy the fruits of your labor.

## Ceramic Making In San Antonio

The San Antonio Women's Cooperative are a group of ladies still trying to preserve culture in this modern world. These artisans

will demonstrate the “coil method,” and you will be able to try your hand at creating something of your own. Following this, make corn tortillas on a traditional fire hearth.

Walk the rest of the property and learn about the sustainable processes used to fire the ceramic kilns and sustain an organic

garden. Enjoy a meal starting with warm atole, followed by roasted chicken, fresh salsa, and your recently prepared corn tortillas.
