Lincoln Steakhouse & Bar Details

### About Lincoln

Order a perfectly prepared steak and savor it with breathtaking mountain views at Lincoln - a steakhouse in Scottsdale offering delicious American cuisine, butcher-cut steaks from area farms, and plentiful sides. Enjoy indoor or outdoor dining.

Learn More

### Eat Local

Lincoln partners with local farms to get the freshest, tastiest ingredients to be used in everything from the appetizers to the cocktails.

### Cuisine

Discover our modern American menu that spotlights the area farms our steakhouse restaurant in Scottsdale sources from. Enjoy simple fare with a Camelback spin. Savor dishes like Caesar Hearts with Vella dry jack cheese and a Lincoln Bone-in Ribeye with King Crab Oscar.

### Ambiance

Choose indoor or outdoor seating, both featuring compelling views of our Scottsdale, Arizona setting in the Sonoran Desert, surrounded by mountains. Honoring the American steakhouse tradition in Scottsdale with its sleek contemporary decor. Soak up 180-degree mountain views on our patio.

### Private Events

Private events are a natural fit for our resort setting. Whether planning an intimate family gathering, a company dinner or spectacular reception, our team of talented culinary artists and catering professionals combine with beautiful indoor and outdoor spaces to ensure you receive only the finest in food, service and atmosphere. Contact Markanne Benich Restaurant Sales Coordinator for reservations and menus.

Rita's Cantina & Bar Details

### About Rita's Cantina & Bar

From fajitas to margaritas, our Scottsdale restaurant focuses on Southwestern fare. The vibe is casual and comfortable in the dining room, while crowds at the bar enjoy live music Thursday – Saturday and happy hour specials.

### Authentic Local & Regional Cuisine

If you consider dining to be an art form, then Rita's Kitchen will most definitely inspire your palate. Rita's Cantina & Bar features fresh, regional Southwest cuisine and nightly live entertainment.

### Cuisine

Featuring the bold colors and zesty flavors of contemporary Southwestern cooking, Rita's Kitchen infuses its food with the essence of the region. Locally grown and seasonal ingredients take over dishes, from a Beet & Goat Cheese Salad with Arizona pecans to Chicken Enchilada Rojas topped with Cotija cheese.

### Ambiance

Inspired by the Sonoran Desert setting, our lively Southwestern Scottsdale restaurant is an inviting space that plays into our heritage and traditions. The natural beauty of the land is celebrated on Rita's large stone patio. From here, views of nearby Mummy Mountain and desert flora and fauna are plentiful.

### Private Events

Choose from several private dining venues at Rita's Cantina & Bar. Browse our private dining menus with a Southwestern flare, which are customizable to suit your needs. Our expert staff stands ready to help you plan every detail of your event.
