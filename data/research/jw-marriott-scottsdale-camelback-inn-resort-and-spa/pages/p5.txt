Prettily perched near the Phoenix Mountains Preserve, the area is a favorite with third-home owners and Marriott, which inaugurated the JW brand here. Western themes are judiciously presented, from the hacienda style, the chimineas sending out gentle scent, and the casita accommodations, worth the upgrade to signature view. Flowers bloom everywhere at this historic desert gem.
