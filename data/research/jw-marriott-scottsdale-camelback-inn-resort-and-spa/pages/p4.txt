### Outdoor Fitness

Overlooking the iconic Camelback Mountain and set against a backdrop of the Sonoran Desert in Scottsdale, our spa resort offers plenty of opportunities for outdoor recreation, including hiking, walking, golf, tennis, and more.

### Resort Tennis Courts

Tennis players can look forward to 6 hard courts and a variety of exercise and stretching classes. Courts are open daily 8am to 5pm and are available at a hourly rate. Racquet rental is also available. Hourly lessons, both private and semi-private can be reserved.

### Pickleball

Take some time to get outdoors in the beautiful Scottsdale sun and test your pickleball skills on the court. Rackets and supplies can be picked up at the Bell Stand. 1 hour of pickleball – complimentary with resort amenity fee.

## Stargazing Experience

Grab a blanket and scope out a prime viewing spot for Camelback Inn's Stargazing Experience, available nightly! Each experience includes a keepsake Camelback Inn branded blanket to use then take home, chef curated charcuterie selection, house-made cookies, spiced nuts, two bottled waters, your choice of house red, white, or sparkling wine, and a map of the best vantage points around the resort to take in the desert skies. Delivery of your Stargazing Experience basket will be made to your guest room during the timeframe selected when booking. Once basket is delivered, you may enjoy your Stargazing Experience at your leisure anytime throughout the evening. When finished with your basket, simply press the included button and our staff will come pick it up from your guest room, along with any dishes. Price is $99 per experience. Price is exclusive of tax and service charge. Reservations must be made at least 24 hours in advance and are available for resort guests only.

Learn More

## Wellness Wanderer Experience

Embark on your next adventure with our Wellness Wanderer Experience, available daily! Delivered directly to your guest room, this thoughtfully curated package includes a cooler backpack stocked with locally inspired and house-made snacks, hydration packs, two bottles of refreshing water, sunscreen and detailed maps for our hiking trails, scenic bike routes, and desert excursions. Perfect for exploring both our resort grounds and the breathtaking Arizona desert, it’s everything you need for a day of discovery and wellness. Delivery of your Wellness Wanderer Experience will be made to your guest room during the timeframe selected when booking. Once delivered, you may enjoy your Wellness Wanderer Experience at your leisure anytime throughout your stay. Price is $59 per experience. Price is exclusive of tax and service charge .Reservations must be made at least 24 hours in advance and are available for resort guests only.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
