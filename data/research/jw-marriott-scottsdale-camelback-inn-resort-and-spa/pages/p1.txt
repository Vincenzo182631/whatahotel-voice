# Welcome to JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa

## Rejuvenate your energy at our resort in Scottsdale, AZ

Discover JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa, an award-winning hotel in Scottsdale, near Camelback Mountain in Paradise Valley, AZ. Our luxury Scottsdale resort welcomes you with attentive service, deluxe amenities, heated outdoor pools, garden terraces, and desert trails in the Sonoran Desert. Take our free shuttle to our 18-hole championship golf courses or treat yourself to a refreshing...See More massage in our tranquil day spa. Savor gourmet dining experiences in Scottsdale in our resort restaurants and bars, or relax in privacy with our resort's casitas and suites boasting sophisticated furnishings and outdoor living spaces with views of Camelback Mountain. Our pueblo-style, pet-friendly accommodations feature kitchenettes and deluxe amenities, with a select few featuring private pools. Those in town with business needs or wedding plans will appreciate our elegant, large-scale event venues. JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa wants to make your stay in Scottsdale, AZ, truly remarkable.See Less

### Rita's Cantina and Bar

A local icon and desert original channeling the sultry energy of its namesake drink, the margarita. The restaurant and bar in Scottsdale complement the resort's 1930s-era casitas and offer traditional Mexican cuisine. Rita's Bar is open 10AM - 12AM daily.

### Camelback Golf Club

Located at our Scottsdale resort, Camelback Golf Club features 36 championship holes. Two scenic courses, Ambiente and Padre, boast native grasses, lakes, lofty trees, and mountain views. The terrain, with its elevation changes, bunkers, and water holes, challenges golfers of all skill levels. Play a round spectacularly on your next Arizona vacation.

## Camelback Golf Club

## History

Designed by architect Edward Loomis Bowes, the resort cost $75,000 to build and accommodated 75 guests. The resort immediately attracted wealthy travelers. Early guests included many celebrities and business leaders, including Mrs. Dwight D. Eisenhower, Clark Gable, Jimmy Stewart, Bette Davis, and J.W. Marriott, Sr., who shared the Stewart's love for Camelback Inn.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa is Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX). PHX is located approximately 9.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Scottsdale Camelback Inn Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
