### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City?

The largest occupancy room at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City is the Deluxe Two Queen Beds with a maximum occupancy of 3.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City?

No, the rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City have in-room jettubs?

Yes, some room types at Las Alcobas, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Mexico City have jettubs. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
