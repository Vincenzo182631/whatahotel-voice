# Diverse Restaurants in Downtown San Antonio

## Dining

At The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio, dining is woven into the fabric of the city’s story. Our collection of restaurants and lounges brings elevated flavor and refined energy to the...See More heart of downtown, blending timeless elegance with contemporary taste. Savor seasonal, locally sourced ingredients at Gallery On the Park, where Texas flavors shine in a setting as iconic as the hotel itself. Discover the bold, modern spirit of Esencia, our newest culinary experience celebrating vibrant ingredients and expressive cuisine. Or linger over craft cocktails inside The St. Anthony Club, the storied lounge famously adorned in the 1950s by Dorothy Draper — an enduring symbol of glamour and gathering.See Less

A Legacy of Dining, Thoughtfully Reimagined

Dining at The St. Anthony Hotel reflects a rich culinary legacy shaped by history, culture, and refined craftsmanship. Each venue offers a distinct experience, from relaxed mornings to elegant evenings, all rooted in a strong sense of place.

### Complimentary Valet Parking for Dining Guests

Enjoy complimentary valet parking when dining at Esencia, Anacacho Coffee & Cantina, Gallery on the Park, or St. Anthony Club. Valet parking is located at 300 East Travis Street.
