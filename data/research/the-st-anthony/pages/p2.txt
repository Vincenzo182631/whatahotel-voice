Do the rooms at The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio have a work space?

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio?

The largest occupancy room at The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio is the Suite with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Some rooms at The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
