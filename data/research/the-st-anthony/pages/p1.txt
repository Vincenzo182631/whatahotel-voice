# Welcome to The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio

## Elevate your story at our luxury hotel in San Antonio, Texas

Overlooking downtown San Antonio and Travis Park, The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio offers a AAA Four Diamond experience steeped in history. Our hotel places you in a storied setting...See More where historic grandeur meets the refined experience of a luxury hotel in downtown San Antonio. Opened in 1909 as the city’s first grand hotel, it blends marble detailing and Corinthian columns with modern sophistication, steps from The Alamo and the River Walk. As a family-friendly hotel in downtown San Antonio, we offer a welcoming stay with comfort in mind for every traveler. Experience the contemporary spirit of a modern hotel in downtown San Antonio, brought to life through thoughtful amenities and tailored service. Dining reflects the city’s heritage, from the global influences of Esencia and The St. Anthony Club to artisan coffee and cocktails at Anacacho Coffee & Cantina. Thirteen venues, rooftop cocktails, and vintage‑inspired rooms with marble baths and velvet accents complete a timeless stay.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio?

The check-in time at The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio?

On-site parking is available at The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio. Self parking is available for $60.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio?

The closest airport near The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio is San Antonio International Airport (SAT). SAT is located approximately 8.5 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Anthony, a Luxury Collection Hotel, San Antonio has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
