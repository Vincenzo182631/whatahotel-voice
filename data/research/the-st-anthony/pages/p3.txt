### At the Heart of Historic San Antonio

Located in the heart of downtown, overlooking Travis Park, The St. Anthony places guests steps from the Alamo, the River Walk, and San Antonio’s cultural landmarks. This historic 1909 hotel blends timeless architecture with modern luxury, offering an iconic setting for dining, celebrations, and memorable stays.

Learn More

### A Destination for Elevated Dining

Dining at The St. Anthony is a destination where historic elegance meets modern culinary expression. Enjoy Texas inspired flavor at Gallery On the Park, bold fare at Anacacho Coffee + Cantina, or the newly opened Esencia, celebrating San Antonio through thoughtful plates and warm hospitality. Evenings continue with handcrafted cocktails at The St. Anthony Club, a glamorous 1950s space designed by Dorothy Draper.

Learn More

### Iconic Spaces for Inspired Gatherings

Create unforgettable weddings, meetings, and celebrations at The St. Anthony, where historic grandeur meets modern capability. From the ornate Peraux Ballroom adorned with 19th-century French tapestries to the Sky Terrace overlooking downtown, each venue offers a distinctive setting enhanced by expert planning, curated catering, and seamless service.
