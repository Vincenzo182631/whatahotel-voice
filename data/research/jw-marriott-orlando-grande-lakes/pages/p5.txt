# Holiday Events at JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes

## Festive

Celebrate the holidays at Grande Lakes, where elegance, warmth, and joyful gatherings come together. Savor exceptional seasonal cuisine, share meaningful moments with family and friends, and create treasured memories in a setting designed for festive celebration and refined comfort. This is a holiday escape where every detail is crafted to delight and inspire.

New Experiences

### Ice Skating & Fire Pits

Grande Lakes is thrilled to offer Orlando's only resort ice skating rink, an exclusive holiday experience for guests of all ages. Gather with loved ones to take to the ice, then unwind beside our glowing fire pits and explore the charm of our annual Christmas Market. It's the perfect holiday getaway for fun and festive cheer.

Festive Escape

Plan your holiday escape to JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes, where winter is warmer and celebrations shine brighter. Book early to enjoy exclusive savings and delight in the enchantment of Grande Lakes.

Book Today

### Ice Skating

Glide across our ice skating rink set beside the scenic lake, welcoming both seasoned skaters and first-timers alike. Skating under the winter sky is the perfect festive activity that will bring joy and happiness to everyone. So, come and join us for some winter fun!

Book Now

### Fireside Lounge

Warm up this winter beside our cozy fire pits overlooking the ice skating rink. Enjoy an unforgettable evening featuring a one-hour ice skating session for up to four guests, seasonal rinkside cocktails, a savory charcuterie board, and a sweet s'mores kit to share.

Reserve Now

### Christmas Market

Discover the bliss of the holiday season with our Christmas Market. Stroll amongst European-inspired cottages featuring traditional holiday delicacies, tasty beverages, and local artisan crafts. This holiday affair includes hourly snowfall and many advent surprises that the entire family will enjoy.

Learn More

### Gingerbread School

Join the pastry team to build your own gingerbread house. The fun-filled activity begins by building the house with pre-baked gingerbread panels on a wooden foundation. Then, guests can select from dozens of different types of candy and other edible details to decorate and personalize their gingerbread house.

Book Now

### Santa's Teddy Bear Tea

Sip into the holidays with the most cherished tradition. Choose from our lavish selection of teas and savor thoughtfully curated sweets and culinary delights. Engage with crafts, storytelling, live entertainment, and a special visit from Santa Claus.

Book Now

### The Magic Show: Holiday Edition

Prepare for an extraordinary event presented by acclaimed magician and America's Got Talent finalist, Drew Thomas and his award-winning colleagues. The Magic show combines mind-bending magic, large-scale illusions, and humor to create an electrifying atmosphere. This show will captivate you with suspense and immersive audience participation, promising an unforgettable evening brimming with laughter and wonder.

Book Now

### Festive Dining

Indulge in an unforgettable festive culinary journey at JW Marriott, where every meal is a celebration of flavor and elegance. Savor exquisite à la carte menus inspired by the season's finest ingredients, or delight in lavish buffet spreads featuring timeless holiday favorites. Enjoy a warm, inviting ambiance complemented by exceptional service, and celebrate the joy of the holidays with us.

Christmas Dinner

## Feast of the Seven Fishes

Join us at Primo, a MICHELIN Recommended restaurant, for a Christmas celebration rooted in Italian tradition with the Feast of the Seven Fishes. This event features exquisite seafood dishes that honor the Italian-American way of celebrating Christmas, making your holiday season both festive and delicious.

Reserve Now

Christmas Buffet

## Citron

Celebrate the magic of the season with a festive Christmas buffet at Citron. Our beautifully decorated space sets the perfect scene for making holiday memories - and be sure to catch a special appearance from Santa Claus himself! Guests ages 21 and over will enjoy a complimentary glass of wine or beer.

Reserve Now

New Year's Eve

## Primo Grande New Year's Eve

Celebrate New Year's Eve at Primo with a sumptuous dinner and sparkling champagne. Choose an early 5:00 pm seating for a four-course gourmet dinner or the 8:00 pm seating for a six-course culinary experience with private balcony access, offering a premium view of the fireworks.

Reserve Now

New Year's Eve

## EvrBar NYE Party

Step into 2027 with an elegant New Year's Eve party at EvrBar. Reserved exclusively for hotel guests, this celebration features premium seating, a live DJ, and a dazzling ball drop to usher in the new year with luxury and flare. Raise a glass, soak in the vibrant atmosphere, and make your first moments in 2027 truly unforgettable.

### New Year's Eve Market & Fireworks

Ring in the New Year at our exclusive New Year's Eve Market, a celebration reserved for our valued guests. Featuring shimmering snowfall, an array of gourmet treats, and a host of unexpected surprises. Then step toward the shores of Grande Lake, where a breathtaking fireworks display will illuminate the night sky, welcoming 2027 in a spectacle of light and brilliance.
