# Welcome to JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes

## Stay at our luxury resort in Orlando, FL

Make your Florida escape an extraordinary one at JW Marriott Orlando Grande Lakes. Located on a lush, 500-acre property, our family resort in Orlando is the ideal base for those wishing to explore the area—or for sunny family vacations. Experience our Grande Lakes Water Park, featuring a lazy river, Headwaters Slide Tower with three waterslides, restful lagoons, and luxury cabanas, or try our challenging...See More 18-hole golf course, designed by PGA legend Greg Norman. Stretch out in modern rooms offering luxury bedding, marble bathrooms, 65-inch HDTVs, and sweeping views of our luxury resort in Orlando. Select from several enticing in-house dining options, from Italian fare at Primo to a farm-to-table menu and craft beer at Whisper Creek Kitchen. Take advantage of our resort's excellent location to explore gorgeous central Florida. JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes, provides an exceptional experience that you and your family will never forget.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes. Self parking is available for $42.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $58.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes is Orlando International Airport (MCO). MCO is located approximately 10.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
