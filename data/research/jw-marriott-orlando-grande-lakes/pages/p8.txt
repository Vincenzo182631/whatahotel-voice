Set the scene for us.
The towering JW Marriott Orlando sits on 500 acres at the headwaters of the Florida Everglades. Step through the grand, marble-tiled lobby to the outdoor patio and relax with a glass of wine by the fire pit, where you can also roast marshmallows for s’mores on special nights.
What's the story behind this hotel?
As part of the Grande Lakes resort complex, the JW Marriott shares many of its facilities with the neighboring Ritz-Carlton Orlando, including an 18-hole Greg Norman–designed championship golf course and a 40,000-square-foot spa.
What are the rooms themselves like?
Rooms are spacious and serene with neutral decor and pastel highlights. Business travelers will appreciate the large desk and ergonomic chair; the pillowtop mattress and superb bedding guarantee an excellent night’s sleep. Internet-equipped TVs are primed for Netflix and Hulu. Marble-tiled bathrooms are roomy, with separate walk-in showers and soaking tubs.
Once we've settled in, where should we go for our first meal?
Whisper Creek Farm: The Kitchen uses ingredients grown onsite to create small plates, which pair nicely with the craft beers. The large, varied breakfast buffet is another highlight.
What's the guest demographic like?
Half business travelers attending an event in the resort’s 80,000 square feet of meeting space; half stroller-wielding families bopping between theme parks.
How does it fit into the surrounding area?
The resort is centrally located near Disney World,
Universal Orlando
, SeaWorld, and the outlet malls, but thanks to its lush, natural setting, it feels removed.
Anything else we should be sure to check out if we're staying here?
The JW Marriott has one of the best hotel pools in Orlando. Float past waterfalls and under bridges on the lazy river, or take a dip in one of the many onsite pools. Lounge chairs and shade umbrellas are plentiful.
So why would you recommend it?
This is a conveniently located resort with many benefits of the Ritz-Carlton next door.
