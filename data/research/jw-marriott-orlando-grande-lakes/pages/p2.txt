### The Presidential Suites

Our recently renovated Presidential Suites in Orlando offer an extraordinary retreat, combining the grandeur of a private residence with the finest comforts of resort living. The expansive one-bedroom suite features 1.5 baths, a sophisticated living room, a full dining area, and a dedicated workstation with an ergonomic desk and high-speed Wi-Fi.
