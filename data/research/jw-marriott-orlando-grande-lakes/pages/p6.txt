# New Luxury Awaits

A Reimagined JW Marriott Experience Welcomes Guests

## THE GRANDE REVEAL. A NEW ERA OF EXCELLENCE

Designed for excellence, group meeting spaces have just been reimagined. Inspired by the natural beauty of Florida, the resort-wide renovations that began with guest rooms and restaurants and now extends to group spaces establishes a serene, elevated design. From ballrooms to breakout rooms to outdoor venues, the bright, airy and sophisticated design makes events flow beautifully. Round out the agenda with equally beautiful accommodations, award-winning service, world-class dining and a range of luxurious activities and amenities to help guests recharge and refresh.

## Newly Renovated Meeting Space

Introducing our JW Marriott Orlando meeting space renovation. Enjoy newly renovated pre-function space, meeting rooms and ballrooms featuring soft but rich tones with an overall serene and bright aesthetic to seamlessly integrate outdoor and indoor experiences. Discover dynamic furniture with ease of connectivity to inspire networking and productivity.

## Reinvented Rooms and Suites

Every one of the 1,010 accommodations has been reinvented to delight guests with serene and bright textiles and furnishings inspired by the lush grounds and lakes that adorn Grande Lakes Orlando. Seven new executive suites offer growing families the choice of two and three bedrooms featuring built-in bunk beds for teens and children, king-sized beds for parents, and separate living areas.

## Grande Lakes Waterpark Now Open

The new Grande Lakes Waterpark is sure to be a splash with guests seeking both rip-roaring family fun or exclusive relaxation and escape. Exciting and refreshing, Grande Lakes Waterpark features six distinct zones and is home to a lazy river, Headwaters Slide Tower with three waterslides, restful lagoons, and the Oasis VIP firepit lounge. There’s something for everyone, including a new poolside eatery.

## Tasty Eats Poolside at Knife Burger

Renowned chef John Tesar’s steak and seafood restaurant, Knife & Spoon at The Ritz-Carlton, earned one Star in the inaugural MICHELIN Guide Florida 2022. Now chef Tesar brings much of the same tantalizing flavor poolside for creative and casual fare at Knife Burger. Guests can enjoy the best-tasting burgers in town without leaving the waterpark, all sourced from high-quality Texas cattle, and paired with favorites like crispy fries and handspun, frosty shakes.

## Ever Delightful, Ever Inviting, EvrBar

EvrBar is the reinvented, re-energized lobby bar. Inspired by the nearby headwaters of the Everglades and the natural beauty of the lush grounds, EvrBar features signature cocktails, craft beer, and light bites. Located next to acclaimed restaurant Primo, EvrBar overlooks the new Grande Lakes Waterpark and is ideal for relaxing with your favorite drink.

## Mediterranean and Italian Fare from Primo

Primo continues to be led by two-time James Beard Award-winning Chef Melissa Kelly, though the space has been redesigned and refreshed, including a completely new layout and a 50-seat terrace. The design incorporates Chef Kelly's notion of being stewards of the land and interconnectedness with nature. The inspired menu of the MICHELIN Guide Florida 2022-recommended restaurant focuses on a farm-to-table experience sourced by Whisper Creek Farm and the Primo garden.
