## Golf

The Central Florida landscape provided inspiration to legendary golf course designer Greg Norman, who worked with nature to create challenging greens, wide fairways, and hazards. The 18-hole championship course is a Certified Audubon Cooperative Sanctuary and tests the abilities of every skill level. Our Orlando golf resort also features a clubhouse, restaurant, and lessons for both children and adults.

Welcome to our nature-inspired Spa retreat in Orlando, a sanctuary spanning 40,000 sq. ft. Yes. JW Marriott Orlando, Grande Lakes offers a fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
