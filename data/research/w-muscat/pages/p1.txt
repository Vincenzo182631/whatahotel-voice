# Welcome to W Muscat

## Relax and Unwind in Pure Luxury at W Muscat Hotel

W Muscat is a luxury, beach hotel in Muscat situated on the upbeat Shatti Al Qurum beachfront, offering insider access to what’s up and coming in the city. Step inside one of our 279 luxury hotel rooms and suites where Omani heritage meets contemporary design. One of the best hotels in Muscat city, we offer rooms boasting breathtaking views of the azure waters of the Gulf of Oman. Six tantalizing restaurants...See More at our family hotel in Muscat offer an exciting experience for every foodie. Enjoy culinary prowess and trendy mixology at our sophisticated rooftop seasonal pop-up Zuma, experience a modern approach to Chinese cuisine at Ba Ban, savor mouthwatering steaks at CHAR, dine in style at Harvest, mix and mingle at Living Room, and live it up at the WET Deck. With over 8,500 square feet of meeting space with natural daylight, let our expert meeting planners elevate your event to the next level. Our Whatever/Whenever® service takes care of everything else.See Less

Discover W Muscat: Your Vibrant Escape by the Sea

Set along Muscat’s beautiful beachfront, W Muscat blends modern design, local culture, and signature W energy. Enjoy stylish rooms, vibrant dining, relaxing spa experiences, and stunning sea views, all within easy reach of Muscat’s key attractions.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Muscat?

The check-in time at W Muscat is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Muscat allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Muscat. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of ر.ع.20.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Muscat?

On-site parking is available at W Muscat. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Muscat have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Muscat has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Muscat booking.

What is the closest airport near W Muscat?

The closest airport near W Muscat is Muscat International Airport (MCT). MCT is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Muscat have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Muscat does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
