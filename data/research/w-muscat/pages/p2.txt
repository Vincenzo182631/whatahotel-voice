## Accommodations

Escape the unexpected with our 279 suites and hotel rooms in Muscat. Fall asleep in the comfort of our signature bed with its pillow-top mattress, fluffy goose-down duvet, and 300-thread count Egyptian cotton sheets.

### Penthouse II

Step into the Penthouse II at W Muscat and be captivated by its audacious design. A signature circular bed, adorned with bold animal prints and statement statues, pays homage to nomadic traditions. Rich ornate carpets contrast with vibrant block-colored sculptures and striking unique artifacts. Beyond the interiors, bask in breathtaking sea vistas from dawn to dusk. Elevate your stay with game table hawalis, a dedicated DJ space, and a luxurious bathtub crowned by star constellation lighting. Experience a fusion of Oman's timeless charm with avant-garde allure.

## Cozy Room

Wonderful Room, an emblem of inspiration, exudes W-style elegance with a touch of Arabic charm. Zanzibar-inspired prints grace the corner Majlis seating, echoing the Omani Kumma, complemented by a coffee table paying homage to Qahwa. The ceiling mirrors a desert tent's slanted structure, with tilted white paneling, fostering the warmth of a desert abode. Evoking a travel theme, the room boasts a saddle-like chair, a vibrant mustard stool, and a faux fur feature wall, portraying a unique take on nomadic voyages. The MIXBAR and closet are fashioned from stacked luggage trunks and draped in exotic silks, symbolizing the nomadic spirit. Interchangeable pieces in the majlis area reflect Omani hospitality, with some rooms featuring a camping-style mat, adding to the enchanting journey. Art imitates the metallic sandwaves of sea and desert, creating a dynamic, rippled colour scheme, bursting with energy.

## Penthouse I

Elevate your experience to the Penthouse I at W Muscat. Dive into 205sqm of unparalleled luxury, primed as the quintessential after-party hub. Dominated by a regal Double King Bed and enveloped in primal prints and audacious animalistic motifs, this space encapsulates the nomadic essence, complemented by panoramic sea views. With unique Omani abstract art touches and a discreet MIXBAR, the Penthouse I stands as a testament to exclusivity. Step in, and indulge in the iconic W lifestyle.
