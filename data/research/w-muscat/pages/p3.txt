# Dining W Muscat

## Dining

Satisfy any craving. From fresh remixes of classic beverages to daring local-inspired dining, W Muscat is a destination for inventive cuisine. Take in killer views and bold beats over a drink at Siddharta Lounge® by Buddha-Bar, or sample traditional Chinese flavors at Ba Ban. Make it a group outing at Harvest, with communal seating and classic fare from around the world.
