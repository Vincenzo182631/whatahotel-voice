### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an offers a fitness center available for a fee to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an have an on-site spa?

Yes, The St. Regis Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an?

Shenzhen Uniwalk Qianhai, OH Bay, Nantou Ancient City, Xiwan Mangrove Park, Bay Opera of Shenzhen and more are nearby The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an.
