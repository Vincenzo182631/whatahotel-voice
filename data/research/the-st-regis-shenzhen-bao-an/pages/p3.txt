Family Traditions at St. Regis

### The St. Regis Bar

The bar offers an exotic escape into a world of oriental charm, evoking the good old days of Bao’an. At the center of the bar, a 4-meter-wide mural hand-painted by a local artist tells the untold stories of Bao’an, celebrating its heritage and providing a captivating focal point for conversations.

The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao’an Dining

### Yan Ting

An elegant venue specializing in fine Cantonese cuisine, with a focus on seasonal offerings and premium ingredients for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. A long interactive kitchen, combined with our authentic culinary philosophy, sets the stage for a dynamic dining experience, celebrating the art of cooking.

### The Drawing Room

Indulge in a luxurious experience at our elegant lobby venue, where an extensive selection of aromatic teas, gourmet coffees, and delectable bites awaits. Every afternoon, immerse yourself in our modern interpretation of Afternoon Tea, a tribute to the refined legacy of Caroline Astor from the early 1900s, offering a perfect blend of tradition and contemporary flair.

### The St. Regis Bar

The St. Regis Bar offers a diverse selection of classic and contemporary cocktails, along with premium whiskeys, fine wines, rare spirits, and champagnes. From tranquil early evenings to lively late nights, the bar provides a unique and refined atmosphere.

### St. Regis Astor Terrace Bar

St. Regis Astor Terrace Bar highlights the beauty and tranquility of the outdoor rooftop terrace, offering a curated selection of vintage wines, champagnes, premium beverages, and signature cocktails.
