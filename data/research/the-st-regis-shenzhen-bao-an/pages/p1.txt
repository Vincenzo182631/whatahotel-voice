# Welcome to The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an

The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao’an enjoys a prime location in the heart of the thriving Bao’an District. Featuring 289 elegantly appointed guest rooms and suites, each offering breathtaking views of the city skyline or the bay, the hotel promises a refined and unforgettable stay. Guests can indulge in a variety of dining experiences across four exquisite restaurants and bars, each offering a harmonious blend of St. Regis’s signature elegance and the vibrant flavors of local cuisine. The hotel also boasts over 1,000 square meters of versatile event space, including the Astor Ballroom and several meeting rooms, making it the ideal destination for both business and leisure. With personalized butler service, a state-of-the-art Fitness Center, and a luxurious spa, The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao’an redefines the standard of luxury in Shenzhen, offering an exceptional fusion of elegance, service, and innovation.

## Versatile Banquet Spaces for Customizable Events

Total of I,063 sqm of meeting space, ideal for hosting conferences, gala dinners, and various social and corporate events. The 696 sqm pillar-less Astor Ballroom can be divided into 2 mid-sized function rooms, each equipped with 3 built-in projectors and screens. A gorgeous foyer area is connected to the Astor Ballroom, providing a luxurious atmosphere for guests to enjoy tea breaks.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an. Self parking is available at The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an, with rates of ¥15.00 per hour, ¥35.00 per day, ¥245.00 per week, and ¥15.00 perstay.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an is Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport (SZX). SZX is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Shenzhen Bao'an does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
