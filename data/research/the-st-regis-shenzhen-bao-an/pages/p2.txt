# Rooms & Suites

## Accommodations

The 289 hotel rooms and suites shine with a uniquely stylish and comfortable aesthetic. Starting from 50 sqm, each room offers spacious layouts, thoughtfully designed to provide both comfort and sophistication. The rooms feature dark oak parquet, dusty grey veneer, and Carrara marble, with highlights like oriental-inspired timber screens, bronze and glass wall sconces, and intricate marquetry on the headboard, reflecting the brand's personalized spirit.
