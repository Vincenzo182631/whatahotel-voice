## Signature Creations

Discover Vault Bar’s signature creations, thoughtfully crafted to reflect the bar’s distinctive character and spirit. Beyond the menu, our bartenders can create bespoke cocktails, tailoring each drink to your preferences, mood and palate for a truly personal experience.

## Tasting Series

Discover a series of intimate tasting experiences hosted in Vault Bar’s private space, where exceptional spirits and luxury brands take centre stage. Each gathering is thoughtfully curated to introduce guests to distinctive flavours, rare expressions and the stories behind some of the world’s most renowned names.
