### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino?

The check-in time at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does MGM Grand Hotel & Casino allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 100.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino?

On-site parking is available at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino. Self parking is available for $20.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $40.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

What is the closest airport near MGM Grand Hotel & Casino?

The closest airport near MGM Grand Hotel & Casino is Harry Reid International Airport (LAS). LAS is located approximately 2.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does MGM Grand Hotel & Casino have an airport shuttle to Harry Reid International Airport (LAS)?

No, MGM Grand Hotel & Casino does not have an airport shuttle to Harry Reid International Airport.

Does MGM Grand Hotel & Casino have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, MGM Grand Hotel & Casino has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
