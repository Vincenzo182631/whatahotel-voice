### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino have a work space?

No, the rooms at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino?

The largest occupancy room at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino is the Skylofts with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino?

Some rooms at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino have fridges?

No, rooms at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at MGM Grand Hotel & Casino have hair dryers.
