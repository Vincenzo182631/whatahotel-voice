FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Where is Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel?**

Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel is located on Old Ursula Road in Victoria, 15 minutes from Victoria Falls on the Zimbabwe side.

**How do I get to Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel?**

Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel is a 20-minute drive from Victoria Falls Airport, and transfers can be arranged from the two nearby airports.

**Does Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel offer airport transfers?**

Yes, Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel offers airport transfers for a fee and with advance booking.

**Does Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel offer parking?**

Yes, Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel offers limited parking for hotel guests.

**What leisure facilities are there at Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel?**

Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel has an outdoor swimming pool.

**What dining options are there at Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel?**

Experience the pinnacle of refinement with Gin Tasting, a Chef's Table, Wine Pairing, Afternoon Tea, and a selection of exquisite local and international dishes at 1871 Restaurant. There is also in-room dining available at Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel.

**What are the check-in and check-out times at Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel?**

Check-in starts at 2:00 pm and check-out is up to 10:30 am on your departure date at Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel.

**Are there wheelchair-accessible rooms at Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel?**

Yes, Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel has wheelchair-accessible rooms.

**Is Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel family friendly?**

Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel welcomes families, with family accommodation and kids’ activities. However, we ask that you assist us in maintaining a peaceful environment for the enjoyment of all our guests.

**Are pets allowed at Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel?**

Anantara Stanley & Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel is not pet-friendly.
