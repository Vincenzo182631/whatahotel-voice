# A Celebration of Land and Sea

## Dining

At TA’AKTANA, A Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Labuan Bajo, every meal is a voyage through time, culture, and the rich bounty of the island. Inspired by Flores’ Land Dwellers and Sea Nomads, our restaurants...See More and bars honor the traditions of those who have long thrived on this land – farmers who cultivate the verdant highlands and seafarers who navigate the endless blue. With an unwavering commitment to sustainability and authenticity, our chefs work in harmony with local fishermen, organic farmers, and artisans to present culinary experiences that reflect the true essence of the island. From the freshest seafood, responsibly sourced to preserve marine ecosystems, to vibrant produce handpicked from Kebunku, our on-site chef’s garden, every ingredient tells a story. Here, dining is more than sustenance – it is an invitation to savor tradition, revel in discovery, and indulge in a feast for the senses.See Less

### Epicurean Moments

Inspired by the Land Dwellers and Sea Nomads of Flores, our curated Epicurean Moments honor the region’s rich culinary heritage, blending age-old traditions with modern refinement. From the golden glow of sunset rituals to hands-on encounters with Indonesia’s rarest spirit or setting sail on an epicurean voyage, every experience is designed to immerse guests in the flavors,stories and craftsmanship...See More that define Labuan Bajo.See Less

### Epicurean Daily – Sunset Ritual

There is a moment in Labuan Bajo where time stands still - the instant the sun melts into the horizon, casting the sky in radiant shades and streaks of gold, blush, and violet. At Nera Lounge, Perched at the heart of the resort, we invite guests to savor this fleeting beauty through our Sunset Ritual.

### Epicurean Weekly – Sopi Masterclass

Traditional spirit crafted from the Arenga pinnata palm tree – more than just a drink, it is a symbol of heritage, resilience, and the enduring bonds of the Flores people. Rooted in celebrations, sopi embodies the essence of shared moments and the deep connection between land and culture. Honoring this tradition, our Sopi Masterclass invites guests on a journey into the heart of Indonesia’s storied libation.

### Epicurean Journey – Fishing & Cooking Class

For the people from the Bajo tribe, the act of fishing is for more than mere survival – it is a sacred tradition, passed down through generations. At TA’AKTANA, we invite guests to experience this profound connection to the sea through our Fishing & Cooking Class – a journey that begins at sunrise and ends in a feast.
