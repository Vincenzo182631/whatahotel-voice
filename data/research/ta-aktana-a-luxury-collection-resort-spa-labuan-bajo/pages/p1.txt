## Embrace style and heritage at our Labuan Bajo luxury resort

For those who yearn to not just discover Labuan Bajo but embrace it, dive beneath the surface and explore like a local at TA'AKTANA, a Luxury Collection Resort and Spa, Labuan Bajo. Every aspect of our...See More hotel pays homage to the traditions and culture of the extraordinary island of Flores. Where Legends of Land and Sea Converge

### A Gateway to Flores’ Untold Stories

Once a tranquil fishing village, Labuan Bajo has evolved into an enchanting haven for explorers, where the echoes of seafaring nomads and indigenous storytellers still linger. This was a land of trade and tradition, where the Bajo people, revered as the "Sea Nomads," once traversed the tides, harvesting pearls that graced the palaces of Europe. Alongside them, the Manggarai people, descendants of distant...See More lands, cultivated the earth with their ancient agrarian wisdom, shaping the region’s iconic spider web rice fields.
Today, Labuan Bajo is a place where past and present exist in perfect harmony – a vibrant port town that beckons modern adventurers to dive into crystal-clear waters, trek mist-veiled hills, and fall in love with the soul-stirring magic of Flores’ untouched beauty.See Less

Ta’Aktana Traditions

### Sunset Ritual

At Nera Lounge, time pauses as the sun sinks into Labuan Bajo’s horizon, painting the sky in gold, blush, and violet. Celebrate this fleeting beauty with our Sunset Ritual—a complimentary cocktail infused with local botanicals, reflecting the twilight glow. Honoring tradition, our signature creation features sopi, Manggarai’s artisanal spirit, symbolizing community and connection.

### A Butler for Every Journey

At TA’AKTANA, luxury is personalized, intuitive, and seamless. Every villa is accompanied by a 24/7 Butler service, ensuring that each guest’s journey is uniquely their own. From arranging private sunset cruises to curating bespoke culinary experiences, our Butlers are more than attendants – they are storytellers, experience curators, and the guardians of unforgettable moments.

### A Lantern To Light Your Way

As the sun dips below the horizon, guests will find a handcrafted traditional lantern in their suite or villa – a symbol of the Manggarai people, once used to guide villagers through the night. At TA’AKTANA, it serves as a reminder of the resort’s mission: to illuminate the beauty, heritage, and untapped potential of Labuan Bajo.
