Island-Hopping

## The Allure of the Flores Archipelago

Set sail into the legendary waters of Komodo National Park amidst emerald peaks and azure seas, where encounters with the mythical Komodo dragon in their natural habitat make for stories that last a lifetime. Trek to the iconic viewpoint of Padar Island, unwind on the pink-hued sands of Pink Beach, and dive into the crystal-clear waters of Taka Makassar and Siaba, a sandbar paradise surrounded by vibrant coral reefs and untouched marine sanctuaries.

Beyond the famed destinations, venture to Tatawa, Manjarite, and Gili Lawa Laut, where serene waters, rich biodiversity, and unspoiled beaches offer moments of absolute seclusion.

For an elevated island experience, embark on a private yacht adventure, where a dedicated butler, personalized itinerary, and a gourmet beachside picnic transform island-hopping into an otherworldly luxury voyage.

Land-Tour

## A Journey Through Culture & Time

Step beyond the resort and into the heart of Manggarai heritage, where centuries-old traditions, natural wonders, and the warmth of local hospitality come to life. Wander through a traditional Manggarai settlement and visit Melo Village, where conical thatched houses stand as a testament to time-honored craftsmanship. Witness the Caci Whip Dance, a sacred ritual symbolizing strength and unity. Observe master artisans as they weave intricate patterns into hand-dyed fabrics, each motif a visual story of ancestry, belief, and cultural identity. At Batu Cermin Cave, sunlight filters through ancient rock formations, casting a mirror-like reflection on the cavern walls.

Wellness

### DI’a Spa

Step into Di’A Spa, where the ancient beauty of Flores meets the art of modern well-being. Inspired by the sacred caves of Rangko and Batu Cermin, this tranquil retreat cocoons guests in a world of natural stillness, where time melts away and deep renewal begins. The spa’s design echoes the formations of stalactites, stalagmites, and coral-laced caverns, with walls that shimmer in tones of turquoise and stone, mirroring the crystal-clear waters of Labuan Bajo’s hidden grottoes.

Signature Treatment

### Hyggee Harmony Hair Spa Treatment

A ritual of scent, art, and relaxation, inspired by Korean wellness traditions. Incorporating time-honored Korean hair therapy, this multi-step scalp and hair treatment fuses ancient botanicals and modern innovation to nourish the scalp, strengthen hair, and soothe the senses, leaving hair soft, fragrant, and rejuvenated.

### DI’a Ritual for Two

Escape into blissful serenity with our ‘Journey for Two’ spa experience in a private suite. Begin with a tranquil foot cleansing ritual, a luxurious Labuan Bajo coffee and brown sugar exfoliation, and a Cooling Marine Algae wrap. Enjoy a soothing whirlpool bath, tailored massage with custom oils, and conclude with a revitalizing Niance express facial.

### Loke Ngerrang

Inspired by Nggerang’s radiant beauty, this spa package offers indulgence and rejuvenation through Niance anti-aging treatments that boost collagen, combat cellulite, and restore firmness. Enjoy a soothing foot ritual, lulur scrub, warm oil massage, Niance facial, and a refreshing hair wash and mask for total renewal.
