## Sunset Ritual

At Nera Lounge, time pauses as the sun sinks into Labuan Bajo’s horizon, painting the sky in gold, blush, and violet. Celebrate this fleeting beauty with our Sunset Ritual—a complimentary cocktail infused with local botanicals, reflecting the twilight glow. Honoring tradition, our signature creation features sopi, Manggarai’s artisanal spirit, symbolizing community and connection.

## A Butler for Every Journey

At TA’AKTANA, luxury is personalized, intuitive, and seamless. Every villa is accompanied by a 24/7 Butler service, ensuring that each guest’s journey is uniquely their own. From arranging private sunset cruises to curating bespoke culinary experiences, our Butlers are more than attendants – they are storytellers, experience curators, and the guardians of unforgettable moments.
