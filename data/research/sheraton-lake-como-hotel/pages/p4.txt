# Where to Eat in Lake Como

## Dining

Welcome to our Lake Como restaurants. Sheraton Lake Como offers a delightful culinary experience with two inviting Lake Como bars and three distinct restaurants. For an unforgettable dining experience,...See More indulge in gourmet cuisine at our intimate Michelin-star restaurant in Lake Como, Kitchen, which opens onto the park. For a modern twist on Italy’s renowned flavours, visit Gusto Restaurant. The al fresco Kincho Restaurant & Garden, offers an all-day dining experience in a seasonal, park-side setting. Finally, unwind at Fresco Bar with a cocktail or a glass of wine, completing your Lake Como culinary journey.See Less
