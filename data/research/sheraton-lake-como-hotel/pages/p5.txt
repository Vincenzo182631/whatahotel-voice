## The Spa

An oasis of more than 700 sqm dedicated to wellbeing with treatment rooms, wet area and fitness studio. All our hotel guests have complimentary daily access, while external guests are allowed with a daily entrance fee. For all guests, reservation is mandatory and subject to availability. For kid’s and children policy please refer to the sections at the end of this page.

## Membership and Daily Entrance Fee

Our SPA is a pure haven of relaxation and tranquillity open to both hotel and not hotel guests. Hotel guests have complimentary access to the SPA and its facilities, while external guests may have access to the humid area (for 90 minutes) and the fitness studio (for unlimited time) at a daily entrance fee of 55 € per person. Included into the experience: bathrobe, slippers and a personal locker. Rituals are not included. For those ones want to have a more exclusive entry conditions or just want to have access to the fitness studio, other options are available starting from 200 € per month. Advance reservation is compulsory and it is subject to availability. Free parking and free wifi are always included.

To reserve or want more info mail at spa@sheratonlakecomo.com or call the spa at +39 031 516700

## Wet Area

A wet area is available to all guests with a 12-metre indoor swimming pool plus an hydromassage tub overlooking the greenery; sauna, Mediterranean bath, emotional showers, ice waterfall and relaxation room will complete the area.

## Indoor Pool

The indoor pool surrounded by lush greenery, invite guests to immerse themselves in the soothing waters while soaking in panoramic views of the surrounding natural beauty. Adjacent to the pool lies the hydropool, a therapeutic oasis designed to melt away tension and stress. Guests can indulge in the gentle massage of hydrotherapy jets as they surrender to a state of blissful relaxation.

## Relax Area

Unwind in our relaxation lounge and enjoy your favourite herbal tea. After your wellness ritual or moments spent in the wet zone, take the time to savor the tranquility and refresh your senses.

## Fitness Studio

Open 24/24, 7 days per week, our fitness studio is located next to the lobby in the SPA area and it’s equipped with the latest Technogym machines. The access is complimentary for all hotel guests, external guests can access to the fitness studio upon availability and reservation at a daily entrance fee including also the access to the humid area of the SPA.

## Private Suite Experience

The Private Suite Experience is your ultimate wellbeing retreat. Indulge in a couple's massage and exclusive use of the wet area in total privacy after the closing hour paired with fine bubbles and gourmet treats. The experience is fully customizable and it is available by reservation for both hotel and external guests. For inquiries, email us.

## INSÍUM

The rituals and all the facial and body treatments of the new Sheraton Lake Como SPA will be developed in partnership with the Italian skincare brand INSÌUM. INSÍUM is a Clean, Cruelty Free and Vegan skincare line that uses scientifically advanced biotechnological ingredients to ensure anti-aging results. The word INSÍUM comes etymologically from the Latin "in somnum", during sleep and in ancient Friulian it means "dream". The Brand was born from the idea of reconstructing an inviolable escape zone, a moment of physical and mental regeneration dedicated only to ourselves, in which every element is pure, simple, truly effective as well as profoundly pleasant. A moment in which we take care of something we often forget: our well-being.

## INSÍUM

This line provides a unique vision of beauty in which effectiveness and ethics are inseparable. Effective anti-aging cosmetics thanks to special high-tech active ingredients, which manage to transform the beauty routine into a moment of physical and mental regeneration. Sustainability and clean formulations represent INSÌUM's commitment to skin health and the future of the planet. In fact, only substances that the skin can accept and the environment can dispose of are used in INSÌUM cosmetics. The vegan and cruelty free vision has allowed INSÌUM to obtain PETA (People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals) certification, the most important American organization in the defense of animals. This vision extends to "airless" packaging in which technological innovation and a "green" soul are fused by the inspiration coming from important contemporary artists such as Jeff Koons.

Access

The Spa is located adjacent to the hotel lobby. Complimentary access is granted to hotel guests, while external visitors may enter subject to a fee.

All shared spaces, including the wet area, the pool and the fitness studio, are designed for independent use and are not supervised.

Exclusively from October to March, minors accompanied by an adult are permitted access daily until noon. From noon until closing, access to the Spa remains strictly reserved for adults.

Advance reservation is mandatory for all guests and subject to availability. The access to the wet area is limited to 90 minutes per entry.

Children’s policy

Access to the Spa is reserved for guests aged 12 and over.

Guests between 12 and 18 years old must be accompanied by an adult at all times. Children under 12 are normally not admitted.

However, from October to March, children accompanied by an adult are exceptionally welcome from opening time until noon. After noon, the Spa remains exclusively dedicated to adults, ensuring a serene and relaxing atmosphere.

Spa treatments remain available for guests of all ages.

Reservations

To make a reservation, please contact the Spa at +39 031 516700 write at spa@sheratonlakecomo.com or contact us via whatsapp message (no whatsapp call allowed by our system) at +39 388 862 2103

We recommend reserving in advance to make sure your preferred time and treatment will be available

To access the wet area, reservation is mandatory. Staying in the wet area is permitted for a maximum of 90 minutes per entry.

Cancellation policy

Please be aware that you will be charged in full if you do not cancel your reservation 24 hours ahead of your scheduled treatment time

Health conditions

Please advise us of any health conditions, allergies or injuries which could affect your choice of treatment when making your spa reservation.
