# Sheraton Lake Como is Nested Amidst the Wonders of Lake Como

## Experiences

Near the Rhaetian Alps and lush hillsides, Lake Como is an iconic destination in Northern Italy. Its sapphire waters are surrounded by flourishing gardens, magnificent villas and charming villages. Our...See More hotel near Lake Como is only a few steps from the boat pier.See Less

### Swimming Pool With Whirlpool Area and Solarium

Unwind during your holiday at our Lake Como swimming pool, or relax in the heated whirlpool area. Nested in our private park, the pool is a blue gem (the biggest in Como). It’s open only in Spring and Summer, and can also be used by our little guests.

## CASABIANCA Modern Art Collection

CASABIANCA is a singular destination for modern and contemporary art in the heart of Como, open to the public. Rare masterpieces from the 20th and 21st centuries are housed within a magnificent villa, conceived as a place where art, architecture, and time exist in refined harmony—an experience offered to the city and to the world. Guests are also invited to enjoy the culinary excellence of Cova Casabianca, the café and bistro by the iconic Cova of Milan. For further information and to arrange your visit, our concierge will be pleased to assist you.

Discover More

## A Smarter Way To Explore Lake Como

Discover the beauty of Lake Como with Freely electric scooters and enjoy a convenient, eco-friendly way to explore the destination. Ride through scenic streets, reach hidden gems, and reduce your environmental impact while travelling in comfort. Renting is quick and easy through the Freely app, where you can book and pay in just a few taps. Should you need any assistance or local recommendations, our Concierge team will be delighted to help you make the most of your sustainable journey.

## Admire Lake Como from an Enchanting Perspective

Discover Lake Como in its most iconic way: a private traditional boat tour. Glide across shimmering waters, admire timeless villages and grand villas from the lake, and indulge in a perfect blend of relaxation and effortless glamour.

## An Unforgettable Experience Aboard a Hot Air Balloon

Experience Lake Como from a breathtaking new perspective with a hot air balloon tour. An unusual and incredibly romantic way to admire its villas, mountains and sparkling waters from above, suspended in silence between sky and lake.

### Explore Our Rich History

Como invites you to explore its timeless charm, starting from the majestic Como Cathedral and wandering through the ancient walled city steeped in history.

### Tour In Style

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sheraton Lake Como Hotel have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Sheraton Lake Como Hotel offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Sheraton Lake Como Hotel have a pool on-site?

Yes, Sheraton Lake Como Hotel has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Indoor Pool (Indoor)Outdoor Pool - Complimentary (Outdoor)Indoor Whirlpool Pool (Indoor)Heated jacuzzi pool - Complimentary (Outdoor)Whirlpool

Does Sheraton Lake Como Hotel have an on-site spa?

Yes, SPA is located on-site at Sheraton Lake Como Hotel. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Sheraton Lake Como Hotel?

Villa Olmo, Villa del Balbianello, Villa Carlotta, Basilica di Sant'Abbondio, Teatro Sociale di Como and more are nearby Sheraton Lake Como Hotel.
