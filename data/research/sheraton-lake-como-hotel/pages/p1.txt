# Welcome to Sheraton Lake Como Hotel

## Enjoy your favorite luxury Lake Como family resort

On the iconic Lake Como, the Sheraton Lake Como hotel offers 135 comfortable guestrooms and suites. If you're looking for a family-friendly hotel in Lake Como, we've got you covered with our family solutions....See More All rooms are decorated with Made in Italy furniture, free Wi-Fi and Sheraton signature beds. This unique luxury Lake Como resort is nested in an enchanting private park with swimming pool, solarium and whirlpool area. A brand new SPA with indoor pool and fitness studio complete the facilities. Enjoy endless dining opportunities choosing from the gourmet experience at a 1 Michelin-starred Kitchen restaurant, the traditional Italian delicacies at the Gusto restaurant and the all-day-dining experience at Kincho, by the pool. Relax and unwind with a few cocktails, made by one of our talented bartenders at our two bars: the Fresco bar inside the hotel and Kincho bar. Plan your next wedding or private event in Lake Como thanks to Spazio Como Auditorium or the internal event spaces.See Less

## The SPA

A haven of over 700 sqm dedicated to wellbeing with treatment rooms, nail studio and a wet area equipped with an indoor swimming pool, hydropool, sauna, Mediterranean bath, emotional showers and ice waterfall. The entrance is complimentary for all hotel guests. External guests are welcomed at a daily fee. For both reservation is compulsory and subject to availability.

## Swimming Pool and Pool Club KK

Nested in our park, our guests can enjoy one of the biggest private pools in the area with jacuzzi-heated bath and solarium. The pool is open during the mild season in Spring and Summer. During autumn and winter at guests disposal there’s the adults-only indoor pool of our SPA.

### An Enchanting Private Park

Enjoy the great outdoors in a lush green park of over 11,000 m², where you can sunbathe, go jogging, organize a lunch party, or even a picnic on the grass.

### Discover Lake Como with Our Electric Mopeds!

Rent now one of our electric mopeds to discover Lake Como in a green way. Ask our concierge for more info and how to rent them.

### We Love Pets!

We are proud to be pet friendly and welcome all your little four-legged friends. Please notify us at the time of reservation and we will be happy to provide in your room a comfortable dog mat and a set of bowls for food and water. And for the sweet tooth: a special pet menu! Do not miss our rooms with terrace to allow your little friend to enjoy outside the nature of Lake Como!

## Our Healthy Corner: "Le Luci del Kitchen" bio vegetable garden

Our chef Andrea created in a corner of our park a bio vegetable garden to growth herbs and vegetables for his creations. We called it "Le Luci del Kitchen" thanks to the wonderful lights created by our designer. It will be your favourite corner to relax and enjoy in the green a glass or wine or an aperitivo. Available at Kitchen Restaurant!

## Our Commitment for Gender Neutrality

Sheraton Lake Como is among the first hotels in Italy to obtain the certification established by the decree of the Presidency of the Council of Ministers 125/2022 which certifies the adoption of measures to guarantee gender equality in the workplace.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Lake Como Hotel?

The check-in time at Sheraton Lake Como Hotel is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Lake Como Hotel allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Sheraton Lake Como Hotel. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Lake Como Hotel?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Lake Como Hotel. The daily self parking fee is €25.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Lake Como Hotel have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Lake Como Hotel has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Lake Como Hotel booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Lake Como Hotel?

The closest airport near Sheraton Lake Como Hotel is Milano Malpensa Airport (MXP). MXP is located approximately 55.4 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Lake Como Hotel have an airport shuttle to Linate Airport (LIN)?

Yes, Sheraton Lake Como Hotel has an airport shuttle to Linate Airport (LIN).

Does Sheraton Lake Como Hotel have an airport shuttle to Milano Malpensa Airport (MXP)?

Yes, Sheraton Lake Como Hotel has an airport shuttle to Milano Malpensa Airport (MXP).

Does Sheraton Lake Como Hotel have an airport shuttle to Orio al Serio International Airport (BGY)?

Yes, Sheraton Lake Como Hotel has an airport shuttle to Orio al Serio International Airport (BGY).

Does Sheraton Lake Como Hotel have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Lake Como Hotel has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
