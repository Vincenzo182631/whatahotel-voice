# Welcome to The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou

## Experience modern hotel luxury in Hangzhou, China

The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou is perched on the bank of the Qiantang River, overlooking tidal bore. Our hotel provides unfettered access to the vibrant heart of Hangzhou, China's...See More Central Business District, and is near Six Harmonies Pagoda, the West Lake, Eight Diagrams Field and more. A captivating world within a layered urban landscape, the hotel presents an indoor pool, a tranquil spa and a fitness center, as well as two restaurants featuring river views and epicurean offerings. A majestic grand ballroom and seven spacious meeting rooms ensure indelible events. Our 205 guest rooms including 20 suites provide elevated comforts and luxury furnishings, set against a breathtaking backdrop, which includes venerable views of the Qiantang River through large floor-to-ceiling windows. Discerning service and premier technology ensure a truly distinguished hotel stay.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou?

The check-in time at The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou.

What are the parking options at The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou?

On-site self parking is available at The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou booking.

What is the closest airport near The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou?

The closest airport near The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou is Hangzhou Xiaoshan International Airport (HGH). HGH is located approximately 25.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Azure Qiantang, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Hangzhou does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
