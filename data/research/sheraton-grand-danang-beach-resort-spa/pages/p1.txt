# Welcome to Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa

## Retreat by the Longest Infinity Pool in Danang

Perched on tranquil Non-Nuoc Beach, Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & SPA offers a transformative experience. Find our luxury beachfront hotel in Danang, Vietnam, just a short drive from Danang International...View More Airport, near attractions like Hoi An Ancient Town and the Marble Mountains. Dine at the outstanding restaurants within the resort, including The Grill, our signature steak and seafood restaurant. Take a dip in our 250m infinity pool as you gaze at the sea or challenge yourself in our on-site fitness center before easing your tired muscles with a spa treatment. When it comes to entertaining the kids, families love our kids club, playground, and mini-water park. If you're planning an event, our luxury Danang hotel offers a range of expansive, adaptable venues and outdoor spaces. Embrace a seamless blend of modern luxury and natural beauty in our luxury Danang beach resort’s spacious rooms and suites with smart TVs, complimentary Wi-Fi, marble bathrooms, and private balconies with a view.View Less

## Golfer Destination

Sheraton Grand Danang Resort is a top choice for a golf vacation in Danang. Our beach resort is near Legend Danang Golf Resort, which features a 36-hole golf course designed by golf legend Greg Norman & Jack Nicklaus, offering stunning ocean views and a challenging layout. In addition to convenient location for golf, the resort provides luxurious accommodations, dining, spa, and the longest 250m swimming pool in Danang.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa is Da Nang International Airport (DAD). DAD is located approximately 14.3 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Sheraton Grand Danang Beach Resort & Spa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
