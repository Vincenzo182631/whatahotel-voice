### More About La Plage

La Plage in Danang offers fresh seafood in a contemporary beachfront setting. Kick back and relax on one of the daybeds overlooking the endless ocean, treat your taste buds to traditional local fare at our alfresco deck, or simply sip ice-cold drinks, including a wide selection of cocktails at our cool cocktail bar.

Sublime Seafront Delicacies

La Plage showcases an amazing array of seafood from the spectacular central coast of Vietnam. Our highly-skilled chef handpicks the finest ingredients from local fishing villages, ranging from freshly-caught fish and sumptuous shellfish to beautiful blue crabs and luxurious lobsters, then combine them with local herbs to add some zip and zing to your beachfront feast.

Beachfront Indulgence

If you’re seeking a tropical retreat to savor authentic local flavors and sip sundown cocktails, La Plage is a perfect choice. Whether you choose to unwind on the elegant outdoor terrace, overlooking the turquoise sea, on the beach-chic upper deck, or in our intimate beach bar in Danang, this stylish venue promises exceptional experiences.

Creative Cocktails & Light Bites

Savor an extensive selection of creative cocktails, all crafted with passion and flair by our talented mixologists. Whether you order a timeless classic or opt for our signature concoctions, featuring local ingredients, our cocktails are the perfect accompaniment to your evening. Enjoy them alongside comforting cuisines such as pizzas, burgers, or banh mi at our beach bar in Danang.

## Sublime Seafood

At our seafood restaurant in Danang, our chefs source the freshest ingredients from local fishermen, including premium fish, succulent shellfish, and exquisite lobsters from Nha Trang. Combined with locally grown herbs and vegetables, these ocean-fresh ingredients are crafted into exceptional dishes. Savor a wide selection of ocean-fresh delicacies.

## Prime Cuts

Diners can choose from an extensive range of 100% premium cuts, imported from the world's top beef-producing nations including Australia, Japan, and the USA at this restaurant in Danang. Discover contemporary international cuisine including fresh local seafood, prime steaks and an extensive wine list, all in a stylish setting with a terrace overlooking the infinity pool. The open kitchen creates a vibrant atmosphere.
