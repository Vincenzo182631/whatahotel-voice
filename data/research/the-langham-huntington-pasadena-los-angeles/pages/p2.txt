# Stay

The Langham Huntington, Pasadena has been welcoming visitors to southern California in a tradition of style and luxury for over 100 years. Experience refined rooms and stylish suites featuring unique offerings for an unforgettable stay. Guests of Club Executive Rooms and Suites enjoy complimentary access to The Langham Club, a serene retreat with personalized service.
