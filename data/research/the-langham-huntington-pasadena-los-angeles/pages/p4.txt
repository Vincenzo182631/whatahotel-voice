Rooms at this Pasadena landmark displaying Pueblo Mission Revival architecture are decorated with rich woods and furnished with chaise lounges; those on the ground floor have French doors opening onto patios. The Terrace serves contemporary California cuisine in a "poolside setting," while small dishes are the norm at The Bar. The 11,000-square-foot spa was renovated in 2010 and has treatments based on traditional Chinese medicine.
