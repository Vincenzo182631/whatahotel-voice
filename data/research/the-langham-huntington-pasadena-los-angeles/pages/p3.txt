# SPORTS AND ENTERTAINMENT

### ROSE BOWL STADIUM

1001 Rose Bowl Dr, Pasadena, CA 91103

Majestic and iconic, the Rose Bowl has hosted the legendary New Year’s Day game for nearly a century and is home to the UCLA Bruins. Take a tour to explore its history — or feel the excitement live at a game or concert under its famous arches.

CONTACT CONCIERGE

### PASADENA PLAYHOUSE

39 S El Molino Ave, Pasadena, CA 91101

Established as the official State Theatre of California, the Pasadena Playhouse is the perfect spot for anyone who wants an unforgettable theatrical experience. If you're a fan of Tony Award and Pulitzer Prize-winning plays, it's time to book your tickets.

CONTACT CONCIERGE

### BIG BUS TOURS LOS ANGELES

6763 Hollywood Blvd. Los Angeles, CA 90028

Enjoy open-top double-decker bus tours of LA's most iconic landmarks and celebrity homes from Hollywood to Santa Monica, with hop-on, hop-off privileges.

CONTACT CONCIERGE
