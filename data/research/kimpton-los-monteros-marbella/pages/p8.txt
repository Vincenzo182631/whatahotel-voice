# Kimpton Los Monteros MarbellaLocation

Marbella, located on Spain's Costa del Sol, is renowned for its warm climate, beaches, and luxurious lifestyle. The Los Monteros neighborhood, east of the city, features some of the most exclusive and opulent villas in the region. This area is known for its stunning beachfront properties and tranquility. Popular attractions include La Cabane beach club and Santa Clara Golf Course, offering a sophisticated and relaxed way of life.
Marbella's nightlife is vibrant and diverse, offering something for everyone. From upscale beach clubs like Nikki Beach to lively bars and nightclubs in Puerto Banús, the city comes alive after dark. The Old Town, or "Casco Antiguo," is particularly special with its charming narrow streets, historic buildings, and the picturesque Plaza de los Naranjos. Here, you can enjoy traditional tapas, boutique shopping, and a warm, lively atmosphere that captures the essence of Andalusian culture.

## Things to do

### La Cabane by Dolce and Gabbana

C. Jabalí, Urb. Los monteros, Marbella, Málaga 29603

La Cabane by Dolce & Gabbana in Marbella is a chic beach club located in the upscale Los Monteros area. With its stylish design and sophisticated vibe, it offers a luxurious experience right by the Mediterranean Sea. Known for high-end events, delicious food, and top-notch service, guests enjoy the infinity pool, Balinese beds, and stunning views. It's a favorite spot for the international jet set looking to relax and enjoy the good life.

Learn more

### Artola Dunes

Located on the Cabopino beach in Marbella, these are important Mediterranean coastal dune fields. This area is protected as a Natural Monument because of its high ecological value and biological diversity.

### Mijas Pueblo

Charming village on the Costa del Sol with typical Andalusian architecture with white houses adorned with flowers. This mountain village offers narrow cobbled streets, beautiful squares and viewpoints with panoramic views.

### Gibraltar

British Overseas Territory on the southern coast of Spain with wonderful views of North Africa. World famous for being one of the two mythical Pillars of Hercules.

### Caminito del Rey

Caminito del Rey - Acceso Norte, Ardales, Malaga 29550

Spectacular route of walkways and paths built at the beginning of the 20th century and restored in 2015. With a length of 7.7 km and heights of up to 100 metres, it offers breathtaking views and a picturesque scenery.

Learn more

### Ronda

A town northwest of Malaga that combines natural beauty, rich history and vibrant culture. Known for its breathtaking location above a deep defile called El Tajo.

### La Alhambra

C. Real de la Alhambra, s/n, Centro, 180, Granada, Granada 18009

Majestic palace and fortress of Islamic architecture located in Granada. Built during the 14th century, it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

### Sevilla

Capital of Andalusia. On the banks of the Guadalquivir River, it is a historic city, full of culture and art.
