## FAQs for Los Monteros Marbella

Does Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella have a pool?

Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella has 1 indoor and 1 outdoor pools.

INDOOR POOL AT MAISON CODAGE PARIS SPA

Start or end your day in our indoor pool at 33ºC, with hydro-massage beds and water cascades to release tension. Enjoy a relaxing bath and unwind during your holidays in Marbella.

OUTDOOR POOL AT COSTA CLUB AND ESCONDIDO ROOFTOP

Enjoy the main pool surrounded by gardens at Costa Club Pool Bar, with over 300 days of sunshine a year, hammocks, Bali beds, live music, and DJs.
At Escondido Rooftop, relax in the infinity pool with spectacular views of the Mediterranean.

Pool information

How much is parking at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella?

Complimentary parking is available at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella for hotel guests.

For your convenience, we offer Valet Parking at €25 per day. We recommend booking this service in advance to ensure availability.
Alternatively, guests can make use of self-parking options in the surrounding area.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella.

Pet policy description. As an animal loving team, we welcome any pet that can fit into our elevator. However, there is one exception: we cannot accommodate untamed animals or those that may be poisonous. Pet accommodation is free of charge.

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella has 4 restaurants.

Is there free breakfast available at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella?

Breakfast is free with your stay at the Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella.

When is breakfast at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella?

Breakfast at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella is available from 7:30 AM to 11:00 AM. On weekends, breakfast is available from 7:30 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella?

Check-in at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella is conveniently located near Malaga Airport-Costa de Sol (AGP), approximately 21.75 miles away (35 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella?

Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- La Cabane by Dolce and Gabbana - 0.06 miles away (0.1 kilometers)
- Artola Dunes - 4.97 miles away (8 kilometers)
- Mijas Pueblo - 22.37 miles away (36 kilometers)
- Gibraltar - 53.44 miles away (86 kilometers)
- Caminito del Rey - 47.22 miles away (76 kilometers)
- Ronda - 42.25 miles away (68 kilometers)
- La Alhambra - 130.49 miles away (210 kilometers)
- Sevilla - 162.18 miles away (261 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
