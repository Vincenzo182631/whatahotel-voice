## Azul Lounge Bar

A refreshed classic of Los Monteros, the perfect spot for coffee, juices, and light bites all day, followed by a glass of wine after dinner.

Azul Lounge Bar

### Azul Lounge Bar

Azul Lounge Bar is the perfect meeting point for locals and travelers alike; a space where Mediterranean style comes to life, from breakfast to the last drink. Shareable tapas, vermouths, and signature cocktails are served in a relaxed, authentic atmosphere. A renewed classic of Los Monteros with a contemporary soul that reflects the true spirit of Kimpton.

Bar Hours: 9:00 AM - 2:00 AM

## Restaurant + bar highlights

### La Cabane by Dolce&Gabbana

Experience culinary delights crafted by the renowned chef Dani García while relaxing in a space designed by Dolce & Gabbana. Enjoy private sunbeds and cabanas right on the beachfront.

### Rachel's Eco Love at Kimpton Los Monteros

Inspired by a healthy lifestyle, Rachel’s Eco Love lands at Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella with its unique wellness and sustainability concept. Colorful, flavor-packed dishes made with fresh, lovingly sourced ingredients. Here, we don’t count calories—we count colors.

### New Rooftop with Infinity Pool and Ocean Views

One of the highlights of Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella is Escondido, a new rooftop with an infinity pool and unparalleled sea views. With a California vibe and a hint of tequila and salt, it becomes the place to be in Marbella, where you can savour signature cocktails and delicious Mexican delicacies while watching the sunset.

Learn more

### Breakfast Buffet

Included in all room rates, our breakfast is a culinary experience by Michelin-starred Chef José Carlos García. Enjoy live cooking stations, artisan bread, premium cold cuts, fresh fruit, and a touch of champagne. Served daily at Jara Restaurant from 07:30 AM to 11:00 AM.
Non-hotel guests: €50/adult, €25/child (under 12).
