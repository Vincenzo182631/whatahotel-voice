### Could have been better

18 days ago

We had extremely noisy neighbours, and the room was very poorly soundproofed, making it impossible for us to get any sleep. The only compensation offered was chocolate, which was quite disappointing given the seriousness gof the situation.

As a Customer Experience Manager myself, I am particularly sensitive to how customer complaints are handled and how appropriate solutions are provided. Unfortunately, in our case, I felt that the situation was not taken seriously at all. I repeatedly had to return to reception to ask whether anything had been done to resolve the issue, rather than being proactively informed by the hotel team.

We were told that both the noise and the smell of cannabis were coming from the neighbouring room. However, even after the neighbours had left, we were still unable to get any rest. To make matters worse, we received an incorrect wake-up call at 5:30 a.m., which woke up the entire family.

As a result, we barely slept that night, and our experience was completely ruined. It is particularly disappointing because staying at your hotel was part of our holiday plans, and we had expected a much higher standard of comfort and customer care.

I believe that a situation like this deserved a more appropriate response, genuine concern for the guests affected, and a meaningful solution rather than simply offering chocolate. It is truly a pity that what should have been an enjoyable part of our holiday turned into such a frustrating experience
