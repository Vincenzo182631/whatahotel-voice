# Rooms

Timeless and tranquil, the guest rooms at The Lake Como EDITION feature European white oak floors, ivory plaster walls, and Calacatta Turquoise marble accents. Designed for restorative stays, each room includes plush Italian linens, custom furnishings, and spa-like marble bathrooms with fluted glass detailing and Le Labo amenities. Select rooms offer private balconies with lake views.

# Suites

Blending space and sophistication, the suites offer a heightened sense of comfort with bespoke furniture by Neri & Hu, custom wool carpets, and tailored white oak millwork. Thoughtful layouts invite relaxation, while select suites feature expansive terraces and panoramic views. Bathrooms elevate the experience with double vanities and enhanced amenities.

# Penthouses

Designed for unforgettable stays, the EDITION penthouses are contemporary lakefront residences in spirit. With generous indoor-outdoor living areas, curated lounge and dining spaces, and private landscaped terraces, they provide an unparalleled vantage point over Lake Como. Deep soaking tubs, fluted glass showers, and exclusive Calacatta marble finishes bring spa luxury to the everyday.
