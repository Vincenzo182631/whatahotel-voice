# Lobby Bar

**Lobby Bar**

Aperitivo unfolds beneath five-meter vaulted ceilings and celadon marble elegance. With curated cocktails and Italian bites served against a backdrop of rose plaster and modern art, the Lobby Bar channels the spirit of a contemporary Italian salon.

For additional information please contact us at info.como@editionhotels.com.

**Cetino by Mauro Colagreco**

RESERVE VIEW WEBSITE

Cetino is Mauro Colagreco’s first restaurant in Italy, a landmark opening that redefines expectations on Lake Como. Rooted in the region’s natural terroir and shaped by Colagreco’s role as UNESCO Ambassador for Biodiversity, Cetino brings circular gastronomy to life with hyper-seasonal ingredients, local producers, and a deep respect for nature’s rhythm. But this is more than refined dining, it’s a new kind of luxury. Convivial, emotional, and effortlessly refined, Cetino blends world-class cuisine with cocktails, connection, and a spirit of generosity that invites guests to truly feel the place.

For additional information please contact us at cetino.como@editionhotels.com

**Renzo**

RESERVE VISIT WEBSITE

An all-day dining destination inspired by the warmth of Italian family gatherings. Renzo offers a relaxed yet refined atmosphere, where seasonal dishes are designed for sharing. The space opens onto a pergola-shaded terrace framed by Mediterranean herbs, citrus trees, and lake views bringing a rustic Italian garden feel to the table. Open from morning through night, Renzo moves with the rhythm of the day: Nourishing breakfasts, leisurely brunches, long lunches, and slow aperitivos. It’s a place where contemporary Italian cuisine meets the understated sophistication of EDITION, effortless, inviting, and quietly extraordinary.

For additional information please contact us at renzo.como@editionhotels.com

**The Pool**

A relaxed yet elevated take on poolside dining, where refined comfort meets bold simplicity. Mediterranean at heart, the menu plays with bright global accents and a spirit of effortless indulgence. High-quality, locally sourced ingredients, designed for sharing, grazing, and long afternoons by the water. Expect fresh flavours, salads, pizza and dishes made to be passed around the table with ease. As the light softens, the mood shifts naturally into aperitivo hour: sun-drenched spritzes, salty bites, and lakeside lounging with an understated glow.

For additional information please contact us at thepool.como@editionhotels.com
