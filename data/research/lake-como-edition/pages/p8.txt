# The Longevity Spa at The Lake Como EDITION

**The Longevity Spa at The Lake Como EDITION**

View Details

Share

Powered by The Longevity Spa, the spa is the first of its kind on Lake Como, offering a science-based approach to wellness rooted in diagnostics, holistic therapies, and biohacking. Guests begin with an expert-led assessment, measuring sleep quality, inflammation, oxidative stress, and more to build a personalized longevity journey.

The spa features 7 treatment rooms, an indoor vitality pool with floor-to-ceiling lake views, a steam bath, herbal sauna, a plunge pool, and outdoor relaxation terraces surrounded by Mediterranean flora. The full experience is designed to blur the line between body care and emotional reset.

SPA FACILITIES9:00 AM – 8:00 PM

TREATMENTS9:30 AM – 7:30 PM

For additional information please contact us at +39 0344 490588 or spa.como@editionhotels.com.

Please be informed that minimum age for spa access is 18 years.

**Gym**

An expansive 150 sqm fitness studio designed to reflect the energy of the lake and mountains. Outfitted with state- of-the-art Technogym equipment, the space supports strength training, cardio, and functional fitness routines. The light-filled interior features natural oak floors and mirrored walls, creating a motivating, modern environment for movement.

Personal trainer, Yoga and Pilates classes upon request.

OPEN 24 HOURS
