# Getting Here

- From Como San Giovanni Station
- From Lugano Airport (LUG)
- From Bergamo Airport (BGY)
- From Turin Airport (TRN)
- From Milan Linate Airport (LIN)
- From Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP)

## From Como San Giovanni Station

## Private Transfer:

Contact the hotel to have your private transfer organized

## Taxi:

Taxis are available directly outside the station.

For a seamless experience, a private transfer can also be arranged upon request.

Travel time: Approximately 45 minutes

Approximate cost: €80-€150

## Driving:

From the station, take Via Regina Teodolinda to Viale Franklin Delano Roosevelt, then continue on SS340 to Via Regina/Via Statale in Griante.

Travel time: Approximately 45 minutes, depending on traffic.

## From Lugano Airport (LUG)

## Private Transfer:

Contact the hotel to have your private transfer organized

## Taxi:

Taxis are available outside the terminal. We recommend to book in advance or reserve a ride. The journey includes an international border crossing Switzerland and Italy (tolls and luggage fees may apply).

Travel time: Approximately 40 minutes, depending on traffic

Approximate cost: € 120–150 (CHF 120–150)

## Train:

If traveling light, public transport may be a suitable alternative.

From Lugano Airport, you take a 10 minute walk to the train station of Serocca.

Upon arrival at Como San Giovanni station, we recommend continuing by taxi or rental car.

For a seamless experience, a private transfer can also be arranged upon request from Como San Giovanni station.

Travel time: 2 hours

Approximate cost: €30–€40 (CHF 30–40) (excluding tax, see more information under the "From Como San Giovanni station" section).

## Driving:

Take Via Piodella, Via al Laghetto and Via Antonio Riva to Via Clemente Maraini/Route 2, then the SS340 to Via Regina/Via Statale in Cadenabbia.

Travel time: Approximately 1 hour

## From Bergamo Airport (BGY)

## Private Transfer:

Contact the hotel to have your private transfer organized

## Taxi:

Taxis are available outside the terminal. We recommend to book in advance or reserve a ride.

Travel time: Approximately 1 hour and 50 minutes, depending on traffic

Approximate cost: €200-250 (tolls and luggage fees may apply).

## Train:

If traveling light, public transport may be a suitable alternative.

Take the flibco bus from Bergamo Airport Bus station to Milan Centrale.

From Milano Centrale, board onto the EC150 train to Como San Giovanni station.

Upon arrival at Como San Giovanni station, we recommend continuing by taxi or rental car.

For a seamless experience, a private transfer can also be arranged upon request from Como San Giovanni station.

Travel time: 3 hours

Approximate cost: €20-30 (excluding taxi, see more information under the "From Como San Giovanni station" section).

## Driving:

From Bergamo Airport, take the SS671 in Bergamo from SP116, Via Aeroporto. Follow A4/E64, A8 and A9/E35 to SS340/SS708 in Como. Take exit Lago di Como from A9/E35, then follow SS340 to Via Regina/Via Statale in Griante.

Travel time: Approximately 1 hour and 30 minutes, depending on traffic.

## From Turin Airport (TRN)

## Private Transfer:

Contact the hotel to have your private transfer organized

## Taxi:

Taxis are available outside the terminal. We recommend to book in advance or reserve a ride.

Travel time: 1 hour and 40 minutes, depending on traffic.

Approximate cost: €350-500, depending on availability and pre-booking.

## Train:

If traveling light, public transport may be a suitable alternative.

Take the bus from Turin Airport to Torino Porta Susa to Milano Centrale.

From Milano Centrale, take a regional train to Como San Giovanni.

Upon arrival at Como San Giovanni station, we recommend continuing by taxi or rental car.

For a seamless experience, a private transfer can also be arranged upon request from Como San Giovanni station.

Travel time: 3 hours 30 minutes

Approximate cost: €50–€70 (excluding taxi, see more information under the "From Como San Giovanni Station" section).

## Driving:

From Turin Airport, follow signs for the SP2 to Raccordo Autostradale Torino - Caselle/RA10 in Caselle Torinese. Then take the A4/E64 and A9/E35 to Via Cecilio in Como. Take exit Como Centro from A9/E35. Continue on Via Cecilio and take the SS342 to Viale Carlo Cattaneo

Travel time: Approximately 2 hours

## From Milan Linate Airport (LIN)

## Private Transfer:

Contact the hotel to have your private transfer organized

## Taxi:

Taxis are available outside the terminal. We recommend to book in advance or reserve a ride.

Travel time: Approximately 1 hour and 30 minutes, depending on traffic

Approximate cost: €180-250 (tolls and luggage fees may apply).

## Train:

If traveling light, public transport may be a suitable alternative.

Take the Linate Airport Shuttle to Milano Centrale (Piazza Luigi di Savoia).

From Milano Centrale, board a regional train to Como San Giovanni station (40-50 minutes).

Upon arrival at Como San Giovanni station, we recommend continuing by taxi or rental car.

For a seamless experience, a private transfer can also be arranged upon request from Como San Giovanni station.

Travel time: Approximately 2 hours and 20 minutes, depending on traffic.

Approximate cost: €20-30, (excluding taxi, see more information under the "From Como San Giovanni station" section).

## Driving:

From Linate, follow signs to Tangenziale Est (A51) and merge onto A9/E35 toward Como/Chiasso toward Como. Exit at Lago di Como toward SS340/SS708 and follow directions to the lakefront.

Travel time: Approximately 1 hour and 30 minutes

## From Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP)

## Private Transfer:

Contact the hotel to have your private transfer organized

## Taxi:

Taxis are available outside the terminal. We recommend to book in advance or reserve a ride.

Travel time: Approximately 1 hour and 10 minutes, depending on traffic.

Approximate cost: €160-250 (tolls and luggage fees may apply).

## Train:

If traveling light, public transport may be a suitable alternative.

Take the Malpensa Airport Express train to Saronno station. Then change to the direct train to Como San Giovanni station (1 hour).

Upon arrival at Como San Giovanni station, we recommend continuing by taxi or rental car.

For a seamless experience, a private transfer can also be arranged upon request from Como San Giovanni station.

Travel time: Approximately 2 hours

Approximate cost: €11-20, (excluding taxi, see more information under the "From Como San Giovanni station" section).

## Driving:

From Malpensa Airport, follow signs to SS336. Merge onto A /E35 and continue until the Lago di Como exit. Follow local signs to the lakefront, following SS340 to Via Regina/Via Statale in Griante.

Travel time: Approximately 1 hour, depending on traffic.
