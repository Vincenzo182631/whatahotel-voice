# Family & Friends

## Reconnect With Your Loved Ones — and Yourself — at Secret Bay

Unplug and make your memories in a ‘Nature Island’ experience that will become your family’s and friends’ new treasured tradition. For those seeking to reconnect with the ones you love, nature and yourself, Secret Bay’s stand-alone architectural villa residences and interconnected multi-villa estate offerings create the ultimate sanctuary for friends and families of any size. With access to secluded beaches, exhilarating adventures for kids, young adults, parents and best friends paired with unparalleled six-star amenities, Secret Bay is here to be your ultimate seaside hideaway.

## Discovery & Experiences

On the Nature Island, let your inner curiosity flourish as you find yourself again, discover tropical wonders for the first time and fully immerse yourself in nature. Search for hidden treasures through lush landscapes, discover colorful sea creatures while snorkeling and taste the magic of the island during a private cooking class with renowned chefs. Allow Secret Bay to introduce you to our favorite experts, like famed ornithologist Dr. Birdie, who will take your entire family on a journey to discover some of the island’s most illustrious fauna. This will truly be an unforgettable experience with memories to last a lifetime. Ready to check out the fun? Take a look at our curated guides for every member in your family.

## Friends & Family Villas and Multi-Villa Estates

With spectacular jungle-to-ocean views that extend from the Nature Island’s tallest mountain, Morne Diablotin, to endless sunset views over the Caribbean Sea, Secret Bay’s 1.5-, 2- and 2.5-bedroom villas offer an idyllic retreat for friends and families. Enjoy up to 3,303 square feet of seamless indoor-outdoor living space, including King bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms, guest bedroom or half-bed layout with Queen or Double sleeper sofa, extra bathrooms, outdoor rain showers, private plunge pools, deck loungers, shaded hammocks fit for the entire group, and every modern amenity one could desire.

VIEW FRIENDS & FAMILY VILLAS

Spacious and secluded, Secret Bay’s Multi-Villa Estate offerings are designed for family, friends, or groups of any size to reconnect in their own luxury, seaside sanctuary. Guests may combine select neighbouring villas connected by private pathways to create a private estate, perfect for all occasions including multigenerational family gatherings, couples’ trips, wedding parties, corporate retreats or simply bringing together the ones you love

## Just For Parents

Allow us to curate your stay with a variety of tailored experiences and six-star amenities that will provide you with much deserved relaxation time, and your family with daily activities that will leave you wanting more. With Secret Bay’s exclusive kids concierge and young adult offerings, parents have the opportunity to reconnect with themselves and nature. Start with a rejuvenating yoga class at the Bwa Mang Wellness Pavilion, pamper yourself with a massage at Gommier Spa, indulge in decadent fare at Bwa Denn restaurant, then reunite with your family for an evening of cheers on the Vetivert Sunset Deck.
