# Resort Amenities

Treehouse spa, oceanfront pool, farm-to-table dining, and two private beaches at Dominica's #1 resort,

Secret Bay’s amenities are shaped by the same landscape in which they sit. Begin your day with yoga at the Bwa Mang Wellness Pavilion, suspended over the Cario River. Unwind at the Gommier Spa, a treehouse-style retreat overlooking Tibay Beach. Gather at Bwa Denn, where food, art, and community converge. Secure one of twenty seats at Zing Zing for a six-course Discovery Menu that changes nightly. And cap your evening with cocktails on the Vetiver Sunset Deck as the sun drops into the Caribbean Sea.

## Mouben

Featuring modern, biophilic design, our resort hub connects guests to the complete Secret Bay experience with our Welcome House, Concierge, Funicular and more.

## Welcome House

Your grand entrance into Secret Bay with open air sitting rooms and jungle to ocean views

## Concierge

Meet our on-call concierge and expert guides, ready to craft an unparalleled, bespoke adventure through the untouched wonders of Dominica

## Funicular

Ascend 400 linear feet into our coastal jungle, effortlessly accessing your Clifftop, Hillside or Summit villa

## Electric Vehicles

Access our fleet of electric vehicles for transportation to your Peninsula or Hillside villa

## Accessibility

Get around with wheelchair-accessible ramps, lifts, electric golf carts, shuttles and seamless access to the Caribbean's longest funicular

## Bwa Denn

Where food, art, and community converge. This modern, minimalist space is home to 100+ Caribbean and Latin American art pieces, local art installations, an island-to-table restaurant, tapas terrace, full-service bar, kombucha microbrewery and more.

## Our Commitment

We strive to source ingredients that are as local, organic and traceable, ensuring that each dish reflects the vibrant flavors of the island while promoting the health of our community and planet. This ethos guides every decision we make, and we are proud to share this commitment with you.

## Island-to-Table

Indulge in traceable and sustainable dining with farm-raised meats, organic poultry, hand-caught local fish, and fresh vegetables, all sourced from the property or neighboring farms.

## Zing Zing

Secret Bay's open-air restaurant accommodates only 20 guests per evening. Chef Allison Lo curates a six-course Discovery Menu built around what the island provides each day, with three handcrafted cocktails included. There is no fixed menu. Each evening's direction is guided by availability, creativity, and the freshest ingredients Dominica has to offer.

## Restaurant

Open-air dining of local cuisine and chef’s innovative “no menu” concept set to 180 degree views of the Caribbean sea, open seasonally for private or group dining

## Discovery Menu

An innovative, “no menu” dining concept helmed by Chef Allison Lo who curates six courses where each dish is tuned to your preferences.

## Botanica

Feast the senses with an outdoor dining experience nestled into the Chef’s organic garden by the Cario river. Enjoy true garden-to-fork decadence with wholesome options such as fresh vegetables, fragrant herbs, and aromatic spices sourced directly from the organic garden.

## Chef’s Table

Experience an outdoor dining experience nestled in an organic garden by the Cario river

M, W, F 11a-330p (cooking classes)

## Organic Garden

Planted with locally sourced seeds and saplings, the garden produces exceptional flavors, rooted in sustainability

## Foraging

Forage for fresh vegetables, fragrant herbs and aromatic spices alongside our talented chefs

## Gommier

In this treehouse-style serene space overlooking Tibay Beach, guests can indulge in an array of treatments and wellness experiences including our Spa, Pavilion and Fitness studio. Whether you wish to relax tense muscles after hiking, unravel the effects of everyday stressors brought from home, or find your inner peace, our therapists will cater each service to your needs.

## Spa

A three-walled, treehouse-style serene espace for solo, family and couple’s treatments overlooking Tibay Beach

## Pavillion

Recharge, refresh and rejuvenate with intimate yoga and wellness classes suspended over the magical Cario river

## Fitness

Rebalance the body and spirit at our zero-emissions, open-air fitness and play facility featuring self-powered equipment

## Gwiyavyé

Suspended between sky and sea, these floating platforms nestled in Guava trees bring a whole new magic to our amenities. Overlook the sparkling Caribbean sea and Cabrits National Park, as you soak up the sun’s rays, let your mind unwind as you enjoy your favorite book or taste the flavors of the island with one of our craft cocktails.

## Lap Pool

Set in-nature, amid an orchard of guava trees relax oceanfront at our lap pool

## Bar

## Watersports

## Heights

## Beach

Walking paths from your villa lead to serene waters of Tibay Beach with shaded lounging and easy access to kayaks, paddle boards, snorkel gear, and sea adventures

## Cove
