# Welcome to The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki

## Embrace Hawaiian luxury at the Pink Palace of the Pacific

Opened in 1927 and known as the "Pink Palace of the Pacific," The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki is an oasis of timeless luxury on the island of Oahu, Hawaii. This Waikiki beachfront...See More resort offers unprecedented amenities, refined accommodations and exclusive services designed for a 5‑star luxury experience. Honoring its storied past, this luxury resort in Waikiki features exquisite rooms and suites in the landmark Historic Wing and the magnificent Mailani Tower, along with world-class dining that embodies Hawaii’s rich culture. As a celebrated beachfront hotel in Waikiki, guests can embark on an epicurean journey at Azure Restaurant, the original Mai Tai Bar, the Royal Hawaiian Bakery and A Royal Hawaiian Luau – Aha Aina, Waikiki’s only oceanfront luau with views of Diamond Head. ### Only at The Royal Traditions

## A Living Cultural Expression

To arrive at The Royal Hawaiian is to take a step back in time, from the sweeping front drive to the arched porte cochere. Opened in 1927, the resort sits on the lush grounds of the Summer Palace of Hawaiian royalty. Guests are invited to join the popular Historic Tour at this luxury resort in Waikiki, to wander the museum arcade, or view the annual Matson Art Exhibit.

Learn More

## Mele and Moolelo

As the sun sets over the Pacific, the spectacular Ahaaina Luau begins in a joyous gathering of music, cultural arts and indigenous epicurean experiences. Hosted on Mondays and Thursdays on Ocean Lawn, this in the pinnacle of immersive moolelo (storytelling). The iconic “Royal Hawaiian Hotel” mele (song) is played at every luau, and at midday daily by the resort’s musicians.

### Royal Hawaiian Beach

From the comfort of a private pink striped umbrella set, enjoy panoramic views of the Pacific Ocean and the world-famous Diamond Head. The famous Waikiki Beach Boys are available for surfing lessons and watersports rentals.

### Malulani Pool

Offering a quiet oasis under swaying coconut trees, this exclusive poolside enclave is lined with lounge chairs and a selection of cabanas featuring plush daybed-style seating.

### Helumoa Pool

The Helumoa Playground is an award-winning family friendly water complex featuring waterfalls, waterslides and cabanas. It is a shared pool for guests of The Royal Hawaiian and Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort.

### An Inviting Sanctuary, An Elegant Retreat

Discover the hotel rooms at our luxury resort in Waikiki. At The Royal Hawaiian, guests may choose between two experiences: the Historic Wing, with its custom carved doors, polished wood accents and bespoke pink-hued wallpaper, or the Mailani Tower Wing, with its oceanfront proximity to Waikiki Beach.

## Floating Yoga at Edge Infinity Pool

Discover serenity at sunrise or twilight with exclusive yoga sessions at the Edge Infinity Pool, located at Sheraton Waikiki, The Royal Hawaiian’s sister resort. Begin your day with sun salutations and Diamond Head views or unwind after hours as LED lights shimmer beneath you in the pool. Each guided class takes place on anchored boards for balance and tranquility. Reservations required.

Rooms Fit For Royalty

### The Historic Wing & Mailani Tower

The Historic Wing, constructed in 1927, is nestled in the lush gardens of Helumoa, with the majority of rooms overlooking the resort’s groves and courtyards. The Historic Wing rooms are serene sanctuaries, beloved for their details such as hand carved wooden doors and platinum and rose walls. In contrast, the Mailani Tower is a view to the ever-changing sky and sea of Waikiki, with every room offering stunning ocean views and a private balcony to enjoy the trade wind breezes. In contrast to the historic wing, Mailani Tower guest rooms are appointed with sophisticated, contemporary furnishings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I lost something on-property at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort?

You are able to report a lost item and/or initiate a search of items found here at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort through https://www.chargerback.com/.

Walk-ins, phone calls, and emails through our direct channels are also available to inquire about lost items.

What are the Marriott Bonvoy benefits at The Royal Hawaiian?

Gold Elite members are eligible for a complimentary room upgrade and 2pm late check out, both based upon availability. Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members are eligible for a complimentary room upgrade and 4pm late check out, both based upon availability. In addition, Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador members may select a welcome gift of their choice: 1,000 bonus points or complimentary daily continental breakfast for the member plus one guest.

Does The Royal Hawaiian offer rollaway beds?

Yes, rollaway beds available. $155 per night for adults and $125 per night for children (tax not included).

Where are the ice machines located?

In the Historic Wing, the ice machine is located on the first floor near room 106. On floors 2-6, the ice machine is located near the rooms ending in 96. In Mailani Tower, the ice machines are nearest rooms ending in 04.

Where are the water refill stations located?

Water refill stations available at the Fitness Center adjacent to the Royal Hawaiian Bakery. There are also ice machines on each floor of the Mailani Tower that serve as both water refill stations as well as ice dispensers.

How do I contact the concierge at The Royal Hawaiian?

Book your activities online, call our main line and ask to be transferred to the concierge, or email our concierge team for personalized assistance planning experiences across Oahu.

What are the easiest ways to get to The Royal Hawaiian?

Please call the main line and we will transfer you to the concierge for assistance.

Is there a daily resort fee charge at The Royal Hawaiian?

Yes, you can learn more about what's included in the Resort Fee on our What to Expect page.

Where can I learn about what the on-site experience at The Royal Hawaiian will be like?

Please visit our What to Expect page for updated resort information and a list of activities.

Is there luggage storage available at The Royal Hawaiian?

We would be happy to help store luggage at our bell desk. However, overnight storage of luggage is not available.

Does The Royal Hawaiian have multi-lingual staff?

Yes, The Royal Hawaiian’s staff includes a number of multi-lingual employees. We pride ourselves in delivering exceptional service. If you have any particular needs, please reach out to our concierge.

Is there a doctor’s office near The Royal Hawaiian?

Our sister property Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort has a full medical facility and is a 2-minute walk from the resort.

The clinic is open from 8 am to 6 pm daily, and is equipped to perform non-emergency medical care including x-rays, blood tests, and a variety of other physician services.

What is the minimum age a guest must be to check in at The Royal Hawaiian?

The guest must be 18 years old to check in at our Honolulu resort.

Do you offer babysitting services at The Royal Hawaiian?

We recommend Sheraton Waikiki’s Poppins Keiki Hawaii, which offers professional babysitting services and a kids program. Please visit their website for more details.

Is the Royal Hawaiian located directly on Waikiki Beach?

Yes, our Oahu hotel is located on the beach.

### Frequently Asked Questions

The check-in time at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki.

What are the parking options at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki?

On-site parking is available at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki. Self parking is available at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki, with rates of $7.50 per hour, and $55.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $65.00 per day.

Does The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki have in-room Wi-Fi?

Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki booking.

What is the closest airport near The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki?

The closest airport near The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki is Daniel K. Inouye International Airport (HNL). HNL is located approximately 9.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki have electric vehicle charging stations?
