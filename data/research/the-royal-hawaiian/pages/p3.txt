### Mai Tai Bar, Home of the Original Mai Tai

The original Mai Tai cocktail was created by “Trader Vic” Bergeron in 1944 and brought to Hawaiʻi in 1953, where it first found a home at The Royal Hawaiian. Today, the resort offers more than a half dozen variations. Try them all while enjoying sweeping Diamond Head views at this iconic, always‑lively oceanfront Waikiki beach bar.

## Stay at One, Dine at All

The “Stay at One, Dine at All!” program invites guests of The Royal Hawaiian to sign their restaurant check to their room at any restaurant or bar at any of the four Waikiki Collection resorts, including Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort, the Moana Surfrider, and Sheraton Princess Kaiulani. By signing the charges to your room, Marriott Bonvoy members will also earn additional points. Explore the options across the four hotels and resorts: click below for a list of participating restaurants.
