# Discover Honolulu From Our 5-Star Hotel on the Beach

## Experiences

For those who yearn to not just discover a new destination, but to embrace it, to dive beneath the surface, to explore like a local: The Royal Hawaiian awaits. As the crown jewel of Waikiki, our 5-star...See More hotel on the beach offers a wide range of nearby adventures in the city of Honolulu. An endless array of outdoor and beach activities, cultural exploration opportunities & tantalizing restaurants are a short journey from the hotel. Shangri-La Museum, adjacent to Diamond Head on the eastern end of Waikiki Beach, is a breathtaking experience. Allow our concierge to suggest where to shop in Honolulu, including International Marketplace Waikiki and The Royal Hawaiian Shopping Center, which are 5-minute walks from the hotel. Luxury Row and Ala Moana Shopping Center are also located nearby. Both English and Japanese are spoken in local business establishments, while business hours vary from shop-to-shop.See Less

## The Royal Hawaiian's Historic Gallery

Newly opened in anticipation of the Pink Palace’s 100th birthday, this gallery showcases rare photographs and vintage art from Matson, the resort’s original developer. Highlights include a detailed timeline tracing the hotel’s journey from 1925 to today, and fashion through the decades. The gallery is open daily from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. and is complimentary for all guests.

### Royal Hawaiian Hotel History

Long before Waikiki was as a tourist destination, King Kamehameha I had his home where The Royal Hawaiian stands today, and Queen Kaahumanu’s Summer Palace was located on what is now the resort’s Coconut Grove. Built on 15 acres of beautiful beach frontage, the luxurious hotel with its distinctive Moorish-style architecture, painted pink, was promoted world-wide as a premier visitor destination. On...See More February 1, 1927, The Royal Hawaiian Hotel opened – a $4 million investment by the Matson Navigation Co., intended to be a luxurious for Matson luxury liner passengers. During WWII, the United States Navy recreation and morale office leased The Royal Hawaiian, transforming the resort into a major rest and relaxation center for the Navy personnel. The resort was restored to its pre-war elegance in 1947. Today, The Royal Hawaiian and its associates continue to carry on the legacy of the rich heritage and inspiring and distinctive character of the “Pink Palace of the Pacific.”See Less

Poolside Details

For our guests' safety. floats, rafts and toys are not allowed in Malulani Pool. To ensure that all Royal Hawaiian guests are able to utilize the Malulani Pool facilities, pool chairs left unattended for more than 2 hours will be released to other waiting guests.  Pool and beach side food and beverage service begins daily at 11:00am. Please note that alcohol is not allowed on the beach.

Helumoa Playground is designed to delight families. Choose from its two freshwater swimming pools, a 15-foot-high by 70-foot-long waterslide, two large whirlpool spas, available for guests 16 years of age and over, with sweeping views of Waikiki Beach, an interactive fountain area for kids, and water chaise lounges. Guests can borrow towels and purchase pool goods at a pool hut.

The Royal Hawaiian Beach Boy Program

Make the most of the sun-dappled waves of the Pacific with a little help from The Royal Hawaiian Beach Boy Program. Our Beach Boys provide surf and stand-up paddleboard lessons and rentals, as well as outrigger canoe rides along the gently lapping waves of Waikiki. Thanks to their expert instruction, you will soon be watching the sunrise from your perch on a freshly waxed surfboard—eagerly awaiting the next wave. Enjoy complimentary surf demonstrations and other authentic island activities. Secure reservations at the Royal Beach Pool deck at the Beach Boy Hale on Waikiki Beach. Please call (808) 931-7595 for additional details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there lifeguards on Waikiki Beach?

Yes. The closest lifeguard station to The Royal Hawaiian is about 100 meters down the beach towards Diamond Head.

Are there lifeguards at Malulani Pool or Helumoa Playground?

There are no lifeguards at Malulani Pool or Helumoa Playground.

Is there a fee for pool chairs at Malulani Pool and Helumoa Playground?

No, there is no fee for pool chairs. However, chairs are available at a first come, first serve basis.

Where can I learn to surf at Waikiki Beach?

The Waikiki Beach Services Beach Boys teach surfing lessons daily.

Where can I rent a surfboard on Waikiki Beach?

Waikiki Beach Services can assist with surfboard rentals on Waikiki Beach, and offer a Beachboys Valet program.

What are The Royal Hawaiian pool's hours of operation?

Helumoa Playground is open from 8 am to 8 pm, while Malulani Pool is open from 8 am to 6 pm.
