The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki
Located in the former playground of Hawaiian royalty, this pink-tinted classic old Hawaii hotel is an oasis right in the middle of Waikiki.

Why book?
The Royal Hawaiian is a go-to for old-school Hawaii glamour, direct access to Waikiki Beach, and one of the most recognizable hotel buildings in America, all set in a surprisingly tranquil setting amid Waikiki's bustle.
Set the scene
The hotel might occupy a prime stretch of Waikiki Beach, but it somehow manages to feel tucked away from the crowds. Called “Pink Palace of the Pacific,” the sprawling pink landmark sits on more than a dozen acres of beachfront land and is still one of Waikiki's defining images nearly a century after opening.
The crowd includes honeymooners and multigenerational families, Marriott loyalists cashing in points, and travelers who appreciate a sense of history. You'll see designer resortwear, linen shirts, and plenty of pink robes. But still—while neighboring resorts can feel hectic, the Royal Hawaiian often feels remarkably serene by comparison.
The backstory
Opened in 1927 in a distinctive Spanish-Moorish style, the Royal Hawaiian helped establish Waikiki as a luxury destination long before Hawaii became a mainstream vacation spot. Over the decades, it has hosted everyone from Hollywood stars to presidents, earning its reputation as one of Hawaii's grand dames. Today, it’s part of Marriott's Luxury Collection portfolio.
The rooms
There are two very different experiences here. History buffs should book the Historic Wing. These rooms are smaller, and some feel a little dated, but the atmosphere is unmatched. Expect dark wood furnishings, pink accents, high ceilings in some categories, and a sense that you're staying in a piece of Waikiki history. Travelers who want comfort above all else should look to the Mailani Tower. These rooms are larger and more contemporary, and each includes an ocean view and a private lanai. The Mailani Ocean Corner rooms are particularly desirable thanks to their panoramic views of Waikiki Beach and Diamond Head. One caveat: Even some of the premium rooms can feel surprisingly basic relative to the nightly rate. The view often does a lot of the heavy lifting.
Food and drink
The beachfront Mai Tai Bar is the beating heart of the property. Come for sunset, order the signature mai tai, and settle in for one of the best people-watching spots in Waikiki. Live music and front-row ocean views don't hurt either. For breakfast, Surf Lanai is famous for its Pink Palace Pancakes, while the Royal Hawaiian Bakery turns out coffee, pastries, and beloved banana bread. Azure serves upscale seafood at dinner. If you're visiting on a Monday or Thursday, you’ll want to attend Ahaʻaina, the hotel’s oceanfront luau that has Hawaiian cuisine, music, storytelling, and views of Diamond Head at sunset.
The neighborhood/area
Kalākaua Avenue's shopping, restaurants, and nightlife are steps away, while Diamond Head, Pearl Harbor, and downtown Honolulu are easy excursions. Yet thanks to the property's mature landscaping and beachfront footprint, the hotel feels more secluded than most of its neighbors. You couldn’t ask for a better location.
The service
Staff members are known for their warm welcomes and thoughtful touches—in fact, it’s somewhat surprising for a large resort like this. The concierge team is attentive, too.
For families
Families have access to Helumoa Playground, a shared pool complex with waterslides, fountains, and multiple pools at the neighboring Sheraton Waikiki. The property also has cultural activities, lei-making classes, hula lessons, and other family-friendly programming.
Accessibility
The resort has elevators and accessible accommodations throughout the property.
Anything left to mention?
Book the view. If you're going to splurge on this hotel, it’s an ocean-facing room that makes the experience memorable. Also know that beach chairs, umbrellas, parking, and resort fees can add up quickly, and some guests might find the historic rooms to be more charming than luxurious.
