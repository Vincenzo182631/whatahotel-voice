## Royal, Reimagined: A Centennial Vision

A new chapter unfolds at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, as guest rooms, historic suites, and resort spaces are revitalized in anticipation of the resort's February 1, 2027 centennial anniversary. Along Waikiki's iconic shoreline, the Pink Palace of the Pacific continues its legacy of luxury and cultural significance through a thoughtful reimagining of its interiors. Across the Historic Wing and Mailani Tower, the revitalization introduces cohesive styling, curated artwork, botanical patterns, and design details inspired by Hawaii's vibrant natural beauty, creating an experience that honors the resort's storied past while embracing the future.

Renderings are subject to change.

## Mailani Tower: Debuting Fall 2026

The Mailani Tower’s 179 newly reimagined guest rooms reveal a refined, contemporary elegance, drawing inspiration from the building’s graceful curves and The Royal Hawaiian’s storied, worldly heritage. Every room in the Mailani Tower faces the ocean, offering uninterrupted views from a private lanai.

## Curated Serenity and Refined Ambiance

Bespoke furnishings, curated textiles, and original artwork by local artists Aloha de Mele and Kaypee Soh create a serene residential sanctuary. Sculptural lighting, intimate seating, and spa-inspired bathrooms enhance refined comfort, while botanical motifs weave a seamless design narrative between the Mailani Tower and Historic Wing guestrooms.

## Crafted with Timeless Elegance

The Suite Collection in the Mailani Tower includes four One-Bedroom Suites, six Family Loft Suites and the distinguished Alii Suite.

### Resort Spaces Revitalization

Beyond the guestrooms, curated enhancements extend throughout the resort. Arriving in Fall 2026, the Art Deco-inspired lobby will serve as a gateway to timeless tradition and gracious heritage. Chandeliers, furnishings, and rich textiles evoke the glamour of the era while celebrating the resort's original Spanish-Moorish architecture, painted ceilings, and enduring sense of place. Azure, the Royal's award-winning fine dining restaurant, will be reimagined with sophisticated furnishings, a jewel-toned bar, and coastal nuances that reflect Waikiki's iconic setting. The storied Monarch Ballroom will unveil elegant new finishes, while the Mailani Tower lobby is transformed with a signature rose-hued palette, creating a distinguished sense of arrival.

## Historic Wing: Debuting Winter 2026

The revitalized Historic Wing invites guests into the romance of travel’s golden age. Design details reminiscent of vintage steamer trunks evoke a time of grand voyages, while a new custom wood entertainment center creates a refined sense of residential ease. The building's 349 renovated Waikiki hotel rooms and suites retain the soaring ceilings and character that have defined the Pink Palace for generations.

## Inspired Details, Enduring Legacy

Room enhancements also include a new seating vignette and artwork by Native Hawaiian designer MANAOLA. The wing's famed guest room doors, featuring the Hawaii state seal, remain a treasured hallmark of the resort. The Historic Wing features 12 Junior Suites, 19 One-Bedroom Suites and three Prestige Suites: the Queen Kaahumanu Suite, The Royal Hawaiian Suite and The Kamehameha Suite.
