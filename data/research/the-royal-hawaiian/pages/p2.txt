Frequently Asked Questions

Are rollaways available in all rooms at The Royal Hawaiian?

Generally, rollaways are only available in King bed rooms and suites for safety reasons.  Please call for more details.

Are there sofa beds available at The Royal Hawaiian?

Sofa beds are available in some rooms and suites, please call for more details.

Are there connecting rooms available at The Royal Hawaiian?

Yes, please call our main line to learn about connecting rooms and/or book connecting rooms.

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki?

The check-in time at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm. Please call ahead to let us know if you will be arriving early or need late check-out.

Do you offer cribs in rooms at The Royal Hawaiian?

Yes, you may request a crib upon booking your room or during check-in. Please note that cribs may be limited by availability.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki?

The largest occupancy room at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki is the Historic 3 Bed Family Rm with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki?

Some rooms at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Royal Hawaiian, a Luxury Collection Resort, Waikiki have hair dryers.
