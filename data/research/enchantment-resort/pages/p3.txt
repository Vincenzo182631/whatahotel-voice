## Tii Gavo Restaurant

Connect with friends and family over a selection of dishes inspired by the American Southwest. Enjoy Tii Gavo each evening in the dining room or on the terrace as you enjoy stunning canyon views.

**Dinner** 3:30pm – 9:00pm \| **Bar** 3:30pm – 10:00pm

Reservations are recommended.

Dining is open to the public seasonally. _(excludes Hummingbird at Mii amo)_ Reservations are required and can be made by calling 844.361.9123 or online via OpenTable.
