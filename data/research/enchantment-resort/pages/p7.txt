Mii amo is a world-class Sedona resort spa located steps from Enchantment. Voted as a #1 Domestic Destination Spa by _Travel + Leisure_ magazine and a recipient of the coveted designation as Hall of Fame honoree, Mii amo is acclaimed for its unique ability to nurture personal wellbeing, offering timeless and inspired methods to guide guests to embrace the power that lives within.

As an Enchantment guest (ages 16+) you will be among the few to enjoy exclusive access to Mii amo.

Enchantment guests may use the Fitness and Movement studios, dine at Hummingbird ( _reservations required_), and are invited to attend the twice-daily Mii amo Crystal Grotto rituals and visit the Juice Bar and the Boutique.

Guests with confirmed reservations for spa services are invited to enjoy the communal areas of Mii amo, which include the Living Room, Pool, Sauna, Whirlpool, and Steam Room on the day of their service. These communal spaces may be accessed 1-hour before and 1-hour following your spa services. Please contact the Mii amo team directly to make arrangements to enjoy these amenities.
