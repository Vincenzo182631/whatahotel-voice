## Where All Trails Meet

Enchantment Resort is Sedona’s premier resort for mountain biking, hiking and Sedona outdoor activities. Trail House serves as an epicenter for all outdoor adventures, offering guided tours, professional-grade equipment rentals, and a retail shop featuring premium accessories and outdoor adventure apparel. To learn more or reserve your private guided ride or hike, please call our Adventure Advisors at 928.203.8407 or reserve your experience online.

### Antelope Canyon Expeditions

Experience Northern Arizona’s Colorado Plateau with two incredible experiences by air and/or land. Explore Upper Antelope Canyon, Rattlesnake Canyon, and Owl Canyon, all located within the Navajo Nation. These three canyons are notable for their stunning natural beauty and are iconic examples of the diverse landscape of the American Southwest.

## Dark Sky Experiences

Outdoor experiences after dark offer a magical connection to nature, from full moon hikes and hiking under the stars to peaceful stargazing sessions. Whether you’re capturing the cosmos through Night Sky Photography, Astrophotography or experimenting with Light Painting Portraits, the night sky becomes both a canvas and a classroom, the perfect opportunity to learn and explore under the stars.

Complimentary stargazing is offered at Enchantment Circle every Tuesday and Saturday evenings with our resort astronomer. _Weather permitting._

### Antelope Canyon Land and Water Expedition

Some landscapes ask to be witnessed from above. Others reveal themselves slowly, from the water's edge. The Antelope Canyon Kayaking Expedition invites you into both on a full day of discovery.

Set out from Antelope Point Marina on a guided 7-mile scenic journey through the canyon's still waters. Where conditions allow, the adventure continues on foot with a guided hike into the landscape itself.

EXPLORE Reserve an Adventure

Kayaking

### Labyrinth Canyon Land and Water Expedition

There are places that ask you to slow down, to let the canyon come to you. The Labyrinth Canyon Boat and Kayaking Expedition is one of them. A full day led by our expert Trail House guides and the team at Lake Powell Adventure Company, designed for those who want to let the canyon set the pace.

Your journey begins at Antelope Point Marina setting out on a scenic one-hour boat cruise to the canyon's entrance. From there, the day unfolds on the water, paddling through the tranquil reaches of Labyrinth Canyon, then continuing on foot with a guided hike into its breathtaking slot canyon.
