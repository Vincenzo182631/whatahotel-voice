Nestled into the corner of the pool deck, discover The Edge, our newest open-air casual dining experience. With covered seating, a dedicated kitchen and bar, and views that capture the essence of the canyon, guests may enjoy relaxed poolside lunches, innovative cocktails and intimate dinners with Boynton Canyon as your backdrop.

**Beverage Service:** 10:30am – 5:00pm \| **Dining**: 11:00am – 4:00pm _(seasonal)_
