##  2026 Wine Spectator's Best of   Award of Excellence

We are delighted to share that Che Ah Chi has been awarded the 2026 Wine Spectator’s Best of Award of Excellence. This award recognizes wine lists that offer a substantial selection spanning multiple wine growing regions including both vintage and regional depth.

Typically offering 300 or more selections, Best of Award restaurants are destinations for enthusiastic wine lovers and show a deep commitment to wine, both in the cellar and through their team.

## **Upcoming Renovation**

For many years, Che Ah Chi has been at the heart of the Enchantment dining experience, bringing guests together for special celebrations, memorable meals and unforgettable sunsets against the breathtaking backdrop of Boynton Canyon. Through the years, it has been a special place where lasting memories have been made, and we are sincerely grateful to the generations of guests who have gathered around our tables and made Che Ah Chi part of their Sedona story.

Che Ah Chi will close on October 19, 2026, as we begin an exciting transformation of the resort dining experiences. In 2027, we look forward to introducing new dining concepts that honor this extraordinary setting while also offering something entirely new. We look forward to sharing more in the months ahead.

## **Dining at Enchantment During the Transformation**

Throughout the renovation, guests can continue to enjoy a variety of exceptional dining experiences across the resort, including Tii Gavo, The Edge, The Market, in-room dining and Hummingbird at Mii amo. Our team is always happy to assist with reservations and personalized recommendations to make every dining experience memorable.
