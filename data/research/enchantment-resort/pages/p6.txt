The Market features breakfast and lunch options, snacks, freshly baked pastries and desserts, as well as specialty beverages.

The coffee bar serves our own Market Blend, hand roasted in small batches by FreeForm Coffee, a locally and independently owned roaster with deep roots in the Sedona community.

We welcome you to stop in before or after your red rock adventures.

Breakfast 6 am – 11 am  \|  Lunch 11 am – 4 pm
