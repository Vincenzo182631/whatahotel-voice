## Luxury Accommodations in Sedona

Nestled at the base of Boynton Canyon reside 218 casita guestrooms and suites. Designed in a Southwestern motif, each casita draws inspiration from Sedona’s iconic landscapes. All rooms feature a private deck or patio overlooking the grandeur of the canyon. Cozy fireplaces, elegant furnishings and authentic Southwestern appointments add the perfect ambiance to your stay.
