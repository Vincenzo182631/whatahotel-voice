## Perfect For Mountain Adventures

Stowe Mountain Resort is legendary among skiers and snowboarders, offering over 480 acres of terrain on Mount Mansfield and Spruce Peak. With an average of 314 inches of annual snowfall, excellent grooming, and runs for every ability level, it’s a winter sports paradise. Beyond downhill skiing, enjoy cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, ice skating, and sleigh rides through snowy landscapes. After a day on the mountain, retreat to Meadowstone Manor’s hot tubs and cozy fireplaces for the ultimate après-ski experience.

When the snow melts, Stowe transforms into a hiker’s and cyclist’s dream. Tackle the challenging trails up Mount Mansfield, bike the scenic Stowe Recreation Path, or explore countless hiking trails through forests and alpine meadows. In fall, witness one of nature’s greatest shows as the mountains explode in brilliant reds, oranges, and golds.

Tennis, pickleball, fishing, golfing, swimming, zip-lining, kayaking, and paddleboarding round out the endless warm-weather activities. Whatever adventure you’re after, our dedicated concierge can arrange it all.

## Discover Vermont Charm

Stowe village is the epitome of New England charm. White-steepled church, covered bridges, farm-to-table restaurants, and local shops line the picturesque streets. Browse art galleries, sample Vermont’s famous craft beers and ciders, or visit a working farm to see how maple syrup and cheese are made.

The village offers excellent dining options, from upscale locally sourced cuisine to cozy cafes and classic Vermont taverns. Don’t miss the Stowe Farmers Market (summer Sundays) or a visit to the historic Trapp Family Lodge, home of the family that inspired “The Sound of Music.”

Beyond Stowe, explore nearby attractions like Ben & Jerry’s Factory, Cold Hollow Cider Mill, and the charming towns of Waterbury and Montpelier. Or simply drive the scenic backroads, stopping at farm stands for fresh produce, maple syrup, and homemade treats.

## Why Stowe?

Stowe offers something rare—a destination that's equally spectacular in every season. Winter brings world-class skiing and snowy mountain landscapes. Spring ushers in wildflowers and rushing waterfalls. Summer offers endless outdoor adventures under blue skies. And fall delivers the most breathtaking foliage displays in the country.

But Stowe is more than just natural beauty. It's a community that has mastered the balance between upscale amenities and authentic Vermont character. You'll find luxury resorts and fine dining alongside working farms and mom-and-pop shops. It's a place where you can challenge yourself on black diamond runs in the morning and browse local art galleries in the afternoon.

Around the area

- Stowe Mountain Resort with 480+ acres of world-class skiing and snowboarding
- Scenic trails up Mount Mansfield, Vermont’s highest peak
- Farm-to-table dining, craft breweries, and local cideries throughout the village
- The historic Trapp Family Lodge and charming covered bridges
- Ben & Jerry’s Factory and Cold Hollow Cider Mill for sweet treats
