## FAQ

What makes Meadowstone Manor unique compared to other luxury Vermont estates?

As the largest vacation rental in Stowe, VT, Meadowstone Manor blends the natural beauty of Vermont with exquisite modern design, spacious accommodations, and resort-style amenities. From panoramic windows, stone and wood detailing, and elegant interiors to dedicated concierge services and proximity to skiing and hiking, every detail is designed to create a seamless elevated experience.

How many guests can Meadowstone Manor accommodate?

Meadowstone Manor comfortably hosts large families across 14 beautifully appointed bedrooms, each with plush linens, ensuite bathrooms, and private patios or terraces.

What types of amenities does Meadowstone Manor offer on-site?

Guests enjoy a full suite of resort-style amenities, including two clay tennis courts, pickleball court, a half basketball court, outdoor heated pool, spring-fed pond with swimming platform, multiple hot tubs, a private gym with Peloton, a spa room, billiards table in the pub, media room, and cozy fireplaces throughout the estate. hiking trails can be found on the 30 acres and in winter snowshoeing, tubing and cross-country skiing can be done on property.

How close is Meadowstone Manor to local attractions and activities?

The estate is just minutes from Stowe Mountain Resort for world class skiing and snowboarding. Stowe’s charming downtown with its vibrant arts and culinary scenes is also only five minutes away.

Does Meadowstone Manor provide complementary concierge services?

Yes! The dedicated concierge team handles everything from grocery deliveries to ski transportation and reservations to local restaurants and events. The concierge can also assist with booking housekeeping services, a private chef, wellness practitioners for Yoga and massage, and many other authentic Vermont experiences. Many guests do covered bridges tours, fall foliage tours and more.

Can we host events or group gatherings at Meadowstone Manor?

Yes! With 14 bedrooms, multiple gathering spaces, fireplaces, a pub, fire pits, and expansive outdoor areas, Meadowstone Manor is perfect for large family reunions, or smaller corporate retreats.

Are there on-site dining or entertainment options?

The property features a fully equipped chef’s kitchen with everything needed for your group. An on-site pub with a pool table, a yoga studio/dance club, a media/theatre room, and multiple fireplaces and lounge areas can be found throughout. Multiple indoor and outdoor dining tables seat your group for dinners.

Is Meadowstone Manor pet friendly?

Yes! The Manor is dog friendly. Reach out with some information to get approval in advance of booking. There is a small pet fee charged per dog.

Are your estates suitable for weddings or vow renewals?

Absolutely. Our properties offer picturesque backdrops for ceremonies and receptions, with complete privacy and end-to-end coordination handled by your required event planner. Maximum guest capacity varies by property but never exceeds 50. To inquire please complete the wedding inquiry form.
