### What are summer activities in Stowe?

Summer in Stowe is all about getting outdoors and enjoying Vermont at its most vibrant. Visitors can hike or bike miles of scenic mountain trails, paddle along rivers and reservoirs, or explore the area’s waterfalls and swimming holes. Warm-weather favorites include gondola rides for panoramic views, fly fishing, golfing, and guided outdoor adventures. The town itself offers charming shops, local dining, farmers’ markets, and seasonal events that showcase Stowe’s laid-back mountain culture. Summer at the property includes a heated pool, 2 clay tennis courts, pickleball, 2 hot tubs, hiking trails, and table tennis.
