In golf, a perfect lie is a rare gift: the ball rests precisely as hoped, inviting confidence, possibility and the promise of a remarkable shot. As Adare Manor @theadaremanor prepares to host the Ryder Cup, that same feeling begins the moment you arrive—a warm certainty that this is exactly where you are meant to be, and that something extraordinary lies ahead.

The Golden Lie captures this spirit in a glass: bright, elegant and quietly assured, like a ray of golden light guiding the way. It reflects a sense of unclouded happiness that has defined the manor for generations. Lady Caroline Dunraven, the first lady of the house, once called Adare Manor her “home of unclouded happiness”—a blessing that still lingers in every welcome, every detail and every memorable stay.

The cocktail contains Jameson Irish Whiskey, Crème de Pêche, House-made Lime Lemonade, and Angostura bitters. $25.

Last call for Le Casablanca at Off the Record. Come savor a journey through Morocco, one sip at a time, before it’s gone.

Off The Record’s Leading Cocktails of the World series continues with “Le Casablanca,” a signature creation from Royal Mansour Casablanca @royalmansourcasablanca, one of our fellow Leading Hotels of the World. Each quarter, we partner with an iconic hotel to bring the spirit of its destination to Washington, DC through an exceptional cocktail.

Inspired by the cultural exchange between Casablanca and Washington, DC, Le Casablanca reflects a pivotal moment in Morocco’s history when American influences helped shape the country’s emerging cocktail culture. Crafted with Maker’s Mark, saffron honey, passionfruit purée, fresh lime, mint, cardamom bitters, whisky barrel-aged bitters, and sparkling water, every sip offers a vibrant taste of Morocco’s rich flavors and traditions.

Only a few tickets left for our next Music Salon, ECHOES OF THE EAST 🎼

Monday, October 5, 2026

Doors open at 5:30 pm

Performance from 6:00 - 7:00 pm

The Hay-Adams Room

On Monday, October 5, explore the musical influences and cross-cultural encounters that helped shape the distinctive sounds of Debussy, Ravel, and Ibert. PostClassical Ensemble presents oboist Fatma Daglar, clarinetist David Jones, and pianist Audrey Andrist in a program that considers how French composers imagined places far east of Western Europe, and wrote them into classical tradition.

Drawing on her Turkish heritage and artistic perspective, Daglar brings a personal lens to an evening of evocative color, rhythmic vitality, and expressive nuance. Enjoy exquisite performances along with commentary by the musicians and PostClassical Ensemble’s music director, Angel Gil-Ordóñez, in the storied Hay-Adams Room.

Tickets at the link in our bio.

Fall at The Hay-Adams is the perfect invitation to explore beyond Washington, D.C. 🍂

Head Concierge Fran recommends planning a family day at Gaver Farm for apple 🍎 and pumpkin picking, hayrides, live animals 🐐 , a kids’ zipline and its famous corn maze 🌽 , featuring an America 250 theme this year. The Fall Fun Festival runs through November 1, with pies 🥧 , jams and seasonal treats waiting at the farm market.

For a leisurely weekend, visit Virginia wine country 🍷 and acclaimed vineyards 🍇 including Linden and Crimson Lane, browse the antique shops and charming streets of Fredericksburg, or head to Annapolis for the annual Sailboat Show, October 15–18. ⛵️

Return to The Hay-Adams for refined comfort in the heart of the capital after a beautiful fall day.
