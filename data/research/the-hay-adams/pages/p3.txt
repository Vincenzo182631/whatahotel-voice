# TheLafayette

Essential DC elegance

Upscale contemporary American cuisine meets modern elegance and personalized service in our fine dining restaurant. By day, sunlight streams invitingly through the windows, and by night, candlelight holds an ambient glow. Perfect for any occasion – from power breakfasts to anniversary celebrations – a meal at The Lafayette is like a hidden glimpse into the real Washington, DC.
