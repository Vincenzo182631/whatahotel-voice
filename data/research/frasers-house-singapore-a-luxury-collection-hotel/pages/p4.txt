Frasers House presents refined private dining experiences across its signature venues. From bespoke Cantonese menus at Man Fu Yuan, to convivial Italian gatherings at LUCE and elegant afternoon moments at The Lobby Lounge, each restaurant in Bugis, Singapore​ offers thoughtfully curated cuisine and intimate spaces ideal for business occasions, celebrations and social gatherings.

## The Lobby Lounge

An elegant setting for a range of occasions, from seasonal dinners and intimate celebrations to wine tastings, masterclasses and cocktail networking events. This luxury Singapore hotel restaurant​ in Bugis also suits product launches, brand showcases, media gatherings and cultural exhibitions, welcoming both large occasions and relaxed social moments centred on refined hospitality.

## Man Fu Yuan

An award-winning Cantonese restaurant suited for refined celebrations, corporate dining and memorable social occasions in Singapore. Featuring several private dining rooms, semi-private dining spaces, and a signature mega table that accommodates up to 26 guests, the elegant setting offers a sophisticated backdrop for intimate gatherings, business lunches, wedding banquets, family celebrations and festive occasions. Signature Cantonese cuisine, impeccable service and thoughtful hospitality come together to create a dining experience rooted in tradition and elevated with contemporary refinement.

From weekend brunches and family occasions to business lunches, festive dinners and intimate wedding receptions, each experience is shaped by authentic Italian flavours, generous buffets and interactive live stations that bring guests together through convivial dining and memorable hospitality.
