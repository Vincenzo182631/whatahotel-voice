Frasers House Experiences

### A Curated Sensory Journey

At Frasers House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Singapore, discovery begins at our doorstep. A Curated Sensory Journey invites guests to explore Kampong Glam through sight, taste, sound, scent and touch, revealing the living heritage and vibrant culture that shape this historic neighborhood.

### Afternoon at the House

Each afternoon at Essence in The Lobby Lounge, guests are invited to discover Singapore through a curated tasting experience created exclusively for Frasers House. Signature cookies reinterpret beloved local flavours such as laksa, chicken rice and ondeh ondeh, paired with TehKita, a refined interpretation of Singapore’s teh tarik tradition, offering a memorable introduction to the city’s culinary heritage.

### The Maker's Table

Each week, guests are invited to discover Singapore’s culinary heritage through a guided hands-on experience inspired by the traditions of Bugis. Local favourites such as Singapore Chilli Crab Sliders and Nyonya Chendol are reimagined with refined craftsmanship, before guests enjoy their creations alongside a refreshing Green Apple Asam Boi.
