Destination Discovery

## The Curated Sensory Journey

The Curated Sensory Journey invites guests to experience Kampong Glam through sight, taste, sound, scent, and touch—revealing the living heritage that shapes both the neighbourhood and our house.

From the architectural grandeur of Parkview Square to the vibrant lanes of Haji Lane, guests at our hotel near Kampong Glam encounter visual storytelling at every turn. Savour traditional putu piring, pause to the call to prayer near Sultan Mosque, explore bespoke fragrances at Sifr Aromatics, and feel the textures of Persian textiles that echo historic trade routes. More than a walk, this is an immersive encounter with place, guided by our Concierge team.

Complimentary for in-house guests

Duration: Every Saturday at 4pm, approximately 90 to 120 minutes

Meeting Point: Hotel Lobby

Advance reservation recommended

Epicurean

## Daily Epicurean Moment: Afternoon at the House

Each afternoon, guests at our hotel near the National Museum are invited to discover the flavours of Singapore through a curated tasting experience. Signature cookies reinterpret beloved local dishes such as Laksa, Chicken Rice and Ondeh Ondeh paired with TehKita and TehKita+, elegant interpretations of Singapore’s Teh Tarik, Malay pulled tea, crafted from black tea, milk and warming spices, available in both mocktail and cocktail forms. This daily Epicurean Moment offers a refined introduction to Singapore through flavour, connecting guests to the culinary heritage and cultural richness of the destination.

Complimentary for in-house guests

Venue: Essence, The Lobby Lounge, Level One

Time: Daily, 3:00pm to 3:15pm

Epicurean

## Weekly Epicurean Moment: The Maker's Table

Led by our culinary team, recreate local favourites while discovering the stories and techniques behind them. Singapore Chilli Crab Sliders reinterpret one of the nation’s most iconic dishes, while Nyonya Chendol showcases the enduring legacy of local dessert with fragrant pandan jelly, coconut milk and gula melaka. The experience concludes with guests enjoying their creations alongside house-made Green Apple Asam Boi, traditional Singaporean drink influenced by Chinese preserved plum culture, combining Calamansi juice with salted dried plum for a distinctive sweet, sour, andsalty refreshment.

Venue: LUCE, Level One

Day: Every Wednesday, 3:15pm to 4:15pm

Price: $38 per guest (price is subject to government tax and service charge)

Please approach the Concierge Desk for more information.

## The Maker's Serambi

A bi-monthly celebration of craftsmanship and creativity, The Maker’s Serambi: A Living House of Craftsmanship reflects Frasers House’s spirit as a House of Ateliers, bringing together artisans, makers and creators through a showcase of handcrafted works. Each edition highlights the traditions, techniques and inspirations behind their craft, offering a closer look at the people and stories behind each creation.

## InterPulse Fitness

Available at the Fitness Centre at Frasers House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Singapore, InterPulse Fitness combines Electronic Muscle Stimulation (EMS) technology with functional movement and resistance exercises for an efficient, full-body workout in 45 minutes. Using low-frequency impulses, EMS enhances the training stimulus by activating multiple major muscle groups simultaneously, engaging up to 90% of muscles at the same time to complement functional strength training.
