Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong
EMBRACE SOOTHING LUXURY AND REJUVENATION
Immerse yourself in a nostalgic experience within a vibrant city at The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong, where Art Deco elegance beautifully merges with contemporary design. Nestled on the upper levels of a stylish 58-floor tower in Lujiazui, the bustling epicenter of Shanghai's commercial, financial, and upscale retail scene, this luxurious hotel offers breathtaking views of the Bund and the iconic Oriental Pearl Tower. It's just one of the unforgettable moments that guests will cherish from their time in Shanghai.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong, with rates of ¥16.00 per hour, and ¥128.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong is Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA). SHA is located approximately 25.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
