Indulge in Italian Cuisine and Panoramic Views in Shanghai
SCENA DI ANGELO proudly welcomes Michelin-starred Italian chef Angelo Aglianó as its head chef. Guests can savor the vibrant, sun-drenched flavors of the Mediterranean while enjoying stunning views of the Bund, as the restaurant continuously elevates the authentic Italian dining experience. In November 2025, SCENA DI ANGELO was honored with the prestigious "Two Forks" award, named "Best Italian Restaurant 2026" by Gambero Rosso — Italy's leading culinary authority, often regarded as the "Italian Michelin Guide."

Enjoy Authentic Cantonese Cuisine at This Michelin-Starred Restaurant
Located on the 53rd floor of The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong, Jin Xuan Chinese Restaurant tempts guests with authentic, creative Cantonese cuisine and regional favorites in China. The Michelin and Black Pearl awarded restaurant is ideal for both of social and business gatherings. Jin Xuan was designed by the renowned interior designer and architect from Hong Kong, Steven Leung. The main dining area seats up 52 guests, while the eight private dining rooms are available for up to 40 guests.

What restaurants does The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong have on property?

Flair Rooftop Restaurant & Bar
Aura Lounge
Scena di Angelo Italian Restaurant
Jin Xuan Chinese Restaurant
Does The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong offer room service?
Yes, room service is available at The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong.
What cuisine is offered at The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong?
The cuisines offered at The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong are:
Italian at Scena di Angelo Italian Restaurant
Multiple cuisines at Aura Lounge
Asian-Fusion at Flair Rooftop Restaurant & Bar
Chinese at Jin Xuan Chinese Restaurant
