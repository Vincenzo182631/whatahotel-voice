Stay in the Heart of Shanghai Near the Oriental Pearl Tower
News Room
Overlooking the vibrant financial and commercial district, The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong is centered amid international corporations and a cultural history dating back centuries. This luxury hotel surrounds guests with more than 300 Fortune 500 companies, the Shanghai New International Expo Centre and modern attractions such as the Shanghai Ocean Aquarium. Discover the ways business and leisure come together in a place of rich traditions, modern innovations and infinite possibilities.
