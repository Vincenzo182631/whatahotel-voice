Art Deco-Inspired Accommodation
Suites & Rooms
The Ritz-Carlton Shanghai, Pudong captures the essence of the city with tastefully extravagant accommodations that overlook the Shanghai skyline. Sophisticated comfort awaits in luxury hotel rooms and suites that have been masterfully designed with modern amenities and Art Deco accents.
The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level
Reserved for those who seek the finest experience possible, The Ritz-Carlton Club Level is exceptional in every way. Located at level 49 of the hotel, the Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge features floor-to-ceiling windows, offering magnificent views of the Bund and Huangpu River. This exclusive area comprises a buffet area, a sofa lounge section, two work stations and two meeting rooms. Appealingly furnished interiors provide a perfect backdrop to enjoy martinis at the bar sitting area or experience the unmatched culinary distinction of the hotel's chefs through five food and beverage presentations daily.
