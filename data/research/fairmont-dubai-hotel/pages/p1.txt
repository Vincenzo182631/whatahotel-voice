## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Dubai located?

Fairmont Dubai is located on Sheikh Zayed Road in Dubai. Our hotel's P.O. Box number is 97555.

Is parking available at Fairmont Dubai?

Yes, there is parking available at our 5-star Dubai hotel.

Does Fairmont Dubai have a spa?

Yes. Our hotel awaits you with a luxury spa in Dubai alongside unique treatments.

Does Fairmont Dubai have a swimming pool?

Yes. Fairmont Dubai offers a swimming pool with pristine blue water to enjoy a pool day with city views.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Dubai?

Check in is at 3PM and check out is at 12PM.

Does Fairmont Dubai have a gym/fitness center?

Yes, our 5-star Dubai hotel offers a modern gym with state-of-the-art fitness facilities and equipment to keep up with your workout routine during your stay with us.

How accessible is Fairmont Dubai for business and exhibition travelers?

Fairmont Dubai offers exceptional accessibility, located directly opposite Dubai World Trade Centre and within walking distance of the World Trade Centre Metro Station, with quick access to DIFC, Downtown Dubai, and Dubai International Airport.
