Bahrain Dining With a Global Taste
Dining
The Seef District restaurants and lounges at The Ritz-Carlton, Bahrain in Manama draw on tradition, design, and setting to create distinct experiences. One draws from the modern Mexican art scene to create a vibrant space, while another complements sea views with Arabian-Mediterranean cuisine.
Changes in Restaurant Operations during Ramadan
During the Holy Month of Ramadan, we would like to inform you that there are changes to our restaurant operations. We invite you to gather with your loved ones to celebrate the spirit of the season at Masaya Pavilion. We appreciate your understanding and look forward to welcoming you.

The Great Indian Thali
Immerse your senses at Nirvana where a culinary journey awaits and our authentic Indian cuisine transports your palate to new heavens. At Nirvana, elegant arches, intricately carved wood screens, jewel-toned fabrics, chefs in crisp whites and the melodies of a live santoor player recall the decadence of dining with Indian royalty. The Manama, Bahrain restaurant meshes a fine-dining setting with traditional cuisine. Platters sizzle with grilled seafood and kebabs, and bowls are piled high with aromatic rice and curries. The restaurant also offers a weekday thali lunch and dinner.

Ta Plage Privée
Located on the resort’s private island off the coast of the Arabian Gulf, Ta Plage Privée offers the utmost in seclusion with the island’s finest and most pristine white sandy beach and the comfort of a cool and cozy tent to beat the summer heat. With an air-conditioned private tent where you can relax, along with a dedicated massage room and your very own exclusive beach, guests will have much more space for themselves while enjoying quality time with family and friends. The new Ta Plage Privée is a haven of tropical peace and tranquility, with palm trees swaying in the breeze and the rhythmic sounds of the waves complementing a well-spent day by the sea. Ta Plage Privée is available through reservations only and includes soft drinks, fresh juices, Nespresso coffee, a fruit basket, assorted mezze for up to 6 persons, and a bouquet of fresh flowers.

Day Pass
Soak up the sun on 600 meters of the white sandy beach while sipping flavorful mocktails on one of the beach cabanas. Enjoy full-day access to the beach, indoor and outdoor pools, tennis courts, gym, and more with an endless opportunity to create unforgettable memories.
