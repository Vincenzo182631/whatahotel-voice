The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level
Located on the 6th and 7th floors, the Club Level offers the comfort, convenience, and intimacy of a hotel within a hotel. Services include daily culinary offerings, complementing business travelers' and family's valuable time on vacation. The beautifully appointed Club Lounge is furnished to resemble a comfortable living area and is well-stocked with books and international magazines and newspapers. It offers panoramic views of turquoise Gulf waters, the resort's golden sand beach and lush green gardens, and the Manama skyline. A dedicated Concierge is on duty to meet any needs, from restaurant reservations to assistance with changing airline flights.

More Info
The Ritz-Carlton, Bahrain Club Lounge is an exclusive sanctuary offering panoramic views of our turquoise waters and golden sands in the comfort of your favorite spot. Whether curling up to a good read with our vast collection of book titles, appreciating the elegantly displayed art pieces from paintings to vases, an adaptable space for privacy while catching up with family and friends or sealing the next business deal in the comfort of our exclusive meeting room, we offer you a world of opportunities and endless possibilities.
Access to the Club Level
Reserve access to the Club Level now and experience the epitome of luxury. A spacious Club Room is the ideal choice for guests seeking an exclusive level of exclusivity and comfort. A Club Suite provides elegant accommodations with a spacious living room area and a partly separated bedroom. The Club Rooms and Suites are all located on the 6th and 7th floors with sweeping views of the Manama skyline and the Arabian Sea.

Culinary Journey Samplers
From the influence of the East to the West and whatever comes in between. A selection of sample dishes from our restaurants will elevate your dining experience and take you on a culinary journey like no other. Rotating daily action stations for dinner service featuring signature delicacies from our award-winning restaurants. Featured daily, also discover traditional samplers of Middle East and Bahraini foods.
Local Flavors
Bahrain was once called the ‘island of a million date palm trees’ and it’s no surprise that our signature welcome beverage at the Club Lounge is inspired by the very holy fruit of the Middle East. Dates are not only cherished for their amazing health benefits, but culturally dates are used as signs of hospitality, generosity and gratitude, and indeed the perfect welcome drink to begin your journey here.

A Dream Crowned and a Fairytale Evening at the Ritz-Carlton, Bahrain
During Line Up, the Club Lounge team learned of a young Polish guest with dreams of becoming Miss Universe. Inspired by her story, Mostafa, Haili, Pau, and the team planned a surprise fit for a queen. That evening, Denny and Avee led the family on a scenic walk to the Marina, where the young girl was crowned amidst cheers and applause. “Congratulations in advance for winning Miss Universe Poland in 10 years!” they said.
Beaming, she declared Bahrain would be her first stop if she won. Denny and Bob arranged a Villa tour, where she imagined giving future interviews. From crown to couch, her dream felt real.
A heartfelt salute to Denny, Avee, Mostafa, Haili, Pau, Bob, and the entire team for turning a simple stay into a magical memory—living out Service Values #1, #2, and #3 with heart.
