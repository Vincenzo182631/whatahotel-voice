A Kingdom Between Two Gentle Seas
Experiences
The Kingdom of Bahrain (Mamlakah al-Bahrain in Arabic) is an independent country in western Asia, comprised of 33 islands on the western side of the Arabian Gulf. It is also the smallest Arab nation in the entire Middle East. The main island, also called Bahrain, lies 24 km (15 miles) east of Saudi Arabia and 29 km (18 miles) west of Qatar. The capital and largest city is Manama, home to many luxurious beach resorts, including The Ritz-Carlton, Bahrain, the perfect place from which to explore this mesmerizing country. Set within lushly landscaped gardens and sandy beaches just steps from the sea, our Manama hotel with a private beach is a place where guests can immerse themselves in the local culture and return to a tranquil retreat with unforgettable memories of the day’s adventures.

Spa FAQ
Prior to Arrival
Who can guide me in choosing my treatment?
Unique spa combinations are specially designed to offer you a selection of our most popular spa treatments. Our Spa Reservation agents will be delighted to assist you in the planning of your perfect spa experience, including the best order for your treatments.
How can I make a reservation?
Spa services should be scheduled in advance. Reservations can be made by calling the Spa directly on: +973 1758 6808, or you can email: rc.bahrz.spa@ritzcarlton.com. A credit card guarantee is required for booking.
What if I have special health considerations?
You deserve the best possible care. We therefore request that you notify our Spa Reservation Department before booking your treatment if you have high blood pressure, allergies, any other physical ailments or disabilities, or if you are pregnant. If you have any concerns at all, please let us know.
When should I arrive?
To enhance your Spa experience, ladies please arrive 30-60 minutes before your scheduled treatment. This will allow you time to relax in the relaxation room, sauna, steam & Thalasso Pool. Gentlemen, please arrive 10 minutes before your scheduled appointment.
How will my comfort be ensured?
This is your time, and you should delight in the experience to the fullest. Whether it's the room temperature, the amount of a massage pressure, or the volume of music, notify us of any preference; during the treatment please notify your therapist immediately. We request that mobile phones and pagers be switched off in the Spa.
Are Gift Certificates available from the Spa?
You may purchase Gift Certificates for a specific dollar amount or for particular services.
Hours of Operation
The Spa is open daily from 6:30 a.m. – 10 p.m.
Treatment times are scheduled daily from 9 a.m. - 9 p.m.
Spa Policies
Is there a minimum age?
An adult must accompany all spa guests under 16 years old. Minimum age for guest to enter the spa is 16 years old.
Is Smoking allowed in the Spa?
The Spa is a non-smoking environment.
What is the cancellation policy?
To avoid a cancellation fee of 100% of the treatment price, guests may call at least 24 hours in advance if they need to reschedule, change or cancel the booking, to avoid a cancellation fee being applied.
What if I am late for my appointment?
Arriving late will simply limit the time of your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest will not be delayed. The full value of your treatment will be applied.
What do I wear in the Spa?
Bath robes, towels & slippers are available in the spa. Shorts are required for all male guests receiving treatments. In consideration of the other guests, kindly use a bathing suit when using the spa facilities.
What if I am pregnant?
While there are a few services that should be avoided during pregnancy, there are many beneficial treatments that can be enjoyed. Please notify our Spa Reservation Agents if you are pregnant when you make your appointments so that they can offer guidance. Kindly inform our therapist of any other health concerns you might have.
Where should I keep my jewelry and valuables?
Please leave such items in the safe in your hotel room or in the lockers available for your personal items in the spa facility. In an effort to maintain a tranquil environment in which to relax, we kindly ask that all mobile phones are switched to non-ringing mode and request that all mobile calls are taken in the reception area.
What about gratuities?
Gratuities are dispersed to the Spa staff members that served you during your visit. Additional gratuities are at your discretion.
Payment
How do I pay for spa services?
We accept all major credit cards and traveler's checks. You may also charge Spa services to your hotel bill.

Area and Activities
Discover Manama Attractions Both Ancient and Modern
From a launching point at one of the most luxurious hotels in Bahrain, and located near the well-known City Centre and Seef Malls, explore everything the city of Manama has to offer -- from mosques and minarets to some of the city's most treasured attractions. In true Middle Eastern style, the country is a blend of the old and the new, with dazzling skyscrapers and majestic homages to Islamic art, culture and architecture. City Centre Bahrain is actually a shopping mall, much like Seef, the favourite of many visitors to Bahrain. A city centre as such in western terms, is something that doesn't exist in Bahrain, the city is split into several different areas, each with it's own "centre", charm and heritage.

Highlighted Activities
Sea Adventures
Boat Trips
Take to the sea on our resort’s Pearl 1 boat for daily excursions that will have you exploring hidden enclaves, cruising the kingdom’s coastline, watching beautiful dolphins, and more. Tours are available for 3 to 6 hours, for up to 6 passengers and include your choice of a picnic lunch, along with bottled water and soft beverages. Trips are subject to weather and tidal conditions. Advanced reservations required.
Excursion Destinations
All Excursions are available for 3 to 6 hour periods for up to 6 passengers and include your choice of a picnic lunch, along with bottled water and soft beverages. Trips are subject to weather and the tidal conditions. Advanced reservations required.
Jarada Island
Enjoy an excursion to the secluded Jarada island, an archipelago wonder located approximately 14kms away, and one of the best sea attractions in Bahrain, with its golden sands, blue clear waters and sunshine. Maximum of 6 passengers per trip.
City Tour
Take a voyage across the North-Western coastline of Bahrain to marvel at the island’s beautiful seas and take in its majestically modern city. A modern and developing country, Bahrain has been featured on international media for its innovative architectural projects, with special focus on the “Bahrain World Trade Center.”
Dolphin Watching
Meet the island’s dolphins in a special 2 hour and 30 minute excursion into the turquoise waters of the Arabian Gulf. A fun trip for the whole family, guests can enjoy dolphin spotting and admire these beautiful creature’s impressive acrobatics.
Frequently Asked Questions
What time should we arrive?
We suggest passengers arrive 15 minutes before departure time at the Royal Beach Club entrance. Here you will be greeted by our “Pearl 1 Experience” ambassador and taken to the marina to embark on your sea journey with our resident Captain Hussain.
What should we bring?
Passengers are required to bring their original passport and/or a resident identification card. This is mandatory and required by law during your journey at sea in the Kingdom of Bahrain.
What clothing should I wear?
Passengers should wear proper bathing and swimming attire, closed shoes or sandals as well as protective sun wear, like a sun hat and glasses. SPF lotions are provided on board; however, passengers are encouraged to bring their own.

The Royal Golf Club Bahrain
International golfing legend Colin Montgomerie is responsible for the magnificent design of the 18-hole, par 72 championship golf course at the Royal Golf Club. The Montgomerie championship course offers a truly unique blend of links and desert golf, taking advantage of Bahrain’s natural rough desert terrain and combining it with vast manmade lakes. The desert feel is retained through the use of sandy waste bunkers and indigenous vegetation.
