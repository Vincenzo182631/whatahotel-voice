Ramadan Experience at Masaya Pavilion by STC
The Ritz-Carlton Bahrain is delighted to announce a visionary collaboration with STC to present a Ramadan experience that celebrates the bonds of community while embracing the enchanting allure of a Moroccan-inspired décor.

The Ritz-Carlton Bahrain launches Le Jardin
The Ritz-Carlton Bahrain takes another stride towards sustainability with the launch of Le Jardin, a unique restaurant dedicated to offering an unparalleled dining experience with a focus on sustainability and locally sourced produce.

New Enchanting Mystical Brunch at La Med
Prepare to embark on a whimsical adventure like no other with the alluring Mystical Brunch at La Med, a bewitching Friday experience that promises to enchant and delight. Guests will be transported to a realm where culinary mastery meets mystic wonders, creating an unforgettable dining experience.

Relaunching our Italian Restaurant, Primavera
Led by new Executive Sous Chef, Heros de Agostinis, who oversees all the facets of the culinary operations while at the same time putting his signature on Primavera’s plates, the relaunch will take the restaurant’s high standards to the next level making it the talk of the town.

Join Us On A Culinary Journey - Opens Ammuri
The Ritz-Carlton, Bahrain today announces the launch of its latest pop-up restaurant concept named Ammuri. The exciting addition which showcases Middle Eastern specialties brings the sixth offering to a series of pop-up concepts on the famous alfresco venue, Nikmati.
