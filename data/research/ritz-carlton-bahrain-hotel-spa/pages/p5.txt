The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton, Bahrain Club Lounge is an exclusive sanctuary offering panoramic views of our turquoise waters and golden sands in the comfort of your favorite spot. Whether curling up to a good read with our vast collection of book titles, appreciating the elegantly displayed art pieces from paintings to vases, an adaptable space for privacy while catching up with family and friends or sealing the next business deal in the comfort of our exclusive meeting room, we offer you a world of opportunities and endless possibilities.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.
Reserve Now
Club Lounge Access
Reserve access to the Club Level now and experience the epitome of luxury. A spacious Club Room is the ideal choice for guests seeking an exclusive level of exclusivity and comfort. A Club Suite provides elegant accommodations with a spacious living room area and a partly separate bedroom. The Club Rooms and Suites are all located on the 6th and 7th floors with sweeping views of the Manama skyline and the Arabian Sea.
