San Antonio Activities

Explore the Countless Things to do in San Antonio, Texas

Rich in history and bursting with energy, San Antonio, Texas offers a bit of something for everyone. Sample dynamic cuisine experience local culture and enjoy the endless array of things to do in the city. Whether you're visiting for a family vacation on a romantic weekend getaway, The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio places you close to the best things to see and do in the area.

San Antonio Zoo

Bring along the kids for an adventure to discover fascinating wildlife at the San Antonio Zoo, one of the city's most popular things to do. Feed a giraffe, get up close to a tortoise or meet an okapi for lasting memories with the family. Situated just over five miles away from The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio, the San Antonio Zoo is one of the city's most beloved destinations. Spanning 35 acres and showcasing

Alamo

Located near the Alamo, Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio offers hotel guests unbeatable access to this American landmark. Originally known as Mission San Antonio de Valero, the Alamo San Antonio once served as home to missionaries and their Indian converts. The Alamos situated under a half a mile away and can be easily reached by foot.

Fiesta San Antonio

Fiesta San Antonio began in 1891 has since evolved into one of the nation’s premier festivals boasting over 100 events. The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio welcomes you to San Antonio for Fiesta. Fiesta San Antonio began in 1891 as a one-parade event and has since evolved into one of the nation’s premier festivals.

Alamodome

Stay near the center of the action at The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio, a well-placed luxury hotel located moments from the Alamodome. Catch an exciting sporting event or world-class concert at the Alamodome, one of San Antonio's top entertainment destinations. The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio places hotel guests near the Alamodome, granting unbeatable access to the ever-changing array of special events.

Tobin Center

Located less than one mile from The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio, The Tobin Center of Performing Arts is home to San Antonio symphony, ballet and opera, and draws some of the biggest names in music and comedy, as well as a wide variety of musical productions.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Treadmills, Stairmaster, Stationary Bikes, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio?

Historic Market Square, Blue Star Arts Complex, Mission Concepción, Confluence Park, The Pearl Brewery and more are nearby The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio.
