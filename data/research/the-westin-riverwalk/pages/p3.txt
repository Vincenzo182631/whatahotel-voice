# Vibrant dining on the San Antonio Riverwalk

## Dining

Experience an array of culinary options at The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio, featuring two restaurants and a poolside bar offering a relaxing spot after your day on the Riverwalk. Step into the newly renovated Zocca Cuisine d’Italia restaurant, serving authentic Italian cuisine and robust fine wines in a relaxed, contemporary setting ideal for everything from a family meal to a romantic date night. Or savor your meal and drink outside on the restaurant patio overlooking the idyllic Riverwalk. In the morning, pick up a breakfast sandwich and a Starbucks® coffee from Cafecito, our grab-and-go restaurant for convenient and freshly made meals. After a swim, the Pool Bar provides handcrafted cocktails and bar bites in the warm sunshine of San Antonio.

Zocca Cuisine D'Italia: Join us for Happy Hour!

Enjoy Happy Hour every Monday - Friday from 3pm - 6pm at San Antonio's favorite Italian restaurant. Sit on the patio and relax with views of the River Walk. Take advantage of our special pricing on beer, house wine, cocktails and appetizers.
