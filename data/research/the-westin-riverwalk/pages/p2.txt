## Accommodations

Wake up energized at The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio. Our downtown hotel offers newly renovated, spacious accommodations with incredible views of the Riverwalk or cityscape. Inspired by San Antonio's...See More storied heritage, every room is adorned with Spanish Colonial décor, featuring rust-red colors, dark wood and iron accents. Feel refreshed after a night’s sleep in a Westin Heavenly® Bed, as the sunlight filters through your private balcony or terrace. Begin the day under your spa-style shower in a marble bathroom, followed by a fresh cup of coffee or tea brewed in your room. After exploring San Antonio, return with your furry friend to our pet-friendly accommodations and unwind with a film on your flat-screen TV or check social media using our high-speed Wi-Fi.
