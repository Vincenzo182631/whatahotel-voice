# Welcome to The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio

## Relax in style at our San Antonio Riverwalk Hotel

Perfectly placed on the famous River Walk, The Westin Riverwalk is a premier San Antonio Riverwalk hotel offering extraordinary experiences in the heart of the city. As a leading hotel in downtown San...See More Antonio, guests enjoy river-view balconies, Zocca Cuisine D'Italia's indoor-outdoor bar and patio, live music on select days, river parades, and culture-centric events. Walk to The Alamo, Spanish Governor's Palace, Aztec Theatre, world-class museums, shopping, and nightlife, or explore nearby attractions like The Pearl District and San Antonio Zoo. Our versatile event venues bring weddings and corporate gatherings to life, with select spaces overlooking the river. Relax at the outdoor pool, WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio, Collaboration Center, or serene lobby with River Walk views. As a pet-friendly hotel in San Antonio Riverwalk that visitors can enjoy with their furry companions, we welcome the whole family. ## Concierge Desk

Whether it's tickets to a Spurs game or a tee time at one of San Antonio's beautiful golf courses, our concierge is pleased to make all arrangements for you, including transportation and equipment rentals, if needed.

## Pet Friendly

We are a dog-friendly hotel. Guests are welcome to bring a canine friend weighing up to 40 pounds. We are happy to provide a Westin Heavenly Dog Bed, pet welcome kit, and food bowls with mat. Guests are required to sign a pet waiver upon check-in and the hotel reserves the right to charge the guest’s credit card for additional cleaning and excessive damage caused by their dog. Please note that dogs are the only pets allowed.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio?

The check-in time at The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio?

On-site valet parking is available at The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio?

The closest airport near The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio is San Antonio International Airport (SAT). SAT is located approximately 8.5 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Riverwalk, San Antonio does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
