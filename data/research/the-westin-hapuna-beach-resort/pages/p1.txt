# Welcome to The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort

## Enjoy Kohala Coast Wellness at Our Hapuna Beach Resort

Located on beautiful Hapuna Beach within Mauna Kea Resort, The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort offers a contemporary Hawaiian retreat on the Island of Hawaii’s renowned Kohala Coast. Surrounded by lush gardens, lava fields, and cultural landmarks, the resort blends natural beauty with modern comfort. Guests can enjoy multiple dining venues, an award-winning 18-hole Arnold Palmer-designed golf course, two ocean-view pools including an adults-only infinity pool, and direct access to one of Hawaii’s most celebrated white-sand beaches. Wellness amenities include the WestinWORKOUT Center, CrossFit Studio, and The Spa at Mauna Kea. The resort also features more than 85,000 square feet of refreshed event space, making it an ideal destination for business meetings, conferences, weddings, and social gatherings.

## History Of Hapuna

Our Hapuna Beach hotel namesake translates as "Ha" (the breath, the life) and "Puna" (the spring). Our transformed resort will beckon travelers who appreciate Hawaii’s unique perspective and Hapuna’s sense of place.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort?

The check-in time at The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 75.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort. Self parking is available for $40.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $40.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort?

The closest airport near The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort is Kona International Airport at Keahole (KOA). KOA is located approximately 22.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
