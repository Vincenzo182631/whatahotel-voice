# Relax in Tranquil Kohala Coast Suites and Guest Rooms

## Accommodations

The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort features spacious and well-appointed accommodations near Hapuna Beach including resort suites and hotel rooms, all with beautiful ocean and sunset-facing views from their...See More private lanai (balconies). Five-fixture bathrooms, a double-door system allowing breezes to flow, HD flat-screen TVs and the latest high-tech data ports and outlets are among the guestroom conveniences to meet your lodging needs.See Less

### Tropical Breezes

Our Hapuna Beach resort suites and guest rooms are designed for Hawaii’s tropical ocean breezes. A brilliant double-entry door design allows guests to open the solid door and secure an outer one, with its wooden privacy louvers, for airflow. With the lanai door also open, sweet tropical breezes, and sounds of the ocean fill the room.

## Westin Heavenly® Bed

Whether you're adjusting to a new time zone or recovering from your travels, restful sleep is the key to renewal. Dream away in the Westin Heavenly® Bed so you can meet the new day head on.

## Sleep Well Menu

In order to enhance a restful night, enjoy one of our sleep-enhancing superfoods developed in collaboration with SuperFoodsRx. Use our Sleep Well Balm amenity to ease tension and soothe the senses in the comfort of your suite or oceanview room near Hapuna Beach.
