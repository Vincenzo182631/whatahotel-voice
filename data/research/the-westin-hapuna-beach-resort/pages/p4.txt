# Improve Your Game at the Hapuna Beach Golf Course

## Experiences

The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort is set amongst the world-famous, white-sand beaches of Kohala Coast near Waimea, HI. Visit the recreation area from our hotel near Hapuna Beach State Park or take in the...See More pastoral beauty of Waimea. There's something here for everyone, including Hapuna Beach golf course.See Less

### Hapuna Golf Course

Located on the Kohala Coast, Hapuna Beach Golf Course is renowned for its lush greens framed by dramatic lava fields, sweeping ocean views, and championship-level play. Beginning at sea level and rising to 700 feet, the course winds through rugged desert terrain for an unforgettable golf experience. Hapuna Golf Course has completed its improvements, including a full turf conversion to Seashore Paspalum, which will provide more consistent playability and elevate the championship course conditions upon reopening.

Book Now

## Signature Hole

Teeing off at just under 700 feet of elevation, the signature par 4, 12th hole provides one of the most spectacular panoramic views on Hawaii's Big Island, including Maui in the distance. Enjoy teeing off on the best Hapuna Beach golf course.

## Driving Range & Facilities

Hapuna Beach Golf Course is within easy walking distance of the resort, with a full range of Hapuna Beach golf facilities and services.

### Course Details

One of the most popular Hapuna Beach golf destinations, the Arnold Palmer / Ed Seay Hapuna Beach golf course is a championship "links-style" design that was built in 1992 and stretches to just under 6,900 yards.

## Hapuna Beach

If you can tear yourself away from the Hapuna Beach golf course, we are only steps away from beautiful white sand Hapuna Beach, known as one of the best beaches in the world. An easily accessible snorkeling area called Turtle Point lets you see plenty of colorful reef fish, and often sea turtles.

## Magnificent Swimming Pools

Our resort with pools near Hapuna Beach boasts two swimming pools; a 6,800 square foot freeform family pool and an adult-exclusive infinity pool.

### Explore Hapuna

Hapuna means “spring of life” in Hawaiian and refers to the area’s underground springs. In ancient times, these were known to bubble to the surface during times of heavy rain, creating natural artesian fountains.

Resort Activity Highlights

## Ala Kahakai

A portion of the 175-mile Ala Kahakai National Historic Trail traverses across Hapuna Beach north to Kauna'oa Bay at Mauna Kea Beach Hotel Put on some sturdy shoes (running shoes or tennis shoes are fine) and take a hike.

## Hawaiian Cultural Activities

Start your day by joining our Aloha Ambassador at the Hapuna Beach Lawn. Greet the sunrise, clear your thoughts and mind, hear the Hawaiian oli (chant) and find peace within. Immerse yourself in other Hawaiian culture experiences including chanting, lei making, hula lessons and more.

## Hapuna Beach Activities Hut

In addition to all the fun to be had at our Big Island, Hawaii, golf resort, enjoy a fun and adventurous day in the ocean. Check in with our Beach Activities Hut for paddle boards, kayaks, body boards and snorkel rentals. Located on Hapuna Beach directly in front of the hotel.

## Manta Moonlight Night Snorkel

When you're not enjoying a round of Hapuna Beach golf, join manta ray researcher and advocate James Wing for a private tour with the Mauna Kea mantas. The snorkel includes in-water guide, a naturalist briefing, area orientation, safety lights, snorkeling gear, swim shirts, swim vests and towel. The activity is 75-90 minutes from start to finish.

Local Attractions

Blue Hawaiian Helicopter Tours

Capture an adventure on a spectacular “flightseeing” tour. A state-of-the-art helicopter awaits to transport you on an unforgettable aerial adventure. Travelers can explore the dramatic action at the volcano and lush green valleys and waterfalls along the way.

Stargazing

Mauna Kea’s summit is home to an international astronomy community that is the envy of stargazers worldwide. Experience a peek into worlds beyond and learn a bit about Polynesian celestial navigation that guided canoe voyagers to Hawaii for the first time with Stargaze Hawaii.

Hawaii Forest and Trail

Ignite your sense of adventure at our hotel near Hapuna Beach State Park with Hawaii volcano tours, educational bird watching tours and an inspiring Mauna Kea tour. Hawaii Forest Trail nature tours allow you to experience majestic nature in a small group, guided tour setting.

Hahalualele Sailing Canoe

Experience a one of a kind sailing adventure on board the Hahalualele, a 35 foot double hull sailing canoe. Departing from Mauna Kea Beach Hotel, the sail takes you across the coast enjoying the breathtaking scenic views while the crew share the history and the culture of the Hawaiian people.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort offers a complimentary, 5:30 AM-6:30 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, TRX system and Peloton bicycles, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa at Mauna Kea is located on-site at The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort. Click here to book a service now.

Does The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort offer golf course access nearby or at the hotel?

Yes, hotel guests have access to Hapuna Golf Course. Click here to learn more.
