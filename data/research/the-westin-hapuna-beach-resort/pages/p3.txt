# Enjoy fine dining on the Kohala Coast

## Dining

The Puako restaurants at The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort provide a taste of Kohala Coast paradise through fresh, local foods prepared and served with a touch of aloha. Enjoy Mediterranean-inspired cuisine at Merida or grab a quick bite from Piko Coffee and Bar. Our restaurants near Hapuna Beach feature menus enhanced with herbs and produce sourced from the hotel’s on-site, half-acre garden.
