## The Luxury Collection Signature Sleep Experience

At Matild Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, sleep becomes a transcendent experience. Each guestroom features the exclusive Andrea Collection by Frette—an iconic name in fine Italian linens since 1860. Crafted with exquisite precision, our 400-thread-count bed linens, jacquard duvet cover, and plush pillows offer unparalleled softness and sophistication. The signature pattern, a graceful interplay of classical heritage and modern design, evokes the grandeur of Venice and the timeless elegance of The Luxury Collection. Every bed is artfully made to perfection, promising a night of exceptional rest and regal comfort. From the altars of St. Peter’s Basilica to the suites of Matild Palace, Frette’s legacy of refinement continues to dress the world’s most remarkable beds.

### Historic Grandiose

The interior aesthetic reflects a careful balance of old world charm, classic art nouveau features and a modern new world. Highlighted with an inspiring yet calming heritage colour palette incorporating striking pops of teal, gold and copper.
