### Majestic Nights on the Top of Budapest

Hidden gem among Budapest's famed roofscape, The Duchess is a secret liquor library that shines a new perspective on Budapest vistas. Inspired by the Archduchess Maria Klotild, who commissioned the Matild Palace to include a clandestine rooftop section as a rendezvous retreat for the Decadence Movement, the uber-chic bar is accessed through a hidden entrance of private elevators that beam the guests right up to another world. The bar serves a collection of its finest liquors, unique cocktails and exceptional wines.
