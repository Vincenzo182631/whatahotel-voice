Suite Experiences
Step into a world of refined elegance as you enter our suites, where meticulous attention to detail ensures a perfect balance of luxury and comfort. Choose the suite that aligns perfectly with your needs and delight in an unforgettable stay, complete with tailored, luxurious service.
Complimentary Club Lounge Access
The Ritz-Carlton Suite
Experience the epitome of opulence with our most lavishly appointed suite, boasting a private library, gourmet kitchenette, and elegant dining area. Enjoy breathtaking views of the city skyline and majestic mountains, complemented by exclusive access to the Club Lounge for unparalleled comfort and relaxation. Elevate your stay with personalized shopping experiences and the dedicated attention of our impeccable butler service. This suite embodies sophistication and convenience with its separate meeting space and additional kitchenette. Luxuriate in the sumptuous primary bathroom and guest bathroom, completing the ultimate in refined indulgence.

The Club Level Experience
Club guests at The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty have access to the exclusive Club Lounge located on the 26th floor. Often referred to as a hotel within a hotel, a devoted Concierge inclines to individual desires in a warm atmosphere. You can enjoy spectacular views of Almaty City and the Alatau Mountains with continuous culinary offerings presented throughout the day. Guests will also enjoy a relaxed setting with a separate library, business center stations, TV room and special amenities for Club guests. The Club Lounge is open daily from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.
