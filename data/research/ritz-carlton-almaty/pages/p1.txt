Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty
Savor luxury at our 5-star hotel in Almaty.
We craft unforgettable experiences that linger in your memory long after your stay. Explore the magnificence of the Alatau Mountains, embark on thrilling hiking trails, or immerse yourself in the heritage of the city at our Almaty business district hotel. The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty is a place you can return to in your memories whenever you like, simply by closing your eyes.

Our rooms and suites are elegantly designed to ensure a splendid and pleasant stay. From the moment our guests step into their rooms, they are immersed into the atmosphere of refined elegance and sophistication. Where Modernity Meets Wonders of the Silk Road
Our 30th-floor check-in area is designed to provide a seamless and exceptional experience. A cozy lounge area awaits, where you can relax and unwind while Ladies and Gentlemen take care of the check-in procedures. Enjoy a cup of special recipe Apple tea as a gesture of Kazakh hospitality and admire the breathtaking panoramic views. Take the opportunity to try local Aport apples, which are the main symbol of Almaty city and learn about the exclusive amenities and services available to guests. ​ At The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty, we strive to familiarise our guests with Kazakh traditions and culture. That is why we offer a unique ceremonial serving of Kazakh national sweets and tea in the lobby on the 30th floor, daily from 18:30 to 19:00 hours.

Celebrate Your Dream Wedding
Our experienced wedding specialists are dedicated to assist you every step of the way. From the initial planning stages to the execution of your dream wedding, our Ladies & Gentlemen are here to ensure the special day exceeds expectations and becomes a cherished memory of a lifetime. ​Choose from two stunning venues, each with their own unique features. With its generous size and flexible layout, our ground-floor Ballroom offers a versatile space to host weddings, receptions, and other grand events. During the summer months, enjoy the Terrace for a breath of fresh air, beautiful view and refined ambience. The smaller Altitude Ballroom located on 28th floor, boasts breathtaking views of the city, can offer a private reception and bar area, as well as a private dining room for more intimate gatherings. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 10.0 kgs.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty. Self parking is complimentary for guests.
Off-site parking is also available near The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty is Almaty International Airport (ALA). ALA is located approximately 25.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty have an airport shuttle to Almaty International Airport (ALA)?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty has an airport shuttle to Almaty International Airport (ALA).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Almaty offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
