Dining Experiences
Private & In-Room Dining
Guests have the option of an extensive and delicious in-room dining menu available 24 hours. Also, the hotel can arrange for a special private dining experience on the 30th floor of the hotel for groups of ten or more. Private Dining Room is private event space featuring panoramic views of Alatau Mountains and Almaty Uptown; there is also a seasonal option to dine on the outdoor Terrace 77.
In-Flight Catering
Make your travel more enjoyable with superb dishes from our Executive Chef. Take The Ritz-Carlton Dining Experience in the air. In-flight catering is available for pre-order. 24-hour advance reservation and pre-payment is required.
