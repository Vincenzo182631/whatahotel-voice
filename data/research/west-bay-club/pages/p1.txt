# West Bay Club

## Luxury Oceanfront Resort

## Beachside Serenity

Discover the serene beauty of West Bay Club. Nestled on the widest stretch of Grace Bay Beach, one of the most celebrated beaches in the world, our intimate oceanfront resort offers just 46 accommodations, perfect for families seeking adventure and couples looking to unwind. From lounging on the beach to sailing to snorkeling, there’s no better place to experience the beauty of Turks & Caicos

## More Than a Hotel

Contemporary Caribbean style defines every studio and suite at West Bay Club, where spacious layouts, private terraces, and thoughtful luxury touches come together to create a true home away from home. From intimate studios to expansive multi-bedroom suites, each accommodation is designed with comfort and refinement in mind, featuring oceanfront living spaces, spa-inspired bathrooms, and fully equipped kitchens or kitchenettes. Whether you’re settling in for a quiet couples retreat or spreading out across 3,800 square feet with the whole family, there’s a perfect space waiting for you.

View our Rooms and Suites

## Activities & Excursions

Turks and Caicos adopted the slogan “Beautiful by Nature” for good reason, as there are countless magnificent sights to behold both on our island of Providenciales, and beyond! From short kayak tours to full-day excursions to uninhabited islands, we have a variety of options certain to please. Our pre-arrival concierge will be happy to make excursion or activity arrangements prior to your arrival, and our expert team of personal concierges will be thrilled to discuss, recommend and book any excursion you wish after you’ve arrived.

## Dining Guide

Grace Bay Resorts is home to a collection of dining experiences where every venue tells its own story. From candlelit French cuisine at Grace’s Cottage and an elevated oceanfront evening at Infiniti Restaurant to cliffside Italian dining at Vita, handcrafted cocktails in the natural stone walls of Cave Bar, and fresh seafood and wood-fired pizza by the water at Lua Beach House, there is always somewhere worth discovering. From the most intimate dinner to the most laid-back afternoon, every visit leaves you with a reason to return.
