# Wan Chai, Excitement in the heart of Hong Kong Island

## Experiences

Enjoy exceptional access to the very best of the city during your next stay at The St. Regis Hong Kong. Best explored on foot, Wan Chai district offers a wealth of heritage hideaways and modern hot spots. It is one of most chic areas in Hong Kong with a heady mix of old and new, East meets West, and where hip bars and restaurants mix with old tenement buildings and hidden temples among towering skyscrapers....See More Positioned at the heart of Hong Kong Island, our luxury hotel is within walking distance of the Hong Kong Convention & Exhibition Center and Victoria Harbour. And a close distance to Admiralty, Central and Causeway Bay where high-end luxury shopping centers are located. Allow our concierge and butler to recommend and curate a personalised Hong Kong trip, from dining, exploring, shopping, enjoying the famous Hong Kong nightlife and skyline, our butler team can assist in creating your Hong Kong memories.See Less

### Restore and Revitalise

Ignite Your Inner Sparkle. Bring back your relaxed self by indulging in exclusive and high-performance spa treatments, each beginning with thorough one-on-one consultations to ensure your needs are addressed. Leave the rest to our therapists who will then tailor and make adjustments to each experience to meet your wellness aspirations.

### Stretch and Flex

Build Up Your Strength. Take your mind off your daily routines and realise your workout plans with a variety of unparalleled cardio and weight training equipment. Talk with our fitness trainers for professional advice, and sign up for one-on-one training sessions or regular weekend programmes to accomplish your health goals.

### Dip and Splash

Freshen Up Your Mind. Rain or shine, look up to the city jungle and take a swim at our heated outdoor swimming pool, or simply soak up the sun or catch the raindrops by relaxing in our sun loungers. Order a drink or some snacks from our Verandah Pool Bar to spice up your experience.

## Get Dressed

Prepare for your wellness pursuit, or wrap up your journey on a sweet note at our relaxing dressing rooms, which include lockers, specially-designed dressing tables, Dyson hairdryers and a steam room, as well as individual basins with adjoining dressing counters.

## Relax and Recharge

Looking for an alternative to a sweaty workout? Get clad with your swimsuit and swim your stress off. The 19.5-metre long and 6.8-metre wide heated outdoor swimming pool is accompanied by relaxing sun loungers on the side and chaise lounges, redolent of a mansion’s luxuriant private garden where family and guests laze and enjoy cocktails al fresco.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Hong Kong have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Hong Kong offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Hong Kong have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Hong Kong has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Hong Kong have an on-site spa?

Yes, The Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Hong Kong.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Hong Kong?

Avenue of Stars, Hong Kong Museum of Art, Hong Kong Park, Victoria Park, sky100 Observation Deck and more are nearby The St. Regis Hong Kong.
