# Conrad Spa

Conrad Spa is a sanctuary shaped by the living traditions of the Wixárika (Huichol) people — a culture whose deep reverence for nature, sacred ceremony, and inner balance has informed healing practices for generations. Here, those ancestral rituals meet contemporary technique in treatments designed to restore harmony, quiet the mind, and renew the body. From Temazcal ceremonies and cacao rituals to bespoke massages and culturally rooted body therapies, every experience is an invitation to reconnect with yourself and the land that surrounds you.

THE DESTINATION

## Temazcal

#### A Journey With the Ancients

Meaning “house of heat,” the temazcal is a traditional dome-shaped chamber made of earthen clay. Representing the womb of Mother Earth, it has been used by pre-Hispanic cultures for curative ceremonies over thousands of years. On this deeply spiritual journey of health and purification, you’ll be led by a local shaman (healer), who warms stones on a fire outside, brings them in to warm up the temazcal, and transforms your body and spirit through heat, herbal steam, and song. The 90-minute ritual leaves you detoxified, stimulated, challenged, and connected.
