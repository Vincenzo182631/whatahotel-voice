# Explore Riviera Nayarit

**Undiscovered Mexico**

Nestled between the lush jungle and the sparkling ocean in Western Mexico, Riviera Nayarit invites you to uncover its hidden treasures and embark on an extraordinary journey. This enchanting destination stretches along nearly 200 miles of pristine beaches, encompassing nature reserves, untamed rainforests, and picturesque villages with authentic charm. Riviera Nayarit is a sanctuary for the curious traveler seeking a rich tapestry of culture and endless adventures.

Situated north of Puerto Vallarta, Riviera Nayarit is home to several of Mexico’s prized “Pueblos Mágicos” (magical towns), each with unique allure. Wander the winding cobblestone streets, immerse yourself in the vibrant local restaurants, and explore the artisan shops that showcase the region’s craftsmanship. Experience the deep-rooted traditions of healing and spirituality that permeate these communities, creating an atmosphere of profound cultural significance.

One of the most captivating features of Riviera Nayarit is its collection of uninhabited islands, with the Marietas Islands standing out as a must-visit destination. Located just a 50-minute journey from Conrad Punta de Mita, these islands offer a breathtaking escape. Explore the hidden beaches and marvel at the extraordinary natural formations, including the famous Playa Escondida (Hidden Beach), a secluded paradise accessible through a unique tunnel-like opening. Snorkel in the crystal-clear waters, discovering a vibrant underwater world teeming with marine life.

For those seeking thrilling outdoor experiences, nearby beach destinations such as Sayulita, San Francisco (San Pancho), and Playa La Lancha beckon with their distinctive allure. Sayulita, a mere 20-minute drive from the resort, is renowned as a surf mecca, attracting wave enthusiasts from around the globe. Stroll along the streets adorned with colorful banners, explore charming boutiques offering artisanal crafts, and savor the authentic flavors of local cuisine in the town’s vibrant restaurants.

Within a 30-minute drive from Conrad Punta de Mita, you’ll find the enchanting town of San Francisco, affectionately known as San Pancho. Immerse yourself in its bohemian atmosphere, discover art galleries, and embrace the town’s creative spirit. And just moments away lies Playa La Lancha, a pristine beach cherished for its unspoiled beauty and tranquil ambiance, providing an idyllic setting for relaxation and reflection.

With the Conrad Punta de Mita as your base, these captivating destinations are easily accessible, offering a plethora of unique experiences to enhance your Riviera Nayarit adventure. Immerse yourself in the wonders of this undiscovered gem, where nature, culture, and adventure intertwine to create an unforgettable journey.

### Whale Watch

From December to March, Western Mexico hosts one of the world’s magnificent wildlife spectacles: the annual whale migration. Hundreds of humpback, blue, sperm, and other whales leap, spout, and dive as they follow our shore. Watch these majestic creatures from close range on a private boat tour — and don’t forget your camera.
