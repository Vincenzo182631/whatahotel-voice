#### Embrace the Wonder

Idyllic and encompassing, the undisturbed beauty of Punta de Mita is a natural sanctuary for rest and seclusion. Surrounded by birdsong, lapping waves, and swaying trees, our rooms and suites offer an interwoven tapestry of modern design and local culture, furnished with Huichol-inspired art, private terraces, and unspoiled ocean views. All room and suite categories receive Kids Eat Free and complimentary Turi Kids Club amenities.

View Virtual Tour

## Curated Comfort by Compadre Concierge

You will enjoy the exclusive service of a dedicated Compadre. More than a host, they are your personal guide to comfort, discovery, and memorable experiences. Before arrival, they will connect to learn your preferences and special requests, crafting moments inspired by the spirit of Punta de Mita. From in-room arrivals to private dinners by the beach and culinary experiences, every detail is handled with attention to detail and care.
