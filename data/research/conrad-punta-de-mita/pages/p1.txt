## Día de Muertos

#### A celebration of color, memory, and joy on the Pacific.

The festive season opens with one of Mexico’s most revered traditions. Catrinas and candlelit altars fill the resort with color, while live face painting transforms guests into the iconic figures of the holiday. Artisan pop-ups bring regional sweets, Wixárika craftsmanship, and whimsical alebrijes inspired by the stories they carry. As evening falls, music and the scent of marigold move through the resort, and families gather beneath the stars to close the day together.

## Where the Jungle Meets the Sea

Located on the Pacific coast of mainland Mexico, not far from Sayulita, Punta de Mita is an intimate community surrounded by coastal rainforest, a vibrant local culture, sleepy fishing villages, and beautiful, undisturbed nature.
