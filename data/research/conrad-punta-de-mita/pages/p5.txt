# Elevated Mexican & Italian Cuisine

## Arbol

Árbol now offers two distinct culinary experiences in one inviting setting. Mornings feature a lavish Mexican buffet breakfast and à la carte selections, showcasing favorites such as chilaquiles, huevos rancheros, and made-to-order tortillas crafted with fresh, local ingredients. In the evening, Árbol becomes Árbol Cucina, a seasonal Italian pop-up celebrating the rich traditions of Italian cuisine with handcrafted pastas, pristine local seafood, and seasonal vegetables prepared with timeless techniques and modern creativity.
