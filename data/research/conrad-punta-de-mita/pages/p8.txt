# Dia de Muertos

### at Conrad Punta de Mita

Step into the vibrant spirit of Día de Muertos, where tradition comes alive in a tapestry of color and remembrance. Over three days, the resort fills with marigold, copal, and the music that defines one of Mexico’s most beloved holidays.

The celebration unfolds with Catrinas and luminous altars, live face painting for guests of every age, and artisan pop-ups at Colibri showcasing Huichol craftsmanship, alebrijes, and regional sweets. Younger guests have a season of their own, from costume workshops to a sweet hunt through the resort. Each evening closes on the beach with a ceremony honoring those who came before.

## A Table Set   by Memory

#### The dishes that live in Mexican homes

Through the first week of November, Árbol opens each morning with the food that anchors this holiday. Moles, tamales, barbacoa, pipian, pozole, and arroz con leche, prepared by Chef Elba and the Árbol culinary team. At Colibri, traditional Pan de Muerto is baked fresh with notes of orange, cinnamon, and vanilla, shaped in bone and marigold. On the final night, Árbol and Tuki close the celebration with cocktails, music, and an evening built around the culture the holiday belongs to.

## Experiences to Discover

From artistry to tradition, each experience is designed to engage guests of all ages and immerse them in the spirit of Día de Muertos.

### The Altar Reveal

The centerpiece of the celebration. In collaboration with José Cuervo, guests gather for the unveiling of the resort’s altar, with cocktails, canapés, and a first look at a tradition centuries in the making.

### Huichol Art and Alebrijes

Local artisans share their craft at Colibri, with intricate beadwork, vivid alebrijes, and artisanal sweets offering a window into the traditions of the region.

### Face Painting

Children and adults are invited to transform into Catrinas and Catrines, the iconic figures of the holiday.

### Family Traditions

Costume workshops, a sweet hunt through the resort, and open-air cinema under the stars, with programming built for younger guests across all three days.

### The Catrina Procession

A symbolic parade through the lobby, where each department of the resort presents its own interpretation of the Catrina.

### Sunset Ceremony

Each evening closes on the beach with a ceremony of gratitude and remembrance, as the light goes and the day is given back.
