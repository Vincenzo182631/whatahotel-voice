## Toes in the Sand, Under the Stars

# Mezquite

Mezquite wood burns hot, grilling the most delicious steak, seafood, chicken, and more with oceanfront views and palm-laden skies. Offering reimagined Mexican and American favorites, Mezquite’s open-air setting and seasonal menu put the element of fire and smoke at the forefront, grilling each dish to perfection. Open for all-day dining, from breakfast through dinner and evening cocktails.
