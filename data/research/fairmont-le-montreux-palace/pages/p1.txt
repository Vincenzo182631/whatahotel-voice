## THE _pearl_ OF THE SWISS RIVIERA

Fairmont Le Montreux Palace invites you to indulge in timeless elegance, set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Alps and Lake Geneva. This iconic Belle Époque hotel, established in 1906, offers effortless access to the treasures of the Swiss Riviera and its natural splendor. Discover unparalleled hospitality as you explore Montreux’s vibrant culture and scenic landscapes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Le Montreux Palace?

Check-in is at 3:00 pm and check-out is at 12:00 pm.

Does Fairmont Le Montreux Palace have a spa?

Our luxury hotel offers a 2000 sqm Fairmont Spa open daily from 6:30 am to 10:00 pm.

What is the closest airport to Fairmont Le Montreux Palace?

The Geneva Airport is located 1h by train or car from the hotel.

Is parking available at Fairmont Le Montreux Palace?

Yes, an indoor parking is available at CHF 42.- per day (CHF 48.- valet parking).

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Le Montreux Palace?

We offer accessible hotel rooms and all of our restaurants and spa are accessible to ensure all guests have a wonderful stay with us.

Is there a concierge service at Fairmont Le Montreux Palace?

Our knowledgeable concierges are here to help you in any way possible throughout your stay. Recommendations, reservations and transportation assistance, they know the region and all its secrets. Concierge.montreux@fairmont.com or +41 21 962 11 65.
