SPA MENU

For guests of Trump International Hotel™ Las Vegas, rejuvenation is never far away. Whether in search of an extended getaway or a day spa experience, The Spa at Trump® is a serene sanctuary featuring luxurious treatment rooms with services for women, men, and couples.

Pamper yourself with a relaxing full-body massage, clarifying or detoxing facial, soothing body treatment or one of the many other ways to recover from the excitement of Las Vegas.

**Spa Policy for GuestsUnder 18**

Guests under the age of 18 must have a Minor Consent Form completed and signed by a parent or legal guardian.

Guests ages 12 to 16 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian during their service and will be scheduled with a same-gender service provider.

Guests under 12 are welcome to enjoy salon and nail services only.

* * *

**Spa Arrival and ParkingInformation**The Spa at Trump is conveniently located on the Pool Level and can be easily accessed via the main elevators from the hotel lobby. Guests enjoy the added convenience of complimentary valet parking at the hotel.

We recommend arriving 30 minutes before your appointment so you can unwind and fully enjoy your spa experience.
