# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self Parking

Grand Hyatt Macau is pleased to offer our guests the convenience of indoor self-parking. For your convenience, we have 150 secure parking spaces available. Visitors can enjoy 3 hours of free parking per day and MOP 25 per hour thereafter. Please proceed to the entrance of the City of Dreams parking lot and follow the signs to the nearest parking area at Grand Hyatt Macau.

## Transportation

Getting here

Transportation Options

City of Dreams Shuttle Bus

Complimentary shuttle service via City of Dreams Shuttle Bus >> Access to Macau International Airport, Border Gate, Taipa Ferry Terminal, Hengqin Border, Studio City and Altira. To schedule, please contact the hotel at +853 8868 1234

Taipa Ferry Terminal4 kilometers4 kilometers

The hotel is about a 15-minute drive from the Taipa Ferry Terminal.​

Getting around

Limousine Services

Grand Hyatt Macau provides limousine service for all guests, whether traveling to and from the airport or within Macau. Guests can choose from a Maybach, a Mercedes-Benz S500 or a multipurpose vehicle. For traveling to Taipa, Cotai, the airport or the Taipa Ferry Terminal: MOP 460/hour (MOP 550/hour from 11 p.m.–8 a.m.) For traveling to Macau Peninsula, Macau Ferry Terminal, Coloane or Hengqin: MOP 550/hour (Maybach: MOP 1,200/hour) Cross-border limousine service provides high-quality transfer among Macau and Chinese Mainland. To reserve your limousine, please contact our Concierge at +853 8868 1234.​

Macau Light Rapid Transit (MLRT)

Grand Hyatt Macau is located near the MLRT Cotai East Station, exit A, and just eight minutes from the Taipa Ferry Terminal and Macau International Airport. MLRT service hours: 6:30 a.m.–11:15 p.m. Ticket fare from Macau International Airport station to Cotai East station is MOP 6 for adults and MOP3 for children, seniors and people with disabilities. Ticket fare from Taipa Ferry Terminal station to Cotai East station is MOP 8 for adults and MOP 4 for children, seniors and people with disabilities.

Taxis

Taxis are readily available at the entrance of our hotel 24 hours a day.
