## EXPLORE

Outdoor Pool

Grand Hyatt Macau features a 40-metre, temperature-controlled outdoor pool that is open year-round. Guests can relax on the lounge chairs while enjoying refreshments available on the pool menu. Lifeguards are on duty during service hours, making this an excellent venue for families.​

Hours​​

8:00 AM to 7:00 PM​​

Fitness Centre

Our spacious 24-hour fitness centre in Grand Hyatt Macau features free weights and the latest cardio and resistance machines, equipped with multimedia docking and personalised LCD screens. ​​

Isala Spa

Spread over approximately 25,000 square feet (2,323 square meters), Isala Spa includes 15 treatment rooms and suites, six of which are designed for couples. Each spa suite includes a private bathroom and the majority feature natural daylight. Elsewhere in the spa, guests can indulge in outdoor hot tubs, steam rooms and freeze showers. There is also a dedicated foot massage room overlooking private landscaped gardens.

## EXPLORE MACAU

A stay at Grand Hyatt Macau puts you close to the area’s best culture, dining, shopping and entertainment. Learn more about the city’s ancient culture by visiting A-Ma Temple or Mandarin’s House. Shop and dine at Senado Square, Macau’s urban centre, or attend a conference at Macau Tower Convention and Entertainment Centre. For family-friendly attractions, take the kids to Macao Giant Panda Pavilion, less than 4 kilometres from our hotel.
