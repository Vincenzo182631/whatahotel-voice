### Our Neighborhood: Cherry Creek

One of Denver's most premier destinations, Cherry Creek is filled with an abundance of sophisticated shops and restaurants. Home to the annual Cherry Creek Arts Festival, boutique shops at Cherry Creek North and luxury stores in Cherry Creek Shopping Center, visitors are sure to fine their muse at every turn – especially when they stay at Hotel Clio.

### Cherry Creek North

Step outside our doors and find yourself in Denver's premier outdoor retail and dining destination with an impressive collection of art galleries.

### Cherry Creek Shopping Center

Refresh your look for the season, featuring 160 stores and multiple dining outlets, Cherry Creek Shopping Center is Denver's epicenter for entertainment.

### Annual Events

Cherry Creek is Denver's epicenter for entertainment with annual events including Cherry Creek Art Festival, Sidewalk Sale, Cherry Creek Farmers Market and Cherry Creek North Food & Wine.

### Explore the Mile High City

Sitting one mile or 5,280 feet above sea level, Colorado's capital city is often referred to as the Mile High City and provides visitors with endless opportunities for urban exploration with a vibrant cultural, food and sports scene.

### Coors Field

Located in nearby downtown Denver, Coors Field is Colorado's home for Major League Baseball. At 5,200-feet above sea level, Coors Field is the highest park in the majors, giving it the reputation as a homerun-friendly stadium.

### Red Rocks Park Amphitheater

Located 30 minutes from Cherry Creek, Red Rocks Amphitheater is a top-destination for concert-goers and outdoor enthusiasts. Whether you're visiting to see a concert, watch a film, hike, or dine, there's no experience more quintessentially Colorado than a trip to Red Rocks.

### Denver Botanic Gardens

Walking distance from Hotel Clio, the Denver Botanic Gardens is a 23-acre park located in the heart of the city. This world-renowned destination showcases a wide array of plants set within a variety of gardens, including a traditional Japanese stroll garden, a tropical greenhouse, a perennial walk, and the hands-on, family-friendly Mordecai Children’s Garden.

### Empower Field at Mile High

Empower Field, home of the NFL’s Denver Broncos, is minutes from downtown Denver and hosts a variety of events, concerts and sporting events year-long.

### Cherry Creek Trail

A 40-mile trail, crossing through the heart of Denver, Cherry Creek Trail is a popular destination for bikers, joggers, and visitors alike. The sunken Cherry Creek Trail passes through the heart of the Cherry Creek neighborhood and provides access to a number of city parks, including Alamo Placita Park and Four Mile Historic Park.

### Premier Art Destination

Discover Denver’s prolific art scene by exploring a vast array of galleries and museums, including Denver Art Museum which features an extensive collection of over 70,000 works of arts; Meow Wolf Denver’s permanent and interactive installation Convergence Station; and Clyfford Still Museum, a space dedicated to showcasing the depth of this 20th century expressionist painter.
