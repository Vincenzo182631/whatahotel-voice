## Frequently Asked Questions

Does Fairmont St Andrews have a swimming pool?

Yes, our luxury Fairmont Spa is home to a 16-metre indoor pool. Along with an aromatherapy steam room, sauna and jacuzzi.

Does Fairmont St Andrews have a spa?

Our luxury Spa offers an inspired collection of personalised treatments hosted in one of our 10 treatment rooms. Indulge in massages and facials enhanced by products from ESPA Skincare and the Hebridean luxury brand ishga.

Is there a concierge service at Fairmont St Andrews?

Our concierge team will be delighted to assist with making your 5-star stay seamless and memorable while you visit our golf resort and help you explore the local areas around St Andrews.

Does Fairmont St Andrews have an airport shuttle?

Our resort transport department operates an airport 'Meet and Greet' service, private transfers and tours with our fleet of executive vehicles. We require 24 hours for any transport requests. Email transport.standrews@fairmont.com to book.

Is Fairmont St Andrews pet-friendly?

We're delighted to welcome your dog. We recommend the stunning beaches to let your dogs run free or a free-play at St Andrews Dog Run. Pets are allowed in the Lobby, Atrium and hallways alongside certain pet-friendly rooms - for full details email: standrews.scotland@fairmont.com

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont St Andrews?

Check in from 4:00 pm. Check out id by 12:00 pm.

We require a Credit Card / Debit Card to be provided upon arrival. This is to ensure any expenses charged back to the room during your stay. A £50/per day/per day authorization will be held and released upon check-out.
