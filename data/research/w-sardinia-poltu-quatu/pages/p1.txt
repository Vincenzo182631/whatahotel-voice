# Welcome to W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu

## ROOTED ENERGY

Sardinia is a destination unmatched in its location, history and iconic beauty. W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu is just that: an icon that is remembered, revisited and reposted. Surrounded by a unique landscape and rich history, our Sardinia resort hotel is nestled among the charming village of Poltu Quatu (which means “hidden port” in local Sardinian language) and located at the edge of a buzzy marina, minutes...See More aways from beaches with pristine turquoise waters alongside natural sculptures of cliff sides rocks. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu?

The check-in time at W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 10.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu?

On-site parking is available at W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu booking.

What is the closest airport near W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu?

The closest airport near W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu is Olbia Costa Smeralda Airport (OLB). OLB is located approximately 34.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Sardinia - Poltu Quatu has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
