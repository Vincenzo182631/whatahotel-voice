## The “Hidden Port” of Sardinia

The name Poltu Quatu translates from the local Sardinian dialect as “hidden port”. Nestled within a natural fjord of granite cliffs, the village remains hidden from view when passing the entrance from the sea, revealing itself only once you sail inside. This charming destination is alive with energy and is home to signature restaurants, stylish shops, and vibrant entertainment, inviting you to explore and indulge.

## Reachable by Boat

Just a few steps from our hotel lies the charming Poltu Quatu Marina, the perfect departure point for exploring the breathtaking La Maddalena Archipelago. With a short boat ride, you can reach this extraordinary national park — a sanctuary where unspoiled nature reigns and the sea reveals its most vibrant shades of turquoise.

Reach out to our Whatever/Whenever team for more information or assistance to plan your visit.

## Fjord: preferred nautical partner

There is no better way to discover Sardinia than by sea with FJORD, our preferred nautical partner! With a distinctive design, smooth performance and exceptional comfort, the FJORD 41 XL redefines the art of cruising. Designed for those who appreciate style and freedom at sea, it offers the perfect way to explore Sardinia’s spectacular coastline, anchor in hidden coves and experience the island from a truly privileged perspective. A seamless blend of style, privacy and pure Mediterranean freedom.

For any information or request, please reach out to our concierge team.

## PIRELLI 30: Experience the Sea in Style

There is no better way to discover the coastline than from the sea, aboard the PIRELLI 30. With its distinctive design, dynamic performance and refined comfort, the PIRELLI 30 interprets navigation with character and unmistakable Italian style. Designed for those who appreciate freedom, excitement, and the pleasure of life at sea, it brings together performance, design and Mediterranean lifestyle in an experience with an authentically PIRELLI character.

## Liquid Art System: Resident Art Gallery

Discover a curated journey through Liquid Art System, where internationally recognized masterpieces transform the hotel into a living, diffused gallery. From poetic bronze sculptures to dreamlike paintings, each collectible piece creates a dialogue between contemporary art and the Mediterranean soul — an invitation to start a new conversation with beauty.

### Wine Experience

Experience the rich wine heritage of northern Sardinia with visits to local vineyards and wineries. Explore the vineyards, meet the winemakers, and savor a selection of wines that capture the unique flavors of the land. An authentic journey into the traditions and tastes of Sardinian viticulture.

### Cooking Classes

In our cooking classes, you’ll immerse yourself in the island’s culinary traditions, learning the techniques and secrets behind our most iconic dishes. Guided by expert hands, you’ll explore local ingredients, age-old recipes, and the stories that have shaped Sardinian cuisine for generations — turning each preparation into a true taste of the island.

### The Shepherd’s Table

Step into the heart of Sardinian tradition with a tasting experience led by a local shepherd. Surrounded by nature, you’ll discover the island’s most authentic flavors - from handcrafted cheeses to time-honored specialties - all prepared and shared in their natural environment. It’s a moment of connection with the land, its people, and the timeless rhythms that define Sardinian rural life.

### The Open-Air Market of San Pantaleo

Step into the lively atmosphere of San Pantaleo’s open-air market, where tradition, color and flavor come together. Stroll through stalls brimming with local crafts, fresh produce, artisanal goods and unique souvenirs. This is more than a market — it’s a glimpse into Sardinian culture, where artisans and locals share their stories, skills, and passion. A perfect way to experience the authenticity of island life.

### The Nuragic Heritage of Arzachena

Step back in time and explore the fascinating nuragic landscape of Arzachena, where ancient stone towers and monumental tombs tell the story of a remarkable civilization. Highlights include Nuraghe La Prisgiona and the Giant’s Tombs of Coddu 'Ecchiu, sites that offer a unique glimpse into Sardinia’s prehistoric past and the traditions of its earliest inhabitants.

### Pevero Health Trail

Pevero Health Trail is a scenic outdoor route that winds through Mediterranean landscapes. Ideal for both active pursuits and slow-paced exploration, it welcomes runners, hikers, mountain bikers, and anyone looking to enjoy peaceful paths surrounded by native vegetation, panoramic viewpoints and traces of traditional rural life. A natural escape where movement and landscape come together in perfect harmony.
