# Soothing Tones and Views of Natural Beauty

## Accommodations

At W Sardinia – Poltu Quatu, our Sardinia accommodations combine comfort, style, and stunning views. From elegant Deluxe and Studio Suites with private terraces to charming Superior Rooms, each space is crafted for a refined and relaxing escape.

Contact Us

For assistance with reservations, our dedicated Reservations team will be pleased to assist you at reservations.wsardinia@whotels.com. For personalized arrangements and to curate every detail of your upcoming stay, our Concierge awaits you at concierge.wsardinia@whotels.com.

### Deluxe Suite

The Deluxe Suite offers spacious living areas designed for comfort and luxury. Featuring a modern yet elegant style, the suite is refined with design elements inspired by the warm tones of the earth. This harmonious blend creates an inviting atmosphere where sophistication meets natural beauty, perfect for a relaxing and memorable stay.
