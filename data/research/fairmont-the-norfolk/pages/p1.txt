## Nairobi's most _iconic_, luxurious and storied hotel

Uncover the blend of history and luxury at Fairmont The Norfolk, nestled along Harry Thuku Road, just 10 minutes from the airport. As Nairobi's most historic and luxurious hotel, immerse yourself in one of the most vibrant cities in the world. From city safari game drives and thrilling nightlife to African fashion pop-up markets and historic city tours.

### Embark on unrivaled comfort while exploring Nairobi's rich history

Book your Nairobi getaway at Kenya’s most historic hotel. At 121 years old, Fairmont The Norfolk continues to offer luxury and exceptional experiences. Choose from 125 richly appointed guest rooms and suites, including the elevated Fairmont Gold experience. Surrounded by lush tropical gardens, Fairmont The Norfolk invites you to savour exceptional dining, unwind in refined comfort, and experience the charm of Nairobi's most iconic city retreat.
