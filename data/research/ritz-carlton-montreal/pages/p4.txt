Retreat to Spa St. James in This Downtown Montreal Hotel
Spa
Waterfalls, fireplaces, and an indoor saltwater pool overlooking Montreal set the scene at our spa in downtown Montreal. Treatments are luxurious in both experience and products, featuring celebrated skincare lines and natural ingredients including Canadian maple sugar and banana leaves. Signature therapies include an aromatherapy escape for couples and a results-driven, nonsurgical facial that uses safe, powerful technology. A salon and nail suite, relaxation lounge, steam room, and sauna are also featured.

Montréal Maple Sugar Massage

Using maple balm derived from organic vegetable oils like apricot, natural maple aromas and antioxidants. This decadent massage incorporates reflexology, hot stones, and a warm oil scalp massage. A pleasantly sweet regional treat that is as therapeutic as it is luxurious.

The St James Signature ‘Facelift’
That’s right: no surgery needed. This non-surgical facelift is a facial muscles workout. With a very gentle micro-current, our skilled technician will re-educate your muscles, leaving you with a refreshed and rejuvenated look. Added bonus? Your body will be completely destressed too.
