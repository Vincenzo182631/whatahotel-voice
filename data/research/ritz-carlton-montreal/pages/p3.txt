Banquets
Pairing polished service with elegant meeting space, the Ritz-Carlton Montreal is the ideal venue for meetings and celebrations. Our restored spaces in our downtown Montreal hotel marry historic charm with modern elegance, appealing to both traditional and contemporary ceremonies. The downtown hotel offers 12,000 square feet of event space with the flexibility to host an intimate board meeting for 12 or a gala for 300. Our team is at your disposal to make your events unforgettable. With many years of experience, our team will create banquets that live up to your standards. The Ritz-Carlton Montreal, along with its partners and local producers, is committed to offering high-quality dishes, using products, meat and poultry from environmentally responsible agriculture and farming.
French Cuisine Crafted With Local Ingredients in Montreal
Legendary chef Daniel Boulud created his namesake Maison Boulud to reinterpret French classics. At his downtown Montreal restaurant, artisanal Quebecois products and local ingredients are used to craft modern dishes. The fine dining setting includes a terrace overlooking Sherbrooke Street, a year-round greenhouse with a view of the open kitchen, an inviting bar and private dining.

Romain Valicon, Executive Chef
Originally from Lyon, Chef Romain Valicon begun his formidable career with Christian Têtedoie, Gilles Maysonnave, Daniel Ancel and the renowned Paul Bocuse. From his time in the Caribbean, Spain, France, and cruise ships he was elected to the Culinary Academy of France and named Chef of the year, then won his 4 AAA diamonds for his Bamboo restaurant in Punta Cana. In 2022 he became Maître Cuisinier de France and Joseph Favre Gold Medalist before joining the kitchens of the Ritz-Carlton in Montreal.

Claude Guérin, Pastry Chef
Claude Guérin is from the Ain department in France, near the Swiss border. Born into a family restaurant business, she knew from a young age that she too wanted to work in the restaurant industry. Thanks to her rigour and her creativity, she produces creations that combine taste, design and beauty. Joining the Ritz-Carlton Montreal in 2014, she continues to bring her experience, creativity and inventiveness to its kitchens. Through diverse creations, Claude Guérin demonstrates a talent in every aspect of high-end pastry-making. In 2018, she received the title of Best Pastry Chef in Canada by Canada's 100 Best.
