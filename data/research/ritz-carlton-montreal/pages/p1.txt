Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal
Historic luxury hotel in downtown Montreal Canada
When it opened in 1912, The Ritz-Carlton Montreal set the standard for luxury in Canada. The Ritz-Carlton Montreal is indeed the only AAA Five Diamond hotel in Quebec and one of the city's most esteemed five-star hotels in Montreal. Year after year, The Ritz-Carlton Montreal has achieved a captivating balance between tradition and innovation. More than 110 years and a $200 million renovation later, our historic hotel in Montreal, Canada, offers a striking blend of modern touches with historic elements. The Ritz-Carlton Montreal invites you to experience the comfort and luxury of the most elegant address in the city. A European-inspired retreat, our luxury hotel in Montreal features French cuisine prepared with local products at Maison Boulud, afternoon tea served in the beautiful Palm Court where numerous details from 1912 remain, a spa with advanced treatments, and an indoor saltwater pool with a skyline view. Historic Montreal Hotel
In downtown Montreal, The Ritz-Carlton is an extension of the city itself: a modern tech hub with European-inspired charm. This luxury hotel downtown Montreal is an invitation to experience the attention and indulgences of a past era, set within a landmark building. Canada's influence is seen throughout: spa treatments featuring maple sugar, local artists displayed at the hotel's Cosner Art Gallery, and regional products showcased in dishes at Maison Boulud.

Cosner Art Gallery
The Cosner Art Gallery at the Ritz-Carlton Montreal presents the great Canadian painters to the guests of the hotel. Come admire the works of the Masters who shaped the history of art in Canada. The paintings of painters such as Jean-Paul Riopelle, Marc-Aurèle Fortin or Alexander Jackson alongside International artists. Located between the walls of the Ritz-Carlton Montreal, Galerie Cosner is right in the heart of the historic Montreal area recognized for art across Canada. The members of the art gallery will be happy to guide you and inform you about the different works presented. Cosner Gallery can deliver anywhere in the world.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 22.6 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal. Self parking is available for $55.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $55.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal is Montreal-Trudeau International Airport (YUL). YUL is located approximately 18.9 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
