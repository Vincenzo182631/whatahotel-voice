Why book?
With a tony address on rue Sherbrooke, all the luxury you’d expect from a Ritz-Carlton, and a pedigree that few hotels can match, this property delivers a sense of occasion that represents the best of Montreal.
Set the scene
Amidst the hustle and bustle of rue Sherbrooke, your taxi pulls up to a glittering, gilded porte-cochère, and a doorman expertly opens the door, whisks away your baggage, and directs you through the Palm Court to the check-in desk. From here on out, you’re greeted by name and a warm smile, and every need is attended to. This is not just another high-end Montreal hotel (of which there are many). This is a Ritz-Carlton—incredibly refined, but always approachable.
The backstory
Opened on New Year's Eve 1912, this was the first Ritz-Carlton in North America and remains one of the most luxurious hotels in the province of Québec. It’s been through many renovations throughout the years, all of which have honored its Belle Èpoque bones while updating everything behind the scenes, ensuring the hotel feels relevant without sacrificing its historical pedigree. It’s also the birthplace of Ritz-Carlton Afternoon Tea—now a global ritual for the brand.
The rooms
All 96 rooms and 33 suites have been wonderfully updated with things like proximity key cards and bedside control panels. The palette is warm and serene—lots of creams, teals, eggplants, and great windows looking out on the surrounding cityscape. Marble bathrooms come with heated floors, Toto electronic toilets, and decadent soaking tubs—all crucial for a luxury experience in the winter months. Looking to make an impression? Book the Royal Suite: at 4,700 square feet with 12 rooms, it’s the largest in the city.
Food and drink
Everything here is on the fancier, more refined end—from the elevated French fare at Maison Boulud, to the Dom Pérignon by the flute served at the Champagne bar in the Palm Court. Twenty-four-hour in-room dining ensures you’ll never have to go hungry, but where’s the fun in that? We suggest posting up in the Palm Court, ordering some drinks and snacks, and observing as Montreal’s glitterati float on by. The people-watching here is fantastic.
The spa
Spa St. James offers some fantastic, exclusive treatments that feel right at home here, like a results-driven “High Performance Facial” or the Maple Sugar Nail Treatment.
The neighborhood/area
You’re in the center of the Golden Square Mile here, with museums, luxury shopping, nightlife, and McGill University all within mere blocks—and winter travelers will appreciate nearby access to Montréal’s underground city.
The service
Impeccable and intuitive. There’s a reason companies like Apple turn to Ritz-Carlton to learn a thing or two about exceptional service.
Eco effort
This hotel takes sustainability seriously—something you don’t always see from heritage hotels–by using smart lighting and temperature regulation in guest rooms.
Accessibility
The building has been fully modernized several times over since its 1912 debut, so there are fully accessible rooms, elevators, and public spaces throughout.
Anything left to mention?
Don’t miss the hotel’s best-kept secret: a luxurious rooftop heated saltwater pool with fabulous views of downtown.
