The St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort, Puerto Rico
Newly renovated and everything you've come to expect from a St. Regis.

Set the scene for us: What's this resort like?
Twinkling, polished elegance pervades the entire St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort oceanfront complex, where pathways wind through perfectly manicured gardens, opening into an expansive, two-mile long beach.
What's the story behind the St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort?
Built on a former sugar cane and coconut farm, the St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort originally opened in 2010. The property is committed to conservation, as it's a certified Audubon sanctuary (so keep an eye out for
Puerto Rican
parrots and other exotic birds) and hosts a program to rehabilitate the Leatherback Turtle population.
How about the rooms themselves—what are they like?
The St. Regis's 139 dolphin-gray and sand-dollar-white rooms were designed by celebrated local designer Nono Maldonado. You could hold a convention in the giant bathrooms, which have rain showers and soaking tubs. Terraces add to the already-generous space.
Is there a charge for Wi-Fi?
Wi-Fi is part of the daily $99 resort fee.
What are our best dining options on-site?
Seagrapes is the casual, poolside option; Paros, the upscale Mediterranean restaurant. In the lobby bar, “The Long Awaited Voyage," a mesmerizing mural by local artist Arnaldo Roche Rabell, stands as symbol of hope and optimism not only because of its vibrant colors, but because the work was impeccably restored after hurricane damage. Try the local spin on the bloody Mary, served with crushed plantain chips, and don’t miss the nightly Champagne sabering, a regular occurrence at St. Regis properties all over the world.
How's the service?
Friendly white-glove service.
What type of travelers will you find here?
Well-heeled guests dressed in requisite Ralph Lauren.
What is there to see in the surrounding area?
The hotel is a 35-minute drive from SJU airport. Lakes for boating and fishing are just a 10-minute hop away, while El Yunque rainforest is a 20-minute drive away.
Anything else we should be sure to check out while staying here?
The Iridium spa has a Jacuzzi, steam rooms, cold pools, a sauna, and yoga and Pilates classes. The massage villas incorporate rum, coffee, and tropical fruits into treatments. Other highlights: the sparkling, curving pool; a Robert Trent Jones Jr.–designed golf course; a boathouse with kayaking and paddleboarding; pickleball courts; and the Tortuga Kid’s Club.
What's the bottom line?
Top-notch service and seclusion that comes at a price—and it should.
