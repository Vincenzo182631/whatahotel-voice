# Savor authentic Colorado cuisine in our fine dining restaurants

## Dining

The dining experiences at The St. Regis Aspen Resort are a celebration of Colorado’s vibrant flavors and a culinary reflection of our iconic mountain destination. Our Aspen Mountain restaurant, Velvet Buck, offers modern mountain cuisine with a contemporary twist; Mountain Social Bar & Lounge serves bespoke cocktails alongside an elevated menu; and The Snow Lodge combines fine dining with curated musical experiences.
