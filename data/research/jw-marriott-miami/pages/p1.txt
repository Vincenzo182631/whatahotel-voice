# Welcome to JW Marriott Miami

## Explore our hotel in Brickell, Miami's trendy neighborhood

The JW Marriott Miami is located in the heart of Brickell, Miami's hot destination for dining, shopping, and nightlife, and is a short walk from the popular shopping and entertainment destination, Brickell City Centre. The sophisticated luxury hotel in Brickell, Miami, boasts 296 guest rooms, the latest technology, and over 25,000 sq ft of flexible meeting space. Guests are not far from all that Miami...See More has to offer, including the Perez Art Museum, Frost Science Museum, Kaseya Center, home of the Miami Heat, Adrienne Arsht Center, Port of Miami, South Beach, Coral Gables, Coconut Grove, and Miami International Airport. The first JW Marriott hotel outside of Washington, DC, the iconic property is complete with a British-styled pub on the lobby level, and a casual al fresco restaurant overlooking Brickell Avenue for breakfast, lunch, or dinner. Guests will also enjoy the stylish stainless steel pool on the 7th floor.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Miami?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Miami is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Miami allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Miami.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Miami?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Miami. The daily valet fee is $76.29 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Miami have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Miami has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Miami booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Miami?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Miami is Miami International Airport (MIA). MIA is located approximately 8.2 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Miami have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Miami does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
