### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Miami have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Miami offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Miami have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Miami has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Miami?

Mary Brickell Village, Kaseya Center - Home of the NBA Miami Heat, Frost Museum of Science, Port of Miami, South Beach/ Art Deco District and more are nearby JW Marriott Miami.
