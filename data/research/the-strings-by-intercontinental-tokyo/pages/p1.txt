- Notice of Hotel Name Change

Our hotel name will change effective December 1, 2026.

Previous Name: The Strings by InterContinental Tokyo

New Name: InterContinental Tokyo Shinagawa

Existing reservations remain unchanged and guests may enjoy their stay as booked.

The new name will be updated on our official website and reservation platforms in due course.

Thank you for your continued support.
- Changes to the Accommodation Tax

Due to revisions to the Tokyo Metropolitan Accommodation Tax system, the tax will change for stays from April 1, 2027, regardless of the reservation date.

■ Through March 31, 2027

JPY 200 per person, per night

■ From April 1, 2027

3% of the room rate (per person, per night, excluding tax) for room rates of JPY 13,000 or more.

Thank you for your understanding.
- On Sunday, January 17, 2027, our hotel will conduct its annual electrical facility maintenance, as required by Japanese law. During this period, the building will undergo a complete power shutdown from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

To ensure a smooth experience for our guests, we kindly ask for your attention to the following schedule on this day:

\- Check-out: by 9:00 a.m.

\- Check-in: from 6:00 p.m.

### Unrivaled Location in Shinagawa, Tokyo

The Strings is conveniently located in Shinagawa, one of Tokyo’s leading business districts. An internal walkway leads directly from the property to Shinagawa Station, a major transport hub that connects Tokyo and other destinations around Japan. The station also provides direct rail access to both Narita and Haneda airports.

Learn more

### Club InterContinental

Discover what it means to live the InterContinental Life. Guests staying in our Club InterContinental rooms or suites enjoy access to one of Tokyo’s most exclusive lounges. From the moment you step into the Maestro Lounge, you’ll be greeted with private check-in, a refreshing welcome drink and panoramic views of Tokyo — a colorful orchestra awaits.

Learn more

### The Score Dining Experience

The Score Dining, on level 26, resonates with the sounds of daily live music. With an award winning culinary team, it is one of Tokyo’s most unique, upscale food, drink and entertainment destinations. It features an impressive culinary program that’s sure to satisfy even the most discerning tastes.

Learn more

### Meeting and Events

Whether you plan to host a business meeting, social function, or memorable meal, you’ll find everything you need at The Strings. Our inviting venues are able to accommodate a variety of gatherings, and our dedicated event coordinator will work closely with you to make sure everything goes smoothly and meets your expectations.

Learn more

### Offering a Safe and Clean Environment

As the world adjusts to new travel norms and expectations, The Strings by InterContinental Tokyo is enhancing the experience for its hotel guests by redefining cleanliness and supporting guests’ personal well-being throughout their stay. Using new, science-led protocols and strengthened service measures, we aim to give guests greater confidence.

## FAQs for InterContinental The Strings Tokyo

Does InterContinental The Strings Tokyo have a pool?

No, InterContinental The Strings Tokyo does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental The Strings Tokyo?

At InterContinental The Strings Tokyo, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 3000 JPY .

Parking is provided as an additional service and the hotel cannot be held responsible for any damages, thefts, losses or destruction of the vehicles and/or belongings left in them.

Parking fee: 3000 JPY per day

In and out privileges

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental The Strings Tokyo?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental The Strings Tokyo.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental The Strings Tokyo for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental The Strings Tokyo have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental The Strings Tokyo has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental The Strings Tokyo?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental The Strings Tokyo.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental The Strings Tokyo?

Check-in at InterContinental The Strings Tokyo is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental The Strings Tokyo offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental The Strings Tokyo offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental The Strings Tokyo from the nearest airport?

InterContinental The Strings Tokyo is conveniently located near Haneda Airport (HND), approximately 6.84 miles away (11 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental The Strings Tokyo?

InterContinental The Strings Tokyo is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Tokyo Disney Land
- Sensoji Temple
- Kabuki-za Theatre
- Yanesen & Yanaka Ginza
- Happo-en Garden
- The Imperial Palace
- Tokyo National Museum
- Meiji Shrine
- National Museum of Nature and Science

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental The Strings Tokyo puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
