### Best hotel in tokyo

22 days ago

This is the second time I am staying here. First of all start with the location - it is literally next to Shinagawa station which is like 5 minutes walk so you are connected to entire Tokyo as well as connection via Shinkansen (bullet train). When you enter, the concierge at ground floor will help with the luggage so you don't have to bother yourself with it. Just go to floor 26 which the concierge will help too. The reception team is lovely and very fast. They will help you check in and send your luggage to your room. The WiFi is lightening fast even with the number of guests that are staying and still works like a beauty. The breakfast is a buffet and I can assure you that buffe cannot be beaten by anyone since I travel entire globe and stay in many other 5-star hotels... but this was one of the best. The rooms are ultra clean, massive bathrooms, great amenities and noise-proof.
I cannot point to any one thing that could have been 9/10 rather the entire hotel, staff, courtesy, amenities, food, services everything is 10/10 here.
