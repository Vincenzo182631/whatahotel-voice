# Welcome to JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa

## Be inspired and unwind at our beachfront resort in Cancun

Indulge in a revitalizing retreat at one of the best Cancun resorts, JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa, where personalized service, modern rooms with private balconies and elegant suites showcase the Mexican Caribbean experience. Enjoy spacious accommodations with access to the area's most desirable attractions. Explore nearby Mayan ruins and discover Cancun’s vibrant nightlife. Watch the sunrise over...See More the turquoise Caribbean Sea, unwind with a dip in the infinity pool, relax in a beachfront cabana, or embrace well-being through the resort’s expanding wellness experiences, including sunrise yoga, sound healing baths and mindful activities designed to inspire balance. At the heart of this journey is Sens-yah, our Mayan-inspired spa, proudly awarded Four Stars by Forbes Travel Guide. Savor global flavors at SYBIL, Sasi Thai, Gustino Italian Grill, Bella Candela, Bravio Spanish Cuisine and La 16 Mexican Cuisine, then raise a glass with creative cocktails from the JW Lobby Bar’s 50 Margaritas Menu.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa is Cancun International Airport (CUN). CUN is located approximately 11.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa have an airport shuttle to Cancun International Airport (CUN)?

Yes, JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa has an airport shuttle to Cancun International Airport (CUN).

Does JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa have a Club 91 lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Cancun Resort & Spa has Club 91 lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Club 91 lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services.
