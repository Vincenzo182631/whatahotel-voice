Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa has 7 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa?

Check-in at InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa is conveniently located near Ras Al Khaimah International Airport (RKT), approximately 18.64 miles away (30 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa?

InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Desert Safari - 8.7 miles away (14 kilometers)
- Jebel Jais Mountain - 60.89 miles away (98 kilometers)
- Bear Grylls Explorers Camp - 40.39 miles away (65 kilometers)
- Water Sports - 8.08 miles away (13 kilometers)
- National Museum of Ras Al Khaimah - 4.54 miles away (7.3 kilometers)
- Jebel Jais Zipline - 7.39 miles away (11.9 kilometers)
- Suwaidi Pearl Farm - 20.94 miles away (33.7 kilometers)
- Al Jazirah Al Hamra Old Village - 5.34 miles away (8.6 kilometers)
- Bedouin Oasis Camp - 17.21 miles away (27.7 kilometers)
- Dhayah Fort - 5.34 miles away (8.6 kilometers)
- Mangrove Forest - 28.21 miles away (45.4 kilometers)
- Mina Al Arab

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa puts you within easy reach.
