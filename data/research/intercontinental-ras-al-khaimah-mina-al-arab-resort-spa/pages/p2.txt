# InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and SpaRooms & suites

Experience 45 sqm of spacious, light-filled luxury in our Classic Sea View Rooms at InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah. Thoughtfully designed with elegant Arabian décor, each room offers a serene retreat with stunning Gulf views. Step onto your private balcony and breathe in the warm coastal breeze. Inside, sink into plush king or twin beds and enjoy sophisticated smart-room technology — perfect for couples, families, and business travellers seeking premium sea view accommodation in Ras Al Khaimah.
