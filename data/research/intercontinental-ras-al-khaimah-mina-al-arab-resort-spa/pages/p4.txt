### SPA InterContinental

Indulge in soothing skincare treatments, relaxing body therapies, and traditional Hammam rituals at SPA InterContinental—a serene oasis of peace and tranquility.

Learn more

### Exclusive Club InterContinental

An elevated stay awaits in one of our Club InterContinental rooms, within the exclusive club building. With access to a separate check-in area, Club exclusive pool and exclusive food and beverage offerings throughout the day, Club InterContinental offers a resort experience like no other.

Learn more

### Seafront Living in Private Villas

Discover enchanting beachfront private pool villas and seafront villas amid our lush gardens, just steps from the white sandy beach. Each villa offers beautiful living spaces, in-villa dining, and sophisticated amenities with ample room for family and friends.

Learn more

### Planet Trekkers Kids' Club

Parents rest easy knowing their kids are enjoying their vacation just as much as you. Our Planet Trekkers Kids' Club activities are designed to inspire even the youngest travellers, with experiences focused on growth, curiosity, and creativity. Open daily from 10:00 to 20:00.

Learn more
