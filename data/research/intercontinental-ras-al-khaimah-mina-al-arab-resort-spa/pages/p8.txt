# InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and SpaDestination

Discover adventures near our Ras Al Khaimah beach resort — hike the UAE's highest peak at Jebel Jais, kayak through stunning mangroves, spot flamingos, and explore Bedouin heritage at historic forts. The best of Ras Al Khaimah experiences awaits.

## Things to do

### Desert Safari

Rally through undulating sands on a dune-bashing desert tour, or trek across the terracotta terrain on camel back.

### Jebel Jais Mountain

Take in majestic mountain views and explore the highest peak in the United Arab Emirates, 1,934m above sea

### Bear Grylls Explorers Camp

If you're an aspiring adventurer who thrives on pushing your limits and acquiring survival skills, The Bear Grylls Explorers Camp offers exclusive challenges that will truly test you, your family, or your team.

### Water Sports

Head down to the resort beach to try kayaking and stand-up paddling, or go further and see what you can catch on a Deep-Sea Fishing excursion. For something more relaxed, the traditional Luxury Dhow Cruise gently cruises around the turquoise waters of the Arabian Gulf, passing through creeks and mangroves as you go.

### National Museum of Ras Al Khaimah

Discover a remarkable collection of archaeological and ethnological treasures within this former fort and gain insight into the rich history of the Nature Emirate.

### Jebel Jais Zipline

Take on the thrill of the world’s longest zip wire, gliding through dramatic canyons and over towering cliffs.

### Suwaidi Pearl Farm

Immerse yourself in the country's timeless tradition of pearl farming and embark on a quest through the mangroves in a Dhow boat to search for underwater treasures.

### Al Jazirah Al Hamra Old Village

Visit Al Jazeera Al Hamra, the only remaining historical pearling village in the entire Gulf region, which stands as a unique testament to the region's heritage and cultural significance.

### Bedouin Oasis Camp

Located in the heart of the towering dunes and Ras Al Khaimah’s glorious rural landscape, the Bedouin Oasis offers an experience like no other. Immerse yourself in the traditional Bedouin way of daily desert life in the emirate’s most authentic camp.

### Dhayah Fort

Dating back to the 16th century, Dhaya Fort, a castle-like structure, is the sole remaining hill fort in the United Arab Emirates.

### Mangrove Forest

Stretching from the coast into the city, lose yourself in the natural beauty of the mangroves as you gently explore via kayak.

### Mina Al Arab

Discover Mina Al Arab, a beautiful locale with vibrant retail centers, gourmet restaurants, and leisure facilities, including fitness centers and private marinas. This destination hosts bustling commercial hubs and cultural venues that foster a dynamic atmosphere, providing endless opportunities for entertainment, social engagement, and community events.
