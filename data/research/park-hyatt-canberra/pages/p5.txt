### Great Place to Stay in Canbarra

Excellent stay at the Hyatt Hotel Canberra. The location is ideal—convenient to the city center, government offices, major attractions, and dining—while still offering a quiet and refined atmosphere.

The accommodations were excellent: comfortable, well-appointed, and maintained to a high standard. However, the carpet does need to be replaced. The hotel has a classic character that distinguishes it from more generic modern properties, and the staff were consistently professional, courteous, and helpful.

Overall, a very enjoyable experience and an excellent choice for either business or leisure travel in Canberra. I would gladly stay here again.
