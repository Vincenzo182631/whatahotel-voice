## 5 Star Accommodation in Canberra

Hyatt Hotel Canberra – A Park Hyatt Hotel is nestled beside the prestigious National Triangle, home to Australia’s most iconic cultural institutions. For more than a century, the hotel has been the preferred residence for heads of state, diplomats, captains of industry and well-heeled travelers from near and far. With 252 rooms and suites, it offers timeless elegance and historic charm, reimagined for today’s modern traveler.

## Discover Park Hyatt Canberra

Fitness Centre

There’s no need to compromise your fitness routine when travelling. The Clubhouse at Hyatt Hotel Canberra – A Park Hyatt Hotel offers a fully equipped gymnasium, personal trainers, indoor pool, sauna, fitness classes and bike hire.

Open daily 6 a.m.–9 p.m. The Clubhouse is staffed between 6 a.m.–noon and 4–7 p.m. Outside these hours, members and guests can access the gym using their swipe card.

Business Centre

During your stay enjoy the tranquil setting of our historic Rose and Lavender Courtyards. A perfect spot to savor a light meal in the warmer months or group gatherings and celebrations.

Pool

Enjoy a peaceful dip in our heated swimming pool. The Clubhouse at Hyatt Hotel Canberra features a 15-metre edgeless heated swimming pool. Escape the weather and relax in a comfortable indoor environment. Aqua aerobic classes are available at $20 per class. Contact the Clubhouse at +61 2 6269 8540 for session times.

## Things to Do

Whether it’s history, outdoor activities, shopping or dining, a stay at Hyatt Hotel Canberra – A Park Hyatt Hotel puts you close to all Canberra has to offer.
