## The Promenade Café

Positioned at the heart of Hyatt Hotel Canberra, The Promenade Cafe is a vibrant and immersive dining experience for hotel guests and visitors alike.
Elegantly furnished to complement the hotel's Art Deco style, the restaurant offers a premium breakfast buffet and serves an a la carte dinner daily. In the evenings, The Appetiser Table cold buffet also showcases local Providores and Artisans with fresh, sustainable seafood and exceptional produce. The newly launched Grill menu features premium cuts of meat, cooked to perfection.

## The Tea Lounge

Celebrate Melbourne Cup in the Gardens

Reminiscent of the sitting rooms of a bygone era, modern luxury and old-world charm meet at The Tea Lounge in our heritage-listed residence. It’s the perfect place to gather with family and friends for a meal or indulge in our traditional afternoon tea service. Our famous High Tea stands with an array of finger sandwiches, freshly baked scones with jam and cream, and a selection of tempting cakes and slices. Reservations essential.

High Tea Thursday and Friday: $79 per person \| $50 per child (3-12 years)
High Tea Saturday and Sunday: $85 per person \| $50 per child (3-12 years)

Please note a Sunday surcharge applies.
