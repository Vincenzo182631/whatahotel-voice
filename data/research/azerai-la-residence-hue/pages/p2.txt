# Accommodations

Azerai La Residence, Hue is set on a two- and-a-half-hectare site on the fabled Perfume River. Its two complementary wings, built in 2005, are contemporary in design yet echo elements of period aesthetics and embrace an expansive tropical garden and salt-water swimming pool.
