### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Marquis Miami?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at JW Marriott Marquis Miami. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami have fridges?

No, rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at JW Marriott Marquis Miami have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at JW Marriott Marquis Miami have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
