## Enliven Spa & Salon

Enliven means to renew, rejuvenate, purify and bring new life. Symbolized by the lotus flower, enliven is the timeless and continual process of birth and rebirth. Our spa and salon treatments, performed by our staff of expert therapists and estheticians, bring purity and new life to your mind, body and soul.

## Fitness

Guests of our spa can also enjoy Met2 Fitness Center and Yoga, located on the same floor of our spa. Personal training also available.  Make your reservation today to start a new you.Show More

**Met2 Fitness Center**

Met2 Fitness Center, located adjacent to the spa, offers unlimited access to state-of-the-art "technogym" with cardiovascular machines, strength training equipment, free weights and stretching rooms. Guests also enjoy elite features including complimentary head phones, chilled towels, bottled water, organic fruits, a sumptuous relaxation area, private luxury locker rooms and sauna. To top it off, guests enjoy unparalleled views of the bay with spectacular natural light while working out.

**Yoga Studio**

Yoga classes are also available. Yoga by Enliven features yoga classes for yogis of any level, from beginner to advanced. For more information on the yoga classes, please call Enliven Spa and Salon at 305-421-8770.
