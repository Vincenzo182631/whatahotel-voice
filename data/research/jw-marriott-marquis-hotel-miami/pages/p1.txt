# Welcome to JW Marriott Marquis Miami

## Discover reimagined luxury at our hotel in downtown Miami

Nestled in the vibrant heart of downtown Miami, JW Marriott Marquis Miami offers a luxurious escape amidst the bustling city. Located near the Port of Miami, Kaseya Center, Wynwood, Frost Science Museum, and Pérez Art Museum in the Financial District, our Miami hotel seamlessly blends sophistication with culture. With easy access to the Miami Design District and Brickell, as well as a short drive to...See More South Beach, our location is unrivaled. Indulge in culinary delights at Daniel Boulud's Boulud Sud before unwinding by our stainless-steel sky pool, treating yourself to spa services at Enliven, or practicing your skills at our NBA-approved basketball court. Our 313 stunning rooms and suites feature state-of-the-art technology, ensuring an elevated stay. With recent renovations enhancing our meeting spaces, guestrooms, restaurants, and public areas, our hotel in Downtown Miami offers unparalleled versatility for both business and pleasure. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Marquis Miami?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Marquis Miami is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Marquis Miami allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Marquis Miami.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Marquis Miami?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Marquis Miami. The daily valet fee is $76.29 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Marquis Miami have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Marquis Miami has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Marquis Miami booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Marquis Miami?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Marquis Miami is Miami International Airport (MIA). MIA is located approximately 8.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Marquis Miami have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Marquis Miami does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
