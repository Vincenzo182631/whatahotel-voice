Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, San Francisco
Festive Mystique at a Nob Hill Landmark
Set within San Francisco’s prestigious and picturesque neighborhood of Nob Hill,
The Ritz-Carlton, San Francisco invites guests to commemorate the holidays with a myriad of joyous events. As you marvel at the sparkling holiday décor throughout the city landmark hotel, join us for Thanksgiving and Christmas Day dining, traditional Teddy Bear Tea for the kids, Tree Lighting Ceremony, New Year's Eve at Parallel 37 and more.
Holiday Celebrations
Teddy Bear Tea
Children are invited for Teddy Bear Tea in the winter wonderland of The Ritz-Carlton Ballroom featuring a lively holiday show, visits with Santa, teddy bear gifts, and keepsake photos. Children may bring an unwrapped new toy to place under the holiday tree to be donated to UCSF Benioff Children’s Hospitals. Proceeds will benefit UCSF Benioff Children’s Hospitals and Make-A-Wish® Greater Bay Area.
Reserve Now
Tree Lighting Ceremony
Join us at our annual Tree Lighting Ceremony, hosted by Betty Yu (KTVU-TV Fox 2) and Liam Mayclem (KCBS Radio) and a Make-A-Wish® Greater Bay Area child. The complimentary-to-the-public event will feature live entertainment with Dickens-era carolers and trumpeters, the legendary tree lighting at the hotel’s front drive, and photo opportunities with Santa Claus. DECEMBER 2 from 5:30 p.m. to 7:30 p.m.
Letters to Santa
From December 2-25, children are invited to write and mail a letter to Santa in the Lobby. In addition, they enjoy complimentary candy canes on Fridays and Saturdays in December.
Festive Dining
Thanksgiving Day Menu at Parallel 37
Gather with loved ones and enjoy a special three-course Thanksgiving menu at Parallel 37 featuring traditional favorites and fall specialties. $195 USD per adult; $100 per child, exclusive of tax and 21% gratuity.
Reserve Now
Christmas Day Menu at Parallel 37
Celebrate Christmas at joyously decorated Parallel 37 indulging in a three-course Christmas Day menu presenting holiday classic dishes and seasonal inspirations. $195 USD per adult; $100 per child, exclusive of tax and 21% gratuity.
Reserve Now
Discover More
Luxury Holiday Family Travel
Families are invited to create new holiday memories in spacious connecting accommodations. Experience quality moments with loved ones in two-bedroom connecting hotel suites in San Francisco with beautiful city views and access to the landmark cable car right outside our doors.
Reserve Now
Holidays on Club Level
Families are invited to upgrade to Club Level to enjoy complimentary food and beverage presentations, dedicated Club Concierge services, and exclusive holiday offers. Kids receive holiday surprises and delights while adults can imbibe in the Naughty or Nice Eggnog Cart serving a classic holiday blend or a spirited twist with premium spirits on the weekends.
