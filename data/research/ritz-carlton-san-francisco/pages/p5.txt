Experience our hotel near Union Square, San Francisco, CA
Experiences
Situated in Nob Hill, near the Financial District and Union Square, The Ritz-Carlton, San Francisco resides at the intersection of some of the city's most attractive destinations. Step back in time on a cable car ride through the city — all the lines converge in our neighborhood of Nob Hill — or browse the food hall and artisan shops of the San Francisco Ferry Building. Indulge in luxury shopping at Union Square, or spend an afternoon among the artworks of the San Francisco Museum of Modern Art. Discover the best of the city at our hotel near Embarcadero, San Francisco, curated by the experts at The Ritz-Carlton.

Explore California's Natural Wonders
Venture across the Golden Gate Bridge to Muir Woods National Monument to experience the awe-inspiring magic of nature's skyscrapers.
Hidden Gems
One of the most eclectic cities in America, San Francisco is filled with neighborhoods that have diverse and rich histories, with much of those histories well-preserved. (Where else can you find cable cars still roaring through streets?) Trace the path of the beatniks, who called North Beach their home, or visit the ruins of the city’s old bathhouse on the water. And don’t miss the many museums that can stand up to those of New York, London, and Paris.
