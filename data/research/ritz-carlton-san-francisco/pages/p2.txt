The Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge
The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining intimacy, comfort, and exclusivity with personalized service and attention to detail that are the hallmarks of The Ritz-Carlton. This oasis is often referred to as a hotel within a hotel, in a well-appointed, relaxed lounge environment. Overlooking the San Francisco city skyline and the tranquil courtyard below, the Club Lounge is the perfect place to meet with family and friends, conduct business, or simply unwind. Our hotel’s exclusive amenities include continuous culinary offerings throughout the day (breakfast, light lunch, evening hors d'oeuvres, desserts, and cordials), accompanied by a full-service Club Concierge to see to every need including access to extraordinary travel experiences.
