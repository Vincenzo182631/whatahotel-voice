## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Dallas located?

Our luxury Dallas hotel is located in the Arts District at 1717 N Akard Street, TX 75201. It provides the perfect location for a cultural getaway in Texas while also being within walking distance to the business district and renowned dining.

Is parking available at Fairmont Dallas?

Yes, our hotel offers valet parking with unlimited access at a cost of $56 USD/night plus tax. Alternative covered and open parking lots and garages are also available in the surrounding area.

Is Fairmont Dallas pet-friendly?

Fairmont Dallas is pleased to welcome pets for a $75 fee, per pet, per stay. There are no weight limitations for pets, however, pets must be on a leash at all times and must not be left unattended in the room. Any damage incurred to the room by the pet is the responsibility of the guest.

Does Fairmont Dallas have a swimming pool?

Yes, our luxury hotel offers a Junior Olympic-sized swimming pool at our terrace with stunning city views of Dallas and delicious specialty cocktails, lunch, and drinks. Poolside services are offered seasonally as weather permits.

Does Fairmont Dallas offer any business services?

Fairmont Dallas is perfect for business travelers with an onsite UPS Store located on our O-level which offers access to computer, printing, shipping, and design services.
