Delivering the perfect Maui resort fantasy.

Why book?
For Maui’s greatest hits—epic ocean sunsets, walkable beach access, rich flora and fauna—set against a wellness-forward, family-friendly stay that feels distinctly Hawaiian.
Set the scene
The Westin Maui Resort & Spa has significantly evolved since its opening in 1987 when it came on the scene during an era of growth across Maui’s Kā‘anapali resort corridor. While it has retained its sprawling, indulgent island vibes, the property has since adopted an ethos around immersive wellness, elevated dining, and the value of cultural education. With ongoing programming and on-site amenities that rise to meet this shift, travelers can expect an experience as enriching or relaxing as they prefer.
The rooms
Accommodations can be booked in two different towers—the Kūkahi Tower or the Hōkūpa‘a Tower. While rooms across both share design elements of light woods and creams with pops of ocean-inspired blues, and both sit on the same beachfront property along Kāʻanapali Beach, guests searching for an elevated experience should look to Hōkūpa‘a Tower. As the resort’s premium hotel-within-a-hotel experience, Hōkūpa‘a Tower guests will not only find larger indoor and outdoor room layouts, but will also gain access to The Lānai, a private lounge-style space with complimentary food and drinks, a private bar, and infinity-edge cocktail pools.
My husband and I stayed in the Hōkūpa‘a Tower’s one-bedroom luxury suite where we had panoramic views of the property stretching out to the ocean. With a private bedroom (featuring ample room for our toddler’s bed), a soaking tub, and a breakfast nook with wraparound benches, it was a wonderful home base that felt like luxury apartment living. One word of warning: Thanks to jet lag and a toddler, we did find ourselves falling asleep before 9 p.m. the first few nights and the noise from the live entertainment down below did occasionally pipe into our room. If you’re also 600 years old and think you’ll be early to bed, just pack a sound machine (or use the one on your phone) to get ahead of any sound issues.

Food and drink
The hotel's crown jewel, Ulu Kitchen, is led by classically trained, award-winning chef Peter Merriman. You’ll find modern takes on Hawaiian classics with a strong emphasis on locally sourced ingredients, all set against world-class oceanfront views. I dined here twice during my stay, once for breakfast and once for dinner, and both meals were the best I had during my week bouncing between islands. For breakfast, the Mai Tai flapjacks, served with Kula rum butter syrup, honey passionfruit foam, and island pineapple, are a must-order. Come evening, start your meal with the famous Mai Tai cocktail and the crunchy broccoli salad.
While you may want to dine at Ulu for every meal (and I can’t blame you), you can also branch out to a number of other food and beverage options, including outdoor lounge Hale Moʻolelo for classic island fare and live local musicians, or Mahele Market for counter-style and grab-and-go options like build-your-own poke bowls and prepared sandwiches. There’s also an always-bustling Starbucks café on-property.

The spa
When you need a break from the buzz of the resort, Heavenly Spa by Westin offers a perfect balance of tranquility and approachability. It leaves behind the sterility sometimes found in ultra-luxe spas, and instead leads with warmth, rich earth tones, and open-air lounge spaces that offer sunshine and oceanfront views. Instead of isolating you from the island, guests are enveloped by the smells, sounds, and sights of the paradise they’re in.
For treatments, you’ll find the expected selection of wellness therapies, ranging from facials, massages, and various body treatments, but with the incorporation of Hawaiian botanicals and island ingredients. I scheduled an appointment for the custom organic facial for my very first day at the hotel, which was the best decision I could have made given it made my skin glow for the rest of my trip.
The neighborhood/area
Lahaina, a historic town on Maui’s west side and only a five-minute drive from the property, was unfortunately devastated by the
2023 wildfires
and isn’t yet back to being the tourism hub it once was. However, the coasts of Lahaina are still in operation as one of Maui’s main ocean gateways where you can find various water excursions that still depart from town. A personal favorite was a two-hour whale and dolphin watching
boat ride
we booked through TripAdvisor where we had multiple whale sightings—including mom and calf pairs!—despite the fact that we were just outside of peak whale-watching season. The two-hour limit (and the complimentary snacks of watermelon and chips) also make this a great choice for families.
If you’re looking to stay on land, a short drive heading north from the hotel will take you to the laid-back seaside community of Honokōwai where you can find casual dining options and snag an
award-winning
cinnamon roll at
Papi’s Ohana
(but only if you show up before they inevitably sell out).
The service
Warm, jovial, and filled with smiles as is typical of island hospitality.
For families
My husband and I stayed for four nights with our almost three-year-old and we can confidently confirm that The Westin Maui Resort & Spa is an incredible choice for families. Our daughter woke up every morning asking to go to the splash pad where she squealed and bounced through various spray jets, waded in a shallow sensory pool filled with sand, and even went down a mini waterslide. If you have older kiddos, there’s a proper children’s pool right next door that has an even larger waterslide sure to keep your little ones entertained for hours.
Families with children ages 5–12 can also utilize the Westin Family Kids Club which provides supervised children’s programming designed to blend fun with Hawaiian culture. And if you’re really aiming for parent of the year, the hotel’s on-site gaming and entertainment space will make your job quite easy. The Valley Alley offers all your favorite arcade games along with a bowling alley and Topgolf suites.
One final note for traveling families:
BabyQuip
offers a gear rental service and can deliver anything you might need at your arrival airport or hotel. We used them for the first time on this trip and are true converts. We had a car seat waiting for us at Kahului Airport and a toddler bed, sound machine, and an entire bin of new toys greeting us in our room at The Westin Maui Resort & Spa. It was truly seamless and made for a much less stressful travel day.
Eco effort
The resort prioritizes sustainability, with one of its largest investments coming from a partnership with Carbon Lighthouse. The partnership resulted in the implementation of energy efficiency measures across the HVAC and lighting systems that are projected to reduce carbon emissions by an amount equivalent to avoiding the import of approximately 7,200 barrels of oil.
Water conservation is also a key pillar of the team’s sustainability efforts where a recent $160 million renovation led to the installation of high-efficiency plumbing fixtures, smart irrigation systems that adjust based on weather data, and the use of recycled water for landscaping.
Complimentary reef-safe sunscreen dispensers are available to guests property-wide and beach clean-ups are held regularly with guests invited to join.
Accessibility
The property is fully ADA-compliant, with accessible rooms and routes across the property, including to all restaurants, meeting spaces, the spa, fitness center, and business center.
Anything left to mention?
It’s almost blasphemous to come to Hawaii and not see a proper luau. The right experience can offer an excellent evening of cultural education and local storytelling. The Wailele Lū‘au, offered on-site at The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, is a great—and convenient—choice for those looking for an evening of vibrant performances and a full-on traditional island-style buffet dinner (Tip: If you splurge on the premium seating tickets, you’ll get called to the buffet line first.) Come hungry, be ready for a three-hour night of entertainment, and make sure you stay until the end for an incredible fire show.
