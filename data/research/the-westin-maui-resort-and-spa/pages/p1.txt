# Welcome to The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali

## Discover an authentic connection at our Maui, Hawaii, resort

Set on the legendary sands of Ka'anapali Beach, our oceanfront resort brings together six inviting pools, cascading waterfalls, restorative wellness, and living Hawaiian culture — all just steps from the Pacific. Discover Hokupa'a, the new luxury experience at the Westin Maui, with access to the exclusive Lanai where experiences are designed to introduce guests to the rich culture of Hawaii and enhance their wellbeing.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali?

The check-in time at The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali. Self parking is available at The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali, with rates of $55.00 per hour, $55.00 per day, $0.00 per week, and $0.00 perstay. Valet parking is available for $60.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali?

The closest airport near The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali is Kapalua Airport - Maui (JHM). JHM is located approximately 3.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali have an airport shuttle to Kapalua Airport - Maui (JHM)?

No, The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali does not have an airport shuttle to Kapalua Airport - Maui.

Does The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka'anapali has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
