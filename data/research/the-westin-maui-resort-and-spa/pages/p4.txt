# Luxury Maui Spa

## Experiences

The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka‘anapali offers so many things to do in Maui with abundant on-property activities in a world-class tropical setting. Stroll Ka‘anapali Beach at sunset or relax in a cabana with a cocktail. Dine by the hotel pool or follow your ear to an authentic Maui luau with live music, endless drinks, and a lavish buffet. When you finish your massage at Heavenly Spa by Westin™ and are ready to venture off-property, discover many fun activities just steps from our hotel. Walk a block to Whalers Village and discover upscale restaurants, shops, and entertainment venues. Take our hotel shuttle to nearby attractions in West Maui. From Haleakala National Park to Honolua Bay, there is no shortage of attractions to visit in Maui. Aside from snorkeling, swimming, sailing, and dozens of other water-related activities, families love to stop by Hana, an undeveloped natural area of unbridled, splendid beauty.

Outdoor Pools for Adults & Children

### Kawaiola Aquatic Playground

Six stunning pools overlooking Ka‘anapali beach and spanning 87,000 square feet of pool playground. Highlights include a serene adults-only pool deck with an infinity pool and hot tub. Families find so many fun things to do in Maui with a newly designed family area including an interactive splash zone and our iconic 270 feet water slide. Open Mon-Sun, 07:00 AM - 10:00 PM. Towels provided.

## Adults Pool

The newly redesigned adults-only pool deck area features a whirlpool and an infinity edge pool with uninterrupted views of the Pacific and the neighboring islands of Molokai and Lanai. Scattered across the deck and pool area are dew-drop-shaped cabanas that were exclusively designed for the resort. Open Mon - Sun, 07:00 AM - 10:00 PM. Towels provided.

## Kids Pool

While parents soak up the sun, the little ones can keep cool in the new interactive Keiki Splash Zone in the family pool area. A dozen green spray jets sprout from the ground turning sun time into fun time. A mini water slide, waterfall, and kids pool offer hours of playtime and fun things to do in Maui. Open Mon - Sun, 07:00 AM - 10:00 PM. Towels provided.

## Westin Maui Waterman Program

The Westin Maui Resort & Spa makes waves with a new wellness program that keeps guests active while giving back. Allowing guests to participate in thoughtfully designed ocean adventures with champion standup paddle boarders, lifelong boat captains, outrigger canoe racers, and others who want to share their respect for the water and passion for the environment with travelers while giving back to the local community.

Read More About The Westin Maui Waterman Program

The Westin Maui Waterman program will be introduced in three levels - Novice Enthusiast, Advanced Adventure and Personal Perfection. Guests can participate in the monthly Novice Enthusiast activity that includes a beach clean-up and conversation, beginning on the ocean lawn of the resort once a month at 8:00AM. The brand's nourishing Westin SuperFoodsRx and signature Westin Fresh by the Juicery refreshments will be served during the two-hour session. For those keen keen to continue their journey to the Advanced Adventure level of the program, the resort has partnered the on-site Ka'anapali Surf Club to present a new standup paddleboard course just over a mile long from North Ka'anapali Beach that lands guests beachfront of The Westin Maui Resort & Spa.

### Local Area Experiences

The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Ka‘anapali is located 27 miles from Kahului Airport on beautiful Ka‘anapali Beach just a few steps away from local shopping and restaurants. Take a short stroll on the beach walk and discover Whaler's Village - the only oceanfront shopping destination in Hawaii.

## Whaler's Village

Well-known as the premier shopping center in Maui, Whaler's Village is only one block from our resort. It's one of the best things to do in Ka‘anapali on any day of the week. From Billabong to Kate Spade and Louis Vuitton, the 67 stores and 12 restaurants at this shopping mall offer nearly endless recreation and pleasure. Dine at Hula Grill Kaanapali, Monkeypod Kitchen by Merriman, Leilani's on the Beach, Nikki's Pizza, and more.

## Haleakala National Park

Located on the island of Maui, in Hawaii, Haleakala National Park is a wilderness area and a sacred landscape, continuously reverberating with the echoes of Hawaiian culture and ancestry. Experiencing the Sunrise Ceremony at the park is one of the most popular things to do in Maui. The park offers ample opportunity for hiking, learning about the plants, animals, and history, guided activities, and beautiful natural scenery.

## Hana

Isolated from the rest of Maui, this wild, undeveloped area is one of the most remote and breathtakingly beautiful unspoiled areas left in the world today. It is truly a tropical paradise not to be missed on any trip to Maui, Hawaii.

## Honolua Bay

As a vital part of the Maui Marine Life Conservation District, Honolua Bay does not allow fishing. Honolua Bay does offer snorkeling and scuba diving tours, a beautiful beach, great surfing, and lush hiking trails through Honolua Valley. Set on the wild northwestern shore of Maui, Hawaii, this magnificent beach area offers so many things to do in Ka'anapali with something for everyone in your family or group to enjoy.
