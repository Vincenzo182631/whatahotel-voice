# Sleep in our luxury suites.

At Le Barthélemy, interiors reflect the sea and natural beauty of its spectacular St. Barts setting. Supremely luxurious, composed of natural materials and bathed in light, our 44 rooms and suites and 2 luxury villas in St Barts, fully accessible for guests with reduced mobility, are serene and stylish, designed by Sybille de Margerie with a deft hand to conjure an emotional response that complements and never competes with its island backdrop. Invisible drop-down screens easily transform outdoor terraces into private enclaves, shady and comfortable but without disturbing the view. Adding to this modern interpretation of tranquil Caribbean style, is the thoughtful integration of technology and in-room amenities by Diptyque.

- ### Experience an Exceptional Suite in Saint Barthélemy

Le Barthélemy Hotel invites you to discover the art of refined living with an exceptional suite in Saint Barthélemy, where design and comfort harmonize with breathtaking nature. Perfectly located on the island’s most pristine shores, every suite reflects the unique spirit of St. Barts—chic, intimate, and inspiring.

As part of St Barts Hotel, our suites are more than accommodations; they are sanctuaries crafted for discerning travelers. Guests can also Discover Saint Barthélemy to enhance their stay, embracing the island’s elegance and authenticity, while nearby hotels in the Caribbean highlight why Saint Barthélemy is celebrated worldwide.

- ### Spacious Comfort with Elegant Decor

Each suite is designed to offer spacious comfort and refined style. The interiors combine beautiful finishes, contemporary architecture, and thoughtful decor enhanced by rich natural fabric. Every element is carefully chosen to embody the island’s relaxed yet elegant atmosphere.

Floor-to-ceiling windows open to sweeping views of the bay, bathing the rooms in tropical light. Outdoor terraces invite guests to immerse themselves in the tropical setting, creating an ambiance where luxury and nature are perfectly intertwined.

Choose between a St. Barts hotel with view or a luxury hotel room for an equally enchanting experience, each crafted with care and attention to detail.

- ### Privacy and Ideal Stays for Families and Friends

Privacy is a hallmark of every **suite in Saint Barthélemy**, offering serene escapes where tranquility reigns. Each is designed to provide the ultimate sense of **privacy**, ensuring restful moments far from the bustle of everyday life.

The suites are also **ideal** for gatherings with **family and friends**, offering **large** interiors, private terraces, and the option to connect with villas for even more space. Whether celebrating milestones or simply enjoying time together, these suites create memorable stays.

For inspiration, discover a **luxury hotel stay in Saint Barthélemy** or explore the **types of accommodations and activities in St Barts** to tailor your journey.

- ### A Tropical Escape with Refined Services

Beyond the beauty of the suites lies the excellence of the hotel’s services. Guests may indulge in a **spa** journey that redefines wellness, blending natural treatments with modern expertise. After a day of discovery, enjoy a fine dining experience at our signature **restaurant**, where Caribbean flavors meet French artistry.

Every moment is enriched by attentive hospitality, ensuring a seamless blend of luxury and relaxation. For more, explore our **luxury hotel services in St Barts**, rejuvenate with a **spa stay in Saint Barthélemy**, or savor gourmet experiences at the **luxury hotel restaurant in St Barts**.

- ### Explore Beaches and Exclusive Surroundings

The suites place you within reach of the island’s most iconic landscapes. From your terrace, admire the shimmering bay, then set out to explore the island’s famous shores.

  - Beach St Barts offers endless stretches of sand and crystal waters.
  - Hotels in Saint Barthélemy with private beach ensure unmatched exclusivity, and Le Barthélemy stands among them.
  - For vibrant escapes, Gustavia beach and St. Jean beach capture the island’s social spirit.
  - Nature lovers will adore Colombier beach and Toiny beach, untouched havens of raw beauty.
  - Flamands beach, one of the island’s longest, is perfect for long walks along turquoise waters.
  - Anse des Cayes beach offers waves ideal for surfers, while Corossol beach invites you into the heart of island tradition.

Every beach tells its own story, completing the island experience in ways both relaxing and inspiring.

- ### Romantic Escapes and Weddings in St Barts

A **suite in Saint Barthélemy** is also the perfect choice for couples seeking intimacy. With panoramic views and bespoke services, the suites transform into havens for honeymoons or special getaways. As part of a **romantic hotel in Saint Barthélemy**, they offer the perfect setting for love to flourish.

Le Barthélemy is also renowned for hosting elegant ceremonies. Those dreaming of a **wedding in Saint Barth** will find the suites an integral part of their celebration, ideal for pre-wedding preparations, family gatherings, or newlywed escapes.

- ### Your Gateway to Luxury in Saint Barthélemy

At Le Barthélemy, each suite embodies the island’s most refined qualities: intimacy, beauty, and sophistication. From the serenity of private terraces to the joy of shared celebrations, every detail ensures a stay that is both unique and unforgettable.

This is not just a place to stay—it is the heart of your St. Barts experience, where elegance meets authenticity and every sunrise promises something new.
