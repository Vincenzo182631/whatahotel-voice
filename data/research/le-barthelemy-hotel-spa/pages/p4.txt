# Beachfront hideaway in our luxury hotel in St Barts.

Welcome home. This is your private retreat. A sophisticated and refined testament to high standards and service amenities. Here you will find timeless elegance, warm authenticity and stunning beachfront settings of the Caribbean. At Le Barth, there are no guests, just family returning home.

After nine consecutive years in the Top 10 Caribbean Resorts by the Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards, and having been named the #1 Resort in the Caribbean in 2022, 2020 and 2018, Le Barthélemy Hotel & Spa has been named among the Top 100 Hotels in the World in the 2026 World’s Best Awards and ranked the 3rd Best Resort in the Caribbean by Travel + Leisure.
