### Winery

Impressive glass wine cellar at Lobby Lounge offers more than 300 fine Slovak and international wines and is the biggest hotel wine cellar in Slovakia. Whether you're a wine connoisseur or just beginning to explore the world of wine, our cellar has something to satisfy every palate.
