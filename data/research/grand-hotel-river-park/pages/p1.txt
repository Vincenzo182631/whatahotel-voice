# Welcome to Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava

## Luxury hotel in Bratislava Old Town

Grand Hotel River Park is a 5-star hotel in Bratislava and a top place to stay in the city. Positioned on the vibrant Danube river promenade, a scenic 15-minute walk from the historic Old Town, our luxury...See More Bratislava spa hotel is reachable via Vienna and Bratislava airports, rooftop heliport, private river pier, and the Danube cycle path. ZION Spa Luxury at our hotel offers 1500 square meters of wellness and fitness space with a pool, two Jacuzzis, three saunas, and panoramic views. Guests praise the riverside setting, walkability, spacious rooms, and service. RIVERBANK Restaurant and the Lobby Lounge offer terrace dining with river promenade views, while 11 elegant function rooms and a business center support executive stays and celebrations. This top Bratislava city hotel features 231 rooms and suites from 35 square meters, with wireless high-speed internet, a mini-bar, and individually controlled air conditioning. ## Discover Bratislava

Situated right on the banks of the Danube, Grand Hotel River Park serves as your gateway to the best of Bratislava and its surroundings. Begin your journey steps from our hotel with a scenic riverfront promenade leading into the heart of the historic Old Town. Uncover the regal heritage of Bratislava Castle, admire architectural gems like St. Martin’s Cathedral, and experience the thriving cultural scene of Slovakia’s capital. Enrich your stay with unforgettable day trips: marvel at the breathtaking medieval fortress of Devín Castle, indulge in fine wine tastings along the Small Carpathian Wine Region, or admire contemporary art surrounded by water at the world-class Danubiana Art Museum.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava?

The check-in time at Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava?

On-site parking is available at Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava. Self parking is available at Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava, with rates of €3.50 per hour, and €42.00 per day. Valet parking is available for €42.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava booking.

What is the closest airport near Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava?

The closest airport near Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava is Bratislava Airport (BTS). BTS is located approximately 10.3 KM from the hotel.

Does Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava have an airport shuttle to Bratislava Airport (BTS)?

Yes, Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava has an airport shuttle to Bratislava Airport (BTS).

Does Grand Hotel River Park, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Bratislava have an airport shuttle to Vienna International Airport (VIE)?
