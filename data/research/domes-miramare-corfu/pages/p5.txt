## Emerald Suites & Residences

The sea or beachfront view Emerald Suites are the spacious hideaways that consist of one bedroom and a separate living room, both spaces luxuriously designed and furnished. Enjoy the magnificent Mediterranean sea views past a lavish century-old olive garden and large open lawn area or directly standing on the idyllic beachfront. Located at the Seafront Wing with impressive views of the Ionian Sea through the olive grove and open lawn, the Emerald Residences of 1 or 2 Bedrooms offer an ultra-spacious accommodation option for those who wish for ample space. Consisting of one or two bedrooms, and a separate living room with an open-plan seating area, the residences also have a dining area and a fully equipped kitchenette. Indulge in the luxurious design and furnishings, artwork items, and different lighting scenarios. Location: Seafront Wing

## Grand Opal Suites

Breathtaking views of the Ionian Sea, gracefully blending island and minimalistic elements of creamy and light brown palettes, and the unique feeling of a spectacular and cozy getaway are what these spectacular suites combine. Featuring an open-plan setup, with a double bed of great comfort and a living room, this accommodation selection will surely provide the homey aura of a much-needed private sanctuary. The patio or balcony is the crown jewel of the room, for all those moments of relaxation you so deserve. Enjoy moments of endless Sea views, along with your Haute Living Selection perks. Location: Panoramic Wing

## Pavilion Retreats

These stylish and elegant semi-detached single story rooms offer garden or sea views and a large, luxurious living area. Located in the seafront wing of the resort, enjoy large private gardens, with a wooden decked seating area, outdoor Jacuzzi, outdoor shower and great views, through palm trees, grass areas and low rise plants with the option of direct access to the sea. Location: Seafront Wing

### Pavilion Suites

Luxurious, semi-detached, single story suites, that offer sea view with direct access to the sea or garden view and a large living area. Enjoy luxurious design and furnishings, local and international artwork, two large flat screen TVs and multi mood lighting scenarios. The Pavilion Suites are located in the seafront wing of the resort and offer large private gardens, with a wooden decked seating area, a private swimming pool, outdoor Jacuzzi, outdoor shower and enjoy garden views or sea views through palm trees and direct access to the waterfront past the lawn. Location: Seafront Wing

## HRH Pearl Villa

Her Royal Highness Pearl Villa is of an exceptional luxury, enhanced privacy and design in pearl tones. This waterfront, fully-detached, single story villa, offers uninterrupted sea views and a remarkable living area. Located in the "Ultra Exclusive Area" of the seafront wing of the resort, this is your seaside ticket to peacefulness. Location: Seafront Wing

## HRH Ivory Villa

His Royal Highness Ivory Villa is of an exceptional luxury, enhanced privacy and design in ivory tones. This waterfront, fully-detached, single story villa, offers uninterrupted sea views and a remarkable living area. Settle for nothing but the best in this unique and astonishing hideaway. Location: Seafront Wing
