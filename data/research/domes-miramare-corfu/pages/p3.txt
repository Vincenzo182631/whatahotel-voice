### 1962 By the Sea Restaurant

Allow the culinary treasures of 1962 By the Sea Restaurant to introduce you to the enticing mixture of Greek, Mediterranean, and Corfiot flavors. The creations of our award-winning chef, will unlock a whole new tasteful world while showcasing the astonishing quality of seasonal, local ingredients, combined with the chef’s mastery and respect for the rich Corfiot tradition.

### Blu Bar

The legendary Blu bar, where jet setters, royalty, and the elite of Europe, once hosted their majestic parties, is reintroduced to its guests, redesigned, yet providing the same poise and allure, along with some modern elegance. The setting is a magnificent mixture of handpicked art pieces, wooden elements, and dashes of aristocratic silver marble details. Enjoy cocktail progressions and beverages of choice.

### Antonino’s – A Taste of Italian Nostalgia

Antonino’s captures the carefree spirit of childhood summers along Corfu’s sun-drenched shores. Evoking the charm of 1950s and 60s beachside memories, this lively venue blends retro style with inventive Italian cuisine, adding playful touches like pizzas served with scissors. Savor poolside bites, take in sweeping sea views, and enjoy a vibrant menu crafted for sharing, unwinding, and celebrating life.

### Verde Pool Bar

Domes Miramare, could not have been complete without the most refreshing day bar. Push the button at your lounger and be served with refreshments, coffee, cocktails, and a variety of tasteful snacks that will keep you energized and hydrated while enjoying endless hours of sunbathing or swimming.

### Panorama Pool Bar

Relax in style at the stunning, newly opened Panoramic Wing Pool. Lounge on a chaise longue and soak in breathtaking views of the Ionian Sea while sipping on refreshing, handcrafted summertime cocktails. Enjoy delicious, freshly made snacks, refreshing salads, and finger foods, all while grooving to live DJ sets. Indulge in a day of luxury and fun at the Panorama Pool Bar.

## A Phenomenal Poolside Experience

The newly designed Pool Bar is bound to leave you breathless. Captivating sea views, meet the grandeur of the total white poolside, with the spectacular arches of the main building standing tall, and elegant in the background, perfectly completing the surroundings. Guest will enjoy a refreshing drink, accompanied by a series of handpicked dishes while taking in the glorious, endless views of the Ionian Sea. The Pool Bar features state-of-the-art sunbeds of unparalleled comfort and refined aesthetic.
