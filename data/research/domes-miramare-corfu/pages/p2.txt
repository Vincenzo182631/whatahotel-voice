# Ηospitality With Soul — Aristocratic Design

## Accommodations

A refined, enchanting refuge built for VIPs is ready to house your summer dreams. Discover what is simply one of the most lavish and quiet resorts in Corfu, preferred by literal and metaphorical royalty...See More – including Aristotle Onassis and Jackie O. In fact, some still call our hotel the “Onassis hotel in Corfu” because of the legendary magnate’s preference for it. But when the Domes group acquired ownership of the historic hotel, we set to work with a vision to not just meet but exceed the expectations of 21st century jetsetters and sophisticated travelers. That’s why we turned to the best and brightest to update our spaces and furnish our luxurious adults-only Corfu hotel with exquisite furniture and decorations. The result is our pride and joy. A seaside haven where the essence of relaxation awaits for everyone to grasp, housed comfortably in one of its sleek retreats, suites, or superb Corfu villas.See Less

### Where Classic Elegance Meets the Modern Chic

The new expansion of Domes Miramare is set to redefine the luxury beach resort experience on Corfu. Domes Miramare carries the torch of the glorious past and keeps evolving with the creation of new spaces that maintain its aristocratic and luxurious essence. The glorious arches of the hotel’s façade are the signature of the main building and all fine characteristics ensure that this emblematic property enters the new era with the sole purpose of providing a holistic, sophisticated, and unforgettable experience.

## Exquisite New Suites — Pristine Interiors of Great Comfort

The suites reflect a chic elegance using modern materials such as fine wood, glass, and marble. The exquisite interior design features calming earthy tones and a refined less-is-more ambiance guaranteeing that guests will experience elevated hospitality like never before. The suites feature amazing views and incomparable comfort. Featuring an open-plan setup with a palette of cream tones that evokes tranquility and simplicity, our brand-new suites make a unique and cozy summer getaway.

### The Emeralds

These luxurious open-plan or two-bedroom units, offer a soothing cream-beige aesthetic, gracefully blending with marble elements and naturally fallen wood. Handpicked pieces of furniture meet the mosaic sink and spacious walk-in shower, completing the suite’s unique design. The top-quality hardwood flooring is stating its presence, leading the way to your balcony or terrace. Location: Seafront Wing

### The Pavilions

Directly on the waterfront, the Pavilion area provides full access to the Haute Living Selection. An aura of aristocracy, in tones of pearl and ivory, and enticing sea views, distinguish the experience from anything else. Decorated with marble elements, designer furniture, and artwork, relax & unwind on a private wooden deck in your garden with jacuzzi and/or pool, below the palm trees. Location: Seafront Wing

### The HRH Villas

The HRH Villa series is the epitome of exceptional luxury and enhanced privacy. The interior combines classic timelessness with contemporary indulgence and comfort in pearl ivory tones, while celebrating the glorious, uninterrupted sea view. Located in the secluded Ultra Exclusive Area of the seafront wing, with direct access to the sea, it is meant to be your ideal spot to relax. Location: Seafront Wing

### Jade Suites

Spacious and minimalistic with shades of white, elegant design, and a homely aura, our brand-new jade suites are an intimate escape to comfort and luxury. Location: Panoramic Wing

### Opal Suites

These exquisite suites come with breathtaking views of the Ionian Sea and feature minimalistic elements of cream and light brown palettes, that encapsulate the warmth and relaxation of these romantic sanctuaries. The Grand Opal Suites are part of the Haute Living Selection. Location: Panoramic Wing
