### EVENTS AT L’AND

L’AND is the perfect place to turn your ideas into reality, whether large or small events, along the Resort and allied to the client preference. L’AND offers exclusive places and services, for your event to be an amazing experience.

### CORPORATE EVENTS

Corporate events can be organized in a cellar room with 174m2, so that L’AND’s concept with the vineyard, can be part of the event. The cellar room  is fully equipped with a screen, projector, sound system, flipchart, water and free wi-fi access.

As a complement to the event, group menus, coffee breaks and welcome drinks can be discussed upon the client interest.

Also, for a fully L’AND experience, accommodation is available upon previous request.

### WEDDINGS

Weddings are a dream, so imagine getting married in the middle of the vineyard with a castle in the background.

Weddings at L’AND have a maximum capacity of 120 people indoor and 250 outdoor. When choosing the Resort for this type of celebration, L’AND has the availability of the exclusive rental of the hotel, including the 25 suites, for a minimum of two nights stay.

Other conditions may be applied, so contact the sales & events department to know more.
