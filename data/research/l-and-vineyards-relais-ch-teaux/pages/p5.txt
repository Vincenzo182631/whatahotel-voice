### VINOTHERAPY SPA by VINOBLE COSMETICS

Set in the heart of the vineyards, the Vinotherapy Spa by renowned Austrian, vegan brand VINOBLE COSMETICS focuses on high-quality care products for face and body as well as sustainability.

The Vinoble spa treatments use wine by-products like grapes, seed, skins, pulp and stems to provide exceptional face and body rituals and massages that are completely unique.

Covering an area of 800m2, the Vinothérapie Spa by Vinoble is a tranquil sanctuary of relaxation and rejuvenation. Designed by Marcio Kogan, the spa interior is a symphony of natural effects. The warmth of wood and the contrasting elegance of the stone effect creates a serene environment.

The spa is complemented by an indoor heated pool (adults-only), sauna, hammam and treatment rooms suitable for the personalised sessions of treatment and relaxation.
