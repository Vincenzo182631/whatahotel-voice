### L’AND VINEYARDS

More than just a hotel, L’AND Vineyards is an exclusive wine retreat, just one hour drive from Lisbon, featuring only 35 suites and 9  pool villas, blending contemporary architecture with nature, creating an atmosphere of  sobriety, natural beauty, and tranquility.

Nested in the heart of the Alentejo, in a gently sloping south-facing valley of vineyardsoverlooking the skyline of the medieval Montemor Castle, L’AND Vineyards is a sanctuarywhere guests can immerse themselves in organic wine culture, make their own  wine at thewinery , and literally sleep beneath the star-filled Alentejo night sky in our Sky Suites

Designed by Promontório and Márcio Kogan, the hotel houses an exclusive art collection, including works by Michael Biberstein inspired by representations of the sky, as well as a curated selection of exclusive design pieces.

### ALENTEJO \| “THE PUREST LIGHT UPON EARTH”

L’AND Vineyards is located about 45 minutes from Lisbon’s International airport, 4 km from Montemor-o-Novo and only 20 minutes from Évora, the historic capital of Alentejo considered world heritage by UNESCO – it is the perfect starting point to discover the region. Discover one of the finest wine regions in the world, the wild Costa Vicentina beaches washed by the Atlantic Ocean, a unique landscape of open plains dotted with oaks, vineyards, olive groves and orange orchards, ancient, white-washed hilltop towns graced with nesting storks, and singular culture influenced by Neolithic civilizations, Phoenicians, Romans and Arabs.

#### DISCOVER MORE

### WINE CULTURE

Wine and wine culture are at the root of L’AND vineyards’ identity. Surrounded by six hectares of vineyards, composed of Touriga Nacional, Touriga Franca, Alicante Bouschet and Syrah red grapes varieties, and Gouveio white grape variety. The vineyard is organic, respecting biodiversity and the terroir’s potential. Guests have access to a wide range of wine related experiences. Guests and property owners may become members of the exclusive **L’AND Wine Club** which aims to promote wine knowledge and unveil the wine making experience.
