### STAR WATCHING

When was the last time you have looked up at the stars?

Night’s skies in Alentejo are simply bursting with stars and minimal light pollution make for a rather spectacular show.

We propose that you stop and contemplate the stars under the starry sky of Alentejo.

Right in the middle of Alentejo, there is a protected area which is internationally certified as a Dark Sky Reserve, or "Starlight Tourism Destination", which extends over an area of about 10.000 square kilometres.

Whether you’re an established astral enthusiast or a nebula newbie, with a powerful telescope and professional guidance on hand, the sky’s the limit. Guests can join a team of stargazing experts/astronomers to spend a different evening watching the wonders the sky has to show.

### CULTURAL ALENTEJO

TOUR IN MONTEMOR-O-NOVO CITY

A town where battles were fought for the nation’s freedom, where heroes were born and left their mark in history, Montemor-o-Novo has kept itself hugely humble until today.

VISIT TO MONTEMOR-O-NOVO CASTLE

In its day, the castle in Montemor-o-Novo was an impressive citadel perched on a hill. Today, the journey to the past begins with the entrance from Port Vila, guarded by the lofty clock tower, and continues through the old streets where guests can admire the remains of a medieval village.

GUIDED TOUR THROUGH ÉVORA

Led by a professional guide, discover the ancient culture and unique heritage of the city of Évora – The historic centre is encircled by a ring of medieval walls, and the city itself is marked by the presence of the Neolithic, Roman and Arab civilizations too. Évora has been classified by UNESCO as a Cultural Heritage site.
