#### VENETIAN STYLE

## Our Rooms

The Venetian materials may be opulent – brocade, silk, prized wood and marble – but the effect is light, intimate, playful, made for relaxation yet effortlessly stylish. Motifs borrowed from historic Veneto villas waltz over the walls and dance a pas de deux across the floor. We are always refreshing and making little improvements to our rooms and suites, teasing out the personality of every room, from the largest signature suites to the characterful classic rooms.

#### SIMPLY BREATHTAKING

## Suite Jules Verne Lagoon View

As original and inventive as the great French writer after whom it is named – who sojourned at the hotel in 1884 – this fifth-floor suite offers magnificent views over the lagoon and the island of San Giorgio from its many windows. Refinement and exuberant brio meet in the chic design scheme of a suite that features a bedroom, a separate living room and two bathrooms (one with bathtub and one with walk in shower). Complimentary champagne on arrival, a free minibar, homemade cookies and chocolates and breakfast served in the suite at no extra charge are included in the room rate.

#### Your Venetian Pied à Terre

## Suite Borges Lagoon View With Balcony

Dedicated to one of our most famous twentieth-century guests, the Argentine writer and fabulist Jorge Luis Borges, this recently renovated suite is an homage to a man of refinement and great personal style. Precious Venetian design details including Fortuny lamps and Murano vases adorn a suite that feels like an apartment, with its spacious living room, separate double bedroom and elegant marble-lined bathroom. With full lagoon views, the suite also features a small balcony. Complimentary champagne on arrival, a free minibar, homemade cookies and chocolates and breakfast served in the suite at no extra charge are included in the room rate.

#### Rooftop Romance

## Top Floor Suite With Panoramic Terrace Overlooking the Lagoon

Meticulously designed and adorned with exquisite furnishings, this suite stands out in its uniqueness, providing a sanctuary where every detail has been carefully curated to enhance your stay. It has a very large terrace, from which the view extends from the San Marco Basin to the Grand Canal, until reaching the roofs and bell towers of Venice, connecting guests to the timeless atmosphere of the city. In this terrace you can organize a romantic candlelight dinner getting in touch with our LPV Restaurant team. Complimentary champagne on arrival, a free minibar, homemade cookies and chocolates and breakfast served in the suite at no extra charge are included in the room rate.
