# LONDRA PALACE VENEZIA

The ultimate Venetian insider’s hotel, Londra Palace Venezia has been welcoming curious independent travellers since 1853. A short stroll from San Marco, it is both elegant salon and private retreat, stylish pied à terre and gateway to the authentic Venice few casual visitors discover.

## Rooms & Suites

Venice’s seductive meld of antique splendour and contemporary brio is reflected in the design mood of the hotel’s 52 rooms and suites.

Each one is different, each one plays on the colours, motifs and textures of this most ancient and modern of cities to conjure a warm, stylish ‘room of one’s own’.
