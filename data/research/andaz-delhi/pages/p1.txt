# 5-Star Lifestyle Hotel in Aerocity, New Delhi

Located just 7 minutes from IGI Airport, Andaz Delhi, by Hyatt, combines effortless connectivity with bold design and a distinct sense of place. With direct access to Gurugram, Dwarka and Yashobhoomi via the Dwarka Expressway tunnel, the hotel is well connected to key business, convention and residential hubs across the city. Set in Aerocity’s vibrant hospitality district, it also offers convenient access to Vasant Kunj and the diplomatic enclave of Chanakyapuri. Reflecting Delhi’s creative spirit through 401 art pieces inspired by the city’s culture and stories, Andaz Delhi features some of the largest guest rooms in Aerocity, many with runway views. Balancing the energy of the city with a sense of calm, the hotel offers an experience shaped by contemporary design, local storytelling and warm, intuitive hospitality.​

## Rooms & Suites

Each guest room is thoughtfully designed and decorated with locally inspired details, and each contains one of 401 unique artworks inspired by Delhi. If you are interested in our Hyatt Delhi Residences, which offer contemporary fully serviced apartments, please visit our Residences website to learn more.

## DINING

Details & Reservations

### AnnaMaya

Annamaya is a refined culinary destination offering an elevated all-day dining experience, celebrating the diversity of global cuisine in a sophisticated and inviting setting. Bathed in natural light, the expansive two-level space overlooks tranquil pool views, creating an elegant ambience that blends indoor comfort with a sense of openness. The thoughtfully curated menu brings together globally inspired flavours, culinary craftsmanship and an indulgent selection of signature dishes. Complemented by wholesome beverages and artfully crafted cocktails, Annamaya invites guests to savour a distinctive culinary experience where exceptional flavours and contemporary dining come together.

Details + Reservations

### Juniper Bar

Juniper, one of India’s first gin bars, is a vibrant space to unwind and connect. Enjoy 40 handcrafted gin and tonic infusions inspired by the botanical legacy of juniper, along with signature cocktails like the exclusive Delhi’s Fire. Sip at the communal bar, meet our expert bartenders, and savor pure ingredients, fresh herbs, and creative in-house infusions, celebrating gin every day at Juniper Bar.

### Soul Pantry

Soul Pantry celebrates a thoughtful approach to food, inspired by India’s rich culinary heritage and its diverse range of traditional grains. Rooted in an appreciation for wellbeing and mindful eating, the menu brings together fresh ingredients, vibrant flavours and a nature-inspired perspective, led by an all-women culinary team. A soulful dining experience awaits, with fresh salads, refreshing juices, pizzas served on nutrition-rich flatbreads, a daily selection of desserts and an array of beverages.

Details + Reservations

### In-Room Dining

In-Room Dining at Andaz Delhi is available round the clock to make sure you have everything you need. Whether it’s a glass of wine while you watch a movie, dinner for the kids or breakfast in bed, we’ll be happy to bring it up to your room. Call us on extension 0 to order. Savour it like never before with Hyatt Dining Club membership.
