Disappointing service and lack of transparency

Our booking included a USD 100 Hyatt Privé credit, but Andaz Delhi provided only INR 8,300, equivalent to USD 87.59. When challenged, the hotel failed to explain the exchange rate clearly and claimed a complimentary seven-minute airport transfer, supposedly worth USD 25, justified the shortfall. An Uber comparison showed a fare of around USD 4.50.

We also received conflicting explanations about who sets the exchange rate. Rather than resolving the issue professionally, the hotel directed us to our travel agent and defended its position.
