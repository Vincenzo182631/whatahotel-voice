# Reservations

For after-hours bookings, guests may play until 9PM with an advance reservation made prior to closing at 12PM.

Court, paddle, and racquet fees apply at all times. Play is available from 7AM–9PM.

Bike rentals are available during shop hours, and the gym is open 24 hours.

### Pickleball

La Quinta Resort is proud to offer 8 pickleball courts for resort guests and members.

Pickleball is the fastest growing sport in the United States and our staff of certified IPTPA professionals is here to introduce you to the game.

Lessons and clinics are available, and open play time is available for $40 per court per hour (paddles and balls not included).

For reservations, please call 760.564.7655 or visit the Tennis Shop before 3pm. Courts are available daily from 7am–9pm.
