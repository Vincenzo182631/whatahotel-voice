What are check-in times?

Check-in times at La Quinta Resort & Club begin at 4 P.M. We recommend arriving a bit early to enjoy our amenities while your room is being prepared.

What are check-out times?

Check-out time is 11 A.M. We understand travel plans can vary, so please inquire about late check-out options. Our team will do their best to accommodate your needs.

Can I modify bookings?

Yes, you can modify your bookings through our website or by contacting our reservations team. Please note that changes may be subject to availability and potential fees.
