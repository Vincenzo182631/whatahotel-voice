### Pool Rules & Safety

Please keep in mind that our pools are shared spaces for everyone to enjoy. For the comfort and safety of all guests, we ask that inflatables remain under 36 x 60 inches, personal music be listened to with headphones, and that coolers, glass containers, and outside food and beverages stay outside the PLUNGE and Main Pool areas. In accordance with county health regulations, dogs are not allowed on the pool decks or in the water. Thank you for helping us keep the pool experience relaxing and enjoyable for all.
