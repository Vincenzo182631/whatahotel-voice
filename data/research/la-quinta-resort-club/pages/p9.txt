Fall in the desert is something special. Cooler mornings, golden evenings, and seasonal touches around every corner make La Quinta Resort the perfect autumn retreat. Whether you’re here for the holidays or just a long weekend, here’s your guide to making the most of the season.

## Start at the Spa

Relax into the season with Spa La Quinta’s fall specials. The Citrus Balsam Swedish Massage, Harvest Glow Facial, and Autumn’s Vibrant Glow Body Therapy are available through November 30. Don’t miss the Great Gourd Manicure and Pedicure for a festive touch.

## Savor the Flavors of Fall

From maple-glazed turkey and bourbon pecan tart at Twenty6 to Adobe Grill’s bold turkey roulade and barbacoa de borrego, fall menus bring comfort and creativity to the table. Cooking and cocktail demos on Morgan’s patio make it interactive and delicious. Swing by the Marketplace for holiday treats and the return of the pumpkin spice latte.

## Celebrate the Season

Thanksgiving weekend includes a breakfast buffet, chef-led experiences, and festive menus across the resort. Join us for the pumpkin carving competition, Breakfast with Santa (November 30 and December 14), and the Tree Lighting Ceremony at 5 PM on November 30. Every Saturday night from November 29 to December 20, enjoy roasted nuts and desserts on the Plaza from 7 to 9 PM.

## End the Day Right

Back at your Starlight Casita, unwind under the desert sky. Stargazing from your private balcony is the perfect finish to a fall day.

## Bonus Holiday Magic

As the season shifts, the resort transforms into a winter escape with snow machines, movies on the lawn, roasted chestnuts, and cookie decorating demos. Stay cozy, and let the celebrations continue.
