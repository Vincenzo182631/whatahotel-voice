## Spa Policies & Etiquette    Services

(Prices include a 22% service charge)

### Day Facility Use & Passes

Access to the spa facilities are included for the day of your scheduled spa treatment. Spa access is not included with salon services unless a spa treatment has been scheduled or a day facility pass is purchased. Spa access is not included for children. A day facility pass can be purchased for $65 per person based on availability.

### Reservation & Cancellation Policy

Should you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, we ask that you provide 24 hours’ notice to avoid incurring the full therapy fee. Cancellations or modifications in less than 24 hours will require a charge of 100% of the treatment price.

### A Moment of Tranquility

Our spa is a haven of serenity. For your peace and privacy, please silence all electronic devices and ensure the use of spa whispers.

### Prices & Service Charge

Please note: Spa Pricing varies based on demand and peak periods. Service providers and spa staff who assist you during your visit will receive gratuities included in the cost of the treatment total. All prices include a 22% service charge to cover these gratuities. Additional gratuities are a matter of personal discretion, and should reflect your satisfaction with our services.

### Treatment Duration

To facilitate your time management, the therapy durations indicated correspond to the length of your treatment at Spa La Quinta. We respectfully request that you arrive at least 20 minutes prior to your appointment so that we may acquaint you with our facilities.

### Late Arrivals

Late arrivals may result in lost treatment time unless our schedule enables us to offer you the full service.

### Personal Belongings

We provide lockers for your personal belongings, but regret that we cannot be responsible for lost or damaged items. Please do not wear your jewelry to the spa. Due to the space limitation, we cannot accommodate luggage or sports equipment.
