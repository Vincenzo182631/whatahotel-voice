### Painted Canyon/Ladder Canyon Mecca Hills

Formed by convergence of North American and Pacific tectonic plates along San Andreas Fault. Strenuous, self guided hike through multiple slot canyons.

Be sure to have a map and plenty of water.
Hike with caution.
Must have four-wheel drive or similar as dirt road leads to trailhead (prone to flash flooding).
Contains ladders and ropes to ascend canyons.

### Tahquitz Canyon

Area near Palm Springs on the Agua Caliente Band of Cahuilla Indians reservation. Home to 60 foot waterfall, rock art, and native wildlife. Ranger-led and self-guided interpretive hikes.

Ranger-led hikes approximately 2.5 hours.
Self-guided hikes 7:30 am – 3:30 pm.

### Indian Canyons

Ancestral home of the Agua Caliente band of Cahuilla Indians, and especially sacred. Ancient trails, rock art, house pits, and ancient foundations still exist.

Palm Canyon – moderately graded, good for picnicking and hiking.
Andreas Canyon – tranquil one mile hiking loop and year-round flowing stream.
Murray Canyon – foot and equestrian trails lead to recreation area.
Ranger-led and self-guided hikes.
