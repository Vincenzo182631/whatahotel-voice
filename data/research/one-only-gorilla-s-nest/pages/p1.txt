# Heart of Rwanda

Discover the destination

#### Eye to eye with swaying eucalyptus trees, One&Only Gorilla’s Nest is the closest resort to Volcanoes National Park – a historic rainforest home to a diverse tapestry of wildlife, including 12 families of endangered mountain gorillas.

TRANSFERS

### Seamless arrivals

One&Only Gorilla’s Nest is a 2.5-hour scenic drive (or 25-minute flight) from Kigali International Airport. We can arrange transfers in a luxury SUV or a chartered helicopter.

NATURAL CONNECTIONS

### Rwandan roots

The volcanic mountains of Volcanoes National Park were famously beloved of Dian Fossey. Our resort fosters deep cultural connections with the surrounding areas of Kinigi and Musanze, supporting co-operatives and empowering local people.

SUSTAINABILITY

### Protecting the planet

Our resort runs a range of initiatives aimed at social development and conservation. Our craft programme and coffee farming cooperative transform the lives of local women.

AWARDS

### Global recognition

In 2025, we were one of 20 destinations named within Forbes Travel Guide’s The Edge List; we also ranked #8 in the Top Resorts in Africa at the Condé Nast Readers' Choice Awards.
