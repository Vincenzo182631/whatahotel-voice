Inspired by Italy’s southern islands

Isola Ristorante offers a casual dining experience with an authentic menu featuring Italian classics and a wide range of wines from the best wine-producing regions in Italy. An invitation to savour the most authentic essence of Italy’s southern islands through a curated selection of the best ingredients and traditional recipes that enhance the most popular dishes from the culinary heritage of the islands of Capri, Sicily and Sardinia with a modern design flair. Along with other restaurants in Dubai, Bodrum, Marbella and Bouznika, Isola Restaurant is a Sunset Hospitality Group experience.

### Our culinary philosophy

A dish that seems simple and familiar at first sight, but which reveals a surprising explosion of flavors with the first bite: This is the essence of the culinary philosophy of our chef Francesco Bagnato at Isola Ristorante. This restaurant has made simplicity and clarity of flavor its hallmark. Chef Salvatore De Muro brings this vision to life through meticulous research and a selection of the finest local ingredients.

,regionOfInterest=(2737.5,4104.5))

### A unique atmosphere

Located on the top floor of the iconic Palazzo Cordusio Gran Meliá, Isola Restaurant presents a unique dining experience in the vibrant heart of Milan. This exquisite venue is nestled in a bustling area where haute couture meets refined elegance, creating an ambiance that perfectly reflects the city's rich cultural tapestry. Whether you fancy a romantic dinner, a sophisticated lunch, or a lively gathering with friends, Isola Restaurant promises a memorable experience infused with the spirit of Italy’s southern islands.

,regionOfInterest=(1433.5,2150))

### An exclusive wine collection

The wine list celebrates the essence of Italy’s major islands: Sicily, Sardinia, and Capri. These regions, rich in history and tradition, embody the Mediterranean culture of the South and inspire the selection of wines. To cater to an international clientele, the list features both Italian and foreign labels, reflecting the prestige of Palazzo Cordusio, frequented by guests from all corners of the globe.

## Culinary spaces

### Isola Bar

Elevating cocktails with an authentic Mediterranean flair

Isola Bar offers Mediterranean-inspired cocktails that enhance contemporary classics, using authentic ingredients from the south of Italy. The selection of liquors places a special emphasis on Italy, featuring products like Etna liqueur, along with a versatile range of spirits. Highlights include exquisite options like Louis XIII cognac and Johnny Walker Blue, regarded as the finest blended Scotch whisky. Each season, Isola Bar introduces a new beverage menu to keep the experience fresh and exciting.

,regionOfInterest=(2150,1434))

### Isola Terrace

Experience Milan from above

Isola Terrace is a charming outdoor space located next to Isola Ristorante, perfect for those looking to enjoy a unique atmosphere with breathtaking views. This al fresco area offers stunning views of Milan's Duomo and the city skyline, making it an ideal setting for a relaxed lunch or an evening aperitif.
