## Culinary spaces

### Intentional Design

A refined interior

Within the interior space of Isola Bar, aesthetics and comfort coexist in perfect balance. Iconic pieces of contemporary furniture and exquisite attention to detail create a sophisticated yet welcoming atmosphere, designed for unhurried enjoyment in a setting with its own distinct identity.

### Isola Terrace

With views of the Duomo

A privileged setting where the city unfolds before you. With the spires of the Duomo as a backdrop, the terrace invites you to enjoy open-air cocktails in an elegant ambiance, where the charm of Milan is experienced with naturalness and style.
