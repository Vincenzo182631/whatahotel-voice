## Gastronomic proposal

,regionOfInterest=(533.5,800))

### Robata grill

The grill enhances bold, smoky flavours through slow cooking over glowing embers, offering an immersive dining experience that pays tribute to the art of Japanese cuisine. With masterful precision, our chefs grill premium seafood, choice meats, and seasonal vegetables, elevating each flavour to its fullest expression.

,regionOfInterest=(2000,1333.5))

### Sushi and sashimi

Our product selection is a tribute to the artistry of traditional Japanese cuisine. Each piece is meticulously crafted using only the freshest, highest-quality fish, hand-selected and prepared with the utmost care and respect. From delicately sliced sashimi to expertly formed nigiri, every bite delivers the perfect balance of texture, temperature, and taste.

,regionOfInterest=(2150,1433.5))

### Wine, sake and whisky

Our curated selection of wines, premium sake, and Japanese whisky is designed to complement and elevate the flavours of our menu. We carefully source from both renowned international producers and exclusive boutique labels, offering a unique tasting journey that blends tradition and innovation.

,regionOfInterest=(2000,1333.5))

### Elegant spaces for every occasion

Versatile and refined, Sachi offers the ideal setting for private events. Discover an open kitchen, a breathtaking rooftop terrace, and a modern ambiance that can be tailored to suit any occasion. From corporate dinners to exclusive parties, every event is elevated by our refined Japanese cuisine and impeccable service.
