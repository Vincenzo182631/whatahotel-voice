## Luxury and design, a stone's throw from the Duomo and the fashion district

The first Gran Meliá in Milan is a 19th-century architectural jewel that combines timeless Italian style with service that reflects warm Spanish hospitality. This elegant hotel, the brand's flagship, is located in the heart of the city, near the Piazza del Duomo and the Quadrilatero della Moda, connecting you with the rich cultural and artistic heritage of Milan. Enjoy next-level Italian and international cuisine while taking in the spectacular 360° views of the city of Milan.

Discover the Mediterranean heritage of “the good life”, immersed in the elegance, beauty and rich traditions of Milan. Located in the historic Palazzo Venezia, this luxury hotel and masterpiece by Luca Beltrami has been redesigned as a place where timeless elegance and avant-garde design converge in harmony. Every detail tells the story of Milan: from its iconic legacy of fashion and design to its deep cultural roots. This hotel is a member of The Leading Hotels of the World.

## Culture & Essence

### An elegant take on history

The former headquarters of Assicurazioni Generali, where this hotel is located, were designed in the 19th century by the famous architect Luca Beltrami, who designed the Piazza La Scala and the restoration of the Castello Sforzesco. Following the arrival of the first Gran Meliá in Milan, the splendour of its façade has been restored, maintaining its eclectic flair and elegant reliefs, such as the lion of San Marco and the niche with the allegorical mosaic of Providence.
