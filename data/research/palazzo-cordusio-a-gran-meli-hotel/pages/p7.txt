Guardians of the art of classic mixology

Welcome to Giardino, where each cocktail is a work of art. Located inside Milan’s elegant Gran Melia Palazzo Cordusio, this classic bar prides itself in upholding traditions that have withstood the test of time and changing trends. Gather and enjoy the best of Italian mixology, alongside a variety of light bites crafted to impress even the most discerning tastes.

### Cocktails

Embark on a magical journey like no other. Each artistically presented cocktail is a masterpiece, paying tribute to the perfect harmony between botanical inspiration and timeless craftsmanship, as our skilled bartenders lead a joyful dance of liqueurs, creating authentic elixirs to delight both the palate and eye. Our menu has something to suit all tastes, from silky classic Martinis, bold Negronis and refreshing spritzes.

,regionOfInterest=(1824,2736)),regionOfInterest=(1824,2736))

### Light bites

Embark on a journey of flavour and experiences, with classic Italian delights with an international flair, ranging from classic focaccia and pasta-based dishes to the more adventurous lobster rolls with homemade brioche and salads. Perfect for sharing or enjoying alone, head chef Andre Nani lovingly creates hot and cold savoury delicacies to please all palates, as well as a rich variety of sweet treats.

,regionOfInterest=(1824,2736)),regionOfInterest=(1824,2736))

### Negroni in Anfora

Preserved in a special ‘Anfora’, this classic delight offers a perfect fusion of fresh citrus notes, thanks to a skilful blend of Martini Rubino Riserva, Martini Ambrato Riserva, China Martini, Tanqueray No.TEN, Martini Bitter Riserva and Cocchi Americano Bianc. Served in an old-fashioned cocktail glass over ice, this bold blend is not for the faint-hearted.
