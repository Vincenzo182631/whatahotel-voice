### Weekend getaway

6 days ago

Top level service like A Leading Hotel of The World
From the very first moment, arriving on the property with my car and my ESA dog it was a Breeze and so welcoming.

– William,, the Bellman, who took my luggage and remembered me from My last day with my dog. Greeted me with welcome back.
– The valet so quick to give me detail details for my Car via text
– The Front Desk young man, Eastern European his name started with the letter T, Was incredibly welcoming. He said thank you for being an IHG Member And welcome. He was unbelievably friendly. When I gave him my papers for my ESA dog, he said yes we have you in the system so he didn’t need anything. I just made it so much easier.
-The club lounge was so friendly. Thank you to Maria Amelia Davila in the lounge for helping my stay and helping me check out at the end. I love having the club access, especially since I have my dog with me. It’s so convenient and puts me at ease
\- I stayed in the loft suite and I absolutely loved it for my short weekend away. It was so relaxing. I felt like I had my own apartment. It rained most of the weekend which I love so I could look out the window. My dog loved the space as well. She especially loved The windowsill.
I especially love that there are two entrances to the loft. The top level at the bedroom where I use the closet and safe. And I use the bottom level entry after coming back walking my dog.
\- My two room service deliveries were also super friendly staff and the tray is beautifully set.
\- check out was a breeze as I did it with Maria Amelia Davila at the club while they had A cappuccino and cookie.

-forgot to mention that the king bed Mattress and pillows are like Heaven. The sheets are also so soft
