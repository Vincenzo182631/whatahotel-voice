## Artful Beginnings

The InterContinental Miami began in 1982 with art: Henry Moore's 18-foot, 70-ton Spindle sculpture. Architect Pietro Belluschi designed the Grand Lobby around it, creating an elegant entrance with Italian marble and precious African wood.

## Fun facts about InterContinental Miami

InterContinental Miami hosts 500K visitors annually. Originally a casino, it hosts the famous Orange Drop on New Year's Eve (25+ years). The hotel has hosted 6 Super Bowls and the Presidential Hemispheric Conference.

How far is InterContinental Miami from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Miami is conveniently located near Miami International Airport (MIA), approximately 7.2 miles away (11.59 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
