## A Modern Makeover

Our guest rooms and suites blend modern elegance with tropical style, featuring new furnishings and 55"-75" HD Smart TVs. The newly reimagined 29th-floor Club InterContinental Lounge offers stunning views, personalized service, and exclusive privileges for Club guests.

## Always Complimentary

Enjoy our compliments: in-room Nespresso coffees and teas, bathrobes, and slippers. All guests receive complimentary access to our 24/7 Fitness Center, Steam Room, and Sauna. We also have a filtered water station in the lobby with free, unlimited refills.

## Suite Inclusions

Suite and Club InterContinental rooms include access to the Club Lounge. Enjoy exclusive benefits: Daily breakfast, Afternoon tea, and Evening Happy Hour—a luxurious addition to your stay.

## Elevated Enhancements

Enjoy enhanced luxury with bedside USB & C outlets, perfect for charging. Our rooms feature thoughtful details like a curated mini-bar, personal mini fridge, individual reading lights, and a bistro table for comfortable in-room dining.
