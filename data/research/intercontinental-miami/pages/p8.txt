## South Beach

Explore South Beach: famed for its glamorous scene, beaches, and celebrity-chef eateries. Stroll Lincoln Road Mall for shopping, or visit Ocean Drive for Art Deco architecture and outdoor cafes. Discover modern art at the Wolfsonian-FIU Museum.

## Nikki Beach Miami Beach

Nikki Beach Miami Beach is a chic hot spot and restaurant right on the sand. Enjoy delicious seafood and expertly crafted cocktails in a vibrant, stylish atmosphere, making it a must-visit destination for beachfront luxury.

## Recreation

### PortMiami

PortMiami, the world’s largest cruise passenger port, is a major seaport in Biscayne Bay at the mouth of the Miami River. Formerly known as the Port of Miami and officially styled as the Dante B. Fascell Port of Miami in some references, it is also a vital U.S. cargo port in Miami, Florida.

Learn More

### Star Island

Explore Star Island, an exclusive man-made island neighborhood in Biscayne Bay, off Miami Beach. Located south of the Venetian Islands and east of Palm Island and Hibiscus Island, this coveted area is known for its luxury homes and iconic Miami waterfront views.

Learn More

### Zoo Miami

Zoo Miami, the Miami-Dade Zoological Park and Gardens, is the largest zoo in Florida. This renowned zoological park and garden offers an exceptional experience, making it a premier attraction for wildlife enthusiasts visiting Miami.

Learn More

### MiamiCentral Station

MiamiCentral Station provides access to Brightline intercity rail and Tri-Rail commuter service. Located in Downtown Miami, it offers seamless connectivity throughout South Florida, making travel and exploration easy.

Learn More

### Bayfront Park

Miami's oldest public gathering space, established in 1896. Enjoy a short walk to this historic waterfront landmark, featuring lush green areas and the popular Bayfront Park Amphitheatre, directly from your luxury Miami base.

Learn More

### Kaseya Center

The Kaseya Center is a multi-purpose arena located on Biscayne Bay in Miami, Florida. It is the official home court for the Miami Heat of the National Basketball Association (NBA).

Learn More

## Museums

### 3251 S Miami Ave, Miami, FL 33129Vizcaya Museum and Gardens​

Visit the magnificent Vizcaya Museum and Gardens (formerly Villa Vizcaya), the historic estate of businessman James Deering. Located on Biscayne Bay in Miami's Coconut Grove, it offers a stunning glimpse into Gilded Age luxury and beautiful, historic grounds.

### 1101 Biscayne Blvd, Miami, FL 33132Phillip & Patricia Frost Museum of Science

Explore the Phillip & Patricia Frost Museum of Science, a hands-on facility offering fascinating weather & technology exhibits, an immersive planetarium, and a state-of-the-art aquarium. Perfect for curious minds.

### 1103 Biscayne Blvd, Miami, FL 33132Perez Art Museum Miami

Visit the Pérez Art Museum Miami (PAMM), a contemporary art museum located in Maurice A. Ferré Park in Downtown Miami. This cultural institution, officially the Jorge M. Pérez Art Museum, is a highlight of the city’s vibrant arts scene.

## Shops

### 1620 Drexel Ave Suite 100, Miami, FL 33139Lincoln Road Mall

Explore the vibrant Lincoln Road Mall, a pedestrian shopping area featuring premium brands. Enjoy nearby dining and easy access to the beach. InterContinental Miami offers an ideal location for exploring this cosmopolitan destination.

### 701 S Miami Ave, Miami, FL 33130Brickell City Centre

Brickell City Centre is a cosmopolitan destination featuring upscale shopping, dining, and entertainment. This vibrant hub, located near our hotel, offers a blend of modern luxury and activity for guests seeking world-class experiences

### 4000 NE 1st Ave, Miami, FL 33137Miami Design District

Explore the Miami Design District, known for its sleek modern architecture, upscale fashion, art galleries, and celebrity-chef dining. Discover unique public art, including the Fly’s Eye Dome, and cutting-edge work at the Institute of Contemporary Art (ICA).

### 401 Biscayne Blvd, Miami, FL 33132Bayside Marketplace

Visit Bayside Marketplace, a two-story, open-air shopping center located on the banks of Biscayne Bay in Miami. Situated next to the City of Miami marina, it offers a vibrant mix of shopping and entertainment right on the waterfront.

### Miami, FL 33131Flager Street

Flagler Street is Miami's main 12.4 mile east–west road, serving as the latitudinal baseline for the Miami-Dade County grid. It divides all local streets into North or South, making it a pivotal thoroughfare in the city.

### Miami, FL 33131Little Havana - Calle Ocho

Bienvenido to Little Havana! The vibrant heart of Miami’s Cuban culture is Calle Ocho (SW 8th Street). Experience the energy of music, food, and history, marked by colorful, six-foot rooster sculptures—proud emblems of Cuban resilience.
