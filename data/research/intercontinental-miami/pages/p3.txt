# InterContinental MiamiDining

Discover world-class Miami dining experiences. Indulge in Latin-inspired dining at Toro Toro, poolside cocktails at Bluewater, and daily full-service dining at Ole, including a breakfast buffet. For added convenience, enjoy Starbucks and in-room dining.

## Toro Toro

**Location:** Lobby

**Phone:** 1-305-3724710

Toro Toro is a modern steakhouse known for dry-aged USDA Prime steaks cooked over a wood fire and served rodizio-style. We infuse Miami’s vibrant Latin cuisine into small plates and hot dishes, featuring the finest churrasco, picanha, and lomo.

Signature Steakhouse

## Starbucks

**Hours:** 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM

At InterContinental Miami, our on-site Starbucks offers a welcoming space to start your day. Enjoy a thoughtfully prepared cup of coffee in the comfort of our lobby as you prepare for the experiences ahead.

## Bluewater

**Location:** Rooftop

**Restaurant phone:** 1-305-3724710

Reserve a private poolside Cabana, then meander to our stunning rooftop bar. Enjoy refreshing drinks and light bites with tranquil, memorable views of the cruise ships in port. Tables and bar seating are available for food and drink service.

**Lunch:** 10:00 AM-6:00 PM

## Ole

**Location:** Lobby

**Restaurant phone:** 1-305-5771000(6005)

Start your day peacefully at our bright, friendly breakfast restaurant. Indulge in our expansive breakfast buffet or splurge on a la carte favorites like steak and eggs. We offer a perfect blend of healthy and decadent options to energize your morning.

**Breakfast:** Saturday-Sunday: 7:00 AM-12:00 PM

Monday-Friday: 7:00 AM-11:00 AM

## In-Room Dining

Indulge in the comfort of your room with our thoughtfully curated In-Room Dining service.

**Breakfast:** Sunday-Thursday, from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM

Friday-Saturday, from 7:00 AM to 12:00 PM

**All-Day Dining:** Daily 11:00 AM to Midnight
