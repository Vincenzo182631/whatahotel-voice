# Reside in a contemporary Suzhou Mansion by Jinji Lake \| Park Hyatt Suzhou

## Presenting the rich history of traditional Suzhou Mansion and the natural beauty of Jinji Lake through modern interior design

Stay in a contemporary version of a traditional Suzhou manor while exploring a city steeped in over 2,500 years of history. The ‘Venice of the East’ offers myriad attractions including the UNESCO World Heritage-listed ‘Classical Gardens of Suzhou’.

## Discover Park Hyatt Suzhou

The Spa

The Spa, located on B1, is dedicated to improving our guests’ physical, mental and emotional wellbeing through personalized treatments. In five tranquil treatment rooms—each with a Suzhou flower theme—our highly skilled therapists utilize the best of Eastern and Western healing techniques to relax, rejuvenate and reenergize.

### Living Room

A modern interpretation of the traditional Ladies’ Court comprises three distinctively designed spaces—a feminine room with hand-painted murals; a more formal, elegant area; and a striking space with a double-story ceiling dominated by slatted timber and an oversized lantern. Enjoy light snacks, afternoon tea and premium drinks throughout the day.
