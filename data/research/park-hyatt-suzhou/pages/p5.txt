### Mediocre hotel with good location

The Hyatt Regency was about what I expected it to be given the price point. The location is good, adjacent to a mall and some of the built up areas of Jinji Lake. The gym was dark and not appealing. The room was basic, showing signs of age, but also totally fine. I wish the water pressure in the shower were higher. The breakfast was quite good- the shengjian bao were excellent. It's a good bang for your buck, but not exactly a place that you'd look forward to coming back to.
