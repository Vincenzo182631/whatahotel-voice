## TravellingtoVelaa

##### Velana International Airport, Male

The island of Velaa is reachable by a scenic seaplane ride of 45 minutes either by Velaa’s customised plane on a private charter (9 seater) or on a commercial seaplane (15 seater).

All guests are greeted upon arrival by a Velaa airport butler and directly escorted to the private lounge, with refreshment facilities and a place to rest before the seaplane transfer.

##### Maafaru International Airport, Noonu Atoll

Private jets can comfortably land at Maafaru International Airport, with just a 7-minute seaplane distance or a relaxed 35-minute luxury yacht ride from Velaa.
