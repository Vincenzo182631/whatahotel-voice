# Spaceto _move&play_,theislandway

Velaa is designed to help you stay at your best. Under open skies and surrounded by greenery, even training feels different. A match, a climb, a session in the gym or yoga on the sand. Movement blends effortlessly with the flow of your day.

# Thebeautyof

# _theperfectswing_

The Velaa Golf Academy is a golfer’s idea of paradise, and it shows. Designed by the legendary José María Olazábal, our 9-hole Par-3 course celebrates the art of control and precision. With one-on-one PGA coaching, a TrackMan simulator, and advanced swing analysis, every session brings pro-level focus.
