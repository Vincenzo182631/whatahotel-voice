# Wellbeing

Velaa Wellbeing Village is a vast world of care, unlike anything else in the Maldives. Bespoke treatments, thoughtful movement, and mindful dining come together with ease. It is a place to reconnect with what feels most true to you.

# Yourmostpersonaljourney, _areturntoyourself_

Think of our Village as a place for gentle transformation. Nothing is rushed. It begins with listening and quiet observation, at the hands of specialists who notice the small details that often go unseen. Each session moves at a natural pace, letting your body and mind respond in their own way, on their own time.

View Wellbeing Programme

## Theonlyexperienceofitskind, _withEveylaaatitsheart_

Eveylaa is care shaped around how you want to feel. By blending Ayurvedic traditions with modern diagnostics, we create a map of your wellbeing. The result is a tailored plan that supports balance while you're here, and is easy to carry with you when you leave.

BODY TREATMENTS

## Readingthebody _asonewhole_

We look beyond the surface to understand what you need. It might be the way you carry yourself, a slight hesitation in your step. These subtleties tell us how to best offer relief.

- Holistic and Ayurvedic treatments

- Physiotherapy and manual therapies

- High-tech diagnostic wellness screening

- Blood, food sensitivity, and allergy testing

MINDFULNESS

## Quietingthemind _tounderstandit_

In a world that rarely stops, you finally hear yourself clearly. We soothe the mind back to its natural state of calm and give you the tools to keep it there.

- Yoga and meditation practices

- Breathwork and sound healing

- Mind therapy and inner balance techniques

- Cloud 9 and Dream Pod experiences

NUTRITION

## Sustainingwhat’sbeen _restored_

True wellbeing satisfies all senses. As you settle into alignment, we create rich, replenishing meals that sustain your progress, without ever feeling like a restriction.

- Tailored menus and long-term nutritional guidance

- Fresh juices, healing teas, and probiotic drinks

- Seasonal dishes aligned with your programme

- Personalised guidance from wellness consultants

FITNESS

## Movingwith _revivedpurpose_

Fitness isn’t about pushing through. We design a plan that evolves with you. No pressure, no judgment. Just tailored routines that let progress come naturally.

- Personal training and functional workouts

- Boxing, circuits, aqua training, spinning, TRX

- Pilates, Tabata, HIIT, and fitness classes

- State-of-the-art Technogym equipment

- InBody composition analysis
