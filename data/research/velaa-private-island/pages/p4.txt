# KidsClub

Velaa belongs to our little guests just as much as the adults. Whether they're discovering the fun of Maldivian baseball on the sand or finding their light in a talent show, these are the moments they will always remember. A time when anything was possible.

# _Newactivities_  aroundeverycorner

Lha Velaa Kids Club is a joyful mix of island quests, wonderfully messy crafts, and high-spirited fun — designed to match the boundless energy of childhood. And there's always a new event popping up, for nonstop surprises. More than just play, it's about giving kids the freedom to explore in all their favourite ways.

## Madeforgiggles,bravehearts _&curiousminds_

- Water and outdoor play for sunny, splashy adventures

- An energy studio and games that get everyone moving

- Sensory experiences that bring sound, light, and motion to life

- Cooking classes and experiments that make learning a blast

- Indoor climbing and play spaces built for restless explorers

- Quiet creative corners with reading nooks and cosy spaces
