# Dining

From rare Champagnes to the theatre of Japanese teppanyaki, dining at Velaa is about more than food. It’s about the right atmosphere, for the right moment — whether that calls for a curated evening or a relaxed drink with sand beneath your feet.

##### Chef Gaushan De Silva

ARAGU

## Theheightofelegance,

## _inspiredbytheocean_

The inimitable Aragu is where East meets West in a setting of calm sophistication. Our award-winning kitchen prepares dishes with artful precision, paired with exceptional wines and the gentle sound of the piano. An evening designed to be savoured slowly.

Dining hours from 7 PM - 11 PM.

TAVARU

## Thetowerofdiscovery,where _firemeetsthesky_

High above the palms, Tavaru offers a dramatic teppanyaki experience. The grill crackles, sparks fly, and all around you, the sweeping view that puts Velaa in the palm of your hand.

## TasteyourwaythroughtheMaldives’ _mostexclusivewinecollection_

Hidden within the Tavaru Tower lies the Maldives’ largest private wine cellar. It's our pleasure to invite you inside, for anything from first discoveries to deep explorations of rare international labels.

FAIY

## Freshfromthegarden,

## light _&_ _fulloflife_

Nestled among lush greenery, Faiy focuses on balanced, seasonal dishes as vibrant as the nature around you. Clean food, beautifully simple, and made to nourish — in the most delicious way.

ATHIRI

## _Belovedflavours_,

## cookedtoperfection

Athiri is the centre of daily life at Velaa. Chefs from 14 nations prepare global dishes with a twist, blending the comfort foods you love with local ingredients. All served in a warm, easygoing space ideal for a long lunch or a spontaneous snack between swims.

CRU

## Atoasttothehorizon

## with _thefinestChampagne_

With the sea as its backdrop, Cru is a space of quiet refinement. Our sommeliers guide you through premium cuvées and rare vintages, always with something new to discover. Unhurried, personal, and perfectly chilled.

AVI & BEACH BAR

## _Barefootluxury_,fromsunsetcocktailstosimplepleasures

AVI is your poolside lounge for creative drinks and live music as the day turns to dusk. For something more playful, the Beach Bar offers crisp drinks and light bites right by the water.

# Makeanymoment _distinctlyyours_

Some occasions call for more than a table. From family picnics to proposals and extravagant galas, simply tell us what you imagine. Whether it's a milestone or a lovely lunch on the beach enjoyed just the way you like it — we'll be delighted to bring it to life.
