# Villas

Whether you seek a quiet one-bedroom retreat or a sprawling residence of 1,300 m², our homes adapt to you and the life you bring with you. We believe true luxury is comfort you can’t quite put into words. It’s not just what you see. It’s what you feel.

# Inharmonywithnature,designed _forthesoul_

At Velaa, nature is the architect. Every home is filled with soft natural light, endless ocean views, and the calming colours of sand and sea. Materials are chosen to breathe with the island — simple, elegant, quietly honouring the surroundings.
