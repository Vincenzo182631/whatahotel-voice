# Jara by Martín Berasategui

At Jara, flavour and celebration come together high above the Dubai skyline. Beautiful seafood and charcoal-grilled steaks are prepared in the lively open kitchen, filling the restaurant with irresistible energy. Celebrated chef Martín Berasategui brings his renowned culinary craft to Dubai, creating a vibrant dining destination where fresh ingredients and unforgettable evenings take centre stage.

THE CHEF

The mastery of Martín Berasategui

Martín Berasategui is one of the most decorated chefs in the world and the Spanish chef with the most Michelin stars to his name. At Jara, he brings his remarkable culinary craft and passion for exceptional ingredients to Dubai.

Meet the chef

THE CONCEPT

Fire, flavour and celebration

Beautiful seafood and charcoal-grilled steaks are prepared over fire in the open kitchen, creating a celebratory atmosphere. Inspired by chef Martín Berasategui's Basque heritage, the restaurant captures the warmth and generosity of great dining.

THE BAR

Dining above Dubai's skyline

As the fire dances in the open kitchen, the city unfolds below. From Jara’s elevated setting, sweeping skyline views become part of the experience, adding a sense of drama to every table and making each evening feel unmistakably Dubai.

THE VIEW

Where the evening begins

At Jara Bar, the evening begins with beautifully crafted cocktails and small plates inspired by the restaurant’s fire-led cooking. Its lively energy and sweeping views make it the perfect place to gather before dinner or linger long into the night.

> I'm inspired every day by the ingredients I’m able to use, and discovering new ways of cooking with them. There are so many incredible elements in Dubai; the sheer quantity and variety of cuisine here is mind-blowing.

Martín Berasategui

Executive Chef

Land & sea

Beautiful seafood and charcoal-grilled steaks define the menu at Jara. From pristine shellfish to prime cuts cooked over fire, every dish celebrates bold flavour and the pleasure of incredible ingredients.

A sweet finale

No evening at Jara is complete without dessert. From the restaurant’s signature Basque cheesecake to warm almond cake and Martín Berasategui’s celebrated torrija, each dish offers a deliciously indulgent finale to the night.

Cocktails and pintxos

Begin the evening at Jara Bar with beautifully crafted cocktails and vibrant pintxos – Spanish bites designed for sharing. Inspired by the restaurant’s fire-led cooking, they’re perfect with drinks before dinner.
