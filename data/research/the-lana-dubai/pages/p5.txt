For those in the know

An exclusive lounge, serving more than 45 cigars from Cuba to the Dominican Republic, Nicaragua and Honduras, where those with discerning taste can settle in with the spirit of their choice.

THE CONCEPT

A new angle on Dubai

Take your seat near the soaring windows for a vantage point over Dubai's shimmering skyline. As the sun goes down, you'll see the city's towers light up in a glittering show that lasts long into the night.
