Warm, wooden panelling, banquette seating and distinctive artworks create a striking first impression, distinguishing it from the lobby beyond. Settle yourself in at your chosen table and wait for the trolley to come to you.

Pre-dinner cocktails

This is where cocktails are mixed and muddled, shaken and stirred. At the beginning of your evening, when you’re considering what possibilities lay ahead, why not kick-start proceedings with a cocktail or two and set things off in style.

After-dinner cocktails

Who says the night has to end? For those evenings when you want the night to go on beyond dinner, we highly recommend stopping by for a signature cocktail or a comforting nightcap. Choose from our honey-inspired menu or opt for something bespoke.

A beautiful beginning

We've started crafting your cocktail long before any liquid hits the shaker. First, we mix our own homemade syrups to add sophistication, depth and creativity to every sip. Then we make sure we've sourced the finest spirits, imparting just the right flavour to each drink.
