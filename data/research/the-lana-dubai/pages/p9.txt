## A taste of the Mediterranean

# Riviera by Jean Imbert

Throughout the day and into the evening, Riviera evokes the feeling of the South of France, with a breezy freshness and boundless sunshine. Overlooking the sparkling waters of Marasi Bay Marina, expect modern Mediterranean cuisine inspired by Jean's own experiences.
