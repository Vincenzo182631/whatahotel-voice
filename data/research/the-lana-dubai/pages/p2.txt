Frequently asked questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is at 12pm (noon).

How many rooms qualify as a group booking?

Bookings of six or more rooms at The Lana, Dubai, are treated as group reservations.

Does The Lana have suites?

Yes, we offer an array of luxury suites in Business Bay, Dubai. All of our suites have private balconies, floor-to-ceiling windows, and cinematic, soaring views.

How much does an extra bed cost?

An extra bed for guests aged 18 or over is AED 450 per night. For children and infants, an extra bed or cot is complimentary.

How do I make a reservation?

Please contact the hotel directly for reservations and any pre-booking requirements. You can reach us by phone at +971 4 541 7777, or by email at info.LAN@dorchestercollection.com.

Can I bring my dog to the room?

We regret that we are unable to accommodate pets in rooms, with the exception of service dogs.

Is breakfast included with my room?

Breakfast is generally charged separately (from AED 190 per person); please check your specific rate for inclusions.

How can I extend my stay or arrange a later departure?

Early check-in or late check-out may be possible depending on availability: however, please note that a charge of the full nightly rate will apply. If this is something you require, we suggest including an extra night in your stay on booking.

Is parking included with my stay?

Yes, parking is complimentary with our valet service. Please note there are no electric vehicle charging ports at The Lana; the closest charging points are at Dubai Mall.

Is an extra bed available for children?

Yes, a complimentary extra bed is available for children aged under 18.
