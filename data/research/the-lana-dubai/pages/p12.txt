What are the spa opening hours?

Dior Spa The Lana is open every day, from 7am to 9pm.

Is this the only Dior Spa in Dubai?

Yes, our spa is the only Dior Spa in Dubai. It's also the first Dior Spa in the Middle East.

Can I get a pool day pass?

If you're staying at the hotel, you're welcome to use the pool whenever you choose during opening hours (7am to 8pm for in-house guests). If you're just joining us for the day, you can purchase a pool day pass to enjoy our Business Bay location, soaring views, and glittering water. Opening hours for day pass guests are 10am to 5.30pm.

When is the pool open?

The pool is open daily from 7am to 8pm.

Can children use the pool?

Children must be accompanied by a parent until the age of 16. After 5pm, only adults aged 21 or above are allowed in the pool.

Can non-residents use the pool?

Yes, non-resident guests can access the pool with a pool day pass at (AED 300 per person). Pre-booking and full prepayment are required.

What are the pool day pass hours for non-residents?

Day guests are welcome from 10am until 5.30pm. Note that last call for food and drinks is at 5pm.

What facilities does the spa offer?

Dior Spa The Lana offers treatments and a fully equipped gym.

Can I book spa treatments?

Yes, treatments are available daily at Dior Spa The Lana from 9am to 9pm. Please contact us to book. You can reach us by phone at +971 4 541 6688, or by email at diorspa.LAN@dorchestercollection.com.

What time does the gym close?

Our gym is exclusively for in-house guests, and is open 24 hours.
