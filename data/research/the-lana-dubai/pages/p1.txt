Frequently asked questions

Where is The Lana located?

Our luxury hotel is located at Marasi Drive, Business Bay, Dubai.

Is The Lana near Dubai Mall and the Burj Khalifa?

Yes, our five-star hotel overlooks the Burj Khalifa. It's also near Downtown Dubai and Dubai Mall.

How far is the hotel from the airport?

We're approximately 20 minutes' drive from Dubai International Airport.

What's close to the hotel?

We're in the vibrant Business Bay area, overlooking the Burj Khalifa and close to Dubai Mall.

How do I contact the hotel to book?

Please contact the hotel directly for reservations and any pre-booking requirements. You can reach us by phone at +971 4 541 7777, or by email at info.LAN@dorchestercollection.com.

Is there a minimum size for a group booking?

Group booking rates apply from 10 or more rooms.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

No pets are allowed unless they are service dogs, for which there is no fee.

Is there a parking fee?

Parking is complimentary. Please note there are no electric vehicle charging ports at The Lana; the closest charging points are at Dubai Mall.

What facilities does the hotel offer?

We have Dior Spa The Lana with a gym, an infinity pool on the rooftop, and an extensive collection of restaurants, bars and lounges.

Are there electric car charging points on site?

No, there are no EV charging ports at The Lana; the closest charging points are at Dubai Mall.

What makes The Lana distinctive?

Since opening in 2024, The Lana has stood as a new icon of contemporary elegance, redefining what it means to live and stay in Dubai. As part of Dorchester Collection’s portfolio of legendary hotels, it carries forward a legacy of excellence while shaping a new chapter for the brand in the Middle East.
