## Know Before You Stay

**What kind of place is Element 52?** Element 52 is an intimate collection of luxury private residences in Telluride, combining the space and comforts of a vacation home with Auberge Collection amenities and personalized service. Residences offer two to five bedrooms, spacious living areas and full kitchens, making Element 52 ideal for longer stays and trips shared with family or friends.

**Where is Element 52 located?** Element 52 is located at 398 South Davis Street along the San Miguel River in Telluride, Colorado. The property is within walking distance of historic downtown Telluride and offers direct access to the slopes of Telluride Ski Resort.

**How far is Element 52 from the airport?** Element 52 is approximately 15 minutes from Telluride Regional Airport (TEX) and about 75–90 minutes from Montrose Regional Airport (MTJ). Private airport transportation can be arranged for guests.

**When is the best time to visit Element 52?** Element 52 is a year-round Telluride destination. Winter offers direct ski-in/ski-out access and an array of snow-filled adventures, while warmer months bring hiking, biking, fly-fishing, horseback riding, rafting and Telluride’s celebrated festivals and cultural events.

**Is Element 52 a good fit for groups?** Yes. Element 52 is ideal for groups traveling together in Telluride, with spacious two- to five-bedroom private residences featuring full kitchens, generous living areas and room to gather. Guests enjoy the privacy and flexibility of a private home alongside personalized Auberge service and resort-style amenities.

**What experiences and activities are available at Element 52?** Element 52 puts the mountains and culture of Telluride within easy reach, with experiences ranging from hiking, cycling, horseback riding, fly-fishing and whitewater rafting to 4x4 adventures through the high country. Winter brings skiing, snowboarding, snowshoeing, snowmobiling and backcountry adventures throughout the San Juan Mountains.

**Is Element 52 ski-in/ski-out?** Yes. Element 52 offers direct ski-in/ski-out access to Telluride Ski Resort, along with an on-site ski valet, rentals, ski storage and a private funicular connecting guests to the slopes. The Ski Valet can prepare equipment at the property’s back door for convenient access to the mountain.

**What amenities and services are available at Element 52?** Guests enjoy the privacy of a residence alongside resort-style amenities and personalized service, including two heated outdoor soaking pools, a private spa, fitness center, ski lounge, heated underground parking, daily housekeeping and a year-round concierge. Pre-arrival grocery service, private chefs and local transportation can also be arranged.
