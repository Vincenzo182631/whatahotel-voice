### Outstanding Grand Cayman

17 days ago

Great location and excellent service from staff members. Plenty to do right on property and nearby. Seven mile beach, Bonny Moon beach club, Cafe Aura, Pom Pom are all excellent options. Breakfast buffet overpriced for quality of food and for the options that were offered. Room was very clean and new with outstanding views. Pool was very modern but the pool beds in the actual pool need to be cleaned or replaced. Property overall very well maintained and ice machine with filter water station on every floor was a great touch. Slight miscommunication with concierge service but if you are willing to splurge a bit a phone call ahead of schedule should be able to place a bottle of anything in a room or anything that doesn’t require it being actually prepared. Other than that like I mentioned earlier staff was very attentive, accommodating, friendly and informative. Grand Cayman felt welcoming, relaxed, safe, seamless with getting to and from everywhere including the airport and would recommend as a place to visit and return. Dining options all over the island were top notch and you can tell it is a culinary destination in addition to the pristine surrounding landscapes and amazing locals.
