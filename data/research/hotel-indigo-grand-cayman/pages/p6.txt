Steps away from world-famous “Seven Mile Beach”, Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman delivers the ultimate Caribbean vacation experience boasting gorgeous sunrise & sunset ocean view guest rooms, a wide selection of luxurious dining options and abundant event spaces. Spend a day at The Beach Club with chairs, umbrellas, towels and a selection of casual bites, or kick-back, relax and catch rays at our immaculate pool deck - featuring infinity swimming pools surrounded by towering palm trees. From the effervescent pink seashell paint to the visionary local artwork, our resort has garnered accolades for its vibrant, inspired design, including the Cayman Islands Hotel Architecture Award and the Cayman Islands New Hotel Construction & Design Award for 2023-2024.

### Say 'I Do' in Grand Cayman

Celebrate your wedding with timeless Caribbean elegance. Couples may begin with a beach ceremony at our sister resort, Kimpton Seafire, then move seamlessly to a reception at Hotel Indigo’s elegant ballroom.

Learn more

### Local Experiences

From the crystal-clear waters and soft, white sand of Seven Mile Beach to the memorable snorkeling excursions at Stingray City, you’ll find an excellent selection of things to do in the Cayman Islands during your stay.

### Meet + Celebrate

An unforgettable gathering awaits you. From group retreats to one-of-a-kind meetings or birthday trips to anniversary celebrations, each event at Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman is enveloped by the island’s unforgettable natural beauty.

### Sip and Savor

Enjoy California-inspired plates and botanical-and-bright cocktails at Catalina. A mouth-watering Latin-inspired menu on Pom Pom’s rooftop. Fresh sushi at Milkberry. By-the-pool bites at Bata Bar. Café Alula marketplace for grab-and-go snacks and souvenirs. And the piece de resistance: Rasa’s world-class tasting menu and exhibition kitchen.

## FAQs for Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman

Does Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman have a pool?

Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman has 1 outdoor pool.

Pool and Beach Club

You’re invited to dip in our refreshing swimming pool. Or enjoy poolside service (and a cocktail) from the luxe canopy of our beach cabanas. One thing remains: year-round Cayman Islands sunshine.

How much is parking at Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman?

Complimentary parking is available at Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman for hotel guests.

Valet or Self Parking available. No charge for hotel guests, as it is inlcuded in Resort Fee. Parking for restaurant and private events rate will vary. Electric vehicle charging available.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman?

Check-in at Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman offers free WiFi!

How far is Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman from the nearest airport?

Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman is conveniently located near Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM), approximately 10 miles away (16.09 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman?

Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Camana Bay - 3.38 miles away (5.44 kilometers)
- North Sound Golf Club - 4.67 miles away (7.52 kilometers)
- Stingray City - 8.69 miles away (13.99 kilometers)
- Queen Elizabeth II Botanic Park - 35.88 miles away (57.74 kilometers)
- Rum Point - 29.7 miles away (47.8 kilometers)
- Starfish Point - 32 miles away (51.5 kilometers)
- Dolphin Discovery - 3.7 miles away (5.95 kilometers)
- Cayman Turtle Farm - 3.7 miles away (5.95 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
