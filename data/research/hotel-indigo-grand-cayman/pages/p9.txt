Kimpton Seafire Resort & Spa
A stylish, family-friendly resort on Grand Cayman's lovely Seven Mile Beach

Reviewed by
Iona Brannon
Why book?
For a seaside resort that is welcoming to families and offers incredible food options, a stunning spa, and thoughtful details that make the luxury tangible.
Set the scene
The family-friendly ambiance is immediately noticeable, with children laughing and playing throughout the resort. However, the adults-only pool area (with a swim-up bar) provides a retreat for those seeking a quieter escape. The attention to detail in the resort’s thoughtful touches—yoga mats in every room, reusable water bottles, and family-friendly movie nights under the stars—makes Seafire a standout for travelers who appreciate both luxury and storytelling. The crowd mixes families and couples looking for an upscale yet laid-back environment. The fashion leans resort chic: think linen shirts and breezy sundresses.
The backstory
Opened in 2016, Kimpton Seafire was the first new hotel on Seven Mile Beach in over a decade and Kimpton’s first resort in the Caribbean. The brand, known for its boutique, pet-friendly properties with strong design elements, brought a fresh, contemporary aesthetic to the island. The hotel broke away from traditional colonial-style resorts with Caymanian tributes like the fully restored traditional Cayman catboat hanging in the library. It’s now part of the IHG family, maintaining Kimpton’s signature service and stylish, community-focused approach.
The rooms
Bright and modern, the rooms at Seafire have a refined beach house vibe with whitewashed wood, coral-hued accents, and floor-to-ceiling windows leading to private balconies with stunning views of the Caribbean. Bathrooms feature woven baskets and porcelain walls reminiscent of patinaed steel boat hulls. Local and regional artwork cover the walls, and custom wooden sculptures inspired by palm seed pods add an organic touch. These thoughtful details extend beyond aesthetics; outdoor lighting is turtle-friendly, ensuring that nesting sea turtles and their hatchlings are guided by moonlight back into the water rather than artificial illumination.
The oceanfront suites are worth the splurge, with expansive ocean views and private outdoor soaking tubs that offer a front-row seat to Grand Cayman's stunning sunsets. For a more private experience, the bungalows are ideal. Families will love the spacious suites, while couples can retreat to the more secluded upper-floor rooms with wraparound terraces. Then there’s the Family Suite, which includes a separate bedroom for parents, two bathrooms, and a living area with bunk beds for the kids.
Food and drink
Although the resort is not all-inclusive, there are nice offerings like free coffee during in the mornings and evening happy hours. A personal favorite was the Korean BBQ taco with sriracha aioli at Coccoloba. The breakfast buffet at the hotel’s sit-down restaurant, Ave, offers an incredible spread with fun additions like Caribbean classic breadfruit and saltfish. During the day, they serve a coastal Mediterranean menu featuring local seafood and produce.
The piña colada at the beachside bar and grill Coccoloba is a standout, but the real surprise lies in the creative drinks beyond the typical beachside cocktails. Far from your average hotel bar, Library By The Sea won the 2024 London Essence Best New Opening Award and ranked #35 on North America’s 50 Best Bars list. You’ll find inventive drinks inspired by literature, from The Little Prince to The Three Musketeers. I tried I Am Kira, a dark, moody cocktail inspired by Death Note, which arrived in a sleek black glass, fitting the drink’s bold flavors. Another standout was Fables, a whimsical, herbaceous drink paying homage to Aesop’s Fables. Every cocktail feels like a deep dive into storytelling, and for a bookworm like me, this bar was an absolute highlight.
The spa
The Seafire Spa has a beautiful indoor/outdoor hydrotherapy pool. Treatments incorporate local ingredients like coconut, and the signature Sea of Life massage uses organic marine botanicals to enhance relaxation. The spa also features a hammam-inspired steam room with tiles flown in from Turkey and an apothecary bar where guests can create custom-blended oils. It was closed for repair while I was there, but I was told it is one of the only hammams on the island.
The neighborhood/area
Seven Mile Beach is Grand Cayman’s crown jewel, and the resort sits on its quieter northern stretch, away from the busier central areas. Here, the atmosphere is more relaxed, with fewer crowds and a more laid-back beach experience, making it an ideal spot for those who prefer tranquility over the busier stretches lined with high-rise hotels. The hotel is a short drive from the island’s best restaurants, Cayman Spirits Co. distillery, and the famous Stingray City sandbar.
The service
Kimpton’s signature hospitality shines here—staff members greet you by name after the second day, and service is warm without feeling over the top. While I never took advantage of it, the concierge can arrange everything from scuba diving trips to private yacht charters. The hotel also offers complimentary kayaks, paddleboards, and bicycles for those who want to explore.
For families
The Kimpton is very family-friendly, with a dedicated kids club, a family-friendly pool, and spacious suites. The resort has activities like cooking classes, nature walks, and snorkeling excursions to keep the kids entertained. SPLASH at Seafire, the resort’s interactive water park, was an incredible play area designed for different ages.
Eco effort
Seafire is one of the few resorts in the Caribbean with a LEED Silver certification, awarded to buildings that meet a certain standard of environmentally-friendly design. Some of the resort’s sustainability efforts include solar panels, an on-site herb garden, and reduced single-use plastics. Rainwater is collected to water the resort grounds, and the outdoor lighting is turtle-friendly.
Accessibility
The resort is fully ADA compliant, with accessible rooms, ramps, and elevators. The pool and beach areas also have wheelchair-accessible entry points.
Anything left to mention?
While the Kimpton Seafire is more oriented towards families, those looking for a more hip, younger vibe will find it at Hotel Indigo, the resort’s sister property next door. Hotel Indigo’s rooftop bar, Pom Pom, offers a spectacular beach view paired with great music and creative cocktails.
