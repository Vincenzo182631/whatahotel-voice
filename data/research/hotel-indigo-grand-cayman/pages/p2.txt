# Hotel Indigo Grand CaymanRooms & suites

A companion to Cayman Island’s lush surroundings, our tranquil, clean-lined rooms feature a mix of whites, creams and peaches framed with blonde woods. Rooms are dotted with local art and glass vases of leafy native palms. Modern trimmings include HDTV, Bluetooth radio and rain shower heads.
