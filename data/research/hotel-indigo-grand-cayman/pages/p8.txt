# Hotel Indigo Grand CaymanLocal area

Conveniently set in Grand Cayman, steps from world-renown Seven Mile Beach, Hotel Indigo Cayman Islands offers an ideal location to explore a wealth of golden-sand beaches, world-renowned water attractions, cultural gems, art museums and local shopping.

1. Local Experiences

### Local Experiences

From the crystal-clear waters and soft, white sand of Seven Mile Beach to the memorable snorkeling excursions at Stingray City, you’ll find an excellent selection of things to do in the Cayman Islands during your stay.

## Things to do

### Camana Bay

10 Market St, KY1-9006

From high fashion to farmers markets, Camana Bay charms locals and visitors alike. Plan to spend a day discovering boutiques and restaurants and taking in the water views. Stroll through beautifully landscaped streets, enjoy a meal at one of the many restaurants, or catch a movie at the state-of-the-art cinema.

### North Sound Golf Club

557 Safehaven Dr,

Discover the premier golfing destination in the Cayman Islands at North Sound Golf Club, the island's only 18-hole championship course. This par 71 course features stunning ocean views, meticulously maintained greens, and water hazards on 15 holes. Enjoy a full-service practice facility, aqua driving range, and open-air bar. Perfect for golfers of all skill levels.

Learn more

### Stingray City

Stingray City is one of Grand Cayman’s most famous destinations and is just a charter boat ride away from Kimpton Seafire. The bottom of the sandbar is bright white, and the water is super clear, so you can see the rays clearly, even without a mask. Mingling with stingrays definitely qualifies as one of those we-can't-do-this-at-home moments.

### Queen Elizabeth II Botanic Park

8R8J+9RQ, North Side,

Explore the natural beauty and cultural heritage of the Cayman Islands at Queen Elizabeth II Botanic Park. This 65-acre garden and woodland preserve features themed gardens, including a heritage garden, orchid boardwalk, and the Blue Iguana Conservation facility. Perfect for nature lovers and families, the park offers a serene escape with its vibrant flora, native wildlife, and tranquil lake.

### Rum Point

Spend an afternoon or evening at this popular spot on the northern tip of Grand Cayman. Discover the charm of Rum Point with it's beautiful views, clear shallow waters, and famous "Mudslides". Perfect for swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing. Rum Point offers beach hammocks, shady trees, picnic tables, and delicious food.

### Starfish Point

Discover the serene beauty of Starfish Point, a secluded beach on the side of Grand Cayman. Known for its shallow, crystal-clear waters and abundance of starfish, this tranquil spot is perfect for snorkeling and exploring marine life up close.

### Dolphin Discovery

Dive into an unforgettable experience at Dolphin Discovery in Grand Cayman. Interact with friendly dolphins through various programs, including swimming, belly rides, and handshakes. Located in West Bay, this attraction offers educational insights into marine life and conservation efforts.

### Cayman Turtle Farm

Dive into an adventure at the Cayman Turtle Centre, the island's premier wildlife conservation facility. Swim with yearling Green Sea Turtles, slide down a 100-foot water slide, or chill out at the pool. Home to majestic green sea turtles, this unique attraction offers interactive experiences like hand-feeding tropical birds and exploring the Predator Reef.
