# Hotel Indigo Grand CaymanDining

The island's hub for food, drink, and community, Hotel Indigo Grand Cayman brings five exciting food concepts together under one roof. Discover the blend of cultural tradition and modern culinary innovation that makes us an irresistible social hotspot.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

1. Sip and Savor

### Sip and Savor

Enjoy California-inspired plates and botanical-and-bright cocktails at Catalina. A mouth-watering Latin-inspired menu on Pom Pom’s rooftop. Fresh sushi at Milkberry. By-the-pool bites at Bata Bar. Café Alula marketplace for grab-and-go snacks and souvenirs. And the piece de resistance: Rasa’s world-class tasting menu and exhibition kitchen.

Third Thursday of Every Month \| 8PM – 9PM

### Travesía: A Curated Cocktail Journey

Travesía means “journey,” and this intimate cocktail experience at Pom Pom is designed to take you on one.

Travel through South America across five chapters, five cocktails, and five thoughtfully paired bites, with each stop inspired by the flavors, ingredients, and stories of the continent.

Throughout the evening, our team will guide you through each chapter, presenting the cocktails and sharing the inspiration behind every pour and pairing.

## Pom Pom Rooftop Lounge

**Bar Phone Number:** 1-345-7433600

**Location:** Rooftop

Discover an alluring, truly one of a kind Cayman experience, high above it all at the island’s first and only rooftop lounge and restaurant. Our rooftop is named after the orchid trees that bloom throughout the Cayman Islands and Pom Pom is just as spectacular. Join us for stunning 180-degree tropical views of Grand Cayman, exotic Latin-inspired cocktails and small plates to share, lush places to lounge, live DJs who never miss a beat, and an effortlessly cool vibe.

**Hours of Operation:**

Sunday - Thursday: 4:00 PM - 11:00 PM

Friday - Saturday: 4:00 PM - 12:00 Midnight

## Bata Bar

**Bar Phone Number:** 1-345-7433600

**Location:** Pool

All Fresh, No Fuss. Relax by the warm, crystal-clear waters of our refreshing pool and let us mix you a cocktail influenced by the Caribbean & infused with the freshest local ingredients. Enjoy inspired fare from watermelon poke and tuna ceviche, to classic poolside bites like our Fried Jerk Chicken Sandwich. From the dining booths to the menu, to the unobtrusive service, and the breathtaking views, Bata Bar is intended help you unwind and enjoy.

**Hours of Operation:**

Monday - Sunday: 11:00 AM - 7:00 PM

## Café Alula

**Bar Phone Number:** 1-345-7433600

**Location:** Lobby

We carry a sunny disposition and everything you need to recharge. Featuring an assortment of espressos and coffees, juices, healthy snacks, delicious pastries, and more, you will find plenty of island favorites and souvenir items perfect for by the pool, island exploration, or to bring home as gifts. Our menu changes to offer artisan sandwiches, fresh salads and fun, boozy drinks to match your vacation vibes.

**Hours of Operation:**

Monday - Sunday: 7:00 AM - 7:00 PM
