## ICE District / Entertainment District

Located in the heart of downtown Edmonton, ICE District is Canada’s largest mixed-use sports and entertainment district—and the second largest in North America. This 25-acre destination is home to premium office space, luxury residences, upscale dining, boutique shopping, and the JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District. At its center is Rogers Place, a world-class venue hosting major concerts, sporting events, and live entertainment. Guests of the hotel enjoy unparalleled access to the energy of the city’s premier entertainment destination—whether here to stay, work, or play.

Learn More

### Muttart Conservatory

Enter the Muttart Conservatory, a striking quartet of glass pyramids housing tropical, temperate, and desert gardens, plus a seasonal feature gallery. Meander lush pathways under soaring glass, breathe in curated displays of orchids and succulents, and retreat to the café and shop. A peaceful, refined escape near downtown.

### Edmonton Riverboat

Cruise the North Saskatchewan River aboard the Edmonton Riverboat, enjoying sweeping city views from open decks or inside the elegant dining salon. Select from afternoon, sunset, or evening sailings (60–90 mins) while sipping refreshments and soaking in the river valley’s ambiance under impeccable service.

### River Valley Adventure Co.

Discover Edmonton’s parkland with River Valley Adventure Co.’s guided Segway tours, premium bike or e-scooter rentals, and seasonal adventures like rafting or snowshoeing. Expert guides handle all logistics; you focus on the trails. Pause at the cozy café before or after for a perfect complement to your active day.

### Old Strathcona

Home to Edmonton’s main arts and entertainment district, Old Strathcona embraces a Bohemian spirit that is on display through the area’s independent theatres, historic brick shopfronts, trendy boutiques and delightful collision of subcultures. You will find plenty of unique dining experiences, cafés, bars and music shops.

### The 4th Street Promenade

The 4th Street Promenade, curiously located on 104th Street in the heart of downtown Edmonton is a historic neighborhood of cute, cozy cafés, stylish boutiques, revitalized warehouse lofts and street markets. This blend of sophisticated urban cool and casual hangouts makes it one of the city’s “it” destinations.

### 124 Street

Extending down 124 Street you will find a district that is rich in independent and creative boutiques, galleries, live music venues, bars and restaurants. The area is home to over 100 retailers, 12 of Edmonton’s premier art galleries and rows of gorgeous 19th-century homes.
