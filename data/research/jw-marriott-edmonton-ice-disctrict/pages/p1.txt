### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $75.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District?

Off-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District is Edmonton International Airport (YEG). YEG is located approximately 29.6 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Edmonton ICE District has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
