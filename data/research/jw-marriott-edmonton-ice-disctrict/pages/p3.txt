A progressive steakhouse, Braven embraces a “go big or go home” attitude and warmly invites guests to embrace adventure and enjoy life to its fullest. Serving breakfast, lunch and dinner and wood-fired weekend brunch, Braven offers a range of bespoke dining experiences. Braven proudly features some of the best beef Alberta has to offer, grilled to perfection and served with mouthwatering accompaniments like jumbo shrimp, whipped potatoes and king crab legs.

## Fresh, Casual All Day Dining

Kindred is a haven for creative and comforting dishes with an Italian accent. From seasonally inspired salads and light crispy fritto misto, to fresh handmade pasta and perfectly sharable thin crust pizza, Kindred can cater to any mood, day and night. Featuring a large, humming dining room, inviting lounge seating, and a sunny streetside patio in the warmer months, Kindred’s airy atmosphere and contemporary design are polished enough for a late-morning meeting and laid-back enough for an easy-going meal with friends.
