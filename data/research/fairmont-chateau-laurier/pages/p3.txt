Located next to the Parliament Buildings, this 1912 hotel offers the allure and grace of old-world charm, just steps away from everything. It resembles a French château made of granite blocks, with a copper roof and a marble staircase in the lobby. The food is top-notch at Wilfrid's Restaurant, which serves regional cuisine overlooking the Parliament and the Ottawa River. At Zoe's Lounge, sip a champagne cosmo under the glow of chandeliers. Don't miss buffet breakfast before heading out to watch the changing of the guard.
