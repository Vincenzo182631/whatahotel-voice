## A GRAND _icon_ IN THE HEART OF OTTAWA

Stay at a historic castle of unmatched splendor in the heart of Canada's capital. Located in the downtown core, poised between ByWard Market and the Rideau Canal locks, this 5-star luxury hotel is Ottawa's grand icon: a soaring historic château playing host to political leaders, film stars, and royalty for over a century.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Château Laurier located?

Fairmont Château Laurier is located at 1 Rideau Street, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada K1N 8S7.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Château Laurier?

Check-in time is 4:00 p.m. Check-out time is 12:00 p.m.

Does Fairmont Château Laurier have a swimming pool?

Yes, Fairmont Château Laurier has a beautiful, indoor art-deco pool located on the lower level of the hotel.

Is parking available at Fairmont Château Laurier?

Valet parking is limited and available only for overnight guests. The daily rate is $67.80 CAD\*, including tax, with in/out privileges.

_\*rate subject to change_

Self-parking for restaurant and day guests is available within a short distance from the hotel.

Is Fairmont Château Laurier pet-friendly?

Yes, Fairmont Château Laurier accepts cats and dogs for an additional fee of $60 CAD per room per stay, plus applicable taxes. Pets may not be left alone in guest rooms. Pets must be leashed at all times and are not allowed in hotel restaurants or lounges. Guide dogs are complimentary.

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Château Laurier?

The hotel has thirteen ADA rooms. They feature wider entrance doors, lower light switches and grab bars in the bathroom. Wheelchairs are available through the Concierge Desk, subject to availability.
