## Sustainability at JW Marriott Hotel Nara

JW Marriott Hotel Nara is committed to sustainability and harmony with the local community. We reduce our environmental impact by minimizing single-use amenities and food packaging, while utilizing the AI-powered system Winnow to help reduce food waste. We also optimize water pressure in guest rooms and back-of-house areas to conserve water resources.

In recognition of these efforts, the hotel was awarded the internationally recognized Green Key certification in October 2025. Green Key is granted to properties that meet strict standards in environmental management, energy efficiency, waste reduction, and sustainable operations.

We also celebrate Nara's cultural heritage by showcasing local crafts and works by regional artists throughout the hotel. Through environmental responsibility and community engagement, we strive to create a more sustainable future.

## Artist-in-Residence Program Launches at JW Marriott Hotel Nara

JW Marriott Hotel Nara has launched its Artist-in-Residence Program, welcoming Nara-based contemporary artist Chiharu Yoshina. The program aims to preserve local culture and offer guests meaningful artistic experiences. Chiharu’s work blends delicate colored-pencil layers with traditional Japanese materials such as raden and hand-crafted washi, expressing themes of life cycles, time, and the universe. Her pieces Multidimensional Blue and Meguru are displayed in the hotel’s lobby and Flying Stag areas, creating a space where architecture and art harmonize. Through this program, the hotel supports emerging local artists and deepens cultural engagement for guests.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Nara?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Nara is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nara allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Nara.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Nara?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Nara. Self parking is available for ¥2000.00 per day. Valet parking is available for ¥5000.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nara have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Nara has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Nara booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Nara?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Nara is Osaka International Airport - Itami (ITM). ITM is located approximately 45.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nara have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Nara has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nara have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Nara has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
