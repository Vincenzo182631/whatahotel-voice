# Dining in JW Marriott Hotel Nara

## Dining

With an atmosphere inspired by the architectural style of an ancient treasure warehouse, Azekura offers authentic Japanese cuisine: teppanyaki, kaiseki, and sushi. Indulge your five senses by relishing in the refined cuisine prepared by the seasoned chefs at our Nara, Japanese restaurant. Enjoy world cuisines in an open kitchen at our all-day dining restaurant, Silk Road Dining. The ancient capital...See More of Nara was the final stop on the Silk Road. With this inspiration in mind, our chefs carefully selected fresh ingredients from Nara and around the world, highlighting the best of Eastern and Western cuisines. Flying Stag is a relaxing and stylish lounge bar, surrounded by beautifully designed sofas, a hearth, and specially curated artworks. During the day, it serves an authentic afternoon with sweets and a plentiful tea menu. At night, you can enjoy a comprehensive selection of original cocktails, vintage wine, and whiskey.See Less

### Asian Promotion

Building on the Asian flavors loved by guests last year, this season’s signature chilled cappellini combines delicate spices, refreshing citrus notes, and tropical fruits to create an elegant taste of summer, inspired by the vibrant spirit of Southeast Asia.

### Christmas Collection

This festive season, discover our Samarkand Blue Collection, inspired by Samarkand, Uzbekistan, a sister city of Nara. Featuring a striking Christmas cake and cookie tin in iconic blue hues, the collection captures the spirit of the “Blue City.” Also available are the KOTOKA Strawberry Christmas Shortcake, Japanese Chestnut Mont Blanc Tart, and a selection of hotel-made festive gifts.
