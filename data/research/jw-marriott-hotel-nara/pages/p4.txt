### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nara have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Hotel Nara offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Medicine ball, Balance ball, Yoga mat, Kettle bell, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nara have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Nara has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Does JW Marriott Hotel Nara have an on-site spa?

Yes, SPA by JW is located on-site at JW Marriott Hotel Nara.
