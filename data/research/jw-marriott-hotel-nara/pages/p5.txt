# Seasonal Events at JW Marriott Hotel Nara

## Christmas & New Year Events

This year, we celebrate the 5th anniversary of the sister-city partnership between Nara and Samarkand, Uzbekistan, with “Samarkand Christmas.” Connected by the Silk Road, these two ancient cities share a rich cultural heritage. Experience an enchanting Christmas where Nara and Samarkand come together in elegant harmony. As the New Year begins, immerse yourself in the beauty of Japanese traditions and...See More enjoy a serene and relaxing stay at JW Marriott Hotel Nara.See Less

Christmas Cake Collection

### Samarkand Éclat

Inspired by Samarkand, the legendary “Blue City,” this festive chocolate cake features hazelnut praline mousse, lemon-infused chocolate cream and delicate hazelnut biscuit. Finished in vivid blue with elegant chocolate ornaments, its bittersweet chocolate, citrus and anise notes evoke the exotic beauty of Samarkand.

Book Now

### KOTOKA Strawberry Christmas Shortcake

A timeless Christmas classic featuring Nara’s premium KOTOKA strawberries. Delicate sponge is layered with JW Marriott Hotel Nara’s signature Chantilly cream, perfectly complementing the strawberries’ rich sweetness and gentle acidity. Finished with fresh KOTOKA strawberries and elegant golden ornaments for a refined festive touch.

Book Now

### Japanese Chestnut Mont Blanc Tart

A festive wreath-inspired Mont Blanc tart showcasing premium Japanese chestnuts. A crisp tart shell is layered with almond cream and silky custard, then finished with rich Mont Blanc cream. Adorned with strawberries, berries and chestnuts, this elegant creation brings warmth and festive splendour to Christmas in Nara.

Book Now

Festive Gifts

### Signature Stollen

JW Marriott’s Signature Stollen is generously filled with dried fruits and nuts, enriched with buttery aromas and warm spices. Inspired by traditional German methods and carefully matured to deepen its flavours, this refined festive classic develops in richness as Christmas approaches.

Book Now

### Samarkand Cookie Tin

Inspired by Samarkand, the legendary “Blue City,” this elegant tin features five handcrafted cookies with Gotoku miso, dried fruits, spices, matcha and honey. At its heart, a marbled blue cookie evokes Samarkand’s iconic tiles, bringing together the flavours of Nara and the exotic allure of the Silk Road.

Book Now

### Pound Cake Assortment

A colourful assortment of four pound cakes: Strawberry, Nut & Chocolate, Matcha & Black Soybean, and Blueberry inspired by this year’s festive theme. Individually crafted in a shareable size, this vibrant selection is perfect for festive afternoon tea or as an elegant Christmas gift.

Book Now

Festive Afternoon Tea & Cocktails

### Christmas Afternoon Tea : A Festive Journey

Celebrating the 5th anniversary of Nara and Samarkand’s sister-city partnership, this festive afternoon tea brings together the heritage of two ancient capitals. Silk Road-inspired sweets feature saffron, pistachio, mango and chai, complemented by elegant savories blending the flavours of Nara with the exotic charm of Samarkand.

Book Now

### Christmas Cocktail “Samarkand Blue”

Samarkand Blue captures the enchanting beauty of the “Blue City” in a glass. Vodka and white chocolate liqueur are layered with oriental spices and Nara botanicals, including Yamato Toki and Yamato Tachibana. Its deep blue hue evokes Samarkand’s skies and iconic tiles, bringing the spirit of Samarkand and Nara together in a festive creation.

Christmas Courses

### Christmas Lunch & Dinner Courses

Silk Road Dining presents special Christmas lunch and dinner courses showcasing seasonal flavours. Dinner begins with Bluefin Tuna and Striped Jack Carpaccio, complemented by Nara’s Gotoku miso, tapenade and a refreshing sauce vierge. Celebrate the season with an elegant dining experience shared with someone special.
