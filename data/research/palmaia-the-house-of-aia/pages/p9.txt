Palmaïa’s deep-rooted journeys into authenticity and culture… made possible by you.

If you leave The House, it has to be for a good reason. Through DEPTH, you’ll connect with authentic Mayan communities and discover how ancestral wisdom, local culture, and nature inspire purposeful living and preserve enduring traditions.

This shared experience is available on a biweekly basis, offering the opportunity to visit the community of Nuevo Durango and discover the projects made possible with the profits received from private sessions booked by guests.

## How to Book?

Book your DEPTH experience in advance by emailing us at depth@thehouseofaia.com

Reservations close two days before departure at 5:00 pm.

\*\*If you have dietary restrictions or food allergies, please let your Nomadic Guide know in advance so we can do our best to accommodate your needs.

## The Projects

Each visit offers the opportunity to discover a thoughtfully curated selection of community-led projects. Rooted in local knowledge, creativity, and collective purpose, these initiatives provide an authentic glimpse into craftsmanship, education, environmental conservation, and regenerative development:

### Chulub Chac Organic Garden

### Kab Hau Community Project

### Nojoch Keej Environmental Conservation Center, including the Melipona Bee Project

### Lol Che Community Café

### Utzibali Kuxtal Artisan Jam

### Benito Juárez Elementary & Middle School

The experience evolves with the rhythm of the community, highlighting the projects that are most active and meaningful at the time. Experiences may vary according to community activities and seasonal availability.

## The Community

##### Doña Addy

Entrepreneur

Doña Addy Pech and her family create traditional jams and Mexican sauces using produce from their sustainable organic garden and local seasonal harvests. Visit her home and discover a beloved community business filled with authentic flavors.

##### Don Manuel

Community Leader

Driven by a deep commitment to conservation, he leads projects that protect the region’s wildlife, native flora, and cultural heritage. His vision has transformed the area into a place where nature, education, and community thrive together.

##### Don Daniel

Sustainable Agriculturist

A guardian of ancestral farming techniques, he cultivates the land in harmony with nature and shares this knowledge with future generations. His work strengthens food sovereignty while creating new opportunities for the community.

##### Don Carlos

Beekeeper

Together with his family, he cares for native Mayan hives that help sustain the surrounding jungle through pollination. Their artisanal honey provides an important source of income while preserving a centuries-old tradition. We highly recommend taking a jar home.
