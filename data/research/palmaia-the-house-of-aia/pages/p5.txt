# AïA\\Wellness

Our Wellness Experience leads you to Optimal Health

AïA\\Wellness is Palmaïa’s holistic offering of wellness experiences for every guest. It is comprised of four distinct pathways, each representing an essential part of a complete, integrated approach to well‑being. When experienced together, the AïA\\Wellness program inspires you to deliberately and mindfully transform the way you think about and practice wellness. Every touch point, every experience, and every interaction has been intentionally designed to create an environment where well‑being becomes woven into everyday life—lasting far beyond your stay at Palmaïa.

AïA\\Wellness offers a gentle detox experience designed with vacation relaxation in mind. Rooted in natural connection, this wellness retreat at one of the best hotels in Playa del Carmen combines personalized support with serene, all-inclusive resort amenities. Select the retreat program that suits you best, at your own pace, and effortlessly integrate vitality and flow into your lifestyle, continuing well beyond your stay.

# The Four Pathways of AïA\\Wellness

**AïA\\Wellness** uses a gentle approach to health—after all, you’re on vacation. It puts a connection to nature at its core, supported by the resort’s guides and practitioners, the Architects of Life. By joining the sessions that feel right for you, when they feel right, you begin to weave vitality and flow into your vacation lifestyle, carrying it effortlessly beyond your stay. Choose as many or as few activities as you like—a variety of over 50 options each week—so there’s truly something for everyone.

## Nourishing Biome

The Nourishing Biome is a unique food program only available at Palmaïa, where everything you eat is designed for optimal health. Instead of dictating what you should eat, we simplify by removing the most toxic ingredients in the modern food system. This empowers you to freely experiment and enjoy healthy food options.

## **Portals of Practice**

More than 50 holistic and health activities carried out every week by our in-house and visiting guides. AïA\\Wellness is designed to include an integration structure around the program which includes one-on-one\* sessions with our Guides as well as the support of all our Community.

Discover More

\*1:1 Sessions have an extra cost. All profit from sessions goes to our Depth program, where we empower local community members to start their own business.

## Rituals of Sound

No wellness experience can be complete without the use of energy, frequency and sound as part of the healing process. The Rituals of Sound create casual gatherings for this purpose, held at beautiful locations where deep beats nourish the soul.
