## Consciously Evolving the Culture of Wellness

Palmaïa is a groundbreaking wellness concept nestled between the jungle and the ocean on Mexico’s Riviera Maya and is designed for guests who share an affinity for self-improvement. From Mindfulness Seekers who are just starting out by practicing yoga and meditation, to Transformation Trailblazers who operate at the highest level of vibration and consciousness, Palmaïa is a place that welcomes all.

You don’t need to worry about being short-changed here, no Wellness Resort in the world includes as much as we do.

Our greatest hope is for you to take what you learn at Palmaïa and gift it to others. We hope that like so many others, when you come here, you are also inspired to gift your skills and knowledge and contribute to the community. Only by working together do we make the magic of Palmaïa possible.

## Words from our Founder

Alex Ferri

There’s something special about this place called Palmaïa. Maybe it’s the hallowed ground the property sits on, still bearing remnants of Mayan sacred sites. Maybe it’s the jungle, untouched by humans, that gives way to the warm sands and turquoise waters of the Caribbean Sea. Or maybe it’s the overall feeling, the energy, and the vibration you feel the minute you pass through the entry portal, leaving the world behind. This is a place to truly forget about your outside life to find the you within.

When we designed Palmaïa, we set out to create an experience that defied definition—a ground-breaking wellness concept where there are no prescribed agendas, itineraries, and schedules that add stress to an experience that is supposed to be the complete opposite. Instead, Palmaïa meets you where you are, offering experiences throughout the day, every day that you can partake in to whatever degree you want to engage.

But just because we launched and operate Palmaïa doesn’t mean the journey is over. We’re always searching for, finding inspiration in, and developing new wellness classes, concepts, treatments, and rituals to add to our ever-evolving definition of what Palmaïa wellness is. And it’s more than just experiences. It’s holistic. It’s all-encompassing. It’s the very heartbeat of Palmaïa that radiates out to every guest interaction and touchpoint. Everything you see, hear, touch, smell and taste during a stay—from the decor in a restaurant, the sax player who accompanies a live DJ on the beach, to the ever-changing plant-based menus at a restaurant, a new art class that brings out your creative side, or emotionally connected ancient healing treatments that heal your body and soul at our spa—every touch point is consciously curated to cultivate a connected experience with you.

We’re the best kept secret in wellness—a place that many of our guests discover almost by accident. Yoga instructors and healers, practitioners and teachers from around the world help spread the word about the incredible experiences we offer, in a place built for those of us who want to have life-changing experiences, be it one, or many. Most importantly, our team is here to guide, support, and provide space for you to be inspired to explore on your terms. It’s this culture of wellness, of being not only mindful, but singularly focused on creating a memorable experience for each and every guest that drives us.

Above all, I hope that Palmaïa offers you a beautiful experience and helps you embark on your own healing journey. The path to healing isn’t always clear or easy, but the most important step is starting. Knowledge is endless, and learning never stops. I believe that everything happens in its perfect time because divinity loves us all, and love is eternal.

I have learned so much from the people I have met while creating Palmaïa—especially our guides and special guests. Health does not need to be complicated. Our goal is to empower you to take control of your well-being so you can be the master of your own health. I firmly believe that if we live a healthy lifestyle, and do the work, there is no reason to ever fall chronically ill. Nature holds the answers to everything; we just need to observe, learn, and show respect.

I look forward to welcoming you to Palmaïa.
