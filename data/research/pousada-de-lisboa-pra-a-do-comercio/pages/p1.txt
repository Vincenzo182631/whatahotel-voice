Overview

### Elegance and History

It was once the residence of kings and headquarters of ministries. Today, Pousada Lisboa, located in the majestic Praça do Comércio, just a few metres from the River Tagus, is a true treasure to be discovered in the heart of the city, internationally recognised as a ‘Small Luxury Hotel of the World’.

From the imposing stone staircases to the most exquisite rooms, including works of art of historical importance, provided by the Lisbon Museum, Pousada Lisboa is an iconic building, with 90 rooms spread over four floors, which feature an elegant, contemporary and sophisticated decor, where every detail has been thought of to offer an unforgettable stay. The prestigious British brand _Molton Brown_ provides the amenities in all rooms, offering guests a distinctive sensory experience.

With a prime location, Pousada Lisboa is the perfect starting point for exploring Lisbon, with easy access to some of the city’s most important historical and cultural landmarks.

The square, also known as Terreiro do Paço, is a symbol of the city, having been the centre of Portugal’s royal and administrative power, with monuments such as the Rua Augusta Arch and the Equestrian Statue of King Joseph I. Today, it is a vibrant meeting point, surrounded by cafes, restaurants and museums.

At the end of the day, upon returning to this charming hotel, guests can choose to enjoy a space that includes a discreet indoor swimming pool, massage room and a gym

The Restaurant

RIB Lisboa

RIB Lisboa is a sophisticated restaurant located in the iconic Praça do Comércio. This space is known for its specialisation in high-quality meats and a selection of exquisite wines, offering a unique gastronomic experience.

RIB Lisboa is housed in a historic building, which gives it a unique charm. The restaurant’s architecture combines classic elements with contemporary design, creating an elegant and welcoming environment. The interior is characterised by modern decor, with clean lines and grand materials, which contrast harmoniously with the historic details of the building.

Choose your spot, either in the cosy interior or on the terrace, protected by the arches of Praça do Comércio, and enjoy an unforgettable meal.

RIB Lisboa is the second of the brand’s units, after RIB Porto, in the picturesque Ribeira area of Porto.

Activities & Destination

### Enjoy the **journey**

Places to seeItinerary

Comércio Square

One of the largest squares in Europe, lined with 18th-century buildings and bordered by the Tagus River. Stroll along Ribeira das Naus, the former shipyard that is now a riverside beach.

See more about Comércio Square

Castelo de São Jorge (Saint George’s Castle)

Located in the parish of Santa Maria Maior (Castelo), the Castelo de São Jorge stands on the highest hill in the Historic Centre, providing its visitors with one of the most beautiful views over the city. Here you can explore the archaeological remains, stroll through the gardens and take part in guided tours.

See more about Castelo de São Jorge (Saint George’s Castle)

Belém Tower

Classified as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and voted one of the Seven Wonders of Portugal, the Belém Tower is an icon of Manueline architecture and one of Lisbon’s greatest landmarks.

See more about Belém Tower

Rossio

The Praça do Rossio (Rossio Square) is one of the city’s busiest squares. Considered to be the heart of Lisbon, the Praça do Rossio is composed of old Pombaline-style buildings, shops, cafés and the famous Estação do Rossio (Rossio Station), one of the world’s most beautiful train stations. Here you will feel like a native of Lisbon.

See more about Rossio

Oceanário

Considered the “best aquarium in the world” by TripAdvisor, the Oceanário features a large central aquarium and four marine habitats. Discover birds, invertebrates, fish, amphibians, mammals and plants.

See more about Oceanário

Alfama

Along the narrow streets of the oldest neighbourhood in Lisbon you can find fado houses, typical restaurants and popular festivities, especially in June. Visit the Chafariz del Rei palace, the National Pantheon, São Vicente Church and Santo Estevão viewpoint, with its spectacular views over the city.

Lisbon Book Fair

Held in Eduardo VII Park, the Lisbon Book Fair is a highlight of the city’s calendar, with stalls, book launches, book signing sessions and a varied cultural programme. Perfect for those who enjoy browsing books in the open air.

See more about Lisbon Book Fair

Popular Saints’ Festivals

In June, Lisbon celebrates the feast of Saint Anthony with traditional street parties, grilled sardines, parades along Avenida da Liberdade, music and entertainment throughout the city. Lisbon’s historic neighbourhoods offer visitors a particularly warm welcome.

See more about Popular Saints’ Festivals

FAQs

How do I get from Lisbon Airport to Pousada Lisboa by metro?

The nearest metro station is Terreiro do Paço (Blue Line), next to Praça do Comércio. From Humberto Delgado Airport, take the Red Line and transfer to the Blue Line (e.g., at São Sebastião) to Terreiro do Paço. The journey takes about 25-30 minutes.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Pousada Lisboa - Small Luxury Hotels?

Check-in at Pousada Lisboa - Small Luxury Hotels is from 3:00 p.m., and check-out is until 12:00 p.m..

Is Pousada Lisboa suitable for couples, honeymooners, and families?

Yes. It's a 5-star hotel, a member of Small Luxury Hotels of the World, geared towards couples and honeymooners (elegant suites, some with views of Terreiro do Paço, spa and heated indoor pool); it also welcomes families, with rooms for 2 to 3 people.

Does Pousada Lisboa have a spa?

Yes. It has a wellness area with an indoor heated pool (8:30 am - 8:00 pm), sauna, gym (8:30 am - 8:00 pm) and massage room with treatments by appointment (10:00 am - 8:00 pm).

What kind of breakfast is served at Pousada Lisboa - Small Luxury Hotels?

Breakfast options include buffet.

What types of rooms and suites are available at Pousada Lisboa?

There are 90 rooms and suites: from Cozy (Queen) to King Classic, Mezzanine, Superior, Deluxe and Premium, up to the King suites (Suite, Suite Áurea, Suite Praça do Comércio and Suite Presidencial), some with views of Terreiro do Paço.

What food & drink options are available at Pousada Lisboa - Small Luxury Hotels?

Pousada Lisboa - Small Luxury Hotels has 1 restaurant: RIB Lisboa.

What is the best time to visit Lisbon and stay at Pousada Lisboa?

Spring (April to June) and early autumn (September and October) are the best times, with mild weather and fewer crowds to explore Baixa and Terreiro do Paço; summer is hotter and busier, and the Pousada has a heated indoor pool for year-round use.

Does Pousada Lisboa - Small Luxury Hotels have a pool?

Yes, this hotel has an indoor swimming pool.

Is Pousada Lisboa located in Praça do Comércio in a historic building?

Yes. Pousada Lisboa is located in Praça do Comércio (Terreiro do Paço), in Baixa Pombalina, in a historic building that is over two centuries old, erected after the 1755 earthquake, which housed the former Ministry of Internal Administration before being converted into a hotel by the Pestana Group in 2015.

Are there opportunities to exercise at Pousada Lisboa - Small Luxury Hotels?

Yes, guests have access to a gym during their stay.

Which popular attractions are close to Pousada Lisboa - Small Luxury Hotels?

Nearby attractions include Praça do Comércio (Terreiro do Paço), Castelo de S. Jorge and Lisbon Cathedral.

Can I park a car at Pousada Lisboa - Small Luxury Hotels?

Yes, paid public parking nearby is available to guests.
