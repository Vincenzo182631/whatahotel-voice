## Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto

## Step Into a Sanctuary For The Senses In Kyoto

Indulge in tranquil massage and spa facilities with natural hot springs and steam saunas. Located in the heart of Kyoto, each treatment at Banyan Tree Spa Higashiyama Kyoto draws on traditional Japanese healing practices to revitalise the senses. For a more traditional experience unique to Kyoto, guests can unwind in the onsens, where warm, soothing waters gently restore both body and mind.

## 8 Pillars Of Wellbeing

At Banyan Tree Spa Higashiyama Kyoto, our signature approach to wellness is guided by the 8 Pillars of Wellbeing. These pillars offer simple, intentional practices that foster a balanced and meaningful life, both during your stay and beyond.

Drawing from the rich tapestry of Japanese tradition and the wisdom of local nature, we create a sensory world filled with aroma, touch, and heritage, inviting you to explore the depths of your wellbeing.

## OUR SPA MENU

From authentic Thai body scrubs to fragrant massage oils, our spa treatments are meticulously crafted using only fresh and natural ingredients. These treatments rejuvenate the body and refresh the mind, enhancing your overall wellbeing.

### Onsen Indulgence

A Banyan Tree Spa Higashiyama Kyoto exclusive, this combination of a private onsen experience and a full-body treatment restores your vitality and unlocks deeper states of relaxation.

### Royal Banyan

Our skilled therapists guide you through 150 minutes of luxurious relaxation in this distinctive treatment that combines Eastern and Western techniques, embodying the essence of the Banyan Tree Spa philosophy.

Personalize your massage experience by selecting from our aromatic treatment oils and extensive range of treatments, then surrender yourself to the touch of our professionally trained and skilled therapists.

### Facials

Using specially formulated facial products exclusively created by Banyan Tree Spa, this treatment hydrates and brightens your skin, guiding you toward a vibrant and healthy complexion.

## Wellbeing Activities

Discover harmony, mindfulness, and cultural beauty through experiences inspired by our 8 Pillars of Wellbeing. Begin your day with morning yoga as Kyoto awakens, find inner stillness through meditation rooted in ancient wisdom, and immerse in the artistry of geiko and maiko performances that reflect Japan’s timeless heritage.

### Morning Yoga

Begin your day with gentle renewal. Guided by our instructors, experience morning yoga overlooking Japanese gardens, restoring balance to body and mind while infusing your day with renewed energy and clarity.

### Meditation

Discover inner peace through our guided meditation sessions. Experience true stillness and harmony in this special sanctuary, surrounded by the beautiful nature of ancient Kyoto.

### Geiko and Maiko Performance

Immerse yourself in the graceful world of Kyoto’s geiko and maiko. Discover how beauty, tradition, and mindfulness come together through elegant dance and music in this unforgettable and unique performance.

## Personal Onsen Experience

Indulge in an intimate hot spring retreat within our elegantly designed spa treatment rooms. Our natural hot spring waters offer therapeutic benefits, providing relief from chronic lower back pain and rheumatic conditions. As you soak in the traditional hinoki cypress bathtub, breathe in the gentle, naturally aromatic fragrance that emanates from the wood, creating a deeply restorative experience for both body and mind.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

Expand All

##### Opening Hours

Our Spa and Gallery are open from 11:00 to 20:00. The latest start time for a 60-minute treatment is 18:30.

##### About Advance Booking

We kindly recommend booking in advance. For same-day appointments, please contact us and we will be pleased to check availability.

##### About Appointment Times

Please arrive at the Spa reception 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment. Do note that late arrivals may result in a shorter treatment time.

##### Cancellation Policy

Should you need to amend or cancel your appointment, please contact us at least 4 hours in advance. Cancellations made within 4 hours will incur a 50% charge of the treatment fee. In the case of a no-show without prior notice, a 100% charge will apply.

##### Gift Vouchers

While we do not offer gift vouchers, we would be delighted to assist you if you wish to gift a Spa treatment or merchandise.

##### Use of the Spa

Spa treatments are available to both hotel guests and external visitors.

##### About Pre-existing Conditions

For your comfort and safety, please inform us of any health concerns, allergies, or pregnancy when making your reservation. Some treatments may require adjustments or may not be suitable, and our team will be pleased to guide you.
