## Space to relax in a modern Japanese atmosphere

Enjoy your stay in Kyoto in a space that combines traditional Japanese techniques with modern, natural elements.Perched on a hilltop in Higashiyama, Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto offers thoughtfully curated Kyoto accommodation with natural hot springs, tatami mat floors and breathtaking garden or city views. Other amenities of our Kyoto hotel rooms include wooden soaking bathtubs, cozy sitting areas and separate walk-in showers. Carefully selected furnishings blend traditional Japanese sensibilities with modern comforts to create a sanctuary for the senses.

Book a Kyoto hotel with onsen in room for a truly relaxing escape in the heart of Japan’s ancient capital.

##### Early Arrival/ Late Departure Policy

- Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability.
- Late check-out fee:
  - From 12:00 hours to 14:00 hours will be charged 30% of the Best Available Rate of the day.
  - From 14:00 hours to 16:00 hours will be charged 40% of the Best Available Rate of the day.
  - From 16:00 hours to 18:00 hours will be charged 50% of the Best Available Rate of the day.
  - From 18:00 hours will be charged 100% of the Best Available Rate of the day.

##### Best Price Guarantee

When you book your stay directly on banyantree.com, you'll be guaranteed the best room rate. Should you find a lower price elsewhere, we will match that rate and give you an additional 20% discount.

##### Cancellation Policy

The cancellation policies vary depending on the offers.

##### Child Policy

- Infants and toddlers, who are 4 years old or younger, accompanied by an adult, dine and stay in existing bedding with our compliments.
- Children 11 years old or younger, accompanied by an adult, can stay on existing bedding with our compliments and dine at 50% off regular breakfast prices, except for kid’s menu.
- Children 12 years old or older are considered adults.
