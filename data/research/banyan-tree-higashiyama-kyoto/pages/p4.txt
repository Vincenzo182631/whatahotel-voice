# Grand Onsen Retreat King

## Thoughtfully curated, where harmony, comfort, and beauty blend seamlessly with a private Onsen

The Grand Onsen Retreat King offers 56-58 square metres of refined comfort, where Japanese and Western design elements flow in harmony to forge a peaceful ambience.

It features contemporary furnishings that offer business and leisure travellers a relaxing retreat that awakens the senses. The plush King-Size bed is the star of the living space, which is dressed in 100% cotton linens, making sure guests are able to unwind in maximum comfort.

Every amenity is designed to make your stay effortless, from a cosy sitting area, a homey sofa, to a modern coffee machine to fuel you before dawn breaks. For deep restoration amidst stillness, the bathroom with a Japanese-style wooden soaking bathtub awaits your private ritual of renewal. Your private onsen room Kyoto experience begins here, in an oasis where modern contemporary converges with unfiltered nature with ease.
