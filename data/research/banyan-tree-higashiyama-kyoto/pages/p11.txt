# Facilities

## Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto

## A sanctuary with a unique experience that exceeds expectations

It is the only hotel in Kyoto with a traditional Noh theater stage, “Bamboo Pavilion“, which is connected to its special location. Refined Japanese cuisine at kaiseki restaurant "Ryozen," tranquil moments in the beautiful bamboo garden, rejuvenating natural hot spring baths, and restorative spa facilities will provide travellers with an unforgettable Kyoto experience.

### Bamboo Pavilion

The Bamboo Pavilion stands majestically against the backdrop of a lush bamboo forest amidst lush greenery.

A beautiful architecture and unique stage where you can experience performances that evoke traditional Japanese culture and contemporary art.

### Natural Hot Spring Bath

Heal the fatigue of your travels in the natural hot springs gushing out from within the premises.​

There are inside and outside bathtubs.​

**Location: 1F**

​

### Gym

### Bamboo Garden

Step into our enchanting bamboo garden, where towering bamboo groves create a natural sanctuary of tranquility in the heart of Kyoto. This serene oasis offers a contemplative escape, where the gentle rustling of bamboo leaves and filtered sunlight create an atmosphere of profound peace

### Banyan Tree Gallery

It is the only hotel in Kyoto with a traditional Noh theater stage, which is connected to its special location. Refined Japanese cuisine at kaiseki restaurant "Ryozen," tranquil moments in the beautiful bamboo garden, rejuvenating natural hot spring baths, and restorative spa facilities will provide travellers with an unforgettable Kyoto experience.

**Location: BF1**

### Banyan Tree Spa

Indulge in luxury massage and spa facilities with natural hot springs and steam saunas at our Kyoto hotel spa. Each therapeutic treatment draws on traditional Japanese healing practices to revitalise the senses. For a uniquely Kyoto experience, unwind in our gender-separated onsen, where warm, soothing waters restore mind, body, and soul.

**Location: BF1**
