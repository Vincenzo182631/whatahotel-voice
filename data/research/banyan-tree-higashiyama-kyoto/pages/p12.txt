# Experiences

## Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto

Discover a gateway to uncovering the timeless splendours of Kyoto, the ancient capital. Elevate your stay at Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto with a curated selection of experiences that ignite curiosity and wonder. Wander through serene bamboo groves, savour the refined artistry of kaiseki cuisine, and marvel at the majestic beauty of historic temples and shrines.

Each experience is thoughtfully crafted to awaken profound emotions and leave you with unforgettable memories of this eternal city's enduring beauty.

#### Tours

Stroll along ancient stone-paved streets, glide through historic neighborhoods in a traditional rickshaw, or wander the Philosopher's Path during cherry blossom and autumn seasons. Explore the hidden layers of the thousand-year-old capital, from ancient temples to iconic cultural landmarks.

#### Guest Activities

Immerse yourself in the timeless beauty of Kyoto through carefully curated cultural experiences that create unforgettable memories with your loved ones. From morning yoga sessions in our serene gardens shrouded in mist to the mystical world of Noh theater, the graceful artistry of Maiko performances, and the harmonious melodies of koto and shakuhachi, each activity is thoughtfully designed to connect you with the profound spirit and aesthetic of Japan's ancient capital.

#### Local Festival

Kyoto's millennium of history unfolds through enchanting seasonal festivals. Witness the refined elegance of Aoi Matsuri, the spectacular floats of Gion Matsuri, the historical pageantry of Jidai Matsuri, and the mystical flames of Gozan Okuribi. Discover the cultural heartbeat of Japan's ancient capital.

#### Geiko and Maiko Performance

Experience the refined artistry of skilled geiko and maiko performing traditional dance accompanied by shamisen music against the backdrop of a historic Noh stage. Witness the graceful movements of these artists adorned in exquisite kimono, offering an authentic encounter with Kyoto's cultural heritage and the essence of Japanese aesthetics.

#### Noh Performance
