# Ryozen

## An unforgettable dining experience next to the Bamboo pavilion

Set beside the tranquil Bamboo Pavilion, Ryozen is a Kyoto hotel restaurant defined by seasonality, artistry, and attentive hospitality. Every bite is packed with fresh, seasonal, local ingredients. Enjoy a variety of dining experiences, with a distinct flavour profile for each option. Guests may choose from refined kaiseki courses or immersive counter dining, where chefs prepare each course with quiet precision over the grill.
