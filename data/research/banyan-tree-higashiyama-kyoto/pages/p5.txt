# Banyan Onsen Retreat

## Indulge in profound relaxation and serene pleasure with magnificent views and a private natural Onsen.

For those seeking more spacious accommodations with a private natural Onsen, Banyan Onsen Retreat is the ideal option.
This beloved Kyoto Onsen hotel room boasts an impressive 74 square metres of living space - perfect for travellers who seek utmost indulgence. The lavish King-Size Bed equipped with 100% cotton bed linens takes centre stage, ensuring a tranquil and comfortable sleep experience every night spent in this sanctuary.

Other details further elevate your stay, from the traditional tatami mat floor, a sitting area with magnificent views, to a comfortable sofa perfect for relaxation and a coffee machine that makes every moment an opportunity to indulge yourself. When your body craves some pampering, step into the separate walk-in shower or a wooden soaking bathtub to recharge with ease.
