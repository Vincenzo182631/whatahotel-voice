# Bar Ryozen

## TASTE LOCAL SAKE WHILE LISTENING TO THE STORY OF RICE

Delight your palate with a curated selection of Japanese sakes and premium cocktails at our Kyoto sake bar, where each pour reflects the heritage of local ingredients. Flavourful expressions from nearby Fushimi and other regions shine the spotlight on traditional Japanese sakes, which are served alongside Kyoto-style afternoon tea and locally crafted sweets.

Ideal for intimate gatherings or a gentle prelude to dinner, the experience embraces the essence of Japanese bar culture with every sip. Here, flavours are savoured slowly, and the story of rice is shared through taste and tradition, all being enhanced by attentive hospitality.
