# Onsen Retreat King

## A comfy room with Japanese courtyard garden views is adjacent to your private Onsen.

Set alongside a Japanese courtyard garden, the Onsen Retreats offers a rare Kyoto private onsen experience within a tranquil setting. Nestled quietly on the second floor of Banyan Tree Higashiyama, each retreat comes with a tatami mat floor, a sitting area, a comfortable sofa, a coffee machine, and a bathroom with a wooden soaking bathtub. Start your day with a hot cup of coffee while kicking back on the sofa overlooking the botanical gardens, and end it on a high note with a nice bath before having a well-earned rest on the lavish King-Size bed, which is equipped with 100% cotton bed linens. Every moment spent here helps you appreciate the goodness in life.

Guests staying in Onsen Retreat can enjoy complimentary snacks and refreshments from the minibar, exclusive of alcohol.

\*The benefits offered above are only applicable to bookings via banyantree.com.
