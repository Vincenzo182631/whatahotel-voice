# Ryozen Sen Bettei

## An Exclusive Culinary Journey at Banyan Tree

Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto welcomes Ryozen SEN Bettei, an exclusive outpost of the acclaimed Michelin-starred restaurant "SEN" from Kyoto's historic Kiyomizu Gojo district.

At our restaurant, we offer a carefully curated, special dining experience. In a setting inspired by traditional Noh theatre, guests witness the seamless blend of centuries-old kaiseki traditions with contemporary culinary artistry, creating an unforgettable dialogue between chef, cuisine, and the serene beauty of Higashiyama.
