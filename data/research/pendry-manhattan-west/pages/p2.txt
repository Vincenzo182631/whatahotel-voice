# Manhattan Accommodations

## A Midtown West Manhattan hotel with guestrooms and suites soaked in Pendry style.

A new side of the city. Pendry Manhattan West is a beacon of contemporary style and elegant comfort with 164 guestrooms and 30 hotel suites located in a Manhattan luxury hotel that blends a California-inspired sensibility with New York’s inimitable sense of place, all brought to life with stunning Midtown accommodations, amenities including signature dining, in-room dining, and more, and proximity to iconic New York City landmarks at every turn.

## Pendry Manhattan West Accommodations FAQs

### What views do rooms at Pendry Manhattan West offer?

Pendry Manhattan West guestrooms and suites offer views of the city or Manhattan West Plaza, depending on the room or suite selected.

### Is Pendry Manhattan West family friendly?

Yes. Pendry Manhattan West welcomes families with a range of room and suite options suited to different group sizes. Queen Queen Guestrooms accommodate up to 4 guests, Two Bedroom Suites accommodate up to 5 guests (maximum 4 adults), and select connecting guestrooms and suites are available on request, subject to availability. The hotel also welcomes dogs up to 60 pounds for an additional fee.

### Which Pendry Manhattan West accommodations are best for a romantic getaway?

The One Bedroom Suites are among the most popular choices for couples, ideal for anniversaries, honeymoons and other special occasions. Each suite features two separate living areas, one with a large L-shaped sofa, along with a walk-in closet with dressing area, guest washroom and city views.

### Is Pendry Manhattan West a good hotel for business travelers?

Yes. Pendry Manhattan West is located on Manhattan’s west side, a 6-minute walk from Moynihan Train Hall and Penn Station and a 13-minute walk from the Jacob K. Javits Convention Center. The hotel also offers a variety of meeting and event venues for business gatherings of any size.

### Is Pendry Manhattan West pet friendly?

Yes. Pendry Manhattan West welcomes dogs up to 60 pounds for an additional fee.

### Are connecting rooms available at Pendry Manhattan West?

Yes. Pendry Manhattan West offers select connecting guestrooms and suites. Since connecting accommodations are limited and subject to availability, they should be requested at the time of reservation rather than at check-in.

### Which Pendry Manhattan West room type is best for a longer stay?

For longer stays, Pendry Manhattan West’s suites are a popular choice, offering additional living space with separate sitting areas to relax or work during an extended stay. The Pendry Suite also includes a kitchenette with a refrigerator, oven and bar for guests staying longer term.

### How many guests can each Pendry Manhattan West room type accommodate?

Pendry Manhattan West offers guestrooms and suites across a range of group sizes, with connecting options available in select configurations.

- Queen Queen Guestrooms accommodate up to 4 guests.
- King Guestrooms accommodate up to 2 guests.
- Suites include Studio Suites, Alcove Studios, One Bedroom Suites, Two Bedroom Suites and Specialty Suites, accommodating up to 2-5 guests depending on configuration and rollaway bed options. Two Bedroom Suites accommodate up to 5 guests, with a maximum of 4 adults.

### What are check-in and check-out times at Pendry Manhattan West?

Check-in at Pendry Manhattan West begins at 4:00pm and check-out is at 12:00pm. Early check-in and late departure may be available on request and are subject to availability. Guests should contact the hotel in advance to request either.

### Does Pendry Manhattan West offer accessible accommodations?

Yes. Pendry Manhattan West offers ADA accessible guestrooms and suites featuring accessible bathrooms with roll-in showers and handrails, lowered closet rods, lowered light switches, bedroom wheelchair access and city views. Guests can view accessible room options or contact the hotel directly to request accessible accommodations.
