# Restaurants in Manhattan West

## Dine in New York City Style

Where Pendry meets Manhattan. A collection of carefully crafted gathering places just steps from Madison Square Garden. Welcoming hotel guests and locals alike with distinct design, understated service and some of the best food and drinks in the city. Whether it’s coffee in Garden Room, dinner in Zou Zou’s, or a nightcap in Bar Pendry, Pendry Manhattan West is setting the table in style.

Bar Pendry

Cocktail Bar in Manhattan

Elevating the expected. Our signature lobby bar is an intimate, richly designed space that evokes the essence of old New York with the modern grandeur of today. Finishes are warm and comfortable with an air of luxury refinement, signature drinks are hand-crafted and served with impeccable attention to detail, and spirited gatherings set the scene with conversation, camaraderie and perhaps an extra cocktail. Or two.

- Discover Bar Pendry

Garden Room

Coffee to Cocktails in Manhattan

Come for coffee, stay for cocktails. Steps from the hotel’s entrance, the Garden Room is a light, design-centric space where you’re welcome to sit back in style, for work or for play. High ceilings, natural light, lush greenery and ample seating set the backdrop for an all-day coffee menu by Vittoria, classic cocktails and evenings with friends.

- Discover Garden Room

Zou Zou’s

Eastern Mediterranean Restaurant in Manhattan

Lively and modern, Zou Zou’s is an Eastern Mediterranean restaurant from some of the most notable culinary names in the city, helmed by Executive Chef Juliana Latif. Offering a menu inspired by and celebrating the cuisine and flavors of the Eastern Mediterranean region, bringing in influences from Lebanon, Israel, Turkey, Egypt, Syria, and Jordan, among others to our New York City hotel.

- Discover Zou Zou’s

Chez Zou

Cocktail Lounge in Manhattan

Where more is more. Welcome to Chez Zou, a bold, lush and colorful cocktail lounge from our friends at Quality Branded where wildly inventive, handcrafted drinks are served alongside a menu of small bites full of international flavor and flair. With a very Pendry twist.

- Discover Chez Zou

Vista Lounge

Breakfast Restaurant in Manhattan

Come, sit, stay a while. A daytime outpost with an easygoing allure, Vista Lounge is a retro-meets-modern dining space on the fourth floor offering daily breakfast for hotel guests and locals alike.

- Discover Vista Lounge

In-Room Dining

Room Service at Pendry Manhattan West

At your service. Day or night. Pendry’s signature in-room dining features a specially crafted menu of small bites, entrées, beverages and sweets, specific to our Midtown hotel and the surrounding local culinary influences, all carefully delivered to your guestroom to satisfy any craving with convenience and comfort top of mind.

## Pendry Manhattan West Dining FAQs

### How many restaurants and bars are located at Pendry Manhattan West?

Pendry Manhattan West offers 5 restaurants and bars, including Zou Zou’s, an Eastern Mediterranean restaurant serving vibrant, shareable dishes, Chez Zou, a cocktail lounge known for inventive drinks and small bites and Bar Pendry, the hotel’s signature cocktail bar. Guests can also enjoy light bites and drinks at The Garden Room and breakfast and daytime dining at Vista Lounge.

### Does Pendry Manhattan West offer in-room dining?

Yes, Pendry Manhattan West offers in-room dining, allowing guests to enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner and late-night fare delivered directly to their guestroom or suite at our luxury hotel in New York City.

### Do any restaurants at Pendry Manhattan West offer outdoor dining or scenic views?

Yes, Zou Zou’s offers seasonal outdoor seating, creating a vibrant setting for al fresco dining in Midtown. Chez Zou features a fourth-floor terrace patio with open-air seating overlooking the plaza.

### Where can I go for light bites and signature cocktails at Pendry Manhattan West?

For light bites and signature cocktails at Pendry Manhattan West, head to Bar Pendry. This stylish lounge offers a curated menu of craft cocktails, wines and sharables in a vibrant yet relaxed atmosphere, perfect for a night out or an after-work gathering.

### What is the largest party size each restaurant can accommodate?

The maximum reservation size at each of Pendry Manhattan West’s restaurants is 10 guests per table. For parties larger than 10, we recommend exploring one of our private event spaces located our fifth-floor, which are designed to accommodate larger groups and special events.
