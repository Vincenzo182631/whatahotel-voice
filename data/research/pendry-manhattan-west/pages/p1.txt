## See A New Side Of New York City. Only At Pendry Manhattan West.

Pendry Manhattan West hotel is an understated, California-inspired Midtown destination that blends elegance and ease with seamless style in the very heart of the city. With the stunning signature façade, airy and relaxed interiors and the highly personalized luxury service and amenities, we are an extension of the city’s always unique energy with a pulse that is all our own.
