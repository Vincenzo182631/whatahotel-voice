## Our Rooms

### at Park Hotel Kenmare

Every room at Park Hotel Kenmare tells its own story. Following the complete renewal of all guest rooms and suites, completed in 2026, our accommodation combines the warmth and character of one of Ireland's most historic hotels with the comfort and craftsmanship expected of a five-star stay.

Original antiques have been carefully restored, period features preserved, and every space thoughtfully redesigned to create rooms that feel both elegant and deeply personal. Alongside timeless interiors, guests will discover one of Ireland's most significant private contemporary art collections, with works by internationally renowned artists displayed throughout the hotel.

Throughout the rest of the hotel, calming, neutral tones and natural fabrics - wool carpets, linen upholstery - create a quiet backdrop for the antiques and the art works.  Bathrooms have been fully upgraded with italian marble and underfoot heating, and modern comforts, from USB charging to locally made mini-bars, which are tucked in without disturbing the period feel.

Whether you're planning a romantic escape, a luxury weekend break in Kerry or a longer stay along the Wild Atlantic Way, our guest rooms and suites offer a place to slow down, settle in and feel at home.

Since 1897, Park Hotel Kenmare has welcomed guests to the heart of County Kerry. Today, following the thoughtful renewal of every guest room and suite, the hotel continues to evolve while remaining true to its remarkable heritage.

Explore our collection of luxury guest rooms and suites and discover a five-star stay where history, art and comfort come together in the heart of Kenmare.
