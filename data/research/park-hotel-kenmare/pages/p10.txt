## Location

Set on the shores of Kenmare Bay in the heart of County Kerry, Park Hotel Kenmare is just a short stroll from the colourful heritage town of Kenmare. A place of independent shops, welcoming pubs, galleries and cafés, Kenmare offers the charm of a traditional Irish town while serving as the gateway to some of the country's most spectacular scenery.

Our location makes Park Hotel Kenmare the ideal base for exploring the Ring of Kerry, the rugged beauty of the Beara Peninsula, the dramatic coastline of the Wild Atlantic Way and Killarney, Dingle and Cork City within a short drive. Whether you're setting out on a scenic drive, walking mountain trails, discovering hidden beaches or visiting historic houses and gardens, the very best of the south-west is within easy reach.

While there's plenty to explore beyond our gates, many guests choose to slow down and enjoy the peace of the hotel. Stroll through our gardens, take in the ever-changing views across Kenmare Bay or simply wander into town whenever the mood takes you. Here, the tranquillity of the countryside and the warmth of one of Ireland's most beloved towns come together, creating a destination that feels both wonderfully secluded and effortlessly connected.

Visit Kenmare.ie to find out more about Kenmare, Discover Kerry to find out more about Kerry, Beara Tourism to find out more about the Beara Peninsula and Ring of Kerry to find out more about the Ring of Kerry.

## Getting Here

By Air

- Kerry Airport, located 40 km from Kenmare, has daily connections to Dublin, London, and Manchester.
- Cork International Airport, located 100 km from Kenmare, has options for international flights around the world.
- Shannon International Airport, located 160 km from Kenmare, has direct flights from many airports in the U.S.A. and England, as well as Europe.

By Road

Check AA Route Finder for all routes and updated road information. If travelling from Dublin it is advisable to travel from Dublin to Cork and then follow the signs for Tralee and Kenmare.

By Rail

Train services from Dublin and other major cities in Ireland stop at Killarney, located just 30 km from Kenmare.
