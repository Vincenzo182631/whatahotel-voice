# 12-Acre Estate Gardens & Grounds: Park Hotel Kenmare

## Hotel Grounds

## Grounds

The Park Hotel Kenmare is situated on 12 acres of expansive gardens. Guests are welcome to wander through the grounds, which include an abundant herb garden frequented by both the bees and our chefs, a croquet lawn and tennis courts, an abundance of native flowers and even our resident horse. Continue through the gates to Reenagross Park, where trees grow every which way and you can dip your toes into the Kenmare River. The stunning gardens are the perfect backdrop to a marvellous visit. Feel free to say hello or ask a question to our dedicated gardeners.
