# Luxury Spa Breaks & Wellness in Kerry

Landscape is our new spa at Park Hotel Kenmare. It’s built around the belief that our relationships - to land, water, ourselves and one another - are what make us well. Not an escape from this place, but a deeper encounter with it. A place that asks nothing of you except that you allow yourself to be exactly where you are.

Landscape is open daily from 7:30am to 7:30pm.  Treatments are available from 10am to 6pm. Memberships are available for those who would like to make Landscape part of their daily routine.
