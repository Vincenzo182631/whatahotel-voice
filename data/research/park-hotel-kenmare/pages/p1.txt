## Welcome

At the end of the winding, flower-studded drive and past the twisting iron lamppost is Park Hotel Kenmare. A meeting point between the land and the sea, our hotel rests on the rich soil of Kerry, a potent land that feeds the spirit of who we are.

With a spectacular team that offers the best of Irish hospitality, Landline, our seasonally driven restaurant that transforms the local harvest into something magnificent, a ground-breaking collection of poignant and internationally acclaimed art, and an ever-present feeling of warmth and openness, Park Hotel Kenmare offers a new kind of destination.

Wellness at the Park includes Landscape, Our new Spa, a quiet space inspired by the relationship between the Irish landscape outside and our inner landscape. Alongside the 25-metre lap pool, guests can enjoy spa treatments by Susanne Kaufmann and Seabody, wellness technology therapies, outdoor baths, sauna and steam, yoga and Pilates, a fully equipped gym and a programme of classes and wellness experiences.

Our stained glass windows cast a transformative hue over Kenmare Bay, providing a beam of sunlight across the dancing shadows of the Caha Mountains and the Wild Atlantic Way.

## History of the Hotel

The Lawrence Photograph Collection French, Robert, 1841-1917 photographer

Park Hotel Kenmare has been welcoming guests from around the world since 1897. Initially constructed as a railway hotel, Park Hotel Kenmare was designed as a place to stay whilst travelling in the southwest of Ireland. Throughout the centuries, this iconic 5 star hotel has retained the charm, year-round wood burning fireplace, eccentricity, and elegance it was built with, while integrating modern comforts and amenities.

Today, Park Hotel Kenmare is proudly run by Bryan and Tara Meehan, who believe that owning a hotel is more than running a business–it's the chance to build community, celebrate Irish culture, and showcase a new type of hospitality.

Under their guidance, the sun streaming through the stained-glass windows shines a new light on the Park Hotel Kenmare, which is transforming into a place that invites guests into a place of comfort, connection, and storytelling, while honouring the rich history of the Kerry land we rest upon.
