# Landline Restaurant

## Landline

Landline, taking its name from a Sean Scully piece, celebrates food from where the land meets the sea.  Start your day with a cup of Blue Bottle Coffee and cooked to order breakfast, using only the best seasonal and locally sourced ingredients.

Our dinner menu is artfully amplified to something beautiful, seafood from Spillanes in Killarney and Star Seafoods here in Kenmare, complement meat sourced from Peter O’Sullivan in Sneem and Rosscarbery Meats.

With views of the Caha Mountains and Kenmare Bay, and the backdrop of world renowned art, Landline is a remarkable place to experience some of the best of Ireland’s culinary offerings.

Breakfast – 7am to 10:30am weekdays, 7am to 11am weekends

Dinner – 6:30pm to 9pm

We are delighted to welcome families to our 6:30pm dinner sitting.  The dining rooms is reserved for adult guests for later sittings.
