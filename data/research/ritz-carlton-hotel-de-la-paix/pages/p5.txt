Body, Mind, Spirit - Wellness Rituals
Discover holistic wellness rituals that unite body, mind, and spirit. Rooted in celestial harmony and the power of transformation, our signature Wellness Rituals are designed to elevate your inner state—inviting deep renewal, radiant energy, and a profound sense of balance.
Reserve
Star Gazing
Embrace a moment of stillness in the warmth of your private hammam. Renew your skin with the Perfect Body Smoother ritual, followed by a 60-minute Energy & Vitality Massage. Infused with La Vallée’s Boost Vitality Oil, this experience awakens inner energy and restores natural vitality.
Stellar Radiance
Indulge in a full-body ritual to restore and deeply hydrate. Enjoy exfoliation, a pink clay mask, and a rich nourishing balm. A Caviar face refresher and 24kt Gold Eye Mask complete this radiant journey—leaving your skin plumped, renewed, and glowing with stellar beauty.
Celestial Connection For Two
Designed for unforgettable shared moments, begin with a tranquil hammam escape. Enjoy a 90-minute massage with warm volcanic stones to melt tension, followed by a 30-minute Caviar Collagen facial—leaving you refreshed, radiant, and deeply connected.
High Technology
Waves of Wellness: Welnamis Treatment
For a heightened sensory experience, elevate your ritual with the Welnamis bed—a state-of-the-art innovation combining gentle vibrational therapy with harmonizing sound waves. This immersive technology promotes deep relaxation and balance. Welnamis may also be enjoyed on its own as a standalone treatment.

At Time of Reservation
Please inform us of the following: allergies or medical conditions including pregnancy; if you have a request for a specific Spa Therapist; if you are celebrating any special occasion.
Arrival Time
Please arrive 10 minutes prior to your scheduled treatment. Arriving late will limit the time for your treatment, thus decreasing its effectiveness and your enjoyment. Your ritual will end on time, so the next guest is not delayed. The full value of your treatment will be charged.
Cancellation Policy
If you need to reschedule or cancel any spa appointments, please notify us eight hours in advance. Missed appointments or cancellations without proper notice are subject to a fifty percent charge for the reserved service.
