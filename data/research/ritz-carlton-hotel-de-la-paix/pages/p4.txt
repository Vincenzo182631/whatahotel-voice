Fusion-Forward Cuisine on the Lake
Dining
Dining at The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva honors Switzerland’s vibrant culinary scene. The Living Room offers internationally inspired menus all day, including weekend brunch and à-la-carte breakfast, while Fiskeabar offers a cuisine off the beaten path, led by a chef with boundless creativity. The hotel also houses Dans le Noir? a restaurant where darkness reveals a sensory and human experience guided by visually impaired personnel.

The Chef
