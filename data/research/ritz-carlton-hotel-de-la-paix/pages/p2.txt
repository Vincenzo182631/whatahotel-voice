Redesigned Luxury Accommodations
Suites & Rooms
Awash in natural light, our suites and luxury hotel rooms in Geneva blend warmth with contemporary sophistication. Expansive layouts, bespoke details, and impeccable service complement breathtaking views of Lake Geneva, the Alps, and the Old Town—offering a stay as effortless as it is extraordinary.
Make Space for Opulence
Signature Suites
At The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva, each suite is a tribute to timeless elegance. From the regal allure of the Grace Kelly Suite to the tranquil sophistication of the Mont-Blanc Suite, history and modern luxury intertwine effortlessly. Framed by breathtaking views, these storied spaces have welcomed world figures and witnessed moments of history — now, they invite you to create your own.
