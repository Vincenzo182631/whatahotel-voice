Distinctive
Signature Suites
At The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva, each suite is an invitation to experience the apogee of sophistication and comfort. From the regal charm of the Grace Kelly Suite, to the serene elegance of the Mont-Blanc Suite, our accommodations seamlessly blend 160 years of storied heritage—witness to historic world events and renowned luminaries—with fine contemporary luxury. Framed by breathtaking views, these suites provide an exquisite setting to craft your own iconic story in Geneva.
Majestic Tranquility by the Lake
The 2-Bedroom Lakefront Suite
A sanctuary of bespoke luxury, the 2-Bedroom Lakefront Suite weaves the serenity of Lake Geneva with the understated grandeur of The Ritz-Carlton. Spacious and refined, it is the perfect canvas for your own story of Geneva.
Views
A Panoramic Masterpiece
Step onto your private balcony to marvel at sweeping views of Lake Geneva, the Jet d’Eau, and the timeless majesty of Mont Blanc. Floor-to-ceiling windows flood the suite with natural light, framing the iconic landscape as a constant reminder of Geneva’s beauty.
