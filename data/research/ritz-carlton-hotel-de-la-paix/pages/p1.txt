Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva
Geneva's Lakeside Haven, 1865
Welcome to an emblem of Swiss legacy, reimagined for today. Rooted in more than 160 years of history, where world events were once shaped within our walls, the hotel now welcomes travellers seeking both refinement and renewal. Framed by Lake Geneva and the Alps, our boutique retreat offers the intimacy of a private residence with the grandeur of a global icon. Here, timeless heritage and contemporary artistry merge in a suspended dimension of time, where transformative and curated experiences unfold, wellness becomes a journey of renewal, and gastronomy awakens the senses to wonder. Every detail is designed to leave you not only enriched, but transformed. We invite you to live Geneva not as a guest, but as an author: with the city as your white canvas, and your stay with us the beginning of your own Swiss story.

Design
The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva reveals a renewed expression of contemporary living where heritage meets modern design with quiet precision. Light-filled guest rooms and suites reveal noble materials and calm proportions. In the lobby, the hotel’s signature chandelier rises above a warm architectural palette. Dining venues orient toward panoramic views across Lake Geneva.
History
The Hotel de la Paix was given this name for a reason. His story begins in the XIX century, in a very complex but fascinating historical moment: citizens from different countries were fighting for their independence and war was in the air. This hotel hosted many crucial meetings, including the International Congress of Peace that re-shaped the destiny of Europe.
Learn More
Heritage
Princess Grace Kelly, who was usually staying at the suite that is now named after her, loved the beautiful view of the luxurious landscape that can still be enjoyed from its windows. The duality of Grace Kelly's beauty, defined by Alfred Hitchcock as cold and sensual at the same time, is reflected in the room's details, offering sophisticated art deco design and gold leaf, creating an elegant sanctuary.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 10.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of ₣150.00 per night applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva?
Off-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva. The daily valet fee is ₣55.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva is Geneva Airport (GVA). GVA is located approximately 7.3 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva have an airport shuttle to Geneva Airport (GVA)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva does not have an airport shuttle to Geneva Airport.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton Hotel de la Paix, Geneva does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
