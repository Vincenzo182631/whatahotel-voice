Highlights
The Water Fountain
Since 1891 it has graced the centre of Geneva's harbour, and is the must-see tourist attraction! Used initially for distributing the Rhône's propelling force to the city's craftspeople, the magnificent water jet is 140 meters high, with 500 liters of water passing through it per second at a speed of 200 km per hour!
Carouge, Geneva’s Small Italy
Just a stone's throw from the city centre, Carouge will transport you to Southern Europe. Relax by strolling through shady terraces, numerous independent shops and artisanal workshops in Carouge's alleys and passageways. At night, experience the area's unique ambiance thanks to its many trendy restaurants and bars.
Geneva Wine Region
Although not known for its wine, Geneva’s winegrowing region is easily the most picturesque in the world and the largest in Switzerland producing 13.5 million liters annually. Walk through beautiful vineyards and enjoy wine tastings and open cellar weekends, though some by appointment only.

Activities in Geneva
Plage des Eaux-Vives
A lovely sand beach and green grass area equipped with tire swings and a jungle gym ideal for families with small children.
Bastions Park
A must-visit park located in the heart of the city. There you will find the renowned Reformation Wall, the black and white giant chessboards, a restaurant and a big playground for kids.
Bâtie Woods
A wood in the middle of the city with inspiring walks through 12 hectares of natural forest. Located next to the Arve River, the forest hosts an anima park and a large play area for the children.
Biking
Rent a bike for all the family and explore the beautiful shores of Lake Geneva with new eyes.
Bubbles Kids Club
A multi-activity club offering a various range of sport and art classes.
Conservatory and Botanical Garden
With a living collection of 14,000 species from around the world and a historical herbarium with 6 million botanical specimens, the Botanical Gardens are a living museum with greenhouses, herbarium and a zoo.
Escape Game
An interactive adventure played in small group where all the players are locked in theme rooms to solve riddles in order to get out.
Laser Game
Held in a labyrinth built on multiple floors, the laser game is all about speed and precision. The mission is to hit as many players with your laser gun.
Les Corsaires
Discover the best address for boat trips on pedal boats, powerboats and sailboats, as well as its sailing courses.
Madeleine Carousel (during Winter)
Take a tour on Geneva most famous merry-go-round located in the Old Town.
Distance: 750 m / Walking distance: 10 min
Miniature Boats
From April to June, British ferries, cruise and steamships take on board young captains who play in the inflows of Lake Geneva.
Mini Train Tour
Explore Geneva on a Mini Train taking you on a 30-minute journey along Geneva’s left bank to discover its landmarks and parks.
Museum of the History of Science
Located in the beautiful Mon Repos Park, the museum highlights a collection of ancient scientific instruments from the 17th to the 19th century and documents the scientific progress in different fields such as astronomy, microscopy, electricity and meteorology.
Natural History Museum
Discover the largest museum of natural history of Switzerland with four floors of exhibits and activities all year round.
Palais des Nations
Be one of the 100,000 visitors to discover the United Nations headquarters in Geneva. From The Salle des Pas Perdus to the Assembly Hall, the guides will will walk you through the history of the palace as well as the current UN activities.
Yatouland Park
A true paradise from children up to 10 years hold, Yatouland Park offers bouncy castles, karaoke, laser game, paintball, escape game, a water park and different playground areas.
Activities outside Geneva
Alpine Garden
The ideal setting to relax and walk through a magnificent collection of plants and rock formation.
Aquatis – Aquarium and Vivarium in Lausanne
Immerse yourselves in a journey on the five continents to discover the world’s freshwater ecosystems.
Aquaparc
Let your children live memorable adventures in the wave pools, pirate ship and lazy river.
Cailler Chocolate Factory
A sweet adventure awaits at Maison Cailler in Broc. Explore the history of chocolate through multi-sensorial experiences and enjoy the finest variety of flavors during the chocolate tasting.
Gavotte's Farm
Let your children interact with farm animals and ride ponies in the company of specialists in Geneva’s beautiful countryside.
Horse riding
Three miles from the center of Geneva, the Onex stables offer riding school and pension for horses and ponies.
Mont Salève Cable Car
The cable car of Mont Salève carries you to an altitude of 1,100 meters in less than five minutes. Immense playground for sports lovers, it’s the place to be to experience paragliding, climbing, mountain biking, trekking and more.
On’Kart
Take fun karting lessons on a circuit from 700 to 1500 meters followed by snacks and drinks.
Signal de Bougy
One of Switzerland’s biggest adventure parks, Signal de Bougy features a treetop park, playgrounds, mini train, mini gold, kids club and boating activities.
Ski at St Cergues
Enjoy skiing in the closest Swiss resort from Geneva. St Cergues is a small domain suitable for kids and beginners. Ask our Concierge to organize trip and lessons.
Toboganning Park
Snowtube in the Vaudois Alps and combine fun and adrenaline in the park’s slopes.
Vivarium
Observe superb snakes, lizards, crocodiles and turtles from the all around the world in a natural environment.
Zoo Servion
At Servion Zoo, Siberian tigers, lions, monkeys, panthers and many other animals fascinate children and adults alike. Open all year round, the largest animal park in French-speaking Switzerland is a must for family holidays.
