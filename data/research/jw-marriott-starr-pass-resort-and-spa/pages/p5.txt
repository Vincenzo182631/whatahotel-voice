### Fauna & Flora

See the massive saguaro in the Tucson Mountains. The saguaro cactus can live as long as 200 years, growing over fifty feet tall and weigh more than 10 tons. The saguaro has become an icon of the desert, with its distinctive tall trunk and numerous curved arms that branch upward.

## Hiking

Explore four scenic mountain ranges - Tucson, Tortolita, Santa Rita and Santa Catalina. Hiking is available year-round, but we recommend an early morning adventure during the summer months. The trail-head for several hiking and biking runs can be found at our hotel near the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum, Tucson​.

## Your Gateway to the University of Arizona

Located just minutes from the University of Arizona, JW Marriott Tucson Starr Pass Resort & Spa offers a convenient and comfortable retreat for parents and alumni alike. Just a short, 15-minute drive from campus, it provides easy access for university visits, graduation weekends, and game days. Whether visiting your student, reconnecting as an alumnus, or cheering on the Wildcats during football and basketball season, guests can enjoy a seamless blend of proximity and resort-style relaxation—making every visit to Tucson both effortless and memorable.

Local Area & Activities

Hiking

Arduous journeys. Some call it hiking. We prefer to call it trekking. Where we go is not always easy. It is work, and sometimes hard work, but with many rewards. The Southwest region offers much more than what many imagine deserts to be. The diversity of flora and fauna found in this region affirm the harmony of nature. We have hikes right out our back door. We offer a diverse selection of trails that start on the hotel's property. Whether you explore on your own or contract with our guides, you will appreciate the beauty of The Sonoran Desert. Our resort recommends Southwest Trekking guide services. Hiking outside of Tucson offers many diverse and fun adventures, including kid-friendly ones. Southwest Trekking has supplied thousands of guided hiking trips in and around the Tucson area. When it comes to hiking in Tucson, Southern Arizona, the Southwest and Mexico, we know the best trails available. Come join us for a custom guided adventure.

Starr Pass Biking

Take an escorted Mountain Bike ride among majestic, Saguaro-filled hills known as the Tucson Mountain Park. Within minutes, you'll feel transported back to a time when men and women lived off the land and relied on the desert habitat for survival. Whether it's your first time cycling in many years, or you're looking to work a few miles into your fitness plan, we can provide a ride to suit your needs. Extreme to sublime. It's your choice. Ask us about family biking outings.

Rock Climbing

Rock climbing is only a short drive from our Tucson resort. The Santa Catalina Mountains have become a popular destination for climbers around the world. However, this unique range, with quality rock from elevations of 3,000 feet through 9,000 feet, has remained a haven for solitude and true wilderness experience. Come climb with us in the beautiful Coronado National Forest, all levels of experience welcome.

Trail Running

Join us for an early morning awakening of body, mind and spirit in Tucson. Running on the earthen trail, you experience the desert stretching out before you. Our hotel recommends Southwest Trekking guide services. Guides are trained and experienced to facilitate you with a positive experience. We can and will adapt to your needs and ability. Come trek with Southwest Trekking, the professional guide service.

Southwest Trekking

For beginning hikers, family hiking groups or seasoned veterans, Southwest Trekking knows the right trail for all. The diverse basin and range topography of the Tucson, Arizona offers a wide variety of terrain options featuring rocky, hilly or gentle trails. If you're looking for challenging climbs followed by perilous descents or hikes through gently rolling grasslands on dirt roads we can find the right trail for you.
