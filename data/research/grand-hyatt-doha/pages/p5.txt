### Exceptional staff and lovely hotel

A beautiful hotel, close to everything in the area via uber. The staff were all very attentive and polite. We had a great welcome at reception, had a lovely clean room with a lovely view towards the city. We enjoyed breakfast every morning with the friendly staff. Gabriel in the club lounge gave exceptional service. The hotel itself is beautiful, although there are parts that require some maintenance and update.
The children/ family pool area is in need of essential maintenance to the flooring around that area and the pool bar.
We walked around by the villas and felt the pool there is also needing the same attention. The villa area felt less looked after and I wouldn't personally stay there (didn't go inside the villas)
The infinity pool is beautiful and this should be the standard of the other pools. The monkey bar is really nice inside- it's a bit more clubby with the music and a bit louder if you like that kind of thing. I personally feel it would be better as more of a chill out beach club vibe, rather than house music and banging tunes!
Overall we loved our stay, the staff made it special with the level of service they gave.

### Very nice stay at a beautiful hotel

The overall stay was wonderful. Impeccable beautiful grounds, very clean, wonderful breakfast. We stood as guests of honor in a gorgeous suite for 2 nights to explore Doha. The room was big, bathroom had a large tub, very spacious shower, Balmain brand and no need to bring any toiletries. The view was amazing.
However, due to severe snow storm, our flight was cancelled and we were stranded for 2 more nights in Doha. We wanted to add 2 more nights to our existing reservation, obviously, paid with points, however, that wasn't accomodated. We were downgraded to a regular room without ability to use GOH benefits and had to pay for the club access. That was a little disappointing as I am a very loyal Hyatt customer, staying exclusively at Hyatt whenever I travel.
Everything else was exceptional at this hotel. Staff at the club and Santa Monica restaurant (which is included for breakfast if you have club benefits) was very kind and helpful. The breakfast was very delicious, many choices and varied daily. The club food was also very very good.
Overall, I would definitely return to this hotel, if all goes as planned, but considering the stress of rebooking flights and scrambling with changed plans during unforseen circumstances, don't expect status recognition or reservation flexibility.
