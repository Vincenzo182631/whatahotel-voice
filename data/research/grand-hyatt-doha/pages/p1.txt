## Discover the perfect blend of grandeur and comfort at our luxury hotel in Doha

Nestled along the scenic shoreline of West Bay Lagoon, Grand Hyatt Doha Hotel & Villas is a luxurious five-star urban resort offering spacious villas, stunning Arabian architecture, and lush landscaped gardens. Spanning over 150,000 square metres, the hotel features multiple swimming pools, a 400-metre private beach, and world-class amenities for both leisure and business travelers. Conveniently located just a short drive from Hamad International Airport, the hotel is directly across from Lagoona Mall and near popular destinations like The Pearl-Qatar and Lusail City, Doha’s newest commercial and entertainment hub.

Give the Gift of Grand Experiences

Discover a world of exceptional experiences with the Grand Hyatt Doha Hotel & Villas Gift Card. Whether treating yourself or someone special, our thoughtfully designed gift cards offer the perfect way to enjoy the finest our resort has to offer.

From luxurious stays and rejuvenating spa treatments to memorable dining experiences and more, the gift card can be redeemed across the property for a wide range of services and experiences.

**Gift card values start from QAR 250 and can be customized to your preferred amount**, making it easy to find the perfect gift for any occasion.

Give the gift of choice, relaxation, and unforgettable moments at Grand Hyatt Doha Hotel & Villas.

Jaula Club & Spa

Jaula Club & Spa echoes the symmetry and splendor of a royal palace. Using the most advanced European spa techniques, our expert therapists provide attentive, exquisite service. Discover the unparalleled beauty of Qatar’s capital city and the majesty of living well.

Beach and Pools

Make your stay even more spectacular by spending some time in one of our pools—four outdoor, one indoor—or on our private beach, one of the largest in Doha. The beach is open 8 a.m.–6 p.m.

Fitness Centre

Our 24/7 fitness centre offers the latest equipment along with personal-training options for guests looking to get in shape or stay fit. A range of available classes—plus outdoor areas with views of landscaped gardens and the sea—means there’s no better place to get some exercise.

Villas

Our superbly designed and luxuriously furnished villas are adjacent to the hotel and offer long- or short-term rentals complete with fully equipped kitchens plus access to two swimming pools and our private beach.

### Rocca

Italian

Rocca offers more than just Italian classics, it’s a relaxed spot where you can enjoy authentic cuisine and light bites by the pool. Located next to one of the hotel’s swimming pools and known for it's best tiramisu in town, Rocca also serves freshly prepared dishes from a lively open kitchen, with pizzas and pastas cooked to perfection in a traditional wood-fired oven. As the weather cools, the outdoor terrace becomes the ideal setting for al fresco dining, combining great food with a laid-back atmosphere.
