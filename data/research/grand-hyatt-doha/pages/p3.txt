## Rocca

Rocca offers more than just Italian classics, it’s a relaxed spot where you can enjoy authentic cuisine and light bites by the pool. Located next to one of the hotel’s swimming pools and known for it's best tiramisu in town, Rocca also serves freshly prepared dishes from a lively open kitchen, with pizzas and pastas cooked to perfection in a traditional wood-fired oven. As the weather cools, the outdoor terrace becomes the ideal setting for al fresco dining, combining great food with a laid-back atmosphere.

## Majlis

Majlis offers a contemporary Middle Eastern dining experience on the 1st floor, with private seating for added comfort and intimacy. Enjoy traditional favorites with a modern twist, alongside a selection of shisha. When the weather allows, unwind on the outdoor terrace by the promenade with views of lush gardens and The Pearl in the distance.

## Isaan

Isaan, A Michelin Bib Gourmand Restaurant, located on the 2nd floor, offers a vibrant and contemporary Thai dining experience, perfect for those seeking a relaxed yet flavorful evening. Whether you're enjoying a casual dinner or hosting an intimate gathering in our private dining room, you'll be treated to authentic Thai cuisine crafted with care. Savor an à la carte menu or explore signature dishes, while watching the energy of our open kitchens, where every dish is prepared live. Discover the true taste of Thailand in a setting that's warm, inviting, and uniquely Isaan.

## Dunes Bar

Entry for 21 years and over only.

Dunes Bar is the go-to spot for a lively night out, offering a mix of live entertainment, themed nights, and regular happy hours to keep the energy high. Whether you’re looking to unwind with friends after a long day or enjoy a fun-filled evening with great music and crafted cocktails, Dunes sets the stage for memorable moments. With an inviting atmosphere, an extensive drink menu, and a spacious terrace overlooking West Bay Lagoon, it's the perfect place to chill, socialize, and enjoy the vibrant nightlife.

Take your time here up a notch with a variety of games to enjoy: choose from Jenga, Ludo, billiards, foosball, and more while you relax and unwind.

## Santa Monica Breakfast Club

Santa Monica Breakfast Club brings a taste of the West Coast to Doha, serving up comforting favorites with a healthy twist and a splash of SoCal charm. With its bright, playful atmosphere and relaxed vibe, it’s the perfect spot for a casual breakfast, laid-back brunch, or a mid-day pick-me-up. Whether you're craving fluffy pancakes, hearty fajitas, or vegan, SMBC delivers feel-good food with an upbeat energy that fits right into the city.
