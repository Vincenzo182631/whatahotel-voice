# Rooms, Suites and Villas

Check-In3:00 PM

Checkout12:00 PM

Indulge in luxury at our elegant accommodations in the West Bay Lagoon area, featuring 249 rooms and suites sized from 40 to 205 sqm, plus 91 spacious villas. Each room offers a spacious balcony, chromecast on all our LED TVs for seamless streaming, and a spa-like bathroom with an oversized tub and walk-in shower. Enjoy plush bathrobes, 24-hour room service, high-speed internet access, and an in-room safe for a truly relaxing and secure stay.
