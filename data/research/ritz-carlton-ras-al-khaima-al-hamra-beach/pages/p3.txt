Dining Inspired by the Sea
Dining
Experience exceptional coastal dining at The Ritz‑Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach, where every meal is shaped by fresh flavors and breathtaking views. Enjoy locally sourced seafood and grilled specialties at Shore House, offering a relaxed indoor‑outdoor ambiance overlooking the Arabian Gulf. For a more intimate setting, indulge in bespoke dining under the stars or in the privacy of your villa. Wherever you choose to dine, our culinary experiences elevate each moment, creating unforgettable meals by the sea.
