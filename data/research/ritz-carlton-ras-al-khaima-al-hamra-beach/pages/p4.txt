A Ras Al Khaimah Spa Inspired by the Arabian Gulf
Spa
Balance is easily restored at The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Al Hamra Beach, where an uninterrupted sea view and the soothing sounds of the Arabian Gulf inspire a sense of peace upon arrival. Private, waterside pavilions are the setting for locally sea-inspired treatments prepared with flowers, native spices and other natural ingredients and facials harnessing the strength of products from luxury brands ESPA and Natura Bissé. Guests can enjoy an access to the hotel's well-equipped fitness center, open 24 hours a day.
The Ritz Carlton Spa, Al Hamra Beach
Experience ultimate relaxation at our seaside sanctuary with stunning ocean views. Enjoy locally inspired treatments crafted with native flowers, spices, and natural ingredients in cozy spa pavilions, making it a premier spa in Ras Al Khaimah.

Arrival & Health Questions
Reservation
Advanced booking before your arrival is recommended to secure your preferred date and time of treatment. A credit card number is required at the time of booking.
When does the spa open?
The Spa opens from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. and the last treatment finishes at 8:00 p.m.
When should I arrive?
Please check in at the spa reception at least 15 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment to avoid reduced treatment times.
Is it safe to go on a spa break if I am pregnant or have a special medical condition?
Guests who have high blood pressure, heart conditions, are pregnant or have any other medical complications are advised to consult their doctors before signing up for any spa services. Kindly inform your spa therapist of any existing medical conditions.
Spa Policies
Can I use a mobile phone or other electronic devices in the Spa?
To ensure that guests can enjoy the peaceful sanctuary of Al Hamra Spa, we respectfully request that all visitors keep noise to a minimum. Cellular phones and electronic devices are discouraged.
Can I smoke and consume alcohol at the spa?
Smoking and consumption of alcohol within the Spa is prohibited.
What happens if I need to cancel my spa booking?
A 24-hour cancellation notice is required to help us reschedule your appointment, subject to space availability. Any cancellation with less than 4 hours' notice will incur a 50% cancellation fee. Full charges will be imposed for a "no-show".
Can I ask for a refund?
Treatment packages and spa pre-payment are non-refundable, non-transferable or non-exchangeable.
How can I pay for my treatment?
We accept cash and all major credit cards. All prices are quoted in UAE Dirhams and include a 10% service charge and 5% Value Added Tax (VAT). Prices are subject to change without prior notice.
When does the spa open?
The Spa opens daily from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.
Is it safe to go on a spa break if I am pregnant or have a special medical condition?
Guests who have high blood pressure, heart conditions, are pregnant, or have any other medical complications are advised to consult their doctors before signing up for any spa services. Kindly inform your spa therapist of any existing medical conditions.
Frequently Asked Questions
Does The Ritz Carlton Spa, Al Hamra Beach require an appointment?
An appointment is required at The Ritz Carlton Spa, Al Hamra Beach.
Can you book reservations for The Ritz Carlton Spa, Al Hamra Beach online?
Online reservations are not available for The Ritz Carlton Spa, Al Hamra Beach.
