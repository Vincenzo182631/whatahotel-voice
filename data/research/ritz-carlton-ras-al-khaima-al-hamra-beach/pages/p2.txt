Beachfront Villas Near Dubai
Suites and Rooms
Nestled along the shores of the Arabian Gulf, our beach villas in Ras Al Khaimah offer a secluded escape where contemporary luxury meets natural beauty. Each villa is thoughtfully designed with spacious living areas, refined interiors, and bespoke touches that reflect the tranquility of the coastal landscape. Featuring private pools, expansive sun decks, and direct beach access, these villas invite moments of quiet indulgence, from sun-soaked afternoons to al fresco dining under the stars. For those seeking villas with private pool within Dubai’s reach, our coastal sanctuary presents the perfect balance of intimacy, elegance, and effortless connection to the city.
Luxury Villa Experience
Al Shamal Ocean View Villa
Find every comfort and more met in this luxurious ocean-view villa. Relax on the sundeck with ocean views and a private pool or retreat to the garden with a personal cabana.
