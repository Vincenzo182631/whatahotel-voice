Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach
Stay at one of the top Ras Al Khaimah luxury hotels
Nestled along pristine shores, The Ritz‑Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach blends coastal serenity with refined luxury, offering an intimate escape in 32 private pool villas with direct beach access. Privacy and tranquility define the experience, complemented by serene spa pavilions, bespoke activities, and elevated beachfront dining. Whether unwinding in a tented beach pool villa or exploring exclusive resort packages, guests discover a secluded oasis where sophisticated comfort meets the natural beauty of Ras Al Khaimah.
Advisory - Travel to Middle East
We continue to closely monitor the current regional situation in the Middle East as it evolves. Our hotel remains open and operational and our teams are focused on supporting our guests at this time.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach is Sharjah International Airport (SHJ). SHJ is located approximately 50.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
