Uncover the Wonders of Ras Al Khaimah
Experiences
Set along a tranquil stretch of coastline, The Ritz‑Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach invites guests to enjoy a curated selection of coastal activities. Glide across Ras Al Khaimah’s shimmering waters with paddle boarding or kayaking, or play 18 holes of PGA‑certified beachfront golf at the nearby Al Hamra Golf Club. Guests may also visit our sister resort in Al Wadi Desert to explore the nature reserve, meet falcons and owls, or try their hand at traditional archery.
The Ritz Carlton Spa, Al Hamra Beach
Experience ultimate relaxation at our seaside sanctuary with stunning ocean views. Enjoy locally inspired treatments crafted with native flowers, spices, and natural ingredients in cozy spa pavilions, making it a premier spa in Ras Al Khaimah.

Where Ocean Adventures and Serenity Meet
Experiences
Set along a pristine shoreline, The Ritz‑Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach invites guests to explore a curated collection of coastal experiences. Enjoy paddle boarding or kayaking across the calm Arabian Gulf, or unwind with beachfront yoga, sound‑healing sessions, and tranquil wellness rituals by the sea. Families can delight in engaging activities crafted for young explorers, while nearby the Al Hamra Golf Club offers 18 holes of PGA‑certified beachfront play, creating a balanced blend of leisure, play, and coastal serenity.

Adventures on Land and Sea
The Ritz-Carlton, Al Hamra Beach offers a curated collection of activities for both adults and young explorers. Guests can glide across calm waters by kayak or paddleboard, join beachside yoga or sound‑healing sessions, or try their hand at archery along the coast. Little ones can enjoy engaging, nature‑inspired activities crafted especially for them, creating meaningful moments for the entire family.

Experiences
Archery - 45 Mins
Archery has long been a part of Bedouin life, and guests are invited to try their hand at the sport. Day and evening classes are held on the private white sand beach and are available for adults and children ages seven and older.
Stand Up Paddle Boarding (SUP) - 30 or 60 Min
First practiced in Hawaii, Stand-Up Paddle boarding has grown to become a most popular water-sport across the globe, and more so, in the UAE. Paddle boarding is practiced as a hobby with the sport proving to be good for fitness and peace of mind for people of all age groups.
Kayaking - 30 or 60 Mins
Grab a kayak and go exploring. Kayaking is great exercise and gives you a low-to-the-water view that really immerses you in the experience. You will feel in harmony, kayaking in the Arabian ocean.
Ritz Kids
The Ritz-Carlton, Al Hamra Beach offers an inspiring collection of activities designed especially for our youngest guests. From shelter‑making and nature‑inspired crafts to treasure hunts along the shore, each experience encourages creativity, discovery, and adventure. Children can enjoy daily games, arts sessions, and movement activities such as kids’ fitness or Zumba, while guided programs immerse them in playful learning shaped by the coastal landscape.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach have a pool on-site?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:
Shore House Main Pool (Outdoor)
In Villa Private Pool (Outdoor)
Does The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach have an on-site spa?
Yes, The Ritz Carlton Spa, Al Hamra Beach is located on-site at The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach.
What are some local attractions and things to do near The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach?
Ferrari World, Burj Al Arab, RAK Museum, Al Marjan Island, Bedouin Oasis and more are nearby The Ritz-Carlton Ras Al Khaimah, Al Hamra Beach.
