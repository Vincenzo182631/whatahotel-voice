A Wellness Spa Inspired by the Earth’s Energy
Spa
The Ritz-Carlton Spa at The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands is a ring-shaped sanctuary nestled over the turquoise lagoon. Our luxury spa in the Maldives offers nine spa treatment rooms, including a spa suite with its relaxation lounge, a salon, and a boutique. Cardinal directions of each treatment room exude powerful energy complemented with healing massages and an organic body and skincare collection by Bamford, offering guests a luxurious spa experience for their visit to the Maldives.​
Deep Blue Prescription
Encounters with wellness experts, visiting heroes, therapeutic landscapes and daily rituals, as part of the resort’s brand new ‘Blue Rate’, celebrate the science behind water-associated peace.

Contact Us
Reach out to The Ritz-Carlton Spa team effortlessly. For inquiries or appointments, please contact us. Our team is dedicated to ensuring your spa experience is seamless and serene.
Your Spa Oasis in Maldives
Bamford Signature Journey
Exclusively designed for The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands, this healing journey combines Japanese Shiatsu and Swedish motions to release tension and balance meridians, followed by wave-like massages to the ocean’s rhythm. 150 minutes.
Bamboo Massage
This massage penetrates deep into the tissue by using heated bamboo sticks, and kneading the muscles to stimulate blood circulation and enhance flexibility. 90 minutes.
Maldivian Signature Rejuvenation
Feel the Maldives in this luxurious island-inspired treatment that incorporates organic coconuts and pearly-white sand on a full-body massage to nourish, relax and rejuvenate. 180 minutes.
The Ritz-Carlton Spa Salon
Experience opulent hair treatments, hairstyling, and men's trimming at The Ritz-Carlton Spa. Our skilled stylists craft perfection in every detail. Pamper yourself with impeccable nail services, including gel manicures, leaving you impeccably polished. Discover a realm of refinement and rejuvenation. Book your indulgence today.
Discover & Collect
A treasure trove of Bamford skincare, room fragrances, and exquisite candles. Elevate your senses with these indulgent offerings. Unearth specially curated The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands mementos like stylish tumblers, elegant notebooks, and chic t-shirts, perfect as cherished souvenirs. Immerse yourself in a world of luxury and take a piece of Maldives experience home.
Spa Special Deal: Bamford Wellness Facial
Discover the Bamford Signature Experience and feel the waves of harmony flow through your mind, body, and soul in our ring-shaped sanctuary.​ Targeting skin health from the outside in, the Bamford Wellness Facial combines two massage techniques including Gua Sha to ease muscle tension, support lymphatic flow, and reduce puffiness in the face, neck, and décolleté.​
Swimming Pool
The swimming pool at our resort is nothing short of luxurious. Its round shape and crystal-clear waters create the perfect ambiance for relaxation and rejuvenation. The pool also offers stunning views of the Indian Ocean that will leave you breathless. Next to the pool, EAU Bar serves tasty food and drinks for you to enjoy while lounging poolside. Whether you're soaking up the sun, admiring the view, or just relaxing with a book and a drink, we have all you need for an unforgettable stay.
Fitness Centre and Yoga Studio
At our Fitness Centre, we proudly showcase a trio of standout classes: Engage in a heart-pounding HIIT Workout, unleash your power with Fit Boxing, and enhance flexibility through static stretching.
