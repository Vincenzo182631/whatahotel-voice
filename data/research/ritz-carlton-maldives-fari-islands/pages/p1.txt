Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands
Embrace The Circle of Island Life
From Sangu conch shell blown on arrival, evoking the ancient Maldives, legendary Ritz-Carlton service by an island butler, to the celebration of sunsets with an elegant performance of Bodu Beru drums that honors the oldest surviving tradition of the Maldives, followed by a flaming ceremony marking the transition from day to evening, The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands invites you to embrace island life. This private island resort features 7 restaurants and bars between the dedicated Culinary Island, Grand Sunset Beach, and Fari Marina Village, offering curated experiences for all tastes. From your Maldives luxury resort accommodation, wake up to the aromas of freshly brewed coffee in La Locanda, where Southern Italian cuisine and customs unfold; savor indulgent mezze platters and succulent kebabs at Arabesque; embrace the Mediterranean at the family-friendly Beach Shack; or appreciate Oriental flavors at our contemporary Cantonese restaurant, Summer Pavilion.
Exclusive Rewards
Embrace island life with half-board dining, 24-hour Aris Meeha, your dedicated island butler service and return luxury shared transfers included. Book by October 31, 2026.
Luxury Island Escape
Suites & Villas
Set above azure waters or nestled along beach coves, the villas at The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands, blend island living with modern design. Blurring the line between indoors and out, each villa at our luxury Maldives resort offers water views, an infinity pool, a sundeck, and the attention of an Aris Meeha personal butler.​

Sustainable Living and Learning
Designing The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands, was driven by a deep commitment to sustainability, with careful consideration of the local ecosystem. From eco-conscious building practices to our dedication to preserving the natural beauty of land and sea, sustainability is woven into every aspect of our Maldives design resort. Guests can embrace this through our Land and Sea Experience, we continue to inspire environmental stewardship and conservation.
Learn More
An Inspiring Journey
At The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands, sustainability is integral to our resort experience. From eco-conscious beginnings of the resort to coral restoration efforts, we are committed to preserving this pristine destination.
Our Land and Sea Experience
The Our Land and Sea Package at The Ritz-Carlton, Maldives offers an immersive experience that combines sustainability with the island’s natural beauty. Guests engage in activities like snorkeling, coral restoration, and learning about local traditions and climate change, deepening their connection to the environment and the Maldives’ conservation efforts.
Visiting Hero Program
In addition to our Naturalists who are heading the resort’s sustainability initiatives, our Visiting Hero programme invites scientists, marine biologists, conservationists, and organizations to the resort to create meaningful guest activities, engage in knowledge-sharing with our Ladies and Gentlemen, and connect with the local community through educational initiatives.

Embark on a mesmerizing 360° virtual journey through a captured moment static tour or a captivating video tour and immerse yourself in The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands. Explore the island’s pristine natural wonders, the unique design of the private villas, the iconic infinity pool, renowned restaurants, the award-winning The Ritz-Carlton Spa, and more. Immerse yourself in the circle of island life and plan your dream getaway in the Maldives at our luxury island resort on North Male Atoll.

La Locanda
Begin your day with artisan Italian coffee and a world inspired breakfast. As the day unfolds, enjoy Italian inspired cocktails, then dine overwater or alfresco on authentic Italian cuisine, from homemade pasta to signature dishes, paired with fine wines.

Summer Pavilion
Set in a lantern-inspired space at our North Male Atoll hotel, this restaurant celebrates modern Cantonese cuisine. Signature dishes such as stir-fried Maldivian lobster and Peking duck are paired with Chinese teas, fine wines, and a Chef’s tasting menu.
Explore
IWAU
Meaning “to celebrate” in Japanese, IWAU delivers a theatrical dining experience with interactive service. Two lively Chef’s Tables showcase locally sourced seafood and premium meats, complemented by a curated selection of Japanese sake and rare whiskies.
Explore
EAU Bar
This vibrant venue flows from day to night around the iconic round infinity pool. By day, enjoy light bites, cocktails, and Japanese inspired lunches. By evening, it transforms into refined dining with inventive dishes, signature cocktails, and DJ sets.

Beach Shack
Enjoy a barefoot dining experience just steps from the Indian Ocean. Beach Shack serves vibrant Mediterranean cuisine with bright flavors, beachside setting, complemented by special evenings and beverages inspired by iconic coastal destinations.

Arabesque

The Ritz-Carlton Spa
A Wellness Spa Inspired by the Earth’s Energy
A ring-shaped sanctuary nestled over the turquoise lagoon, The Ritz-Carlton Spa at The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands, offers nine spa treatment rooms, including a spa suite with its relaxation lounge, a salon, and a boutique. Cardinal directions of each treatment room exude powerful energy complemented with healing massages and an organic body and skincare collection by Bamford, offering guests a luxurious wellness experience for their visit to our Maldives private island resort.​

Maldives Luxury Family Resort
Explore, Play, Learn, Create, Enjoy
At The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands, families, and young guests are invited to engage with the destination through Ritz Kids. Designed for those ages 4 to 12, the program uses its four pillars—land, water, environmental responsibility, and culture—to create a schedule of daily activities, including sports, arts and crafts, cooking classes, and more. The experience is based out of the Ritz Kids Club, a futuristic indoor-outdoor space with playgrounds, napping pods, an area for video games and movies, and a pool with slides and jets.​ Babysitting service is available at a charge upon request.​

Green Initiatives
Sustainable gastronomy efforts at our Maldives Island luxury resort include fresh produce from the garden, extensive plant-based options, and a zero-waste approach.
Solar Energy
Our eco-friendly Maldives island resort partly operates on solar energy, which is continuously expanding.
Recycling
Reusable glass water bottles, bamboo-based personal consumables, and bamboo and stainless-steel straws are part of our commitment to offering guests a plastic-free stay.​
Visiting Hero Program
Alongside our Naturalists leading sustainability efforts, the Visiting Hero programme brings scientists, conservationists, and organisations to create guest activities, share knowledge with our team, and engage the local community—encouraging guests to explore and connect with the Maldives’ unique ecosystem.
Renewable Wood
The timber used to build the resort comes from sustainable forests in Europe.​
Plastic Free
Villa amenities are replenished in reusable bottles at the onsite Bamford liquid reﬁlling system.​
Water Sustainability
The island's still and sparkling drinking water comes from the resort’s dedicated plant, with purifying patented water ﬁlters.​
Frequently Asked Questions
Who is required to pay the Green Tax?
The Green Tax must be paid by all tourists staying in tourist accommodations such as resorts, hotels, guesthouses, and liveaboards.
Luxurious Roundtrip Journey
Our on-demand roundtrip speedboat transfers operate 24 hours. The transfer fee is USD 1,027 net per adult & USD 514 net per child. Fees include 10% service charge and 17% government tax.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands?

The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands is Velana International Airport (MLE). MLE is located approximately 45.0 KM from the hotel.
