Dining

Explore the Maldives in the Water and On Land
The desire to disconnect may be what brings you to the Maldives, but as guests discover, plugging in—to nature, to family, to self—can be more rewarding. At The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands, activities are designed to explore the breathtaking beaches, both above and below the water’s surface; bring families together; spark curiosity in young minds; create friendly competition and challenge the notion that getting away from it all means sitting still. ​
Maldives Private Beach Dining
Dining by Design
Choose from four breathtaking venues: the enchanting Mystique Garden, the golden shores of Grand Sunset Beach, the secluded charm of Beach Cove, or a private dining experience in North Male Atoll at Sandback, surrounded by the ocean. Elevate the experience with a Cinema Under the Stars, blending fine dining with the magic of Maldivian nights.
