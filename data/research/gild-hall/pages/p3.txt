# Restaurants & Bars

Indulge in one of our stylish onsite restaurants and wine bar as you savor an Italian-inspired neighborhood experience in the Financial District like a NYC local. Dine on seasonal handmade Tuscan fare in a rustic yet sophisticated atmosphere at Felice Ristorante. Plush banquettes and cozy settings invite intimate gatherings and welcome private events. The casual bar at La Soffitta calls for cocktails after a day on Wall Street or exploring Lower Manhattan.

## Felice Ristorante

A favorite Financial District spot amongst local clientele, Felice invites wine lovers and serious foodies to settle into its custom-made tables and leather bench seats to enjoy Italian classics inspired by rustic Tuscan fare. The restaurant is also available for large groups and private events.

## La Soffitta

Head upstairs to the 35-seat La Soffitta wine bar, which offers a cozy setting amongst the hustle and bustle of the Financial District for cocktail hour, relaxed date nights and private events. An expertly curated collection of more than 100 Italian wines encased in custom shelves gives the bar its nightly glow and adds to the restaurant's ambiance. Grab an intimate table for two or a perch at the six-seat bar and share a toast over wine, classic cocktails and Felice's soulful Italian cuisine.
