# Hotel With Parking \| Gild Hall, A Thompson Hotel, by Hyatt

# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Valet parking is available at our Lower Manhattan hotel. Guests can drop their vehicle off at the lobby entrance, which is located at 22 Platt Street 7AM – 11PM. This does not include in-and-out privileges and the garage and cannot accommodate large vehicles or pickup trucks.
