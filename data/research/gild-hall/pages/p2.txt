Discover a mid-century-inspired palette in each of our 130 rooms and suites. The accommodations in our boutique hotel located in the heart of Lower Manhattan’s Financial District feature custom leather headboards designed by Jim Walrod and 400-thread count Sferra linens, wrapping you in style and comfort. For an elevated experience, our Penthouse Suite features 850 square feet of living and dining space with a separate lounge area and clawfoot soaking tub in the bathroom — a luxury New York City escape like no other.
