# Boutique Hotel in Lower Manhattan \| Gild Hall, A Thompson Hotel, by Hyatt

## EXPLORE FIDI FROM OUR BOUTIQUE HOTEL IN LOWER MANHATTAN

Welcome to a Lower Manhattan original. Steps from Wall Street, One World Trade Center and the Seaport, Gild Hall, a Thompson Hotel is an elegant New York City retreat in the bustling Financial District, known as the FiDi. From the stately lobby to the chic guest rooms and suites, each interior in our Lower Manhattan hotel showcases the design aesthetic of mid-century modern expert Jim Walrod and the photography of the iconic Slim Aarons. Take a stroll through The Battery, spend the day at Pier 17 or catch the ferry to Brooklyn, then return to the understated luxury of our boutique hotel for delicious Italian cuisine or craft cocktails at our restaurant or wine bar.

## rooms & suites

Step into chic, luxury accommodations that reflect a midcentury-inspired palette and bespoke details, as imagined by midcentury modern expert designer Jim Walrod. Our boutique hotel’s rooms and suites feature tufted leather headboards, 400-thread-count linens, and marble-floored bathrooms, setting the stage for a memorable and relaxing stay in Lower Manhattan’s FiDi.

### Pets are Welcome

Gild Hall is a pet-friendly hotel and welcomes your dog on your next stay in New York’s Financial District. Guests must provide notice of traveling pets prior to check-in and pay a $75 one-time fee per room, per stay. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
