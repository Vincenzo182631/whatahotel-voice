# The Royal

The Royal is Atlantis Paradise Island’s iconic centerpiece, offering the widest range of accommodations—from renovated rooms to spacious family suites—with stunning views and a central location. Steps from Aquaventure, The Dig, and the Atlantis Casino, guests enjoy effortless access to world-class dining, marine experiences, and all resort amenities, making it the most sought-after stay for family vacations and romantic getaways alike.
