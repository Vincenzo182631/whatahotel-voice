# The New MANDARA SPA

Mandara Spa Atlantis has officially reopened, unveiling a stunning transformation inspired by the serenity and depths of the ocean. Soft coastal hues transition into rich aquamarine and deep ocean blues, creating an immersive journey of relaxation and renewal.

The reimagined spa features luxurious relaxation lounges, wet and thermal circuits with steam, sauna, and cold plunge experiences, along with a full-service beauty salon, men’s grooming and barbering, and new Medi-Spa offerings including advanced aesthetic treatments and IV therapies.
