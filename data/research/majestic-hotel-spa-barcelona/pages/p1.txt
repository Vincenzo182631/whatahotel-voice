# 5 Star Hotel in Barcelona. Grand Luxury

The Majestic, a symbol of hotel excellence in the heart of the Paseo de Gracia

The Majestic Hotel and Spa is an exclusive **5-star hotel in Barcelona, Spain**, located in the heart of the city’s most prestigious district. Just a block away from **Gaudí’s world-famous La Pedrera (Casa Milà)** and the iconic Passeig de Gràcia, this hotel is the perfect gateway to experiencing the authentic essence of Barcelona.

Immerse yourself in the city’s vibrant culture with access to Michelin-starred restaurants, luxury designer boutiques, and stunning examples of Catalan modernist architecture, all within walking distance. Whether visiting for business or leisure, the Majestic Hotel and Spa redefines comfort, elegance, and sophistication, making it the ideal choice for discerning travelers seeking a **luxury stay in Barcelona**.

## Rooftop 'La Dolce Vitae', Restaurant & Bar

A high-end gastronomy with Premium view over Barcelona

La Dolce Vitae is the spectacular terrace located on the roof of the hotel, from where you can see practically the entire city of Barcelona with one glance, along with its most emblematic architectural icons such as the Sagrada Familia, Casa Batlló and the Glòries Tower. It is the ideal place to relax and sample the cuisine menu created by our chef David Romero, accompanied by the best selection of wines, champagnes, and cocktails, to the rhythm of the Majestic Sunset Sessions with our resident DJ and other recognized international DJs during the summer nights. This unique space has become one of the most exclusive and recognized meeting and entertainment spots in the center of Barcelona.

see Book

## Restaurant SOLC

An ode to local products and Mediterranean cuisine

Immediacy, freshness, origin and respect are concepts linked to the hotel's culinary initiative. To this end, the establishment itself has a farm in the Maresme area (a few kilometres from the restaurant and just a few metres from the Mediterranean Sea) where the vegetables harvested daily that diners will find on the menu are grown and which becomes a showcase for celebrating seasonal produce.

The menu is completed by fish brought in daily from the Barcelona fish market and exquisite meats from local producers whose quality is the ultimate symbol of trust.

see Book

## El Bar del Majestic

A classic meeting point in the city center

The Majestic Bar is an intimate and relaxed space, completely open to the city, where you can enjoy anything from breakfast to an aperitif, lunch, a glass of wine or a special cocktail or dinner. It has a varied wine, cava and champagne list and our expert bartenders prepare classic cocktails and other more innovative ones for a fun evening with friends.

Additional incentives to visit this intimate corner are the menu created by the chef David Romero and the pianist who livens up the evenings several days a week.

## The SOLC Gourmet Brunch

Recognized in the "Best Luxury Hotel Brunch" category at the "Beyond Luxury Awards 2023".

SOLC's Gourmet Brunch has quickly become a classic on Sundays in Barcelona. This formula devised by our chef David Romero offers a proposal that follows the "Farm to Table" concept of SOLC Restaurant.

A Brunch that promises an incomparable gastronomic experience of luxury and local products typical of the Mediterranean diet that includes appetizers of all kinds, salads, smoked foods and sweets complemented with a showcooking where the kitchen team prepares proposals on the spot.
