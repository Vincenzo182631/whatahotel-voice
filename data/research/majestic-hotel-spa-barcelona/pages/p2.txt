About us

## Majestic Hotel Management

Majestic Hotel Management is a hotel management company with over 100 years of experience in the tourism industry.

Specialising in Luxury and Lifestyle hotels:

- With its own personality
- Integrated into the culture and aesthetics of their location
- They are part of the community

## Services

Services we provide

Assistance and support in the creation of new hotel projects and/or the replacement of existing hotels.

- Hotel concept and brand creation
- Business plan preparation and financial projections
- Design and support in the construction phase (Capex)
- Staff recruitment and training
- Launch plan and start-up
