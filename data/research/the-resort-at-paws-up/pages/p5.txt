## Frequently Asked Questions

Are homes pet-friendly?

Yes, select Paws Up homes are pet-friendly, allowing you to enjoy your Montana getaway alongside your dog. Please note that cats are not permitted. Advance notice is recommended to ensure the best possible accommodations for you and your pet. For more information, please view ourPaws Up Pet Policy.

How close are homes to activities and dining?

Homes are thoughtfully positioned throughout the property, offering both privacy and convenient access to dining venues and activities. Our team can assist with transportation or help coordinate your daily plans to ensure everything is within easy reach. For a better sense of the layout, guests are encouraged to explore our property maps.

How will I get around the property?

Each Paws Up home includes a Lexus SUV for your personal use, making it easy to explore the resort’s 37,000 acres at your own pace.
