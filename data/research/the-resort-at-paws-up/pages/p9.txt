## Gourmet Dining Under the Big Sky

At Paws Up, every meal is an experience, and every bite is a celebration of Montana’s bounty. As one of the best wine and culinary experiences in Montana, each dish is a tribute to the land, honoring seasonality, sustainability, and craftsmanship. Dine indoors or al fresco, where the crackle of a fire and mountain views enhance every bite, and there’s something to please every palate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is room service available?

Yes, guests staying in Paws Up homes can enjoy in-home dining for a more private, relaxed experience. For Luxury Camping, food and beverages are not permitted inside the tents due to the natural surroundings and local wildlife. Instead, guests enjoy a private, personalized dining experience in their camp’s dedicated dining pavilion, just steps from their accommodations.

Can I customize my dining experience or menu selections?

Our culinary team is happy to accommodate dietary restrictions and preferences whenever possible, ensuring each meal feels both personal and memorable.

What makes Paws Up a great destination for food and wine lovers?

Paws Up pairs a sophisticated culinary program with the character of a Montana ranch. James Beard-nominated chefs lead a variety of dining experiences, from continental cuisine with a Western flair at Pomp and tasting menus at Social Haus to seasonal outdoor dining at Shed and rustic riverside meals at Chuck Wagon. An extensive wine cellar features selections from renowned regions around the world, complemented by a diverse spirits collection and thoughtful wine and cocktail experiences. With indoor and outdoor dining, dedicated camp chefs and dietary accommodations, Paws Up offers food and wine enthusiasts an elevated culinary experience alongside Montana adventure.
