With 37,000 acres of pristine wilderness, over 100 miles of trails, and 10 miles of the legendary Blackfoot River, Paws Up sets the stage for rugged adventure and quiet luxury. Here, you will discover an authentic Western escape on our working cattle ranch, where each day is an invitation to experience something extraordinary.

## 365 Days of Unforgettable Adventures

### Adventures

With more activities than any other ranch resort in Montana, Paws Up invites you to explore the wild at your own pace, your own way. Whether you’re venturing into the backcountry, honing new skills, or stepping out of your comfort zone, our expert guides make sure every experience is rooted in authenticity and connection to the land.

### Spa & Wellness

A sanctuary of renewal, Spa Town features private canvas treatment tents with heated massage tables, open-air meadow views, and a full menu of restorative treatments—from river rock massages and organic facials to IV therapy and even pet massages. Go beyond the spa with outdoor yoga, sound baths, breathwork, and forest bathing for a deeper connection to nature.

### Culinary

Every meal at Paws Up is a celebration of Montana’s bounty—crafted with seasonal, sustainable ingredients and meticulous care. Dine indoors or al fresco, where crackling fires and mountain views elevate every bite. With seven unique options, from fine dining to rustic chuck wagon dinners, every taste is thoughtfully covered.
