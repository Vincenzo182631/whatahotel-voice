## Cast Your Line in Montana’s Pristine Waters

There’s almost nothing in life that rivals the feeling of a hefty trout striking your fly, followed by the ensuing fight as you reel him in. With that in mind, we’d like to welcome you to a slice of Montana fly fishing heaven: Paws Up, located within the “golden triangle” of Western trout fly fishing, which provides ideal access to the Blackfoot and Clearwater rivers. Whether you’re checking off a few more Montana rivers from your wish list or are ready to pioneer into some true trout wilderness, this is the place for you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Will I get wet during the fly-fishing experience?

Ideally, no. Whether you’re enjoying a half-day guided trip by boat or a private lesson from shore, our experienced guides do their best to keep you dry throughout your time on the water.

What should I wear for fly fishing?

We recommend comfortable, weather-appropriate layers and quick-drying clothing. A hat and sunglasses are also encouraged for sun protection. For footwear, sandals with a heel strap or any shoes you’re comfortable getting wet are ideal.

What types of fish might I catch?

Guests may encounter a variety of species, including Westslope Cutthroat Trout, Rainbow Trout, Brown Trout, Rocky Mountain Whitefish, Cutbow Trout, and Northern Pike Minnow—each native to Montana’s pristine waters.

Do I need a fishing license?

Yes, a Montana state fishing license is required. Our team is happy to assist with the process and can arrange your license in advance of your trip using the information provided upon arrival.

Do we keep the fish we catch?

No, Paws Up practices catch-and-release fishing to help preserve the natural ecosystem and maintain healthy fish populations for future generations.

Are we guaranteed to catch a fish?

While we can’t guarantee a catch, we do guarantee plenty of opportunities—along with expert guidance to make the experience both enjoyable and rewarding!
