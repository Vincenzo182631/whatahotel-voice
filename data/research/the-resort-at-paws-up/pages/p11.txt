# Our Partners

## Our Featured Partners

Paws Up Montana is proud to be a preferred partner of DESTINATIONS by Lexus, which allows you to enjoy exclusive vehicle access, the convenience of private transportation, and personalized luxury travel experiences throughout their stay.

## Lexus Vehicle Use

Paws Up Montana invites all guests staying in our private homes and Green O accommodations to enjoy complimentary, on-property use of a luxury LexusSUV during their stay. Get behind the wheel of some of the latest Lexus vehicles and explore the resort’s 37,000 acres of land.

Permitted users must have a valid U.S. driver’s license with current valid U.S. automotive insurance and must be at least 21 years of age. Lexus vehicles are available for on-property usage only.

## Lexus Shuttle Service

All guests receive complimentary Lexus shuttle service to and from Missoula Montana Airport and throughout the property during their stay.

## Lexus Owner Benefits

Already a Lexus owner? Lexus owners can enjoy a $400 experience credit during their stay. Visit Lexus Benefits to learn more about owner benefits and how to claim this special offer.
