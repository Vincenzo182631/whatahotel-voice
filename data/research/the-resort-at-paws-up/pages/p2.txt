## Adventures in Wellness

Discretely nestled on the edge of an expansive meadow, in the shade of fragrant and towering pine trees, sits Spa Town®, a sanctuary of renewal that is truly the heart of the resort’s wellness offerings. Here, guests can enjoy spa treatments such as massages and facials; wellness treatments including yoga and sound baths; and our newly renovated fitness center. This peaceful haven of rejuvenation gives new meaning to the term “getting lost” in the woods.

Wellness in the Wild

## Spa Town

Spa Town features a colony of pristine tents stitched together by a wooden boardwalk that runs alongside a babbling brook. The welcome tents include full baths and showers, while private treatment tents are fashioned with heated massage tables, sinks, and open-air views of the meadow, often spotted with horses. There is both an outdoor yoga deck and a glass-walled indoor yoga studio, and the Huff-and-Puff fitness center is just steps away.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is the spa experience available year-round?

Yes, spa and wellness experiences are available year-round at Paws Up. Guests can enjoy Spa Town, which includes spa services, wellness experiences, and a fully equipped fitness center. During the winter months, the Morris Ranch House is transformed into an intimate spa setting, offering a cozy, seasonal take on relaxation and restoration.

Does Paws Up offer spa and wellness packages?

Yes, curated experiences like the AdrenZen Getaway combine outdoor adventure with restorative wellness for a balanced, immersive stay.

What should I wear to the spa?

Comfortable, casual attire is ideal. Robes, slippers, and all necessary amenities are provided, allowing you to fully unwind from the moment you arrive.
