## An Adults-Only Woodland Hideaway

Tucked among towering pines, Green O is a luxurious, hidden gem for adults 21+ seeking solitude, inspiration, and connection—whether with nature, a partner, or oneself. Its 12 high-design hauses, in four unique styles, blend indoor-outdoor living with forest views. Each accommodation includes floor-to-ceiling windows, an ultramodern eco-kitchenette, private outdoor hot tub, and Lexus SUV to drive on-property. Guests enjoy exclusive access to the award-winning Social Haus restaurant, promising unforgettable eight-course meals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How is The Green O different from the main Paws Up resort?

The Green O offers a more intimate, adults-only experience set apart from the main Paws Up resort. With just a collection of architecturally distinct hauses, it emphasizes privacy, design, and a slower, more refined pace. While guests still have access to thePaws Up property and its wide range of activities, The Green O provides a quieter, more secluded retreat with exclusive dining and a heightened sense of personalization.

Can Green O guests access the main Paws Up resort?

Yes, guests have access to the full Paws Up property, including its expansive 37,000 acres and wide range of activities, while still enjoying the privacy of The Green O retreat.

Is The Green O ideal for couples or special occasions?

Yes, The Green O is thoughtfully designed for couples and milestone moments, offering a private, refined setting that feels both intimate and memorable. Experiences like the Romance Package further elevate your stay, featuring three nights in a Green O Haus, nightly dinners, a private trail ride, a wellness session, and thoughtful touches such as champagne and an in-home dessert.

How many guests can stay at The Green O at one time?

The Green O is intentionally designed for a highly intimate experience, with just 12 private hauses on property. This limited number of accommodations allows for a small number of guests at any given time, ensuring a quiet, secluded atmosphere and a more personalized level of service.Each haus sleeps two guests.
