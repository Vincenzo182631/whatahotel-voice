# Groups at Paws Up

## Where Group Getaways Go Beyond Expectations

At Paws Up, gatherings transcend the ordinary. Whether it’s a birthday celebration, family reunion, milestone moment, or corporate retreat, connection comes naturally. Set against the backdrop of our Montana ranch resort, every occasion is elevated by memorable experiences and moments that bring people closer together.

## Corporate Groups

Paws Up is a one-of-a-kind destination to inspire and reward. Bond over fly-fishing, team build on horseback, unwind with wellness, or connect over elevated dining. Meet in unique venues—indoors or out—and stay in spacious accommodations, where all-inclusive amenities, anticipatory service, and the spirit of the American West create the perfect setting for planning sessions and incentive trips.
