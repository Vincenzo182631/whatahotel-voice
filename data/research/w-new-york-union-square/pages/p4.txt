# EXPLORE THE CITY FROM OUR HOTEL NEAR UNION SQUARE, NYC

## Experiences

Looking for a low-key but only-in-New York morning? Just steps from W New York – Union Square, you’ll find the famed Greenmarket, one of the city’s year-round farmer’s markets. From there, head south for a sunlit stroll through the historic East or West Villages. Once twilight hits, the energy is amplified: our hotel is also near MoMA, NYC, and some of the city’s buzziest restaurants, theaters, nightclubs,...See More and more. Manhattan truly is a playground, and W New York – Union Square is at its heart. When you’re here, you’ll experience the city like never before.See Less

### See It All

While staying at our hotel near Greenwich Village in New York, make sure to visit one of the city’s renowned observation decks. The Empire State Building is nearby, as is The Edge at Hudson Yards. Or, hop on the subway (there are multiple lines just steps from W New York – Union Square) and head to 1 World Trade Center. The views – from all – are unforgettable.

### Get Fit

Tone up your muscles and wake up your mind at our bespoke FIT gym, located on W New York - Union Square's basement level. All overnight guests enjoy complimentary access, 24/7. Sleek corridors rendered in green gloss lead you to this subway-inspired area, with supersized mosaics referencing NYC's famed metro stations.

### Your Workout, Your Way

You'll find state-of-the-art equipment at our hotel, with a Peloton bike room in New York (5 bikes total), the latest Technogym gear for cardio and weights and Hydromassage technology, available for guests 24/7.

### Whatever/ Whenever®

If you prefer a guided custom workout, our Whatever/Whenever® service will hook you up with a personal trainer, tough love optional.

## Whatever You Want, Whenever You Want

Craving specialty pizza from Little Italy, but don’t feel like leaving your room? Looking for tickets to a buzzy new art house movie? Tap the Whatever/Whenever® button on your phone, and our team will do the rest. Whatever you want, whenever you want, as long as it’s legal!

## Union Square to the World

Union Square in and of itself has plenty to offer visitors. A broad array of dining options can be found in close proximity to our hotel near Union Square, along with classic NYC bars and some great shopping, too. The best part of Union Square, though? Kicking back and watching the ebb and flow of the city in real time. It really is unique.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W New York – Union Square have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W New York – Union Square offers a fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, HydroMassage Beds, Peloton Room, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W New York – Union Square?

Union Square Park, Theodore Roosevelt Birthplace National Historic Site, Madison Square Garden, SUMMIT One Vanderbilt, Washington Square Park and more are nearby W New York – Union Square.
