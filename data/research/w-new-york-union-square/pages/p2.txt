# EXPERIENCE OUR UNION SQUARE GUEST ROOMS AND SUITES

## Accommodations

Inspired by the city and reimagined in bold style, W New York – Union Square’s New York City hotel suites and guest rooms are in a league of their own. Each brim with color and thoughtful accents (black and white stripe accents recall NYC’s crosswalks, for example), while layouts optimize an equal balance of work and play. Welcome to your new home on the Square.

### Penthouse I Suite

Our luxury hotel suites in Union Square, NYC blend transitional hues and luxurious fabrics to create an oasis-like atmosphere about Union Square. An eye-catching minibar stands as the suite’s centerpiece, creating ample areas for dining, working, relaxing and recharging. The bedroom area features a meticulously crafted ombré pattern, original artwork and plenty of natural light.

## Guest Rooms

Embedded in the designs of our Union Square, New York hotel suites and rooms are wall sconces that evoke the lighting found at the entry to NYC’s subway stations, chessboard tables for a playful touch, and curved lacquer panels to soften the architecture. Each is an intimate haven amidst the city’s buzz – and each is an homage to the city itself.

Do the rooms at W New York – Union Square have a work space?

No, the rooms at W New York – Union Square do not have a work space.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W New York – Union Square?

No, the rooms at W New York – Union Square do not permit rollaway beds.
