## In-Room Dining

Bold flavors. Luxe comfort. All without leaving your suite. From champagne on call to elevated all-day offerings, in-room dining at W Union Square is room service, reimagined — effortless, indulgent, and always in style.

View Menus

### Brunch at Seahorse

A quiet corner of Union Square turns into a two-hour hideaway: oysters on ice, spritzes that never stop flowing, and a live jazz band setting the tone for the weekend. Bottomless Aperol, Hugo & Amaro spritzes, four oysters, and a brunch entrée of your choice — book the full table and let Saturday take its time. Every Saturday from 12-3PM.

Reserve Now

### Sunday, Above the Square

Ninety minutes up top, with Manhattan laid out on every side. An open bar runs throughout, a DJ keeps the energy building, and the table is served two Wagyu sliders and two lamb chops per person. This is Sunday above the noise, above the square at Guardian at the W. Every Sunday from 12-3PM.

Reserve Now

The Art of Holiday Dining

### The New Year's Eve Table

Close out the year at Seahorse with a four-course dinner and champagne toast on arrival. Seatings are available at 8:00 PM and 10:00 PM, at $185 per person (food only). An à la carte menu is offered from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM.

Reserve Now

### Midnight, Above The Rooftop

Welcome the new year atop Manhattan at Guardian, from 9:00 PM to 1:00 AM on the city's most iconic night. Enjoy a four-hour premium open bar, chef-curated passed hors d'oeuvres, and a live DJ, building to a midnight countdown and champagne toast. Tickets range from $225 to $285 per person.

Reserve Now

### Mistletoe & Mimosas

Toast the season every Saturday at Seahorse with 90 minutes of unlimited spritz, four oysters per person, and your pick of a brunch entree, from brioche French toast to steak and eggs. $85 per person, full table participation required.

Reserve Now

### Sundays Above The City

Spend Sunday at Guardian with Bowery Meat Company, from 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM. Enjoy 90 minutes with full bar access, plus standout plates like New Zealand lamb lollipops and wagyu cheeseburger sliders. $110 per person.

Reserve Now
