# Welcome to W New York – Union Square

## PLAY YOUR ANGLE AT OUR UNION SQUARE, NYC HOTEL

W New York – Union Square stands at one of Manhattan’s most dynamic crossroads. The area is a transit hub, a gathering spot, a market, a stage, and an enclave. It is as multifaceted as any neighborhood in the Big Apple. We channel the spirit of our setting within our walls, too. With dynamic programming, plentiful public spaces, bold design inspired by the Square, and local-favorite cuisine and bar...See More offerings, our luxury hotel in New York's Union Square is a place to choose your adventure, be exactly as you are, and play your angle.See Less

### Historic Building & Union Square Location

## Icons Only

Built-in 1919, the site of our boutique hotel in Union Square, NYC was first commissioned by Germania Life Insurance. Fast forward a century, and we’ve kept some of that old-school grandeur and infused it with hyper-modern, bold, and alluring design. We honor the then, live in the now–and always think about what’s next. Carpet design inspired by the Faig Ahmed artwork “Doubts,” used with permission.

## The City's Best

Our luxury hotel is located in Union Square, NYC in one of the best-situated spots, right at the intersection of Uptown and Downtown. (We’re also not far from the eastern boroughs.) In any direction, you’ll find the very best of art, music, fine dining, shopping and so much more. Ask our W Insider team for tips and recommendations.

W New York – Union Square stands as the second ever W built, worldwide. It’s also housed in a historic structure, built a century ago for a prominent insurance company. The legacy of our Union Square hotel is strong, and from 2024 onward with our renovation complete, we’re looking forward to writing a new chapter.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W New York – Union Square?

The check-in time at W New York – Union Square is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W New York – Union Square allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W New York – Union Square. One pet is allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W New York – Union Square?

On-site valet parking is available at W New York – Union Square. The daily valet fee is $100.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W New York – Union Square have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W New York – Union Square has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W New York – Union Square booking.

What is the closest airport near W New York – Union Square?

The closest airport near W New York – Union Square is LaGuardia Airport (LGA). LGA is located approximately 10.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does W New York – Union Square have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W New York – Union Square does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
