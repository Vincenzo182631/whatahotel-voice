# Epicurean Experiences

Hotel María Cristina, a Luxury Collection Hotel

## Our Culinary Outlets and Events

Located in the center of San Sebastian, one of the most coveted culinary destination worldwide, Hotel María Cristina, a Luxury Collection Hotel features a collection of gastronomic experiences.

Discover the delicious María Cristina breakfast, a delightful and energizing way to start the day, sip a masterfully crafted cocktail at Dry Martini by Javier de Las Muelas San Sebastian, unwind at the elegant The Gallery or be delighted by the tantalizing Asian cuisine at Café Saigón.

More information and bookings:

Phone: +34 943 437 600

Email: mariacristina.concierge@luxurycollection.com

Every Sunday, experience our celebrated Brunch in our Ibaia ballroom or in our outdoor terrace. An extensive selection of sweet and savory dishes to enjoy to the rhythm of elegant live music. The ideal backdrop for a celebration with family and friends at Hotel María Cristina, a Luxury Collection Hotel.
