## Eduardo Chillida Suite

Hotel Maria Cristina in San Sebastián honors Eduardo Chillida, the eminent Basque artist, with a captivating suite. Influenced by his homeland, Chillida's sculptures embody themes of space, nature, and humanity, blending abstract forms with weight and presence. His global acclaim is evident in public installations and museums, showcasing his monumental iron and steel works. The Chillida Suite mirrors the hotel's elegance, adorned with his works and personal images. Guests immerse in Chillida's world through decor, including a bespoke carpet and furnishings featuring his motifs. Reference materials enrich the experience, and guests receive tickets to the Chillida Leku museum for a unique immersion.

## Bette Davis Suite

The Bette Davis Suite, dedicated to the iconic actress, offers a glimpse into her last days in San Sebastian. Despite her frail health, Davis meticulously planned her public appearances here in 1989. Adorned with her iconic photos, the suite features a Belle Époque-inspired living room with modern amenities. The tranquil bedroom boasts river views and a luxurious king-size bed. Amenities include a flat-screen TV, complimentary high-speed internet, and a selection of Davis's films.

## Maria Cristina Royal Terrace Suite

Our Royal Maria Cristina Suite offer unparalleled luxury, the rooftop Royal Terrace Suite boasts expansive terraces with majestic river views, furnished for relaxation. Inside, timeless crown molding and contemporary photographs line the entryway, leading to a grand oval-shaped living room with elegant décor. State-of-the-art amenities include a 55-inch flat-screen TV, work desk, and complimentary high-speed internet. The master bedroom offers ultimate comfort with a king-size Luxury Collection bed and luxurious bath amenities in the oversized marble bathroom.
