Our story

## The place to be for more than 160 years

Raffles Europejski Warsaw was built in 1857 by Enrico Marconi and quickly became the city’s creative and artistic hub, resonating with the glamour of the period. Having been lovingly restored and restyled to reflect the needs of today’s sophisticated travellers, the hotel is once more at the heart of life in Warsaw.

## Frequently asked questions

### What amenities does Raffles Europejski Warsaw offer?

Experience luxurious rooms, a world-class spa, and fine dining options that cater to your every desire.

### How can I explore Warsaw from the hotel?

The hotel's prime location allows easy access to iconic landmarks, museums, and vibrant neighborhoods, perfect for sightseeing.

### What dining options are available at Raffles Europejski?

Indulge in a variety of culinary delights, featuring local flavors and international cuisine crafted by expert chefs.

### Why is Raffles Europejski considered a cultural hub?

With a rich history and artistic events, the hotel has long been a gathering place for creatives and influencers in Warsaw.

### When is the best time to visit Raffles Europejski Warsaw?

Each season offers a unique charm, but spring and fall provide mild weather and vibrant city events, making them ideal for a visit.
