## Discover the Soul of Shahpura Royalty

Perched high above the Rajasthani landscape, Alila Fort Bishangarh is a 236-year-old warrior fort thoughtfully reimagined as a tranquil retreat. Here, the raw stone walls whisper of royal legacies, while carefully restored interiors celebrate the artistry of Shahpura heritage. Tucked between Jaipur and Delhi, the fort invites you to slow down, explore centuries-old traditions and connect deeply to the soul of Northern India.

## Accommodations

Every detail is crafted to provide a serene retreat amidst Rajasthan’s rich heritage.

## Alila Moments

Shaped by land and craft, by culture and care, Alila Moments reveal a different way of seeing. These personalized experiences offered at our Alila properties draw you closer to the world around you, and your place within it. These are our Moments, crafted just for you.

Explore Alila Moments

The Fortress Kitchen: A Rajasthani Cooking Experience

From harvesting honey and vegetables to milking goats and mastering spice blends, this hands-on cooking journey connects you with the land—and the locals who live from it.

**Duration:** 120 minutes

**Recommended Dress:** Resort casual attire is appropriate; guests are encouraged to dress with seasonal temperatures in mind

**Price:** INR 10000++ per couple

**Reserve Now**

**Additional Details:** Please inform us of any food allergies in advance. Chef caps and aprons are provided for this activity. We recommend removing rings, bracelets and watches, as you’ll be selecting fresh ingredients and cooking alongside the Chef.

Forgotten Trails—A Jeep Adventure through Rajasthan’s Outback

Hop into a 4x4 and explore Bishangarh’s sun-bleached landscapes, ancient legends, and candlelit ruins on a journey that feels timeless—and thrilling.

**Duration:** 120 minutes

**Recommended Dress:** Resort casual attire is recommended, with consideration for the season's temperature. For this experience, we suggest wearing sports shoes for added comfort and safety.

**Price:** INR 6000++ per couple including the jeep ride & hi tea \| \| INR 4000++ per couple without hi-tea

**Inquire**

**Additional Details:** Please inform us of any food allergies in advance. This experience involves sitting on low stools; if you have any knee concerns, do let us know—we’re happy to make accommodations.

Back to the Roots—A Tour of Rajasthan’s Artisanal Heritage

Get a glimpse into Rajasthan’s creative soul as you spend an intimate day with the region's best artisans on this artisan trail through Shahpura. Gain a newfound appreciation for ancient crafts—and reconsider your connection to "stuff"—during this enlightening outing.

**Duration:** 60-70 minutes

**Recommended Dress:** Resort casual attire is appropriate; guests are encouraged to dress with seasonal temperatures in mind.

**Price:** INR 6000++ per couple

**Reserve Now**

**Additional Details:** Guests are encouraged to support local craftsmanship through small donations or by purchasing locally made crafts. A bag will be provided should you wish to take home a piece. Please note that pottery activities may result in dirty hands—your facilitator will be happy to offer a fresh towel and hand sanitizer following your session.

Daya ki Rasoi—A Culinary Excursion

Witness the art of authentic Rajasthani cooking in a rural village home. Grind spices, cook over a wood-fired stove and share stories with local villager Daya as she explains her family's traditions and her secret recipes.

**Duration:** 60-70 minutes

**Recommended Dress:** Resort casual attire is appropriate; guests are encouraged to dress with seasonal temperatures in mind.

**Price**: INR 6000++ per couple

**Inquire**

**Additional Details:** Please inform us of any food allergies in advance. Cooking activities may result in dirty hands; please feel free to ask your facilitator for a fresh hand towel and hand sanitizer afterward.

## Discover Alila Fort Bishangarh

Sustainable Tourism

Beyond our environmental responsibilities, Alila Fort Bishangarh strives to respect and preserve the local way of life and traditions in all our activities, while also benefitting the community. The restoration of the ancient fort harnessed traditional Rajasthani construction techniques using local stone, combining it with contemporary architecture to create a uniquely relaxing environment.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

Alila Fort Bishangarh was designed to exist in harmony with the environment and preserve the region’s extraordinary natural beauty. To mitigate any environmental impact from the resort’s operation, we adhere to a comprehensive policy of environmental sustainability centered around minimising energy consumption, emissions and pollution, and we recycle and employ solar energy systems to reduce our carbon footprint.

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

The Alila Experience

Curated for the spirited traveller, Alila Experiences are designed to provide a multifaceted outlook on the traditions, culture and heritage of Rajasthan. Indulge in a range of activities native only to Bishangarh: With morning safaris, midday helicopter adventures, sunset horseback riding and much more, there’s something for just about everyone.

Kachchawa Deck

Kachchawa Deck & Lounge has been a part of the fort from its origin. The room was used by noblemen to share and whisper state secrets to one another. Now it’s quiet exclusive space for intimate gatherings.
