### "Living Like Royalty at Alila Fort Bishangarh" 👑🏰

Had a wonderful and peaceful stay at Alila Fort Bishangarh. The property is a beautifully restored fort that perfectly blends royal heritage with modern luxury. From the moment you arrive, you feel transported into a different era while enjoying all contemporary comforts.

The rooms are exceptionally spacious, elegantly designed, and offer stunning views of the surrounding hills and countryside. Every corner of the fort reflects grandeur, history, and attention to detail. The ambiance is calm and serene, making it an ideal destination for a relaxing staycation away from the city's hustle.

### Unique Property infused with Pure Luxury infused with the character of Rajasthan.

Our stay at Alila was a truly special experience—pure luxury, infused with the warmth and character of Rajasthan. Set within a historic fortress, the resort makes a memorable first impression, from the scenic drive up to the village nestled at its foothills.

The property’s layout, spacious rooms, welcoming staff, beautifully presented food and serene spa spaces all offer thoughtful ways to experience Rajasthani culture and traditions. Every detail feels considered, blending local heritage with modern comfort.

The food and beverage experience was exceptional. The Rajasthani cuisine was a highlight, especially the dishes prepared in traditional clay ovens by local women. The farm-to-table experience was marvellous, and the table settings felt both modern and sustainable.

Alila’s commitment to sustainability is something you can see and experience throughout your stay. With its beautiful pool villas, warm hospitality and sense of place, it’s a property we can’t wait to return to.
