# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self Parking

Park Hyatt Shanghai offers limited parking spaces exclusively for in-house guests, on the hotel’s ground floor driveway. Additionally, 24-hour indoor parking is available for hotel guests or visitors at the Shanghai World Financial Center, located on levels B1 to B3, with charging stations for electric vehicles.
