### Exceptional Hospitality with the Best Views in Park Hyatt Shanghai

We had a wonderful 7-night stay at Park Hyatt Shanghai, and it exceeded our expectations in every way.

What stood out the most was the exceptional hospitality. Every team member made us feel genuinely welcome. A special thank you to William, Flora and Henry from the Concierge for their professionalism and helpful recommendations, Susie at Reception for her warm service, and Meddy, the Sales Director, for ensuring our stay was perfect. We were also delighted with the complimentary room upgrade.

My heartfelt appreciation goes to Ms. Gu from Housekeeping. Every day she beautifully cared for our room and left a handwritten note in Chinese. Her thoughtful handwritten note in Chinese truly warmed my heart. It was such a personal and meaningful gesture that I treasured it and even brought it home as a souvenir. It truly reflects the genuine care and attention to detail that Park Hyatt Shanghai is known for.

The hotel itself is stunning. The contemporary architecture is elegant, and the panoramic views over the Huangpu River, the Oriental Pearl Tower, and Jin Mao Tower are simply breathtaking. Breakfast on the 91st floor was a highlight every morning, with an excellent selection and one of the most spectacular dining views in Shanghai.
Another highlight was the hotel's dining experience. The restaurant, led by its renowned chef Yang, served exceptional cuisine. Every dish was beautifully presented and absolutely delicious. Combined with the spectacular skyline views, dining at Park Hyatt Shanghai was truly a memorable experience.

As a travel advisor, I stay at many luxury hotels around the world, and Park Hyatt consistently delivers the exceptional service and refined luxury the brand is renowned for. We thoroughly enjoyed our stay and will confidently recommend Park Hyatt Shanghai to our clients.

Thank you to the entire team for making our visit so memorable. We look forward to returning on our next trip to Shanghai.
