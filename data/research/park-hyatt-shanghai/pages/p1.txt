# 5 Star Hotels in Shanghai , China丨Park Hyatt Shanghai丨Hyatt

## A Modern Chinese Residence in the Heart of Lujiazui, Pudong

Spanning the 79th to the 93rd floors of the Shanghai World Financial Centre (SWFC)—one of the world's tallest buildings—Park Hyatt Shanghai is a sophisticated modern-Chinese residence combining true sophistication with understated luxury. Featuring award-winning restaurants and unparalleled event spaces, Park Hyatt Shanghai offers a refined home-away-from-home experience.

## Discover Park Hyatt Shanghai

Fitness Center

Located on level 85, our Fitness Center is equipped with the latest gym equipment, providing an ultimate workout setting during your stay.

Spa

Inspired by Chinese traditions, Water’s Edge Spa provides a selection of treatments that focus on the concept of health, wellbeing and harmonious balance.

Open 10 a.m.–11 p.m.

Design & Philosophy

Designed by award-winning New York-based designer Tony Chi, Park Hyatt Shanghai was conceived as a sophisticated modern-Chinese residence. With respect for traditional geometry and architecture, the hotel offers all the comforts of a stately home.

To preserve the quality of the stay and maintain the level of service and attention-to-detail, the number of guest rooms has been limited to 173. Every room features views of either the Huangpu River or the Shanghai cityscape. Non-suite rooms measure 55 to 80 sqm and, coupled with the generous 3.1-meter ceiling height, feel particularly spacious.

Park Salon Privilege

Perched on Level 86, Park Salon is a perfect space for business engagements and leisurely staycations. Open exclusively for in-house guests, this intimate space features all-day champagne and premium wines free-flow, healthy light bites stations and a curated selection of fusion cuisines.

**All Day Free-flow:** 11:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m.

**Light Snack:** 11:30 a.m.–2:00 p.m.

**Afternoon Break:** 2:30 p.m.–5:00 p.m.

**Social Hour:** 5:30 p.m.–7:30 p.m.

Book Suites under eligible rates and guestrooms under the "Park Salon Privilege" rate to elevate your stay now.

Infinity Pool
