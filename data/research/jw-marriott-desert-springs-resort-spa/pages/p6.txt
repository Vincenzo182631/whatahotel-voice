Palm Desert, California

### Welcome to JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa

This Palm Springs family resort has enjoyed over 38 years as a desert icon, thanks to 450 acres of stunning mountain views, tranquil lakes — home to swans and the famous JW pink flamingos — five outdoor pools, two Ted Robinson-designed championship golf courses, the Tennis Complex, the Spa at Desert Springs, and the JW Entertainment Zone.

## One Resort, 100 Adventures

This extraordinary family-friendly resort in Palm Springs has offerings for every age and interest. With over 350 days of sunshine a year, Desert Springs is truly your oasis in the desert.

###### Dine + Delight

## Rockwood Grill

Savor innovative cuisine made from the freshest local ingredients at the Rockwood Grill. This popular Palm Desert restaurant offers amazing water views from its patio, serving breakfast, lunch and dinner.

## Mikado Japanese Teppanyaki Steakhouse

The ancient tradition of the samurai lives on at Mikado where knife-twirling chefs use fire and steam to create a mouthwatering menu of steak, chicken, and seafood delights as they expertly chop, juggle, and prepare an incredible meal right before your eyes.

###### Dine + Delight

## Aquifer65 & JW Sushi Bar

Stop by Aquifer65 and the JW Sushi Bar nightly for elevated small plates, hand rolled sushi, tempting craft cocktails and signature JW views. Enjoy live entertainment on Friday & Saturday evenings.

Did you know Frank Sinatra's last public performance took place at the Resort? Join us in Aquifer65 at 4:05pm daily for a complimentary tasting of the Sinatra Smash cocktail honoring Frank's Desert Springs legacy.

###### Dine + Delight

## Spa Bistro

The Spa Bistro features salads, smoothies, sandwiches, sushi, as well as tempting offerings of champagne and wine to toast to a beautiful day in Palm Desert. Savor every bite and sip inside or beside the spa pool.
