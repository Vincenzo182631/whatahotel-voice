# Welcome to JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa

## Discover luxury at our Palm Springs resort

Experience the beauty of the desert at JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa. This luxury Palm Springs resort and spa features personalized service, versatile venues, magnificent views and family-friendly amenities. From the moment you arrive on the lush grounds, you will feel a sense of relaxation. Bask in our modern hotel rooms and suites with private balconies and indulgent bathrooms with marble...See More tiles and lush bedding. Take a dip in one of the pools, and test your swing on our championship golf courses or our world-renowned tennis and pickleball courts. Rejuvenate at our luxury Palm Springs resort's day spa, featuring a Wellness Lounge, and satisfy your cravings at one of our signature restaurants. From unforgettable weddings to corporate seminars, our luxury Palm Springs hotel offers more than 234,000 sq. ft. of indoor and outdoor event spaces. The resort is minutes from Acrisure Arena, the Living Desert Zoo & Garden, the Coachella Valley Music & Arts Festival, and the Indian Wells Tennis Garden.See Less

### One Resort - 100 Adventures

### The JW Garden

Inspired by Alice Marriott’s love of gardening, the JW Garden is a meditative, aromatic garden that features herbs that are staples of the hotel's culinary program. Featuring California-native plants and edible herbs like the signature sage plant, Desert Springs invites guests to pause, rest and reflect as they explore the garden.

Designed by course architect Ted Robinson, Desert Springs Golf Club features two championship golf courses - the Palm and the Valley - that welcome players of all levels. Test your skills on the challenging fairways while taking in the beauty of our resort's Palm Springs locale.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa, with rates of $18.00 per hour, and $29.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $45.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa is Palm Springs International Airport (PSP). PSP is located approximately 12.2 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
