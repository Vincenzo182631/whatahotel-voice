# Relax and rejuvenate at our Palm Springs spa resort

## Spa Desert Springs

Enter a sanctuary of wellness at our luxury Palm Springs spa. Spanning 38,000 square feet with 47 private treatment rooms, Spa Desert Springs features state‑of‑the‑art facilities and thoughtful amenities designed to enhance every experience. Discover our Spa Zone treatments, inspired by Blue Zone lifestyle principles to support longevity and holistic well‑being.

## State of the Art Facilities

Inspired by the stunning physical, historical and social environments of Palm Desert, our luxury Palm Springs spa offers intimacy and privacy throughout with gender-separate and co-ed relaxation lounges, Aromatic Hammam, Finnish Sauna, Turkish Steam Room, outdoor saltwater lap pool and Spa Bistro.

Learn More

### Fitness Center

At 4,000 square-feet, our full-service Fitness Center features an array of state of the art Technogym strength and weight machines, cardiovascular equipment, free weights, and an 800 square-foot yoga and Pilates studio. Regularly scheduled fitness classes and private training are available. Complimentary access for Resort guests and guests receiving spa services.

### Spa Shop

Take your spa journey home with the Spa Shop's unique collection of luxurious lifestyle products, apparel and gift items. The retail shop includes popular brands including Priori, Lululemon, Kerstin Florian, Eminence Organic Skin Care (handmade products), TARA Spa Therapies (Ayurvedic inspired), Hypervolt and Barker/Kardashian Wellness Products. Please call us at 760-341-1880

### Dine & Delight at the Spa Bistro

The Spa Bistro features delicious, healthy cuisine, including salads, smoothies, sandwiches, and sushi, as well as tempting offerings of champagne and wine. Savor every bite and sip inside or beside the spa pool.

## A ‘Suite’ Escape

Elevate your escape with an exclusive spa suite retreat at our luxury couples spa in Palm Springs. Discover the romantic Couples Revive experience, featuring side‑by‑side massages, a mineral‑laden bubble bath, and champagne. For the ultimate indulgence, choose the Sanctuary Suite, offering a private entrance, dedicated treatment rooms, a courtyard whirlpool, and personalized service from a private butler.

Reserve a Couples Revive Experience

## Plan Your Spa Ritual

Please allow yourself time to indulge in a complete spa experience. To improve the benefits of both your treatment and overall well-being, begin your visit to our luxury Palm Springs spa with preparatory hot-and-cold contrast hydrotherapy, for a duration of 30 minutes or less.

##### Hours & Reservations **:**

**Hours**

10:00am - 6:00pm Friday and Saturday

10:00am- 4:00pm Sun, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday

Closed - Tuesday

**Reservations**

Reservations must be canceled 24 hours in advance or will be charged for service.

A credit card is required to guarantee services at the time of booking. Reservations are subject to availability.

We recommend that you arrive at least 30 minutes prior to your appointment to shower and prepare for your treatment. Arriving late will deprive you of precious minutes from your Spa treatment.

**Minimum Age**

Guests 18 and older are welcome in the Spa.

**Service Charge**

A 20% Staff Charge is included in each spa service and is distributed directly to the employee(s) providing the service. Additional gratuities may be offered at your discretion.

**Spa and Fitness Day Passes**

$125 per person per day

Includes: Outdoor Whirlpools, Turkish Steam Room, Finnish Sauna, Aromatic Hammam, Relaxation Lounges, Saltwater Lap Pool, Personal locker with bathrobe and slippers, Body and Beauty Amenities, 4,000 Sq. Ft. Fitness Center with Free Weights, Cardio and Strength Training Equipment, Stationary Bicycles, Cardio Theater, and Daily Fitness Classes.
