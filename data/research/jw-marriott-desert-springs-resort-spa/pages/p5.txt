# Holiday Events at JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa

## Seasonal Wonder Awaits at Our Oasis in the Desert

The holidays invite us to pause—to reconnect, reflect, and savor time with those who matter most. At JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa, we thoughtfully curate seasonal experiences inspired by local traditions and the spirit of togetherness. Let the desert’s quiet beauty set the stage for your perfect holiday escape. Celebrate with us, where every moment is designed to be cherished.

### A Hauntingly Unforgettable Journey

Claim your seat on the ghost ship for this year’s Spooktacular Boat Ride! Set sail, if you dare, on a 25-minute haunted cruise around the JW Marriott Lake filled with eerie sights, mysterious sounds, and family-friendly frights. Enjoy sweet and savory bites included with your journey. Kids 12 and under in costume receive a complimentary spooky sip!

Reserve Your Activities Now

### A Thanksgiving Worth Savoring

Celebrate the season of gratitude with a beautifully curated Thanksgiving Buffet in Rockwood Grill. Indulge in an abundant spread of holiday classics, gourmet selections, and decadent desserts while creating unforgettable moments with those who matter most.

Reserve Now

### Jingle Bell Boat Cruise

Get ready to set sail into a world of twinkly lights and holiday cheer! We are trading Santa’s sleigh for a 15-minute festive boat ride, and you are invited to come aboard. Watch the lakeside sparkle as we glide across the water. When we return to the dock the magic keeps growing as we create an unforgettable moment with Santa and Mrs. Claus.

Reserve Your Activities Now

### The Magic of Christmas, Beautifully Served

Celebrate the magic of the season with an extraordinary Christmas Buffet in Rockwood Grill. From timeless holiday classics to elevated culinary creations, our Christmas Buffet offers a festive dining experience where every detail is crafted to inspire meaningful moments and cherished memories.

Reserve Now

### Holiday Moments Made for Family

Celebrate the season with Family by JW programming at JW Marriott Desert Springs Resort & Spa. Discover curated holiday activities including arts and crafts, interactive games, movie nights, and more—designed to bring families together in the spirit of joy, creativity, and connection.

Reserve Your Activities Now
