### Fisherman's Landing

Fisherman's Landing is open daily for lunch and dinner! Perfectly prepared seafood tops the menu, along with a variety of steaks, chicken and lamb. Buckets of Fried Jumbo Shrimp and Calamari can be ordered along with their famous Fish & Chips, sandwiches, soups, salads and starters. Taco meals and a Raw Bar are also among menu favorites.

### Spa Bistro

The Spa Bistro features delicious, healthy cuisine, including salads, smoothies, sandwiches and sushi, as well as tempting offerings of champagne and wine to toast to a beautiful day in Palm Desert. Savor every bite and sip inside or beside the spa pool. Available to guests 18 years of age and older receiving a treatment or service.

### Starbucks®

Your favorite specialty coffee and tea can be found at our on-site Starbucks®. A great stop for a cup of coffee, sweet pastries, sandwiches or on-the-go snacks before heading out to explore Palm Desert.

## Blue Star Lounge

California cuisine and hand-crafted artisanal cocktails top the menu of our Blue Star Lounge. Regardless of what you order, you will be intrigued not only by the food and drink but by the stunning water views and the inviting hue of fireside dining. Our delectable offerings of starters and flatbreads are perfect for sharing and pairing with your favorite libation. Live music on Friday and Saturday.

## Oasis Poolside Bar & Grille

Casual American cuisine served for lunchtime dining at our Oasis Poolside Bar & Grille. Experience the height of luxury poolside lounging in one of our newly upgraded cabanas or relax poolside in one of our plush daybeds. Cabana, Daybed and VIP Chaise Lounge Chair rentals available.

### Time & Temperature Innovation Kitchen

Dynamic and revolutionary, our T&T Innovation Kitchen, is set behind the scenes of the resort. Our talented culinary team will curate a multi-course menu specific to your event. Hosting the entire affair, our chefs mingle with guests to share and comment on the art and style of each course.​​​ This luxury hotel restaurant experience in Palm Springs is available for private events.
