# Relax and recharge at our accommodations in Palm Desert, CA

## Accommodations

Our luxury Palm Springs hotel rooms and suites have been thoughtfully tailored to provide an intimate retreat within our expansive desert resort – the perfect place to recharge the mind and body. Accommodations feature plush bedding, hardwood floors, modern furnishings, and mountain-inspired murals in hues of blue, gray, and crisp white. ## Vice Presidential Suite

Enjoy the breathtaking views of the Santa Rosa Mountains and surrounding Coachella Valley from our Vice Presidential luxury accommodations in Palm Springs, California, featuring one king bedroom, a generous living area, and thoughtfully appointed with all the amenities you need to make for a truly special stay. Connecting rooms available.

## Griffin Suite

The majority of the Griffin Suites feature a king bedroom and a separate living room, perfectly fit to unwind with family and friends in Palm Desert. There is a slight variation to this layout in a handful of the Griffin Suites.
