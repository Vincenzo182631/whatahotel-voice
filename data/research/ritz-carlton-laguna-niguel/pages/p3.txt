Residential Serenity
Discover our ocean view suites near Laguna Beach
The resort near Monarch Beach features ten meticulously designed two-bedroom suites. To evoke a residential feel, the dining room, living room, bathrooms, and two bedrooms all feature a distinct design motif. Ocean-facing suites feature long stretches of floor-to-ceiling glass doors that open up onto patios framing a timeless California coastal scene.
