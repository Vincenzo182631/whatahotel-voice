Indulge and relax in our Laguna Beach suites
Suites & Rooms
Perched atop a seaside bluff overlooking the pristine shores of Salt Creek Beach, The Ritz-Carlton, Laguna Niguel invites guests to experience coastal luxury reimagined. Our newly redesigned rooms and hotel suites near Laguna Beach blend the soulful charm of Southern California with timeless Spanish elegance, featuring serene palettes, organic textures, and panoramic views of the Pacific Ocean. Elevate your stay with exclusive access to The Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge, where exclusive benefits welcome you to a unique enclave within the resort. Whether you're gazing out at iconic sunsets or indulging in personalized service, every moment at our oceanside resort is designed to inspire and rejuvenate.
Signature Suites
The resort features ten beautifully designed two-bedroom hotel suites near Laguna Beach, each with Club Lounge access, elevated in-room amenities, and sweeping ocean views.

Experience the Exceptional
The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level
Upgrade your stay by reserving a Dana Point hotel room or suite with access to The Ritz-Carlton Club®. Guests of The Ritz-Carlton Club® are invited to enjoy an array of exclusive amenities designed to enhance every moment of their stay. From savory culinary offerings served throughout the day—including breakfast, light snacks, hors d’oeuvres, beverages, and desserts—to a curated selection of California wines, every detail is thoughtfully crafted. A dedicated Club Lounge Concierge is on hand to assist with your travel needs, while complimentary garment pressing and spectacular coastal views of the Pacific Ocean elevate the experience. Whether you're planning a family outing, conducting business, or simply unwinding in a relaxed, well-appointed setting, The Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge is your sanctuary.

Club Packages
Treat You
Extend your vacation on the Southern California coast with one night complimentary when you stay four nights. Enjoy spectacular artwork featured exclusively on The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level with exhibits curated by Dawson Cole Fine Art. Savor oceanfront dining, iconic sunsets over the Pacific Ocean, and luxurious accommodations with the Treat You package.
Rejuvenate You
Discover your perfect spa retreat on the Pacific coast. Relax in The Ritz-Carlton Spa with two signature spa treatments, savor lunch in front of the ocean, and re-energize in the morning with oceanfront Yoga and Pilates classes. Indulge in a vacation of pampering with the Rejuvenate You package.
