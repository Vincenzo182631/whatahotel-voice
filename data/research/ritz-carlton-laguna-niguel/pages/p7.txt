Enjoy our Laguna Beach Resort with Club Lounge
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
Step into a world of refined comfort and contemporary elegance with our newly renovated Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge. Thoughtfully redesigned to blend modern sophistication with inviting warmth, the lounge offers an exquisite setting for relaxation, social gatherings, and memorable experiences. Whether you seek a tranquil retreat or a vibrant space to connect, our hotel with a Club Lounge in Dana Point welcomes you to enjoy its enhanced amenities, stylish interiors, and exceptional service—a destination where every visit feels extraordinary.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services at our hotel with a Club Lounge in Dana Point. *Please Note: Club access requires booking a Club Level room or suite. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
