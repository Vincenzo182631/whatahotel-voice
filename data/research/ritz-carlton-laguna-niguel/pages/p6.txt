Elegant Cliffside Resort Near Laguna Beach
Experiences
The Orange County enclave of Laguna Beach stretches along the Southern California coast and is a haven for artists, surfers, and all those who seek the sunshine and natural beauty. Our resort is positioned on the bluff just above Salt Creek Beach, a favorite surfer location. The resort offers guests a chance to experience the California Soul true to the community with a variety of activities based around the Pacific ocean including whale-watching excursions, surf lessons, award-winning golf, beaches, artist studio tours, cooking classes, and other things to do for adults, kids, and families in Dana Point.

Year-Round Activities for All Ages
Laguna Niguel Experiences
The Ritz-Carlton, Laguna Niguel presents eco-adventures that inspire you with Southern California’s unique ecosystems and showcase Dana Point’s natural wonders. Led by expert Naturalists, embark on stimulating excursions where the local surroundings become a natural classroom and living laboratory. Whether you want to channel the quintessential California surfer or take an exciting E-bike tour of Dana Point, there is something for everyone to enjoy. Adults, families, and children learn through experience, instilling a deep appreciation and respect for nature.

Salt Creek Beach
Hop on the Beach Shuttle and enjoy personalized beach services and a relaxing day at the legendary surf cove, Salt Creek Beach. The Beach Butler services include beach chair and umbrella set up, providing towels, sunscreen, and after-sun lotion.
Surf Experience
Learn how to surf in true Southern California style with an expert surf instructor this summer. Communicated in a way that will make surfing accessible to everyone, beginners will enjoy the thoroughness of the lesson, while experienced surfers will love the personal coaching. The Surf Experience includes transportation to and from the surf spot, a 1.5-hour lesson, all equipment, and more.
Dana Point Harbor
With access to the calm waters of Dana Point Harbor, stand up paddleboarding (SUP) and kayaking are amazing ways to explore the Southern California shoreline. Experience a SUP or kayaking lesson including 2 hours of instruction by a CPR/First Aid certified instructor including all equipment.
Explore by Bike
Zip around on our private custom-guided E-bike Tour. Take off directly from the resort and explore the coastal town’s bike paths and beautiful scenery with the comfort of Electric bicycles assisting with those uphill sections. Join our expert naturalists as they lead you to amazing viewpoints and local hidden gems, while pointing out the local history, flora, and fauna.
Nature Hike
Presenting a variety of local plants and knowledge of the natives who cared for these lands in the past. Whether it be a calming stroll, or an intense physical workout, this is a great activity to learn about our Chaparral ecosystems and the history of the area.
Whale Watching
Embrace your sea legs on a whale watching excursion and see first-hand why Dana Point is the Whale Watching Capital of the World. Enjoy our local dolphin inhabitants frolicking in the Southern California waters.
Yoga
Find your center amidst serene landscapes and tranquil waters at our resort's daily Yoga class. Embrace wellness as our experienced instructor guides you through revitalizing sessions tailored to all levels. Whether you seek to stretch & strengthen the body or soothe the mind, we invite registered resort guests to join us to rejuvenate your body, calm your spirit, and discover inner peace. 9am | 60 minutes
Pilates
Strengthen your spirit with our daily Pilates classes tailored to all levels. Deepen your body-mind connection through movement as our experienced instructor guides you through dynamic and energizing practices. Registered resort guests are invited to join us for a transformative holistic fitness experience while immersed in the breathtaking beauty of Dana Point’s cliffs. 10:15am | 60 minutes
Capture Your Memories
Whether it’s just the two of you, a group of colleagues, or the whole family, everyone can anticipate stunning and candid photos. For more information regarding your complimentary photoshoot at The Ritz-Carlton, Laguna Niguel, email info@atventurephotography.com or call (904) 201-9577.

Plan a Golf Vacation at Our Laguna Beach Resort
The rolling green hills of the golf course contrast with the vast blue of the ocean and the white sands of the beach, beautiful enough to distract any player from the game at hand. While there are a number of top golf courses within 30 miles of our Southern California hotel near Laguna Beach, many guests choose to play at the adjacent Monarch Beach Golf Links. One of the few ocean courses in the area, Monarch Beach offers a Scottish links-style golf course designed by Robert Trent Jones, Jr. that presents players with both a variety of holes and sweeping sea views.

Monarch Beach Golf
Among the few ocean courses in Southern California, it wasd esigned by master architect, Robert Trent Jones, Jr. and features an intriguing layout in the Scottish links tradition.
This course also provides sweeping views of the sea and Par-70 championship course with an unending variety of play.

Talega Golf Club
Located eight miles southeast of The Ritz-Carlton in charming San Clemente and nestled just three miles from the Pacific Ocean this course features distinctive white marble sand bunkers and exceptional guest service. This course was selected "Best Daily Fee Golf Course" by OC Golf, LA Golf and SD Golf Magazines.

Tustin Ranch Golf Club
Located 25 miles north of The Ritz-Carlton, this course was designed by renowned course architect Ted Robinson and offers a lighted driving range and private caddie service. This course has been consistently voted one of the "Best Orange County Golf Courses" by the readers of the Orange County Register and ranked as a 4-Star "Place To Play” by Golf Digest Magazine.

Tijeras Creek Golf Club
Located 20 miles from The Ritz-Carlton in beautiful Rancho Santa Margarita, this course features gently rolling terrain surrounded by rugged hillsides and was designed by renowned course architect Ted Robinson.

Oak Creek Golf Club
Located 30 minutes north of The Ritz-Carlton and set against the majestic backdrop of the Saddleback Mountains, this course was designed by master course architect Tom Fazio.
Featuring tapered fairways, well-manicured greens, beautiful lakes, and bull-nose carved bunkers, this course provides a picturesque challenge for golfers of all skill levels.
