# Welcome to   Jade Mountain

**A Cornucopia of Organic Architecture in Celebration _of One of Nature’s Most Alluring Settings_**

Perched majestically above Anse Chastanet’s 600-acre beachfront estate, Jade Mountain Resort in St. Lucia offers a striking blend of organic architecture and awe-inspiring panoramas. Designed by architect-owner Nick Troubetzkoy, Jade Mountain harmonizes with the lush Caribbean environment, celebrating the breathtaking beauty of St. Lucia.

## Jade Mountain

#### Bold Architectural Vision

Jade Mountain St. Lucia stands as a beacon of innovative design. Each sanctuary is accessed via individual bridges leading to private infinity pool sanctuaries, with rugged stone columns that soar into the sky. These architectural marvels provide unobstructed views of the iconic Pitons and the vast Caribbean Sea—designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

## Cuisine

#### Resort Dining

At the Jade Mountain Club, experience the one-of-a-kind ‘Jade Cuisine’ crafted by James Beard Award-winning Chef Allen Susser and his team. Set against the vibrant hues of a colorful infinity pool, the Club offers an al fresco dining experience like no other. Just above, the Celestial Terrace beckons as the perfect venue for sunset cocktails and stargazing, inviting guests to dine under the vast Caribbean sky. Enhance your culinary journey by booking a cooking class, exploring our chocolate lab, or visiting the organic farm to see where your food is sourced.

## Service

#### Experience Attentive Personalized Service

Our dedicated team of butlers, called major domos and resort staff provide impeccable service, ensuring a seamless experience.

Every detail of your stay is thoughtfully anticipated by our devoted team of major domos, whose sole purpose is to elevate your experience. From arranging personalized excursions to curating in-suite dining under the stars, they are masters of discretion and attentiveness. Alongside our resort staff, they ensure your every need is met with warmth and precision, creating an atmosphere of effortless luxury. Whether it’s a perfectly timed morning coffee or an unforgettable surprise to mark a special occasion, their commitment to service transforms moments into cherished memories.

#### Exclusive Access

## Exclusive

#### Access And Adventure At Anse Chastanet

Guests of Jade Mountain enjoy privileged access to the full suite of amenities at the adjoining sister Anse Chastanet Resort, where they can dine at several beachside and hillside restaurants—including a vegan option—visit bars, boutiques, and an art gallery. The two secluded Caribbean beaches of the resorts offer serene escapes with soft, sandy shores and sparkling blue waters, just a brief stroll or a quick 2-minute shuttle ride away.

Adventure seekers can explore over 12 miles of hiking and mountain biking trails or dive into excitement with our PADI 5 Star scuba operation and snorkeling right off the shore. While Jade Mountain is celebrated for its tranquil sanctuaries and romantic seclusion, Anse Chastanet provides the perfect contrast, offering thrilling activities and beautiful landscapes for a perfectly

balanced getaway.
