# Activities

#### Discover the Wonders of Jade Mountain Resort, St. Lucia

View Island Excursions \| Download Our Tours & Excursions Flyer \| View Resort Activities

**Choose Your Activities & Adventures in St. Lucia**

At Jade Mountain Resort in St. Lucia, there’s an array of activities to indulge in. The Jade Mountain concierge and guest services teams are available at all times to arrange resort-based activities or island excursions for Jade Mountain guests. A handful of carefully selected tour guides are available to share their wealth of information about the history and culture of our beautiful island. Being situated in the southern part of the island, major sightseeing attractions such as the rainforest, Botanical Gardens, Drive-In Volcano, and Sulphur Springs are all within easy reach.

Chocoholics will be thrilled with a visit to the resort’s Chocolate Lab, where chocolate is produced from organically grown cocoa right on the property. You can also participate in chocolate-making classes and tasting sessions. A visit to the resort’s Emerald Estate, where a variety of organic produce is grown, including mango, avocado, guava, and cocoa, is a delightful exploration. Tree-to-Bar Chocolate Tours and Cooking Classes are offered in combination with a farm visit. There is even a microbrewery for beer tasting. Animal lovers may wish to peek into the family-run Helpaws Animal Shelter on the way to the farm.

Underwater enthusiasts can explore the Anse Chastanet Reef for a scuba diving or snorkeling adventure, taking advantage of our award-winning PADI-certified facilities at Scuba St. Lucia. The diverse marine life promises an enchanting experience.

For a more active pursuit, partake in the daily guided walks and hikes offered on a complimentary basis on the resort property or ask the team to arrange for a guided hike up the Gros Piton, one of St. Lucia’s iconic volcanic spires. The view from the top is nothing short of breathtaking.

Relaxation awaits at the beautiful Anse Chastanet and Anse Mamin beaches, accessible on foot (a short walk) or by resort shuttle service. For more unwinding, Jade Mountain’s Kai En Ciel Spa offers an array of treatments, from massages to facials and body wraps, as well as wellness programs that include yoga and fitness classes.

Adventure lovers can tackle the resort’s custom-designed jungle biking trails with mountain bikes available for rental from Bike St. Lucia.

Remember, your experience at Jade Mountain can be tailored to your preferences, with the resort staff ready to help you make the most of your stay.

For a serene ending to the day, enjoy the beauty of St Lucia’s coastline from the water on a sunset cruise.

### Available Activities Include:

- Two soft sand beaches with calm Caribbean waters
- Pristine coral reefs just offshore for snorkeling and scuba diving
- Scuba St. Lucia PADI 5-Star resort scuba center with full diving program
- Bike St. Lucia jungle biking center with 50 top-of-the-line Cannondale mountain bikes
- Coastal kayaking adventures with specialist sit-inside Prijon Kayaks
- Non motorized water sports including paddleboards, hobie cats, sunfish sailing, windsurfing, sit-on-top kayaks
- Tennis
- Day and sunset sailing on the resort yachts and Jazzy Sunset Cruise on our 60-foot catamaran Miss Babsie
- Daily escorted walks and hikes
- Cybex Fitness Studio
- Visits to the resort organic farm Emeralds
- Daily sunrise and sunset yoga (private sessions available at a charge)
- Cooking Classes
- Creole History Class
- Rum Tasting and Mixology Classes
- Twice weekly al fresco art classes with the resident art teacher
