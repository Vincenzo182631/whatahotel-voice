## Beach Club de Hacienda Pinilla

Authentic experiences to enrich your passions. The oceanfront pool at our luxury resort boasts a whirlpool for relaxing or soaking tired muscles, plus lounge chairs and umbrellas for enjoying carefree hours in the shade or basking in the sun on the Costa Rica​ beachfront.

### JW Adventure Kids Club

Discover. Explore. Delight. The JW Adventure Kids Club at our luxury Guanacaste Beach resort​ offers daily supervised activities for children 4-12 years old staying at our family resort.

## Rain Forest

## Mansita Beach

Lovely sunsets every day. Relax with the warm breeze, sound of the waves and breath-taking views of the Pacific in Mansita Beach. Make the most of your Costa Rica vacation in this beautiful setting.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Lagoon Shape outdoor pool with infinity edge (Outdoor)Whirlpool

Does JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa by JW Marriott is located on-site at JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort?

Diamante Eco Adventure Park Papagayo, Rescue Center Las Pumas, Waterfall Llanos del Cortés, National park Marino Las Baulas Tamarindo, Horseback Riding Hacienda Pinilla and more are nearby JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort.
