There is nothing more musical than a sunset. When the sun is setting, we invite you to leave whatever you are doing to come and enjoy a house cocktail accompanied with delicious appetizers. With live music and the sound of the waves crashing on the beach, this will be an evening to be remembered.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for $10.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort is Daniel Oduber Quiros International (LIR). LIR is located approximately 74.8 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Guanacaste Beach Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
