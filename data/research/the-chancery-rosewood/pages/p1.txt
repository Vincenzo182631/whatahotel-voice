# Dining

A culinary destination featuring global debuts and an ever-evolving experience of taste

### LONDON IS SERVED

From bold global flavours to moments of lively conversation

Dining at The Chancery Rosewood is a celebration of London’s diverse and cosmopolitan heritage. From bold global flavours in grand settings to moments of quiet indulgence and lively conversation, our distinctive fine dining restaurants are united by a devotion to detail and a love of ingredients.

##### Private Dining Rooms

Located within Serra and Tobi Masa, our Private Dining Rooms seat up to 12 guests. Floor-to-ceiling windows and generous daylight create a calm, considered setting for private occasions.
