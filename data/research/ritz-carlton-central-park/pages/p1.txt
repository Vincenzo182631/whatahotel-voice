Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park
Experience luxury at our hotel in Central Park
The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park reimagines luxury in Midtown Manhattan, drawing inspiration from the sights, sounds, and colors of Central Park and the timeless elegance of a private city residence. Steps from Manhattan’s finest attractions and celebrated cultural institutions, this 253-room hotel is rated as a Forbes Five-Star and AAA Five Diamond Hotel. Featuring the all-day gastro lounge Contour, The Ritz-Carlton Club® Lounge, and a collection of wellness experiences, including the first stateside La Prairie Spa and innovative Movement Studio, this iconic Manhattan address creates unforgettable moments for every guest.

Broadway

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 60.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $300.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park?
Off-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park. The daily valet fee is $135.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park is LaGuardia Airport (LGA). LGA is located approximately 7.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
