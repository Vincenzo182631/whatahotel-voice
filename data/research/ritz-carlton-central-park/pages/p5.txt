Featured Treatments
Glass Facial
Experience the next level of coveted glass skin with 60 min ritual enriched with powerful illuminating antiaging molecules to reduce dullness and even out skin tone. This treatment includes the benefits of cryotherapy and lymphatic drainage massage techniques, which support your body's natural collagen and elastin production and promotes detoxification and skin repair.
Timeless Beauty
Inspired by La Prairie's heritage and quest for timeless beauty, this rejuvenating treatment combines a relaxing 90-minute custom massage with an age defying 60-minute Caviar Firming Facial that hydrates, lifts and firms.
Body Ritual Renewal
Begin your wellness journey with the restorative warmth of our Infrared Sauna Blanket, complemented by a stimulating scalp massage designed to encourage relaxation and an enhanced sense of well-being. This 45-minute ritual supports full-body circulation from head to toe, leaving you feeling renewed, balanced and deeply restored.
