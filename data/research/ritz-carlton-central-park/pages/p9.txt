Elevate Your Stay
Explore our NYC Club Lounge Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club elevates the guest experience with an exceptional expression of luxury, comfort and personalized service. Often described as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club offers access to a private lounge, a dedicated Concierge, thoughtful in-room amenities and exclusive experiences inspired by the best of New York City. From personalized recommendations to moments of quiet respite throughout the day, every detail is designed to make your stay feel distinctly your own.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.

Club Lounge Access
Experience an elevated level of personalized service at our New York hotel, where a dedicated Club Concierge and refined surroundings create a private retreat throughout your stay. Enjoy a series of complimentary culinary presentations, from breakfast and lunch to evening hors d’oeuvres, accompanied by a curated selection of beer, wine and cocktails. Club guests also enjoy complimentary Wi-Fi and pressing of two garments per stay.
