Discover our holiday hotel in New York, Midtown
Experience the enchantment of the holiday season at our hotel on the Thanksgiving parade route in New York City, The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park.
In the Heart of New York City
Experience the Magic of Thanksgiving
Experience our exclusive Thanksgiving Parade Package at The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park. Overlooking the iconic parade route, our coveted accommodations offer a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to watch the world-famous Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade from the comfort and elegance of your room. Perfect for families, this unforgettable experience blends luxury, tradition, and cherished holiday moments, making it one of the most sought-after Thanksgiving celebrations in the city.

Delight in the Season
The Ultimate Rockefeller Holiday Experience
Experience the magic of the holidays with exclusive VIP access to Rockefeller Center. Skate on The Rink, relax in a private chalet, enjoy sunset views from Top of the Rock, private Beam and SKYLIFT experiences with full photo packages, and a Champagne toast. Cap the evening with a family photograph and once-in-a-lifetime, up-close and personal visit under the Rockefeller Center Christmas Tree.
Thanksgiving Day Parade
Experience our exclusive hotel stay on the Thanksgiving parade route in New York City. Overlooking the iconic parade route, our coveted accommodations offer a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to watch the world-famous Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade from the comfort and elegance of your room.

Home for the Holiday
Delight in a beautifully decorated in-room Christmas tree, complete with a special FAO Schwarz ornament to take home as a keepsake. Gather with loved ones and enjoy quality time with a festive holiday puzzle, creating cherished memories in the comfort of your luxurious room. Make this holiday season unforgettable with a cozy and personalized celebration.
