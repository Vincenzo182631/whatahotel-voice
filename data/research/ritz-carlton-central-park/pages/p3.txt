Discover our two-bedroom suites in New York
The Signature Suites at The Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park blend classic elegance with modern design, drawing inspiration from the timeless character of Upper East Side townhomes. Each suite offers a refined residential sensibility, where contemporary sophistication and understated luxury come together to create a distinctive hotel stay in the heart of Manhattan.
The Royal Suite
This bold and luxurious 2175 sqft suite offers unique handcrafted wood accents, a curated collection of artwork, and playful furnishings. Enjoy panoramic views of Central Park from your spacious living and dining areas.
Host & Entertain
The Royal Suite is the perfect setting for entertaining and quality time with family. With an expansive living room, a formal dining room that seats ten, and a well-appointed kitchenette, the suite offers a spacious, residential-style retreat ideal for intimate dinners, meaningful conversations, and unforgettable moments together.
