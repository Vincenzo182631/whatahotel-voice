About May Fair Kitchen

There are few better addresses in Mayfair for lunch, dinner or Afternoon Tea than Stratton Street. May Fair Kitchen brings together contemporary Japanese izakaya and Italian cuisine, a short walk from Green Park and Berkeley Square.

The Japanese menu draws on izakaya tradition, with sashimi, tempura, robata-grilled dishes, A5 grade Wagyu and a seafood platter. The Italian menu features homemade pasta, grilled specialities and seasonal dishes inspired by regional Italy. Both menus are designed for sharing, with cocktails, sake and fine wine served throughout.

Champagne Afternoon Tea is available Thursday to Sunday, 12:30pm to 4pm, created in collaboration with Charbonnel et Walker, the Bond Street chocolatiers producing truffles and chocolate since 1875. Choose from traditional, children’s, vegan and vegetarian menus, served with fine loose leaf teas from Newby Teas and a glass of Champagne.

Enjoy drinks and the lounge bar menu in the May Fair Kitchen Lounge Bar or the adjoining May Fair Bar.
