## WHAT’S INSIDE

Grand, stately, and romantic, our Executive Suites exude an _air of nobility_.

Crown moldings with elegant relief details frame a living room dripping in delicate details that create an atmosphere of quiet nobility. Our Executive Suites feel regal and refined. The living quarters open onto comfortable bedrooms complete with a stylish and bright en-suite marble bathroom.

A convenient guest bathroom is attached to the sitting room to preserve privacy. From the windows, gaze over the manicured gardens of Villa Borghese and feel the romance in the view.

## SIGNATURE SIGHTS IN THE VICINITY

Relive the _glamor of classic Hollywood_ at Hassler Roma. Become the stars of your very own _Roman Holiday_ in the footsteps of Audrey Hepburn and Gregory Peck, as the hotel is situated at the top of the iconic Spanish Steps featured in the film.

_Pantheon_

Pause in the lively piazza and take in the buzz of people moving to and fro as you gaze upon the structure and marvel at its grandeur.
