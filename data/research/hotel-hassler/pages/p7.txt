## WHAT’S INSIDE

Hassler Roma's penthouse _sprawls out_ over the 8th, and final, floor of the hotel, guaranteeing _breathtaking views_ over Rome's city center. Admire the Spanish Steps, Piazza di Spagna, and the famed Via Condotti, all from the comfort and complete privacy of your suite.

A room with a view: the best view of Rome. While the interiors have been thoughtfully designed, what you will notice first is the view. Large windows frame the Roman skyline. After drinking in the view, notice the grand piano and the stylish and fully stocked bar area. Sink into one of the plush settees in the living room, or wander onto one of the two panoramic terraces, where sunlight glints off the travertine marble. Cushy king-sized beds wait to lull you to sleep in each of the two bedrooms. Indulge in a chromotherapy rain shower or immerse yourself in a luxurious soaking tub, savoring the view in tranquil privacy. Every modern comfort, from high-speed Wi-Fi to a state-of-the-art music and lighting system, ensures your stay is effortlessly relaxing.

You’ll want for nothing, as our private butler service is ready to discreetly attend to your every need.

## SIGNATURE SIGHTS IN THE VICINITY

Perched above the iconic Spanish Steps, Hassler Roma places you in the _heart of the city_, where Rome’s history, elegance, and vibrant energy converge for an unforgettable stay.

PantheonPiazza NavonaSpanish StepsThe ColosseumTrevi FountainVatican Museums & St. Peter’s BasilicaVilla Borghese Gardens

_Pantheon_

Pause in the lively piazza and take in the buzz of people moving to and fro as you gaze upon the structure and marvel at its grandeur. As you step beneath the immense columns marking the threshold of the Pantheon, feel the presence of centuries of Roman citizens in the hush of the shadows. Bring the mysteries of the Pantheon’s architecture and the symbolism of its interior artwork to life with a guided tour curated by the Hassler Roma.
