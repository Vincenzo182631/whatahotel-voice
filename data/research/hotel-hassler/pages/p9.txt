## WHAT’S INSIDE

The _7th-floor Villa Medici Penthouse_ greets you with cool _marble floors_, wooden archways, and a _timeless_ antique-filled sitting area, which gives way to a more _moder_ n dining room and bedroom that maintain the same rich color accents.

Large windows frame the marvelous city views waiting just outside. Step onto the equally spacious terrace and enjoy the panorama of the city over a romantic cocktail or an intimate dinner.

Every detail has been carefully curated to provide a sophisticated atmosphere, perfect for relaxation. Wooden panels curve into gracious archways that separate the airy living quarters into distinct areas. The door to the dining room is flanked by two white classic columns, marking a transitional area from historic elegance to contemporary comforts. A large dining table waits to host you and your loved ones for shared moments.

A sliding door discreetly separates the master bedroom from the rest, engulfing you in quiet comfort and understated elegance. Unpack into a stylish built-in wardrobe with space for everything you have brought for your Roman sojourn. Plush bathrobes hang in the bathroom, decorated with warm beige tiles, a large glass-walled shower, and double sinks, with plenty of space for all your toiletries.

## SIGNATURE SIGHTS IN THE VICINITY

Perched above the iconic Spanish Steps, Hassler Roma places you in the _heart of the city_, where Rome’s history, elegance, and vibrant energy converge for an unforgettable stay.

PantheonPiazza NavonaSpanish StepsThe ColosseumTrevi FountainVatican Museums & St. Peter’s BasilicaVilla Borghese Gardens

_Pantheon_

Pause in the lively piazza and take in the buzz of people moving to and fro as you gaze upon the structure and marvel at its grandeur. As you step beneath the immense columns marking the threshold of the Pantheon, feel the presence of centuries of Roman citizens in the hush of the shadows. Bring the mysteries of the Pantheon’s architecture and the symbolism of its interior artwork to life with a guided tour curated by the Hassler Roma.
