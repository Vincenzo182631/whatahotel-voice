## STAY

Every room and suite at _Hassler Roma_ is individually designed, offering a distinctive blend of _sophistication and warmth_. Enter a _serene hideaway_, where our attentive staff waits to welcome you with _traditional hospitality_.

Located just steps away from the famed Piazza di Spagna and the refined Villa Borghese, the Hassler Roma invites you to sojourn in spacious rooms bathed in natural light, where graceful interiors frame unforgettable vistas across Rome’s storied skyline.

Suites

_The_

Hassler Penthouse

A room with a view: the best view of Rome. While the interiors have been thoughtfully designed, what you will notice first is the view. Large windows frame the Roman skyline. After drinking in the view, notice the grand piano and the stylish and fully stocked bar area. Sink into one of the plush settees in the living room, or wander onto one of the two panoramic terraces, where sunlight glints off the travertine marble. Cushy king-sized beds wait to lull you to sleep in each of the two bedrooms. Indulge in a chromotherapy rain shower or immerse yourself in a luxurious soaking tub, savoring the view in tranquil privacy. Every modern comfort, from high-speed Wi-Fi to a state-of-the-art music and lighting system, ensures your stay is effortlessly relaxing.

Suites

_Villa_

Medici Penthouse

Gain exclusive access to one of the city’s most gorgeous private terraces. The twin domes of the Trinità dei Monti church seem to stand sentinel over you as you gaze upon the city. Sophisticated beige Botticino marble floors glide smoothly under your feet as you enter the spacious living room. Warm wood-paneled archways elegantly divide the space into distinct areas. Stately white columns mark the entrance to the dining area, decorated with white walls and luscious teal furniture precisely matched to the textured wallpaper of the same sumptuous color.

Suites

_San_

Pietro Presidential Suite

Intricate wood paneling graces the ceilings and walls of the living area, drawing you inside. The living area exudes a restrained elegance with antique furnishings, like a fold-out writing desk. Modern comforts like a minibar and a plasma TV seamlessly blend into the room without detracting from the timeless feel. Step out onto the private terrace that runs the length of the suite, and bask in the magnificent view of St. Peter’s Basilica and the Altar of the Fatherland.

Suites

_Trinità_

dei Monti Presidential Suite

Classic black-and-white marble floors give way to soft black and white carpeting in the bedrooms. A curated selection of original paintings and prints graces cream-colored walls accented with crown molding in a room where contemporary style meets classic sophistication. Swarovski crystals set into the dining area ceiling glitter and shine, evoking the romance of dining under a starry sky.

Suites

_Grand_

Deluxe Suite Forum

Dark wood, Doric columns, and cream-colored marble create a feeling of subdued grandeur, accented by magnificent paintings, for a stay in the heart of Rome that feels as regal as the location. Find peace and relaxation while soaking in the jacuzzi tub, nestled into a bathroom lined with marble fixtures and a decorative mosaic floor. Complete last-minute work atop the antique desk before stepping out onto the balcony to gaze upon the city. Receive guests in your sitting room, but maintain your own privacy with a separate guest bathroom.

Deluxe Suite Hassler

Suites

_Executive_

Suite

Crown moldings with elegant relief details frame a living room dripping in delicate details that create an atmosphere of quiet nobility. Our Executive Suites feel regal and refined. The living quarters open onto comfortable bedrooms complete with a stylish and bright en-suite marble bathroom. A convenient guest bathroom is attached to the sitting room to preserve privacy. From the windows, gaze over the manicured gardens of Villa Borghese and feel the romance in the view.

Suites

_Classic_

Suite

Warm polished wooden panels shine in the light from bright windows that look onto gardens or partial city views. Sit at the stately antique desk in the living room to finish some emails, or relax on one of the graceful sofas. Through a framed doorway, your bedroom awaits welcomingly with fresh linens. Both the en-suite bathroom and the guest bathroom are fitted with cool marble furnishings. For those traveling in groups or requiring additional space, the suites can be connected to one of our double rooms for your convenience.

Il Palazzetto Rooms

_Il_

Palazzetto

Meaning “little palace”, the Palazzetto rooms are housed in a historic building standing alongside the Spanish Steps. Three rooms offer direct views over the steps, creating a feeling of intimacy with the monument, while one room faces Vicolo del Bottino. Feel intimately connected to the Spanish Steps but remain swathed in a feeling of privacy in rooms thoughtfully designed for discretion and comfort.
