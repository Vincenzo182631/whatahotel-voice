# Find Italy Hotels

### About Italy

Accommodations range from deluxe international resorts to dumps and include fabulous local inns, tiny pensions, bed-and-breakfasts, campgrounds, ski resorts and excellent small hotels. We recommend staying in properties with as much historic ambience as you can afford. The good ones are expensive, but the locations, setting, architecture and/or service usually make them worth it.
