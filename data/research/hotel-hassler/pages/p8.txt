## WHAT’S INSIDE

Individually _designed rooms_ imbue each stay with its own exclusive feel, _characterized by signature Hassler elegance_.

Cool black and white marble decorates the luxurious-feeling bathrooms, while walk-in closets provide space for you to unpack and unwind. Enter a room where classic decor is balanced with the comforts of modern life.

Choose to gaze out onto our quiet garden courtyard, or revel in the romantic Roman skyline. For added convenience, many of our Grand Deluxe rooms can be connected to suites so that your whole equip can stay together.
