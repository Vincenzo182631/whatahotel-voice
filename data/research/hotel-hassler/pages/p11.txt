## WHAT’S INSIDE

The grandeur of the Spanish Steps feels intimately close from the _Grand Deluxe suite_, located on the first floor of the hotel. Fall in love with the _gentle sophistication of the decor_, chosen to enhance the splendor of the views over the _Eternal City_.

Timeless artwork and beautiful antique furniture grace the living room and bedroom of the suite. The neutral tones of the interior blend with the cityscape for a space that feels bright, airy, and relaxing.

For groups traveling together, the suite can be connected to two adjoining double suites to house your entire party together in discreet comfort. Unwind in understated elegance.

## SIGNATURE SIGHTS IN THE VICINITY

Relive the _glamor of classic Hollywood_ at Hassler Roma. Become the stars of your very own _Roman Holiday_ in the footsteps of Audrey Hepburn and Gregory Peck, as the hotel is situated at the top of the iconic Spanish Steps featured in the film.

_Pantheon_

Pause in the lively piazza and take in the buzz of people moving to and fro as you gaze upon the structure and marvel at its grandeur. As you step beneath the immense columns marking the threshold of the Pantheon, feel the presence of centuries of Roman citizens in the hush of the shadows.
