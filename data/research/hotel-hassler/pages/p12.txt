## WHAT’S INSIDE

Four _exclusive rooms_ for four uniquely _enchanting stays_, in the comforting ambiance of a _private residence._

The grandeur of the Spanish Steps awaits just outside the windows of three of our comfortable rooms, while one room offers charming views over the Vicolo del Bottino. An elegant curved marble staircase leads to rooms filled with natural light and stately furnishings for an air of modern luxury. Selecting these rooms means basking in the welcoming warmth of a private residence while enjoying all the services available to guests at the Hassler Roma. Savor breakfast at Salone Eva or the seasonal Palm Court Garden, indulge in the Amorvero Spa, or relax as you gaze over the city, cocktail in hand, at our panoramic 7th Floor Terrace.

## SIGNATURE SIGHTS IN THE VICINITY

Relive the _glamor of classic Hollywood_ at Hassler Roma. Become the stars of your very own _Roman Holiday_ in the footsteps of Audrey Hepburn and Gregory Peck, as the hotel is situated at the top of the iconic Spanish Steps featured in the film.

_Pantheon_

Pause in the lively piazza and take in the buzz of people moving to and fro as you gaze upon the structure and marvel at its grandeur. As you step beneath the immense columns marking the threshold of the Pantheon, feel the presence of centuries of Roman citizens in the hush of the shadows. Bring the mysteries of the Pantheon’s architecture and the symbolism of its interior artwork to life with a guided tour curated by the Hassler Roma.
