## Overview

**Description**

Set above the Spanish Steps; Classic Hotel with antique furnishings, restaurant offering fine dining & panoramic views of Rome.

**Location and History**

This hotel is a love-it-or-leave-it kind of place. In one of Rome's loveliest locations, it faces the Piazza Trinita dei Monti adjacent to the church, at the top of the Spanish Steps (Italy's most-photographed landmark after the Colosseum).

Contrasting with the larger, more commercial Excelsior and St. Regis, this superb, family-run independent hotel remains the powerhouse of old-world service and style, built and maintained for discerning individuals and couples.
