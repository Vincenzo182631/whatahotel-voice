## Experiences

### _The_ Colosseum

This historic arena has captured imaginations around the globe with its imposing architecture, impressive size, and captivating history of gladiator battles and thrilling moments. Enter its storied arena, where echoes of epic battles can be felt. For a truly immersive experience, we recommend a guided tour to bring the thrilling history to life.

Discover

### _Vatican_ Museums & St. Peter’s Basilica

To truly uncover the secrets and marvels of the Vatican and St. Peter’s Basilica, let our concierge service arrange a private guided tour to satisfy your every curiosity.

Discover

### _Piazza_ Navona

Wander down cobblestone streets, past storied palazzi and sunlit cafés, until the square opens before you. Bernini’s Fountain of the Four Rivers stands at the heart, its Baroque beauty a timeless centerpiece surrounded by history and quiet grandeur.

Discover

### _Pantheon_

Pause in the lively piazza and take in the buzz of people moving to and fro as you gaze upon the structure and marvel at its grandeur. As you step beneath the immense columns marking the threshold of the Pantheon, feel the presence of centuries of Roman citizens in the hush of the shadows. Bring the mysteries of the Pantheon’s architecture and the symbolism of its interior artwork to life with a guided tour curated by the Hassler Roma.

Suites

_The_

Hassler Penthouse

A room with a view: the best view of Rome. While the interiors have been thoughtfully designed, what you will notice first is the view. Large windows frame the Roman skyline. After drinking in the view, notice the grand piano and the stylish and fully stocked bar area. Sink into one of the plush settees in the living room, or wander onto one of the two panoramic terraces, where sunlight glints off the travertine marble. Cushy king-sized beds wait to lull you to sleep in each of the two bedrooms. Indulge in a chromotherapy rain shower or immerse yourself in a luxurious soaking tub, savoring the view in tranquil privacy. Every modern comfort, from high-speed Wi-Fi to a state-of-the-art music and lighting system, ensures your stay is effortlessly relaxing.

_Villa_

Medici Penthouse

Gain exclusive access to one of the city’s most gorgeous private terraces. The twin domes of the Trinità dei Monti church seem to stand sentinel over you as you gaze upon the city. Stately white columns mark the entrance to the dining area, decorated with white walls and luscious teal furniture precisely matched to the textured wallpaper of the same sumptuous color.
