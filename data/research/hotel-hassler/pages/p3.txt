## WHAT’S INSIDE

Enter our _Deluxe Rooms_, and discover a space where every detail has been carefully selected to provide you with comfort, _modern amenities_, and a pleasing aesthetic.

Cream-colored walls pair with rich, textured wallpaper and stately crown moldings in these stately rooms. Tradition and innovation are blended seamlessly into the classic decor for a stay as refined as it is comfortable. Deluxe Rooms are fitted with inviting king-sized beds, perfect for couples or single travelers looking to luxuriate in spacious comfort, while others are fitted with equally cozy twin beds for families, friends, or business travelers. Elegant marble bathrooms wait to freshen guests in style. For families or groups traveling together, rooms can be interconnected, allowing for easy shared moments and privacy when needed.

Select a garden-side view for a retreat that feels protected and hushed, or choose a city view to revel in the beauty of Rome. Retreat into quiet luxury.

## SIGNATURE SIGHTS IN THE VICINITY

Perched above the iconic Spanish Steps, Hassler Roma places you in the _heart of the city_, where Rome’s history, elegance, and vibrant energy converge for an unforgettable stay.

PantheonPiazza NavonaSpanish StepsThe ColosseumTrevi FountainVatican Museums & St. Peter’s BasilicaVilla Borghese Gardens

_Pantheon_

Pause in the lively piazza and take in the buzz of people moving to and fro as you gaze upon the structure and marvel at its grandeur. As you step beneath the immense columns marking the threshold of the Pantheon, feel the presence of centuries of Roman citizens in the hush of the shadows. Bring the mysteries of the Pantheon’s architecture and the symbolism of its interior artwork to life with a guided tour curated by the Hassler Roma.
