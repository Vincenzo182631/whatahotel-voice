### Explore The Marqués de Riscal City of Wine

Located in the medieval town of Elciego, the Hotel Marques de Riscal and its surrounding City of Wine provide the ideal starting point from which to explore the nature, tradition and exquisite gastronomy associated with wine. From the Marqués de Riscal's winery itself, the area’s first wine cellar built in 1858, to the Museo del Vino Dinastía Vivanco, Europe’s largest wine museum, to the nearby villages...See More of La Guardia and Cenicero where ancient wine-making rituals continue today—guests may expect to stimulate their senses and satisfy their thirst for adventure.
The Santiago Pilgrimage route—representing centuries of European history—passes only a few kilometres from the hotel. The area is also ideal for a bike ride or stroll through rolling vineyards and along the Ebro river, the longest in Spain. Our Luxury Collection Concierge will be pleased to assist guest with recommended day trips and guided tours.See Less

### Marqués De Riscal Winery

The expansive vineyard, dates back to 1858, making Marqués de Riscal the oldest winery in the region. Guests can learn more about wine-making on one of the daily tours of the vineyard’s state-of-the-art facilities. The fascinating journey from grapevine to bottle unfolds as visitors make their way from fermenting rooms to aging rooms stacked high with oak barrels to the enormous cellar housing eight million bottles of wine. A veritable viticulture ‘cathedral’, the cellar contains vintages dating back to 1862.

## Rooftop Lounge & Library

Perched on the top floor of Frank Gehry’s architectural masterpiece, The Library is a contemporary space perfect for chilling-out with a coffee, cocktail or glass of wine at any time of day. Those interested in learning more about the region, its wine production, and its history will love the floor-to-ceiling bookcase filled with beautiful and informative literary works. During cooler months, the caramel leather sofas and armchairs in front of the fireplace are the perfect spots to retire to with a hot chocolate or a cocktail. When the sun shines, admire the estate’s natural beauty from the large outdoor terrace suspended among the titanium waves swirling over the building’s exterior.

Hotel Activity Highlights

Guided Cultural Tours

Our concierge is pleased to recommend a variety of guided tours that explore the region’s extraordinary history and centuries-old passion for wine making.

Bicycle Tours

The hotel is nestled in a scenic area, and exploring its surroundings is easy on one of the hotel’s complimentary bicycles (subject to availability). Follow winding pathways through the vines, past ancient stone chapels and along the Ebro River, pausing at vantage points to relish the stunning panorama. Request a delicious picnic hamper and bottle of Marqués de Riscal wine to enjoy en-route when hunger strikes.

## The Marqués de Riscal City of Wine

The Marqués de Riscal City of Wine is located in Elciego, among medieval towns, where the winemaking tradition, nature, the exquisite cuisine and the splendid views perfectly complement each other to stimulate your senses.

### Museums

### Dinastía Vivanco

Europe's largest wine museum devoted to the relationship between man and wine over 8,000 years of history.

### Museum de la Rioja

The Museum de la Rioja is in a palace built in the mid-eighteenth century. A collection from prehistory to the present day.

### Guggenheim (Bilbao)

Contemporary art museum designed by Canadian architect Frank O. Gehry.

### Laguardia

Just a short drive away is the medieval town of La Guardia, where age-old wine making traditions are faithfully upheld.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Hotel Marqués de Riscal, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Elciego have a pool on-site?
