# Gastronomic Discoveries at Our Rioja Alavesa Restaurants

## Dining

Treat yourself to delicious food in our Rioja Alavesa restaurant with panoramic views over the Sierra de Cantabria. Discover all Marqués de Riscal wines in the Vinoteca & Bistro or chill out at any time...See More of day with a coffee, cocktail or glass of wine in our top-floor Library Lounge. After a superb meal, relax by simply taking your favorite drink to the rooftop lounge and library. Our Asador Torrea serves seasonal Rioja setting specializing in grilled meats. A selection of the 1860 Tradición dishes and other culinary delights may also be found on our in-room dining menu. Regardless of where our guests choose to dine, the stunning juxtaposition of Gehry’s iconic designs with the picturesque views of rolling vineyards creates unforgettable backdrops for an array of multi-layered gourmet experiences.See Less

## A La Carte Menu

A variety of regional specialties, made with fresh local products, reflect the culinary experience of Chef Francis Paniego. From accorn-fed iberian ham, to meatballs with truffle, the 1860 Tradición menu covers a wide selection of local specialties with a twist.
