# Discover Our Luxury Accommodation in Rioja Alavesa

## Accommodations

Staying at the Hotel Marques de Riscal is an experience unlike any other, where Frank O. Gehry’s avant-garde, neoclassical style permeates throughout, providing splendor, joy, and surprise around every...See More corner. From bedside cloud lamps made of white fabric balloons to the spacious green and black Italian marble baths to blackout drapes that shield late risers from the bright morning sun, every detail is designed to delight and inspire. In honor of the region's extraordinary wine-making tradition, all guests enjoy a complimentary bottle of wine upon arrival. Discover hotel suites in Rioja Alavesa with state-of-the-art technology, including large-screen high-definition televisions, high-speed Wi-Fi access, and voicemail. Suites come additionally furnished with Nespresso coffee machines and Porsche-designed electric kettles. The comfort of our youngest guests is ensured with special bath products, bottle warmers, as well as edge and socket guards.See Less

## Gehry Suite

The Icon. Located in the Gehry Wing, this alluring loft-like Suite offers an iconic level of design as well as an exquisite private terrace. Asymmetrical playfulness combined with extraordinary design sensibility make the ultimate experience for architecture fans.

## Riscal Suite Terrace

The loft-style Riscal Suites Terrace, 65 sqm, in the Spa wing, connect to Gehry building via glass bridge by Frank O. Gehry. These spacious rooms feature exclusive terraces with lounge chairs, dining area, offering stunning views of Gehry Wing and Elciego medieval town. Amenities include high-speed internet, 55” satellite TV, refreshment center (fees apply), Marques de Riscal wine, marble bathroom with walk-in shower, bathtub, dual washbasins, plush bathrobes, slippers, Caudalie products. Beds: king or twin, feather duvets, pillows. Services: daily housekeeping, turndown, in-room safe, tea/coffee facilities, newspaper (on request). Complimentary Marques de Riscal winery tour included.

### Immersive Views

Indulge in sweeping vistas from the Hotel Marqués de Riscal, offering enchanting panoramas of the renowned Marqués de Riscal vineyards and wine estate, the picturesque Elciego medieval village, and the striking architecture of the hotel itself, a masterpiece by Frank Gehry. Relax and revel in the beauty of the surroundings with a glass of exquisite wine in hand.

### Toast to Luxury

Every room and suite at Hotel Marqués de Riscal greets guests with a complimentary bottle of Rioja wine, ensuring a warm welcome and a taste of the region's renowned hospitality. Relax and unwind with a glass of this exquisite wine, setting the tone for an unforgettable stay amidst the beauty of the Rioja Alavesa countryside.

### Step Into the Future: Visionary Architecture

Experience the awe-inspiring architecture of Hotel Marqués de Riscal, a true masterpiece crafted by renowned architect Frank Gehry. With its avant-garde design characterized by flowing lines and titanium ribbons, the hotel stands as an icon of modernity amidst the traditional landscape of Rioja Alavesa. Immerse yourself in this architectural marvel and discover a world of unparalleled luxury and sophistication.
