### BecauseJameshasGreatTaste

**At The James, curiosity leads the way**. You live for what’s next—we create with that same sense of discovery. In our bars and restaurants, every dish is an invitation to explore, every cocktail a moment of unexpected delight.

Here, menus are driven by imagination and crafted with intention. Our award-winning culinary team doesn’t follow trends—they spark them. What’s on the plate is bold, artful, and entirely of the moment. What’s in the glass? A story waiting to be told—shaken, stirred, or simply poured with purpose.

Our mixologists read the energy of the room and respond with drinks that surprise and satisfy in equal measure. Whether it’s an unorthodox pairing or a playful twist on something familiar, there’s always something new to discover.

In partnership with celebrated chefs and cultural visionaries, The James is reimagining hotel dining as a creative, ever-evolving experience—equal parts vibe and flavor.
