## FAQs

### What is The James Hotels brand?

The James is a timeless brand with a luxe lifestyle approach to hospitality. Driven by warm sophistication, genuine connections, and a spirit of curiosity, The James delivers an experience that is as classic as it is modern. With destination-worthy food and beverage concepts, direct access to sought-after local experiences, and a residential sensibility reflected in every facet of each hotel, The James offers the getaway guests desire, every time.

### When does The James Nautilus Miami Beach open? Can I book now?

The James Nautilus Miami Beach is anticipated to debut in early 2027. Reservations are available now, allowing guests to be among the first to experience the newly transformed Miami Beach hotel.

### Is The James Nautilus Miami Beach the same hotel as Nautilus Sonesta Miami Beach?

Yes. Nautilus Sonesta Miami Beach is being transformed into The James Nautilus Miami Beach. The oceanfront hotel at 1825 Collins Avenue will retain its iconic Miami Modern character while introducing redesigned guestrooms and suites, refreshed public spaces, a transformed pool experience, and new dining concepts.

### Where is The James Nautilus Miami Beach located?

The James Nautilus Miami Beach is located at 1825 Collins Avenue, in the heart of South Beach, offering direct beach and boardwalk access near Lincoln Road, New World Symphony, Ocean Drive, and other top Miami Beach attractions.

### When was the hotel built/renovated?

Originally built in 1951 by famed architect Morris Lapidus, the historic Miami Beach hotel was fully redesignedand renovated in 2026 before debuting as The James Nautilus Miami Beach in early 2027.

### Does The James Nautilus Miami Beach have a restaurant? Is room service available?

Yes. Food is a huge part of The James experience, and The JamesNautilus Miami Beach offersseveral dining and nightlife experiences in partnership with LDV Hospitality, as well as pool and beach service. Full room service is also available 24 hours a day.

### Does The James Nautilus Miami Beach Hotel offer ocean view rooms?

Check-in begins at 4:00 PM, and checkout is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late checkout may be available upon request, subject to availability and additional fees.

### What time can I check in and check out?

James welcomes guests for check-in from 4:00 p.m. and checkout is at 11:00 a.m.

### Is there a minimum age requirement for check-in?

Yes. Guests must be at least 18 years old to reserve and check into a room at The James Nautilus Miami Beach. The credit card holder must be present at check-in.

### What is the reservation cancellation policy?

We get it, plans change. Generally, reservationsmust be canceled by 4:00 p.m. two days before arrival to avoid a charge equal to one night’s stay. Policies may vary by rate or offer, so please review the specific terms of your reservation.

### Is there Wi-Fi access at The James Nautilus Miami Beach?

Yes, what would we do without it? Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout The James Nautilus Miami Beach, as part of the daily resort fee.

### Are any rooms at The James Nautilus Miami Beach Hotel pet-friendly?

Check-in begins at 4:00 PM, and checkout is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late checkout may be available upon request, subject to availability and additional fees.

### Can I bring my pet?

Yes. They’re part of the family, after all. The James Nautilus Miami Beach welcomes up to two dogs or cats per guest room. Pets must be registered at the front desk, accompanied by an adult, and may not be left alone in the room. A pet fee of $75 applies, howeverservice animals are exempt.

### What is the resort/amenity fee, and what does it include?

The daily resort fee is $65 plus tax. It includes resort-wideWi-Fi, access to the 24-hour fitness center, two bottles of water daily, in-room Nespresso coffee, two beach chairs and towels, two pool chairs and towels, local calls, and bicycle rentals available from 8:30 a.m. until dusk. Beach umbrellas, pool cabanas, and spa services are available for additional charges.
