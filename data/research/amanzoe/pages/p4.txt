**Amphitheatre**

Enjoy a Greek degustation menu in Amanzoe’s open-air Amphitheatre, which offers space and privacy within a curving stone embrace beneath the stars. Ideal for intimate meals, it is also a superb venue for private events.

\\
**Private Dinner in the Library**

Celebrate a special occasion or simply savour the moment with a private candlelit dinner in the Library. Set beneath cathedral-inspired architecture, this intimate space offers a bespoke menu served by a dedicated host for an unforgettable evening.

\\
**Akari Moriawase**

A curated assortment of seasonal specialties, carefully selected by our chefs and presented as a harmonious tasting of flavours and textures in a guided dining experience. Enjoy a direct view into the kitchen from your table, offering an intimate insight into the craft behind each dish.
