The Anantara New York Palace, a luxury hotel in Budapest, is an architectural ode to Europe’s Belle Époque. A marble façade is adorned with ornate carvings and a clock tower, while inside, impressive columns, chandeliers and frescoes transport you to another era.

This 5-star hotel in Budapest is situated on the elegant Erzsébet Körút Boulevard. It provides a welcome retreat near the theatres, restaurants, and bars on the Pest side of the Danube, close to visitor attractions and transport links.

Wander the nearby Danube, then return to unwind in old world glamour mixed with contemporary luxury. Mingle at New York Café, host to the city’s poets and artists for over a century and enjoy 24-carat-gold coffee and cakes.

Frequently Asked Questions

**Are meeting rooms available at the Anantara New York Palace Budapest Hotel?**

Yes, this luxury hotel in Budapest has 11 meeting rooms and additional two event rooms in its facilities for conferences and corporate events. The rooms are fully equipped and can accommodate up to 600 people.

**What are the main points of interest close to the Anantara New York Palace Budapest Hotel?**

The Anantara New York Palace Budapest Hotel is located on Erzsébet Körút Boulevard. You can find the Madach Theatre and the Hungarian State Opera House, as well as the Blaha Lujza metro station and Jewish Quarter close by the hotel.

**What are some of the property amenities at the Anantara New York Palace Budapest Hotel?**

The hotel has a 24-hour gym, a spa with a perfect atmosphere to unwind with various treatments and a swimming pool; all these services are free for hotel guests.

**Does the Anantara New York Palace Budapest Hotel offer any food or drink options?**

Yes, you have the Deep Water Breakfast Room for a buffet breakfast or à la carte, the New York Café and White Salon Brasserie for traditional and modern Hungarian dishes, and The Poet Bar for drinks.

**Does the Anantara New York Palace Budapest Hotel offer a parking service?**

Yes. The Anantara New York Palace Budapest, a luxury hotel in Budapest, offers parking services, including valet parking and bicycle parking.
