Activities & Experiences in Peninsula Papagayo, Costa Rica
Signature Activities & Resort Amenities
At Nekajui, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve located in the heart of Peninsula Papagayo, guests can enjoy a multitude of activities. Explore nature with guided hikes and wildlife tours carefully curated by Papagayo Explorers or challenge yourself at The Outpost; surf the waves with the female-led team at SurfX; just adjacent to the resort, play the 18-hole par-72 Arnold Palmer Signature Golf Course or a game of tennis or pickleball. Additional experiences at this luxury spa resort include water sports, whale watching, sunset cruises, or charter fishing, mountain biking or jeep tours, and a myriad of other adventures in Costa Rica’s stunning landscape. Within minutes of the resort, explore the UNESCO Guanacaste World Heritage Site.
Camp Nekajui
Curated for young explorers, Camp Nekajui invites children ages 4–12 into a vibrant world of creativity, nature, and imagination. Inspired by Peninsula Papagayo’s biodiversity, daily activities include nature walks, sensory play, arts and crafts, and science experiments—all guided by expert counselors. While kids discover and connect, parents enjoy the reserve knowing their little ones are engaged, cared for, and inspired. Hours of operation: Daily 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Discover Papagayo Park: Where Adventure Meets Community
Papagayo Park is the ultimate destination for active living and family fun at Peninsula Papagayo. From tennis, pickleball, and padel courts to a lap pool and a first-of-its-kind pump track, the park offers endless ways to move and play. Kids will love the splash pad and playground, while creatives can explore their passions in the Art Studio. With a dog park, basketball court, and Espira Shala for wellness and starlit gatherings, plus dining at Patio Social and sweet treats at Cono Loco, Papagayo Park is your vibrant hub for connection and unforgettable experiences.

For the Golf Enthusiasts
The Ultimate Golf Getaway
Indulge in the ultimate eco-golf experience at the 18-hole, par-72 Arnold Palmer Signature golf course at Peninsula Papagayo. Enjoy stunning views of white sand beaches, turquoise waters, and lush seashore paspalum turf as the course winds through forests, valleys, and seacliffs. The beauty of the surroundings and the chatter of white-faced monkeys in the trees make every game unforgettable.

Racquet Sports Program
Racquet Sports in the Tropics
Peninsula Papagayo offers a dynamic racquet sports program across a variety of state-of-the-art facilities, including 8 tennis courts, 6 pickleball courts and 2 padel courts. Four Har-Tru clay surface tennis courts are located immediately adjacent to our Costa Rica spa resort, while four Rebound Ace hard surface tennis courts, six pickleball courts and two padel courts are just a short drive away at Papagayo Park. Guests can reserve a court time, join weekly clinics, or book private lessons tailored to their skill level. All lessons and court reservations include complimentary equipment rentals, making it easy for players of every age and ability to get on the court and play.

Family-Friendly Activities
Adventures with Papagayo Explorers
The Papagayo Explorers program offers guests the opportunity to discover and immerse in Costa Rica’s natural beauty through curated outdoor pursuits on land, water, and all the way up in the tree canopy. Explore one of the world’s last remaining tropical dry forests at The Outpost, set within the 250-acre protected Palmares Preserve. Spanning three different ecosystems in less than a square mile, The Outpost is the place to go in search of amazing wildlife encounters, ziplining and aerial trekking, off-roading, mountain biking, wilderness hikes, bird watching and more.
