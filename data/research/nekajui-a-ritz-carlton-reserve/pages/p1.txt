A Luxurious Resort in the Stunning Peninsula Papagayo
Originating from the Chorotega word for “lush garden,” Nekajui Peninsula Papagayo, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve offers guests a luxury sanctuary for exploration and inner serenity. Nestled deep within the protected lands of Peninsula Papagayo, our five-star resort offers exclusive accommodations facing ethereal views of Playa Pochote and the Pacific Ocean. As one of the premier luxury resorts in Guanacaste, Costa Rica, Nekajui delivers sophisticated dining experiences, from artisanal coffee rituals to private treetop settings and modern Peruvian cuisine, bringing local ingredients to life. Our spa is set in perfect harmony with the land’s natural rhythms, rooted in ancient ways of living that inspire renewed energy.

Café Rincón
Named after the majestic Rincón de la Vieja volcano, Café Rincón celebrates Costa Rica’s coffee and cacao heritage with roasted-to-order brews, artisan chocolates, and vibrant local flavors crafted to delight both curiosity and comfort.

Puna
Under Chef Diego Muñoz’s creative direction, Puna fuses Peru’s culinary heritage with Costa Rican freshness. This fine dining experience blends bold flavors and minimalist presentation for a refined take on heritage cuisine in Papagayo.

Niri Beach Club

Residences at The Reserve
Architectural Elegance Meets Local Essence
Nekajui Peninsula Papagayo, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve Residences take you on a timeless architectural journey. Natural wonders integrate with Guanacaste’s multicultural narrative in a rich environment deeply rooted in the nature of place. Woven gently into the hillside above Playa Pochote, the Residences at this luxury oceanfront resort in Costa Rica portray a comfortable and elegantly understated midpoint between traditional and modern. It is a celebration of craft and place, with simple yet sophisticated architectural lines and a serene palette created from local materials and colors, including Costa Rican wood and stone. These thoughtfully designed residences reflect the elevated experience sought by travelers exploring the best hotels in Papagayo, Costa Rica.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at Nekajui Peninsula Papagayo, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve?
The check-in time at Nekajui Peninsula Papagayo, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does Nekajui Peninsula Papagayo, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at Nekajui Peninsula Papagayo, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at Nekajui Peninsula Papagayo, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve?
On-site parking is available at Nekajui Peninsula Papagayo, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.
