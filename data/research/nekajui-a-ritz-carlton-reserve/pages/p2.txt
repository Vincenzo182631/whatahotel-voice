Sophisticated Rooms & Suites in Peninsula Papagayo, Costa Rica
Suites & Rooms
The luxurious accommodations at Nekajui Peninsula Papagayo, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve are an authentic representation of Guanacaste architecture bringing together rich details from the Spanish and Sabanero cultures in harmony with the stunning natural surroundings. Rooms and suites feature comfortably elegant accommodations, expansive terraces and balconies - many with plunge pools - and spectacular views. Glamping tents blend luxury with nature, featuring spacious, open-air designs and private plunge pools. Residences range from two to five bedrooms, with modern Guanacaste hacienda architecture, private terraces, infinity pools, and state-of-the-art amenities. Each luxury villa seamlessly integrates indoor and outdoor living, providing an intimate connection to the surrounding tropical dry forest and coastline of Costa Rica.
Exclusive Amenities for Suites & Tents
Suite & Tent stays include a complimentary minibar with a full honor bar, including alcoholic beverages.
The Art of Elevated Living
From oceanfront sanctuaries to immersive treetop retreats, each Papagayo hotel suite with views over the Pacific Ocean is designed to connect guests to the beauty of Costa Rica.
