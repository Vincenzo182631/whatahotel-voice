The Ocean Course at Peninsula Papagayo
Play 18 holes on our ocean course at Peninsula Papagayo
Ranked among Golf Digest’s “Top 100 Courses Outside the United States,” the Ocean Course at Peninsula Papagayo is a private, 18-hole, par-72 course that winds through 125 acres of old-growth forest, scenic valleys, and sea cliffs shaped by ancient lava flows. Regarded as one of the premier destinations for golf in Costa Rica’s Guanacaste region, the course offers ocean views on 14 of 18 holes and showcases the peninsula’s dramatic natural beauty at every turn. Players of all skill levels will appreciate the wide fairways and large greens, while elevation changes and a richly textured landscape ensure a fair yet exhilarating challenge from the first tee to the final putt.
Arnold Palmer Signature Design
A Course Designed by the Land Itself
Carved through lava flows, rolling valleys, and coastal hills, the Arnold Palmer Signature Course is shaped by the land itself. Wide fairways invite confident play, while elevation shifts, contoured greens, and ocean breezes reward strategy and precision. Each round unfolds with balance, rhythm, and immersive beauty.
Nature Without Borders
Eco-Golf in Costa Rica
Featuring panoramic vistas on 14 holes, players will immerse themselves in the vibrant sights and sounds of Peninsula Papagayo’s wild side. From the curious chatter of monkeys overhead to the more than 100 species of birds that call the peninsula home, this exceptional destination for golf in Costa Rica’s Guanacaste region offers an unforgettable playing experience. This eco-golf triumph is a Certified Audubon Cooperative Sanctuary, recognized for its commitment to conservation and environmental protection. Each hole provides unique opportunities to encounter rare species and other colorful members of Papagayo’s biological community.

Frequently Asked Questions
Are guests able to make online reservations to Ocean Course at Peninsula Papagayo?
Online reservations are not available at Ocean Course at Peninsula Papagayo. Please call us at +506 0-4081 1221 to learn more about reserving a tee time.
Does Ocean Course at Peninsula Papagayo have a clubhouse?
There is a clubhouse at Ocean Course at Peninsula Papagayo.Enjoy a bite to eat or a cool refreshment at our clubhouse restaurant.
What is par at Ocean Course at Peninsula Papagayo?
Ocean Course at Peninsula Papagayo is a par-72, 18-hole course, with a total length of 6788 yards. To learn more about the specifications of Ocean Course at Peninsula Papagayo, please call +506 0-4081 1221.

A driving range is located at Ocean Course at Peninsula Papagayo. Amateur and experienced golfers alike can warm up or practice their shot ahead of their next round.
