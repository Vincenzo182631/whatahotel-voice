Luxury Spa & Wellness in Peninsula Papagayo, Costa Rica
Naku Wellness & Spa
Nestled within the forests of Peninsula Papagayo, Naku Wellness & Spa draws on Costa Rica’s healing traditions, native botanicals, and the restorative power of water. This exceptional Costa Rica spa resort offers personalized wellness journeys inspired by Chorotega heritage and local ingredients, inviting guests to reconnect with nature, restore balance, and experience wellbeing through a profound sense of place.
A Wellness Journey Rooted in Place
The Essence of Costa Rica
At Naku, nature is the source of healing. Costa Rica’s native botanicals, medicinal plants, cacao, volcanic minerals, and local ingredients are woven into wellness rituals inspired by generations of knowledge. Each experience reflects the beauty, culture, and spirit of Guanacaste.

Inspired by Juanilama
At the heart of Naku is juanilama, a treasured Costa Rican herb known for its calming and restorative properties. Featured in aromatherapy blends, essential oils, and signature body rituals, it is paired with notes of mandarin and bergamot to soothe the senses, release tension, and promote a deep sense of wellbeing through scent, touch, and intention.
The Hydrothermal Experience
Water is woven throughout the Naku wellness journey. A thoughtfully designed circuit guides guests through contrast pools, sauna, steam, and sensory rituals that encourage relaxation, balance, and renewal. Surrounded by panoramic Pacific Ocean and lush forest views, this wellness resort in Costa Rica harnesses the restorative power of water to calm the mind, awaken the senses, and restore overall well-being.
Beyond Traditional Spa Experiences
Naku embraces a holistic approach to wellbeing, nurturing body, mind, and spirit through immersive experiences beyond the treatment room. Sound healing, mindful rituals, movement practices, and personalized wellness journeys foster balance and connection, inviting guests to slow down, restore, and reconnect through the healing power of nature.
The Taste of Wellness: Cacao, Coffee, & Costa Rican Rituals
The spirit of Costa Rica is woven throughout the Naku experience. Locally sourced cacao and coffee inspire sensory rituals that honor the land’s rich heritage through aroma, texture, and tradition, connecting guests to the landscapes, communities, and culture that define the destination.

Our Philosophy: Wellness Through Connection
At Naku, wellbeing begins with connection: to nature, culture, and self. Locally inspired rituals, personalized care, and moments of intentional stillness invite guests to reconnect with the wisdom of the natural world. Rooted in Costa Rican traditions and elevated through modern wellness practices, Naku has become a sought-after spa in Guanacaste, Costa Rica, where each experience leaves a lasting sense of renewal.
