# Wilderness Adventure Spa Massages

Add an extra level of relaxation to your vacation by choosing one of our many massage options and customizing it just for you.

### Deluxe Massages ~

* * *

#### Custom Massage   — $155 for 60 minutes or $195 for 90 minutes

Choose from Swedish, Deep Tissue, Sports Massage or a combination of all three to suit your needs. All Custom Massage treatments include Eucalyptus infused hot towels. Upgrade your massage to include aromatherapy massage oil.

* * *

#### Hot Stone Massage   — $210 for 90 minutes

Let the heat of the river stones gently melt away soreness and tension as your skilled therapist provides for you the ultimate experience in relaxation.

* * *

#### Teton Forest Massage   — $210 for 90 minutes

Start with a mineral scrub to heal dry climate skin with mud bubbling on your spine. After rinsing, we follow with a White Pine and Basil full body massage.
