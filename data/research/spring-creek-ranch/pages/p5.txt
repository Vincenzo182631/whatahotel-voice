# Summer Recreation in Jackson Hole

During summer months, Jackson Hole transforms into an eclectic cultural mecca set smack dab in an adventurer’s dreamscape. The opportunities for exploration are as seemingly endless as the mountains that ring this magnificent valley. We promise that there will be nothing typical about your days spent in Jackson Hole. Spend your mornings riding the Snake River or discovering our National Parks while afternoons can be devoted to wandering downtown and delighting in the diversity of galleries and boutiques that line Town Square. End your evening with a sampling of the area’s world class cuisine and marvel at the number of activities you managed to fit into a single day.

Plan a trip to Jackson and stay with us at Spring Creek Ranch. Our guests agree, Spring Creek is the perfect location to experience the paradise that is summer in Jackson Hole.
