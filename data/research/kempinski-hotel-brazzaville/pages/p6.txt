# Our Hotel

### Welcome to Kempinski Hotel Brazzaville

Set along the majestic banks of the Congo River, Kempinski Hotel Brazzaville is the city’s first premier five-star luxury address. Perfectly located in the heart of the capital, the hotel offers discerning business and leisure travellers a refined blend of European elegance and African warmth.

Our 197 rooms and suites promise sophisticated comfort, contemporary design and breathtaking views of the river or the city skyline. Our dining journey features signature restaurants and lounges, presenting international flavours alongside locally inspired cuisine for unforgettable culinary experiences. The world-class spa and wellness centre provides a sanctuary for relaxation, complemented by a swimming pool, fitness facilities and exclusive amenities for a complete and luxurious stay.

For business and events, our hotel offers state-of-the-art meeting rooms, versatile banquet venues and dedicated services tailored to host everything from high-level conferences to private celebrations. Whether for corporate travel, diplomatic gatherings or weekend escapes, every moment at Kempinski Hotel Brazzaville reflects sophistication and attention to detail.
