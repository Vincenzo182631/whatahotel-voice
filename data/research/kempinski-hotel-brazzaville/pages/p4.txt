# Location

### Where to Find Us

**ADDRESS**

Kempinski Hotel Brazzaville

Avenue de l’Amilcar Cabral, Centre-Ville

Brazzaville, Republic of Congo

**DIRECTIONS FROM THE AIRPORT**

From Maya-Maya International Airport (5 km away), head right onto "Avenue de l'Aéroport". Continue straight to the "IFC (formerly CCF)" roundabout, then turn right onto Boulevard Denis SASSOU NGUESSO. Continue straight to the "Marien NGOUAGI Museum", then turn right onto Avenue Amilcar Cabral. Kempinski Hotel Brazzaville is located on this avenue, in the city center, overlooking the Congo River. This journey, rich in discoveries of the city, takes approximately 15 minutes by car.

**AIRPORT PICK-UP SERVICE**

Our concierge team will be delighted to assist with transfers. Luxury transportation can be arranged upon request, with options ranging from executive sedans to spacious SUVs for groups and families. For reservations, please contact us on info.brazzaville@kempinski.com.
