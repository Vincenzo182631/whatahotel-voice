### What is Republic of Congo Independence Day?

Celebrated annually on 15 August, Republic of Congo Independence Day commemorates the nation’s independence and celebrates its rich cultural heritage. The capital comes alive with official state ceremonies, vibrant military and civilian parades and public concerts that resonate throughout the city. It is a time of reflection, unity and celebration, attracting visitors from across the country and around the world to the political and cultural heart of Brazzaville.
