# Welcome to Brazzaville

### The Pearl of the Congo River

Facing the majestic Congo River, Brazzaville captivates with its unique charm, in this place where nature, history and culture come together in harmony.

As the capital of the Republic of Congo, Brazzaville blends its riverfront beauty with a rich cultural heritage expressed through art, music and timeless traditions. A true city of discovery, travellers are invited to explore colourful markets, iconic landmarks and authentic neighbourhoods, while enjoying the serenity of its lush riverbanks. From the striking Sainte-Anne Basilica to the lively quarters of Poto-Poto and Bacongo, every corner tells a story and promises a one-of-a-kind experience.

Cosmopolitan and welcoming, Brazzaville offers the perfect balance between urban energy and warm hospitality, making it an ideal destination for both business and leisure travellers. Here, every stay at Kempinski Hotel Brazzaville is a refined and memorable journey where modernity embraces the soulful traditions of Central Africa.
