## An Elegant Luxury Yorkville Hotel near Downtown Toronto

Welcome to our luxury hotel nestled in Yorkville near downtown Toronto. Central to Bloor and Avenue Road, Park Hyatt Toronto puts you just steps away from world-renowned museums, art galleries and historic sites. After a day full of Midtown adventures, retreat to our hotel to enjoy a treatment at Stillwater Spa or visit our rooftop bar for deliciously crafted cocktails and stunning city views.

Stillwater Spa

Discover elegant relaxation in the heart of Yorkville. Stillwater Spa offers 11 treatment rooms, two couples’ suites and manicure and pedicure services. Experience urban wellness designed to promote natural rejuvenation and deep relaxation at our luxury Toronto hotel.

**Spa Gift Certificates**

Global Chef Series

Park Hyatt Toronto’s Global Chef Series, inspired by the spirit of Park Hyatt’s Masters of Food & Wine, invites guests to experience some of the world’s most celebrated culinary talents through an extraordinary series of dining engagements.

Hosted at Joni Restaurant, this exclusive program welcomes award-

winning guest chefs from around the globe for highly limited residencies,

offering rare opportunities to experience their culinary artistry in Toronto.

Each residency brings together a renowned guest chef and Park Hyatt

Toronto Executive Chef Jonathan Williams to create immersive, multi-

course tasting menus that showcase the distinctive flavours, techniques,

and signature dishes that define the visiting chef’s home city and

culinary perspective.

Every experience is complemented by a thoughtfully curated selection of fine

wines and premium spirits, carefully chosen to elevate each course.

### Pets Are Welcome

Don't leave your furry friends behind. As a dog-friendly hotel, Park Hyatt Toronto loves to roll out the welcome mat for them. Walk Yorkville, Bloor Street, Queen’s Park and more nearby walkable areas with your dogs. In the room, your pet will enjoy its own dog bed and water bowl on arrival. For those staying 30+ nights, a fee will be charged at the hotel’s discretion.

#### Pet Fees

- 1–6 nights: **$150** _/ STAY_

- 7–30 nights: Additional cleaning fee: **$150**

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  50Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 75Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.
