# Restaurants & Bars

Experience iconic views of downtown Toronto from our rooftop bar and an elevated fine dining experience at Joni Restaurant. Park Hyatt Toronto invites you to indulge your senses when you stay, and eat, at our luxury hotel in the chic Yorkville neighbourhood.
Embark on a culinary journey at Joni Restaurant with our Tasting Menu, an exclusive opportunity featuring a selection of dishes chosen by our talented culinary team. Follow this up with an evening at Writers Room Bar, our rooftop lounge with views overlooking Queens Park and the city skyline, cocktail in hand.

## Joni Restaurant

Joni Restaurant offers a refined culinary experience that celebrates the best of each season, guided by the vision of Executive Chef Jonathan Williams. Honouring the richness of our region, our dishes are crafted from locally sourced, sustainable ingredients, featuring the freshest produce, meats, and seafood - all prepared in-house.

## Writers Room Bar

On the 17th floor, Writers Room Bar reimagines the classic cocktail lounge, celebrating the literary voices behind its 50-year story. Cocktails are expressive and inspired by storytelling, complemented by sweeping Toronto skyline views. Guests 19 and older can savour a setting where every sip opens a new chapter.
