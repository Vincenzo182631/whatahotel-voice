### The Park Hyatt Toronto Lives Up to Its Brand

I would recommend a stay at the Park Hyatt Toronto and will likely return for a stay if we visit Toronto again. At check in we were welcomed with an arrival drink which is always a nice way start to our stay. We are a family of 5 and had requested rooms near each other. At check in we were given connecting rooms which was perfect for our family! My Globalist benefits were explained clearly and applied appropriately, however complimentary breakfast was not extended to our 3rd child upon request. Breakfast at Joni as well as in room dining were both exceptional including food and service. Housekeeping kept the room tidy for our two night stay and valet services were quick and easy. Late checkout was offered and confirmed at check in. I think this property lived up to the level of service and luxury you would expect to receive when staying at the Park Hyatt brand.
