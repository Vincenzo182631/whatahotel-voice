# Rooms

Each detail has been carefully selected to offer a space of calm and peace. Neutral color palettes and timeless design distinguish each of the guest rooms. Accents of walnut and marble complement the design, which is punctuated with authentic local detail such as an iconic chair designed by Oscar Tusquets and artwork by Spanish artist Chema Madoz.

# Suites

Flooded with natural light, luxury suites offer an airy, spacious layout with the hotel’s signature touches as well as spacious bathrooms with marble bathtubs with large windows that offer stunning views of the Gothic Quarter or the beautiful and colorful Santa Caterina market. Selected suites and penthouses benefit from beautiful fully furnished and oversized private terraces.
