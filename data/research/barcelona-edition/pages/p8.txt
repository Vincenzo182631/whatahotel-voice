# Services & Amenities

Discover the luxury amenities, timelessly elegant accommodation, design, and exceptional location of The Barcelona EDITION. This luxury boutique hotel welcomes visitors to Spain with curated amenities chosen to create a memorable travel experience.

### For further information

Phone +0034 93 626 33 30 or info.bcn@editionhotels.com

Share

GUEST SERVICES

- 100 sophisticated rooms and suites, including 2 penthouses with stunning views of the city from their fully-furnished private terraces
- The Roof bar and lounge with outside pool and stunning city views
- Veraz restaurant serving delicious breakfast and a product driven cuisine
- Punch Room bespoke cocktail bar
- Customized banquet menus for private events, social gatherings, luncheons, dinner parties and business meetings
- 24-hour room service
- Concierge service
- Twice daily housekeeping service
- Laundy, dry cleaning and press service
- Shoe shine service
- Complimentary high-speed WIFI access throughout the hotel
- Complimentary use of the 24-hour Gym
- Newspapers of your choice delivered to your door upon request
- Valet parking service
- Multi-lingual staff

POOL

- Outdoor urban plunge pool with panoramic views located on The Roof, open from 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM

GYM

- State-of-the-art fitness equipment including cardiovascular machines with personal flat screen TVs
- Free weights
- Stretching area
- Complimentary water and towels
- Accessible 24 hours a day

KIDS & FAMILY

- Multiple connecting rooms and suites available perfect for a once-in-a-lifetime family getaway in the heart of Barcelona.
- Connecting rooms for up to 8 guests, confirmed at the time of booking and full floor allocation might be possible in advance.
- For more details, please contact our team at +34 93 626 33 30 or email us at reservations.bcn@editionhotels.com

ACCESSIBILITY

- Mobility accessible rooms available for certain room types
- Additional accessibility information available here

RECREATION

- More than 15 iconic Barcelona sights and historic landmarks are walking distance from the hotel, avoiding the need for any transportation while discovering Barcelona’s best kept secrets, restaurants and local shopping.
