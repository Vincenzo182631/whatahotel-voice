# Getting Here

- From Sants Train Station
- From Cruise Port Terminals
- From Josep Tarradellas Barcelona-El Prat Airport

## From Sants Train Station

## FROM SANTS TRAIN STATION

_Private Car:_

A private car to/from Sants can be arranged by the hotel concierge at +34 93 626 33 30 prior to your arrival/departure.

_Taxi:_

Taxis are available at the train station on a first-come, first-served basis and are approximately €25 to €30 each way. The time to the hotel is approximately 20 min.

_The Metro:_

It takes approximately 25 minutes to reach the hotel by metro.

• Take the L1 (red) on Plaça de Sants

• Get off at Urquinaona (same line)

• Exit the station and walk down Via Laietana toward Plaça d’Antoni Maura

• Avinguda de Francesc Cambó is on the left (the hotel is located just next to Santa Caterina Market)

## From Josep Tarradellas Barcelona-El Prat Airport

## FROM JOSEP TARRADELLAS BARCELONA- EL PRAT AIRPORT

_Private Car:_

A private car to/from Josep Tarradellas Barcelona – El Prat can be arranged by the hotel concierge at +34 93 626 33 30 prior to your arrival/departure.

_Taxi:_

Taxis are available at the airport on a first-come, first-served basis and are approximately €35 to €45 each way. The time to the hotel is approximately 30 minutes, but can take up to 1 hour during peak times.

_The Metro:_

The metro takes approximately 45 minutes to reach the hotel.

• Take the L9 Sud (orange) Line to Torrassa

• There, change to Line1 (red)

• Get off at Urquinaona

• Exit the station and walk down Via Laietana toward Plaça d’Antoni Maura

• Avinguda de Francesc Cambó is on the left (the hotel is located just next to the Santa Caterina Market)

_Aerobus:_

Operating 365 days a year Aerobus is a comfortable option. A trip with only three stops away from the Airport, Terminal 1 (A1) and Terminal 2 (A2), arriving at Plaça Catalunya which is 10 minutes walking distance to the hotel. Buses depart daily every 5 to 10 minutes.

_Driving:_

This route will take approximately 30 minutes

• Get on C-31 from Av. de l'Aeroport

• Follow C-31 and B-10 to Passeig Josep Carner in Barcelona. Take the exit toward 21 Ciutat Vella/Paral.lel/La Rombla/Montjuïc/Port Vell from B-10

• Continue on Passeig Josep Carner. Take Passeig de Colom and Via Laietana to Av. de Francesc Cambó, 14.
