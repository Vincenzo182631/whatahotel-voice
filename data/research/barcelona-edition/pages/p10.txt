# Álvaro Salazar x Veraz

**Álvaro Salazar x Veraz**

RESERVE

Explore a unique collaboration between the Chef Álvaro Salazar, one of the most renowned figures in Spanish haute cuisine, and Veraz at The Barcelona EDITION.

Álvaro Salazar, the creative force behind VORO restaurant in Mallorca, awarded two Michelin stars and three Repsol Suns, brings his talent to Veraz with an exclusive signature gastronomic experience conceived as a journey through his culinary evolution.

Immerse yourself in a carefully orchestrated universe of flavors through a five-course tasting menu that explores the acclaimed chef’s creative world. A culinary journey inspired by Spain’s rich gastronomic heritage, deep respect for the natural environment, and Mediterranean and Andalusian roots, all interpreted through a contemporary and creative lens.

Available through October 2026, this collaboration invites guests to discover at Veraz Salazar’s exquisite culinary creations, celebrating product, territory, and emotion in every detail.

For reservations, please contact our team at +34 93 626 33 60 / +34 93 626 33 51 or email us at reservations.bcn@editionhotels.com.

**Veraz**

RESERVE

True to the EDITION vernacular, a spectacular bar serving locally produced vermouth, craft beer and bespoke cocktails unites the hotel’s reception with an informal restaurant offering à la carte breakfast and affordable dishes focusing on the freshest, market seasonal ingredients from the neighbouring Santa Caterina Market.

With oversized floor-to-ceiling windows and its own separate entrance, residents and locals alike are captivated by an enormous central bar piece that occupies the double height space. Hanging from the high ceilings above the bar, this awe-inspiring aged brass element glows elegantly. The magnificent wall of 410 backlit liquor bottles creates a warm crepuscular light reminiscent of a Mediterranean sunset.

Lush greenery with potted palm trees that emulate an interior garden create a sense of intimacy and calm within the vibrant city setting, and a selection of original art pieces by famous Catalan artists Joana Biarnés, Colita, Xavier Miserachs and Oriol Maspons accompany the seating arrangements, helping to forge a strong connection between the city, the local culture and casual dining.

For reservations, contact our team at +34 936 263 351 or email us at reservations.bcn@editionhotels.com.

**The Roof**

The Roof is an iconic destination in Barcelona's social life and an intimate oasis in the heart of the city. The Mediterranean terrace, located on the 10th floor of The Barcelona EDITION offers Mediterranean food, signature cocktails and unparalleled panoramic views.

The terrace is decorated with iroko wooden parquet, small tables, sofas, armchairs and sunbeds upholstered in ivory, gray and blue tones, abundant vegetation with aromatic plants and a colorful bougainvillea that creates a garden atmosphere in the sky. Lit by dim candlelight, the sunsets at The Roof are a unique experience that gives you the feeling of being on a private rooftop with the most sophisticated ambience.

The panoramic views from the small urban outdoor pool and from the entire terrace offer an incomparable setting made up of a sea of roofs. One can admire the small historic buildings of the Barrio del Born in the Gothic heart of the city, the unmistakable silhouette of the Gothic cathedral of Santa Maria del Mar, the colorful and wavy roof of the Mercat de Santa Caterina- designed by the Miralles-Tagliabue architectural studio- simulating the wind-blown awnings of the old food market, as well as the blue of the Mediterranean sea in the distance.

The bar is a unique piece of travertine marble where the best bartenders in Barcelona offer a fresh and innovative seasonal menu consisting of cocktails and various healthy options such as natural juices, smoothies, kombuchas and mocktails.

For colder and rainy days, the service continues as the glass sliding walls come to a close, offering year-round day and night service for guests and the local community alike.

No reservations accepted, walk-ins only. For general inquiries and large group reservations, please contact our team at +34 936 263 349 or email us at reservations.bcn@editionhotels.com.

**Punch Room**

Expanding on the success of the London EDITION location, the Punch Room brings creativity and sophistication to the Barcelona bar scene. Accessed by a dramatic spiral staircase, the speakeasy-inspired lounge highlights local spirits and ingredients in its curated cocktail menu and specialty punches, served in vintage silver bowls. Punches are complemented by a selection of light bites.

The space itself is as carefully crafted as the punches and features a Castilian-style pool table with a golden cloth, a black-granite fireplace, leather and green velvet furnishings and oak-paneled walls lined with images from local photographer Andrea Torres.

Punch Room has been recognized in the Global Rank 2022 by Top 500 Bars and shortlisted Top 10 Europe’s Best International Hotel Bar 2022, Top 10 Europe’s Best New Cocktail Bar 2020, Top 4 World’s Best New Cocktail Bar 2019 by Tales of the Cocktail Spirited Awards and as one of the Best Cocktail Bars in Spain and Portugal by Top Cocktail Bars.
