# Welcome to Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado

## Expect luxury in Peru's Sacred Valley

Discover an unparalleled luxury experience at Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado. Our hotel in Sacred Valley, Urubamba, Peru, offers stunning views of the Andes Mountains and...See More the Vilcanota River. Relax in elegant rooms and suites, savor authentic Peruvian cuisine crafted with local ingredients and produce from our organic garden, and rejuvenate at our spa, heated indoor pool, and fitness center. Adventure, culture, and wellness come together at our luxury Sacred Valley hotel and an unforgettable destination.See Less

## Discover the Sacred Valley, Naturally

Set in the heart of the Sacred Valley, Tambo del Inka offers an intimate connection to one of Peru’s most storied landscapes. From serene moments in the spa and quiet walks through the gardens to inspired dining and immersive explorations of the valley’s cultural and natural wonders, each day at our luxury Sacred Valley, Peru, resort unfolds at its own unhurried pace.

## Sustainability

Tambo del Inka celebrates the connection between culture, nature, and sustainability. Through its Biosphere Sustainable certification and numerous additional initiatives of its own, the resort reaffirms its commitment to tourism that respects and enriches the Sacred Valley.

### In the Heart of the Andes

Following a Legacy Carved Into the Andes

### Tambo del Inka

Set along the legendary Incan path, beside the flowing Urubamba River, Tambo del Inka draws inspiration from the ancient tambos that once offered rest and refuge to travelers on their journey to Machu Picchu. Surrounded by the Sacred Valley’s towering mountains and ancestral landscapes, the resort invites guests to follow the footsteps of a remarkable civilization—where culture, nature, and spirituality remain deeply intertwined, alive in local traditions and the enduring rhythm of the land.

## Boulevard x Tambo del Inka

Boulevard x Tambo del Inka is a vibrant destination designed to showcase the essence of the Sacred Valley through authentic experiences that bring together culture, art, gastronomy, design, and tradition. Inspired by the connection between travelers and the local community, it features a carefully curated collection of experiences that celebrate regional talent and heritage. Every visit is an invitation to discover unique stories, flavors, and expressions of the destination. Boulevard x Tambo del Inka: Where Everything Connects.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado?

The check-in time at Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado.

What are the parking options at Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado?

On-site self parking is available at Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado booking.

What is the closest airport near Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado?

The closest airport near Tambo del Inka, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Valle Sagrado is Alejandro Velasco Astete International Airport (CUZ). CUZ is located approximately 60.0 KM from the hotel.
