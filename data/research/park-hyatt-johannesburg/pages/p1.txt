# Luxury 5-Star Hotel in Johannesburg \| Park Hyatt Johannesburg

## A Rare Johannesburg Retreat

Set within a restored landmark by Sir Herbert Baker in the heart of Rosebank, Park Hyatt Johannesburg presents a rare expression of contemporary luxury shaped by architectural heritage, refined design and deeply personal service. Intimate in scale and exceptional in character, this exclusive ​5-star ​retreat combines the privacy of a distinguished private residence with the elegance, exclusivity and exceptional hospitality sought by today's luxury traveler.
Home to ​​31 guestrooms and suites, the hotel offers a level of exclusivity seldom found among luxury hotels in Johannesburg. Original architectural details, tranquil courtyard gardens and exquisitely appointed interiors create a setting defined by beauty, fine taste and a genuine sense of place. Park Hyatt Johannesburg reimagines one of the city's most distinguished heritage addresses as an intimate luxury retreat, where impeccable hospitality, architectural character and contemporary sophistication come together effortlessly.
