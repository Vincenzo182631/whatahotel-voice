# Restaurants & Bars

Experience the epitome of luxury dining at Park Hyatt Johannesburg. At Room 32, fire-cooking techniques transform every dish into a sensory masterpiece. The Lounge offers a refined atmosphere with artisanal cocktails and light bites, perfect for any occasion. The Terrace provides alfresco dining with stunning views of Rosebank, blending elegant cuisine with a relaxed ambiance. Reserve your table today for an unforgettable culinary journey.

## Room 32

Built around the art of cooking over flame and the finest locally sourced ingredients, Room 32 celebrates the richness and diversity of South African flavors through elegant à la carte selections and immersive tasting menus. Exceptional wines, seasonal produce and beautifully balanced dishes come together in a dining experience that showcases contemporary South African gastronomy at its finest.
