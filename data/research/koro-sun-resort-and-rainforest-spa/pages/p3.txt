## Location

Our 160 acre rainforest sanctuary sits on Fiji’s Northern island of Vanua Levu, in Savusavu– a short flight from Nadi International airport to either Savusavu or Labasa Airports. Flights can be booked at www.fijiairways.com.

Complimentary ground transfers are provided from Savusavu (SVU) Airport or Labasa (LBS) Airport (complimentary transfers from Labasa require a 3 night minimum.)

## Restaurants

**Palm Grove** is the primary dining outlet within the central clubhouse of the resort serving breakfast, lunch & dinner.

**Latitude 17 Seafood and Steak** restaurant offers seaside dining with touches of Asian Fusion. The prime position on the resorts serene Edgewater Lagoon boasts spectacular views. L17 is open for dining Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturdays.

**Sand Bar & Grill** offers a casual, family friendly waterfront dining experience. Situated on the edge of the Floating Bure Lagoon, you can enjoy a delicious meal or snack al fresco.

Our Bars – the **Levu Bar**, **Latitude 17 Bar**, **Sand Bar** & swim-up **Wai Bar** – serve fresh tropical cocktails, beers & a range of wines from around the world.

## Rainforest Spa

Our spa boasts a truly one-of-a-kind setting, deep in the stunning rainforest. The sounds of the running water, coupled with the warmth of the skilled Fijians will leave you with a unique spa experience that you will always remember. Our Rainforest Spa menu offers massages, scrubs, facials, manicures and pedicures.
