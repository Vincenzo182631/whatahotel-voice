Best for
: Couples, families, and the culturally attuned seeking effortless hospitality
Location:
Downtown Santa Fe, opposite the Palace of the Governors and steps from the historic Plaza
Can’t miss feature:
Chef Cristian Pontiggia's Anasazi Restaurant serving contemporary American cuisine inspired by local ingredients, accompanied by an extensive wine cellar
In a nutshell
: More than just a place to rest your head, the Anasazi is a luxury hotel that is deeply connected to the cultural fabric of Santa Fe.
Patrick Gray
Patrick Gray
Rooms
As befits the renowned and ever expanding Rosewood collection, rooms and suites at the Anasazi set the benchmark for a luxury stay. Each room is flooded with amber Southwestern light, framed by chunky wooden beams, and the massive beds are adorned with local textiles. My room even featured a cozy traditional New Mexican Kiva fireplace in the corner.
The world-class service you’d expect from a Rosewood property manifests in unexpected thoughtful touches. I arrived from an afternoon hiking through the craggy lunar landscapes of Ghost Ranch before check-in, and as I rushed to dinner, left my iPhone charger and dusty sunglasses strewn on the bedside table. When I returned back from dinner, the cables were tidily tied up, a lens wipe placed next to the dusty sunglasses, and a luxury ruched sleep mask set on each pillow. The sincerity of service that Anasazi delivered was faultless throughout.
Patrick Gray
Patrick Gray
Food and drink
The Anasazi Restaurant sits at the heart of the property, led by charming head chef Cristian Pontiggia. Several dishes are worth a special mention: A mushroom cappuccino amuse-bouche served in a cup shaped like a face kissing another face inset in the saucer; the (Not a) Caesar Salad, a re-construction of the classic with crunchy iceberg lettuce and a punchy blue cheese; and the potato pavé with Petrossian caviar which melted exquisitely in my mouth. Dessert was a rich chocolate torta with local Chimayo red chiles conceived by the up-and-coming local pastry chef; it readied me for a long slumber.
The discernment extends to the wine list, which Anasazi takes very seriously, with an expansive collection of over 900 wines, curated by the in-house wine director. All wines are stored in the basement wine cellar which doubles as a private dining room that can be reserved for intimate gatherings. The wines are of the highest caliber, with Champagnes such as Pol Roger’s “Sir Winston Churchill” cuvée to the Nino Negri Sfursat 5 Stelle, a sultry Lombardian red wine from the vineyard across from the house where chef Cristian Pontiggia was raised.
Back on ground level, The Patio is a more relaxed bar straddling the entrance of the restaurant. Come in the afternoon for a convivial atmosphere when they serve their signature margaritas to locals and visitors alike.
Patrick Gray
Patrick Gray
Amenities and experiences
The name Anasazi refers to the Native American civilization that once inhabited the Four Corners region, renowned for their cliff dwellings and recognized as the ancestors of today's Pueblo peoples. The Inn of the Anasazi remains rooted in the local Santa Fe community. Managing director Lutz Arnhold serves as a board member for the
Santa Fe Farmers’ Market
, a must-visit on Saturdays.
Anasazi offers a complimentary car service, as well as free bikes, so you can easily immerse in the cultural scene around town. Take a gentle cycle up to the gallery-lined Canyon Road for an afternoon of whimsical art, or the more modern galleries like
Site Santa Fe
, whilst of course there is the famous
Georgia O’Keefe Museum
. Santa Fe also offers endless shopping and right next door is
Shiprock
which is a good starting point, with their remarkable selection of native ceramics, jewelry, and textiles.
There is no spa or pool on-site, but the hotel's car service can whisk guests up to the serenity of
Ten Thousand Waves
for a soak amongst the fresh green mountains above town.
Insider tip
The warm welcome and care extends to every aspect of the hospitality that the Anasazi offers. Sit back and enjoy.
