## Gourmet moments

For those who crave a taste of FAUCHON, FAUCHON L’Hotel Kyoto offers endless opportunities to experience ultimate culinary pleasures at any time of the day or evening, and in any place you choose. FAUCHON Meets Kyoto on the palate – see, taste, smell, and feel the delights of FAUCHON’s artisan chefs who masterfully marry French gourmet innovation with Japanese twists, spices, and fusions through our food, beverages, and sweet treats.

### Grand Café FAUCHON

An impressive and stunning space on the top floor of FAUCHON L’Hotel Kyoto, The Grand Cafe FAUCHON invites diners to enjoy a rare panoramic view of Higashiyama. The design is as imaginative as FAUCHON’s pastry creations featuring impressive lighting descending from the ceiling, instantly creating a backdrop as mesmerizing as the scenery outside the windows. Welcome to the world of FAUCHON where the chefs’ elegantly designed and tastefully fashioned casual French cuisine offer a dining experience that has never before been achieved.

Welcome to Kyoto’s trendy new address for gourmet fare.

### Salon De Thé FAUCHON

The penthouse tea salon Salon de Thé FAUCHON with the view of the ancient capital, Kyoto city awaits in all of its magnificence. Enjoy a truly unique afternoon tea experience in an elegant space with a sophisticated design, and signature FAUCHON style. Forget the cares of daily life with a compelling selection of FAUCHON brand teas and creative pastries and sweets which are sure to delight. It’s time for ultimate indulgences once again.

### Le Bar FAUCHON

Le Bar FAUCHON is THE place to sip and savor. Come relax after a hard day’s work or sightseeing while watching the sunset paint the city of Kyoto in brilliant hues of orange. Either alone, with a special date, co-workers, or friends, Le Bar FAUCHON is a wonderful spot to relax with FAUCHON’s signature tea cocktails, a La Delicieuse beer, fine wine, or a glass of FAUCHON Champagne. Kanpai!

### Pâtisserie & Boutique FAUCHON

The facade of the FAUCHON Patisserie and Boutique beckons passersby inside to indulge in something wonderful. Revered throughout the world, FAUCHON is synonymous with its signature pastries, savory delicacies, and other iconic sweet and savory treats. The jewel-like pastries displayed in the showcase and gourmet boutique of carefully selected Fauchon products draw visitors from around the world. This is the place to find FAUCHON’s incredible macarons and fruit-flavored teas which are imported directly from Paris and only available at this address.
