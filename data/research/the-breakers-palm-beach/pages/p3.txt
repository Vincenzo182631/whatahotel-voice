## Into the _blue_

Four pools, six whirlpool spas, a name inspired by the waves—we have a certain infatuation with water.

Inside, The Breakers stuns with its blend of old and new—historic architecture modernized with finesse. Outside, our resort captures the splendor of Palm Beach with equal measure. Stroll down the Ponce Promenade, past the Ocean Lawn, and enter The Beach Club—a private outdoor oasis: a series of four glittering pools, dotted by restaurants, bars, chaise lounges and chic bungalows.

Your luxury beachfront experience includes:

- Expansive Main Pool
- Infinity-style Adult Pool, offering a more serene experience for guests 21 and older
- Zero-entry Active Pool, ideal for families
- Infinity-style South Pool, home to many of our private bungalows
- Six relaxing whirlpool spas
- Private beach
- A collection of poolside bungalows available for daytime rental

## Watersports

Up for an adventure? Enjoy paddleboarding and kayaking—plus, tubes and boogie boards, all just beyond The Breakers’ back door.
