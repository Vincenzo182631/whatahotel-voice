## High _court_

Fringed with palm trees and meticulously kept, our gorgeous grounds transform the game into something more.

Tennis, pickleball and padel—served The Breakers way. Our resort offers guests 16 state-of-the-art courts, a comprehensive Pro Shop with the latest fashions and equipment, as well as clinics and private sessions with certified professional instructors.

* * *

Emerging from a full-scale renovation in December 2024, the 104,000-square-foot complex showcases open-air tennis, pickleball and padel courts.

### Meet the new Golf & Racquet Club courts:

#### Tennis

- Four CapillaryFlow hydroponic grass courts incorporate underground irrigation and oxygen control, maintaining optimal growing and playing conditions, while achieving water savings of up to 85% - this is the first installation worldwide of this cutting-edge court technology
- Six clay hydro courts with a subsurface hydration system produce a consistent playing surface and water savings of 50%
- Two cushioned hardtop courts feature a more durable, softer surface and blended lines for combined tennis/pickleball play

#### Pickleball & PADEL

- Two dedicated pickleball courts
- Two dedicated padel courts

#### Featured amenities

- Shaded Structures: Six open-air structures sustain cover and protection from the sun and sub-tropical climate; situated above four tennis and two pickleball courts.
- Stretching Deck: Designated area with equipment and accessories for warming up and cooling down.
- LED Lighting: Allows for evening play while using half the energy consumption.
- Comfort Stations: Situated adjacent to every court, these open-air, shaded pergolas offer a refreshing, courtside retreat and a selection of premium amenities.

- The Veranda: A refined retreat for players and spectators, this reception, observation and lounge area encourages guests to relax, socialize and watch from above.
