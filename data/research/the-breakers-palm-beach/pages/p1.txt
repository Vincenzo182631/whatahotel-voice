## The Shops at The Breakers

Just beyond the grand lobby, you’ll find a quaint stretch of storefronts that surround the picturesque Palm Courtyard. Featuring some of the world’s most recognized and pursued brands, our boutiques bring the best of Palm Beach shopping to the hotel. From high fashion and cosmetics to children’s apparel and gourmet treats, The Shops at The Breakers have everything you want—and some things you didn’t know you needed.

### Absolutely Suitable

Daily: 10 AM – 6 PM

One visit here and you'll know who's who in swimwear. Thoroughly modern and utterly cool, Absolutely Suitable features a large, sculptural wave protruding from the ceiling and an artful floor that mimics the sidewalks of Rio de Janeiro. Visit to find apparel, swimwear, and accessories from a long list of European and American designers, including women's swim styles by SHAN and Karla Colletto; men's swimwear from Peter Millar and Vilebrequin, and sunglasses by Oliver Peoples, Tom Ford and more.

### Guerlain

Daily: 10 AM – 6 PM

Rare, iconic, incomparable—Guerlain’s legacy in luxury fragrance, skincare and makeup is second to none. Founded in 1828, the legendary French beauty brand operates only a handful of storefronts in the United States, including its stunning boutique at The Breakers. Explore a colorful spectrum of cosmetics, or peruse the label’s capsule of exquisite fragrances, such as L'Art & La Matière - an exclusive collection of 21 Eaux de Parfum that presents a unique customization experience. For the skin, work with a personal consultant to create a custom regimen, incorporating products like the coveted Orchidée Impériale - a molecular extract from a flower known for its extraordinary longevity.

### Mary Lily's

Daily: 11 AM – 9 PM

Here to make your stay even sweeter. Mary Lily’s offers a delightful array of indulgences for kids of all ages—including a selection of Häagen-Dazs ice cream flavors. Plus, enjoy specialty chocolates and candies. Offering a touch of nostalgia, the shop's name honors Mary Lily Kenan, wife of The Breakers' founder Henry Morrison Flagler.

### Match at The Breakers®

Daily: 10 AM – 6 PM

A perfect complement to Mix, Match features a thoughtful, fashion-forward edit of footwear—from easy flats and sandals to chic wedges and espadrilles. For something more formal, slip into heels from a slew of sought-after designers, such as Aquazzura, Alexandre Birman, Loeffler Randall and Veronica Beard. Plus, enjoy menswear from designers such as Ferragamo, SWIMS and OluKai.

### Mix at The Breakers®

Daily: 10 AM – 6 PM

Embrace the power of the perfect accessory: a striking necklace that elevates a simple white top, or an artisan bracelet that keeps you in vacation mode long after the trip is over. This chic boutique is your destination for unique, artful jewelry and accessories from labels like Lana, Phillips House, Sydney Evan, Gas Bijoux and Monica Rich Kosann. Mix, match, then mix some more.

### News & Gourmet

Daily: 6 AM – 9 PM

With an ever-changing selection, News & Gourmet is a whimsical café and emporium you’ll never tire of visiting. In the morning, go for the paper, artisanal coffee—including cold brew and Seattle-style pour over—and freshly-baked pastries. Mid-day, it’s perfect for quick, light bites, offering salads, sandwiches and snacks to enjoy in the adjacent Palm Courtyard. Beyond the café, you’ll find a well-curated collection of gifts and unique decorative accessories from around the world. Pick up sundries or peruse an array of greeting cards and best-selling novels.

### Polo Ralph Lauren

Daily: 10 AM – 6 PM

What to wear? Turn to the purveyor of classic American style, focused on elegant yet relaxed fashions for men and women. Our Polo Ralph Lauren boutique features the season’s newest pieces from Polo, including luxury apparel, on-trend accessories, footwear, leather goods, eyewear and signature fragrances.

### Polo Ralph Lauren Kids

Daily: 10 AM – 6 PM

Classic American style isn’t just for adults. Designed to reflect the timeless heritage and modern spirit of Ralph Lauren’s collections for men and women, the new kids' store offers the season’s newest pieces for boys, girls and baby, including signature apparel, outerwear, sneakers and accessories.

### Signature Shop

Daily: 10 AM – 6 PM

Sport The Breakers’ stamp of luxury with a selection of apparel, athleisure wear, accessories and décor accented with our logo. This boutique is your go-to for tees, tote bags, hats, glassware, luxury towels and more. You’ll also find our much-loved Sea Glass line of body and hair care products—the same ones provided in our guest rooms and suites—and the world famous Acqua di Parma fragrance collection.

### Sports & Beach Essentials

In addition to our luxury boutiques surrounding the Palm Courtyard, discover The Breakers’ other shops located throughout the resort.

### Beach Shop

Daily: 9 AM – 5 PM

Found in the heart of The Beach Club on the main pool deck, this go-to store is open daily for resort guests’  everyday needs. Visit The Beach Shop for sunny day essentials like sunscreen, sunglasses, hats, flip flops, beach toys, non-alcoholic beverages and snacks.

### Sports Pro Shop

Daily: 7 AM – 6 PM

Stroll down The Breakers’ palm-dotted drive to the golf and racquet clubhouse - home to this luxury shop - and browse an extensive selection of performance sportswear and accessories for the links and courts. Collections include Polo Golf, Greyson, Peter Millar, Sport Haley, Tory Sport, lululemon and more.

Need equipment too? Ask an associate for help selecting clubs, racquets or extras like footwear and accessories.

## Via Flagler by The Breakers

Blending Palm Beach chic and island ease, Via Flagler is a charming alfresco plaza that features a curated collection of boutiques, eateries, galleries and more. Set among swaying palms, lush courtyards and Mediterranean-style architecture, this is the perfect place to shop, dine and unwind. Located on Royal Poinciana Way—just a short stroll from The Breakers.

### Main Street by The Breakers®

Daily: 6:30 AM - 6 PM

223 Royal Poinciana Way, Palm Beach, FL 33480

(561) 659-8571

Part café, part gallery, Main Street offers coffee, donuts and baked goods, a selection of Häagen-Dazs ice cream and a collection of gifts, decor and more. Building on Henry Flagler’s legacy, Main Street resides on the town’s original thoroughfare in the heart of Palm Beach - known today as Royal Poinciana Way. Swing by for a cup of our signature blend coffee, grab a smoothie or donut and shop our array of unique items.

### SHAN

Tuesday thru Sunday: 10 AM - 6 PM

Located in the Via Flagler courtyard

225 Royal Poinciana Way, Suite 1, Palm Beach, FL 33480

(561) 659-8570

This collaboration with one of Canada’s most iconic fashion brands showcases innovative swimwear and ready-to-wear clothing, as well as accessories for men and women. All of SHAN’s products combine superior quality with unique, avant-garde design and are made by professional craftswomen in Canada, using fabrics from renowned Italian and French textile houses.

All store hours subject to change.
