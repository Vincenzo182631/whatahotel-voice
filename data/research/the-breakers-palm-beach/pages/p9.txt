## For the Whole Family

From a child’s first swing on the green to carefree days spent splashing on the shore, the most meaningful moments at The Breakers are those shared together. With a private beachfront, four pools, two golf courses and a world‑class racquet center, every experience is thoughtfully designed to bring families closer and create lasting memories.

Explore an array of offerings for every age and interest:

- Daily resort activities
- Seaside Family Entertainment Center (8 AM — 6 PM \| Bungalows 1-5) offering an exciting selection of activities and events for children ages 12 and under: Gamer’s Bay, Cabanas, Pirate’s Nook, Craft House and Toddler Cove
- Gulfstream Arcade (8 AM — 11 PM \| Gulfstream 5) featuring classic and contemporary games
- Lessons and clinics for golf, tennis, pickleball and padel
- Watersports such as paddle boarding, kayaking, boogie boards and tubes
- Signature events like movie nights, trivia, bingo and more
- Holiday carnivals
