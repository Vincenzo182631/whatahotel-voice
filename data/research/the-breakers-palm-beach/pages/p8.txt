## Our private Beach Club just got more private—and more luxurious.

An island-chic retreat, our bungalows are the perfect daytime escape.

Outfitted with beach-inspired furniture for endless relaxation, our collection of bungalows range from 300 to 400 square feet and are ideal for up to six guests.

Complete with a picturesque ocean view, our poolside bungalows include:

- Dedicated concierge to meet your every need
- Lounge area with living room furniture and dining table
- Full bathroom with luxury amenities and shower
- Refrigerator stocked with complimentary non-alcoholic beverages
- Exclusive food and beverage service
- Flatscreen TV
- Complimentary WiFi
- Customized amenities available upon request
