# Unique 3-Day Hangzhou Itinerary

Save your energy crafting your own itinerary. You can explore this enchanting place with a unique 3-day Hangzhou sightseeing tour. You can take a ride on a sculling boat around the Xixi Wetland to see the Lingyin temple and the Baochu Pagoda. Tour the West Lake Classic, with authentic villages, boat trips and temples.

## Day 1

Morning

Begin the day with a breakfast buffet at Waterlight Court before boarding a sculling boat to visit the Lingyin Temple, the oldest Buddhist temple in Hangzhou, dating all the way back to the 4th century.

Afternoon

Return to the resort to enjoy a scrumptious lunch at Bai Yun. Afterwards, you can relax in your room or check out the hotel's beautifully decorated library where you can peruse a wide selection of classical books.

Then, we take a walk over to the Xixi National Wetland Park and have afternoon tea on a traditional sculling boat - an hour cruise on the lake.

Evening

Treat yourself to an indulgent spa session to unwind from the day's jampacked activities.

## Day 2

Morning

Start the day with a yoga session at the yoga pavilion and follow it with a buffet breakfast at Waterlight Court. Take a trip down to Hefang Street Hangzhou for a relaxing stroll and tour of historical buildings, stopping by one of the local restaurants for lunch.

Afternoon

Travel on to West Lake for some sightseeing. Take a boat trip to cruise on the West Lake

Evening

Have dinner in the area around West Lake. Cap the night with a spectacular Impression West Lake Show, before returning to the resort for a good night's sleep.

## Day 3

Morning

Wake up to a hearty and energizing breakfast in the comfort of your room and spend a lazy morning exploring the Xixi National Wetland Park.

Afternoon

After lunch, take a trip over to Longjing Tea Village for a tea-making and tea-tasting experience.
Return to the resort and go swimming in the indoor pool, or indulge in another spa session.

Evening

End the day with one of Banyan Tree Hangzhou’s unique Destination Dining experiences.
