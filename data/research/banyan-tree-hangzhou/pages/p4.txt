# Water Terrace Suite – King

## Relax in a spacious suite with views of the wetlands

Overlooking the still waters and lush greenery of Xixi, this Hangzhou hotel suite welcomes guests to a spacious retreat immersed in the quiet charm of the wetlands. A warm fireplace and a spacious balcony to admire the beautiful Xixi Wetlands makes this luxury Hangzhou suite special. Large windows and the private balcony draw attention to the changing scenery outside, allowing guests to feel closely connected to nature throughout their stay. Enjoy the large living area, the comfortable king-size bed, a jumbo deep bathtub – and of course the gorgeous wetland views. Designed for rest and slower living, the suite combines generous space with understated elegance in one of Hangzhou’s most peaceful settings. Ideal for couples seeking an expansive living space with premium amenities, or those with an appreciation for the unfiltered beauty of nature.
