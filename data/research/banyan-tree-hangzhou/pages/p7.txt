# Destination Dining

Take your romantic dining experience to the lake, with our signature Destination Dining. Let us take you on a private love boat dinner, where we will prepare and serve exquisite local cuisine amidst the spectacular nature.

## Afternoon Tea Sculling Boat

Relish scrumptious savories and sweets, complemented by fragrant Chinese tea, as you cruise along the pastoral lake on a traditional “Jiang Nan” sculling boat. Melodic birdsong, the rhythmic ripples of the marshland waters and the flourishing greens surrounding this idyllic setting fill the senses.
