# Dining

## Banyan Tree Hangzhou

## Dining in Hangzhou

Dining at Banyan Tree Hangzhou is so much more than eating; it’s an experience. Featuring both local and western food, Banyan Tree Hangzhoe restaurants can cater for all palates. Complemented by the peaceful scenery of the wetlands, each Xixi hotel restaurant at Banyan Tree Hangzhou brings about a distinct dining experience shaped by local culture and seasonal flavours.

Traditional Hangzhou cuisine, international favourites, and intimate destination dining experiences come together within elevated settings. Guests may enjoy leisurely meals overlooking serene waterways, afternoon tea aboard a sculling boat, or private villa dining designed for quiet evenings in. Across all venues, considerate hospitality and delightful dishes bring the slower pace and natural beauty of Xixi closer to you.

Elevate your Hangzhou dining experience.
