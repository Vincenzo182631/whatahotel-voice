## Spa Treatments

### Sense of Place - Banyan Balance

Inspired by our poetic city, this experience harnesses locally-sourced Longjing tea, also known as the tea of emperors. Immerse yourself in body treats, a healing bath, and a rejuvenating massage.

### Thai Traditions

Allow heated Thai herbs to soothe your body and alleviate tension, soak in an uplifting ginger bath, and gently enhance ﬂexibility while nurturing your inner glow.

### Chinese Traditions

A massage technique used for centuries, Tui Na corrects the ﬂow of Qi in the body. Restore harmony and balance through this powerful practice, and enjoy complete relaxation.

## Wellbeing Activities

Stretch, sweat, and breathe into presence with impactful activities designed to awaken and restore. Influenced by our 8 Pillars of Wellbeing, our calendar of activities — including baduanjin, biking, yoga and more — are an expression of harmony between self and nature.

Activity Calendar Book now

### Wetland Bike Ride

Embrace nature through a leisurely cycle around Xixi Wetland. Take in the refreshing negative air ions, relieve pressure, and regulate both body and mind in a tranquil setting.

### Baduanjin

Baduanjin is one of China’s oldest health practices, with origins tracing back over 800 years. Restore balance in an accessible, gentle way.

### Yoga

Encounter ancient knowledge through yoga, a practice that cultivates unity and balance through breath, movement, and meditation. This gentle journey invites you to reconnect with yourself and restore harmony from within.
