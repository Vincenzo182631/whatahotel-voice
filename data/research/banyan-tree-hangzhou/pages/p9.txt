### Library

With Xixi’s illustrious history of attracting writers, we have put in place an extensive library at our Hangzhou resort. The library has an excellent selection of classic novels, and features retro décor including an antique typewriter and a vintage phonograph.

Opening Hours: 24 hours

### Team Building at the outdoor MICE venue

Accommodating up to 150 people, the outdoor Mice venue provides the perfect backdrop for every type of company event or teambuilding activity. It is available all year round and is a charmingly fresh open-air setting during the spring and autumn months.

Opening Hours: 24 hours

### Banyan Tree Gallery

Bring home the little touches that make Banyan Tree unique. With an enchanting collection of handicrafts, resort apparel and signature spa amenities, you will find every item imaginable to enjoy your Banyan Tree experience even after you’ve returned home.

Opening Hours: 09:00 am - 09:00 pm

### Unforgettable Romance in the Wetland Garden

The Wetland Garden perfectly captures Xixi Wetland Park's uniquely lush ambience. This garden provides stunning views of flourishing greenery and a limpidly flowing stream.

Opening Hours: 24 hours
