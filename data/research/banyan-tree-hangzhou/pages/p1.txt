## Discover the Xixi Wetlands

Enter into an authentic water village set in the picturesque Xixi Wetlands Nature Reserve - a Hangzhou haven found near the west lake.

Hidden within the tranquil waterways of Xixi Wetlands, this 5-star spa resort in Xixi is where one comes to find a rare sense of seclusion while remaining close to the cultural heart of Hangzhou. Traditional Jiangnan architecture and serene water village surroundings create an atmosphere that captures the region’s heritage. Throughout the resort, nature becomes part of everyday life, not just as simple backdrops. Quiet canals, lush greenery, and seasonal wildlife shape the rhythm of each stay with ease.

Immerse yourself in the lush greenery and tranquil atmosphere of the park. Enjoy it all from the quiet comfort of Banyan Tree Hangzhou hotel, a luxury spa resort within the Wetlands Park. Whether exploring nearby temples and pagodas or simply slowing down beside the wetlands, guests are invited into an experience defined by privacy and subtle elegance.

##### Are there any popular attractions near Banyan Tree Hangzhou?

The spa resort is conveniently situated near several popular attractions, including:

- Xixi Wetland: This scenic spot in Hangzhou is just a 5-minute walk from the resort.
- West Lake: The hotel is near the west lake, approximately 6.5 km from one of the most famous attractions in Hangzhou.
Lingyin Temple: The oldest Buddhist temple in Hangzhou, dating all the way back to the 4th century, is around 5.5 km away from the resort.
- Lingyin Temple: The oldest Buddhist temple in Hangzhou, dating all the way back to the 4th century, is around 5.5 km away from the resort.
- The Grand Canal: Known as the longest and largest ancient canal in the world, the Grand Canal is situated about 8 km from the resort.

##### Can Banyan Tree Hangzhou help arrange activities like city tours or visits to cultural sites?

At Banyan Tree Hangzhou, our dedicated concierge team is more than happy to assist you in arranging various activities, including city tours and visits to cultural sites. If you need more information, contact our concierge team at +86 571 8586 0000 or hangzhou@banyantree.com.

##### How far is Banyan Tree Hangzhou from the Hangzhou city centre?

We are conveniently situated just 7 km away from the city centre of Hangzhou, China.

##### How far is Banyan Tree Hangzhou from Hangzhou Railway Station?

The Hangzhou Railway Station is 20 km away from away from the resort, an approximately 45-minute drive.

##### How far is Banyan Tree Hangzhou from Hangzhou Xiaoshan International Airport?

The resort is about 43 km away from Hangzhou Xiaoshan International Airport (approx. 1-hour drive).
