# Things to Do in Hangzhou

Make the most of your trip with these exhilirating things to do at the Xixi Wetlands. Prepare to fall in love with the natural beauty of Hangzhou.

## A Journey to Longjing Tea Village

Longjing tea is the best and most famous tea in Hangzhou, and one of the top ten most famous teas in China. In Spring, you can journey to the Longjing Tea Village, which is a wide expanse of lush greenery - a plantation resembling a stairway to heaven. There you can pick tea leaves and stir-dry them with local tea farmers. At the end of your tour of this delicate tea making process, you can have a taste of authentic Longjing tea yourself.

Time: From March to May

## Xixi Wetland Kayak

Enjoy the unique scenery of Jiangnan Water Town of Xixi through a kayak expedition with your family or significant other. Challenge them to a race or take a chill kayak ride to appreciate the beauty of nature around you.

Time: Daily 09:00 am - 03:00 pm

## Fisherman's Journey

Take a sculling boat to fish with local boatmen and pick vegetables. Enjoy your lunch cooked with the fish, shrimp, and vegetables you picked. After lunch, you can resume your tour of Xixi by boat.

Time: Daily 09:00 am - 04:00 pm
