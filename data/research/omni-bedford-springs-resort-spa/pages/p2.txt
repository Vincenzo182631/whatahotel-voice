## Bedford, Pennsylvania

### Experience our historic Pennsylvania resort

* * *

### Travel + Leisure World's Best Awards 2024 Winner

* * *

Located in the Allegheny Mountains of Pennsylvania, Omni Bedford Springs Resort & Spa is the perfect place to reconnect. Our Bedford, PA resort is home to many “firsts.” We are home to the first indoor swimming pool, one of the country’s first golf courses and spa treatments inspired by the natural healing of springs first used by Native Americans. Guest rooms and suites embody the heritage of Pennsylvania coupled with modern amenities and expansive balconies. Six welcoming indoor and outdoor dining venues offer the finest local produce and signature drinks, served alongside spectacular views from our Bedford resort. Guests can also explore the local area easily, with downtown Bedford on your doorstep and a variety of historic tours walking distance.

## Traveler's Insights

### What are the property amenities at Omni Bedford Springs Resort & Spa?

Omni Bedford Springs Resort & Spa is home to world-class amenities including:

- Two resort-style indoor and outdoor pools
- A full-service spa
- 18 hole golf course
- A full-sized tennis court
- A fully-equipped fitness center
- Biking leisure trails and outdoor activities
- 20,000 square feet of meeting and event space
- Seven dining outlets - four full-service restaurants, a coffee shop, a lobby bar and a pool grill
- Family-friendly resort activities
- Self-and valet parking included in your resort fees

### Where is Omni Bedford Springs Resort & Spa located?

Omni Bedford Springs Resort & Spa is located in South Pennsylvania’s scenic Cumberland Valley, about 90 minutes from Pittsburgh, two hours from Washington, D.C., and three hours from Philadelphia. The resort's physical address is 2138 Business 220, Bedford, Pennsylvania 15522

### What is the closest airport to Omni Bedford Springs Resort & Spa?

Omni Bedford Springs Resort & Spa is located near the following airports:

- Pittsburgh International Airport (PIT)

- Harrisburg International Airport (MDT)

- Reagan National Airport (DCA)

- Dulles International Airport (IAD)

- Baltimore/Washington International (BWI)

Pittsburgh, Harrisburg, Dulles, Reagan and Baltimore airports are all within a three-hour drive of the resort.

Bedford County Airport (HMZ) is 4 miles from Omni Bedford Springs Resort and serves private aircraft only.

### How much is parking at Omni Bedford Springs Resort & Spa?

Valet parking is included in the resort service charge. Please allow our professional staff to valet your vehicle at the resort main entrance. Self-parking is also available on the resort property. Electric Charging Stations are provided on a first-come, first-served basis.

### What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 4 PM and check-out is 11 AM. Guests must be 21 years of age to book a room.

### Is Omni Bedford Springs Resort & Spa pet friendly?

Dogs are not only welcomed but also pampered at the Omni Bedford Springs Resort & Spa. Our expert chefs have created a special menu just for your canine companion with delectable selections no dog can resist. For details on the menu offered as well as your dog's stay, please see below. A non-refundable cleaning fee of $200 + tax will be charged per stay. Pet-friendly rooms are available only in the Anderson House. Luxury spa rooms or suites are not pet-friendly.
