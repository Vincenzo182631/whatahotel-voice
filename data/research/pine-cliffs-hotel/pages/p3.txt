# Exceptional restaurants in Albufeira

## Dining

At Pine Cliffs Hotel, a Luxury Collection Resort, a world of flavors awaits you. With 15 restaurants and bars throughout the resort, dining becomes an unforgettable experience. Savor contemporary and authentic...See More cuisine, inspired by the terroir and local culture, or explore some of the world's richest culinary traditions. There is a cuisine and ambiance to please every guest: from Algarve’s traditional seafood specialties at Pescador restaurant, to a casual family-style buffet at O’Grill, to light and healthy snacks at Zest and succulent steaks at Piri Piri Steakhouse. The restaurants and bars at Pine Cliffs Hotel, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve are invitations to discover the best cuisine from Portugal and around the world. Restaurants and bars are subject to seasonal opening, please consult our Concierge on the daily openings: concierge.algarve@pinecliffs.com or +351 289 500 100See Less

Dining Hours
