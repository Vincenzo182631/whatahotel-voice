# Exhilarating Activities at Our Portugal Resort

## Experiences

Nestled between the mountains and the sea, the Algarve is one of Portugal's most enchanting and welcoming regions. Known for its endless beaches, warm waters and for being the sunniest destination in Europe,...See More this little paradise offers far more surprises than first meets the eye, and its mild Mediterranean climate makes it a year-round delight. Boasting one of Europe’s most stunning coastlines in Europe, with a remarkable variety as you travel along the shore, the Algarve invites you to discover and savor its many layers at your own pace. Strategically located near the beach, Pine Cliffs Hotel, a Luxury Collection Resort, is the perfect starting point to get to know an array of unique places, such as the Fishermen Village of Albufeira, Sagres Fortress, Ria Formosa Natural Park or Monchique Springs. Visitors will be delighted with the wonders of the region and upon arrival at the resort can enjoy the numerous facilities and activities available for all preferences.See Less

### Practice Your Swing at This Algarve Beach Resort

Pine Cliffs Hotel is a golf resort in Portugal that takes advantage of its privileged setting in Albufeira, and turns your golf break into a unique and unforgettable experience. Extended across 168 acres of naturally beautiful land along the Southern Portugal coastline, Pine Cliffs is set above a stunning cliff top with a golden sand beach and the deep blue water of the Atlantic Ocean lying underneath,...See More making it one of the most popular Algarve family resorts. The dark red of Albufeira iconic sandstone cliffs contrasts sharply with the immaculate green of the 9-hole golf course. ## Annabel Croft Tennis Academy

The Annabel Croft Tennis Academy features five floodlit courts—two clay and three all-weather hard courts—ideal for day or nighttime play. Located on the beautifully landscaped grounds of the Pine Cliffs Hotel, the ATP standard courts are managed by our two full-time tennis pros along with friendly staff happy to arrange matches for solo players as well as fun clinics to guests of all ages and skill levels. The courts are walking distance from the hotel's main building; although a hotel tram is also available. Court time must be pre-booked with The Luxury Collection Concierge. Racket and balls are also available for hire. The Annabel Croft Tennis Academy team offers players a range of tennis services from court hire and racquet rental to coaching courses and tournaments. The Annabel Croft Tennis Academy is one of Europe’s premier tennis coaching programmes. The Annabel Croft Tennis Academy is open daily from 9am to 5pm (this may vary depending on season and weather).

## Explore Algarve

With a rich history and stunning beaches, Algarve captivates its visitors and offers countless unique places to explore.Along Portugal's southernmost shores, the picture-postcard region known as the Algarve captivates travellers with its dramatic red sandstone cliffs marching toward an imperial blue Atlantic Ocean. Renowned for its gorgeous beaches, warm waters, and exceptional golfing, the Algarve's year-round sunshine makes it an ideal holiday retreat. With a rich history dating back thousands of years to the Phoenicians and Carthaginians, it was the arrival of the Arabic Moors in the eighth century that would have the most significant and lasting influence on the Algarve—the traces of which are seen today in the whitewashed architecture, narrow streets and flower gardens typical throughout the region.

Learn More

- The Luxury Collection Concierge recommends the following as unique places to explore, all accessible by car or organised tour:

- Explore the medieval village of Silves—from the impressive walls of its Moorish castle to the Ponte Romana Bridge recalling the presence of the Romans.
- Journey to Monchique, the highest point in the Algarve, nestled between the mountains of Foia and Picota and offering spectacular panoramas of rolling hills and the ocean beyond. Famed for its thermal spas, an exploration of its steep cobble-stoned streets leads to ancient churches, monastaries, and the small doorways of local artisans.
- Discover Alte, an extremely pretty village amid unspoilt and hilly countryside. Noted for its whitewashed architecture, narrow streets, gardens filled with flowers, and hanging baskets, Alte is typical of the Algarve region.

- Portuguese is the national language spoken throughout the Algarve, although most locals will also speak some English. The Euro is the accepted currency and most stores and restaurants take all major credit cards and European debit cards. There is a shopping complex just 15 kilometres from the hotel, accessible by car, which is open daily from 10am to 11pm.
