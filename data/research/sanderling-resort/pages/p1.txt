**November 13-15**

Throughout this intimate three-day experience, guests will learn from acclaimed artists **Molly Reeder** and **Claire McCarty,** whose workshops celebrate the natural beauty of the coast. Individual workshops are available for purchase.

### Bird Watching

Explore the skies and shores of the Outer Banks with our complimentary birdwatching program. Guests can borrow binoculars, guidebooks, and journals to spot and document the region’s unique birdlife on the ocean, sound, and resort grounds.

Materials to be requested at the Front Desk.

### Bicycles

Peddle your way around The Sanderling Resort and the town of Duck. Complimentary Bikes available to all resort guests on a first come first served basis and can be requested at the Front Desk.

### Board Games & Puzzles

Continue Family Game Night on vacation! The Sanderling Resort offers popular card and board games such as Scrabble, Connect 4, Backgammon & more in our Beach House Loft.

### Coastal Painting Kits

Guests are invited to slow down and tap into their creativity during a relaxing self guided painting experience inspired by the surrounding coastal landscape. Stop by the front desk to gather a canvas, paint palette & brushes to interpret the colors and textures of the shoreline in your own unique way.
