## A Retreat Designed Around You

The retreat includes mahjong for experienced players at every level, with optional strategy sessions and open social play throughout the weekend. Welcome receptions, a cocktail and mocktail experience, and cozy evening gatherings are all built in for real conversation and connection.

There’s also plenty of time for spa visits, beach walks, boutique shopping, and resort relaxation. Shared meals are part of the weekend as well, including a hosted lunch and a celebration dinner.

## The Weekend

Four days of mahjong, connection, and rest, from a welcoming arrival evening to a farewell brunch. In between, strategy sessions and open play sit alongside spa time, beach walks, and evening gatherings, so every day offers a mix of tiles and time to unwind.

## Welcome & Arrival

Thursday, January 14

Arrive, check in, and settle into your accommodations. The evening opens with a Welcome Reception, complete with heavy hors d’oeuvres, wine, beer and mocktails, followed by casual play at The Mahj Lounge. Enjoy light social play and a chance to get acquainted — the focus is simply on meeting new people, enjoying conversation, and beginning the weekend together.

## Learn, Play & Explore

Friday, January 15

Enjoy a breakfast buffet followed by your choice of a Mahjong Strategy Session or Open Social Play at The Mahj Lounge, then spend the afternoon between the Mahj Lounge, the spa, local shopping, or simply resting. The day winds down with an interactive Cocktail and Mocktail Experience and an evening at “PJs & Pungs,” a cozy gathering with mahjong, dessert, and coffee in casual loungewear.

## Play Your Way

Saturday, January 16

The morning begins with a casual breakfast followed by a second round of strategy sessions or open play, a hosted group lunch, and an afternoon of themed games, raffles, and free time. The evening builds to a Celebration Dinner and “Sweet Endings,” featuring a champagne toast (or sparkling non-alcoholic alternative), dessert, and one final night of play.

## The Mahj Lounge

The social center of the retreat. The Mahj Lounge is staffed during scheduled windows throughout the weekend, offering a space for open play and connection without requiring a full-time commitment.

## Expert Instruction with Let’s Mahj RVA

Your choice of strategy session with expert instructors from Let’s Mahj RVA. Emilie Rabke and Lauren Wilson are the women behind Let’s Mahj, a Richmond, Virginia–based mahjong community built on a simple belief: the game brings us to the table, but the connection around it keeps us coming back.

Let’s Mahj has grown into a thriving community of more than 250 women gathering each week for lessons, club play, and special events. Together, Emilie and Lauren create welcoming experiences where women can learn, laugh, connect, and feel like they have a seat at the table.

At Sanderling, they’re bringing that same spirit to the coast—mahjong, strategy, beautiful meals, plenty of laughter, and time to simply exhale.
