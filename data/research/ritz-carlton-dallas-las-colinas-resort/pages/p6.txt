Golf at The Ritz-Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas
Experience great conditions at our golf resort near Dallas
With two 18-hole championship golf courses near Dallas, custom club fittings, personalized PGA-certified instruction, and an expansive pro shop, The Ritz-Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas offers an unrivaled experience for golfers of every skill level. You will enjoy every twist and turn from tee to green while appreciating the pristine conditions of TPC Las Colinas, designated a certified Audubon Cooperative Sanctuary by Audubon International; or challenging the rolling terrain at Cottonwood Valley Golf Course. Cottonwood Valley Golf Course tee times are based upon availability and may vary pending maintenance and tournament play.

TPC Las Colinas
Designated a Certified Audubon Cooperative Sanctuary by Audubon International, TPC Las Colinas is an 18-hole, par-70 golf course near Dallas that pairs a dynamic design fit for every level of golfer with world-class conditions, and priceless views of the local vegetation, rolling hills, and curious wildlife—all only steps from our lobby doors.

Cottonwood Valley Golf Course
Discover this 18-hole, par-71 Las Colinas golf course that is exclusive to resort guests and members. The beautifully manicured course spans some of the area’s most scenic rolling terrain in our area, creating a pristine golfing experience. Be sure to make note of the green on the first hole that’s shaped like the state of Texas with a sand trap shaped like Oklahoma.

Pro Shop
The expansive Pro Shop offers a wide range of products to help improve your game and enjoy everything that comes with this challenging sport, including golf clubs, balls, bags, men’s, women’s, and children’s apparel (polo shirts, pants, shorts, skirts, hats, and visors), shoes, accessories (sunglasses, gloves, headcovers, ball markers, etc.) and other golf merchandise.
Private Instruction
Private instruction and clinics can be an absolute game changer when exploring ways to improve your golf game while at The Ritz-Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas. PGA-certified pros at our golf resort near Dallas offer private, personalized instruction designed to take your game to the next level.
Custom Club Fitting
Learn how clubs personalized for you can greatly enhance the way attack the ball. Work directly with the fitters who analyze the high-tech intel from the Foresight Launch Monitor to evaluate carry distance, ball speed, spin, and launch to customize a single club or your entire bag.
