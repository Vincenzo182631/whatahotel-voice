Luxury Accommodations Near Dallas
Signature Villas
Nestled in the rolling hills of Las Colinas, awaits Texas' original luxury resort. Discover premium accommodations that draw inspiration from the destination. Situated on 400 expansive acres, rolling hills, sage trees and blossoming flowers beckon you to experience indulgent comfort. From tower accommodations overlooking TPC Las Colinas to private villas featuring views of plush fairways, a tranquil escape beckons.
Poolside Villa
The Poolside Villa offers a relaxed yet refined retreat just steps from the Resort Pool, perfect for a couples getaway blending effortless access with serene privacy. Choose between a king bed or double queens and enjoy a private patio overlooking the pool, complete with direct entry for sun‑soaked afternoons or quiet moments of escape. A personalized honor bar adds a bespoke touch to your stay, while a dedicated Villa Concierge curates exclusive experiences and coordinates every detail of your visit.
