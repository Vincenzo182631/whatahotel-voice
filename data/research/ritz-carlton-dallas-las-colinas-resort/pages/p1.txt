Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas
Experience Luxury and Sophistication at Our Dallas Resort
Set across 400 acres in the heart of Las Colinas, The Ritz-Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas is a luxury resort near Dallas where contemporary sophistication meets the spirit of Texas. Located minutes from DFW Airport and downtown Dallas, the resort offers an immersive escape with championship golf, elevated dining, wellness experiences, private villas, and resort amenities designed for both leisure and discovery. Guests can choose from thoughtfully appointed guestrooms, suites, and luxury villas overlooking TPC Las Colinas or the resort’s signature pool experience. Home to a 14,000-square-foot spa, curated seasonal programming, and distinctive culinary destinations, The Ritz-Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas redefines the modern Texas resort experience for travelers seeking a luxury getaway in Dallas-Fort Worth.
