The Villas
A secluded retreat within the resort, The Villas at The Ritz‑Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas blend residential comfort with bespoke service. Featuring private patios, expansive living spaces, and dedicated concierge care, each villa is designed for guests seeking a private Dallas staycation or luxury resort getaway - whether a romantic escape, family getaway, or bucket list golf trip.

The Tower Executive Suite
Located in the tower, the Executive Suite is 700 square feet of plush accommodations. Featuring two bathrooms, a dedicated living space, wet bar and sleeping room - the Tower Executive Suite is the perfect suite for a luxury staycation.
Premier Tower Suite
Towering over the Resort Pool and TPC Las Colinas is the Premier Tower Suite. Featuring a corner balcony with patio furniture to lounge, separate living room and dedicated work space, this luxury hotel suite near Dallas is ideal for work and play.
The Ritz-Carlton Suite
Our largest suite, The Ritz-Carlton Suite provides sweeping views of Downtown Las Colinas, TPC Las Colinas and the resort pool. 2,300 square feet of dedicated living, dining, cooking and sleeping space provide plush comforts in the tower. Let gold and blue hues inspired by Texas sweep you to relaxation in our presidential suite near Dallas, Texas.
Guaranteed Early Check-In & Late Check-Out
For those looking to extend their stay at our accommodations in Dallas, The Ritz-Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas is pleased to offer Guaranteed Early Check-In and Late Check-Out. Please note that Guaranteed Early Check-In and Late Check-Out are only available on select dates for the original room type reserved and are only available on standard room types.
