Explore Dallas and Las Colinas
We welcome you to Las Colinas, a tranquil retreat just minutes from Downtown Dallas, DFW International Airport, and Dallas Love Field. Offering convenient access to premier shopping, dining, sports, and cultural attractions throughout Dallas-Fort Worth, Las Colinas serves as an ideal home base for exploring the destination.
Things to Do in Irving, Texas
Mustangs of Las Colinas
Visit Williams Square to witness one of Las Colinas’ most iconic attractions. Designed by sculptor Robert Glen, this pack of galloping bronze mustangs represent the state’s trailblazing spirit, history, and heritage.
Learn More
Northpark Center
Browse through over 200 high-end retail stores, including everything from Apple and Anthropologie to Versace and beyond, in one of the city’s largest shopping centers.
Learn More
AT&T Stadium
Welcome to the home of our Dallas football team, which features a retractable roof. Casual followers and dedicated fans can cheer their team to victory free from the elements or enjoy world-class entertainment when larger-than-life artists come to town.
Learn More
Sixth Floor Museum
The site of the infamous JFK Assassination in 1963, the Sixth Floor Museum located in Dealey Plaza now honors the life and legacy of former President John F. Kennedy through immersive exhibits and engaging events.
Learn More
Dallas Arts District
Spanning 10 blocks of downtown Dallas, the Arts District covers an array of historical and cultural diversions and experiences—including must-see architectural marvels, legendary museums, and acclaimed eateries.
Learn More
Fort Worth Stockyards
Travel back in time and get a glimpse of what the world of the Wild West was like in Fort Worth, from this area’s old-time saloons and authentic entertainment to its period-correct experiences and beyond.
Learn More
