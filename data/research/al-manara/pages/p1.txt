## Al-Manara Beaches

From the golden beaches of Al-Manara to the white sandy beaches of Saraya Beach Club. Live a transformative journey by the majestic Red Sea and experience unparalleled summer days.

The Discovery Journey

### Saraya Beach Club

A luxurious oasis nestled on the shore of The Red Sea where sunsets are magical; The Beach Club located in Saraya Aqaba, by Eagle Hills provides one of a kind experience that exceeds expectations with the highest level of service, dining, and weekly social activities in a comfortable family friendly atmosphere, perfect for those who are looking for excitement and enjoyable moments.

### Saraya Waterpark

Get ready to experience the beauty of our nation in a refreshingly different way, blending the excitement of adventure with the pure joy of aquatic bliss. Dive into Jordan's ultimate aquatic haven, with over 25 thrilling slides and unique experiences, it's the perfect destination for the whole family.

### Amwaj Art Hub

A transformative journey, overlooking the Red Sea. Delve into stories told by Ghassan Abu Laban’s brushstrokes, and Hazem Al-Zubi’s sculptures, to discover how each story becomes a timeless masterpiece at Art Hub by Amwaj. Open daily for your exploration.

## Guided by Discovery

Every stay at Al Manara is guided by discovery. Our concierge team crafts personalized journeys that connect guests to Aqaba’s authentic character and hidden beauty. With their insight and dedication, they turn moments into memories, helping each guest experience the destination in a way that feels personal and meaningful.

Leading the team is Suliman Goussous, Chief Concierge and member of Les Clefs d’Or, the prestigious global association of hotel concierges. One of only five in Jordan to hold this honor, Suliman exemplifies the art of service through thoughtful recommendations, local expertise, and a genuine passion for storytelling. Through his guidance, guests are invited to explore Aqaba beyond its landmarks, uncovering experiences shaped by the culture, people, and traditions that define the destination.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba?

The check-in time at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of د.ا70.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba?

On-site parking is available at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba booking.

What is the closest airport near Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba?

The closest airport near Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba is Aqaba Airport (AQJ). AQJ is located approximately 10.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
