## Dining

At Al Manara, every meal carries the flavor of Jordan. From authentic Mediterranean dishes at Taybeh Al Manara Restaurant to the traditional Levantine warmth of Kubba Levantin Restaurant, where local produce...See More and fresh seafood shine indoors and under the open sky. Amwaj brings the energy with live stations, DJ nights, and sunset views across indoor and outdoor spaces. Wind down at Aqwas Al Manara Lobby Bar, one of the best bars in Aqaba, where Jordanian inspired cocktails and a flavorful menu invite you to linger. Our poolside bar keeps the afternoon simple, with cold drinks and nothing to rush for. Jordan offers unique scenery, fascinating ruins, and thrilling adventures to arrange numerous journeys. However, if time is of the essence and you are seeking to explore the country's most famous sights in one shot, Jordan's Golden Triangle is a perfect choice.
