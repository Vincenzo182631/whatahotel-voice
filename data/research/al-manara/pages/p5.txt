# Treat Yourself to a Massage at Our Hotel Spa in Aqaba

## Experiences

Discover our hotel with a private beach in Aqaba and explore Jordan's Golden Triangle, from the coral reefs of the Red Sea to the landscapes of Wadi Rum and the ancient ruins of Petra. The city's landmarks,...See More including historic sites and a grand mosque, offer a deep insight into Islamic culture and Middle Eastern history. Back at Al Manara, restore at our hotel spa, book a massage in Aqaba, or let the day unfold at our private beach and pool. Our hotel with a pool in Aqaba also offers a fully equipped fitness center with advanced cardio machines and a complete range of weight equipment for a stay that balances rest and movement.See Less

## Dive Into the Waters of Aqaba

Experience a breathtaking variety of colorful hard and soft coral at Aqaba's seaside. Catch the majestic marine life, or the history-rich underwater sites.

## A True World Wonder

The jewel in Petra's crown, visit the treasury after spending a day walking down ancient ruins dating from the 1st Century AD.

## Amwaj Art Hub

A transformative journey, overlooking the Red Sea. Delve into stories told by Ghassan Abu Laban’s brushstrokes, and Hazem Al-Zubi’s sculptures, to discover how each story becomes a timeless masterpiece at Art Hub by Amwaj. Open daily for your exploration.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have a pool on-site?

Yes, Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have an on-site spa?

Yes, Heavenly Spa is located on-site at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba. Click here to book a service now.
