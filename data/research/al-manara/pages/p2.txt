Jordan offers unique scenery, fascinating ruins, and thrilling adventures to arrange numerous journeys. However, if time is of the essence and you are seeking to explore the country's most famous sights in one shot, Jordan's Golden Triangle is a perfect choice. This three-pronged strategy will allow you to discover Aqaba's attractive seaside city, Petra's historic city, and Wadi Rum's spectacular desert scenery.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba?

The largest occupancy room at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba is the 2 Bedroom Family Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba?

Some rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have fridges?

No, rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at Al Manara, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Saraya Aqaba have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
