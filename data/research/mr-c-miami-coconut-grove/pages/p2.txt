## An oasis within bustling South Florida surrounded by breathtaking views of **Biscayne Bay** and the **Coconut Grove Skyline**

Rooms & Suites

## The height and heart of **Modern Luxury**

Embrace the fusion of modern luxury and timeless European sophistication in our Coconut Grove accommodations. Nestled in the vibrant heart of Miami, our hotel offers a serene escape, marrying contemporary comforts with a touch of personalized, discreet service. Our rooms and suites reflect a harmonious blend of modern amenities and classic elegance. Each space is crafted to offer guests a tranquil retreat, complete with plush furnishings and panoramic views of the stunning Biscayne Bay and the dynamic Coconut Grove skyline.

Dining Options

## Simple. Refined. Cipriani Cuisine

Experience the flavors of Italy through a collection of distinctive dining experiences inspired by the Cipriani tradition of hospitality. From the rooftop elegance of Bellini, where classic Italian cuisine and contemporary creations are paired with sweeping views of Biscayne Bay, to the garden-inspired charm of Il Giardino, offering relaxed breakfasts, leisurely lunches and cocktails amid lush surroundings, each setting brings its own character and sense of place. For added comfort and convenience, guests may also enjoy thoughtfully curated in-room dining, bringing the flavors of Mr. C directly to their private retreat.

Wellness

## Wellness, Reimagined

Inspired by the belief that true luxury begins with well-being, our wellness experiences are designed to promote balance, restoration, and renewal. BeAtala Wellness Spa offers a thoughtful blend of personalized treatments, innovative therapies, and holistic practices tailored to each guest's individual needs. For those seeking movement and vitality, our fully equipped fitness center provides a modern space to energize and recharge. Together, these experiences create a sanctuary where mind, body, and spirit are nurtured in the heart of Coconut Grove.

Events at Mr. C Miami Coconut Grove

## Gather in Style

From elegant weddings and private celebrations to executive meetings and corporate retreats, Mr. C Miami Coconut Grove provides a sophisticated setting for every occasion. Overlooking Biscayne Bay and inspired by the timeless elegance of the Cipriani tradition, our versatile indoor and outdoor venues combine exceptional service, thoughtfully curated cuisine, and stunning waterfront surroundings. Whether gathering for business or celebration, every event is designed to be seamless, memorable, and uniquely tailored to your vision.

Rooftop Pool

## Time to Cool Off

Set high above Coconut Grove, the rooftop pool offers an elevated escape for sun-soaked relaxation and sweeping Miami views. Reserved for hotel guests, this serene outdoor retreat features elegant cabanas, thoughtful amenities, and a refined atmosphere designed for leisure at every pace. From refreshing swims to quiet moments beneath the South Florida sun, the pool invites guests to unwind while taking in panoramic views of Biscayne Bay, Coconut Grove, Brickell, and South Beach.
