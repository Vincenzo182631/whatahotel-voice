# Welcome to W Riyadh - KAFD

## Luxury Hotel in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District

Anchored in the heart of Riyadh’s ultra-modern King Abdullah Financial District, W Riyadh – KAFD marks the brand’s bold debut in the Kingdom. Rising within the capital’s most future-forward skyline, this new chapter for the brand brings together striking design and forward-thinking ambition to set a new standard of luxury in the Kingdom. Designed for a new generation of global tastemakers, W Riyadh...See More – KAFD captures the city’s dynamic energy through a distinctly elevated lens. It’s where contemporary design, curated experiences, and a refined social spirit converge to create a new benchmark for modern luxury. With 210 impeccably styled rooms and suites, immersive culinary destinations, and spaces designed to spark connection and conversation, W Riyadh – KAFD invites guests to experience the capital in an entirely new rhythm.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Riyadh - KAFD?

The check-in time at W Riyadh - KAFD is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Riyadh - KAFD allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at W Riyadh - KAFD.

What are the parking options at W Riyadh - KAFD?

On-site parking is available at W Riyadh - KAFD. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is available for ر.س60.00 per hour.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Riyadh - KAFD have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Riyadh - KAFD has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Riyadh - KAFD booking.

What is the closest airport near W Riyadh - KAFD?

The closest airport near W Riyadh - KAFD is King Khaled International Airport (RUH). RUH is located approximately 34.3 KM from the hotel.

Does W Riyadh - KAFD have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Riyadh - KAFD has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
