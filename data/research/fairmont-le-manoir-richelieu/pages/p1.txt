## THE _iconic_ MANOIR OVERLOOKING THE MAJESTIC ST. LAWRENCE RIVER

Nestled between mountains, forest, and the St. Lawrence River in Charlevoix, Fairmont Le Manoir Richelieu enjoys a dramatic setting. Built in the late 19th century in French-chateau style, our luxury hotel offers 405 stylish rooms, dining options, pools, a top golf course, and a range of activities, all just 90 minutes from Quebec City.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in time is from 4:00 pm, and check-out is by 11:00 am.

Does the hotel offer family-friendly activities?

Yes! Fairmont Le Manoir Richelieu is a playground for all ages, with activities year-round—from hiking and mini-golf in summer to ice skating and snowshoeing in winter. Plus, special programs and entertainment are offered during holidays and major events for unforgettable family moments!

Is there a spa at the hotel?

Yes, the hotel features Moment Spa, a tranquil retreat on the banks of the St. Lawrence River, offering high-end massages, facials, and body treatments. Guests also have access to the Multi-Sports Centre, with indoor and outdoor pools, steam rooms, and an exercise room for complete relaxation.

Are there dining options available at the hotel?

Yes! Fairmont Le Manoir Richelieu offers diverse dining, from gourmet meals at Le Saint-Laurent to family-friendly options at Le Bellerive. In season, Le Point Cardinal serves a scenic brunch by the golf course. Special menus and events enhance the experience year-round!

Is the Fairmont Le Manoir Richelieu accessible for guests with disabilities?

Yes! The hotel is accessible for guests with disabilities, offering 10 adapted rooms, some with widened doorways for wheelchairs. Tub benches and raised toilet seats are available through Housekeeping, and wheelchairs can be obtained from Security for added convenience.

Is parking available at the hotel?

Yes! Parking is available at Fairmont Le Manoir Richelieu. Self-parking is free, while indoor valet parking is $39 CAD/night with in-and-out privileges. EV charging stations are available. Snowmobilers booking the Ski-Doo Package get free heated indoor parking for up to two snowmobiles!
