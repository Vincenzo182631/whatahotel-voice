# About the hotel

## **Voted No.1 hotel in Ireland in Condé Nast Traveller 2025 Readers’ Choice Awards and No.1 hotel in Dublin in Travel + Leisure World’s Best Awards 2025**

The Westbury, a member of Leading Hotels of the World, is a luxury 5 star hotel in central Dublin. This elegant landmark surrounds its guests in sophistication and style, placing them in the heart of Dublin’s social and cultural riches.

From the moment guests ascend the hotel’s central staircase, they are sure to be charmed. Take the gracious Gallery, with its captivating art collection and legendary
Afternoon Tea. Or, visit
The Sidecar cocktail bar for a deliciously crafted tipple, before enjoying a fine dining experience at the hotel's signature restaurant
WILDE. Completing the picture are luxurious
guest rooms and
suites, showcasing the best of Irish and international design.

Yet, it is the warm and genuine welcome that sets the seal on this haven of luxury.

**\\_\\_\\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_**

The Westbury is owned and managed by The Doyle Collection, an Irish family-owned luxury hotel group with hotels in Dublin, London, Washington DC and Cork.
