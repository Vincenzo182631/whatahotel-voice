## The Westbury Hotel

Grafton Street, Saint Stephen's Green, Dublin, Ireland
 -

Close to attractions

4.5/5

·
5673 reviews

Featuring a shared lounge and a hair salon, the 5-star The Westbury Hotel Dublin is just a 5-minute walk from the O'Neills Pub & Kitchen. Boasting a fitness club, the hotel is in the very heart of the city, right near National Natural History Museum of Ireland.

Location

This Dublin hotel is only 500 metres from the city centre park "St Stephens Green" and only 1.5 km from the EPIC The Irish Emigration Museum. You will enjoy your visit to National Gallery of Ireland, in the vicinity of the Westbury Dublin. The accommodation also provides access to such sports attractions as the large Aviva Stadium, which is 2.3 km away. Dublin Terminus bus stop is 250 m from this recently renovated hotel and within 10 minutes' walk of is enough to get to Tara Street train station.

Rooms

The Westbury Hotel features 205 rooms with a mini-fridge-bar, coffee and tea making facilities, and such comforts as an air conditioner. The rooms come with a private bathroom. Staying at this property, guests can enjoy views of the city.

Food & Drinks

The Westbury has an à la carte restaurant and a cocktail bar. Bunsen, which serves American specialities, is 150 metres away.

Leisure & Activities

The on-site entertainment features wedding services.
