# Discover luxurious Bali accommodation for your dream stay

## Accommodations

From spacious one-bedroom balcony suites spanning 92 square meters with lush garden or ocean views, to luxurious one- and two-bedroom villas with direct access to our expansive lagoon-shaped saltwater pool, and elegant one-bedroom villas offering direct beach access and unparalleled privacy, to grand four-bedroom residences with private pools and exclusive beachfront access, our luxury accommodations in Bali at The St. Regis Bali Resort are among the largest and most beautifully appointed on the island. Each option blends European elegance with exquisite Balinese craftsmanship, complemented by Egyptian cotton bedding from Ploh, premium toiletries, curated artwork, and our renowned St. Regis butler service.

### Butler Service

The exceptional St. Regis butler service has been a hallmark of the St. Regis experience for over 100 years. This signature service anticipates and fulfills your every need with impeccable attention to detail—from a perfectly pressed suit for an important meeting, to a cherished book for evening reading, to a thoughtful last-minute gift for a loved one. No request is too small or unattainable, and it is always fulfilled with grace, no matter the hour.
