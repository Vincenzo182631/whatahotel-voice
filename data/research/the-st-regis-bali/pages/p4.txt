### Wellness Experiences at The St. Regis Bali Resort

Wellbeing at The St. Regis Bali Resort is shaped by experiences that nurture relaxation, balance, and connection. From restorative treatments at the award-winning Iridium Spa and moments immersed in nature at the Chef’ s Garden to personalized wellness experiences, guests are invited to embrace renewal in the tranquil beauty of Bali.

## Curated Wellness Program

At The St. Regis Bali Resort, wellness is guided by a highly personalized approach. Led by Wellness Curator Rohit, each experience is shaped around individual goals, whether seeking relaxation, renewed energy, balance, or deeper rest. Sessions begin with a one-on-one consultation to understand the needs of the body and mind, followed by a combination of mindful movement, breathing practices, and restorative techniques. Designed around personal intentions, every session offers a meaningful opportunity to restore balance and reconnect with oneself.

## Chefs Garden

Among the highlights of the Chef' s Garden is a dedicated Aloe Vera section, showcasing one of nature ' s most restorative botanicals. Valued for its soothing qualities, aloe vera offers a welcome complement to Bali' s tropical climate while reflecting the resort' s commitment to enhancing guest wellbeing through nature.

## Iridium Spa

A peaceful sanctuary nestled among reflective pools, Balinese sculptures, and lush tropical gardens, Iridium Spa is a defining wellness destination at The St. Regis Bali Resort. Spanning 2,800 square meters, the spa features 12 treatment spaces, including private suites with outdoor bathtubs, hydrotherapy pools, aromatherapy steam rooms, a facial studio, and a full-service beauty salon. Inspired by traditional Indonesian healing rituals and complemented by European, Thai, and Ayurvedic philosophies, each treatment is tailored to restore balance, renew the senses, and inspire lasting well-being.

## Champagne Rituals

A celebration of champagne' s timeless elegance, this 150-minute ritual is designed to restore, rejuvenate, and indulge the senses. The experience unfolds through a specially blended champagne elixir, the signature Iridium massage with nourishing grape seed scrubs and oils, and a detoxifying body mask crafted with France ’ s finest bubbly and softening white clay. A serene flower bath completes the ritual, before guests retreat to the privacy of a tranquil gazebo to enjoy a glass of champagne.

## Bloody Mary-inspired Wellness Ritual

Inspired by the signature St. Regis Bloody Mary cocktail, this 150-minute ritual transforms the ingredients of the iconic libation into a distinctive wellness experience. A refreshing tomato, pineapple, and wasabi body scrub is followed by a purifying vodka and tomato clay wrap, a detoxifying massage, and a soothing tomato vermouth salt bath. The ritual concludes in the privacy of a tranquil gazebo, where freshly shucked Irish oysters and coriander tomato water provide an elegant finale to this uniquely St. Regis indulgence.

## Fitness

Open 24 hours a day, the fitness center at The St. Regis Bali Resort provides guests with the flexibility to maintain their wellness routines at any time. Equipped with state-of-the-art Life Fitness® equipment, the facility features dedicated spaces for strength training, cardiovascular exercise, and yoga and movement practices. A schedule of complimentary fitness classes is available, while bicycle hire offers an opportunity to explore the surrounding area at a leisurely pace.

## Stand-Up Paddle Yoga

Combining the principles of yoga with the challenge of stand-up paddleboarding, this unique experience takes place within the resort’s tranquil lagoon-shaped freshwater pool. Designed for all levels, the sessions focus on balance, posture, and breathwork while encouraging a deeper sense of focus and connection. Led by expert instructors, stand-up paddle yoga offers guests of all ages an engaging and refreshing way to support their wellbeing.

## Yoga

As the sun rises over the Indian Ocean, yoga at The St. Regis Bali Resort offers a peaceful way to begin the day. Held daily at Bale Pantai, a beachside pavilion overlooking the ocean, the sessions unfold against a backdrop of shifting hues, from deep indigo and gold to lilac and soft blue. Suitable for all levels, a variety of yoga practices are available, inviting guests to cultivate balance, focus, and wellbeing in a serene oceanfront setting.

Book Now

## The Expansive Salt Water Lagoon

One of the defining features of The St. Regis Bali Resort is its expansive saltwater lagoon, among the largest in Bali. Framed by tropical gardens and elegant pavilions, the lagoon invites leisurely days spent floating, swimming, and unwinding, with floating sun loungers, sunken seating areas, and gentle water features that enhance its tranquil ambiance. Filled with natural saltwater, it offers a refreshing swimming experience and a soothing alternative to traditional chlorinated pools, enhancing the sense of relaxation that defines a stay at the resort.

## Secluded Beachfront Paradise

Set along one of Nusa Dua’s most tranquil stretches of coastline, the beachfront at The St. Regis Bali Resort offers a serene retreat framed by soft golden sands and calm waters. Ideal for swimming and moments of relaxation, the expansive bay provides a peaceful contrast to Bali’s rugged shoreline and rolling surf. Whether unwinding on a sunbed or enjoying attentive beachside service with refreshing treats and fresh coconuts, guests can embrace the unhurried rhythm of the Indian Ocean.

## Coral Discovery at The St. Regis Bali Resort

Rooted in a commitment to marine conservation, Coral Discovery offers a unique opportunity to explore Bali’s underwater world. Through guided snorkeling sessions at the resort’s cultivated reef, guests can encounter thriving corals and marine life while gaining insight into the importance of reef preservation. For a more hands-on experience, coral planting allows guests to contribute to the reef’s continued growth, creating a lasting connection to Bali’s coastal environment. Available year-round, subject to weather conditions.

## Children’s Club

As part of the St. Regis Family Traditions program, the Children’s Club at The St. Regis Bali Resort invites young guests to explore, create, and discover through enriching experiences inspired by Bali’s culture and natural surroundings. Designed to engage curious minds and growing imaginations, the activities cater to children of different ages and interests, from exploring nature within the resort’s gardens to discovering the rhythms of traditional Balinese music. Each experience encourages curiosity, creativity, and cultural appreciation while creating meaningful holiday memories for the entire family.

### Family Traditions

St. Regis Family Traditions offer exceptional experiences with activities and personalized attention for young guests and their families. At The St. Regis Bali Resort, surrounded by stunning beauty and culture, every moment becomes a cherished memory. Inspired by Mrs. Astor's legacy, our signature children's rituals ensure fun for young travelers, while our thoughtful amenities make your family’s stay at our beachfront resort in Nusa Dua effortless and memorable.

Welcome Ritual

Upon arrival of the family, welcoming attention is tailored for the younger guests by having them experience the check-in process with a welcome packet and Astor library card to check-out up to 2 library books from the mobile library. A welcome gift awaits in the suite/villa.

Turndown Ritual

Our St. Regis Butler delivers a thoughtful turndown experience at our beachfront resort in Nusa Dua, complete with a personalized message, delectable treats, and a special Astor Library bookmark. For younger guests (6 years and below), we provide a child-sized robe, slippers, and a step stool in the bathroom, ensuring a comfortable and delightful end to their day.

Tiny Afternoon Tea

Delight in a whimsical Tiny Afternoon Tea, set up in an enchanting teepee tent right in the comfort of your villa. Inspired by Mrs. Astor’s famous tea gatherings, this charming experience includes Lollipop Cake, Cupcake Animals, a Donut Tower, Milkshakes, and a St. Regis Teddy Bear, creating a magical treat for young guests.

Evening Outdoor Cinema

Set against the breathtaking backdrop of the sky and stars, the Evening Outdoor Cinema at Cloud Nine Terrace or The Amphitheater offers a magical experience for families. As the sun sets and the stars begin to twinkle, relax and enjoy a movie with delicious popcorn and a selection of refreshing soft drinks and juices.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Bali Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Bali Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
