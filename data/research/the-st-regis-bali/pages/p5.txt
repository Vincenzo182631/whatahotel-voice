# Enjoy luxury beach villas in Bali for your next stay

## Exclusive Villas & Residences

Set amidst lush tropical surroundings with views of the Indian Ocean, The St. Regis Bali Resort's collection of 41 luxury villas and 2 residences offers an exceptional retreat in Nusa Dua. Each elegant sanctuary blends bespoke furnishings, crystal chandeliers, and Balinese craftsmanship, with floor-to-ceiling glass doors opening onto private outdoor living spaces. Featuring lush gardens, inviting lounges, and private pools, select villas and residences enjoy direct access to the resort's saltwater lagoon or the beach.

Embark on a 360° virtual tour of our exquisite beachfront and lagoon villas where timeless elegance meets natural serenity. Wander through our oceanfront residence and tranquil saltwater lagoon villa, each a sanctuary where the whispers of the sea and the stillness of the surroundings evoke a sense of refined escape.

## Gardenia Villas

Nestled within a lush tropical garden, our one-bedroom Gardenia Villas offer an intimate sanctuary surrounded by fragrant tropical flowers. These villas seamlessly blend indoor and outdoor living, featuring a separate living room, a luxurious bedroom, and a decadent marble bathroom. Outdoors, unwind on expansive terraces with a traditional Balinese-style gazebo, or relax by the turquoise private pool, ensuring the utmost peace, privacy, and tranquility.

## Lagoon Villas

The one and two-bedroom Lagoon Villas offer an unparalleled luxury experience with direct access to the resort's expansive saltwater lagoon. Inside, the villas feature spacious living areas, a canopied king-size bed, and marble ensuite bathrooms. The outdoor spaces boast private pools, landscaped gardens, and traditional Balinese-style gazebos, ideal for relaxation and al fresco dining. The two-bedroom option includes both a king and a twin bedroom, along with oversized bathrooms, a kitchenette, and a separate butler's quarters. Seamlessly blending indoor and outdoor living, these villas provide an extraordinary escape for families or couples.

## Strand Villas

With direct access to the white-sand beach and the sparkling Indian Ocean, the one-bedroom Strand Villas offer the perfect setting for honeymooners and romantic getaways. These private villas provide expansive indoor and outdoor living spaces, a marble bathroom with an oversized soaking tub, and a sun terrace featuring a private turquoise pool. Set within a secluded tropical garden, each villa also includes a garden pavilion and ample outdoor space, ensuring the ultimate privacy and luxury, just steps from the beach.

## The Strand Residence

Nestled along the resort's beachfront, The Strand Residence represents the pinnacle of residential luxury at The St. Regis Bali Resort. This expansive two-storey retreat features three or four bedrooms, panoramic Indian Ocean vistas, and direct access to the beach. A flower-draped terrace, private swimming pool, and curated Balinese artworks create an elegant sanctuary defined by privacy, space, and island sophistication. For festive season bookings, please contact our reservations team.

### Indulge in Exceptional Dining

At The St. Regis Bali Resort, we elevate the art of fine dining with a collection of distinctive venues, each offering a unique culinary experience. Start your Sunday with a lavish brunch at both Kayuputi and Boneka, where diverse and exquisite dishes are thoughtfully prepared to delight every palate. Kayuputi pairs authentic Asian delicacies with breathtaking ocean views, while Boneka brings a vibrant atmosphere and world-class brunch offerings.

For a taste of traditional Balinese cuisine, visit Dulang, nestled in a charming colonial-era gazebo and serving locally inspired dishes made from the freshest ingredients. Gourmand Deli offers gourmet snacks, wines, and specialty coffees with a twist, perfect for a quick bite or a leisurely treat. The St. Regis Bar provides a lively ambiance with an extensive Bloody Mary menu, live jazz, and a breezy setting ideal for gathering with friends. Finally, enjoy panoramic ocean views and exotic tropical cocktails at Vista Bar, a true Bali beach retreat with a swim-up pool bar.

Kayuputi

Perched against the breathtaking backdrop of the Indian Ocean, Kayuputi delivers an exceptional fine dining experience, showcasing Pan-Asian-inspired haute cuisine. Elevate your weekends with the island’s most exquisite brunch, masterfully crafted by our award-winning team, where every dish is a harmonious blend of artistry and flavor.

Learn More

Boneka

Inspired by the charm of Indonesia's traditional puppet theater, Boneka Restaurant presents a diverse culinary journey, offering a blend of Asian specialties and international favorites. Its renowned Sunday Brunch features a unique combination of three dining experiences: an indulgent buffet, refined à la carte selections, and delightful pass-around creations, ensuring a memorable feast for every guest.

Dulang

Elegantly nestled within an intricately carved gazebo, Dulang exudes colonial-era charm and sophistication. Offering an authentic Balinese dining experience in Nusa Dua, it showcases locally sourced ingredients, traditional flavors, and iconic beverages like brem and kopi luwak. The setting beautifully complements Balinese culture, creating an atmosphere of grace and refinement.

The St. Regis Bar

The St. Regis Bar invites you to unwind with a curated selection of sparkling wines and tropical-inspired cocktails from its full bar menu. Each evening, immerse yourself in the enchanting atmosphere with live jazz performances and witness the spectacular evening ritual, where a sommelier masterfully sabers a bottle of champagne using an Indonesian kris, a traditional ceremonial dagger.

Gourmand Deli

Discover a relaxed yet sophisticated haven at Gourmand Deli, offering an exceptional selection of fine foods and beverages. Indulge in handcrafted chocolates, specialty cakes, gourmet coffees, and an array of delectable treats, perfect for any occasion or a moment of pure indulgence.

Vista Bar

Nestled along the beachfront, Vista Bar is the ultimate swim-up retreat where you can cool off in crystal-clear waters while savoring a diverse menu of handcrafted cocktails, refreshing beers, fine wines, and fresh coconuts. Bask in the serenity of panoramic views of the Indian Ocean and the pristine Nusa Dua beach, creating the perfect setting for relaxation and indulgence.

## Iridium Spa

Step into Iridium Spa and discover a serene sanctuary for both body and soul. Indulge in luxurious treatments that blend traditional Balinese healing practices with the restorative benefits of nature, offering a truly transformative experience.
