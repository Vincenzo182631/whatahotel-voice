Each restaurant in Bali, Nusa Dua boasts a distinctive character and extraordinary cuisine, complemented by the legendary bespoke service of St. Regis. Awaken to the splendor of a seaside sunrise picnic, where the tranquility of the morning and breathtaking ocean views create unforgettable memories in a serene setting.

### Astor Sunday Brunch at Kayuputi Restaurant

The most glamorous brunch you’ll find in Bali, Astor Brunch draws friends and families to an elegant, airy Kayuputi Restaurant every Sunday. Commencing with complimentary aperitifs, this weekly ritual always feels celebratory and it’s recommended you come hungry. Gourmet treats on offer here include the freshest caviar and Sakoshi oysters, alongside multiple foie gras specialties, steaks and decadent desserts.

Learn More

### Quintessential Sunrise Picnic

Bali’s pristine Nusa Dua beach is at its most peaceful and beautiful, as dawn breaks over the gentle Indian Ocean, and early starters who book our Quintessential Sunrise Picnic get this spectacle all to themselves. Served under a parasol, this barefoot breakfast enjoys an uninterrupted view of the sun’s silken ascent of colors. The spectacular start to the day is complemented by a bespoke breakfast spread.

### A Romantic Dinner at the Cloud Nine Chapel

A tailored evening designed for just one couple, A Romantic Dinner at the Cloud Nine Chapel begins thoughtfully with the presentation of a fragrant bouquet of colorful flowers and welcome sundowners on the seafront terrace. Thereafter, a sublime seven-course menu is served under the dance of flickering candles, as a pianist plays and the Indian Ocean glows under starlight.

### Romance by the Beach

Illuminated by a spiral of dancing candles, the soft sands of Nusa Dua are transformed into a lustrous golden carpet during this memorable evening dinner. Seated on the shore, with the Indian Ocean before them, the sound of gently lapping waves and Bali’s sultry night air, couples can indulge in a degustation menu.

### Boneka Sunday Brunch

Begin your Sunday Brunch experience with complimentary cocktails including our signature Bloody Mary at The St. Regis Bar from 11:00 AM. Then commence with brunch from 12:00 PM at Boneka. In the chic ambiance of Boneka, known for its refined taste and flavor, guests can experience the three brunch concept all at the same time: buffet, a la carte, and endless pass arounds.

### St. Regis Bali Brunch at Kayuputi Restaurant

A gastronomic indulgence beyond expectation is now available every Saturday. An elegant beachfront brunch showcasing Kayuputi’s Pan-Asian inspired haute cuisine, handcrafted by Executive Chef Agung Gede and the exemplary culinary team.
