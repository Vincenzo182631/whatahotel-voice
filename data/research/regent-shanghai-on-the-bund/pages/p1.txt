# Regent Shanghai on the Bund

##### Allure and sophistication

On the banks of the Huangpu River, lies a serene riverside retreat where modern elegance melds with timeless charm.

Within a contemporary floral wonderland of blooming magnolias, a glistening tree chases light, while a magnificent vista sweeps from historic buildings on the Bund to the iconic skyscrapers of Pudong.

Inspired by a rich heritage as a haven for global mariners and East-West cultural exchange, sensory journeys blend art, culture and cuisine into a unique Shanghai experience.

## Go beyond

Explore the North Bund and discover a part of the city that emanates the Beauty of Contrast with its dazzling views of the Huangpu River spanning from the glistening skyscrapers of Lujiazui in Pudong to the heritage architectural complex on the Bund.

From our prime location at the crossroads of the Suzhou River, just next to the famous Garden Bridge and the Grand Halls, a world class convention centre incorporating historical warehouses, you can experience Shanghai’s storied heritage on the Bund, Nanjing Road and Yu Garden. Then witness its dynamic transformation in Pudong and continuous rebirth at Suzhou Creek.
