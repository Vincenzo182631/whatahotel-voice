# Rooms & Suites

###### Regent Shanghai on the Bund

## Rooms & Suites

Discover the ultimate in refined modern luxury and comfort in one of the most spacious guestrooms in the city. Designed as a Personal Haven, each guestroom feels like an elegant shipboard stateroom with an Oasis Bathroom, the majority overlooking the Huangpu River and Shanghai skyline.

#### Seagull Suite

- Bed
1 King

- Size
80 sq. m. / 861 sq. ft.

Experience bird’s eye views of the Bund and Shanghai skyline ensconced in an elegant residential oasis inspired by our heritage as the Seagull Hotel. Choose your Personal Haven, be it the cosy balcony, spacious living area or Oasis Bathroom, for pampering with bespoke service on your terms.

#### Penthouse on the Bund

- Bed
1 King or 2 Twins

- Size
418 sq. m. / 4499 sq. ft.

In the ultimate residential oasis atop the hotel, discover unparalleled scenic beauty with 270° framed views of the Shanghai skyline and historic Bund. Melding Chinese sophistication and sleek modern style, the Penthouse on the Bund is home to a grand reception hall and outdoor terrace for entertaining, while the master suite’s intimate living room and Oasis Bathroom with an indulgent marble bath, skyline views and private sauna are one-of-a-kind Personal Havens.
