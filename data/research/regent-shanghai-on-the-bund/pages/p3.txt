# Fine Dining

## Immersive culinary journeys

Embark on a multi-sensory dining odyssey. Each dining experience possesses a distinctive character, showcasing creativity, a contemporary interpretation of accessible gastronomy with craft beverages and inspiration from the city’s vibrant culture.

* * *

## Condé

CONDÉ exudes sophistication with its sculptural ceiling of shimmering dark metals, refractive curved walls and marble floors that contrast beautifully with the dazzling views of the Shanghai skyline. The ambience at CONDÉ creates a sense of luxury and elegance, promising an unforgettable experience for all who walk through its doors.

* * *

Western

## Harmonia

Satisfy cravings all day over indulgent breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea and dinner experiences in a light-filled setting with a garden courtyard and terrace overlooking the Bund. The open kitchen is a showcase of chefs crafting a fusion of global cuisine for the semi-buffet and à la carte menu.

* * *

## Condé Boutique

Just off the lobby, Condé Boutique redefines the traditional pastry shop concept.

A stunning show kitchen reveals the creation of artisanal pastries, while the decadent display, convivial bar counter and garden terrace are a social hub over light breakfast and indulgent afternoon tea experiences.

* * *

- Cuisine
Lobby Cake Shop & Lounge

- Hours
Daily 10:00am to 10:00pm

- Location
First Floor

Reflecting the rich tapestry of Chinese culinary heritage, Jin Lin is a haven for these seeking authentic flavours, heartfelt hospitality and an immersive journey through China’s diverse regional cuisines.
