# Wellness

## An oasis for wellbeing

An invigorating swim in the indoor pool rejuvenates body and soul, while the 24-hour Fitness Centre is a source of renewed energy.

## Swimming Pool

At the indoor swimming pool, natural light streams through tall windows with old Shanghai style mullions as the subtle spray of a fountain echoes the mist of the river. Whether an invigorating swim or a refreshing dip, be immersed in a blissful escape from the frenzied pulse of the city.
