# The ROOF

**The ROOF**

RESERVE

THE ROOF is located on the hotel’s 7th floor and offers panoramic mountain and ocean views. The best place from which to enjoy the endless bright summer evenings as well as the magical northern lights in the colder months. Floor-to-ceiling glass doors open onto a large wrap-around seasonal outdoor terrace, scattered with comfortable seating, while the slick all-black interiors create a discreet background that doesn’t distract from the views.

For additional information contact our team at +354 582 0000

or email us at rooftop.rek@editionhotels.com.

**Tides**

VIEW WEBSITE RESERVE

TIDES is the hotel's signature restaurant celebrating the best seafood from the pristine waters of Iceland along with locally sourced meats and ingredients. TIDES features simplicity in its cooking techniques, characteristic of Nordic cuisine, allowing the quality of ingredients to shine.

**Tides Cafe**

Tides bakery and café - serves locally roasted coffee, brewed in-house. Alongside this is a selection of quick bites and fresh sandwiches, as well as freshly baked pastries. The café also serves delicious sourdough or rye bread sandwiches and grab-and go meals that you can eat in or take away.

**Lobby Bar**

The Lobby Bar is a dynamic social space featuring an open-flame fireplace, serving locally produced craft beers, specialty cocktails with an Icelandic twist and wine by the glass, alongside a carefully selected snack menu; it is the ideal meeting place for guests and visitors.

**TÖLT**

Named after the unique fourth gait Icelandic horses are best known for, TÖLT is a cozy bar, designed as a hidden sanctuary. Drawing inspiration from Icelandic culture and featuring local Icelandic spirits, TÖLT offers an intimate atmosphere unlike any other, the perfect spot for pre-dinner drinks or late-night cocktails.

#### Menus      Hours     Dropdown

- DRINK MENU

**Sunset**

In true EDITION style, Sunset is a cool underground night spot. Along with a killer cocktail menu and an ongoing roster of events, the club will play host to some of the world’s top DJs and performers, earmarking it as the latest unmissable destination in Reykjavik’s thriving nightlife scene.
