# Rooms

The Reykjavik EDITION has 253 guest rooms, which includes 26 spacious suites and a Penthouse with stylishly modern decor and posh detailing by Ian Schrager. Enjoy luxury amenities and large floor to ceiling windows that feature stunning views of the North Atlantic Ocean and Harpa Concert Hall.
