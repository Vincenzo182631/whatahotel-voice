### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio and Strength Training Equipment, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa have a pool on-site?

Yes, Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Infinity Pool (Outdoor)Kids Leisure Pool (Outdoor)

Does Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa have an on-site spa?

Yes, Explore Spa is located on-site at Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa?

Dhobi Waterfall, Venna Lake, Chinaman Waterfall, Connaught Peak, Pratapgad Fort and more are nearby Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa.
