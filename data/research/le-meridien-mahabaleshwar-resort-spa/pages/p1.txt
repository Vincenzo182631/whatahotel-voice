# Welcome to Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa

## Experience luxury at our 5-star resort in Mahabaleshwar

Le Meridien Mahabaleshwar Resort and Spa sits on 27 acres of evergreen forests in the Western Ghats. This spa resort in Mahabaleshwar, Maharashtra, offers a 5-star experience with contemporary rooms and...See More suites featuring private balconies with incredible views, deep soaking tubs and luxury bedding. Enjoy time with kids in the leisure pool with slides, or relax in the infinity pool overlooking the valley. Gather with friends at The Hub or Longitude Bar, or enjoy on-site dining. Treat yourself at Explore Spa, work out in the 24-hour fitness center or explore nature around the grounds. The resort is near Mahabaleshwar attractions, including Krishnabai & Shiva Temple, Arthurs Seat, Mapro Gardens and the local market. Whether visiting for business, a family vacation, a wedding or a romantic getaway, enjoy an unforgettable stay.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa?

On-site parking is available at Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa is Pune International Airport (PNQ). PNQ is located approximately 135.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa have an airport shuttle to Pune International Airport (PNQ)?

Yes, Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa has an airport shuttle to Pune International Airport (PNQ).

Does Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Le Méridien Mahabaleshwar Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
