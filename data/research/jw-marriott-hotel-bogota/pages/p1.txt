### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Bogota?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Bogota is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Bogota allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Bogota.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Bogota?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Bogota. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Bogota have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Bogota has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Bogota booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Bogota?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Bogota is El Dorado International Airport Bogota (BOG). BOG is located approximately 6.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Bogota have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Bogota does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Bogota have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Bogota has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
