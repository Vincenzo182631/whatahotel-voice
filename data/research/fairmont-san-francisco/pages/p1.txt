## THE _original_ FAIRMONT ATOP   SAN FRANCISCO'S NOB HILL

Fairmont San Francisco is a 5-star luxury hotel and iconic Beaux Arts landmark atop Nob Hill. For more than a century, generations of San Franciscans and travelers have gathered here for celebrations and historic occasions, with sweeping city and Bay views, grand surroundings and warm, genuine service.
