# Beachfront Hotel in Ibiza

## Experiences

Escape to a place where vital energy and limitless pleasure collide. Manifesting the magnetic attraction of the island, here parallel realities effortlessly mingle. Whether it’s spending chill moments in AWAY Spa, or sweating-it-out in FIT - whatever the vibe, W Ibiza is at the center of the action.

## Seasonal Experiences

Discover a curated programme of seasonal experiences designed to celebrate creativity, culture, gastronomy and connection. From vibrant Pride celebrations and immersive art showcases to exclusive Full Moon evenings and special dining events, W Ibiza brings unforgettable moments to life throughout the season. Explore what’s happening and join us for unique experiences by the Mediterranean.

### Dive Into Art: La Isla Bonita

La Isla Bonita is more than a place: it is an invitation to inhabit the present. The exhibition unfolds the island as an emotional landscape in constant motion, evoking the beauty of the ephemeral and the essential, where time is irrelevant, all that matters is being… here and now. On the island.

### Full Moon Nights at GLOW

Experience a series of exclusive monthly evenings inspired by the energy of the full moon. Combining signature cocktails, curated gastronomy, entertainment and immersive storytelling, each event offers a unique sensory journey under the Mediterranean sky. Capacity is limited and advance reservation is required.

### Olas Gallery: Under the Surface

Discover Under the Surface, a photography exhibition by Ibiza-born artist Ned Irving, presented by Olas Gallery at the W Lounge and Hotel Lobby. Shot entirely in Ibiza, the collection captures the interplay of light, water and movement, creating dreamlike compositions that blur the boundaries between photography and painting and reveal the island’s hidden beauty.

## Glow Inside Out

Escape to a realm of beauty and wellness at our new treatment cabins. Embrace summer radiance with our curated beauty rituals, ensuring you glow inside out with moisturizing products to combat damaged skin by sun exposure and draining treatments specially selected to soothe the swelling and fluid retention resulting from travel. Experience the ultimate pampering and give your nails a color touch with our manicure and pedicure services.

## FIT

The ultimate powerhouse, FIT is a magnet for gym lovers. Open 24/7, this is where state-of-the-art Technogym equipment, free weights and a medley of private classes with specialized trainers, meet graffiti-emblazoned walls in a color-drenched temple to fitness.

## Experiences & Services

Discover everything W Ibiza has to offer beyond your stay. From dining and poolside moments to private transfers and island experiences, make the most of your time with us.

Only 15 minutes from Ibiza town and just 21km from Ibiza Airport, you can style it out among the chic marina and hotspots in no time. Santa Eulalia totally owns its relaxed pace and cosmopolitan attitude. It is known for its calm, authentic island vibes and beautiful beaches, attracting families and visitors alike....See More A charming Mediterranean bay known for its pristine and shallow waters, long promenade and stunning views of Formentera in the distance. ## Santa Eulalia, the New Ibiza Hotspot

Offering the perfect blend of laidback-luxury lifestyle, Santa Eulalia totally owns its relaxed pace and cosmopolitan attitude. Stroll along its famous beachfront promenade that at night becomes perfect for bar-hopping. Cruise down to the resort’s very own chic marina to check out the luxury yachts moored in the port ideal to spend a day in Formentera or its surrounding coastline known for its pristine and shallow waters. Cala Llonga, Cala Llenya, Cala Pada and Cala Martina are just some of the top picks. Mornings are perfect to walk up to the fortified 16th century church of Puig de Missa, a beautiful spot to stop and take in the stunning hilltop surroundings. If you prefer a touch of adventure during your holiday, the inland villages such as Santa Gertrudis and Sant Carles are all nearby, as well as the only golf course on the island or San Juan market for an authentic boho experience.
