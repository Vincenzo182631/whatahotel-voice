# Welcome to W Ibiza

## WHITE HEAT, BOHO BEAT

Open seasonally from April 29 to October 17, 2027, W Ibiza luxury beach hotel strikes a pose on the palm-fringed, sand-drenched beachfront of Santa Eulalia. The hotel is located on the east coast, only a 20-minute drive from Ibiza city. It's the third-largest town on the island, with a relaxed pace and cosmopolitan attitude. As one of the most luxurious hotels, this is an exclusive boho-chic resort...See More by the sea with a distinctive design inspired by the essence and hippy culture of the island. Crafted to channel energy towards the horizon, feel the Mediterranean in each of our elegant 116 rooms with a walk-on balcony and 38 unique hotel suites with spacious terraces with sea views in Santa Eulalia. Our hotel in Ibiza offers an elevated dining experience with the best local cuisine at our new Yellow Fish restaurant and Italian dishes at STEPS.See Less

### STEPS

STEPS is where timeless Italian flavours meet the vibrant soul of Ibiza. Savour wood-fired pizzas served late into the night, alongside a tempting selection of antipasti, fresh pasta, and more. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Ibiza?

The check-in time at W Ibiza is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Ibiza allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Ibiza. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 15.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €125.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Ibiza?

On-site self parking is available at W Ibiza. The daily self parking fee is €25.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Ibiza have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Ibiza has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Ibiza booking.

What is the closest airport near W Ibiza?

The closest airport near W Ibiza is Ibiza Airport (IBZ). IBZ is located approximately 21.6 KM from the hotel.

Does W Ibiza have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Ibiza has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
