## Yellow Fish Restaurant

Overlooking the shores of Santa Eulalia, Yellow Fish Restaurant captures the essence of the Mediterranean with an effortless free-spirit elegance. Locally sourced grilled fish, vibrant Spanish tapas, and refreshing sangrias are served in a setting designed for slow, sun-drenched moments. With music in the air and a lively yet refined ambience, every experience unfolds with the relaxed, magnetic spirit of Ibiza.

### Sip and Savor

Our expert mixologists are on standby to create drinks magic. We’ve got a tantalizing Sangria menu, loads of tasty cocktails or if bubbles are your thing, a Champagne menu, as well as bottle service.

### DJ Sessions

Get caught up in the rhythm as live DJ sessions create music alchemy with a blend of chilled sounds by day and high energy beats at night .

### Dive Into the Scene

A fab poolside setting, WET Deck is where DJ sessions featuring Balearic and morning sets meets all-day dining and cocktails. GLOW with rooftop infinity pool and premium sun loungers looks out across Eulalia Bay and offers locally interpreted snacks, creative mixology and Champagne.

## Golden Hour at Glow

A magnet during the day, magnetic at night, GLOW is a beachfront hangout surrounded by mountains, that adds stunning 360° views across Santa Eulalia, the marina and city to the mix. It’s set up for night swims, tasty food, expert mixology, and in-demand weekend DJ sessions.

### STEPS

STEPS is where timeless Italian flavours meet the vibrant soul of Ibiza. Celebrating the art of handcrafted pizza, freshly prepared dough is baked to perfection in a lively seafront setting in Santa Eulalia. Alongside wood-fired pizzas served late into the evening, savour a tempting selection of antipasti and fresh pasta, all infused with Mediterranean character. With views over the Mediterranean Sea, it’s the perfect place to unwind with a spritz, sip fine liqueurs, and soak up the relaxed yet vibrant energy of Ibiza.
