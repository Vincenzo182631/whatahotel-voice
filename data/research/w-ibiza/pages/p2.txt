# Luxury Santa Eulalia Accommodation

## Accommodations

Experience the ultimate stay and play in our accommodation in Santa Eulalia, Ibiza. It’s all about ease of lifestyle and in a relaxed vibe our 116 hotel and 38 luxury Ibiza suites. Mixing up kaleidoscopic splatter paints and colorful fabrics with the vibrancy of hippie summers, these crash pads transcend tradition and spark an alternative mindset. From bijou guest rooms to bold sea view suites, whatever the mood, there’s a set-up to suit. Designed to raise the alternative luxury of the island, each one of our luxury hotel rooms in Ibiza is kitted out with a signature W Bed, requisite plush bedding, dream-easy pillows and scatter cushions. Many have a walk-on balcony and some add-on jaw dropping views of the Mediterranean.

## Luxe Island Lifestyle

At W Ibiza, we want to make sure our guests live it up to the max. Big on comfort, our pet-friendly rooms and suites come with signature W bed, decked out in perfectly plumped pillows and duvet. Fancy an in-room drink? Consider Mixbar your personal cocktail service, complete with designer martini glasses. When it comes to tech, we put you in control. Surf channels on the Smart TV, connect to Marshall Bluetooth speakers or adjust mood lighting - it's all a touch away.

## Designed for a Modern Tribe

Eclectic from the outset, artistic license has been used so that each space embraces its bohemian roots. Muted tones and varied textures collide in bold design that radiates island vibes. Hand-blown glass lamps and textured embellishments meet reflective surfaces and crisp white linen. It's a heady mix of culture and style that feels oh so Ibiza.
