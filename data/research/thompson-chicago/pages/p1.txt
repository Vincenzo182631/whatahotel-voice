# Gold Coast Boutique Hotel Near Downtown Chicago \| Thompson Chicago, by Hyatt

## EXPERIENCE GOLD COAST GLAMOUR AT OUR BOUTIQUE HOTEL NEAR DOWNTOWN CHICAGO

Poised against the vastness of Lake Michigan, Thompson Chicago, by Hyatt, takes center stage on the iconic Gold Coast. A short stroll from our boutique hotel, discover Oak Street’s trendy boutiques, the Magnificent Mile and the beauty of Lake Michigan at Oak Street Beach. Explore downtown Chicago’s grand architecture, renowned culinary scene and legendary nightlife. End the day in one of our 247 spacious hotel rooms and suites overlooking the vibrant city.

## rooms & suites

Take in views of the twinkling Gold Coast and Lake Michigan from your suite at Thompson Chicago, right near downtown and just steps from the Magnificent Mile. Surround yourself with sophistication in one of our 247 elegant Chicago hotel rooms and suites, featuring 400-thread count Sferra linens, rainfall showers, mid-century modern furnishings and inspired artwork. Upgrade to a balcony suite for unparalleled views, or live like a local in one of our two-story Penthouse Suites, featuring a billiards table and a sprawling outdoor terrace to keep your guests entertained late into the night.
