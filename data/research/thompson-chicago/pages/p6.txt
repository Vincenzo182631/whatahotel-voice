# Hotel Policies

Destination Fees

To enhance your stay, a daily destination fee of $25.00 (subject to change) will apply to each room of your stay at Thompson Chicago to provide you with the following services:

- 10% Discount at Lux Bar
- Receive Bruschetta or Cookie Dessert at Lou Malnati's Pizzeria
- 15% Discount to Milk & Honey Spa
- 25% Discount on All-Day Passes to FFC Formula Fitness Club
- Special Discounts to over 30 Shops, Spas, Restaurants and Entertainment at the 900 N Michigan Shops (see Front Desk for details)
- 20% Discount to the 360 Chicago Observation Deck
- 10% Discount at La Colombe Coffee
- AIRE Ancient Baths Chicago - Complimentary add-on of 30 minutes extra bath time & hydrogel eyepatches with every experience
- Bottled Water provided daily

Pet Policy

Thompson Chicago is a Dog-friendly hotel. Dogs cannot be left unattended in guest rooms and all fees are subject to change.

We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy.
