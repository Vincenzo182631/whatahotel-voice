### Perfect Chicago Marathon Hotel: Central Location, Great for Hyatt Points

I stayed at the Thompson for the Chicago Marathon, and it was a 10/10 experience. If you have World of Hyatt points, this is the place to use them for race weekend.

The location is central to everything, so you’re close to the marathon events and the race-weekend energy all weekend long. It’s also surrounded by restaurants, which made carb loading easy the night before the race. Getting around downtown Chicago was simple from here.

Highly recommend this Hyatt for any runner traveling to the Chicago Marathon, or anyone who wants a central base in the city.

### Missed the mark

This hotel is definitely in need of a refresh. The first room I was given had an AC that wasn’t working properly, and the couch had a large hole in it. I was moved to a second room, but unfortunately, there were issues there as well. Several of the switches weren’t operational, and the bathroom light flickered like a strobe before eventually staying on.

The shower setup was also awkward. I’m personally not a fan of the open shower design, which made the overall bathroom experience less enjoyable.

I’ve stayed at quite a few Thompson properties and have generally had great experiences, but unfortunately, this location just didn’t meet the standard I’ve come to expect from the brand.

On the positive side, the location is very convenient, with great shopping and plenty of good restaurants nearby. Despite that, given the condition of the rooms and the maintenance issues I experienced, I wouldn’t choose to stay here again.
