### Café Goldie

Nestled just off the Living Room, Café Goldie is W Hollywood's all-day café serving specialty coffee, fresh pastries, light bites, and grab-and-go favorites. Whether you're fueling up before exploring Hollywood or settling in for a casual meeting, Goldie offers a relaxed setting with a distinctly local energy. From morning espresso rituals to midday refreshments, Café Goldie is designed for guests and locals alike—perfect for a quick stop, a leisurely catch-up, or anything in between.
