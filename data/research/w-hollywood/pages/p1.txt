# Welcome to W Hollywood

## THE PERFECT SET

In the heart of Hollywood and at the edge of the Hills, the fully reimagined W Hollywood stands at the epicenter of Los Angeles. As a hotel in the center of Hollywood and a hotel in the LA Entertainment District, it places you steps from the city. With bold glamour, our thoughtfully designed dining, beverage, and community spaces inspire connection while providing a haven from a day on the scene at...See More our hotel near the Hollywood Sign. Find easygoing comfort and daring style in guest rooms and suites designed to captivate, many featuring picture-perfect views of the landmark. Positioned between Hollywood and Sunset Boulevards, W Hollywood blends local energy with global appeal. Move seamlessly from day to night within intimate yet lively spaces, lush indoor-outdoor settings, and our hotel with rooftop pool in Hollywood. W Hollywood is a place to tap into your own star power and discover.See Less

### Our Suites

Every detail is crafted to cater to your needs. Whether you’re chasing the spotlight, closing deals or seeking a vibrant escape. With accents reflecting LA's sunshine and spaces designed for connection, our rooms are the ultimate backstage pass to the city of dreams.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Hollywood?

The check-in time at W Hollywood is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Hollywood allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Hollywood. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at W Hollywood?

On-site valet parking is available at W Hollywood. The daily valet fee is $95.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Hollywood have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Hollywood has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Hollywood booking.

What is the closest airport near W Hollywood?

The closest airport near W Hollywood is Hollywood Burbank Airport (BUR). BUR is located approximately 8.5 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Hollywood have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Hollywood has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
