## W Suite

Stretch out and unwind in the elevated comforts of our one-bedroom W Suite. Featuring a spacious bathtub, a fully stocked wet bar, a separate living room and a window seat perfect for taking in the views, this studio-style suite offers roomy comfort and high-brow amenities.

## Supreme Suite

Unwind in our Supreme Suite. Soak up panoramic views of the Hollywood sign, Capitol Records building and Griffith Observatory from this suite’s prime corner-location. With a separate living room optimized for hosting meetings or entertaining, your stay here will be elevated to a whole new level of luxury.

## Vista Suite

Our Vista Suite is your ultimate retreat for work or play. This spacious one-bedroom suite, complete with a separate guest bathroom, is designed with contemporary Hollywood style in mind. Host a meeting or an intimate dinner party–or just lounge. Escape to a space that always impresses.

## Mega Suite

Our one-bedroom Mega Suite brings your Hollywood dreams to life. Featuring a spacious design, bold contemporary style and a dining table for six. This suite is perfect for hosting intimate gatherings, private dinners or exclusive meetings. It’s a setting that’s both sophisticated and unforgettable.

## Oasis Suite

Elevate your private event in the Oasis Suite, a California-style residential retreat designed for effortless entertaining. Featuring a separate billiards room, full wet bar and dining table for 10. This suite is the perfect setting for intimate dinners or gatherings. Step onto your private terrace with a full-height decorative fireplace and patio seating to create an unforgettable experience.

## Penthouse Suite

Experience ultimate Hollywood experience in the Penthouse Suite. Featuring a spacious living room with a serpentine sofa, panoramic views and a fully equipped bar. Ideal for intimate gatherings or executive meetings, this suite includes a caterer’s kitchenette and a dining area seating up to seven.

## Entertainment

Step into the spotlight with our premium suites, where iconic views set the stage and expansive living areas steal the show. Entertain effortlessly with a sleek wet bar, designed to elevate every moment.

## In-Room Amenities

Every suite at W Hollywood is a sanctuary of style and comfort. Refresh with luxurious Davines bath products and Comfort Zone soaps and lotions, then wrap yourself in the plush embrace of our iconic W robes. Sink into the ultimate relaxation with the W Signature Bed, designed for the perfect night’s sleep.

## In-Room Dining

Craving something delicious? Our in-room dining delivers bold flavors and signature bites straight to your door. Whether it’s breakfast in bed, a midday snack, or a late-night feast, we’ve got you covered.
