Why book?
If you want to stay in the heart of Hollywood with a side of glitz and a splash of nightlife, W Hollywood is your spot. With a Rockwell Group-led redesign, the hotel now channels Southern California’s natural beauty and Hollywood’s legendary film history. Perfect for those who want a hotel that feels like stepping into a movie set.
Set the scene
This isn’t your quiet, tucked-away retreat. Sitting right on Hollywood Boulevard, this W is a magnet for influencers, entertainment industry insiders, and party-goers. The triple-height Living Room, with its green velvet seating and mirrored ceiling installation, sets the tone for a bold yet sophisticated atmosphere.
The backstory
Opened in 2010, this property is part of the W Hotels brand, known for its trendy, nightlife-driven properties. The hotel recently underwent a full renovation, ditching its old flashy aesthetic for a more sophisticated, modern vibe.
The rooms
The 319 guest rooms and suites reflect Southern California’s textures and colors, with Venetian plaster-inspired walls, curved furniture, and oversized window seats. Some suites feature wet bars and private terraces, while the Mega Suite even boasts a private library and billiards lounge.
Food and drink
The new There There restaurant brings a theatrical, Angeleno dining concept to life with Asian- and Latin-inspired small plates. Standouts include Dungeness Crab Tostadas, truffle croquettes, and matcha cheesecake, paired with inventive cocktails like Purple Haze and Fire and Ice. The Living Room Bar remains a hotspot for cocktails and live music, while a pair of WET Deck rooftop bars (Sunrise and Sunset ) offer panoramic views of the Hollywood Hills and the iconic Hollywood Sign.
The spa
The Bliss Spa spans two floors and offers luxury treatments, but it’s more about relaxation than cutting-edge wellness.
The neighborhood/area
You’re right in the middle of Hollywood, with the Pantages Theatre, Capitol Records Building, and Farmer’s Market mere blocks away. Though if you’re looking for beach vibes, this isn’t the place; Santa Monica is a 45-minute drive away (with no rush hour traffic).
The service
The friendly staff leans towards hip and casual than white-glove treatment. The concierge can help with reservations and nightlife recommendations, but don’t expect miracles.
Accessibility
The hotel meets ADA standards, with elevators, ramps, and wheelchair-accessible rooms.
Anything left to add?
W Hollywood teamed up with Tempo AI to turn shared spaces into ever-changing soundscapes with an interactive music experience that shifts based on guest presence, weather, and time of day. Up on The Loft rooftop, a high-tech audiovisual setup brings artist showcases and creative events to life, making music a big part of the hotel’s vibe.
