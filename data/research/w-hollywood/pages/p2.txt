### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Hollywood have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Hollywood have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Hollywood?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at W Hollywood. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Hollywood?

No, the rooms at W Hollywood do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at W Hollywood have fridges?

No, rooms at W Hollywood do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W Hollywood have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at W Hollywood do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Hollywood have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Hollywood have hair dryers.
