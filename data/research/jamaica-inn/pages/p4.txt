# Find us

## Nestled in Ocho Rios

One of the premier private beaches in Jamaica, intimate suites and cottages showcase enchanting views of the Caribbean Sea.

### Getting Here

Jamaica Inn is located approximately an hour and a half from both Donald Sangster International Airport in Montego Bay (MBJ) and Norman Manley International Airport in Kingston (KIN). Ian Fleming International Airport in Ocho Rios (OCJ) is a 15-minute drive to Jamaica Inn and is suitable for smaller charter or private flights. We recommend booking private transportation through Jamaica Inn, or alternatively renting a car.

### Entry Requirements - Online Passenger Declaration

Effective September 1, 2023, Jamaica has implemented a crucial update for all travellers visiting the island. It is mandatory that every traveller to Jamaica complete the Online Passenger Declaration Form before embarking on their journey. This essential requirement ensures a seamless and efficient travel experience for both visitors and locals.

### Private Airport Transfers

We are happy to make arrangements for your private transfer to Jamaica Inn, it is recommended that transfers are pre-booked - please contact our team at resinfo@jamaicainn.com to organise your private transfer.

After clearing customs, proceed outside where your driver will be waiting with a Jamaica Inn sign, ready to transportation to the resort. Along the way, unwind with an ice-cold Red Stripe or coconut water, and converse with your driver to learn more about Jamaica.

### Club Mobay / Kingston VIP Airport Service

Jamaica Inn recommends arranging the services of Club Mobay or Club Kingston, which offers an expedited airport process upon arrival and departure in Montego Bay or Kingston airports. Additionally, guests have access to Club Mobay's VIP lounge, which serves snacks and refreshments.
