# About Jamaica Inn

## Our Jamaican Home

Since 1958, the Morrow family have lovingly run this iconic island hotel.

A truly special place, where it’s possible to switch off all pressure and enjoy time just for you. Sensing what’s needed in the moment – and beyond – our team takes great pride in doing a proper job – and pleasure in putting our guests instantly at ease.

It’s what sets Jamaica Inn a world apart.

## Shadow

Puppy kisses.

The hotel is famous for several black Labradors who have been part of the Inn over the years, dating back to the 1970s. Shadow V is our resident canine and even has her own cottage on property, too.
