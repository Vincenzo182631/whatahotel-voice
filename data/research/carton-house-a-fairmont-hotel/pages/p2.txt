Formerly the home of the Earls of Kildare, the house dates back to the 1700s and was the filming location for the BBC miniseries, "The Aristocrats". The 1,100-acre property has expansive park land with walking paths—I felt like a character in Jane Eyre, meandering the grounds on a broody, overcast day. Vast grassy knolls, bluebell-lined pathways, small streams and a medieval tower stir your imagination.
—Charissa Fay
