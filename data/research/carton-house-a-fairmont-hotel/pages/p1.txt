The original period details of the manor house merge harmoniously with the contemporary luxury touches one would expect to find from a luxury resort, perfectly paired with a warm Irish welcome to create an unforgettable guest experience.

## Getting Here

### NEARBY AIRPORTS

Dublin International Airport is located just 32km from Carton House via the M50/M4 motorways. This journey takes no more than 25 minutes in non-peak traffic time.

Carton House can happily arrange a private transfer to and/or from the Airport for guests. Quotations available upon request.

Taxi service also available from the Airport to Carton House.

### DRIVING INSTRUCTIONS

**From Dublin**

From Dublin, take the main M4 motorway westbound. From the motorway, take the "Leixlip West" exit (Junction 6) and follow the signs to Carton House. Carton House is approximately a 5-minute drive from the M4 motorway exit.

**From The West**

From the west, take the M4 motorway to Dublin and approximately 20km from Dublin city, take the "Leixlip west" exit (Junction 6) and follow the signs to Carton House. Carton House is approximately a 5-minute drive from the M4 motorway exit.

### METRO & RAIL STATIONS

Carton House is located in Maynooth and lies on the Western Suburban Railway Line which serves three City Centre stations (Pearse Station, Tara Street Station and Connolly Station) as well as eight other suburban stations (Drumcondra, Broombridge, Ashtown, Castleknock, Coolmine, Clonsilla, Leixlip Confey and Leixlip Louisa Bridge).

Information on prices and timetables are available from the Irish Rail website **here**.

A taxi journey from Maynooth Station to Carton House will take approximately 5 minutes.

### PARKING

Carton House offers complimentary car parking on resort for all guests and visitors.

Valet parking available upon request, subject to additional charge.
