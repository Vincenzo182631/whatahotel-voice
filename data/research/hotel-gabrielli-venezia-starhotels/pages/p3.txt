# Gabrielli Restaurant & Bar

## A sensory journey through flavors and refinement

Hotel Gabrielli offers an exclusive culinary experience, where the Venetian gastronomic tradition blends harmoniously with elegant and contemporary design. The Felice al Gabrielli restaurant and the refined K Lounge Bar welcome guests in a sophisticated and inviting atmosphere, enhanced by golden details, green tones, precious mirrors, and fine materials such as Canaletto walnut wood and marble. While the Gabrielli Rooftop Terrace is a haven of tranquility, offering unique views over the Lagoon.

The Breakfast

## The Breakfast

Sweet and savory buffet, fresh juices, smoothies, herbal teas, and made-to-order specialties: breakfast at Hotel Gabrielli celebrates Italian tradition with a variety of flavors to start your day with taste.

Felice al Gabrielli

## Felice al Gabrielli

The Felice al Gabrielli restaurant offers a culinary experience that combines Venetian tradition with modern design, enhancing the seasonality and freshness of local ingredients, reinterpreted with creativity for an authentic and contemporary journey.

discover

K Lounge Bar

## K Lounge Bar

K Lounge Bar is an elegant retreat in the Venetian lagoon, perfect for signature cocktails and fine wines. With a sophisticated atmosphere and impeccable service, it offers an exclusive experience in a captivating setting.

discover

Gabrielli Rooftop Terrace

## Gabrielli Rooftop Terrace

Located on the sixth floor, Terrazza Gabrielli offers a unique view of Venice, stretching from the Lido to the Dogana. A serene retreat to sip a drink at sunset or enjoy dinner under the stars, immersed in the elegance and charm of the city.

Wine Cellar

## Wine Cellar

With refined furnishings and prestigious labels, the Wine Cellar is the perfect setting for events and private tastings in an elegant and welcoming atmosphere.
