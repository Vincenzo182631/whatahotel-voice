# The Hotel that tells the story of Venice

Overlooking the evocative Riva degli Schiavoni, just a few steps from Piazza San Marco, the Biennale Gardens, and the Arsenale, Hotel Gabrielli is a jewel of exclusivity and charm in the heart of Venice. Held by the Perkhofer family for five generations, the hotel has reopened its doors following an exceptional restoration that enhances its historic allure, securing its place among the most exclusive destinations in the lagoon city.

The timeless appeal of its history blends harmoniously with excellence in comfort and contemporary design, enriched with references to Venice’s illustrious past. Guests will have the opportunity to enjoy an extraordinary experience thanks to the panoramic terrace with breathtaking views, the 600-square-meter private garden, the private dock, and the luxurious spa, a true sanctuary for wellness and relaxation. Each space has been designed to offer an atmosphere of exclusivity and tranquility, ideal for an unforgettable stay.

The elegant Felice al Gabrielli restaurant offers an extraordinary culinary journey with refined dishes and selected ingredients, while the cozy K Lounge Bar invites guests to enjoy a signature cocktail in a sophisticated and relaxing setting. A place where history, beauty, and contemporary comfort meet to offer an authentic and timeless experience in the heart of Venice.

Craft Experiences

Strolling through the calli, campi and sestieri of Venice is in itself a unique experience, but even more fascinating is discovering its craft workshops, the source of the city’s universally admired beauty. Its beauty is not only artistic, but also elegant and mysterious, symbolised by the sumptuous costumes and masks of its ancient Carnival.
