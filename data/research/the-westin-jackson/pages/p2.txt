### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Jackson have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Jackson have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Jackson?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at The Westin Jackson. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Jackson?

Some rooms at The Westin Jackson permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

No, rooms at The Westin Jackson do not have a fridge.
