# Welcome to The Westin Jackson

## Find our downtown Jackson hotel in the heart of Mississippi

Find the right balance of inspiration and wellness at The Westin Jackson, a premier hotel in downtown Jackson, MS. Located in the heart of Mississippi’s capital city, guests are within walking distance...See More of the Mississippi Museum of Art, Federal Courthouse, Planetarium, and Thalia Mara Hall. Its central location also makes it a favorite for business and leisure travelers, with the Jackson Convention Complex and Mississippi Coliseum nearby. Savor local flavors at Estelle Southern Table, then unwind at Soul Spa, a leading spa in Jackson, MS, or recharge in the 24-hour fitness studio and indoor pool. Guest rooms feature separate living and sleeping areas, flat-screen TVs, and 24-hour room service. As a welcoming pet-friendly hotel in Jackson, MS, The Westin Jackson makes traveling with your furry companion easy. Whether visiting for business, leisure, or events, you'll find everything you need at The Westin Jackson.See Less

## Music Legends of Mississippi

The Westin Jackson offers an innovative program which celebrates the Birthplace of America’s Music by showcasing exact replica guitars from six legendary pioneers of American Music. Guests are invited to play one of these legendary replicas in the comfort of their own guest room when they book our Music Legends of Mississippi package.

## Pet Policy

We offer a limited selection of pet-friendly guest rooms, and guests are welcome to bring a dog weighing up to 40 pounds. No other pets are permitted. We are happy to provide a Heavenly® Dog Bed, a pet welcome kit, and food bowls with a mat. Room assignments are subject to floors designated as pet-friendly. Not all room types can accommodate a pet. Pets may not be left unattended in a guest room. Contact us for more details.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Jackson?

The check-in time at The Westin Jackson is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Jackson allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Jackson. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Jackson?

On-site valet parking is available at The Westin Jackson. The daily valet fee is $28.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Jackson have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Jackson has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Jackson booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Jackson?

The closest airport near The Westin Jackson is Jackson-Medgar Wiley Evers International Airport (JAN). JAN is located approximately 11.6 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Jackson have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Jackson does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
