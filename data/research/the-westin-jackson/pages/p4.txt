## Mississippi Coliseum

Stay at The Westin Jackson when attending events at the Mississippi Coliseum to make your experience even better. Located just one mile away from our contemporary hotel, The Mississippi Coliseum is home to concerts, special events, sporting events and more. Since its inception in 1962, the 6,500 seat arena has hosted acts as varied as ZZ Top, Willie Nelson, Deanna Carter, Joe Cocker, Vince Gill and many more. It is also the home of The Dixie National Rodeo. The Mississippi Coliseum sits on the grounds of the Mississippi State Fairgrounds, alongside the Kirk Fordice Equine Center.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Jackson have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Jackson offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Free Weights, Treadmill(s), and Yoga mats & Kettle bells, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Jackson have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Jackson has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The Westin Jackson have an on-site spa?

Yes, Soul Spa is located on-site at The Westin Jackson. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Jackson?

Mynelle Gardens Arboretum & Botanical Center, Mississippi Sports Hall of Fame & Museum, LeFleur's Bluff State Park Mayes Lake, Old Capitol Museum, Medgar and Myrlie Evers Home National Monument and more are nearby The Westin Jackson.
