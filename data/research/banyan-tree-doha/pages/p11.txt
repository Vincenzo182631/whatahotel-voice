# Local Attractions

## Located in the heart of Doha

The hotel’s prime position offers guests easy access to key commercial and residential areas as well as the Doha Metro. It’s a 20-minute drive from the Hamad International Airport and in close proximity to Doha’s cultural attractions, nightlife venues, shopping malls and entertainment hub.

### Souq Waqif

A labyrinth full of colors and scents, the Souq Waqif "standing market" is the place to be for those who seek truly authentic vibes. It is home to traditional and modern restaurants and cafes, art galleries, and local retail shops that offer a wide range of goods, from spices and desserts to clothes and textiles. (2.1 km from hotel)

### Qanat Quartier

This Venice-inspired neighbourhood brings a piece of the Mediterranean to Doha. Boasting turquoise canals flanked by candy-coloured buildings and gracefully arched bridges, Qanat Quartier abounds with Instagram-worthy vistas. Grab a coffee, stick your toes in the sand, and take in stunning views of the Arabian Gulf from Qanat Quartier’s beachfront promenade. (16.1 km from hotel)

## Katara Cultural Village

Katara Cultural Village is Doha’s destination for culture and entertainment. A central amphitheatre stands against a backdrop of traditional architecture and cobbled streets, combining art galleries and workshops, theatres and performances venues, and exceptional restaurants set on a waterfront promenade.

Adjoining the main development, a spacious public beach offers water sports and equipment rentals. (9.9 km from hotel)

### Al Bidda Park

The park overlooks Doha Corniche and includes large gymnasiums, including one for women, in addition to outdoor exercise equipment, sports, open and shaded playgrounds, and an open-air theatre which can seat 850 spectators, as well as tracks for bicycles, horses and camels, water fountains, and other recreational and service facilities.

In addition, activities in the park include biking, football, basketball, tennis, dog walking, bird watching and more. (650 m from hotel)

### Al Khor Park and Zoo

Al Khor Park is one of the oldest in the country. The park has an aviary, well-manicured green spaces, a waterfall, water fountains, a miniature golf course, a battery-operated train that can hold 36 people, and a small zoo with oryx, emu, zebras and exotic birds, among others.

There is also a large children’s play area, a basketball court, amphitheater, skating area, restrooms, and a couple of cafeterias. The park is open daily—except on Friday mornings—from 8am to 10pm daily. (47.4 km from hotel)
