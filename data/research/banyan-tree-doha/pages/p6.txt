# Dining

## Banyan Tree Doha at La Cigale Mushaireb

## A haven for gastronomic connoisseurs

From modern Thai cuisine to unique Italian and Indian creations, embark on a culinary escapade at our hotel's celebrated restaurants in Doha, Qatar.

Our collection of sky-high dining pavilions invites global gourmands to explore an ambitious culinary journey, where each distinct Qatar restaurant is made for sensory discovery.

Going beyond the city's gastronomic narrative, our master chefs weave time-honoured culinary traditions with contemporary techniques. Whether you are seeking a classic flavour paired with authentic European recipes or looking to explore the fragrant complexity of eastern spices, our venues carry a collection of refined settings for every occasion. Each plate highlights premium, seasonally sourced ingredients, turning routine corporate luncheons and intimate twilight dinners into unforgettable memories.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

Expand All

##### What dining options does Banyan Tree Doha offer to guests?

Guests can enjoy dining at Jardin, Saffron, Qalamkarri, Il Galante, Vertigo, and In-Room Dining.

##### Can you provide details about the menu and cuisine options available at Banyan Tree Doha?

- Saffron is Banyan Tree’s signature award-winning restaurant. It offers authentic Thai dishes made with fresh and indigenous ingredients.
- Jardin offers a refined dining experience featuring Arabic and Mediterranean cuisine
- Il Galante delivers the true taste of Italy in an elegant ambiance.
- Savour the fusion of traditional Indian flavours and contemporary techniques for a unique dining experience at Qalamkarri.
- Located on level 28, Vertigo Doha is one of the highest clubs in the Mushaireb area with incredible 360-degree views of Doha’s Skyline, Corniche, and old city.

##### Are there any specialty dining experiences or signature dishes at Banyan Tree Doha?

At Banyan Tree Doha, each outlet has its own dining experience and unique signature dishes that are truly one-of-a-kind.

The signature Thai restaurant “Saffron” with an appealing rich menu.

The unique modern twist of “Q Umm Ali” dessert at QalamKarri is a must-try.

The Cold & Hot appetizers of mezze platters with their different flavor combination, and the signature blue Eye dessert at Jardin.

The Authentic Italian dining experience at Il Galante where each flavour tells a story in Italy.

##### Which restaurant is available for guests staying at Banyan Tree Doha?

All the restaurants are open for in-house guests.

##### Does Banyan Tree Doha cater to specific dietary requirements?

Yes, we cater to specific dietary requirements upon request.

##### Can guests make reservations in advance?

Yes, guests can make reservations in advance by reaching out to the restaurants reservation team: +974 4410 3310

##### Are there any outdoor dining options or rooftop restaurants at Banyan Tree Doha?

Yes, Jardin offers outdoor dining options.

##### Which restaurants are suitable for families/ couples at Banyan Tree Doha?

Jardin, Saffron & Qalamkarri are suitable for families, while Il Galante is ideal for couples.

##### Can I book a brunch at Banyan Tree Doha?

Yes, brunch can be booked at Jardin every Friday & at Saffron every Saturday

##### Do you have a dress code in your restaurants at Banyan Tree Doha?

All our restaurants have smart-casual dress code.

##### Do you provide non-smoking areas at Banyan Tree Doha restaurants?

Yes, all indoor restaurant areas are non-smoking.

##### Is there a minimum age policy at Banyan Tree Doha restaurants?

There is no minimum age policy for all restaurants except Vertigo, where the age policy is 18 years old and above.

##### Can I book the entire restaurant for my occasion?

Yes, the entire restaurant can be booked for your occasion, subject to availability and terms and conditions.

##### Do you offer group menu/drinks packages?

Yes, we offer a group menu and drink packages upon request for each outlet.

##### Do you have vegan/ vegetarian menu options?

Yes, we offer vegetarian and vegan dishes across our restaurants.

##### Do you have gluten-free/ dairy-free/nut-free dishes?

Yes, our restaurants offer gluten-free, dairy-free, and nut-free dishes.

##### Do you serve halal food?

Yes, all our outlets serve halal food.
