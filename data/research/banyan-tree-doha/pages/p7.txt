## OUR SPA MENU

From soothing massages and rejuvenating facials to immersive rituals and signature therapies, each experience is tailored to your personal needs by our expert therapists. With every touch, scent, and detail, we invite you to let go, renew your energy, and embrace the serenity that defines Banyan Tree Spa Doha.

### Royal Banyan

Royal Banyan delivers the best of Eastern and Western massage techniques to improve blood circulation and ease muscle aches.

### Balinese

A medium- to strong-pressure massage, where your therapist uses deep thumb and palm techniques in slow, rhythmic strokes. This treatment uplifts the senses and eases muscle tension, leaving you fully invigorated.

### Thai Essence

A medium- to strong-pressure massage inspired by Thai techniques, performed without oil and in a comfortable two-piece outfit. Palms and thumbs apply focused pressure to key points, relieving tired muscles.

## The Rainforest

Banyan Tree Spa presents a fusion spa experience, combining the best of its signature spa treatments with its state-of-the-art hydrothermal facilities within The Rainforest to maximize your wellness experience.

## Spa Sanctuary

The spa features exclusive male and female spaces equipped with treatment rooms, heated beds, a vitality pool complete with a Jacuzzi, relaxation areas, a brine steam room, a herbal sauna and a rain walk.

## Banyan Tree Gallery

Bring the serenity of Banyan Tree Spa Doha home with our curated collections of bath, body, and aromatherapy products. Each item is thoughtfully selected to extend your spa experience, allowing you to nurture your wellbeing in the comfort of your own space.
