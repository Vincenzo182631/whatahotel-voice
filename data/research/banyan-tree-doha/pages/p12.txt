#### Activities & Experiences

Expand All

##### Day 1

Morning

Start your day with a visit to the Museum of Islamic Art, a treasure trove covering 14 centuries of Islamic artistry. Afterwards, take a walk through the nearby park, then pause for a coffee with a panorama of West Bay.

Afternoon

Savour your lunch at the Orient Pearl, then head to the National Museum of Qatar.

Evening

Wind down at Banyan Tree Doha with a delectable meal at Saffron, followed by an unforgettable evening at Vertigo.

##### Day 2

Morning

After breakfast, go on a desert safari. Feel the thrill of dune bashing and take a cooling dip in the Inland Sea.

Afternoon

Have a light lunch at the desert camp, then head back to Doha.

Evening

For dinner, enjoy authentic Italian cuisine at our Il Galante restaurant, all set against a backdrop of Doha's mesmerising skyline.

##### Day 3

Morning

Watch the sunrise while enjoying your breakfast, either in your room or at our all-day dining, Il Galante restaurant. Then, visit the architectural splendour that is the Imam Abdul Wahhab Mosque, also known as the Qatar State Grand Mosque.

Afternoon

Head to Souq Waqif for lunch, then enjoy an afternoon at the country's cultural shopping centre.

Evening

Savour contemporary Indian dishes at Qalamkarri. As the day draws to a close, take a walk along the Doha Corniche and bask in the city's twilight views.

##### Day 4

Morning

After breakfast, embark on a traditional Dhow cruise. Take a cooling dip in the Arabian Sea, then enjoy a BBQ lunch onboard before heading back to the city.

Afternoon

Go on a city tour to learn about Doha's rich history and explore iconic landmarks.

Evening

Indulge in local Qatari delicacies at Souq Waqif, a historic shopping centre in Doha.

##### Day 5

Morning

After breakfast, head to Al Khor Park and Zoo for a leisurely stroll.

Afternoon

Visit Lusail City for a range of entertainment venues, restaurants, and cafés.

Evening

End the day with dinner by the marina at the Pearl, then head back to the hotel.
