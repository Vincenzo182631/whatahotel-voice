# Facilities

## Banyan Tree Doha at La Cigale Mushaireb

## Discover your personal wellbeing journey

Banyan Tree Doha offers everything discerning travellers can expect in a luxury urban resort, including an award-winning spa and an executive lounge. Our hotel with indoor and outdoor swimming pools in Qatar also boasts family-friendly facilities and amenities, such as a fully-equipped fitness centre and a Kids Club.

Built to serve as a dedicated urban retreat for active restoration and leisure, our state-of-the-art health pavilions cater to your physical wellbeing with every ritual. Guests seeking a refreshing escape from the desert heat will discover an architectural masterpiece in our hotel with swimming pool in Qatar, featuring pristine indoor and outdoor swimming lanes framed by comfortable lounging areas. The ambient temperature and natural surroundings converge to form an idyllic haven for leisurely afternoons centred on relaxation.

In this resolved environment, you can swim laps to realign your stamina or simply unwind poolside with a crisp, refreshing botanical drink.
