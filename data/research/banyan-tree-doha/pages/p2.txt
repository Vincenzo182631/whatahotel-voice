## A Serene Stay

Discover your private sanctuary at Banyan Tree Doha. Our elegant hotel rooms and suites seamlessly blend contemporary luxury design with distinct Arabic influences.

Each of our 101 rooms and 111 suites exudes the utmost comfort and serenity. Admire clear, unobstructed views of Doha through expansive floor-to-ceiling glass windows. For a truly luxurious experience, our accommodations feature spacious bedrooms, inviting living spaces, and elegant bathrooms, each with its own separate bathtub and walk-in shower.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

Expand All

##### Early arrival / Late departure policy

• Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability.
• Late check-out from 12:00 pm to 6:00 pm will be charged 50% of the Best Available Rate of the day.
• Late check-out from 6:00 pm will be charged 100% of the Best Available Rate of the day.

##### Best price guarantee

When you book your stay directly on banyantree.com, you'll be guaranteed the best room rate. Should you find a lower price elsewhere, we will match that rate and give you an additional 20% discount.

##### Cancelation policy

Enjoy the flexibility of free cancellation up to 24 hours before your arrival. Cancellations made after this period will incur charges equivalent to one night's accommodation.

##### What is the size of a king bed/ twin beds/ extra bed?

A king bed measures 200 x 200cm, while twin beds measure 200 x 100 cm and Extra beds measure 200 x 100 cm

##### Do you provide a free extra bed/ baby cot?

Extra beds are chargeable, and Baby cots are free depending on the availability

##### Do rooms, suites & residences have balconies?

We don’t have balconies in the rooms and Suites. However, all our residences have balconies.

##### Do you have smoking rooms?

No, we don’t

##### Do I need to pay a security deposit?

Yes, a security deposit of QAR 400 per person per night is required.

##### How can I request an early check-in?

Early Check-ins are subject to availability. Don't hesitate to get in touch with our reservations department by calling +974 4410 3333 to request it.

##### Can you use the hotel's facilities before check-in?

Yes, you can use all hotel facilities while waiting for your check-in.

##### What is included in the Half Board package?

The Half Board package includes breakfast and dinner.

##### Are there tea and coffee-making amenities in the rooms?

Yes, each room is equipped with a kettle and a Nespresso coffee machine.

##### Does the hotel cater to people with special needs in their rooms?

Yes, we have several rooms set up for people with special needs.

##### Do rooms come with an iron and ironing board?

Yes, each room has an iron and ironing board.

##### Are all the residences fully equipped and fully furnished?

Yes, all our residences are fully furnished with the latest equipments.

##### How can I arrange for a late check-out?

Late check-out can be arranged with the hotel reception directly or at the time of booking through reservations and is subject to availability.

##### Can I still use the hotel facilities after checking out?

Yes, you can still access our restaurants.

##### How can I provide feedback on my stay?

Please share your feedback with us through platforms like TripAdvisor, Google Review, and Booking.com, or through our WhatsApp message & email sent from our end.

##### How can I get a copy of my bill?

A copy of your bill will be emailed to you immediately after your departure or printed upon checking out.

##### I left something at the hotel, can you help me find it?

Please contact the hotel by calling +974 4410 3333 we will assist you in locating your lost item.
