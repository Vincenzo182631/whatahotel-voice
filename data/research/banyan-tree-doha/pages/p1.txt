## An elegant urban sanctuary in the vibrant city of Doha

Located in the heart of Mushaireb, Banyan Tree Doha is a premier 5-star hotel that blends unparalleled luxury with modern comfort and architectural splendour.
Rising above the shimmering skyline, our urban sanctuary embraces the essence of design-led elegance, establishing itself as a premier 5-star hotel in Doha.

Every detail of the property represents a harmony of traditional Asian principles and rich Arabian heritage, masterfully orchestrated by visionary interior design. Guests are welcomed into a light-filled oasis that serves to shield the senses from the frantic pace of the capital outside.

From the one-of-a-kind vantage points to the personalised service that is available throughout your stay, the entire environment functions as a spacious home base for global citizens who long for uncompromising luxury and comfort.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

Expand All

##### Does Banyan Tree Doha Qatar offer special packages that include local attractions or experiences?

Yes, we offer several stay packages, including access to Quest Theme Park, and special offers on F&B. For more details, please visit our offers page at https://www.banyantree.com/qatar/doha/offers

##### Can I request a specific view or room location when booking at Banyan Tree Doha Qatar?

Yes, you may request a specific view or room location when booking with us, subject to availability. To request in advance, kindly contact our team at 974 4410 3333 or email us at info-doha@banyantree.com.

##### What on-site activities and amenities are available at Banyan Tree Doha Qatar?

At Banyan Tree Doha Qatar, we offer a diverse range of on-site amenities for our guests to enjoy:

- Signature Banyan Tree Spa with Rainforest facility
- Indoor and outdoor pool
- Four restaurants
- Club lounge
- Bar lounge
- Patisserie.
- Direct access to Quest Theme Park, Vox Cinema, Printemps Doha, and Padel Court

##### Are there any popular attractions near Banyan Tree Doha Qatar?

We are strategically located between West Bay and the Old City, with proximity to Souq Waqif, Corniche, Qatar National Museum, Museum of Islamic Art and Musheireb Museums. Nestled within Doha Oasis, we provide direct access to the indoor theme park Quest and the largest luxury department store in the Middle East, Printemps Doha, as well as direct access to Doha Oasis Padel Court.

##### Can Banyan Tree Doha Qatar help arrange activities like city tours or desert safaris?

Yes, our concierge team is available to assist you in curating and booking the most popular touristic activities in Doha.

##### Does Banyan Tree Doha Qatar offer on-site parking for guests?

Yes, we offer free on-site parking and valet parking service for guests at Banyan Tree Doha Qatar.

##### How far is Banyan Tree Doha Qatar from the nearest city centre?

We are located in the heart of Msheireb, a 10-minute drive from the City Centre mall in the West Bay district.

##### Do you provide transportation from and to the airport at Banyan Ttree Doha?

Airport pick-up & drop-off are available for an extra cost. Please feel free to contact Concierge-Doha@banyantree.com{"target":null} for arrangements.

##### How much is a taxi from Doha Airport to Banyan Tree Doha?

The taxi fare from Hamad International Airpot to Banyan Tree Doha ranges from QAR 40 to QAR 50 depending on the peak hours.

##### Can guests enjoy private beach access or exclusive beach experiences at Banyan Tree Doha?

Guests can enjoy free access to West Bay Beach which is only 10-min drive from the hotel.

##### Are the residences located in the hotel tower?

No, Banyan Tree Doha Residences are located adjacent and conveniently connected to the hotel tower, only a few steps away from the main hotel lobby.

##### Are the residences for long or short term stays?

Banyan Tree Doha Residences are for long & short-term stays. The check-in for residences is done in the hotel lobby. The guests are then escorted to their residence by a golf cart.

##### Do you provide housekeeping and room service in the residences?

Yes, both services are provided daily for short-stay guests.
