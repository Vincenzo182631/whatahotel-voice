# Wildlife Excursions

Caribou grazing along coastal barrens, foxes running up craggy hills, and whales swimming past our shores — life does get wild on Fogo Island.

Explore our natural world with one of our Outdoor Adventure guides to enhance your encounters with local fauna. While winter promises glimpses of our elusive caribou herd and of seals arriving on ice floes, summer is best for whale watching and for spotting the colonies of puffins nesting on nearby Little Fogo Islands.

Combine Wildlife Excursions with Hiking or elevate your experience with a boat trip to Little Fogo Islands, available for an additional fee.
