# Hiking

The best way to explore Fogo Island is by foot, wandering through scenery that will often surprise you.

Hike or walk countless paths and trails to discover abandoned communities, towering art studios, craggy shores, and boreal forests. Go your own way or follow one of our Outdoor Adventure Guides who will identify flora and fauna and share the stories of our natural world. Explore our Hiking Trails to see what awaits.

Combine Hiking with our Foraging,Bushcraft, or Wildlife Watching.

- ##### Good to know

  - Trails and paths vary by activity levels; let us recommend which ones are right for you.

  - Trails can be muddy, snowy, or rocky. While rubber boots are available, we recommend guests bring their own hiking boots or shoes.

  - Active apparel, including outerwear, pants, hats, gloves and crampons, as well as insect repellent and head nets, are available if required.

  - Treats and a packed lunch can be provided if requested.

  - Experience this activity on your own or accompanied by one of our knowledgeable Outdoor Adventure Guides.

  - Find our Guest Experiences team in the Tea Room to plan your excursion.
