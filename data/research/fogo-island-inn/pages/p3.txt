# Rates & Inclusions

### Included with your stay

Experience full-board hospitality rooted in community-centred values. There is no private gain from your stay: all operating surpluses are reinvested in the community through Shorefast.

By choosing a stay at Fogo Island Inn, you’re making a direct and meaningful impact on our community. Want to know more? See our Economic Nutrition CM label to understand how our commitment is reflected in our rates.

### Included with your stay

Beyond your preferred room or suite, our rates also include all the services and experiences that come together to create an inimitable stay, without hidden costs, tips, or resort fees.

### Dining at the Inn   and across the island

Every dish here tells a story. Sample modern interpretation of Fogo Island's foodways through every meal included in your stay.

- Breakfast, lunch, and supper daily
- Welcome snack and departure lunch pack
- 24-hour In-Between menu
- Daybreak service, Tea Room treats, and lunches for hikes
- All non-alcoholic beverages
- Special meals, including traditional dishes in The Shed
- Meals at any restaurant on the island

Discover Food & Drink

### Spaces & Amenities

Your room is your refuge, but so is every corner of the Inn. Make the most of all available amenities, services, and more.

- Carbon offset for your entire stay, including ground transportation
- Fast and reliable WiFi
- 24-hour room service
- Nightly turndown service
- Use of sustainably made robes, slippers, and more in-room amenities
- Access to all amenities at the Inn, including Cinema, Art Gallery, Hot Tubs, and more
- Valet service, including departure ferry coordination

Explore our spaces

### Island experiences

When you stay at Fogo Island Inn, you’re also a guest of the island. Immerse yourself in its nature, culture, and heritage through included experiences.

- Outdoor guides and personalized itineraries for land excursions and hikes
- An island orientation with a Community Host
- On-island shuttle service
- Use of Fat Bikes, E-Bicycles, and regular bicycles
- All hiking essentials and weatherproof outdoor apparel

View all activities

### Enhance your stay

Select services, beverages, and experiences are available for an additional cost. Consult with our Guest Itinerary Advisors before your stay to enhance your stay with:

- Alcoholic beverages, including wine pairings selected by our Sommelier and cocktails crafted with local ingredients by our expert bartenders
- Seasonal excursions offered by local entrepreneurs, such as boat or helicopter tripsand fishing
- Transport to and from Fogo Island

- ##### Terms & Conditions

All rates exclude a 15% harmonized sales tax (HST). Rates are calculated per night, per room, and based on 2 guests per room. Suite rates are based on 2 to 5 guests per room, occupancy permitting. Solo travellers receive a CAD $300 per night reduction. Children aged 8 and older are welcome to stay with us. A minimum of 3 nights is required, but a 4-night stay is recommended to enjoy a fulfilling Fogo Island experience.
