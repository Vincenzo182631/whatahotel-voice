# Bushcraft

Embrace Fogo Island’s still-wild world through one of our favourite activities to share with guests.

Follow our Outdoor Adventure Guides on an immersive, off-the-grid experience for active adventurers. You’ll set out to a bushcraft camp where guides will share their knowledge on foraging, as well as bushcraft skills and crafts. Enjoy tea made from foraged plants, learn how to whittle your own tools, and immerse yourself in the wilderness.

Learn more about the experience in this blog. Combine Bushcraft with a Traditional Boil-Up Lunch, Foraging, or Hiking.

- ##### Good to know

  - This excursion takes place off-road; trails can be muddy or rocky.

  - Treats, a packed lunch, or a traditional outdoor lunch can be provided if requested.

  - Rubber boots are available, but we recommend guests bring their own hiking boots.

  - Active apparel, including outerwear, is available if required.

  - Experience this guided activity accompanied by one of our knowledgeable Outdoor Adventure Guides.

  - Find our Guest Experiences team in the Tea Room to plan your excursion.
