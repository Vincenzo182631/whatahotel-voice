# Island Orientation

Fogo Island is home to 11 distinct communities, each with their own stories to tell.

Orient yourself on the island through this immersive guided tour led by one of our Community Hosts. Learn the culture, history, and traditions of our island, pop into one of the many museums, craft shops, or art studios, admire the fishing stages and saltbox houses, and stop for a chat with our local community members.

Combine anIsland Orientationwith Museums of Fogo Island or Hiking.

- ##### Good to know

  - Treats and a packed lunch are provided on request for half-day or full-day excursions. Dining at one of the island’s restaurants is also included in your stay.

  - Experience this activity accompanied by one of our Community Hosts. For a self-guided journey, guests may borrow a car from our fleet, while your Community Host will be your driver for the guided option.

  - Find our Guest Experiences team in the Tea Room to plan your excursion.
