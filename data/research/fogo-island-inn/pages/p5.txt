# Sea Kayaking

Experience the community of Joe Batt’s Arm as our ancestors would have seen it, from the quiet solitude of the water.

Kayak in the North Atlantic with our Outdoor Adventure Guides. Paddle around the harbour while learning about Fogo Island’s history and sense of place. Wave to community members cleaning fish on their stages and begin to understand how the ocean supported the island and its people for centuries. Don’t forget to capture an iconic photograph of the Inn from the water!

Combine Kayaking with a visit to the Punt Premises or a stop at Growlers.

##### Number of Guests

Max. 6 per group

- ##### Good to know

  - Activity depends on the weather; favourable conditions are necessary.
  - We recommend a high level of fitness to fully enjoy this kayaking experience
  - Life jackets are provided. Active apparel, including outerwear, wetsuits and hats, are available if required.
  - Treats and a packed lunch can be provided if requested.
  - Experience this guided activity accompanied by one of our knowledgeable Outdoor Adventure Guides.
  - Activity is offered twice a day, twice a week. Find our Guest Experiences team in the Tea Room to join an excursion.
