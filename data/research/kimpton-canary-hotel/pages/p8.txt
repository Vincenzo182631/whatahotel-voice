# Kimpton Canary HotelLocation

Kimpton Canary Hotel sits in the heart of downtown Santa Barbara, between the Santa Ynez Mountains and the ocean. Stroll vintage shops and cafes on State Street or tour tasting rooms on the Urban Wine Trail, then hit the beach for sun, sand and surf.

## Things to do

### State Street

State Street is one of those amazing city arteries that in one street, captures every color, flavor and nuance of the town. And in seductive Santa Barbara, that's saying a lot. At the Kimpton Canary Hotel, we know how lucky we are to be one the only luxury hotel near State Street Santa Barbara, which stretches from the north end of town all the way to the oceanfront's Stearns Wharf.

Learn more

### Sterns Wharf

217 Stearns Wharf, Santa Barbara, CA 93101

Stern’s Wharf is one of Santa Barbara’s hot spots and you’ll find it just over a mile from Kimpton Canary. Consider it a catch all for amazing local dining, sightseeing, shopping and exploring. It’s the longest deep water wharf between San Francisco and Los Angeles and steeped in history which is why the Santa Barbara Museum of Natural History Sea Center also calls the Wharf home.

Learn more

### Santa Barbara Museum of Art

1130 State Street, Santa Barbara, CA 93101

Founded in 1941, it is home to both permanent and special collections, the former of which includes Asian, American, and European art that spans 4,000 years from ancient to modern.

Learn more

### East Beach

East Beach located in Santa Barbara, California is often the first beach seen upon entering the city limits. East Beach is separated from West Beach by Stearn's Wharf, and is proximal to downtown Santa Barbara.

Learn more

### Wine Country

Taste some of the finest wines produced in Santa Barbara County, all within blocks of downtown and the beach.

Learn more

### Funk Zone

Several walkable blocks encompassing art galleries, tasting rooms, boutique shops and fine foods.

Learn more

### Butterfly Beach

A true local's beach perfect for people watching, swimming, hiking and off-leash fun with pets.

Learn more

### Hendry's Beach

Located on Cliff Drive next to the Boathouse, an upscale popular seafood restaurant. This off-leash pet-friendly beach is ideal for surfing swimming or nearby trails.

Learn more
