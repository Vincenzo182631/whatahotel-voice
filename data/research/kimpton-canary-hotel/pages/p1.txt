Relax and indulge at the iconic Canary Hotel, a true Santa Barbara luxury retreat. With thoughtful perks like a complimentary wine hour, rental bikes, and a yoga mat in each room, we offer a truly unique and luxurious stay. Book directly and let our dedicated staff guide you to your next Santa Barbara adventure.

### Rooftop Pool + Lounge

Our rooftop pool terrace will captivate you with its evocative ambiance and breathtaking 360-degree vistas that run from the mountains to the sea, and encompass Santa Barbara’s dynamic downtown. Soak in the pool or recline with a cocktail and savor every relaxing moment.

Learn more

### Specialty Suites

Imagine staying in the height of comfort and style: lingering over a decadent breakfast at your dining table, lounging with a glass of local wine in your living room, gazing at the sunset from your balcony. With a variety of specialty suites on offer, you have your choice of luxurious lodging for a truly elevated stay.

Learn more

### Finch & Fork

Indulge in our specially crafted menu at Finch & Fork, where the essence of Santa Barbara's vibrant food scene comes to life. Immerse yourself in the coastal charm with our succulent oysters, diver scallops, and the buttery flavor of King salmon. Each plate is a masterpiece at our Santa Barbara seafood restaurant.

Learn more

### Weddings

The Kimpton Canary makes for a beautiful backdrop for weddings, receptions, and rehearsal dinners, from sophisticated Mediterranean-style ballrooms to the dreamy romance of our outdoor terrace. Crowd-pleasing local catering, convenient room blocks and detail-oriented staff guarantee your big day will be flawless; and the focus is on the two of you.

Learn more

### Rooftop Event Space

What special twist could you add to your next event to make it a sure-fire success? How about celebrating, meeting, awarding – whatever moves you – on top of the world at downtown Santa Barbara's largest rooftop pool? Let us to help you elevate your event at our 1,750-square-foot rooftop event space, accented with Mediterranean flair & furnishings.

## FAQs for Canary Hotel

Does Kimpton Canary Hotel have a pool?

Kimpton Canary Hotel has 1 outdoor pool.

Rooftop Pool

Serene rooftop with panoramic views spanning the ocean and mountains. Indulge in crafted cocktails expertly prepared by our skilled mixologists, and savor delectable delights from a chef-driven menu that promises a symphony of local flavors.

How much is parking at Kimpton Canary Hotel?

At Kimpton Canary Hotel, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 55 USD .

Kimpton Canary Hotel offers overnight valet parking services for $55 per night. Valet vehicles are available 24 hours a day. Day parking is available for $33.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Canary Hotel?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Canary Hotel.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Canary Hotel?

Check-in at Kimpton Canary Hotel is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is Kimpton Canary Hotel from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Canary Hotel is conveniently located near Santa Barbara Municipal Airport (SBA), approximately 9.7 miles away (15.61 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Canary Hotel?

Kimpton Canary Hotel is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- State Street
- Sterns Wharf-1.5 miles away (2.41 kilometers)
- Santa Barbara Museum of Art-0.3 miles away (0.48 kilometers)
- East Beach-2.6 miles away (4.18 kilometers)
- Wine Country-30 miles away (48.28 kilometers)
- Funk Zone-0.5 miles away (0.8 kilometers)
- Butterfly Beach-4.5 miles away (7.24 kilometers)
- Hendry's Beach-3.8 miles away (6.12 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Canary Hotel puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
