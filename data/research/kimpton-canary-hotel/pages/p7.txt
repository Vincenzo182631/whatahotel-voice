### Past it Prime Property Lacks Value

11 days ago

I really wanted to like Kimpton Canary Hotel and there were things I did like: its boutique size, friendly, attentive staff and its location right in the heart of San Barbara. Unfortunately, the dated hard product and amenities couldn't overcome the $900+/night weekday rate charged. It's not a bad place to stay, but there are similar properties in the area offering better value that just make this one hard to justify in its current state.
