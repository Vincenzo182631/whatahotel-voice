# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self-Parking

Park yourself in luxury—whether you roll in with style or let our valet take the wheel, Andaz Capital Gate Abu Dhabi has your ride covered. While we don’t offer shaded or indoor spots, our concierge team is here to guide you every step of the way. \*Prices may vary during peak and exhibition periods. Complimentary for guests visiting restaurants/spa with a minimum spend of AED 100/-. Visitor parking fee of AED 50 applies.

### Valet Parking

Complimentary for all resident guests.

Complimentary for all resident guests

Free

### Electric Vehicle Charging

2 Power points for EV charging available on ground floor. Each universal power socket supports up to 32amps at 220 – 240 volts. EV Charging complimentary for resident guests or guests visiting restaurants/spa with a minimum spend of AED 100. Visitor EV Charging fee of AED 50 applies.

## Transportation

Getting here

Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH)16 miles16 miles

We’re happy to arrange a limousine service for our guests arriving from Abu Dhabi International Airport to the hotel. Please contact our Andaz hosts on +971 2 596 1234 or abudhabi.capitalgate@hyatt.com for bookings. Special airport taxis are also available from the Arrivals Hall.

Transportation Options

Taxi

Clean, safe and reliable taxi service is easily available throughout the city.
