# Experience Our Luxury Hotel, Distinctively Local in Every Sense

Andaz Capital Gate Abu Dhabi, located only 15 minutes from the airport and key attractions and adjacent to ADNEC, is situated in the iconic Capital Gate, which leans 18 degrees and stands tall as the gateway to our luxury lifestyle hotel. Arrive a visitor and depart a local, as the hotel’s art gallery, locally inspired details and curated dining experiences transform your stay.

## Rooms & Suites

Gaze upon the capital with unsurpassed views from floor-to-ceiling windows. Enjoy eclectic local art and inspired stories woven into every room, with seamless designs for living, working and sleeping that give you the freedom to be you.

## DINING

View All Dining

### Andaz Lounge

Discover the Andaz Lounge, tucked inside the iconic Guinness World Record-holding Leaning Tower with its striking 18-degree tilt. This chic space is designed for connection and indulgence, offering a tempting array of vegan pastries, artisanal confections, specialty cakes, and velvety coffee blends served 18 floors above the city.

Details + Reservations

### Cyan Brasserie

A modern brasserie that artfully blends the classic charm of a Parisian eatery where French-rooted cuisine harmonizes with the warm hospitality of the Middle East, inviting guests to indulge in a sophisticated dining experience. With a focus on seasonal ingredients for social dining, Cyan celebrates the essence of both regions.

Details + Reservations

### 18 Degrees Bar & Terrace

18 Degrees Bar & Terrace offers innovative cocktails in a sleek, contemporary setting within the world’s most iconic leaning tower. Perfect for pre-or post-dinner drinks with colleagues or friends, enjoying breathtaking sunset views, or sipping under the stars for an unforgettable evening.

Details + Reservations

### The Lean

Suspended high above the city, this reimagined pool deck blends refined style with playful sophistication, inviting guests to unwind in a calm, sunlit setting by day and immerse themselves in a vibrant, sensory atmosphere by night. With its bold aesthetic, creative energy, and distinctive Andaz flair, The Lean stands as Abu Dhabi’s most stylish rooftop escape, a place where art, architecture, and authenticity flow together seamlessly.

Details + Reservations

### Room Service

Indulge in the comfort of your own space with Andaz In-Room Dining. Thoughtfully crafted menus showcase globally inspired flavors with a local touch, available throughout the day and into the night. Whether a relaxed breakfast, a late-night craving, or a private dining moment, enjoy effortless service designed around you. To order, dial extension 1461/1462 from the in-room phone.

# The Cyan Local Table

Thoughtfully designed for sharing, The Cyan Local Table brings people together over familiar flavors and comforting traditions. Enjoy warm stone-baked pita with a generous mezze selection, followed by a vibrant mixed grill with classic sides. The experience concludes with Arabic coffee, Moroccan mint tea, and dates, a warm celebration of regional hospitality and togetherness. Served both during lunch and dinner hours at Cyan Brasserie for AED 499 for a table of four guests.

Designed as both an introduction and an invitation, this experience offers a glimpse into the depth of our creations and calls you back for more, At AED 200 per person.
