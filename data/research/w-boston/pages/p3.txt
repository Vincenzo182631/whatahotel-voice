## Meet Our Music Curator

DJ Braun Dapper came up in the Boston club scene in the early 2000's, Braun took his aggressive quick paces mixing style to venues all over the city. Braun has played with underground masters such as DJ Riz and Kenny Dope and mega pop superstars like Steve Aoki and David Guetta. Whether it's a house parts, his residency at W Lounge, or any numerous corporate events with Reebok or Red Bull, Braum Dapper rocks the spot.
