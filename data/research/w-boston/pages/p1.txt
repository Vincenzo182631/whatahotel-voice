# Welcome to W Boston

## Hit the scene at W Boston, our Boston, MA luxury hotel

Immerse in the music, fashion and art of Downtown Boston. W Boston is a contemporary downtown Boston luxury hotel with style and sophistication. Comfort awaits in our boutique hotel rooms showcasing signature amenities, custom décor and stunning views of our iconic Theatre District. Your enhanced accommodations include slate-finished bathrooms and custom murals. Savor New England staples and international...See More dishes at the Gallery Restaurant and W Lounge. As a downtown central Boston hotel, we also offer vibrant event spaces for any gathering in this historic destination. As a pet-friendly Boston hotel, when you’re ready to tackle the city, you can attend a Broadway show, shop locally or explore celebrated attractions, including Fenway Park, New England Aquarium and Chinatown.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Boston?

The check-in time at W Boston is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Boston allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Boston. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at W Boston?

On-site valet parking is available at W Boston. The daily valet fee is $76.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Boston have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Boston has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Boston booking.

What is the closest airport near W Boston?

The closest airport near W Boston is Boston Logan International Airport (BOS). BOS is located approximately 4.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Boston have an airport shuttle to Boston Logan International Airport (BOS)?

No, W Boston does not have an airport shuttle to Boston Logan International Airport.

Does W Boston have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Boston has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
