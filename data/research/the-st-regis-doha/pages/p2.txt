### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Doha have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Doha have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The St. Regis Doha?

The largest occupancy room at The St. Regis Doha is the Royal Suite with a maximum occupancy of 9.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The St. Regis Doha?

Some rooms at The St. Regis Doha permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Doha have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Doha have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Doha have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Doha have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Doha have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Doha have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The St. Regis Doha have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The St. Regis Doha have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
