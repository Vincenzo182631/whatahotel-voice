# Welcome to The St. Regis Doha

## Your Luxury Resort Destination at the Finest Address in Doha

Escape to an unforgettable stay at a luxury beach resort in our 5-star hotel in Doha, Qatar, steeped in timeless traditions. Majestically positioned along the Arabian Gulf in West Bay, The St. Regis Doha is a luxury hotel in West Bay, Doha, embodying refined hospitality and Middle Eastern heritage. Discover world-class restaurants, from authentic local flavors to renowned international cuisine, with views of Pearl Island and proximity to Katara Cultural Village. Guests praise the attentive service, elegant facilities, and immaculate spaces, while the signature St. Regis Butler Service enhances every stay. Anchored by 335 luxury rooms and suites with sea views, sophisticated décor, and state-of-the-art technology, this West Bay hotel in Doha, Qatar is an exceptional setting for weddings, celebrations, and business events. The Vilebrequin La Plage collaboration elevates beach experiences with French Riviera style, while the award-winning Guerlain Spa offers bespoke treatments for ultimate relaxation.

## The Sustainable Living in Doha

At The St. Regis Doha, our commitment to sustainability is at the heart of everything we do. We recognize our responsibility to protect and preserve our environment for future generations.

This commitment is exemplified by "Athar" (The Fertile Loop), a regenerative initiative introduced with Al Fardan Corporation. We transform organic waste into nutrient-rich compost for local farms, which in turn supply our kitchens with fresh produce. Guests can trace this sustainable journey from the soil to their plate by scanning QR codes on our curated menus, experiencing a modern expression of care where nothing is wasted.

Learn More

Sustainable Environmental Practices

We are committed to specific eco-certifications and practices, including the Green Key certification, to decrease our carbon footprint and maintain high environmental standards.

1\. Energy & Water Conservation

High-Efficiency Lighting in Guest Rooms & Public Areas

To maintain a consistency of sustainable practices throughout the hotel, our guest rooms and public areas are equipped with high-efficiency lighting. This ensures equivalent lighting quality while consuming significantly less electricity than standard bulbs.

Occupancy Sensors

Our occupancy sensors detect the presence of a person, enabling us to control lights and energy-saving thermostats. This helps us ensure energy is not wasted when spaces are unoccupied.

Water Conserving Fixtures

A key part of our conservation strategy includes water-efficient taps and toilets throughout the property.

Smart Irrigation Practices

Our smart irrigation technology helps us preserve resources, using systems to monitor conditions and adjust watering schedules to the actual needs of the site.

Regular Preventative Maintenance

Our monitored schedule includes regular adjustments and repairs to ensure all systems are running efficiently.

2\. Waste Minimization & Environmentally Responsible Purchasing

Recycling

The hotel utilizes a recycling program that reduces the amount of waste produced. This includes safely handling hazardous substances, the safe disposal of batteries, and the use of refillable toiletry containers.

Organic Waste Composting

We practice organic waste composting and utilize compostable food containers and cutlery to minimize landfill impact.

Environmentally Preferred Suppliers & Products

The St. Regis Doha supports responsible purchasing policies by selecting environmentally preferred suppliers and products.

Sustainable Food and Beverage Options

We locally source food and beverages to support local strategies and reduce transport emissions. Additionally, our menus feature extensive vegetarian and vegan meal options.

3\. Enhanced Indoor Environmental Quality

Integrated Pest Management Practices

We use a broad-based approach that integrates practices for economic and safe control of pests.

Low-Emitting Materials

To improve indoor air quality, we utilize low-emitting materials such as paints, flooring, and furniture throughout the hotel.

4\. Raising Awareness

Property-Level Green Council

We maintain a property-level council to oversee and drive our sustainability initiatives.

Sustainable Meetings and Practices

We offer sustainable meeting options to help contribute to our objective of environmental responsibility.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Doha?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Doha is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Doha allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Doha.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Doha?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Doha. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Doha have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Doha has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Doha booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Doha?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Doha is Hamad International Airport (DOH). DOH is located approximately 23.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Doha have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Doha does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
