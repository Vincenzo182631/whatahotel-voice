## Olympic Pool

Our Olympic pool offers guests a luxurious and tranquil space to relax and unwind, overlooking the Arabian Gulf Sea. From the elegant design to the well-maintained temperature and poolside amenities, every aspect has been carefully considered to provide an exceptional experience. Whether you're looking to swim laps, bask in the sun, or enjoy a refreshing drink, The St. Regis Doha outdoor Olympic pool is the perfect destination for a relaxing day by the pool.

## Beach Escape

Our private beach offer a truly exceptional seaside experience for spending time with your love ones or unforgettable family time. The pristine beach and well-maintained, with crystal-clear waters and soft, white sand where guests can relax in the sun on comfortable lounge chairs or take a dip in the ocean.

## Outdoor Padel Court

From the high-quality materials used to construct the court to the thoughtful amenities provided for players, every detail has been carefully considered to provide an exceptional padel experience. Whether you're looking for a relaxing game of padel or a competitive tournament, The St. Regis Doha outdoor padel court is the perfect destination for players of all skill levels.

## Family Traditions

Family Traditions at St. Regis provides an experience beyond expectation. With captivating activities and thoughtful, welcoming attention tailored for younger guests and their families, each St. Regis celebrates the art of play and being together.

## Kids Learning Center

Introducing the The St. Regis Doha Kids Learning Center, a unique haven for young minds that goes beyond play, offering the first and only educational oasis of its kind in Doha. Children will engage in a spectrum of enriching activities, from unleashing their creativity through drawing and coloring, gaining insights with tailored educational lessons, exploring their musical talents, and enjoying outdoor adventures. Our interactive workshops foster critical thinking, and even culinary adventures await, all within a luxurious setting. More than a play area, this center nurtures a lifelong love of learning, guided by dedicated mentors who inspire young minds to thrive, creating unforgettable family memories. Children must always be accompanied by a parent, guardian, or nanny while using the Kids Club facilities.

Family Amenities & Services

Recreation

Open to children of all ages, the Olympic-size pool and the crystal clear waters of the Arabian Gulf make ideal settings for families to spend time together.

Dining

We recommend the poolside Vilebrequin La Plage for a relaxing afternoon, Vine—for all-day dining--or Astor Grill for an intimate evening, which offers a more casual setting, or Private Dining to be enjoyed in the comfort of your room. Menus that appeal to all ages are available at any of these dining options.

Necessities & Provisions

Our hotel is supplied with a variety of family necessities such as wipes, diapers, cribs, and rollaway beds. Allow St. Regis Butler Service to assist you with preparing these items for you during your stay or before your arrival at the hotel. Please contact the eButler for assistance.

Family Experiences

As part of the Family Traditions at St. Regis program, families can choose from a selection of activities to experience together while exploring the enriching culture of the destination. We look forward to creating indelible memories for your family to share.

These experiences are perfectly suited for a family of four (two adults and two children), but can always be customized. The Concierge is pleased to provide more details and to help make arrangements. To reserve an experience, it is recommended that guests contact the Concierge at least 24 hours in advance.

Desert Safari

Journey to the South of Qatar where professional drivers will host you and your family on an exciting desert adventure. Upon reaching the desert boarders, the tires will be deflated for a thrilling dune exploration. Enjoy the sprawling dune vistas and visit the sparkling inland sea that borders Qatar and Saudi Arabia. A desert camp will host the whole family for a BBQ feast, swimming, a game of volleyball, table tennis, sand boarding, a camel ride, or a quad bike ride in the desert. The day's schedule can be set at the time of booking, leaving early in the morning or late afternoon, upon guest preference. Please note this experience is appropriate for children ages four and above.

## Jet Ski

Experience the thrill of adventure on the azure waters of the Arabian Gulf at The St. Regis Doha, where guests are invited to embark on an exhilarating jet ski escapade along the hotel's private beach. Feel the rush of the wind and the splash of the sea as you navigate the pristine waves on your own personal watercraft. Our expertly trained staff ensures a safe and unforgettable experience, whether you're a seasoned jet ski enthusiast or a first-time rider.

## House of Celebrations: Where Happenings Become Legendary

At St. Regis, we redefine what it means to celebrate. Our House of Celebrations presents a series of bespoke events designed to captivate and inspire, ensuring that each moment becomes a lasting memory of elegance and grandeur.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Doha have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Doha offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Free Weights, Smith Machine, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Doha have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Doha has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Doha have an on-site spa?

Yes, Guerlain Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Doha. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Doha?

City Center Shopping Mall, Museum of Islamic Art, The Pearl Qatar, Katara Cultural Village, Souq Waqif and more are nearby The St. Regis Doha.
