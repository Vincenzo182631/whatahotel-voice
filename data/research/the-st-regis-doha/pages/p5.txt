## The St. Regis Doha Invites You to Live Exquisite

Set along the Arabian Gulf, our address blends bespoke luxury with the storied legacy of St. Regis. From the signature rituals to the art of impeccable service, every moment is thoughtfully curated to inspire and enchant. Discover an extraordinary destination where refinement knows no limits, and every experience is an invitation to indulge.

## Summer at the   St. Regis Doha

Bask in the golden sun and embrace the essence of summer at The St. Regis Doha. Lounge by the shimmering infinity pool, where the waters mirror the endless blue of the Arabian Gulf. Stroll along our private beach, a serene escape with soft sands and refreshing sea breezes. Indulge in exhilarating water sports, or unwind in a private cabana with bespoke service at your fingertips.

## Vilebrequin La Plage

Where effortless elegance meets the spirit of the Riviera. Vilebrequin La Plage is a sanctuary of seaside sophistication, offering a refined yet relaxed beachfront experience. Indulge in Mediterranean-inspired cuisine, sip on signature cocktails, and revel in the sun-drenched ambiance of Doha’s most exclusive beach club. Whether dining under the stars or enjoying a leisurely afternoon.
