## The Artisan Istanbul MGallery

### Welcome

* * *

_“Istanbul is a unique soul with a long and magnificent history and an amazing 21st-century destination blending its historical spirit with the modern vibe of the world. This fabulous city setting along the Bosphorus waterfront offers history, culture, art, culinary, shopping, and nightlife at the same time and succeeds in tantalizing every sense._

_Providing an expansive list of cultural icons to visit, Istanbul will keep you busy throughout your stay, however, I recommend you not to leave the city before feeling the breeze and smell of the Bosphorus coast, sipping Turkish coffee, experiencing the mystical ambiance of the bazaars while enjoying the new, modern, and bohemian side of the city as well._

_Just a few steps from the most famous leisure district, Istiklal Street and Pera, lies a boutique hotel in a surprisingly quiet street from Taksim to Dolmabahçe, The Artisan Hotel Istanbul – MGallery Collection as a showcase of sublime Turkish hospitality. Our hotel is located mere steps from the Bosphorus coast and within walking distance from Taksim. It’s also very convenient in terms of being a ten-minute drive from Karakoy which is recently the most popular and vibrant neighbor, having Galataport and Istanbul Modern Museum and Nişantaşı, which reflect the modern, chic, and elegant part of the city, having luxury brand stores, stylish cafes, and well-known restaurants._

_Nearly 100 works of art are exhibited throughout The Artisan Hotel Istanbul – MGallery Collection, the premium category brand of the Accor Group. The resident collection gives you an opportunity to encounter traces of Istanbul and see works by local artists in addition to works by globally renowned artists. Richard Mas’ female and male sculptures with heads shaped like lips, recognized for his surrealist work playfully welcome you at the lobby entrance. Rooms, corridors, convention halls, lobby, and terrace -the works of art we encounter at each stride lend new and rich meanings to travels for different purposes._

_I wish you all to enjoy this amazing city of Istanbul and leave our little property with tasty memories.”_

Eylem Atalay

### Location

* * *

The Artisan Hotel Istanbul – MGallery Collection is a wonderful choice if you’re looking for a hotel located at the heart of Istanbul.

You could take a walk from 280 years **Taksim Square** to **Istiklal Street** on which historical **Cercle D’Orient** building, 150 years of **Flower Passage** and **Egypt Apartment** is placed.

Blended with the natural beauty and the smell of the sea, peace and quite **Princes Islands** derived its name from the banishment of the Princes of the Empires that rules Istanbul throughout the history, you could use **Pier Kabatas** for the ferry to Princes Island. It is from 5 minutes walking distance from The Artisan Hotel Istanbul – MGallery Collection.

Hagia Sofia, Blue Mosque, Basilica Cistern, Topkapı Palace… Old City offers you an invitation to a magical world if you would like to accept this invitation you could take tram from  **Kabatas Station** which is only 7 minutes walking distance from the hotel to the Old City.

You could use **Taksim Havaist** shuttle or  private taxi on your way to the hotel from **Istanbul Airport**.

The Artisan Hotel Istanbul – MGallery Collection offers you a comfortable transportation advantage to each part of Istanbul.
