# Cloister

### Iconic Landmark

For a century, Cloister has embodied the romance of South Florida, where Mediterranean-inspired architecture, secluded courtyards, and lush tropical gardens have created an unmistakable sense of place. Now, we invite you to be a part of a new chapter.

Book Now

## Where It All Began

The original heart of The Boca Raton, Cloister has been welcoming guests since 1926. Its grand architecture, lush courtyards, and hidden gardens tell the story of a Florida icon, while newly redesigned guestrooms and suites bring a fresh perspective to its storied setting.

Beyond its 273 guestrooms, including 24 spacious suites, Cloister is a place of discovery, with rotating art collections, fashion collaborations, and unexpected moments woven throughout. Its central location puts some of the resort’s most celebrated experiences within easy reach, from our historic Golf Club and expansive Racquet Club to Forbes Five-Star Spa Palmera.

- Newly redesigned rooms in fall 2026
- Classic style, modern finishings
- Ideal location and accommodations for groups and families
- Full resort access

## Cloister Rooms, Redesigned

Even icons evolve. Following a reimagination for today’s lifestyle, Cloister begins its next chapter—speaking to the way we travel today: effortless, personal, and always connected to a sense of place. The guestrooms honor the hotel’s architectural character through a fresh, modern lens, with playful details that capture the vibrant spirit of The Boca Raton.
