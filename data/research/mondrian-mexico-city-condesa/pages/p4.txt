# MONDRIAN Mexico city condesa FAQ’s

From the art-filled streets of La Condesa to the vibrant social spaces of Mondrian Mexico City, we want every moment of your stay to feel effortless. Find everything you need to know about your visit right here, and if something’s missing, our team is always just a conversation away.

BOOKINGS & RESERVATIONS

**How can I make a reservation at your hotel?**

Reservations at Mondrian Mexico City Condesa can be made directly through our website, by phone, or by email:

- Online: Visit our website, select your preferred dates and room type, and complete your booking securely in just a few steps.
- Phone: +52 55 8889 0356
- Email: reservations.mexicocity@mondrianhotels.com

**What is your cancellation policy?** Mondrian Mexico City Condesa offers two rate types with the following cancellation policies:

- Flexible Rate: No cancellation charge applies if cancelled before 4:00 PM (local time), up to 2 days prior to arrival. Beyond this period, the amount due is non-refundable, even in the case of cancellation or modification.
- Prepaid Rate: The full amount of the stay is charged at the time of booking and is non-refundable.

**Do you offer flexible booking options or special rates during uncertain times?** Yes, we periodically offer flexible booking options, seasonal promotions, advance purchase discounts, and exclusive packages for direct bookings.

**How can I modify or cancel an existing reservation?**

To modify or cancel an existing Mondrian Mexico City Condesa reservation, please contact our Reservations team directly:

- Email: reservations.mexicocity@mondrianhotels.com
- Phone: +52 55 8889 0356

Please note that changes and cancellations are subject to the terms and conditions of your selected rate.

**Do you offer group rates or event bookings?** Yes, Mondrian Mexico City Condesa offers customized rates and tailored packages for group stays, corporate meetings, weddings, social celebrations, private events, and special gatherings. Our dedicated Events & Sales team can assist with venue selection, room blocks, catering, and personalized event planning. To submit an enquiry, complete our Request for Proposal (RFP) form on our website or contact our team directly.

**Is breakfast included? How much does it cost?** Breakfast inclusion at Mondrian Mexico City Condesa depends on the rate booked. If breakfast is not included in your reservation, it can be added directly at the Front Desk for an additional charge of USD $20 per person, per day, plus applicable taxes and service charges. Guests can enjoy a selection of fresh, locally inspired dishes at our on-site dining venues.

**What payment methods do you accept?** Mondrian Mexico City Condesa accepts all major credit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express, as well as selected digital payment methods.

**Is a deposit required to book a room?** Yes, a security deposit of MXN $2,000 per night is required at check-in at Mondrian Mexico City Condesa to cover any incidental charges during your stay. The deposit may be authorized on a credit card or paid in cash and will be refunded upon departure, less any outstanding charges.

**What is your policy regarding children staying at the hotel?** Mondrian Mexico City Condesa warmly welcomes families. Children aged 0-11 years stay complimentary when sharing existing bedding with their parents or guardians. Guests aged 12 and over are considered adults for accommodation and rate purposes.

LOCATION & TRANSPORT

**How close is Mondrian Mexico City Condesa to major attractions such as Chapultepec Park, Reforma Avenue, Polanco, Roma Norte, and the Historic Center?**

Mondrian Mexico City Condesa is ideally located in the heart of La Condesa, one of Mexico City’s most vibrant and walkable neighborhoods. Guests are just minutes from iconic attractions including Chapultepec Park, The National Museum of Anthropology, Paseo de la Reforma, Roma Norte, Polanco, and the Historic Center. Whether exploring world-class museums, renowned restaurants, cultural landmarks, shopping districts, or nightlife, the city’s most celebrated destinations are easily accessible from the hotel by foot, bicycle, car, or public transportation.

**What is the La Condesa neighbourhood like, and what dining, bars, and local attractions are available on the doorstep?**

Mondrian Mexico City Condesa is located in the heart of La Condesa, one of the city’s most vibrant and sought-after neighborhoods. Known for its tree-lined streets, Art Deco architecture, lively cafés, renowned restaurants, boutique shops, galleries, and nightlife, Condesa offers an authentic blend of culture, creativity, and local charm.

Just steps from the hotel, guests can explore Parque México and Parque España, discover acclaimed dining destinations, enjoy craft cocktails and rooftop bars, or stroll through the neighboring districts of Roma Norte and Juárez. Major attractions such as Chapultepec Park, the National Museum of Anthropology, Paseo de la Reforma, Polanco, and the Historic Center are all within easy reach, making Mondrian Mexico City Condesa the perfect base for experiencing the best of Mexico City.

**What should I know about local customs, currency, or travel requirements for visiting Mexico?** Mexico City’s official currency is the Mexican Peso (MXN). Major credit cards are widely accepted throughout the city, though it is advisable to carry some cash for smaller establishments. Spanish is the primary language, though English is commonly spoken in hotels, restaurants, and tourist areas. A valid passport and/or visa may be required depending on your nationality; we recommend checking the latest official travel requirements before your trip. Please note that Mexico City sits at an altitude of over 2,200 metres (7,200 feet), so guests may wish to stay hydrated and allow time to acclimatize upon arrival.

**What is the best way to get to Mondrian Mexico City Condesa from Benito Juárez International Airport (MEX), and how can I book a transfer?** Mondrian Mexico City Condesa is located approximately 25–35 minutes from Benito Juárez International Airport (MEX), depending on traffic conditions. Guests may arrive by taxi, private transportation, or other ground transportation services. For a seamless arrival experience, our Concierge team can assist with arranging private airport transfers prior to your stay. For transportation assistance and transfer bookings, please contact concierge.m exicocity@mondrianhotels.com.

**What public transportation options (MRT, bus, taxi) are available near Mondrian Mexico City Condesa for getting around the city?**

Mondrian Mexico City Condesa enjoys a prime location with easy access to Mexico City’s extensive public transportation network. Guests are within walking distance of Chilpancingo Metro Station (Line 9) and Chilpancingo Metrobús Station (Line 1), providing convenient connections to key destinations such as Paseo de la Reforma, Chapultepec Park, the Historic Center, Polanco, Roma Norte, and the World Trade Center.

Guests can also take advantage of Uber, taxis, and Ecobici bike-sharing stations located throughout the neighborhood. Many of Condesa’s most popular restaurants, cafés, parks, galleries, and nightlife venues are just steps from the hotel, making it one of the most walkable areas in Mexico City.

LOYALTY & MEMBERSHIP

**What loyalty or membership programme does Mondrian Mexico City Condesa offer?**

Mondrian Mexico City Condesa’s membership programme is Dis-loyalty, a travel and dining membership that unlocks savings across more than 120 hotels and 200 restaurants, bars and cafés worldwide. Members enjoy 20% off their first stay at every participating hotel, 10% off return stays, 10% off food and drink, and one complimentary barista-made drink every day. Unlike traditional hotel loyalty programmes, Dis-loyalty provides immediate savings from your first booking, with no points, tiers or blackout dates.

**How do I join Dis-loyalty and how much does membership cost?**

Membership is a 12-month subscription priced in local currency, with equivalent pricing of $218 / £144 / €180 / AED720 per year. You can join online at dis-loyalty.com, when making a booking on mondrianhotels.com, or via the Dis-loyalty app on iOS and Android. Your digital membership card is activated immediately after subscribing, so you can start using your benefits straight away.

**Can I use Dis-loyalty at Mondrian Mexico City Condesa, and can I book more than one room?**

Yes. As Mondrian Mexico City Condesa’s membership programme, Dis-loyalty gives members 20% off their first stay and 10% off return stays when booking eligible rates through the Dis-loyalty platform, along with access to member-only offers. You can book up to two rooms per stay, with the second room discounted at 10%; all bookings are flexible and paid on arrival. Reservations must be made through Dis-loyalty at least four days in advance, and the member must be one of the staying guests.

**Can I use Dis-loyalty in the restaurant, bar or café without staying at the hotel?**

Yes. You don’t need to stay at Mondrian Mexico City Condesa to enjoy Dis-loyalty dining benefits. Members receive 10% off food and drink at participating restaurants, bars and cafés for groups of up to six, and can redeem one complimentary barista-made drink per day by presenting their digital membership card when sitting in.

**What other benefits does Dis-loyalty membership include?**

Beyond stay and dining discounts, members get early access to selected hotel openings and up to 50% off newly opened hotels during launch periods, plus exclusive member-only promotions throughout the year. Benefits vary by property and are subject to availability.

**Can I be a member of both Dis-loyalty and ALL Accor?**

Yes. You can be a member of both Dis-loyalty and ALL Accor. The two programmes operate independently, and many guests choose to use both. However, Dis-loyalty and ALL benefits cannot be applied to the same transaction. For example, you may choose to book your Mondrian Mexico City Condesa stay using ALL and earn or redeem Reward points, while using your Dis-loyalty membership separately for dining discounts, complimentary drinks or other eligible member benefits during your visit.

**Can I use ALL Accor benefits at Mondrian Mexico City Condesa?**

Yes. Eligible guests can earn and redeem ALL Reward points when booking qualifying stays at Mondrian Mexico City Condesa through Accor channels, subject to the terms and conditions of the programme. ALL is separate from Dis-loyalty. While ALL focuses on points earning and rewards, Dis-loyalty provides immediate savings on hotel stays, dining and drinks.

**How do I join ALL Accor?**

ALL Accor is free to join and provides access to member rates, points earning and reward benefits across participating hotels. You can enrol directly through ALL.com and begin earning Reward points on eligible stays at Mondrian Mexico City Condesa, subject to programme terms and conditions.
