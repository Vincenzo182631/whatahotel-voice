# A Modern Cultural Gallery in the Heart of La Condesa

Perfectly located between Roma and Condesa — two of Mexico City’s most vibrant neighborhoods — Mondrian Mexico City Condesa blends bold design, local culture, and effortless style.

Surrounded by top restaurants, art galleries, leafy parks, and the city’s most dynamic culinary scene, discover a stay that captures the creative energy of Mexico City — immersive, inspiring, and distinctly local.

Book Now

### STAY

Set within a masterpiece by renowned architect José Luis Benlliure, Mondrian Mexico City Condesa is a study in art, design, and modern expression.

Each of the 183 rooms and suites is defined by a signature Ara Starck mural — bold, unexpected, and immersive — set against serene, contemporary interiors.

### LA TERRAZA by mondrian

La Terraza by Mondrian is a serene patio offering vibrant bar snacks and a thoughtfully curated selection of classic cocktails. With its inviting ambiance, it provides the perfect setting for guests and locals to relax and unwind in a laid-back yet sophisticated atmosphere, whether before or after dinner.

### THE FLOWERSHOP

The FlowerShop is a vibrant concept store that seamlessly blends a florist, café, and wine bar into a single, inviting space. Guests can enjoy artisanal pastries, expertly crafted coffee, and wholesome small plates made with fresh, high-quality ingredients.

Guests at Mondrian enjoy access to a 24-hour fitness center with state-of-the-art equipment and Pasana Spa, where guests can indulge in treatments that blend ancient experiences with modern techniques. Both are conveniently located at the property next door, with spa treatments available at an additional charge.
