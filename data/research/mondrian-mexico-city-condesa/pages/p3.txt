# dine & drink

**ACCLAIMED CULINARY AND COCKTAIL EXPERIENCES**

Mondrian enhances Condesa’s vibrant culinary scene with a diverse selection of dining and cocktail experiences, perfect for both travelers and locals. From the open-air charm of **La Terraza**, set in a cozy patio with bold flavors, to the intimate and inviting atmosphere of **The Flower Shop**, each venue offers a unique gastronomic journey.

La Terraza is a serene patio offering vibrant bar snacks and a thoughtfully curated selection of classic cocktails. With its inviting ambiance, it provides the perfect setting for guests and locals to relax and unwind in a laid-back yet sophisticated atmosphere, whether before or after dinner.

EXPLORE LA TERRAZA

The Flower Shop is a vibrant concept store that seamlessly blends a florist, café, and wine bar into a single, inviting space. Guests can enjoy artisanal pastries, expertly crafted coffee, and wholesome small plates made with fresh, high-quality ingredients.
