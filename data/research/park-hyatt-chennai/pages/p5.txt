### Park Hyatt does not disappoint

Its a convenient location near the airport. A beautiful property with attentive staff and good security. Staff is very kind and helpful. The breakfast buffet had so many selections including additional items you can order from your table. The rooms were well appointed and large. The shower pressure was very good and with hot water that comes right away. The bed was very comfortable and so were the pillows. Overall this is a good value and highly recommended. The rooms were very clean and even though its not far from the airport its not in the flight path and was very quiet overall.
