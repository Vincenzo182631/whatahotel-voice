## The Flying Elephant

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

The Flying Elephant just got an upgrade. Now with a brand new menu and interiors designed to take your palate places even your imagination couldn’t have.

Informal, fun, dynamic & vibrant are just some of the attributes that describe the ambience of The Flying Elephant. The restaurant aims to speak to all senses, offering its guests breathtaking architecture and a multi-sensory dining experience that is unmatched in the city.

The new menu encompasses familiar signature dishes, served differently. It pays an evolutional tribute to the regions it represents. The food is passionately prepared, thoughtfully plated and interactively served right out of the various show kitchens around the dining floor. Ingredients have been carefully selected, while traditional recipes and preparation methods are respected and fused with modern textures and flavors.

The drinks menu is a curated selection of known classics with modern twists and is like the food menu, ever evolving. It celebrates fresh ingredients and simple preparation methods, focusing on the key flavors and ingredients.

## Mr. Ong - The Flavours of Singapore

Mr. Ong – The Flavours of Singapore is Chennai's only authentic Singaporean restaurant.
Inspired by the fast-paced Singaporean hawker-style food, Mr. Ong serves delicious signatures rooted in the culinary origins of Singapore, Malaysia, and Indonesia using fresh, locally sourced ingredients and authentic preparation methods. Enjoy delectable Singaporean meals out of our brand new show kitchen, located right next to the serene and laidback lily pond.
All dishes are prepared with a wide range of authentic ingredients, thoughtfully sourced, and carefully prepared following the highest levels of hygiene by Executive Chef Balaji Natarajan and his team. Mr. Ong is authentic, fresh, appealing to all senses and serves fuss free, fast paced Singaporean cuisine.
