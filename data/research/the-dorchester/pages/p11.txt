# Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester

Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester is our three Michelin-starred restaurant in London. Characterised by its contemporary French cuisine and distinctive fresh flavours, each plate is executed with a light and surprising twist that reflects its sophisticated location.

CHEF PATRON

Jean-Philippe Blondet

Jean-Philippe Blondet has been working alongside Alain Ducasse for over a decade, interpreting Ducasse’s cuisine in a contemporary and refined way, in keeping with his mentor's philosophy of the finest ingredients being the key.

CUISINE

Delicious Innovation

Indulge in a fine dining experience in one of the most acclaimed French restaurants in Mayfair. Jean-Philippe Blondet is inspired by both the changing seasons and the fresh provenance of his ingredients to create a collection of characterful dishes that define London’s contemporary French cuisine scene.

Private dining

A spectacular dining experience

There's something truly delicious about private dining. Choose from the exclusive Table Lumière, intimate Salon Privé, or spacious Salon Park Lane, with space for up to 30 guests. Enjoy tailored menus and impeccable service for an unforgettable private dining experience in Mayfair.
