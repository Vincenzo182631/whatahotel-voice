The concept

A place to gather. A reason to return

Polished, playful and proudly London. Laughter travels across tables, talented chefs cook in full view and the team brings the whole place to life. Whether you're softly starting the day with fresh salmon rosti or delighting in a delicious Sunday lunch while visiting Mayfair, every moment is unmistakably The Dorchester.

The drinks

## Our signature serve

Whether you’re after something chilled, stirred or shaken, our tipple menu reflects London’s vibrant spirit. Each cocktail is delicately balanced yet refreshingly complex, and always crafted to surprise.

The space

## An iconic Park Lane restaurant

Softly lit lamps sit on white linen tables. Sweeping burgundy curtains frame the copper pudding bar. Deep red leather booths await. Despite being in the heart of Mayfair and moments from Hyde Park, The Grill’s warm glow makes you feel right at home.

The chef

## Martyn Nail

Bringing a wealth of experience from 40 years of working at the finest luxury hotels, Martyn Nail helms our culinary direction. A classically-trained chef, Nail’s menu features wonderfully British dishes refreshed with skill, finesse and surprising flavour pairings.

Private dining

## A private dining room in Mayfair

The Chef’s Table is our private dining space, tucked away within our renowned kitchen. With seating for up to 12 guests, we invite you to gather around our table and encounter the buzz, energy and intimacy of this unique private dining experience, one of the most exclusive in London.
