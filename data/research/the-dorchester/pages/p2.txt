Rooms & suites FAQs

What types of rooms and suites are available at The Dorchester?

The Dorchester offers luxury rooms, suites, and signature suites. They range from intimate rooms designed for comfort and relaxation, to some of London's most prestigious suites.

How many rooms and suites does The Dorchester have?

The Dorchester features 173 rooms and 68 suites, providing a range of accommodation options for leisure and business travellers.

Do rooms at The Dorchester have views of Hyde Park?

Many room and suite categories offer views over Hyde Park. These include Deluxe Park View Rooms, Executive Park View Rooms, the Hyde Park Suite, the Dorchester Suite, the Regal Suite and The Royal Suite.

What is the best suite at The Dorchester?

'Best' is different for everybody, and each of our suites has its own unique charm. However, if you're looking for The Dorchester's most exceptional stay, The Royal Suite is a prime contender. One of the hotel's most prestigious suites, it's defined by breathtaking design, an incredibly spacious layout, and panoramic views over Hyde Park and Park Lane.

Are rooms at The Dorchester suitable for families?

Yes. The Dorchester's family-friendly hotel rooms offer the option to add an extra bed in selected rooms and suites, and complimentary cots for infants.

Can I request an extra bed in my room at The Dorchester?

Yes. An additional bed can be arranged in suitable rooms and suites for guests aged 13 and above for £84 per night.

Does The Dorchester provide cots for babies?

Yes, The Dorchester offers complimentary cots for infants.

Have rooms at The Dorchester been recently renovated?

Yes. The Dorchester recently completed a landmark four year renovation, refreshing all its guest rooms and suites while celebrating the hotel's heritage and design legacy.

What makes The Dorchester's rooms unique?

The Dorchester's rooms and suites combine classic British elegance with contemporary luxury, featuring bespoke interiors, rich fabrics, exceptional craftsmanship and views of either Hyde Park or Mayfair.

Are there luxury suites at The Dorchester?

Yes. The Dorchester is renowned for its collection of luxury suites, including signature suites that offer expansive living spaces, individual design concepts and exceptional levels of privacy and service.

Which rooms are best for a luxury stay in London?

The Dorchester's suites and signature suites are ideal for guests seeking the ultimate luxury London experience, with spacious layouts, premium amenities and prestigious Mayfair surroundings.

Are pets allowed in The Dorchester rooms?

Yes. In our dog-friendly luxury hotel rooms in London, we allow up to two small dogs weighing no more than 7 kg each. A daily pet fee (£35) applies.

Is smoking permitted in The Dorchester's rooms?

No. The Dorchester maintains a strict non-smoking policy inside the hotel to ensure a comfortable environment for all guests. Smoking is permitted in outdoor smoking areas only.

What is included when staying in a suite at The Dorchester?

Selected suites at The Dorchester include exclusive benefits such as dedicated butler service, complimentary round-trip airport transfers, access to a chauffeur-driven car (subject to availability) and a complimentary Speed Nap Experience at The Dorchester Spa for each guest.

Are The Dorchester suites good for special occasions?

Yes. The Dorchester's suites are a popular choice for celebrations such as anniversaries, birthdays, proposals and milestone events, thanks to their generous space, elegant design and exceptional dedicated butler service.

What's the difference between a room and a suite at The Dorchester?

Our rooms are elegant, intimate places to stay, while our suites provide additional living space and a more residential luxury experience in the heart of Mayfair. With a suite, you'll enjoy more rooms – from private studies to work in and sitting rooms to relax or host in, to The Royal Suite's fully-equipped kitchen, multiple bathrooms and eight-seater dining room.

Why stay in a suite at The Dorchester?

A suite at The Dorchester offers more space, greater comfort and an elevated luxury experience, making it ideal for longer stays, special occasions or travellers seeking the highest level of accommodation in London.

What time is check-in at The Dorchester?

In our luxury hotel in London, check-in begins at 3pm and check-out is by 12pm.
