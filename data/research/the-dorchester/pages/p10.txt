Dress code

Although London forever strides ahead, elegance never goes out of style. The Dorchester is very keen on the habit of dressing brilliantly. We kindly ask guests to refrain from wearing shorts and sportswear in the evening.

Guests

At China Tang at The Dorchester, children under 10 can dine until 9pm, when joining us for the last sitting at 7pm.

The original flavour

Dim sum. Bite-size pieces of delight. The name means ‘touch the heart’, and they were originally designed to be a quick snack. Our dim sum are so delicious that we have to serve them all day in the bar.
