Frequently asked questions

Does The Dorchester have a spa?

Yes. Our luxury spa in Mayfair, London, offers treatment rooms, expert therapists, wellness therapies and a Carol Joy London hair salon.

What facilities are available at The Dorchester Spa?

The Dorchester Spa offers a range of wellness facilities, including treatment rooms, a relaxation room, an MLX Quartz treatment bed, and the Carol Joy London hair salon. Our hotel gym in Mayfair has state-of-the-art Technogym equipment, and offers personal training services.

Can non-guests use The Dorchester Spa?

Yes. The Dorchester Spa welcomes both hotel guests and non-residents, subject to availability and treatment bookings.

Where is The Dorchester Spa located?

The Dorchester Spa is located within The Dorchester hotel in Mayfair, offering a tranquil wellness retreat just moments from Hyde Park and some of London's most famous attractions.

What treatments are available at The Dorchester Spa?

The Dorchester Spa offers a wide range of spa treatments on Park Lane, including advanced facials, massages, body treatments, beauty services, haircare treatments and personalised wellness experiences. They use renowned luxury brands including La Prairie and Carol Joy London.

Do I need to book treatments at The Dorchester Spa?

Advance booking is recommended, particularly during weekends, holidays and busy periods, to secure preferred treatment times. To book an appointment use https://thedorchesterspa.try.be/

Can hotel guests use The Dorchester Spa?

Yes. Hotel guests can enjoy access to The Dorchester's wellness facilities and may book treatments during their stay.

What is the Speed Nap Experience at The Dorchester Spa?

The Speed Nap Experience is a wellness treatment set in our serene relaxation room. The session takes place on our Welnamis lounger, where soothing vibrations and a calming binaural soundtrack ease body and mind.

Do suite guests receive spa benefits at The Dorchester?

Yes. Guests staying in selected suites receive a complimentary Speed Nap Experience at The Dorchester Spa, with one treatment included per person, per stay.

Is The Dorchester Spa good for relaxation?

Yes. The Dorchester Spa is designed as a peaceful sanctuary, offering wellness treatments and calming spaces where guests can unwind away from the pace of central London.

Can I visit The Dorchester Spa during a London staycation?

Yes. The Dorchester Spa is a popular choice for luxury staycations, spa days and wellness escapes, combining expert treatments with the renowned service of one of London's most iconic hotels.

What is the MLX Quartz treatment bed at The Dorchester Spa?

The Dorchester Spa features an MLX Quartz treatment bed, an advanced wellness bed designed to enhance comfort and relaxation during selected spa treatments.

Does The Dorchester Spa offer personal training?

Yes. Guests can book personal training sessions tailored to their individual fitness goals, whether they are maintaining a routine or starting a new wellness journey.

Does The Dorchester have a hair salon?

Yes. The hotel is home to the Carol Joy London hair salon, offering professional hair services as part of The Dorchester's luxury beauty and wellness offering.

Can I book a beauty treatment at The Dorchester?

Yes. Guests can complement their spa visit with beauty and grooming services, including treatments available through the Carol Joy London hair salon.

Does The Dorchester have an indoor swimming pool?

While The Dorchester does not have a pool within the hotel itself, guests enjoy access to the 20-metre indoor swimming pool at nearby sister property 45 Park Lane. The pool is available exclusively to hotel guests and offers a luxurious wellness experience in the heart of Mayfair.

Can I buy a spa gift voucher for The Dorchester?

Yes. Guests can gift luxury spa experiences, including relaxing treatments and massages at The Dorchester Spa, providing a thoughtful wellness experience in the heart of Mayfair.

What facial treatments are available at The Dorchester Spa?

Guests can choose from a selection of results-driven facial treatments, including the La Prairie Skin Caviar Facial, Collagen and Hyaluronic Acid Facial, Collagen and Vitamin C Facial, 24-Carat Gold Facial, Oxygen Facial, Diamond Facial, Fire and Ice Facial and Classic Facial. These treatments are designed to improve hydration, firmness, radiance and overall skin health.

Does The Dorchester Spa offer La Prairie treatments?

Yes. The Dorchester Spa offers exclusive La Prairie facial treatments using the brand's renowned skincare collections, including the signature Skin Caviar range, to help lift, firm and smooth the skin.

Does The Dorchester Spa offer massages?

Yes. The Dorchester Spa offers a range of massage and bodywork treatments designed to ease tension, promote relaxation and support overall wellbeing. Guests can choose from traditional massage therapies and specialist wellness treatments.
