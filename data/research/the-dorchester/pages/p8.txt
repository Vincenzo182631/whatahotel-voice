DELECTABLE DINING

As the day unfolds

Mornings awake to indulgent breakfasts, fabulous coffee and refreshing juice. Then, not to be missed are lunches of superfood salads and hearty favourites. As evening descends, the intimate atmosphere glows with good company and delightful cuisine.

LIVE MUSIC

## The Promenade's shining soundtrack

Liberace’s famous piano serenades our diners under the skilful hands of our resident pianist. We’re not sure if it’s the dazzle of the mirrors or the memory of the maestro himself, but something special happens every time this piano is played.

- Wednesday to Sunday: solo piano at lunch, piano vocalist in the evening

EVENINGS AT THE PROMENADE

## Evenings made to last longer

Sink into your seat, dine in the heart of Mayfair and let the night find its rhythm. Live music sets the mood as the Gibson Trolley rolls to your table: pickled, stirred, yours. A bespoke martini made exactly as you like it.

Meanwhile, food is kept simple. The kind of late-night classics that never need explaining. Simple plates, big flavour, no fuss.

THE GIBSON TROLLEY

## Choose your adventure

The Promenade’s Gibson Trolley brings the art of the martini directly to your table. It's your bespoke, tableside ritual built around one of the cocktail world’s most singular garnishes. Select from our curated collection of house-pickled accompaniments, and let our bartenders craft something entirely your own.
