## Bask in supreme comfort

Extraordinary experiences, in the heart of Mayfair. Inspired by the colours and motifs of a classic English garden, our beautiful rooms and suites invite you to unwind in wonderful comfort.

THE SUITE

London mastered

This is The Dorchester at its most assured, and its most extraordinary.

A palatial checkered marble lobby opens onto grand reception rooms, an intimate dining space and a private bedroom suite, all overlooking Hyde Park and enriched by exceptional details.

THE VIEW

The heart of the capital at your feet

From its elevated position above Hyde Park, the view stretches across treetops, open sky and London’s iconic landmarks. By day, light moves softly over the greenery below; by evening, the city glows with possibility.

THE BATHROOM

A sanctuary in marble

Marble surfaces, refined detailing and generous in size. The serene space is designed for restoration, creating a sense of cool calm.

THE DESIGN

Classic refinement and modern warmth

Designed by Pierre-Yves Rochon, the suite blends refined elegance with a welcoming warmth.

A 1950s spirit runs throughout, with pastel tones and floral references taking their cue from Hyde Park, while artworks lend the space depth, character and an enduring sense of taste.

ROYAL PARKS

Supporting London’s parks

From the floral design to the treetop views, the suite’s close connection to Hyde Park extends to the charity that protects it.

When you book a stay at The Royal Suite, we’ll donate a portion to The Royal Parks, helping to support the preservation of London’s historic and natural parklands.

Complimentary lounger session

We've made your stay at The Dorchester even more relaxing: book The Royal Suite to enjoy a complimentary speed nap experience in the spa. Set in our serene relaxation room, the session takes place on our Welnamis lounger, where soothing vibrations and a calming binaural soundtrack ease body and mind.
