Dining FAQs

What restaurants are available at The Dorchester?

The Dorchester is home to several renowned dining destinations, including the three-Michelin starred Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester, The Grill at The Dorchester, China Tang at The Dorchester, and The Promenade. Each offers a distinct dining experience, from fine French cuisine to modern British dishes, Cantonese specialities and classic afternoon tea.

Does The Dorchester have a Michelin-starred restaurant?

Yes. Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester holds three Michelin stars. One of the UK's finest restaurants, it serves contemporary French fine dining opposite Hyde Park.

What is Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester known for?

Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester is our three-Michelin starred restaurant on Park Lane. It's celebrated for its fine French cuisine, exceptional service and sophisticated tasting menus. It's one of London's most prestigious fine dining experiences.

What type of food does The Grill at The Dorchester serve?

The Grill at The Dorchester serves modern British cuisine in a relaxed, stylish setting. It's known for seasonal ingredients, contemporary dishes and a vibrant atmosphere.

What cuisine does China Tang at The Dorchester serve?

China Tang specialises in authentic Cantonese cuisine inspired by the glamour of 1930s Shanghai. It's one of London's leading Chinese dining destinations.

Where is afternoon tea served at The Dorchester?

Afternoon tea is served in The Promenade, one of the hotel's most iconic spaces. Guests can enjoy classic afternoon tea in Mayfair, London, with freshly baked scones, pastries, cakes and a selection of delightful teas.

Is afternoon tea at The Dorchester famous?

Yes. Afternoon tea at The Dorchester is one of London's most celebrated luxury afternoon tea experiences and has long been a favourite among visitors and locals alike.

Do I need to book a restaurant at The Dorchester?

Advance reservations are recommended for all restaurants and afternoon tea experiences, especially for Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester, China Tang and weekend dining periods.

Is there a dress code for dining at The Dorchester?

Yes. The Dorchester encourages elegant attire. Guests are kindly asked not to wear shorts or sportswear during afternoon tea service or in the evening.

Are children welcome in The Dorchester restaurants?

Yes. Families are welcome at The Dorchester, and several dining venues offer a welcoming environment for guests of all ages.

Can dietary requirements be accommodated at The Dorchester?

Yes. The Dorchester's restaurants can cater to a range of dietary requirements and food preferences. If you can, please let us know when booking.

Does The Dorchester have bars?

Yes. The Dorchester is home to several bars, including the renowned Vesper Bar and Artists' Bar, where guests can enjoy expertly crafted cocktails in a sophisticated setting.

What is Vesper Bar at The Dorchester?

Vesper Bar is The Dorchester's stylish cocktail bar, known for its creative drinks, elegant atmosphere and expert mixologists. It's a popular destination for both hotel guests and London visitors.

Which restaurant at The Dorchester is best for a special occasion?

Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester is a popular choice for celebrations, thanks to its three Michelin stars and exceptional dining experience. The Grill at The Dorchester and China Tang are also frequently chosen for birthdays, anniversaries and special celebrations.

Why dine at The Dorchester?

The Dorchester offers some of London's most iconic dining experiences, combining Michelin-starred cuisine, acclaimed restaurants, exceptional service and elegant surroundings in the heart of Mayfair.
