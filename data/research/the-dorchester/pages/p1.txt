Frequently asked questions

Where is The Dorchester located?

Park Lane, London W1K 1QA. It's in the heart of Mayfair: overlooking Hyde Park, halfway between Buckingham Palace and Marble Arch.

Has The Dorchester been renovated recently?

Yes. In 2026, The Dorchester completed a major four-year renovation that refreshed its rooms, suites and public spaces while preserving the hotel's historic character.

What is The Dorchester known for?

Set on Park Lane, Mayfair, The Dorchester is one of London's most iconic luxury hotels. It's renowned for its exceptional service, world-class dining, and a long history of hosting royalty, celebrities and international visitors.

How far is The Dorchester from Hyde Park?

The Dorchester sits directly opposite Hyde Park, offering many rooms and suites with views across the park.

What restaurants are available at The Dorchester?

The Dorchester is home to several acclaimed dining venues, including three Michelin-starred Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester, The Grill at The Dorchester, China Tang at The Dorchester and The Promenade.

Does The Dorchester serve afternoon tea?

Yes. Afternoon tea is served in The Promenade and is one of London's most celebrated luxury afternoon tea experiences.

Does The Dorchester offer Michelin-starred dining?

Yes. Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester holds three Michelin stars and is among the UK's most acclaimed fine-dining restaurants.

Is The Dorchester suitable for families?

Yes. The Dorchester welcomes families and offers a range of services and amenities designed for guests travelling with children including connecting rooms.

Does The Dorchester host weddings and events?

Yes. The Dorchester is a popular venue for weddings, celebrations, private gatherings and corporate events, with a range of elegant event spaces and dedicated planning services.

What attractions are near The Dorchester?

Nearby attractions include Hyde Park, Buckingham Palace, Bond Street, Harrods, Green Park, Piccadilly, the West End and many of London's leading cultural landmarks.

How far is The Dorchester from Heathrow Airport?

The Dorchester is approximately 45 minutes by car from Heathrow Airport, depending on traffic conditions.

Can non-guests dine at The Dorchester?

Yes. Non-residents are welcome to enjoy The Dorchester's restaurants, bars and afternoon tea experiences, subject to availability and reservations where required.

Does The Dorchester have a spa?

Yes. The Dorchester's luxurious spa offers treatment rooms, fitness facilities and personalised wellness services with leading experts. Guest at The Dorchester also have access to the pool at neighbouring hotel 45 Park Lane.

Does The Dorchester have parking?

Yes, The Dorchester offers valet parking for guests and visitors. Located on Park Lane in Mayfair, the hotel provides a convenient parking service for those arriving by car in central London. The cost is £85 per day.

Does The Dorchester allow pets to stay?

The Dorchester is a pet-friendly London hotel. We welcome up to two small dogs (max 7 kg/15 lbs) with a daily fee of £35, but ask that they remain in your room while you visit our bars, Alain Ducasse, China Tang, The Grill, spa and gym, or are kept in a carrier in The Promenade.

Does The Dorchester provide extra beds?

Yes. The Dorchester can arrange an extra bed for guests aged 13 and above for £84 per night, subject to room suitability and availability.

Does The Dorchester provide cots for babies?

Yes. Infant cots are complimentary, making The Dorchester a convenient choice for families travelling with babies or young children.

Is there a dress code at The Dorchester's restaurants?

Yes. The Dorchester encourages elegant attire throughout its dining venues. Guests are kindly asked to avoid wearing shorts and sportswear during afternoon tea service and in the evening.

Does The Dorchester have accessible rooms?

Yes. The Dorchester offers two accessible guest rooms designed to accommodate guests with accessibility requirements.

Is smoking allowed at The Dorchester?

No. The Dorchester maintains a strict non-smoking policy inside the hotel to ensure a comfortable environment for all guests. Smoking is permitted in outdoor smoking areas only.

How do I contact the reservations office?

To contact the reservations office, email reservations.tdl@dorchestercollection.com.

When is Cake & Flowers at The Dorchester open?

Cake & Flowers is open daily from 10am to 7pm. Guests can visit the boutique in person, arrange Click & Collect orders, or order cakes and flowers for delivery within London.
