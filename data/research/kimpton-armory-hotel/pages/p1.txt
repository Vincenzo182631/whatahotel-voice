## The highlights

2. Tune Up

3. Sky Shed

4. Music Hall

### Fielding's

The namesake of Fred Fielding Wilson, architect of the original Armory building, Fielding's is our all-day, ground-floor restaurant serving seasonal fare from local growers and purveyors. Pair every meal with selections from our Wine Spectator 2026 Award of Excellence-winning wine program.

Learn more

### Tune Up

This basement-level bar is underground for a reason. It’s where you go to really let your hair down. It’s where you can sip whiskey or shoot it. It’s where live music from local bands and traveling acts, great drinks, savory snacks and that elusive good-time rock ‘n roll energy converge for a uniquely Bozeman bar experience.

Learn more

### Sky Shed

No matter the season, head up to the roof of Kimpton Armory hotel and kick back with a drink in hand in our custom Sky Shed. Light, airy and modern, this glass-enclosed gathering place is perfect for unwinding with a cocktail while you enjoy views of not one but four (yes, four) mountain ranges visible from Downtown Bozeman.

Learn more

### Music Hall

Able to accommodate up to 600 people, Armory Music Hall is our premiere music and event space. It’s the ultimate local destination to take in top musical acts from around the country or host unforgettable large-scale events, from grand weddings to corporate blowouts and beyond.

Learn more

### Gibson Guitar Program

If downtown Bozeman's music scene inspires you to want to play music yourself (we've seen it happen), Kimpton Armory Hotel has acoustic guitars available for use while staying on property. We're partnered with Gibson and have created the Pick & Play guitar loaner program.

Learn more

How far is Kimpton Armory Hotel Bozeman from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Armory Hotel Bozeman is conveniently located near Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport (BZN), approximately 10 miles away (16.09 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

Kimpton Armory Hotel Bozeman is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Yellowstone National Park-78 miles away (125.53 kilometers)
