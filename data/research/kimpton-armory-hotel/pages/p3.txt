## Restaurant + bar highlights

### Fielding's

The namesake of Fred Fielding Wilson, architect of the original Armory building, Fielding's is our all-day, ground-floor restaurant serving seasonal fare from local growers and purveyors. Pair every meal with selections from our Wine Spectator 2026 Award of Excellence-winning wine program.

Learn more

### Sky Shed

No matter the season, head up to the roof of Kimpton Armory hotel and kick back with a drink in hand in our custom Sky Shed. Light, airy and modern, this glass-enclosed gathering place is perfect for unwinding with a cocktail while you enjoy views of not one but four (yes, four) mountain ranges visible from Downtown Bozeman.

Learn more

### Tune Up

This basement-level bar is underground for a reason. It’s where you go to really let your hair down. It’s where you can sip whiskey or shoot it. It’s where live music from local bands and traveling acts, great drinks, savory snacks and that elusive good-time rock ‘n roll energy converge for a uniquely Bozeman bar experience.
