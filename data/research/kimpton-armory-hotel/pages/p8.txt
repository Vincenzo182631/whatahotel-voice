# Kimpton Armory Hotel BozemanLocation

In the heart of downtown Bozeman, Kimpton Armory Hotel is steps from the boutiques, restaurants and art deco buildings of Main Street. Epic outdoor adventures are within easy reach, including skiing, fishing and hiking in mighty Yellowstone National Park.

### Music Hall

Able to accommodate up to 600 people, Armory Music Hall is our premiere music and event space. It’s the ultimate local destination to take in top musical acts from around the country or host unforgettable large-scale events, from grand weddings to corporate blowouts and beyond.

## Things to do

### Yellowstone National Park

PO Box 168, Yellowstone National Park, WY 82190

Yellowstone National Park is a nearly 3,500-sq.-mile wilderness recreation area atop a volcanic hot spot. Mostly in Wyoming, the park spreads into parts of Montana and Idaho too. Yellowstone features dramatic canyons, alpine rivers, lush forests, hot springs and gushing geysers, including its most famous, Old Faithful. It's also home to hundreds of animal species, including bears, wolves, bison, elk and antelope.

### Bridger Bowl

15795 Bridger Canyon Rd, Bozeman, MT 59715

Twenty minutes north of Bozeman you’ll find Bridger Bowl, a favorite ski area for locals. Spread over 2,000 acres with 2,600 vertical feet, this skiing and snowboarding hotspot is ideal for novices and experts alike, with 50% of runs set at intermediate or beginner level, a ski and snowboard school and more.

Learn more

### Montana State University

Culbertson Hall, 100, Bozeman, MT 59717

Montana State University is a public land-grant research university in Bozeman, Montana. It is the state's largest university.

### Big Sky

50 Big Sky Resort Rd, Big Sky, MT 59716

If you’re willing to venture from the city, you’ll find Big Sky Resort, one of the most renowned ski destinations in the US. With 300 runs across 4 connected mountains as well as ziplining, snowshoeing and snowmobile tours, dog sledding and much more, there’s something for every winter warrior.
