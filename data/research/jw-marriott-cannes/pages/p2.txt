### Enjoy the Extra Space

Experience spacious rooms at JW Marriott Cannes, where comfort and luxury come together for an unforgettable stay. Ideal for families or groups of friends, our accommodations are designed to create lasting memories in the heart of Cannes.

### Art and Design

At JW Marriott Cannes, each room features a headboard with iconic photos of Cannes Film Festival stars, paying tribute to our hotel’s legacy as the former Palais des Festivals. This cinematic touch adds glamour and nostalgia, making your stay even more unforgettable. Immerse yourself in the world of cinema while enjoying luxurious comfort in the heart of Cannes.

### Sea View

At the JW Marriott Cannes, the rooms offer breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea. Enjoy the coastline, the sea breeze and the picturesque Lérins Islands from the comfort of your room or balcony. Although there are no palm trees in front of the hotel, the landscape offers a serene and unforgettable experience.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Cannes have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Cannes have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Cannes?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Cannes is the 2 Bedroom Presidential Suite with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Cannes?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Cannes permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Cannes have fridges?

No, rooms at JW Marriott Cannes do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Cannes have coffee/tea makers?
