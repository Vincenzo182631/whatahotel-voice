# Welcome to JW Marriott Cannes

## Stay at our luxury beachfront 5-star hotel in Cannes, France

Treat yourself to a luxury stay on the Croisette at a Cannes hotel with contemporary design, sea views, and a prime seafront setting. JW Marriott Cannes offers a memorable experience surrounded by waterfront walks, luxury shopping, renowned restaurants, and Palais Stéphanie Beach, the hotel’s partner beach. This luxury Cannes hotel with a rooftop swimming pool is a 5-minute walk from the Palais des...See More Festivals and close to the town's main attractions. Built on the former Palais des Festivals site, our 5-star hotel in Cannes, France, offers luxury rooms, event spaces, and pet-friendly stays. For luxury leisure, business events, or family visits, relax in spacious, modern rooms with French Riviera views. Savor champagne on the Sunset Terrace or dinner at Scalini Cannes. Guests praise the seafront location, service, and rooftop views. For business, our hotel in Cannes, France, offers 14 meeting rooms, including the 820-seat Theatre Croisette, across 2,400 sqm of event space, with a rooftop terrace for events.See Less

## Kids Club – a Joyful Return for Summer 2026

Where imagination blooms and memories begin.

Throughout July and August 2026, JW Marriott Cannes welcomes your little ones back to its enchanting Kids Club, a vibrant haven designed for laughter, creativity, and discovery.

In a bright and secure setting, children will enjoy a curated program of fun, age-appropriate activities, guided by our warm and attentive team. From creative workshops to joyful games, every day unfolds into moments to cherish.

While the young guests play, explore, and make new friends, parents can relax and indulge in the timeless elegance of the JW Marriott experience, a world where every detail inspires serenity and pleasure.

A family escape to remember, available by reservation only.

### Unique Location Facing la Croisette

The JW Marriott Cannes is ideally located on the Croisette, just steps from the beach, luxury boutiques, and the Cannes Film Festival. With stunning views and a vibrant atmosphere, it offers the perfect base to experience the city’s glamour.

Discover La Croisette

### Hotel History

Built on the site of the original Palais des Festivals, the hotel honors its cinematic legacy, with rooms featuring portraits of iconic movie stars, a tribute to the world of film. The design blends contemporary elegance with cinematic heritage, creating a chic and inviting atmosphere. With 262 rooms, including spacious suites, each guest can enjoy a luxurious and comfortable stay amidst the glamour of Cannes.

### Family-Friendly Luxury in the Heart of Cannes

Families are welcome, with spacious connecting rooms for a comfortable and memorable stay. Whether you're here for business, leisure, or the festival, the JW Marriott Cannes ensures a luxurious stay in the heart of the city.

## Two Unique Experiences for Cannes Fireworks Nights

Every summer, let the magic of the Festival of Pyrotechnic Art illuminate your evenings in Cannes. At JW Marriott Cannes, experience the fireworks in an unforgettable way with two exclusive settings: a refined dinner under the stars on our panoramic rooftop, offering sweeping views of the bay, or a feet-in-the-sand dinner on our partner private beach, where the fireworks feel close enough to touch. Two perspectives, one extraordinary night to watch the fireworks in Cannes.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Cannes?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Cannes is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Cannes allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Cannes. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 6.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Cannes?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Cannes. Self parking is available for €31.60 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near JW Marriott Cannes. The daily valet fee is €50.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Cannes have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Cannes has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Cannes booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Cannes?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Cannes is Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE). NCE is located approximately 23.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Cannes have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Cannes has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
