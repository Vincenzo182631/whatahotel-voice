# JW Marriott Dining and Restaurants in Cannes

## Dining

Explore the hotel restaurants and varied dining options at JW Marriott Cannes, where authentic culinary experiences await. Scalini, our Italian restaurant, serves traditional, generous Italian cuisine in an intimate and elegant setting. Palais Stéphanie Beach, the hotel’s partner beach, offers an international menu alongside signature Japanese specialities in a stunning seaside setting with views towards...See More the Lérins Islands and Estérel mountains. Start the day with breakfast at Scalini, then enjoy Riviera-inspired seasonal cuisine made with local, organic ingredients, handcrafted cocktails, and panoramic views over the Bay of Cannes, the Lérins Islands, and the Estérel mountains at the seasonal Panorama Rooftop, open until 15 September. ## One View, Two Unique Experiences for Cannes Fireworks Nights

This summer, let the magic of the Festival of Pyrotechnic Art illuminate your evenings in Cannes. At JW Marriott Cannes, experience the spectacle in unforgettable fashion with two exclusive settings: a refined dinner under the stars on our panoramic rooftop, offering sweeping views of the bay, or a feet-in-the-sand dinner on our partner private beach, where the fireworks feel close enough to touch. Two perspectives, one extraordinary night.

## Walton Street Scalini – the Essence of Authentic Italian Cuisine

Experience the charm of traditional Italian cuisine in a contemporary and inviting setting at Scalini. Each dish is a true masterpiece, crafted from authentic recipes and the finest, freshest ingredients. Open daily for lunch and dinner, our restaurant offers an unforgettable culinary journey, where the meticulous preparation of authentic dishes by our chefs in an open kitchen adds a touch of transparency and authenticity. Enhance your meals with a wonderful selection of Italian and French wines, or indulge in freshly crafted cocktails by our mixologists at the S-Bar.

## Japanese and International Experience at Partner Palais Stephanie Beach

Indulge in a remarkable dining experience at our partner Palais Stéphanie Beach, where our chefs blend international flavors with the art of Japanese cuisine against the stunning backdrop of the Cannes Bay. Savor sushi crafted to perfection by our master, Phailinh Ouparavong, with 16 years of experience. Chef Kevin Yahiaoui-Debourge specializes in dishes from around the world, skillfully combining traditional flavors with innovative touches. Join us on our partner private sandy beach to enjoy exceptional specialties made from fresh, seasonal ingredients that will tantalize your taste buds and create unforgettable memories.

### JW Cheesecake

Indulge in our Signature JW Cheesecake, a perfect balance of smoothness and crunch. The light and creamy filling sits atop a crispy speculoos biscuit, while seasonal fruits add a refreshing, tangy touch. A gourmet and refined experience to be enjoyed without moderation.
