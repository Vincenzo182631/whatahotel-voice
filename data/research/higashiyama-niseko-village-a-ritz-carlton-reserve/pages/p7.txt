Japan's Legandary Winter Wonderland
Higashiyama Niseko Village, the newest resort in the prestigious Ritz-Carlton Reserve portfolio, is thrilled to welcome guests to Japan's top winter sports destination of Niseko.
August 17, 2020
Opening This Winter 2020
Higashiyama Niseko Village, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, will make its anticipated debut in Niseko, Hokkaido on 15 December 2020 as the winter season unfolds at the powder mecca of Japan.
November 17, 2020
Self-Discovery Experiences
A dedicated team of Higashiyama Reservists will curate inspiring journeys for guests to explore the transient beauty of nature and to encourage independent discoveries.
