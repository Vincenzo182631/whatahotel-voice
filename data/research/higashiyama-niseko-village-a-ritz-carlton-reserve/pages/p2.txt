Suites & Rooms
There is a special comfort that encapsulates the mountain experience in Hokkaido, a harmonious blend of warmth and serenity. The resort rooms and suites at Higashiyama Niseko Village, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve are designed around that experience, inviting the beauty of nature to flourish within. Floor-to-ceiling windows look out over Mount Yotei or Niseko Annupuri and the décor of our Niseko hotel rooms blends classic and contemporary aesthetics to acknowledge the beauty of Japan.

Features And Amenities
Mountain Views
Nature is both a calming and inspiring presence with rooms offering a breathtaking view over Mount Yotei.
A Guiding Presence
A Do San joins each guest on their resort journey, offering a single point of contact for every wish, want or need. Meaning “person of Hokkaido,” each Do San is a wealth of knowledge about local history, culture and traditions.
Bathroom Sanctuary
Named in the 20 Most Beautiful Hotel Bathrooms in the World list by Travel + Leisure, the guest bathrooms are inspired by the local onsen tradition, featuring an oversized soaking tub, separate rainforest shower and large window offering natural light and a mountain view.
In-Room Dining
Beautifully plated with an authentic Japanese touch and a special treat from the chef. Available whenever you desire.
Wellness & Beauty
Showcasing Hokkaido’s connection to the land, bathroom amenities include locally produced products with a natural wood scent.
