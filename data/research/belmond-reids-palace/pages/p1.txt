## Restaurants & Bars

Explore - Restaurants & Bars

Reid’s Palace boasts some of the best restaurants in Funchal. Go all out at Michelin-starred William; enjoy a casual dinner on the terrace of Ristorante Villa Cipriani or plan a traditional Afternoon Tea overlooking the ocean.

Explore

Overlooking the Atlantic Ocean, Reid's Palace has remained the ultimate place to unwind in the Madeiran sunshine since its opening by the Reid family in 1891.

Over the years, the luxury hideaway has earned a reputation as one of the best hotels on the island. All found what they were seeking throughout their stay at Reid’s – a slice of paradise amidst lush gardens with majestic views over the ocean.
