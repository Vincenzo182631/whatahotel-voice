### MORE To Discover

#### Dining

Upper Union Restaurant serves Chef Amori Burger's bold seasonal menus designed for sharing. Breakfast starts your Cape Town day, evenings bring hotel guests and locals together over South African wines and inventive cocktails in a space that hums with life.

#### spa and wellness

A wellness centre featuring TheraVine™ Cape botanical treatments from surrounding winelands. Two temperature-controlled pools, fitness centre, and treatment rooms where city adventures pause. Restoration is woven naturally through your stay.

#### Location

Kloof Street Neighbourhood in Gardens - Cape Town's creative heart with Table Mountain rising behind. Restored 1800s National Monument, twenty minutes from the airport, five-minute walk to galleries, restaurants, and independent shops where locals actually go.
