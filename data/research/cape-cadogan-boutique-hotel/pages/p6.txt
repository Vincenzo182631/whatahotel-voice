Two Family Stable Suites provide our largest hotel accommodation with two-bedroom configurations and interleading options. Separate lounge areas and kitchenettes give multigenerational families or groups seeking privacy and flexibility space to find their own rhythm whilst staying close together. Some feature courtyards or Juliet balconies overlooking the neighbourhood. Guests of all ages welcome.
