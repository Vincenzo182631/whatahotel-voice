# A luxury lifestyle hotel in Munich’s Schwabinger Tor

Experience a more local way to stay at Andaz Munich Schwabinger Tor, a 5-star luxury hotel near the English Garden. Designed for a modern, design-conscious traveler, the hotel combines generous, light-filled interiors with a strong connection to the city.
Views extend toward the Olympic Park from the spa, while Munich’s highest rooftop bar offers a striking vantage point over the city. A collection of restaurants, cafes and bars shapes a distinctive culinary scene, each space with its own identity. With Schwabing just outside, Munich feels close and easy to explore.

## Rooms & Suites

Experience sophisticated details at Andaz Munich Schwabinger Tor, such as wooden floors, designer sofas, inspiring art and modern bath amenities that create a comfortable residential feel where you can relax in style.

## DINING

### The Lonely Broccoli & Terrace

A modern meat house with a clear point of view. Charcoal-grilled and slow-roasted cuts define the menu, with a focus on dry aging and bold, precise flavors.
The open kitchen keeps the craft in view, bringing natural energy to the room. Outside, the terrace extends the experience into the open air, with a setting that feels social, relaxed and unmistakably Munich.
Private dining sits slightly apart, offering a more discreet setting, while signature cuts and roasts are also available to order for takeaway, for those evenings that are better kept to yourself.

Details + Reservations

### In Room Dining

When the room becomes the setting, a full menu is available to order in. From breakfast in bed to a late-night dish or a bottle of wine, the experience shifts to a more private pace.
Each dish reflects the same approach as the hotel’s restaurants, with a focus on quality, seasonal and regionally sourced ingredients. Flavors are clear and confident, brought together in a way that feels easy, flexible, and entirely on your terms.
The menu moves from lighter options to more substantial plates, alongside a curated selection of drinks, delivered directly to the room, making it easy to stay in a little longer.

### Bicicletta & Terrace

A cafe shaped by Munich’s cycling culture. Coffee, freshly prepared juices, and healthy smoothies sit alongside sandwiches, lighter dishes, and a selection of simple, well-made snacks that carry easily through the day. Simple, full of flavor, and built around what works.
Open directly to the street, the space connects naturally with the neighborhood, while the terrace extends outdoors in warmer months. Andaz bicycles are available to rent, offering a more active way to explore the city.

Details + Reservations

### M’Uniqo Rooftop Bar & Restaurant

Set on the 13th floor, M’Uniqo Rooftop Bar opens onto sweeping views across Munich and toward the Bavarian Alps, with the terrace bringing a distinctly social energy to the experience.
Mediterranean-influenced dishes and cocktails bring a lighter, more vibrant edge, while the rooftop is also available for private events, set apart as one of the city’s most sought-after settings.
Children under the age of 15 are only permitted when accompanied by a legal guardian and until 10:00 PM. Guests aged 16–17 must also be accompanied by a legal guardian. Pets may be allowed until 10:00 PM with a reservation. Please contact the team in advance.

Details + Reservations

### Andaz Lounge Bar

A setting that moves naturally between lounge, bar, and social hub, shaped by an open energy within the hotel. Curated design gives the setting its character, creating a place where guests and locals naturally come together.

Coffee and lighter dishes define the daytime, giving way into the evening to handcrafted mixology and a more relaxed energy, with the bar becoming a natural meeting point within the hotel. From early mornings through to late evenings, the space holds a steady presence, whether for a quick coffee, a casual meeting, or a drink at the bar.

Details + Reservations

### Schwabinger Tor Bräu Brewhouse & Brewery

A neighborhood Wirtshaus and in-house brewery rooted in Bavarian tradition and the contemporary energy of Schwabing. House-brewed beers and straightforward, quality-driven dishes set the tone, served with a warm, easygoing sense of hospitality. From after-work drinks to long Stammtisch evenings, the setting remains authentic, social, and unmistakably local.
Rooted in a more honest, uncomplicated approach, the focus stays on what matters, good beer, proper food, and a sense of place that feels genuine and unforced.

Details + Reservations

### Breakfast, Your Way

Mornings move at their own pace. Settle in for the full breakfast buffet at The Lonely Broccoli, choose à la carte favorites in the Andaz Lounge or enjoy breakfast in your room with Room Service.
For something quick and easy, Bicicletta Café offers grab-and-go choices and a self-service à la carte breakfast.

Starting before sunrise? Order our Early Bird Breakfast the day before and have it ready when you are.

## Reviews

### 4.5/ 5

Tripadvisor

Tripadvisor Traveler Rating

1,035 reviews

We recently stayed at Andaz Tor in Munich for 3 and 2 nights (twice, before and after Malta) and it was an unforgettable experience!

On the first stay, we had two king bed rooms, and they were the best, we loved the hotel so much that we decided to upgrade to two suites for our second stay. Every aspect of our stay was outstanding—from the smooth check-in and welcome drinks to the trendy and luxurious room, ultra-comfortable mattress, and cozy bedding. Breakfast is exceptional, I usually don’t have breakfast but I couldn’t resist. Tips: outdoor seating is the best.
The team here takes hospitality to the next level!

A special shout-out to Nikolas, who went above and beyond to make us feel welcome. His exceptional service was so impressive that if the rating scale went beyond 5 stars, we’d gladly give Andaz a 6!

The hotel amenities were fantastic—a well-equipped gym, a stunning indoor pool (stainless steel pool, one of the best we’ve seen) with breathtaking views. There’s also a convenience store and a few great restaurants nearby and car rental co just a 10-minute walk away.

We loved our stay so much that we’d return to Munich just to experience this hotel again. Andaz Tor truly redefines luxury and comfort —highly recommended!
