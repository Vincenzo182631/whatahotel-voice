200 sq mCity View

Floor Plan

Set on the 11th floor, the Penthouse Suite unfolds across 2,152 sq ft of open-plan space, with sweeping views across Munich and toward the Alps. The generous layout features a grand king bed, separate living room and dining area designed for up to eight guests.

The spacious bathroom features a freestanding hot tub and clean, contemporary design.
