## The Lonely Broccoli & Terrace

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

A modern meat house with a clear point of view. Charcoal-grilled and slow-roasted cuts define the menu, with a focus on dry aging and bold, precise flavors.
The open kitchen keeps the craft in view, bringing natural energy to the room. Outside, the terrace extends the experience into the open air, with a setting that feels social, relaxed and unmistakably Munich.
Private dining sits slightly apart, offering a more discreet setting, while signature cuts and roasts are also available to order for takeaway, for those evenings that are better kept to yourself.

## In Room Dining

When the room becomes the setting, a full menu is available to order in. From breakfast in bed to a late-night dish or a bottle of wine, the experience shifts to a more private pace.
Each dish reflects the same approach as the hotel’s restaurants, with a focus on quality, seasonal and regionally sourced ingredients. Flavors are clear and confident, brought together in a way that feels easy, flexible, and entirely on your terms.
The menu moves from lighter options to more substantial plates, alongside a curated selection of drinks, delivered directly to the room, making it easy to stay in a little longer.

## M’Uniqo Rooftop Bar & Restaurant

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

Reservations are required.

Set on the 13th floor, M’Uniqo Rooftop Bar opens onto sweeping views across Munich and toward the Bavarian Alps, with the terrace bringing a distinctly social energy to the experience.
Mediterranean-influenced dishes and cocktails bring a lighter, more vibrant edge, while the rooftop is also available for private events, set apart as one of the city’s most sought-after settings.
Children under the age of 15 are only permitted when accompanied by a legal guardian and until 10:00 PM. Guests aged 16–17 must also be accompanied by a legal guardian. Pets may be allowed until 10:00 PM with a reservation. Please contact the team in advance.

## Schwabinger Tor Bräu Brewhouse & Brewery

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

Schwabing is brewing again!

A neighborhood Wirtshaus and in-house brewery rooted in Bavarian tradition and the contemporary energy of Schwabing. House-brewed beers and straightforward, quality-driven dishes set the tone, served with a warm, easygoing sense of hospitality. From after-work drinks to long Stammtisch evenings, the setting remains authentic, social, and unmistakably local.
Rooted in a more honest, uncomplicated approach, the focus stays on what matters, good beer, proper food, and a sense of place that feels genuine and unforced.
