# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self-Parking

The garage at Schwabinger Tor is directly connected to the hotel, with over 900 parking spaces, including 23 EV charging stations.
Please note that the parking facility is independently operated and not affiliated with Hyatt or Andaz Munich. Parking fees and terms are set by Park One GmbH.
