Wine & Dine

What's Italian food without wine? Poised at the gateway to a valley full of world-class vineyards, we've carefully curated an award-winning wine list recognized by Wine Spectator for several years in a row.

Brunch, Homegrown & Homemade

The bounties of our own vineyard garden and chicken coop shine bright in brunch delights, served daily. Begin the day lightly with a mushroom frittata or salmon benedict, or savor a chicken parm sandwich and sweet Italian pastries.

## Meet The Chef

Chef Patrick Callarec

A native of Perigueux, France, Patrick Callarec entered a special French culinary academy when he was only 13 years old. His remarkable culinary career has since spanned over 40 years in kitchens around the world and he was awarded the prestigious title of Maîtres Cuisiniers de France in 2023.

## Paws on the Patio

Yappy Hour at Olive & Hay

Gather on the patio for Yappy Hour, a playful event series made for you and your pup to enjoy together. Sip, savor and mingle with fellow dog lovers while enjoying Yappy Hour food and drink menus. Each gathering offers a fresh reason to return for a relaxed Wine Country evening with your four-legged companion.
