## FAQ's

Do you offer shuttles to downtown Napa?

Yes — The Meritage Resort and Spa offers a complimentary shuttle to Downtown Napa. Shuttles depart from the hotel hourly from 4:00 PM–10:00 PM, with return pickups from downtown in the evenings as well.

Do you have a pool for families?

For families, we offer a family-friendly pool with cabanas, kid-friendly swimming spaces and poolside snacks available for purchase. No lifeguards are on duty, so parents are advised to watch children.

What outdoor activities do you offer for kids?

We offer several ways for the little ones to play! Outdoor activities for kids include:

- Lawn games
- Outdoor movie nights in summer
- Vineyard walking trails, working chicken coop, and Chef’s garden
- Seasonal events like ice skating, s’mores, and holiday activities
- Pool activities and cabanas
- Nearby bike rentals and family-friendly exploration around Napa Valley

Do you offer indoor activities for kids?

For indoor activities, The Meritage Resort and Spa offers:

- Bowling at Crush Lounge (fun for the whole family!)
- Family movie rentals
- Seasonal indoor family programming and entertainment spaces

What dining options are available for kids?

We offer several kid-friendly dining options, including several casual and family-friendly restaurants and markets:

- Olive & Hay
- Crush Lounge
- Blend Café
- Fivetown Grocery

The resort also offers kid-friendly dining options from our in-room dining menu.
