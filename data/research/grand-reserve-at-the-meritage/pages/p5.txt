Game On

Get a group together to catch the game, or have your own friendly competition. With 12 high-def TVs, six lanes of bowling, bar games and a mouth-watering food and drink menu, a night out at Crush means fun for everyone.

Bites, Brews & Boozy Shakes

Enjoy laid-back drinks and dinner, or indulge in late-night cravings. Crush serves shareable appetizers, fish tacos, crisp salads, burgers and more. For the ultimate sweet, spiked treat, end with one of our signature boozy milkshakes.

### Private Events

Strike the perfect balance of food and fun for your next birthday party, corporate teambuilding event or holiday celebration. Group reservations for bowling, full venue buyouts and special menus are available for 10 or more guests.
