## An Urban _Oasis_ in the Heart of Downtown Austin

Experience unrivaled service at a vibrant, luxury hotel in downtown Austin. Discover the best of the city through an array of amenities, chef-driven cuisine, and the renowned Fairmont Spa Austin. Unwind in world-class accommodations, celebrate unforgettable events and bask in the sun at Austin’s largest rooftop pool. A memorable luxury Texas getaway awaits.

### Welcoming Luxury with an Austin Flair

Book an Austin getaway that is designed to exceed expectations. Choose from 1,048 richly appointed guest rooms and suites and the elevated Fairmont Gold, our exclusive hotel-within-a-hotel experience. Expect seamless service along with stunning views of Austin’s skyline and direct access to The Confluence at Waterloo Greenway, where scenic trails, public art and lush green space begin just steps from the hotel.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is parking available at Fairmont Austin?

Yes, valet parking, self-parking, and electric vehicle charging are available at Fairmont Austin. Self-parking hourly rates range from $25-$55. Valet parking hourly rates range from $36-$68. View more information regarding parking here.

Does Fairmont Austin have a spa?

Yes. Fairmont Spa Austin offers a day spa experience and features nine treatment rooms including two couples suites along with two lavishly appointed solariums showcasing panoramic skyline views. Each solarium offers a saline soaking pool, eucalyptus steam grotto, and European dry heat sauna.

Does Fairmont Austin have a gym/fitness center?

Yes. Fairmont Austin has a revolutionary fitness center located on the 6th floor and is open 24 hours. The expansive 2,800sq/ft fitness center features modern equipment and floor-to-ceiling windows, overlooking the lush greenery of Palm Park.

Is Fairmont Austin pet-friendly?

Yes, our dog-friendly hotel, Fairmont Austin welcomes pets weighing up to 25 pounds for a one-time fee of $100. Pets must be on a leash at all times and not be left unattended in the room. Any damage incurred to the room by the pet is the responsibility of the guest. Service animals are always welcome and exempt from charges.

How can I get to Fairmont Austin from the nearest airport?

Fairmont Austin is located approximately 10 miles from Austin Bergstrom International Airport. Directions from the airport:

From Presidential Blvd, take TX-71 W.

Then take I-35 N/US-290 E to N I-35 Frontage Road.

Exit 234B, then follow to E 1st St/E Cesar Chavez St. to Red River St.

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Austin?

Yes, Fairmont Austin strives to meet the needs of all guests with disabilities. Please contact the hotel for any special requests relating to accessibility or if you require any additional information about the hotel’s facilities and services.
