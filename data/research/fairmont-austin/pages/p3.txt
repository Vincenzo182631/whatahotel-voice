In a nutshell
: A comfortable, spacious Austin stay with enough amenities to make you want to stay on property, despite the super central location.
I stayed at the Fairmont Austin for a few nights in late summer as part of my brother's wedding block, and it made for an excellent home base for our family and friends. Given the busyness of the wedding weekend, we were happy to have booked Fairmont Gold rooms, which gave us access to a private check-in experience and the lounge, where we met most mornings for breakfast and most afternoons for canapes and cocktails from the all-day honor bar. The pool was another favorite space, both for getting some sun in the morning and cooling off in the hot Austin afternoons. Le Labo bath amenities, cozy bathrobes, spacious rooms, and a warm and welcoming staff rounded out the memorable stay.
