## Accommodations

Enjoy an unforgettable stay at our London accommodation. All our spacious rooms, residences, and hotel suites in London are filled with light, with an airy ambience that brings a sense of calm away from...See More the busy city. After a restful night in our signature Heavenly® Bed, a refreshing Heavenly® Shower and a healthy breakfast to set you up for the day, step out to experience the City or enjoy a walk along the River Thames. For families and longer stays, interconnecting and family rooms offer added flexibility, while guests often highlight the comfortable beds, spacious rooms and spotless surroundings.See Less

Discover your perfect suite escape in London

Make your London escape truly special in one of The Westin London City’s signature suites. Choose the St. Paul’s Duplex, Executive or Presidential Suite and enjoy Champagne, a Chef’s Hamper, Personal Concierge, breakfast, Westin Club access and a guaranteed 1pm late check-out.

Learn More

### St. Paul's Duplex Suites

These one-of-a-kind Duplex Suites feature floor-to-ceiling windows over two floors offering the ultimate London experience. These exclusives suites offer a one-bedroom mezzanine with a Heavenly® Bed and feature a large sitting area with an ample desk.

### Executive Suite

The Executive Suite offers a residential feel within our contemporary hotel. The large light filled bedrooms is complemented by a separate living and dining area. Through floor-to-ceiling windows, London floods into this suite, bringing light to the space. A generous bathroom features both a walk-in shower and a Heavenly® bathtub.

### Presidential Suite

The Presidential Suite offers the ultimate London living experience. Watch the sun setting on St. Paul’s from the comfort of your private terrace. This exclusive residential-style suite features one bedroom, two bathrooms, a kitchenette, living room and dining room for up to 10 guests making the ideal spot for entertaining and taking striking views.

### Luxurious Residences Overlooking the River Thames

Explore the allure of The Westin London City residences, where modernity, elegance, and breathtaking panoramas converge. Each residence boasts three bedrooms, a balcony overlooking the River Thames, and a fully equipped kitchen. Dive into exclusivity with access to exceptional amenities like the WestinWORKOUT Fitness Studio and the rejuvenating Heavenly Spa by Westin. Unwind in Mosaic or Hithe + Seek, abundant spaces for relaxation and leisure, while surrounded by the beauty of the City of London. Elevate your well-being in this oasis of luxury.

## Heavenly Amenities

Nothing restores your mind and body like a restful experience. That’s why we offer a range of bespoke amenities to elevate your mood. The Westin Heavenly Bed is uniquely designed with plush, pillow-top construction for premium comfort and support. Slip into the Westin Heavenly Bed and experience a night of restorative sleep to get you ready for the day ahead. Awaken your senses with the soothing scent of the white tea amenities as our nourishing blend of aloe and essential oils revitalize your mind.

## Design-Driven

Encompassing modern elegance, the interior design of the guest rooms and suites has been curated to enhance well-being. The palette of natural colours has been carefully selected to evoke feelings of tranquillity whilst furnishings seek inspiration from the natural world in its purest form and feature oak timber panelling, rich green hues, matt finishes, and curved lines.

## Westin Club

Located on the hotel’s fourth floor and set away from the other communal areas sat on the bridge across Upper Thames Street, the Westin Club provides guests with an exclusive area for quiet contemplation. Open daily, experience daily breakfast, all-day snacks and soft drinks with high-speed internet access and more.

### Pet Friendly

We are welcoming mid-sized dogs and smaller ones (so long as your friend weighs up to 15kg). Contact us directly for more information.

### Westin Family Experience

Enjoy family-friendly moments with Westin! Our programs ensure every guest, including kids, leaves feeling better. At check-in, kids receive a Westin Family Travel Journal and Local Activity Guide with engaging activities. We prioritize nutritious options with the Westin Eat Well Menu and the Heavenly Spa by Westin offers special Kids time for wellness. Explore Westin Family for an adventure-filled vacation!
