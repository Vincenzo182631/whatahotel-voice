## Hithe + Seek, a Destination Bar and Kitchen Overlooking the River Thames

Introducing Hithe + Seek, nestled in the heart of London's bustling City, a bar and kitchen that's a world of contrasts for city dwellers. It's not just a venue; it's a meticulously crafted adventure offering an array of wines, cocktails, and small plates.
As the sun sets and the city lights come alive, immerse yourself in a unique setting, savouring carefully selected wines paired with delightful food combinations. Looking for something different? Explore our newest addition: an afternoon tea experience with a front-row view of the River Thames. We've reimagined the traditional afternoon tea with a Hithe + Seek twist.

Learn More

### Afternoon Tea at Mosaic

Experience the timeless charm of a traditional afternoon tea at Mosaic. Whether you combine it with a relaxing spa treatment or simply savour it with a loved one, Mosaic offers the perfect backdrop to enjoy this iconic experience in a contemporary atmosphere.

See the Menu

### The Curator Corner

This exceptional dining spot provides a captivating view overlooking the River Thames. Whether you’re celebrating a special occasion, hosting a gathering with friends, or simply indulging in a memorable meal, this space ensures an unforgettable dining experience for up to 12 guests.

Learn More

### Cocktail Masterclass

Let your hair down and enjoy an interactive cocktail-making masterclass with friends and colleagues. Each masterclass includes two cocktails of our mixologists' choice, demonstrated and then made and enjoyed by your group. The masterclass also comes with a delicious selection of charcuterie, cheese and bread to share. Masterclasses are available for two to six persons.

Book Now

## Mosaic, Convivial and Conscious Eating

Mosaic, an open and spacious restaurant with a city-facing view, offers all-day dining. The menu includes sustainably sourced international cuisines, nutritious recipes, and local favorites, complemented by selections from the Westin Eat Well menu.

Learn More

### Westin Fresh by The Juicery

Revitalize during your stay with an assortment of nourishing, healthy juices and smoothies developed by Westin in partnership with the experts at The Juicery.

### Westin Eat Well Menu

Our Chef has crafted these delicious dishes with your well-being in mind, giving you the freedom to choose when it comes to portion size, nutritional balance and quality of ingredients.

### Taittinger Champagne Bar

As night falls, sip on chilled flutes of Taittinger whilst overlooking the bustling city beneath. In addition, the carefully curated ‘Crafted at Westin’ cocktail menu features a collection of expertly crafted beverages infused with natural ingredients and fresh local flavours.
