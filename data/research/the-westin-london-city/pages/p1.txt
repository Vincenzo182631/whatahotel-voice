# Welcome to The Westin London City

## The essence of well-being in The City of London

The Westin London City boasts an impressive riverside location with superb views over London's River Thames, overlooking Shakespeare's Globe and Tate Modern. Our luxury hotel in London offers 226 guest...See More rooms, suites and residences with the Westin Heavenly Bed, sleek bathrooms, and free high-speed Wi-Fi, ideal for business travelers, couples' city breaks, and restorative spa stays. Guests praise attentive service, spotless rooms, and convenient City access. Experience Hithe + Seek, a stunning bar with panoramic London views, and Mosaic, our convivial all-day dining restaurant. We are also one of the top pet-friendly hotels in London. For meetings and events, choose from seven thoughtfully designed spaces, including the signature Westin Ballroom. The WestinWORKOUT Fitness Studio is complemented by Heavenly Spa by Westin with six therapy rooms, a tranquil pool area and innovative thermal treatments. On-site parking and a nearby off-site option add convenience. A 5-star hotel and spa in London experience awaits.See Less

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I have a river view room?

None of our hotel rooms face the River Thames however you will be able to enjoy the river view from our beautiful wine bar Hithe + Seek.

What identification do I need to check in?

All non-UK residents must provide their passport. All UK residents must provide identification in the form of a driving license or passport. Failure to do so may result in being unable to check into the property. NB – if you are on a corporate rate you may be asked for your corporate identification also for proof of rate usage.

Do I need a payment method to check in?

Yes - Please be advised all reservations are subject to a £75 per day authorisation giving you the freedom of the property to fully immerse yourself in the hotel and charge your consumptions to your room with no hassle or worry. Please note that we are operating a cashless policy.

Top Tip: If you have an advance purchase reservation don’t forget your card you paid with, don’t worry if you have forgotten it, we will refund the initial payment card and use an alternative method of payment on your arrival.

Is your main entrance your only entrance?

No, we advise all guests arriving via car to our main entrance on Upper Thames Street but if arriving on foot or via tube (closest station: Mansion House) please use our north entrance via Huggin Hill. Please be advised from 22:00 – 07:00 this door is accessible with hotel guest keys only.

Where can I eat within the hotel?

We have two fantastic outlets on property as well as our 24 hour In-Room Dining service. Mosaic is a convivial city all-day fine dining venue featuring global dishes, nutritious recipes and local favourites sat alongside the Westin Eat Well menu. Hithe + Seek is a wine bar set in the heart of the City of London, thoughtfully created as an interactive and explorative experience. Offering a unique setting when the sun goes down and the city light go up, sip on carefully selected wines complimented by food pairings.

What is the difference between a Classic Room and a Signature King Room?

The only difference between these two categories is that our Classic Rooms offer accessible features. Did you know? All new build hotels must offer 10% inventory as accessible rooms by law.

Where is the closest Car Park to the property?

The closest car parking facilities to the property are the NCP Thames Vintry, this is a locally managed parking facility that is circa 10-minute walk to the property. Top Tip: park your car before coming to the property, due to the one-way system around the hotel it can take time to get back to the car park once arriving at the hotel due the London traffic.

Can I bring balloons and decorations for my room?

We do not allow external decoration packages as this causes extensive damage to our rooms, please be advised deep cleaning and damage fees will apply. The great news is our in-house Guest Experience Team are more than happy to organise this on your behalf with a range of different packages for different occasions, please do reach out to guest.experience@westinlondon.com for more information.

Can I have a bath in my room?

Please be advised most of our rooms are shower only, our room categories with bathtubs are our Signature Family and Luxury Family rooms along with our Executive and Presidential Suite. Please do reach out to our reservations team ( reservations@westinlondon.com) for more information.

Is there an airport shuttle?

We do not offer an airport shuttle, however, please do reach out to our concierge team ( concierge@westinlondon.com) who can offer an array of Train, Tube and Car options for you to get to the property.

What is the age restriction for guests checking in to a room?

Guests need to be 18 years of age or older to check into a room. Those under 18 years old are not permitted to stay at The Westin London City unless a parent or a guardian is also staying at the hotel. We reserve the right to request valid photographic proof of identity and age, otherwise where requested you/and or your guests will not be permitted to stay.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin London City?

The check-in time at The Westin London City is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin London City allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin London City. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 15.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of £50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin London City?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin London City. The daily self parking fee is £65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin London City have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin London City has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin London City booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin London City?

The closest airport near The Westin London City is London City Airport (LCY). LCY is located approximately 7.9 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin London City have an airport shuttle to London City Airport (LCY)?

No, The Westin London City does not have an airport shuttle to London City Airport.

Does The Westin London City have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin London City has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does The Westin London City have a Westin Club lounge?

Yes, The Westin London City has Westin Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Westin Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
