### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin London City have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin London City offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Technogym Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin London City have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin London City has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:
