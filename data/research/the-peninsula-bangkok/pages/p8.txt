The Fitness Centre provides the latest in state-of the-art equipment, ensuring you get the most out of working out while away from home. Guests are invited to take advantage of the range of treadmills, elliptical, cycle and stair-climbing machines by Life Fitness, complete with personal television monitors and headphones. A wide selection of weight and strength training equipment is also available, from a full range of free weights to TRX kettle bells.
