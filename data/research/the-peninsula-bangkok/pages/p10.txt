The Peninsula Spa

Rejuvenate Your Senses The Peninsula Spa item 2

The Peninsula Spa is located in a stunning three-story Thai colonial-style building by the river, which houses a number of private suites with steam showers and an en-suite whirlpool overlooking the Chao Phraya River.

Travel may open your eyes, but nothing awakens the senses like a world class pampering experience at The Peninsula Spa. Maintain your physical well-being at our fully equipped The Fitness Centre or simply lounge by the pool with a nutritious Naturally Peninsula meal and relax in our sauna, steam room and plunge pools.

Traditional methods from European, Oriental and Ayurvedic philosophies seamlessly combine with contemporary comforts at The Peninsula Spa. Select from a menu of indulgent massages and facials or request that one of The Peninsula Spa’s highly trained therapists create a bespoke treatment tailor-made to suit your specific needs for a truly wholesome and enlightening experience.

## Additional Information

#### Arrival Time

You are encouraged to arrive 30 minutes prior to your appointment in order to change, relax and enjoy the exclusive facilities at The Spa. Please be advised that late arrivals will result in a reduction of treatment time. You are advised to arrive at The Spa wearing the robe and slippers provided in the guest room, and keep valuables in the in-room safe.

#### Spa Etiquette

Our Spa environment is one of pure relaxation and tranquility. Please respect the right of all our guests to enjoy privacy and serenity. In light of this, please turn off your mobile phones, pagers or other handheld devices. Smoking is strictly prohibited. The Spa will provide towels, slippers, shower caps and all amenities required for use during your visit. A minimum age of 16 is required for access and treatments.
