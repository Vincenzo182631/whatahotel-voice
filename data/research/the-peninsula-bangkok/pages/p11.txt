The Peninsula Bangkok Travel in Style item 2

Experience Bangkok with effortless elegance through The Peninsula Bangkok’s exceptional selection of luxury transportation. From the refined comfort of a fleet of BMW 7 Series or Mercedes-Benz van to the exceptional convenience of helicopter transfers via the hotel’s private helipad, every journey is designed with sophistication in mind.

For a more immersive perspective of the city, guests may explore Bangkok in true Thai style aboard the hotel’s customised three-wheeled tuk-tuks, or glide along the Chao Phraya River aboard one of four beautifully crafted, rice-barge-inspired vessels. These iconic vessels provide seamless connections to the BTS Skytrain, ICONSIAM and selected public piers upon request, offering a distinctive and memorable way to discover the city.

## Rolls-Royce Silver Spur

The iconic Rolls-Royce Silver Spur is available for display at your special events, embodying classic British refinement and understated luxury; its distinguished styling and finely appointed leather interior create an elegant statement that complements The Peninsula’s tradition of impeccable service and timeless sophistication.

## BMW 7 Series

Travel in luxury with The Peninsula Bangkok’s BMW 7 Series, finished in signature Peninsula Green. Set against the Chao Phraya River, its sleek design and composed presence reflect understated elegance. Inside, a finely appointed cabin offers exceptional comfort and privacy, ensuring each journey is seamless and every arrival quietly distinguished.

A private BMW 750e is available at THB 4,900 net per car per one way.

(Maximum capacity: 3 passengers and 2 pieces of regular-sized luggage)

EXPLORE OUR SELECTION

## Tuk Tuk

Experience Bangkok in distinctive Peninsula style aboard our signature Tuk Tuk and Electric Tuk Tuk, offering convenient drop-off and pick-up service at ICONSIAM. Elegantly designed and unmistakably Thai, both bring local character to every journey, while our Electric Tuk Tuk reflects The Peninsula’s commitment to sustainability and a more environmentally conscious approach to this iconic Thai experience.

## The Peninsula Boat

The Peninsula Boat offers an elegant way to travel along the Chao Phraya River. Reserved exclusively for hotel guests, these beautifully crafted boats provide a refined and comfortable journey, seamlessly connecting the hotel with nearby riverside destinations. Combining timeless style with attentive service, The Peninsula Boat reflects the hotel’s signature sophistication while showcasing the charm of Bangkok from the water.

## Helicopter

Experience Bangkok from a breathtaking new perspective with The Peninsula Bangkok’s helicopter service. Offering swift and stylish transfers above the city’s vibrant skyline, this exclusive experience combines remarkable views with the hotel’s renowned standards of luxury, comfort and impeccable service.
