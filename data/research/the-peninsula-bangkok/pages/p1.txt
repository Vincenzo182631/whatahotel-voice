# The Peninsula Bangkok

**An Oasis on the River of Kings**

Nestled on the western bank of the Chao Phraya River in Thonburi's vibrant heritage district, The Peninsula Bangkok offers the best of both worlds: convenient access to the city's treasures and a tranquil sanctuary. The resort's lush waterside property features a sweeping lawn, an expansive infinity pool surrounded by tropical gardens, 367 elegant rooms and suites, and a range of sumptuous onsite amenities. These include four restaurants serving world-class Eastern and Western cuisine, a state-of-the-art fitness centre, and a luxurious spa offering a variety of therapeutic treatments and daily wellness activities.
