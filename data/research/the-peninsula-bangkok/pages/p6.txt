## Reimagine the timeless glamour of travel in modern Asia

Blending signature Peninsula elegance with authentic Thai charm, The Peninsula Bangkok is a tranquil riverside retreat in the heart of the city. Honoured with MICHELIN Keys and the Forbes Five-Star rating, the hotel is renowned for exceptional service and world-class hospitality, reflecting the shared excellence of all 12 Peninsula properties worldwide.
