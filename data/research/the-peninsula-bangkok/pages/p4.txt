#### DINING

# THIPTARA THAI RESTAURANT

Overlooking the Chao Phraya River, Thiptara is a soulfull expression of Thai dining, framed by traditional teakwood salas and tropical gardens.

Led by Chef Luk, the menu draws on tradition and technique, creating a focused expression of local dining that plays out against the rhythm of the river and Bangkok.
