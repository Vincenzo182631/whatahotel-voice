### Pickleball at The Peninsula Bangkok

Discover one of the world’s fastest-growing sports with pickleball at The Peninsula Bangkok. Combining elements of tennis, badminton, and table tennis, pickleball offers a fun, social, and accessible fitness experience suitable for beginners and seasoned players alike.

Our hotel-instructor-led pickleball sessions take place on professionally maintained courts, with paddles and balls provided for your convenience. Guests may enjoy casual court play, guided knocker sessions, or structured coaching designed to enhance technique, coordination, and match play confidence.
