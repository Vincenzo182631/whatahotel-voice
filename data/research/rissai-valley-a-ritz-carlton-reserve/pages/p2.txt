Majestic Mountain Retreat
Suites & Rooms
Overlooking picturesque mountains and ancient Tibetan villages, villas at Rissai Valley offer a rare experience: the sense of being alone yet part of something much greater. Designed as modern interpretations of traditional area dwellings, villas are a celebration of the surroundings. Materials, textiles and furnishings are a reflection of strong local traditions, and expansive private terraces provide a peaceful space to observe yaks roaming the land or stars twinkling in the vast night sky.

Features and Amenities
Mountain Views
The Minshan mountains are integrated into the villa experience with floor-to-ceiling windows and an expansive terrace.
Personal Host
Guests are guided by a dedicated Nieba who serves as a source of local knowledge and connection to resort experiences. Named for the Tibetan stewards who managed nobles and leaders, Nieba are available 24/7 and can arrange any need or wish.
In-Room Dining
Beautifully plated with a local touch and a special treat from the chef. Available whenever you desire.
Bathroom Sanctuary
Bathrooms feature an oversized walk-in tub, separate rainforest shower and double vanity.
Wellness & Beauty
Bathroom amenities were created in collaboration with Parisian “perfume publisher” Frédéric Malle.
