A Gift From Nature
In Jiuzhaigou along the Tibetan plateau, visitors discover that nature is more than a simple setting. At Rissai Valley, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, soil is the source of flavor and delight, mountain peaks are the blueprint for striking architecture, ancient plants are the salve for modern stress, and cascading water is the only sound that breaks the peaceful silence.
Rissai Spa
The six wonders of Jiuzhaigou, from its snow-peaked mountains to its emerald-green lakes, are transformed into well-being rituals crafted to restore peace and renew energy.
Experiences
Connections to culture and nature are nurtured through experiences that remind us that the world we know is a fraction of what there is to discover.
