Equally abundant in nature and culture, Jiuzhaigou is where exploration is as much an inner journey as an external one. Technicolor lakes, spectacular waterfalls and lush mountains provide the backdrop for both quiet reflection and outdoor adventure. Surrounding Rissai Valley, nine ancient villages are a testament to centuries of tradition and an opportunity to carry some of that wisdom home.

Rissai Spa
Rissai Spa is both an homage to the exquisite scenery of Jiuzhaigou and an invitation to be immersed within it. Drawing a connection between the healing elements of nature and the centuries-old rituals of Tibetan culture, the spa experience is one of transformation in which change is reflected on the outside yet felt deeply within.
Spa Details
Set amid the breathtaking landscape of Jiuzhaigou, Rissai Spa features seven treatment rooms, including two couple’s suites and a private suite, and a heated indoor pool with a panoramic mountain view. For spa details or reservations, please call the front desk.
Featured Treatments
Eco Cellular Renew Ritual
Nature’s powerful antioxidants are showcased in this anti-aging facial that stimulates cellular metabolism and collagen production, leaving skin firm, hydrated and radiant.
Breath Flow Ritual
The ability of breath to calm, cleanse and reenergize has been known for centuries. Breathwork is paired with an uplifting massage to enhance circulation, ease muscle tension and bring a sense of renewal and bliss.
Eaglewood Vibration Ritual
There is no better healer than deep, restful sleep. The perfect end to a day of adventure, this massage combines the soothing vibrations of Tibetan singing bowls with therapeutic agarwood oil to encourage a deep state of calm.
Fitness Center
The 24-hour fitness center offers Technogym cardio machines, weigh training equipment and a dedicated a movement studio for yoga and other classes.
A Story of the Seasons
In southwest China’s Jiuzhaigou Valley, each season begins a new chapter in a story that goes back centuries. Named for the nine Tibetan villages nestled in the surrounding mountains, the valley is an example of the beauty that flourishes when nature is left untouched. In this UNESCO World Heritage Site, mountain peaks glisten with snow, lakes are a rich emerald green, waterfalls tumble into rivers and streams, and forests are a seemingly endless canopy of color.
Nature Experiences
Explore Jiuzhaigou’s 180,000-acre nature reserve with hiking and biking excursions or an evening of stargazing.
Tibetan Journeys
The surrounding villages present an opportunity to connect with Tibetan culture and traditions through home visits, incense workshops and other immersive experiences.
Mind and Body
Nature’s ability to center and calm is evident in offerings including mountain Pilates, waterfall yoga and plant painting.
Indoor Activities
Rediscover the joy of friendly competition with a game of mahjong, billiards or table tennis.
Kid's Activities
Kids are invited to discover Jiuzhaigou and the resort with arts and crafts, treasure hunts, kite-flying and organic farming.
The Journey Matters, Too
Arriving to the Resort
We offer limousine transportation to and from Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport. Guests may also travel from Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport via helicopter to a nearby helipad where we can arrange transportation to the resort.
Around the Resort
Touring the resort or simply getting from one experience to the other can be done via chauffeured buggy. Rides can be requested through reception.
Exploring the Area
We can arrange guided and non-guided transportation to the national park and other destinations for groups and individuals.
Worry-free for National Park Ticket
With seamless access to scenic wonders, dwell in serenity beneath the snow-capped peaks.
