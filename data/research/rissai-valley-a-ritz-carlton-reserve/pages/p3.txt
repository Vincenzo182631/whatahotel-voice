Dining
In Jiuzhaigou, food creates connections among people, but it also connects people to the place itself. An exploration of both the familiar and the unknown, dining at Rissai Valley draws from its physical and cultural surroundings to present cuisine that imprints the palate and marks the soul.

La Montagne
An Ode to the Mountains
Inspired by the breathtaking mountain views in Jiuzhaigou and across Europe, La Montagne brings the two worlds together with fusion dishes crafted from local ingredients and service that reflects the elegance and warmth of both cultures.
Dine and a Show
With a direct view into the kitchen, the private dining room provides a front-row seat to the craftsmanship applied to transform regional, seasonal ingredients into modern, elegant dishes. With space for up to 10 guests, the private venue serves as a special experience for personal or professional gatherings.
Cai Lin Xuan
Translated as “colorful forest,” Cai Lin Xuan brings the beauty, colors and bounty of the surrounding landscape to the table through traditional Sichuan, Tibetan and Cantonese cuisines.
Lobby Lounge
In Tibet, the expression Alaqiangse is an invitation to partake in a “fine drink.” In the Lobby Lounge, this invitation takes shape in Afternoon Tea, classic cocktails, small-batch drinks from around the world and signature beverages inspired by local flavors and traditions.
Bo Ri Village
A Bo Ri village is a close-knit community where meals are shared among friends and family. At Bo Ri Village restaurant, guests are invited to come together to explore Asian and Western cuisines in a market-inspired dining experience.
