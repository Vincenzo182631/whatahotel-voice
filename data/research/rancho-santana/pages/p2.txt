## Why Stay at Rancho Santana

A stay at Rancho Santana gives guests the chance to enjoy the natural beauty of Nicaragua’s Emerald Coast with the comfort of accommodations designed for relaxation and privacy. Guests have access to five beautiful beaches, farm-to-table dining, outdoor activities, and a landscape that encourages both adventure and calm moments.

With unique options to choose from,  every stay offers a grounded sense of place and the peaceful luxury that defines life at The Ranch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What types of accommodations are available at Rancho Santana?

Rancho Santana offers several stay options that include The Inn, Residential Suites, Ocean View Homes, Pacific Condos, and Garden View Casitas. Each category provides a different layout and setting so guests can choose the option that best fits their travel needs.

### Do any accommodations include kitchens?

Garden View Casitas include fully equipped kitchens as confirmed on the Stay page. Other accommodation types vary in layout, and guests can review individual listings for specific amenities.

### How close are the accommodations to the beach?

All guests have access to Rancho Santana’s five beaches. Pacific Condos are located close to Playa Santana, while other accommodation types are situated throughout the property with either coastal or garden surroundings.
