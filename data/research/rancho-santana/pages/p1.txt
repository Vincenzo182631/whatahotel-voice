## Surf at Rancho Santana

Surf Nicaragua’s Pacific coast from Rancho Santana, where three beaches offer different conditions for surfers of varying experience levels. Take a lesson, book professional coaching, join a guided surf excursion, or simply paddle out and enjoy the waves. With year-round surf and easy access to the water, Rancho Santana makes it easy to build a surf-focused getaway around everything else The Ranch has to offer.

## Surfing at Rancho Santana: Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Rancho Santana a surf resort?

Rancho Santana is a luxury beach resort and residential community on Nicaragua’s Emerald Coast with three surf beaches on the property, surf lessons, professional coaching, guided surf excursions, and surf-focused experiences.

### Is Rancho Santana good for beginner surfers?

Yes. Playa Los Perros offers a more approachable surf break for beginners, with surf lessons and rentals available through Rancho Santana.

### How many surf beaches does Rancho Santana have?

Rancho Santana has three surf beaches on the property: Playa Los Perros, Playa Santana, and Playa Rosada. Guests can also access additional surf breaks through guided excursions along Nicaragua’s Pacific coast.

### Does Rancho Santana offer surf lessons?

Yes. Rancho Santana offers surf lessons, professional coaching, guided surf experiences, and surf-focused packages for guests looking to improve their skills or explore Nicaragua’s waves.
