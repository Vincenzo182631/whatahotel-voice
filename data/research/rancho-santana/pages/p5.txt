## A Coastal Boutique Hotel Experience in Nicaragua

The Inn is Rancho Santana’s boutique luxury hotel, offering an intimate stay at the center of the resort’s walkable village. Set along Nicaragua’s Emerald Coast, it combines handcrafted design, coastal views, and a relaxed yet refined atmosphere.

Its location gives guests convenient access to beaches, trails, resort amenities, and nearby cultural experiences throughout Tola. This makes The Inn an ideal home base for discovering Rancho Santana and the surrounding region.

From your room, you can walk to the Clubhouse and La Finca y El Mar, book a surf lesson at Playa Santana, or unwind at The Spa in El Bosque.

## All stays at The Inn include:

- Daily breakfast per person at La Finca y El Mar
- A welcome beverage
- Complimentary Kids Club access (5-12 years old)
- A guided monkey-viewing tour
- Homes of Rancho Santana: Real Estate Tour
- Access to all of Rancho Santana’s resort facilities
- Airport transfers for stays of 4 nights or more

_\*Inclusions do not apply to some special offers and group bookings._

## **Who The Inn is best for**

The Inn suits travelers who want the ease of a hotel with the character of a private retreat. Its central location puts the beaches, trails, dining, and the spa within a short walk, which makes it a natural choice for a first visit to Rancho Santana, as having the resort’s amenities close by keeps the stay simple.
