Consider these properties your ocean-view homebase for an action-packed trip.

Rancho Santana’s luxury ocean-view homes are private vacation rentals set across 2,700 acres of Nicaragua’s Emerald Coast. Homes range from three to nine bedrooms and sleep 6 to 18 guests, with options for beachfront access, private pools, and walkable proximity to the Clubhouse and beaches. Guests staying in the homes also have access to the resort’s restaurants, spa, surf, and activities.

With more than 25 homes across five beaches, the right fit usually comes down to group size, how close you want to be to the sand, and the amenities you are after. The homes are organized into three collections: the Luxury Collection for top-tier homes with the most amenities, the Signature Collection for added comfort alongside ocean views, and the Adventure Collection as a home base for an active trip. For the largest groups, homes such as Alma Del Mar (sleeps 16) and Villa Paraíso (sleeps 18) offer the most space. Use the filters above to sort by bedrooms, beach, and amenities, or call the reservations team to match a home to your group.
