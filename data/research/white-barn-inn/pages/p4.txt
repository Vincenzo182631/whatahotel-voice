# Dine at White Barn InnA Culinary Legacy. An Unforgettable Setting.

## Unforgettable Dining

Rooted in seasonality and a reverence for place, our contemporary New England cuisine celebrates the finest ingredients from coastal Maine and the surrounding region. Sourced through longstanding relationships with the region’s most respected farmers, fishermen, and foragers, each ingredient is chosen at its finest. The result is a collection of exquisitely crafted dishes that embody the refinement and natural splendor of coastal New England.

## Executive Chef Cornelia Sühr

As Executive Chef, Cornelia Sühr brings a refined, globally informed perspective to the culinary vision of White Barn Inn. Shaped by an upbringing rooted in the culinary arts and a career alongside some of the world’s most respected chefs, her style is defined by seasonality, restraint and a deep respect for exceptional ingredients. Chef Cornelia’s cuisine balances technical precision with an intuitive sense of place, allowing the natural character of each ingredient to guide the experience. Equally committed to mentorship and craftsmanship, she leads with intention—honoring the Inn’s storied heritage while thoughtfully shaping its next chapter.
