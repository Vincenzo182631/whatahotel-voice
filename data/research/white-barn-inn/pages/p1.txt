A coastal landmark for over 150 years, White Barn Inn, Auberge Collection, offers New England hospitality, rustic charm, and relaxed luxury.

Insider Tip

Learn how to prepare signature White Barn Restaurant dishes in a personalized hands-on cooking demonstration alongside the executive chef.

Where You’re Staying

Restored rooms, suites, and cottages blend original details with modern décor inspired by the coast. Rooms can overlook the woods and some include double-sided gas-burning fireplaces. All suites also include fireplaces and oversized jetted or soaking bathtubs. Private cottages boast outdoor sitting areas that overlook the river.

Please note: If you have requests for specific accessibility needs, please contact the property. Upper floors accessible by stairs only.

Where to Dine

The award-winning White Barn Inn Restaurant specializes in contemporary New England cuisine, showcasing ingredients sourced from local farmers, fishermen, and foragers. Little Barn brings the local Maine farmstead and fisheries to the table with flavorful and approachable dishes.

Where to Relax

White Barn’s Spa specializes in treatments designed to rebalance, rejuvenate and revitalize. Choose from a menu of facials, massages, body treatments, mini-treatments, and multi-treatment packages.

What to Do

During lobster season, spend the day experiencing a day in the life of a true Maine lobster fisherman where you’ll try your hand at lobster fishing before returning to the inn to dine on your catch. Take a private cycling tour along the coast, or set off for a beach picnic. Visit Maine’s iconic lighthouses or learn about local artisan cheesemakers with a wine and cheese pairing class.
