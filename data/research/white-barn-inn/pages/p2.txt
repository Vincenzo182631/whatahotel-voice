##### Know Before You Stay

**Where is White Barn Inn located?** White Barn Inn is located at 37 Beach Avenue in Kennebunk, Maine, near Kennebunkport and the Maine coast.

**How do I get to White Barn Inn from the nearest airport?** White Barn Inn is about 25 miles from Portland International Jetport (PWM); the hotel can help secure car service but does not provide airport pickups or shuttle service.

**When is the best time to visit White Barn Inn?** White Barn Inn is appealing year-round, with beaches, boating and outdoor pool time in warmer months and cozy dining, coastal walks and winter activities in cooler seasons.

**Is White Barn Inn pet-friendly?** White Barn Inn welcomes pets in select accommodations; pets are not permitted in the Main Inn except registered service animals, so guests should contact the hotel for eligible room types and fees.

**Does White Barn Inn have a pool?** White Barn Inn has a seasonal outdoor pool with full amenities during the summer months.

**Does White Barn Inn have a spa?** White Barn Inn has a full-service spa offering massages, body treatments, facials, a couples spa suite and a sensory rain shower.

**What are the dining options at White Barn Inn?** Dining at White Barn Inn includes the Forbes Five-Star and AAA Five-Diamond White Barn Restaurant, the more casual Little Barn and private dining experiences across the property.
