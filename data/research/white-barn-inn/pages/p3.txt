## Houseboat Origin Story

The Cora, Nettie, Ivory and Grace Houseboats honor White Barn Inn’s rich history and generation-spanning connection to Kennebunk, Maine. Each is named for a member of the founding family of Cora Toothaker, who transformed the historic barn into an inn nearly a century ago. Inspired by the intrepid spirit and warm hospitality that defined her legacy, these thoughtfully named retreats welcome visitors from near and far - inviting guests to experience the very best of coastal Maine, rooted in history and elevated by modern luxury.
