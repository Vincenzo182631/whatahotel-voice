InterContinental Fujairah Resort
Fresh off a complete overhaul, the hotel is neat, modern, and a serene place for a beachfront getaway in the UAE.

Reviewed by
Laura Ratliff
How did it strike you on arrival?
Unlike other hotels in the area, the InterContinental Fujairah Resort consists of 291 rooms dispersed among low-rise Arabic-style village buildings. Rooms are recently refurbished, making the whole property feel fresh and modern.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
Rooms mimic the adjacent sea: white duvets are topped with blue-green blankets and furniture is sandy-hued. All rooms have balconies for admiring the views.
How about the little things, like mini bar, or shower goodies. Any of that find its way into your suitcase?
All rooms have Bose sound systems, as well as alarm clocks with iPhone chargers. Lights and air-conditioning are controlled from your bedside.
Please tell us the bathroom won’t let us down.
Deluxe suite bathrooms have sliding glass windows over the tub that look out into the room (and ideally, onto the view). Toiletries are by Amouage.
Maybe the most important topic of all: Wi-Fi. What’s the word?
Free.
Anything stand out about other services and features? Whether it’s childcare, gyms, spas, even parking—whatever stuck with you.
The Club Lounge has its own infinity pool, and other activities abound—croquet, paddle ball, tennis, and yoga, among others.
What was most memorable—or heartbreaking—about your stay?
Fresh off a complete overhaul, the hotel is neat, modern, and a serene place for a beachfront getaway in the UAE.
