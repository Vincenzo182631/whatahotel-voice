## Wellness

Wellness at InterContinental Fujairah Resort draws on its striking surroundings, where the Hajar Mountains meet the Indian Ocean. O Spa by L’Occitane offers deeply restorative treatments, while yoga and time outdoors bring a sense of balance and renewal.

Learn More

## Pool

Along the shores of Al Aqah Beach, four temperature-controlled pools trace the coastline. An infinity pool stretches toward the Indian Ocean, while family areas and a secluded Club pool create distinct moments at every hour at this Fujairah beach resort.

## Endless Relaxation

Along Al Aqah Beach, time moves with the changing light. Mornings feel fresh beside the water, while the afternoons drift between sea and pool. As the evening settles in, the mood softens and gradually becomes more social, drawing people together.
