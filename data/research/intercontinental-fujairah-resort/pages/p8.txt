# InterContinental Fujairah ResortDestination

Cradled between the magnificent Hajjar Mountains and exquisite Aqah Beach, InterContinental® Fujairah Resort enjoys breathtaking panoramas of the turquoise Indian Ocean. This enthralling paradise is located a mere 45 km from Fujairah, or 165 km from Dubai

### Where the Hajar Mountains meet the Indian Ocean

Set along Al Aqah Beach with the Hajar Mountains behind it, InterContinental Fujairah Resort overlooks the Indian Ocean and is around 90 minutes away from Dubai. Peaceful yet well-connected, our resort makes it easy to enjoy the coast at your own pace.

### Explore the Many Sides of Fujairah

Beyond the resort, Fujairah invites you to explore coral reefs, mountain trails, historic forts, and coastal villages. It is a destination where every outing feels different, making it one of the most rewarding places to experience a memorable stay.

### The Al Aqah Beach

On a secluded stretch of Al Aqah Beach, the shoreline opens wide to the Indian Ocean, with the Hajar Mountains standing just beyond. Sunlight moves across the water, and the sense of space gives this Fujairah beach resort a rare and natural openness.

## Snoopy Island

Just offshore from Fujairah’s coastline, Snoopy Island is a haven for snorkelling, kayaking and swimming. Its crystal-clear waters and vibrant coral reefs are home to colourful marine life, including rays and sea turtles.

## Fujairah Fort

Dating back to 1670, Fujairah Fort is the oldest fort in the UAE and a symbol of the emirate’s rich heritage. Explore its restored towers and nearby heritage village for a glimpse into traditional Emirati life.

## Al-Bidyah Mosque

The UAE’s oldest mosque, Al-Bidyah Mosque offers a fascinating insight into the region’s history and architecture. Its distinctive design and centuries-old origins make it one of Fujairah’s most treasured landmarks.

## Fujairah Museum

Discover the story of Fujairah through a collection of archaeological finds, ancient artefacts and cultural exhibits. The museum offers a captivating journey through the emirate’s rich history and heritage.
