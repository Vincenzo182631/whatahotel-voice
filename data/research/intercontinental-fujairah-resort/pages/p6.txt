## FAQs for InterContinental Fujairah Resort

Does InterContinental Fujairah Resort have a pool?

InterContinental Fujairah Resort has 4 outdoor pool.

Infinity Pool

Take in uninterrupted views of the Arabian Sea from our temperature-controlled Infinity Pool, where refreshing waters meet the serene beauty of Fujairah’s coastline and Hajar Mountains.

Family Pool

A welcoming space for families to unwind, our temperature-controlled Family Pool offers a relaxed setting surrounded by the natural beauty of Fujairah’s coastline and Hajar Mountains.

Kids Pool & Splash Pad

Designed for little ones to play and explore, our temperature-controlled Kids Pool and Splash Pad offer a fun-filled space for refreshing moments and family time.

Club InterContinental Pool

Exclusively for Club InterContinental guests, the temperature-controlled Club Pool offers a tranquil setting to unwind while taking in the serene coastal surroundings.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Fujairah Resort?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Fujairah Resort for hotel guests.

Valet parking is offered free of charge.

Vehicles allowed: Bus, Rv, Trailer, Truck

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Fujairah Resort?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Fujairah Resort.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Fujairah Resort for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Fujairah Resort have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Fujairah Resort has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Fujairah Resort?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Fujairah Resort.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Fujairah Resort?

Check-in at InterContinental Fujairah Resort is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Fujairah Resort offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Fujairah Resort offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Fujairah Resort from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Fujairah Resort is conveniently located near Dubai International Airport (DXB), approximately 93.83 miles away (151 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Fujairah Resort?

InterContinental Fujairah Resort is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Snoopy Island - 4.04 miles away (6.5 kilometers)
- Al Bidya Mosque - 5.16 miles away (8.3 kilometers)
- Fujairah Fort - 29.52 miles away (47.5 kilometers)
- Fujairah Museum - 29.83 miles away (48 kilometers)
- Wadi Wurayah National Park - 20.51 miles away (33 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Fujairah Resort puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
