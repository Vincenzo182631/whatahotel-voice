# A Boutique Townhouse

Discover the enchanting Egerton House Hotel this summer, a boutique townhouse nestled in the heart of Knightsbridge, within the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea. As a proud part of The Red Carnation Hotel Collection, this elegant retreat invites you to immerse yourself in its unique character and beautifully traditional style. Savour a delicious breakfast, acclaimed as the best in the UK, delight in our quintessentially British afternoon tea, or indulge in one of London’s most famous martinis.
