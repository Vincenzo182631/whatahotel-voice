# Dining & drinks

The Egerton House Hotel, and our local Knightsbridge area, is a five-star gourmet's paradise. In our beautiful Drawing Room, discover a carefully curated menu of comforting dishes and signature classics; in our Dining Room, indulge in our exceptional breakfast, recently voted the UK's best. Enjoy a deliciously indulgent traditional afternoon tea, or settle into our bar and sip on one of London’s most famous martinis.

### The Bar

Relax in our elegant bar and sample a choice of expertly-mixed cocktails accompanied by homemade bites, including our signature Martini - known as one of London's best.
