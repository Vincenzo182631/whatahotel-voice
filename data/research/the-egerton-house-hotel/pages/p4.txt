# V&A Museum Privileges

Just moments from Victoria and Albert Museum, The Egerton House Hotel offers guests rare and privileged access to one of the world’s leading cultural institutions. Just a short walk from the hotel, the V&A is moments away, allowing you to arrive early without the need for public transport and ahead of the crowds.

We have a long-standing partnership with the Victoria & Albert Museum, the world’s greatest museum of art and design, with collections unrivalled in their scope and diversity. Through this exclusive relationship, we offer complimentary tickets to the V&A’s feature exhibitions, available exclusively for our guests.

Enquire

### VIP Access, Reserved for Our Guests

Each day, a limited number of VIP access cards are available at reception, offering a truly elevated museum experience.

With a VIP card, guests enjoy:

- Entry to all temporary exhibitions, including those that are sold out

- Access to the V&A Members’ Lounge, a calm and elegant space to relax

- Priority entry via the members’ queue

- A 10% discount in the V&A shop

This is an opportunity to experience the museum beyond the public realm, with access typically reserved for members. Our team will be delighted to assist with arranging your access on the day.

### A Neighbourly Partnership

Just a short stroll away, the Victoria and Albert Museum opens the door to a world of creativity, from iconic fashion exhibitions and landmark design showcases to photography, sculpture and immersive cultural retrospectives. With a dynamic programme of ever-evolving exhibitions, no two visits are ever quite the same.

From headline exhibitions that draw global attention to more intimate, thought-provoking displays, the V&A offers a journey through art and design in all its forms. With priority access and the ability to experience even sold-out exhibitions, guests of The Egerton are invited to explore at their own pace, without the usual constraints.

After your visit, return to the intimacy and elegance of The Egerton, perfectly placed for a day of art, design and discovery. And, of course, a perfectly crafted martini.

### A World of Art, Fashion and Design

From the theatrical elegance of Schiaparelli to groundbreaking contemporary design, the Victoria and Albert Museum presents a constantly evolving programme of world-class exhibitions.

Discover iconic fashion houses, celebrated designers and cultural movements through immersive displays that span centuries and continents. From couture and costume to photography, interiors and innovation, each exhibition offers a new perspective on creativity and craftsmanship.

Whether you are drawn to headline exhibitions that define the cultural calendar or quieter galleries rich in detail and discovery, the V&A invites you to explore at your own pace, returning time and again to uncover something new.

### A Rare Privilege in Knightsbridge

Access to VIP cards is strictly limited and offered on a first come, first served basis each day. Guests are invited to collect their pass from reception during museum opening hours and return it following their visit.

Discover our idyllic rooms and beautiful suites whilst we curate you a visit to the V&A like no other. Our best suite, aptly named the V&A Suite, celebrates our treasured partnership with the Victoria and Albert Museum.
