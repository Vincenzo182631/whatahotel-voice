## Do you also want up to 15% discount on your stays?

Then join our **STAY**& **SAVE** Reward Program. As a member, you’ll get free access to a wide range of advantages.

Among other things, you’ll enjoy exclusive accommodation offers, including **up to 15% off** your stays – with or without breakfast.

You’ll also earn points for each stay, which can be redeemed for free nights. When you book directly on our websites, you’ll already have enough points for a free night after just 5 nights.

## Get access to these benefits:

- As a member, you're guaranteed exclusive offers, including up to 15% off accommodation – with or without breakfast
- Welcome bonus of 500 points upon registration
- 500 points per night that you can use for free nights
- Double points when booking through our own websites (2x 500 points per night)
- A free night after just 5 stays when booking directly on our websites
- Receive news and exclusive member offers directly in your inbox
- 10% discount at the restaurant Tivoli Brasserie at Tivoli Hotel & Congress Center

### STAY&SAVE includes the following hotels:

Phoenix Copenhagen, 71 Nyhavn Hotel, Copenhagen Strand, Imperial Hotel, The Square, Tivoli Hotel & Congress Center, Copenhagen Island, and Gentofte Hotel. Our hotels are all centrally located in the heart of Copenhagen.

## How free nights work

Every time you stay overnight at one of our hotels, you earn points that can be used for free nights. When you book your stays directly on our websites, you will already have earned enough points for a free night after just five nights!

We will always try to accommodate your wishes when you book with points, but reservations are subject to sold-out dates and general high demand.

Prices for free nights for one or two people, including breakfast:

- Standard room: 5.000 points
- Superior room: 10.000 points
- Executive room: 15.000 points

Earned points **cannot** be used to upgrade room categories. It is not possible to redeem **fewer** than 5.000 points.
