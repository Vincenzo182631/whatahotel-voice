# Rooms and suites

If our walls could talk, they would tell you stories about hard working seamen, clattering coins, and sacks of exotic spices. Even though the walls of our old warehouse cannot put the life of Nyhavn through two centuries in words, you still get an explicit feeling of the presence of history. As a guest at 71 Nyhavn Hotel, you can look forward to an exclusive hotel stay and wake up beneath grained beams of Pomeranian pinewood that runs, side by side, in our preserved buildings. They accommodate 130 Executive rooms and suites, placed in The Yellow Warehouse and The Red Warehouse. No matter what you choose, you get a breakfast included with your stay.

Our Suite Deluxe View of more than 50 square meters is the ideal choice for you who want a ’crème de la crème’ experience during your stay in Copenhagen. We walk you to your suite and private entrance that give you access to two separate rooms, which both are furnished with a double bed from the luxury brand Carpe Diem.

When you wake up beneath the exposed beams of Pomeranian pinewood, you can start the day with an included buffet and morning stories in our breakfast restaurant. The French glass doors in the suite invites plenty inflows of light inside and offer you a view of the vivid harbour life and The Inner Harbour Bridge that connects the city area with Christianshavn.

The suite also offers free minibar, exclusive bath products, and freshly brewed coffee and tea from the Nespresso machine.

Suite Deluxe View: Over 50 sqm and two double beds of 180 cm.

Our bright Junior Suites are the ideal choice for those looking for a luxurious stay in the heart of Copenhagen.

Each suite is individually designed across two levels, connected by a spiral staircase, with a separate sleeping area and a lounge area with a sofa, desk, and minibar located on the opposite level.

The bathrooms in these suites are all equipped with a bathtub and located on the lower level.

The location of the sleeping and lounge areas varies, and the bed may be placed either on the same floor as the bathroom or on the upper level. This cannot be guaranteed when booking.

As a guest staying in a Junior Suite, breakfast buffet and complimentary coffee from the Nespresso machine are included. You can also look forward to a luxurious Carpe Diem bed, bathrobe, slippers, and exclusive bathroom amenities.

Junior Suite: 24 sqm and a 160 cm double bed

**Good to know:** _The Junior Suites are arranged across two levels. The bathroom is always located on the lower level and may therefore be separated from the sleeping area by a spiral staircase._
