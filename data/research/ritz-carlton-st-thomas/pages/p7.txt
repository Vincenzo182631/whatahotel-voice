Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, St. Thomas
The Art of the Caribbean Holiday
The Caribbean has a way of making every celebration feel entirely its own, warm breezes, golden light, and days made for savoring the season. Our Festive Experience Guide brings that spirit to life across every corner of the resort, offering a collection of experiences designed to help you slow down, reconnect, and rediscover the simple pleasure of time well spent. Wander into wellness with sunrise yoga on the sand, a guided reef snorkel, or a spa ritual crafted just for you. Gather the family for a treasure hunt across the property, an afternoon of gingerbread house building, or a spirited game of beach pickleball. As the sun sets, toast to the season at a sunset bar, savor a holiday inspired dinner, or dance beneath the stars to island rhythms. However you choose to spend your days, this guide is your invitation to a holiday defined by warmth, discovery, and moments shared with those who matter most, all set against the endless blue of Great Bay.
New Year's Eve Countdown Party
Ring in the new year with a Countdown Party where island energy meets timeless celebration. Dance beneath the stars to vibrant live music, feel the anticipation build with electric entertainment, and savor a curated spread of festive bites and elevated fare, each dish crafted to delight through the final moments of the year. From the first note to the final countdown, every beat, bite, and toast builds toward a shared moment of pure celebration and joy that carries into midnight. Surrounded by the beauty of the island, gather with those who matter most and welcome in the new year together, a night beautifully set not only with music and movement, but with joy, connection, gratitude, and the promise of all that lies ahead in the upcoming year.

Festive Experiences
Calypso Sunset Bar
Unwind at the Calypso Sunset Bar, where crafted beverages meet the golden glow of evening over Great Bay. Feel the warm breeze drift in as the sky shifts into shades of amber and rose, calypso rhythms setting a gentle soundtrack to the moment. A polished escape to close the day, savored one sip at a time. Available 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM, December 23 - Decmber 30.
Reef Discovery: A Guided Snorkel
Dive into the art of ocean stewardship with an immersive snorkel experience rooted in education and discovery. Learn about our Blue Flag Certification and its meaning for local waters, along with insights into reef health, marine awareness and ocean protection. After a safety review, guests swim together, exploring the natural wildlife just beneath the surface, a rare glimpse into the world we work to protect.
Gingerbread House Workshop
Head to Ritz Kids for a festive gingerbread house workshop the whole family can enjoy. Guests are invited to build and decorate their own gingerbread house with a generous array of sprinkles, icing, candy and more, creating a joyful hands-on memory to cherish together.
A Sweet Harvest Dessert Buffet
Cap off your Thanksgiving dinner with a dessert buffet as rich in tradition as it is in flavor. Alongside our evening's Thanksgiving specials, indulge in a curated spread of classic and island inspired sweets, each bite a fitting close to a day of gratitude and gathering.
Tinsel and Palm Bar
Step into the living room courtyard, aglow with festive lights and holiday charm, for a curated selection of beverages and light bites. A polished escape tucked within the resort, perfect for a toast, a bite, or simply a moment to soak in the season's warmth. Available 3:00 PM - 10:00 PM, December 23 - December 30.
Christmas Day Luncheon Buffet
Gather for a Christmas Day luncheon where timeless tradition meets island inspiration. Savor the rich aroma of roasted turkey and warm spiced stuffing alongside vibrant Caribbean favorites, each dish layered with local flavor and holiday spice. Enveloped by the island's beauty, gather with those who matter most, a table set with abundance, warmth, connection, and the lasting joy of the season shared together.
Additional Resort Offerings
The Ultimate Accommodation
Elevate your holiday stay in our Three-Bedroom Presidential Suite, ideal for families and groups. Reserve it on its own or with a connecting Oceanfront King Suite and Oceanfront Double for up to 8 guests with Club Level benefits. Enjoy three bedrooms, five bathrooms, a spacious living room terrace, and three oceanfront balconies—perfect for gathering and celebrating the season.
