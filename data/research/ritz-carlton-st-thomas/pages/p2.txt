Two-Bedroom Suite
Elevate your stay in our Two-Bedroom Club Ocean View Suite -1,539 sq. ft. of refined comfort with stunning ocean views, two elegant bedrooms, three full bathrooms, a plush pull-out sofa, and spacious living/dining areas. Ideal for up to seven guests, perfect for family escapes or group getaways.

Club Oceanfront Suite
The Club Oceanfront Suites offer luxurious accommodations for up to five guests, featuring expansive private balconies with panoramic views of the Caribbean Sea, elegant living spaces, marble bathrooms, and plush bedding. Guests enjoy exclusive access to the Club Lounge, where personalized service, gourmet culinary presentations, and a serene atmosphere create the perfect island retreat.

Food Offerings and Personalized Services
The Club lounge provides four food and beverage offerings each day including breakfast, light lunch, heavy hors d’oeuvres at dinner, and a dessert buffet at night. The Club Lounge offers personalized packaged items for each guest. Breakfast omelet stations and breakfast meats are set up behind the station and served by our esteemed chef. The lounge has been re-configured to allow adequate spacing between tables and seating areas. Games and other common-use items are available upon request.
