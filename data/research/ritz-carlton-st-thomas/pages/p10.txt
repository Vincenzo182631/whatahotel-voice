Rooms

After a remodel and reopening in 2019, the Ritz-Carlton’s St. Thomas property still focuses on understated elegance. Its 180 rooms and suites have been designed in neutral colors and delicate fabrics, leaving the focus on the expansive white, sandy beaches just outside the window. Activity highlights include leisurely day or night cruise on the resort’s private, 53-foot catamaran, the Lady Lyndsey II, dining with views of the crystal blue waters, a spa session at the new seaside spa cabana, or learning how to make art out of shells and sand personalized custom souvenirs.
