Elevate Your Stay
The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with the personalized service and attention to detail for which The Ritz-Carlton is known. As a premier St. Thomas hotel with a club lounge, it offers a dedicated Concierge, private Club Lounge, exclusive in-room amenities, and unique destination-inspired experiences. Often described as a “hotel within a hotel,” the Club enhances every aspect of your stay with elevated luxury and convenience.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.
