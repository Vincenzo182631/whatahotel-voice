Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, St. Thomas
The ultimate gateway to the Caribbean
Escape to an island oasis surrounded by crystal blue waters, white sand, and lush tropical greenery at our St. Thomas beachfront hotel. A breathtaking 30-acre, oceanfront luxury resort, The Ritz-Carlton, St. Thomas is a premier hotel on St. Thomas VI, offering an unforgettable Caribbean escape. This luxury hotel on St. Thomas is a marvel of traditional island architecture, thoughtfully updated with all the contemporary comforts and exceptional service for which The Ritz-Carlton is renowned. Experience extended-stay accommodations, stunning ocean views, and world-class amenities in one of the Caribbean’s most celebrated beachfront destinations.

Community Footprint
Rooted in stewardship, education, and shared success, our Community Footprint reflects our commitment to caring for both the natural beauty of St. Thomas and the people who call it home. Through responsible environmental practices, meaningful partnerships with local schools, and the sharing of our service expertise with island businesses, we strive to create a positive, lasting impact. Every initiative is guided by our belief that luxury hospitality should uplift communities, protect our environment, and contribute to a thriving, sustainable future for generations to come.
Blue Flag
Great Bay Beach holds the Blue Flag Certification, reflecting our commitment to meet top standards in water quality, safety, and environmental management. Guests can enjoy a beautifully maintained shoreline supported by responsible operations, environmental education, and strong protection of local wildlife. Great Bay Beach it’s a model of sustainability and care for our island’s future.
Supporting Local Talent Through Education
The Ritz-Carlton partners with Ivanna Eudora Kean High School (IEKHS) through its Internship and On-the-Job Training Program, welcoming students to gain firsthand experience in luxury hospitality—from culinary and guest services to operations and administrative roles. We are honored to contribute to the growth of St. Thomas’s future workforce and strengthen our connection to the community we call home.
Crafting Excellence Across Our Community
At the heart of exceptional hospitality lies exceptional service. Each quarter, we proudly extend our Legendary Service Training to local businesses, helping elevate the guest experience across St. Thomas with the same standards that define our brand. By sharing our service philosophy with local partners, we help foster a culture of excellence that benefits residents, visitors, and businesses island-wide.
