Let Ocean-Inspired Relaxation of Our Spa Resort in St. Thomas Envelop You
Spa
Discover a gentle place at The Ritz-Carlton Spa, St. Thomas, set against the turquoise waters of the Caribbean. From a rejuvenating massage on St. Thomas to one of the Virgin Islands’ most diverse selections of therapies and spa treatments, our seaside enclave offers ocean-inspired relaxation and island indulgence. Let us calm your senses and rejuvenate your body in an authentic tropical island sanctuary at our St. Thomas spa.
Find Serenity in St. Thomas
Spa Appointments
Let us calm your senses and rejuvenate your body in an authentic tropical island sanctuary. For information or to reserve a treatment, please contact the spa at (340) 775-3333.
Hours of Operation:
Spa Treatments and Spa Facilities: 10:00a.m-5:00p.m
Book Spa Appointment
Island Indulgence Massage - 80 Min
As you embark on your sensory island journey and indulge in a variety of massage techniques inspired by tropical scented oils and hydrating tropical enhanced sugar scrubs, discover a unique experience sure to relieve stress, induce relaxation and leave you feeling rejuvenated. Includes full body tropical-inspired aromatic massage, exfoliation and mask for the feet, warm coconut oil hair and scalp treatment.
Seaside Cabana Massage - 80 Min
Experience a full body massage while you indulge in the tranquility of the waves and gentle breezes allowing you to relax and unwind at the water’s edge. Can also be shared with someone special as a couples massage.
The Platinum HydraFacial
Indulge in the ultimate rejuvenation with our Platinum HydraFacial experience. Your journey begins with soothing lymphatic drainage to detoxify and prepare your skin. Next, the Signature HydraFacial deeply cleanses, exfoliates, extracts, and hydrates while targeting your unique skin concerns with a customized booster of your choice.
Spa and Fitness Center
Discover Tranquility
Daily fitness classes in St. Thomas include Cardio Blast, Water Tai Chi, and property walks from 7-9 AM. Or, ask an attendant about the use of tennis courts, including rackets and balls.

Activities & Experiences
The Lady Lynsey II
Lady Lynsey II, a world-class catamaran, continues the legacy of the original Lady Lynsey with top-tier style and service. Each excursion features gourmet food and drinks for a luxurious experience.
Fitness Classes
Choose from a variety of fitness classes that will challenge your mind & body. All levels are welcomed.
Beach and Pool
Poolside Cabana Rentals
Relax and reservelyour private cabana at the adult pool or family pool, available from 10:00 am to 5:00 pm. A personal attendant will assist with any food and beverage requests during your pool day.
Pool & Beach Day Pass
Enjoy a Pool & Beach Day Pass at The Ritz-Carlton, St. Thomas with pools, splash pad, beach access, towel service, food, Wi-Fi and more. Valet $35. Kids under 12 need an adult. Fees apply for extras.
Children's Activities
Ritz Kids
Ritz Kids engages your children to interact with their surroundings in a new & exciting way through our pillars of Ritz Kids discovery: Water, Land, Environmental Responsibility and Culture.
Ritz-Kids Summer Camp - Single Day
Designed for children ages 5 to 15, our immersive summer program offers a unique blend of fun, learning, and exploration in a supportive and engaging environment
In-Room Amenities
In-Room Amenities
Indulge in Ritz-Carlton St. Thomas in-room amenities: local rum, craft beer, gourmet cakes, cookies, charcuterie, tropical fruits, and premium wines—perfect for celebrating or relaxing in style.
Sports & Recreation
Topgolf Swing Suite
Located in the Grand Palazzo, Topgolf Swing Suite features a simulator bay and virtual games like baseball, football, hockey and soccer—great for parties, meetings, team events or practice.
