Relaxed, Island-Inspired Dining
Dining
On the island of St. Thomas, food is fresh and from the land, and warm, welcoming service reflects the Caribbean culture. Restaurants at this beachfront resort embrace these local elements, offering diners the opportunity to enjoy creative cuisine in beautiful settings.
Savor Your Stay in the Virgin Islands
Exclusive culinary experiences await with customized menus and live entertainment on the shores of Great Bay. Embark on a journey through the Caribbean and savor the flavors of the land and sea each week through our tailored activities.
