### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square?

The check-in time at JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square?

On-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square. The daily valet fee is $84.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square?

The closest airport near JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square is San Francisco International Airport (SFO). SFO is located approximately 14.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
