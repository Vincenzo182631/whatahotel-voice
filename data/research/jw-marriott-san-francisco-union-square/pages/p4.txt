## Ghiradelli Square

Discover a historic landmark and a popular shopping and dining destination located in close proximity to Fisherman's Wharf.

## San Francisco China Town

Thriving, vibrant and historic; San Francisco's China Town is the largest Chinatown outside of Asia.

## Fisherman's Wharf

Located on the northern waterfront, Fisherman's Wharf is a popular tourist destination known for its scenic views of the San Francisco Bay and its historic waterfront, which is home to many restaurants, shops, and attractions.

### Art

San Francisco is a city of artists. Creators of all background are here turning each street into unique works of art. From the actual Art Museums to the architecture, creators of products, food and even tech. Just walk the streets to feel the creativity flow.

### Sports

Home to some of the biggest names in sports, the Golden State Warriors and The San Francisco Giants present opportunities for exhilaration that the whole family will enjoy.

### Daycations

Spend the day soaking in the eclectic charm of San Francisco classics. Take a stroll across The Golden Gate Bridge, on the trails through Salesforce Park or through Union Square. Then grab your wallet to browse the shops at Metreon.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square offers a fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio, weights, core balls, bikes, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square?

California Academy of Sciences, Oracle Park - home of MLB San Francisco Giants, Alcatraz Island, Cable Cars/Powell Street, Chinatown and more are nearby JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square.
