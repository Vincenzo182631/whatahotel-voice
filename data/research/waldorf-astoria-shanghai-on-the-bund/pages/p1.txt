## Shanghai treasure on the historic Bund

Once the renowned Shanghai Club, our hotel retains much of its heritage design and combines modern comfort with views of the Huangpu River. We're one block from the iconic Bund, featuring waterside strolls and shopping. Unwind at our luxury spa, and enjoy personal concierge services, a fitness center, and several acclaimed restaurants.

## Rooms & Suites

Experience breathtaking panoramic views of the city skyline right from the comfort of your room or suite. Enjoy plush bedding, spacious bathrooms, and exclusive amenities.

Our spa provides a serene sanctuary that combines traditional Chinese practices with modern techniques to soothe jet lag.

## Our Historic Location

As the first Waldorf Astoria hotel to open in Asia, our hotel is located on Shanghai’s iconic Bund, showcasing our unique charm and allure.

The Heritage Building is a landmark structure constructed in 1911. Once the legendary Shanghai Club, this architectural gem has been lovingly restored using archival photographs and historical records.
