You’ve Arrived Somewhere Special.

Unique accommodations to meet your needs – rooms, suites, and Residences. Surrounded by the river, salt marshes, and the sea. Elegant. And playful. Welcome to The Cloister.

## Main Building

Finely detailed, spacious, welcoming. Each suite has its own charm. All are impeccably designed, many with unique architectural features such as vaulted ceilings, exposed wood beams, and handcrafted Oriental rugs.

When you stay here, you’ll find yourself riverside, near The Spa at Sea Island and Fitness Center, Coastal Experience Center, and Tennis Center. The Sea Island Beach Club and our gorgeous five-mile stretch of private beach are right across the street.

View Suites

## Main Building Wings

The Garden Wing is bright and fresh, with many rooms overlooking our beautiful grounds. Steps away from the Main Building, a stay in the Garden Wing is like its own secluded retreat. Meander outdoor corridors, past koi ponds, through lush, captivating gardens, and manicured grounds to a light and airy room to call your own.

The North and South Wings, adjacent to the Main Building, are spacious and elegant. Located along the river, it’s hard to imagine a better sunset view on Sea Island, right from your very own private patio or balcony. Rooms in the North and South Wings offer the same fine Cloister details, charm, and elegance as the Main Building.

View Rooms and Suites

## Beach Club

Signature Spanish Mediterranean décor, including Oriental rugs, exposed beam ceilings, many rooms with expansive ocean views. Rooms and suites feature down bedding, custom linens, and Molton Brown products. Suites include compact, open kitchens with a comfortable living and dining area.

At the Beach Club, you’re steps away from our five miles of private beach, three swimming pools, and many water sports and activities.

View Rooms and Suites

## Ocean Residences

Spacious and inviting rooms and suites featuring our signature Spanish Mediterranean décor and varying views of the Atlantic Ocean, golden marshes, tidal river, and sand dunes. Suites include full, open kitchens with a washer and dryer, as well as a large living and dining area.

Ocean Residences give you the ultimate seclusion in a private, gated enclave with its own oceanfront infinity pool. Yet just a few minutes walk from the Beach Club and The Cloister Main Building.

## Full of Charm & Comfort

With Forbes Five-Star accommodations tucked away on a private island, The Cloister is legendary, elegant, fun. And spacious, inside and out.

View Cloister Rooms

### The Cloister at Sea Island

Comprising of rooms in the Main Building, The Cloister Wings, and Beach Club, The Cloister offers vibrant rooms and suites on the lush Georgian coast. Here you’re surrounded by activity, and close to wherever your day will take you.

Explore The Cloister

### Tavola

An authentic Italian experience with Southern charm, where delicious family-style meals are enjoyed around a lively table. Homemade ravioli, fresh seafood, salads, pizza fresh from the Mugnaini wood oven, a well-stocked bar with wines from all 20 Italian wine regions, and classic cocktails reinvented to reflect the country. From the river side of the Colonial Lounge in The Cloister, take a right down the corridor and Tavola is on your left.

Explore Tavola Reserve

### Beach Club

Rooms and suites adjacent to and above the Sea Island Beach Club, with its three pools, water sports, activities, and Camp Cloister.

Explore The Cloister

### The Cloister Ocean Residences

With one, two, or three bedrooms and an infinity-edge pool, The Cloister Ocean Residences comprise a private enclave within a second gate. With ocean and nature views.

### River Bar

Fun and familiar, but served with with French flair or a Southern twist. Perfect for lunch, warm and friendly for dinner, ideal for drinks. Part of everyone’s visit to The Cloister. Make your way down the riverside hall of The Cloister.

Explore River Bar

### Fitness Center

Located at Sea Island’s Forbes Five-Star Spa, a short walk straight down the lush path from The Cloister main entrance (on your way to the Beach Club). Join us for a fitness class, personal training session, or a workout with state-of-the-art equipment in yoga, Pilates, strength, cycle, and cardio studios. Also features indoor heated lap pool, squash courts, steam and sauna, whirlpool, and cryotherapy.

Explore Fitness

### The Spa at Sea Island

Our Forbes Five-Star spa features a water-inspired wellness journey complete with a variety of massage services, body treatments, and experiences including hydrotherapy, cold plunges, and hot pools. Also enjoy the services of our Salon with hair, makeup, manicures, and pedicures, as well as a Fitness Center with state-of-the-art weight and cardio equipment, studios, shop, and indoor lap pool. Take a left off the walkway that leads from The Cloister to the Beach Club.

Explore The Spa

### Sea Strike & Pub

Beautiful bar, punch-bowl cocktails, creative gastropub-style menu, and a lot of fun in a warm atmosphere. A favorite Sea Island activity, enjoy a few strings of bowling catch the big game, or just settle into a comfy couch with a snack. Located right at the entrance of the Beach Club, straight up the lush walkway from The Cloister.

Explore Sea Strike & Pub

### Southern Tide

Thanks to stunning al fresco dining, Southern Tide opens up to the most beautiful thing we have to offer—the ocean. It’s oceanfront dining and fresh seafood make it a Sea Island beloved favorite. Perfect for a family catch-up, romantic night out, or simply an incredible culinary experience. Go down the hall on your left as you enter the Beach Club, mid-way down on your right.

Explore Southern Tide Reserve

### The Lodge at Sea Island

Forbes Five-Star boutique hotel in a secluded private setting. The Lodge is welcoming, comfortable, and the hub of its own Sea Island world, with championship golf right next door. Make your way to the stunning Avenue of the Oaks, Retreat Avenue, on St Simons Island, off the roundabout at Frederica Road and Kings Way.

Explore The Lodge

### The Inn by Sea Island

A casual comfortable offering on St. Simons Island with 85 rooms, a vibrant outdoor area with heated pool, continental breakfast, complimentary bicycles, and a Topgolf Swing Suite. Located on Sea Island Road, a 10-minute drive from the Sea Island Golf Performance Center at The Lodge, The Inn is the perfect base to explore St. Simons Island and Georgia’s beautiful Golden Isles.

### Sea Island Cottages

A home to call your own. Sea Island Cottages are self-contained and fully equipped. With the ocean at your feet and groceries delivered to your door. Renting a cottage is a treasured tradition for Sea Island families. Why not make it yours?
