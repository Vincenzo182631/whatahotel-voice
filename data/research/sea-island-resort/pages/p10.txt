# Recreation & Activities

From fishing, tennis, and sailing to biking, kayaking, horseback riding, and more. Explore our shores. Meander along our rivers. Glide through salt marshes. Count sea turtle nests, go paddleboarding, or enjoy the thrill of having a Harris hawk swoop down and perch on your outstretched arm. Discover stories worth telling long after the moment’s gone.

Coastal Experience Center Inshore Fishing Trips

Cruise through the marshes in search of trout, redfish, flounder, and whiting. Fly fish from Sea Island’s shallow water flats boat in search of the tailing redfish, or travel a creek for a big school of speckled trout. For one to four people.
