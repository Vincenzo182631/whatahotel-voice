# Broadfield, A Sea Island Sporting Club and Lodge

The great outdoors, indeed.

Authentic, traditional. And completely enchanting.

Surrounded by expansive live oaks, fanning palmettos, towering pines. And quail, pheasant, and a lake full of largemouth bass. The landscape blissfully quiet, the land beautifully serene. For hunting, yes. But this is the ultimate place to get away from it all. Find outdoor adventure, solitude, a warm fire, a welcoming porch, and even more welcoming hospitality. This is Broadfield, Sea Island’s 5,800-acre Sporting Club and Lodge.

## Broadfield

A hunting lodge that is both elegant and comfortable. The natural elements of the Lodge’s décor mimic its surroundings—antlers, warm wood-paneled rooms, and the gorgeous erratic details of the pecky cypress ceilings. Accommodations at Broadfield are unique, intimate, and flexible.

Reserved as a compound for the most idyllic secluded getaway, with a variety of accommodations arrangements including our stunning Lodge, quaint Guest House, Guest Cabin, and charming Pool House. Sleeps up to 18 people with double occupancy.

## Main Lodge

Comfortable accommodations with delightfully rustic details that only add to Broadfield’s charm. Two bedrooms (1 king, 2 queens), each with their own full private bathroom.

## Master Suite

Located on its own, secluded from the Main Lodge, the intimate master suite features one king bed and plenty of warm, natural surroundings.

## Guest House

Attached to Main Lodge by a covered breezeway, the Guest House offers three comfortable bedrooms with two queens each.

## Guest Cabin

Cozy and quaint, the Guest Cabin sleeps four in two bedrooms, with four twins and three bathrooms.

## Pool House

Charming additional lodging, the Pool house is perched near the edge of the outdoor pool, offering one bedroom with two singles.

## A uniquely traditional experience

Peaceful charm set among thousands of pristine acres, Broadfield is the ultimate Southern sporting club. Where you’re greeted by a welcoming porch and a few friendly pups. Hunting one minute, and savoring a Low Country Boil the next.

### Broadfield

Located off of the main Sea Island property less than an hour drive south near Woodbine is Broadfield, A Sea Island Sporting Club and Lodge. Reserved as a compound for idyllic secluded getaways for up to 18 people, its natural setting make it perfect for hunting, shooting, falconry, and fishing, as well as farm-to-table meals from Broadfield’s own chef.

## Homegrown

Broadfield’s own chef delights and satisfies guests with meals prepared with farm-to-table herbs and vegetables, meats cured in the smokehouse, and fresh local seafood.

## Membership

Enjoy access to all Broadfield has to offer, including trophy-managed deer hunts, spring turkey hunting, wild boar hunts, dove shooting, unlimited rifle and pistol range use, specially priced quail and pheasant shooting, sporting clays, and invitations to annual member events. Limited availability. Contact Lee Barber at leebarber@seaisland.com

## Corporate Events and Groups

Looking for an event for an outdoor-savvy group? From small continental pheasant shoots to round robin activities—pheasant shooting, quail hunting, 5-Stand, freshwater fishing, falconry, and rifle/pistol shooting— Broadfield is your destination. Enjoy oyster roasts, “pig pickin’s,” Low Country Boils, and more as well as large lawn areas for concert space.
