# The Lodge

Small and intimate. Big on coastal Southern charm.

A distinctive, top-rated resort. A warm, boutique hotel. At The Lodge we live beautifully in between seemingly opposite worlds. Grand, from the moment you follow the stunning Avenue of Oaks. Romantic, secluded away from it all. Familiar, as soon as you step in the front door. The Lodge has a way of welcoming you home. Even if it’s your first time.

## Rooms & Suites

Private, secluded, surrounded by a lush natural setting. With only 43 rooms, it’s equal parts intimate hideaway and Forbes Five-Star hotel. Rich wood. Exposed beams. A view of the sea from your balcony or one of two championship golf courses within arm’s reach. With 24-hour personal butler service, even a luxurious rose-petal bath, drawn just to your liking.

View Rooms & Suites

## Lodge Cottages

A short stroll along the path from the English-style manor of The Lodge brings you to seven beautiful cottages. Relax on your own welcoming front porch, step into an inviting living room with white-paneled walls, warm wood beams, and a cozy fireplace. When it’s time to call it a day, in the bedrooms, you’ll find elegant furnishings, luxurious bedding, and paintings by local artists.

View Lodge Cottages

## King Cottage

Named for the King family that owned the land where the cottage is located, the King Cottage is a space truly fit for royalty. It comes with its own hitting bay, four bedrooms and baths, a large great room and dining space, kitchen, and porch overlooking the driving range. Wide plank hardwood floors, beams, iron lighting and fireplaces create a comfortable atmosphere. And with 4,200 square feet overlooking the Atlantic, it’s also an idyllic retreat.

View King Cottage

## The Lodge Residences

A winding path under live oaks leads from The Lodge to the newest accommodation at Sea Island – The Lodge Residences. Located adjacent to the Sea Island Golf Performance Center, these new Residences are the perfect destination for your family, group golf trip, or quiet retreat. Overlooking the practice greens, marsh, and St. Simons Sound beyond, and offering a priceless view of the sunset each evening.

View The Lodge Residences

## Luxury at Every Turn.

Complimentary BMW during your stay.

Take a spin on Sea Island and St. Simons Island in the unparalleled luxury of a BMW. We have a map—if you need one. But we have a feeling you’ll want to take the long way.

Pre-register for your BMW test drive, then visit the Bell Stand at The Cloister or the Concierge Desk at The Lodge to pick up your keys during your stay. Cars are available on a first-come, first-served basis for up to two hours of driving.
