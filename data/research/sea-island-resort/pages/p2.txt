#### St. Simons Tree Spirits

Meet Blackbeard, the newest tree spirit at The Inn by Sea Island on St. Simons Island! Legend has it that Blackbeard roamed the Georgia coast in the early 1700s—and that his buried treasure still exists, waiting to be found.

In the 1980s, a local artist decided to make their mark on St. Simons Island, carving about 20 faces into the island’s famous oak trees. Each unique face is hand-carved, taking the artist between two and four days to complete.

Begin your tree spirit adventure at www.goldenisles.com!

## St. Simons Island and Surrounding Area

Perfectly located between the Jacksonville and Savannah international airports, and an easy drive from Atlanta and many other cities, The Inn is close to everything St. Simons Island has to offer—shopping, restaurants, attractions, historic sites, beaches, parks, and more.

Explore St. Simons Island

### The Inn by Sea Island

A casual comfortable offering on St. Simons Island with 85 rooms, a vibrant outdoor area with heated pool, continental breakfast, complimentary bicycles, and a Topgolf Swing Suite. Located on Sea Island Road, a 10-minute drive from the Sea Island Golf Performance Center at The Lodge, The Inn is the perfect base to explore St. Simons Island and Georgia’s beautiful Golden Isles.

### Seaside Golf Course

Enjoy the beauty and tradition of legendary Scottish Links. A beautiful ocean-side course with sweeping dunes mixed with native grasses and wildflowers. A challenging and fun course, where you’re often at the mercy of the wind. Located at the Sea Island Golf Club on Retreat Avenue.

View The Course

### Retreat Course

An additional 300 yards of length and wider fairways give Retreat an open feeling with a lower profile.

View The Course

### Plantation Golf Course

A historic course that was re-designed to bring back some traditional features and character. Along with the neighboring Seaside Course, it is the home of the PGA TOUR’s RSM Classic, a FedEx cup event hosted by Davis Love. Located at the Sea Island Golf Club on Retreat Avenue.

Explore The Course

### Fort Frederica

Remains of a colonial fort & settlement established by James Oglethorpe in 1736.

### Pier Village

Located on the south end of the Island, St. Simons’ Pier Village district serves as the downtown social hub and is a popular destination for shopping, dining and gathering on the pier and nearby lighthouse, playground and picnic areas.
