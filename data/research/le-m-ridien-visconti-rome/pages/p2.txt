## “Water for Life”: Refill, Reuse, Reduce

During your visit to Rome, quench your thirst with our environmentally friendly and reusable R-PET bottles made from 100% recycled plastic.
You can fill this bottle either from the ice-water stations located from the 1st to the 6th floor of the hotel and from Roman “nasoni”. You will find a list of the “nasoni” on the bottle, public fountains pften located in historic and picturesque places in rome, characterized by a long curved iron tube resembling a nose, hence the name.
Drinking water from the roman aqueduct network promotes a more responsible use of resources.
