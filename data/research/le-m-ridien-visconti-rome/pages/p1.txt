# Welcome to Le Méridien Visconti Rome

## Enjoy a perfect stay at our hotel in downtown Rome, Italy

Just a short walk from the Spanish Steps, the Vatican, and the Tiber River, Le Méridien Visconti Rome is a central hotel in the heart of the Eternal City. Ideal city explorers, families, and Rome business...See More hotel stays, it places guests near shopping, culture, nightlife, and transport links. Our central Rome hotel's interior design draws on 1950s modernism, pairing Roman heritage with contemporary style across 242 bright rooms and suites. As a pet-friendly hotel in central Rome, it offers a comfortable base for short breaks and corporate travel. Guests often highlight the prime location, cleanliness, helpful staff, and comfortable rooms. Longitude 12 Bistrot & Jardin serves authentic local flavors in an informal indoor garden setting, while Paparazzo Bar & Rooftop brings design and mixology together with creative cocktails and views inspired by La Dolce Vita. Our hotel in downtown Rome, Italy, also offers a multifunctional conference center with 9 high-tech meeting rooms and a fully equipped fitness center.See Less

### Modern and Elegant

Located in the charming Prati district, a few steps from St. Peter’s and Piazza di Spagna, it is the ideal destination for both business and leisure travel, as well as shopping and nightlife. From the lighting to the furnishings, the interior design is a contemporary interpretation of mid-century modernism. Sleek lines, organic curves, and geometric forms combine to create a refined interplay of materials that transcends time.

### Our Daily Commitment to a More Responsible Future

During your stay at Le Méridien Visconti Rome, our commitment to sustainability translates into concrete, responsible choices. From reducing waste to promoting low-impact solutions, we invite you to discover Rome in a more authentic and mindful way. Small daily gestures become part of a thoughtful, conscious stay that respects the local area.

### E-bike

Our guests can now rent e-bikes directly from the hotel: a sustainable and convenient way to explore Rome in total freedom. Ride through charming alleyways, historic squares and iconic views of the Eternal City, enjoying an authentic, low-impact experience. Dedicated tours will be available, designed to guide guests through the city by bike, along exclusive itineraries and past unforgettable sights.

### Green Key

Green Key is the international eco-label for hospitality establishments committed to sustainability. Awarded by the Foundation for Environmental Education, it recognises hotels that meet rigorous, verified criteria. We are proud to have received this recognition, a symbol of our commitment to the environment, the local area and the future.

### Water for Life

During your stay, we invite you to use our R-PET bottles, made from 100% recycled plastic, reusable and sustainable. You can refill them at the dispensers in the hotel or at the iconic Roman “nasoni” fountains throughout the city. A simple gesture to stay hydrated, reduce plastic use and experience Rome while respecting the environment and its resources.

## Meetings & Events

The ideal space for business conferences, corporate gatherings, cocktail parties and gala dinners in the centre of Rome. Nine high-tech multi-purpose venues up to 446 square meters equipped with state-of-the art audio and video equipment and High-Speed Internet Access. Each meeting room features wooden walls with top acoustic system totally soundproof, screen projectors, wired stereo system and tablets able to customize the scenarios set of each room. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Le Méridien Visconti Rome?

The check-in time at Le Méridien Visconti Rome is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Le Méridien Visconti Rome allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Le Méridien Visconti Rome. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 7.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at Le Méridien Visconti Rome?

Off-site valet parking is available at Le Méridien Visconti Rome. The daily valet fee is €35.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Le Méridien Visconti Rome have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Le Méridien Visconti Rome has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Le Méridien Visconti Rome booking.

What is the closest airport near Le Méridien Visconti Rome?

The closest airport near Le Méridien Visconti Rome is Leonardo da Vinci International Airport (FCO). FCO is located approximately 30.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Le Méridien Visconti Rome have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Le Méridien Visconti Rome has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
