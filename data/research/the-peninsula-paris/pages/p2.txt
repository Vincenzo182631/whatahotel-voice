In a nutshell
: The Peninsula Paris is an art-drenched hotel housed in a Beaux-Arts building in a residential feeling neighborhood in Paris yet minutes away from both the Eiffel Tower and the Arc de Triomphe.
Dana Kravis
Dana Kravis
Rooms
The hotel's 200 rooms and 86 suites are among the most spacious in Paris; five suites come with private rooftop gardens or terraces with sweeping city views. Every room is outfitted with the Peninsula's signature tech—Japanese toilets, mood lighting in the bathrooms, digital tablets controlling everything from curtains to in-room dining—but it's the art, curated throughout both guest rooms and public spaces, that sets the hotel apart and makes it feel more like a fantasy of the perfect Parisian loft rather than a hotel. I stayed in the Rooftop Eiffel Suite on the sixth floor, whose private balconies and rooftop terrace delivered exactly the view its name promises. I spent my favorite moments on one of these balconies, coffee and flaky croissant in hand, watching Paris wake up. There were two weights of bathrobe waiting—a thicker cozy one and a lighter option, ideal for a July stay—the kind of amenity that made it feel as though every detail had been considered.
Dana Kravis
Dana Kravis
Food and drink
The hotel boasts six dining and drinking venues. The Lobby is an opulent all-day dining room that also serves a standout breakfast (get the pastry basket and thank me later). LiLi, the Cantonese restaurant, hosts live traditional Chinese music Thursday through Saturday. Start your meal with the Cognac Infusion Ritual cocktail mixed tableside and the dim sum. L'Oiseau Blanc, the aviation-themed rooftop restaurant, adds yet another vantage point over the city. For a nightcap, Bar Kléber's oak paneling and gilded moldings recall the building's earlier life as the Majestic, when it hosted some of the era's most celebrated artists, including James Joyce, Marcel Proust, and Pablo Picasso.
Dana Kravis
Dana Kravis
Amenities and experiences
The Spa is a dreamy subterranean spot that feels like a true respite from the bustle of the city of lights. Inside, you’ll find a 20-meter indoor pool along with two vitality soaking pools. Along with the usual slate of massages, La Maison Lutéia, the Paris-based tech-forward medi-spa, created an exclusive facial for the property.
What to do nearby
This is Paris, so “what to do” is almost too much to put into words. However, even within the radius of the hotel you have no shortage of monuments and sites to see nearby. The hotel is literally steps away from the Arc de Triomphe, while The Musé d’Art Moderne de Paris is a lovely 15 minute walk and the Eiffel Tower is slightly longer (or four metro stops away). If shopping is your thing, you’re right near the Champs-Elysées and the famed Galeries Lafayette Champs-Elysée.
Dana Kravis
Dana Kravis
The backstory
Originally opened in 1908, the building spent decades as the Hôtel Majestic before a four-year restoration transformed it into the Peninsula brand's first European outpost in 2014, earning Palace-grade status—one tier above five-star—ever since. The public spaces are the result of that restoration: a muted cream palette set off by restored original woodwork, decorative stonework, and 40,000 pieces of gold leaf. More than 80 works of art, curated by Hong Kong–based Sabrina Fung Fine Arts, thread through the hotel, starting with Luděk Hroch's 800 hand-blown glass "Dancing Leaves" which greeting guests in the lobby, and continuing to Xavier Corberó's stone-and-marble sculpture "Moon River" and Ran Hwang's crystal installation marking the entrance to LiLi.
Insider tip
The service is what stayed with me longest. On my last morning, I realized I needed to leave before my pre-ordered breakfast was due to arrive. I called the kitchen at 5:30 a.m. and asked if they could pack it to go—two minutes later, my pastries were at my door, perfectly wrapped for the trip home.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Yes, kids are welcome in the spa’s indoor swimming pool and The Lobby’s breakfast bakery basket will delight kids and grown-ups alike
Accessibility:
Six
accessible rooms
are available, including three Superior Rooms, one Premier Room, one Junior Suite and one Superior Suite; other accessible features include Braille or raised signage, an accessible path of travel, and accessible parking
Wellness:
Spa, massage, beauty treatments, fitness center, indoor swimming pool, sauna, aromatherapy steam rooms, and relaxation lounges
