# L'OISEAU BLANC

Welcome aboard L’Oiseau Blanc and its elegant terrace: a panoramic setting above Paris, witnessing day and night its beauty and magic. The experience pays tribute to the adventurous spirit of Charles Nungesser and François Coli, French aviation pioneers whose striking biplane replica appears still in flight, seemingly heading towards the Eiffel Tower.

Under the direction of Chef David Bizet, L’Oiseau Blanc offers a poetic and refined cuisine rooted in French tradition and open to modern inspiration. Awarded two Michelin stars, it reflects a deep connection to nature and terroir, inspired by the Perche region and a strong commitment to sustainable gastronomy, where each dish celebrates the seasons with precision and emotion.

## Meet The Chef

In every aromatic note, I discover an ode to the terroirs of France, where the richness of our soils meets the subtle art of gastronomy. Like a declaration of love to the roots of culinary art, each of my dishes celebrates history, tradition, and an infinite passion for the ingredients that surround us.

#### David Bizet

L'Oiseau Blanc is thrilled to have chef David Bizet in its ranks, where the culinary maestro flexes his creative and culinary prowess refined over years spent in some of France’s top dining destinations. He embarked on his culinary career at the Four Seasons Hotel George V, in Paris. He remained at the hotel for 18 years, eventually taking on the role of Chef at Le V restaurant in 2014, and then of Chef at L’Orangerie restaurant where, in 2017, he was awarded his first Michelin star. In 2018, Chef Bizet moved on to join the team at Paris’s storied Le Taillevent restaurant, where he received his second Michelin star in January 2020.

#### Finest Experience

The aviation-themed L’Oiseau Blanc restaurant celebrates the venture of ex-First World War flying aces Charles Nungesser and François Coli who attempted to cross the Atlantic in 1927 from Le Bourget. A unique collection of aviation memorabilia includes a 75% size replica of the L’Oiseau Blanc biplane in the adjacent courtyard, and Bernardaud has created the chinaware for the L’Oiseau Blanc, with a bespoke design featuring the plane.

#### Carnet de Chasse

Discover "Carnet de Chasse", an exceptional seasonal menu imagined by two-Michelin-starred Chef David Bizet. Inspired by his childhood in the Perche region, this gastronomic journey celebrates the richness of game and autumnal flavours, culminating in his signature "Lièvre à la Royale", a dish that earned him the title of World Champion.
