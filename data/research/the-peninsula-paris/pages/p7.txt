Maintain Personal Goals The Fitness Centre item 2

The wellness area at The Peninsula Paris features a spacious 20-metre indoor pool, complemented by two jacuzzis for a truly relaxing experience. **Nearby, two fitness rooms—fully renovated in March 2025—offer state-of-the-art Technogym equipment for both strength training and cardio workouts.**

Our dedicated fitness advisors are on hand to provide personalized support and to design bespoke wellness programs tailored to your individual goals.

Discover our brochure.

Lower Ground Floor

### Hours

Fitness open from 06:30am to 10:00pm (24 hours a day on request)

Running Paris at dawn

### The "Morning Rise" experience

**Every Tuesday at 7am, treat yourself to an inspiring wellness escape.**

Set off on a gentle run with a certified coach, passing iconic Paris landmarks from the Eiffel Tower to Pont Alexandre III, with pauses to breathe, admire, and reflect. Back at the hotel, enjoy a healthy rooftop snack, followed by stretching, then time to unwind: hammam, sauna, and pool.

_Exclusively for hotel guests and Spa members. Booking required via the Spa \| Duration: 45 min \| Complimentary refreshment included_

The Peninsula Spa

### Saturday Morning Yoga

Every Saturday, The Peninsula Spa offers a Vinyasa yoga class led by a certified instructor. This fluid and accessible practice blends breath, movement and mindfulness in an exceptional setting. Held under the Rotonde’s dome in winter or on the rooftop in summer, the session includes 15 minutes of meditation and exclusive access to the Spa’s wellness facilities.

**Every Saturday at 9:00 am – 1h15 of yoga & meditation. Spa access until 1:00 pm (pool, sauna, hammam, fitness).**

**€150 per person – Max. 10 guests – This experience is complimentary for all our hotel guests and requires advance booking with the Spa.**

Contact: 01 58 12 66 82 or spappr@peninsula.com
