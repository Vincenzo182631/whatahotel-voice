#### The Peninsula Paris

## Supporting the fight against breast cancer throughout October

Since its opening in 2014, The Peninsula Paris has shown its commitment to the fight against breast cancer through its annual Peninsula in Pink campaign. This October, the hotel continues this longstanding commitment and is proud to support Ruban Rose for the first time, with its iconic façade illuminated in pink throughout the month and a series of dedicated initiatives across the hotel.

Guests are invited to join this collective effort through specially curated stay, dining and wellness experiences, with proceeds supporting Ruban Rose and its work in the fight against breast cancer.

## Ruban Rose

Founded in 2003, Ruban Rose is a French non-profit organisation dedicated to the fight against breast cancer. Its mission is twofold: to raise awareness about breast cancer and the importance of early detection, and to support medical and scientific research through the annual Ruban Rose Awards.

In 2026, The Peninsula Paris is proud to support Ruban Rose for the first time as part of its annual Peninsula in Pink campaign, contributing to its efforts to advance research and raise awareness throughout October.

## A subtle touch of pink...

Throughout October, The Peninsula Paris adds a subtle touch of pink to every stay in support of breast cancer awareness. Guests are invited to discover a special Peninsula in Pink welcome amenity in their room or suite. For every night booked during the month of October, €2 will be donated to _Ruban Rose_.

## Afternoon Tea is dressed in pink

Throughout October, Afternoon Tea at The Lobby embraces the colours of Peninsula in Pink. Our Chefs have imagined delicate pink creations especially for the occasion, to be enjoyed in the elegant setting of The Lobby. For every Afternoon Tea enjoyed during the month, €3 will be donated to _Ruban Rose_.

## Peninsula in Pink - Signature Desserts

For Peninsula in Pink, our Pastry Chefs have created a selection of signature desserts inspired by the campaign’s iconic colour. Available throughout October at L’Oiseau Blanc, LiLi and The Lobby, these creations combine indulgence with a meaningful cause. For every Peninsula in Pink dessert enjoyed, €2 will be donated to Ruban Rose.

## Le Bar Kléber turns pink

Throughout October, Le Bar Kléber joins Peninsula in Pink with two creations imagined especially for the occasion: a signature cocktail and a non-alcoholic creation, both inspired by the campaign’s iconic pink hues. For every cocktail or mocktail ordered during the month, €2 will be donated to Ruban Rose.

## The Peninsula Spa & Wellness Centre

This October, The Peninsula Spa highlights its signature Peninsula Longevity Facial as part of Peninsula in Pink. Combining expert techniques with a holistic approach to wellbeing, this treatment offers a moment dedicated to oneself while supporting an important cause. For every treatment booked during October, €10 will be donated to Ruban Rose.
