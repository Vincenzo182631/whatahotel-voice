Where East Meets West The Peninsula Spa item 2

The Peninsula Spa, the largest hotel spa in Paris, invites you to experience bespoke wellness journeys. Subtly blending the elegance of French beauty with the ancient traditions of Asian treatments, this haven of relaxation offers a holistic collection of the world’s most prestigious treatments. Within its tranquil setting, you will find six individual treatment rooms and two private suites, as well as a 20-meter indoor pool and two vitality pools.

Discover our brochure.

_Please note that our fitness centre and swimming pool are exclusively reserved for hotel guests and Peninsula Spa members._

_Prices are net in euros, service included; cheques are not accepted._

### Location

Basement floor

### Hours

Treatments - daily from 9am to 9pm.

Fitness facilities are accessible from 6:30am to 10pm, and 24 hours a day on request.

The swimming pool is open from 06:30am to 10:00pm only.

_Access to treatments must be booked in advance._

### Saturday Morning Yoga

Every Saturday, The Peninsula Spa offers a Vinyasa yoga class led by a certified instructor. This fluid and accessible practice blends breath, movement and mindfulness in an exceptional setting. Held under the Rotonde’s dome in winter or on the rooftop in summer, the session includes 15 minutes of meditation and exclusive access to the Spa’s wellness facilities.

**Every Saturday at 9:00 am – 1h15 of yoga & meditation. Spa access until 1:00 pm (pool, sauna, hammam, fitness).**

**€150 per person – Max. 10 guests – This experience is complimentary for all our hotel guests and requires advance booking with the Spa.**

Contact: 01 58 12 66 82 or spappr@peninsula.com

## For a successful experience atThe Peninsula Spa & Wellness Centre

**SPA FACILITIES**

Prior to your treatments we encourage you to enjoy the facilities at the Spa, including separate female and male, Steam Rooms, Saunas, Experience Showers, Ice Fountains, Changing Rooms and Relaxation Areas.

**OPENING HOURS**

Treatments - Daily from 9:00 am to 9:00 pm

Fitness - Pool\* Daily from 6:30 am to 10:00 pm

\\* Exclusively reserved for hotel residents and spa members.

**ARRIVAL TIME**

You are encouraged to arrive 60 minutes prior to your appointment time in order to change, relax and enjoy the facilities or at least 15 minutes beforehand to complete your well-being sur vey. In case you are delayed, depending on therapist's availability, we will do our best to provide the same experience. In case we cannot find availability, please note your treatment may be shortened to continue with our following appointments. We recommend that hotel guests arrive at the spa wearing the robe and slippers provided in the room and would advise that valuables are left in the safe in the room. Please note that the duration of the treatment includes the time of consultation and settling in.

**SCHEDULING AN APPOINTMENT**

Please speak with our Spa Reception team who will guide you to the right choice. Hotel guests can press the Spa button on your in-room telephone. From outside the hotel please call +33 (0)1 58 12 66 82. To ensure your preferred time and service is available we recommend advance booking. Please contact the Spa Reception to book Beauty cares.

**CANCELLATION POLICY**

A cancellation within 24 hours will incur a 100% charge.

**HOMECARE**

To continue your spa regime and experience at home, products used in our treatments are available for purchase from the Spa Reception.

**SPA ETIQUETTE**

Our Spa environment is one of relaxation and tranquility. Please respect all spa guests’ right to privacy, serenity and comfort. The Spa team provides well-being massages with no therapeutic purpose. The Spa is a smoke-free zone, and the use of mobile phones, personal digital assistants and other personal electronic devices is not permitted. The Spa provides luxurious towels, slippers, shower caps and all amenities required for use during your spa visit. The minimum age is 16 for access to treatments and heat facilities at The Peninsula Spa. Please note that the pool is reserved for hotel residents and members only. If you have any medical concerns, allergies or injuries that may affect your service, please inform us in advance when you book your Spa experience. Pets are not allowed in our spa.

**PREGNANCY**

We have a Mother-to-be massage available at the end of the first trimester of pregnancy. Please allow our Spa team to guide you in selecting which treatments are most suitable for you during this special time, taking into account the advice of your doctor.
