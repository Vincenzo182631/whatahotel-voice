# The Pool

The serene pool at The Peninsula Paris boasts sleek décor that features a striking floor-to-ceiling water cascade.

Boasting a 20 m x 5 m (65 ft x 16 ft) indoor pool, two Jacuzzis, eight private treatment booths including two suites for couples, a relaxation room as well as two fitness rooms equipped with state-of-the-art machines, The Peninsula Spa offers a relaxation experience unlike any other in the heart of the City of Light.

All children must be accompanied by a parent or guardian when using the pool. The pool is only available to hotel guests and members of The Peninsula Spa.

### Location

Lower Ground Floor

### Hours

Swimming pool open 06:30am to 10:00pm
