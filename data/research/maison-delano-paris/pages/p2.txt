# Rooms & Suites

Rooms and suites at Maison Delano Paris envelop guests in a refined and sophisticated sensibility. Equal parts inspiration and comfort, rooms are appointed with modern furnishings rendered in an understated neutral palette, and layered with rich olive green hues, ethereal curtains, and contemporary artwork in a decidedly modern expression of five-star luxury.

## Suites

## Rooms

Retire from the lively streets of Paris to one of our signature suites, each designed with exquisite attention to detail.

### Junior Suite

Accommodating up to three guests, the Junior Suite invites moments of relaxation in its intimate living area, offering a peaceful retreat from the vibrant energy of Paris.

40 sqm / 430 sqft \| 3 Guests \| Double or Twin

Book Now

### Deluxe Suite

Designed as a refined Parisian apartment, the Deluxe Suite features a separate living room, a spacious walk-in wardrobe and a bathroom with both a walk-in shower and a bathtub. Overlooking the hotel’s tranquil inner courtyard, it provides the perfect setting to unwind and recharge.

65 sqm / 699 sqft \| 3 Guests \| Double or Twin \| Courtyard View

Book Now

### Grand Prestige Suite

The exceptional Grand Prestige Suite reflects the rich history of the building through its beautifully restored painted ceilings, soaring proportions and original 18th-century mouldings. Open Delano’s signature sheer curtains to step onto the second-floor balcony and enjoy views over the prestigious Faubourg Saint-Honoré.

The Grand Prestige Suite can be connected to the neighbouring Grand Historic Suite to create an expansive two-bedroom suite.

70 sqm / 753 sqft \| Double or Twin \| 3 Guests \| Rue d’Anjou View

Book Now

### Grand Historic Suite

A true jewel of Parisian heritage, the Grand Historic Suite celebrates the elegance of the 18th century through its herringbone parquet flooring, original painted ceilings, marble fireplaces and a sweeping balcony overlooking the Faubourg Saint-Honoré. Blending seamlessly with Delano’s contemporary design aesthetic, the suite offers a timeless way to experience both the historic and modern spirit of Paris.

The Grand Historic Suite can be connected to the neighbouring Grand Prestige Suite to create an expansive two-bedroom suite.

120 sqm / 1291 sqft \| 3 Guests \| 1 Double \| Rue d’Anjou View

Book Now

### Maison Delano Presidential Suite

Combining the two largest suites at Maison Delano Paris, the Presidential Suite is distinguished by magnificent herringbone parquet flooring, remarkable 18th-century architectural features, soaring frescoed ceilings, and two luxurious bathrooms, including one with a freestanding bathtub and marble fireplace. Arranged in a classic succession of grand reception rooms, the suite reveals a series of spacious living areas and bedrooms, offering an authentic interpretation of a Parisian grand apartment where gilded heritage details harmoniously meet Delano’s contemporary design signature.

190 sqm / 2044 sqft \| 6 Guests \| Double or Twin \| Rue d’Anjou View

Book Now
