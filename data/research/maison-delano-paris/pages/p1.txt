Located in the heart of the 8th Arrondissement, Maison Delano Paris is a luxury hotel of 56 extraordinary rooms and suites. Guests are welcomed to an irresistible environment that tempers aristocratic grandeur with modernity to create an experience that will stand the test of time, and is unmistakably Delano…

## Restaurants & Bar

Maison Delano Paris is home to Delano Café, a vibrant French-Mediterranean restaurant and bar nestled in the courtyard, and Delano Bar, serving innovative cocktails with the city’s most attentive service.

### Concierge

Allow us to show you the way. From airport transfers and museum tickets to in-room massages, a hair stylist on demand, our paid parking facilities, and even accommodating your pets (additional fees apply). Any request is met with pleasure.

Original marble fireplaces, painted ceilings and intricate mouldings take center stage in the Presidential Suite – an authentic celebration of the building’s provenance updated with a modern spirit and an exceptional space for private events.
