# Rooms & Suites

Rooms and suites at Maison Delano Paris envelop guests in a refined and sophisticated sensibility. Equal parts inspiration and comfort, rooms are appointed with modern furnishings rendered in an understated neutral palette, and layered with rich olive green hues, ethereal curtains, and contemporary artwork in a decidedly modern expression of five-star luxury.

## Suites

## Rooms

Deluxe and Prestige rooms are designed with the warmth of soft minimalism and the character of fine craftsmanship.

### Deluxe Room

The Deluxe Room offers generous space in a serene and soothing atmosphere, making it the perfect setting for a Parisian escape.

30 sqm / 322 sqft \| 2 Guests \| Double or Twin

### Prestige Room

The Prestige Room showcases Delano’s signature understated aesthetic. Overlooking either the streets of the City of Light or the hotel’s historic courtyard, it offers an invitation to experience the authentic charm of Paris.

35 sqm / 376 sqft \| 2 Guests \| Double or Twin

### Accessibility

#### Spaces with accessible routes from entrance

- Reception & Concierge
- Lobby
- Restaurant Indoor & Outdoor Courtyard
- Hotel & Restaurant Restrooms
- Delano Bar

#### Our Spaces

Lobby, Restaurant & Bar.

#### Accessible Hotel Features

- Reserved Parking Spaces: Designated parking spots for guests with reduced mobility, located near the hotel.
- Accessible Elevators: Elevators with accessible-height buttons, Braille markings, and audible announcements.
- Wheelchair on Request
- Visible Emergency Exits + Fire Alarm

#### Accessibility within rooms

- Rooms with Wider Doors
- Roll-in Showers
- Walk-in showers with grab bars
- Raised toilets with grab bars.
- Adapted Telephones
- Visual Alarms
- Television Closed Captioning.
- Assistance Service
