### Idyllic Beachfront

Nestled on the shores of Bluewaters Dubai, Delano boasts 250 meters of unspoiled private beachfront that is without equal in the city.

Natural waves lap a quiet coastline that is uniquely positioned to catch full days of sun and unobstructed sea views of the shimmering Arabian Gulf – all only moments from the Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah and the heart of the city.
