# The World of Delano

Delano is honoring a celebrated legacy while shaping a bold new global chapter. In every location, Delano nurtures a refined spirit of community, welcoming the world’s most curious, creative and well-traveled.

## Delano Locations

## Delano Miami Beach

```
Great loves always come back. Delano opens its doors in Miami once again, presenting a refined, reimagined experience that celebrates the artistic and cultural landscape of Miami. Discover elegant rooms - including signature penthouses and coveted poolside bungalows - vibrant dining, curated wellness and two feature pools – all located on a pristine Miami beachfront.
```

## Maison Delano Paris

```
Perfectly located in the heart of the 8th Arrondissement, Maison Delano Paris promises an intimate experience with sophisticated design, refined culinary experiences, and unparalleled service, blending timeless charm with modern allure.

## Delano Dubai

```
Set on unspoiled, private beachfront that is unparalleled in the city, Delano Dubai connects a global community through a shared language of elegance and elevated experience. Contemporary and serene rooms and suites wrap around the Delano pool, while a dynamic culinary scene and a calendar of events and gatherings engages guests and embraces the rhythm of Dubai.
