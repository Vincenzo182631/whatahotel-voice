So kick your boots off, soak in the warm Southern hospitality, and let the vibrant energy of Austin inspire your next adventure. Here, every stay is as unique as you are.

#### Accessible Features

At Hotel Van Zandt, we believe everyone deserves a seamless and unforgettable stay. We’re proud to offer an array of accessibility features to ensure all our guests feel right at home.

Accessibility Features

- Valet Parking
- Elevators
- Accessible rooms and restrooms
- Service animals welcome
- Braille and tactile signage provided for permanent rooms and spaces
- Hotel employees will read menus and other printed/visual information to guests who are blind/low vision

Accessibility Spaces

- Accessible entrance
- Easy-access registration desk and concierge
- Pathway to Hotel Entrance
- Accessible restaurant
- Accessible meeting space and business center

Communications Features

- Telephone ring signaler
- Bed shaker
- Visual alarm clock
- Telephone in-line amplifier
- Door knock alert
- Visual fire alarm
