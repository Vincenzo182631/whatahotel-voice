# Plant Yourself Right Here

Brass Poppy sprouts its spirit from two enduring muses: the poppy flower and the pulse of music.

The poppy, both delicate and resilient, and graceful yet bold, reflects the duality we embrace in every details. Music, ever-present and effortlessly transportive, shapes the rhythm of this space just as it shapes our city. Together, they inspire a setting where vintage charm meets modern edge, with a soft amber light and a playlist that flows as seamlessly as the service.

Planted inside Hotel Van Zandt, Brass Poppy is a neighborhood bar with a distinct point of view. Whether you’re in denim or dressed to the nines, you’ll feel entirely at ease. No gimmicks, just a little whimsy. We revive overlooked guilty pleasures and reimagine the classics, serving them with style, balance, and just the right touch of wit. You’ll find well-made cocktails, nostalgic flavors redefined, and the kind of hospitality that invites you to linger.

Brass Poppy: where guilty pleasures get their redemption story, and look exceptional doing it.

### Bloom + Beats

Experience **Bloom + Beats**, Brass Poppy’s late-night happy hour, every Friday and Saturday from **9:00–11:00 p.m.** Enjoy live music from resident DJ Ben Green alongside handcrafted cocktails, premium champagne, and chef-driven bites in a sophisticated lounge setting.

Late-night happy hour offerings include:

- **$12 Signature Cocktails**
- **$8 wells**
- **50% Off the Entire Food Menu**

_No cover charge or reservations required._
