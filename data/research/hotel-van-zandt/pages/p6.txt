# One Bedroom Spa Suite

Elevate your relaxation game in our One Bedroom Spa Suite. Stretch out in the spacious living area, perfect for unwinding after a day exploring Austin. When it’s time to rest, the separate bedroom offers a private oasis for a blissful night’s sleep. In the en-suite bathroom, indulge in a spa-like experience with an oversized soaking tub and a walk-in shower. Created with your comfort in mind, this suite’s luxurious amenities and ample space will leave you feeling revitalized and inspired.

- 1 King
- Sleeps 2
- 500 Sq. Ft.
