# Corner King

After a day of exploring the city, retreat to our luxurious Corner King room. Nestled away from the hustle and bustle, this stylish room offers a peaceful haven with a cozy seating area. Sink into the plush comfort of your king-size bed, or kick back in the cozy seating area, perfect for unwinding with a good book or catching up on your favorite shows. This room is more than just a place to rest—it’s your hideaway to relax, recharge, and get ready for your next Austin adventure.

- 1 King
- Sleeps 2
- 360 Sq. Ft.
