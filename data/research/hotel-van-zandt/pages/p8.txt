# Spa Queen/Queen Junior Suite

After a day of adventure, escape to pure bliss in our Spa Queen/Queen Junior Suite. Soak away the day in a luxurious oversized soaking tub and feel your worries fade. With two comfy queen-size beds and a plush seating area, you’ll have all the space you need to unwind in style. Welcome to relaxation, turned up a notch.
