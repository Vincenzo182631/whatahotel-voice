# Explore Restaurants in Downtown Austin

## Bold Flavors Meet Southern Charm

If you’re searching for restaurants in Downtown Austin that offer a unique culinary experience beyond the expected, look no further than Hotel Van Zandt. From sunrise breakfast to midnight cocktails, you’ll find bold flavors and warm Southern hospitality in every bite. Come hungry and leave with a story to tell.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Hotel Van Zandt have room service?

Guests can enjoy in-room dining daily. Find our full menu and hours here.

Does Hotel Van Zandt offer poolside dining?

We are proud to offer our guests a rooftop poolside dining experience at The Rooftop Bar. Enjoy dining, drinks and even private poolside cabanas.

Does Hotel Van Zandt have an on-site bar?

Brass Poppy is our neighborhood cocktail lounge with a distinct point of view. Where guilty pleasures get their redemption story, and look exceptional doing it.

Does Hotel Van Zandt have an on-site café?

Check out Café 605 and grab something portable for a picnic or savor a sweet treat after a long day of sightseeing.

Does Hotel Van Zandt have a live music venue?

At Geraldine’s, experience a mix of live music and modern Mexican cuisine with southern influence. Geraldine’s is perfect for a big celebration, a casual hang with friends, or just vibing solo
