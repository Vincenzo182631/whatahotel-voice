# Pool View King

Wake up with a view that’s as refreshing as your morning coffee. Our Pool View King rooms greet you with a stunning view of our sparkling rooftop pool, setting the perfect tone for your Austin adventure.  Savor a freshly brewed coffee then step into a spacious walk-in shower to start your day feeling refreshed. As the day winds down, retreat to the plush comfort of your king-size bed. From dawn to dusk, the Pool View King Room is your ultimate Austin home base, offering a blend of cozy comfort and luxurious elegance.
