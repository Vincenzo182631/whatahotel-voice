# InterContinental ShenzhenRooms & suites

Our guest rooms begin at a spacious 55 square meters and offer a curated selection of styles, from the vibrant essence of Spain and the refined elegance of Chinese motifs to sleek modern and timeless classic designs. Most rooms feature a private viewing balcony, presenting panoramic views of our Caribbean-inspired garden landscape. Every interior detail exemplifies exceptional taste, complete with an LCD television, a Bose sound system, and a stylish, comfortable work area offering high-speed wired and wireless internet. The expansive bathroom seamlessly integrates a spa-style bathtub with entertainment, creating a private sanctuary for relaxation. Each room is thoughtfully designed to craft a haven of comfort that embodies luxurious sophistication and delivers an extraordinary experience.
