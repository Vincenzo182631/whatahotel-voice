# InterContinental ShenzhenDestination

Shenzhen is a bustling metropolis known for its luxury shopping destinations, contemporary buildings like the Pin An International Finance Centre, and several amusement parks.

## Things to do

### Window of the World

深南大道9037号, 南山区, 深圳市, 广东省 518000

Window of the World opened in June 18, 1994, is the first 5A tourist scenic attraction, covering 480,000 square meters. It is divided into eight regions of the World Square, Asia, Oceania, Europe, Africa, Americas, World Sculpture Park and International Street according to regional location and activities. ### OCT HARBOUR

滨海大道2008号, 南山区沙河街道, 深圳市, 广东省 518000

OCT HARBOUR brings together the good life of the city, creating an open space of "waterfront single building + outer swing space + street channel" combined into the commercial district Qushui Bay, bringing deep sea immersion experienceand integrating nature, art and humanities themes of large-scale multimedia laser water show "Deep Blue Secret Environment".

Learn more

### Splendid China

深南大道9003号, 南山区华侨城, 深圳市, 广东省 518000

Splendid China is the first cultural theme park in China, and is the most popular theme park with near 100 sites distributed according to their location in China. Splendid China folk village is home to Chinese traditional culture, composed of miniature scenic spots and China Folk Culture Village. The entire park looks like a huge map of China. People can have a panoramic view of the country in a few hours. Folk Culture Village brings together the construction customs of 56 ethnic groups.

Learn more

### Happy Valley

侨城西街18号, 南山区华侨城, 深圳市, 广东省 5180000

Happy Valley is a gorgeous theme park owned by Overseas Chinese Town. A vast investment of 2 billion RMB makes Happy Valley one of the first lot of Grade-5A National tourism attractions, a contemporary Chinese theme park with a coverage space of 350,000 square meters, integrated with happiness involvement, sight viewing, entertaining amusement and endless fun.
