### Caribbean garden with beach and outdoor pool

Our immaculately landscaped 10,000 square metre Caribbean garden encircles our expansive swimming pool, creating an oasis of calm. This haven is perfect for relaxing on holiday or away from work. Children will be delighted by an outdoor play area, Spanish galleon, kids' pool, waterfalls, and a real sand beach.
