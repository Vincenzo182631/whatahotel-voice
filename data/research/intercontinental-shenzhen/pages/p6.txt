## FAQs for InterContinental Shenzhen

Does InterContinental Shenzhen have a pool?

InterContinental Shenzhen has 1 indoor and 1 outdoor pools.

Indoor Pool

The hotel provides an indoor heated swimming pool equipped with a wealth of fitness facilities, including gym, sauna, steam bath, yoga studio, hair and nail salon, etc..

Outdoor Pool & Beach

The 10,000-square-meter Caribbean garden features a real sand beach and a 2,000-square-meter outdoor pool, anchored by iconic Spanish boat and palm trees that presents a sense of urban resort.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Shenzhen?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Shenzhen for hotel guests.

Parking are available at east and west wing, complimentry for inhouse and restaurant guests.

Vehicles allowed: Bus, Truck

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Shenzhen?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Shenzhen.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Shenzhen for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Shenzhen have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Shenzhen has 3 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Shenzhen?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Shenzhen.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Shenzhen?

Check-in at InterContinental Shenzhen is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Shenzhen offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Shenzhen offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Shenzhen from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Shenzhen is conveniently located near Shenzhen Baoan International Airport (SZX), approximately 16.16 miles away (26 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Shenzhen?

InterContinental Shenzhen is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Window of the World - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- OCT HARBOUR - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Splendid China - 0.12 miles away (0.2 kilometers)
- Happy Valley - 0.87 miles away (1.4 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Shenzhen puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
