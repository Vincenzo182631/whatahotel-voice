## Mercado Restaurant & Cafe

Inspired by the iconic plazas of Madrid and vibrant Spanish food markets, Mercado Cafe brings the spirit of Andalusia to Shenzhen, with graceful arches, mosaic tiles and warm Iberian tones. A sunlit courtyard and palm lined terrace create a relaxed resort atmosphere. Local Shenzhen flavors meet Mediterranean specialties, from fresh juices and artisan breads to vibrant salads and grilled seafood. An open kitchen and Chef X weekend buffets bring global flavors to life.
