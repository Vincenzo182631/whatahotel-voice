The St. Regis Atlanta
With its central Buckhead location, famed restaurant and bar, and top-notch spa, this luxury hotel is worth the price.

Reviewed by

Where are we, and what's your first impression?
St. Regis
Atlanta
has one heck of a location: It’s across the promenade from
Buckhead Village
, home to Lucchese, Emilia George, and other high-end boutiques. The lobby feels like a stately mansion, with hardwood floors, dual curved staircases, and glittering chandeliers. Day and night, it stays busy with guests and locals heading to and from the spa, restaurants, and bar.
Who else is checking in?
Spa-goers, shopaholics, and generally convivial types.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
The Macassar ebony bed in my Deluxe Room felt like heaven, what with the pillow-top mattress and 300-thread-count Egyptian-cotton sheets. I loved the white-marble bathroom with double vanities, Reméde products, and LCD TV recessed behind the mirror. But my favorite thing about the room was the perfect little Juliette balcony overlooking the promenade.
What's the best thing about the accommodations?
No matter which room you select, you’ll be assisted by a butler who’ll fetch you ice, press your suit, or even replace that book you left on the plane.
And if you’re lucky enough to score the newly renovated Empire Suite (all 3,000 square feet of it), you’ll have your own state-of-the-art kitchen, media room, and personal gym with a Peloton.
So, what else is going on at the hotel?
The hotel is chock full of amenities: The Pool Piazza, open during the summer, is a tranquil place to read a magazine or simply listen to the sounds of cascading water flowing from the pool’s fountain. The sixth-floor Remède Spa is a local favorite, thanks to a creative menu of treatments such as the tension-reducing "Buckhead Escape." (Note, there is a sauna but no steam room or whirlpool. A major relaunch is coming soon.) The St. Regis Bar is arguably the most popular hotel bar in Atlanta, with wood-paneled walls that give it a clubby, masculine feel. It’s a quiet spot to have a pre-dinner martini and or a nightcap among lively locals after 10 p.m. Transportation within a two-mile radius is complimentary, while valet parking has unlimited in-and-out privileges ($62/day). You can also opt to take one of the hotel’s two Mercedes-Benz vehicles out for the day. On weekends, the hotel hosts afternoon tea. Every evening at 6 p.m., guests gather in the Grand Hallway to watch the hotel sommelier open a bottle of champagne by saber. It’s a tradition that traces its roots to Napoleon and was ritualized some years ago at the
St. Regis New York
.
And finally, at the hotel’s on-site restaurant—featuring bold American cuisine from chef Freddy Money, you’ll sit inches from original works by Picasso, Van Gogh, Chagall, and Matisse, all part of owner Joe Lewis’ private collection. It’s no wonder it was one of only a handful of restaurants to earn one Michelin star in 2023. For small plates and a more casual atmosphere, opt for a table in the Tavern at Atlas. Don’t leave without browsing the library lounge, which displays a curated selection of required Atlanta reading like A Man In Full.
Should we splurge and book a room?
With its central location, fantastic food and drinks, and extraordinary amenities, this hotel is worth the price—and it has recently undergone a full renovation.
