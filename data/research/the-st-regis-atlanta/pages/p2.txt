### The St. Regis Atlanta Butler Service

A Commitment to Excellence for more than a century. St. Regis Butler Service has been a treasured hallmark of The St. Regis experience for over one hundred years. From a perfectly pressed suit ready for an important meeting, to a favorite book at hand for an evening read, to a thoughtful, last minute gift for a loved one, no request is too small or unattainable no matter what the hour of the day.

Learn More

Learn More

As one of the hallmarks of the St. Regis brand experience, guests can expect an elevated level of discreet, anticipatory service from the St. Regis Butlers. Available any time, day or night, the St. Regis Butlers are at the ready to provide personalized comforts for each guest, from packing and unpacking of clothing to shoe shines, garment pressing and beyond. It is their pleasure.

##### St. Regis Butler Service Desk

At every St. Regis hotel, guests have access to St. Regis Butler Service via the Butler Service Desk. Should you need something for your travels or just for your convenience, our Butler Service will be sure to have on hand, or source your required item. Whatever the request, guests may dial for prompt service on their guest room phone.

##### Beverage Service

Upon arrival, allow a St. Regis Butler to arrange coffee or tea service in your room. And if you wish, a St. Regis Butler will arrive after your morning wake-up call to draw your shades, deliver your coffee or tea as well as the day’s newspaper and weather forecast. Complimentary in-room beverage service is available at any point during your stay.

##### Garment Pressing

Once unpacked, simply let your Butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. Generally, two garments may be pressed complimentary.

##### Unpacking & Packing Services

Allow a St. Regis Butler to take care of the unpacking and storage of your luggage—so that you can simply relax and enjoy. Similarly, before you depart, simply contact the Butler Service Desk to dispatch a butler to assist with gathering and folding your garments to prepare your luggage for the journey home.

##### Shoe Shine

Allow our St. Regis Butler to ensure your appearance is impeccable. Complimentary shoe shine service is available through your St. Regis Butler.
