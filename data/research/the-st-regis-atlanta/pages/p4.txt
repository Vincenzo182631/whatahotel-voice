## Distinctive Shopping in Buckhead Atlanta

Experience world-renowned shopping during your stay at The St. Regis Atlanta. Our hotel in Buckhead is near Lenox Square Mall and Buckhead Village, all of which showcase the city's finest retail and dining opportunities. Allow The St. Regis Atlanta to facilitate your luxury shopping excursion in Buckhead and accommodate your stay at our AAA Five Diamond hotel.

Legendary Shopping

##### Buckhead Village

**5- minute walk; 0.2 mile**

Buckhead is buzzing about Atlanta's newest luxury shopping and dining location, The Shops Buckhead Atlanta. Guests will find world-renowned shopping featuring prestigious names such as Hermes, Jimmy Choo, Tom Ford, Christian Louboutin and more, within walking distance of The St. Regis Atlanta. After a day of shopping, sip on a rooftop cocktail at The Southern Gentleman or delight in classic French dishes and wines at Le Bilboquet.

##### Phipps Plaza

**11-minute drive; 1.8 miles**

Boasting upscale retailers such as Saks Fifth Avenue, Gucci, Jeffrey, Nordstrom and Versace, and a 14-screen AMC movie theater with reclining chairs, Phipps Plaza is one of Atlanta's premier shopping and entertainment destinations. Whether you plan to spend time with family at LEGOLAND, shop in one of the mall's premium stores or wind down with a Happy Hour at The Tavern, you are guaranteed to enjoy your experience at Phipps Plaza.

##### Lenox Square

**11- minute drive; 1.6 miles**

One of Atlanta's oldest shopping destinations, Lenox Square offers three full-service restaurants, including the popular True Food Kitchen, and notable stores such as Neiman Marcus, Bloomingdale's, Louis Vuitton, Cartier and more - just across the street from the mall's sister center, Phipps Plaza. After you enjoy a day of legendary shopping, make sure you stop in Sprinkles Cupcakes & Ice Cream for the proper "icing on top" of a successful shopping excursion.
