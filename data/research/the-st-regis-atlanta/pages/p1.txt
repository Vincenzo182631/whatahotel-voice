### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Atlanta?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Atlanta is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Atlanta allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Atlanta. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 35.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Atlanta?

On-site valet parking is available at The St. Regis Atlanta. The daily valet fee is $70.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Atlanta have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Atlanta has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Atlanta booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Atlanta?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Atlanta is Dekalb-Peachtree Airport (PDK). PDK is located approximately 7.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Atlanta have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Atlanta has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
