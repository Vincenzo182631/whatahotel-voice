### The St. Regis Rituals

The St. Regis brand is distinguished by its rich historical heritage and time-honored traditions, experienced across its global portfolio of over sixty hotels. Central to the St. Regis experience are its Signature Rituals, including the iconic Afternoon Tea, Bloody Mary Ritual, Evening Ritual, and Midnight Supper, all reflecting the elegance and legacy established by the Astor family since the hotel's...See More founding in 1904. These rituals offer guests a glimpse into the brand's refined heritage and contribute to a luxurious and memorable stay.See Less

### Bloody Mary

A St. Regis ritual that honors the Bloody Mary cocktail created by barman Fernand Petiot in 1934. The West Paces Mary, our local interpretation of the cocktail, is inspired by the historic flavors of Paces Ferry Road.

Learn More

### Sabering Ritual

The Art of Sabrage. Celebrate the tradition of the day into the night with our charming sabering ceremony, The St. Regis signature ritual transforming evenings into superb celebrations. Hosted Daily at 6:00 PM on the fourth floor.

Learn More

### Afternoon Tea

The St. Regis Atlanta Afternoon Tea ritual is an avant-garde experience that combines the refined traits of Afternoon Tea with live entertainment, artfully blended teas, and decadent bites. The Afternoon Tea experience at our Buckhead, Atlanta, restaurant takes place in Astor Court on Saturdays and Sundays.
