## Exquisitely inviting in garden pavilions

Take your seat in one of five elegant private dining rooms set in individual pavilions overlooking our lotus ponds. Your menu showcases China's finest regional cuisines – a specialism reflected in the name Bai Wei which means 'a hundred flavours'. Executive Chef Gee Kok Dan Danny takes you on a memorable gourmet journey through Cantonese, Hunan and Szechuan flavours alongside Bei Wei signature dishes and Hainan specialities.

## Frequently asked questions

### What types of cuisines are featured at Bai Wei Dining?

Bai Wei Dining showcases Cantonese, Hunan, and Szechuan cuisines, along with signature dishes and Hainan specialties.

### How can I make a reservation at Bai Wei?

You can reserve your dining experience by calling (86) 898 8338 9888 or emailing hainan@raffles.com.

### What is the ambiance like at Bai Wei Dining?

Bai Wei Dining offers an exquisite ambiance with private dining rooms set in elegant pavilions overlooking serene lotus ponds.

### Who is the Executive Chef at Bai Wei?

The culinary journey at Bai Wei is led by Executive Chef Gee Kok Dan Danny, known for his expertise in regional Chinese cuisines.

### Are there vegetarian options available at Bai Wei?

Yes, Bai Wei Dining offers a variety of vegetarian dishes that highlight the rich flavors of regional Chinese cuisines.
