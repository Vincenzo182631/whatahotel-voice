## Discover exciting new perspectives on Hainan Island's south coast

From Raffles Hainan Clear Water Bay, on the south coast of China's smallest province, just across the Gulf of Tonkin from Vietnam, the views across the crystalline waters stretch out forever, beckoning you to explore. Let us take you on a magical safari by air, land and sea, to show you this tropical coastline from a uniquely Raffles perspective.

Come together with your loved ones, family or friends and join this vibrant three-night adventure. Take a helicopter flight over the gentle crescent of our famous beach; board a private charter for a two-hour yacht sail around the deep blue bay; and travel by limousine to the botanical gardens and a private photo shoot. Enjoy downtime in our spa, or on the top-flight neighbouring golf course. Relax in the glorious sunny climate and dine on the fine sands of our Singing Beach - all taken care of by your Raffles Butler and recorded for ever on your personalised vlog, if you wish. An escapade you will never forget.

Day 3: Daily breakfast for 2 adults and 2 children aged 12 and under (7.00am-11.00am). Yacht Club experience for one hour for 2 adults and 2 children. Family set menu at Baiwei Chinese restaurant for 2 adults and 2 children. Balinese massage 60mins for two. Enjoy a photo shoot experience, to take home 30 photos and a 15-second video.
