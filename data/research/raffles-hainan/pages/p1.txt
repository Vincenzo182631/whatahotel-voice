Exquisitely positioned on a crystalline seashore, Raffles Hainan Clear Water Bay is an oasis of luxurious comfort and style combining light, open Balinese-style architecture with an authentic local feel. All of our generously spacious rooms, suites and villas have views that stretch out forever across the glittering bay.

### Clear Water Bay Safari by Air, Land & Sea

Let us take you on a magical safari by air, land and sea, to reveal this tropical southern Chinese coastline and its 'singing sands' from the unique perspective of a Raffles Butler.

### Hainan Clear Water Bay Golf Club

Let us arrange for you to play, spend practice time at the driving range or organise a lesson with the pro.

Discover

### Nanwan Monkey Island

A state-protected nature reserve, Nanwan Monkey Island is home to 400 species of tropical plant, 100 animal species and a population of 2,500 macaque monkeys.

Discover

### Wuzhizhou Island

Just off our beautiful coastline, in the clear waters of the bay, sits Wuzhizhou Island. A perfect location for a swimming and diving trip, the island is a magnet for wildlife.

Discover

### Bespoke Spa Days

## An idyllic beachside escape

Beautifully located in the far warm south of China, on an island with an idyllic climate, magical coastline, championship golf and unique cultural connections, Raffles Hainan Clear Water Bay is the complete resort experience.

Dining

## The joy of a hundred flavours

Bai Wei restaurant – its name meaning ‘a hundred flavours’ in Chinese – is a showcase for China’s finest regional cuisines. Be charmed as you step into one of five elegant private dining rooms set in individual pavilions overlooking the resort's lotus ponds.
