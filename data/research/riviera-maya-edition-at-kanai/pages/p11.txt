# Services & Amenities

The first of its kind in Mexico, The Riviera Maya EDITION at Kanai introduces a completely new concept of laidback luxury to the Caribbean coastline.

### For further information

Please contact our team at guestexperience.rivieramaya@editionhotels.com or +52 984 122 4999.

SUSTAINABILITY

- Thoughtfully designed with a minimal construction footprint to protect and preserve the natural mangrove reserv
- Collaboration with local environmental authorities and the governmental protection agency to safeguard the surrounding natural ecosystem
- 2 property biologists to lead environmental protection of resort including the mangroves, the wetlands, the sea turtle conservation program, and the local flora and fauna
- Initiation of holistic sustainability program centered around 6 fundamental principles:
  - Flora Preservation
  - Fauna Conservation
  - Waste Management
  - Environmental Monitoring
  - Security Measures Regarding Wildlife
  - Guest and Staff Education
- Low energy consumption lighting throughout property
- Reusable glass water bottles in every room to adhere to EDITION’s Stay Plastic Free initiative
- Water is filtered on property in a controlled process, adhering to the highest quality standards
