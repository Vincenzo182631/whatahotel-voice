# Rooms

The 182 guest rooms, suites and one unique suite pay homage to the Riviera Maya’s rich heritage and the timeless sophistication of Ian Schrager’s visionary design. Each room is thoughtfully positioned to offer captivating views of the Caribbean and lush mangrove.

# Villa

From the Beachfront Villas, set steps from the sea to the Sky Villa, North America’s most expansive penthouse, each space defines modern luxury. Private plunge pools, panoramic views, and seamless indoor-outdoor living create two distinct yet equally elevated experiences.
