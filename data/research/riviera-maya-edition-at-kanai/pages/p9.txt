# The Spa

**The Spa**

Share

The holistic experience at The Riviera Maya EDITION at Kanai is completed through The Spa. Echoing the cenote-inspired architecture of our surrounding area, The Spa features a discovery journey of hydrotherapy pools, a private outdoor pool, a Turkish Hamman and a steam and chromotherapy treatment bed, alongside six private indoor-outdoor treatment rooms. The grounding experience extends to The Pool Bar, bringing you closer to your true self through health-conscious offerings that help you to continue your wellness journey by fostering your connection with nature and heightened sensory awareness.

DAILY9:00 AM - 7:00 PM

For additional information please contact us at spa.rivieramaya@editionhotels.com or +52 984 122 4999 ext 6485.

Your wellness journey continues whenever the mood strikes. The space features a fully equipped Techno-gym with personal flat screen TVs, a Spin Studio, free weights, a stretching area, complimentary towels, headphones and refreshments.
