# LAIDBACK LUXURY IN KANAI

The Riviera Maya EDITION at Kanai opened on February 27, 2024.

Sited on a 620-acre nature reserve with two miles of pristine white-sand beaches, The Riviera Maya EDITION at Kanai rises up from a forest of mangroves to introduce a completely new concept of sustainable luxury to the Caribbean coastline. Featuring 182 guest rooms and suites, a lagoon-sized pool, versatile meeting spaces, a state-of-the-art ballroom, and North America’s largest penthouse suite, Mexico’s first EDITION marries its signature brand of hospitality with the Yucatan Peninsula’s modern and ancient cultures.

A destination in and of itself, The Riviera Maya EDITION brings two of Mexico’s most renowned chef-restauranteurs to Kanai through five food and beverage venues including an innovative tasting menu by Chef Paco Ruano and a Mediterranean-inspired beach club by Chef Tomas Bermudez. Complete the holistic experience through a cenote-inspired destination spa to live in harmony with the surroundings of The Riviera Maya EDITION.

Designed with a minimal environmental footprint to protect and preserve Kanai’s ecosystem, the ancient Mayan land delivers nothing short of its name, meaning “Paradise on Earth”.

## HOME AWAY FROM HOME

Thoughtfully oriented to provide captivating views of the ocean and our lush mangrove forest, our 182 guest rooms and suites, including the largest penthouse in North America, The Sky Villa, pay homage to Riviera Maya’s rich heritage while reflecting the timeless sophistication of Ian Schrager’s visionary design. For those seeking a deeper connection to the sea, private terraces complete with plunge pools offer a secluded escape.

## UNFORGETTABLE IMPRESSIONS

Introducing a series of versatile event and meeting spaces to Kanai, The Riviera Maya EDITION sets the tone for state of the art venues complete with cutting-edge technology, natural light, 18-foot high ceilings, and indoor-outdoor features. From professional functions to extraordinary weddings, our team delivers exceptional experiences reflective of the hotel’s modern aesthetic among an ecological oasis.
