# Welcome to W Montreal

## Discover our vibrant luxury hotel in Montreal

Get swept up by the downtown dynamic of W Montreal, defying your definition of play and amplifying your lust for life in Canada's second-most populous city. We have transformed the co-living spaces in our luxury hotel in Old Town, Montreal, and recharged them with our own vibe. With a foot in historic Old Montreal and a finger on the pulse of the downtown scene, W Montreal sits in its own unique space....See More Follow the beat of our Living Room, a coworking space designed to boost focus and creativity. When the days are done, the space turns into a mingling bar. Tune in to our Living Room series of music gatherings and live DJs every week. Discover the cocktail culture and cuisine infused with Montreal flavors at our hotel restaurant and bar. Call in our Whatever/Whenever Service to get whatever you need, whenever you need it. Go further and connect with our W Insider to gain access to off-the-map attractions. Our downtown Montreal luxury hotel offers everything you need for an unforgettable getaway.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Montreal?

The check-in time at W Montreal is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Montreal allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Montreal. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Montreal?

On-site valet parking is available at W Montreal. The daily valet fee is $58.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Montreal have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Montreal has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Montreal booking.

What is the closest airport near W Montreal?

The closest airport near W Montreal is Montreal Saint-Hubert Longueuil Airport (YHU). YHU is located approximately 11.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Montreal have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Montreal has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
