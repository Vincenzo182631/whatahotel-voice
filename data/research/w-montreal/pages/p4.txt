# Drink downtown at our Montreal hotspot

## Taste

At W Montréal, every bite and every sip tell a story — bold, stylish, and rooted in the city’s energy. Enjoy Italiano-Mediterranean cuisine at Tbsp., our signature hotel restaurant in downtown Montreal, where zero-waste cooking and fresh local ingredients take center stage. Slide into Living Room for high-end mixology and creative cocktail experiences in a dynamic downtown setting. Or take it underground...See More at Vinyl Bar, our DJ-powered speakeasy, for beats, bites, and elevated drinks. ## La Tavolata at Tbsp.

Inspired by the Italian tradition of gathering around the table, La Tavolata at Tbsp. is a shared dining experience designed to bring people together. Enjoy a generous selection of chef-curated starters, mains, and sides served family-style, encouraging conversation, connection, and discovery with every course.

### Made To Share

### Sound, Poured Neat

Hidden behind an unmarked door, Vinyl Bar is an immersive speakeasy where analog sound and mixology collide. Explore a collection of over 3,000 vinyl records, sip expertly crafted cocktails, and become part of the soundtrack by handing your favorite record to the DJ.

### Where the Night Begins

The heartbeat of W Montréal, the Living Room evolves throughout the day—from a refined lounge for casual meetings and cocktails to a vibrant social hub as the evening unfolds. Curated soundtracks, handcrafted drinks, and the city's creative energy set the scene for unforgettable moments.

## In-Room Dining
