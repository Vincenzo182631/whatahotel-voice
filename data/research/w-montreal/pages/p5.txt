### Mural Festival

Every summer, the MURAL Festival turns Saint-Laurent boulevard into an open-air gallery. Even off-season, Montreal buzzes with bold murals, urban art, and an ever-changing creative landscape.

Learn More

### Osheaga

Montreal’s biggest music festival takes over Parc Jean-Drapeau every summer. Osheaga blends global headliners, emerging artists, and visual art installations in one electrifying weekend.

Learn More

### Phi Centre

Located in Old Montreal, the Phi Centre brings together art, tech, and immersive creativity. From virtual reality to cutting-edge installations, it’s a space that constantly redefines the art experience.

### Igloofest

More Local Area & Activities

- **Old Montreal**
- Old Montreal is the true heart of the city, and one of the oldest urban areas in North America; a visit to this enchanting district should be at the top of your list of things to do. Stroll along cobblestone streets, and discover buildings dating back to the 17th century.
- **Montreal Museum of Fine Arts**
- Visit the Canada's largest museum spread across four pavilions. Located on Montreal's historic Golden Square Mile, the Montreal Museum of Fine Arts is home to over 41,000 works of art, including those by Picasso, Renoir, Monet, and Rembrandt.
- **Bota Bota**
- Spa, body and facial treatments are offered at the Bota Bota in the old port of Montreal. Welcome inside an invented world, where "floating" is the key word.
- **Spade and Palacio**
- Non-Touristy Tour. Small groups, no script, just Montréal.
- **Grand Prix Weekend**
- Be in the heart of the action! Get ready for Grand Prix Weekend at W Montréal. Step inside the worlds of Design, Music & Fashion and experience the city’s pulsating atmosphere during Formula 1 Weekend.
- **Shopping**
- Indulge in a day of unparalleled retail therapy. Fantastic shopping centres are located throughout the city. Browse the shops along Rue St. Catherine, or explore the world's largest Underground City, which connects shopping centres all over.
