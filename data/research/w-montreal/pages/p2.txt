### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Montreal have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Montreal have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Montreal?

The largest occupancy room at W Montreal is the Premier Deluxe with a maximum occupancy of 4.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Montreal?

No, the rooms at W Montreal do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at W Montreal have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Montreal have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at W Montreal have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Montreal have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Montreal have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Montreal have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Montreal have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Montreal have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
