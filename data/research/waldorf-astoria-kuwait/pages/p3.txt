# Ikaros Club

Ikaros Club

Our guests staying in our Ikaros Club rooms, Ikaros Club Suites, Presidential Suite, and Royal Suite, can enjoy exclusive access to The Ikaros Club. Unwind and relax in this majestic and luxurious setting. The Ikaros Club offers an international culinary experience, including breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea, dinner, dessert, and complimentary beverages.

## Exclusive Benefits

Enjoy many benefits, such as private check-in/out, a complimentary selection of international newspapers and magazines, high-speed Internet, and a private meeting room for every business need.

Ikaros Club’s dedicated Personal Concierge will anticipate and understand your individual requirements and will be on hand to provide travel assistance and bookings across the city.
