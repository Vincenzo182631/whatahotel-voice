# Dining & Drinks

Dining & Drinks

From afternoon tea with a dash of Old World nostalgia to modern Japanese cuisine in a warm, contemporary setting, we offer unforgettable dining experiences to suit every mood and palate.

## Ava

Chefs prepare fresh and light fare with Mediterranean flair at this stylish eatery that showcases the captivating flavors of the French Riviera, Southern Italy, and the Greek islands.

Discover Ava

## Peacock Alley

Drawing inspiration from stylish cafes in Paris and New York, Peacock Alley serves fresh salads, gourmet sandwiches, exquisite chocolates, and macarons in an elegant setting.

Explore Peacock Alley

## ROKA

This award-winning restaurant’s modern Japanese cuisine features sushi, sashimi, and dishes prepared over hot coals on a traditional robata grill.

Explore ROKA

## In-Room Dining

In the privacy of your room, enjoy breakfast in bed, a light lunch, an intimate dinner, or midnight snacks. Our extensive in-room dining menu is available in every room.

## Cabanas

Our outdoor cabanas offer guests unforgettable experiences, whether it’s for family and friends gatherings, romantic dinners, or a cozy café setting.

_Hours: 9 am – midnight_
