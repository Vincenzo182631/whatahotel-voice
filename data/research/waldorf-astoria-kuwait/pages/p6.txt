## Activity Overview

### Al Shaheed Park

Al Shaheed Park is one of Kuwait City’s most ambitious urban destinations, combining landscaped gardens, cultural attractions, recreation and entertainment. Its earlier phases offer walking and jogging paths, museums, water features and beautifully designed gardens, while the newly opened Phase 3 adds an entirely new dimension.

Visitors can skate at the Olympic-sized ice rink, experience Snow World, race electric karts, try indoor skydiving, or explore the skateboarding, parkour and outdoor adventure areas. The 2.4-kilometre walking and running track extends through the new development, alongside family play areas, a maze and landscaped spaces.

Phase 3 also introduces Al Soor Theatre, a 2,300-seat Roman-style venue, an outdoor cinema and spaces for concerts, performances and cultural events. With cafés, entertainment and activities for all ages, Al Shaheed Park has evolved into a destination where visitors can easily spend several hours.

**Open from 5 pm to 11 pm\| Monday – Sunday**

_Best enjoyed from late afternoon into the evening, when the gardens, architecture and illuminated pathways create a particularly atmospheric setting._

Entry FeeFree

### Abdullah Al Salem Cultural Centre

Abdullah Al Salem Cultural Centre is one of Kuwait City’s most impressive cultural destinations, bringing together art, science, history and innovation within a striking contemporary setting. The expansive complex features thoughtfully curated museums and interactive exhibitions that explore Kuwait’s heritage, the natural world, space, science and global cultures.

Modern architecture, landscaped spaces, and engaging displays create an experience that is both educational and visually compelling. More than a traditional museum, the centre serves as a vibrant hub for discovery and cultural exchange. For travelers seeking to understand Kuwait beyond its modern skyline, Abdullah Al Salem Cultural Centre offers an insightful and memorable introduction to the country’s cultural landscape.

Open from 9 pm to 7 pm \| Monday – Saturday

### Alive Yoga

Alive Yoga is a welcoming yoga and wellness studio in Kuwait, offering a range of practices designed to improve strength, flexibility, balance, and mindful movement. With classes suitable for different experience levels, the studio provides a supportive environment for both beginners and experienced practitioners.

The studio focuses on creating a holistic experience that connects movement, breath, and mental well-being, making it a suitable choice for travelers looking to maintain their wellness routine while visiting Kuwait.
