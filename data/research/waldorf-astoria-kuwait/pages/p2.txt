# Rooms & Suites

Rooms & Suites

Blending timeless elegance with a modern sensibility, our 200 well-appointed rooms and suites offer tranquil spaces to recharge after a day of work or play.

## Rooms

Our spacious rooms are outfitted with modern comforts to ensure a relaxing stay. All rooms feature complimentary WiFi, 65-inch HDTVs, espresso machines, and 400-thread-count sheets.

## Suites

Experience a taste of luxury in one of our stylish suites, which feature separate areas for work or entertaining and relaxation. Suites feature complimentary WiFi, HDTVs, and elegant and spacious bathrooms.

## Ikaros Club

Reserve an Ikaros Club room or suite, the Presidential Suite, or the Royal Suite, and enjoy exclusive access to The Ikaros Club.
