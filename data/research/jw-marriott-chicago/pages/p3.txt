### Frequently Asked Questions

What restaurants does JW Marriott Chicago have on property?

The on-property restaurants at JW Marriott Chicago are:

Lobby BarHenriettaSavor by JW (in-room dining)The Executive LoungeJW Cafe

Does JW Marriott Chicago offer room service?

Yes, room service is available at JW Marriott Chicago.

What cuisine is offered at JW Marriott Chicago?

The cuisines offered at JW Marriott Chicago are:

Italian at HenriettaAmerican at Lobby Bar, Savor by JW (in-room dining), The Executive Lounge, JW Cafe

Does JW Marriott Chicago offer breakfast?

Yes, breakfast is available onsite at Henrietta, Savor by JW (in-room dining), The Executive Lounge, and JW Cafe for a fee.
