### Spring Getaways & Long Holiday Weekends

As Chicago emerges from winter, spring brings renewed energy, cultural highlights, and ideal long weekends. Plan a getaway around Memorial Day and enjoy blooming parks, river walks, theatre season, and outdoor dining. JW Marriott Chicago’s prime Loop location offers a refined home base, enhanced with romantic, spa, and wellness packages.

### Summer Holidays & Iconic Chicago Escapes

Summer is Chicago’s most vibrant season—defined by lakefront living, music festivals, and extended daylight. Plan your stay around Memorial Day, Fourth of July, or Labor Day, and experience iconic events like outdoor concerts, neighborhood festivals, river cruises, and rooftop dining. Spacious Luxury Suites accommodating up to four adults offer room to relax and celebrate between unforgettable summer days.

### Fall Holidays & Seasonal Celebrations

Fall in Chicago offers cooler temperatures, rich cultural programming, and perfectly timed long weekends. Ideal for Labor Day extensions, Columbus Day, anniversaries, birthdays, and milestone moments, the season features theater openings, food festivals, architectural tours, and crisp city exploring. JW Marriott Chicago provides an elevated setting for meaningful fall escapes in the heart of downtown.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Chicago have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Chicago offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Chicago have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Chicago has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does JW Marriott Chicago have an on-site spa?

Yes, Spa at JW Chicago is located on-site at JW Marriott Chicago. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Chicago?

Chicago Riverwalk, Museum of Contemporary Art (1.9 Miles), Griffin Museum of Science & Industry (7.7 Miles), Navy Pier (2.1 Miles), Shedd Aquarium (2.1 Miles) and more are nearby JW Marriott Chicago.
