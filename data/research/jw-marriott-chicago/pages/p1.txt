### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Chicago?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Chicago is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Chicago allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Chicago.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Chicago?

Off-site valet parking is available at JW Marriott Chicago. The daily valet fee is $88.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Chicago have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Chicago has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Chicago booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Chicago?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Chicago is Chicago Midway International Airport (MDW). MDW is located approximately 11.1 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Chicago have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Chicago does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Chicago have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Chicago has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
