### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Chicago have a work space?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Chicago have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Chicago?

The largest occupancy room at JW Marriott Chicago is the 1 Bedroom Suite with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Chicago?

Some rooms at JW Marriott Chicago permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
