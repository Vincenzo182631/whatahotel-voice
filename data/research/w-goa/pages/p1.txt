# Welcome to W Goa

## Escape to the edge at our hotel in Goa

Set on Vagator Beach, W Goa places you at the heart of North Goa’s vibrant energy, with the Arabian Sea on one side and the rhythm of the destination on the other. Designed for those seeking a luxury escape, W Goa blends bold design, immersive experiences and the free spirit of Goa. Chase sunsets at ROCKPOOL, catch the beat at WET®, or explore the destination at your own pace. Recharge at AWAY® Spa...See More with cryotherapy, sound healing and restorative therapies. Rally at the Padel & Pickle Jungle, power through a workout at FIT®, or simply unwind by the sea. Savour distinctive dining experiences, from contemporary Chinese flavours at Spice Traders to relaxed all-day dining at The Kitchen Table. Retreat to stylish rooms, suites, villas or chalets, step onto your Portuguese-inspired balcony and let the outdoors in. More than a stay, W Goa is a destination without a script, offering endless ways to play, pause and explore. Come for Goa. Stay for the W Goa State of Mind.See Less

### The Ultimate Sunset Destination in Goa

### Spice Traders

Spice Traders is an inspired modern Chinese restaurant drawing from the philosophies of China's Eight Great Cuisines. A social dining destination, it pairs bold flavors with inventive cocktails, capturing the vibrant W energy in every plate and pour.

Explore

### The Kitchen Table

Ignite your appetite at The Kitchen Table. Located by the pool, adjacent to WET®, our indoor/outdoor, all-day restaurant transforms local ingredients into hip riffs on international faves. Grab a bite, soak up the Goa sun, and sip a drink from the bar.

Explore

### Rockpool

Experience ROCKPOOL, perched on a stunning cliffside with sweeping views of the Arabian Sea. As the sun dips below the horizon, sip on signature cocktails with live music setting the vibe at this Goa cocktail bar.

Explore

### WOOBAR

Lounge by day and dance by night at WOOBAR. By day, soak up the sun with fresh cocktails and bar bites. As the sun sets, the energy rises with live DJ beats and vibrant vibes. Laid-back afternoons turn into unforgettable nights at this Goa cocktail bar.

Explore

### Sylvia

While our ground floor speaks a flamboyant language over food at Spice Traders, Sylvia up-above lures you towards a more daring and magical atmosphere with forged cocktails and botanicals that linger on your palate long after your visit to Sylvia.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Goa?

The check-in time at W Goa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Goa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Goa. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 15.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of ₹7000.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Goa?

On-site self parking is available at W Goa. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near W Goa. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Goa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Goa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Goa booking.

What is the closest airport near W Goa?

The closest airport near W Goa is Manohar International Airport (GOX). GOX is located approximately 37.8 KM from the hotel.

Does W Goa have an airport shuttle to Goa International Airport (GOI)?

Yes, W Goa has an airport shuttle to Goa International Airport (GOI).

Does W Goa have an airport shuttle to Manohar International Airport (GOX)?

Yes, W Goa has an airport shuttle to Manohar International Airport (GOX).

Does W Goa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Goa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
