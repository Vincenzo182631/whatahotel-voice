# Relax in Our Vagator Beach Villas

## Accommodations

Stay close to the energy. Step away when you need to. Perched above Vagator Beach, W Goa's 121 rooms, suites, villas and chalets make space for every version of Goa, where the Arabian Sea, coastal air and the spirit of the destination are never far away. This is luxury hotel accommodation in North Goa that moves to its own rhythm.

### Oasis Villa II

More room to stay. More room to connect. Featuring two bedrooms and a separate living room, Oasis Villa II balances shared moments with space to unwind at your own pace.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Goa have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Goa have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Goa?

The largest occupancy room at W Goa is the Oasis Villa II with a maximum occupancy of 5.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Goa?

Some rooms at W Goa permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Goa have fridges?

No, rooms at W Goa do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W Goa have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at W Goa do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Goa have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Goa have hair dryers.
