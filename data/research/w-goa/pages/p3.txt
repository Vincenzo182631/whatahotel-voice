# Unique and Delicious Restaurants in Goa

## Dining

Satisfy your appetite at our resort in Goa. Good food, great company and a reason to stay a little longer. Spice Traders serves modern Chinese flavours with sweeping views of the Arabian Sea. The Kitchen Table keeps the day moving with poolside favourites and comfort-led classics. WOOBAR® and the Living Room set the scene for cocktails, conversation and whatever comes next. Up on the cliffs of Vagator...See More Beach, ROCKPOOL takes over at sunset with handcrafted cocktails, sea views and energy that lingers late into the evening.
