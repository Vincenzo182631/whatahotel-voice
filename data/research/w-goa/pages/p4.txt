# Discover Our Hotel Near Orzan Beach, Goa

## Experiences

Looking for a quieter side of Goa? Start with a sunrise at Vagator Beach or a walk up to Chapora Fort. When you're ready for more, North Goa unfolds in every direction, from local markets and coastal trails to water sports, beachside cafés and cultural landmarks. As the sun goes down, the energy shifts. Restaurants fill up, music drifts through the air and the coastline takes on a different rhythm....See More Among the most distinctive Goa hotels near Vagator Beach, W Goa puts you close to the experiences that make the destination impossible to do just once. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Goa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Goa offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Treadmill, Cross Trainer, Exercise Cycle, Lat Pulldown, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Goa have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Goa has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Vitality Pool (Indoor)WET® Outdoor Pool (Outdoor)Rockpool (Outdoor)

Does W Goa have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY® SPA is located on-site at W Goa. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Goa?

Museum of Goa MOG, The Goa Collective Bazaar, Basilica of Bom Jesus, Goya The Nightclub, Thalassa and more are nearby W Goa.
