Located on the 21st floor, Absheron Spa offers breathtaking views of Baku city and the Caspian Sea. Immerse yourself at our Spa with luxuriously appointed treatment rooms and artfully crafted signature rituals. Embedded in Absheron spa and enhanced by indigenous elements there are 5 treatment rooms, one of which is designed for couple treatments, steam room, Finnish sauna, indoor swimming pool and state-of-the-art fitness center.

The gift vouchers are available to help a loved one unwind, with a chance to spend on our retail products as well as our treatments.

### Swimming Pool

Our top-floor heated swimming pool with jacuzzi is a real sanctuary within the urban buzz of the city's business district. Indoor pool with floor-to-ceiling windows is designed especially to bring out all the beauties Baku has to offer.

Area & Activites

### Old City

Classified as a World Heritage Site by UNESCO, old town was once the medieval capital of the Shirvan Shahs. Today, it’s the capital’s heart with Maiden Tower, traditional carpet weavers, bars and restaurants.

### Fountain Square

The pedestrianized Fountain Square, is one of the must-see Baku attractions. Explore dozens of fountains filling the area stretching from Nizami Street to the eastern walls of Icheri Scherer.

### Government House

Located just opposite the hotel, the opulent baroque-style Government House on Neftchiler Avenue was built in 1952. The main attraction is the architectural beauty, especially when it lights up at night.

Local Area & Activity Highlights

### Baku Boulevard Promenade

Baku Boulevard promenade hugs the Caspian Sea with an amusement park, bars and cafés along with a cacti garden, Mini-Venice, and the Baku Eye. Beginning at National Flag Square and ending at White City Boulevard, this is the best spot for views of the Flame Towers and a relaxing stroll along the coast.

### Heydar Aliyev Center

Late architect Zaha Hadid’s design for the Heydar Aliyev Centre won the Design Museum’s 2014 Design of the Year Award, and is famed for its fluid, wave-like form. Visit Treasures of Azerbaijan exhibition, which showcases the best of Azerbaijani culture.
