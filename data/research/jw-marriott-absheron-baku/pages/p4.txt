## Sustainability

JW Marriott Absheron Baku has implemented significant measures to underscore sustainability and promote the flourishing of our environment. To address the critical issue of sustainability, our culinary team has embraced a conscientious approach to minimize waste in our kitchens. We have set an ambitious target of reducing food waste by 50% by the year 2025, and we believe this goal can only be achieved through collective responsibility. Together, we have the power to make a substantial impact on the future of our planet. By joining forces with our valued guests and staff, we can forge a sustainable path forward and create a brighter future for generations to come.
