Boston’s Best Experiences, From Boston Common to Freedom Trail
Experiences
Located in downtown Boston, The Ritz-Carlton, Boston offers hotel guests a wealth of nearby attractions. History can be found around every corner, starting in Boston Common across from the hotel. The 50-acre park the oldest in the country and is the beginning of the Freedom Trail, home to many of Boston’s beloved historic sites. The hotel is also near the Theater District and Financial District, letting you stroll to your meeting or to a performance at the nearby Citizens Bank Opera House.
Ritz Kids
At The Ritz-Carlton, Boston, family vacations are opportunities to inspire and remember. This is especially true for young guests, who are greeted with a red wagon full of toys that they can enjoy during their stay. This magical introduction to the hotel sets the tone for a family-friendly experience enhanced by the special amenities, treats and kid-friendly menus of the Ritz Kids program.

Things to Do
New England Aquarium
Conveniently located on Boston’s waterfront, the New England Aquarium features more than 20,000 animals of 600 different species.
http://www.neaq.org/index.php
Boston Duck Tour
"One big splash and the rest is history!" This fully narrated historic tour takes place in authentic WWI amphibious vehicles through the streets of Boston and into the Charles River.
http://www.bostonducktours.com/
Boston Children’s Museum
Boston Children’s Museum’s exhibits and programs emphasize hands-on engagement and learning through experience, employing play as a tool to spark the inherent creativity, curiosity, and imagination of children.
http://www.bostonchildrensmuseum.org/
Museum of Science
The Science Museum is a world-renowned educational institution with over 400 interactive participatory exhibits featuring live animal and physical science demonstrations, courses, traveling exhibits, a computer center, the Mugar Omni Theater, and Charles Hayden Planetarium.
http://www.mos.org/
Freedom Trail
Boston's Freedom Trail is a red (mostly brick) path through downtown Boston which leads to sixteen significant historical sites. The 2.5 mile (4 km) walk from the Boston Common to the Bunker Hill Monument in Charlestown is popular with tourists. Part of the Freedom Trail overlaps with the much longer Boston Harbor Walk. The Freedom Trail is a unit of Boston National Historical Parks.
http://www.thefreedomtrail.org/
Immerse Yourself in Boston’s Cultural Legacy From This Theater District Hotel
When it comes to offering authentic experiences of the city, The Ritz-Carlton, Boston remains unsurpassed. Take advantage of the hotel’s downtown location to walk to the Theater District and indulge in world-class performances at the Boch Center. Watch history come alive along the Freedom Trail, or gaze upon 19th-century architecture in Back Bay. From the arts to modern entertainment, shopping to national treasures, Boston awaits your discovery.

Equinox Sports Club
At the adjoining Equinox Sports Club, hotel guests enjoy access to 114,000 square feet of sunlit space featuring state-of-the-art equipment and a wide range of fitness and wellness programs. Challenge yourself on the full basketball court or one of four international squash courts, then refresh with a swim in the 25-yard indoor pool. Guests 18 and over may access the Club; admission is complimentary with Club Lounge access or available for $20 per day.
