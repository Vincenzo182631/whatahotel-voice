Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Boston
Luxury Hotel in Boston, MA
Perfectly situated in the heart of downtown Boston, The Ritz-Carlton, Boston offers guests an unmatched blend of refinement and comfort. Recognized as a historic hotel in Boston, MA, our property reflects a legacy that has flourished since 1927, continually redefining luxurious hospitality. As a luxury hotel in Boston travelers seek, we offer 193 lavishly appointed rooms and suites with panoramic city views. Guests searching for a pet-friendly hotel in Boston, MA will appreciate our welcoming accommodations for both comfort and convenience. Relax in our downtown Boston hotel suites in downtown Boston and enjoy versatile meeting spaces for events and celebrations. Savor New England-inspired cuisine at Artisan Bistro or craft cocktails at Avery Bar in our sophisticated lobby lounge.
Rooms and Suites
Take in the spectacular views of Boston’s skyline and the historic Boston Common from our stylish rooms and suites while relishing the contemporary design inspired by the region. The Ritz-Carlton, Boston makes a big impression with our expansive views, comfortable interiors and residential-like suites.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Boston?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Boston is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Boston allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Boston. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 60.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Boston?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Boston. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Boston, with rates of $16.00 per hour, and $70.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $70.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Boston have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Boston has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Boston booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Boston?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Boston is Boston Logan International Airport (BOS). BOS is located approximately 3.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Boston have an airport shuttle to Boston Logan International Airport (BOS)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Boston does not have an airport shuttle to Boston Logan International Airport.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Boston have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Boston does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Boston have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Boston offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
