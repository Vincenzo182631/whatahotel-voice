Dining
The Ritz-Carlton, Boston brings sumptuous sophistication to the downtown Boston restaurant and bar scene. Our hotel's Artisan Bistro and Avery Bar feel airy and modern while menus showcasing farm-to-table fare, signature martinis, and handcrafted cocktails create a sense of approachable authenticity.

Private Dining
For intimate occasions, special celebrations, or entertaining corporate clients, savor your meal in the semi-private dining area of Artisan Bistro. We’ll take the time to carefully consider every aspect of your dining experience, giving you the space to enjoy a decadent dining experience with your guests. A few of the personalized services we offer include personalized menu cards for each guest, customized menus for your special occasion, and exceptional wine pairing recommendations from our Restaurant Manager.
