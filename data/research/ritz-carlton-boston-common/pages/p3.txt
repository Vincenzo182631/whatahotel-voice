An Elevated Stay in Our Most Distinguished Accommodations
Signature Accommodations
The Premier Suites at The Ritz-Carlton, Boston offer an elevated residential-style stay, defined by expansive layouts, refined furnishings, and sweeping views of the city skyline and Boston Common. Thoughtfully designed living and sleeping areas create a seamless balance of comfort and sophistication, inviting guests to settle in with ease while enjoying the highest level of personalized service. For booking inquiries, please contact Hilary Green at Hilary.Green@ritzcarlton.com
The Presidential Suite
The One-Bedroom Presidential Suite is the hotel’s largest accommodation, offering 1,680 square feet of expansive space with sweeping views of Boston Common and the city skyline. Designed for both entertaining and privacy, the suite features a generous living and dining area, a private study, and two-and-a-half bathrooms. The living room is centered around a gas fireplace, with floor-to-ceiling windows framing the city below. A dining table for six and an adjoining butler’s kitchen create an ideal setting for in-suite dining or private gatherings. For added flexibility, the suite may be adjoined to a Deluxe King guest room. Suite accommodations include access to the Club Lounge.

Luxury Parkview Suites
The Park View Suites offer a refined residential-style stay overlooking Boston’s oldest park. Featuring approximately 1,100 square feet of thoughtfully designed space, each suite includes a separate living room and study, a dedicated dining area, and a king bedroom with a sofa bed. One-and-a-half bathrooms, including a whirlpool tub, provide added comfort, while expansive windows frame serene park views. Suite accommodations include access to the Club Lounge.
