# Alila Moments

## Alila Moments

Journey into the living pulse of the land through our signature, immersive encounters. An Alila Moment is more than a point in time; it is a gateway to presence, a bridge between the destination’s spirit and your own. By weaving storytelling and ritual into the fabric of the senses, we create experiential narratives that transcend the temporary, becoming a permanent part of who you are.

### WINDS OF RENEWAL

A sacred odyssey toward the center of the soul, where presence is reclaimed and self-love is restored. The journey begins within the hush of a private sanctuary, as shared intentions weave a collective spirit. Here, you shall compose a mandala of the earth before treading a secluded path, guided by the ethereal resonance of healing melodies that draw the gaze inward.

The trail unfolds into the light of an open cenote for a Mayan water blessing—a communion with the source of all renewal. In the velvet silence that follows, you are led to a hidden cenote for a golden honey ritual and a transformative immersion. As the ceremony closes with a gratitude to the four horizons, you emerge grounded in reverence: a profound remembrance that love is not sought, but embodied, softened and eternally renewed.

### EARTH & CLAY

Enter a sanctuary of deep healing where the Earth’s bounty meets the sweetness of the soul. As the ceremony reaches its climax, you are guided to the ocean for a ritual immersion—a moment of intentional release. In these waters, physical and emotional burdens dissolve, making way for a profound sense of self-acceptance and a body grounded in the perfection of the natural world.

### SACRED FIRE

A ceremonial encounter centered on fire as a living force - source of life, transformation, and renewal.

Rooted in ancestral traditions of the Anáhuac, this experience honors fire not as a symbol, but as a presence: a giver of warmth, light, and continuity. Across cultures, fire has been recognized as one of the essential elements that sustain life on Earth-an energy that transforms, purifies, and connects.

Held in an intimate outdoor setting, guests gather around the flame and are gently guided into a space of awareness and respect. Through the warmth of the fire, moments of stillness, and intentional offering, participants are invited to reflect, release, and reconnect with their own inner vitality.

This is not presented as a traditional rite, but as a contemporary ceremonial experience-where fire becomes a mirror for transformation, a witness to intention, and a reminder of the life force that moves through all things. Guests leave with a sense of grounding, clarity, and connection, carried by the quiet power of the element itself.

**Location**: On property

**Additional cost** applies

**Dress code**: Resort casual

### IXCHEL WATER BLESSING

A sacred tribute to the divine feminine, the Ixchel water purification is a transformative and tender immersion into the Mayan worldview, celebrating water as the sacred, feminine source of all life, governed by the goddess Ixchel (patron of water, the moon and healing). Through a gentle ritual utilizing soft elements like water, clay vessels, flowers and the ancestral chants of the Mayan healer, guests are wrapped in a loving embrace, allowing them to feel nurtured and accepted as children of Mother Earth. This profound journey fosters deep personal purification, self-acceptance and energetic renewal, while connecting participants to the wisdom of the lunar cycles. By reflecting on nature's constant phases of rest, death and rebirth, guests gain resilience and understand that there is a perfect time for every stage of their own personal growth.

**Location**: On property
