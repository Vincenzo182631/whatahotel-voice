Set in the heart of Mexico's Riviera Maya, Alila Mayakoba is defined by two distinct yet seamlessly connected luxury resort experiences. The Beach is Alila's Caribbean expression where the mood is social, vibrant and anchored by a lively beach club, taqueria and oceanfront dining. The Lagoon is Alila at its purest, a nature-led, wellness-focused sanctuary where mangroves, Spa Alila and immersive culinary experiences create a slower, quieter and more intimate retreat. Guests move effortlessly between both worlds from the comfort of their thoughtfully designed suites, making Alila Mayakoba the only resort in the Alila portfolio where both expressions coexist in one place.
