# Drink & Dine

Taste the authentic and fresh flavors of the cuisine of the Riviera Maya and beyond in our indoor and outdoor, chef-driven dining venues. From casual poolside snacks and drinks to elegant atelier experiences, our eco-conscious restaurants and bars will take you on a gastronomic adventure, relying on farm-to-table ingredients and exacting execution, through traditional Mexican recipes and global influences.

## Casa Amate

Once the residence of the De León family, this historic lagoon-side property now houses a restaurant. Shaped by journeys across Latin America, its rooms hold a collection of flavours, art, objects, and memories, centred around the amate tree at its heart. At the table, Latin American cuisine meets distinctive regional flavours and an exceptional wine collection.

## El Huerto/Chef’s Atelier

A thoughtfully appointed home-style kitchen sits beside a dedicated herb garden, offering an intimate look at Alila Mayakoba’s culinary and beverage story. Guests join the team to cook, taste, mix, and discover through hands-on cooking and mixology, tastings, and chef encounters. Each experience explores the ingredients, techniques, producers, and traditions that shape the resort’s culinary identity.

## Na Cocina Local

Yucatán cuisine from southeastern Mexico is prepared in an open kitchen, using local ingredients, bright citrus, earthy achiote, and slow-roasted techniques. Beside the lagoon and surrounded by mangroves, the restaurant offers a contemporary take on the region, connected to its traditions and the land around Alila Mayakoba.

## Ninguno Taquería

Ninguno captures the easy going spirit of a neighbourhood Mexican taquería. Guests tick their choices on a simple checkbox menu before settling in for classic tacos, micheladas, aguas frescas, and margaritas. Colourful, informal, and a little messy in the best way, Ninguno is about great tacos, relaxed service, lively music, and the easy energy of the Mexican Caribbean coast.

## Ascua Mezcalería

Tucked by the lagoon, The Fire of Agave is Na Restaurant’s dedicated mezcalería. Its selection of small-batch artisanal mezcals is constantly changing: each expression is available by the bottle, and once it’s gone, a new one takes its place. Guests can explore different agaves, regions, and producers alongside Pica Pica snacks and open-air fire pits.
