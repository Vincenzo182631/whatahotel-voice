## More Than a Brooklyn Hotel Room

Your room is only the beginning of a stay at The William Vale.

Start the morning with coffee and breakfast at Little Fino, spend summer afternoons beside Vale Pool, sit down to Southern Italian dining at Leuca, or head 22 floors above Brooklyn for cocktails and Manhattan skyline views at Westlight.

Beyond the hotel, Williamsburg puts some of Brooklyn’s best dining, shopping, nightlife, and waterfront spaces within easy reach. Explore the neighborhood during the day, then return to your private balcony and watch New York change as the sun goes down.

## Brooklyn Hotel Rooms FAQs

Do rooms at The William Vale have private balconies?

Yes. Every guest room and suite at The William Vale features a private balcony, giving guests their own outdoor space during their stay in Williamsburg.

Which rooms at The William Vale have Manhattan views?

Several accommodations are specifically designed around Manhattan skyline views, including the Manhattan View Queen, Manhattan View King, and Manhattan View Corner Suite. Review individual room details when booking to find the view that best suits your stay.

Does The William Vale have hotel suites in Brooklyn?

Yes. The William Vale offers several Brooklyn hotel suites, including the Junior King Suite, Manhattan View Corner Suite, King One Bedroom Suite, and Vale Garden Residence.

Which room is best for a couples' getaway?

Couples looking for a special stay can choose from rooms and suites with private balconies and Manhattan views, with larger suites providing additional living space for longer stays and celebrations.

Does The William Vale offer accessible hotel rooms?

Yes. Accessible rooms and suites are available across several accommodation categories, including King and Queen rooms, Junior King Suites, Manhattan View Corner Suites, One Bedroom Suites, and the Vale Garden Residence.

What can hotel guests enjoy at The William Vale?

Guests can start the morning with coffee and breakfast at Little Fino, dine at Leuca, enjoy cocktails and skyline views at Westlight, and make the most of hotel amenities and seasonal experiences such as Vale Pool.
