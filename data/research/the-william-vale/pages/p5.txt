## Italian Cafe Culture Meets Brooklyn

Little Fino takes inspiration from the rhythm of Italian café culture while feeling right at home in North Williamsburg.

“Fino” means “until” in Italian, and the cafe is designed for the different ways a New York morning can unfold. Grab coffee and a pastry from the counter before heading out, meet over breakfast, or take a seat and give yourself a little longer before the day begins.

## Little Fino FAQs

What is Little Fino?

Little Fino is a morning cafe at The William Vale in Williamsburg, Brooklyn, serving La Colombe coffee, Italian-inspired pastries, pressed juices, and breakfast.

Does Little Fino serve coffee and breakfast?

Yes. Little Fino serves La Colombe coffee alongside freshly baked pastries, pressed juices, and a dine-in breakfast menu.

Can you visit Little Fino without staying at The William Vale?

Yes. Little Fino welcomes both guests staying at The William Vale and visitors looking for coffee or breakfast in Williamsburg.

What other restaurants are at The William Vale?

The William Vale is also home to Leuca, serving Southern Italian cuisine, and Westlight, the hotel’s rooftop destination overlooking Brooklyn and the Manhattan skyline.

What are Little Fino's hours?

Little Fino is currently open daily from 7am to 11am. Check current opening hours before visiting.
