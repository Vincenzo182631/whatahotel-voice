Discover Brooklyn hotel amenities designed for every season, from rooftop pool days and wellness experiences to fitness, coworking, and pet-friendly stays. An Urban Oasis

## Vale Park

Escape the pace of the city at Vale Park, our elevated green space featuring 15,000 square feet of landscaped gardens, open lawns, and skyline views. It’s a peaceful retreat in the heart of Williamsburg where guests can relax, take in the scenery, and enjoy one of Brooklyn’s most welcoming outdoor spaces for both travelers and their dogs.

## FAQs

What wellness experiences are available at The William Vale?

Wellness is woven throughout the guest experience, from seasonal offerings like Winter Spa to personalized skincare treatments at Williamsburg Beauty Spa. Guests can also maintain their routine at the 24-hour fitness center or unwind in the green spaces of Vale Park.

What makes The William Vale's amenities different from other Brooklyn hotels?

The William Vale combines wellness, recreation, coworking, dining, and outdoor experiences in one destination. From Vale Pool and Winter Spa to Vale Park, Little Fino, Westlight, and The Malin coworking space, the amenities are designed to reflect the creative energy of Williamsburg.

How can guests make the most of their stay at The William Vale?

Many guests build their stay around the property’s amenities, starting the day with fitness or coworking, spending time at Vale Pool or Winter Spa, and exploring Williamsburg’s restaurants, shops, and waterfront parks before returning to the hotel in the evening.

Do you need to be a guest of The William Vale to enjoy the amenities?

While some amenities are reserved exclusively for hotel guests, others can be enjoyed by visitors as well. Westlight, Leuca, Little Fino, and Vale Park welcome both locals and travelers, while Vale Pool may offer public access through day passes during select seasons. Access to certain amenities and experiences may vary throughout the year.

What seasonal experiences can guests enjoy at The William Vale?

Experiences evolve throughout the year, from summer programming at Vale Pool to wellness-focused offerings at Winter Spa during the colder months. Seasonal events, dining experiences, and activations ensure that every visit feels unique.
