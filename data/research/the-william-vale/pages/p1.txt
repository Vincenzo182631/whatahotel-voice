The Neighborhood

## The Heart of Williamsburg

Just steps from McCarren Park and the East River, The William Vale is in the center of Brooklyn’s most creative neighborhood, filled with coffee shops, indie bookstores, cafes, and concept shops. Let us make a recommendation! Our team of locals know the city inside and out, and we’d love to help personalize your Brooklyn experience. Plus, with the L and G trains just a few blocks away, you’re one stop from Manhattan, and in a prime spot for exploring.

## FAQ's

What are the check-in and check-out times at The William Vale?

Check-in time is 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM.

Is The William Vale pet-friendly?

The William Vale welcomes pets. A $50 cleaning fee will be added to your stay if a pet enters your hotel room.

Do all rooms at The William Vale have outdoor space?

All guest rooms and suites feature private balconies, offering open-air vantage points over Brooklyn and the Manhattan skyline.

Is The William Vale suitable for both leisure and business stays?

Yes. With design-forward rooms, flexible gathering spaces, and direct access to Manhattan, the hotel supports both weekend stays and weekday business travel.

What is the atmosphere like at The William Vale?

The atmosphere blends urban sophistication with Brooklyn’s local spirit, contemporary, creative, and elevated yet approachable.

Why stay in Williamsburg instead of Manhattan?

Williamsburg offers skyline views and easy Manhattan access, alongside a neighborhood defined by culture, dining, and contemporary design.
