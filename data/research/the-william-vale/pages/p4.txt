# Westlight

Perched atop the 22nd floor of The William Vale, Westlight is one of Brooklyn’s most iconic rooftop destinations. Offering 360-degree views overlooking Manhattan skyline, the East River, and the Williamsburg, Westlight combines panoramic views with craft cocktails, rare spirits, seasonal dishes, and small plates from acclaimed chef Andrew Carmellini. Whether you’re joining us for sunset drinks, rooftop dining, or a night out in Williamsburg, Westlight offers a distinctly Brooklyn perspective on New York City.

## A Menu Designed for Sharing

At Westlight, the menu is designed to complement the rooftop experience, pairing panoramic skyline views with globally inspired dishes from acclaimed chef Andrew Carmellini. Guests can share favorites such as Tuna Tartare with Asian pear and yuzu, Shrimp Dumplings with mango and Thai basil, Tempura Bass Buns, and the Dry-Aged Burger, alongside signature cocktails, wine, and rare spirits. Whether you’re joining us for sunset drinks, a casual dinner, or a celebration with friends, the menu brings together bold flavors and a distinctly Brooklyn approach to rooftop dining.

## A Different View of New York City

While many rooftop bars place you in the center of Manhattan, Westlight offers a different perspective. From Williamsburg, the skyline becomes the backdrop, creating sweeping views across the East River and the city beyond. The result is a rooftop experience that feels both energetic and relaxed, capturing the creative spirit that has made Williamsburg one of Brooklyn’s most sought-after neighborhoods.

## FAQs

What makes Westlight one of the best rooftop bars in Brooklyn?

Westlight combines panoramic Manhattan skyline views, craft cocktails, seasonal dining, and a prime location atop The William Vale in Williamsburg. Perched on the 22nd floor, the rooftop offers a distinctly Brooklyn perspective on New York City, making it a popular destination for both visitors and locals.

Is Westlight a good spot for celebrations and group gatherings?

Westlight is a popular choice for birthdays, anniversaries, group dinners, and special occasions. The combination of skyline views, shareable plates, craft cocktails, and a lively atmosphere creates a memorable setting for gathering with friends, family, or colleagues.

What is Westlight Presents?

Westlight Presents is a seasonal event series featuring rooftop parties, DJs, special programming, and curated experiences throughout the year. These events bring together the energy of Brooklyn nightlife with one of New York City’s most iconic rooftop settings.

Why is Williamsburg a popular destination for rooftop dining?

Williamsburg offers a unique perspective on New York City, with sweeping skyline views, destination restaurants, and a vibrant neighborhood atmosphere. Its combination of waterfront scenery, nightlife, and creative energy has made it one of Brooklyn’s most sought-after destinations for rooftop dining and cocktails.

Do you need a reservation for Westlight?

Reservations are recommended, particularly during weekends, sunset hours, holidays, and special events. While walk-ins may be accommodated based on availability, booking ahead is the best way to secure a table during peak times.

What is the dress code at Westlight?

Westlight maintains a smart-casual atmosphere. Guests often visit for date nights, celebrations, and evenings out, so elevated casual attire is encouraged, particularly during evenings and special events.

Can you visit Westlight without staying at The William Vale?

Westlight welcomes both hotel guests and visitors. Whether you’re stopping by for cocktails, rooftop dining, or a special event, the venue is open to anyone looking to experience one of Brooklyn’s most iconic rooftop destinations.
