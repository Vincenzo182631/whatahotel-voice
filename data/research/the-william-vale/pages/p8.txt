About The William Vale

## A Brooklyn Icon since 2016

Since 2016, The William Vale has been a striking fixture in the heart of Williamsburg for travelers and locals alike. We stand out, and not only in design. Nowhere else in Brooklyn can you find views like ours, coupled with unparalleled access to the Brooklyn scene. We’re more than a hotel; we’re a neighborhood icon with a vibe all its own, thanks to our popular rooftop bar Westlight, summertime favorite Vale Pool, and a rotating lineup of cultural events that feed off Brooklyn’s creative energy.

Few get to see New York City the way we get to see New York City. With private balconies and floor-to-ceiling windows in each room, you get an obstructed view of magical skyline that is Manhattan, but with all the vibe of Brooklyn. After all, Manhattan is nice to look at, but we all know Brooklyn is more fun.

Community Impact

## EOS Hospitality

As part of EOS Hospitality, it’s The William Vale’s philosophy to “do well and do good,” by giving back to the incredible destinations that have given us so much. This is why 1 % of our room revenue goes back to the community in support of hyperlocal, non-profit organizations, based on their track record and expertise. We believe that supporting the community is a win-win for all, positively influencing our employees, local environment and guests.
