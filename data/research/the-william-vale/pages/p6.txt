# Dining

In a dining scene as competitive as Brooklyn’s, the restaurants at The William Vale raise the bar. Designed by James Beard Award-winning chef Andrew Carmellini and NoHo Hospitality, our standout culinary offerings have become destinations in and of themselves, as some of the most talked about spots in all of Brooklyn. From Westlight, our popular rooftop bar to Leuca with its heartwarming Italian comfort food and Little Fino’s coffee and pastries, dining at The William Vale is the new standard for hospitality.
