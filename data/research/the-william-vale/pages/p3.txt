# Leuca

Located inside The William Vale, Leuca brings the bold flavors of Southern Italy to Williamsburg with handmade pasta, wood-fired pizza, seasonal ingredients, and an inviting neighborhood atmosphere. Whether you’re joining us for a leisurely brunch, date night, family dinner, or celebratory meal, Leuca offers one of Brooklyn’s most distinctive Italian dining experiences, combining traditional recipes with a contemporary New York perspective.

## Southern Italian Dining in Williamsburg

Inspired by the traditions of Southern Italy, Leuca showcases seasonal ingredients, house-made pasta, fresh seafood, premium meats, and vibrant vegetable dishes. The menu celebrates rustic Italian cooking while embracing Brooklyn’s dynamic dining scene, making it a destination for both locals and hotel guests seeking authentic Italian cuisine in Williamsburg.

-

## Handmade Pasta & Wood-Fired Pizza

From freshly made pasta to wood-fired pizzas prepared in Leuca’s custom-built oven, every dish reflects a commitment to quality ingredients and traditional techniques. Guests can enjoy signature pizzas, seasonal pasta specials, antipasti, and Italian-inspired desserts alongside a thoughtfully curated wine list and handcrafted cocktails.

-

## A Williamsburg Restaurant for Every Occasion

Whether you’re planning a romantic dinner, weekend brunch, business lunch, family celebration, or gathering with friends, Leuca offers a warm and stylish setting within The William Vale. Its welcoming atmosphere makes it one of Williamsburg’s favorite destinations for everything from happy hour to special occasions before rooftop drinks at Westlight or The Turf Club.
