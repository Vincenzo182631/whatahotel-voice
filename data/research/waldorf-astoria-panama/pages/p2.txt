# Rooms & Suites

Rooms & Suites

Take in city or ocean views from our rooms and suites. Relax under fine linens and a down-filled comforter. Get ready at the vanity in your bright marble bathroom. The Nespresso coffeemaker and minibar are well-stocked for your stay.

Virtual Tour

## Rooms

Relax in your 344 square feet room with views of Panama City. Use the workspace to get more done and find Sodashi amenities in the marble bathroom.

## Condo Suite

Spend time together in your condo suite with a stylish living area and kitchen with dining table. The marble bathroom has a vanity and walk-in rain shower.

## Studio Suite

The studio suite offers Panama Bay and Pacific Ocean views. Make a meal in the full-size kitchen and enjoy it together at the dining table.
