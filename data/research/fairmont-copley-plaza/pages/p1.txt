## BOSTON'S MOST _celebrated_ ADDRESS

One of the best hotels in Boston, Fairmont Copley Plaza is a luxury downtown landmark that has been a symbol of the city's rich history and elegance since its gala opening in 1912. Centrally located in Boston's historic Back Bay, our 4-diamond hotel sits steps away from area attractions, popular restaurants, and a variety of unique shopping experiences.

It's more than an entrance; it's a gateway to cherished memories and unparalleled hospitality in a truly distinguished setting.
