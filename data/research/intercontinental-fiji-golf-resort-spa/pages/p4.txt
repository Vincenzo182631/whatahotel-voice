### Embrace tranquility at Spa InterContinental

Indulge in Spa InterContinental, an award-winning haven for relaxation. Our Fiji resort offers Eastern and Western treatments, infused with local herbs and honey to restore balance. With skilled therapists and a range of spa experiences, our spa offers an escape to serenity and rejuvenation.

Learn more

### Exclusive Location on Viti Levu

An hour from Nadi International Airport and set on one of the most beautiful beaches in the world, our five-star resort offers an unworldly island experience. Wander our 35 acres of tropical gardens, lounge on Natadola Bay, or slip off to the nearby town of Sigatoka.

Learn more

### Authentic Island Entertainment

Snorkel the aquamarine waters, play a round of golf on our 18-hole championship course, and slip into the spa for an array of treatments. Follow the palm-lined path to Kama Lounge for the nightly torch-lighting ceremony and spectacular South Pacific sunset. Join in authentic traditional Polynesian fire dancing, island dance, and traditional meke.

Learn more

### Natadola Championship Golf Course

Natadola Bay blends world class golf architecture with dramatic landscapes from rolling pastures through to absolute beachfront. With the sparkling backdrop of coral reefs and the Pacific Ocean on 15 out of the 18 holes, keeping your concentration will certainly be a challenge!

Learn more
