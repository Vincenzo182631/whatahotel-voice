### Embrace tranquility at Spa InterContinental

Indulge in Spa InterContinental, an award-winning haven for relaxation. Our Fiji resort offers Eastern and Western treatments, infused with local herbs and honey to restore balance. With skilled therapists and a range of spa experiences, our spa offers an escape to serenity and rejuvenation.

Learn more

### Romantic cabana dining with oceanfront views

Celebrate the spirit of romance and experience the luxury of dining in a private cabana, set against an ocean backdrop. Indulge with a lavish "Seafood Sensation" menu or an indulgent set menu with wine, served as the warm sea breeze and gentle waves set the mood for a romantic evening.

Learn more

### The perfect venue for "I do"

Nothing says destination wedding quite like exchanging vows against the backdrop of the azure South Pacific stretching out to the horizon, framed by palm trees and a pristine sandy beach.

Learn more

### Bee Happy Experience

Our bee-happy experience offer a unique opportunity to connect with nature and learn about the crucial role bees play as pollinators. These hands-on, immersive sessions provide a close-up view, allowing you to witness the fascinating behavior of honey bees. Afterward, enjoy a specially curated honey high tea and sample some delicious honey.

Learn more

### Exclusive Location on Viti Levu

An hour from Nadi International Airport and set on one of the most beautiful beaches in the world, our five-star resort offers an unworldly island experience. Wander our 35 acres of tropical gardens, lounge on Natadola Bay, or slip off to the nearby town of Sigatoka.

Learn more

## FAQs for InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa

Does InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa have a pool?

InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

In addition to our pristine beach and sparkling azure sea, we offer three outdoor pools, including a family pool, an adult-only pool and a Club pool. Our pools are open from sunrise to sunset.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa for hotel guests.

Complimentary daily self-parking is available for all resort guests.

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa has 4 restaurants.

Is there free breakfast available at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa?

Breakfast is free with your stay at the InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa?

Breakfast at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa is available from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa?

Check-in at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa is conveniently located near Nadi International Airport (NAN), approximately 27.96 miles away (45 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa?

InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Natadola Bay Championship Golf Course
- Scuba Diving
- Hydro Sports
- Hot Glass Fiji - 23.61 miles away (38 kilometers)
- Sigatoka River Safari
- Off-Road Cave Safari
- Skydive Fiji - 30.45 miles away (49 kilometers)
- Ecotrax Fiji
- Kula Wild Adventure Park - 24.61 miles away (39.6 kilometers)
- Shopping in Sigatoka Town - 19.82 miles away (31.9 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
