# InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & SpaDestination

Every dream ever conjured of a South Pacific vacation comes true at InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa. From snorkeling and diving to swimming in pristine aquamarine waters, to golf, spa, and taking in the most glorious sunsets, cocktails in hand.

## Things to do

### Natadola Bay Championship Golf Course

InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa, Maro Road, Natadola,

Natadola Bay blends world class golf architecture with dramatic landscapes from rolling pastures through to the absolute beachfront. With the sparkling backdrop of coral reefs and the Pacific Ocean on 15 out of the 18 holes, keeping your concentration will certainly be a challenge!

Learn more

### Scuba Diving

Onsite,

Discover the enchanted underwater world in Fiji and explore the colourful marine life on display here at Natadola Bay with activities ranging from Snorkelling, Scuba Diving to Shark Diving.

Learn more

### Hydro Sports

Onsite,

Feel the adrenaline course through your veins as you soak in the water-sports activities available here at the InterContinental Fiji Golf Resort & Spa. A thrilling experience you will never forget. A certified and experienced team will ensure you are safe, gain the most out of each experience, and capture all the amazing memories. We eagerly look forward to teaching all ages, and levels of ability in their chosen activity.

Learn more

### Hot Glass Fiji

Sunset Strip, Korotogo, Sigatoka,

Fiji’s glass-blowing studio is located along the tranquil Sunset Strip in Korotogo, on the Coral Coast. The workshop and gallery are perched on the hillside overlooking the sea. Glass blowing is fantastic to watch and great fun to go at! You can make your own unique glass piece, a special memento of your trip to Fiji to take home with you.

Learn more

### Sigatoka River Safari

Tappoos Sigatoka Store, Main Street, Sigatoka,

Sigatoka River Safari is Fiji's original and the world's first jet-boat and village safari. It offers a half-day eco and cultural adventure that transports up the magnificent Sigatoka River via jet boats to visit authentic Fijian villages and experience a day in the life of the real 'kaiviti' (Fijian).

Learn more

### Off-Road Cave Safari

Tappoos Sigatoka Store, Main Street, Sigatoka,

Fiji’s original and only Off-Road Cave Safari offers a half-day adventure on ATV bikes along the ‘Road less Travelled’ deep into the Fijian interior. This journey of discovery, rich in Fijian history, enables you to see Fiji’s largest cave system, the Naihehe Cave, home to the Nabuavatu clan during tribal warfare days. You will learn how this cave system was used for refuge and to defend from enemies until the end of the tribal war in Fiji centuries ago.

Learn more

### Skydive Fiji

Denarau Road, Nadi,

Be ready to take a dive from 14,000 feet in the air, roll out from the plane and prepare to have an exhilarating fall down towards the island! Exhilarating!

Learn more

### Ecotrax Fiji

Cuvu, Sigatoka,

Let the rhythm of the rails carry you along through beautiful bushes, rock cuttings, villages and fields while enjoying the magical scenery of deserted beaches that stretch for miles as you traverse untouched tracts of the famous Coral Coast. Pedal or simply use your accelerator to cruise along. Stop and stretch your legs, swim and snorkel in the turquoise clear water, and snack on local fruits and coconut drinks at remote beach locations.

Learn more

### Kula Wild Adventure Park

Korotogo, Sigatoka

As Fiji's only official breeding centre for endangered Fiji wildlife, Kula Wild Adventure Park offers a unique opportunity to meet and photograph Fiji’s wildlife up close and personal. Set in 12 acres of coastal rainforest, you will have the chance to interact with and take pictures of some of the rarest animals in the world.

Learn more

### Shopping in Sigatoka Town

Sigatoka,

Explore the scenic charm of Sigatoka, a rural town nestled at the mouth of the majestic Sigatoka River. Known as "The Salad Bowl of Fiji," the town’s municipal market is a haven of farm-fresh fruits and vegetables. Discover cherished keepsakes at the treasure trove of local handicrafts and souvenirs, reflecting Fiji's rich culture and traditions. Experience an authentic shopping journey in Sigatoka town, embraced by warm Fijian hospitality, creating unforgettable memories of your Fiji adventure
