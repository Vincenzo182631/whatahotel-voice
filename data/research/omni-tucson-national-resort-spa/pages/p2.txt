## Tucson, Arizona

### Escape to our Tucson resort near the Santa Catalina Mountains

Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa is inspired by the colors of the Arizona landscape - a place to relax and unwind amid scenic views with family and friends. Our 130 guest rooms and suites, including newly renovated Hacienda and Casita Suites, are welcoming retreats with private terraces, balconies and more. Discover the perfect place to relax after days exploring Arizona hiking trails, diving into resort pools or golfing championship courses. Unwind with a Mokara Spa treatment and indulge in reimagined, regionally inspired cuisine or signature cocktails overlooking the Catalina Mountains at our on-site restaurants. There is so much to see and do near our Tucson resort. Discover culture and arts in Downtown Tucson, tour Old Tucson Movie Studios or explore Biosphere-2, a world-renowned ecosystem experiment nearby. You can also plan your next meeting, wedding, conference or gathering at the resort, with over 10,000 square feet of available event space. Whether visiting our resort in Tucson, AZ for business or pleasure, you’ll be welcomed with warm, personal service and sent off with lifelong memories.

## Traveler's Insights

### What are the property amenities at Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa?

Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa is home to world-class amenities including:

- A full-service spa
- Two championship golf courses
- Two pools and cabana rentals
- A fully-equipped fitness center with Peloton bikes
- Catalina Activities Center, including tennis and pickleball courts, sand volleyball and the Catalina Pool
- Family-friendly resort activities including games, hiking, biking or even trivia nights
- 10,500 square feet of meeting and event space
- Six dining outlets - two full-service restaurants, a café, a lobby bar, a sports bar and a pool bar
- Complimentary self-parking and valet parking with a nightly charge

### Does Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa have pools?

Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa has two pools. View pool details here.

### Where is Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa located?

Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa is located at 2727 West Club Drive, Tucson, Arizona, 85742.

### What is the closest airport to Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa?

Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa is located near the following airports:

- About 20 miles (35 minutes away) from the Tucson International Airport (TUS).
- About 100 miles (90 minutes away) from the Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport (PHX).

### How much is parking at Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa?

On-site covered and open parking is available.

**Overnight self-parking:**Complimentary (included in resort charge)

**Overnight valet parking:** $15/night

### What time is check-in and check-out?

- Check-in: 4 PM
- Check-out: 11 AM

### Is there an age requirement to check in at Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa?

The person whose name is on the reservation must be at least 18 years of age to check into an Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa. Photo identification is required for check-in. Any person who has made a reservation, is staying alone and is not at least 18 years of age will not be allowed to check in.

### Is the resort pet friendly?

Yes, Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa is a pet friendly hotel for animals under 50 pounds. There is a one-time non-refundable pet fee of $150 per reservation for up to 2 pets. Service animals accompanying guests with disabilities are always welcome and will not be required to pay the one time pet charge (no documentation necessary). Read more of our hotel policies.

### What is included in the Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa resort fee?

Omni Tucson National Resort & Spa charges a $35 per night resort fee to allow guests access to the many different amenities throughout the property, including:

Access to Sweet Water and Catalina pools

Complimentary access to Tennis, Pickleball and Sand Volleyball Courts

Discounted green fees on The Catalina and Sonoran Golf Courses for resort guests

For a complete list of inclusions, visit the property details page.
