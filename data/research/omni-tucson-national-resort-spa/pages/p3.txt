Golf may not be the only main event at Omni Tucson, but with its PGA schedule and two name-designed (as in Van Hagge/Devlin and Lehman) courses, it certainly ranks high. If the links don’t rivet, work out the kinks at the full service spa or relax poolside in a private cabana at this Catalina beauty.
