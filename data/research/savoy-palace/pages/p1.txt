# Tribute Cosmopolitan Resort

Savoy Palace is a superior 5-star hotel located on Madeira Island, which provides the ideal choice for guests

seeking relaxation and luxury with a perfect blend of refinement, modern comfort and warm hospitality.

Member of The Leading Hotels of the World, and Virtuoso, Savoy Palace brings a new level of luxury to the hospitality scene of Madeira Island and Portugal.

Unique in its values, the hotel offers exclusive and unforgettable experiences to its visitors. With individually created areas within the property,

Savoy Palace offers the atmosphere of a boutique hotel and is guaranteed to satisfy everyone's tastes. Taking inspiration from the lush landscapes and

elaborate traditions of Madeira Island, the resort pays homage to the island and its unique characteristics.

# Terreiro Fite Pit

On 6 June, Fire Pit returns to the gardens of Terreiro, next to Savoy Palace, for an opening party celebrating the very best of open-air dining in Funchal.

Designed to bring together flavour, atmosphere and a signature touch of excellence, the evening will feature guest Savoy Signature Chefs, drinks included, and a selection of dishes prepared over the fire.

Let yourself be immersed in a setting where Madeiran tradition meets a contemporary approach.

Join the party for a memorable evening in Madeira, filled with fire, warmth and distinctive flavours.

LAUREA SPA

# A place to relax. A Palace to feel.

**Welcome to our dream of Laurissilva**

Revitalize yourself in the most beautiful way.

The magical garden of Madeira island was the inspiration for the birth of LAUREA SPA. Here, nature offers us several ways to make you feel relaxed and renewed.

Find tranquility where the forest merges with the water. Laurissilva is a unique forest type, native to the island. With vivid greens and a floral palette that stands out, it hugs us with stimulating aromas that blend with the salty Atlantic breeze, as beautiful as it is healthy. In all this stimulating diversity, you will find countless ways to feel the calming effects of our treatments.
