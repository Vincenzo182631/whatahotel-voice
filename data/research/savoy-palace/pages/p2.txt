# Discover more of Savoy Signature   during your stay!

**Your stay in Madeira gives you access to a selection of experiences at other hotels within the Savoy Signature collection.**

As well as enjoying the restaurants, bars and spas across all hotels, you may also access  swimming pools, sunbathing areas and sea access points. The options available vary depending on the hotel where you are staying.

**Discover more ways to elevate your stay in Funchal and Calheta.**

# The Reserve

Guests staying at The Reserve have access to the following facilities at Savoy Palace, Royal Savoy, Saccharum, NEXT, Calheta Beach and Gardens:

\- Swimming pools

\- Sunbathing areas

\- Sea access

# Savoy Palace

Guests staying at Savoy Palace have access to the following facilities at Royal Savoy, Saccharum (Premium Sundeck, subject to the applicable access conditions), NEXT, Calheta Beach and Gardens:\- Swimming pools

\- Sun terraces

\- Sea access

# Royal Savoy

Guests staying at Royal Savoy benefit from access to the following areas:

\- The Calhau Beach Club sunbathing area and Premium Sundeck pool at Saccharum, subject to the applicable access conditions

\- Swimming pools at Calheta Beach and Gardens

# Saccharum

Guests staying at Saccharum may access the following facilities:

\- Savoy Premium Experience guests may access the swimming pools at Savoy Palace and NEXT

\- Sea access at Royal Savoy

\- Swimming pools at Calheta Beach and Gardens

# NEXT

Guests staying at NEXT have the following options available:

\- The Calhau Beach Club sunbathing area at Saccharum, subject to the applicable access conditions

\- Swimming pools at Calheta Beach and Gardens

# Calheta Beach

Guests staying at Calheta Beach may enjoy the Calhau Beach Club sunbathing area at Saccharum, subject to the applicable access conditions.

# Gardens

Guests staying at Gardens have access to the Calhau Beach Club sunbathing area at Saccharum, subject to the applicable access conditions.
