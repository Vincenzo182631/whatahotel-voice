## Access to the Park

With the goal of preserving and enhancing the park, a new visitor access model has been implemented. This measure ensures the protection of this natural historical heritage, while enhancing the quality and tranquility of the experience in the park.

A portion of the proceeds goes to the Vidago Volunteer Fire Department, contributing to our ongoing support of the local comunity.

**• Conditions (starting in Aprill):**

• Visitors: € 2.50 per person

• Children up to 12 years old (inclusive): free

**• Free access for:**

• Guests of the Vidago Palace Hotel;

• Clients with confirmed reservations for hotel services (restaurants, spa, golf);

• Residents of the Union of the Parishes of Vidago, Arcossó, Selhariz, and Vilarinho das Paranheiras and Oura.

**• Entrance Points**

Access to the Park is permitted exclusively through the designated entrance points on site.

**• Moving Around the Park**

Visitors must stay on authorized paths and respect restricted areas, specifically the interior of the hotel and the golf course.

**• Park Hours**

• November to April: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM

• May to October: 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM

**• Guided tour**

To explore the hotel's interior, including a brief historical presentation and a water tasting directly from the source, we recommend our guided tours (subject to availability and prior booking via +351 279 990 901\* or reception@vidagopalace.com).

To ensure a peaceful and hassle-free experience, we recommend that you review the Park’s access conditions in advance.

If you need further clarification, our team is fully available to assist and provide support.

\*Call for national network
