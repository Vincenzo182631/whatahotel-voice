## Pet Friendly

At Vidago Palace Hotel your pet is welcome and we welcome your best

friend with pleasure and kindness. Your pet will be received with VIP

treatment enjoying a 5\* stay.

Attached you can find important information for the well-being of our four legged friends:

• We provide a feeding and drinking bowls and a surprise gift in the room;

• Bookings are only available in rooms on the ground floor: Garden and Garden Deluxe

• Only dogs (up to 20 kg) and/or cats are accepted. Maximum of 1 per room;

• Movement in the public areas of the Hotel is not permitted;

• Pets are not allowed in the public areas of the Hotel, namely in the Spa, Bar, Pool Bar, 4 Seasons Room, Wine Shop, restaurant "Salão Nobre" and "Jardim de Inverno", Club House, Event Rooms and Kids Club;;

• The circulation in the Hotel's park is allowed, provided your pet is on

a leash;

• It is not possible to leave your pet alone in the room for more than 2 hours. Please contact the Concierge or Reception Desk for information on PET Sitting services.

In order to guarantee availability, we ask you to book this service in advance

**Price:** 60.00€ per night.

For more information or reservations contact our reservations department on +351 276 990 920 or via e-mail reservations@vidagopalace.com
