# Welcome to W Punta de Mita

## Enjoy relaxation at our Punta de Mita luxury resort

Celebrate golden hours and vibrant nights at W Punta de Mita, a luxury beachfront resort where every sunset sets the scene. Just 20 minutes from Sayulita and 10 minutes from Marina La Cruz, this stylish escape connects you to sun, surf, and the spirit of Riviera Nayarit. Experience the allure of a luxury hotel in Punta de Mita, Mexico with signature cocktails and live beats on the Huichol-inspired...See More WET Deck or exclusive dining at Mesa 1 and Rubra by Daniela Soto-Innes. Unwind in bold rooms and suites at our beachfront hotel in Punta de Mita featuring private balconies with ocean or jungle views, the signature W Bed, and Davines amenities. Recharge at AWAY Spa, stay fit at FIT, and explore the nearby Marietas Islands, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. Elevate your stay in our Punta de Mita resort, where every sunset is worth celebrating. Discover why W Punta de Mita stands among the finest 5-star hotels in the region.See Less

## Remarkable Hotel

Perched on the ocean in the heart of one of the region’s most gorgeous beaches, our luxury hotel in Punta de Mita, Mexico invites you to immerse yourself in a truly remarkable experience. Colonial grandeur blends seamlessly with the extraordinary artistry of the indigenous Huichol peoples.

### Chevycheria

Enjoy fresh ceviches and bold Latin American recipes at our signature Punta Mita, Mexico beachfront restaurant, steps from the Pacific. Our recipes blend tangy citrus with local seafood.

### Spice Market Punta de Mita

Warm shades of red hang intimately over nooks adorned in plush tapestries, silhouetting the wraparound bar. Plates boast flavors from Vietnam, Thailand, and Malaysia. Indulge in the moment with the chef's personal recommendations.

### Mesa 1

Steal the scene with a seven-course dinner at Mesa 1, our private outdoor dining experience in Punta de Mita, Mexico, on an island in the center of a spring-fed lake. Available by reservation only, this enchanting venue hosts from two to 12 guests.

Explore

### Venazú

Join us from sunrise to sunset at Venazú Restaurant. This all-day Punta de Mita, Mexico hotel restaurant serves fresh and contemporary Mexican flavors in an inviting, laid-back setting with sweeping patio views of the Pacific Ocean.

Explore

### WET DECK

Chill out in a day bed with some of the freshest guacamole, shrimp tacos and coconut cocktails in our Punta de Mita bar. WET DECK lets you cozy up with buzzy sips, local spirits, craft beers and bumping DJ beats.

Explore

### Living Room Bar

Vibrant neon lights and a 480-candle chandelier set the mood at one of the most iconic Punta Mita bars, blending bold design that pays homage to the belief in a higher power and represents the Día de Muertos tradition.

### Rubra

Rubra by Chef Daniela Soto-Innes offers contemporary Mexican cuisine where every detail and flavor tells a story. The experience includes a coffee shop, curated wine selection, La Palapa tequila tasting with Casa Dragones, and an authentic dinner service.

Explore

### WET Too

Revel at WET Too, an adults-only playground with unobstructed views of the Pacific Ocean. Lounge the day away, soaking up playful cocktails and connecting with new people while listening to energizing beats.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Punta de Mita?

The check-in time at W Punta de Mita is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Punta de Mita allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Punta de Mita. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Punta de Mita?

On-site parking is available at W Punta de Mita. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Punta de Mita have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Punta de Mita has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Punta de Mita booking.

What is the closest airport near W Punta de Mita?

The closest airport near W Punta de Mita is Gustavo Diaz Ordaz International Airport Puerto Vallarta (PVR). PVR is located approximately 31.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Punta de Mita have an airport shuttle to Gustavo Diaz Ordaz International Airport Puerto Vallarta (PVR)?

Yes, W Punta de Mita has an airport shuttle to Gustavo Diaz Ordaz International Airport Puerto Vallarta (PVR).

Does W Punta de Mita have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Punta de Mita does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
