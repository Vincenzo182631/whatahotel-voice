# Experience the fresh cuisine of Punta de Mita

## Dining

Fuel your adventure in Punta de Mita, Mexico with custom bar cocktails and bold flavors magnified by the region's sunshine and beachside setting. All day every day, our hotel restaurant, Venazú, combines authentic Mexican cuisine with a casual yet celebratory atmosphere. Living Room provides light bites and savory sips at any hour. Outside, find refreshing drinks and snacks at WET® Deck. Chevychería...See More serves a seaside restaurant and bar menu of raw seafood, ceviches and craft beers from a converted 1950s Chevrolet 3800 truck. Eat like a local at Salero while soaking in the infinity views next to WET Too. Mesa 1, one of the most exclusive restaurants around, invites you and up to 11 of your closest friends to indulge in a one-of-a-kind, seven-course dinner. On the flip side is our Pan-Asian Spice Market featuring Punta de Mita’s traditional dishes with a creative twist from Chef Jean-Georges Vongerichten.See Less

## Family Style

Settle in at our Punta de Mita, Mexico beachfront restaurants and select from the menu’s pioneering plates—served family style. Dishes include spiced chicken samosas with cilantro-infused yogurt, tuna tartare and Pad Thai.

Mesa1

### Mesa 1: Dining Rooted in Water

With just one seating per night, this exclusive outdoor dining venue sets the stage for an intimate culinary journey in Punta de Mita, Mexico. Mesa1 features four distinct seven-course menus: Water, Earth, Mexican, and Vegetarian, each inspired by Mexican roots and time-honored techniques. Designed for up to nine guests, it’s the perfect setting to dine while taking in an unforgettable sunset..

Venazú Restaurant

## Vibrant Local Accents

Start the fiesta at the live cooking station for eggs every morning or stop by for guacamole and chips before hitting the scene. Try our signature Mula Mita Insignia cocktail with mezcal; kick off your lunch and dinner with a fresh appetizer ranging from tacos de Cochinita pibil, calamari rings and shrimp empanadas; later choose between any of our classics: wood-fired pizza or catch of the day. For young guests, ages four to 12, we have a special menu with tried-and-true favorites: salads, pasta, nuggets, and sandwiches.

## Rubra by Daniela Soto-Innes: A Forbes-Favorite Flavor Fest

While staying at W Punta Mita, guests have exclusive access to Rubra, the culinary destination by Chef Daniela Soto-Innes recently featured in Forbes as a multifaceted masterpiece of contemporary Mexican cuisine. Blending innovation, sustainability, and tradition, Rubra offers a complete dining journey—from its coffee shop and curated wine selection to the La Palapa tequila tasting and an authentic dinner service. A must-visit for travelers and food lovers seeking one of Punta Mita’s most remarkable gastronomic experiences.
