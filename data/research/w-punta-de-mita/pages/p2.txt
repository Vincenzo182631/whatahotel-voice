### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Punta de Mita have a work space?

No, the rooms at W Punta de Mita do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Punta de Mita?

The largest occupancy room at W Punta de Mita is the Oasis with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Punta de Mita?

Some rooms at W Punta de Mita permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Punta de Mita have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Punta de Mita have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at W Punta de Mita have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Punta de Mita have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Punta de Mita have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Punta de Mita have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Punta de Mita have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Punta de Mita have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
