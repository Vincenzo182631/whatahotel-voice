## About Select Planner Credits

Just 100 Omni Credits unlocks a Free Night at any Omni Hotel or Resort. Earn Free Nights faster with 5 Omni Credits per $1,000 contracted in a qualified event, and achieve higher Select Guest Status according to the table below:

| Contracted Revenue | Select Guest Status Granted |
| --- | --- |
| $2,500-$49,999 | Insider |
| $50,000-$249,999 | Champion |
| $250,000+ | Icon |

A minimum contracted spend of $2,500 USD or CAD is required and the Member must work directly with the Omni Hotels & Resorts Sales Team.

To learn more about Select Planner contact an Omni Hotels & Resort Global Sales Office, call 1-800-843-6664 or see complete program terms and conditions.
