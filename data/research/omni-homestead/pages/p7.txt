### Champion

Level up every stay with unique perks, such as early check-in and late check-out, room upgrades, and complimentary beverages delivered to your room every morning.

At $4,000 Tier Dollars, you earn Champion Status. Benefits include:

- Earn 10 Omni Credits per room night
- Earn 1 Omni Credit per $100 of purchases beyond your room
- Free upgrade by 1 room type level\*
- Check-in as early as 1pm\*
- Check-out as late as 3pm\*
- Daily free bottled water
- Daily beverage delivered in the morning, if desired
- Free Deluxe Wi-Fi, free bottled water on arrival
- Four items pressed or four shoeshines for free
- Local market amenity upon arrival
- Guaranteed room availability with 24 hours prior notice

\*Subject to availability

### Icon

An iconic stay is yours as a Select Guest Icon. Access top-tier privileges at over 50+ destinations - chef-created amenities, morning check-in, evening check-out, room upgrades, and more.

At $8,000 Tier Dollars, you earn Icon Status. Benefits include:

- Earn 10 Omni Credits per room night
- Earn 2 Omni Credits per $100 of purchases beyond your room
- Free Premium Wi-Fi
- Free upgrade by 2 room type levels\*
- Check-in as early as 9am\*
- Check-out as late as 6pm\*
- Suite upgrade when redeeming free nights\*
- Guaranteed room availability up to 4 p.m. on the day of arrival (limit one room per member)
- Evening housekeeping service, including turndown
- Daily free bottled water
- Daily beverage delivered in the morning, if desired
- Unlimited complimentary pressing and shoeshines
- Chef-inspired amenity/snack and choice of beverage on night of arrival
- Ability to gift Champion status to 1 person

\*Subject to availability
