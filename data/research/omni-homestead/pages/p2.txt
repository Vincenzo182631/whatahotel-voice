## Hot Springs, Virginia

### Discover Historic Hot Springs and the Allegheny Mountains at America's First Resort

As America's first resort, The Omni Homestead Resort & Spa has been a premier destination delighting travelers for over 250 years, even hosting 24 U.S. presidents. A $170 million renovation restored the historic character to our Allegheny Mountain resort in Virginia, from the Great Hall to the guest rooms and the bathhouses at the iconic Warm Springs Pools. Our one-of-a-kind Hot Springs resort features a two-acre water park and top-ranked golf courses, including the nation’s oldest first tee in continuous use. Enhance your experience with a soothing massage, facial or body treatment at The Spa and spend a relaxing afternoon at the adults-only Serenity Garden. Enjoy local favorites at ten unique restaurants and bars and taste wines from fine regional vintners. However you choose to spend your vacation at our resort in Hot Springs, VA, it’s guaranteed to be memorable.

## Traveler's Insights

### What are the property amenities at The Omni Homestead Resort & Spa?

The Omni Homestead Resort & Spa is home to world-class amenities including:

- An indoor pool, a spring-fed garden pool and a two acre water park
- A full-service spa
- Access to historic warm springs pools
- Two historic golf courses
- Seasonal skiing and snowboarding for all levels in the Allegheny Mountains

Four Har-Tru tennis courts, pickleball, hiking and biking trails, archery, kayaking, fly fishing and more outdoor activities
- A fully-equipped fitness center
- Horseback riding
- Zip line tours
- 76,000 square feet of meeting and event space
- 11 dining outlets - six full-service restaurants, a grab-and-go market, a spa café, a theater, a taproom and a lobby bar
- Family-friendly resort activities
- KidsClub Adventure Days
- Valet parking with a nightly charge

### Is The Omni Homestead Resort & Spa good for families?

Yes, The Omni Homestead Resort & Spa is a kid and family-friendly resort in Hot Springs, VA. Our resort offers a variety of on-site resort activities for guests of all ages including an indoor swimming pool, mini bowling, nightly showings of kids’ movies at Theater 1923, wagon rides, falconry, lawn games and more. You can also enjoy seasonal outdoor activities like skiing, snow tubing and ice skating with the family during the winter months. For the ultimate kid-friendly experience, explore our KidsClub, offering both daytime and nighttime adventures for our youngest guests.

### WHERE IS THE OMNI HOMESTEAD RESORT & SPA LOCATED?

The resort's physical address is 7696 Sam Snead Highway, Hot Springs, Virginia 24445. The Omni Homestead Resort & Spa is nestled in Western Virginia and the Allegheny Mountains and is known for its southern hospitality and charm. Our resort has been designated a National Historic Landmark and makes up most of the city of Hot Springs, Virginia.

Please visit our directions page for full details, or contact us for assistance.

### WHAT IS THE CLOSEST AIRPORT TO THE OMNI HOMESTEAD RESORT & SPA?

The Omni Homestead Resort & Spa is near the following airports:

- About 50 miles (about 1 hour) from Greenbrier Valley Airport (LWB)

- About 73 miles (about 1 hour and 40 minutes) from Roanoke-Blacksburg Airport (ROA)

- About 174 miles (about 3 hours and 15 minutes) from Richmond International Airport (RIC)

- About 202 miles (about 3 hours and 25 minutes) from Dulles International Airport (IAD)
- About 5.5 miles (about 15 minutes) from Ingalls Field **(general aviation and charter service only)**

Please visit our directions page for full details, or contact us for assistance.

### HOW MUCH IS PARKING AT THE RESORT?

##### Overnight Parking

- Overnight parking only. Fee is $39 + 5.3% sales tax per night ($41.07). Three Tesla electric chargers are available to resort guests and members only. Public access is not permitted.

### Are there electric car chargers?

Yes, there are three Tesla car chargers that are open to resort guests and members only. Chargers are available at a first come first serve basis.

### Does The Omni Homestead Resort & Spa have on-site meeting and wedding venues?

Yes, The Omni Homestead Resort & Spa has several on-site meeting and wedding venues to accommodate your Hot Springs events of all kinds. With 76,000 square feet of meeting space and 12 stunning indoor and outdoor wedding venues, our resort is the perfect backdrop for your next celebration.

### What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 4PM

Check-out is at 11 AM

If you arrive prior to 4 PM, you may check your luggage with the bell desk and enjoy our resort amenities until your room becomes available.

### Is the resort pet friendly?

Yes, The Omni Homestead Resort & Spa is a pet-friendly resort for dogs only. Two dogs may be registered to a room with a valid credit card on file. Each room with a pet will incur a one-time non-refundable fee of $250 for the first night and a nightly fee of $50 per dog thereafter. Service animals accompanying guests with disabilities are always welcome and will not be required to pay the one-time pet charge. All dogs will require onsite identification. Please read our pet policies prior to your stay to ensure the comfort of you and your dog.
