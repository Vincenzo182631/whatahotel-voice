Vienna Restaurants Inspired by the City
Dining
Indulge in the culinary excellence of luxury restaurants in Vienna at The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna. Our dining experiences blend innovation with tradition, offering something for every palate. Start your evening at D Bar, where expert mixologists craft signature cocktails in an elegant, intimate setting. For a more elevated experience, head to our Ritz-Carlton rooftop for exquisite dishes and creative cocktails paired with breathtaking views. Our Vienna rooftop bar is one of the finest in the city, making it the perfect spot to unwind while taking in panoramic vistas of the city’s skyline. Whether you're in the mood for fine dining or a relaxed, stylish atmosphere, The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna offers the perfect backdrop for any occasion. Just like each restaurant and bar within the hotel, our steakhouse in Vienna, is designed to provide a unique culinary journey, ensuring an unforgettable dining experience in the heart of one of Europe’s most captivating cities.
The Ritz-Carlton Brunch Affair
The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna unveils a new quarterly brunch concept, transforming the entire hotel into a culinary journey. Indulge in live cooking stations, signature cocktails, and gourmet creations as the ground floor becomes the ultimate brunch experience. Each edition features a world-class Ritz-Carlton chef. Be part of this exclusive event and reserve your spot today.

Destination Dining
Dstrikt Steakhouse
Dedicated in our passion for shaping memorable moments, Dstrikt brings thoughtfulness and flair to a steakhouse experience. Enjoy rare prime cuts and more in one of Vienna's prime locations and share our enthusiasm for steaks that are "made in Austria".
Pastamara - Bar Con Cucina
The Bar con Cucina is open to Vienna and the whole world and invites you to eat, to drink, chat and to chill out while day turns into night.
D-Bar
D-Bar is a classic setting, serving some of the most exciting cocktails in the city. For its sophisticated mixes with a story, inspiring ceremonies and fine spirits, the American cocktail bar overlooking Vienna's Ring Boulevard has been named repeatedly as the best hotel bar in Austria.
Atmosphere Rooftop Bar
Atmosphere Rooftop Bar is the place one comes to feel the fresh lifestyle of Vienna. To treat yourself, away from the hustle and bustle of the city. A place to escape from everyday life and a relaxed, elegant meeting place on the highest level. Sit back and discover our elegant 8th-floor Atmosphere Rooftop Bar with its magnificent view across the city center.
