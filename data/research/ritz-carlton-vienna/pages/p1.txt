Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna
Luxury Hotel in Vienna
A warm welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna, an urban retreat where art, history, and music converge in the heart of one of Europe’s most storied capitals. Set along the iconic Ring Boulevard and housed within four historic palaces, our hotel invites you to arrive as the first note of a masterpiece and depart as a symphony.
The Ritz-Carlton Christmas Suite
Discover your own Christmas sanctuary within our festively adorned suite, where twinkling decorations create magical moments with loved ones. Wake to daily breakfast included, savor warm Buchteln on the Winter Terrace, and let little ones find surprises beneath the tree.

The Art of Living Vienna

Our Ladies and Gentlemen are the composers of these moments, crafting each encounter with refined grace, heartfelt service, and insider knowledge that unveils Vienna’s most treasured secrets. Here, you are not simply staying in Vienna, you are living its story.

Experience Modern Elegance
Suites & Rooms
In a city renowned for its beauty and culture, The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna distinguishes itself with a unique blend of modern luxuries, fine dining, and spa experiences, all housed within four 19th-century palaces. Situated in the heart of downtown Vienna, along the iconic Ringstrasse and adjacent to the Stadtpark, our luxury hotel offers 200 elegant guestrooms, including 43 exquisite suites, allowing you to indulge in tasteful living, distinctive designs, and breathtaking views.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna. with a maximum weight of 15.0 kgs.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna, with rates of €5.00 per hour, and €65.00 per day. Valet parking is available for €65.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna is Vienna International Airport (VIE). VIE is located approximately 19.5 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Vienna offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
