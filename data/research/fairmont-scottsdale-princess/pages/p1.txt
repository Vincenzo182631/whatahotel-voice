## Frequently Asked Questions

What are some of the property amenities at Fairmont Scottsdale Princess?

As the longest running AAA Five Diamond hotel in Scottsdale, whether you are here to meet, play or simply relax, we wish to make your stay memorable. Enjoy six pools, a championship golf course, award-winning dining, and a rejuvenating spa. Every detail is designed for your ultimate comfort and delight.

Does Fairmont Scottsdale Princess have a spa?

Yes. Indulge in an oasis blending tranquility and the majestic beauty of the Sonoran Desert at Well & Being Spa. Renowned for its innovative offerings, we feature a comprehensive range of services, from rejuvenating massages and facials to invigorating fitness classes and therapeutic body treatments.

Does Fairmont Scottsdale Princess have a swimming pool?

Absolutely! We have six pools, offering relaxation and fun. Dive into our sparkling waters, unwind in a private cabana, or experience our white sand beach pool. Whether you're seeking a serene escape or a family adventure, our pools provide the perfect backdrop for your unforgettable stay.

Is Fairmont Scottsdale Princess pet-friendly?
