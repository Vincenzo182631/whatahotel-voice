**Sushi masterclass**

Offering a hands-on approach to dining, Amanpuri’s new Sushi Masterclass invites guests to embrace the art of sushi-making under the expert guidance of Akari's seasoned chefs. A craft of precision and skill, the experience includes selecting the finest ingredients, fish slicing techniques and hand-rolling, before enjoying the creations as a rewarding lunch.

**Beach Dinner**

Set beneath the open sky, this intimate dining experience invites you to savour thoughtfully curated dishes - from Mediterranean and Thai to plant-based and classic favourites - served at your private table on the shore. As the sun sets and the waves roll in, the evening becomes a moment of stillness, flavour and connection to nature.

**Coconut Masterclass**

Discover Amanpuri’s coconut plantation through a series of sustainability workshops dedicated to the ‘tree of life’. Begin with an al fresco breakfast beneath the shade of fruit-bearing fronds, followed by an introduction to the coconut tree’s life cycle and a live demonstration by a skilled coconut climber. In the afternoon, guests can enjoy a coconut dessert-making class, showcasing the versatility of this fragrant fruit.

**Aman 35 Boat Adventure**

Explore the captivating islands surrounding Phuket with Amanpuri’s state-of-the-art Aman 35 MTI powerboat. At nearly 13 metres (42 feet) in length, the vessel offers space for up to 10 guests and provides access to Thailand’s spectacular islands, including Phi Phi, Similan and Phang Nga Bay.
