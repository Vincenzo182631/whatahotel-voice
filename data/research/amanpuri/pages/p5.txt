\\
**Barbecue by candlelight**

The Amanpuri team can set up an intimate barbecue dinner in a secluded spot on Pansea Beach. Choose from an Italian, Thai or Vegan menu, served alongside breath-taking views of the Andaman Sea.

\\
**Ocean Platform Dining Experience**

Amanpuri's Ocean Platform Sunset Experience sees guests transported to their own private island platform in the serene waters of the Andaman Sea. With the ocean as an infinite backdrop and the sun slowly dipping below the horizon, canapes and Champagne complete the occasion – perfect for those celebrating a special occasion.

This Experience is available from November to March.

\\
**Private venues**

With a multitude of available locations from the comfort

and convenience of your private sala to an intimate table for

two on our golden sand beach, Amanpuri can offer private dining with flexible menu options to suit any special requirement.

\\
**Flavours of Japan**

Savour the artistry of Japanese cuisine with our four-course private dining experience. As you step into a candlelit setting of unparalleled beauty, immerse yourself in the aromas and flavours of authentic Japanese dishes. Each dish is a masterpiece that reflects the culinary traditions and innovations of this remarkable culture, and our chefs are eager to share their expertise with you. From the first course to the last, you'll be captivated by the exquisite presentation and taste of each dish.

\\
**Royal Thai Dinner**

Embark on a journey to the ancient Ayutthaya with our royal Thai cuisine private dining experience. Delve into the opulent world of Thai royalty and treat your palate to a delectable, authentic Thai cuisine. As you savour the flavours and textures of each dish, let your eyes wander over the exotic Andaman Sea or take in the symphony of nature in your very own secluded garden.
