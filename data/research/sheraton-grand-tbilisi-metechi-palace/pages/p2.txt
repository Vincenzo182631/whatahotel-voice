## Comfortable Guest Rooms

Our rooms have all the necessary amenities and facilities to make your stay comfortable. Smell the fresh mountain air from your balcony while sipping on your hot morning Nespresso coffee.

## Royal Suite

Enjoy an exceptional stay in our Royal Suite featuring a spacious bedroom with ensuite bathroom, living space, kitchen, salon for a private meeting or dining and enjoy a magnificent mountainous view right from your Royal Suite balcony.

## Presidential Suite

Conceived with today’s business traveler in mind, the workspaces blend formality, and functionality, accommodating the needs of today’s tech-savvy travelers. A separate living room, dining room, and lounge area reflect the look and feel of a modern penthouse apartment, enhanced by natural light and stunning, panoramic views of Old Tbilisi.

## Club Lounge

When you upgrade to the Sheraton Club, you’ll find a place to connect with co-workers and gather with friends. All in a stylish space that feels like it was designed exclusively with you in mind. Complimentary breakfast, all-day snacks and beverages and free Wi-Fi keep you fueled and connected throughout your day. The breakfast working hours: 06:30 AM to 11:00 AM.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace?

Some rooms at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace have fridges?

Do the rooms at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace have coffee/tea makers?

Do the rooms at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace have hair dryers?
