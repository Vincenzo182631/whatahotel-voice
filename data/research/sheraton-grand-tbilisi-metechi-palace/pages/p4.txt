## Abanotubani Panorama

Our hotel location brags stunning, panoramic views of the city and the Old Town.

Metechi Bridge

Metechi Bridge is located at the eastern bank of the Mtkvari River at the foot of Narikala fort across Metekhisubani, the historical part of Tbilisi, just 10 minutes walk from our hotel.

Bridge of Peace

We are located just minutes from the Bridge of Peace, which stretches 150 meters (490 ft) over the Mtkvari River, creating a contemporary design feature connecting Old Tbilisi with the new district.

Tbilisi by Night

Spectacular views of Tbilisi, surrounded by mountains, overlooking Narikala fortress and river Mtkvari.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Functional training rigs, squat racks Boxing room, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
