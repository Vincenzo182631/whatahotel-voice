# Welcome to Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace

## Heritage and innovation define the spirit of Georgia

As a 5-star hotel in Tbilisi, Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace blends tradition with innovation and attentive service. This hotel near Old Tbilisi places the city's iconic sights within easy reach and is just a 10-minute drive from Tbilisi International Airport. Since 1991, this Tbilisi, Georgia hotel has welcomed locals and international travelers with consistent quality. Our 220 spacious rooms and suites, many with private balconies, offer panoramic views of the historic skyline. For family stays, business trips and wellness breaks, enjoy varied dining, from ATI Rooftop Restaurant's modern Georgian cuisine to Craft House, plus P'auza Spa and our unique outdoor pool. Home to one of the city's largest ballrooms, 11 versatile meeting spaces and an expert banqueting team, we are ready for conferences, weddings and celebrations. Guests often praise our service, spacious rooms, dining and views, making us a Tbilisi family hotel for memorable stays.

## Georgian Wine Shop at the Hotel

Located near the lobby area, the RTVELISI Brand Shop & Bar invites you to immerse yourself in the authentic world of Georgian winemaking, where centuries-old tradition blends seamlessly with modern technology and craftsmanship.

Created by one of Georgia’s distinguished wine producers, RTVELISI showcases the country’s finest wines, aged brandies, and artisanal Chacha. Every bottle tells a story of heritage, innovation, and passion, from the vineyard to the glass.

Guests are welcome to enjoy a refined tasting experience in our indoor and outdoor lounge areas, explore designed RTVELISI tasting boards, and discover the artistry behind Georgian viticulture and distillation. Our friendly sommeliers will guide you through unique flavor profiles and help you find your favorite bottle to take home.

In addition to premium beverages, the boutique offers a curated selection of Georgian artisanal products, a perfect souvenir or gift for those who wish to take a piece of Georgia with them.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace?

The check-in time at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 20.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace?

The closest airport near Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace is Tbilisi International Airport- Nova Alexeevka (TBS). TBS is located approximately 14.2 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace have an airport shuttle to Tbilisi International Airport- Nova Alexeevka (TBS)?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace has an airport shuttle to Tbilisi International Airport- Nova Alexeevka (TBS).

Does Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace have an Executive lounge?

Yes, Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
