# Restaurants and Bars at Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace Hotel

## Dining

At Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace Hotel, guests can savor modern Georgian cuisine at ATI Rooftop Restaurant, international and local dishes at Feast, and craft beer in Tbilisi at Craft House. Our Tbilisi, Georgia restaurants and bars also include the unique Wine Library, the Lobby Bar and the Sheraton Club Lounge, all ideal for connecting with friends or colleagues in a warm setting. Guests praise the breakfast variety, quality dining, rooftop atmosphere and flavorful food across our restaurants and bars.

Discover the flavors of the season

Elevate your dining experience at ATI, our modern Georgian rooftop restaurant, where bold tradition meets diverse cuisine with seasonal creativity. Discover a newly curated menu inspired by fresh, locally sourced ingredients and reimagined classics.

## ATI Rooftop Restaurant

Your Living Room in Tbilisi. ATI is a Rooftop Restaurant, bar and lounge located on the 10th floor of the prominent Sheraton Grand Tbilisi Metechi Palace, boosting picture-perfect views of the city. Designed as the ideal spot for any occasion, it blends the cool rooftop bar and lounge with a Georgia inspired restaurant serving modern interpretation of the classic Georgian specialties from the regions. Top it up with fantastic ambiance complimented by crafted cocktails, selective entertainment and impeccable service. Whether you want to chill out by the fire pit with a glass of wine at sun set, pamper your taste with our signature drinks at the bar or discover the traditions behind the Georgian supra culture, ATI has it all covered for you.

### Craft House New Menu of Georgian Delights

Big flavors, bold choices—our new menu is here, and it's packed with Georgian favorites! From rich, hearty dishes to must-try specialties, we’re serving up something exciting and delicious.

### Rooftop Terrace

## ATI Times Better Experience

sights and sounds while immersing in the essence of some of the

drinks and pre and post dinner curated events, A touch of theatrical at-the-table experiences to guide the customers through the long-lasting traditions of the Georgian Supra.

## Crafted Here. Shared Together.

Pleasant interior with unfailing graceful service and welcoming ambiance. Bringing visitors over for delicious, freshly brewed coffee, flavorful food, and exceptional craft cocktails. The bar broadcasts the best of live sports events and becomes the gathering place for avid sports spectators. A perfect place for an afternoon getaway to relax, indulge in a game of pool with friends and family away from the hustle and bustle of the city.

A New Era of Georgian Cuisine

The spirit comes to life from our 10th-floor bar and restaurant, with a panoramic view of the Tbilisi Old Town that captures every feature. Thrill your senses in our elevated Georgian dining hall or seize the night with our delicious cocktails. More than just a Georgian grill, we combine the most robust parts of Georgian cuisine and energize it with a dynamic array of international influences.

Wine: An Elegant List for Any Occasion

The story of Georgia is the story of wine. The choice is yours: from qvevri-made to cask-aged, both Georgian and international, we've prepared an elegant list for any occasion.
