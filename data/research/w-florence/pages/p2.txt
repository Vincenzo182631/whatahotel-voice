# Indulge in luxury rooms and suites in Florence, Italy

## Accommodations

Experience Florence’s timeless allure in every room at W Florence. Interior details reinterpret materials inspired by Italian Renaissance architecture, from elegant marble to plush furnishings and geometric patterns. Each modern space is finessed with the iconic W bed and A-class amenities for comfort without compromise. For style-led stays, discover our luxury accommodation in Florence, Italy, where...See More thoughtful design and contemporary comfort come together.See Less

## Signature Suites

Designed to celebrate the city’s rich history and artistic flair, every square meter of our 17 suites will make you feel both at home and in awe. Marble accents, vivid colours, and geometric shapes reflect the beauty of Florence through the W design lens. State-of-the-art amenities, plush furnishings and the iconic W bed round off every stay, making it tempting to never leave the room.

Learn More

Literally, High-End

## Penthouse

Let the skyline unfold before you, offering postcard views of Florence. From the Medici Chapel to the Mercato Centrale, you’ll want to do a full 360 just to take it all in. Inside, no-expense-spared luxury indulges the eye with Carrara marble, travertine, rich wood, and sculptural curves. Illuminated 3D arches nod to the city’s architecture, blending past and future in bold design. Settle into 115 square meters of statement living—where a minibar is called cocktail station, and the only thing traditional is our taste for detail.

## P.A.W. Pets Are Welcome

Upon request, your room can be equipped with everything your four-legged friend needs, including a pet bed, food and water bowls, wee pads, waste bags, and more. We're happy to provide pet-friendly recommendations and arrange whatever you and your companion need for maximum comfort.
