Rise with the City, Connect Above it

## Meet Us On Top

Zefiro Rooftop is where Florence comes alive in motion. Above the city, it begins as a golden-hour escape and unfolds into a vibrant night scene. Inspired by the west wind, it flows from aperitivo to after dark with curated cocktails, music, and a dynamic crowd. Rooted in Florence and reimagined by W’s bold energy, it’s a place to gather—from Duomo-view sunsets to late-night rhythm.

## The Taste of Zefiro

Inspired by the myth of Zefiro and Florence’s timeless beauty, the cocktail menu celebrates transformation and the art of living well. Each drink blends seasonal ingredients with global influences, refined yet unexpected. Rooted in Italian aperitivo culture, it’s made to be enjoyed slowly, outdoors, in good company—from fresh, lighter serves to layered creations.

## Florence from Above

Take in sweeping views of Florence from a new perspective. From the Duomo to the city’s historic rooftops, Zefiro opens onto a skyline where past and present meet. As the light shifts from golden hour to night, the view becomes part of the experience, a backdrop that evolves with the energy of the rooftop. Set above it all, every moment feels elevated, immersive, and unmistakably Florentine.
