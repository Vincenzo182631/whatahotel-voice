## Signature Suites

Step into the suites that redefine the W Florence experience. Designed for those who want more space, more style, and more of the city, each Signature Suite brings residential comfort together with bold design and unmistakable Florentine character.From city-view balconies to expansive living spaces and a rooftop terrace overlooking the Duomo and Capelle Medicee, these suites elevate every stay. Whether hosting, celebrating, or simply unwinding above the city, this is Florence from a different perspective. Each stay is further enhanced with personalized amenities curated for every suite, tailored to individual preferences and special requests.

Penthouse Suite

## The Top of Florence

Spanning 115 sqm inside with an expansive 140 sqm private terrace, the Penthouse Suite opens to sweeping views of the Duomo and Cappelle Medicee. Outdoor lounge seating, sunbeds, and a dining table for eight set the scene for stylish open-air entertaining above the city. Inside, generous living spaces are elevated by Cotto di Impruneta flooring inspired by the Duomo, alongside a marble table for six, prep kitchen, and dressing room, creating an elevated Florentine retreat made for celebrations.

Supreme Suite

## Space to Host, Space to Stay

Set on the 4th floor, the Supreme Suite expands the Signature Suite experience with generous space to live, host, and unwind above Florence. A private balcony opens to sweeping city views, bringing natural light into the suite, while a large separate living room with a marble table for six is ideal for private dining or gatherings. A Florentine wine window adds a distinctive local touch. Inside, a dressing room, guest powder room, fireplace feature, and a spacious bathroom with shower and bathtub complete a refined and social retreat.

## Elevated City Living

Set on the first floor, the Urban Suite introduces the Signature Suite experience with a refined residential feel and views over Florence. A private balcony brings in natural light and a connection to the city below. Inside, a separate dressing and make-up room adds space to settle in, while an artificial fireplace creates a warm, inviting atmosphere. ### The Signature Suite Upgrade

Stay above the ordinary with the Signature Suite Upgrade, an invitation to experience Florence from a new perspective with elevated style. Book any Signature Suite and discover a curated leather bag by fashion designer Domenico Orefice waiting in your room upon arrival, created exclusively for W Florence. Crafted with the individuality and artisanal spirit that define the city, it is a keepsake designed to travel forward with you. Your stay also includes a €100 dining credit per stay to explore bold flavors and inventive cocktails across our signature venues, from vibrant aperitivo moments to memorable dinners in the heart of the hotel.

## Florence Through a Modern Lens

Florence has long shaped the worlds of fashion, craftsmanship, and design, and the Signature Suites at W Florence reflect that legacy through a bold contemporary lens. Designed by AvroKO and inspired by Italian fashion houses, the interiors reinterpret classic artistry with modern attitude. Rich color palettes, glossy finishes, lacquered woods, and statement furnishings create expressive, layered spaces. Each suite channels the city’s creative energy while honoring its tradition of craftsmanship, resulting in a stay that feels both rooted in Florence and unmistakably modern.

Design Story

### Inside the Details

Marble showers, warm wood dressing areas, and sculptural details set the tone, while bold color, graphic art, and plush lounge spaces add a vibrant edge. Each corner blends modern comfort with expressive design, creating a space that feels elevated and distinctly W Florence.

### Private Transfer

Private transportation can be arranged for guests seeking a seamless arrival or departure experience. Whether traveling to or from the airport, train station, or exploring the city, dedicated transfer services offer comfort and convenience throughout your stay. Please note that private transfers are available upon request and are not included in the room rate. Contact the concierge for more information.

### Wellness Area

Unwind at the Wellness Area at W Florence, a calming retreat designed for relaxation and renewal. The space features a soothing relax lounge, steam room, and sauna, creating the perfect setting to reset after a day in the city. Separate men’s and women’s lounges offer added comfort and privacy. No appointment is required, allowing guests to enjoy the experience at their own pace. Open daily from 10:00 to 20:00

### Zefiro Rooftop

Zefiro brings the spirit of Florence to new heights. Set above the city, the rooftop pairs sweeping skyline views with bold cocktails, vibrant energy, and an atmosphere that shifts effortlessly from sunset aperitivo to late-night celebration. It is where the breeze, the music, and the city come together.

Discover More

### Tratto

Tratto is where bold flavors meet laidback Italian style. Created by Trattoria Contemporanea in Como, the concept draws on tradition while embracing experimentation. Familiar ingredients are reimagined with unexpected twists, bringing fresh energy to classic Italian dining and creating a setting that feels vibrant, social, and anything but ordinary.

Discover More

### Akira Back

Akira Back is a tribute to Japanese cuisine shaped by bold creativity and immersive design. Led by Chef Akira Back, the restaurant transforms dining into an artful experience. Each dish reflects his journey, where refined Japanese technique meets a daring Korean edge, creating flavors that are expressive, unexpected, and distinctly personal.

Discover More

### W Lounge

Wrapped in retropolitan energy, this space evolves with the rhythm of the day. Begin with shared plates and drinks in a relaxed, social setting, then ease into aperitivo as the atmosphere builds. As evening falls, live music and DJ sets bring the room to life, all unfolding against the backdrop of sweeping views over Piazza dell’Unità Italiana.
