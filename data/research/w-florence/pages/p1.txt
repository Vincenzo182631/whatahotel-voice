# Welcome to W Florence

## WONDER FORWARD

With front-row access to Florence’s iconic landmarks, our hotel in central Florence, Italy, is a crossroads of culture. Situated opposite Santa Maria Novella and within Florence’s historic center, global influences meet local tradition, and contemporary design fuses with the city’s rich heritage across 119 rooms and suites at our Florence hotel. Our culinary experiences span from vibrant restaurants...See More to a rooftop with skyline views from Santa Maria Novella to the Duomo, open seasonally to guests staying at the hotel. With impeccable service and dynamic programming, including workshops, art showcases, and exclusive events, you’ll feel like a local and wish you lived here. Artistic abundance and bold collaborations celebrate creativity, making our luxury hotel in Florence a beacon for visionaries and tastemakers. Here, every moment sparks curiosity and connection. This is where the world meets Florence, and Florence meets the world.See Less

## Reception

From the moment you step in, you’re greeted by an impressive custom mural. A Florentine Babylon garden featuring exotic animals once owned by the Medici family hijacks your attention. Many were gifts from foreign monarchs, including the Sultan of the Ottoman Empire in 1514. This unique piece also includes modern elements of Italian culture, fashion, and lifestyle.

## Right Where It’s Happening

Step out and dive into Florence’s artistic heartbeat. Every cobbled street, every hidden alley hums with history and discovery, all just a stroll away. No wonder the city center with its iconic landmarks is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Lace up, wonder awaits.

Design Narrative

### Audacious Between Antiques

Nestled among Tuscan landmarks, the W Florence rises with a sleek design, overlooking a vibrant piazza. Vertical fluted steel moldings and bronze accents on the facade embody the idea that amidst the echoes of history, a new chapter begins. The interior is a nostalgic nod, featuring vibrant colours, plush furnishings, and glossy finishes. Geometric marble flooring honours Tuscan tradition while seamlessly complementing the retro-futuristic theme. This is where history and contemporary design shake hands, creating an experience as culturally significant as the city itself.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Florence?

The check-in time at W Florence is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Florence allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Florence. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 10.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €120.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Florence?

W Florence does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Florence have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Florence has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Florence booking.

What is the closest airport near W Florence?

The closest airport near W Florence is Florence Airport, Peretola (FLR). FLR is located approximately 7.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Florence have an airport shuttle to Florence Airport, Peretola (FLR)?

No, W Florence does not have an airport shuttle to Florence Airport, Peretola.

Does W Florence have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Florence does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
