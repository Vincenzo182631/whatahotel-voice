# Savor the city at W Florence, Zefiro Rooftop

## Dining

Engage with a team that blends hospitality with storytelling at our Florence restaurant, bringing Florence’s creative pulse to life. From surprise menus to immersive experiences, each moment is crafted with spontaneity, elegance, and a touch of irreverence. Take it inside, then out to our open-air courtyard and rooftop spaces for al fresco bites, cocktails, and views. Unscripted and always evolving,...See More this is where flavors, people, and ideas collide and meals become a cultural exchange.See Less

## Breakfast Blizz

Answer your appetite with a lineup of morning crowd-pleasers. Savoury French toast, Eggs Florentine, and detox juices set you up for the day ahead. Served from 7 to 11 AM at Tratto, so even late risers won’t miss out.

## Akira Back's Signature Aperisushi

Please, take a seat. Akira Back's Signature Aperisushi is about to be served. Served from Thursday to Saturday, from 5 to 8 PM.

Exceptional Food & Cocktails

### Gateway To Good Taste

The W Lounge thrives on its social energy and transitions into different offerings as the sun rises and sets. Shareable snacks and curated drinks fuel conversations and beg to come back for an afternoon aperitivo, the perfect excuse for natural wines. As the BPM rises, sip world class cocktails and dance into the night.

### Greatest Hits at Akira Back

Chef Akira Back’s signature dishes practically demand a voluntary food coma. Think Tuna Pizza with umami aioli, Charred Octopus with chipotle sauce, and next-level sashimi.

### Cocktails and Skylines

When drinks go beyond taste: playing with texture, aroma, and mood, you know you're in for something special. This is mixology with a view: a masterclass above the rooftops of Florence. Gather here to close the day with a sunset and a cocktail that screams gourmet.
