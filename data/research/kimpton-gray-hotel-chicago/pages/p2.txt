Kimpton Gray Hotel
Part of the fun and funky Kimpton family, this hotel in the Loop has great value for style and location.

Reviewed by
Elaine Glusac
How did it strike you on arrival?
Very gray-flannel-with-paisley-lining. Buttoned up, with ample opportunities to button down at the bars. Befits its locale in a former insurance building.
What’s the crowd like?
Loop-bound lawyers and financiers who appreciate a good cocktail.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
Sleek platform beds and pendant lamps suggest mid-century modern, but the dark wooden armoire with the floral interior speak to a wilder streak.
We’re craving some deep, restorative sleep. They got us?
Excellent beds with leather headboards.
How about the little things, like mini bar, or shower goodies. Any of that worth mentioning?
Loved the pendant globe lamp beside the bed that warms the room like a sun. Great photo-driven art hung asymmetrically above the bed. Interesting coffee table books on design.
Please tell us the bathroom won’t let us down.
It's worth upgrading to a Spa room, where the shower room—complete with a freestanding tub—is glassed-in and tiled in abstract designs. Nice feng shui touches with succulents on the sink counter.
Maybe the most important topic of all: Wi-Fi. What’s the word?
Good and included.
Anything stand out about other services and features?
There are outstanding bars here for whatever mood you're in. The lobby bar Vol. 39 has the leathery look and bookshelves of a law office given a romantic makeover, with low seating and flickering votives. On the top floor, hit Boleo if you're feeling high-energy (it has a nightclub vibe, but the Latin food is very good). There's ample free coffee in the lobby in the mornings and a spacious gym as a remedy to all that.
Any other tips?
Check out the hosted social hour in the lobby living room (nightly, 5 p.m.-6 p.m.)—so long as you also find time to enjoy the experience at Vol. 39.
Bottom line: Why are we booking here?
Good value for style and location in
