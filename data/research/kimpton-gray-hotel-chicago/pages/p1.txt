Think of Kimpton Gray Hotel like a perfectly bespoke suit from London’s Savile Row — beautiful, bold, and with no detail out of place. Clad in marble The Gray has been a tastemaker for more than a century, first as an office building and now as an elevated-experience hotel. When the 9-to-5 day is done in the Financial District, those in the know flock to the on-site lounges and restaurants - Vol. 39, a law library-themed bar, Boleo, an Argentinean-inspired lounge with retractable glass roof, and Fillmore, a chef-driven new American restaurant. Consider us for business travel or a weekend getaway. Blocks away from Millennium Park, restaurants and museums.

### Rooftop Bar + Lounge

Embrace the good vibes at Boleo, a rooftop restaurant & lounge (with a retractable glass roof) on the 15th floor of Kimpton Gray Hotel. Innovative Latin-influenced cocktails, enticing food, vibrant music & the sleek-yet-lush design intermix to evoke the dynamic energy of South America.

Learn more

### Vol. 39

The dimly lit, law library–themed Vol. 39 off the lobby beckons bibliophiles and bankers alike to flip through its pages. Inside, you’ll find gilded carts with caviar and champagne, along with a refined wine menu — rare, hard-to-find vintages served in high-end stemware are the specialty here.

Learn more

### Weddings

Kimpton Gray Hotel offers wedding experiences from simply elegant ceremonies to lavish soirees. Each celebration is tailor-made to exceed your every desire. Custom menus, elegant appointments, group room rates, and a grand marble staircase (serving as an ideal photo backdrop) are icing on the cake.

Learn more

### Curated Concierge

At Kimpton Gray Hotel, no detail goes unnoticed. We take this philosophy to the next level with our Concierge Services, and guest experience team. Specializing in curated partnerships and programming, creating unique experiences and personal touches. Our team is at your service, and can't wait to welcome you!

Learn more

### Wedding Weekends

Why go anywhere else? Kimpton Gray has it all! Your wedding is the occasion to celebrate in epic or quiet style, as you wish. We suggest maximizing the milestone all weekend long. The Kimpton Gray Hotel Chicago has a variety of venues with thoughtful design elements that make each space unique—from intimate and exclusive to spacious and sprawling.

## FAQs for Gray Hotel

Does Kimpton Gray Hotel have a pool?

No, Kimpton Gray Hotel does not have a pool.

How much is parking at Kimpton Gray Hotel?

At Kimpton Gray Hotel, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 64 USD and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 83 USD .

Valet parking is available for 83USD per night.

Parking fee: 64 USD per day

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Gray Hotel?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Gray Hotel.

Pet policy description. We invite you to bring your pet no matter their size, weight, or breed, all at no extra charge. If your pet fits through the door, we will welcome them in.

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Gray Hotel for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Gray Hotel have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Gray Hotel has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Gray Hotel?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Gray Hotel.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Gray Hotel?

Check-in at Kimpton Gray Hotel is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is Kimpton Gray Hotel from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Gray Hotel is conveniently located near Chicago Midway Airport (MDW), approximately 11 miles away (17.7 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near Kimpton Gray Hotel?

Kimpton Gray Hotel is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Art Institute of Chicago-0.4 miles away (0.64 kilometers)
- Lyric Opera - Civic Opera House-0.3 miles away (0.48 kilometers)
- Michigan Avenue-1.1 miles away (1.77 kilometers)
- Millennium Park-0.5 miles away (0.8 kilometers)
- Navy Pier-0.4 miles away (0.64 kilometers)
- Willis Tower Observatory-0.3 miles away (0.48 kilometers)
- Grant Park-0.6 miles away (0.97 kilometers)
- Union Station-0.6 miles away (0.97 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, Kimpton Gray Hotel puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
