Spa Rituals Inspired by Tenerife Traditions
The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Abama
The Ritz-Carlton Spa offers a range of restorative and transformative treatments inspired by the natural world and local traditions. Attuned to different times of the day, the innovative, flexible program at our Tenerife spa resort offers a range of health-enhancing wellness experiences, all tailored to your personal requirements. The on-site Fitness Center offers an array of state-of-the-art cardiovascular exercise machines, with spinning bikes on the outdoor terrace (at selected times, reservation is required). There are also jogging and hiking trails through botanical gardens. With golf and tennis in easy reach, The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama provides the perfect place for active travelers to relax, restore and energize.
Featured Treatments
Relaxation Massage
Unwind and de-stress with this aromatic bespoke treatment. This soothing aromatherapy massage uses light to moderate pressure and a blend of essential oils to melt away tension.
Back, Neck and Shoulders Massage
A popular pressure massage that focuses on the build-up of stress and muscle tension in all areas around the neck, shoulders and across the back. This massage encourages relaxation, relieves stress, improves circulation and relaxes muscles.
Earth, Wind and Fire
Inspired by El Teide, the highest volcano in Spain, this massage combines the benefits of lava sand exfoliation to rejuvenate the skin and powerful heated volcanic stones known to create a feeling of well-being.

Prior to Arrival
When should I arrive for my scheduled appointment?
For your enhanced comfort and relaxation, we encourage you to arrive 45 min before your scheduled treatment to allow time to relax and experience our water facilities. Our water facilities are public, swimsuits should be worn.
Should I shave before a treatment?
Shaving is recommended but not necessary for men prior to a facial. If you do choose to shave prior to your facial, be sure to do so at least 8 hours prior to your scheduled appointment. Shaving is not recommended prior to any body treatments or hair removal services.
Spa Services
Take advantage of the treatment offers available from our award-winning Spa, for an even more relaxing and memorable stay.
Discover our daily offers directly at The Ritz-Carlton Spa, or by email, at
abamaspa.reservation@ritzcarlton.com

What if I have a special health condition?
Please notify the spa reservations agent or your therapist if you take medication or have high blood pressure, allergies, physical ailments, disabilities or are pregnant. Before treatment, your therapist will ask you to complete a confidential Health questionnaire.
What if I am pregnant?
While a few services should be avoided during pregnancy, there are treatments that can be enjoyed.
How do I make an appointment?
Spa reservations can be made by:
By email at
abamaspa.reservation@ritzcarlton.com
By dialing 8520/8524 from your room phone
We encourage you to reserve treatments at least 2 days in advance.
If you prefer a male or a female therapist, please make your gender request when scheduling your treatment.
When is the fitness center open?
The Resort Fitness Center is open 24 hours a day (please note that closing times and days may vary according to seasonality).
What should I wear during my treatment?
Most body treatments are enjoyed without clothing or disposable underwear is suggested. However, please wear whatever is most comfortable for you. During all treatments the body is fully draped except for the area being worked on and selected treatments where the protocol invites to work on the whole body in one time.
Spa Policies
What is age restriction?
Guests of the Spa must be at least 18 years of age or older to experience Spa treatments and Rituals. Beauty Salon services are available to guests of all ages.
May I use the Spa without booking a treatment?
The full use of all spa facilities is included with the purchase of a spa treatment for that day. Hotel guests can also experience the Water facilities (Hydrotherapy pool, Turkish hammam, sauna, herbal steam, cold plunge pool, kneipp walk and world of showers) without having a spa treatment for a 25 € entrance fee.
What is the Spa cancellation policy?
If you must cancel or change your Spa appointment, please notify us six hours before your appointment to avoid being charged the full service fee. Cancellation of a program or journey requires twelve hours’ notice.
Where may I place my personal items?
We cannot be responsible for lost or stolen items. If you are guest in-house, please leave valuable items in the safe in your room. Lockers are available to change in the Spa. Linen and disposable flip flops are provided.
How may I pay for my treatments?
A 6.5% of IGIC taxes will be added to your bill. Spa services can be charged to your hotel bill. All major credit cards and cash are accepted.
Please present vouchers, gift certificates or any loyalty cards at the time your services are rendered.
Reductions do not apply to Spa packages, promotions, special events or holidays. Gift certificates are available. Prices and services are subject to change without notice.
What if I am late for my appointment?
Arriving late for your scheduled treatment will simply limit the time of your treatment. It will end on time so the next guest is not delayed and the full value of your service will be charged.
Wellness Experiences
Wellness Circuit
Inspired by the baths and steam rooms of Ancient Rome, our water circuit allows you to experience a variety of extreme hot and cold temperatures, helping to eliminate toxins and revitalize the immune system. The Ritz-Carlton Spa Wellness Experience includes a Turkish-style herbal steam bath, which can be flavored using essential oils with wonderfully delicate fragrances.
World of Showers
Our “World of Showers” stimulates the senses by exposing the body to different water experiences, from tropical rain to hot showers with lateral jets and a Finnish shower that offers an invigorating blast of cold water. The cold cabin allows guests to cool down with refreshing Arctic rain between sessions in the sauna or Hammam.
Kneipp Circuit
The Kneipp circuit helps to improve blood circulation, with our feet and legs exposed to the texture of volcanic rocks. Walking on these rocks stimulates our muscles, helping to boost the immune system. The suggested final stage is a session of hydrotherapy and chronotherapy in our plunge pool to re-acclimatize the body to an ideal temperature.
Fitness Center
Revitalize your mind and body at the Fitness Center and discover the pleasure of a luxurious haven where a holistic and unique wellness experience awaits. The Ritz-Carlton, Abama Fitness Center is open 24h, with room key access and available for reservations, allowing enough time for enhanced cleanliness procedures. A fully equipped health club offers state-of-the-art cardiovascular and training systems. Use of the Fitness Center is reserved for hotel guests aged 16 and over. For your safety, proper footwear and fitness attire are required.
