Modern Island Luxury in Tenerife
Suites & Rooms
Modern elegance and resort vibes infuse our collection of Suites & Rooms. Color palettes are inspired by the turquoise of the ocean, the emerald green of the lush botanical gardens and the terracotta hues of our Moorish architecture. To meet the different needs of modern travelers, The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama offers a variety of hotel accommodations in Tenerife. While The Citadel will transport you into the buzz of the lively resort life with daily celebrations, events and live music, The Retreat is the perfect choice for those seeking an elevated experience, where privacy and calm are enhanced with an extensive list of special features & benefits. From private pools and adult-only areas to all-day sips & refreshments, you will find yourself pampered by our legendary service and attention to detail. The Citadel
Let us transport you to a mystical place, where the charm of old architecture meets the buzz of a chic resort lifestyle. Here the lively vibes of the resort converge: The Citadel is the perfect hub for those who wish to stroll exclusive shopping outlets, be surrounded by restaurants & lounges options, and be closer to the center of events & happenings.
