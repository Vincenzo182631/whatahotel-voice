The Retreat at The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama
The Retreat
Experience the essence of island serenity at The Retreat, an exclusive enclave within The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama. Designed to feel effortless, personal and deeply enriching, our luxury villas in Tenerife offer unique views where ocean and sky meet and time seems to stop. Created for families and adults seeking tranquility, our Tenerife villas with private pools are nested in a sanctuary of lush gardens, water and luminous spaces, where serenity and connection gently coexist. Guided by dedicated Ambassadors, every stay begins with a personalized welcome and flows through daily rituals and curated programming that capture the timeless elegance of The Ritz-Carlton and the natural beauty of Tenerife.
Retreat One Bedroom Suite With Private Pool
Discover a world of serenity and connection in The Retreat One Bedroom Suite with Private Pool. Designed for both couples seeking tranquility and families in search of shared moments, it features a spacious bedroom, elegant living area, and private terrace with pool surrounded by lush gardens and Atlantic views, an effortless expression of island luxury.
