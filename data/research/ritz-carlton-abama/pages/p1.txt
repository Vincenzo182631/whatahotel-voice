Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama
Luxury family resort, where every season feels like summer
On the volcanic shores of Tenerife, The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama, rises as an island sanctuary where architecture and nature come together. Framed by the Atlantic Ocean, Mount Teide and lush gardens, our 5-star resort offers 459 rooms, suites and penthouses designed for a true sense of place in Tenerife. A destination within itself, our it unfolds through distinct environments: The Citadel, the lively heart of social life; The Retreat, offering privacy for adults and families; and The Penthouses Collection, blending residential comfort with sophistication. Renowned as one of Europe’s leading culinary destinations, the resort features Michelin-starred dining alongside exceptional cuisine, grounded in sustainable sourcing. Well-being is elevated at the 2,500 m² Spa & Fitness Center, while families enjoy Ritz Kids and the vibrant Splash Park. From Abama Beach's exclusive guest areas to endless ocean views, the luxury resort invites guests to embrace this oasis of eternal summer in Tenerife.
A new chapter at The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama
Set against the dramatic landscapes of Tenerife, our oceanfront resort invites you to experience the island through exceptional gastronomy, serene pools, our recently unveiled Splash Park and reimagined family experiences designed to create unforgettable memories long after your departure.

The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama
Suites & Rooms
Inspired by the tribal heritage of the Guanches – the ancient indigenous people of Tenerife - the resort’s rooms and suites combine a contemporary and luxurious aesthetic with elements of the island's volcanic landscapes and lush gardens. Organic textures, natural materials and clean modern lines create an intimate and relaxing atmosphere with minimalist décor. The color palette varies from the shades of blues at The Citadel to the earthy tones of The Retreat, featuring red and orange elements in the bedrooms adding grandeur, while touches of emerald green and toasted wood bring serenity to the lounge areas. These colour schemes enriche the visual experience and reflects the refined luxury and deep connection to the island's natural beauty.

The Ultimate Resort Experience Awaits
Discover the Retreat
Gently nestled against the hillside and surrounded by leafy subtropical gardens, The Retreat consists of four lanes of two-story casitas—Spanish for little “mansions”—with privileged views over the lush gardens and the ocean. The rooms and suites at The Retreat offer the ultimate resort experience in privacy and comfort, with dedicated pools, adults-only areas, and exclusive spaces designed to deliver special benefits and elevated services.

Sustainability
Cradled between the deep blue of the Atlantic and the timeless embrace of volcanic earth, The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama resides within a sanctuary of rare biodiversity—home to native flora, vibrant birdlife, and delicate coastal ecosystems. Here, sustainability is not an initiative, but a philosophy woven into the soul of the resort. From thoughtful reductions in waste and single-use plastics to the quiet hum of energy-efficient systems, each gesture is a tribute to the land that sustains us. Our culinary philosophy follows the same path, celebrating seasonal ingredients and local produce to craft dishes that honor the island’s richness while treading lightly on its resources. In preserving its beauty, we honor our community, enrich our island’s legacy, and walk gently toward a more harmonious future.

A Commitment to a Greener Future
At The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama, our commitment to sustainability is enduring. As a Biosphere-certified resort, we reduce energy and water use, manage waste responsibly, and protect biodiversity. By staying with us, you join a vision of mindful luxury that preserves our island’s beauty and supports our community.
Sustainability Woven In
At The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama, we care for natural resources with mindful practices. Towels are replaced only when left on the floor, bed, or chair, or if soiled. Bed linens are refreshed every other day, upon departure, or by request. These steps help reduce water and energy while ensuring your comfort.
Guardians of Land and Sea
At The Ritz-Carlton Tenerife, Abama, we care for land and sea. With Innoceana, we help restore seagrass meadows vital to marine life and climate balance. On land, we protect endemic species, manage landscapes, and preserve botanical richness. Together, we nurture harmony with nature for future generations.
