Discover Our Hotel Suites With a Private Pool in Tenerife
Signature Suites
Find your home-away-from-home thanks to our Penthouses & Suites Collection. Reclaim your time and space for the most comfortable of escapes. Whether you choose a one or two bedroom suite, a royal suite or the penthouse, our accommodations are the epitome of elegant living in Tenerife, where your personal space is the perfect luxury.
At the Citadel
The Imperial Suite
Perched on the top floor of the iconic Citadel, the Imperial Suite is a sanctuary where the sky meets the sea in endless harmony. Bathed in golden light, the vast terrace with its own private pool whispers of distant horizons, while each room, draped in subtle elegance, invites serenity. Here, time slows down, and every moment becomes a dance of relaxation and nature’s beauty, a perfect symphony for the soul to unwind and dream.

The Luxury of Your Own Time and Space
