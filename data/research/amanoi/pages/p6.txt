# What's on at Amanoi

Every Monday to Friday evening

### Spanish Culinary Nights with Chef Rey

Savour the vibrant flavours of Spain with Amanoi’s Executive Chef, Reynaldo Deluna (Rey), in the culinary celebration inspired by the country’s rich regional traditions. Using seasonal, locally sourced ingredients, Chef Rey presents modern interpretations of classic Spanish dishes, balancing bold flavours with refined technique.

Every Saturday evening

### Barbecue Dinner

Join us every Saturday at the Beach Club restaurant for a barbecue dinner with the ocean as a spectacular backdrop. The evening features a thoughtfully prepared selection of seafood and grilled dishes, shared in a relaxed yet refined setting on the sand.

From 6 October 2026 - 1 January 2027

### Musical Duo - Tarsha and Jamelo

A dynamic duo from Cape Town, Tarsha and Jamelo brings their soulful energy and six-year musical partnership to Amanoi's shores. Renowned for their versatility across genres, from smooth jazz to contemporary classics their sound is both refined and deeply engaging. With performances spanning the globe, they now take the stage in Vietnam, performing five nights a week to elevate the evening ambience at Amanoi's bar.

Available year-round

### Vietnamese Cooking Class

Discover the flavours of Vietnam in a private hands-on cooking class: a delicious introduction to the country’s culinary tradition. Select three dishes with favourites including phở bò (beef noodle soup) and gỏi cuốn (rice paper rolls). Afterwards, sit down to savour the dishes you created.

Available year-round

### The Lost Kingdom of Cham

Discover the enduring heritage of the Cham people, an ancient civilisation that once shaped Vietnam’s southern coast. The journey begins with a guided tour of the Cham Museum and continues at the 13th-century Po Klong Garai Cham Towers, remarkably preserved structures offering sweeping views across the landscape.

Available year-round

### Cham Pottery Class

Try your hand at Cham pottery, a centuries-old craft still practiced in the nearby village of Bau Truc, where each piece is hand-shaped and fired with rice husks and straw. Join a local artisan for an immersive workshop and learn to create pottery the Cham way.
