# Luxury Midtown Hotel Near International Convention and Exhibition Center I Park Hyatt Shenzhen

## Unfolding a Journey of Rare and Intimate Experiences

Park Hyatt Shenzhen is a sophisticated 5-star hotel in the heart of the Futian business district. Featuring world class bars and restaurants and unparalleled event venues, the hotel is an Asian-inspired sanctuary designed to restore, re-energize and ignite the imagination.
