# Luxurious Rooms and Suites in Shenzhen \| Park Hyatt Shenzhen

# Accommodations

Check-In3:00 PM

Checkout12:00 PM

Experience the finest in luxury accommodations at Park Hyatt Shenzhen, with 195 oversized guestrooms including 24 suites from 48 to 300 square meters. Guestrooms span floors 36 through 44, fitted with floor-to-ceiling windows offering breathtaking views of Shenzhen city center and all residential comforts.
Each room comes with 500-thread-count linen and down duvet, 55-inch LED TV with an extensive selection of TV programs and a Nespresso coffee machine.
Designed by renowned designer Yabu Pushelberg, all guestrooms feature unique custom-made furniture and art, distinct dressing zone separated by French sliding doors, separate bathtub and rainshower, powder room for privacy with automatic washlet, motion sensor activated lighting and enhanced day & night lighting modes.
