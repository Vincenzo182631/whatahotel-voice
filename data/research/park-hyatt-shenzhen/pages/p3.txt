## The Attic

The Attic is a “speakeasy-esque” bar, located at the apex of the hotel. Blending heritage with modern inspiration in every signature cocktail, the bar boasts an extensive collection of premium gins and curated spirits. As live music sets the evening’s rhythm, guests can fully immerse themselves in the elevated ambiance. For those seeking a more exclusive experience, the sophisticated KTV room provides the perfect setting for high-end parties and bespoke celebrations, making every moment unforgettable above the clouds.​

## The Glasshouse

Perched on the 47th floor, The Glasshouse is a sun-drenched sanctuary under a magnificent glass ceiling, offering panoramic 270 degree views of the Shenzhen skyline. This sky-high oasis serves a refined selection of global cuisine paired with curated wines. From sophisticated weekday lunch buffet and vibrant weekend brunch buffet to a lavish evening buffet featuring lobster, seafood, and premium beef, every meal is an artistic blend of tradition and creativity. Against a backdrop of shifting light and glittering cityscapes, our open kitchen invites you to an elegant, soulful dining experience above the clouds.​
