# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self-Parking

Park Hyatt Shenzhen offers complimentary self-parking for guests. For every CNY200 spent at the hotel, visitors receive a coupon for 1 hour of complimentary parking. For guests not staying at the hotel, the parking fees are as follows:

First 15 minutes (complimentary)

Free
