# Where passion and precision are one

## Properly   _Inventive_

Since first opening the doors of Corinthia Palace in Malta six decades ago, we have sustained our legacy by creating iconic addresses in some of the world's most celebrated cities. Today, our family of hotels continues to grow, each location renowned for its prestigious setting and elegant interiors. But what truly sets us apart is not just where we are, but how we are: our commitment to captivating service and a special spirit that breathes life into every stay.

## Moments   _of Magic_

With each new chapter in our story, we evolve the essence of luxury hospitality, masterfully blending heritage with innovation. It’s a fusion that creates meaningful and memorable experiences. Or, as some might say, moments of magic.

## Our partners

As the famous saying goes, you are the company you keep, so at Corinthia Hotels we have always prioritised building quality relationships with brilliant partners.

-

### Hannah Van Ongevalle

### Hannah Van Ongevalle

Join Hannah Van Ongevalle at Under The Stairs for inventive drinks in a cosy atmosphere. After establishing the renowned cocktail bar The Pharmacy in Knokke, Hannah embarks on her first solo venture, bringing her expertise and flair from her many successful adventures.

-

### Tom Kerridge

### Tom Kerridge

In Kerridge’s Bar & Grill at Corinthia London, renowned British chef Tom Kerridge prepares a unique twist on the nation’s beloved dishes. Alongside Tom, head chef Tom Childs reimagines classic British cuisine, skillfully employing traditional techniques to cater to contemporary tastes.

-

### Emilie Bazzocchi

### Emilie Bazzocchi

Meet Emilie Bazzocchi, General Manager for Sisley BeLux and the visionary behind Corinthia Spa, Brussels. Now, her expertise has transformed Brussels' newest spa into an icon for the future, setting a new standard for wellness in the city.

-

### Salvatore Calabrese

### Salvatore Calabrese

'Maestro' Salvatore Calabrese, renowned drinks expert and author, infuses his passion for mixology into Velvet by Salvatore Calabrese at Corinthia London. Drawing inspiration from the Roaring Twenties, Salvatore transports guests to the glamorous era of speakeasies and jazz clubs with panache and theatrical flair.

-

### Francesco Mazzei

### Francesco Mazzei

Mazzei has been recognised as one of the great Italian chefs for over four decades, having honed his skills in five-star hotels and Michelin-starred restaurants around the world. At Villa Corinthia in Corinthia Palace, Francesco and his team delight guests with Italian classics and signature favourites.

-

### Carlo Cracco

### Carlo Cracco

Italian celebrity chef Carlo Cracco, celebrated with Three Forks by Gambero Rosso and Five Hats by La Guida dell’Espresso, brings his culinary artistry to the ground floor salons of Corinthia Rome. As a pioneer of progressive Italian cooking, Carlo offers a unique twist on Italian gastronomy, promising a scintillating dining experience for all occasions and throughout the day.

-

### Casa Tua

### Casa Tua

Masterfully overseen by Miky Grendene and Leticia Herrera-Grendene, Casa Tua is an exclusive private members' club with distinguished outposts in Miami, Aspen and Paris. Now to debut its Italian flair in New York at The Surrey, A Corinthia Hotel, Casa Tua is set to become a favoured retreat for global celebrities, marking its first foray into the city.

-

### Samy Sass

### Samy Sass

Portuguese entrepreneur Samy Sass brings the Sass' brand to Corinthia Bucharest, extending the legacy established by Monaco’s iconic Sass' Café. This thrilling collaboration promises world-class service and electrifying entertainment, capturing the vibrant energy that has made Sass' one of the industry’s most sought-after dining experiences.

-

### Marvin Gauci

### Marvin Gauci

Marvin Gauci, a celebrated Maltese chef and restaurateur, is renowned for his innovative culinary ventures across Malta and Hungary. A longstanding collaborator with Corinthia Hotels, Marvin expertly oversees five of the group’s dining venues, including the award-winning Caviar & Bull at Corinthia Budapest.

-

### Jude Cassidy

### Jude Cassidy

Textile artist Jude Cassidy blends tradition with contemporary artistry. Woven with 10,000 pearls in over 250 hours, her bespoke tapestry now graces our lobby as a tribute to time-honoured craftsmanship.

-

### David Martin

### David Martin

Experience the culinary artistry of Chef David Martin at La Paix, his acclaimed two Michelin-starred restaurant, coming soon to Corinthia Brussels' magnificent heritage setting.

-

### Christophe Hardiquest

### Christophe Hardiquest

Experience brasserie-style dining with Chef Christophe Hardiquest at the helm. After achieving acclaim at the Michelin-starred Menssa and previously 2 Michelin-starred Bon Bon, Christophe revitalises his celebrated Bon Bon brand in this new brasserie.

-

### Bianca Olimpia Abăcioaei

### Bianca Olimpia Abăcioaei

Bianca’s bespoke print, chosen through a competition celebrating local talent, now features across our hotel’s amenities. Inspired by the elegance of the Belle Époque era, her exceptional work brings a unique artistic touch to Corinthia Bucharest.

-

### Simon Casson

### Simon Casson

Contemporary artist Simon Casson has adorned our hotel lobby with majestic murals, seamlessly weaving Romania’s regal past into the present. These striking works of art reimagine history with creativity, depth and elegance.

-

### Cristina Balan

### Cristina Balan

Perfumer Cristina Balan, Romania’s first graduate of the Grasse Institute of Perfumery, has crafted an exclusive scent for Corinthia Bucharest. A fragrance shaped by history, designed for today.
