## History  _finds its modern muse_

In Rome’s historic heart, Corinthia Rome opens the doors of a grand palazzo on Piazza del Parlamento. Long associated as a stronghold of the Bank of Italy, the landmark is reborn as a noble Roman residence where majestic architecture meets the intimate spirit of boutique hospitality. Carlo Cracco’s dining sets the social rhythm, leafy courtyards evoke Rome’s tradition of lively, open-hearted hospitality and beneath the palazzo, a subterranean spa brings restoration and renewal. This is Rome at its most intimate and expressive - a place to stay, to linger and to belong.

## Corinthia Spa, Rome

**Beneath the neoclassical grace of Corinthia Rome lies a hidden spa sanctuary.**

Once the bank’s historic vault, the space now protects something far more precious: time for yourself. Inspired by the ritual of Roman bathing, the Corinthia Spa unfolds as an intimate subterranean retreat shaped by water, mineral stone and softened light, offering a quiet contrast to the energy of the city above.

## Discover Rome

Located on Piazza del Parlamento in the heart of the historic Campo Marzio district, Corinthia Rome celebrates the Colonna area’s storied past and creative spirit. The Pantheon, Roman Forum and Spanish Steps echo with centuries of history, while neighbourhoods like Trastevere and Monti pulse with contemporary energy.
