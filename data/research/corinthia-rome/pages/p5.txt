## Peter Roth

###### President, Hotel Operations

MEET PETER

Peter is responsible for overseeing the operational performance of Corinthia’s existing portfolio while leading operational readiness for the Group’s upcoming openings in Italy, Doha, Riyadh, Dubai, the Maldives and Chengdu.

He joins Corinthia with more than three decades of international luxury hospitality leadership experience across Europe, the Americas and the Middle East. Throughout his career, he has built a reputation for driving operational excellence, developing high-performing leadership teams and delivering exceptional guest experiences across some of the world’s most respected luxury hospitality brands.

## Alberto Ramos

###### Chief Financial Officer

Meet Alberto

With over 15 years of financial leadership in the hospitality industry and a proven track record of driving commercial success and operational efficiency, Alberto's career spans on-property, regional, and corporate office roles with major hotel owners and operators. Most recently, he served as Group Finance Director for the hotel division of the Virgin Group, where he oversaw a diverse portfolio of ultra-luxury and luxury lifestyle properties across multiple jurisdictions. Alberto's professional profile includes an MBA, and he is also a Chartered Accountant.

Alberto leads the finance team and is responsible for driving Corinthia's financial strategy, ensuring robust financial performance, and supporting strategic growth initiatives. He champions financial excellence, fosters accountability, and supports the company’s long-term objectives.

## Christian Renz

###### Chief Commercial Officer

Meet Christian

Christian focuses on leading our commercial teams including Sales, Marketing, PR, Communications, Digital, Revenue Management and Loyalty for all Corinthia hotels, both in our existing portfolio and new openings.

Previously he served as the Senior Vice President of Sales and Revenue Management at Rocco Forte Hotels. He has also held senior management roles at Kempinski Hotels, The Walt Disney Company and Starwood and Marriott, with extensive experience in Europe and the United States, and holds an MBA.

## Kirsten Lord

###### CHIEF PEOPLE & CULTURE OFFICER

Meet Kirsten

Kirsten leads our global People and Culture function, working alongside our talented team to drive initiatives that support our mission to create inspiring destinations and deliver exceptional guest experiences. She also plays a crucial role in further embedding the Spirit of Corinthia throughout our organisational culture, fostering an environment that promotes employee well-being and growth.

Kirsten brings over 20 years of leadership experience in people and culture initiatives having held senior roles at prominent organisations, including BVLGARI Hotels and Associated British Foods.

## Roderick Micallef

###### Chief Projects & Corporate Services Officer

Meet Roderick

Roderick is responsible for Corinthia’s Projects and Corporate Services functions, supporting the Group’s ambitious growth strategy and the successful development and delivery of its expanding portfolio. As Corinthia enters its next phase of growth, he has been entrusted with a broader role in supporting the Group’s expansion and future development.

With extensive experience and a long standing career at Corinthia Hotels, he brings a broad understanding of the business, having held several senior positions across the Group, including Chief Operating Officer, Vice President of Operations and General Manager at Corinthia Lisbon.
