# Discover Corinthia Hotels

Discover Corinthia's collection of luxury five-star hotels across Europe, North Africa and the Middle East. From landmark city hotels in London, New York, Rome and Brussels to Mediterranean resorts in Malta, every Corinthia hotel reflects the character of its destination while offering exceptional hospitality, elegant design and thoughtful experiences that inspire every journey.

CORINTHIA LONDON

## London's most luxurious hotel

One of London's finest luxury hotels, Corinthia London enjoys an exceptional Whitehall address, moments from Westminster, Trafalgar Square, Covent Garden and the River Thames. Behind its grand Victorian façade, Biome by Corinthia London redefines wellbeing, while acclaimed restaurants including Mezzogiorno by Francesco Mazzei and Kerridge's Bar & Grill, alongside Velvet by Salvatore Calabrese, make the hotel one of the capital's most exciting culinary destinations. Elegant rooms, spacious suites and spectacular penthouses complete a five-star stay at the heart of London.

The Surrey, A Corinthia Hotel

## A five star stay on New York's Upper East Side

A luxury five-star hotel on Manhattan's Upper East Side, The Surrey is a celebrated design icon reborn. Just moments from Central Park, Fifth Avenue and Museum Mile, it offers an intimate residential atmosphere in one of New York's most prestigious neighbourhoods. Home to luxury rooms and suites designed by Martin Brudnizki, Casa Tua and a jewel-box spa with a secluded terrace dedicated to wellbeing, The Surrey blends timeless sophistication with contemporary luxury for an authentic Upper East Side experience.

CORINTHIA ROME

## Stay in the heart of historic Rome

A luxury hotel in the heart of Rome, Corinthia Rome brings new life to a magnificent neoclassical palazzo that once served as a Bank of Italy building. Nestled within the artisan quarter of Campo Marzio, just moments from the Pantheon, Trevi Fountain and Spanish Steps, the hotel pairs Roman grandeur with contemporary elegance.Its 60 rooms and suites offer an intimate residential feel, while Michelin-starred chef Carlo Cracco oversees its distinctive restaurants and bars, while the intimate Corinthia Spa, created within the building's former bank vaults, offers a remarkable sanctuary in the Eternal City.

DISCOVER CORINTHIA ROME

CORINTHIA BRUSSELS

## Brussels' luxury landmark hotel

A landmark luxury hotel in Brussels, Corinthia Brussels marks the celebrated return of the iconic Grand Hotel Astoria on prestigious Rue Royale. Beautifully restored Belle Époque architecture frames elegant rooms and suites, Michelin-starred dining by David Martin and Christophe Hardiquest, and inventive cocktails from Hannah Van Ongevalle. With the Corinthia Spa, heritage salons and a vibrant connection to the city's creative community, it reclaims its place as one of Brussels' most remarkable addresses.

DISCOVER CORINTHIA BRUSSELS

CORINTHIA PALACE

## A Mediterranean escape in Malta

The original Corinthia hotel in Malta, Corinthia Palace is where the story of the brand began in 1968. Set beside the beautiful San Anton Gardens and within easy reach of Valletta and Mdina, this tranquil retreat celebrates the island's rich heritage through warm hospitality and exceptional cuisine. Home to an intimate collection of elegant rooms and suites, Villa Corinthia by Francesco Mazzei, Michelin-starred BAHIA, Secret Garden, the plant-based Josephine's café and the restorative Athenaeum Spa, it remains a true love letter to Malta.

CORINTHIA BUCHAREST

## Bucharest's Belle Époque landmark hotel

An all-suite luxury hotel in Bucharest, Corinthia Grand Hotel du Boulevard breathes new life into one of Romania's most distinguished Belle Époque landmarks. Perched on the edge of the Old Town, the hotel offers just 30 beautifully appointed suites, creating an intimate and refined stay. Discover vibrant Mediterranean dining at Sass' Restaurant & Lounge, elegant cocktails at Heritage Bar, a luxurious spa and one of the city's most glamorous and historic ballrooms.

DISCOVER CORINTHIA BUCHAREST

Corinthia Lisbon

## Stay beside Lisbon's Águas Livres Aqueduct

A luxury five-star hotel in Lisbon, Corinthia Lisbon enjoys a privileged location overlooking the historic Águas Livres Aqueduct, with easy access to the city's cultural landmarks and vibrant neighbourhoods. Alongsideelegant rooms and suites andone of the city's leading spa and wellness experiences, guests can enjoy contemporary Portuguese dining at Erva Restaurante, a fusion of Latin America and Asian cuisine at Soul Garden and relaxed al fresco moments at Olivae Restaurant & Terrace. Blending exceptional wellbeing with vibrant culinary experiences, Corinthia Lisbon offers an elegant base from which to discover the Portuguese capital.

Corinthia Budapest

## A five star stay in grand Budapest

One of Budapest's most iconic luxury hotels, Corinthia Budapest has welcomed guests from its grand boulevard address since 1896. A masterpiece of Neo-Renaissance architecture, the hotel is home to the beautifully restored Royal Spa, one of the city's most celebrated wellness destinations, alongside refined rooms and suites, elegant restaurants, stylish bars and magnificent event spaces. Just moments from Andrássy Avenue and the city's famous thermal baths, it offers an unforgettable five-star stay in the Hungarian capital.

corinthia st petersburg

## Stay along the Neva River in St Petersburg

A luxury hotel on St Petersburg's famous Nevsky Prospekt, Corinthia St Petersburg places guests at the heart of one of Europe's great cultural capitals. Within easy reach of the Hermitage Museum, theatres, palaces and elegant canals, the hotel combines refined accommodation with acclaimed dining and versatile event spaces. Its historic setting and long-standing connection to the city's cultural life make it an enduring address for discovering St Petersburg.

## Luxury seafront resort in Malta

Corinthia St George's Bay enjoys a spectacular waterfront setting overlooking the Mediterranean. Spacious rooms and suites, multiple restaurants and bars, rejuvenating spa experiences and direct access to the coastline create an effortless island escape. Close to both Valletta and Malta's vibrant entertainment district, it balances relaxed resort living with opportunities to explore the island's rich history and culture.

DISCOVER CORINTHIA TRIPOLI

## Tripoli's leading luxury hotel

A luxury hotel in Tripoli, Corinthia Tripoli overlooks the Mediterranean, where ancient history and modern ambition meet. From the UNESCO-listed Medina and bustling souks to the city's growing commercial district, Libya's capital offers a unique perspective on North Africa. Ideally positioned on the waterfront, Corinthia Tripoli combines spacious accommodation, renowned dining and panoramic sea views with easy access to the city's cultural landmarks and business centres, making it a distinguished base for both business and leisure travellers.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Why stay with Corinthia Hotels?

Whether you're planning a luxury city break, a relaxing resort escape, a family holiday, a business trip or a milestone celebration, Corinthia's collection of luxury five-star hotels offers distinctive experiences in some of the world's most remarkable destinations. From award-winning spas and acclaimed restaurants and bars to landmark architecture and thoughtfully curated experiences, every stay is designed to inspire meaningful connections with the destination and the people around you.

Guided by our philosophy of Grand Boutique, we combine the intimacy and personal service of a boutique hotel with the elegance and presence of a grande dame, creating experiences shaped by genuine hospitality, Mediterranean warmth and a deep sense of place.

Where are Corinthia Hotels located?

Corinthia Hotels can be found across Europe, North Africa and the Middle East, with luxury city hotels, coastal resorts and landmark properties in destinations including London, Rome, Bucharest, New York, Brussels, Malta, Budapest, Lisbon, St Petersburg and Tripoli. Exciting future hotel openings for Corinthia Hotels include Doha, Riyadh, Lake Como, Tuscany, Chengdu, Maldives, Dubai and Turks & Caicos.

Are all Corinthia Hotels five-star?

Yes. Every Corinthia hotel offers five-star accommodation, combining thoughtful service, distinctive design and authentic hospitality in exceptional destinations.

Which Corinthia hotels feature acclaimed culinary partners?

Several Corinthia hotels are home to celebrated restaurants created in partnership with internationally renowned chefs. Discover Viride by Carlo Cracco at Corinthia Rome, Villa Corinthia by Francesco Mazzei at Corinthia Palace in Malta, Mezzogiorno by Francesco Mazzei and Kerridge's Bar & Grill by Tom Kerridge at Corinthia London, Palais Royal by David Martin and Le Petit bon bon by Christophe Hardiquest at Corinthia Brussels, and more.

Which Corinthia hotels have luxury spas?

Many Corinthia hotels feature award-winning spa and wellness experiences, including Biome by Corinthia London, the Royal Spa in Budapest, a Corinthia Spa in both Brussels and Rome, the Athenaeum Spa at Corinthia Palace, THE SPA by Corinthia Lisbon, and more.

Which Corinthia hotels are in Malta?

Malta is home to Corinthia Palace, where the Corinthia story began and Corinthia St George's Bay, a luxury seafront resort overlooking the Mediterranean.

Are Corinthia Hotels suitable for families?

Yes. Many Corinthia hotels welcome families with spacious rooms and suites, interconnecting accommodation at selected hotels, family-friendly dining and personalised experiences for all ages. Whether you're planning a city break in London, Rome or Budapest, or a Mediterranean escape in Malta, Corinthia offers thoughtful hospitality designed to make travelling together effortless.
