# About Corinthia

## Grand   _Boutique_

Founded six decades ago in Malta by the Pisani family, Corinthia Hotels has evolved into a globally renowned luxury brand with properties in some of the world’s most prestigious destinations. Each hotel is an emblem of grand boutique luxury, offering the exclusivity and personal touch of a boutique establishment combined with the grandeur of a grande dame.

A note from our Founder

"Corinthia began as a family business, and while it has grown into something rather different today, we still retain all the precious values of a family: caring for one another and offering each other personal support each and every day. We listen to and spur each other on, to bring out the best in one another – as professionals, and more importantly, as human beings."

_Alfred Pisani_ Executive Chairman & Founder

## Our story

Rooted in a rich history and an unwavering commitment to excellence, Corinthia Hotels embodies the vision and determination of its founder, Alfred Pisani. What began as Villa Refalo, a modest restaurant, was transformed into the inaugural Corinthia Hotel in 1968. Today, it stands as the illustrious Corinthia Palace, forming the cornerstone of the Corinthia Hotels brand.

## The Spirit of Corinthia

Beyond our prime locations, luxurious interiors and personalised service, Corinthia’s unique spirit genuinely sets us apart.

## The next chapter

Since its inception at the Corinthia Palace, Corinthia Hotels has expanded significantly. Today, while remaining deeply rooted in the vibrant Mediterranean, our family of hotels continues to flourish, with prestigious locations in London, New York, Rome, Brussels, Bucharest, Doha, Riyadh and beyond.
