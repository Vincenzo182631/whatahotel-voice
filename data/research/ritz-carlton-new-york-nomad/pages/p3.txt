# Suites with Views in New York City

## Suites

At The Ritz-Carlton New York, NoMad, our luxurious suites offer an elevated residential-style experience in the heart of Manhattan. Featuring spacious layouts, sophisticated living areas, and thoughtfully curated amenities, these accommodations provide the comfort and privacy of a refined city retreat. Ideal for extended stays, family getaways, or simply enjoying extra space, guests can unwind in an atmosphere that feels distinctly personal.

### The Ritz-Carlton Suite

The Ritz-Carlton Suite features decorative touches, evoking a sense of place in the heart of NoMad. At an expansive 2,100 square feet, this luxury hotel suite in Manhattan, New York, is complete with a dining area, bedroom, media room and wellness area.

### The Liberty Suite

The Liberty Suite offers an elevated retreat above Manhattan. Spanning 850 square feet, this expansive suite features a sophisticated living room, a luxurious terrazzo marble bathroom, and sweeping views of Downtown Manhattan and the Statue of Liberty. Elevate your stay with the addition of a second bedroom by connecting your suite to an adjoining Skyline Double, creating a spacious two-bedroom retreat. For an even more exclusive experience, upgrade to a Liberty Club Suite and enjoy daily access to The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge.

The Liberty Suite

### A Sophisticated Stay

Enter elevated design and a uniquely peaceful atmosphere in the Liberty Suite. Located on levels 31-33 is situated high above our vibrant NoMad neighborhood. An inviting living room, sophisticated furnishings, and a spa-inspired terrazzo marble bathroom create an environment that encourages guests to slow down and settle in.

### The Madison Suite

Above the heart of NoMad, the Madison Suite offers a sophisticated perspective on the city, with views stretching along Broadway toward Downtown Manhattan with a direct view of the Empire State Building. Elevate your stay with the addition of a second bedroom by connecting your suite to an adjoining Empire State Double, creating a spacious two-bedroom retreat.

The Madison Suite

### City Living in a Suite

Perched above the heart of Manhattan, the Madison Suite embodies the sophistication of modern city living. Captivating views stretch across NoMad to the Empire State Building and Downtown Manhattan place you at the center of it all. A signature highlight is the terrazzo marble bathroom, where skyline views transform everyday rituals into memorable moments.
