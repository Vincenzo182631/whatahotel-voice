## Discover Madison Square Park

NoMad is home to Madison Square Park, a public space that has existed since 1686. Its rich history makes it a draw for local residents and travelers. This icon is surrounded by iconic buildings such as the Flatiron Building and serves as a place for gatherings, open-air exhibitions, dining, performances and is home to Jemmy’s Dog Run.

## The Flower District

Since the early 1890’s, 28th Street in Manhattan has been home to NYC’s top flower markets – a hidden botanical gem in the bustling city located steps away from the hotel. According to the New York Times, in 1977, more flowers were bought and sold in the Flower District than anywhere else in the world. Visit as early as 6 AM to choose your favorite blooms or to take in the greenery and vibrant flowers that line Sixth and Seventh Avenue.

## Little Island

Located on Pier 54, Little Island is a truly unique public park with distinct architecture that celebrates the evolution of New York City’s waterfront. Visitors can experience direct immersion in art and nature with multi-generational and educational programming, live performances, and collaborations with local artists.

### Endless Attractions in New York

Start your next adventure at our luxury hotel near Hudson Yards in New York City, north of Madison Square Park. Walk to Madison Square Park, shop at Hudson Yards, and visit the art galleries in Chelsea or the cultural institutions on Museum Mile.

## Connect to Art

New York City is the center of art for enthusiasts of all kinds. From ancient to contemporary art, you can start on the Museum Mile and head to Chelsea for blue-chip galleries. Venture further to Brooklyn where you can find galleries featuring emerging artists in Williamsburg.
