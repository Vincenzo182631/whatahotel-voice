# Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton New York, NoMad

## A New York City Holiday

Celebrate the season in the heart of Manhattan at The Ritz-Carlton New York, NoMad. Enjoy thoughtfully curated holiday guest offerings, including festive in-suite décor, exclusive seasonal amenities, and access to our private Club Lounge. Discover the magic of New York City's iconic holiday attractions, then unwind with distinctive culinary experiences from the José Andrés Group, creating cherished memories throughout your holiday stay.

Delight in the Season

### The Ritz-Carlton Suite

Make The Ritz-Carlton Suite your home for the holidays. Experience the magic of New York City’s most enchanting season from the comfort of an extraordinary residential-style retreat. Thoughtfully curated festive touches, exclusive holiday experiences, and access to a coveted seasonal event create an unforgettable stay, all set against the backdrop of Manhattan’s iconic holiday traditions.

### A Ritz Kids Holiday

Enjoy an exclusive Ritz Kids experience in the comfort of your suite or residence. The youngest of guests will enjoy a holiday tradition facing the city’s unrivaled views complete with seasonal treats by José Andrés Group.

### Enhance Your Stay

Enter your guestroom and be greeted by festive cheer with a festive holiday tree carefully decorated by our Ladies and Gentlemen. This unique holiday offering will add memorable touches to your stay experience. To learn more, contact Guest Relations.

### Club Lounge

Festive season in The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge will feature one-of-a-kind offerings this holiday season. Led by the Ladies and Gentlemen of our Club Lounge team exclusive and unique experiences await guests for the season. Available to guests reserved in a Club Level guestroom, suite or penthouse residence.

### The Gift of Relaxation

Indulge in the gift of serenity this holiday season for yourself or someone you love. At The Ritz-Carlton Spa, bespoke holistic treatments await, thoughtfully tailored to restore balance and renew your spirit as you step into the new year.

### The Holidays, José's Way

Stay tuned for upcoming and exciting culinary programming by José Andrés Group celebrating Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year’s Eve.
