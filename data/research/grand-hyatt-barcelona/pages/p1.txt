## A Grand Address in Barcelona's Most Exclusive District

Grand Hyatt Barcelona sits at the heart of Pedralbes, one of the city's most exclusive and sought-after districts, offering a five-star retreat defined by contemporary design and striking, sophisticated grandeur. Football fans will find themselves just steps from Spotify Camp Nou, home of FC Barcelona, while the hotel's setting in Alta Barcelona keeps the city's business and cultural landmarks within easy reach. The property's culinary program takes center stage: Leña brings Dani García's signature wood-fired cooking to Barcelona for the first time, while Maymanta lifts Peruvian cuisine to a dramatic 19th-floor rooftop, pairing bold flavors with breathtaking skyline views. Guests can carry that sense of discovery into Oasis Wellness Spa by Natura Bissé, an immersive sanctuary where a hydrotherapy pool, sauna and Turkish bath create a place to slow down between meetings or a day exploring Barcelona.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Grand Hyatt Barcelona's 415 rooms, from 24 to 29 square meters, bring a striking luxury design infused with Mediterranean spirit to every stay. Custom-made furniture, a separate rain shower and WC and Natura Bissé bathroom amenities complete each room, with sweeping panoramic views of the city throughout. Choose from the Premium King Room, Deluxe King Room, Queen Room or Premium Twin Room.

For a more expansive stay, the hotel's 50 suites range from 59 to 319 square meters, with Art Deco-inspired design, many featuring a walk-in closet and exclusive access to Grand Club. From the Junior Suite to the Iconic Suite, Grand Suite and Penthouse, each is set on the hotel's higher floors with stunning views of Barcelona.

Autism Friendly Certification

Grand Hyatt Barcelona has received "Autism Friendly" certification and the "Hotel Autism Friendly" award, reflecting the hotel's commitment to an inclusive experience for all guests. Staff have completed specialized training and adaptations throughout the hotel, including the use of pictograms, help create a welcoming environment for guests on the autism spectrum.

Grand Hyatt Barcelona continues to adapt so that every guest, regardless of neurological condition, can feel at home.

SUSTAINABILITY COMMITMENT

Our commitment to sustainability is reflected in two distinguished recognitions that affirm our pursuit of excellence in environmental and social responsibility. We have been awarded the **Biosphere Sustainable certification**, acknowledging our alignment with the United Nations’ 2030 Agenda and its 17 Sustainable Development Goals, as well as our dedication to engaging our teams and fostering meaningful impact across our operations. Complementing this achievement, the **LEED Gold certification** for Building Operations and Maintenance underscores our adherence to rigorous global standards in sustainable building performance.

Together, these certifications highlight a continuous and purposeful commitment to responsible progress.

## EXPLORE BARCELONA

Grand Hyatt Barcelona sits close to iconic landmarks including Casa Batlló, Casa Milà (La Pedrera) and the Basilica of the Sagrada Familia, making it a well-placed base for exploring the city's extraordinary cultural heritage. Just steps from the hotel, the Monastery of Pedralbes, the Real Club de Tennis Barcelona and the local stalls of Les Corts Market reveal a quieter, residential side of the city, while Park Güell remains a short ride away. Beyond the city, guests can venture to the beaches of Castelldefels or take part in cava tastings in the surrounding countryside.
