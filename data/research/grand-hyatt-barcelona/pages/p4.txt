### Grand Hyatt Barcelona – Exceptional Stay!!!

Grand Hyatt Barcelona is one of those rare hotels where the property is beautiful enough to impress you when you walk in, but the service is what you’re still thinking about after you leave.

I stayed in room 1535 from October 16 through October 20, and from the moment I arrived, the experience was exceptional. Check-in was quick, personalized and genuinely warm. My Hyatt status was immediately recognized, and I was thanked for my loyalty. I even recevied a nice little upgrade to a premium King with a beautiful view. The room itself was excellent and positioned on what I believe was the west side of the hotel, which gave me incredible sunset views every evening. The bed, pillows and overall sleep quality were phenomenal, and housekeeping consistently did an excellent job throughout the stay. The service across the hotel was equally impressive. From the doormen and front desk staff to the concierge, bartenders, everyone I interacted with was attentive, professional and genuinely welcoming.

This hotel also has an excellent collection of dining venues, and I made it a point to visit nearly all of them. Leña was outstanding! The atmosphere is beautiful, the room has a great energy to it, and the entire experience felt polished without becoming stiff or overly formal. Maymanta is another highlight and something I would strongly recommend to anyone staying at the hotel. The rooftop setting alone is worth visiting, especially around sunset, but the food was even better than expected. I had one of their ceviche dishes and it was one of the best ceviches I have ever tasted. It was the kind of dish where you stop halfway through eating it and realize, “Okay, this is on another level.”

One of the funniest moments of my stay was room service. Late one night, close to 3:00 a.m., we ordered pizza, chicken fingers and fries. It sounds simple, but I am not exaggerating when I say the chicken fingers were some of the best I have ever had. What made it even more special, was the order was personally delivered by the chef. At that hour of the night, after a long day, having the actual chef arrive at the room with the food was an unexpected level of service that made the entire experience feel incredibly personal.

I also want to address the location because I think some travelers may initially look at the map and assume the hotel is too far removed from central Barcelona. It really is not. The city center is an easy taxi ride away, public transportation and the metro are accessible, and there is plenty happening in the surrounding neighborhood. In fact, I actually preferred the location because it felt less touristy and gave the stay a little more of an authentic feel. You can easily get into the heart of the city when you want to, but when you return to the hotel, you are stepping away from some of the congestion and intensity of the most heavily visited areas. For me, that was a plus, not a drawback.

I stay at Hyatt properties regularly, and my personal favorite remains Alila Mayakoba, but Grand Hyatt Barcelona is now comfortably at #2. I would absolutely stay here again, and I would strongly recommend it to anyone visiting Barcelona.

Do yourself a favor, have a drink at Leña, watch the sunset from Maymanta, order the ceviche, and do not sleep on the 3 a.m. chicken fingers.

### Very good hotel with one real weak spot

The suite itself is genuinely impressive — spacious, high floor, great views, and cleverly laid out with four large sliding doors that let you separate the living and dining area, the bedroom, and the bathroom. The bathroom is excellent, and they replaced the bath salts daily, which was a nice touch. One of the four sliding doors was broken, though: the handle was damaged and it would only close halfway.

The bigger in-room issue was the air conditioning, which wouldn’t go below 23°C no matter what we set it to. It read as a capacity problem rather than a deliberate limit — with the curtains drawn it would get colder, but this was a very hot August and it wasn’t quite cool enough for sleeping.

The spa circuit — included with the suite — was fabulous. Cold plunge, hot and warm pools, infrared sauna, wet and dry saunas, all beautifully done. The breakfast buffet, also included, was one of the better ones I’ve had at a Grand Hyatt or comparable property. We ate at Maymanta, the Peruvian rooftop restaurant, and it was very good with a terrific view. The café on the lobby level was the one miss: overpriced, and the service was odd.

The elevators are the weakest link and it’s a genuine problem. The system assigns you to a car, and several times during our stay it simply told us no elevator was available — everything full — leaving us stranded on the 18th floor. This building is tall enough that the 18th floor feels like the 25th elsewhere, so the stairs aren’t a realistic option. Waits were long enough that people would press repeatedly and assign themselves to multiple cars, so doors would open with nobody waiting. Occasionally a car would stop and the doors would seem to try to open and then not. For a hotel this new, it’s surprising.

One thing to know going in: there are a lot of children here. We were on 17 and had kids running and jumping in the hallways above and around us. That’s not a complaint about the hotel exactly — we’d come straight from an adults-only property in Mykonos, so it was an adjustment.

Would we stay again? Yes. Fix the elevators and it’s excellent.
