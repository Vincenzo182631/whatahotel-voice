# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self-Parking

Private self-parking is available for guests of Grand Hyatt Barcelona. Our parking area has electric vehicle chargers including three Tesla chargers and a universal charger (Mennekes International). There is no additional charge. For reservation requirements and availability of charging stations, you can check directly with our parking staff once you arrive at the hotel.
