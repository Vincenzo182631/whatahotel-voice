Grand Hyatt Barcelona's rooms and suites pair contemporary design with an unmistakably grand sense of sophistication, just steps from Spotify Camp Nou, home of FC Barcelona and a natural retreat after a match or stadium visit. Chic aesthetics, premium amenities and plush bedding define every stay across the hotel's 415 rooms and 50 suites, with stunning views over Barcelona and its surroundings.
