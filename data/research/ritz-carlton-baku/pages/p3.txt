A Home of Your Own
Luxury Apartments in Baku, Azerbaijan
Experience the epitome of luxury and comfort at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku. Perfect for both business travelers and long-stay guests, our spacious apartments combine elegance, privacy, and convenience. Each apartment comes with benefits and features a fully equipped kitchen with a coffee machine, complete sets of cutlery, crockery, and cooking utensils, as well as a dedicated laundry room—providing all the comforts of home. With our legendary Ritz-Carlton service, every moment is crafted to be exceptional.
The Apartments
One-Bedroom Apartment
A refined urban retreat designed for urban professionals, the One-Bedroom apartment blends sophistication and functionality. Expansive living spaces accommodate both work and relaxation, while floor-to-ceiling windows fill the apartment with natural light. A fully equipped kitchen and integrated laundry facilities provide effortless convenience for extended stays. Every detail reflects a commitment to exceptional living, providing a calm sanctuary within the dynamic cityscape.
One-Bedroom Apartment
Signature Services
The apartments offer modern conveniences for digital nomads, including high-speed internet, complimentary car parking, and access to a fully equipped gym. The swimming facilities are thoughtfully arranged, with a dedicated adults’ pool separated from the family pool, creating spaces for both tranquility and leisure. Concierge assistance ensures a seamless living experience.

Imagine waking up to gentle sunlight streaming in, the aroma of breakfast drifting through the air, and laughter echoing as the day begins - a space where every moment nurtures both togetherness and serenity.
