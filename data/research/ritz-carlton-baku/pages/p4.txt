Restaurants & Bar in Baku, Azerbaijan
Dining
In Azerbaijan, the table has always been the center of everything. The Ritz-Carlton, Baku offers two places to experience that truth. Tribeca is an American brasserie open from early morning through late evening, where the kitchen draws from the hotel's own Mystique Garden and the breakfast has become something guests plan their mornings around. The Blind Tiger is Azerbaijan's first speakeasy bar, a quiet door off the lobby that opens into an entirely different evening. Beyond the daily rhythm, The Signature Journey brings Michelin-starred and Top 50 chefs to Baku for exclusive dinners and masterclasses. Weekend brunch, afternoon tea and 24-hour room service complete a dining programme that runs from the first coffee to well past midnight.

Blind Tiger Cocktail Journey
Six cocktails. Six tigers. Each drink in the Journey represents one of the world's remaining tiger species, with a story card on the table explaining the inspiration behind every creation. The mixologist prepares and narrates each pour as light bites arrive between courses. At the end of the evening, the cards are yours to take. Alcoholic, low-alcohol, and non-alcoholic variations available throughout.
Reserve Now
Azerbaijani Chef’s Table
Follow the story of Azerbaijani cuisine from garden to table. Begin in the Mystique Garden, where our Chef introduces herbs and produce grown for the kitchen, then continue to Tribeca’s open kitchen as each course is prepared before you. Stories of ingredients, family traditions, and local craft unfold throughout the meal, while curated wine pairings deepen a dining experience rooted entirely in place.

The Signature Journey
Twice a year, The Ritz-Carlton, Baku invites one of the world's most recognized bartenders to take over for two evenings: bringing their city, their stories, and their approach to the craft with them. The next chapter features Marco Dongi of Bar Sathorn, Bangkok, No. 17 on Asia's 50 Best Bars 2026, at Blind Tiger on 2 and 3 October from 9 PM. Two nights. One bartender. A menu that exists nowhere else.

Afternoon Tea
Every weekend at Tribeca, afternoon tea is served from 3 PM to 5 PM. In October, the menu follows an autumn flavors concept: seasonal pastries, house-made confections, and a choice of two cocktails, alcoholic or non-alcoholic, alongside a full tea selection. A quiet hour in the middle of the day, done properly.
More Details
Blind Tiger Live Music
Every Saturday evening, Blind Tiger fills with live music from 9 PM to closing. The bar program runs in full alongside the performance: cocktails, the full drinks menu, the atmosphere that makes a Saturday in Baku worth staying in for. No tickets required. Walk in or reserve a table to be sure of your seat.

The Ritz-Carlton Brunch
On 18 October, Tribeca goes full autumn: seasonal dishes, live cooking stations with the kitchen open and on show, warm flavors that feel exactly right for the time of year. Live music sets the pace while the children's zone runs its own parallel universe of entertainment. The Ritz-Carlton Brunch in Baku takes place every month at Tribeca: new theme, new menu, and new dishes to talk about on the way home.

The Mystique Garden
From Stem to Plate
Sustainable dining at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku starts in the Mystique Garden, where the herbs that supply Tribeca's kitchen are grown on-site throughout the season. Growing on-site reduces the kitchen's reliance on imported produce and keeps the menu rooted in what the land around Baku actually yields. What grows here in the morning can be on the plate by evening. The garden is open to all guests.
Morning at Tribeca
In Azerbaijan, breakfast is so much more than just a meal. It is a cherished morning ritual: a time for tables set with genuine care, moments shared without rush, and dishes that carry the warmth of local tradition. This same heartfelt spirit fills Tribeca every single morning. Kosher meals, vegetarian dishes, and any other dietary preference are woven into the experience before a guest needs to ask. For those eager to explore the richness of Azerbaijani cuisine, the Ladies and Gentlemen at Tribeca love nothing more than sharing the stories behind the food: from the origins of a classic recipe to the local ingredients that make it particular to this place. Whether stepping out for a full day or simply lingering over tea, the morning unfolds at exactly the pace that feels right.

Two Ways to Breakfast
Breakfast at Tribeca runs as two things at once: a full hot menu and an expansive buffet, both included and available from 6.30 AM to 11 AM every morning. The kitchen turns out shakshuka, eggs Benedict, porridge, and omelettes made to order, alongside a seasonal selection that shifts with whatever is best at the time. The buffet carries fresh fruit, delicate pastries, artisan breads, and kombucha brewed in-house. Halal options are available. Every morning, warm croissants from the oven find their way to each table.

For Every Taste
Not every guest arrives at the table with the same needs, and at Tribeca and Blind Tiger, that is entirely expected. Kosher and halal meals are prepared with careful attention. Guests with allergies or intolerances are looked after from the moment a reservation is made. For anything that falls outside the printed menu, a conversation with the team is all it takes: the kitchen is genuinely happy to work around a preference, a restriction, or simply a craving that needs a little coaxing into existence. Dining in Baku at The Ritz-Carlton should feel personal, and here, it usually does.

In-Room Dining
Available Around the Clock
In-room dining at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku is available 24 hours a day, every day. Whether a full meal at midnight or something light before an early departure, the kitchen is ready. Orders are taken at any hour, prepared fresh, and delivered to the room without delay. For guests with dietary requirements or specific requests, the same care that applies in the restaurant applies here. Room service in Baku does not stop when the rest of the city does.
