Spa at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku
Spa
Venture into the wellness haven that is The Ritz-Carlton Spa Baku, a blissful escape with seven treatment rooms including one couple's room, a relaxation room, saunas, steam rooms, hammams, a salon, a gym, and two swimming pools. A holistic and tranquil journey, the spa is home to a curated set of facials, massages, and treatments by ESPA that pays homage inspired by the natural beauty of Azerbaijan.

Experiences. The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Baku
Escape the buzz of the city and recharge in tranquility. The Ritz-Carlton Spa in Baku is a haven of relaxation and self-care, where you can embark on a tailored wellness journey with personalized treatments and trademark Ritz-Carlton service. Our signature rituals exclusively available at The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Baku will enliven your senses — mind, body, and soul.
Private Pilates Reformer Sessions
Find balance through a personalized Reformer experience that brings together mindful movement, strength and flexibility. Thoughtfully tailored to individual needs, each session supports improved posture, mobility and core strength while offering a restorative approach to overall wellbeing.
At this hotel
Hammam
An age-old tradition brought to life in style, the two Hammams at the hotel are a gateway to relaxation while easing tensions and revitalizing the skin. Open daily: 6:00 AM-11:00 PM.
Sauna & Steam
Offering a variety of health and wellness benefits, sauna bathing includes benefits such as increased metabolism, blood circulation, and effects of anti-aging, skin rejuvenation, and calmness. The hotel features two saunas and steam rooms. Open daily: 6:00 AM-11:00 PM.
Salon
Servicing both Ladies and Gentlemen, the salon at The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Baku offers a variety of hair care, manicure, and pedicure services. Prior reservation is required for all services. Open daily: 10:00 AM-10:00 PM.
Gym
Our fitness center boasts an all-encompassing approach to wellness, featuring three sections: CrossFit, Cardio Zone, and an equipped studio. Each section is outfitted with state-of-the-art equipment provided exclusively by Technogym. Open everyday: 24/7.
Opening Times
Our Spa Facilities are open daily from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Spa treatments are available from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Gym is accessible 24/7. Join us and elevate your wellness journey at The Ritz-Carlton Spa in Baku.
Adult and Family Pools
The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Baku features 2-indoor pools. One for the whole family and the other dedicated to adults only. Both of the pools are heated and open from 6 AM to 11 PM daily.
