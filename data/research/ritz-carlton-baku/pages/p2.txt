Spacious By Design
Rooms & Suites
Space is the first element guests notice at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku, and the last they want to leave. Our accommodations span eight distinct categories, ranging from generous 63-square-meter Deluxe Rooms to a commanding 608-square-meter Presidential Suite. Designed by Kristina Zanic Consultants using a visual language rooted in Azerbaijani craft, each retreat features marble bathrooms, Diptyque bath amenities, and a custom White Tea scent created exclusively for this property. Floor-to-ceiling windows draw the city inward. In select rooms, a private balcony frames the Heydar Aliyev Cultural Center, a view guests consistently photograph and often request upon booking.
The Art of Anticipation
Ladies & Gentleman
No two stays are identical because no two guests are the same, a philosophy our Ladies and Gentlemen understand instinctively. During one brief stopover, a team member noticed a guest traveling with a broken suitcase wheel. The luggage was quietly repaired before the guest even departed their room, requiring no request and no disruption to their day. Every person walking through our doors carries a distinct vision of a perfect stay.
Apartments for Family Stays
Traveling with children brings both energy and joy, and family trips worth remembering are the ones where someone else has already thought of everything. At The Ritz-Carlton, Baku, we have, so that parents can simply arrive and exhale. Our one-, two- and three-bedroom apartments, each with a fully equipped kitchen, private bathrooms for every bedroom and in-room laundry, were designed for exactly this kind of stay.

The Ritz Kids Welcome
Young travelers receive an arrival experience all their own through the Ritz Kids program. Children are greeted with a playful Leo the Lion tote bag filled with activities and a sweet welcome amenity from our culinary team. Upon request, a cozy in-room tent can be arranged, transforming a corner of the apartment into their personal retreat. Throughout the stay, families enjoy access to a dedicated children's pool, drawing supplies, and tailored dining menus to make our youngest guests feel entirely at home. With 24 hours of notice, our culinary team hosts a complimentary cooking masterclass for children, turning a quiet afternoon into a highlight the whole family will discuss long after departure.

Time for Two
A Shared Rhythm
For couples seeking a romantic retreat in Baku, the Grand Deluxe rooms at The Ritz-Carlton provide a sanctuary for genuine connection and unhurried comfort. Deep soaking marble bathtubs rest beside expansive windows, where the urban skyline shifts from afternoon light into evening shadows. Enhanced by signature Diptyque amenities and our custom White Tea scent, these generous spaces are made for lingering conversations and restorative stillness.

For Business Travelers
Discover the Club Level
For solo business travelers in Baku, the Club Level Rooms at The Ritz-Carlton offer a quieter, more considered stay. Soundproofing ensures the city noise stays outside, while the Ritz-Carlton Club on the 13th floor provides a private space to work, meet and unwind throughout the day. An Illy coffee machine, a pillow menu, two complimentary garment pressings daily, and 24-hour in-room dining ensure that every practical need is anticipated before it is even requested. Marriott Bonvoy Elite members can check in on the go with mobile check-in and digital key access, so the room is ready the moment they are.
