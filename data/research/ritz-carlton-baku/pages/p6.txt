Things To Do in Baku: A City of Old and New
Experiences
Situated at the crossroads of the Middle East, Europe and Asia, the vibrant capital of Azerbaijan is where antiquity meets modernity. Perched on the Caspian Sea and known as the "Land of Fire," Baku is a city full of enticing juxtapositions. With its proximity to both the historical Sherq Bazaar and the strikingly modern Heydar Aliyev Center, The Ritz-Carlton is a prime hotel near the Old City of Baku. Be sure to save time to explore the ancient Inner City, wandering within its fortified walls and past medieval landmarks.
Time Travel: Culture, Design & Nostalgia
Step into another era as a vintage automobile carries you through Baku toward the sculptural Heydar Aliyev Center. Explore its classic car exhibition at an unhurried pace before returning in timeless style. The journey concludes with a thoughtful amenity inspired by the Center’s iconic form, leaving you with a new perspective on the city’s past, design, and enduring elegance.
Learn More
Weaving Memories Together
Enter the living story of Azerbaijani carpet-making, where every motif preserves memory, identity, and craft. A guided visit to the Carpet Museum leads to a private weaving session with skilled artisans, inviting you to slow down and experience the patience behind each thread. Later, a miniature carpet created during the experience awaits in your room as a personal keepsake of Baku.
Learn More
The Clay Atelier: Create, Shape, & Take Home
Creating by hand at a local clay atelier rooted in Azerbaijan's ceramic heritage. Guided by skilled artists, shape and decorate a piece reflecting your imagination while discovering techniques passed through generations. The session ends over a tea ritual and The Ritz-Carlton Cake. The finished piece can be shipped home within a month, a quiet reminder of an afternoon in Baku. Shipping at the guest's expense.
Learn More
Blind Tiger Cocktail Journey
Six handcrafted cocktails, each revealing a different sense of place: the Caspian Sea, local tea culture, wild botanicals and regional spices. The mixologist prepares and narrates every creation as light bites punctuate the evening. Seasonal ingredients ensure no two visits feel the same, and each cocktail is available in non-alcoholic or low-alcohol variations for those who prefer a lighter pour.
Reserve now
Karabakh Rider’s Reset
Inspired by the grace of the Karabakh horse and the movement of UNESCO-recognized Chovqan, this 45-minute ritual translates equestrian balance into mindful movement. Through standing balance, core activation, mobility, and postural work, the body reconnects with strength and control. Hosted outdoors or in the fitness center, the experience closes with a restorative recovery moment for body and mind.
Azerbaijani Chef’s Table
Follow the story of Azerbaijani cuisine from garden to table. Begin in the Mystique Garden, where our Chef introduces herbs and produce grown for the kitchen, then continue to Tribeca’s open kitchen as each course is prepared before you. Stories of ingredients, family traditions, and local craft unfold throughout the meal, while curated wine pairings deepen a dining experience rooted entirely in place.
Reserve Now
The Naftalan Journey
Discover a wellness tradition found only in Azerbaijan. Inspired by centuries of Naftalan rituals, this host-led journey introduces the story behind the renowned oil before flowing into warm, restorative treatments shaped by local practice. An Azerbaijani tea ceremony brings the experience to a quiet close, creating a deeply rooted pause for reflection, renewal, and connection with the destination.
Learn More
Making My Way Down Old Town
Walk beyond the familiar landmarks of Icherisheher and into the stories that still live behind its ancient walls. Led by one of our Concierge Ladies and Gentlemen, the journey winds through hidden corners to the home and studio of ceramicist Mir Teymur Mamedov, then pauses for tea and fresh bread where Old Baku feels untouched. A hand-drawn map becomes both guide and personal keepsake.
Learn More
The Signature Journey
Step into a world of curated elegance with The Signature Journey—a series of intimate and enriching experiences. This exclusive program invites guests to explore bespoke moments across culinary, wellness, and mixology and offers access to unforgettable highlights and meaningful privileges in the heart of Baku.
Outdoor Padel Courts
Embrace the energy of the outdoors with a game of padel in an inviting open-air setting. Combining movement, coordination and social connection, the experience offers an enjoyable way to stay active, recharge and make time for recreation during your stay.
