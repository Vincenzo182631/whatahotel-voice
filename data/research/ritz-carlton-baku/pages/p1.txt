Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Baku
Historic Baku, where stories are told, memories are made
Set steps away from the Heydar Aliyev Cultural Center, The Ritz-Carlton, Baku offers an inviting retreat in the heart of Azerbaijan's vibrant capital. Spacious rooms, elegant suites and residences create a sense of comfort and ease, while thoughtful experiences reflect the warmth, culture and character of the destination.Explore Baku's historic Old City, striking architecture and dynamic cultural scene, then return to moments of relaxation at The Ritz-Carlton Spa, serene indoor pools and distinctive dining venues inspired by local and international flavors.From meaningful cultural encounters to quiet moments of rest, every stay is enriched by the legendary service, intuitive care and personalized attention of our Ladies and Gentlemen.

A Ritual Eight Centuries in the Making
The city outside holds its pace. Inside, at precisely 6:00 PM, something quieter settles over the lobby. The Whaletone grand piano — a concert instrument found in only a handful of places on earth — opens the evening with live Azerbaijani melodies. Traditional tea is poured alongside pakhlava and local sweets, offered with the unhurried grace of our Ladies and Gentlemen. It is an introduction to a culture that has gathered around tea and music for eight centuries. For some, it is a moment of discovery. For others, it is the anchor of their evening.

The Ritz-Carlton Experiences
Six cocktails in the Blind Tiger — each one a story rooted in the flavors and folklore of Azerbaijan. Follow a quiet doorway off Baku's celebrated Nizami Street into an intimate ceramics atelier, where local craftsmanship is experienced through your own hands. The piece you create is later delivered to your home, carrying with it the spirit of the destination. These encounters are gateways to a city defined by its depth. Baku is a vital crossroads of the Silk Road where the legacy of centuries is written in stone and woven into silk. Beneath the modern skyline lies a heritage eight centuries in the making — one that reveals itself only to those who know where to look. Azerbaijan, in all its richness, is a legacy we know intimately and share with care. Every experience has been locally rooted and prepared in advance. All that remains is to choose.

Baku, Brought Closer
Complimentary Shuttle Service
Baku is best discovered on your own terms, and our complimentary daily shuttle makes that easier. Five times a day, the service carries guests from the hotel to Boulevard Park, the beloved seafront promenade at the heart of modern Baku, and onward to the Maiden Tower at the Old City: the UNESCO-listed historic district where eight centuries of Azerbaijani life are written into every stone. Departures from the hotel run at 10:00, 12:00, 15:00, 16:45, and 18:10, with return journeys from the Old City at 10:40, 12:40, 15:40, 17:20, and 18:50. The full round trip takes just over an hour, leaving plenty of time to explore between rides. Seats are reserved in advance. The Concierge team is happy to arrange your preferred departure.

Hospitality With Purpose
The Ritz-Carlton, Baku holds a Green Key certification — an internationally recognized standard for sustainable hospitality — and sustainability is woven into the fabric of every stay. We invite you to experience a more considered way of travel: single-use plastics replaced by glass water bottles, refillable bath dispensers, and digital press. The hotel goes further still: linen-saving practices, an on-site herb Mystique Garden for the kitchen, soap recycling, and electric vehicle charging on the premises. Beyond the guest room, the property participates in Marriott's Community Footprints program, contributing to the broader environmental and social commitments of the Marriott Bonvoy portfolio. Your arrival is equally thoughtful: mobile check-in and digital key access remove the need for plastic cards, ensuring your journey begins as effortlessly as it continues.
Meetings & Weddings
Where Important Things Happen
Every gathering deserves a backdrop that resonates. At The Ritz-Carlton, Baku, 2,200 square meters of event space, including the Shusha Ballroom named after Azerbaijan's cultural capital, offer a naturally lit, considered environment for meetings, celebrations, and everything in between. Fifteen distinct rooms, eight with natural daylight, a dedicated ballroom entrance, and a private outdoor terrace ensure no two events feel the same. A dedicated specialist supports your vision from the first conversation through the final farewell. Built-in audiovisual technology and centralized sound are provided at no additional charge, alongside over 200 complimentary parking spaces. The culinary team builds custom menus from the ground up, and for weddings, the garden terrace offers an open-sky setting where the Heydar Aliyev Cultural Center frames every moment beyond the glass.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Baku allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 10.0 kgs.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Baku, with rates of ₼2.50 per hour, and ₼0.00 perstay. Valet parking is available for ₼5.00 perstay.

Does The Ritz-Carlton, Baku have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Baku has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Baku booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Baku?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Baku is Heydar Aliyev International Airport (GYD). GYD is located approximately 24.5 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Baku have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Baku has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
