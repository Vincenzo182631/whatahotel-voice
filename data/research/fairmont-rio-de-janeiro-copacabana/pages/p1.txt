## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Rio de Janeiro Copacabana located?

Fairmont Rio de Janeiro Copacabana is located at the iconic Copacabana beach in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Our hotel address is Av. Atlântica, 4240, Copacabana, Rio de Janeiro, RJ, 22070-002, Brazil.

Does Fairmont Rio de Janeiro Copacabana have a spa?

Spanning 400 square meters, our luxury Spa features five massage rooms, including a couples' suite, as well as a sauna, beauty salon and a Spa Suite. Inspired by the Amazonian biodiversity and the beauty of Rio's beaches our treatments are designed to balance energy using the best of nature.

Does Fairmont Rio de Janeiro Copacabana have a swimming pool?

The hotel has two swimming pools: an infinity pool that welcomes the morning sun and has the iconic view of Copacaba beach and Sugarloaf Mountain, with daily live music and the relax pool where you can enjoy the afternoon sun.

Is Fairmont Rio de Janeiro Copacabana pet-friendly?

Dogs and cats up to twenty-five kilos (25kg) are welcome in our pet-friendly hotel. Please, check our pet policy for more information.

What is Fairmont Gold?

Considered a world apart within Fairmont Rio, Fairmont Gold is an elevated hotel-within-a-hotel experience, offering the highest level of personalized service to each guest, with completely privileged experiences such as the Fairmont Gold Lounge, featuring buffet services for breakfast, happy hour, as well as 24-hour butler service and exclusive concierge.

Are there accessible accommodations at Fairmont Rio de Janeiro Copacabana?

Fairmont Hotels & Resorts is particularly committed to ensuring that each and every person feels welcome in our hotels. At Fairmont Rio de Janeiro Copacabana we have 19 apartments suitable for handicap (wheelchair) and 19 for other disabilities (not wheelchair accessible).
