# Suiten Your Stay

W Nashville

About W Nashville Opens a new window Secondary CTA Opens a new window

## Indulge in our hotel suites in Nashville

W Nashville offers 346 guest rooms and 60 luxury suites in Nashville, Tennessee. With 7 different suite types ranging from 478 to 1,800 square feet–many of which have separate living and sleeping areas–we’ve got a comprehensive array of luxe options. In fact, we have one of the most dynamic suite offerings in the city.

## In Room Amenities

Every one of our luxury suites in Nashville, Tennessee, features Davines bath products and Comfort Zone soaps and lotions. Also included: Our W Signature Bed, W robes, W MixBar, slippers, and more.

## Design Driven

W Nashville’s aesthetic was developed by the renowned Rockwell Group.

Every room has a hint of the retro jet-age, with sleek lines complementing pronounced curves.

Yet there’s also a sense of warmth, with soft uplighting and Nashville-inspired artwork.

## Local Art

An edition of FAILE’s “Southern Skies” hangs in every guest room at W Nashville. This exclusively commissioned piece alludes to the different facets of Nashville's scene–with particular respect to the grit it takes to succeed in the music industry.

## Welcome Den

The Welcome Den is an exclusive space for suite guests only. Consider it a place to hang before check-in, or a spot to relax with your morning coffee.

### Suites

Whether you're looking for a cozy luxury corner hotel suite in Nashville or a spacious Presidential-style suite, we have the perfect option to elevate your stay.

## Penthouse Suite

Live the life of luxury in W Nashville's version of a Presidential Suite. The Penthouse Suite in Nashville features 1,800 square feet of space complete with a large living room, a table to gather, a kitchen and wet bar. When you're done connecting in the living space, retire to a separate bedroom with a soaking tub, dual vanities and rain shower. When you wake up in the morning, soak in the sunrise against the city skyline while sipping coffee on your oversized balcony. The distinct room design blends industrial polish with warm, soothing tones as well as a mix of raw materials including leather and exposed cement.

## W Suite

Featuring an open and inviting layout, the W Suite in Nashville, TN, is a sizable 1,145 square feet luxury hotel suite. Each suite is outfitted with one king-sized bed, a separate sleeping suite, dining, and living areas (plus a bonus half bathroom in the living room), with some of the most captivating city views.
