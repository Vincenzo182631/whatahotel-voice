# Welcome to W Nashville

## Remix Luxury at our hotel in The Gulch Nashville

Taking center stage in Nashville's vibrant Gulch neighborhood, W Nashville puts you at the intersection of music, culture, and nightlife. Just moments from Broadway, Music Row, 12 South, SoBro, Midtown, and the Arts District, our luxury lifestyle hotel offers a fresh perspective on Music City. Expect the unexpectedwith our Whatever/Whenever approach to Southern hospitality, featuring modern guest rooms...See More and suites, and dynamic social spaces that capture Nashville'screative spirit. Every experience is designed to keep pace with the rhythm of the city. Start your day with artisanal coffee from Barista Parlor, recharge at FIT andthe outdoor yoga lawn, enjoy retail therapy at The Shop, and soak in the sun from Glowbird at WET Deck, Nashville’s largest rooftop pool venue. As golden hourhits, take in sweeping skyline views at Butterfly, our rooftop bar, then meet us for a nightcap in Living Room. Whether you're here to explore, connect, orsomewhere in between, W Nashville is where Music City comes together.See Less

## José Andrés Group’s Nashville Debut

James Beard Award–winning chef José Andrés has launched several of his celebrated concepts at W Nashville—Zaytinya, Bar Mar, and Butterfly. This exclusive Nashville debut is complemented by the addition of Glowbird at WET Deck, a fully reimagined seasonal bar and refreshed outdoor space crafted for an immersive day‑to‑night experience to elevate every moment of your Music City stay.

Rooftop cocktail lounge, vibrant energy. Butterfly at W Nashville pairs panoramic views of The Gulch with expertly crafted agave-forward beverages and shareable bites, including ceviches and tacos. Experience Nashville’s go-to rooftop destination.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Nashville?

The check-in time at W Nashville is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Nashville allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Nashville. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 60.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $200.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Nashville?

On-site valet parking is available at W Nashville. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Nashville have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Nashville has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Nashville booking.

What is the closest airport near W Nashville?

The closest airport near W Nashville is Nashville International Airport (BNA). BNA is located approximately 8.7 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Nashville have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Nashville has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
