## Dining

W Nashville’s dining is lively, diverse–and mouthwatering. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and brunch, morning cold brew to poolside margarita to evening nightcap: we've got something for every taste at our restaurants and bars in The Gulch, Nashville.

## José Andrés Group’s Nashville Debut

At W Nashville, José Andrés Group introduces a collection of restaurants and bars that celebrate flavor, energy, and connection. Each concept offers its own perspective—from the bright, shareable mezze at Zaytinya to the coastal influence of Bar Mar, the high-energy rooftop experience at Butterfly and poolside bliss at Glowbird at WET Deck. Together, they create a dining destination designed for everything, where great food, thoughtful drinks, and vibrant atmosphere come together seamlessly.

### Private Dining

Our restaurants provide the ideal setting for private and semi-private events, from celebratory dinners and corporate gatherings to sophisticated social occasions.
