# Recharge at our luxury hotel rooms in Nashville, TN

## Accommodations

We have rooms for everyone, from solo travellers to groups of friends. Ranging from 380 to 1,800 square feet, our luxury accommodations in downtown Nashville are fuelled by an ultra-comfortable, design-forward approach. Nashvillian cues are observed throughout with Music City artwork, wood accents and other details influenced by Nashville's rich history.

## The Den

The Den is an exclusive retreat within our Welcome area, reserved for suite guests with key-card access. It’s your private haven to unwind—a serene space to pause and recharge before check-in or the perfect spot to savor your morning coffee in comfort and style.

## Personalized Plans

Make your stay all the more memorable at our luxury accommodation in Nashville. From personalised room décor and stunning floral arrangements to curated local experiences, we create the perfect atmosphere for any occasion.

### In-Room Amenities

Our luxurious hotel rooms in Nashville are thoughtfully equipped with Davines bath products and Comfort Zone soaps and lotions to elevate your stay. Enjoy additional amenities such as personal steamers, our signature W Bed, plush W robes, cozy slippers, and a Mixbar for your convenience.

### Detail Oriented

W Nashville’s design, crafted by the renowned Rockwell Group, effortlessly blends retro jet-age sophistication with Nashville charm. Each room features sleek lines paired with bold curves, creating a stylish yet inviting atmosphere. Warm uplighting and locally inspired artwork add a touch of comfort and a nod to the city’s creative spirit.

### Pet Friendly

Your pets are welcome at W Nashville. Through our P.A.W. program, we offer pet beds, food and water bowls, bone packs, waste removal bags, litter boxes, and a selection of toys in our pet-friendly hotel rooms in downtown Nashville, TN. Inquire with our Insider team today for more information.
