# Discover Music City from our hotel in The Gulch, Nashville

## Experiences

We redefine luxury and amplify the soul of our community through elevated experiences and thoughtful design inspired by the rich history of Nashville. Every corner of our hotel tells a story, with curated installations from local artists that honor the city’s heritage while adding a contemporary touch. We bring together the best of the city in one unforgettable space with dynamic happenings for both...See More hotel guests and Nashvillians to experience. We are W Nashville, writing a song just for you.See Less

## Spanish Steps

Our Spanish Steps are a focal point at W Nashville. Connecting the WET Deck to the 2nd floor, where you’ll find our FIT (Gym), Yoga Lawn, and more, they’re an ideal place to hang out day or night–and, depending on the time, you might even catch a live musician serenading the stars.

## The Secret Garden

The Secret Garden is a seasonal outdoor area located next to Barista Parlor. Here, you can enjoy your coffee, read the news or just relax in the morning sunlight.

### Immerse Yourself at W Nashville

Welcome to W Nashville, where vibrant entertainment, local museums, award-winning dining, and constant lineups of music are within walking distance of our hotel near the Country Music Hall of Fame. Explore everything both on the property and outside its doors.

Stay Connected

## Insider Guide

From our rooftop, enjoy views of the iconic “Batman Building” and Downtown Nashville. Just around the corner, snap a photo at the famous “What Lifts You” wings mural by Kelsey Montague, also featured on our southern exterior. Located in The Gulch—a vibrant, LEED-certified neighborhood—W Nashville connects you to the city’s best, with our W Insider ready to plug you into the ultimate Nashville experience.

Destination Guide

## Creative Spirit

W Nashville is filled with work inspired by The Music City. From our in-room art—a hyper saturated Nashville-influenced piece dubbed “Southern Skies” by FAILE—to custom elevator panels by the Nashvillian leatherworker Joey Verzilli, our hotel pays homage to the rhythm and the vibrancy of this extraordinary town.

### The Shop

Nestled in The Gulch, our in-house boutique—just steps from the Living Room—offers a curated mix of luxury and local finds. Shop standout styles from brands like L Space and Paige, plus Nashville favorites like Ranger’s Station. From elevated womenswear and menswear to unique local gifts and exclusive W Nashville merch, it’s your go-to for a night out or the perfect souvenir from Music City. Open Monday – Saturday, 9AM – 6PM and Sundays 10AM - 5PM.

### Work Out, Plug In

Our hotel in Nashville with a gym is designed to feel like your local gym–it’s not your typical travel fitness center. Spacious, modern, and packed with natural light, FIT makes that jet lag workout just a little bit more enjoyable.

Our state-of-the-art equipment is fully integrated with the Internet and TV. We also have Peloton bikes logged in under our house account. Guests at our hotel in The Gulch, Nashville, don’t need their profiles to take a spin (though if you’d like to use your own, you may).

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Nashville have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Nashville offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Stationary Bicycles, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Nashville have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Nashville has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Nashville?

Tennessee State Museum, Grand Ole Opry, Cheekwood Estates & Gardens, Tennessee Performing Arts Center, Carter Vintage Guitars and more are nearby W Nashville.
