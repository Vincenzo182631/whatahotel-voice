# Fitness Center

Make use of premium cardiovascular, strength and weight training equipment, or customise your workouts with one of our personal trainers, available on request, for one-on-one coaching sessions in yoga and Pilates.

The Fitness Center makes it easy to keep in shape while away from home regardless of time zone or busy schedule. Every detail of The Fitness Center is state-of-the-art, from the impressive Woodway treadmills and Peloton bikes to the range of Life Fitness equipment, including cycles and elliptical trainers, to strength and weight training equipment. Take your time post-workout in The Fitness Center’s spacious locker rooms, complete with Himalayan Salt Saunas, showers and a designated relaxation area.

\*You can also request the In Room Workout Kit to stay active in the privacy of your hotel room with guided workouts on the Wellness Portal.
