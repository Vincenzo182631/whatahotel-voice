# A Celebration 35 Years in The Making

## A Legacy Built. A Future Inspired.

Thirty-five years is measured in more than time. It is measured in the people who shape a place, the rituals that become tradition, and the ideas that continue to inspire what comes next.

At The Peninsula Beverly Hills, we have always believed that hospitality extends beyond service. It lives in the moments people remember but cannot always explain—a conversation that lingers, a meal that becomes a memory, a wish made, a story that changes the way we see the world.

Our thirty-fifth anniversary honors these enduring traditions and the philosophy behind them.

Enjoy a two-night stay with Peninsula Time, a $350 Experience Credit to personalize your stay, and the traditions that have become a part of our story. Because an anniversary isn't simply about looking back. ## Afternoon Tea: Hope Deserves a Ritual

For decades, Afternoon Tea has marked the rhythm of each day. This anniversary introduces a new tradition: a commemorative coin inspired by our _Flight to Infinity_ fountain and a Wish Cake, honoring one of humanity’s oldest customs—the act of making a wish.

## Table 35: Every Great Table Tells a Story

Long before recipes were written, stories were shared around a table. Meals have always carried more than ingredients—they preserve tradition, celebrate craftsmanship, and pass culture from one generation to the next. Our anniversary dining series, begining with the Caviar Experience explores the stories behind the food we share. Every course is part of a larger narrative. Every meal becomes another chapter.

## The Library of The Peninsula Beverly Hills

Travel has always inspired new ways of seeing the world. Sometimes that perspective is found in a place. Sometimes it arrives through the book read before bed, the record that becomes the soundtrack to a stay, or the story that parallels our own.

Our Library is a collection of books and vinyl records celebrating the relationship between travel, imagination, and memory. Each selection is chosen to accompany the journey, reminding us that every destination has its own stories—and every traveler leaves with one.
