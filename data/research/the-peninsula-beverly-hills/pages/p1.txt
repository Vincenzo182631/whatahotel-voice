## An Icon of Style and Grace

Set among lush tropical gardens in the heart of Beverly Hills, The Peninsula Beverly Hills offers a distinctly personal approach to luxury. Defined by long-standing relationships and intuitive service, guests are welcomed by name, preferences remembered, and every stay shaped by genuine familiarity. A rare sense of belonging is what guests remember most—and what brings them back, time and again.

**The Rooftop Pool Cabanas**

The Peninsula Beverly Hills’ rooftop swimming pool epitomises Hollywood glamour, surrounded by a dozen spacious guest cabanas equipped with 24-inch flat-screen TVs, private safes, ceiling fans and elegant white draperies for privacy. Wi-Fi, iPhone docks, phones and faxes are also available for guests who want to turn their cabana into a mini private office. Guests who wish to be pampered poolside can select from a special menu of spa rituals including reflexology, Moroccan oil treatments, manicures and pedicures.

### Margot McKinney

Experience the brilliance of Margot McKinney’s jewelry, where rare Australian opals, pearls, and precious gemstones become one-of-a-kind wearable masterpieces. Inspired by nature’s wonders, each piece is a testament to Margot's artistry, passion, and heritage. Explore these breathtaking designs, exclusively at The Peninsula Beverly Hills.

Read More

### Travel In Style

The Peninsula offers a variety of luxury vehicles. The hotel’s complimentary house car drives guests within Beverly Hills and Century City in a choice of a classic Rolls-Royce or another luxury vehicle, while guests who book any of the hotel's elegant suites can take advantage of the ‘Suite Ride’ promotion and enjoy complimentary use of one of the hotel’s stylish vehicles throughout their stay – perfect for driving along the coast and exploring the Southern California sights.

Read More

### Shop Beverly Hills

Fashionistas will be pleased to know that The Peninsula is located just steps from the Beverly Hills' ‘Golden Triangle’ and Rodeo Drive, home to the most luxurious and sought-after names in jewellery, watches, designer clothing, shoes and accessories. For guests who foresee their arms filled with shopping bags, The Peninsula offers a complimentary chauffeur service within Beverly Hills so you can shop and ride in style.

Read More

### Peninsula Airport Concierge

As the only hotel with its own on-site team at LAX, The Peninsula Beverly Hills’ dedicated Airport Concierge aims to ensure a seamless and effortless travel experience from start to finish. A five-star experience awaits from touchdown at LAX where a Peninsula Airport Concierge will meet guests at the baggage claim (domestic flights only), collect their luggage and escort them to a waiting hotel car. Guests are invited to check in to their rooms and order room service favorites, which will be awaiting them upon arrival. For a stress-free departure, Concierge will meet the guests curbside and assist with their luggage. For additional inquiries please contact our Concierge team.
