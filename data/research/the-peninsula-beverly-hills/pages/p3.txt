# The Roof Garden

The Roof Garden moves with the rhythm of Los Angeles, from sunrise to sunset. Breakfast begins in the morning light, fresh and refined. By midday, beneath iconic yellow and white umbrellas, it becomes a vibrant, produce-driven lunch scene. The menu is rooted in seasonal, locally sourced ingredients, paired with bright juices and polished cocktails. Poolside cabanas offer a more private setting, with warm, intuitive service and sweeping rooftop views throughout.

#### An Al Fresco Luncheon

The Roof Garden attracts hotel guests and local patrons alike to dine al fresco all day. With it's open atmosphere and sweeping city views, the rooftop restaurant offers a delightful menu of delicious contemporary Cal-Fusion cuisine, including a variety of fresh salads, flavorful tacos and seasonal dishes. Must-try menu items include the Avocado Toast, Charred Lobster Roll and Roof Garden Frittata.

#### A Juice Boost

The Peninsula Beverly Hills makes it easy to stay healthy while traveling by providing a selection of nutritious breakfast options. A favorite with both hotel guests and Los Angeles locals, The Roof Garden’s signature natural juice "The Green Juice" is a delicious blend of cold-pressed apple, cucumber, spinach, celery, parsley, ginger, kale and lemon.
