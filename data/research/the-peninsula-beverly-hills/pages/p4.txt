Rooftop Indulgence The Pool item 2

The hotel’s iconic rooftop has been refreshed in signature Peninsula style - where white and yellow stripes sway in the sunlight and enhanced cabanas redefine Beverly Hills poolside luxury. Lie back in a plush chaise lounge and enjoy a signature cocktail, refreshing juice or smoothie and light fare from the Naturally Peninsula menu.

**POOLSIDE CABANAS**

Hotel guests can rent one of (8) private standard cabanas and (2) private deluxe cabanas overlooking the hot tub for the ultimate Beverly Hills' poolside experience. Fitted with modern comforts and amenities, each cabana includes:

- A 50 high-definition television, Apple TVs offering the ability to connect personal phone contents to TVs (on demand) Bluetooth sound system (on demand), overhead heater, fan, safe, refrigerator, USB charging ports, and pool side Wi Fi
- A Farmer’s Market crudité platter with a rotating Chef’s Garden Dip
- Daily Juice Amenity
- Unlimited Fiji Flat or San Pellegrino Sparkling Water and soft drinks for the day
- 1 bottle of Chateau Marguerite Fantastique Rosé
- Refreshing passed fruit offering every 90 minutes
- 2 Sunscreens

The cabana dining menu is available all day.

Standard Cabanas can accomodate up to (4) guests and a Deluxe Cabana can accomodate up to (6) guests maximum.

**POOLSIDE SPA SERVICES**

The Spa at Peninsula Beverly Hills offers poolside manicures, pedicures in our spa cabana, and reflexology in a private pool cabana seven days a week. Please call 310.975.2854 or email spapbh@peninsula.com to reserve.
