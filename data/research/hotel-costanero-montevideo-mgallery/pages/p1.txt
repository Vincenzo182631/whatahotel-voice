## Description

Hotel Costanero Montevideo - MGallery Collection

Boutique hotels with personality for a remarkable travel experience

Right in Montevideo, by Pocitos Bay, Costanero Montevideo is a boutique hotel designed for memorable experiences. Its uniquely designed rooms are perfect for relaxing with sea views. Also with Cauce's locally influenced international cuisine, and Bar Cauce, with flavors and aromas offering a unique sensory experience. Take advantage of the meeting rooms andwellness area with swimming pool, gym and sauna.

La ubicación del Hotel Costanero Montevideo es perfecta para quienes quieren descubrir el destino y coleccionar experiencias memorables. Comience su recorrido en la tradicional Feira Tristán Narvaja, una feria callejera que se realiza todos los domingos. Pero hay mucho más que hacer en Montevideo. Algunas atracciones turísticas de visita obligada son el Teatro Solís, el Museo del Carnaval, el Auditorio del Sodré, el Punta Carretas Shopping y el Mercado del Puerto, todos a poca distancia del hotel.

If you have time, visit Montevideo's Agricultural Market to taste the city's flavors or enjoy the sunset in Parque Rodó, while relaxing in the evening. Book now and see the best of Uruguay with MGallery!

> Located in the noblest point of the Bay of Pocitos. We offer a magnificent experience with stunning sea views, a wonderful gastronomic variety and a genuine Staff to make your stay a memorable moment

Francisco Lorente, Hotel Management
