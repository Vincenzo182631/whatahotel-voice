# Satisfy Your Every Craving

## Dining

Expect an unforgettable culinary journey featuring fresh Atlantic crudos, succulent North American beef, and tender New Zealand lamb, all infused with vibrant Latin spices. At CASSAVA, our signature all-day restaurant in Punta Cana, Dominican Republic, soaring greenery-filled ceilings set the stage for a space that feels equal parts home kitchen, and date-night celebration spot. Beyond dining, discover distinctive venues that elevate every moment. The St. Regis Bar invites you to unwind with craft cocktails, caviar tastings, or seafood snacks after a round of golf, while The Amber Room delivers a speakeasy vibe with smoky rum creations and premium cigars. For rooftop views, Cielo Mio serves tiki-style drinks with a Mexican twist, and Marola tempts with Mediterranean flavors and wood-fired pizzas by the sea. Coffee lovers can indulge at Kawa’s interactive bean-to-blend café, and Carey Bay’s beach bar keeps the island spirit alive with clams, sandwiches, and cocktails served in coconuts

Experience The Art of Savoring—a three-day celebration of culinary mastery and timeless indulgence, unfolding along the turquoise shores of the Caribbean.
