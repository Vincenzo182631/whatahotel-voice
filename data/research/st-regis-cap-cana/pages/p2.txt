### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have a work space?

No, the rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 6 at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort?

Some rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have fridges?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have a Refrigerator (full size).

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
