# Welcome to The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort

## Where luxury meets perfection in the heart of Cap Cana

Nestled within the breathtaking enclave of Cap Cana, The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort offers 200 elegantly appointed rooms, including refined suites and swim-out accommodations, where guests experience unparalleled comfort. Discover eight distinctive culinary venues, from the artistry of our signature restaurant Nina to the relaxed sophistication of barefoot dining along the pristine shores of Carey Bay. Rejuvenate at The St. Regis Spa with bespoke treatments or retreat to exclusive spaces such as The Amber Room cigar bar. For celebrations and gatherings, our beachfront Cap Cana resort presents elegant venues including the Poinciana Ballroom, designed for moments that matter. From the timeless allure of our luxury hotel accommodations in Punta Cana to exceptional dining and personalized wellness experiences, every detail reflects the signature sophistication and impeccable service of St. Regis. Welcome to The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort, where each moment is thoughtfully crafted, and luxury knows no limits.

## Discover a Festive Escape Inspired by Natural Beauty and Vibrant Culture

Along the coveted coastline of Cap Cana, the festive season unfolds through extraordinary moments, cherished traditions, and meaningful connections. Surrounded by the natural beauty of the Dominican Republic, each experience is thoughtfully crafted to inspire celebration, discovery, and the art of living exquisitely.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 30.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort is Punta Cana, Dominican Republic (PUJ). PUJ is located approximately 15.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
