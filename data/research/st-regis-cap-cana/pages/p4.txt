# Holiday Events at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort

## Discover a Festive Escape Inspired by Natural Beauty and Vibrant Caribbean Culture

Along the coveted coastline of Cap Cana, the festive season unfolds through extraordinary moments, cherished traditions, and meaningful connections. Surrounded by the natural beauty of the Dominican Republic, each experience is thoughtfully crafted to inspire celebration, discovery, and the art of living exquisitely.

Starting November 25, 2026 to January 1, 2027

### The House of Celebration

Where cherished traditions, exquisite gatherings, and unforgettable moments come together in the signature elegance of St. Regis.

### Festive Calendar

### Thanksgiving Dinner

Gather with family and friends on November 26, 2026, for an exquisitely curated Thanksgiving dinner at Cassava Restaurant. Savor seasonal specialties and signature culinary creations in an elegant setting that celebrates the spirit of gratitude and togetherness.

### Christmas Dinner

Celebrate the magic of Christmas on December 24, 2026, with an exquisite dining experience at Cassava Restaurant. Gather with loved ones to enjoy festive flavors, seasonal specialties, and the signature elegance of The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort.

### New Year's Eve Celebration

Ring in 2027 on December 31, 2026, with an elegant New Year's Eve celebration at The St. Regis Cap Cana Resort. The evening begins with a festive celebratory dinner, followed by dancing, live entertainment, and unforgettable festivities as we welcome the new year in signature St. Regis style.

November Activities

### Autumn Harvest Workshop

Celebrate the season with an elegant hands-on experience inspired by autumn’s rich colors and Thanksgiving warmth. Guided by artisans, guests will craft and personalize beautiful keepsakes that blend creativity with seasonal charm. This curated workshop offers a way for families and friends to connect, create memories, and enjoy gathering in true St. Regis style. Available November 25.

### Thanksgiving 5K Turkey Trot

Begin Thanksgiving Day with an invigorating Turkey Trot, a festive race celebrating wellness, community, and the spirit of the season. Guests are invited to enjoy a scenic run through the resort grounds while embracing a holiday tradition. Whether pursuing a personal best or sharing the experience with family and friends, memorable moments await. November 26, 8:00 AM.

### Puppet Turkey Workshop

Celebrate Thanksgiving with a delightful hands-on workshop designed to spark creativity and festive cheer. Guided by local artists, guests will craft a decorative turkey puppet using colorful materials and seasonal accents. Blending creativity, storytelling, and holiday traditions, this experience invites all ages to create a keepsake and enjoy the spirit of gratitude. Available November 26.

### Thanksgiving Volleyball Tournament

Teens are invited to join an exciting volleyball tournament that combines friendly competition and teamwork. Participants will compete in teams, showcasing their skills and sportsmanship in a dynamic beachside setting. Designed to encourage camaraderie and confidence, this event offers a way to connect with fellow guests and create memorable holiday moments. Available November 26.

### Thanksgiving Turkey Sack Race

Celebrate Thanksgiving with a lively outdoor activity filled with laughter, friendly competition, and festive spirit. Inspired by the holiday turkey, this themed sack race invites guests to test their balance, speed, and teamwork as they hop toward the finish line. Perfect for families and friends, this event creates memorable moments while embracing the joy of the season. Available November 26.

### Gratitude Tree

Celebrate the true spirit of Thanksgiving with a meaningful interactive experience that inspires reflection, connection, and gratitude. Throughout the day, guests are invited to share messages of appreciation on decorative leaves added to a collective Gratitude Tree. As it flourishes with thankfulness, the tree becomes a symbol of community, generosity, and seasonal traditions. Available November 26.

### Autumn Harvest Tote Bag Workshop

Embrace the beauty of the season with a creative experience inspired by the colors and traditions of autumn. Guided by local artists, guests will personalize elegant tote bags with harvest-inspired designs. Blending artistry, seasonal charm, and meaningful moments, this workshop invites guests of all ages to celebrate gratitude and creativity. Available November 26.

### Autumn Harvest Hat Decorating Workshop

Celebrate the season’s warmth and creativity with a charming autumn-inspired workshop for guests of all ages. Guided by local artists, guests will customize stylish hats with harvest-inspired embellishments, creating a personalized keepsake that reflects the beauty of the season. This curated experience embraces gratitude and celebration. Available November 27.
