## From the Maasai Mara to Tsavo West

Why choose one safari when you can experience two? Begin at Mahali Mzuri in the Maasai Mara, where sunrise game drives bring you face to face with lion, elephant and the wildlife that roams these legendary plains. Then head to Finch Hattons in Tsavo West National Park, where ancient cloud forests, volcanic landscapes and views of Mount Kilimanjaro create a strikingly different adventure.

From floating over the Mara in a hot air balloon to hiking through lush forests fed by natural springs, each camp offers its own unforgettable perspective on Kenya's wild beauty. Together, they make for one extraordinary safari, twice the adventure.
