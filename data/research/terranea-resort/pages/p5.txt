# Oceanfront Dining

Located in one of Palos Verdes' best outdoor dining spots, Nelson's offers a warm, inviting atmosphere with breathtaking views of the Pacific Ocean. Perfectly nestled amid outdoor fire pits, this casual dining destination is ideal for a laid-back meal while enjoying unparalleled sunset vistas. Savor the essence of Southern California cuisine, featuring coastal flavors and an inviting ambiance that enhances your dining experience.

## Meet Our Chef

Chef de Cuisine, Chef Juan Nunez, a pillar of Nelson's for nearly 10 years, began as a line cook under Chef Andrew Vaughan. He's instrumental in the successful Summer Concert Series, uniting top local bands with flavorful cuisine. Juan mentors his team daily in this exclusive ocean-side dining experience. Signature dishes include Ahi Tuna Burger, Thai Green Curry, and the area's best Burgers and Brews.
