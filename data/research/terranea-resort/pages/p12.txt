# Tee Off At The Links

The Links at Terranea is a 9-hole, par-3 Southern California oceanfront golf course set on the bluffs above the Pacific. The routing follows the land rather than fighting it, threading through coastal terrain that shapes each shot. A multi-tiered short-game area with chipping and putting greens rounds out the setting.

Artful bunkering and open sightlines reward strategy over power on the Los Angeles coast. And because golfers walk The Links, the round unfolds at the pace the game was meant to keep, with the coast alongside them the whole way.
