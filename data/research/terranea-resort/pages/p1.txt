# _Luxury_ on the Southern California Coast

Nestled along 102 oceanfront acres on the scenic Palos Verdes Peninsula, Terranea Resort is more than just a destination; it’s an extraordinary experience. With luxury accommodations, impeccable service, and 270-degree panoramic views, Terranea offers an immersive retreat where comfort and serenity blend seamlessly with adventure and excitement in a captivating coastal haven.
