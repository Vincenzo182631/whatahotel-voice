# California-Inspired Dining

Perched at Terranea Resort with breathtaking views of Catalina Island and the Pacific Coast, Catalina Kitchen offers an unparalleled dining experience. Indulge in exquisite cuisine, enjoy impeccable service, and savor unique dining occasions. With panoramic views enhancing every bite, Catalina Kitchen promises an extraordinary culinary journey.

Our menu is a celebration of New American cuisine with a coastal twist, crafted with high-quality, seasonal, and locally sourced ingredients, showcasing the rich bounty of Southern California.

## Meet Our Chef

Chef de Cuisine, Robert Montes, brings a passion for coastal cuisine and refined hospitality to Catalina Kitchen. Blending rustic warmth with precision, he delivers creative, approachable dishes and polished service. From guiding the open kitchen during weekend brunch to dinner menus, his attention to detail and love for the craft shine through every plate.
