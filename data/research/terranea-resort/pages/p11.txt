# Experience Signature Wellness Offerings by the Ocean

Nestled atop the stunning Palos Verdes Peninsula, The Spa at Terranea invites you to surrender to the rhythmic embrace of its oceanfront sanctuary. Allow the ebb and flow of the ocean’s currents to rejuvenate your senses as you indulge in our signature wellness offerings. Thoughtfully curated to harmonize with the energy of the tranquil seas, our treatments offer the ultimate relaxation experience.

For more information and to book a treatment, please call 855.711.1282.

## Explore, Relax, Enjoy

Discover a wide array of leisure activities at Terranea Resort, from oceanfront golfing to relaxing spa treatments and tranquil beach days. Whether you're seeking adventure or relaxation, our resort offers the perfect setting to unwind and enjoy the beauty of the Palos Verdes Peninsula.

### Fitness and Wellness Center

Embark on a transformative fitness journey at the Palo Verdes Wellness Center, featuring state-of-the-art cardio equipment, personalized training sessions, and a variety of fitness classes. From yoga and Pilates to circuit training and seaside cycling, we offer classes for every fitness level, including our signature boot camp, Nature's Gym.
