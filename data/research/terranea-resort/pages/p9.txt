# Cocktails with a View

Overlooking the Pacific, The Lobby Bar & Terrace offers craft cocktails, fine wines, locally inspired fare, and live evening entertainment. Whether gathering by the fireplace or enjoying sunset views, every moment is designed for connection, conversation, and relaxation.
