# Westminster Suite

63 m² / 678 ft² on average

Max 2 adults and 2 children

Shower and bathtub

Views of Westminster and Piccadilly

Living Area

Champagne, fruit and flowers

24hr VIP service

Chauffeur-Driven Car

The Westminster Suites are among London’s most exclusive, with only three available in the entire hotel. Each suite includes a chauffeur-driven Mercedes-Benz S-Class sedan or V-Class minivan for up to 5 hours daily. Enjoy breathtaking views of London landmarks such as Piccadilly Circus, Westminster, St James, and Regent Street.

Designed by Sir David Chipperfield, the suite features a contemporary style with soft Portland Stone and abundant natural light. The bedroom offers a king-size bed, a working desk, a large seating area, and an expansive dressing area. The Carrara marble bathroom includes rainfall showers, double sinks, and a separate bathtub.

Accommodating up to two adults and two children (up to age 12), the Westminster Suite can be connected to Grand Regent Suites to create a two-bedroom private residence.
