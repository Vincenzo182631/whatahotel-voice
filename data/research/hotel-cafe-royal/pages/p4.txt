# Loft Suite

68 m² / 732 ft² on average

Max 2 adults and 2 children

Shower and bathtub

Living Room with work space

Walk-In Wardrobe

Champagne, fruit and flowers

24hr VIP service

Chauffeur-Driven Car

The Loft Suite, located on the top floor, offers a chauffeur-driven Mercedes-Benz S-Class sedan or V-Class minivan for up to 5 hours daily. Designed by Sir David Chipperfield, this one-bedroom suite features warming fumed English Oak décor and contemporary design.

The spacious living area includes a large entertainment system, seating, and a guest bathroom with a rainfall shower. It can also be converted into a second bedroom, ideal for families or extended stays. The master bedroom boasts a king-size bed, an entertainment system, a walk-in closet, and a working desk. The Carrara marble bathroom features a mirror TV screen, dual vanity sinks, a rainfall shower, and a solid marble bathtub.

For larger groups, the Loft Suite can be combined with an adjacent Grand Deluxe Guestroom to create a two-bedroom retreat.
