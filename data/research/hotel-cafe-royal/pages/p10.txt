# Superior Room

33 m² / 355 ft² on average

Max 2 adults

King Bed

City View

Rainfall Shower

Nestled in the eaves of the hotel, our Superior Rooms look out to London’s rooftops. Designed by Sir David Chipperfield, contemporary elements are beautifully woven with classic materials, such as fumed English Oak panelling, to offer an exceptional sense of place. A generous lobby leads into the bedroom and serene Carrara marble bathroom, featuring a rainfall shower and a mirror TV screen.

For stays of two nights or more, enjoy £75 credit to spend at Akasha, our subterranean spa. All guests receive complimentary access to our gym, pool, and wellness classes, and can make use of our dedicated 24/7 WhatsApp service, shoe shine, and a daily newspaper, delivered to your door on request.
