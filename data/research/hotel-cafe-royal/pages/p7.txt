# Deluxe Suite

64 m² / 689 ft² on average

Max 2 adults and 2 children

Shower and bathtub

Living Room with work space

Dressing Area

Champagne, fruit and flowers

24hr VIP service

Chauffeur-Driven Car

The Deluxe Suites, among just four available, offer the exceptional convenience of a private chauffeur-driven Mercedes-Benz S-Class or V-Class minivan for up to 5 hours daily throughout your stay. Designed by the renowned architect Sir David Chipperfield, these one-bedroom suites are crafted with a contemporary flair in rusticated Portland Stone.

Each suite is thoughtfully appointed with modern amenities, including a separate living area and a Carrara marble bathroom equipped with mirror TV screens, a rainfall shower, and a separate marble bath. The bedroom features a luxurious king-size bed and a functional working desk.

For families or groups, Deluxe Suites can be seamlessly connected with adjacent bedrooms to form an expansive three-bedroom family suite.
