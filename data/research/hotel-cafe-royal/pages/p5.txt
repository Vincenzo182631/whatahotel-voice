# Oscar Suite

108 m² / 1,163 ft²

Max 4 adults

Shower and bathtub

Views of Regent Street

Kitchenette and Living room

Champagne, fruit and flowers

24hr VIP service

Chauffeur-Driven Car

The Oscar Suite is a spacious one-bedroom retreat overlooking Regent Street and London’s rooftops. Enjoy the luxury of a chauffeur-driven Mercedes-Benz S-Class or V-Class minivan for up to 5 hours daily.

Designed by David Chipperfield with fumed English oak, the suite features a contemporary yet intimate living area with a large golden Sienna marble dining table for six, lounge seating, a large entertainment system, a guest bathroom, and an expansive butler’s pantry.

The bedroom, accessed through double doors, includes ample wardrobes and a large desk, leading to a Carrara marble bathroom with natural daylight, a rainfall shower, a separate marble bathtub, double sinks, and a mirror screen TV. The Oscar Suite accommodates up to four adults and can be combined to create a two-bedroom or up to a 12-bedroom wing.
