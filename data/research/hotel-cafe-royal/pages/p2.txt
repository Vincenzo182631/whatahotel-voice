## Extraordinary Suite Inclusions

Seamless Arrival, Effortless Departure

Enjoy complimentary return transfers within a two-hour radius, thoughtfully included as part of your suite experience - perfect for elevating a staycation from the very start. From the moment you leave your doorstep to your effortless return home, every detail is taken care of, allowing you to fully switch off, settle in, and savour a truly seamless weekend escape.

Fresh Flowers by the Award-Winning Florist Jamie Aston

Jamie Aston, renowned for his award-winning floral designs, and his skilled team carefully handcraft seasonal floral arrangements to adorn our Deluxe suites and higher categories.

Luxury Suites with Complimentary Chauffeur Service

Enjoy a dedicated chauffeur throughout your stay. On arrival day, you'll be collected from the airport or station; on departure day, you'll be returned there. On the days in between, your chauffeur is available for up to 5 hours each day. Guests staying in our suites can choose between a Mercedes-Benz S-Class sedan or V-Class minivan. Those in our Royal Suite or Dome Penthouse can indulge in the luxury of a Rolls Royce.

An Award-Winning Warm Welcome

Begin your stay with a truly unique and playful welcome treat from one of the World’s Best Pastry Chef, Albert Adrià. Showcasing his signature creativity and artistry, this delightful indulgence sets the tone for an extraordinary experience at Hotel Café Royal.

Historic Dining at Café Royal Grill

As part of your suite experience, enjoy a three-course seasonal dinner at Café Royal Grill. Each dish is crafted with the finest ingredients of the season, paired with a thoughtfully curated wine list to complement your meal. With its elegant setting and exceptional service, this dining experience captures the history of Hotel Café Royal.

VIP Service - 24/7

Our VIP service, available 24 hours a day, is dedicated to fulfilling your every desire, whether it's day or night. From gourmet delights to personalised assistance, our team are ready to enhance your experience with continuous attention. Services include unpacking and packing during arrival and departure, daily shoe-shine or garment pressing.

Laundered Luggage Service

We present an exclusive Laundered Luggage Service. Upon your arrival, our attentive team will unpack your belongings before transporting your suitcase (of any brand) for laundering, following the standards set by TUMI. If you have any damaged TUMI luggage, our team ensures swift access to the repair shop and for those seeking new luggage, our team is pleased to organise a VIP shopping service at the TUMI store.

## Terms and Conditions

**Availability**

- Suite inclusions are available when booking direct.
- Available for new bookings only, as of September 1st 2025
- 24-hours’ notice is required to book the chauffer service or supercar
- Suite inclusions apply only to the suite category booked and are not applicable to complimentary upgrades from lower room types
- Should the guest wish to reserve their personal chauffeur car, then confirmation must be provided at least 24 hours prior to the required start time.
- \*The daily chauffeur-driven service is only available for up to 5 consecutive hours per full day – not applicable for day of arrival of departure.

**Rate, Tax and Service Charge**

- Rates in our booking engine are based on double occupancy and are quoted inclusive of 20% VAT. Rates are subject to 5% discretionary service charge

**Offer Conditions**

- The private chauffeur service are only applicable from Deluxe Suite stays and higher. The 5-hour consecutive service starts from the requested pick up time.
- The 5-hour daily chauffeur service starts from the day after arrival until the day before departure.
- We will always provide an electric or hybrid vehicle where available, unless a petrol or diesel model is specifically requested.
- Return transfers are available exclusively for guests staying in Deluxe Suites and above. Depending on your suite category, you will enjoy a chauffeur-driven Mercedes S-Class sedan or V-Class minivan for journeys within a two-hour radius of Hotel Café Royal.
- Inclusions which occur once per stay are not applicable for consecutive bookings.
- 24-hours’ notice is required to book the chauffeur service.
- **Dining Inclusions**
- The Cafe Royal Grill dinner is available once per stay.
- The three-course dinner included in Deluxe Suites and above applies to our standard à la carte or set menus – This inclusion does not apply to special festive dining events, including Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year’s Eve. Guests may apply a dining credit toward these festive menus if desired. Thanksgiving dinner may be included as part of the package, subject to availability.
- Laundered Luggage Service limited to a maximum of 3 pieces of luggage per stay.
- The suite inclusions do not apply to ‘Two bedroom Family Room’ Stays.
