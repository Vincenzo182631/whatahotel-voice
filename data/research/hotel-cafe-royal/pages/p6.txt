# Regent Suite

90 m² / 969 ft² on average

Max 2 adults and 2 children

Shower and bathtub

Views of Regent Street

Kitchenette and Living room

Champagne, fruit and flowers

24hr VIP service

Chauffeur-Driven Car

Regent Suites are designed to resemble spacious central London apartments, with the added luxury of a chauffeur-driven Mercedes-Benz S-Class saloon or V-Class minivan for up to 5 hours daily. Created by Sir David Chipperfield, these one-bedroom suites feature a contemporary style with calming rusticated Portland Stone and offer views of elegant Regent Street.

The suite includes a large living room with a Siena marble dining table for six and expansive windows. The separate bedroom is filled with natural light and features a king-size bed and entertainment system. The Carrara marble bathroom includes double sinks, a rainfall shower, a marble bathtub, and a mirror TV screen. Additionally, there is a half bathroom located in the generous lobby and corridor.

The suite also features a kitchenette and minibar. Regent Suites accommodate up to two adults and two children up to age 12.
