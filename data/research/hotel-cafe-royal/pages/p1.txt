## Our Charity Partner

Hotel Café Royal is proud to support Honeypot Children’s Charity, whose work provides essential care, respite and long-term support for young carers and vulnerable children across the UK. We believe it is important to use our platform to help charities that create meaningful change. In addition to hosting an annual Patron’s Club Reception at the hotel, we support Honeypot throughout the year by contributing experiences and donations to their wider fundraising initiatives, helping to extend the impact of their vital work.
