# Club Suite

105 m² / 1,130 ft²

Max 4 adults

Shower and bathtub

Views of Regent Street

Kitchenette and Living room

Champagne, fruit and flowers

24hr VIP service

Chauffeur-Driven Car

Designed by Sir David Chipperfield, this unique and luxurious suite combines Café Royal’s French heritage with contemporary design. For added convenience and luxury the suite offers an exclusive chauffeur-driven Mercedes Benz for up to 5 hours daily. The living room is bathed in original rich red and gold gildings, and restored mirrors are complemented by a golden Sienna marble dining table and sumptuous seating.

In contrast, the spacious bedroom with a sitting area is a calming space decorated in soft Portland stone with views of elegant Regent Street. This suite also boasts a guest bathroom, a spacious dressing area, and a Carrara marble bathroom with double sinks, a rainfall shower, a separate marble bathtub, and a mirror screen TV.

The Club Suite can be extended to form the Presidential Suite or Presidential Wing and sleeps up to four adults.
