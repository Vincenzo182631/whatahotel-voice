What's the vibe?
The parade of Rolls-Royces, Teslas, and Range Rovers lining the semicircular driveway is the first sign that you've entered a rarefied world. The Ritz-Carlton, Charlotte is indeed the granddaddy of luxury hotels in the area, and it has all the bells and whistles you might expect. Also, the building is LEED gold.
How are the rooms?
The rooms and suites have a traditional spirit with soft colors and plenty of polished wood. Splurge on the Ritz-Carlton Suite and your room comes with a Peloton bike.
Is there a charge for Wi-Fi?
The Wi-Fi is free.
Tell us about the food and drink.
The restaurants and bars lure as many local as they do hotel guests. BLT Steak is the signature restaurant; the K Lounge is a bar with light bites. Bar Cocoa isn't just a dessert bar—it offers baking classes as well.
Thoughts on the service?
Exemplary and standard-setting.
What's the crowd like?
Well-dressed, well-educated, and well-traveled.
How's the neighborhood?
The Ritz-Carlton is in the heart of the action; however, it's so serene inside that it feels like its own world.
Any other hotel features worth noting?
The bilevel spa is stunning and features multiple relaxation lounges, though the place to be is the Aqua Lounge with indoor pool and poolside dining.
Bottom line: Worth it? Why?
Yes—this is Charlotte's ultimate luxury experience.
