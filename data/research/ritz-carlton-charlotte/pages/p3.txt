Thoughtful Cuisine & Local Ingredients
Dining
Local flavors define culinary offerings at The Ritz-Carlton, Charlotte's restaurants. Locally-cultivated herbs garnish dishes and cocktails at upscale lounges, with decadent selections at the dessert bar. Each dining experience offers an elevated taste of local cuisine in this hotel restaurant in Charlotte, North Carolina.

The Ritz-Carlton, Charlotte welcomes Sweet July Cafe, where California wellness meets Caribbean hospitality. Enjoy fresh salads, smoothies, pita foldies, and signature treats in a vibrant setting designed to nourish, inspire, and delight.
