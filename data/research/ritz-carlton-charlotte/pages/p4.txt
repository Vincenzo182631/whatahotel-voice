Relax and Rejuvenate at Our Luxury Charlotte Spa
Unwind with a CBD treatment in Charlotte, North Carolina
The Ritz‑Carlton Spa, Charlotte is an elevated urban retreat designed to restore balance, renew the senses, and nurture wellbeing from the inside out. Nestled within the heart of Uptown, the luxury spa in Charlotte, North Carolina blends tranquil lounges, therapeutic treatments and advanced skincare rituals to create a sanctuary of relaxation and revitalization. Guests are invited to unwind in luxurious wellness spaces—including the Aqua Lounge, Relaxation Lounge and Vitality Lounges—before enjoying tailored massages, facials, body therapies and enhancements thoughtfully crafted to rejuvenate mind, body and skin.
Welcome to Your Urban Sanctuary
Step into a soothing haven above the bustle of Uptown Charlotte. The Ritz‑Carlton Spa offers a peaceful escape featuring the Aqua Lounge, tranquil Relaxation Lounge, and therapeutic Vitality Lounges—each designed to ease tension and inspire rest. Whether indulging in a treatment or simply unwinding between services, guests are invited to enjoy serene spaces that elevate every moment of their journey at this wellness retreat in Charlotte.
