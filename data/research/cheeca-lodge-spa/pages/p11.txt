# RESORT VIEW ONE BEDROOM SUITE

PreviousNextRESORT VIEW ONE BEDROOM SUITE - View gallery

Enjoy premier views of our Florida Keys luxury hotel and a location within easy reach of our tennis courts and tropical gardens while staying in this suite. In addition to an inviting aesthetic this Resort View One Bedroom Suite offers a spacious living room with Queen sized pull out sofa, screened in balcony with seating for two, kitchenette and much more.

**Additional Features:**

- Kitchenette with full refrigerator, microwave, stovetop burner and dishwasher
- 681 sq. feet with plush King Bed
- Separate living room with Queen Sized Pull Out Sofa
- Can accommodate two adults and two children 16 and under
- Spacious screened in balcony with seating for two
- Deep soaking tub and separate glassed in shower
- Close proximity to tennis courts and tropical gardens
- Need extra space? This guestroom can connect with the Resort View Double Queen Guestroom to accommodate up to eight.
