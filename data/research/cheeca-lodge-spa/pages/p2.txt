# LUXURY ISLAMORADA stays

Since 1946, Cheeca Lodge & Spa has set the standard of luxury in Islamorada, widely regarded as the most enchanting destination among the world-famous Florida Keys. Infused with both elegance and an effortless sense of comfort, Cheeca Lodge & Spa offers a wide variety of accommodation types. Choose from 243 rooms and suites and enjoy views of the white sands and turquoise waters, lush tropical gardens, cascading waterfalls, and our sprawling executive golf course. Or, indulge in one of our 43 new premier oceanfront rooms featuring spectacular, unobstructed views of the Atlantic Ocean and private open-air tubs.  Make paradise yours at our Islamorada resort.
