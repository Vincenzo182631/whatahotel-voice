About the Suite:

Spread out and relax among 681 sq. ft. of paradise in the Florida Keys. Discover your own personal oasis in our Golf Course One Bedroom Suite at Cheeca Lodge & Spa.

Additional Features:

- 681 sq. feet with plush King Bed and luxurious rain shower
- Separate living room with Queen Size Pull Out Sofa
- Spacious screened in balcony with seating for two
- Close proximity to Main Lodge and Beach area
- Sprawling views of Executive Golf Course
- Can accommodate two adults and two children under 16

Also available as a pet-friendly room, view our FAQs for pet policy
