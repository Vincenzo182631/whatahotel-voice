# DISCOVER PARADISE AT OUR FLORIDA KEYS RESORT

Turquoise water. Towering palms. Calm breezes. Discover barefoot luxury and let the laid-back lifestyle of Islamorada become your new standard of perfection. At Cheeca Lodge & Spa, the pursuit of leisure extends far beyond the ordinary, promising a blend of thrilling activities, unparalleled relaxation, luxurious accommodations, and exceptional dining within the embrace of our 27-acre oceanfront Islamorada resort.

## MEETINGS & TEAM BUILDING

Our Florida Keys meeting venues are the ideal setting for sales meetings, corporate retreats, trainings, conferences and more. In addition to stunning venues encompassing more than 15,000 square feet, Cheeca Lodge offers a host of team-building events and activities that will bring your team together beyond the boardroom, from culinary-based teambuilding to spa treatments and more.

# ADVENTURE YOUR WAY

Our oceanfront location allows us to offer a vast array of activities for fun-seekers. Guests are welcome to enjoy snorkeling in North America's sole living coral barrier reef, embarking on a kayaking adventure along the picturesque coast, or taking a plunge into the depths with a scuba excursion.
