# LAGOON VIEW ONE BEDROOM SUITE

PreviousNextLAGOON VIEW ONE BEDROOM SUITE - View gallery

Enjoy use of a private balcony, marble bathroom with oversized soaking tub and rain shower, breakfast bar and fully equipped kitchen. After a day full of sun, sand and sea indulge in a relaxing retreat to the master bedroom with a plush pillow-top King Bed or stay up in the separate living area with Sleeper Sofa.

**Additional Features:**

- 681 sq. feet with plush King Bed
- Separate living room with Queen Size Pull Out Sofa
- Can accommodate two adults and two children 16 and under
- Spacious screened in balcony with seating for two
- Deep soaking tub and separate glassed in shower
- Serene view and sounds of Lagoon Waterfall
- Need extra space? This guestroom can connect with the Lagoon View Double Queen Guestroom to accommodate up to eight.
