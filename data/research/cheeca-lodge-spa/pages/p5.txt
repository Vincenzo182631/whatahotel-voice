# RESORT VIEW BUNGALOW

PreviousNextRESORT VIEW BUNGALOW - View gallery

This room boasts 415 sq. feet of living space, a vaulted wood beam ceiling, classic Florida Keys décor, and a private screened in balcony offering views of the beautifully landscaped grounds and some with partial views of the pool or beach. Come experience one of the top Florida Keys luxury resorts and enjoy extra space in this Resort View Bungalow.

**Additional Features:**

- 415 sq. feet with plush King Bed accommodating two guests
- Vaulted wood beam ceiling and sitting area
- Spacious screened in balcony with seating for two
- Oversized bathroom with shower
- Just steps away from the free-form family pool and 1,200’ beach
