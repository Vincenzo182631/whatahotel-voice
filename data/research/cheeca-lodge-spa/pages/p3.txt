Enjoy an oceanfront oasis that stretches over 1,400 sq. ft. offering large living and sleeping spaces in addition to spectacular views of the Atlantic Ocean and beyond.

- Spectacular views of the Atlantic Ocean and beyond
- Large, fully equipped kitchen
- Stunning marble bathroom with bathtub and shower

- Conveniently located in the Main Lodge
- Adjoining resort view king room to accommodate 2 more guests
