# OCEANFRONT TWO BEDROOM CASITA

PreviousNextOCEANFRONT TWO BEDROOM CASITA - View gallery

Boasting expansive views of the Atlantic Ocean and private beach, your private Oceanfront Two Bedroom Casita provides the ultimate space to relax. Beautiful ash wood and Mexican tile flooring, full kitchen, large living area and porch. Your luxury Casita experience includes butler service.

**Additional Features:**

- Large furnished porch
- Fully appointed kitchen with stainless steel appliances
- Washer & Dryer with laundry supplies
- Bathrobes and slippers
- Molton Brown bath products
- Rain shower
- Access to all Cheeca Lodge & Spa property amenities
