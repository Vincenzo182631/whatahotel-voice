# About the hotel

Ranked #12 in Conde Nast Traveller’s 2025 Readers’ Choice Awards

Located in the heart of South Kensington, one of London’s most charming neighbourhoods, The Kensington is a luxury 5-star hotel that sits only moments from near The Royal Albert Hall, The Victoria and Albert Museum, Kensington Gardens and the world-famous department store, Harrods.

Featuring open fires, high ceilings and heritage décor, the hotel’s elegant drawing rooms evoke a sense of Victorian grandeur and provide the perfect setting to read a good book or catch up with friends. Indulge in lunch, dinner or the quintessentially British tradition of
Afternoon Tea within the stunning environs of a regency period
Townhouse setting, or discover delicious cocktails and our expansive wine list in the warm surrounds of the
K Bar \- a hidden gem nestled within the heart of South Kensington.

Guestrooms and
suites at The Kensington reflect a delicate blend of period charm and modern comfort while the hotel’s signature suites take luxury to a new level. In essence, it all combines to lend a personal, familiar ambience - reminiscent of a beautifully appointed private home.

**\\_\\_\\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_**

The Kensington is owned and managed by The Doyle Collection, an Irish family-owned luxury hotel group with hotels in Dublin, London, Washington DC and Cork.
