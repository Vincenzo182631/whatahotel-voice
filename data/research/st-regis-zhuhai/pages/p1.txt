# Welcome to The St. Regis Zhuhai

## Unlock classic luxury in our elegant hotel in Zhuhai, China

Rising over the clouds in a beautiful coastal city, The St. Regis Zhuhai leads guests on a compelling journey curated with St. Regis' signature Butler service and rituals. Taking up the top 31 floors of the iconic, 72-story Zhuhai Tower, our luxury hotel is a waterfront haven in Wanzai, a beautiful coastal city known as the “Chinese Riviera.” Find an upscale haven in elegantly styled accommodations with signature amenities, refined design elements and breathtaking views of the vast Greater Bay and Zhuhai and Macao landscape. Thrill your palate with diverse and delectable cuisine at our hotel restaurants. Explore compelling attractions including Chimelong Ocean Kingdom and The New Yuan Ming Palace. Celebrate and succeed in more than 1,500 square meters of sophisticated event space at our business hotel in Zhuhai. Punctuate your day with a restorative massage, beauty or wellness treatment at our luxurious hotel spa. Recraft the classics in a modern way at our ocean view hotel in Zhuhai, China.

34th Macao International Fireworks Display Contest

Save the dates—September 12, 19, 25, and October 1 & 4, 2026. From The St. Regis Zhuhai, take in the brilliance of Macau’s fireworks against the night sky. With the sea and skyline as your backdrop, savour an elegant evening and an unforgettable moment of your journey.

### Yan Ting 宴庭中餐厅

Located on the 42nd floor overseeing the stunning city views of Macao and Zhuhai, Yan Ting offers classic Cantonese, à la carte fine dining, serving individually-crafted restaurant dishes in Zhuhai.

### Social 秀

The signature buffet restaurant is located on the 41st floor of our Zhuhai hotel. Social restaurant draws inspiration from the first-class dining saloons on board cruise vessels typically set aside for the wealthy in the early 1900’s.

Explore

### LaBrezza 意大利餐厅

Located on 71st floor, the Italian fine-dining restaurant LaBrezza offers Southern Italian cuisine. Enliven your senses with an open kitchen and table show experience, a cheese and cold cuts room and a wine cellar with over 400 labels in Zhuhai.

### The St. Regis Bar 瑞吉酒廊

The St. Regis Bar is an elegant destination for the influential and well-heeled. Located on the 41st floor, it offers Signature Afternoon Tea, Pearly Mary & Bloody Mary’s from The St. Regis worldwide and Gin Tonic selection.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Zhuhai?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Zhuhai is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Zhuhai allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Zhuhai.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Zhuhai?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Zhuhai. Self parking is available at The St. Regis Zhuhai, with rates of ¥5.00 per hour, ¥120.00 per day, ¥840.00 per week, and ¥120.00 perstay.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Zhuhai have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Zhuhai has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Zhuhai booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Zhuhai?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Zhuhai is Macau International Airport (MFM). MFM is located approximately 14.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Zhuhai have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Zhuhai does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
