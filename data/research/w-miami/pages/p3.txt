## Miami Spice At TULUM Rooftop Bar & Restaurant

From August 1 through October 31, 2026, enjoy a flavorful Miami Spice Lunch for $40 per person (plus taxes and service charge), available daily at TULUM Rooftop Bar & Restaurant. This three-course experience includes your choice of infused water, soft drink, or iced tea, with standout dishes like our Salmon Tostada, Ropa Vieja, Peruvian-Style Quarter Chicken, and indulgent Churros or Tres Leches Verrines for dessert. ## Brickell Brunch Sessions

Get ready to elevate your weekend vibes with Brickell Brunch Sessions at TULUM Rooftop Bar & Restaurant, every Saturday and Sunday from noon to 4PM! Indulge in crave-worthy bites like Short Rib Nachos, Tres Leches Waffles, and Skirt Steak with Nopal Chimichurri, paired with handcrafted cocktails and bold flavors. Saturday brings the energy with a live DJ spinning beats, turning brunch into a full-on vibe. Whether you're here for the tacos or the tunes, this is your weekend go-to in the heart of Brickell.
