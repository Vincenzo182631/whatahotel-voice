### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Miami have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Miami have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Miami?

The largest occupancy room at W Miami is the Penthouse Suite, 2 Bedrooms, 1 King Bed, 2 Queen Beds, Bay View, Mobility Accessible with Roll-In Shower, Sofa bed, Larger, Living Room with a maximum occupancy of 8.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Miami?

Some rooms at W Miami permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Miami have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Miami have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at W Miami have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Miami have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Miami have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Miami have hair dryers.
