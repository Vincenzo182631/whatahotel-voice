W Miami is an urban oasis centrally located in the Brickell District. The hotel has 146 rooms, each with a private balcony and views of either the city or the Miami River as well as floor-to-ceiling marble bathrooms with W-signature Bliss amenities. W Miami is home to new restaurant, ADDiKT, located 15 floors above Brickell Avenue, that redefines New American cuisine. Whether it’s detoxing at the on-site spa, lounging on the WET Deck at the 15th floor pool, or sipping on signature cocktails before exploring Downtown Miami, W Miami offers the ultimate city experience.
