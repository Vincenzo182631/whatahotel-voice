# Welcome to W Miami

## Stay at our urban-meets-luxury ﻿downtown, Miami, FL, hotel

Own the moment at W Miami, a luxury hotel in the heart of Brickell—Miami’s dynamic financial district. Discover our hotel in Brickell, Miami, a pet-friendly retreat with sweeping views of the Miami River and downtown skyline. As a hotel on Brickell Ave, Miami, W Miami offers effortless access to South Beach, PortMiami, Kaseya Center, and more. This downtown Miami hotel features thoughtfully designed guest rooms and suites with floor-to-ceiling windows, select private balconies, marble bathrooms with signature W amenities, and bold, contemporary style. Savor local and global flavors at TULUM Rooftop Bar & Restaurant, complemented by panoramic views of Biscayne Bay. With more than 4,000 sq. ft. of flexible indoor and outdoor event space, W Miami sets the stage for inspired meetings, celebrations, and weddings. Guests also enjoy access to the Icon Brickell pool, spa, and fitness center (operated independently from the hotel). At W Miami, elevated style meets the vibrant pulse of the city.

Building Façade Enhancement & Restoration Project

The Icon Brickell community, which W Miami is a part of, is undergoing an exterior façade restoration. During this upgrade, access to balconies in some guest rooms & suites will be prohibited & views may be hindered. Also access to the Icon Brickell 15th Floor pool deck will be limited to weekends.

### Amenities at Icon Brickell

W Miami is a part of the Icon Brickell complex, a luxury community in the heart of Brickell featuring the hotel, residences, and first-class restaurants, Cipriani and Cantina La Viente. As a registered guest of the hotel, you will have exclusive access to the Icon Brickell Pool, Spa, and Fitness Center. Access to these facilities are available from the 15th floor deck, and guest registration is required at entry. The amenities are managed and operated by Icon Brickell and are currently open Saturday and Sunday, subject to change, and access is strictly limited to registered guests only.

### Parking Information

Valet parking is available on-site, operated by Icon Brickell. Please note that hourly rates will apply, and in-and-out privileges are not included with valet service. Self-parking options are also available within walking distance of the hotel. Guests may choose from several nearby facilities for their convenience.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Miami?

The check-in time at W Miami is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Miami allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Miami. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Miami?

On-site valet parking is available at W Miami. The daily valet fee is $55.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Miami have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Miami has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Miami booking.

What is the closest airport near W Miami?

The closest airport near W Miami is Miami International Airport (MIA). MIA is located approximately 7.9 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Miami have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, W Miami does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
