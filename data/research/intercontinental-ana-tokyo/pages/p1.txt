Important Announcements

- Children aged 12 and under stay free when sharing a bed with an adult (one child per bed). Children aged 13 and above are considered adults. Breakfast is not included for children sharing a bed, even with a breakfast-inclusive package. Breakfast at Cascade Cafe is available for JPY 2,600 per child (ages 6–12). Breakfast is complimentary for children aged 5 and under.
- For children aged 4–12 sharing an existing bed, access to the Club InterContinental Lounge is available at an additional charge of JPY 5,000 per child per night. Club InterContinental Lounge is exclusively available to guests staying on eligible Club InterContinental room rates.
- On 12 January 2027, there will be an electricity outage from 12:00 a.m. to 5:00 a.m. for annual maintenance. Internet connection will be down from 11:30 p.m. on 11 January to 4:30 a.m. on 12 January. We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your kind cooperation.

### Club InterContinental

Exclusively for guests in our 162 Club rooms and suites, the Club InterContinental Lounge offers a refined sanctuary high above the city. Spanning the 35th and 36th floors, it is Tokyo's largest two-floor lounge, providing a luxurious space to work, relax, or unwind in private.

### Le Petit Chef - The World's Smallest Chef

ANA InterContinental Tokyo invites you to experience the best of theatre and dining in an immersive culinary journey following Le Petit Chef - a popup restaurant, the world's smallest chef! Come and enjoy a unique and flavorful foodie experience!

Learn more

### Best meetings and event venue in Tokyo

ANA InterContinental Tokyo offers 22 versatile venues, including the renowned Prominence Ballroom, mid-size suites, bright sky-high spaces on the 37th floor, and a dedicated small-meetings center. Combined with our event team's expertise and the hotel's acclaimed cuisine, these facilities provide the ideal setting for a successful event.

### Unkai Japanese Restaurant

Overlooking the manicured perfection of a traditional garden and pond designed to highlight each season’s beauty, Unkai showcases seasonal specialties and the essence of Japanese cuisine. For an unforgettable experience, private tatami dining rooms offer refined surrounds in which to savor traditional cuisine such as the Kaiseki degustation meal.

## FAQs for ANA InterContinental Tokyo

Does ANA InterContinental Tokyo have a pool?

ANA InterContinental Tokyo has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Our outdoor pool offers guests a chance to soak up the sunshine and relax while in the heart of the city. The swimming pool is only open during the summer.

Pool information

How much is parking at ANA InterContinental Tokyo?

At ANA InterContinental Tokyo, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 3900 JPY .

Can I bring my pet to ANA InterContinental Tokyo?

No, pets are not allowed at ANA InterContinental Tokyo.

Can I cancel my booking at ANA InterContinental Tokyo for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does ANA InterContinental Tokyo have a restaurant on site?

ANA InterContinental Tokyo has 13 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at ANA InterContinental Tokyo?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at ANA InterContinental Tokyo.

What are the check-in and check-out times for ANA InterContinental Tokyo?

Check-in at ANA InterContinental Tokyo is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does ANA InterContinental Tokyo offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the ANA InterContinental Tokyo offers free WiFi!

How far is ANA InterContinental Tokyo from the nearest airport?

ANA InterContinental Tokyo is conveniently located near Haneda Airport (HND), approximately 8.08 miles away (13 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near ANA InterContinental Tokyo?

ANA InterContinental Tokyo is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Asakusa - 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)
- Shibuya - 1.86 miles away (3 kilometers)
- Hie-Jinja Shrine - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Imperial Palace East Garden - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Tokyo Tower - 0.93 miles away (1.5 kilometers)
- Ginza
- Mori Art Museum - 0.75 miles away (1.2 kilometers)
- Yebisu Garden Place - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- Ark Hills - 0.01 miles away (0.01 kilometers)
- Roppongi
- Odaiba - 3.11 miles away (5 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, ANA InterContinental Tokyo puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
