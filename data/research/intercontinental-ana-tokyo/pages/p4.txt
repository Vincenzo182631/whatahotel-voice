### Club InterContinental

Exclusively for guests in our 162 Club rooms and suites, the Club InterContinental Lounge offers a refined sanctuary high above the city. Spanning the 35th and 36th floors, it is Tokyo's largest two-floor lounge, providing a luxurious space to work, relax, or unwind in private.
