### Trip to Japan

11 days ago

We spent five nights at the InterContinental ANA Tokyo at the beginning of our trip, and we couldn’t have chosen better. The hotel is ideally located in Akasaka, the rooms are spacious and spotless, and the city views are stunning. The service lives up to Japan’s reputation: attentive, discreet and always welcoming.

The Club InterContinental experience was a real highlight of our stay. Under the leadership of Loïc Dupupet, the Club team delivers truly exceptional service: warm, personalised and attentive to every detail. The lounge was a wonderful place to relax at any time of day, and we always felt like valued guests.

A very special thank you to Loïc Dupupet and Raphaël Avenier for their invaluable advice, outstanding service and excellent recommendations. Thanks to them, we experienced Tokyo at its best and discovered places we would never have found on our own. Their availability and kindness truly made all the difference.

We look forward to coming back on our next visit to Tokyo. Thank you to the whole team!

### Great Club lounge.

15 days ago

We stayed 2 visits over the summer. Location is good, close to the Ginza line, with easy access to the tourist areas of Tokyo.

The rooms are plush and the club lounge has great views over the city. Staff in the lounge are lovely, with special mention to Ms Shimada, Carlito and also the two French gentlemen. Afternoon tea is superb, evening cocktails include Moët champagne, small hot dishes brought to the table and a selection of cold items including deserts. With a great atmosphere it’s certainly worth adding lounge access to any booking. We are looking forward to returning for Christmas.
