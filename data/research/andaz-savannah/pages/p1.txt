# Escape to Our Boutique Downtown Savannah Hotel at Ellis Square

Warm southern hospitality meets modern luxury at Andaz Savannah, located in the heart of downtown Savannah’s Historic District on the lively Ellis Square. Just steps to the city’s top shopping, dining and historic sites, our boutique hotel makes it easy to explore River Street, the vibrant City Market, the serene Forsyth Park and more. After a day of exploring the city, recharge in your spacious hotel room or suite with a balcony view, followed by Southern cuisine at our in-house 22 Square Restaurant and Bar.

## Rooms & Suites

Take in the views of the city or our pool terrace from your charming room or suite. All our spacious hotel rooms draw upon downtown Savannah’s distinctive style and charming green squares. Indulge in an Andaz Deluxe Suite or Andaz Deluxe Loft for a kitchenette, balcony and other upgraded amenities. Let us welcome you to the Hostess City of the South.

### 22 Square Restaurant

Taste the flavors of the Georgia coast at 22 Square, the trend-setting eatery at Andaz Savannah. With a simple approach to cooking, our downtown Savannah restaurant is a community table where friends gather and share modern Southern-inspired cuisine made from locally grown produce and sustainable ingredients, from breakfast to late-night bites.

### 22 Square Bar

22 Square Bar is a cozy neighborhood spot where mixologists shake, stir, pour and play the perfect host. Alongside the Southern cuisine of 22 Square Restaurant, sip classic and contemporary cocktails hand-crafted from fresh juices, artisanal liquors and homemade syrups and tonics. Join us on Saturday evenings for live music by Savannah's own.

### Pool Terrace

This little oasis is waiting for you to spend an afternoon swimming or lounging poolside right in downtown Savannah. Treat yourself to a relaxing cabana for more privacy or grab the perfect spot by the pool. Enhance your time by the water with cocktails or light bites in the sunshine and nightly s’mores around the firepit in the evening.

## Pets Are Welcome

For your visit to downtown Savannah, our boutique hotel welcomes your most cherished travel companion. To allow your dog to stay in our guest room pet program, please inform the hotel that you’ll have a dog(s) staying with you at least three days in advance of your arrival by calling 912-233-2116. In addition, only dogs weighing 50 pounds and under or two dogs under a combined weight of 75 pounds are permitted. All pet friendly rooms are located on the 2nd floor. For guest tranquility, while exploring the city, treat your pet to a day of bliss at blissfulbuddhapetservices.com. For 30+ nights, the pet fee is at the hotel’s discretion.

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

\*Pet Waiver must be signed at check-in. While on property, dogs must be leashed in all common areas. For the comfort of all guests, Food and Beverage outlets are reserved for registered service animals only. Pets cannot be left unattended.
