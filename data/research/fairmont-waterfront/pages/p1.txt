## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Waterfront?

Check-in time at Fairmont Waterfront is from 3:00 p.m. and check-out time is at 12:00 p.m.

Does Fairmont Waterfront have a swimming pool?

At Fairmont Waterfront, our rooftop pool, indoor hot tub, and change rooms are open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Our fully equipped fitness centre is accessible 24 hours a day.

Does Fairmont Waterfront have a gym/fitness centre?

Yes! Our fitness center is open 24 hours, seven days a week, with inspiring views overlooking Stanley Park and the North Shore Mountains.

Is there parking at Fairmont Waterfront?

- Overnight parking is available at the hotel and is in effect from 6:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. the next day.
- Self parking is available at $58/night. (guests must register license plate at the front desk).
- Valet service is available at $75.00/night.
- Over-height vehicle parking is available at $125.00/night.

Pricing includes in-and-out privileges and all taxes.
