## Das Stue

### Welcome

Right in the heart of Berlin’s embassy district. Just steps from the zoo. And yet far removed from the hustle and bustle of the city.

Das Stue is a sanctuary for all the senses: quiet, elegant, and unique. Artful interior design, French cuisine, and a bar with character come together to create a space that energizes—and relaxes at the same time.

A hotel for people who want to experience Berlin.

And who, in between, just want to take a deep breath.
