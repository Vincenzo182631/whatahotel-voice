### Vitality Pool & Sun Deck

**Hours of Operation: 8:00 am to Sunset**

Located on our Eighth Floor Urban Escape, our Vitality Pool allows long distance swimmers and those taking a relaxing dip with an appealing infinity pool design.  The Sun Deck also features a custom hot tub and comfortable private cabanas…all with views of the Pacific Ocean.

The perfect way to experience Halepuna Waikiki is to indulge and relax in one of our semi-private cabanas overlooking our vitality pool deck. Please inquire with our Guest Relations Desk for availability, or reserve your cabana ahead of time.

Complimentary sunscreen and cooling gel available.

**Pool & Water Features**

- Vitality Pool & Sun Deck
- Lap Pool for leisure and exercise
- Heated whirlpool hot tub

### SpaHalekulani

**Hours of Operation: 9:00 am to 6:00 pm**

Guests of Halepuna Waikiki are able to choose from a selection of exclusive Spa and Salon treatments.

In-Suite and services are also available.

**Bath Amenities**

Our exclusive SpaHalekulani collection is inspired by the healing springs of Loko O’o and the cleansing ritual “hi’uwai,” known to purify the body and spirit and restore a state of balance. Using locally sourced Hawaiian oils and extracts, the SpaHalekulani’s philosophy of renewed well-being can now be experienced by guests at Halepuna Waikiki.

### THE GARDEN

**Hours of Operation: 8:00 am to 8:00 pm**

A peaceful environment for small gatherings or self-reflection, The Garden is open and natural in design, featuring a unique Reflexology Path designed to stimulate the soles of the feet while balancing the mind and body. Colorful Hawaiian botanicals reflect the island’s natural beauty, and this unique open space provides a dynamic environment for your own, unique special event in the heart of Waikiki.

**The Garden Features**

- Cushioned lounge chairs and shaded areas
- Complimentary organic sunscreen
- The Garden Sanctuary

- Quiet spaces for relaxation and reflection
- Native Hawaiian botanicals
