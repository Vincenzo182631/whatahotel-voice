Holidays
Welcome to a Season of Silver and Sapphire, where cherished traditions meet the effortless beauty of Grace Bay. Surrounded by shimmering turquoise waters, silver moonlight, and the warmth of Caribbean hospitality, we invite you to celebrate a holiday season filled with meaningful moments, unforgettable dining, and joyful family experiences.
Dear Guests
Every experience has been designed by our Ladies and Gentlemen to inspire connection, discovery, and celebration. Whether you gather around a festive table, watch the sunset over sapphire seas, or create new traditions together, we hope your time with us becomes part of your family's story for years to come.
Warm Holiday Wishes,
HUGO LECANDA
GENERAL MANAGER

Moments to Discover
Christmas
Christmas Eve
Celebrate Christmas Eve on Thursday 12.24, with the following events: Christmas Delights, Festive Afternoon Tea, Holiday Cocktails Social, Pizza Masterclass, and Holiday Bliss Escape.
Christmas Day
Embrace the Christmas spirit on Friday 12.25, with the following events: Christmas Dinner, Christmas Delights, Festive Afternoon Tea, Holiday Cocktail Social, Wine Tasting Experience, Holiday Bliss Escape and Christmas Day Snorkel Sail.
New Year's
New Year's Eve
Celebrate the final day of the year on Thursday 12.31 , with the following events: New Year's Dinner, Color Fest After Midnight, New Year New Radiance Spa experience, New Year's Fireworks Sail.
New Year's Day
Welcome the New Year on Friday 01.01, with the following events: Silver Sliders Social, and New Year New Radiance Spa experience.

Childhood Memories Begin With Moments of Wonder
Like hidden pearls waiting to be discovered, every day brings a new adventure for our youngest guests. From festive crafts and island explorations to magical moments with Santa, each experience is designed to inspire joy, creativity, and lifelong memories.
Children's Activities
Christmas Eve
On December 24, join us at Ritz Kids for two festive holiday activities. From 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM, little ones can meet Santa and pose for memorable family photos, creating a treasured holiday keepsake. Then, from 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM, bring holiday traditions to life by decorating your own gingerbread masterpiece with colorful candies, icing, and festive imagination.
Christmas Day
On December 25, experience the joy of the holiday season with a special visit from Santa and his cheerful companions at Grace Bay Beach at 10:30 AM, where warm greetings and delightful surprises await. Later, from 5:15 PM to 7:15 PM, celebrate Christmas with a festive party featuring music, games, dancing, and sweet treats, creating a joyful gathering especially for our youngest guests.

The Festive Season is Best Savored Together
From elegant dinners and vibrant island flavors to leisurely celebrations that linger into the evening, each dining experience is an invitation to gather, indulge, and create memories around the table.
