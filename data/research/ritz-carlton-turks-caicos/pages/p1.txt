Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos. Self parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos is Providenciales International Airport (PLS). PLS is located approximately 12.3 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Turks & Caicos offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
