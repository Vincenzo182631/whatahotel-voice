Coffee House
Ritual Coffee Culture
Step into an extraordinary coffee experience at Ritual Coffee Culture, designed to captivate coffee enthusiasts. This new coffee shop offers expertly brewed illy coffee selections. The modern coffee bar aesthetic enhances the artisanal coffee experience, complemented by the unique warmth of the Turks and Caicos Islands. The menu includes homemade croissants, delightful sweet treats, savory options, nutritious parfaits and acai, and overnight oats to start your day. For lunch, choose from fresh salads and hearty sandwiches, perfect for any time of day. Enjoy exceptional coffee and cuisine, where each visit becomes a cherished ritual. Opening Date: JUNE 15, 2024. Restaurant Highlights
BLT Steak
This modern interpretation of an American steakhouse and a vibrant bar combines classic bistro ambiance with a menu featuring specially selected steaks, fresh seafood and the chef's daily blackboard menu.
Coralli
Enjoy a subtle combination of fresh local ingredients inspired by traditional Mediterranean flavors in a captivating setting. Coralli celebrates the soulful cuisine and convivial spirit from the Coastal areas of Italy, Greece and the Middle East.
Casa Nonna
Casa Nonna is our tribute to authentic flavors and quality ingredients that are the hallmark of true Italian cuisine. From salumi, cheese and antipasti platters, to house-made pastas, the menu is available à la carte and family style, overflowing with options ideal for sharing.
