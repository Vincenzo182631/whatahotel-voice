# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

We offer valet service for hotel guests and visitors.

Free for our guests and visitors

Free

### Self-Parking

We offers a secure indoor parking facility at no additional cost for in-house guests, residents and guests visiting outlets and functions. Designated spaces are available close to the hotel’s entrances for patrons with disabilities.

Free for our guests and visitors

Free

### Electric Vehicle Charging Station

The EV charger is available exclusively for guests and is subject to availability. EV Charger Specifications Output power: 22kW max Outlet connector: IEC 62196 Type-2 Output voltage range: 400V Output current range: 32A max Charging outlet cable: standard 5-meter length.
