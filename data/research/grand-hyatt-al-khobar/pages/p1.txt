## A Bold 5-Star Landmark in Al Khobar’s Prestigious Olaya District

Grand Hyatt Al Khobar Hotel and Residences, a striking 5-star destination in the city’s prime district with direct access to Al Rashid Mall, is the ideal choice for bold travelers, leaders, and dreamers. Blending iconic design with world-class facilities, it offers state-of-the-art venues, elegant ballrooms, diverse dining, a luxurious spa, and serviced residences—perfect for business, leisure, or long-term stays.

Pool

Take an exhilarating dip in our glorious lap swimming pool, featuring mood lighting, relaxation beds, and a fabulous snack bar. Savor one of our enticing mocktails or enjoy a freshly squeezed fruit juice surrounded by sublime tranquility and grand architecture. Open 6 a.m.–10 p.m.

Grand Residences

Grand Hyatt Residence is a striking, boldly designed accommodation, situated in Al Khobar’s most envious location: the Al Olaya district. The Residences are ideal for both business and leisure activities, offering guests the best service and experiences in the region.

Nirvana Spa and Fitness Center

Immerse yourself in an energizing workout at our Nirvana Spa and Fitness Center, located on the S Floor. Featuring the latest in weights and cardio equipment, you will be genuinely inspired. Enjoy complimentary infused water, fruits and cold towels to round off your session. Cardio equipment includes LifeFitness treadmills, bicycles and cross trainers, and you’ll find spinning bikes and a weightlifting bank that includes dumbbells and free weights. The gym is open 24 hours, while the other facilities and treatments are available 9 a.m.–10 p.m
