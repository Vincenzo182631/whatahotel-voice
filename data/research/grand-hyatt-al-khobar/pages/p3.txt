## Asmahan Restaurant

A modern take on Arabian cuisine. You are welcomed to this imaginative dining experience with unabashed and mysterious lantern-filled decorations.
Open for breakfast, lunch and dinner, Asmahan is the hotel’s all-day dining outlet with an exceptional, full of flair buffet and a la carte options boasting both Western and Arabian culinary choices.

Awarded as Best All-Day Buffet Dining in Middle East & North Africa by World Luxury Awards 2025, and Best Mediterranean Cuisine in Saudi Arabia by Haute Grandeur Global Awards 2025

## Rosalie Café

Expect to be struck by the rapturous smell of freshly baked hot bread, croissants, and cakes when entering this sublime French Patisserie.
You can expect a glorious choice of sandwiches, snacks, desserts, coffees, and teas to entice your taste buds. With its location next to the mall entrance, Rosalie is a fantastic place to catch your breath after a dynamic shopping spree.
