# Restaurants & Bars

From the ground floor to the top floor, the restaurant and rooftop bar at Thompson Nashville deliver inspired cuisine matched only by the exceptional skyline views of The Gulch and downtown Nashville. Our signature restaurant, Marsh House, pairs a curated wine list with modern Southern-style dishes and seafood, while up on our lively indoor-outdoor rooftop bar and dining, L.A. Jackson serves light bites, shareables and hand-mixed cocktails and also hosts seasonal DJ sets.

## Marsh House

Located on the ground floor, Marsh House highlights responsibly sourced seafood and Southern-inspired cuisine, with a seasonal menu and standout raw bar. The wine list features more than 400 labels. Brunch is served daily, and weekday happy hour includes $1.50 oysters Monday through Friday from 5:00 to 7:00 PM in The Gulch.

## L.A. Jackson

Head to the top floor of Thompson Nashville, where L.A. Jackson pairs indoor and outdoor rooftop views of The Gulch with craft cocktails, local and regional beers, and shareable bites. DJs set the tone on select weekends. Primarily walk-in, with tables available to reserve for larger groups and special events. 21 and over.

## Killebrew Coffee

Start your day off in our boutique hotel’s lobby-level coffee shop, Killebrew Coffee, in The Gulch. Take your pick from a variety of coffee drinks with seasonal specials, as well as the pastry counter, brimming with treats like croissants, muffins and stuffed cookies. Find a nook to enjoy your morning coffee, focus on work or catch up with friends.
