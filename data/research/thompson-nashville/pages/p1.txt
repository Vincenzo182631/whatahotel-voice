# Boutique Gulch Hotel Near Broadway \| Thompson Nashville, by Hyatt

## A BOUTIQUE HOTEL IN THE HEART OF THE GULCH

Thompson Nashville, our boutique hotel located in The Gulch neighborhood near Broadway, sets the stage for your downtown Nashville experience. Welcome to Music City, where a vibrant nightlife, amazing museums, chef-driven dining and a continuous lineup of live shows provide an unforgettable getaway. Our Nashville hotel is located just steps from art and culture — Broadway’s Honky Tonks, Bridgestone Arena, Music Row, the Frist Art Museum, Geodis Park, and endless boutiques. Inspired by our locale, our luxury hotel melds classic country soul with urban sophistication, from the guest rooms and suites to the restaurants, meeting and wedding venues and more.

## Rooms & Suites

The energy, atmosphere and beating heart of Music City play out just steps from our front door. Take in downtown Nashville through floor-to-ceiling windows in each of our 224 guest rooms and luxury hotel suites, where sliding barn doors, chic midcentury-inspired furnishings and rainfall showers ensure your stay is everything you need it to be. To experience The Gulch in elevated style, select the 767-square-foot Executive Suite — boasting a separate bedroom with a king bed, living area and dining room.

### Pets are Welcome

Thompson Nashville is a pet-friendly hotel in downtown Nashville’s popular Gulch neighborhood. We welcome dogs at no additional charge. Guests must provide notice of traveling pets prior to check-in. Upon check-in, a signed pet agreement is required. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

#### Pet Fees

- Price: Free

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  40Pounds

- Combined pets weight limit: 50Pounds

- Maximum number of pets is 2.
