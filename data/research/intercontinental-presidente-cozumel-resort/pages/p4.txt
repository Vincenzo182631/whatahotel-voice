### Cozumel hotel and dive packages

Experience world‑class scuba diving in Cozumel with Scuba Du. For 32 years we’ve delivered safe, tailored adventures led by PADI‑certified instructors aboard state‑of‑the‑art boats. Discover dramatic walls, shipwrecks, coral formations and thriving marine life on the planet’s largest reef—live the adventure!

Learn more

### Renew Your Body and Mind at Ikal Spa

Experience Ikal, a Spa in Cozumel where Mayan rituals meet advanced therapies to relax body and soul. Indulge, renew, and revitalize in the jacuzzi, cold plunge pool, sauna, steam room, beauty salon, and temazcal. Open daily 9:00 am–7:00 pm. Tel: +52 987 872 9500 Ext. 88.

### Exclusive Temazcal in Cozumel

Experience a purification ritual guided by a shaman at the temazcal of Presidente InterContinental Spa. Enjoy a unique wellness experience in a natural and elegant setting. Reservation required. Phone: +52 987 872 9500 Ext. 88

### Private Cabanas by the Sea

Experience luxury in our seaside cabanas with gourmet dining, attentive service, and breathtaking views. Located at Le Cap Beach Club and the main pool of Presidente InterContinental Resort & Spa Cozumel. Contact our Concierge for details: +52 987 872 9500 Ext. 6320.
