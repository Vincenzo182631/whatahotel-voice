## FAQs for InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa

Does InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa have a pool?

InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa has 1 outdoor pool.

Infinity Pool

Enjoy the perfect 535 m2 pool with views of the Caribbean Sea that blend with the horizon. All our pools offer towel service and are located close to sanitary services.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa for hotel guests.

Parking is free for guests.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa.

Pet policy description. Up to 2 small pets 30 kg max per room are allowed with a charge of 60 USD each. They must be supervised at all times and not access common areas. Exempt from charge: guide or assistance dogs with documentation.

Pet fee per night: 60 USD

Pet weight limit: 30

2 pets allowed

Pets allowed: Only dogs and cats allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa has 4 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa?

Check-in at InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa is conveniently located near Cozumel International Airport (CZM), approximately 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa?

InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Punta Sur - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- Chankanaab - 1.8 miles away (2.9 kilometers)
- San Gervasio Archaeological Zone
- Isla Pasión
- El Cielo

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Presidente Cozumel Resort Spa puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
