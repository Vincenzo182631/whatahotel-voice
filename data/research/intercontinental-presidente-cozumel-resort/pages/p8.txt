## Things to do

### Punta Sur

Carretera Costera Sur, Km. 9, Zona Hotelera Sur, COZUMEL, Quintana Roo 77600

Punta Sur is one of the top attractions in Cozumel, featuring an ecological park, a museum, a beach area, and beach clubs.

Learn more

### Chankanaab

Chankanaab in Cozumel is a beautiful natural park offering white sandy beaches, crystal-clear waters ideal for snorkeling, tropical gardens, and a spectacular cenote. It’s a perfect destination to experience the natural beauty of Mexico and discover top Cozumel things to do.

Learn more

### San Gervasio Archaeological Zone

The Mayan Ruins of San Gervasio in Cozumel, Mexico, are a fascinating attraction in Cozumel. This archaeological site offers a unique view of temples and ceremonial pathways that tell the story of an advanced society. Exploring these ruins is an immersion into the mystery and grandeur of a lost culture. Discover more at the top attractions in Cozumel.

### Isla Pasión

Isla Pasión is a paradisiacal corner in Cozumel, Mexico. With white sand beaches and turquoise waters, it is perfect for romance and tranquility. Its palapas and lush vegetation create an idyllic setting. Ideal for romantic getaways and unforgettable experiences.

Learn more

### El Cielo

El Cielo in Cozumel is an amazing sandbar in the middle of the Caribbean Sea. With crystal clear waters and natural umbrellas, it is an earthly paradise for swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing. When it comes to Cozumel things to do, visiting El Cielo is unquestionably one of the most outstanding experiences for nature and marine beauty lovers.
