# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self-parking available

As a hotel with parking in Madrid city center, Thompson Madrid gives guests arriving by car access to a convenient self-parking at the public Telpark – Plaza del Carmen car park, shared with Thompson Madrid. Clearly marked spaces reserved for hotel guests are available, with an exclusive elevator offering direct access to the hotel for added convenience.
Please note that Thompson Madrid is located within Madrid Central, the city’s low-emission zone. Local traffic regulations apply. For more information, please visit the official traffic authority website.
