Located in the heart of the city, Thompson Madrid by Hyatt places you just moments from Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor, Gran Vía and San Miguel Market, while inviting you to discover the quieter streets, independent boutiques and neighborhood cafés that reveal another side of the Spanish capital.
Inspired by residential living, our guestrooms and suites are rooted in local character, bringing together natural wood, leather and marble with contemporary design. Large windows and private terraces in select accommodations offer panoramic views across the city, creating spaces that feel connected to Madrid while providing a calm retreat above the vibrant streets.
Whether you’re returning from a day of gallery hopping, business meetings or late evenings in the city, step inside, settle in, and experience Madrid from a different perspective.
