# Luxury Boutique Hotel in Madrid Center \| Thompson Madrid, by Hyatt

## Luxury Hotel in Madrid - Unmatched Elegance Awaits

A New Way to Stay in Madrid.
Welcome to the place where Madrid’s historic center and its creative spirit come together. Thompson Madrid brings contemporary luxury to one of the Spanish capital’s most dynamic neighborhoods. Set beside Plaza del Carmen, just moments from Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor, Gran Vía and Plaza de España, our 5-star luxury hotel in Madrid places culture, dining and design within easy reach.
Inside, thoughtful architecture, curated interiors and locally inspired experiences create a stay that feels distinctly connected to the city. Spacious guestrooms and suites, destination dining, a rooftop pool with panoramic views and vibrant social spaces reflect the energy of Madrid while offering you a place to slow down and settle in.
Whether you’re discovering the city’s renowned museums, exploring the boutiques of Chueca or the expansive greenery of El Retiro, or visiting for business, Thompson Madrid by Hyatt offers a refined urban retreat in the heart of the city. Experience one of the most distinctive luxury hotels in Madrid city center, where every stay is shaped by culture, creativity and genuine hospitality.

## rooms & suites

At Thompson Madrid, our sophisticated rooms and suites, including the impressive two-story Penthouse Suite, elevate the experience of luxury boutique hotels. As a five-star hotel in Madrid, each space has been designed with wooden floors and residential inspiration, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere. The interiors reflect the Madrilenian style with details in leather and marble, while cutting-edge technology ensures a comfortable and modern stay in our hotel in Madrid, where design merges with functionality

## Take a Look Around

Get to Know Us

Restaurants & Fine Dining

Discover the future of dining at Thompson Madrid.  Our main restaurant,  innovative kitchen lab, turns each visit into a backstage experience, where guest chefs create limited-time dishes and every meal is a glimpse into the next culinary destination.  On the rooftop, enjoy a selection of aperitifs and cocktails while taking in panoramic views of the city.

Rooftop Pool

Our rooftop pool is the perfect place to unwind without leaving the heart of Madrid. With 360-degree views of the city skyline, this guest-only space features sun loungers, umbrellas, and a relaxed atmosphere to enjoy the sun, take a refreshing dip, or sip a cocktail from our pool bar. A different way to experience the city from above.

Luxurious Accommodations

Pamper yourself within sumptuous and authentically designed, marble- and leather-clad rooms and suites. Each includes inviting and locally inspired décor, referencing waterfalls, sculptural silhouettes, and earth tones, with high-end extras: luxury linens, D.S. & Durga bath amenities, a rain shower and more. Upgrade from a room to a suite for extra space and an optional soaking tub, pool table, or bar.

Things to Do in Madrid

Dive headfirst into the dynamic culture of Madrid, as you shop, dine, and dance your way through the vibrant New Golden Mile and other iconic downtown destinations near our hotel. Our neighbours include not only designer flagships, parks, flamenco theatres, Puerta del Sol tapas bars and the Triángulo del Arte, but convenient public-transport connections farther afield. Simply hop on the metro at nearby Gran Vía or Sol station.

### Makáá

Discover Makáá, Thompson Madrid’s rooftop restaurant, where Mediterranean cuisine, open-fire cooking and panoramic city views come together. Just moments from Gran Vía and Puerta del Sol, Makáá offers one of the most distinctive rooftop dining experiences in Madrid, pairing exceptional food with sweeping views of the Madrid skyline.

### Pets are Welcome

Thompson Madrid, by Hyatt, is a pet-friendly hotel that welcomes both dogs and cats. Pets are allowed, but they cannot be left alone in the room at any time. To ensure the comfort of all guests, dogs are not permitted in dining areas, such as the hotel's bar and restaurants. –
At check-in, it is mandatory to present an updated copy of the liability insurance policy covering any damages to third parties caused by the pet. Please contact our team for more information.

#### Pet Fees

- Per Night : **€50**

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  10kilograms

- Maximum number of pets is 1.
