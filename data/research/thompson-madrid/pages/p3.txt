# Restaurants & Bars

Discover a collection of distinctive dining experiences at Thompson Madrid, where contemporary cuisine, handcrafted cocktails and panoramic city views come together in the heart of Madrid. Just steps from Gran Vía, Thompson Madrid is also home to some of the best-loved bars in the city. Savor Mediterranean flavors and open-fire cooking at the rooftop restaurant Makáá, unwind with a cocktail in the stylish lobby bar, or settle into the intimate atmosphere of Jack’s Club, an elegant piano bar inspired by classic speakeasies. Looking for a quieter evening? Enjoy seasonal local dishes and drinks from the comfort of your own room.

## Makáá

Discover Makáá, Thompson Madrid’s rooftop restaurant, where Mediterranean cuisine, open-fire cooking and panoramic city views come together. Expertly grilled meats, fresh seafood and seasonal vegetables are complemented by creative starters and desserts. Just moments from Gran Vía and Puerta del Sol, Makáá offers one of the most distinctive rooftop dining experiences in Madrid, pairing exceptional food with sweeping views of the Madrid skyline.

Please note that this outlet is not eligible for World of Hyatt points.
