# Nijinsky Suite with Terrace

This exceptional suite designed by Olga Polizzi, our Director of Design, pays tribute to the graceful poise of esteemed ballet dancer and choreographer, Vaslav Nijinsky, who was a frequent visitor to Hotel de Russie. The exquisite Nijinsky Suite makes a statement as a grand Roman residence. Its composition of indoor and outdoor space mirrors Nijinsky’s elegance and symmetry of form. Italian antiques harmoniously blend with bespoke modern artworks.

Vibrant red walls and terracotta cabochon flooring pay homage to Rome’s beating heart, while a curated collection of 19th-century and Valadier-era objets d'art contribute to the feeling of historical sophistication....Read More
