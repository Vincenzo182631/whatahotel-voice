# Family Stays

Our Rocco Forte Kids programme ensures younger guests will be just as pampered as the grown-ups. We welcome children of all ages – our programme is tailored for babies (ages 0-3), kids (ages 4-12) and teens (ages 13-16).

## Rocco Forte Kids

Each of our hotels has a special animal theme with details that children will love, from personalised Rocco Forte Kids passport at check-in and welcome treats, to pint-sized bathrobes and slippers and animal cuddly toys. Older guests can enjoy bespoke itineraries created by our Concierge team, special spa treatments or interactive entertainment in their rooms.

Hotel de Russie’s baby frog lives in the shade of the hotel’s terraced Secret Garden. His favourite food is our Tiramisù.
