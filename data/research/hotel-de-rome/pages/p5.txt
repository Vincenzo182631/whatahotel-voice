### Our Story

The best ideas are often dreamt up over a drink between friends. And so it was, whilst sipping tequila, that Lydia Forte and Edgardo Osorio were inspired to create Aquazzura Bar at the Hotel de Russie.

Additional Information

We wish for everyone to feel comfortable, so whilst we do not operate a dress code, we kindly recommend that our guests are respectful of the environment we have created here at Hotel de Russie and refrain from wearing sportswear, shorts and beach shoes in our restaurants. Our guests within the hotel usually opt for a smart-casual style.
