## Designed to stay together

140 sqm / 1,506 sqft · Up to 7 guests · 3-bedroom suite

Bringing together two Junior Suites and one Single Room, this three-bedroom suite is connected by a private corridor, creating a spacious, well-defined layout. Three bathrooms are thoughtfully distributed throughout the suite.

The layout allows everyone to stay close while maintaining privacy across separate bedrooms, making it ideal for families. Windows overlook the interior courtyard of the Eixample for a quieter atmosphere, with butler service available on request.
