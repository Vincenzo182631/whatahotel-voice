## A quiet room above the city

35 sqm / 377 sqft · 3 guests · King-size or Twin beds

Set on the hotel’s upper floors, these rooms offer elevated views across Barcelona, with a French balcony and full soundproofing for privacy.

Interiors combine original details, including a fireplace selected by César Ritz, with contemporary pieces such as a Roche Bobois desk and seating area. The marble bathroom features a walk-in rain shower.
