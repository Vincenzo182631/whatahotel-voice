## An inviting suite, with more room to settle in

55 sqm / 581 sqft · 3 guests or 2 adults and 2 children · King-size or Twin beds and a sofa bed

Defined by generous proportions, these one-bedroom suites feature a separate living room with a sofa bed and large desk. Windows overlook the sunlit inner courtyard of the Eixample, offering a calm, residential feel.

The marble bathroom includes a freestanding bathtub, walk-in double shower, and twin washbasins. A guest powder room adds comfort and privacy.
