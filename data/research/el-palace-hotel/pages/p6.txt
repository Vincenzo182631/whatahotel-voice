## A spacious suite, designed for comfort

40 to 51 sqm / 431 to 549 sqft · 3 guests or 2 adults and 2 children · King-size or Twin beds and a sofa bed

Interiors include a sofa bed, large desk, and original architectural details. The marble bathroom features a freestanding bathtub, walk-in rain shower, and twin washbasins.
