## Two bedrooms, one seamless family stay

76 sqm / 818 sqft · 4 guests · King or Twin beds

Spanning two beautifully appointed interconnecting rooms, the Family Room is designed for families who want both comfort and space. City views stretch from each French balcony, and interiors balance heritage charm with modern ease — making it the ideal base for a family stay in the heart of the city.

Both rooms can be configured as King or Twin beds, adapting effortlessly to each family's needs.

Two marble bathrooms, each with a walk-in rain shower, ensure effortless comfort for every member of the family.
