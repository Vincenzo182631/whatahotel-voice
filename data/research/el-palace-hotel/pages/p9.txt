## A suite with more room to unwind

50 sqm / 538 sqft · 3 guests or 2 adults and 2 children · King-size or Twin beds and a sofa bed

A sense of privacy shapes this one-bedroom suite, where a separate living room offers space to relax or work, creating a more residential feel. Windows overlook the inner courtyard of the Eixample, adding a quieter atmosphere.

The marble bathroom features a freestanding bathtub and walk-in rain shower, with twin washbasins.
