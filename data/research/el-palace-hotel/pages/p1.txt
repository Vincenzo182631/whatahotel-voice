## Welcome to El Palace Barcelona

A luxury 5-star hotel originally born as The Ritz, thoughtfully reimagined for today’s traveler.

Just steps from Passeig de Gràcia, the hotel offers refined rooms and suites where history, style, and modern comfort come together effortlessly, creating a genuine sense of place in one of the city’s most iconic locations.

Discover Amar Barcelona by chef Rafa Zafra, take in panoramic views from the Rooftop Garden, unwind in the serene Mayan-inspired spa, and experience curated cultural moments that blend heritage, gastronomy, and warm hospitality in a way that feels timelessly Barcelona.
