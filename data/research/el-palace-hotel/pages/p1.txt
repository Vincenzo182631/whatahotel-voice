# Hotel El Palace Barcelona

## Discover El Palace Barcelona

Since opening its doors in 1919, El Palace Barcelona has welcomed monarchs, aristocrats, artists, and musicians alike. History itself has been written within the walls of this iconic hotel in the heart of Barcelona. For over a century, El Palace Barcelona has stood as a timeless symbol of luxury and elegance in the city’s vibrant center. Born as the former Ritz of Barcelona, the hotel continues to embrace its rich heritage, blending timeless glamour with contemporary experiences and impeccable service, creating a truly elegant and memorable stay. While its rich past will forever define its character, the Hotel continues to look ahead. It has completed an ambitious full refurbishment of its public areas, suites, and rooms, as well as the creation of the breathtaking Rooftop Garden, a true urban oasis set to delight guests for years to come.

## Key Selling Points

- Located in the epicenter of Barcelona and over 100 years of history.
- 120 rooms and suites in its historic building. The carefully selected furnishings ensure total comfort and relaxation.
- Exclusive, original and unique, the Art Suites and El Palace Suites are each inspired by an art (painting, dance, architecture, literature, sculpture, and music) through a person who is a reference to it and that boasts a close relationship with the hotel.
  - **Cesar Ritz Art Suite** is dedicated to the creator of the contemporary concept of luxury hotels.
  - **El Palace Suite by Josephine Baker** is dedicated to this big screen star who would perform at the Hotel when she visited Barcelona.
  - **Salvador Dali Art Suite** is in the same suite where Dali lived for long periods while working on his art.
  - **Joan Miro Art Suite** is dedicated to one of most influential artists of the twentieth century and surrealism.
  - **Carlos Ruiz Zafon Art Suite** is dedicated to the award winning writer.
- **El Palace Suite** by Ronnie Wood is an art deco suite that has a very different personality from the rest of the art suites as it embodies the legendary guitarist of the Rolling Stones.
- **AMAR Barcelona,** a collaboration between El Palace Barcelona and chef Rafa Zafra, and a declaration of love to the city, the Mediterranean, and an honest, product driven cuisine.
- **The Great Hall** hosts a variety of immersive experience and activities, including evocative art exhibitions, refined gastronomic experiences such as the iconic afternoon tea, and live music performances.
- Inspired by the romantic garden at the palace of Francesc Cambo, the **Rooftop Garden** is the most exclusive in all of Barcelona. This open-air garden, you will find some of the most exciting seasonal activities, such as yoga, Pilates, master classes and an open-air cinema.
- **El Jardin** hosts breakfast each morning surrounded by a beautifully sunlit garden.
- **Mayan Luxury Spa** where sounds, rituals, and traditional treatments are combined with high quality French cosmetic products from the exclusive and prestigious Anne Semonin.
