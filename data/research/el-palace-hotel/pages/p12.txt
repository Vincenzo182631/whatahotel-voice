## A suite where Salvador Dalí stayed

1-Bedroom Suite · 110 sqm / 1,184 sqft · 2 guests · King bed

2-Bedroom Suite · 150 sqm / 1,614 sqft · Up to 4 guests · King bed

The suite preserves the décor from Dalí’s stays at El Palace Barcelona, offering a direct connection to the artist’s time in the city.

Separate living and dining areas, city views, and a Roman-style marble bathroom sit alongside restored antiques and layered details that reflect his presence.

"Each morning when I awake, I experience again a supreme pleasure – that of being Salvador Dalí"

Salvador Dalí

Born in Catalonia, famous around the world

Salvador Dalí was a frequent client of the hotel during the decade of the 1970s. During that time, he photographed models in the same Roman bath that is in the suite today.

The birthday present

One of our most surreal memories with Dalí was when he sent a taxidermied white horse up the main staircase of the hotel to the room where he was staying with his wife, Gala, presenting it to her as a birthday present.
