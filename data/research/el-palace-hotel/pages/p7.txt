## A light-filled space with expansive city views

35 sqm / 377 sqft · 2 guests · King-size or Twin beds

Expansive city views and full soundproofing ensure total tranquility in these light-filled rooms, opening onto a French balcony. Interiors balance 1919 heritage with modern comfort, featuring an original fireplace, Roche Bobois desk, and seating area.

The marble bathroom features a walk-in rain shower, offering a refined setting in the heart of the city.
