Additional Dining at Buoy Haus Beach Resort

### Sugarfin

Redefine beachfront indulgence with a daily breakfast buffet and sparkling Bellini Bar, followed by all-day Caribbean-Asian fusion dining that marries signature sushi with bold new creations blending vibrant island flavors and masterful Asian techniques.

### Salt Shack

Salt Shack is located on Morningstar Beach at Buoy Haus. Grab your wood-fired Caribbean dishes and hand-crafted cocktails from the shack and settle in at our picnic-style communal tables. Caribbean flavors, generous plates—enough to savor solo or share by the shore—pair perfectly with our rotating lineup of vibrant entertainment under the stars.

### Isla Blue

Embark on a culinary journey that transcends borders, from Mexico to the Caribbean to Japan, as we artfully blend vibrant island flavors with the freshest seafood and innovative preparations.

### SandBar

Sip. Savor. Scene.™ Just steps away from Morningstar Bay, taste fresh shellfish, tapas, Spanish pizzettes, & paella while sipping Sangria de Fresa at our oceanfront sanctuary.
