### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa?

The largest occupancy room at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa is the 3 Bedroom Presidential Suite with a maximum occupancy of 12.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa?

Some rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have fridges?

No, rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
