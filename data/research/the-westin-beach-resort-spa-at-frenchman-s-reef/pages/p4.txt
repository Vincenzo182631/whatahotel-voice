### Plan Your Stay - Suggested Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrive & Relax

Welcome to St. Thomas!

Settle into your room and gaze out to your breathtaking view of the Virgin Island's natural beauty.

Sip poolside craft cocktails and take in the endless ocean views at the three stunning pools.

Enjoy fresh sushi bites and watch the golden sunset on Sugarfin's Terrace.

Day 2 – Adventure on the Sea

Enjoy a casual breakfast buffet with a stunning view of Charlotte Amalie at Shorebird.

Set sail on adventure aboard the Flying Frenchman with Dockside Watersports - snorkel with the turtles at Maho Bay, eat tacos made fresh on a boat at Lime Out, and more.

Take a beachside stroll at sunset down to Salt Shack for a locally-inspired menu of island favorites such as grilled whole snapper or blackened mahi tacos.

Day 3 - Heavenly Relaxation

Breakfast on the beach at Salt Shack.

Experience relaxation and rejuvenation with a massage and facial at the Heavenly Spa.

Enjoy the sauna, steam room, serenity pool and relaxation room.

Grab and paintbrush and a cocktail and unleash your creativity in an Art Uncorked - Seaside Sip n' Paint Class.

Day 4 - Caribbean Culture

Breakfast overlooking Charlotte Amalie at Shorebird.

Visit Charlotte Amalie and see the historical landmarks such as Fort Christian, 99 Steps, and the 3 Queens Statue.

Experience a journey of local rums from the Caribbean in a rum.

Dance with your toes in the sand at the Beach Club after a Caribbean BBQ.

Day 5 - Beach Bliss

Have fun in the sun at Morningstar Beach.

Take the adventure to the sea with use of complimentary watersports such as Hobie sail cats, kayaks, paddleboards, snorkel gear and more.

Experience golden hour at Sandbar with premium craft cocktails and paella.

Watch movies under the stars and with your toes in the sand at the Beach Club.

Day 6 - Explore the Virgin Islands

Explore St. John by private charter boat or ferry.

Hike one of the many trails in the National Park.

Snorkel one the world's top beach - Trunk Bay or walk the historical sugar mill ruins.

Shop the many local boutiques of Cruz Bay.

Listen to live music and have dinner at one of the many beachfront restaurants in Cruz Bay.

Day 7 – Pool & Departure

Reserve a piece of poolside paradise for the morning in a private cabana.

Soak in the views and rays before your final departure.

Depart in a private transfer for St. Thomas Airport.

We wish you well and hope to see you again soon!

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton, Fitness Mirror, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
