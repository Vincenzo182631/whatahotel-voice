## Two World-Class Resorts. One Paradise

Against a stunning backdrop of tropic blue, you are immersed in a dreamy, world-class luxury resort in St. Thomas, Virgin Islands. One that draws inspiration from the abundance and diversity of the natural elements and life that surrounds us. Within this thriving ecosystem, we distill the very best of our island environment and seamlessly translate it to you; creating an entirely unique travel experience that’s as richly colorful and lively as the coral reefs that ring these islands. Guests at The Westin Beach Resort & Spa are offered the convenience of accessing the Sandbar, Isla Blue, and Salt Shack restaurants at Buoy Haus Beach Resort St. Thomas, Autograph Collection. Tide Pool, The Buoy Haus Fitness Center and lounge chairs within the Buoy Beach section of Buoy Haus Beach Resort St. Thomas, Autograph Collection are restricted to guests of Buoy Haus.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 20.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa?

On-site valet parking is available at The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa. The daily valet fee is $45.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa is Cyril E. King International Airport (STT). STT is located approximately 5.3 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have an airport shuttle to Cyril E. King International Airport (STT)?

Yes, The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa has an airport shuttle to Cyril E. King International Airport (STT).

Does The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin St. Thomas Beach Resort & Spa does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
