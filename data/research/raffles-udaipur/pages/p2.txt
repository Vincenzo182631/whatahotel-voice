Rooms & Suites

# Havens of _serenity_

Discover a warm and welcoming sanctuary of beauty, charm and gentility.

-

### Rooms

Our spacious, impeccably designed rooms are a harmonious blend of Rajasthani, Mughal and European influences, with exquisite furniture handcrafted by local artisans, a selection of beautiful artworks, and luxurious furnishings. All have a generous balcony with breathtaking lake views; many have a private pool, a beautifully manicured garden or both.

Discover
-

### Suites

The opulence of our spacious suites reflects Udaipur’s regal heritage. Every suite has a panoramic view of the lake and is a veritable palace in its own right, with furniture exquisitely handcrafted by local artisans, a selection of carefully chosen artworks, luxurious furnishings, a manicured garden and private plunge pool.

### Lakeshore Accomodation

Set within the tranquil non-island Lakeshore residences, Lakeshore Accommodation at Raffles Udaipur offers refined rooms and suites overlooking Udai Sagar Lake. Designed for space, privacy, and unhurried stays, guests enjoy signature Raffles service with seamless access to island dining and experiences.

## A rare hushed _elegance_

The City of Lakes is known for its extravagant royal residences, and Raffles Udaipur follows this stately tradition, evoking a grand and comfortable country house and a passion for everything authentic, beautiful to the eye and agreeable to the heart. Our gracious Raffles Butlers are on hand 24/7, ready to elevate your hotel experience to the sublime. Relax. Indulge. Enjoy.
