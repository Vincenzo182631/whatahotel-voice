## AN ISLAND _escape_   IN UDAIPUR

_A **Two-Michelin Key Hotel** set amid the beauty of Udaisagar Lake_. Raffles Udaipur features 137 rooms and suites across two serene settings, each offering a distinctive expression of the destination. On the private island, 101 rooms and suites open to Baroque-inspired gardens and tranquil lake views, while 36 rooms and suites at Raffles Lakeshore Udaipur offer a quieter, more intimate retreat by the water.

Our story

## Exceptional luxury in a natural setting

Raffles Udaipur opened in 2021, its ornamental gardens, tumbling fountains and stately Indo-Saracenic architecture describing a love story between India and the West. Set on a 21-acre private island rich with birdlife and exotic flora and fauna, this contemporary reimagining of a country mansion is the first Raffles hotel in India.

Dining

## Sensational cuisine from times gone by

Sawai Kitchen lovingly revives forgotten recipes from the region’s royal households. Using traditional techniques and seasonal produce to reinterpret dishes for modern palates, the creative menu offers diners a unique opportunity to explore a bygone era.

Discover Reserve

Spa

## Your sanctuary for health & wellbeing

A haven for sensorial journeys based on ayurvedic principles, our Raffles Spa blends ancestral healing practices with modern-day comforts. Six spacious treatment rooms, including a luxurious couples suite, are complemented by an outdoor temperature-controlled leisure pool, fitness centre, badminton court, lawn tennis court and an array of activities for all ages. Come and discover a place of peaceful pastimes and a sanctuary for emotional wellbeing.

## Frequently asked questions

### What unique dining experiences does Raffles Udaipur offer?

You can savor a range of culinary delights, from local Indian flavors to international cuisine, all crafted by expert chefs.

### How can I book a stay at Raffles Udaipur?

Simply visit the Raffles website or call their reservation number to check availability and make your booking.

### What activities are available near Raffles Udaipur?

Explore the rich culture of Udaipur, visit historic palaces, or enjoy boat rides on Lake Pichola for a memorable experience.

### When is the best time to visit Udaipur?

The ideal time is from September to March when the weather is pleasant for outdoor activities and sightseeing.

### Why choose Raffles Udaipur for your stay?

This luxury hotel combines stunning architecture, lush surroundings, and exceptional service, ensuring an unforgettable experience.
