# A timeless 5-star hotel with spa in Florence

Steeped in history and glamour, Helvetia & Bristol has been enchanting guests since 1885, when Florence was a popular stop on the Grand Tour. A recent renovation added a new wing with interiors by celebrated **designer Anouska Hempel** and the largest **luxury spa** of the historic center, spanning 550 square meters, with calidarium, tepidarium, sudatorium, frigidarium and two spectacular spa pools. The hotel is also a culinary destination with a daily breakfast buffet including pastries by **Maestro Iginio Massari** and a restaurant and bar by **Cibrèo**, a true institution in the Florentine food scene.

Helvetia & Bristol Firenze - Starhotels Collezione is part of the Leading Hotels of the World, Virtuoso and Altagamma.
