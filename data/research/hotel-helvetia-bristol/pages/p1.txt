## Overview

**Description**

Restored Palace-style Hotel dates from 1890; antique furnishings and artwork blend with modern comforts in a formal ambience.

**Location and History**

A long-time favorite with a wonderfully central location near the Duomo, Uffizi and Via de Tornabuoni's upscale shops, just a few short blocks from Piazza della Repubblica, this hotel ranks high in the race for boutique-hotel superiority in this city.

A peach in Starhotels' constantly growing portfolio, this place bucks the chain's commercial trend providing an air of blue-blooded authenticity in the original five-story palazzo and adjoining building.
