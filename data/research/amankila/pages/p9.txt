**Weddings**

Spectacular settings to celebrate the once-in-a-lifetime union abound on the resort’s dramatic clifftop and beachside location with panoramic views over the Lombok Strait. High above the jungle-edged coastline beside the resort’s iconic three-tiered pool, say ‘I do’ with the sacred mountains as the only witness. Alternatively, exchange vows on the black sand beach, before walking an aisle strewn with flowers by local Balinese children to the reception in a coconut grove.

\\
**Celebrations**

Whether an intimate party or a festive gathering, Amankila’s expert team can tailor exceptional experiences for the most memorable of stays. Overlooking Amankila’s signature swimming pool, Sandikala and Terrace Bar claim the loftiest sea views on the property, while the poolside Terrace offers open-air bales seemingly perched above the sea. Events can be held poolside in the resort’s stilted villas, at the Beach Club, and on the beach. Cruises on the Lombok Strait, mountain biking to picnic breakfasts in the hills and exploring the artisanal villages of East Bali are just a few of the activities on offer to create a multi-day retreat – ideal for those wishing to prolong the celebrations.

\\
**Private Dining**

Amankila celebrates the conviviality and warmth of traditional family dining with a seasonal, social and sustainable sourced focus. Serving exquisite Indonesian and international cuisines with the produce sourced from the resort's own organic farm and garden or foraged from forest floors or plucked fresh from the oceans. Perched on the hillside, Sandikala, Amankila's signature dining venue, offers mesmerising views of the Lombok Strait. Down on the shore guests and can enjoy private dining celebrations.

\\
**Entertainment**

Indonesia’s vibrant culture is felt throughout Amankila, with celebrations offering a subtle nod to the traditions of the surrounding region. To become at one with the resort’s setting, guests are invited to incorporate local music and dancing into their occasion, with musicians from nearby villages serenading the occasion, or dancers providing graceful entertainment.

\\
