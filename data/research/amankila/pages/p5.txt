### Beach Dinner

Two candlelit tables set apart on the sand, each with its own chef grilling dishes over open flame. When the meal concludes, a private balé awaits beside the bonfire, where the warmth of the fire and the tide’s gentle rhythm create a lingering calm that lasts long after the last course.

### Rijstaffel Dinner

Including ten vegetarian, seafood and meat dishes with rice, our Rijsttafel Dinner is rounded off with a trio of sorbets. As well as at Sandikala, the experience can also be enjoyed in the privacy of your pavilion.

\\
**Batu Jati Sunset**

Toast the sunset from our Batu Jati Rock Platform overlooking the Lombok Strait and Amankila’s black sand beach. As the sky changes colour, sip locally crafted cocktails accompanied by a selection of delicious canapes.

\\
**Cooking Class**

Learn the secrets of Indonesian cuisine in this hands-on cooking class with Amankila’s Balinese sous chef. After picking your own fresh herbs and vegetables from our garden, you’ll be guided step-by-step in the preparation of a multi-course meal.

### Megibung Dinner

Gather for our Megibung Dinner and immerse in the cherished East Balinese tradition of sharing a vibrant platter centred on _Nasi Tumpeng_ \- a cone-shaped offering of rice symbolising Mount Agung and the island's sacred abundance.

### Traditional Afternoon Tea

Every afternoon at The Bar, guests are invited to come together for our Traditional Afternoon Tea, with Balinese coffee, teas and handmade treats crafted by mothers from a nearby village - an inviting taste of local home-style indulgence.
