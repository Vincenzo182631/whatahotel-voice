## Our destinations

## Hoanib River Valley

In Namibia’s remote northwest, the Hoanib River Valley stretches across vast desert plains and rugged mountains within the wild landscapes of the Kaokoland.

## The Okavango Delta, Botswana

A beautiful melee of floodplains, woodlands and waterways, the Okavango Delta is undoubtedly one of the most famous wildlife-watching destinations in the world — and it lives up to every expectation.

## Makgadikgadi Pans, Botswana

A haunting and intriguing landscape, the Makgadikgadi Salt Pans form a large part of the Kalahari Basin and the shimmering, salt-baked landscape is iconic in its beauty.

## Conservation & community

## The Learning Tree Centre

Established by Tawana Camp in 2024, The Learning Tree Centre in Shorobe Village is helping to nurture the next generation of young minds in Botswana.

## Meet Our Anti-poaching Unit in Kwhai Private Reserve

Meet the brave men who stand as the last line of defence against poaching in Botswana’s Khwai Private Reserve – one of Africa’s last truly wild places.

## The Natural Selection Foundation

In keeping with our purpose “to be a force for good in the protection of natural habitats”, 1.5% of every guest’s stay at Natural Selection camps is committed to our conservation & Community projects.
