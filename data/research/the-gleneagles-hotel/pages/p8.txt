# Here’s to the soirees, shindigs and celebrations

On this page: Overview Dine & Drink Weddings Pursuits Groups

Here at Gleneagles, we know how to mark an occasion. Since 1924, we’ve been popping the corks, filling the dancefloor and pulling out all the stops to create magnificent celebrations worthy of the history books. Our wide range of event spaces makes sure celebrations at Gleneagles can be as small or as big, and as fun or as formal, as you want them to be.

Celebrate in style

## A place for camaraderie  and communion

From the tingling anticipation of first arrivals to the warm afterglow of a party well done, Gleneagles is a place for gathering the great and the good of your world for events that will linger in the memory – of organisers and guests alike – for years to come.

Why Gleneagles?

- We tailor every element of your celebration from birthdays and anniversaries to retirements, graduations and weddings
- Our dedicated team are on hand before, during and after your event to tie all your arrangements up neatly
- The easiest of journeys: an hour from Edinburgh or Glasgow, or arrive at our very own train station

## A Tailor-Made Menu

At the heart of most gatherings is the glorious breaking of bread, wonderful for both the literal and metaphorical joy it brings. At Gleneagles we don’t let the chance of a good meal pass us by and pull out all the stops for parties. Here’s a little taste of what to expect.

#### Food Made For Sharing

Whether you’re looking for a multi-course menu or a casual sharing spread, our team will work with you to fine-tune the details, helping to come up with your dream menu.

#### Carefully Crafted Drinks

Our bar team are just about as passionate as you’d find anywhere and exactly the kind of people you want to be crafting drinks for your big bashes.

#### The Finest Ingredients

We make no compromises when it comes to ingredients, gathering the best supplies from Scotland’s larder and further afield to fill your plate with the finest fare.

## Weddings

If you can dream it, we can do it. Weddings at Gleneagles are a glorious reflection of the couples who choose to get married under the Ochil Hills, in amongst some of Scotland’s most beautiful scenery.

From our ballroom to our bedrooms, our century-old spaces offer a glamorous slice of history where you can embark on your future.

## Fresh air and clear skies

Gleneagles is a place for adventurous souls. It’s a place to unleash your potential. A dead-eye on the clays, perhaps. Or a demon on the greens. Fearless behind the wheel of a 4×4. If you’re up for escapades, we’re ready with suggestions, world-class instruction and all the equipment you could ever need.

**Axe Throwing**\\
\\
Unleash your inner hunter-gatherer with an addictive round of axe-throwing. Held down at the Trail Yard, the home of woodland adventures at Gleneagles, this is a wonderfully competitive activity to put you through your paces. Go at it alone or join up into teams to see who can wield their weapon with the most accuracy.

EXPLORE MORE

**Climbing Wall**\\
\\
Reach all new heights with one of the most technically challenging climbing walls in the country. With a timed speed racing area, bouldering sections, cliff hangs and multiple climbing routes, there’s something for all abilities under the watchful eyes of our top-class instructors. With space for your whole party to play, this is the perfect place to give in to unadulterated fun.

EXPLORE MORE

**Golf**\\
\\
Tee off on a truly memorable round across one of our three championship courses. Fuel up with a relaxed breakfast at The Dormy before heading out to the greens, where friendly rivalry and impressive fairways set the scene for a standout day with friends, family or colleagues.

EXPLORE MORE

**Clay**\\
\\
Take aim amid our breathtaking grouse moor with guidance from our expert shooting instructors. Whether you’re a first-timer or a seasoned shot, our clay sessions blend skill, focus and a healthy dose of competition.

**Fishing**\\
\\
Both calming and captivating, fishing offers a rare chance to slow down and reconnect with nature. Accompanied by some of the country’s finest fly fishers, your group can cast into our rolling rivers or serene lochs and learn how to land a day’s catch.

## Clay Shooting

Enquire Now

DESCRIPTION

Suitable for all abilities of shot looking for a 75-minute experience of adrenaline-filled fun whilst learning from the best in their field. Our highly experienced instructors are at the top of their game and will help you to develop your skills. This experience at our world-renowned shooting range includes shotgun hire, cartridges and clays, safety glasses and hearing protection.

Suitable for ages 10 and up.

PRICE

75 minute lesson for 1 or 2 guests – £165 per person (up to 50 cartridges)

105 minute lesson for 3 or 4 guests – £165 per person (up to 50 cartridges)

Supplement -£20 per box of 25 Cartridges thereafter

_Prices exclude 12.5% discretionary service charge._

EXTRA DETAILS

Clay target shooting is available from age 10 upwards. Children will be introduced to all correct safety requirements.

## Archery

Enquire Now

DESCRIPTION

Using our recurve bows your instructor will teach, inform and develop your skill in this ancient sport.

More accomplished archers can test their abilities under the eye of their instructor, who will enhance their mastery of this challenging target sport. Archery is a sport that doesn’t require strength, but rather style, technique and co-ordination. It is a great family and group activity.

Suitable for ages 8 and up, children will be introduced to all the correct safety requirements.

Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.

PRICES

45 minute lesson – two guests £140

Supplement – per person thereafter £60 (maximum 5 people)

_Prices exclude 12.5% discretionary service charge._

EXTRA DETAILS

Archery is available from age 8 onwards. Children will be introduced to all correct safety requirements. Children under 10 must be accompanied by an adult.

DESCRIPTION

Suitable for ages 5 and up.

45 minutes and £140 per person

Minimum age 8 years

_Prices exclude 12.5% discretionary service charge._
