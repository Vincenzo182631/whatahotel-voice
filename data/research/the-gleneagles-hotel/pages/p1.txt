# About Gleneagles Hotel

The Gleneagles Hotel is a beautiful hotel set among the rolling hills of Scotland. The hotel is small and elegant, with just 232 hotel rooms and 26 luxurious suites. The dining at Gleneagles is exquisite, and its golf courses have been voted the best in the world. Another large attraction at Gleneagles is the spa, which combines healing waters and a variety of treatments for ultimate rejuvenation.
