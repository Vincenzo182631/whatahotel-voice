# The Gleneagles Sporting Club

Gleneagles Sporting Club is home to tennis, climbing and the fastest growing racquet sport in the world, padel. With a bright, spacious and relaxing lounge area, it’s a space to enjoy a coffee whilst catching up on emails or just kicking back in style when a well-deserved break is in order.

## Padel  & Tennis

Game, set and match…we have your racquet sports covered at Gleneagles with 8 tennis courts and three padel courts. Whether it’s a friendly hit on our outdoor tennis courts to a competitive game of padel on our indoor courts, racquet sports can be played all year round at Gleneagles.

Look no further than one of our qualified coaches to help you perfect that forehand, backhand, lob or volley with one to one or group lessons.

## Equestrian School

When it comes to horse riding in Scotland, the Gleneagles Equestrian School stands head and shoulders above the rest. We offer indoor and outdoor coaching, horse riding lessons and horse walking tours for guests at all levels, from age four up.

Our lessons and the scenery on our 50-acre hacking and cross-country courses make Gleneagles one of the best and most stunning places to experience horse riding in Perthshire. So, visit us for an unforgettable experience, whether you are a seasoned rider or just want to give it a go for the first time. Everyone, regardless of ability, is welcome at our equestrian school in Scotland.

## Climbing  & Bouldering

Gleneagles Sporting Club is also home to our 7m high and 27m wide indoor climbing and bouldering wall.

Come hail, rain and high winds our new indoor climbing and bouldering wall means the weather can’t stop the adrenaline filled feeling of scaling the heights. Great for all ages and abilities, indoor climbing and bouldering has never been so popular. With 40 ways to reach the top as well as a timed climb section, there’s a challenge for everyone.
