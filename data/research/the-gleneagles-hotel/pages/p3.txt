# We’re popping the corks and laying the table for unforgettable experiences

From bar snacks and brasserie classics to family fare and fine dining, Gleneagles is home to some of the best restaurants in Scotland and a culinary destination to savour.

On this page: Fine Dining All-Day Dining Bars

## Classical fine dining in Scotland

Fine Dining

## The Strathearn

Immerse yourself in the glamour of a bygone age in our contemporary fine dining restaurant. Expect theatre from the traditional gueridon service, melodic notes from the grand piano and exceptional modern Scottish dishes from some of the best chefs in the country, all served by candlelight.

VIEW RESTAURANT

Fine Dining

## Andrew Fairlie

Andrew Fairlie is one of Europe’s finest restaurants. This intimate two-Michelin-starred gastronomic delight is renowned for simple yet indulgent French-inspired cooking and exemplary service. If you are looking for the best fine dining in Scotland, you’ve found it here.

VIEW RESTAURANT

## Casual dining

## The Birnam

With its crackling open fire and light-drenched winter garden, The Birnam’s friendly, approachable air helps to maintain its footing as a firm favourite. The Italian-leaning menu majors in handmade pasta, steaks cooked over open flame, and tableside specials like the now-famous chicken Caesar salad.

VIEW RESTAURANT

**The Dormy**\\
\\
Overlooking immaculate greens and rugged hills, The Dormy’s laid-back vibe is a firm favourite for families, golfers and anyone who enjoys stone-baked pizzas, Tandoor curries and hearty classics.

VIEW RESTAURANT

**Afternoon Tea in the Glendevon**\\
\\
Each of our seasonal Afternoon Tea menus is as beautiful as the landscape that changes through the Glendevon's windows. Served with a flourish in plush surrounds, with all the airs and graces you want and none of the stuffiness, tea trays are laden with delicate fancies and properly posh sandwiches and savouries.

VIEW RESTAURANT

#### The Garden Cafe

The heart of our hotel, The Garden Cafe is the meeting point for morning coffee or the venue for luxurious long lunches. With ice cream and pick ‘n’ mix plus an impressive display from our in-house pastry kitchen, it’s a perfect pitstop for the whole family. It’s dog-friendly too, with plenty of space in the atrium to bring your four-legged friend.

VIEW CAFE

## Bars

## The American Bar

Our team of world-class mixologists are ready to welcome you to the opulent surroundings of our very own 1920s cocktail lounge, where award-winning signature serves, vintage Champagnes and classic cocktails are served with aplomb.

View bar

**The Century Bar**\\
\\
Whoever you’re meeting and whatever time of day, The Century Bar offers a warm welcome, a beautiful setting and a menu of delightful classics that span club sandwiches to lobster rolls. With 400 whiskies, a carefully curated wine list and endless expertly made cocktails, there’s never a bad time to pull up a pew.

VIEW BAR

**Auchterarder 70**\\
\\
With big comfy armchairs, oversized cushions, craft beers, comfort food classics and views over our manicured greens, Auchterarder 70 is the perfect place to raise a toast as the sun sets over The Glorious Playground.

VIEW BAR

## Still Room Whisky Tasting

Discover the art of whisky with our expertly guided tastings. From a deep dive into Scotland’s finest drams in our Whisky Masterclass to an indulgent journey through rare and exceptional selections, each experience offers rich storytelling, expert insight, and unforgettable flavours.

**Whisky Masterclass**

Embark on an exclusive journey into the captivating world of Scottish whisky. This masterclass offers a sensory exploration of five distinctive pours, guided by our Senior Whisky Ambassador. Delve into the rich history of whisky, develop your own ‘Vocabulary of Flavour’, and gain the confidence and expertise to discover your next favourite dram.

Perfectly tailored for participants of all levels, from novice to the more proficient.

Time: 4.00-5.00pm, Monday to Thursday. Spaces are limited, please book in advance (The Still Room may be closed during this time)

Price: £85pp – please speak to the Still Room team or call our Restaurant Reservations team to book.

_Prices exclude 12.5% discretionary service charge._

**Fine & Rare Whisky Tasting**

Indulge in an extraordinary experience as our Senior Whisky Ambassador presents a curated selection of five rare and exceptional whiskies. This exclusive tasting focuses on flavour, complemented by fascinating historical insights and detailed tasting notes for each whisky.

Ideal for enthusiasts of all levels, from beginners to whisky connoisseurs.

Time: 4.00-5.00pm, Friday to Sunday. Spaces are limited, please book in advance (The Still Room may be closed during this time)

Price: £125pp – please speak to the Still Room team or call our Restaurant Reservations team to book.

_Prices exclude 12.5% discretionary service charge._
