## Meet the team

“It’s a joy to say my job involves using my passion to create positive nature-based learning experiences for our younger guests and their families. Our ambition is to help young people develop an appreciation for the precious natural environment around them”

Alan McGonagle

Outdoor Activities Manager

#### Alan McGonagle

With a lifelong passion for his native Scottish countryside, Alan brings years of sporting and adventure experience to his role at Gleneagles – where he delivers a host of outdoor activities that use Perthshire’s beautiful natural landscape to educate, entertain and inspire.

“We are all a very hands-on multi-talented team of instructors, guides and ghillies, who love what we do and take great pride in delivering unforgettable memories for all our guests"

David Harris

Shooting & Fishing School Manager

#### David Harris

Having studied Game Keeping & Wildlife Conservation Management at Elmwood College before moving on to gain valuable experience across a number of well known Scottish sporting estates, David joined the team in 2018. David loves what he does and he now leads a team of 18 instructors.

“The best aspect of my job is meeting people, especially when they’ve never held a rod before, and I get to see them land their first fish. On my days off, you’ll generally find me on the river, trying to fool a few fish with a fly.”

Gerry Rattray

Head Ghillie

#### Gerry Rattray

Perthshire-raised Gerry Rattray fished for Scotland and had a 22-year stint in the Army before joining Gleneagles five years ago. His passion for rod and gun never left him and still finds his work puts a smile on his face.
