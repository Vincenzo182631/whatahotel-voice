## Resident Spa

With beautiful new spaces for relaxation, restoration and rejuvenation; a range of Gleneagles oil blends featuring natural botanicals from the Perthshire countryside; and spa treatments and experiences designed in collaboration with some of the world’s leading wellness brands, the Spa at Gleneagles in Scotland is not an opus on indulgence, but a vital celebration of health, happiness and life itself.

Put yourself in the caring hands of our specialist team of therapists and indulge in signature treatments, natural remedies and our very own healthy café.
