## Easter Breaks

We’ve got a whole host of activities for children of all ages during Spring with our fully-supervised Easter programme. ## May Breaks

The arrival of warmer days at Gleneagles signal it’s time to shed the extra layers of clothing and enjoy the great outdoors. From our trees bursting into green leaf to the birds nesting in the hedgerows, it’s a splendid time of year to escape to the countryside. With a wide selection of outdoor, trail-based activities from bug hunting and nature frames to tree climbing and woodland skills, as well as family favourites like stable tours, tennis and golf camps, and many more, we’ve got plenty to keep the whole family entertained.
