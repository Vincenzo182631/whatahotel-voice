FAQs

Will the renovation be noisy?

The construction will be taking place on Level 1 only on weekdays from 8:30am to 5pm. Currently, jackhammering and loud machinery is not anticipated as this pre work has already been completed.

Where can I dine at the hotel?

Located on Level 3 in Great Rooms, Breakfast Buffet available 6:30AM - 11AM. Dinner Buffet available 5PM - 9PM. We will not have a food outlet available from 11am - 4pm from 6/19 - 9/9.

Will TRACE Restaurant still be open?

TRACE restaurant will be closed permanently on June 19th. Reopening on September 9th as a new extension of our Living Room.

Will Living Room still be open?

Yes. Living Room will be relocated to Level 2 in Upstairs Bar, with operating hours from 4PM - 12AM, 7 days a week.
