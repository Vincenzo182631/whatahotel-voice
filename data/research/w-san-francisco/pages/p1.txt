# Welcome to W San Francisco

## Urban Luxury Meets Downtown Energy

Known for its cutting-edge technology scene, avant-garde spirit and cosmopolitan ethos, San Francisco is a destination unlike any other. Embodying the city's unique energy and style, W San Francisco places guests at the center of it all at a hotel steps from downtown must-sees like Union Square, Fisherman's Wharf and Oracle Park. Embrace new luxury at our hotel in San Francisco, which dazzles with...See More our signature Whatever/Whenever® service and wow-worthy amenities. Hotel rooms and suites boast sweeping downtown San Francisco views and offer sumptuous modern comforts like Davines bath amenities, pillowtop mattresses and creatively stocked minibars. Get your heart pumping at FIT®, our sleek fitness destination, and refuel anytime at The Living Room, our on-site restaurant. As the traditional home of epic events, our luxury hotel in San Francisco, California, is on the pulse of what's new, now and next.See Less

Daily Destination Fee

A daily destination fee of $30 plus tax will be charged to your folio. The daily destination fee includes a daily $25 beverage and food credit, two SFMOMA tickets (per stay), e-bike rentals (2 max per day), and daily enhanced high-speed internet access.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W San Francisco?

The check-in time at W San Francisco is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W San Francisco allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W San Francisco. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W San Francisco?

On-site valet parking is available at W San Francisco. The daily valet fee is $97.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W San Francisco have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W San Francisco has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W San Francisco booking.

What is the closest airport near W San Francisco?

The closest airport near W San Francisco is San Francisco International Airport (SFO). SFO is located approximately 11.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does W San Francisco have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W San Francisco has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
