### Premier Terrace

Stay golden in the modern comfort of a Premier Terrace. Day or night, escape the city streets and revel in the room design highlighting the work of gold miners carving out places to sleep, relax and live. Pan for gold in the beveled tent structure to expand your accommodations with an indoor / outdoor glamping experience. Make yourself at home with ample space to lounge, plus overhead heat lamps to keep you warm and cozy 24/7.
