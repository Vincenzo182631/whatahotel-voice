Welcome to the Ritz-Carlton Spa
Where Rejuvenation and Relaxation Await
Our state-of-the-art facilities cater to both ladies and gentlemen, offering a holistic experience that combines fitness, relaxation, and luxurious treatments. Our spa features a fitness area and a spacious indoor swimming pool for a serene atmosphere. Indulge in exclusive treatments designed to relax and rejuvenate your body and mind. Gentlemen can enjoy a dedicated relaxation area with a Jacuzzi, steam room, and sauna, perfect for soothing muscles. Ladies have their own tailored space with a fitness area to meet their specific needs. Whether you're here to relax, rejuvenate, or stay fit, our spa offers the perfect environment to escape, unwind, and feel your best.

Spa Guidelines
Revival Retreat Experience Pre-Requisites
The service and facilities of the Ritz Carlton spa are for use of gentlemen 18 years of age and older, lockers are available for personal items. However if you are a hotel guest, it’s recommended that you leave the valuable items at the safe in your room.
Shaving is required but not essential, for gentlemen prior to your facial treatment: please do so at least two hours before the treatment. Shaving is not required before the body treatment.
When enjoying first class spa experience, worries about what to do and where to go should be the last thing in your mind .here is some advice to ensure that the maximum pleasure from your visit to the Ritz-Carlton Spa. The Spa is the place of quiet refuge.
We need to insure that you are pampered. In revival retreat, to relinquish this experience to your fullest, we request you to insure that your mobile in placed in silent mode and you speak in low tone both for your own comfort and the comfort of the your fellow guest.

Explore the History and Culture of Saudi Arabia at the National Museum
The story of Saudi Arabia’s past is told through antiques, manuscripts, documents and displays at the National Museum. Located near our Riyadh hotel, the museum is among the top attractions in the city and offers a glimpse into the past. Our hotel concierge is happy to arrange a visit to the museum or any other Riyadh attractions.
