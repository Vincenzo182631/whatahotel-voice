# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Valet parking is offered at the hotel with in/out privileges.

### Self-Parking

Self-parking is offered at the hotel. The hotel has 4 universal electric vehicle charging stations located on the west side of the surface parking lot between the Pavilion and the hotel building. Please note that self-parking and charging fees apply.

Overnight guest

### Electric Vehicle Charging

Self-parking is offered at the hotel. The hotel has 4 universal electric vehicle charging stations located on the west side of the surface parking lot between the Pavilion and the hotel building. Please note that self-parking and charging fees apply.

Getting here

Tampa International Airport (TPA)3 miles3 miles

Transportation Options

Airport Shuttle

For airport shuttle service to the hotel, please Call 502-579-0053 to arrange your pick-up during the hours of 6A-11P. Proceed to the Ground Transportation/Baggage Claim area and follow signs for Hotel Shuttle, we will arrive within 30 minutes of your request. To schedule ADA transportation, please contact us at 813-874-1234 and we will set up your transportation.
