## RELAX, REFRESH AND RECHARGE AT OUR WATERFRONT HOTEL IN TAMPA BAY

For 40 years, Grand Hyatt Tampa Bay has welcomed generations of guests. Set on 35 waterfront acres near Tampa International Airport, the hotel offers a peaceful retreat with wildlife views and two pools. Guests can stay in villa style casitas near Oystercatchers. This anniversary year brings refreshed spaces and new experiences honoring its legacy.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Retreat to our waterfront hotel’s newly renovated, coastal-inspired rooms, many with water or city views. Upgrade your stay in Tampa to a spacious suite or a casita, a villa-style room located near the bayside pool and Oystercatchers restaurant.

Hotel Activities

Take in the sunset views along our dock and at our firepits. Enjoy a bicycle ride on the 9.5-mile scenic trail or a walk along the nature trail on our 35-acre property. Enjoy a variety of offerings and sports right here on our property, including tennis and pickleball. The hotel also connects to several hiking and biking trails that run along the water’s edge.

Fitness Center

Take advantage of the 24-hour fitness center. Our spacious 2,200-square-foot facility features Life Fitness cardio equipment, free weights, yoga mats and more. Take your workout outdoors and enjoy the scenic hiking and jogging trails along our Tampa Bay hotel property.

Oystercatchers Sunday Brunch

A beloved tradition returns at Oystercatchers with the Sunday brunch buffet, served every Sunday beginning February 1. Enjoy a generous spread of brunch favorites in a waterfront setting—everything longtime guests love, thoughtfully refreshed for today.

Celebrating 40 Years of Grand Experiences

Grand Hyatt Tampa Bay celebrates 40 years of welcoming guests and creating lasting memories. From thousands of events to being a cherished gathering place, this waterfront landmark is part of Tampa Bay’s culture. Join us as we honor our legacy and look ahead to new experiences and celebrations.

Pools

Soak up the Tampa Bay sun while sipping on your favorite refreshment at one of our two pools. The hotel’s 35-acre nature preserve provides an ideal background for guests to relax, refresh and recharge.

Oystercatchers

Overlooking beautiful Tampa Bay, Oystercatchers offers fresh, local seafood with stunning views and a serene atmosphere. Private dining spaces are also available for your next group dinner, banquet or event.

### Pets Are Welcome

Grand Hyatt Tampa Bay is a pet-friendly hotel, with dog-friendly rooms in our casitas. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

#### Pet Fees

- Up to six nights (Nonrefundable): **$100**

- Over seven nights (Additional fee): **$100**

#### Weight Limits

- Individual pet weight limit:  60Pounds

### Destination Fee

A daily destination fee of $35.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.
