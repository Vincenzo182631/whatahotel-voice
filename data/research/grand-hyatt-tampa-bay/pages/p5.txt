### Waterfront location, central to a quick Uber ride anywhere in Tampa

Four night Casita stay for a reunion was excellent! Rooms are well appointed, very clean and up to date. Hospitality by the Staff was much appreciated and unlike what most hotel experiences are now. The Casita offered a 'non-hotel feel' to the stay and friends were not far. Shuttles pick up and transport back to the hotel when you call and the ability to contact Housekeeping via text, was great. Deliveries were prompt with no need for a reminder call. Some construction at the back of the hotel was noted prior to checkin but was not obtrusive. Breakfast buffet was fresh, creative (with Chorizo gravy & biscuits) and had an international flair; also not off the charts expensive. We LOVED the water availability - stop at the Front Desk and pick up bottled water at any time or a replenishment for your Coffeemaker in the room. Nice gym and enjoyed the pool outside of Oystercatchers - a short walk.
