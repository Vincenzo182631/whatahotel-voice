# Hotel Policies

Destination Fee

A daily destination fee of $35.00 (subject to change) is applied to each room of your stay in order to provide services and amenities, which enhance the guest experience. Please contact the hotel directly for more information.

- Airport Shuttle Service
- 1 hour bike usage
- Tennis, Pickleball, and Basketball  Courts, Basketball including equipment
- Premium Internet in Guest Room
- 2 Bottled Waters Daily
- Hotel activities including Fitness classes, lawn and pool games
- Beach kits with chairs and umbrellas
- Amuse Bouche at Oystercatcher's with purchase
- Sun Block
- Infused Water in lobby
- Sauna Access
- Exclusive Nature Trail Access
- 10% Discount on Day Bed Rentals
- Firepits and Daily S'mores Kits
- 10% Discount on Kayak/Paddleboards

Pet Policy

You don’t have to leave your canine companions behind when you travel to our pet-friendly hotel in Tampa, offering pet-friendly rooms in the casitas. Service animals are not subject to the pet policy. Please contact the hotel for more information.

**Fees:**

A nonrefundable pet fee of $100 per stay (up to six nights) will be charged to your room. An additional $100 will be added for stays over seven nights.

**Weight limits:**

Up to 60 lbs

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Package Handling

We are pleased to assist with guest package handling. A $10 per package fee will be applied for all items received and delivered on behalf of guests. This fee covers processing, secure storage, and delivery to your room or pickup at the front desk.
