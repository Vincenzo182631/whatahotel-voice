# Dining

Dine where the locals dine without leaving the hotel. Savor delicious seafood and gorgeous views at Oystercatchers, named to “Tampa Bay’s 25 Most Iconic Restaurants” by the Tampa Bay Times. Try locally inspired cuisine at 1823 Kitchen & Bar, named for the year Tampa was settled. Whether you’re dining on your own or wish to accommodate a larger group, you won’t need to travel far to experience two of the best restaurants Tampa has to offer.

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

## Oystercatchers Waterfront Restaurant

Participating Restaurant

Experience waterfront dining in Tampa at Oystercatchers, where fresh Gulf seafood, oysters, seasonal coastal specialties, and handcrafted cocktails are served alongside sweeping views of Tampa Bay. From its celebrated Sunday brunch to weekday happy hour, Oystercatchers is a favorite for locals and visitors seeking one of Tampa's premier waterfront restaurant experiences.
