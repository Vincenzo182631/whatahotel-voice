## Elevate your stay

Discover an exclusive sanctuary reserved for guests in our Pearl Club rooms, Waldorf Astoria suites, Chairman’s Suite, and Royal Suite. Enjoy breathtaking vistas, top-tier service, exceptional culinary experiences, and tailored amenities for business and leisure.

## Four culinary presentations

Unwind amidst a picturesque backdrop while enjoying four culinary presentations at The Pearl Club. Indulge in breakfast specialties, afternoon high tea, evening libations, and gourmet hors d’oeuvres and canapés served throughout the day.

| Breakfast Buffet | 8 AM – 11 AM |
| Afternoon High Tea | 3 PM – 5 PM |
| Evening Libations | 5 PM – 8 PM (12 and above only) |
| Hors d'oeuvres & Canapés | 6 PM – 8 PM (12 and above only) |

## Access and Availability

Open daily from 8:00 am to 9:30 pm, this serene retreat offers exclusive lounge privileges, refined elegance, and personalized hospitality.

Upgrade your stay with Pearl Club access for AED 430 per person.

## Dress Code

Maintain a smart casual dress code during your visit.

**Gentlemen** \| Long trousers, collared or polo shirts, and closed-toe shoes required.

**Ladies** \| Elegant resort attire; dress sandals and strappy heels are welcome.

**Not Permitted** \| Flip-flops, athletic slides, and see-through garments.
