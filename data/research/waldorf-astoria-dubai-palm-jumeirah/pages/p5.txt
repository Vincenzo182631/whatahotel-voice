## Kids & Family

Experience effortless family stays with supervised Kids Club activities, tailored room amenities, and dedicated children’s dining across our resort.

### Coco's Kids Club

Unwind while young guests aged four to twelve enjoy supervised adventures at Coco's Kids Club. From mini-Olympics and team games to arts and crafts, painting, and origami, children discover engaging activities designed to spark creativity while parents enjoy quiet resort time.

OpenDaily

Weekdays9 am - 4 pm

Weekends9 am - 6 pm

### Kids Amenities

Enjoy seamless family travel with extra beds, baby cots, strollers, and vehicle safety seats prepared upon request. Young guests delight in child-sized bathrobes, plush slippers, coloring books with markers, and a custom Children's Directory designed for a comfortable stay.

### Kids Dining

Discover family dining across our venues. Dedicated children's menus are available at Peacock Alley, Palm Avenue, in-room dining, and LAO. Children five and under dine free at Mezzerie's buffet, with 50% off for ages six to 12. Social welcomes guests six and older.

### Once Upon a Brunch

Enjoy a vibrant, enchanting family brunch where children up to age 14 dine for free. Discover themed dishes across five global cuisines, a dedicated children's play area, strolling princesses, a roaming magician, and live entertainment featuring a DJ and a singer.
