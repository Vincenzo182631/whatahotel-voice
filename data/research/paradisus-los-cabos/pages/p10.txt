## Offsite Destination Inclusive® Activity

### A chocolate factory tour and “Barrio Mágico” walk

Discover the sweet essence of San José with an exclusive tour of a local chocolate factory. Uncover the fascinating journey of this iconic fruit, from its origins in America to its global impact, while indulging in a tasting of five distinct cocoa varieties. Next, enjoy a guided tour through the vibrant streets and charming squares of San José, one of Mexico's original "Magical Neighborhoods”. Subject to availability, prior reservation required.

Terms and conditions

The Destination Inclusive® experience is designed for individual reservations only and is not applicable for groups. Additional terms may apply and will be communicated upon arrival at the hotel. Contact your Destination Concierge to coordinate the excursion. Subject to change and availability.

Visit the chocolate factory and walk through the "Barrio Mágico"

- Exclusive activity for The Reserve guests consisting of a tour of a chocolate factory and a walk through the old town.
- An excursion to San José del Cabo downtown with a visit to a chocolate factory that will enable you to learn about the origins of this iconic fruit and also enjoy a Tequila tasting experience.
- The meeting point is the hotel lobby at 9:45 am and The Reserve team will welcome you and walk you to the transportation.
- Activity available three days a week (Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday) lasting approximately 3 hours, including transfers (from 10 am – 1 pm).
- The experience includes transfer, guide and cold water.
- You can make your reservation through your designated Destination Concierge.
- Maximum number of participants: there is no minimum or maximum number of participants but it is recommended for 10 people or more.
