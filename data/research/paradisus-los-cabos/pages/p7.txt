### Destination Inclusive ®

You can expect more as a The Reserve guest, Destination Inclusive being no exception. In addition to experiencing the destination’s essence in the décor, surroundings and our authentic cuisine and life-enriching activities, you'll also have the unique opportunity to enjoy offsite Destination Inclusive experiences within the local community. Discover the charm of San José with a delightful visit to a chocolate factory and a guided tour through the streets and squares of one of Mexico's Magical Neighborhoods.

### Exclusive spaces

The Reserve also promises privileged access to several private areas that cater exclusively to you. In addition to The Reserve Lounge, feel free to relax and unwind in a special area by the main pool, or take a dip or two in your very own private pool. You also have an open invitation to dine at the renowned Santé restaurant overlooking the Sea of Cortez. It's all part of The Reserve experience.

### The Reserve benefits

The Reserve pampers you all the way, from its impressive rooms and suites to its enviable amenities and preferential services. Enjoy privileged access to The Reserve Lounge and a special welcome cocktail and complimentary bottle of your favorite alcoholic beverage. Nothing is more relaxing than an exclusive aromatherapy menu with turndown service every evening, while your personal Concierge assists you. As a The Reserve guest, you also enjoy priority reservations for activities and restaurants, and exclusive access to Santé restaurant and offsite Destination Inclusive activities.

### Concierge service

Need help settling in? Want a personal tour of the resort and grounds? Whatever you need, whenever you need it, your personal Concierge is at your service. Just send an instant message and your Concierge can assist with everything from restaurant and activity reservations to collection and delivery of laundry (with charge), ironing (1 garment per person per day, non-delicate materials), and 24-hour wake-up service.
