# A Sanctuary of Natural Wellbeing

Set feet from the bay, the Spa is a place of restoration, balance, and renewal. Every treatment is performed with ESPA, pairing natural ingredients with modern therapeutic techniques.

## Introduction

Drawing on the natural beauty of our surroundings and enhanced by ESPA's expertise in advanced natural skincare, our treatments offer visible results and a deeply sensory experience, allowing you to slow down and reconnect while immersed in the beauty of Hermitage Bay.

## The Spa

Our open-air spa is wrapped in lush gardens, with the sea before you. Waves, warm breeze, botanical oils. Our therapists tailor every ESPA face and body treatment to you, whether you need to rest, renew, or restore.

Find out more

## In Partnership with ESPA

One of the world's most respected luxury spa brands, ESPA draws on the finest natural ingredients and pure essential oils to deliver immediate sensory benefits and lasting skin health. Every treatment here uses ESPA products, and the full collection is available on the property.
