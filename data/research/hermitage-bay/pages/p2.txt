# Dining at Hermitage Bay

ENDLESS WAYS TO DINE, ALWAYS WITH A VIEW

Bay Front Dining - Breakfast and Dinner

## The Restaurant at Hermitage Bay

The restaurant sits just steps away from the beach and is open on all sides to the sea breeze. Tiered levels give every table a view across the bay while the main terrace offers dining under the palms and fragrant trees. Menus change daily with the season and morning catch of the day.

The Restaurant

## La Petite Maison Antigua

Straight from the French Riviera, this celebrated restaurant brings its sun-kissed Mediterranean menu to Hermitage Bay for an exclusive Caribbean residency.

Enjoy a carefree lunch with signature dishes like truffle rigatoni and entrecôte grillée, alongside signature cocktails like the Tomatini, all served with the joie de vivre that has made La Petite Maison a Riviera classic for over 30 years.

Playful, elegant, with live music daily, this is where the Riviera meets the Caribbean.

## The Tree Bar

Tucked beneath the branches at the edge of the beach, the circular Tree Bar is where the day slows down and the evening begins. Settle in as the sun sinks into the Caribbean Sea, putting on a spectacular show every evening.

The Tree Bar

## A Seamless Experience

Our aim is to make dining selections as enticing and effortless as possible.

Each day one can enjoy:

\- Breakfast, lunch and dinner with seasonal, rotating ingredients

\- Afternoon snacks

\- Canapés and fresh sushi at cocktail hour

\- House beverages including a selection of wine, spirits and champagne.

_Premium beverages and a comprehensive wine list are offered at an additional charge. We are happy to arrange room service, private dining and picnic lunches for an additional cost._

## Organic Kitchen Garden

We believe in using only the freshest ingredients in our restaurants and bars. That's why we grow many of our own seasonal fruits, vegetables, and herbs right here on the resort in our organic garden.

Not only does this keep to an absolute minimum the number of miles food must travel to reach us, but it ensures that the quality remains second to none.

For those looking to find out more, we are pleased to offer guided tours of the garden with our gardeners.
