# Welcome to Your Caribbean Home

ABOUT OUR RESORT IN THE CARIBBEAN

If there is one word that sums up the magic of Hermitage Bay, it is ‘Ubuntu’.  A philosophy sometimes described as ‘humanity to others’ or ‘I am because you are’.

## Awards & Recognition

We are honored that Condé Nast Traveler has celebrated our achievements as Antigua's only resort to achieve the Triple Crown, recognizing our ongoing presence across the Hot List, Gold List, and Readers' Choice Awards. Additionally, for two years in a row, the MICHELIN Guide awarded us Two Keys for "an exceptional stay."

## Our Hermitage Bay Family

In our home-grown family of staff, many of whom have been here more than a decade, kindness is paramount. Kindness to each other and to our guests, who we welcome into our island home, and into our community.

Our aim is that our guests feel that this is their island home too. A home into which they are welcomed and looked after, with care and kindness from our hearts.  And a home that wherever they are from in the world, they know is here waiting patiently for their return.
