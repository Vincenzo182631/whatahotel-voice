# Villas

FIVE-STAR CARIBBEAN LUXURY

Long a favorite of those in the know, Hermitage Bay is constantly renovating and enhancing while keeping its beloved soul intact. The interiors capture the beauty of the Antiguan landscape, with each villa's layout offering plenty of space for indoor-outdoor living.

Our greatest strength remains our unparalleled location. Overlooking a private bay framed by lush gardens and dramatic hillsides, Hermitage Bay has an enduring place among the Caribbean's finest resorts.

Whether traveling for a honeymoon, a birthday, a special celebration, or simply the chance to disconnect and breathe, our Hillside Ocean Pool Villas, Hillside Garden Pool Villas, Beachfront Pool Villas, and Beachfront Villas are designed to welcome you. For those traveling together, our three-bedroom Sanctuary Villa provides a secluded retreat where all can gather in privacy.

Arriving in early 2027, the Hermitage Villa is our most spacious retreat yet, created for couples seeking the ultimate Hermitage Bay experience, with panoramic sea views, expansive outdoor living and a private pool.
