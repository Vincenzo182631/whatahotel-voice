Welcome to Hermitage Bay

# The Caribbean, Perfected

Hermitage Bay sits alone on Antigua’s west coast, its namesake bay shared with no one else. Facing the Caribbean Sea, 32 villas are scattered between the hillside and the shore. Some are steps from the water, others tucked into the trees, and all have a private pool or hot tub. Each is designed for stillness: to wake to birdsongs, to let the day unfold at your own pace, and to fall asleep to the sound of the sea.

Your stay is yours to shape. Choose the all-inclusive plan, or keep things simple with bed and breakfast, whichever suite the way you like to travel.

Accommodation

## Your Choice of Private Villas

Six different options, your choice.  Do you wish to stay on the beach and fall asleep to the sound of the waves? Or would you prefer a hideaway in the lush gardens of our hillside, surrounded by tropical flora and fauna, with your own private pool and views of the horizon?

Each villa comes with air conditioning, an outdoor shower, and a generous stand-alone tub. Wooden decks are furnished with sun loungers, day beds, swings, and either private pools or hot tubs, for the ultimate in privacy and relaxation at any time of day or night.  This includes a three-bedroom Sanctuary Villa, which is designed to be a haven amongst tropical gardens.

Dining & Drinks

## Culinary Excellence, Island Style

Cuisine at Hermitage Bay begins close to home, with local ingredients and produce from our own gardens. Chefs offer an array of cuisines across multiple dining venues, for a blend of Caribbean, Mediterranean, and international dishes. Evenings begin with handcrafted cocktails at sunset, followed by live music each night beneath the glow of flickering lights.

Wellness

## Breathe and unwind

Begin your days with guided yoga or Pilates in our pavilion, or keep your routine going in our Technogym fitness center. A team of expert instructors is on hand to meet your every need.

When it's time for recovery, head to our beachside spa, just steps from the waves. Healing rituals and skilled therapists restore and rejuvenate, leaving you renewed in body and mind.

## Antigua's Only Triple Crown Winner

We are honored that Condé Nast Traveler has celebrated our achievements as Antigua's only resort to achieve the Triple Crown, recognizing our ongoing presence across the Hot List, Gold List, and Readers' Choice Awards. Additionally, for two years in a row, the MICHELIN Guide awarded us Two Keys for "an exceptional stay."

Our proudest achievement isn't an award. It's the guests who return year after year, and whose votes and kind words made every accolade possible.
