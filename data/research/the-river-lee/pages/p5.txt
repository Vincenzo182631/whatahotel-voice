# About the hotel

The River Lee is a luxury boutique hotel in Cork set on a picturesque bend in the river, just a short walk from the city centre.

The River Lee hotel occupies a unique position in this charming and historic city, with a lively cultural hub featuring an internationally regarded opera house, independent art galleries, museums and theatres.

Cork also offers a vibrant nightlife and hosts several festivals throughout the year – notably culinary, music and film.

Additionally, Cork boasts a thriving foodie scene centred on the famous English Market alongside a number of standout eateries, and is ideally placed for visits to kiss the legendary Blarney Stone, the historic fishing town of Kinsale and the rest of Ireland’s ruggedly beautiful southern coastline.

The River Lee hotel is one of Cork’s leading business and social destinations. All aspects of Cork local life are played out here, from corporate events and family celebrations to glamorous get-togethers in the hotel’s highly popular
River Club Bar,
Terrace&
The Grill Room, the perfect place to eat, drink and socialise.

Resident guests also have complimentary access to the hotel’s in-house
health and fitness club, which features a 20m swimming pool among other modern facilities.

The River Lee is owned and managed by The Doyle Collection, an Irish family-owned luxury hotel group with hotels in Dublin, London, Washington DC and Cork.
