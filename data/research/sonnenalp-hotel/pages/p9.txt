## Winter Activities in Vail, Colorado

### Experience Winter in Vail

Winter in Vail offers much more than world-class skiing. From snowshoeing through quiet mountain trails and ice skating in Vail Village to snowmobiling adventures and memorable après-ski experiences, there are countless ways to enjoy the Colorado Rockies throughout the season.

Sonnenalp's location in the heart of Vail Village makes it easy to experience the best winter activities in Vail. Spend the day exploring the mountain, discover activities beyond the slopes, then return to the comfort of the hotel for dining, spa treatments, and a relaxing evening in the village.

## Top Winter Activities in Vail

When snow blankets the Vail Valley, the mountains come alive with outdoor recreation. Many visitors come for skiing, but there are many other things to do in Vail, Colorado in the winter beyond the slopes.

### Popular Vail winter activities include:

- Alpine skiing and snowboarding
- Cross-country skiing through scenic mountain trails
- Snowshoeing through quiet forests and alpine meadows
- Snowmobiling adventures in the surrounding backcountry
- Shopping, dining, and exploring Vail Village

## Guided Winter Hiking & Snowshoeing in Vail

### Ages 13+ (Private tours available for all ages)

Step beyond the bustle of Vail Village and discover the peaceful side of winter in Vail with a professionally guided hiking or snowshoeing tour in Vail, Colorado. These scenic excursions lead guests through quiet pine forests, snow-covered meadows, and breathtaking alpine landscapes that showcase the beauty of the Rocky Mountains in winter.

Whether you’re looking for a gentle walk through snowy trails or a more active outdoor adventure, our guided experiences are designed to suit a variety of fitness levels and interests. Each tour offers the option of snowshoes or microspikes, allowing your guide to choose the best traction for current conditions while ensuring a comfortable and enjoyable outing.

As one of the most peaceful winter activities in Vail, snowshoeing provides a unique opportunity to explore the mountains away from the ski slopes while enjoying stunning winter scenery.
