## Vail Summer Activities

Experience the best of Vail in the summer, where alpine adventure meets luxury and relaxation. From scenic hikes and mountain biking to festivals, dining, and family-friendly events, Vail, Colorado offers summer activities that appeal to everyone.

Whether you're planning a weekend getaway or an extended stay, summer in Vail is filled with unforgettable ways to explore the mountains.

## Why Visit Vail in the Summer?

Vail, Colorado in summer transforms into a vibrant mountain destination with mild temperatures, wildflowers in bloom, and endless outdoor experiences.

### Vail Visitors Love:

- Comfortable weather perfect for outdoor adventure
- Fewer crowds than peak winter ski season
- A wide range of things to do in Vail in the summer
- Walkable charm throughout Vail Village

## Outdoor Summer Activities in Vail

## Walking & Hiking Tours

Look no further for Vail summer activities. Escape the village and experience our beautiful mountain surroundings on a peaceful hike along forest trails and in local open spaces. Our professionally guided walks & hikes are uplifting, informative, surprisingly relaxing and provide just the right amount of challenge for any skill level.

Nature Walks  Hiking Tours

Nature Walks

Engage your senses during a relaxing 1-2 hour stroll along a local trail or open space. Offered anytime from sunrise to sunset, this outing will leave you feeling rejuvenated.

Ages 13+: $60.00 / person

Ages 6-12: $40.00 / person

Hiking Tours

Discover the beauty, tranquility and grandeur of the Rocky Mountains along one of several hiking trails chosen by our guide to fit your desired terrain, duration and activity level.

Recommended start time is between 8:00am-10:00am.

**2-3 Hour Hike:**

Ages 13+: $60.00 / person

## Optional Themes for Nature Walks and Hiking Tours:

## Wildflower Hike

Enjoy one of several wildflower hotspots near Vail to take in the colors of summer. Along the way, you’ll learn more about Colorado’s native wildflowers and have extra time to observe and photograph them up close.

## Kid - Friendly Family Nature Experience

Engaging children in the wonderland of nature stimulates an irresistible sense of exploration & discovery, stimulates learning & creativity, and helps burn some of that boundless energy! We have trails for all ages, offering rocks & boulders to climb on, meadows to explore, creeks & streams to play in and rugged or smooth terrain to lead you to a memorable destination.

## Nature Fix

Be guided through a more mindful visit to nature by connecting with it through all your senses. Experiencing nature in a quieter, more focused way has been scientifically proven to reduce stress, improve mood, blood pressure, mental clarity and creativity. This immersive outing through beautiful forests, meadows and beside rushing streams is truly therapeutic.

## Full Day Destination Hike

For our more experienced guests, we offer full day destination hikes to your choice of an alpine lake or mountain summit with panoramic views. Destination hikes depart by 8:00am and are 6-8 hours in duration, including drive time to/from the trailhead. $150 / person, box lunch included.

## Fitness Hike

Get your heart rate up and burn some calories during a steady & sustained uphill hike. Once the mission is accomplished, enjoy a more leisurely decent with time for sightseeing and photos. The trail, duration and pace is tailored to your desired activity level.
