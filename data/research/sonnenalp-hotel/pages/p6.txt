## Things to Do in Vail Year-Round

One of the reasons visitors return to Vail year after year is that every season offers a completely different experience. Winter brings world-class skiing, snowshoeing, and festive holiday events, while summer offers hiking, mountain biking, golf, and outdoor concerts. Fall delivers spectacular aspen colors and seasonal festivals, and spring provides a quieter opportunity to explore the village, restaurants, and surrounding mountains.

Whether you're planning a ski vacation, a summer getaway, or a relaxing mountain retreat, Vail offers activities and experiences for every season and every traveler.

### Winter Activities

#### Wintertime Adventures in Vail

From the exhilarating thrill of downhill skiing to the serene beauty of snowshoeing, Vail offers a diverse tapestry of winter activities. Nestled in the heart of the Colorado Rocky Mountains, this haven of adventure presents not only snowy escapades but also captivating ice skating, scenic gondola rides, and snowboarding on world-renowned slopes. As the snow glistens and the mountains stand majestically, there's truly no limit to the adventures awaiting you in this iconic mountain paradise.

## Explore Vail Village Just Steps from Sonnenalp

Sonnenalp's location in the heart of Vail Village places guests within walking distance of shopping, dining, galleries, outdoor concerts, and seasonal events. Spend the morning enjoying coffee and breakfast, explore the boutiques and pedestrian streets throughout the village, and finish the evening with dinner, cocktails, or live entertainment.

The ability to experience Vail without needing to drive is one of the unique advantages of staying at Sonnenalp and allows guests to fully enjoy the charm and energy of Vail Village.
