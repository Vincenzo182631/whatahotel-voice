## Modern European Luxury Suites & Hotel Rooms in Vail Village

At Sonnenalp, accommodations are designed to feel more like a private mountain residence than a traditional hotel room. Located in the heart of Vail Village, our luxury suites and hotel rooms combine European-inspired design, spacious layouts, and personalized hospitality to create an unforgettable mountain retreat.

Guests can choose from cozy hotel rooms, one-bedroom suites, and expansive multi-bedroom accommodations ideal for families, groups, and extended stays. Many suites feature separate living areas, gas fireplaces, balconies, heated marble bathroom floors, and views of Gore Creek or Vail Village. Whether you're visiting for skiing, golf, hiking, or a relaxing getaway, Sonnenalp offers some of the most spacious luxury accommodations in Vail.

## Why Choose a Suite at Sonnenalp?

Unlike many traditional hotel rooms, Sonnenalp's accommodations are designed to provide extra space and comfort for mountain vacations.

Benefits include:

- Separate living and sleeping areas in many suite categories
- Cozy gas fireplaces for relaxing after a day outdoors
- Heated marble bathroom floors and luxury bath amenities
- Balconies overlooking Gore Creek or Vail Village
- Spacious layouts ideal for families and longer stays
- In-room dining and personalized guest services
- Convenient access to Vail Mountain, dining, shopping, and the spa

Whether you're traveling as a couple or bringing the entire family, a suite provides room to unwind and enjoy the best of Vail in comfort.

## Family-Friendly Suites in Vail

Traveling with family often requires more space than a standard hotel room can provide. Sonnenalp's one-bedroom and two-bedroom suites offer separate sleeping areas, generous living spaces, and flexible bedding configurations that make family vacations easier and more comfortable.

With convenient access to Vail's ski slopes, hiking trails, summer activities, restaurants, and family amenities, Sonnenalp serves as an ideal home base for year-round adventures in the Colorado Rockies. Larger suite options can accommodate families and groups while maintaining the privacy and comfort expected from a luxury resort experience.

## Luxury Accommodations in the Heart of Vail Village

Location is one of the most important factors when choosing where to stay in Vail. Sonnenalp places guests within walking distance of Vail Mountain, shopping, restaurants, seasonal festivals, and outdoor recreation.

After a day on the slopes or exploring the mountains, return to spacious accommodations overlooking Gore Creek or the charming pedestrian streets of Vail Village. The combination of luxury lodging and an unbeatable location makes Sonnenalp one of the most sought-after places to stay in Vail year-round.
