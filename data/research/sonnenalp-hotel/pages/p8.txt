## Sonnenalp Club: Premier Golf Experience in Vail, Colorado

At Sonnenalp, we extend an exclusive invitation to our esteemed hotel guests to immerse themselves in the golfing experience of a lifetime. Challenge yourself on our private, 7,100-yard championship links-style golf course. With every swing and putt, embrace the unparalleled beauty of our meticulously designed course, ensuring an unforgettable journey from the first tee to the last hole.

Golfer Information

## Attire

All players and spectators must be appropriately attired on the golf course and practice range at all times.

**MEN** must wear a shirt with a collar at all times. T-shirts, short shorts, swim, tennis, running attire and any type of denim are not considered appropriate attire for golf.

**LADIES** are not required to wear a collared shirt but may not wear short shorts, swim, tennis, running attire, tank tops, halter tops, midriffs and any type of denim.

## Pace of Play

The desired pace of play is four hours and fifteen minutes (4:15). Players will be informed that they are out of position if there is an open hole in front of them and they are behind the desired 4:15 pace.

## Learn the Game

Private and group instruction from our staff of PGA Professionals is available and should be booked in advance through the Pro Shop 970-477-5372.

## Golfboards

New! Golfboards are available for rent at the Pro Shop starting at only $30.

### 7,100-Yard Link-Style Course & Extended Season

The Sonnenalp Golf Club is a championship, 7,100-yard links-style course set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Rocky Mountains. Rolling fairways, pristine greens, and natural water features create a one-of-a-kind layout that challenges seasoned players while remaining approachable for beginners. Thanks to its Edwards location—just minutes from Vail Village—the course enjoys one of the longest golf seasons in the Vail Valley, often stretching from April through late October.

### Preferred Tee Times for Hotel Guests

Guests of the Sonnenalp Hotel enjoy exclusive access to preferred tee times at the club, making it easy to plan your round during peak summer days. Advance booking is recommended, and staff are happy to help coordinate your tee times before or during your stay.

## Tour the Course

The Sonnenalp Club is perfectly located in the sun drenched valley of Edwards, 16 miles west of Vail Village. This down valley location gives our members and guests an extended playing season stretching from early April to late October.

Sonnenalp hotel guests have preferred access to golf tee times.

## Why Golf in Vail, Colorado is Special

### Breathtaking Scenery & Elevated Play

Golfing in Vail is unlike anywhere else. At nearly 7,500 feet above sea level, your ball flies farther, the air is crisp, and the views of the Gore Range and Sawatch Mountains are unforgettable. Every hole at Sonnenalp Golf Club highlights the natural beauty of the Colorado Rockies.

## Vail: A Golfer's Alpine Paradise

Vail, renowned globally for its world-class ski slopes, also emerges as a sought-after golf destination during the warmer months. The region boasts an array of beautifully designed golf courses that seamlessly blend with the majestic backdrop of the Colorado Rocky Mountains. These courses, with their impeccable greens and strategically placed challenges, offer golfers of all skill levels a unique alpine golfing experience. The cool mountain air combined with stunning panoramic views makes every round in Vail unforgettable. Whether you're a seasoned pro or a casual player, golfing in Vail promises a blend of sport and nature, unlike any other destination.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Golf in Vail

### When is golf season in Vail, Colorado?

Golf season in Vail typically runs from late spring through early fall, depending on weather and course conditions.

### Are there public golf courses in Vail?

Yes. Vail and the surrounding area offer several public and resort golf courses with mountain views and alpine terrain.

### What makes golfing in Vail unique?

Vail golf courses feature high-elevation play, cooler summer temperatures, scenic mountain landscapes, and championship-caliber course design.

### Can I stay at Sonnenalp for a golf vacation?

Yes. Sonnenalp offers luxury accommodations, dining, spa amenities, and convenient access to golf courses throughout the Vail Valley.

### Does staying at Sonnenalp include access to the Sonnenalp Club golf course?

Yes! One of the unique perks of staying at Sonnenalp is guest access to the private Sonnenalp Club and golf course. Hotel guests can enjoy exclusive access to the championship course, practice facilities, dining, and club amenities typically reserved for members, making it a standout experience for golfers visiting the Vail Valley.
