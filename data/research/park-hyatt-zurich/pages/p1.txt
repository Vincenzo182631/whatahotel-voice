Park Hyatt Zurich’s Afternoon Tea is one of the most renowned in Zurich and a signature experience of the hotel. It features a selection of delicate teas, exquisite champagne, and an array of sweet and savory delights crafted by Pastry Chef Tilo Herber.

## Things To Do

Situated in Zurich’s city center, this luxury hotel is close to the area’s best attractions, cultural sites, shopping and more. Just 1.5 kilometers from Park Hyatt Zurich is the world-renowned Bahnhofstrasse, where you’ll find the most exclusive, high-end shopping in Switzerland. We’re also close to Old Town, the city’s historic downtown center, lined with cobblestone streets and original architecture. The hotel is also close to several museums, the city zoo and the Limmat River.
