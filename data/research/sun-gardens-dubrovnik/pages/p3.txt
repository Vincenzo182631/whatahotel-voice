# A true holiday

RESORT EXPERIENCE

The versatile offer of Sun Gardens resort offer promises a great time whether you visit by yourself, with your loved ones or in a group of friends. With everything at your fingertips, all you need to do is relax and enjoy your holiday.

With a diverse selection of restaurants and bars, three swimming pools, a beach with a VIP area, Spa and wellness oasis, sports center offering more than a dozen sports and activities, Marco Polo club for kids, and evening entertainment program, you will spend every moment of your stay exactly the way you like.

## Resort

## Our Story

Located at the most beautiful part of the Adriatic coast, Sun Gardens Dubrovnik is a holiday destination second to none.

## Restaurants & Bars

Sun Gardens Dubrovnik's choice of restaurants and bars offers a diverse mix of tastes and venues for every occasion.

## SPA Centre

The luxurious and award-winning Spa Centre is meant for moments of absolute indulgence.

## Pools & Beach

Find your favourite lounging spot at one of the three swimming pools at the resort, or visit the beach just a few steps away and reserve your place in a private VIP area.

## Sports Centre

Challenge yourself and stay active during your stay. Choose from a large offer of water sports, indoor and outdoor activities at the resort.

## Marco Polo Kids Club

Enter an enchanting world of fun for children of any age. Enjoy a varied day and evening schedule filled with adventures that your little ones will remember for a lifetime.

## Location

The fully integrated destination resort on the beautiful Adriatic coast, located in the community of Orašac, 11 kilometres from the UNESCO World Heritage city of Dubrovnik.

## Resort Map

Navigate your getaway with ease! Explore our resort map for a seamless experience discovering all the amenities, activities, and scenic spots nestled along the picturesque Adriatic coast.

## Sun Gardens FESTA

## Children Sports Academy

Our Sports Academy offers football, tennis, squash, swimming and water-sports weekly programmes to children 7 to 15 led by experienced coaches in a fun and safe environment.

## Gourmet Experiences

There are plenty of treats for gastronomy lovers at Sun Gardens Dubrovnik with Dalmatian cuisine known for its simplicity, the freshness of ingredients and traditional recipes, passed through generations.

## Summer Animation

Get ready to be amazed this summer with a variety of activities and make the most of your holiday!

## Outdoor & Nature

Whether you prefer hiking, running or cycling, set your own pace and choose the route that suits you best, exploring spectacular views and fascinating sights on one of our suggested paths.
