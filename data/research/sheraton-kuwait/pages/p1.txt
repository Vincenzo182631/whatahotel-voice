# Welcome to Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City

## Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel in Kuwait City

Situated in the heart of the bustling business district, Sheraton Kuwait is just minutes from pristine Arabian shores, top destinations like Liberation Tower and Old Souq, and world-class shopping attractions....See More With a host of 5-star facilities, ranging from a health club and a heated outdoor pool, our hotel provides an unforgettable setting for business and leisure travelers to Kuwait City. Renowned on-site restaurants like Riccardo and Alhambra place you just steps away from some of the best dining in the city. Upon entering our hotel's breathtaking marble lobby, immerse yourself in an atmosphere of enchantment that persists throughout our 80 rooms and suites. Each hotel room features priceless antiques, contemporary technology, and 5-star amenities, showcasing an effortless blend of comfort and opulence. Allow Sheraton Kuwait to provide you with everything needed for a 5-star experience in Kuwait City.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City?

The check-in time at Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 1:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City?

On-site parking is available at Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City?

The closest airport near Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City is Kuwait International Airport (KWI). KWI is located approximately 18.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Sheraton Kuwait, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kuwait City does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
